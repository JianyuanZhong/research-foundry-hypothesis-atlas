# Admission-anchored, dictionary-independent procedure transition and early physiology in HCC

## Scientific deliverable and substantive advance

The future solver must newly freeze an adult first-observed HCC-coded admission cohort, an exhaustive admission-anchored terminal state defined without an order dictionary, exact assay/time rules, and an auditable secondary liver-directed/order dictionary. It must fit matched B0 and B1 models plus the substantive irregular B2 alternative on one patient-level split, produce locked predictions for every eligible admission, estimate the all-admission early-physiology increment with proper scoring uncertainty, report dictionary-independent and dictionary-dependent results separately, and emit time audits, missing-time bounds, falsifications, and a claim-to-output table.

This child repairs two residual vulnerabilities in the parent. First, a primary “liver-directed” label necessarily depends on a locally invented procedure vocabulary; the primary endpoint is therefore changed to a semantics-agnostic transition in the procedure table, while liver-directed families remain a prespecified secondary clinical characterization. Second, the parent’s standardized probability difference is not by itself an adequate measure of incremental prognostic information. The primary model comparison is now held-out outcome-linked log loss/Brier improvement and calibration, with the standardized all-admission risk contrast reported as a clinically readable secondary estimand. No later order or post-landmark observation is conditioned on.

## Supported evidence, unresolved question and hypothesis

The local HCC data support encounter admission/discharge times, HCC-like diagnosis strings, numeric laboratory values with native times, non-drug orders with several time/status fields, and procedure rows with native start/end times. A bounded source audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 7,781 distinct procedure strings, and 15,874 rows without a native start time. The procedure vocabulary includes liver-directed-looking strings and many non-liver procedures; no field proves completion, indication or intent. Diagnoses have no time, clinical documents/pathology have no native time, and several catalog tables are identifier-only.

The strongest supported claim is only that these recorded fields can define a time-bounded local documentation/physiology analysis after explicit audits. Existing evidence and the demonstrations do not establish that an HCC code is pathology-confirmed HCC, that an order means intent, that a procedure row means completed treatment, or that early laboratory changes cause a pathway decision.

The unresolved question is:

> Among adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission, does numeric hepatic/renal information observed during [0,24) improve prediction of a subsequent recorded procedure-table transition during [24,72), across all eligible admissions, and is its association different when the later row is secondarily classified as liver-directed with versus without preceding pre-24 order-table evidence?

The primary hypothesis is that early physiologic information adds reproducible, clinically relevant held-out information about the subsequent recorded procedure transition. The secondary mechanism hypothesis is that the increment differs by tiered pre-24 order evidence, but this is a documentation mechanism question—not intent, unplanned care, treatment effect, or clinical utility.

A supportive result therefore advances knowledge about whether early numeric physiology carries robust information about a locally recorded care transition beyond admission/pre-admission and observation opportunity. It does not show that physiology caused the transition or that a clinician should act on a prediction.

## Primary dictionary-independent terminal estimand

Use procedure rows without classifying their text. A row is time-orderable only when native `procedures.开始时间` parses and can be compared with admission and discharge. Define the first row in [0,72) that starts before discharge as the recorded procedure transition. The mutually exclusive terminal state is:

- P0: a time-orderable procedure row starts in [0,24) before discharge;
- P1: no P0, and a time-orderable procedure row starts in [24,72) before discharge;
- Uproc: no established earlier P0/P1, but a procedure row has missing/invalid `开始时间` or cannot be ordered in a way that could change [0,72);
- D0: no P0/P1/Uproc and discharge occurs before 72 hours;
- N0: no P0/P1/Uproc, discharge is not before 72 hours, and no procedure row starts by 72 hours.

If a procedure row has a valid start after discharge or after 72 hours, retain it in the audit but do not label it P1. If missing-time rows could precede a valid event, Uproc precedes D0/N0; if a valid event is demonstrably first, retain that event and report the unresolved row. These rules use only table presence and native time, not an order or procedure-family dictionary. The primary target is the full vector {P0,Uproc,P1,D0,N0}; the all-admission event estimand is the P1 probability (and separately P0 probability) standardized over every locked-test admission. No category means no clinical change, no intent, or no treatment.

The primary early-physiology estimand is the held-out improvement from B1 over B0 for this vector: mean locked-test multiclass log-loss reduction and Brier-score reduction, with calibration and per-state probability error. Report the standardized P1 probability contrast `Delta_P1 = mean[p_B1(P1)-p_B0(P1)]` only as a secondary risk-scale summary, alongside its two-sided patient-clustered bootstrap interval. A relevance threshold of 0.01 absolute probability points applies to Delta_P1 as a protocol scale, not clinical utility. A score improvement is not interpreted as support unless the state distribution is calibrated and the direction is stable.

## Liver-directed and order evidence as secondary, tiered mechanism analysis

In fitting/selection data freeze a clinician-reviewable procedure dictionary for liver-directed-looking families, preserving exact strings and exclusions. Candidate families may include therapeutic hepatic-artery embolization/interventional (E), liver-lesion ablation (A), liver resection (S), and X for mixed/transplant/other/unassignable. The dictionary is secondary: if semantics cannot be frozen prospectively, all primary P states remain valid and every liver-directed contrast is inconclusive.

For orders, use only `orders.开立时间` to establish pre-24 recorded evidence, never `开始时间` as an intent proxy. Exact terms may be mapped to E/A/S only after fitting review. Exclude routine nursing, diagnostic imaging, cancellations and composite/unassignable terms from a single-family match, but retain exact strings, `医嘱状态`, `医嘱期限`, `频次`, `开始时间`, `结束时间`, duplicate rate and missing-time rate in the audit. An order is recorded evidence only; status/duration/frequency do not prove intent or completion.

For every admission, define P0-order tier from orders opened in [admission, admission+24): K if exactly one E/A/S family, M if multiple families or potentially therapeutic unassignable evidence, N if no qualifying family, and U if potentially qualifying text has missing/invalid `开立时间`. A later procedure family is matched only if its valid native procedure start is in [24,72) and the P0 tier is K with the same family. Report later liver-directed-looking rows as matched K, unmatched Q, or unresolved/uncertain, with Q subdivided Qclear, Qmulti, Qnone and Qunknown. These are recorded-data tiers, never clinical intent.

The secondary mechanism estimand is an interaction/contrast of B1-minus-B0 score improvement or P1 risk-scale contrast across the ordered tiers, using only primary P1 events and all-admission risk sets. Do not use P0-order tier, P1, later order density, procedure text, discharge, or any post-24 field in B0/B1/B2 adjustment for the primary analysis. A large change after adding P0 tier is documentation-capture dependence, not evidence of a plan effect.

## Fitting, evaluation and uncertainty

B0, B1 and B2 use the same eligible admissions, target, preprocessing policy, patient split and locked test set. Fit all choices in buckets 0–59, tune only prespecified choices in 60–69, and evaluate once in 70–79. Produce a row-level provenance map for every feature/state and never allow an inaccessible bucket to be called external validation.

Report locked multiclass log loss, multiclass Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and observed-versus-predicted probability for each primary state, P1 discrimination as a descriptive metric, Delta_P1, state-wise uncertainty, and B2-minus-B1 changes. Use at least 500 patient-clustered bootstrap resamples (1,000 preferred), preserving the split and using a declared refit or locked-prediction procedure. Report intervals for score differences, Delta_P1, state probabilities, calibration and tier contrasts. Pool only as prespecified; do not merge Uproc with N0 or U-order with N-order.

## Falsification, missing-time bounds and robustness

The solver must emit:

1. A timestamp proof that all B1/B2 primary laboratory inputs are in [0,24), all primary procedure states use native `开始时间`, and no discharge, post-24 lab, P1, later order density or outcome field enters prediction.
2. Within-patient exact-assay timestamp permutation preserving values/counts/missingness, plus within-assay value permutation. A physiology-specific signal should attenuate.
3. Primary terminal-state permutation within service/era strata, preserving state frequencies and pre-24 covariates. Score gains and contrasts should disappear.
4. Removal/addition of pre-24 observation count, gap, density, missingness and valid-time fractions; substantial attenuation identifies observation opportunity as an explanation.
5. Procedure duplicate/same-time rules, midnight-boundary and missing-time bounds; exact dictionary perturbation and leave-one-assay/family/string-out analyses only for secondary semantics.
6. P0-order tier permutation within service/era, preserving tier frequencies; survival of the tier interaction weakens the documentation-mechanism interpretation.
7. Extreme Uproc bounds assigning unresolved rows to earliest plausible P0/P1 or to no-event states, reported as missing-time bounds rather than observed truth; sensitivity excluding Uproc.
8. A same-source routine/central-line/venipuncture workflow endpoint as a workflow negative control, never as a clinically null outcome.
9. Complete-case clear-K analysis only as a secondary selection diagnostic, explicitly compared with the all-admission estimand.
10. Secondary [72,144) procedure rows among primary members without a P0/P1, using native time and competing discharge; never use this to redefine the primary target.

Falsification of the primary physiology interpretation is declared if score improvement persists after terminal-label and lab timestamp/value permutation, vanishes when opportunity variables are represented, is dominated by a single assay/service/era or by Uproc, or is unstable across exact duplicate/missing-time rules. Adverse findings for the order mechanism include null/opposite tier interaction, survival after tier permutation, domination by U-order or unresolved procedure times, or instability after dictionary perturbation.

## Interpretation and limits

Supportive primary evidence requires complete state/time audits, no leakage, calibrated B1, a positive outcome-linked B1-versus-B0 score improvement with an interval excluding zero, attenuation under both laboratory permutations and terminal-label permutation, and a stable Delta_P1 direction. Stronger support requires B2 to improve calibrated per-state prediction over B1 and preserve the direction without being explained by observation intensity. Adverse results weaken or refute this recorded-data hypothesis; they do not prove physiology clinically irrelevant. Inconclusive results include dominant Uproc/N0, sparse event states, nonconvergence, wide intervals, incomplete local capture or failure to freeze the secondary dictionary.

A supportive result means only that early recorded laboratory information is associated with prediction of a later recorded procedure-table transition in this HCC snapshot. It does not establish a liver-directed treatment, treatment response/failure, unplanned care, recurrence, progression, organ failure, mortality, intent, appropriateness, causal effect, clinical utility, or benefit from acting on a prediction. Pathology confirmation, stage, tumor burden, vascular invasion, resectability, performance status, indication, recommendation, scheduling, completion, reason for change/discharge/absent documentation, assay units/reference ranges, complications, death/transplant and outside-hospital care require clinical adjudication, linkage or another study. Prospective chart review and external validation are required before clinical deployment.

The demonstrations were used as method context only. The natural-history source supports dated structured-history modelling and held-out calibration, but its UK Biobank/Danish data and external-validation setting are unavailable. The Bayesian source supports an EHR-only irregular latent-trajectory adaptation, but its genetic inputs are unavailable. Only the cancer supplement was available for modality-ablation context; the main cancer article and complete STAR Methods remain unavailable, and no HCC images are present. No reproduction is claimed.

Text/image models should be revisited only with time-stamped, validated text/images and clinical labels.
