## Proposed relationship

Among UK Biobank participants with valid second-assessment body-composition MRI, at similar total adiposity, higher visceral fat may be more specifically associated with subsequent metabolic disease, while higher posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) may be more specifically associated with subsequent functional decline or falls. The test is whether fat distribution adds outcome-specific information beyond total adiposity, not whether a generic health score can be predicted.
