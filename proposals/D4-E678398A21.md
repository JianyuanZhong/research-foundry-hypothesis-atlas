## Scientific opening and exact hypothesis

Shorter leukocyte telomere length (LTL) is compatible with cellular aging, but a blood measurement can also reflect leukocyte mixture, inflammation, smoking, occult disease, and general age-related mortality. The inspected literature does not settle the relevant distinction. Gao et al. [K1] report heterogeneous cancer-type associations for a genetically predicted telomere score, including signals for hematologic cancers, but that is not a measured LTL analysis and does not establish a composition- or CRP-adjusted phenotype. Mons et al. [K2] found that shorter LTL predicted all-cause mortality beyond age, whereas cardiovascular and cancer mortality estimates were imprecise. Wentzensen et al. [K3] found inconsistent colorectal reports and a non-significant pooled prospective association. These findings support an association worth checking, not the proposed claim.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among UKB participants free of registered malignant disease at the assessment landmark, does longer baseline measured leukocyte telomere length retain a disease-specific association with incident colorectal cancer and broad hematologic cancer that differs from its association with vascular mortality after adjustment for measured leukocyte composition, CRP, smoking, age, sex, BMI and assessment calendar year?

The directional expectation is that longer LTL is inversely associated with vascular death, while the CRC association is materially less inverse or null; the hematologic association is expected to depart from CRC rather than be forced to a direction because [K1] reports opposing signals within hematologic subtypes. The primary falsifiable contrasts are therefore:

- beta_CRC - beta_vascular > 0 on the log cause-specific hazard scale (CRC is less inverse than vascular mortality);
- beta_hematologic - beta_CRC != 0 (hematologic disease is not exchangeable with CRC);
- the vascular coefficient remains below zero after composition, CRP and smoking adjustment.

This is an associative disease-specificity hypothesis. It is not a claim that changing telomere length changes disease, that LTL measures a mechanism, or that the marker is clinically useful.

Clinical importance: a vascular-only gradient would support interpreting LTL primarily as a general-risk/vascular aging marker; a stable cancer-specific departure would motivate disease-specific biologic and clinical validation; a signal that disappears after composition, smoking, CRP, or washout would prevent overinterpreting LTL as a telomere-specific cancer marker.

## Population, code handling and temporal estimand

Primary population:

1. Start with one row per `eid`; fail readiness if duplicate IDs or the five-table ID equality check fails.
2. Parse T0=`53-0.0`. Convert numeric fields from their source string representation; do not treat UKB missing code `-3` in smoking as a category.
3. Require finite `22192-0.0`, positive WBC, lymphocyte, neutrophil and CRP, age, sex, and smoking in {0,1,2}. Smoking is retained as categorical never/former/current according to the field dictionary; the solver must print its decoded labels and counts.
4. Exclude anyone with a paired registry event `40005-j.0`/`40006-j.0` on or before T0 whose code begins C00-C97. Pair only the same j. A date with a missing code is not assigned a cancer type; a code with a missing date is not assigned an event time.
5. Primary delayed entry is T0+2 calendar years. Exclude anyone with any malignant event or death before that delayed entry. This is a landmark/washout design, not a claim that occult disease has been eliminated.
6. Follow from delayed entry to the earliest relevant event, death, or 2022-12-31. Confirm the observed maximum dates and that no death precedes entry. Report 2-, 5- and 10-year cumulative incidence when estimable; administrative censoring is retained for longer follow-up.

Outcome definitions:

- CRC: the first paired registry code beginning C18, C19 or C20 after entry.
- Hematologic cancer: the first paired registry code beginning C81-C96 after entry. Report C81-C91 and C92-C96 subtype counts as descriptive secondary analyses only; do not change the primary outcome after seeing counts.
- Vascular mortality: death `40000-0.0` after entry with underlying cause `40001-0.0` beginning I00-I99.
- For competing-risk estimation, other malignant cancer, nonvascular known-cause death, and unknown-cause death are retained as competing/censoring categories according to the stated estimator. Unknown cause is never silently called nonvascular. A known-cause sensitivity censors unknown cause; an all-cause sensitivity uses death date regardless of cause.
- If multiple paired codes have the same date, classify the person as having each prespecified target present on that date; do not impose an arbitrary array order. A target event after another malignant event remains a target event for its cause-specific analysis, while the primary “free of cancer at entry” rule prevents prevalent malignancy from entering the risk set.

Exposure and adjustment:

- Primary exposure is `22192-0.0), standardized to one SD using the training/development population only. `22191-0.0` is a locked measurement-scale sensitivity, not an alternative selected after results.
- Use log-transformed WBC and the log lymphocyte/WBC and neutrophil/WBC fractions to represent absolute burden and measured composition; all three source counts must be positive. A sensitivity uses log absolute counts without fractions. These are adjustment variables, not proof of mediation.
- Use log1p(CRP), categorical smoking, age at T0 (restricted cubic spline locked before holdout only if the solver can implement it identically; otherwise linear age), sex, BMI and T0 calendar year. BMI is a prespecified covariate but not a claim of complete frailty adjustment.

Primary estimands are cause-specific LTL log-hazard coefficients for each of CRC, hematologic cancer and vascular death, their two log-HR contrasts above, and standardized 10-year cumulative-incidence differences between the 25th and 75th LTL percentiles. The estimand is conditional on surviving and remaining malignant-disease-free to T0+2 and is observational.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted, not a reproduction: a frozen data-processing manifest; three full-model cause-specific coefficients with robust uncertainty; the CRC–vascular and hematologic–CRC contrasts; standardized CIFs; washout/measurement/composition sensitivity tables; and a locked temporal-holdout comparison in which the learned model's outputs are calibrated and interpreted. Completion requires these computed outputs and an uncertainty-linked conclusion; readiness or a successful import is not a hypothesis result.

Interpretable baseline, primary:

- Fit separate cause-specific Cox models for CRC, hematologic cancer and vascular death with delayed entry. The primary full model uses the same source inputs as the learned alternative: LTL, WBC, lymphocyte/WBC, neutrophil/WBC, log1p(CRP), age, sex, smoking, BMI and calendar year. A minimal age/sex model and sequential additions (composition, then CRP/smoking) quantify descriptive attenuation.
- Estimate cause-specific hazards with robust/sandwich uncertainty and Aalen–Johansen CIFs treating the other target, other cancer, and death categories as competing events. Report per-SD HRs, 25th-versus-75th percentile CIF differences, calibration-in-the-large, Brier score and log score.
- Use 500 participant-level bootstrap resamples stratified by T0 year and event category when feasible; otherwise use the model's influence/sandwich interval and label bootstrap unresolved. The bootstrap must refit the full pipeline and never tune on the holdout.
- The baseline is scientifically informative because its coefficients and sequential attenuation directly test whether LTL retains an outcome-specific signal after measured cell composition, CRP, smoking and age. It does not identify a causal effect or mechanism.

Learned alternative, same question and same inputs:

Use person-year rows from entry through year 10; target class is CRC, hematologic cancer, vascular death, other cancer, other known-cause death, or no event in that interval. ## Rival explanations and falsification

1. Leukocyte composition: compare the age/sex model with the composition-adjusted model, report WBC, lymphocyte and neutrophil coefficients and LTL attenuation, and repeat with absolute-count versus fraction parameterizations. A disappearance or reversal with plausible composition handling weakens a telomere-specific interpretation.
2. Smoking and inflammation: report never/former/current strata and prespecified CRP tertile or low/high strata, plus the sequential CRP/smoking addition. Strong attenuation confined to current smokers or high CRP is adverse to a broadly telomere-specific claim. These adjustments do not remove unmeasured exposure history.
3. Reverse causation/occult disease: repeat with 0, 2 and 5-year washouts, with the same code rules and no outcome-driven choice. A signal limited to the 0-2-year window is adverse to a durable prediagnostic interpretation; a 5-year estimate with very wide uncertainty is inconclusive, not a null.
4. Generic aging/mortality: report all-cause death and known nonvascular noncancer death sensitivities, age-adjusted and fully adjusted. If CRC, hematologic cancer and vascular death have indistinguishable LTL gradients, the disease-specific claim is not supported even if all are associated.
5. Ascertainment and calendar: report results by T0 year and use the locked 2007-08/2009-10 temporal holdout. If the contrast changes materially across calendar eras or fails holdout calibration, it is an ascertainment/transportability warning, not evidence of biology.
6. Registry/cause measurement: repeat with paired registry codes only (primary), with `41270`/ `41280` only as a clearly labeled inpatient-diagnosis sensitivity if the same namespace fields are verified, and with known-cause versus all-cause death. Unknown cause is not vascular or nonvascular.
7. Selection from complete measurements: compare complete versus incomplete baseline participants on available age, sex, smoking, T0 year and registry event support; use inverse-probability-of-completeness weights only as a sensitivity with overlap diagnostics. Do not impute missing LTL or CRP using outcome information.
8. Computation/label falsification: permute LTL within sex-by-age-band-by-T0-year strata and repeat the baseline pipeline. A similar outcome-specific pattern after permutation indicates a label, join, or implementation failure. Report the permutation distribution rather than seeking a favorable draw.
9. Measurement scale: repeat with `22191-0.0`, report the correlation and sample overlap with `22192-0.0`, and lock any clipping to training quantiles. Disagreement is an assay/scale limitation, not an invitation to select the favorable measure.

Supportive result: after the two-year washout, the full adjustment retains a vascular HR below one, the CRC–vascular contrast is positive with a 95% interval excluding zero, the hematologic–CRC contrast is nonzero and directionally stable in the 2-5 and 5-10-year checks, and the core pattern is reasonably calibrated in the later-year holdout. This supports a reproducible observational disease-specific association and justifies external replication or mechanistic sampling; it does not establish causality, mechanism, or clinical utility.

Adverse result: full adjustment removes the LTL coefficients; all three outcomes share the same gradient; the contrast is confined to the pre-washout window; the result reverses across composition/smoking/CRP handling; permutation produces a comparable signal; or learned interactions fail temporal holdout. This argues against the proposed telomere-specific disease contrast in this dataset, while still allowing LTL to be a generic aging marker.

Inconclusive result: sparse subtype cells, missing cause, poor calibration, large selection changes, or wide intervals leave the direction/contrast unresolved. The solver must not convert an imprecise null into refutation.

## What the data cannot establish

UKB registry codes and underlying cause fields do not provide colorectal screening intensity, symptom onset, stage, histology, molecular subtype, treatment, recurrence, clonal hematopoiesis, telomere assay batch details, or cell-specific telomere length. The source does not supply a separate LTL collection date. Missing/ambiguous death cause is material. Therefore neither automatic computation nor a supportive pattern can establish a molecular mechanism, causal effect of telomere length, treatment response, a clinical threshold, or benefit from intervention. Those claims require adjudicated clinical records, assay validation, mechanistic sampling, external validation, and/or a prospective study.
