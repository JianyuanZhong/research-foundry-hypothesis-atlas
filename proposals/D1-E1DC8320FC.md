# Scan-trigger / observation-process model: is the post-treatment AFP-trajectory prognostic signal for radiologic progression explained by when imaging happens?

Island: hcc (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records). Episode 9, generation branch
(complementary independent method) on the SAME question as the parent
`[prior hypothesis]` (mu ~31.6, valid, reference-delivery complete).
Status: pre-specified design + probe-verified bindings; **RESULTS ARE NOT COMPUTED HERE**
(discovery only, no compute jobs launched).

## 1. Question, opening, clinical importance

The parent (landmark Cox at t* = index+180 d) reports whether the within-patient AFP slope in
(index, t*] adds prognostic information for a dated radiologic progression event beyond baseline
AFP and beyond the same-draw ALBI (albumin/total-bilirubin) slope. Its own remaining objection:
imaging is not scheduled at random. If a rising AFP brings the next scan forward, a rising AFP
slope will *stochastically precede* a progression report merely because it triggered an earlier
look — informative observation / lead-time — with no faster tumour growth. The parent added a
detection-timing stratum (E_early vs E_late ≥90 d) but a stratifier is descriptive; it cannot
identify the *mechanism* because it does not model the scan-generating process. [K3] confirms AFP
is a routine surveillance analyte whose result feeds recall/imaging decisions, so the trigger is
clinically plausible, but the organism cannot quantify a trigger effect from a consensus text.

Opening (observed vs conjectured): **Observed** — the `examinations` table holds ~1.7 M dated
reports with progression/recurrence descriptors (parent probe P1), so *both* the observation times
(scan dates) and the event dates are recoverable. **Conjecture to test** — that the AFP-slope →
progression association is fully produced by AFP → earlier-scan → earlier-detection, i.e. the
visit process is informative.

Clinical importance: if AFP only predicts progression because it triggers scans, the correct
change is surveillance-INTERVAL policy (AFP-triggered imaging), not AFP-triggered treatment
escalation. If AFP predicts progression *conditional on the realised visit process*, the reading
is tumour-activity information supporting escalation. A clean null (ALBI/liver reserve alone) also
changes monitoring policy. These are different decisions, so the mechanism is decision-relevant.

## 2. Substantive advance over the parent, method alternative

Preserved (frozen, NOT re-opened): population (HCC cohort), index = earliest liver-directed
procedure, landmark t* = index+180 d, exposure fit only on draws in (index, t*], same-draw AFP +
albumin + total-bilirubin pairing within ±2 d on `检验时间`, ALBI definition, progression =
dated radiologic descriptor in `检查`, `患者主索引`+`就诊号` join, censoring via `出院时间`.

Two scientifically substantive additions, both pre-specified:

**(A) Observation-process (scan-hazard) model — "does AFP predict WHEN the next scan occurs?"**
Fit a per-patient recurrent-event hazard for the next imaging study in `检查` (modality keyed on
`检查`/`机器型号`; abdominal CT/US/MR), as a function of the *current* AFP slope (time-varying),
baseline AFP, ALBI slope, treatment type and age/sex. This directly tests the trigger mechanism:
a positive, well-estimated AFP-slope coefficient in the **scan** hazard is the signature of
informative observation.

**(B) Progression hazard conditional on the realised observation process.** Model the progression
hazard with the observed scan history as a time-varying covariate (time since last scan,
cumulative scan count / observation intensity), and jointly/robustly estimate the AFP-slope effect.
The estimand is the AFP-slope hazard ratio **conditional on** observation intensity, plus the
decomposition of how much of the marginal AFP-slope effect is explained by (A).

**Baseline (must be beaten to justify the extra structure):** the parent's landmark Cox (M0:
baseline AFP + ALBI grade + age/sex/treatment) and the parent's substantive M4 (joint AFP+ALBI
mixed-effects slopes → landmark Cox). The alternative is only worth its complexity if (i) the scan
hazard shows a non-null AFP-slope term (the assumption of non-informative visits fails) AND (ii)
conditioning on observation intensity materially changes the AFP-slope progression HR.

**Negative-control-analyte slope (pre-specified discriminator).** Choose ONE HCC-unrelated but
routinely drawn analyte from `检验` labels that (a) is measured in the same encounter streams and
(b) is biologically implausible as a tumour-activity marker (pre-specified candidate set fixed
before fitting; e.g. creatinine/urea or a CBC index), and fit its within-patient slope through the
identical pipeline. Under a *biological* AFP signal the negative-control slope must be null; under
*informative observation* a generic "sicker/being-watched" mechanism can inflate its slope too.
Selection, expected null and interpretation follow negative-control guidance [K1]:
a non-null negative-control slope is treated as evidence of residual observation/intensity
confounding, not as a new prognostic finding.

What the alternative reveals that a landmark Cox loses: the Cox fixes the visit process as random
and can only *stratify* detection timing; the scan-hazard + observation-adjusted model *estimates*
the visit process, quantifies how much AFP-slope signal is attributable to scan timing, and yields
a conditional estimand that is the quantity a surveillance-interval decision actually depends on.
[K2] supplies the tractable joint/two-part longitudinal–survival fitting (one-dimensional
integration, EM) that we extend with an explicit observation-process hazard and two-part exposure.

## 4. Fitting target, split, evaluation, budget

- **Targets (fit, not given):** (i) scan-hazard coefficients incl. AFP-slope (time-varying); (ii)
  progression-hazard AFP-slope HR conditional on observation intensity; (iii) marginal (landmark
  Cox) AFP-slope HR for decomposition; (iv) negative-control-analyte slope HR; (v) bootstrap C-index
  deltas vs M0/M4.
- **Split:** temporal split on index date (e.g. earliest 70% of index dates train / latest 30%
  held-out confirmation), fixed before fitting; also a random split as sensitivity. All pre-processing
  fit on train only.
- **Evaluation/uncertainty:** hazard-ratio CIs via bootstrap (≥1000); ΔC-index with bootstrap CI;
  scan-hazard fit assessed by integrated Brier score / calibration; report the AFP-slope coefficient
  *and* its attenuation after observation-intensity adjustment (a pre-specified proportion).
- **Budget:** CPU only. Landmark/Cox + mixed-effects + recurrent scan hazard on a single-institution
  cohort: ~4 CPU, ~16 GB, well within the ≤1200 s discovery envelope and the solver's compute limits;
  no GPU justified (tabular/low-dimensional joint fit; GPU would not add scientific value).

## 5. Falsification: supportive / adverse / inconclusive mapping

Primary falsification is **conjunctive**: a *biological* AFP signal requires
(a) AFP-slope HR for progression with 95% CI excluding 1 AND
(b) the scan hazard's AFP-slope term NOT explaining it (adjusted HR retains ≥ a pre-set fraction of
the marginal HR) AND
(c) the negative-control-analyte slope HR CI includes 1.

- **Supportive (tumour-activity reading):** (a)∧(b)∧(c). AFP-slope adds prognostic information
  conditional on observation intensity, and the negative control stays null → supports escalation
  interpretation; still NOT causal without adjudication.
- **Adverse (informative-observation reading):** scan hazard shows a strong AFP-slope term AND the
  observation-adjusted AFP-slope HR attenuates toward 1 (and/or negative-control slope is non-null)
  → the parent's signal is largely explained by the scan-trigger process; supports surveillance-
  interval, not escalation. This is the hypothesis's own refutation path.
- **Inconclusive:** CIs wide / event counts or scan counts too sparse / negative control unstable →
  no directional claim; report the identification ceiling and defer.

## 6. What is NOT identifiable without radiologist adjudication (explicit limits)

- The progression flag is an **unvalidated lexical extraction** from `检查所见`/`检查诊断`; the exact
  event date = report date, not true radiographic growth onset. Without radiologist review we cannot
  confirm lesion-level progression or separate true progression from reactive/benign findings.
- Scan `检查`/`机器型号` text must be curated to true abdominal cross-sectional imaging; ambiguous
  modalities leave the observation process partially misclassified.
- Even a correct scan-hazard model identifies *timing* association, not the clinician's trigger
  policy (we do not observe the order "AFP value → order scan"); a causal trigger claim needs order
  data (order timestamps in `data_医嘱(非药品)...`) plus expert review, or a prospective design.
- No mRECIST PFS / mortality ascertainment → no survival claim; single institution; lab numeric
  results have no unit column, so cross-assay pooling is unsupported.
- The negative-control-analyte choice is a design judgement; an imperfect negative control can be
  non-null for reasons other than observation bias — treated as a limitation, not proof.
