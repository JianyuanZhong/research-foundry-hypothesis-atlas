# Source-bound successor: workload-proxy envelope for dual-capacity HCC documentary-M2 allocation

Parent anchors: `[prior hypothesis]` (dual patient-slot/whole-accession capacity and source-bound HCC experiment) and `[prior hypothesis]` (identity/content robustness and coupled source/onset/reader/pathology states).

## 1. Targeted repair and unresolved decision question

The inherited experiment correctly fixes two different resources: at most (K) offered patients and at most (C) eligible whole-accession units. It also correctly charges every eligible accession, including one whose stored report is not exposed at the decision cutoff. The remaining clinically consequential weakness is that even a whole-accession count is not a workflow-burden measure. Accessions can contain different numbers of stored components and very different report payloads; the source has no image-series count, protocol complexity, radiologist minutes, staffing, queue-delay, RVU, or non-countable-activity field.

The repair therefore preserves the inherited unit budget as the primary contract and adds a locked, source-computable workload-proxy envelope. Each proxy is calibrated to the same 10% development-work budget and is applied to the identical ranked prefixes, patients, states, and tie keys. No proxy is asserted to be actual time. The question is:

> Does the frozen report-semantic queue retain its documentary-M2 patient advantage, with zero identity/content loss, when the same patient-slot allocation is constrained successively by whole-accession units, distinct stored components, and report-payload complexity tokens?

This matters because a positive patient-slot result that disappears when high-complexity documentary bundles consume more capacity would not justify archive-linked silent-mode validation for a scarce review service. Conversely, a result stable across the envelope would identify a stronger retrospective resource-robustness signal, while still requiring local workflow calibration before staffing or deployment decisions.

The primary hypothesis is falsifiable:

> At the inherited 2020-selected (q^*<1), G has strictly greater than 5 documentary-M2 cases per 100 offered patient slots than B, R, Gmask, and every frozen random baseline in both 2020 and untouched 2021, with zero patientwise identity/content loss, under the inherited accession contract and every nonvacuous member of the preregistered workload-proxy envelope; all source, onset, reader/pathology, temporal-quarantine, deletion, and EMPTY-position gates pass.

This is a finite-census documentary allocation hypothesis. It is not a hypothesis about radiologist minutes, report availability, biological microvascular invasion (MVI), treatment, safety, fairness, benefit, or future-patient performance.

## 6. New workload-proxy envelope

For each eligible nonblank whole accession u = (patient, encounter, Examination Number), let R_u be all raw examination rows in that accession within the inherited CT/MRI window, and let D_u be the set of distinct payload digests under the inherited exact-byte rule. Costs are computed in the same coupled source/operation/accession world as the queue, before any favorable onset, reader, pathology, or deletion state is selected.

Define three locked documentary burden contracts:

1. **Unit contract (U):** b_U(u) = 1. This is exactly the inherited whole-accession budget.
2. **Component contract (P):** b_P(u) = max(1, |D_u|). Distinct payload components in a multirow accession consume separate tokens; identical repeated rows do not create extra components. A conflicting accession therefore consumes all distinct stored payload components rather than being made artificially cheap.
3. **Report-complexity contract (T):** b_T(u) = 1 + ceil(L_u / 512), where L_u is the sum of UTF-8 byte lengths of the raw `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis` strings over the distinct payload digests in D_u. Empty report-surface text has L_u = 0 and still costs one token. No semantic tokenization, language model, truncation, or favorable row selection is allowed.

These are documentary burden proxies, not actual workload. They are deliberately computed from source fields available in the examination table and are not predictors, ranks, labels, or evidence that a report was viewable. They charge the complete reserved bundle even when M = 0. A zero-accession patient remains in the denominator with zero cost; a patient with an ambiguous accession is represented in its coherent source worlds and fails CICA, never silently receiving a cheap cost.

For each j in {U,P,T}, compute W_ref,j as the minimum total sum_i b_j(i) across all required 2015–2019 development/lock coupled states, then freeze C_j = floor(0.10 W_ref,j) in the signed 2019 artifact. Retain inherited K = floor(0.10 N_ref), with N_ref the minimum eligible patient count across those states. Require N_ref >= 500, K >= 50, every C_j >= 50, and at least 50 documentary-M2 events in each primary year/world. The 5% and 20% patient and burden fractions are locked descriptive sensitivities only; they cannot rescue the 10% confirmatory result.

For method M and contract j, rank patients by the unchanged frozen score/tie key. Select the longest prefix S_M,j containing the first m ranked patients, where m is the greatest integer satisfying both m <= K and cumulative burden sum from rank 1 through m <= C_j.

If the next patient exceeds the burden budget, stop. Never skip, backfill, rerank, alter K, substitute a favorable cost, or pool states. Report m, used burden, unused burden, utilization, stopped rank, and every EMPTY position. The inherited U queue remains the primary contract; P and T are the predeclared workload-proxy robustness contracts. Also report the joint conservative queue whose prefix satisfies all three cumulative budgets, but do not substitute it for any marginal contract.

The all-content reference queue, fixed B/R/G/Gmask comparators, 256 hash-random permutations, and anchored patient deletions use the same contract j, cost ledger, patient set, state, and tie key. An anchored deletion removes all source rows for one patient, freezes surviving ranks and costs, and leaves the selected position EMPTY; it never reclaims or reallocates capacity.

    Delta_G,C,j = 100 * (H_G,j - H_C,j) / K.

## 9. Falsification, interpretation, and external bridge

Primary status precedence:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: any source/hash/schema/ordinal, parser, join, clock, quarantine, model/book, identity, onset, exact arithmetic, queue, cost, EMPTY, deletion, patientwise-max, output-linkage, or q*-freeze failure.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: required N/K/C, event count, reader/pathology completeness, nonvacuity, or any contract has insufficient support.
3. `selection_adverse_no_subcomplete_threshold`: the unchanged inherited U rule has no valid q*<1.
4. `dual_capacity_2020_adverse`: at frozen q*, the inherited U contract fails a required 2020 comparator, random, deletion, or patient-content gate.
5. `workload_proxy_2020_nonrobust_adverse`: U passes in 2020 but P or T has any 2020 strict-margin, identity/content, deletion, or source failure; this is the targeted falsification and cannot retune q*.
6. `temporal_nonreplication_adverse`: all required 2020 U/P/T gates pass but untouched 2021 fails under any required contract.
7. `patient_slot_artifact`: the inherited K-only diagnostic is supportive but U or the proxy envelope is adverse.
8. `finite_corpus_workload_proxy_supportive`: q*<1, U/P/T all pass in 2020 and 2021, all inherited gates pass, and witnesses replay exactly.
9. Append `lifecycle_availability_unknown` to every status: HCC has no report lifecycle evidence.
10. Append `workflow_minutes_unidentified` to every status: no local time-motion/RVU/image-burden calibration exists.

Supportive means only that the locked queue is robust in this finite HCC snapshot to the three declared documentary cost contracts, patient identity/content uncertainty, coupled reader/pathology/onset/source states, and temporal replication. Adverse means the specified contract or witness fails; it does not prove semantic imaging is useless. Inconclusive means computation, feasibility, or missing evidence prevents the relevant claim.

Automatic verification can establish source lineage, declared joins, exact byte-based costs, coupled states, queue arithmetic, comparator/random/deletion gates, threshold freeze, and whether prose follows computed witnesses. It cannot establish that component count or UTF-8 length measures human reading time; that report text was authored, finalized, released, retrieved, or viewed; that pathology is specimen-linked biological MVI; or that a queue improves clinician action, safety, equity, outcomes, cost-effectiveness, transportability, or patient benefit.

Verifier adversaries must include: equal K and equal U cost but different P/T costs; identical repeated rows versus distinct payload components; blank report text retaining one token; an unexposed accession retaining its full reserved cost; an over-budget next patient with a lower-ranked feasible patient proving no skip/backfill; a selected-patient anchored deletion leaving EMPTY; a 2021-only mutation proving q* and all 2019/2020 freeze artifacts are invariant; a valid U result with adverse P or T; and an invalid attempt to retune q after P/T failure. Claim-linkage fixtures must pair correct computation with false statements of measured minutes, report availability, biological MVI, or benefit and reject those statements. Separate valid fixtures must accept narrow interpretations of supportive, adverse, and computationally/feasibility-inconclusive outputs, including an adverse hypothesis result as scientifically valid rather than a solver failure.

The external bridge is a separate local archive/workflow study: immutable report-version IDs and hashes; authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrieved/readable/view timestamps; image-series/protocol and report-complexity fields; accession-level expert time-motion or validated work-RVU calibration; staffing and queue-delay measurements; specimen-linked pathology adjudication; and prospective silent-mode measurement of reader action. That study may calibrate or reject P/T; it cannot retroactively turn this retrospective result into a clinical deployment claim.
