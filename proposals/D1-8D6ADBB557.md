# Prospective verification of EHR restart accuracy, including pump-native rescue missed by EHR

## Purpose and substantive advance

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It retains the parent’s completed, no-sampling retrospective audit and every failed causal, positivity, prognostic, and mechanical-circulatory-support (MCS) gate. The substantive repair is to make the prospective validation a full consecutive diagnostic-accuracy cohort rather than a PPV-focused review of EHR-positive restarts. Every eligible stop is verified, including an EHR-negative stop that has a pump-native rescue. Thus a false-negative pump-native rescue cannot disappear through positive-only sampling, post-index exclusion, or an unavailable-reference category.

The clinically important decision is whether a charted restart is a defensible component of a future vasopressor-weaning endpoint. The strongest claim supported by the available MIMIC audit is limited: a six-hour EHR endpoint is computable but restart-dominated and unstable under plausible transaction joins. It does not establish that an EHR restart reflects pump delivery, a genuine cessation, clinician intent, rescue, or treatment effect. The unresolved hypothesis is prospective:

> In consecutively observed adults meeting the prespecified dual-low-dose NE-plus-VP stop phenotype, the six-hour EHR restart index has adequate arm-specific diagnostic accuracy for an intent-qualified pump-native rescue event, and its PPV and sensitivity are symmetric between NE-first and VP-first observed-stop arms.

A supportive result would justify carrying a validated restart component into a later, separately designed trial. An adverse result would prevent a chart-only restart from being used as if it were rescue. Neither result recommends which drug should be stopped first, establishes safety, or estimates benefit.

* Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, the observed assigned-strategy actions were only 211 NE-first (3.49%) and 167 VP-first (2.76%), below the frozen 5% positivity gate. The 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls.
* The observed first-stop cohort has 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate failures; it is not adequate for a direct arm comparison. Observed stop order is not randomized or exchangeable.
* Of 136 determinate inherited episodes, 73 met the raw composite; 59 failures involved a stopped-agent restart and 36 were restart-only. The narrow transition-compatible screen yielded 13 restarts, including six restart-only labels, but removing those six would still leave 67 failures.

This is label instability, not a false-positive rate.

* The compact prognostic models failed the inherited gates: approximately 0.000174 and 0.000111 Brier improvements, uncertainty spanning zero for the former, and adverse later transport. No score, threshold, subgroup, or prognostic enrichment advances.
* MIMIC medication transactions are documentation signals, not pump delivery or clinician intent. No causal discontinuation-order effect, bedside recommendation, safety claim, or efficacy claim is estimated.

## Stage 1: reproducible retrospective audit, unchanged

The retrospective population is exactly the inherited 137-episode cohort: one first qualifying non-simultaneous documented stop in the first ICU stay for each adult. `t0` is the inherited end of the first-stopped agent’s reconstructed positive interval, after at least six hours of concurrent NE and VP while the comparator remains active. Eligibility was known by `t0`: the patient was alive and in ICU; NE was greater than 0 and at most 0.20 mcg/kg/min; VP was greater than 0 and at most 0.04 U/min; neither was increasing in the preceding 30 minutes; no active epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine was present in the preceding two hours; and the inherited pre-stop MAP coverage/stability rule was met. Future restart, MAP, ICU exit, and death remain outcomes or ascertainment variables.

The retrospective window is `(t0, min(t0 + 6 hours, ICU exit, death)]`. Positive normalized infusion intervals require a positive rate, supported units, and `endtime > starttime`. NE item `221906` is retained in mcg/kg/min or converted from mcg/min using recorded `patientweight` only for 30–300 kg; VP item `222315` is converted from units/hour to units/minute or retained in units/minute. Values above 5 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.2 units/minute for VP are rejected. `orderid` and `linkorderid` are continuity signals only; `statusdescription`, `storetime`, `caregiver_id`, `isopenbag`, and `continueinnextdept` do not prove delivery.

The exact symmetric reconstruction rules remain: R0 row-exact (overlap/exact contiguity), R1 shared chain within 15 minutes, R2 shared chain plus 0.8–1.2 dose ratio within 60 minutes, R3 shared chain within 60 minutes, and R4 0.8–1.2 dose ratio within 60 minutes without a chain. ## Stage 2: locked prospective population, index, and reference event

### Population and index time

At each participating ICU, screen **every consecutive** adult who meets the phenotype below during the accrual period. Do not enrich for an EHR restart, review-priority stratum, deterioration, arm, site, or MCS status. Retain all eligible stops, all EHR-negative stops, and all cases in which pump and EHR traces later cannot be linked; an unlinked case is an ascertainment result, not a reason to omit the case.

Eligibility is assessed using only data available at the stop landmark `t0`:

1. NE and VP have both been supported concurrently for at least six hours.
2. NE is >0 and <=0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP is >0 and <=0.04 U/min.
3. Neither infusion increased during the preceding 30 minutes.
4. No epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine is active during the preceding two hours.
5. At least three valid MAP observations cover three of the four preceding 30-minute bins, including the final bin; pre-stop median MAP is >=65 mmHg and minimum is >=55 mmHg.
6. The patient is alive and in the ICU at `t0`.

The index is the first clinician-executed or documented **non-simultaneous stop** of one drug while the comparator remains active. The arm is `NE-first` if NE is stopped first and `VP-first` if VP is stopped first. This is an observed arm label, not assignment. The EHR index `I` is positive exactly when the first normalized positive interval for the stopped drug has `starttime` strictly in `(t0, min(t0+6 hours, ICU exit, death)]`; otherwise `I=0`. No future information is used to define eligibility, arm, or `I`. A death or ICU exit before six hours is a valid truncated ascertainment window if the synchronized data are complete through exit/death; it is not silently excluded.

### Reference event, including false negatives

The primary reference event `T` is a binary, intent-inclusive **pump-native rescue** definition within the same six-hour window. `T=1` only when all of the following are met:

* a synchronized pump trace demonstrates a genuine stopped-drug off interval of at least **5 minutes** after `t0`;
* the same stopped drug is subsequently delivered by the pump at a rate at least **10% of its pre-stop delivered rate** for at least **60 cumulative seconds** within the window; and
* two blinded critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators, using the time-local packet, document that the resumption was intended for hemodynamic support rather than a documentation split, bag/syringe or channel transition, transfer continuation, technical pause, procedure, or correction.

`T=0` requires a complete synchronized packet and adjudication establishing no qualifying intent-inclusive pump-native rescue during the observable window. If pump delivery, clock linkage, or intent cannot be resolved, `T` is indeterminate (`T=U`), not negative. The thresholds are locked before opening arm-specific accuracy results after a technical run-in. The 1-, 5-, 10-, 30-, and 60-minute off-duration analyses and 1%, 10%, and 25% delivered-rate sensitivity analyses are secondary and cannot replace the primary definition.

Crucially, a pump-native rescue with no subsequent EHR interval is explicitly classified `T=1, I=0` and contributes a false negative to the index. It remains in the all-consecutive denominator and is reported separately by arm, site, unit, year, and capture quality. A charted restart with no delivered pump drug is `I=1, T=0` (or `T=U` if the packet is incomplete), not a presumed rescue. Uninterrupted delivery split by documentation or consumable transition, technical/procedural pause, transfer continuation, timestamp mismatch, and EHR-only interval are prespecified error categories rather than post hoc cleaning opportunities.

A secondary pump-only reference (`T_p`) removes the intent requirement but retains the same pump-off and delivered-rate criteria. This separates technical concordance from clinical-intent validity; the primary conclusion is based on `T`, not on `T_p`. Actual MCS state is captured from a device/registry source where possible. No missing MCS record is coded as absence and MCS is not part of a retrospective exclusion rule.

## Required prospective capture and adjudication

For every consecutive stop, collect source identifiers and synchronized timestamps for:

* pump/channel ID, drug-library identity, concentration, programmed and delivered rate, start/stop/pause/resume, bolus, alarm, channel association, seconds-level clock, export gaps, and downtime;
* bag/syringe identity and replacement, empty-container, line or concentration change, channel handoff or replacement, transport/procedure pause, transfer, and reconciliation;
* EHR medication data corresponding to MIMIC `inputevents`: drug/item, `starttime`, `endtime`, `storetime`, rate/unit, amount/unit, total amount/unit, weight, `orderid`, `linkorderid`, status and administration audit trail;
* timestamped clinician stop/restart intent, permanent discontinuation versus temporary pause/titration, reason, rescue intent, and relation to hypotension or perfusion concern; missing intent remains unknown;
* high-frequency MAP with arterial/non-invasive source and quality flags, all vasoactive channels, fluids/boluses, death and ICU exit; and
* MCS device type, insertion/removal, active/standby state, flow/support setting, and independent device/registry confirmation.

The packet is assembled for every stop from five minutes before `t0` through six hours after `t0`, with a further 30-minute context margin for clock reconciliation. Two independent adjudicators are blinded to arm-level accuracy, model predictions, and aggregate outcomes. They classify delivery and intent independently before assigning the primary `T`; disagreement is resolved by a third reviewer. Report raw agreement, chance-corrected agreement, the number and reasons for indeterminate cases, and adjudicator-specific agreement. A technical run-in locks the minimum off-duration, delivered-rate threshold, clock offset tolerance, and packet completeness rules before primary arm-level results are inspected.

## Co-primary estimands, denominators, and full verification

The analysis set is all consecutive eligible stops with a defined observed arm. No positive-only verification and no outcome-dependent sampling are permitted. The primary accuracy estimands, separately for arm `a` in `{NE-first, VP-first}`, are:

* **PPV:** `P(T=1 | I=1, T is determinate, arm=a)`, with denominator `N_{I=1,T∈{0,1},a}`;
* **sensitivity:** `P(I=1 | T=1, T is determinate, arm=a)`, with denominator `N_{T=1,a}`.

The co-primary symmetry estimands are the absolute arm differences `|PPV_NE - PPV_VP|` and `|Sensitivity_NE - Sensitivity_VP|`. The prespecified symmetry margin is 0.10 for each. The primary report includes the cross-tabulation `(I,T)` in each arm, including `T=1,I=0` false-negative pump-native rescues. Exact two-sided 95% intervals are reported for each accuracy estimate and a prespecified stratified/bootstrap or Newcombe-type interval for each arm difference, with site as the resampling/stratification unit if multi-site data permit. The statistical analysis plan must not replace a confidence interval by a point estimate.

Secondary diagnostic estimands use the same all-consecutive cohort: specificity `P(I=0 | T=0)`, NPV `P(T=0 | I=0)`, false-negative rate `P(I=0 | T=1)=1-sensitivity`, false-positive rate `P(I=1 | T=0)`, and the pump-only confusion matrix. Denominators are always stated. `T=U` is excluded from determinate PPV/sensitivity/specificity/NPV numerators and denominators but remains in the overall eligible denominator, is reported by arm, and is incorporated in conservative bounds: for a validity gate, assign indeterminates adversely in the direction that lowers each accuracy estimate and increases each absolute arm difference. A complete-case estimate may be shown only alongside these bounds, never instead of them. Missing pump export, unresolvable clock, and unavailable intent are indeterminate unless an independent source proves `T=0`.

A false-negative rescue can therefore affect both co-primary dimensions: it lowers sensitivity and, if its frequency differs by arm, can breach the symmetry margin even when PPV looks excellent. The study cannot declare the endpoint valid from PPV alone.

## Accrual, precision, and sequential stopping

Each site first processes 50 consecutive eligible stops as a blinded technical run-in. The site proceeds only if at least 95% have linkable pump/EHR clocks and at least 90% have complete pump export for the candidate window. A failed site pauses, repairs capture, and repeats a new consecutive 50-stop run-in; if the gates cannot be restored, it contributes feasibility data only and is not represented as a successful validation site.

Validation accrues all consecutive eligible stops until each observed arm has, if feasible, at least 150 index-positive stops (`I=1`), 150 reference-positive stops (`T=1`), and 150 reference-negative determinate stops (`T=0`), or the fixed cap of 2,000 eligible stops per arm or 36 months, whichever comes first. These are precision targets, not quota-based verification: every intervening and otherwise eligible stop remains enrolled and adjudicated. If prevalence makes a target unreachable, the cap yields an honest precision assessment rather than positive oversampling.

There is no early declaration of success. After at least 50 determinate index-positive cases in an arm, an independent monitor may stop that arm for adverse PPV only if its multiplicity-adjusted one-sided 98.75% upper exact binomial bound is <0.80. After at least 50 determinate reference-positive cases, the analogous rule applies to sensitivity. A site stops for capture failure when the clock/export gates fail and cannot be repaired. If, after at least 50 cases in the relevant denominator, the indeterminate fraction exceeds 5% in an arm and cannot be reduced by resolving capture defects, the result is adverse for operational validity or inconclusive if the reference itself is intrinsically unavailable; the distinction is documented rather than hidden.

At the final look, the endpoint-validity claim is supportive only if, in both arms, PPV and sensitivity point estimates are at least 0.80, their conservative lower 95% bounds are at least 0.80, each absolute arm difference has an upper 95% confidence bound below 0.10, indeterminate cases are below 5% (and their adverse bounds do not overturn the gates), and results are not materially concentrated in one site, unit, pump system, calendar period, or clock-quality stratum. The 0.80 thresholds and 0.10 margins are operational validation criteria, not estimates of patient benefit. If the final cap does not supply these denominators or precision, the outcome is inconclusive rather than a pass.

## Interpretation and falsification criteria

**Supportive.** Both arms meet the PPV and sensitivity lower-bound gates and the two symmetry gates, with low indeterminacy, adequate clock/export capture, stable results across the locked threshold sensitivities and sites, and no substantial arm-differential false-negative stratum. This supports using a restart component with the validated pump/EHR capture process in a subsequent prospective endpoint study. It does not validate the other six-hour composite components, prove clinical importance, establish safety or efficacy, establish MCS absence, or identify a superior stopping order.

**Adverse.** Either arm fails PPV or sensitivity, either false-negative rate is high, either absolute accuracy difference reaches 0.10, or a material fraction of EHR-positive intervals are uninterrupted delivery, technical pauses, transfers, clock artifacts, or no-delivery events. This falsifies raw EHR restart as a sufficiently accurate and symmetric rescue endpoint. A high PPV with poor sensitivity is specifically adverse to a complete endpoint: it means pump-native rescue is being missed and cannot be ignored. The remedy is pump-native endpoint capture or a newly validated index, not positive-only reporting.

**Inconclusive.** The reference or capture is too often indeterminate, clock/export gates fail, either arm lacks the required denominators, confidence intervals remain too wide, sites are nontransportable, or the 0.10 symmetry margin cannot be resolved by the cap. Inconclusive is not evidence of validity or invalidity and cannot be converted to success by lowering thresholds, excluding EHR-negative cases, or treating MCS non-records as absent.

## Computational verification boundary

A verifier can reproduce the 137-episode no-sampling audit; exact item/unit normalization; first-stay and time-window rules; R0–R4 adjacency and invalidation counts; raw restart and MAP chronology aggregates; provenance hashes; and, in the prospective package, all consecutive-cohort inclusion flags, `(I,T)` confusion matrices, denominators, exact intervals, indeterminate bounds, arm-difference intervals, threshold sensitivity tables, and stopping decisions. It can detect a claim of high sensitivity when computed false negatives contradict it.

A verifier cannot decide whether a pump actually delivered drug, whether an off interval was genuine, whether a restart was intended as rescue, whether MCS was absent, whether the reference threshold is clinically appropriate, whether missing data are ignorable, or whether a future stopping-order trial is safe, ethical, or beneficial. Those claims require synchronized device evidence, blinded clinical/pharmacy adjudication, expert review, and another study. The locked conclusion from MIMIC remains adverse for a single chart continuity repair and noncausal; the child’s prospective experiment is the only proposed route to test the endpoint without ignoring pump-native rescue missed by EHR.
