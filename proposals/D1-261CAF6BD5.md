# A sealed domain-balanced transport audit of pulmonary-nodule admission routing

## Decision bottleneck and targeted repair

The inherited clinical question is unchanged: in the frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC population, can admission-level `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routing be reproduced with sufficiently low text-defined unsafe cross-route contamination to justify only a prospective *silent* validation? One unsafe minority recommendation unit still controls the entire admission. The target is not guideline appropriateness, patient safety, follow-up completion, or clinical benefit.

The assessed-valid parent repaired incorporation and differential-verification bias with separated recommendation, context, and discharge-plan views; two sealed index lanes; two untouched audit lanes; route-independent review of every sampled admission; locked component matching; exhaustive unit states; and adverse finite-population inference. Its remaining consequential weakness is that a pooled probability audit can average away a clinically important failure concentrated in a later era proxy or a distinct service workflow. A pooled `AUTO` contamination bound of 5%, for example, can coexist with substantially higher contamination in a minority service, and pooled physician-hours can conceal an infeasible reconciliation burden in that same service.

This child therefore preserves the parent's clinical estimand, population, chronology, canonical artifact, unit definitions, component views, sealed audit, route mapping, and strict noncausal ceiling, but changes the *outcome-blind allocation and confirmatory analysis* to make temporal-proxy and service-domain failures visible. It adds:

1. a deterministic, conservative possible-calendar interval derived from `anchor_year_group`, with a sealed late-domain holdout and an explicit prohibition on treating shifted MIMIC years as calendar time;
2. frozen pre-departure terminal-service and service-path domains, including unknowns rather than deleting them;
3. domain-balanced stratified sampling with positive inclusion probability in every occupied sampling-stratum-by-era-by-service cell while retaining the inherited fixed total sample formulas and 900-admission ceiling;
4. leave-one-service-domain-out audit reporting for a route that is never fitted on any real record;
5. domain-specific route-purity, unsafe-minority, capture, drift, precision, and workload gates with familywise simultaneous uncertainty; and
6. an adverse rule under which a sparse or unsupported domain is inconclusive, never silently rescued by pooling or shrinkage.

“Held out” here means external to all real-record tuning and sealed until the protocol and complement outputs are frozen. It does **not** mean an external hospital. MIMIC contains one deidentified site collection, and no result in this experiment can support an external-site transport claim.

### Unresolved claim

After all definitions and route code are frozen without real-record tuning, are pooled route purity, unsafe-minority capture, and physician workload stable in a conservatively defined late possible-calendar domain and across pre-departure service workflows, or does pooling conceal a domain in which `AUTO` is contaminated, `RECON`/`DEFER` misses unsafe or uncertain units, or review workload becomes operationally infeasible?

### Falsifiable hypothesis

For the same frozen `N_A`, the parent's pooled route gates and each prespecified domain-transport gate will hold simultaneously under adverse assignment of disagreement, nonresponse, redaction failure, unknown clocks, unknown calendar placement, unknown service, and unit-boundary ambiguity. Specifically:

* every claimable held-out service domain and the late possible-calendar holdout will have a simultaneous upper bound for `C_AUTO` no greater than `tau_AUTO=0.05` and for `leak_AUTO` no greater than 0.02;
* route confirmation/capture gates will remain at their inherited margins;
* absolute between-domain contamination drift will not exceed 0.02 and workload inflation will not exceed a preapproved 1.50 ratio or the preapproved absolute physician-hour budget; and
* no domain's unsafe minority-unit event is masked by a favorable pooled estimate.

Failure in one domain falsifies transport stability for that route. Sparse support, wide bounds, an unresolvable calendar domain, or absent workload-budget approval makes the transport claim inconclusive rather than favorable. A pooled pass cannot override a domain failure.

## Outcome-blind domain construction

Construct domains for every frozen `N_A` admission before sampling, packet generation, or any route/component label. Hash the domain code and complete domain manifest. Domains are hidden from all reviewers.

### Conservative possible-calendar domain

Never split on raw `year(admittime)`. Parse `anchor_year_group` as the inclusive interval `[a_i,b_i]`. Let `q_i=year(admittime)-anchor_year`. Because the exact real anchor year and month/day mapping are unavailable, define a deliberately widened possible admission-calendar interval

`C_i=[a_i+q_i-1, b_i+q_i+1]`.

The one-year widening prevents a year-boundary assumption from creating false temporal precision. Assign mutually exclusive domains:

* `T_EARLY` if `upper(C_i)<=2013`;
* `T_LATE` if `lower(C_i)>=2017`;
* `T_BRIDGE` otherwise; and
* `T_UNKNOWN` for missing, malformed, contradictory, or out-of-catalog values.

`T_LATE` is the deterministic temporal holdout. Its IDs, packets, and sealed labels are not released to the analysis team until the protocol/code hashes, margins, complement (`T_EARLY/T_BRIDGE`) output template, and signed no-change declaration are frozen. Review can occur concurrently under balanced reviewer assignment, but route outputs remain cryptographically sealed. Any change after complement release invalidates the late holdout.

This construction establishes only nonoverlapping *possible-calendar bands* for `T_EARLY` and `T_LATE`; it does not recover exact admission dates. `T_BRIDGE` and `T_UNKNOWN` remain in pooled inference and receive separate adverse summaries. If `T_LATE` has insufficient support, the temporal result is inconclusive. Under no circumstance may a raw shifted-year split, seasonality analysis, or exact year trend replace this design.

### Pre-departure service domains

For each admission, order `hosp/services` rows satisfying exact `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, nonmissing `transfertime`, and `transfertime<=D` by `(transfertime,input_row_number)`. The terminal code is the `curr_service` at the maximum `transfertime`. Multiple distinct codes at that same maximum timestamp, a missing code, no eligible row, contradictory linkage, or parser failure gives `S_UNKNOWN`; source row order cannot break a timestamp tie.

Freeze the following operational code groups before seeing `N_A` outcomes:

* `S_MED_CODES={MED,CMED,NMED}`;
* `S_PROC_CODES={SURG,CSURG,NSURG,PSURG,TSURG,VSURG,ORTHO,TRAUM,GU,GYN,ENT,EYE,DENT}`;
* `S_OBS={OBS}`;
* `S_PSYCH={PSYCH}`;
* `S_OMED={OMED}`; and
* `S_OTHER_UNKNOWN` for `NBB`, any previously unseen code, tie, missing code, or missing pre-departure service row.

These are code groups, not claims about a standardized specialty taxonomy. Report exact terminal codes inside every group so a grouped pass cannot mask an exact-code signal. A code group may not be reassembled after outcomes.

Secondary workflow domains are prespecified from the same pre-departure data: service-row count `P_0`, `P_1`, or `P_2PLUS`; any change in `curr_service` (`P_CHANGE`/`P_NOCHANGE`/`P_UNKNOWN`); and exact admission-linked pre-`D` ICU exposure (`ICU_YES/ICU_NO/ICU_UNKNOWN`). These secondary domains diagnose service-path heterogeneity but do not substitute for terminal-service gates.

## Fixed domain-balanced probability sample

Retain the parent's five outcome-blind sampling strata and definitions exactly: certainty `S_SENTINEL`; then `S_TIMECLOCK`, `S_ZERO`, `S_PARTIAL`, and `S_COMPLETE`. Sentinel assignment still precedes every noncensus stratum and is based only on deterministic chain, clock, artifact, parser, boundary, multiplicity, conflict, target-decision, or discharge-span signals. Sampling strata are not routes.

Retain the parent's fixed total within-stratum sample sizes:

* census every `S_SENTINEL` admission;
* for `S_ZERO`, the larger of 300 or the finite-population size for worst-case 0.5 and ±4 percentage-point 95% precision, capped at `N_h`;
* for each other noncensus stratum, the larger of 120 or the corresponding ±7-point size, capped at `N_h`;
* `n_h=ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25/{e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`; and
* retain the fixed 900-admission ceiling. If census plus formula samples exceed 900, obtain resources or declare feasibility failed; never reduce a required sample.

The repair is allocation within each noncensus `h`. Cross-classify by `c=(T,S)`, where all four temporal and six service domains, including unknowns, are eligible cells. Let `N_hc` be frozen population counts. Allocate integer `n_hc` before labels by deterministic constrained optimization:

1. `sum_c n_hc=n_h`, `0<=n_hc<=N_hc`, and every occupied cell receives `n_hc>=1`;
2. for each occupied service margin, target at least `min(N_hS,15)` sampled admissions when `n_h>=120`; for each occupied temporal margin, target at least `min(N_hT,20)`; for `S_ZERO`, increase these targets to 30 and 40, respectively;
3. after satisfying feasible minima, allocate remaining slots to minimize `sum_c (n_hc-n_h*N_hc/N_h)^2/(N_hc+1)`, with lexicographic `(h,T,S)` tie-breaking; and
4. if the stated marginal constraints are jointly infeasible, solve the same optimization maximizing the minimum achieved fraction of each target, publish the failed targets, and mark affected domain gates unsupported. Never merge domains or increase workload after looking at labels.

Within each `h,c`, use simple random sampling without replacement from canonical admission keys using a seed derived from the sealed protocol hash. The draw, ordered selected IDs, `N_hc,n_hc`, seed, and explicit no-replacement list are frozen before packets.

For sampled admission `i` in cell `(h,c)`, `pi_i=1` in the sentinel census and otherwise `pi_i=n_hc/N_hc>0`; `w_i=1/pi_i`. Within a noncensus cell, `pi_ij=n_hc(n_hc-1)/[N_hc(N_hc-1)]`; across independently drawn cells `pi_ij=pi_i*pi_j`. A sampled cell with `n_hc=1` has no internal variance estimate and its domain result is automatically inconclusive unless censused or bounded by exact inversion. Certainty cells contribute zero sampling variance.

This is fixed sampling: no route, disagreement, unsafe event, review time, response, or interim estimate may alter `n_h`, `n_hc`, selection, replacement, stopping, or domain composition. Every selected admission gets every inherited index, audit, redaction, and minority-unit review. No real `N_A` admission is used for training or tuning; synthetic fixtures only.

## Domain estimands and leave-one-domain-out evaluation

The inferential unit remains the admission, which is also one per subject. For every pooled endpoint `theta`, compute `theta_g` conditional on each temporal and service domain and `theta_-g` in its complement using the same admission labels and design weights. For totals,

`T_yg_hat=sum_{i in s} w_i I(G_i=g)y_i`.

For domain proportions or conditional risks, use the Hájek ratio of the corresponding HT totals and always report numerator and denominator totals. Route units remain nested and unweighted.

Required primary domain endpoints are:

* route burdens `B_{r,g}` for `AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,MIXED`;
* `C_{AUTO,g}`, retaining the parent's adverse event definition triggered by either initial index `AUTO` and any conflicting index/audit route, safety-auditor finding, missing audit label, boundary issue, redaction breach, or unknown compatible with non-`AUTO`;
* `D_{MIX,g}`, newly found additional/unsafe unit rate, and deterministic-one-unit-to-multiple-unit error rate;
* route confirmation/raw agreement, AC1, `Se_RECON,g`, `PPV_RECON,g`, `PPV_TIMING,g`, `Se_DEFER,g`, and `leak_AUTO,g`; and
* workload: selected packets, units, reviews, nonresponse, minutes per component/packet, physician-hours, and design-weighted hours per 100 `N_A` admissions in each domain.

Define drift contrasts before labels:

* `Delta_C(g)=C_AUTO,g-C_AUTO,-g` and the early-to-late contrast `C_AUTO,LATE-C_AUTO,EARLY`;
* analogous differences for `leak_AUTO`, confirmation, `Se_RECON`, and `Se_DEFER`;
* route-burden differences, reported as workflow drift rather than accuracy drift; and
* workload ratio `RR_W(g)=W_g/W_-g`, early-to-late workload ratio, and absolute difference in physician-hours per 100 admissions.

Because the route is locked and no real record is used to fit parameters, “leave-one-service-domain-out” is an evaluation stress test, not model training: for each service `g`, compute all complement results while the `g` output remains sealed, sign a no-change declaration, then release `g` and apply the frozen gates. Any code, matching, redaction, definition, margin, packet, or reviewer-role change prompted by a complement result voids all held-out service claims. This sequential presentation cannot create external-site evidence.

### Positivity and support

Design positivity requires `pi_i>0` in every occupied `(h,T,S)` cell. Estimand-specific support additionally requires a nonzero estimated denominator and adequate effective sample size. Report maximum weight, normalized maximum weight share, Kish effective sample size overall, by domain, and for every ratio denominator. Also report cell sampling fractions and the proportion of each domain represented by certainty admissions.

A route-domain gate is claimable only with at least 30 sampled route-positive/reference-positive admissions as applicable, at least 10 from non-sentinel cells, and route-denominator effective sample size at least 50; inherited pooled thresholds remain higher where specified. `AUTO` additionally requires at least 30 sampled candidate-`AUTO` admissions in that domain. `RECON` needs at least 30 index `RECON` and 30 audit unsafe-content admissions. `TIMING` needs 30 index `TIMING`; `DEFER` needs 30 independent-audit uncertain-reference admissions. If a domain lacks events, has zero estimated denominator, is represented only by certainty cases, has `max(w_i)/sum_g(w_i)>0.10`, or has unstable exact bounds, report it as unsupported/inconclusive. Zero sampled unsafe events never implies zero risk.

## Finite-population inference, rare events, unknowns, and multiplicity

Treat `(h,T,S)` as the survey strata. For scalar `y`, use the inherited HT variance summed over noncensus cells:

`V_hat(T_y_hat)=sum_{h,c} N_hc^2*(1-n_hc/N_hc)*s_y,hc^2/n_hc`.

For a ratio `R`, use Taylor linearization with `z_i=y_i-R_hat*d_i` and divide the corresponding total variance by `T_d_hat^2`. Keep every admission and all its units/views/labels together.

Generate at least 20,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates independently within each noncensus `(h,T,S)` cell; certainty admissions remain fixed. The same replicate drives every pooled, temporal, service, service-path, ICU, workload, support, and drift endpoint. Use a max-statistic over the full prespecified family: all inherited pooled gates; all primary `T_EARLY,T_LATE` and six service-domain endpoints; all leave-one-domain-out contrasts; and the early/late contrasts. Exact-code, service-path, ICU, `T_BRIDGE`, and unknown-domain summaries are a second simultaneous diagnostic family and cannot rescue a primary failure.

For rare/zero events, invert the exact stratified finite-population distribution cell by cell. Use hypergeometric inversion for unconditional binary totals and the parent's multivariate-hypergeometric enumeration of `{unsafe AUTO,safe AUTO,non-AUTO}` for `C_AUTO`. Report the widest valid simultaneous limit from exact inversion, bootstrap, and adverse partial identification. If computation requires conservative Bonferroni allocation across domains/cells, use it rather than silently dropping domains.

No selected admission is replaced. Missing/late/schema-invalid labels, unreadable packets, access failures, withdrawals, missing safety audits, unknown calendar or service, redaction failures, contradictory service timestamps, boundary disagreement, and unknown clocks remain explicit. For every domain estimand, optimize all compatible route/event assignments to produce sharp sample-level adverse lower and upper endpoints; ratio endpoints use linear-fractional optimization inside every replicate. An index `AUTO` with missing audit evidence is unsafe in the upper bound. Missing domain information contributes to every compatible domain's adverse endpoint and to `UNKNOWN`; it cannot be allocated to the favorable domain. Complete-response and model-assisted estimates are diagnostics only.

The first-eligible rule structurally removes repeated-patient clustering from `N_A`. If implementation finds duplicate `subject_id` values after population freeze, stop: do not use a cluster-robust patch to excuse a broken first-eligible selection. Reviewer clustering is handled by balanced random reviewer assignment across `T` and `S`, reviewer-level workload summaries, and a secondary two-way admission/reviewer bootstrap; primary safety bounds remain design-based and adverse rather than relying on exchangeable reviewer effects.

## Baselines

Apply identical samples, weights, domains, audit labels, and uncertainty to:

1. the parent's pooled-only gate, demonstrating whether pooling masks a failing domain;
2. marginal-majority route assignment;
3. inherited deterministic sampling-stratum routing;
4. recommendation-unit majority routing, expected to miss unsafe minority units;
5. terminal-service-majority and anchor-era-majority routes;
6. a service-blind and era-blind analysis that reports no domain endpoints;
7. favorable consensus, complete-case unweighted, and incorporated-reference sensitivities, all prohibited from selection; and
8. a sentinel-only “safety” audit, which must fail whenever unsafe cases occur only in non-sentinel service/era cells.

The substantive comparator is not a high-performing prediction model. It is the parent's pooled audit. The repair succeeds methodologically only if the analysis can expose a preplanted domain-specific failure that the pooled audit passes.

## Prespecified pooled and transport gates

Governance must approve every margin and the absolute physician-hour budget before real labels are released. Missing approval yields `NO_SELECTION`. Retain pooled defaults `tau_AUTO=0.05`, confirmation `rho=0.90`, AC1 `0.70`, `sigma_RECON=0.95`, `pi_RECON=0.80`, `pi_TIMING=0.95`, and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`, plus all inherited source, retrieval, redaction, denominator, effective-sample-size, precision, and route-event gates.

A pooled route must first pass every inherited gate. Transport stability then requires all of the following, with adverse-compatible simultaneous bounds:

1. **Temporal support:** at least 100 `N_A` admissions and overall domain ESS at least 80 in both `T_EARLY` and `T_LATE`; route-specific event thresholds above; `T_UNKNOWN<=5%` by upper burden bound. Otherwise temporal transport is inconclusive.
2. **Service support:** every one of the six frozen service domains with population burden at least 5% is claimable for the route. Smaller domains remain combined in a rare/unknown adverse envelope whose upper `AUTO` contamination and leakage bounds must still meet margins. `S_OTHER_UNKNOWN` burden must have upper bound `<=5%`; otherwise unrestricted service transport is inconclusive.
3. **AUTO purity:** in every claimable `T_EARLY`, `T_LATE`, and service domain, upper `C_AUTO<=0.05`, upper `D_MIX<=0.10`, upper `leak_AUTO<=0.02`, lower confirmation/raw agreement `>=0.90`, lower AC1 `>=0.70`, no safety-auditor-confirmed unresolved minority unit remains compatible with `AUTO`, and lower `B_AUTO>0`.
4. **RECON/DEFER/TIMING capture:** apply the inherited route gates separately in each claimable primary domain: `Se_RECON>=0.95`, `PPV_RECON>=0.80`, `PPV_TIMING>=0.95`, `Se_DEFER>=0.95`, confirmation `>=0.90`, and no adverse-compatible uncertain admission routed `AUTO`.
5. **Contamination drift:** simultaneous upper limits for `|Delta_C(g)|` and `|C_AUTO,LATE-C_AUTO,EARLY|` are `<=0.02`; upper absolute leakage drift is `<=0.01`. A nonsignificant difference is not equivalence.
6. **Capture drift:** simultaneous lower limits for domain-minus-complement confirmation, `Se_RECON`, and `Se_DEFER` are no worse than `-0.05`.
7. **Workload stability:** each domain's upper mean physician-hours per 100 admissions is below the governance-approved absolute budget, and upper `RR_W(g)<=1.50` plus upper early-to-late workload ratio `<=1.50`. Report medians, 90th/95th quantiles, reviewer-level spread, and uncertainty; low workload never implies safety or benefit.
8. **No pooled rescue:** if any supported domain fails, the corresponding unrestricted transport claim fails even if pooled estimates pass. If a domain is unsupported, the unrestricted claim is inconclusive. A service-restricted prospective silent study may be nominated only for explicitly listed frozen service domains that independently pass; all other service/unknown admissions must remain `DEFER` prospectively.

The multiplicity family includes all routes and domains. Routes still pass independently. A `RECON`-only silent validation may be nominated if its domain gates pass while `AUTO` fails. No gate permits retrospective automation.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. Recompute all source/schema hashes, row counts, canonical keys, `N_A`, first-eligible selection, reciprocal links, unit clocks, domains, cells, sample allocations, `pi_i`, `pi_ij`, and ordered sample from sealed inputs. Any unexplained drift stops analysis.
2. Verify raw shifted years are never used for temporal splitting. Inject identical shifted years with different `anchor_year_group`; domain code must follow only the conservative possible-calendar interval. Malformed groups must become `T_UNKNOWN`.
3. Boundary trap: generate possible-calendar intervals crossing 2013/2017. They must be `T_BRIDGE`, never assigned to the favorable early or late group.
4. Service-time trap: add a service row after `D`; it cannot alter the domain. Missing times, same-time distinct terminal codes, unseen codes, and absent rows must become `S_OTHER_UNKNOWN`.
5. Domain-masking trap: plant unsafe `AUTO` events only in one service or in `T_LATE`, at a rate that leaves pooled `C_AUTO<0.05` but domain `C_AUTO>0.05`. The pooled analysis may pass; unrestricted transport must fail.
6. Workload-masking trap: hold pooled hours within budget while multiplying one domain's review time by two. The domain workload gate must fail.
7. Positivity trap: create an occupied joint cell with one sampled admission and high weight. It must remain in estimates but cannot be declared transport-supported.
8. Allocation trap: rerun integer allocation and random sampling from hashes/seeds; route labels or processing order must not change `n_hc` or selected IDs.
9. Rare-event trap: zero sampled unsafe cases in a noncensus domain must produce a positive upper bound. A pooled zero cannot be copied into domain bounds.
10. Multiplicity trap: a nominal 95% interval may pass one of many domains while the simultaneous interval fails; only the latter controls selection.
11. Unknown trap: concentrate missing service, calendar, clock, audit, or boundary evidence in unsafe high-weight admissions. Complete cases may pass, but adverse domain and pooled bounds must block selection.
12. Patient trap: duplicate a subject in `N_A`. The run must stop. Duplicating safe recommendation units within an admission must not change route or weight.
13. Leave-one-domain-out leakage trap: modify route code after seeing a complement result. Hash/no-change verification must invalidate the held-out claim.
14. Exact-code masking trap: place failures in a rare exact terminal code inside a grouped domain. Exact-code diagnostics and the rare/unknown envelope must retain the signal; regrouping is forbidden.
15. Sentinel trap: put all unsafe events in non-sentinel `S_COMPLETE` or `S_ZERO` cells. Sentinel-only evidence cannot establish safety; domain-balanced estimates must recover the burden with nominal simulation coverage.
16. Reviewer drift trap: assign slower or systematically discordant reviewers only to one era/service in a synthetic schedule. The reviewer-balance check and secondary reviewer bootstrap must expose it; reassignment after labels is forbidden.
17. Retain every parent trap for chronology identity, role packet leakage, cross-role linkage, append-only seals, incorporation, differential verification, unit matching, minority-unit precedence, unequal sampling fractions, nonresponse, threshold perturbation, POE nonleakage, and unsupported claims.
18. Claim checker rejects any narrative asserting exact calendar trend, external-site generalizability, guideline appropriateness, clinician failure, actual viewing, clinical safety, causal benefit, follow-up completion, or patient outcome.

The verifier can mechanically check source and packet hashes, joins, time rules, domain construction, fixed allocation, sampling probabilities, seals, route code, survey estimators, exact/replicate bounds, multiplicity, support, gates, and whether conclusions match computed outputs. It cannot establish clinical truth, utility margins, service taxonomy meaning, exact real dates, external-site transport, appropriateness, or patient benefit.

## Interpretation

**Supportive** means the unchanged route process passes pooled gates and adverse simultaneous gates in the late possible-calendar holdout and every adequately supported service domain, without material contamination/capture/workload drift. This supports only a site-local prospective silent validation of the frozen process in the explicitly supported workflows.

**Adverse** means adequate evidence of excessive unsafe `AUTO` contamination, missed unsafe/uncertain states, degraded confirmation, a hidden minority-unit failure, or excessive workload in any temporal/service domain; or drift beyond an equivalence margin. This falsifies unrestricted transport of that route even if pooled performance is favorable. A domain-specific negative finding is clinically useful because it prevents a pooled average from licensing an unsafe workflow.

**Inconclusive** includes inadequate temporal/service support, sparse route events, wide simultaneous bounds, excessive unknown domains, nonresponse, broken seals, retrieval undercoverage, unapproved workload budget, mandatory census above 900, or an unavailable exact calendar mapping. Failure to reject heterogeneity is not evidence of stability. Unsupported domains cannot be generalized from their complements.

## Required next evidence and strict ceiling

Even a complete pass remains retrospective, stored-text, single-site evidence. A prospective silent study must use consecutive adult discharges and immutable report finalization/addendum, report availability/viewing, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing risks, and final transmitted artifact. It must prespecify actual calendar windows and accrue sufficient route-positive admissions in every claimed service domain. Only such data can test genuine forward temporal transport; another hospital with the same frozen protocol is required for external-site transport.

Clinical appropriateness, actionability, safety, utility, completion, and benefit require independent expert adjudication and ultimately a governed prospective trial. Neither stable domain-specific text routing nor low audit contamination proves any of them.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The parent ensured that a route could not validate itself. This child ensures that a large pooled service/era cannot validate a route on behalf of a smaller unsafe or unworkable domain. It converts service and coarse temporal heterogeneity from optional subgroup tables into outcome-blind sampling dimensions, positivity checks, simultaneous adverse gates, and explicit no-transport decisions. It also preserves fixed sampling and workload while preventing post-event top-ups.

Important uncertainty remains. `anchor_year_group` permits only a widened possible-calendar interval, not an exact admission date; thus the temporal audit is conservative and within-site, not proof of prospective drift stability. Terminal `curr_service`, transition count, and ICU exposure are workflow proxies, not validated ownership or responsibility. Small domains and rare unsafe events may remain unresolvable within 900 admissions. Independent clinicians may share correlated interpretive error. The canonical `DS` and storage clocks remain incomplete proxies for the actual transmitted artifact and opportunity to act. Retrieval can miss semantic opportunities. Therefore a favorable result advances the evidence only from pooled retrospective reproducibility to internally domain-stressed retrospective reproducibility; it does not establish external-site generalizability, clinical safety, or benefit.
