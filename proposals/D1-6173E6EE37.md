# Book-specific label-stable retention frontier for HCC documentary-M2 review

## 1. Episode-84 repair and clinically important unresolved question

This targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` preserves the complete adult first-eligible-resection design, coherent operation-clock states, 24-hour decision deadline, 90-day CT/MRI window, 365-day recorded-prior-treatment lookback, 2015–2018 development/2019 lock/separate 2020 and 2021 tests, whole-accession units, complete immutable reader books, locked B/R/G/Gmask scores, fixed-K queues, comparator and random-capture margins, coupled source/onset/reader/pathology worlds, q_req/q_M2/q_dec, anchored deletion, and nonclinical limits.

The parent added `q_stable`, but its set
`P_common(a)=intersection_b {i: i in S_max(a,b), Y_i(a,b)=1}`
requires a patient to be selected by the maximum-content queue under **every** pathology book as well as labeled documentary M2 by every book. Because development labels can change the fitted locked model and maximum-content ranking across pathology books, a patient can be M2-positive in every book yet be selected by `S_max` under only one book. The parent then excludes that label-stable positive from `P_common`. Consequently, `q_stable<1, q_M2=1` does not by itself localize all failing patients to pathology-label instability: it may hide label-stable positives behind pathology-book-induced model/queue membership instability.

That distinction changes the next evidence decision. A lost patient who is label-stable but selected under only one book still demands report/version/access investigation and model-sensitivity review. A lost patient whose M2 status varies by book demands both report-access investigation (because incomplete content changed selection) and pathology adjudication (because the documentary target is uncertain). Neither pattern licenses a pathology-only explanation or a deployment claim.

The substantive advance is one corrected, nested frontier, `q_LS`: the minimum complete-patient report-bundle coverage at which every patient who is documentary-M2-positive in all complete pathology books and selected by the maximum-content queue under the **current book's own fitted model** remains selected under incomplete bundles. The parent's cross-book queue-intersection quantity is retained as `q_common`, a diagnostic only. Required nesting is
`q_req <= q_common <= q_LS <= q_M2 <= q_dec`
whenever the corresponding nonvacuity conditions hold.

## 5. Corrected book-specific label-stability estimand

For fixed nonpathology state a and complete pathology books `B_path`, define the all-book label-stable positive universe

`U_LS(a)={i: Y_i(a,b')=1 for every b' in B_path}`.

This definition depends only on documentary labels across complete books, not on maximum-content queue membership.

Retain the parent's cross-book common-queue set, renamed transparently:

`P_common(a)=intersection over b' of {i: i in S_max(a,b') and Y_i(a,b')=1}`.

For each current book b define the corrected protected set:

`P_LS(a,b)=S_max(a,b) intersect U_LS(a)`.

Also retain the inherited any-book/current-book set:

`P_M2(a,b)={i: i in S_max(a,b) and Y_i(a,b)=1}`.

Then, for every a,b:

`P_common(a) subseteq P_LS(a,b) subseteq P_M2(a,b) subseteq S_max(a,b)`.

Define:
- `J_common(a,b)=1[P_common(a) subseteq S_G(a,b)]`;
- `J_LS(a,b)=1[P_LS(a,b) subseteq S_G(a,b)]`;
- inherited `J_M2(a,b)=1[P_M2(a,b) subseteq S_G(a,b)]`;
- inherited `J_dec(a,b)=1[S_G(a,b)=S_max(a,b)]`.

For q in R, `LS_PASS(q)=1` only if inherited `PASS(q)=1` and `J_LS(a,b)=1` for every eligible 2020/2021 state with `Q>=q`. If maximum-content and feasibility gates pass, and every required `P_LS(a,b)` is nonempty, define

`q_LS=min{q in R: LS_PASS(q)=1}`.

The parent diagnostic is similarly `q_common`, but an empty `P_common(a)` makes only that diagnostic attribution-inconclusive; it cannot veto a nonempty, estimable `q_LS`. Emit and verify:

`q_req <= q_common <= q_LS <= q_M2 <= q_dec`

for defined quantities. If q_common is undefined because of empty common sets, verify only `q_req<=q_LS<=q_M2<=q_dec`.

At every threshold and immediately lower boundary, emit protected patient-set hashes and counts for `U_LS, P_common, P_LS, P_M2, S_max\S_G`; split losses into:
1. label-stable and common-queued;
2. label-stable but not common-queued (the defect hidden by the parent);

## 6. Falsification and interpretation

Apply this precedence-ordered one-hot primary interpretation:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: any source replay, header/hash, raw ordinal, accession grouping, clock, coupled state, book completeness, score/tie replay, fixed K, deletion, threshold, set/hash, boundary witness or invariant check fails.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: any N/K/event/n_A/fixed-frame requirement fails.
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: maximum content fails any inherited comparator/random/year/reader/source/deletion gate.
4. `aggregate_capture_reserve_adverse`: maximum content passes but q_req is undefined or equals 1.
5. `label_stability_attribution_inconclusive`: any required `P_LS(a,b)` is empty. Do not accept vacuous inclusion.
6. `book_specific_label_stable_retention_adverse`: q_req<1 but q_LS is undefined or equals 1. Missing bundles can drop a patient who is documentary-M2-positive in every pathology book and prioritized by at least the current book's maximum-content fitted model. This falsifies the attribution hypothesis.
7. `label_stable_retention_supportive_any_book_adverse`: q_LS<1 but q_M2 is undefined or equals 1. Every failing protected positive is book-variable in label, but report incompleteness still caused queue loss. The correct next step is a joint report/version-access audit plus pathology adjudication (or a prespecified conservative policy), not pathology-only attribution.
8. `dual_M2_retention_supportive_queue_fragile`: q_M2<1 but q_dec is undefined or equals 1. Label-stable and any-current-book documentary-M2 patient retention survive; other substitutions remain and cannot be called harmless.
9. `full_documentary_queue_supportive_bridge_pending`: q_dec<1 and all minimality/boundary witnesses pass.
10. `external_bridge_pending`: mandatory suffix for categories 8–9, and for any category interpreted operationally, because HCC lacks actual decision-time finalization/release/readability/view evidence.

Additionally report `parent_common_intersection_optimistic` when q_common<1 but q_LS is 1/undefined, or when `q_LS>q_common`; this is a diagnostic flag, not a second primary label. Equality to 1 fails. The 12/48/72-hour, rho=.05/.20, pooled-year, average-book, favorable-state or favorable-deletion results cannot rescue failure.

Supportive means only that the frozen finite-corpus stored-content experiment met its exact conditions. Adverse means a prespecified robustness claim was falsified in at least one allowed state. Inconclusive means provenance, feasibility or nonvacuity prevented the test; it is neither support nor adverse biological evidence.
