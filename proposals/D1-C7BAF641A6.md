# Proposal — Early postoperative liver-function trajectory vs day-5 snapshot for post-hepatectomy liver failure in HBV-cirrhosis-dominant HCC

**Status:** Lead working proposal, episode 2. Recovered and completed from episode-1 draft (prior attempt timed out before registration). Data bindings verified by probe [research job] (observed). Key works inspected (see §8).

## 1. Unresolved question

In HBV-cirrhosis-dominant HCC inpatients undergoing hepatic resection, do **early postoperative (POD 0–2) liver-function trajectories** — rise in total bilirubin (TBIL), prolongation of prothrombin time ratio/INR (PTR/INR), and albumin (ALB) decline relative to the preoperative baseline — predict **severe post-hepatectomy liver dysfunction (PHLF) at POD 3–7**, prolonged recovery, and in-hospital death, **beyond** (a) static preoperative liver reserve (ALBI-grade components: preop TBIL + ALB, plus platelets, age, sex, cirrhosis/HBV flags) and (b) the conventional **day-5 snapshot threshold** (50-50-style criteria)?

### Clinical importance

PHLF is the leading cause of death after hepatectomy [K1]. The 50-50 criteria (PT <50% AND bilirubin >50 µmol/L on POD 5) predict >50% mortality, but are only decision-relevant on day 5 — Balzan et al. note the criteria were met 3–8 days before clinical evidence of complications, and that in the average course PT nadir occurs on POD 1 and bilirubin peak on POD 3 with recovery by POD 5 [K1]. Rescue decisions (hepatic arterial embolism work-up, transplant referral, escalation) would benefit from trustworthy information at POD 0–2, a day or more earlier. Current early-prediction evidence either requires specialized assays — Jo 2026: ATIII decline ≥30% at POD 3, ALBI grade B, ICG R-15 ≥15%, AUC 0.750, N=151 [K3] — or 55-variable ensembles with novel regeneration biomarkers (GATA3, RAMP2, VEGFA, PEDF) and POD-3 phosphorus in the strongest model (Shen 2025 PILOT, N=1071) [K2]. **Nobody has isolated, in a large HBV-cirrhosis-dominant population, whether the interpretable SHAPE of routine POD 0–2 labs carries incremental, decision-relevant information beyond preoperative reserve — the deployable minimum in any hospital EHR.**

### Competing explanations (differing observable predictions)

- (a) **Failing regeneration / evolving liver failure:** persistent or monotonically worsening TBIL/PTR through POD 0–2 → predicts POD 3–7 dysfunction and death. Prediction: trajectory persistence adds large incremental discrimination.
- (b) **Transient perioperative insult (hemodilution, transient portal triad clamping, transfusion):** early peak then recovery → early deviations do NOT predict later dysfunction. Prediction: trajectory features add little after baseline adjustment.
- (c) **Baseline severity/selection:** preop reserve explains everything; early labs add nothing. Prediction: preop-only model ≈ trajectory-augmented model.
- (d) **Measurement/timing artifacts:** POD-0 labs reflect operative-day physiology, not trajectory; unit heterogeneity across assays/labs dominates. Addressed by within-encounter standardization and sensitivity analyses.

Discriminating observable: incremental discrimination (ΔAUC, IDI/NRI) of POD 0–2 trajectory features over the preop-reserve model, plus whether a POD-2 assessment can substitute the day-5 50-50 snapshot.

## 2. Study design (Harbor experiment)

- **Population:** encounters (就诊号) in the HCC dataset with a hepatectomy procedure row (手术 containing 肝切除) with valid 开始时间 (surgery anchor), HCC diagnosis (肝细胞癌), ≥1 preoperative lab within 7 days before surgery start, ≥1 lab in POD 0–2, and ≥1 lab in POD 3–7 (outcome window). One hospitalization per patient for model fitting (first qualifying encounter; patient-level split).
- **Exposure (trajectory features, POD 0–2 vs preop baseline):** TBIL, PTR/INR, ALB — within-encounter z-like standardization; features = slope over POD 0–2, value at POD 1/2, change-from-baseline, persistence (number of consecutive rising measurements / monotonicity index).
- **Outcomes:** (1) lab-defined severe PHLF analog in POD 3–7: Balzan/50-50-style (peak TBIL > 50 µmol/L-equivalent within-encounter standardization AND PTR/INR above equivalent threshold) plus ISGLS-like peak-TBIL variant as sensitivity; (2) prolonged LOS (>14 d or >median); (3) 死亡诊断 flag (in-hospital death, known incomplete — secondary, exploratory).
- **Baseline model:** logistic regression on preop ALBI components (preop TBIL, ALB), platelets, age, sex, HBV-cirrhosis/HBV flags.
- **Substantive alternative:** trajectory-augmented logistic (same preop covariates + POD 0–2 trajectory features) — the mechanistic alternative that trajectory shape matters. **Learned comparator:** gradient boosting on the same curve features, testing nonlinear/interaction temporal structure only if it answers a substantive uncertainty.
- **Evaluation:** patient-level split (70/30, stratified); ΔAUC with bootstrap 95% CI, IDI/NRI, calibration; **temporal-order permutation control** (shuffle lab times within encounter — trajectory signal should collapse if real); sensitivity: unit-anomaly exclusion (encounters with implausible TBIL ranges), first-vs-all encounter definitions.
- **Falsification criteria:** Supportive = trajectory features give statistically significant incremental discrimination (ΔAUC bootstrap CI excluding 0), robust to permutation and unit checks, AND a POD-2 model materially approaches day-5-snapshot performance. Adverse = no incremental value (ΔAUC CI includes 0) — interpreted as day-5 timing being informationally irreducible from routine labs. Inconclusive = <100 outcome events, or unit heterogeneity dominating diagnostics.
- **Computational claim vs clinical adjudication:** the incremental-information question is computable from bound data. Causal mechanism (regeneration failure vs insult) requires evidence beyond this design (e.g., ICG, imaging volumetrics); mortality conclusions are limited by the incomplete death flag.

## 4. Method alternatives record (per shared instructions)

- **Baseline (decision-relevant comparator):** static preoperative logistic (ALBI components + demographics + HBV/cirrhosis). Justification: represents current preoperative reserve assessment.
- **Mechanistic alternative:** trajectory-augmented interpretable logistic — tests whether early shape adds information. This is the primary scientific comparison.
- **Learned alternative (conditional):** GBM on curve features — only to test whether nonlinear/interaction temporal structure carries information the linear trajectory misses; deferred unless linear features show signal or their absence is ambiguous.
- **Resources:** CPU-only adequate (~3.7k qualifying visits, tabular); solver envelope 16 CPU / 28,800 s; no GPU requested (no GPU allocated to science; no scientific need identified — small tabular fits).
- **Selection/deferral reason:** interpretable models directly serve the clinical decision question (earlier risk assessment); complexity is justified only by a substantive uncertainty (nonlinear temporal structure), not by predictive-gain chasing.

## 5. Experiment card (solver deliverable)

Fit: (1) preop-reserve baseline logistic; (2) trajectory-augmented logistic; (3) day-5 snapshot logistic (50-50-style features at POD 5); (4) conditional GBM comparator. Estimate ΔAUC/IDI/NRI with bootstrap CIs, calibration, permutation control, unit-anomaly sensitivity. Outputs: model cards, discrimination/calibration tables, feature effects, honest event counts. Establishes completion: reported incremental-discrimination estimates with uncertainty, not confirmation of the hypothesis.

## 6. Alternatives and threats (rubric: Alternatives)

Age, sex, baseline severity (ALBI), cirrhosis/HBV status, platelets adjusted in all models. Residual threats: indication-specific measurement timing (sicker patients measured more often — addressed by requiring ≥1 measurement in both windows for all, and measurement-count adjustment), transfusion/hemodilution artifact (ALB/PTR deviations) — mitigated by relying primarily on TBIL-based trajectories and the permutation control; unit heterogeneity — within-encounter standardization + sensitivity exclusion; immortal-time/conditioning on surviving to POD 3 — outcomes are lab-defined at fixed windows, with LOS/death as secondary. External validity: single-center tertiary Chinese HBV-dominant cohort — claimed as such.

## 7. Demonstration dispositions (three research demonstrations)

1. **Delphi (natural-history transformers):** adaptation conceivable for sequence learning, but our HCC source lacks validated outcome-rich structured disease chronology; our bounded temporal-information question is better served by interpretable trajectory models. Not pursued; revisit if sequence ordering becomes the key uncertainty.
2. **Oncoformer (multimodal):** images unavailable in HCC source (CSV structured data only) → missing dependency; lab-only adaptation already covered by trajectory design.
3. **ALADYNOULLI (Bayesian longitudinal):** genetics unavailable; EHR-only latent-trajectory modeling is a possible later alternative, not needed for the bounded first pass.

## 9. Honest limitations

- Lab-defined sPHLF analog, not clinically adjudicated PHLF; ISGLS requires bilirubin normalization context we approximate with within-encounter baselines.
- No unit column → absolute thresholds (e.g., 50 µmol/L) are approximated via standardization + sensitivity analyses; flagged as the largest measurement threat.
- Death flag incomplete (288 死亡诊断 rows) → mortality analysis exploratory only.
- Single-center tertiary cohort; findings generalize to HBV-dominant HCC populations, not Western steatosis-dominant cohorts.
- Positive incremental discrimination would NOT establish a causal mechanism (regeneration failure vs insult) — mechanism requires ICG/volumetrics/imaging evidence unavailable here.
