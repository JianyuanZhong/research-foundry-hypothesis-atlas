# Incremental decision value of timestamp-safe routine data for preoperative high-grade MVI (M2) triage in HCC

## Successor question and hypothesis

This is a substantive evolution of [prior hypothesis]. The parent asks whether preoperative radiology-report language and laboratory data predict pathology-defined M2. The remaining clinically important question is narrower and harder: does the report text add enough calibrated information beyond what a clinician could obtain from routine tumor burden, liver reserve, demographics, and laboratory measurements to change an M2 triage decision in a temporally stable way?

The decision is whether an apparently resectable HCC patient should be escalated for multidisciplinary review of wider/anatomic resection, a neoadjuvant investigation, or intensified perioperative planning because high-grade MVI is plausible. This experiment does not decide which treatment is best.

**Established evidence.** MVI, particularly high-grade MVI, is associated with postoperative recurrence and pathology is the available reference ascertainment. The inspected 2025 review (Li et al., DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746, PMCID PMC12309289) describes preoperative models as promising while emphasizing unresolved MVI-related management. Recent small single-center studies similarly report exploratory preoperative models and explicitly call for external validation. These findings support clinical importance, not the performance or utility of this model.

**Unresolved claim.** In this institution’s later-calendar patients, timestamp-safe information from ordinary CT/MRI reports adds transportable, decision-relevant information for M2 beyond a structured routine baseline.

**Primary falsifiable hypothesis.** In the locked 2023-01-01 through 2025-11-11 test cohort, an elastic-net model using preoperative report text plus exact routine labs will outperform an otherwise identical structured baseline by at least 0.05 AUPRC for M2 versus M0/M1, and will improve decision-curve net benefit by at least 0.02 probability-weighted benefit over a contiguous risk-threshold interval of width >=0.10 wholly inside 0.20-0.40. The text-plus-lab model must also have calibration slope 0.80-1.20 and PPV >=0.50 at sensitivity >=0.70 using the threshold frozen in 2022. The claim is about prediction/triage validity only; it is not a causal or outcome-effect hypothesis.

The substantive advance over the parent is an explicit incremental estimand and a clinically interpretable utility test against a deployable routine baseline. A high AUPRC from a model that merely restates tumor size or liver reserve will not count as support.

## Label adjudication and predictor construction

The primary reference outcome is M2 versus M0/M1. Parse M2 only when same-encounter pathology explicitly contains MVI提示风险分级：M2, M2(高危组), or a pre-registered equivalent. Controls require explicit M0 or M1; positive-ungraded text is not a control. If multiple pathology rows disagree, exclude from the primary analysis.

Before model fitting, two clinically qualified Chinese-reading reviewers blinded to predictors and model output independently review at least 100 M0, 100 M1, and 100 M2 parser-positive cases, all parser conflicts, and 200 imaging reports for tumor size/count and macrovascular language. Report class-specific PPV/sensitivity and Cohen’s kappa; a third reviewer resolves disagreement. Require parser PPV >=0.95 and sensitivity >=0.90 for each grade and a prespecified acceptable imaging-feature agreement before unblinded performance analysis. Automatic Harbor verification cannot establish pathology adequacy, Chinese semantic scope, or whether the pathology sample represents the whole tumor.

For both models, use age, sex, modality indicators, missingness indicators, last values of the exact assays, and rule-extracted maximum diameter, lesion count, capsule, satellite/peritumoral lesion, and macrovascular thrombus from the eligible report. The structured baseline (B) contains only these variables, with all lab transformations learned in development. The primary extension (B+T) adds character 3-5-gram TF-IDF features from 检查所见 and 检查诊断, trained only in development. A secondary gradient-boosted structured model tests nonlinearity without changing the primary estimand.

Strip dates, identifiers, accession numbers, boilerplate, pathology/specimen terms, explicit MVI/微血管侵犯, and text segments copied from pathology. Exclude examinations whose 检查 indicates pathology/specimen. Audit the 100 highest-weight n-grams before test unblinding. A label proxy or postoperative artifact invalidates the locked analysis and requires a declared child redesign; it cannot be repaired silently.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** label and imaging adjudication gates pass; no leakage is found; B+T exceeds B by >=0.05 AUPRC with a CI that excludes a delta below 0.05; the frozen threshold reaches sensitivity >=0.70 and PPV >=0.50; calibration slope is 0.80-1.20; and B+T has >=0.02 incremental net benefit over B, flag-all, and flag-none across a contiguous >=0.10 interval in 0.20-0.40, without domination by one modality or calendar year. The conclusion is that timestamp-safe routine report text adds temporally evaluated information that merits prospective multicenter and impact validation.

**Adverse:** B+T has delta-AUPRC <=0, no qualifying incremental net-benefit interval, materially poor calibration, or net harm in a clinically important prespecified subgroup. This rejects deployment of the text extension in this setting, even if development discrimination is high, and suggests the incremental signal is unstable or not worth its false-positive burden.

**Falsified:** the upper 95% CI for delta-AUPRC is <0.05, or the upper 95% CI for incremental net benefit is <0.02 throughout every candidate interval, or the frozen operating threshold misses PPV/sensitivity requirements. One primary failure falsifies the clinically useful incremental-value claim.

**Inconclusive:** fewer than 500 locked-test M2 cases, failed label/imaging adjudication, unresolved report-template or assay drift, non-overlap between B and B+T risk distributions, CIs crossing the prespecified margins, or subgroup precision too poor to assess harm. Inconclusive is not negative and cannot be converted into support by selecting a favorable sensitivity analysis.

Even supportive results cannot show that wider resection, neoadjuvant/adjuvant treatment, or intensified planning improves recurrence, survival, quality of life, or cost. The snapshot lacks raw images, slides, pathology sampling protocol, pathologist/radiologist identity, treatment intent, tumor-board decisions, complete surveillance, death registry, patient-reported outcomes, and costs. Those stronger claims require expert clinical adjudication, raw-image/pathology validation, an independent-center cohort, and a prospective impact trial.

The compiler must preserve the frozen population, time windows, estimand (B+T minus B), outcome, baselines, thresholds, and falsification criteria. A proposed change to any of those is a scientific child proposal, not a mechanical repair.

Computationally checkable outputs include source paths/hashes and required columns; exact observed procedure/assay dictionaries; composite-key joins and one-patient-one-index invariant; attrition; patient-disjoint chronological splits; strict pre-index predictor timestamps; absence of pathology/untimed-document predictors; frozen vocabulary/preprocessing/threshold; metric and paired bootstrap outputs; calibration and decision curves; subgroup/sensitivity outputs; and traceability of every reported number to machine-readable files.

Adversarial verification must include: (1) correct metrics paired with a claim of improved survival or a treatment recommendation, which must fail; (2) supportive outputs permitting only incremental predictive/triage conclusions; (3) adverse outputs forcing rejection of deployment rather than cherry-picking development results; (4) inconclusive outputs preserving uncertainty; (5) an injected pathology/post-index text feature causing leakage failure; and (6) perturbed test labels/predictions reproducing metrics independently.
