# Episode 62 targeted repair: primary decision comparison is R1 versus physiology-augmented R0

## Scope and unresolved claim

This is a targeted child of the exact-reconciled Episode 62 proposal. It changes only interpretation and verifier wording around decision-curve baselines. It does **not** change the audited cohort, temporal boundaries, predictors, outcome, estimand, model, materiality margin, uncertainty procedure, falsification criteria, source files, table names, joins, or evidence limits.

At the fixed 24-hour reassessment after an early recorded norepinephrine entry, the unresolved question is whether a released late lactate carries distinct hospital-mortality decision information after the released non-lactate laboratory snapshot, entry lactate, measurement timing, and six-hour heart-rate, respiratory-rate, and SpO2 context are represented, or mainly summarizes instability already captured by those streams.

**Primary falsifiable hypothesis:** in the exact inherited age-inclusive EICU 24-hour risk set, adding the released 12-to-24-hour lactate to the fixed physiology-augmented laboratory model increases uniformly threshold-averaged decision-curve net benefit over hospital-mortality thresholds 0.30–0.50 by at least **1 net true-positive-equivalent review per 100 people**.

This is a modeled incremental prognostic/decision-value claim conditional on survival to the landmark and repeat lactate measurement. It is not an effect of ordering lactate, monitoring, resuscitation, or acting on either prediction rule.

The primary contrast is **R1 minus physiology-augmented R0** at every threshold and in the integrated summary. “Baseline” in the primary estimand means R0, never treat-all, treat-none, or the labs-only curve.

At each threshold,
[
NB_{default}(p)=max{NB_{treat-all}(p),NB_{treat-none}(p)}.
]
The supportive guardrail requires R1 to beat this better default at all 21 thresholds. This is a separate clinical-reference check; it does not redefine ΔINB. A positive primary increment over R0 that fails this guardrail is Mixed, not Supportive.

The verifier must reject a primary ΔINB computed against treat-all, treat-none, or labs-only R0; a labs-only difference presented as the primary result; a positive default comparison presented as proof of incremental value beyond physiology; or a supportive label based only on beating a default. It must accept only a primary scalar obtained from the 21 values (NB_{R1}(p)-NB_{R0}(p)), with default comparisons checked separately.

## Supported evidence versus claim under test

The strongest executed evidence is Episode 58. Among 1,084 APACHE-IVa-complete people in 31 hospitals, adding observed late lactate to entry lactate and retrospective APACHE-IVa predicted mortality produced ΔINB +1.982 per 100 (2,000-refit 95% interval +0.483 to +3.351), improved AUROC, Brier score, and log loss, and had positive fixed-prediction deletion estimates in all 31 hospitals. This supports a positive modeled increment against a sparse, partly untimestamped comparator. It does not establish the locked +1 margin because +1 lies inside that interval, nor ordering or treatment benefit.

Episode 62 remains untested: its exact source audit establishes feasibility and reproduces the inherited population, but does not test the primary ΔINB hypothesis. The unresolved claim is incremental decision value of late lactate **after** released laboratories and six-hour physiology.

In 4,440 critically ill adults with sepsis, adding 24-hour lactate to recalibrated APACHE IV increased the C-statistic from 0.62 to 0.64; admission plus 24-hour lactate had the best reported fit. This supports modest serial prognostic information, not incremental decision value beyond this released laboratory-plus-physiology record. Supplementary material was not inspected.

## Models, estimand, uncertainty, and gates

Use hospital leave-one-out prediction. In each training fold, winsorize observed continuous predictors at training 1st/99th percentiles, standardize with training mean and population SD, and mean-impute missing continuous values after scaling to zero. Missing indicators remain unscaled. Apply transformations unchanged to held-out hospitals. A feature with no observed training values or nonfinite/zero post-winsorization SD makes the fold noncomputable; no feature may be dropped.

Fit unweighted L2 logistic R0 and R1 with intercept, C=1, `lbfgs`, max_iter=10000, tol=1e-8, no class weighting, seed 20260961. Each person gets exactly one finite held-out prediction per model. Held-out outcomes inform no preprocessing, fitting, calibration, threshold, or feature decision.

For p={0.30,0.31,…,0.50}, (NB=TP/N-FP/Ncdot p/(1-p)). Treat-none=0; treat-all uses pooled prevalence.

The **only primary decision scalar** is
[
Delta INB={100over0.50-0.30}int_{0.30}^{0.50}[NB_{R1}(p)-NB_{R0}(p)]dp,
]
using the trapezoid rule over all 21 points. The 21 R1-minus-R0 cells are primary threshold outputs. No R1-minus-default or labs-only contrast substitutes.

Sample 33 hospitals with replacement 2,000 times, seed 20260961. Copies of an original hospital remain one held-out group and are all excluded from that fold’s training; multiplicity weights preprocessing, fitting, and evaluation as materialized row copies would. Refit both models and use finite converged replicates for percentile 95% intervals.

## Frozen ordered falsification and meaning

After computability gates:

- **Supportive:** primary R1-minus-physiology-R0 ΔINB 95% lower bound ≥+1; R1 calibration slope 0.80–1.20; absolute calibration-in-the-large ≤0.05; R1 beats the better default at all 21 thresholds; no primary threshold ΔNB below −0.5/100; ≥80% site deletions positive; Brier and log-loss points improve.
- **Mixed:** primary lower bound clears +1 but any supportive guardrail fails.
- **Opposite:** primary upper bound <0.
- **Adverse to materiality:** primary upper bound <+1 but ≥0, absent an earlier branch. This refutes the locked +1 increment for this model/setting, not every smaller increment.
- **Inconclusive:** no preceding branch, including an interval spanning +1.
- **Not computable:** any source, cohort, coverage, feature, fit, prediction, bootstrap, threshold, or deletion gate fails.

Support means only a material out-of-hospital modeled increment among selected repeatedly measured EICU survivors relative to physiology-augmented R0, under assumed thresholds, plus passage of the default guardrail. Adverse weakens the fixed +1 claim. Opposite means the fixed extension worsened modeled decisions relative to R0, not that repeat testing caused harm. Mixed/inconclusive require independent data, not threshold narrowing, stream removal, clearance, or a favorable secondary metric. Positive ΔINB plus default-guardrail failure is Mixed; it does not turn the default into the primary baseline.
