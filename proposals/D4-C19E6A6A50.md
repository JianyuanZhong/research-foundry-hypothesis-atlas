## New outcome and estimand

After all population, source, roster, leakage and policy-freeze gates pass, trim/uppercase each `41270-0.j` diagnosis and pair it only with the same-position `41280-0.j` date for j=0,...,258. Let
`T18_1=min{date_j: code_j begins exactly "N18", t1 < date_j <= t5}`.
Let `Tdeath` be the earliest strictly parsed nonblank date from `40000-0.0` and `40000-1.0`. A first N18 qualifies only when death is absent or `T18_1<Tdeath`; same-day death competes.

Define E18P=1 when there is a second distinct position/date pair with exact N18 prefix and date `T18_2` satisfying `T18_1+90 calendar days <= T18_2 <= t5` and death is absent or `T18_2<Tdeath`. Same-day duplicates, a second record before the first, a second record after t5, or a second record on/after death do not qualify. A nonblank N18 with blank/unparseable paired date, or a nonblank unparseable death date, is endpoint-integrity failure, not no event. Death never counts as E18P. Descriptively report first-N18 cases dying before confirmation, but do not use that diagnostic as an endpoint or rescue.

For immutable list S_p, p in {AFG1,AF1}, the child estimand is:
`Y18P(p)=1000*sum(i in S_p) E18P_i/466`;
`Delta18P=Y18P(AFG1)-Y18P(AF1)`.
The one-event resolution margin is `m=1000/466=2.145922746781116) events per 1,000 slots. Retain and report inherited `Delta18`, its first-N18 numerator/yields and label as a mandatory nonrescuing companion. Never divide by survivors, event counts, complete cases or the 62-person swap set.

## Analysis, uncertainty and decision rules

Use the inherited participant-level paired bootstrap over canonical-eid-sorted O1_hold, with the exact parent PCG64DXSM seed stream and 2,000 draws. Keep memberships, denominator, fits and all labels fixed in each draw; resample shared participants so covariance between policies is retained. Report point estimates, paired SE, two-sided 95% interval, one-sided limits, finite-draw count, and all list/overlap/swap numerators. Run the complete computation independently twice and require identical event vectors, numerators, intervals, labels and output hashes.

Persistence gates, in addition to all inherited gates, are at least 10 E18P events in the union, at least 3 in each list, at least 4 in the 62-person symmetric difference, at least 95% finite bootstrap draws and positive finite variance. Any failed gate, artifact mismatch, malformed endpoint, or source disagreement yields `n18_persistence_inconclusive`.

Emit exactly one persistence label:
- `n18_persistence_supportive` iff all gates pass, `Delta18P >= m`, and the one-sided 95% lower limit is >0.
- `n18_persistence_adverse` iff all gates pass and the one-sided 95% upper limit is <m.
- `n18_persistence_inconclusive` otherwise, including sparse events, equality at a boundary, uncertainty crossing the decision condition, or integrity failure.

Supportive means only uncertainty-supported enrichment of repeated recorded inpatient N18 under this frozen internal contrast. Adverse falsifies the prespecified durable-record enrichment claim at this quota. Inconclusive is unresolved, not equivalence or no effect.

Joint nonrescuing interpretation is prespecified:
- Both Delta18 and Delta18P supportive: stronger evidence of repeated recorded kidney-disease enrichment; still requires prospective adjudication.
- Delta18 supportive but Delta18P adverse: first-code advantage is not durable under this operational definition; no deployment claim.
- Delta18P supportive but Delta18 not supportive: discordant endpoint layers; do not upgrade the policy.
- Either layer inconclusive: overall clinical question remains unresolved.
- Both adverse: no support for this frozen grip increment on either recorded endpoint.

The inherited record-density control, if present in the direct parent package, remains a required companion: persistence that disappears after prespecified pre-index nonrenal record-density standardization is not kidney-specific evidence. This control is an ascertainment proxy, not a validated negative control.

## Falsification, verification and limits

Falsification is a valid adverse persistence label, not a failed study. The verifier must check source/catalog/snapshot identity, exact field presence, one-to-one joins, code/date position pairing, strict t1/t5/death chronology, 90-day separation, duplicate handling, immutable rosters, denominator arithmetic, paired bootstrap, gates, and that every conclusion names computed output keys and uncertainty. Fixtures must include one N18 only; same-day duplicate; second N18 at 89 and exactly 90 days; second N18 after death or t5; malformed paired date; persistence supportive/adverse/inconclusive; upstream failure with favorable downstream arithmetic; changed roster/denominator; outcome leakage; and unsupported claims that E18P proves CKD, measured GFR, causality, prevention, safety, fairness, cost-effectiveness or benefit. Correct arithmetic with unsupported conclusions must fail.

Automatic verification can establish only computational facts: data identity/availability, joins, parser chronology, frozen membership, endpoint arithmetic, bootstrap, gates, labels and output linkage. It cannot establish complete inpatient/outpatient capture, adjudicated persistent CKD, renal mechanism, measured GFR, censoring/emigration, actionability, workflow, cost, equity, transportability, safety, or patient benefit. Those require longitudinal outpatient labs, measured GFR, complete kidney-replacement and censoring linkage, chart/nephrologist adjudication, external validation, workflow/cost/equity review and a prospective comparative testing-strategy study.
