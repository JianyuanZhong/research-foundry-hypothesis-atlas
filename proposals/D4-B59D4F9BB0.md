## Question, evidence and advance

The strongest evidence currently supports only a structural, data-bound question: the export contains pre-cancer physiological fields, cancer code/date arrays, inpatient diagnosis code/date arrays and death dates. The parent therefore supports testing association with recorded inpatient I50 events and fixed-capacity ranking within cancer sites. It does not establish biological reserve, applicability to all cancer survivors, adjudicated heart failure, or referral benefit.

The unresolved clinical question is whether reserve information remains useful for the broader eligible cancer-survivor population rather than only people with complete physiological measurements. This is consequential because a review or testing policy cannot be judged from an assay-complete convenience subset when measurement availability is related to health, calendar era, cancer site or access to care.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis: among people with an incident cancer, no recorded heart failure at the cancer landmark, and broad pre-cancer eligibility, transporting the reserve-panel signal from the measurement-complete subset to that broader target improves held-out, site-balanced 10%-capacity capture of first recorded inpatient I50 events versus a transported control-only baseline; the increment remains directionally consistent in late no-refit and leave-one-supported-site-out tests. If the increment disappears after fold-local selection transport, or occurs only in the measurement-complete subset, the broad-population claim is adverse.

This is a substantive advance over the parent’s site-transport claim: it separates within-measurement value from target-population transport. It does not claim causality, treatment effect, or deployment utility.

## Baseline, alternative and analysis

Participant folds are SHA256(UTF8(decimal(eid))) mod 5. Fit on cancer landmarks 2010-2015 and apply with no refit to 2016-2018. All imputation, standardization, splines, thresholds, site eligibility and selection models are training-fold operations.

M0 is a transparent discrete-time complementary-log-log competing-hazard model with age, sex, cancer site, cancer year and TC-T0. M1 adds the reserve panel linearly. M2 uses the identical cohort, split, endpoints, controls, reserve inputs and capacity, replacing reserve terms with degree-3 restricted cubic splines plus prespecified grip×eGFRcr and 102-0.0×systolic interactions. M1 is the primary interpretable test. M2 is a substantive learned/nonlinear alternative that can reveal thresholds or physiological coupling lost by M1; it is not included merely to chase a small predictive gain.

Within each training fold estimate measurement-completion odds from the selection covariates, use stabilized inverse-probability transport weights with a prespecified training-derived truncation rule, and report overlap and effective sample size. Apply weighted M0/M1/M2 to the broad target. Report unweighted measurement-complete results, weighted broad-target results, and complete-case/weighting sensitivity separately. Weighting cannot repair unmeasured access, stage, treatment or outpatient ascertainment.

In the late test, rank by five-year I50 cumulative-incidence function, select the top 10% within each supported site, and combine equal site weights. Report site-balanced and pooled queues, with pooled queue secondary. Fit early-period models on all other supported sites and apply without refit to each held-out site in the late period. Require predictor and predicted-risk overlap before interpreting transport.

Report integrated Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, CIF, top-10% capture, selected-versus-unselected CIF, site and leave-one-site-out contrasts, transport diagnostics and paired list changes. Use 1,000 participant-level bootstrap replicates for primary contrasts, never resampling person-years, and report fold/site/era variability.

## Falsification and interpretation

Require source/header/key, semantics/units, code-date pairing, event-support, observation and weight-overlap gates. Support requires at least 100 selected people, 50 qualifying I50 events overall, 10 events in selected and unselected groups, a 95% interval excluding zero for weighted M1-M0 site-balanced capture, consistent direction in the late or cross-fold test, no single site dominating, and no material calibration harm. Transport support additionally requires at least two supported sites with stable leave-one-site-out direction and ESS pass. M2 support requires reproducible late improvement over M1 and a stable prespecified threshold/interaction surface.

Adverse evidence includes null/reversed reserve concentration, calibration harm, disappearance after site balancing or weighting, failure in held-out sites, or survival after breaking reserve-outcome linkage. A pooled improvement without within-site improvement is adverse to the transportable claim. Inconclusive evidence includes inadequate events, unsupported strata, failed observation/weight overlap, unstable intervals, unresolved units/labels, malformed pairs, only one usable site, or contradictory weighted/unweighted results.

Audits: permute I50 dates within site/year/fold; permute reserve values within age-band/sex/site/year; randomize capacity labels; shift one code/date position and require integrity failure; omit site controls; compare complete-case with fold-local imputation; permute landmark year or early/late labels; exclude the first 90 days after TC; and use a prespecified negative-control non-cardiac inpatient endpoint. A signal surviving linkage-breaking permutations is falsified as reserve attribution.

Support means only conditional association with recorded inpatient I50 events and, if gates pass, target-transported ranking under this UKB split. It does not establish biological reserve, adjudicated heart failure, causality, referral benefit, treatment selection, cost-effectiveness or external validity. Stage, recurrence, oncology treatment, echocardiography, outpatient completeness and access mechanisms require additional data and expert adjudication.
