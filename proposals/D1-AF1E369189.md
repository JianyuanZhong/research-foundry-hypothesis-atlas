# Narrow child proposal — documented respiratory-support initiation after minute 360

Status: design only; substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. No cohort count, fitted coefficient, prediction, metric, bootstrap interval, or study result is claimed.

## Scientific opening and revised claim

The parent’s cross-stream idea is valuable, but its union endpoint combines three events with different meanings: a ventilator interval start, an interval end, and a raw airway-type change. The bounded audit gives a concrete reason to narrow it: 865,381 `respiratoryCare` rows across 44,772 stays were observed, but 770,432 `airwaytype` values were blank and many interval boundary fields were incomplete. A union event can therefore be positive because of a start, end, or ambiguous label, while still being impossible to call ventilation or deterioration.

The strongest available evidence supports that eICU is a linked, multi-center archive of timestamped clinical documentation and that temporal EHR prediction can be evaluated across hospitals [K1][K2]. It also supports treating irregular sampling, missingness, and preprocessing decisions as explicit objects of analysis [K3]. None of these works validates the clinical semantics of this `respiratoryCare` extract. The parent’s proposed models have no fitted result to inherit.

This child tests a narrower and falsifiable claim:

> Among adult first-ICU stays with more than 360 minutes of recorded ICU time and no valid *documented* active respiratoryCare ventilator interval at minute 360, the parent pre-landmark state/process vector has a directionally consistent, hospital-held-out increment for a later, unambiguous documented respiratoryCare ventilator-interval start in (360,720], after accounting for ICU exit as a competing risk. The increment should be reproducible in both an elastic-net hazard and a capacity-matched histogram-boosting hazard, and should not be explained by respiratory-record availability, blank/unknown airway fields, duplicate/conflicting rows, or documentation timing.

The operative label is deliberately `R_start_doc`, “documented respiratoryCare ventilator-interval start,” not clinical deterioration. It is not invasive ventilation unless a source audit and clinician adjudication establish that the accepted airway vocabulary denotes invasive support. If the audit cannot do that, the scientifically valid result remains a documentation/workflow association.

This changes the parent’s claim from “early information corroborates a later respiratory-care transition” to the more precise question “does early information predict a single, auditable support-start boundary, and under which ascertainment strata?” It removes the non-initiation components that made a union event difficult to interpret. A positive result would not validate the parent’s union endpoint wholesale; it would support only the strict start component.

## What competing explanations predict

The leading interpretation is a reproducible operational support-start signal: pre-landmark state/process information contains information about later documentation of a new respiratory-care interval.

Important rivals are:

1. **Availability/selection.** P may predict whether respiratoryCare is recorded, not whether a support start occurs. This predicts a P increment for any respiratory row, but attenuation or disappearance for `R_start_doc) within comparable observation coverage.
2. **Baseline-support misclassification.** A missing or open baseline boundary may make an already-supported patient look newly started. This predicts more starts among blank/unknown or interval-incomplete strata and sensitivity instability when ambiguous baseline records are excluded.
3. **Duplicate/conflicting documentation.** Same-offset rows or backfilled interval boundaries may manufacture a start or shift its apparent time. This predicts a material change after conflict exclusion or when event time is replaced by recording/status time.
4. **Discharge/timing artifact.** Documentation density or impending ICU exit may drive the endpoint. This predicts concentration near exit, a signal in exit-time bands, or a large change when `respcarestatusoffset` replaces `ventstartoffset`.
5. **Clinical support initiation.** If the operational start is genuinely a useful proxy for support initiation, the start-time signal should remain in held-out hospitals and across documented-support/unknown ascertainment strata, with no dominant availability or timing explanation. Even this pattern would not establish indication, invasiveness, physiology, or treatment effect.

These explanations can be separated only operationally with this archive. They are not fully distinguishable without device/order provenance, clinician review, and a reliable clinical event time.

## Population, landmark, and competing risks

Time zero is ICU admission; all offsets are ICU-relative. Preserve the parent cohort exactly:

- adult first-ICU stays: finite `patientunitstayid`, `hospitalid`, `unitdischargeoffset`, and `unitdischargestatus); `unitvisitnumber=1`, `unitstaytype=admit`, numeric `age >= 18), retaining the source’s `>89` value;
- for duplicate qualifying stays sharing `uniquepid`, retain the smallest finite `unitadmitoffset), then smallest `patientunitstayid);
- S6 eligibility is `unitdischargeoffset > 360). Do not require survival to 720;
- predictors use exactly [0,360], including a row at offset 360 only in the predictor window;
- follow-up is strictly (360,720], truncated at the earlier of minute 720 and recorded `unitdischargeoffset).

The risk set is stays with no **valid documented active vent interval** at minute 360. This is an operational absence criterion, not proof of no clinical support. A valid interval has finite `ventstartoffset`, and either finite `ventendoffset > ventstartoffset` or a missing end treated as open. It is active at 360 when `ventstartoffset <= 360` and the valid end is greater than 360 or open. A valid interval beginning after 360 does not exclude the stay.

For each stay, estimate the first cause in follow-up:

- `R_start_doc`: strict, unambiguous documented start, as defined below;
- ICU exit alive before a start: `unitdischargestatus == "Alive"`;
- ICU exit expired before a start: `unitdischargestatus == "Expired"`.

An expired discharge is a competing cause but has no inferred death time. Stays without a start or exit through the administrative end are event-free for this recorded-documentation estimand only, never clinically event-free. Cause-specific pooled discrete-time hazards use (360,480], (480,600], and (600,720].

## Raw respiratory audit and endpoint construction

The solver must first save a row-level audit before fitting models. All raw `airwaytype` strings, including null, empty, whitespace-only, and unknown values, are retained with counts by hospital, baseline stratum, interval-validity class, and follow-up outcome class only after outcomes are frozen. Preserve raw values; normalization is an audit view.

### 1. Valid interval and duplicate rules

A candidate interval has finite `ventstartoffset`. If `ventendoffset` is present it is valid only when finite and strictly greater than the start. A missing end is an open interval and remains valid for start detection, but its open-boundary flag is mandatory. A missing start, nonfinite offset, end equal to start, or end before start is invalid and saved as an invalid-boundary record.

Before any event is assigned:

- exact duplicates are collapsed only on `patientunitstayid,currenthistoryseqnum,ventstartoffset,ventendoffset,airwaytype`;
- preserve `respcareid`, `respcarestatusoffset`, `priorventstartoffset`, and `priorventendoffset` for the duplicate/backfill audit;
- rows with the same stay, sequence, and start but different end, airway, status, or prior-boundary values are conflict-flagged, not silently selected;
- if all conflicting rows agree on the finite start offset and the start itself is valid, they support a *documented start time* but remain conflict-flagged; they cannot support an invasive-airway subtype;
- if conflicting rows disagree on start offset, the candidate is `start_ambiguous`; it is not a primary event. Report an interval-bracket sensitivity using the earliest and latest conflicting start, without assigning a clinical time.

The primary endpoint is therefore conservative about the time of initiation while not throwing away the fact that documentation conflicted. A conflict-excluded sensitivity removes all conflict-flagged candidates. A conflict-inclusive sensitivity counts a same-start conflict as a documentation start but never upgrades its airway semantics.

### 2. Primary `R_start_doc`

For each eligible risk-set stay, identify the earliest finite `ventstartoffset` in strictly (360,720] and before the earlier of 720 and `unitdischargeoffset`, attached to a valid interval under the rules above. Require no conflicting start offsets at that time and no evidence from a valid baseline interval that the same support episode was already active at minute 360. If the same start is represented by exact duplicates or same-start end/airway conflicts, retain the event time and set flags; do not resolve the end or airway label.

The event record must include `patientunitstayid`, event time, raw `airwaytype), `respcarestatusoffset`, `currenthistoryseqnum`, `respcareid`, start/end/open flags, duplicate/conflict flags, prior-boundary values, and the source row identifiers used. The primary label is “documented respiratoryCare interval start.” It is not “invasive ventilation.”

### 3. Airway-support interpretation gate

Create an audit-only `R_start_airway_candidate` by retaining the primary start and reporting whether `airwaytype` is nonblank, blank/unknown, or conflicting. Normalize only for grouping: trim Unicode whitespace, case-fold, and preserve the unmodified token. Do not map tokens to invasive/noninvasive/none from intuition.

A blinded vocabulary audit may declare an airway-support subtype only if the raw vocabulary is nonempty and stable across at least five viable hospitals, same-offset conflicts do not change subtype assignment, and an external source dictionary or clinician review establishes the mapping before outcome/model inspection. If those conditions are not met—which is plausible given the observed blank count—the subtype is unavailable and the primary estimand remains `R_start_doc`. Never recode blank `airwaytype` as “no support” or “noninvasive.”

### 4. Baseline support/ascertainment strata

Assign strata using only [0,360]:

- `documented_support_0`: a valid vent interval overlaps [0,360]; excluded from the no-active-support risk set and reported in the source audit;
- `documented_no_active_vent_0`: at least one valid respiratoryCare row in [0,360], no valid vent interval overlaps [0,360], and the vocabulary audit supplies an explicit non-support category. This is still documented non-support, not clinical absence;
- `no_respiratory_record_0`: no valid respiratoryCare row in [0,360]; this is no respiratoryCare documentation, not no support;
- `R0_unknown`: invalid boundaries, conflicting/ambiguous baseline records, blank/unknown airway semantics where classification would be required, or other unresolved baseline status.

The primary cohort is “no valid documented active vent interval at 360,” with all non-supported strata retained and reported. The no-active and no-record strata are not silently merged into a clinical no-support group. If `documented_no_active_vent_0` cannot be made auditable, it is withheld and `R0_unknown` is reported instead.

For each stratum save counts, raw-row coverage, valid/open/invalid boundary counts, blank/unknown airway counts, same-offset conflicts, first/last status offsets, and follow-up ascertainment. These are prerequisites and uncertainty descriptors, not post-hoc exclusions.

### Schedule-preserving null and timing checks

Preserve the parent’s X-state, trajectory-state, unrestricted within-hospital process-permutation, +180-minute displacement, laboratory/vital block, selection, EOL, observation-weight, and discharge-time nulls. Add:

- **schedule-preserving start null:** within hospital, baseline respiratory stratum, exit-time band, and respiratory/vital coverage bins, permute start times across stays while preserving event counts and censoring. Compare observed first-start incidence/timing to 2,000 cluster-bootstrap null replicates;

- **conflict null:** exclude all conflict-flagged rows and report whether assignment, event time, or model increment changes;

The observed-versus-null timing comparison is descriptive concordance, not causal dependence.

## Falsification criteria and interpretation

The endpoint passes readiness for substantive analysis only if source paths/hashes and schemas match, the start logic is reproducible, at least five hospitals contribute, the required baseline strata and follow-up coverage have adequate events and positivity, and ambiguity/conflict accounting is complete. A failure is an honest endpoint-deferral result, not permission to broaden the union endpoint.

Supportive evidence requires all of the following: a stable strict-start audit; a coherent held-out `Q_P^T) in both elastic-net and boosting; no material explanation by availability, baseline ambiguity, conflict exclusion, recording-time substitution, exit band, or one hospital; and observed timing beyond the schedule-preserving null. This would support a reproducible association between pre-landmark information and later documented respiratoryCare start. It would not establish invasive ventilation, physiologic deterioration, clinical indication, treatment effect, or an alert benefit.

Adverse evidence means an adequately supported `R_start_doc` has no coherent P increment after T, or the increment collapses under availability, timing, conflict, baseline, or exit controls. If the vital labels remain positive while the strict start is null, the strongest claim remains same-stream recorded-vital association; the parent’s cross-stream corroboration is not supported. If the start is positive only when blank/unknown or conflict rows are included, the result favors documentation ambiguity rather than clinical support initiation.

Inconclusive/deferral means the airway vocabulary remains uninterpretable, the endpoint has too few events or hospitals, baseline support cannot be classified, open/conflicting intervals materially change assignment, observation-weight positivity is poor, or uncertainty spans both negligible and meaningful effects. An imprecise null is not refutation. If a strict start is too sparse, do not revert to the parent union; report the unresolved dependency and retain the parent’s narrower recorded-vital conclusion.

## Clinical limits and next decision

The archive does not provide ventilator-device settings, order placement and cancellation, tube-placement/procedure confirmation, clinician indication, bedside event review, reliable distinction between backfill and care time, staffing/workflow logs, or a gold-standard respiratory severity trajectory. `airwaytype` is a raw documentation field; blank is unknown. `ventstartoffset` is a source timestamp, not automatically the time of clinical initiation. Clinical adjudication would require source-interface/device/order provenance and blinded critical-care review of sampled episodes, followed by an external or prospective cohort.

The smallest decision enabled by this experiment is:

- continue to adjudication if strict starts are supported and transportable after the falsification checks;
- revise to an ascertainment/workflow study if availability or status timing explains the signal;
- defer the invasive/clinical interpretation if airway semantics or baseline support remains unresolved;
- retain only the bounded recorded-vital or documentation association if respiratory corroboration is absent.

## Matched alternatives and actual deliverable

Before selecting the analysis, two equally specified alternatives were retained.

**Simple baseline:** elastic-net pooled discrete-time cause-specific logistic hazards for `R_start_doc` and exit causes over the three follow-up intervals, with X/X+P and T/T+P, identical folds, preprocessing, strata, observation-weight options, seeds, and bootstrap. It estimates an additive process increment and exposes whether the result is stable without flexible interactions.

**Substantive learned alternative:** capacity-matched histogram gradient-boosting cause-specific hazards with exactly the same inputs, targets, fold assignments, preprocessing, weights, null assignments, uncertainty, and output metrics. It can reveal nonlinear state-by-process and ascertainment-stratum interactions that an additive model averages away. A small learner-only discrimination gain without calibration, utility, timing-null robustness, and endpoint stability is not a substantive scientific advance.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted, leakage-safe predictions and uncertainty for `R_start_doc`, its exit causes, and the parent’s two recorded-vital labels; a frozen raw respiratory audit; stratum/availability/timing/conflict outputs; paired model increments; schedule-preserving nulls; and a conclusion report that links each claim to computed rows. No result is claimed in this proposal.

A raw-event neural sequence model is deferred because the available fields do not establish device/order semantics and sequence complexity could learn recording order. Revisit only if endpoint adjudication succeeds and a bounded diagnostic shows that fixed source summaries lose clinically meaningful start timing. A causal model is deferred because treatment assignment, intervention provenance, event-time validity, and exchangeability are unavailable. A hierarchical hospital model is descriptive-only; hospital-held-out transport remains the estimand.

The future solver envelope is inherited and unverified: up to 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB RAM, and 28,800 seconds for selected-column extraction, raw audits, endpoint construction, two tabular learner families, nulls, and 2,000 cluster-bootstrap replicates. CPU is provisionally selected because this is a tabular, audit- and bootstrap-heavy workload. No GPU is required by the current alternatives; GPU use cannot repair missing airway semantics. A bounded readiness job may measure extraction, raw audit, one fold of each model, and bootstrap throughput, but readiness is not a study result.
