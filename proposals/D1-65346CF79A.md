# Episode 19 generation — physiologic trajectory versus latent severity

## Scientific opening and falsifiable hypothesis

The parent established a useful but bounded provenance question: in an adult first-ICU eICU risk set, a treatment row can be close in recorded time to a later respiratoryCare event, and that relationship can be audited against respiratoryCharting clinical and entry offsets, duplicate/backfill patterns, schedule-preserving nulls, observation opportunity, and hospitals held out from fitting. The remaining clinically important ambiguity is patient state. A treatment/respiratory pair may be recorded near the time at which a patient is becoming physiologically unstable, so its apparent clinical-offset-linked signal could be a severity marker rather than evidence about an operational respiratory transition. Alternatively, the respiratoryCare endpoint may preferentially be ascertained in patients who remain in the ICU, receive dense monitoring, or enter an end-of-life pathway.

The closest inspected evidence bounds, rather than resolves, this opening. The eICU paper describes a linked multi-center archive but notes that continuously streamed bedside data are only partly archived [K1]. Temporal EHR representations can be useful across centers, but representation and interoperability do not validate the clinical semantics of an individual timestamp [K2]. Reproducible structured-EHR preprocessing supports frozen, auditable construction but does not turn an eICU treatment or respiratory row into a bedside measurement [K3]. The parent’s strongest supported claim is therefore a reproducible recorded-event timing association under its stated audits, not treatment delivery, physiologic deterioration, or mechanism.

Falsifiable hypothesis: among adult first-ICU stays in the parent S6 risk set with no auditable active respiratoryCare interval at minute 360, the parent’s pre-landmark respiratory-treatment process signal and its clinical-offset-linked treatment/respiratory pairing will retain a materially reproducible association with a later strict respiratoryCare interval-start after adjustment for an independently stored, strictly pre-landmark vital trajectory (levels, changes, variability, and measurement availability), and this residual will transport to held-out hospitals. The latent-severity rival predicts substantial attenuation after the vital block, especially for oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, heart rate, and blood-pressure trajectories; the ascertainment/selection rival predicts concentration in high observation density, early EOL documentation, discharge-opportunity, or source-entry strata and similar increments for non-respiratory controls.

“Retain” is a prespecified comparison, not a claim about a particular effect size: the treatment block must improve held-out discrimination/calibration or the predeclared incremental deviance contrast with a confidence interval excluding the null in at least five viable hospitals, with no single hospital or starting stratum dominating, and must survive the listed falsification checks. If that gate is not met, the design reports attenuation or inconclusive precision rather than calling the hypothesis false. The clinical estimand is an association with a recorded respiratoryCare endpoint; it is not a treatment effect, causal effect, or proof of physiologic deterioration.

This is a substantive advance over the parent because the new comparison asks whether the signal survives an independently measured trajectory of patient state, while also making endpoint observability and landmark selection part of the estimand. A positive result would justify a prospective source-validation/adjudication study of treatment order, administration, device state, and bedside timing. A negative result would redirect interpretation toward severity or workflow. Neither result alone establishes that a treatment was delivered or that a respiratoryCare start was a physiologic onset.

## Strict pre-landmark physiologic trajectory

Use only finite-valued rows from vitalPeriodic and vitalAperiodic with 0 <= observationoffset <= 360. The independent source block is:

- temperature, sao2, heartrate, respiration, and systemicmean from vitalPeriodic;
- noninvasivemean from vitalAperiodic.

For each of these six channels, make an immutable patient-level feature record from exact ICU offsets, never forward-fill across the landmark and never use respiratoryCare, respiratoryCharting, treatment, EOL, or endpoint labels to select values. Use a common 12-bin grid of 30-minute bins over [0,360] only for the matched model inputs. In each bin retain the finite median and finite observation count; retain a binary missingness mask. Also derive, when data permit, early [0,60] median, late [300,360] median, late-minus-early change, centered least-squares slope per 60 minutes, robust spread (IQR), and the last finite value and its offset in [0,360]. A channel’s change/slope is missing, with its availability flag retained, unless it has at least one finite observation in each half-window and at least two finite observations overall. Do not impute a physiologic value as a clinical normal; fold-isolated median imputation is allowed only after the missingness/availability indicators have been frozen.

The trajectory is an independently stored measurement stream, not an untreated severity gold standard. Vital values may themselves be charted, monitor-derived, treatment-influenced, or missing not at random. Therefore the design tests whether the parent signal is compatible with observed physiologic trajectory information; it cannot identify latent severity that is not represented in these fields. Keep source-specific counts, last-observation offsets, and same-offset duplicate flags, because a change in observation schedule can mimic a change in state.

The vital block is evaluated in three prespecified forms: (V-level) bin values and late/early levels; (V-trajectory) V-level plus change, slope, spread, and last-offset features; and (V-availability) V-trajectory plus source/channel observation counts, missingness masks, and last-observation offsets. The primary adjustment is V-availability, while V-level and V-trajectory show how much attenuation is attributable to state versus measurement opportunity.

## Analysis and matched alternatives

Construct all preprocessing, treatment vocabulary, physiologic summaries, imputation values, scaling, feature screening, hyperparameters, endpoint opportunity rules, and null assignments inside training folds. Split by hospital using a frozen held-out-hospital list or leave-one-hospital-out when feasible; no patient from a held-out hospital contributes to fitting, vocabulary selection, imputation, or threshold choice. A hospital may contribute only to the held-out evaluation fold.

Use the same 12-bin trajectory values/masks, scalar parent state/process features, treatment features, availability controls, targets, intervalization, competing exits, folds, censoring weights, and uncertainty procedure for both methods:

1. Transparent baseline: cause-specific discrete-time elastic-net hazards. Flatten the shared 12-bin × (six vital values plus six masks) representation and concatenate the shared scalar features. Standardize and impute fold-isolated values; retain missingness flags. Select the elastic-net penalty and mixing parameter by training-fold likelihood with a prespecified one-standard-error rule. Report signed coefficients, grouped vital/treatment/availability contributions, and partial contrasts. This model is the audit-friendly test of whether treatment-specific information remains after linear adjustment for observed physiology and ascertainment.

2. Scientifically substantive nonlinear alternative: a small temporal-convolution model with a shared 12-bin channel/mask input, a short two-layer one-dimensional convolutional temporal branch with global pooling, and a dense branch for the same scalar parent/treatment/availability features, followed by cause-specific hazard heads. Use the same fold isolation, outcome, censoring weights, hospital holdouts, early stopping rule, and parameter budget across methods. The purpose is not a small score chase: it tests whether clinically different shapes—such as a late oxygenation fall, a respiratory-rate rise, or cross-channel changes—interact nonlinearly with treatment timing and observation opportunity in ways a flattened additive model loses. Its interpretation is limited to learned trajectory-shape associations. If a CPU feasibility check cannot meet the solver envelope, defer this alternative and preserve the elastic-net study; do not silently replace it with a fitted result or claim that nonlinearity was absent.

Compute and method-selection budget: this discovery episode did not fit either model and reports no feasibility result. The future solver planning envelope from inputs.json is at most 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB RAM, 28800 seconds, and up to 8 allocated GPUs; this is a planning ceiling, not a promise. Gzip extraction, six-channel binning, sparse elastic-net fits, interval joins, hospital-held-out predictions, and cluster resampling are provisionally CPU-first. A bounded planning estimate is 4–8 hours on the permitted CPU/memory envelope for the full audit, nested fits, nulls, and bootstrap; this estimate is unmeasured. The small temporal convolution is expected to fit within the same wall-clock envelope on CPU with early stopping, but if measured profiling shows a matrix bottleneck, request one allocated A100 and use cuda:0 inside the job. GPU use is optional and carries no scientific merit. The temporal convolution is retained because it can reveal trajectory-shape and cross-channel interactions that the transparent baseline cannot; it is deferred—not silently simplified—only if the documented solver envelope cannot support it.

The model sequence is nested and identical across methods: (M0) parent baseline state/process and hospital covariates; (M1) M0 plus generic vital/treatment observation availability; (M2) M1 plus V-level/V-trajectory/V-availability; (M3) M2 plus pre-landmark treatment-specific timing/process features. The primary scientific contrast is M3 minus M2, evaluated before and after the trajectory block; the attenuation contrast is the change in the treatment block between M1 and M2. The post-outcome C_clin/C_entry/C_clinchart timing audit is not included in M0–M3.

Report paired held-out AUROC/PR-AUC only as secondary predictive summaries, cause-specific Brier score and calibration-in-the-large/slope, cumulative incidence at 720, integrated calibration error, and treatment-block incremental log likelihood/deviance. Report net benefit at a 10% 360-minute risk threshold only if training-fold prevalence and decision analysis justify that threshold; otherwise do not invent a clinical decision claim. Use 2,000 hospital-cluster bootstrap replicates, or an equivalent prespecified cluster method if the number of viable hospitals makes that unstable, paired across models, strata, endpoints, and nulls. Include per-hospital estimates, hospital ranges, starting-stratum estimates, and effective sample sizes. A nonlinear gain without stable treatment-block contrast is descriptive model behavior, not evidence for the hypothesis.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

Before fitting, write a frozen manifest containing source hashes, headers, row/ID audit counts, interval-integrity flags, raw treatment vocabulary and class rules, vital trajectory construction, hospital folds, censoring/weight rules, and output filenames. Required outputs are: source/read-only audit; adult first-ICU and S6 manifests; strict trajectory table with channel availability; treatment and respiratory raw-pair audit; C_clin/C_entry/C_clinchart lag tables; R_start_doc/R_stop_doc/documentation endpoint outcomes; nested held-out predictions; paired metrics and cluster uncertainty; hospital/stratum/EOL/exit tables; timing and schedule null outputs; and a claim-to-output conclusion table.

Run these falsification checks without reselecting favorable windows:

- Timing/source-coupling nulls: cyclically shift treatment offsets within each stay’s observed endpoint opportunity and independently permute treatment rows within hospital and offset-density strata, preserving counts, raw labels, and schedule. Recompute pair lags and concordance. These nulls test timing coupling, not independence.
- Physiologic linkage null: permute each patient’s complete pre-landmark vital trajectory within hospital × S6 starting stratum while preserving its 12-bin missingness pattern and observation density. The treatment increment should not acquire a reproducible trajectory-specific contrast under this null. A failure means the trajectory adjustment or availability controls are encoding site process rather than patient state.
- Observation control: compare M1, M2 and M3; repeat after matching/weighting on vital and charting observation density. If treatment association disappears with availability alone, the ascertainment rival is favored; if it disappears only after trajectory levels/changes, latent observed-state severity is favored.
- Endpoint control: repeat against R_stop_doc and the documentation-only endpoint. A treatment signal that reproduces for both generic documentation and non-respiratory treatment is not respiratory-specific.
- Lead/lag and tie controls: use [0,330], exclude exact-offset and duplicate/backfill pairs, and compare clinical, chart-clinical, and chart-entry anchors. Entry dominance, exact ties, or null sensitivity is adverse provenance evidence, not a positive result.
- Transport control: require the direction and uncertainty of the primary treatment-block contrast to be checked per held-out hospital. No pooled result is supportive if it is supplied by one hospital or one source-state stratum.

Supportive evidence would be: adequate positive opportunity in at least five hospitals; stable trajectory availability; prelandmark treatment/process association with the later strict endpoint; a nontrivial residual treatment-block contrast after V-availability and landmark/EOL safeguards; similar direction in elastic-net and temporal convolution; clinical-offset pair alignment not dominated by entry offsets, ties, duplicates, schedule nulls, or documentation/non-respiratory controls; and no single hospital or stratum dominating. This establishes a reproducible association compatible with—but not proving—a respiratory operational transition beyond the measured vital trajectory.

Adverse evidence would be strong attenuation after V-trajectory adjustment, a trajectory-specific or observation-density-specific signal with no treatment residual, matching non-respiratory/documentation endpoints, concentration near discharge or EOL, failure under held-out hospitals, or loss under tie/backfill/schedule controls. The conclusion should then favor observed severity or ascertainment/workflow as the more economical explanation and should not call the parent signal treatment corroboration.

Inconclusive evidence includes fewer than five viable hospitals, insufficient channel coverage or positivity, unstable treatment vocabulary, low effective sample size after landmark/EOL weights, wide paired intervals, incompatible endpoint semantics across hospitals, or a treatment-side timestamp gap that prevents a clinical-versus-entry comparison. An imprecise null is not evidence that no physiology exists. The experiment is complete only when all required outputs and the claim-to-output table are present; readiness, source availability, or a model score does not complete it.

## Clinical adjudication limits and actual deliverable

Available fields cannot establish treatment order, administration, indication, dose, delivered respiratory support, bedside action, ventilator device state, physiologic onset, or reliable death time. Vital rows are not guaranteed raw waveforms or untreated measurements. The design cannot identify unmeasured latent severity, causal treatment effects, or a mechanism. Stronger claims require expert adjudication of raw treatment/respiratory records against orders, administration/device logs and bedside time, ideally with waveform and FiO2/ventilator data, plus an independent cohort or prospective validation. It also requires clinical review of the frozen treatment vocabulary and endpoint interval rules.

The newly fitted/estimated deliverable is not a new cohort count or a confirmatory result. It is the frozen trajectory feature manifest; the nested, hospital-held-out elastic-net and temporal-convolution estimands; the paired uncertainty and falsification outputs; the post-outcome clinical/entry timing audit; and a conclusion table linking every supportive, adverse, or inconclusive statement to a computed file and stating which claims remain adjudication-dependent. No fitted result is claimed here.

Alternatives not chosen: a raw-sequence neural model is deferred because treatment lacks an entry timestamp and the scientific target is provenance separation, not maximum predictive accuracy; the small temporal convolution is retained only as a bounded shape-sensitive alternative on the same shared inputs. A causal treatment-effect model is deferred because assignment, indication, administration, and delivery are unavailable. A larger mechanistic ventilator model is deferred because waveforms, FiO2, and validated device-state labels are absent. These choices should be revisited only if treatment-side provenance, richer physiologic streams, or expert adjudication becomes available.
