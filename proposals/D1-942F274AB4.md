# Persistent lactate under improving perfusion: does asynchronous organ recovery change the early deterioration signal?

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

The strongest evidence currently available is data availability, not a clinical result. The configured MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot has timestamped ICU boundaries, repeated lactate/MAP/organ measurements, vasoactive and support intervals, urine output, and admission/death fields. The asynchronous-recovery expert seed is explicitly untested. The local demonstrations justify comparing interpretable longitudinal summaries with learned temporal representations, but they do not establish that this MIMIC phenotype is biological recovery or that it changes treatment benefit.

The parent asks whether persistent hyperlactatemia has a smaller 48-hour adverse association when measured perfusion is improving than when it is worsening. Its unresolved clinical interpretation is whether “improving perfusion” is reassuring when another organ system is not recovering. A patient with falling vasopressor support and improving MAP may still have falling urine output, rising creatinine, or persistent/increasing respiratory support. If that discordance identifies materially different near-term risk, a single lactate-by-perfusion label is insufficient for early surveillance decisions.

The new hypothesis is:

> Among first eligible ICU stays alive and still under ICU observation 12 hours after entry, the lactate-by-perfusion interaction is less negative (or positive) in patients with asynchronous observed renal/respiratory recovery than in patients with concordant recovery.

Define the parent interaction within organ stratum (g):

`Delta_g = [CIF(A | persistent lactate, improving perfusion, g) - CIF(A | nonpersistent lactate, improving perfusion, g)] - [CIF(A | persistent lactate, worsening perfusion, g) - CIF(A | nonpersistent lactate, worsening perfusion, g)]`.

The primary new estimand is:

`Theta_async = Delta_async - Delta_concordant`.

The directional hypothesis is `Theta_async > 0`: any apparent reassurance associated with improving perfusion is attenuated when renal and respiratory trajectories are asynchronous. The clinically meaningful null region is +2 to -2 percentage points around zero for `Theta_async`; a positive estimate within that region is not support.

This is clinically important because it tests whether a readily communicated perfusion context is adequate for early risk interpretation, not whether a complex model predicts slightly better. Support would motivate prospective measurement of paired organ recovery before treating improving MAP/pressor burden as reassuring. An adverse result would show that discordance does not explain the parent interaction under this operational definition. An inconclusive result would establish that the available EHR cannot adjudicate this decision without denser support data or clinical review.

## Parent lactate and measured-perfusion variables

Use the parent definitions exactly.

Primary lactate rows are `hosp/labevents` item `50813`, with finite numeric `valuenum`, accepted `valueuom=mmol/L`, valid `charttime/storetime), and distinct `labevent_id`.

## New observed organ-trajectory phenotype

This phenotype is an operational EHR phenotype, not adjudicated kidney or lung recovery. Each component must be observed in both six-hour halves; otherwise the patient is organ-indeterminate and excluded from the organ-stratified primary cells but retained in the coverage flow and parent analysis.

### Renal trajectory

Use `hosp/labevents` item 50912 (Creatinine), requiring finite `valuenum), canonical `valueuom=mg/dL), distinct `labevent_id), and both times available by `tF`. In each half require at least one valid creatinine value. Define the relative creatinine change as late median minus early median.

Use `icu/outputevents` item 226559 (Foley), requiring finite nonnegative numeric `value), `valueuom=mL), valid `charttime/storetime), and at least two valid rows in each half. Sum event volumes within each half and divide by the fixed six-hour half duration to obtain an observed Foley volume rate in mL/hour. Do not impute missing output as zero, interpolate between sparse rows, or interpret output as measured GFR. A sensitivity uses creatinine alone and a sensitivity uses urine-output direction alone.

Renal-improving is pre-specified as creatinine decreasing by at least 0.1 mg/dL or at least 10% from the early median, while Foley rate does not decrease by more than 10%. Renal-worsening is creatinine increasing by at least 0.1 mg/dL or at least 10%, or Foley rate decreasing by more than 10%. All other renal patterns are renal-indeterminate. These fixed thresholds are chosen before outcome inspection and are not changed for event support.

### Respiratory support trajectory

Use the highest observed support state in each half, with explicit observation requirements:

- 3 = active invasive ventilation: an overlapping valid `icu/procedureevents` interval for item 225792 (Invasive Ventilation), or a valid item 226260 (Mechanically Ventilated) chart row indicating active support;
- 2 = active non-invasive ventilation: an overlapping valid `procedureevents` interval for item 225794 (Non-invasive Ventilation);
- 1 = documented supplemental oxygen without the above: valid numeric `icu/chartevents` item 223834 (O2 Flow, L/min) greater than zero or item 223835 (Inspired O2 Fraction) above room-air level, after prespecified unit normalization;
- 0 is not assigned from absent documentation. A half without an observable support state is respiratory-indeterminate.

The respiratory support audit also records items 220277 (O2 saturation pulseoxymetry), 220210 (Respiratory Rate), and 220339 (PEEP set) when valid, but these corroborative measurements do not create a support state by themselves. For oxygen fraction, accept either a fraction in (0,1] or a percentage in (1,100] only when the item dictionary/unit audit supports that representation; do not guess unsupported units. Require at least two valid respiratory chart observations per half and valid clinical/recording times. The procedure dictionary must verify item/link `procedureevents) before use.

Respiratory-improving is a late support score at least one level lower than early. Respiratory-worsening is at least one level higher. Equal scores are respiratory-stable, not improving. A valid intubation record item 224385 can be retained as a transition-process audit but cannot by itself define recovery.

### Paired state

The primary paired states are:

- concordant recovery: renal-improving and respiratory-improving;
- asynchronous recovery: exactly one of renal-improving and respiratory-improving, with the other component renal-worsening, respiratory-worsening or stable;
- neither-recovering: neither component improving, retained as a pre-specified secondary stratum;
- paired-indeterminate: any renal or respiratory indeterminate component, excluded from organ-stratified inference but reported.

This definition intentionally does not call renal-worsening/respiratory-worsening “organ failure,” and it does not treat lack of charting as recovery. The primary `Theta_async` comparison requires concordant and asynchronous cells; neither-recovering and paired-indeterminate results are secondary/audit outputs.

## Fixed endpoint and frozen support gates

Use the parent’s exact fixed endpoint through `(tF,tF+24h]):

- R: first new target-pressor interval after `tF), first qualifying `icu/procedureevents` support interval after `tF) (224385, 225792, 225794, 225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809 or 225955), or later ICU stay in the same admission with a different `stay_id);

- N: no R/D/S while ascertainable;
- U: missing/contradictory death or endpoint fields preventing ordering.

A later ICU admission and support start at the same time are one R event with component flags. Report the exact parent composite A=R-or-D and separate R, D, S, N and U. This remains an operational ICU-record endpoint: ward oxygen escalation, treatment intent, appropriateness, preventability and complete rescue ascertainment are unavailable.

Apply the parent endpoint-independent coverage gate before outcomes/modeling and then add a new organ gate, both frozen before any effect estimate:

- parent fixed gate: at least one valid lactate, at least two distinct-time MAP observations and at least one valid target-pressor interval in each half;
- renal gate: creatinine in both halves, Foley output in both halves with the stated row counts, accepted units, and no unresolved timing/unit failures;
- respiratory gate: at least two valid respiratory observations in both halves, an observed support state in both halves, and valid support interval/chart timing;
- support gate: the parent’s >=40 subjects and >=10 A events per four lactate/perfusion cell, >=8 and >=3 A events per held-out test cell, U <=10% overall and <=20% per cell, plus >=40 subjects and >=10 A events in each concordant and asynchronous organ-stratified cell within each lactate/perfusion cell. If integer partitioning prevents test support, `Theta_async` is not estimable.

No cell may be merged, redefined, or dropped after seeing outcomes. Component-specific R/D interactions require at least five events of that component in every reported organ/lactate/perfusion cell. The organ gate is an additional generalizability restriction and cannot invalidate the parent’s estimates.

## Models and scientific comparison

All models use the same organ-gated subjects, source whitelist, target, split and held-out test set.

- **B0:** the parent penalized discrete-time cause-specific complementary-log-log hazard model for R, D and S, using demographics/admission context, terminal lactate/MAP/organ values, support status, masks and process summaries. This is the transparent latest-state baseline.
- **Borgan (primary transparent alternative):** B0 plus the fixed renal state, respiratory state, paired concordant/asynchronous/neither indicators, and pre-specified lactate-by-perfusion-by-paired-state terms. Its primary output is `Theta_async) and standardized CIFs, not a generic feature-importance ranking.
- **Bmask:** context and observation/process indicators without physiologic values or organ states. If Borgan’s apparent interaction is matched by Bmask, measurement intensity rather than physiology is a more plausible explanation.
- **Jorgan:** a small masked chronological GRU over six two-hour bins containing the same lactate, MAP, vasoactive, creatinine, Foley, respiratory and support-process whitelist as Borgan. Hidden size 32 or 64, five fixed seeds, dropout and early stopping are chosen only in train/validation. Jorgan can reveal whether temporal coupling, recovery reversal or persistence adds information discarded by fixed direction rules; a gain without calibration, Borgan separation or chronology-shuffle attenuation is not mechanistic evidence.

The transparent Borgan is selected for scientific interpretation because it directly tests whether the parent interaction changes by a pre-specified observed recovery state. Jorgan is retained as a substantive alternative because it can discover time-local coupling and nonlinear recovery patterns. A larger transformer and a latent biological mixture are deferred: missing adjudicated organ states and selective support documentation would make added capacity difficult to interpret. A treatment-effect model is deferred because MIMIC input/procedure records do not supply reliable treatment intent, counterfactual outcomes, fluid responsiveness or sufficient control of time-varying confounding.

## Analysis, uncertainty and falsification

Fit cause-specific discrete-time hazards and derive 6-, 12- and 24-hour CIFs for R, D, S and A. Report all four parent lactate/perfusion cells within each paired state, `Delta_g), `Theta_async), the neither-recovering secondary contrast, component-specific contrasts, transition-aware multiclass and composite Brier scores, calibration intercept/slope, and paired Borgan-B0, Borgan-Bmask and Jorgan-Borgan held-out differences.

Use 1,000 subject-level bootstrap replicates where the solver envelope permits, resampling subjects rather than rows and refitting when feasible. Otherwise report the exact completed number and distinguish conditional prediction bootstrap from full-refit uncertainty. Bootstrap standardized CIFs, `Delta_g), `Theta_async), Brier differences and calibration. Keep the +/-2 percentage-point null region separate from uncertainty intervals. Do not call a clinically meaningful result from a small AUROC/AUPRC change.

Required falsifications and sensitivities include:

- source dictionary/link/unit audit; accepted/rejected units; impossible values; interval clipping; exact duplicate identity audit;
- join, first-index, time-zero, first-transition, U-label and subject-split audits;
- prohibited-column and no-post-cutoff clinical/recording-time scans;
- chart-time-only versus chart-plus-storetime availability;
- Borgan versus Bmask, parent phenotype-only/process-only controls, and organ-state permutation across subjects;
- early/late order permutation preserving masks/counts, endpoint-label permutation within partition with complete refit, and chronology shuffle for Jorgan;
- fixed 2.0 versus fixed 4.0 mmol/L lactate threshold;
- MAP-only, arterial-MAP-only, pressor-only, MAP/pressor direction reversal, Foley corroboration, creatinine-only and urine-only organ sensitivities;
- respiratory support definitions using procedure-only versus chart-supported state, and oxygen-unit representation audit;
- terminal-bin omission, 4/6/8-bin history, no rate/amount pooling for parent pressors, and no missing-output-as-zero audit;
- separate R, D and S results, U-bound analyses, 7-day live-exit parent sensitivity, and the unchanged parent live-exit q selection audit;
- age/sex, care-unit, anchor-year-group and measurement-intensity strata where frozen support permits.

A valid temporal/organ signal should attenuate under organ-state permutation and early/late order permutation, while a process-only signal should be exposed by Bmask. A Jorgan gain that survives chronology shuffle is a falsification of the claimed temporal interpretation.

## Interpretation and claim boundaries

Support for `Theta_async>0) requires the organ gate and all relevant cell-support gates to pass, an interval for `Theta_async` entirely above +2 percentage points, lower or less-reassuring fixed-landmark A CIF for persistent lactate in asynchronous versus concordant states under improving perfusion, Borgan separating from Bmask, calibrated held-out performance, and stability across timing, unit, respiratory-definition, creatinine/urine and U-bound sensitivities. This supports a reproducible prognostic heterogeneity pattern in observed EHR recovery/support states.

An adverse result is a reliably nonpositive or reversed `Theta_async`, a signal explained by Bmask, disappearance under available-by-cutoff timing, instability under organ-definition sensitivity, failure of dictionary/timestamp audits, endpoint-label permutation survival, or a Jorgan gain surviving chronology permutation. It challenges the operational hypothesis; it does not prove that asynchronous recovery is safe, harmful, or biologically causal.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed paired coverage/event support, high paired-indeterminate fraction, sparse component events, poor overlap/ESS, U above the frozen bounds, intervals wider than six percentage points, unstable calibration, or disagreement that cannot separate measurement process from clinical trajectory. It is not permissible to relax the organ phenotype or move `tF) after seeing outcomes.

Computationally checkable claims are provenance, schemas, dictionary labels, joins, time ordering, flow, gate flags, phenotype construction, split integrity, fits, predictions, CIFs, calibration, uncertainty, contrasts, overlap and falsifications. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for true renal/respiratory recovery, measured GFR, fluid status, microcirculatory dysfunction, lactate etiology, complete ward rescue capture, treatment intent/effect, appropriateness or preventability of rescue, discharge policy, monitoring benefit, net utility and transportability.

## Evidence, alternatives and compute

The asynchronous organ-recovery seed (`[starting question]`) supplies the clinical rationale but is untested; its cited inflammatory-recovery paper was inspected only as a related rationale, not as validation of this ICU phenotype. The AKI-discordance seed (`[starting question]`) is less strong for this evolution because measured kidney function is unavailable and its treatment/physiology interpretation would remain confounded. The transfusion-heterogeneity seed (`[starting question]`) is less strong because active bleeding and ischemia are incompletely observed and causal treatment heterogeneity would not be identified by this snapshot. Diuretic timing has the same severe confounding-by-indication problem. The selected child therefore uses asynchronous recovery to repair the parent’s measurement interpretation while retaining an observational prognostic estimand.

The baseline-versus-alternative reasoning is explicit: B0/Borgan is preferred for the primary scientific question because it makes the three-way contrast auditable and estimates a clinically interpretable absolute-risk quantity. Jorgan is retained because ordered coupling and reversal may reveal information that fixed half-window labels discard. Bmask tests whether apparent organ heterogeneity is merely documentation intensity. A renal-only model, support-only model, latent mixture and larger transformer are deferred or secondary because they either lose the paired clinical question, cannot distinguish biology from measurement process, or add capacity without available adjudication. Exact inputs, target, split, uncertainty, baseline comparison and approximate budget are specified above.

Measured feasibility facts are archive/catalog availability, complete schemas, archive members and dictionary labels/links/units. Unverified are full prevalence, strict gate survival, paired cell support, overlap/ESS, bootstrap duration and Jorgan convergence. The future solver planning envelope from `inputs.json` is 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB memory, up to 8 A100-SXM4-80GB GPUs and 28,800 seconds.
