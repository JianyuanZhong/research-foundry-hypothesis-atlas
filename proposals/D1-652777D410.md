# Episode 97 successor: does the repeat lactate value earn a mortality-review slot?

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

At the fixed 24-hour reassessment after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry, clinicians may have a repeat lactate for some ICU patients. The unresolved clinical-information question is narrower than whether lactate-guided care improves survival:

> After the pre-repeat state and the observation process (whether a repeat was obtained, how old it was, and the non-lactate surveillance recorded near 24 hours) are represented, does the numeric repeat lactate itself place more near-term hospital deaths into a fixed-capacity review queue?

This matters because repeat testing consumes blood, staff attention and review capacity. A positive result would justify prospective testing of a rule that uses the released value to prioritize review, not a recommendation to order lactate or to change treatment. A null or adverse result would support avoiding value-based escalation of a repeat-test review pathway and would show that the apparent signal is principally the testing process or baseline severity.

The strongest available evidence supports only modest prognostic association and measurement-process concern. The inspected full text of Baysan et al. (2022, DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750, PMCID PMC9444407) reports a small incremental association of 24-hour lactate in a selected sepsis cohort; inspected Sisk et al. (2020, DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242, PMCID PMC7810439) supports the concern that whether and when measurements occur can be prognostic. Neither establishes incremental fixed-capacity value of the numeric result, cross-practice transport, or patient benefit. The demonstration files were used as standards for longitudinal design and transport/calibration discipline; the cancer main paper and STAR Methods were unavailable, so only its metadata and supplement are relied on.

## Primary hypothesis and estimand

Use the inherited EICU all-baseline/L24 cohort and define two outcome-blind hospital repeat-practice directions, Low-to-High and High-to-Low. At capacity c=0.10, each target hospital contributes k_h=ceil(0.10 n_h) slots.

For each direction d, fit source-only models and score target patients:

- R_state: pre-repeat state, including age, early norepinephrine time, baseline lactate and the inherited strictly pre-L24 non-lactate laboratory/physiology summaries.
- R_process: R_state plus repeat availability, repeat age and repeat-age-missingness, but not the repeat numeric value.
- R_full: R_process plus the numeric repeat lactate and its square, with source-fold preprocessing and imputation.

Within each target hospital, rank by raw score descending, break ties by ascending patientunitstayid, and calculate D7 deaths among the first k_h patients. Let Y_m,d be the sum of D7 events in the model-m queue divided by the number of slots across target hospitals. The single primary estimand is the worst-direction incremental numeric-value queue yield:

  Delta_value = min_d [Y_full,d(0.10) - Y_process,d(0.10)].

The prespecified falsifiable hypothesis is Delta_value >= 0.03 deaths per equal-capacity slot (equivalently at least 3 additional source-defined D7 deaths per 100 slots), with the two-sided 95% copied-hospital interval lower endpoint required to be at least 0.03 in both directions. The margin is a decision-relevance anchor for this retrospective experiment, not a validated utility threshold, preventable-death estimate or treatment effect.

Guard estimands are reported but cannot rescue a failed primary:

- Delta_process = min_d[Y_process,d-Y_state,d], separating the observation process from the value.
- Delta_full-random = min_d[Y_full,d-Y_random,d], where random queues are deterministic SHA-256-sorted capacity-matched queues; this tests absolute informativeness.
- Delta_full-local = min_d[Y_full,d-Y_local,d], where the local model is target-stratum, held-out-hospital historical benchmarking only; this tests cross-practice transport, not a deployable policy.
- Dinf versions are horizon audits only; D7 remains the sole primary outcome.

Supportive means the lower endpoint of Delta_value is >=0.03 in both directions, all finite-score/cardinality/gate requirements pass, and no direction-specific contradiction or deletion instability occurs. Adverse means the upper endpoint of Delta_value is <0.03; this specifically falsifies the material numeric-value queue hypothesis. Inconclusive means the interval crosses 0.03, only one direction passes, sparsity or deletion instability prevents a stable joint interpretation, or the process/random/local guards disagree in a way that makes the value claim non-robust. Not-computable is reserved for provenance/schema mismatch, cohort leakage, nonfinite fitting/scoring, incorrect queue arithmetic, altered frozen definitions or fewer than 1,900 finite joint resamples. A supportive process or random comparison with an adverse value comparison means the observation process is informative but the numeric result did not add the prespecified material value.
