# Fold-aware null repair for the frozen UKB localization experiment

## Purpose and lineage

This is a targeted substantive repair of `[prior hypothesis]`, with executed ancestor `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen population, temporal boundaries, target, Z, M0/Mc definitions, primary stratum interaction H, capacity estimands and quotas, 4,000-draw conditional bootstrap, margins, privacy boundary, and evidence limits. Only the falsification procedure and its interpretation are repaired; the observed-data analysis is unchanged.

## Defect requiring repair

The proposed 200 outcome-label permutations keep the original outcome-stratified fold assignments fixed. Those folds were constructed from the observed labels, so the placebo labels are not exchangeable with respect to the fold-construction mechanism. Moreover, under a permuted-label null, Mc has an extra fitted parameter and its finite-sample held-out log loss is not generally centered at exactly zero relative to M0. Therefore requiring every placebo statistic's 98% interval to contain zero, and treating zero as the null center for Delta_LL, H, and capacity differences, is not a valid universal decision rule. A failure of that zero-centering requirement could be caused by ordinary nested-model optimism or the fixed fold/label dependence rather than outcome-linked signal.

## Repaired, explicit falsification

For each b=1,...,200, use seed 520242 to generate labels independently permuted **within the frozen baseline strata**. For that permuted label vector, reconstruct deterministic outcome-stratified round-robin five-fold assignments using the identical fold algorithm and repeat seeds used in the observed analysis. The observed-data fold assignments remain frozen and are not changed. Refit M0 and Mc in each of the ten repeats and five newly generated placebo folds, with identical training-only knots/scaling and logistic settings. Recompute all-cohort and stratum log-loss increments, H, global-capacity and within-stratum capacity differences. Preserve the 200-refit requirement; do not substitute prediction-only permutation or orientation reversal.

The placebo is a conditional null diagnostic for outcome-linked incremental information given the observed stratum membership and predictor distribution. Its null reference is the empirical distribution of each statistic, not the point value zero. Report the empirical mean/median and 1st/99th percentiles, but do not require the interval to contain zero. The falsification gate passes only when (a) all 200 permutations complete with finite fits, coverage, and exact capacity arithmetic; (b) the observed primary H and all-cohort Delta_LL are more favorable than the prespecified 98th-favorable-tail cutoff of their corresponding empirical null distributions (with randomized-plus-one tail fractions reported); and (c) no capacity placebo distribution exhibits a reproducible directional anomaly relative to its own empirical null, assessed descriptively rather than by an unjustified zero-center requirement. If the observed statistic is not more extreme than its empirical null, or any fit/integrity gate fails, localization is uncertified. This diagnostic cannot upgrade a failed global capacity gate.

A stricter optional check may compare each placebo statistic with its own null median (or with a nested-model null generated by the same refitting process), but it must not reinterpret a nonzero null mean as evidence against the observed result. Permutation evidence remains conditional on complete repeat attenders and does not establish clinical utility, CKD, mechanism, causality, or transportability.

## Computational feasibility and stop rule

The repaired placebo entails 20,000 logistic fits (200 permutations x 10 repeats x 2 models x 5 folds), each on at most 16,372 participants and 14 non-intercept columns, plus preprocessing and ranking. This is CPU-feasible in principle but must be demonstrated under a managed job with declared inputs, outputs, at least 8 CPUs, sufficient memory, and a timeout materially above the ordinary 600-second default. A pilot should time a fixed 10-permutation block before committing to all 200. The pilot is feasibility-only and cannot be used to alter the 200 count or decision rule. If the full run cannot complete within declared resources, the falsification gate is uncertified and no localized/diffuse support may be reported.
