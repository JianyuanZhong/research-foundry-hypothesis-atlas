# Baseline kidney-function discordance and future inpatient AKI in UK Biobank

## Question and hypothesis

The clinically important unresolved question is whether a person whose creatinine-based eGFR appears preserved but whose cystatin-C-based eGFR is low has a materially higher future risk of acute kidney injury (AKI), particularly when grip strength is low. This would matter because routine creatinine-based assessment could miss people for whom closer medication review, avoidance of nephrotoxins, or cystatin-C confirmation is reasonable. The prespecified hypothesis is:

> Among UK Biobank participants with baseline eGFRcr >=60 ml/min/1.73 m2, the subgroup with eGFRcys <60 has higher 5-year cumulative incidence of a first inpatient-coded AKI (ICD-10 N17.x) than participants with both eGFRcr and eGFRcys >=60, and this contrast is larger among participants with low baseline grip strength.

This is an association/decision-support hypothesis, not a claim that cystatin C is a causal mediator or that a test changes outcomes.

## Evidence and advance

The available critical-illness paper (Haines et al., CJASN 2023, frozen as `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt` and its PDF) studied 38 mechanically ventilated ICU patients with serial creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance and rectus-femoris ultrasound from January 2019 to August 2020. It supports the strongest narrower claim that creatinine-based eGFR can overestimate measured kidney function during prolonged critical illness with muscle loss. It does not establish prospective AKI prediction in community-dwelling adults, the value of a baseline discordance threshold, or effect modification by grip strength. The proposed study tests that missing longitudinal claim in a much larger cohort, with a simple clinical baseline and a prespecified sensitivity model.

The seed's limitation is retained: inflammation and other non-GFR determinants can affect cystatin C, and grip is not a direct measure of muscle mass.

## Population, time zero and exclusions

Use the initial UK Biobank assessment only (field instance `0.0`; approximately 2006-01-01 through 2010-12-31). Include participants with nonmissing baseline age, sex, assessment date, creatinine, cystatin C, and at least one valid baseline grip measurement. Time zero is the date in `53-0.0`, not the file row order. Exclude participants with an observed inpatient N17.x, N18.x or N19.x diagnosis dated on or before time zero, and exclude impossible/nonpositive lab values after the field-level missing-code policy is verified. Do not assert that these exclusions remove outpatient disease or disease before the available hospital-record window.

Follow participants from time zero until the first qualifying outcome, death, last available outcome date, or 2023-03-31, whichever comes first. The catalog's UKB convention says `41280` dates are first inpatient diagnosis dates (episode start or, if absent, admission date), not disease onset. A diagnosis on the time-zero date is excluded; dates after time zero are eligible. Death is a competing event for AKI and CKD, using the earliest nonmissing `40000-0.0`/`40000-1.0` date after time zero.

## Exposure construction and estimand

Convert creatinine from umol/L to mg/dL and calculate 2021 CKD-EPI eGFRcr and eGFRcys using age and the sex-specific constants; retain the exact formula and unit check in the run manifest. Primary exposure is the binary discordance contrast:

* discordant-low: eGFRcr >=60 and eGFRcys <60;
* concordant-preserved reference: eGFRcr >=60 and eGFRcys >=60.

Participants with eGFRcr <60 are described and analyzed in a separate secondary category, not silently pooled into the primary contrast. Grip is the maximum valid of left/right baseline readings, standardized within sex; low grip is below the sex-specific 20th percentile calculated once in the eligible baseline cohort. The primary estimand is the adjusted 5-year cumulative-incidence difference for first N17.x inpatient AKI between discordant-low and concordant-preserved participants, reported separately for low and not-low grip and as an interaction contrast. Report risk differences, risk ratios, cause-specific hazard ratios and 95% confidence intervals; use nonparametric bootstrap clustered by participant for cumulative-incidence uncertainty.

## Analysis and baselines

First produce a flow table, missingness table, exposure-cell counts, follow-up, outcome counts and covariate balance. The primary analysis is a cause-specific Cox model for AKI with death as a competing event and restricted cubic splines for age, plus sex, BMI, systolic/diastolic BP, HbA1c and self-reported diabetes. The primary model includes discordance, low grip and their interaction; do not adjust for post-index measurements or diagnoses. Report Fine-Gray/subdistribution results only as a sensitivity analysis, not as a replacement estimand.

The clinically relevant baseline is the same model with eGFRcr (continuous/spline) but without eGFRcys/discordance. Compare it with a model adding eGFRcys and the prespecified interaction. Assess calibration at 5 years, Brier score and optimism by bootstrap. A bounded learning alternative (elastic-net Cox, with hyperparameters selected only inside training folds and a fixed eid-hash 70/15/15 train/tune/test split) may use the same baseline variables; it is secondary and cannot convert a small predictive gain into clinical utility. No GPU or foundation-model training is needed.

Prespecified sensitivity analyses are: (1) exclude AKI/CKD diagnoses in the first 365 days to reduce occult pre-index illness/reverse causation; (2) use eGFRcys-eGFRcr continuously and test the stated threshold only as primary; (3) repeat-assessment participants using `53-1.0`, `46/47-1.0`, `30700/30720/30750-1.0`, with time zero at the repeat date; (4) stratify by sex and age; (5) complete-case versus multiple imputation performed within folds; and (6) negative-control inpatient outcome defined in advance from an unrelated ICD-10 chapter (for example traumatic injury S00-T14) to expose broad frailty/coding artefacts. Never change the outcome code list after seeing results.

## Falsification and interpretation

Support requires a positive, clinically nontrivial discordant-low contrast in the direction above, a prespecified interaction estimate reported with uncertainty, and consistent direction after the 365-day washout and repeat-assessment sensitivity. Incremental discrimination alone is not supportive. An adverse result is a clearly opposite association, a null interaction with adequate event support, or a positive association that disappears under the washout/negative-control checks; it would argue against the proposed hidden-risk interpretation. An association only in the negative-control outcome falsifies a kidney-specific interpretation.

Results are inconclusive if units/missing-code interpretation cannot be verified, there are fewer than 100 primary AKI events overall, fewer than 10 primary events in either exposure-by-grip cell, or confidence intervals remain too wide to distinguish clinically relevant benefit from harm. Do not replace an underpowered AKI analysis with an easier CKD question. CKD N18.x cumulative incidence, all-cause death, and eGFR decline are secondary descriptive outcomes.

Computation can establish field availability, cohort counts, temporal ordering, code-based event associations, model calibration, uncertainty, and whether the results meet these prespecified criteria. It cannot establish AKI stage, outpatient events, measured GFR, cystatin-C specificity, causal effect, or that cystatin-C testing changes medication safety or patient outcomes. Those claims require linked laboratory trajectories, medication data, adjudicated AKI and CKD, inflammation and muscle-mass measures, clinical expert review, and prospective external validation.

## Demonstration dispositions

* The natural-history transformer paper (`references/research-ambition/natural-history/article.readable.txt`, with XML/PDF also available) motivates explicit disease-time ordering and competing morbidity. A concrete local adaptation is the dated ICD-10 cause-specific/competing-risk analysis above; reproducing its generative model is unnecessary and local raw data do not support its full external-validation claim.
* The cancer demonstration's main article is unavailable; only bibliographic metadata and its supplement were inspected (`references/research-ambition/cancer/supplement.readable.txt`). Its multimodal routine-EHR/X-ray and treatment-response setting is not pursued because UKB has no raw images, verified narrative extraction, staging, or treatment records in this snapshot.
* The Bayesian longitudinal EHR/genetic paper (`references/research-ambition/bayesian/article.readable.txt`, plus supplement/reporting summary) supports the value of explicit longitudinal likelihoods and calibration. A concrete non-reproduction here is the transparent cause-specific model and optional bounded elastic-net sensitivity; UKB's `genomics` table is explicitly derived metadata rather than a variant/PRS bundle, so no genetic discovery claim is made.
