# Working proposal (episode 18): Is the RA Mpos-macrophage -> FLS program a required intra-coculture MerTK-linked step beyond Mpos abundance?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis]
(valid, gen 5, board leader mu 32.0; itself the repair of [prior hypothesis], seed D3-M2).
Seeds engaged: D3-M2 (directional macrophage->FLS crosstalk), benchmarked by D3-M1 (composition vs
within-cell state) and D3-M3 (persistence vs remission).
Status: hypothesis-stage, data-bound, with an EXECUTED bounded screening ([research job]).
NO confirmatory/causal result is claimed. The parent's genome-wide E-MTAB-8316 screen is cited only as prior
exploratory evidence, not re-run.

## 1. Unresolved question
Alivernini 2020 [K1] associates a MerTK+CD206+ (Mpos) synovial macrophage state with drug-free RA remission,
but it is cross-sectional and does not show the state DRIVES other joint cells. The parent branch asked
whether the macrophage->FLS program requires MerTK signalling. It left two blockers: (a) with only 6
inhibitor-vs-none stand-alone co-culture pairs, a genuine signalling requirement cannot be separated from
FLS-donor/composition structure; (b) the ligand/receptor side (GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3) is not measured by the
targeted E-MTAB-8873 panel, so the mechanism cannot be resolved by co-expression. This episode adds, within
one dataset, a composition dose gradient AND matched-composition inhibitor arms on the same FLS readout,
which is exactly what the stand-alone 6-pair design lacked.

Unresolved, falsifiable claim:
H1 (intra-coculture MerTK-linked step): at matched Mpos composition, pharmacological MERTK blockade
   (UNC106238) still shifts the co-cultured FLS transcriptome in a direction that does NOT simply reproduce
   the Mpos-abundance (composition) effect.
H0 (abundance-only rival): the FLS program tracks only how many Mpos macrophages are present; the MERTK
   inhibitor arm adds nothing beyond the composition dose (its effect vector is collinear with, or explained
   by, the composition effect vector).

## 2. Clinical importance
If H1 holds, engaging MERTK in the resolution-state macrophage is an effector node shaping the fibroblast
compartment -- supporting MerTK/resolution-directed strategies and giving a mechanism for why
remission-associated Mpos cells predict drug-free remission. If H0 holds, the remission association is a
composition marker without an independent intra-coculture signalling step; this argues against MerTK-directed
monotherapy and redirects toward macrophage-composition strategies. Either resolution -- including a bounded
inconclusive result -- changes the next experiment. This is a mechanism-discrimination question, not another
expression plot.

## 3. Binding to the strongest rival and the discriminating observation
- Reverse-direction rival [K2]: FLS also provide instructive niche cues to STM precursors, so an FLS
  response to Mpos co-culture may reflect a fibroblast-set niche rather than a macrophage->FLS signal.
- Interpretation bound [K3]: MERTK loss-of-function is not cleanly separable from paralog (AXL/TYRO3) and
  MERTK-independent effects; a MERTK-inhibitor FLS response is therefore a perturbation-sensitivity readout,
  not proof of a MERTK-specific causal step.
- Discriminating observation: the angle between the composition effect vector (Mpos vs Mneg) and the
  matched-composition inhibitor effect vector (Mpos+inhibitor vs Mpos). Collinear vectors => H0
  (abundance/identity contributes no separate step). Roughly orthogonal, reproducible vectors => H1.

## 5. EXECUTED bounded screening (this episode; exploratory, NOT confirmatory)
Managed job [research job] (CPU, succeeded) -> work/lead/screen_8873.json.
Method: log2(CPM+1) per sample; composition effect = mean(Mpos) - mean(Mneg); inhibitor effect =
mean(Mpos+inhibitor) - mean(Mpos none); Pearson/Spearman angle between the two 445-feature vectors.
RESULT (computed): composition-vs-inhibitor Pearson r = -0.140, Spearman rho = -0.111; MERTK log2CPM mean
Mpos 4.571 vs Mneg 4.793; panel as verified above.
READING (bounded): the two effect vectors are near-orthogonal, i.e. the MERTK-inhibitor arm is NOT collinear
with the Mpos-abundance axis -- consistent with an intra-coculture contribution beyond composition (a weak
H1-consistent signal). LIMITATIONS: this is a crude unpaired mean contrast; the 2 `preInhibitor` labels may
reflect different timing; labels/timing must be reconciled before inference; n is tiny. It does NOT
establish a MERTK-specific causal step and does NOT replace the donor-aware model below.

## 6. Design (baseline vs substantive alternative, same question)
Baseline (simple): per-gene log2(CPM+1) ~ Mpos fraction f (pooled dose-response) with a paired sign test of
the inhibitor effect; null by sign-flip permutation within matched FLS/macrophage donor sets.
Substantive alternative (what the baseline loses): donor-aware repeated-measures linear mixed model with FLS
donor and macrophage donor grouping factors, fixed effects {f (composition), inhibitor, LPS axis}, plus a
low-rank factor model that absorbs donor/composition latent structure; a "program" is credited only if it
survives removal of the dominant donor/composition factors. This separates a within-condition signalling
contribution from donor/identity variance that a pooled dose-response cannot.

## 7. Falsification and outcome meanings
- Supportive (H1): inhibitor effect survives donor-aware model + low-rank residualization and is
  near-orthogonal to the composition vector, consistent across matched sets.
- Adverse (H0): inhibitor effect is collinear with / fully attenuated by the composition-dose vector.
- Inconclusive: weak but non-zero signal swamped by donor/composition variance, or too few matched sets.
Falsifier: if the inhibitor effect is explained by the composition vector (residual ~0) under the
donor-aware model, H1 is falsified for this readout.
