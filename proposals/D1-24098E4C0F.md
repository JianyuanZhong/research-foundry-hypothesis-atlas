# A transport-gated, rare-route audit of pulmonary-nodule admission routing

## Decision bottleneck and substantive repair

The inherited clinical decision is whether a locked admission router should proceed to a **prospective silent validation**, never whether it should autonomously manage follow-up. The frozen population, all-opportunity denominator, discharge chronology, exhaustive unit states, worst-unit admission routing, split-view audit, and noncausal evidence ceiling are unchanged.

The assessed parent repaired incorporation and differential-verification bias. [prior hypothesis] then addressed pooled masking with service and coarse-era domains. That is a real threat, but its late possible-calendar holdout and leave-one-domain-out presentation can be mistaken for evidence of prospective transport even though no MIMIC table records actual report viewing, draft/edit/sign/transmission, responsibility, scheduling, or consecutive future workflow. A second residual threat is therefore more consequential for the clinical decision: **a retrospective audit can pass, and a domain can appear stable, while the nomination itself silently upgrades storage-text reproducibility into prospective workflow validity**. Fixed workload and rare-route uncertainty amplify this error: a 900-admission audit may have no informative `AUTO` denominator or may measure reviewer burden rather than deployment burden.

This child keeps the domain-masking repair, but converts it from a transport claim into a conservative **transport-readiness firewall**. It makes three decisions distinct and computable:

1. `ROUTE_AUDIT_PASS`: pooled and domain-specific retrospective text-process gates pass under adverse finite-population uncertainty.
2. `PROTOCOL_READY`: the exact protocol, workload envelope, rare-route evidence, and prospective data specification are sufficient to register a silent study.
3. `SILENT_NOMINATION`: never emitted by MIMIC alone. It requires a new prospective dataset satisfying the predeclared transport fields and independent review. A MIMIC pass can at most produce `PROTOCOL_READY`.

The main hypothesis is therefore not that MIMIC proves transport. It is that the sealed process can identify whether retrospective evidence is strong enough to justify collecting the missing prospective evidence without confusing that prerequisite with validation. A domain-specific failure, rare-route insufficiency, or workload uncertainty blocks the corresponding protocol rather than being rescued by pooling.

This is not a cosmetic duplicate of the domain child: it removes the unsupported interpretation of `T_LATE`/leave-one-domain-out as transport evidence, adds an explicit route-specific rare-event assurance decision, and replaces mean audit-time feasibility with a predeclared prospective workload envelope and missing-workflow-data gate.

## Evidence-supported and untested claims

**Strongest claim supported before this experiment.** The local MIMIC snapshot contains admission-linked reports, addenda, discharge artifacts, service paths, ICU exposure, and storage timestamps sufficient to construct a reproducible retrospective text-defined routing audit. It does not contain validated evidence of actual report viewing, author responsibility, editing, transmission, scheduling, outside-plan communication, follow-up completion, or patient benefit.

**Untested claim.** That a route passing a retrospective text audit will remain sufficiently reproducible, uncontaminated, and operationally feasible when applied prospectively to consecutive discharges in a live workflow.

**Falsifiable retrospective hypothesis.** In the frozen `N_A`, split-view labels and an untouched all-route audit will yield domain-specific and pooled route estimates whose adverse simultaneous limits meet the preapproved gates, with enough route-positive admissions to bound rare unsafe `AUTO` contamination and enough measured component-time data to define a conservative workload envelope. If any of these conditions fails, the corresponding route is not protocol-ready.

**Transport hypothesis deliberately not tested by MIMIC.** No MIMIC result may reject the possibility of prospective transport failure. The prospective transport hypothesis is registered, not accepted: in a future consecutive-discharge dataset, the same frozen route will meet the prespecified fields, domain support, route-specific purity/capture, and workload gates. It requires another study.

## Frozen population, chronology, states, and split-view audit

Before any route label, stream all radiology and detail rows and freeze code, lexical/exam dictionary, included/excluded IDs, and a probability sample of retrieval negatives. Candidate reports are exact admission-linked rows with `charttime in [admittime-12h,D]` containing locked pulmonary/nodule/opacity, follow-up/recommendation, 3/6/12-month, chest/thoracic CT, or exam-code/name signals. Two eligibility physicians receive reciprocal radiology and permitted admission/death/disposition fields with pulmonary target/action/timing/recipient/order/preference spans redacted. Disagreement is `U_R`; third opinions do not erase it. Seal the complete frame before sampling.

`N_A` is exactly one first eligible admission per subject, sorted by `admittime` then `hadm_id`: adult, incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular opacity, and unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months. Exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional/optional plans, explicit no follow-up, in-hospital death, locked hospice/comfort-only transition, and recommendations first introduced after `D`. Never infer size, smoking, risk, appropriateness, life expectancy, or missing context. Later disagreement cannot redefine `N_A`.

Reciprocal report/addendum links must agree in both directions and be subject/admission consistent. Every operative component has `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`; one-way, contradictory, missing, unreadable, or unresolved links remain `U_R`. Require exactly one canonical `DS` row per `N_A` admission; missing text/link/storetime remains `U_D`; `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`. Preserve exhaustive `Y={F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}`, `V`, `H`, eventual `Z`, and every `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A` cell. For each unit retain `R_store,u`, `DS_store`, and `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u`; verify `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`. Only unit-specific `G_u` controls opportunity, and every active unresolved unit must have `G_u>=24h` for content routing.

A unit is the smallest `(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity)` tuple. Co-mentioned nodules sharing action/window are one unit; different actions/windows are separate; reciprocal restatements are deduplicated; unresolved splitting/coreference is `U_BOUNDARY`. Units are nested, never sampled or weighted independently. Whole-admission precedence remains: any `U_DEFER` gives `A_DEFER`; otherwise any `U_TIMING` gives `A_TIMING`; all resolved/closed gives `A_NOACTION`; `A_AUTO` requires every open unit to be wholly omitted with ample clock and all other units reproducibly resolved/closed; incomplete/conflicting/mixed states give `A_RECON`, with `A_MIXED` nested in `RECON`.

Preserve the parent’s two independent index lanes and two independent untouched audit lanes (`R-I,K-I,S-I` and `R-A,K-A,S-A`), separate eligibility/redaction/component/safety roles, role-specific pseudonyms, packet hashes, append-only seals, locked cross-view tuple/offset matching, full compatible cross-products, and an all-sample minority-unit safety auditor. Every probability-selected admission receives the same audit regardless of apparent route; audit never sees index labels, routes, strata, weights, or expected direction. Redaction leaks, missing labels, boundary uncertainty, and adverse-compatible disagreements remain unsafe/unknown. No consensus or favorable matching controls a gate.

## Outcome-blind sampling, domains, and fixed workload

Retain outcome-blind sentinel, time-clock, zero, partial, and complete strata. Census sentinels. For `S_ZERO`, use the larger of 300 or the finite-population sample size for worst-case 0.5 and 4-point precision; for other noncensus strata use the larger of 120 or the analogous 7-point formula

`n_h=ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25/{e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`.

The total selected admissions, including the sentinel census, is fixed before labels and may not exceed 900. No top-ups, replacements, adaptive stopping, route-based allocation, or response-based selection are permitted. If the required census/formula sample exceeds 900, the result is feasibility failure.

To retain the anti-masking benefit without pretending that a balanced sample is a future workflow sample, cross-classify each noncensus stratum by outcome-blind domain `(T,S)`. `T` uses only a conservative possible-calendar interval from `anchor_year_group`: if the catalog interval is `[a,b]` and `q=year(admittime)-anchor_year`, set `[a+q-1,b+q+1]`; assign `T_EARLY` if upper `<=2013`, `T_LATE` if lower `>=2017`, `T_BRIDGE` otherwise, and malformed/missing to `T_UNKNOWN`. Raw shifted years, exact calendar trends, and seasonality are prohibited. `S` is terminal pre-`D` `curr_service` from `hosp/services`, with timestamp ties, missing rows/codes, unseen codes, and contradictory times assigned `S_OTHER_UNKNOWN`; frozen groups are `MED/CMED/NMED`, procedure/surgical codes, `OBS`, `PSYCH`, `OMED`, and other/unknown. Report exact codes. Secondary domains are service-row count, path change, and pre-`D` ICU exposure, all with explicit unknowns.

Within each occupied `(h,T,S)` cell allocate the inherited `n_h` before labels, with positive inclusion probability wherever feasible, deterministic integer optimization, proportional allocation after minimum domain targets, and lexicographic tie-breaking. Publish infeasible target cells and mark their domain claims unsupported. For cell `c`, `pi_i=n_hc/N_hc>0`, `w_i=1/pi_i`, and within-cell `pi_ij=n_hc(n_hc-1)/(N_hc(N_hc-1))`; certainty cells have `pi=1`. A cell with one sampled admission has no design variance estimate and cannot pass a domain gate. Preserve admission-level sampling and all unit observations together.

This fixed 900 ceiling is itself a falsification target. Report actual eligibility/retrieval/redaction/component/audit/safety reviews, units, minutes, physician-hours, nonresponse, maximum weight, normalized weight share, Kish ESS, and weighted hours per 100 `N_A`. Separate (a) retrospective audit workload, (b) expected prospective implementation workload, which is **not observed**, and (c) unmeasured workflow work. MIMIC can bound only (a). Any prospective workload budget is a preregistered requirement, not an observed MIMIC outcome.

## Estimands and rare-route assurance

For `r in {AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,MIXED}`, estimate finite-population burdens `B_r=100T_r/N_A` with HT totals and domain-conditional Hájek ratios. Retain `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, `leak_AUTO`, route agreement, chronology, all `Y/V/H/Z/J_A`, and domain-specific versions and complements. `C_AUTO` is unsafe-event total over admissions with either initial index `AUTO`; an unsafe event includes any non-AUTO index/audit-compatible route, safety-auditor minority finding, missing audit, redaction/boundary failure, or adverse unknown. Report numerator and denominator, never only a percentage.

The rare-route repair is an explicit **assurance status**, not a relaxed threshold. For every route and domain, publish the exact finite-population upper bound for unsafe contamination under zero, one, and observed events, the route-denominator ESS, maximum weight, and the smallest contamination margin that the fixed sample can exclude. A route is `RARE_UNRESOLVED` and cannot be `PROTOCOL_READY` if it has fewer than 30 sampled route-positive/reference-positive admissions as applicable, fewer than 10 non-sentinel events, ESS below 50 (or inherited pooled thresholds), zero denominator, or an adverse exact bound crossing its margin. Zero events never imply zero risk. A domain represented only by certainty admissions may be reported descriptively but cannot be generalized as stable unless fully censused. The same rule applies to `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER`; a pooled route cannot borrow events from a different domain.

Use stratified-cell HT variance, Taylor-linearized ratio variance, at least 20,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates with certainty cells fixed, and exact hypergeometric/multivariate-hypergeometric inversion for rare totals and `{unsafe AUTO,safe AUTO,non-AUTO}`. One max-statistic family covers pooled routes, primary temporal/service domains, drift, workload, and route-specific confirmation/capture. For every incomplete selected admission, optimize all compatible states; missing audit for index `AUTO` is unsafe in the upper bound, unknown domain membership enters every compatible adverse endpoint, and complete-case/model-assisted estimates are diagnostics only. No reviewer clustering, patient duplication, or unweighted analysis may excuse a denominator failure.

## Decision gates and transport firewall

Governance must approve margins and a prospective physician-hour budget before labels. Retain pooled defaults `tau_AUTO=.05`, `rho=.90`, `AC1=.70`, `sigma_RECON=.95`, `pi_RECON=.80`, `pi_TIMING=.95`, `sigma_DEFER=.95`, inherited denominator/retrieval/redaction/ESS/precision gates, and domain anti-masking gates: in every adequately supported temporal band and service domain, adverse simultaneous `C_AUTO<=.05`, `leak_AUTO<=.02`, required confirmation/capture gates, no unresolved minority unit compatible with `AUTO`, and no absolute contamination drift above `.02`; pooled passing cannot rescue a domain failure. Unsupported domains are inconclusive, not stable. Exact service-code and unknown-domain diagnostics cannot rescue primary gates.

Add the following non-overlapping statuses.

* `ROUTE_AUDIT_PASS` requires the unchanged pooled gates plus every supported domain gate, fixed-sample compliance, rare-route assurance, intact split-view/audit seals, and adverse simultaneous uncertainty.
* `PROTOCOL_READY` additionally requires: (i) no claim that MIMIC establishes prospective or external transport; (ii) an immutable prospective data dictionary and consecutive-admission sampling plan; (iii) a transport packet specifying report finalization/addendum, report availability/viewing, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, responsibility, every unit, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing-risk context, final transmitted artifact, and actual calendar/workflow domain fields; (iv) a prespecified prospective route-positive accrual requirement of at least 100 route-positive admissions and 30 in each claimed route, or an explicitly approved accrual stop; (v) a prospective independent expert audit blinded to silent routes and later outcomes; and (vi) a locked prospective workload budget and implementation-time measurement plan.
* `SILENT_NOMINATION` is **not an output available from this MIMIC experiment**. It can be issued only after the prospective packet is collected and independently audited under the frozen protocol. If governance uses “nomination” to mean permission to register that prospective study, the permitted wording is `PROTOCOL_READY_FOR_PROSPECTIVE_COLLECTION`, never “validated,” “safe,” “transportable,” or “ready for automation.”

A service-restricted future study may claim only domains whose prospective support and gates pass; all unsupported/unknown workflows remain `DEFER`. A late possible-calendar MIMIC band and leave-one-domain-out complement are sensitivity/stress analyses for heterogeneity, not temporal holdout validation and not transport evidence.

## Interpretation and advance

Supportive MIMIC results mean only `PROTOCOL_READY_FOR_PROSPECTIVE_COLLECTION`: retrospective route definitions are reproducible and domain-stressed, rare-route uncertainty is not hidden, and the missing prospective measurements are specified. Adverse results block the affected route or workflow and may support reconciliation-only prospective study. Inconclusive results include sparse route/domain support, positive rare-event bounds, excessive unknowns/nonresponse, domain failure, workload-budget uncertainty, retrieval undercoverage, broken seals, or absent prospective fields. A pooled pass is never enough.

The clinical advance is a safer decision boundary. [prior hypothesis] made pooled masking visible; this child prevents the more damaging interpretive leap that an internally stable retrospective text process is already prospectively transportable. It also treats a rare `AUTO` denominator and fixed 900-review budget as inability to establish a safety prerequisite, rather than allowing a favorable pooled average or a low audit-time mean to license silent deployment. The remaining uncertainty is explicit: MIMIC cannot observe live workflow, exact real calendar time, external sites, clinical truth, or benefit; service codes and storage clocks are proxies; independent experts may share error; and prospective transport remains an untested hypothesis requiring new data.
