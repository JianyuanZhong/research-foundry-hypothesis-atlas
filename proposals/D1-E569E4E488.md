# Proposal: reassessment-anchored, capture-adjusted post-TACE recorded care

## Parent, objective, and substantive repair

This is a substantive child of assessed valid parent `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s HCC snapshot, 24-hour procedure-episode audit, TACE/alternate/mixed/non-liver pathway ontology, day-45 landmark, days 46–365 horizon, administrative end-of-local-observation state, patient-level temporal split, capture-adjusted transparent baseline, irregular-time alternative, uncertainty, and strict limits on clinical interpretation.

The strongest remaining clinical-identification problem is not the model family or the broad procedure vocabulary. Even a pathway-coherent TACE-containing episode does not identify why the action was recorded. It may be a planned/scheduled local intervention, a response to disease assessment, a technical/bundled code, or care selected by who remained observable. The source has no validated indication, eligibility, intent, radiologic response, mortality, or outside-care field. The parent’s administrative observation stress test identifies disappearance, but its primary TACE state still treats all later recorded TACE episodes as one clinical pathway.

The repair is a two-part recorded-care ontology. Every day-46–365 therapeutic episode receives a frozen, pre-event observation-context label: whether a dateable local reassessment opportunity occurred before the episode. The primary endpoint is the first TACE-containing episode preceded by that opportunity; TACE without such an opportunity, alternate/mixed pathways, non-liver capture, and administrative end-of-local-observation remain explicit competing states. The parent’s unqualified first TACE-containing episode remains a locked secondary/sensitivity estimand. This asks a narrower and falsifiable question about the local care process while avoiding the unsupported claim that “reassessment-anchored TACE” means recurrence, progression, response failure, or appropriate treatment.

A same-encounter HCC-diagnosis restriction was audited as a possible repair and rejected as non-discriminating: the read-only scan found 105,044 HCC-diagnosis encounter keys among 105,044 encounter rows, and all 106,963 TACE-lexicon rows matched such a key. It therefore cannot identify clinical indication in this snapshot.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

The strongest supported claim is operational. The HCC source contains linkable dated encounters, procedures, laboratories, and examinations. The parent’s procedure audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 7,781 raw procedure names, and 106,963 rows matching the frozen TACE lexicon. Encounters contain local timestamps; labs and examinations contain their own timestamps; diagnoses have no timestamp and inherit encounter time. These are availability and coding facts, not evidence of clinical intent or disease state.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among adults with an HCC diagnosis and a first recorded TACE-like episode who have no liver-directed therapeutic episode through day 45, does an early AFP/hepatic trajectory add reproducible information for a later TACE-containing episode that follows a dateable local reassessment opportunity, beyond pre-index history and measured local-observation features, and is that increment more specific to this reassessment-anchored pathway than to TACE without such an opportunity or to a non-liver capture control?

Primary hypothesis, frozen before locked evaluation:

> On the locked temporal test, B2 (capture-adjusted baseline plus the prespecified AFP/hepatic trajectory) will reduce the alert fraction versus B0 by at least 10 absolute percentage points at 90% sensitivity for the first reassessment-anchored TACE episode during days 46–365, with a 95% patient-bootstrap interval excluding no reduction. The gain will remain directionally favorable in the continued-local-observation stratum, and the B2-versus-B0 gain for reassessment-anchored TACE will exceed the corresponding gain for TACE without a prior local reassessment opportunity by at least 5 percentage points. There will be no comparable gain for the non-liver capture-control outcome or the pre-index placebo.

This is a prognostic hypothesis about a reproducible local recorded-care sequence. It is not a treatment-effect, response, recurrence, progression, survival, or recommendation hypothesis. The competing outcome contrast is a falsification of generic procedure/capture prediction, not proof of disease specificity.

## Population, index, landmark, and temporal boundaries

Use one index per patient, preserving the parent’s cohort unless a declared data-quality failure prevents it.

- Join child tables to `encounters` only after aggregating each child table to (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). Never form a many-to-many join.
- Group qualifying procedure rows within patient into an episode when parsed `开始时间` values are within 24 hours. Retain every raw name, source, start/end time, encounter key, row count, and all family hits. Rows with missing `开始时间` cannot define an episode time and are reported separately.
- The index is the earliest episode containing a TACE-like row: case-insensitive literal `TACE`, a procedure name containing `动脉化疗栓塞`, or a name containing both `肝动脉` and `栓塞`. Retain composite index episodes and flag them; do not silently reinterpret their bundles.
- Require age >=18 and nonmissing `性别` in the joined index encounter.
- Require a diagnosis name containing literal `肝细胞癌` on a joined encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after the index. Diagnosis time is inherited from `就诊时间`; the diagnosis table has no temporal column. This is a cohort criterion, not evidence of TACE indication.
- Include index dates through 2025-01-01, so the nominal 365-day horizon ends within the supplied local snapshot.
- Day-45 eligibility requires no post-index episode after the first 24 hours through day 45 containing a liver-directed therapeutic family. Report early TACE, alternate, and mixed episodes rather than silently excluding them as missing.
- The landmark is day 45 after index; all prediction features stop at day 45. The primary follow-up is days 46–365.

The population estimand is the first-event cumulative incidence of a reassessment-anchored TACE-containing episode by each day through 365 among day-45-eligible patients, with the following mutually exclusive states: reassessment_anchored_TACE, TACE_without_prior_reassessment, alternate_liver_directed, mixed_liver_therapeutic, non_liver_procedure, and end_of_local_observation. Patients with no state remain observed_no_action through day 365 under the declared local-observation rule. The unqualified first TACE-containing episode is a secondary estimand with the parent’s definition.

## Frozen procedure and observation-context ontology

Use the parent’s frozen family rules and precedence:

- `TACE_like`: literal `TACE`, `动脉化疗栓塞`, or both `肝动脉` and `栓塞`.
- `resection_or_transplant`: liver-context `肝` near `切除`, `肝移植`, or `移植`, with the parent’s exclusions.
- `ablation`: liver-context `肝`, `肝肿瘤`, or `肝病损` near `消融`, `射频`, `微波`, or `冷冻`, excluding biopsy-only names.
- `radiotherapy`: liver-context terms near `放疗`, `放射`, `粒子`, `碘-125`, `伽马刀`, or `立体定向`.
- `other_liver_therapeutic`: only a term frozen from the pre-lock vocabulary audit; otherwise retain as unclassified.
- Technical/diagnostic-only terms such as biopsy, imaging guidance, angiography, drainage, and catheter placement are not therapeutic by themselves.
- Episode precedence is TACE_rechallenge, alternate_liver_directed, mixed_liver_therapeutic, non_liver_procedure, and unclassified_procedure exactly as in the parent. A TACE plus any alternate family is mixed.

For every therapeutic episode at time t in days 46–365, define a context flag using only records strictly before t and not the episode’s encounter:

`prior_local_reassessment_30d = 1` if, in days t-30 through t-1, there is at least one of:

1. a nonmissing encounter `就诊时间`, `入院时间`, or `出院时间` in `encounters`, with the contact timestamp defined as the maximum valid timestamp; or
2. a parseable lab row for one of the frozen assay-label families (AFP, albumin, total bilirubin, the observed coagulation-ratio assay, or platelets) at `检验时间`; or
3. a dated examination satisfying the parent’s frozen liver/abdominal imaging-name screen at `开始时间`.

Use the strict version requiring a dated liver-relevant lab or examination as the primary context label; use encounter-only and 14-day/30-day windows as locked sensitivities. “Reassessment” is shorthand for a dateable local-record opportunity, not a claim that a clinician reassessed tumor, response, or treatment need. The current episode’s encounter, procedure time, order, or same-day examination cannot create its own prior opportunity.

An episode is `reassessment_anchored_TACE` only when it is TACE_rechallenge and the strict flag is 1. A TACE_rechallenge with flag 0 is `TACE_without_prior_reassessment`. Alternate and mixed pathways retain their family states and receive the same context audit but are not relabeled as TACE. A non-liver procedure is a capture-control state. If exact event-time ties are unresolved, retain a tie state and run both allowed orderings; do not use the future event to manufacture context.

This context decomposition is a measurement-process construct. It does not establish a visit was clinically relevant, that a test was completed for HCC, or that a TACE was indicated.

## Observation and censoring strategy

Use the parent’s administrative end-of-local-observation rule. From joined encounter rows, define contact as the maximum valid `就诊时间`, `入院时间`, or `出院时间`. If no classified procedure occurs after day 45, end_of_local_observation is one day after the last valid contact after day 45 when within the horizon; if no such contact exists, use day 46; if last contact is day 365, no administrative event occurs within the horizon. Repeat using `就诊时间` alone because admission/discharge fields are missing in the audited source.

For the primary competing-risk analysis, treat end_of_local_observation as an administrative state, not death or clinical deterioration. Estimate censoring weights using only pre-event, day-45-known variables; truncate only at a prespecified percentile and publish overlap. The primary state decomposition includes the administrative state; a sensitivity censors at first unclassified episode or loss of local observation with IPCW. Report both the capture-inclusive and continued-local-observation analyses.

The continued-local-observation stratum is at least one dated contact in days 276–365 and no administrative end-of-local-observation before day 365. If it has too few anchored-TACE events for the declared interval, the result is inconclusive.

## Predictors and leakage control

All models use identical cohort, outcome ontology, context definition, landmark, split, and uncertainty procedure. Features are generated relative to index and frozen at day 45.

- AFP: last parseable numeric value in days -30 to -1 and first in days 7–45, using log1p, continuous change, the parent’s improvement indicator, pair availability, exact assay label, and missingness.
- Albumin, total bilirubin, the observed coagulation-ratio assay, and platelets: last pre-index and first early-post values, exact assay identity, continuous changes, and missingness. No cross-assay pooling, unit inference, ALBI/MELD construction, or unit-dependent threshold is allowed because `labs` has no separate unit column.
- Baseline history: age, sex, index year, department, index procedure family/composite status, pre-index procedure and diagnosis-history counts/indicators, and pre-index assay summaries/missingness.
- Capture features known by day 45: encounter counts/timing/departments, dated laboratory rows/days, assay availability, examination opportunity, non-liver procedure counts, unclassified counts, and (sensitivity only) dated order opportunities. An order is not completed care.
- The primary model excludes clinical-document and examination report text. Dated examination opportunity is a capture feature, not a response label.

No post-day-45 record, including the reassessment context attached to a future event, is a predictor. The future event’s context flag is computed only when defining the outcome. All vocabulary rules, assay dictionaries, parsing, imputation, weights, and thresholds are frozen before locked evaluation.

## Matched transparent baseline and substantive irregular-time alternative

B0 is a regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazard model for the six states, using baseline history and day-45 capture features. B2 is B0 plus the paired AFP/hepatic trajectory, continuous changes, exact assay identity, pair availability, and biomarker missingness. B0/B2 produce cause-specific risks, competing-risk cumulative incidence, calibrated predictions, and the 90% TACE-specific operating boundary.

M3 is a low-rank irregular-time joint state-space model with separate latent activity and hepatic-function trajectories, patient intercepts/slopes, assay-specific observation submodels, and observation intensity. It links the latent day-45 state and uncertainty to the same six-state hazards, including the anchored-vs-unanchored TACE split, and fits only the day-46–365 recorded event process. It cannot use future tests to smooth the day-45 state. M3 can reveal asynchronous biomarker shape, discordance, and measurement intensity that B2’s paired summary loses; it cannot recover tumor burden, intent, eligibility, response, death, outside care, or access.

The scientific comparison is not a complexity contest. The primary contrast is B2 versus B0 for anchored TACE and the difference-in-gains between anchored and unanchored TACE. M3 is supportive only if it converges, is calibrated, and adds reproducible information beyond B2 on this same context-stratified estimand. A transformer is deferred because the present identification uncertainty is the meaning of the recorded episode and its observation context; sequence capacity cannot repair absent indication or outcome adjudication. Revisit with validated procedure semantics, larger independent longitudinal data, and a clinical endpoint sample. Causal treatment-effect models and radiology-text/image models are deferred because intent, eligibility, benefit, adjudicated response, images, mortality/outside-care linkage, and external data are unavailable.

## Split, uncertainty, and falsification

Use patient-level temporal splits by index date: 2010–2022 fit, 2023 tuning, and 2024–2025-01-01 locked test. If calendar support fails, use deterministic patient hashing and label the result internal. No patient crosses splits. Select the largest tuning threshold meeting training-only-IPCW sensitivity targets of 80%, 90%, and 95%; never retune on locked data. Bootstrap threshold selection on fit/tuning data and report a fixed-threshold sensitivity analysis.

Report locked-test:

- cause-specific and competing-risk-aware sensitivity, alert fraction, PPV, false-alert fraction, alerts per event, and anchored/unanchored event counts;
- cumulative incidence and calibration for all states;
- B0-to-B2 gain for anchored TACE, unanchored TACE, non-liver control, and their gain difference;
- calibration-in-the-large/slope, Brier and log score per state;
- patient-clustered bootstrap intervals, IPCW overlap/truncation diagnostics, threshold uncertainty, and event-context rates;
- vocabulary, mixed/unclassified, exact-tie, missing-time, context-window, last-contact, and continued-observation audits;
- a pre-index placebo using the same trajectory construction against a predeclared pre-index local-procedure/capture outcome, with no locked-outcome tuning.

Supportive evidence requires all of: (i) the primary >=10-point B2-vs-B0 alert reduction at 90% anchored-TACE sensitivity with an interval excluding zero; (ii) anchored gain directionally stable in continued local observation and under 14-day/30-day context and encounter-time sensitivity; (iii) anchored gain exceeds unanchored-TACE gain by >=5 points with an interval excluding zero; (iv) no comparable gain for non-liver control or pre-index placebo; (v) no adverse pathway-specific calibration/CIF pattern; and, if attempted, (vi) M3 convergence and a prespecified >=5-point alert reduction versus B2 or >=0.02 mean log-score improvement with calibrated intervals. These are local recorded-care/model-information criteria, not clinical utility.

Adverse evidence includes no/reversed anchored gain; equal or larger gain for unanchored TACE/non-liver/placebo; gain confined to administrative disappearance or high-observation strata; material changes across context windows or last-contact definitions; unstable procedure rules; poor weight overlap; calibration failure; high same-time or unclassified rates; or M3 nonconvergence. These results weaken the claim that the trajectory is specific to a reassessment-anchored recorded pathway.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few anchored events, sparse reassessment context, insufficient continued-observation events, high missing procedure/lab times, unresolved ties, inadequate weighting overlap, wide intervals spanning the margins, insufficient calendar support, or no feasible M3 convergence. Do not relax rules after locked outcomes.

## Scientific deliverable and interpretation boundary

The future solver must newly construct and fit the design, not claim discovery completion from readiness:

- `analysis_manifest.json` with snapshot/catalog/source/schema hashes, ordinary-file archive members, exact regexes, episode/context rules, windows, splits, software, seeds, exclusions, and resources;
- `cohort_flow.csv`, `index_manifest.csv`, `landmark_manifest.csv`, `split_manifest.csv`;
- `procedure_vocabulary_audit.csv`, `procedure_episode_manifest.csv`, `event_context_audit.csv`, and `observation_state.csv`;
- `predictions.parquet`, `metrics.json`, `bootstrap_intervals.json`, `decision_boundary.csv`, `operating_curves.csv`, `threshold_selection.json`;
- `pathway_cif.json`, `pathway_calibration.json`, `capture_decomposition.json`, `context_gain_comparison.csv`, `boundary_stability.csv`, `negative_control.csv`, `weight_diagnostics.json`, and `model_parameters.json`;
- `interpretation.json` mapping each conclusion to output fields, estimates, intervals, and exactly one of supportive/adverse/inconclusive;
- `limitations.csv` marking each claim as computationally checkable, requiring adjudication, requiring unavailable evidence, or requiring another study.

Completion means fitted B0/B2 and, if feasible, M3 parameters, locked-test predictions, uncertainty, context/pathway audits, and a justified interpretation. It does not mean the hypothesis is solved in this episode. Computation can establish reproducible local recorded-care stratification and the stability or failure of the context decomposition. It cannot establish treatment indication, intent, eligibility, recurrence, viable tumor, radiologic response, treatment failure, treatment benefit, survival, death, outside-care status, appropriateness, clinical utility, or external validity. Those require expert procedure/intent adjudication, time-valid radiology or pathology, validated laboratory units/assays, mortality and outside-care linkage, prospective evaluation, and independent external data.

## Demonstration dispositions and alternatives

The three demonstrations were used as methodological context through the inspected `README.md`, `methods-and-compute.md`, and available local files, not as reproduction targets:

- The natural-history/Delphi example motivates a bounded dated-history representation. Its original cohort, code path, and external validation are not reproduced; HCC local data lack the same validated cohort.
- The Bayesian longitudinal example motivates the M3 irregular-time latent-state adaptation. Its genetic analysis is not claimed or imported; no genetic inputs are required for this EHR-only estimand.
- The cancer/Oncoformer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable in the supplied bundle. Only the accessible supplement/metadata limitations are respected; no unavailable main-paper claim is used. HCC has no image files, so multimodal imaging is deferred.

A sequence transformer is recorded as deferred because this question is limited by endpoint/context validity, event support, and no external clinical endpoint—not because neural methods or GPUs are prohibited. A causal treatment-effect model is deferred because there is no validated treatment intent, eligibility, mortality, outside-care, or response outcome. A report-text or pathology model is deferred because text is untimestamped/unvalidated for response and pathology lacks a time field. Evidence to revisit these alternatives is a validated clinical-context label set, independent radiology/adjudication, richer timestamps, mortality/outside-care linkage, and an external cohort.

## Compute and method-selection record

A local read-only Python stream over encounter, diagnosis, and procedure sources completed in approximately 7 seconds in the discovery shell and verified the 105,044 encounter rows, 1,810,646 diagnosis rows, 344,383 literal HCC-diagnosis rows, 338,040 procedure rows, and 106,963 TACE-lexicon rows. This is measured data audit time, not fitting time. The discovery science budget is 7,200 seconds; no paid model fit was launched.

B0/B2, episode/context construction, and bootstrap summaries are expected to use CPU with up to 16 CPUs and memory declared by the future solver; exact preprocessing and bootstrap runtime are unverified. M3 should begin with sparse CPU fitting and checkpoints. If a bounded feasibility probe shows a material runtime benefit for repeated state-space optimization, request one allocated A100 through `ehr_job_submit`, use `cuda:0` within that allocation, and report the infrastructure/image dependency separately. Ordinary-shell CUDA visibility is not evidence of GPU absence. No proposer or solver weights are trained.

The selection reason is scientific: B0 is the transparent capture-adjusted reference, B2 isolates the paired trajectory, and M3 tests whether irregular shape plus the measurement process carries information that thresholded summaries lose on the same reassessment-anchored estimand. The actual deliverable is newly fitted parameters, predictions, context/pathway CIFs, uncertainty, and the prespecified supportive/adverse/inconclusive classification. A positive result would justify expert adjudication and external validation; it would not itself justify a clinical action.
