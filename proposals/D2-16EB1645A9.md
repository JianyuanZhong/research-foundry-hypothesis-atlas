# Provisional rival-aware hypothesis: context-specific MerTK-inhibitor response in E-MTAB-8873

## Status and scope

This is a planned, non-causal, intervention-associated hypothesis for the E-MTAB-8873 targeted assay. No expression effect estimate has been computed here, and this note does not claim direct MerTK action, causality, protection, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.

## Proposed relationship

Among the available macrophage–fibroblast coculture samples, the MerTK-inhibitor-associated transcriptional response differs between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage contexts. The primary estimand is a prespecified context-by-inhibitor interaction in pseudobulk expression, conditional on the observed design and donor structure. The scientific advance is to test whether that interaction is specific to the macrophage context rather than being generated by generic treatment, donor reuse/mixing, pseudobulk composition, or targeted-feature measurement artifacts.

The inherited evidence supports biological plausibility: MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets are associated with remission and fibroblast repair responses [K1]; macrophage reprogramming can alter inflammatory homeostasis [K2]; and macrophage–fibroblast repair circuits are context- and niche-dependent [K3]. These works do not establish that UNC106238 acts directly on MerTK, that the E-MTAB-8873 interaction exists, or that any response transfers to patients.

## Strongest rivals and falsifiable discrimination

The leading rival is that a context-by-inhibitor contrast is an artifact of generic treatment, donor reuse/mixing, or pseudobulk composition. The test must therefore compare the interaction against matched non-inhibitor and composition controls, not merely against an unadjusted mean.

1. **Contrast audit.** Use only verified `condition`, `treatment`, and context labels. The five MerTK-inhibitor samples are the relevant intervention-associated samples; compare them with verified untreated controls in the same macrophage context where available. LPS samples are a separate treatment contrast, not evidence for or against MerTK specificity. Mixed Mpos/Mneg conditions are retained as a separate stratum or excluded from the primary pure-context interaction, with the choice prespecified before fitting.
2. **Primary interaction.** Fit a donor-aware additive baseline for condition, treatment, age, sex, tissue, cell_type, cells, and contributing-donor structure. Compare it with the prespecified context-by-inhibitor interaction. Use donor-clustered or donor-permutation uncertainty that respects repeated and mixed donors. Report the number of independent donor units, missing/mixed-donor flags, and estimates at both feature and prespecified program level.
3. **Generic-treatment rival.** Within each context and donor stratum, compare inhibitor-associated changes with the verified untreated/control contrast. If a similarly sized response occurs for LPS or other non-MerTK treatment contrasts, or is indistinguishable across Mpos and Mneg, the specificity claim is weakened. LPS is not a negative control for MerTK biology; it is a generic perturbation comparator.
4. **Composition rival.** Standardize or stratify by `cell_type`, contributing-donor composition, and available `cells` where the design permits, and compare the adjusted interaction with the unadjusted one. A composition-sensitive interaction that disappears after this control is evidence against context specificity. Because all records are type B synovial cells, this control cannot establish cell-type resolution; it can only test whether the observed pseudobulk structure explains the contrast.
5. **Targeted-feature measurement rival.** Preserve the 445-feature source identifiers and run a prespecified feature-definition audit. Do not infer macrophage-linked or fibroblast-linked programs from unmeasured genes. Use sensitivity analyses that vary only prespecified feature definitions and report whether the interaction depends on a small number of highly expressed or assay-ambiguous features. A result driven by one feature, duplicate-like feature instances, or a definition chosen after seeing outcomes is not supportive.
6. **Donor reuse/mixing rival.** Do not treat samples as independent. Use a contributing-donor model or donor-level permutation/clustered uncertainty, and report results by independent donor support. If the interaction is supported only by one reused donor set or by mixed-donor samples, classify it as inconclusive rather than specific.

Supportive evidence requires a reproducible prespecified interaction that remains directionally consistent after composition and treatment controls, with adequate uncertainty and independent-donor support. Adverse evidence is a null/reversed interaction, disappearance after composition adjustment, a generic treatment effect indistinguishable across contexts, or dependence on a single reused donor or a small feature subset. Inconclusive evidence includes unstable contrasts, missing matched controls, insufficient independent donor information, ambiguous feature definitions, or imprecise estimates. Supportive results justify a follow-up mechanistic study; they do not establish direct target action or clinical benefit.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The simple baseline is the donor-aware additive model for condition, treatment, age, sex, tissue, cell_type, cells, and contributing-donor structure. The substantive alternative adds the context-by-inhibitor interaction plus the matched treatment, composition, donor-dependence, and targeted-feature sensitivity comparisons. It can reveal a context-specific response that the additive baseline averages away, while exposing whether the apparent effect is donor, composition, generic-treatment, or measurement structure. Both use the same 33-sample inputs, 445 measured features, split, and uncertainty evaluation. CPU is adequate for this tabular analysis; no GPU is required. The future solver must newly fit both models and produce sample-level and program-level outputs, independent-donor support, and uncertainty. No full study result is claimed here.

## Missing evidence and claim boundaries

The assay lacks whole-tissue or cell-state-resolved measurements, independent clinical cohorts, verified temporal labels, patient outcomes, and direct target perturbation evidence. Drug-associated expression does not identify direct target action. Direct-target, mechanistic, causal, and therapeutic claims require perturbation/mechanistic evidence, expert review, and independent validation.

## Alternatives not chosen

A generic predictive model is deferred because it would not distinguish context-specific biology from donor or treatment composition. A full neural sequence model is deferred because no verified longitudinal patient trajectory or temporal outcome is available; this is a dependency decision, not a blanket rejection of learned methods. Revisit if verified temporal or independent-unit data become available.
