# Episode 9: an overlap-matched challenge to the concurrent pH-conditioned SpO2 blind spot

## Question, evidence boundary, and clinical advance

At a monitor SpO2 of 96–97%, does the materially higher probability of an observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg in acidemia persist after comparison is restricted to observations with the same hospital, released SpO2, similar FiO2 and PaCO2, and the same ICU day, or is the parent association compatible with residual case mix?

The inspected full-text XML of Delgado et al. (Antioxidants 2026; DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712) supports an association, not this hypothesis. It describes a retrospective study at one tertiary center (University Hospital Zurich, November 2017–December 2022), 21,406 adults and 717,064 paired measurements; arterial gases were aligned to median SpO2 and FiO2 in the preceding five minutes. Acidemic states had higher PaO2 at comparable SpO2, FiO2 modified hyperoxemia probability, and SpO2 96–97% generally corresponded to physiologic PaO2. It does not establish a +10-point EICU contrast, within-person robustness, multi-hospital transportability, causality, treatment benefit, or a decision-time alert.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is therefore only that the physiology is plausible, a recent single-center association exists, and the exact EICU experiment below is computable. The unresolved claim tested here is a conjunctive one:

1. In all 705 exact parent-cohort observations, the B2 observation-standardized acidemic-minus-normal risk difference (RD) for PaO2 >120 mm Hg has a lower 95% confidence bound of at least +10 percentage points.
2. In the prespecified hospital-and-physiology overlap population, the overlap-weighted acidemic-minus-normal RD also has a lower 95% bound of at least +10 points.
3. At least six of the eight outcome-blind adequate hospitals have positive unadjusted RDs, with the parent's heterogeneity and leave-site-out gates satisfied.

This is more discriminating than the parent because full-cohort model support cannot by itself justify “robust to measured case mix.” It remains a concurrent measurement association. Even conjunctive support would be consistent with, but would not prove, a physiologic pH-conditioned relation.

## Outcomes, estimands, baselines, and computation

Exposure is concurrent acidemia versus normal pH. The primary outcome is observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg. Secondary outcomes are PaO2 >150, continuous PaO2, and PaO2 <60; none may rescue a failed primary result.

### Full-cohort estimand

Retain the parent's observation-weighted marginal standardized RD over all 705 observations. B0 contains an intercept and median SpO2 centered at 96.5. B1 adds linear FiO2 per 10 points, age per decade, sex, unit type, unit admission source, six-hour block 0–11, PaCO2 per 10 mm Hg, and a PaCO2-missing indicator. B2 adds acidemia and acidemia×linear FiO2. Collapse categorical levels with fewer than 10 rows to Other using predictor counts before viewing PaO2; missing categories are levels. Fit population-average logistic GEE with exchangeable correlation by `uniquepid`. Set acidemia to 1 and 0 for every row, preserve all other observed covariates, average predicted risks, and subtract normal from acidemic. This is descriptive g-computation, not an intervention.

Report crude group risks/RD separately; report B0, B1 and B2 coefficients, B2 standardized risks/RD, and model/overlap diagnostics. Use 2,000 hospital-cluster bootstrap resamples for the 95% interval, copying every row and person in a sampled hospital and assigning copied hospital/person IDs.

### Overlap-matched discriminator

For each of the 57 frozen cells (s), let (n_{1s}) and (n_{0s}) denote acidemic and normal counts. Give each acidemic row weight (n_{0s}/(n_{1s}+n_{0s})) and each normal row weight (n_{1s}/(n_{1s}+n_{0s})). The total weight is identical between groups. Normalize within group, compute each weighted observed risk, and subtract normal from acidemic. This is the overlap-population RD: it targets observations whose measured hospital/oxygenation context could plausibly occur in either pH group. It is not the 705-row RD and is not a causal effect.

Report cell counts, group weight sums, effective sample sizes ((sum(w))^2/sum(w^2)), maximum normalized weight, weighted covariate summaries for age, sex, unit type, admission source, exact FiO2, exact PaCO2, exact draw offset, and the fraction of each original exposure group excluded. Do not fit a high-dimensional outcome model to ten events.

For uncertainty, perform 2,000 hierarchical bootstrap replicates: sample hospitals with replacement; within each sampled hospital copy, sample `uniquepid` with replacement and carry all their rows; give copied hospitals and people new IDs; rebuild the fixed cells and overlap weights; compute the RD. Report empty/undefined replicates. More than 10% failures, either group ESS <50, any one site contributing >50% of total overlap weight, or direction reversal on deletion of any site with >=10 matched rows makes the overlap result inconclusive regardless of its nominal interval.

Fixed overlap sensitivities are five-point FiO2/ten-mm Hg PaCO2 bands with 24-hour block; ten-point/twenty-mm Hg bands with 12-hour block; earliest eligible row per person; equal-person weighting; complete PaCO2; and the acidemic-population ATT weighting `1` for acidemic rows and `n1s/n0s` for normal rows, reported with its weight ESS and concentration diagnostics. The audited strict demographic specifications are descriptive infeasibility checks, not alternate primaries.

### Multi-hospital and inherited robustness requirements

Retain the parent's eight-site reporting and gates. For every frozen site report group denominators, events, unadjusted RD and `uniquepid`-cluster bootstrap interval. Do not remove a site for zero events, separation, or an unfavorable estimate. Full transportability requires at least six positive site point estimates, no adequate-site deletion reversing the full-cohort B2 direction, a random-effects prediction interval not extending to <=-5 points, and the inherited leave-one-hospital-out B2-vs-B1 portability limits: Brier increase <=0.01, log-loss increase <=0.02, and calibration slope 0.8–1.2. Report tau-squared, I-squared, prediction interval, calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, log loss, AUROC, and AUPRC.

Retain the parent's repeated-observation and data-definition sensitivities: earliest row per stay, equal-stay weighting, earliest row per stay-specific six-hour block, first 24 hours, last rather than median SpO2, 60-minute FiO2 lookback, chart-offset FiO2 without entry restriction, alternate FiO2 labels separately, complete PaCO2, FiO2 bands, closed nonzero ventilation intervals, broader airway proxy, and deletion of each adequate hospital. APACHE variables remain untimestamped QC only. The prior-pH alert remains prohibited: only 24 availability-safe acidemic/normal rows exist at 5–60 minutes and no balanced hospital.

## Falsification and allowed interpretation

Support for the clinically material, measured-case-mix-resistant association requires both the full-cohort B2 and overlap-matched RD lower 95% bounds to be >=+10 points, plus all multi-hospital gates. The strongest allowed conclusion is: “Within this retrospective EICU construction, a >=10-point concurrent pH-conditioned difference was robust to prespecified measured hospital and oxygenation-context overlap and was sufficiently reproducible to justify prospective synchronized validation.” It cannot be called physiologic proof.

Adverse to the +10-point claim means either primary RD has an upper 95% bound <+10. An upper bound <0 supports an opposite association in that estimand. A precise +6-point result is adverse to material magnitude but supports a smaller association. If B2 supports +10 while the overlap RD is adverse, report that the full-cohort association does not survive the measured-overlap challenge; do not attribute the discrepancy uniquely to case mix because estimand populations differ. If overlap supports but B2 is adverse, the conjunctive hypothesis fails and the result is limited to the overlap population.

Inconclusive includes an interval crossing +10; >10% bootstrap failures; failed count reconciliation; inadequate ESS or site weight concentration; direction reversal under specified data-definition, person-weighting, or leave-site-out analyses; or full-cohort support with overlap imprecision. A wide -2 to +22 interval is inconclusive, not supportive. Fewer than six positive parent sites is adverse to transportability, not proof of no pooled association.

The self-controlled question is explicitly unresolved in EICU, not adverse: eight events and six outcome-and-exposure-discordant people cannot distinguish physiology from stable patient case mix. Concurrent results may not rescue the infeasible pre-draw alert or self-controlled branch.
