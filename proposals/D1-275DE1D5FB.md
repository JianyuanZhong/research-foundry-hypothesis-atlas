## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE/resection/ablation), follow-up repeats serum AFP, albumin and
bilirubin and periodic abdominal imaging. Question: **does the within-patient AFP slope add
prognostic information beyond baseline AFP and beyond same-draw liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics for
time to radiologic progression?**

The parent bounded (did not identify) informative observation. Its positive identification contrast
classifies each post-landmark abdominal study as lab-triggered vs interval-scheduled and tests a
slope×stratum interaction. [K3] shows landmark designs are used to reduce immortal-time bias but
do not address scan-triggering. **The still-open objection this child attacks:** even if the
AFP-slope association *survives* in the interval-scheduled stratum, a **threshold** pattern (signal
only at an extreme, high slope) is exactly what a detection-triggering artifact predicts, whereas a
genuine tumour-activity trajectory should be **graded** across the whole slope distribution. A
slope×stratum interaction cannot separate graded tumour activity from an extreme-value triggering
artifact. [K1] documents that early progression/refractoriness is clinically consequential; [K2]
shows the joint-model family can relate a within-patient marker trajectory to progression after
TACE, but a single hazard does not test monotonicity.

**Clinical importance.** If the association is graded and survives in interval-scheduled imaging,
marker-informed escalation and earlier scheduled reassessment are supported. If it is a
threshold/triggering artifact, the actionable change is **fixed-interval imaging policy** and
de-emphasising AFP-only monitoring. A precise null argues for ALBI/liver-reserve-based follow-up.
These imply different surveillance decisions.

## 3. THE ONE ADDED ELEMENT — graded-vs-threshold (monotone dose-response) falsification

Within the inherited strata, estimate the AFP-slope → progression hazard **both** as a continuous
per-1-SD term **and** as **response quartiles Q1–Q4** (quartile cut-points fixed from the pooled
exposure distribution BEFORE any outcome is seen), adjusted for baseline AFP, ALBI slope, age, sex,
index year, treatment modality.

Pre-declared discriminating statistic: the **quartile HR profile** and a rank-based **trend test**
(Cochran–Armitage style, p_trend) across quartile medians.

- **Supportive (graded tumour signal):** in the interval-scheduled stratum, HRs increase monotonically
  across Q1→Q4 (p_trend < 0.05) **AND** Q2-vs-Q1 HR CI excludes 1 (signal is present below the
  extreme, not only at the top), **AND** ≥ 30 interval-scheduled progression events.
- **Adverse (threshold / triggering artifact):** association confined to Q4 (Q4 CI excludes 1 while
  Q2 and Q3 do not) or a non-monotone profile, OR the continuous signal is present only in the
  triggered stratum.
- **Precise null:** both continuous and quartile AFP effects null with Δ-C-index equivalence vs M0
  within ±0.01.
- **Inconclusive:** < 30 interval-scheduled events, or < 10 events in any quartile (pre-declared
  fallback: collapse to tertiles; if still sparse, report the continuous model only and label
  inconclusive).

## 4. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative), recorded before selection

- **M0 (baseline, primary comparator):** landmark Cox with baseline AFP + baseline ALBI grade +
  age/sex/index-year/treatment modality + draw intensity. Uses only the pre-landmark *level*.
- **M4 (learned/mechanistic alternative):** joint linear-mixed-effects sub-model for the AFP and
  ALBI trajectories + Cox sub-model entering the fitted subject-specific slopes (BLUP), fit on the
  same `(index, t*]` draws. M4 recovers **change-vs-level** and **shrinkage across sparse draws**
  that M0's single baseline value loses, giving a less measurement-noise-driven slope.
- **What M4 adds scientifically:** if the M4 slope effect is graded and survives interval-scheduled
  imaging while the M0 baseline effect does not, the extra information is genuine within-patient
  change, not cross-sectional severity. If M0 and M4 agree, the simpler baseline suffices.
- **Deferred (with justification):** the shared-parameter observation-process joint model (episode 8)
  and Fine–Gray competing-risks model. Revisit if the monotonicity test is inconclusive or if the
  competing `转移` event is non-negligible.
- **Budget:** tabular fits, statisticsmodels/survival + numpy/scipy; CPU-only, minutes per fit;
  GPU not required and not claimed. Approximate total: minutes–tens of minutes per full run.

## 5. Falsification criteria (conjunctive)

The claim "the AFP slope adds **graded** prognostic information beyond baseline AFP and same-draw
ALBI kinetics, not explainable by scan-triggering" is falsified if any of: (i) interval-scheduled
p_trend ≥ 0.05; (ii) association present only in Q4 / non-monotone; (iii) continuous signal confined
to the triggered stratum. An imprecise interval (wide CI, sparse strata) is inconclusive, not
supportive.
