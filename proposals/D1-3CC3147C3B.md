# Stability certificate and archive-first external bridge for fixed-quota documentary-M2 review

## 1. Successor direction and unresolved decision

This targeted successor to [prior hypothesis] preserves the parent's clinical estimand:

Among adults at their first eligible HCC resection episode, does a locked semantic ranking select more same-encounter pathology-linked documentary M2 cases than fixed-capacity alternatives when a retrospective stored report object is available?

A positive case remains an encounter-documentary M2 label. It is not a claim of biological microvascular invasion, report availability or viewing, treatment, workload, action, or patient benefit.

The remaining weakness is the bridge from a robust retrospective result to a consequential next evidence step. The parent produces an exact preference frontier, but a favorable 24-hour result could still depend on a less plausible operation clock, the exact pathology terminal, or stored-content exposure. Conversely, visibility- or pathology-sensitive results should lead to an archive/access or adjudication study, not directly to silent mode.

This successor adds a separately reported, threshold-independent stability certificate and a predeclared archive-first decision. It does not turn the snapshot into a clinical-use study and does not invent a utility threshold.

Primary bridge question:

In each of 2020 and 2021, does G retain its fixed-quota documentary-M2 capture advantage over the locked equal-capacity comparators and random allocation in every coherent stored-content, reader, pathology, operation-clock, qualifying-mask, and anchored-deletion state, including all four locked offsets? If so, is the appropriate next step a bounded external archive audit rather than a clinical or silent-mode deployment claim?

The falsifiable stability hypothesis is:

- H1: for each test year y, every retained admissible state and each d in {12, 24, 48, 72} hours has 100(h_G-h_C)/K > 5 for C in {B,R,Gmask}, and N h_G > K E against random fixed-K review.
- H0/falsifier: one attained state with 20(h_G-h_C) <= K, or N h_G <= K E, falsifies the corresponding year-level stability claim. Equality is failure, not rounding uncertainty.

The 24-hour state remains the primary clinical-clock estimand inherited from the parent. Other offsets are a prespecified robustness envelope for deciding whether an external archive/access audit is warranted; they do not redefine the primary estimand.

## 2. Evidence boundary and substantive advance

The strongest supported claim is only that this HCC snapshot can support an exact, outcome-blind retrospective comparison of locked rankings over stored narrative units, under explicitly enumerated clock, pathology-terminal, reader, content-mask, and deletion worlds. It cannot establish that a report was visible to a clinician, that a score was generated before the operation, that pathology belongs to a particular specimen or slide, that review capacity or burden was measured, or that identifying documentary M2 changes management or outcomes.

Vickers, van Calster and Steyerberg (2019, DOI 10.1186/s41512-019-0064-7, PMCID PMC6777022, frozen source [source checksum]) explains that decision-curve thresholds encode a minimum probability for action and should be considered over clinically reasonable preferences; it does not supply a clinically valid preference range for this review action. Neves and Soares (2026, DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028, PMCID PMC13360217, frozen source [source checksum]) reports that the HCC early-recurrence AI literature is predominantly retrospective and single-center, with limited independent validation and heterogeneous calibration/decision-curve practice. These sources motivate an evidence bridge, but neither validates this endpoint, quota, comparator, or preference.

The advance is a decision rule for what the retrospective study can responsibly authorize:

1. an exact worst-state stability certificate for the stored-content signal; and
2. if the certificate is computationally valid and favorable, authorize a narrowly scoped external archive audit; if a valid adverse witness isolates visibility, clock, or pathology uncertainty, authorize that same audit only to resolve the witnessed gap, never as validation of G.

A favorable certificate does not authorize silent mode or patient-care use. A failure identifies what the next study must resolve.

## 7. Exact stability outputs

For every z=(y,d,world,reader pair,mask,deletion,book), let N_z be the binary-outcome roster, E_z the documentary-M2 count, and h_M,z the exact number of cases in policy M's top K. Store f_M,z=K-h_M,z, all integer witnesses, and raw state hashes.

For each year, offset, and comparator calculate:

- Delta_C,y,d = min_z 100(h_G-h_C)/K.
- epsilon_y,d = min_z (N h_G-K E)/(K N).
- p_safe,y,d = min_z h_G/K.
- B_max,y,d = max_z (K-h_G)/h_G, with +infinity when h_G=0.

p_safe and B_max are finite-corpus preference frontiers, not calibrated risk, biological probabilities, or clinically accepted preferences.

The stability certificate is a tuple:

- Q: exact computation, replay, leakage, fit, tie, mask, deletion, and witness checks pass.
- A: every required test state has a qualifying mask and meets pathology-grade/IN1 sufficiency.
- S_full: for each year, all admissible states at all four offsets satisfy Delta_C,y,d>5 and epsilon_y,d>0.
- S_content: the predicates remain true in every qualifying full-content and 80/80 state.
- S_clock: the predicates remain true at all four offsets and all finite clock interpretations.
- S_path: the predicates remain true over every retained pathology terminal/reader/book world.
- S_delete: the predicates remain true after every required anchored singleton deletion.

Report each exact minimum and maximum, the state hash and raw backpointers of every extremum, and a decomposition identifying the first failing axis. Do not replace this finite envelope with a confidence interval. Bootstrap is descriptive only. Do not average over states or assign probabilities to uncertainty worlds.

The primary 24-hour result remains separately labeled. The all-offset certificate is supportive only when the primary result and robustness envelope are both reported; passing an average or one test year never passes the certificate.

## 10. Falsification and interpretation

Supportive means exact Q/A pass, both test years pass the primary and all-offset stability predicates, and no comparator/random failure under any retained state. This supports only a robust finite-corpus stored-content ranking signal and justifies the external archive audit. It does not support biological MVI, report access/viewing, clinical action, benefit, transport, or silent-mode safety.

Adverse means a maximum-content comparator/random witness falsifies the stored-content hypothesis, or an 80/80, clock, or pathology witness identifies dependence on unresolved evidence. Adverse is scientifically useful because it directs the next evidence step or stops this line; it does not prove that no biological association exists.

Inconclusive/structural means timeout, omitted state, non-lossless parser, failed replay, leakage, missing witness, changed survivor set, insufficient IN1, absent qualifying mask, or undeclared fit/tie change. It supports no scientific direction. Reference execution demonstrates computation only, not truth.
