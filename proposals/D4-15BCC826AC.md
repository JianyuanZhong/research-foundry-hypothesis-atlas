## Falsification, interpretation, and limits

Supportive requires all certification states, exact cohort reconstruction, finite converged models, fold isolation, comparator/calibration/support gates, at least 8/10 positive repeat-specific contrasts, 2,000 successful bootstrap replicates, positive pooled `Delta_Brier` with two-sided 95% interval excluding zero and p<0.05, and then positive `Delta_rank10` with interval excluding zero and p<0.05. The attendance model must not show complete nonoverlap, and no prespecified timing band may show a sign-reversal pattern that makes the pooled claim misleading; if the timing diagnostic is unsupported, say so rather than treating it as supportive. The conclusion is limited to reproducible baseline-only incremental information and fixed-capacity protocol ranking among certified complete repeat attenders.

Adverse means a primary contrast is wholly below zero, M1 materially worsens calibration while M0 passes, repeat-specific results reproducibly favor M0, or the attendance/timing diagnostics expose severe nonoverlap or a prespecified instability that invalidates the pooled protocol-ranking interpretation. Inconclusive means an interval includes zero, signs are unstable, technical gates fail, bootstrap completion fails, or the selection/timing diagnostics are unsupported without a clear adverse pattern; it is not equivalence. `NEUTRAL_STOP` means field semantics, assay interpretation, or date semantics were not certified and no biomarker inference was attempted.

Computationally checkable claims are source identity, schema/headers, keys and joins, certificate matching, certified missing-code handling, equation arithmetic, cohort masks, baseline-only feature sets, fold isolation, tuning, predictions, calibration, ranking, bootstrap, attendance diagnostics, timing strata, and state transitions. Assay identity/validity, specimen provenance, chronicity, true GFR, clinical meaning of a threshold crossing, reasons for attendance, missing outcomes among nonattenders, treatment effects, utility, and transportability require steward metadata, expert adjudication, external validation, a longitudinal outcome source, or a decision-impact/randomized study. No stronger clinical recommendation follows from this proposal.
