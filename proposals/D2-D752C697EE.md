# MerTK/CD206pos macrophage brake: a reproducibility-tested dose-response test (episode 17)

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2. Operation: evolve. Parent: `[prior hypothesis]` (valid, reference-complete, mu 33.58).
Key works [K1],[K2],[K3]. Screening status: **planned, not executed**.

## Unresolved question
Is the MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) synovial macrophage state a *graded, causal brake* on the RA
fibroblast-like synoviocyte (FLS) matrix-degrading/inflammatory effector program, or is the
pos-vs-neg effector difference a *macrophage-load / state composition artifact*? The parent
tested a single pos-vs-neg pair and ONE ratio pair and explicitly recorded an "n=1 titration arm"
limit.

## Verified correction driving this advance
Direct inspection of `E-MTAB-8873.design.json` (join key `sample_id`) shows the ratio-titration
arms exist in **two independent FLS-donor systems**, not one:
- R2 / FLS donor SA258R (shared across arms): FLS_only (0.0 pos), `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_1Mpos_SA266A_3Mneg` (0.25 pos),
  `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_3Mpos_SA266A_1Mneg` (0.75 pos), `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_Mpos` (1.0 pos) — a 4-point within-FLS-donor dose series.
- R5 / FLS donor SA263R: `R5_SA263R_FLS_only` (0.0), `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_1Mpos_SA277A_3Mneg` (0.25), `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mpos` (1.0) — a 3-point series in an independent system.
- MerTK-inhibitor arms exist (e.g. `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos_Inhibitor`, `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos_preInhibitor`) and LPS arms (`..._Mpos_preLPS`, `..._Mneg_preLPS`).
The parent's "n=1" limitation is therefore incorrect: a dose-response can be fit within a single
FLS donor and partially replicated in a second, independent FLS donor.

## Clinical importance
~1/3 of RA patients do not sustain remission. If raising the MerTK/CD206pos macrophage fraction
gradedly and reversibly lowers the FLS effector program, the MerTK axis is a titratable target;
if the effector signal instead tracks total macrophage load or Mneg state, the "brake" is a
composition artifact and MerTK modulation would be misdirected.

## Estimand and analysis
Primary: within-FLS-donor regression of a pre-registered 9-gene FLS effector score
(MMP1/3/9/13, IL6, CXCL8/10, CCL2, CCL5) on `pos_fraction` (continuous), fit within each
FLS donor, then compared for a shared slope across the two independent FLS donors.
Secondary: MerTK-inhibitor reversal (effector score restored toward pos-only level) and an
LPS-context interaction. Rival: effector score tracks total `cells` (load) or Mneg identity.

## Falsification
Supportive: monotone-negative, FLS-donor-shared slope, inhibitor-reversible.
Adverse: no dose relation, or slope absorbed by load/state covariates, or non-reproducible sign.
Inconclusive: n too small / panel limits; report effect bounds.

## Limits
Targeted 445-feature panel; pseudobulk; cross-sectional 8322; no protein/wet-lab causality;
`donor_status=requires_contributing_donor_model` for co-culture arms. Not a clinical-efficacy claim.
