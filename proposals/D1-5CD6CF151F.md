## 5. Normative radiology-reader envelope

### 5.1 Pre-outcome roster and blinding

Before any pathology narrative/grade, model score, top-K membership, event count, or test result is exposed to radiologists, freeze a roster of every CT/MRI whole unit reachable as a possible selected unit from any structural `S+` state. Roster generation may use keys, modality candidates, acquisition intervals, canonicalization, and context/quarantine status but not `Y`. Each usable `clean-imaging-context` unit is assigned to the same two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists for independent full review and to a third qualified radiologist for adjudication. Screens show only deidentified whole-unit `V`, modality, and immutable field/component boundaries; they hide patient/encounter/accession identifiers, dates/year role, other modality, pathology, diagnoses, model outputs, and other readers during independent review.

Study-generated forms record `item_hash`, `V_hash`, parser/rubric/version hashes, blinded reader code and role, assignment/start/submit times, every concept level, every required `V` and reversible source-byte span, signed whole-text review for absent/not-mentioned calls, explicit `uncertain` responses, form status, and append-only revision history. These timestamps describe annotation workflow only; they are not source report authorship/finalization/release/view times.

Both independent forms must be submitted for every usable roster item. Operational noncompletion, lost/import-corrupt form, absent lineage, unresolved qualification, or an unfinished adjudication at freeze is a mandatory global inconclusive gate. It cannot remove an item, shrink a denominator, become a negative concept, or be encoded as `source-text-unavailable`. Semantic uncertainty belongs in the form’s existing `uncertain` level. A reader who cannot bound a concept using the rubric must record `uncertain`; inability of the panel to express a complete finite vector under the rubric makes the experiment inconclusive.

### 5.2 Whole-form finite domains; no synthetic reader

For each usable unit `u`, let `f_1(u)` and `f_2(u)` be the two independently submitted complete concept-and-span vectors. After seeing both signed forms, the third reader submits one complete adjudicated vector `f_A(u)` and a reason for every changed field. Define

`D_u = unique{f_1(u), f_2(u), f_A(u)}`.

Each member of `D_u` is indivisible. The optimizer may not combine capsule from one form, margin from another, or labels from one form with spans from another. No majority vote, probability, reliability weight, Dawid–Skene latent label, averaging of numeric values, or post-outcome adjudication creates a primary label. For quarantined, parse-ambiguous, or source-text-unavailable units, `D_u` is the singleton joint unavailable block required by the parent; hidden `A` content never enlarges it.

If a unit is reachable in multiple source states, one chosen `f in D_u` follows that unit everywhere within a world. It cannot be relabeled by patient role, year, outcome, score, or model arm. Distinct accessions are distinct blinded assignments and may select different supplied forms; within an accession the full joint vector is preserved. Every domain member must pass the parent’s span-lineage and context rules. A failed member is repaired with provenance before freeze or causes inconclusive computation; it is not silently deleted because it is inconvenient.

The primary world is `omega=(s,r)`, where `s` is one admissible coupled parent source/outcome state per patient and `r` maps every reachable usable accession to exactly one member of its frozen `D_u`. Call the resulting complete relational universe `Omega_read+`. The adjudicated-only subuniverse `Omega_A`, with `r(u)=f_A(u)`, is a secondary consensus diagnostic and can never rescue the primary.

Because accessions are patient-specific, robust-loss separation remains patientwise after enforcing within-accession consistency; normalized losses retain the parent’s exact Dinkelbach separation. Test extrema add finite reader choices to the parent’s exact state-expanded optimization. If the implementation cannot certify that every `D_u` choice and every reachable source state was included, the result is inconclusive.

### 5.3 Reliability summaries are descriptive, not truth models

Before fitting, report exact per-concept cross-tabs, raw agreement, missing/uncertain/not-mentioned frequencies, span-overlap summaries, and prespecified prevalence-aware agreement statistics by modality and calendar role, together with whole-vector domain widths `|D_u|`. Also report counts of adjudicator agreement with reader 1, reader 2, or neither. Reader identity, agreement, disagreement, adjudication path, form time, domain width, and year never enter `Z,S,Q,X_clean` or any model.

There is no kappa/AC1 threshold for scientific support. Low agreement can coexist with a reader-robust ranking, and high aggregate agreement can coexist with one clinically decisive threshold reversal. Agreement quantifies reproducibility within this declared panel and rubric; it does not estimate correctness, image truth, biological truth, or performance for unseen readers.

## 7. Falsification and interpretation

Mandatory gates add to, rather than replace, every parent gate:

- roster completeness for every possible selected usable unit in `S+`;
- two independent complete forms and one complete adjudication form per usable unit, frozen before outcome/model access;
- immutable form/rubric/parser hashes and zero outcome, year, score, or top-K leakage;
- whole-form domains only, within-accession consistency, and zero conceptwise Cartesian mixing;
- valid `V`/source-byte spans for every span-requiring label and zero semantic lineage from `A`, another accession, pathology, or a post-cutoff row;
- no complete-case deletion or substitution of human noncompletion by unavailable/negative labels;
- reader identity/metadata absent from all model matrices;
- byte-identical `Z,S,Q` in `R_clean,G_clean,Gclean_mask`, same-fit masking, and all inherited event, denominator, grid, fit, state, quotient, replay, top-K, and exact-solver certificates.

After gates pass, each coordinate is supportive iff `delta_e^->5`, source-and/or-reader heterogeneous iff `delta_e^-<=5<delta_e^+`, and all-world adverse iff `delta_e^+<=5`; equality to 5 fails the strict hypothesis. The global labels remain mutually exclusive:

1. `reader-robust-universal-supportive` only if all four primary `I_e^read` lower endpoints exceed 5;
2. `decisive-threshold-heterogeneity` if some admissible coordinate/world exceeds 5 and some fails, annotated `reader-threshold-sensitive` exactly for coordinates with `C_e=1`;
3. `universal-adverse-without-positive-coordinate` if every primary upper endpoint is at most 5;
4. `inconclusive` if any mandatory gate fails.

Any lower endpoint at or below 5 falsifies the universal conjunction. An adjudicated-only supportive result with a non-supportive reader envelope is reader-fragile and cannot be reported as support. A positive `C_e` proves only that supplied text-defensible reader vectors reverse the documentary ranking threshold while source/outcome state is held fixed. If `C_e=0` but the combined envelope fails, the failure cannot be attributed specifically to reader disagreement. `unsafe_raw` remains diagnostic only and cannot rescue any label.

Support permits only: “Within this frozen eventual-report corpus, source-byte-traceable concepts from the canonical text-node surrogate, after declared context quarantine, added more than 5 encounter-documentary M2 captures per 100 fixed positions under every declared source, outcome-completion, and reader-form world in both test years.” It does not establish that labels are accurate or reliable, that reports were visible preoperatively, that features existed on images, that M2 is specimen-linked biology, or that ranking would help patients.

Adverse or heterogeneous results falsify the strict universal margin but do not prove semantics useless, identify a correct reader, or rule out smaller effects. Inconclusive means the source, annotation, event, fit, or exact-computation contract failed and is not negative evidence. The bounds are attained properties of a frozen finite corpus, not confidence intervals; no p value, bootstrap, sampling-population, causal, transport, deployment, or utility claim is permitted.
