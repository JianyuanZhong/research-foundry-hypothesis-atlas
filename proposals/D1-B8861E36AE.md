# Episode 12 — A report-unit availability frontier for documentary-M2 review before first HCC resection

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and the Episode-12 advance

The decision question remains narrow: 24 hours before an adult patient's first eligible liver resection, can routinely stored CT/MRI report semantics add enough information to a laboratory-independent flag to justify multidisciplinary review for pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)?

The flag is a historical classification/review trigger. It is not a recommendation for a wider resection margin, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment, surveillance, or any other intervention.

**Strongest claim supported before the experiment.** Direct catalog, schema, header, and row inspection establishes that this HCC snapshot contains operation start times, CT/MRI acquisition start times, eventual report bodies, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. It does not contain radiology report authored/final/release/view times, pathology times or specimen identifiers, raw images/slides, a pathology sampling protocol, recurrence, survival, clinician actions, treatment effects, harms, or costs. The laboratory source has one ambiguous `检验时间` and no collection, verification, release, view, accession/panel, or unit field, so laboratories remain prohibited from every primary feature, model, state, estimand, and gate.

A full-row Episode-12 feasibility audit scanned 338,040 procedure rows and 419,996 examination rows. A deliberately broad lexical resection rule, before pathology, prior-treatment, age, final dictionary, and calendar exclusions, yielded 12,775 first-resection patients; a broad liver/upper-abdominal CT/MRI rule found 5,896 with a selected acquisition, 6,054 selected nonempty accession-time units, and 158 patients with both a CT and MRI unit. All 6,054 selected units were single source rows after exact-row deduplication. Thus CT-only/MRI-only partial visibility is empirically possible but uncommon in this provisional frame, while no within-accession multirow report was observed. These are feasibility counts, not cohort attrition, prevalence, performance, or evidence that the final clinically reviewed dictionaries will reproduce the same multiplicity. The frozen execution must repeat the audit after every final population filter.

The inspected public evidence remains context, not validation. Li et al. (2025; DOI `10.1111/jcmm.70746`, PMCID `PMC12309289`; frozen full XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) describes MVI as pathology-dependent, clinically prognostic, and unavailable directly before surgery, and reviews the Chinese M0/M1/M2 classification. Huang et al. (2026; DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`; frozen full HTML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports a retrospective multicenter binary-MVI model using CT margin irregularity and AFP positivity (487 development, 256 external validation; AUC 0.740 and 0.781), with blinded image review and complete-data eligibility. Neither validates M2 report semantics, report availability, this dataset, or treatment benefit.

**Audit finding about the parent.** The parent's patient-level frontier is not invalid: for each patient it already enumerates none, CT only, MRI only, and both where attainable, and minimizes over which report is exposed. A lower bound conditional on at least `k` patients having any report is therefore conservative for exactly that guarantee. But it is a weak deployment interface. “Any report” treats one-of-two and two-of-two visibility identically, and an external patient-coverage audit can meet its guarantee without stating how many selected reports were actually visible. The model state, source-system timestamp audit, and workflow requirement should use the same report unit. Episode 12 therefore makes the report-unit frontier primary and retains the patient-any frontier as a secondary diagnostic. It also prevents a hidden atomic-bundle assumption if a final accession-time group contains nonidentical report rows.

**Untested stored-content hypothesis P.** In the locked 2020–2021 whole target frame with every selected nonempty report unit exposed, the same-fit semantic content contrast `D=S-S0` and complete-strategy contrast `C=S-B` each exceed 0.01 net-benefit units at threshold 0.30 on a single simultaneous 95% family-wise band; both point lower bounds remain positive at every threshold 0.25–0.35, and S beats flag-all and flag-none.

**Untested deployment hypothesis R.** If at least a fraction `rho` of all selected report units is genuinely visible at `t_dec`, do D and C retain the same clinical margin under the least favorable assignment of those visible units to patients and modalities and under all pathology outcomes not identified by the snapshot? The experiment estimates the smallest sufficient report-unit coverage `rho*`. R is supported only when an independent source-system audit using the identical report-unit construction and decision clock provides a one-sided coverage lower bound at least `rho*`. The HCC snapshot alone cannot test actual visibility.

The advance over existing knowledge is a falsifiable separation of (1) action changes caused by stored report semantics within one fitted pipeline, (2) value of the complete semantic strategy over an independently optimized baseline, and (3) the report-unit workflow availability required for deployment. It does not substitute model complexity for clinical value.

## Pathology-only documentary endpoint and whole-frame uncertainty

`Y=1` only for pathology-documented M2; `Y=0` for M0 or M1. `V=1` means a trustworthy assignment to the index resection specimen under a frozen position-aware parser and blinded adjudication. Missing grade, positive-ungraded MVI, conflict, uncertain specimen linkage, or inadequate documented sampling is `V=0`, never an exclusion.

Assign grade only from the immediate selected value following an anchored field such as `MVI提示风险分级[:：]`, stopping before definitions, explanatory bullets, historical clauses, or a new specimen section, or from a frozen synonymous selected-value construction accepted during development review. Handle negation, `未见`, multiple specimens, and discordance. Unanchored “M2,” binary MVI, absent language, boilerplate definitions, and conflicts cannot assign grade.

Two independently implemented parsers must agree on locked test records. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, every test `V=0`, and at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 sampled by year/template; a third resolves disagreements. Require class PPV ≥0.98, sensitivity ≥0.95, Cohen kappa ≥0.90, and parser disagreement ≤5%. Review may derive grade only from explicit focus-number/distance language under the frozen definition.

For every `V=0` test patient, all estimands enumerate `y in {0,1}`; for `V=1`, use adjudicated Y. The denominator N is the entire target frame. No uncertain patient is dropped or imputed. Raw slides, specimen IDs, block counts, vessel-distance measurements, and sampling protocols are unavailable, so the endpoint is documentary index-specimen M2, not latent biological M2.

## Report-level units and partial-visibility states

After exact-row deduplication of `examinations`:

1. Form an acquisition group by `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号,开始时间)`. If `检查号` is empty, the source row is its own acquisition group. Resolve CT/MRI membership from the frozen exact-`检查` dictionary; mixed/ambiguous groups fail dictionary review rather than being silently assigned.
2. Select the latest eligible CT group and latest eligible MRI group by `开始时间`, then `检查号` and normalized `检查` for ties.
3. Within each selected group, collapse only rows with identical normalized `(检查,检查所见,检查诊断)`. Every distinct triple is a separate **report unit**, identified internally by a salted hash of the grouping keys and triple; raw text or hash is never a predictor or public output. This conservative split avoids assuming that nonidentical source rows were finalized/viewed together. If a source-system data dictionary later proves a shared report identifier and shared release/view clock, changing the unit requires a Lead-approved child.
4. Empty-body units have state zero only. Let `q_i` be the number of selected units with nonempty eventual text and `Omega_i^R={0,1}^{q_i}`. A bit means that exact report unit is exposed; zero means unavailable. No probability, independence, modality symmetry, or relation to outcome is assumed.
5. At zero, mask all body-derived items from that unit: size, count, thrombus, formatting, and semantic findings. Preserve only acquisition-safe facts: modality from `检查`, acquisition time, selected acquisition count, source-row/report-unit multiplicity, distinct-`检查号` count without the identifier, and development-frequency machine category. At one, expose that unit's stored body. Aggregate visible report variables by frozen rules: modality-specific extraction first, then maximum documented lesion diameter, highest documented count category, any explicit macrovascular thrombus, and present/absent/unknown semantic coding with conflict mapped to unknown. A hidden unit contributes nothing, not an availability proxy.
6. The same unit ordering and state r are used for every compared score. Emit `q_i`, `2^{q_i}`, multirow groups, distinct-body units, and partial states after final filters. If the prespecified exact-state budget is exceeded, the analysis is inconclusive; the compiler may implement an algebraically exact optimizer but may not merge units.

The Episode-12 broad audit found no multirow selected group and only 158 dual-unit patients, supporting computational feasibility but not authorizing atomic grouping or replacing final counts.

Laboratory rows may be audited only when valid `检验时间` lies in `[t_dec-30 days,t_dec)`. No laboratory field or derivative—including existence, assay, specimen, value, count, missingness, or time—may enter a primary matrix, model, state, threshold, stratum, or gate. Execution must prove zero lab lineage.

## Models and semantic-content attribution

All primary models are interpretable elastic-net logistic regressions using patient-wise worst-state convex loss in 2015–2018, worst-state 2019 tuning and recalibration, every bootstrap refit, and every matched-null refit.

`B` contains age, sex, acquisition-safe CT/MRI facts and machine-frequency category, plus—only from exposed units—frozen maximum lesion diameter, lesion-count category, and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus with unknown indicators.

`S` is exactly B plus semantic block `C_R`: frozen present/absent/unknown extraction for capsule complete/interrupted/absent, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite or peritumoral lesion/enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity.

`S0` is never refitted. Score it using S's exact coefficients, intercept, preprocessing, selected penalty, and recalibration while setting only `C_R` to its state-zero reference vector; all B-type report variables remain at the same state. Therefore `score_S(i,r)=score_S0(i,r)` whenever every semantic unit is masked. This invariant isolates action changes caused by runtime semantic values inside the fitted S pipeline. It does not establish causal or biological meaning.

Retain the parent's workflow/provenance `W` and nested `A=W+C_R` as secondary process-confounding diagnostics with the same unit masks and robust fitting. Unrestricted text remains a diagnostic ceiling and cannot rescue primary claims.

For model M, fit development coefficients by minimizing
`n_dev^{-1} sum_i max_{r in Omega_i^R} ell(y_i, eta_Mi(r)) + elastic_net_penalty`.
Use one epigraph variable per patient or an algebraically equivalent exact convex implementation; 2019 tuning minimizes patient-wise worst-state loss and recalibration minimizes the same objective with slope constrained nonnegative. Equal-state, all-report, and no-report fits are diagnostics only.

Two blinded radiologists review extraction reliability by year, modality, and one-versus-multiple-unit provenance. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in ≥90%, count-category agreement ≥90% and kappa ≥0.80, and per-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity ≥0.85 for every binary semantic feature. This validates stored-text extraction, not visibility.

## Estimands and the report-unit frontier

At threshold p, let `w=p/(1-p)`, `I_Mi(r,p)=1[score_Mi(r)>=p]`, and for contrast `j=(M1,M0)`
`d_i^j(y,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)] [I_M1i(r,p)-I_M0i(r,p)]`.

Primary contrasts are semantic content `D=(S,S0)` and complete strategy `C=(S,B)`. Also compute S versus flag-all and flag-none. A–W and S–W are descriptive.

**All-stored-report potential.** Evaluate D and C with every nonempty selected report unit exposed. This tests P only.

**Primary report-unit coverage frontier.** Let `b_i(r)` be the number of exposed report units, `Q=sum_i q_i`, and for every integer `h=0,...,Q`, `rho=h/Q`:
`L_j^R(h,p)=N^{-1} min sum_i d_i^j(y_i,r_i,p)`,
`U_j^R(h,p)=N^{-1} max sum_i d_i^j(y_i,r_i,p)`,
subject to `sum_i b_i(r_i)>=h`, shared r within each contrast, adjudicated Y for V=1, and `y_i in {0,1}` for V=0. Compute this exactly by multiple-choice cardinality dynamic programming over visible report counts, or an algebraically equivalent exact algorithm validated against brute-force fixtures. No weighting or visibility probability is allowed. Coordinatewise extrema need not describe one latent world.

**Secondary patient-any frontier.** Recompute the parent's `a_i(r)=1[b_i(r)>0]` frontier over at least k visible patients. It quantifies how much the answer changes under the coarser guarantee but cannot establish report-unit deployment sufficiency. Report the gap between frontiers and the distribution of zero/partial/full visibility states.

At p=0.30 define `rho*` as the smallest attainable h/Q such that, for every h'≥h: simultaneous lower confidence limits for D and C exceed 0.01; the simultaneous lower net benefit of S exceeds both defaults; and point lower bounds for D and C remain positive at every p=0.25,...,0.35. If no h qualifies, `rho*` is undefined. Checking every higher h prevents selection from a nonmonotone frontier.

The external audit needed to test R must recover report final/release time and preferably clinician-view time for every selected report unit in a patient-clustered sample from the same workflow/calendar domain; reproduce the exact index, `t_dec`, imaging windows, acquisition selection, report-unit split, and empty-text handling; and report unit-level visibility plus patient-level any/partial/full visibility overall and by CT/MRI. Order/accession/acquisition time and eventual nonempty text are insufficient. Use a one-sided 97.5% patient-clustered lower confidence bound for report-unit coverage. Compare it with a 97.5% simultaneous model/frontier bound so Bonferroni controls the joint error at ≤5%; if samples overlap or components are dependent, use prespecified joint clustered resampling. A patient-any audit alone cannot support R.

## Inference, gates, and falsification zones

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps resampling within calendar period. Rebuild report units and states, refit preprocessing and robust B/S/W/A models, retune and recalibrate, reconstruct same-fit S0, and recompute all-report and both frontiers. Construct one max-deviation simultaneous family over lower/upper endpoints for D, C, S–flag-all, and S–flag-none at every attainable report coverage h, the frozen patient-coverage summary grid, and thresholds 0.20–0.40. Report pointwise intervals only as descriptive. More than 2.5% failed fits/replicates fails inference.

Run 1,000 process-matched semantic-null repetitions by permuting the entire `C_R` block within frozen year, modality provenance, report-unit cardinality, and formatting-family availability in development/tuning only; robustly refit and reconstruct S0. Observed all-report D at 0.30 must exceed the 97.5th percentile. Label-permutation and post-cutoff leakage fixtures must behave as null and leakage controls.

Global interpretation requires test N≥1,000; at least 75 adjudicated V=1 M2 outcomes; V=1≥85%; review of every test V=0; all pathology/radiology reliability gates; eventual text in ≥80% of selected units; size/count jointly extractable in ≥70%; no required-feature missingness shift >15 percentage points from 2019 to either test year; at least 100 patients and 20 V=1 M2 in any claimed CT-only/MRI-only stratum; exact convergence; complete simultaneous inference; shared states; and zero forbidden/laboratory lineage. Among V=1 report all-report/no-report AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV. A global strategy claim additionally requires slope 0.80–1.20, intercept -0.10–0.10, sensitivity ≥0.70, and PPV ≥0.50 at 0.30 in both diagnostics.

- **Supportive P:** every gate passes; all-report simultaneous lower limits for D and C exceed 0.01 at 0.30; threshold-band point lower bounds are positive; S beats both defaults; and the semantic-null test passes. Conclude only that stored report semantics produced materially favorable action changes in the frozen S pipeline and the complete S pipeline beat B in this historical documentary-M2 frame under maximal stored-report exposure.
- **Adverse:** gates pass and the simultaneous upper limit for all-report D is ≤0.01 (no clinically material content gain), or ≤0 (no favorable gain); similarly classify C. A negative upper bound supports harm at the stated threshold. Favorable AUC cannot overturn adverse net benefit.
- **Mixed:** D passes but C fails (semantic values change S favorably, but the complete strategy does not beat optimized B); C passes but D fails (refitting/other strategy differences, not demonstrated semantic-content value); report-unit and patient-any frontiers diverge; CT and MRI differ; or results change sign across 0.25–0.35. Report the conflict without a global/deployment claim.
- **Inconclusive:** any gate fails, intervals span the margin, V=0 bounds dominate, inference fails, `rho*` is undefined, or state complexity exceeds the exact budget. This is not evidence of no effect.
- **Supportive R:** P is supportive, `rho*` exists, and a conforming external report-unit audit has a joint-error-controlled lower coverage bound ≥`rho*`. Even then conclude only availability-robust documentary-M2 review value in the audited workflow—not treatment or patient benefit.
- **Adverse/inconclusive R:** an audit upper bound below `rho*` is adverse for that workflow; absent or mismatched report clocks/units leave R inconclusive regardless of P.

## Compiler and verifier contract

The compiler must preserve the first-resection frame, t_dec/windows, temporal split, documentary Y/V and whole-frame denominator, report-unit construction, exact partial states, zero-lab lineage, robust fitting, independently optimized B, fitted S, same-fit S0, D/C/default contrasts, all-report potential, report-unit primary frontier, patient-any secondary frontier, simultaneous clinical-margin inference, and interpretation zones. Reverting to atomic nonidentical accession rows, patient-any coverage as the deployment criterion, a visibility distribution, independently refitting S0, or treating eventual text as visible changes the science and requires Lead approval.

Machine-checkable claims include source/header/hash agreement; exact dictionaries; dedup/joins; periods/windows; report-unit keys and hashes; state masks/order; same state within contrasts; forbidden-feature lineage; worst-state objective arithmetic; S0 identity; score/action equality with C_R masked; Y/V enumeration and whole-frame N; report-count dynamic programming versus brute force; `rho*`; bootstrap family; semantic-null control; and conclusion-to-output links.

Verifier fixtures must include correct supportive P/R, adverse, mixed, and inconclusive outputs; one- versus two-report patients; CT-only, MRI-only, both, neither, and partial visibility; nonidentical rows sharing accession/time; external report-unit audit present/absent; and differing patient-any versus report-unit frontiers. Reject separately refitted S0, atomic bundling of nonidentical rows, fabricated report times, eventual-text-equals-visible, equal-state weighting, complete-case V, mismatched states, pointwise-as-simultaneous inference, cherry-picked coverage/threshold, patient-audit-only deployment, favorable AUC with adverse net benefit, or numerically correct outputs followed by treatment, recurrence, survival, biological-M2, contemporary, transport, threshold-acceptable, clinician-benefit, or unconditional deployment claims.

An automatic verifier cannot establish Chinese clinical semantics, reviewer qualifications, actual report finalization/view, specimen linkage, sampling adequacy, biological M2, threshold/workload acceptability, clinician action, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, or patient benefit. These require clinical adjudication, source-system evidence, stakeholder review, prospective workflow/outcome data, or another study. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility and justified conclusion linkage, not scientific truth.
