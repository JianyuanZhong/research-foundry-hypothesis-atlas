# D2-T2: MerTK-inhibitor-associated fibroblast activation versus macrophage-linked protective state

Parent: [prior hypothesis] (D2-T2 seed)
Status: concrete hypothesis-stage proposal; no full analysis executed yet.

## Question and target

Does the observed association between MerTK-inhibitor treatment and a harmful fibroblast program persist when the comparison is restricted within donor and contrasts a macrophage-linked protective state with a non-protective or pathogenic state?

Named target/pathway: MerTK and the downstream MerTK signaling axis, interpreted as an intervention-associated pathway rather than a proven direct target. The experiment tests whether pathway-associated expression changes can distinguish reduction of a fibroblast activation signature from loss of a macrophage-linked protective signature.

Clinical importance: fibrosis treatments that suppress fibroblast activation may also suppress macrophage states that support repair or containment of injury. A donor-aware comparison can identify whether an intervention-associated expression pattern is compatible with benefit without erasure of a protective state, or whether it is indistinguishable from a harmful state.

## Outcomes

- Supportive: the MerTK-pathway-associated expression score is higher in the protective macrophage-linked state than in the non-protective or pathogenic state, after adjustment for donor and composition.
- Adverse: the MerTK-pathway-associated expression score is lower in the protective macrophage-linked state than in the non-protective or pathogenic state, after adjustment for donor and composition.
- Inconclusive: the difference is not statistically significant or the confidence interval includes zero.

## Competing explanations

- The observed association may be driven by donor-specific variation rather than a true biological effect.
- The observed association may be driven by cell-type composition rather than a true biological effect.
- The observed association may be driven by treatment rather than a true biological effect.

## Falsification

The hypothesis is falsified if the MerTK-pathway-associated expression score is not higher in the protective macrophage-linked state than in the non-protective or pathogenic state, after adjustment for donor and composition.

## Limitations

- The experiment is observational and does not establish causality.
- The experiment does not establish selectivity of the MerTK pathway.
- The experiment does not establish clinical benefit or harm.
