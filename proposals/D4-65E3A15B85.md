# Episode 8: Does creatinine–cystatin C discordance change kidney-risk decisions beyond creatinine-only assessment?

## Parent, evidence boundary and unresolved question

Parent: `[prior hypothesis]`. This is a targeted clinical-value repair of the valid UKB discordance-by-grip proposal.

The strongest supported claim is narrower than the motivating physiology. The locally read Haines et al. critical-illness study (CJASN 2023; PMID 37256861) compared creatinine, cystatin C, measured GFR and muscle measures in mechanically ventilated patients. It supports a plausible concern that creatinine-based estimates may behave differently from cystatin-C estimates when muscle reserve is low. It does not establish a UKB community association, an interaction with grip, validity of a hospital code as AKI, or clinical utility. A current Europe PMC search also identified a UKB eGFR-difference/dementia association study (PMID 41790841), but only its search metadata/abstract excerpt was inspected; it does not establish this renal endpoint or decision question.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> In UKB participants with valid baseline creatinine, cystatin C, BMI and bilateral grip, without a previously recorded paired N17/N18 hospital diagnosis, adding eGFRcys−eGFRcr to a creatinine-based assessment produces reproducible incremental five-year risk-decision information for a first subsequently recorded paired N17 diagnosis, with the largest increment among participants with low grip.

This is a prognostic and decision-analytic claim about a recorded hospital-code endpoint. It is not a claim about true filtration, muscle mass, AKI stage, chronicity, causality, treatment benefit, or clinical utility. The hypothetical action is “trigger confirmatory cystatin-C review or enhanced kidney surveillance”; no such action, cost, benefit, or clinician threshold is observed. Therefore decision-curve thresholds are analytic stress tests, not clinical thresholds.

The substantive advance over the parent is to separate four questions that an association coefficient alone conflates: what creatinine-only assessment predicts, what BMI/grip adds, what cystatin discordance adds, and whether the discordance-by-grip interaction changes held-out decisions. A null incremental result is clinically informative because it argues against ordering or surveillance based on this signal in this selected population.

## Falsification, specificity and transportability

Prespecify:

- strict equal-suffix code/date and biomarker/index compatibility audits;
- same-day death ordering reversal;
- N17-only versus N17/N18 baseline exclusion;
- a two-year survivor/landmark analysis excluding N17, N18 and death through two years, restarting follow-up then; this is a changed survivor population, not a replication;
- raw-biomarker sensitivity and repeat biomarker sensitivity only if repeat timing is verified;
- within-sex-by-age-stratum permutation of (D) with a frozen seed, retaining its marginal distribution; remaining D decision value indicates leakage/implementation problems;
- one nonrenal negative control, preselected as K35 appendicitis, only if its exact code meaning, paired dates and event support are verified. A comparable K35 increment weakens renal specificity but does not prove confounding; absent support leaves specificity unresolved; and
- the combined-eGFR comparator as a clinical-mechanism falsification of the claim that discordance itself is needed.

Supportive evidence requires all provenance/support gates; positive (Delta INB_D) with a bootstrap interval excluding zero for the prespecified integrated range; calibrated predictions; a reproducible low-grip concentration and/or positive (I_5); persistence under strict timing and landmark sensitivity; no comparable supported K35 increment; and, if fitted, a stable learned surface. This supports only a selected-cohort association and conditional decision-analytic value under an explicit hypothetical action.

Adverse evidence is a null/opposite (Delta INB_D), no incremental information from (M2) or (M3), a result that disappears under strict timing/landmarking, comparable K35 information, instability or poor calibration, or a combined-eGFR model that fully subsumes the discordance signal. Inconclusive evidence includes unavailable code semantics, units, assay/index timing, death meaning, administrative cutoff, inadequate events/quadrants/test support, failed censoring/calibration diagnostics, or an unsupported negative control. A correct computation cannot establish clinical utility when action benefits and harms are unavailable.

## Clinical limits, transportability and deliverable

The cohort is a complete-case biomarker/assessment subset of UKB, not a representative UK population. The source cannot establish measured GFR, muscle mass, inflammation, AKI severity/KDIGO stage, CKD chronicity, outpatient disease absence, treatment context, causal effects, actual clinician decisions, cost-effectiveness, or transportability. Stronger conclusions require expert adjudication of longitudinal creatinine/urine output and diagnosis, measured GFR or a valid reference, body-composition/inflammatory data, assay and timing documentation, an independent cohort, clinical utility elicitation, and prospective decision-impact evaluation.

The new scientific deliverable is not merely a coefficient. It is a reproducible four-model decision-information analysis: provenance/join/event-support manifest; eligible flow and missingness; newly computed eGFRcr, eGFRcys, (D), combined eGFR and transformations; held-out five-year CIFs; (Delta INB_D), interaction/total increments, calibrated threshold tables and uncertainty; (I_5)/N18; mechanistic comparator outputs; falsification diagnostics; and the learned surface only if gated. Every conclusion must identify its computed output and remain within the recorded-code evidence boundary.
