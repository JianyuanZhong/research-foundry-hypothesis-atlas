# Common-fallback report-semantics capability for preoperative M2 triage

## Episode-11 advance and residual threat

The parent asks whether independently fitted semantic strategy `S` beats independently fitted structured strategy `B` by at least 0.01 net-benefit units at threshold 0.30 uniformly over every radiology-report availability state. The same construction is used for `A-W`. This leaves a substantive attribution and deployment flaw.

When every selected report is unavailable, `S` and `B` receive the same non-semantic patient information, but their predictions need not agree because adding `C_R` during a separate fit can change the intercept, age, machine, modality, size/count/thrombus coefficients, penalty, and recalibration. Thus a nonzero no-report `S-B` contribution can be generated without a single semantic value being visible. The analogous problem affects `A-W`. A favorable worst-state bound can therefore be driven by refitting shared predictors rather than by the operational availability of report semantics. The parent's matched semantic permutation does not force equality in the no-report state and cannot repair this policy definition.

This child imposes a **common fallback**. It fits `B` once and freezes it. The semantic strategy `S†` is that exact `B` policy plus a gated, radiology-semantic offset; if no report is available, the gate is zero and `score_S†=score_B` bit-for-bit. Likewise, `A†` is frozen `W` plus the identical semantic-offset design and equals `W` when no report is available. Because a genuine semantic add-on cannot improve a decision when no report is visible, the parent's strictly positive claim over a state space containing no-report is retired. The primary estimand becomes worst-state incremental capability when at least one selected report is available; the full deployment envelope remains explicitly unidentified.

This changes the estimand, fitting analysis, and falsification logic. It is not a compiler repair. The first-resection population, clocks/windows, pathology-only documentary M2 endpoint, zero laboratory lineage, B and `C_R` variables, calendar split, thresholds, whole-frame denominator, pathology uncertainty, report-state enumeration, reliability requirements, and bounded non-treatment claims remain unchanged.

## Report states and common-fallback policies

Let `q_i` be the selected bundles with nonempty eventual text and `Omega_i={0,1}^{q_i}`. A bit is one only in the counterfactual state where that report is visible at `t_dec`; no probability, independence, or monotonicity is assumed. Empty text contributes no bit. At zero, mask every report-body derivative. Acquisition time, modality, bundle count, and development-frequency machine category remain. Define `Omega_i^+={r in Omega_i:sum(r)>0}`; if `q_i=0`, it is empty and incremental contribution is zero.

`B` is unchanged: age, sex, modality/acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category, and—only from available reports—frozen maximum lesion diameter, count category, and explicit macrovascular thrombus with unknown indicators. Fit B using the parent's exact patient-wise worst-state elastic-net objective over all `Omega_i`. Select its penalty by 2019 worst-state loss. The selected development linear predictor is the offset used to fit development `gamma`; 2019 then supplies B's frozen recalibration and all semantic-penalty/scalar selection. The final calibrated B preprocessing, coefficients, penalty, intercept, and slope are immutable in S†.

`C_R` is unchanged: report-available present/absent/unknown extraction for complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity. It contains no unrestricted text, format, year, diagnosis, procedure, pathology/MVI term, lab field, accession, raw machine ID, or post-cutoff content.

Create `z_Ci(r)` from C_R only, with no intercept or availability column; every component is zero for unavailable reports. Fit a penalized semantic offset on development patients with `q_i>0`, holding frozen B as offset:

`gamma_hat=argmin_gamma n_+^-1 sum_i max_{r in Omega_i^+} ell(y_i,eta_Bi(r)+z_Ci(r)'gamma)+P(gamma)`.

Tune the semantic penalty on 2019 worst-positive-state loss. A final nonnegative scalar `c` may be fitted in 2019 on the semantic increment only, with no intercept and no change to B:

`eta_S†i(r)=eta_Bi(r)+1[sum(r)>0]c z_Ci(r)'gamma_hat`, `c>=0`.

Therefore `eta_S†i(0)=eta_Bi(0)` exactly. Re-estimating a B coefficient, adding an availability intercept, or recalibrating the whole S† score is forbidden.

`W` is the parent's workflow control: B plus acquisition count/time, reviewed non-semantic protocol from exact `Examination`, and—when visible—character/line/section counts and outcome-blind formatting family. Fit/tune/recalibrate/freeze W. Construct `A†` from W using identical `z_C`, grid, objective, and scalar rule; A†=W at no-report. W/A† are not deployable.

Keep independently refitted robust S/A as diagnostic `J` and report no-report score/action differences. Keep equal-weight, all-report, no-report, and unrestricted-text fits diagnostic only. None may support C+/A+.

Two blinded radiologists retain the parent's review: at least 200 reports for size/count/thrombus and, in each test year and CT/MRI provenance, at least 150 enriched for each C_R feature and parser-negative examples. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in ≥90%, count agreement ≥90% with kappa ≥0.80, and per-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity ≥0.85 per binary C_R feature.
