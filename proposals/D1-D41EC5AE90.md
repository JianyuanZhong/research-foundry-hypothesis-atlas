# Retention-decomposed postoperative HCC surveillance selection

Status: substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. A bounded aggregate audit was executed against the unchanged cohort and source snapshot; no hypothesis model was fitted. The fixed outcome-independent risk set is preserved. Biological recurrence remains outside the experiment.

## Scientific opening, claim hierarchy, and advance

The strongest evidence-supported clinical claim is that, in 549 selected resected HCC patients with preoperative AFP above 400 ng/mL, incomplete AFP response measured at a 4–6-week postoperative visit was associated with worse recurrence-free and overall survival [K1]. This does not establish that persistent AFP is residual tumor, that availability of follow-up AFP is ignorable, or that changing surveillance improves outcomes.

Routine EHR observation is produced by care. In 669,452 patients at two hospitals, the presence and timing of common laboratory orders were strongly associated with survival independently of recorded values [K2]. A methodological review distinguishes patient presence from longitudinal informative observation, describes explicit observation-process and latent-structure approaches, and warns that prediction and explanation need different interpretations [K3]. These works support treating observation as data; they do not identify why a particular postoperative HCC patient was measured.

The unresolved claim is: among adults at a day-60 landmark after first observed HCC resection, does pre-landmark marker history predict subsequent HCC-targeted measurement beyond (i) continued local-system contact, (ii) general inpatient testing, (iii) liver-injury monitoring, and (iv) oncology-panel bundling? A secondary question asks whether it predicts hepatic rather than chest CT/MR. The leading explanation is residual target-specific surveillance selection. Rivals are patient retention or outside-care loss, ordinary severity/contact, same-encounter panel bundling, service/era workflow, and timestamp misalignment.

The parent correctly put all patients on an outcome-independent 44-week grid, but treated one generic-lab and one pooled nonhepatic-imaging pair as if they were exchangeable negative controls. The present advance is a prespecified decomposition: preserve the full grid for total local capture; separately model observed contact without censoring; and require target specificity to survive a hierarchy of comparators matched on care setting, organ context, oncology workflow, modality, and calendar. Imaging is secondary because the audit shows weak patient overlap between hepatic and pooled nonhepatic imaging. No negative control is assumed exchangeable merely because it is not the target.

This is an ascertainment study. It does not test recurrence, tumor biology, treatment benefit, or clinician intent.

## Hypotheses and estimands

Let H be the pre-landmark marker-history exposure. Among marker-observed patients, primary continuous inputs are preoperative within-label/era rank and the latest day-15:60 to preoperative within-label log ratio; a train-frozen high/persistent versus low/clearing contrast is used for standardization. Marker-observed versus marker-absent is a separate full-cohort ascertainment-state contrast. No value is imputed for absent patients, and raw values are never pooled across marker labels or eras because assay units/platforms are unavailable.

Every eligible patient contributes the same 44 weekly bins from day 61 through day 365. Let A(t) be encounter-only contact: an interval from admission/visit/start through valid discharge overlaps week t, falling back to the encounter date when an interval cannot be formed. A0 uses encounter start only. A is a modeled mark/covariate, never an eligibility restriction. Labs, imaging, procedures, medications, targets, and controls never create or extend A. “No local mark” means no mark in these institutional files, not alive, disease-free, retained, or absent from outside care.

Primary laboratory estimand:

- total local-capture specificity on the full grid, Δ_LM:k = [R_LM(H_high)−R_LM(H_low)]−[R_k(H_high)−R_k(H_low)], for each comparator k separately: generic labs N-L (sodium/creatinine/hemoglobin), liver-injury labs L-I (ALT/AST/bilirubin/albumin/INR/platelets), and workflow-matched other tumor-marker panel O-TM (exact CEA and CA19-9 labels after review);
- standardized 365-day risks, risk ratios, ratio-of-risk-ratios, and patient-cluster bootstrap intervals for every component;
- a decomposition into H association with observed contact A and, secondarily, H association with mark choice among A=1 weeks. The latter is not substituted for the full-grid estimand.

O-TM is a workflow comparator, not a biological negative control: CEA/CA19-9 may be ordered for differential diagnosis, another cancer, or a panel. L-I can reflect hepatic severity. N-L can reflect admission intensity. Concordance across all three is required to separate target-specific measurement from these rivals.

Secondary imaging estimand compares hepatic/upper-abdominal CT/MR S with chest CT/MR N-I-chest on the full grid and among A=1. Pooled nonhepatic imaging is descriptive only. Chest imaging is the only supportable modality-matched comparator, but it can itself be cancer staging; therefore even a positive contrast establishes anatomical targeting in this record, not clinician motive or recurrence.

Predictor history is day −90 through +60; outcomes and A are day 61 through 365. The day-60 landmark prevents predictor/outcome overlap. ## Executed bounded audit

The attached aggregate-only audit ran on HCC snapshot `[source checksum]` using 8 CPUs/64 GiB in 100 seconds. It reproduced N=15,575 and the parent’s mark counts; it did not estimate H associations.

Administrative boundary: every index date had at least 365 days before the observed maximum of encounters, labs, examinations, procedures, and medications. Minimum index+365-to-table-maximum margins were 369.8, 368.9, 370.2, 369.7, and 432.0 days, respectively. This rejects simple file-end right truncation as the explanation for absent one-year marks, but table maxima do not prove complete capture.

Retention: only 1,975/15,575 (12.7%) patients had any A after day 60; 13,600 had none. Any encounter occurred in 6.24%, 5.76%, and 6.51% of patients during days 61–151, 152–242, and 243–365. Annual any-A rates varied from 6.25% (2012, n=80) to 17.66% (2020), with 10.8–13.6% in most years. Pre- and post-landmark marker-observed patients had any-A rate 26.6%, versus 11.5% when neither period had a marker. These are availability-state descriptions, not marker effects. They establish that local retention is sparse and heterogeneous and cannot be repaired by censoring at last contact.

Comparator support:

- L-M and N-L occurred in 1,554 and 1,645 patients; 1,548 overlapped (patient Jaccard 0.938), with 3,133 same-week co-occurrences. They are strongly workflow-matched, but N-L had 777 A=1 weeks without L-M versus 76 L-M weeks without N-L, so bundling and asymmetric sensitivity must be modeled. L-M/L-I overlap is similarly high (1,552 patients; Jaccard 0.941; patient-week Jaccard 0.785).
- S and pooled N-I occurred in 478 and 498 patients; only 222 overlapped (Jaccard 0.294), with 196 same-week co-occurrences. Pooled N-I is not exchangeable enough for a single primary imaging contrast.
- Chest imaging accounts for 511/642 audited nonhepatic rows and is the only plausible modality comparator; however S/chest patient overlap remains only 204 (Jaccard 0.287; patient-week Jaccard 0.194), so imaging remains secondary. Rarer neuro, musculoskeletal, pelvic, and other strata are descriptive.
- O-TM has 9,436 rows/1,526 patients, led by exact labels “糖类抗原19-9” (4,075 rows/1,519 patients) and “癌胚抗原” (4,065/1,524). L-M/O-TM patient overlap is 1,524 (Jaccard 0.979) and patient-week Jaccard is 0.898, making it the closest workflow comparator after blinded label/purpose review.
- Target and comparator rows are concentrated in the same hepatobiliary oncology services, but timing differs: median days from encounter start are 1.85 for L-M, 2.80 for N-L, 2.81 for L-I, 1.15 for O-TM, 1.10 for S, and 1.12 for chest imaging. Four S and one chest-imaging row precede the linked encounter start by implausible years and must be quarantined and reviewed.

These observations justify a child because they falsify administrative truncation as the dominant rival, demonstrate unresolved local attrition, and replace an overstrong pooled-control interpretation with a matched comparator hierarchy.

## Frozen split and shared preprocessing

Assign patients by `int(SHA256(患者主索引)[:8],16) mod 100`: 0–59 train, 60–69 validation, 70–79 locked test, 80–99 untouched reserve. Freeze cohort, dictionaries, comparator roles, transformations, thresholds, and model selection before test access. Both methods use the identical 44-week grid, patients, time-varying inputs, and split.

Binary weekly marks are A, L-M, N-L, L-I, O-TM, S, and N-I-chest; multiple same-mark rows collapse within week, while co-occurring different marks remain multilabel. Features available through t− only include lagged A/mark counts and recency; baseline pathology/severity, age, sex, index year, service, index stay, prior treatment; pre-landmark injury/marker observation; exact label/specimen; and PIVKA-interference flags. Contemporaneous A is allowed only in the decomposed choice analysis. Primary full-grid models use lagged A only. Quarantine timestamp-incoherent rows before fitting and report them.

Calendar is not a nuisance scalar: use week spline, index-year strata (2012–16, 2017–19, 2020–21, 2022–23), year-by-mark interactions, and service-by-mark interactions. Report within-era effects and leave-one-era-out stress fits; no era may be silently pooled after a sign reversal.

## Transparent baseline

Fit a stacked discrete-time recurrent-mark model on all 685,300 weeks, with mark-type intercepts, common week spline/covariate basis, marker-history × mark interactions, comparator-specific era/service interactions, and patient-cluster-robust inference. Fit A separately on the same full grid using baseline and lagged history; do not inverse-weight or censor the primary analysis by fitted retention. Standardize risks by g-computation over the empirical covariate distribution and observed lagged histories; use patient bootstrap intervals.

Report:

1. H-to-A association and calibration;
2. full-grid component risks and Δ for L-M versus N-L, L-I, and O-TM;
3. secondary S versus N-I-chest;
4. A=1 mark-choice contrasts with identical comparator denominators;
5. calibration-in-the-large/slope, Brier/log loss by mark, split, era, and service;
6. sensitivity to lagged-A-only, A0, within-patient weeks, pre-first-T follow-up, broad therapy exclusion, and exclusion of 2020.

The baseline transparently reveals which comparator rejects specificity. It loses persistent unobserved retention/panel states, exact ordering within a week, and residual covariance among marks.

## Substantive learned/mechanistic alternative

Fit a joint semi-Markov multistate marked-process model on the identical grid and split. It is a mechanistic model of the *recording process*, not disease biology.

- U is a generic local-contact state anchored positively by A and permitted to load on every mark.
- Q is an organ/severity monitoring state anchored by L-I and allowed to load on N-L and HCC targets.
- B is an oncology-panel/bundle state anchored by O-TM and permitted to load on L-M and imaging.
- V is an added HCC-target-enrichment state allowed to load on L-M and S but constrained to zero on A/N-L/L-I/O-TM/N-I-chest.
- Transitions use only baseline and t− history; index era/service enter U, Q, and B. H may affect U/Q/B in the rival model and V only in the expanded model.
- Weekly emissions are multilabel; A=0 target events remain observed. Exact event ordering is used in a secondary continuous-time emission likelihood.

Compare nested models U+Q+B (retention/severity/bundling rival) versus U+Q+B+V. The alternative can reveal whether residual target covariance persists after decomposing contact, hepatic monitoring, and oncology bundling—information compressed by the regression baseline. It cannot identify retention itself, clinician intent, outside care, or tumor biology.

Require null/shared-contact/target-specific simulation recovery, three-seed loading/state stability, 90–98% interval coverage, no label switching, and posterior predictive reproduction of A/target/control cross-cells by era and service. Select V only if it reduces validation target-mark deviance by ≥1% without worsening any comparator deviance by ≥1%, improves test target score directionally, and leaves calibrated comparator counts. Otherwise the transparent baseline is the completed analysis.

Planned future-solver resources: baseline 8–16 CPUs, ≤64 GiB, 1–2 hours; joint model one allocated A100, 16 CPUs, ≤128 GiB, 4–6 hours with checkpoints. These are unverified planning estimates within the 16-CPU/8-GPU/256-GiB/8-hour envelope. Discovery extraction measured 100 seconds on 8 CPUs/64 GiB. A CPU hierarchical marked-process fallback is acceptable if GPU dependencies fail. A generic transformer remains deferred because predictive gain would not distinguish retention, bundling, and target selection.

## Gates, falsification, and interpretation

Before fitting, require in train/validation/test separately: ≥20 patients for each primary mark; ≥100 effective exposed/comparator patients after overlap weighting diagnostics; no separation in label/era strata; and ≥100 reviewed records across L-M/N-L/L-I/O-TM plus ≥100 across S/N-I-chest. Reviewers blinded to H must confirm label, service, timestamp semantics, and whether event time plausibly follows encounter start. CEA/CA19-9 and chest imaging remain comparator marks only if ≥0.85 PPV for their prespecified label/anatomic class; intent need not and cannot be established. All impossible pre-entry times are quarantined.

Supportive for **laboratory target-specific selection** requires the L-M marker-history contrast to exceed each N-L, L-I, and O-TM contrast: Holm-adjusted 95% intervals for all three specificity differences exclude zero; each point risk-difference specificity is ≥0.02 and each ratio-of-risk-ratios ≥1.25; direction replicates in locked test; no era/service reverses direction; marker permutation within observation-state/era/service erases L-M specificity while preserving contact structure; and the result is not confined to A or 2020. The learned V result is corroborative only if recovery and held-out gates pass.

Supportive for **anatomical imaging targeting** requires analogous S>N-I-chest evidence with ≥1.25 ratio-of-risk-ratios and locked-test replication. It remains secondary and does not prove a surveillance purpose.

Adverse evidence is a precise result in which L-M does not materially exceed all three comparators, especially if O-TM or L-I equals/exceeds L-M, and the rival U+Q+B model matches the V model within 1% target deviance. This favors ordinary contact, severity monitoring, or panel bundling over target specificity. Similar S/chest effects are adverse to anatomical specificity.

Inconclusive evidence includes wide intervals spanning adverse/supportive regions; sparse overlap; dictionary/timestamp failure; sign reversal by era/service; poor calibration; unstable latent states; inability to distinguish H-to-A from H-to-target association; or discordance among comparators without a prespecified explanation. An imprecise null is not adverse. Because 87.3% lack post-landmark A, a full-grid association that disappears among contact strata must be reported as retention-dominated, not target-specific.

Administrative truncation is rejected only as a simple table-end explanation. Outside care, death, migration, and silent local care remain observationally indistinguishable. Resolving them requires linked mortality/claims/enrollment data or prospective scheduled follow-up.

## Alternatives retained and revisit triggers

- Fixed full grid: retained as primary; censoring at last contact and A=1-only eligibility remain rejected.
- Simple file-end truncation: audited and rejected as dominant explanation; complete capture is not claimed.
- Single N-L/N-I negative-control pair: rejected as overstrong. N-L, L-I, O-TM, and N-I-chest have distinct rival roles.
- Pooled nonhepatic imaging: descriptive only because patient overlap with S is weak.
- Transparent stacked baseline: mandatory and sufficient for completion if the learned model fails.
- U+Q+B+V joint model: selected only after recovery and target-specific held-out improvement.
- 14-day encounter window from the context sibling: retained as sensitivity, not ancestry or primary estimand, because it excludes unequal event shares and was selected after support inspection.
- Orders-based scheduled opportunity: revisit only after order-status/purpose adjudication.
- Biological recurrence: frozen until the inherited report-content gate or prospective/linked truth is available.
