## Unresolved claim and falsifiable test

The unresolved claim is that the positive-macrophage condition has a reproducible FLS response beyond donor, cell composition, treatment, batch, and assay artifacts. The planned test is a donor-aware comparison of macrophage-positive versus FLS-only and macrophage-positive versus macrophage-negative conditions, using the 33 condition-level measurements as the unit of analysis and treating repeated contexts as dependent observations. A simple baseline is a prespecified condition contrast with donor/context-aware permutation or mixed-effects uncertainty; a substantive alternative is a structured model that separates condition, donor, treatment, and repeated-measurement structure and tests whether the positive-macrophage signal is specific rather than a general donor or batch effect. The alternative can reveal whether apparent effects are condition-specific or merely shared across donors/contexts, which the simple contrast loses.

Supportive results would require a reproducible positive-macrophage-specific pattern with uncertainty that accounts for donor/context dependence and is not explained by the negative controls. Adverse results would be no reproducible contrast, equal or stronger effects in controls, or instability across donors. Inconclusive results would include insufficient independent donor information, unresolved feature mapping, or wide uncertainty; these do not refute the hypothesis.
