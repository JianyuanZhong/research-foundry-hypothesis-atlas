## Hypothesis to test

People with higher pre-illness levels of endothelial or coagulation-related proteins experience a larger increase in cardiovascular risk after infection.

## Study population and main data

Pre-illness proteomics, hospitalization for infection, and cardiovascular events.

## Suggested approach

Treat infection as a time-varying exposure and compare people hospitalized for infection with those hospitalized for noninfectious conditions.

## Scientific significance

Identify groups who may warrant closer follow-up after infection.

## Main limitations

Proteins measured before illness cannot be interpreted as mediators arising after infection.
