Among 158 complete triplets, M0 RMSE was 3.2677 and M1 RMSE was 3.1683. The primary normalized RMSE improvement was `0.02031`; normalized MAE improvement was `0.03251`. Arm-specific normalized RMSE improvements were `0.03256` in the 40 systemic rows and `0.01484` in the 118 comparator rows. Both bounded partition estimates were positive (`0.02031`, `0.01444`). The 20-resample fixed-pipeline bootstrap interval was `[-0.00558, 0.05070]` with one-sided Monte Carlo p-value `0.1429`, so it crosses zero and does not support a definitive positive increment. The observed estimate exceeded the 95th percentile of the 10-permutation linkage-destruction null, but ten permutations are only an execution check and not inferentially adequate.

### Total bilirubin (`总胆红素`)

Among 160 complete triplets, M0 RMSE was 9.8360 and M1 RMSE was 9.8909. The primary normalized RMSE change was `-0.00402`; normalized MAE change was `0.00656`. Arm-specific normalized RMSE changes were `-0.00550` in the 41 systemic rows and `-0.00315` in the 119 comparator rows. Both bounded partition estimates were negative (`-0.00402`, `-0.00678`). The 20-resample fixed-pipeline bootstrap interval was `[-0.02916, 0.01144]` with one-sided Monte Carlo p-value `0.5714`. The observed estimate did not exceed the 95th percentile of the 10-permutation null. Thus this bounded result provides no demonstrated incremental RMSE value for remote bilirubin history and is directionally adverse to the positive hypothesis, but it is not a precise-null result.

## Falsification and interpretation

The executable reconstruction assertions are computationally checkable and passed. The assay-specific positive hypothesis is not supported merely by a positive point estimate: it requires uncertainty and robustness results consistent with improvement. On this bounded run, albumin is suggestive but computationally inconclusive because its bootstrap interval crosses zero and the diagnostic breadth is deliberately small. Total bilirubin is directionally adverse/no-demonstrated-increment, also with an interval spanning zero. These divergent results reinforce why the assays must not be pooled.

Supportive full results would mean only that B reproducibly improves prediction of the next observed same-assay Y within this selected, complete-triplet cohort. Adverse full results would mean adding B worsens held-out prediction under the locked pipeline. Inconclusive results include intervals spanning zero, material partition/timing/influence sensitivity, or reconstruction failures. Clinical meaning, treatment administration, causal effects, clinical utility, and transportability require adjudication and additional data or another study and cannot be established by an automatic verifier.
