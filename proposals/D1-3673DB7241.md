# Episode 18 child: does the acidemic SpO2 phenotype predict mortality after a pre-index severity audit?

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

The selected Episode-17 proposal asks whether, among the first eligible acidemic paired oxygenation observations in the first 72 ICU hours, a recorded SpO2 of 94-95% rather than 96-97% is associated with at least a 10-percentage-point higher downstream hospital-mortality risk after overlap on hospital, charted FiO2, PaCO2, pH, and ICU day. Its audited construction is feasible (556 source observations, 440 earliest-stay indices, and 156 outcome-blind overlap stays), but that overlap does not include a global baseline-illness measure. The observed parent point diagnostic was -2.97 points and was deliberately not treated as an inferential result.

The unresolved successor claim is narrower and more clinically interpretable: after restricting time zero to strictly after the first 24 ICU hours, so that Apache IVa admission severity precedes the index, does the lower recorded acidemic SpO2 band still carry at least a 10-point higher observed hospital-mortality risk after balancing on baseline severity and index context?

This matters because a downstream risk signal that disappears or reverses after pre-index severity adjustment should not be promoted into risk recognition or selective blood-gas validation. Persistence would justify independent prospective validation of whether the paired phenotype adds prognostic information beyond standard early severity. Neither result establishes that SpO2, oxygen treatment, FiO2, acidemia, or blood-gas testing causes outcome differences.

## Evidence boundary

The strongest supported evidence before this experiment is not a mortality effect. It is that the parent’s exact, time-linked recorded phenotype is computable and that lower-band observations map differently to recorded PaO2 categories under measured overlap. The parent’s mortality point estimate was adverse-looking but had no bootstrap interval.

The successor tests whether a downstream association survives an explicit severity audit. Apache IVa acutephysiologyscore, apachescore, and predictedhospitalmortality are used only when the index occurs after 1,440 minutes. actualhospitalmortality, actualhospitallos, actualventdays, and all discharge-derived fields are excluded from covariates. The score is an admission/early-ICU summary, not a gold-standard baseline adjudication; it can still contain treatment-influenced components and does not eliminate confounding or ABG-selection bias.

External demonstrations motivate, but do not answer, this question. The inspected Ding and Chen Critical Care 2025 report used an early SF/PF oxygenation pair and 28-day mortality in MIMIC-IV, but did not test this EICU acidemic SpO2 contrast. The inspected Delgado report supports pH-dependent SpO2-PaO2 mapping in a different cohort. The inspected OXY-BREATHES review does not establish a causal oxygen target here. No unavailable supplement or main paper text is treated as inspected.

## Primary estimand and adjustment

The primary exposure is the recorded paired phenotype: lower SpO2 94-95% versus higher SpO2 96-97%, conditional on concurrent acidemia. It is not an assigned oxygen target.

Define an outcome-blind propensity score using only index-available variables: continuous Apache logit log((predictedhospitalmortality+0.005)/(1.005-predictedhospitalmortality)), FiO2, pH, PaCO2 with a missingness indicator, draw, and hospital fixed effects. Fit ridge logistic regression with prespecified C=1.0. The primary is symmetric stabilized inverse-odds weighting, normalized separately within band, with no outcome in model fitting. Do not trim, drop, or substitute observations because of outcome. Report propensity ranges, effective sample size, maximum row weight, hospital-weight fractions, covariate standardized mean differences, and whether any propensity is outside [0.05,0.95].

Estimate Delta_18 = P_w(hospital death | lower recorded band) - P_w(hospital death | higher recorded band). The locked materiality hypothesis is Delta_18 >= +0.10. The +10-point margin is a transparent decision anchor inherited from the parent; it is not a validated MCID, causal effect margin, or claim that a 10-point difference changes care. The primary bootstrap is a 5,000-replicate hierarchical hospital/person bootstrap, seed 20260918: resample hospitals with replacement, then uniquepid clusters within copied hospitals, carry all indexed stays per person, assign new copy IDs, refit the outcome-blind propensity model within each replicate, recompute weights and Delta, and report percentile two-sided 95% intervals. Never pool duplicate copies under original IDs.

## Baselines, sensitivity hierarchy, and feasibility gates

Report, but do not substitute, the crude lower-minus-higher risk difference; the parent’s original 0-72-hour symmetric-cell estimate; the complete 164-row landmark cohort before Apache linkage; C=0.1 and C=10 propensity fits; no-hospital-fixed-effect fit; and a model using Apache acute physiologic score rather than predicted risk. Each sensitivity must refit its adjustment and report missingness, overlap, ESS, and weight concentration.

Primary support requires all of:
- at least 140 Apache-complete indexed stays, at least 50 total deaths, and at least 25 stays in each band;
- at least 20 contributing hospitals;
- no group ESS below 50;
- <=10% failed bootstrap replicates;
- no propensity outside [0.05,0.95] after the locked C=1 fit;
- maximum hospital weight fraction <=50%;
- maximum absolute weighted standardized mean difference <=0.10 for Apache logit, FiO2, pH, PaCO2, and draw;
- no required sensitivity or leave-one-hospital-out estimate with at least 10 rows reverses the sign of Delta.

These are feasibility gates, not proof of adequate causal control.

The full-source audit verifies the support profile before bootstrapping: 158 Apache-complete rows, 99 higher and 59 lower, 60 deaths, 31 hospitals. In the outcome-blind propensity audit, C=1 produced ESS 56.20 lower and 95.16 higher, maximum normalized row weights 2.29% and 1.66%, maximum clinical covariate SMD 0.042, maximum site SMD 0.225, and one propensity outside [0.05,0.95]. The point RD was -9.60 percentage points lower-minus-higher. C=0.1 similarly balanced clinical covariates (max SMD 0.095), with RD -8.59 points; C=10 produced RD -13.67 and lower-band ESS 49.35, failing the ESS gate. These are feasibility/QC and sensitivity diagnostics, not inferential conclusions.

## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive: the primary 95% interval lower bound is >=+0.10, all feasibility gates pass, and all required sensitivities preserve a positive material contrast. Report only that the lower recorded acidemic phenotype is associated with at least 10 points higher observed hospital-mortality risk in this post-24-hour Apache-complete EICU target. The next step is prospective external validation of incremental prognostic value, not an oxygen-target or routine-ABG recommendation.

Adverse to the material hypothesis: the interval upper bound is <+0.10. If the interval is entirely below zero, report an opposite observed association; do not call it protection or recommend lower SpO2. If the interval crosses zero but remains below +0.10, report that the material claim is adverse while direction is unresolved.

Inconclusive: the interval includes +0.10, bootstrap/gate failure, sensitivity conflict, severe weight concentration, or insufficient post-24-hour Apache-complete overlap. The pilot audit’s negative point estimates do not by themselves establish no association. The parent’s 0-72-hour result cannot rescue this child, and the child cannot retroactively alter the parent.

The verifier must reject correct computation paired with claims of causal oxygen/FiO2 effect, lower/higher target recommendation, safety, harm, tissue hypoxia, oxidative injury, treatment benefit, device validity, prediction transportability, or routine blood-gas utility. It should accept correct adverse, supportive, and inconclusive interpretations tied to the computed interval and gates.

## Unavailable evidence and required next studies

EICU lacks verified arterial specimen identity, arterial saturation/co-oximetry, raw waveform/artifact flags, device calibration, delivered FiO2, exposure duration, clinician target intent, exact ventilation persistence, complete treatment-limitation adjudication, cause/exact death time, and post-discharge outcomes. Apache severity is not an adjudicated baseline and may include treatment-influenced physiology. The experiment cannot remove selection into blood-gas testing or residual confounding, prove prediction transportability, validate the +10 anchor, or establish clinical utility. Clinical review is required for specimen and ventilation truth, implausible values, and goals of care. Prospective synchronized ABG/waveform/delivered-FiO2 validation and independent prediction/decision-impact studies are required before any monitoring or treatment recommendation; a randomized assigned-target study is required for safety or benefit.
