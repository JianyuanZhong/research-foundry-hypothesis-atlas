## Hypothesis to test

The extent of deterioration in metabolic measures provides information about cardiovascular and kidney multimorbidity beyond their current levels.

## Study population and main data

Participants with repeated metabolic measurements.

## Suggested approach

Start follow-up at the second measurement and compare the information provided by changes with that provided by current levels.

## Scientific significance

Identify changes that precede disease progression.

## Main limitations

The repeat-measurement subset is smaller, and two measurements cannot establish a precise turning point.
