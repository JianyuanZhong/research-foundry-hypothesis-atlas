# Lead-time strata to distinguish report-to-summary race from persistent content discordance

## Decision question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The clinically consequential choice is whether a future pulmonary-nodule handoff intervention should first move the trigger earlier (radiology finalization and a true departure-time availability check), reconcile content in the discharge artifact despite adequate lead time, or defer to tracking/exchange/communication infrastructure. The assessed parent establishes a useful institutional calibration: a discharge `storetime` after `dischtime` is a storage-latency signal and must not be called a visibility or communication failure. It still leaves a mechanism ambiguity. A late or discordant discharge artifact may occur because the recommendation was stored only shortly before departure and the two documentation workflows had little observable lead time, or because the recommendation had been stored with ample lead time but was not faithfully propagated.

This successor adds a prespecified, within-opportunity chronology experiment. It does not change the inherited all-opportunity denominator or call a `storetime` an availability, signature, author-awareness, transmission, communication, adherence, causal, or benefit measure. It asks two falsifiable descriptive questions:

1. Is the delayed-summary state `A` concentrated when the recommendation component's observed storage precedes `D = dischtime` by only a short lead time, and does it persist when that lead is at least 72 hours?
2. Among summaries observed stored by `D`, does nodule-plan discordance remain high after the recommendation had at least 72 hours of observed storage lead, rather than being confined to the near-discharge stratum?

The strongest claim this experiment could support is that, within this deidentified MIMIC-IV snapshot and under blinded expert adjudication, the joint distribution of delayed storage and content states differs across prespecified recommendation-to-discharge observed-storage lead strata, with the pattern more compatible with a workflow-race phenotype, a persistent content-reconciliation phenotype, both, or neither. It cannot establish that anyone could see the report, that an author knew of it, that a summary was signed or transmitted, that a patient or clinician received or followed a plan, or that an intervention improves imaging, cancer outcomes, safety, or benefit. Those require prospective event-level/UI/communication timestamps, clinical review, and an intervention or linked-outcome study.

The advance over the parent is an estimand that uses chronology rather than only generic institutional latency. A high `A` rate in a near-discharge stratum is not automatically a mechanism claim; it is interpreted jointly with the high-lead stratum, the timely-summary content multinomial, report-to-summary storage gaps, ordinary-discharge controls, and negative controls. A high `A` rate that remains after ample observed lead is explicitly not relabeled a visibility failure; it is a persistent storage-process phenotype whose content counterpart must be assessed.

## Population, chronology reconstruction, and frozen denominator

The unit remains the first eligible admission per subject. Freeze the eligible opportunity denominator before examining discharge text, discharge `storetime`, downstream traces, or calibration-control outcomes. Include adults with an inpatient radiology report linked to an admission and charted from `admittime - 12 hours` through `D = dischtime`. Exclude in-hospital death, hospice/comfort-only transition, screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, explicit no-follow-up, conditional/optional plans, and recommendations first introduced after `D`. Require a patient-specific unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months after an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity. Pack-years, complete measurements, appropriateness, risk category, and other unavailable clinical inputs remain unknown rather than being imputed.

Reconstruct radiology report/addendum chains only through reciprocal subject- and admission-consistent detail links. Preserve all rows and ordinals. A recommendation component is eligible pre-discharge evidence under the inherited rule only when its `charttime <= D` and `storetime <= D`; if the first introduction or material change is post-`D`, it is a late candidate and not silently admitted to `N`. Missing nodes, contradictory links, missing timestamps, and chart/store conflicts form `U_R` and remain reported, bounded, or adjudicated as unresolved; they are never converted into an omission or availability failure. The radiology-trained reviewers, blinded to discharge text, discharge `storetime`, later imaging, and control labels, adjudicate CT coverage, incidental status, recommendation, interval, addendum meaning, reciprocal-chain membership, eligibility, and timestamp uncertainty. The final `N`, the lexical candidates, metadata-linked candidates, exclusions, post-discharge candidates, and `U_R` cascade are frozen and reported separately.

The discharge artifact is the unique `note/discharge` row of `note_type == DS` for the admission in this snapshot. If a rerun finds duplicate DS rows, it must stop rather than select a row mechanically. `D`, `D+24h`, `D+72h`, and `D+168h` are locked descriptive boundaries; the added 72-hour boundary is a lead-time contrast, not a clinical grace period.

Retain the inherited mutually exclusive all-`N` primary states at `D`:

* `F`: canonical DS stored by `D`, classifiable, and faithfully carries the patient-specific nodule, chest/thoracic CT action, and exactly compatible interval/date.
* `C_O`: DS stored by `D`, classifiable, but lacks the patient-specific actionable plan.
* `C_I`: DS stored by `D`, but nodule/action/timing is incomplete or discordant without documented alternate resolution.
* `R`: DS stored by `D` with a documented alternate resolution such as refusal/preferences, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, or scheduled outside follow-up; this is neither `F` nor content failure.
* `A`: a canonical DS has known `storetime` but is not observed stored by `D`; this is storage timing only.
* `U_D`: missing DS `storetime`, absent/unreadable/contradictory linkage, or unclassifiable text.

A DS stored before the recommendation component is a stale-artifact chronology subtype and is not blamed for omission. `A` remains `A` at the primary `D` boundary even if later text becomes `F`, `C`, or `R`; transitions at +24h, +72h, and +168h are secondary descriptive matrices. No post-storage subset replaces the all-`N` denominator.

## The lead-time exposure and exact chronology strata

For every frozen `N`, identify the adjudicated recommendation-bearing report or addendum component, retaining its exact `note_id`, chain role, `charttime`, and `storetime`. Define, without using discharge text or later trace outcomes:

* `R_store = storetime` of the recommendation-bearing component. This is an observed database-storage timestamp, not a draft, finalization, visibility, signature, author-awareness, or communication timestamp.
* `L_R = D - R_store`, in hours. Larger `L_R` means more observed storage lead before discharge; it does not mean more human exposure.
* `DS_store = storetime` of the canonical DS and `L_DS = DS_store - D`; missing `DS_store` is unknown.
* `G = DS_store - R_store`, the observed storage-time gap. If `G < 0`, the DS is stale relative to the recommendation component; this is a chronology marker only.
* `K_R = I(R_store <= D)` under the inherited eligibility rule. A missing or contradictory `R_store` is `U_R`, not imputed.
* `B_b = I(DS_store <= D+b)` for `b in {0,24,72,168}` when `DS_store` is known.

The primary four known-lead strata are locked before outcome classification: `S1: 0 <= L_R < 6h`, `S2: 6 <= L_R < 24h`, `S3: 24 <= L_R < 72h`, and `S4: L_R >= 72h`. `S0` is unknown/contradictory lead and remains outside the complete-case lead comparison. The primary near-versus-ample contrast is `S1` versus `S4`; `S2` and `S3` show the ordered gradient. A sensitivity analysis uses 12-hour and 168-hour cut points and treats `L_R` continuously with a prespecified restricted cubic spline only if every primary stratum has adequate support; it cannot replace the locked categorical estimand.

For timely DS artifacts (`B_0=1`), additionally classify `G` as stale (`G<0`), near (`0<=G<6h`), intermediate (`6<=G<24h`), or ample (`G>=24h`). This secondary gap variable is not used to redefine `N` or to infer a workflow race. It tests whether content states depend on observed report-to-summary storage chronology after separating stale artifacts.

## Primary estimands and mechanism decision rules

Report the complete joint table `P(S=s, Y=y)` over all frozen `N`, where `Y` is `F, C_O, C_I, R, A, U_D`, plus `S0/U_R` and missingness counts. This protects against denominator changes. Also report `P(Y=y | S=s)` only with the stratum denominator and unknownness explicitly shown. The following are prespecified descriptive estimands, not causal effects:

1. **Near-lead race contrast.** `RD_A^near-ample = P(A | S1) - P(A | S4)`, with the full all-`N` table and `U_D`/unknown bounds alongside it. A positive contrast means delayed DS storage is more frequent in the near observed-storage-lead stratum; it does not establish that a report-to-summary workflow race occurred.
2. **Persistent content contrast.** Among `B_0=1` and known `S`, report the multinomial distribution `F, C_O, C_I, R` by stratum and `RD_C^ample-near = P(C_O+C_I | B_0=1,S4) - P(C_O+C_I | B_0=1,S1)`. `R` is always separate. Also report the denominator-free joint probability `P(S=s,B_0=1,C_O+C_I)` over all `N`; the conditional contrast cannot be used to hide a large `A` or `U_D` stratum.
3. **Adequate-lead content estimand.** `P(C_O+C_I | S4,B_0=1)` is the principal persistence quantity, with `F` and `R` displayed beside it. A high value supports a content-reconciliation phenotype only as an observable artifact result, not as evidence that a clinician ignored a visible report.
4. **Storage-process persistence.** `P(A | S4)` and `P(A | S1)` are both reported. If `P(A|S4)` is high, the result is persistent discharge-storage latency despite ample observed recommendation storage lead, not proof of late visibility.
5. **Gap sensitivity.** Within timely summaries, compare content states by `G` and report stale-artifact counts. Any apparent content excess confined to `G<0` is a chronology warning and not a reconciliation failure.

The directional mechanism interpretation is conjunctive and deliberately modest. A race-compatible pattern is a materially higher `A` in `S1` than `S4`, a declining `A` gradient across `S1`-`S4`, no comparable lead gradient in chest negative controls after calibration, and no high-lead `C` excess. A persistent-content-compatible pattern is a nontrivial `C` rate in `S4` with a simultaneous interval excluding the prespecified near-lead margin, while `A` is not the sole explanation and results persist after removing stale `G<0` artifacts. A bundled pattern has both near-lead `A` concentration and high-lead `C`. A null or mixed pattern does not select an intervention mechanism.

Operational margins are locked before computation rather than chosen from results: race-support requires the simultaneous lower bound for `RD_A^near-ample` to exceed 0 and its point estimate to be at least 0.05; persistence-support requires the simultaneous lower bound for `RD_C^ample-near` to exceed 0 and its point estimate to be at least 0.05. These are decision rules, not validated clinical thresholds. If the study cannot support these margins, it reports estimates and bounds without selecting a mechanism.

## Calibration and controls

Retain the parent's controls and their order of construction.

* `O`: all adult admission keys with a unique DS row and nonmissing `dischtime`/`storetime`, excluding every frozen eligible nodule opportunity and its subject's index admission. It calibrates ordinary DS storage and has no radiology lead exposure.
* `I`: all linked inpatient chest/CT radiology reports in `[admittime-12h,D]` that fail the nodule-recommendation retrieval screen, preserving report `charttime`, `storetime`, note type, and admission linkage. It is a negative control for nodule-specific report timing, not proof of visibility.
* `M`: up to four deterministic controls per `N` from `O`, selected using only pre-index structure: admission type, discharge location, ICU-stay indicator, length-of-stay bins `(0-1, >1-3, >3-7, >7 days)`, and terminal service family. Shortfalls are retained. Exact strata and fallback are locked before any delay or content state is inspected. Full `O` inverse-stratum standardization is primary for calibration; `M` is sensitivity only.

For each `N`, `O`, and `M`, estimate `q_g(b)=P(DS_store>D+b)` at `b=0,24,72,168h`, reporting unstandardized `O`, standardized `O`, and matched `M`. The inherited calibration contrast `Delta_b=q_N(b)-q_O^std(b)` remains required. It is a generic storage contrast, not a substitute for the lead-time estimands. Report report chart-to-store lag for `N` and `I`, but never use charttime alone as observed availability.

For positivity, publish counts and weighted effective sample size for every `N` stratum and each standardized control stratum. Require at least 30 known-lead opportunities in both `S1` and `S4`, at least 20 events in any contrast whose event-specific conclusion is invoked, no empty calibration-control cell used for standardization, and no extreme standardization weight that yields effective sample size below 50. These are feasibility gates; failure is inconclusive, not a negative mechanism result. Preserve the inherited minimum 200 adjudicated `N`, `U_R <=5%`, `U_D <=5%`, reviewer agreement/reliability, and simultaneous interval-width requirements where invoked.

Controls are not exchangeable causal comparators. No adjustment variable may be selected after seeing `A`, `C`, `S`, or downstream observation. Descriptive covariates from patients, admissions, services, transfers, ICU, diagnoses, and dictionary tables may be tabulated or used in a predeclared sensitivity standardization only.

## Analysis, uncertainty, and unknown handling

The primary analysis is an all-`N` multinomial/joint-probability analysis plus the two prespecified lead contrasts. Use subject-cluster bootstrap, resampling the one index admission per subject, with a single bootstrap replicate carrying all boundaries, all lead strata, and all primary contrasts. Construct simultaneous 95% intervals across the two mechanism contrasts, the four boundary contrasts, and the ordered stratum family using a max-statistic or prespecified multiplicity method. Report exact or Wilson/Newcombe intervals where appropriate, but do not mix interval families opportunistically.

Do not exclude `U_R`, `U_D`, missing `R_store`, missing `DS_store`, contradictory links, or stale artifacts silently. Give worst-case partial-identification bounds by assigning unresolved lead to either `S1` or `S4` and unresolved content to the most mechanism-favorable compatible state; also provide a conservative analysis that retains unknown as its own category. This is a sensitivity/bound, not imputation. Report reviewer agreement separately for eligibility, recommendation interval, chain linkage, and discharge-state classification; low agreement blocks mechanism selection.

The secondary observation appendix is inherited unchanged. For every frozen `N`, define the exact calendar-month due date plus a fixed 42-day window, begin at `D`, and classify same-system timely chest CT/CTA, death before window with no prior timely CT, later MIMIC radiology/admission trace without timely CT/death, no later trace (outside-care/loss-of-capture unknown), and indeterminate CT/death ordering. Use `max(D,DS_store)` only as an artifact-timed sensitivity. `dod` remains interval-censored to `[dod 00:00,dod+1 day)` unless a more precise death time exists. Do not condition the primary process estimands or this observation vector on later trace, and do not call any state adherence or nonadherence.

## Falsification and robustness tests

All tests are locked before final inference.

1. **Institutional-latency calibration:** the nodule `A` contrast must be compared with full `O`, standardized `O`, and `M` at all four boundaries. A similar signal in ordinary DS artifacts blocks a nodule-specific mechanism claim.
2. **Radiology-lag negative control:** compare report chart-to-store lag and `K_D` for the recommendation component with `I`; a signal as large in non-nodule chest imaging, or disappearance after radiology-lag stratification, blocks nodule-specific interpretation.
3. **Charttime placebo:** repeat chronology using report `charttime` only as a placebo descriptor. It must not replace `R_store`; a charttime-only association cannot support any storage or visibility conclusion.
4. **Stale-ordering falsification:** report `G<0` separately. If discordance is concentrated in stale artifacts, the result is chronology ambiguity, not evidence of content reconciliation failure.
5. **Threshold stability:** repeat the lead classification with 12-hour and 168-hour sensitivity cuts and exact zero/12-hour report-to-admission linkage windows. A mechanism sign reversal blocks selection. Sensitivity thresholds do not create new opportunities.
6. **Within-stratum permutation:** permute lead-stratum labels within exact pre-index admission-structure strata, preserving stratum sizes and subject clustering. The lead contrasts should collapse to the null apart from Monte Carlo error. Failure indicates leakage, matching imbalance, or a coding error.
7. **Comparator reversal:** compare full `O` and `M`; a material reversal (absolute difference at least 0.02 for a primary contrast) is a calibration warning and blocks mechanism prioritization.
8. **Duplicate/timestamp audit:** any duplicate DS key, non-reciprocal addendum used as canonical, impossible time ordering, or changed source hash stops the confirmatory run and requires a new candidate rule.

A negative result is useful. If `A` is near the ordinary comparator but high-lead `C` persists, the observable priority shifts toward content reconciliation, with prospective confirmation still required. If both are ordinary/low, the relevant unresolved process may be downstream tracking or unmeasured communication. If both are high, the correct conclusion is a bundled process phenotype rather than selection of one intervention.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

**Supportive for a race-compatible timing signal** requires reliable blinded adjudication, frozen denominator and source hashes, adequate `S1/S4` overlap, stable positive `RD_A^near-ample`, no comparator or radiology-lag explanation, and no material high-lead content excess. It supports designing a prospective timestamp-crosswalk intervention study that measures report finalization, signature, EHR availability, transmission, and communication. It does not support a claim that the discharge plan was unavailable or that earlier storage improves outcomes.

**Supportive for persistent content discordance** requires reliable adjudication, adequate high-lead support, stable positive `RD_C^ample-near`, content discordance remaining after stale-artifact separation and generic-latency calibration, and a displayed all-`N` joint table showing the result is not created by removing `A` or `U_D`. It supports prospective structured reconciliation and expert review of appropriate content, not a claim of author failure, communication failure, adherence failure, causality, or benefit.

**Supportive for a bundled phenotype** requires both mechanism contrasts to meet their locked directional rules and neither to be explained by controls. The appropriate next study is a bundled prospective workflow with separable timestamps and components, not an intervention selected from MIMIC alone.

**Adverse for a proposed mechanism** includes a contrast close to ordinary controls, disappearance after radiology-lag calibration, content confined to stale artifacts, sign reversal under locked chronology windows, or persistence of the apparent signal in chest negative controls. It redirects the decision without asserting an opposite clinical mechanism.

**Inconclusive** includes fewer than 30 known opportunities in a primary lead stratum, sparse events, poor overlap/low effective sample size, excess `U_R`/`U_D`, reviewer disagreement, duplicate DS rows, contradictory links, simultaneous intervals crossing the operational margin, comparator reversal, failed permutation, or a source/hash mismatch. Inconclusive is not evidence that no timing or content problem exists.

## Verifier scope and claims requiring another study

A computational verifier can check source hashes and archive members; complete-source row counts; exact filters and joins; one-DS-per-admission; frozen-`N` ordering; reciprocal report/addendum linkage; recommendation component identity; `D` and boundary arithmetic; lead-stratum exhaustiveness; stale ordering; all-`N` state exhaustiveness; unknown/bound calculations; full/standardized/matched controls; overlap and effective sample size; subject-cluster bootstrap and simultaneous intervals; negative-control and permutation outputs; and that every reported conclusion points to a computed output. It should include adversarial cases with a high `A` signal caused only by generic DS delay, a high-lead `C` signal hidden by complete-case conditioning, stale artifacts mislabeled as content failure, and unsupported causal/visibility conclusions.

The verifier cannot determine whether `storetime` represented physical visibility, a draft or signed artifact, author awareness, transmission, communication, patient receipt, recommendation appropriateness, outside-care completion, malignancy, safety, causal effect, or patient benefit. It cannot clinically adjudicate alternate resolution, interval appropriateness, or whether a nodule was truly incidental without expert review. Those claims require prospective event-level/UI/communication data, clinical adjudication, external linkage, and/or a randomized or otherwise appropriate workflow study. No conclusion may exceed the storage-and-text evidence boundary.

## What changed and remaining tradeoffs

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, the corrected provenance, frozen all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal chronology, ordinary/chest/matched controls, calibration boundaries, downstream unknown states, and strict noncausal limits are retained. The substantive change is to make the recommendation's observed-storage lead before discharge a primary chronology axis, to report its joint distribution with `A/F/C_O/C_I/R/U_D`, and to separate near-lead delayed storage from high-lead content discordance using explicit estimands, overlap gates, stale-artifact handling, and simultaneous uncertainty.

The tradeoff is that the design is more decision-valid but less statistically efficient: conditioning the content analysis on timely DS storage is secondary and can be sparse, while the all-`N` joint table prevents denominator manipulation. A high recommendation lead may correlate with report type, illness course, or discharge timing, so this is not causal mediation and no observational adjustment can repair unavailable semantics. The result can distinguish observable chronology patterns, not the human mechanism itself. That limitation is the reason the next step after a supportive result is a prospective timestamp/UI/communication study rather than an asserted workflow intervention.
