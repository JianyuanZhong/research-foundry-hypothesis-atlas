# Exact ventilation care-plan labels show specification-sensitive, component-mixed proxy degradation rather than a resolved general predictive defect

## Question and advance
Parent `[prior hypothesis]` validly showed that sparse, site-concentrated exact eICU care-plan labels fail its prespecified broad-deployment fitness gate: adding the three-state feature slightly worsened leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO) composite-proxy log loss in every bounded-airway cohort. Two ambiguities remained: uncertainty resampled only held-out stays conditional on fitted models, and the composite could conceal calibration shift or one component/model specification.

**Supported before this child:** the exact vocabulary is absent in most evaluable deployment hospitals, concentrated at hospital 420, and does not provide reproducible incremental information for the frozen composite under the parent's C=1 models. This is structured-documentation evidence only.

**Hypothesis tested:** the adverse result represents a reproducible, broadly distributed predictive degradation if its sign survives training-plus-test stay resampling, intercept-only local recalibration, regularization sensitivity, and component decomposition. If degradation disappears after local intercept recalibration or is heterogeneous across components/specifications, the strongest conclusion must narrow accordingly.

This child freezes the target-independent population, landmarks, label classification, baseline, outcome timing, broad-deployment hospital criteria, target concentration findings, and proxy-only clinical interpretation. Its advance is diagnostic: it distinguishes robust general degradation from calibration/specification dependence rather than relabeling a small loss difference as clinical harm.

## Analysis and falsification
The inherited primary C=1 logistic pipelines and LOHO broad-deployment folds are unchanged. This child adds:

1. **Training-plus-test uncertainty:** for the unbounded primary cohort, resample `sid` stays within every hospital for both training and held-out sets and refit both models in each replicate. The executed bound was 10 replicates (seed 20260911), so quantiles are explicitly exploratory and low-resolution, not definitive 95% intervals. The parent's 300-replicate fixed-fit held-out bootstrap remains reported separately.
2. **Calibration decomposition:** fit a diagnostic intercept-only shift separately to baseline and label predictions in each held-out hospital, then compare equal-hospital log loss after local prevalence correction. This uses held-out outcomes and is not deployable without local calibration data; it diagnoses intercept shift only.
3. **Regularization sensitivity:** repeat identical folds with C=0.1 and C=10, in addition to C=1.
4. **Component outcomes:** independently evaluate reductions in FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate. Each has its own unchanged broad criterion (at least 20 paired rows and outcome variation); therefore component estimands differ in available rows/sites and are diagnostic, not a replacement primary endpoint.

The inherited gate remains authoritative. A stronger claim of general predictive degradation additionally requires bounded signs to persist across C, remain adverse after local recalibration, show concordant component degradation, and have joint refit uncertainty excluding improvement. Calibration-only evidence means raw loss worsens but the equal-hospital delta approaches zero after intercept correction without concordant discrimination loss. Otherwise the mechanism is inconclusive.

## Executed findings
The exact C=1 primary results reproduce the parent: micro/macro delta log loss (label minus baseline) was +0.000707/+0.000398 unbounded, +0.000587/+0.000964 at 24 h, +0.000798/+0.001005 at 12 h, and +0.001674/+0.001804 at 6 h.

Regularization did not reverse any bounded sign. At C=0.1, micro/macro deltas were +0.000452/+0.000652 (24 h), +0.000252/+0.000539 (12 h), and +0.000404/+0.000598 (6 h); at C=10 they were +0.001948/+0.001265, +0.002724/+0.001498, and +0.005452/+0.003226. Stronger label coefficients therefore worsened loss more, while shrinkage attenuated but did not reverse it.

Local intercept recalibration materially attenuated the equal-hospital delta, especially unbounded: C=1 recalibrated macro deltas were -0.000012 unbounded, +0.000470 (24 h), +0.000727 (12 h), and +0.000970 (6 h). Thus the unbounded difference is compatible with hospital calibration/intercept mismatch. Bounded effects remain adverse after correction, but smaller; their concomitant AUROC changes (-0.00123, -0.00303, -0.00240) indicate they are not purely intercept shifts.

The 10-replicate unbounded joint training/test bootstrap gave micro median +0.001124, interval -0.000352 to +0.005808, P(worse)=0.80; macro median +0.001170, interval -0.000476 to +0.002034, P(worse)=0.70. Because both intervals cross zero and only 10 refits were feasible, training uncertainty is unresolved rather than confirmatory. This supersedes any implication that the fixed-fit bootstrap alone establishes model-training robustness.

Component results were heterogeneous. FiO2 (3,292 rows; 26 hospitals) worsened micro/macro loss by +0.001546/+0.000844 and AUROC by -0.001917. Pressure support (684 rows; 9 hospitals) worsened by +0.002182/+0.001515 and AUROC by -0.005477. PEEP (3,165 rows; 23 hospitals) improved by -0.000880/-0.000564 and AUROC by +0.004714. Ventilator rate (2,391 rows; 16 hospitals) improved by -0.000321/-0.000166 and AUROC by +0.001390. Recalibrated macro signs remained the same. The composite adverse result is therefore not a single-component artifact, but neither is it a uniform physiologic-setting result.

**Result:** the inherited adverse broad-deployment fitness verdict remains valid for the prespecified C=1 composite proxy. The proposed stronger mechanism claim is **inconclusive**: bounded degradation is directionally robust to regularization and not purely intercept-driven, but training-refit uncertainty is low-resolution and crosses zero, while component effects split two adverse/two favorable. The strongest defensible evolution is that exact labels lack reliable general incremental composite fitness and their small adverse loss is calibration-, regularization-, and component-dependent—not a proven universal within-hospital predictive defect.

## Meaning and limits
A supportive result would justify further prospective evaluation only as a documentation feature. An adverse diagnostic result would support avoiding unqualified multicenter use under this exact proxy/model. The observed inconclusive mechanism means the parent warning remains, but claims of universally harmful prediction or uniform respiratory-setting degradation are falsified/narrowed.

Nothing here establishes actual invasive ventilation, daily extubation evaluation, SBT performance, extubation readiness or occurrence, durable liberation, clinician intent, care quality, benefit, harm, or causality. The component endpoints are charted-setting proxies, not device-verified changes. Stronger claims require more refit replicates, prespecified external hospitals, device/airway data, direct SBT/extubation records, workflow adjudication, blinded chart review, and prospective validation.

An automatic verifier can check hashes, exact filters, folds, stay grouping, refits, C values, calibration arithmetic, component construction, metrics, and whether conclusions follow. It cannot adjudicate bedside semantics or clinical quality/benefit/harm.
