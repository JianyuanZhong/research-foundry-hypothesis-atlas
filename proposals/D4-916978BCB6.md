## Lineage and substantive repair

This is a substantive successor to assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`, preserving its Direction-A cohort, out-of-fold comparison, equations, seeds, and aggregate-only reporting. The selected parent is scientifically strong but has two operational gaps. It names the UKB biomarker columns and applies clinical equations although the current local catalog explicitly says that per-field labels, units, and missing-value meanings are unavailable. The repair is a fail-closed certification state machine and a formally separated estimand. It never infers units, assay identity, missingness, collection timing, or clinical validity from column names, plausible numerical ranges, or agreement with expected counts. The experiment can produce a biological or predictive result only after an immutable steward/metadata certificate is supplied and verified. If the required certificate is later supplied, the inherited computation runs unchanged, but the primary capacity contrast is described as a schedule-marginal protocol-ranking estimand, not a repeat-testing decision or clinical utility.

The strongest existing evidence remains narrow and inherited: in the selected complete-biomarker first-repeat-attender cohort, the parent reported an association between baseline `eGFRcys_0 < 60` and repeat creatinine-derived threshold status after fixed linear adjustment. The new unresolved claim is: conditional on a certified interpretation of these fields, does binary baseline cystatin-C threshold discordance add reproducible out-of-sample information beyond a flexible creatinine/age/sex/time comparator among people who actually have a qualifying repeat visit? The experiment does not test whether ordering cystatin C changes repeat attendance, improves care, or benefits patients.

## Certified cohort and equations, conditional on passing the gate

Once all certification states pass, reconstruct the parent's cohort exactly. Let `B` require the three-way one-to-one intersection, certified sex code 0/1, certified parseable initial assessment date, finite positive baseline age, and finite positive baseline creatinine and cystatin C after the certified missing-code policy. Let `R` require finite positive repeat age, repeat creatinine, repeat cystatin C, and repeat date, with certified dates satisfying
`2 <= (date_1 - date_0)/365.25 <= 8`.
Apply `R` jointly with `B`; do not define repeat eligibility over participants outside the baseline-eligible set.

For the certified creatinine unit, use `Scr = creatinine/88.4` only if the certificate says the exported value is micromol/L and that conversion is required. For sex code 0 (female) and 1 (male), respectively, use

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/kappa,1)^alpha * max(Scr/kappa,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^age * female_factor`,

with `(kappa, alpha, female_factor)=(0.7,-0.241,1.012)` for female and `(0.9,-0.302,1)` for male. Use the certified cystatin unit in

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^age * female_factor`,

with female factor `0.932` and male factor `1`. Use age at the same certified assessment instance: baseline age for baseline estimates and repeat age for `eGFRcr_1`.

Define `T_A = B ∩ {eGFRcr_0 >= 60}`, `S_A = T_A ∩ R`, `E = 1{eGFRcys_0 < 60}`, `Y = 1{eGFRcr_1 < 60}`, and `t=(date_1-date_0)/365.25`. `S_A` is explicitly a selected complete-biomarker, observed-repeat-attender protocol cohort. It is not a baseline population and `Y` is not an adjudicated CKD, measured-GFR, or progression endpoint.

Retain the parent's two algorithms exactly. `M0` is ridge logistic regression using only baseline `eGFRcr_0`, baseline age, sex, and observed repeat interval `t`, with training-only restricted cubic splines (five eGFRcr knots at 0.05, 0.275, 0.50, 0.725, 0.95; four age and time knots at 0.05, 0.35, 0.65, 0.95), all eGFRcr-by-time products and sex-by-eGFRcr products, training-only centering/scaling, unpenalized intercept, and the fixed lambda grid `10^(-4),10^(-3.5),...,10^4`, selected independently by nested five-fold log-loss one-standard-error rule. `M1` adds only `E` and `E*(eGFRcr_0-60)/10`, with the same penalty and no repeat biomarker.

Use ten repeated outer five-fold cross-fits with seeds `[260911,260912,260913,260914,260915,260916,260917,260918,260919,260920]`, stratifying fold allocation by `E x Y` only. Inner seeds are `outer_seed + 1000 + outer_fold`; all preprocessing and knots are fit within inner training data. Every test participant must have zero `eid` overlap with all training/tuning rows used to obtain that prediction. Average the ten out-of-fold probabilities per participant; clip only log loss at `[10^-6,1-10^-6]`, and report clipping counts.

The primary accuracy contrast remains
`Delta_Brier = 1000*[mean((Y-p0)^2)-mean((Y-p1)^2)]`.
For `K=ceil(0.10*N)`, rank participants by each model's averaged out-of-fold probability, break exact ties with the locally retained hash `SHA-256("direction-A-capacity-v1|"+eid)`, and define
`Delta_rank10 = 1000*(events in top-K M1 - events in top-K M0)/K`.

It is not “additional events found by ordering cystatin C,” net benefit, a treatment rule, or a repeat-testing recommendation. Report promoted/displaced counts and event counts only as aggregate protocol diagnostics.

The fixed sequence tests positive `Delta_Brier` first and `Delta_rank10` second only if the first passes. Use paired participant bootstrap over the frozen ten-column prediction matrices, 2,000 successful replicates with seed 260921 and maximum 2,500 attempts, percentile 95% intervals, and the plus-one symmetric null-centered p-value. Preserve the parent's convergence, cell-support, calibration, comparator-signal, split-sign, and aggregate privacy gates. ## Schedule-marginal versus clinically actionable claims

The estimand is formally conditional on `A=1`, where `A` means a qualifying repeat visit with complete repeat cystatin C, and on its observed interval `t`: the reported score contrasts estimate performance for `P(Y=1 | X, A=1, t in [2,8])`, not `P(Y=1 | X)` at baseline. The data contain neither a baseline decision time with an available future horizon, a counterfactual repeat-testing action, the reason for return, outcomes for nonattenders, assay costs, harms, treatment/referral consequences, nor a utility function. Consequently no value-of-information, net-benefit, number-needed-to-test, ordering, referral, treatment, or patient-benefit estimand is identified.

A result may support only the statement that, **within this certified selected repeat-attender protocol**, adding `E` improved cross-fitted probability accuracy and/or concentrated observed protocol-defined repeat threshold events at the fixed ranking capacity. It cannot support “test cystatin C,” “repeat testing should be intensified,” or “patients benefit.” A clinically actionable repeat-testing claim requires a separate baseline-deployable horizon with follow-up for both attenders and nonattenders, an explicitly prespecified action policy, action consequences and costs, and external/clinical validation. This proposal does not silently promote `Delta_rank10` into that claim.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

Supportive requires: all source/schema and semantic/assay/temporal certificates pass; exact cohort reconstruction; all fold and event-support checks; finite converged fits; calibration and comparator gates; at least 8/10 positive repeat-specific contrasts; at least 2,000 successful bootstrap replicates; positive `Delta_Brier` with two-sided 95% interval excluding zero and p<0.05; and, after the fixed sequence advances, positive `Delta_rank10` with its interval excluding zero and p<0.05. The conclusion is limited to reproducible incremental information and fixed-capacity protocol ranking in `S_A`.

Adverse means a primary contrast is wholly below zero, M1 materially loses calibration while M0 passes, or repeat-specific results reproducibly favor M0. This weakens the claim that the parent's association translates into out-of-sample information under the frozen comparator. Inconclusive means an interval includes zero, gates fail, signs are unstable, or bootstrap completion fails; it is not equivalence. Neutral stop means the data interpretation cannot be certified and no biomarker inference was attempted.

Computationally checkable claims include exact file identity, columns, joins, certificate matching, missing-code handling, equations conditional on certified units, cohort masks, fold isolation, preprocessing, tuning, predictions, calibration, ranking arithmetic, bootstrap, and state transitions. Assay validity, specimen provenance, chronicity, true GFR, clinical meaning of threshold crossings, attendance mechanisms, and benefit remain unavailable or require expert adjudication, external validation, or a decision-impact/randomized study.
