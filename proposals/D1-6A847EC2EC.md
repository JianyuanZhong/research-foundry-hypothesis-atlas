## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
The parent pools all liver-directed therapy (TACE, resection, local ablation) into one per-1-SD AFP-slope hazard for
time to radiologic local progression after a 180-day landmark. But serial AFP is *ordered and interpreted differently*
across modalities: after TACE, AFP is a routine response marker; after curative-intent resection or ablation, follow-up
is often imaging-driven and AFP is more often a surveillance adjunct. If the pooled hazard is carried by TACE records
(where AFP is measured more often, so a slope is estimable and a rising AFP co-occurs with the more frequent
post-TACE imaging that generates the lexical event), the "*AFP-guided surveillance*" clinical conclusion would **not
generalize** to ablation/resection. This changes a consequential decision: recommend AFP-trajectory-guided imaging
intensity in **all** post-LDT patients vs only in **some** modalities.

## 2. Claims: what evidence supports vs what this experiment tests
- Leading claim under test (unchanged estimand, now checked for poolability): H1 the per-1-SD within-patient AFP
  slope on (index, t*] is associated with shorter time to local progression **and this hazard is invariant across first
  modality**.
- Strongest rival R0 (the reason for this child): the pooled hazard is an **effect-modification / measurement artifact
  of TACE**. In TACE, AFP and post-treatment imaging are both routine, so AFP slopes are estimable and progression is
  frequently documented; in ablation/resection the slope is sparser and the lexical endpoint may behave differently.
  Prediction: the modality-specific AFP-slope HRs are **heterogeneous** and the pooled HR is dominated by TACE.
- Strongest claim the ALREADY-AVAILABLE evidence supports (not this child's test): baseline ALBI grade is prognostic
  in TACE-treated unresectable HCC [K1]; AFP-containing trajectories add only modest discrimination over a
  baseline-biomarker model in mixed-treatment HCC [K3]; lead-time / sensitivity / immortal-time bias are named,
  real pitfalls of serial-biomarker analysis [K2]. None of the inspected works isolates an AFP-slope hazard **by first
  liver-directed modality**, so poolability is untested.
- Unresolved claim this experiment tests: whether the modality-specific AFP-slope hazards are equal (poolable) after
  modality-specific endpoint calibration.

## 4. THIS EPISODE'S ONE ADDED ELEMENT: first-modality effect-modification test (TACE vs ablation vs resection)
The pooled per-1-SD AFP-slope hazard is **not assumed transportable**. Pre-specify, before any outcome is computed:
(a) **Modality-stratified primary hazard.** Re-estimate the primary per-1-SD AFP-slope HR separately within
    first-modality strata TACE / ablation / resection (same population, landmark, exposure window, same-draw pairing,
    censoring and endpoint as Section 3). Report each stratum HR with 95% CI and event count.
(b) **Formal AFP-slope x modality interaction test.** A single Cox model with `AFP_slope_SD` + `modality` +
    `AFP_slope_SD:modality` (TACE = reference), covariates as in the primary model. Test the 2-df interaction by
    likelihood-ratio test. **Pre-declared falsification band (poolability):** the pooled claim is *rejected as
    non-transportable* if BOTH (i) interaction LRT p < 0.05 AND (ii) at least one modality-specific slope HR ratio
    (modality HR / TACE HR) falls outside [0.50, 2.00]. The pooled claim is *supported as transportable* if interaction
    LRT p >= 0.10 AND every modality-specific HR ratio lies within [0.80, 1.25] AND every stratum 95% CI overlaps the
    pooled point estimate. `0.50/2.00` and `0.80/1.25` are fixed here, before estimation, and are not tuned to the data.
(c) **Modality-specific endpoint calibration.** The lexical local-progression descriptors may behave differently after
    resection than after TACE (the probe below already shows descriptor-positive prevalence 60.5% TACE vs 48.1%
    ablation vs 49.9% resection among post-landmark abdominal exams). Therefore run the parent's stratified
    adjudication audit **within each modality** (pre-registered floor 0.80 per modality) and re-estimate per-modality
    Se/Sp; propagate modality-specific Se[0.70,0.95]/Sp[0.80,0.98] Monte-Carlo (20k) into each **modality-specific** HR.
    A modality-specific conclusion is drawn only if that modality's audit floor is met.

### Pre-declared outcome branches (mutually exhaustive, decided in this order)
- **Supportive (pooled claim survives):** all modality audit floors met AND interaction LRT p >= 0.10 AND all modality
  HR ratios in [0.80,1.25] AND each bias-corrected modality-specific AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 with concordant sign.
  Meaning: AFP-trajectory-guided surveillance can be recommended across modalities; the modality term is not needed.
- **Adverse (pooled claim overturned):** interaction LRT p < 0.05 AND >=1 modality HR ratio outside [0.50,2.00]
  (i.e. the TACE-dominated pattern), OR the pooled signal is confined to TACE while ablation/resection modality-
  specific HR CIs include 1. Meaning: do NOT recommend AFP-trajectory-guided surveillance for the non-TACE modality
  (or modalities) that fail; the decision becomes modality-specific.
- **Inconclusive:** any modality audit floor < 0.80, OR any modality stratum < 30 events or < 10 events/quartile
  (tertile fallback pre-declared) so the interaction cannot be estimated; OR modality-specific Se/Sp correction makes
  the modality HR unidentifiable. Meaning: escalate to radiologist mRECIST adjudication / pooled multi-centre data;
  no surveillance recommendation change is licensed.
- **Precise-null (transportability equivalence):** interaction terms and every modality HR ratio lie inside the
  equivalence band [0.85,1.18] on the ratio scale with the joint 95% interval inside it. Meaning: the pooled hazard is
  statistically equivalent across modalities; a single modality-agnostic conclusion is defensible.

## 5. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative) - recorded BEFORE selection
Same question, split, endpoint, uncertainty evaluation as the parent.
- **M0 (baseline):** baseline-value landmark Cox (baseline AFP + baseline ALBI + age/sex + modality), entering the
  **level** of AFP.
- **M4 (alternative / primary):** joint mixed-effects models for AFP and ALBI slopes (random slopes), individual BLUPs
  entered into the same landmark Cox; modality interaction tested on the M4 slope.
  What M4 recovers that M0 loses: **rate of change** rather than a single anchor level, and shrinkage across sparse /
  irregular draws (critical here, because draw density differs by modality - the very structure R0 invokes).
- **Target:** per-1-SD AFP-slope HR (pooled and per modality) and quartile response, on (index,t*].
- **Split:** temporal split (earlier index for fit, later for held-out C-index delta) plus bootstrap CI; interaction
  tested on the full pre-specified cohort with a bootstrap distribution for the modality HR ratios.
- **Baseline comparison:** M0 is the equivalence comparator for the precise-null branch and the baseline-only rival.
- **Resource budget:** CPU-only, minutes-scale (procedures 35 MB / 338,040 rows; exams 1.1 GB streamed).
  **Measured this episode:** the full examinations scan + procedure classification ran in **15.1 s** on 4 CPU / 16 GiB
  ([research job]). The M4 joint fits add seconds-to-minutes; **no GPU needed** and none requested.
  This is a measured estimate for the streaming/aggregation step only; the mixed-model fit time is **unverified**.
- **Deferred with revisit conditions:** shared-parameter observation-process joint model (addresses informative
  observation, which is exactly the modality-linked measurement-pattern rival) and Fine-Gray competing-risks model;
  both computable from the same tables, revisited if the audit floors pass and the informative-observation objection
  survives the modality interaction test.

## 7. Honest limits
- Progression flag is a lexical extraction requiring radiologist mRECIST adjudication; the per-modality audit and
  modality-specific bias analysis reduce but cannot eliminate misclassification.
- The modality effect-modification test can fail to be estimable (low event counts, sparse follow-up in ablation).
- No mortality/mRECIST PFS ascertainable -> no survival claim. Single institution; labs lack units.
- **NO outcome model computed in discovery:** stratum HRs, interaction LRT and the audit are the solver's first outputs.
- The leading claim (transportability) and the rival (TACE-driven artifact) are computationally separable only under
  the pre-declared band; a causal claim (why TACE differs) requires expert review or another study.
