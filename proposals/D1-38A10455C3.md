# Episode 65: outcome-independent airway closure for the hour-48 recorded-loop policy

Parent candidates: `[prior hypothesis]` (Episode 61) and `[prior hypothesis]` (Episode 49).

The executable contract for this successor is [episode65_preX_boundary_contract.json](episode65_preX_boundary_contract.json). It takes the parent population, recorded-loop policy, bounded outcomes, recording-time cohorts, uncertainty procedure, and noncausal evidence boundary as frozen inputs, and repairs one identification/endpoint interaction.

## Remaining weakness and substantive advance

The inherited policy closes treatment at

`C=min(X,V.endtime)`,

where `V` is the ventilation interval linked to the first qualifying extubation task `X`. Thus the same future extubation event that supplies the principal airway outcome can determine whether a loop start is considered “while the airway interval remains open.” A subject who has already left the baseline ventilation interval but has not yet generated the extubation task can therefore be classified using a future outcome anchor. This is clinically material: it can exclude or admit loop starts according to how an airway outcome was documented, and it makes a favorable extubation record part of the exposure-window definition. The parent’s recording-time safeguards do not remove this future-`X` dependence; they govern whether rows were available by `t0`, not whether the treatment boundary is defined independently of the outcome.

The repair is to define the index decision window from the baseline invasive-ventilation interval alone. Let `V0` be the accepted same-stay item-225792 interval crossing `t0`, selected before any post-`t0` extubation task is read, and set

`C0=min(t0+12 hours,V0.endtime)`.

The primary A12 strategy is the first qualifying loop `starttime` strictly in `(t0,C0)`; B12 is no qualifying loop through `C0`. A loop exactly at `C0` is ambiguous. Ventilation re-entry after `V0.endtime` does not reopen the index policy window. The extubation task `X` is then used only as a post-decision airway outcome and for endpoint ascertainment. This preserves the clinical question—whether recorded loop initiation occurs while the baseline invasive-airway episode remains open—while removing the airway outcome from policy eligibility and closure.

The strongest available evidence supports only that MIMIC-IV contains computable, imperfect recorded timestamps, ventilation procedures, loop-input records, physiology, fluid accounting and same-admission outcomes. The parent audits establish that `inputevents.starttime/endtime/storetime` and `outputevents.storetime` exist and can be parsed, but do not establish real-time clinician awareness, actual medication receipt, physiologic congestion, extubation readiness, renal safety, exchangeability or causality. Current fluid-removal literature cited and inspected in the inherited line describes an important evidence gap around timing and clinical judgment; it does not validate this phenotype or imply a treatment benefit. The research-ambition demonstrations were inspected as demonstrations only. The unavailable cancer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not claimed as read.

## Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

Among adults who meet the frozen hour-48 post-shock, fluid-positive, invasively ventilated recorded phenotype, the outcome-independent C0 policy will yield a feasible measured-overlap A12-versus-B12 recorded-policy association with a lower bound above +0.05 for the primary five-day recorded airway-event-free extubation proxy, while the one-sided upper bounds remain below +0.03 for recorded creatinine-rise/terminal harm and +0.05 for RRT harm. This result is hypothesized to remain in the same result branch as the inherited X-anchored analysis, with no material reversal under recording-time, airway ascertainment, observation, balance, treatment-form and weight sensitivities.

The hypothesis is falsified in any of these prespecified ways:

- the C0 pipeline is infeasible, does not meet frozen minima, or cannot reconstruct `V0` and `C0`;
- the primary airway lower bound is at or below +0.05 (margin-falsified if every validity and adverse gate passes);
- a prespecified adverse family crosses its margin after Holm control;
- C0 and the inherited X-anchored version differ in result branch, move an airway bound by at least 0.05, or reverse midpoint signs with both absolute midpoint magnitudes at least 0.02;
- recording-time concordance, observation, endpoint, positivity, balance, ambiguity, contamination, or process-control validity fails.

A feasible result that does not satisfy the support, adverse, or margin-falsification rule is statistically inconclusive. No treatment effect is estimated by this proposal or its development audits.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Use the earliest adult ICU stay ordered by `(intime,stay_id)` with:

1. age at ICU admission `anchor_age + calendar_year(intime)-anchor_year >=18`;
2. `t0=intime+48h` strictly before ICU `outtime` and admission `dischtime`, and at or after `admittime`;
3. an accepted same-stay invasive-ventilation interval `V0`, item 225792, with coherent keys, `starttime<=t0<endtime`, `endtime>starttime`, status `FinishedRunning` or `Stopped`.

Never reopen a later stay. Apply the inherited clinical-time eligibility gates to form E_R:

- a valid recent pressor item in `(t0-24h,t0-6h]`, with six recorded pressor-free hours before `t0`; pressor items are 221289, 229617, 221662, 221749, 229630, 229631, 229632, 221906 and 222315;
- final-three-hour MAP median at least 65 from items 220052/225312, otherwise 220181;
- valid weight 30–300 kg from item 226512, falling back to 224639;
- six-hour urine above 0.1 mL/kg/hour from the enumerated urine output items 226557, 226558, 226559, 226560, 226561, 226563, 226564, 226565, 226567, 226584, 226627 and 226631;
- latest decision-available potassium at least 3.0 using 50971 preferentially, then 52610;
- unit-safe cumulative input minus output balance through `t0` at least +50 mL/kg with accepted events in all eight six-hour bins; +30 and +100 mL/kg are mandatory variants;
- no ECMO at `t0`, using procedure items 229529/229530 and the inherited flow chart items 224660/229270;
- no active RRT at `t0`, using inherited procedure/input/chart item sets and ICD-9 3995/5498 or ICD-10 5A1D00Z/5A1D60Z/5A1D70Z/5A1D80Z/5A1D90Z;
- no qualifying or ambiguous loop course intersecting `(t0-12h,t0]`, with loops item 221794, 228340 or 229639.

All intervals are half-open. Unknown units, invalid times, nonpositive/contradictory rows, paused/cancelled/order-only rows, unresolved ties and ambiguous gate evidence are not silently coerced. The exact inherited gate rules, item values and recent-course definitions remain in the Episode-61 proposal and support contracts.

The primary discovery estimate is retrospective clinical-time E_R. The parent’s three recording-time objects are retained unchanged:

- E_T is the audit-only maximal timestamped-gate reconstruction using rows with nonmissing `storetime<=t0`; it may add subjects and is never analyzed for treatment association.
- E_M is `E_R intersect E_T intersect {late_conflict=false}`, where a late/missing row is a conflict if adding it changes a gate, selected value, threshold, course category or ambiguity state. E_M is the principal non-adding recording-time sensitivity.
- E_G is the nested subset requiring every accepted or gate-relevant ambiguous row in a timestamped pre-`t0` gate to have `storetime<=t0`. Structural admissions/ICU/transfers/patient rows and ICD date evidence remain clockless residuals and are flagged, not called decision-observable.

Report `|E_R|,|E_T|,|E_R intersect E_T|,|E_T-E_R|,|E_R-E_T|,|E_M|,|E_G|`, Jaccard concordance and retained fractions. Jaccard <0.90 or E_M retention <0.90 is temporal-validity-inconclusive. G sparsity is a measurement-limit result, not adverse treatment evidence.

## Repaired recorded-policy strategies

From the frozen opportunity, select V0 before reading any post-`t0` extubation task. Define `G12=t0+12h` and `C0=min(G12,V0.endtime)`. Use the retrospective clinical interval endpoint for policy chronology; `storetime` can only enter E_T/M/G and timing diagnostics. The future `V0.endtime` is not placed in `X0`, a pre-boundary covariate, or any denominator history; it closes the compatible clones only when that clinical boundary is reached. The retrospectively known endpoint is used to reconstruct the state machine, not to claim that it was known at `t0`.

Qualifying loop exposure uses input items 221794, 228340 and 229639 after exact-duplicate collapse and `linkorderid` course grouping. A row requires coherent encounter keys, `endtime>starttime`, accepted status `FinishedRunning`, `ChangeDose/Rate` or `Stopped`, and positive finite amount in mg or positive finite rate in mg/hour. Routine one-minute drug pushes remain included. Unknown units, nonpositive/contradictory rows, `Paused`, literal `Bolus`, cancellations/order-only records and unresolved exact ties are ambiguous. Earliest qualifying `starttime` is onset; eMAR is not used to redefine it.

- **A12:** first qualifying loop onset in `(t0,C0)`.
- **B12:** no qualifying loop onset through `C0`.
- a loop at exactly `C0` is ambiguous in the primary analysis;
- a loop after `C0`, including after V0 ends and ventilation later re-enters, cannot satisfy A12 and does not reopen the policy window;
- if V0 ends before G12 without a loop, both clone paths close at C0 with the same shared closure; this is not a factual B label for an alternative airway trajectory;
- death, exact ambiguity, positive-gap ICU exit, ordered loop/C0, then G12 follow the inherited precedence.

This is still a retrospective recorded-policy object, not a live bedside rule. It does not claim that a clinician could know `V0.endtime` at `t0`, that an input row proves administration, or that the policy is causal.

For the primary endpoint only, select `X` after treatment classification: the earliest accepted same-stay item-227194 extubation task with `starttime` in `(t0,t0+5d]`, exact duplicates removed, and the parent’s linkage/contradiction rules retained as endpoint ascertainment. No `X`, X-linked `V`, later airway task, or post-treatment endpoint state may alter E_R, E_T, E_M, E_G, A12/B12 classification, clone entry, or denominator-model history. The inherited X-anchored analysis is a named sensitivity, not the primary design.

## Estimand, covariates and analysis

The prespecified estimand for execution is the measured-overlap equal-clone A12-minus-B12 risk-difference interval for recorded endpoints under assignment-compatible clone follow-up. It is an association estimand; it is not a causal treatment effect.

Create one A12 and one B12 clone with equal initial weight for each eligible subject. Censor each clone at the first incompatible loop/avoidance decision, C0, death or observation closure according to the inherited state machine. Fit five subject-fold, cross-fitted arm-specific L2 logistic compatibility models using only `X0` and history available strictly before each clone-hour: demographics, unit/era, prior completed-admission use/diagnoses, loop history, MAP/urine/balance trajectories, creatinine/lactate/potassium, FiO2/PEEP/SpO2/ventilation mode/sedation, pressor recency/duration/course, crystalloid/albumin and missingness. For E_T, E_M and E_G, rebuild every covariate and gate from that cohort’s as-of rows; never reuse E_R folds, models, weights, endpoints or imputations.

Use modeled probabilities only in denominator-only stabilized inverse compatibility weights, clipped to [0.01,0.99], with pooled 1st/99th-percentile truncation and fixed L2 C=1 (prespecified C=0.1 fallback only). Use normalized Hajek arm means. For bounded outcomes, report `DeltaL=pA_lower-pB_upper` and `DeltaU=pA_upper-pB_lower`; never complete-case delete or midpoint-impute unknown states.

Run 1,999 successful subject bootstraps, PCG64 seed 480048, at most 2,499 attempts and at most 5% failed attempts. Rebuild cohorts, clones, folds, preprocessing, compatibility models, weights, endpoints and all statistics in every draw. Apply centered one-sided bootstrap p-values and Holm step-down FWER 0.05 to the inherited eight adverse-oriented families: airway, RCR48, RCRD48, RRT7, RRTD7, RTHD28, true mortality and ANHATD28. Report point risks, lower/upper endpoint bounds, risk differences, one-sided limits, raw/Holm p-values, ESS, balance/calibration/positivity diagnostics and failure reasons.

Frozen minima remain E_R eligible n>=200, A12 satisfaction>=100, B12 completion/shared closure>=100, ESS>=75 each arm and >=100 definite primary events; apply independently to E_M and E_G before any inferential output. Fewer than 1,999 successful bootstraps or >5% failed attempts is computationally infeasible. Do not lower minima or filter after fitting.

The mandatory endpoint sensitivities are:

1. exact `V0.endtime=X` linkage;
2. inherited narrow and wide `V-X` linkage windows;
3. procedure-only versus chart-augmented airway failure;
4. continuous-ICU-only follow-up and the ward-gap-as-unknown bound;
5. an admission/discharge observation restriction;
6. date-only ICD airway discordance, used only as a sensitivity because it cannot order events;

A non-extubated subject is not a success merely because no event was recorded. An accepted ventilation interval that ends without `X` produces an unknown airway state, not success. No durable extubation, tube removal, spontaneous-breathing readiness, or clinical airway independence is asserted.

## Falsification, baselines and interpretation

The primary baseline is the inherited X-anchored Episode-61 analysis with all population, endpoint, model, bootstrap and margin clauses held fixed except the policy boundary. It is a falsification comparator for outcome-dependent closure, not a preferred answer. Additional baselines are: B12 avoidance through C0; +30/+100 mL/kg balance thresholds; recent-pressor duration variants (any recent, >=1h, >=6h union, >=6h contiguous, all-prior >=6h); on-time, <=1-hour-lag, no-negative-lag, FinishedRunning-only, amount-only and rate-only recording sensitivities; chart/continuous-ICU/ward-gap endpoint variants; 2.5/97.5 and untruncated weights; and hour-only denominator models. Every inferential variant independently rebuilds the pipeline.

The verifier must test, at minimum:

- a loop after V0 ends but before X is B/closed under C0, never A; a loop before V0 ends is eligible subject to exact boundaries;
- a loop exactly at C0 is primary ambiguous;
- X cannot change eligibility, strategy arm, clone entry, weights or denominator history;
- an accepted V0 ending without X is unknown airway follow-up, not success;
- X-anchored/C0 material reversal yields endpoint-boundary-inconclusive, even if the C0 numbers look supportive;
- late `storetime` affects only E_T/M/G diagnostics and never moves clinical treatment onset;
- W48 is not described as continuous observation; unknown outcomes are never converted to negative;
- correct numerical outputs paired with causal benefit, actual receipt, renal safety, durable extubation, equivalence or recommendation language fail verification.

Result branches are separate execution, validity and direction axes:

- **Supportive-recorded:** required computation and minima pass; all validity gates pass; no Holm-rejected adverse family; primary airway lower bound >+0.05; chart-augmented and continuous-ICU lower bounds >0; renal/RRT/terminal upper limits remain below +0.03/+0.05/+0.03; disposition is not adverse; E_T/M/G concordance and all feasible sensitivities show no material reversal, especially C0 versus X.
- **Adverse-FWER:** validity passes and at least one inherited adverse family is Holm-rejected. This is an observational record concern, not proof of toxicity or treatment harm.
- **Margin-falsified:** validity passes, no adjusted adverse family, and primary airway upper bound <=+0.05. This rejects the prespecified recorded-proxy improvement only.
- **Endpoint-boundary-inconclusive:** C0 cannot be reconstructed, or the C0/X comparison materially reverses. It directly identifies the repaired weakness and licenses no directional claim.
- **Temporal/measurement-validity-inconclusive:** Jaccard <0.90, E_M retention <0.90, G sparsity/discordance, or other source, observation, positivity, calibration, balance, ambiguity, contamination or process gate fails. Estimates are descriptive and safety alerts remain exploratory.
- **Recording-time-inferential-infeasible:** E_M or E_G fails minima; E_R remains a separate retrospective object, but no recording-time directional claim follows.
- **Statistically inconclusive:** computation and validity pass but no other directional branch is met. This is not no effect, equivalence or safety.
- **Primary infeasible:** source/hash/header/state-machine/required endpoint/bootstrap or E_R minimum failure. No directional conclusion follows.

Supportive results would justify external validation of an outcome-independent recorded-policy association, not a clinical protocol. Adverse results would prioritize blinded critical-care/nephrology review and a safety-focused study. Inconclusive results would show that the available EHR cannot separate the competing airway-boundary interpretations or lacks sufficient observation; they would not support treatment or safety claims.

## Clinical adjudication and evidence boundary

Computationally checkable claims include archive/catalog hashes, headers and schemas, item dictionary labels, encounter joins, `t0`/`V0`/`C0`/`X` ordering, gate/cohort membership, late-conflict reasons, loop classification, clone state transitions, bounded endpoint states, weights, bootstrap accounting, Holm ordering, diagnostics and whether final prose follows those outputs.

Automation cannot establish actual loop administration or nonreceipt, route, clinician awareness or intent, shock recovery, perfusion, congestion, urine accuracy, airway tube removal, extubation readiness, spontaneous-breathing-trial result, durable airway independence, true KDIGO AKI, renal safety, goals of care, exchangeability, causality, transportability or actionability. Those require blinded critical-care/nephrology adjudication of fuller medication, nursing, airway, hemodynamic, imaging and note evidence; validation against an external source; replication; and preferably prospective randomization. The current design does not estimate treatment effects and cannot make a bedside recommendation.
