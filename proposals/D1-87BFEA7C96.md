# Working Proposal — Episode 9 (HCC island)

## Scientific question

In cirrhotic HCC patients undergoing TACE, does the EARLY (first post-procedure)
relative laboratory response — change in total bilirubin, ALT, platelets, fibrinogen,
albumin, hemoglobin versus the pre-TACE baseline — predict NEW 90-day decompensation
(ascites, variceal/GI bleeding, hepatic encephalopathy, liver failure) beyond baseline
MELD and pre-TACE portal-hypertension history?

## Episode-9 revision (vs parent)

The parent's central weakness, measured in episodes 6-7, is the measurement gap:
diagnosis-coded new decompensation occurs in 1.0% of the follow-up cohort (87/8,542)
while unselected 90-day readmission is 32.4% — strong evidence of under-coding, and the
coded arm is underpowered for an incremental-prediction test. This child keeps the same
exposure and scientific question but restructures the deliverable into a two-arm design
with an explicit decision rule, so that EITHER arm can resolve the question:

- **Arm A (primary, powered):** early lab deltas vs UNSELECTED 90-day readmission,
  beyond a baseline model (MELD + age + sex + pre-TACE portal-hypertension history
  flags from diagnoses). Episode-9 re-measured: n=27,845 with >=3 common lab
  deltas, readmission 37.4% (10,421); episode-5 (stricter cohort n=16,438,
  32.4%) measured MELD-only AUC 0.504, +deltas LR 0.541, deltas-only GBM 0.567.
- **Arm B (clinical-decisive, event-limited):** early lab deltas vs diagnosis-coded
  NEW 90-day decompensation (terms: 腹水, 失代偿, 消化道出血, 静脉曲张/静脉破裂出血,
  肝性脑病, 肝衰竭; pre-existing decompensation excluded). Episode-9 re-measured:
  2,105/27,845 (7.6%) have any decomp-coded diagnosis in the index encounter
  (pre-existing prevalence); NEW 90-day events to be derived by the solver with the
  pre-existing exclusion (episode-6 figure 87/8,542 under a stricter cohort is
  superseded for cohort size, not for the under-coding conclusion).
- **Measurement audit (required deliverable):** quantify the gap between coded
  decompensation and readmission (prevalence, overlap, term-level counts) and report
  it as an explicit under-coding estimate with its implications.

### Decision rule (pre-specified)
- If Arm A shows incremental value (test AUC gain >= 0.02 with a significant
  likelihood-ratio test, and a graded quartile trend in at least 2 of 6 deltas) AND
  Arm B is concordant in direction (even if underpowered) → SUPPORTIVE: the early
  lab response carries prognostic information; the coded-decomp arm's event count
  bounds the claim, and clinical adjudication of a sample of readmission episodes
  (chart review) is the required next step before any decompensation-specific claim.
- If Arm A shows no incremental value (AUC gain < 0.02 or LRT non-significant) →
  ADVERSE: the early lab response adds nothing beyond baseline severity for
  near-term outcomes; the branch is closed.
- If Arm A is significant but Arm B is discordant (opposite direction) →
  INCONCLUSIVE: the signal may be driven by non-decompensation readmissions
  (planned re-TACE, tumor follow-up); defer and require adjudicated outcomes.
- Arm B alone with <100 events is never sufficient for a supportive claim
  (event-count floor), regardless of effect size.

## Baseline vs learned alternative (same question)

- Baseline: logistic regression, MELD (pre-TACE creatinine/INR/bilirubin) + age + sex
  + portal-hypertension history flags. MELD missingness ~44% → full-cohort deltas-only
  arm retained (as in parent).
- Alternative: HistGradientBoosting with the same covariates + 6 deltas; tests
  nonlinearity/interactions the linear model loses (e.g. threshold effects in
  bilirubin delta, delta × baseline interactions).
- Split: patient-level 70/30 (no patient in both). Metrics: AUC, Brier, calibration
  (5 bins), quartile outcome rates per delta.

## Falsification / interpretation (mapped to decision rule above)

Supportive = incremental signal in the powered arm with concordant coded arm.
Adverse = no incremental signal in the powered arm. Inconclusive = discordant arms or
Arm B underpowered without Arm A signal. A supportive result establishes prognostic
association only; causality, mechanism and any treatment implication require
adjudicated outcomes and external validation (not available in this dataset).

## Key references (inspected this episode; receipts in support/key-refs-ep9.json)

- [K1] Siriwardana et al. 2015, BMC Gastroenterol (PMID 26239844, DOI
  10.1186/s12875-015-0329-8): post-TACE acute hepatic decompensation in 11.7% of
  111 sessions (84 cirrhotic HCC patients); pre-procedure elevated bilirubin
  (OR 1.517) and tumour >5 cm (OR 2.410) predicted AHD. Closest work on the
  decompensation endpoint + analytes; pre-procedure levels only. Abstract-only.
- [K2] Mason et al. 2015, HPB (PMID 26374137, DOI 10.1111/hpb.12487):
  post-embolization syndrome (36.1% of 144) independently predicts death
  (HR 2.0, 95% CI 1.2-3.3). Supports the early-response premise; symptom-based,
  mortality outcome. Abstract-only.
- [K3] Khalaf et al. 2019, Radiology (PMID 30299233, DOI 10.1148/radiol.2018180257):
  pre-procedure PES prediction model, test AUC 0.62 (370 patients / 513 TACE).
  Bounds the baseline-only prediction ceiling; leaves the post-procedure
  lab-response question open. Abstract-only.

All three distinct from the parent's references (Hsin 2011, Leyh 2022, Hund 2023);
