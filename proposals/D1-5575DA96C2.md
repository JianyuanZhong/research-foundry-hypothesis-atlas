## Scientific question and repair

The unresolved question is whether the ordered final 24-hour physiology/support pattern at ICU outtime predicts an early failed transition beyond last state and documented care process, and whether that increment remains when the observation process is modeled as potentially informative. This is clinically consequential for prospective review of post-ICU monitoring and ICU-bed decisions: a superficially reassuring last value may conceal persistence or oscillation, but a heavily measured patient may also look riskier because care teams measure selectively.

The strongest claim supported before fitting is limited. The configured snapshot contains repeated dated structured vital/support observations, ICU transitions, careunit/service/transfer history and in-hospital outcomes. Residual oxygen/hemodynamic abnormalities and changing workflow are clinically plausible markers. No available evidence establishes a validated recovery phenotype, the effect size of an ordered trajectory, that an ICU return was unplanned, or that altering discharge or ward monitoring improves outcomes.

The parent’s value-free care-process null is retained. The remaining weakness is that a null based on masks, counts and workflow covariates does not itself model informative observation: measurement can be a response to latent deterioration, and conditioning on process can either leave residual ascertainment bias or remove part of a clinically meaningful signal. The substantive repair is therefore an explicit measurement-aware latent-state sensitivity, while keeping the primary claim conditional and prognostic.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis: in the full defined adult landmark cohort, B1’s prespecified ordered recovery/persistence/oscillation/support-transition block improves locked 48-hour cause-specific prediction of the first subsequent recorded ICU admission over B0, and its increment over B0/P0 is not explained by temporal-order or value permutation. A stronger, separately testable measurement-robustness claim is that the small joint state/observation model M2 yields a calibrated latent ordered-state signal beyond B1, while predicting held-out observation availability/counts; if M2 fails these checks, B1 remains an observed conditional association rather than evidence of a latent physiologic mechanism.

This proposal tests predictive information, not a causal discharge, retention, surveillance, treatment, readmission-intent, patient-benefit or policy effect. “ICU return” means a later recorded ICU stay in the same hospitalization.

## Population, landmark and temporal safety

Join icustays to admissions on subject_id,hadm_id, and to patients on subject_id. Retain one index transition per hadm_id: the chronologically first ICU row with finite intime < outtime, ICU duration at least 24 hours, and anchor_age >= 18. Preserve MIMIC’s age-over-89 representation (91). Require alive at the landmark: deathtime null or strictly later than index outtime. Do not impose a minimum observation count. The parent’s six-observed-bin population is a locked secondary sensitivity, not the primary cohort.

Report counts for invalid intervals, duplicate hospitalization/index rows, multiple ICU stays, age exclusions, short stays, missing joins, death-at-or-before-outtime, and ICU outtime after hospital discharge. The selection rule is defined before looking at outcomes.

The landmark is index icustays.outtime. No predictor may use any charttime, storetime, starttime, endtime or transfertime at or after outtime. When both event/measurement and availability times exist, both must be strictly before outtime. For an interval, require availability before outtime, intersect it with [outtime-24 hours, outtime), and cap it at outtime. Count contradictory or non-monotone source times and omit the affected feature rather than silently repairing them.

## Predictors

Use the final 24 hours in four consecutive six-hour bins. For HR, arterial/non-invasive MAP, RR, SpO2, both temperatures after Fahrenheit-to-Celsius conversion, oxygen flow and FiO2, use numeric valuenum only, with fixed validity ranges HR 20–250, RR 3–80, SpO2 50–100, MAP 20–200, temperature 25–45 C, oxygen flow 0–80 L/min and FiO2 0.21–1.0. Retain rejected-value, unit-conversion and arterial-versus-non-invasive source counts. Within a bin derive median, last value, count, valid mask and time from bin end to last valid observation. Prefer arterial MAP within a bin while retaining its source indicator. O2 device text is audit-only unless a fixed mapping is documented before fitting.

From procedure events 225792/225794, create interval presence per bin and at the landmark, using starttime, endtime and pre-outtime storetime; invasive ventilation takes precedence. Oxygen flow and FiO2 are support proxies, not complete device or waveform measurements.

The fixed B1 recovery block is:

- first-to-last change and robust four-bin slope for HR, RR, MAP, SpO2 and temperature;
- signed net change, total absolute change, sign-change count, and fraction of observed bin medians outside MAP <65, HR >100, RR >24, SpO2 <92, temperature <36 or >38 C;
- FiO2 and oxygen-flow change/slope;
- support escalation/de-escalation count, maximum support class and final-bin support class;
- a domain-availability indicator for each derived feature.

If fewer than two valid bins exist, its trajectory feature is missing, not normal-imputed. Preserve zero-, one-, two-, three- and four-bin observation patterns. Primary density strata are 0–1, 2–3 and 4 observed valid bins for the primary physiology domain; collapse only under an auditable event-support rule.

For care process, use only transfers rows with intime < outtime and services rows with transfertime < outtime. Derive first/last ICU careunit, number of pre-landmark transfer episodes, number intersecting the final 24 hours, last pre-landmark careunit/eventtype, pre-landmark service-change count and last service, ICU duration, time since last transfer/service change, six-hour counts and recency of chart events, valid measurements and procedure documentation, total distinct domains, masks and gaps. These features use no recorded physiology value. Do not use admissions.discharge_location, post-landmark destination, or any post-outtime transfer/service row as a predictor. A descriptive post-landmark destination audit is allowed only after primary prediction is frozen.

## Outcomes and exact competing-event estimands

Start follow-up at index outtime. The primary horizon is 48 hours (eight six-hour bins); the secondary horizon is seven days (28 six-hour bins).

The first subsequent recorded ICU admission is the earliest later icu/icustays.intime > outtime for the same subject_id,hadm_id, within the horizon and before death. Death is hosp/admissions.deathtime > outtime within the horizon and before any later ICU admission. If later ICU admission and death tie at the same timestamp, mark ordering unresolved and exclude it from the primary timed competing-risk analysis, reporting the count and a prespecified sensitivity. If hospital_expire_flag=1 but deathtime is missing, timing is unresolved: exclude it from the primary timed analysis and run a sensitivity assigning death at dischtime only when no later ICU stay is recorded.

Censor at the earliest of horizon, first event, hospital dischtime or unresolved event timing. Death after a later ICU admission remains descriptive and does not change the first-event estimand. Report exactly these linked estimands:

1. Cause-specific cumulative incidence of first subsequent recorded ICU admission, with death a competing event.
2. Cause-specific cumulative incidence of first death before later ICU admission, with ICU admission a competing event.
3. Composite first recorded transition failure, the first of ICU admission or death, used only for threshold/deployment contrast.

These are recorded in-hospital transitions, not adjudicated deterioration or readmission intent.

## Models and estimands of model comparison

Use deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 splits under five fixed hash seeds; no subject_id or hadm_id crosses splits. Fit imputation, scaling, decay, category encoding, latent-state normalization and all observation models on training subjects only. All models use the same cohort, landmark, windows, outcome labels, horizons and test subjects.

### B0: strong interpretable baseline

Fit L2-regularized pooled-logistic discrete-time cause-specific hazards for ICU admission and death with interval indicators. Inputs are age, gender, admission type/location, ICU duration, first/last ICU careunit, last valid physiologic values and support state, recency/count/mask/gap for every domain, and explicitly missing process channels. This is the clinical comparator for last-state risk plus ascertainment intensity.

### P0: value-free care-process null

Fit the same hazard architecture with no valuenum, support intensity, trajectory value or physiologic threshold. Inputs are demographics/admission metadata, ICU duration, first/last careunit, transfer/service features, chart/procedure counts, domain masks, recencies and gaps. P0 is an observability/workflow benchmark, not a causal adjustment. P0 versus B0 quantifies the added information in last-state values; it cannot prove what any residual means.

### B1: interpretable ordered recovery model

Fit B0 plus the fixed B1 recovery block. The primary estimands are the test-set difference in 48-hour ICU-admission CIF/Brier/calibration between B1 and B0 and between B1 and P0. A physiology-isolated model without transfer/service/measurement-intensity features is a descriptive decomposition only and must not replace the adjusted comparison.

### M2: measurement-aware latent-state alternative

Fit a two-dimensional latent state z_t, t=1,...,4, with a prespecified low-dimensional transition model over the four ordered bins. Standardized domain values are generated from z_t with robust Student-t errors and fixed support indicators. For each domain and bin, availability/count/recency is modeled by a Bernoulli/Poisson observation submodel conditional on z_t and the P0 process covariates. Two competing-risk hazard heads use z_t and the same baseline/process covariates to produce the three event predictions. Estimate parameters on training subjects by a fixed-seed variational-EM or equivalent bounded optimizer, validate joint loss and held-out observation-channel likelihood, and early-stop at a fixed patience with a maximum of 40 iterations/epochs. The latent dimension, four-bin structure, error family, regularization and optimizer are fixed before test evaluation.

M2 is not a claim that the latent state is “true physiology.” It asks whether a compact ordered state and an explicit informative-observation model explain held-out values, observation patterns and outcomes better than B1. Its required nulls are: (a) M2 with measured values removed from the observation equation, retaining P0 process information; and (b) within-patient bin-order permutation with marginal values and masks retained. If the observation submodel is poorly calibrated or M2 is non-identifiable, its mechanistic interpretation is not supported even if its hazard score is favorable.

Report 48-hour and seven-day cause-specific Brier scores, integrated Brier score, CIF calibration intercept/slope, AUROC/AUPRC and five-seed variability. Use 2,000 subject-level paired bootstrap replicates when event support permits; otherwise report the effective number and reason. Report M2 held-out observation-channel likelihood/calibration, posterior state uncertainty, transition/loadings diagnostics and M2-minus-B1 uncertainty. Do not use an absolute Brier reduction such as 0.005 as a clinical-utility threshold.

## Clinically meaningful contrasts

For each locked model, report 48-hour predictions for the composite, ICU-admission CIF and death CIF. At thresholds 1%, 2%, 5% and 10%, report per 1,000 index transitions: flagged fraction, observed composite events among flagged/unflagged, sensitivity, false-positive fraction, B1-versus-B0 reclassification counts and event composition, and net benefit under a predeclared threshold-to-harm convention. For ICU admission, show a competing-risk decision curve using ICU-admission CIF and display deaths before ICU admission separately.

Use identical thresholds and treat-all/treat-none references for B0, P0, B1 and M2. Stratify by observation density and last careunit only where support permits. These are observed-risk stratification summaries. They do not estimate benefit from ICU retention, extra ward monitoring or a discharge policy. Actionability requires clinician-defined harms/benefits, new-site calibration, adjudication of plannedness/goals of care and prospective impact evaluation.

## Falsification and robustness

Required computational audits are zero post-landmark predictor timestamps; one row per index hospitalization; no identifier crossing splits; complete exclusion and unresolved-event ledgers; first-event ordering and competing-event counts; observation-density flow; rejected-value/unit-conversion counts; transfer/service timing; and post-landmark destination exclusion.

Run these locked perturbations:

1. Last-bin-only model.
2. P0 and B0 process/value decomposition.
3. Value permutation within observation-density and event-rate strata, preserving masks/counts/process.
4. Within-patient bin-order permutation when at least two valid bins exist.
5. Removal of masks/delta-time channels.
6. Removal of careunit/transfer/service channels.
7. Label permutation within event-rate strata.
8. M2 process-only observation null and observation-submodel ablation.
9. Parent six-observed-bin cohort as a secondary sensitivity.
10. Composite, cause-specific CIF and the stated unresolved-death sensitivity.
11. Descriptive post-landmark destination audit, never a primary predictor.
12. If event support permits, an anchor-year-group holdout as an internal drift diagnostic; it cannot establish external transportability.

Support for the conditional recovery claim requires B1 to improve B0 on the locked full cohort for the primary 48-hour ICU-admission estimand with uncertainty compatible with a nonzero increment, show no material death-calibration harm, and lose its trajectory increment under value and order permutation. The increment must not be confined to only the most-observed stratum and should remain directionally consistent after the prespecified care-process removal sensitivity. Stronger measurement-robustness support additionally requires M2 to improve or match B1 with calibrated observation-channel predictions, stable latent-state diagnostics across seeds, and loss of the ordered increment under M2 order/value perturbation. These criteria support only conditional observed prognostic information.

Adverse results are P0 matching/exceeding B1, no B1 increment after B0, gain limited to highly observed patients, disappearance after care-process adjustment, worsened death calibration, persistence after value/order permutation, label-permutation discrimination, reversal under competing-event handling, or M2 improving B0 but not B1. A favorable M2 hazard score with poor observation-channel fit is adverse to the measurement-aware interpretation. These findings refute the specified incremental recovery claim, not every possible use of discharge-time data.

Inconclusive results include sparse events or poor positivity in density/careunit strata, unresolved event timing, invalid/contradictory timestamps, weak joins, broad uncertainty, seed instability, M2 non-identifiability or convergence failure, and observation-model miscalibration. Failure of a threshold contrast does not prove absence of predictive information. No automatic analysis can establish plannedness, intent, preventability, bedside readiness, treatment limitation, causal benefit or clinical appropriateness.

## Availability limits and reference disposition

The core and note files provide structured ICU/hospital events, careunit/service/transfer labels, measurement availability times, oxygen/support proxies, admissions outcomes and narrative notes. Bedside work of breathing, functional readiness, cognition, family preference, treatment limitations, clinician intent, transfer rationale, actual oxygen-device fidelity and planned versus unplanned ICU return are unavailable or not reliably adjudicated. MIMIC is a single source/site snapshot; the internal anchor-year sensitivity is not external validation.

The three research-ambition demonstrations are context, not a reproduction target. The structured disease-sequence example supports considering a bounded ordered-history adaptation, but its UK Biobank population and external claims do not transfer here. The Bayesian demonstration’s genetic inputs are not configured for this MIMIC question, so that branch is deferred. The cancer demonstration’s main article and complete STAR Methods are unavailable in this bundle; only its supplement is available, so no full multimodal method claim is used. No demonstration is treated as evidence for this hypothesis.

The residual-instability MIMIC seed and related AKI, diuresis, antimicrobial-intent and asynchronous-recovery seed branches remain separate questions. A text model is deferred pending validated, leakage-safe extraction and clinical review. Raw-waveform/image models are unavailable. A causal discharge-policy analysis is deferred because treatment assignment, intent, action outcomes and adequate clinical adjudication are not identified.
