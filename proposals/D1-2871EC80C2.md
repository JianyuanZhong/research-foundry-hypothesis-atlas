# Episode 12: a threshold-free stochastic-dominance challenge to the pH-conditioned SpO2 mapping

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

When released monitor SpO2 is 96–97%, is the acidemic PaO2 distribution broadly shifted upward across clinically relevant sub-hyperoxemic thresholds, or is the episode-11 restricted-mean contrast compatible with curve crossing or a difference confined to selected portions of the distribution?

This matters because a pooled restricted mean can be positive even when acidemic observations are more likely to have low PaO2 over part of the range. Before a pH-aware oxygen-assessment strategy is prospectively tested, clinicians need to know whether acidemia signals a consistently upward recorded SpO2-to-PaO2 mapping, rather than an average assembled from opposing distributional changes.

The strongest available EICU evidence supports only feasibility and an inconclusive average displacement: the parent reconstructed 822 SpO2-band observations, 705 acidemic/normal observations, and an outcome-blind measured-overlap population of 237 rows in 57 cells and nine hospitals. Its symmetric overlap-weighted difference in E[min(PaO2,120)] was +5.497 mm Hg (95% hierarchical-bootstrap interval −2.673 to +11.852), with 5,000/5,000 replicates defined, ESS 87.7/105.7, maximum site weight 45.3%, and no leave-site-out sign reversal. This neither supports nor falsifies the parent’s +10-mm Hg magnitude claim.

The parent’s inspected full-text Delgado study (Antioxidants 2026; DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712) supports physiologic plausibility and a large single-center concurrent association, including pH and FiO2 modification, but not EICU transportability or a uniform shift. The inspected OXY-BREATHES systematic review (Critical Care Medicine 2026; DOI 10.1097/CCM.0000000000007031; PMCID PMC13134654) shows that oxygen targets are clinically consequential and treatment effects remain uncertain; it does not test pH-conditioned measurement mapping. The research-ambition demonstrations were inspected only for availability and rigor expectations and are not clinical evidence for this topic.

**Primary unresolved hypothesis:** in the parent’s exact 237-row measured-overlap population, the acidemic weighted survival probability P(PaO2>t) exceeds the normal-pH weighted survival probability by at least 5 percentage points at every prespecified threshold t in {70, 80, 90, 100, 110} mm Hg.

This is a simultaneous broad-distribution claim. It is stronger and more clinically interpretable than a positive average alone, while avoiding the sparse ten-event PaO2>120 endpoint. The five-point margin is a prespecified population-level probability contrast, not a validated individual MCID.

## Variables, estimand, baseline, and analysis

For cell s with counts n1s and n0s, weight each acidemic row by n0s/(n1s+n0s) and each normal row by n1s/(n1s+n0s), then normalize separately by exposure.

For each t in T={70,80,90,100,110}, compute

`D(t) = weighted P(PaO2>t | acidemic) - weighted P(PaO2>t | normal)`.

The primary estimand is `M = min_{t in T} D(t)`. The hypothesis is `M >= +0.05`. Report every D(t), its two group risks, M and the threshold attaining M. Do not replace M by an average, best threshold, p-value, or model-selected cutpoint.

The fixed baseline is the parent restricted-mean contrast `Delta_RM120` and its group means. Other descriptive baselines are the weighted median contrast, within-cell probability that an acidemic PaO2 exceeds a normal one (ties 0.5), and inherited PaO2>120 risk difference. These cannot rescue the primary.

Use the parent’s deterministic 5,000-replicate hierarchical bootstrap with seed 20260910: sample nine hospitals with replacement; within each hospital copy sample its `uniquepid` clusters with replacement; assign new hospital-copy and person-copy IDs; rebuild typed overlap cells using hospital-copy ID; discard replicate cells without both exposures; recompute weights, all five D(t), and M. Report defined/failed replicates and percentile intervals for each D(t) and M.

Primary diagnostics force an inconclusive classification if >10% replicates are undefined, either exposure ESS <50, one original site supplies >50% of total overlap weight, or deletion of any site with >=10 overlap rows makes any D(t) negative. Report weight sums, ESS, maximum normalized row weight, site fractions, and leave-site-out D(t).

Outcome-blind sensitivities rebuild overlap and repeat the full vector/M after earliest row per person, earliest row per stay, person-within-exposure downweighting, tighter 5-point FiO2/10-mm Hg PaCO2/day cells, 12-hour blocks, first 24 hours, last rather than median SpO2, 60-minute FiO2 lookback, chart-offset FiO2 without entry restriction, complete PaCO2, closed nonzero ventilation intervals, and deletion of each relevant site. Alternate FiO2 labels and broader airway proxies remain clearly secondary. Any prespecified sensitivity with an adverse M forces overall robustness-inconclusive language.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** lower 95% bootstrap bound for M is >=+0.05 and all diagnostics pass. Allowed conclusion: “Within this retrospective measured-overlap construction, acidemic observations had at least a five-point higher probability of exceeding every prespecified PaO2 threshold from 70 through 110 mm Hg.” This does not establish a causal shift, physiology, or action.

**Adverse:** upper 95% bound for M is <+0.05. If the bound is below zero, at least one threshold is inconsistent with even nonnegative broad dominance. If all pointwise D(t) are positive but M’s upper bound is below .05, report a smaller/nonmaterial broad pattern, adverse to the five-point claim.

**Inconclusive:** interval for M contains .05, any mandatory diagnostic fails, or required sensitivities conflict. A favorable restricted mean with adverse/inconclusive M means an average upward displacement without simultaneous five-point dominance; a supportive M with adverse PaO2>120 RD means broad sub-120 dominance without evidence of increased hyperoxemia-tail risk. Neither permits promotion of another endpoint.

Fixtures: M=.07, CI .052–.11 with all diagnostics passing is supportive; M=.03, CI −.02–.09 is inconclusive; M=.03, CI .01–.045 is adverse to +.05 but supports a smaller positive minimum; M=−.04, CI −.08–−.01 is opposite/adverse; M=.08, CI −.01–.16 is inconclusive. Correct computation paired with causal, treatment, device, tissue-hyperoxia, or recommendation language must fail verification.

## Evidence limits and verifier boundary

EICU cannot establish arterial specimen identity, co-oximeter SaO2, waveform synchronization/artifact, device make/calibration, delivered FiO2, temperature, 2,3-DPG, exact ventilation persistence, clinician intent, or missing-at-random measurement. Clinical adjudication must validate specimens, airway status, and implausible/extreme values. Prospective synchronized measurement is needed for physiologic validation or a pH-aware pre-draw rule; external validation is required for transportability; an interventional study is required for any claim about extra ABGs, oxygen titration, toxicity, benefit, or harm.

The verifier can check hashes/headers, exact labels/ranges, revision conflict handling, joins/times, cohort/cell reconciliation, weights, D(t), M, bootstrap copying/reconstruction, diagnostics, sensitivities, and whether conclusions cite computed outputs and uncertainty. It cannot establish specimen/device truth, uniform physiology beyond recorded measurements, unmeasured-confounding control, clinical validity of the five-point margin, transportability, causality, utility, or treatment benefit.

The attempted episode-12 FiO2 subgroup audit is not evidence: its first run failed before results due to a filter-expression coding error, and the repaired run returned zero rows inconsistent with the independently frozen 237-row parent reconciliation, indicating an implementation mismatch. Neither run may be cited to support or refute effect modification. All four configured datasets remain read-only accessible; derived files remain in the workspace and no private rows or notes were sent to public search.
