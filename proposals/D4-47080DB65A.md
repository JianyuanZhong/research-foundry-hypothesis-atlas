# Certified baseline discordance with an explicit capacity-to-target assessment policy

## Lineage and substantive advance

This is a substantive successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parents' core Direction-A estimand: baseline-only incremental information for an exported, observed repeat kidney-status state among participants with a complete qualifying repeat assessment. It preserves the selected cohort, current UKB snapshot, exact source and schema bindings, no-imputation and fail-closed semantic/assay/date certificate, repeated nested cross-fitting, paired Brier primary contrast, fixed-capacity secondary contrast, falsification controls, aggregate-only reporting, and strict noncausal limits.

The unresolved decision gap is not whether a biomarker is associated with a later value. It is whether a certified score can support a transparent workload rule for prioritizing confirmatory assessment. The prior protocols report a fixed 10% capacity contrast, but do not predeclare how an assessment service would translate capacity into a target event-concentration requirement, or how to distinguish “the target is achievable within the available capacity” from “the target is not attained.” This child adds one prespecified, identifiable capacity-to-target estimand without claiming that ordering a test improves outcomes.

The advance is a policy characterization, not a new clinical endpoint: at fixed capacities, estimate observed repeat-state concentration, and additionally estimate the minimum prespecified score capacity needed to attain a prespecified observed-state capture target. The target and capacity grid are operational protocol constants, not asserted clinical utilities or treatment thresholds. No result may be described as net benefit, cost-effectiveness, harm avoided, diagnostic accuracy for disease, or testing benefit.

## Evidence boundary and hypotheses

The strongest claim supported by the current local files is structural only: the UKB rectangular export contains the required field-instance columns in one-to-one `eid`-joinable tables. Local metadata certifies field 31 coding (`0=Female`, `1=Male`) and field 21022 as age at recruitment in years, but does not certify the identities, units, assay/platform, missing-value meanings, or instance comparability of fields 30700 and 30720, nor the exact semantics of field 53 dates. The local files do not establish CKD, chronicity, measured GFR, progression, testing decisions, outcomes, utilities, or causal effects. Expected parent anchors are diagnostics only and are not data.

Conditional on an approved certificate passing every gate, the confirmatory hypothesis is:

> In participants with certified baseline measurements, baseline creatinine-equation eGFR at least 60, and a complete exported instance-1 observation whose certified assessment-date interval is 2–8 years, adding a prespecified baseline cystatin-C discordance indicator to a frozen baseline creatinine-eGFR/age/sex model yields reproducible out-of-sample information for the exported repeat creatinine threshold state and can reduce the capacity required to capture a prespecified fraction of those observed repeat states.

The primary estimand remains the paired out-of-fold Brier improvement. The policy estimand is schedule-marginal and conditional on the selected observed complete-repeat cohort. It is not a fixed-horizon risk, incidence, disease progression, first occurrence, causal treatment effect, test-ordering effect, utility, or benefit estimate.

## Models and primary estimand

Freeze all features before reading repeat outcomes for model fitting. `M0` uses sex, a fixed restricted-cubic-spline basis for baseline eGFRcr and baseline age, with the parent-prespecified interaction terms and training-fold preprocessing. `M1` contains every `M0` term plus exactly `E` and `E*(eGFRcr_0-60)/10`. No continuous cystatin value, repeat biomarker, date, `t`, additional covariate, outcome-guided transformation, or alternate threshold is permitted. Use the parent's fixed model family, equal non-intercept penalization, nested five-fold tuning, outer seeds `[260911,260912,260913,260914,260915,260916,260917,260918,260919,260920]`, identical stratified folds within all `E×Y` cells, and fold-local preprocessing. Retain ten outer out-of-fold probabilities per participant and average arithmetically. Clip only log-loss probabilities to `[10^-6,1-10^-6]`, and report aggregate clipping counts.

The primary contrast remains

`Delta_Brier = 1000 * [mean((Y-p0)^2) - mean((Y-p1)^2)]`.

The inherited secondary fixed-capacity contrast at `q=0.10` remains

`Delta_rank10 = 1000 * (sum_{topK(M1)}Y - sum_{topK(M0)}Y)/K`, with `K=ceil(0.10*N)`.

Top-set ties use ascending SHA-256 of UTF-8 `direction-A-capacity-v2|` concatenated with the participant identifier; the hash and identifier are never published. The contrast is event concentration among the same observed attenders, not testing benefit. Use the inherited paired participant bootstrap (seed 260921, 2,000 successful replicates, maximum 2,500 attempts, percentile 95% interval and symmetric null-centered plus-one p-value) after all primary gates.

## New prespecified capacity-to-target policy estimand

This is the substantive child advance. Treat `q` as the fraction of `S_A` that a hypothetical review queue can prioritize, not as a treatment assignment. Use the fixed grid `Q={0.01,0.02,...,0.20}` and, for each model `m`, select exactly `K_m(q)=ceil(q*N)` eligible participants by descending frozen OOF probability with the inherited deterministic tie rule. For each selected set define

`C_m(q) = sum_{i in top K_m(q)} Y_i / sum_{i in S_A} Y_i`  (fraction of observed repeat states captured),

`PPV_m(q) = sum_{i in top K_m(q)} Y_i / K_m(q)`  (observed state fraction in the selected set), and

`Delta_C(q) = 100 * [C_1(q)-C_0(q)]` (percentage-point capacity contrast).

If the denominator is zero, all capture fractions and the policy estimand are undefined and the declared analysis stops as sparse/inconclusive; it must not report a fabricated zero.

Predeclare the operational target `gamma=0.50`: capture at least one half of the observed repeat `Y` states. Define the capacity-to-target functional on the fixed grid, without interpolation,

`q_gamma(m) = min{q in Q: lower_98_CI[C_m(q)] >= gamma}`,

where the lower simultaneous 98% bootstrap bound is computed over all `q in Q` for that model. If no grid point satisfies the target, record `NOT_ATTAINED_WITHIN_20_PERCENT`; do not extrapolate. The policy contrast is

`Delta_qgamma = q_gamma(M0)-q_gamma(M1)`,

with positive values meaning that M1 reaches the operational capture target at a smaller observed review capacity. If either model is not attained, report the censored ordered result (`M1 attained / M0 not attained`, `both not attained`, etc.) rather than assigning a numeric capacity. The target `gamma` and grid are protocol constants for workload characterization only; they are not clinical utilities, diagnostic thresholds, or claims about the value of testing.

The policy component is tested only after the primary Brier gate passes. It requires (a) at least 8/10 positive repeat-specific Brier gains, (b) successful bootstrap completion, (c) identical eligible membership and exactly `ceil(qN)` selected members for every q, (d) no material fold or repeat instability, (e) M0 outperforms the intercept-only prevalence predictor, and (f) no materially positive exposure-permutation or random-feature gain. A policy-supportive result requires a positive `Delta_qgamma` or a strictly favorable censored ordering, with simultaneous 98% uncertainty and the prespecified `gamma` actually attained by M1. A capacity improvement without primary Brier support is not sufficient for a supportive conclusion. A favorable `q_gamma` result with `C_m(q)` intervals that cross the target is inconclusive, not a threshold recommendation.

Report the full aggregate curve at every q, including `C_m(q)`, `PPV_m(q)`, selected event counts, and paired `Delta_C(q)`, but do not select a more favorable q after seeing results. The inherited `q=0.10` contrast is retained for continuity, not promoted to a new co-primary. A descriptive threshold-crossing curve or alternative gamma is prohibited unless registered as a separate future study.

## Falsification and leakage controls

Perform the inherited fold-local exposure-label permutation within each outer training partition and its inner folds, leaving each test-fold exposure untouched for evaluation and preserving model dimensions, seeds, tuning and feature construction. Repeat all ten outer repeats using a distinct fixed seed. A materially positive permuted gain, especially with comparable sign, blocks support. Replace `E` and its interaction with a separately seeded training-fold-only random Bernoulli feature preserving training-fold prevalence; it must not reproduce the primary or policy gain. These controls are diagnostics, not alternative hypotheses.

Before policy interpretation audit that every outer test identifier is disjoint from every fitting/tuning row, removal of `t` changes no eligibility or feature/tuning/ranking input, top-q sets have the same eligible membership and exact cardinality, q-grid calculations use only frozen OOF predictions, no q or gamma was chosen after outcomes, the event denominator is invariant across model selections, and no one outer repeat drives the capacity-to-target contrast. Run a reversed-label or outcome-permutation null only as declared in the inherited protocol, with complete refitting and matched folds; incomplete null refits block interpretation.

## Decision branches and limits

**Supportive** requires certificate authorization, every state transition, exact cohort reconstruction, finite fits, comparator signal, primary calibration/support/event gates, at least 8/10 favorable repeat-specific Brier gains, successful bootstrap, a positive Brier interval excluding zero with the inherited p-value gate, and then a policy result satisfying the fixed `gamma=0.50` and simultaneous-98% rules. The conclusion is limited to reproducible baseline incremental information and a workload-capacity characterization for the protocol-defined exported repeat state among `S_A`.

**Adverse** is a wholly negative authorized primary contrast, reproducible calibration loss, repeat-specific signs favoring M0, or a reproducible policy worsening under the fixed grid. This is evidence against incremental information or concentration under this frozen comparator and selected schedule; it does not show that cystatin C has no value in other populations or uses.

**Inconclusive** includes intervals crossing zero or the target, failure of repeat stability, conflicting primary and policy metrics, sparse events, unstable fits, failure to attain the target for both models, or incomplete bootstrap/null analysis. No q or gamma recommendation follows.

**Neutral technical stop** includes absent, stale, contradictory, or unverifiable certificate evidence; source/schema mismatch; unresolved units, missing codes, assay comparability or date semantics; equation test failure; or invalid state transition. It is not adverse or inconclusive biological evidence.

Automatic verification can establish file and schema identity, joins, certificate digests and evidence coverage, state ordering, certified conversion and missing-code handling, equation arithmetic conditional on certification, masks, fold isolation, baseline-only use, exact top-q cardinalities, q-grid and target functional arithmetic, falsifier execution, bootstrap completion, and branch consistency. It cannot establish the truth of external certificate evidence, assay clinical validity, measured GFR, CKD/chronicity/progression, why participants attended, outcomes for nonattenders, testing benefit, harms, utility, cost, treatment effects, or transportability. Those require steward metadata, clinical adjudication and a prospective decision-impact study with observed testing decisions, downstream outcomes, costs, preferences and harms.

No participant identifiers, rows, hashes keyed to identifiers, or individual predictions may be published. No execution result is claimed by this proposal.
