TITLE
Auditable 48-hour documented-liberation proxy with a preregistered gate for noncausal comparison in eICU

SUBSTANTIVE CHANGE FROM PARENT
This child resolves the parent's remaining executable gap: it makes the 48-hour durability outcome an auditable state machine with explicit observation, discharge, death, recurrence, and unknown states, and it specifies a deterministic gate before any comparative analysis. The parent had named a union/durability algorithm but left ambiguity about when a landmark was genuinely observed for 48 hours, how discharge and death were handled, and which recurrent markers invalidated durability. This version separates a strict observed-48-hour proxy from a discharge-bridged sensitivity proxy, prevents discharge-censoring from being silently coded as success, and fixes the analysis population and estimand before looking at comparative results. The association remains explicitly noncausal and is forbidden when measurement concordance, overlap, documentation density, placebo, or outcome information is inadequate.

CLINICAL QUESTION AND FALSIFIABLE HYPOTHESIS
Among adult ICU stays with invasive-ventilation evidence and a strict, pre-decision physiologic screen, do the eICU care-plan states “Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation” and “Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial” identify reproducibly different documented liberation trajectories? Clinically, the unresolved question is whether a routinely recorded daily-evaluation decision contains information about subsequent sustained liberation beyond measured oxygenation, support, respiratory rate, saturation, hemodynamics, and admission/trajectory factors. This could inform whether a prospective readiness workflow is worth testing, but cannot establish that a patient was bedside-ready or that an SBT occurred.

Validation hypothesis: each label will have sufficient, temporally ordered agreement with independent liberation markers (RT Vent On/Off = Off and/or exact endotracheal-tube-removal treatment), similar marker availability across strategies and hospitals, and low pre-landmark placebo signal to justify a conditional association analysis.

Gated secondary hypothesis: only if every validation/overlap gate below passes, daily-evaluation documentation is associated with a higher 72-hour probability of the strict durable documented-liberation proxy than no-trial documentation in the restricted overlap population. The estimand is a standardized, adjusted association under observed documentation, not an intervention effect, effect of an SBT, or recommendation about extubation practice.

SUPPORTED VERSUS UNTESTED
The completed full-source feasibility scan supports that the exact labels and candidate component tables exist and that a strict cohort is computable. It retained 540 first-qualifying-stay person-landmarks (309 evaluation, 231 no-trial), with only three hospitals having at least 10 landmarks per strategy. Preliminary 72-hour component patterns were sparse for independent markers: two marker types within 24 hours occurred in 18/309 evaluation and 4/231 no-trial landmarks. These are feasibility results, not liberation rates or treatment effects. No available result establishes true extubation, SBT performance, readiness, or benefit. The proposed durable state machine and gates are the untested, falsifiable contribution.

COHORT, LANDMARK, AND PRE-LANDMARK FILTERING
Use fixed L in {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}. A candidate has its first exact exposure label in [L,L+360]; the exposure time D is that first label offset. Same-offset contradictory strategy labels are excluded as ambiguous. Require adult age, observation beyond D (unitdischargeoffset > D), strict pre-L screen, invasive evidence strictly before L, no pre-L EOL discussion, and no Spontaneous - adequate marker in (L-1440,L). Retain all eligible landmarks in the selected lowest-unitvisitnumber stay, cluster repeated landmarks by uniquepid, and report attrition by every filter. No rows after L may define eligibility or covariates.

Strict screen is pre-L: FiO2 <=0.50; PEEP <=8; at least two SaO2 observations in the preceding 120 minutes with median 88 to <100; latest RR 8-35/min; at least one MAP observation with median >=65. FiO2 and PEEP are extracted from the specified respiratoryCharting labels (FIO2/FiO2/Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen and PEEP/PEEP-CPAP); exact label matching variants are listed in the executable codebook. Missing is not normal. Invasive evidence requires a pre-L ventilated care-plan state and/or corroborating respiratoryCare ETT/endotracheal/tracheostomy plus settings, with the component used recorded. Admission and trajectory covariates may use patient, admissionDx, apacheApsVar/apachePatientResult, diagnosis, lab, medication, infusionDrug, prior care-plan states, measurement counts, and time since last measurement, but all values must be strictly before L. Point infusionDrug charting cannot prove absence or duration of vasoactive/sedative support.

48-HOUR DURABLE PROXY: DETERMINISTIC STATE MACHINE
For each landmark, search component offsets in the closed interval (D, D+4320] and select the earliest timestamp; exact ties retain all components. Components are:
A = carePlanGeneral exact Spontaneous - adequate;
B = respiratoryCharting exact RT Vent On/Off with value Off;
C = treatment exact endotracheal tube removal string.
The primary initiation algorithm is independent-first: B or C, because A is a care-plan state and cannot by itself validate a liberation event. Report A-only separately. The union algorithm (A/B/C) is a sensitivity and validation algorithm, never silently substituted for primary.

For an initiated component at t, define the strict observation window W=(t,t+2880]. Search all recurrence channels in carePlanGeneral, respiratoryCharting, treatment, and respiratoryCare. A recurrence at r invalidates durability if r is in W and is any of: RT Vent On/Continued; exact ETT-insertion treatment string when available; a Ventilated care-plan state; or respiratoryCare airwaytype containing ETT/endotracheal/tracheostomy together with a ventilator setting. A same-minute recurrence is retained and invalidates the candidate. Care-plan recurrence is labeled lower specificity; results are reported both with all recurrence channels and with independent-only recurrence (RT On/Continued, ETT insertion, or airway+settings).

Classify exactly one endpoint state in this order:
(i) durable_strict=1 if t+2880 <= unitdischargeoffset, the patient is not dead before t+2880, and no recurrence occurs in W;
(ii) death_before_durable if death discharge occurs after t but before or at t+2880;
(iii) recurrent_invasive if a qualifying recurrence occurs before t+2880 and before death;
(iv) unknown_discharge if live discharge occurs after t but before t+2880 without a qualifying recurrence (there is no 48-hour in-ICU observation);
(v) unknown_truncation if ICU observation ends or source follow-up is otherwise insufficient before the window and cannot be classified above.
A candidate with no component is no_initiation, not durable failure. Candidates with an initiation after death/discharge are impossible by construction. Report first marker time, marker type, exact recurrence timestamp/type, time observed after t, and all missing channels.

The discharge-bridged endpoint is a prespecified sensitivity only: count live discharge to a non-ICU location as bridged_durable when t precedes discharge and no recurrence is observed before discharge; never merge it with durable_strict, and never interpret it as proof of extubation. Use 48 hours as primary; 1-, 12-, and 24-hour no-recurrence windows are sensitivity analyses. Report competing death, unknown discharge, and unknown truncation rather than treating them as negative or positive by default. Also search outcomes through D+10080 for recurrent invasive markers, ICU death, and discharge.

VALIDATION AND COMPARATIVE GATES (FROZEN BEFORE ASSOCIATION)
Compute by strategy, L, hospital, and marker channel with exact denominators and 95% Wilson intervals:
1. At least 3 hospitals have >=10 landmarks in each strategy.
2. For every component required for the independent-first strict endpoint, availability within those restricted hospitals is >=10% in each strategy; care-plan-only and independent-only algorithms are estimable in both.
3. Absolute strategy difference in component availability and in ascertainment of the strict 48-hour observation window is <=20 percentage points, unless an adjustment/sensitivity plan is specified before unblinding outcomes.
4. At least 50 landmarks per strategy remain after hospital restriction and at least 10 strict durable events per strategy are observed; otherwise no model is fitted.
5. Among union-positive landmarks, at least 10% in each strategy have two distinct marker types with first timestamps within 1440 minutes, and the difference in that concordance is <=20 percentage points. A-only is not concordance.
6. The propensity/overlap audit has at least 80% of restricted landmarks with estimated propensity in [0.10,0.90], effective sample size >=50 per strategy after overlap weighting, no individual weight >10, and all hospital-strategy cells retained in the analysis have observations in both arms.
7. Temporal placebo uses the identical component algorithms in (L-1440,L] before the landmark, with event initiation requiring t <= L. The post-decision and pre-landmark standardized risk differences must not have the same direction with placebo magnitude >0.05; if the placebo 95% interval is wide enough to include a clinically material value, label the gate inconclusive rather than passing.
8. Recurrent-marker channel availability, strict observation, and death ascertainment are sufficient to calculate uncertainty; missing recurrence channels are explicitly disclosed and cannot be imputed as no recurrence.
Any failed criterion blocks the association and yields validation adverse or inconclusive according to the rules below. Thresholds are not relaxed after observing the effect.

GATED NONCAUSAL ASSOCIATION
If and only if all gates pass, define time zero as D and retain one observed strategy, the earliest label in [L,L+360]. The primary estimand is the standardized 72-hour risk difference for durable_strict among the restricted overlap population, with death_before_durable and no initiation/unknown states reported as competing or unavailable categories. Use a prespecified multinomial outcome model or weighted empirical risks with hospital fixed effects, and overlap weighting or doubly robust standardization based only on pre-L covariates, decision delay D-L, and landmark L. Do not use post-D markers, treatment components, or discharge information as covariates. Use hospital-level bootstrap and uniquepid-clustered inference; report point estimate, 95% CI, effective sample size, weight distribution, covariate standardized mean differences, hospital-specific estimates, and leave-one-hospital-out estimates. A model is not fitted if separation, positivity, sparse outcomes, or unstable weights occur. The former clone/censor/weight emulation is not primary; it may be an independently reviewed sensitivity only after this gate and only if risk sets are verified.

RESULT INTERPRETATION AND FALSIFICATION
Supportive validation requires the independent-first component to be sufficiently available, two-type timing to be coherent, strict observation and recurrence ascertainment similar across strategies, no material temporal placebo, adequate overlap, and no material reversal between independent-only, care-plan-only, union, and durability definitions. If the gated association is positive with uncertainty excluding zero, it supports a robust association worth prospective validation, not benefit or causality. Concordant null results can support that these labels do not discriminate documented trajectories under this operationalization.

Adverse validation is substantial strategy-specific availability/observation imbalance, high marker discordance, a material same-direction pre-L placebo, or systematic reversal of algorithms. This means the labels/proxies may reflect documentation practice or unmeasured trajectory; it does not mean daily evaluation is harmful or ineffective. A negative gated association argues against assuming a beneficial association but cannot establish causal harm.

Inconclusive validation is failed overlap, fewer than three minimally overlapping hospitals, sparse independent markers/events, unstable estimation, or intervals spanning clinically important benefit and harm. The already observed three-hospital overlap and sparse independent concordance make inconclusive results plausible and scientifically valuable. Unknown discharge/truncation must remain visible; a high rate means the endpoint is not validated, not that liberation failed.

UNAVAILABLE EVIDENCE AND CLAIMS REQUIRING ANOTHER STUDY
Public eICU structured data cannot establish an actual SBT, clinician intent or reason for no trial, bedside readiness, airway protection, secretion burden, cough, planned procedures, complete sedation contraindications, exact extubation/reintubation, post-ICU recurrence, or causal clinical benefit. Narrative note sections are removed and raw waveforms are unavailable; vitalPeriodic is a five-minute summary. Expert adjudication against ventilator waveforms and bedside records, or a prospective study with true extubation/SBT timestamps, is required for those claims. An automatic verifier can check source hashes, joins, exact labels, offsets, attrition, state-machine transitions, gate arithmetic, weights, uncertainty, and whether conclusions match outputs; it cannot adjudicate clinical readiness or causal assumptions.
