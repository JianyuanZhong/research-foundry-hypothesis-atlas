## Proposed relationship
Among UK Biobank participants with two valid assessment-center visits, no diabetes or cancer before the second visit, a comparable HbA1c rise may carry higher subsequent incident-diabetes risk when accompanied by weight loss than when accompanied by weight gain or stability. The contrast is prognostic, not causal.
