# Care-plan labels and a persistent, independently defined respiratory trajectory in eICU

## Substantive evolution and clinical importance

This is a targeted child of the two assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. The parents establish that the two literal Ventilation care-plan labels are sparse, strongly hospital-concentrated, unstable as semantic mappings, and not rescued by an outcome-blind expansion to the four exported `Ventilated -` values. They also show that generic documentation opportunity and a single post-window charted-setting reduction do not justify unqualified multicenter use.

The remaining clinically consequential question is not whether another string can be found. It is whether a care-plan label, when present, adds information about a **persistent respiratory-support trajectory** beyond pre-decision patient state and documentation opportunity. A transient or copied numeric value is not a sufficient respiratory trajectory, and a care-plan label is not itself proof of an evaluation, spontaneous breathing trial, extubation readiness, liberation, or benefit. Resolving this question matters because a positive result could justify prospective adjudication of a label-assisted monitoring feature, whereas a negative result would argue against investing in label-based decision support and quality measurement.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the frozen target-labelled eICU person-landmark cohort, adding the exact care-plan label (and, secondarily, the outcome-blind broader `Ventilated -` identity indicators) to a pre-D patient-state and documentation-opportunity model improves hospital-held-out prediction of a subsequently persistent numeric reduction in respiratory-support settings, with a direction and magnitude that are absent in a temporally prior placebo window and not reproduced for an unrelated non-respiratory trajectory.

The evidence boundary is explicit. The strongest supported claim before this experiment is about structured eICU documentation coverage, transportability, and chart-proxy associations. The experiment tests incremental information for an independently algorithmized respiratory chart trajectory. It cannot establish the intended meaning of a label, bedside delivery of a setting change, SBT performance, extubation, durable liberation, appropriateness, quality, clinical benefit, harm, or causality.

## Frozen population, exposure, and temporal separation

The primary population is the parent-frozen artifact `eicu_strict_cohort.csv`, [source checksum], containing 540 eligible person-landmarks from 443 first qualifying ICU stays/persons in 74 hospitals. It retains all eligible landmarks in the first qualifying stay, at L = 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes after ICU admission. Repeated landmarks within `sid` are retained for the estimand but are never treated as independent evidence; all uncertainty resampling moves all landmarks for a sampled stay together.

Exposure is frozen exactly as in the transportability parent: the first `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue` in `[L,L+360]` equal, after trimming/case-folding only for comparison, to either:

- `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` (`evaluation`), or
- `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` (`no_trial`).

The decision time D is that row's `cplitemoffset`, not L. Rows with contradictory first qualifying labels at the same time, missing D, discharge at or before the analysis horizon, absent inherited pre-L invasive-airway evidence, a prior end-of-life discussion, a prior 24-hour `Spontaneous - adequate` marker, or failure of the parent strict pre-L physiology screen remain excluded. No care-plan value observed after D is used as a feature. The inherited artifact and its one-to-one join to the parent action-row artifact must be hash-checked before analysis; a mismatch stops the run.

The primary post-D outcome window is `(D+360, D+1080]`, leaving a 6-hour separation from classification to outcome onset and avoiding direct use of the label's 6-hour documentation window. Require `unitdischargeoffset > D+1080`. This is a predictive temporal ordering, not a causal intervention window.

## Outcome: persistent numeric respiratory-support trajectory

The primary endpoint is designed to avoid a single copied or transient value. For each of four pre-specified setting families—FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set respiratory rate—parse only numeric values from the canonical `respchartvaluelabel` families used by the parent. Reject impossible values and retain the raw label/value audit privately. Do not combine unlike units or treat a missing component as a non-event.

For each component, define a baseline as the median of valid observations in `(D-720,D]`, requiring at least two observations separated by 30 minutes. Define a qualifying post-D decrease when:

- there are at least two valid observations in `(D+360,D+1080]` separated by at least 30 minutes;
- the post-window median is lower than the baseline median by at least the parent clinically pre-specified threshold (0.05 FiO2, 2 cm H2O for PEEP or pressure support, or 2 breaths/min for set rate); and
- at least two post observations individually lie at or below the baseline median minus the threshold, with neither being the sole last observation.

The primary binary outcome is any component meeting all rules. A secondary stringent endpoint requires two qualifying components, each with the same repeated-observation rule. Component-specific endpoints are reported without multiplicity-driven claims. The primary trajectory is numeric and time-stamped but remains a chart proxy; it is not an adjudicated reduction in delivered support.

An independent corroboration sensitivity requires that a qualifying component's post window contain at least one respiratory-care row with a compatible continuing airway record, while preserving the original endpoint as primary. Because `respiratoryCare.airwaytype` and interval fields are not a gold-standard device feed, failure or success of this sensitivity is not called bedside validation.

## Baseline and label increments

The pre-D baseline model contains only information available before D:

- landmark as a categorical variable and time from L to D;
- the pre-D medians, slopes, ranges, observation counts, and missingness indicators for the four numeric settings;
- pre-D oxygen saturation, respiration, systemic mean pressure, and noninvasive mean pressure summaries from the bound vital tables;
- inherited airway evidence and recency from `respiratoryCare`;
- pre-D respiratory-chart opportunity (`respchart` count, distinct labels, and timestamp density) without post-D values; and
- hospital-independent stay variables available before D, with no hospital identifier as a predictor.

A process model adds only generic care-plan opportunity in `[L,L+360]`: any care-plan row, row count, distinct group count, any Ventilation-group row, Ventilation row count, and distinct Ventilation-value count. These are documentation-process proxies, not clinical care.

The exact-label model adds a three-state identity (`evaluation`, `no_trial`, and no qualifying target receipt) and a missing/receipt indicator. The broader model instead adds four identity-preserving indicators for every outcome-blind normalized value beginning `ventilated -`: the two exact labels, `Ventilated - rapid wean/extubation`, and `Ventilated - chronic dependency`. It never treats the four strings as clinically interchangeable. An all-eight exhaustive-value sensitivity is descriptive only and is not a ventilated phenotype.

## Primary estimand and hospital-held-out analysis

Use leave-one-hospital-out prediction over hospitals with at least 20 outcome-observed rows and both outcome classes. Every held-out hospital is scored using a model trained only on other hospitals; no hospital-level target prevalence, vocabulary, threshold, or calibration is learned from the held-out rows. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss as primary metrics, with Brier score, AUROC, calibration slope/intercept, and decision-curve-like net benefit omitted unless prespecified clinically interpretable thresholds can be defended. The main contrasts are:

1. process model minus baseline;
2. exact-label model minus process model; and
3. broader model minus process and exact models.

All pipelines, imputers, scalers, categorical encoders, and any regularization are refit inside each training fold. Use logistic regression with one-hot categorical landmark, median imputation plus missing indicators, standardization, `C=1`, no class weighting, and `max_iter=2000` as the locked primary specification. A complete-case and penalization sensitivity may be run only after the primary output is frozen.

Uncertainty is estimated with at least 1,000 nested stay-cluster bootstrap replicates. Within each training and held-out hospital, sample `uniquepid`/stay clusters with replacement and move all eligible landmarks for the selected stay together. Refitting occurs for every fold and every model in every replicate. The number and identity of held-out hospitals are fixed and reported; this is not inference to unobserved hospitals.

Because the exact labels are receipt-conditioned and sparse, the primary deployment analysis includes all eligible rows with a no-receipt state, rather than restricting to label recipients. A secondary conditional analysis among the 540 target-labelled rows preserves the parent transportability denominator. This distinction prevents a receipt model from being misrepresented as a clinically generalizable label effect.

## Falsification and robustness tests

The following are locked before looking at outcome results.

1. **Prior-window placebo.** Reapply exactly the same repeated-observation endpoint algorithm in `(D-1440,D-360]`, using a baseline in `(D-2160,D-1440]` when available and retaining only rows with adequate prior observation. The label occurs after the placebo outcome window begins; incremental label information should not reproduce the post-D trajectory if the post-D result reflects a newly informative care-plan state. This is a temporal negative control, not proof of exchangeability.
2. **Unrelated trajectory negative control.** Define a non-respiratory chart trajectory from `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean` and `vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean`: repeated post-window change from a pre-D median by a prespecified 10% threshold, requiring two observations separated by 30 minutes and no respiratory-chart fields. The same process/exact/broader models are evaluated for this outcome. A comparable label increment for this outcome indicates general documentation/stay propensity rather than respiratory-specific information. Sparse or clinically uninterpretable blood-pressure trajectories yield an inconclusive, not negative, result.
3. **Peak-pressure exclusion.** Peak inspiratory pressure is excluded from the primary endpoint and every supportive analysis because the parent showed it dominates the earlier composite. A peak-only secondary analysis is displayed solely as a documentation artifact diagnostic.
4. **Landmark and airway recency sensitivity.** Repeat the complete analysis at each of the five landmarks and under inherited unbounded, 24-hour, 12-hour, and 6-hour latest-airway definitions. These are fixed sensitivity analyses, not opportunities to select a favorable denominator.
5. **Component and persistence sensitivity.** Report each setting family separately, the any-component primary, the two-component stringent endpoint, and the airway-corroborated sensitivity. A result driven only by one component, one landmark, or one hospital is not called a robust respiratory trajectory.
6. **Site influence.** Report every hospital's held-out delta, the equal-hospital distribution, and leave-one-hospital-out summaries. A pooled result that reverses after removal of one hospital is adverse to transportable incremental information.
7. **Documentation timing check.** Re-estimate with classification-window generic process counts computed using only rows strictly before D. If the label gain disappears when process opportunity is aligned to D, it is consistent with timing/documentation intensity rather than patient-state information.

## Prespecified interpretation gates

These gates are decision rules, not significance tests.

- **Supportive:** the exact-label increment over process controls improves both micro and macro log loss by at least 0.005 in the primary persistent any-component endpoint; the nested 97.5th percentile of the added-minus-process loss is below zero; improvement is present in at least three airway definitions and at least four held-out hospitals; no held-out hospital reverses the direction after excluding a single dominant site; the two-component or airway-corroborated sensitivity is directionally concordant; and the label increment is absent or materially smaller in the prior placebo and unrelated non-respiratory negative control.
- **Adverse:** the exact-label increment is non-improving or worse in both primary weighting schemes, with nested probability at least 0.75 that macro loss is worse, in at least three airway definitions; or any apparent gain is driven by one hospital, one setting component, the peak-only diagnostic, or is reproduced for the unrelated outcome. Broader indicators must separately meet their own gate; vocabulary expansion is not credited for an exact-label failure.
- **Inconclusive:** insufficient outcome support, fewer than four usable held-out hospitals, broad intervals spanning the supportive threshold, discordant component/corroboration results, or a failed negative-control endpoint due to missingness. Sparse outcome data are not interpreted as evidence that labels lack bedside value.

A supportive computational gate would justify a next study of blinded clinician adjudication, local interface semantics, device-linked settings, SBT/extubation endpoints, and prospective external validation. An adverse gate would support not using these exported labels as a stand-alone multicenter respiratory trajectory feature, while not proving that the underlying bedside process is absent or harmful. An inconclusive gate would require improved measurement and support before clinical interpretation.

## Computation status and verification boundary

No new source computation is claimed in this proposal. The parent artifacts, source hashes, and exact definitions must be verified before execution. Derived code, row-level audit files, bootstrap outputs, and logs must be written under this workspace; raw sources remain read-only. The execution should record byte hashes, row counts at each filter, joins, missingness, component support, held-out hospital membership, and every bootstrap failure.

An automatic verifier can check the frozen artifact identity, source hashes, exact windows, label classification, pre-D feature restriction, numeric thresholds, persistence/separation requirements, endpoint independence, hospital-held-out separation, stay-cluster refitting, negative-control construction, and gate arithmetic. It cannot determine whether a numeric chart value reflects delivered support, whether a label means daily evaluation or a trial, whether care was appropriate, or whether any predictive increment improves patient outcomes. Those claims require clinical adjudication, device and workflow metadata, validated extubation/liberation outcomes, and prospective study.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to the assigned parents, this child changes the scientific target from vocabulary/semantic transportability and a single charted-setting reduction to a persistent, repeated-observation respiratory-support trajectory with a 6-hour post-classification separation. It adds patient-state features available before D, receipt-inclusive deployment analysis, fully refit stay-cluster hospital-held-out evaluation, a temporally prior placebo, a non-respiratory negative control, and component/airway corroboration sensitivities. It preserves the source-bound denominator, exact labels, broader identity-preserving vocabulary, and all prior semantic limits.

The proposal does not claim that the endpoint or models have been executed, and it does not resolve missingness, unit comparability, local workflow semantics, or bedside validity. Those are deliberate uncertainty gates rather than hidden assumptions. The intended advance is a falsifiable test of whether care-plan labels add patient-state information for a robust respiratory trajectory, rather than another audit of label strings.
