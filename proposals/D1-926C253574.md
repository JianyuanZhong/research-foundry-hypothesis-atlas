# Availability-stress repair of comparator-closed whole-panel HCC documentary-M2 evidence

## 1. Assigned successor and one repaired flaw

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s first-eligible resection population, preoperative clocks and windows, whole-accession source grain, eventual-report/documentary-M2 boundary, six immutable corpus-wide reader books and nine fixed radiology×pathology pairs, coupled source/pathology states, chronology quarantine, 2015–2018/2019/2020/2021 split, additive fixed-capacity `B/R_clean/G_clean/Gclean_mask` models, fixed `K∈{K_.05,K_.10,K_.20}`, strict >5-per-100 margins, exact top-K ties, and whole-patient singleton deletion.

The remaining flaw is an interpretation gap. The parent’s source and reader worlds vary source reconstruction, annotation, and pathology-terminal choices, but they contain no report-authored/final/release/view clock. The parent therefore evaluates eventual stored CT/MRI report meaning as if the whole report-derived block were supplied to the ranking rule, while correctly admitting that real-time availability is unknown. A universal >5 result can consequently be documentary evidence for the eventual corpus yet be too optimistic for the proposed preoperative review decision.

The repair is an exact, adversarial report-supply stress experiment. It does not impute report timing, add a timing state, split an accession, change the cutoff, or claim that any report was or was not available. For each test world, it withholds all report-derived surface, semantic, and reason-free report-block-availability features from one whole eligible patient at a time, retaining that patient’s acquisition metadata, other non-report predictors, pathology terminal, and outcome. Scores, fits, preprocessing, reader books, K values, and ties remain locked. This asks whether the six existing >5 claims survive the smallest nontrivial perturbation in the evidence actually missing from HCC.

## 2. Evidence, unresolved claim, and clinical importance

### Untested hypothesis

At confirmatory `K=K_.10`:

> In the frozen first-eligible source-documented HCC-resection frame, the locked `G_clean` ranking’s six universal documentary-M2 gains (>5 captures per 100 fixed review positions over independently fitted `R_clean` and `B`, and over same-fit `Gclean_mask`, separately in 2020 and 2021) remain strictly above 5 after withholding the complete report-derived predictor block from any one whole eligible patient, in every coupled source/pathology terminal and fixed reader-book world; the same remains true after deletion of any one whole frozen test patient.

The report-supply stress is a robustness hypothesis about a locked documentary ranking, not a report-availability estimate. A failure means that one latent unavailable report block can reverse the stated margin in some admissible world; it does not prove that this happened in the source or that reports are generally unavailable.

The advance over the parent is a decision-relevant separation between:
1. robust performance when eventual stored report content is present; and
2. robustness to a minimal patient-level loss of that report-derived content.

A supportive result strengthens the case for obtaining real report timing/version logs before a silent-mode study, because the retrospective signal is not concentrated in a single report supply unit. An adverse stress result makes the timing audit a prerequisite and withholds any interpretation that the eventual-report result is operationally ready. Neither result establishes clinical benefit.

## 6. New exact report-supply stress frontier

For an eligible patient `h`, define `S_h^avail` as every predictor block whose provenance depends on stored CT/MRI report content from all eligible whole accessions `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` for h: `S`, `X_clean`, and `Q` for those report blocks. Acquisition fields in `Z` remain present. Pathology, procedure, treatment, reader-book choice, source state, year, and Y remain present.

The one-patient supply shock `σ_h` replaces `S_h^avail` with the already frozen training-defined unavailable representation in every model arm, without refitting or changing any coefficient. `B(Z)` is unchanged; `R_clean`, `G_clean`, and `Gclean_mask` receive the corresponding shocked common block. If h has no eligible report unit, `σ_h` is a documented no-op, not an exclusion. This is a whole-patient shock composed of whole accession units; it never splits an accession or selectively removes a phrase.

For `H⊆U_T`, replay `T_M^{σ_H}(a,k,ω)` and
`Δ_c^{σ_H}=T_G^{σ_H}-T_{comp}^{σ_H}`
using the same world and ties. The primary new shock endpoint is the exact minimum over every one-patient H={h}:
`L_{e,s}^0=min_{ω,h}Δ_c^{σ_h}(a,k,ω)`.
The deletion-coupled endpoint is
`L_{e,s}^{-1}=min_{j∈U_T,h∈U_T\{j},ω} Δ_c^{σ_h}(a,k,ω\{j})`,
with a sharp post-deletion `N≥k`, grade, and event gate. Emit the corresponding upper endpoints and all attaining witnesses. Do not select h after viewing outcomes in an operational analysis: the universal endpoint deliberately asks for the worst permitted supply loss. For diagnosis only, compute the finite frontier
`q^*_{e,c}=min\{|H|: min_ωΔ_c^{σ_H}≤5\}`,
with `q^*=N_{test}+1` if no subset crosses 5. The frontier is a stress/concentration diagnostic, not an estimated missingness rate or a confidence interval.

All stress and deletion endpoints are exact finite optimization problems. Use state-expanded top-K MILP with one coherent state per patient, exact score ordering, and a shared coupled selector; enumerate all one-patient shocks and verify against the cardinality formulation. No sampled shock subset, beam, cap, favorable incumbent, approximate bound, or timeout may support or falsify a claim.

## 7. Falsification and one-hot conclusions

For every year × contrast coordinate at K=.10, retain the parent’s primary classification:
- stable-supportive iff `L^0_e>5` and `L^{-1}_e>5`;
- singleton-fragile iff `L^0_e>5` and `L^{-1}_e≤5`;
- world-heterogeneous iff `L^0_e≤5<U^0_e`;
- all-world-adverse iff `U^0_e≤5`;
- gate failure is inconclusive.

Define the new stress classification analogously using `L^0_{e,s}` and `L^{-1}_{e,s}`. Equality to 5 fails. The overall confirmatory outcome is mutually exclusive:

1. **inconclusive** if any mandatory annotation, source, population, capacity, fit, stress-gate, or exact-solver certificate fails;
2. **availability-robust-supportive** if all six parent coordinates are stable-supportive and all six one-patient stress coordinates are stable-supportive;
3. **availability-fragile-adverse** if all six parent coordinates are stable-supportive but at least one one-patient stress coordinate is not stable-supportive;
4. **documentary-hypothesis-adverse** if the parent’s six-coordinate joint claim is not stable-supportive.

The parent’s diagnostic `semantic-evidence-only`, `capacity-policy-only`, and `falsified-localized` labels remain emitted inside outcome 4; they cannot be promoted to support. For each adverse outcome, label the exact year, comparator, source/pathology world, deleted patient, shocked patient, endpoint, and attained witness. Mark `all-world-adverse` only when the relevant upper endpoint is ≤5; a mixed endpoint is heterogeneous/fragile, not all-world adverse.

Supportive means only that the locked full ranking’s documentary capture margin survives the declared finite stress. It supports prioritizing accession-linked report authored/version/final/release/view logs as the next evidence acquisition and, only after that external review, consideration of a prospective silent-mode study. Adverse means the retrospective ranking claim may be true with all eventual text present but is not robust to one whole report-derived supply loss; acquire timing/version evidence before any real-time or silent-mode inference. Documentary-hypothesis-adverse means the parent’s universal >5 claim failed and supplies no positive decision signal. Inconclusive is neither support nor adverse evidence.

No result is a biological M2 claim, report-availability rate, report-timing estimate, actual MDT capacity, workload feasibility, prospective performance guarantee, treatment effect, patient benefit/harm, cost-effectiveness finding, fairness assessment, deployment authorization, or population/transportability inference.
