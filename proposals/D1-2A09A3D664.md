### Union-observed estimand and partial identification

For each sampled inherited candidate-`AUTO`, define

`Q_i = max(X_inherited,i, B_i, H_i)`,

where `X_inherited,i` retains all inherited lanes, audit routes, safety findings, missingness, and adverse unknowns. An adverse flag from any method is never overruled. Define the atom-specific union-observed stored-text contamination

`C_union,g = sum_i I(g_i=g)d_i Q_i / sum_i I(g_i=g)d_i`,

estimated by the same Hájek/HT design and adverse finite-population machinery as `C_AUTO,g`. Define analogous union-observed `leak_AUTO`, capture, additional-unit, and boundary-failure variables; the inherited estimands remain reported unchanged so the incremental yield of B and H is visible.

All methods may still share a real-case miss. Let

`lambda_g = P(X_text=1 | d=1, Q=0, g)`

be the fraction of union-negative candidate-`AUTO` admissions in atom `g` that are truly unsafe **in the stored packet** but missed by all methods. With no gold standard, the data identify neither `lambda_g` nor clinical truth. For a sensitivity value `lambda in [0,1]`, the sharp monotone upper endpoint is

`C_star,g(lambda) = C_union,g + lambda*(1-C_union,g)`.

Within every survey/bootstrap replicate, first maximize `C_union,g` over missing labels, compatible cross-products, and finite-population uncertainty, then apply this transform. Report the simultaneous curve `U95[C_star,g(lambda)]` on a fixed grid `lambda=0,0.005,...,0.10,0.15,...,1`, and the atom tipping point

`Lambda_crit,g = max(0, (tau_AUTO-U95[C_union,g])/(1-U95[C_union,g]))`,

set to zero when `U95[C_union,g]>=tau_AUTO`. Apply the same construction to calibrated leakage with its 0.02 margin. No conditional-independence latent-class estimate is primary; three-method Hui-Walter, log-linear, and capture-recapture models are diagnostics only because correlated errors and population homogeneity assumptions are unverified.

Before any real labels are released, clinical governance must either (a) freeze a nonnegative real-note common-miss cap `Lambda` and document the independent evidence/rationale supporting it, or (b) declare `Lambda` unavailable. A calibrated atom may pass only if `Lambda` was frozen without access to results and the simultaneous endpoint passes for **every** `lambda<=Lambda`; the implemented check is the endpoint at `Lambda` by monotonicity. `Lambda` may not be inferred from artificial controls, reviewer agreement, or a favorable latent-class model. If it is unavailable, the curve and tipping point remain scientifically useful outputs but every atom is unreleasable. Choosing or lowering `Lambda` after results is a protocol violation.

For an evidence-bounded robustness claim, additionally require `Lambda` not be smaller than the widest upper 95% joint-false-negative bound observed among method pairs on controls; this is a conservative floor on the sensitivity range, not evidence that the real common-miss rate is below it. Report atom-specific method-positive overlaps, marginal added yield, span/defect categories, disagreement, and nonresponse with simultaneous intervals. Small overlaps do not prove independence; large dependence or concentrated all-method uncertainty is adverse and can only widen or block interpretation.

Within each noncensus `h`, allocate its fixed `n_h` across final certifiable cells and `RESIDUAL_h` by integer optimization performed before labels:

1. give every occupied cell `min(2,N_hg)`;
2. among certifiable cells, maximize lexicographically the minimum expected sampled support across atoms, then the minimum temporal margin, exact-service margin, and workflow margin;
3. allocate remaining positions as close as possible to proportional allocation while penalizing any projected normalized maximum weight above 0.10; and
4. break exact ties lexicographically by `(h,E,service,W)`.

The complete objective, constraints, solver/version, and unique solution are sealed. If no allocation can keep every claimable cell's projected normalized maximum weight at or below 0.10, the affected atom remains sampled but cannot be certified.

Within each `(h,g)` or residual cell, draw SRS without replacement using a seed derived from the protocol hash. For admission `i`, `pi_i=1` in census cells and otherwise `pi_i=n_hg/N_hg>0`; `w_i=1/pi_i`. Within a sampled cell, `pi_ij=n_hg(n_hg-1)/[N_hg(N_hg-1)]`; across independently sampled cells, `pi_ij=pi_i*pi_j`. A noncensus cell with `n_hg=1` cannot estimate internal variance and is unsupported. Freeze counts, allocation, seeds, ordered IDs, probabilities, and an explicit no-replacement list.

Every selected admission receives every inherited index, audit, redaction, and minority-unit review plus Methods B and H. The current expert-review frame remains fixed at no more than 900 distinct admissions; Method B/H duplicate readings, holistic readings, tie review, artificial controls, and 5% repeated packets are recorded as additional physician-hours and cannot trigger fewer admissions, replacements, top-ups, or a higher admission count. Before labels, governance must approve an absolute **total** retrospective physician-hour ceiling covering inherited and added work. If the fixed calibration workload cannot be staffed under that ceiling, declare feasibility failure rather than weaken review or sampling.

## Preserved estimands and domain-conditional estimands

The inferential and sampling unit remains the first eligible admission; recommendation units are nested observations. Preserve all inherited pooled totals, burdens, chronology states, route agreement measures, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, `leak_AUTO`, additional-unit rate, deterministic-one-to-multiple error, and workload.

For route `r`, preserve `T_r=sum_i I(R_i=r)` and `B_r=100*T_r/N_A`, estimated by HT total `sum_s I(R_i=r)/pi_i`. Preserve the parent's adverse event definition: `d_i=1` if either initial index lane calls `AUTO`; `x_i=1` when `d_i=1` and the other index lane, any audit-compatible route, minority-unit safety audit, missing audit, redaction/boundary failure, timing limitation, or adverse unknown is compatible with `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`. Then

`C_AUTO=sum_i x_i/sum_i d_i`,

estimated by the Hájek ratio of HT totals with numerator and denominator always reported. This is text-defined audit contamination, not clinical harm.

For every final atlas atom `g`, estimate the same quantities conditionally using

`T_yg_hat=sum_{i in s} w_i I(G_i=g)y_i`

and Hájek ratios of domain HT totals. Also estimate the atom's design-weighted total physician-hours per 100 atom admissions, separately and jointly for inherited, B, H, tie, and control work; packet-time quantiles; nonresponse; number of units; certainty-stratum share; maximum weight; normalized maximum weight; and Kish effective sample size for every denominator. Calibration reviews are not sampled second-phase observations: all sampled admissions receive them, so the inherited `pi_i` and `pi_ij` remain the sole population-design probabilities.

Define atom-level certification vector

`Theta_g={C_AUTO,g,C_union,g,C_star,g(Lambda),Lambda_crit,g,D_MIX,g,leak_AUTO,g,leak_union,g,confirmation_g,AC1_g,Se_RECON,g,PPV_RECON,g,PPV_TIMING,g,Se_DEFER,g,control validity_g,dependence diagnostics_g,workload_g}`.

For a proposed route allow-list `L`, define its minimax safety summaries as the **worst simultaneous confidence endpoint**, not the worst point estimate:

* `M_AUTO(L)=max_{g in L} U95(C_AUTO,g)`;
* `M_UNION(L)=max_{g in L} U95(C_union,g)`;
* `M_CAL(L,Lambda)=max_{g in L} U95(C_star,g(Lambda))`;
* `m_TIP(L)=min_{g in L} Lambda_crit,g`;
* `M_LEAK(L)=max_{g in L} U95(leak_union,g)` after the analogous common-miss transform at `Lambda`;
* `m_CONFIRM(L)=min_{g in L} L95(confirmation_g)`; and
* analogous minima for capture/control validity and maxima for dependence and total workload.

No population weighting, standardization, hierarchical shrinkage, empirical-Bayes estimate, regression interaction, pooled denominator, or favorable consensus may satisfy a primary atom gate. Pooled estimates and early/late contrasts are required diagnostics, but direct atom-specific absolute margins govern release. A stable difference around an unsafe level does not pass; a noisy difference between two individually safe supported atoms does not by itself fail.

## Finite-population inference, multiplicity, and unknowns

Treat each `(h,g)` and residual cell as a survey stratum. For scalar total `T_y`, use

`V_hat(T_y_hat)=sum_hg N_hg^2*(1-n_hg/N_hg)*s_y,hg^2/n_hg`,

with zero census contribution. For a ratio `R`, use Taylor linearization `z_i=y_i-R_hat*d_i` and divide the corresponding total variance by `T_d_hat^2`. Report numerator and denominator totals; do not report a ratio with zero estimated denominator.

Generate at least 20,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates independently within every noncensus design cell, retaining all units, inherited views, B/H initial labels, tie labels, response states, domains, and workload for an admission together; certainty admissions remain fixed. Artificial-control uncertainty is generated in the same replicate index by independent binomial inversion/bootstrap at the control level, never by treating controls as population admissions. The same replicate drives pooled, atom, residual-envelope, inherited/calibrated minimax, sensitivity-curve, diagnostic-domain, and workload quantities. Use a max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over all inherited pooled gates, every final atlas atom's original and calibrated primary route gates, all prespecified `lambda` grid points through 0.15 plus the frozen `Lambda`, and the minimax endpoints. The late possible-calendar challenge is included in that family. Secondary exact-code/path/admission/disposition diagnostics and latent-class models form a separately adjusted family and cannot rescue primary failure.

For rare or zero events, also invert the exact stratified finite-population distribution. Use hypergeometric inversion for unconditional binary totals and multivariate-hypergeometric enumeration of `{unsafe AUTO,safe AUTO,non-AUTO}` for `C_AUTO`, respecting cell sample counts. Use the widest valid simultaneous bound from exact inversion, bootstrap, and adverse partial identification. Zero sampled unsafe events yields a positive upper bound unless the entire relevant finite population is censused.

No selected admission is replaced. Missing, late, schema-invalid, unreadable, withdrawn, or access-failed reviews; missing safety audit; redaction breach; unknown clock; domain parse failure; service tie; and unit-boundary disagreement remain explicit. For every estimand, optimize all compatible exhaustive route/event assignments for sharp adverse lower and upper endpoints; use linear-fractional optimization for ratios inside each replicate. An index `AUTO` with missing audit evidence is unsafe for the upper bound. Unknown domain membership contributes to every compatible diagnostic domain and to residual, never to a favorable release atom. Complete-case, unweighted, model-assisted, and consensus estimates are diagnostics only.

The first-eligible rule requires unique `subject_id` in `N_A`; a duplicate stops the run rather than inviting a cluster correction. Balance reviewer assignments across era, service, and workflow before labels. Report reviewer-specific response, disagreement, and time, with a secondary reviewer-resampling sensitivity; primary bounds remain design-based and adverse.

## Baselines

Apply the identical frozen sample, original labels, calibration labels, weights, controls, and uncertainty to:

1. the parent's pooled gate;
2. a macro-group service/era analysis that would pool rare exact signatures;
3. marginal-majority route assignment;
4. deterministic sampling-stratum routing;
5. recommendation-unit majority routing;
6. terminal-service-majority and possible-era-majority routing;
7. a sentinel-only safety analysis;
8. favorable consensus, incorporated-reference, complete-case, and unweighted analyses;
9. a hierarchical shrinkage sensitivity shown only to quantify how borrowing can make an unsupported atom appear certifiable;
10. inherited same-codebook audit alone;
11. alternate-codebook B alone;
12. holistic H alone;
13. simple majority and latent-class/capture-recapture combinations that assume conditional independence; and
14. the adverse union plus the full partial-identification curve, which alone may enter calibrated nomination.

None of the baselines can nominate a route. The targeted repair is falsified computationally if a planted failing atom is allowed through because pooled, same-codebook, shrunken, majority, consensus, or conditional-independence estimates pass; if an adverse B/H label is erased; or if `Lambda` is learned or reduced from observed results.

## Prespecified certification and nomination gates

Governance must approve all inherited margins, the total absolute workload budget, and either a fixed common-miss cap `Lambda` with external rationale or an explicit declaration that no such cap is supportable, before any real labels are released; otherwise `NO_SELECTION`. Retain inherited defaults: `tau_AUTO=0.05`, confirmation `rho=0.90`, AC1 `0.70`, `sigma_RECON=0.95`, `pi_RECON=0.80`, `pi_TIMING=0.95`, and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`. Add fixed control gates of one-sided 95% lower sensitivity and specificity at least 0.90 for each method and an upper joint false-negative bound no greater than 0.05 for every method pair. These are operational silent-validation prerequisites, not acceptable harm rates or proof of expert validity.

Every route first must pass all inherited pooled source, retrieval, denominator, chronology, redaction, seal/access, event, effective-sample-size, precision, and adverse-bound gates, plus calibration roster/manual/packet seals, complete B/H review or adverse missingness, control validity, dependence, common-miss, and total-workload gates. In particular retain at least 200 `N_A`, at least 60 `E_A`, overall design ESS at least 150, normalized maximum weight at most 0.10, and simultaneous all-`N_A` burden half-width no greater than 5 per 100. Broken firewalls, absent governance, canonical duplication, calibration leakage, a post-result `Lambda`, or mandatory review above 900 distinct admissions blocks all selection.

An atom `g` is route-claimable only if it is in the final pre-label atlas, all domain fields are known, at least 30 sampled route/reference-positive admissions exist as applicable, at least 10 arise outside the sentinel census, route-denominator ESS is at least 50, normalized maximum weight within the atom is at most 0.10, and the route burden has a positive simultaneous lower bound. Lack of these conditions is unsupported, never a pass.

For each claimable atom:

* **AUTO:** at least 30 sampled candidate-`AUTO` admissions; lower confirmation/raw-agreement bound `>=0.90`; lower AC1 `>=0.70`; upper inherited `C_AUTO<=0.05`; upper union-observed `C_union<=0.05`; governance-frozen `Lambda` available and `U95[C_star,g(Lambda)]<=0.05`; `Lambda>=` the widest pairwise control joint-false-negative upper bound; upper `D_MIX<=0.10`; calibrated upper leakage after the same transform `<=0.02`; every control/dependence gate passes; no inherited, B, or H reviewer or safety auditor leaves an unresolved minority unit compatible with `AUTO`; and upper total workload below the approved absolute budget.
* **RECON:** at least 30 index `RECON` and 30 union-reference unsafe-content admissions; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower calibrated `Se_RECON>=0.95`; lower `PPV_RECON>=0.80`; upper calibrated `AUTO` leakage among these cases `<=0.02`; every control/dependence gate passes; and total-workload pass.
* **TIMING:** at least 30 index `TIMING`; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower union-reference `PPV_TIMING>=0.95`; no use of an index-only clock when another active unit is under 24 hours; every control/dependence gate passes; and total-workload pass.
* **DEFER:** at least 30 union-reference uncertain admissions; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower calibrated `Se_DEFER>=0.95`; no adverse-compatible inherited/B/H uncertain admission routed `AUTO`; every control/dependence gate passes; and total-workload pass.

The proposed allow-list for a route is exactly the set of pre-label atlas atoms that independently pass. The minimax endpoints over that set must also pass. There is no unrestricted nomination unless every occupied known atom passes and the simultaneous upper population burden of residual/unknown atoms is below 1%; otherwise silent validation must enforce the explicit allow-list and map all other signatures to `DEFER`. At least one atom and a positive lower bound on its route burden are required; an empty allow-list is `NO_SELECTION`.

Two-lane silent validation requires atom-level passes for both `AUTO` and `RECON`. A `RECON`-only allow-list may be nominated if its atoms pass while `AUTO` fails. A pooled pass cannot rescue a supported failing atom; a passing neighboring atom cannot rescue an unsupported one.

2. regrouping exact service codes, moving a rare/unknown atom into the atlas, changing calendar cutoffs, or redefining workflow after labels;
3. route-, event-, disagreement-, response-, calibration-yield-, workload-, or result-based top-ups, replacements, stopping, atom merging, sample reallocation, or calibration subsampling;
4. restricting any inherited or calibration audit to `AUTO`, sentinels, omissions, apparent difficult cases, or favorable atoms;
5. sampling or weighting recommendation units;
6. using index evidence to repair audit/calibration labels, calibration evidence to repair index labels, or either codebook to revise the other after real cases;
7. favorable unit matching, tie/consensus deletion, complete-case deletion, response weighting, or model imputation in a primary gate;
8. treating artificial controls, agreement, capture-recapture, or latent-class estimates as a gold standard or as evidence that `lambda_g` is zero;
9. choosing, lowering, or atom-tailoring `Lambda` after labels, or passing an atom without a prespecified cap and external rationale;
10. certifying a rare, residual, unknown, absent, or newly observed signature through pooling, adjacency, standardization, shrinkage, calibration borrowing, or clinical intuition; and
11. claiming forward or external transport, clinical truth, guideline appropriateness, clinician awareness/failure, actual communication, live safety, causal benefit, follow-up completion, or patient outcomes from stored shifted-date text.

Any affected route/atom is invalidated. Data may motivate a new child, but cannot be silently reused as untouched confirmation.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. Recompute source/schema hashes, row counts, `N_A`, unique subjects, reciprocal chains, clocks, exhaustive states, domains, atlas, sample allocation, `pi_i`, `pi_ij`, and ordered draw from sealed inputs. Unexplained drift stops analysis.
2. Shifted-date trap: two subjects with the same raw shifted year but different `anchor_year_group` must follow their widened possible intervals. Boundary-crossing intervals must be `E_BRIDGE`; malformed values must be unknown.
3. Service trap: post-`D` rows cannot alter workflow; tied distinct terminal codes, missing times/codes, and absent rows become unknown. Source order cannot resolve a tie.
4. Atlas trap: create many small atoms. The deterministic support-pruning algorithm must move atoms to residual before labels and never merge them after seeing outcomes.
5. Allocation trap: rerun the integer optimizer and SRS from hashes/seeds. Processing order or labels cannot change cells, sample sizes, or IDs.
6. Positivity trap: every admission, including residual/unknown, must have `pi_i>0`; pairwise probabilities and no-replacement behavior must match the declared design.
7. Domain-masking trap: place unsafe `AUTO` events only in one supported late/service/workflow atom at a rate that leaves pooled `C_AUTO<0.05` but atom `C_AUTO>0.05`. Pooled analysis may pass; that atom and unrestricted nomination must fail.
8. Rare-domain trap: put all failures in a rare residual atom. The experiment may estimate a residual burden, but must never certify that atom or let a macro-group pass expose it to `AUTO`.
9. Shrinkage trap: make an unsupported atom's model estimate favorable through borrowing. The model result cannot alter certification.
10. Workload trap: keep pooled hours acceptable while doubling one atom's workload. That atom must fail without contaminating evidence for independently passing atoms.
11. Unknown trap: concentrate missing service/date/clock/audit/boundary evidence in high-weight unsafe admissions. Adverse bounds must block favorable certification.
12. Zero-event trap: zero unsafe events in a noncensus atom must yield a positive finite-population upper bound.
13. Multiplicity trap: nominal atom intervals may pass while simultaneous intervals fail; only simultaneous adverse limits govern.
14. Minority-unit trap: nine pure omissions plus one conflict must be `A_MIXED` within `RECON`; duplicating safe units changes neither route nor weight.
15. Differential-verification trap: every apparent route receives identical audit components and safety review.
16. Leakage/incorporation traps: forbidden packet canaries, shared pseudonyms, altered append-only labels, route-before-seal execution, or index-informed audit adjudication must fail controls and leave primary results unchanged or invalidate the run.
17. Nonresponse trap: concentrate missing labels among unsafe high-weight cases; favorable complete-case estimates cannot pass adverse bounds.
18. Finite-population trap: small synthetic populations with unequal cell fractions must recover known HT totals and nominal exact/simulation coverage; unweighted estimates should fail.
19. Prospective-signature trap: a novel service code or workflow combination must map to `DEFER`, never to its nearest retrospective atom.
20. Common-codebook trap: make both inherited lanes agree on a planted boundary error while alternate-codebook B or holistic H detects it. The union must be adverse and the atom must not pass on inherited agreement.
21. Holistic-minority trap: one H physician detects a planted second unit while the other and tie reviewer call safe. The immutable adverse initial H label must remain in `Q_i`.
22. Alternate-ontology trap: emit an alternate B object that cannot map uniquely to the common decision faces. It must become `UNCERTAIN`, not be coerced to safe.
23. Control trap: conceal a defect family's truth flags until seal, then make a method miss enough positive controls that its one-sided sensitivity bound is below 0.90. All calibrated nomination must stop; artificial controls cannot be dropped or relabeled.
24. Dependence trap: give all methods highly correlated control false negatives. A favorable independence latent-class estimate cannot nominate, and the widest joint-false-negative upper bound must set the minimum reported sensitivity range.
25. Common-miss trap: make all three methods miss a known fraction among union negatives. Verify `C_star=C_union+lambda(1-C_union)`, monotonicity, simultaneous propagation, and failure when the endpoint at frozen `Lambda` exceeds 0.05.
26. Cap-leakage trap: write `Lambda` after real labels or choose a smaller cap for a failing atom. Hash chronology must reject the run; no cap means sensitivity output but no release.
27. Workload trap: calibration leaves <=900 distinct admissions but pushes total physician-hours beyond the frozen absolute budget. Selection must fail rather than remove methods or admissions.
28. Stored-text truth trap: a fixture includes an undocumented spoken plan or inaccurate source note. The system must say this is outside identification and cannot infer clinical safety or communication.
29. Claim checker rejects exact-calendar trend, external-site generalizability, guideline appropriateness, clinician awareness/failure, live safety, causal benefit, follow-up completion, patient outcome, or “gold-standard expert truth” language.

The verifier can mechanically check sources, joins, times, domain/atlas code, fixed allocation, probabilities, packet/access separation, manual and label seals, calibration roster disjointness, artificial-control construction and scoring, route code, adverse union, partial-identification algebra and frozen-cap chronology, finite-population estimators, exact/bootstrap bounds, adverse assignments, minimax gates, allow-list mapping, workload, and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It can establish reproducibility against stored packets and known artificial text perturbations. It cannot establish clinical truth, the completeness or correctness of source documentation, actual artifact transmission/viewing/understanding, service ownership, appropriateness, utility margins, actual communication, the real common-miss cap, external transport, or benefit. `Lambda` requires prospective independent evidence and clinical governance; an automatic verifier can check that it was prespecified and applied, not that it is true.

## Interpretation

**Supportive** means the inherited pooled audit passes and at least one support-qualified atom independently satisfies every original and calibrated simultaneous adverse route, control, common-miss, dependence, and total-workload gate; all atoms in the stated allow-list pass, and minimax endpoints pass at the governance-frozen `Lambda`. This supports only that the frozen process is reproducible against the available stored MIMIC text and remains below operational contamination margins under the declared stored-text common-miss sensitivity bound. It may nominate a prospective silent test in those exact observable signatures, with all other signatures forced to `DEFER`. It does not support clinical truth or safety.

**Adverse** means an added independent method finds enough stored-text unsafe content, boundary failures, timing limitations, uncertainty, correlated errors, or workload to cross a gate in a supported atom; or the calibrated endpoint at frozen `Lambda` crosses a margin. That atom is excluded even if inherited same-codebook and pooled performance are favorable. This would substantively show that agreement/sealing alone understated text-route contamination, while still saying nothing about downstream clinical harm.

**Inconclusive** includes no feasible atlas within 900 admissions; inability to staff all calibration work; sparse route events; poor atom ESS; wide simultaneous bounds; excessive unknown/residual burden; retrieval undercoverage; nonresponse; broken seals; failed/artificial-control coverage; excessive dependence; unavailable or scientifically undefended `Lambda`; a tipping point below the frozen cap; unapproved total workload; or unavailable exact calendar mapping. Unsupported is not adverse, but it is unreleasable. If union-observed estimates pass but no defensible common-miss cap exists, report the full curve and tipping points and make `NO_SELECTION`; agreement is not proof that all methods are jointly correct.

## Explicit prospective bridge and evidence ceiling

A retrospective allow-list is only a nomination. Before any live route is shown or used, freeze the extractor, eligibility rules, reciprocal-chain logic, unit schema, component views, matching/route code, margins, allow-list signatures, and default-`DEFER` mapper. Conduct a separately approved prospective silent study of consecutive adult discharges using real calendar dates and immutable report finalization/addenda, report availability/viewing, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing-risk context, and final transmitted artifact.

Prospective records matching a certified retrospective service/workflow signature enter a silent bridge stratum; any novel code, unknown field, changed service semantics, new workflow combination, or out-of-support signature is quarantined as `DEFER` and reported, never mapped by similarity. Independent experts remain blinded to silent routes and later outcomes, and prospective adjudication must repeat the alternate-codebook and codebook-minimal holistic challenge rather than treating the retrospective union as truth. Prespecify at least 100 silent route-positive admissions overall and at least 30 per claimed atom, or an accrual stop; apply the same all-route minority-unit audit and simultaneous adverse contamination/capture gates. Actual-calendar early-versus-current transport is tested only there. This prospective study requires a new approved workload and is not smuggled into the present fixed <=900-admission retrospective burden.

Only after a successful silent bridge could a governed route-stratified trial compare usual care with clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation in validated `AUTO` signatures and side-by-side reconciliation in validated `RECON` signatures. Clinical appropriateness, correction/dismissal, inappropriate insertion, responsibility, communication, scheduling, follow-up completion, harms, utility, and benefit require new workflow/outcome data and expert governance.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The parent already ensures that a route cannot validate itself and that a large pooled domain cannot validate a smaller unsupported or unsafe atom. This child addresses a different failure mode: reproducible common-mode interpretation error. Its principal advance is a computable, falsifiable triangulation layer in which a separately authored duplicate ontology and a codebook-minimal holistic panel challenge the inherited atomization on every sampled admission; any added adverse evidence enters a one-way union; known artificial perturbations validate only text-level detection mechanics; and all-method misses are exposed through atom-specific partial-identification curves and a governance-frozen cap rather than assumed absent. The inherited deterministic support atlas, minimax gates, design probabilities, and <=900 distinct-admission ceiling are unchanged; additional readings are transparent workload, not new sampling opportunities.

The evidence-supported claim is correspondingly narrow: the source files can reproduce bounded stored-text packet states, and a passing result can demonstrate robustness to observed alternate-method challenges plus a declared stored-text common-miss sensitivity range. The untested claims remain whether the chart is complete/correct, the final plan was delivered or understood, reviewers share an unmeasured conceptual blind spot, `Lambda` reflects reality, the route is clinically appropriate, or it improves care. `anchor_year_group` supports only widened possible-calendar bands; service/ICU fields remain workflow proxies; retrieval can miss semantic opportunities; strict atoms may be sparse; and no available MIMIC field supplies a clinical gold standard. Those uncertainties can yield `NO_SELECTION` and require prospective artifacts, access/communication evidence, target-linked follow-up, complete-context adjudication, and ultimately another comparative study—not weaker retrospective gates.
