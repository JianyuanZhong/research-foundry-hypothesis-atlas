# Post-classification observation and competing ICU-exit states do not yet rescue the adverse multicenter fitness result

## Question and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. Those parents showed, respectively, that exhaustive outcome-blind expansion of the exported Ventilation vocabulary adds only 1–3 alternative rows per airway definition from one hospital and worsens bounded broad-deployment prediction, and that zero labels reflect mixed exported interface footprints rather than one absent-opportunity mechanism. Both primarily compared prediction after restricting to rows with a complete later respiratory-chart outcome window.

The unresolved, falsifiable question is whether that restriction conceals informative early ICU death, live ICU discharge, or later respiratory-documentation loss strongly enough to reverse the prior adverse fitness-for-use conclusion. The hypothesis tested here is that post-freeze observation completeness depends on baseline, hospital, process documentation, and receipt, and that inverse-probability or all-row competing-state sensitivity materially changes the broader-versus-process/exact comparison. The clinical advance is an explicit, outcome-blind ascertainment audit using all classification-complete rows before outcome restriction, rather than treating a complete chart window as innocuous. No discharge is called success, and no chart state is called ventilation, SBT, extubation, readiness, quality, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Computable ascertainment states and analysis

For each retained classification-complete row, mutually exclusive states are defined from exact available fields:

1. `icu_death`: `unitdischargestatus == Expired` and `unitdischargeoffset <= L+1080`.
2. `live_icu_discharge`: `unitdischargestatus == Alive` and `unitdischargeoffset <= L+1080`; this is not clinical success.
3. `complete_computable_outcome`: discharge after `L+1080` and nonmissing inherited `robust_reduction`.
4. `still_adequate_chart_no_paired_outcome`: discharge after `L+1080`, missing `robust_reduction`, and at least two distinct `respchartoffset` values in `(L+360,L+1080]`.
5. `still_sparse_no_respiratory_documentation`: discharge after `L+1080`, missing `robust_reduction`, and fewer than two distinct later respiratory-chart offsets.

The primary estimand is hospital-held-out incremental log loss for baseline, baseline plus generic post-freeze process controls, baseline plus process and exact label, and baseline plus process and four outcome-blind broader indicators. Baseline uses landmark and pre-landmark `respiratoryCharting` summaries (`pre_chart_n`, `pre_distinct_labels`, `pre_robust_n`, `pre_fio2`, `pre_peep`, `pre_pressure_support`, `pre_vent_rate`). Process controls use care-plan presence/count/distinct groups and Ventilation-group presence/count/distinct values in `[L,L+360]`; they are documentation-process variables, not care delivered.

For identifiable fixed-hospital overlap, cross-hospital observation models estimate computable-outcome probability from baseline, landmark, process, exact, and broad fields; probabilities are clipped to `[.05,.95]`, inverse weights capped at 10 and normalized within held-out hospital. Complete-case outcome models are compared unweighted and weighted. A five-state multinomial model is a sensitivity over all classification-complete rows; it predicts exported observation/competing states without ranking discharge or death. Repeated landmarks are kept together by `sid` in hospital-held-out stay-cluster bootstrap. Due to the episode deadline, the executed bootstrap uses a disclosed bounded `B_BOOT = 20` resamples per contrast, not a high-precision inferential interval; this does not manufacture certainty.

## Actual filtering and results

The executable `[internal dataset path]` scanned all 3,115,018 `carePlanGeneral` rows, 20,168,176 `respiratoryCharting` rows, and 200,859 patient rows. It retained 130,792 care-plan rows for frozen stays, 5,219 rows in unique classification windows, and 193,228 later respiratory-chart rows for the retained keys. Exact receipt and inherited later-chart row-count positive controls had zero mismatches; hospital/discharge joins had zero mismatches; unexpected early discharge statuses were absent.

Across the four definitions, classification-complete row counts were 3,671 (unbounded), 2,575 (24 h), 1,748 (12 h), and 1,159 (6 h). Complete computable outcome rates were 89.78%, 88.27%, 88.96%, and 89.73%, respectively. In the unbounded definition, the 3,671 rows comprised 34 ICU deaths, 59 live ICU discharges, 24 still-adequate-chart/no-paired-outcome rows, 3,296 complete computable outcomes, and 258 still-sparse/no-respiratory-documentation rows. Receipt-associated complete-case rate differences were +1.86 percentage points for exact receipt and +2.10 points for broader receipt; generic care-plan and Ventilation-group presence were associated with lower completeness, consistent with process-dependent observation but not a clinical interpretation. Across hospitals with at least 20 rows, complete-case rates ranged from 0.725 to 1.0 in the unbounded definition.

The binary IPW analysis was feasible in 20, 16, 13, and 12 held-out hospitals for unbounded, 24 h, 12 h, and 6 h. Effective-sample-size fractions were 0.907, 0.847, 0.861, and (as reported in the result artifact) adequate for the corresponding bounded fit; there was no broad weight explosion, but this does not establish missing-at-random. In the unbounded IPW comparison, broader minus process log-loss deltas were +0.00035 micro and +0.00090 macro; in the 24 h and 12 h definitions they were +0.00864/+0.00529 and +0.01149/+0.00642. The bounded point estimates therefore remained adverse to broader encoding, while the reduced bootstrap intervals are explicitly imprecise.

The all-row five-state sensitivity did not provide a concordant rescue: in unbounded, broader minus process was -0.01192 micro and -0.00195 macro, whereas broader minus exact was +0.00106 micro and +0.00091 macro; in 24 h, broader minus process was -0.02481/-0.01046 but broader minus exact was +0.00061/+0.00321. These mixed comparator directions fail the prespecified rescue gate. The executable gate therefore reports `supports_selection_rescue = false`, `adverse_reinforcement_ipw = true`, `adverse_reinforcement_multinomial = false`, and overall `inconclusive_selection_sensitivity`. The adverse/reinforcement conclusion is not asserted because the all-row sensitivity was not concordant; the prior adverse fitness result is not declared unchanged under the strict gate.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence would require adequate overlap and concordant weighted and five-state improvements versus both process and exact encodings in unbounded and at least two bounded definitions, with uncertainty excluding zero. Adverse evidence would require no rescue plus at least two bounded definitions showing high probability of worse broader macro loss under both IPW and multinomial sensitivity. The executed result meets neither complete adverse rule nor supportive rule. It is therefore inconclusive about whether informative observation can alter the earlier fitness conclusion, despite descriptive evidence that early exits and documentation loss are nonzero and receipt/process are associated with completeness.

This experiment can establish only computable selection states, source filtering, joins, model separation, and bounded predictive contrasts. It cannot establish that live ICU discharge is recovery, that death or missing charting encodes a respiratory clinical outcome, that care-plan values represent bedside ventilation/SBT/extubation, or that any association is causal. Resolving those claims requires direct device and airway-event evidence, local interface/workflow metadata, blinded clinical adjudication, and prospective external validation. Fixed observed hospitals and only 20 bootstrap resamples limit uncertainty and transportability.
