# Target-linked longitudinal documentation falsification layer

## Decision question and substantive repair

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent establishes whether one exact support-atom/inherited-route cell is measurable and precise as a stored-text construct. Its unresolved clinical-scientific flaw is more fundamental than sparse-cell precision: a reproducible pre-departure text route may have no observable relationship to later documentation that would be expected if the recommendation refers to a clinically recognized finding. MIMIC cannot establish that a patient received care or completed imaging, but it can provide a separately analyzed, target-linked documentation anchor and explicit failure modes.

The child therefore adds an ancillary longitudinal falsification layer without changing the inherited population, target, route, sampling, weights, phase-2 endpoints, or conclusion ceiling. It asks whether target-linked radiology documentation is observed near the prescribed due interval, conditional on the existence of MIMIC-observable documentation opportunities, while treating outside care, silent records, and death as distinct limitations. It never converts no record into no care, and it never uses a later association as evidence of causality, appropriateness, safety, benefit, responsibility, or actual completion.

## Evidence versus untested claim

Available evidence supports only that the snapshot has longitudinally keyed admissions, patient death fields, and radiology reports with subject-level identity and chart/store times. An exploratory feasibility profile found 546,028 admissions for 223,452 subjects; 2,321,355 radiology reports for 237,427 subjects; 1,144,758 radiology reports have nonmissing `hadm_id`; and, across all admissions (before the inherited pulmonary frame), 198,356 had at least one radiology report in the 90-day post-discharge interval, 235,860 in 180 days, and 270,101 in 365 days. These are not frame-specific yields and do not imply completion.

The unresolved claim is narrower and falsifiable: in the frozen target cell, target-linked documentation is more frequent in the registered due window than in registered placebo windows and non-target negative-control packets, among records observable in this MIMIC snapshot, with adequate positive-control and opportunity coverage. The strongest possible result remains an evidence-bounded anchor for prospective workflow measurement, not validation of the route as clinically correct.

## Population and temporal boundaries

The ancillary population is exactly the parent's realized phase-2 selected admissions whose parent target-cell membership is already sealed. No new admissions are recruited, and no target is replaced. The inherited index is `i`, with departure `D_i=admissions.dischtime`, recommendation due interval `m_i in {3,6,12}` and due date `T_i = calendar_add(D_i,m_i)` using the parent calendar rule. If a target has multiple recommendation units, the parent's all-unit admission route remains unchanged; the ancillary target-linked outcome is admission-level and does not sample units independently.

For each selected index admission, construct these fixed windows using within-subject times (cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid):

1. **Due window** `W_i^due=[T_i,T_i+30 calendar days]`.
2. **Pre-due placebo window** `W_i^pre=[D_i+30 calendar days,D_i+60 calendar days]` for 3-month recommendations, and, generally, the interval ending 30 days before `T_i` with the same 30-day width: `[T_i-60d,T_i-30d]`. It is not interpreted as absence of care.
3. **Post-due placebo window** `W_i^post=[T_i+180d,T_i+210d]` when those dates are observable; it is a negative-control timing window, not a claim that later care is impossible.
4. **Target-window opportunity horizon** `H_i=[D_i,T_i+30d]`, used only to report whether any MIMIC radiology report or subsequent admission makes the subject observable. Reports with `charttime<=D_i` are excluded from post-departure outcomes even if stored later.

A report is eligible for the ancillary layer if its `charttime` is in the relevant window, its `subject_id` equals the index subject, and it is a distinct report identity. A nonmissing `hadm_id` may be linked to a subsequent `hosp/admissions` row by `(subject_id,hadm_id)` for descriptive care-contact classification; a missing `hadm_id` is retained as subject-linked documentation but cannot be assigned to a subsequent admission. Reports are deduplicated only at the note identity level `(subject_id,note_id)` and the admission-level endpoint is `any`, so repeated report/addendum text cannot create multiple patient-level events.

Death and censoring are explicit. Define `death_before_window` only when a death is recorded in `admissions.deathtime`, `admissions.hospital_expire_flag`, or `patients.dod` before `T_i`; this is recorded death evidence, not exhaustive mortality ascertainment. Define subject-level last observable timestamp `C_i` as the latest available relevant `admissions.dischtime`, `admissions.deathtime`, `radiology.charttime`, or `radiology.storetime` for that subject in the supplied snapshot. If `C_i<T_i+30d`, the due window is right-censored in MIMIC. A missing post-D report is therefore coded `not_observed/unknown`, never `not_completed`; outside care, unlinked care, unrecorded care, and absent documentation cannot be separated.

## Route-concealed documentation labels

After parent phase-2 C/S readings, allocation, weights, and the parent endpoint are sealed, a separate documentation packet is generated for each selected admission. The packet contains all eligible post-D radiology reports and their ordered `radiology_detail` fields in the due and placebo windows, plus the minimum admission/date context needed to assess timing. It does not expose the inherited route label, phase-2 route decisions, target order, reviewer labels, or parent endpoint. Readers are blinded to whether a packet is target or negative-control, and post-D text is never fed back to any upstream decision.

Two independent trained readers classify each admission-window packet:

* `L=1` target-linked documentation: at least one report explicitly refers to the same pulmonary nodule/indeterminate focal opacity or its interval follow-up/comparison, and the report's text supports that link without relying on the inherited route. The reader records the supporting note identity and a short evidence span.
* `L=0` qualifying radiology documentation is present but no target-linked statement is supported.
* `K_U` report text is insufficient, contradictory, unavailable, or reader cannot adjudicate. Missing reports are not `L=0`; they are `M0` (no qualifying MIMIC report observed) with care status unknown.
* `P` positive-control signal: the report is a clearly pulmonary/chest radiology report or contains a lung/chest anatomical reference, independent of target linkage. This is a document-availability and extraction control, not a completion endpoint.
* `N` negative-control signal: a report in the same window has a clearly non-pulmonary/non-chest anatomical focus and no target-linked pulmonary statement. It is used only as a specificity control; unclear body region is `K_U`.

The exact lexical cues and reader codebook are frozen in a support file before any real packet is opened. A deterministic text-only screen may prioritize packets, but it cannot assign `L`, `P`, `N`, or `K_U`; all packets with reports receive the same independent adjudication opportunity. The two-reader agreement and blinded re-read subset are reported. This is adjudication of documentation, not diagnosis or imaging interpretation.

## Estimands and comparators

All estimands are finite-population, admission-level, design-weighted descriptive estimands for the frozen target cell. Let `w_i=1/(pi1_i*q_i)` be the inherited path weight, `N_T` the known finite-population target-cell count represented by the phase-1 frame, and `Y_i^due=1` only when both readers support `L=1` in the due window. Report the Horvitz–Thompson total and the Hájek ratio with the known target-cell denominator; retain raw counts and the number of `M0`, `K_U`, death, and right-censored cases.

Primary documentation estimand:

`theta_due = N_T^{-1} * sum_{i in target cell} I(Y_i^due=1)`.

This is the proportion with a *recorded target-linked documentation signal in this MIMIC snapshot*, not the proportion completing imaging. The primary contrast is a paired/placebo contrast using the same design weights:

`Delta_pre = N_T^{-1} * sum_i [Y_i^due - Y_i^pre]` and
`Delta_post = N_T^{-1} * sum_i [Y_i^due - Y_i^post]`,

with each window's denominator and censoring counts reported. These contrasts test temporal concentration of observed documentation, not actual due-date adherence. A secondary opportunity-conditional descriptive quantity is `theta_due|R = P_w(Y_due=1 | at least one adjudicable post-D radiology report in H_i)`, explicitly labeled selection-prone and not a completion rate.

Report the following jointly rather than imputing silent records: `P_w(M0)`, `P_w(K_U)`, `P_w(death_before_window)`, `P_w(right_censored)`, `P_w(any subsequent admission before T_i+30d)`, and `P_w(any post-D radiology report before T_i+30d)`. The last two are MIMIC-observed care-contact/documentation opportunities, not proof of actual care access or completion.

Comparators and negative controls are pre-registered: (a) the within-admission pre-due and post-due placebo windows; (b) non-target anatomical `N` packets in the due window; and (c) a positive-control pulmonary/chest `P` signal to show whether reports are sufficiently informative for anatomical classification. No cross-subject calendar matching, outcome-adaptive comparator, post-hoc interval, or favorable subgroup is permitted.

## Inference and feasibility contract

The layer reuses the inherited `pi1_i`, `q_i`, joint inclusion probabilities, and path weights; it does not pretend that all target admissions are observed. Use the same sequential HT/Hájek machinery and at least 20,000 nested Rao–Wu replicates, reproducing first-stage PSUs, phase-1 bands, phase-2 subcells, micro-censuses, and the frozen target allocation. Bootstrap every ancillary contrast and missingness category. Use one simultaneous 95% max-statistic family covering the parent gates, parent primary endpoint, `theta_due`, both placebo contrasts, opportunity categories, reader agreement, and negative/positive controls.

Reserve at most 240 physician-hours and 6 reviewer-hours per week for this ancillary layer within the inherited 1,400-hour, 96-reviewer-hour, 6-hours/week, 32-week ceilings. Use the worst-case number of reports and `K_U` handling from a synthetic rehearsal fixed before real text exposure; if the added layer cannot fit without reducing inherited phase-2 coverage or changing its allocation, return `ANCHOR_NOT_RUN` and preserve the parent result. No mean workload is allowed. No ancillary reader can read the same packet in a parent C/S role.

The anchor is interpretable only if the target has at least 60 selected admissions as inherited, every selected admission retains positive path probability, both-reader adjudication is available for all observed reports or records `K_U`, positive-control prevalence is not degenerate, and all due/placebo denominators and pair probabilities are defined. Report design-effective sample size and event support; do not use complete cases as the primary estimator.

## Falsification and interpretation

The longitudinal anchoring hypothesis is falsified or materially weakened by any of the following: (1) a prespecified simultaneous interval for `Delta_pre` and `Delta_post` is compatible with no temporal concentration while MIMIC radiology opportunity and positive-control rates are adequate; (2) target-linked `L` is no more specific than the non-target `N` control; (3) due-window signal is driven by a single report, ambiguous addendum, or reader disagreement that fails the agreement gate; (4) substantial `M0`, right censoring, recorded-death competition, or `K_U` prevents distinguishing absent documentation from unobservable follow-up; (5) missing-`hadm_id` handling, shifted-time ordering, reciprocal detail reconstruction, or packet blinding fails; or (6) an analyst treats `M0` or no subsequent admission as noncompletion, uses post-D data upstream, or claims causality.

* Supportive: parent gates pass, the ancillary packet and controls are valid, `Delta_pre` and/or `Delta_post` show preregistered positive temporal concentration with simultaneous bounds, target-linked specificity exceeds `N`, and opportunity/censoring categories are reported. This supports only that the stored-text construct has a MIMIC-observable longitudinal documentation anchor and may justify a separately approved prospective silent bridge.
* Adverse: adequate opportunity and controls are present but temporal concentration/specificity fails or target-linked documentation is contradicted. Downgrade the route's bridge priority; do not relabel it `RECON`, infer unsafe care, or search for another target.
* Inconclusive: `ANCHOR_NOT_RUN`, sparse events, undefined probabilities, high censoring/death, low positive-control information, reader disagreement, or workload/roster failure. Preserve the parent result but conclude that MIMIC cannot anchor the route; obtain an actual workflow log or a better linked dataset.

Even a supportive result cannot establish report delivery, patient viewing, scheduling, imaging completion, clinical appropriateness, diagnosis, responsibility, safety, benefit, or causality. The required next study remains a consecutive-admission, actual-calendar, probability-ascertained prospective silent bridge with immutable workflow logs and concealed routes. A governed comparative study is required for any claim about operational use or patient outcomes.
