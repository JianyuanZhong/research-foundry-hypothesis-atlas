# Donor-aware macrophage-to-FLS communication in E-MTAB-8873

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

In rheumatoid arthritis synovium, reciprocal macrophage–fibroblast-like synoviocyte (FLS) signaling has been proposed to sustain inflammation and failed resolution [K1][K3]. A clinically consequential unresolved question is whether exposure to MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages induces a reproducible, condition-specific transcriptional response in FLS that can be distinguished from FLS-only and macrophage-negative controls. Resolving this would determine whether the available paired coculture data support a testable stromal–myeloid mechanism, or whether apparent effects are better explained by donor, cell composition, batch, treatment, or assay artifacts.

This is a hypothesis, not a computed result. The closest inspected experimental work supports the value of preserving macrophage-containing synovial interactions in RA models [K2], but does not establish this targeted assay’s response or direct ligand-receptor causality.

## Falsifiable experiment

Population: all 33 verified E-MTAB-8873 condition records with a defensible contributing-donor representation; do not infer missing donor identity. Primary contrasts, restricted to treatment=none, are positive-macrophage versus FLS-only and positive-macrophage versus macrophage-negative, within the same FLS donor context where available. Mixed macrophage conditions are a secondary composition check. LPS and inhibitor conditions are mechanistic context, not independent causal replicates.

For each feature, estimate the condition contrast in log-expression space after a small count offset. Use a donor-aware paired/stratified contrast or contributing-donor random-effects model, with uncertainty from donor/condition-preserving permutations. Pre-specify an effect-size threshold (for example log2 fold change 0.25) and donor-level false-discovery/permutation threshold 0.05, contingent on the design supporting the estimand. Require consistency across donors and separation from both controls. Report feature effects, uncertainty intervals, permutation results, donor support, and negative-control behavior.

Supportive: a reproducible condition-specific signature with donor-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable negative-control signal. Adverse: no separation, reversal, or failure after donor/condition adjustment; a signal confined to one donor or feature is artifact-prone. Inconclusive: unresolved donor mapping, insufficient independent donor contexts, low detectability, or uncertainty too large to distinguish communication from composition or technical artifacts.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Baseline: a simple donor-aware paired/stratified contrast per feature with donor-level uncertainty and condition-preserving permutation. It directly tests the prespecified condition effect and exposes which donors contribute.

Substantive alternative: a hierarchical/regularized model across the 445 measured features, retaining donor and condition effects while borrowing information across features. It could reveal coordinated pathway-level structure that single-feature contrasts lose, but cannot establish direct ligand-receptor action or causality. It is deferred unless metadata integrity and adequate feature-level signal are established. Both methods use the same inputs, contrasts, donor-stratified split/permutation, outcome, and uncertainty evaluation. No neural model is justified at this stage because the scientific uncertainty is condition specificity, not predictive performance.

The actual scientific deliverable is a fitted donor-aware contrast table and prespecified condition-specific signature test, including effects, uncertainty, permutation results, negative-control behavior, and an explicit supportive/adverse/inconclusive interpretation. No full study result is claimed.

## Competing explanations and limits

The strongest rivals are FLS donor identity or age/sex differences; macrophage composition or ratio; batch/technical effects; LPS or MerTK-inhibitor treatment rather than macrophage exposure; and targeted-panel measurement or cell-count differences. Paired donor contexts, FLS-only and macrophage-negative controls, condition-preserving permutations, and explicit treatment labels address but do not eliminate these rivals.

Expression association cannot establish direct target action, ligand-receptor causality, clinical transition, or treatment benefit. Clinical phenotype, treatment history, age completeness, and independent external validation are not assumed available. Causal interpretation requires perturbation or independent experimental evidence.

The E-MTAB-8873 direction is pursued because its paired controls and exact local files are available. The multimodal EHR demonstration is not pursued because imaging and longitudinal EHR inputs are not bound to this molecular source. The proteomic aging-clock demonstration is not pursued because no verified local aging-clock inputs are bound to this hypothesis. These are dispositions, not reproduction claims.
