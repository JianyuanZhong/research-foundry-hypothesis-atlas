## One coherent repair and unresolved boundary

This child preserves parent [prior hypothesis], including its R28/R42 observation-selection estimand, weights, eight-label assay trajectory, O0/O1/O2 hierarchy, patient-disjoint split, elastic-net baseline, matched GRU sensitivity, and clinical limits. It adds one repair: a prespecified lead-time stratification of inherited O1 corroboration, with an order-only scheduling proxy and an examination-led pre-event endpoint.

The strongest supported parent claim is only that a pre-index-to-day-42 recorded assay trajectory may improve held-out prediction of a later operational repeat-TACE record after measured observation-selection adjustment. The unresolved stronger claim is that the increment reflects a durable post-TACE state signal rather than near-event workflow/documentation, planned retreatment, or semantic-code ascertainment.

The leading explanation predicts that a real state signal retains a directional increment for a repeat-TACE record corroborated by an examination recorded before the procedure with meaningful lead time. The operational rival predicts an increment concentrated in same-visit or order-only records near the procedure, which can encode planning, execution, or documentation. The semantic-ascertainment rival predicts dependence on procedure label/source alias rather than pre-event clinical corroboration. This is a descriptive/predictive endpoint-validity test, not a treatment-response, causal, or mechanistic test.

## Falsification and decision gates

Run once after definitions and support rules are frozen.

- Support gate: publish counts for O0/O1_lead/O1_orderlead/O1_sameonly/O2_same, procedure aliases/sources, missing and untimed event times, corroborator channels, lead-time bins, same-visit matches, order status/open-start timing, R28/R42, events, censoring, ESS and weight tails. Inadequate O1_lead or O1_orderlead support is INCONCLUSIVE, not pooled away.
- Lead-time gate: SUPPORTIVE requires the trajectory increment to survive R42 weighting and retain direction for O1_lead in [te-42,te-8], with no materially stronger increment restricted to [te-7,te+7]. A monotone loss as lead time increases favors operational timing.
- Scheduling rival gate: if O1_orderlead matches O1_lead in paired uncertainty, the result supports workflow-associated prediction, not durable state information.
- Same-visit gate: if O1_sameonly is positive but O1_lead absent, classify the corroboration as near-event-documentation-compatible. This does not refute the trajectory for a true clinical endpoint.
- Semantic-code gate: repeat fixed contrasts by 手术 alias/source strata and retain the parent early semantic-procedure alias audit. Direction confined to one code/source is adverse to a general endpoint claim.
- Retain parent falsifiers unchanged: unweighted versus R42-weighted results, A_traj28 versus S28, exclusion of lab/process rows in (t0+35,t0+42], process-only block, within-patient timestamp/value permutation, [43,90]/[91,181) horizons, and inherited O1/O2 restrictions.

SUPPORTIVE means O0 and O1_lead both have positive simultaneous lower bounds for trajectory-over-snapshot and trajectory-over-A0, survive R42 weighting and parent falsifiers, while O1_orderlead and O1_sameonly do not supply the matching explanation. It supports a durable recorded trajectory signal for an operationally corroborated repeat-TACE event, not tumor response, intent, benefit, or mechanism.

ADVERSE means the increment is absent in O0, confined to O1_sameonly/O2_same, reproduced by O1_orderlead, collapses with increasing lead time, or varies only by semantic code/source. Narrower workflow-associated or procedure-prediction findings may remain.

INCONCLUSIVE means endpoint support, positivity/ESS, uncertainty, model-representation, or clinical-adjudication limitations prevent discrimination. Freeze the direction and acquire timed imaging/response and treatment-intent evidence before strengthening the claim.

## Alternatives, resource estimate and selection

The confirmatory baseline is the parent auditable elastic-net pooled-logistic discrete-time hazard. The matched learned alternative is a compact time-aware GRU over the identical event stream: label identity, numeric/qualitative/inequality content, relative time, time since prior event and channel. Both use the same split, endpoint family, metrics, uncertainty, R42 weights and endpoint ablations. Elastic-net preserves transparent nested increments; GRU can reveal nonlinear ordering and irregular timing but cannot recover intent, imaging, units or outside care. Primary materialization plus selection, endpoint stratification, bootstrap and permutation controls is estimated at 1–4 CPU hours on 4 CPU/16 GiB; GRU sensitivity at 2–6 hours on 4 CPU/16 GiB with one allocated A100 using cuda:0 if support is adequate. Estimates are planning estimates, not measured runtimes; no GPU is required for the primary. Do not adopt GRU for a small predictive gain.

Not chosen: larger transformers, NLP of untimed documents, latent intent/causal models, and mechanistic response models; these require unavailable validated temporal text, expert intent labels, assay units, images/reports or outside-care capture. Revisit only if a linked timed imaging/intent source becomes available.

## Three inspected works

[K1] Lencioni and Llovet (2010), abstract-only. It distinguishes response-rate/surrogate endpoint measurement from overall survival and explains that locoregional HCC response needs viable-tumor imaging criteria; it notes validation against pathology remains needed. This bounds the proposal: a repeat procedure record is not imaging response. The child adds a lead-time endpoint-validity test for the recorded procedure.

[K2] Ramachandran, Farley and Bentley (2025), abstract-only. It describes how continuous follow-up requirements can induce time-related/immortal-time bias and recommends attrition reporting and design changes. It motivates retaining R28/R42 weighting and adding endpoint lead-time checks; it does not establish bias in this HCC source.

[K3] Mohammadi, Campbell, Sturkenboom and Vaz (2026), abstract-only. Its systematic review reports heterogeneous construction and validation across EHR phenotype libraries and argues for transparent computable definitions. It supports the literal/semantic alias-source audit and explicit clinical validation limit; it does not validate this HCC predicate.
