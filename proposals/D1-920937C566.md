# Episode 81 unit-reconciled, feasibility-audited successor: is numeric repeat-lactate queue value portable across hospital testing practice?

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

At a 24-hour reassessment after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry, a repeat lactate may be available because a hospital tests more intensively, not because the numeric result itself adds information. The selected Episode 79 design asks whether the numeric repeat value improves an equal-capacity mortality-review queue across capacities from 5% through 20%, after representing repeat availability, repeat age, and non-lactate surveillance. It still permits a pooled gain to be driven by hospitals with one testing culture while the gain is absent or reversed in hospitals with another. That is the clinically consequential unresolved question: could a numeric-lactate prioritization rule be used across materially different ICU measurement practices, or would it require local validation and recalibration?

This is an internal practice-portability test, not external transportability. It uses an outcome-blind hospital repeat-availability split as a measurable proxy for testing practice. It does not call that proxy staffing, clinician attention, order behavior, or workload.

**Primary falsifiable hypothesis H-port:** in each frozen hospital practice stratum (low and high repeat-lactate availability) and at every prespecified capacity in C={0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20}, adding the numeric repeat value to R_process increases the source-defined seven-day death fraction among equal-capacity review slots by at least 0.03 deaths per occupied slot—equivalently, at least 3 additional D7 deaths per 100 occupied slots. The fraction 0.03 must never be labeled 0.03 deaths per 100 slots. Define
`Delta7_s(c)` as the within-stratum fixed-slot event-capture contrast and
`phi7=min_{s in {low,high},c in C} Delta7_s(c)`. Support requires the two-sided 95% lower bound for `phi7` to be at least 0.03, with the parent’s pooled `theta7=min_c Delta7(c)`, equal-hospital, eventual-death, and deletion guards also passing.

Three deaths per 100 slots is an operational materiality anchor inherited from the parent, not a validated utility threshold. A supportive result would justify a new prospective silent validation that deliberately samples hospitals with contrasting testing practice. It would not show that lactate should be ordered, that results are visible or reviewed, that review changes care, or that deaths are preventable.

## Ordered falsification and interpretation

1. **Not computable:** any source/hash/header/schema mismatch; unreconciled inherited flow; stratum or outcome gates fail; changed cohort, clocks, features, capacity grid, model, tie or revision rules; post-L24 leakage; nonfinite/nonconvergent fit; fewer than 1,900 finite required bootstrap replicates; missing artifact; copied-hospital leakage; or queue algebra failure.
2. **Adverse opposite practice-capacity direction:** the upper 95% bound for any `Delta7_s(c)` is <0.
3. **Adverse eventual-capture tradeoff:** the lower bound for `phi7` is >=0.03 but the upper bound for `phiInf` is <0.
4. **Adverse heterogeneous materiality across the practice proxy:** the lower bound for one stratum’s minimum is >=0.03 while the upper bound for the other stratum’s minimum is <0.03, without triggering state 2. This falsifies a common material increment across the two frozen EICU strata; it does not establish biological effect modification or explain the heterogeneity.
5. **Adverse below materiality:** the upper bound for `phi7` is <0.03.
6. **Supportive portable numeric increment:** all gates pass; lower bounds for `phi7`, pooled `theta7`, `phi7_macro`, and `phiInf` are at least their prespecified nonnegative/materiality margins; deletion proportions pass.
7. **Inconclusive:** every other computable pattern, including intervals crossing zero or 0.03, deletion instability without a contradictory bound, or favorable pooled points with a failing stratum guard.

Support means only that the numeric repeat value yielded a material, same-direction modeled queue increment in both outcome-blind EICU practice strata across the locked capacity range. Adverse means the specified direction, materiality or portability claim failed; it does not mean lactate has no prognostic value. Inconclusive means this snapshot cannot resolve the claim and is not equivalence.
