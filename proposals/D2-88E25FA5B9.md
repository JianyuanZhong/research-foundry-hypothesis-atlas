# Episode 9 provisional hypothesis

Seed: D2-T1. Status: provisional, evidence-bound; no inferential result has been computed.

## Proposed relationship

In the configured E-MTAB-8316 rheumatoid-arthritis FLS/monocyte coculture experiment, the intervention-associated FLS transcript response depends on macrophage/coculture context rather than being a context-independent response. The prespecified estimand is an intervention-by-context interaction, tested after accounting for the four FLS donor clusters and nested monocyte-donor structure.

This is an intervention-associated expression hypothesis. It is not a claim of direct drug-target action, causal efficacy, mechanism, or clinical benefit.

## Grounded observations and evidence limits

The prior episode-8 board candidate ([prior hypothesis]) records that the configured assay has 12 condition samples, 58,302 features, four verified FLS donor clusters (SA049, SA051, SA104, SA114), six nested monocyte donors, and repeated context measurements. It also records the read-only E-MTAB-8316 matrix, SDRF/IDF, repository metadata, prepared count matrix, prepared design, and assay registry paths and hashes. Those records support data availability and structure only. They do not establish an interaction, direct target action, clinical efficacy, or mechanism. The exact execution-time design columns, join key, and assay-registry contrast labels still require verification; no result is claimed.

## Falsifiable test

Use all 12 verified condition samples, restricted to the four FLS donor clusters and their documented nested monocyte donors. Average repeated measurements within donor/context before exploratory inference and do not treat samples as independent donors. Compare a donor-aware additive model (intervention + macrophage context, no interaction) with the same model plus the prespecified intervention-by-context interaction. Report interaction effect size, uncertainty, donor-level stability, and batch/technical adjustment where fields exist. A nonzero, stable interaction with uncertainty excluding the null is supportive only of context-dependent intervention-associated expression. A precise reproducible null or reversal is adverse to the proposed context dependence. Wide uncertainty, ambiguous labels, failed joins, or insufficient independent donors is inconclusive.

## Missing evidence and next decision

Clinical outcomes, direct target measurements, validated mechanism, and independent technical replicates are unavailable. Cell-specific interpretation requires protocol reconciliation. A supportive result would justify a prespecified follow-up perturbation or independent-cohort test; an adverse result would redirect target prioritization away from this context interaction; an inconclusive result would require assay/protocol verification rather than a stronger claim.

## Method comparison

The simple baseline is a donor-paired descriptive contrast: average within donor/context, calculate intervention contrasts, then compare contrasts across contexts. The substantive alternative is the donor-aware additive-versus-interaction model, which tests the scientific uncertainty directly and estimates uncertainty while preserving donor dependence. A high-dimensional predictive or neural model is deferred because only four independent FLS donor clusters are available and prediction accuracy is not the scientific question. Revisit it only with more independent donors or a different estimand.
