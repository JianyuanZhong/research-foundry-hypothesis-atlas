## Scientific question

Among adults who remain alive in an index ICU at a fixed 72-hour landmark and have an observed early creatinine rise, early observed Foley oliguria, apparent creatinine recovery, dense Foley support, and a W0 weight, is creatinine recovery without late observed Foley-output recovery associated with higher subsequent same-admission in-hospital mortality than concordant recovery?

The strongest available evidence supports only a measurement concern: selected critical-illness patients can have creatinine- and cystatin-C-based kidney estimates that diverge. It does not support the proposed MIMIC prevalence, prognosis, true GFR, mechanism, or causal treatment claim. A supportive result would motivate prospective validation with cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle and volume assessment, and adjudicated urine capture; it would not justify changing care.

## Population, time and phenotype

Unit: one row per admission’s earliest valid ICU stay, sorted by `intime`, then `stay_id). Require `anchor_age>=18`, valid `intime<outtime`, `t0=intime+72h<outtime`, nonmissing `dischtime>t0`, and no `deathtime<=t0`. The estimand is conditional on survival and remaining in ICU at t0 and on observed paired support. Multiple admissions are separate rows; all rows of a subject stay in one split and uncertainty clusters by subject.

Use half-open windows relative to index `intime`: W0=[0,24), W1=[24,48), W2=[48,72). Clinical inclusion uses `charttime` or `starttime`, never `storetime`. Primary observed support additionally requires nonmissing `storetime<=t0`; charttime-only rows are a separate observability sensitivity.

After exact duplicate removal, same-time valid creatinine values across sources are summarized by median with source and disagreement flags retained. Positive numeric creatinine values must have audited mg/dL units. Require at least one primary-observable creatinine in each W0/W1/W2. Let C0=min(W0), C1=max(W1), Cpeak=max(W0∪W1), Clate=min(W2). Early rise is C1 >= C0+0.3 or C1/C0 >=1.5. Apparent recovery is Clate <= Cpeak-0.3 or Clate/Cpeak <=0.75.

For item 226559, valid nonnegative mL values are aggregated into hourly bins. In both W0 and W2 require at least 18 distinct observed chart hours, first-to-last span >=20 hours, and maximum gap <=4 hours. Sum recorded volume over the full 24-hour window and divide by positive W0 weight (226512, else 224639), never by observed hours. Require W0 output <0.5 mL/kg/hour; W2 recovery is >=0.5 and non-recovery <0.5. Absent hour remains missing; recorded zero remains zero.

Primary cells, conditional on rise and recovery, are creatinine recovery plus W2 Foley non-recovery (discordant) versus creatinine recovery plus W2 Foley recovery (concordant). Emit all four creatinine/output cells, density strata, missing-weight and no-early-oliguria strata regardless of primary-cell eligibility.

## Outcomes and estimands

Primary outcome: all-cause in-hospital death with `t0<deathtime<=dischtime); `hospital_expire_flag` is a consistency check. Report missing/impossible/death-flag discordance. Secondary descriptive outcomes: first qualifying post-t0 RRT, later same-admission ICU readmission (`later.intime>index.outtime` and `later.intime<dischtime`), death/RRT first events, and death-or-RRT composite.

Primary association: unadjusted cell risks, risk difference and risk ratio with subject-clustered bootstrap 95% intervals, followed by a standardized regularized logistic contrast using only variables available by 48h and not defining W2 exposure: age, sex, unit/admission context, C0/C1/rise severity, W0 output/coverage, pre-48 MAP/HR/RR/SpO2/lactate/vasopressor/ventilation/RRT summaries and missingness, with CKD/ESRD only as a retrospective sensitivity proxy. Never adjust for Clate, W2 Foley, W2 density, post-t0 treatment or the outcome. A cause-specific death/RRT analysis is sensitivity, not a causal model.

## Directly comparable methods

Same population before phenotype label, same post-t0 death target, same deterministic subject-level 60/20/20 train/validation/locked-test split, and same locked test uncertainty.

Baseline: ridge logistic regression with age, sex, first unit/admission context, creatinine trajectory through t0, pre-48 physiology/support summaries, observation counts and missingness, and CKD/ESRD sensitivity proxy. Exclude all Foley/output/fluid channels.

Learned alternative: CPU HistGradientBoostingClassifier on six-hour bins over [0,72), adding Foley output, weight-normalized output, output density, input-minus-output proxy, MAP/HR/RR/SpO2/lactate, vasoactive/ventilation/RRT exposure, slopes, within-bin variation, counts and missingness. Fit full and urine/fluid ablation on identical split and target. It tests whether timing, persistence, nonlinear thresholds and interactions in observed output/fluid trajectories add information beyond creatinine snapshots. Report AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, curves, grouped feature-family summaries and subject-cluster bootstrap intervals. Require >=20 events and >=20 non-events in train and test for interpretable model metrics; otherwise model comparison is inconclusive.

## Falsification and interpretation

Predeclare 18-hour versus denser coverage, observation-gap strata, storage-available versus charttime-only, hospital-lab-only versus ICU-chart-only creatinine, absolute/relative thresholds, unweighted output, alternative W0 weight, pre-t0 RRT exclusion, within-stay output timestamp permutation preserving values/counts, label permutation within support/creatinine strata where feasible, and a charting-process-only model.

Supportive means the discordant risk is higher with a null-excluding interval, direction persists under dense/storage/source/threshold checks, process features do not explain it, and urine/fluid adds reproducible calibrated information. Adverse means a precise null/reversal under strongest support or process-explained attenuation; temporal model gain alone is adverse for the discordance claim. Inconclusive means incomplete support, inadequate cells/events, irreconcilable outcomes, leakage, unstable calibration/uncertainty or resource failure.

This remains an observed prognostic association, not KDIGO/true GFR, mechanism, treatment effect, causal intervention, bedside utility or external validity. Those claims require cystatin C/measured GFR, complete urine capture, muscle/volume data, clinical adjudication, expert review, prospective monitoring and external validation.

## Method alternatives and deferral

The six-hour tree is chosen because it answers a substantive information question about persistence and nonlinear output trajectories within the CPU budget. A sequence transformer/latent GP was deferred: added representation assumptions would not solve missing GFR or selective Foley evidence. A mechanistic fluid-balance model was deferred because complete intake/output, volume status and treatment indications are unavailable. The alternatives remain recorded for reconsideration only if the support manifest shows adequate paired events and the tree’s output-channel comparison is scientifically underpowered.
