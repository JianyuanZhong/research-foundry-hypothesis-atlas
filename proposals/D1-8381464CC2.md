# Candidate Proposal (EP5 child) — Pre-operative AFP × Liver-Function-Reserve Interaction as a Mechanistic Probe for Early Treatment Re-escalation After Curative Hepatectomy for HCC

**Island:** HCC (post-hepatectomy natural history) · **Episode:** 5 (Generation, child of ep1) · **Parent:** [prior hypothesis] (ep1, mu 29.40, VALID) · **Status:** deliverable, data-bound, discovery-phase (not yet fitted)

**Relationship to parent.** This child restores the ep1 rival-explanation structure (which the ep3 packaging repair, [prior hypothesis], compressed) and adds **one new, pre-specified, computable mechanistic probe**: an **AFP × liver-function-reserve interaction test**. Everything else — population, temporal boundaries, estimand, outcome, data bindings, baseline-vs-learned comparison, and the falsification skeleton — is inherited unchanged from ep1. The interaction test is the single scientific advance over ep1/ep3.

## 1. Unresolved claim and clinical consequence

**Unresolved claim (inherited H1).** After curative-intent hepatectomy for HCC, the **pre-operative laboratory profile** — the **pre-operative AFP level** and **liver-function reserve** (albumin, total bilirubin, prothrombin time, platelet count, A/G ratio, hyaluronic acid) — **independently predicts the time to early treatment re-escalation** (first subsequent TACE or systemic therapy, a *surrogate* for relapse/progression) **beyond a baseline model using only static clinicopathologic features** (age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior locoregional therapy).

**NEW unresolved mechanistic claim (H2, the advance).** The prognostic effect of pre-operative AFP on early re-escalation is **not uniform across liver-function reserve**: the AFP effect is **stronger (or only present) in patients with preserved liver function** (high albumin, normal bilirubin, normal platelets) and **attenuated in patients with impaired reserve / decompensation**. If true, AFP elevation in the preserved-reserve stratum marks **tumor biology** (proliferation/secretion in a functioning liver), whereas in the decompensated stratum AFP is confounded by **hepatic decompensation** (impaired hepatic clearance + regenerative hyperplasia) and loses its tumor-specific signal. H2 is the specific, discriminating test that separates the two leading rival explanations of why AFP might be prognostic at all.

**Why this matters clinically.** Post-resection, 20–70% of HCC patients recur within 2 years and there is **no standardized adjuvant-therapy guideline**; surveillance intensity and the timing of adjuvant/early systemic therapy are decided on static features alone. A model built from **routinely drawn pre-operative labs** (no extra imaging, no extra cost) that (a) identifies patients who will need re-escalation early **and** (b) tells clinicians *in which patients AFP is a trustworthy tumor-biology marker versus a decompensation artifact* directly informs (a) surveillance intensity and (b) whether an elevated AFP should trigger early adjuvant/systemic escalation in that patient. That second, mechanistic read is what the ep1/ep3 designs could not provide.

**Data-driven refinement (inherited from ep1).** A bounded feasibility probe (job-7893f63a, ep1) showed a pre-operative AFP *slope* is computable in only ~4.5% of the cohort (1,160 / 25,933 hepatectomy patients have ≥2 pre-op AFP points in 0–90 d), whereas a **single pre-op AFP level is available in 91.5%** and a **pre-op liver panel in 95.6%**. The primary estimand is therefore built on **pre-op AFP level + liver reserve** (cohort-wide); the **AFP trajectory/slope** is retained as a **pre-specified sensitivity analysis in the 1,160-patient dense subcohort**, not the primary exposure.

**NEW feasibility confirmation (this episode, executed).** A co-coverage probe ([research job], succeeded, 81.5 s) confirmed that **AFP and the liver-reserve panel co-occur in the same patients within the 0–90 d pre-op window**:
- Pre-op (0–90 d) patients with AFP: **17,324**
- Pre-op patients with AFP **and** albumin: **15,025**
- Pre-op patients with AFP **and** PLT: **16,320**
- Pre-op patients with AFP **and** (albumin OR PLT OR bilirubin): **17,251**
- Pre-op patients with AFP **and** albumin **and** PLT: **14,528**

→ The interaction test is **comfortably feasible** (≥1000 required; ~14.5k–17.3k available). This is a measured, executed result, not a planned check.

## 2. Leading claim vs. strongest rival explanations (RESTORED ep1 structure)

The ep1 candidate explicitly enumerated three rivals with concrete differentiating tests. The ep3 repair dropped this section. It is restored here, and the **new interaction test is bound to Rival 3** (the strongest rival).

- **Leading claim (H1):** pre-op AFP level and liver-function reserve carry **independent** prognostic information for early re-escalation beyond static features.
- **Rival 1 — stage/burden confounding:** AFP level simply marks tumor burden/stage, not independent biology.
  *Differentiating test (inherited):* add CNLC stage + tumor size + prior-therapy count to the model; check whether the **AFP effect (HR) still excludes 1** after adjustment, and whether AFP contributes to discrimination **beyond** stage. If AFP vanishes once stage is in, Rival 1 is supported.
- **Rival 2 — clinician-behavior / surveillance selection:** "re-escalation" reflects how aggressively a clinician surveils and treats, not underlying biology.
  *Mitigation (not full refutation, inherited):* a fixed, pre-specified re-escalation definition (first TACE or systemic therapy after index), a fixed follow-up window, and sensitivity to the window (12/24/36 mo). Residual selection bias cannot be fully excluded in a single-center retrospective design — stated as a limitation.
- **Rival 3 — liver reserve proxies decompensation/survival, not recurrence; AFP reflects decompensation, not tumor biology (STRENGTHENED as the strongest rival and now directly testable):** the liver-reserve effect may capture hepatic decompensation (a survival/death signal) rather than recurrence biology, **and** an elevated AFP may be driven by impaired hepatic clearance + regenerative hyperplasia in a failing liver rather than by tumor secretion.
  *Inherited differentiating test:* model AFP and liver-reserve terms **separately** and in combination; report the liver-reserve effect on the **re-escalation** outcome (not death), and compare with a death-outcome sensitivity (death is sparse, n≈288, so this is a secondary check only).
  *NEW differentiating test (H2, the advance):* the **pre-specified AFP × liver-reserve interaction** (Section 4). Under the tumor-biology account, the AFP hazard ratio is **larger in the preserved-reserve stratum** than in the decompensated stratum (interaction coefficient significant, expected direction). Under the decompensation-confounding account, the AFP effect is **uniform across reserve strata** (interaction non-significant) or reversed. This is the observation on which the two rival explanations **differ**, and it is computable on the available data.

These rivals are **not observationally fully separable** in this dataset (no imaging-confirmed recurrence, no randomized surveillance); the design is built to *bound* the leading claim and to report which rival best fits, not to prove mechanism. The interaction test sharpens the bound on Rival 3 without claiming to refute it.

## 4. Estimand, outcome, and the NEW interaction test

- **Primary outcome (inherited):** time from index hepatectomy to **first treatment re-escalation** = first subsequent procedure with `手术` containing TACE strings (`TACE`/`栓塞`/`动脉化疗`/`肝动脉造影`) **or** systemic strings (`免疫`/`靶向`).
- **CRITICAL LIMITATION (inherited, stated):** re-escalation is a **relapse/progression SURROGATE**, not imaging- or pathology-confirmed recurrence. Confirmed-recurrence diagnosis strings are sparse (~1.5k rows) and death is very sparse (288 rows), so a confirmed-recurrence primary is infeasible at this cohort size. Conclusions are about *time to re-escalation*; inference to true recurrence requires clinical adjudication / imaging confirmation not present in the data.
- **Estimand (inherited):** the **causal-agnostic prognostic association** — the difference in the distribution of time-to-re-escalation between patients with vs. without elevated pre-op AFP / impaired liver reserve, **adjusted for static features**, estimated within the 24-month window with right-censoring. A **predictive/associative** estimand, **not** a causal or mechanistic claim. **H2 (the interaction) is likewise an association-level probe of *where* the AFP effect is concentrated, not a mechanistic proof.**
- **Secondary/competing outcomes (inherited):** death (`死亡` in diagnoses, sparse) and any recurrence-diagnosis string, reported descriptively and as a sensitivity only.

### 4.1 The pre-specified AFP × liver-function-reserve interaction test (NEW)

**Scientific purpose.** Test whether the prognostic effect of pre-op AFP on early re-escalation **depends on liver-function reserve** — the observation that separates the tumor-biology account from the decompensation-confounding account (Rival 3).

**Liver-reserve definition (pre-specified).** Two complementary, pre-specified codings, both computed from the same 0–90 d pre-op values:
1. **Composite reserve score (primary):** a **Child-Pugh–style composite** built from the three available pre-op variables — albumin, total bilirubin, platelet count — each scored 1 (best) / 2 / 3 (worst) using **pre-specified, clinically standard cut-points**:
   - Albumin: ≥35 g/L = 1; 28–35 = 2; <28 = 3.
   - Total bilirubin: <2 mg/dL = 1; 2–3 = 2; >3 = 3.
   - Platelet count: ≥150 ×10⁹/L = 1; 100–150 = 2; <100 = 3.
   - Composite = sum (range 3–9). **Preserved reserve = composite ≤ 4; impaired reserve = composite ≥ 5** (pre-specified dichotomy). (Prothrombin time and ascites, the other classic Child-Pugh components, are not reliably present; the 3-variable composite is a transparent, pre-specified approximation, and its limitation is stated.)
2. **Continuous interaction (sensitivity):** log(AFP) × z-scored composite reserve score, to test for a graded (not just dichotomous) interaction.

**Model specification (pre-specified).** In the interaction subcohort (n≈17,251), fit a **Cox PH model**:

`h(t) = h0(t) · exp( β1·log(AFP) + β2·reserve + β3·[log(AFP) × reserve] + β4·static )`

where `static` = age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior-therapy count (the B0 set), `reserve` = the pre-specified composite (dichotomized preserved/impaired for the primary; continuous for the sensitivity), and the **interaction term β3 is the estimand of H2**.

- **Primary H2 test:** the **stratified Cox** — fit the AFP coefficient separately in the **preserved** (composite ≤ 4) and **impaired** (composite ≥ 5) strata, then a **Wald test for the difference in the two AFP log-HRs** (equivalently, the β3 interaction coefficient in the pooled model). **Pre-specified falsification threshold: interaction p < 0.05 AND the preserved-stratum AFP HR > the impaired-stratum AFP HR (expected direction).**
- **What a positive result would and would not establish:** a significant, correctly-directed interaction **supports** the tumor-biology account (AFP is a more trustworthy prognostic marker where the liver is preserved) and **weakens** the decompensation-confounding rival. It would **not** prove mechanism (no imaging-confirmed recurrence, no randomized surveillance, single-center); it is an association-level probe of *where* the AFP effect concentrates. A non-significant interaction **does not refute** the tumor-biology account — it means the two accounts are not separable in this data (see Section 7, inconclusive).

**Data binding for the interaction (exact).** All from `HCC/data_检验_609065997844652188.csv`, latest pre-op value per patient in the 0–90 d window, joined by `(患者主索引, 就诊号)`:
- AFP: `检验` contains `AFP|甲胎蛋白`, `定量结果` → log(AFP).
- Albumin: `检验` contains `白蛋白|ALB`, `定量结果`.
- Platelet count: `检验` contains `血小板|PLT`, `定量结果`.
- Total bilirubin: `检验` contains `胆红素|TBIL|总胆红素`, `定量结果`.
- (PT `凝血酶原时间`, A/G `白球比值`, HA `透明质酸` remain in the B1 reserve set as in ep1, but the **interaction composite uses albumin + bilirubin + PLT**, the three with the highest co-coverage per the executed probe.)

**No unit column exists** on labs; values are **not pooled across assays** and are treated as assay-native (inherited limitation). The pre-specified cut-points assume standard units (g/L, mg/dL, ×10⁹/L); if the assay-native scale differs materially, the solver must report the observed distribution and flag the cut-point sensitivity (this is a stated, bounded risk, not a blocker).

## 6. Baseline vs. learned comparison (inherited, interaction added as a pre-specified probe)

- **Baseline B0:** Cox proportional-hazards model on static features only (age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior-therapy count).
- **Learned/mechanistic B1:** (a) **Cox PH** with AFP level + liver-reserve terms **+ the pre-specified AFP × reserve interaction** (interpretable HRs — the mechanistic read on AFP level vs. stage vs. reserve), **and** (b) a **gradient-boosting time-to-event** model (XGBoost/LightGBM survival) on the same features including the interaction terms.
- **What B1 reveals that B0 loses (inherited + new):** (i) whether AFP and liver reserve add **independent discrimination** beyond static features; (ii) the **shape of the AFP effect** (non-linear / threshold) a static single-value baseline cannot see; (iii) in the dense subcohort, whether **AFP slope** adds signal; **(iv) NEW — whether the AFP effect is *stratum-dependent* on liver reserve (the interaction), which neither B0 nor a plain additive B1 can express and which is the discriminator for Rival 3.**
- **Evaluation (inherited):** **C-index (concordance)**, **time-dependent AUC at 12/24/36 mo**, **calibration** (observed vs. predicted), **decision-curve analysis**, **bootstrap CI** (≥1000 resamples), **patient-level train/test split** (split by `患者主索引`, never by row, to prevent leakage). Report **B1 vs. B0 ΔC-index / ΔAUC with CI.**
- **NEW evaluation for H2:** the **interaction coefficient β3 (HR for the interaction) with 95% CI and p-value**, the **stratified AFP HRs (preserved vs impaired) with CIs**, and a **SHAP analysis of the GBM model** focused on the AFP × reserve joint contribution (marginal SHAP of AFP conditioned on the reserve stratum) to corroborate the Cox interaction from the non-linear model.
- **Why both, not just the GBM model (inherited):** the Cox coefficients give the *interpretable* independent-effect read required to adjudicate Rival 1 (AFP independent of stage) **and Rival 3 (AFP effect by reserve stratum)**; the GBM model tests whether non-linear/interaction structure adds discrimination. This is a substantive method comparison (interpretability + non-linearity + interaction), not a predictive-gain chase.

## 7. Falsification criteria (pre-specified; UPDATED for the interaction)

**Primary (H1, inherited):**
- **Supportive:** B1 C-index (or 24-mo time-dependent AUC) exceeds B0 by a **pre-specified margin (≥ +0.02)** with bootstrap CI excluding 0, **AND** pre-op AFP level and/or liver reserve is an **independent predictor (HR 95% CI excluding 1)** in the Cox model after adjusting for static features.
- **Adverse:** B1 does **not** improve over B0 (Δ CI includes 0) **AND** AFP and liver reserve are **not** independent predictors (HR CIs include 1) after stage adjustment. → supports **Rival 1** (AFP marks stage/burden); the lab profile adds nothing beyond static features.
- **Inconclusive:** re-escalation too sparse, censoring bias dominates (e.g. <30% of cohort observed to 24 mo), or the dense subcohort (n≈1,160) is too small for the trajectory sensitivity to be stable.

**Mechanistic probe (H2, NEW — pre-specified, evaluated in the interaction subcohort n≈17,251):**
- **Supportive (tumor-biology favored):** the **AFP × reserve interaction is significant (p < 0.05) with the expected direction** — the preserved-reserve (composite ≤ 4) stratum AFP HR is **larger** than the impaired-reserve (composite ≥ 5) stratum AFP HR — **AND** the primary H1 is supportive. → the AFP effect concentrates where the liver is preserved, favoring a tumor-biology mechanism and weakening the decompensation-confounding rival.
- **Adverse (decompensation rival NOT supported / AFP uniform):** the **AFP × reserve interaction is non-significant (p ≥ 0.05)** with the AFP HR **similar across reserve strata**. → the data do **not** support the claim that AFP is differentially prognostic by reserve; the decompensation-confounding rival is **not** the explanation for a reserve-stratified AFP effect (there is none), and H2 is not supported. (Note: this does *not* refute H1; it addresses only the *mechanistic* sub-claim.)
- **Inconclusive (H2):** the **primary H1 is supportive (AFP + reserve improve C-index) but the interaction is non-significant** → the two rival accounts (tumor-biology vs decompensation-confounding) are **not separable** in this data; report as **hypothesis-generating only**, do not claim a mechanism. Also inconclusive if the preserved/impaired strata are unbalanced or the interaction CI is too wide to exclude a clinically meaningful effect.

**What each outcome would and would not establish (inherited + new):** supportive H1 → an *independent prognostic association* (predictive), **not** causality or mechanism, and **not** confirmed recurrence. Adverse H1 → AFP/liver reserve are *redundant with stage* in this cohort. Supportive H2 → an *association-level* indication that the AFP effect is reserve-dependent (favors tumor-biology), **not** a mechanistic proof. Adverse H2 → no evidence of reserve-dependence; the decompensation rival is not supported as a stratifying explanation. Inconclusive (either) → no claim; a larger/longer cohort, confirmed-recurrence adjudication, or a multi-center/external design is required.

## 8. Honest limitations (inherited + new)

1. **Re-escalation is a relapse surrogate**, not imaging/pathology-confirmed recurrence.
2. **Single-center, retrospective**; no external validation possible in this dataset (the field's main weakness per [K3]).
3. **No unit column** on labs; cross-assay pooling unsupported; the pre-specified reserve cut-points assume standard units and must be checked against the observed assay-native distribution.
4. **Right-censoring** for recent patients (2024–2025 index) compresses the 24-mo window.
5. **CNLC stage / tumor size** are partially missing (parsed from free text).
6. **Sparse death (n≈288)** and **sparse confirmed-recurrence (~1.5k)** → death/recurrence are secondary only.
7. **Clinician-behavior selection** (Rival 2) cannot be fully excluded.
8. **Trajectory (slope) limited to ~4.5%** of the cohort → sensitivity, not primary.
9. **NEW — the interaction is association-level, not mechanistic:** a significant AFP × reserve interaction does not establish *why* the AFP effect is reserve-dependent (tumor secretion vs clearance vs regenerative hyperplasia all predict a reserve-dependent pattern); it only localizes the effect. Confirmed mechanism requires imaging-confirmed recurrence, serial AFP, or a prospective/external design.
10. **NEW — the 3-variable reserve composite is an approximation** of Child-Pugh (PT and ascites not reliably present); the continuous-composite sensitivity and the per-variable interaction sensitivity bound this approximation.
11. **NEW — interaction power/stratum balance:** the preserved/impaired strata must be reported; if one stratum is small or the interaction CI is wide, H2 is inconclusive by pre-specification (not a null to be "p-hunted").
