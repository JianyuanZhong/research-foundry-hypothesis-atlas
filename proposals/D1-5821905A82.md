# Provisional hypothesis: persistent discordant AKI recovery beyond current severity

Status: provisional and evidence-bounded. No cohort counts, effect estimates, or study results are claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adults with an ICU stay and a prespecified creatinine-defined AKI episode who survive to a fixed 48-hour landmark, persistent creatinine improvement without concurrent urine-output improvement will identify higher subsequent renal deterioration or in-hospital death than concordant recovery, after adjustment for the same landmark severity, treatment state, and observation intensity.

This is an incremental prognostic hypothesis, not a mechanism, causal treatment effect, or proof of true renal recovery. The leading rival is that the apparent discordance is generated by current illness severity, selective measurement, diuretic/RRT exposure, or clinician-driven treatment rather than a persistent renal vulnerability.

## Evidence boundary

The inherited MIMIC-IV branch and its attached inspected works support a measurement concern: creatinine and urine output can change discordantly, and creatinine-based recovery may be misleading in critical illness. The unresolved claim is whether the persistent discordant pattern adds information beyond current severity and measurement intensity. Existing evidence does not establish prevalence, an outcome association, or that discordance is biologically persistent.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence would be reproducible incremental held-out information for the discordant trajectory after adjustment, with uncertainty excluding the prespecified null and robustness to measurement-density and treatment sensitivity analyses. Adverse evidence would be no incremental information, reversal, or attenuation after adjustment, indicating that the cross-sectional mismatch is not clinically useful or is explained by severity/observation artifacts. Inconclusive evidence would be sparse measurements, unstable estimates, or sensitivity analyses that cannot distinguish the explanations.

The experiment cannot establish fluid status, measured GFR, cystatin C, a causal treatment effect, or renal mechanism without additional clinical adjudication or evidence.

## Alternatives and feasibility

The simple baseline is a prespecified landmark logistic/Cox model with current measurements and confounders. The learned alternative is a trajectory model (for example, a regularized recurrent or functional model of creatinine and urine-output series) trained on the same rows and evaluated on the same held-out split. The alternative could reveal persistence and timing information lost by a cross-sectional baseline; it is deferred if the verified measurement density is too sparse or if it does not improve the scientific comparison.
