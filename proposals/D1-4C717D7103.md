## 1. Unresolved question (unchanged)
After circulatory stabilisation in sepsis/septic shock, does renal-vs-respiratory recovery **asynchrony** carry
**organ-specific** information — the matched limb predicts its OWN organ's 7-day failure more than the cross limb
(asymmetry > 0) — or does it reduce to one shared severity/observation trajectory sampled unevenly across beds
(asymmetry ≈ 0)?

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival (unchanged; discriminating prediction)
- **H1 (organ-specific):** matched-limb recovery predicts own-organ failure more strongly than cross-limb recovery,
  after holding a shared inflammatory axis (M2*) and observation intensity (M3); asymmetry > 0.
- **H0 (shared trajectory):** asynchrony is downstream of a single shared severity/observation trajectory; both beds
  fail about equally, asymmetry ≈ 0, and any residual renal signal is creatinine/RRT measurement coupling.
- The two differ on the **sign and magnitude of the matched-minus-cross asymmetry**; a signed, paired contrast after
  M0+M2*+M3 is a falsifying prediction, not merely a performance comparison.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries (no future information)
- Population: MIMIC-IV ICU stays (`icu/icustays`; keys `stay_id`/`hadm_id`/`subject_id`) meeting a sepsis3-style
  definition: suspected infection (`hosp/microbiologyevents` specimen + `hosp/prescriptions`/`hosp/emar` antibiotics
  within the window) plus SOFA-relevant organ support; fallback sensitivity = `hosp/diagnoses_icd` sepsis codes
  (A41*, R65.2*, 995.91/995.92, 038*). One stay per `hadm_id` (first ICU stay) to avoid within-admission dependence.
- **t\*** = first timestamp of a **sustained MAP ≥ 65 mmHg for ≥ 2 consecutive hours with no vasopressor escalation**
  (`icu/chartevents` MAP itemids 220052/220181; vasopressor rate from `icu/inputevents` itemids
  221906/221289/222315/221749/221662/221653). "No escalation" = norepinephrine-equivalent dose during the 2 h window
  does not exceed the preceding 2 h window. t\* computed strictly before any outcome measurement.
- Predictor window **[t\*-24h, t\*]**; outcome window **(t\*, t\*+7d]**; censor at ICU `outtime`/death.
- Split: one **locked** 70/30 split by `icustays.intime` (earliest 70% = development, latest 30% = holdout).
  **Honest caveat:** MIMIC-IV shifts dates per subject into 2100–2200; cross-subject absolute order is therefore
  scrambled, so this is a **leakage-safe ordering proxy for temporal validation, not a true calendar-era split**.
  It is still locked before any modelling and never re-drawn.

## 6. Variables
- **Per-organ predictor (recovery), from [t\*-24h,t\*]:**

  - Respiratory z_P: standardized P/F-ratio trend from FiO2 (223835) + pO2 (50821) + ventilation state
    (225792/225794/227194).
- **Orthogonal renal outcome:** **new RRT after t\*** (inputevents/procedureevents RRT itemids) among patients
  **RRT-free at t\*** → breaks predictor/outcome measurement coupling (K3). Sensitivity: **delayed-KDIGO** creatinine
  stage increase requiring a ≥ 24 h predictor→outcome separation.
- **Respiratory outcome:** reintubation / invasive-vent escalation after t\* (227194, 225792/225794).
- **M0 (snapshot severity at t\*):** MAP, creatinine 50912, lactate 50813, bili 50885, platelets 51265, WBC 51301,
  RASS 228096, norepinephrine-equivalent dose, vent status.
- **M2\* (independent shared inflammatory recovery axis, K1):** WBC 51301 decay, platelets 51265 recovery, absolute
  lymphocytes 51133 over [t\*-24h,t\*] — deliberately NOT built from the two organ limbs.
- **M3 (observation intensity):** count of labs/charts/vitals per organ in [t\*-24h,t\*].

## 7. Sharpened estimand (compilable paired contrast)
For each patient i and organ k ∈ {R,P} define a per-organ scalar z_k and outcome y_k on the locked holdout. Fix one
covariate set **C = M0 + M2\* + M3** a priori.
- **Own-arm** for organ k: `y_k ~ C + z_k`. **Cross-arm** for organ k: `y_k ~ C + z_{other}`. Both fitted on the
  **development** set, evaluated on the **same holdout rows**.
- **Per-bed asymmetry** `Δ_k = AUROC_own(k) − AUROC_cross(k)` (paired, identical rows/covariates).
- **Single-model coefficient asymmetry:** pooled `y_k ~ C + z_k + z_other`, `β_asym,k = β_{z_k} − β_{z_other}`
  (predictors standardized) — reported per bed and pooled.
- **Primary estimand** `Δ = mean_k Δ_k`; **secondary** standardized `β_asym`. **Uncertainty:** 1000-sample patient-
  level bootstrap of the holdout (resample patients, recompute Δ_k, Δ, β_asym) → 95% percentile CIs.
- **Locked choices** (frozen before fitting): C, z_k definition/standardization, split, margin (0.01 equivalence),
  supportive threshold (0.02). No post-hoc covariate or threshold changes.

## 8. Falsification criteria (prespecified) + power gate
- **Power gate (from §4.2):** require ≥ 100 renal RRT events and ≥ 100 respiratory events in the holdout, with
  ≥ 50 in the smallest matched/cross cell. If not met → declare **inconclusive (underpowered)**, do not interpret sign.
- **Supportive (H1):** some bed k has `Δ_k ≥ +0.02` AUROC with 95% bootstrap CI excluding 0 **and** `β_asym,k > 0`
  with CI excluding 0, reproduced under the orthogonal (new-RRT) renal outcome **and** the delayed-KDIGO sensitivity;
  permutation control (§) covers 0.
- **Adverse (H0):** both `Δ_R` and `Δ_P` ≤ 0, **or** both 95% CIs lie within ±0.01 equivalence margin of 0 (shared
  trajectory / no organ-specific information).
- **Inconclusive:** CI wide and straddling 0.02 without excluding 0; or the effect is driven entirely by M3; or the
  permutation control is non-null (design fails internally, not interpreted); or the power gate fails.
- **Negative control:** permuted-limb assignment (swap z_R ↔ z_P before fitting) must yield Δ and β_asym CIs covering 0.

## 9. Method exploration (baseline vs alternative on the SAME question)
- **B0 (baseline, interpretable):** per-bed penalized logistic regressions on C + z_own + z_cross; asymmetry from
  ΔAUROC and standardized β_asym. Transparent, but removes shared variance only through the pre-specified observed
  covariates M0/M2\*/M3; it cannot tell whether a residual shared **latent** factor still drives both beds.
- **A1 (alternative, mechanistic):** joint latent-variable / dynamic-factor model over the multivariate
  [t\*-24h,t\*] trajectory — one **shared severity factor** f_shared (loads on both organ limbs + inflammatory
  markers) and two **organ-specific factors** f_R, f_P (each loads only on its own organ's markers), fit by EM / PPCA
  (sklearn `FactorAnalysis` / custom EM), **CPU only**. Then predict y_k from the residualized organ-specific factor
  vs the shared factor. **What A1 reveals that B0 loses:** whether the asymmetry is organ-specific variance *after
  explicitly subtracting a shared latent severity factor*, and a factor-loading test (does f_R load on y_R more than
  f_P does). B0 conflates "no asymmetry" with "asymmetry absorbed into observed covariates".
- **Same data, split, outcome and evaluation for B0 and A1.** Neither needs a GPU.
- **Resource/time:** extraction of per-organ scalars + C from streamed members ≈ 10–20 min; B0 fits ≈ seconds;
  A1 EM (≤ 5 factors, ≤ 20k dev rows) ≈ minutes. **Total < 1 h, CPU only, no GPU requested.** (These are estimates;
  the §4.2 probe will confirm exact sizes within budget.)

## 10. What each outcome would / would not establish
- **Supportive** → evidence for organ-specific divergence beyond a shared inflammatory axis and observation intensity;
  still association, not mechanism or causality, and not creatinine-independent.
- **Adverse** → asynchrony reduces to a shared severity/observation trajectory; organ-asynchrony monitoring adds no
  organ-specific information and the line should be parked.
- **Inconclusive** → retain; needs a larger cohort or a renal marker independent of creatinine.

## 12. Limitations (honest)
- Single-centre retrospective; association only — **no causal/mechanistic claim**.
- Renal limb (creatinine/urine) biased by muscle mass and RRT (K3); **cystatin C is unavailable in MIMIC-IV** →
  renal asymmetry attenuated/uncertain. Mitigated, not removed, by the orthogonal new-RRT outcome + delayed KDIGO.
- Observation intensity adjusted (M3), not eliminated; MIMIC-IV per-subject date shift makes the split an ordering
  proxy, not a true calendar-era validation.
- Cohort prevalence/power not yet observed (design only); the §8 gate may return this as inconclusive/underpowered.
- **Requires clinical adjudication / new data:** confirmatory renal marker (cystatin C); external multi-centre
  validation; sepsis-definition adjudication; no claim of asynchrony as a treatment target.
