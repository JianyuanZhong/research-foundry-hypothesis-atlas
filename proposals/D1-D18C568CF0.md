## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

After a TACE-like episode, a falling AFP can be compatible with a favorable tumor-marker trajectory, but the available local record cannot establish tumor response. If AFP falls while albumin is stable and total bilirubin is not worsening, the recorded pathway may differ from the pathway after an AFP fall accompanied by worsening hepatic reserve. The unresolved clinical question is whether this discordance is reproducibly associated with later recorded liver-directed care versus alternate/mixed pathways or loss of local observation, beyond measurement intensity, baseline history and procedure-capture artifacts.

Primary hypothesis, stated as a recorded-pathway hypothesis:

> Within the full eligible day-45 risk set, the joint pattern of early AFP direction and hepatic-reserve direction is associated with a different cause-specific distribution of first recorded pathways from days 46–365. In particular, AFP decrease with stable hepatic markers will have a higher subsequent probability of a specific recorded liver-directed therapeutic pathway than AFP decrease with hepatic deterioration, while AFP non-decrease with preserved hepatic markers will not be interchangeable with either pattern. The interaction and its direction should persist for the parent’s explicit/bundle procedure tiers and encounter-proxy HCC-supported outcomes, and should not be reproduced by a non-liver capture control or a pre-index placebo.

This is falsifiable: the prespecified interaction may be absent, reverse, disappear under same-encounter high-specificity outcomes, or be explained by observation/capture variables. The claim concerns local recorded pathways, not viable tumor, treatment response, indication, intent, completion, benefit, survival, mortality or causal effects.

The strongest available evidence supports only a linkable local substrate. It contains dated encounters, procedures, labs and examinations, but diagnoses lack a native timestamp; labs have no unit column; and procedure names are heterogeneous. The local audit confirms AFP and hepatic-marker names including \`alpha-fetoprotein\`, \`albumin\`, \`total bilirubin\`/bilirubin variants and platelet-related names, with numeric and qualitative results mixed. This supports within-assay, time-aligned contrasts but not ALBI/MELD, cross-assay pooling or unit-dependent clinical cutoffs. Direct procedure and diagnosis counts and the parent’s specificity audit remain evidence of availability only, not treatment completion or disease truth.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

All models use the same eligible cohort, episode ontology, exposure window, target, competing states, split and uncertainty procedure.

- **B0/Bobs:** regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazards from age, sex, index year/department, pre-index diagnoses/procedures, day-45 encounter intensity, measurement availability, examination/reassessment opportunity, procedure-source mix and local-observation variables. Bobs adds testing/access and lab availability but no numeric trajectory.
- **Badditive:** Bobs plus AFP and hepatic changes as separate main effects, component missingness and timing. It asks whether the interaction is unnecessary.
- **Bint (primary transparent interaction model):** Badditive plus the prespecified AFP-direction × hepatic-direction cross-classification and interactions with specificity/capture strata. It estimates interpretable pathway contrasts and is the substantive test of the hypothesis.
- **M3 irregular-time multi-output state-space alternative:** a low-rank state-space model with separate latent AFP and hepatic-reserve states, assay-specific observation models, an observation/contact intensity and competing transition hazards. Fit only on pre-landmark history, evaluate on identical locked outcomes and use patient-clustered uncertainty. It can reveal asynchronous sampling, nonlinear trajectories and discordance that endpoint changes or categories lose. The learned/mechanistic alternative is retained because the scientific uncertainty is the joint latent state and sampling process, not a small score gain.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

All rules, margins, bootstrap units and category definitions are frozen before the locked test.

Supportive computation requires: (1) Bint’s prespecified interaction contrast is directionally and materially nonzero for the full-index primary broad transition, with patient-clustered interval excluding the null and acceptable calibration/overlap; (2) the same direction is present for \`S_explicit\` and \`S_bundle\`, with at least 100 locked-test outcome events per requested overlay or an explicit sparse/inconclusive label; (3) the interaction changes pathway-specific cumulative incidence, not just an aggregate score, and is not erased by Bobs/availability adjustment or training-only observation weighting; (4) the direction is not similar for the non-liver capture control, pre-index placebo, or high-contact-only stratum; and (5) Bint is calibrated and stable under encounter-only versus patient-time episode grouping and the parent’s 14/30-day opportunity sensitivities. If M3 is run, convergence plus a predeclared improvement in interaction log score or pathway calibration on the same locked target is required before describing latent discordance as informative.

Adverse computation includes no interaction after Bobs adjustment; reversal across specificity tiers; concentration in a single source, assay, high-contact group or non-disappearing subgroup; similar interaction for non-liver/placebo controls; poor overlap/calibration; outcome reversal under encounter-constrained grouping; or M3 nonconvergence. These findings support measurement, coding, episode or local-retention explanations—not a clinical tumor-response or liver-toxicity conclusion.

Inconclusive computation includes insufficient paired numeric support, excessive qualitative/inequality results, sparse discordance cells or locked events, temporal-split failure, positivity failure, unstable assay vocabulary, bootstrap intervals crossing the prespecified margin, or inadequate follow-up. A null or adverse result is scientifically useful and does not prove absence of biological response. The verifier can check construction, model fitting, uncertainty and interpretation-to-output linkage, but cannot adjudicate TACE indication, intent, completion, radiologic response, viable tumor, death, outside care, appropriateness, utility or benefit. Expert clinical review and a separately adjudicated or linked prospective/external study would be required for those claims.
