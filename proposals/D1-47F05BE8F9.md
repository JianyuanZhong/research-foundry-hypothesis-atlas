# Episode 23 HCC child: assay content versus assay opportunity

Status: substantive design only. No fitted result, endpoint rate, effect estimate, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

Parent: [prior hypothesis]. This child preserves the parent’s first-index TACE population, day-28 competing observation audit, day-42 landmark, O0–O4 operational endpoint hierarchy, patient-held-out split, uncertainty family, and non-causal limits. It adds a decision-changing decomposition that is not just another missingness safeguard: whether a day-42 assay increment remains after equating the opportunity and care process that generated assay rows.

## Why this is the remaining scientific boundary

The strongest evidence-supported claim is narrow. In a separately adjudicated HCC cohort, post-TACE laboratory trajectories were clinically associated with survival and progression [K3]. Longitudinal record models can predict from dated recorded trajectories when time and same-time leakage are controlled [K1], and stable person heterogeneity should be separated from temporal deviations with explicit selection assumptions [K2]. None of those works establishes that a recorded HCC assay change is biological, that a later TACE row is intended or completed treatment, or that an assay value adds information beyond the process that caused it to be measured.

The parent already asks whether stable pre-index profile versus within-person Delta42 survives a pre-outcome day-28 observation audit. A remaining boundary is that assay content and assay opportunity are still easily conflated: a patient who has more laboratory panels, more orders, and denser care can both generate a larger apparent Delta42 and be more likely to receive a later recorded TACE. A timing-only model is useful but does not force an assay-opportunity-equated comparison.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among eligible patients with the same measured stable profile, day-28 observation history, and day-42 assay/care opportunity, does the content of assay results through day 42 add held-out information for a later operational repeat-TACE record?

Leading explanation: a within-person assay-content signal remains after opportunity is equated. Strong rivals: (1) care intensity or clinician surveillance generates both assay values and repeat-TACE records; (2) the number and composition of ordered/recorded panels, rather than their results, carry the signal; (3) stable severity and measurement selection explain it; (4) the apparent increment is a day-29–42 boundary artifact. This is a descriptive predictive comparison, not a causal treatment or biological mechanism test.

A positive result would justify proceeding to clinical adjudication of assay meaning and treatment intent. A null content increment with a positive opportunity increment would redirect the next study toward capture/workflow measurement rather than assay biology. An adverse or unsupported result would stop escalation to a more complex biological model.

[K1] supports strict event-time masking but also bounds the interpretation to recorded trajectories. [K2] motivates separating stable, temporal, and selection structure without making weighting causal. [K3] supplies the clinical rationale for caring about post-TACE laboratory trajectories while its adjudicated survival/progression outcomes remain unavailable here. The exact inspected excerpts and corrected hash-linked receipts are attached in this branch.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Let t0 be the first parseable TACE procedure start for a patient. Retain the parent’s symbols:

- Hpre = [t0−365 days, t0); H28 = [t0, t0+28 days]; H42 = [t0, t0+42 days].
- A0 contains baseline demographics, stable pre-index assay profile P_stable, pre-index observation/care features, and time/availability clocks.
- Delta42 is assay-specific within-person change from the development-fold-frozen pre-index reference to valid H42 assay results. No zero is substituted for a missing baseline.
- O0 is a later literal-code TACE in [t0+43 days, t0+181 days); O4 is the parent’s closed-coherent operational endpoint. The primary outcome is O4 when support permits, with O0 as the mandatory broader sensitivity.
- Q28-42 is the parent’s mutually exclusive day-28 observation outcome. Q is diagnostic and never a predictor or endpoint relabeler.

Define a frozen assay-content set before model fitting from exact normalized assay labels observed in the source: 白蛋白, 总胆红素, 甲胎蛋白, and 血小板. These are analyzed within assay; no cross-assay numeric pooling is allowed because the catalog documents no unit column. For each patient, assay-content rows require a parseable 检验时间 in the relevant window and a nonblank numeric 定量结果; qualitative-only rows are retained as availability but not treated as numeric content.

Define opportunity/process features from the same source rows, without result values:

- L_opp42: per-assay and total counts of lab rows, distinct assay labels, valid-timestamp indicators, days sampled, first/last lab time, and result-present versus result-missing indicators in H42. The result-present indicator means a result was recorded, not that it was clinically valid.
- O_opp42: counts and valid-time/status completeness of non-drug orders in H42 from 医嘱(非药品), 开立时间, 开始时间, 结束时间, 医嘱状态, and 频次.
- C_opp42: encounter, examination, medication, and non-assay lab activity counts and timestamp coverage; these are process features, not clinical severity.
- L_content42: only the assay-specific numeric values and Delta42, with stable P_stable separated from within-person change.

The primary content increment is the held-out paired loss contrast:
G_content,j = L_w(A_clock42 + P_stable + L_opp42 + O_opp42 + C_opp42,j) − L_w(A_clock42 + P_stable + L_opp42 + O_opp42 + C_opp42 + L_content42,j).
Report this for O4 and O0, ordinary and the parent’s day-28-frozen R42 transport arm. The opportunity increment is:
G_opp,j = L_w(A_clock42,j) − L_w(A_clock42 + L_opp42 + O_opp42 + C_opp42,j).
The stable-profile increment remains G_stable as inherited. These are nested predictive information contrasts, not causal effects.

A prespecified sensitivity uses exact opportunity strata: no target-assay result, one target-assay opportunity, and at least two; within each, report content increments only where paired support exists. A second sensitivity replaces assay values with within-patient permutation among rows sharing assay label, sample type, and H42 time bin, preserving counts, missingness, and opportunity. All feature and stratum definitions are frozen before test evaluation.

Supportive evidence requires a positive simultaneous lower bound for G_content,O4 and G_content,O0 (or a clearly prespecified primary O4 with O0 concordance), in both methods and ordinary/weighted arms, while G_opp does not fully account for the parent’s increment, the within-opportunity estimate is directionally consistent, content permutation and result-removal falsifiers fail, and Q/positivity/ESS are adequate. This supports only an observation-robust record-level assay-content increment.

Adverse evidence is a null or reversal after opportunity adjustment, an opportunity-only increment matching the full model, a result-removal model matching the content model, a surviving content permutation, or an effect confined to one opportunity stratum/late window. This favors surveillance, care workflow, stable severity, or representation artifacts; it does not identify which unmeasured cause is responsible.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse paired opportunity strata, unsupported Q transport, extreme weights, malformed assay times/results, method disagreement, wide intervals, or O4 closure/capture unknowns. Do not change assay labels, windows, strata, endpoint rules, seeds, or gates after test inspection. Continue only with assay calibration, richer capture, adjudicated treatment intent/response, or an external cohort.

## Matched methods and compute

Both methods use the same patients, endpoints, opportunity definitions, Q labels, H28/H42 boundaries, risk grid, right-censoring masks, folds, and test rows. Q and all post-tp rows remain prohibited predictors.

Transparent baseline: pooled-logistic discrete-time hazard with intercept and risk-bin indicators, elastic-net alpha 0.5. Fit nested blocks: clock only; clock plus stable profile; clock plus stable profile plus opportunity/process; and clock plus stable profile plus opportunity/process plus assay content. Fit the Q multinomial model separately. Parser dictionaries, assay label normalization, numeric parsing, scaling, imputation, reference construction, hyperparameters, Q weights, and weight truncation are learned inside five grouped development folds.

Substantive learned alternative: the matched two-stream GRU, one layer, hidden size 32, dropout 0.10, 32/16 head, Adam 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, maximum 50 epochs, eight-epoch early stopping, seeds 17/29/41. One stream contains clock/care/opportunity features and the other stable/within-person assay content; fit the same nested variants and Q model on identical folds. It could reveal nonlinear interaction between panel opportunity and assay combinations that a block-additive baseline loses, but a predictive gain alone does not establish biology. The elastic-net is selected as primary for auditability; the GRU is retained to determine whether the content-versus-opportunity conclusion is representation-sensitive.

Planning estimates, not measured results: deterministic source/join/opportunity audits on 4 CPUs/16 GiB for 1–3 hours; elastic-net, Q weights, paired bootstrap and permutations on 4 CPUs/16 GiB for 1–3 hours; three-seed GRU on 4 CPUs/16 GiB for 2–5 hours. A single allocated A100 is optional for the GRU (gpus=1, explicit cuda:0); CPU remains scientifically adequate for the baseline. The future solver envelope is 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, up to 8 GPUs, and 28,800 seconds. These are planning estimates, not discovery measurements.

## Split, uncertainty, and falsification

Sort index patients by t0: earliest 80% development and latest 20% untouched test, with no patient in both. Fit every parser, opportunity feature, assay dictionary, scale/imputation, Q model, weight rule, hyperparameter, and early-stopping choice only within development folds. The untouched test cannot change outcome or feature definitions.

Report patient-level paired log loss, Brier and integrated Brier scores, calibration, risk-bin counts, discrimination secondarily, opportunity frequencies, paired support by exact opportunity stratum, Q frequencies, weights, ESS, and event/risk-set counts. Use 1,000 patient-level paired bootstrap resamples of the untouched test with a max-|t| simultaneous 95% family over O0/O4, G_stable, G_opp, G_content, R28/R42, ordinary/weighted arms, opportunity strata, and falsifiers.

Frozen falsifiers:

1. Remove all assay result values while retaining lab rows, assay labels, timestamps, missingness, and the full opportunity/process block.
2. Permute numeric results within patient and assay label/sample type/time bin, preserving opportunity and missingness.
3. Fit timing/source/opportunity-only models with no assay content; a matching result undermines the content interpretation.
4. Remove all assay rows in (t0+35,t0+42] while retaining earlier opportunity and the same endpoint.
5. Compare exact opportunity strata and report whether paired support is adequate; no favorable rebinning after test inspection.
6. Repeat the parent’s R28 pre-outcome content/opportunity contrast and Q audit; day-42-only content is boundary-sensitive.
7. Compare O0 and O4 and the parent’s O_planproxy_cap process control; do not create a clinical endpoint from absence.
8. Report content increments by capture latency [0,24) and [24,72] for O4 candidates; no interpretation is made for empty bands.
9. A development-only permutation of opportunity covariates is a process diagnostic, not an exchangeability test.

## Alternatives, seed review, and decision

The UKB expert seeds were reviewed as research context. The feasible seeds concern MRI/strength, CRP/GlycA, CBC discordance, or other UKB field groups and are bound to different participant namespaces and source groups. They cannot be imported into this HCC campaign, and no UKB-to-HCC join is available. The strongest seed-adjacent question (repeated CBC discordance and later cancer-type events) is not a valid HCC restart because HCC lacks the UKB repeated-field namespace, registry cancer outcomes, and comparable temporal event definitions. It was therefore not published as an HCC candidate.

A new HCC radiographic-response direction was considered but deferred: examinations expose 检查所见 and 检查诊断, yet no validated narrative temporality, image payload, response/progression adjudication, or outside-care capture is available. It could become the next study after expert adjudication of a time-anchored report sample; a text model now would only convert an unvalidated record into another unvalidated endpoint.

The selected repair is assay-content versus assay-opportunity because its required inputs are present as structured, timestamped HCC rows, its adverse result changes the next study decision, and it tests a substantive interpretation boundary rather than a small predictive improvement. Revisit the deferred radiographic direction only if an expert-reviewed report endpoint with reliable time anchoring and sufficient held-out support is supplied. If this child’s opportunity-adjusted content increment is adverse or inconclusive, stop adding model complexity and prioritize assay units, capture completeness, and endpoint adjudication. If supportive, continue only to a clinically adjudicated replication.
