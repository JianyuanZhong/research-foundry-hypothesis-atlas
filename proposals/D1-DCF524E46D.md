# Lagged ascertainment audit and child proposal: discordant-AKI recovery

Status: proposed clinical-methods child; no cohort scan, model fit, or scientific result was run.

Parent: [prior hypothesis]. This branch preserves its MIMIC-IV source, population question, item bindings, three landmarks, component endpoints, and baseline-versus-sequence comparison. It repairs temporal eligibility and tie/washout accounting so that the discordance used to predict an outcome is observed before the component clock begins.

## 1. Audit finding and scientific opening

The parent correctly made hospital death and ICU-recorded RRT separate estimands. Its remaining ambiguity is consequential:

1. Its primary exposure window ends at L, while an exposure-defining creatinine/urine/input observation can be charted at the same boundary at which follow-up begins. A favorable result could therefore be co-temporal ascertainment rather than a lagged prognostic signal.
2. “First eligible ICU stay” and same-time event handling are not executable enough to prevent later-stay substitution, ambiguous RRT-at-discharge ties, or inconsistent death-time records.
3. The parent reports pre-landmark attrition and process density, but it does not explicitly identify the six-hour interval between the exposure and landmark as a washout/selection interval. Death, ICU exit, and RRT in that interval can determine who reaches the risk set.
4. The component clocks are conceptually correct but need a deterministic rule for missing/deathtime ties and a strict distinction between hospital death observed after ICU exit and ICU-only RRT that is no longer observable after that exit.

The strongest evidence supports only bounded claims. [K1] shows that creatinine-based kidney-function estimates can overestimate measured function during prolonged critical illness as muscle declines. [K2] shows that urine-output window and persistence choices alter mortality prediction. [K3] associates positive recorded fluid balance and severity with ICU AKI/mortality while following patients through ICU discharge or death. None establishes that MIMIC discordant recovery is intrinsic renal dysfunction, that ICU-recorded RRT is complete, or that the association is causal.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among adults reaching a prespecified 24-, 48-, or 72-hour post-AKI landmark alive and in the first eligible ICU stay, does a creatinine-recovery/urine-or-recorded-balance discordance measured at least six hours before the landmark predict hospital death or ICU-recorded RRT over the following seven days, after accounting for severity and recording opportunity?

The leading rival is residual severity and surveillance: a sicker patient may have more charting, less urine, more recorded input, earlier discharge/death, and more RRT recording. A second rival is creatinine measurement artifact from muscle loss. The lagged component design makes predictions differ: a signal that remains with the same direction across landmarks, opportunity strata, and a six-hour lag is more reproducible as a prognostic marker; a signal that appears only with boundary observations, unknown exclusion, dense charting, or the mixed union is weakened. It still does not prove mechanism or causality.

## 4. Lagged exposure and landmarks

For L in {24, 48, 72} hours after t0, let `Ltime=t0+L`. The primary lagged assessment interval is:

`A_L = [max(t0, Ltime-24h), Ltime-6h)`.

Thus no primary exposure value is recorded in the six-hour washout `[Ltime-6h,Ltime)` or after Ltime. A_L contains three six-hour bins. At L=24 it begins at t0 and still contains three bins; at L=48 and 72 it is the preceding 18 hours. The solver must report the exact bin boundaries and counts.

Use the most recent valid creatinine in A_L, peak creatinine from t0 through the end of A_L, and recovery defined as peak minus lagged creatinine >=0.3 mg/dL; a peak/landmark ratio <=0.8 is a sensitivity. The primary urine set is `icu/outputevents` itemids 226559 Foley, 226560 Void, 226561 Condom Cath, 226563 Suprapubic, and 226567 Straight Cath. Numeric zero is an observed zero; an empty bin is unknown, not zero. Convert to mL/kg/h using the nearest valid weight at or before the lagged endpoint, within 48 hours, from `icu/chartevents` itemids 224639, 226512, 226531, with units checked against `icu/d_items`.

Primary urine classifiability requires valid urine information in at least two of three bins; sensitivity analyses require all three, or at least one. Exclude 226564/226565 nephrostomy, 226627/226631 OR/PACU, 226566/227489 mixed GU-irrigant, 226713 text estimate and 227519 Apache-derived output from the primary phenotype; these are measurement-error sensitivities only.

For recorded balance, use `icu/inputevents` intervals overlapping A_L and one prespecified mutually exclusive volume rule: never add `amount` and `totalamount` from the same row. Convert only compatible mL quantities, retain item-level composition and non-mL/unresolved fractions, and calculate recorded input minus direct primary urine. Recorded balance is a documentation proxy, not true total-body balance. A classifiable balance state requires valid input and direct-urine opportunity; otherwise it is unknown.

The mutually exclusive primary states are concordant recovery, low-urine-only, positive-recorded-balance-only, both, unknown/insufficient opportunity, and no recovery. The parent binary discordant state (low urine OR positive recorded balance) is retained. Unknown is never imputed. The current-window [L-24h,Ltime) exposure is a prespecified sensitivity to quantify boundary co-temporality.

The six-hour washout is part of the audit, not silent censoring: report death, ICU exit, RRT, missing creatinine, insufficient opportunity and exposure-state loss in `[Ltime-6h,Ltime)). Subjects must be alive, in the retained ICU, and without pre-L incident RRT at L to enter R_L. No subject with a washout event is assigned a later exposure state; it remains in the attrition flow. This makes the lagged result a landmark prognostic estimand, not an onset-cohort or causal effect.

## 5. Component-specific outcome clocks

For every L, report all causes and exceptions before estimating contrasts.

Hospital death D_L,7 is the first valid `hosp/admissions.deathtime` in `[Ltime,Ltime+7d)` while the admission is still active. Alive `dischtime` is a competing event; an admission alive at day 7 is administrative censoring. Hospital death remains observable for D after ICU exit and before hospital discharge. `hospital_expire_flag` checks consistency but does not create an event time. If death time is missing or inconsistent, report an exception and do not silently place the event at discharge. If `deathtime=dischtime`, classify as death only when the death flag/time is internally consistent; otherwise exception.

ICU-recorded RRT R_L,7 is the first `icu/procedureevents` item in {225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809, 225955} with a valid `starttime` in `[Ltime,Ltime+7d)` and strictly before `icustays.outtime), while the patient is alive and in the retained ICU. The endpoint is not all hospital RRT. ICU discharge alive and hospital death before RRT are competing events; ICU discharge wins when it occurs first, and death wins when its valid time is earlier or tied. An RRT start exactly at ICU outtime/deathtime is not an RRT event and is reported as a tie exception. Missing RRT start time is unobservable for this endpoint and is counted separately.

Use Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence at seven days and risk differences (discordant minus concordant) standardized over the observed R_L. A cause-specific six-hour discrete-time hazard model is an analysis implementation, not a replacement for the competing-event estimand. For D, causes are death and alive discharge; for R, causes are RRT, alive ICU exit and death. Report person-time, events, competing events, administrative censoring, and time exceptions. The union death-or-ICU-RRT remains exploratory only, with its mixed ascertainment shown beside both components.

It is a surveillance/severity diagnostic, not a negative-control outcome and not evidence that documentation causes death.

Repeat component contrasts in prespecified opportunity strata (1, 2, or 3 urine bins; input/urine both observed versus unknown; low versus high non-renal density), without post-L adjustment or outcome-derived strata. Compare unknown-state size and outcome rates. Report a complete-opportunity sensitivity requiring all three bins and a current-window sensitivity. A process-only gradient comparable to the kidney contrast, or attenuation when opportunity is restricted, weakens interpretation. Persistence across lagged landmarks and strata supports reproducible prognostic association but cannot establish true GFR, fluid status or mechanism.

For classifiable states at both 24 and 48 hours, estimate lagged state transitions and separate D/R outcomes after 48 hours. Subjects with earlier death, exit, RRT or lost opportunity remain in the flow and do not receive a transition. Use subject-clustered bootstrap or an influence-function method across landmarks.

## 9. Interpretation and falsification

Supportive results require a directionally consistent, clinically nontrivial lagged component contrast at more than one landmark, with uncertainty reported, no dependence on boundary observations, attenuation smaller than the process-only rival, and compatible results in supported opportunity strata. Persistent discordance being higher risk than resolving discordance would support a reproducible prognostic marker. The conclusion remains limited to the component: death is not proof of kidney dysfunction; ICU-RRT is not hospital-wide RRT.

Adverse results are disappearance or reversal after the six-hour lag, a contrast confined to the current-window sensitivity, a process-density gradient as large as the kidney contrast, unknown-state selection, a union signal driven only by ICU surveillance, or a contrast present only in contaminated item definitions. These weaken this prognostic interpretation but do not prove normal kidney function, benign fluid status, or absence of treatment effect.

Inconclusive results include sparse RRT or transition cells, wide clinically important intervals, inconsistent death times, large unknown opportunity, strong discharge selection without adequate support, or a sequence-model gain without a reproducible component distinction. Do not call an imprecise null decisive refutation.

Computationally checkable claims are source/member/schema binding, joins, first-stay and t0 filters, lag/washout boundaries, event precedence, flows, competing-event counts, cumulative-incidence estimates, uncertainty, process diagnostics and held-out metrics. True GFR, muscle mass, total-body volume, insensible losses, treatment intent, complete ward RRT, clinician goals of care, causal effects and transportability require clinical adjudication or another study. The configured automatic verifier cannot establish them.

## 10. Unavailable evidence and alternatives not chosen

MIMIC lacks cystatin C, measured GFR/iohexol, muscle mass, tubular biomarkers, true total-body fluid balance, insensible losses, treatment intent, complete bedside collection adjudication, reliable ward-only RRT capture and prospective discharge status. CVP is exploratory, not a volume gold standard. This is an adaptation, not reproduction of any cited work.

The fixed current-window death-or-ICU-RRT union as primary was rejected because it mixes outcome ascertainment and can be surveillance-driven; it remains a sensitivity. Ordinary censoring of ICU exit for RRT was rejected because ICU exit is informative and ends observation. A single sequence model as primary was deferred because it cannot resolve ascertainment. Inverse-probability correction for discharge/ward RRT was deferred because the required observation model and complete ward endpoint are unavailable. A causal treatment analysis was not selected because treatment intent and complete exposure are missing. A future external or adjudicated study should revisit these branches with cystatin C/measured GFR, complete fluid/RRT capture and explicit follow-up after ICU discharge.
