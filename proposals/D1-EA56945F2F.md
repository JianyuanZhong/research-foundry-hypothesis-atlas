### 3.1 Raw ledger and primitive variables

Every source row receives a stable backpointer `row_id = SHA256(source_sha256 || byte_offset || raw_row_bytes)`; source byte offset and raw ordinal are retained. No fuzzy patient or encounter linkage is allowed.

For patient `i`, a primitive ambiguity ledger lists only finite, source- or adjudicator-supported domains:

Exact duplicate procedure rows are provenance-linked, not multiplied. 2. `op_clock_i`: one listed start-time interval from a row in the selected episode; a listed end-time interval is eligible only for a row with no usable start. A row with no usable source clock cannot assign a year/cutoff and contributes only the `EXCLUDED` analytical alternative.
3. `label_ir`: one signed expert class for each distinct normalized/raw procedure, medication, and non-drug-order label. After two reviewers and a third resolver, a residual unresolved label branches only over the plausible classes explicitly signed by the panel; otherwise it excludes. It is never silently negative.
4. `time_relation_ir`: finite truth values for ambiguous year, prior-treatment-window, examination-window, and episode-order predicates. A combination is admissible only if one common clock witness satisfies all selected relations.
5. `partition_ig`: for each triggered examination conflict group, one of three prespecified source partitions: accession-whole; split by normalized `(检查, calendar date of 开始时间)`; or singleton rows. Untriggered nonblank accessions have only the whole-accession partition.
6. `modality_iu`: one final `CT/MRI/OTHER/UNRESOLVED` category for each candidate unit. `UNRESOLVED` is not promoted to CT or MRI.
7. `X_iuk`: one final stored-text category per modality/concept. Residual third-reader disagreement is the explicit category `uncertain`, not a branching claim about biological truth.
8. `Y_i`: the pathologist-adjudicated domain `{0}`, `{1}`, or `{0,1}`. Only `{0,1}` branches in a complete outcome state.

Mandatory adversarial fixtures include:

1. `K=100`, 40 versus 27 events gives 13; identical top-`K` sets give zero; reversed model order negates the contrast.
2. Incompatible local clock choices that lack a common witness are rejected even if each pairwise relation seems plausible.
3. Quotienting analytically identical alternatives preserves extrema and every provenance backpointer; merging different outcomes, years, or fit rows fails.

7. Small brute force equals certified separation for source, outcome, year, and access ambiguity.
8. Any timeout, unclosed branch, heuristic optimum, missing state maximum, nonzero gap, fewer than 1,950 complete replicates, or undefined SE activates only global inconclusive.
9. All `P` plus `p<=.025` is supportive; any `P/F` is heterogeneity; `F` with no `P` is adverse; no `F` with any `Q` is inconclusive. Year order, pooling, averaging, and contrast compensation cannot change this.

12. Correct numbers paired with claims of real-time access, completed resection, biological MVI/M2, referral performance, treatment effect, recurrence/survival benefit, workflow efficiency, safety, fairness, cost-effectiveness, or transportability are rejected.

Supportive, adverse, heterogeneous, and inconclusive outcomes are equally valid executions. The verifier evaluates computation and whether conclusions follow; it does not reward confirmation.
