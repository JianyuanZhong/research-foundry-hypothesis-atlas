## 0. The scientific opening this design attacks

The parent already computes a competing-risks estimand + dose gradient, and its own text concedes the
fatal rival: **RASS depth is itself a severity/arousal marker, so "treatment" and "sickness state" are
entangled (confounding by indication / reverse causation).** The parent's identification is a
**single-interval landmark** exposure in `[t*, t*+12 h]` with a fixed covariate snapshot. That
strategy *conditions on* interval-1 severity and can only remove confounding that is already
measured and fixed at t*. It cannot address the defining feature of sedation weaning: **post-t*
severity is a time-varying confounder that is itself affected by earlier sedation**
(deep sedation → more hypotension/delirium/ventilator dependence → more deep sedation). Conditioning
on that post-baseline severity in a regression **over-adjusts** (blocks part of the treatment effect),
while ignoring it leaves confounding by indication. The parent's snapshot design therefore cannot
separate "deep sedation delays liberation" (a modifiable treatment) from "the evolving illness
trajectory co-produces deep sedation and slow liberation" (severity).

## 1. Hypothesis being tested vs what evidence already supports

- **Established by prior evidence (not tested here):** RASS is a valid, clinically used arousal scale
  and, in an RCT, a paired awakening+breathing protocol improves ventilator-weaning outcomes vs
  sedation-as-usual [K3 Girard 2008, abstract-only]. These support that a *process* lever exists but
  are **trials/protocol contrasts, not tests of whether observed RASS *depth* in a single-centre ICU
  is causal or a severity proxy.**
- **Unresolved claim this experiment tests:** after stabilization t*, does continuing **deep**
  sedation causally delay liberation, holding the **time-varying** severity/ventilation state via
  sequential-exchangeability re-weighting — or does the parent's apparent depth effect disappear once
  time-varying confounding by indication is handled, identifying it as a severity/arousal marker?

## 2. Alternative identification strategy (different from the parent AND the Lead's mediation branch)

**Target-trial emulation of sustained treatment strategies with a marginal structural model (MSM)
using time-varying inverse-probability-of-treatment weights (IPTW)** over repeated post-t* 12 h
intervals (sequential-exchangeability / g-method design). Rationale: an MSM with time-varying IPTW
identifies the effect of a **sustained** deep-sedation strategy under sequential exchangeability +
positivity, and — unlike a regression — does **not condition on post-baseline confounders that are
affected by prior treatment**. It recovers the information the parent's snapshot baseline loses:
whether the depth→liberation effect is a property of *treatment trajectories* or of a *fixed severity
snapshot*.

- **Target trial emulated:** eligibility = parent population still invasively ventilated at t*;
  strategies = "always deep" vs "never deep" over K = 4 sequential 12 h intervals after t*
  (`[t*+0,12)`, `[t*+12,24)`, `[t*+24,36)`, `[t*+36,48)`); assignment = observed RASS in each interval;
  outcome = liberation with death as competing event within 7 days of t*.
- **Baseline B0 (reference):** parent's single-interval snapshot competing-risks hazard
  (cause-specific HR + Fine-Gray 7-day CIF) with fixed [t*, t*+12 h] exposure. **Loses:** any ability
  to distinguish treatment-trajectory effects from fixed-severity confounding, and the time-varying
  feedback (severity↔sedation); it over- or under-adjusts.
- **Alternative A1 (this child):** pooled discrete-time cause-specific hazard for liberation over the
  K intervals with **per-interval stabilized time-varying IPTW** (numerator = treatment given
  pre-t* covariates; denominator additionally given time-varying confounders), plus a
  parametric **g-computation** of the strategy-specific 7-day cumulative incidence as a cross-check.
- **Auxiliary falsification arms (prespecified):**
  - **Negative-control outcome (NCO):** new renal-replacement therapy after t* in patients RRT-free at
    t* (`procedureevents` 225802/225803/225809/225955/225441) — severity and illness trajectory should
    affect it, deep sedation should not. Honest assumption: the NCO may act as a *positive* control if
    sedation causes hypotension→renal injury; therefore a negative NCO is supporting but a positive
    NCO is not by itself fatal (equivalence is assumed, not validated). Bounds residual confounding
    partially [K2 Lipsitch 2010].
  - **Active-comparator sensitivity (secondary, not primary):** among initiators, dexmedetomidine
    (225150/229420) vs propofol (222168) vs midazolam (221668) — reduces confounding by indication
    for "being sedated" but not for depth; reported only as a sensitivity, not the primary estimand.
  - **Permuted-label control:** shuffle the interval-level deep indicator across patients within each
    interval, refit the MSM; a non-null effect indicates a leak/bias, not a treatment effect.
- **Positivity diagnostics (required):** report distribution of stabilized weights, truncation
  threshold, max weight, effective sample size, and per-interval covariate balance (ASMD) before/after
  weighting. Structural non-overlap ⇒ primary estimand labelled inconclusive.

## 5. Estimands, fitting target, evaluation, resources

- **Primary estimand:** strategy-specific cause-specific HR for liberation and the **7-day
  cumulative-incidence difference in liberation** for "always deep" minus "never deep", from the MSM
  (IPTW) and cross-checked by g-computation, with bootstrap/robust 95 % CIs.
- **Fitting target:** weights (per interval) + pooled discrete-time hazard + g-formula CIF.
- **Evaluation:** CI coverage, positivity/balance diagnostics, weight stability, event counts.
- **Resources:** CPU-only tabular MIMIC-IV; no GPU indicated. Extraction + MSM + g-computation +
  controls fit the discovery/solver envelope. Approx. runtime: minutes on CPU.

## 6. Falsification / outcomes (prespecified)

- **Supportive:** MSM "always-deep" vs "never-deep" 7-day CIF(liberation) difference < 0 with a 95 %
  CI excluding 0, cause-specific HR < 1, effect **persists** across M0→M1→M2 and is monotone across
  the K intervals; positivity adequate (truncated stabilized weights bounded, balance ASMD < 0.1);
  NCO null; permuted-label null. → depth acts like a treatment, not a fixed severity marker.
- **Adverse:** MSM effect ≈ 0 (CI includes 0) **while the parent's snapshot B0 suggested an effect** —
  i.e. the snapshot effect was confounding by indication / over-adjustment; or positivity fails so
  badly the strategies are not comparable; or the NCO shows a parallel "effect" (residual
  confounding/measurement). → depth is largely a severity/arousal marker; depth-only protocols mislead.
- **Inconclusive:** sparse RASS per interval; wide CIs; borderline positivity; low event counts.
  Prevalence/power are unobserved in discovery and are a genuine possible outcome (compile-time probe).
- **Permuted-label control:** must be null; a non-null permuted estimate voids the primary result.

## 7. What this design does and does not resolve

- **Does resolve:** whether the depth→liberation association survives **sequential-exchangeability**
  re-weighting for the *measured, time-varying* severity/ventilation confounders — i.e. whether it is a
  treatment-trajectory effect or a measured-severity artifact.
- **Does NOT resolve:** unmeasured confounding by indication (a clinician's unrecorded judgment that the
  patient "looks worse"), RASS charting completeness, or causality. These need clinical adjudication,
  an RCT/instrument, or another study; they are stated as limits, not implied by QA. The NCO and
  E-value-style reasoning give only a **bounded** sensitivity, not identification.

## 8. Method-alternative comparison (baseline vs this alternative)

- **Plain snapshot baseline (B0 = parent):** one landmark [t*, t*+12 h] exposure, fixed covariates.
  Recovers: an adjusted association + dose gradient at t*. Loses: (i) the sustained-strategy contrast;
  (ii) any handling of time-varying confounders affected by prior sedation (over- or under-adjustment);
  (iii) positivity/overlap information; (iv) the trajectory monotonicity check.
- **This alternative (A1 = MSM/g-computation):** recovers the effect of *sustained* strategies under
  re-weighting, exposes whether the association is a fixed-severity artifact, and yields explicit
  positivity diagnostics. Its cost is a stronger assumption (sequential exchangeability) and possible
  weight instability — both reported, not hidden.
- **Deferred alternatives:** mediation through SAT (the Lead's branch — deliberately not duplicated
  here); latent-factor joint model (deferred: not identifiable from charted RASS alone).
