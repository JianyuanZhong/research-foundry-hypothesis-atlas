# Adjuvant post-resection TACE in HCC: adding a pre-specified dose-response + quantitative-bias internal-validity test for the MVI-stratified effect

Island: **hcc** (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records; snapshot `[source checksum]`).
Evolution of parent `[prior hypothesis]` (valid, board leader; operation = evolve, episode 31).
The parent design is inherited **VERBATIM** (Sections 1–3, 6, 8 below restate it unchanged). Exactly **ONE**
decision-changing element is added (Section 4A). No other element is modified.

---

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance (unchanged, inherited verbatim)

After curative-intent hepatectomy for HCC, post-operative adjuvant TACE (PA-TACE) is used in Chinese practice for
"high-risk" patients (microvascular invasion, MVI; portal-vein tumour thrombus), on the rationale of eradicating
microscopic residual disease. Yet the newest adjuvant-therapy evidence is negative for early-stage HCC as a whole
([K2] KEYNOTE-937: active surveillance remains standard of care; [K3]: adjuvant approaches show limited/inconsistent
phase-III benefit and unresolved patient selection). Whether PA-TACE has a **causal** benefit and whether that benefit
is **concentrated in MVI+ patients** is unresolved and decides a real choice: whether to give adjuvant TACE to an
MVI+ resected patient. [K1] shows PA-TACE responders are identifiable and that AFP + microvascular infiltration enter
the prognostic model, i.e., MVI is a plausible effect modifier worth a proper causal test.

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival (unchanged, inherited verbatim)

- **H_effect (leading):** PA-TACE reduces early recurrence in resected HCC, concentrated in MVI+ patients (residual
  microscopic disease) and ~null in MVI-negative patients.
- **R_conf (strongest rival):** any crude association is **confounding by indication + immortal-time / lead-time
  bias** — higher-risk, fitter patients are both more likely to receive PA-TACE and to survive to receive it, and
  are monitored more intensively (detection bias). Under R_conf the MVI-stratified "benefit" is a selection artefact.
- **R_null:** no causal effect; association is entirely explained by measured confounding + monitoring intensity.

## 3. Design (frozen, inherited verbatim)

- **Population:** patients with an HCC diagnosis (diagnoses table) AND >=1 **curative-intent hepatectomy** procedure
  (procedures `手术` label family: 肝叶切除 / 肝叶部分切除 / 腹腔镜下肝部分切除 / 特殊肝段切除 / 半肝 / 段切除).
- **Index** = date (`开始时间`) of the earliest qualifying resection. **Landmark** t0 = index + 90 d.
- **Exposure:** **adjuvant TACE** = a TACE/embolization procedure (`手术` matching 栓塞|TACE|化疗栓塞|介入) whose start
  date is in `(index, index+90d]`, ascertained **only inside the landmark window** (removes immortal time);
  comparator = no TACE in `(index, index+90d]`. Late/other TACE is not the exposure.
- **Effect modifier:** **MVI status** from pathology (`病理` / `检查所见` / `检查诊断` containing MVI|脉管癌栓|微血管|血管侵犯).
- **Outcome:** early recurrence (<=24 months post-landmark) via the branch's lexical imaging endpoint on examinations
  (`检查所见`/`检查诊断` containing 复发|新发|进展|转移|增多), with death as a competing event; censor at last contact
  (`出院时间`/last record). Endpoint requires radiologist mRECIST adjudication (stated limitation).
- **Baseline model (M0):** single-cohort Cox/logistic recurrence ~ adjuvantTACE + MVI + adjuvantTACE x MVI + era/
  age/ALBI/AFP covariates. Reports crude and adjusted HRs and the interaction.
- **Alternative model (M1, target-trial emulation):** same landmark, plus **propensity/overlap weighting** for PA-TACE
  indication (age, ALBI, AFP, tumour burden, resection type, era), a **negative-control outcome** (a TACE-implausible
  event), and imaging-cadence (monitoring-intensity) adjustment / IOO weighting.
- **Evaluation / split:** temporal split (fit earlier era, evaluate later) + 5-fold CV; adjusted HRs with 95% CI,
  interaction LRT, C-index/AUROC with calibration, decision-curve net-benefit vs "treat all/none".

## 4. THE ONE ADDED ELEMENT (this episode): dose-response + quantitative-bias internal-validity test

**Rationale / what it is meant to separate.** A binary "any adjuvant TACE vs none" contrast cannot, by itself,
separate a *genuine MVI-concentrated causal benefit* from *confounding by indication*: clinicians plausibly give
adjuvant TACE preferentially to higher-risk patients (including MVI+), which alone can produce a crude protective-
looking association through differential surveillance and outcome ascertainment. The parent already adjusts and
weights for indication, but with a single binary exposure. This element adds an **independent internal-validity
prediction test** with a different failure mode: a true causal effect (eradication of residual microscopic disease)
should scale with *how much* adjuvant TACE is delivered, whereas confounding-by-indication need not produce a
monotone protective gradient and can produce a **flat or reversed** one (sicker patients receive *more* sessions but
recur more). The element also attaches a **quantitative bias bound** (E-value) and reuses the parent's **negative-
control outcome** as an internal falsification check. Nothing else in the frozen design changes.

### 4A(i) Pre-specified dose-response sub-analysis
- **Dose variable:** among **exposed** patients only (adjuvant TACE in `(index, index+90d]`), count the number of
  TACE sessions from the **procedures** table: rows whose `手术` matches `栓塞|TACE|化疗栓塞|介入` and whose
  `开始时间` ∈ `(index, index+90d]`. `n_tace` = non-negative integer session count. Model both an **ordinal**
  (`1`, `2`, `>=3`) and a **continuous per-session** form; the continuous form is the primary trend estimator.
- **Estimand:** the **MVI-stratified per-session hazard ratio** for early recurrence, `HR_dose = exp(beta_dose)`, in
  a Cox model `recurrence ~ n_tace (+ covariates)` **within each MVI stratum**, plus the `n_tace x MVI` interaction.
  The causal prediction is **monotone benefit with more cycles** (`HR_dose < 1`), strongest in MVI+.
- **Comparison/contrast:** the parent M1 binary contrast yields only a single exposed-vs-unexposed HR; it cannot
  detect whether the benefit is dose-graded. This sub-analysis recovers the **monotonicity prediction** that is
  unique to the causal explanation (H_effect) and is not implied by R_conf or R_null.

### 4B(ii) Quantitative bias analysis (E-value)
- For each of the (a) MVI+ stratum HR and (b) the estimated **effect-modification ratio** (MVI+ HR ÷ MVI- HR,
  equivalently the interaction HR), compute the **E-value** (VanderWeele & Ding): for a protective estimate use
  `RR* = 1/HR`, `E = RR* + sqrt(RR*(RR*-1))`; apply the same transform to the 95% CI limit nearest the null.
- **Pre-specified bias factor:** we require the E-value (point estimate) to exceed the association an unmeasured
  confounding-by-indication confounder could plausibly have with *both* adjuvant-TACE delivery and recurrence.
  We pre-specify a **robustness threshold of E >= 2.0 for the MVI+ HR** and **E >= 1.5 for the interaction**; below
  these the finding is judged fragile to plausible residual confounding.

### 4C(iii) Negative-control reuse (unchanged variable)
- Reuse the parent's **negative-control outcome** (a TACE-implausible event) exactly as specified in M1, as an
  internal falsification check: a "protective" signal there indicates surveillance/selection artefact rather than
  a TACE effect.

## 5. Pre-specified falsification criteria (conjunctive; dose-response + E-value + negative control)

The decision the study informs: **whether to give adjuvant TACE to an MVI+ resected patient.** Branches are fixed
before any outcome or HR is computed (none is computed in discovery).

- **Supportive (H_effect strengthened):** (i) within MVI+, per-session `HR_dose < 1` with 95% CI excluding 1 and a
  significant monotone trend (ordinal trend p < 0.05); AND (ii) the `n_tace x MVI` interaction is < 1 with p < 0.05
  (benefit concentrates in MVI+); AND (iii) E-value of the MVI+ HR **>= 2.0** and of the interaction **>= 1.5**;
  AND (iv) the negative-control outcome shows **no** protective signal.
- **Adverse (R_conf / confounding-by-indication — the decisive rival):** ANY of — (i) the MVI+ dose gradient is
  **flat or reversed** (per-session `HR_dose >= 1`, or a monotone trend in the *harmful* direction: more sessions ->
  higher recurrence hazard); OR (ii) the MVI+ dose effect **disappears (moves >= 50% toward null) under overlap
  weighting**; OR (iii) E-value of the MVI+ HR **< 1.5** (finding fragile to plausible confounding); OR (iv) the
  negative-control outcome **reproduces a spurious protective signal**. Any one of these rejects the MVI-
  concentrated causal-benefit claim for this dataset.
- **Inconclusive:** point estimates consistent with monotone benefit but 95% CI includes 1 in MVI+, or the MVI+
  dose sub-stratum is underpowered (too few `>=3`-session MVI+ patients to estimate the ordinal contrast); or
  E-value in the ambiguous band 1.5–2.0 for the MVI+ HR.
- **Pre-specified precise-null (informative negative):** if the per-session 95% CI lies **within [0.95, 1.05]** and
  the interaction CI within [0.95, 1.05], this is a *precise null on the dose-response scale* — it argues the
  binary "benefit", if any, is **not a dose-graded causal effect**, i.e. disfavours H_effect and is consistent with
  R_conf/R_null. This branch is only claimed when achieved with adequate precision; otherwise report Inconclusive.

Interpretation rule: only the **Supportive** conjunction advances the causal claim; any **Adverse** condition
retracts it for this dataset. The negative-control and E-value are internal falsification aids, not proof of
causality; they cannot by themselves establish H_effect.

## 8. Baseline-vs-alternative method comparison (what the addition recovers that the parent loses)

- **M0 (parent single-cohort Cox + interaction):** one binary contrast, naive adjustment for the same confounders
  that drive the indication — cannot distinguish effect from indication, and gives no internal validity check.
- **M1 (parent target-trial emulation):** weighting + negative control + monitoring adjustment removes some
  confounding but still reads a **single binary exposure**, so a null/positive M1 HR cannot distinguish "PA-TACE
  works only at sufficient dose" from "selection into any PA-TACE" or from an artefact.
- **Added element (this episode):** a **within-exposed dose-response gradient** (a different identifying
  prediction, not a different covariate set) + the **E-value** (converts "residual confounding may remain" into a
  falsifiable robustness bound) + the reused negative control. **Information the parent loses that this recovers:**
  *whether the MVI-concentrated effect is dose-graded* (causal-eradication prediction) *and how strong unmeasured
  confounding would have to be to overturn it*. Chosen because it is the smallest check whose result would change
  the decision ("give adjuvant TACE to an MVI+ resected patient?") rather than another covariate addition.
- **Scientific alternative not chosen (recorded):** a per-session *drug/embolic dose* model or a marginal-structural
  model with a continuous exposure would be a stronger dose model, but the dataset records sessions/labels, not
  delivered chemotherapy dose per session (only `手术` labels; the medication table is orders/administrations, not
  TACE-cycle dose), so this is deferred as **missing evidence** rather than replaced by an invented proxy.

## 9. Honest limits

- Dose is itself confounded: number of sessions reflects tolerability, liver function, response-adaptive selection,
  and re-treatment for interval progression — plausibly *positively* associated with risk (sicker get more), so a
  flat/reversed gradient is genuinely ambiguous between "no benefit" and "indication reversal". Only the
  **conjunction** with E-value and negative control is informative; no single branch is proof.
- TACE sessions counted from `手术` include any-indication TACE; only the `(index, index+90d]` window is the
  exposure, and a resection index is required to isolate it.
- E-value assumes the unmeasured confounder is not interacting with MVI and uses HR≈RR approximation; the E-value
  for the interaction ratio is approximate. E-values bound confounding **strength needed**, not whether such a
  confounder exists.
- Lexical recurrence endpoint is unvalidated; requires radiologist **mRECIST adjudication** (clinical, not
  computable here). MVI string extraction is imperfect. Recurrence ascertainment depends on imaging follow-up
  (monitoring intensity addressed but not eliminated). Single institution; no lab units recorded.
- **No outcome model, HR, interaction, E-value, or net-benefit is computed in discovery.** Those are solver
  outputs. Not a survival claim; no cross-dataset join.
