## Falsification and interpretation

A result is materially reversed if a mandatory branch changes direction, either airway bound moves by at least 0.05, or midpoint signs reverse with both absolute midpoint magnitudes at least 0.02. Failed computation, source/header/hash check, item/unit/status rule, ambiguity control, recording-time audit, timestamp diagnostic, positivity/calibration diagnostic, observation bound, renal ascertainment, bootstrap/Holm accounting or required recent-window branch is validity-inconclusive.

- Supportive-recorded: the parent’s airway and renal/terminal criteria pass in the retrospective primary; the recent-window branches and, if feasible, the recording-time restricted branch do not materially reverse; all diagnostics pass. This supports external validation or randomization of the question only. It remains retrospective and cannot be called actionable or causal.
- Adverse-FWER: a prespecified adjusted adverse family is rejected with validity passing. This is an observational recorded-proxy concern, not toxicity or renal safety.
- Margin-falsified: the prespecified recorded-proxy airway-improvement margin is not supported while no adjusted harm conclusion is established. This rejects the recorded association target, not a clinical treatment effect.
- Statistical-inconclusive: a feasible, valid branch has neither prespecified support nor adverse/margin-falsification.
- Validity-inconclusive/infeasible: a required branch or diagnostic fails or the recording-time restricted branch is below minimum. It is not evidence for or against loop treatment.
- Measurement-limit: sparse/coherent-lag failure or discordant database recording is reported as a limitation. It is not adverse evidence.
