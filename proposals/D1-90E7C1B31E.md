# Episode 9: test whether the concurrent pH-conditioned hyperoxemia contrast survives within-stay control

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

At SpO2 96–97%, acidemia may be associated with a higher concurrent PaO2 because the oxygen–hemoglobin dissociation relation is shifted rightward. The clinically consequential unresolved question is narrower than causality: does the parent’s clinically meaningful +10 percentage-point acidemic-versus-normal contrast persist when the comparison is made within the same ICU stay, reducing stable patient/stay case mix, rather than only between different patients?

**Episode-9 hypothesis (H9).** In the parent’s exact concurrent EICU cohort, the acidemic-minus-normal standardized risk difference for observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg is at least +10 percentage points in the pooled observation-weighted analysis and remains at least +10 percentage points in an equal-stay-weighted, within-stay fixed-effect analysis restricted to stays that contain both exposure groups. The robustness claim additionally requires a positive within-stay contrast in at least four of the six parent hospitals with exposure-balanced within-stay transitions.

This is a concurrent measurement association. It does not test what changing pH would do, whether oxygen treatment caused the PaO2, whether a device is valid, whether hyperoxia causes injury, whether lowering FiO2 helps, or whether a prior pH can trigger an alert.

The strongest inspected evidence supports only the premise, not H9. Delgado et al., *Antioxidants* 2026;15:235 (DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712), was read from its frozen Europe PMC full-text XML source. It reports one tertiary-center retrospective study with 21,406 adults and 717,064 paired measurements, finding higher PaO2 in acidotic states at comparable saturation and strong modification by FiO2. That supports a clinically plausible pH-conditioned PaO2–saturation relation. It does not establish this EICU +10 threshold, multi-hospital consistency, resistance to stable case mix, or clinical utility. The EICU parent’s full-file audit separately establishes that the concurrent cohort is computable and that a genuinely available 5–60-minute prior-pH alert is not.

The advance is therefore a falsifiable robustness test: pooled support with a materially smaller or reversed within-stay contrast would identify residual case mix or time-varying selection as a serious explanation; concordant within-stay and pooled results would make the concurrent measurement relation more credible without making it causal.

## Feasibility gates and the selected design

The complete-source audit found 54 exposure-discordant stays among 544 primary stays, 57 adjacent transitions, and only five eligible persons with more than one eligible stay. It therefore supports a within-stay design but not a cross-stay person-level self-controlled design.

A tight matched branch was prespecified before interpretation as: same stay, same six-hour block `floor(draw/360)`, same FiO2 band (21–39, 40–59, 60–100), and absolute FiO2 difference <=5 percentage points. It yields only four candidate pairs in three hospitals and zero discordant outcomes. It is a feasibility result, not an adverse biological result; it is not loosened to manufacture sample size.

The H9 robustness gate is met for computation if there are at least 50 exposure-discordant stays and at least four parent hospitals with at least two such stays. The audit gives 54 discordant stays and six parent hospitals meeting that hospital-level transition gate (122=2, 183=5, 184=2, 420=3, 443=12, 458=21). Hospitals 318 and 394 remain in the original concurrent analysis but have no within-stay exposure transition and are not silently assigned a within-stay estimate.

## Estimands and analysis

The episode-8 pooled estimand and model remain the primary population analysis:

- observation-weighted marginal standardized risk difference across all 705 rows;
- B0: intercept + centered median SpO2;
- B1: B0 + FiO2 per 10 points, age per decade, sex, unit type, unit admission source, six-hour block, PaCO2 per 10 and PaCO2-missing indicator;
- B2: B1 + acidemia and acidemia × linear FiO2;
- population-average logistic GEE with exchangeable correlation within `uniquepid`;
- 1,000 hospital-cluster bootstrap resamples, copying all rows in sampled hospitals and assigning copied person/hospital clusters new IDs.

The new H9 estimand is a distinct robustness estimand, not a mechanical replacement of the frozen parent design. Let \\(S\\) be the 54 stays containing at least one acidemic and one normal eligible row. Fit an unconditional stay-fixed-intercept logistic model:

`logit P(Y_it=1) = alpha_i + beta*A_it + gamma1*(FiO2_it/10) + gamma2*SpO2_it + gamma3*(A_it*FiO2_it/10) + block_it + PaCO2_it/10 + missingPaCO2_it`.

Use all rows from those 54 stays. Stable age, sex, unit type, admission source, hospital, and all other time-invariant stay/person traits are absorbed by `alpha_i` and are not separately estimated. Do not include any post-draw information. Report convergence, separation, sparse cells, and the number of stays contributing information.

Compute the equal-stay standardized within-stay risk difference:

`RD_W = (1/|S|) sum_i (1/n_i) sum_t [p_hat_it(A=1) - p_hat_it(A=0)]`,

where the observed FiO2, SpO2, time block, PaCO2 value/missingness, and stay intercept are held at their observed values. This is a descriptive within-stay standardization, not an intervention contrast. Report the corresponding row-weighted RD as a sensitivity, the fixed-effect odds ratio, and the unadjusted equal-stay mean of within-stay event-rate differences.

For uncertainty, resample complete stays within each of the six transition-capable parent hospitals with replacement 1,000 times, retaining all rows in selected stays and assigning copied stay IDs. Also report hospital leave-one-transition-site-out estimates and a six-site cluster bootstrap interval as a separate site-uncertainty display. A failed fit in >10% of bootstrap replicates, complete separation, absent overlap, or a non-estimable fixed-effect contrast is inconclusive, not support.

Pre-specified repeated-observation and measurement sensitivities are: first eligible observation per stay; equal-stay weighting; first draw per stay-specific six-hour block; last rather than median SpO2; 60-minute FiO2 lookback; chart-offset FiO2 without entry restriction; alternate FiO2 labels separately; complete PaCO2; fixed FiO2 bands; closed nonzero ventilation intervals; broader airway proxy; deletion of each adequate hospital; and linkage inverse-probability weighting based only on pre-outcome patient/hospital descriptors, draw block, and chart availability. These do not create a prior-pH alert and cannot use PaO2 or post-draw data for retention modeling.

The adjacent-transition table is descriptive: list transition direction, gap, FiO2 change, SpO2 change, PaCO2 availability, and paired outcome. It cannot be treated as a causal before/after analysis because disease trajectory and care can change between gases.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive for the full H9 robustness claim** requires all of the following:

1. The frozen parent B2 RD has a two-sided 95% interval whose lower bound is >=+10 points.
2. The equal-stay within-stay `RD_W` has a lower 95% bound >=+10 points.
3. The within-stay point estimate is positive in at least four of the six transition-capable parent hospitals.
4. No adequate-hospital deletion reverses the pooled direction; the within-stay site prediction interval does not include a materially opposite contrast; and the main measurement/repeated-observation sensitivities do not reverse direction.
5. The model and overlap feasibility gates pass.

The strongest allowed conclusion is that the clinically material concurrent acidemia-associated PaO2>120 contrast is reproducible in the audited multi-hospital cohort and persists in comparisons within the same ICU stay, making stable case mix a less adequate explanation and motivating prospective synchronized validation. It remains an association compatible with, but not proof of, a pH-conditioned measurement relation.

Interpret results as a matrix rather than forcing one verdict:

- **Pooled supportive, within-stay supportive:** robust concurrent association; still no causal or alert claim.
- **Pooled supportive, within-stay adverse (upper bound <+10):** the broad contrast is not robust to stay control; stable case mix or time-varying selection is a serious alternative. A smaller positive within-stay estimate may still be reported.
- **Pooled adverse (upper bound <+10), within-stay supportive:** evidence is limited to the transition-enriched subset and does not support the broad multi-hospital claim.
- **Any interval including +10:** inconclusive for the +10 threshold, even if the point estimate is positive.
- **Upper bound <0:** adverse to the direction in that estimand, not proof that pH has no physiologic relevance.
- **Pooled support with fewer than six positive parent sites:** pooled concurrent support only; transportability is adverse or inconclusive.
- **Fit failure >10%, poor overlap, direction reversal under entry timing/ventilation/repeated-observation sensitivity, or a site interval spanning both <=-5 and >=+10:** inconclusive.
- **Four tight matched pairs:** feasibility/QC only; they cannot support, refute, or rescue H9.

A negative or inconclusive within-stay result does not prove the Bohr effect absent: same-stay pH changes may be too sparse, pH may be measured at non-comparable physiologic moments, and the EICU fields do not establish specimen identity or synchronization. Conversely, a positive within-stay association does not establish that changing pH causes PaO2 changes.

The 5–60-minute prior-pH alert remains infeasible and excluded. The parent availability audit found only 28 target-band rows with an available prior pH in that window (17 acidemic, 7 normal, 4 alkalemic), from 23 stays and six hospitals, with no hospital meeting five acidemic and ten normal observations. Episode-9 concurrent or within-stay support cannot validate that alert.

## Clinical adjudication and unavailable evidence

EICU cannot establish shared arterial specimen identity, true arterial provenance, actual airway persistence, delivered FiO2, monitor make/calibration/artifact, waveform synchronization, or whether a value was available to a clinician before the draw. Structured notes cannot restore removed narrative. It cannot establish that acidemia caused the association, that observed PaO2 represents tissue hyperoxia, that oxygen toxicity occurred, or that pH-guided repeat gases, FiO2 reduction, or any treatment improves patient outcomes.

The next evidentiary step requires expert device/clinical review and a prospective synchronized study with specimen identity, raw or quality-controlled SpO2 waveform, delivered FiO2, blood-gas sampling and result-availability timestamps, concurrent hemodynamics and acid-base context, and adjudicated ventilation. Clinical utility and treatment benefit require a separate decision-impact study or randomized intervention. None of those stronger conclusions is assigned to this retrospective verifier.
