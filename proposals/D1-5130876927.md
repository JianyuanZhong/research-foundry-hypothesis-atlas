# Episode 78 successor: does the repeat-lactate value add queue value after its observation process?

## Unresolved clinical question and advance

At the fixed 24-hour reassessment after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry, a hospital may have a repeat lactate for some patients but not others. Episode 77 evaluated a fixed-capacity, seven-day mortality-review queue without pretending that EICU contains a review intervention. Its primary contrast of R_full with R_attention is useful as a policy contrast, but it combines two signals: the fact, age, and timing of obtaining a repeat lactate, and the numeric value itself.

Those signals have different clinical implications. An observation process can be prognostic because clinicians obtain more tests for people who appear ill, while the value itself may or may not add information after that process is represented. The unresolved decision is whether the released numeric repeat lactate should change a fixed review queue after repeat availability/age and non-lactate surveillance are accounted for. This is a substantive successor: it changes the primary comparator and estimand to isolate the clinical-information claim while preserving the audited population, clocks, outcome, model family, and uncertainty procedure.

**Primary falsifiable hypothesis (H-value):** in the frozen all-baseline/L24 EICU population, a hospital-held-out queue using R_full will capture at least 3 additional in-hospital deaths occurring within seven days after L24 per 100 fixed review slots compared with the otherwise identical R_process queue. Support requires the pooled 100 times Delta_value_slot 95% lower bound to be at least +3.0 percentage points, a nonnegative lower bound in both frozen repeat-availability strata and in the equal-hospital macro estimand, the inherited deletion and 20%-capacity stability gates, and a nonnegative lower bound for eventual-discharge death capture in the same D7-fitted queues. Failure of the +3 margin is adverse-to-materiality even if the point estimate is positive. If uncertainty spans zero or a required gate fails, the result is inconclusive or not computable as specified below.

The +3-per-100 threshold and 10% capacity are operational anchors inherited from Episode 77; they are not stakeholder-elicited benefit/harm utilities. A supportive result justifies independent prospective silent validation of a value-using prioritization policy. It does not show that a repeat lactate should be ordered, that its result was visible to clinicians, that review prevents death, or that patients benefit.

## Existing evidence and claim under test

The strongest evidence available before this experiment supports only a narrower premise. Baysan et al. (2022, DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750, PMCID PMC9444407) was inspected in the frozen full-text XML source identified by the prior lineage (source ID [source checksum], SHA-256 [source checksum]). In 4,440 critically ill adults with sepsis it reported a modest improvement in recalibrated APACHE IV discrimination after adding 24-hour lactate, C-statistic 0.62 to 0.64. That supports possible incremental prognostic information in a lactate-measured cohort; it does not establish incremental value after informative repeat availability, surveillance matching, seven-day fixed-slot ranking, or multicenter hospital-held-out evaluation.

Sisk et al. (2021, DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242, PMCID PMC7810439) was also inspected in the frozen full-text source identified by the prior lineage (source ID [source checksum], [source checksum]). It motivates representing informative observation/presence rather than treating missingness as innocuous, but does not validate the EICU surveillance proxies as attention, order, or result visibility.

Thus evidence supports that lactate values and the process generating observations can be prognostic. The unresolved claim is narrower and falsifiable: after repeat-lactate availability/age and contemporaneous non-lactate surveillance are represented, does the numeric repeat-lactate value produce material, internally portable fixed-slot improvement for a prespecified near-term in-hospital-death endpoint? The three demonstration papers in references/research-ambition/README.md are ambition examples only. I inspected the available Bayesian article text for general longitudinal-EHR context; I do not use those demonstrations as evidence for this lactate claim. The cancer main article remains unavailable and is not cited as inspected.

## Endpoint and horizon

Set H7=L24+10080=t0+11520 minutes. Define D7=1 iff hospitaldischargestatus=="Expired" and hospitaldischargeoffset<=H7; otherwise D7=0.

Retain every eligible person. An Expired discharge by H7 is a D7 event. An Alive discharge by H7 is an observed alive discharge and competing state. A status-valid discharge after H7 is known not to have an in-hospital death by H7 and is a D7 non-event, regardless of later status; this person is still hospitalized/event-free at H7. Equality at H7 is included.

No post-L24 field enters predictors, no inverse-censoring weight is used, and no one is excluded for later discharge. The prior audit reported 607 D7 events and 1,695 D7 non-events, with 611 alive discharges by H7 and 1,084 still hospitalized without death; all 30 hospitals had both D7 classes. A discrepancy requires a documented source-version explanation and renewed gate review, never horizon shopping.

This is a seven-day in-hospital cumulative-death endpoint in the L24 risk set. hospitaldischargeoffset is an administrative discharge/death proxy, not an adjudicated death timestamp. Death after an alive discharge is not captured by D7.

## Models and primary estimand

Use the same four unweighted L2 logistic models as the selected parent, fitted to D7. The only inferential change is the primary contrast.

R_state includes age and age-squared; sex indicators with Other/Unknown reference; t0/1440 and square; baseline collection lag; ln(B) and square; latest valid L24 non-lactate laboratory values, ages, and missing indicators; and last/median/minimum/maximum/resolved-timestamp count/no-valid-observation summaries for heart rate, respiration, and SpO2 over [L24-360,L24].

The 16 non-lactate streams and exact units/ranges are: potassium | mmol/L | [1,10]; sodium | mmol/L | [100,180]; chloride | mmol/L | [50,150]; BUN | mg/dL | [1,300]; glucose | mg/dL | [20,1000]; creatinine | mg/dL | [0.1,20]; bicarbonate | mmol/L | [2,60]; calcium | mg/dL | [2,20]; Hgb | g/dL | [2,25]; platelets x 1000 | K/mcL | [1,2000]; WBC x 1000 | K/mcL | [0.1,500]; albumin | g/dL | [0.5,8]; total bilirubin | mg/dL | [0.1,50]; paO2 | mm Hg | [20,700]; paCO2 | mm Hg | [10,200]; pH | blank/missing unit | [6.5,8.0]. Apply the same greatest-revision, final-tie conflict, smallest-labid, and latest-resolved-timestamp rules, with values revised by L24.

R_attention adds six [t0+720,L24] surveillance summaries: log(1+routine marker-time pairs) for the first 13 streams; log(1+blood-gas marker-time pairs) for paO2, paCO2, pH; log(1+distinct labresultoffsets) across all 16 streams; observed-stream fraction; recency from L24 to latest qualifying row divided by 720; and no-qualifying-row indicator. These are surveillance proxies, not verified orders, clinician concern, workload, or viewing.

R_process adds F_available, repeat age (L24-F_offset)/720 when present, and missing-age indicator. R_full adds ln(F) and ln(F)^2 to R_process; missing continuous F terms are training-fold-imputed while availability remains explicit. Therefore R_full-R_process tests numeric value after the repeat observation process has already been represented. Save availability, age, and feature-origin audits.

For each hospital h with unchanged complete-cohort size n_h, set k_h=ceil(0.10*n_h). With leave-one-hospital-out predictions, rank raw linear predictors within h and break exact ties by ascending patientunitstayid. Let Q_full,h and Q_process,h be top-k queues and K=sum_h k_h.

Primary:
Delta_value_slot = [sum_h sum_{i in Q_full,h} D7_i - sum_h sum_{i in Q_process,h} D7_i] / K.

Report 100 times Delta_value_slot as additional D7 deaths per 100 fixed review slots. The primary margin is +3.0. The primary comparison cannot substitute R_attention, R_state, treat-all, treat-none, a repeat-measured-only cohort, or an outcome-trained model.

Retain R_full-R_attention as process-plus-value and R_process-R_attention as process-component decomposition; these are secondary and cannot rescue or veto H-value. A conditional F-measured R_full-R_process analysis is sensitivity only because conditioning on repeat measurement is informative.

For Dinf evaluate the same D7-fitted queues:
Delta_inf_samequeue = [sum_h sum_{i in Q_full,h} Dinf_i - sum_h sum_{i in Q_process,h} Dinf_i] / K.
This is an eventual-death no-tradeoff guard for the D7 policy, not an eventual-outcome-trained queue.

For transparency define additions A_h=Q_full,h minus Q_process,h, removals R_h=Q_process,h minus Q_full,h, M=sum_h |A_h|, and rho=M/K. For o in {7,inf}, G_o=sum_h(sum over A_h of D^o minus sum over R_h of D^o); if M>0 define Psi_o=G_o/M. Equal quotas imply Delta_o=G_o/K=rho*Psi_o and |Delta_o|<=rho. If M=0, G and Delta are zero and Psi is undefined. Report K, M, rho, G values, addition/removal event proportions, substitution hospitals, and s_h/k_h. Never report a yield without M/K or use a yield to rescue the primary.

Secondary checks are D7 contrasts at 20% capacity using new ceil(0.20*n_h) quotas; R_full-R_state; R_process-R_attention; queue overlap, exact ties, sensitivity, unflagged deaths, and switch composition; D7 death, alive-discharge, and still-hospitalized event-free composition in queues and switches; availability strata; and fixed-design hospital deletions. No secondary changes the primary label.

## Fitting, uncertainty, and falsification

For every outer hospital exclude it completely. In training hospitals only, winsorize continuous predictors at training 1st/99th percentiles, standardize using training mean and population SD, and mean-impute missing continuous values to zero after scaling; do not scale binary indicators. Fit logistic regression with intercept, L2 penalty, C=1, lbfgs, max_iter=10000, tol=1e-8, no class weights, seed 20260973, and no feature dropping. Score only the excluded hospital with training constants. Any nonfinite training value, zero post-winsorization variance, nonconvergence, or nonfinite score makes that model noncomputable.

Use raw linear predictors for ranking. Probabilities are descriptive only for AUROC, Brier/log loss and calibration plots; no probability-threshold decision curve is primary. Save fold membership, feature order, preprocessing constants, convergence, score, queue membership, and outcomes per person.

Use 2,000 copied-hospital refit bootstrap replicates, seed 20260973. Sample original hospitals with replacement; copies contribute evaluation rows and training weight, but all copies of one original hospital remain one exclusion group. Repeat preprocessing, four D7 fits, predictions, unchanged 10% quotas, primary value contrast, decomposition, eventual guard, paired identities, and 20% analysis. Save joint tuple (K,M,rho,G7,Delta_value_slot,Psi7,Ginf,Delta_inf_samequeue,Psiinf) per replicate and enforce the algebra. Use percentile 95% intervals and require at least 1,900 finite replicates for pooled primary Delta, macro primary Delta, both availability-stratum primary contrasts, and eventual guard. Report a yield interval only if at least 1,900 replicates have M>0; otherwise mark it denominator-fragile without continuity correction.

The macro estimand is Delta_value_macro=mean_h(P_full,h^7-P_process,h^7). Report pooled Delta and macro Delta separately in low- and high-F-availability strata. Support additionally requires positive primary Delta in at least 80% of finite fixed-design hospital deletions, positive in at least 70% of each availability stratum deletions, positive macro Delta in at least 70% of deletions, and inherited 20% stress gates. These are frozen before outcome inspection.

Ordered states:

5. Adverse to numeric value: computation passes but the primary interval is wholly below +3.0, or the eventual guard is materially adverse under its predeclared interval rule. This argues against retaining numeric value for this modeled policy/horizon, not against lactate testing generally.

7. Not clinically adjudicable from EICU: no conclusion about order indication, specimen validity, turnaround, visibility, clinician review, workload, preventability, causal treatment effect, safety, patient preference, utility, external transportability, or survival benefit is permitted.

For deterministic interpretation, wholly below +3 means upper bound <+3, and wholly below zero means upper bound <0. Higher-priority states control.
