# Hospital-transportable, opportunity-residualized test of the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Purpose and substantive successor

This proposal is a substantive successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It retains the Lead-frozen scientific schema exactly: the inherited 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons, 74 hospitals, first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`, landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission, the first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`, `D = cplitemoffset`, all inherited exclusions, exact literal arms, parser, B/E/T windows, thresholds, five-state precedence, all-row competing-exit estimand, and repeated-landmark clustering by `patientunitstayid`.

The unresolved claim remains deliberately narrow: after conditioning on pre-D respiratory state and the opportunity to document it, does the exact current label add reproducible information about a later independently constructed respiratory chart trajectory in hospitals held out from model fitting, rather than merely reflecting site-specific documentation opportunity, source density, repeated-stay persistence, or exit ascertainment? A secondary operational question asks whether that incremental information can prioritize rows for manual respiratory-chart review. Neither question identifies bedside SBT/extubation intent, delivered treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The advance over the parents is a transportability-first analysis contract. It makes the unit of honest generalization explicit (a complete held-out hospital), derives every state/opportunity cutpoint and overlap rule from training hospitals only, distinguishes a descriptive all-row target from supported common-overlap diagnostics without silently deleting rows, and uses a valid complete-stay label-vector permutation. It also makes sparse support and uncertainty fail closed: unsupported hospitals, cells, bootstrap replicates, or null arrangements remain unresolved and cannot be converted into a negative or positive clinical conclusion.

## Evidence boundary and clinical importance

Available evidence supports only that the two exact care-plan values are sparse structured documentation and that apparent associations can be generated by patient state, opportunity to chart, hospital workflow, entry lag, repeated landmarks, and competing exits. The release does not establish that either value is an SBT, extubation evaluation, clinician intent, readiness assessment, treatment, or outcome. The clinically important unresolved issue is whether a label can be transported as a reproducible chart-review signal after the strongest measurable documentation and state alternatives are separated. A positive result would justify external chart adjudication and prospective workflow testing; an adverse result would prevent treating the label as a portable proxy; an inconclusive result would identify missing support rather than reassure users.

The strongest result this experiment can support is incremental prediction or prioritization of named structured chart constructs in held-out eICU hospitals. It cannot support a causal treatment effect, bedside semantic validation, patient benefit/harm, quality assessment, or recommendation. Those require expert adjudication, local interface/workflow dictionaries, synchronized device logs, adjudicated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, follow-up, and external/prospective replication.

## Endpoint and unchanged all-row competing-exit estimand

* `T = (D+720,D+2160]`

## Held-out hospital design and transport estimands

Use deterministic leave-one-hospital-out folds. The held-out unit is the hospital, and all rows from each `patientunitstayid` remain together in that hospital fold; repeated landmarks never split. Do not use a hospital identifier, held-out-site intercept, or held-out-site outcome in a predictor. All fitting, imputation, scaling, feature selection, regularization, calibration, state/opportunity cutpoints, overlap rules, and threshold selection occur using training hospitals only.

For each held-out hospital `h`, report two distinct estimands:

1. **All-row transport estimand.** Apply the frozen observed M0/M1 pipeline to every held-out row and report proper multiclass K loss, Brier score, calibration, and all-row `K` probabilities/contrasts. No row is removed for poor overlap; unsupported rows are labeled in the output and remain in the all-row accounting.
2. **Common-support diagnostic estimand.** Among rows satisfying the predeclared training-derived overlap and cell-support rule, report the M1-minus-M0 incremental loss and descriptive K contrasts. This is not a replacement for the all-row estimand and cannot be generalized to excluded rows.

Use a fixed low-dimensional multinomial logistic model for the five K states, with deterministic imputation/scaling and a fixed predeclared regularization grid/tie-break. The exact grid and solver tolerances are written in `compute_manifest.json` before fitting. Fit each observed fold once, cache out-of-fold predictions and fold manifests, and derive all downstream metrics from these caches. A fold with fewer than two observed K classes remains in micro all-row scoring with proper multiclass log loss and is marked support-limited; it is excluded only from a separately reported equal-hospital macro denominator.

Report both row-weighted micro performance and equal-hospital macro performance. Equal-hospital macro is computed only over hospitals satisfying the explicitly declared test-support denominator, with the number and identities of included hospitals recorded. No site is silently dropped.

The primary residualized diagnostic requires exact held-out cells to have at least 10 rows per arm and at least 20 total rows. For a training-only propensity audit, fit a fixed logistic model of the exact label on permitted pre-D state/opportunity features and landmark in the training hospitals only. ## Valid complete-stay label-vector permutation null

The null is conditional on pre-D information and is constructed at the complete-stay level. It is not a randomization test and does not establish causality.

For each held-out hospital and fold, define each qualifying stay's complete pre-D signature as the ordered vector over every observed landmark of `(landmark, training-derived state bin or outside-support status, training-derived opportunity bin or outside-support status, row availability/status)`. Include the full five-landmark pattern, including missing or unavailable rows, and never include the exact label, K, endpoint, exit, H state, or any post-D field. A permutation block contains stays in the same hospital with identical complete signatures and identical row pattern. The whole vector of exact labels across all landmarks is permuted across stays within a block; individual landmark rows are never permuted.

This construction preserves each stay's repeated-landmark composition, hospital membership, complete signature, per-landmark label counts within each block, and within-stay label-vector dependence. Before accepting a permutation, assert that stay IDs and signatures are unchanged, every stay receives exactly one complete label vector, vector lengths match, every label is one of the two exact arms, per-landmark and per-cell arm counts within each block are unchanged, and no fold or hospital boundary is crossed. A failed invariant invalidates that arrangement and is recorded; it cannot be silently repaired.

Use deterministic seed `61061` with a documented independent stream for null generation. For each supported block set, enumerate all unique permutations if the block has at most 8 stays; otherwise draw 1,000 unique-attempt permutations with deterministic Fisher-Yates shuffles, deduplicating identical complete-vector arrangements. The observed arrangement is retained separately and is not counted as evidence against itself. Do not pool blocks, hospitals, or unsupported signatures merely to obtain permutations. If no block has two exchangeable stays, fewer than five hospitals contribute supported blocks, fewer than 20 valid global arrangements remain, or any required invariant fails, the conditional-null result is `inconclusive`.

For each valid null arrangement, retain observed fold definitions, training preprocessing maps, state/opportunity cutpoints, and fixed model hyperparameters. Refit only the M1 label coefficient on the cached fold design matrix using the same deterministic solver; M0 and Mopp remain cached. Compute the same held-out M1-minus-M0 statistic and its null envelope. The observed estimate is the fully fitted honest cross-fitted estimate; the null envelope is a conditional diagnostic. Report attempted, valid, unique, duplicate, invariant-failure, block, hospital, and cell counts. If the observed increment is typical of the conditional null, that is adverse to incremental patient-state information even if a broad hospital-by-landmark null differs. If the observed increment is outside the null, it supports only residual predictive information under this particular exchangeability model.

The inherited broad hospital-by-landmark opportunity-preserving null, site-balanced envelope, leave-one-site-out influence, first-versus-repeated-landmark persistence audit, whole-stay sequence null when exactly feasible, placebo/exposure-shift tests, component/source ablations, exit decomposition, and clinical-time versus as-entered sensitivity remain secondary falsification diagnostics. ## Interpretation and falsification criteria

A computationally supportive label-information result requires all of the following: valid source/artifact/schema/leakage checks; M1 improves both micro and equal-hospital held-out K loss over M0 in the all-row analysis and at least two prespecified endpoint/observation sensitivities; stay-cluster uncertainty is favorable; transport and overlap gates pass; the observed increment is not reproduced by the broad or complete-stay state/opportunity-preserving null; and the gain is not confined to exits, unobservability, opportunity, entry lag, persistence, one hospital, one source, or extrapolated cells. A decision-relevant result additionally requires the fixed review-task criteria.

An adverse result is evidence that the label does not robustly add information for these structured chart constructs under the specified transport and residualization tests, or that the apparent signal is explained by opportunity, site, persistence, exits, or the conditional null. An inconclusive result means inadequate support, positivity/overlap, vital opportunity, exchangeable complete stays, convergence, fold isolation, calibration, timing, or bootstrap computation. Inconclusive is not absence of information.

Even a supportive result establishes only transported incremental prediction or operational prioritization of named eICU structured constructs. It does not establish that the label means bedside evaluation, treatment, readiness, benefit, harm, quality, or causation. The configured release lacks raw waveforms, synchronized device logs, complete narrative workflow evidence, local interface dictionaries, adjudicated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, and post-ICU follow-up.
