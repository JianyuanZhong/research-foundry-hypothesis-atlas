It preserves from [prior hypothesis]: the identity-robust lower-orthant guarantee, coupled source/reader/pathology minima, the demonstrated same-coverage/different-identity counterexample, and the distinction between an exact accession census and an aggregate audit.

The substantive repair is to use one monotone five-level whole-version onset state per accession, but define the confirmatory availability contract only at 24 hours and take every 24-hour aggregate guarantee over every compatible accession identity, onset state, and source/operation world. The 12-, 48-, and 72-hour results are trajectory sensitivities from the same state set; they are monotone by construction and cannot veto or rescue the 24-hour finding.

The consequential decision is whether a scarce, fixed-capacity preoperative review service could prioritize patients whose eventual first-resection encounter documents M2 (a documentary pathology category) better than acquisition-only, report-surface, same-fit unavailable-report, and capacity-matched random review. Support is only for this finite HCC documentary allocation policy under stored-content and reader/pathology assumptions. It does not establish biological MVI prediction, report availability in practice, clinician action, workflow benefit, patient safety, recurrence, survival, or treatment benefit.

The clinical advance is a decision-relevant guarantee: it asks whether the policy remains useful when both the time at which a complete report became readable and the identity of reports covered by an aggregate archive audit are adversarially uncertain. Existing retrospective MVI models do not answer that operational question.

## 2. Current MVI context and unresolved gap

The acquired public source for “Intratumoral and Peritumoral Radiomics Based on DCE-MRI for Prediction of Microvascular Invasion Grading in Solitary HCC (<=3 cm)” (PMC12132667; DOI 10.2147/JHC.S519578) reports a retrospective resection cohort and a clinical-radiological-radiomics model with strong reported discrimination, with intratumoral plus 3-mm peritumoral DCE-MRI features among the better configurations. Relevant passages describe non-smooth margins and intratumoral arteries as associated with higher MVI grade. This motivates why preoperative information may matter, but the study uses MRI/radiomics and a selected pathologically confirmed cohort; it does not validate this report-text policy, documentary M2 endpoint, real-time availability, or clinical utility.

The public PMC/PubMed record for “Current Advances in Classification, Prediction and Management of Microvascular Invasion in Hepatocellular Carcinoma” (PMC12309289; DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746) identifies a 2025 review of MVI grading, preoperative clinical/radiomics/AI prediction, and management. The record supports the context that MVI classification and preoperative prediction remain active and consequential. It does not supply evidence for this ranking, fixed-capacity threshold, or report-availability assumptions. No unavailable full text or supplementary methods are claimed as inspected.

The unresolved question is therefore not whether MVI-related imaging correlates exist. It is whether, in a realistic first-resection documentary cohort, a locked report-semantic ranking adds robust fixed-capacity prioritization value when complete report identity and time-of-availability are uncertain. The configured data cannot answer the biological or workflow portions without external evidence.

## 5. Monotone whole-version onset states

Let cutoffs be ordered chronologically as 72, 48, 24, and 12 hours before operation. For patient-episode i, cutoff d, operation/source world omega, and whole accession u, define E_i,omega,d,u=1 iff the current operation state is valid, u is source-clean and nonblank, its CT/MRI membership is valid, and its validated acquisition interval is wholly inside [t_i,omega,d minus 90 days, t_i,omega,d). Otherwise it is zero. Acquisition after an earlier cutoff cannot be exposed at that cutoff.

Conditional on E=1, assign one onset category to the complete source version:

- O=0: final, released/ingested, and readable no later than t72;
- O=1: first eligible whole-version readability in (t72,t48];
- O=2: first eligible whole-version readability in (t48,t24];
- O=3: first eligible whole-version readability in (t24,t12];
- O=4: not eligible/readable by t12, or only later/never evidence.

The onset belongs to the whole source version, never to a phrase or component. Its visibility vector in 72/48/24/12 order is V(O)=(1{O=0},1{O<=1},1{O<=2},1{O<=3}). Therefore it is monotone: a version cannot be visible at 48 and disappear at 24. Exposure is M_i,omega,d,u=E_i,omega,d,u V_d(O_i,omega,u). A source-clean unit with missing external timing evidence remains in the onset uncertainty set; it is bridge-pending, not observed visible. Invalid crosswalk, hash mismatch, or contradictory lineage is forced unavailable.

For an audited interval, retain exactly the contiguous onset categories compatible with its interval; never use a midpoint. Without an external archive, the HCC-only set is {0,1,2,3,4} for each otherwise source-clean accession. This is interval-censored stored-content sensitivity and cannot be described as real-time availability.

## 7. Identity-robust 24-hour lower orthants without an unbounded curve census

At d=24, for each complete world omega define A_i,omega,24 as all eligible clean whole CT/MRI accessions and g_i,omega,24=|A|^{-1} sum_u M_i,omega,24,u when |A|>0, and zero when |A|=0. Zero-accession patients remain in N. Let T_omega,onset(alpha)=N_omega,24^{-1} sum_i 1{g_i,omega,24>=alpha}.

Use exact threshold-equivalence cells. The only relevant alpha thresholds are Q24={0} union {j/m: 0<=j<=m, m is an attainable positive eligible-accession count in a complete 24-hour world}; equal rational thresholds are one cell. Relevant q thresholds are R24={k/N: 0<=k<=N} for attainable N. Do not enumerate every n-dimensional attained curve or every global mask as a report.

For a registered finite contract L consisting of threshold cells (alpha,q), define Z24(L)={(omega,onset): T_omega,onset(alpha)>=q for every (alpha,q) in L}. This is an identity/source lower orthant. A one-cell contract states that at least q of eligible patients have at least alpha of their eligible 24-hour whole accessions exposed. A multi-cell contract represents an audited lower band. Only alpha>0 and q>0 contracts are nontrivial. All primary minima range over the same complete (omega,onset) in Z24(L), so an aggregate audit cannot select a favorable identity arrangement.

Compute the Pareto boundary by a monotone boundary oracle over exact integer threshold predicates: query feasibility of each alpha,q cell and query the coupled worst-case outcome extrema restricted to Z24(L). Use branch-and-bound or multi-valued decision diagrams over local accession subsets and monotone onset categories, exact rational counters, and raw primal witnesses. Cache only complete identical vectors of exact score bits, eligibility, coverage counts, endpoint terminals, and cutoff outputs. Emit only nondominated contracts and raw worst-state witnesses, not an unbounded curve census. If a finite complete enumeration is tractable it may be a cross-check, but is not required for a valid result.

The primary contract passes only when, for every z in Z24(L), separately for 2020 and 2021, d=24, rho=.10, every global reader pair, and every deletion including none, all three D_G,C>5, D_G,rand>5, and p*(L)=min_z H_G(z)/K>0. The minimum is an attained exact rational witness. Stakeholders provide an outcome-blind interval [p_L,p_U] of required documentary-M2 captures per offered position. Preference passes only if p_U<p*(L); p_L>=p*(L) is preference-adverse; overlap is preference-heterogeneous. Equality at p*(L) is not support.

The 12/48/72 trajectories use the same compatible Z24(L) states and their monotone onset-derived bits. They report exact stable or localized-adverse flags and cannot rescue or veto the 24-hour contract. This keeps one confirmatory estimand and prevents an appealing cutoff from changing it.

## 9. One-hot interpretation contract

Apply this precedence, with all non-primary cutoff/capacity flags reported separately:

1. computationally_inconclusive: any incomplete source/state/score/threshold/witness/replay proof.
2. feasibility_inconclusive: computation complete but a primary N, K, or event gate fails.
3. maximum_content_signal_adverse: all-clean-content G fails a primary year/comparator/random/deletion gate; reduced availability cannot rescue it.
4. availability_guarantee_adverse: maximum content passes but no nontrivial identity-robust 24-hour contract passes.
5. finite_corpus_support_inference_unresolved: a robust contract passes exact finite-corpus gates but simultaneous uncertainty bounds fail or assumptions are unaccepted.
6. frontier_estimable_bridge_pending: finite and inferential gates pass but no qualifying external archive/version audit and stakeholder interval are supplied.
7. aggregate_audit_inconclusive: an aggregate audit lower band enters no passing robust lower orthant; it cannot become an optimistic attained mask.
8. audited_availability_adverse: a valid exact-version audit restricts states, but every compatible cell fails.
9. preference_adverse: p_L>=p*(L).
10. preference_heterogeneous: p_L<p*(L)<=p_U.
11. externally_bridged_documentary_supportive: exact identity or simultaneous aggregate lower-band evidence identifies a robust contract and p_U<p*(L).

Supportive means only robust concentration of the historical encounter-documentary endpoint per offered position under locked models. A negative finding is valuable: maximum-content adverse falsifies the locked semantic policy; availability-guarantee adverse shows that coverage quantity is insufficient to guarantee it; preference adverse shows stakeholders require more captures than the policy can guarantee. Inconclusive means unresolved, not that the policy failed. None of these labels supports a biological MVI, treatment, recurrence, survival, workflow, safety, or patient-benefit conclusion.
