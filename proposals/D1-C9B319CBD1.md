## 1. What is already supported vs the unresolved claim (hypothesis vs conclusion)

**Supported by evidence (not re-tested here):**
- SHR is *associated* with short-term mortality in selected ICU populations (K1 Wu/Wang 2026 adjusted HR 1.37; K3 Zhang 2026 threshold association). This is a conclusion already largely settled.
- HbA1c/SHR is **selectively measured** in MIMIC-IV, and prior authors concede the resulting selection bias (K3).

**Unresolved claim this experiment tests:** at the same absolute admission glucose and severity, does the *relative* (acute-on-chronic) glycemia component carry prognostic information that (i) is **not** explained by absolute glucose, and (ii) survives when chronic glycemia is estimated **without same-stay selective HbA1c**? Distinguish descriptive/predictive from causal: this tests **incremental prognostic information / identifiability**, not mechanism.

**Clinical importance / what changes:** SHR is being promoted as a cost-free risk-stratification and trial-enrichment marker (K1, parent). If the relative component is separable without HbA1c, triage/enrichment on a relative-metabolic axis is defensible; if the apparent "independent" SHR effect is HbA1c-measurement selection, SHR must **not** be used to enrich trials. Either result changes a decision.

**Rivals (differing predictions):** H-relative (relative component informative → non-null relative effect after absolute-glucose + severity; null negative-control outcome; smooth dose-response). R-absolute (null relative component once absolute glucose/severity in model). R-select/HbA1c-invalidity (signal is measurement selection → attenuates under propensity weighting; non-null negative-control outcome; unstable across anemia/transfusion/renal strata).

## 2. Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcomes (frozen design)

- **Population:** adults ≥18, **first ICU stay per subject** (earliest `icustays.intime`), with a measurable admission glucose AND, for the primary exposure, an **HbA1c-independent chronic anchor** available: either (a1) a **prior-admission** glucose/HbA1c from an earlier `hadm_id` (before index `intime`, within a prespecified 365-day lookback), or (a2) an outpatient `hosp/omr` result (`result_name` matching glucose/HbA1c-style rows) before index. Exclusions: no anchor; admission glucose outside valid range; HbA1c-based SHR incalculable for sensitivity arm.
- **Index time `t0` = ICU `intime`.** **Admission glucose** = first `labevents` glucose (itemid **50931** Chemistry; sensitivity **50809**, **52027**, **52569**) in `[t0-6h, t0+24h]`. **Same-stay HbA1c** = itemid **50852** ("% Hemoglobin A1c"; sensitivity **50854** "Absolute A1c") in `[t0-24h, t0+24h]` — used **only** for the negative-control exposure and sensitivity arm, **not** for the primary exposure.
- **Primary exposure (HbA1c-independent):** relative glycemia `RG = log(admission glucose mg/dL) - log(chronic-anchor glucose mg/dL)`, anchor = most recent prior-admission glucose (or OMR glucose) before `t0`; sensitivity anchor = prior-admission HbA1c converted by 28.7·HbA1c%−46.7. **Negative-control EXPOSURE:** chronic-anchor glycemia level **alone** (no acute term) — pre-registered to be null after adjustment.
- **Primary outcome:** **28-day all-cause mortality**, event from `admissions.deathtime` (28d after `intime`); interval-censored at `admissions.dischtime` when `deathtime` is null → recorded as *in-hospital/hospital-recorded* mortality. **Negative-control OUTCOME:** in-hospital non-glycemic deterioration, operationalized as a prespecified composite not plausibly driven by glycemia (e.g., new renal-replacement `procedureevents`/`inputevents` dialysis item or biopsy-proven non-glycemic event) — prespecified and bounded.
- **Keys/joins:** `subject_id`, `hadm_id`, `stay_id`. `labevents.subject_id`/`hadm_id`; `icustays.hadm_id↔stay_id` (`intime`/`outtime`); `admissions.deathtime`/`dischtime`/`admittime`; `patients.anchor_age`/`gender`/`dod`; `diagnoses_icd`+`d_icd_diagnoses` (diabetes, anemia, renal); `omr.chartdate/result_name/result_value`. **Cross-admission anchor** joins a subject's earlier `hadm_id` by `subject_id` and `charttime < t0` (never join across datasets; MIMIC-IV only).
- **Covariates:** age, gender, admission_type, sepsis flag, known-diabetes flag, baseline creatinine (itemid **52546**/50912), anemia/transfusion flags, a severity/SOFA-style score from `chartevents`/`labevents`.
- **Adjustment ladder:** M0 = absolute glucose + severity + age/sex; M1 = M0 + RG; M2 = M1 + HbA1c-measurement/care-intensity propensity; M3 = negative-control exposure and negative-control outcome.
- **Temporal boundaries / split:** locked earliest-70% / latest-30% by `icustays.intime` (MIMIC-IV date-shifting makes this an ordering proxy, not calendar time). Lookback window for the chronic anchor: 365 days pre-`t0`.

## 3. Methods — baseline B0 and substantive alternative A1 (same question)

- **B0 (interpretable, CPU):** penalized logistic regression on M0/M1 reporting adjusted ORs for absolute glucose and RG with 95% CI + restricted cubic spline for shape (K1/K3 report nonlinearity/thresholds). Fast, auditable.
- **A1 (substantive, CPU, bounded):** a **measurement-selection-aware, HbA1c-independent structural model** — a two-equation (pattern-mixture/Heckman-style) fit where (i) the HbA1c/care-intensity **measurement-propensity** selection equation and (ii) the mortality outcome equation share a latent factor, with chronic glycemia supplied by the prior-admission anchor rather than same-stay HbA1c. Fit target: bias-corrected RG effect + share of the naive SHR effect attributable to selection. Split: the locked 70/30; uncertainty via bootstrap CI. **Baseline comparison:** naive B0 SHR coefficient vs A1 selection-corrected RG coefficient and their difference. What A1 reveals that B0 loses: B0 conditions only on the measured subset, so it cannot separate a genuine relative-glycemia effect from selection-on-measurement; A1's shared latent factor is the minimal machinery that can.
- **Approx budget:** tabular, small N; both B0 and A1 fit on CPU. Estimate < 20 min CPU for the full cohort build + models; no GPU required (stated, not measured). Solver envelope per `inputs.json.solver_planning_limits`.
- **Alternatives considered/not chosen:** a full latent-variable Bayesian joint model (deferred: heavier CPU, no expected identifiability gain over A1); a neural survival model (deferred: no scientific uncertainty addressed beyond A1, GPU not justified).

## 4. Falsification criteria (prespecified)

- **Supportive (H-relative):** adjusted **RG** effect > 1 with CI excluding 1 in M1 **AND** persists (attenuation < 25%) under M2 measurement-propensity weighting **AND** negative-control exposure ≈ null **AND** negative-control outcome ≈ null **AND** smooth spline shape with stable sign across anemia/transfusion/renal strata.
- **Adverse (R-absolute / R-select / R-validity):** RG effect CI includes 1 once absolute glucose+severity are in M0 (R-absolute); OR attenuation > 50% under M2 **and** negative-control exposure non-null (R-select); effect unstable/flips across validity strata (R-validity). Any of these is an informative negative.
- **Inconclusive:** wide CIs; **bounded power gate not met** (too few events / too few anchored patients / too few prior-admission anchors to support strata) → report inconclusive; do NOT substitute an easier question.

## 5. Power gate and exact data required

- **Exact data required per row:** `subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, intime, outtime, dischtime, deathtime, anchor_age, gender, admission_type, admission glucose (+itemid), chronic-anchor glycemia (+itemid+charttime), same-stay HbA1c (+itemid), sepsis flag, diabetes flag, creatinine, anemia/transfusion flags, negative-control-outcome flag`.

## 6. Honest limits, missing evidence, clinical adjudication

- Single-centre retrospective MIMIC-IV; **association / prognostic-information only**, no causal claim.
- **Mortality is hospital-recorded** (`deathtime`); out-of-hospital death is not observable in the configured tables → 28-day mortality is partly interval-censored. National Death Index linkage is **not available**.
- **HbA1c reliability in acute illness** (anemia, transfusion, renal failure) is a **clinical/laboratory adjudication** issue; R-validity can only be bounded here, not resolved.
- The **negative-control outcome must be pre-specified and clinically defensible**; final itemid selection needs clinical adjudication (a decision, not a computation).
- **Cross-facility anchoring assumption:** only same-`subject_id` records inside MIMIC-IV (one centre system) are used; prior glycemia from outside the system is unobservable. `omr` two-decimals format must be parsed and rows with non-glucose `result_name` filtered.
- Mechanism-level claims (microcirculatory vs stress) require **another study** — not resolvable here.
