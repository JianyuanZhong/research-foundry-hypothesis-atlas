## Outcome-blind decomposition

For every selected event and each assay, freeze these fields before reading Y values.

1. `event_contact`: event encounter key is present and has valid inherited contact time; reconstruction check only.
2. `BP_eligible`: inherited B/P availability and strict clock conditions.
3. `contact_0_72h`: at least one *distinct* encounter key for the same patient with contact time strictly after event time and at or before event time +72 hours. Event encounter itself is separately labeled and cannot count as follow-up.
4. `assay_opportunity_0_72h`: at least one lab row for the assay with native `Test Time` in the Y window and valid numeric uncensored result; report whether linked to the event encounter key or another post-event encounter.
5. `Y_observed`: inherited selected Y under the exact parent rule.

Report by assay, systemic/comparator arm, calendar block, and train/future status: selected events; B available; P available; BP eligible; no post-event recorded contact; contact without qualifying assay; qualifying assay outside the selected event encounter; valid Y; and complete triplet. Give exact counts and Wilson intervals. These categories are diagnostics and may overlap when multiple contacts/labs occur; no category changes the complete-triplet estimand. Also report first-contact timing and encounter count in 0–3, 0–30, and 0–90-day windows, and native lab opportunity in 0–72 hours and 0–90 days.

The parent’s locked M0/M1 forward fit is unchanged. Add training-only logistic diagnostics for (a) any recorded post-event contact and (b) assay observation conditional on contact, using only pre-Y B, P, P lead, TACE2-to-repeat days, age, sex, and arm. ## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive measurement-process evidence requires exact reconstruction plus a reproducible era difference localized to contact versus assay-given-contact coverage. This can explain why transport is untestable but cannot rescue sparse RMSE.

Adverse evidence is similar contact and assay-given-contact coverage despite complete-Y loss, or decomposition instability under reasonable duplicate/contact-clock checks. That rejects this proposed explanation and points to other missingness mechanisms.

Inconclusive evidence includes absent native contact time, sparse arms, denominator drift, multiple uncoupled encounter keys, missing lab units, or inability to distinguish assay ordering/specimen collection from result availability. The fallback conclusion remains that only the supported block is local predictive evidence and later transport is inconclusive.

No configured HCC source supplies reliable mortality, validated progression, tumor burden, radiologic response, toxicity, external-care capture, or medication administration. A subsequent TACE, encounter, or lab is a process/utilization endpoint, not benefit. Clinically meaningful endpoint claims require linked mortality, validated imaging adjudication, expert pathology/response review, or prospective decision/outcome data. Causal and clinical-utility claims are prohibited.
