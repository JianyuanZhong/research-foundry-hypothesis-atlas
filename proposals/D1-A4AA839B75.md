# Episode 79 successor: is numeric repeat-lactate value material across a fixed quota range?

## Lead decision, unresolved question, and advance

The assigned parent is scientifically valid. It asks the right information question: after representing whether and when a repeat lactate was obtained and contemporaneous non-lactate surveillance, does the numeric repeat value itself improve a seven-day hospital-held-out ranking? Its remaining weakness is that materiality is anchored to one ceiling-rounded 10% quota, while 20% is only a permissive stress. A favorable 10% result could therefore be a cut-point artifact.

Capacity robustness is defensible and computable here only as an analytic quota-envelope question. EICU has no calendar dates, simultaneous eligible census, staffing, review logs, or measured workload. The fractions below are not realistic daily capacities and cannot establish workflow feasibility. They are frozen ranking stresses spanning half to twice the inherited 10% anchor.

Freeze C={0.05,0.10,0.15,0.20}. Four coarse points avoid pretending EICU identifies a continuous operational capacity-response curve. Ten and 20 percent are inherited; 5 percent halves the anchor and 15 percent is an interior point. At each point use the same held-out ranking and ceiling-rounded within-hospital quota.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis H-range-value: in the frozen all-baseline/L24 EICU population, the D7-fitted hospital-held-out R_full queue will capture at least 0.03 more source-defined D7 deaths per analytic queue position than the equal-capacity R_process queue at every capacity in C. Formally, theta7=min_c Delta7_value(c)>=0.03. Support requires a simultaneous 95% lower bound for theta7 of at least +0.03, nonnegative simultaneous worst-capacity lower bounds for the equal-hospital macro and both frozen repeat-availability strata, prespecified deletion stability, and a nonnegative simultaneous worst-capacity lower bound for eventual hospital-discharge death capture in the same D7-fitted queues.

The +0.03 margin remains an inherited decision anchor, not an elicited utility. Requiring it at every grid point is stronger than averaging: a large gain at one capacity cannot hide an unhelpful capacity. The result pertains only to the four grid points and this retrospective snapshot; it does not interpolate between points.

A supportive result would justify an independent prospective silent study of a value-using ranking under locally measured workload. It would not show that a repeat lactate should be ordered, that its value was available or viewed at bedside, that a review occurred or altered care, that any death was preventable, or that the queue has utility or benefits patients.

## Capacity-envelope estimands and exact quotas

For each c in C and hospital h with frozen size n_h, define k_h(c)=ceil(c*n_h). Rank each model within h by descending held-out raw score and ascending numeric patientunitstayid for exact ties. The top k_h(c) rows form Q_m,h(c); set K(c)=sum_h k_h(c). Report all n_h, k_h(c), K(c), and realized fraction K(c)/2302. Because of ceiling, the realized fraction exceeds the nominal fraction; call it ceiling-rounded, never exact pooled workload. Queues are nested within a model/hospital because only the cut point changes.

For outcome o in {7,inf}, define S_m,h^o(c)=sum over i in Q_m,h(c) of D_i^o and P_m,h^o(c)=S_m,h^o(c)/k_h(c). The capacity-specific pooled numeric-value contrast is:

Delta_o,value(c)=[sum_h S_full,h^o(c)-sum_h S_process,h^o(c)]/K(c).

The primary scalar is theta7=min_c Delta_7,value(c). Its +0.03 margin means at least three additional source-defined D7 captures per 100 analytic positions at every grid capacity. Do not average capacity effects, pool numerators across capacities, interpolate, or select argmax; those answer different questions.

Hospital weighting is explicit. The pooled estimand weights hospitals by ceiling-rounded slots. Define Delta_7,macro(c)=mean_h[P_full,h^7(c)-P_process,h^7(c)] and theta7_macro=min_c Delta_7,macro(c) so each hospital has equal weight. For stratum s in {low,high}, define a pooled contrast using only hospitals in s and its own slot denominator, and theta7_s=min_c Delta_7,s(c). These are internal site/process guards, not external transport estimates.

The eventual guard is thetainf=min_c Delta_inf,value(c) on the same D7-fitted scores and assignments. It guards only against losing capture of eventual in-hospital deaths; it is not a safety, survival, or treatment-effect estimand.

At each c and h, define additions A_h(c)=Q_full,h(c) minus Q_process,h(c), removals R_h(c)=Q_process,h(c) minus Q_full,h(c), and s_h(c)=|A_h|=|R_h|. Let M(c)=sum_h s_h(c), rho(c)=M(c)/K(c), and for each outcome G_o(c)=sum_h[sum_A D_i^o-sum_R D_i^o]. If M(c)>0, define Psi_o(c)=G_o(c)/M(c). Enforce Delta_o,value(c)=G_o(c)/K(c)=rho(c)*Psi_o(c) and |Delta_o,value(c)|<=rho(c). If M=0, G and Delta must be zero and Psi missing. A 2M denominator is wrong. Report K, M, rho, G, Psi, addition/removal event proportions, overlap, substitution hospitals, and s_h/k_h; never report yield without M/K or let yield alter the primary state.
