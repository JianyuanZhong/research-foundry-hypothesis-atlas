## 1. The unresolved question and why it matters

Current ICU practice (the ABCDEF / "light-sedation" bundle) treats deep sedation as a modifiable hazard, resting largely on observational cohorts [K1, K3]. But the only adequately powered randomized test of minimizing sedation - NONSEDA, nonsedation vs light sedation (RASS -2..-3) - found NO mortality benefit and NO ventilator-free-day benefit [K2]. This leaves a genuine, clinically consequential tension:

- If continuing DEEP sedation AFTER a patient has already stabilized is harmful (delays liberation), then bedside sedation-lightening is a lever worth enforcing at that decision point.
- If the deep-sedation association is mostly confounding by indication / severity (deep sedation marks sicker, e.g. ARDS/ICP/seizure patients rather than causing delay), then the association should shrink toward the null after credible severity adjustment.

The unresolved claim is NOT "deep sedation is associated with worse outcomes" (already supported [K1, K3]) but: at a prespecified, objective stabilization landmark, does a persistent deep-sedation state add prognostic/decision information about failed ventilator liberation beyond a careful marker of concurrent severity?

## 2. Hypothesis and rival (falsifiable)

- **H1 (leading):** Among invasively ventilated adults meeting a prespecified stabilization criterion, a persistent deep-sedation state (RASS <= -3) at/after the landmark is associated with failure of ventilator liberation within 48h and a worse 7-day composite (extubation failure OR in-hospital death), after adjustment for concurrent severity.
- **H0 / strongest rival (confounding by indication + severity):** After adjustment for a rich concurrent-severity marker set (vasopressor dose, oxygenation ratio, GCS, lactate where available, age), the deep-sedation association attenuates to the null and adds no incremental discrimination - exactly what NONSEDA's null result predicts if the association is non-causal [K2].

## 4. Population and temporal boundaries

- Adults (anchor_age >= 18), first ICU stay per subject, invasively ventilated (225792).
- **Landmark t\*** = first time within the first 72h at which the patient is (a) invasively ventilated, (b) off/low vasopressor (no vasopressor start in preceding 6h, or NE-equivalent below a small prespecified threshold), and (c) adequately oxygenated (SpO2 >= 92% with FiO2 <= 0.5, or PaO2/FiO2 >= 200). No future information used.
- **Exposure window:** sedation depth in the 6h after t\*: persistent deep = min/median RASS <= -3; lighter = RASS >= -2.
- **Outcomes:** 48h liberation failure; 7-day composite (extubation failure OR in-hospital death). Successful liberation = documented extubation/SBT success (227194 / 224717) or end of invasive ventilation alive without re-institution within 48h.

## 5. Analysis (baseline vs substantive alternative)

- **Baseline:** logistic regression of 48h liberation failure on concurrent-severity markers (age, NE-equivalent vasopressor dose, oxygenation ratio, GCS, lactate, ICU type) - the severity-only comparator.
- **Extended:** baseline + deep-sedation exposure and a RASS dose-response term.
- **Substantive learned alternative:** gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost) with/without the sedation term, to probe non-linear/threshold structure a logistic baseline cannot express (whether the signal is a monotone dose-response vs a proxy for severity).
- **Metrics:** adjusted ORs with bootstrap CIs, incremental AUROC (DeLong), calibration, dose-response across RASS bins.
- **Negative control:** repeat using Goal RASS (228299) to probe measurement/documentation artifact.

## 6. Falsification and interpretation

- **Supportive:** adjusted OR materially > 1 with CI excluding 1, monotone RASS dose-response, incremental AUROC > 0 after severity adjustment.
- **Adverse (rival favored):** adjusted association near null (CI includes 1), no incremental discrimination - deep sedation explained by concurrent severity, consistent with NONSEDA [K2].
- **Inconclusive:** wide CIs, insufficient RASS at landmark, or effect reversals under the Goal-RASS control -> cannot separate exposure from measurement practice.
