## Targeted successor and falsifiable hypothesis

This targeted synthesis of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]` changes only the patient-level availability contract and the identification boundary between stored-content potential and real-time report availability. The two parents expose opposite defects. The equal-threshold reach gate is mathematically redundant because a patient's report fraction is at most that patient's any-report indicator, so mean patient-balanced completeness at least 0.80 already implies any-report reach at least 0.80. The complete-packet rule is distinct, but treats four of five reports exactly like zero of five even though the model receives materially different information. Neither scalar reveals whether missing content is concentrated in a few patients, diffusely leaves many patients just below adequate coverage, or consists of high-completeness partial packets.

The replacement is a **balanced-packet contract**. For each full-roster patient, compute the exact fraction of eligible CT/MRI units visible. A mask qualifies only when:

1. the all-roster mean of those patient fractions is at least 0.80; and
2. at least 80% of all roster patients individually have at least 80% of their own eligible packet visible.

The complete-packet rate remains a prespecified nested comparator, not the primary gate. Exact disjoint patient states and an exact `(n_i,k_i)` packet histogram expose partial patterns rather than collapsing them.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> In each of 2020 and 2021, every coherent source/pathology/reader world has at least one version-faithful full-roster report mask satisfying the balanced-packet contract; and every such mask preserves all six strict >5 documentary-M2 capture margins—G over independently optimized acquisition-only B, G over independently optimized report-surface R, and G over same-fit semantic mask Gmask—before deletion and after anchored deletion of any one test patient.

`q_i(r)=k_i/n_i` if `n_i>0`, otherwise 0.

Source-unavailable and blank units remain denominator units with forced-zero bits; zero-unit patients remain in N with q=0. No patient is dropped after report inspection.

Assign exactly one state per patient:

- `structural-zero`: `n_i=0`;
- `none-visible`: `n_i>0, k_i=0`;
- `low-partial`: `0<5k_i<4n_i`;
- `high-partial`: `4n_i<=5k_i<5n_i`;
- `complete`: `n_i>0, k_i=n_i`.

Thus four of five is high-partial; one of two is low-partial; one of one is complete. These integer predicates are disjoint and exhaustive, including unequal n_i.

Define:

`C_mean=(1/N) sum_i q_i`.

`C_ind80=(1/N) sum_i 1(n_i>0 and 5k_i>=4n_i)`.

`C_all=(1/N) sum_i 1(n_i>0 and k_i=n_i)`.

`C_any=(1/N) sum_i 1(k_i>0)`.

Because `C_mean<=C_any`, an additional same-threshold C_any gate is redundant and is not used.

`R_bal,.80(omega)={r: r<=r_HCCmax, 5*sum_i q_i>=4N, and 5*sum_i e_i>=4N}`,

where `e_i=1(n_i>0 and 5k_i>=4n_i)`. The first inequality is evaluated with exact rationals; the second is exact integer arithmetic. A certified arbitrary-precision common denominator or native exact-rational solver is required—floating tolerance cannot determine membership.

`R_all,.80(omega)={r: r<=r_HCCmax and 5*sum_i 1(n_i>0 and k_i=n_i)>=4N}`.
