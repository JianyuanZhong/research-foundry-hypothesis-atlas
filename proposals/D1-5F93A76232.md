# Absolute missing-patient tolerance for a 24-hour documentary-M2 review queue

## Targeted successor and the repaired question

This child preserves the assessed-valid design in [prior hypothesis]: adult first-eligible HCC resection, coherent operation and treatment clocks, whole-accession CT/MRI units, five-level monotone content-onset states, a 24-hour confirmatory cutoff, patient-disjoint 2015–2018 development/2019 lock/separate 2020 and 2021 tests, nine global radiology/pathology reader-book pairs, an encounter-documentary M2 endpoint, locked B/R/G/same-fit-Gmask scores, fixed K, strict comparator/random gates, and anchored whole-patient deletion.

It repairs one remaining substantive weakness. The parent calls any q_req<1 supportive. In a finite corpus that can mean the locked queue tolerates loss of only one patient's report bundle. Such a mathematically positive reserve is falsifiable but need not be consequential for a review service and can change with denominator granularity. The repair replaces that primary claim with an absolute, capacity-referenced tolerance: can the queue lose complete report bundles for at least one entire review-cycle's worth of imaging-eligible patients, chosen adversarially, and still retain every documentary-M2 capture gate?

The historical experiment still does not claim that reports were actually available, that a clinician viewed them, that the pathology is specimen-linked biological MVI, or that review changes treatment or outcomes.

Current full-text evidence supplies context, not validation. Li et al. (2025, PMCID PMC12132667, DOI 10.2147/JHC.S519578; frozen XML [source checksum]) retrospectively studied 131 resected patients with confirmed MVI and reported test AUC 0.917 for a combined DCE-MRI radiomics model; it did not test report text, report availability, or fixed-capacity review. Li et al. (2025, PMCID PMC12309289, DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746; frozen XML [source checksum]) describes MVI as associated with recurrence and survival, notes that diagnosis depends on postoperative pathology, and describes management as controversial. Neither establishes this dataset's documentary endpoint, model performance, threshold, or benefit.

The unresolved HCC-computable claim is whether semantic ranking has a clinically nontrivial engineering reserve to missing whole-patient report bundles. The confirmatory hypothesis is:

> At the 24-hour cutoff and frozen 10% review capacity, maximum stored content passes every G-versus-B, G-versus-R, G-versus-same-fit-Gmask, and G-versus-capacity-matched-random gate in both 2020 and 2021; and the largest adversarial number of imaging-eligible patients whose complete report bundles may be unavailable while every gate still passes, b_star, is at least K_.10.

K_.10 is an analytic capacity reference, not observed staffing. Requiring b_star>=K_.10 means tolerance of at least one complete review-cycle's worth of missing patient bundles; it is a predeclared stress standard, not a claim that this is a clinically acceptable availability rate. Report the parent's q_req and fractional reserve secondarily, without using q_req<1 as support.

## Disjoint result meanings and falsification

Apply this precedence:

1. computationally_inconclusive: source replay, state construction, fitting, ranking, exact optimizer certificate, quotient replay, deletion, or witness verification fails.
2. feasibility_inconclusive: computation succeeds but a locked N/K/event or required reader/adjudication gate fails.
3. maximum_content_signal_adverse: any all-content 24-hour comparator/random/deletion gate fails in 2020 or 2021. More report availability cannot rescue this prespecified model.
4. absolute_reserve_adverse: maximum content passes but b_star<K_.10. This falsifies the consequential missing-patient-tolerance hypothesis even if q_req<1.
5. hcc_absolute_reserve_supportive_bridge_pending: maximum content passes, b_star>=K_.10, and boundary certificates verify. This supports only a historical finite-corpus engineering reserve for documentary-M2 allocation.

A positive AUC, isolated cutoff/capacity/year, favorable partial mask, point estimate, q_req<1, or externally favorable report audit cannot rescue an adverse HCC-stage result. Conversely, an adverse result is scientifically useful: it shows that report-semantic allocation is too brittle under the prespecified stress standard.

An external archive bridge must outcome-blindly crosswalk every (患者主索引,就诊号,检查号) and source ordinal/hash to immutable report-version and predecessor/amendment IDs; complete input hashes; authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrievable/readable/retracted times; timezone/clock provenance; validated operation clocks; and the exact scoring-pipeline input. A census can identify actual states. A probability audit must retain nonresponders and provide simultaneous design-based upper bounds on the absolute number of non-complete imaging-eligible patients in each year/world. Only an exact census that passes directly, or a compatible upper bound <=b_star, can earn externally_coverage_bridged_documentary_supportive. Invalid crosswalks, clocks, versions, denominators, sampling, or simultaneous bounds are archive_inconclusive; an upper bound >b_star is coverage_contract_not_established, not proof of policy harm.

Even externally bridged documentary support leaves clinical_action_status=not_identified. Prospective silent-mode workflow data must measure user availability/view, actual roster/capacity and competing work, actions, delays, failures, harms, costs, and outcomes; stakeholders must define utilities. Biological MVI and sampling require specimen/slide linkage and pathology review. Treatment effects, recurrence, survival, transportability, safety, and patient benefit require another study.
