# Whole-panel, whole-patient stability of documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Synthesis decision and clinical question

This targeted child combines valid `[prior hypothesis]` with the distinct deletion question in valid `[prior hypothesis]`, but it does not import the latter's patient-local cross-reader form product. The primary parent's six immutable corpus-wide books and nine radiology×pathology book pairs remain the only reader worlds. Deletion is evaluated inside each world and never creates a patient-specific reader mosaic.

The clinical question remains deliberately documentary: among adults in a source-defined first eligible HCC-resection corpus, do clean, source-byte-traceable meanings in eventual stored pre-cutoff CT/MRI report text add strictly more than 5 encounter-documentary M2 captures per 100 fixed review positions beyond both an independently optimized clean reduced comparator and the same fitted full score after complete semantic masking, in every declared source/pathology-completion/whole-reader-book world, separately in 2020 and 2021? The successor adds: does each of those four universal margins remain strictly above 5 after removing any one whole test patient while keeping books, temporal partition, fitted models, and review capacity fixed?

This is clinically useful because a universal endpoint just above 5 can still be carried by one patient whose deletion changes both arms' top-K replacements. Exact single-patient stability is an interpretable finite-corpus prerequisite for spending resources on report-release evidence and a prospective silent-mode capacity study. It is not a confidence interval, transport claim, or treatment-effect analysis.

## 4. Source objects, complete books, and compatibility rule

A radiology item is `u=(patient master index,encounter number,examination number)` with exact-deduplicated components in raw order and all field boundaries. Blank `examination number` cannot supply primary semantics. The inherited parser constructs disjoint visible-text `V`, markup/attribute `A`, and parse-error lineage `E`; semantics trace reversibly to bytes in V. Parse disagreement, nonreversibility, treatment/pathology/specimen/MVI-grade contamination, or unresolved context makes the whole unit unavailable. No phrase is selectively removed and no patient is dropped.

A pathology item is `v=(Patient Master Index,Encounter Number,composite_hash)`: exact-deduplicate all six-field same-encounter rows, retain raw ordinals, and serialize every `Pathology,Examination Findings,Examination Diagnosis` field in row/field order with boundaries and offsets. With no pathology item/time/specimen key, all selected-encounter rows form one indivisible documentary composite. Blank composites deterministically yield `OUT` and are not missing forms.

Before semantic/outcome access, a steward freezes the full reachable radiology and nonempty-pathology rosters. Two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists and a third radiology adjudicator each complete every radiology item; two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists and a third pathology adjudicator each complete every pathology item. Immutable books are `B^R_j={f^R_j(u):all u}` for `j∈{R1,R2,RA}` and `B^P_k={f^P_k(v):all v}` for `k∈{P1,P2,PA}`. Adjudication is a complete signed form, never a fieldwise merge. Missing/corrupt/incomplete/provenance-deficient expected forms cause global inconclusiveness, not complete-case deletion or source-unavailable substitution.

Each pathology form stores one HCC-frame judgment and conditional grade judgment and deterministically maps, indivisibly, to a subset of `OUT,IN0-A,IN1-A,IN0-U,IN1-U`. HCC and grade cannot be borrowed across forms; uncertain grade is not negative.

The only reader-panel set is `G={R1,R2,RA}×{P1,P2,PA}`. For `g=(j,k)`, that pair applies to the entire corpus, all years/offsets/stages/arms, and every deletion. Source states select relevant already-read objects, never readers. A terminal varies only within the selected pathology form's finite set. Repeated item appearances share the same form. Quotienting retains all original backpointers.

Deletion restricts a frozen book to retained patients; it never changes book identity, roster denominator, annotation, or hash. Deleted-patient forms remain in the audit book but contribute to no state, rank, or endpoint. This is the central compatibility rule. The secondary parent's patient-local cross-reader Cartesian product is retired.

## 5. Fixed K and exact deletion estimands

Before test forms are exposed, calculate on the undeleted design

`N_ref=min_{a=2015,...,2019;d∈{12,24,48,72};g∈G;omega∈Omega_g} N_a(omega,d)`

and `K_rho=floor(rho*N_ref)` for `rho∈{.05,.10,.20}`. Confirmatory `K=K_.10`; require `N_ref≥500`, `K≥50`, and at least K eligible patients in every undeleted 2020/2021 world. Five and twenty percent are sensitivities only. K and `100/K` never change after deletion.

For test year a, model M, book g, and coherent world omega, rank eligible whole patients by locked predictor, patient SHA-256 tie key, and raw episode backpointer; select exactly K; and compute `T_M=100/K sum_{i∈TopK_M}Y_i`. Four co-primary coordinates are `e=(a,C)` for `a∈{2020,2021}` and `C∈{G_clean-R_clean,G_clean-Gclean_mask}`; both arms use the identical world.

For deletion set D, define
`L_e(D,g)=min_{omega∈Omega_g(U_T\D)}Delta_e(g,omega)` and
`U_e(D,g)=max_{omega∈Omega_g(U_T\D)}Delta_e(g,omega)`.
Deletion removes every row/state/episode alternative/form contribution/arm contribution for listed patients. Retained patients may take any admissible source state and same-form terminal after deletion; g stays fixed. The undeleted interval is
`I_e^0=[min_g L_e(empty,g),max_g U_e(empty,g)]`.

The primary deletion-stability estimand is
`L_e^(-1)=min_{p∈U_T}min_{g∈G}L_e({p},g)`.

Before interpreting it, require for each year the sharp gate
`min_{p∈U_T,g∈G,omega∈Omega_g(U_T\{p})}N_a(omega)≥K`.
Failure means fixed-K deletion stability is undefined and the experiment is inconclusive. Lowering K, omitting a world, changing denominator to remaining cohort size, or coding nonmembership negative is forbidden.

Report 3×3 matrices of undeleted intervals and singleton minima and every minimizing witness. Inherited anchored-grade fraction (`≥0.85`) and event (`≥50`) gates are evaluated undeleted. Post-deletion minima are diagnostics, not rerun gates: otherwise intentional removal of an influential positive could hide as insufficient events. Only `N≥K` is mathematically required to define the deletion contrast.

Secondarily compute the exact minimum capacity-valid multi-patient deletion count:
`q_e*=min{|D|:min_g L_e(D,g)≤5 and min_{g,omega}N_a(omega;U_T\D)≥K}`.
Thus q=0 is undeleted failure, q=1 a singleton witness, and q>1 proves stability to every capacity-valid smaller deletion set. If no adverse set exists before fixed-K support is exhausted, report `not-attained-before-support-exhaustion`, not infinity. Top-K replacement is non-monotone: q=2 does not mean every pair or larger deletion fails. Never normalize q into a probability, confidence level, contamination fraction, or population guarantee.

For each witness, replay a paired undeleted world with the same book and identical retained-patient states/terminals, adding an admissible state for each deleted patient. Report before/after top-K hashes, replacements, outcomes, ranks, and arithmetic. Global minima may use different worlds; causal language about a patient's effect is prohibited.

Conditional on g, compile finite coherent patient domains `D_i^g`, enforcing repeated-item consistency and storing eligibility/year, Y if eligible, Z/S/Q/X, each locked score, source/form/terminal hashes, tie key, and raw backpointers. For one coordinate/book, binary `x_ik` chooses a retained state, `z_i` deletes the whole patient, and `t_ik^M` selects that state in arm M:

- `sum_k x_ik=1-z_i`;
- `t_ik^M≤x_ik h_iak` and `t_ik^M≤1-z_i`;
- `sum_i,k t_ik^M=K` per arm;

Fix z for undeleted/singleton endpoints. For q, minimize `sum z_i` subject to `Delta≤5` and capacity validity. Solve separately for nine books and take exact extrema. Independently enumerate zero and every singleton; agreement with the joint MIP is mandatory.

Require zero unresolved integer gap, numerical feasibility and transformed-loss residual ≤`1e-8`, no violated inversion, raw/quotient replay, state/order invariance, all tied maximizers, exhaustive equality on synthetic fixtures through 12 patients and real-data shards through 20, and complete backpointers. ## 7. Falsification and meanings

Mandatory inherited gates cover source/header/hash agreement; complete state enumeration; chronology; dictionaries/parsers/rubrics; complete six books; terminal regeneration; leakage exclusions; N_ref/K, undeleted test size, anchored-grade and event minima; model grid/fits; same-fit masking; nine-cell extrema; and exact replay. Add singleton fixed-K support, patient-wide deletion integrity, frozen role/model/book/K hashes, and exhaustive singleton checks.

After gates pass, equality to 5 fails:

1. **Stable whole-panel universal supportive:** all four undeleted lower endpoints and all four singleton minima exceed 5.
2. **Finite-corpus supportive but single-patient fragile:** all four undeleted lower endpoints exceed 5, but at least one singleton minimum is ≤5.
3. **Universal adverse without a positive coordinate:** every undeleted upper endpoint is ≤5.
4. **Decisive heterogeneity:** gates pass and neither above label applies; declared worlds/coordinates differ across the threshold. Attribute radiology, pathology, or joint sensitivity only with the parent's fixed-other-components paired witnesses.
5. **Inconclusive:** any mandatory gate fails, including inability to compute fixed K after every singleton, incomplete books, or unresolved exact bounds. This has precedence.

For stable results, q may add `multi-patient-fragile-at-q` or `not-attained-before-support-exhaustion`; neither changes the singleton label. A fragile patient is an influence witness, not necessarily an error/outlier. Support is only a frozen-corpus statement about eventual-report concepts, declared books, documentary labels, fixed positions, and deletion. It does not establish correct labels, unseen readers, biological M2, preoperative visibility, population performance, confidence coverage, capacity, benefit, or deployment readiness.
