## Primary endpoint: REC-WARD7

Set `H7=t0+7 days`. Define one binary outcome for every equal clone.

`Y_REC-WARD7=1` if and only if:

1. The index `icu/icustays` row has finite `outtime` with `t0 < outtime <= H7`.
2. At that exact `outtime`, there is exactly one exact-deduplicated same-admission `hosp/transfers` row with a frozen non-ICU `careunit`, satisfying `intime <= outtime < transfer.outtime`, with finite clocks. The frozen non-ICU list is the parent's list: Cardiac Surgery, Cardiology, Cardiology Surgery Intermediate, Discharge Lounge, Emergency Department Observation, Hematology/Oncology, Hematology/Oncology Intermediate, Labor & Delivery, Med/Surg, Med/Surg/GYN, Med/Surg/Trauma, Medical/Surgical (Gynecology), Medicine, Medicine/Cardiology, Medicine/Cardiology Intermediate, Neuro Intermediate, Neuro Stepdown, Neurology, Observation, Obstetrics (Postpartum & Antepartum), Obstetrics Antepartum, Obstetrics Postpartum, Oncology, Psychiatry, Surgery, Surgery/Pancreatic/Biliary/Bariatric, Surgery/Trauma, Surgery/Vascular/Intermediate, Surgical Intermediate, Thoracic Surgery, Transplant, and Vascular.
3. No other same-admission `icu/icustays` interval is active at that exit clock (apart from the index interval that ends at the clock).
4. There is no definite death at or before the non-ICU transfer `intime`: exact `admissions.deathtime <= transfer.intime`, a consistent `hospital_expire_flag=1` or `discharge_location='DIED'` with `dischtime <= transfer.intime`, or a `patients.dod` day interval whose upper bound is <= the transfer clock. A date-only death interval intersecting the transfer clock is not success; it is an endpoint-ambiguity flag.

All other records receive `Y_REC-WARD7=0`, including definite death, continued ICU occupancy, direct ICU-to-home/facility/hospice/acute-hospital closure, no active admission or out-of-system closure before H7, no valid ward transfer, transfer/ICU disagreement, unseen/PACU/Nursery careunits, multiple simultaneous candidate transfers, and missing or malformed clocks. Out-of-system closure is a competing failure of the recorded-transition endpoint, never recovery and never an imputed success.

The endpoint measures a documented transition out of the index ICU into a recorded non-ICU location. It intentionally does not require remaining there through D7 and does not count direct ICU discharge as ward de-escalation. Report components: valid transition, death before candidate transition, continued/open ICU, direct closure without ward transition, out-of-system closure before transition, ambiguous topology, and missing/malformed endpoint clocks.

Run the same endpoint at `H48=t0+48 hours` and `H72=t0+72 hours` as timing sensitivities. A timing variant is materially discordant if its A-minus-B bound shifts by >=0.05 or its midpoint changes sign while both absolute midpoints are >=0.02.

## Falsification and interpretation

- **Supportive recorded de-escalation:** computation, population, exposure, temporal, measurement, positivity, balance, bootstrap, harm and endpoint-integrity gates pass; the one-sided 95% lower limit for REC-WARD7 A-minus-B exceeds +0.05; no Holm-adjusted harm rejects; H48/H72 sensitivities do not materially discord; and RINO does not create an unacknowledged composition reversal. This supports only a stronger association with documented ICU-to-ward transition.
- **Adverse recorded outcome:** valid computation with a Holm-rejected inherited adverse family. Name the family and contrast; do not call it toxicity or causality.
- **Margin-falsified:** valid analysis with no adjusted adverse family and the one-sided 95% upper limit for REC-WARD7 at or below +0.05. This falsifies the five-point recorded-transition association, not equivalence, safety, or physiologic benefit.
- **Endpoint-composition inconclusive:** timing variants materially discord, ambiguous/malformed topology changes the result, direct-closure or out-of-system components differ sharply by arm, or the RINO interval reverses the REC-WARD7 interpretation. No directional clinical statement is made.
- **RINO partial-identification inconclusive:** Delta_RINO,L does not exclude +0.05, its bound width is large, or weighted unknownness exceeds the inherited feasibility rule. This is a measurement limitation, not a negative treatment finding.
- **Primary infeasible:** required source members/headers, state machine, population, exposure grammar, weights, bootstrap or formal minima fail. No direction is reported.
