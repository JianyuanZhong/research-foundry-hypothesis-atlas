# Admission-anchored early physiology and subsequent recorded procedural transition in HCC-coded inpatient care

## Scientific deliverable and substantive repair

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s admission anchoring, [0,12)-laboratory/[24,72)-outcome buffer, exhaustive early and late observed-care states, and patient-level split. The remaining limitation is evidentiary: the parent’s primary E/S/A/M/O endpoint depends on a locally authored procedure-text dictionary. Reproducible lexical labels do not establish procedure indication, intent, receipt, completion, or HCC-directedness.

This child changes the primary endpoint to a semantics-independent, discharge-aware **first recorded procedural transition**. A procedure is an observed row with a valid native `开始时间` before discharge; its name is not used to define the primary outcome. Route families become a secondary, explicitly gated characterization. The narrower estimand is more defensible: whether early information predicts subsequent documented procedural care relevant to coordination/resource planning, rather than a presumed treatment choice.

The future solver must newly fit and evaluate on one frozen patient split:

1. an admission-anchored sequential competing-state baseline (B0);
2. a matched transparent assay-summary model adding only valid [0,12) laboratory history (B1); and
3. an irregular assay-specific latent-state model (B2) targeting the same states.

Completion is established by the frozen cohort/time audit, exhaustive state construction, locked patient-level predictions, proper-score/calibration uncertainty, and falsifications. Null and inconclusive results are valid completion outcomes.

## Supported evidence and unresolved claim

The HCC snapshot contains:

- `encounters`: patient/encounter keys, age, sex, department, native `入院时间` and `出院时间`;
- `diagnoses`: `诊断名称` and `诊断类型`, without native diagnosis time;
- `labs`: assay `检验`, qualitative/quantitative result fields, specimen type, and native `检验时间`;
- `procedures`: `手术`, native `开始时间`/`结束时间`, and `手术来源`.

A fresh read-only audit of the verified source files found 42,692 provisional first-patient admissions, 146,703 linked procedure rows, 86,025 linked rows with midnight starts, 9,077 linked rows with missing/unparseable starts, 25,527 valid starts in [0,24), and 51,332 valid starts in [24,72). These are feasibility figures, not locked study results; the solver must recompute them after frozen join and missing-time rules.

The snapshot does not establish active HCC, diagnosis timing, tumor burden, stage, resectability, indication, scheduling, consent, receipt, completion, response, toxicity, mortality, or outside-hospital care. It has no lab-unit/reference-range column, so unlike assays must not be pooled or interpreted as a validated liver/renal score.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among all adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission, does assay-specific laboratory information observed strictly in [0,12) improve held-out calibrated prediction of the complete first-transition distribution through 72 hours—early procedure, early discharge, unresolved procedure timing, later procedure, later discharge, unresolved later timing, or no recorded transition—beyond admission/pre-admission information; and, within [24,72), does it improve prediction of a first valid procedure record rather than only competing discharge/no-transition states?

This is a prognostic recorded-care information hypothesis, not a treatment-effect, active-disease, treatment-intent, or clinical-utility hypothesis. A prospective utility or bedside-threshold study would be separate.

## Exhaustive semantics-independent endpoint

For every eligible admission, construct the primary state from procedure presence and native time only. Do not use procedure text, orders, medications, documents, pathology, or future labs to label it.

For [0,24), use row-level ordering audits and the following states:

- `P0`: valid procedure start in [0,24) before discharge;
- `D0`: discharge at or before 24 hours before any valid early procedure;
- `U0`: missing/invalid procedure timing whose unknown time could change procedure-versus-discharge or boundary ordering;
- `R24`: no established `P0`, `D0`, or `U0`.

The solver must tabulate procedures before admission, after discharge, invalid dates, duplicates, multiple same-time rows, and discharge/procedure ties. Conservative missing-time handling assigns `U0` whenever an unresolved row could alter ordering; an optimistic bound treats it as outside the window only when source times logically prove that. The primary tie rule is `D0` (procedure-wins is a sensitivity). Missing time is never silently coded as no procedure. Valid starts outside [0,72) are audited but do not create events.

Among `R24`, define the first later state in [24,72):

- `P1`: first valid procedure record in [24,72) before discharge;
- `D1`: discharge in [24,72) before any valid later procedure;
- `U1`: unresolved procedure timing that could change later ordering;
- `N72`: no established `P1`, `D1`, or `U1` by 72 hours.

The primary all-admission estimand is the mean terminal probability vector over all eligible admissions at `t_0`. Estimate early states for all admissions, estimate the later conditional distribution among `R24`, and marginalize to the complete terminal vector `P0,D0,U0,P1,D1,U1,N72`. Report the conditional `R24` distribution secondarily. No state is removed because its clinical meaning is inconvenient.

This endpoint means only a locally recorded procedural transition. It does not mean HCC-directed treatment, appropriate treatment, or benefit.

## Matched models, split, uncertainty, and compute

Use participant hashing to keep all encounters/modalities for a patient in one partition: buckets 0–59 fit, 60–69 preprocessing/regularization selection, 70–79 locked evaluation; 80–99 inaccessible and not external validation. Freeze cohort, time/tie/missing rules, assay inclusion, transformations, state dimension, hyperparameters, and endpoint before test predictions.

B0 is a transparent sequential multinomial/cause-specific hazard model using age, sex, department, calendar era, admission time-of-day, prior encounter count, and assay-specific pre-admission nearest/count/recency/missingness summaries from lab rows with native time < (t_0). It has early `P0/D0/U0/R24` and later `P1/D1/U1/N72` heads; it excludes post-cohort diagnosis text/type, procedure text, orders, medications, documents, pathology, examinations, and current-window labs.

B1 uses identical split, target, preprocessing, regularization, and model family, adding only valid assay-specific lab history in [0,12): first/last value, change, elapsed time, count, missingness/density, and slope only with at least two native timestamps. Numeric assays stay separate because units are absent; qualitative results stay assay-specific. Unparseable `检验时间` or missing assay-specific result rows are excluded from primary features and counted.

B2 is a substantive learned alternative: an assay-specific irregular state-space model with learned offsets/noise, native observation times, shrinkage, posterior state uncertainty, and coupled early/later competing-state heads for the same target. It may use two low-dimensional latent coordinates, but they must not be called validated liver/renal reserve scores. B2 can reveal nonlinear level/slope coupling and informative observation timing lost by B1 summaries. A count/density/missingness-only process model is required; if it explains all B1 gain, the physiology interpretation is not supported.

Evaluate locked patient-level multiclass log loss, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope or reliability curves, observed-versus-predicted terminal probabilities, and pathway-independent `P1` contrasts. Use at least 500 patient-clustered paired bootstrap resamples (1,000 if within budget), preserving each patient’s complete sequence; report 95% intervals. Locked-prediction bootstrap is primary; refit bootstrap is sensitivity. Sparse states and route contrasts are descriptive/inconclusive.

Future compute is CPU-first for B0/B1 and bootstrap; full fit time is unverified. B2 CPU memory/convergence are unverified; if a bounded implementation demonstrates material need, request one allocated A100 and use `cuda:0` inside the allocation. The solver planning envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, 8 GPUs, and 28,800 seconds; discovery allowed 7,200 science seconds. No solver fitting or proposer/solver training was performed here.

## Falsification and interpretation

Freeze these tests before evaluation:

1. Shuffle lab times within patient/assay, then values within assay; a time-linked gain should attenuate.
2. Compare B1 with the count/density/missingness-only model; a fully reproduced gain is documentation intensity, not physiology.
3. Permute early/later labels within calendar era and department; surviving gain indicates leakage or label artifact.
4. Use [12,24) labs in a forbidden-window sentinel predicting an outcome beginning before those data are available; it must not improve a valid time-safe target.
5. Repeat exact-boundary, midnight, same-time-tie, and conservative/optimistic unresolved-time rules; material reversals make the transition estimand inconclusive.
6. Exclude midnight-start procedure rows as a timing-quality sensitivity; do not select the primary result from it.
7. Apply strict/broad route dictionaries only after primary predictions are locked; unstable route results are inconclusive.
8. Report performance by pre-admission observation availability and admitting department as transportability diagnostics, not external validation.

Supportive evidence requires prespecified B1 improvement over B0 in held-out full-vector log/Brier score with a 95% paired patient-cluster interval excluding no improvement, acceptable calibration, attenuation after time/value shuffling, and no forbidden-window or label-permutation signal. B2 is supportive only if it improves over B1 beyond uncertainty and is stable on locked predictions. Neither establishes mechanism.

Adverse evidence is no B1 improvement, worse calibration, persistence after shuffling, or full reproduction by the process-only model. It falsifies the incremental physiology explanation while still describing recorded care.

The result is inconclusive if unresolved-time states dominate, P1/D1/U1/N72 support is sparse, timing policies reverse estimates, or uncertainty is too wide. Route claims are inconclusive without lexical coherence and adjudication. Inconclusive is not confirmation.

## Evidence boundary and required next study

Computationally checkable claims are cohort counts, key/time audits, frozen state construction, predictions, scores, calibration, state probabilities, bootstrap intervals, and falsifications. They can establish an association with locally recorded procedural transition and whether early labs add predictive information in this snapshot.

Clinical adjudication or another study is required for active HCC, indication/intent, receipt/completion, appropriateness, benefit, treatment effect, mortality, utility, or external transportability. Promoting route results requires clinician-blinded route/indication/timing review and a validated label protocol. A clinical decision claim requires time-linked imaging/pathology, stage/resectability and liver-function context, complete medications/outcomes, and prospective calibration/net-benefit evaluation.

The substantive advance over the parent is an evidence-bounded estimand: the study can test whether early record information predicts the complete subsequent **recorded procedural transition**, without turning local procedure strings into treatment decisions or benefit claims.
