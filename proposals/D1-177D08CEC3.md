# Compiler-ready child: fail-closed reconstruction and endpoint audit for the frozen eICU label estimand

## Status and substantive change

This child is a feasibility-first successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not change the frozen scientific question, target population, exposure contrast, time origin, or estimand. The question remains whether, within the fixed exact-label person-landmarks, `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` versus `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` contains reproducible information about a later persistent, independently charted respiratory-state trajectory beyond strictly pre-D patient state and documentation opportunity. The estimand remains a descriptive conditional association/predictive increment, evaluated with hospital-held-out prediction; it is not a treatment effect and does not identify SBT performance, extubation readiness, liberation, benefit, harm, or quality.

The substantive advance is an executable resolution of the remaining endpoint and feasibility ambiguities. It makes parent reconstruction and divergence a hard pre-outcome gate; freezes the parser and label dictionary without using arm-specific or endpoint summaries; defines repeated separated observations and all endpoint-state precedence; separates ascertainment missingness from death, discharge/transfer, and unclassifiable truncation; and turns leakage, training-only preprocessing, cluster uncertainty, and interpretation gates into machine-checkable artifacts. No results are claimed here.

## Evidence boundary and unresolved claim

Existing lineage evidence supports only that the two literal care-plan values and their prior structured chart mappings are sparse, hospital-concentrated, peak-pressure-dominated or otherwise heterogeneous, and that independent bedside liberation validation is unavailable or insufficient in this snapshot. It does not establish what clinicians intended or delivered.

The unresolved claim is narrower: conditional on the already locked labelled denominator and pre-label state/opportunity, does the evaluation label have a positive, reproducible association and out-of-hospital predictive increment for a later persistent safety-preserving non-peak respiratory-state improvement? The experiment can falsify that claim through a pre-D placebo, lead-time placebo, opportunity negative control, component/source ablations, held-out hospital reversals, or failure of endpoint support. A positive result remains a chart-proxy result only.

## Outcome-blind parser and repeated-observation contract

The parser version, dictionary, bounds, rejection rules, and interval arithmetic are written and hashed before any arm-specific endpoint table, outcome summary, model fit, or gate calculation is read. A full source-wide label inventory is allowed only to verify coverage and unmatched labels; it cannot alter the frozen dictionary. No label expansion, synonym discovery, threshold tuning, or component selection may use post-D values, arm counts, or endpoint rates.

The exact primary component labels are:

- FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`;
- PEEP: `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`;
- pressure support: `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`;
- set rate: `Vent Rate`, `VS RESP RATE`;
- safety signals: numeric `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and numeric `vitalPeriodic.respiration` only.

Matching trims outer whitespace and is case-sensitive; no substring matching or synonym normalization is permitted. Peak/plateau/mean airway pressure, pressure-control, peak-flow, spontaneous/total-rate, and oxygen-flow labels are excluded from the primary component map. Peak pressure can be retained only as a prespecified secondary ablation.

For every numeric field, trim whitespace and accept only a complete signed decimal/scientific-notation token matching `^[+-]?(?:\\d+(?:\\.\\d*)?|\\.\\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\\d+)?$`. Reject blanks, units, ranges, inequalities, nonnumeric values, and non-finite values. Apply the inherited fixed field bounds before aggregation: FiO2 `[0,100]`, PEEP and pressure support `[0,60]`, set rate `[0,100]`, SpO2 `[0,100]`, respiration `[0,200]`. Retain raw value, parsed value, label, reason, and parser version. Do not convert units, interpolate, carry forward, or substitute components; preserve the inherited FiO2 threshold and raw source scale exactly rather than silently rescaling it.

The pre-D trajectory comparison is `P=(D-1440,D-720]` versus baseline `B=(D-720,D]`; primary post-label outcome is `O=(D+360,D+1800]`. For every component and safety signal, a window is observed only if it contains at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. Use the median of all valid observations. Values exactly on boundaries obey these half-open intervals. Record counts, unique times, first/last time, median, maximum gap, and rejection/missing reason. A single copied setting cannot create a transition.

A support reduction is the inherited raw-scale decrease threshold: FiO2 at least `0.05`, PEEP at least `2`, pressure support at least `2`, or set rate at least `2`, each comparing B with O. The primary support domain is at least one non-peak component. Safety passes only when both `sao2` and respiration meet the inherited limits (post SpO2 no more than 2 percentage points below pre-D median; post respiration no more than 4 breaths/min above pre-D median). Missing either required safety signal in either required window is endpoint-missing, not safe. Airway corroboration requires at least one O-window `respiratoryCare` row with nonempty `airwaytype` or a nonmissing ventilator interval field; it is corroboration, not device truth.

## Endpoint states, missingness, and competing exits

For each of the 540 rows, classify in this fixed precedence order:

1. **Competing exit** if `patient.unitdischargeoffset` is nonmissing and `<= D+1800`. `unitdischargestatus` exactly equal to trimmed, case-insensitive `Expired` means death; otherwise a classifiable status/location means discharge/transfer; unclassifiable status/location means truncation. A row exiting before the post window cannot be improvement or ordinary observed non-event. Also report whether exit is at or before `D+360` as no post-washout opportunity.
2. Otherwise, **endpoint missing** if required repeated observations, safety, or corroboration are absent. Use separate mutually exclusive first reasons plus flags for all reasons: no pre-D support, no post-D support, no pre/post safety, no airway corroboration, parser rejection, or source absence.
3. Otherwise, **persistent improvement** if non-peak support reduction and safety pass and airway corroboration pass.
4. Otherwise, **observed non-improvement** if all primary observation requirements are complete but the improvement condition is false, including safety failure.

The compiler must report the four-state distribution and every missingness/exit reason by arm, hospital, and landmark before any complete-window contrast. It must not drop rows because missingness differs between arms. The primary complete-observed risk difference is evaluation minus no-trial among non-competing rows with complete endpoint ascertainment, accompanied by the all-row four-state distribution and a sensitivity treating death and non-death exits as distinct competing events. Predictive models use all rows with explicit state/missingness/competing-exit categories; a binary complete-endpoint sensitivity uses only prespecified pre-D inverse-observation weights, never outcome-derived weights.

Secondary support-only and setting-only/no-corroboration endpoints are fixed sensitivity analyses and cannot replace the primary. Any airway-transition sensitivity uses an exact predeclared `No Artificial Airway` respiratoryCare rule or exact treatment candidate followed by 48 hours without invasive-airway respiratoryCare records; it is unavailable/inconclusive if fewer than 10% of rows in each arm are observed. No sensitivity may redefine the primary question after outcomes are seen.

## Predictors, leakage audit, and held-out analysis

The baseline feature table contains only measurements and opportunity available by clinical time `<=D`. It excludes care-plan values/counts/groups, post-D respiratory values, post-D source counts, post-D treatment/respiratory-care rows, and outcome-derived availability. Entry lag may be used only when both clinical and entry timestamps are pre-D. A machine audit records maximum clinical predictor time, maximum entry time, violations, source/column, and offending row; any violation is a fail-closed stop.

The state-plus-opportunity baseline uses pre-D respiratory state summaries, pre-D trends, source presence, counts, distinct labels, distinct clinical times, inter-record gaps, and entry-lag summaries. The opportunity-only model uses counts/times/presence/lag but no respiratory values, care-plan strings, or label semantics. The label-added model adds only the binary exact arm to the identical pre-D state-plus-opportunity feature set. Hospital and landmark are training strata/covariates only; held-out outcome or label rates cannot be encoded into features.

For each leave-one-hospital-out fold, fit imputation, scaling, feature selection, regularization, calibration, and any observation weights using training hospitals only. Never fit a held-out calibration intercept, target rate, label rate, threshold, or missingness model on held-out outcomes. Report fold row/stay counts, label support, four-state support, row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss/Brier, calibration slope/intercept when defined, AUROC only when both classes exist, and observed/predicted evaluation-minus-no-trial contrasts. A fold with one label cannot supply a within-fold arm contrast and is not repaired by relabeling.

Uncertainty is a stay-cluster bootstrap, fixed seed `61061`, resampling complete `patientunitstayid` clusters within hospital strata while retaining all landmarks of each selected stay and refitting the full pipeline. Use 1,000 replicates, or at least 500 with the shortfall recorded before looking at results. A sensitivity clusters by `uniquepid`. Label permutations are within hospital-by-landmark strata with both arms, preserve arm counts and all rows, and use 1,000 permutations when at least 20 valid strata exist; otherwise report the exact number and mark that gate inconclusive.

## Falsification and decision gates

Run, without changing the parser or thresholds, the P-versus-B pre-D placebo; a lead-time placebo shifting exposure time to `D-720`; leave-one-component-out and source-family ablations; stricter 60-minute separation; documentation-density negative controls; and the valid within-hospital-by-landmark permutation. Report all attrition and bounded airway-recency sensitivities (24, 12, and 6 hours before L) without replacing the frozen primary cohort.

A computationally **supportive** result requires all of the following: the parent/cohort and parser gates pass; at least five hospitals each have at least 10 complete endpoint-observed rows under each label and those hospitals contain at least 50% of complete rows, with no arm above 40% from one hospital; the primary improvement risk difference is positive with at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions positive; label-added versus identical state-plus-opportunity prediction improves both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss by at least 0.01 with a favorable stay-cluster 95% interval excluding zero; the increment survives component/source ablations, both placebos, and the valid permutation null; missingness/competing exits do not explain the value-based gain; and airway corroboration is present in at least 10% of each arm. Passing supports only incremental information in this chart construct.

An **adverse** result requires adequate endpoint/overlap support and a prespecified failure such as no improvement over state-plus-opportunity or opportunity-only, a pre-D/lead-time/placebo-sized increment, a valid permutation-compatible increment, hospital direction reversal, dependence on one component/source/hospital, or documentation-density gain without value-based trajectory gain. It does not prove absence of bedside evaluation or prove documentation artifact.

The result is **inconclusive** if parent identity or source/parser verification fails; fewer than five hospitals meet endpoint support; no held-out arm contrast is estimable; endpoint support, airway corroboration, or competing-exit separation is too sparse; missingness differs so strongly that state and ascertainment cannot be separated; bounded definitions disagree; or uncertainty spans a meaningful increment and zero. No relaxed threshold, pooled row bootstrap, post-hoc label expansion, or complete-case-only conclusion is allowed.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Compared with the two assessed-valid parents, this child makes the endpoint implementation fail closed at the earliest possible point: it explicitly orders parent identity verification before any outcome read; requires a hashable, outcome-blind parser freeze and full label-coverage inventory; resolves repeated observation, safety availability, endpoint-state precedence, exit classification, and reason-level missingness; separates pre-D predictor construction from post-D ascertainment; specifies training-only preprocessing and calibration under hospital holdout; and makes cluster-bootstrap/permutation feasibility and the supportive/adverse/inconclusive gates mechanically auditable. The scientific question, frozen denominator, labels, D, windows, and descriptive estimand are unchanged. It remains uncertain whether the parent artifacts can be reproduced byte-for-byte in the compilation environment and whether the 540 labelled rows provide enough complete, independently charted observations for the endpoint; if not, the correct conclusion is feasibility failure/inconclusive, not an invented estimate. Bedside meaning remains unavailable from these structured eICU files.
