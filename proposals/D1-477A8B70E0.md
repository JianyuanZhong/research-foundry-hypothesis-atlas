# Patient-balanced, version-faithful visibility frontier for documentary-M2 review

## Targeted successor and falsifiable hypothesis

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that valid parent's first-eligible HCC-resection population, coherent operative-clock envelope, 24-hour decision cutoff and locked sensitivities, 90-day imaging and 365-day treatment lookbacks, temporal quarantine, encounter-documentary M2 outcome, whole-accession report radiology units, indivisible pathology composites, six corpus-wide reader books and nine global radiology-by-pathology book pairs, patient-balanced locked B/R/G/Gmask models, fixed absolute review capacity K, three comparator-closed contrasts, strict margin of more than five documentary-M2 captures per 100 review positions, exact source-world optimization, version-faithful effective-visibility bits, and restriction of one full-roster mask after singleton deletion.

It repairs one remaining clinical denominator defect. The parent constrains the fraction of accessions exposed, although the decision, outcome, fixed review slots, and deletion unit are patients. When patients have unequal numbers of eligible accessions, an accession quota gives prolific-imaging patients more availability weight. Eight visible accessions belonging to one patient can satisfy as much quota as one visible accession for each of eight patients even though only one clinical candidate receives information. That threshold is mathematically defined but not a patient-level service requirement.

The new unresolved hypothesis is:

> In each of 2020 and 2021, every admissible version-faithful full-roster mask achieving at least 0.80 patient-balanced effective visibility preserves all six strict >5 documentary-M2 capture margins—G over the independently optimized acquisition-only B, G over the independently optimized report-surface R, and G over its same-fitted semantic mask Gmask—both before deletion and after removal of any one whole test patient, while every survivor retains the identical accession bits and features.

A margin equal to five fails. A world in which 0.80 patient-balanced visibility is unattainable also falsifies the primary hypothesis rather than passing vacuously. The complete patient-balanced frontier and its failure boundary are reported, but the accession-weighted frontier is diagnostic only and cannot authorize the primary conclusion.

This is a retrospective finite-corpus documentary-review experiment. It does not test biological M2, treatment selection, report timeliness in the source hospital, actual capacity, clinician action, recurrence, survival, safety, or patient benefit.

## Whole source objects, outcome, and adjudication

Blank `检查号` cannot supply primary semantics. A reversible parser separates visible text, markup/attributes, and parse errors. Semantic spans may arise only from reversible visible bytes. Freeze radiology books R1/R2/RA and pathology books P1/P2/PA. Exactly nine global book pairs apply corpus-wide. Patient-, item-, year-, mask-, model-, outcome-, or deletion-specific reader switching is forbidden.

Radiology forms encode the parent's predeclared lesion burden, capsule, margin, enhancement/washout, peritumoral features, satellites, venous tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites with uncertain, not-mentioned, and source-unavailable terminals. Pathology terminals remain `OUT, IN0-A, IN1-A, IN0-U, IN1-U`; IN1 is encounter-documentary M2 and OUT has no outcome. ## Version-faithful patient-balanced visibility

For every temporally eligible nonblank accession u, construct the parent's immutable eventual pipeline-input hash:

`H_HCC(u)=SHA256(schema_version || parser_version || length-delimited ordered tuples(source_row_ordinal, field_name, exact raw bytes, V/A/E lineage))`

for every exact-deduplicated `检查所见` and `检查诊断` component. It commits to component count/order, field boundaries, empty fields, and parser lineage. Similar wording, impressions alone, edit distance, equal model score, or reader judgment cannot substitute for exact identity.

The hypothetical bit r_u exposes the whole eventual eligible report block when 1 and replaces every body-derived S/Q/X block by its development-defined operational-unavailable block when 0; acquisition-only Z remains. The same S/Q mask applies to Gmask and B is unchanged. Operationally, r_u may equal 1 only if an external archive proves before c_d that the exact whole version was finalized, retrievable by the intended scoring service, uniquely crosswalked to `检查号`, and byte-identical at the complete pipeline input to H_HCC(u). Preliminary, amended, retracted, unmatched, inaccessible, or nonidentical versions cannot count. Missing archive evidence is unknown, not proof of unavailability.

For full test-year world omega, let I_a(omega) be the complete eligible patient roster, N=|I|, and let E_i(omega) be all temporally eligible nonblank CT/MRI accession units for patient i before semantic-source availability filtering. Let n_i=|E_i|. Source-unavailable units remain in E_i but have r_u forced to zero. A patient with n_i=0 contributes zero and is never dropped.

Define patient-balanced effective visibility:

`C_PB(r,omega) = (1/N) * sum_i [ 1(n_i>0) * (1/n_i) * sum_{u in E_i} r_u ]`.

Thus each patient contributes at most 1/N regardless of scan count; a patient with ten eligible accessions contributes one tenth of that mass per accession, while a patient with one contributes the same total mass when fully visible. The maximum `C_PB^max(omega)` is computed with every version-releasable unit visible and every forced-unavailable unit zero. This definition simultaneously penalizes patients with no eligible accession and unusable accessions; it does not condition the denominator on successful parsing or external access.

For c in the frozen grid `{0,.01,...,1.00}`, define the full-roster family `R_PB,c(omega)={r:C_PB(r,omega)≥c}`. Empty families never pass. At every c, optimize the six undeleted lower endpoints over a coherent world and common mask. For each whole test patient j, remove j and all of j's states, outcomes, and units, and restrict that same mask to survivors. Do not recompute N, n_i, weights, c, or a quota; do not expose a replacement accession. Models, books, roles, preprocessing, K, ties, states, and every survivor's bit and feature hash stay byte-identical. Require N^{-j}≥K.

The primary 0.80 claim requires, in every world, `C_PB^max≥.80` and all six undeleted plus all six anchored-singleton lower endpoints over `R_PB,.80` strictly >5. Report the smallest nonempty grid value `c_PB*` at which the conjunction holds through all higher attainable grid values; if there is a higher attainable failure or no such value, it is undefined. Also directly maximize C_PB over every failing coordinate/deletion to report the attained highest-coverage failing mask `b_PB`; a correct 0.80 supportive result requires `b_PB<.80`. The two computations must agree.

Recompute the parent's accession-weighted frontier on the identical worlds and masks as a secondary multiplicity diagnostic. It cannot support the patient-balanced claim. Report overall, CT-only, MRI-only, and dual-modality patient-balanced coverage descriptively; no modality claim is made without its event/roster gates.

## Exact computation and falsification

Compile finite patient domains carrying membership/year/Y, Z/S/Q/X, accession sets and rational patient weights, locked scores, source/form/terminal hashes, ties, and raw backpointers. Exact MIP or a certified equivalent chooses one coherent state per patient and one full-roster whole-accession mask, then ranks exactly K patients per arm. Encode `C_PB` with exact rationals or a proved lossless integer scaling. Each contrast uses the same world and mask across arms.

Require zero integer gap, numerical feasibility and residual ≤1e-8, complete rank-inversion constraints, all tied maximizers, raw/quotient replay, and exhaustive agreement with brute force on fixtures through 12 patients and real shards through 20. A timeout, state cap, sampled masks, favorable incumbent, unexplained branch, missing witness, rational-scaling loss, changed survivor bit/feature, or failed replay is inconclusive.

The primary estimand is the exact frozen finite corpus. Bootstrap diagnostics cannot create a population or confidence claim and cannot change the exact primary label.

One-hot result:

1. `inconclusive`: any source, dictionary, roster, annotation, chronology, event, N/K, leakage, fit, exact-weight, replay, solver, or mask-invariance gate fails.
2. `patient-balanced visibility-robust supportive`: all gates pass, every world can attain C_PB≥.80, all-content checks pass, and all six undeleted and all six anchored-singleton 0.80 lower endpoints are >5, equivalently b_PB<.80.
3. `patient-balanced coverage infeasible`: gates pass but some world's C_PB^max<.80. This falsifies the prespecified stored-content service threshold in this corpus; it does not prove reports were late.
4. `all-content supportive but patient-balanced visibility-brittle`: all-content margins pass but an attained mask with C_PB≥.80 makes any required margin ≤5; report year, comparator, world, mask, coverage, and deletion witness.
5. `accession-only artifact`: the old accession-weighted 0.80 criterion passes but the patient-balanced criterion fails. This is evidence that multiplicity weighting changed the conclusion and cannot support escalation.
6. `semantic-evidence-only`: both years' Delta_R and Delta_mask pass the patient-balanced all-content/singleton requirements but a Delta_B coordinate fails.
7. `quota-policy-only`: both Delta_B years pass but not all semantic coordinates pass.
8. `falsified-localized`: gates pass and none above applies. A coordinate is all-world adverse only when its attained upper endpoint is ≤5.

A supportive result means that within this frozen eventual-report corpus, any version-faithful mask giving every patient equal total availability weight and reaching 0.80 preserves the strict documentary-M2 capture margins and those margins are not created by one patient's inclusion under fixed report supply. A brittle or adverse result falsifies that universal stress claim, not the usefulness of all radiology information. Inconclusive means the evidence or exact computation cannot resolve it. Favorable AUC, an accession-weighted pass, or an isolated reader/world cannot rescue a failed primary result.
