## Unresolved question and evidence boundary

Among adults recorded as invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48 after recent vasopressor support, marked positive recorded fluid balance, adequate recorded MAP/urine/potassium, and no active RRT/ECMO or recent loop course, is a qualifying ICU input-record start/restart of furosemide or bumetanide during the next 12 hours associated with at least a five-percentage-point increase in directly observed non-ICU topology at ICU hour 120, without prespecified recorded renal, RRT, death, or terminal-disposition harm margins?

The strongest available evidence supports only equipoise and feasibility: heterogeneous deresuscitation/diuretic studies do not establish benefit for this post-shock ventilated phenotype, and the MIMIC snapshot contains relevant clinical-time and recording-time fields. Neither supports efficacy, safety, causal effect, clinician intent, bedside awareness, or a recommendation. H120-RINO is a recorded topology endpoint, not recovery, extubation, survival after discharge, functional outcome, or freedom from ICU care outside MIMIC.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the frozen final eligible population, the assignment-compatible A12-minus-B12 lower partial-identification contrast for H120-RINO is >0.05, with a one-sided 95% subject-bootstrap lower confidence limit >0.05 and all prespecified validity, overlap, endpoint, and harm gates passing.

Supportive results are only recorded-topology associations. Adverse results are valid adjusted recorded harms or failed harm margins, not automatically drug toxicity or causality. A valid contrast whose interval supports neither direction is statistical-inconclusive. Failure of source, cohort, exposure, topology, timing, overlap, model, or bootstrap gates is infeasible/measurement-inconclusive and produces no effect direction.

## Substantive Episode-75 repair

The parent correctly separates clinical starttime from storetime, but calls the derived cohort E_DA “decision-observable.” MIMIC documentation makes storetime an entry/validation clock. It can establish that a row was recorded no later than a chosen time; it cannot establish that a clinician saw it, knew the patient state, intended a treatment, or that the patient received the drug. The same limitation applies to any eMAR concordance attempt.

The amendment renames the companion cohort E_RT (recording-time-compatible), not E_DA, and restricts its claim to a documentation-timing sensitivity:

- Primary E_R is unchanged. It uses clinical start/end times and the recorded ICU input policy. Storetime never changes membership, onset, V0, C0, arm assignment, covariates, outcomes, or weights.
- For an A12 onset, including every row in a linked/touching segment sequence needed to establish that onset, compute recording_by_onset = all(parseable storetime <= clinical starttime). Missing or late storetime is recording-time unknown, not an untreated or reconstructed start.
- For a B12 clone, compute recording_by_C0 = all(parseable storetime <= C0) over every valid loop row with clinical starttime <= C0 that could contradict avoidance. A loop row whose clinical onset is after C0 is irrelevant. Missing/late storetime is unknown, never proof of B12.
- Report E_R and E_RT counts, arm overlap, late/missing-store reasons, negative lags, and subject-level concordance. Rebuild models, histories, weights, endpoints, and bootstrap draws on E_RT only if its independently prespecified feasibility gates pass.
- Label E_RT “recording-time-compatible” or “entry-time sensitivity.” No text, table, or verifier fixture may call it bedside-decision, contemporaneous-awareness, intent, receipt, or administration evidence.
- The eMAR stream is a separate measurement diagnostic. It may classify name/time/admission-level concordance and discordance, but cannot promote or demote an inputevents exposure, and eMAR absence cannot certify B12.

A large E_RT/E_R disagreement is measurement-inconclusive about relevance to a live decision, not evidence for or against furosemide/bumetanide benefit.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive recorded evidence requires all E_R gates, adjusted harm margins, uncertainty, and required sensitivities to pass with the one-sided lower limit for DeltaL_H120 > +0.05. It does not establish decongestion, extubation, recovery, actual receipt, renal safety, causality, or a recommendation.

A recording-time-compatible supportive result additionally requires E_RT feasibility and no prespecified material E_R-versus-E_RT reversal. If E_R supports but E_RT is infeasible or reverses, report “recorded-supportive, recording-timing-inconclusive,” not bedside-decision support. Large eMAR/input discordance has the same measurement interpretation.

Adverse recorded evidence requires a feasible analysis and Holm-adjusted adverse family rejection. Margin-falsified requires feasible analysis with no adjusted adverse family and an upper limit for DeltaU_H120 <= +0.05; it rejects the recorded-topology hypothesis only. Horizon/endpoint-inconclusive follows material H168 or sustained-H120 reversal when feasible. Statistical-inconclusive follows a feasible result satisfying neither directional branch. Primary-infeasible follows source, cohort, arm, event, ESS, model, bootstrap, or audit failure. No branch may call the result a treatment effect, drug toxicity, equivalence, actual administration, or live-policy recommendation.
