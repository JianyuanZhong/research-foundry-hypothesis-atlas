# Context-dependent fibroblast response to macrophage conditioning

## Seed and question
Develops expert seed D2-T1 (`[prior hypothesis]`). The unresolved question is whether a fibroblast transcript response to a defined intervention is context-dependent on the macrophage phenotype present in the coculture, rather than being a uniform fibroblast response.

## Clinical importance
Fibroblasts are a major target of fibrotic disease, but their pathogenic program may be induced, amplified or restrained by macrophage signals. A target that is responsive only in a particular macrophage context could be a more selective intervention point than a target whose expression merely differs between disease and control. Resolving this question can prioritize candidates for perturbation and reduce false prioritization from a global expression contrast.

## Evidence and closest work
The available data include E-MTAB-8316 (four FLS donor clusters, six nested monocyte donors, twelve condition samples) and GSE95588 (coculture HT-Seq counts, including r1/r2 derived from earlier GSE57723 and therefore not independent validation). E-MTAB-8316 has an explicit FLS/monocyte context design; GSE95588 provides a complementary coculture transcriptomic view. The closest inspected work on macrophage-fibroblast crosstalk in fibrosis is [K1], which documents context-dependent transcriptional programs but does not test whether a specific intervention response is macrophage-context dependent. [K2] provides a methodological framework for donor-aware mixed-model analysis of nested donor designs. [K3] describes the biological rationale for macrophage conditioning of fibroblast phenotype in wound healing and fibrosis.

## Hypothesis
In E-MTAB-8316, the fibroblast transcript response to a defined intervention (e.g., TGF-β stimulation) is significantly modified by the macrophage phenotype present in the coculture, beyond what is explained by donor identity and baseline fibroblast state.

## Design
- Population: four FLS donor clusters, six nested monocyte donors, twelve condition samples in E-MTAB-8316; complementary GSE95588 coculture HT-Seq counts.
- Temporal boundaries: condition samples represent defined time points; no longitudinal modeling is attempted.
- Variables: fibroblast transcript expression (outcome), macrophage phenotype (condition), donor identity (random effect), baseline fibroblast state (covariate).
- Outcome: differential expression of fibroblast genes under intervention, stratified by macrophage phenotype.
- Baseline: donor-aware matched contrasts (paired t-test or linear model with donor fixed effects).
- Alternative: mixed-model analysis with donor random effects and macrophage phenotype as a fixed effect, testing the interaction between intervention and macrophage phenotype.
- Falsification: if the interaction term is not significant and the main effect of intervention is uniform across macrophage phenotypes, the hypothesis is not supported.

## What supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results would mean
- Supportive: a significant interaction between intervention and macrophage phenotype, with directionally consistent effects in GSE95588.
- Adverse: no significant interaction, or a uniform intervention effect across macrophage phenotypes.
- Inconclusive: insufficient power, high variability, or conflicting results between E-MTAB-8316 and GSE95588.

## Limitations and next tests
The data are transcriptomic and do not establish direct target action; perturbation validation is required for causal claims. GSE95588 r1/r2 are derived from earlier GSE57723 and are not independent validation. The next test is a targeted perturbation experiment in a fibroblast-macrophage coculture system to confirm the context-dependent response.
