## Result vector and verifier

Report clinical and information-time axes together.

- Supportive-recorded: valid/feasible; no Holm adverse; airway lower >+.05; chart/continuous lower >0; RCR48/RCRD48 upper <+.03; RRT upper <+.05; terminal/mortality upper <+.03; disposition nonadverse; no required feasible reversal. Joint support additionally requires all temporal thresholds and feasible timestamp pipelines. It supports validation/randomization only.
- Adverse-FWER: adjusted adverse family rejected with validity passing; a recorded-proxy concern, not toxicity/safety proof.
- Margin-falsified: no adjusted adverse and airway upper ≤+.05; rejects only the five-point recorded association.
- Statistical-inconclusive: feasible/valid and none above; not equivalence or safety.
- Measurement-limit: J/rM/rA fails, or feasible timestamp/coherent-lag branch reverses; falsifies information-time robustness only.
- Timestamp-inferential-infeasible: M or A funnel computable but any inferential minimum fails; primary may still be categorized, joint support unavailable.
- Validity-inconclusive: required source/rule/ambiguity/positivity/calibration/observation/renal/bootstrap/Holm/process diagnostic fails.
- Primary-infeasible: source/state construction, primary minimum, model, strategy, endpoint or bootstrap fails; no direction follows.

Verifier checks hashes/headers, joins, items/units/statuses, earliest freeze, clocks, P/Q/M/A arithmetic, no Q-only inference, late conflicts, residual clockless flags, equal clones, treatment-before-airway, independent rebuilds, bounds, minima, bootstrap/Holm and output-linked prose. Automation can verify source/schema, timing, eligibility, concordance, compatibility, recorded outcomes, estimates and uncertainty.
