# Care-plan interface opportunity does not explain exact Ventilation-label absence in the frozen eICU cohort

## Substantive child and unresolved question

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the inherited target-independent cohort, first qualifying stay selection, landmarks (1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, 7,200 minutes), four airway definitions (`exact_unbounded`, `exact_airway_24h`, `exact_airway_12h`, `exact_airway_6h`), inclusive post-freeze classification window `[L,L+360]`, inherited baseline, complete outcome restriction, robust non-peak structured respiratory-setting outcome in `(L+360,L+1080]`, broad hospital-held-out estimand, and all semantic limits. It does not reselect on care-plan receipt or change outcome meaning.

The unresolved structural-missingness question is whether absence of the two exact values `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` reflects an absent opportunity to write any `carePlanGeneral` documentation, or instead reflects selective local use/workflow/value choice within an interface that is otherwise present. This matters before absence can be used as a patient/process state, cohort exclusion, or decision-support feature. The parent established that outcome-blind broader Ventilation vocabulary adds only 1–3 rows in one hospital and does not rescue cross-hospital predictive fitness; it did not establish whether exact zeros were interface zeros.

**Strongest evidence-supported claim before this child:** exact labels are sparse, concentrated, and not rescued by outcome-blind lexical broadening in the observed eICU export. **Untested claim:** exact-label absence is caused primarily by a hospital/interface-level lack of documentation opportunity rather than selective vocabulary/workflow. This audit tests only the latter documentation-opportunity claim; it cannot identify an unexported interface, clinician workflow, bedside state, or clinical effect.

## Hypotheses, controls, and falsification gates

The **structural-opportunity hypothesis** predicts that target zeros are concentrated in keys/hospitals with zero care-plan rows, and that target receipt is uncommon only because the table/window is absent. A zero is structural only for the observed `carePlanGeneral` export/window when *all* care-plan rows are absent; a target zero in the presence of other care-plan rows is not structural.

The audit pre-specifies these negative controls:

* generic opportunity: any `carePlanGeneral` row, row count, distinct group count;
* non-target opportunity: any row from a non-Ventilation group and its row count/distinct-group count;
* non-target Ventilation: any Ventilation-group value outside the two exact literals, including spontaneous, non-invasive, missing, and other `Ventilated -` values.

A supportive result would require target-zero hospitals to be mostly care-plan-zero, target receipt to be common among opportunity-positive keys/hospitals, and non-target controls to be absent in the same places. It would support an interface-opportunity explanation for this export, not local workflow equivalence or clinical meaning. An adverse result requires at least five hospitals with target-zero keys but non-target care-plan opportunity, at least three with target-zero keys but Ventilation-group opportunity, and sparse/concentrated receipt among opportunity-positive keys. It would falsify *pure* interface absence while leaving selective local workflow/value choice and unmeasured mapping unresolved. Fewer than those common-support counts, contradictory controls, or too few target events is inconclusive.

## Prespecified conclusion and limits

The observed result is **adverse to a pure absent-care-plan-interface explanation** and supports selective local Ventilation-group vocabulary/workflow or export behavior as the more plausible remaining explanation for exact-label absence in this snapshot. This conclusion is falsifiable and bounded: it rests on many target-zero hospitals with other care-plan rows and, in at least three bounded variants, target-zero hospitals with Ventilation-group rows. It does not show that clinicians selectively chose a value, that a bedside evaluation or SBT occurred, that extubation or readiness was present, or that missingness is harmful. The all-key footprint is outcome-blind; outcome-linked summaries are descriptive and do not alter selection or infer causality.

The result is not proof that hospitals had the same interface, that an unexported local vocabulary did not exist, or that care-plan rows represent care delivered. The configured `hospital` table has no interface/version field, and the care-plan export does not expose UI opportunity, author role, display state, or upstream mapping. A stronger conclusion requires local interface/data-dictionary metadata, source-system versioning, direct device/airway/SBT/extubation records, and blinded clinical adjudication. An automatic verifier can check source hashes, all-row scan, normalization, frozen joins/windows, exact receipt reconstruction, structural-zero counts, common-support gates, and inherited bound preservation; it cannot establish workflow intent, bedside semantics, quality, benefit/harm, causality, or transport to unseen hospitals.
