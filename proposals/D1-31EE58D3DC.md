# Transportable preoperative M2 triage under selective pathology grading and template drift

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

The decision remains a **preoperative M2-risk flag for multidisciplinary review**, not a treatment recommendation. The strongest external evidence supports three narrower claims: microvascular invasion (MVI) is pathology-defined and associated with recurrence; M2 denotes a more extensive pattern than M0/M1; and MVI grading depends on standardized sampling and can be underestimated when non-tumoral margin tissue is inadequate (Li et al., 2025, DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746; Chen et al., 2025, DOI 10.3350/cmh.2025.0167). The latter full text states the Chinese MVI-TTG definitions and explicitly warns about underestimation with inadequate non-tumoral tissue. These facts support clinical importance and endpoint fragility, not model performance.

The HCC source and the parent's feasibility file support computability, but not validity: the parent counted 25,694 preliminary index resections, of which only 11,631 (45.3%) were assigned M0/M1/M2, 5,862 were positive-ungraded, and 8,201 were unknown. A new read-only aggregate audit found an abrupt change in explicit grade-field/template language around 2021–2023 and partial return thereafter. It also found repeated explanatory template legends containing multiple grade names, so a keyword-anywhere parser can produce false M2 labels. These are feasibility observations from preliminary rules, not final cohort counts or evidence that clinical practice itself changed; an extraction-system change is an equally plausible explanation.

**Unresolved claim and primary hypothesis.** Among all adults proceeding to first resection for untreated, pathology-confirmed HCC in 2022–2025—not merely those whose electronic pathology text contains an explicit grade—the report-plus-lab extension (E) has at least 0.01 greater net benefit than the structured baseline (B) at the fixed risk threshold (p_t=0.30). The hypothesis is supported only if the lower 95% confidence limit of the assumption-free lower bound for target-population paired (Delta NB_{0.30}) exceeds 0.01, the verification-weighted analysis agrees under adequate overlap, calibration and event gates pass, and no test year shows net harm. The data have not yet supported this claim.

The substantive advance over the parent is that missing explicit grades are now part of the target population and the estimand. The experiment distinguishes outcome absence from predictor missingness, uses an anchored parser immune to explanatory legends, quantifies calendar/template selection, and partially identifies utility without pretending that M2 can be recovered from ungraded text.

## Target population, outcome-observation mechanism, and time lock

Unit: one patient (`患者主索引`), one index operation.

1. From `procedures`, select each patient's earliest valid `开始时间` whose frozen, clinician-reviewed `手术` dictionary indicates partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resection. Freeze and publish every included/excluded procedure string and counts before model fitting. Exclude transplant, donor surgery, biopsy, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ resection, and obvious non-HCC procedures.
2. Require age >=18 and same-encounter resection pathology that explicitly identifies HCC. Exclude mixed/non-HCC primaries and pathology encounters that cannot be linked by both `患者主索引` and `就诊号`. **Do not require an MVI grade for target-population membership.**
3. Define (R=1) only when the same-encounter pathology contains exactly one parser-assigned, adjudication-valid M0/M1/M2 value. For (R=1), (Y=1) for M2 and (Y=0) for M0/M1. Positive-ungraded MVI, no grade, conflicting assigned fields, parser uncertainty, and missing pathology text have (R=0), not (Y=0). Pathology remains outcome-only.
4. The primary preoperative decision time is fixed at (t_0=) index `开始时间` minus 24 hours. Require at least one eligible liver CT or MRI with examination `开始时间` in [(t_0-90) days, (t_0)); labs use [(t_0-30) days, (t_0)). No predictor at or after (t_0) is permitted.
5. Exclude prior HCC-directed resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, molecular therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during [(t_0-365) days, (t_0)). As in the parent, a sensitivity cohort retains these patients and adds frozen treatment-history indicators.
6. Calendar eras are fixed after the aggregate availability audit and before any model-performance calculation: development 2015–2020, calibration 2021, and locked transport evaluation 2022 through snapshot end 2025-11-11. Earlier years are used only to display label availability. This replaces the parent's infeasible use of 2022 for calibration after the observed documentation discontinuity. The aggregate availability of test-era labels has been inspected; test-era predictor-outcome performance has not.
7. All target patients receive both predictions, including (R=0). Development-only preprocessing, vocabulary, verification model specification, model penalties, calibration map, threshold, bounds, and gates are frozen before computing test outcomes. Patients and all their encounters stay in one era.

The examination timestamp records acquisition, not report finalization. A prespecified 72-hour decision-time sensitivity reruns the full feature extraction and frozen modeling protocol with (t_0^{72}=) surgery minus 72 hours. Stability there is required for any “planning-time” interpretation, but neither cutoff proves actual content availability at tumor board or consent; that requires source-system finalization timestamps or chart audit.

## Anchored pathology endpoint and adjudication

The label parser is a state machine, not a bag of keywords. It normalizes Unicode and punctuation; enters only a clinician-frozen assigned-value field such as `MVI提示风险分级` or `MVI病理分级`; captures its immediately following M0, M1, M2, M2a, or M2b value; maps M2a/M2b to M2; and terminates at the next field delimiter. It ignores explanatory legends, definitions, recommendations, and grade strings outside an assigned-value field. Multiple pathology rows are exact-deduplicated and combined only within the same patient-encounter; discordant assigned values set (R=0). A secondary high-sensitivity parser can nominate nonstandard phrases for review but cannot create primary labels.

Two qualified Chinese-reading pathology reviewers, blinded to preoperative predictors and predictions, independently review at least 100 random parser-assigned cases per class, every conflict, and 200 (R=0) cases in each of four fixed strata (2015–2020, 2021, 2022–2023, 2024–2025), oversampling positive-ungraded records. A third resolves disagreement. Against this review, each class needs PPV >=0.95 and sensitivity >=0.90, and kappa >=0.80. Review may recover a clearly stated grade missed by the parser; it may not infer M2 from “MVI present,” tumor features, recurrence, or treatment. Raw slides, block counts, sampled locations, and the pathology protocol are unavailable, so neither code nor text review can establish an unreported grade or sampling adequacy.

From the preliminary resection inception denominator, report annual attrition into no same-encounter pathology, non-HCC/mixed pathology, and pathology-confirmed HCC; within pathology-confirmed HCC report no MVI statement, explicit negative without grade, positive-ungraded, assigned M0/M1/M2, conflict, and parser uncertainty. Also report grade-field template fingerprints and `机器型号` distributions by year as documentation diagnostics, never as predictors.

## Models and fixed operating rule

The nested elastic-net logistic models and their clinical roles are retained.

**Structured baseline B:** age (`年龄`), sex (`性别`); maximum lesion diameter, tumor count (1, 2, >=3), CT-only/MRI-only/both, and explicit macrovascular tumor-thrombus language from the latest eligible CT/MRI; and last exact-assay albumin (`白蛋白`), total bilirubin (`总胆红素`), INR (`国际标准化比值`), and platelet count (`血小板计数`). Continuous variables use development-fitted transforms and explicit missingness indicators.

**Extension E:** every B variable plus rule-extracted capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, and enhancement pattern; development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF from `检查所见` and `检查诊断`; and last exact-assay AFP (`甲胎蛋白`), ALT (`丙氨酸氨基转移酶`), AST (`门冬氨酸氨基转移酶`), GGT (`γ-谷氨酰转移酶`), ALP (`碱性磷酸酶`), and creatinine (`肌酐`), all with missing indicators. Imaging combination, identifier/date/pathology stripping, copied-pathology exclusion, assay handling, n-gram audit, and within-assay/year-rank sensitivity are exactly as in the parent.

Training uses only (R=1) outcomes. Primary B and E losses are weighted by development-era inverse verification probabilities estimated as below, with identical folds, weights, model family, penalty selection, and calibration. Unweighted complete-case fits are sensitivity analyses. Both models predict every target patient. Flag if predicted M2 risk >=0.30; (w=p_t/(1-p_t)=3/7). The threshold is only an evaluative false-positive:false-negative tradeoff and still requires multidisciplinary preference elicitation before use.

## Verification-bias analysis and target estimand

The primary estimand remains paired incremental utility, but now in all locked-test target patients:

[
Delta NB_{0.30}=N^{-1}sum_i (A_{Ei}-A_{Bi}){Y_i-w(1-Y_i)},
]

where (A_E,A_B) are fixed threshold flags. For (R=1), the contribution is observed. For (R=0), compute sharp, assumption-free bounds: an E-only flag contributes in [-(w),1], a B-only flag in [-1,(w)], and concordant flags exactly zero. Sum observed contributions and these extrema over the full target denominator. Use 2,000 patient bootstraps of locked-test rows to report confidence limits for both bound endpoints. Apply the same construction to E versus flag-none and E versus flag-all. Publish counts of graded and ungraded E-only, B-only, both, and neither flags overall and by year; these counts make bound width auditable.

A secondary missing-at-random analysis estimates (e(Z)=P(R=1|Z)) separately in development/calibration and locked test using prespecified cross-fitted penalized logistic splines. (Z), observed for (R=1) and (R=0), consists only of index year/month, age, sex, operation class, eligible examination/lab counts and recency, modality, extracted size/count/macrovascular findings, all predictor missingness indicators, and within-assay/year ranks for the ten named labs. No pathology grade, MVI wording, postoperative treatment, recurrence, or outcome-dependent field enters (Z). Test utility uses Horvitz–Thompson contributions (R_i/e_i) divided by the full target (N); report untruncated and weight-truncated-at-10 estimates. A prespecified auxiliary analysis adds pathology row count and `机器型号` solely to the verification model to probe template-system selection; these never enter B or E.

Verification-overlap gates are: e in [0.05,0.95] for >=95% of target rows; <=5% of observed rows truncated at weight 10; effective weighted M2 events >=500 overall and >=100 in each modality stratum; weighted standardized differences <0.10 for every (Z) feature between (R=1) and the full target; and no test year with zero observed M2. These gates make the MAR estimate interpretable but do not prove (Yperp R|Z). Report selection-model discrimination, annual availability, standardized differences before/after weighting, weight distributions, effective sample size, and complete-case/IPW differences. A tipping-point sensitivity varies the odds of M2 among (R=0) versus (R=1), within year and B/E discordance stratum, over odds ratios 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, and 4. This is assumption-dependent and cannot supersede the sharp bounds.

## Drift, performance, and falsification gates

Before outcome evaluation, report target-population attrition separately from outcome observation. Required non-label gates retain the parent's standards: nonempty eligible report text >=80%, size/count jointly extractable >=70%, each baseline assay observed >=70%, each extension assay >=60% in every era; no missingness shift >15 percentage points; >=90% of test patients have at most one continuous predictor beyond development 0.5th–99.5th percentiles; each test modality has >=100 development patients; median test n-gram vocabulary coverage >=80%; every row receives both predictions; no leakage; and report-feature adjudication passes.

In (R=1), report paired delta-AUPRC, AUROC, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, Brier score, calibration-in-the-large, calibration slope, and year/calibration curves both complete-case and IPW-adjusted. Preserve supporting targets delta-AUPRC >=0.08, PPV >=0.50, sensitivity >=0.70, calibration-slope 95% CI within 0.80–1.20, and intercept 95% CI within -0.10–0.10. Report all sharp and IPW utility results by test year and modality, and descriptive checks by sex, age <65/>=65, AFP observed/missing, and M0 versus M1 controls. The 72-hour cutoff, unweighted fits, no free text, CT-only, MRI-only, within-assay/year ranks, high-sensitivity adjudicated labels, and previously treated cohort are prespecified sensitivities.

**Supportive:** all parser/adjudication, overlap, event, availability, shift, leakage, calibration, and decision-time gates pass; the lower 95% confidence limit for the sharp lower bound of target (Delta NB_{0.30}) exceeds 0.01; the IPW point estimate is >=0.01 with lower 95% bound >0; E exceeds flag-all and flag-none with lower bounds >0; delta-AUPRC and PPV/sensitivity targets pass; and no year's sharp lower point bound or IPW (Delta NB) is negative. The strongest allowed conclusion is that timestamp-eligible reports and labs add target-population M2 triage utility beyond structured burden/reserve at this center despite selective electronic grade availability, justifying independent prospective validation.

**Adverse:** all validity/overlap gates pass but the sharp upper 95% confidence limit or IPW upper 95% limit for (Delta NB) is <0.01; E is inferior to B or a default strategy; delta-AUPRC upper bound <=0; or a prespecified year/modality shows net harm. Conclude that E is unsuitable for this triage rule; do not rescue it with another threshold, complete-case-only results, or development performance.

**Inconclusive:** the sharp interval includes both material benefit and no benefit, bounds are widened by ungraded discordant flags, MAR overlap fails, weighted results disagree materially with sharp/complete-case results, a template/parser/adjudication gate fails, events are insufficient, calibration is unidentified, the 24-hour result is not stable at 72 hours, or any other drift/leakage gate fails. Report the estimates and exact reason; do not call this a negative or supportive validation.

## Verification contract and claim limits

Automatically checkable: source hashes/headers; frozen dictionaries and parser grammar; one-row-per-patient joins; target versus (R=1) attrition; calendar split; all feature timestamps; absence of pathology/post-(t_0) predictors; label-legend unit tests; identical B/E rows and threshold; discordance-cell counts; exact sharp-bound algebra; verification weights/overlap/effective sample size; metrics/CIs/gates; and links from every numeric conclusion to machine-readable outputs. An independent implementation must recompute labels, flags, bound endpoints, IPW estimates, and uncertainty.

Adversarial tests must reject: keyword-anywhere M2 labels from explanatory legends; treating (R=0) as M0; reporting complete-case (Delta NB) as target-population utility; a supportive claim when any bound/overlap/calendar gate fails; threshold switching after an adverse result; and any survival or treatment recommendation. Tests must include correct computations paired with unsupported supportive, adverse, and inconclusive narratives.

Not automatically checkable: whether ungraded specimens truly contain M2, adequacy/location of pathology sampling, clinical truth of phrase interpretation, whether an acquisition-timestamped report was finalized at the decision, acceptability of the 0.30 threshold, or benefit from model-guided care. Even supportive results do not establish that changing resection extent, surveillance, or neoadjuvant/adjuvant treatment improves recurrence, survival, quality of life, harms, or cost. Those claims require pathology protocol/slide review, source-system sign-off timestamps, independent-center prospective validation, preference elicitation, and an impact or randomized treatment study.
