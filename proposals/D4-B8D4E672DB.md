# Certified, baseline-only repeat kidney-status prioritization with equation and field provenance audit

## Successor purpose and substantive changes

This successor is a substantive child of the Lead-assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves their clinically consequential question and estimand: among people with complete baseline kidney markers who subsequently complete the first qualifying repeat assessment, does baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance improve prioritization of a low repeat creatinine-equation eGFR state beyond routinely available sex, age and baseline creatinine-equation eGFR? The target is the observed next qualifying repeat in the inclusive 2–8-year interval, marginal over its observed schedule; it is not a fixed-horizon risk, a causal effect of testing, or a CKD diagnosis.

The successor closes four substantive provenance/interpretation gaps found in the parent audit.

1. **Field lineage is made non-ambiguous.** The primary analysis uses the split tables, not the 57-billion-byte main export, and asserts that each selected field occurs in exactly one declared source table. The main export is a read-only cross-check only; it can never silently supply a value or resolve a conflict. Baseline age is explicitly `21003-0.0` and repeat age `21003-1.0`; `21022-0.0` is not substituted because it is a different field and the local metadata only certifies its label, not the semantics of 21003. A field-level certificate must separately certify these fields.

2. **Equation provenance is corrected and versioned.** The cystatin-C-only 2021 CKD-EPI exponent is frozen as `0.9961^Age` (not the rounded `0.996` in one parent), with a source excerpt and hash from Inker et al. (PMCID PMC8822996). The creatinine equation remains `0.9938^Age`. The protocol records the equation family, publication identifier, exact coefficient vector, unit convention and implementation hash; an equation citation cannot substitute for certification that UKB fields contain the required analytes and units.

3. **Certification and computability are separated.** A missing field certificate produces a deterministic technical stop for biological interpretation, but the executor may run a separately labelled `COMPUTATIONAL_AUDIT_ONLY` branch on raw numeric values to verify joins, masks, parsing, fold isolation and arithmetic. That branch must not calculate a biologically named eGFR or report prediction/decision results. This makes the available snapshot useful for executable QA without allowing unsupported assay assumptions to become clinical evidence. If the certificate passes, only then are eGFR values and model results released as aggregate scientific results.

4. **Schedule and selection interpretation is tightened.** The first qualifying repeat means the first available export instance satisfying the prespecified 2–8-year date rule, not the first chronological clinical test. Because exact event dates and testing decisions are unavailable, attendance and the observed schedule remain part of the target population; an attendance model is a sensitivity description, never inverse-probability identification of unobserved outcomes. Capacity results are global deployed-selection event contributions, not separate within-band policies. Threshold net benefit is explicitly assumption-indexed mathematics, not observed clinical utility.

The substantive advance is therefore not a new predictive algorithm. It is an evidence-bounded test that can distinguish (a) a reproducible baseline prioritization signal, (b) a technical non-result due to absent assay/date provenance, and (c) a negative signal, without confusing any of these with benefit from testing or persistent kidney disease.

## Evidence boundary and unresolved hypothesis

The strongest evidence available before this successor is that the parents define a feasible, baseline-only predictive experiment and that an earlier, pre-repair aggregate audit reproduced approximately 502,370 joined participants, 468,887 baseline-complete participants, 16,546 qualifying repeat-complete participants, 16,372 with baseline eGFRcr at least 60, and 309 repeat creatinine-equation threshold events. That earlier audit reported a near-zero 2% incremental net-benefit contrast and an uncertain 10% event-yield contrast, but it preceded the present certificate, equation and execution rules and is not evidence for this protocol.

The Inker et al. article supports that race-free creatinine and cystatin-C equations were developed and externally validated against measured GFR in research datasets; it does not certify the analyte identity, units, platform, missing codes, or instance-date meaning of these UKB export columns. The article also uses measured GFR and does not make an equation-derived eGFR threshold equivalent to CKD. The local UKB metadata explicitly says units and missing-value meanings are absent for the renal fields, that instance indices are not elapsed time, and that exact dates are server-only in the metadata. Consequently the unresolved, falsifiable claim is narrower:

> Conditional on a passing field/assay/date certificate, in complete UKB participants with baseline eGFRcr >=60 who have a first qualifying observed repeat at 2–8 years, adding baseline continuous `Z = log(eGFRcys/eGFRcr)` improves out-of-fold prioritization of `eGFRcr_1 < 60` by both a prespecified practical 2% threshold net-benefit floor of 0.001 and a 10% capacity event-yield floor of 5, without material calibration deterioration or null-compatible/leakage-only gains.

A failure of certification is neither support nor refutation. A certified negative result is evidence against the proposed incremental prioritization signal in this selected schedule-marginal population, subject to uncertainty and the prespecified practical floors.

## Exact equation and units

After certification, use float64 and the race-free 2021 CKD-EPI equations of Inker et al., DOI `10.1056/NEJMoa2102953`, PMID `34554658`, PMCID `PMC8822996`.

For serum creatinine `Scr` in mg/dL, `F=1` for certified female and `F=0` for certified male, use `(k,a)=(0.7,-0.241)` for female and `(0.9,-0.302)` for male:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^F`.

For cystatin C `Scys` in mg/L use:

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.9961^Age * 0.932^F`.

The certificate must establish creatinine in micromoles/L before the exact conversion `Scr = raw_30700 / 88.4`, or supply a certified alternative conversion. It must establish cystatin C in mg/L or supply a certified conversion. Retain raw values privately for audit. Do not use the 2012 cystatin equation, undocumented rounding, a race coefficient, or a substitution of 21022 for 21003. The equation source supports formulae and measured-GFR validation in its own development/validation data; it does not validate UKB assay comparability.

Define baseline `Z=log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)`, primary `Ycr`, and secondary `Ycys`. Record equation text, coefficient hash, source-cache identifier and implementation hash in aggregate output.

## Baseline-only models and evaluation

M0 contains only baseline sex, baseline age and baseline eGFRcr. M1 adds baseline continuous `Z`. No date, repeat age/lab, elapsed time, attendance, outcome, or future-derived tie-breaker may enter a score. Use a deterministic four-column restricted-cubic basis for age (bounds 40,80) and eGFRcr (bounds 60,120): clip x to bounds, knots at lower, one-third, two-thirds and upper, construct `b1=u`, `b2=d1-d3`, `b3=d2-d3`, `b4=d3`, where `d_j(t)=max(t-k_j,0)^3/(k4-k_j)`, and center/scale each column using training-fold mean and population SD (zero SD becomes 1). Standardize sex and Z with training moments. M0 therefore has 10 non-intercept columns and M1 11.

Fit `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(penalty='l2', C=1.0, solver='lbfgs', fit_intercept=True, class_weight=None, max_iter=1000, tol=1e-10, random_state=0)` in float64. Record exact Python, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn and patsy versions and lockfile hashes. Convergence warnings, nonfinite probabilities, or `n_iter_ >=1000` are hard execution failures, not silently repaired.

Use ten deterministic outcome-stratified five-fold repeats with seeds `104729,104759,104773,104779,104789,104801,104827,104831,104849,104851`. Stable-sort participants by `(Ycr,eid)` before round-robin assignment within each label; use ascending numeric eid as the only tie order. For each repeat, assign fold IDs 0–4 round-robin separately within labels. Reconstruct exactly the same procedure in each label permutation using the permuted label. Every prediction is held out, and M0/M1 predictions are paired for the same participants.

For q in `{0.01,0.02,...,0.20}`, select top `ceil(q*n)` by paired OOF risk and report capture count, event fraction, PPV and `DeltaC(q)`. The primary capacity region is contiguous 5–15%, with q=0.10 highlighted. For threshold grid `{0.001,0.0025,0.005,0.0075,0.010,0.015,0.020,0.030,0.040,0.050,0.075,0.100,0.150,0.200}`, report `NB=TP/n-FP/n*p_t/(1-p_t)`, DeltaNB, test-all and test-none. These are mathematical contrasts under an assumed threshold action, not clinical net benefit.

At 2%, report paired reclassification cells, direct-versus-entrant/leaver identities and repeat-specific contrasts. At 5% and 10%, allocate quotas across held-out folds by floor plus largest remainder, resolve equal remainders by fold index, rank within fold by stable descending prediction with eid ties. For n=16,372, quotas are 819 and 1,637. Report overlap, Jaccard, entrants/leavers, selected events and exact identity checks. Timing-band contributions restrict the already deployed global selections; they do not rerank within bands and are labelled event contributions.

Require intercept absolute value <=0.10, slope in [0.90,1.10], and absolute observed-minus-predicted risk <=0.002 for the calibration gate. If a software limitation requires L2 `C=1e12`, compare against direct binomial likelihood to 1e-6 and label the fallback.

## Uncertainty, nulls and falsification

Use 4,000 paired participant bootstrap draws with `default_rng(520243)`, carrying frozen outcomes, strata, timing bands, predictions and selections without refitting or reranking. Report 98% percentile intervals for DeltaNB at 2% and Delta event yield at 10%. For the exact secondary simultaneous family, use the parent eight-member order: DeltaNB at 1% and 5%, DeltaE5, borderline and far 10% global-selection contributions, and three timing-band DeltaNB-at-2% contrasts. Studentize each bootstrap deviation with ddof=1; zero/nonfinite SD is a hard failure. Use the 98th percentile of the maximum absolute studentized deviation and report point plus/minus critical value times SD.

Use 200 label permutations with seed `520242`, permuting Ycr within baseline eGFR strata `[60,75)` and `>=75`, reconstructing folds, refitting both models and recomputing all primary curves. Report statistic-specific null means, medians, 1st/99th percentiles and randomized-plus-one tails; do not force null contrasts to zero. Permute Z within baseline eGFRcr deciles and sex for the predictor-null; run reversed-label `1-Ycr`; run a private elapsed-time leakage sentinel that cannot be included in a score or policy result. A gain reproduced by the predictor-null, reversed label, or leakage sentinel is not supportive evidence.

Falsification is prospective: the hypothesis is unsupported if the certified observed incremental curve is null-compatible, practically below the prespecified floors, calibration worsens materially, gains occur only in an isolated q, supported strata disagree materially, or a null/leakage control reproduces the gain. Failure of data certification or nonfinite/incomplete computation is technical inconclusive, not adverse biological evidence.

## Gates and decision-value interpretation

A supportive predictive-prioritization result requires CERTIFIED status; exact hashes/joins/masks; complete valid computation; calibrated M1; completed uncertainty and nulls; a simultaneous 98% lower bound above zero throughout a contiguous 5–15% capacity region; stability in supported strata and repeats; observed statistics more favorable than corresponding fold-aware label nulls; no comparable predictor-null, reversed-label or leakage gain; and no material M1 calibration deterioration. A threshold result additionally requires DeltaNB at 2% point >=0.001, lower 98% bound above 0, at least 8/10 positive repeat contrasts, and M1 above test-none and test-all. Capacity requires DeltaE10 point >=5, lower 98% bound above 0, and at least 8/10 positive repeats. Both are required for the joint “worthwhile on both predeclared axes” label.

Adverse evidence is a certified, reproducible negative incremental curve with its upper interval below zero and at least 8/10 adverse repeats, or a certified calibration harm, provided computation and assay compatibility pass. Practical-floor failure with intervals not below zero is inconclusive rather than adverse. `STOP_FIELD_UNCERTIFIED`, anchor mismatch without a certified explanation, unstable timing strata, extreme selection weights/poor positivity, zero-SD bootstrap failure, or incomplete nulls are technical inconclusive.

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema hashes, field presence, joins, row uniqueness, certificate status, masks, parsing diagnostics, equation implementation, baseline-only allowlist, fold isolation, convergence, predictions, calibration, capacities, threshold arithmetic, bootstrap/null completion and gate consistency. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for assay traceability not supplied by the certificate, persistent CKD/chronicity, measured GFR accuracy, true first clinical testing, non-attender outcomes, causal benefit or harm from repeat testing, threshold appropriateness, cost-effectiveness, patient preferences, treatment changes, and transportability. Even supportive output is conditional on this selected complete-repeat-attender population and observed schedule; it does not justify ordering a test or changing care.
