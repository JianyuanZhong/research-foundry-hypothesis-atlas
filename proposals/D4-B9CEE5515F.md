P2: Does baseline log creatinine unexpectedly high conditional on baseline log cystatin-C and the same certified core covariates predict higher repeat log cystatin-C conditional on baseline log cystatin-C?

EXPOSURES, OUTCOMES, AND ESTIMANDS
After positivity checks, use natural-log concentrations. Assign deterministic folds by SHA-256: integer(first eight hexadecimal characters of SHA256("renal-fold-v1|" + eid)) modulo 10. Folds are invariant to row order and are assigned before preprocessing.

For P1, within each nine-fold training set regress baseline log cystatin-C on a natural cubic regression spline of baseline log creatinine, recruitment age, sex, age-by-sex, and baseline-center indicators. Apply the training design to the held-out fold; define R_cys|cr as held-out observed minus predicted log cystatin-C divided by the training residual SD. The final stacked held-out model predicts repeat log creatinine standardized by the training-fold outcome mean/SD from R_cys|cr, a training-fold natural cubic spline of baseline log creatinine, recruitment age, sex, age-by-sex, a training-fold natural cubic spline of actual elapsed time, fold indicators, and identifiable accepted center terms. Beta1 is repeat-log-creatinine SD per training residual SD of unexpectedly high baseline cystatin-C.

For P2, reverse creatinine and cystatin-C: residualize baseline log creatinine on baseline log cystatin-C and the same core covariates, then predict training-standardized repeat log cystatin-C conditional on baseline log cystatin-C and the same terms. Beta2 is repeat-log-cystatin-C SD per training residual SD of unexpectedly high baseline creatinine.

Natural cubic splines were used because they extrapolate linearly for a held-out extreme outside training-fold boundary knots; training-fold B-splines failed rather than leaking held-out extrema into knot construction. All continuous spline construction and outcome standardization are learned outside each held-out fold. One stacked estimating regression is fit per direction; fold coefficients are not averaged. The current script’s HC3 intervals, partial R-squared, and stacked-sample RMSE deltas are diagnostics. The RMSE deltas are not an independently refitted outer-loop predictive assessment, and log predictive density has not yet been computed.

PRIMARY INFERENCE AND HOLM LOGIC
The unchanged required primary inference is a participant bootstrap with at least 1,000 replicates. Each replicate resamples participants and reruns fold-specific residualization, training-fold spline construction, scaling, and the stacked final regression; original deterministic fold assignments may remain fixed. For each direction, report beta, a two-sided full-pipeline bootstrap 95% interval, and a one-sided bootstrap p-value for H: beta<=0. Apply Holm correction across exactly P1 and P2 at familywise alpha 0.05. Also report HC3 inference explicitly as diagnostic.

A shared bidirectional observable-persistence conclusion requires both Holm-adjusted one-sided tests to reject and both direction-specific simultaneous lower bounds to exceed zero. One significant direction is not a shared signal. The provisional smallest relevant standardized association is 0.05, not a validated clinical threshold. A material bidirectional label requires both adjusted lower bounds above 0.05. If both directions are positive but either estimate is below 0.05 or its adjusted interval includes 0.05, report statistically supported persistence of small or uncertain substantive magnitude, never clinical usefulness. A precise adjusted upper bound below 0.05 in either direction falsifies the material bidirectional claim while allowing a smaller positive association.

Two biomarker waves do not identify a latent GFR slope or decompose stable participant renal level, stable marker-specific non-GFR determinants, visit-specific biological variation, assay error, same-visit cross-marker error covariance, or cross-visit error covariance. Cross-fitting and cross-marker ANCOVA reduce direct mathematical coupling but do not remove correlated biological or assay error. There is no empirically calibrated no-decline null and no latent-GFR estimand. The following claims are prohibited from these results: measured GFR decline; CKD progression; acute kidney injury; causality; renal specificity without the matched paired bootstrap and biological review; clinical benefit from ordering cystatin-C; improved staging, dosing, referral, or outcomes; assay stability beyond the inspected documentation; transportability outside eligible repeat attenders; or representativeness of the UK population.

FALSIFIABLE RESULT BRANCHES
Supportive for observable bidirectional persistence: source, field, assay, and date integrity pass; both full-pipeline bootstrap directional tests reject after Holm correction; both simultaneous lower bounds exceed zero; temporal sensitivity does not reverse either estimate; converged attendance sensitivity does not reverse either estimate; and available baseline-center checks do not show one-center dependence. Controls need not be null, but any unresolved similarity must remain explicit. A material label requires both simultaneous lower bounds above 0.05.

Adverse/falsifying for shared persistence: either adjusted upper bound is at or below zero; P1/P2 signs conflict; a correctly implemented stratified permutation test contradicts nominal inference; a prespecified temporal sensitivity reverses a direction; or baseline-center diagnostics attribute the result to one center. A precise adjusted upper bound below 0.05 in either direction falsifies material bidirectional persistence even if both coefficients are positive. The present P2 HC3 interval is diagnostically adverse to the material label but is not the required bootstrap decision.

Inconclusive: required date/field/assay certification fails; bootstrap intervals include both zero and relevant positive effects; only one direction is supported; primary bootstrap is unexecuted; attendance optimization/positivity is unacceptable; weighted and unweighted findings materially conflict; paired control differences are imprecise or similar; or center/date structure cannot be adequately evaluated. Inconclusive is not evidence of absence.

They provide evidence that the inherited experiment is computationally feasible under approximately 925 MB peak resident memory after narrow projection. At present, the strongest defensible interpretation is: positive diagnostic bidirectional observable persistence among 16,546 eligible repeat attenders, with P2 diagnostically too small for the provisional material threshold and with weak unresolved P2 separation from matched controls. The experiment remains inferentially inconclusive pending full-pipeline bootstrap/Holm inference, paired control bootstraps, convergence-verified attendance sensitivity, baseline-center checks, and completion of the remaining implementation-validity diagnostics.
