## Scientific opening and decision relevance

The supported claim at present is narrow: in the inherited first dated exact-code TACE population, a day-42 assay trajectory may predict a later operational TACE record better than a day-42 assay snapshot after accounting for baseline severity, assay opportunity, observation and care workflow. The available evidence does not support calling that record treatment failure, radiographic progression, retreatment intent, or benefit.

The unresolved claim is whether any trajectory increment persists when a later TACE record is required to have evidence of downstream same-visit activity after the procedure, and whether the increment is absent from a pre-procedure-order-only documentation pattern. The leading explanation is that a physiologically informative trajectory predicts a subsequent coherent procedure episode. The strongest rival is a workflow/selection explanation: clinicians order, schedule, document, and monitor high-intensity care in ways that create both the assay trajectory and the literal code, without the code itself identifying a completed, intended, or response-driven TACE episode.

This matters because a positive O0 result alone could encourage a clinically misleading interpretation of the assay trajectory. If O3 agrees with O0 while the planned-care negative control does not, the next decision can justify blinded chart/intent/imaging validation of a narrower operational prediction. If O0 is positive but O3 and the negative-control contrast move together, the direction should be redirected toward workflow and endpoint acquisition rather than clinical assay interpretation.

[K1] shows that longitudinal records can encode useful temporal structure while also inheriting data-collection bias. [K2] motivates explicit hazard and selection handling for longitudinal EHR occurrence processes. [K3]’s inspected supplement makes missingness, data quality, and testing-related shortcuts an explicit concern in routine clinical prediction. None of these works validates a local HCC TACE code or supplies unavailable intent/response evidence; this child adds a direct provenance check to the parent’s assay-content question.

### Added provenance endpoint O3

Add one predeclared endpoint-provenance refinement without replacing O0/O1/O2:

> **O3 post-procedure-coherent TACE:** the first later candidate that satisfies inherited O2 and has at least one qualifying same-patient/same-visit corroborator whose event/start time `u` is strictly after `tp` and no later than `tp+72 hours`.

The strict inequality `u>tp` prevents a same-second order or examination from being called downstream evidence. The 72-hour limit is frozen before held-out scoring and is a provenance window, not a change to the [43,181) clinical estimand. A qualifying post-procedure source is one of:

- `examinations.start time` with nonmissing time, joined on the exact procedure visit key;
- `orders.start time` with nonmissing time and an order status that is not blank and does not contain the normalized cancellation tokens `cancelled`, `voided`, `revoked`, or `discontinued`;
- `medications.start time` with nonmissing time, preserving `drug type` and `medication method` for audit but not inventing a drug-specific treatment meaning.

No free-text semantic dictionary is introduced by this repair. The parent’s O1/O2 semantic predicates and same-visit rules are reused exactly; the added information is only source family and temporal direction. A patient can have O0 without O3; such a patient remains an O0 event and is not relabeled as a clinical non-event. ### Planned-care negative control

The order’s `Start time` and `End time` are retained in the audit; `Order time` is the scheduling/documentation signal. The candidate may also have pre-procedure examination or medication rows, but no post-procedure qualifying source is allowed. This label is a negative-control outcome for workflow/planned documentation, not evidence of a treatment event.

If a candidate satisfies both an O3 rule and the preplanned rule because of multiple source rows, classify it as O3 and report the overlap audit; it is not used as a planned-only negative-control event. If the status or time needed for the order rule is missing, classify the candidate as `unresolved_provenance`, not as planned-only. Keep counts of O0-only, O3, O_preplanned, overlap, and unresolved classes. This conservative missingness rule prevents missing downstream documentation from being silently treated as planned care.

The learned alternative remains the compact time-aware GRU: the same eight assay labels, event content, sample type, relative time, inter-event time and opportunity tag from `t0-365` through `t0+42`, with M0/workflow covariates at the hazard head. One GRU layer, hidden size 32, dropout 0.10, two-layer head 32/16, Adam 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, at most 50 epochs, eight-epoch early stopping, and seeds 17/29/41 remain fixed. It receives the same O0/O1/O2/O3/O_preplanned targets, right-censoring masks, weights, folds and output contrasts as the baseline. A time-only ablation and within-patient content permutation remain fixed.

The alternative comparison is scientifically necessary because a simple pooled hazard can audit endpoint provenance and feature-block contribution transparently, while the GRU can test whether ordered, irregular assay patterns produce a reproducible increment that deterministic summaries lose. Neither method can identify intent or mechanism. A GRU gain without O3/negative-control separation is representation-sensitive workflow prediction, not evidence of liver biology.

The repair is supportive only if all of the following are met:

1. O0 and O1 retain the parent’s positive, simultaneous lower-bound trajectory-versus-snapshot contrast after R42 weighting, with ordinary estimates directionally consistent.
2. O3 passes its event/ESS/overlap support gate and shows the same direction in both elastic-net and GRU, with uncertainty not materially wider solely from method instability.
3. The O3 increment survives the parent’s late-window removal `(t0+35,t0+42]`, R28 arm, workflow-only and time-only comparisons, and within-patient content permutation.
4. O_preplanned has no comparable positive trajectory increment, or its contrast is clearly weaker under the predeclared simultaneous resolution rule. A positive O3 result accompanied by a similar O_preplanned result is adverse to the coherent-event interpretation.
5. O0/O1/O2/O3 provenance counts and source-family patterns are stable across the untouched temporal test set and do not depend on a favorable subgroup or method.

Supportive output establishes only that day-42 assay content adds held-out information for an operational endpoint with stronger local downstream documentation and is less compatible with the specific pre-opened-order-only artifact. It does not establish radiographic response, retreatment intent, treatment failure, causal effect, mechanism, or benefit.

Adverse output includes: the O3 event support gate failing; O3 eliminating the increment while O0 remains positive; O3 matching the workflow/time/permutation controls; O_preplanned showing the same increment; R42 weighting or R28/late removal erasing the effect; or elastic-net/GRU direction instability. This would retain at most a narrower association with recorded care workflow and justify acquisition of intent/response data rather than extra model complexity.

Inconclusive output includes insufficient O3 events or ESS, unresolved order status/time dominating candidates, high post-source missingness, wide simultaneous intervals, or endpoint-class instability. Do not change the 72-hour window, select a subgroup, or choose a favorable seed after seeing test results. Freeze the repair and seek expert adjudication or better longitudinal capture.

## Interpretation boundary and next decision

O3 is a targeted repair of the endpoint interpretation threat, not a claim that downstream documentation proves completion or intent. A supportive result warrants a blinded clinical adjudication study using procedure intent, imaging, and outside-care capture, or prospective validation of the post-procedure source pattern. An adverse result warrants shifting the study toward workflow/observation measurement and not increasing model complexity. An inconclusive result warrants deferral until the missing timing, status, intent, response, or outside-care evidence is obtained.

The unavailable evidence is explicit: no images or radiology reports suitable for response adjudication, no validated procedure-intent/scheduling field, no reliable timed narrative temporality, no outside-care capture, no treatment indication, no dose/technical TACE details, no toxicity, survival, utility, or patient benefit.
