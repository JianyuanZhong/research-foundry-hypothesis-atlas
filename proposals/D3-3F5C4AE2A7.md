# Directional macrophage→FLS communication in RA: does macrophage STATE imprint a reproducible FLS transcriptional response?

Seed parent: D3-M2 ([prior hypothesis]). Domain: disease_mechanisms.
Claim stage: hypothesis. Screening: executed (bounded CPU probe, [research job]).

## Unresolved question

RA synovial pathology is driven by crosstalk between synovial tissue macrophages and
fibroblast-like cells (FLS). Prior work established that (i) a defined macrophage state
(HBEGF+ inflammatory macrophages) promotes fibroblast invasiveness in an EGFR-dependent
manner [K1], (ii) MerTK-defined macrophage subsets differ across active RA and remission
and carry homeostatic vs inflammatory function [K2], and (iii) a fibroblast-intrinsic
NOTCH3 program drives the pathogenic FLS identity [K3]. What remains unresolved and
quantitatively untested is: **does the macrophage STATE (MerTK/CD206pos "Mpos" vs
MerTK/CD206neg "Mneg") imprint a reproducible, DIRECTIONAL transcriptional response in
FLS that is distinguishable from donor identity, culture effects, and macrophage
contamination?**

The leading claim is state-specific directionality: Mneg (inflammatory) macrophages
imprint a stronger, more reproducible FLS response than Mpos (homeostatic) macrophages.
The strongest rival is that the observed "FLS response" is dominated by (a) donor
identity / culture effects, (b) coculture per se (any macrophage), or (c) differential
macrophage contamination of the FLS-only baseline. A third, fibroblast-intrinsic rival
is that the FLS program is NOTCH3-driven and independent of macrophage state [K3].

## Clinical importance

Identifying which macrophage state drives the pathogenic FLS program would (i) explain
why some RA patients' synovium is more invasive, (ii) suggest which macrophage state a
therapy must target (or preserve in remission [K2]), and (iii) provide a donor-controlled
readout for testing whether a drug shifts the FLS response (the Inhibitor arms in the
data are a first step). A useful negative result — no state-specific directionality —
would redirect effort toward fibroblast-intrinsic [K3] or composition-based mechanisms.

## Estimand and analysis

Primary estimand: per-FLS-donor paired contrast log2(FLS+Mpos/FLS-only) vs
log2(FLS+Mneg/FLS-only), sign concordance across donors (baseline: transparent
per-gene effect sizes + sign concordance). Learned/mechanistic alternative: rank the
donor-consensus response genes and test enrichment for candidate ligand-receptor
programs (HBEGF/EGF-pathway genes present in panel: ADAM17, COL1A1/2, COL3A1, MMP1/3/9/13;
NOTCH3 program ABSENT from panel — enrichment not computable there). The alternative
reveals pathway-level mechanism attribution that per-gene concordance loses.
Split: discovery in E-MTAB-8873 only; concordance in GSE95588/E-MTAB-8316 pre-specified,
never used for feature selection.

## Executed probe (observed, [research job])

- Mneg-specific signature (>=4/5 FLS donors up, <=1/5 Mpos up): 41 genes, mean log2FC
  1.128, permutation p=0.021 (10,000 perms, panel-preserving background). Includes
  CCR2, CD1B, CD80, CXCR4, FCN1, IL1B, IL7R, LIF, S100A12, TREM1, COL3A1, MMP13,
  TGFBR1, TNFSF13 (pro-inflammatory/chemotactic/matrix-remodeling program).
- Mpos-specific signature: 4 genes (APOE, CD7, IL25, NCAM1), p=0.236 — not significant.
  Directional asymmetry (Mneg >> Mpos) is itself an observation.
- preLPS and MerTK-Inhibitor arms: INCONSISTENT per-donor direction on the Mneg-specific
  set → LPS spillover and MerTK inhibition are not consistent drivers.
- Mixed-ratio dose-response (2 donors): 1Mpos:3Mneg > 3Mpos:1Mneg on the Mneg-specific
  set — consistent with a dose-dependent Mneg effect (underpowered, n=2).
- Macrophage contamination: FLS-only samples contain CD14 (90–559), CD163 (14–201),
  ITGAX (5–129) MolsPerCell → the FLS-only baseline is NOT pure FLS. This is the
  STRONGEST competing explanation and is not yet excluded.

## Falsification (exact)

- Supportive: >=5 genes with consistent within-donor direction (>=4/5 donors,
  |mean log2FC| >= 0.25) AND concordant direction in >=1 external dataset AND >=1
  candidate program enriched (permutation p<0.05) in the state-specific set.
- Adverse: no such gene set; direction flips across donors; external datasets opposite;
  or the state-specific signature is fully explained by macrophage contamination.
- Inconclusive: <3 donors with a paired arm; external concordance only on unresolved
  donor columns; contamination not excludable with available data.
Current status: discovery-side gene-count/consistency thresholds met for Mneg (41 genes,
p=0.021); external concordance and program enrichment NOT yet computed; contamination
NOT yet excluded → overall INCONCLUSIVE, with a concrete next test.

## What each outcome would and would not establish

- Supportive: establishes state-specific directionality in the bound coculture system;
  would NOT establish the ligand (HBEGF vs others) or in vivo relevance without the
  contamination control and external concordance, and without wet-lab validation.
- Adverse: would argue against a macrophage-state-driven FLS imprint on this panel and
  shift weight to fibroblast-intrinsic [K3] or composition mechanisms; would NOT refute
  macrophage effects measured by other assays.
- Inconclusive: underpowered or confounded; next tests above are the decision rule.
