The strongest available evidence supports only a stored-corpus claim. Current full-text reviews motivate the question but do not establish this endpoint or threshold. These findings support rigorous utility evaluation; they do not validate HCC's documentary M2, Chinese reader semantics, `p*=0.10`, 80/80, K, or benefit.

Unresolved hypothesis: in each of 2020 and 2021, for every coherent source/pathology/reader world and every version-faithful mask under which at least 80% of the complete patient roster has at least `ceil(.8*n_i)` of its own eligible whole report units exposed, the frozen semantics-augmented G policy will (a) retain all three inherited top-K capture margins strictly above five per 100 review positions and (b), at the prespecified documentary-review threshold `p*=0.10`, have strictly positive capacity-constrained net benefit and strictly greater net benefit than B, R, and same-fit Gmask, both undeleted and after anchored deletion of any one test patient.

This is decision-relevant because it asks whether report semantics improve allocation of a limited specialist-review queue after charging for unnecessary reviews, rather than merely enriching a forced top-K list. It does not test whether review changes management or benefits patients.

For rational threshold `t`, the full-roster policy is

`A_M(t) = the first min(K, #{i:p_Mi>=t}) patients ranked by the inherited score among those with p_Mi>=t`.

Use the inherited outcome-blind tie convention and enumerate every admissible top-K tie completion. Thus the policy may abstain, never exceeds K, and does not force review below the utility threshold. Confirmatory `p*=1/10`: one documentary-M2 capture receives weight 1 and one reviewed Y=0 receives weight `p*/(1-p*)=1/9`; positive net benefit is equivalent to a realized review yield above 10%. This threshold is an investigational value judgment—willingness to spend up to ten reviews to find one documentary-M2 case—not a stakeholder-endorsed treatment threshold.

## Primary and decision-curve estimands

Retain the parent's forced-quota ranking endpoint

`T_M=100/K * sum_i Y_i I(i in top-K_M)`

and all six year-specific `Delta_B`, `Delta_R`, `Delta_mask`; every required endpoint must remain strictly `>5`. This preserves the clinically material enrichment contract and exposes whether any new decision result is driven only by abstention.

For each capped policy, compute per 100 complete eligible patients

`NB_M(t)=100/N * [TP_M(t) - t/(1-t)*FP_M(t)]`.

At `p*=.10`, the new confirmatory coordinates are absolute `NB_G>0` (review-none) and `dNB_C=NB_G-NB_C>0` for C in B, R and same-fit Gmask, separately in 2020 and 2021. Equality fails. Comparator arms share the same roster, world, reader pair, mask and deletion. Report review count, TP, FP, PPV, sensitivity among anchored Y, number needed to review, NB, and dNB. Also report the expected capacity-matched random-K policy and conventional review-all curve only as labeled references; review-all is infeasible when N>K and cannot satisfy support.

The prespecified decision curve uses `t in {1/20,3/40,1/10,1/8,3/20,1/5}` (.05-.20), with .10 confirmatory and all other thresholds sensitivity diagnostics. No threshold is selected from test results. Show separate years and pointwise attained minima/maxima; do not average over worlds, readers, masks or years.

## Falsification and interpretation

Let Q mean every source/hash, chronology, roster, reader, leakage, fit/calibration, mask, solver, replay and conclusion-link gate passes; A means every required 80/80 family is nonempty. Let Fcap be any inherited margin <=5. Let Fnb be any `NB_G(.10)<=0` or any `dNB_C(.10)<=0`. Define full-content failures analogously.

- Supportive: Q and A and no Fcap or Fnb in any undeleted/deleted coordinate. Strongest conclusion: in this frozen stored corpus, under the declared 1:9 documentary-review tradeoff and every enumerated uncertainty/missing-content state, G robustly improves a capacity-capped documentary-M2 review queue. It supports obtaining report-version/readability and prospective silent-workflow data next; it does not support deployment.
- Adverse: Q and A and a full-content Fcap or Fnb. If `NB_G<=0`, the proposed G review policy is no better than reviewing nobody at the specified tradeoff in at least one required state. If only dNB fails, semantic G does not uniformly beat that comparator. A failure confined to allowed nonmaximum masks is visibility-brittle. None proves report semantics generally useless or harmful.
- Inconclusive: Q fails, any required mask family is empty, or minimum sample/event/grade gates fail. An empty family is structural patient-sufficiency infeasibility, not robustness. Missing operational archive evidence is `not_estimable`, never evidence that a report was unavailable.

Report an exclusive primary label (`inconclusive`, `patient-sufficiency infeasible`, `maximum-content non-supportive`, `patient-sufficiency visibility-brittle`, `decision-robust supportive`) plus nonexclusive mechanism flags (`capture`, `NB<=0`, `B`, `R`, `Gmask`, `threshold abstention`). Curves may reveal utility at another threshold but cannot overturn the .10 label.

It must not be silently automated. The `p*=.10` 1:9 value and 80/80 rule are prespecified research choices, not clinically endorsed thresholds; hepatobiliary radiology/surgery, workflow and patient/stakeholder review are required before prospective use.
