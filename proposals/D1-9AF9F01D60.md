# Episode 8 provisional hypothesis: discordant AKI recovery beyond current severity

Status: provisional and untested; no cohort count, effect estimate, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adults with an ICU stay and a prespecified creatinine-defined AKI episode who survive to a fixed 48-hour landmark, persistent creatinine improvement without concurrent urine-output improvement will identify higher subsequent renal deterioration or in-hospital death than concordant recovery, after adjustment for the same landmark severity, treatment state, and observation intensity. The claim is incremental prognostic information, not a mechanism, causal treatment effect, or proof of true renal recovery.

## Evidence boundary

The persistent MIMIC-IV branch and the expert seed support the measurement concern that creatinine and urine output can change discordantly and that creatinine-based recovery may be misleading in critical illness. The unresolved claim is whether a persistent discordant pattern adds information beyond current severity and measurement intensity. Existing evidence does not establish prevalence, an outcome association, or that discordance is biologically protective or harmful.

## Falsifiable test and alternatives

Use a leakage-safe landmark: define AKI and the 48-hour recovery state from data available by the landmark, then measure subsequent deterioration/death after it. Compare a current-state baseline (landmark creatinine, urine output, severity, treatment and observation intensity) with a persistent-trajectory alternative that uses the pre-landmark creatinine/urine-output trajectory. A supportive result requires incremental held-out discrimination/calibration and a prespecified clinically meaningful discordance contrast with uncertainty; an adverse result is no incremental information or a reversed contrast; an inconclusive result is imprecision, inadequate event/measurement density, or instability across measurement-density sensitivity analyses. The trajectory alternative can reveal whether the pattern is persistent rather than a noisy cross-sectional mismatch, which the baseline loses.

## Data binding and missing evidence

The experiment will be bound to the configured MIMIC-IV source archive and its documented ICU, lab, and outcome tables, using the documented patient/stay join keys and timestamps. Exact item IDs, final AKI/deterioration definitions, cohort feasibility, and measurement-density adequacy must be verified before execution. Measured GFR/cystatin C, fluid-status adjudication, and causal treatment effects are not available as established evidence here and must not be inferred.

## Method comparison

A simple baseline is a prespecified logistic/Cox model using only landmark measurements and clinical covariates. The substantive alternative is a trajectory model using the pre-landmark creatinine and urine-output sequence, with the same outcome, split, uncertainty evaluation, and held-out test. The alternative is selected only if it tests the scientific uncertainty of persistence versus current severity; it is not justified by model complexity or a small predictive gain. CPU is adequate for the planned tabular/sequence fits; GPU is not required unless a later sequence implementation demonstrates a material bottleneck.

## Actual deliverable

The future solver must fit both models on the same eligible cohort, report the landmark cohort and event/measurement counts, estimate the discordant-versus-concordant contrast with uncertainty, and produce held-out discrimination/calibration and measurement-density sensitivity outputs. Completion is established by these computed outputs, not by confirming the hypothesis.
