# Catalog-bounded outcome-observation and proxy feasibility repair for frozen HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B validation

## Purpose and substantive change

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s frozen repeat-TACE cohort, event reconstruction, clocks, complete-triplet primary endpoint, nested forward models, noncausal estimand, annual support gates, 2022-only supportive interpretation, and later-era inconclusive interpretation. It does not replace, augment, or relabel Y with another clinical endpoint.

The repair closes two feasibility gaps before any missing-Y robustness claim is made:

1. It binds a full-catalog, read-only availability audit to exact HCC source files, tables, columns, keys, and source hashes, and requires its counts to be retained as provenance.
2. It adds a prespecified proxy-substitution firewall. Administrative, order, diagnosis, examination, pathology, and narrative-document fields may be used only as outcome-observation/context diagnostics after an explicit temporal and semantic screen. None may enter the primary RMSE estimand or be called a clinical outcome unless independent clinical adjudication establishes validity.

The new audit has now been executed over all rows in the substantive HCC sources. Its aggregate output is `[internal dataset path]`, [source checksum]. It is an availability audit, not evidence that any proxy is clinically valid.

## Question and claims

The strongest supported claim remains limited to the observed complete-triplet population: the parent’s outcome-blind audit found 242 BP-eligible episodes per assay; 2022 had 29 BP-eligible episodes (10 systemic, 19 comparator), 28 observed outcomes, and 0.966 observation; 2023 had 43 BP-eligible episodes (9 systemic, 34 comparator) and 29 observed outcomes; 2024 had 70 (9 systemic, 61 comparator) and 14 observed outcomes. Later blocks are not transport evidence because observation and systemic-arm support collapse.

The unresolved question is whether the frozen prognostic contrast can be bounded under training-only assay-range completions, while determining whether any available non-Y field supplies a temporally aligned, semantically defensible clinical outcome proxy. The experiment tests this question; it does not claim that missing Y is missing at random, does not impute a clinical outcome, and cannot establish treatment effects, safety, survival, mortality, utility, actionability, or patient benefit.

## Frozen cohort, endpoint, and clocks

Reconstruct exactly the parent’s 319 systemic patients, 1,491 comparator patients, 159 selected systemic repeat events, 526 selected comparator repeat events, and complete-triplet counts of 40/118 for albumin and 41/119 for total bilirubin. Match case-insensitive `TACE` or literal `化疗栓塞`, require valid procedure `开始时间`, collapse same-patient same-calendar-day duplicates, and retain the first eligible adjacent 14–180-day TACE1/TACE2 pair and strict repeat event 15–90 days after TACE2. Apply the inherited medication ontology and exclusions without changing it.

Use exact assay clocks. B is the latest valid exact assay (`白蛋白` or `总胆红素`) on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1; P is the latest valid assay in the selected repeat encounter in `[event_time−72 hours,event_time)`; Y is the valid assay in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, nearest +24 hours under the inherited deterministic tie rule. Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`, positive P lead, one patient-assay row, and outcome-blind B/P eligibility. The primary models and estimand remain:

```text
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
standardized held-out gain = [RMSE(M0)-RMSE(M1)] / SD_test(Y)
```

The primary fit, folds, tuning, predictions, paired patient-level bootstrap, and complete-triplet denominator are unchanged.

## Missing-Y bounds

For each assay and origin, fit only on pre-origin observed complete triplets. Define `L_train=min(Y_train)` and `U_train=max(Y_train)` after the exact parser and before reading future outcomes. A degenerate range or any observed future Y outside that range makes the temporal bound unsupported; do not widen it. A full-source assay range may be shown only as a secondary non-temporal descriptive check.

For every future BP-eligible row, retain fixed M0/M1 predictions and observed/missing status. For missing Y, use no single imputation. For model j, the missing-row squared-error minimum is at `clip(pj,L_train,U_train)` and maximum is at one of the endpoints. For the direct M0-minus-M1 error contrast, use its affine endpoint extrema. Sum observed errors and missing-row extrema to obtain sharp all-BP-eligible MSE intervals. Report observed complete-triplet RMSE gain separately, because `SD_test(Y)` is not identified when Y is missing.

Report overall and by descriptive arm: observed counts, missing counts and fractions, Wilson intervals, bounded MSE/RMSE-difference intervals, all-missing-at-L and all-missing-at-U patterns, per-row least-favorable endpoint pattern, and the smallest common displacement from the observed-case mean that reaches zero when such a displacement exists in `[L_train,U_train]`. These are mathematical support scenarios, not plausible clinical imputations.

## Proxy screen and allowed use

Before any proxy is considered, require all of the following: a native timestamp or a deterministic encounter-time inheritance documented in advance; one-to-one episode-level aggregation without many-to-many multiplication; a prespecified semantic rule; outcome-blind construction; a denominator reported among BP-eligible episodes; and an independent adjudication plan. A field failing any requirement is a context/observation diagnostic only.

Allowed diagnostics are:

* `encounters.入院时间`, `出院时间`, and `就诊科室`: contact opportunity, discharge-record availability, and length-of-stay-style descriptors. They do not establish survival, hepatic failure, or benefit.
* `diagnoses.诊断名称`, `诊断类型`: encounter-level disease-context and documentation labels. `死亡诊断` may trigger manual mortality ascertainment but cannot be counted as validated death without chart/external adjudication.
* `examinations.检查`, `检查所见`, `检查诊断`, `开始时间`, `检查号`: report-presence and exploratory report-language descriptors. They cannot be called imaging response because images and validated tumor-burden measurements are unavailable.
* `clinical_documents.诊疗经过`, `出院情况`, `出院诊断`, `手术经过`: documentation-presence or exploratory lexical flags only. No native document time and the configured text detector is not validated for comprehensive diagnosis, temporality, or outcome extraction.
* `pathology` report fields: disease-description context only; no dynamic post-event response endpoint.
* `medications` and `orders`: recorded order-pattern and contact-intensity diagnostics only. They are not administration records and cannot establish toxicity, treatment delivery, or response.

Do not fit these diagnostics as competing endpoints, append them to M0/M1, use them to define Y, or use them to rescue a failed support gate. No additional field in the catalog meets the current standard for a validated clinical outcome proxy that can be substituted without changing the scientific schema. The exact counts above make this conclusion auditable rather than based on dataset name alone.

## Falsification and interpretation

A bounded result is supportive only if reconstruction and clocks match, the block passes inherited outcome-blind and complete-triplet gates, the parent’s observed-case criterion is met, and the all-BP-eligible bounded contrast retains its sign without material arm-specific reversal. If the interval contains zero, label the result selection-sensitive; if a supported block reverses under an admissible endpoint, the observed contrast is not robust. If a support gate fails, a training range is unsupported, test SD is unidentified, or an arm is sparse, label the result inconclusive/unbounded regardless of point estimate.

The 2022 result may be interpreted as supportive only for the selected short-horizon prognostic association if all locked criteria pass. 2023 and 2024 remain inconclusive under the parent’s observation rates and arm counts, especially 2024 systemic with one observed complete outcome. A favorable bounded sign does not establish transport across eras, causal effects, assay clinical validity, mortality, toxicity, imaging response, survival, utility, or patient benefit. Clinical adjudication and an independent later/external/prospective cohort with assay units/reference ranges and reliable outcome capture would be required for those claims.

## Compiler and verifier requirements

The compiler must consume the five frozen endpoint sources plus the catalog-audit artifact as provenance; it may read the other four substantive sources only for the explicitly bounded proxy screen. It must emit aggregate JSON/CSV results with source hashes, exact denominator assertions, proxy availability counts, support flags, bounds, tipping points, and branch labels. Outputs must contain no patient identifiers, raw laboratory values, raw notes, or free text.

The verifier must check full scans and hashes; exact event, assay, and temporal filters; one-event-per-patient uniqueness; inherited cohort/triplet counts; outcome-blind risk-set construction; training-only bounds; endpoint optimization; primary complete-triplet-only RMSE; and proxy non-substitution. It must reject a conclusion that calls a diagnosis/document/examination/order field a clinical outcome, treats `死亡诊断` as independently validated mortality, interprets a later favorable point estimate with failed support as transport evidence, or calls a zero-containing bound robust. Computational verification cannot establish clinical semantics, assay units, severity, causal effects, or patient benefit.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Changed: the proposal now has executed, hash-bound full-catalog evidence for availability and a formal gate preventing abundant administrative or narrative fields from being mistaken for clinical outcomes. It also makes proxy denominators, timing limitations, and permitted diagnostic roles compiler-checkable. The frozen endpoint and all scientific population, clocks, estimand, and interpretation boundaries are unchanged.

Uncertain: the missing-Y bound itself still requires compilation and execution; no finite temporal bound may be supported if future observed outcomes exceed the pre-origin training range. The catalog cannot resolve laboratory units, external mortality, post-TACE tumor response, hepatic failure, toxicity, or patient benefit. Any such claim requires clinical adjudication or another study.
