# Catalog-bounded outcome-observation and proxy feasibility repair for frozen HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B validation

## Frozen cohort, endpoint, and clocks

Reconstruct exactly the parent’s 319 systemic patients, 1,491 comparator patients, 159 selected systemic repeat events, 526 selected comparator repeat events, and complete-triplet counts of 40/118 for albumin and 41/119 for total bilirubin. Match case-insensitive `TACE` or literal `chemoembolization`, require valid procedure `Start Time`, collapse same-patient same-calendar-day duplicates, and retain the first eligible adjacent 14–180-day TACE1/TACE2 pair and strict repeat event 15–90 days after TACE2. Apply the inherited medication ontology and exclusions without changing it.

Use exact assay clocks. B is the latest valid exact assay (`Albumin` or `Total bilirubin`) on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1; P is the latest valid assay in the selected repeat encounter in `[event_time−72 hours,event_time)`; Y is the valid assay in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, nearest +24 hours under the inherited deterministic tie rule. Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`, positive P lead, one patient-assay row, and outcome-blind B/P eligibility. The primary models and estimand remain:

```text
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
standardized held-out gain = [RMSE(M0)-RMSE(M1)] / SD_test(Y)
```

The primary fit, folds, tuning, predictions, paired patient-level bootstrap, and complete-triplet denominator are unchanged.

## Missing-Y bounds

For each assay and origin, fit only on pre-origin observed complete triplets. Define `L_train=min(Y_train)` and `U_train=max(Y_train)` after the exact parser and before reading future outcomes. A degenerate range or any observed future Y outside that range makes the temporal bound unsupported; do not widen it. A full-source assay range may be shown only as a secondary non-temporal descriptive check.

For every future BP-eligible row, retain fixed M0/M1 predictions and observed/missing status. For missing Y, use no single imputation. For model j, the missing-row squared-error minimum is at `clip(pj,L_train,U_train)` and maximum is at one of the endpoints. For the direct M0-minus-M1 error contrast, use its affine endpoint extrema. Sum observed errors and missing-row extrema to obtain sharp all-BP-eligible MSE intervals. Report observed complete-triplet RMSE gain separately, because `SD_test(Y)` is not identified when Y is missing.

Report overall and by descriptive arm: observed counts, missing counts and fractions, Wilson intervals, bounded MSE/RMSE-difference intervals, all-missing-at-L and all-missing-at-U patterns, per-row least-favorable endpoint pattern, and the smallest common displacement from the observed-case mean that reaches zero when such a displacement exists in `[L_train,U_train]`. These are mathematical support scenarios, not plausible clinical imputations.

## Proxy screen and allowed use

Before any proxy is considered, require all of the following: a native timestamp or a deterministic encounter-time inheritance documented in advance; one-to-one episode-level aggregation without many-to-many multiplication; a prespecified semantic rule; outcome-blind construction; a denominator reported among BP-eligible episodes; and an independent adjudication plan. A field failing any requirement is a context/observation diagnostic only.

Allowed diagnostics are:

* `encounters.Admission time`, `Discharge time`, and `Encounter department`: contact opportunity, discharge-record availability, and length-of-stay-style descriptors. They do not establish survival, hepatic failure, or benefit.
* `diagnoses.Diagnosis name`, `Diagnosis type`: encounter-level disease-context and documentation labels. `Death diagnosis` may trigger manual mortality ascertainment but cannot be counted as validated death without chart/external adjudication.
* `examinations.examination`, `Examination Findings`, `Examination Diagnosis`, `Start Time`, `Examination Number`: report-presence and exploratory report-language descriptors. They cannot be called imaging response because images and validated tumor-burden measurements are unavailable.
* `clinical_documents.Treatment Course`, `Discharge Status`, `Discharge Diagnosis`, `Operative Course`: documentation-presence or exploratory lexical flags only. No native document time and the configured text detector is not validated for comprehensive diagnosis, temporality, or outcome extraction.
* `pathology` report fields: disease-description context only; no dynamic post-event response endpoint.
* `medications` and `orders`: recorded order-pattern and contact-intensity diagnostics only. They are not administration records and cannot establish toxicity, treatment delivery, or response.

Do not fit these diagnostics as competing endpoints, append them to M0/M1, use them to define Y, or use them to rescue a failed support gate. No additional field in the catalog meets the current standard for a validated clinical outcome proxy that can be substituted without changing the scientific schema. The exact counts above make this conclusion auditable rather than based on dataset name alone.

## Falsification and interpretation

A bounded result is supportive only if reconstruction and clocks match, the block passes inherited outcome-blind and complete-triplet gates, the parent’s observed-case criterion is met, and the all-BP-eligible bounded contrast retains its sign without material arm-specific reversal. If the interval contains zero, label the result selection-sensitive; if a supported block reverses under an admissible endpoint, the observed contrast is not robust. If a support gate fails, a training range is unsupported, test SD is unidentified, or an arm is sparse, label the result inconclusive/unbounded regardless of point estimate.

The 2022 result may be interpreted as supportive only for the selected short-horizon prognostic association if all locked criteria pass. 2023 and 2024 remain inconclusive under the parent’s observation rates and arm counts, especially 2024 systemic with one observed complete outcome. A favorable bounded sign does not establish transport across eras, causal effects, assay clinical validity, mortality, toxicity, imaging response, survival, utility, or patient benefit. Clinical adjudication and an independent later/external/prospective cohort with assay units/reference ranges and reliable outcome capture would be required for those claims.
