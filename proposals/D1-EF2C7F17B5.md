## 1. Unresolved question, clinical importance, and the substantive advance

ICU teams titrate fluids/vasopressors to a MAP target, and more time below MAP 65 mmHg is widely
reported to track higher creatinine-based AKI risk. The parent executed only the *detection
negative control* (Spearman(burden, creatinine draws) − Spearman(burden, non-renal draws) = +0.0031),
which is **near-null and sign-flips** versus its grandparent's reported −0.063 — so the
"monitoring artefact" reading is criterion-fragile, not established. The parent's own pre-specified
**fitted AIPW risk difference across conditioning sets was never executed**, and it flagged an
**unreconciled exposure fraction (19.3% vs 75.7%)**. Both are resolved here.

Three rivals with different clinical consequences remain unseparated:
- **H-dose:** the (0,24]h MAP<65 burden is a physiologic ischemic dose that independently raises
  (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI risk → "time below target" is a treatment target.
- **H-observation:** hypotension triggers more monitoring/lab testing, so AKI is *detected* more
  without proportionate injury → the apparent target partly reflects who was tested (Agniel 2018 [K2]).
- **H-treatment:** the burden is a proxy for vasoactive/fluid **treatment intensity**, which may
  itself injure the kidney (PHACS trial, [K3]).

Clinically consequential decision at stake: whether "minutes below MAP 65" is a modifiable
resuscitation target or largely a marker of how sick / how closely observed / how aggressively
treated the patient was. This decides whether a permissive-hypotension strategy [K3] is a
reasonable default or a hazard.

**Advance over existing knowledge.** The closest inspected prior work [K1] reports a base logistic
model of log time-weighted MAP<65 burden predicting KDIGO AKI (AUC 0.725) with **no monitoring- or
treatment-intensity channel separation**. No candidate on this island has produced a fitted,
channel-adjusted risk difference. This episode produces one (below), on real frozen eICU data.

## 3. Estimand, method comparison, baselines

**Estimand:** adjusted risk difference (RD) in 24–72 h KDIGO-AKI for high vs low (0,24]h MAP<65
burden, under three nested conditioning sets, hospital-clustered SE, 5-fold cross-fitting.

- **Baseline B1** — single logistic model of AKI on covariates+exposure with g-computation
  (K1-style standardisation), full covariate set.
- **Alternative A1** — cross-fitted **AIPW** (double-robust) RD across
  - `S0_static`: age, sex, apachescore, predictedicumortality, admission source
  - `S1_monitoring`: S0 + n_map obs, creatinine draws, baseline creatinine
  - `S2_treatment`: S1 + vasopressor intensity (any, rows) + fluid volume
  - `S2n_treatment_only`: S0 + treatment block only
- **Deferred A2** — flexible/spline dose-response (needs ≥2 build attempts; not run here).

**What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses:** A1 separates *how much* of the crude
association is carried by monitoring intensity (S0→S1) versus treatment intensity (S0/S1→S2);
a single baseline logistic cannot attribute the attenuation to a channel and is not doubly robust
to propensity/outcome mis-specification.

## 4. Falsification / decision rules (pre-specified)

- **Supportive H-dose:** RD in `S2_treatment` materially >0 with cluster CI excluding 0 and
  attenuation vs `S0_static` <50%.
- **Adverse H-treatment:** attenuation vs `S0_static` ≥50% driven by the treatment block
  (S1≈S0, S2≪S1) and/or S2 CI crossing 0.
- **Adverse H-observation:** crude S0 RD >0 is largely removed by monitoring block
  (S0→S1 attenuation ≥50%) plus a non-null draw-intensity contrast.
- **Inconclusive:** wide CI overlapping 0 with unstable point estimates across sets.

## 7. Honest limits

Observational (association, not mechanism or causal treatment effect); eICU-internal, **no external
validation**; administration-derived vasopressor/fluid intensity, not dose-normalised; outcome is
detection-dependent; negative control is a proxy; A2 flexible model deferred. A causal MAP-target
or treatment-effect claim would require randomisation ([K3]) or richer time-varying confounding
control — not claimed here.

## 5b. Executed results ([research job], succeeded, ~300 s, 6 CPU, chunked)

Frozen eICU 2.0, real numbers from `ehr_out/results.json`:

- **Exposure reconciliation (resolves the parent's unreconciled 19.3% vs 75.7%):** among ALL adult
  ≥24 h first stays, any MAP<65 in (0,24]h = **19.18%**; among stays with ≥6 MAP obs in (0,24]h,
  any MAP<65 = **74.53%**. The two figures are the same construct on **different denominators**
  (all adult stays vs monitored stays). Median interpolated burden among monitored = **58.1 min**;
  57.9% ≥30 min; 49.6% ≥60 min.
- **Outcome:** 87,428 stays eligible (≥1 creatinine each window); KDIGO creatinine-AKI
  (24,72]h = **16.04%**.
- **Fitted AIPW RD (high vs low burden; hospital-clustered SE):**
  - `S0_static` RD = **+0.0646** (95% CI 0.0521–0.0771)
  - `S1_monitoring` RD = **+0.0520** (0.0435–0.0606)
  - `S2_treatment` RD = **+0.0456** (0.0366–0.0547)
  - `S2n_treatment_only` RD = **+0.0559** (0.0425–0.0694)
  - baseline `B1_gcomp_logit_S2` RD = +0.0299 (cluster IF SE 0.00024 — understated; ignores nuisance
    estimation, flagged, not used for inference)
- **Attenuation S0→S2 = 29.4%** (<50%): monitoring block removes ~19%, treatment block a further
  ~12% of the crude RD; a sizable residual RD remains with CI excluding 0.
- **Negative control:** Spearman(burden, creatinine draws) = +0.0819, Spearman(burden, non-renal
  draws) = +0.0791, contrast **+0.0028** (near-null, same sign-fragile pattern as the parent).

**Pre-specified decision → SUPPORTIVE H-dose:** under `S2_treatment` the RD is materially >0 with CI
excluding 0 and attenuation <50%. **Adverse H-treatment and adverse H-observation are NOT
supported**: neither channel removes the majority of the crude association. This is bounded to
*association after channel adjustment*, not a causal MAP-target or treatment effect.

**Honest caveats on these numbers (require follow-up, not hiding):** (i) the binary exposure at the
analysis median puts unmonitored stays (missing `burden_min`) into the "low" arm, so "low burden"
mixes minimal-hypotension with minimal-monitoring — S1/S2 monitoring adjustment partly, not fully,
addresses this; a monitored-only fit is the required sensitivity check. (ii) `drugname` is not
dose-normalised; fluid `cellpath` filter is a proxy. (iii) eICU-internal only, no external
validation; outcome is detection-dependent. (iv) A2 flexible/spline dose-response deferred. (v) the
B1 g-computation SE is understated and not used for inference.
