## Unresolved clinical question and substantive advance

When a person has a current biomarker panel and only a fixed number of people can receive additional review, does retaining the immediately preceding panel improve the composition of that one shared review list for a subsequent recorded cardiovascular, renal, or metabolic event, beyond the current panel and the interval since repeat assessment?

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the prespecified paired UKB cohort and current-risk middle band, adding the prior eight-field panel to current values, age, sex, calendar time, and repeat spacing increases held-out capture of at least one qualifying C/R/M recorded event among exactly K unique reviewed people, with no material calibration harm, and the increment is directionally consistent when an early-era fit is applied without refitting to the 2013 assessment cohort.

This is a consequential monitoring/triage information question. A clinic, registry, or research program often has a finite review capacity; separate top lists for C, R, and M do not define a finite workload because the same person may occupy multiple lists. The substantive advance is a stable, one-person-one-slot estimand that tests whether longitudinal history changes resource allocation, while retaining domain-specific capture as a secondary scientific result. It does not test whether ordering a repeat panel, reviewing a person, or treating them improves outcomes.

The domain utility is deliberately prespecified and weight-free: the primary target is the indicator of any qualifying C/R/M event, Y_any. A person has one rank based on the held-out probability of Y_any. No arbitrary clinical severity or cost weights are introduced. The joint and domain-specific probabilities are retained to describe what the shared policy captures. Pathway assignment or treatment recommendations are outside scope.

## Evidence boundary

The strongest available evidence is structural: the catalog and inspected headers contain one-row-per-eid structured tables, repeated field-instance columns, assessment date fields, 259 position-matched diagnosis code/date pairs, and death-date fields. Existing UKB-09 expert material and related papers motivate, but do not establish, a benefit from repeated measurements. No fitted result, event count, biological interpretation, or clinical benefit is claimed.

The experiment tests conditional predictive information in a selected repeat-assessment cohort. It cannot establish analyte identities or mechanisms, disease onset, outpatient disease, severity, specimen timing unless certified, causal effects of repeat testing, treatment effects, clinical utility, action thresholds, or external validity. “Event” means a qualifying recorded code/date under the frozen rules below; “no event observed” is never called disease-free.

## Population, timing, and endpoint

Define T0 = `53-0.0` and T1 = `53-1.0` only after an evidence gate certifies that field 53 is a comparable assessment-centre date at both instances. Retain participants with valid dictionary-certified sex `31-0.0`, age `21022-0.0`, valid T0<T1, 0.25 < L=(T1-T0)/365.2425 <= 5 years, T1 <= 2013-12-31, and complete numeric values for all 16 panel cells. Exclude anyone with a qualifying target or death on/before T1. This is a repeat-assessment population and does not imply disease-free status.

For every j=0,...,258 pair `41270-0.j` only with `41280-0.j`; never independently sort, shift, or nearest-date-match the arrays. Normalize nonempty codes by trimming whitespace, uppercasing, and taking the ICD-10 category before a decimal, only after an inspected codebook certifies the code system and date meaning. The primary domain families are C = I00-I99, R = N00-N29, and M = E08-E13. These mappings are execution gates, not claims established by the field numbers.

Let H be T1 plus five calendar years, with leap-day handling recorded. The earliest valid post-T1 same-position code/date no later than H defines the domain. Same-day events in multiple domains form a joint category in the primary outcome and are not arbitrarily assigned. Death uses the earliest valid `40000-0.0`/`40000-1.0`; disagreement or malformed dates is an integrity failure. Death on or before a target date takes precedence when it occurs first or the same day. The primary joint outcome is Y_any = 1 for any C/R/M event before death, 0 otherwise, with a competing D category for death first and N for no qualifying record observed. N is not disease-free.

The primary study may be interpreted as a five-year prospective analysis only if a supplied linkage/ascertainment record certifies observation through H for each included person and specimen/assessment timing is clinically appropriate. Without it, stop before fitting or report only a fail-closed structural certificate; do not use incidence, survival, onset, or clinical recommendation language. Any malformed accepted code/date pair, death disagreement, or failed semantic gate stops the affected analysis.

## Policy population and shared capacity

Use early T1 <= 2012-12-31 for fitting and frozen risk-band definition, and late T1 in 2013 for no-refit temporal application. These boundaries and the endpoint are fixed before outcome inspection. Use one deterministic participant-level five-fold split, `integer(SHA256(UTF-8(decimal(eid)))) mod 5`; all imputation (none for the complete-panel primary), scaling, regularization, tuning, calibration, risk cutpoints, and selection weights are fold-local.

Fit M0 using current panel, age, sex, and T1 calendar year. From early out-of-fold M0 probabilities for Y_any, define the middle current-risk band A as q20 <= risk < q80, with q20/q80 fixed from the early out-of-fold distribution and applied unchanged to late predictions. This focuses the decision question on people for whom review prioritization is plausible and avoids allowing the augmented model to define its own eligible population.

Before observing outcomes, set K=floor(0.10*n_A,early), require K>=40 and n_A,late>=K, and use this same absolute K in early and late A. In each era, select exactly K unique people by descending held-out P(Y_any=1), with deterministic eid-hash tie breaking. The primary shared-capacity policy is therefore one list, one person per slot, one fixed workload. Report list overlap and additions/removals between M1a and M1b, but do not add domain-specific lists to the primary workload.

For each selected person report the held-out domain probabilities and the maximum-domain label only descriptively; this does not authorize routing care. Secondary analyses evaluate C, R, M capture and yield among the same shared list and report joint-event composition, but do not change K or select extra people. A secondary equal-quota policy (K/3 per domain with deduplication and a predeclared tie rule) may show the policy dependence; it cannot support the primary claim.

## Models and method choice

M0 defines A. M1a is the transparent incumbent: current eight-panel values, age, sex, T1 year, and L. M1b adds the eight prior values to M1a. The primary comparison is M1b versus M1a using regularized multinomial probabilities for T-any, C/R/M joint composition, D, and N.

M2a and M2b are matched shallow gradient-boosted multiclass models using exactly the M1a and M1b inputs respectively, the same participants, outcome, folds, small training-fold-only hyperparameter grid, calibration, and held-out evaluation. The learner is scientifically useful because it can reveal current-by-prior discordance, threshold, and interval-dependent structure that the additive transparent model loses. A learned score alone cannot establish a biological trajectory or justify clinical action. If M2 tuning is unstable or calibration worsens, retain its diagnostic output as inconclusive and do not use it to rescue M1b.

The simple M1a baseline is selected because current values plus repeat spacing represent information available at the current assessment and isolate the incremental claim about history. M1b is the direct transparent test of prior-history information. The learned M2 is an alternative for substantive shape discovery, not a competition for tiny predictive gains. A direction-free movement summary and signed deterioration models are secondary/deferral branches: movement requires field comparability and does not distinguish regression to the mean or assay drift; signed worsening directions are not certified locally. A mechanistic organ-reserve model is deferred because analyte identities, units, specimen dates, assay quality, and organ mappings are unavailable. A dated disease-history transformer is deferred because the UKB export is sparse field-position arrays, not a certified dated disease sequence. Genetics/PRS, imaging, sleep accelerometry, CHIP, infection severity, and cancer-stage branches are deferred for missing verified modality, chronology, or adjudication. Revisit only with the named dependency.

## Estimands, uncertainty, and deliverable

For model m and era e, let B_mi(K) indicate membership on the one shared K-person list. Primary weighted and unweighted outputs include:

- shared-list capture C_any(m,e) = (# Y_any events in the K list)/(# Y_any events in A);
- shared-list yield P_any(m,e) = (# Y_any events in the K list)/K;
- paired contrasts Delta_C(e)=C_any(M1b,e)-C_any(M1a,e), Delta_P(e)=P_any(M1b,e)-P_any(M1a,e);
- transport contrast tau_C=Delta_C(late)-Delta_C(early);
- C/R/M and joint-event capture/yield evaluated on those same selected people;
- Brier score, log loss, calibration intercept/slope and reliability for Y_any and each domain/competing category;
- list overlap, additions/removals, missed-event counts, and reviewed/non-reviewed D/N frequencies.

The primary claim is supportive only if all semantic, timing, endpoint, ascertainment, integrity, support, and overlap gates pass; K is identical across eras; there are >=100 Y_any events, >=40 early and >=40 late A participants with events where the corresponding estimate is reported, K>=40, and the two-sided 95% participant-bootstrap interval for Delta_C(late) is wholly above a prespecified 0.05 research margin with no early-to-late sign reversal, while calibration degradation is <=0.05 intercept and <=0.10 slope relative to M1a. The 0.05 margin is an analytical research criterion, not a validated clinical benefit threshold. If the study is underpowered, weighted ESS is poor, or a domain has sparse support, that result is inconclusive rather than pooled.

Use 2,000 participant-bootstrap replicates stratified by fixed era-by-A cells, preserving held-out predictions, K, folds, and frozen weights; report failed-support replicates and whether model-training uncertainty is omitted. Apply a declared Holm correction to the primary capture, yield, and transport family. Selection sensitivity models repeat-panel eligibility using only pre-T1 T0 panel, age, sex, and T0 year, fitted in early folds and applied unchanged late; report overlap, stabilized weight range, balance and ESS. This is not causal correction. A reversal under supported weighting, interval strata, or no-refit transport is adverse for generalization; poor positivity is inconclusive.

The future solver must newly produce the source/header/key audit, gate certificates, cohort flow, fold manifest, fitted M0/M1a/M1b/M2a/M2b models, held-out probabilities, the exact K-list ledgers for early and late eras, domain composition, paired contrasts, bootstrap intervals, calibration, selection sensitivity, all null controls, and an interpretation file linked by path to each output. Completion is either these outputs with an uncertainty-linked supported/adverse/inconclusive interpretation, or a complete fail-closed certificate naming the missing gate. Readiness or a limited probe is not a scientific result.

## Falsification and interpretation

Run endpoint-category permutation within fold/era; prior-panel permutation within current-risk, interval, and selection strata; an independently permuted prior-panel control; code/date same-position permutation and a deliberately shifted-position control that must fail the integrity audit; pre-T1 negative-control endpoint; complete-case versus fold-local missingness audit; random-K policy control; early/late label permutation; calibration, tie, leakage, fold-stability and policy-arithmetic checks. The prior permutation must remove any alleged history increment while preserving current values, L, missingness, risk-band membership and workload. If the gain remains, attribution to prior history is falsified. A null permutation gain or variable K is an implementation failure.

Supportive results mean a stable, calibrated, selection-aware improvement in the one shared list, with domain composition reported honestly. Adverse results include no improvement, a negative/reversed contrast, gain explained by spacing/current risk/selection, calibration harm, instability, or no extra events captured despite score improvement. Inconclusive results include failed semantics or ascertainment, malformed pairing, sparse events, inadequate ESS/positivity, wide intervals, or contradictory recorded versus certified prospective modes. A positive result supports only conditional incremental predictive information in the defined UKB repeat-assessment population; it does not support causality, biological worsening, treatment, repeat-testing policy, or clinical utility. Those require adjudicated endpoints, treatment/indication data, complete follow-up, external validation, expert review, and a prospective policy study.

## Demonstration and seed dispositions

The three demonstrations were methodological context. Delphi provides verified evidence for learning dated structured disease sequences, but this UKB export does not provide a certified dated disease sequence, so only the matched tabular learner is adapted. ALADYNOULLI demonstrates longitudinal latent modeling, but its genetic analysis cannot be reproduced without verified genetic inputs; an EHR-only latent model would change the question and is deferred because the current uncertainty is directly tested by M1a/M1b. Oncoformer demonstrates multimodal longitudinal learning, but the main article and complete STAR Methods are unavailable and the configured UKB data do not provide chest X-rays; a lab-only adaptation would not improve this estimand over the same-input learner and is deferred. No unavailable paper text is claimed.

UKB-09 is the relevant expert seed about metabolic deterioration and multimorbidity; it is untested source material. Its repeated-measurement idea is retained as the prior-history contrast but narrowed to a verified recorded C/R/M endpoint and one shared workload. UKB-04's imaging/fat-distribution direction is not pursued because raw imaging is unavailable. The other UKB seeds remain revisitable but lack a verified dependency or a better data-bound question for this episode. All four configured dataset islands remain accessible; no rows or clinical notes are sent to public search.
