# Does ventilatory burden delay liberation or destabilize the first liberation attempt?

## Scientific deliverable and unresolved claim

This is a substantive evolution of the assessed eICU-05 respiratory proposal. The future solver must newly fit an uncertainty-quantified, two-stage respiratory transition analysis: (1) whether early ventilatory burden changes the chance of reaching a documented ventilator-Off state, and (2) conditional on that observable transition, whether a later documented Start occurs within 24 hours. It must produce a frozen cohort-flow and source/value audit, 30-minute respiratory-state ledger, paired transition fits, standardized 48-hour cumulative-incidence contrasts with uncertainty, held-out-hospital transport results, and all prespecified falsifications. Discovery fitted no target-cohort model and establishes no clinical result.

The strongest supported evidence is limited to data availability and an audit of recorded status/mechanics fields. In a full scan of respiratoryCharting, 2,741 stays had an RT Vent On/Off status, including 2,462 with Continued, 2,382 with Start, 953 with Off, and 142 with Suspended; 43 stays had Off followed by Start within 1,440 minutes under the parent ordering rule. Mechanics were recorded for 40,097 stays for TV/kg IBW, 45,541 for PEEP, 23,390 for Plateau Pressure, 23,939 for Pressure Support, and 11,748 for Compliance. These are availability counts, not eligible-cohort rates or associations.

The unresolved claim is whether high early ventilatory burden is associated mainly with delayed liberation, with instability after a documented liberation attempt, or with neither. Primary hypothesis: among adults with explicitly documented active ventilation at a six-hour landmark, after adjustment for measured baseline risk and observation coverage, the prespecified 25th-to-75th full-burden contrast will produce a clinically meaningful (at least 2 percentage-point) difference in the 48-hour cumulative incidence of the documented active-to-Off transition or its competing exits. A secondary pathway hypothesis is that, among those reaching Off, the same burden contrast increases documented Off-to-Start within 24 hours; this component is explicitly event-gated and may be inconclusive because the audit found only 43 such stays before the final cohort restrictions.

Clinical importance follows the distinction. A robust active-to-Off association would identify a potentially useful liberation-review risk marker; a robust Off-to-Start association would motivate prospective assessment of instability after an attempted transition. If all association is confined to charting opportunity, the finding would warn against interpreting a respiratory model as physiology. None of these outcomes establishes extubation failure, reintubation, ventilator injury, treatment effect, or a bedside threshold.

## Population, time and states

Use eICU 2.0 snapshot [source checksum]. Use one row per patientunitstayid; age >=18, parse age='> 89' as 90 and retain an age-censored flag; valid admissionheight 100-250 cm; unitdischargeoffset missing or >=360; at least one first-six-hour respiratory row indicating O2 Device=Ventilator or Oxygen Delivery Method=Mechanical ventilator; at least two valid numeric respiratory observations in [0,360]; and an explicit RT Vent On/Off status of Start or Continued at or before minute 360 with no later Off or Suspended before that landmark. Repeated stays remain separate, but all stays sharing uniquepid stay in one split, with a uniquepid-clustered sensitivity.

Time zero is ICU admission and all offsets are ICU-relative minutes. Build exposure features only from [0,360] on a 30-minute grid, carrying a value forward no more than 30 minutes and recording coverage and gaps. Valid ranges are TV/kg IBW >0 and <=20 mL/kg, Tidal Volume (set) >0 and <=2,000 mL, Plateau Pressure >0 and <=60 cmH2O, PEEP 0-40 cmH2O, Pressure Support 0-50 cmH2O and Compliance >0 and <=200 mL/cmH2O. Use respchartoffset primarily; respchartentryoffset is a revision-timing sensitivity.

Define end_i=min(2880, unitdischargeoffset_i) for a valid nonnegative unitdischargeoffset. The primary state ledger is active ventilation at landmark; documented Off is the first later RT Vent On/Off value Off or off; documented restart is the first later Start within 1,440 minutes of Off; durable documented Off is Off followed by 1,440 minutes without Start, with a subsequent status row or nonfatal unit exit. Death, alive unit discharge/transfer, and loss of respiratory-status observation are competing exits. Suspended is excluded in the primary definition and treated as Off in a sensitivity. Do not use respiratoryCare.ventendoffset as extubation: it is an audit field and prior inspection found it overwhelmingly zero-coded. Use the exact label 'documented ventilator-Off' and 'documented restart'; never relabel either as extubation or reintubation.

The primary estimand is the development-fitted, standardized 48-hour CIF for active-to-Off versus death/alive exit/unknown observation loss, contrasting the prespecified burden profiles at a common adjustment distribution. The restart estimand is the 24-hour Off-to-Start CIF among those with Off before end_i, reported only if at least 100 qualifying restart events and adequate hospital support are reached; otherwise it is inconclusive and not a negative clinical finding. Report a decomposition of total 48-hour pathways into active-to-Off, Off-to-durable-Off, Off-to-Start, death, alive exit and unknown.

## Baseline, alternative and analysis

Fit matched models on identical cohort, ledger, time boundary, split, endpoints, uncertainty and evaluation.

Baseline: a transparent 30-minute discrete-time competing-risk pooled-logistic model using TV/kg IBW mean/IQR/slope and coverage, plus age, sex, ethnicity, height, admission source/type, unit type, device/status coverage, first-six-hour vital summaries and hospital context. Estimate active-to-Off and competing exits. For the sparse Off subset, estimate Off-to-Start as a prespecified secondary only. Fit nested TV-only and full-burden versions.

Substantive alternative: an ordered piecewise-exponential multi-state model with active ventilation, documented Off, durable Off, restart, unit death, alive unit exit and unknown respiratory observation loss. Add time-varying 30-minute summaries of plateau pressure, PEEP, pressure support, inverse compliance, TV/kg IBW, persistence/recovery and observation coverage. Estimate separate active-to-Off and Off-to-Start hazards and path-specific CIFs. This alternative reveals whether burden changes access to liberation versus instability after Off, and whether apparent transitions reflect status-record opportunity; the scalar baseline cannot make that distinction. A GRU/transformer using the same history is deferred because event density and residual scientific value are not established; revisit only after >=100 restart events, stable status semantics and reproducible held-out residual structure.

Before any fitting, freeze label/value maps, source selection, valid ranges, deduplication and the hospital split. Use a deterministic 80/20 hospital split, keep uniquepid in one partition, and require at least 10 hospitals in each role. Fit only on development hospitals, including scaling, imputation, inner validation, and any knots. Use hospital-cluster bootstrap (500 replicates if feasible) and a uniquepid-clustered sensitivity. Report coefficients/hazards, transition counts, standardized CIF contrasts, calibration, Brier/log scores where estimable, AUROC/AUPRC only as secondary summaries, and held-out-hospital transport.

## Falsification and interpretation

Support requires an active-to-Off full-burden versus TV-only standardized contrast >=2 percentage points with a two-sided hospital-cluster interval excluding zero, concordant direction in the multi-state model, stable held-out-hospital calibration and direction, and disappearance under within-stratum burden permutation. Restart-specific support additionally requires >=100 events, adequate hospital overlap, the prespecified >=2-point contrast, and stability under the status and timing sensitivities.

Adverse evidence is a null or reversed contrast, calibration harm, failure to transport, disagreement between models, disappearance after coverage adjustment, concentration in one status label or hospital, or a similar contrast after burden permutation. It weakens or rejects the corresponding recorded-pathway interpretation but does not prove ventilatory safety or treatment ineffectiveness.

Inconclusive evidence includes fewer than 100 restart events, sparse active-to-Off events, excessive unknown respiratory censoring, unstable status/value maps, poor overlap, nonconvergence, or uncertainty intervals spanning the clinical-importance margin. Restart sparsity must not be silently converted into a null.

Computationally checkable claims include headers/schema hashes, source paths, label/value maps, counts, cohort flow, feature validity, state ordering, split integrity, fitted parameters, CIFs, scores, calibration, transport and sensitivity results. Clinical adjudication or another study is required to determine whether Off means extubation or a temporary pause, Start means reintubation or another device transition, records are complete, an SBT occurred, settings were administered as charted, or a model should change care. Missing are validated extubation/reintubation, SBT, sedation/delirium, cough/secretions, blood gases, clinician intent, raw waveforms and post-ICU outcomes.

## Resource plan and completion

Discovery used a bounded CPU source scan; its elapsed time and counts are measured, but full solver runtime is unverified. The future solver planning envelope is 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB memory, up to 8 A100 GPUs and 28,800 seconds. CPU-first is adequate for chunked I/O, pooled-logistic and piecewise-exponential fits; GPU is optional and should only be requested if a learned sensitivity is scientifically justified. Completion requires newly fitted paired models, the state ledger, held-out predictions, standardized transition/CIF contrasts with uncertainty, endpoint-gated restart analysis, all falsifications, and an output-linked interpretation.
