# A leakage-resistant audit of an admission-level pulmonary-nodule handoff gate: split evidence views, held-out route purity, and partial identification

## Decision question, unresolved claim, and substantive advance

The decision is whether the conservative retrospective `AUTO/RECON/TIMING/DEFER` gate from the parent is reproducible and sufficiently pure at the all-opportunity admission level to merit a prospective **silent** validation. It is not whether a copied radiology recommendation is clinically appropriate, whether a clinician erred, or whether any route improves care.

The parent established a defensible routing estimand: recommendation units are enumerated, but a whole admission is routed by its worst decision-relevant unit so that a copyable omission cannot hide a competing conflict. Its remaining threat is label circularity. Eligibility/unit boundaries, observable context, superseding plans, and concordance are text judgments, and overlapping reviewers can see enough of the same canonical discharge artifact to infer the downstream route. Consensus could then make a route look reproducible because the codebook, component labels, and route were refined on the same evidence.

This child preserves the parent’s population, chronology, state systems, unit clocks, routing logic, comparator/calibration roles, and noncausal boundary. It changes the **measurement experiment**, not the clinical schema. It preregisters three mutually separated evidence tasks, generates routes only by locked code after labels are sealed, repeats the complete measurement process in an untouched subject-level audit set with independent reviewers and redactors, carries individual disagreement through compatible route sets, and requires leakage negative controls, route-specific event counts, precision, reliability, workload, and purity gates before nomination.

The strongest claim already supported by source inspection is only feasibility of reconstruction: the required MIMIC admissions, radiology, radiology-detail, discharge, discharge-detail, service, transfer, patient, and ICU fields exist; within-admission storage intervals can be computed; canonical `DS` rows and reciprocal report/addendum links can be tested. The inherited complete-source scan found 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 unique canonical `DS` admission keys, 17 missing `DS storetime` values, and 3,764 retrieval-proxy pulmonary reports. Those proxies are not adjudicated opportunities and establish no route prevalence or clinical validity.

The unresolved falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the frozen first-eligible, one-admission-per-subject all-opportunity population, independently elicited radiology eligibility/unit labels, discharge-context/superseding-plan labels, and recommendation-to-discharge concordance labels will yield stable admission-level `A_AUTO`, `A_RECON`, `A_TIMING`, `A_DEFER`, and `A_NOACTION` routes under all individual-reviewer-compatible and unknown-adverse assignments. On a subject-level audit set never used for codebook, redaction, model, or threshold refinement, nominal `A_AUTO` will have a governance-approved upper contamination bound no greater than 0.10, mixed-admission risk and manual-review workload will remain within preapproved limits, and all route, reliability, event, precision, and leakage-control gates will pass.

A supportive result justifies testing the frozen gate silently in a live system. It does not establish recommendation appropriateness, safety, utility, benefit, actual awareness, or intervention effectiveness.

## Frozen denominator, report chains, canonical artifact, and clocks

The child does not redesign the cohort. Before reading discharge text, discharge `storetime`, route labels, later traces, or audit assignment outcomes, stream the complete sources and create a hashed radiology eligibility manifest. `N_A` remains the parent’s first eligible admission per subject:

* adult;
* exact admission-linked radiology report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional or optional plans, explicit no-follow-up, in-hospital death, hospice/comfort-only transition under the locked rubric, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* never infer measurements, smoking exposure, malignancy risk, life expectancy, recommendation appropriateness, or missing clinical context.

Report/addendum chains must be reciprocal, subject-consistent, and admission-consistent in `radiology_detail`. Every operative component must satisfy `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. Only a later material pre-`D` component within that reciprocal chain may supersede an earlier component. Missing nodes, nonreciprocal/contradictory links, unresolved operative components, or missing/contradictory recommendation times are `U_R`, retained in the cascade and bounds and never called omissions.

The canonical artifact is the sole `note/discharge` row with exact `note_type=="DS"` for `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. A duplicate canonical key stops the confirmatory run. Missing artifact, text, linkage, or `storetime`, and unreadable or contradictory content remain unknown. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

For the denominator-defining operative component retain `R_store`, canonical `DS_store`, and `G=DS_store-R_store` in hours. For each additional unit retain `R_store,u` and `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u`. `G` and `G_u` are storage-to-storage opportunity proxies, not evidence of clinician viewing, awareness, editing, or accountability.

## Preserved exhaustive state systems

Every admission in `N_A` retains the parent’s departure state:

`Y={F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}`

where `F` is a faithful classifiable canonical artifact stored by `D`; `C_O` is an omitted patient-specific actionable plan in an artifact stored by `D`; `C_I` is incomplete/discordant content without a departure-frozen alternate resolution; `R` is a text-supported alternate resolution in an artifact stored by `D`; `A` is known `DS_store>D` regardless of later text; and `U_D` is missing, unreadable, contradictory, or unclassifiable artifact/store/linkage/text. These states are mutually exclusive, exhaustive, and sum to `N_A`; later fidelity cannot change `A`.

Retain without alteration:

* `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`;
* `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`;

* `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A`, publishing zero and unknown cells;
* `E_A={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`.

No row with `G<0` or `DS_store>D` enters primary content routing. Verify row-wise `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; the identity does not redefine opportunity. Non-`E_A` admissions remain in all-opportunity accounting as chronology/timing states.

## Recommendation units and worst-case admission routing

After `N_A` is frozen, radiology-only reviewers enumerate all eligible recommendation units. This creates `N_R` nested in `N_A` and never adds an admission or subject. A unit is the smallest operative tuple

`U=(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, recommendation polarity)`.

Co-mentioned nodules sharing one action and due interval are one unit; different actions or due windows are separate units; repeated wording and reciprocal addendum restatements are deduplicated; a material addendum modifies the operative version rather than creating an extra event. Indeterminate coreference creates `U_BOUNDARY`, with minimum- and maximum-unit compatible inventories. Unit IDs are deterministic functions of `(subject_id,hadm_id,source note/component,normalized span offsets)` and never encode route or split. Resampling, multiplicity, readiness, and intervention routing remain admission-level.

The inherited locked route function is preserved. At unit level:

* `U_RESOLVED` for text-supported `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, or `S_EQ`;
* `U_OPEN` for observable `K_OPEN`, unresolved `S_0/S_PART/S_CON`, and `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING` for observable open unresolved content but `G_u<24h` or unresolved unit clock;
* `U_CLOSED` for `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER` for `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, `U_BOUNDARY`, unreadable content, redaction failure, or decision-relevant reviewer-compatible assignments crossing statuses.

Aggregate by worst case:

1. any `U_DEFER` gives `A_DEFER`;
2. otherwise any `U_TIMING` gives `A_TIMING`;
3. all units resolved/closed gives `A_NOACTION`;
4. among admissions with `U_OPEN`, `A_AUTO` requires every open unit to be an unambiguous wholly omitted `K_OPEN+S_0` unit, every other unit reproducibly resolved/closed, no boundary uncertainty, and no cross-unit target/action/timing conflict;
5. any incomplete, conflicting, interaction-dependent, or nonindependently representable open unit gives `A_RECON`;
6. at least one pure omission plus at least one reconciliation unit is `A_MIXED`, a reported subset of `A_RECON`, never `A_AUTO`.

No reviewer sees or assigns these route labels. Only versioned, tested code applies this function after component-label files are sealed.

## Preregistered split-adjudication architecture

### Phase 0: split and protocol freeze

After the radiology-only `N_A` manifest and basic pre-outcome strata are fixed, assign subjects by keyed cryptographic hash, stratified only on the denominator-defining interval (3/6/12 months), admission type family, ICU exposure, and terminal-service family:

* 50 admissions chosen without route or discharge inspection form a development pilot;
* 60% of all nonpilot subjects form the production/census stratum;
* 40% form the untouched audit stratum.

The 40% fraction is fixed to provide route-positive audit events; it is not adapted after route inspection. If audit event gates fail, the result is inconclusive rather than supplemented post hoc. Pilot admissions remain members of `N_A`; their exploratory labels are destroyed, packets are regenerated, and they are re-reviewed under the locked production protocol. Thus every admission contributes exactly once to census burden estimates. “Audit” means withheld from protocol tuning, not excluded from the all-opportunity population.

The pilot may refine only wording definitions, redaction rules, span boundaries, packet rendering, and software. It cannot change the frozen population, clocks, state systems, route hierarchy, estimands, or gates and cannot set exclusions or margins from observed route frequencies. Before any audit packet is generated, freeze and hash the codebook, packet builders, redaction dictionaries, route code, split manifest, reviewer roster/role, margins, statistical analysis, and synthetic fixtures. Audit reviewers and audit redactors cannot have served on the pilot or production review for the same task.

A production measurement is performed on all `N_A` admissions using the locked protocol. Audit-stratum admissions then undergo a second, independently generated and independently reviewed measurement. Production labels/routes for the audit stratum are sealed before audit labels are opened. Audit labels cannot trigger codebook repair, reviewer retraining, threshold changes, or exclusions in the confirmatory analysis.

### Task E: recommendation eligibility and unitization

Two independent thoracic-radiology reviewers receive only the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology report/addendum chain and radiology-detail provenance necessary to interpret it. They do not receive the canonical discharge artifact or its existence, `DS_store`, `G/G_u`, `V/H/Y/Z`, context, later notes/events, audit/production status, any panel label, or any route/intervention vocabulary.

They determine eligibility, final operative component, target clusters, action/modality, exact due interval/date, recommendation polarity, and minimum/maximum compatible unit inventories. Every decision cites source `note_id`, chain node, and character offsets. A third reviewer may adjudicate but cannot erase the two individual compatible inventories. A packet with unresolved reciprocity, chronology, or unit boundary remains `U_R` or `U_BOUNDARY`.

### Task K: context and superseding-plan extraction without recommendation comparison

Task K uses two independently rendered views and two independent physician reviewers per view.

**K-context view.** Reviewers receive the pre-`D` canonical `DS` plus allowed non-target admission/disposition context. All pulmonary target, recommendation, action, timing, recipient, order, preference, and target-linked plan spans are replaced by length-neutral typed masks. Radiology text, payload values, clocks/gap bins, Task E/C labels, later data, and route language are absent. Reviewers assign only:

* `K_OPEN`: no observable non-target departure-frozen stop to possible pulmonary disposition action;
* `K_CLOSED`: explicit non-target evidence closes any such action, such as a global decision against follow-up; this triggers denominator audit;
* `K_UNKNOWN`: the available record cannot establish openness.

This is observable context compatibility, not clinical appropriateness.

**K-plan view.** A separate pair receives only target-linked plan spans and enough immediately adjacent canonical-DS text to resolve grammar, with recommendation values and all radiology text absent. Stable random tokens replace target identifiers; clocks, route terms, context labels, and later evidence are absent. Reviewers extract, without comparing to radiology, whether the artifact asserts: target-linked informed non-pursuit (`P_DEC`), definitive diagnostic/therapeutic management (`P_DEF`), a future surveillance payload with action/timing/recipient fields (`P_FUT`), target recognition without executable payload (`P_PART`), no target-linked plan (`P_0`), or uninterpretable linkage/meaning (`P_UN`). They quote offsets and independently populate the discharge-side payload. A generic “PCP follow-up” cannot become an executable target plan.

The locked crosswalk later combines K-plan extraction with Task C comparison: `P_DEC/P_DEF` can support `S_DEC/S_DEF` only when explicit; a complete same-or-more-protective compatible `P_FUT` can support `S_EQ`; incompatible complete plans become `S_CON`; incomplete plans become `S_PART`; no plan becomes `S_0`; ambiguity becomes `S_UN`. K reviewers never see the source recommendation against which “compatible” is judged.

### Task C: concordance/content comparison without context or opportunity

Two independent physicians receive neutralized paired payload packets after Task E and K-plan files are sealed. Packet side/order is randomized. One side contains the operative radiology unit payload and one contains the extracted discharge-side target-plan payload or an explicit `NO_TARGET_PAYLOAD` token. The packet contains only target cluster tokens, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity/active-status claims, and any quoted span needed to resolve semantics. It excludes document names where feasible, full-note context, `charttime/storetime/D`, `G/G_u`, `V/H/Y/Z`, admission/disposition data, reviewer identities, confidence, K labels, route terms, downstream records, expected direction, and intervention language.

For each source unit Task C records a relation, not a route: `C_FAITHFUL`, `C_WHOLE_OMISSION`, `C_INCOMPLETE`, `C_CONFLICT`, `C_RESOLUTION_COMPATIBLE`, or `C_UNKNOWN`. “Same or more protective” is an observable timing/action relation under the frozen rubric, not a clinical judgment. If target mapping itself is ambiguous, use `C_UNKNOWN`; reviewers may not resolve it from hidden context. Task C never decides whether the unit had editing opportunity or whether propagation is appropriate.

### Independence, sealing, and masking audit

Reviewers are assigned to one task only in the confirmatory run; no person reviews the same admission in two tasks or in both production and audit measurement. Review is asynchronous and individual labels are immutable. Adjudication appends a third label; it never overwrites primaries. Packets use random IDs generated independently of `subject_id/hadm_id`, route, and split; packet order is randomized within reviewer. Crosswalk keys are held by a data manager not serving as reviewer.

Every packet manifest stores the source row hashes, allowed fields, removed span offsets, rendering-code hash, reviewer/task, and prohibited-token scan result. Real dates are converted to within-packet relative intervals only when essential to the assigned semantic task; no opportunity gap is exposed. Any cross-panel label, route string, audit/production indicator, post-`D` row, outcome, POE row, or prohibited timestamp in a packet is a confirmatory failure.

## Individual-reviewer partial identification

There is no consensus-only reference standard. For each admission, retain every primary Task E, K-context, K-plan, and C label; the third label only enlarges the documented evidence set. Construct the Cartesian set of logically compatible component assignments subject to source chronology and the frozen crosswalk, then apply the route function to every assignment. Preserve:

* eligibility inclusion/exclusion compatible sets;
* minimum/maximum unit inventories for `U_BOUNDARY`;
* all individual `K`, plan, and concordance labels;
* favorable and adverse interpretations of missing/redacted context;
* unknown clocks and unreadable evidence.

The production compatible route set is `R_prod(a)` and independent audit set is `R_audit(a)`. A singleton set is route-identified. Any set spanning `AUTO` and another active route is adverse to `AUTO`; any decision-relevant cross-route set becomes `A_DEFER` in the conservative primary census route. Consensus and majority-vote routes are secondary descriptions only.

Report sharp finite-sample lower/upper bounds by optimizing each count over compatible admission assignments while keeping all units of an admission together. Do not replace reviewer disagreement with an assumed error rate, latent-class “truth,” or model-predicted label. If logical constraints produce an empty compatible set, classify the packet as protocol/data failure, not a forced route.

## Held-out audit estimands

All burden estimands retain denominator `N_A`. The production measurement supplies one locked census route for every admission. The independent audit measurement estimates reproducibility and contamination on the prespecified 40% subject sample using inverse split probabilities, although stratification should make weights nearly constant.

### Census workload and route burden

Per 100 `N_A`, report `B_AUTO`, `B_RECON`, `B_MIXED`, `B_TIMING`, `B_DEFER`, and `B_NOACTION`, plus `100*n(E_A)/N_A` and every non-`E_A` chronology state. They must exhaust `N_A`. Report recommendation units per 100 admissions, within-admission unit counts, review minutes per admission/unit/task, packet failure/redaction rework, and projected manual minutes per 100 `N_A` for each route. `N_R` is never treated as independent.

Retain

`D_MIX=P(A_RECON or A_DEFER | at least one unit-level pure omission)`

at the admission level. This is the risk that a recommendation-unit auto rule encounters a competing reason not to auto-route.

### Audit route purity and reproducibility

For a production-nominated route `r`, define strict audit purity

`PPV_strict(r) = sum_a w_a I[R_prod(a)={r} and R_audit(a)={r}] / sum_a w_a I[R_prod(a)={r}]`

within the audit stratum. Any audit disagreement, unknown, packet defect, or compatible set containing another route counts as not strictly pure. Also report a weaker “contains-r” sensitivity, but it cannot pass the readiness gate.

For nominal `A_AUTO`, define audit contamination

`Q_AUTO = 1-PPV_strict(A_AUTO)`.

Report its exact/binomial and subject-bootstrap upper confidence bounds; the larger is decision-facing. Decompose contamination into audit `RECON/MIXED`, `TIMING`, `DEFER/unknown`, eligibility/unit-boundary, and masking defects. A production `A_AUTO` whose audit set contains `A_RECON`, `A_TIMING`, or `A_DEFER` is adverse even if consensus says `AUTO`.

Report weighted exact route agreement over all audit admissions, route-specific positive agreement, full confusion matrices, reviewer/task agreement, reviewer-specific route rates, and leave-one-reviewer-out compatible route stability. `A_DEFER` purity is interpreted as reproducible need for manual review, not proof of clinical uncertainty.

### Margins, events, precision, and workload gates

All operational margins must be signed by clinical governance before audit labels are opened. The inherited auto-contamination/mixed-risk margin is 0.10. It is a validation-worthiness margin, not a safety or harm threshold. If governance does not approve it prospectively, only descriptive estimates are allowed and no lane is nominated.

A route may be nominated only if all common gates and its route-specific gates pass:

1. `N_A>=200`, `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, no duplicate canonical `DS`, at least 60 admissions in `E_A`, and unknown/boundary/unreadable component states no more than 10% of `E_A`.
2. At least 30 audit admissions nominated to each route for which purity is claimed; at least 60 audit route-positive admissions for a two-lane claim. `A_AUTO` additionally requires at least 30 audit nominations even if no contamination is observed.
3. At least 90% raw agreement and kappa or Gwet AC1 `>=0.70` for each component task where defined; report both when prevalence is extreme. Overall exact production-audit route agreement must be at least 90%, with simultaneous lower confidence bound at least 85%. Each nominated route requires positive agreement at least 85% and a simultaneous lower bound at least 75%.
4. For `A_AUTO`, the simultaneous upper 95% bound for `Q_AUTO` and for `D_MIX` must each be `<=0.10`. Equivalently, strict `A_AUTO` purity must have simultaneous lower bound `>=0.90`. No favorable consensus sensitivity may substitute.
5. For `A_RECON`, `A_TIMING`, or `A_DEFER` nomination, strict audit purity must have simultaneous lower bound `>=0.80`; this is reproducibility of a workflow lane, not clinical truth.
6. Simultaneous half-width must be no more than 5 admissions per 100 `N_A` for every claimed all-population burden and no more than 10 percentage points for route purity, `Q_AUTO`, and `D_MIX`.
7. Report production and audit review workload. Governance must preapprove a maximum manual-review budget in minutes per 100 `N_A` and maximum `A_DEFER` burden. If either margin is absent or exceeded, no scalable validation priority is selected even if label purity is high.
8. The same route conclusion must hold under all individual-reviewer, unknown-adverse, redaction-error, and min/max-unit assignments and after leaving out each reviewer. Consensus cannot repair a failed gate.
9. Every masking, duplicate-packet, nuisance-feature, and target-permutation negative control below must pass.

Low counts or wide intervals are inconclusive, not evidence that a route is absent. If all route events are zero, report the simultaneous upper bound and compare it only with a separately preapproved operational minimum.

## Leakage and circularity negative controls

All controls, random seeds, metrics, and thresholds are frozen before audit labels are opened.

1. **Canary/prohibited-field control.** Synthetic packet fixtures place route words, `G/H/V/Y/Z`, split status, raw timestamps, post-`D` events, panel labels, and intervention cues in every supported field and formatting variant. The packet builder/scanner must detect and block 100% before any human review. One missed canary blocks the confirmatory run.
2. **Independent duplicate-packet control.** A cryptographic 10% sample in each task is rerendered under a new random ID, order, neutral token mapping, and nonsemantic whitespace/layout. Reviewers are unaware of duplication. Component agreement must be `>=95%`; derived compatible-route agreement must be `>=95%`; no directional paired-label asymmetry may have multiplicity-adjusted `p<0.01`. Failure indicates packet-cue or reviewer-instability leakage.
3. **Nuisance-prediction control.** Before crosswalk unsealing, fit a prespecified cross-fitted multinomial baseline using only packet ID hash fragments, queue position, reviewer, packet length, note/span count, redaction density, rendering template, and review duration. It must not predict final route more than 0.05 absolute macro-balanced-accuracy above the label-permuted/majority baseline with permutation `p<0.01`. An excess satisfying both conditions blocks nomination; this model is diagnostic and never used to assign labels.
4. **Constrained target-permutation control.** After K-plan extraction is sealed, permute discharge-side payloads among admissions within interval, admission-type, terminal-service, unit-count, and payload-presence strata, retaining superficial style and missingness. Across 10,000 fixed-seed permutations, observed target-specific faithful/resolution compatibility must exceed the 99th percentile of the permuted distribution, while whole-omission rates in the explicit `NO_TARGET_PAYLOAD` stratum should remain invariant within Monte Carlo error. Failure indicates that concordance relies on generic document style rather than target-specific content.
5. **Route-irrelevant permutation control.** Permuting reviewer queue position, random packet IDs, display side/order, whitespace, and neutral target tokens must leave locked route outputs bit-for-bit unchanged when labels are held fixed. Any change is a software leakage failure.
6. **Clock isolation control.** Replacing real `G/G_u` with independently permuted values must not change E, K, or C human labels, because those panels never receive clocks. It may change only the code-derived `TIMING` versus content-eligible status. Any component-label association with the permuted clock beyond the frozen multiplicity-adjusted diagnostic threshold blocks nomination.
7. **Audit-seal control.** Hashes must prove that the codebook, redaction dictionary, packet builders, reviewer roster, route code, margins, and production route file predate audit-label opening. Any post-opening change makes the result exploratory and ineligible for nomination.

These controls can detect specific leakage paths; passing them does not prove absence of all cognitive or documentation bias.

## Inference and multiplicity

The subject/index admission is the resampling unit; all recommendation units and both production/audit measurements of an admission receive the same bootstrap weight. Use at least 10,000 fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap replicates, preserving split strata and audit weights. Each replicate carries exhaustive states, burdens, compatible-set bounds, workload, route purity, agreement, `D_MIX`, and calibration/comparator contrasts.

Use a max-statistic simultaneous 95% family for all decision-facing quantities: six route burdens, audit strict purity for each nominated lane, `Q_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, overall/route-specific agreement, defer burden, manual workload, portable comparator contrast, and retained availability contrasts. Exact one-sided binomial limits for contamination are also shown; use the less favorable limit for the gate. Descriptive confusion matrices and pointwise intervals cannot drive nomination.

Do not fit a latent truth model, use an NLP classifier as the reference, optimize thresholds against audit labels, or treat recommendations as independent. Any exploratory model is quarantined after the confirmatory result and cannot revise it.

At `b={0,24,72,168}`, estimate `q_g(b)=P(DS_store>D+b)` for `N_A`, standardized `O`, and `M`, and `Delta_A(b)=q_N(b)-q_O^std(b)` with missingness bounds. ## Verifier-checkable invariants and adversarial fixtures

1. Recompute all source and schema hashes; stream complete sources; stop on drift.
2. The eligibility-manifest hash and `N_A` are identical across chronology, unitization, split, production, audit, calibration, and comparator exclusion. Each subject contributes at most one index admission.
3. Reciprocal chain edges are subject- and admission-consistent; every operative component has `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. Nonreciprocal and post-`D` fixtures become unresolved/excluded, never eligible omissions.
4. Exactly one `note_type=="DS"` row exists for every classifiable canonical key. Duplicate keys stop; missing keys become `U_D`.
5. `Y`, every `V/H/Z` joint cell, `E_A` versus non-`E_A`, and all admission routes plus chronology states each exhaust `N_A`; all zero/unknown cells are emitted.
6. Verify `G=DS_store-R_store`, every `G_u`, and `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`. Only these storage clocks define `H`; `charttime` cannot substitute.
7. Shared action/interval nodules form one unit; different due windows form separate units; repeated/addendum restatements deduplicate; ambiguity emits min/max `U_BOUNDARY` inventories.
8. Packet manifests contain only task-allowed fields. Any route, other-panel label, split status, raw opportunity clock, post-`D` row, downstream outcome, or POE content fails the packet.
9. E labels are invariant to any discharge-text substitution; K labels are invariant to any radiology recommendation substitution; C labels are invariant to `G`, context, and route substitutions.
10. Reviewers never directly vote `AUTO/RECON/TIMING/DEFER/NOACTION`; deleting all route vocabulary from the review interface leaves labels unchanged. Locked code alone derives routes.
11. “PCP follow-up” without target/action/timing is `P_0/P_PART`; explicit target-linked non-pursuit is `P_DEC`; definitive replacement is `P_DEF`; complete compatible future payload can become `S_EQ`; unexplained incompatible payload is `S_CON`; ambiguity is unknown. Later evidence never upgrades a state.
12. One pure omission plus one conflict is `A_MIXED` within `A_RECON`, never `A_AUTO`. Duplicating a unit cannot change the admission route or weight.
13. Any decision-relevant individual disagreement, unknown, redaction failure, or unit-boundary ambiguity that spans routes is adverse to strict purity and conservatively derives `A_DEFER`.
14. Pilot exploratory labels are absent from confirmatory files; pilot admissions are re-reviewed. Every `N_A` admission has one production measurement; audit admissions additionally have one sealed independent measurement.
15. Production routes and all protocol hashes predate audit opening. Threshold or code changes after opening cannot yield a confirmatory nomination.
16. Synthetic perfect, contaminated-auto, mixed, timing-limited, unknown-heavy, sparse, wide-interval, and workload-excess datasets must produce respectively supportive, adverse, or inconclusive outputs exactly under the frozen gate.
17. All negative controls execute and publish their seeds, denominators, null distributions, and pass/fail results. A failed control cannot be hidden by aggregate reliability.
18. Bootstrap weights are constant within subject/admission and shared by all its units/measurements. Recommendation-unit duplication cannot narrow intervals.
19. Removing comparator and POE appendices leaves pulmonary labels, routes, purity, and readiness unchanged.
20. Conclusions are generated from a checked result-to-claim table: supportive, adverse, and inconclusive synthetic outputs must reject unsupported claims of appropriateness, safety, benefit, clinician failure, or deployment readiness.

Computational verification can establish source/key/clock consistency, packet-field separation, frozen splits/hashes, route derivation, finite compatible-set bounds, controls, counts, intervals, and whether prespecified gates were met. It cannot establish that reviewers’ judgments are clinically correct, that omitted context was irrelevant, that clinicians saw a report, or that any intervention is safe or useful.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive for prospective silent validation of the full gate** requires every common gate and negative control to pass; production and audit routes to be stable under all compatible assignments; at least one route to have adequate all-`N_A` burden, events, and precision; strict route purity to exceed its preapproved margin; and workload/defer burden to fit the preapproved manual-review budget. This supports only P1 silent measurement of the unchanged gate.

**Supportive for an `AUTO` lane** additionally requires simultaneous upper bounds `Q_AUTO<=0.10` and `D_MIX<=0.10`, adequate audit `AUTO` events, strict lower purity `>=0.90`, and no compatible audit assignment that exposes reconciliation, timing, or defer contamination beyond the margin. The prospective lane remains clinician-confirmed prepopulation, not silent insertion.

**Supportive for `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` lanes** requires adequate route events, strict lower audit purity `>=0.80`, acceptable agreement/precision, and manageable workload. High reproducible `A_MIXED` or failed `D_MIX` is supportive of admission-level reconciliation and adverse to recommendation-unit-only automation.

**Adverse** to auto nomination includes contamination or mixed-risk above 0.10, target-permutation failure, nuisance prediction, timing leakage, route-cue leakage, unstable unit boundaries, cross-route reviewer sets, or any audit evidence that nominal auto cases include conflicts/uncertainty beyond the approved margin. Adverse to the entire retrospective gate includes systematic split-task disagreement, failed audit sealing, or inability to reconstruct source chronology. An adverse gate result is scientifically informative and should block nomination rather than trigger post hoc rubric repair.

**Inconclusive** includes inadequate `N_A/E_A` or route events, wide simultaneous intervals, extreme unknowns, failed reliability/precision gates without directional evidence, absent governance margins, or a workload ceiling that was never prospectively approved. Comparator failure is specificity-inconclusive only. Sparse `AUTO` is not proof that automation is unnecessary; sparse `RECON` is not proof that discharge handoffs are adequate.

## Mandatory next studies and evidence boundary

After a supportive result, freeze the extractor, packet schema, component crosswalk, route code, margins, and controls. P1 enrolls consecutive live eligible discharges and runs the gate silently. It must capture actual report finalization/addendum and availability/view events, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission events, trigger time, responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestations, preferences/competing risk, and final departure artifact. Independent experts blinded to system route and later outcomes adjudicate route confusion, strict `AUTO` positive predictive value, mixed-admission misses, defer/workload, temporal leakage, drift, and site/service transportability. Require at least 100 route-positive admissions and at least 30 in every claimed route or a prespecified maximum accrual period. Any purity, temporal, or drift failure stops progression.

Only after P1 may a governed P2 route-stratified workflow trial compare usual care with clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation for validated `A_AUTO` and side-by-side reconciliation for validated `A_RECON`. Lanes remain separate. Outcomes include inappropriate insertion, clinician correction/dismissal and reason, signed-artifact fidelity, time/alert burden, responsibility assignment, target-linked ordering/referral/scheduling and communication. Clinical outcomes, harm, and longer-term completion require additional follow-up and independent safety adjudication.

MIMIC can establish only reproducibility of explicitly defined, departure-frozen **text compatibility** states, storage opportunity, conservative route burdens, workload, and whether those measurements pass a preregistered validation-worthiness gate. It cannot establish guideline appropriateness, actual report finalization/viewing, author awareness, draft editing opportunity, recipient accountability, communication, outside plans, patient preference, life expectancy, malignancy, validated follow-up ordering/scheduling/completion, harm, utility, intervention effect, or benefit. The canonical `DS` is a stored artifact, not the complete contemporaneous workflow. Independent expert agreement reduces circularity but is not clinical ground truth.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to the assessed parent, the population, clocks, exhaustive states, ample stratum, unit schema, worst-case admission routes, comparator, calibration, and noncausal claims are unchanged. The substantive repair is that no panel now performs two decision-defining tasks: E sees radiology only; K-context sees target-redacted non-target context; K-plan extracts discharge claims without the source recommendation; C compares neutral payloads without opportunity, context, or route cues. Routes are derived only after sealed component labels. A 40% subject-level audit set is withheld from all tuning and independently remeasured, while still contributing to the full census denominator. Strict audit purity counts every disagreement and unknown adversely. Negative controls directly test target-specificity, nuisance cues, clock isolation, duplicate stability, prohibited fields, and audit-seal integrity. Readiness now depends jointly on workload, route purity, event counts, interval precision, reliability, and falsification tests rather than consensus or apparent route prevalence.

Residual uncertainty is explicit. Complete cognitive blinding may be impossible because payload meaning can reveal that text is absent or discordant; redaction may remove context or leave cues; independent reviewers share medical conventions; the stored note may omit real plans; and a storage interval is not true exposure. The held-out audit estimates measurement reproducibility, not clinical correctness. These limits are why even a fully supportive retrospective result can nominate only prospective silent validation.
