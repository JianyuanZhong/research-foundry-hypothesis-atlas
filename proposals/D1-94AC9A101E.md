# Episode 13: alive day-28 non-acute disposition after post-shock IV-loop initiation

## Substantive repair, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

The parent made alive index-hospital discharge by day 28 (AIHD28) fully computable, but calls hospice, acute-hospital transfer, long-term acute-care (LTAC) transfer, and discharge followed by death before day 28 successes. Those states are clinically unlike recovery and can be affected by discharge practice. This child preserves the population, hour-48 treatment decision, two-clock information contract, unit-safe fluid phenotype, delivered strategies, measured-overlap estimand, clone-censor analysis, ALDL7 respiratory bounds, mortality/RRT safeguards, and exact source bindings. It changes the primary outcome to a fixed-horizon state that cannot reward those transitions.

The strongest available intervention evidence supports only that a bundled conservative-fluid/active-deresuscitation strategy removes fluid and, in a modest randomized secondary analysis, produced no statistically detectable worsening in lactate, AKIRisk, or urinary cystatin-C. McMullan et al. (2026; DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404), inspected frozen full text [source checksum], says clinically important effects remain compatible with its imprecision. The 2026 renal-congestion review (Orieux et al.; DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083), inspected frozen full text [source checksum], calls congestion hypothesis-generating without validated diagnostic or therapeutic implications. Neither establishes benefit, renal safety, or the effect of initiating one delivered loop diuretic after shock.

MIMIC-IV 3.1 documentation, inspected from frozen PhysioNet page [source checksum], says patients.dod combines hospital and state death records, collected two years after final discharge, with death dates censored only after one year from the last discharge. The configured data can therefore classify recorded day-28 vital status, subject to date-only boundary ambiguity and linkage error; it cannot establish function, residence, preferences, or perfect mortality capture.

Hypothesis: among invasively ventilated adults at ICU hour 48 who have recovered from vasopressor-treated shock, have unit-safe cumulative balance at least +50 mL/kg, retained urine output, potassium at least 3.0 mmol/L, and measured treatment overlap, a sustained strategy of initiating an actually delivered ICU IV loop within 24 hours increases by more than 5 percentage points the probability of being alive at day 28 after documented index-hospital discharge by day 28 to a prespecified non-acute, non-hospice destination, versus no loop by any route, without a definitively adverse day-7 respiratory-liberation signal or excess day-28 death or observed day-7 RRT.

Call the primary endpoint ANAD28: alive at day 28 after documented non-acute/non-hospice index discharge by day 28. It is not home recovery, functional independence, freedom from readmission, actual residence, or causal truth.

### Analysis and falsification

The t0 treatment model uses only decision-available pre-t0 demographics, admission type/year/unit/service, prior completed-admission comorbidity/loop history, weight, fluid/urine/creatinine/lactate/potassium trajectories, MAP/pressor trajectory and cessation time, FiO2/PEEP/support, sedation, crystalloid/albumin, oxygenation, and validated timely radiology at 3/6/12-hour lookbacks with missingness indicators. Never use response, final index diagnoses, future notes, destination, or endpoint components.

Use baseline overlap weights plus stabilized hourly adherence/censor weights truncated 1st/99th percentiles. Estimate arm risks/RD by cross-fitted doubly robust estimator with at least 500 subject bootstraps. Unweighted, conventional adjusted, and all-eligible associations are baselines. Report state risks rather than an irreversible discharge-time cumulative-incidence curve, because later day-28 death can negate primary success.

Freeze attrition, strategy/deviation counts, exclusions, overlap, calibration, standardized differences, weight tails, adherence, and ESS before outcomes. Person-hour treatment probability <0.01 or >0.99 in >10%, <100 adherent subjects/arm, ESS <75/arm, or extensive removal by overlap makes the claim infeasible/inconclusive. Eventual treatment must not predict pre-t0 six-hour creatinine, urine, or MAP slopes after adjustment; standardized difference >0.10 or multiplicity-controlled P<0.05 blocks causal interpretation. Weighted trajectories require standardized differences <=0.10. Expected balance/urine direction verifies delivery only. Report an unmeasured-readiness bias grid needed to move RD to 0 and +0.05.

## Result branches

Supportive: overlap/adherent ANAD28 RD point estimate >+0.05 with 95% lower limit >0; AIHD28 and home/home-health sensitivities are compatible; no state-category reversal; ALDL7 is not definitively adverse (95% upper limit of RD_U >0); upper 95% excess death <+0.03; upper 95% excess observed RRT <+0.05 under both brackets; and all process, positivity, ESS, missingness, negative-control, residual-bias, and sensitivity gates pass. This supports external validation/randomization only.

Adverse: 95% upper ANAD28 RD <=0; or 95% upper ALDL7 RD_U <=0; or lower confidence bound for death harm >=+0.03; or lower bound for observed RRT harm >=+0.05. Fluid balance or ordinary AIHD28 cannot override this.

Inconclusive: interval permits both no benefit and >+0.05 benefit; safety upper gates are uncleared without definitive harm; endpoint events are sparse; destination/death coding is differential; date brackets disagree; AIHD28/home/state results conflict; modest residual confounding reverses the estimate; overlap/adherence/ESS fails; temporal anomalies exceed 1% or arm standardized difference 0.10; controls fail; or analyses materially reverse. Nonsignificance is not equivalence/safety.
