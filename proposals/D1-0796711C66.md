## 1. Unresolved question, clinical importance, and advance [K1][K2][K3]

After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / ablation / resection) for HCC, clinicians serialise serum AFP
and must decide when to re-image. The parent branch asks whether the *decay-completed* post-LDT AFP
set-point (`mu_i`) predicts radiologic local progression within the imaging-negative landmark stratum,
beyond the periprocedural AFP *release magnitude* (`A_i`) and beyond liver reserve (ALBI). Even after the
parent's measured identifiability gate, one objection remains unresolved and is the single strongest
threat to interpretation:

- **Leading claim (burden):** the plateau AFP level after the periprocedural spike decays tracks
  *residual viable tumour burden*, so a higher `mu_i` predicts earlier local progression.
- **Strongest rival (release artefact):** the early post-LDT AFP rise is driven by hepatocyte necrosis
  and regeneration (and can include a transient rebound) rather than viable tumour [K1]; on this rival,
  any apparent `mu_i`–progression association is carried by non-tumour AFP dynamics plus baseline
  severity, not by burden.

**The advance this child adds (exactly ONE pre-specified, decision-changing element).** A competing,
*n ecrosis-insensitive* HCC burden marker — DCP / PIVKA-II (`异常凝血酶原`) — is measured in the same
labs table. DCP is not released by hepatocyte necrosis and is an accepted independent HCC burden
biomarker [K2], and prior work already combines AFP + PIVKA-II + ALBI after TACE [K3]. The two rivals
make **opposite-signed, pre-specified predictions on a comparison where they differ**:

| Quantity | Burden claim predicts | Release-artefact rival predicts |
|---|---|---|
| `mean_i` (post-spike decay-completed AFP) vs same-window DCP | positive, non-trivial rank association | weak / near-zero association (AFP level is not burden-driven) |
| `mu_i` → progression HR after adjusting for same-window DCP | attenuates but stays > 1 | collapses toward 1 (no residual AFP signal) |
| `A_i` (release magnitude) vs same-window DCP change | positive (bigger burden = bigger spike) | weak / negative (spike is necrosis-driven) |

This is an estimand/validity advance, not a cosmetic variable addition: the parent could not separate
these two explanations, and the outcome can differ (a real `mu_i` signal may vanish once DCP is
accounted for).

Clinical importance: if `mu_i` survives DCP adjustment, serial AFP set-point estimation is a legitimate
surveillance-intensity signal after LDT; if it does not, the parent's AFP design should be parked in
favour of DCP-based monitoring — a directly consequential monitoring decision.

## 2. Closest inspected prior work and contribution [K1][K2][K3]

- [K1] Yang & Cao 2026 (doi:10.62347/pnpk1326): AFP kinetics are the joint result of tumour-clone
  elimination, treatment-induced liver damage/regeneration and transient rebound; a clean burden
  read-out is not guaranteed. Bounds the burden claim and motivates the rival.
- [K2] Ma et al. 2026 (doi:10.1111/apt.70793): AFP, AFP-L3 and PIVKA-II are each used for recurrence
  prediction, with open standardisation. Establishes DCP as an accepted independent burden marker.
- [K3] Yang H et al. 2026 (doi:10.2147/jhc.s621537): closest prior work — in 139 HCC patients on PD-1
  inhibitor + TACE, both AFP and PIVKA-II reductions independently predicted longer PFS and a combined
  AFP-ALBI-PIVKA-II-albumin score outperformed single biomarkers. **What remains:** K3 uses a >=50%
  reduction threshold, no mechanistic set-point estimand, no imaging-negative stratification, and no
  adjustment for surveillance intensity, so it cannot tell burden from release artefact. This proposal
  tests exactly that discriminating question on a real post-LDT progression endpoint.

## 3. Frozen design (inherited verbatim from the parent)

- **Population:** patients with an HCC diagnosis (`诊断名称` containing `肝细胞癌` / `肝癌`) and >=1
  liver-directed procedure in `手术` (`TACE`/`tace`, `肝动脉化疗栓塞`, resection, ablation; diagnostic
  `肝动脉造影` excluded).
- **Index time:** earliest qualifying LDT `开始时间`.
- **Landmark:** `t* = index + 180d`. Exposure fitting/window uses only draws in `(index, t*]`.
- **AFP:** labs `检验='甲胎蛋白'`, value `定量结果`, time `检验时间`.
- **ALBI:** same-draw albumin + bilirubin within +/-2d of `检验时间`; `ALBI = 0.66*log10(bili) - 0.085*alb`.
- **Endpoint:** modality-restricted abdominal local-progression event from `检查所见`/`检查诊断`
  (`开始时间`), with `转移` as competing event; censor via `出院时间` (encounters table).
- **Join keys:** `患者主索引` + `就诊号`. Source read-only; derived files in workspace.
- **Imaging-negative stratum (IMGneg):** no local-progression-positive imaging in `[t*-45d, t*+45d]`.

## 4. The ONE added element: DCP-adjudicated discriminator (pre-specified)

1. **DCP exposure.** Labs `检验='异常凝血酶原'` (exact match; exclude look-alikes `凝血酶原`,
   `凝血酶原时间`, `凝血酶原活动度`), value `定量结果`, time `检验时间`. Same-window = within +/-2d of the
   AFP set-point draw time.
2. **Co-availability gate (measured, pre-committed).** Among the AFP set-point-evaluable population,
   require >=40% to have a same-window DCP draw. **FAIL = primary arm inconclusive (parked), not
   negative.**
3. **Discriminating comparisons.**
   - D-A: rank correlation of `mu_i` (decay-completed AFP set-point) with same-window DCP, and of `A_i`
     (release magnitude) with same-window DCP change.
   - D-B: primary Cox of local progression (within IMGneg) on `mu_i` (per 1 log10), **adjusted for
     same-window log10-DCP**, vs the parent's unadjusted model, with `A_i` as rival exposure and
     surveillance-intensity adjustment retained.
4. **Simple baseline vs alternative (recorded before selection).**
   - **M0 (simple baseline):** all-draw OLS slope of log10 AFP over `(index, t*]` as the sole AFP
     trajectory exposure, Cox on progression.
   - **M_pulse (mechanistic alternative):** per-patient pulse-decay NLME set-point `mu_i` (post-spike
     decay-completed level). M_pulse can reveal a *decay-independent* residual-burden level that M0's
     single slope averages over; M0 cannot separate spike magnitude from plateau. Both are compared on
     the same question and outcome; the added DCP-adjustment applies to whichever trajectory exposure
     wins on AIC.
   - *Resource estimate:* CPU-only, tabular fits, minutes to tens of minutes; GPU not required. Not
     measured beyond the availability probe.

## 5. Falsification and interpretation (pre-committed)

- **Supportive (burden):** `mu_i` HR > 1 with CI excluding 1, >=30 IMGneg events, dAIC > 10 vs M0,
  AND `mu_i`–DCP rank association materially positive, AND `mu_i` HR attenuates toward 1 but stays > 1
  after DCP adjustment.
- **Adverse (release artefact):** `mu_i` HR ~ 1 while `A_i` HR > 1, OR `mu_i` HR collapses to within
  +/-0.10 of 1 after DCP adjustment, OR dAIC <= 0, OR near-zero `mu_i`–DCP association.
- **Inconclusive:** co-availability gate FAIL; or <30 IMGneg events; or CI spans 1 with wide interval.
- **Precise-null:** `mu_i` HR within +/-0.10 of 1 with a flat ALBI negative control.
- **Interpretation guard:** an association surviving DCP adjustment does not by itself identify a
  mechanism; supportive results establish prognostic utility of the set-point, not causality.

## 6. Honest limits

- Progression flag from `检查所见`/`检查诊断` is a **lexical** extraction requiring radiologist mRECIST
  adjudication; a pre-registered audit and fallback to the retreatment proxy are retained from the
  parent.
- No mortality / mRECIST PFS is ascertainable -> **no survival claim**.
- Single institution; no lab units in the source -> DCP analysed on the log10 scale within the cohort,
  no external cutoff.
- DCP co-availability is the key feasibility uncertainty (probe: 6,054 DCP rows vs 37,400 AFP rows in a
  bounded prefix scan); the gate above governs it.
- Causal claims require expert review or another study.
