# Source-robust delayed CBC cancer-type contrast under ascertainment falsification

Status: proposed substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. Planned Harbor experiment only: no cohort scan, model fit, bootstrap, or participant-level result was run.

## Scientific opening and falsifiable hypothesis

The leading CBC contrast—higher platelets with lower haemoglobin, versus the reverse—could reflect CRC biology, but it could also be generated by observation. CRC work-up may bring a CBC close to registry diagnosis; haematologic disease may be preferentially captured during inpatient care; coding and referral change by calendar era; and people with a later repeat assessment are selected. An absolute-risk label is therefore descriptive unless it survives these alternatives.

The strongest supported claim is narrower. [K1] reports retrospective longitudinal CBC trajectories in a CRC-only cohort, but calls the finding investigational and requiring prospective external validation. [K2] finds blood-test trend evidence fragmented and exploratory and identifies informative observation as a bias theme. [K3] finds CRC prediction studies often report discrimination while calibration and internal/external validation are limited. These works do not establish a delayed CRC-versus-haematologic contrast in an unselected cancer-free population, do not establish registry or inpatient dates as biological onset, and do not establish clinical utility.

Hypothesis:

> Among UKB adults with a complete first CBC and no qualifying cancer or death recorded in either source at T0, a fixed platelet-high/haemoglobin-low versus platelet-low/haemoglobin-high profile has a positive, clinically material CRC-over-haematologic absolute-risk contrast during T0+2 to T0+5 that remains positive in the weaker of registry and inpatient outcome series, is not confined to 0–90 days or one calendar era, and is not explained by selection into a future repeat assessment.

The substantive advance is a source-robust primary estimand: the decision to proceed to an indication-rich validation study is based on the weaker source, not a favorable source or relative coefficient. The +1 percentage-point boundary below is a descriptive next-study scale marker, not a referral threshold, treatment effect, diagnostic probability, or minimum clinically important difference. This is an observational population-standardized association, not a causal, mechanistic, screening, stage, treatment, transportability, or clinical-utility claim.

## Primary estimand and outcomes

Define g_C as platelets at development mean +1 SD and haemoglobin at mean −1 SD; define g_H as platelets at mean −1 SD and haemoglobin at mean +1 SD. WBC and RDW are fixed at development means. Freeze all means/SDs before holdout scoring. These are hypothetical profiles, not treatment assignments or observed labels.

Use separate outcome series s in {R,I}: registry (R) and inpatient (I). For each source classify CRC as ICD-10 C18–C20, H as C81–C96, Other primary as C00–C97 excluding CRC and H and excluding secondary C77–C79, and death as 40000-0.0.

For the common delayed-entry population, follow each source independently from T0+2 through T0+5, first source-specific CRC/H/Other event, death, administrative censoring at 2022-12-31, or window end. Exclude any qualifying cancer or death in either source through T0+2 before entering this delayed risk set. This is descriptive delayed entry, not a causal survivor effect.

Let F_s,c(g;2–5) be model-standardized cumulative incidence and define Δ_s,c = F_s,c(g_C;2–5) − F_s,c(g_H;2–5), T_s = Δ_s,CRC − Δ_s,H, and primary M = min(T_R,T_I), the weakest-source delayed CRC-over-H contrast. Report all four CRC/H component risks, both deltas, T_R, T_I and M in percentage points with participant-bootstrap 95% intervals. The minimum is a robustness functional, not a claim that sources measure identical disease processes. Repeat quantities for 0–90 days and 90 days–2 years as rival-explanation windows.

Also report Δ_s,Other, total burden B_s = F_s,All(g_C)−F_s,All(g_H), with All = CRC+H+Other, and residual display S_s = Δ_s,CRC − 0.5(Δ_s,H+Δ_s,Other). Comparable Other contrast or large B_s with near-zero S_s supports generic burden/capture rather than CRC specificity.

## Baseline and learned alternative

The auditable baseline fits independently by source four cause-specific Cox models for CRC, H, Other and death using standardized instance-0 WBC, haemoglobin, RDW, platelets, age, sex, BMI, smoking, CRP and T0 year. Use robust variance and record proportional-hazard diagnostics. Convert hazards to profile-standardized competing-risk incidence. Provide Aalen–Johansen curves/tables as an observed source-specific incidence check; Aalen–Johansen alone cannot answer the fixed-profile estimand.

The same-input learned alternative is a CPU gradient-boosted discrete-time multi-task competing-risk model, separately for registry and inpatient outcomes, with separate hazards for CRC, H, Other and death. It uses exactly the same people, exclusions, first-CBC inputs, source-specific outcomes, windows, split, profiles and uncertainty. Use a first 90-day interval and yearly intervals thereafter. T1/T2 and post-T0 outcomes are forbidden as predictors. It can reveal nonlinear CBC ranges, interactions and time-varying source/class hazards that Cox compresses; it cannot recover symptoms, indication, utilisation, pathology, stage, treatment or biological onset. This compares scientific structures, not small predictive gains.

On holdout report all F, Δ, T, M, B, S, calibration intercept/slope and plots, 2-year/5-year cumulative-incidence Brier and log scores, and 500 participant-bootstrap intervals. AUC/Uno C-index is secondary. If fewer than 50 held-out H events exist, type-specific learned and source-robust conclusions are inconclusive; never replace the split or endpoint. Baseline is primary for auditable decomposition; the learned model is retained for substantive nonlinear/time-varying uncertainty. A transformer or GP is deferred because three CBC assessment instances do not identify a rich latent trajectory and missing indication/pathology would remain missing. No GPU is required. Future solver estimate: 8–16 CPU cores, 32–64 GiB RAM, 2–4 hours; unmeasured. Planning envelope: 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB and 28800 seconds.

## Bounded falsification hierarchy

Run in order and preserve adverse results.

1. Binding/integrity: verify source ID/hash/namespace, one-row-per-eid, paired indices, date parsing, event support, cohort flow and split. Unresolved pairing or namespace is a computational failure.
2. Diagnostic proximity/source timing: report 0–90 days, 90 days–2 years, and 2–5 years. An effect only in 0–90 days, or lost after removing it, supports diagnostic proximity. For same-eid same-class registry/inpatient observations, report paired counts, date displacement median/IQR/5th–95th percentiles and fractions crossing 90 days and 2 years. This is a bridge diagnostic only; never merge dates or call one biological onset. Fewer than 50 paired class events makes this interpretation inconclusive.
3. Generic burden: compare ΔCRC, ΔH, ΔOther, B and S by source. Other comparability or generic-burden dominance is adverse to CRC specificity.
4. Calendar: define T0-era bins from development dates only, apply them unchanged to holdout, and report source-specific contrasts and T0-year interaction. Era confinement is adverse.
5. Repeat selection: R=1 only if T1 is strictly after T0, 2–8 years later, and all four instance-1 CBC fields are present; otherwise R=0. R never enters primary prediction. Estimate source quantities in R=1/R=0; fit a development-only R model from baseline CBC/covariates and report stabilized inverse-probability-weighted versus unweighted estimates, weight tails, effective sample size and positivity. This is a stress test, not an identified intervention effect. Poor positivity or fewer than 50 held-out H events is inconclusive; favorable trimming cannot rescue it.
6. Implementation specificity: within development permute CRC/H labels while preserving event times, censoring and source; contrast should collapse. Re-run excluding invalid/missing date-code pairs. This tests implementation, not biology.
7. Absolute-risk consistency: compare relative coefficients with M, component risks, calibration, Brier and log scores. A coefficient or ranking gain without calibrated absolute separation is adverse to decision-facing interpretation.

Supportive requires both delayed T_s positive with intervals excluding zero; lower 95% bound for M above +1 point; CRC contrast larger than Other and positive S in both sources; no early/era/repeat confinement; adequate overlap and weighted/unweighted agreement; permutation collapse; and no learned-model overturning of calibrated absolute interpretation. This justifies external, indication-rich validation, not deployment.

Adverse includes negative/source-reversing delayed effects, boundary crossing, source disagreement changing the decision, Other comparability, generic burden, early-only/era/repeat confinement, or learned-only discrimination gain. It weakens a stable CRC-specific interpretation but does not refute nonspecific CBC/cancer association.

Inconclusive includes fewer than 50 held-out H events, fewer than 50 paired same-class bridge events, poor positivity, invalid pairing, nonconvergence, or intervals spanning both zero and +1 point. A wide null is not refutation.

## Alternatives not chosen and evidence for revisiting

Chosen: source-separated Cox/Aalen–Johansen plus same-input CPU boosted discrete-time competing risks. Cox is primary for auditability; the learned alternative is scientifically justified to expose nonlinear and time-varying structure.

Deferred: transformer/GP latent trajectories pending dense dated CBCs and observation metadata, or a reproducible nonlinear structure that remains calibrated. Deferred causal mediation/treatment effects pending a prospective indication-rich cohort, valid intervention timing, adjudicated onset and treatment/stage data. These are not computational failures and no fitted result is claimed.
