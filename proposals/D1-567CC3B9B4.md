# A report-availability frontier for clinically interpretable M2 triage: separate semantic content from refitting effects

## Clinical question, current evidence, and the unresolved claim

The decision question remains deliberately narrow: 24 hours before an adult patient's first eligible liver resection, could routine CT/MRI report semantics add enough information to a laboratory-independent risk flag to justify multidisciplinary review for pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)?

The output is a historical classification/review flag. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

**Strongest claim supported now.** Direct catalog, schema, header, and row inspection establishes that the HCC snapshot has operation start times, imaging acquisition start times, eventual CT/MRI report text, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. It does not have a radiology report authored/final/release/view time, a pathology time or specimen identifier, raw images/slides, a documented sampling protocol, recurrence, survival, treatment benefit, or clinician response. The laboratory table has only one ambiguous `检验时间` and no collection, verification, release, view, panel/accession, or unit field; therefore no laboratory value or process derivative is primary.

Direct full-file inspection counted 338,040 procedure rows and 46,395 pathology rows. Under an explicitly auditable 2015–2021 liver-resection rule, there were 11,658 first-index patients; 11,328 also had same-encounter pathology text identifying HCC, including 2,087 recorded laparoscopic and 9,240 non-laparoscopic index resections (one converted/mixed record was retained for exclusion audit). The corresponding index-hospitalization length of stay was computable for all 11,328 in this preliminary join, and 1,262 had a subsequent institutional encounter within 30 days of discharge. These are feasibility counts, not final endpoint or causal estimates; the final experiment uses its frozen clinical dictionary and reports every attrition step. Direct source inspection also found that `procedures` contains explicit laparoscopic labels, while `encounters` supplies admission/discharge clocks and `labs` supplies same-assay values but no units. An unanchored M2 lexical search remains invalid because definitions and boilerplate can mention M2 without assigning it to the index specimen.

The inspected full XML of Li et al. (2025; DOI `10.1111/jcmm.70746`, PMCID `PMC12309289`, frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) supports that MVI is pathology-defined, prognostically relevant, and unavailable for direct preoperative decision-making; it reviews the Chinese M0/M1/M2 classification and limitations of preoperative models. The inspected full HTML of Huang et al. (2026; DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`, frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports a retrospective multicenter binary-MVI model using CT margin irregularity and AFP positivity (487 development, 256 external validation; AUC 0.740 and 0.781), with complete-data eligibility, blinded raw-image review, specified pathology sampling, and exploratory decision curves. It does not validate M2, routine report semantics, report availability, this dataset, or treatment benefit.

**Residual defect in the parent.** The parent robustly fits `S` and `B` as separate models and then minimizes their paired net-benefit difference over report states. When all reports are unavailable, the semantic fields in both models are masked, but independently fitted coefficients, penalties, and recalibration can still make `S` and `B` act differently on shared features. Thus a positive “semantic” lower bound can arise in a state where no semantic content is visible. Because the all-unavailable state is permitted for every patient, a genuine runtime content increment must be exactly zero there. This is a scientific estimand problem, not a compiler repair.

**Unresolved stored-content hypothesis P.** In the locked 2020–2021 whole target frame, when all selected stored CT/MRI reports are exposed, does the semantic block change decisions in a clinically material direction relative to an ablation of the *same fitted semantic model* with only that block masked, while the complete semantic strategy also outperforms an independently optimized structured/basic-report baseline? At `p=0.30`, both paired net-benefit increments must be at least 0.01 and their simultaneous lower confidence limits must exceed zero; both must remain positive at every threshold 0.25–0.35.

**Unresolved deployment claim D.** Would the semantic strategy add material value under the reports actually visible at `t_dec`? The HCC snapshot cannot identify that claim. The experiment will compute an exact worst-case value frontier as a function of a hypothetical minimum fraction `c` of patients with at least one selected report visible and derive the minimum sufficient coverage `c*`. D is supported only if independent source-system evidence, using the same report unit and clock, supplies a lower confidence bound on actual coverage at least `c*`. Without that external audit, even a favorable Harbor result supports stored-content potential and quantifies a prospective workflow requirement; it does not support historical availability or deployment value.

The substantive advance is to separate (i) within-pipeline action value of runtime semantic content, (ii) value of the complete refitted strategy versus an optimized baseline, and (iii) the unknown report-access mechanism. It converts an apparently availability-robust semantic claim into an exact coverage-response question that the available data can partially answer and a source-system audit can complete.

**Claims deliberately unchanged from the leader.** The patient-level unit, first eligible resection frame, 24-hour decision cutoff, 90-day imaging window, 12/48/72-hour sensitivities, 365-day treatment-history window, 2015–2018 development / 2019 tuning / 2020–2021 locked test split, pathology-only documentary M2 endpoint, whole-frame `V=0` denominator, report-state Cartesian product, patient-wise worst-state convex fitting, zero laboratory lineage, B/S/W/A definitions, thresholds, bootstrap and permutation discipline, and all non-treatment claim limits are retained. The only primary-claim change is attribution: the parent’s uniformly report-state-robust S–B value remains a named secondary diagnostic, while the new primary P is the identifiable same-fit S–S0 stored-content contrast and D is explicitly conditional on an external visibility audit.

## Pathology-only documentary endpoint

`Y=1` only for pathology-documented M2; `Y=0` for M0 or M1. `V=1` means a trustworthy assignment to the index resection specimen under a frozen position-aware parser and blinded adjudication. Missing grade, positive-ungraded MVI, conflict, uncertain specimen linkage, or inadequate documented sampling is `V=0`, never an exclusion.

Assign grade only from the immediate selected value after an anchored field such as `MVI提示风险分级[:：]`, stopping before definitions, explanatory bullets, historical clauses, or another specimen section, or from a frozen synonymous selected-value construction accepted in development review. Handle negation, `未见`, multiple specimens, and discordance. Unanchored M2, binary MVI, positive-ungraded MVI, absent language, definitions, and conflicts cannot assign grade.

Two independently implemented parsers must agree on locked test records. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, every test `V=0`, and at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 M1 sampled by year/template; a third resolves disagreements. Require class PPV ≥0.98, sensitivity ≥0.95, Cohen kappa ≥0.90, and parser disagreement ≤5%. Review may infer grade only from explicit number/distance language under the frozen definition.

Raw slides, specimen IDs, block counts, vessel-distance measurements, and sampling protocols are unavailable. The endpoint is documentary M2, not latent biological M2. Biological ascertainment requires specimen/slide review in another study.

## Report states and laboratory prohibition

For patient `i`, let `q_i` be the number (0, 1, or 2) of selected CT/MRI bundles with nonempty eventual report text and `Omega_i^R={0,1}^{q_i}`. One means that stored bundle text is exposed; zero means unavailable. Empty stored text has only state zero. No probability, independence, or relation to outcome is assumed.

At state zero, mask every report-body-derived item from that bundle: size, count, thrombus, formatting, and all semantic findings. Preserve acquisition facts only: modality from `检查`, acquisition time, bundle count, distinct-`检查号` count without exposing the identifier, and development-frequency machine-availability category. The same state and bit ordering apply to every compared score for a patient.

Laboratory rows may be audited when valid `检验时间` is in `[t_dec-30 days,t_dec)`, but no laboratory field or derivative—including row existence, assay/specimen, value, count, missingness, or time—may enter a primary feature matrix, fit, recalibration, state, threshold, stratum, or gate. Execution must prove zero lab lineage.

## Models and the semantic-content ablation

All fitted models are interpretable elastic-net logistic regressions using the parent's exact patient-wise worst-state convex loss for 2015–2018 fitting, 2019 tuning, 2019 nonnegative-slope recalibration, every bootstrap refit, and every matched-null refit. Equal-state, all-report, and no-report fits are diagnostic only.

`B`, the independently optimized clinical baseline, contains age, sex, CT/MRI acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category, and—only in report-exposed states—frozen maximum lesion diameter, lesion-count category, and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus with unknown indicators.

`S` contains exactly B plus the semantic block `C_R`: frozen present/absent/unknown extractions from `检查所见` and `检查诊断` for capsule complete/interrupted/absent, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite or peritumoral lesion/enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity. S contains no raw identifier, report code, year, pathology/diagnosis/procedure term, unrestricted text, laboratory field, or post-cutoff content.

`S0` is not refitted. For every patient and report state, compute S0 from S's exact fitted coefficients, intercept, preprocessing, penalty-selected pipeline, and recalibration, but set only `C_R` to its frozen state-zero reference vector. Keep B-type report variables and every nonsemantic input at the same report state. Therefore `score_S(i,r)=score_S0(i,r)` whenever no selected report is exposed. This equality is a required machine-checkable invariant. The S–S0 contrast isolates decision changes caused by runtime semantic values within the fitted S pipeline; it does not establish biological or causal meaning.

Retain the parent's `W` workflow/provenance model and nested `A=W+C_R` as secondary process-confounding diagnostics, with identical masks and robust fitting. They cannot rescue the primary S–S0 content or S–B strategy claims. The unrestricted-text ceiling remains diagnostic only.

Two blinded radiologists review extraction reliability by year and CT/MRI provenance. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in ≥90%, count-category agreement ≥90% and kappa ≥0.80, and per-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity ≥0.85 for each binary semantic feature. This assesses stored-text interpretation, not availability.

## Estimands and exact coverage-constrained bounds

At threshold `p`, `w=p/(1-p)`, action `I_Mi(r,p)=1[score_Mi(r)>=p]`, and paired contribution for contrast `k=(M1,M0)` is

`d_i^k(y,r,p) = [y-w(1-y)] [I_M1i(r,p)-I_M0i(r,p)]`.

Primary contrasts are content `D=(S,S0)` and complete-strategy value `C=(S,B)`. Compute descriptive A–W, S–W, and each model versus flag-all/flag-none.

For `V=0`, enumerate `y in {0,1}`; for `V=1`, use adjudicated Y. No patient is dropped or imputed.

**Stored-content potential.** Compute D and C with all selected nonempty reports exposed. The primary potential claim P uses D; C is a necessary co-gate showing the complete strategy also beats an independently optimized baseline.

**Coverage frontier.** Define `a_i(r)=1` if at least one selected nonempty report is exposed in state r and zero otherwise. For each attainable integer `k=0,...,sum_i 1(q_i>0)`, let `c=k/N` and constrain joint patient states to `sum_i a_i(r_i)>=k`. Compute

`L_j(c,p)=N^-1 min sum_i d_i^j(y_i,r_i,p)`,
`U_j(c,p)=N^-1 max sum_i d_i^j(y_i,r_i,p)`,

for j in {D,C}, with shared r within each contrast and y enumeration for V=0. Use exact multiple-choice cardinality dynamic programming (or an algebraically equivalent sorted binary-cost solution verified against brute force on fixtures); no probability model or equal weighting is allowed. Report infeasible coverage above `c_max=N^-1 sum_i 1(q_i>0)`. Coordinatewise extrema need not represent one latent world.

At p=0.30, define `c*` as the smallest attainable c such that, for every attainable c' from c through c_max, both D and C have point lower bound ≥0.01, simultaneous lower confidence limit >0, and lower net benefit for S above both defaults; D and C must also have positive point lower bounds at every p=0.25,...,0.35. If no such c exists, `c*` is undefined. This all-higher-coverage rule prevents claiming a threshold from a nonmonotone frontier.

The actual source-system audit required to resolve D must identify, for every selected bundle in a patient sample from the same calendar/workflow, a report finalization/release timestamp and preferably clinician-view timestamp; apply `t_dec`, the same bundle selection, and the same “at least one visible report” unit. It must report patient coverage and a one-sided 95% lower confidence bound overall and by CT/MRI. An order/accession time alone is insufficient. This evidence is unavailable in the configured HCC snapshot and cannot be imputed from nonempty eventual text.

## Inference, falsification, and gates

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps resampling within calendar period. Rebuild states, refit preprocessing, robust B/S/W/A models, tune and recalibrate, reconstruct the same-fit S0 ablation, and recompute all-content and frontier endpoints. Use the 95th percentile of the maximum absolute centered deviation over L/U endpoints for D and C at all attainable coverage levels summarized on a frozen 0.05 grid plus c_max and at all thresholds 0.20–0.40 for one simultaneous band. Pointwise intervals are descriptive. More than 2.5% failed fits/replicates fails inference.

Retain 1,000 process-matched semantic-null repetitions: permute the entire C_R block within frozen year, CT/MRI provenance, report-state cardinality, and formatting-family availability in development/tuning only; robustly refit S and A and reconstruct S0. Observed all-report D at 0.30 must exceed the 97.5th percentile. Label permutation and post-cutoff leakage fixtures must behave as null and leakage controls.

Global interpretation requires test N≥1,000, at least 75 adjudicated V=1 M2 outcomes, V=1≥85%, review of every test V=0, all parser/radiologist thresholds, report text nonempty in ≥80%, structured size/count jointly extractable in ≥70%, no required-feature missingness shift >15 percentage points from 2019 to either test year, at least 100 patients and 20 V=1 M2 in each claimed CT-only/MRI-only stratum, exact robust-fit convergence, complete inference, shared states, and zero forbidden/laboratory lineage. Among V=1, report AUPRC, AUROC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV for all-report and no-report diagnostics. Calibration slope must be 0.80–1.20, intercept -0.10–0.10, sensitivity ≥0.70, and PPV ≥0.50 at 0.30 in both diagnostic states for a global strategy claim.

**Supportive P result:** every gate passes; all-report D and C are each at least 0.01 at 0.30 with simultaneous lower limits >0; both are positive throughout 0.25–0.35; S beats both defaults; and the semantic-null control passes. The strongest supported conclusion is that the S-versus-S0 contrast shows materially favorable action changes when the semantic block is replaced within the frozen S scoring pipeline and that the complete S pipeline outperformed B in this historical documentary-M2 frame under maximal stored-report exposure. It does not establish report visibility or deployment benefit.

**Adverse result:** gates pass but the simultaneous upper limit for all-report D is <0.01, D is nonpositive, C cannot materially beat B/defaults, the null control is not exceeded, or no attainable coverage has both lower frontiers supportive. This falsifies clinically material semantic-content potential in the tested frame under the prespecified strategy; more availability cannot rescue failure in the all-report state. Discrimination alone cannot rescue it.

**Inconclusive result:** any gate fails, final M2 count is too small, endpoint/extraction adjudication fails, simultaneous intervals cross required boundaries, or frontier behavior does not define c*. A positive point estimate, AUC, unrestricted-text result, equal-weight fit, or isolated threshold is not support.

**Mixed results:** D supportive but C adverse means semantics change actions favorably within S but the full strategy does not beat an optimized baseline; do not recommend S. C supportive but D adverse means refitting or shared-feature differences may help, but runtime semantic content is not shown valuable; do not call it semantic gain.

**Deployment-conditional result:** only after a valid external source-system audit may D be called supported, and only if its overall and relevant modality-specific lower coverage confidence bounds are at least c* and all other gates pass. If audit coverage is below c*, deployment value is adverse under the prespecified robust criterion. If no audit exists or its clock/unit differs, deployment remains inconclusive regardless of P.

## Compiler and verifier contract

The compiler must preserve the first-resection frame, t_dec/windows, temporal split, pathology Y/V and whole-frame denominator, zero-lab lineage, exact report states, robust fitting, independently optimized B, fitted S, same-fit S0 ablation, D and C contrasts, all-report potential analysis, exact coverage frontier/c*, simultaneous inference, and result zones. Replacing S0 with a refitted model, allowing S and S0 to differ at all-unavailable state, assuming a visibility distribution, or treating nonempty stored text as visible is a scientific change requiring a Lead-approved child.

Computationally checkable claims include source/header/hash agreement; dictionaries; dedup/joins; periods/windows; state masks/order; same states within contrasts; forbidden-feature absence; robust objective arithmetic; S0 identity to S except C_R; exact score/action equality when C_R is masked; Y/V enumeration; whole-frame N; dynamic-programming frontier versus brute force; c* rule; bootstrap family; matched-null test; and conclusion-to-output links.

Verifier fixtures must include correct supportive stored-content, adverse, inconclusive, and mixed outputs; an external-audit-present conditional-support fixture; and an external-audit-absent fixture that rejects deployment wording. Bad solutions must include a separately refitted S0, fabricated report times, eventual-text-equals-visible, equal-state weighting, complete-case V, different states across compared scores, pointwise-as-simultaneous inference, cherry-picked coverage/threshold, favorable AUC with adverse net benefit, and numerically correct outputs followed by treatment, recurrence, survival, biological-M2, contemporary, external-transport, threshold-acceptable, clinician-benefit, or unconditional deployment claims.

An automatic verifier cannot establish Chinese clinical semantics, reviewer qualifications, actual report finalization/view, specimen linkage, sampling adequacy, biological M2, threshold/workload acceptability, clinician action, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, or patient benefit. Those require expert adjudication, source-system evidence, stakeholder review, prospective workflow/outcome data, or another study. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility and conclusion linkage, not scientific truth.
