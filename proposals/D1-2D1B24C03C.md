# Episode 8 evolution: day-42 HCC assay state with support-gated workflow and non-TACE checks

Status: substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`; design only. No cohort fit, model result, or clinical response conclusion is claimed.

## Decision and scientific deliverable

The primary scientific question is unchanged:

> Among patients observable at day 42 after a first locally recorded exact-code TACE, does the recorded assay-state channel add patient-held-out information about a later locally recorded exact-code repeat-TACE event beyond matched observation and care-process information?

The important unresolved boundary is whether an apparent assay increment is reusable recorded state information or merely the workflow that creates both assay records and a later procedure record. The available evidence supports only that imaging-rich models can predict repeat-TACE prognosis in a selected, externally tested cohort ([K1]), that HCC treatment response requires modality- and criterion-specific imaging rather than an arbitrary examination row ([K2]), and that recorded response estimates are vulnerable to ascertainment, missingness and treatment-selection limitations ([K3]). None establishes that a local assay value is tumor response, that a repeat-TACE code is a clinical response label, or that an association is causal.

This child makes the minimum useful experiment explicit:

1. The sole primary decision is the overall held-out assay increment `D_state = L(A) - L(B)`, where `A = M0 + P_obs + P_care` and `B = A + S`.
2. `W_packet` is retained only as one support-gated workflow-accounting secondary. It asks whether the assay increment remains after the exact, pre-outcome review/completion pathway is made visible. It is not a proxy-stratum discovery exercise.
3. `Y_NC` is retained only as one support-gated negative-control-like secondary, using the first non-TACE procedure. It tests generic care-process transport; it is not a clinical control outcome and cannot establish specificity.
4. Neither secondary can rescue, replace, down-rank or redefine the primary. Neither may be substituted with `E_any`, `O_open`, a broad status list, an encounter, medication, laboratory or other endpoint.

Completion is a frozen cohort and leakage audit, newly fitted development-only weights and elastic-net/GRU models, untouched patient-held-out predictions and losses, calibration/uncertainty, the two secondary gate reports, falsification outputs and a claim-output map. Completion is not a favorable finding.

## Competing explanations and what the experiment can establish

The leading explanation is that measured assay values or qualitative states contain recorded information about patient state. The strongest rivals are:

- workflow/selection: an order, review, encounter or planned care pathway produces both an assay record and a subsequent procedure;
- lead time: records close to day 42 encode a near-term plan rather than a durable state;
- severity/measurement: unmeasured severity or access drives assay recording, review intensity and procedures.

The overall state contrast tests predictive/descriptive incremental information. A positive result is not a causal effect, tumor-response estimate, treatment-benefit estimate or treatment rule. The workflow secondary tests whether the increment attenuates after the exact review/completion packet is represented; it cannot remove all unmeasured workflow. The non-TACE secondary tests whether a similar increment follows generic procedure care; it cannot prove biological specificity.

A state-consistent pattern is a primary increment that remains directionally consistent under observation weighting, explicit `W_packet`, seven-day lead-time removal and assay value/time permutations, with a smaller or unsupported increment for `Y_NC`. A workflow-consistent pattern is attenuation after `W_packet`, concentration in its supported pathway, disappearance under the lead-time control, or a comparable `Y_NC` increment. Both patterns remain compatible with residual severity confounding.

## Frozen review proxy and deterministic secondary gates

- `O_open`: an orders row with valid `order time`;
- `O_done`: an orders row with valid `End Time` and exact observed `Order Status = Examination Completed`;

Missing order end cannot create a completed link.
