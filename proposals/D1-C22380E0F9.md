# First-day monitored invasive-MAP burden, latent full-clock burden, and subsequent creatinine-defined AKI

## Status and actual scientific deliverable

This is a substantive crossover of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves invasive-MAP exposure in the half-open ICU-clock interval `[0,1440)`, the fixed hour-24 landmark, and a creatinine outcome using only values strictly after minute 1,440. Neither parent opened the target MAP–AKI association, and this crossover does not treat either outcome-blind audit as an association result.

The solver must newly construct one frozen, outcome-blind index encounter per person; fit the observation models O0/O1, transparent outcome models B0/B1/B2, observed temporal model T1, and target-free measurement models M0/M1; evaluate held-hospital performance and uncertainty; and, only if M1 passes its sealed gate, fit the secondary latent-burden outcome model L1. Completion means producing the prespecified cohort, measurement, observation, outcome, transport, uncertainty, and claim-link outputs. Confirmation of the hypothesis is not required.

The design resolves the parents' central disagreement as follows:

- **Primary exposure and estimand:** duration-normalized **monitored-time burden**, which is computed only from observed valid invasive MAP bins and is not called all-clock exposure.
- **Gated secondary exposure and estimand:** posterior **latent full-clock burden** from M1, analyzed only after a target-free held-hospital pseudo-missingness gate. Failure of M1 is adverse evidence for the latent reconstruction claim but does not veto the directly observed monitored-time experiment.
- **Person rule:** one reproducibly sampled eligible health-system encounter per `uniquepid`, selected before inspecting MAP or creatinine support. eICU lacks cross-hospital dates, so this is an index sample, not a first-ever or chronologically first admission.
- **Temporal alternative:** T1 uses observed hourly MAP summaries, masks, time since observation, and pressor-entry masks. It remains estimable if M1 fails. A separate M1-derived latent extension is secondary.

## Scientific opening, evidence, and hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the inspected literature is limited. A systematic review found highly heterogeneous ICU hypotension definitions; most included studies associated hypotension with AKI, but the aggregate AKI meta-analysis was not confirmatory [K1]. In 423 severe-sepsis patients, FINNAKI found lower first-day time-adjusted MAP associated with five-day AKI progression under prospectively validated dense monitoring [K2]. KDIGO defines AKI from timed creatinine and urine-output changes while warning that creatinine-only and incompletely observed implementations miss or misclassify cases and do not identify etiology [K3].

The unresolved primary hypothesis is:

> Among adults represented by one outcome-blind index eICU encounter, alive and still in ICU at hour 24, with at least 202 of 288 valid first-day invasive-MAP bins, an admission and pre-landmark creatinine, no creatinine-defined progression through hour 24, and at least one strictly later creatinine, greater duration-normalized monitored time below MAP 65 mmHg is associated with higher held-hospital risk of subsequent creatinine-defined AKI beyond a flexible MAP-nadir model.

The gated secondary hypothesis is that an observation-aware M1 model can recover full-clock burden with prespecified held-hospital error and calibration, and that this latent burden retains a positive association beyond nadir after propagating reconstruction uncertainty. Passing pseudo-missing validation is necessary but cannot establish true MAP during undocumented arterial-line absence.

This is a conditional prognostic/descriptive question. “Beyond nadir” is a measured conditional contrast with standardization, not randomization at a common nadir. A robust positive result would justify retaining monitored cumulative hypotension, rather than only a single nadir, in prospective monitoring studies. A precise adverse result would argue against promoting this noisier summary in the defined population. Neither result chooses a treatment threshold or proves that raising MAP prevents AKI.

The leading explanation is that sustained low perfusion contains information lost by nadir. The strongest rival is a coupled care process: severity influences arterial-line retention, vasopressor entries, creatinine testing, and discharge, so apparent duration may reflect surveillance and treatment. The predictions differ. A monitored-burden signal should survive exact missingness features, renal-trajectory adjustment, fixed-window ascertainment, observation weighting, within-hospital comparison, and hospital holdout. A care-process signal should track line boundaries, site conventions, pressor response, or testing opportunity and attenuate under those checks. Association after adjustment cannot identify a renal-perfusion mechanism.

The observed-outcome cohort contained 1,456 aggregate events across 160 hospitals. Six stays passed an old inclusive coverage count but failed the corrected half-open rule. Testing opportunity varied by 7.5 percentage points across burden strata and 8.3 points across nadir strata; these gradients are below the inherited 10-point gate but are not evidence that observation is ignorable. A further outcome-blind stress test on the same 10,998-person opportunity cohort found any creatinine by hour 72 fell from 87.3% in the narrowest logical-width quartile to 76.0% in the widest (11.3 points), crossing the gate. Observation geometry predicted monitored burden weakly on held hospitals (transparent ridge R² 0.066, Spearman 0.308), which supports neither a physiologic signal nor a complete care-process explanation. It does establish that logical width belongs in the observation gate.

The encounter-level measurement audit showed why monitored and full-clock burden cannot be conflated: among 14,397 broad-support stays, 46.9% lacked support near one or both day boundaries, 52.0% had an internal gap longer than five minutes, and the logical full-clock burden interval had median/p75/p90 widths of 1.50/3.67/5.50 hours. The 70–79% coverage band had median width 5.92 hours versus 0.50 hours at at least 90% coverage. These facts justify a latent-burden gate. `vitalAperiodic` remains a separately bound modality sensitivity; it cannot identify true invasive pressure or line presence during missing invasive bins and is never merged into the primary exposure.

## MAP construction and the two exposure targets

Exposure time is exactly `0 <= observationoffset < 1440`. Define bins (k=0,…,287) as `[5k,5(k+1))`. Parse numeric `systemicmean`; reject values below 20 or above 180 mmHg. First collapse duplicate stay/offset values by median, then take the median within each stay/bin. Require at least 202 nonempty bins. Minute 1,440 is never exposure.

Retain, but do not automatically exclude, flags for systolic below diastolic, MAP outside diastolic−5 to systolic+5, adjacent-bin change over 40 mmHg, off-grid offsets, source multiplicity, first/last observed offset, coverage, boundary missingness, internal missing minutes, maximum gap, gap following low MAP, longest adjacent low run, episode count, and four six-hour burdens. Triplet-flag exclusion is a sensitivity.

M0 produces:

- nadir: minimum valid bin MAP;
- monitored fraction: `n(MAP<65)/n(valid)`;
- **primary monitored-time burden:** `M = 24 * n(MAP<65) / n(valid)`, in 24-hour-equivalent monitored hours;
- observed low hours: `5 * n_low / 60`;
- logical full-clock interval: `[5 * n_low / 60, 5 * (n_low + n_missing) / 60]` hours;
- 24 observed hourly low fractions, hourly nadirs, masks, and time-since-last-observation.

M is not literal elapsed hypotensive time when bins are missing. The lower/upper logical bounds and coverage features accompany every report.

M1 is fit without AKI labels. It is a two-state autoregressive latent-pressure model with a separate observation hazard. Inputs are all 288 MAP values/masks, time since observation, clock hour, prespecified pressor-entry masks, and the same landmark-available static covariates used by B0. The observation hazard depends on prior observed MAP, latent state, pressor entry, clock time, and a training-hospital intercept; held hospitals integrate over the training intercept distribution rather than fit a test-site intercept. Observed low bins remain fixed, so posterior full-clock burdens must remain inside each logical interval. Outputs are posterior full-clock burden and uncertainty plus hourly low-state probabilities and pressure/observation trajectories. They are not proof that an arterial line was physically present.

### Sealed M1 gate

Within training hospitals, identify at least 95%-coverage, boundary-supported stays (first observation no later than minute 30, last no earlier than 1,410, no gap over 15 minutes). Hide contiguous blocks sampled from empirical training-hospital gap lengths and starts, stratified by prior MAP below 65, pressor mask, and clock hour. Fit/tune without AKI labels and evaluate unchanged on held hospitals against two baselines: observed-bin rescaling and last observation carried forward for at most 60 minutes.

M1 passes only if, on held hospitals, it:

1. reduces burden MAE by at least 20% versus **each** baseline;
2. has absolute burden MAE at most 0.5 hour;
3. gives nominal 90% intervals with 85–95% empirical coverage and median width at most 2 hours; and
4. has no prespecified hospital-size stratum with MAE over 1 hour.

All thresholds and strata are frozen before opening target outcomes. Pseudo-missing observed blocks cannot validate truly undocumented intervals. If any gate fails, L1 is not fit or interpreted; report measurement failure as adverse to the latent full-clock claim and continue only with M/B1 and observed T1.

## Creatinine construction and strictly post-landmark outcome

Use numeric rows where `lower(trim(labname))='creatinine'`, system/interface units identify mg/dL case-insensitively, and value is 0.1–20 mg/dL. Collapse same stay/`labresultoffset` values by median. Report excluded or conflicting units. `labresultoffset`, not revision time, defines biology.

Admission creatinine is the earliest valid value in `[-360,360]`. Require at least one valid creatinine in `(-360,1440]`. Exclude a person if any value through and including minute 1,440 is at least 0.3 mg/dL above admission or at least 1.5 times admission. Minute 1,440 is pre-landmark.

Only values in `(1440,min(10080,unitdischargeoffset)]` can define the outcome. Let `t_last` be the offset of the last valid pre-landmark creatinine and `t_adm` the offset of admission creatinine. Subsequent creatinine-defined AKI is the earliest strictly later value satisfying:

- at least 0.3 mg/dL above the last pre-landmark value with `0 < t - t_last <= 2880` minutes; or
- at least 1.5 times admission with `0 < t - t_adm <= 10080` minutes.

Thus the 0.3-mg/dL criterion is an actual 48-hour pair that straddles the landmark, not merely any test during the first 48 hours after it. A general all-pairs 48-hour criterion allowing a post-landmark reference and later post-landmark rise is a sensitivity. The observed-label primary analysis requires at least one strictly later creatinine and reports that selection. Untested people are never coded as non-events. Day-1 `dialysis=1` exclusion is a sensitivity because timing and chronicity are unavailable. Urine-output AKI, RRT initiation, pre-illness baseline, community-acquired AKI, and etiology are not identified.

## Observation process and competing events

Use all 10,998 otherwise eligible index people before requiring a post-landmark test.

- O0 predicts any creatinine in `(1440,4320]` from nadir, age, admission/last pre-landmark creatinine, pre-landmark change/slope/count/timing, non-BP/non-creatinine severity, MAP coverage/boundary/artifact features, and training-hospital effects. Actual future discharge, death, and test counts are never baseline predictors.
- O1 adds M and its flexible interaction with nadir and coverage.
- Separate discrete-time models predict live ICU discharge and death before a qualifying test from landmark-available variables.

Fit out of fold within the frozen hospital scheme. Report calibration, Brier/log loss, O1−O0 gain, propensity overlap, and testing/discharge/death rates by hospital, burden, nadir, coverage, and landmark-to-discharge time.

The observation gate fails if a primary partition has a burden-, nadir-, or logical-width-stratum testing gradient over 10 percentage points, an untruncated inverse-observation weight over 20, or truncated-weight effective sample size below 50% of observed labels. The already observed 11.3-point cohort-wide logical-width gradient means the gate is provisionally failed; the solver must reproduce it within frozen partitions and cannot present an opportunity-cohort transport claim unless the prespecified partition-level gate passes. Failure does not erase the tested-subset estimate, but opportunity-cohort and transport claims become inconclusive. Passing does not prove measured-MAR.

Primary B-model inference is conditional on a later test. Required companions are stabilized out-of-fold inverse-observation weighting truncated at the 1st/99th percentiles; the fixed `(1440,4320]` tested subset; at least two post-landmark tests; exclusion of tests in the first 60 post-landmark minutes; ICU-at-hour-72 and discharge/death-before-test tables; and pattern-mixture tipping analyses assigning unobserved AKI odds from 0.5 to 2 times matched observed odds. Post-landmark test count never enters a clinical predictor.

A parallel discrete-time competing-event analysis models first detected AKI, live discharge, and death through day 7. It is a sensitivity to the primary observed-label risk model, not a claim that unmeasured AKI before discharge is absent.

## Matched outcome models and estimands

B0, B1, B2, and T1 use the same indexed population, raw MAP/mask and infusion sources, frozen hospital split, outcome, observation weights, and train-only preprocessing.

**B0, nadir baseline.** Penalized logistic risk model with a training-set restricted cubic spline of nadir; age, sex, ethnicity, admission source, unit type, weight, admission and last pre-landmark creatinine, admission-to-last change and slope, pre-landmark count/timing, MAP coverage/boundary/internal-gap/artifact fields, missing indicators, and non-BP/non-creatinine `apacheApsVar` physiology.

**B1, primary monitored burden.** B0 plus a restricted cubic spline of M and prespecified M-by-logical-width and M-by-nadir interactions. No automatic feature search.

The primary association estimand is the held-test standardized risk difference and risk ratio when setting M to its training-set 75th versus 25th percentile within 5-mmHg nadir strata and empirical common support, holding measured covariates and monitoring features fixed. This is conditional on strictly later creatinine observation. Also report stratum-specific spline contrasts.

The primary predictive estimand is paired held-test `ΔLL = logloss(B0)-logloss(B1)`; positive favors B1. Report Brier score, AUROC, AUPRC, calibration intercept/slope/curve, descriptive decision-curve net benefit, pooled and equal-hospital weighting, within-hospital fixed-effect sensitivity, and 1,000 hospital-cluster bootstrap refits with all tuning confined to train/validation.

**B2, treatment/severity rival.** B1 plus prespecified day-1 vasopressor agent presence, distinct entry counts, first/last entry, and hourly entry masks. Attenuation is reported but not called mediation because pressors may be response, confounder, mediator, or collider.

**T1, observed temporal alternative.** A penalized functional logistic model with the same B0 static covariates plus 24 observed hourly low fractions, hourly nadirs, observation masks/time-since-observation, hourly pressor-entry masks, longest-run length, episode count, and low-MAP→pressor and pressor→recovery transitions. A six-basis cubic spline functional coefficient with a validation-selected second-difference penalty preserves order without a high-capacity sequence learner. Compare T1 with B1 by paired held-hospital Δlog loss, calibration, and three frozen ablations: permute hour order within person while preserving aggregate burden/nadir; remove observation masks/time-since-observation; remove pressor channels.

B1 loses order, fragmentation, recovery, and treatment co-occurrence. T1 can reveal whether late, persistent, fragmented, or pressor-coupled patterns contain transportable information beyond total monitored burden. It cannot identify renal autoregulation or a vasopressor effect.

**L1, gated latent-burden secondary.** Only after M1 passes, replace M with posterior full-clock burden and propagate uncertainty by at least 50 posterior draws through fitting and standardization. Estimate the same held-test p75-versus-p25 nadir-stratified risk difference/ratio, compare L1 with B0 and B1, and report between-draw plus sampling uncertainty. A positive L1 contrast supports only a model-conditional latent association. No L1 result overrides an adverse monitored-time result.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

### Primary monitored-burden claim

Support requires all of:

1. B1's standardized p75-versus-p25 risk difference is positive with a 95% hospital-bootstrap interval excluding zero.
2. Held-test ΔLL versus B0 is positive with its paired 95% interval excluding zero and no material calibration deterioration.
3. Direction persists in fixed-window, inverse-observation-weighted, pattern-mixture odds 0.75–1.5, within-hospital, equal-hospital, at least 90%-coverage/boundary-supported, triplet-exclusion, and high-leverage-hospital-removal analyses.
4. Direction does not reverse in the prespecified observation-weighted and pattern-mixture analyses. An observation-gate failure restricts support to the later-tested population; it does not by itself negate that smallest conditional estimand.

M1 success is not required for this monitored-time claim. A stronger claim transporting the contrast to all otherwise eligible hour-24 survivors additionally requires the observation gate to pass, adequate overlap, and stable weighted/pattern-mixture estimates.

Adverse evidence is an upper 95% bound at or below +1 absolute risk percentage point together with no held-test log-loss gain, or a stable reverse association across observation and hospital checks. This argues against clinically meaningful incremental monitored-burden information in this design, not that hypotension is harmless or nadir causal.

Inconclusive evidence for the tested-patient estimand includes an interval spanning both zero and +1 point; inadequate common support; sign changes under fixed-window, logical-width, or selection analyses; calibration failure; or unstable hospital effects. Observation-gate failure makes only the opportunity-cohort extension inconclusive unless the conditional estimate also becomes unstable. Do not relax the landmark, coverage rule, or outcome after test opening.

### Latent and temporal claims

Latent full-clock support requires M1 to pass every measurement gate, L1's risk-difference interval to exclude zero in the hypothesized direction, positive held-test ΔLL versus B0, and stable posterior-draw uncertainty. If L1 does not improve on B1, report that reconstruction added no predictive information over monitored burden. M1 failure is adverse to reconstruction, while a passed M1 with an imprecise L1 effect is biologically inconclusive.

T1 is separately supported only if held-test log loss improves over B1 with a paired 95% interval excluding zero, calibration does not worsen, validation/test direction agrees, and nonconstant temporal structure survives order, observation, and pressor ablations. T1 failure does not negate aggregate B1 support.

A supportive primary result establishes a hospital-transportable conditional prognostic association among later-tested index encounters. It does not establish causal hypoperfusion, a treatment effect, an optimal MAP threshold, or a vasopressor recommendation. A supportive L1 result remains conditional on an unverified model of truly missing intervals. True line insertion/removal and clinical intent, delivered pressor dose, fluids, cardiac output, nephrotoxins, pre-ICU renal baseline, urine-output/RRT AKI, and injury etiology require device metadata, medication/nursing adjudication, longitudinal data, clinician review, external validation, or another prospective study.

## Alternatives, resources, and revisit record

The simple transparent comparison is B0 versus B1. T1 is the matched substantive alternative on the same population, target, split, raw channels, and uncertainty framework; it preserves timing and treatment-response information B1 discards. M0 versus M1 separately tests whether full-clock pressure can be reconstructed rather than assuming missing bins are random.

A primary restriction to at least 90% coverage/boundary support is deferred because it discards the informative sparse tail and changes the target population; it remains a required sensitivity. Pure logical bounds are assumption-light and always reported, but inherited widths are too broad to provide a point estimate. A 288-bin temporal convolutional network could learn within-hour morphology but is deferred because the indexed audit found 1,456 events, below the inherited 1,500-event revisit trigger, and T1 has not yet shown residual temporal value. Revisit it only if an expanded prespecified cohort has at least 1,500 events, T1 is supported, and residual within-hour structure remains; then use five seeds and at most one A100, 4 CPUs, 32 GiB, two hours. Causal g-methods are deferred because delivered doses, fluids, nephrotoxins, urine output, and key time-varying treatment intent are not adequately identified.

Inherited measured diagnostics used 8 CPUs and 32–48 GiB for 40.6–75.97 seconds; the additional outcome-blind observation-rival stress test used 8 CPUs/48 GiB and completed in 44.24 seconds. The full future experiment is planned, not measured, at 16 CPUs, 64 GiB, no GPU, at most six hours, within the 16-CPU/262,144-MiB/28,800-second solver envelope. CPU is appropriate for roughly five million bins, a two-state model, penalized splines, and parallel cluster bootstraps.
