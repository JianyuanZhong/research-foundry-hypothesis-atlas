# Does hypotension DURATION add AKI risk beyond DEPTH? A depth-conditioned, dual-ascertainment test in eICU 2.0

Lead working proposal — eICU 2.0 island, **episode 30**. Operation: **evolve** of the true parent
`[prior hypothesis]` (valid, delivery-complete, mu 32.10; episode-29 restart rooted in
expert seed `[starting question]`, "Duration of hypotension"). Status: **readiness, not a solved
result** — no solver/reference estimator was executed at discovery. This is design work.

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance

ICU teams titrate fluids and vasopressors to a mean arterial pressure (MAP) target. Prior work shows a
time-weighted MAP<65 burden predicts creatinine-based KDIGO AKI (base AUC 0.725 [K1]) and that graded
duration/area below MAP 65 tracks a downstream organ-adjacent outcome [K2]. But those scalar burdens
**conflate depth and duration**, so the decision-facing question is unresolved: once you know the
**deepest MAP** a patient reached, does the **cumulative time/area** below threshold add risk? If yes,
"time below target" is a monitoring target beyond a single worst value; if no, depth alone suffices and
duration-based alarms add no information. The leading claim (H1: duration adds beyond depth via
cumulative renal hypoperfusion) has a serious rival (R1: duration is only a marker of overall unstable
/severity time and is fully explained by depth once severity is fixed) plus an ascertainment rival
(R2: signal is generated by creatinine-sampling intensity, a detection/practice artifact).

## 2. What this episode changes vs the parent (substantive, frozen science)

The parent compared a depth-only hospital-fixed-effects logistic baseline against an additive
duration/area alternative. That additive contrast **cannot tell "duration adds beyond depth" from
"duration and depth are collinear"**. The single substantive change is to make the duration-vs-depth
question directly testable, without changing the population, windows, outcome definition, landmark or
binding keys:

1. **Depth-conditioned test (primary).** Estimate the duration effect **within strata of the minimum
   MAP reached** (min-MAP quintiles) and fit a duration × min-MAP **interaction** on the log-odds
   scale. Two pre-specified sub-contrasts: (a) duration slope inside the deepest and mid min-MAP
   strata; (b) duration effect within the *shallow* min-MAP (mild) stratum.
2. **Dual-ascertainment AKI (secondary).** Primary outcome = KDIGO creatinine AKI (as in the parent);
   secondary = KDIGO **urine-output** criterion from `intakeOutput`. Creatinine is order-driven
   (detection-sensitive); urine output is nursing-recorded at higher cadence. Concordance of the
   duration signal across the two channels is a channel-disjoint way to separate a mechanistic
   cumulative-hypoperfusion signal from a creatinine-detection/order artifact.

Frozen from the parent (unchanged): adult eICU 2.0 stays, age>=16, `unitdischargeoffset>=1440` min
(alive at the 24 h landmark), a baseline and a follow-up creatinine; exposure window (0,24] h; landmark
24 h; outcome window (24,72] h; severity adjustment with `apachescore`/`predictedicumortality`;
hospital fixed effects; leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO) transport; creatinine-sampling-intensity
detection control; negative-control outcome.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcome

- Population: `patient` adult stays (age>=16, not ">89" handled as missing/upper-bound), `hospitalid`

- Exposure (0,24] h from `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean` at `observationoffset` (minutes): min MAP

- Outcome (24,72] h: KDIGO serum-creatinine AKI stage >=1 from `lab` (`labname`=="creatinine",
  `labresultoffset`, `labresult`) using baseline (pre-24 h) vs follow-up values; secondary KDIGO
  urine-output criterion from `intakeOutput` (urine `celllabel` cells; `intakeoutputoffset`,
  `cellvaluenumeric`) — cellpath filtering is a bounded implementation step.
- Covariates: `apachescore`, `predictedicumortality` (`apachePatientResult`), age, sex; creatinine
  sampling intensity in (0,24] h as detection control; vasopressor/ventilation strings from
  `infusionDrug`/`treatment` for the negative-control and severity context.

## 4. Baseline vs substantive alternative (method comparison)

- Baseline B1: hospital-fixed-effects logistic, depth only (min MAP) + severity.
- Baseline B2 (parent's alternative): B1 + linear duration/area terms (additive).
- Substantive alternative A1 (this episode): **depth-conditioned model** = min-MAP-stratified duration
  effects + duration×min-MAP interaction, fit on the same hospital-held-out split; the estimand is the
  within-stratum adjusted OR per 30 min MAP<65.
- Bounded learned alternative A2: gradient-boosted trees on MAP-trajectory features (duration, area,
  episode count, min/median MAP, worst-30-min MAP, time above 65), evaluated on the same
  leave-one-hospital-out split. A deep sequence/neural trajectory model is **deferred** with reason: K3
  notes sparse, irregular EHR sampling can make rich dynamic models underdetermined, and B1/A1 already
  target the specific depth-vs-duration identification question; A2 tests whether non-linear trajectory
  shape adds beyond linear terms.
- Evaluation: within-stratum ORs + interaction test (primary); dAUROC, calibration, O:E ratio, Brier,
  net benefit; LOHO transport; agreement between creatinine- and urine-output AKI ascertainment.
- Rejected branch (recorded): using `carePlanEOL` timed transitions as the primary mechanism is
  deferred — it does not separate depth from duration and is under-coded.

## 5. Falsification criteria (pre-specified)

- **Supportive (H1):** within deep and mid min-MAP strata the duration OR per 30 min is >1 with 95% CI
  excluding 1, and the interaction does not attenuate it to the null; the signal is concordant across
  creatinine- and urine-output ascertainment; LOHO transport retains direction.
- **Adverse (R1 / refutes H1):** duration effects are null within every min-MAP stratum (depth explains
  duration), OR the duration slope is significant **only** in the shallow min-MAP stratum (consistent
  with a global severity gradient, not depth-specific burden).
- **Adverse (R2):** the duration signal is present for creatinine AKI but absent for urine-output AKI,
  and tracks the creatinine-sampling-intensity control — a detection artifact.
- **Inconclusive:** CI includes 1 in all strata / underpowered (report MDE); or the two ascertainment
  channels disagree without a sampling intensity pattern.
