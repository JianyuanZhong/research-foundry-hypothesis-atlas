Its remaining weakness is the primary endpoint. DRR28 requires respiratory liberation and favorable hospital disposition jointly, but these answer different questions: extubation is proximal to the proposed pulmonary effect of deresuscitation, whereas day-28 discharge timing and destination also reflect rehabilitation needs, social support, bed supply, and institutional practice. DRR28 is retained only as a secondary joint decomposition alongside its respiratory and disposition components.

The falsifiable hypothesis is: among adults in the frozen first-opportunity overlap population, assignment to initiate a qualifying ICU-input-system recorded furosemide/bumetanide delivery before the 24-hour decision closure, versus avoid that same recorded event until closure, increases SRL7 risk by more than 5 percentage points, while ruling out more than 3 points higher recorded death/hospice/acute-hospital/LTAC discharge through day 28, more than 3 points lower favorable day-28 disposition, and more than 5 points higher observed RRT through day 7.

The advance is a bedside-relevant test of timely respiratory liberation whose confirmation window follows extubation literature and does not punish ward transfer, while keeping disposition and renal/terminal outcomes as explicit guards. It is not a claim that loop delivery causes extubation or that a recorded extubation is true durable functional recovery.

Supportive requires the one-sided 95% lower limit of the conservative SRL7 effect bound to exceed +0.05; the one-sided upper limit for recorded death/hospice/acute/LTAC RD to be below +0.03; the one-sided lower ANHATD28 RD limit to exceed -0.03; the one-sided upper observed-RRT7 RD limit to be below +0.05; and every partition, source, time, state, ambiguity, overlap, calibration, balance, weight, negative-control, bootstrap, and bound diagnostic to pass. This supports trial prioritization only.

Adverse if the one-sided upper limit of the conservative SRL7 effect bound is at most 0; the lower harmful-terminal/disposition RD limit is at least +0.03; the upper ANHATD28 RD limit is at most -0.03; or the lower observed-RRT7 RD limit is at least +0.05 under both chartdate brackets. These are adverse record-defined outcomes, not proof of toxicity.

Margin-falsified if the one-sided upper SRL7 limit is at most +0.05. This rejects the prespecified clinically meaningful recorded-liberation margin, not every physiologic benefit.

Inconclusive/infeasible covers all other results, including precise effects between 0 and +0.05; wide SRL7 bounds; eligible n<200; qualifying A<100; B completions/compatible closures<100; arm ESS<75; over 10% of person-hours with treatment/censor probability outside [.01,.99]; over 1% candidate-loop ambiguity or key/time/state contradiction; over 5% B eMAR loop contamination; over 1% late exposure confirmation; over 25% E-first decision closures; over 10% SRL7 unknown in either arm or more than 5 percentage points differential unknownness; over 10% planned extubations unmatched within six hours; material sensitivity/era/service/destination reversal; failed negative controls; or unreconciled endpoint/bound arithmetic. Do not relax matching, count unplanned events as planned, condition on complete outcomes, broaden exposure units/statuses, use eMAR-positive eligibility, or add later population opportunities to rescue feasibility.

Supportive means the available record is compatible with a clinically important association worth prospective study. Adverse means the record-defined strategy has a prespecified harmful pattern. Margin-falsified means a >5-point SRL7 advantage is not supported. Inconclusive means the design or data did not resolve the question; it is not evidence of equivalence.
