# Does post-classification outcome-window selection distort the adverse fitness result?

## Unresolved question and substantive advance

The assessed parent `[prior hypothesis]` found that adding the literal-prefix broader `carePlanGeneral` vocabulary did not improve prediction of a later structured respiratory-setting reduction, with only a very small and hospital-concentrated coverage gain. That result was estimated after restricting to rows that remained in the ICU through the whole post-classification window and had a computable paired respiratory outcome. The restriction could be informative: ICU death, live ICU discharge, and loss or sparsity of respiratory-chart documentation may differ by receipt and hospital. The second assessed parent, `[prior hypothesis]`, established the target-independent, classification-complete interface footprint but did not resolve this outcome-ascertainment problem.

This child tests the falsifiable selection hypothesis that the adverse semantic-undercoverage/fitness result is materially distorted by that complete-case restriction. It retains every classification-complete row before assigning later observation/competing states, quantifies attrition by receipt, documentation process, hospital, and landmark, and applies fixed-observed-hospital held-out observation weighting plus an all-row five-state sensitivity. The substantive advance is a direct selection-bias stress test rather than another vocabulary expansion. It can rescue, reinforce, leave unchanged, or fail to decide the prior result. It does not interpret an exported label as bedside ventilation, a spontaneous breathing trial, extubation, quality, benefit, harm, or causality.

The strongest supported claim before this experiment is limited to exported structured documentation: exact and broader care-plan receipt is sparse and hospital-concentrated, and the broader encoding had not improved the inherited paired-outcome predictive task. The unresolved claim tested here is whether informative ICU exit or respiratory-documentation loss explains that adverse comparison. Neither the prior evidence nor this experiment establishes clinical semantics or clinical effect.

## Mutually exclusive observation states

For each retained row, define `endpoint=L+1080` and use this precedence:

1. `icu_death`: `unitdischargeoffset <= endpoint` and exact `unitdischargestatus=Expired`.
2. `live_icu_discharge`: `unitdischargeoffset <= endpoint` and exact `unitdischargestatus=Alive`.
3. `complete_computable_outcome`: `unitdischargeoffset > endpoint` and inherited `robust_reduction` is nonmissing.
4. `still_adequate_chart_no_paired_outcome`: `unitdischargeoffset > endpoint`, `robust_reduction` missing, and at least two distinct `respchartoffset` values in `(L+360,L+1080]`.
5. `still_sparse_no_respiratory_documentation`: `unitdischargeoffset > endpoint`, `robust_reduction` missing, and fewer than two distinct later chart offsets.

The fourth state means repeated structured respiratory documentation only. It is not an inference of ventilation, airway status, a bedside action, or data quality. A positive control confirmed that the complete state equals `complete_outcome_window & robust_reduction.notna()` exactly. All early discharge statuses were either `Alive` or `Expired`.

## Analysis and falsification rules

Attrition is tabulated by exact receipt, broader receipt, generic/process indicators, hospital, landmark, airway definition, and state. Complete-case selection is compared by risk differences across these exposures and by hospital dispersion.

For rows still in the ICU through `L+1080`, the binary observation analysis defines observation as `complete_computable_outcome`. Fixed hospitals qualify for held-out modeling only when they have at least 20 computable outcomes, both binary outcome classes, and at least three noncomputable rows. The cross-hospital observation model uses inherited pre-landmark variables, landmark, process controls, exact `label3`, and four broader indicators. Propensities are clipped to `[0.05,0.95]`; `1/p` is capped at 10 and normalized within held-out hospital. The estimand is predictive loss among still-through-window rows under a missing-at-random sensitivity, not a causal treatment effect. ICU exits are not assigned an invented paired respiratory outcome.

The complementary sensitivity fits hospital-held-out multinomial models to all classification-complete rows for the five exported observation/competing states, comparing process, exact, and broader feature sets using five-state cross-entropy. It does not rank death or discharge as harm or success.

Uncertainty is clustered by `sid` within each fixed hospital, so repeated landmarks for a stay move together. Cross-fitted predictions and weights are held fixed during resampling. The executed bounded run used 20 stay-cluster bootstrap replicates rather than the originally intended 2,000 because of the execution deadline; bootstrap quantiles therefore provide only limited sensitivity evidence and are not definitive confidence intervals. Hospitals are the fixed observed set and no inference transports to unseen hospitals.

A selection rescue would require adequate overlap/ESS, IPW broad improvement over both process and exact in micro and equal-hospital-macro loss with all relevant bootstrap upper bounds below zero, concordant five-state improvement, and support in the unbounded plus at least two bounded definitions. Adverse reinforcement would require no rescue and broad-worse macro loss with probability at least 0.75 in at least two bounded definitions under both IPW and the five-state analysis. Unchanged would require neither rescue nor reinforcement, no favorable sign reversal after weighting, weighted-versus-unweighted macro shifts no greater than 0.005 for every definition, and no multinomial support. Otherwise the result is inconclusive.

## Executed results

The five-state counts and complete-case rates were:

| definition | ICU death | live ICU discharge | adequate chart/no paired outcome | complete computable outcome | sparse/no respiratory documentation | complete-case rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| exact_unbounded | 34 | 59 | 24 | 3,296 | 258 | 0.8978 |
| exact_airway_24h | 27 | 42 | 16 | 2,273 | 217 | 0.8827 |
| exact_airway_12h | 21 | 22 | 12 | 1,555 | 138 | 0.8896 |
| exact_airway_6h | 16 | 17 | 10 | 1,018 | 98 | 0.8783 |

The exact receipt reconstruction, raw post-window respiratory row-count reconstruction, inherited hospital/discharge-offset join, and complete-case reconstruction each had zero mismatches. Every definition had sufficient fixed-hospital overlap for both sensitivity analyses: 20, 16, 13, and 8 hospitals respectively for the binary observation/IPW analysis. IPW effective-sample-size fractions were 0.907, 0.847, 0.861, and 0.846.

The IPW broad-minus-process macro log-loss differences were positive (worse for broader) in all four definitions: 0.00090, 0.00529, 0.00642, and 0.01485. The 20-replicate bootstrap probabilities that this difference was positive were 0.50, 0.70, 1.00, and 1.00; the corresponding 95% bootstrap intervals were [−0.00276, 0.00350], [−0.00628, 0.01442], [0.00147, 0.01051], and [0.00684, 0.02411]. Thus weighting did not reverse the adverse point direction and made it more adverse in bounded variants, but the limited bootstrap run is not enough to claim formal reinforcement.

The all-row five-state broad-minus-process macro differences were −0.00195, −0.01046, −0.01245, and −0.01647, favoring the broader feature set for state prediction, while broad-minus-exact macro differences were positive in all four variants (0.00091, 0.00321, 0.00426, and 0.00088). The five-state and binary estimands therefore answer different questions: adding broader indicators can help predict observation/competing-state labels without improving the original computable respiratory outcome and without validating clinical meaning. The five-state bootstrap intervals for broad-minus-process macro loss were [−0.00659, 0.00592], [−0.01878, 0.00110], [−0.02458, −0.00267], and [−0.03390, −0.00322] under the 20-replicate bounded run. Because the two sensitivity estimands are discordant, the prespecified overall gate is `inconclusive_selection_sensitivity`; neither rescue nor the joint adverse-reinforcement gate passed.

## Interpretation and limits

Supportive evidence for a selection distortion is absent from the primary outcome estimand: IPW does not produce a favorable sign reversal, and the broader representation remains worse than process in all bounded variants. The multinomial result shows that state observation is predictable from the feature sets, but that is not evidence that missingness caused the prior adverse result. The limited run is therefore best interpreted as failing to rescue the prior concern while remaining inconclusive about definitive adverse reinforcement.

This experiment can computationally verify source scans, hashes, exact joins, time-window logic, state exclusivity, attrition, held-out prediction, weighting arithmetic, stay clustering, and the prespecified gate. It cannot establish that a care-plan value denotes invasive ventilation, daily extubation evaluation, an SBT, extubation readiness, extubation, successful live discharge, quality, benefit, harm, or causality. It also cannot distinguish unavailable local interfaces from time-window mismatch, conflicting entries, upstream mapping, or undocumented bedside care. Stronger claims require local interface/data-dictionary and workflow metadata, time-resolved device and airway records, direct SBT/extubation evidence, blinded clinical adjudication, and prospective multisite validation.
