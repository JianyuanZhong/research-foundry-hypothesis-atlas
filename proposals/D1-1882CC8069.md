## Nested phase-2 probability design, adaptive stratification, and inclusion probabilities

The phase-2 sample is adaptive only to sealed phase-1 information. After every
phase-1 unit label, packet-validity result, control result, unit inventory, and
band are complete and cryptographically sealed—but before any phase-2 packet is
exposed or any phase-2 seed is generated—form the frozen strata
`d=(h,z,c)`, where `h` is the inherited outcome-blind phase-1 sampling stratum,
`z` is the sealed admission band (`Z1`, `Z2`, or `Z3`), and `c` is the exact
pre-label support cell `(g, inherited route)`. `c` is a refinement for
precision, not an outcome discovered in phase 2: it is computed from the
inherited frame/route/atom construction and is fixed before phase-1 labels.
There is no post-phase-2 refinement. The phase-1 screen changes only `z`.

Let `M_d` be the realized number of phase-1 admissions in occupied `d`.
Empty strata have no allocation. Singleton strata are certainty strata:
`M_d=n_d=1`, `q_i=1`, and no within-stratum pair quantity is defined. For
`2<=M_d<=4`, set `n_d=M_d` (conditional census), avoiding an unauditable
one- or few-unit stratum. For `M_d>4`, every supported noncensus stratum must
have `n_d>=2`; a proposed supported `n_d=1` is infeasible, not silently
pooled. No stratum is merged, borrowed from, dropped, or rescued by a pooled
estimate. Unsupported residual strata remain descriptive and cannot support an
atom-route conclusion.

Within each occupied `d`, draw `n_d` admissions by SRS without replacement.
For distinct admissions, conditional on the realized sealed phase-1 sample,

* `q_i=n_d/M_d`;
* `q_ij=n_d(n_d-1)/(M_d(M_d-1))` for two members of the same `d` when
  `M_d>1`; and
* `q_ij=q_i q_j` for members of different `d` strata.

When `n_d=1` in an ordinary noncensus stratum, the same-stratum pair
probability is exactly zero and the implementation must not divide by a
nonexistent pair. When `M_d=1`, there is no pair at all. A census has
`q_i=q_ij=1` for all defined pairs. The compiler stores all realized
`M_d,n_d,q_i,q_ij`, ordered IDs, frozen stratum definitions, seed, and seal
proof before phase-2 access.

The admission estimator remains the sequential double-expansion estimator
`T_hat_y = sum_(i in s2) y_i/(pi1_i q_i)`. It is unbiased by iterated
expectation for the finite-population total when phase-1 strata are treated as
the realized adaptive design state and every admission retains positive
conditional phase-2 probability. The realized product `pi1_i q_i` is a path
weight, not an unconditional inclusion probability. In particular, do not
report `pi1_ij*q_ij` as an unconditional pair probability: the phase-1 band
composition is random before phase 2. For unconditional pair quantities define

`Pi_i=E[I(i in s1)I(i in s2)]` and
`Pi_ij=E[I(i,j in s1)I(i,j in s2)]`,

where the expectation integrates over all possible phase-1 samples and their
sealed band compositions. Equivalently,
`Pi_i=pi1_i E[q_i(S1)|i in s1]` and
`Pi_ij=pi1_ij E[q_ij(S1)|i,j in s1]`; the expectation cannot be replaced by
the q from one realized sample. The estimator and variance engine must keep
this distinction explicit.

1. set every occupied `M_d<=4` to census and require at least one admission in
every other occupied stratum;
2. require `n_d>=2` for every supported noncensus `d`;
3. enforce 120 <= `sum_d n_d` <= 300 and all worst-case workload, roster,

### Unconditional pair variance and simultaneous precision contract

Use the law of total variance, not a one-stage survey variance and not
verified-only analysis. The conditional phase-2 variance is computed from the
realized SRS-without-replacement design using the stored `q_i,q_ij` (or an
exact Sen–Yates–Grundy equivalent), including conditional census strata. The
outer phase-1 component is propagated with the inherited first-stage pair
probabilities and a conservative finite-population bound. For a bounded binary
endpoint in inherited cell `h`, use
`S2_max=N_h/[4(N_h-1)]` when `N_h>1` and zero when `N_h=1`; physician minutes
must have a rehearsal-registered finite range or execution stops. Add the
conditional component without treating phase-1 labels as phase-2 truth.

Primary intervals are finite-population design/concentration intervals with one
frozen max-statistic 95% family over inherited gates, supported atom-route
endpoints, phase-1/phase-2 discrepancies, calibration, workload, and shared-
error outputs. The required >=20,000 nested Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap is a
coverage diagnostic and reproducibility check only; it cannot narrow or replace
the conservative outer bound. Every reported cell must include HT total,
finite-population denominator, Hájek ratio, conditional variance, outer
variance, half-width, and effective support. Zero observed adverse events retain
a positive one-sided design-based upper bound. Undefined pair probability,
negative variance, failed exact small-population enumeration, sparse effective
support, or width above the frozen margin maps the cell to `DEFER`.

The pre-seed design gate requires positive probability in every occupied band,
tiny-stratum rules, a certified allocation, all workload/roster/calendar
bounds, and the simultaneous width guarantee. The post-label analysis gate
requires all selected admissions retained, all units coded, exact pair and
variance audits passed, and every supported cell within its margin. This is an
adaptive-stratification precision improvement only: it cannot change the

## Measurement-integrity and sampling controls

The parent's six-family, 48-pair-per-task ontology calibration remains unchanged for each clinical roster. Machine schema, expected transition, Cartesian mapping, all-unit precedence, pair generation, selection, and weight fixtures require zero implementation errors. In each task and phase, simultaneous lower decisive-transition accuracy must exceed simultaneous upper semantic-invariance flip rate, and route-realization flip must not exceed matched nuisance/semantic flip. Missing control responses are errors. These controls show ontology responsiveness, not clinical truth.

Additional locked two-phase controls are:

1. **Selection concealment:** phase-2 seed cannot exist and no phase-2 roster can access packets until all phase-1 labels and bands are sealed.
2. **Positive-probability audit:** executable fixtures cover empty, singleton, census, and ordinary `d`; every phase-1 ID must have `q_i>0`; exact `q_i,q_ij` sums and random-draw frequencies must match theory.
3. **Verification-bias challenge:** a synthetic finite population has phase-1 false-negatives concentrated in `Z3`; the weighted two-phase estimator must recover the known total while verified-only and phase-1 substitution fail.
4. **All-unit sentinel:** one adverse minority unit among otherwise portable units must place the admission in `Z1` at phase 1 when observed and must make the phase-2 admission adverse; no unit-level weighting is permitted.
5. **Nonresponse/high-weight challenge:** selected missing phase-2 readings become `K_U` at the original high weight; replacement or complete-case output must fail verification.
6. **Atom masking:** pooled-safe/atom-unsafe fixtures must block only the exact failing atom and cannot be rescued by pooling, adjacent service, era, workflow, shrinkage, or phase-1 majority.
7. **Workload bound:** a draw containing every `Umax_d` must remain under the registered ceiling; overload, roster collision, weekly excess, late completion, or use of mean rehearsal time stops the run.
8. **Estimator audit:** enumerate small finite populations over both sampling stages and verify unbiased totals, stagewise variance, interval coverage, census behavior, zero-event upper bounds, and monotone shared-error curves.
9. **Claim checker:** reject any conclusion containing operational `AUTO/RECON`, clinical truth, appropriateness, responsibility, safety, benefit, transport, or causality from MIMIC-only output.

2. every master-sample admission has phase-1 all-unit measurement or an adverse missing/invalid state, and every occupied `d` has audited positive `q_i`;
3. at least 120 phase-2 admissions are selected under the frozen allocation, every selected admission is retained, and the four-reader phase-2 all-unit endpoint is complete or adversely coded;
4. ontology, leakage, roster, selection, probability, estimator, phase-1/phase-2 verification-bias, and all-unit controls pass their frozen rules;
5. the sequentially weighted simultaneous atom-route bound passes the unchanged inherited adverse margin, no forbidden phase-2 adverse state is observed in the candidate route cell, and the simultaneous lower shared-error breakdown count is strictly greater than zero;
6. actual and worst-case planned retrospective workload remain within `B_H`, weekly, reviewer-total, and elapsed-time ceilings; and the simultaneous upper prospective measurement workload for the atom fits the bridge's separately registered capacity.

The only allowed conclusion is: “Within this frozen MIMIC stored-text construct and exact support atom, a nested probability validation was internally measurable and sufficiently feasible to warrant a prospective silent study of actual workflow and measurement failure.” The bridge may calculate concealed candidate routes solely for evaluation, but every output visible to operations or care is suppressed/`DEFER`.

### Adverse

A supported atom is adverse if phase 2 finds candidate-`AUTO` compatibility with choice, completion/stop/replacement, unknown, or packet failure; candidate-`RECON` compatibility with no-action or prohibited uncertainty; a phase-1 false-safe pattern; route anchoring; decisive controls indistinguishable from nuisance; atom-specific adverse bounds beyond inherited margins; zero breakdown; or workload beyond capacity. This excludes the atom from bridge priority even if phase 1, a majority, pooled analysis, or all four phase-2 readers appear favorable. A negative result is clinically useful because it prevents premature automation/reconciliation work and identifies where stored-text measurement fails.

### Inconclusive and fail closed

`NO_SELECTION` or atom-level `DEFER` follows if the inherited atlas is infeasible within 900 admissions; fewer than 120 phase-2 admissions fit the frozen workload; any occupied band lacks positive verification probability; labels or probabilities are missing; phase-2 selection is exposed early; phase-2 completion fails; ESS/event support is sparse; simultaneous intervals are too wide; variance cannot be validated; controls fail; shared-error curves are nonmonotone; or exact workload/roster ceilings fail. Inconclusive does not imply a safe `RECON` queue. No pooled estimate, favorable phase-1 screen, imputation, consensus, model, utility, or neighboring atom can rescue it.

MIMIC cannot observe the physical-departure artifact, draft/edit/sign/transmit sequence, report delivery/view/acknowledgement, accountable clinician, patient understanding/preferences, outside-system plan, target-linked replacement rationale, orders/referrals/scheduling, completed or duplicate imaging, radiation/contrast burden, downstream cascades, malignancy resolution, anxiety, displacement workload, safety, utility, or benefit. `storetime`, service, transfer, and ICU fields remain proxies.

Only after that evidence—and then a separate governed comparative study with safety monitoring, intervention receipt, unnecessary imaging, workload, patient-reported effects, diagnostic outcomes, and causal estimands—could operational routing or clinical benefit be tested.

The substantive advance is a feasible but non-weakened validation architecture with a certified adaptive-precision contract: complete phase-1 all-unit adverse surveillance prevents favorable-case selection; positive-probability phase 2 supplies four-reader construct measurement with explicit verification-bias correction; finite-population support-atom estimands and shared-error frontiers remain intact; and the attainable MIMIC conclusion is aligned with the missing evidence.
