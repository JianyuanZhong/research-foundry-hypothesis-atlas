# A leakage-resistant probability-verified admission-routing gate: separated component seals and a held-out route-purity audit

## Clinical decision bottleneck and targeted advance

The unresolved retrospective prerequisite is whether pulmonary-nodule discharge opportunities can be assigned reproducibly to conservative admission-level routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` without apparent performance being created by label circularity. In the parent, atomic rather than direct route voting was an important safeguard, but some reviewers could still encounter overlapping evidence: eligibility and unit definition came from the same radiology material later supplied to discharge-plan reviewers; context reviewers saw a redacted version of the same discharge artifact; and an AUTO verifier was selected because an earlier reviewer had already called AUTO. Even if nobody intentionally voted a route, knowledge transferred across components, imperfect redaction, reviewer reuse, or outcome-triggered verification could make the derived route look more reproducible and pure than it is.

This child preserves the parent's frozen first-eligible all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum chronology, unique canonical discharge summary, unit-specific storage clocks, exhaustive departure states, deterministic safety-sentinel census, positive-probability admission sampling, all-unit review within every sampled admission, finite-population inference, worst-unit admission routing, and strict noncausal scope. It makes one targeted repair:

1. eligibility, radiology-unit, chronology, departure-context, and discharge-plan components are reviewed by roster-disjoint panels using genuinely separated masked evidence views;
2. every initial component label is written to an append-only, independently sealed file before any cross-component join or route derivation;
3. locked code, never a reviewer or consensus conference, takes the Cartesian product of compatible sealed component labels and derives the robust route;
4. a prespecified probability subset of the master sample is hidden from all primary analysts and receives an entirely separate duplicate component review system; it is opened only after the protocol, margins, route code, primary component files, and result shells are sealed;
5. route purity and the incremental workload of leakage-resistant adjudication are gated on this untouched audit, not on an outcome-triggered AUTO re-review.

The clinical importance is narrow but consequential. An unsafe apparently copyable admission could suppress needed reconciliation, hide a timing limitation, or convert uncertainty into false confidence. Conversely, indiscriminate deferral makes a routing gate unusable. This experiment tests whether those routes are a stable property of strictly departure-frozen source evidence under a leakage-resistant measurement process. Passing can nominate only a prospective silent validation of the frozen gate. It cannot justify insertion into a discharge document or any change in care.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

**Strongest claim currently supported.** The frozen MIMIC sources contain the report, reciprocal-link, discharge-artifact, admission, and storage-time fields required for a departure-frozen experiment. The parent recorded a complete local profile of 2,321,355 radiology rows (`RR=2,295,635`, `AR=25,720`), 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 discharge rows all typed `DS`, 331,794 unique `(subject_id,hadm_id)` canonical keys, no duplicate canonical keys, 17 discharge rows with missing `storetime`, and 25,735 rows each carrying `parent_note_id` and `addendum_note_id` detail fields. The present inspection confirmed the bound source headers. These are source-feasibility facts, not evidence of semantic eligibility, route prevalence, purity, appropriateness, or benefit.

**Unresolved claim tested.** After eliminating cross-component reviewer overlap and outcome-triggered verification, locked worst-case code will still derive reproducible routes; its `AUTO` route will remain sufficiently free of independently detected unsafe minority components; `RECON` and `DEFER` will capture unsafe or uncertain components; and the design-weighted estimates will have enough precision and feasible workload over the complete frozen population.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** In the frozen first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity population, a prespecified stratified probability verification design with a safety-sentinel census, independently sealed masked component labels, and an untouched probability-sampled audit will show all of the following simultaneously:

* lower simultaneous bounds for route confirmation and raw agreement meet governance-fixed reliability margins;
* the held-out one-sided upper bound on unsafe contamination among index-system `AUTO` admissions is below the fixed `tau_AUTO` margin;
* lower held-out bounds for capture of unsafe-content and decision-relevant uncertainty meet their route-specific margins;
* no adverse assignment of disagreement, redaction uncertainty, unit-boundary uncertainty, missing evidence, or unknown clocks leaves an unsafe admission compatible with `AUTO`;
* route burdens, event counts, effective sample sizes, finite-population precision, and adjudication workload satisfy their prespecified gates.

Failure of a route-specific gate gives `NO_SELECTION` for that route. Passing RECON cannot rescue failed AUTO. Full support only nominates prospective silent validation.

## Stage 0: exhaustive denominator construction before route evidence

Before inspecting discharge text, discharge `storetime`, any route label, or any later trace, stream all radiology and radiology-detail rows. Freeze and hash source hashes, extraction code, lexical/exam candidate dictionaries, every included and excluded `note_id`, reciprocal-chain output, eligibility manual, and retrieval-negative audit seed.

The deliberately broad candidate frame contains every exact admission-linked radiology report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]` that has any pulmonary/nodule/opacity term, follow-up/recommendation term, 3/6/12-month or equivalent calendar expression, chest/thoracic CT expression, or locked high-recall exam-code/name combination. Retrieval may increase workload but cannot use discharge text or be tuned against routes. Every deterministic positive, parser failure, and chain ambiguity receives independent dual eligibility review by an **eligibility roster that serves on no later panel**. Eligibility packets contain the operative radiology chain, admission interval, age criterion, and only the disposition evidence needed for exclusions; they contain no discharge text, discharge `storetime`, sample stratum, plan detector output, route term, or later evidence. The two labels are sealed before the canonical DS is made available to any downstream packet generator. Disagreement is retained as `U_R`; consensus cannot silently exclude it.

The primary `N_A` is exactly one first eligible admission per subject satisfying all of the following:

* age at admission at least 18 by the anchored-age formula;
* exact admission-linked report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclusion of screening, established surveillance, cancer staging or oncologic surveillance, conditional/optional plans, explicit no follow-up, in-hospital death, locked hospice/comfort-only transition, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* no inference of nodule size, smoking, malignancy risk, life expectancy, recommendation appropriateness, or unobserved clinical context.

Sort eligible admissions per subject by `admittime`, then numeric/string-stable `hadm_id`, and retain the first. Apply this rule once before outcome evidence and reuse its manifest hash everywhere. `U_R` admissions remain in denominator bounds and cannot be promoted to AUTO.

A locked probability sample of deterministic retrieval-negative exact admission-linked radiology reports is reviewed, stratified by chest CT exam name/code and pulmonary lexical fragments. Use at least the finite-population size under which zero misses yields a simultaneous one-sided 95% upper bound of 0.5 missed eligible opportunities per 100 negatives in each audit stratum, or census a smaller stratum. Any confirmed missed eligible report, or an upper bound implying denominator undercoverage greater than 5% relative to estimated `N_A`, invalidates retrieval and blocks all routes. Revision requires a new Stage 0 without inspecting route outcomes. This frame audit never changes route-sample probabilities after draw.

## Reciprocal chains, canonical artifact, exhaustive states, and unit clocks

A report/addendum chain is operative only when subject and admission are consistent and detail links are reciprocal: the `AR` parent relation and corresponding `RR` addendum relation must agree. Every operative component requires `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. Within a valid reciprocal chain, a later material pre-`D` component supersedes the earlier component. Missing nodes, one-way or cross-admission links, contradictory links, unresolved operative text, or missing/contradictory recommendation time remain `U_R`. They are safety sentinels and cannot become AUTO.

For each `N_A` admission, require exactly one `note/discharge` row with `note_type=="DS"`. Any duplicate `(subject_id,hadm_id)` canonical key stops the run. Missing artifact, text, linkage, or `storetime`; unreadable text; or contradiction is retained as unknown. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

Preserve the exhaustive departure state `Y` for every `N_A`: `F` faithful, `C_O` omitted, `C_I` incomplete/discordant, `R` text-supported alternate resolution, `A` known `DS_store>D`, and `U_D` unknown artifact/content. Preserve availability `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, artifact age `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, eventual text state `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}`, and all zero/unknown cells of `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A`. Later faithful text cannot change departure state `A`.

For each operative recommendation unit `u`, retain `R_store,u`, canonical `DS_store`, and the unit clock

`G_u = DS_store - R_store,u` in hours.

The checked identity is `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`. The parent's index-unit `G` remains descriptive, but only each unit's `G_u` controls opportunity. The primary ample subset `E_A` remains index `V_D` with index `H in {H_24_72,H_72plus}`. No admission is content-routable unless every active unresolved unit has resolved `G_u>=24h`. A unit with `G_u<24h`, unknown clock, stale artifact, or post-departure artifact cannot become a content failure.

Deterministic unit extraction runs exhaustively over all `N_A` before sampling and creates minimum and maximum inventories. The semantic unit remains the smallest operative tuple `(target cluster,action/modality,due interval/date,polarity)`: co-mentioned nodules sharing action/window are one unit; different actions or windows are different units; reciprocal restatements are deduplicated; a material addendum revises rather than duplicates a unit. Unresolved coreference or splitting is `U_BOUNDARY`. Deterministic multiplicity and ambiguity are sampling signals only. Human inventory is established for sampled admissions, and every possible unit in each sampled admission is reviewed. Units are never independently sampled, weighted, dropped, or treated as independent observations.

## Stage 1: master probability sample and untouched audit split

### Outcome-blind strata and safety-sentinel census

After `N_A`, canonical keys, reciprocal chains, clocks, and deterministic minimum/maximum span inventories are frozen—but before any physician component label—assign each admission to exactly one hierarchical sampling stratum:

1. `S_SENTINEL`: reciprocal-chain defect; missing/contradictory time; missing/unreadable canonical artifact; parser failure; `U_BOUNDARY`; at least two possible recommendation units; different actions or due windows; negation/conditionality conflict; target-linked decision/non-pursuit/definitive-treatment language; or discordant pulmonary-plan spans in the DS. This stratum is sampled with probability one.
2. `S_TIMECLOCK`: no sentinel, but any deterministic unit has `G_u<24h`, unknown `G_u`, `DS_store>D`, or stale chronology.
3. `S_ZERO`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and no deterministic target-linked pulmonary plan span in the DS.
4. `S_PARTIAL`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and target text is detected but an action, timing, responsibility, polarity, or linkage element appears absent or discordant.
5. `S_COMPLETE`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and a deterministic target/action/timing/responsibility or replacement-plan pattern is present.

These labels select review workload; they are not routes, are never shown to reviewers, and never enter component adjudication. All non-sentinel strata retain positive sampling probability because the screen can be wrong.

Within each non-sentinel stratum sample admissions by simple random sampling without replacement from the frozen ordered frame. For `S_ZERO`, use the larger of 300 or the finite-population size for worst-case proportion 0.5 and ±4 percentage-point 95% precision, capped at `N_h`. For every other non-sentinel stratum, use the larger of 120 or the corresponding size for ±7 percentage-point precision, capped at `N_h`:

`n_h = ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25 / {e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`.

Add all `S_SENTINEL` admissions. This union is the **master sample** `M`, with inclusion probability `pi_i=1` for sentinels and `pi_i=n_h/N_h` otherwise. Record `N_h,n_h,pi_h`, protocol-derived seed, frozen order, selected IDs, no replacements, packet completion, and reasons. Every selected admission remains in inference even if unreadable or unreviewed, with adverse labels.

The inherited ceiling is 900 master admissions. The held-out audit consumes admissions within, not in addition to, that ceiling. If the sentinel census plus formula samples exceed 900, obtain more resources or declare feasibility failed; do not lower probabilities, drop sentinels, or inspect labels before deciding.

### Prespecified hidden audit subset

Immediately after drawing `M`, a data custodian using a second protocol-derived seed draws an audit subset `A` by SRSWOR within every nonempty stratum of `M`. Set

`a_h = min[n_h, max(1, min(30,n_h), ceil(0.30*n_h))]`.

Thus at least one and ordinarily at least 30 admissions per sufficiently large stratum are held out, while approximately 30% of large strata are reserved. For sentinel admissions, `n_h=N_h`, and the same randomized audit split applies; every sentinel is still reviewed in either the primary arm or audit arm, preserving the safety-sentinel census. An admission's unconditional audit probability is `q_h=a_h/N_h`; every admission in every nonempty stratum has `q_h>0`. The non-audit primary component sample is `P=M\A`; its unconditional probability is `(n_h-a_h)/N_h` when positive. If a nonempty stratum would have no primary admission, it remains audit-only and primary estimates for that stratum are explicitly unavailable rather than moving an admission after inspection.

The custodian writes separate HMAC-derived arm identifiers and seals the crosswalk. Audit IDs, packet counts by outcome-like detector, text, labels, and review status are inaccessible to primary adjudicators, route programmers, statisticians, and governance until all of these are sealed: protocol and margins, route-code container/hash, packet-generation code, primary component labels, primary analysis code, replicate seeds, and empty output tables. No audit admission may be used for dictionary development, examples, reviewer training, redaction tuning, parser repair, code debugging, margin choice, or primary result interpretation. Any exposure breaks the audit and forces `NO_SELECTION`; no replacement audit can be drawn after labels exist.

## Stage 2: component firewall, masking, and independent seals

### Separation rules applying to every arm

The index system used in `P` and `A` has five components: eligibility/chain, radiology units, chronology/artifact, departure context, and discharge-plan attributes. Eligibility is already sealed at Stage 0. For the remaining components:

* Rosters are disjoint by component and arm. No clinician, redactor, packet quality reviewer, adjudicator, or audit reference reviewer may review the same admission in more than one component or both index and audit-reference systems. Reviewer IDs and conflicts are checked mechanically before labels are joined. Staffing failure is an inconclusive feasibility failure, not a reason to relax separation.
* The packet service alone holds source-key crosswalks. It performs no clinical labeling. For each panel it emits a panel-specific pseudonym `HMAC_SHA256(panel_secret,subject_id|hadm_id|protocol_hash)`, so reviewers cannot link the same admission across views. Panel secrets and crosswalks are sealed separately.
* Packets contain only departure-available fields explicitly permitted for that component. Automated prohibited-field scans and planted canaries test for post-`D` text, later encounters, POE, route names, stratum names, detector scores, another component's labels, expected direction, and shared pseudonyms.
* Each initial reviewer submits a fixed-schema append-only record containing categorical fields, quoted source spans where text is visible, explicit missingness, and confidence only as descriptive metadata. The record is timestamped and hashed. After both initial records for a component are present, the custodian signs a component manifest (packet hash, reviewer IDs, schema hash, record hashes) and seals it before releasing any input needed for another component or any cross-component analysis.
* No consensus replaces an initial label. A third reviewer, when prespecified, receives the original packet without initial labels and creates a separately sealed additional record. Corrections are additive tombstone-plus-replacement records; both old and new values remain compatible and are assigned adversely unless a documented source-integrity error is proven before unmasking.
* Route words do not appear in manuals or forms. Reviewers never vote `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, `NOACTION`, or `MIXED`, and they never see a route generated from their work.

### Component R: reciprocal radiology-unit abstraction

Two independent thoracic/radiology-capable physicians see only the operative reciprocal radiology chain. Calendar dates and identities are replaced by within-chain order plus flags `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`; exact `R_store,u` and `G_u` are withheld. They do not see the DS, structured departure context, deterministic span inventory, sampling stratum, or chronology label. Each enumerates all possible units and quotes target cluster, action/modality, exact due interval/date, polarity, operative component, and supersession evidence. They also label conditionality, established surveillance/staging, and unresolved boundary/coreference without deciding admission eligibility anew.

Locked bipartite matching code aligns reviewer units by quoted offsets and semantic tuple. Unmatched or ambiguously matched units enter the union-compatible inventory. Boundary disagreement produces minimum and maximum inventories and `U_BOUNDARY`; a blinded third opinion can add a compatible inventory but cannot erase either initial one. The sealed output contains all initial inventories and matches, never a consensus inventory.

### Component T: chronology and artifact integrity

Chronology is primarily deterministic code over `admittime,D`, reciprocal links, each component's `charttime/storetime`, canonical `DS_store`, and the identity `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`. A roster-disjoint dual review sees only pseudonymized metadata rows—not clinical text, plan detectors, context, or routes—when links or times are missing, contradictory, tied, or unparsable. Labels are limited to `T_PRE_D_VALID`, `T_LT24`, `T_AMPLE`, `T_POST_D`, `T_STALE`, `T_UNKNOWN`, reciprocal-link validity, and artifact integrity. Any reviewer difference, equation failure, missing value, or unresolved time is compatible with `T_UNKNOWN`. Exact clocks are supplied only to route code after all text-component seals exist.

### Component K: departure-context abstraction from target-blinded evidence

A separate redaction service, sharing no personnel with any clinical panel, removes pulmonary target, action, timing, recipient/order, preference, replacement-treatment, and decision/non-pursuit spans from the canonical departure-available DS. It replaces each span with a length-preserving neutral token and emits no reason code. Two independent discharge-context physicians see this redacted DS plus only `admission_type`, `admission_location`, `discharge_location`, terminal pre-`D` `curr_service`, descriptive ICU exposure, and the pre-`D` transfer chronology. They do not see radiology text or unit cards, exact clocks, unredacted pulmonary content, deterministic strata, plan labels, or routes. They label only:

* `K_OPEN`: no observable departure-frozen non-target stop;
* `K_CLOSED`: quoted non-target evidence establishes a text-supported stop under the locked manual;
* `K_UNKNOWN`: missing, conflicting, redacted, or insufficient evidence.

`K_OPEN` is not a claim of guideline appropriateness, life expectancy, preference, capacity, or clinical need. A third masked opinion is additive only.

Before context labels are opened, an independent redaction-audit roster reviews paired source/redacted packets for every `K_CLOSED` candidate and a protocol-seeded probability sample of at least 10% of all other packets, including audit packets but without arm disclosure. It records target-plan leakage and removal of decision-relevant non-target evidence. Any observed rate above 2% or simultaneous one-sided upper bound above 2% blocks all context-dependent route claims. A detected leak makes that admission `K_UNKNOWN`; it is not simply re-redacted after outcomes are known.

### Component S: discharge-plan attributes from masked unit cards

Only after Component R records are sealed does the packet service generate one neutral unit card per member of the union-compatible radiology inventory. A card contains a random unit token and the sealed target/action/due-window/polarity abstraction, but no raw radiology text, report author, exact report/storage time, eligibility conclusion, boundary vote, sampling stratum, or context label. Two independent plan physicians see these cards and the unredacted canonical departure-available DS, with dates shifted to relative expressions that preserve whether the DS itself states the correct interval but conceal `G_u` and `D-DS_store`. They do not see Component K evidence/labels, Component T labels, detector output, later text, POE, routes, or audit status.

For every possible unit they record atomic fields and exact DS spans: target linkage; target mention; compatible chest/thoracic action; exact timing/date; responsible recipient/team or scheduling; active versus historical/conditional status; explicit informed non-pursuit; explicit definitive replacement; and cross-plan conflict. Locked code maps each reviewer's attributes separately to `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, `S_EQ`, `S_CON`, `S_PART`, `S_0`, or `S_UN` using the strict hierarchy. Vague “PCP follow-up” is not an executable plan; generic specialty mention without action and timing is insufficient; an unlinked order does not resolve a unit; post-`D` content is invisible. Disagreement, absent cards caused by a competing inventory, redaction-like corruption, or unreadable text remains compatible with `S_UN`.

This sequential packet construction does not allow the radiology panel to influence S labels beyond its sealed unit abstraction. The S panel cannot see radiology prose, context, or opportunity clocks and therefore cannot derive the final route.

## Stage 3: locked code route derivation, never reviewer routing

The route executable and exhaustive unit-test fixtures are hashed before any component labels are joined. The join service verifies all component seals and roster-disjointness, then uses a one-time crosswalk to combine records. It evaluates the Cartesian product of both initial labels, every prespecified third opinion, minimum/maximum unit inventories, redaction-audit findings, chronology-compatible values, and explicit unknown assignments. It does not pair “favorable” reviewers across panels.

For each compatible assignment, derive unit status:

* `U_RESOLVED`: `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, or `S_EQ` with all required explicit evidence;
* `U_OPEN`: `K_OPEN`, `S in {S_0,S_PART,S_CON}`, and resolved `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING`: active/unresolved but `G_u<24h` or the clock is unresolved;
* `U_CLOSED`: `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER`: `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, boundary ambiguity, unreadable evidence, redaction failure, or any compatible disagreement crossing unit statuses.

Aggregate all units for an admission in the fixed precedence:

1. any `U_DEFER` gives `A_DEFER`;
2. otherwise any `U_TIMING` gives `A_TIMING`;
3. if all units are resolved or closed, give `A_NOACTION`;
4. `A_AUTO` only if every open unit is wholly omitted (`S_0`), every other unit is reproducibly resolved/closed, every unit clock is ample, and there is no boundary uncertainty, redaction uncertainty, cross-unit conflict, component disagreement crossing status, or unreadable evidence;
5. any incomplete, conflicting, interaction-dependent, or mixed admission gives `A_RECON`; label the nested subtype `A_MIXED` when at least one pure omission coexists with a reconciliation unit.

One unsafe minority unit controls the admission. Duplicating safe units cannot alter route or weight. The **robust route set** is the union of routes across the complete compatible component product. A robust singleton exists only if that union has one member. Otherwise the decision-facing route is `A_DEFER`, and all compatible alternatives are retained. Direct physician route labels are prohibited even as a secondary outcome.

## Stage 4: untouched duplicate-component route-purity audit

Every audit admission receives two fully independent systems:

1. an **index audit system**, using new arm-specific R/T/K/S rosters and the exact locked manuals, masking, seals, and route executable used for `P`;
2. a **reference audit system**, using a second set of clinicians, redactors, pseudonyms, packets, and component secrets with no person or identifier shared with the index system or any primary panel.

Both systems review every possible unit in every audit admission. Reference reviewers see only their own separated component view and are blinded to index evidence, labels, derived route, stratum, and audit purpose. The reference system uses the same conservative atomic schemas so the comparison is computable; it is not declared clinical truth. Its component files are sealed before either route is derived. Only after both complete seal trees pass does locked code derive `R_index,A` and the union-compatible `R_ref,A`.

An audit unsafe-AUTO event occurs whenever `R_index,A=A_AUTO` but any compatible reference component assignment yields `A_RECON`, `A_TIMING`, or `A_DEFER`, or identifies an additional unit, boundary, redaction defect, clock defect, unreadable fact, or unknown that is decision-relevant. Audit nonresponse or broken seal is unsafe for AUTO and uncertain for capture metrics. An audit reference unsafe-content admission has any incomplete, conflicting, mixed, interaction-dependent, additional, or boundary-uncertain unit. A reference uncertain admission has any decision-relevant unknown in K, S, R, T, artifact integrity, or redaction.

The audit is not an outcome-triggered AUTO verification: all audit admissions and all their units receive duplicate review before routes are known. It therefore measures route purity, missed minority components, cross-system reproducibility, and workload without selecting verification because AUTO was already called. If audit AUTO, unsafe-content, timing, or uncertainty events are too sparse for their route-specific requirements, that route is inconclusive—not supplemented with primary cases.

## Finite-population estimands

The population denominator is always `N_A`; the sampling and inference unit is the admission, which is also the first and only admission per subject. Recommendation units remain clustered within admissions.

### All-population route burdens from the master index system

Using `w_i=1/pi_h`, estimate for `r in {AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,MIXED}`:

`T_hat_r = sum_(i in M) w_i I(R_index,i=r)`

and

`B_hat_r = 100*T_hat_r/N_A`.

`MIXED` is nested within RECON and is never double-counted. Robust singleton routes are primary. Each component reviewer, compatible route sets, and favorable complete-case labels are sensitivity displays only. Estimate all `Y`, `V/H/Z`, chronology, non-`E_A`, and unknown margins so exhaustive weighted cells reconcile to `N_A`.

### Component and route reproducibility

Within `P`, and independently within `A`, report weighted component-specific exact agreement, category-specific positive agreement, directional confirmation, Gwet AC1, and kappa for R inventory fields, T status, K state, and every S attribute. Route reproducibility is computed only after locked derivation. Report weighted raw route agreement, route-specific positive agreement, AC1, kappa, and the lower of the two directional confirmation probabilities. Report crossings of AUTO versus `{RECON,TIMING,DEFER}` and RECON versus `{AUTO,NOACTION}`. Consensus is not an estimand or gate.

### Held-out AUTO purity and missed-unit risk

Audit inference uses unconditional audit weights `v_i=1/q_h=N_h/a_h`. Define

`C_AUTO^A = P_A(reference-unsafe event | R_index=A_AUTO)`

as the Hájek ratio of weighted unsafe index-AUTO admissions to weighted index-AUTO admissions in `A`. Report its simultaneous one-sided randomization upper bound. Zero observed events must still yield a positive upper bound.

Also estimate only from `A`:

* `D_MIX^A = P(reference RECON or DEFER | index system sees any pure omission)`;
* `ADD_UNIT^A`, the probability the reference system discovers an additional or boundary-uncertain unit not represented by the index system;
* `leak_AUTO^A`, the probability an audit reference `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` admission is index `AUTO`;
* `Se_RECON^A`, probability index `RECON` or `DEFER` captures reference unsafe content;
* `PPV_RECON^A`, probability reference unsafe content exists among index RECON;
* `PPV_TIMING^A`, probability every unresolved unit preventing content routing has `G_u<24h` or unresolved clock among index TIMING;
* `Se_DEFER^A`, probability index DEFER captures reference decision-relevant uncertainty.

These compare two independently measured text-defined systems. They do not establish clinical appropriateness.

### Workload

Capture wall-clock handling time automatically from packet open to sealed submission, excluding breaks over a locked threshold; also record reviewer-entered active minutes. Report counts and hours for eligibility review, master index R/T/K/S reviews, third opinions, redaction and audits, duplicate audit-reference reviews, seal failures, packet regeneration, units per admission, and analysis. Report `N_h,n_h,a_h,pi_h,q_h`, maximum weights, Kish effective sample sizes overall and for each denominator, sentinel share, audit share, and design-weighted physician-hours per 100 `N_A` admissions with intervals. Workload is a feasibility result, not patient or clinician benefit.

## Inference and baselines

Use exact stratified without-replacement estimators and finite-population variances. Generate at least 10,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates respecting each frozen stratum and carrying all units of an admission together. Master estimates use `pi_h`; audit estimates use `q_h`. For zero rare events, also invert the appropriate stratified hypergeometric distribution and use the wider valid one-sided bound. Propagate the full compatible component-label set inside each replicate before confidence limits.

A max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers route burdens, primary and audit component/route agreement, `C_AUTO^A,D_MIX^A,ADD_UNIT^A,leak_AUTO^A,Se_RECON^A,PPV_RECON^A,PPV_TIMING^A,Se_DEFER^A`, redaction errors, and key workload rates. Audit intervals cannot borrow outcome information from `P`. Report exact event counts and denominators beside every estimate.

Prespecified baselines are:

1. marginal-majority route, with identical weights;
2. deterministic sampling-stratum proxy (`S_ZERO` AUTO-like, `S_PARTIAL` RECON-like, `S_TIMECLOCK` TIMING-like, `S_SENTINEL` DEFER-like), which is never shown to reviewers;
3. recommendation-unit majority routing, expected to hide minority unsafe units;
4. a deliberately leaky pooled-review reconstruction in which one synthetic reviewer record combines favorable component labels, shown only to quantify optimism and never eligible to pass;
5. favorable consensus that removes disagreement;
6. complete-case unweighted sample estimates;
7. an outcome-triggered AUTO-only verification emulation, compared with the all-admission held-out audit to show verification-selection bias.

The leakage-resistant design need not outperform every baseline in route prevalence. A baseline that looks purer only after dropping disagreement is evidence of optimism, not support.

## Governance margins and computable gates

Governance must approve all margins before labels are released. Default confirmatory feasibility margins, if approved unchanged, are `tau_AUTO=0.05` maximum held-out unsafe AUTO contamination; `rho=0.90` minimum route confirmation/raw agreement; AC1 lower bound `0.70`; `sigma_RECON=0.95` minimum unsafe-content capture; `pi_RECON=0.80` minimum RECON PPV; `pi_TIMING=0.95`; and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`. These are silent-validation nomination thresholds, not acceptable harm rates.

All routes require stable hashes/schemas/denominator; retrieval-audit pass; no duplicate canonical DS; `U_R<=5%`; `U_D<=5%`; redaction pass; complete seal/roster firewall; no audit exposure; at least 200 `N_A`, 60 `E_A`, overall master design ESS at least 150, audit ESS at least 80, maximum normalized master or audit analysis-weight share at most 0.10, and simultaneous all-population route-burden half-width no greater than 5 admissions per 100. The upper bound on any packet-view leakage or decision-relevant over-redaction must be at most 2%. The upper `ADD_UNIT^A` bound must be at most 0.05 for AUTO nomination.

Route gates are simultaneous and independent:

* **AUTO:** at least 30 master index AUTO admissions and at least 20 audit index AUTO admissions; master route-denominator ESS at least 100 and audit AUTO-denominator ESS at least 60; lower primary and audit route confirmation/raw-agreement bounds `>=rho`; lower audit AC1 `>=0.70`; upper `C_AUTO^A<=tau_AUTO`; upper `D_MIX^A<=0.10`; upper `leak_AUTO^A<=0.02`; upper `ADD_UNIT^A<=0.05`; and no reference unsafe minority, boundary, redaction, clock, or unknown state remains compatible with AUTO under adverse assignment. Lower `B_AUTO>0`.
* **RECON:** at least 30 master RECON, 20 audit RECON, and 30 audit reference unsafe-content admissions; relevant audit ESS at least 60; lower route confirmation `>=rho`; lower `Se_RECON^A>=sigma_RECON`; lower `PPV_RECON^A>=pi_RECON`; upper AUTO leakage among unsafe reference admissions `<=0.02`; lower `B_RECON>0`.
* **TIMING:** at least 30 master and 20 audit TIMING admissions; audit ESS at least 60; lower confirmation `>=rho`; lower `PPV_TIMING^A>=pi_TIMING`; zero observed index-only clock use when another active unit has `G_u<24h`; lower `B_TIMING>0`.
* **DEFER:** at least 30 master and 20 audit uncertain-reference admissions; audit ESS at least 60; lower confirmation `>=rho`; lower `Se_DEFER^A>=sigma_DEFER`; and no adverse-compatible unknown routed AUTO.

A two-lane silent validation requires AUTO and RECON to pass. Reconciliation-only silent validation may be nominated if RECON passes while AUTO fails specifically because leakage-resistant purity, multiplicity, or additional-unit bounds fail, provided uncertainty is reported. Missing margin approval gives `NO_SELECTION`.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. Recompute source/schema hashes and stream complete files. Hash/header/denominator drift or duplicate canonical DS stops analysis.
2. Reconcile full source counts to the inherited profile of 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 detail rows, 331,794 unique DS keys, and 17 missing DS `storetime`; explain any mismatch and freeze a new protocol before labels.
3. Prove reciprocal links in both directions. One-way, cross-subject, or cross-admission links cannot establish an operative component.
4. Verify every row-wise identity `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`; changing the index clock cannot rescue a minority unit below 24 hours.
5. Reproduce `N_A`, strata, master draw, audit draw, `pi_h`, and `q_h` from hashes and seeds. Any replacement, selected-case deletion, sentinel omission, or audit redraw fails.
6. Prove audit concealment from access logs. Any audit text/ID/label access before the complete primary/code/output-shell lock fails all selection.
7. Prove roster disjointness across eligibility, R, T, K, S, redaction, primary/audit, and audit-reference roles at admission level. A collision changes the admission to adverse unknown and, if systematic or unresolved, fails the firewall gate.
8. Verify component seal order. Route derivation or cross-component join before all required seals fails. Post-seal edits remain compatible adverse values.
9. Seed packet canaries inside and outside dictionaries. Any prohibited later text, POE, route/stratum, shared pseudonym, exact withheld clock, or another component label fails that packet and contributes to leakage.
10. Redaction fixtures place target/action/timing/recipient phrases adjacent to decisive non-target context. Leakage and over-redaction are both measured; either above bound blocks context routes.
11. Synthetic finite populations place rare unsafe cases in sentinel, non-sentinel, multi-unit, and apparently complete strata. Verify unbiased master/audit estimates and nominal simultaneous coverage; unequal-fraction unweighted estimates must fail.
12. A fixture with nine pure omissions and one conflict must be `A_MIXED` nested in RECON, never AUTO. Duplicating nine omissions cannot alter route or weight.
13. A missed extra unit in any audit admission counts against `ADD_UNIT^A`, `C_AUTO^A` when relevant, and leak/capture metrics. It cannot be called a harmless abstraction difference.
14. Cartesian-product tests must include all initial and third labels, min/max inventories, unknowns, redaction defects, and corrected records. Selecting a favorable one fails.
15. Audit all admissions, not only AUTO. An emulated AUTO-triggered verifier may be reported only as a biased baseline and cannot contribute to gates.
16. Zero audit unsafe events must still emit a positive one-sided upper bound. A zero point estimate alone cannot pass.
17. Selected unreadable/unreviewed admissions remain weighted and adverse. Complete-case deletion fails.
18. Threshold sensitivities at `G_u>=12h` and `>=72h` retain `N_A`; AUTO/RECON nomination requires no adverse route reversal at 72 hours. Stale/post-departure artifacts remain excluded.
19. Adding or removing optional POE analysis cannot alter units, states, routes, burdens, audit events, or gates.
20. Claim verifier accepts only computed source feasibility, finite-population route burden, reproducibility, text-defined purity/capture, uncertainty, and workload. It rejects claims of guideline appropriateness, safe deployment, clinician awareness/error, causal benefit, communication success, follow-up completion, or patient outcome.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

**Supportive for a prospective silent AUTO validation** means all source, denominator, retrieval, reciprocal-chain, packet-firewall, seal-order, roster, redaction, and audit-concealment checks pass; all-population AUTO burden is nonzero and precise; independent component and route reliability pass; and the untouched audit's adverse one-sided bounds for unsafe AUTO, missed units, mixed admissions, and leakage all remain below locked margins with adequate audit events and ESS. This supports only a live silent test of unchanged extraction and routing. It does not support automatic insertion or even clinician-visible suggestions.

**Supportive for reconciliation-only silent validation** means RECON reproducibility, held-out unsafe-content capture, PPV, event, precision, and workload gates pass but AUTO fails because independently separated evidence exposes contamination, unit multiplicity, route crossing, or uncertainty. This is a clinically useful negative result against copy-oriented routing. A two-lane silent study requires both AUTO and RECON support.

**Adverse** includes a held-out reference unsafe or uncertain component among index AUTO admissions above the margin; excess additional-unit discovery; lower purity after removing reviewer overlap; component or route disagreement crossing clinical categories; timing misclassified as content failure; RECON/DEFER failing capture; or workload made infeasible by required separation. These reject the corresponding retrospective nomination, not every possible future system.

**Inconclusive** includes retrieval undercoverage, excessive `U_R/U_D`, failed redaction, packet leakage, roster collision, broken or late seals, any audit exposure, sparse audit route events, inadequate ESS, wide simultaneous intervals, sentinel census above resources, selected-case nonresponse, unknown-compatible route crossing, or absent governance approval. Sparse AUTO is not evidence that automation is unnecessary; report its upper burden bound. No primary result can replace an underpowered held-out audit.

## What is computationally checkable and what is not

An automatic verifier can recompute hashes, headers, keys, reciprocal links, chronology equations, denominator rules, strata, probabilities, sample and audit draws, packet manifests, roster collisions, seal order, route-code fixtures, finite-population estimates, intervals, gates, and whether narrative claims match outputs. It can verify that adverse assignments were propagated and that route words or prohibited fields were absent from packet schemas.

Clinical adjudication is essential for incidental-nodule eligibility, semantic unit boundaries, whether DS text faithfully links target/action/timing/responsibility, and whether non-target evidence supports an observable closure. Those labels can be checked for protocol compliance and reproducibility but not proven clinically true by code. MIMIC cannot reveal actual report viewing, draft/version chronology, physical departure, outside plans, clinician intent, patient preference, communication, future adherence, or harm. Claims involving those facts require new data, expert governance, and another study.

## Required evidence after any supportive result

A supportive retrospective result freezes the extractor, dictionaries, reciprocal-chain logic, unit schema, packet views, redaction rules, component manuals, route executable, margins, and audit procedures for a prospective silent study of consecutive adult discharges. That study must collect immutable report finalization/addenda, report availability/viewing, DS draft/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, every candidate unit, clinician/team responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences and competing-risk context, and final artifact. The silent gate changes no care. New independent component panels blinded to routes and later outcomes assess purity, mixed-unit miss rate, timing, deferral capture, drift, and workload, with at least 100 route-positive admissions and 30 in each claimed route or a prespecified accrual stop.

Only after successful silent validation could a governed route-stratified trial compare usual care with clinician-confirmed prepopulation in validated AUTO admissions and side-by-side reconciliation in validated RECON admissions. Correction/dismissal, inappropriate insertion, artifact fidelity, time/alert burden, responsibility assignment, communication, orders/scheduling, completion, and harms require prospective workflow and outcome data.

## Substantive advance over the parent and remaining uncertainty

This child does not change the clinical population, temporal boundaries, denominator, source bindings, state definitions, unit clocks, worst-unit route hierarchy, sampling unit, design-weighted estimands, or noncausal interpretation. Its substantive advance is a measurement-design repair. Each route component now comes from a roster-disjoint masked view; initial labels are independently sealed before cross-component joining; locked code derives every route from all compatible component products; safety sentinels remain a census and every master admission retains known positive inclusion probability; and the key purity/capture claims come from a hidden probability sample receiving duplicate all-unit component adjudication regardless of its eventual route. Thus apparent AUTO purity can no longer be manufactured by selecting AUTO for verification, reusing reviewers, resolving disagreement favorably, or allowing one panel to see the evidence needed to infer a complete route.

Important uncertainty remains. Separated expert panels can share the same cultural interpretation despite no direct leakage. The audit reference is an independent text-defined measurement system, not clinical truth. A deterministic retrieval frame can still miss opportunities, and probability sampling bounds rather than eliminates unsampled rare events. The canonical DS is only a stored artifact; `G_u` is storage-to-storage opportunity, not actual viewing or editing time. Redaction can remove decisive context, and MIMIC lacks preferences, outside plans, responsibility, and downstream workflow. Governance margins are operational thresholds, not learned utilities or acceptable harm rates. Therefore even a fully supportive result establishes only that this exact retrospective routing process is sufficiently reproducible and leakage-resistant to merit prospective silent validation.
