# Does cystatin-C discordance identify a clinically actionable kidney-surveillance group?

## Unresolved hypothesis and decision

The strongest available evidence is the locally read critical-illness study, `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`: in 38 mechanically ventilated adults, serial creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance and rectus-femoris ultrasound showed that creatinine-based eGFR could overestimate measured dysfunction after muscle loss. That establishes biological plausibility in a selected ICU cohort, not prognostic value in UK Biobank or the meaning of low grip in community adults.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is: among UK Biobank adults without a recorded renal event at biomarker assessment, a creatinine-reassuring/cystatin-C-discordant pattern (positive eGFRcr minus eGFRcys), especially with low grip, identifies a clinically meaningful excess risk of a first subsequent hospital-coded N17 event beyond a routine creatinine-based risk model. The consequential decision is whether this pattern justifies repeat kidney testing or closer surveillance. This study estimates prognostic association and incremental information only; it does not test ordering cystatin C, changing therapy, or clinical benefit.

Define continuous discordance (D=eGFRcr-eGFRcys), where positive values are the hypothesized creatinine-reassuring direction. Define grip (G) as the maximum valid left/right value and retain weaker-side and sex-standardized grip sensitivities. ## Analysis and scientific alternatives

The primary estimand is the 5-year cause-specific AKI hazard and competing-risk cumulative-incidence contrast for prespecified low/median/high grip profiles, per training-set SD of (D), including the (D	imes G) interaction. Also report 5-year risk differences, uncertainty intervals, calibration and decision-relevant risk classification.

Fit nested transparent cause-specific Cox/Aalen–Johansen models on the same participant-level split:

- M0: routine information—age, sex, BMI/weight, grip, creatinine-derived eGFRcr and prespecified verified covariates;
- M1: M0 plus cystatin-C-derived eGFR/eGFR discordance (D);
- M2: M1 plus the prespecified (D	imes G) interaction and restricted cubic splines for eGFRcr and age.

Report whether M1/M2 improve held-out 5-year Brier score and calibration slope/intercept, and whether they change the proportion crossing a prespecified high-risk threshold. A small AUC/C-index gain alone is not evidence of clinical value. Use Fine–Gray only as a sensitivity analysis, not as a replacement for the competing-risk estimand. Bootstrap participant-level uncertainty; estimate standardization, knots, thresholds and all tuning only in training data.

The substantive alternative is a CPU gradient-boosted discrete-time survival model using exactly the M0–M2 inputs, outcome definitions, participant-level split and 5-year target. Shallow trees/early stopping are adequate. It tests whether nonlinear thresholds or D-by-grip heterogeneity are missed by the transparent model; it is not selected for a small predictive gain. Report held-out Brier/calibration, risk-threshold contrasts and accumulated-local-effect curves with bootstrap intervals. Expected post-extraction budget is <=4 GB RAM and <=20 CPU minutes; GPU is unnecessary.

Use a deterministic SHA-256 hash of `eid` for a 60% train/20% tuning/20% test split from accessible discovery buckets 0–79; reserved 80–99 are not external validation, and `eid` is never a feature. If the primary N17 event count, date ordering, or exposure support is inadequate, report an inconclusive feasibility result rather than substituting an easier outcome.

The deferred alternative is the prior episode's joint latent measurement-error/competing-risk model using repeat assessments. It could distinguish persistent discordance from visit noise and regression to the mean, but it is more selected, requires adequate repeat/event support and convergence, and does not answer the first question of whether cystatin C adds information in the broad eligible cohort. Revisit it if this experiment shows a stable, clinically material signal. Delphi-like sequence modeling is deferred because the question is biomarker increment, not disease-order generation. A Bayesian measured-GFR model is deferred because no measured-GFR anchor exists.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires a positive, clinically non-negligible D-by-low-grip contrast with uncertainty compatible with the prespecified direction; persistence under primary-code and broad renal-history exclusions; and held-out calibration/Brier improvement for M1/M2 over M0, with a stable threshold contrast in the learned alternative. This supports incremental prognostic association in this selected UKB cohort only.

Adverse evidence is a null/reversed interaction, intervals compatible with clinically negligible effects, disappearance under primary-code or renal-code sensitivity, no held-out calibration improvement, or poor calibration after adding cystatin C. That argues against using this pattern to prioritize surveillance in this setting; it does not show that cystatin C lacks biological value.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse events, severe missingness, date/code mispairing, unstable split/bootstrap results, or intervals spanning both no effect and the prespecified clinical-information threshold. It leaves the question unresolved.

Computation can verify source hashes, one-to-one joins, counts, validity filtering, CKD-EPI calculations, paired code/date ordering, risk sets, split integrity, model fits, uncertainty, Brier/calibration and interpretation labels. It cannot establish true AKI severity, measured GFR, causality, treatment benefit, transportability, or a recommendation. Those require adjudicated AKI and laboratory trajectories, measured-GFR/body-composition anchors, treatment data, expert review and independent/prospective validation.
