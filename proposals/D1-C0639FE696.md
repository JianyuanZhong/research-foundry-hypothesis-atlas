# Opportunity-conditional target-link documentation experiment

## Purpose and targeted repair

This child combines the clinically proximal target-linked downstream test in `[prior hypothesis]` with the frozen probability, allocation, and measurement foundation of `[prior hypothesis]`. It repairs one remaining ascertainment error: a subject's latest timestamp in MIMIC does not establish continuous capture through a future window. In particular, a maximum timestamp that is itself a radiology timestamp is circular evidence and cannot make an interval fully observable.

The first-eligible adult pulmonary-opportunity population, one admission per subject, pre-departure chronology, exact canonical DS construction, exact support atom `g=(E, terminal curr_service, W)`, route-concealed ontology, all-unit precedence, inherited phase-1/phase-2 probability design, target selection, precision gates, workload ceilings, and silent-bridge-only conclusion are unchanged. This layer is downstream of the frozen phase-2 admission sample and sealed target. It must not use later observations to alter eligibility, route, target, sampling, allocation, weights, or parent endpoints.

The unresolved, falsifiable question is narrower than completion or follow-up: among admissions for which an explicit radiology-document opportunity is actually present in a prespecified window, is the probability that the stored report is target-linked higher in the due window than in paired placebo windows, and is it more specific than a nonpulmonary document control? The experiment deliberately does not estimate the probability of a report opportunity, because absence of a report is not evidence of no care, no imaging, or no MIMIC coverage.

The strongest supported claim before this experiment is only that the supplied MIMIC snapshot contains the required structured and narrative fields. The experiment tests a stored-text, opportunity-conditional documentary association. Even a supportive result cannot establish a completed study, report delivery or viewing, scheduling, clinical appropriateness, responsibility, safety, benefit, or causality.

## Explicit opportunity and deterministic report handling

An explicit radiology-document opportunity for window `w` is a distinct source row with nonmissing `note_id`, matching `subject_id`, nonmissing `charttime` in `w`, and a retained document record, regardless of whether its text is empty, its `hadm_id` is missing, or it links to the index admission. This is a source-observed document opportunity, not evidence that imaging was ordered, performed, delivered, viewed, or clinically indicated. A missing `hadm_id` never removes an opportunity; when present, `(subject_id,hadm_id)` is retained as a linkage diagnostic only.

Deduplicate only exact `(subject_id,note_id)` identities. For physical duplicate rows with that key, retain one deterministic row after sorting by: greatest count of nonmissing fields, then lexicographically smallest serialized field tuple; record the number and values of discarded duplicates. Do not deduplicate by text, `hadm_id`, `note_seq`, note type, addendum relation, or chart/store time. Each distinct note identity is one opportunity. Retain all note types and addenda. Sort packets by `(charttime null-last, storetime null-last, note_id)`; null `charttime` cannot enter a window. Detail rows are joined by `(subject_id,note_id)`, ordered by numeric `field_ordinal` with serialized field/value tie-breaks, and are display reconstruction only; detail rows never create a time or opportunity.

The same admission may have multiple opportunities. The primary unit remains the admission, so a packet-level positive is `any` eligible report supporting the label and addenda cannot multiply an admission. For a report-count sensitivity analysis, report identities and admission weights are retained and the analysis is explicitly secondary; it cannot replace the admission-level estimand.

## Blinded adjudication and labels

Two independent blinded readers adjudicate every packet with at least one explicit opportunity, using a codebook frozen before real packets. They are masked to route, target order, phase-2 labels, parent endpoint, and whether the packet came from due or placebo timing; window identity is replaced by a blinded packet key. The reader records `note_id` and an exact supporting text span.

`L=1` requires explicit text support that the same pulmonary nodule, indeterminate focal opacity, or its interval follow-up/comparison is the subject of the report. `L=0` means every report in the packet has sufficient text and both readers agree that no such target link is supported; it is a certified nonpositive packet, never a statement about absent care. `K_U` means at least one report has insufficient or contradictory text, unavailable text, or unresolved reader judgment. Critically, no packet with zero reports is ever adjudicated as `L=0`; it is `M0`/no observed opportunity. A packet is `A=1` (fully adjudicable) only if every retained report has readable text, independent reader classifications agree, and the packet-level any-report rule is resolved; otherwise `A=0` and its outcome is `K_U`. `P=1` is a clearly pulmonary/chest report or anatomical reference, independent of target linkage. `N=1` is a clearly nonpulmonary/nonchest focus with no target-linked pulmonary statement; unclear anatomy is `K_U`. An admission-window label is positive only when both readers support the same label. Agreement, exact-span availability, and blinded duplicate reread agreement are reported.

## Probability design and estimands

Use the inherited sealed two-phase probability design without modification. Phase-1 strata, outcome-blind screens, fixed target-cell selection, phase-2 whole-admission allocation, positive inclusion probabilities, independent reader roles, path weights `1/(pi1_i*q_i)`, census handling, no replacement/top-up, and nested Rao-Wu replicate procedure are inherited verbatim from the parent protocol. The target and allocation are frozen before any phase-2 text label is exposed. No opportunity count, linkage label, or observed latest timestamp may alter selection.

For each selected admission and window define:

* `O_iw=1` if at least one explicit opportunity exists; `O_iw=0` is never interpreted as no imaging or no care and is reported as `M0`/no observed opportunity. For `O_iw=1`, classify the packet into the mutually exclusive states `L+` (A=1 and L=1), `L0` (A=1 and certified L=0), or `K_U` (A=0, including any unreadable, contradictory, or disagreement packet). A packet cannot be both `L+` and `L0`; all retained reports are evaluated under the packet-level any rule. `N+` and `P+` are additional control indicators, not replacements for this partition.
* Death is a competing state ordered before opportunity classification only when a reliable death timestamp `d_i` satisfies `d_i<a` (death-before-window) or `a<=d_i<b` (death-in-window). Such admissions are not assigned `M0`, `L0`, or `K_U` for that window, even if a radiology row is present after the death timestamp; post-death rows are retained in an audit but excluded from the opportunity outcome. A death after `b` does not censor or reclassify the window. This produces the exhaustive mutually exclusive window partition `DEATH_BEFORE`, `DEATH_IN`, `M0`, `L+`, `L0`, `K_U`; the protocol reports all six states. If death timing is unavailable, no death state is assigned and the record remains in the ordinary opportunity partition.
* `L_iw=1` if the packet is `L+`; among observed opportunities, `L_iw=0` is reported only for `L0`, while `K_U` remains unknown. `N_iw=1` if the packet has at least one two-reader-supported nonpulmonary control and no `L+`; `P_iw=1` if a pulmonary/chest opportunity is present, regardless of target link. `N` and `P` may coexist with `K_U` and are not used to fill missing outcomes.

The primary estimand is the design-weighted opportunity-conditional target-link probability `Pr_w(L=1 | O=1, fully adjudicable)`, estimated separately for due, pre, and eligible post windows using the common admission-level opportunity denominator and HT total/Hájek ratio. The primary contrasts are `Delta_pre = p_due - p_pre` and `Delta_post = p_due - p_post`, with the exact common opportunity-observed, non-K_U paired denominator and every exclusion/state count disclosed. A contrast is undefined when its common opportunity denominator is empty; it is not set to zero.

Specificity is tested in the due opportunity population using the prespecified common, fully adjudicable due-opportunity denominator: report `Pr(L=1|O=1)`, `Pr(N=1|O=1)`, and the paired difference only where the packet supports the corresponding complete classification. `P` is an anatomical document-opportunity control. The negative control is not an all-admission negative and is never compared with `L` after different hidden exclusions.

All-admission results are explicitly partial-identification summaries, not substitutes for opportunity-conditional estimates. Report the design-weighted number and proportion of selected admissions with an observed positive target-linked report in each window as a **positive-record lower bound**: `LB_w = Pr(L+)`, where the exhaustive partition above makes `L+` a subset of all admissions. Report raw and weighted counts for every partition state. For a declared unresolved set `U_w` consisting of `M0`, `K_U`, and any death state for which the target outcome is not logically defined, report `UB_w = LB_w + Pr(U_w)`; if the estimand treats deaths as nonpositive rather than undefined, omit those death states from `U_w` and state that convention. Never include certified `L0` in `U_w`, because it is observed nonpositive. The envelope is valid only after machine-checking that `U_w` is disjoint from `L+` and `L0` and that `L+ ∪ L0 ∪ U_w` covers the declared denominator. It is a logical envelope, not a sensitivity assumption and not a claim that unresolved admissions had target-linked documentation: `LB_w <= Pr(target-linked stored record) <= UB_w` under the stated state convention. The due/pre/post opportunity denominators and all-admission lower-bound denominators are never conflated.

Use at least 20,000 nested Rao-Wu replicates preserving first-stage PSUs, phase-1 bands, phase-2 subcells, censuses, and frozen allocation. One simultaneous 95% max-statistic family covers parent gates/endpoints, opportunity rates, `LB`/unknown envelopes, opportunity-conditional due/pre/post probabilities, L-vs-N specificity, P, agreement, death/censor states, and workload. No complete-case estimate is allowed to replace the primary weighted result; complete adjudicability is a displayed condition of the opportunity estimand.

## Falsification and interpretation

The anchoring hypothesis is weakened or falsified, among adequate observed opportunities, if simultaneous bounds are compatible with no due-over-placebo target-link concentration, if due target linkage is no more specific than `N`, if the signal disappears under the prespecified addendum/duplicate sensitivity audit, or if it is driven by reader disagreement or `K_U`. A low or unequal opportunity rate is not itself adverse evidence; it is an ascertainment limitation and contributes to the unknown envelope.

A supportive result requires parent gates, valid additive workload/rosters, nonempty common opportunity denominators, blinded-reader and control checks, and simultaneous bounds showing the preregistered due-versus-placebo concentration and L-over-N specificity. It supports only a MIMIC stored-documentation anchor for consideration of a separately approved prospective silent actual-workflow bridge. An adverse result means adequate explicit opportunity packets exist and the target-link temporal or specificity contrast fails; it lowers bridge priority but cannot imply noncompletion, unsafe care, or absence of imaging. An inconclusive result includes `ANCHOR_NOT_RUN`, empty or sparse opportunity denominators, high `M0`/`K_U`/death burden, undefined paired contrasts, low P, reader disagreement, or workload/roster failure; it preserves the parent result and identifies the need for actual workflow logs or a better linked dataset.

Computationally checkable claims are source bindings, chronology, deduplication, opportunity-state assignment, inclusion probabilities, weighting, allocation, simultaneous interval calculation, control construction, and workload compliance. Whether text truly identifies the same clinical nodule, whether an explicit document reflects an actual clinical opportunity, and any clinical appropriateness, responsibility, safety, benefit, completion, or causal claim require expert adjudication or another governed prospective study. The strongest evidence ceiling remains silent-bridge-only, and even that bridge requires consecutive admissions, actual-calendar timestamps, probability-ascertained workflow opportunities, immutable logs, and concealed routes.
