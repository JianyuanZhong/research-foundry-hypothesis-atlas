# Working proposal (episode 10): Is the Mpos-macrophage -> FLS program a reproducible state-shaping signal, or donor/composition identity?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis]
(episode-3 selection; seed D3-M2, benchmarked by D3-M1/D3-M3).
Seeds engaged: D3-M1 (composition vs within-cell), D3-M2 (directional crosstalk), D3-M3 (persistence vs remission).
Status: hypothesis-stage, data-bound, with an EXECUTED exploratory screening (below). No causal claim is made.

## 1. Unresolved question
The parent claim is that MerTK+CD206+ (Mpos) RA synovial macrophages drive a distinct,
MerTK-associated resolution-like FLS program [K1]. The parent itself recorded that donor/composition is
confounded with the within-condition (Mpos vs Mneg) contrast. The unresolved question:
**Is the FLS response attributed to the Mpos macrophage state reproducible across independent,
donor-matched Mpos-vs-Mneg comparisons (a state-shaping program), or is the dominant reproducible
axis macrophage/FLLS donor identity (composition), with the Mpos contrast not reproducing?**

## 2. Clinical importance
If Mpos macrophages actively shape FLS state, then therapeutically preserving/inducing the Mpos
program (or its MERTK axis) is a candidate remission-maintenance strategy. If the signal is donor
composition, then Mpos frequency/marker readouts are a disease-activity correlate, not an
intervention target, and MERTK-directed strategies would be mis-specified. The distinction changes
the next experiment, not just a correlation.

## 3. Leading claim vs strongest rival and the observation that separates them
- Leading claim (H1): a shared within-condition Mpos-specific FLS transcriptional program exists,
  so independent donor-matched Mpos-vs-Mneg effect vectors should be positively concordant.
- Rival (H0): the apparent program is dominated by donor identity/composition and/or replicate noise
  [K2][K3], so donor-matched effect vectors need not agree, while contrasts that differ in *the same
  macrophage donor pair* will agree.
- Discriminating observation: between-pair concordance (Pearson/Spearman r) of Mpos-vs-Mneg effect
  vectors across independent donor pairs, benchmarked against (i) same-macrophage-donor-pair contrasts,
  (ii) a donor-label permutation null, and (iii) a replicate/noise ceiling [K3].

## 5. EXECUTED screening (this episode; exploratory, NOT confirmatory)
Script: work/lead/screen_8873.py ; log: work/lead/screen_8873.out
log2CPM per sample; effect vector = log2CPM(Mpos well) - log2CPM(Mneg well), same FLS donor, treatment=none.
- Two donor-matched pairs: MATCHED_SA256A_SA259A and MATCHED_SA257A_SA266A.
- Three contrasts that ALSO differ in macrophage donor: CONF_SA266A(277A_Mneg,278R_Mpos),
  CONF_SA263R(277A_Mneg,278R_Mpos), CONF_SA258R(266A_Mneg,267R_Mpos).
Observed (Pearson r / Spearman rho):
- MATCHED vs MATCHED:            r = -0.031, rho = -0.148   (no reproducible Mpos program detected)
- MATCHED_SA256A vs any CONF:    r <= +0.112
- MATCHED_SA257A vs CONF_SA258R: r = +0.597, rho = +0.388
- CONF_SA266A vs CONF_SA263R (same macrophage donor pair SA277A/SA278R): r = +0.787, rho = +0.611
Interpretation: the strongest concordance is between contrasts that differ in the SAME macrophage donor
pair, while the two truly donor-matched Mpos-vs-Mneg contrasts do not agree. Consistent with H0
(donor/composition dominance), NOT with a reproducible Mpos-specific FLS program. n=2 matched pairs is
small; this is an exploratory signal, not a validated result, and the noise ceiling is not yet established.

## 6. Methods: baseline and substantive alternative (same question)
- Baseline: paired log2CPM Mpos-vs-Mneg difference vectors + Pearson/Spearman concordance across donor pairs.
- Substantive alternative: donor-aware variance decomposition, e.g. feature ~ condition + macrophage_donor +
  FLS_donor (+ treatment) linear/mixed model, and a low-rank factor model (PCA/NMF on the 445x33 log2CPM matrix)
  scored for association with condition vs donor covariates. The alternative reveals HOW MUCH of the
  reproducible variance is donor/composition vs condition, and whether a single latent factor carries a
  condition signal — information the pairwise-concordance baseline (which only asks whether effect vectors
  agree in sign) cannot provide.
- Continuous covariate `cells` (per-sample cell counts) is available and must be modeled/checked (pseudoreplication).

## 7. Outcomes (estimate these before interpreting)
- Supportive (H1): between-donor-pair Mpos-vs-Mneg concordance is significantly >0 and exceeds the
  donor-label permutation null AND the replicate/noise ceiling; donor+FLS variance components are small
  relative to the condition term.
- Adverse (H0): concordance is ~0 or negative after donor blocking, while same-donor-pair contrasts remain
  high and donor terms dominate variance -> Mpos signal is composition/donor-driven.
- Inconclusive: the panel cannot resolve the Mpos effect above the replicate ceiling (too few cells/donors),
  or results depend on including the related/excluded donors, or ligand-side members are needed (GAS6/PROS1
  unmeasured) to state a MERTK-axis claim.
- Falsification criterion (explicit): the parent's "Mpos-specific FLS state-shaping program" claim is falsified
  if independent donor-matched Mpos-vs-Mneg vectors are not positively concordant above the permutation null.

## 8. Honest limits
- n=2 donor-matched pairs in E-MTAB-8873 -> low power; must not overstate.
- E-MTAB-8873 is a targeted 445-feature panel; absence of GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3/NOTCH3/HBEGF means ligand-side
  and other mechanisms cannot be tested here (no claim of evaluating them).
- Association vs mechanism: this design tests reproducibility, not causation; a state-shaping causal claim
  needs an orthogonal perturbation with a verified inhibitor identity/dose.
- Exact MerTK-inhibitor identity/dose and UNC106238 mechanism remain unverified from the prepared metadata.
- Cross-sectional donors: transitions cannot be established.
- Key works inspected at abstract level only; full text/supplementary methods were NOT inspected.
