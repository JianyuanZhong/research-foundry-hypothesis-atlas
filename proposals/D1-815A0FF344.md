# Documented-plan-conditioned, timestamp-robust pathway information in HCC

## Successor decision and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent repaired the major temporal flaw by moving the landmark to 72 hours and restricting laboratory predictors to [0,24) hours, but its primary outcome is still a locally recorded E versus R pathway and does not directly test whether laboratory information remains useful once the care team has already documented a pathway-related plan.

This child makes that unresolved clinical-validity question primary and separates three clinically different recorded pathways: E (embolization/interventional therapeutic), A (local liver-lesion ablation), and S (liver resection), with X for mixed/ambiguous liver-directed events and D for discharge before an event. At the 72-hour landmark, pre-landmark orders are a documented-care-plan proxy. They are not intent, recommendation, eligibility, scheduling, or ground truth.

The primary estimand asks whether first-24-hour laboratory trajectories add calibrated, held-out, pathway-differential information beyond the plan proxy and admission/pre-admission information for the next recorded pathway during [72,144) hours. This is more decision-relevant than predicting a heterogeneous procedure label: it tests whether early physiologic information could identify patients whose documented pathway may warrant reassessment. It remains a recorded-care-process association, not a treatment-selection or treatment-effect estimand.

The 48-hour predictor-to-event buffer is preserved: no primary predictor is later than 24 hours, and the event window begins at 72 hours. The parent E-versus-combined-R estimand remains a labelled bridge sensitivity only. A 48-hour version is not permitted to overturn the primary conclusion.

## Falsifiable hypothesis and evidentiary boundary

Among adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission, no recorded liver-directed procedure in [0,72) hours, continued inpatient observation at 72 hours, and a valid pre-landmark documented-care-plan proxy, adding numeric laboratory trajectories observed in [0,24) hours improves held-out calibration and pathway-specific cumulative-incidence/risk stratification for the first recorded E/A/S/X pathway in [72,144), beyond B0 containing admission/pre-admission information and documented-plan indicators. The prespecified physiological contrast is that a worsening hepatic/renal-reserve pattern has a larger standardized incremental risk contrast for E than for S; the direction may be null or reversed.

The strongest available evidence supports feasibility only. The HCC snapshot supplies encounter admission/discharge times, diagnosis-linked lexical HCC proxies, dated laboratory records, dated procedures and dated non-drug orders. The parent procedure audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 7,780 nonempty procedure strings, 15,874 missing starts, and many midnight starts among relevant E/R rows. A direct read-only scan of the orders source found 16,730,319 rows, 91,850 broad provisional pathway matches and 65,288 unique encounter keys; all matched rows had a nonempty 开立时间. Common matches included 介入科护理常规|0, diagnostic imaging, duplicate/composite orders and stopped/cancelled orders. These are availability diagnostics, not event support or evidence that an order represented intent.

The unresolved claim is whether early dated lab information adds reproducible, calibrated information beyond a documented workflow proxy under a temporally insulated landmark. The experiment cannot establish that any pathway was indicated, intended, feasible, chosen among equal options, beneficial or clinically appropriate.

The primary estimands are 72-hour-landmark CIF_E, CIF_A, CIF_S and CIF_X under the observed competing process, and incremental standardized risk contrasts from adding labs to B0. The principal contrast is Delta_E-S = standardized lab-associated risk contrast for E minus that for S; report secondary E-A and S-A contrasts. Estimate cause-specific hazards with E/A/S/X/D competing and derive CIFs. Do not interpret a cause-specific hazard ratio as a marginal risk or treatment effect.

The secondary process-validity estimand is pathway-plan alignment: within each pre-landmark plan stratum, estimate the observed proportion whose first post-landmark event is the same family, a different family, X, D or censoring. This is not a gold-standard intent measure and cannot replace the primary estimand; it tests whether the proxy is coherent and whether lab signal is confined to poorly documented plans.

## Baselines, alternative and selection

B0 is a transparent six-hour discrete-time competing-risk model using age, sex, admitting department, admission calendar era/time descriptors, diagnosis-type descriptors, pre-admission encounter/procedure summaries where supported, exact-assay pre-admission lab nearest value/recency/count/missingness, and pre-E/pre-A/pre-S documented-plan indicators. No post-72-hour information enters B0.

B1 adds first/last value, change, elapsed time, number of valid values, distinct timestamps, missingness, slope when at least two timestamps exist, and assay-specific density for each exact assay in [0,24). No cross-assay arithmetic is used without verified identical units. B0 and B1 use identical patients, bins, competing causes, preprocessing and patient-level split.

The substantive alternative is a low-dimensional irregular continuous-time coupled-state model using the same early assay tokens, assay identity, elapsed time, qualitative-result/missingness indicators, density and B0 plan covariates. It fits separate latent hepatic-reserve and renal-stress states with assay-specific offsets/noise, irregular gaps, patient shrinkage, posterior uncertainty and E/A/S/X/D heads. Report latent levels, recent slopes, coupled contrast, uncertainty and plan-conditioned predictions. These are representations, not diagnoses. The alternative can reveal nonlinear timing, coupled movement and uncertainty lost by B1 summaries, while explicitly testing observation-process and plan dependence.

Fit parameters only in 0–59; choose dictionaries, assay semantics, state dimension, regularization, bins and observation-process specification using 0–59 and 60–69; lock and evaluate 70–79. No patient ID crosses a partition.

CPU-first is appropriate for sparse fits, aggregation, CIFs, calibration, patient bootstrap and the low-dimensional state model under the solver planning envelope of 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds. Full lab construction and repeated state/bootstrap duration are unmeasured, so checkpoint derived tables and fits. A one-layer GRU/temporal-attention model is deferred, not rejected because it is neural or GPU-capable: it might reveal nonlinear ordering interactions, but the current uncertainty is whether lab information survives plan adjustment and the timestamp buffer, which the auditable state model addresses. Revisit requires residual evidence and a new scientific child if it changes the estimand. Text/image models are deferred because local images are unavailable, documents/pathology have no native times and expert labels are absent.

## Evaluation, calibration, transportability and falsification

On locked test buckets 70–79 report E/A/S/X/D cause-specific and competing-risk Brier/integrated Brier scores, CIF calibration intercept/slope, calibration plots and observed-versus-predicted CIF by prespecified risk bins. Report held-out discrimination/PR-AUC only secondarily, plus absolute risks, event prevalence, standardized CIF contrasts and Delta_E-S. Use at least 500 paired patient bootstrap resamples for B1-B0, state-B1, calibration, CIFs and contrasts.

Internal transportability is a limited estimand: in locked buckets 70–79, report calibration intercept/slope, Brier and absolute CIF error separately by supported admitting-department groups (就诊科室) and admission-calendar-era groups derived from native 入院时间. Report a stratum only with adequate event support. Report performance by procedure 手术来源 only as an ascertainment diagnostic, never as a predictor. A model accurate only in one department/era, or with material calibration shifts, does not support transport beyond that local stratum. This is not external validation; buckets 80–99 and other hospitals are unavailable. Predeclare a 1 percentage-point absolute Delta_E-S relevance threshold and 0.05 absolute CIF calibration-error tolerance; if support is inadequate, report intervals without a directional/transportability claim.

Falsifications:

1. Compare with and without plan indicators. Loss after plan adjustment means lab signal was redundant with documented workflow, not that either component is causal.
2. Report same-family versus discordant plan alignment; if status, duplicates or composites drive it, the proxy is incoherent.
3. Permute lab timestamps within patient and assay while preserving values. Attenuation supports timing/trajectory; persistence implicates value, density or care intensity.
4. Add/remove measurement count, missingness, gaps and observation-process terms. Disappearance is surveillance/selection evidence.
5. Repeat same-assay/same-timestamp aggregation and duplicate exclusion; prove no lab is outside [0,24) or outcome before 72 hours.
6. Remove procedure rows with 00:00:00 starts. A large change despite the buffer is adverse recording-artifact evidence.
7. Retain X as a competitor and exclude it only as a labelled sensitivity. Forcing mixed strings into a family invalidates the pathway claim.
8. Leave out one procedure string and order dictionary member at a time; report contribution. Dictionary dependence is adverse.
9. Use central-line/venipuncture-only workflow terms as a negative-control endpoint. Comparable gain indicates care intensity, not pathway-specific physiology.
10. Compare competing-risk CIFs with a naive analysis ignoring D and other causes; naive results are not primary.
11. Use the parent [0,48) to [48,120) design and combined-R endpoint only as bridge sensitivities; bridge-only findings cannot support this child.
12. Use post-landmark orders only in a labelled leakage audit.
13. Report calendar-era and department calibration drift. Good average metrics with poor supported-stratum calibration are not transportable.
14. Keep examinations, documents, pathology, medications, vitals, transfers and front-page available for read-only audits but out of the primary model.

Support requires a coherent dictionary; at least 100 locked E/A/S events; no leakage; calibrated held-out B1 over B0; nonzero, directionally coherent Delta_E-S meeting its uncertainty/1-point criterion; and no explanation by timestamps, observation intensity, one source/department/era/string or negative-control performance. If the state model is claimed as a substantive contribution, it must add prespecified calibration/CIF information over B1; otherwise retain B1.

Adverse results include null/opposite Delta_E-S, no incremental B1 value, no state value, disappearance after plan/observation adjustment, timestamp-permutation persistence, comparable negative-control performance, poor calibration, source/era/department instability, X/missing-time domination or dependence on one string. Inconclusive results include unfrozen semantics, inadequate events, excessive X/missing times, insufficient labs, incomplete discharge, nonconvergence, unstable bootstrap/CIFs or wide intervals. Supportive results justify expert adjudication and prospective review of pathway reassessment, not choosing E/A/S or changing treatment.

## Evidence limits and scientific deliverable

Computationally checkable claims are hashes, dictionaries, audits, cohort flow, split integrity, native-time leakage checks, concordance, convergence, predictions, CIFs, calibration, bootstrap intervals and falsifications. The snapshot cannot establish validated HCC diagnosis, pathology confirmation, stage, burden, vascular invasion, resectability, indication, intent, scheduling, recommendation, necessity, comparative eligibility, benefit, toxicity, mortality, recurrence, progression, transplant eligibility or outside-hospital events. It cannot validate lab units/reference ranges or translate latent states into liver failure, AKI or treatment suitability.

Clinical adjudication is required for exact procedure/order dictionaries, especially diagnostic/therapeutic composites and same-time conflicts. A stronger study requires acquisition-timestamped imaging/pathology, stage/resectability/burden, intent and multidisciplinary recommendation, lab units/reference ranges, complete death/transplant/outside-hospital linkage and external validation. Only then could treatment selection, comparative effectiveness, policy utility or causal benefit be studied.

Completion requires newly fitted B0, B1 and the selected state alternative, with verified hashes; exact dictionaries and exclusions; cohort flow/split and boundary proof; assay and plan features; model parameters/convergence; locked predictions; CIF/Brier/calibration/contrast/bootstrap outputs; all falsifications and a claim-to-output table. This is a research design, not a solved clinical result; compilation/readiness cannot establish clinical truth.
