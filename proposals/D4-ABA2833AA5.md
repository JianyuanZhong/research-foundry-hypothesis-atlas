## Unresolved question and clinical importance

Plasma-proteomic screens (UKB-Olink, SomaScan) nominate single circulating proteins as
disease-specific preclinical biomarkers (e.g. GDF15, FABP1, CHI3L1, IL6/CRP family). The
parent asked whether a protein signature's disease association is genuinely disease-preferential
(claim A) or a shared systemic morbidity/inflammation/treatment axis that many diseases load onto
(claim B). It restricted to *diseased-only, held-out* individuals and measured a cross-disease
specificity gap `Delta_D` between disease D and a comparison disease D', *holding operative
treatment state fixed* by keeping only people who had undergone a procedure from an "unrelated"
OPCS-4 family. Crucially, the parent *asserted* which procedure families are unrelated and which
diseases form D'. That assertion is the parent's own named unfinished deliverable, and it is the
hinge of the whole design: if the "unrelated" procedure stratum is actually disease-related, the
contrast silently conditions on the disease (a collider/selection artefact) and the
treatment-safe estimand is not identified.

**Clinical importance.** A protein that looks disease-specific in an unadjusted case–control
comparison but is explained by co-occurring morbidity or by the effect of medical/surgical
treatment is a poor candidate for a diagnostic or drug target; the reverse error (discarding a
genuinely specific protein because it is shared with a treated comorbidity) also misallocates
effort. Resolving whether a *defensible, pre-specified* treatment-safe stratum exists — and, if
so, whether any disease-preferential residual survives in it — changes whether single-protein
biomarker nomination from these screens can be trusted at all.

## What changed in this evolution (and what did NOT)

- **Added (new, COMPUTED this episode):** a data-driven ICD-10 x OPCS-4 unrelatedness diagnostic
  on the frozen read-only parquets (`work/lead-ep24/unrelatedness_diagnostic.json`,
  sha256 `[source checksum]`; script
  `work/lead-ep24/unrelatedness_diagnostic.py`). For each candidate disease block D it ranks
  OPCS-4 3-char procedure blocks by co-occurrence lift and reports those that are BOTH
  well-populated and minimally associated.
- **Changed (design):** the treatment-safe stratum is now selected by a **pre-registered,
  data-driven rule** rather than by clinical assertion. Rule: for disease block D, a procedure
  block P qualifies as *treatment-safe/unrelated* iff `n(D and P) >= 200`, `n(P) >= 500`, and
  `lift(P|D) <= 1.05` (lift = observed/expected under independence within the Olink subset).
  Ties broken by highest `n(P)` (power). If no block meets the threshold, the treatment-safe
  estimand for that D is declared **not identified** and the claim is narrowed to `Delta_D^raw`
  with that limitation stated.
- **Unchanged:** the estimand family, age/sex residualization, within-stratum permutation null,
  1-D shared-axis baseline vs learned elastic-net/latent-axis alternative, and the honest
  cross-sectional limits.

## Hypothesis being tested (explicit)

- **Strongest claim supported by existing evidence (not re-tested here):** many plasma proteins
  associate with more than one disease, and cross-disease protein sharing is widespread
  ([K1] reports 422 aptamers shared across >1 disease; 265 shared between CVD and T2D), so a
  naive "one disease <-> one protein" reading of these screens is not supported.
- **Unresolved claim this experiment tests:** *conditioned on diseased-only individuals, age, sex,
  a coarse comorbidity-count stratum, and a pre-registered treatment-safe procedure stratum,
  do at least some plasma proteins retain a disease-PREFERENTIAL residual between D and D'
  (claim A), beyond what a shared 1-D systemic axis plus that conditioning predicts (claim B)?*
- **Leading rival (B):** the transfer gap collapses to the shared axis / conditioned co-morbidity
  once treatment state is held fixed by the data-driven stratum.

## Estimand and analysis

Primary (treatment-safe specificity), on a participant-level split (e.g. 60/20/20
train/tune/held-out, stratified by D/D' label and sex x age-decile):
`Delta_D^casecase = AUC_casecase(D vs D' | held-out, treatment-safe stratum) - null_band`,
where `null_band` is the 1,000-permutation within-stratum null (permute the D/D' label within
age-decile x sex x comorbidity-count strata of the diseased-only treatment-safe set). Baseline:
a single 1-D shared-axis score (first principal component of the protein matrix, i.e. the
shared systemic axis). Alternative: elastic-net / low-rank latent-axis multi-protein model fit
on the SAME estimand. **What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses:** the 1-D axis
compresses all shared structure into one direction, so a protein-specific residual orthogonal to
the axis is invisible to it; the elastic-net/latent model can isolate a sparse set of proteins
whose D-vs-D' signal is *not* explained by the shared axis (i.e. separate A from B), at the cost
of more fitted parameters. Secondary on the same split: `Delta_D^raw` (no treatment restriction)
and `Delta_D^resid` (age/sex/comorbidity residualized).

Positive-control disease pair and a V01–Y98 (external-cause / non-disease) negative-control
block are pre-specified. Uncertainty: bootstrap CIs on AUC and on `Delta_D`; report the full
permutation null distribution, not just a p-value.

## Falsification (pre-specified)

- **Supportive:** for >=1 pre-registered disease block D with an *identified* treatment-safe
  stratum, `Delta_D^casecase` exceeds the permutation band (and the learned alternative beats the
  1-D baseline by a pre-stated margin on the same held-out estimand).
- **Adverse:** `Delta_D^raw > 0` but `Delta_D^casecase ~ null` -> the apparent specificity was
  shared morbidity/treatment state; claim A is not supported in the treated-safe population.
- **Inconclusive:** no disease block has a well-populated minimally-associated procedure stratum
  (or too few co-diagnosed cases / sparse strata) -> the treatment-safe estimand is under-identified
  and no specificity claim can be made either way. (Observed diagnostic already shows I10/E78 are
  identified and K21/K44/I25/M19 are near the threshold, so partial identification is the expected
  situation.)

**What each outcome would/would not establish:** supportive would establish a disease-preferential
*association* in this cross-sectional cohort — not causation, not a mechanism, and not clinical
utility. Adverse would bound against over-reading unadjusted single-protein specificity. Inconclusive
would not refute A; it would say the available procedure coding cannot identify a treatment-safe
stratum for that disease.

## Honest limitations and evidence needed to go further

- Cross-sectional/prevalent ICD-10 and OPCS only; no draw/death date in the namespace -> no
  incidence, no causal direction.
- Inpatient under-ascertainment of ICD-10/OPCS may bias co-occurrence and lift.
- The data-driven *lift* rule is a **statistical proxy for clinical unrelatedness**, not a
  substitute for expert code-list adjudication; the specific qualifying blocks (e.g. OPCS Q18,
  Q07, B28 for I10/E78) must be reviewed clinically before any treatment-mechanism claim.
- Comorbidity count is a coarse severity proxy; `n_21003_0_0`/`n_31_0_0` are self-reported.
- OPCS coding here is captured from the main-procedure instance columns (`s_41200_*`); the
  secondary-procedure field should be added in a follow-up to reduce under-ascertainment.
- A causal or mechanism claim would require a documented cross-namespace crosswalk/residual
  temporal anchor and independent replication — neither is available in this island.
