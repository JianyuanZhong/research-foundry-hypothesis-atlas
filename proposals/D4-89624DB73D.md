# Episode 17 — posterior-thigh MFI and temporally separated falls reports

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

Among UK Biobank `ukb672073` participants with visit-2 thigh MRI and a classifiable visit-3 falls response, is higher bilateral posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) at visit 2 positively associated with a fall report for the year before visit 3 when the visits are at least 730 calendar days apart? Does that association remain after adjustment for visceral adiposity, thigh muscle distribution, prior falls and measured function, and is it sensitive to measured item-response selection among MRI/MFI-observed returnees?

Falls can cause injury, fracture, disability and loss of independence. A stable association would justify independent prospective validation of muscle-quality phenotyping; a precise null or adverse association would lower its priority. The principal advance is not that thigh MRI or falls are novel: it asks whether posterior MFI adds an association beyond muscle distribution/function after a full reported recall year has elapsed following MRI. This is a selected-returnee association, not an estimate of true-event incidence, causality, screening utility or treatment benefit.

## Evidence, hypothesis and strongest rival

The UKB thigh-MFI VTE cohort supports relevance of MFI to disease outcomes, not falls [K1]. The closest direct overlap reports a cross-sectional posterior/anterior thigh-volume association with “falls in the last year” [K2]; therefore broad thigh-MRI/falls novelty is not claimed. The myosteatosis review describes unresolved aging versus disease/disuse explanations and a need for longitudinal evidence [K3]. None establishes the proposed posterior-MFI association with a full post-MRI recall window.

**Hypothesis being tested:** in the prespecified 730-day selected-returnee population, one SD higher visit-2 bilateral posterior-thigh MFI has a positive adjusted association with classifiable visit-3 any-fall report, beyond measured VAT, muscle distribution and baseline falls/function.

**Strongest rival:** MFI is a marker of pre-existing frailty, illness, inactivity/disuse, adiposity or prior falls; those factors and reporting/return behavior may generate the association. The longer lag resolves recall-window overlap by construction only if the item’s “last year” wording and assessment dates are correct. It does not remove confounding, time-varying illness, conditioning on MRI/return, recall error or MNAR selection. Persistence after adjustment or weighting does not identify a biological mechanism. The cited review’s etiologic uncertainty is not resolvable with these data.

## Estimands, analyses and comparisons

Primary estimand: adjusted respondent-conditional odds ratio (OR) for classifiable any-fall report (codes 2/3 versus 1) per 1-SD higher visit-2 bilateral posterior MFI among eligible complete cases with >=730 days between visits. Prespecify adjustment for age, sex, BMI/body fat, smoking, activity, walking pace, grip, prior falls, VAT and posterior/anterior thigh-volume distribution; use a fixed covariate set with explicit special-value handling. Report complete-case N/events and all exclusions before estimates.

**Simple baseline:** logistic regression with linear MFI effect and the fixed covariate set; report OR, 95% CI and two-sided p-value, plus absolute standardized predicted-risk contrast as a descriptive model-based quantity. **Substantive learned alternative:** same-row, shrinkage-penalized GAM/logistic model with a prespecified smooth for MFI and shrinkage smooths for selected continuous confounders, plus prespecified MFI-by-prior-falls and MFI-by-grip terms. It tests whether the association is nonlinear or concentrated in measured frailty/function strata—information a linear log-odds slope loses—not whether prediction improves by itself. Compare models on the same cohort using likelihood-based fit and bootstrap uncertainty for the MFI curve/contrast; control effective degrees of freedom before outcome inspection, and report calibration and sensitivity to influential observations. No post-hoc subgroup search.

Retain the parent’s measured-response sensitivity only as a separate, MAR-dependent estimand: among participants with visit-2 MRI/MFI, complete baseline predictors and a valid >=730-day visit-3 date, model classifiable-item response (codes 1/2/3 versus missing/unclassifiable) from visit-2 predictors using cross-fitting; apply stabilized inverse response weights to classifiable respondents. Report propensity calibration, overlap, weight distribution/truncation, effective sample size and balance before/after weighting, then weighted OR/CI alongside the unweighted primary estimate. This sensitivity is conditional on having a timed visit-3 date; it does not correct MRI attendance, missing visit dates, nonreturn, MNAR or event/report validity. If positivity/ESS or balance gates fail, label weighting uninterpretable rather than rescue the primary result.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive: positive primary OR with CI excluding 1, no influential-data dependence, a compatible GAM curve, and materially similar weighted estimate with adequate overlap. This supports a reproducible association in selected returnees and motivates independent validation with prospective fall surveillance; it does not establish causality, mechanism, true-event risk or clinical utility.

Adverse: precise primary estimate at/below null (CI excludes a positive association), or a positive signal that disappears under prespecified frailty/distribution adjustment or is highly sensitive to measured-response weighting with sound overlap. This lowers priority for MFI as an additional falls phenotype in this cohort; it does not prove no effect in other populations or exclude measurement error.

Inconclusive: wide intervals spanning null and a potentially useful positive association, insufficient covariate-complete event support, model instability, failed codebook/quality/positivity gates, or divergent models without clear precision. Report uncertainty and do not call an imprecise null a falsification. If the stricter cohort proves unestimable, do not revert silently to 366 days; return to the Lead for a substantive design decision.

Computationally checkable: exact filters/joins, row-flow counts, fitted models, estimates/intervals, bootstrap/calibration diagnostics, weighting overlap/ESS, and whether narrative claims match output. Requires expert review or another study: outcome-code adjudication beyond inspected codes, MRI segmentation/quality, falls-event truth and recall accuracy, residual frailty/disuse/treatment explanations, MNAR selection, transportability and clinical utility. A verifier must reject correct computation paired with unsupported causal/event-truth claims and recognize appropriately limited supportive, adverse and inconclusive interpretations; automated verification cannot establish clinical truth.

## Alternatives, compute and delivery

The simple logistic baseline is retained because its conditional OR directly tests the prespecified question. The GAM is retained for a scientifically substantive uncertainty—nonlinearity or measured frailty-stratum concentration—not a marginal predictive gain. A sequence transformer/Delphi-style disease-history model is deferred: the tested signal is a single MRI exposure with one later retrospective endpoint, not dated disease sequences; no sequence adds the missing event adjudication or resolves confounding. No GPU is justified for this tabular model; CPU fits/bootstraps suffice. A bounded Parquet scan was measured at 3.6–3.9 seconds on 4 CPUs using selected columns. Full raw CSV extraction/fitting time is unmeasured; estimate budget up to 4 CPUs, 16 GiB RAM, 2 hours for one selected-column extraction plus paired logistic/GAM fits and 500 bootstrap replicates, to be verified in solver. Solver planning envelope is 16 CPUs, 256 GiB RAM, 8 GPUs and 8 hours, but this study requests no GPU. Discovery diagnostics are not a completed scientific analysis.

New scientific deliverable: newly fit the prespecified logistic and GAM outcome models and the measured-response propensity/weighted sensitivity, reporting the complete-case flow, coefficients/curve, uncertainty, model comparison, propensity and diagnostics, plus output-linked interpretation. Independent positive and negative verifier fixtures must cover correct computation with an overclaim, an adverse result, and an inconclusive result. Readiness/package checks alone do not complete the research objective.
