# Scan-triggering negative-control test of the AFP-vs-ALBI post-treatment trajectory for HCC progression

Island: `hcc` (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records). Episode 15. Operation `evolve`,
parent = board leader `[prior hypothesis]` (valid, mu ~34.98, reference_delivery
complete). Status: design + inherited probe-verified bindings; **RESULTS ARE NOT COMPUTED HERE**.
The Lead owns this file; a delegated Evolution worker develops an independent complementary branch
under `work/evo-scancontrol/`.

## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats serum AFP,
albumin and bilirubin. The branch question: **does the within-patient AFP trajectory add prognostic
information beyond baseline AFP and beyond liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics measured in the SAME draws,
for time to radiologic progression?**

The parent fixed endpoint misattribution (modality-restricted abdominal imaging; local progression vs
`转移` as competing events) and added an E_early/E_late detection-timing stratum plus a mandatory dual
landmark-and-time-dependent Cox. **The still-unresolved objection:** the parent's own critique noted
that *both* the E_early and E_late strata are defined by potentially informed imaging, so the stratum
does not by itself separate "AFP prompts the scan" from "AFP reflects tumour activity". The same class
of timing artefact is demonstrated by [K1] (a dated EHR exposure whose apparent survival benefit
reversed sign under a time-dependent model and a landmark model) and bounded by [K2] (landmark
estimates biased toward the null and unable to recover the causal contrast, whereas time-dependent
regression is more robust).

**Opening / evidence the scenario is real:** the parent's probe P1 established ~1.7M dated `检查`
reports with progression/recurrence descriptors (`转移` 25107, `增大` 13115, `复发` 4973, `进展` 3645 in
the first 420k rows), so study timing and descriptors are recoverable. [K3] shows serum AFP
*trajectories* associate with HCC outcomes after locoregional/HAIC therapy, i.e. the exposure-outcome
question is live and clinically relevant, but [K3] groups trajectories and does not test AFP change
against same-draw liver reserve under an informative-observation control.

**Clinical importance.** If AFP kinetics predict progression only because a rising AFP brings the next
scan forward, the actionable reading is "AFP prompts imaging" and the change is **surveillance-interval
policy**; if AFP kinetics carry tumour-activity information that survives a scan-triggering control,
the reading supports **AFP-triggered treatment escalation**. A clean null (liver reserve alone) is also
decision-relevant because it argues for de-emphasising AFP-only monitoring. These imply different
clinical decisions.

## 3. Added by this child (ONE pre-specified element)

**A scan-triggering negative-control test.** In addition to the progression-report hazard, fit an
"imaging-intensity" hazard of **time to the next incident abdominal imaging study** (any study in
`检查` after `t*`, regardless of descriptor) on the SAME AFP-slope and ALBI-slope exposures by
Andersen-Gill/Cox. Pre-specified discriminating predictions:

- Pure informative observation ("AFP merely triggers scans"): the AFP slope predicts the scan-timing
  hazard strongly and at least as strongly as it predicts the progression report.
- Tumour activity: the AFP slope predicts the progression report beyond what it contributes to scan
  timing.

Pre-registered decision rule on `Δ = log(HR_scan) - log(HR_prog)` for the AFP slope (HRs on a
standardized per-SD slope scale), each branch mapped to a distinct result (Section 5). This is distinct
from the parent's E_early/E_late *event-detection* stratum: it quantifies the AFP->imaging pathway
directly rather than stratifying an endpoint that is itself imaging-defined.

## 4. Method comparison recorded before selection

- **M0 (baseline, simple):** landmark Cox with AFP-slope + ALBI-slope + baseline AFP + age + sex;
  linear slope per patient; complete-case triples; naive time-to-event. Establishes whether ANY
  trajectory signal exists and is the falsification anchor.
- **M4 (learned/mechanistic alternative, substantive):** joint linear mixed-effects model of the
  AFP and ALBI longitudinal processes with patient-level random slopes, whose fitted slopes (BLUPs)
  feed the Cox model; optionally (M4') a shared-parameter joint model of the longitudinal processes
  AND the observation/sampling process (draw times) to model informative observation explicitly.
  **Information M4/M4' recovers that M0 loses:** (i) it separates measurement noise from true
  within-patient change (regression-to-the-mean and peri-procedural transient artefacts attenuate or
  inflate a naive slope), and (ii) it uses all draws and models the missingness/draw-timing process
  rather than discarding patients with incomplete triples, which is exactly the informative-observation
  structure under test. Target to fit = the patient-level slope and its uncertainty; split = k-fold
  over patients (no leakage: a patient's draws stay in one fold); outcome/uncertainty evaluation =
  the same Cox HRs with bootstrap CIs plus delta-C-index against M0; baseline comparison = M0 vs M4
  on identical cohorts and the identical primary endpoint. Approximate budget: CPU-only, minutes for
  M0 and low tens of minutes for M4/M4' on the server (no GPU required; the tabular/longitudinal fits
  are small). Selection reason: M4/M4' is justified only because it targets a specific rival
  (measurement/observation structure) that M0 cannot address, not to chase a small predictive gain.
  Deferred: a fully Bayesian joint model with an explicit scan-scheduling submodel (revisit if the
  negative-control test is inconclusive on frequency grounds).

## 5. Falsification (conjunctive) and interpretation

Supportive (tumour-activity reading) requires ALL of:
1. AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 in the primary landmark Cox (M0) and remains in the same direction with
   CI excluding 1 in the time-dependent Cox (parent's dual-model requirement).
2. Negative-control `Δ = log(HR_scan) - log(HR_prog)` CI includes 0 (AFP slope not preferentially
   driving scan timing).
3. Baseline AFP alone does not already carry the same information (baseline-AFP-only model's AFP-slope
   contribution reported).

Adverse (scan-triggering reading): `HR_scan` CI excludes 1 with `|log HR_scan| >= |log HR_prog|`
(AFP predicts getting scanned at least as strongly as it predicts progression) -> surveillance-interval
interpretation, AFP-only escalation de-emphasised.

Inconclusive: AFP-slope HR CI includes 1 (imprecise null) OR `Δ` CI too wide OR event counts too low
after modality restriction. Declared fallback: report the retreatment-proxy concordance endpoint and
the E_early/E_late stratum, and state that the trajectory question is unresolved at this sample size.

Equivalence-null conjunctive rule (parent): a NULL claim requires BOTH slope HR CIs include 1 AND a
held-out delta-C-index bootstrap CI within +/-0.01 of M0.

## 7. Honest limits (retained, not hidden)

- The progression flag is an UNVALIDATED lexical extraction; a pre-registered audit with an agreement
  floor and an explicit fallback to the retreatment proxy is carried from the parent.
- Mortality / mRECIST PFS are unascertainable in this source -> NO survival claim.
- Single institution; no lab units (ALBI assumes umol/L and g/L as in the parent).
- The negative-control hazard for "any next imaging study" uses the same `检查` table and shares its
  modality/lexical limitations; a scanner-schedule field is not available.
- **NO outcomes are computed in discovery.** Event counts, modality-restricted counts and the
  scan-timing hazard rate are the solver's first feasibility outputs and the first falsification
  relevant checks.
- What would require expert adjudication or another study: radiologist-adjudicated mRECIST progression,
  true event rate, causal/mechanistic validity, and multi-institution replication.
