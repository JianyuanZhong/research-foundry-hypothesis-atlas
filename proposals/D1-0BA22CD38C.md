# A preregistered chronological bridge-and-workload gate for the frozen pulmonary handoff protocol

## Parentage and targeted repair

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not restart the pulmonary question and does not replace the selected route protocol. It preserves, verbatim, the selected parents' first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity denominator; reciprocal pre-discharge radiology report/addendum chronology; `D = hosp/admissions.dischtime`; unique canonical discharge summary; exact target atom `g=(E, terminal curr_service, W)` and its inherited route; route-concealed component and holistic panels; independent correlated-error challenge; all-unit enumeration and adverse/worst-unit admission routing; target-freeze state machine; positive-probability two-phase whole-admission sampling; five inherited sampling strata, sentinel census, fixed controls and one charged reserve; design weights and pairwise inclusion probabilities; finite-population Horvitz–Thompson/Hájek and nested uncertainty; domain/era holdouts and no-rescue rules; the maximum of 900 distinct reviewed admissions; and the silent-validation-only, noncausal conclusion ceiling.

The parents make the documentation construct substantially safer to audit. The remaining weakness is operational: a source can be identifiable and textually actionable at `D`, yet the protocol still leaves an independent implementation team to decide (i) what exact pre-label readiness threshold is used, (ii) whether a late or unstable stored artifact is treated as a prospective bridge failure, (iii) how chronological calibration is separated from the unseen replay, (iv) how much panel work is charged before labels, and (v) what happens when a packet is too large, a time block is sparse, or a reviewer is unavailable. Those choices can otherwise be made after seeing labels and can turn a retrospective documentation rate into an unsupported claim of prospective readiness.

This successor adds one substantive, frozen **bridge release contract**. It makes source identity/actionability, temporal stability, calibration, abstention, and reviewer workload jointly executable before any target-dependent result is opened. It adds no new clinical outcome and does not use post-discharge care, orders, prescriptions, or outcomes to repair a route. All unknown, late, conflicting, over-capacity, or unstaffed cases remain adverse or inconclusive and cannot be promoted to `AUTO`.

## Evidence boundary and falsifiable claim

The available data support a reproducible, admission-linked comparison of radiology text and discharge text at a stored-document cutoff, with structured admission departure time and service-transition history. They support measurement of source identity, exact spans, target linkage under a sealed text rubric, storage-time availability, actionability as a documentation construct, supersession/confirmation as a documentation relation, chronological degradation within the de-identified MIMIC snapshot, and reviewer workload. They do not show that a recipient saw, understood, transmitted, accepted, or acted on the information; they do not establish medical appropriateness, responsibility, patient preference, follow-up, safety, benefit, or causal effects. Because MIMIC timestamps are subject-specific shifted timestamps, cross-subject calendar-year transport is unavailable.

The unresolved hypothesis is deliberately narrower than clinical effectiveness:

> Conditional on the frozen parent denominator, exact atom/route, sample, and labels, a route that passes the source/actionability gate in an earlier calibration block will retain that gate and its pre-specified abstention behavior in the later chronologically ordered bridge block, while meeting a pre-label additive review-workload contract; failures will be exposed as `DEFER`, `RECON`, timing failure, unknown, or an inconclusive atom rather than silently counted as safe `AUTO` documentation.

The primary test is whether the weighted lower confidence bound for source-and-actionability-qualified non-deferred decisions in the unseen bridge block meets the fixed readiness bound. A supportive result means only that the frozen stored-documentation process is internally reproducible and operationally replayable in this MIMIC snapshot. It does not imply prospective clinical performance.

The new fields are parallel audit fields, not replacements for inherited targets or routes.

## Pre-label packet and panel separation

After population and sampling are frozen, serialize exactly one immutable packet per selected admission, with pseudonymous `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, `D`, inherited cutoff, source row keys, note types/sequences, reciprocal chain, ordered detail rows, exact unit spans, terminal service and `G`, explicit missingness states, and hash of the unredacted artifact. The packet hash and source row keys are retained in the audit ledger.

The actionability panel receives the reciprocal radiology chain and permitted departure metadata but not canonical DS plan text, route, target label, atom/domain/stratum, design weights, outcomes, or other panel labels. The supersession panel receives the same frozen unit view plus canonical DS and ordered DS-detail rows, but not actionability labels, route, atom/domain/stratum, weights, outcomes, or other labels. Actionability and supersession rosters are disjoint from each other and from all inherited component, holistic, and challenge reviewers. Every panel seals an append-only record before any join to another panel. Broken masking, roster overlap, nonresponse, or packet corruption makes the affected atom inconclusive and excludes `AUTO`.

## Literal source/actionability and supersession ledger

For every inherited unit, record `source_note_id`, source table, note type/sequence, chart/store times, source role, zero-based exact span start/end, exact-text SHA-256, normalized-whitespace hash, source-row key, and `available_at_D`. A valid span satisfies `0<=start<end<=len(text)` and reproduces bytes from the frozen source row. Multiple source notes are stored as ordered separate spans, never concatenated. Copied-forward status is `UNKNOWN` unless an explicit frozen rule establishes it.

Use the sealed four-field codebook for target, action/modality, due timing, and polarity. Each field is one of `PRESENT_EXPLICIT`, `ABSENT_EXPLICIT`, `CONFLICTING`, or `UNKNOWN`; reviewers may not infer an unstated fact from medical knowledge. The released actionability state is exactly one of:

* `A_ACTIONABLE`: all four fields are explicit and internally consistent, the target is linked to the frozen unit, an unconditional action/modality and explicit due interval or unambiguous relative timing are present, the source is available by `D`, and no unresolved negation, conditional/history/hypothetical language, contradiction, or boundary ambiguity remains.
* `A_NONACTIONABLE`: the source is available and explicitly negates follow-up, makes it conditional/optional, marks it historical/hypothetical, or explicitly lacks a required atom field. This is a documentation state, not proof that follow-up was unnecessary.
* `A_UNKNOWN`: source missing/late/unreadable, target or coreference unresolved, timing unparseable, reciprocal chain unresolved, serialization malformed, or independent disagreement remains. It is never converted to a negative.

Two reviewers record fields, spans, availability, and reason codes independently. A third adjudicator sees only the permitted packet and the two sealed records. Any unresolved required field releases `A_UNKNOWN`; raw disagreement remains `U_A` even if adjudication produces a state.

For supersession, use only the inherited canonical `note_type==DS`. Record DS source spans, note identity/storetime, target-link evidence, old/new action/modality/timing/polarity, and precedence reason. Apply this exact precedence: `S_UNKNOWN` for missing/nonunique/late/unreadable/contradictory serialization or unresolved linkage; `S_CONFLICT` for explicitly incompatible contemporaneous same-target plans with no textual precedence; `S_SUPERSEDES` for an explicit same-target cancel/replace/change/decline with extractable changed action or no-follow-up/alternative; `S_CONFIRM` for explicit same-target repetition/continuation of action, modality, and due interval; and `S_NONE` when no explicit same-target competing, cancelling, changing, or confirming plan is observed. Generic “follow up with your doctor,” nonspecific problem-list mention, author/service order, or absence cannot establish supersession.

## New bridge release contract (the substantive repair)

The contract is frozen in a governance manifest before opening any target, route, actionability, or supersession label. It contains the protocol/codebook/parser hashes, all numerical values below, packet-workload formula, temporal-block algorithm, reviewer rosters/capacity, estimands, and sign-off time. These are operational nomination criteria, not clinical harm thresholds.

### A. Three explicit bridge states and route compatibility

For each admission and each unit, derive an audit state without changing the inherited route:

* `BRIDGE_READY`: every inherited route-relevant source span has valid identity and hash, `available_at_D=TRUE`, actionability `A_ACTIONABLE`, no `S_SUPERSEDES`/`S_CONFLICT`/`S_UNKNOWN`, and every active unit has inherited `H>=24` hours.
* `BRIDGE_RECON`: documentation is available and action-ready, but an explicit superseding/competing plan or a resolvable mixed-unit state requires human reconciliation. It is not eligible for `AUTO`.
* `BRIDGE_DEFER`: any missing/late/ambiguous source, unknown/contradictory actionability or supersession, insufficient inherited clock, or broken panel/packet state. `TIMING` is retained as the inherited route subtype and summarized in this family only when its established barrier is insufficient `H`.

At admission level, all units are evaluated. `AUTO` is bridge-compatible only when every unit is `BRIDGE_READY`; `RECON` is compatible only for the explicit reconciliation state; all unresolved states are `DEFER`. The inherited route column and new bridge-state column are never overwritten. A unit with no unique source is `UNRESOLVED` and forces `DEFER`.

### B. Exact temporal replay, with no invented calendar transport

After eligibility and the two-stage sample are frozen, order distinct eligible admissions by ascending stored `D`, then `(subject_id,hadm_id)` as a deterministic tie-break. This rank order, not de-identified calendar date, defines the replay. If a parent has already frozen a temporal partition, that partition is immutable; otherwise use exactly 60% development, 20% calibration, and 20% bridge evaluation by the ordered admission list, with cut points `floor(0.60N)` and `floor(0.80N)` and the final block receiving the remainder. Blocks are never split within an equal `(D,subject_id,hadm_id)` key.

The development block is used only to execute the already inherited route rule and to test software mechanics. The calibration block is used to freeze the operational abstention map. The bridge block is opened once, last, and is never used for tuning, codebook modification, threshold selection, reviewer retraining, or packet repair. No label from a later block can inform an earlier block.

The abstention map is not optional. For each inherited route and each source-status family (`A_ACTIONABLE`, `A_NONACTIONABLE`, `A_UNKNOWN`), report weighted counts and exact confidence bounds. If the parent emits a probability, calibrate it on the calibration block using the parent-specified method; if it emits no probability, do not invent one. In the no-probability case, the frozen map is the deterministic rule above, and calibration is reported as counts/rates only. The bridge replay therefore cannot silently introduce an isotonic model or a learned threshold.

Add a temporal-stability audit that does not use post-`D` evidence. For each route and each occupied final atlas atom, compute the weighted bridge-ready proportion and unresolved proportion in calibration and bridge blocks. A bridge stratum is `SUPPORTED` only when both blocks have positive known denominators, the pre-label minimum-count rule below is met, and the absolute difference in point estimates is at most `0.10`; otherwise it is `DRIFTED` or `INCONCLUSIVE`. The 0.10 margin is a preregistered operational drift margin, not a claim of clinical equivalence. A simultaneous 95% interval for the difference must also include zero for a supportive stability claim; if it does not, report adverse temporal degradation even when both point estimates exceed the readiness bound. Sparse blocks are inconclusive rather than pooled.

### C. Exact pre-label readiness thresholds

The following thresholds are fixed before labels and cannot be learned from the bridge block:

* For every claimable atom, the lower simultaneous 95% finite-population bound for `BRIDGE_READY` among candidate non-deferred `AUTO` units is at least `0.90`.
* The upper simultaneous 95% adverse-compatible bound for `A_UNKNOWN`, unresolved source identity, invalid span, late source, or panel nonresponse among candidate `AUTO` units is at most `0.05`.
* Among units independently identified as requiring reconciliation or defer because of an explicit same-target supersession/conflict, the lower simultaneous 95% capture by `RECON` or `DEFER` is at least `0.95`, and the upper bound for retaining `AUTO` is at most `0.05`.
* The upper simultaneous adverse-compatible fraction of apparently actionable/confirmed units that remain incompatible under any admissible independent reading is at most `0.05`.
* Reviewer disagreement, nonresponse, and packet missingness have an upper simultaneous bound of `0.05` per claimable atom; otherwise the atom is inconclusive.
* Temporal stability requires the absolute calibration-to-bridge difference no greater than `0.10` and a simultaneous 95% difference interval containing zero. This gate is in addition to, never a replacement for, every inherited event, effective-sample-size, route-capture, precision, leakage, domain, challenge, and multiplicity gate.

Use inherited exact finite-population inversion, design weights, pairwise inclusion probabilities, nested Rao–Wu uncertainty, and adverse-compatible partial-identification bounds. Unknowns are not complete-case deleted. The reported bounds use the widest valid result among exact inversion, nested design replicates, and the admissible set implied by unresolved labels. Latent-class or reliability models are diagnostic only and cannot narrow the primary result absent a pre-label independent identifiability constraint.

### D. Exact workload and replay feasibility contract

The inherited admission ledger remains controlling. Every selected admission receives all inherited component, audit, redaction, minority-unit, holistic, challenge, and fixed-control work, plus one charged reserve exactly as the parent specifies. No additional admission is recruited, and duplicate readings never enlarge the distinct-admission denominator.

The added panel charge is fixed before labels at 46 physician minutes per selected admission: actionability A1 6, A2 6, actionability adjudicator 8, supersession S1 8, S2 8, and supersession adjudicator 10. A transformed challenge control receives the relevant same-cost panel work. Let `n_sel` be the frozen selected-admission count, `q_ctrl` the frozen number of added control packets, and `n_res` the inherited reserve count. Record the additive ledger

`L=(n_sel,n_res,q_ctrl,M_parent_actual,M_parent_cap,M_add_base,M_add_ctrl,M_total_cap,h_total,h_per_atom,max_packet_minutes,nonresponse_count,adjudication_count)`

with `M_add_base=46*n_sel`, `M_add_ctrl=46*q_ctrl`, and `M_total_cap=M_parent_cap+M_add_base+M_add_ctrl`. Before labels, verify `M_total_cap` and every atom-specific worst-case charge, including fixed controls and reserve if charged by the parent, are within the same approved absolute budget and preserve the inherited 1.50 workload-ratio margin. If the inequality fails, return `NO_SELECTION`; never lower minutes, drop a reviewer, remove a control, reduce the sample, or recruit a replacement.

To make replay workload computable rather than merely aspirational, pre-label packetize the complete selected sample and calculate for each packet `bytes_unredacted`, `characters_visible_to_A`, `characters_visible_to_S`, `n_units`, and `n_source_spans`. The packet-size distribution is an audit endpoint and does not change eligibility. Any packet exceeding the governance serialization cap of 20,000 visible characters in either panel is `PACKET_OVER_CAP`: it is retained, charged, and forced to `BRIDGE_DEFER`; it cannot be truncated or sampled. This cap is a software/human-review feasibility boundary, not a clinical threshold. Any packet that cannot be serialized losslessly is invalid/inconclusive. Record actual start/end minutes by role, interruptions, nonresponse, and adjudication; the declared 46-minute cap remains the feasibility charge even if a reviewer finishes early.

The bridge block is replayed in fixed batches of 24 ordered admissions solely for workload reporting. A batch is feasible only if its worst-case declared charge (24 times the fixed per-admission panel charge plus its frozen controls) fits the approved session capacity of 1,104 physician minutes; this is a ledger check, not permission to alter labels. If a batch cannot be staffed, all affected bridge endpoints are `INCONCLUSIVE` and remain `DEFER`; there is no route-triggered reassignment, top-up, or retrospective batching. Because timestamps are de-identified, this is a relative replay workload test and not evidence of actual calendar throughput.

## Controls and falsification criteria

Freeze controls, packet transformations, and expected outputs before labels.

1. Remove target/action/interval spans from the actionability view. Output must be `A_UNKNOWN` or `A_NONACTIONABLE`, never `A_ACTIONABLE`.
2. Replace a canonical-DS target span with an unrelated target while retaining provenance. `S_SUPERSEDES` must become `S_NONE` or `S_UNKNOWN`, never remain linked.
3. Shift DS `storetime` after `D` without changing text. The primary state must become `S_UNKNOWN`, and a late artifact cannot repair it.
4. Inject one explicitly incompatible modality or interval. Output must be `S_CONFLICT` or `S_SUPERSEDES`, and `AUTO` must be excluded.
5. Set one clean unit to `A_UNKNOWN` or `S_CONFLICT`. Whole-admission `AUTO` must be excluded.
6. Reorder detail rows, addenda, or packet serialization without changing source identities. State and route must be invariant; duplicate detail rows cannot create a unit.
7. Permute irrelevant formatting or author-like tokens. Actionability/supersession must be invariant; author/service cannot establish responsibility or precedence.
8. Permute labels relative to routes and source rows. Any association surviving the locked null permutation indicates linkage or leakage.
9. Plant supersession in a held-out service or possible-calendar atom while pooled results pass. The atom-specific gate must fail; pooled performance cannot rescue it.
10. Truncate a packet at the 20,000-character cap. The integrity check must detect loss and force `BRIDGE_DEFER`/inconclusive rather than silently review an incomplete artifact.
11. Move one admission across the calibration/bridge boundary or alter a tie-break. The manifest hash and block membership check must fail.
12. Recompute the bridge with a post-`D` note, target label, or outcome. Dependency validation must reject the run.

A run is invalid if a panel sees route, atom/domain/stratum, weights, outcomes, another panel's labels, or later transformed text; if any threshold or control is chosen after labels; if unknowns are favorable; if panel rosters overlap; if packet cap failures are truncated; if a batch is adaptively reassigned; or if a route is nominated before all required seals and workload checks.

## Estimands, interpretation, and decision rules

The primary added finite-population estimand is

`Theta_ready = sum_i w_i I(all units in admission i are BRIDGE_READY) / sum_i w_i`,

reported overall, by inherited route, exact atom, service/calendar domain, and temporal block. Secondary endpoints are `Theta_recon`, `Theta_defer`, excess `AUTO` (`r_i=AUTO` but not all units bridge-ready), missed supersession/conflict capture, unresolved/late/invalid-source mass, packet-over-cap rate, actual and declared minutes, calibration-to-bridge drift, and the source-blind baseline that has identical text, sample, chronology, model, and labels but omits source identity/actionability fields. No endpoint conditions away all adverse units without also reporting the all-unit adverse result.

Supportive results require a valid manifest, all inherited gates, at least one pre-label-supported atom with positive inclusion probability and required event/ESS counts, the exact readiness and supersession thresholds, temporal stability, packet/workload capacity, control invariance, and no leakage. They nominate only a site-local prospective **silent** validation in which the same route is logged but no alert, order, referral, copied recommendation, or patient-facing action is generated. All unqualified signatures remain `DEFER`; no retrospective result authorizes deployment.

Adverse results include an upper unknown/late/source-failure bound over 0.05, lower readiness under 0.90, missed supersession capture under 0.95, excess `AUTO` over 0.05, temporal drift over 0.10 or a difference interval excluding zero, a control flip, packet truncation, leakage, broken seal, or atom-specific workload failure. They falsify the affected documentary bridge or route nomination, not a medical plan and not clinical benefit.

Inconclusive results include sparse temporal blocks, zero or nonpositive support, wide intervals, broad compatible sets, failed staffing, nonresponse, source ambiguity, packet corruption, infeasible control pairs, or inadequate approved budget. Inconclusive never counts as safe and never permits `AUTO`.

The verifier can check source hashes, row identity, joins, reciprocal chronology, `D` cutoff, unit and route derivation, actionability/supersession code execution, temporal partition, threshold arithmetic, inclusion probabilities, workload ledger, packet cap, controls, and dependency leakage. It cannot determine clinical correctness, guideline appropriateness, recipient awareness, transmission, responsibility, delivery, follow-up, safety, utility, benefit, or causal effect. Those require independent clinical adjudication, source-system viewing/transmission audit, and a governed prospective silent or comparative study with actual workflow and patient outcomes.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, this successor converts its strong actionability/supersession rubric into an explicit chronological bridge release contract: fixed block construction, no-probability fallback, exact stability criterion, pre-label thresholds, packet-cap handling, batch-level relative workload replay, and an additive minute ledger that can fail closed before labels. Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, it removes optional calibration language and vague inherited-threshold references, makes the unseen-block rule deterministic, and integrates source identity, actionability, and temporal stability without changing the frozen denominator or target.

The remaining uncertainty is irreducible and explicit. Storage time is only a proxy for availability; a source span may not be what a recipient saw; reviewers may share errors; actionability and supersession are documentation constructs rather than clinical truth; the 20,000-character and 0.10 operational margins are governance choices; de-identified rank order is not real prospective calendar time; MIMIC lacks UI, viewing, transmission, accountability, preference, outside care, and outcome evidence. Consequently, even a fully supportive result establishes only a source-auditable, workload-bounded, chronologically replayable stored-documentation construct and a reason to consider a separately governed silent validation—not live use, safety, effectiveness, transportability, or causality.
