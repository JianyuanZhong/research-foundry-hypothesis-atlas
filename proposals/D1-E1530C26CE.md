# Hemodynamically stable loop-diuretic initiation after resuscitated shock

## Question, evidence, and falsifiable hypothesis

Positive fluid balance after critical-illness resuscitation is associated with poor outcomes, but that does not establish that actively removing fluid improves patient-centered outcomes. The indexed RADAR-2 abstract (180 randomized patients; DOI `10.1007/s00134-021-06596-8`, frozen Europe PMC source `[source checksum]`) supports feasibility and separation of fluid balance, not a precise mortality or ventilator benefit; its full text was not inspected here. A 2026 review (Orieux et al., DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`; full XML inspected from frozen source `[source checksum]`) calls ICU renal congestion hypothesis-generating: diagnostic criteria, therapeutic targets, and outcome-driven interventional evidence remain unvalidated.

The strongest supported claim is therefore that active deresuscitation can lower recorded fluid balance. The unresolved claim is whether giving a loop diuretic at a defined stable decision point improves liberation from ventilation without clinically unacceptable mortality or renal harm. This matters because clinicians repeatedly trade persistent positive balance against hypotension, electrolyte loss, and kidney injury.

Hypothesis: **among invasively ventilated adults at ICU hour 48 who previously received vasopressors, have been vasopressor-free for six hours with MAP at least 65 mmHg, and have at least 50 mL/kg documented net ICU fluid balance, initiation or re-initiation of an actually administered IV loop diuretic within 12 hours increases mean 28-day days alive and free of invasive ventilation by at least one day versus no loop through 24 hours, while excluding more than 3 percentage points excess 28-day mortality and more than 5 points excess 7-day new RRT.** A secondary hypothesis is larger benefit with a pre-t0, result-available chest report showing pulmonary edema. That is a text-derived imaging phenotype, not validated renal congestion.

## Target trial

Use participant SHA-256 discovery buckets 0-79 and all records for those subjects; buckets 80-99 are inaccessible and are not external validation. Select the first chronologically eligible ICU stay per `subject_id`. Let `t0 = icu/icustays.intime + 48 hours`.

Include age at `intime` >=18; `outtime>t0`; invasive ventilation (`procedureevents.itemid=225792`) overlapping t0; at least one completed norepinephrine, epinephrine, phenylephrine, dopamine, or vasopressin infusion before t0-6h; none overlapping (t0-6h,t0]; median valid MAP in (t0-3h,t0] >=65 mmHg; documented net volume from intime through t0 >=50 mL/kg; weight 30-300 kg; urine output >0.1 mL/kg/h in (t0-6h,t0]; latest potassium measured and available in (t0-12h,t0] >=3.0 mmol/L; and no loop diuretic in (t0-12h,t0].

“Documented net volume” is not true total-body accumulation: sum only completed `inputevents` rows with `amountuom` convertible from mL/L, prorating intervals crossing t0, and subtract nonnegative `outputevents` values with mL-convertible units. Exclude rewritten/cancelled rows and freeze item/unit/status rules. Divide by admission weight (`chartevents` 226512, then first valid daily weight 224639, then median `inputevents.patientweight`). Report missing charting by six-hour block. Primary eligibility requires urine output in each 12-hour block; repeat with six-hour completeness and continuous balance. Call this documented positive balance, never measured congestion or “5% fluid overload.”

Exclude RRT before t0 (`procedureevents` 225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809, 225955), ECMO overlapping t0, and comfort-only status only if a timestamped structured order establishes it before t0. Do not use index-discharge diagnoses or notes. Do not exclude baseline AKI; freeze KDIGO stage at t0 as a modifier. Earlier loop use from ICU admission to t0-12h is allowed because this is a start/restart decision and is adjusted and stratified; a never-treated new-user cohort is required sensitivity analysis. This repairs the parent's ambiguous six-hour washout.

Strategies:

- A: at least one actual IV administration of furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide, or ethacrynic acid in (t0,t0+12h].
- B: no loop diuretic by any route in (t0,t0+24h].

For eMAR, require `event_txt` indicating administration, `complete_dose_not_given` not indicating omission, nonzero dose, and IV `route`. `inputevents` itemids 221794/228340 capture completed furosemide bolus/infusion. Deduplicate cross-source events within 15 minutes; retain discordant single-source exposure in primary analysis and require concordance in sensitivity. Freeze drug/route/status strings and IV furosemide-equivalent conversion before outcomes. Oral loop during either grace period deviates from both strategies.

Clone each patient to both strategies at t0. Censor A at 12h if untreated and B at first loop through 24h; a first loop during hours 12-24 follows neither protocol. After grace assignment, management is unrestricted. The estimand is the per-protocol adjusted mean difference A minus B in 28-day days alive and free of invasive ventilation.

Construct ventilation from all `procedureevents` 225792 intervals across every ICU stay in the indexed hospitalization and observed readmission before day 28. Count each 24-hour period alive and without invasive ventilation; death by day 28 gives zero VFD. Discharge alive implies alive/unventilated unless a later MIMIC ICU ventilation interval or `patients.dod` contradicts it. Repeat a hospital-restricted endpoint because out-of-network ventilation is unavailable.

Secondary outcomes: successful liberation (48 continuous hours alive without invasive ventilation) by day 7; 28-day death; new RRT by day 7; ICU/hospital-free days; new vasopressor use, MAP<55, potassium<3.0, and KDIGO worsening by day 7. Documented 24/48h net balance and urine output are positive-control/process outcomes, not proof of benefit.

The imaging modifier is the latest chest report acquired in (t0-12h,t0] and available by t0 (`storetime<=t0`), classified by a frozen section/negation-aware rule as explicit pulmonary vascular/interstitial edema, isolated pleural effusion, neither, or no report. The primary effect does not require imaging. Treat interaction as exploratory unless two blinded clinicians adjudicate >=200 stratified reports, a third resolves disagreements, and edema PPV is >=0.80. Chest radiography cannot establish renal venous congestion.

## Analysis, baselines, and falsification

Report complete attrition and an unadjusted landmark comparison. Create person-hour clones through hour 24 and estimate inverse-probability-of-adherence/censoring weights with pooled logistic models using information available by each hour: age, sex, race, practice year, admission/ICU/service, prior-admission comorbidity, earlier loop exposure/dose, weight, cumulative/hourly inputs and outputs, KDIGO/creatinine trajectory, urine output, MAP/heart rate, lactate, potassium, FiO2, PEEP, oxygenation, vasopressor dose/duration/time since cessation, sedation, crystalloid/albumin, and result-available imaging. Do not use information after clone censoring or post-grace mediators in outcome models.

Estimate weighted VFD means and risk differences with subject-clustered robust intervals and 500 subject bootstraps. Report standardized differences, missingness, 1st/99th percentile weight truncation, strategy ESS, and adherence probabilities. Baselines are unadjusted landmark, conventional 12h propensity-overlap weighting, and primary clone-censor weighting.

Required sensitivities: never-treated new users; 6h/12h washouts; 30/100 mL/kg and continuous balance; stricter six-hour accounting; 6h/24h initiation; exclude cardiac surgery; heart-failure and baseline-KDIGO strata; exclude albumin co-initiation; eMAR/inputevents concordance; alternate ventilation/hospital-restricted outcomes; calendar era; quantitative bias analysis. As a temporal falsification diagnostic, test whether eventual strategy predicts already observed pre-t0 six-hour creatinine and urine-output slopes after the same adjustment. Material residual differences force an inconclusive causal branch. A process response without VFD benefit is mechanistic separation only.

Before outcomes, declare infeasible if either observed adherent strategy has <100 patients, weighted ESS<75, >10% have adherence probability <0.01, or exposure-source discordance >20%. The inherited source pass found 1,122 broad landmarks before adult/MAP/urine/RRT refinements, 212 initiating furosemide by 12h and 348 by 24h; 599/1,120 had a prior-12h radiology report. These are feasibility counts, not effect estimates, and did not apply this child's six-hour vasopressor washout, 12-hour loop washout, report-availability, or output-completeness rules. Revised attrition is mandatory and may make the experiment inconclusive.

Interpretation is mechanical:

- Supportive: VFD 95% CI excludes zero with point estimate >=+1 day; upper 95% bound for excess 28-day death <+3 points and for 7-day RRT <+5 points; no severe hypotension/electrolyte harm interval excludes zero; all feasibility, concordance, positivity, and falsification gates pass. Imaging support additionally needs PPV validation and interaction CI excluding zero in the prespecified direction.
- Adverse: VFD upper bound <=0, or mortality harm lower bound >=+3 points, or RRT harm lower bound >=+5 points. Favorable balance cannot rescue harm.
- Inconclusive/mixed: every other pattern, including VFD uncertainty, either safety margin not excluded, failed gates, discordant components, or unvalidated imaging. Nonsignificance is not equivalence or safety.

Support justifies external validation and a multicenter pragmatic trial, not standard care. Adverse findings apply only to this state/strategy. Recommendations require clinician review, indication/hemodynamic adjudication, protocolized volume assessment (ideally ultrasound/VExUS), external data, and preferably randomization.
