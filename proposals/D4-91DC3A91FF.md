## Proposed relationship and substantive advance

The unresolved claim is whether this divergence survives a stronger test of the leading rival: that apparent specificity reflects baseline severity, MRI-selection, measurement timing, or outcome ascertainment rather than distinct downstream risk phenotypes.

The substantive advance over the parent is an explicit selection/ascertainment contrast: the primary analysis uses a fixed second-assessment index and incident outcomes, while a prespecified inverse-probability-weighted or selection-stratified sensitivity asks whether the visceral-versus-MFI outcome-specific contrast remains in participants selected for MRI follow-up.
