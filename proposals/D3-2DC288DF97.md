## 3. Adjudication (parent's pre-specified rules, applied mechanically)

The paired CI spans the 0.2 log2FC floor and the Model-2 treatment variance component sits at the boundary: **inconclusive for the 0.2-level claim** — exactly as the pre-registered in-script rule returned. The result is nevertheless informative and bounded:

- **Bounded null extended to the non-LPS baseline at panel scale**: the 95% CI (-0.95, +0.70) excludes module-level effects >=1.10 log2FC (the panel MDE); the REML treatment-variance share is 0% (<= the parent's 5% supportive threshold), with all donor components interior and the optimizer converged. Adverse outcome (CI excluding 0 above floor) is **refuted at panel scale**.
- The magnitude asymmetry is mechanistically informative: macrophage LPS-state pretreatment moves the module ~2.85 log2FC in the same 5-pair-design family while acute MerTK inhibition moves it -0.13 (CI spanning zero). This is the panel-scale replication of the parent's E-MTAB-8316 quantitative dominance (LPS axis 1.64-1.81 log2FC vs null inhibitor axis) and further supports the redundant-circuit reading [K1] over the single-arm MerTK-instructs-stroma reading [K2].

## 4. Unresolved claim (narrowed)

Whether acute MerTK-pathway inhibition shifts FLS inflammatory output in the **0.2-0.73 log2FC range at the non-LPS baseline** remains open: the n=5 panel design has module MDE 1.10 and per-gene median MDE 0.73. At this within-pair SD, MDE=0.45 needs ~30 donor pairs and the parent's 0.2 floor needs ~150 (MDE ~ 1/sqrt(n)); the realistic next stage is deeper (RNA-seq) readout with donor pairing, not more panel wells. Escape hatches (b) sparse per-gene effects <0.73 and (c) unmeasured UNC106238 target engagement remain wet-lab-only.

## 5. Competing explanations and limitations

Donor heterogeneity (one Mpos pair, SA259A, carries -1.07; the other four are |d|<=0.75); mixed-well pseudobulk attribution (whole-well targeted assay; no cell-of-origin deconvolution); UNC106238 off-target risk; unmeasured MerTK target engagement; the excluded ambiguous well; marker-based (MerTK/CD206) subset definition [K3]; cross-sectional coculture (no longitudinal transition claims). No wet-lab validation. The targeted panel does not measure absent genes (no NFKBIA, PTGS2, HBEGF, IL1A).
