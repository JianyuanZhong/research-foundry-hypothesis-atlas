# Mechanism-specificity test of the post-resection adjuvant-TACE effect in HCC: is the MVI-concentrated benefit specific to microscopic residual disease, or a generic high-risk / surveillance artefact?

Exactly **ONE** new decision-changing element is added (Section 4). Status: design + this-episode bounded

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance

After curative-intent hepatectomy for HCC, postoperative adjuvant TACE (PA-TACE) is widely used in Chinese practice
for "high-risk" patients — most often patients with **microvascular invasion (MVI)** — on the rationale of eradicating
**microscopic residual disease**. The decision it drives is concrete: *should an MVI+ resected patient receive
adjuvant TACE?* Its importance is sharpened by negative systemic-adjuvant evidence: the phase III KEYNOTE-937 trial
failed to show recurrence-free survival benefit and current evidence does not support adjuvant therapy after curative
treatment in early-stage HCC ([K2]); across phase III trials adjuvant approaches show limited/inconsistent benefit
and the central open problem is patient selection without validated predictive biomarkers ([K3]); and PA-TACE
benefit is argued to be heterogeneous/identifiable with microvascular infiltration entering a prognostic model ([K1]).

The **still-unresolved** question — not answered by [K1]–[K3] and not resolved by any prior board branch — is not
"is PA-TACE associated with less recurrence?", but: **is the PA-TACE effect specific to the mechanistic target
(microscopic residual disease / MVI), or does the "MVI-concentrated benefit" merely track a generic high-risk label
and post-operative surveillance intensity?** These have different clinical consequences: a mechanism-specific effect
supports using MVI to select PA-TACE; a non-specific effect argues against using MVI alone as the selector.

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival (pre-specified)

- **H_effect (leading, mechanistic):** PA-TACE reduces early recurrence in resected HCC and the benefit is
  **specific** to microscopic residual disease — greatest in MVI+ / macrovascular-invasion-negative / R0-resected
  patients, attenuated where residual disease is macroscopic (macrovascular invasion / portal-vein tumour thrombus),
  and attenuated where residual disease remains at the cut surface (positive margin).
- **R_generic (strongest rival, selection + surveillance):** any crude association is *confounding by indication +
  surveillance/detection intensity*: higher-risk but fitter patients are both more likely to receive PA-TACE and to be
  monitored more intensively, so the "benefit" appears **non-specifically** across all high-risk strata and tracks
  overall risk rather than the microscopic-residual-disease mechanism.
- **R_null:** no causal effect; the association is explained by measured confounding plus monitoring intensity.

H_effect and R_generic are **separated by a signed, specificity prediction**, not just by adjustment: H_effect predicts
a protective PA-TACE × MVI interaction that is **not** matched by a protective interaction with macrovascular invasion
or margin; R_generic predicts comparable protective interactions across all high-risk strata, or an MVI interaction that
vanishes once global risk / surveillance intensity is included. R_conf, the parent's rival, is already attacked by the
parent's dose and timing gradients; this element attacks the **specificity** of the mechanism claim, a distinct failure
mode that dose/timing gradients cannot exclude.

## 3. Inherited frozen design (verbatim in the parent; condensed here)

- **Population:** HCC diagnosis (`diagnoses`) AND ≥1 curative-intent hepatectomy procedure (`procedures.手术` label
  family: 肝叶切除 / 肝叶部分切除 / 腹腔镜下肝部分切除 / 特殊肝段切除 / 半肝 / 段切除).
- **Index** = earliest qualifying resection `开始时间`. **Landmark** t0 = index + 90 d.
- **Exposure:** adjuvant TACE = a TACE/embolization procedure (`手术` matching 栓塞|TACE|化疗栓塞|介入) with start
  date in `(index, index+90d]`; comparator = no such TACE in the window. Immortal time removed by ascertaining
  exposure only inside the landmark window. Late/other TACE is not the exposure.
- **Modifier (inherited):** MVI from pathology text (`病理`/`检查所见`/`检查诊断`).
- **Outcome:** early recurrence (≤24 months post-landmark) via the branch's lexical imaging endpoint on `examinations`
  (`检查所见`/`检查诊断` containing 复发|新发|进展|转移|增多), death as a competing event; censor at last contact
  (`出院时间`/last record). Endpoint requires radiologist mRECIST adjudication (stated limitation).
- **M0 baseline:** single-cohort Cox/logistic recurrence ~ PA-TACE + MVI + PA-TACE×MVI + era/age/ALBI/AFP.
- **M1 alternative:** target-trial emulation — same landmark plus propensity/overlap weighting for PA-TACE indication,
  a negative-control outcome, and imaging-cadence (monitoring-intensity)/IOO adjustment; temporal split + 5-fold CV;
  adjusted HRs with 95% CI, interaction LRT, C-index/AUROC with calibration, decision-curve net-benefit.
- **Kept parent elements:** (a) within-window TACE-session **dose-rate gradient** + E-value; (b) **timing/initiation
  gradient** T60 (early ≤60 d vs 60–90 d) entered jointly with the session term with a VIF/collinearity pre-check,
  plus a pre-committed negative-control retraction rule.

## 4. THE ONE ADDED ELEMENT: a pre-specified mechanism-**specificity** modifier panel

Compute, on the M1 (target-trial-emulation, overlap-weighted) model, three additional pre-specified effect modifiers
besides the inherited MVI term, each interacted with PA-TACE, all from `pathology` free text with mandatory look-alike
control and an extraction-agreement audit:

1. **MVI** (inherited): microscopic residual disease marker.
2. **Macrovascular invasion / portal-vein tumour thrombus (PVTT)** — macroscopic residual disease marker
   (`检查所见`/`检查诊断`/`病理` containing 门静脉|癌栓 within an invasion context; 门静脉 and 癌栓 are present).
3. **Resection-margin status** (R1 vs R0) — residual disease at the cut surface (`切缘` context: 阳性/累及/近切缘).
4. **Satellite nodule** (卫星灶) — intra-hepatic dissemination marker (secondary, exploratory-only, FDR-controlled).

**Targeted estimand:** the PA-TACE × modifier log-hazard-ratio for each modifier, adjusted (M1 weighting) and
FDR-controlled (Benjamini–Hochberg across the 3 confirmatory interactions; the parent's two gradients are separate,
pre-existing tests), plus a joint heterogeneity test of H0: equal PA-TACE effect across modifier strata.

**Specificity decision (why it is discriminating):** H_effect requires the protective interaction to be carried by
MVI (microscopic) and **not** reproduced by macrovascular invasion (macroscopic) or margin (R1). R_generic predicts the
protective interaction is present with comparable magnitude across all high-risk strata, or an MVI effect that is
explained by a global-risk composite / surveillance-intensity term. A pre-specified **risk-adjusted re-test** adds a
baseline-risk composite (age, ALBI, AFP, tumour-burden proxy, era) and a post-landmark imaging-cadence term; the MVI
interaction must survive these for a mechanistic read.

**Measurement-validity sub-check (pre-specified):** because all four modifiers are lexical, require an internal
agreement audit — a random sample of pathology notes manually re-coded by the solver's keyword rules against an
independent second rule set, requiring Cohen's κ ≥ 0.6 (else the primary specificity test is declared **inconclusive**),
and cross-field agreement between `病理` and `检查所见`/`检查诊断`.

## 5. Baselines, alternative methods, and what each recovers

- **M0 (baseline, parent):** single-cohort Cox with a *single* MVI interaction → recovers only whether the binary
  high-risk label is modified; cannot separate a specific mechanism from generic selection.
- **M1 / M6 (added structured alternative):** overlap-weighted target-trial emulation with the **4-modifier
  specificity panel** + global-risk and surveillance re-tests → recovers *whether the effect tracks microscopic
  residual disease specifically*, which M0 structurally loses.
- **Learned alternative (secondary, revisit-conditioned):** a causal-forest / gradient-boosted conditional
  average treatment effect (CATE) model on the same modifiers and covariates (CPU, minutes-scale), evaluated by
  held-out *CATE* calibration and by whether it recovers modifier structure the Cox panel already identifies. This is
  included to test a substantive uncertainty — whether pre-specified linear interactions miss nonlinear PA-TACE-effect
  heterogeneity — not to chase a small predictive gain. **Deferral reason:** if the structured panel already yields a
  decisive specificity result, the learned CATE model adds little; revisit only if the panel is null but non-linear
  heterogeneity is plausible. Exact inputs, split and target are identical to M1 (same cohort, same t0, same endpoint,
  held-out temporal split), so the comparison is fair.

## 7. Falsification criteria (pre-committed; conjunctive)

- **Supportive (mechanism-specific):** adjusted overlap-weighted PA-TACE × MVI interaction HR < 1 with 95% CI
  excluding 1 and ≥30 events in the MVI stratum; macrovascular-invasion interaction NOT protective (CI includes 1 or
  HR > 1); margin interaction null; MVI interaction persists after the global-risk + surveillance re-test; joint
  heterogeneity test does not attribute the effect equally to all strata; extraction κ ≥ 0.6; negative-control
  outcome clean.
- **Adverse = R_generic / non-specific:** comparable protective interactions across MVI, macrovascular invasion and
  margin (joint heterogeneity p > 0.10 among the three effects); **or** the MVI interaction attenuates to the null
  (CI includes 1) after adding the global-risk composite / imaging-cadence term → the "MVI-concentrated benefit" is a
  generic high-risk/selection-surveillance artefact.
- **Adverse = detection/surveillance:** the PA-TACE effect tracks post-landmark imaging cadence (examination counts)
  rather than the pathology modifier.
- **Inconclusive:** any confirmatory stratum < 30 events; extraction κ < 0.6; undated pathology; or (trivially) no
  PA-TACE-exposed MVI+ patients. `门静脉`-sparsity → macrovascular interaction inconclusive (secondary, does not by
  itself overturn a clean MVI-specific read).
- **Precise-null:** all three confirmatory interactions within ±0.10 of 0 on the log-HR scale with tight CIs → no
  evidence of modifier-specific benefit, even if a main effect exists → MVI is not a valid PA-TACE selector.
- **Pre-committed retraction rule (inherited):** if the negative-control outcome shows a non-null PA-TACE "effect",
  all supportive reads retract.

## 8. What each outcome would and would NOT establish

- **Supportive** would support a *residual-microscopic-disease mechanism* and inform using MVI (not the generic
  high-risk label) to select PA-TACE; it would **not** establish causality or a survival/PFS benefit (no mortality or
  mRECIST PFS is ascertainable here), and would still require radiologist mRECIST adjudication and expert review.
- **Adverse (R_generic)** would argue against MVI-specific biologic targeting and against MVI alone as the selector;
  it would not prove PA-TACE is ineffective.
- **Inconclusive** → need a larger/external cohort or adjudicated outcomes; an imprecise null does not refute H_effect.
- The analysis can be **computationally checked** (correct cohort, extraction, model fitting, CI/heterogeneity
  computation) but the *clinical* interpretation (mechanism, treatment recommendation) requires clinical adjudication,
  missing evidence (mortality/PFS, external validation) or another study; an automatic verifier cannot establish it.
