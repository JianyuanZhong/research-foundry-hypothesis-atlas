# Creatinine–cystatin C discordance, low muscle reserve, and recorded kidney events: an executable UK Biobank successor

## Unresolved question and supported evidence

Creatinine-based eGFR is influenced by creatinine generation, while cystatin C is less directly dependent on muscle mass but can be affected by nonrenal factors. The unresolved clinical question is whether a lower cystatin-C-based estimate relative to a creatinine-based estimate identifies prospective renal vulnerability among people whose low grip strength suggests reduced muscle reserve. If so, this could identify when a cystatin-C measurement adds clinically relevant risk information to creatinine-only assessment.

The strongest available evidence is narrower. The locally inspected readable Haines et al. (CJASN 2023) article studied 38 mechanically ventilated ICU patients with serial creatinine, cystatin C, measured GFR and muscle ultrasound; it supports a critical-illness physiological concern about creatinine-based overestimation during muscle loss. It does not establish a community UK Biobank association, an interaction with grip strength, AKI severity, measured filtration accuracy in UKB, or benefit from cystatin-C testing.

## Hypothesis and estimand

Among UK Biobank participants aged 40–69 at recruitment with valid baseline creatinine, cystatin C, bilateral grip, BMI, age, sex and assessment date, and without a previously recorded hospital N17 or N18 diagnosis, a lower discordance (D=eGFRcys-eGFRcr) is associated with a larger risk of a first subsequently recorded hospital N17 diagnosis at lower baseline grip strength, after accounting for eGFRcr, age, sex and BMI.

The primary model parameter is the coefficient of standardized (D 	imes G) in a cause-specific Cox model for first post-index recorded N17, where (G) is mean bilateral grip standardized within sex. The clinically interpretable primary estimand is a model-standardized five-year risk difference-in-differences:

[
Psi = [R_5(D=-1,G=-1)-R_5(D=0,G=-1)]
      -[R_5(D=-1,G=+1)-R_5(D=0,G=+1)].
]

Here (R_5) is the cumulative incidence of a first recorded N17 by five years, obtained from the AKI and death cause-specific hazards; age, sex, BMI and eGFRcr are averaged over their observed eligible-cohort distribution. Values are in standardized exposure units and are predictions under the fitted prognostic model, not causal interventions. The prespecified direction is (Psi>0): moving toward lower D should add more risk at low grip than at high grip. Report the interaction hazard ratio, (Psi) with a 95% bootstrap interval, and the four component risks. A clinically meaningful lower-bound criterion is a positive 95% interval for (Psi); if event frequency makes a minimally important absolute difference clinically defensible after the audit, state that threshold before fitting and report it separately. Do not invent a threshold from the observed result.

The first secondary outcome is a first post-index recorded hospital N18/N18.x diagnosis, using the same cohort and temporal rules. It is labelled a recorded hospital CKD diagnosis, not biological incident CKD. The descriptive contrast of eGFRcr ≥60 and eGFRcys <60 versus concordant estimates is reported only if all cells have adequate support and never replaces the continuous estimand.

## Baselines and nonlinear alternative

The transparent primary comparison uses nested cause-specific Cox models:

1. eGFRcr + age + sex + BMI + grip;
2. model 1 + D;
3. model 2 + D×grip.

The interaction model is prespecified and is not replaced after inspecting results. Report robust participant-level uncertainty, model diagnostics, risk-set support, influence diagnostics, five-year standardized risks and (Psi). Restricted cubic splines for D and grip are a sensitivity functional-form analysis.

The same-input nonlinear alternative is a shallow gradient-boosted discrete-time competing-risk hazard model. Use exactly eGFRcr_z, D_z, grip_z, age, sex and BMI—no outcome-derived, treatment, utilization, imaging or extra laboratory variables. Create one-year intervals through five years and fit separate AKI/death hazards; remove participants after the first event. Use depth 2–3 trees, fixed minimum leaf size and regularization, with iteration count selected only on validation.

Split by participant `eid), frozen seed, 60% train/20% validation/20% test. Learn transformations, tuning and any imputation in training only. Fit Cox and boosted models on the same splits. On untouched test data report five-year IPCW Brier score, time-dependent AUC, calibration slope/intercept and plots, participant bootstrap intervals, and the prespecified D-by-grip absolute-risk surface. Summarize a threshold/non-additive region only if stable across bootstrap resamples and supported by adequate cells. A metric improvement alone is not a scientific advance; the alternative matters only if it reveals a stable clinically interpretable departure from the transparent interaction model. Budget: at most 4 CPU, 8 GiB RAM and 2 hours including bounded uncertainty. Defer it for too few events, inadequate interval support, failed calibration or unstable surfaces.

## Falsification, sensitivity and interpretation

Prespecify these diagnostics before fitting:

- a two-year landmark: exclude participants with recorded N17/N18 through two years and begin follow-up at two years; persistence reduces concern about occult baseline disease/early reverse causation but changes the population;
- strict index-date and same-day-death alternatives;
- N18 secondary outcome and raw-biomarker sensitivity;
- a nonrenal hospital negative control using K35 only after its Showcase code semantics and event support are verified; a comparable D-by-grip signal weakens renal specificity but does not prove confounding;
- permutation of D within sex-by-age strata using a frozen seed; a surviving signal is a leakage/pipeline warning, not evidence;
- missingness, inclusion selection and eGFRcr/eGFRcys cell-support audit.

Supportive evidence requires verified prerequisites, adequate paired events and risk-set support, a positive prespecified (Psi) with a 95% interval excluding zero, consistency in the strict-timing and two-year-landmark analyses, and no comparably strong negative-control association. A stable nonlinear surface may clarify where the association operates but is not required for support.

Adverse evidence is a sufficiently precise null or opposite (Psi), no incremental signal after eGFRcr and grip, dependence on early/same-day records, a comparable negative-control signal, or an unstable nonlinear surface. These are useful negative findings. Inconclusive evidence includes failure to verify units, baseline biomarker timing, code semantics, death-date meaning or administrative end; too few events, substantial code/date discordance, unsupported cells, poor calibration or nonproportional hazards that invalidate the prespecified interpretation. Inconclusive results do not support a clinical conclusion.

The study can establish only a prognostic association with recorded hospital codes in a selected UKB biomarker/assessment subgroup, and computationally checkable model performance and risk contrasts. It cannot establish measured filtration accuracy, causal muscle mediation, treatment benefit, clinical utility, outpatient disease absence, AKI severity/KDIGO stage, or transportability. Those require clinical adjudication with longitudinal creatinine/urine output, measured GFR or an external reference, richer muscle/inflammation and treatment data, independent validation, expert review, and a prospective decision-impact study.
