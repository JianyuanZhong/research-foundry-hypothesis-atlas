# Calibrated falsification of nodule-specific discharge-boundary delay

## Decision question and substantive advance

The actionable question is not whether many pulmonary-nodule discharge summaries have `storetime > dischtime`. That fact can arise from ordinary completion/storage latency in every discharge workflow. The falsifiable question is whether the nodule-opportunity process has excess delayed storage or content discordance beyond comparable discharge documentation, and whether that excess persists at boundaries that are less vulnerable to routine end-of-stay latency.

The strongest evidence already supported by the parent is narrower: a frozen all-opportunity decomposition can separate faithful, incomplete/omitted, alternate-resolution, delayed-storage, and unknown discharge states, while later MIMIC traces can separate same-system CT, death, continued observation, and loss of capture. The parent does not establish that `storetime` means physical departure-time visibility, signature/finalization, communication, or patient receipt. The proposed child tests the unresolved claim that an apparent discharge-boundary A state is nodule-specific and sufficiently stable, relative to ordinary workflow, to justify prioritizing a pre-discharge availability intervention.

The substantive advance is an internal calibration and falsification layer, not a redefinition of the clinical opportunity or of A. Raw A remains reported over the parent’s all-opportunity denominator. A separate calibrated estimand compares that raw process with prespecified internal controls at four locked horizons. Mechanism prioritization is prohibited if timestamp semantics, overlap, calibration precision, or stability are inadequate.

## Population and frozen index opportunity

The index population is the parent’s first eligible recommendation admission per subject. Include adults, live at discharge, non-hospice/comfort-only disposition, and admissions with a discharge time. Define age using `anchor_age + year(admittime)-anchor_year`, with the deidentified 91 representation treated as 90+.

Find candidate reports in `note/radiology` and reconstruct base/addendum chains only through reciprocal, subject- and admission-consistent links in `note/radiology_detail`. Require a CT/CTA chest or explicitly supported lung-base coverage linked by `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, with report `charttime` in `admittime-12 hours` through `dischtime`. Require a patient-specific, unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months after an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity. Exclude screening, already ongoing surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, explicit no-follow-up, optional/conditional recommendations, hospice/comfort-only transitions, and in-hospital death.

At primary boundary D, a recommendation component is pre-discharge knowledge only if `charttime <= D` and `storetime <= D`. If event timing is uncertain, contradictory, or linkage is unresolved, assign `U_R`; never convert it into an opportunity, omission, or delay. Two independent radiology-trained reviewers adjudicate eligibility, incidental status, unconditionality, interval, CT coverage, addendum meaning, and timestamp uncertainty while blinded to discharge text/timing and later outcomes. Select the first eligible admission only after adjudication, with deterministic tie-breaking by base charttime, storetime, and note_id.

Freeze N after this adjudication and before reading canonical discharge content/timing, assigning discharge states, or inspecting later CT/death/trace. Preserve the complete cascade: lexical candidates, metadata-linked candidates, exclusions, late recommendations, U_R, and final N. No calibration control may be allowed to alter N.

## Primary all-opportunity discharge states

Use the sole discharge row for each `(subject_id,hadm_id)` in this snapshot as the canonical artifact. If a refresh contains multiple rows for a key, stop and create a new locked version rule. Preserve the parent’s mutually exclusive, exhaustive states over all N:

* `F`: canonical discharge text stored by D (`storetime <= D`), classifiable, and faithful to the adjudicated patient-specific chest/thoracic CT action and exact interval/date.
* `C_O`: stored by D and classifiable, but omits the patient-specific actionable plan.
* `C_I`: stored by D but incomplete or discordant in nodule/action/timing, without an explicit alternate resolution.
* `R`: stored by D with a documented alternate resolution such as refusal/preference, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, or scheduled outside follow-up. Assess documentation, not appropriateness.
* `A`: canonical artifact exists with known `storetime > D`. A is a storage-delay signal only, never proof of unavailable visibility, communication failure, or failed finalization.
* `U_D`: missing row, missing storetime, unreadable/contradictory linkage, or unclassifiable text.

Report the full multinomial vector over all N, including `p_A`, `p_C=p_C_O+p_C_I`, `p_F`, `p_R`, and `p_U_D`. Preserve stale-artifact chronology: if the discharge text was stored before the recommendation became available, code it stale and do not blame it for omission; if stored after D, it remains A regardless of later faithfulness. `charttime` cannot substitute for `storetime`.

At `D+24h`, `D+72h`, and `D+168h`, report transitions from the original A state to later F/C/R as descriptive delayed-artifact matrices. Do not retroactively change the primary A state. The boundaries are locked before results are viewed: H = 0, 24, 72, and 168 hours.

## Calibration controls and estimands

Construct controls only after applying the same adult/live/non-hospice/discharge-summary eligibility to admissions. The primary ordinary-control pool is admissions with a canonical discharge row, excluding admissions linked to broad pulmonary-nodule/action retrieval and broad nonpulmonary incidental/action retrieval. A sensitivity control is broad nonpulmonary incidental/action admissions, excluding pulmonary-nodule opportunities; this is a content/opportunity control rather than a generic workflow control. A second sensitivity uses ordinary control admissions from the same subjects as nodule opportunities, when available, but does not replace the population-level control because recurrent subjects are selected and may have different care contexts.

Broad retrieval screens are sensitivity-oriented and are not clinical truth. At minimum, the retrieval audit uses pulmonary/lung plus nodule/nodular/opacity, actionable recommendation/follow-up/surveillance/repeat, chest/CT, and 3/6/12-month language; the nonpulmonary screen uses imaging plus adrenal/renal/hepatic/pancreatic/thyroid/ovarian lesion/nodule/mass/cyst and actionable interval language. Final content-control status requires the same blinded clinical adjudication framework for a patient-specific unconditional action opportunity. If that adjudication is unavailable, the nonpulmonary contrast is labeled a feasibility screen and cannot support a content-mechanism claim.

At each H, define `Y_H=1(storetime > D+H)` for a known storetime, and retain missing storetime as unknown rather than silently treating it as timely. The primary calibration estimand is the standardized risk difference

`Delta_H = P(Y_H=1 | nodule opportunity) - P(Y_H=1 | ordinary control)`.

Report a secondary `nodule minus nonpulmonary-action` contrast. These are process contrasts, not effects of an intervention. For content, define a separate estimand among adjudicated action opportunities whose canonical artifact is stored by the same horizon: risk of C_O/C_I versus F/R, with the same horizon-specific inclusion rule disclosed. Do not infer omission from ordinary summaries that had no source recommendation.

Standardize the control risk to the nodule-opportunity distribution over prespecified, pre-discharge variables: `anchor_year_group`, admission type (elective/urgent/acute), discharge disposition group (home/postacute/other; hospice/death excluded), terminal recorded service, ICU exposure from `icu/icustays`, and LOS bin (`<=1`, `1-3`, `3-7`, `7-14`, `>14` days). Terminal service is the last `services.transfertime` row at or before D; any service row after D is retained as a data-quality diagnostic and cannot define a pre-discharge covariate.

Primary implementation is exact-cell standardization when each nodule cell has at least five controls. Estimate the nodule-cell weighted control risk and Delta at all four H. If exact support is insufficient, use coarser prespecified cells omitting terminal service, but only as a labeled sensitivity. A regression residualization sensitivity may use the same covariates and interactions, subject-clustered bootstrap intervals, and only adequate event counts and positivity. It cannot rescue unsupported extrapolation. Nearest-neighbor matching is allowed only within represented-era, admission-type, disposition, ICU, and LOS strata with replacement and a prespecified caliper; report effective sample size and unmatched fraction.

Difference-in-differences is not primary and must not be used merely because there are multiple horizons. It is permitted only if a genuine repeated pre/post contrast is defined using a pre-index discharge opportunity and an index opportunity, with treatment status determined before the post period and no post-index conditioning. If these conditions are not met, DiD is explicitly omitted; comparing H=0 with H=24/72/168 is a horizon analysis, not DiD.

## Calibration validity, overlap, uncertainty, and gates

Before estimating Delta, report cell counts, nodule/control support, standardized mean differences, maximum control weight, effective sample size, and the proportion of N represented by supported cells. Positivity fails if any prespecified essential stratum has no controls, if exact support covers less than 80% of N, if the looser sensitivity covers less than 90%, if maximum standardized weight exceeds 10, or if effective sample size is below 100. These are design gates, not clinical quality thresholds. The same thresholds are declared before looking at the contrast.

Use cluster bootstrap by subject for repeated admissions, with resampling of subjects rather than rows. Compute simultaneous 95% confidence bands for the four Delta_H values, using a max-statistic or joint bootstrap. Report Wilson/exact intervals for unadjusted risks and a sensitivity partial-identification interval that assigns missing storetime first to timely and then to delayed. For content, report reviewer raw agreement and kappa or AC1; failed agreement blocks mechanism selection.

Primary calibration support requires all of the following:

1. Storetime semantics remain explicitly unresolved, but the source audit shows valid parses, no unexplained time inversion pattern, and a prespecified charttime-versus-storetime diagnostic. If storage metadata cannot be distinguished from a generic extraction artifact, stop.
2. At least 200 adjudicated nodule opportunities and at least 20 known delayed and 20 known content-failure events where the corresponding estimand is attempted; otherwise descriptive feasibility only.
3. Overlap and effective-sample-size gates pass for the primary standardization and do not materially fail in the prespecified looser and same-subject sensitivities.
4. The simultaneous 95% lower bound for Delta_H exceeds +0.05 at H=24, 72, or 168 hours, or the pattern is stable across at least two post-discharge horizons. A departure-only excess is insufficient to prioritize availability.
5. The result is directionally stable under exact standardization, looser standardization/regression, and the same-subject sensitivity, with no reversal caused by timestamp or unknown-state bounds.
6. The nodule excess is not matched by the broad nonpulmonary-action control, and is not explained by a generic charttime/storetime artifact affecting all discharge workflows.
7. If a content mechanism is proposed, the content contrast must exceed the adjudicated nonpulmonary action-opportunity contrast; generic ordinary controls cannot establish content specificity.

If the lower bound for Delta_H is below +0.05 at all horizons, or the upper bound for the nodule-minus-control contrast is within the prespecified decision margin, do not call storage delay nodule-specific. If Delta is positive only at H=0 and disappears by H=24, interpret it as compatible with ordinary end-of-stay documentation latency. If controls have a comparable or larger delay, the availability-first mechanism is falsified for this dataset. If content discordance alone remains excess against analogous action controls, content reconciliation may be considered, still noncausally. If neither timing nor content excess survives calibration, redirect the research question toward tracking, scheduling, communication, or cross-system capture without selecting among those mechanisms from MIMIC.

## Secondary observation outcomes and competing states

For every N, retain the parent’s all-denominator outcome. Due date is index report charttime plus 3, 6, or 12 calendar months, with a fixed 42-day window. Starting at D, classify mutually exclusively: (1) same-system timely chest CT/CTA before an interval-certain death; (2) death before the window ends with no prior timely CT; (3) no timely CT/death but a later MIMIC radiology or admission trace; (4) no later trace, outside-care/loss-of-capture unknown; (5) CT/death ordering indeterminate when intervals overlap. Use `dod` as `[dod 00:00,dod+1 day)` unless a more precise death time exists. Report an artifact-timed sensitivity beginning at `max(D, DS.storetime)` without changing N. Do not equate state 3 with adherence or state 4 with nonadherence, and do not condition the process calibration on later observation.

## Falsification, interpretation, and unavailable evidence

Supportive results mean only that an excess nodule-associated storage/content phenotype survives the locked internal controls and horizons sufficiently to justify a prospective timestamp-crosswalk and workflow study. They do not show that an intervention improves communication or surveillance. Adverse results mean ordinary controls explain the A state, timing excess disappears after 24 hours, content does not exceed analogous opportunities, or alternate resolutions/unknownness dominate; this falsifies an availability-first nodule mechanism and redirects priorities. Inconclusive results include sparse adjudication, unresolved reciprocal chains, missing timing, failed agreement, poor overlap, unstable estimates, confidence bounds crossing the margin, or a generic charttime/storetime artifact.

Automatic verification may check source hashes, archive members, complete row counts, joins, reciprocal links, frozen-N provenance, state exhaustiveness, horizon arithmetic, standardization weights, overlap, clustered resampling, uncertainty calculations, and whether conclusions match computed outputs. It cannot establish physical departure, EHR visibility, signature/finalization, clinician awareness, handoff conversation, notification/transmission, patient receipt, recommendation appropriateness without image review and missing clinical context, referral/order/appointment completion, outside surveillance, malignancy, radiation harm, survival, adherence, causality, or patient benefit. Those claims require timestamp semantics validation, expert adjudication, prospective workflow observation, linkage to external care, and/or another clinical study.
