# Recommendation-to-artifact content opportunity, not recommendation-to-discharge lead, in pulmonary-nodule handoff

## Decision question, strongest supported evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

The clinical design decision is whether a future pulmonary-nodule handoff intervention should primarily (1) make a discharge artifact available before departure, (2) give a canonical discharge artifact enough observable time after a radiology recommendation is stored and reconcile its content, (3) repair a generic cross-action discharge-content process, or (4) move downstream to tracking, exchange, and communication. These are different targets and MIMIC-IV can distinguish only storage-and-text phenotypes, not the human mechanisms themselves.

The strongest evidence already supported is narrow. A complete, unsampled workflow audit of this MIMIC snapshot found 331,794 unique `DS` admission keys, 17 missing discharge `storetime` values, and substantial ordinary discharge storage latency; it also found a retrieval-only pool of 3,764 possible pulmonary recommendation reports. The assessed parent correctly freezes every adjudicated opportunity before looking at discharge timing/content, reconstructs reciprocal report/addendum chains, keeps `F/C_O/C_I/R/A/U_D` exhaustive, calibrates delayed storage against ordinary discharge workflow, and treats `storetime` as database storage rather than visibility, signature, communication, receipt, adherence, or benefit.

The parent’s unresolved content-clock flaw is consequential. Its primary content comparison uses `D - R_store`, the time from recommendation storage to discharge. That is not the observable interval in which the canonical discharge artifact could incorporate the recommendation. If the canonical `DS` was stored immediately after, or even before, the recommendation, a recommendation may have 72 hours of “lead before discharge” but the artifact had little or no positive content opportunity. Conversely, a `DS` stored after discharge can have a long recommendation-to-artifact gap but was not an observed departure-time artifact. The parent also requires discordance to be at least five percentage points higher in an ample than a near recommendation-to-discharge stratum. Clinically important discordance that is uniformly high at every content-opportunity duration would therefore fail its persistence rule.

This child tests the following unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis:

> Among frozen pulmonary-nodule opportunities whose canonical discharge artifact was stored by discharge and at least 24 hours after the recommendation-bearing radiology component was stored, the conservative all-state probability of omitted or interval-incomplete/discordant nodule action remains above a locked 10% operational margin. Whether this persistence is nodule-specific is tested separately by the excess over blinded, same-clock, nonthoracic incidental-imaging recommendations with analogous explicit actions and intervals.

The content hypothesis no longer requires ample-gap discordance to exceed near-gap discordance. A uniformly high gap profile is a positive finding under the absolute criterion. The analogous-action calibration determines whether the phenotype is nodule-specific or a generic discharge-content problem. A separate availability estimand retains the inherited comparison of `DS_store > D` with ordinary institutional latency. None of these descriptive estimands establishes that a report was visible, an author had read it, a document was signed or sent, a patient received a plan, follow-up was appropriate or completed, or an intervention would improve outcomes.

## The repaired content-opportunity clock and all-N joint state

For each frozen opportunity define:

* `R_store`: `storetime` of the adjudicated recommendation-bearing report/addendum component;
* `DS_store`: `storetime` of the unique canonical `DS`;
* `G = DS_store - R_store`, in hours: the only primary observed content-opportunity clock;
* `V`, the canonical artifact storage state relative to departure: `V_D` if `DS_store <= D`; `V_24` if `D < DS_store <= D+24h`; `V_72` if `D+24h < DS_store <= D+72h`; `V_168` if `D+72h < DS_store <= D+168h`; `V_late` if `DS_store > D+168h`; and `V_U` if unresolved;
* `H`, the recommendation-to-artifact gap state when both storage times are known: `H_stale` if `G < 0`; `H_0_6` if `0 <= G < 6h`; `H_6_24` if `6 <= G < 24h`; `H_24_72` if `24 <= G < 72h`; `H_72plus` if `G >= 72h`; and `H_U` otherwise;
* `Z`, the eventual canonical artifact text state `F_text, C_O_text, C_I_text, R_text, U_text`, using the same blinded content definitions regardless of storage time. `Z` is descriptive for delayed artifacts and cannot overwrite `Y=A` at departure.

The primary data object is the complete joint all-`N` table `J_N = count(V,H,Z)/N`, accompanied by the inherited `Y` table. It must include zero cells and unknown cells. This table makes three chronology distinctions that the parent’s recommendation-to-discharge lead could not make:

1. `V_D,H_stale`: the artifact was observed stored by discharge but before the recommendation, so there was no positive observed incorporation window;
2. `V_D,H_0_6` through `H_72plus`: the artifact was stored by departure after the recommendation, with the actual positive storage-to-storage content opportunity measured by `G`;
3. `V_24/V_72/V_168/V_late`: the eventual artifact may have a positive and even long `G`, but it was delayed at the departure boundary and remains `A` in the primary state.

The primary ample, timely content-opportunity stratum is locked as

`E_N = {V = V_D and H in (H_24_72, H_72plus)}`.

The 24-hour threshold is an operational decision margin, not a proven human editing threshold. `H_72plus` is displayed separately and is a prespecified stricter sensitivity. Near-gap strata are reported but are not the reference required to establish persistence. `D - R_store` is retained only as a descriptive decomposition satisfying `D - R_store = (D - DS_store) + G`; it cannot define a content opportunity stratum or support a mechanism conclusion.

Within every `V_D,H` stratum, report all five `Z` categories, not only classifiable cases. Define conservative discordance as

`p_C,N(h) = [n(C_O_text)+n(C_I_text)] / n(V_D,H=h)`,

so `R_text` and `U_text` remain in the denominator rather than being deleted. Also report multinomial proportions for `F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text` and the all-opportunity joint burden `j_C,N(h)=n(V_D,H=h,C)/N`. For `E_N`, combine the two ample bins by the same rule to obtain `p_C,N(E)`. A complementary upper content bound assigns `U_text` to `C`; membership bounds additionally assign unresolved `DS_store`/`R_store`/chain states to or away from `E` in all logically compatible ways. A supportive content conclusion must survive the conservative lower-compatible assignment; unknowns can prevent support but cannot manufacture it.

`H_stale` is never included in a positive-gap content-failure estimand. It remains visible in `J_N`, and its `Z` distribution is reported as stale text concordance/discordance only. Delayed `V` strata retain eventual `Z` to describe transition patterns at +24h, +72h, and +168h, but no delayed artifact enters `E_N` and eventual fidelity cannot be called a departure-time handoff.

## Prespecified estimands and decision rules

All thresholds are locked before adjudicated outcomes are computed. They are operational intervention-prioritization margins, not validated clinical safety thresholds.

### 1. Complete all-opportunity state

Publish `J_N=count(V,H,Z)/N`, `Y=count(F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D)/N`, and their deterministic crosswalk. Also publish `J_X` over `N_X`. Every conditional estimate must be accompanied by its all-denominator joint numerator and all unknown/stale/delayed counts.

### 2. Persistent pulmonary content discordance

The primary absolute estimand is `p_C,N(E)` in timely artifacts with a positive gap of at least 24 hours. Persistence is **supportive** when the simultaneous 95% lower confidence bound of the conservative lower-compatible `p_C,N(E)` is greater than `tau_abs=0.10`. It is **adverse** to a clinically material persistent-content hypothesis when the simultaneous 95% upper bound is below or equal to 0.10. Otherwise the absolute result is indeterminate. There is no ample-minus-near requirement.

A specifically **uniform** persistence label is added if each adequately supported positive timely-gap bin (`H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus`) has a simultaneous conservative lower bound above 0.10. Failure of that stronger label does not negate the primary ample-gap persistence result. In particular, synthetic or observed discordance of 30% in every bin with a near-zero gap gradient must be classified as persistent, not null.

### 3. Nodule specificity versus generic action-content failure

A nodule-specific observable content phenotype requires absolute persistence plus a simultaneous conservative lower bound for `Delta_C,X` greater than `tau_excess=0.05`. If both pulmonary `p_C,N(E)` and standardized analogous `p_C,X(E)` have lower bounds above 0.10 but `Delta_C,X` does not exceed 0.05, label the result **generic cross-action persistent discordance**, not null. If the upper bound for `Delta_C,X` is at or below 0.05, that is adverse to a material nodule-specific excess while leaving the absolute/generic result intact. If `X` fails adjudication, overlap, or precision gates, nodule specificity is inconclusive; the absolute pulmonary conclusion can still be reported if it independently passes every pulmonary gate.

### 4. Short content-opportunity concentration

Report `RD_C,short-ample = p_C,N(H_0_6)-p_C,N(E)` with all categories and unknown bounds. A short-window-concentrated storage chronology pattern requires a simultaneous lower bound above 0.05, a short-gap discordance lower bound above 0.10, and an ample-gap upper bound at or below 0.10. This is compatible with limited observed artifact-construction time but does not prove editing, awareness, or workflow race. It is secondary and is not the negation required for persistence. Both short concentration and ample absolute persistence can be present, yielding a bundled content phenotype.

### 5. Departure-time artifact availability

A nodule-opportunity delayed-storage phenotype requires `Delta_A(0)` to have a simultaneous lower bound above 0.05, the same direction at +24 hours, and no ordinary-control, matched-control, radiology-lag, or comparator-reversal warning. If `q_N(0)` is high but calibrated `Delta_A(0)` is at or below the margin, report generic institutional storage latency rather than no problem. Availability and content conclusions are separable: high `A` cannot hide high ample-gap discordance, and faithful eventual text cannot repair `A` at departure.

### 6. Joint clinical interpretation

* availability only: prioritize a prospective true availability/signature/transmission timestamp crosswalk before selecting a content intervention;
* short-window concentration without ample persistence: test earlier artifact construction or a real-time structured reconciliation trigger prospectively;
* absolute ample persistence with nodule-specific excess: test nodule-targeted structured reconciliation;
* absolute ample persistence with analogous actions also high: test an institution-wide action-recommendation handoff process;
* availability plus either content phenotype: test a bundled intervention with separable components and timestamps;
* low bounded availability excess and low bounded absolute content discordance: the remaining priority may be downstream tracking/exchange/communication, which MIMIC cannot establish.

These labels are descriptive evidence for choosing the next study, not intervention-effect estimates.

## Falsification and robustness tests

1. **Source and key stop rule.** Recompute all five source hashes and complete row/key diagnostics. A changed hash, duplicate pulmonary canonical `DS` key, nonreciprocal addendum used as canonical, impossible timestamp parse, or changed schema stops the confirmatory run.
2. **Clock identity and exclusivity.** Verify numerically for every known row that `D-R_store = (D-DS_store)+G`; verify that exactly one `V`, one `H`, one `Z`, and one inherited `Y` state is assigned. `G`, not `D-R_store`, must drive every content stratum.
3. **Delayed-artifact leakage test.** Reclassifying the eventual text of any `V_24/V_72/V_168/V_late` row must not change its departure state `Y=A`, `q_N(0)`, or membership in `E_N`. A deliberately faithful delayed artifact is an adversarial test.
4. **Stale-artifact leakage test.** Every `G<0` row must remain `H_stale`, outside all positive-gap content estimands. A deliberately matching stale summary must not count as evidence of incorporation; a discordant stale summary must not count as positive-window failure.
5. **Uniform-high adversarial test.** A synthetic table with equal 30% discordance in all positive gap bins and adequate precision must pass absolute persistence while `RD_C,short-ample` is null. Any implementation requiring a positive ample-minus-near contrast fails verification.
6. **Analogous-high adversarial test.** If pulmonary and standardized analogous-action ample discordance are both 30% with no material difference, the code must label generic cross-action persistence and adverse/not supportive for nodule specificity; it must not label the result null.
7. **Institutional latency calibration.** Compare `N` with full `O`, standardized `O`, and `M` at `D,+24h,+72h,+168h`. A difference of at least 0.02 between standardized and matched primary availability contrasts, a sign reversal, empty cells, or low effective sample size blocks availability prioritization.
8. **Analog comparator reversal.** Compare full standardized `X` with deterministic matched `X`. A sign reversal or absolute difference of at least 0.05 in `Delta_C,X` blocks nodule-specific labeling but leaves the uncalibrated absolute pulmonary result visible.
9. **Radiology-lag negative controls.** Compare `storetime-charttime` for pulmonary recommendation components with `X` and `I`, and stratify the content analysis by locked radiology-lag bins (`<6`, `6-24`, `>=24h`). Disappearance or reversal of specificity after this locked calibration is adverse to nodule specificity, not proof of a different mechanism.
10. **Charttime placebo.** Repeat plots using `charttime` in place of `R_store` only as a labeled placebo. Placebo results cannot support a storage/content conclusion. Material disagreement is a timestamp-semantics warning requiring prospective crosswalk.
11. **Threshold stability.** Repeat ample `G` thresholds at 12 and 72 hours and the report-admission linkage window at exact admission and admission-minus-12-hours. A primary persistence label requires no sign reversal and the 72-hour estimate must be directionally compatible; sensitivity windows may not add candidates to frozen `N`.
12. **Permutation check.** Within fixed admission-structure, gap, and interval cells, permute pulmonary versus analogous labels and recompute `Delta_C,X`; within pulmonary timely positive-gap rows, permute gap labels and recompute `RD_C,short-ample`. Null-centered permutation distributions are a software/leakage check, not inferential evidence. Failure blocks the affected contrast.
13. **Reviewer-label falsification.** Report pre-consensus confusion matrices. If discordance is driven by one reviewer, one action type, one interval, or unresolved alternate-resolution language, the broad mechanism label is inconclusive unless the locked leave-one-component-out analyses retain direction.
14. **Denominator audit.** The hash and row count of `N` must remain identical across eligibility, availability, content, control, transition, and downstream outputs. The sum of all `J_N` cells and inherited `Y` cells must each equal `N`; analogous outputs must equal `N_X`.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

**Supportive for persistent pulmonary content discordance** means every pulmonary adjudication/unknown/event gate passes and the simultaneous conservative lower bound for `p_C,N(E)` exceeds 0.10. This remains supportive when near and ample gap rates are equal or all gap rates are high. It supports a prospective structured reconciliation study, not an assertion that a human ignored an available report.

**Supportive for nodule-specific persistence** additionally requires an adequate analogous comparator and a simultaneous conservative lower bound for `Delta_C,X` above 0.05, stable under standardization/matching, radiology-lag stratification, thresholds, and reviewer leave-outs. It supports testing a nodule-targeted component prospectively.

**Supportive for generic cross-action persistence** requires the absolute pulmonary criterion and an analogous-action lower bound above 0.10 without a material nodule-specific excess. This is not adverse to the existence of a handoff problem; it redirects intervention design toward a general actionable-recommendation workflow.

**Supportive for short-window concentration** requires the separate locked short-minus-ample and absolute short-gap criteria while the ample upper bound is at or below 0.10. It supports only a limited-storage-window chronology phenotype. **Supportive for availability delay** requires the separately calibrated `Delta_A` rule. If availability and content criteria both pass, the finding is bundled.

**Adverse to persistent content as a material phenotype** requires the simultaneous conservative upper bound for ample timely pulmonary discordance at or below 0.10. **Adverse to nodule specificity** requires the calibrated excess upper bound at or below 0.05 or a comparable/higher analogous-action rate with adequate precision. **Adverse to a short-window explanation** includes uniformly high discordance, no bounded short-minus-ample excess, or persistence at `G>=72h`. **Adverse to nodule-specific availability delay** includes no calibrated excess over ordinary controls, comparator reversal, or a matching ordinary workflow signal. These adverse findings redirect the next study; they do not prove the opposite human mechanism.

**Inconclusive** includes insufficient `N`, `N_X`, ample-gap support, or discordant events; excess `U_R/U_D/U_text`; poor reviewer reliability; failed reciprocal-chain or duplicate-key audit; low overlap/effective sample size; extreme weights; simultaneous intervals spanning the operational margins; threshold sign reversal; comparator reversal; permutation/software failure; or source/schema mismatch. If pulmonary absolute persistence passes but `X` is sparse, the absolute phenotype is supportive while nodule specificity alone is inconclusive. Inconclusive is never reported as absence.

## Verifier scope and evidence boundary

A computational verifier can check hashes, archive members, schemas, complete scans, joins, exact filters, first-subject selection, frozen-manifest ordering, reciprocal chain rules, canonical `DS` uniqueness, all timestamp arithmetic, `V/H/Z/Y` exhaustiveness, the `G`-based `E` definition, stale/delayed exclusions, all-N numerators, unknown bounds, control construction, weights/effective sample size, bootstrap/simultaneous intervals, margins, robustness outputs, and whether result labels follow the computed rules. It must run the adversarial uniform-high, analogous-high, stale, delayed-faithful, missing-time, and denominator-change cases. It must reject a conclusion driven by `D-R_store`, a complete-case denominator, an ample-minus-near requirement, or eventual delayed fidelity.

The verifier cannot clinically determine incidental status, appropriate interval, alternate resolution, or semantic fidelity without expert adjudication. Neither computation nor adjudication can establish report visibility, draft/final/signature status, author awareness, transmission, clinician/patient communication, patient receipt, outside-system follow-up, adherence, malignancy, causal mechanism, intervention effect, safety, or patient benefit from these sources. Those stronger claims require prospective UI/event/signature/transmission timestamps, communication and order data, external linkage, clinical governance, and a separable prospective or randomized workflow study.
