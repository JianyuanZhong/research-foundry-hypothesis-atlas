Define `R` jointly with `B` and require finite/positive repeat age, repeat creatinine, repeat cystatin C, repeat date, and inclusive elapsed time

` t = (date_1 - date_0)/365.25 in [2, 8] `.

Use the parent's unrounded equations exactly. Convert creatinine by `Scr_mg/dL = Scr_umol/L / 88.4`. For sex-specific 2021 race-free creatinine eGFR,

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/kappa,1)^alpha * max(Scr/kappa,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^age * female_factor`,

with `(kappa, alpha, female_factor) = (0.7, -0.241, 1.012)` for sex 0 and `(0.9, -0.302, 1)` for sex 1. For the parent's 2012 cystatin-C-only equation,

## Models and analysis (unchanged)

Use the exact parent comparator `M0`: ridge logistic regression with intercept, sex, a four-column restricted-cubic-spline basis for baseline `eGFRcr_0` using training quantiles 0.05/0.275/0.50/0.725/0.95, three-column RCS bases for baseline age and `t` using training quantiles 0.05/0.35/0.65/0.95, all 12 products of the four eGFRcr and three `t` bases, and four sex-by-eGFRcr products. Use standard Harrell restricted-cubic truncated-power basis with listed boundary knots. Center/scale nonbinary columns using training-only means and standard deviations; zero variance or coincident knots invalidate that fit.

`M1` contains every `M0` term plus exactly `E` and `E*(eGFRcr_0-60)/10`. No continuous cystatin value, repeat cystatin, combined-marker equation, alternative threshold, extra covariate, screening, or outcome-guided transformation is allowed. Penalize all non-intercept coefficients equally; tune each model separately on the fixed lambda grid `10^(-4), 10^(-3.5), ..., 10^4`, using nested inner-fold unweighted Bernoulli log loss and the largest one-standard-error lambda.

Use ten outer repeats with seeds `[260911,260912,260913,260914,260915,260916,260917,260918,260919,260920]`, five outer folds stratified within all four `E×Y` cells by seeded permutation and round-robin assignment, and five inner folds with seed `outer_seed + 1000 + outer_fold`. Use identical assignments for M0 and M1, preserve participant isolation, and retain ten out-of-fold probabilities per participant per model. The primary prediction is the arithmetic mean across the ten predictions; clip only for log loss at `[10^-6,1-10^-6]` and report clipping counts. Repeat variables are never predictors; `t` is observed-repeat timing, so this is not baseline-deployable.

The two primary paired contrasts remain exactly:

- `Delta_Brier = 1000 * [mean((Y-p0)^2) - mean((Y-p1)^2)]`;
- with `K=ceil(0.10*N)`, `Delta_yield10 = 1000 * (events_in_topK_M1 - events_in_topK_M0)/K`, where top-K ties use the parent's local SHA-256 rule.

Test `Delta_Brier` first and `Delta_yield10` only after the first passes, each with the parent's two-sided 95% participant-bootstrap interval and symmetric null-centered plus-one p-value. Preserve all calibration, support, event-scarcity, comparator-signal, split-sign, convergence, and 2,000-successful-bootstrap gates. Freeze all ten prediction columns before the paired participant bootstrap with seed 260921 and maximum 2,500 attempts. Report aggregate results only; never publish `eid`, rows, predictions, ranks, folds, hashes, or participant-linked coefficients.

## Actionability firewall and interpretation gates

The capacity contrast is explicitly a **schedule-marginal ranking estimand**, not a treatment or testing policy estimand. “Top 10%” means the highest predicted observed repeat-event risk among `S_A` at equal analytic capacity; it does not mean that 10% of a clinical population should be tested. Do not use words such as “screen,” “order,” “refer,” “benefit,” “harm,” or “net benefit” in a scientific conclusion unless they are explicitly preceded by “not identified here.”

Any supportive result must pass the parent’s full gates and state only that the added baseline indicator improved the frozen out-of-fold performance contrast within `S_A`. It may report event concentration at fixed analytic capacity, but must state that the experiment does not observe testing consequences, attendance causes, costs, false positives, downstream decisions, or patient outcomes. An adverse result means M1 degrades performance, fails calibration relative to M0, or reproducibly favors M0 under the frozen estimand; it does not prove cystatin has no information in other populations or outcomes. An inconclusive result includes intervals crossing zero, conflicting metrics, failed gates, unstable splits, sparse cells, or bootstrap failure; it is not equivalence.

There are two separate stop classes. `STOP_FIELD_UNCERTIFIED` is a technical provenance failure and permits no scientific interpretation. A certified run can still be biologically/clinically inconclusive or adverse. Certificate passage does not validate assay accuracy, equation performance, chronicity, measured GFR, or threshold meaning.

## Falsification and claim boundary

Predeclared falsifiers are unchanged: M0 must beat the intercept-only prevalence predictor; M1 must pass calibration and support gates; the two primary gains must be positive with intervals excluding zero and p<0.05 in sequence; at least 8/10 repeat-specific gains must be positive; and the fixed capacity must not be rescued by descriptive AUC or log-loss diagnostics. Permuting or relabeling outcomes is synthetic QA only unless separately predeclared by the Lead; no post hoc alternative outcome or threshold is allowed.

Computational verification can establish source/hash/column checks, certificate parsing and stop behavior, joins, masks, equations as certified, timing, folds, leakage isolation, model dimensions, predictions, calibration, capacity arithmetic, bootstrap arithmetic, and consistency between computed outputs and the assigned interpretation branch. It cannot establish assay validity, analyte interchangeability, measured GFR, CKD/chronicity, why someone attended, whether a clinician should order a test, causal utility, benefit, harms, missing-follow-up completeness, or transportability. Those require authoritative laboratory evidence, clinical adjudication, external validation, decision-impact work, or a randomized/pragmatic study.
