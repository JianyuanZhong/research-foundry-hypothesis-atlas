# Observation-aware acute respiratory successor to the post-shock IV-loop target trial

## Decision question and evidence boundary

Among adults who remain invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48 after vasopressor-treated shock has stabilized, does initiating or re-initiating an ICU-recorded IV furosemide or bumetanide within the next 24 hours increase the chance of being alive and continuously liberated from invasive ventilation for at least 48 observed hours by hour 168?

This successor keeps the hour-48 population and 24-hour initiation decision. The primary endpoint is shortened to an acute, sustained-liberation outcome because the configured MIMIC release has ICU-timestamped ventilation but no complete ward or post-discharge ventilator record.

The strongest supported claim is narrower: heterogeneous conservative-fluid/deresuscitation bundles can reduce recorded balance and may improve ventilator-free days, while randomized evidence is heterogeneous, underpowered for clinical outcomes, and does not establish the benefit or renal safety of starting an actual IV loop at this landmark. The 2026 renal-congestion review is explicitly hypothesis-generating and says outcome-driven interventional evidence is lacking (frozen Europe PMC response source [source checksum]; parent evidence also records the inspected full-text review source). This experiment therefore tests a treatment effect, not the existence of renal congestion.

The falsifiable hypothesis is that strategy A increases the per-protocol risk of a 48-hour continuously observed liberation by day 7 by at least 5 percentage points without an excess of 7-day death or observed new RRT. A positive result would justify external validation and a pragmatic randomized trial; it would not establish standard care, renal safety, or benefit for unselected shock survivors.

## Primary endpoint: sustained observed liberation by day 7

Build valid invasive-ventilation intervals from icu/procedureevents item 225792 with valid starttime, endtime, storetime, accepted delivered status, and the interval clipped to every linked icustays.intime,outtime for the subject. Union overlaps and preserve readmissions. The primary horizon is [t0, t0+7 days].

For each clone construct three states:

- Definite success (YL=1): observed extubation/end of invasive ventilation by t0+5 days, followed by a continuous 48-hour interval entirely covered by linked ICU observation, alive, and with no overlapping ventilation.
- Definite failure (YU=0): death by t0+7 days; or the person remains observed and invasively ventilated through t0+7 days; or no qualifying liberation occurs while complete observation covers the relevant day-7 risk window.
- Unknown (YL=0, YU=1): ICU observation ends or has a gap before required 48-hour confirmation, or alive hospital discharge occurs before a qualifying observed interval. No unobserved alive time is called non-ventilated.

This is a hospital/ICU-observed acute endpoint, not a claim about ventilation after discharge. Report observed ICU coverage, gaps, extubation, reintubation, discharge, and death. Report primary binary bounds and the fully observed-case result. The per-protocol identification region is:
RD_L = mean_w(A,YL) − mean_w(B,YU);
RD_U = mean_w(A,YU) − mean_w(B,YL),
with subject-clustered bootstrap intervals around each bound. The primary clinical gate is the lower 95% bound for RD_L > +0.05, requiring benefit to survive the worst admissible completion. Report parent VFD28 VFD_L/VFD_U as a secondary, non-primary analysis using ICU union and no alive-discharge carry-forward. 6-hour and 24-hour sustained-liberation sensitivities and a day-14 version must not replace the primary endpoint.

## Falsification and result logic

Test whether eventual strategy predicts pre-t0 six-hour creatinine, urine-output, or MAP slopes after baseline adjustment. A material standardized difference >0.10 or multiplicity-controlled P<0.05 makes the causal interpretation inconclusive. More-negative 24-hour balance and greater urine output under A are process-positive controls, not evidence of benefit.

Supportive requires adequate arm size/ESS and positivity; passed falsification; lower 95% bound for RD_L above +0.05; no adverse 7-day mortality gate; observed RRT-harm upper bound below +0.05 under both date brackets; expected process direction; and no material ICU/eMAR or endpoint-capture conflict. This supports replication, not practice.

Adverse is upper 95% bound for RD_U <= 0, or a prespecified mortality/RRT harm gate crossed under the lower ascertainment bound. Favorable balance cannot rescue respiratory or safety harm. Inconclusive includes a region spanning the clinical threshold, wide bounds, early discharge/observation gaps dominating unknowns, nonpositivity, eMAR conflict, failed falsification, discordant hospital-observed sensitivity, RRT bracket changing classification, or missing ward capture. Nonsignificance is not equivalence or safety.

The verifier can check source hashes/members/columns, partition and reserved buckets, exact population and 24-hour treatment window, item/status/unit maps, intervals, discharge linkage, endpoint bounds, RRT allowlist/bracket, weights/ESS, uncertainty, falsification, and conclusions linked to computed fields. It cannot establish exchangeability, treatment indication, goals of care, complete ward/post-discharge ventilation or RRT, validity of note-text classification, renal-congestion biology, or transportability. Those require clinical adjudication, additional longitudinal data, and preferably randomization.
