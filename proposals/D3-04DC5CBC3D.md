# Do remission-associated macrophage averages reflect captured state representation?

## Question, hypothesis, and deliverable

**Question:** Among rheumatoid-arthritis synovial membrane macrophages sampled once in E-MTAB-8322, does the observed remission-versus-treatment-resistant average-expression contrast have a robust, estimable component attributable to differences in captured macrophage expression-state proportions, or is within-state expression change at least as large?

**Falsifiable hypothesis (untested):** Conditional on the prespecified technical-overlap and state-support gates, more than half of the squared multigene remission/resistant macrophage-expression distance is attributable to state-proportion differences rather than state-conditional expression differences, and that attribution is stable to donor resampling and leave-one-donor-out state fitting. The 50% threshold defines “dominant” before analysis. Composition and within-state regulation may coexist. “State” means a reproducible expression cluster inferred in this assay, not a stable lineage, transition, or mechanism.

**Deliverable:** newly fitted train-only, leave-one-donor-out (LODO) expression-state maps and held-out assignments; per-donor state proportions and conditional expression vectors; a signed two-factor Shapley decomposition and donor-level uncertainty/influence outputs; plus an equal-donor pseudobulk baseline on the same response-blind gene universe. No screening has been executed and no result is claimed.

**Clinical importance:** An average synovial signature alone can motivate the wrong next experiment. Robust composition dominance would motivate a follow-up measuring recruitment/retention and tissue localization; within-state dominance would motivate targeted cell perturbation or functional testing; an unstable/unidentifiable decomposition means obtain clinical, spatial, or technical evidence before choosing either direction. No result alone selects or changes a patient’s treatment.

## Evidence boundary and competing explanations

The strongest evidence-supported claim remains limited to the inspected abstract-level literature: synovial macrophage heterogeneity and remission-associated macrophage observations [K1], HBEGF+ macrophage–fibroblast invasiveness as a plausible within-state/tissue-crosstalk alternative [K2], and a spatial LYVE1+ macrophage network associated with treatment response in another spatial/longitudinal study [K3]. These works establish plausibility and boundaries, not the decomposition tested here. All three attached works remain abstract-only; no full text or supplementary methods are claimed. This source cohort may overlap the closest remission literature [K1], so novelty of the cohort biology or an unreported result is not asserted.

The unresolved claim is whether the mean expression contrast in these specific 13 donors is quantitatively composition-dominant by the prespecified signed decomposition, conditional on the assay-level gates. The strongest biological rival is within-state transcriptional regulation. A more fundamental rival is response-linked disease severity, therapy/history, biopsy selection, cell recovery/capture, or sequencing/library structure. These can affect both cell-state proportions and expression. The prepared `treatment` annotation is empty/null and disease-activity scores and longitudinal clinical records are unavailable. Spatial niches are unmeasured. Stable adjusted association would not establish causality, recruitment, a transition, mechanism, or treatment effect. A clinician must adjudicate response-label meaning and biopsy/therapy timing before stronger clinical interpretation.

## Estimand: explicit signed two-factor Shapley decomposition

Let R denote remission and S treatment-resistant. For donor i, `p_ik` is the fraction of its captured, mapped cells assigned to aligned state k; `m_ik` is its mean transformed expression vector over cells assigned to k and fixed genes. First average donors equally within each group to obtain `p_gk` and `m_gk`; do not weight donors by cell number. Use the common aligned state set; failed support is not silently dropped. Let `mu(p,m)=sum_k p_k*m_k` and `F(p,m)=||mu(p,m)||_2^2` on the fixed gene universe. Compute all four counterfactuals:

- `F_RR = F(p_R,m_R)` (remission proportions, remission conditional means).
- `F_SS = F(p_S,m_S)` (resistant proportions, resistant conditional means).
- `F_RS = F(p_R,m_S)` (remission proportions, resistant conditional means).
- `F_SR = F(p_S,m_R)` (resistant proportions, remission conditional means).

Total observed squared distance `D = F_RR + F_SS - 2*F_RS` is not the valid distance formula for a generic counterfactual; therefore instead define the contrast function `Q(p,m)=||mu(p,m)-mu(p_S,m_S)||_2^2` for all counterfactuals, where the resistant profile is the fixed origin. Denote `q_RR=Q(p_R,m_R)`, `q_SR=Q(p_S,m_R)`, `q_RS=Q(p_R,m_S)`, and `q_SS=Q(p_S,m_S)=0`. This gives total `T=q_RR=||mu_R-mu_S||²`, the exact observed squared distance. Signed composition Shapley is `C=0.5*((q_RR-q_SR)+(q_RS-q_SS))`; signed within-state Shapley is `W=0.5*((q_RR-q_RS)+(q_SR-q_SS))`. Thus `C+W=T` algebraically. The primary scalar is `C/T`, without clipping; cancellation can yield values below 0 or above 1, so never describe it as an ordinary percent explained in those cases. Report C, W, T and each q, and verify `|C+W-T| <= 1e-10*max(1,|T|)`; failure is a computation error. If `T <= 1e-12` on this transformed scale, ratio is undefined and classification inconclusive; still report component outputs. These equations use both factor-swap orders and a fixed resistant-origin squared-distance game; they preserve signed attribution, not an assumption that both components are nonnegative.

## Methods compared and compute limits

**Simple baseline:** equal-donor log-CP10k pseudobulk group profiles on the fixed gene universe; donor-level deletion and exact label permutation. It answers whether an average contrast exists and how donor-sensitive it is, but cannot partition state representation from conditional expression.

**Substantive alternative:** train-only LODO expression-state fitting and held-out centroid assignment on the same donors, features and normalization, then the signed Shapley decomposition. It can reveal whether the aggregate contrast is carried primarily by captured expression-state proportions or by expression conditional on state, information the baseline necessarily discards. It remains descriptive and vulnerable to response-linked technical capture and state instability. Both approaches are retained.

**Resources:** CPU only is currently appropriate for 13 sparse donor matrices and modest PCA/clustering; no GPU evidence or need. Estimated solver envelope from the audit is 2–4 CPU, 8–16 GiB, and up to 7,200 s. These are planning estimates, not measured. The 13-fit LODO core and 10,000 donor-summary bootstrap algebra are unbenchmarked in this branch; request 4 CPU, 16 GiB, 7,200 s, write per-fold/checkpoint outputs, and record wall time/peak memory. This is not a claim that nested 10,000x13 refits fit; that workload is explicitly not the chosen bootstrap because it is unverified and needlessly conflates donor sampling with repeated map refitting. If the summary-bootstrap or LODO core cannot complete in the configured envelope, stop with partial checkpoint and report unresolved feasibility to Lead—do not reduce donors, feature threshold, bootstrap draws, estimand, or claim without Lead review. The conditional interval’s limitation is explicit; if future evidence requires full refit uncertainty, benchmark/authorize that separately.
