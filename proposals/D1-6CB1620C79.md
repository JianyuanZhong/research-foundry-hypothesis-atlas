## Scientific opening, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

The parent’s cross-stream respiratoryCare design asks whether early recorded-vital state/process information converges with a separately stored respiratory-care transition. A remaining clinically consequential boundary is whether a treatment.csv.gz row adds any respiratory-specific timing concordance beyond the opportunity to write any treatment row, shared scheduling, documentation density, and discharge/end-of-life workflow. This matters because a positive cross-table signal could otherwise be mistaken for evidence that a treatment stream independently corroborates care-state onset. Conversely, a negative result would identify that the treatment table contributes no information beyond workflow, without invalidating the parent’s narrower recorded-vital association.

The three inspected works support only bounded premises:

- [K1] Pollard et al. describe eICU as a multi-center linked ICU archive with treatment information, but emphasize that continuously streamed bedside data are only partly archived and that archive fields are not automatically bedside truth. This supports a measurement/availability stress test, not treatment semantics.
- [K2] Rajkomar et al. show that temporally structured EHR representations can support multi-center prediction and discuss semantic interoperability limits. This supports retaining a transparent temporal baseline alongside a learned alternative, not interpreting model performance as mechanism.
- [K3] Tang et al. show that structured EHR preprocessing can be made reproducible and demonstrate an eICU proof of concept. This supports frozen, fold-isolated preprocessing, not the claim that a treatment row is an order, administration, delivery, or independently timed clinical action.

The strongest claim supported by the parent and these works is therefore: linked eICU tables can support a reproducible, hospital-transportable analysis of recorded events, while data capture and field semantics may create availability and workflow artifacts. Neither the parent nor the inspected literature establishes that treatmentstring identifies a delivered therapy, that treatmentoffset is the time of care rather than a record/schedule time, or that respiratoryCare starts are physiologic deterioration.

### Falsifiable hypothesis

Among the unchanged adult first-ICU S6 risk set without an auditable active respiratoryCare interval at minute 360, the parent’s pre-landmark state/process vector has a treatment-concordant increment for the strict documented respiratoryCare interval-start endpoint beyond the increment for generic treatment-record opportunity and a frequency/hierarchy-matched non-respiratory workflow record. Specifically, the held-out-hospital association for the independently defined composite C_RT_doc (a strict respiratoryCare start with a qualifying respiratory treatment row in a prespecified time window) will be directionally coherent with, and not materially weaker than, the association for R_start_doc, after the parent’s fixed state/process adjustment, treatment/chart availability audits, timing and exit safeguards, and schedule-preserving null.

The competing explanation is generic documentation or scheduling: the same early state/process vector predicts that a stay will generate rows, that rows are generated near routine schedules or discharge, or that the respiratory and treatment tables share an interface. Under that explanation, the process increment will be similar for T_any_record, the matched non-respiratory control, and C_RT_doc, or will disappear after availability weighting, shifted-window controls, or schedule-preserving treatment-offset permutation.

This is a descriptive recorded-documentation hypothesis. It does not test treatment effectiveness, treatment indication, a causal effect, physiologic respiratory failure, or whether a therapy was delivered.

### Why this could change clinical understanding

If treatment-specific concordance survives the negative control, schedule null, hospital holdout, and availability stress tests, the next justified step is blinded clinical adjudication of a sample of respiratoryCare/treatment pairs and validation against orders, administration/device, or ventilator data. If it fails those tests, the important finding is that apparent corroboration is a workflow/recording phenomenon; future work should improve measurement rather than build a respiratory deterioration alert from this pattern. Either result separates a cross-table operational regularity from a clinically overinterpreted treatment claim.

## Prespecified interpretation and falsification criteria

### Supportive pattern

Call the recorded-concordance hypothesis supportive only if all gates are satisfied:

- source/header/hash checks pass; raw treatment classes are frozen before outcomes; at least five viable hospitals remain with positive R and treatment-concordance opportunity;
- treatment and respiratory event semantics are computationally auditable, with adequate positivity and ESS;
- C_RT_doc(30) and R_start_doc show directionally coherent held-out-hospital process increments in both elastic-net and boosting, with uncertainty excluding the inherited negligible margin where the metric is estimable;
- C-versus-R distinction persists after availability weighting, ±10/±60 sensitivity, shifted timing controls, schedule-preserving permutation, duplicate/conflict audit, discharge-adjacency restriction, and non-exit-adjacent analysis;
- T_nonresp_control and T_any_record do not show the same process increment, and the result is not dominated by one hospital, one baseline stratum, one raw-string vocabulary, or one documentation-time convention;
- conclusion is limited to independently concordant recorded respiratory documentation and does not call it delivered therapy.

Support would justify a next study with blinded respiratory/critical-care adjudication and order/administration/device linkage. It would not justify a treatment recommendation, alert deployment, physiologic deterioration claim, or causal claim.

### Adverse pattern

Treat these as evidence against treatment-specific corroboration, while preserving any narrower recorded association:

- the increment predicts T_any_record or generic availability but not C;
- C disappears after observation weighting, schedule permutation, shifted controls, exit/EOL safeguards, or hospital holdout;
- T_nonresp_control has the same magnitude and timing pattern;
- concordance is unstable across ±10/±30/±60, broad/candidate vocabulary, duplicate handling, or clinical-entry versus documentation-entry time;
- the association is discharge-adjacent, EOL-associated, one-hospital, one-stratum, or appears only in boosting;
- the parent’s R association persists but treatment adds no independent evidence.

This supports revising toward archive/interface and observation-process validation, not concluding that treatment was absent or ineffective.

### Inconclusive or deferral pattern

Do not call a null a refutation when it is imprecise. Defer if fewer than five viable hospitals remain; strict starts or C opportunities are too rare; positivity or ESS is inadequate; treatment vocabulary is unstable; offset meaning is unresolved; documentation lag dominates; intervals span both meaningful and negligible effects; or independent treatment provenance is unavailable. Retain the parent’s bounded recorded-vital result if this treatment module fails.

## Verifier-checkable claims and clinical limits

A verifier can check exact source paths and hashes; the treatment header and required fields; read-only joins; ordinary gzip/member status; first-ICU deduplication; [0,360] and (360,720] boundaries; strict interval-start logic; competing exits; raw-string preservation and outcome-blind freeze; independent T onset; concordance windows; negative-control matching; schedule and shifted nulls; no future leakage; fold isolation; predictions, metrics, uncertainty, positivity, ESS; and whether conclusions point to computed output rows.

A verifier cannot establish that a treatment row is an order, administration, delivered therapy, ventilator action, or clinical indication; that treatmentoffset is care time rather than documentation or schedule time; that respiratoryCare intervals are complete or represent actual bedside support; that the sources are institutionally independent; that a recorded transition is physiologic respiratory failure, clinically necessary, beneficial, or causal; or that a treatment or alert should change practice.

Those claims require order/administration/device or ventilator data, reliable indication and event timestamps, interface provenance, expert adjudication of a blinded sample, and external or prospective validation. No private clinical notes, unavailable supplementary methods, fitted result, or clinical conclusion is claimed here.

## Alternatives not selected and revisit conditions

The selected design is the smallest check that can distinguish treatment-specific recorded concordance from generic workflow while preserving the parent estimand. The alternatives are explicit:

- Additive elastic-net is retained as the interpretability and transport baseline.
- Capacity-matched histogram boosting is retained because nonlinear state/process/availability interactions are scientifically relevant, not because a small score gain is valuable.
- A neural raw-sequence model is deferred until raw sequence, vocabulary, and compute/interpretation dependencies can be justified; revisit if tabular models show a stable heterogeneous pattern that sequence order could discriminate.
- A causal treatment model is deferred until treatment assignment, indication, order/administration/device exposure, and clinically meaningful confounders are available; prediction from recorded rows cannot supply them.
- If no valid treatment class survives the outcome-blind vocabulary audit, abandon semantic corroboration and retain only the predeclared generic-record negative-control analysis, or stop the treatment branch.

Evidence to continue is a stable cross-hospital, treatment-specific, schedule-robust recorded signal. Evidence to revise is a generic availability or workflow signal. Evidence to defer is inadequate event support or unresolved source semantics.
