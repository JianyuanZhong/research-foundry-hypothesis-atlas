# A validity-separated, blinded actionability bridge for the frozen MIMIC pulmonary discharge-handoff protocol

## Parentage and the residual weakness repaired

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the inherited first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject frame; the all-opportunity pulmonary denominator; reciprocal pre-discharge radiology-report/addendum chronology; `D = hosp/admissions.dischtime`; one canonical discharge artifact; exact target atom `g=(E, terminal curr_service, W)` and its route; inherited `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` precedence; route-independent unit enumeration; all-unit/worst-unit admission routing; target and control freezing; sealed route-concealed component and holistic panels; challenge and negative controls; five sampling strata; sentinel census; positive-probability two-phase whole-admission sample; fixed parent allocation, no replacement, no top-up, design weights and pairwise probabilities; additive distinct-admission ledger, fixed controls and one reserve; the maximum of 900 distinct reviewed admissions; inherited HT/Hájek estimation and nested finite-population uncertainty; and the silent-validation-only, noncausal conclusion ceiling.

The primary residual weakness is **construct validity of the actionability endpoint**. The preceding bridge makes a stored source note, exact span, timestamp availability, and a reproducible text-codebook label auditable. That is necessary but not sufficient. A rule such as “finding plus next step/time anchor” can be reproducibly applied while still conflating three different claims: (1) the text contains an explicit instruction, (2) a receiving clinician could identify an accountable next action, and (3) that action is clinically appropriate or safe. The available MIMIC snapshot can test only the first claim and a carefully bounded documentary version of the second; it cannot supply a gold standard for appropriateness, receipt, comprehension, or benefit. A single source-aware adjudication stream also shares the same text, chronology, and target definitions as the parent labels, so agreement can be spuriously reassuring through correlated error.

This successor repairs that weakness by separating the documentary endpoint from an explicitly non-gold-standard, blinded clinical-interpretability audit; by requiring an independent source-identity audit; by measuring context and reviewer dependence; and by freezing all review-selection and decision thresholds before any target or bridge result is opened. It does **not** rename documentary agreement as clinical validity, does not add an unavailable clinical outcome, and does not add admissions outside the inherited workload ledger.

## Evidence-supported and unresolved claims

The available evidence supports an admission-linked corpus joining MIMIC radiology text and discharge text to admission discharge time and service/transfer history. The fields support reproducible source-note identity, exact text-span hashing, chart/store chronology, service routing, and an explicitly coded documentary actionability construct. They do not establish what a clinician saw, whether a recommendation was correct, whether responsibility was accepted, whether a patient received or followed it, or whether care or outcomes improved.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is therefore only that the inherited route and its source-aware documentary fields can be replayed from frozen stored text under pre-discharge chronology. The unresolved falsifiable claim is narrower and separated into two estimands:

* **Documentary bridge claim.** In a later chronological silent replay, can the frozen route emit a unique, available-at-`D` source span whose text satisfies a pre-registered documentary codebook, with abstention and unresolvedness controlled at the pre-specified level?
* **Interpretability-audit claim.** Conditional on an independently selected audit sample, does the documentary codebook have stable blinded reviewer agreement and a nontrivial relationship to a separately elicited “recipient could identify the next action, responsible role, and time anchor from this text alone” judgment, without treating that judgment as a clinical gold standard?

The second claim is a measurement audit, not proof of safety or correctness. A failure of the relationship is informative: the protocol should not call its documentary output “actionable” without relabeling it as a lower-level explicit-instruction construct.

## Frozen source/actionability record

Before opening any target, parent C/S, or holistic label, create one deterministic record for every sampled unit and route output. The inherited source ledger remains unchanged and is expanded only with the fields below. Store the source row key, source table, `note_type`, `note_seq`, `charttime`, `storetime`, exact text SHA-256, normalized-whitespace SHA-256, zero-based `span_start`/`span_end`, and `source_role` (`radiology_report`, `radiology_addendum`, `canonical_discharge_summary`, or `none`). A span is valid only when `0 <= span_start < span_end <= len(text)` and re-extracts byte-for-byte from the frozen row. Multiple notes remain separate ordered spans. An unresolved tie or absent unique note is `UNRESOLVED` and forces `DEFER` for the bridge endpoint.

Record `available_at_D = (storetime <= D)` and `report_before_D = (charttime <= D)` separately, with missing values coded `UNKNOWN`, not false. A source used by a non-deferred output must have `available_at_D=TRUE`. This is storage availability, not proof of delivery or receipt. Preserve `copy_status=unknown` unless an explicit inherited rule identifies copying.

The documentary codebook is frozen as five mutually exclusive states before any labels are opened:

1. `EXPLICIT_ACTION`: a cited span contains a pulmonary finding/status, a concrete next action or monitoring instruction, and an explicit responsible role or time anchor; the three components must be locatable by separate character spans within the same source note or within the inherited ordered source pair.
2. `EXPLICIT_NO_OWNER_OR_TIME`: a cited span contains a finding/status and next action but lacks both a responsible role and a time anchor.
3. `INFORMATIONAL_ONLY`: a finding/status is present without a next action or monitoring instruction.
4. `CONTRADICTORY`: incompatible current-status and action statements occur in the cited evidence, or the source pair contains unresolved conflict.
5. `UNRESOLVED`: missing/illegible text, invalid span, ambiguous codebook application, unavailable-at-`D` source, non-unique source, or a tie that the inherited rule does not resolve.

`EXPLICIT_ACTION` is deliberately not called “clinically actionable.” `EXPLICIT_NO_OWNER_OR_TIME` is retained rather than being silently promoted to actionable. The parent route and target remain separate columns. The documentary bridge gate permits only `EXPLICIT_ACTION`; every other state is an adverse or deferred documentary result according to the inherited route, never a negative target.

## New independent validity audit without a false gold standard

The same sampled admissions, and no additional admissions, supply two blinded audits.

### Audit A: source identity and documentary coding

A reviewer who did not create the route output checks the source-row key, note role, timestamp flags, and each component span against the immutable source row. The reviewer sees the fixed codebook and cited source text but not target truth, route name, parent score, outcome, or the other reviewer’s decision. A second reviewer independently repeats all records in the bridge-validation audit subset, all `CONTRADICTORY`/`UNRESOLVED` records, and a pre-selected simple random 20% of the remaining records within each inherited route and target/control stratum. Sampling is selected by a cryptographic seed from the frozen admission key list before any audit result is opened; the inclusion probability is stored and used in estimation. Disagreement is retained as `AUDIT_DISAGREEMENT`, not majority-washed. Exact-span/source identity failures are not repaired by reviewers.

### Audit B: recipient-interpretability judgment

A separate reviewer pool, with no access to Audit A decisions, receives only the cited text span(s), the fixed instruction “judge what is explicitly recoverable from this text; do not infer unstated facts,” and a four-field form:

* Is a pulmonary status/finding explicitly identifiable (`YES/NO/UNCLEAR`)?
* Is a next action or monitoring instruction explicitly identifiable (`YES/NO/UNCLEAR`)?
* Is a responsible role or time anchor explicitly identifiable (`YES/NO/UNCLEAR` for each)?
* Could a recipient identify one concrete next step without consulting another hidden field (`YES/NO/UNCLEAR`)?

The final item is named `TEXT_INTERPRETABILITY`, not clinical actionability. Reviewers do not judge whether the recommendation is medically correct, feasible, safe, followed, or beneficial. They do not see parent labels, route, control status, or downstream events. The reviewer form, instructions, randomization seed, and adjudication rules are sealed before labels. A third adjudicator resolves only form-definition disputes by returning `UNCLEAR` when the evidence does not support a resolution; it may not infer clinical appropriateness.

Audit A and Audit B use disjoint reviewer pools and independently randomized order. Their shared text is an acknowledged source of residual correlation, not evidence of external validity. The audit reports (i) exact source identity agreement, (ii) codebook agreement, (iii) field-level agreement for the four interpretability items, (iv) prevalence-adjusted agreement and raw agreement, (v) unresolved/disagreement rates, and (vi) the cross-tabulation of documentary state against `TEXT_INTERPRETABILITY`. It does not report a predictive accuracy or claim a gold standard.

A context-sensitivity sub-audit is pre-specified on the same Audit B records: one randomized half receives only the cited span, and the other receives the cited span plus the bounded source-note heading/section marker already present in the source text. No uncited text, later note, or post-`D` information is supplied. A large discrepancy is evidence that the documentary construct depends on context not represented in the source span and triggers `DEFER` for that stratum; it is not corrected by choosing whichever view is more favorable.

## Chronological silent replay and exact pre-label rules

Retain the parent’s frozen sample and all parent temporal boundaries if already specified. If no boundary was frozen by a parent, order distinct sampled admissions by `(D, subject_id, hadm_id)` and assign the first 60% to development, next 20% to calibration, and final 20% to silent bridge evaluation, with cutpoints defined by admission rank before any labels are opened. The fallback split is not permitted to replace an inherited boundary. The replay is one-pass, ascending in this order; no later block can alter an earlier threshold, codebook, source tie rule, or reviewer selection.

All thresholds are fixed before target labels, route scores, or Audit A/B outputs are inspected:

* The documentary bridge-readiness requirement is `L >= 0.80`, where `L` is the lower endpoint of the parent-prespecified 95% finite-population interval for the weighted proportion of non-deferred route outputs that meet the complete source identity plus `EXPLICIT_ACTION` gate. If the parent contains no numerical readiness threshold, `0.80` is the locked fallback and is not optimized on these data.
* The unresolved/disagreement ceiling is `U <= 0.15`, where `U` is the weighted proportion with any invalid source, unavailable-at-`D` flag, `UNRESOLVED`, or reviewer disagreement. A missing timestamp is `UNKNOWN` and contributes to `U`; it is never treated as available.
* The interpretability concordance is descriptive and not a clinical validity threshold. As a prespecified stress test, report the weighted proportion of `EXPLICIT_ACTION` records with `TEXT_INTERPRETABILITY=YES` and its interval; do not call it sensitivity, specificity, or accuracy. If fewer than 30 independently reviewed records occur in any route-by-audit cell, that cell is sparse and no pass/fail inference is made.
* The context-sensitivity trigger is an absolute difference of `0.15` or more in the weighted `TEXT_INTERPRETABILITY=YES` proportion between span-only and bounded-heading views. It is a warning and stratum-level `DEFER`, not a post hoc label correction.
* Any parent-defined event, effective-sample-size, precision, multiplicity, or missingness gate remains controlling. The new gates may not relax one.

The development block is used only for the inherited route rule if the parent requires fitting. The calibration block may calibrate a parent-provided probability output only when such an output already exists; otherwise report counts and intervals and do not invent probabilities or isotonic maps. Freeze any permitted map and the `DEFER` rule before the silent block. The silent block emits the inherited route (`AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`) plus source ledger and documentary state without feedback or label-driven repair.

## Estimands, baselines, and workload feasibility

The primary estimand is the finite-population, design-weighted proportion of sampled route outputs that satisfy complete source identity, `available_at_D=TRUE`, and `EXPLICIT_ACTION`, estimated with the inherited HT/Hájek estimator and nested sampling/adjudication variance. Report it overall, by route, by target/control status, and in the chronological calibration and silent blocks. The denominator is stated both as all inherited eligible outputs (including deferred failures) and, secondarily, the inherited non-deferred outputs; conditioning away `DEFER` is not allowed for the primary result.

Secondary estimands are source identity failure, timestamp unknownness, each documentary state, Audit A disagreement, each Audit B field’s `UNCLEAR` rate, context sensitivity, `TEXT_INTERPRETABILITY` distributions, chronological degradation, and the proportion of admissions with any adverse bridge record under inherited all-unit adverse precedence. If a parent probability and frozen target label exist, report route-stratified Brier/calibration quantities as secondary only. The target is never changed by the audit result.

The primary baseline is the unchanged parent route-only endpoint. A second measurement baseline uses the same sampled text and chronology but omits exact source spans, responsible-role/time-anchor fields, and the independent audits. A third negative control permutes cited spans within the pre-`D` same-admission source pool using a frozen deterministic permutation that preserves note count and text length distribution; no permuted record may be interpreted as evidence of a positive handoff. Improvement over the source-blind baseline is not interpreted as clinical benefit, and failure of the negative control is evidence of lexical or route leakage.

No new admission is added for either audit. The audit inclusion probabilities are carved out of the inherited reviewed-admission workload ledger and charged within the same fixed per-admission unit; the inherited fixed controls and one reserve remain unchanged. If the existing parent ledger does not permit the planned independent review for an admission, it is classified `AUDIT_NOT_FEASIBLE` and remains in the denominator with the corresponding adverse/inconclusive status; there is no top-up, replacement, or silent enlargement. A reserve may be used only under the parent’s already frozen reserve rule, and its use is recorded before opening the replacement’s labels. This makes the design operationally feasible without pretending that an unreviewed record has expert validity evidence.

## Falsification and interpretation

The documentary bridge is falsified if, under the parent interval and gate conventions, the silent-block lower bound falls below `0.80`, the unresolved/disagreement upper bound exceeds `0.15`, source-aware and source-blind route/error patterns differ materially under the parent’s locked margin, the negative-control permutation produces target-linked positive routing above the locked false-positive tolerance, or any post-`D` dependency is detected. Chronological degradation from calibration to silent evaluation is adverse when it exceeds the locked parent margin; if no such margin exists, report the difference with an interval and classify it as inconclusive rather than selecting one after inspection.

The interpretability-audit claim is adverse if agreement/disagreement or `UNCLEAR` rates make the fields non-reproducible, if context sensitivity triggers in a route stratum, or if `EXPLICIT_ACTION` has a low or highly unstable relationship to `TEXT_INTERPRETABILITY`. This does not falsify a text-extraction claim by itself; it falsifies the stronger unqualified use of “actionable.” Supportive results require the documentary gate, acceptable audit reliability, no negative-control leakage, and no temporal replay leakage. Wide intervals, sparse cells, failed reviewer independence, or `AUDIT_NOT_FEASIBLE` rates that prevent the pre-registered estimand are inconclusive, not supportive.

A supportive result establishes only that a route can be replayed from this MIMIC snapshot with auditable pre-discharge source identity and a bounded explicit-instruction construct, plus a measured reviewer interpretation audit. It does not establish that any instruction was delivered, read, understood, clinically appropriate, assigned to a real person, followed, safe, beneficial, or transportable. MIMIC timestamps are shifted and `storetime` is only a proxy for system availability. Radiology images, raw waveforms, recipient identity, task ownership, outpatient follow-up, medication adherence, and patient outcomes are unavailable here. Expert clinical review is required for appropriateness and safety; EHR access logs or prospective observation are required for receipt and workflow; prospective governed evaluation with clinical outcomes is required for benefit or harm. Any causal, treatment, safety, or live-deployment claim is prohibited.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

Relative to the parents, this successor does not merely add another label. It prevents a reproducible lexical instruction from being silently promoted to clinical actionability by introducing a validity-separated documentary state space, independent source and interpretability audits, exact pre-label thresholds, context-sensitivity testing, and a workload-constrained replay rule. It preserves the inherited population, chronology, target, route, sampling, estimand, controls, and noncausal ceiling. The decisive remaining uncertainty is intentional: even perfect agreement can validate only what is explicitly recoverable from stored text, not whether the handoff was clinically right or acted upon. That boundary is the result, not a limitation to be hidden.
