# Episode 51: Does an observed late lactate add material 24-hour mortality-review value beyond APACHE IVa and pressor-entry lactate?

## Unresolved question, strongest supported claim, and clinical importance

The selected Episode 50 parent established a narrow result: among 1,229 repeatedly measured adults from 33 EICU hospitals who survived in hospital to a 24-hour norepinephrine-entry landmark, adding continuous lactate trajectory to a model that already knew the late lactate produced a small point gain in threshold-averaged modeled net benefit (+0.448 per 100; 95% copied-hospital refit interval -0.463 to +1.580). That result is inconclusive for both direction and the prespecified +1-per-100 materiality margin. It does not answer whether the late measurement itself contributes enough information to matter once admission severity and the pressor-entry lactate are known.

The unresolved primary hypothesis is:

> Among APACHE-IVa-complete, repeatedly lactate-measured adults alive and still hospitalized 24 hours after early norepinephrine entry, adding the observed 12-to-24-hour lactate to a model containing retrospective APACHE IVa predicted hospital mortality and pressor-entry lactate increases uniformly threshold-averaged net benefit for modeled high-mortality-risk review by at least 1 net true-positive-equivalent review per 100 people across predicted mortality thresholds 0.30-0.50.

This is an incremental-prognostic hypothesis about an already recorded measurement in a selected measured cohort. It is not the causal effect, cost-effectiveness, or safety of ordering a repeat lactate. A material cross-hospital increment would justify external prospective validation of a repeat-lactate-informed review strategy; it would not justify ordering, resuscitation, or treatment recommendations.

The inspected full-text XML of Baysan et al. (2022; DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750; PMCID PMC9444407; frozen source ID [source checksum]; XML [source checksum]) reports an external MIMIC validation in 4,440 critically ill adults with sepsis. Adding 24-hour lactate to recalibrated APACHE IV increased the C-statistic from 0.62 to 0.64, and admission plus 24-hour lactate had the best AIC. This supports modest serial prognostic information, not decision-curve value, a vasopressor-defined phenotype, multi-hospital transportability, or benefit from obtaining the test. The linked supplementary material was not inspected.

The inspected Vickers et al. decision-curve guide (PMCID PMC6261531; frozen HTML source ID [source checksum]; [source checksum]) supports comparing models with treat-all and treat-none over clinically reasonable thresholds and treating thresholds as harm-benefit preferences. It does not validate 0.30-0.50 for this review decision.

## Complete-source feasibility and actual filtering

No source rows were sampled. The complete gzip CSVs were scanned; pandas chunking of infusion and laboratory files is only a memory-control mechanism. Direct headers and representative rows were inspected, and catalog schemas and hashes were checked.

The inherited extraction reproduces 200,859 patient rows; 200,234 adult rows, including 7,081 literal/numeric older-age mappings to 90; 16,299 early-pressor stays; 14,786 deterministically selected people; 14,672 known hospital outcomes; 13,023 hospitalized through the landmark; 5,077 with a valid baseline lactate; 3,117 with baseline lactate at least 2 mmol/L; and 1,506 with a valid late lactate across 96 hospitals.

Among the 1,506, exactly 1,123 people in 34 hospitals have one finite APACHE IVa predicted hospital-mortality value strictly between 0 and 1. An outcome-blind gate of at least 15 such people per hospital retains 1,084 people in 31 hospitals; only after freezing that list are 449 deaths and 635 survivors materialized. Maximum hospital share is 9.32%. A separate exploratory sparse-benchmark calculation in 1,256 measured people/34 hospitals yielded a fixed point ΔINB of +4.304 per 100, but it came from an interrupted exploratory execution, omits APACHE, has no completed refit interval, and is not evidence for the primary hypothesis.

## Population, clocks, variables, and outcome

1. Parse age; map literal `> 89` and numeric values above 89 to 90; retain age at least 18.
2. In `infusionDrug`, define `t0` as the earliest `infusionoffset` in [0,1440] whose case-folded `drugname` contains literal `norepinephrine` or `levophed`. This is a recorded infusion entry, not verified administration or dose.
3. Per `uniquepid`, retain the stay with lexicographically smallest `(t0,patientunitstayid)`.
4. Let `L24=t0+1440`. Require `hospitaldischargestatus` in {Alive,Expired} and `hospitaldischargeoffset >= L24`.
5. Baseline `B`: `labname` exactly lactate after trim/case-fold, `labmeasurenamesystem` exactly mmol/L after trim/case-fold, finite `labresult` in [0.2,30], collection `labresultoffset` in [t0-360,t0], and `labresultrevisedoffset <= t0+60`. At a collection, retain the greatest eligible revision; conflicting values at that revision invalidate the collection; identical ties use the smallest `labid`. Choose the latest valid collection and require B>=2.
6. Follow-up `F`: identical analyte/unit/value/revision/conflict rules, collection in [t0+720,t0+1440], revision by L24; choose nearest t0+1080, then earlier collection, then smallest `labid`.
7. In `apachePatientResult`, require trimmed/case-folded `apacheversion="iva"` and finite `predictedhospitalmortality` in [0.000001,0.999999]. Require one row per stay; duplicate IVa rows are a computability failure. Never read `actualhospitalmortality`, `actualicumortality`, actual LOS, or actual ventilation fields as predictors.
8. Before reading outcome labels, retain hospitals with at least 15 complete people. Then define `D=1` for Expired and 0 for Alive. Because hospital discharge occurs at or after L24, D is a post-landmark outcome, although exact death time and cause are unavailable.

## Models, estimand, baselines, and uncertainty

The comparator A0 contains age and age squared; Female and Male indicators with Other/Unknown omitted; t0/1440 and its square; baseline collection lag `(t0-B_offset)/360`; `ln(B)` and its square; and `logit(predictedhospitalmortality)` and its square. This is retrospective APACHE IVa risk recalibration plus pressor-entry lactate.

A1 adds only follow-up measurement age `(L24-F_offset)/720`, `ln(F)`, and `ln(F)^2`. B remains in both models, so A1 can represent joint entry/late information without a binary clearance cutoff.

Use leave-one-hospital-out validation. In every training fold, winsorize each continuous column at training 1st/99th percentiles and standardize with the training mean and population SD; apply constants unchanged to the held-out hospital. Fit unweighted L2 logistic regression with intercept, C=1, lbfgs, max_iter=10000, tol=1e-8, no class weights, seed 20260924. No held-out outcome informs fitting, preprocessing, thresholds, or calibration.

At each p in {0.30,0.31,...,0.50}, compute `NB=TP/N-FP/N*p/(1-p)`. Treat-none is zero; treat-all uses pooled prevalence. A0 is the primary comparator and a training-site prevalence predictor is a sanity baseline. The primary estimand is:

`ΔINB = 100/(0.50-0.30) * integral_0.30^0.50 [NB_A1(p)-NB_A0(p)] dp`

using the trapezoid rule over all 21 locked thresholds. The +1-per-100 margin is carried forward from the parent as a transparent working materiality criterion; neither it nor uniform threshold weighting is a validated clinician/patient preference.

For uncertainty, sample the 31 hospitals with replacement 2,000 times using seed 20260951. Copies of one original hospital remain one held-out group; exclude every copy of that hospital from its training fold; include sampled multiplicity in training and held-out weights; refit preprocessing and both models in every replicate. Report percentile 95% intervals for ΔINB, AUROC difference, Brier improvement, log-loss improvement, calibration slope, and calibration-in-the-large. Also report all 31 fixed-prediction leave-hospital-deletion ΔINBs, 21 threshold cells, decile calibration, absolute probability shifts, and complete cohort flow.

## Falsification and interpretation

First require exact source hashes/headers, exact 1,506→1,123→1,084/31 flow, at least 1,000 people, at least 30 hospitals, at least 200 deaths and survivors, both outcomes in every retained hospital, maximum site share <=15%, finite converged original fits, at least 95% finite converged bootstrap replicates, at least 80% finite deletions, and all 21 thresholds. Failure is inconclusive/not computable.

If computable:

- Supportive: the ΔINB 95% lower bound is >=+1, A1 calibration slope is 0.80-1.20, absolute calibration-in-the-large <=0.05, A1 beats both defaults at every threshold, no threshold ΔNB is below -0.5 per 100, and at least 80% of site deletions are positive.
- Adverse to materiality: the 95% upper bound is <+1 but >=0. Smaller modeled gains remain possible.
- Opposite: the 95% upper bound is <0.
- Inconclusive: the interval includes +1 or zero without meeting another branch; a lower bound >=1 with failed guardrails is mixed/inconclusive.

A supportive result would establish only material out-of-hospital modeled review information under these scenario utilities in selected, repeatedly measured, APACHE-complete EICU survivors. An adverse result would argue against pursuing this locked +1 target, not against all prognostic value. An opposite result would indicate worse modeled decisions, not harm caused by lactate testing. An inconclusive result preserves uncertainty; threshold narrowing, binary clearance, or using the exploratory sparse benchmark cannot rescue it.

## Verifier contract and evidence limits

The executable must retain aggregate and private audit artifacts separately. Private cohort and held-out prediction rows remain in the workspace and are not published. The verifier can check hashes/headers, joins, one-person selection, clocks, laboratory revisions/conflicts, IVa row uniqueness, exclusion of actual-outcome APACHE fields, outcome-blind hospital gating, flow, fold-only preprocessing, fit settings, prediction uniqueness, each DCA cell, integration, copied-hospital multiplicities/refits, scores, calibration, intervals, deletions, gates, and deterministic label.

Verification must include synthetic/fixture outputs for supportive, adverse, opposite, mixed, inconclusive, and noncomputable states. It must reject a numerically correct output paired with claims that repeat testing improves survival, changes care, is cost-effective, or is clinically deployable. It must accept appropriately bounded interpretations whether the hypothesis is confirmed or falsified. Reference execution demonstrates computability and conclusion-output consistency, not scientific truth.

No automatic verifier or this dataset can establish when APACHE predictions were available to clinicians; whether the late lactate was arterial or venous; why it was ordered; verified norepinephrine administration/dose; treatments and physiology between B and F; what clinicians knew or did; preventability, timing, or cause of death; utility of thresholds; test burden; or benefit from repeating lactate. The repeated-measurement population is selected by clinician behavior and survival to L24. Clinical adjudication is needed for specimen and workflow meaning, external prospective validation for transport, and a prospective impact study—randomized if an ordering/treatment policy is proposed—for changed decisions, harms, costs, or patient benefit.
