# Repeat-TACE admission-episode biochemical perturbation: an event-anchored selected-cohort experiment

## Decision and substantive advance

This child preserves the parent’s frozen common-TACE2 baseline cohort, TACE2 time zero, 2018–2024 entry era, day-14 observation landmark, days 1–14 medication ontology/classification, ambiguity exclusions, exact albumin and total-bilirubin labels, parsing rules, and fixed baseline pathway membership. It replaces neither the parent’s cohort nor its pathway definitions.

A complete-source, value-blinded feasibility audit shows that the requested “stress/recovery” experiment is only half computable. A first subsequent strict TACE on TACE2 days 15–90 occurs in 159/319 (49.84%) systemic-record patients and 526/1,491 (35.28%) TACE2-only patients. Exact event-adjacent pre/acute pairs are usable: 57 versus 204 patients have both exact assays in the seven days before and strictly after the procedure through 72 hours. In contrast, post-discharge recovery is effectively absent: only 4 versus 11 have each assay on days 8–21, and only 2 versus 5 have a complete pre/acute/recovery pair. The laboratory table has no unit field. Therefore this proposal rejects recovery as an inferential endpoint and does not import external thresholds or calculate ALBI.

The best computable event-anchored child directly resolves the parent’s admission/procedure-context problem by testing **within-admission biochemical perturbation around an explicitly observed repeat-TACE event**, not stable reserve or recovery. The event is the first strict TACE in days 15–90. The primary comparison is the event-selected, observed-pair association between frozen baseline pathways and event-proximal change from the latest preprocedure assay to a deterministic early postprocedure assay. This is clinically useful as a bounded signal of whether systemic-record patients who were selected for another TACE present a different immediate albumin/bilirubin perturbation under that admission episode; it cannot establish treatment toxicity, recovery, safety, or comparative effectiveness.

## Clinical question, supported evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

### Question and importance

Among members of the frozen common-TACE2 pathways who subsequently undergo a recorded strict repeat TACE within days 15–90, does the systemic-record rather than TACE2-only baseline pathway identify a different immediate within-person albumin and total-bilirubin perturbation around that later procedure?

Repeated TACE can affect liver function, and liver function influences whether further locoregional or systemic therapy is tolerable. Published EHR work has examined acute (0–29 day) and chronic (30–90 day) changes after a TACE, but often selected patients with measurements in all periods and excluded additional TACE or oncologic therapy; it therefore does not resolve the parent’s pathway-specific, repeat-procedure-admission context (Miksad et al., BMC Cancer 2019, DOI 10.1186/s12885-019-5989-2; full text inspected via PMCID PMC6693268). The present advance is not another generic post-TACE deterioration rate. It turns the observed later procedure into an explicit anchor, uses exact timestamps to separate pre- from postprocedure assays, quantifies event selection and observation, and makes recovery failure a prespecified inconclusive branch.

### Strongest claim already supported

The complete HCC source can reconstruct the frozen population exactly and identify an event-proximal assay episode. The audit scanned all 338,040 procedure, 105,044 encounter, 1,810,646 diagnosis, 4,097,517 medication, and 28,159,928 laboratory rows without sampling. It reproduced 78,067 strict textual procedure matches, 72,273 valid-time strict rows, 38,742 patient-day episodes, 6,589 earliest adjacent qualifying TACE pairs, 6,586 HCC/adult pairs, 2,992 era/observation-qualified patients, 1,107 prior exclusions, 319 systemic and 1,491 comparator patients, and the 67 generic and 8 bevacizumab-only ambiguity exclusions.

Within the frozen arms, the first subsequent strict TACE in days 15–90 selects 159 systemic and 526 comparator patients; median event day is 55 in each arm (IQR 43–69 and 44–69). Selection is differential: 49.84% versus 35.28%. All 685 selected repeat-TACE rows link by (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`) to valid admission and discharge timestamps. The repeat procedure occurs a median 0.61–0.62 days after admission and 2.53–2.57 days before discharge. Exact albumin and bilirubin records are nearly co-measured: deterministic exact-clock pre/acute pairs exist for albumin in 57/159 versus 204/526 and bilirubin in 58/159 versus 205/526; 57 versus 204 have both assays paired. Recovery support is 2 versus 5 complete triplets under days 8–21. These are feasibility/context facts only; no assay values were used to choose the design.

### Unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis

Conditional on having a first strict repeat TACE on days 15–90 and having exact preprocedure and early postprocedure assays, the frozen systemic-record pathway is associated with a different immediate biochemical perturbation than the frozen TACE2-only pathway. The clinically favorable direction is a smaller albumin decrease without a larger bilirubin increase; a larger albumin decrease and/or bilirubin increase is adverse. This is a selected-event association, not an effect of systemic medication, not an effect of choosing repeat TACE, and not a recovery or hepatic-reserve hypothesis.

## Explicit selected future-event population and clocks

Within each frozen arm, identify strict TACE patient-day episodes after TACE2 using the same strict ontology. The selected event is the **first** strict episode whose normalized calendar delta from TACE2 is 15–90 inclusive. Retain its earliest valid `开始时间` as event timestamp and its corresponding `就诊号` deterministically (sort timestamp then encounter string if duplicate rows share the earliest time). Do not choose an event based on labs, outcomes, encounter length, or assay availability. Patients without this event remain in selection models and denominators but not in the event-pair estimand.

Report by baseline arm: selected/nonselected counts and proportions with Wilson intervals; event-day distribution; year; time since TACE2; event encounter linkage; time from admission to event and event to discharge; duplicate procedure rows; same-day procedure labels; prior day-15-to-event systemic records; and the four mutually exclusive categories of later systemic record before event and repeat TACE selection. The audit found selected events extending into calendar 2025 because a 2024 baseline TACE2 may have day-15–90 follow-up in 2025; retain them because entry era, not outcome era, is frozen.

The event-selected target population is patients who actually return to this institution, are chosen for another recorded TACE within 90 days, and have event-proximal assays. Repeat-TACE selection is plausibly affected by hepatic reserve, tumor response/progression, imaging, clinician preference, access, tolerance, baseline pathway, and outside care. Conditioning on it can induce collider bias; conditioning additionally on paired assays can induce observation bias. No event-selected estimate may be generalized to all 1,810 frozen-arm patients or interpreted causally.

## Exact assays, selectors, and outcomes

Restrict to exact `labs.检验` labels `白蛋白` and `总胆红素`; preserve `标本类型`. Parse `检验时间`. After trimming and removing commas, accept `定量结果` only if the complete string comprises an optional leading `<`, `>`, `≤`, or `≥` and a signed decimal/scientific numeric literal. Retain inequality marker and numeric part; reject any other text. Report inequality and specimen-type frequencies. Do not harmonize differently named assays, infer units, use reference ranges, calculate ALBI, or apply external clinical thresholds.

For each assay separately around the selected repeat-TACE timestamp:

1. **Preprocedure:** latest valid timestamp in `[event timestamp − 7 days, event timestamp)`; event timestamp is excluded.
2. **Early postprocedure:** valid timestamp in `(event timestamp, event timestamp + 72 hours]` minimizing `(absolute distance from event+24 hours, timestamp)`; thus nearest 24 hours and earlier timestamp on exact tie. Event timestamp is excluded.
3. **Change:** postprocedure minus preprocedure.

Albumin and bilirubin are separate co-primary biochemical outcomes. The principal complete-pair analysis requires each assay’s own pair; a joint hepatic profile sensitivity requires both assay pairs. Report whether selections share encounter/timestamp. The exact-clock audit predicts 57 systemic versus 204 comparator joint pairs. Calendar-day +1–3 selectors are sensitivity only; they must not replace exact clocks.

The 0–72-hour value is an admission-episode biochemical perturbation proxy. Albumin may reflect fluids, exogenous albumin, nutrition and inflammation; bilirubin may reflect biliary obstruction, hemolysis or other illness. Neither is adjudicated TACE injury.

### Recovery feasibility endpoint, not outcome

Before any outcome interpretation, report exact-assay observation on event days 4–7, 8–21 and 8–30, same encounter versus new encounter, and complete pre/acute/recovery triplets. Recovery value would be nearest event day 14 in days 8–21, but it is analyzable only if at least 40 systemic and 120 comparator patients have triplets and systemic effective sample size is at least 30. The complete-source audit found only 2 versus 5 joint triplets, so this gate is expected to fail. If it fails, state “recovery not computable in this snapshot”; do not widen windows or redefine recovery after seeing counts.

## Estimands and analyses

### Descriptive selection estimand in the full frozen cohort

Estimate the arm difference in cumulative incidence of a recorded first strict repeat TACE by day 90 from TACE2, with day-14 landmark already frozen. Because vital status, outside procedures and reliable censoring are unavailable, this is institutional recorded-event incidence, not biological incidence. Report crude risk difference/ratio with robust intervals and a pre-TACE2 adjusted overlap-weighted association. Do not call later TACE beneficial or adverse.

### Primary event-pair estimand

For assay `a`, among patients with the selected repeat TACE and a valid exact-clock pair, estimate

`E_w[a_post − a_pre | frozen systemic-record arm, selected event, paired] − E_w[a_post − a_pre | frozen TACE2-only arm, selected event, paired]`.

This is the measured-confounder-adjusted association between frozen baseline pathway and immediate biochemical change in an event- and observation-selected population.

Fit the parent’s pre-TACE2 propensity model in all 1,810 frozen-arm patients using only: age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, entry year/month, department, prior 30/90/180-day encounter counts, prior 90/180-day procedure counts, prior resection/ablation/transplant flags, encounter-timed cirrhosis/portal hypertension/ascites/encephalopathy/variceal bleeding/portal-vein invasion/metastasis proxies, and closest pre-TACE2 exact albumin, bilirubin, INR, platelet count, creatinine and sodium with missingness. No grace detail, later event, event-admission context, or post-TACE2 value enters this baseline model. Use overlap weights (`1-p` systemic, `p` comparator), report pooled/yearly support, all standardized differences, weights and arm ESS.

Because event and paired observation are selected, separately fit two nested five-fold cross-fitted logistic models from baseline covariates: (1) strict repeat-TACE event by day 90; (2) assay pair among selected-event patients. Include arm and pre-TACE2 predictors, but no post-event assay value. Report arm-specific observed fractions, calibration, AUC, probability distributions and predictors of selection. The primary remains baseline-overlap weighted; locked sensitivity multiplies overlap weights by inverse predicted event probability and inverse predicted pair probability, both untruncated and truncated at the 1st/99th percentiles. This sensitivity addresses measured selection only and does not recover a causal all-cohort effect.

Estimate weighted change regression with arm, flexible event-preprocedure assay, time from TACE2 to event, event year/month, hours from event to selected post value, event admission duration available before the selected post timestamp, and baseline covariates still above absolute weighted SMD 0.10. Use patient-level heteroskedasticity-robust 95% intervals. Event timing covariates improve within-selected description but do not remove collider bias. Report crude and adjusted arm means/differences, denominators, weight distributions, ESS and influence diagnostics. Use raw-scale outcomes only; rank/20% winsorized changes are locked robustness analyses, not replacements.

### Mandatory context and robustness analyses

Without outcome-driven promotion, report and repeat the same selectors/weights for:

- post value restricted to the event `就诊号`, expected to be nearly universal;
- nearest event+48-hour value in `(event, +72h]`;
- first valid value after event within 72 hours;
- calendar days +1–3, excluding day 0;
- preprocedure windows 3 and 14 days;
- exact strict ontology versus the parent’s prespecified broad arterial-embolization sensitivity, while leaving baseline TACE2 fixed;
- excluding selected repeat events after 2024;
- excluding any later systemic ontology record between TACE2 day 15 and the repeat procedure;
- strata by event day 15–59 versus 60–90 and by assay timing ≤24 versus >24 hours, only with at least 20 systemic patients and systemic ESS at least 15;
- negative control: same-assay change from closest day −14 to −8 before the repeat event to the selected preprocedure value, where both exist, using baseline overlap weights. A pre-event arm contrast capable of explaining the post-event contrast falsifies a newly event-associated interpretation.

Restrictions on later systemic records, event timing, or observation are selected-population sensitivities and can induce additional selection. They are not direct effects, as-treated effects, or per-protocol estimates.

## Gates and falsification

1. **Reconstruction:** reproduce every frozen flow and 319/1,491 arms; unresolved mismatch invalidates the experiment.
2. **Event support:** reproduce approximately 159/526 selected first events and disclose the 14.56-percentage-point selection difference. Fewer than 120 systemic selected events makes all event-incidence adjustment exploratory; fewer than 50 systemic exact-clock pairs makes assay contrasts descriptive only.
3. **Clock integrity:** event timestamps, strict pre/post ordering, 72-hour boundary and deterministic tie breaks must pass. Same-timestamp values are never assigned pre or post.
4. **Overlap/balance:** both arms retain support; no year dominates; all core post-weight baseline absolute SMDs ≤0.10. Otherwise descriptive/inconclusive.
5. **Selection/observation:** poor event/pair-model calibration, extreme probabilities, combined-weight systemic ESS <40 or >20% estimate change under reasonable truncation makes the arm contrast inconclusive. Passing does not remove unmeasured collider bias.
6. **Precision:** systemic baseline-overlap ESS <45, total ESS <180, or intervals spanning materially favorable and adverse raw-scale effects makes the assay inconclusive. Clinical importance cannot be automated because units/margins are unavailable.
7. **Recovery:** fewer than 40/120 triplets or systemic ESS <30 makes recovery unavailable. The audit already triggers this gate; no result may be called recovery, reversibility, chronic injury or preserved reserve.
8. **Outcome coherence:** favorable albumin with clearly worse bilirubin or vice versa is mixed/adverse, not supportive.
9. **Timing/ontology:** reversal under locked 24/48-hour, first-post, pre-window or strict/broad checks establishes timing instability. Do not select a favorable result.
10. **Negative control:** a comparable directional pre-event contrast falsifies emergence around repeat TACE; null does not prove exchangeability or TACE attribution.
11. **Context:** procedure-admission linkage is definitional in this event-selected design. Do not infer why repeat TACE occurred or that TACE caused the assay change without adjudication.

## Result branches

- **Supportive but tightly bounded:** reconstruction, clock, overlap, balance, observation and precision gates pass; systemic-record patients have a smaller albumin decline without larger bilirubin rise; timing checks agree; and the pre-event negative control cannot explain the contrast. This supports only a favorable immediate biochemical association among patients selected for and observed around repeat TACE.
- **Adverse:** gates pass but systemic-record patients have greater albumin decline and/or bilirubin rise with coherent robustness checks. This argues against assuming a favorable event-proximal profile; it does not prove medication toxicity or TACE injury.
- **Precise null:** clinically reviewed intervals are narrow on verified source units, both assays agree, and computational gates pass. Report no detectable event-proximal difference, not equivalence or equal safety. In this snapshot units are absent, so automated clinical equivalence is unavailable.
- **Selection-sensitive:** baseline-overlap and event/pair-weighted analyses materially differ, selection models are unstable, or calendar/event support differs. Report that event/observation selection governs inference; do not prefer the favorable estimator.
- **Methodologically falsified:** pre-event negative control reproduces the contrast, timestamps fail, or results reverse across locked selectors/ontology. Reject a newly repeat-TACE-associated interpretation.
- **Inconclusive:** sparse pairs, low ESS, wide intervals, nonpositivity, discordant assays, unknown units, or unmeasured event-selection rationale prevents a stable conclusion.
- **Recovery unavailable:** expected branch; triplet gate fails. State that the database observes inpatient stress but not recovery.

## Evidence limits, required adjudication, and verifier scope

Medication rows are records/orders, not verified administrations; dose, adherence, regimen intent and continuation are unknown. Procedure text/time proves a recorded procedure row, not technical details, embolized territory, selectivity, drug/contrast dose, complications, rationale or success. The snapshot lacks complete imaging/tumor burden and response, reliable performance status, Child-Pugh components, outside-care capture, clinical rationale for repeat TACE, adjudicated toxicity, laboratory units, exogenous albumin/fluid administration adjudication, and complete vital status. Encounter linkage does not prove acute severity. Stronger claims require unit/reference-range validation, medication administration and inpatient fluid/albumin exposure, interventional-radiology details, serial post-discharge labs, imaging and tumor response, reasons for retreatment, complications, outside records and mortality linkage; a causal strategy study also requires time-varying severity and decision covariates with an explicit longitudinal estimand.

An automatic verifier can check exact source counts, joins, frozen arms, event selection, timestamps, assay parsing/selectors, denominators, observation models, weights, balance, ESS, intervals, negative controls, sensitivities and whether conclusions follow computed branches. It cannot determine why repeat TACE occurred, whether systemic drugs were taken, whether assay changes are clinically important on unavailable units, whether TACE caused them, recovery, toxicity, response, progression, safety or benefit. Those require clinical adjudication or another study.
