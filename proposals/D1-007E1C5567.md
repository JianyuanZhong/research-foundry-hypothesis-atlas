## Hypothesis to test

In acute heart failure, outcomes differ between patients whose creatinine rises while congestion improves and those whose creatinine rises with persistent congestion.

## Study population and main data

Patients with acute heart failure; fluid input/output, oxygen therapy, and creatinine.

## Suggested approach

Jointly analyze decongestion and creatinine changes and compare subsequent outcomes.

## Scientific significance

Distinguish different patterns of kidney-function change during treatment.

## Main limitations

Define congestion using multiple indicators; findings cannot establish that a creatinine increase is harmless.
