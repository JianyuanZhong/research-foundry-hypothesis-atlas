# Episode 2: measurement and endpoint validity refinement for the HCC post-TACE trajectory

Status: concrete successor design; planned Harbor experiment, not an executed analysis.

## Disposition and advance over the parent

The parent candidate remains the scientific starting point and its primary question is frozen:

> Among adults with HCC and a first observed TACE, does a 0–7-day post-TACE laboratory trajectory add prognostic information beyond baseline for a new decompensation-coded admission during days 8–90 after index discharge?

This branch does not change the population, time-zero definition, feature window, outcome window, or baseline comparison. It makes the interpretation testable by requiring two validity separations before calling a trajectory clinically meaningful:

1. assay/measurement validity: an apparent change must be traceable to a stable assay-name definition and must retain information after observation count, timing, encounter density and missingness are modeled; and
2. endpoint validity: the event must be a new post-discharge admission with a high-specificity decompensation component, not merely a diagnosis string, any readmission, repeat procedure or increased care intensity.

The parent’s bounded counts (17,944 broad first TACE-like procedures, 6,002 later admissions, 171 broad decompensation-coded encounters and 58 by day 30) are feasibility diagnostics only. They are not results and must be recomputed after the final cohort and endpoint rules.

### Three demonstrations

[K1] Sanai et al., *Predictors and outcomes of hepatic decompensation following transarterial locoregional therapy in hepatocellular carcinoma* (Scientific Reports, 2026) is retained as the clinical anchor: it supports the importance of decompensation and baseline-reserve framing. Its inspected evidence does not establish that routine 0–7-day trajectories predict later coded admission, and it does not remove the need for adjudication.

[K2] Xu et al., *Development and validation of a nomogram model for predicting acute hepatic dysfunction post-intervention in primary hepatocellular carcinoma* (World Journal of Surgical Oncology, 2025) is retained as the closest baseline-comparator precedent. It motivates a baseline-only model but is not reproduced: its acute dysfunction label, internal validation and feature/unit context do not answer the later decompensation question in this HCC source.

[K3] You et al., *Comparison of liver injury and inflammatory response following conventional and drug-eluting bead transcatheter chemoembolization in hepatocellular carcinoma* (Discover Oncology, 2025) is retained to motivate an early, approximately day-5 injury window and the AST/ALT-flare rival. It does not establish later decompensation prediction or distinguish biological injury from measurement intensity.

The inspected source material for all three works is abstract-only frozen evidence inherited unchanged from the parent. The parent’s key-references.json and three UTF-8 excerpts must be attached unchanged to the published child; no inaccessible full text or supplement is claimed.

## Scientific opening, rival explanations and exact claim

The strongest supported claim is narrower than the hypothesis: baseline reserve and acute post-intervention laboratory injury are clinically relevant in prior work, while their incremental relationship to later decompensation-coded care in this longitudinal source is unresolved.

Leading explanation: coordinated early deterioration in synthetic/renal reserve precedes clinically meaningful decompensation. The competing explanations are:

- baseline severity: the apparent trajectory is redundant with pre-TACE reserve and prior disease burden;
- observation/utilization: patients near an admission are measured more often, earlier and across more encounters, creating a trajectory artifact;
- assay representation: different local assays or qualitative/numeric mixtures create non-comparable changes;
- transient injury: AST/ALT rises are expected after embolization and do not imply later decompensation;
- endpoint ascertainment: diagnosis coding or documentation density makes a later “event” a utilization/recording artifact.

The experiment can test incremental prognostic/descriptive regularity and its robustness to these artifacts. It cannot establish that TACE caused decompensation, that the latent state is a biological mechanism, or that acting on a predicted risk improves outcomes.

## Exact population and temporal estimand

Use only HCC. The index is each patient's first observed procedure per patient whose \`手术\` contains one of the parent’s TACE-like terms (TACE, 肝动脉化疗栓塞, 经导管肝动脉栓塞, 动脉化疗栓塞), with parseable \`开始时间\` and a matching \`就诊号\` in encounters. Require age >=18 from encounters. Require an HCC diagnosis before or on the index encounter using transparent rules on \`诊断名称\` (at minimum 肝细胞癌 or 肝恶性肿瘤). Report an explicit-chemotherapy/TACE sensitivity cohort excluding bland-embolization wording.

Exclude any prior or index encounter with the parent’s high-specificity decompensation screen: 腹水, 肝性脑病, 消化道出血, 食管胃底静脉曲张, 肝肾综合征, 急性肝衰竭, 肝功能衰竭, 自发性腹膜炎 or 黄疸. Report, but do not silently use as exclusion, a broader 肝硬化失代偿 flag. Missing laboratory data never excludes a patient.

Time zero is the earliest matched index \`手术.开始时间\`. Baseline is the closest usable assay-specific laboratory result in [-14, 0] days before time zero; [-30, 0] is a prespecified sensitivity window. Features may use only [0, 7] days after time zero. The primary outcome clock is [8, 90] days after time zero and also must be after the index encounter’s \`出院时间\`; the eligible event is the first distinct post-discharge encounter with an admission time in that interval. If discharge is missing, report the case and do not silently treat an encounter-date string as discharge. Index and same-admission rows are not new admissions.

The estimand is the paired, temporal-test-set difference in calibrated prognostic performance between a baseline-only model and the dynamic model for the binary composite outcome. Secondary estimands are the same difference for days 8–30 and for each endpoint component. This is not a treatment effect.

## Assay-measurement validity protocol

The labs table has no separate unit column. Therefore the solver must not pool numeric thresholds across assays, compute a clinically valid ALBI/MELD/Child-Pugh score, or call a numeric change a physiologic reserve change without external assay verification.

Before fitting, freeze an assay dictionary from the observed \`检验\` values. Candidate parent names requiring audit include 总胆红素, 直接胆红素, 白蛋白, 血小板计数, 凝血酶原时间, 凝血酶原时间比值, 肌酐, 钠, 丙氨酸氨基转移酶, 天门冬氨酸氨基转移酶/谷草转氨酶 and 甲胎蛋白. The dictionary must record exact normalized name, raw-name variants, numeric-versus-qualitative coverage, specimen type, timestamp coverage, duplicate rate and whether an expert-confirmed unit mapping exists. The candidate list is not a claim that each assay is present for every patient.

Use only parseable numeric \`定量结果\` for numeric trajectory features; retain \`定性结果\` as a separate missing/qualitative indicator and never coerce it to a number. For an assay with no verified unit, use within-assay training-set standardized values and within-patient changes/ranks only; do not compare raw values across names, hospitals, periods or specimen types. If a clinically verified unit mapping becomes available, prespecify a unit-harmonized sensitivity analysis but retain the unit-agnostic primary analysis. Same-day duplicate results are retained for audit; the primary aggregation uses the result nearest a fixed daily grid (days 0, 1–2, 3–4, 5–7) with an explicit deterministic tie rule, while a sensitivity analysis uses all timestamped observations in the state-space model.

Report, before model metrics, assay coverage at baseline and in days 0–7, unit/qualitative ambiguity, missingness, number of unique assay names, duplicate observations, out-of-range/non-numeric exclusions and whether trajectory direction can be clinically named. If the audit cannot establish comparability for a proposed panel, the output may say only “assay-indexed trajectory”; it must not say “hepatic reserve loss.”

## Utilization and observation-process controls

Construct these from fields available before outcome ascertainment:

- baseline utilization: counts of encounters, admissions, distinct departments, procedures, medications, orders, examinations and laboratory observations in [-90,0) and [-30,0);
- early observation process: number of unique lab timestamps, number of distinct encounters with any lab, number of distinct lab assays, first post-TACE lab lag, last observed day, counts in days 0–2, 3–4 and 5–7, and number of encounters/clinical-document rows in each bin;
- index intensity/context: index admission length when both 入院时间 and 出院时间 parse, procedure source, procedure duration when available, department, and medication/order counts in [0,7].

These variables are controls and diagnostics, not evidence that utilization causes decompensation. Fit a measurement-only model using observation-process variables; add the same controls to the baseline and dynamic models; and report a common-support analysis requiring at least one baseline and one day-3–7 lab. Missingness indicators remain features. A sensitivity analysis stratifies by observation density and uses a pattern-mixture analysis; inverse observation weighting may be reported only if its weights are estimated in the training split and overlap is shown.

The key validity comparison is: trajectory increment without utilization controls, trajectory increment with controls, and the same comparison after restricting to common observation support. A trajectory signal that vanishes in the latter two comparisons is classified as utilization-compatible, not clinically meaningful.

## Endpoint validity protocol

Primary composite: a first distinct post-discharge encounter with admission time in days 8–90 and a high-specificity decompensation flag in diagnoses. Component flags are mutually reported, not silently collapsed: ascites (腹水), encephalopathy (肝性脑病), gastrointestinal/variceal bleeding (消化道出血 or 食管胃底静脉曲张 with bleeding context), renal/hepatic failure (肝肾综合征, 急性肝衰竭 or 肝功能衰竭), and jaundice (黄疸). Define the composite as any component, retain component-specific first dates, and report the number with one versus multiple components.

The primary lexical endpoint uses 诊断名称 only with transparent normalized exact/substring rules frozen before modeling. A sensitivity endpoint requires the same component in at least one of 入院诊断, 出院诊断, 入院情况, 出院情况 or 诊疗经过 in clinical_documents for the same (患者主索引, 就诊号). Report diagnosis-only positive, document-confirmed positive, discordant, and missing-document strata. A code/document string is a screening phenotype, not a validated diagnosis.

Do not label any admission unplanned: the source has no validated planned/unplanned field. Report repeat TACE-like procedure in days 8–90 separately, and report any admission separately. If the signal occurs only for any admission or repeat TACE, it is a utilization/treatment-pathway association, not evidence for decompensation.

The solver must produce an adjudication packet for a blinded clinician sample stratified by composite positive, component, diagnosis/document discordance and negative controls. Adjudication should determine new/worsening decompensation, planned status, temporal relation to TACE, competing illness/HCC progression and whether the text supports the component. The computational experiment cannot establish these clinical facts.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

Negative controls are diagnostics, not automatic proof of causality:

1. Within-patient permutation of post-TACE lab timestamps preserves values, counts and missingness but destroys temporal ordering. Dynamic improvement that survives is evidence for value/measurement intensity rather than ordered trajectory.
2. A pre-index pseudo-window (days -7 to 0) with a matched prediction horizon tests whether the same “post-TACE” signal exists before treatment. Similar performance weakens a post-TACE interpretation.
3. A measurement-only model and the unadjusted-versus-adjusted comparison test whether lab/visit density explains the increment.
4. Repeat TACE and any-admission outcomes test specificity. Stronger association only for these outcomes is not decompensation evidence.
5. AST/ALT-only versus reserve-panel-only blocks test the expected transient-injury rival. AST/ALT-only signal without reserve-panel or endpoint specificity should be classified as flare/utilization-compatible.

Supportive result: the reserve-panel trajectory improves held-out calibration/discrimination over the prespecified baseline comparator with uncertainty excluding a practically negligible increment; the increment persists after observation controls, common-support restriction, timestamp permutation does not reproduce it, the pre-index pseudo-window is weak, and document-confirmed/component patterns are directionally consistent. This supports an incremental prognostic regularity, not mechanism or treatment causality.

Adverse result: the increment disappears after assay audit, controls or common-support restriction; survives timestamp permutation; is reproduced in the pre-index pseudo-window; is driven only by AST/ALT; or predicts any admission/repeat TACE without component specificity. This supports measurement, selection, transient injury or utilization explanations and should revise or abandon a “reserve-loss” interpretation.

Inconclusive result: too few events, split imbalance, poor assay coverage, unstable endpoint concordance, wide paired intervals, or failed state-space convergence. Preserve the estimand and report uncertainty; defer clinical interpretation to adjudication or a linked/external cohort rather than calling it a null.

Computationally checkable claims are cohort rules, joins, timestamps, assay handling, model fitting, held-out metrics, uncertainty and negative-control behavior. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for true decompensation, planned status, causal TACE injury, biological reserve mechanism, mortality benefit, safe action thresholds and external validity. Missing evidence includes verified assay units, imaging/tumor burden, embolized territory and drug/dose, ECOG, portal pressure, death linkage, external validation and prospective adjudication.

## Unchosen alternatives and revisit criteria

A transformer/neural sequence model remains deferred because the immediate scientific uncertainty is whether the observed increment survives assay and utilization controls; its additional temporal capacity would not resolve that validity question. Revisit it only if the state-space/grouped model is adequate, outcome support is larger, assay names are harmonized, and an independent temporal or external test set is available. GPU use is not prohibited but is unnecessary for this tabular state-space workload. A simple baseline remains mandatory because it establishes whether complexity adds clinically interpretable information rather than a small predictive gain.
