## Question, supported claim and falsifiable hypothesis

The inspected evidence supports an association between frailty/handgrip-related reserve and mortality or perioperative vulnerability in colorectal-cancer patients [K1, K2], and supports separating CRC-specific from non-CRC mortality with competing-risk methods [K3]. It does not establish that a prediagnostic, objectively measured reserve profile distinguishes durable host vulnerability from occult cancer decline.

Hypothesis: among incident C18-C20 cases, lower measured reserve at least two years before diagnosis is associated with greater five-year cumulative incidence of non-cancer death than CRC-specific death after adjustment for age, sex, BMI, smoking, calendar time, comorbidity and CRC subsite; adding reserve improves locked-test non-cancer risk prediction over the same routine-data baseline. This is a prognostic/descriptive association hypothesis, not a causal treatment-tolerance, stage, recurrence or mechanism claim.

The leading rival is timing/selection: measurements close to diagnosis may capture occult cancer/cachexia, and UKB volunteer selection or missingness may create the signal. Lag strata, cause-specific comparisons, missingness/selection analyses and an external-cause negative-control endpoint can bound those rivals but cannot establish mechanism.

## Falsification and interpretation

Repeat the analysis in 0-<2, 2-<5 and >=5 years. A signal confined to 0-<2 or absent at >=5 is adverse to durable prediagnostic reserve and supports occult disease/timing.

Compare CRC-specific, other-cancer, non-cancer and unknown-cause patterns. Support requires a persistent >=2-year non-cancer association not reproduced for CRC death, stable under cause sensitivities, calibrated held-out improvement over B0, compatible uncertainty, persistence at >=5, no material selection instability and no matching external-cause signal. This establishes at most a prognostic regularity and motivates external validation/prospective measurement; it does not establish mechanism, causality, treatment tolerance or a treatment decision.

Adverse results include no incremental information, reversal of the differential contrast, CRC-dominant signal, timing-only signal, matching external-cause association, coding/order instability or materially divergent complete-case/imputed/weighted results. Report the corresponding rival explanation.

Inconclusive results include undocumented `tau_source`, sparse events, wide clinically important intervals, severe missingness, cause instability, failed calibration or unavailable B0+D. Do not treat an imprecise null as refutation; defer to verified longer follow-up, registry linkage, expert cause adjudication or an independent cohort. Also report duplicate eids, paired-record discordance, impossible orderings, ties, unknown causes and counts by source; repeat inpatient diagnosis parsing where feasible, compare with naive censoring, and run locked fixed exposure/outcome permutation probes for leakage.
