# Decision-proximal documented workflow trajectories after first recorded HCC admission

## Successor decision and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the incumbent's strict-prior assay-content-versus-capture-intensity question and its fixed later-era calendar-shift test, but changes the primary recorded-care target from a generic procedure/discharge transition to a more decision-proximal, timestamp-defined workflow trajectory.

The new deliverable is a locked, held-out estimate of whether pre-admission assay content adds information about the next documented workflow state at a 24-hour inpatient landmark:

- **O**: a first valid procedure start in [t24,t72) is preceded by at least one nonblank non-drug order opened in [t24, procedure start);
- **P**: a first valid procedure start occurs in that window, but no such order is documented before it;
- **D**: discharge is recorded after t24 and by t72 before any established procedure;
- **N**: no procedure is recorded in the window and discharge is after t72;
- **U**: invalid or missing native procedure timing could change the state.

O is a documentary coordination state, not intent, recommendation, scheduling, receipt, completion, indication, treatment choice, benefit, or clinical truth. The primary definition does not interpret order or procedure text. A separately gated same-family order/procedure concordance analysis asks whether the O/P distinction is more specific when a frozen lexical family match is available; it is not allowed to rescue a failed primary analysis.

Completion requires newly fitting the frozen transparent models and the irregular learned alternative, producing patient-disjoint random and calendar-held-out predictions for all five primary states, paired log-loss/Brier contrasts for V-minus-I and I-minus-Q, calibration and clustered uncertainty, data-flow/timestamp audits, falsification outputs, and a claim-to-output table. A positive result is not required.

## Unresolved question, evidence-supported claim, and hypothesis

Available evidence supports only that EHR assay results, assay identity, and capture intensity can encode information about subsequent locally recorded care. The HCC files do not establish active HCC, stage, tumor burden, liver reserve, indication, intent, receipt, completion, response, toxicity, survival, benefit, or utility. The research-ambition materials support considering dated structured-history learning as an adaptation, but do not establish results in this HCC snapshot; the cancer demonstration's main article and complete STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not treated as inspected.

The unresolved clinical-information question is whether actual prior assay content remains informative once the endpoint is moved closer to a coordination decision: the distinction between an admission that progresses to a documented procedure after an order is opened and one in which a procedure is recorded without a preceding order. This can inform whether a future coordination tool should model result content in addition to record density and assay identity, while acknowledging that the observed state may reflect documentation conventions rather than care intent.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among adults at their first locally recorded HCC-coded inpatient admission who remain in the recorded inpatient risk set at 24 hours, strictly prior assay-specific results and within-assay trajectories improve calibrated prediction of the five-state documented workflow trajectory O/P/D/N/U beyond admission context, early current-admission laboratory information, prior capture intensity, and prior assay identity/occurrence; the V-minus-I increment remains directionally present and calibrated in the locked 2021–2025 calendar era.

This retains the incumbent's V-minus-I estimand and the calendar-shift test. The substantive advance is that the target explicitly captures the documented order-to-procedure transition, rather than treating all procedures as equivalent terminal events. It tests decision proximity, not clinical efficacy.

Support requires, on the locked later-era set, a prespecified V-minus-I reduction in multiclass log loss with a 95% patient-clustered interval below zero, no material worsening of Brier score or calibration, and the same direction on the random patient-held-out set. The V increment must attenuate under within-assay value/result permutation and not be reproduced by I or Q. A learned model is supportive only if its matched S_V-minus-S_I contrast follows the same gates; a learned model cannot convert an unsupported baseline contrast into support.

Adverse evidence is a null or reversed later-era V-minus-I contrast, material miscalibration, a comparable I/Q gain, persistence after within-assay value permutation, or a target association reproduced after order-time scrambling. This would argue that assay content is not robustly informative for the documented workflow state or that the signal is process artifact. Inconclusive evidence includes fewer than 200 eligible admissions in a calendar partition, fewer than 20 examples of any nonempty O/P/D/N state, more than 50% U labels, unstable fitting, wide intervals, inadequate timed procedures/orders, or a failed semantic secondary gate. These dispositions do not establish or refute clinical usefulness.

## Population, joins, and temporal safety

Use HCC snapshot `[source checksum]` and catalog [source checksum]. All HCC sources are read-only ordinary CSV files; no archive member is used.

Join `diagnoses` to `encounters` on the exact pair (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). Normalize Unicode and surrounding whitespace only, retain duplicate and unmatched-key audits, and define an HCC record code as normalized `诊断名称` equal to `肝细胞癌` or containing the case-insensitive English phrase hepatocellular carcinoma. This is a reproducible record-code rule, not an adjudicated diagnosis.

For each patient, sort HCC-coded encounters by native `入院时间`, then `就诊号`. Retain the earliest HCC-coded encounter only if `年龄` is >=18 and <120, `入院时间` and `出院时间` parse, discharge is not before admission, and admission is in [2011-01-01, 2026-01-01). If the earliest HCC-coded record has invalid timing, exclude that patient rather than substituting a later record. Keep early discharges in the audit but do not place them in the 24-hour primary risk set.

Set t0=`入院时间`, t24=t0+24 hours and t72=t0+72 hours. Primary predictor windows are half-open:

- strict prior: [t0-730 days, t0);
- early current labs: [t0,t0+12 hours).

A history row is eligible only when its native event time is valid, lies in the strict-prior interval, and its linked encounter has native `入院时间` strictly before t0. This applies to labs, procedures, orders and medications. Anchor-linked rows with a pre-t0 event timestamp but a non-prior or invalid linked encounter are excluded from primary history and retained as a labelled sensitivity branch. Report invalid times, duplicate/multiplicity, unmatched keys, row inflation, boundary ties, after-discharge rows, left truncation and no-history counts.

The primary 24-hour risk set contains only encounters with parseable `出院时间` strictly after t24 and no established procedure in [t0,t24). If any procedure row linked to the encounter has missing/invalid `开始时间` and could change whether a procedure occurred before t24, classify that encounter as U0 and exclude it from the primary risk set; retain it in the unresolved-time sensitivity. This avoids conditioning on an unobservable absence. Procedure starts in [t0,t24) are excluded from the primary risk set because the question concerns the subsequent workflow trajectory, not an outcome already observed by the landmark.

For each risk-set encounter, inspect procedures and orders only after t24 for the target, with native times and the same encounter key. The first valid procedure start p in [t24,t72) and at or before discharge determines the procedure branch. O requires at least one nonblank `医嘱(非药品)` row on the same (`患者主索引`,`就诊号`) with parseable `开立时间` satisfying t24 <= `开立时间` < p. The order's `开始时间`, `结束时间`, `医嘱状态` and `频次` are retained for audit but are not required to define O; O means only that an order was recorded before the procedure. P is the complementary procedure branch when no such order is documented. An order-procedure exact-time tie is not concordant because the inequality is strict.

If discharge is parseable and t24 < discharge <= t72, and no established valid procedure precedes it in [t24,t72), assign D. If discharge >t72 and no procedure is recorded in the window, assign N. If a missing/invalid procedure time can change first-procedure ordering, window membership, or procedure-versus-discharge ordering, assign U rather than N or D. Apply procedure-wins and discharge-wins precedence to exact ties as fixed primary/sensitivity analyses. Report multiple procedures, procedure starts at midnight, procedures before admission, after discharge, window boundaries, missing procedure times, missing order-open times, and order/procedure ties.

For comparability, also construct the incumbent's exhaustive admission-anchored transition vector from procedure `开始时间` and encounter admission/discharge times as a secondary analysis. The new O/P/D/N/U target is primary; no result may be generalized from it to procedure choice or treatment.

Patient-disjoint random splitting is fixed by SHA-256 of normalized `患者主索引` modulo 100: 0–59 fit, 60–69 selection/preprocessing/calibration, 70–79 locked evaluation, 80–99 inaccessible. All rows for a patient remain together and all dictionaries, scaling, feature rules and calibration are fit only on permitted data.

The calendar-shift evaluation is fixed before fitting: development 2012–2018, selection 2019–2020, locked later-era 2021–2025. Report cross-era patient overlap and exclude overlapping patients from the temporal estimand. The temporal analysis is inconclusive if a partition has <200 eligible risk-set admissions, <20 examples of any nonempty O/P/D state, >50% U labels, or if any essential model fit is unstable. Do not move the boundary post hoc. 2011 is descriptive only.

## Information sets and matched methods

All models use the same risk set, primary O/P/D/N/U labels, preprocessing policy and split.

- **A**: age, sex, admitting department, t0 year/era, admission time-of-day, admission missingness and strict-history availability.
- **L**: A plus assay-specific summaries of labs `检验`, `定性结果`, `定量结果`, `标本类型`, `检验时间` in [t0,t0+12h): first/last valid numeric result, change, elapsed time, count, density/missingness and slope only with two distinct valid times, plus assay-specific qualitative results.
- **Q**: L plus strict-prior capture/process summaries: encounter/source counts, duration/active days, recency, row and distinct-encounter counts for labs/procedures/orders/medications, source-type counts, missing-time counts, left-truncation and no-history indicators. Q excludes assay names, results, values, procedure/order/medication names, doses, routes, frequencies and statuses.
- **I**: Q plus prior lab assay identity/occurrence summaries (assay identity, assay-specific count, recency, span, missingness and number of distinct assays), without qualitative or quantitative results.
- **V**: I plus strict-prior assay-specific `定性结果` and `定量结果`, including dated within-assay changes/slopes when at least two distinct valid times exist. Numeric scaling is fit-only within assay; no cross-assay pooling or unvalidated physiologic score is allowed.

The simple auditable baseline is regularized multinomial logistic regression for A/L/Q/I/V, with a frozen imputation/indicator policy, fit-only vocabulary and scaling, and selection-only regularization/calibration. It directly estimates the nested V-minus-I and I-minus-Q contrasts and is the adequacy floor.

The scientifically substantive alternative is a matched irregular continuous-time marked-event encoder. It consumes exactly the permitted rows in each information set, retaining event source/type, elapsed time, assay identity where allowed, payload mask and assay-specific lab payload; it uses a fixed low-dimensional decayed state with a target-classification head and a masked-payload reconstruction term fit only on the training partition. Predeclare latent dimension 32, exponential decay family, shrinkage, reconstruction weight, three random seeds and selection-only tuning. S_I removes prior lab values/results but retains assay identity; S_Q removes assay identity and values while retaining process/timing. Evaluate the same five-state target, same split, held-out log loss/Brier, calibration, and patient-clustered uncertainty. This alternative can reveal order-sensitive irregular trajectories, nonlinear interactions and missingness patterns that first/last/count summaries discard. It is scientifically useful only if it is seed-stable, calibrated and reproduces a nested content contrast beyond S_Q; a small predictive gain alone is not a reason to prefer it.

A mechanistic ALBI/MELD-like branch is deferred for this question because HCC labs have no separate unit column or validated reference ranges, and the catalog lacks stage, tumor burden, pathology timing and a clinically reviewed assay map. It would not be an honest mechanistic test of liver reserve under these sources. Revisit only with expert-provided units/reference ranges and clinical assay adjudication.

## Secondary order/procedure family estimand

After the primary target is frozen, normalize only fit-frozen, auditable substrings in procedures `手术` and orders `医嘱(非药品)` into embolization/TACE (E), ablation (A), resection/transplant (S), diagnostic/access (X), and other/ambiguous. Preserve blanks, multi-matches and ambiguous strings. A family-concordant trajectory requires the first procedure and at least one order from the same family, same encounter key, with parseable order `开立时间` strictly before procedure `开始时间`, and the order opened no earlier than t24. This is documentary concordance, not intent or treatment receipt.

The secondary gate requires >=100 uniquely family-assigned procedure admissions, >=80% of timed target procedures nonblank and exactly-one family, >=70% of candidate family orders with parseable `开立时间`, no family >90% of concordant events, and <=25% of concordant labels dependent on midnight/tie or unresolved timing. If it fails, report it as inconclusive and retain the semantics-independent primary analysis. The secondary estimand is the V-minus-I contrast for family-specific O/P trajectories, evaluated only on its prespecified gated subset.

## Analysis, uncertainty, and falsification

For both random and calendar-held-out evaluations, report paired V-minus-I and I-minus-Q differences in multiclass log loss and Brier score, state-specific observed/predicted probabilities, calibration intercept/slope and reliability plots. Use at least 1,000 patient-clustered paired bootstrap resamples, preserving all rows for sampled patients and all model pairings. Report capture-intensity strata fixed from fit data, no-history/1–2/>=3 prior encounter strata, 30/180/730-day history sensitivities, and era strata.

Required falsifications are:

1. within-patient and fit-only assay-stratum permutation of prior values/results, preserving assay identity and timing;
2. assay-identity permutation, preserving capture counts;
3. prior-history time permutation preserving counts and payloads;
4. replacement of strict-prior rows with anchor-linked rows;
5. exclusion of the t0-30-day portion of history as a negative-control timing branch;
6. shuffled patient IDs before splitting;
7. forbidden future-window predictors explicitly labelled leakage;
8. procedure-wins versus discharge-wins tie precedence;
9. order-open-time permutation within encounter, preserving order counts and procedure times;
10. target reclassification after coarsening all times to date or dropping midnight-start procedures.

If an order-time scramble preserves O/P performance, the documented workflow label is not being meaningfully identified by temporal ordering. If Q or I matches V after value permutation, the content claim is adverse. If the later-era V-minus-I contrast reverses or loses calibration while the random split is positive, calendar robustness is adverse. If missing timing, small states, or unstable fits prevent these checks, report inconclusive rather than selecting a favorable subset.

Computationally checkable claims are cohort construction, exact join and row-inflation audits, risk-set eligibility, time-window safety, label precedence, feature nesting, held-out scores, calibration, bootstrap intervals, permutation behavior, and whether the calendar gates pass. Active disease, intent, indication, procedure receipt/completion, treatment benefit, clinical utility, safety, biologic mechanism and external transportability require chart review, missing evidence, prospective validation or another study.

## Compute, selection, and revisit record

The future solver planning envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB RAM, 8 allocated GPUs and 28,800 seconds. The run-level discovery budget is 7,200 science seconds. The 2.2 GB orders and 2.21 GB labs are expected to require chunked processing; these times are planning estimates, not measurements. The transparent fits and locked predictions are expected to fit on CPU. The irregular alternative is planned CPU-first for 2–8 hours; GPU use is optional, not a scientific requirement, and would require an allocated probe in the cached `ehr-campaign-gpu:20260908` image confirming imports and a one-device tensor pass. No GPU probe was run in this discovery episode.

The chosen alternative is the irregular continuous-time encoder because the scientific uncertainty is whether assay values and their timing add information beyond identity and capture, and summary regression can lose irregular order, nonlinear interactions and informative missingness. A mechanistic score was deferred for the verified absence of units/reference ranges and clinical assay map. A simple sequence transformer was not selected separately: the event encoder tests the same ordering question with a smaller, auditable state and bounded CPU-first envelope. Revisit the transformer or mechanistic branch only if the encoder is unstable, fails calibration, or expert review supplies the missing clinical mapping. The alternative is not selected for solver difficulty or a small predictive improvement.

## Evidence boundary

A bounded read-only audit used the full encounters and diagnoses files and full linked procedures and orders files, plus schema/header and catalog inspection; it did not fit models or scan all laboratory rows. It found 42,583 eligible first HCC patients before the new 24-hour risk-set restrictions; cohort years were 2011:3, 2012:426, 2013:2100, 2014:1790, 2015:2613, 2016:2351, 2017:1855, 2018:2260, 2019:2334, 2020:2552, 2021:3431, 2022:3208, 2023:4987, 2024:6475, 2025:6198. It found 145,811 linked procedure rows with valid starts and 7,793 missing/invalid starts; 85,238 valid starts were at 00:00. In the provisional [t0+24h,t0+72h) window, 50,950 valid procedure rows occurred across 18,459 admissions. Under a provisional substring dictionary, 31.1% of rows had a preceding same-family order, 58.3% had no preceding classified order, and 10.6% had only another-family orders. These are feasibility observations, not final labels or scientific results.

The audit also found 9,017,539 linked order rows with parseable `开立时间` in the scanned cohort and no missing opening time in that bounded scan. This does not remove the requirement for the solver's complete audit or establish that orders represent plans. The common order terms include transfers and laboratory/diagnostic orders, which is why the primary endpoint uses nonblank order timing without claiming semantic intent and the family endpoint is gated.
