# A departure-grounded and longitudinally falsifiable support-atlas audit for pulmonary discharge routing

## Decision bottleneck and substantive advance

This is a targeted successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC pulmonary population; reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum chronology; one canonical discharge artifact; exhaustive departure, availability, lag, and eventual states; unit-specific clocks; sealed split evidence views; independent all-route auditing; exact possible-calendar/terminal-service/workflow support atoms; pre-label deterministic atlas pruning; maximin positive-probability sampling; all-unit worst-admission routing; adverse finite-population inference; a distinct-admission ceiling of 900; default-`DEFER` and no-rescue governance; and the prospective silent-bridge-only conclusion.

The parent's remaining clinically consequential weakness is reference validity. A reproducible route can still treat copied, tentative, already-completed, or superseded text as a live future obligation. A later note can also be mistaken for proof of completion, while its absence can be mistaken for noncompletion. The repair is an independent, route-concealed **departure-status ledger** anchored to source-timed, target-specific relations, plus a strictly secondary longitudinal challenge. The primary anchor asks only whether the supplied pre-departure artifacts contain an explicit, target-grounded, firm future action not contradicted by a documented completion, cancellation, replacement, or competing plan. The secondary challenge asks whether that departure status is compatible with later target/action-consistent documentation, while treating later documentation as an observation process and falsification signal, never as a clinical outcome or gold standard.

This matters for a consequential workflow decision. If a stored discharge route is to be considered for even a silent prospective test, the route must not systematically copy boilerplate, propagate an action already performed, or call a superseded plan `AUTO`. Conversely, a later absent note must not be used to label a recommendation failed. The proposed advance is therefore not more prediction; it is a clinically interpretable, fail-closed boundary between text that is demonstrably target-grounded at departure, text whose status is unresolved, and domains that cannot be supported by the available evidence.

## Evidence ceiling and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest supported claim before this experiment is limited to data availability. The MIMIC snapshot contains admission-linked radiology and discharge text, report/addendum detail relations, within-subject `charttime` and `storetime`, admission departure times, patient anchor-year groups, service chronology, ICU exposure, and recorded death fields. The source catalog reports 546,028 admissions, 364,627 patients, 593,071 service rows, 94,458 ICU stays, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, and 331,794 unique discharge-note admission keys, with 17 missing discharge `storetime` values. These counts establish computability only. They do not establish pulmonary eligibility, route purity, clinical appropriateness, actual viewing, responsibility, completion, safety, benefit, or external validity. MIMIC timestamps are deidentified and cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid.

The unresolved claim is:

> Within a pre-label support-qualified atom, at least one inherited route has a sufficiently small adverse-compatible fraction of target-mismatched, tentative-only, completed-without-next-step, superseded, conflicting, or unknown units that it can be nominated for a prospective silent validation, and the departure-status class shows a prespecified temporal documentation pattern that is not explained by the registered negative controls.

The primary falsifiable hypothesis is that the route's apparent `AUTO`/`RECON` status survives independent target-grounding and departure-status adjudication. The longitudinal hypothesis is deliberately weaker: among the fixed challenged units, `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` units have greater compatible support for a temporally separated target/action-consistent future text than adverse departure classes, after preserving no-future-note, unlinked, censored, copied, and time-unknown states. A null or reversed pattern is a failure of this proposed textual anchor, not evidence that care did not occur.

A supported atom fails if any adverse-compatible unit can be non-active under the primary route gate, if target/control invariance fails, or if the longitudinal challenge is non-supportive when its opportunity and censoring conditions permit interpretation. A rare, unknown, novel, or infeasible atom is unsupported and remains `DEFER`; it is neither a negative clinical finding nor eligible for pooled rescue.

## Independent departure-status ledger

### Pre-label objective provenance ledger

After the inherited sampled admissions and units are frozen, but before any departure-status labels or longitudinal evidence is shown, run a deterministic source-only ledger. Normalize only Unicode form, case, whitespace, punctuation spacing, and deidentification placeholders. Preserve anatomy, laterality, measurements, modality, timing, negation, and modal verbs. Store source text only in access-controlled packets; public/compiled outputs contain hashes, row identities, and coded states.

For every unit record, without using route, atom, outcome, or reviewer labels:

1. exact and token-longest-common-subsequence similarity between reciprocal recommendation text and the DS candidate span;
2. exact recurrence counts across distinct subjects and admission keys;
3. target-grounding tokens, requiring at least one independently preserved anatomy/laterality, measurement, modality, comparison, or interval relation;
4. a frozen DS section coordinate, with `SECTION_UNKNOWN` if headings are absent/ambiguous;
5. all admission-linked radiology rows with `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D` after the recommendation, including reciprocal addenda; and
6. candidate spans anywhere in DS for future, tentative, completed, cancelled, replaced, changed-interval, competing, and contradictory language.

Copying, recurrence, section, or lexical flags are measurements only. None is a favorable clinical label.

### Two blinded status readings

Every admission in the parent's fixed actionability challenge receives exactly two additional whole-admission readings, with no new admissions and no outcome-triggered selection. If the inherited challenge has fewer than 300 admissions, all are read; if it has more than 300, use the parent's frozen first 300 challenge roster selected before this layer. No recommendation unit may be omitted. The two readers are roster-disjoint from all inherited eligibility, index, audit, redaction, safety, and longitudinal roles; they receive independent pseudonyms, independent codebooks, independent orderings, and no shared examples.

Reader A receives an assertion-centered packet containing each source unit and DS target/action spans in local context with times. Reader B receives a trajectory-centered packet containing the reciprocal chain, all other pre-`D` admission-linked radiology artifacts, and the unhighlighted canonical DS. Both see `charttime`, `storetime`, `D`, missingness, and relation fields, but neither sees routes, domains, weights, inherited labels, expected controls, or the other reader.

For each unit, each reader records target relation (`EXACT`, `COMPATIBLE`, `MISMATCH`, `UNKNOWN`), force (`FIRM_FUTURE`, `TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `INFORMATIONAL`, `NEGATED_OR_CANCELLED`, `CONFLICT`, `UNKNOWN`), action/timing/recipient/responsibility as observed/absent/contradicted/unknown, documented pre-`D` action status, replacement status, provenance class, and a compatible status set:

`R_FIRM_ACTIVE`, `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, `R_TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `R_COMPLETED_NO_REMAINING_ACTION`, `R_SUPERSEDED`, `R_INACTIVE_INFORMATIONAL`, `R_CONFLICT`, `R_UNKNOWN`.

`R_FIRM_ACTIVE` requires an explicit target-grounded future action, firm rather than solely tentative language, and no supplied artifact with an explicit matching completion without a remaining future action, cancellation, replacement, or competing plan. It does not assert that anyone should act. `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE` is firm and target-grounded but lacks a resolvable timing, recipient, responsibility, or plan detail. Ungrounded recurrence cannot enter either firm state. No consensus collapses the readers; the primary compatible set is their union plus states allowed by missing/corrupt evidence. Intersection/majority labels are diagnostics only.

### Anchor controls

Insert controls into the packet queue before any real packet is reviewed, without exposing their expected answers. Controls include a copied but target-grounded firm sentence; the same sentence with target attributes swapped; a firm-to-tentative modal change; an inserted matching completion; an inserted changed-interval replacement; and a section/format-only perturbation. Required invariances are that grounded copying preserves grounding, target swap loses it, modal change changes force only, completion/replacement changes status only, and formatting changes nothing. Control failure invalidates the affected reference run and cannot be repaired by consensus.

High-specificity source anchors are classified from fields, not named to readers:

* explicit negative: target-linked cancellation/no-further-action or replacement in DS;
* documented completion: target-linked matching radiology action is documented pre-`D`/pre-storage and DS contains no explicit next future action;
* tentative-only: all target-linked action clauses are conditional/optional and no firm clause exists;
* firm textual: target, future action, firm modal/imperative, and timing/recipient are present and target-grounded, with no contradiction/completion/replacement; and
* copy provenance: exact source-to-DS or cross-subject recurrence, treated as provenance only.

### Primary ledger estimands and gates

Retain all parent estimands and estimate, by exact support atom and inherited route, compatible masses of firm-active, firm-incomplete, tentative, documented-completed, superseded, inactive, conflict, and unknown states; target mismatch; ungrounded recurrence; anchor recognition; control violations; nonresponse; and physician-hours. Use the inherited inclusion probabilities and admission clusters. Design-weighted upper adverse bounds assign every disagreement, unknown, missing artifact, ambiguous target link, parser failure, packet error, and nonresponse to the adverse state.

An `AUTO` atom remains claimable only if all parent gates pass and every candidate-AUTO unit is compatible only with `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`; the simultaneous upper bound on candidate-AUTO/reference discordance is at most the parent's `0.05` contamination margin; recurrent-ungrounded or target-mismatched upper bound is at most `0.02`; occupied anchor recognition lower bounds meet the inherited `0.95` confirmation requirement; each decisive-control violation is at most `0.05`, invariant-control violation at most `0.02`; and reference missingness/nonresponse remains below `0.05` unless the relevant frame is censused. One adverse-compatible completion, supersession, tentative-only, conflict, or unknown unit blocks `AUTO` under worst-unit precedence.

A `RECON` atom requires every unit to be compatible only with `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` or `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, parent route/precision gates, upper inherited/reference discordance at most `0.10`, upper adverse-status mass at most `0.10`, and all anchor/control/missingness/support/workload gates. `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE` can justify reconciliation, never `AUTO`. `TIMING` cannot override non-active reference status. Unsupported or failed atoms map to `DEFER`.

### Exact workload contract

The ledger does not add admissions. It adds at most two readings for each admission in the parent's fixed challenge roster, at most 600 compact readings. Before any real label is opened, compute the exact inherited worst-case physician-hour and reviewer-hour charge from the sealed parent roster and the exact packet-unit charge from the ledger rehearsal. The run is permitted only if the additive total is no greater than the parent's frozen absolute ceilings (the protocol's 1,400 physician-hour, 96 reviewer-hour, 6 reviewer-hour/week, and 32-week limits where those ceilings are present). If the parent's compiled ceiling differs or is absent, do not silently substitute these values: record `REFERENCE_INFEASIBLE` and retain only the parent. The count of distinct admissions remains the parent's fixed count and never exceeds 900. No route/event/response/workload-based top-up, replacement, or stopping is allowed.

## Route-concealed longitudinal challenge

This layer is secondary and cannot rescue a failed departure ledger or parent atom. After all inherited and departure-status labels, allocations, weights, and route code are sealed, scan every challenged unit's subject across the complete available `note/discharge` and `note/radiology` files through `D+365` days. The scan is deterministic and includes every challenged unit, including those with no later note. It receives no route, atom, stratum, expected class, or outcome during extraction or reading.

Use only within-subject ordered intervals. For due months, define `calendar_add(D,m)` exactly as: add `m` to the `(year,month)` pair; if the original day exceeds the last day of the resulting month, clamp to that month's last day. The due date is `T=calendar_add(D,m)`. This is the single parent/child date function and is tested on leap days, month ends, and year boundaries. Primary separated windows are `(D,D+30 days]`, `(D+30,D+90 days]`, and `(D+90,D+365 days]`; the index-admission post-`D` window is a leakage diagnostic and is excluded from primary separated persistence. A future row is temporally usable only if both `charttime>D` and `storetime>D`; exactly one missing time is `TEMPORALLY_UNRESOLVED`. Same-subject rows with missing `hadm_id` remain `UNLINKED_FUTURE`, never silently absent. Note identity is `(subject_id,note_id)`; addenda remain separate identities unless the frozen detail relation proves their parent relation, and no text is counted twice at the admission endpoint.

Record only hashed/coded features: note identity and admission-link status, chart/store times and window, anatomy/laterality/measurement/modality/comparison/interval matching, firm/tentative/completion/replacement/contradiction markers, exact recurrence/source-copy relation, and new-target versus verbatim recurrence. A roster-disjoint longitudinal reader sees the departure abstraction and chronological future feature packet but no route, atom, labels, or expected result. The reader assigns a compatible set among `PERSISTENT_FUTURE_TEXT`, `FUTURE_REVISED_OR_SUPERSEDED`, `FUTURE_DOCUMENTED_COMPLETION`, `FUTURE_TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `FUTURE_CONTRADICTION`, `FUTURE_NO_OBSERVED_TEXT`, `FUTURE_UNLINKED_OR_TIME_UNKNOWN`, and `FUTURE_TARGET_UNKNOWN`. Copied-versus-new ambiguity, missingness, and disagreement are adverse-compatible. No future observation upgrades a departure status.

The longitudinal estimands are conditional on the fixed inherited challenge roster, not a new sample: weighted support for temporally separated target/action-consistent text, explicit revision/completion/contradiction, no observed text, unlinked future, and temporally unresolved future. Report them by departure class and exact atom without conditioning on future-note availability. The prespecified contrast is the compatible-set difference in future-text support between `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` and non-active classes. Bounds including zero, reversal, excessive missingness, or low positive-control information are non-supportive/inconclusive. Later text is never called completion unless its text explicitly documents completion, and even then it is only documented completion, not actual completion of care.

Required longitudinal fixtures include: index post-`D` exclusion; exact window boundaries; month-end calendar addition; missing-`hadm_id` unlinked state; missing-time state; copied recurrence not automatically persistent; later text with target mismatch; no-future-note denominator retention; recorded-death and right-censoring states; and pooled-safe/atom-unsafe routing. If every challenged unit cannot be scanned or packetized, the longitudinal layer is `LONGITUDINAL_INFEASIBLE` and cannot support selection.

## Inference, baselines, and falsification

Use parent first- and second-order inclusion probabilities, HT/Hájek estimates, admission-clustered Rao--Wu replicates, exact finite-population inversion, and a single max-statistic simultaneous family covering parent gates, ledger masses, controls, workload, and longitudinal contrasts. Preserve all compatible assignments rather than averaging disagreement. Every unknown, absent, missing, unlinked, censored, or unreadable case remains visible.

Run identical frozen-sample diagnostics against: the parent pooled gate; a service/era macro-group; marginal-majority, recommendation-unit-majority, terminal-service-majority, and sentinel-only routing; favorable consensus; complete-case/unweighted estimates; and shrinkage sensitivity. These baselines may expose pooled masking or optimistic missingness, but none can nominate an atom. A synthetic fixture placing unsafe AUTO units in one supported atom must fail that atom even if the pooled estimate passes; an unsupported rare atom must remain unreleasable; a copied-but-grounded sentence must not be automatically marked inactive; target swap, modal, completion, replacement, and formatting controls must behave as declared; and zero future text must remain in the denominator.

The verifier can check source hashes, table joins, reciprocal links, times, canonical DS, units, clocks, domains, fixed sample/allocation/probabilities, packet firewalls, workload ledger, route code, compatible-set inference, controls, and whether a conclusion follows from computed outputs. It cannot establish clinical truth, service responsibility, appropriateness, actual viewing/editing/communication, order/scheduling, care completion, safety, benefit, or external transport.

## Interpretation and evidence ceiling

**Supportive.** Parent pooled and atom-specific gates pass; at least one exact atom independently passes the departure ledger with all candidate route units target-grounded and firm-active under adverse-compatible review; controls and missingness pass; and the longitudinal challenge is feasible and does not show a contrary compatible pattern. This supports only nomination of a prospective within-site silent study for that exact observable atom. All other and novel signatures map to `DEFER`.

**Adverse.** A supported atom has a bounded excess of tentative, completed, superseded, mismatched, ungrounded, conflicting, or unknown units; a control failure; excessive route/reference discordance; or a longitudinal contrast that is adequately observable but null/reversed or dominated by adverse future revisions. The atom is excluded despite favorable pooled performance. This does not prove harm, negligence, inappropriate care, or failure to follow up.

**Inconclusive.** The support atlas is infeasible within 900 admissions; route or anchor events are sparse; simultaneous bounds are wide; reference or longitudinal workload exceeds the frozen ceiling; missingness, censoring, unlinked notes, target ambiguity, nonresponse, or broken seals prevents interpretation; or the source calendar cannot be resolved. Inconclusive defaults to `DEFER`, not to a pass.

MIMIC cannot establish a clinically live obligation at physical departure, actual report delivery or viewing, author responsibility, edits/signing/transmission, communication, patient preference, outside plans, orders/referrals, scheduling, execution, malignancy, appropriateness, safety, follow-up completion, utility, benefit, or causal effects. Even a complete retrospective pass therefore supports only a separately approved, actual-calendar prospective silent bridge with immutable workflow logs and independent blinded review. No retrospective automation, live alert, patient-facing action, causal claim, exact-calendar trend, forward transport, or external-site claim is permitted.
