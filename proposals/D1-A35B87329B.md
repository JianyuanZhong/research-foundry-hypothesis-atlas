# Episode 78 successor: capacity-envelope robustness of the landmark-correct seven-day queue

## Clinical decision, unresolved question, and substantive advance

At the locked L24 reassessment landmark, the Episode 77 policy ranks the complete eligible population within each hospital and assigns a fixed fraction to silent review. Its primary operating point is exactly 10% of each hospital, with only a weak 20% stress requiring that an upper confidence bound not be negative. A favorable result at one quota would therefore leave a consequential decision boundary unresolved: does optional repeat-lactate information improve the surveillance-matched queue throughout a reasonable workload band, or is the finding an artifact of selecting exactly 10% of patients?

This successor preserves the audited cohort, landmark, predictors, models, seven-day endpoint, comparator, hospital-held-out fitting, and copied-hospital uncertainty. Its only scientific change is to replace a single operating point with a prespecified capacity-response experiment over half-to-twice the original workload. This matters because ranking disagreements occur at different cut points as capacity changes; discrimination summaries or one top-k result cannot establish that the same signal remains useful at another feasible workload.

The capacity grid is `C={0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20}`.
These are design stresses bracketing the inherited 10% anchor, not measured staffing limits or stakeholder-elicited utilities. Because EICU lacks calendar dates and review-workflow data, they do not represent concurrent daily queues or prove operational feasibility.

The single primary falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the frozen 2,302-person/30-hospital all-baseline/L24 EICU cohort, the D7-fitted hospital-held-out `R_full` queue will capture at least 0.03 more source-defined seven-day in-hospital deaths per slot than the equal-capacity `R_attention` queue at every prespecified capacity from 5% through 20%: the copied-hospital 95% lower bound for the worst-capacity pooled contrast `theta7=min_c Delta7_slot(c)` will be at least +0.03. Support additionally requires nonnegative worst-capacity lower bounds for equal-hospital and both frozen availability-stratum contrasts, stable positive deletion signs, and a nonnegative worst-capacity lower bound for eventual-death capture in the same D7-ranked queues.

This is a prognostic prioritization hypothesis, not a treatment-effect hypothesis. Support would advance only this locked signal and capacity band to independent prospective silent validation with measured workload and verified information timing. It would not support lactate ordering, live deployment, causality, preventability, bedside visibility, or patient benefit.

The strongest directly inspected external evidence supports only modest prognostic information in lactate and the need to represent the observation process. The frozen full-text XML for Baysan et al. This supports modest incremental prognostic information in a lactate-measured cohort, not optional-result value, surveillance-matched specificity, top-k value, or capacity robustness.

The frozen full-text XML for Sisk et al. (2021; DOI `10.1093/jamia/ocaa242`; PMCID `PMC7810439`; source `[source checksum]`; [source checksum]) defines informative presence and observation and reports that 24 of 36 reviewed articles used missing indicators or summary measures. This supports explicit observation-process features; it does not show that the locked surveillance summaries measure attention or remove confounding.

The strongest EICU evidence is feasibility, not confirmation of the queue hypothesis. The audited source flow contains 2,302 people in 30 hospitals, 607 D7 events and 1,695 D7 non-events, with both D7 classes in every hospital. No model result has yet shown that `R_full` beats `R_attention` at 10% or any other capacity. The unresolved claim is the uniform, internally portable fixed-slot contrast over `C`, without observed loss of eventual-death capture in the same queues. No inspected paper establishes that claim.

The three studies in `references/research-ambition/README.md` were treated only as demonstrations of ambition. Their availability limitations were inspected; none is used as clinical authority here, and no unavailable cancer main text or full STAR Methods is claimed read.

## Capacity-envelope estimands

For each `c in C` and frozen hospital h of complete-cohort size `n_h`, set `k_h(c)=ceil(c*n_h)`. For model m, `Q_m,h(c)` is the top `k_h(c)` held-out raw predictors, breaking exact score ties by ascending `patientunitstayid`. Set `K(c)=sum_h k_h(c)`. Quotas must be equal between models at every hospital and capacity. Scores are fit once per fold; only the nested cut point changes with c.

For outcome `o in {7,inf}`, define `S_m,h^o(c)=sum_{i in Q_m,h(c)}D_i^o` and `P_m,h^o(c)=S_m,h^o(c)/k_h(c)`. The capacity-specific pooled primary contrast is `Delta7_slot(c)=[sum_h S7_full,h(c)-sum_h S7_attention,h(c)]/K(c)`. The primary scalar estimand is the prespecified worst-capacity effect `theta7=min_{c in C} Delta7_slot(c)`. Thus `theta7>=0.03` means at least three additional source-defined D7 events per 100 equal slots at every grid capacity, not on average across capacities.

The equal-hospital portability guard is `theta7_macro=min_c mean_h[P7_full,h(c)-P7_attention,h(c)]`, with analogous minimum pooled contrasts `theta7_low` and `theta7_high` within the frozen low- and high-F-availability hospital strata. The same-queue eventual guard is `thetainf=min_c [sum_h Sinf_full,h(c)-sum_h Sinf_attention,h(c)]/K(c)`. These use the same D7-fitted rankings; no eventual-outcome model is fit.

At every capacity, define additions `A_h(c)=Q_full,h(c)\\Q_attention,h(c)`, removals `R_h(c)=Q_attention,h(c)\\Q_full,h(c)`, `s_h(c)=|A_h|=|R_h|`, `M(c)=sum_h s_h(c)`, and `rho(c)=M(c)/K(c)`. For each outcome, `G_o(c)=sum_h[sum_A D_i^o-sum_R D_i^o]` and `Psi_o(c)=G_o(c)/M(c)` when M>0. Verify `Delta_o(c)=G_o(c)/K(c)=rho(c)*Psi_o(c)` and `|Delta_o(c)|<=rho(c)`. If M=0, G and Delta must be zero and yield missing. Never use `2M` or use yield to alter the result state.

Report pointwise capacity curves for K, model captures, Delta, macro/stratum contrasts, M, rho, G, Psi, queue overlap, D7 true/false positives, sensitivity, unflagged events, exact ties, three-state H7 composition, and Dinf guard. Report `c*=argmin_c Delta7_slot(c)` descriptively only; no capacity may be selected post hoc as a new primary.

Secondary baselines repeat the full grid for `R_full-R_state` and `R_process-R_attention`; define `theta_process` as the minimum capacity-specific `R_process-R_attention` contrast. The measured-only numeric analysis refits `R_full` and `R_process` among F-measured people, uses `ceil(c*n_measured,h)`, and defines `theta_numeric` as its minimum contrast. It is conditional and cannot rescue the complete-cohort primary.

## Copied-hospital uncertainty and deletion stability

Use 2,000 copied-hospital refit bootstrap replicates with seed 20260973. Sample original hospitals with replacement. Copies contribute copied evaluation rows and training weight, but all copies of one original hospital are one exclusion group: an original hospital’s prediction may not be trained on any copy of itself. Repeat preprocessing, all four D7 fits, held-out prediction, every capacity quota, primary/guard/baseline contrasts, and decompositions.

Within replicate b save every capacity tuple `(c,K,M,rho,G7,Delta7,Psi7,Ginf,Deltainf,Psiinf,Delta7_macro,Delta7_low,Delta7_high)`, then save `theta7_b`, `theta7_macro_b`, `theta7_low_b`, `theta7_high_b`, and `thetainf_b` as the minima of that replicate’s capacity-specific values. This joint minimum preserves covariance across capacities and forbids cherry-picking. Use percentile 95% intervals (2.5th/97.5th percentiles) for each capacity and each minimum functional. Require at least 1,900 finite replicates for every required fixed-slot capacity contrast and all five minimum functionals. A yield interval requires at least 1,900 replicates with M>0 at that capacity; otherwise label it `yield denominator-fragile`, report the zero-substitution fraction, and do not impute or continuity-correct.

For fixed-design leave-one-original-hospital deletion, remove one evaluation hospital from the already held-out predictions without refitting, then recompute all capacities and minima. Support requires `theta7>0` in at least 80% of finite pooled deletions, `theta7_macro>0` in at least 70%, and each stratum minimum >0 in at least 70% of deletions that leave that stratum represented. Report deletion signs for `thetainf` and whether one hospital dominates K, D7, Dinf, G, or M at any capacity.

## Ordered falsification and interpretation

Apply the first matching state. Secondary baselines and swap yields never rescue the primary.

1. **Not computable.** Any source/header/hash mismatch; failure to reproduce the frozen flow or horizon audit; missing key/clock; altered cohort, feature, model, comparator, capacity grid, ceiling quota, or tie rule; incorrect revision/conflict/duplicate/H7/competing-state handling; H7 exclusion; hospital leakage; incomplete predictions or unequal queues; nonfinite required fits; algebra failure; or fewer than 1,900 finite replicates for any required capacity contrast or minimum functional.
2. **Adverse—opposite at a capacity.** The upper 95% bound for `theta7` is <0. At least one prespecified workload region is credibly worse for `R_full` than `R_attention`; do not advance a capacity-robust policy.
3. **Adverse—eventual-capture tradeoff.** The lower bound for `theta7` is >=+0.03 but the upper bound for `thetainf` is <0. Uniform near-term enrichment is accompanied by evidence of an eventual-death capture loss somewhere in the same capacity band.
4. **Adverse—surveillance-explained materiality.** The worst-capacity `R_full-R_state` lower bound is >=+0.03 but the primary `theta7` upper bound is <+0.03. Uniform materiality does not survive the surveillance-matched comparator; this does not establish why.
5. **Adverse—below uniform materiality.** The upper bound for `theta7` is <+0.03. This falsifies the claim of at least three additional D7 events per 100 slots throughout the band, while allowing benefit at some capacities or a smaller worst-capacity association.
6. **Adverse—internally nonportable.** The primary `theta7` lower bound is >=+0.03, but the upper bound of `theta7_macro`, `theta7_low`, or `theta7_high` is <0. A pooled uniform gain is directionally contradicted by a prespecified site/process estimand.
7. **Supportive for capacity-robust prognostic prioritization.** The lower bound for `theta7` is >=+0.03; lower bounds for `theta7_macro`, `theta7_low`, `theta7_high`, and `thetainf` are >=0; and deletion proportions meet 80%/70%/70%/70%. The strongest supported statement is that, in this EICU snapshot, the locked full queue captured at least three more source-defined D7 events per 100 equal slots than the locked attention queue throughout the 5%–20% grid, without detected eventual-capture loss and without prespecified internal directional contradiction.
8. **Inconclusive.** Every other computable pattern, including intervals crossing +0.03 or zero, favorable point estimates without required bounds, a failed deletion proportion without an adverse interval, or discordant guards. Inconclusive does not support selecting the best-looking capacity, deployment, or changing the horizon.

Attach the inherited descriptive mechanism label only: `numeric component supported` if the 95% lower bound for `theta_numeric` is >0; `process-dominant pattern` if the point estimate of `theta_process` is at least the point estimate of `theta7` or the 95% upper bound for `theta_numeric` is <=0; otherwise `numeric/process unresolved`. This label is noncausal and does not change the state.
