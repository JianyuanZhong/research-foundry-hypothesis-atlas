## Hypothesis to test

At the same hemoglobin level, the effects of transfusion differ between patients with ischemia or an oxygen supply-demand imbalance and stable patients.

## Study population and main data

Patients without active major bleeding; hemoglobin, transfusion, and indicators of ischemia.

## Suggested approach

Emulate transfusion versus deferred-transfusion strategies and prespecify a small number of effect modifiers.

## Scientific significance

Inform population stratification for transfusion trials.

## Main limitations

Confounding by the indication for transfusion is strong, and ischemic status is incompletely measured.
