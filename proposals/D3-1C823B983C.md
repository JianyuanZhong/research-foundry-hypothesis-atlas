# E-MTAB-8873 pair-reconciliation evidence note

## Provisional hypothesis

Among adult female RA type-B synovial-cell pseudobulks in the frozen E-MTAB-8873 targeted assay, the none-treatment contrast between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage co-culture has a reproducible direction for at least one measured feature instance after accounting for recorded cell count and the complete contributing-donor set. This is an association/context hypothesis, not a causal macrophage-mechanism claim.

The unresolved question is whether any context-associated signal is reproducible across donor sets or is explained by donor composition, cell-count differences, or a malformed donor assignment. A stable signal would justify independent donor-aware validation or perturbation; it would not establish a pathway, treatment effect, or clinical benefit.

## Pair reconciliation

The five none-treatment candidate pairs are:
1. R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mneg versus R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos (491 versus 368 cells; donor set SA256A, SA259A).
2. R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mneg versus R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mpos (396 versus 383 cells; donor set SA257A, SA266A).
3. R2_SA258R_FLS_SA266A_Mneg versus R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_Mpos (1469 versus 1207 cells; macrophage donor composition differs).
4. R5_SA263R_FLS_SA277A_Mneg versus R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mneg (540 versus 440 cells; donor sets are SA263R, SA277A versus SA263R, SA278R).
5. R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mneg versus R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos (1144 versus 1060 cells; donor set SA266A, SA277A).

The disputed fourth pair is not a label typo. The frozen design contains both:
- R5_SA263R_FLS_SA277A_Mneg: treatment none, condition co-cultured with MerTK/CD206neg macrophages, donor_id SA263R, SA277A, cells 540.
- R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mneg: treatment none, condition co-cultured with MerTK/CD206neg macrophages, donor_id SA263R, SA278R, cells 440.

Thus pair 4 is a negative-versus-negative macrophage-donor-composition sensitivity contrast, deliberately testing whether a context signal survives a change in macrophage donor. It is not an Mpos-versus-Mneg contrast and must not be interpreted as independent biological replication. The actual positive sample for donor SA278R is R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mpos (treatment none, MerTK/CD206-positive condition, donor_id SA263R, SA278R, cells 455); it is not substituted into pair 4.

The malformed R1_SA256A_FLS_SA260R_Mpos record remains excluded from the primary contrast because its donor_id is SA256A | SA256A, SA260R and its source metadata contains two conflicting individual assignments. It is retained only in a data-quality report. Mixed positive/negative co-culture samples, LPS samples, inhibitor samples, and not-co-cultured controls are not substitutes.

## Prespecified analysis and interpretation

Primary population: exactly the none-treatment samples with condition equal to co-cultured with MerTK/CD206pos macrophages or co-cultured with MerTK/CD206neg macrophages, excluding the malformed record. Preserve all feature-instance keys and do not collapse duplicate symbols with distinct instances.

Baseline: for each feature instance, calculate Mpos-minus-Mneg on log(count + 1), using recorded cells as a prespecified offset (or equivalent count model), and fit a regularized linear model with condition, cell-count offset, and complete contributing-donor-set indicators. Report pair-specific contrasts, donor-set indicators, and uncertainty.

Substantive alternative: fit a structured hierarchical model with a fixed context effect and random effects for the complete contributing-donor set (and, if estimable, FLS donor), preserving feature-instance identity and feature-level uncertainty. This tests reproducibility across donor sets and dependence; the fixed-indicator baseline loses that decomposition.

Supportive result: stable context direction with uncertainty surviving donor-aware fitting, cell-count adjustment, and the stricter sensitivity subset. Adverse result: disappearance after donor-set matching or cell-count adjustment, dependence on one donor set, reversal in the matched subset, or failure to reproduce across prespecified pairs. Inconclusive result: too few independent donor sets, unstable estimates, failed feature reconciliation, inability to separate context from treatment/batch, or insufficient information for the hierarchical alternative.

Neither supportive nor adverse results establish causality, mechanism, whole-tissue composition, effects on unmeasured genes, or clinical benefit. Those require independent donor replication, complete batch/processing-order covariates, clinical adjudication of macrophage phenotype, and perturbation or another study.

## Missing evidence and limitations

No expression result, model fit, uncertainty estimate, or biological conclusion has been computed at this checkpoint. The three-work inspected literature receipts and key-reference file remain incomplete. The targeted assay does not measure absent genes. The design lacks complete batch/processing-order covariates and independent biological replication beyond recorded donor sets. The assay registry warning against ordinary donor t-tests because donors are reused and multiple donors contribute to samples remains applicable.
