## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens nominate single circulating proteins (e.g. GDF15, IL-6/CRP family) as
predictors of many incident/prevalent diseases. It is unresolved whether a protein–disease
association marks a disease-specific process (A) or a shared systemic "sickness/inflammation/aging"
axis elevated across many diagnoses (B). Resolution decides whether a protein is a disease-specific
early-detection target or only a generic risk/severity correlate — a consequential distinction for
biomarker programs. [K3] reports reproducible shared and disease-enriched cross-disease proteomic
signatures; the open question is how much of a *single* protein–disease signal survives adjustment for
a data-driven shared axis versus a flexible age model.

## Design: baseline vs substantive learned/mechanistic alternative
- Baseline M0: logistic prevalence ~ protein_k + age + sex  -> OR_k per SD.
- Shared-axis M1: add shared factor F = first PCA component of standardized proteins, re-estimated
  with protein_k LEFT OUT (leave-one-protein-out) to avoid circularity; report attenuation
  dOR = 1 - |logOR_M1|/|logOR_M0|.
- Substantive alternative M2: flexible age spline (natural spline, 4 df) + sex, a rival explanation
  that age structure alone (not a "sickness" axis) drives signal.
- Learned alternative A3 (to be fitted): a supervised nonlinear low-rank factor model / multitask
  model trained to predict multi-disease co-morbidity from proteins (excluding protein_k), capturing
  shared structure linear PCA may miss. Compare attenuation and cross-validated discrimination.
- Positive control: inflammatory proteins (e.g. IL6/CRP-family) expected to strongly attenuate.
- Negative control: sex-driven proteins expected unaffected; plus a label-permutation null to calibrate
  the attenuation distribution.

## Falsification criteria
- Supports disease-specificity (A): small dOR (<20%) with CI excluding large attenuation AND M1 no
  better than M2 (flexible age alone explains as much).
- Supports shared axis (B): large dOR (>50%) toward null after F adjustment, consistent across diseases.
- Inconclusive: wide CI, insufficient cases, or M2 (flexible age) explains as much as F.

## Deliverable the solver must produce
Fit the shared axis and per-protein attenuation estimates with cross-fitting; report dOR with
uncertainty, control-fixture results, and the M2 rival comparison. Evaluation on held-out folds.
