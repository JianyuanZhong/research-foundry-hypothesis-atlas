# Episode 75 synthesis: fixed-slot value first, substitution yield as a denominator-qualified decomposition

## Decision, unresolved question, and substantive advance

The decision is whether an optional repeat-lactate result and its measurement process improve a fixed-capacity, silent mortality-review queue beyond an otherwise identical model that already represents late non-lactate surveillance. The queue is modeled, not observed care. It does not order lactate, add review capacity, identify preventable deaths, recommend treatment, or estimate a survival effect.

The Episode 74 parents agree on the audited cohort, information boundary, four models, comparator, 10%-per-hospital capacity, hospital-held-out fitting, and noncausal interpretation. `[prior hypothesis]` selects additional eventual decedents per 100 fixed review slots. `[prior hypothesis]` additionally requires at least 0.10 net eventual decedents per substituted slot. This synthesis resolves that difference rather than treating two rescalings of one numerator as independent evidence.

For equal-capacity queues, fixed-slot gain and swap yield satisfy an exact identity:

`Delta_slot = rho_swap * Psi_swap`,

where `rho_swap` is the fraction of review slots whose occupants change and `Psi_swap` is net eventual decedents among additions minus removals per substituted slot. Swap yield is mathematically coherent and clinically useful for describing how a fixed-slot gain arose. It is not an independent guard on the value of an equal-workload queue. A high yield can be produced by one or a few favorable substitutions and is unstable when churn is tiny; conversely, a material gain spread across many substitutions can fail an arbitrary yield margin even though reviewers receive more eventual decedents at exactly the same workload. Unless a stakeholder supplies a cost or harm per changed assignment, calling the latter “substitution-inefficient” is not decision-valid.

The robust hierarchy therefore makes fixed-slot precision the sole primary benefit estimand and makes the paired churn/yield factorization a required, jointly bootstrapped secondary explanation. Swap yield can neither rescue a failed fixed-slot result nor veto a supportive one. Its numerator, denominator, hospital distribution, and uncertainty must always be exposed; no qualitative “efficient” label is permitted when the denominator is small.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults in the locked EICU baseline/L24 population, replacing the hospital-held-out `R_attention` top-10% within-hospital queue with the equal-capacity `R_full` queue captures at least 3 additional eventual hospital-discharge deaths per 100 review slots in the pooled workload estimand, with a 95% lower bound at least +0.03, while the corresponding lower bounds are nonnegative in both prespecified repeat-availability strata and in the equal-hospital macro estimand.

The +0.03 margin is a prespecified operational materiality anchor, not a stakeholder-validated utility threshold. The hypothesis concerns prognostic prioritization in this EICU snapshot. Even support warrants only independent prospective silent validation.

## Fixed-capacity estimand hierarchy and anti-gaming algebra

For hospital `h` with `n_h` eligible people set `k_h=ceil(0.10*n_h)`. For each model rank outer-hospital-held-out raw linear predictors within hospital; the top `k_h` form `Q_m,h`, with ascending `patientunitstayid` breaking exact score ties. Let `S_m,h=sum(D_i, i in Q_m,h)`, `P_m,h=S_m,h/k_h`, and `K=sum_h k_h`.

The primary pooled workload estimand is

`Delta_slot = (sum_h S_full,h - sum_h S_attention,h) / K`.

Report `100*Delta_slot` as additional eventual discharge deaths per 100 fixed review slots. The materiality margin is +0.03. The prespecified portability estimand is `Delta_macro=mean_h(P_full,h-P_attention,h)`. Compute `Delta_slot` separately in frozen low- and high-F-availability hospital strata using each stratum's slots.

For the required paired decomposition define `A_h=Q_full,h \ Q_attention,h`, `R_h=Q_attention,h \ Q_full,h`, `s_h=|A_h|=|R_h|`, `M=sum_h s_h`, `G=sum_h[sum_{i in A_h}D_i-sum_{i in R_h}D_i]`, and `rho_swap=M/K`. If `M>0`, define `Psi_swap=G/M`; otherwise it is undefined. Equal quotas imply, exactly,

`G = sum_h(S_full,h-S_attention,h)`,

`Delta_slot = G/K = rho_swap*Psi_swap`, and `|Delta_slot|<=rho_swap` because `D` is binary and `-1<=Psi_swap<=1`.

These identities are verification requirements and the anti-gaming guard. A tiny `M` may make `Psi_swap` extreme, but it cannot make `Delta_slot` material: for example, `rho_swap<0.03` makes `Delta_slot>=0.03` impossible except boundary equality. Report `M`, `K`, `rho_swap`, G, addition/removal death proportions, hospitals with `s_h>0`, and the distribution of `s_h/k_h`. Never report yield without its denominator. If `M=0`, yield is undefined and `Delta_slot=0`; this is a computable adverse-to-materiality result, not a model failure.

No yield threshold enters the primary result. `Psi_swap` is a conditional description of changed assignments. If future stakeholders posit a cost `c` per substituted slot on the same utility scale as capturing an eventual decedent, net value per fixed slot is `Delta_slot-c*rho_swap=rho_swap*(Psi_swap-c)`, so yield is the break-even switching-cost quantity. This dataset contains neither `c` nor the benefit of review; utility interpretation therefore requires stakeholder elicitation and prospective workflow data.

Required secondary analyses are: slot-precision contrasts `R_full-R_state` and `R_process-R_attention`; Episode 73 capture fraction `C_m=sum_h S_m,h/sum_h sum_i D_i` and its contrasts; conditional measured-population `R_full-R_process` after refitting both only among F-measured people with new within-hospital 10% quotas; queue overlap, additions, removals, true/false positives, sensitivity, unflagged deaths, score ties, and rank-switch fractions by hospital; and the same slot and decomposition quantities at 20% capacity with `ceil(0.20*n_h)`. No secondary result rescues the 10% primary.

## Evidence limits, required data, and verification boundary

They cannot determine why lactate was ordered, specimen type/quality, true administration or indication, release/display/view time, actual review, clinician concern, workload, treatment response, death timing/cause/preventability, patient harm, switching cost, or counterfactual benefit. Hospital calendar period is also unavailable for temporal transport testing.

Clinical informatics review must adjudicate timestamp and workflow meaning. Independent prospective data need order, specimen, verified result-release, display/view, queue/review action, treatment, workload, and patient-centered outcome times. Stakeholder review must set utilities or costs. External transport requires another cohort; causal benefit requires a prospective impact study, ideally randomized if the policy changes care.

Harbor must retain private row-level score/queue/substitution files and publish only permitted aggregates plus source/header/hash manifests, exact flow and filter logs, revision/conflict audits, feature/fold manifests, convergence, queue cells, bootstrap draws, joint algebra tuples, intervals, deletions, and a structured conclusion.

It must reject a swapped denominator (`2M` changed people versus `M` substituted slots), unequal queues, sign discrepancies, yield reported without M/K, yield used to rescue/veto the primary, and correct numbers paired with unsupported conclusions.
