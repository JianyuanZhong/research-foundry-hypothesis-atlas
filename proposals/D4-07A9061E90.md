## Hypothesis to test

People with normal creatinine-based eGFR, lower cystatin-C-based eGFR, and poor grip strength are more likely to develop severe acute kidney injury.

## Study population and main data

Creatinine, cystatin C, grip strength, and body composition.

## Suggested approach

Stratify jointly by these measures and follow participants for acute kidney injury and subsequent chronic kidney disease.

## Scientific significance

Identify high-risk people who may be missed by routine creatinine testing.

## Main limitations

Inflammation and other factors also affect eGFR discordance; it must not be equated with low muscle mass.
