## 1. Unresolved question (unchanged in substance, now evidence-hardened)

In HBV-cirrhosis-dominant HCC inpatients undergoing hepatic resection, do **early postoperative (POD 0–2) liver-function trajectories** — TBIL rise, PTR/INR prolongation, ALB decline relative to preoperative baseline — predict **severe post-hepatectomy liver dysfunction at POD 3–7**, **beyond** (a) static preoperative liver reserve (ALBI components, platelets, age, sex, HBV/cirrhosis flags) and (b) later snapshot information?

### Clinical importance
PHLF is the leading cause of death after hepatectomy [K1]. The classic 50-50 criteria are only decision-relevant on day 5 [K1]. Earlier trustworthy risk information at POD 0–2 could shift escalation, monitoring intensity, and transplant discussions by days. Current early-prediction evidence requires specialized assays (ATIII decline ≥30% at POD 3; AUC 0.750, N=151 [K3]) or 55-variable ensembles with regeneration biomarkers (PILOT; AUC up to 0.904 postop, N=1071 [K2]). Whether the **interpretable shape of routine POD 0–2 labs** carries incremental information beyond preop reserve — the deployable minimum in any hospital EHR — remains unisolated in a large HBV-dominant population.

   → The parent's primary outcome operationalization (50-50-style conjunction) would yield **n=3 events: definitively non-viable** (below the pre-specified <100-event inconclusiveness threshold). A bilirubin-anchored outcome is required. This boundary finding itself generalizes the [K1] operationalization question: the coagulopathy-centered 50-50 phenotype from a Western 60%-major-resection/12%-cirrhosis cohort does not transfer to this HBV-dominant partial-resection population.

## 3. Study design (Harbor experiment; revised outcome)

- **Population:** qualifying encounters as measured (≈2,444; first qualifying encounter per patient for fitting, patient-level split 70/30 stratified).
- **Exposure (trajectory features, POD 0–2 vs preop baseline):** TBIL, PTR/INR, ALB — slope over POD 0–2, value at POD 1/2, change-from-baseline, persistence/monotonicity index; range-validated per §2.5.
- **Primary outcome (redefined):** severe post-hepatectomy liver dysfunction = **peak TBIL > 50 µmol/L within POD 3–7** (n=235, 9.6%; threshold anchored on [K1]). Pre-specified severity gradient: moderate TBIL > 30 (n=847, 34.7%); very severe TBIL > 50 AND PTR/INR rise ≥ 25% from baseline (descriptive only, expected n≈10). Sensitivity: relative worsening (peak TBIL ≥ 2× preop baseline), ISGLS-style on/after-POD-5 window.
- **Baseline model:** logistic on preop ALBI components (TBIL, ALB), platelets, age, sex, HBV-cirrhosis/HBV flags.
- **Substantive alternative:** trajectory-augmented logistic (baseline covariates + POD 0–2 trajectory features) — the mechanistic test that early shape matters. **Learned comparator (conditional):** gradient boosting on the same features, only to test nonlinear/interaction temporal structure.
- **Snapshot comparators:** (a) day-5 snapshot model where day-5 labs exist (926; missingness pattern itself reported); (b) last-value-before-POD-3 snapshot model (current-information benchmark).
- **Evaluation:** ΔAUC with bootstrap 95% CI, IDI/NRI, calibration; **temporal-order permutation control** (shuffle lab times within encounter); sensitivity: unit/range-anomaly exclusion, first-vs-all encounter definitions, TBIL>30 and 2×-baseline outcome variants.
- **Falsification criteria (measured):** Supportive = trajectory features give significant incremental ΔAUC (bootstrap CI excluding 0), robust to permutation and range checks, AND POD-2 model approaches the best snapshot model's discrimination. Adverse = ΔAUC CI includes 0 → early trajectory carries no information beyond preop reserve (day-5 timing informationally irreducible from routine labs). Inconclusive = effective events < 100 under the primary definition (now measured at 235 → not triggered) or range anomalies dominating diagnostics.
- **Computable vs requiring adjudication:** incremental-information question is computable from bound data. Causal mechanism (regeneration failure vs perioperative insult) and clinical PHLF diagnosis (ISGLS requires adjudicated INR+bilirubin course) are NOT established here; the outcome is a lab-defined analog.

## 7. Threats and alternatives
Sicker-patients-measured-more-often (mitigated: ≥1 measurement required in all windows for all; measurement-count adjustment); transfusion/hemodilution artifact on ALB/PTR (mitigated: TBIL-primary outcome); residual unit/reporting anomalies (mitigated: measured unit inference + range validation); conditioning on surviving to POD 3 (outcome is lab-defined at fixed windows; LOS secondary, death descriptive only per §2.4); single-center HBV-dominant external-validity limit (claimed). Positive ΔAUC would NOT establish mechanism (regeneration failure vs insult) — requires ICG/volumetrics.
