# Cumulative systemic hypotension duration beyond nadir as a predictor of incident creatinine-defined AKI in eICU

Status: proposed Harbor experiment; no full study result has been computed. This is an episode-2 substantive child of [prior hypothesis].

## Scientific opening and hypothesis

The expert seed eicu-03 asks whether, at the same minimum blood pressure, longer-lasting hypotension precedes more AKI. Inspected prior evidence supports only narrower premises: temporal hypotension trajectories have been associated with postoperative complications [K1]; KDIGO provides creatinine-based AKI criteria [K2]; and randomized BP-target evidence warns that prognostic hypotension associations do not establish that raising a BP target improves AKI [K3]. The unresolved claim is incremental, transportable prognostic information, not a causal treatment effect.

H1: among adult eICU ICU stays with adequate early BP and creatinine ascertainment, cumulative covered time with systemic mean arterial pressure (MAP) below 65 mmHg during ICU minutes 0-360 predicts a new creatinine-defined AKI event during minutes 360-2880 beyond nadir systemic MAP, baseline/current severity and observation coverage, and retains information under hospital-held-out evaluation.

Strong rival R1: duration is a proxy for current severity, measurement density, treatment response, or imminent care transition. A residual association would remain descriptive; it would not prove renal hypoperfusion, a physiologic threshold, causality, or that treating duration prevents AKI.

Clinical importance: if H1 survives process, treatment and transportability checks, duration may be a useful risk-summary variable for monitoring and for stratifying future BP trials. If it collapses after those checks, duration should not be treated as a portable renal-risk signal. Both outcomes change the next research decision.

## Population and time boundaries

Use eICU only. Join all tables on patientunitstayid; never join identifiers to HCC or MIMIC. Select the first ICU stay per uniquepid (lowest unitvisitnumber, then earliest unitadmittime24 where needed) to limit recurrent-stay leakage. Adults are age >=18; eICU age strings such as > 89 must be handled by a prespecified documented rule and sensitivity analysis. Index is ICU admission, with longitudinal offsets measured from admission.

Primary exposure window: minutes 0-360. Require at least 12 valid 5-minute bins and at least 240 minutes between first and last covered bin. Do not treat absent measurements as normotension. Use systemicmean from vitalPeriodic, not pamean (the latter is pulmonary artery mean); when systemicmean and noninvasivemean are both available in a bin, preferentially retain systemicmean and record a modality flag, otherwise use noninvasivemean from vitalAperiodic. Accept MAP 20-200 mmHg in the primary analysis; sensitivity 30-180. Do not interpolate across gaps >10 minutes. Compute covered minutes, observation count, median gap, BP modality and missingness.

Exposure variables are covered time below 65, low-time fraction, nadir systemic MAP, low-MAP area below 65, number of low episodes, first-low offset and recovery slope. Threshold sensitivities are 60 and 70 mmHg and common-density resampling.

Primary outcome: new creatinine-defined AKI in minutes 360-2880 (6-48 hours), defined as >=0.3 mg/dL increase from the last valid pre-index creatinine in minutes -24-0. Deduplicate same-clinical-offset revised results by retaining the latest labresultrevisedoffset. Require the lab value to be numeric and audit labname plus labmeasurenamesystem/labmeasurenameinterface and units before accepting it as serum creatinine. A prespecified sensitivity uses a 0-6 hour baseline and is reported as a distinct cohort. Secondary outcome is >=1.5 times baseline within 7 ICU days. Audit urine output before any full KDIGO claim; intakeOutput is not automatically urine and its semantics must be validated.

Death, ICU discharge, transfer and absent follow-up are competing/censoring events, not automatic non-AKI. Report ascertainment and censoring; use competing-risk and IPCW sensitivity analyses. Exclude pre-index AKI or dialysis only for the incident-risk primary analysis, while retaining those stays in descriptive and competing-event sensitivities. carePlanEOL is a sensitivity covariate/exclusion flag, not evidence of clinician intent.

## Baseline, substantive alternative and estimand

B0 is a prespecified regularized logistic model for the same 6-48 hour AKI target using nadir systemic MAP, initial MAP/HR, pre-index creatinine, age, sex, ethnicity, hospital/ICU type and BP source/missingness/process controls. It excludes duration and all post-index variables.

B1 adds duration below 65, low-time fraction, episode count and recovery features with a restricted spline and fixed-nadir bands. The primary estimand is the held-out incremental log loss, Brier score and calibration of B1 over B0, with hospital-clustered bootstrap intervals and AUROC/AUPRC secondary. Assign whole hospitals 60/20/20 to train/validation/test; if too few hospitals, use repeated grouped-hospital cross-validation and state the limitation. Keep all stays for one uniquepid in one split.

M is a small GRU or temporal convolution over the same 0-6 hour 5-minute sequence: systemic/noninvasive MAP, HR, respiration, modality and time-since-last-observation masks, pressor indicators/rates and pre-index severity. Fluid/output summaries enter only if their semantics pass readiness. Fit only on training hospitals, early-stop on validation hospitals, calibrate on validation data, and evaluate once on held-out hospitals. M can reveal whether persistence, recovery slope, oscillation or BP-treatment coupling carries reproducible information that B0 discards; B0 supplies transparent incremental duration estimates and is harder to overfit. Neither separates causal renal hypoperfusion from unmeasured illness.

The method choice is substantive: if B1’s fixed-duration signal is robust and M adds no reproducible trajectory information, retain the interpretable duration summary; if M adds stable trajectory information beyond B1, revise the clinical summary rather than merely report a tiny predictive gain; if both fail after common-density and process adjustment, defer the direction. GPU is optional. Tabular fits should run on CPU; a bounded one-A100 sequence fit is permitted only if measured parsing/training time justifies it. Future solver planning envelope is 16 CPU, up to 8 GPU, 262144 MiB and 28800 seconds; discovery did not execute the full study and does not claim those resources are measured.

Deferred alternatives: latent trajectory classes are deferred because class number/shape would be exploratory; a renal mechanistic state-space model is deferred because these files lack perfusion pressure, congestion imaging, renal biomarkers and clinician-adjudicated tubular injury; a large transformer is deferred because it does not test a distinct uncertainty. Revisit only with new measurements or unstable, reproducible trajectory evidence.

## Falsification and interpretation

Pre-register: within-hospital permutation of trajectory within nadir bands; common-density thinning; systemic versus noninvasive strata; thresholds 60/65/70; a pre-index negative-control creatinine-rise label; treatment-masked, treatment-only and pressor-adjusted ablations; BP/lab coverage controls; strict exclusion of post-index labs, discharge, EOL and outcome-derived fields; and leakage audits for first-stay and hospital splits.

Supportive results require a prespecified improvement in held-out Brier/log loss and calibration for B1 over B0 with uncertainty excluding no improvement, monotonic duration-risk within fixed nadir bands, persistence after common-density/process/treatment adjustment and hospital holdout, and reproducible M trajectory information. This supports only a transportable prognostic summary.

Adverse results include null/non-monotone duration after adjustment, confinement to low-density or one-hospital data, disappearance after treatment adjustment, failure to transport, or apparent gain reproduced by a pre-index negative control. These support R1 and argue against a robust duration signal.

Inconclusive results include wide intervals, insufficient creatinine ascertainment, unstable hospital holdout, exposure-dependent censoring, or unresolved lab/urine semantics. They do not refute H1; they require label adjudication, independent validation or a narrower descriptive BP-process study.

## Deliverable and limits

The solver must newly fit B0, B1 and M and emit cohort flow, field/label audit, held-out predictions, coefficient/spline estimates, calibration and clustered uncertainty tables, fixed-nadir curves, hospital heterogeneity, process/treatment ablations, common-density and negative-control outputs, plus resource logs. Readiness is not a scientific result.

Available data lack clinician-adjudicated AKI, reliable pre-ICU kidney function, complete fluid balance, arterial-line calibration, raw waveforms, renal replacement indication/intent, staffing/bed context and independent external validation. The experiment cannot establish causality, renal mechanism, a treatment threshold, hospital quality, or benefit from changing BP. Those claims require critical-care/nephrology adjudication and a prospective or external validation study.
