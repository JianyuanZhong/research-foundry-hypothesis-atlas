# Endpoint-validity evolution: persistent, target-independent respiratory therapy de-escalation with falsification controls

## Parentage, assigned goal, and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the strongest lineage decisions: adult first qualifying eICU stay, target-independent denominator, five landmarks, post-freeze literal Ventilation classification, four airway recency definitions, strict temporal separation, hospital-held-out estimands, stay-clustered uncertainty, and the distinction between exported structured proxies and bedside truth.

The parent endpoint evolution was clinically closer to respiratory management than chart persistence, but its primary outcome was vulnerable to a one-off minimum setting value and its independent corroboration was still planned. The present evolution repairs endpoint validity rather than adding another vocabulary or documentation audit. It asks whether the two exact care-plan labels, or the outcome-blind four-value `Ventilated -` expansion, add transportable information for a **persistent numeric respiratory-therapy de-escalation event** after conditioning on pre-landmark respiratory settings, physiologic state, airway/status information, and documentation opportunity. It also prespecifies unrelated therapy negative controls and a stricter corroboration sensitivity so an apparent label association cannot be dismissed as either a single copied setting or a general propensity to generate charted care.

A supportive result would be a reproducible incremental prediction signal for a persistent structured therapy-setting decrease, not evidence that an extubation evaluation, SBT, liberation, or clinically appropriate de-escalation occurred. An adverse result would be useful evidence against using these labels as a transportable respiratory decision-support feature. An inconclusive result would identify failure of endpoint support or overlap rather than support for the labels.

## Evidence already supported versus claim tested

The assessed parents support that exact labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated, omitted exported Ventilation strings do not rescue the prior charted-setting proxy, and broader labels can predict respiratory documentation persistence without stably outperforming exact labels. Durable liberation cannot be validly ascertained from the available independent markers. None of those findings establishes that the labels encode a common physiologic state or a bedside respiratory decision.

The untested, falsifiable claim here is narrower: **conditional on an independently selected target-independent adult first-stay landmark and pre-landmark respiratory state, does an exact or broader Ventilation indicator improve hospital-held-out prediction of a persistent, repeated, numeric reduction in respiratory support settings?** The endpoint and its validity checks are defined without using care-plan labels, label receipt, or the prior chart-proxy outcome.

## Endpoint validity hierarchy

### Primary endpoint: persistent numeric setting de-escalation

From `respiratoryCharting`, parse only outcome-blind canonical numeric labels fixed before examining the landmark cohort:

- FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`; convert percentages to fractions and require 0.21–1.00.
- PEEP: `PEEP`; require 0–40 cm H2O.
- Pressure support: `Pressure Support`; require 0–60 cm H2O.
- Set rate: `Vent Rate`; require 0–80 breaths/min.

For each component, define the pre-landmark reference as the median of the last three valid observations in `[L-360,L)`, requiring at least two observations separated by 30 minutes; if no component satisfies that rule it remains missing, not a zero. In `(L+360,L+1080]`, require at least two valid observations of that component separated by at least 120 minutes. A component-specific persistent decrease is present when both the first and last qualifying post observations are at least the prespecified threshold below the pre-landmark reference: 0.05 FiO2, 2 cm H2O PEEP, 2 cm H2O pressure support, or 2 breaths/min set rate. Require the later observation to be no higher than the earlier qualifying observation, preventing a single transient low value from defining de-escalation. Retain all component deltas, counts, spans, and rebound flags for audit.

The primary binary endpoint is one if at least one component meets this persistent-decrease rule. A stricter two-component composite and a continuous standardized support-change score are secondary, not replacements. A single-observation/minimum-value endpoint is retained only as a sensitivity to distinguish a persistence requirement from the parent’s artifact-sensitive proxy.

This is a structured therapy-setting endpoint, not continuous ventilator telemetry. It can reflect copying, delayed entry, interface changes, or a transient adjustment. It does not prove invasive ventilation, clinical intent, appropriate weaning, SBT, extubation, liberation, readiness, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

### Independent corroboration sensitivity and endpoint-invalidity gates

Construct, without changing the primary endpoint, a corroborated sensitivity requiring the persistent numeric decrease plus at least one temporally compatible independent signal in `(L+360,L+1080]`:

1. `respiratoryCare` airway/status transition from an artificial-airway value to `No Artificial Airway`, with `respcarestatusoffset` timestamped in the same window; or
2. the pre-frozen literal `treatmentstring == "pulmonary|ventilation and oxygenation|ventilator weaning"` at `treatmentoffset` in the same window.

Do not interpret either signal alone as extubation or weaning success. Report each corroborator separately, their intersection, timing gaps, and hospital/stay support. The corroborated endpoint is valid for analysis only if the primary event, independent signal, and at least 80% of the contributing rows pass explicit timestamp and key checks, and at least 10 hospitals have 20 or more endpoint-available landmarks with both outcome classes. Otherwise report an endpoint-validity failure or inconclusive feasibility and do not estimate a label association for that sensitivity.

The scan already shows why this gate is necessary: the complete `respiratoryCare` table has 865,381 rows from 44,772 stays, but `airwaytype` is blank in 770,432 rows and only 157 rows have nonzero `ventendoffset`; `treatment` is a structured treatment list, not a validated action log. These are feasibility facts, not evidence of absent bedside care.

## Features and robust baselines

Build all baselines before adding care-plan exposures and without using any future endpoint row.

1. **Respiratory state baseline.** Last-value and robust summaries of FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, and set rate from `[L-360,L)`, including median, slope, variability, observation count, and time since last observation; pre-landmark respiratory-chart density and distinct-label count; recent `respiratoryCare.airwaytype`, `ventstartoffset`, `ventendoffset`, and status recency; and pre-landmark treatment strings restricted to the pre-frozen pulmonary ventilation/oxygenation family.
2. **Physiologic baseline.** Pre-landmark summaries from `vitalPeriodic` (`observationoffset`, `sao2`, `respiration`, `heartrate`, `systemicmean`) and `vitalAperiodic` (`observationoffset`, `noninvasivemean`), including median, variability, trend, minimum observation count, and density. Missingness indicators are retained rather than silently imputed as normal physiology.
3. **Clinical and stay baseline.** Age, gender, ethnicity, unit type, admission source, unit-visit number, unit-discharge horizon, and target-independent admission severity variables already frozen by the parent support. This is adjustment/prediction, not causal confounding control.
4. **Exposure-blind process baseline.** Generic `carePlanGeneral` presence, row count, distinct group/value counts, and non-Ventilation care-plan opportunity in `[L,L+360]`; respiratoryCharting and respiratoryCare observation opportunity in the pre-window. These quantify recording opportunity and must not be described as care delivered.
5. **Incremental exposures.** First add exact three-state `label3` for the two literal labels; separately add all four outcome-blind values beginning `Ventilated -`, keeping the exact-versus-broader contrast. No target-derived label vocabulary is permitted.

Models are prespecified logistic regression with one-hot landmark, hospital-held-out preprocessing and fitting, median imputation plus missingness indicators, standardization of numeric variables, unknown-safe categorical encoding, and regularization selected inside the training hospitals only. Run nested refitting for every held-out fold; no held-out hospital, post-window row, or endpoint-derived label vocabulary may influence preprocessing, tuning, or exposure construction.

## Negative controls and estimands

The primary estimand is the incremental held-out log-loss and Brier-score change for broader minus process, broader minus exact, and process minus respiratory-state/physiology baseline. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro results, hospital-direction counts, calibration, AUROC only as secondary discrimination, and stay-clustered intervals with all landmarks from a `sid` resampled together. Require at least 20 endpoint-available rows and both classes per held-out hospital; show all eligible hospitals and do not silently restrict to hospitals with local label receipt.

Use two outcome-blind negative-control families, chosen before outcome inspection:

- **Unrelated therapy controls.** Predict the later occurrence in `(L+360,L+1080]` of the exact structured treatment strings `cardiovascular|vascular disorders|VTE prophylaxis|compression stockings`, `renal|urinary catheters|foley catheter`, and `endocrine|glucose metabolism|insulin`, using the same LOHO pipeline and pre-window opportunity controls. These are not expected to be clinically independent in every patient, so they are falsification diagnostics, not proof of respiratory specificity; report their results separately and avoid post hoc selection.
- **Placebo timing controls.** Repeat the primary model with a pseudo-endpoint window immediately before the classification window, `(L-1080,L-360]`, using only landmarks with sufficient pre-history. A care-plan label cannot prospectively predict a prior de-escalation event under the intended temporal interpretation. Failure of this placebo is adverse to the endpoint/exposure timing interpretation.

A supportive gate requires the broader exposure to improve over both process and exact models in micro and macro log loss across the unbounded and at least two airway-bounded definitions, with nested stay-cluster 97.5th-percentile deltas below zero, no deterioration in the two-component endpoint, and no materially similar gain for all unrelated therapy controls or the prior placebo. An adverse gate requires no broader improvement over process in any bounded definition, broader deterioration over process in at least two bounded definitions with nested macro probability of worsening at least 0.75, or failure of the corroboration/endpoint-validity gate. Any mixture is inconclusive. Passing the gate supports only a transportable association with this structured proxy and warrants blinded clinical adjudication; it never establishes bedside action or clinical benefit.

## What the results could and could not mean

Support would justify only a stronger structured-proxy hypothesis: care-plan labels carry incremental, cross-hospital information about a repeated numeric support-setting decrease after state adjustment. Adverse results would discourage transportable use and would be consistent with sparse, hospital-specific documentation or no incremental information; they would not prove that clinicians did not evaluate or de-escalate therapy. Inconclusive results would trigger endpoint repair, overlap review, or clinical adjudication rather than a positive or negative bedside conclusion.

Automatic verification can check source hashes, schema columns, joins, landmark inequalities, endpoint non-overlap, label-blind denominator construction, repeated-observation rules, negative-control definitions, nested LOHO separation, stay clustering, and arithmetic for all gates. It cannot determine whether a respiratory chart value records a bedside setting, whether an airway transition is complete, whether a treatment string means an executed intervention, or whether any observed reduction was appropriate or beneficial. Those claims require direct device data, SBT/extubation records, local interface/workflow metadata, blinded clinician adjudication, and external hospital validation.

## Changes from the parents and remaining uncertainty

Changed: one-off minimum-setting outcome to a repeated, separated, monotone persistent decrease; explicit endpoint-validity/corroboration gates; full-source feasibility scan of numeric respiratory, airway/status, and treatment tables; stronger respiratory-state and physiologic baselines; nested LOHO refitting; unrelated therapy and temporal placebo negative controls; and a prohibition on using sparse respiratoryCare/treatment fields as a gold standard.

Preserved: the target-independent adult first-stay denominator, landmarks, airway variants, literal exact/broader exposure definitions, temporal separation, hospital-held-out micro/macro estimands, stay-clustered uncertainty, and strict non-bedside/noncausal interpretation. Remaining uncertainty includes inherited support rather than full eligibility rebuild in this version, incomplete airway/status fields, treatment-string semantics, unobserved ventilator modes and waveform data, possible copied or delayed chart values, fixed observed hospitals, and the need for clinical adjudication and external validation.
