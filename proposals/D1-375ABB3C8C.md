### Frozen ambiguity ledger and two nested envelopes

Exact exclusions from the outer set require unanimous pre-outcome agreement plus a positive reason visible in the procedure label (for example transplant-only, biopsy/puncture-only, ablation-only, or nonhepatic surgery). Lack of confidence is not an exclusion. The reviewed **adjudicated set** may additionally remove clinically impossible linkages, but every removal retains reviewer identities, blinded disagreement, reason code, and raw provenance.

For patient i, `S_i^+` is the outer source envelope generated from all query-set values, all permitted episode partitions, exact or interval clock cells, every time-window boundary, prior-treatment alternatives, accession bundles, encounter-specific HCC/pathology composites, and nonmembership states. `S_i^*` is the nested adjudicated envelope after permitted removals, so `S_i^* subseteq S_i^+`. Independent Cartesian recombination is forbidden.

P-Cert and T-Cert are tested on `S^+`; the `S^*` result is secondary and labeled adjudication-conditional. If `S^*` supports a claim but `S^+` does not, the result is **adjudication-sensitive/inconclusive**, never source-robust. If even the upper endpoint on `S^+` is below a boundary, that is adverse despite a favorable core. A favorable outer result is stronger but remains rule-relative.

### Machine-checkable state-completeness certificate

State generation is a finite directed acyclic graph whose nodes are frozen raw ambiguity atoms and whose edges are rule applications. Each branch must terminate in exactly one of: (1) an emitted raw state, (2) a documented deterministic impossibility in the outer envelope, or (3) a documented adjudicated-only exclusion that is reinstated in `S_i^+`. Invalid or missing clocks create unresolved/nonmember alternatives as specified below; they are not silently dropped.

The certificate reports, by patient and globally:

- input row ordinals and SHA-256 digests; exact duplicates and every one-to-many join;
- candidate procedure values, partitions, index episodes, encounters, clock cells, temporal breakpoints, treatment alternatives, accessions, modality assignments, pathology composites, raw terminal-state count, and retained/excluded counts by reason;
- forward reachability from every ledger atom to a terminal state or allowed reason, and reverse provenance from every state to its atoms;
- membership/role/pattern/Y/V signature counts, certainly/possibly-member counts, and minimum/maximum denominators;
- hashes of frozen dictionaries, parsers, review forms, raw states, quotient states, model inputs, solver inputs, and bootstrap manifests.

No maximum uncertain fraction is an exclusion. No state-count cap, beam search, random state sampling, top-k linkage, approximate column generation, or time-limit incumbent may support P-Cert/T-Cert. Lazy constraint/state generation is allowed only when exact separation proves no omitted violating state and an independent replay reaches the same endpoint. Any unreachable ledger atom, unclassified branch, truncated expansion, unexplained count discrepancy, solver gap above `1e-8`, or failed replay makes the primary result inconclusive.

## Target population, temporal quarantine and estimand

A global reconstruction `q=(s_i:i in U_R)` chooses exactly one admissible state for each patient in role superframe `R`. For test world `q`,

## Interpretation zones

**Supportive P-Frame:** every feasibility gate passes; simultaneous lower limits for the lower identified endpoints of D, C and both default contrasts exceed 0.01 at 0.30; D/C lower endpoints are positive across the threshold plateau and buffer analyses; and the null and source-grain controls pass. The allowed conclusion is only that the stated access vector would suffice under the enumerated source, membership, clock, outcome and access uncertainty. Actual decision-time availability remains unresolved.

**Adverse:** with feasibility established, a simultaneous upper limit for the upper identified endpoint of D or C is at most 0.01 at 0.30, meaning even the most favorable admissible reconstruction does not achieve the clinical margin. An upper limit at most zero is evidence that the action changes are nonbeneficial or harmful on the net-benefit scale. A favorable stable-subset result paired with such an all-frame result is adverse evidence against the Episode-15 identifiability-conditioned claim.

**Mixed:** D is supportive while C is adverse, so semantics alter actions favorably within fitted S but S does not beat optimized B; C is supportive while D is adverse, so complete S may beat B without attributing value to runtime semantics; or P is supportive while T is not, so stored-content value does not establish a workable access level. **Inconclusive:** identification or confidence intervals cross a decision boundary; lower and upper endpoints permit both material and nonmaterial worlds; a support set is empty without an adverse upper bound; any population, adjudication, state-enumeration, sample-size, pathology, extraction, solver, resampling, null or sensitivity gate fails; or a decision-time claim lacks the exact-version audit.
