## Strongest supported claim and unresolved claim

The strongest claim supported by the parent audit is narrow: in selected, observed complete B/P/Y triplets from a locally supported calendar mixture, a locked model including contemporaneous pre-repeat assay P can be compared with an otherwise identical model using remote baseline assay B, but later-calendar reproducibility and missing-Y robustness are unresolved. This is a prognostic association about a subsequent recorded laboratory value, not a treatment response, clinical outcome, or action rule.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among the same frozen selected repeat-TACE episodes, does adding P to the locked B-plus-core forecast produce a reproducible, assay-specific change in a predeclared surrogate laboratory monitoring category, and is that apparent translation still admissible after accounting for assay-specific post-event Y observation and the parent's all-BP-eligible missing-Y identification envelope?

The question is clinically important because a statistically favorable laboratory forecast is not decision-relevant merely because RMSE improves. A monitoring or escalation workflow would need the forecast to move a patient across a stable, prespecified laboratory state boundary, and that conclusion is unsafe if Y is recorded preferentially in episodes with particular downstream care opportunities. The supplied data cannot establish a patient-important threshold, assay units, severity, completed follow-up, treatment administration, utility, safety, or benefit. Therefore the proposed category is explicitly a **surrogate forecast category**, not a clinical risk class or recommendation.

## New surrogate decision-relevance contract

The translation is deliberately within-assay and model-output based. It is not a clinical threshold analysis.

### Training-derived surrogate categories

For each assay and origin, derive `L_train`, `U_train`, and empirical training-Y quartiles `Q25_train`, `Q50_train`, and `Q75_train` from the exact finite observed training Y values used by the parent's parser. If the training range is empty/degenerate, a required quartile is nonfinite, or the IQR is zero, category translation is `unsupported` for that assay/origin. Do not inspect future Y to define categories. The categories are labels `Q1`–`Q4` for a **forecast laboratory value relative to this cohort's training distribution**; they must never be called normal, abnormal, mild, severe, low-risk, high-risk, or patient-important states.

For every future BP-eligible episode with finite frozen predictions, set `p0c=clip(p0,L_train,U_train)` and `p1c=clip(p1,L_train,U_train)`. Assign each to the corresponding training quartile with deterministic left-closed/right-open boundaries and a documented top-bin inclusion rule. Define:

* `movement_i = p1c - p0c`;
* `absolute_standardized_movement_i = |movement_i| / IQR_train`;
* `cross_i = 1[predicted_category(p1c) != predicted_category(p0c)]`;
* `up_i` and `down_i` as category movement in the ordered numeric laboratory scale, without calling either direction favorable.

Report, separately by assay, origin/block, and arm, the number of finite BP rows, median and IQR of `movement_i`, median absolute standardized movement, category-crossing fraction, upward/downward crossing fractions, and the fraction at each M0/M1 category. Suppress or label cells with fewer than ten BP-eligible episodes and do not pool albumin with total bilirubin. A crossing is a model-output change, not proof that a patient's disease state changed.

The primary decision-relevance summary is a two-part, predeclared conjunction for a block: (A) the parent's observed complete-triplet primary G is estimable and passes its inherited support gates, and the all-BP-eligible training-range Delta interval is strictly positive; and (B) the M1-versus-M0 category-crossing fraction and movement summary are estimable in both arms, with a patient-cluster fixed-prediction bootstrap 95% interval for crossing fraction and median movement bookkeeping. Part B is descriptive translation of a surrogate forecast; it cannot rescue a nonpositive/unsupported Delta or failed support. The crossing bootstrap resamples patients within arm with replacement, keeps all selected events and frozen predictions fixed, and does not refit or impute Y. Use a declared seed, 2,000 replicates where resources permit, and explicit failures/insufficient-cluster status. If a block has too few clusters, the crossing summary is `inconclusive`, not zero.

The conjunction is called `surrogate-translation-supportive` only when the primary and bound gates pass, no material differential-observation warning is present, both arms have adequate BP support, and the crossing estimate is not confined to a sparse observation stratum. It means only that the incremental model changes a cohort-relative surrogate forecast category in that selected calendar mixture while the observed-Y prognostic advantage survives the declared training-range envelope. It does not mean that clinicians should act, that a laboratory threshold has clinical meaning, or that outcomes improve.

### Observation-robustness precedence

Apply the following precedence before any favorable wording.

1. `computationally-invalid`: nonfinite predictions, malformed clocks, duplicate multiplication, failed inherited denominator assertions, or failed deterministic output checks.
2. `unsupported`: empty/degenerate training range or quartile/IQR, any observed future Y outside the locked training range for the temporal bound, or no estimable primary/bound quantity.
3. `adverse`: adequately supported primary M1 degradation or nonpositive bounded Delta; category movement cannot override it.
4. `selection-sensitive`: observed-case G favorable but Delta crosses zero, material observed-versus-missing gradient, strongly different assay-specific Y observation fractions, category crossings concentrated in a warning stratum, or crossing is unstable across arm/era.
5. `inconclusive`: failed arm gate, sparse clusters/cells, low transport or observation ESS, nonestimable bootstrap, or dependence on the descriptive full-source range.
6. `surrogate-translation-supportive`: only the conjunction above, and only with the surrogate-level wording.

A favorable category-crossing fraction with no favorable primary/bound result is `descriptive-only`; it is never upgraded by the verifier. Conversely, a robust positive Delta with negligible crossing is prognostically supportive but **not decision-relevant under this surrogate definition**. This makes the claimed advance falsifiable in both directions.

## Falsification and verifier tests

The compiler/verifier must exercise synthetic fixtures without treating them as HCC findings:

* favorable observed G plus a Delta interval crossing zero -> `selection-sensitive`, never supportive;
* positive Delta but zero category crossings or negligible movement -> prognostic association without surrogate translation;
* category crossings in only one arm or a cell below the support threshold -> inconclusive, not pooled support;
* large category movement but an adverse/nonpositive bounded Delta -> adverse, not decision-relevant;
* strongly differential exam/follow-up/document observation fractions or |SMD| >= 0.5 -> selection-sensitive unless the report is explicitly descriptive;
* future Y outside the pre-origin training range -> temporal bound unsupported, with no range widening;
* untimed clinical documents -> never placed in the Y window;
* examinations, encounters, orders, medications, and procedures -> never called completed care, assay interpretation, administration, or patient outcome;
* category cutpoints -> derived from training Y only, with no future leakage and no clinical-normality labels;
* complete-triplet primary fit, fixed-prediction bootstrap denominator, and all-BP Delta bounds -> unchanged by the translation layer;
* deterministic bounds -> never labeled confidence intervals, and model-specific MSE bounds -> never labeled sharp RMSE-difference bounds;
* any positive branch -> never upgraded to causal effect, treatment response, utility, safety, transport, actionability, or clinical benefit.

Computationally checkable outputs are source/header/schema hashes, duplicate-safe joins, inherited population/event/clock assertions, outcome-blind observation funnels, finite training cutpoints, prediction movement/category tables, patient-cluster bootstrap bookkeeping, primary G, Delta interval and tipping coordinate, status precedence, and privacy-safe aggregate serialization. Units/reference ranges, laboratory assay equivalence, clinical severity, imaging response, mortality, hepatic failure, completed follow-up, exposure intent/administration, external care, patient preferences, utilities, and patient-important benefit require adjudication and an independent temporal/external or prospective study.

## Expected meaning of results

Supportive surrogate translation would show only a reproducible, outcome-observation-aware laboratory forecast change in selected repeat-TACE episodes; it would motivate a prospectively adjudicated study of whether the forecast category maps to a clinically meaningful endpoint. Adverse results would weaken the claim that P adds usable prognostic information in this frozen setting. Selection-sensitive results would indicate that differential Y ascertainment prevents a decision-relevant conclusion even if complete cases look favorable. Inconclusive results would identify missing support, sparse arms, or absent assay metadata as the limiting evidence rather than support the hypothesis. No branch permits an action rule or patient-benefit claim from this HCC snapshot alone.
