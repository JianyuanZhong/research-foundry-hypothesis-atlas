The parent also does not establish causal benefit from ordering cystatin C or from prioritizing repeat assessment.

The falsifiable claim to test, conditional on certification, is:

> Among participants with certified baseline measurements and a qualifying complete repeat observation who actually return, does adding a baseline cystatin-C threshold indicator to a prespecified flexible creatinine/age/sex model improve out-of-sample prediction of the protocol-defined repeat creatinine-derived threshold state, and does it improve event concentration at a fixed review capacity?

This can inform the narrower future decision of whether an already-observed baseline cystatin-C result might help prioritize review in a program that has already scheduled and obtained a repeat measurement. It cannot determine whether cystatin C should be ordered, who will attend, whether a clinician should act, whether treatment changes, or whether patients benefit.

## Estimand and models

The primary estimand is an empirical schedule-marginal predictive contrast in the selected-attender population:

`Delta_Brier = E_A[((Y-p0(X0))^2 - (Y-p1(X0,E))^2)]`, with `X0=(eGFRcr_0, age_0, sex)` and `E_A` denoting expectation over the observed joint distribution of selected attenders, including their observed repeat schedules. Report `1000*Delta_Brier`. This is not a population-average risk because the source has no comparable repeat outcome for nonattenders, and it is not a fixed-horizon risk because `t` varies and is not modeled.

`M0` is ridge logistic regression using only baseline `eGFRcr_0`, baseline age, and sex. `M1` adds only `E` and `E*(eGFRcr_0-60)/10`. Use the inherited training-only restricted cubic spline basis for eGFR and age, sex interactions, centering/scaling, unpenalized intercept, fixed lambda grid `10^(-4),10^(-3.5),...,10^4`, and nested five-fold one-standard-error log-loss tuning. The compiler must materialize the inherited spline knot rule in the executable specification; no outcome-informed or full-data preprocessing is allowed. Neither model may use `t`, date, repeat biomarkers, attendance indicators, or any post-baseline field.

Use ten repeated outer five-fold cross-fits with seeds `260911` through `260920`, stratified by `E × Y`; inner seed is `outer_seed + 1000 + outer_fold`. All preprocessing, knots, and tuning are training-only, and every test `eid` is disjoint from training and tuning rows. Average the ten out-of-fold probabilities per participant. Clip only reported log loss to `[10^-6,1-10^-6]`, recording clipping counts; do not clip Brier probabilities.

The secondary estimand is a schedule-marginal, fixed-capacity ranking contrast within the same `S_A`, not a treatment or test-ordering estimand. Let `N=|S_A|`, `K=ceil(0.10*N)`. Rank participants by their participant-level mean out-of-fold probabilities, with exact ties broken by `SHA-256("direction-A-capacity-v2|"+eid)`, and define

`Delta_rank10 = 1000 * (sum_{i in topK(M1)} Y_i - sum_{i in topK(M0)} Y_i) / K`.

Both top-K sets contain exactly K members from the same selected-attender cohort and are formed without `t`; report only aggregate event counts, promoted/displaced counts, K, and the contrast. A positive value means that, under the observed and heterogeneous repeat schedule represented in `S_A`, M1's baseline-only ranking places more protocol-defined Y=1 observations in a fixed review-sized set than M0's ranking. It does not mean that a test order discovers additional cases, that an intervention prevents events, that a threshold has clinical utility, or that the result persists at a fixed time horizon. Report the primary contrast overall and descriptively in prespecified `t` bands `[2,4)`, `[4,6)`, `[6,8]`; bands cannot rescue a failed primary test and are not additional confirmatory hypotheses. Do not reweight or adjust the primary estimand using `t`.

## Inference, falsification, and branch rules

Test `Delta_Brier` first; only if its supportive gate passes may `Delta_rank10` be tested. For each contrast use paired participant bootstrap over the frozen ten-column prediction matrices, seed `260921`, 2,000 successful replicates, maximum 2,500 attempts, percentile 95% interval, and plus-one symmetric null-centered p-value. Preserve the parent's convergence, cell-support, calibration, comparator-signal, split-sign, and aggregate-privacy gates. Record failed fits and bootstrap attempts; never replace them silently.

The primary null is `Delta_Brier <= 0`; the secondary null is `Delta_rank10 <= 0`. Support requires all certificate/audit gates, exact cohort reconstruction, finite converged fits, fold isolation, calibration and comparator checks, adequate `E×Y` cell support, at least 8 of 10 repeat-specific primary contrasts positive, bootstrap completion, and a two-sided 95% interval excluding zero with p<0.05 for the primary. Only then may a positive secondary result be reported as a ranking concentration finding. Supportive language is limited to reproducible baseline incremental information and, conditionally, a schedule-marginal fixed-capacity ranking contrast in `S_A`.

Adverse means the prespecified primary contrast is wholly below zero, M1 reproducibly loses calibration while M0 passes, or the required repeat-specific signs favor M0. This is evidence against the stated incremental-information claim under this comparator and selected protocol. Inconclusive means the interval includes zero, signs are unstable, support/model gates fail, or bootstrap completion fails; it is not equivalence. Missing semantic or assay evidence is a neutral stop, not adverse or inconclusive biological evidence.

Predeclared falsifications are run without changing the primary estimand: (i) permute `E` within each training fold only, preserving the test fold and preventing label leakage; (ii) replace `E` by a training-fold-only random feature generated from a fixed seed and rerun the same pipeline; (iii) check that the apparent gain is not confined to one outer repeat; (iv) verify that removal of `t` causes no row-selection, feature, tuning, or ranking dependency; and (v) audit that top-K comparisons use identical eligible participants and exactly K members. A negative-control failure blocks supportive interpretation and is classified adverse or inconclusive under the same declared gate; it cannot be used post hoc to alter the hypothesis.
