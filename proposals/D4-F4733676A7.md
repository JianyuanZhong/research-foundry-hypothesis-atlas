## New additive recorded-sex estimands

For s in {0,1}, define

`Q_s = 1000/466 * [sum_{i in S_AG1} E18_i*1{sex_i=s} - sum_{i in S_AF1} E18_i*1{sex_i=s}]`.

The denominator is always the total 466 assay slots, never a stratum-specific selected count, survivors, observed events, union, or swaps. Q_s measures that stratum's additive contribution to the service-wide fixed-capacity contrast; it is not a within-sex incidence or prevalence. The exact identities

`Q_0 + Q_1 = Delta18`

and

`Q_s = 1000/466 * [sum_{AG1-only,sex=s}E18_i - sum_{AF1-only,sex=s}E18_i]`

must hold bit-for-bit within floating representation tolerance and exactly at the integer numerator level.

For each s and policy report selected count; E18 numerator; yield per 466; overlap, union, and Jaccard; AG-only/AF-only membership and event counts; C5 counts descriptively; and all additive identities. Do not estimate or report an interaction p-value, sex-specific model performance, stratum-reweighted effect, ratio when its denominator is zero, or a sex-specific policy.

The one-event non-inferiority margin for each Q_s is `-m=-2.145922746781116` per 1,000 total slots. This resolution-aware margin asks whether either stratum has evidence of at least one net event in AF1's favor. It is not a utility, equity, safety, cost, or patient-harm threshold.
