## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens nominate single circulating proteins (e.g. GDF15, CHI3L1, IL-6/CRP family) as predictors of many diseases. Competing explanations for a robust cross-disease signal:
- **A (disease-specific):** the protein marks a process preferential to disease *D*.
- **B (shared systemic axis):** it marks a shared allostatic-load / multimorbidity state ([K2], [K3]).
- **C (treatment/operative correlate):** it tracks surgery / operative management / care intensity rather than disease biology ([K1]).

Unresolved question: **after removing the shared healthy-vs-diseased and disease co-occurrence structure, does any individual protein retain a disease-preferential residual that is not explained by operative-treatment state?** This decides whether a nominated protein is a disease-biology candidate worth advancing to a disease-specific assay, or only a marker of being co-diagnosed / under operative treatment.

## Leading claim vs strongest rival, and where they differ
- Leading claim: at least some proteins retain a **disease-preferential** residual (A) beyond shared axes.
- Strongest rival: the residual is fully explained by **operative/treatment state (C)** and/or shared multimorbidity (B): among diseased-only participants with the SAME (qualified unrelated) operative state, the disease-preferential transfer gap collapses.
- Discriminating observation: the case-case transfer gap Δ_D^treat on a held-out participant half within a fixed qualified procedure stratum, relative to a within-stratum permutation null. A survives if Δ_D^treat exceeds the null band; C is favored if the raw gap is positive but Δ_D^treat collapses.

## Baseline vs substantive learned alternative (method comparison)
- **Baseline:** a 1-dimensional **shared-axis score** (e.g., first PC / aggregate inflammation score, as in [K2] ProAL50-style construction) used to ask whether a protein adds anything beyond the shared axis.
- **Learned alternative:** an **elastic-net / latent-axis (supervised dimensionality-reduction) residual model** fitted on a training half, evaluated on a held-out half, with the disease label as target and the shared axis + age + sex residualized out. It can reveal **protein-specific, non-collinear residuals** (which the 1-D aggregate collapses) and whether a sparse subset recovers Δ_D.
- **Why the alternative matters:** the 1-D score loses per-protein residual structure; a positive learned residual with a sparse signature is *what would advance a specific protein* to a disease-specific assay, whereas a 1-D score can only say "shared". Selection reason: remains CPU-feasible on 53k×2,923 (elastic net / SVD), with GPU optional; baseline is required as the null-comparison floor.
- **Deferral:** deep sequence/graph models deferred — no evidence they add scientific information here over a sparse linear residual, and the binding limit (no temporal anchor) caps interpretability.

## Analysis
1. Split participants into discovery/held-out halves (participant-level, stratified by age-decile×sex).
2. Restrict to the diseased-only case-case contrast for one pre-registered disease pair (e.g., ICD-10 **E78** vs **I25**).
3. **Treatment-safe rule (falsifiable, pre-registered):** an OPCS-3 block *P* qualifies as treatment-stratum for disease *D* iff within the Olink subset: n(P) ≥ 200, n(P∩D) ≥ 500, and lift = P(D|P)/P(D) ≤ 1.05. If no block qualifies, Δ_D^treat is **NOT IDENTIFIED**.
4. Permutation null: 1,000 within-stratum permutations (age-decile × sex × coarse comorbidity) preserving disease marginal.
5. Report Δ_D, Δ_D^treat, and the elastic-net residual with uncertainty; pre-register supportive/adverse/inconclusive bands against the permutation null band.

## Falsification criteria
- **Supportive (A):** Δ_D^treat exceeds the 95% permutation-null band for ≥1 protein, replicated in the held-out half.
- **Adverse (C/B favored):** raw Δ_D positive but Δ_D^treat collapses into the null band, OR the 1-D shared-axis baseline already explains the signal with no learned residual.
- **Inconclusive:** no procedure block qualifies → Δ_D^treat not identified; or strata under-powered (report honestly, do not claim a null).
