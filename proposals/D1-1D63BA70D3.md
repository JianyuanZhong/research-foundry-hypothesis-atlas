## Question, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

After vasopressor-treated shock has resolved, should a markedly fluid-positive, invasively ventilated adult with adequate blood pressure, urine output, and potassium start or restart an ICU-input-system recorded furosemide or bumetanide delivery during the next 24 hours? The possible benefit is earlier durable liberation from an endotracheal airway; the important countervailing harm is an early creatinine rise or renal replacement therapy (RRT).

Existing evidence supports narrower claims only. The parent’s inspected weaning statement and renal-congestion review support a conventional 48-hour post-extubation observation window and continued uncertainty about outcome-guided decongestion, not this loop strategy’s efficacy or safety. The full Europe PMC XML of Chanda et al. 2026 was inspected (PMCID PMC13223704; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2026.101374; frozen source [source checksum]). Its 54,210 ICU admissions show low concordance between KDIGO-criterion and coded AKI (kappa 0.37) and identify missing preadmission creatinine as a limitation. That supports keeping record-defined creatinine change distinct from clinical AKI; it provides no loop-effect evidence. The three local ambition papers remain methodological demonstrations: two main texts were locally available, while the cancer main paper was unavailable and only its supplement could be inspected. None supplies evidence for this clinical effect.

The unresolved primary respiratory hypothesis is unchanged: in the frozen measured-overlap population, the 24-hour ICU-recorded loop strategy is associated with more than a five-percentage-point increase in the parent’s airway-anchored 48-hour extubation-success risk versus recorded avoidance. The separate harm hypothesis is that the same strategy increases an all-eligible record-defined 48-hour creatinine-rise risk by at least three points or observed RRT7 by at least five points. Failure to detect those harm margins is not renal safety.

This child repairs a decisive source contradiction. The parent required follow-up creatinine `storetime<=charttime`, although official MIMIC documentation defines `charttime` as usually specimen acquisition and `storetime` as result availability, usually later. It also invoked a “complete scheduled opportunity,” but `labevents` contains no laboratory-order or schedule field proving complete surveillance. A direct treatment-blind source audit found 4,312,983/4,319,091 item-50912 rows with storetime after charttime and only 6,102 with storetime at/before charttime. In 5,795 broad structural landmarks, the parent rule yielded zero usable post-t0 rows. This is feasibility evidence only; no exposure arms or effects were inspected.
