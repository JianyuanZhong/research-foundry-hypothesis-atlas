## Proposed relationship and clinical importance

At similar total body adiposity, higher visceral fat may be more specifically associated with subsequent metabolic disease, while higher posterior-thigh muscle-fat infiltration (MFI) may be more specifically associated with later functional limitation or falls. If true, body composition would contain clinically distinct information lost by treating adiposity as one generic risk factor. If false, the added complexity of separating these phenotypes is not justified by these data.

The leading claim is that fat distribution carries outcome-specific information beyond total adiposity. The strongest rival is that the apparent divergence is explained by baseline severity, age/sex, measurement timing, MRI selection, or observation practices. This observational comparison cannot establish mechanism, causality, or treatment benefit.
