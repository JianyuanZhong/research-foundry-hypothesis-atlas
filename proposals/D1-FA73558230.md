## Evidence, unresolved claim, and falsifiable hypothesis

The available evidence supports that the MIMIC snapshot contains admission-linked radiology text, discharge text, service history, ICU exposure, and shifted within-subject timestamps from which the inherited frame and stored-text route can be constructed. It does not show that a discharge artifact was visible at physical departure, that a service code identifies the responsible team, that a panel's assertion is clinically correct, or that a route improves care.

The unresolved claim is whether a support-qualified atom remains certifiable when every observed panel output is evaluated under an independently executable finite set of compatible audit states, including arbitrary correlated/shared reviewer error, rather than a favorable consensus. The hypothesis is that each atom placed on an `AUTO` or `RECON` allow-list will pass inherited adverse minimax gates, the independent H1/H2/H3 challenge gates, the control gates, and the atom-specific workload gates under jointly optimized compatible bounds. Falsification is any atom-level bound crossing its preregistered gate, any invariant-control route flip, any decisive-control violation, any unresolved/nonempty-state contract failure, or any ledger overflow. Unsupported and rare atoms remain `DEFER`.

## Executable compatible-set construction and joint bounds

For admission `i`, retain the sealed observations `O_i` from parent lanes and H1/H2/H3. A compatible member is a finite pair `(z,e)`, where `z` is a candidate *audit interpretation* consisting of one complete finite atomic tuple for each observed unit and `e` is a subset of panel IDs marked as potentially erroneous. This is not latent clinical truth and no observed field is edited.

Construct `Omega_i` deterministically as follows.

1. Parse malformed, missing, unreadable, or nonresponsive panel outputs into the fixed `UNKNOWN` observation with an adverse reason code.
2. Enumerate all cross-products of distinct units from the two index lanes, two audit lanes, minority-unit audit, and H1/H2/H3; deduplicate only reciprocal restatements with the same locked unit identity.
3. For each candidate `z`, apply the truth table and locked matcher. If a nonmissing panel field is not equal to `z`, its panel must be in `e`; if it is equal, either membership is permitted or prohibited exactly by the pre-label error-flag rule. An error flag permits any value in the finite `field_value` domain but never deletes, edits, or imputes the observed output. Thus all-panel shared error is explicitly permitted, while the observed panel records remain immutable.
4. Reject `(z,e)` unless every non-error observation is retained exactly, every route/unit precedence predicate holds, and the reason code is complete. For unknown fields, retain both `MATCH` and `NONMATCH` only where the locked matcher permits both; never choose a favorable interpretation solely because it lowers an endpoint.
5. Store at least one state for every nonempty observed pattern. If none exists, emit `OMEGA_EMPTY` and `NO_SELECTION`; never silently replace it with consensus or a model.
6. For each state store route, adverse indicator, route numerator/denominator contributions, workload/control contributions, error flags, and a proof trace to each input panel. Store `|Omega_i|`, state hashes, and lower/upper contribution for every estimand.

For totals, jointly minimize/maximize weighted contributions over the Cartesian product of admission `Omega_i`. For route ratios, optimize numerator and denominator together using exact rational arithmetic and enumerate small cases; use a pinned Dinkelbach implementation only after a fixture proves equivalence. Zero denominators produce `UNDEFINED`, not zero. Never combine a favorable numerator state with an incompatible adverse denominator state. Use design weights from the inherited two-phase design, exact stratified finite-population inversion, and one 20,000-replicate Rao-Wu max-statistic family. The primary reported interval is the widest of compatible, design, and simultaneous intervals. A unanimous H1/H2/H3 observation can therefore retain an adverse shared-error state; agreement is not a gold standard.

Mandatory compatibility fixtures are: unanimous shared-wrong labels with a nonempty adverse state; one-panel missingness widening rather than narrowing the interval; a two-admission ratio where separate extrema are incompatible and joint optimization differs; a contradictory-field state requiring an error flag; an `OMEGA_EMPTY` malformed fixture that fails closed; and a correlated-error case in which no latent-class model is allowed to narrow the primary interval. A latent-class estimate is diagnostic only absent a pre-label identifying restriction and external validation.

## Outcomes, falsification, and conclusion ceiling

Supportive results require all inherited gates plus a complete truth-table/nonempty-state certificate, nonempty compatible sets for every analyzed admission, passing joint adverse bounds, control invariance, challenge support, and both global and atom-specific workload ledgers. They support only nomination of the exact atom signatures for a prospective within-site silent bridge.

Adverse results include a bound crossing an AUTO/RECON/TIMING/DEFER gate, an adverse-compatible AUTO state, unexplained truth-table emptiness, invariant-control flips above 0.02, decisive-control violations above 0.05, missingness/control-infeasibility above 0.05, or any ledger overflow. The affected atom is excluded; a global contract failure invalidates selection.

Inconclusive results include insufficient positive-probability support, fewer than 30 candidate-route admissions or 10 non-sentinel challenges where required, zero/undefined denominators, wide shared-error bounds, sparse atoms, `OMEGA_EMPTY`, infeasible controls, broken seals, unresolved schema/key/time checks, or inability to prove a unique allocation. Inconclusive is not evidence of safety, truth, benefit, or transport.

MIMIC cannot establish actual viewing, finalization or signature semantics, physical departure visibility, communication, responsibility, orders/referrals, scheduling, patient preference, outside care, follow-up, harm, appropriateness, utility, benefit, causality, or external/forward-calendar transport. Stored text and service/ICU proxies are not those constructs; radiology text is not imaging. No retrospective automation or clinical route recommendation is permitted. Any stronger conclusion requires prospective actual-calendar workflow observations, target-linked actions and outcomes, expert clinical adjudication, and a separately governed study.
