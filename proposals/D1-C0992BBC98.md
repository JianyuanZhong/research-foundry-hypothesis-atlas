# HCC day-42 endpoint triangulation for operational repeat-TACE transport

Status: substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. No cohort fit or clinical result is claimed.

## Scientific opening and falsifiable hypothesis

The parent asks whether day-42 recorded assay state adds held-out information for a later locally coded repeat TACE after workflow channels and transport support are audited. The remaining interpretation boundary is the outcome: a literal `手术 == tace` row may be a site-code shortcut, while semantically equivalent hepatic-arterial embolization rows and contemporaneous imaging/order/medication records may mark a more specific operational treatment episode. Conversely, those corroborators may simply identify institutions and visits that document care more completely. The available files make this boundary testable but not clinically adjudicated.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is narrower than intent or response. [K1] supports treatment-adjacent recorded prediction in a selected repeat-TACE cohort; [K2] bounds response as an imaging/necrosis-aware construct; [K3] illustrates ascertainment, documentation and selection limits in treatment-adjacent recorded comparisons. None validates this site's literal code or a composite as intent or imaging response.

Hypothesis H: among inherited eligible patients, the assay-state increment `Delta_S = L(A)-L(B)` remains positive and not materially workflow-confined when the event is redefined as a corroborated operational TACE episode (O1), and has the same direction for a stricter cross-domain episode (O2). The leading rival is workflow/ascertainment: encounter opportunity, order availability and peri-procedure documentation jointly create both an assay record and a composite endpoint. A severity/selection rival is incompletely measured disease state. Replication supports reusable recorded information for these operational events only; a process-matched or lead-time-sensitive increment supports workflow; unsupported composites are inconclusive.

This is a predictive/descriptive endpoint-validation experiment, not a causal treatment, response, mechanism, or benefit study. Resolution matters because replication would justify prioritizing adjudicated state/intent linkage, while process-only evidence redirects acquisition toward scheduling, outside-care and documentation capture.

## Fixed operational dictionaries and exact endpoint criteria

The dictionaries are frozen before outcome scoring, based on inspected field availability and terminology census rather than held-out outcome differences. Unicode normalization is NFKC, case-folding, trim, and removal of spaces/full-width punctuation; retain raw values only for audit. Never search `检查所见` or `检查诊断`.

Let P(s) be true when a normalized procedure label is exactly `tace`, or contains `肝动脉` and `栓塞` and at least one of `化疗` or `灌注`, while containing none of `射频`, `微波`, `消融`, `活检`, `穿刺`, `引流`. This captures literal TACE and hepatic-arterial chemoembolization variants but is still only a procedure record.

Let I(s) be true for an examination/order name containing one of `肝脏`, `肝动脉`, `上腹部`, `全腹部` and one of `增强`, `造影`, `DWI`, `MRCP`, `CT`, `MRI`, `超声`. I is a type/order dictionary, never findings-based response adjudication. Let Q(s) be an order name containing `TACE`, or containing `肝动脉` plus one of `栓塞`, `化疗`, `造影`. Let M(s) be a medication name containing one of `碘化油`, `明胶`, `聚明胶`, `顺铂`, `多柔比星`, `阿霉素`, `表柔比星`. These are not intent labels; contrast/chemotherapy agents have other indications.

A valid encounter link E(p) requires the same patient and visit key and either valid `就诊时间` within two elapsed days of procedure start t, or valid `入院时间 <= t <= 出院时间`. Missing discharge makes the second route false. A corroborator requires valid time: examination `开始时间`, order `开立时间` (availability) or valid `开始时间`, medication `开始时间`.

- O0: inherited first later literal-code TACE at t in [43,181), with inherited population/censoring.
- O1 (primary triangulated): first later procedure p with P(`手术`), valid `开始时间=t`, E(p), and at least one same-visit corroborator: I(`检查`) with `开始时间 in [t-42,t+7]`, or Q(`医嘱(非药品)`) with valid `开立时间 in [t-42,t+7]`. Event time is qualifying procedure t, never an earlier corroborator.
- O2 (secondary stricter): O1 plus same-visit M(`用药`) with valid `开始时间 in [t-1,t+1]`. It requires medication evidence in addition to the imaging/order corroborator.

Windows are inclusive. A P row lacking corroboration is a timed negative for O1/O2 when follow-up reaches the relevant window/horizon, and is retained in a near-miss audit. A literal-code repeat failing O1 is negative for O1 if follow-up is complete, not missing. A valid-time procedure is not negative merely because its end time is absent. Same patient/start duplicates collapse while retaining evidence-row counts; distinct starts use earliest t.

The primary population remains the parent literal-code early-repeat population. Separately audit any timed P procedure in (0,43) as an early-alias history; a sensitivity excludes such patients before O1/O2 fitting. Untimed P rows are flagged, never imputed. O1/O2 are operational episode definitions, not intent, response or benefit.

## Analysis, alternatives and compute

Use earliest 80% of eligible index dates for development and latest 20% untouched test, patient-disjoint, with five grouped development folds. Freeze dictionaries, encoders, imputation/scaling and observation/censoring models in development. Fit transparent elastic-net pooled-logistic discrete-time hazards for each O0/O1/O2 and each parent ablation: M0, M0+P_obs, M0+P_care, A=M0+P_obs+P_care, B=A+S. Exact/semantic procedure events never enter predictors.

For endpoint j report paired held-out ordinary and all-source-censoring-weighted log-loss `Delta_S,j=L(A_j)-L(B_j)`, workflow increment `Delta_W,j=L(M0+P_obs)-L(A_j)`, Brier/integrated Brier, calibration, AUROC/AUPRC, event/risk-set counts, ESS and weight tails. Report `G_j=Delta_S,j-Delta_W,j` as the state-versus-workflow contrast; it is not causal adjustment. Do not compare raw loss magnitude across endpoints without prevalence/null-loss context. O0 remains primary; O1 is the boundary-validation endpoint; O2 is secondary and cannot rescue O1.

Extend the parent's max-t patient-bootstrap family once before fitting across O0/O1/O2 overall state/workflow contrasts, endpoint contrasts and the five non-horizon transport axes for each supported endpoint. Preserve paired patients, person-days, risk sets and weights. The 0.005 weighted-log-loss heterogeneity margin is operational, not a clinical MCID. O1/O2 require >=50 overall events plus inherited per-stratum gates (>=15 development and >=15 test events, >=100 weighted ESS, nondegenerate observation/censoring support, no frozen weight-tail failure). Unsupported rows remain explicit unsupported/inconclusive, never pooled, dropped or treated as null evidence.

Lead-time/ascertainment controls are fixed: (1) corroborators restricted to pre-procedure [-42,-1], with event still t; (2) [-28,-1] and [-7,-1] labelled sensitivities; (3) remove process/assay records in (35,42] and truncate process at day28; (4) ordinary versus development-fold observation weighting; (5) exact O0 versus semantic P, O1 and O2, including literal failures and early aliases. A composite matched by P_obs/P_care, reproduced by timestamp/value/process permutations, or erased by pre-procedure restriction is adverse to a state interpretation and compatible with workflow. Sparse, unsupported, wide, weight-failing or incomplete results are inconclusive.

Supportive means O0 and O1 pass support/uncertainty gates, Delta_S is positive with simultaneous lower bound above zero, G is not workflow-dominated, and direction persists in the pre-procedure control; O2 can be supportive or inconclusive and cannot rescue O1. Adverse rejects only the narrow reusable-state interpretation for that endpoint. Stronger conclusions require chart/imaging adjudication, treatment-intent/scheduling fields, validated medication indications, units/reference ranges, outside-care capture and external validation. The snapshot lacks all of these and examination rows are not adjudicated response.

Baseline selection: elastic-net hazards, 4 CPUs/16 GiB, expected 1-4 hours including materialization/weights/bootstrap; planning estimate, not measured. Learned alternative: compact time-aware GRU over the same -365 through +42 event stream, O0/O1/O2 heads, channels, split, weights, metrics and three fixed development seeds; one allocated A100, 4 CPUs/16 GiB, expected 2-6 hours, unverified. It could reveal nonlinear trajectories and order/exam timing lost by summaries, but cannot resolve intent/response or unsupported strata. GPU work must request one allocated device and use `cuda:0`; an unallocated shell check is not a feasibility test. Elastic-net is selected for the confirmatory decision; GRU is deferred sensitivity. Larger transformers, finding NLP and latent causal/intent models are deferred because missing dependencies do not answer this boundary.
