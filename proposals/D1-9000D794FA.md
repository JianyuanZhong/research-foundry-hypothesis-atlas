# Independent-source corroboration audit of RT Vent On/Off transitions

## Substantive child, clinical question, and hypothesis

This child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` preserves its exact 4,103-event all-stay cohort, transition mapping, 30-minute same-direction deduplication, setting-family definition, half-open six-hour windows, subtype separation, shams, `Continued` controls, complete-window/conflict-clean sensitivity, and frozen-landmark non-overlap result. It addresses the parent's principal unresolved semantic limitation: the explicit `RT Vent On/Off` marker and setting-family signal could be products of the same respiratory-charting workflow.

The falsifiable hypothesis was that independently sourced, value-aware evidence would directionally corroborate a substantial fraction of transitions: a valid `respiratoryCare` interval onset near `Start`, a valid interval endpoint near literal `Off` or `Suspended`, an invasive or explicitly absent airway value near the event, or temporally usable treatment/procedure-note evidence. A second hypothesis was that the inherited setting-family rise/fall would retain its direction within independently defined corroboration and discordance strata and across hospitals.

This question matters because apparent calibration between two fields in one documentation workflow should not be used as validation of invasive ventilation episodes, intubation, extubation, or liberation. The substantive advance is a negative but decision-relevant source audit: it identifies which independent fields are actually usable in this event population, separates endpoint chronology from numeric availability, quantifies discordance and site heterogeneity, and prevents a strong same-workflow discontinuity from being upgraded into a bedside-state label.

## Evidence-supported claim versus unresolved claim

Before this child, the strongest supported claim was that bounded setting-family activity is strongly time-aligned with structured RT transition documentation: parent On changed +86.43 percentage points, Off/Suspended -52.42 points, with expected sham, `Continued`, strict-window, conflict-clean, and hospital sensitivity results. The unresolved claim was whether independent eICU sources corroborate the intended transition direction and thereby reduce the shared-workflow explanation.

The executed child supports a narrower conclusion: setting direction persists in virtually every adequately populated independent-source availability or chronology stratum, but independent clinical-state corroboration is predominantly unavailable, ambiguous, displaced, or temporally discordant. It therefore does not reduce the core semantic uncertainty. In particular, a setting rise after `Start` persists even among 1,154 events whose valid `respiratoryCare` interval ends near `Start`, a pattern incompatible with treating those interval records as straightforward onset confirmation.

## Population, temporal boundaries, and variables

The primary population is unchanged: 2,720 deduplicated `Start` events and 1,383 Off-family events, comprising 1,016 literal `Off` and 367 `Suspended`, across 12 hospitals. The parent pre/post setting outcome remains positivity for at least two distinct inspected setting families in `[T-360,T)` and `[T,T+360)`. No corroboration field selects or removes an inherited event.

Mutually exclusive interval strata were assigned with precedence rules:

1. Start ordered onset: a valid interval start in `[T-60,T+60)`.
2. Start endpoint discordance: absent ordered onset, a valid interval end in `[T-60,T+60)`.
3. Start pre-existing context: absent the above, a valid interval starts before `T-60` and ends after `T+60`.
4. Off ordered end: a valid interval end in `[T-60,T+60)`.
5. Off discordance: absent ordered end, a valid interval spans beyond `T+60`.
6. Off displaced endpoint: absent the above, a valid interval ends in `[T-360,T-60)`.
7. For either transition: other valid interval, `respiratoryCare` present without a valid interval, or no `respiratoryCare` source.

A near endpoint is structured interval chronology, not proof of ventilator initiation or cessation. A displaced endpoint and an interval ending near `Start` are discordance/ambiguity signals, not clinical contradiction. Treatment/note table and path presence were profiled separately and were never temporally imputed.

## Analysis and falsification criteria

For every transition subtype and corroboration stratum, the experiment reports events, stays, hospitals, inherited pre/post rates, paired change, and a fixed-seed 1,000-replicate stay-cluster percentile interval. Hospital-specific changes and leave-one-hospital-out pooled ranges assess concentration. Component availability and discordance are reported before outcome contrasts.

Supportive independent corroboration required non-sparse ordered onset evidence for Start and ordered endpoint evidence for Off, meaningful value-aware airway or temporally ordered treatment/note support, low directional discordance, and persistence of expected setting direction across multiple hospitals. Adverse evidence included predominance of oppositely timed endpoints, explicit conflicting values, or expected setting direction being equally strong in discordant/unavailable strata. Inconclusive evidence was predeclared for sparse ordered components, blank airway values, absent event-stay pathways, missing temporal offsets, or insufficient hospital coverage. The second hypothesis—directional persistence of the setting signal by stratum—is supported if adequately populated Start strata remain positive and literal-Off strata negative with clustered intervals excluding zero and leave-one-hospital-out direction preserved.

### Component availability and discordance

* `respiratoryCare` existed for 2,488/2,720 Start, 930/1,016 literal Off, and 337/367 Suspended events. At least one valid ordered interval existed anywhere in the stay for 1,952, 716, and 198 events, respectively. Global field profiling found 552,257 rows across 18,217 stays with all four interval fields equal to zero; across all rows, 119,611 had end after start, 685,205 equal endpoints, and 60,565 reversed endpoints. Thus non-null numeric fields were not valid evidence.
* Only 10/2,720 Start events (0.37%, three hospitals) had a valid onset within ±60 minutes. In contrast, 1,156 had an interval end within ±60 minutes; after precedence, 1,154 were classified as endpoint-discordant. Another 377 had a pre-existing interval spanning beyond Start. Endpoint profiling showed the nearest valid interval-start distance had median 300 minutes, whereas nearest interval-end distance had median 30 minutes. This is adverse to interpreting the interval fields as independent Start confirmation.
* Only 13/1,016 literal Off events (1.28%, five hospitals) and 2/367 Suspended events (0.54%, one hospital) had a valid interval end within ±60 minutes. For literal Off, 211 endpoints were displaced 1–6 hours before the RT marker; only four intervals spanned beyond it. Ordered support for Suspended was too sparse and its two-event setting contrast was opposite in direction.
* No nearest `airwaytype` was invasive or `No Artificial Airway`. Near-event values were ambiguous/blank for 2,481 Start, 914 literal Off, and 44 Suspended events; the remainder lacked a value in the window. Airway data therefore cannot form an invasive-airway-supported stratum in this cohort.
* No inherited event stay joined to any `treatment` row. Although the global vocabulary contains relevant pathways, treatment provides no event-level or stay-level corroboration here.
* The note table existed for 2,673 Start, 978 literal Off, and 341 Suspended events, but none had exact `Intubation` or `Extubation` procedure-note types. Note presence is therefore availability, not corroboration, and narrative review is unavailable.

### Setting direction within strata

The inherited setting-family direction persisted despite the failed independent corroboration:

* Start endpoint-discordant events (n=1,154; 1,152 stays; 12 hospitals) changed 6.85% to 95.58%, +88.73 points; stay-cluster 95% interval +86.83 to +90.46. Leave-one-hospital-out changes remained +87.75 to +93.79 points.
* Start pre-existing-span events (n=377; 375 stays; 12 hospitals) changed 1.06% to 97.35%, +96.29 points; interval +94.15 to +98.13; leave-one-hospital-out +95.97 to +96.94.
* Start with no valid interval despite `respiratoryCare` presence (n=536; 451 stays; 12 hospitals) changed +84.33 points; interval +80.57 to +88.07. No-source Start events (n=232) changed +87.07 points; interval +81.45 to +92.66. Other-valid-interval Start events (n=411) changed +73.24 points; interval +68.15 to +78.36.
* Ordered-onset Start events changed +90 points, but only 10 events across three hospitals make this inconclusive rather than validating.
* Literal-Off ordered-end events changed -53.85 points (n=13; interval -76.92 to -23.08), but were sparse. Literal-Off displaced endpoints 1–6 hours before the marker (n=211; six hospitals) changed 95.26% to 1.42%, -93.84 points; interval -96.68 to -90.52, with leave-one-hospital-out -100 to -92.07. Other-valid-interval, no-valid-interval, and no-source literal-Off strata changed -63.73, -55.61, and -60.47 points, respectively, all with clustered intervals excluding zero and stable leave-one-hospital-out direction.
* Suspended remained distinct. Its adequately sized strata had much smaller falls: other-valid interval -10.88 points (n=193; interval -16.86 to -5.81), no-valid interval -7.19 points (n=139; interval -15.08 to +0.93), and no source -16.67 points (n=30; interval -33.33 to 0). The two ordered-end events changed +50 points. These findings reinforce that `Suspended` must not be treated as equivalent to literal `Off`.

Across adequately populated strata, setting direction was preserved across hospitals, but this does not establish independence: the strongest Start rise occurred in pre-existing/endpoint-discordant rather than ordered-onset strata, and the strongest literal-Off fall occurred when interval endpoints preceded the RT marker by 1–6 hours. Hospital 73 contributed most events in several strata; leave-one-hospital-out stability addresses pooled direction, not transportability or bedside validity.

## Interpretation

The first hypothesis is adverse/inconclusive. Independent value-aware corroboration is too sparse or unavailable to distinguish invasive-airway-supported from clinically unsupported RT transitions. Valid interval chronology often points to a different documentation boundary: ends cluster around `Start`, while literal-Off ends often precede `Off`. Airway values are blank, treatment rows do not overlap event stays, and procedure-note types are absent. This does not mean the RT markers are clinically false; it means these public structured fields cannot adjudicate them.

The second hypothesis is supported: the parent's setting-family rise after Start and fall after literal Off persists within independently defined availability, displaced, and discordance strata, with clustered uncertainty and hospital sensitivity. That persistence strengthens the claim that the setting discontinuity is a robust documentation phenomenon, while weakening any argument that the same discontinuity has been independently validated as bedside invasive ventilation. `Suspended` is weaker and frequently inconclusive.

The exact inherited controls remain part of the evidentiary chain without redefinition: deterministic shifted shams, `Continued` controls, complete-window/conflict-clean and isolated-transition sensitivities all preserve expected setting direction. The exact frozen-cohort availability finding also remains unchanged: no decisive transition occurred in either ±6-hour landmark bin of any of the 3,788 frozen rows. This child is an all-stay semantic audit, not within-landmark validation.

## What would require additional evidence

Structured concordance cannot prove intubation, extubation, invasive airway state, active invasive ventilation, liberation, continuity of support, causality, quality, or benefit. Those claims require bedside device-connectivity or ventilator audit logs, reliable tube insertion/removal timestamps, modality and mode at event time, genuine RT narrative, and blinded clinical adjudication sampled across hospitals and transition subtypes. A subsequent study should oversample endpoint-discordant Start, displaced literal-Off, and Suspended events and report adjudicated PPV/NPV, sensitivity/specificity, timing error, and inter-rater agreement.

Automatically checkable claims are source hashes, exact cohort counts, joins, parser validity rules, endpoint windows and precedence, component availability, setting rates, clustered intervals, hospital estimates, leave-one-hospital-out ranges, and preservation of inherited controls. Whether any event represents a true bedside state is not automatically checkable and must not be inferred by a compiler or verifier.
