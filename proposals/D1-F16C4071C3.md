# Bounded-airway and interval-field audit of eICU ventilation care-plan receipt

## Scientific question, advance, and hypothesis

Two assessed-valid target-independent reconstructions agreed that the exact eICU Ventilation care-plan targets are received at only about 3% of otherwise eligible person-landmarks and are concentrated in few hospitals, but they produced materially different denominators: 3,722 rows using the latest explicit invasive `airwaytype` at any prior time versus 4,685 rows using a broader textual-airway-or-recent-`RT Vent On/Off` proxy. The unresolved, falsifiable question is whether the adverse label-coverage finding survives clinically plausible limits on how long an airway observation may persist, and whether the parent denominator difference is attributable to invasive-ventilation ascertainment while strict physiology, exclusions, target classification, and target-independent first-stay freezing remain fixed.

The hypothesis tested here is that care-plan target receipt remains below 5% and site-concentrated under 48-, 24-, and 6-hour latest-explicit-airway lookbacks. The strongest claim supported before this child was only that receipt was sparse under two proxy definitions. This child advances the evidence by measuring denominator attrition and row/stay overlap under bounded persistence assumptions and by auditing whether eICU's interval fields can define an active-ventilation denominator. It does not test whether an SBT/evaluation occurred or benefited a patient.

## Frozen design and exact data bindings

Use eICU 2.0 snapshot `[source checksum]` (catalog [source checksum]). Generate landmarks L = 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes. Adults are age at least 18 (`> 89` mapped to 90) and must have `unitdischargeoffset > L`; the complete-window sensitivity requires `unitdischargeoffset > L+360`.

All variants preserve the inherited strict pre-L physiology: latest normalized FiO2 and latest PEEP/CPAP in `[L-360,L)`, FiO2 at most 0.50 and PEEP at most 8; at least two SpO2 values in `[L-120,L)` with median 88 to below 100; latest respiratory rate 8-35; and median MAP at least 65 using periodic and aperiodic values. Exclude an EOL discussion before L and `Spontaneous - adequate` in `[L-1440,L)`. Within each variant, identify qualifying stays without either target, select the first by `uniquepid`, `unitvisitnumber`, then `patientunitstayid`, freeze it, and retain all qualifying landmarks in that stay.

Read-only source bindings are:

- `patient.csv.gz`, table `patient`: `patientunitstayid`, `uniquepid`, `hospitalid`, `age`, `unitvisitnumber`, `unitdischargeoffset`, joined by `patientunitstayid` and ordered as above.
- `respiratoryCare.csv.gz`, table `respiratoryCare`: `patientunitstayid`, `respcarestatusoffset`, `airwaytype`, `ventstartoffset`, `ventendoffset`, `priorventstartoffset`, `priorventendoffset`. The primary unbounded and bounded rules use the latest nonmissing `airwaytype` before L and exact values `Oral ETT`, `Nasal ETT`, `Tracheostomy`, `Double-Lumen Tube`, or `Cricothyrotomy`; bounded ages are `L-respcarestatusoffset <= 2,880`, `<=1,440`, or `<=360` minutes. Interval diagnostics evaluate every respiratory-care status row before L, not only airway-bearing rows.
- `respiratoryCharting.csv.gz`, table `respiratoryCharting`: `patientunitstayid`, `respchartoffset`, `respchartvaluelabel`, `respchartvalue`. FiO2 and PEEP/CPAP use `[L-360,L)`; the reconciliation branch treats `respchartvaluelabel='RT Vent On/Off'` and trimmed values `Continued` or `Start` as positive in that window, matching the values actually present among the broader parent's allowed set (`on`, `continued`, `start`, `started`).
- `vitalPeriodic.csv.gz`, table `vitalPeriodic`: `patientunitstayid`, `observationoffset`, `sao2`, `respiration`, `systemicmean` in `[L-120,L)`.
- `vitalAperiodic.csv.gz`, table `vitalAperiodic`: `patientunitstayid`, `observationoffset`, `noninvasivemean` in `[L-120,L)`.
- `carePlanEOL.csv.gz`, table `carePlanEOL`: `patientunitstayid`, `cpleoldiscussionoffset` before L.
- `carePlanGeneral.csv.gz`, table `carePlanGeneral`: `patientunitstayid`, `cplitemoffset`, `cplgroup`, `cplitemvalue`; the pre-L spontaneous exclusion is applied before selection. Only after each cohort is frozen, Ventilation-group rows in `[L,L+360]` produce four states: `daily_evaluation`, `no_daily_trial`, `other_ventilation_label`, or `no_target_label`. Contradictory exact targets at the earliest same offset are retained as other and flagged.

## Ascertainment variants and prespecified interpretation

The reference is the parent's unbounded latest-explicit-airway rule. Primary sensitivity variants cap that latest airway at 48, 24, and 6 hours. Report for every independently frozen cohort its rows, stays, persons, hospitals, receipt categories, complete-window receipt, hospital coverage, target-free hospitals, landmark counts, and intersection/union/Jaccard plus gained/lost rows and stay intersection versus the reference. Gained rows under a stricter variant are possible because variant-specific first-qualifying-stay freezing can select a different stay for the same person.

A reconciliation branch reproduces the broader parent's code-level airway substring rule (`ett|endotracheal|trach`) or recent positive RT Vent On/Off, while keeping this executable's inherited strict physiology and target logic fixed. Exact denominator equality without row-level support is not identity, so residual discrepancies are reported rather than erased.

Three interval diagnostics are deliberately non-primary: a current closed interval requires nonzero `ventstartoffset < L <= ventendoffset`; a prior closed interval analogously uses `priorventstartoffset` and `priorventendoffset`; an open-zero sensitivity interprets current `ventendoffset=0` as open-ended. The last is not a validated interpretation.

The prespecified supportive gate requires at least one bounded cohort to have receipt at least 10% and targets in at least 75% of denominator hospitals. The prespecified adverse-robust gate requires all three bounded cohorts to have receipt below 5%, at least half their hospitals target-free, and row Jaccard at least 0.70 versus the unbounded cohort. Otherwise the joint robustness verdict is inconclusive. This overlap condition separates stability of the documentation result from stability of the clinical denominator.

## Executed audit

The executed audit scanned all required sources and exactly reproduced all 3,722 unbounded-parent rows on stay, person, hospital, landmark, and four-state category. A separate harmonized executable also independently reconstructed the frozen inherited positive control: 540/540 rows, 443 stays, and 74 hospitals, with zero missing or extra keys and exact agreement on stay, person, hospital, landmark, strategy, and decision offset. Thus the positive control is direct rather than merely inferred from parent identity.

The reference contained 3,722 rows, 2,024 stays/persons, and 77 hospitals, with 108 targets (2.902%), 18 hospitals with any target and 59/77 target-free. The bounded results were:

- 48 hours: 3,150 rows, 1,888 stays, 75 hospitals; 75 evaluation, 28 no-trial, 191 other, 2,856 no target; receipt 103/3,150 (3.270%); 57/75 hospitals target-free. Versus reference, intersection 3,141, union 3,731, Jaccard 0.842; 9 gained and 581 lost rows; 1,880 stays intersected.
- 24 hours: 2,599 rows, 1,648 stays, 70 hospitals; 65 evaluation, 22 no-trial, 165 other, 2,347 no target; receipt 87/2,599 (3.347%); 61/70 hospitals target-free. Intersection 2,574, union 3,747, Jaccard 0.687; 25 gained and 1,148 lost rows; 1,630 stays intersected.
- 6 hours: 1,168 rows, 813 stays, 35 hospitals; 30 evaluation, 8 no-trial, 75 other, 1,055 no target; receipt 38/1,168 (3.253%); 29/35 hospitals target-free. Intersection 1,136, union 3,754, Jaccard 0.303; 32 gained and 2,586 lost rows; 790 stays intersected.

Thus receipt remains between 3.25% and 3.35% and site-concentrated under every bounded rule, but the prespecified joint gate is **inconclusive**, not adverse-robust, because the 24-hour Jaccard is just below 0.70 and the 6-hour population overlaps only 0.303. The clinically important result is therefore two-part: the adverse documentation-coverage signal is directionally stable, while the eligible population is highly sensitive to short airway persistence assumptions. A supportive result did not occur.

The RT-or-exact-explicit union yielded 4,688 rows, 2,577 stays, 89 hospitals, and exactly the broader parent's 124 targets and 25 target-supplying hospitals, but exceeded its denominator by three rows. Applying the broader parent's exact textual-airway-or-RT expression yielded 4,685 rows and exactly matched its overall receipt count and rate (124/4,685, 2.647%), hospital count (89), target-hospital count (25), and evaluation/no-trial counts (89/35). It did not establish row identity: this implementation had 2,575 rather than 2,576 stays, 280 rather than 281 other labels, and different landmark allocation. Consequently, invasive ascertainment explains the denominator size and target-result convergence, but residual implementation/tie/classification differences remain. Relative to the explicit reference, that branch had 3,719 intersecting rows, 4,688 union rows, Jaccard 0.793, 966 gained and 3 lost rows, with 2,022 intersecting stays.

## Interval-field availability and falsification

The full `respiratoryCare` profile contained 865,381 rows, and all four interval columns were numerically populated in every row, but zeros dominated. `ventendoffset` was zero in 865,224/865,381 rows and had only two unique values; `ventstartoffset` was zero in 685,205. Prior starts and ends were zero in 706,951 and 706,869 rows. Current start/end were equal in 685,205 rows and end preceded start in 60,565; prior fields were equal in 706,834. Separately, all 20,168,176 respiratoryCharting rows were scanned and `RT Vent On/Off` had 60,101 records in the independent profile: 55,981 `Continued`, 2,734 `Start`, 1,016 `Off`, 368 `Suspended`, and two lowercase `off`.

When every pre-L respiratoryCare status row was considered, the nonzero closed-current rule selected zero eligible rows and the closed-prior rule selected one row, with no exact target. These definitions are not estimable active-ventilation cohorts in this snapshot. Treating current end zero as open-ended retained 3,661 rows and 1,990 stays across 76 hospitals, with 106/3,661 targets (2.895%), 59 target-free hospitals, and Jaccard 0.981 versus reference. That numerical stability is diagnostic only: it does not validate zero as an open interval or prove continuous ventilation.

## Claims, falsification, and clinical limits

Computable evidence supports that exact care-plan receipt is sparse and site-concentrated across the tested bounded proxies, that short lookbacks materially change which stays/landmarks qualify, that the parent denominator magnitude is largely reconciled by the broader RT/textual-airway rule, and that nonzero closed interval fields cannot operationalize an adequately sized active-ventilation denominator here. It does not support treating the joint robustness gate as passed.

Supportive evidence would have met the stated receipt and hospital-coverage gate. Adverse-robust evidence would have met all low-receipt, target-free-hospital, and overlap conditions. The observed result is inconclusive about denominator robustness because low receipt persisted but overlap failed. A future falsification of this conclusion would be a clinically adjudicated ventilation episode definition that yields materially broader target receipt or substantially different site coverage.

No result establishes actual continuous invasive ventilation, daily evaluation or SBT delivery, extubation readiness, clinician intent, bedside action, quality, liberation, causal effect, benefit, or transportability beyond this snapshot. `no_target_label` is not no trial. Resolving those claims requires chart/note adjudication, validated ventilation episode and treatment timing, workflow metadata, expert review, and a separate outcome or causal study.
