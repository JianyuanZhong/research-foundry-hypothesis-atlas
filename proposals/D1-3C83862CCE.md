# Pretest-fixed offered-slot service-yield benchmark for HCC documentary-M2 review allocation

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The clinically consequential question is whether interpretable semantics in routine preoperative CT/MRI report bodies can help a scarce multidisciplinary service prioritize patients likely to have high-grade microvascular invasion (documentary M2) at resection. The action studied is allocation of review, not surgery type, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment, surveillance, or any other care.

The strongest claim the available HCC evidence supports is narrower. Snapshot `[source checksum]` contains patient/encounter-linked demographics, completed procedures with start/end fields, examination accessions with acquisition-like start times and eventual report text, untimed pathology narratives, and dated medication/order rows. It has no preoperative referral or candidate roster, planned-operation or cancellation ledger, report authored/final/release/view time, immutable report version, pathology time/specimen/slide/block key or sampling protocol, MDT slot ledger, review completion, clinician action, treatment consequence, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or benefit.

The prior design had two material online-validity defects despite its correct equal-completed-workload prefix. First, it set each test-year offered workload as `K_yq=floor(q*N_min,y)`; final 2020/2021 roster size is future information, so a policy cannot both use that K and claim not to use final annual N. Second, its replay roster required the future completed resection and postoperative HCC pathology. Those facts can define a retrospective evaluation cohort, but cannot establish who was actually presented to a review service at `t_dec`. This child freezes one absolute workload from 2015–2019 only and calls the replay what the source identifies: a chronological completed-case pseudo-roster benchmark. A timestamped external preoperative roster is mandatory before any workflow claim.

Current public evidence establishes MVI/M2 prognostic importance and a validation gap, but not the proposed result. Fan et al. (2026, DOI `10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944`, PMCID `PMC13187967`) describe MVI as an important prognostic factor and found M2 in 106/621 resected HCC patients; LDH showed only modest association and discrimination (AUC 0.610 for MVI presence), and the authors explicitly call for incremental external/prospective validation and reject LDH-alone treatment changes. Xu et al. (2026, DOI `10.1002/cam4.71749`, PMCID `PMC13122567`) derived an AFP/tumor-size/tumor-margin MVI score from a global meta-analysis and validated it retrospectively in two Chinese cohorts (C-statistics 0.805 and 0.808 with adequate calibration), establishing a substantive nonsemantic comparator but not clinical impact. Neves and Soares (2026, DOI `10.3390/cancers18132028`, PMCID `PMC13360217`) reviewed 36 HCC early-recurrence AI studies: 35 were retrospective, six reported external validation, and calibration and clinical utility were inconsistent; no model had generalized in practice. Wu et al. (2026, DOI `10.3389/frai.2026.1804004`, PMCID `PMC13385114`) reviewed 59 surgical-oncology MDT decision-support studies, with highly variable concordance and reported triage benefits but a need for prospective real-world impact studies. These sources motivate an incremental, calibrated, fixed-workload service-yield test, but none establishes report-semantic value, a real arrival roster, report visibility, review capacity, or benefit here. The research-ambition README was inspected as a rigor guide; the unavailable Cell main article and full STAR Methods were not read or used as evidence.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Let `s` index the complete outer set of coherent source states. A state jointly fixes patient membership, selected operative episode and clock, selected encounter and age, prior recorded treatment, pseudo-arrival order, examination-unit identity and clock, modality pattern, report-body visibility, pathology composite, and documentary-M2 outcome. Attributes from different states may not be mixed.

For each coherent state and historical year `a=2015,...,2019`, let `N_a(s)` be the number of completed-case pseudo-roster members before pathology grade or report semantics are parsed. Define the pretest reference count

`N_ref = min_s min_{a in 2015:2019} N_a(s)`.

Freeze `K_q=floor(q*N_ref)` for `q in {0.05,0.10,0.20}` and apply the same absolute K to both 2020 and 2021. Freeze the common completed-review prefix `J_q=ceil(0.90*K_q)`. K and J never change by test year, test-state roster size, policy, visibility pattern, outcome, or bootstrap refit. They are available-data workload benchmarks, not observed service capacity. If K<50, J<45, or any coherent test state cannot offer K candidates, that q is infeasible and cannot support the joint claim.

For `M in {S,S0,B}`, replay the frozen prefix-only pseudo-policy in each year and state. At pseudo-arrival i it may use only that member's admissible score, scaled calendar time, cumulative released and used slots, and quantities frozen by 31 December 2019. It may not use future pseudo-arrivals, final test N, future ranks, outcomes, another policy's actions, or end-of-year filling. Allocation occurs only at `t_dec`; there is no queue, recall, replacement, or backfill.

Let `A_i(M)` be allocation and `r_M(i)` its policy-specific acceptance ordinal. The first-J indicator is
`A_i^J(M)=A_i(M) 1[r_M(i)<=J_q]`.
Define the primary fixed offered-slot service yield
`V_K(M)=100/K_q * sum_i A_i(M)Y_i`.
K_q is the same pre-2020 fixed offered workload for every policy, year, state and access cell; an unused offered slot remains in the denominator. Thus V_K is documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered review slots. Retain the completed-prefix quantity only as a secondary diagnostic:
`R_J(M)=100/J_q * sum_i A_i^J(M)Y_i`.
The first-J accepted set `P_M^J={i:A_i^J(M)=1}` is policy-specific; it is not a common online opportunity set. Define utilization `U_M=sum_i A_i(M)/K_q` and used-case capture `C_M=sum_i A_i(M)Y_i/max(sum_i A_i(M),1)`, with `C_M=0` when no case is used, so `V_K(M)/100=U_M C_M`. Versus baseline M0, report the exact symmetric descriptive decomposition
`G_util=(C_S+C_M0)/2*(U_S-U_M0)` and `G_select=(U_S+U_M0)/2*(C_S-C_M0)`; their sum equals `V_K(S)/100-V_K(M0)/100`. G_select compares policy-specific accepted cases and is not a causal selection effect holding a shared opportunity set fixed. Use the same state-coupled bootstrap for both components.
The primary offered-slot contrasts are `D_K=V_K(S)-V_K(S0)` and `C_K=V_K(S)-V_K(B)`; the completed-prefix contrasts `D_J=R_J(S)-R_J(S0)` and `C_J=R_J(S)-R_J(B)` are secondary and cannot rescue a primary offered-slot failure. S0 masks only semantic fields inside S's exact fitted preprocessing, coefficients, calibration, threshold, and state machine; all-semantic masking must make S and S0 bitwise identical. B is independently fitted without report-body semantics.

The unresolved HCC-only hypothesis is:

**H1-offered-slot frontier:** a nonempty report-access frontier exists such that, for every claimed frontier cell, q, 2020/2021 year, decision buffer, and coherent source state, simultaneous 97.5% lower bounds for the primary offered-slot contrasts `D_K,C_K` both exceed five additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered slots, all three policies reach J, worst-state utilization is at least 0.90, S achieves more than 0.80 of the state-matched retrospective top-K oracle capture, and every evidence gate passes. The utilization and policy-specific used-case capture components are reported to explain whether any V_K gain is usage, accepted-case composition, or both; neither component is interpreted as a causal effect.

This tests robust fixed-workload service yield within a future-defined completed-resection cohort. R_J is a secondary completed-prefix diagnostic, not evidence of a common online opportunity set. It does not test arrival-roster performance, actual MDT allocation, or patient benefit.

## Population and temporal boundaries

The person unit is `Patient Master Index`; same-encounter joins use `(Patient Master Index,Encounter Number)`. Retain raw row ordinal and multiplicity before exact deduplication. Two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician freeze a resection dictionary and reconcile `Surgery Source` values, including `Anesthesia System` and `Medical Record Surgery`, into operative-episode alternatives. A clinically linked anesthesia-system `Start Time` is exact only after validation. A midnight/date-like case-record time is an interval `[d,d+24h)`; missing, competing, same-day, and adjacent-day clocks remain explicit states.

Within each coherent state, select the first eligible completed resection. Membership requires age at least 18 on the selected encounter, all-row same-encounter pathology explicitly supporting HCC, and no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[t_dec-365d,t_dec)` using state-matched procedures, medications, and orders. This is neither a decision-time HCC roster nor biological treatment-naivety. Patients outside the completed-case roster are not assumed negative; the source cannot enumerate referred noncompleters, cancellations, or alternative diagnoses.

Set the pseudo-decision deadline `t_dec=t_op-24h`, with locked 12/48/72-hour sensitivities. Develop in 2015–2018, use 2019 only for tuning/calibration/policy selection, then test 2020 and 2021 separately. Any patient with a possible later-period state is quarantined from earlier fitting. Use 2022 onward only for drift/ascertainment audit.

Map every year to `u=(t_dec-Jan1)/(next Jan1-Jan1)` and 366 fixed bins, avoiding leap-year day mismatch. From 2015–2019 pseudo-arrivals only, freeze a monotonized cumulative release fraction `F_ref(u)`; set `L_q(u)=floor(K_q*F_ref(u))` with `L_q(1)=K_q`. The same K and release curve apply to both test years. Interval clocks crossing an arrival or release boundary are enumerated. Exact-time ties use a frozen hash of `Patient Master Index`.

S and B are fit in 2015–2018. For each M/q, choose one constant calibrated-score threshold from `{0,0.005,...,1}` in 2019 to maximize documentary-M2 capture subject to utilization at least 0.90 under the frozen release curve; ties choose fewer false reviews, then the larger threshold. If no threshold satisfies utilization, that M/q cannot support H1. Freeze thresholds before 2020. This finite rule is the whole pseudo-policy; no hidden test-year rank or adaptive threshold is allowed.

## Report unit, outcome, variables, and baselines

A nonblank source accession entity is `(patient master index, encounter number, examination number)`, formed before time filtering, modality assignment, text parsing, or visibility masking. It is a source bundle, not a proven authored report. Retain every component row. If component modality labels conflict or valid component clocks differ by more than 24 hours, report identity is unresolved: the outer envelope contains both the indivisible-accession interpretation and a deterministic partition by normalized `(examination, start-time calendar date)`; each state carries raw backpointers. No favorable partition may be selected. Rows with blank `examination number` remain in the acquisition audit and population, but their text is masked as identity-unknown rather than silently converted into a report.

A source report unit is temporally eligible only if all of its component clocks place it in `[t_dec-90d,t_dec)`; deterministic conservative selection uses the maximum component start, while missing/conflicting clocks remain alternatives. Select at most the latest eligible unit per modality, with ties broken by frozen raw ordinal/hash. Two blinded abdominal radiologists map all component `examination` labels to CT, MRI, non-target, or unresolved. Concatenate `examination findings` and `examination diagnosis` in frozen raw order. Visibility exposes the entire selected source unit; it never splits component rows. Because no final/release/version field exists, exposed text means eventual stored text only.

Compose all exact-deduplicated pathology rows on the selected encounter from `Pathology,Findings,Diagnosis`. A frozen position-aware parser sets Y=1 only for an anchored selected value M2 and Y=0 only for anchored M0/M1. Boilerplate, unanchored mentions, missing/uncertain grade, M0/M1/M2 conflict, uncertain specimen linkage, or inadequate documented sampling yields `Y in {0,1}`, not exclusion. Two Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors adjudicate every test unknown/conflict and every parsed M2; a third resolves disagreement. This is documentary M2, not biological M2.

B contains state-selected age, sex, CT/MRI/other acquisition indicators, eligible source-unit count, acquisition-to-decision recency, component count, report-identity-ambiguity indicator, and development-frequency `machine model` category. It excludes report text, pathology, diagnoses, labs, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff facts. S adds prespecified interpretable semantics: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites, each present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned. Fit S and B by patient-wise worst-state elastic-net logistic loss. Report first-come, frozen seeded lottery, none, age/sex-only, and retrospective top-K as controls; the oracle is never the primary policy.

## Access frontier, uncertainty, and falsification

Use exact access guarantees on a 0.05 grid: `h_C` for CT among C-pattern members, `h_M` for MRI among M-pattern members, `h_A` for at least one visible modality among CM members, and `h_B<=h_A` for both visible among CM members. O/C/M/CM patterns remain in denominators. Modality choice for singly visible CM members, identity partitions, clock/member/outcome states, chronological order, release counters, thresholds, and actions remain jointly coupled. The frontier is the coordinatewise-minimal set of guarantees satisfying H1; if no cell qualifies, it is empty. This is a hypothetical exact-version access requirement, not evidence that reports were available.

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits, rebuilding states, 2019 tuning, calibration, thresholds, pseudo-policies, and endpoints; K, J, release bins, candidate threshold grid, semantic dictionary, and primary margins remain frozen. Use a max-centered 97.5% simultaneous family over four contrasts, both years, all q values, four decision buffers, access cells, identity states, attainability ratios, and null tests. Use 1,000 development/tuning semantic-block permutations stratified by year, modality pattern, source-unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and formatting family, with full refits. Preserve partial-identification extrema over coherent states inside every replicate; never combine table-wise extrema.

Required gates are: `N_ref>=500`; `K_.05>=25`; every test state can offer K; at least 50 adjudicated M2 events per claimed year envelope; anchored grade at least 85%; all test unknowns adjudicated; parser kappa at least 0.90, M2 PPV at least 0.98 and sensitivity at least 0.95; modality agreement at least 0.95 and unresolved at most 1%; each claimed pattern at least 100 members and 20 M2; semantic-feature PPV/sensitivity at least 0.85; no required-feature/pattern shift over 15 percentage points from 2019; all/no-report calibration slope 0.80–1.20 and intercept -0.10–0.10; every policy reaches J; worst-state utilization at least 0.90; exact slot/release accounting; complete state/backpointer replay; optimizer/brute-force equality on N<=12 fixtures; coordinatewise monotone access limits; zero lab/forbidden lineage; and no more than 2.5% failed bootstrap replicates.

Supportive results require every gate, a nonempty frontier, and simultaneous lower limits for both primary offered-slot contrasts D_K and C_K above five additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered slots in both years at every q and claimed cell, with the existing utilization, attainability, adjudication, calibration, null, state, optimizer and bootstrap requirements satisfied. The supported conclusion is only that eventual stored report semantics materially improved fixed-offered-workload service yield in a completed eligible-resection pseudo-roster, robust to enumerated source uncertainty. Report the exact utilization and policy-specific used-case capture decomposition.

Adverse results include a valid simultaneous upper limit at or below five for either primary offered-slot contrast, an empty frontier, or an attainability upper limit below 0.80. A favorable R_J with a primary offered-slot upper bound at or below five is adverse for the clinically interpretable service-yield claim. A favorable V_K with lower utilization is a usage/selection tradeoff; it does not establish that selection improved while holding opportunities fixed. If the utilization component is favorable but the used-case capture component is null or adverse, label the result utilization/timing-explained rather than better case selection. If the oracle is favorable but the prefix policy is adverse, retrospective ranking did not survive chronological release.

Inconclusive results include intervals crossing a margin; any population, identity, adjudication, calibration, null, state, optimizer, utilization, event, or bootstrap gate failing; a policy not reaching J; incompatible year/q/buffer results; or a decomposition whose uncertainty cannot be reproduced from the coupled states. Missing external roster, report-version, or capacity evidence does not erase an HCC-only completed-case result, but always leaves actual workflow performance and deployment inconclusive.
