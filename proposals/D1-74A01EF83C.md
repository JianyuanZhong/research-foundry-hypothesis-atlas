This experiment tests incremental prediction and operational prioritization of named structured chart constructs, not bedside semantics. It supplies no estimates or results in this proposal.

These additions make failure of overlap or resampling validity an explicitly inconclusive result rather than an implicit model or row-level inference. They do not change the cohort, target, estimand, endpoint, or clinical interpretation.

## Locked endpoint, time boundaries, and all-row estimand

All times are ICU-admission-relative offsets in minutes. Use clinical offsets, not entry offsets, for endpoint windows relative to each row's `D`:

* `B = (D-720,D]`
* `E = (D,D+720]`
* `T = (D+720,D+2160]`

Before arm-stratified output, enumerate every `respchartvaluelabel` and `respcharttypecat` source-wide with matched/unmatched counts. Parse only the locked components FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate using the inherited exact aliases, case rules, finite-number and bound checks, fraction/percentage conversion, duplicate collapse, same-clinical-time conflict rules, and recorded parse reasons. A component requires at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. A respiratory state requires at least three of four observed components. Missing is never zero, unchanged, or carried forward. Fixed absolute thresholds are FiO2 `0.10` fraction, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`.

For rows without a competing exit, assign exactly one trajectory in this order. Sustained de-escalation requires observed B and T states, at least two component decreases, no increase, and no E escalation. Escalation requires observed B and T states plus at least two component increases, or a newly documented invasive airway in E/T after no invasive evidence in B. Stable/mixed is the remaining classifiable state. An indeterminate required E airway check cannot create de-escalation and is non-exit unobservable unless an independent increase/airway rule assigns escalation. `respiratoryCare` is airway corroboration/sensitivity only and cannot fill numeric settings; an active interval is `ventstartoffset <= time` and missing or `ventendoffset >= time`.

Every row receives exactly one ordered K state:

`K ∈ {competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.

A finite `patient.unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160` has precedence, including death, discharge, transfer, or inability to complete T. Record exact exit offset, `unitdischargestatus`, `unitdischargelocation`, and `exit_before_washout = 1{unitdischargeoffset <= D+720}`. Invalid or unavailable exit timing is a separate `invalid_exit_time` administrative reason, never alive by default. Do not assign exits to respiratory classes. Assert exactly one K state for all 540 rows.

The primary all-row probabilities retain fixed arm denominators:

`p_k(a) = N_a^{-1} Σ_i 1{A_i=a,K_i=k}` and `Δ_k = p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`.

## Interpretability contract: residual patient state is not documentation opportunity

The unresolved clinical interpretation is separated into three computable information blocks before any model is fitted. This is a descriptive partition of structured-chart information, not a causal mediation or latent-state identification claim.

* `S` (residual patient-state proxy) contains only finite, parsed pre-D respiratory values and value-derived summaries: the four locked B-window component summaries and trends, baseline airway facts derived from `respiratoryCare` at valid clinical offsets, and finite pre-D `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration` summaries. It may include a value's within-window dispersion and last/first valid value only when the value and clinical time pass the inherited parser and spacing rules. A value-derived clinical measurement is not a bedside diagnosis or delivered-setting observation.
* `O` (documentation opportunity) contains no measurement values and no label semantics: counts, distinct clinical timestamps, distinct component labels, minimum-spacing and window-availability flags, missingness/availability indicators, `respchartentryoffset-respchartoffset` lag summaries, respiratoryCare row/interval counts, vitalPeriodic row/timestamp counts and spacing, and treatment row counts. `O` describes the opportunity for a source to be populated, not whether a patient was ill or whether a clinician acted. Entry lag is an audit feature, never a clinical-time substitute.
* `C` contains fixed design/structural covariates available before `D`: landmark, age category, and any inherited pre-D covariates explicitly listed in `pre_D_baseline`. `C` is neither a state measurement nor documentation opportunity.

Every feature is assigned exactly one of `S`, `O`, or `C` in a machine-readable feature dictionary. If a field is ambiguous (for example, a non-finite value, a source-presence flag, or an airway text token without a locked clinical interpretation), it is assigned to `O` or excluded; it is never silently promoted to `S`. The raw current label and all care-plan fields remain exposure-only and are assigned to neither block. No feature is allowed to be both a value and an opportunity surrogate. This block assignment is checked before fitting and is a verification failure if duplicated, post-D, or label-derived.

Fit the following nested, fixed, cross-fitted models for the same five-state `K` target:

* `M_C`: `C` only;
* `M_O`: `C + O`, with no respiratory values;
* `M_S`: `C + S`, with no opportunity features;
* `M_0`: `C + S + O`, without the current label;
* `M_1`: exactly `M_0` plus the exact current two-level label.

`Mopp` in inherited artifacts is the alias for `M_O`; retain that name in output for compatibility. The primary all-row comparison remains `M_1` versus `M_0`, and the frozen all-row five-state estimand remains unchanged. The additional nested increments are interpretation diagnostics: `M_O-M_C` quantifies opportunity-associated predictability, `M_0-M_S` quantifies the incremental contribution of opportunity after state proxies, and `M_1-M_0` is the only candidate label increment. `M_1-M_O` is a context display, not a state claim. None is a causal contribution, and correlated S/O features make these descriptive nested increments non-unique explanations.

For each held-out row, emit a feature-block manifest and out-of-fold predictions for all five models. Recompute all preprocessing, imputation, scaling, regularization, calibration, support maps, and any weighting within training hospitals only. A model is not allowed to use a feature block absent from its definition. Compare micro and equal-hospital macro multiclass log loss and Brier score, with stay-cluster uncertainty, for each nested increment. A putative residual state signal must satisfy all of the following: (i) `M_1` improves on `M_0`, not merely on `M_C` or `M_O`; (ii) the gain is not explained by `M_O-M_C` or by an `O`-only conditional null; (iii) it is present in supported joint `S×O×landmark` cells, rather than being a marginal association created by sparse measurement patterns; and (iv) it transports across held-out hospitals under the inherited gates. If `S` is unavailable in a supported cell, the cell is not evidence against state information and is marked unavailable.

The joint support audit must report, by held-out hospital and landmark, the exact arm counts, distinct stay counts, finite `S` support, `O` support, out-of-training-range values, missing block status, and `S×O` cell status. Use the inherited minimum of 10 rows per exact arm and 20 total rows for a supported cell; require at least five supported joint cells spanning at least five hospitals and both arms, with no cell contributing over 25% of supported rows. Do not collapse cells using `K`, `H`, label, observed direction, endpoint observability, or post-D information. If no deterministic training-only binning can retain this support, the residual-state claim is inconclusive even when an unstratified `M_1` gain exists.

The conditional null is strengthened to match this contract. Within each held-out hospital, partition complete stays by the ordered, label-independent vector of `(landmark, frozen S bin, frozen O bin, C/status)`, and permute complete label vectors only among stays with identical signatures. Validate all per-landmark and per-cell arm counts, stay integrity, and vector lengths after every arrangement. Also report the inherited broad hospital-by-landmark opportunity-preserving null. A gain typical of either null is adverse to a residual patient-state interpretation; failure to obtain the required exchangeable blocks or 20 valid global arrangements is inconclusive, never adverse.

The compiler must emit `interpretability_attribution.json` containing the block dictionary, nested-model definitions, fold-local cutpoints, joint support/overlap, each incremental loss and contrast, null comparisons, and mechanically evaluated state/opportunity gate. It must emit `feature_block_manifest.json` and include their hashes in `source_manifest.json` and `verification.json`. These files supplement, and do not replace, `heldout_metrics.json`, `state_opportunity_cells.json`, `conditional_null_summary.json`, and the primary trajectory artifacts.

* `M0`: pre-D respiratory state plus pre-D opportunity, without the current label;
* `M1`: exactly M0 plus the exact two-level current label;
* `Mopp`: pre-D opportunity only, without respiratory values or label.

Use deterministic leave-one-hospital-out folds. Every row from a `patientunitstayid`, including all repeated landmarks, must be in one fold. No hospital identifier, held-out-site intercept, future exit, endpoint status, or post-D field may be used as a predictor. Use a fixed low-dimensional multinomial logistic regression family, fixed regularization grid, fixed solver/tolerances, and deterministic tie-break. This is a compute and reproducibility bound, not a new estimand.

For each held-out hospital, fit all imputation, finite-value handling, scaling, feature selection, regularization choice, calibration, observation weighting if used, and support cutpoints using training hospitals only. Freeze the resulting maps before touching held-out labels, K states, H states, or review outcomes. The compiler must execute the following overlap and extrapolation audit in every fold. From training rows only, record for every exact arm and every candidate state/opportunity/landmark cell: row count, distinct stay count, finite predictor support, and minimum/maximum or fixed quantiles for each continuous predictor. Freeze the permitted tertile cutpoints for state and opportunity; if every tertile lacks support use a deterministic median split; if still sparse, use the predeclared deterministic collapse rule based only on training support and feature order, never label, K, H, effect direction, or held-out outcomes. Cross these bins with landmark. Apply the frozen maps to test rows. Record test rows in training-range, outside-range, and missing/invalid categories; do not clip or silently extrapolate.

A cell is supported only if it has at least 10 rows in each exact label arm and at least 20 rows total, and its training-derived maps are finite. For each reported held-out cell, report exact arm counts, distinct-stay counts, number of hospitals represented, out-of-range counts, and whether both arms are supported. Unsupported cells are unavailable and are not pooled, merged, reweighted, or converted to a negative finding. A fold with no supported exact-arm cells remains part of all-row micro accounting but is site/support-limited for cell and equal-site claims. The overlap audit is a diagnostic guard, not propensity positivity or causal positivity; no causal interpretation is made.

Report held-out micro and equal-hospital macro multiclass log loss, Brier score, calibration summaries, and all-row K contrasts from cached predictions. Proper multiclass loss is computed even if a test fold has fewer than two observed outcome classes, and that fold is marked site-support-limited rather than dropped. Equal-hospital macro is reported only over prespecified supported test hospitals with denominator shown. Report M1-minus-M0, Mopp-minus-null, and M1-minus-Mopp increments. The fixed supported-cell diagnostic requires the observed M1-minus-M0 increment to be summarized both as an equal-cell mean and a row-weighted aggregate, with no unsupported cell contributing.

This diagnostic does not replace the frozen all-row estimand.

## Valid complete-stay conditional null

The conditional null must preserve repeated-stay clustering and the complete state/opportunity composition. For each held-out hospital, define each stay's complete, label-independent signature before viewing any current label or endpoint as the ordered tuple over all its observed landmarks of `(landmark, frozen training-derived state bin, frozen training-derived opportunity bin, pre-D row-availability/status)`. `row-availability/status` means only pre-D/action-row availability and administrative availability known before endpoint extraction; it must not contain K, H, post-D observability, exit, any endpoint flag, or any field derived from the current label. If a signature depends on a fold-local map, it is the map trained without that held-out hospital.

Partition stays by identical complete signatures within held-out hospital. A permutation block is valid only when it contains at least two stays, and it permutes the entire ordered vector of exact labels across stays, never individual landmark rows. This preserves each stay's repeated-landmark composition, the landmark/cell arm counts, hospital membership, and the within-stay correlation structure. It is a conditional label-exchangeability diagnostic, not a causal randomization test.

For every proposed arrangement, assert that stay IDs and signatures are unchanged, each vector length equals the recipient's observed vector length, every label is one of the two exact arms, all per-landmark/per-cell arm counts equal observed counts, and no stay is split. The deterministic seeded Fisher-Yates generator uses seed `61061` plus a documented block/hospital derivation. Attempt at least 1,000 valid arrangements per supported global block set where feasible, or enumerate all unique arrangements when fewer exist; report attempted, valid, unique, and failed arrangements per block and hospital. If no block has two exchangeable stays, cluster preservation fails, any invariant fails, fewer than 20 valid global arrangements remain, fewer than five hospitals remain, or any supported held-out cell is lost, the conditional-null claim is inconclusive, never adverse or negative. Do not claim preservation if the exact signature construction cannot establish it.

Use a fixed-design bounded null statistic. Retain observed fold definitions, training-only preprocessing maps, supported cells, and model hyperparameters. M0 and Mopp remain cached. For each valid label arrangement, refit only the M1 label coefficient on the cached training design using the same deterministic solver and calculate the predeclared held-out incremental loss/statistic. This is a computational conditional-null diagnostic; its envelope or Monte Carlo proportion is not a confidence interval. Do not nest these permutations inside bootstrap replicates. The observed M1-versus-M0 estimate remains the fully fitted honest cross-fitted estimate.

The broad parent opportunity-preserving hospital-by-landmark permutation, site-balanced envelope, leave-one-site-out influence, first-versus-repeated landmark/persistence audit, whole-stay sequence null when exactly feasible, placebo/exposure-shift tests, component/source ablations, exit decomposition, and clinical-time versus as-entered sensitivity remain required and cannot be replaced by the conditional null. Any apparent increment confined to exits, unobservability, source density, entry lag, one site/component/source, repeated landmarks without first-landmark support, out-of-range rows, or unsupported cells is not patient-state evidence.

## Stay-cluster bootstrap with fold isolation

Use a fixed-seed `61061` stay-cluster bootstrap with a minimum of 500 and target of 1,000 valid replicates. The resampling unit is the complete first qualifying `patientunitstayid` stay, carrying all of its retained landmarks, labels, pre-D predictors, and endpoint states together. Never resample rows independently. Within each hospital, sample complete stays with replacement to the hospital's observed number of stays; preserve hospital membership, the first-stay-per-`uniquepid` rule, and each sampled stay's complete repeated-landmark vector. If a sampled hospital has zero eligible stays or its sampled dataset cannot retain a complete vector, the replicate fails and is reported, not repaired by row resampling.

For each replicate, reconstruct fold membership from the original hospital-held-out map, fit each training-hospital pipeline once using only the sampled training stays, and evaluate on the independently sampled stays of the held-out hospital. Recompute fold-local imputation, scaling, regularization choice, calibration, support cutpoints, overlap/out-of-range audit, state/opportunity cells, transport summaries, all-row K probabilities/contrasts, cached held-out predictions, and fixed review metrics. Duplicate sampled stays retain their cluster multiplicity in row-weighted metrics; equal-hospital metrics use the prespecified site denominator and explicitly report sites lost to replicate support. No original row may appear in both training and held-out sets except as a consequence of distinct sampled copies within their original hospital fold; a hospital remains wholly held out from fitting in every replicate. Repeated landmarks from a stay are never separated.

Do not recompute 1,000 conditional permutations inside each bootstrap replicate. Report the conditional-null Monte Carlo uncertainty separately using its attempted/valid counts and a predeclared binomial interval. For the bootstrap, report attempted, valid, failed, effective-cluster, fold/site-loss, supported-cell, overlap-loss, and out-of-range counts. A replicate is valid only when all one-state K assignments are retained, fold and stay invariants hold, all requested metrics are numerically finite, and its support status is recorded. Unsupported replicate cells are unavailable and are not replaced by a pooled estimate.

Run deterministic chunks with declared CPU, memory, timeout, and checkpoint files. `compute_manifest.json` records fit count, chunk IDs, cache keys, random seeds, completed checkpoints, wall-clock/resource status, and whether each artifact used cached or refit data. If the resource budget cannot obtain 500 valid replicates, or a timeout leaves only partial output, set `bootstrap_status=insufficient_compute` and make uncertainty-dependent claims inconclusive. Never substitute a row bootstrap, unseeded early stopping, a different model, or post-result budget changes. Do not use bootstrap results to choose thresholds, bins, costs, capacities, or hypotheses.

## Support, uncertainty, falsification, and interpretation gates

Feasibility is supportive only with at least 40% of all 540 rows classifiable and at least 10 hospitals having both exact labels among classifiable rows. Below 20% or five hospitals is adverse feasibility; intermediate support is inconclusive. Transport requires at least five hospitals with at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows per arm representing at least 50% of supported rows, no more than 25% directional reversals, and no single site above 25% of supported rows. The state/opportunity claim additionally requires at least five hospitals and five supported cells spanning both arms, with no cell above 25% of supported rows. The conditional null and review claims have their separate minimum-arrangement, overlap, opportunity, and bootstrap gates above.

A computationally supportive label-information result requires M1 to improve both micro and equal-site held-out K loss over M0 in the unbounded primary and at least two prespecified endpoint/observation sensitivities, favorable stay-cluster uncertainty, transport support, no adverse broad or S/O-conditional null, and no gain confined to exit, unobservability, opportunity, entry lag, out-of-range extrapolation, one site/component/source, or persistence. A residual patient-state interpretation additionally requires a valid `S/O/C` dictionary, supported joint S×O cells spanning at least five hospitals and both arms, improvement beyond M0 rather than only M_O, and a conditional-null result not typical of the observed increment.
