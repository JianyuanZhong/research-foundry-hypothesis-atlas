## Parser freeze before arm-aware endpoint inspection

Before any arm-specific endpoint table, endpoint count, model, or gate is read, write and hash `parser_freeze.json`. It contains the versioned exact label map, regex, bounds, half-open windows, duplicate and time-tie rules, spacing, missingness precedence, airway rule, and code hash. The fixed respiratoryCharting map is: FiO2 = `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`; PEEP = `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`; pressure support = `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`; set rate = `Vent Rate`, `VS RESP RATE`. Matching trims outer whitespace and remains case-sensitive; no substring/synonym matching. Peak/plateau/mean airway pressure, pressure-control, peak-flow, spontaneous/total-rate, and oxygen-flow labels are excluded from the primary map; peak pressure is only a declared ablation.

For every numeric field, trim then require a complete signed decimal/scientific token matching `^[+-]?(?:\d+(?:\.\d*)?|\.\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\d+)?$`; reject blanks, units, ranges, inequalities, nonnumeric and non-finite values while retaining raw value, parsed value, label, reason, and parser version. Use fixed raw-scale bounds FiO2 `[21,100]`, PEEP/pressure support `[0,60]`, set rate `[0,100]`, SpO2 `[0,100]`, respiration `[0,200]`. Values such as `0.4`, `2`, or `3` for FiO2 are `unit_ambiguous_or_invalid`; never silently rescale, interpolate, carry forward, or substitute.

## Endpoint and observability contract

For each row, compare pre-D baseline `B=(D-720,D]` with the preceding pre-D process window `P=(D-1440,D-720]`. The primary post period is split into early `E=(D+360,D+1080]` and late `T=(D+1080,D+1800]`; a declared single-window secondary uses `O=(D+360,D+1800]`. Boundaries are half-open. Every component and safety signal in every required period needs at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. Exact duplicate clinical-time/value rows are collapsed only after retaining IDs and deterministic `(clinical_offset, entry_offset, source_id)` order. Distinct valid values at one clinical offset produce `time_tie_conflict` rather than arbitrary selection. Summaries are medians; report count, distinct times, first/last offset, maximum gap, rejected count and reason.

A component is persistently reduced only if its B median and both E and T medians exist and each post median is no greater than B, with the fixed raw-scale decrease threshold: FiO2 >= 0.05, PEEP >= 2, pressure support >= 2, or set rate >= 2. Primary safety uses only `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration`: in both E and T, SpO2 is no more than 2 points below B and respiration no more than 4 breaths/min above B. Missing either safety signal in any required period is missing, not safe. Airway corroboration is a separate required primary observability flag, not device truth: at least one O-window `respiratoryCare` row has nonempty `airwaytype` or a genuinely nonmissing ventilator interval field. A `persistent_improvement` requires persistent non-peak reduction, both-window safety pass, and corroboration.

The compiler must first produce an outcome-blind support table, before label comparison, with one row per frozen person-landmark and component-period. It reports source presence, valid/rejected counts, distinct-time and 30-minute-spacing support, maximum gap, earliest/latest offset, and whether the post window is reachable. This separates parser/source density from endpoint support. After lineage and parser gates pass, produce `trajectory_rows` retaining every frozen row and mutually exclusive state: `competing_exit`, `endpoint_missing`, `persistent_improvement`, or `observed_non_improvement`. Missing rows carry a primary reason and all-reason flags: no pre component, no early component, no late component, no safety, airway not observed, parser rejection, source absence, time-tie conflict, or source error. Never drop rows for differential missingness.

Classify in this order. First, a nonmissing `patient.unitdischargeoffset <= D+1800` is a competing exit. Exact trimmed case-insensitive `Expired` is death; otherwise a classifiable status/location is discharge/transfer; unclassifiable status/location is truncation. Also flag exits by `D+360`, which imply no post-washout opportunity. Second, absent endpoint requirements is endpoint missing. Third, the persistent condition is improvement. Fourth, a fully observed failure is observed non-improvement. Report the four states and all reasons by arm, hospital, and landmark only after the parser/lineage stops have passed. Primary complete-observed risk difference is evaluation minus no-trial among non-competing complete rows; all-row state frequencies and a death-versus-nondeath competing-risk sensitivity accompany it. The single-window secondary diagnoses transient support or persistence attrition and cannot replace the primary.

Predeclared airway sensitivities are not primary endpoint replacements: (i) primary trajectory plus any post-window respiratoryCare airway/ventilator-field observation; (ii) plus an interval overlapping O, requiring `ventstartoffset < D+1800` and `ventendoffset > D+360`; and (iii) latest pre-D exact airway type among `{Oral ETT,Nasal ETT,Tracheostomy,Double-Lumen Tube,Cricothyrotomy}`, at recencies 360, 1440, and 4320 minutes. Latest ties use `respcareid`; `Other` and `No Artificial Airway` do not qualify. An airway sensitivity is unavailable/inconclusive if fewer than 10% of rows in each arm are observed. Treatment strings never define the primary endpoint.

## Endpoint-support diagnostics, falsification, and gates

Before model fitting, report a support matrix by hospital, landmark, arm, and component/window: frozen-row count, reachable post-window count, competing exits (death, discharge/transfer, truncation), complete safety count, complete component count, corroboration count, persistent endpoint count, and each missingness reason. Also report effective arm overlap and the proportion of complete rows contributed by each hospital. These diagnostics are descriptive feasibility outputs, not reasons to redefine the denominator. A model/contrast is estimable only when both arms and required endpoint states are supported in the relevant held-out fold.

Run, with parser and thresholds unchanged, the P-versus-B pre-D placebo; lead-time placebo shifting exposure to `D-720`; single-window secondary; stricter 60-minute spacing; leave-one-component-out and source-family ablations; chart-only versus airway-corrobation variants; 360/1440/4320-minute airway recencies; and a documentation-density negative control. All attrition is retained. A valid within-hospital-by-landmark permutation is required where support permits.

A mechanically **supportive** result requires: lineage and parser gates pass; at least five hospitals each have >=10 complete endpoint-observed rows under each label and those sites contain >=50% of complete rows, with no site contributing >40% of either arm; the primary complete-observed risk difference is positive with >=4/5 estimable hospital directions positive; label-added prediction improves both micro and equal-hospital-macro held-out log loss by >=0.01 over the identical state-plus-opportunity model, with a stay-cluster 95% interval excluding zero; the increment survives component/source ablations and both placebos; it exceeds the valid permutation null; missingness/exits do not explain the value gain; and airway corroboration is observed in >=10% of each arm. This supports only reproducible information in this structured chart construct.

An **adverse** result requires adequate support but no baseline/opportunity improvement, placebo/permutation-compatible increment, hospital direction reversal, dependence on one component/source/site, or documentation-density gain without value-based trajectory gain. It is adverse to the frozen predictive claim, not proof of no bedside evaluation or documentation artifact. **Inconclusive** is mandatory for failed parent identity/schema/hash/row digest, parser mismatch, fewer than five supported hospitals, no held-out arm contrast, persistent endpoint or airway support below threshold, severe arm-differential missingness preventing separation of state and ascertainment, competing exits too concentrated to compare, disagreement across fixed variants, or uncertainty spanning both zero and the meaningful increment. A positive single-window result with failed persistent support is transient/non-persistent and not supportive of the primary claim. No relaxed threshold, post-hoc label expansion, endpoint-derived weighting, or complete-case-only conclusion is permitted.
