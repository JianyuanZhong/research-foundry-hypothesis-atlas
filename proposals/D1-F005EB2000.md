# Proposal: an explicit day-45 risk set for disease-specific care pathways after TACE

## Episode, parent, and scientific deliverable

This episode-7 successor evolves assessed parent '[prior hypothesis]'. It preserves the HCC post-TACE question, paired AFP/hepatic trajectory, competing liver-directed and non-liver recorded actions, observation-process controls, negative-control trajectory, and transparent-versus-latent comparison. The substantive repair is an explicit landmark risk set and ascertainment estimand.

The solver must newly construct the index and day-45 risk-set manifests; fit B0, B1 and B2 transparent competing-risk models and M3; estimate cause-specific hazards, cumulative incidences and calibration/log scores with uncertainty; quantify early liver-directed pathways and local-loss censoring; and produce an observation-specificity report. Discovery has not solved the hypothesis.

## Unresolved clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

After TACE, clinicians must decide whether the early trajectory is compatible with routine surveillance or should prompt reassessment for additional liver-directed treatment. A repeat intervention is a clinician-recorded action, not a direct measure of viable tumor, and the observed action is affected by local follow-up and measurement opportunity.

The strongest supported local-data claim is narrower: the snapshot contains structured HCC diagnosis text, dated TACE-like procedures, dated AFP and hepatic-proxy laboratory rows, dated examination rows, and encounter dates. A bounded audit found 19,496 patients with a first TACE-like index under the frozen vocabulary; 15,605 had another procedure during days 0–45, 1,468 had first TACE-like and 1,491 alternate liver-directed procedures in days 46–365. Only 4,524 had an encounter at least 365 days after index. These facts support feasibility and expose a landmark/ascertainment problem; they do not establish the response hypothesis.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among adults with structured HCC and a first recorded TACE-like index, who have no further liver-directed procedure after the index episode through day 45 and have a paired numeric early trajectory, a prespecified transformed-AFP improvement plus non-deterioration in at least two hepatic proxies is associated with lower 46–365-day cumulative incidence of a first recorded liver-directed action, after adjustment for day-45-known observation opportunity. The association is more specific to liver-directed actions than to the competing non-liver procedure capture control and is weaker for a pre-index placebo trajectory.

This is an observation-adjusted prognostic association with recorded care. It is not a causal TACE effect, a recurrence/response label, or a treatment recommendation.

## Population and temporal estimands

Use one index per patient. In 'procedures', a TACE-like row is a case-insensitive 'TACE', contains 'transarterial chemoembolization', or contains both 'hepatic artery' and 'embolization'. Collapse qualifying rows for a patient within 24 hours into one index episode and anchor on the earliest 'Start Time'; retain all raw names and the rule version. Join to 'encounters' on ('Patient Master Index', 'Encounter Number'), require age >=18 and nonmissing sex, and require a joined diagnosis whose 'Diagnosis Name' contains literal 'hepatocellular carcinoma' in an encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after index. Use index dates through 2025-01-01 for the primary one-year horizon.

The index episode itself includes procedures within the 24-hour collapse. A procedure after index plus 24 hours through day 45 is an early subsequent pathway. The primary day-45 landmark risk set excludes any patient with an early repeat-TACE-like or alternate liver-directed procedure. Those early actions are retained as a separately reported multi-state pathway, not silently treated as later non-events. Non-liver procedures through day 45 may be used only as day-45-known observation features.

For the paired primary exposure, use the last parseable numeric AFP in days -30 to -1 and first in days 7–45. Let z=log1p(AFP), and define AFP improvement as z_post <= 0.5*z_pre; retain continuous change, assay inequality/qualitative flags and missingness. For albumin, total bilirubin, the observed coagulation-ratio assay 'Prothrombin time ratio', and platelet count, use last pre-index and first early-post values. Non-deterioration is early >= pre for albumin/platelets and early <= pre for bilirubin/coagulation ratio. The joint phenotype is AFP improvement plus non-deterioration in at least two proxies. Do not call the assay INR, pool assays, calculate ALBI/MELD, or use units (no lab unit column).

For days 46–365, classify the first subsequent procedure, subject to local observation/censoring, as mutually exclusive: repeat TACE-like; alternate liver-directed (name contains 'resection', 'ablation', 'radiofrequency', or 'microwave', but not TACE-like); or non-liver capture control. Report each category, combined liver-directed, and cumulative-incidence contrasts. A non-event is never called treatment success.

Define local observation time as the latest dated joined encounter (admission, visit or discharge time) available for that patient; censor at the first day after which no local encounter is observed, or at day 365, whichever comes first, unless an action occurs first. The primary analysis estimates recorded-action hazards with training-only inverse-probability-of-censoring weights using baseline and day-45-known capture features, reports weight overlap and truncation sensitivity, and does not impute death or outside care. A prespecified stable-ascertainment sensitivity restricts to patients with an encounter on/after day 365; the audit shows this is a smaller, clinically selected subset. The all-index sensitivity models eligibility for the paired trajectory and landmark risk set from the full index population, with training-only stabilized weights. If censoring or weights are non-identifiable/unstable, the result is inconclusive.

Use a pre-index placebo trajectory with the same two windows (days -120 to -91 and -90 to -46), without calling it response. A comparable association from this placebo is evidence for generic prognosis or selection rather than post-TACE specificity.

## Models and method choice

All primary models use the same paired day-45 risk set, patient-level split, outcome and censoring rules.

- B0: regularized competing-risk discrete-time hazard model using age, sex, department/procedure-family, pre-index HCC/cirrhosis/portal-vein/liver-lung-bone-metastasis/ascites indicators and counts, previous liver-directed procedure counts, pre-window assay values/missingness, early-pathway indicators allowed by the risk-set rule, and day-45 observation features.
- B1: B0 plus the frozen AFP/hepatic phenotype and continuous changes.
- B2: B1 plus assay-specific availability, distinct encounter days, lab row/day counts, examination opportunity/counts/timing, non-liver procedure counts and other day-45-known capture variables. Fit an IPCW version from training data only.
- M3: a substantive irregular-time low-rank state-space model with AFP-informed latent activity and hepatic-function states, patient random intercepts/slopes, assay-specific informative-observation submodels, baseline/day-45 capture covariates, and competing cause-specific action hazards. Estimate the latent state at day 45 using past data only; produce posterior state/slope uncertainty and hazard predictions.

B0/B1/B2 are the necessary scientific baseline because the question is incremental information and observation specificity, not generic prediction. M3 tests whether asynchronous biomarker shape and informative measurement timing explain action-specific association that a threshold phenotype loses; its latent state cannot recover unmeasured tumor burden or access. The method comparison is substantive because it tests whether the result is robust to representation and observation modeling.

A transformer adaptation of the natural-history demonstration is deferred: dated HCC histories exist, but on this single-institution recorded-action endpoint its extra capacity is not needed to identify the landmark/observation question and would make procedure-vocabulary and leakage auditing harder. The cancer demonstration's full multimodal model cannot be reproduced because image files are absent; the lab/EHR idea is represented by B1/B2/M3 without claiming reproduction. The Bayesian demonstration was inspected as support for latent longitudinal modeling, but verified genetic inputs are absent and no genetic analysis is proposed. The expert-seed library was inspected; it supplies no HCC seed, so non-HCC imported seeds were assessed but not forced into this HCC island.

## Splits, uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Primary split is by index date: 2010–2022 fit, 2023 tuning, 2024–2025-01-01 locked test; no patient crosses splits. If date support or cell counts fail, use deterministic patient-hash buckets and record the fallback. All parsing, imputation, model, censoring weights, latent settings and vocabulary decisions are frozen without locked-test outcomes.

Compare cause-specific integrated Brier score, IPCW mean log score, calibration intercept/slope, AUROC/PR-AUC only when event support permits, and one-year cumulative-incidence contrasts. Use patient bootstrap or a declared robust interval method, including uncertainty in the competing-risk predictions and weight diagnostics. Do not claim clinical utility from discrimination.

Supportive evidence requires, on the locked test, the phenotype's liver-directed contrast in the prespecified lower-risk direction with a 95% interval excluding the null (operational threshold ratio <=0.80 for repeat TACE), preserved under literal-name and 90-/180-day sensitivities, <30% attenuation after B2/IPCW relative to B1, stronger liver-directed than non-liver-control association, weaker pre-index placebo association, stable weight overlap, and M3 mean-log-score improvement >=0.02 without worse calibration. These are research criteria, not treatment thresholds.

Adverse evidence is reversal/null, >=30% attenuation after observation adjustment, comparable liver and non-liver-control association, placebo association as strong as the post-index trajectory, early-action or competing-action reversal, unstable ascertainment sensitivity, poor calibration, or M3 improvement disappearing under leakage/observation controls. A strong alternate liver-directed action means a repeat-TACE-only conclusion is incomplete.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few paired day-45-risk-set patients/events, high local-loss censoring, failed weight overlap, wide intervals, sparse or non-comparable assays, M3 nonconvergence, or inability to reproduce the vocabulary audit. Support would establish only a reproducible local recorded-care association and justify prospective biomarker-triggered reassessment; it would not establish treatment benefit or radiologic response. Stronger claims require clinician adjudication and another prospective/external study.
