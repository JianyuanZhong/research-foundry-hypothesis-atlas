# Frozen complete-source experiment: hepatic-reserve change after TACE2 under reconciled recorded-pathway and assay rules

## Clinical question, strongest supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

### Question and clinical importance

Among adults with recorded hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) who reach a second qualifying transarterial chemoembolization (TACE2), is a systemic HCC-medication record during fixed TACE2 calendar days 1–14 associated with a different subsequent within-person hepatic biochemical trajectory than an ambiguity-excluded TACE2-only recorded pathway?

This matters because post-TACE albumin decline or bilirubin rise may influence whether clinicians continue locoregional treatment, add systemic treatment, or regard hepatic reserve as deteriorating. A fixed post-index laboratory level is particularly misleading here: treatment pathways differ in calendar time, baseline liver state, prior utilization, and follow-up testing. The proposed experiment therefore estimates paired exact-assay change and explicitly studies assay observation rather than treating missing follow-up tests as ignorable.

### Strongest claim already supported by complete-source evidence

Complete scans establish feasibility and identify selection problems; they do not establish benefit, harm, or a treatment effect. The frozen construction below yields 319 systemic-record and 1,491 TACE2-only patients in 2018–2024. Exact `白蛋白` and `总胆红素` baseline/follow-up pairs are jointly available for 99/319 and 387/1,491. In the prior independent audit, baseline availability was 172/319 versus 805/1,491, follow-up availability was 169/319 versus 683/1,491, and paired availability differed by 0.0508. A five-fold cross-validated model for paired observation had AUC 0.673. The arms were imbalanced in year (SMD 0.393), TACE1–TACE2 interval (-0.198), prior 90-day procedure count (0.248), baseline albumin (-0.149), and baseline bilirubin (-0.214). These findings support a bias-resistant observation-aware experiment, not a clinical conclusion.

The formerly reported 100 exposed pairs have now been exactly reproduced and localized. They arose from an inherited timestamp baseline definition that admitted a laboratory draw earlier on the TACE2 calendar day. One pseudonymized systemic-arm patient (`fd6ed6d1c055340faff9`) had both assays 1,601 seconds before the TACE2 procedure but no assay on calendar days -30 through -1. The frozen calendar-day baseline excludes that patient, producing 99. This is a temporal-boundary difference, not a laboratory parser or exposure-ontology difference. Five analogous comparator losses explain 393 to 388 under the same parent arm; one additional comparator is removed by the frozen arm ontology, producing 387.

### Untested hypothesis

Conditional on membership in the day-14 institutional-observation population, measured baseline factors, adequate arm overlap, and observed exact assays, the recorded systemic pathway will be associated with less albumin decline (or greater increase) from TACE2 days -30 to -1 to target-nearest day 60 in days 30–90, without a larger bilirubin increase, than the recorded TACE2-only pathway.

This is a hypothesis about associations between recorded pathways and observed biochemical change. It is not a hypothesis of medication administration, adherence, toxicity causation, comparative effectiveness, or survival benefit.

## Frozen population and temporal boundaries

### TACE2 time zero

Parse `procedures.开始时间`. A strict TACE row has `手术` containing case-insensitive literal `TACE` or literal `化疗栓塞`. Require valid `开始时间`. Collapse qualifying rows to patient-local calendar-day episodes by (`患者主索引`, normalized day), retaining the earliest timestamp for deterministic description. Within each patient, sort episodes and choose the earliest adjacent pair separated by 14–180 calendar days. The second episode is TACE2, and its timestamp and normalized calendar day are retained. No later pair may replace it.

Complete-source flow is: 338,040 procedure rows; 78,067 textual strict matches before timestamp validity; 72,273 strict rows with valid start time; 38,742 patient-day episodes; and 6,589 earliest adjacent pairs. The broad-TACE sensitivity additionally admits the parent's prespecified arterial-embolization labels but never changes the primary strict definition after results are seen.

### HCC, adult status, day-14 landmark, and era

Require `diagnoses.诊断名称` to contain literal `肝细胞癌` at an encounter-derived event time on or before the TACE2 calendar day. Choose the latest encounter at or before the TACE2 timestamp for age, sex, and department; parse a resolvable numeric age and require age at least 18. This leaves 6,586 HCC/adult pairs.

Define last institutional observation as the patient-level maximum across nonmissing encounter `就诊时间`, `入院时间`, and `出院时间`. Require it to be on or after TACE2 day 14 and require TACE2 year 2018–2024. This yields 2,992 patients before medication rules. The day-14 condition establishes only institutional observability, not survival.

Parse `开始时间`; only valid-time rows can classify exposure. Remove any row whose `用药` contains `安慰剂` or case-insensitive `placebo` before every prior or post-index systemic rule. Thus placebo is never prior systemic therapy, an exposure, or a regimen partner. Across 4,097,517 medication rows, 34,221 matched the textual list and had valid start time; 754 were placebo rows and 33,467 were nonplacebo.

Prior exclusion is any valid-time, ontology-matched, nonplacebo medication on or before the TACE2 calendar day. Bevacizumab is included in this conservative prior rule because prior bevacizumab is incompatible with a systemic-naive pathway even though its HCC indication cannot be inferred. In the 2,992-person landmark/era base, this excludes 1,107 patients. The inherited code excluded 1,112 because it counted five placebo-only patients as prior treated.

The fixed grace period is TACE2 calendar days 1–14 inclusive:

- **Systemic-record arm:** at least one valid-time, nonplacebo, non-bevacizumab ontology match. The first such day is descriptive initiation timing; it does not prove administration.
- **Bevacizumab handling:** bevacizumab alone never identifies an HCC-directed systemic exposure. It is marked as a regimen component only if the same patient, `就诊号`, and calendar day also contain a non-bevacizumab ontology match. A patient with bevacizumab but no non-bevacizumab match anywhere in days 1–14 is `bev_only_ambiguous` and excluded from both primary arms.
- **Generic procedure ambiguity:** independently flag valid-time `procedures.手术` containing `分子靶向治疗`, `抗肿瘤免疫治疗`, `免疫治疗`, or `靶向治疗` on days 1–14. Such labels do not identify a drug or prove delivery. If the patient is not systemic-exposed and not already bevacizumab-only, classify as `generic_ambiguous` and exclude.
- **TACE2-only arm:** no prior systemic record, no qualifying non-bevacizumab record in days 1–14, no bevacizumab-only ambiguity, and no generic systemic-procedure ambiguity.

Classification order is fixed: prior exclusion, systemic exposure, bevacizumab-only ambiguity, generic-procedure ambiguity, then comparator. Later medications or TACE procedures do not redefine baseline pathway membership and do not censor the primary outcome.

### Exact reconciliation and arm overlap

The inherited implementation produced 317 systemic and 1,492 comparator patients because placebo rows entered prior exclusion and bevacizumab-only patients without generic procedure evidence remained controls. The frozen rules produce 319 and 1,491.

The patient-rule crosswalk contains 13 changed labels:

- five placebo-only prior exclusions become eligible: two enter the systemic arm and three enter the comparator;
- four inherited comparator patients become bevacizumab-only ambiguous;
- four patients already generic-ambiguous are relabeled bevacizumab-only ambiguous, changing the reason but not analytic membership;
- all 317 inherited systemic patients remain systemic.

There are 30 bevacizumab-only grace patients and 522 generic-procedure grace patients in the 2,992-person base before applying classification order; many are already prior-excluded, so raw flag counts must not be summed as mutually exclusive exclusions.

Calendar overlap exists in every year, but early-year exposed cells are small. Frozen systemic/comparator counts are: 2018, 10/164; 2019, 6/111; 2020, 19/161; 2021, 63/227; 2022, 41/164; 2023, 72/259; and 2024, 108/405. This permits pooled modeling with flexible calendar adjustment but makes within-year 2018–2020 estimates descriptive and requires explicit positivity diagnostics.

## Frozen laboratory parsing and deterministic selection

Restrict primary assays to exact `labs.检验` values `白蛋白` and `总胆红素`. Preserve `标本类型` and report its arm/window distribution. Parse `检验时间` and normalize its local calendar day relative to TACE2.

Parse `定量结果` only if, after trimming whitespace and removing commas, the entire value matches an optional leading `<`, `>`, `≤`, or `≥`, followed by a signed decimal or scientific-notation numeric literal. Convert only the captured number; report the inequality marker rather than pretending it is an exact uncensored result in sensitivity checks. Reject strings with other prefixes or suffixes. Do not extract a numeric substring from otherwise nonnumeric text.

For the 2,992-person observation/era base, the complete lab scan found 71,107 exact-assay rows; 71,103 had valid time, and all 71,103 were accepted by both strict and inherited loose parsers. There were zero rows accepted only by the loose parser. Thus parsing does not explain any 100-versus-99 or 393-versus-388 discrepancy, although the strict parser remains frozen for deterministic future execution.

For each assay separately:

1. **Baseline:** among valid strict-numeric rows on TACE2 calendar days -30 through -1 inclusive, select maximum tuple `(calendar day, timestamp)`, i.e. the latest timestamp.
2. **Follow-up:** among rows on days 30–90 inclusive, minimize `(absolute(calendar day - 60), calendar day, negative timestamp)`. This chooses closest day to 60, breaks equal-distance ties toward the earlier day, then chooses the latest timestamp on that day.
3. **Change:** selected follow-up value minus selected baseline value.

The joint hepatic-pair feasibility flag requires a valid baseline and follow-up for both exact assays, but albumin and bilirubin are analyzed as separate outcomes and never collapsed into an unvalidated composite.

The inherited availability definition was `[TACE2 timestamp - 30 days, TACE2 timestamp)` for baseline, not calendar days -30 through -1. It exactly reproduces 100 systemic and 393 comparator joint pairs. Deterministic calendar-day selection produces 99 and 388 under inherited arms. The one exposed symmetric difference is pseudonym `fd6ed6d1c055340faff9`: both baseline assays were recorded on day 0 exactly 1,601 seconds before TACE2, and both had valid day-30–90 follow-up, but there was no day -30 through -1 baseline. Frozen arm classification then removes one of the remaining inherited comparator pairs, yielding final frozen 99/319 systemic and 387/1,491 comparator joint pairs. Do not force the inherited counts by changing the locked baseline.

## Outcomes, estimands, and analysis

### Primary outcomes and estimand

Analyze albumin and total bilirubin separately. For assay `a`, define `Δa = a_follow-up - a_baseline` using the frozen selection above. The primary estimand is the overlap-weighted adjusted mean difference

`E_w[Δa | systemic record, paired observed] - E_w[Δa | TACE2-only record, paired observed]`

in the frozen day-14 population, with patient-level robust 95% confidence intervals. This is a measured-confounder-adjusted recorded-pathway association among patients with observed pairs. Report exact raw and weighted denominators, raw means, raw difference, adjusted difference, confidence interval, weight range, and effective sample size (ESS). Also report the complete-case unweighted estimate and the assay-specific overlap-plus-observation-weighted sensitivity.

A supportive hepatic profile requires the adjusted albumin contrast to indicate less decline/more increase and bilirubin not to show a larger increase. Discordant outcomes are reported separately as mixed or adverse; no composite or significance-counting rule is allowed.

### Pre-index adjustment and overlap weighting

Construct only information available no later than TACE2: age; sex; TACE1–TACE2 interval; year and month; department; encounter counts in prior 30/90/180 days; procedure counts in prior 90/180 days; prior resection, ablation, or transplant text flags; encounter-timed diagnosis proxies for cirrhosis, portal hypertension, ascites, encephalopathy, variceal bleeding, portal-vein invasion, and metastasis; and closest pre-TACE2 exact albumin, bilirubin, `国际标准化比值`, `血小板计数`, `肌酐`, and `钠`, with missingness indicators. AFP may be used only after exact assay-label enumeration is frozen. No post-TACE2 value, grace-period treatment detail, later treatment, or outcome information enters the propensity model.

Fit a prespecified logistic propensity model with flexible calendar terms. Use overlap weights: `1-p` for systemic and `p` for comparator. Report pooled and annual propensity distributions; standardized mean differences before and after weighting; minimum, maximum, and percentiles of weights; fraction of total weight contributed by each arm/year; and arm-specific ESS `(sum w)^2/sum(w^2)`. The annual counts above are the observed arm-support audit, not proof of conditional positivity.

For each assay, fit weighted linear regression of change on arm, baseline assay value (flexibly), and any prespecified core covariate remaining at absolute SMD >0.10 after weighting. Use heteroskedasticity-robust patient-level uncertainty. For bilirubin, report prespecified median/quantile and percentile-winsorized sensitivities while retaining the raw-scale mean as primary; disclose the fixed percentile rule before estimation and do not choose among estimates by favorability.

### Assay-observation experiment

In the full 1,810-person frozen analytic cohort, model separately for each assay: baseline observed, follow-up observed, and paired observed. Predictors are the pre-TACE2 variables above plus arm. Report observed fractions, adjusted arm contrast, five-fold cross-validated AUC, calibration intercept/slope or a prespecified calibration curve, and predicted-probability distributions by arm.

For the paired subset, multiply overlap weights by inverse predicted probability of paired observation. Report untruncated and 1st/99th-percentile-truncated versions, combined-weight distributions, and arm ESS. The existing observation audit found paired fractions 0.310 (99/319) versus 0.260 (387/1,491), risk difference 0.0508, and cross-validated AUC 0.673. These are selection-process findings. Observation weighting can address only measured pre-index predictors; it cannot recover outside tests, deaths, loss to follow-up, clinician rationale, or unrecorded disease severity.

### Negative control and locked sensitivities

The primary falsification outcome is same-assay pre-exposure change: select an early value closest to day -45 within days -60 to -31 and a late value by the frozen baseline selector in days -30 to -1; estimate the same overlap-weighted arm contrast. A material pre-exposure contrast in a direction and magnitude capable of explaining the post-index contrast falsifies the claim that the difference newly emerged after TACE2. A null negative control does not prove exchangeability.

Run all locked sensitivities in one matrix without promotion based on results:

- baseline windows -14 to -1 and -60 to -1;
- follow-up earliest and latest in days 30–90;
- target-nearest day 60 in days 45–75;
- target-nearest day 90 in days 60–120;
- inherited timestamp baseline, clearly labeled boundary sensitivity rather than primary;
- strict versus prespecified broad TACE text ontology;
- an ontology sensitivity in which all bevacizumab-only grace patients remain excluded but bevacizumab component partnering is assessed encounter-wide rather than exact-day;
- 2020–2024 era sensitivity, retaining 2018–2024 as primary.

Repeated-measure trajectories are secondary and descriptive. Fit them only if both arms have at least two follow-up observations in sufficient numbers, convergence succeeds, and the selected repeated-measure subset is characterized. They cannot supersede the paired primary estimate.

## Precision, positivity, falsification, and reporting gates

1. **Reconstruction gate:** source row counts, filter flow, 13 changed patient-rule labels, and the one exposed pair boundary case must reproduce exactly. Any unresolved mismatch makes execution invalid until repaired.
2. **Frozen-count gate:** report 319/1,491 and 99/387 if reproduced. Never alter era, baseline, follow-up, ontology, or assay rules to recover 100. Because 99 is below the inherited exposed-pair threshold, label the primary analysis precision-limited regardless of statistical significance.
3. **Precision gate:** report 95% confidence intervals and overlap/combined-weight ESS. If exposed ESS is below 80, total ESS is below 300, or either assay interval is too broad to distinguish clinically plausible benefit from harm on the observed scale, classify that assay as inconclusive. Because validated units and a clinical margin are absent, the latter judgment requires clinical review; the automatic verifier can check widths and ESS but cannot declare clinical adequacy.
4. **Positivity/calendar gate:** every year must retain both arms; pooled propensity support must overlap; no arm/year may dominate combined weight; post-overlap absolute SMD must be at most 0.10 for core covariates. Failure yields a descriptive or inconclusive result, not extrapolation.
5. **Observation gate:** extremely small predicted observation probabilities, major ESS collapse, poor calibration, or a material direction conflict between complete-case and observation-weighted estimates makes the biochemical finding inconclusive.
6. **Negative-control gate:** a clearly non-null adjusted pre-exposure same-assay contrast capable of accounting for the post-index result falsifies a newly emergent pathway contrast. Null does not validate causality.
7. **Timing gate:** direction reversal across the locked baseline/follow-up selectors, especially dependence on day-0 pre-procedure tests, establishes timing-sensitive ascertainment and blocks a stable clinical interpretation.
8. **Ontology gate:** material direction reversal or unexplained membership change under strict/broad TACE and bevacizumab-partner sensitivities establishes ontology instability.
9. **Outcome-coherence gate:** favorable albumin with clearly worse bilirubin, or favorable bilirubin with clearly worse albumin, is mixed/adverse rather than supportive.

## Falsifiable interpretation branches

- **Supportive:** exact reconstruction succeeds; overlap and observation gates pass; post-weighting balance is adequate; pre-exposure controls do not plausibly reproduce the contrast; and systemic-record patients show less albumin decline/more increase without greater bilirubin increase, with compatible direction across locked timing, era, and ontology analyses. This supports only a more favorable recorded-pathway association among observed patients.
- **Adverse:** the same gates pass, but systemic-record patients show greater albumin decline and/or bilirubin increase robustly. This argues against assuming a liver-preserving association; it does not establish medication-caused toxicity.
- **Precise null:** estimates and clinically reviewed intervals are narrow around no detectable difference and all gates pass. Report no detectable recorded-pathway difference, never equivalence or equal safety.
- **Falsified:** the result depends on the day-0 baseline boundary, is reproduced before exposure, reverses under locked definitions, lacks overlap, or relies on unresolved exposure ambiguity. Report which premise failed.
- **Inconclusive:** 99 exposed pairs, low weighted ESS, wide intervals, unstable observation weights, calendar nonpositivity, discordant assays, timing/ontology sensitivity, or unavailable adjudication prevents a stable interpretation. No favorable alternative window may replace the primary.

## Evidence limits and verification boundary

Medication rows are records/orders and cannot establish administration, adherence, dose delivered, combination delivery, indication, or intent. Diagnosis time is inherited from encounters. The source lacks a laboratory-unit column, complete outside-care capture, reliable performance status and Child-Pugh components, adjudicated TACE refractoriness, treatment rationale, imaging response/progression, toxicity attribution, and linked vital status. Institutional observation is not survival. Therefore do not calculate ALBI, apply external clinical cut points, pool differently named assays, claim toxicity causation, or infer comparative effectiveness.

An automatic verifier can check complete-source row counts, joins, timestamps, calendar windows, ontology matching, mutually exclusive classification, pseudonymous localization, deterministic assay selection, pair counts, weights, balance, ESS, uncertainty, and whether a stated supportive/adverse/null/inconclusive conclusion follows from computed outputs. It cannot establish clinical importance of an observed-scale margin, medication delivery, causation, response, progression, survival, or treatment superiority. Those stronger claims require validated assay units, medication-administration evidence, clinician adjudication, imaging and vital-status linkage, outside-care capture, and preferably external or prospective validation.
