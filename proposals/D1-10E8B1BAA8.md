## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats serum AFP.
The program's central claim (parents through `[prior hypothesis]`) is that a *rising within-patient AFP
trajectory* adds prognostic information for time to local progression beyond baseline AFP and beyond liver-reserve (ALBI)
kinetics measured in the SAME draws. The parent already attacks outcome misclassification. Its ONE explicitly deferred
rival is **informative measurement**: AFP draws are ordered at clinician discretion, so draw *frequency/timing* is itself
driven by patient state. If sicker (or responding) patients are measured more or less often, a naive slope-hazard
association can be an artifact of the observation process rather than tumor biology. This bears directly on the clinical
decision "should serial AFP drive risk-adapted imaging intensity after LDT?" — a decision that would be wrong if the
signal is a measurement artifact.

## 2. Hypothesis (falsifiable)
- H1 (leading claim, inherited): after LDT, a rising within-patient AFP slope in (index, t*] is associated with higher
  hazard of radiologic local progression, adjusted for baseline AFP and concurrent ALBI slope.
- H1-rival (tested here): the AFP-slope hazard is at least partly explained by the **informative AFP-draw process**
  (measurement intensity/timing). Observable difference: the per-1-SD AFP-slope log-HR under observation-process
  correction (shared-parameter joint model / inverse-intensity-of-observation weighting) is attenuated toward the null,
  and a draw-count-only model reproduces a similar "signal".

## 3. Inherited design (verbatim from parent, not re-litigated here)
- Population: patients with an HCC diagnosis (`diagnoses.诊断名称`) AND >=1 liver-directed procedure; index = earliest
  qualifying procedure (`procedures.手术`, `procedures.开始时间`).
- Landmark t* = index + 180 d (90 d sensitivity).
- Exposure: within-patient slopes fit ONLY on draws in (index, t*]; same-draw AFP + albumin + total-bilirubin pairing
  within +/-2 d on `labs.检验时间`; ALBI = 0.66*log10(bilirubin) - 0.085*albumin.
- Endpoint: modality-restricted abdominal local progression derived lexically from `examinations` (`检查所见`/`检查诊断`:
  增大/进展/复发/新发/增多), with 转移 as competing event; retreatment proxy secondary; censor at `encounters.出院时间`.
- Four inherited rivals (immortal time, lead time, baseline-AFP confounding, outcome misclassification); conjunctive
  falsification; monotone graded-vs-threshold dose-response; **no survival / no mRECIST-PFS claim**.

## 4. The ONE added element: informative-observation correction (M5)
Add a pre-specified **observation-process** analysis to the primary AFP-slope estimand:
- M0 (baseline): landmark Cox with baseline AFP (+/- baseline ALBI/Child-Pugh).
- M4 (parent primary): landmark Cox with mixed-model AFP-slope BLUP.
- **M5 (new primary robustness): shared-parameter joint model** linking a gap-time AFP-draw (visiting) process to the
  progression hazard via correlated random effects (justification bound by [K1]; analogous oncology use [K3]); plus a
  CPU-cheap **inverse-intensity-of-observation weighting** cross-check as a fallback if the joint likelihood does not
  converge on the landmark cohort.
- Negative control: a "draw-count-only" model (number of AFP draws in (index, t*] as the sole exposure) and a
  within-patient permutation of draw timestamps.

### Method comparison (recorded before selection)
- M0 loses the within-patient trajectory and cannot separate biology from measurement.
- M4 recovers the within-patient trajectory but **assumes the measurement process is non-informative**.
- M5 recovers a bias-corrected estimand and can reveal that the M4 association is an artifact of observation intensity —
  information the baseline and M4 structurally cannot provide. This is a scientific uncertainty (does the signal
  survive?), not a chase for a small predictive gain.
- Resource estimate: CPU-only. M0/M4 = minutes; M5 joint model = minutes to ~tens of minutes on the landmark cohort.
  Well inside the solver envelope (28800 s, 16 CPUs). GPU not required; deferral reason: no neural/sequence workload.

### Pre-specified outcomes (falsification)
- Supportive: M4 per-1-SD AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 AND remains CI-excluding-1 after M5/IIW within a pre-set
  attenuation bound (|Delta log-HR| <= 0.10) with pre-specified sign AND monotone quartile trend persists (p_trend<0.05,
  Q2-vs-Q1 CI excludes 1, >=30 events).
- Adverse (H1-rival): M4 HR CI excludes 1 but M5/IIW CI includes 1, OR the draw-count-only model explains the signal,
  OR the effect is confined to permutation-sensitive strata.
- Inconclusive: M5 non-convergence without a valid IIW fallback, sparse strata, or CI too wide to distinguish.
- Precise-null: M4 and M0 equivalent within +/-0.01 (no incremental value either way).
