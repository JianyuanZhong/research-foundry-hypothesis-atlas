# Physiology beyond care context: a decomposed, context-shifted test of pre-exit lactate-by-perfusion

## Scientific deliverable and unresolved question

This is a substantive successor to valid [prior hypothesis]. It preserves that candidate's fixed MIMIC-IV landmark, strict pre-landmark coverage, lactate/MAP/vasopressor phenotype, first-event R/D/S/N/U endpoint, fixed 5/10/20% review workloads, and locked leave-one-transfer-careunit-by-anchor-year-group evaluation. It adds the missing scientific decomposition: whether the signal is carried by measured physiology itself, by care/documentation intensity, or by both.

The future Harbor solver must newly fit or audit three feature representations on the same eligible subjects and split: P physiology-only, C care/documentation-context-only, and PC their union, with a phenotype-ablated PC0 and phenotype-augmented PCF. Completion requires row-level provenance and leakage audits, fitted competing-risk models (or a complete gate-failure audit), pooled and context-shifted predictions for every representation, fixed-capacity capture and calibration, R/D-specific outputs, paired uncertainty intervals, falsification results, and a conclusion ledger. Discovery makes no prevalence, fitted-performance, or clinical-effect claim.

The strongest evidence currently available is source/design-level. The audited read-only MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains repeated lactate and MAP observations, audited vasoactive input intervals, ICU support/procedure records, admissions death/discharge fields, and transfer-unit timestamps. Direct archive inspection confirms the relevant dictionary labels and IDs. This establishes that the proposed bindings are available; it does not establish coverage, event support, physiological validity, causal effects, clinical utility, or transportability.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among adults alive and still under ICU observation 12 hours after the earliest valid ICU stay begins, and meeting the fixed pre-landmark lactate/perfusion coverage gate, the prespecified persistent-lactate-by-measured-perfusion phenotype adds held-out fixed-capacity capture of record-defined ICU-level transition or death beyond care/documentation context and the matched order-blind history. The physiology increment should remain directionally positive in supported care-unit/year contexts, and its size may differ for R versus D.

This is clinically important because a risk queue that only identifies heavily tested or treated patients can reproduce local workflow without identifying a physiologic state. Conversely, if measured physiology adds robust signal beyond care context, it supports a more interpretable rationale for prospective validation. The study still cannot show that an alert improves care, that R was necessary, that treatment caused or prevented D, or that the phenotype transports outside this one hospital.

## Fixed population, landmark, coverage, and endpoint

Use the earliest valid ICU stay per hadm_id, retaining rows with non-null subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, intime, and outtime, with intime < outtime, joined to hosp/admissions on subject_id,hadm_id. Restrict to adults using hosp/patients.anchor_age >= 18; preserve MIMIC's age-91 top-code convention. The fixed landmark is t_F = intime + 12 hours. Require t_F < outtime and dischtime > t_F. A patient is live at t_F if deathtime > t_F, or deathtime is null and hospital_expire_flag=0. Death by t_F is ineligible. A null death time with an inconsistent terminal flag remains unresolved U, never a presumed survivor. Keep every candidate and a deterministic exclusion reason.

All feature construction is pre-landmark: clinical time in [intime,t_F], divided into [intime,intime+6h] and (intime+6h,t_F]. Point observations require finite clinical time and storetime <= t_F; interval observations require valid clinical start/end, use clinical time for overlap, and require storetime <= t_F. No post-landmark event, future transfer, dod, terminal last_careunit, dischtime, deathtime, or hospital_expire_flag is a predictor.

The strict, endpoint-independent coverage manifest preserves the parent rules: at least one finite lactate and one finite MAP observation in each six-hour bin; the audited pressor interval source must be sufficiently ascertainable for both-bin target-pressor minutes, with invalid/reversed intervals recorded and no missing pressor interval treated as zero. The solver must report coverage before inspecting outcomes, including clinical-time versus recording-time failures, unit failures, duplicates and joins. Persistent lactate is early and late median item 50813 >=2.0 mmol/L. Improving perfusion is late-minus-early MAP >=5 mmHg and late target-pressor minutes no greater than early; worsening is MAP <=-5 mmHg and pressor minutes no less than early. Discordant or insufficient cases are indeterminate, retained descriptively, and not silently merged. Threshold 4.0 mmol/L, MAP-only, all-MAP, and pressor-only variants are sensitivity analyses, not test-time choices.

Follow (t_F,t_F+24h] and assign the first state deterministically:

- R: a new post-landmark target-pressor interval; a qualifying support/procedure start; or a later ICU stay for the same subject/admission with a different stay_id and intime>t_F. Active intervals crossing t_F are not new starts. Qualifying procedure IDs are intubation 224385, invasive ventilation 225792, non-invasive ventilation 225794, hemodialysis 225441, and CRRT/dialysis 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809, or 225955.
- D: hosp/admissions.deathtime in the window, no later than tied R, respecting discharge.
- S: an alive discharge boundary before R/D.
- N: ascertainable absence of R/D/S through 24 hours.
- U: unresolved death, ordering, linkage, or follow-up ascertainment.

If R and D tie, D precedes R. The adverse composite is A=R-or-D, but R, D, S, N and U are always reported separately. R is record-defined escalation/support, not rescue necessity or clinical deterioration adjudication. Primary scoring uses strict E* excluding U; an all-candidate audit retains U and reports adverse-U/non-adverse-U bounds.

## Representations and leakage-safe physiology/context decomposition

All representations use the same strict cohort, subject-level split, labels, bins, tie rules and fixed workload. They differ only by an immutable feature whitelist.

### P: physiology-only

P contains finite clinical values and value-derived summaries from the two six-hour bins: early/late lactate 50813; the three MAP values summarized across the three item IDs; early/late MAP level and change; early/late target-pressor exposure only when explicitly designated as the phenotype's treatment-response component; and prespecified organ laboratory values from 50861, 50878, 50885, 50912, 50882, 51222, 51265 and 51300 when available. It may contain value-derived medians, ranges, slopes and cross-domain ratios, but no measurement counts, missingness flags, distinct-time counts, store delays, caregivers, item-choice frequencies, careunit, transfer, admission, demographic, order, support-procedure, or intervention-process variables. Optional lab values use training-fold median imputation without missingness indicators; the mandatory lactate/MAP coverage gate is unchanged. This is a predictive physiology representation, not a validated physiologic state.

### C: care/documentation-context-only

C contains no lactate, MAP, organ-lab, or other physiological values. It contains only pre-landmark nonphysiologic context: gender,anchor_age,admission_type,admission_location; ICU elapsed time and first_careunit; the transfer careunit covering t_F; counts, valid-bin indicators, distinct observation times, chart-to-store delays and item-family frequencies from chartevents, labevents, inputevents, and outputevents; and pre-landmark care-intensity/process features including target-pressor interval presence/minutes by the 11 IDs, Foley presence/count, and non-outcome support/procedure presence before t_F. It may include anchor_year_group only as an evaluation-block label, never as a predictor. C is intentionally a care/record context comparator, not a causal treatment history.

### PC, PC0 and PCF

PC is the union P+C. PC0 is PC after removing the prespecified lactate-by-perfusion phenotype terms F while retaining all other physiologic values, care/context features, process features, masks and counts. PCF adds exactly F: persistent lactate (early and late medians of 50813 >=2.0); perfusion direction (improving, worsening, or indeterminate by the fixed MAP/pressor rules); and early/late lactate, MAP, and target-pressor-minute terms and their interaction.

The primary transparent history model B1 is PC0 in the parent's order-blind twelve one-hour-bin implementation, with identical physiologic, support and process domains and no early/late F terms. Bdisc is B1 plus F. Thus the parent contrast is retained exactly but becomes scientifically interpretable as a phenotype increment after a care/documentation-aware base. B0 remains the parent's transparent latest-state/admission/context/support baseline. Bmask is the process/missingness-only control, implemented from C's observation masks/counts and context variables without physiological values. No feature from any representation may use a clinical or store time later than t_F.

## Estimands, evaluation, and context shift

Freeze a deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test split before outcome inspection, with all stays for a subject in one split. Fit all primary transparent models on train, tune penalties and imputation values on train/validation only, and lock them before test scoring. Let E* be the strict test estimable set excluding U, and k_w=max(1, round-half-up(w|E*|)) for w=5%, 10%, and 20%; use identical k, ranking tie-breaks, and subjects for paired model comparisons. For a model M, define fixed-capacity capture for event type y as Q_y(M,k)=TP_y(M,k)/number of i in E* with Y_i=y. Also report PPV, false-alert rate, Brier score, cause-specific calibration intercept/slope and O:E; use a random-allocation queue benchmark.

The primary incremental estimand is the parent-preserving, context-aware phenotype contrast at 10%:

Delta_F|C,A = Q_A(Bdisc,k_10) - Q_A(B1,k_10).

This is the additional fixed-capacity capture of A attributable to the prespecified lactate-by-perfusion representation within the same combined physiology-plus-care/documentation base. It is not a causal effect of measuring lactate, administering pressors, or changing care. The primary R/D-specific estimands are Delta_F|C,R = Q_R(Bdisc,k_10) - Q_R(B1,k_10), and Delta_F|C,D = Q_D(Bdisc,k_10) - Q_D(B1,k_10).

The required representation decomposition is secondary but mandatory:
- Delta_phys|C,A = Q_A(PC,k_10)-Q_A(C,k_10): all physiologic values beyond care/documentation context.
- Delta_C|phys,A = Q_A(PC,k_10)-Q_A(P,k_10): care/context beyond physiology.
- Delta_total,A = Q_A(PCF,k_10)-Q_A(C,k_10): the complete representation beyond context.
Repeat each contrast for R and D and report the non-additivity of queue rankings. These are predictive incremental contrasts, not unique causal or variance decompositions. The primary parent comparison Bdisc versus B1, its R/D contrasts, and the representation ablations must all be reported; a learned model cannot replace them.

Use the same transparent penalized discrete-time cause-specific R/D/S model family for P, C, PC, PC0/B1, PCF/Bdisc, B0 and Bmask. Use cause-specific hazards or competing cumulative-incidence predictions with the first-event tie rules fixed before scoring. Compare Bdisc also with the random queue, B0, Bmask and B1.

Preserve the locked context-shift design. Define context c as transfer-careunit covering t_F crossed with anchor_year_group, grouping only cells below a pre-fit minimum-support rule into an explicitly named pooled context. Determine support, covariate overlap and ESS without outcome-based cell selection. For each supported c, fit on training subjects outside c (validation also outside c), retain the feature whitelist and penalty choices frozen, and score only c at the same 10% capacity rule; do not tune on c. Report macro and event-weighted Delta_F|C,A, Delta_F|C,R, Delta_F|C,D, and the P/C/PC ablations, plus calibration. This is internal context robustness, not external validation or proof of transportability. Unsupported cells are reported, not silently pooled.

## Baseline, learned alternative, and method selection

The simple baseline is B0: transparent latest-state/admission/context/support competing-risk hazards, with no ordered phenotype terms. The matched scientific baseline is B1: the parent's order-blind twelve one-hour-bin history model using the same physiologic, support and process domains but no F terms. B1 is the key comparator because Bdisc-B1 isolates the prespecified phenotype rather than rewarding a larger feature set.

The substantive learned alternative is J1, a small masked chronological GRU with six two-hour bins, separate P and C channel tags, hidden size 32 or 64, dropout, and a three-cause hazard head. Train J1 in three fixed ablations (P, C, P+C) and the phenotype-augmented combined input; use the same labels, subject split, feature whitelist, target, 10% queue, calibration, R/D capture and bootstrap procedure as the transparent models. Select hidden size, stopping epoch and seed aggregation only on train/validation; never select on the held-out context or test outcome. J1 can reveal time-local persistence, reversal and nonlinear physiology-care interaction that fixed summaries lose. It cannot establish that a neural representation is physiologically meaningful, and it cannot rescue a failed transparent phenotype contrast.

The transparent decomposition is selected as the primary instrument because coefficients and ablations make the clinical interpretability claim inspectable; J1 is retained as a substantive sensitivity, not a complexity bonus. A latent trajectory mixture is deferred: without adjudicated physiologic states, it could convert selective recording into apparently meaningful latent classes. A large transformer is deferred because it adds capacity without resolving the care-proxy uncertainty. Treatment-effect modeling is deferred because treatment intent, counterfactual outcomes and adequate time-varying confounding support are unavailable. Multimodal Oncoformer-style work is not reproducible because local CXR images and the main paper/complete STAR Methods are unavailable; no unavailable text is claimed. These branches should be revisited only if adjudicated physiology, treatment intent/response, external data, or images become available. The prior's context-shift-only analysis remains a useful parent sensitivity, but this child is worth selecting over it only if the added P/C decomposition is preserved through compilation and the primary result is linked to it; no superiority or clinical benefit is asserted before execution.

## Uncertainty, falsification, and interpretation gates

Use 1,000 subject bootstrap replicates when the future solver envelope permits, preserving the frozen split, paired rankings, all eligibility gates and context definitions; report the actual number if lower. Include source/schema/dictionary/unit audits, duplicate/reversed interval counts, join coverage, clinical/store-time leakage scans, prohibited-field scans, chart-only versus chart-plus-storetime sensitivity, and overlap/ESS.

Falsification tests are prespecified: complete outcome-label permutation; early/late phenotype permutation; phenotype permutation within coverage and context strata; chronology shuffle for J1; removal or permutation of store-delay and observation-count features; MAP-item substitution checks; 2.0 versus 4.0 lactate threshold; MAP-only/all-MAP/pressor-only phenotype definitions; window/bin/landmark sensitivities; random queue; Bmask/process-only control; and separate R/D/S/N/U reporting. Any process-only or future-time signal surviving label permutation or timing permutation invalidates the relevant interpretation. No test workload, gate, context pooling rule or model family may change after held-out outcomes are viewed.

Supportive evidence requires valid source and no-future-time audits; strict coverage and event/context support; Delta_F|C,A >= 0.05 at 10% with an uncertainty interval excluding zero; Bdisc above the random benchmark and Bmask; no label/timing/permutation artifact; and non-reversal in the prespecified majority of supported context cells with acceptable calibration. For the clinically more specific interpretation, R and/or D directions must be reported separately; a composite-only gain is not described as a mortality or escalation result.

Adverse evidence is a null/reversed Delta_F|C,A, C matching Bdisc while P/F adds little, Bmask matching Bdisc, process/timing/label-permutation persistence, calibration failure, or reversal across context cells. This challenges the record-defined prioritization hypothesis and suggests workflow or documentation dependence; it does not prove that lactate or pressor treatment is harmful. Inconclusive evidence is failed coverage/support/overlap gates, sparse R or D denominators, broad intervals spanning zero and five percentage points, unresolved U bounds, unstable calibration, or unsupported context cells. A null decomposition is scientifically useful: it can show that the apparent parent signal is not distinguishable from care context in this record.

Computationally checkable outputs are source hashes and archive members, flow and exclusion reasons, row-level time/feature provenance, split assignments, representations, labels, predictions, cumulative-incidence estimates, queue captures, calibration, bootstrap intervals, context support/ESS, and falsification results. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for whether R was appropriate/necessary/preventable, whether support represents deterioration, clinician intent, treatment response or causal physiology, whether an alert changes outcomes, net utility, patient preferences, and external transportability. Notes are available in the source catalog but are excluded here; no claim about unobserved ward escalation or clinical intent is permitted.

## Claim boundary and selection decision

A positive result would support only this bounded claim: in the MIMIC-IV record-defined cohort, the pre-landmark lactate-by-perfusion representation adds fixed-capacity retrospective prioritization beyond a matched care/documentation-aware history, with specified R/D patterns and some internal context robustness. It would not support monitoring benefit, treatment benefit, causality, physiological mechanism, adjudicated severity, or external transportability.

This successor is scientifically worth selecting over the mature parent because it tests the parent's central residual ambiguity directly while preserving every parent gate and estimand: whether the apparent phenotype gain survives a prespecified care/documentation-only comparator and whether its R/D pattern is clinically distinguishable. The decision remains validity-gated. A future solver result—not readiness, parent rating, or confirmation of the directional hypothesis—must determine whether the decomposition supports, weakens, or leaves unresolved the clinical interpretation.
