# Provisional HCC trajectory hypothesis (episode 3 repair)

## Proposed relationship
Among adults admitted with an exact documented HCC diagnosis, a prespecified 7-day time-aware representation of liver, coagulation, renal, and tumor-marker laboratory measurements may add held-out information about a subsequent inpatient escalation-of-care proxy beyond a static admission snapshot. The hypothesis is directional only in the sense that trajectory information is expected to be useful; no effect size, clinical harm, or causal mechanism is assumed.

## Concrete evidence already available
The parent audit directly scanned the HCC source catalog and reports:
- encounters: 105,044 rows with patient/admission keys, age, sex, encounter/admission/discharge times, and department; 101,457 nonempty admission times.
- diagnoses: 1,810,646 rows; exact diagnosis name 肝细胞癌 occurs in 336,570 rows and 104,554 patient/admission keys.
- labs: 28,159,928 rows with patient/admission keys, assay name, qualitative and quantitative result, specimen type, and lab time; relevant exact assays include albumin, total bilirubin, INR, AFP, creatinine, ALT, platelet count, prothrombin time, and urea.
- procedures: 338,040 rows with patient/admission keys, procedure name, start/end time, and source; frequent labels include central venous catheterization, TACE/arterial embolization, ablation, and liver resection.
- All detail tables are documented as many-to-one to encounters on patient/admission keys; no cross-database key exists.

These are source-bound observations, not model results. The escalation dictionary, event counts, and assay unit/specimen harmonization remain to be audited in this episode.

## Uncertainties and missing evidence
The outcome is only an inpatient procedure-timing proxy. It is not death, ICU admission, or validated clinical harm. Treatment indication, admission selection, documentation intensity, delayed follow-up, and assay/unit heterogeneity may create apparent trajectory value. The dataset lacks a separate laboratory-unit field and lacks a validated death/ICU field. Any text-derived deterioration label would require a blinded prespecified dictionary and clinical adjudication. The three literature works cited in the parent have not yet been re-inspected in this branch; their claims must not be treated as newly verified here.

## Falsifiable test
Construct the exact-diagnosis, nonempty-admission-time cohort and restrict features to labs from admission through day 7, excluding measurements after discharge. Define the first qualifying procedure after the feature window and before discharge using a prespecified procedure dictionary. Compare a patient-level split, penalized logistic snapshot baseline with a time-aware sequence/state-space model using ordered values, time since admission, specimen type, missingness, and treatment timing. Evaluate held-out discrimination, calibration, uncertainty intervals, and negative-control timing. Support requires improvement beyond a prespecified trivial margin with stable sensitivity analyses; no improvement, degradation, failed negative controls, sparse events, or unstable units are adverse or inconclusive as specified.

No model has been fitted and no effect has been estimated.
