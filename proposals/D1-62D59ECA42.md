Origin seed: `[starting question]` ("continued deep sedation after stabilization delays

This is an **alternative-identification sibling on the same clinical question**, not a

## 0. Scientific opening (the parent's own conceded defect) — evidence vs conjecture

The parent decomposes the total deep-sedation-fraction effect into a path through the daily
wake-up/SAT process (chartevents **223780** "Daily Wake Up Deferred") and a direct path. Its own
text concedes the fatal threat:

- **Observed (established in the parent's own evidence):** 223780 is real and populated but *much*
  sparser than RASS in the frozen archive — 1,239 rows / 87 stays vs 12,456 rows / 541 stays for
  RASS in the parent's bounded sample. A mediator that is charted for a minority of stays cannot
  satisfy the positivity/overlap a natural-effect decomposition needs.
- **Conjecture being tested here (not yet established):** that "continued deep sedation delays
  liberation" is a **sustained-strategy (protocol) effect** of failing to *lighten* sedation, i.e.
  that it lives on the documented RASS lightening axis — which is exactly the axis a randomized
  trial (K3) manipulates — rather than requiring unmeasured mediator confounding on a sparse item.

**Why this is a different identification, not a repair of the same one.** A natural-indirect-effect
decomposition on a sparse, implementation-documenting mediator is *unidentifiable* (positivity) and
answers a non-manipulable "how much ran through this chart item" question. Re-framing the exposure
as the documented lightening strategy and estimating its **total effect** instead targets the very
estimand a trial would run (sustained lightening vs continued deep sedation) and needs only
sequential exchangeability + positivity **on the RASS strategy contrast**, which is far better
documented. This is a substantive scientific gain: it answers the clinically actionable question
even if the parent's mediation is undecidable.

**Leading claim (P, matching the seed):** sustained post-`t*` lightening (documented RASS ≥ −2 in the
prespecified intervals) vs sustained deep sedation (RASS ≤ −3) increases the cause-specific hazard of
early liberation without a commensurate rise in the death hazard.
**Strongest rival (Q, severity/indication):** the strategy contrast is a marker of the underlying
illness trajectory; the association attenuates under M2 toward the null (or the death hazard rises in
parallel), i.e. depth-only protocols are misled.
They differ on a measurable quantity this design computes: the **M2-adjusted sustained-strategy
cause-specific HR of liberation and the paired death CIF** (and their behaviour under the controls).

## 1. Clinical importance and what resolution would change

Time to liberation drives ICU LOS, VAP and delirium; K3 (Girard 2008 RCT) shows a randomized
awakening/lightening protocol raised ventilator-free days and lowered 1-year mortality, so the
strategy axis is causally real and consequential. If the sustained-lightening total effect is real and
survives M2 + controls, the actionable lever is *protocolised lightening* and the emulated target
trial is worth running in this population. If it is largely a severity marker, depth-only sedation
targets mislead. Either outcome changes the next decision; the parent's decomposition cannot decide
this when its mediator is unidentifiable.

## 3. Design: target-trial emulation of sustained lightening vs sustained deep sedation

Inherited UNCHANGED from the parent: population (invasively ventilated adults meeting
stabilisation at landmark `t*`), landmark `t*`, locked earliest-70 % / latest-30 % `icustays.intime`
split, M0→M1→M2 ladder, permuted-label control. **New:** the exposure/estimand/estimator.

**Target trial (K1).** Eligibility: same as parent. Treatment strategies: over **K prespecified 12 h
intervals** J1=(t*, t*+12 h], J2=(t*+12 h, t*+24 h] (primary K=2; K=1 and K=3 as sensitivity). In each
interval the *documented* sedation state is classified from 228096: **deep** if the interval's
RASS summary (modal/last observation) is ≤ −3; **light** if ≥ −2. **Prespecified sustained
strategies:** `gLL` = light in all K intervals; `gDD` = deep in all K intervals; `gDL` = deep in J1
then light in J2 (a dynamic "delighten later" strategy). **Primary contrast: `gLL` vs `gDD`.** No
mediator is measured; the RASS *strategy* is the treatment. Assign-at-`t*`; follow to min(t*+7 d,
ICU discharge, death).

**Estimand.** The **total (strategy-contrast) effect on the cause-specific hazard of liberation**
(227194, no re-intubation 225792 within 48 h), with **death (`admissions.deathtime`) as a competing
event**; report the cause-specific HR for `gLL` vs `gDD` and the 7-day cause-specific cumulative
incidence difference for liberation **and** the parallel death CIF (so liberation delay is never read
without the competing event). Continuous dose-response across the interval-RASS summary is a
secondary gradient (avoids an arbitrary deep/light cut as the only analysis).

**Estimator (prespecified).**
1. **Primary:** time-varying **stabilized IPTW marginal structural model** — discrete-time pooled
   logistic for the interval-specific cause-specific liberation hazard with stabilized weights
   `∏_j f(A_j | Ā_{j−1}) / f(A_j | Ā_{j−1}, L̄_j)`, plus a **censoring weight** for death/ICU discharge,
   `L̄_j` = the M0→M1→M2 confounders updated per interval. Report HR and 7-d CIF difference with
   bootstrap CIs (≥1000 resamples).
2. **Cross-check:** **g-computation** (sequential regression / iterated conditional expectations) on
   the same strategies; concordance between the two is required before any positive claim.
3. **Diagnostics:** propensity/weight distributions, **positivity** (interval-specific propensity in
   (0.05, 0.95); no strategy cell with near-zero support), **balance** (post-weight SMD < 0.1), and the
   **effective sample size** after weighting.
4. **Controls:** **permuted-label control** (shuffle `A_j` across stays → expect null) and a
   **negative-control exposure** = analgesia intensity (fentanyl 221744 / morphine 225154 /
   hydromorphone 221833 equivalents, same intervals) run through the identical MSM (any non-null
   flags residual confounding/time trends).

**G-computation cross-check vs baseline (method exploration).**
- **B0 baseline:** single-run logistic/competing-risks on the M2-adjusted *first-interval* lightening
  flag (ignores the sustained strategy and time-varying confounding) — the parent-equivalent baseline.
- **A1 (this child, primary):** time-varying stabilized IPTW MSM for the sustained-strategy total
  effect + g-computation cross-check. *Reveals* what B0 loses: the per-protocol effect of a
  **sustained** strategy under time-varying confounders affected by prior treatment, i.e. the estimand
  a trial would run. Deferred alternative: a NN/sequence model of the sedation trajectory — deferred
  (no clear scientific gain over the strategy contrast at this budget; revisit only if positivity on
  the RASS strategy fails). *Resource estimate:* CPU-only tabular MIMIC-IV (`lifelines`/`statsmodels`
  + NumPy bootstrap); no GPU needed or claimed; well under the discovery budget — solver fits on CPU.

## 4. Partial-identification sensitivity and the explicit decision rule (parent defect addressed)

- **Parent mediated estimand declared INCONCLUSIVE by its own prespecified rule.** We accept the
  parent's own concession: if 223780 is too sparse to support positivity, the mediated estimand is
  **inconclusive** and **no unplanned mediator is substituted**. This sibling therefore does **not**
  re-run the decomposition; it sidesteps it.
- **If one nevertheless wanted a partial-identification bound on the parent's indirect path,** it
  would require (a) a mediator-outcome unmeasured-confounding bound (e.g. an E-value / Rosenbaum-style
  sensitivity on the mediator→outcome leg) *and* (b) an explicit accounting for the **coarse/imperfect
  mediator** (misclassification of "deferred" vs "performed"), which widens the identified interval to
  [0, upper] and cannot make the natural indirect effect point-identified. This is recorded as the
  honest scope of the parent, **not** executed here.
- **Decision rule (prespecified, no silent substitution):** (R1) if positivity fails on any sustained
  strategy (a `gLL`/`gDD` cell with propensity outside (0.05,0.95) or ESS below the prespecified
  floor), the strategy-specific effect for that contrast is **declared inconclusive** and only the
  well-supported contrasts are reported; (R2) the mediated estimand is **never** revived by substituting
  a different itemid; (R3) the **total sustained-strategy effect is the primary clinical claim**
  throughout, reported with CIs and the paired death CIF.

## 5. Falsification criteria (prespecified)

- **Supportive (lightening-strategy effect real, P):** M2-weighted `gLL` vs `gDD` cause-specific HR for
  liberation > 1 with CI excluding 1, a positive 7-day cause-specific CIF difference, **no** commensurate
  rise in the death CIF for `gLL`, post-weight balance achieved, positivity satisfied, g-computation
  concordant, permuted-label and analgesia negative controls null.
- **Adverse (severity/indication, Q):** HR attenuates to the null across M0→M1→M2, or `gLL` carries a
  parallel rise in the death hazard (mortality conflation), or the analgesia negative control shows a
  parallel spurious effect (indication/measurement artifact).
- **Adverse (identification):** severe positivity failure — one sustained strategy is realized by too
  few stays to weight → the strategy contrast is declared inconclusive (not "no effect").
- **Inconclusive:** wide CIs spanning the null; g-computation and MSM disagree; extreme weights; RASS
  documentation too sparse per interval to classify the strategy. An imprecise null does not refute P.
- **Not established by this design (needs other work):** any causal claim absent an instrument/RCT;
  full sequential exchangeability; whether residual latent severity is absorbed by M2; the parent's
  cross-world no-unmeasured-mediator-confounding assumption.

## 6. Interpretation

Supportive ⇒ prioritise protocolised lightening and run the emulated trial. Adverse ⇒ depth/lightening
is a severity marker; deprioritise depth-only targets. Inconclusive ⇒ fix RASS/strategy documentation
before deciding. **Computationally checkable by a solver:** the weighted HR, CIF difference, death CIF,
positivity/balance/ESS diagnostics, g-computation concordance, and both controls, with CIs.
**Requires clinical adjudication / another study:** sequential-exchangeability, residual latent
severity, and any causal claim.
