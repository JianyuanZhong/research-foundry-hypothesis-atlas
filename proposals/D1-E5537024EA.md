# eICU durable liberation endpoint audit: measurement-validity and falsification result

## Proposal status and parent

This is a measurement-validity/falsification child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s fixed landmarks, exposure labels, strict physiologic screen, first qualifying ICU stay rule, endpoint horizon, and recurrence/documentation definitions. It does not convert the audit into a treatment-effect analysis.

## Clinical question and unresolved claim

The parent design asks whether the documented strategy label at fixed ICU landmarks is associated with subsequent durable liberation from invasive ventilation. The clinically consequential unresolved claim is that a care-plan label can be operationalized as a defensible liberation endpoint, sufficiently observed and sufficiently independent of the label, to support a strategy-outcome comparison.

The strongest claim supported by this audit is narrower: in this eICU snapshot, the exact care-plan and independent chart proxies can be enumerated reproducibly, but the independent markers are too sparse and 48-hour post-event observation is too incomplete for the prespecified validation gates. The audit therefore falsifies readiness for an adjusted strategy-outcome association under the parent’s rules. It does not establish that either strategy causes liberation differences, and it does not establish true extubation, successful spontaneous breathing-trial performance, physiologic readiness, or post-ICU absence of reintubation.

## Fixed population and time design

Reconstruct the parent cohort from the read-only eICU 2.0 source snapshot `[source checksum]`.

Use landmarks L = 1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, and 7200 minutes after ICU admission. At each L, select the first exact care-plan label in [L, L+360] from `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue`: `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` or `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`; define decision time D as its `cplitemoffset`. Exclude contradictory same-time first labels, non-adults, and stays discharged at or before D. Apply the parent’s pre-landmark invasive evidence, no pre-landmark EOL discussion, no prior 24-hour `Spontaneous - adequate` marker, and strict physiology screen. Retain the first eligible ICU stay per `uniquepid`, retaining all eligible landmarks in that stay.

The reconstructed cohort is 540 person-landmark rows: 309 evaluation and 231 no-trial. The cohort CSV hash is `[source checksum]`; the reconstructed parent result exactly matches the frozen parent result hash `[source checksum]`.

## Endpoint algorithms

For each person-landmark, search D < offset <= D+1440 for:

1. `careplan_only`: first `carePlanGeneral` `cplitemvalue = Spontaneous - adequate`.
2. `independent_first`: earliest of respiratory-charting `respchartvaluelabel = RT Vent On/Off` with value `Off` or `off`, and exact treatment string `pulmonary|radiologic procedures / bronchoscopy|endotracheal tube removal`.
3. `union`: earliest of all three signals.

The endpoint is a candidate event, not a claim of true extubation. Recurrence is the earliest post-event invasive marker from exact invasive care-plan states, RT values `Continued`, `Start`, or `Suspended`, exact invasive airway-plus-setting respiratory-care evidence, or prespecified insertion/reintubation/repositioning/tracheostomy treatment strings. `ventendoffset` alone is never a liberation or recurrence marker.

Primary durability requires E < event offset, no recurrence, no intervening ICU death, unit discharge at or after E+2880, and at least one row in every successive 12-hour bin over (E,E+2880] from each of `carePlanGeneral`, `respiratoryCharting`, `respiratoryCare`, `treatment`, `vitalPeriodic`, and `vitalAperiodic`. Sensitivities use 60, 720, 1440, and 2880 minutes. A live discharge to a non-ICU location may bridge only the observed partial bins before discharge, with complete coverage and no recurrence before discharge; this cannot detect post-ICU reintubation.

Marker disagreement is substantial in availability, though not in the small overlap. Across all rows, evaluation had CP-only 54, CP+ETT 7, CP+RT 2, ETT-only 4, and no marker 242; no-trial had CP-only 34, CP+ETT 2, RT-only 1, ETT-only 1, and no marker 193. This means union is overwhelmingly driven by the care-plan proxy, not independently corroborated extubation documentation.

The restricted hospital overlap criterion passed in three hospitals (248, 252, 420), with evaluation/no-trial counts 18/52, 53/96, and 59/25. In the restricted rows, CP availability was 34/130 (26.2%) versus 27/173 (15.6%), but independent availability was 1/130 (0.77%) versus 0/173 (0%). Process-field availability was 48/130 (36.9%) versus 50/173 (28.9%).

The temporal placebo was structurally zero for care-plan-only because the cohort excludes a prior 24-hour spontaneous marker. Independent placebo signals were 3/309 evaluation and 0/231 no-trial (risk difference 0.97 percentage points, Wald 95% interval -0.12 to 2.06 percentage points); this is sparse and inconclusive, not evidence of validity. The union placebo is the same independent-marker signal under this construction.

The descriptive 48-hour strict risk differences were -0.89 percentage points for care-plan-only, +0.43 points for independent-first, and -0.24 points for union; bridged differences were +1.90, +2.37, and +2.87 points, respectively. These are descriptive only, not adjusted or causal, and no association model is authorized after failed gates.

## Gate decision and falsification

Passes: at least three hospitals with at least ten observations per strategy; CP availability at least 10% in each restricted strategy; union signal availability at least 10% in each restricted strategy; restricted union coverage difference no more than 20 percentage points.

Fails: independent availability at least 10% in each restricted strategy (0.77% versus 0%); independent event sufficiency under the explicit audit convention of at least 10 events per strategy (13 versus 4); union putative-event complete 48-hour coverage at least 80% in each strategy (49.3% versus 52.6%).

Inconclusive: temporal placebo, because the care-plan placebo is structurally forced to zero and the independent placebo has only three versus zero events. The audit therefore stops before any adjusted strategy-outcome association. The scientifically useful result is that the endpoint is not validated as an independent, sufficiently observed durable liberation outcome in this snapshot.

## Falsification criteria and interpretation

Support for proceeding would require independent markers in both strategies at adequate frequency, complete 48-hour documentation for at least 80% of putative union events, acceptable concordance or an adjudicated explanation for disagreement, and an informative placebo design not mechanically excluded by cohort construction. Adverse evidence is exactly what was observed: near-absence of independent signals, approximately half of signalled events being strictly observed through 48 hours, and large dependence of the endpoint on unverifiable discharge bridging. Inconclusive results would arise if source availability or marker timing could not be distinguished from true absence; here the full source scan establishes that the specified strings exist globally but are rare in the strict cohort, so sparsity is itself a measurement-validity limitation.

True extubation, SBT completion/performance, readiness, ventilator-free time, and post-ICU reintubation require clinical adjudication, richer ventilator waveforms or device-state data, and post-ICU follow-up. The bridge sensitivity must not be interpreted as observed durable liberation after ICU discharge.
