# Training-plus-held-out-stay uncertainty in broad deployment of exact eICU care-plan labels

## Question and clinical importance

The assessed parent found that the exact eICU care-plan values `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` are sparse, highly concentrated by hospital, and do not improve a bounded-airway structured-documentation prediction task under broad hospital-held-out evaluation. Its remaining inferential limitation was that the stay-cluster bootstrap held fitted models fixed. A small adverse loss difference could therefore reflect held-out-stay sampling alone, unstable training fits, an intercept/calibration shift between hospitals, or one modeling specification or outcome component.

The unresolved hypothesis is: **after jointly propagating training-stay and held-out-stay uncertainty, adding the post-freeze three-state exact-target feature (`evaluation`, `no_trial`, `missing_exact_target`) to the frozen pre-landmark documentation baseline does not improve held-out prediction of the post-classification charted respiratory-setting reduction outcome across observed eICU hospitals; if the adverse loss persists after local intercept recalibration and across regularization strengths, it is not explained merely by a site intercept shift or the selected penalty.**

This is clinically consequential because a sparse, site-specific documentation vocabulary could otherwise be promoted as a generic multicenter indicator of weaning-related care, cohort inclusion, benchmarking, quality measurement, or decision support. The experiment addresses predictive fitness only. It does not test whether bedside evaluation, a spontaneous breathing trial, extubation readiness, or extubation occurred.

## What is already supported and what remains untested

The strongest parent-supported claim is that, in this eICU snapshot and target-independent denominator, exact-target receipt is sparse and concentrated, and its broad-deployment incremental structured-documentation prediction is at best negligible and adverse for bounded airway definitions. The parent’s held-out-stay uncertainty was conditional on fitted models.

This child tests the narrower unresolved inferential claim that the adverse broad-deployment result survives resampling of both training and test stays with refitting of preprocessing and both logistic models. It also tests whether the adverse result is compatible with a local calibration/intercept shift, sensitive to regularization, or concentrated in one charted-setting component.

No result here supports or refutes the clinical proposition that clinicians performed an evaluation or SBT when the exact target was absent. Missing and conflicting structured values remain documentation states, not clinical non-events.

## Frozen population, time ordering, outcome, and variables

The child preserves the parent’s target-independent person-landmark population and all definitions. Landmarks are ICU minutes 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200. The first qualifying stay per `uniquepid` is selected using `unitvisitnumber`, then `patientunitstayid`. Exact invasive-airway eligibility is unbounded or requires evidence within 24, 12, or 6 hours before landmark.

Eligibility remains adult status, stay continuation through landmark, recent valid FiO2 and PEEP, the parent’s strict oxygenation/respiratory-rate/MAP requirements, no prior end-of-life discussion, and no recent `Spontaneous - adequate` state. Exact target classification occurs only after the population is frozen, in `[L,L+360]`, from the care-plan values. The model feature has three states: `evaluation`, `no_trial`, and `missing_exact_target`.

The baseline uses respiratory-charting information in `[L-360,L)`: `pre_chart_n`, `pre_distinct_labels`, `pre_robust_n`, `pre_fio2`, `pre_peep`, `pre_pressure_support`, and `pre_vent_rate`, together with landmark. The outcome is measured only in `(L+360,L+1080]` and is observed only when `unitdischargeoffset > L+1080`. The primary outcome `robust_reduction` is any prespecified reduction in a non-peak charted respiratory setting: FiO2 >= 0.05, PEEP >= 2, pressure support >= 2, or set ventilator rate >= 2. Peak-pressure fields remain excluded.

Component outcomes are reconstructed separately with exactly the inherited labels and thresholds: `reduction_fio2`, `reduction_peep`, `reduction_pressure_support`, and `reduction_vent_rate`. The reconstruction rescans only `respiratoryCharting` for the already frozen `sid`/landmark keys, computes post-window minima, asserts agreement with inherited paired flags, and asserts exact reconstruction of `robust_reduction`. It does not rebuild or change the denominator.

## Computable experiment

For each of the four airway definitions, filter the frozen enriched rows to the definition, `complete_outcome_window`, and nonmissing outcome. Broad-deployment held-out hospitals require at least 20 outcome-observed rows and both outcome classes, with no requirement for local exact-target receipt. For each eligible hospital, fit on all other eligible outcome-observed rows and evaluate only on that held-out hospital. The baseline and label models are the parent pipelines: one-hot landmark (and label only in the augmented model), unknown categories ignored, median imputation with missingness indicators, standardization, logistic regression with no class weighting, `max_iter=2000`, and primary `C=1.0`.

The primary estimand is the difference in augmented-minus-baseline log loss, reported both row-weighted (micro) and as the equal-hospital mean of hospital-specific differences (macro). Brier score, AUROC, mean prediction, and observed-minus-predicted calibration are secondary diagnostics. Repeated landmarks are clustered by `sid`.

The primary uncertainty analysis uses 100 nested stay-cluster bootstrap replicates. In each replicate and each leave-one-hospital-out fold, stays are sampled with replacement within every training hospital and within the held-out hospital; all landmarks from a sampled stay move together. Both preprocessing and both C=1 logistic pipelines are refit for every fold and replicate. Hospitals are fixed, so this propagates training- and held-out-stay uncertainty conditional on the observed hospital set but not uncertainty about an unseen hospital population. A 300-replicate fixed-fit held-out-stay bootstrap is retained for direct comparison with the parent.

Two prespecified mechanism sensitivities are included. First, within each held-out hospital, a five-fold split by stay estimates an intercept-only logit shift on calibration stays and evaluates the shift on disjoint stays; slopes and model fits remain frozen. This uses local held-out outcomes and is not the no-local-data primary deployment estimand. Second, the complete LOHO analysis is repeated at `C=0.1`, `1.0`, and `10.0`.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

The supportive gate is deliberately stringent: the unbounded and at least two of the three bounded variants must improve both micro and equal-hospital macro log loss, and both nested-bootstrap 97.5th percentiles must be below zero. The adverse gate requires no bounded variant to meet that supportive criterion and at least two bounded variants to have nested-bootstrap probability of positive macro loss at least 0.75. Otherwise the result is inconclusive.

If adverse, a stronger mechanism label is allowed only when at least two bounded variants retain positive equal-hospital macro loss after cross-fitted local intercept recalibration and have positive macro loss at all three C values. Failure of that mechanism gate narrows the conclusion to intercept-compatible or regularization-sensitive loss. Sparse component site counts are reported rather than treated as proof: pressure-support analyses have only 9, 6, 4, and 3 eligible hospitals for the four definitions, respectively.

Observed results from the bounded execution were:

* Nested bootstrap completed all 100 replicates for each definition with zero failed fold attempts.
* Primary nested macro delta log-loss probability of worsening was 0.75 unbounded, 0.93 for 24 hours, 0.79 for 12 hours, and 0.89 for 6 hours. The corresponding nested macro 95% intervals were [-0.001047, 0.002421], [-0.000314, 0.003005], [-0.000967, 0.003984], and [-0.000755, 0.008546].
* Point broad-deployment micro/macro delta log loss was +0.000707/+0.000398 unbounded, +0.000587/+0.000964 for 24 hours, +0.000798/+0.001005 for 12 hours, and +0.001674/+0.001804 for 6 hours.
* Cross-fitted local-intercept macro deltas were +0.000065 unbounded, +0.000625 for 24 hours, +0.000788 for 12 hours, and +0.001305 for 6 hours.
* Macro point deltas remained positive at C=.1, 1, and 10 for every bounded variant: 24 hours +0.000652/+0.000964/+0.001265; 12 hours +0.000539/+0.001005/+0.001498; 6 hours +0.000598/+0.001804/+0.003226.
* Component results were heterogeneous. FiO2 was adverse for every definition; pressure support was adverse but sparse; PEEP and ventilator-rate components were not uniformly adverse, including small improvements in some definitions. Thus the composite result is not claimed to be driven by every component.

The executed gate is therefore `adverse_broad_deployment_fitness`. The additional mechanism gate is met for the bounded variants: the adverse macro loss remains positive after local intercept recalibration and across all tested C values. This supports reproducible within-hospital predictive degradation of this composite structured-documentation task, not clinical harm.

Supportive results would justify prospective evaluation of the exact feature as a documentation predictor, not bedside validation. Adverse results falsify unqualified broad multicenter predictive fitness for this frozen population, proxy outcome, and eICU snapshot. Inconclusive results would require more data rather than a negative deployment recommendation.

## Limits and verification boundary

The computation can verify filtering, time separation, joins represented in the frozen artifact, fold separation, model specification, stay clustering, refitting, component reconstruction, calibration sensitivity, regularization sensitivity, uncertainty summaries, and gate logic. It cannot establish actual airway status, device-delivered settings, SBT performance, extubation readiness, extubation, quality, benefit, harm, clinician intent, or causality. Charted reductions are documentation proxies and may not equal delivered care. Hospital transport beyond the observed eligible eICU hospitals remains untested.

Clinical interpretation requires time-resolved device and airway data, direct SBT/extubation records, blinded clinician adjudication, local workflow/data-dictionary review, and prospective external multisite validation. The small loss differences should not be interpreted as patient-level effects.
