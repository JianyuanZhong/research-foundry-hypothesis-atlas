# Provisional repair: discordant AKI recovery in MIMIC-IV

Status: provisional checkpoint; no cohort counts or clinical results are claimed yet.

## Proposed relationship

Among adults in their first MIMIC-IV ICU stay with a documented creatinine-based AKI episode and adequate observation through a prespecified 48-hour landmark, creatinine improvement accompanied by impaired urine output is associated with worse subsequent in-hospital death and/or persistent or worsening kidney dysfunction than concordant creatinine-and-urine-output recovery. This is a prognostic association, not a causal or mechanistic claim.

The exact archive and table bindings, joins, and clinical time fields are in the parent proposal and its evidence note. These are schema observations, not cohort results.

The unresolved clinical question is whether discordant recovery carries prognostic information beyond creatinine recovery itself, rather than merely reflecting monitoring intensity, catheterization, treatment, or illness severity. The strongest rival is measurement/observation artifact: urine output may be absent because no catheter or collection is documented, while creatinine sampling is clinician-selected. Fluid balance is a treatment and monitoring summary and cannot establish fluid overload or tubular injury.
