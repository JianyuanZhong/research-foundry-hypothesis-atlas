## Unresolved clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the inspected local evidence is narrower: baseline metabolic measurements are associated with later coded cardiometabolic disease, and dated structured records can represent progression. The local snapshot and inspected references do not establish that an assessment-linked within-person metabolic change predicts a subsequently recorded additional disease domain beyond current levels, nor whether any incremental signal is coherent deterioration or discordance among markers. Field instances and field 53 provide an assessment-linked date proxy, not verified specimen collection times; ICD code/date pairs are recorded coding, not adjudicated clinical onset.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among UK Biobank participants aged 40–69 at a first valid assessment-linked panel who have exactly one of diabetes, cardiovascular disease, or chronic kidney disease recorded at both panels, remain in that same one-domain state at panel 1, and have complete paired HbA1c, HDL and triglyceride values, assessment-linked metabolic change adds prognostic information for the first subsequent additional cardiometabolic domain beyond panel-1 levels, existing-domain identity, disease duration and prespecified covariates. Conditional on any change signal, a coordinated worsening component and orthogonal marker-discordance components have distinguishable route-specific associations.

This is a conditional internal-prognosis hypothesis among participants observed at a valid second assessment and complete for the primary assay panel. It is not a causal effect of changing a biomarker, a biological slope, a clinical-onset claim, a recommendation to repeat testing, a treatment effect, a monitoring benefit, or a claim transportable to non-returners.

The clinical importance is that clinicians often manage people with one established metabolic/vascular/renal domain who are at risk of a second domain. If dated change adds robust information, it would identify a potentially actionable subgroup for future prospective monitoring or adjudicated risk-utility studies; if it adds no information after current levels and selection adjustment, repeating these assays for this prognostic purpose would have less support. The substantive advance is attribution: the same matched event model tests whether information is a common worsening burden or disagreement between HbA1c, triglyceride and HDL, rather than treating any flexible model improvement as a biological discovery.

## Population, temporal boundaries and outcomes

Use age 40–69 inclusive at d0, valid sex, five-source overlap, valid normalized d0=`53-0.0` and d1=`53-1.0` with d0<d1 and a 365–3,650-day gap. Require exact agreement of duplicate date proxies after normalization. Require finite paired primary assays from `main` at instances 0 and 1; retain the biological_samples agreement status. At d0 and d1, derive exactly one target domain from same-suffix code/date pairs, with the same domain persisting and its earliest recorded date no later than d0. Target domains are diabetes (ICD-10 E10–E14 or ICD-9 250), cardiovascular disease (ICD-10 I20–I25, I50, I60–I69 or ICD-9 410–414, 428, 430–438), and chronic kidney disease (ICD-10 N18/N19 or ICD-9 585). Exclude death on or before d1. Disease duration is (d1 minus the first valid paired date for the existing domain)/365.25.

A domain event exists only for a code and parseable date at the same array position. For each domain use its minimum valid paired date over all specified ICD-10 and ICD-9 positions. For every participant after d1, find the earliest date strictly greater than d1 with a previously absent second domain, and compare it with death. Primary event categories are `SECOND_DOMAIN_UNIQUE` (one new domain at the earliest date), `SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI` (at least two new domains sharing that earliest date), `DEATH` (death on or before the new-domain date, including same-day ties), and `CENSOR`. Unique route claims are secondary; multi-domain ties are never assigned arbitrarily to one route. Time zero is d1. Follow to the first event, `C_obs), or d1+five years, whichever is supported by the censoring rule.

The largest finite date in outcome/death date fields is `C_obs`, not an administrative closure date. Emit a censoring audit with source-level closure evidence, date range, dates after each candidate boundary, and participants whose five-year horizon exceeds supported follow-up. Without documented closure evidence, five-year incidence/calibration/decision-curve language is conditional and cannot be called clinically supportive.

For marker j, compute assessment-linked annualized change (x1-x0)/((d1-d0)/365.25), reverse HDL so worsening is positive, and standardize using fit-partition parameters. Call this assessment-linked change, not a biological slope.

## Matched analysis and alternative methods

All models use the same cohort, event/tie architecture, explicit death hazard, time zero, censoring, source-quality gate, split, preprocessing, weighting, uncertainty and locked evaluation.

- **B0, simple baseline:** route-specific cause-specific Cox hazards for each existing-domain to unique-new-domain transition plus explicit death. Inputs are panel-1 HbA1c, triglyceride, HDL, existing domain, duration, age, sex, gap, BMI, SBP, DBP, smoking and center. No change variables enter B0. This is the clinically interpretable current-state comparator.
- **M_common, mechanistic alternative:** B0 plus C, the fit-partition standardized common worsening component of the three changes.
- **M_full, primary incremental model:** the identical hazards plus C and two orthogonal discordance contrasts D1 and D2, with change-by-domain interactions only where pre-specified event support permits. M_full is dimension-matched to three unrestricted change dimensions; M_common asks whether coordinated worsening alone carries information. M_full can reveal discordant-marker information that B0 and C lose; M_common can reveal coherent deterioration obscured by separate marker effects.

This is not a model-complexity contest. A two-panel recurrent/attention model is deferred because two observations cannot identify useful sequence structure and added capacity cannot resolve return selection, date-proxy timing or coded-onset ambiguity. A latent measurement-error/slope model is deferred because no independent technical replicates or verified specimen times identify assay reliability. A four-instance mixed-effects slope is deferred because verified assay instances 2/3 are absent. Revisit these alternatives with at least three verified dated panels or independent reliability data and a pre-specified scientific gain that survives selection and timing falsification.

Model d1 date observation among d0-eligible participants using only d0-available variables; separately model d1 assay completeness among observed d1 participants. Show positivity and effective sample size. A stabilized, clipped IPCW refit is a sensitivity conditional on observed repeat-panel status, not a correction for nonreturners. A return-weighted exploratory analysis requires explicit d0-MAR assumptions and is inconclusive if positivity fails.
