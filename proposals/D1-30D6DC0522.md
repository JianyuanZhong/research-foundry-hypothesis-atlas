# Fixed-offer service yield for preoperative HCC documentary-M2 review

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The consequential decision is whether a scarce preoperative multidisciplinary review service could use interpretable information already written in routine CT/MRI reports to allocate a fixed number of review opportunities among adults approaching a first eligible hepatocellular-carcinoma (HCC) resection, so that more reviewed patients have high-grade microvascular invasion documented as M2 in the eventual resection pathology.

The tested action is allocation of review only. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment, locoregional or systemic therapy, surveillance, or any other care.

**Strongest claim supported before this experiment.** Direct inspection of snapshot `[source checksum]`, its complete catalog, table schemas, and live CSV headers establishes that HCC has patient/encounter-linked procedure rows with start/end fields, examination accessions with acquisition-like starts and eventual report bodies, untimed pathology narratives, and dated medication/order rows. It does not have a preoperative referral/candidate roster, planned-operation or cancellation ledger, MDT slot ledger, report authored/final/release/view time or immutable version, pathology time/specimen/slide/block key or sampling protocol, clinician review/action, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, or patient benefit. The available HCC computation can therefore test eventual stored-content value within a future-defined completed-resection pseudo-roster, not deployment, treatment choice, biological M2, or benefit.

Current full-text evidence establishes clinical importance and a validation gap, not the proposed result. Neves and Soares (2026; DOI `10.3390/cancers18132028`; PMCID `PMC13360217`; frozen full-text XML [source checksum]) reviewed 36 HCC early-recurrence prediction studies: 35 were retrospective, only 6 reported external validation, calibration and decision-curve analysis were inconsistent, and most had high risk of bias. Fan et al. (2026; DOI `10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944`; PMCID `PMC13187967`; frozen full-text XML [source checksum]) found M2 in 106 of 621 resected HCC patients and only modest LDH discrimination (AUC 0.610); they explicitly cautioned against using LDH alone to change surgical extent and called for incremental, external, and prospective validation. These sources support the importance of MVI and the need for decision-focused validation; they do not establish report-semantic value, this institution's event rate, a clinically acceptable workload, treatment response, or benefit.

The research-ambition README was inspected as a rigor guide. Its Cell main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; neither was claimed as read or used as evidence.

The selected parent correctly froze workload and release pacing from 2015–2019 and honestly labeled the test stream a completed-case pseudo-roster. Its residual interpretive weakness is making each policy's first `J` completed reviews co-primary: those `J` patients can arise over different arrival horizons. A previously proposed “common prefix ending when the slowest policy reaches J” is algebraically null when every policy is capped at J: it reproduces each policy's own first J set and does not create a shared opportunity-set contrast. This child therefore makes **capture per predeclared offered slot** the primary service estimand. It preserves first-J results only as timing diagnostics and adds an exact descriptive decomposition of service yield into utilization and accepted-case composition. It never calls that decomposition causal or common-opportunity selection.

## Unresolved hypothesis and exact estimands

Let `s` index the complete outer set of coherent source states. A state jointly fixes patient membership, selected operative episode and clock, selected encounter and age, recorded prior treatment, pseudo-arrival order, examination accession identity/partition and clock, modality pattern, whole-report visibility, pathology composite, and documentary-M2 outcome. Variables from different states may never be mixed.

For each state and historical year `a=2015,...,2019`, form `N_a(s)` before parsing outcomes or report semantics and define

`N_ref = min_s min_{a in 2015:2019} N_a(s)`.

Freeze `K_q=floor(q*N_ref)` for `q in {0.05,0.10,0.20}`. The same absolute `K_q` applies to both 2020 and 2021, every policy, access cell, coherent state, and bootstrap replicate. It is a source-derived sensitivity workload, not observed service capacity. If `N_ref<500`, `K_.05<25`, or any coherent test state cannot offer K pseudo-candidates, that q cannot support the claim.

Policies are:

- `S`: the structured baseline plus prespecified interpretable report semantics.
- `S0`: the exact fitted S pipeline—same preprocessing, coefficients, penalty, calibration, threshold, release logic, and counters—with only semantic fields masked. When all semantics are masked, S and S0 must be bitwise identical.
- `B`: an independently fitted report-body-free structured baseline.

For policy `M` in a state/year/q/buffer/access cell, let `A_i(M)` be its prefix-only action at pseudo-arrival i, `n_M=sum_i A_i(M)`, `e_M=sum_i A_i(M)Y_i`, utilization `U_M=n_M/K_q`, and accepted-case documentary-M2 proportion `P_M=e_M/n_M`. If `n_M=0`, P and its decomposition are undefined and the result is inconclusive; the utilization gate should ordinarily fail first. Define primary service yield

`R_K(M)=100*e_M/K_q = 100*U_M*P_M`.

The same-fit semantic effect is `D_K=R_K(S)-R_K(S0)`; the complete-strategy effect is `C_K=R_K(S)-R_K(B)`. Both are additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered review slots.

For `X in {S0,B}`, decompose `Delta_K(S,X)` exactly:

`Selection(S,X)=50*(U_S+U_X)*(P_S-P_X)`

`Utilization(S,X)=50*(P_S+P_X)*(U_S-U_X)`

so `Delta_K(S,X)=Selection(S,X)+Utilization(S,X)`.

This is the two-order Shapley decomposition of the algebraic product and shares the interaction equally. It is a descriptive mechanism audit. `Selection>0` means the accepted sets have higher documentary-M2 composition along their policy-specific online histories; it does not identify a causal effect, a common opportunity set, or what either policy would have selected at the other's completion time. `Utilization>0` means S used more of the same offered capacity. Report both terms and their simultaneous intervals for D and C.

For timing diagnosis only, freeze `J_q=ceil(0.90*K_q)`; report each policy's first-J capture, calendar time/arrival ordinal to J, and whether J was reached. These cannot support the primary hypothesis. A favorable first-J contrast with adverse or inconclusive `D_K/C_K` is not evidence of service value.

**Primary hypothesis H1.** A nonempty exact report-access frontier exists such that, at every claimed cell, every q, 2020 and 2021 separately, all four 12/24/48/72-hour decision buffers, and every coherent state:

1. simultaneous 97.5% lower limits for both `D_K` and `C_K` exceed 5 additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered slots;
2. simultaneous lower limits for both Selection decompositions exceed 0, so the service gain is not solely greater slot use;
3. each primary policy has utilization at least 0.90, S reaches J, and S's capture is at least 0.80 of a state-matched retrospective top-K oracle; and
4. every source, adjudication, extraction, calibration, null, state-completeness, optimization, and inference gate passes.

The strongest supportive conclusion would be only that eventual stored CT/MRI report semantics materially improved a pretest-fixed historical service-yield benchmark within completed eligible resections, robust to enumerated source uncertainty, with positive accepted-set composition along the policy's own online history.

## Population, clocks, and temporal boundaries

The person unit is `患者主索引`; same-encounter joins use `(患者主索引,就诊号)`. Preserve physical row ordinal and multiplicity before exact deduplication. Two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician must freeze a resection dictionary and reconcile `手术来源` values, including `手麻系统` and `病案手术`, into operative-episode alternatives. A clinically linked anesthesia-system `开始时间` may be exact only after validation. A midnight/date-like case-record start is an interval `[d,d+24h)`; missing, competing, same-day, and adjacent-day clocks remain explicit states.

Within each coherent state, select the first eligible completed resection. Membership requires age at least 18 at the selected encounter, all-row same-encounter pathology explicitly supporting HCC, and no **recorded** prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[t_dec-365d,t_dec)` using state-matched procedures, medications, and orders. This is not biological treatment-naivety. Nonmembers contribute neither action nor outcome and are never coded negative.

Set primary `t_dec=t_op-24h`, with locked 12-, 48-, and 72-hour buffers. Develop in 2015–2018, tune/calibrate/select the finite policy in 2019, and test once in 2020 and 2021 separately. Any patient with a possible later-period state is quarantined from earlier fitting. Use 2022 onward only for grade/template and covariate-drift audits.

Map each year to `u=(t_dec-Jan1)/(next Jan1-Jan1)` and 366 fixed bins. From 2015–2019 pseudo-arrivals only, freeze monotone `F_ref(u)` and released slots `L_q(u)=floor(K_q*F_ref(u))`, with `L_q(1)=K_q`. The same release curve and K apply to both test years. Arrival intervals crossing order or release boundaries are enumerated. Exact ties use a frozen hash of patient ID. At arrival i, a policy may use only that member's admissible score, scaled calendar time, released/used counters, and objects frozen by 31 December 2019. It may not inspect future arrivals, final test-year N, future ranks, outcomes, another policy's actions, or end-of-year fill.

For each M/q, select one constant calibrated-score threshold from `{0,0.005,...,1}` using 2019 alone, maximizing documentary-M2 capture subject to utilization at least 0.90 under the frozen release curve; ties choose fewer false reviews, then the larger threshold. Freeze it before 2020. No queue, recall, replacement, or backfill is allowed.

## Report unit, outcome, variables, and baselines

A nonblank source accession is `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)`, formed before time filtering, modality assignment, text extraction, or masking and retaining every component row. If component modality labels conflict or valid starts differ by more than 24 hours, the state envelope includes both an indivisible-accession interpretation and a deterministic partition by normalized `(检查,开始时间 calendar-date)`; neither may be selected by outcome. Blank `检查号` rows remain in source and population audits, but their text is masked as identity-unknown.

A report unit is temporally eligible only when its complete component-clock state places it in `[t_dec-90d,t_dec)`; the deterministic conservative rule uses maximum valid component start, while missing/conflicting clocks remain alternatives. Select at most the latest eligible unit per modality with frozen ordinal/hash ties. Two blinded abdominal radiologists map all component `检查` labels to CT, MRI, non-target, or unresolved. Concatenate `检查所见` and `检查诊断` in frozen raw order. Visibility exposes the whole selected unit. Exposed text means eventual stored text only.

Compose every exact-deduplicated pathology row on the selected encounter from `病理,检查所见,检查诊断`. A frozen position-aware parser assigns `Y=1` only for anchored selected-value M2 and `Y=0` only for anchored M0/M1. Boilerplate, unanchored mentions, missing/ungraded MVI, conflicts, uncertain specimen linkage, or inadequate documented sampling gives `Y in {0,1}`, never exclusion. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors adjudicate every test unknown/conflict and every parsed M2; a third resolves disagreement. The endpoint is documentary M2, not biological M2.

B contains state-selected age, sex, CT/MRI/other acquisition indicators, eligible accession count, acquisition-to-decision recency, component count, report-identity ambiguity, and development-frequency `机器型号` category. It excludes report body, pathology, diagnoses, laboratories, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff facts. S adds frozen interpretable fields: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites, each present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned. Fit S and B with patient-wise worst-state elastic-net logistic loss; never average source or visibility states. First-come, seeded lottery, periodic lottery, none, age/sex-only, and retrospective top-K are controls. AUROC/AUPRC and the oracle are secondary and cannot rescue the primary service result.

## Access frontier, inference, gates, and falsification

Use exact access guarantees on a 0.05 grid: `h_C` for CT among C-pattern members, `h_M` for MRI among M-pattern members, `h_A` for at least one visible modality among CM members, and `h_B<=h_A` for both visible among CM members. O/C/M/CM patterns stay in denominators. Integer quotas, singly visible CM modality choice, identity partitions, clocks, membership, outcomes, chronology, release counters, thresholds, and actions remain jointly coupled. The coordinatewise-minimal cells satisfying H1 form the frontier. This is a hypothetical exact-version access requirement, not observed access.

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits. Rebuild states, models, 2019 calibration/thresholds, online policies, `U/P/R`, decomposition terms, and endpoints in each replicate; keep original K/J, bins, dictionary, threshold grid, and clinical margins fixed. Put worst-state extrema inside each replicate. A max-centered 97.5% simultaneous family covers D/C service yields, both decomposition terms, utilization, both years, all q, all buffers, access cells, identity states, attainability ratios, and null tests. More than 2.5% failed replicates invalidates support. The 2.5% model error budget plus a separate 2.5% exact-version roster/access/capacity audit family permits at most 5% by Bonferroni for any later conditional workflow claim.

Run 1,000 development/tuning semantic-block permutations stratified by year, modality pattern, source-unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and formatting family, with full refits. Require all-semantic masking to make S/S0 scores, actions, accepted counts, events, and D exactly equal.

Required gates: `N_ref>=500`; `K_.05>=25`; every test state can offer K; at least 50 adjudicated documentary-M2 events per claimed year envelope; anchored grade at least 85%; all test unknowns adjudicated; parser kappa at least 0.90, M2 PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95; modality agreement at least 0.95 and unresolved at most 1%; each claimed pattern at least 100 members and 20 M2; semantic-feature PPV/sensitivity at least 0.85; no required-feature/pattern shift over 15 percentage points from 2019; all/no-report calibration slope 0.80–1.20 and intercept −0.10–0.10; all policies utilization at least 0.90; S reaches J; exact release/counter accounting; complete state/backpointer replay; optimizer equals exhaustive enumeration for union N<=12; coordinatewise monotone access limits; zero laboratory/forbidden lineage; and complete simultaneous inference.

- **Supportive:** every gate passes; a nonempty frontier exists; all simultaneous lower limits for D_K/C_K exceed 5, both Selection terms exceed 0, utilization exceeds 0.90, and attainability exceeds 0.80 in both years at every q/buffer/claimed cell. Conclude only robust historical stored-content service yield and positive accepted-set composition in the completed-case pseudo-roster.
- **Adverse:** a valid simultaneous upper limit for D_K or C_K is at or below 5, the corresponding Selection upper limit is at or below 0, the frontier is empty, or attainability's upper limit is below 0.80. Report the exact failed year/q/buffer/cell. Favorable AUC or oracle capture cannot rescue it.
- **Mixed/fragile:** service-yield margins pass but Selection does not (utilization-mediated), Selection passes but offered-slot yield does not, D and C disagree, or years/q/buffers/access cells disagree. The joint hypothesis fails; do not average cells or call this support.
- **Inconclusive:** intervals cross a margin; a policy cannot reach the utilization gate; or any population, clock, identity, adjudication, extraction, calibration, state, null, optimizer, lineage, event, or bootstrap gate fails. Missing external roster/report/capacity evidence always leaves workflow and deployment inconclusive, even if the HCC-only result is supportive.
