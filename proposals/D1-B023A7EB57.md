## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, the branch claims a higher
within-patient post-LDT AFP slope predicts subsequent local progression. Its repeated honest limitation is
that the endpoint is a lexical imaging descriptor, and that the strongest *confounding* rival has never
been entered: intrahepatic tumour burden and macrovascular invasion at baseline both raise AFP and drive
progression, so the AFP-slope hazard could be a re-expression of burden rather than an independent
on-treatment tumour-activity signal. If so, AFP-slope-guided surveillance intensification is not justified
and the branch's interpretation is overturned.

## 2. Hypothesis (falsifiable)
- H1: after adjusting for a pre-index baseline tumour-burden / vascular-invasion ordinal, the per-1-SD
  post-LDT AFP-slope HR for local progression remains >1 with a 95% CI excluding 1 and a small |deltaHR|.
- Rival R_burden (leading alternative): the AFP-slope hazard is confounded by baseline burden / invasion;
  the adjusted HR attenuates toward 1 (CI includes 1) while the burden term itself is prognostic.

## 3. Inherited frozen design (verbatim; not modified)
Population = HCC diagnosis + >=1 liver-directed procedure; index = earliest qualifying procedure; landmark
t* = index + 180 d (90 d sensitivity). Exposures (per-patient AFP slope, ALBI slope) fit ONLY on draws in
(index, t*]; same-draw AFP + albumin + total-bilirubin paired within +/-2 d on `检验时间`;
ALBI = 0.66*log10(bilirubin) - 0.085*albumin. Primary endpoint = modality-restricted abdominal
local-progression descriptor event {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多} on `examinations`, with 转移 (metastasis)
as competing event; censoring via `出院时间`; retreatment proxy secondary; conjunctive falsification;
no survival / mRECIST claim. Reused machinery: surveillance-intensity covariates, ALBI negative control,
first-modality stratification, mandatory selection guard.

## 4. This child's ONE added element: baseline tumour-burden / invasion confounding (R_burden)
(a) Build a pre-index burden ordinal from abdominal imaging descriptors in `examinations`
(`检查所见` AND `检查诊断`) over `[index-90 d, index]`: multiplicity (多发/多灶/弥漫/弥漫性), largest-lesion
size tokens when present, macrovascular invasion (门脉癌栓/癌栓/门静脉); explicit `unknown` when no
qualifying pre-index study. (b) Supplement from the `pathology` table descriptors at/near index when
available. (c) Re-fit the frozen landmark Cox adding the burden ordinal and report the per-1-SD AFP-slope
HR adjusted vs unadjusted and deltaHR with 95% CI. A burden x AFP-slope term is reported descriptively only
and never carries the primary claim. (d) Branches:
- Supportive: adjusted AFP-slope HR > 1, 95% CI excludes 1, |deltaHR| small (< 15% attenuation).
- Adverse = R_burden: adjusted HR attenuates toward 1 (CI includes 1) while burden is prognostic.
- Inconclusive: burden-proxy coverage below the pre-stated fraction (to be fixed from the solver's first
  feasibility output) or largely `unknown`.
- Precise-null: tight adjusted CI near 1 (upper bound < 1.5), flat ALBI negative control.
- Selection guard (mandatory): report burden-known vs burden-unknown and IMGneg vs full indexed cohort on
  surveillance-intensity covariates; do not read a covered-only estimate as the population effect.

## 6. Method comparison (recorded before selection)
- M0 baseline (simple): the frozen two-stage landmark Cox — per-patient OLS AFP slope (and ALBI slope) over
  (index,t*] as a fixed covariate; no burden term.
- M_burden (substantive alternative): the same landmark Cox plus the pre-index burden ordinal, plus a
  descriptive burden x slope term.
What M_burden reveals that M0 loses: whether the AFP-slope association is independent of baseline tumour
burden/invasion or is a burden proxy — i.e. whether the estimand is tumour activity or tumour load.
Fit target: HR for AFP slope (adjusted) and the attenuation deltaHR. Split: development vs a held-out
confirmation split (or repeated K-fold) fixed before fitting. Uncertainty: 95% CI (bootstrap for deltaHR).
Budget: CPU only, ~2 cpus / 8 GiB, one fit < 10 min. Selection reason: M_burden is a competing-explanation
test that can overturn the branch, not a performance chase; defer only if burden coverage is inadequate.
