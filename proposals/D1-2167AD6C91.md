# Cross-source, outcome-blind falsification of the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Status and bounded research question

This compiler-ready successor has parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead-frozen scientific schema exactly: 540 person-landmark rows; 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons; 74 hospitals; first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`; landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission; first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`; `D = cplitemoffset`; inherited pre-landmark exclusions; exact literal labels and two arms; fixed B/E/T windows, parser, thresholds, and K-state precedence; repeated landmarks clustered by `patientunitstayid`; and all-row accounting.

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence is only that the literal values are sparse, hospital-heterogeneous structured documentation and that a label/trajectory association could arise from patient state, the opportunity to record information, repeated stay documentation, exit ascertainment, or a respiratory-chart-specific artifact. The data do not establish bedside SBT or extubation semantics, clinician intent, delivered settings, readiness, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The unresolved falsifiable claim is narrower: after pre-D adjustment for respiratory state and documentation opportunity, does the exact current label add reproducible information for the frozen later trajectory in a held-out hospital, and does any increment replicate in a temporally aligned, independently sourced `vitalPeriodic` construct rather than appearing only in `respiratoryCharting`? The substantive repair is a predeclared **cross-source artifact falsification matrix**. It separates (i) patient-state information, (ii) documentation opportunity, and (iii) respiratory-chart artifact by requiring label increments to be evaluated in source-isolated models and against outcome-blind pre-D controls before any post-D endpoint is inspected.

This is descriptive prediction and falsification, not a causal intervention study. Its operational consequence is only whether the label merits further expert chart adjudication; no bedside or patient-utility recommendation is permitted.

## Frozen endpoint and all-row estimand

Use clinical offsets relative to each row's D, never entry offsets:

* `B = (D-720,D]`
* `E = (D,D+720]`
* `T = (D+720,D+2160]`

Enumerate all source-wide `respchartvaluelabel` and `respcharttypecat` values with matched/unmatched counts before arm-stratified output. Parse only locked FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate aliases; require finite numbers, bounds, fixed fraction/percentage conversion, duplicate collapse, same-clinical-time conflict handling, and recorded parse reasons. A component requires at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes; a state requires at least three of four components. Missing is never zero, unchanged, carried forward, or a negative.

Use fixed absolute B-to-T thresholds FiO2 `0.10` fraction, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`. For non-exit rows, sustained de-escalation requires observed B/T states, at least two component decreases, no increase, and no E escalation. Escalation requires observed B/T states plus at least two increases, or a newly documented invasive airway in E/T after no invasive evidence in B. Stable/mixed is the remaining classifiable state. An indeterminate required E airway check cannot create de-escalation and is non-exit unobservable unless an independent increase/airway rule assigns escalation.

`respiratoryCare` is airway corroboration/sensitivity only and cannot fill numeric settings; active interval is `ventstartoffset <= time` and missing or `ventendoffset >= time`. A finite `patient.unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160` has precedence, including death, discharge, transfer, or inability to complete T. Record exact exit offset, `unitdischargestatus`, `unitdischargelocation`, and `exit_before_washout = 1{unitdischargeoffset <= D+720}`. Invalid/unavailable exit timing is a separate administrative `invalid_exit_time`, never alive by default. Assign exactly one ordered state to every row:

`K ∈ {competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.

Primary all-row probabilities remain `p_k(a)=N_a^-1 Σ_i 1{A_i=a,K_i=k}` and `Δ_k=p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`. The conditional classifiable display remains `q_c(a)` and `δ_c`, only after support checks and always with fixed arm denominators, exits, and unobservability. Neither estimand is causal.

## Outcome-blind pre-D controls and source-isolation matrix

Create and freeze `pre_D_control_manifest.json` before extracting any E/T respiratoryCharting endpoint value, endpoint flag, K state, exit subtype, or post-D count. All controls use only fields with clinical time `< D`; the current label is not used to define a control. The manifest records row counts, finite-value rules, missingness, timestamp-spacing rules, and all source-specific feature names before outcome access.

1. **State controls.** From pre-D `respiratoryCharting`, summarize only the B-window four-component values/missingness and their clinical-time spacing. From pre-D `vitalPeriodic`, summarize finite `sao2` and `respiration` values, missingness, row count, and distinct `observationoffset` spacing. From pre-D `respiratoryCare`, summarize airway presence/interval evidence without using post-D fields. These are candidate patient-state proxies, not validated physiologic truth.
2. **Opportunity controls.** Separately summarize value-blind row counts, distinct clinical timestamps, distinct component labels, minimum-spacing indicators, and `respchartentryoffset-respchartoffset` lag (respiratoryCharting); row/airway counts (respiratoryCare); row/timestamp counts (vitalPeriodic); and row counts (treatment). Counts and lags cannot be combined with their values in the opportunity-only block. Entry lags are diagnostics, not clinical time.
3. **Outcome-blind negative controls.** Before post-D extraction, evaluate whether the label increment is present for two fixed pre-D targets that cannot be caused by a post-D trajectory: (a) a B-window vital availability/spacing indicator (`vital_periodic_opportunity`, defined as at least two finite rows at distinct offsets spanning at least five minutes), and (b) a pre-D respiratory-chart component availability indicator (`respchart_component_opportunity`, defined by the locked observation/spacing rule). These are documentation-process controls, not clinical endpoints. A label increment confined to these controls is adverse to patient-state interpretation. Do not call their absence evidence of semantic validity.
4. **Source-isolation matrix.** Fit the same hospital-held-out label increment comparison for pre-D blocks: `M_chart_state` (respiratoryCharting values only), `M_vital_state` (vitalPeriodic values only), `M_airway_state` (respiratoryCare airway only), `M_opp` (all value-blind opportunity only), and `M_all0` (all permitted state plus opportunity). Add the exact current label to each to obtain `M_chart_state+L`, `M_vital_state+L`, `M_airway_state+L`, `M_opp+L`, and `M_all1`. These models are secondary decompositions and never alter M0/M1 or the frozen K endpoint. A label gain only in the respiratoryCharting-only model, with no corresponding gain in the separately evaluated vital or airway target, is a respiratory-chart artifact warning; a gain in opportunity-only models is documentation information, not patient-state information. Because respiratoryCare and vitalPeriodic are imperfect and may be sparse, non-replication is adverse only when the relevant source opportunity and support gates pass; otherwise it is inconclusive.

The controls are pre-D, outcome-blind, and frozen before endpoint reading. They do not create new outcome definitions or permit post-D adjustment.

## Primary prediction, state/opportunity residualization, and hospital holdout

Retain parent models M0 (pre-D respiratory state plus opportunity, no label), M1 (identical plus exact label), and Mopp (opportunity only). Add the source-isolation matrix above. ## Independent physiologic triangulation and respiratory-artifact gate

Use `vitalPeriodic` as a separate secondary target, never pooled into K. In E and T, after preserving exit precedence, define an opportunity-supported window as at least two finite rows at distinct `observationoffset` values spanning at least five minutes. Define separate `H_E` and `H_T` states `{alert,no_alert,physiologic_unobservable}`; among supported windows, alert requires at least two observations satisfying `sao2 < 88` or `respiration > 30`. These are chart-derived threshold constructs, not hypoxemia, tachypnea, respiratory failure, or harm.

Require source-level reporting for K, H_E, and H_T. The stricter label-information interpretation requires: M1 improves both micro and equal-hospital-macro held-out log loss over M0 for all-row K and at least two frozen endpoint/observation sensitivities; state/opportunity cell and conditional-null gates pass; the gain is not confined to exits, unobservability, opportunity, entry lag, persistence, or one respiratory-chart component; and the direction is concordant in at least two supported overlap hospitals for K and at least one independently evaluated H target. If vital/airway opportunity is insufficient, this triangulation gate is inconclusive, not adverse. A gain present only in respiratoryCharting with a supported null/control failure is adverse to a patient-state interpretation. No gate may call a failed nonreplication proof of absence of bedside meaning.

The parent operational manual-review prioritization analysis may be retained as secondary only: fixed predeclared thresholds/costs/capacities, review/no-review/abstain, explicit unresolved states, no patient-utility or intervention claim. It must not rescue a failed source-artifact gate.

## Uncertainty, falsification, and fail-closed gates

Use fixed-seed `61061` stay-cluster bootstrap, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates, refitting the full held-out pipeline and recomputing cells, nulls, source-isolation metrics, and triangulation. Report attempted, valid, failed, effective-cluster, supported-cell, and site-support-loss counts. Do not use row bootstrap. Report hospital-held-out micro and equal-site macro log loss/Brier, calibration where finite, and all-row K accounting.

Feasibility is supportive only if at least 40% of all 540 rows are classifiable and at least 10 hospitals have both exact labels among classifiable rows; below 20% or below five hospitals is adverse feasibility, intermediate inconclusive. Transport requires at least five hospitals with at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows per arm representing at least 50% of supported rows and no more than 25% directional reversals. The residual state gate requires at least five hospitals and five supported state/opportunity cells spanning both arms, no cell over 25% of supported rows, and a valid conditional null. The independent-source gate requires at least two supported overlap hospitals and at least one supported vital target; otherwise inconclusive.

Computational invalidity includes any artifact/source/schema/catalog/hash/header/archive mismatch; duplicate or non-one-to-one join; wrong labels/landmarks/D; parser, boundary, or unit error; post-D leakage into controls or predictors; care-plan contamination; outcome read before control freeze; failure to assign exactly one K; exit-precedence failure; fold/stay leakage; unsupported permutation treated as valid; or conclusions not linked mechanically to outputs. Stop interpretation and mark `verification.json` invalid if any occurs.

Supportive output establishes only replicated incremental prediction of specified structured chart constructs after the prespecified controls. Adverse output means the label increment is not robust for those constructs or is explainable by opportunity, exit, persistence, site, or respiratory-chart composition. Inconclusive output means sparse source opportunity, unsupported overlap, invalid/null infeasibility, or uncertainty too wide. No output establishes bedside label semantics, treatment effect, patient benefit/harm, quality, or causality. Those require expert adjudication, synchronized device logs, local interface/workflow dictionaries, validated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, follow-up, and external/prospective replication.
