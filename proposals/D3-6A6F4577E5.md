## 1. What changed from the parent and why
The parent (episode 26) used E-MTAB-8322 only as a **pseudobulk cross-sectional anchor** and reported
weak/negative concordance (r = -0.343, sign agreement 0.278, 18-gene module). Pseudobulk conflates
two observationally distinct explanations:

- **H1 within-cell state**: remission-vs-resistant differs in the *per-macrophage expression program*
  (the cell's own regulatory state).
- **H2 composition/abundance**: the difference is dominated by the *fraction/abundance* of
  resolution-like macrophages (a cell-mixture shift), not by any within-cell change.
- **H3 assay/scope artifact** (rival to both): the signal is explained by cell-number/depth
  differences between donors or by the curated 18-gene module scope.

The raw per-donor 10x matrices for E-MTAB-8322 are available in the configured source tree, so a
bounded, donor-aware single-cell decomposition is the smallest feasible check whose result could
change the parent's conclusion. This is a decisive-axis evolution of a valid board leader, not a new
question. **No cross-dataset donor join is performed** (donor ID namespaces differ across E-MTAB-8322,
E-MTAB-8316, GSE95588); only directional/module comparisons and within-dataset statistics.

## 2. Unresolved question and competing claims
Question: In the RA synovial macrophage compartment, is the drug-free-remission vs
treatment-resistant difference a **within-cell program** (H1) or a **composition/abundance effect**
(H2), and does the within-cell direction align with the in-vitro MERTK-perturbation module
(E-MTAB-8316 none vs UNC106238) and the GSE95588 d1-4 coculture time course (direction anchor only)?

- Leading claim (H1): per-cell macrophage expression program separates remission from resistant.
- Strongest rival (H2): a resolution-like macrophage subpopulation differs in abundance; within-cell
  residual program, after accounting for composition, is not separable.
- Second rival (H3): differences track cell number/depth or the curated module, not biology.
- Directional anchor (not a claim): in-vitro and coculture MERTK-module direction.

## 4. Simple baseline vs substantive alternative (method exploration)
- **Baseline (parent)**: pseudobulk group mean difference + curated 18-gene module score. Loses all
  cell-level information and cannot distinguish H1 from H2; sensitive to cell-number/depth (H3).
- **Substantive alternative (proposed solver deliverable)**: per-cell model on the raw E-MTAB-8322
  matrices.
  - Exact available inputs: per-donor raw counts (genes.tsv x barcodes.tsv), donor group from sdrf.
  - Fitting target: (a) a **within-cell program score** = per-cell expression of a resolution-like
    macrophage meta-program (MERTK/MRC1/CD163/FOLR2/LYVE1/TREM2/TIMP1 axis) and/or donor-level
    latent factor from a donor-aware model; (b) an **abundance metric** = per-donor fraction of
    cells in the resolution-like cluster (cluster on the shared gene space, per-donor fractions).
  - Split: leave-one-donor-out (LOO) over the 13 group-relevant donors (remission + resistant),
    HC/naive held as external reference only.
  - Model: mixed/GLM with `condition` fixed and `donor` random (or per-donor summary then
    donor-level Wilcoxon), explicit covariate for `n_cells`/`n_umi` (depth) to bound H3.
  - Outcome/uncertainty: effect size + bootstrap CI for the within-cell term vs the abundance term;
    report the **variance decomposition** of the remission-vs-resistant difference into
    composition vs within-cell components.
  - Baseline comparison: pseudobulk mean difference vs the decomposed model on the same donors.
  - Resource/time budget: CPU-only, minutes-to-tens-of-minutes; matrices ~1.2 GB total; no GPU.
  - Why the alternative reveals more: only single-cell data can hold composition fixed and ask
    whether the residual within-cell program still separates groups — the pseudobulk baseline
    cannot express that estimand at all.

## 5. Falsifiable design, outcomes and falsification criteria
- **H1 supported**: a within-cell program separates remission from resistant with a consistent sign
  across LOO donors (e.g. mean effect |d|>=0.5, sign agreement >=0.7) AND that within-cell effect
  survives adjusting for the resolution-like abundance fraction (the composition term does not
  absorb the group difference).
- **H2 supported (adverse to H1)**: the group difference is largely explained by the abundance
  fraction of resolution-like macrophages; the within-cell residual effect shrinks toward 0
  (CI includes 0) after composition adjustment.
- **H3 supported**: effects track `n_cells`/`n_umi` (depth) or collapse when the module is widened
  beyond the curated 18 genes -> inconclusive; re-scope genes/donors.
- **Inconclusive**: LOO instability across donors, or cell counts too small after QC -> defer.
- Falsification: H1 is falsified if the within-cell term is null/opposite-sign across LOO donors
  while the abundance term carries the difference. An underpowered null will not be read as
  refutation.
