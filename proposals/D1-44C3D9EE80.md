# Asynchronous Organ Recovery After Circulatory Stabilization in Sepsis: A Falsifiable MIMIC-IV Experiment

## Parent
Expert seed 1 (`[prior hypothesis]`): "After circulatory stabilization, patients whose kidney or respiratory function remains impaired are more likely to deteriorate again."

## Scientific Question

After circulatory stabilization (a prespecified vasopressor-free landmark in the first 72h of ICU), adult ICU patients with sepsis or shock whose kidney or respiratory function remains impaired (discordant recovery) have a higher risk of re-escalation of support or in-hospital death within 7 days than patients whose organs have recovered.

## Why This Matters

A single SOFA score at admission misses the temporal discordance between organ recoveries. Clinicians use "is the patient improving?" to decide step-down, discharge, and escalation of therapy. If kidney and respiratory recovery are asynchronous and the discordant pattern carries independent prognostic weight beyond baseline severity, that changes monitoring intensity and step-down timing.

### Supported Claim (Prior Literature)
- Inflammatory recovery from sepsis follows a universal trajectory defined by co-regulatory dynamics of WBC and platelet populations, with 9x relative risk of death for patients deviating from this trajectory [K1].
- Vasopressor-free days are a clinically meaningful endpoint in septic shock, and different treatment strategies produce distinct patterns of hemodynamic stabilization and organ recovery [K2].
- AKI is increasingly recognized as a systemic syndrome with consequences extending beyond the kidney, including cross-talk with the lungs and other organ systems [K3].

### Unresolved Claim (This Experiment)
Whether the *discordance* between kidney and respiratory recovery — after circulatory stabilization has been achieved — carries independent prognostic weight for re-escalation or in-hospital death, beyond what is explained by baseline severity (age, sex, SOFA-proxy at landmark, comorbidities).

No prior study has tested whether the *pattern* of organ recovery (concordant vs. discordant) after a prespecified stabilization landmark independently predicts outcomes. Existing work measures organ dysfunction at a single timepoint or as a composite score, not the temporal relationship between organ recoveries.

## Rival Explanations

1. **Baseline severity confounding**: Patients with discordant recovery may simply be sicker at baseline. *Addressed by*: adjusting for age, sex, SOFA-proxy at landmark, and comorbidities in the baseline model; the extended model adds the discordant indicator on top.

2. **Observation-practice artifact**: Testing frequency differs by severity; patients with more frequent labs appear to have "recovered" or "not recovered" based on measurement timing. *Addressed by*: using prespecified time windows around the landmark (±6h) and requiring at least one measurement in each window; acknowledging as a limitation.

3. **Immortal time bias**: Patients who survive to the landmark are selected for survival. *Addressed by*: the landmark design inherently handles this — follow-up begins at the landmark, and only patients who reach it are included.

## Study Design

### Population
- Adult ICU patients (age ≥ 18) with sepsis or septic shock (ICD-10: A40–A49, R65.20, R65.21)
- ICU stay ≥ 24 hours
- At least one vasopressor administered in the first 72h of ICU (shock proxy)

### Temporal Boundaries
- **Index window**: First 72h of ICU stay (from `icustays.intime`)
- **Stabilization landmark**: First time point T within the first 72h such that there is a vasopressor-free interval of ≥ 12h starting at T. T is the end time of the last vasopressor before the free interval.
- **Recovery assessment window**: ±6h around the landmark (T−6h to T+6h)
- **Outcome window**: Landmark T to T+7 days (or ICU discharge, whichever is earlier)

### Stabilization Landmark Definition (Prespecified)
The stabilization landmark is the earliest time T in the first 72h of ICU such that:
1. The patient received ≥ 1 vasopressor before T
2. There is a continuous interval of ≥ 12h starting at T with no vasopressor administration
3. T ≤ ICU intake + 72h

Vasopressors (verified itemids in `icu/inputevents`):
- 221906 (Norepinephrine), 222315 (Vasopressin), 221662 (Dopamine), 221289 (Epinephrine), 221749 (Phenylephrine)

### Per-Organ Recovery Criteria (Prespecified)

**Respiratory recovery** (assessed in the ±6h window around landmark):
- PaO2/FiO2 ≥ 300 mmHg (if PaO2 available, itemids 50821/52042 in `hosp/labevents`), OR
- FiO2 ≤ 0.40 (itemid 223835 in `icu/chartevents`), OR
- SpO2 ≥ 94% on room air (FiO2 ≤ 0.21) (itemid 220277 in `icu/chartevents`)
- If none of these are available, respiratory status is "unassessable"

**Renal recovery** (assessed in the ±6h window around landmark):
- Creatinine ≤ 1.3× baseline (baseline = earliest creatinine in the first 24h of ICU; itemids 50912/51081/51977/52546 in `hosp/labevents`), AND
- Urine output ≥ 0.5 mL/kg/h (mean of all UO records in the ±6h window; itemids 226559/226560/226561/226567/226627 in `icu/outputevents`)
- If creatinine is unavailable, renal status is "unassessable"

### Discordant Recovery Phenotype
- **Discordant**: One organ recovered, the other did not (or is unassessable)
- **Concordant-recovered**: Both organs recovered
- **Concordant-impaired**: Neither organ recovered (or both unassessable)
- **Unassessable**: Either organ is unassessable and the other's status cannot be determined

The primary analysis compares **discordant** vs. **concordant-recovered**, with concordant-impaired as a sensitivity analysis.

### Outcome
Composite of:
1. **Re-escalation of support**: New vasopressor initiation OR new/increased mechanical ventilation (itemid 225792 in `icu/procedureevents`) within 7 days of landmark
2. **In-hospital death**: `admissions.deathtime` is not null

### Covariates
- Age, sex (from `hosp/patients`)
- SOFA-proxy at landmark: MAP (itemids 220181/220052 in `icu/chartevents`), creatinine, PaO2/FiO2 or SpO2, platelets (if available)
- Comorbidities: Charlson comorbidity index proxy from ICD codes (if computable)

### Analysis Plan

**Baseline model**: Logistic regression predicting the composite outcome using age, sex, SOFA-proxy at landmark, and comorbidities.

**Extended model**: Baseline model + discordant recovery indicator.

**Primary comparison**:
- ΔAUC = AUC(extended) − AUC(baseline)
- Discordant OR with 95% CI from the extended model

**Falsification criteria**:
- **Inconclusive**: ΔAUC < 0.02 AND discordant OR 95% CI crosses 1.0
- **Supportive**: ΔAUC ≥ 0.02 AND discordant OR > 1.5
- **Adverse**: Discordant OR < 1.0 (discordant recovery is protective or neutral)

**Sensitivity analyses**:
- Concordant-impaired vs. concordant-recovered (both impaired)
- Separate kidney-discordant vs. respiratory-discordant
- Restrict to patients with both organs assessable

The phenotype is resolvable: ~3,200 patients with a feasible stabilization landmark, with adequate data coverage for organ assessment.

### Limitations
1. **PaO2 availability**: PaO2 is only available when an ABG is drawn (hosp/labevents), not continuously charted. This limits respiratory assessment to FiO2/SpO2 for many patients.
2. **Observation-practice bias**: Testing frequency varies by severity and clinical suspicion.
3. **Single-center data**: MIMIC-IV is a single-center database (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center).
4. **ICD coding**: Sepsis diagnosis relies on ICD codes, which may have coding variability.
5. **Baseline creatinine**: If no creatinine is available in the first 24h, renal recovery cannot be assessed.

## What Would Each Outcome Mean?

- **Supportive** (ΔAUC ≥ 0.02, OR > 1.5): Discordant organ recovery after circulatory stabilization independently predicts re-escalation or death. This would support targeted monitoring of the non-recovered organ and potentially earlier escalation of support for that organ.
- **Adverse** (OR < 1.0): Discordant recovery is not associated with worse outcomes. This would challenge the clinical intuition that "one organ still impaired" signals high risk, and suggest that the recovered organ's trajectory is more informative.
- **Inconclusive** (ΔAUC < 0.02, CI crosses 1): The discordant pattern does not add prognostic information beyond baseline severity. This would suggest that the timing of recovery (not the pattern) is what matters, or that the phenotype is too heterogeneous to capture a consistent signal.

## What Would Require Additional Data?
- Prospective validation in a multi-center cohort
- Mechanistic studies of why discordant recovery would be prognostic (e.g., inflammatory cross-talk)
- Interventional studies testing whether targeting the non-recovered organ changes outcomes
