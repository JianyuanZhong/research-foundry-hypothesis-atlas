## Interpretation, falsification, and verifier contract

Support establishes only higher fixed-denominator yield under these frozen retrospective UKB lists. It does not establish true/persistent CKD, measured filtration, that grip measures muscle mass or mechanism, specimen chronology, complete N18 capture, causal benefit from cystatin testing, actionability, safety, workflow feasibility, cost-effectiveness, fairness, or transportability. Adverse evidence argues against the prespecified incremental-yield claim, not against cystatin C generally. Inconclusive evidence leaves the claim unresolved.

Stronger conclusions require measured GFR; repeated clinically timed creatinine/cystatin/UACR; order/collection/result timestamps; outpatient/primary-care diagnoses, kidney-replacement dates and individual censoring/emigration; medications and downstream actions; chart/nephrologist adjudication; cost/workload/harms and equity review; an external cohort; and a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study with patient-centered outcomes.

The verifier must recompute source/schema/header identities, joins, O/O1/O1_hold/h/n1, transforms, A fit and coefficient order, penalties, ties, scores, memberships and all hashes; verify exact parent AG1/G1 reproduction within declared numeric tolerance and exact roster equality; compute H1/D1, shared-person bounds, fixed-denominator estimands, swaps, bootstrap seeds/draws, family limits, UACR categories, N18/death chronology, decomposition, gates and labels; and require every conclusion to cite its computed output and uncertainty.

Adversarial fixtures must reject: A defined by zeroing grip in AG rather than the separate frozen A fit; any instance-1 outcome refit; roster changes during bootstrap; observed-label denominators; independent missing assignments for overlapping eids; a lower H limit above zero but not +2 called material; unadjusted multiple child endpoints; fallback called observed A1; `<6.7` treated as 6.7; 3.0 put in A1; mismatched diagnosis/date positions; N17 counted; same-day death ignored; sparse/wide results called adverse or equivalent; N18 rescuing biochemistry/parent failure; and correct arithmetic paired with claims of sarcopenia, CKD truth, measured-GFR accuracy, causal action, safety, cost, fairness, transportability or benefit. Separate valid fixtures must reward appropriately bounded interpretations of supportive, adverse and inconclusive outputs.

The automatic verifier can establish reproducible computation and conclusion–output consistency. It cannot automate clinical adjudication, mechanism, chronology absent timestamps, true/persistent disease, complete follow-up, actionability, fairness, utility, transportability or benefit.
