# Repeated UKB biomarker history: a conditional prospective test with a bounded recorded-event estimand

Parent: [prior hypothesis]  
Status: frozen successor proposal; no scientific model has been fitted and no clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Scientific question, advance and hypothesis

Among UK Biobank participants with a verified repeat eight-field blood-biomarker panel, does the earlier panel add decision-relevant information beyond the current panel, age and sex for a first later cardiorenal/metabolic two-domain event?

The clinical decision is monitoring and risk triage after a current assessment: does retaining the previous panel change risk estimates enough to justify trajectory-aware follow-up research? A positive prognostic result would motivate an external prospective monitoring study; it would not establish that ordering a repeat test, changing treatment, or acting at a threshold improves outcomes.

The untested hypothesis is that within-person annualized change from instance 0 to instance 1 improves held-out five-year target-event prediction and decision-curve net benefit beyond current instance-1 values, age, sex and assessment timing. The strongest local evidence supports only availability of paired field-instance values, assessment-date fields, participant joins, diagnosis-code/date arrays and death-date fields. It does not establish biomarker specimen timing, analyte labels or units, diagnosis onset, complete outcome ascertainment, treatment effects, mechanism or transportability.

## Timing and ascertainment decision

The local assessment table contains 53-0.0 and 53-1.0 as date-formatted participant assessment fields. These prove the dates represented by those fields and permit an operational assessment landmark. They do not prove that a biomarker specimen was collected on either date, that instance 0 was collected at or before 53-0.0, or that instance 1 was collected at or before 53-1.0. The local metadata says UKB field-instance arrays are not elapsed time and that exact field labels, units and missing-value meanings are generally absent; every relevant schema has temporal_columns: []. Therefore 53-1.0 is not called a biomarker index without an external approved UKB field/protocol record establishing specimen-to-instance alignment.

Likewise, outcome arrays contain recorded codes and dates but no local individual observation-end date. The existence of an empty array, the maximum date in the file, or no recorded code does not prove event-free follow-up. The local snapshot therefore cannot by itself support a clinical five-year incidence estimand.

The solver must run two explicitly separated modes:

1. Prospective mode, primary only if two external gates pass. Gate A is an approved field/protocol document or linkage record establishing the ordering of biomarker collection relative to the two assessment dates and the meaning, units and validity of the eight fields. Gate B is an ascertainment certificate giving complete diagnosis/death capture through each participant's five-year horizon, or an individual observation-end date and linkage type adequate for right-censoring. If either gate fails, prospective results are not estimable and no prospective or clinical monitoring claim is allowed.

2. Recorded-event mode, the valid bounded alternative under the local files. Use T1 = assessment.53-1.0 only as an assessment landmark and define the observable outcome as a record, not disease incidence. For H_i = T1_i + 5 years, classify R_target as the first qualifying target-domain code/date record after T1 and by H; R_death as a recorded death date by H no later than the first qualifying target record; and R_none as neither qualifying record appearing by H in the supplied arrays. R_none means no qualifying record in this export, not event-free survival. This estimand is a descriptive test of whether the trajectory is associated with future recorded target information and is not evidence for monitoring, treatment, disease onset or causal effect. It uses no IPCW and no claim of complete follow-up. If the code-system/domain dictionary is unavailable, this mode also fails closed and returns a data-audit report rather than inventing disease labels.

This resolves the bottleneck scientifically: assessment dates are not promoted into specimen dates, and a useful, exactly labeled fallback is retained rather than silently discarding unknown observation.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Join the four required tables by eid; audit row uniqueness, join coverage, overlapping-value agreement and parse failures before filtering. Retain participants with all 16 biomarker cells numeric and valid under a supplied per-field dictionary, valid age and sex, valid T0 = 53-0.0 and T1 = 53-1.0 with T0 < T1, T1 <= 2013-12-31, and assessment interval L = (T1-T0)/365.2425 satisfying 0.25 < L <= 5.0 for the primary analysis. A predeclared 0.25 < L <= 10.0 sensitivity reports temporal dilution; impossible or out-of-bound intervals are excluded, not clipped.

Exclude anyone with a recorded target-domain event on or before T1, while labeling this as no recorded prior event. Under prospective mode, require specimen-to-assessment ordering in Gate A and use T1 as the index only after that gate. Under recorded-event mode, call T1 an assessment landmark and do not use the word prospective.

The eight fields are retained by field ID rather than analyte name unless a verified dictionary supplies labels, units, assay quality and missing-value meanings. Primary trajectory features are annualized changes (x1-x0)/L; raw changes are a sensitivity. The model includes L and T1 calendar year in every arm so a trajectory gain cannot be explained only by repeat spacing or calendar drift.

## Outcome construction and competing events

Before fitting, require a supplied dictionary establishing the code system and a prespecified mapping to metabolic (E10-E14), renal (N18-N19) and cardiovascular (I20-I25, I50) domains, if and only if these prefixes are confirmed as the intended code system. Normalize only by trimming whitespace and uppercasing; do not silently map local codes.

For each participant, pair 41270-0.i with 41280-0.i for i=0,...,29. The target date E is the earliest date strictly after T1 on which two distinct target domains have valid paired codes; codes on the same date count together. D is the earliest valid date from 40000-0.0/1.0 after applying the supplied death-field dictionary.

In prospective mode, E and D define mutually exclusive competing risks: target if E < D and E <= H; death if D <= H and D <= E, with same-day ties assigned to death in the primary analysis; otherwise a record is event-free only when ascertainment is certified complete through H. If observation ends earlier, censor at the certified individual end date. Death enters the cause-specific hazard and cumulative-incidence function, not an IPCW weight. IPCW, if needed, handles right censoring only and is estimated within training folds. The primary outcome is held-out five-year target cumulative-incidence Brier score, with death as the competing event.

In recorded-event mode, use the three observable categories R_target, R_death and R_none defined above. There is no event-free or censored claim and no IPCW. Report target-record probability with a multiclass Brier score and calibration; report the death-record category separately. All interpretations must use the word recorded and must not be translated into incidence or prognosis.

## Matched models and scientific alternatives

The scientific comparison is incremental history, not a model-complexity contest. Use the same deterministic participant-level five-fold split, outcome construction, horizon and fold-local preprocessing in all arms.

- M0 current-only baseline: eight instance-1 fields, age, sex, T1 calendar year and L. Prospective mode uses a common discrete-time cause-specific hazard family; recorded-event mode uses a common regularized multinomial or one-vs-rest probability family.
- M1 interpretable trajectory: M0 plus eight annualized slopes, fit with the same family and fold-local scaling/ridge selection. Raw-change M1 is a prespecified sensitivity. The primary scientific contrast is M1 versus M0.
- M2 learned alternative: M1 inputs plus prespecified current-by-slope interactions and nonlinear terms for current level, slope and L, fit with fold-local gradient-boosted discrete-time cause-specific hazards in prospective mode or a fold-local gradient-boosted multiclass model in recorded-event mode. Keep grouped current, slope, timing and interaction summaries. M2 can reveal thresholding, discordant current-versus-slope patterns and heterogeneity that an additive trajectory loses; it is secondary and cannot be retained for a tiny discrimination gain alone.

M0, M1 and M2 have matched participant splits, targets, horizons and uncertainty procedures. All imputation, scaling, feature selection and tuning are inside training folds. No outcome, death, post-landmark field or future biomarker enters exposure construction. A signed mechanistic deterioration score is retained as a deferred alternative, not smuggled into the primary analysis: without verified analyte units, assay quality, specimen dates and more than two observations, its direction and biological interpretation would be invented.

## Estimands, uncertainty and falsification

Prospective mode reports held-out five-year target CIF Brier contrast DeltaBS5 = BS5(M1)-BS5(M0), net-benefit contrasts at 0.05 and 0.10, calibration intercept/slope, time-dependent discrimination, endpoint and competing-death counts, joint slope-block inference and M2-versus-M1 contrasts. Use participant-clustered bootstrap intervals over held-out predictions while preserving folds; estimate censoring weights inside folds. Thresholds are analysis thresholds, not validated action thresholds.

Recorded-event mode reports target-record Brier contrast, multiclass Brier decomposition, calibration, discrimination, category counts and M2-versus-M1 contrasts, with participant bootstrap intervals. It does not report CIF, incidence, censoring-adjusted risk or decision thresholds as clinical actions.

Falsification and integrity controls are deterministic participant-level split audit; no post-index leakage; age/sex-stratified permutation of slope vectors while retaining current values, timing, outcomes and missingness; permutation of outcome labels/dates; raw-change, interval-bound, same-day-tie and domain-definition sensitivities; complete-case versus prespecified missingness handling; checks that breaking code/date positions or reassigning outcome dates cannot improve the primary contrast; and calibration/fold-instability checks, not discrimination alone.

Prospective support requires both gates, at least 100 target events, no integrity failure, DeltaBS5 95% upper bound below zero, positive net benefit at both analysis thresholds without material calibration degradation, and compatible raw-change, tie and domain sensitivities. Adverse evidence is near-null/worse M1, an interval excluding any prespecified decision-relevant gain, no net-benefit gain, or disappearance under permutation/integrity controls; this favors current values alone for the tested prognostic task. Inconclusive evidence includes a failed gate, fewer than 100 target events, inadequate certified follow-up, intervals spanning useful gain and harm, fold instability, or endpoint/missingness sensitivity changing the conclusion.

Recorded-event support means only that M1 improves prediction of a future recorded target category under the supplied export and passes falsification controls. Recorded-event adverse evidence favors M0 for that data-record task; inconclusive evidence includes unavailable code semantics, sparse categories, unstable folds or conflicting handling. Neither mode supports causal treatment, mechanism, threshold, monitoring benefit, disease-free status or transportability. Those claims require clinical adjudication, specimen-level timing and quality, complete linkage metadata, treatment data, external validation and/or a prospective intervention.

## Alternatives, demonstrations and expert-seed dispositions

The closest expert seed is UKB-09, metabolic deterioration and multimorbidity. It is retained as provenance and adapted to the exact eight-field repeated panel, matched M0/M1/M2 comparison and stricter timing/ascertainment gates; it remains an untested seed, not evidence. UKB-04, fat distribution, is deferred because the catalog has no raw imaging bundle and its reduced adaptation does not improve this single monitoring estimand. UKB-01/02/06 require verified proteomics and/or infection staging or time-varying hospitalization; UKB-03 requires verified kidney-function semantics, serial creatinine/AKI adjudication and treatment context; UKB-05 requires accelerometry/sleep regularity and shift-work timing; UKB-07 requires reliable CHIP variant/clone data; UKB-08 requires cancer staging/treatment context and a diagnosis landmark; UKB-10 requires a selected disease, verified PRS and independent validation. These branches are deferred rather than declared false.

The three demonstrations are not reproduced. Delphi's learned dated disease-history idea motivates retaining M2, but two biomarker instances and no verified dated disease-history input cannot reproduce it. ALADYNOULLI's longitudinal latent model is not reproduced because verified genetic inputs and richer longitudinal structure are unavailable; its nonlinear trajectory logic is bounded here only as M2 and must earn value against M0/M1. Oncoformer's main article and full STAR Methods were unavailable in the local bundle, its imaging modality is unavailable, and only the supplement was inspected; no main-paper or multimodal claim is made. The demonstrations are methodological context, not support for this hypothesis.
