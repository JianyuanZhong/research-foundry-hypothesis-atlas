# Provisional rival-focused hypothesis (D2-T1)

Status: provisional and evidence-bound; no inferential result has been computed.

## Proposed relationship

The intervention-associated FLS transcript response is not simply a macrophage-context effect: it is a donor-aware interaction between the intervention and macrophage context. The strongest rival is that apparent context dependence is generated by donor composition or by the fact that the same donors/conditions are reused across interventions, rather than by a reproducible biological interaction.

## Evidence currently supporting the opening

The configured assay registry identifies E-MTAB-8316 as a 12-condition, 58,302-feature assay with four verified FLS donor clusters (SA049, SA051, SA104, SA114) and six nested monocyte donors; its limits explicitly require averaging repeated context measurements within donor/condition and prohibit treating the 12 samples as independent donors. E-MTAB-8873 is a separate 33-sample, 445-feature targeted FLS/coculture assay with no verified single-donor IDs and explicit dependence limits. These are design facts from the local assay registry, not evidence that an interaction exists.

## Unresolved claim and falsifiable test

Test whether intervention-by-macrophage-context interaction remains after donor-aware adjustment, using E-MTAB-8316 as the bounded calibration/control question and E-MTAB-8873 only as macrophage-context calibration unless its donor mapping is independently verified. The primary contrast is the difference in intervention response between macrophage contexts, estimated at the donor level (or a prespecified donor-cluster level if donor identity cannot be recovered), with uncertainty propagated for repeated conditions and nested monocyte donors. A simple additive donor-aware model is the baseline; the interaction model is the substantive alternative. Support requires a reproducible interaction with uncertainty excluding the null and stability across donors; adverse evidence is a robust null/reversal; inconclusive evidence includes failed donor/contrast verification, insufficient independent donors, or wide uncertainty.

## Exact data and missing evidence

Required E-MTAB-8316 inputs are the read-count matrix and SDRF/design metadata joined by the documented sample key, with intervention, macrophage/context, FLS donor, monocyte donor, and technical fields. Required E-MTAB-8873 inputs are its SDRF/design metadata and the targeted expression matrices, with sample-to-donor and condition mappings explicitly verified. The exact source paths, columns, joins, and assay-registry contrasts still require direct inspection; no result is claimed here. E-MTAB-8316 cannot establish direct target action, clinical efficacy, or a mechanism, and E-MTAB-8873 cannot be used to join donors merely by matching IDs across assays.

## Alternatives and limits

The donor-aware additive model is the simplest adequate baseline. The interaction model adds a scientifically meaningful test of whether intervention effects change with macrophage context. A more complex learned model is deferred unless it reveals information the interaction model loses (for example nonlinear context effects), and no such need is currently justified. Missing donor-level replicate structure, protocol reconciliation, direct target measurements, clinical outcomes, and independent technical replicates limit interpretation.
