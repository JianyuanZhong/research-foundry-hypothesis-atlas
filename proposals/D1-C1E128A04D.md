# Candidate Proposal (EP3 repair) — Pre-operative AFP and Liver-Function Reserve Predict Early Treatment Re-escalation After Curative Hepatectomy for HCC

**Island:** HCC (post-hepatectomy natural history) · **Episode:** 3 (repair of ep1 [prior hypothesis]) · **Status:** deliverable, data-bound, discovery-phase (not yet fitted)

---

## 1. Unresolved claim and clinical consequence

**Unresolved claim.** After curative-intent hepatectomy for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), the **pre-operative laboratory profile** — specifically the **pre-operative AFP level** and **liver-function reserve** (albumin, total bilirubin, prothrombin time, platelet count, A/G ratio, hyaluronic acid) — **independently predicts the time to early treatment re-escalation** (first subsequent TACE or systemic therapy, a *surrogate* for relapse/progression) **beyond a baseline model using only static clinicopathologic features** (age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior locoregional therapy).

**Data-driven refinement.** A bounded feasibility probe (job-7893f63a, ep1) shows a pre-operative AFP *slope/trajectory* is computable for only ~4.5% of the cohort (1,160 patients with ≥2 pre-op AFP points). The primary exposure is therefore the **pre-operative AFP LEVEL** (latest pre-op value, 91.5% coverage) plus liver-function reserve (95.6% coverage). AFP trajectory is retained as a pre-specified **sensitivity analysis** on the dense subcohort (hypothesis-generating only).

**Clinical consequence.** After curative-intent resection, 20–70% of HCC patients recur within 2 years. Identifying patients who will need re-escalation early informs surveillance intensity and the timing of adjuvant/systemic-therapy decisions. This is a consequential, falsifiable natural-history question.

## 2. Evidence base and the gap this proposal fills

Three closest inspected works (full-text, frozen receipts attached):

- **[K1] Hsu et al. 2026** (J Hepatocell Carcinoma, Dovepress; PMC via Europe PMC). Multicenter ML study, 1,681 HCC patients curative hepatectomy (Chang Gung 2007–2020) + external validation n=251 (Mackay Memorial). XGBoost external C-index **0.72**. Five pivotal predictors (Cox + SHAP): **sex, preoperative treatment, tumor size, satellite lesions, vascular invasion** — **AFP is NOT among them**, and no liver-function reserve variable is in the model. *Gap: AFP and liver reserve are excluded from the closest high-discrimination model.*
- **[K2] Bui et al. 2026** (Dovepress CMAR; 295 patients, Viet Duc 2015–2024). Multivariate Cox: independent predictors of DFS = TNM stage (HR 2.562), microvascular invasion (HR 1.773), R1 margin (HR 2.254), **Child-Pugh B (HR 2.319, 95%CI 1.234–4.356)**. Nomogram C-index **0.64**. Child-Pugh (liver reserve) is an independent predictor but the model is small, single-center, and **AFP is not an independent predictor in this cohort**. *Gap: liver reserve matters but AFP's independent contribution beyond stage is unresolved; small sample.*
- **[K3] Neves & Soares 2026** (Cancers 18(13):2028; systematic review, 36 studies / 14,716 patients). 91.7% China, 75% single-center, 97.2% retrospective, **imaging-centric** (MRI 41.7%, CT 30.6%), rarely externally validated. *Gap: the field is imaging-dominated; non-imaging (lab-based) models are few and under-validated — a lab-based, data-bound test on a large single-center cohort is the missing piece.*

**Substantive advance.** This proposal tests, on a 26,778-patient hepatectomy cohort with 91.5% AFP and 95.6% liver-panel coverage, whether the pre-op lab profile (AFP level + liver reserve) adds independent prognostic information for early re-escalation beyond static features — directly addressing the gap that the closest high-discrimination model (K1) excluded AFP and the field (K3) is imaging-centric.

## 3. Estimand, population, and data bindings

- **Population:** patients with a curative-intent hepatectomy for HCC (n≈26,778 with valid index time; 25,933 in the feasibility probe).
- **Index time:** hepatectomy procedure start time (procedures.开始时间 where 手术 matches hepatectomy strings).
- **Outcome (primary):** time from hepatectomy to first subsequent TACE or systemic therapy (re-escalation, a relapse surrogate). Censor at last encounter / loss to follow-up.
- **Exposure:** pre-op AFP level (latest pre-op 检验 row, 0–90d window) + liver-function reserve (albumin, tbili, PT, PLT, A/G, HA).
- **Baseline covariates:** age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size (examinations), prior locoregional therapy.
- **Data bindings (exact files/columns/join keys/time fields):**
  - encounters: `HCC/data_基本信息_2500296761891079109.csv` — 患者主索引, 就诊号, 年龄, 性别, 就诊时间, 入院时间, 出院时间, 就诊科室.
  - procedures: `HCC/data_手术_8024330590283626027.csv` — 患者主索引, 就诊号, 手术, 开始时间, 结束时间 (hepatectomy index + TACE/systemic re-escalation events).
  - labs: `HCC/data_检验_609065997844652188.csv` — 患者主索引, 就诊号, 检验, 定性结果, 定量结果, 标本类型, 检验时间 (AFP, albumin, tbili, PT, PLT, A/G, HA).
  - diagnoses: `HCC/data_诊断_7504718184492840569.csv` — 患者主索引, 就诊号, 诊断名称 (肝细胞癌, CNLC staging).
  - examinations: `HCC/data_检查_4203595081195465282.csv` — 患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查, 检查所见, 检查诊断, 开始时间 (tumor size).
  - Join key: (患者主索引, 就诊号). Time fields: 检验时间 (labs), 开始时间 (procedures/exams), 就诊时间/入院时间/出院时间 (encounters).

## 4. Baseline vs learned comparison

- **Baseline:** Cox proportional hazards with static features only (age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior locoregional therapy).
- **Learned alternative:** Cox + gradient-boosting survival (XGBoost-style) using the biomarker profile (AFP level + liver reserve) on top of static features.
- **Comparison metric:** C-index (discrimination) + calibration; interpret AFP coefficient/SHAP.
- **What the learned model reveals that the baseline loses:** non-linear AFP effects and AFP×liver-reserve interactions that a linear Cox baseline cannot capture.

## 5. Falsification (pre-specified)

- **Supportive:** AFP level + liver reserve significantly improve C-index beyond the static baseline (ΔC-index > 0.02, p<0.05) with stable calibration; AFP coefficient positive and significant.
- **Adverse:** no C-index improvement (ΔC-index ≤ 0.02) or AFP coefficient non-significant after stage adjustment → AFP/liver reserve add no independent prognostic value beyond static features.
- **Inconclusive:** C-index improvement present but calibration poor / external generalizability untestable (single-center) → hypothesis-generating only.

## 6. Computable vs adjudication-required claims

- **Computationally checkable:** C-index, calibration, AFP coefficient, ΔC-index vs baseline, sensitivity on dense subcohort.
- **Requires clinical adjudication / another study:** whether re-escalation truly reflects relapse (surrogate validity), external validation, and any causal/mechanistic interpretation. An association surviving adjustment does not identify a mechanism.

## 7. Limitations (honest)

- Re-escalation is a surrogate for relapse, not a hard recurrence endpoint (recurrence diagnosis strings sparse ~1.5k; death sparse 288).
- Single-center; no external validation; no lab units (cross-assay pooling unsupported); right-censoring (recent patients censored early).
