# Calibrated observation-capture and support audit for frozen P-beyond-B repeat-TACE HCC validation

## Goal and targeted repair

This is a compatible successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not change the frozen population, event reconstruction, assay triplets, forward blocks, model comparison, or primary estimand. The targeted repair is to make the observation-selection conclusion testable rather than relying on an uncalibrated observation Brier score and a single observed fraction. It adds (i) a leakage-proof, temporally cross-fitted calibration audit for the probability that `Y` is observed, (ii) a decomposition of missing `Y` into lack of any post-event laboratory capture versus assay-specific absence, and (iii) a prespecified, bounded missing-outcome sensitivity analysis reported separately from the complete-triplet estimand. Calendar support/overlap diagnostics remain outcome-blind and are retained unchanged.

## Clinical question and evidence boundary

The clinically relevant unresolved question is whether an apparent incremental short-horizon prognostic signal from contemporaneous assay `P`, beyond remote assay `B` and a locked decision-time core, is reproducible in later calendar mixtures rather than being an artifact of sparse systemic-arm support or selective observation of `Y`. The predecessor's strongest supported claim is narrower: the adequately supported 2022 block showed held-out improvement for the selected complete-triplet prediction task. It does not establish multi-era transport, treatment benefit, TACE response, or actionability.

The successor tests whether later complete-triplet results remain interpretable after distinguishing measured pre-`Y` covariate extrapolation from observation/capture selection. A favorable complete-case result with poor calibration, severe capture loss, or large bounded sensitivity range is not transport evidence. All conclusions remain prognostic and conditional on repeat-TACE and availability of the relevant records.

## Frozen event and triplet estimand

Retain exact clocks: `B` is the latest valid uncensored assay on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1; `P` is the latest valid assay in the selected repeat encounter in `[event_time−72 hours,event_time)`; `Y` is the valid assay in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, nearest +24 hours with deterministic ties. Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`, positive P lead, one patient-assay row, and no lab-informed event selection.

Use fixed origins `2021-01-01`, `2022-01-01`, `2023-01-01`, `2024-01-01`; train on `event_date < origin` and test on `[origin, origin+365 days)`, with non-overlapping future blocks. Keep the locked nested ridge models exactly:

```
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
```

Preserve training-only age median imputation and missingness indicator, sex coding, variance filtering, centering/scaling, inner leave-one-patient-out alpha selection over `[0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100]`, fixed pre-origin fits, paired arm-stratified patient-frequency bootstrap intervals, and standardized held-out RMSE gain `[RMSE(M0)-RMSE(M1)]/SD_test(Y)`. MAE, bias, arm-specific errors, drift, and influence remain secondary. No new diagnostic changes the primary rows, outcome, fitted model, or estimand.

## Outcome-blind support and overlap, retained and made explicit

For every selected repeat event and assay, construct the funnel before reading or selecting `Y`:

1. selected repeat event;
2. `has_B` under the frozen B clock;
3. `has_P` under the frozen P clock;
4. `BP_eligible`: `has_B`, `has_P`, strict clock ordering, positive P lead, and no future outcome information;
5. only afterward, `Y_observed` and `complete`.

Report overall and systemic/comparator counts in each train/test block. Keep the parent thresholds: at least 20 BP-eligible episodes total and at least 10 in each descriptive arm in both training and future test; the complete-triplet gate is separate. A failed gate makes a block descriptive regardless of its point estimate or interval.

Among BP-eligible episodes, use only pre-`Y` values `B`, `P`, age, sex, P-lead hours, TACE2-to-repeat days, and the prespecified systemic/comparator label for calendar-membership overlap. Report future propensity range versus training, future outside-range fraction, normalized transport ESS (overall and by arm), maximum normalized weight, and same-arm nearest-neighbor distances using the training 95th-percentile leave-one-training-neighbor threshold. These quantities are diagnostics only; do not weight the primary RMSE.

## New observation calibration and capture decomposition

### Temporally honest calibration

Define `R=1` when valid `Y` is observed under the frozen Y window, among BP-eligible episodes. Fit the observation model using only pre-`Y` variables listed above. For each origin, fit on the pre-origin BP-eligible training episodes and generate out-of-fold predictions within training using chronological, non-overlapping folds (at least two folds only when each fold has both R states; otherwise report calibration as unavailable). Apply the final training fit to future BP-eligible episodes. Never use `Y`, post-event values, or future test outcomes as predictors or tuning inputs.

In addition to the parent Brier score and mean observed fraction, report calibration intercept and slope from a logistic recalibration of `R` on logit predicted probability, calibration-in-the-large (mean predicted minus mean observed), decile reliability tables when cell sizes permit, and a binomial calibration test only as descriptive. Report all metrics overall and by arm where estimable. Use a prespecified warning when the 95% interval for slope excludes 1, the interval for intercept excludes 0, absolute calibration-in-the-large is at least 0.10, or any arm has fewer than 10 observed or missing cases. Do not interpret a failed calibration test as proof of missing-not-at-random; it identifies unreliable observation probabilities.

### Capture-opportunity decomposition

For each BP-eligible event, separately record:

* `post_contact`: at least one same-patient encounter with valid `就诊时间`, `入院时间`, or `出院时间` falling in or overlapping `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, using a fixed documented precedence for the three encounter clocks;
* `any_lab_capture`: at least one valid, numeric, timestamped laboratory row of any assay in the same Y window;
* `assay_Y`: the frozen exact-assay `Y` indicator.

The event's own linked encounter is not counted merely because it contains the repeat procedure; only post-event times/overlap qualify. Report the nested funnel `BP-eligible -> post_contact -> any_lab_capture -> assay_Y`, overall and by arm/calendar block. This is a capture diagnostic, not a substitute outcome and not evidence that missingness is ignorable. If the source's broad laboratory rows cannot be safely parsed for `any_lab_capture`, record that component as unavailable rather than silently treating assay absence as no contact.

### Bounded missing-outcome sensitivity (secondary only)

Do not impute `Y` or replace the frozen complete-triplet analysis. As a transparent stress test, among future BP-eligible episodes with missing `Y`, define a standardized latent-outcome shift parameter `delta` in `{-1.0,-0.5,0,+0.5,+1.0}` SD of the assay-specific training outcome: assign missing `Y` only for this sensitivity calculation as the corresponding M0 prediction plus `delta*SD_train(Y)` and recompute M0/M1 RMSE gain on the enlarged BP-eligible set. Use the same fixed pre-origin model fits and report the range, not a single preferred value. This is a pattern-mixture-style diagnostic, not an estimate of the frozen estimand and not a claim about the true missing outcomes. If missing Y is concentrated in one arm, additionally report arm-specific ranges using the same delta grid; do not pool or suppress arm differences.

## Falsification and interpretation branches

* **Supportive:** reconstruction/hash/clock checks pass; the block passes outcome-blind BP and complete-triplet arm gates; overlap has no material warning; observation model is acceptably calibrated; capture funnel does not collapse; and the locked M1 gain is positive with a bootstrap interval excluding zero, with no influential single patient. This supports incremental prediction only in that selected, observed, calendar block. It does not support treatment benefit or actionability.
* **Sparse-arm falsification:** any systemic or comparator BP-eligible arm falls below 10, or any arm-specific ESS is below 10, or excessive same-arm extrapolation occurs. The block is descriptive even if RMSE improves; this falsifies a claim of adequately supported transport, not the local signal.
* **Observation-selection falsification:** Y observation below 80%, clearly below the pre-origin rate by Wilson intervals, observation ESS below 10, calibration warning, capture funnel collapse, or pre-Y observed-versus-missing SMD absolute value at least 0.5. A favorable complete-case gain then cannot be interpreted as transport; it does not prove the signal is absent.
* **Missingness-sensitivity falsification:** the bounded delta grid crosses zero or changes the direction/materiality of the gain. The conclusion is sensitive to unobserved outcomes and therefore inconclusive, not positive or negative.
* **Adverse prediction:** in a gate-passing block, the M1 interval is at or below zero, or degradation repeats across adequately supported blocks. This challenges incremental short-horizon prediction in the selected population, not the sufficiency of B or any mechanism.
* **Inconclusive/data failure:** denominator or clock drift, invalid timestamps, assay-unit ambiguity, unavailable calibration cells, post-event capture fields not parseable, influential observations, or missing clinical variables. These require rerun, expert review, or independent data rather than favorable interpretation.

The computation can verify hashes, full scans, joins, literal filters, denominators, clocks, support/overlap, observation calibration, capture funnels, sensitivity ranges, model fitting, predictions, uncertainty, and influence. It cannot establish laboratory units/reference ranges, tumor burden or imaging response, clinical severity, repeat-TACE rationale, medication administration, external-care capture, mortality, causal effects, utility, or patient benefit. Those claims require validated assay metadata, clinical adjudication, and independent temporal/external or prospective validation.
