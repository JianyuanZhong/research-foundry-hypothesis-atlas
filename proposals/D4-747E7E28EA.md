# Direction-A schedule-marginal prediction with an explicit repeat-testing decision-value stop

## Lineage and purpose

This proposal is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that candidate's exact baseline-only observed-repeat cohort, source snapshot, equations, models, repeated nested cross-fitting, and paired performance estimands. It repairs two unresolved limitations rather than expanding the science schema:

1. It makes field/assay provenance a machine-checkable certification ledger. It does not manufacture labels, units, missing-value meanings, or assay validation that are unavailable in the local UKB metadata. The protocol therefore has an explicit **neutral-stop** state: numeric computations may be reproducible as transformations of encoded fields, but no biomarker or clinical interpretation may be declared when semantic certification is unavailable.
2. It separates a schedule-marginal prediction question from repeat-testing decision value. The fixed-capacity top-10% contrast is explicitly an event-concentration/scheduling estimand among observed repeat attenders, not clinical net benefit. Any claim that cystatin C should change whether or when a person is retested is stopped because repeat nonattendance, testing harms/costs, action consequences, and counterfactual outcomes are not observed.

The resulting falsifiable question is: **within the selected UKB participants who have a qualifying observed first repeat with complete encoded creatinine and cystatin-C fields, does baseline cystatin-C threshold discordance add reproducible out-of-sample information about the encoded repeat creatinine-threshold outcome beyond a flexible baseline-only creatinine, age, sex, and observed-repeat-interval comparator, and does that information improve event concentration at a prespecified scheduling capacity?**

This is not a causal hypothesis. It does not test CKD, chronicity, measured GFR, renal specificity, assay validity, treatment, referral, testing benefit, or transportability.

## What is already supported and what remains untested

The inherited evidence supports only a narrow model-standardized association in the selected Direction-A cohort: baseline `eGFRcys_0 < 60` was associated with repeat `eGFRcr_1 < 60` after the prior fixed linear adjustment. The inherited audit anchors are `B=468,887`, `R=16,546`, `T_A=461,530`, `S_A=16,372`, 309 observed outcomes, 348 exposed participants, and Direction-A cells `(E0Y0,E0Y1,E1Y0,E1Y1)=(15,769,255,294,54)`. These are integrity targets, never values to force.

The untested claim is narrower than clinical usefulness and stronger than association: after a flexible comparator and leakage-safe out-of-sample prediction, the binary baseline cystatin-derived discordance indicator improves paired absolute-risk accuracy and/or fixed-capacity concentration of the observed repeat outcome. The protocol tests this only as a computational property of the frozen encoded-field transformation in this attendance-selected cohort.

## Certification ledger and neutral-stop state

Create an internal, non-published certification record with one row per required field. The record contains source path, source hash, schema hash, table, exact column name, identity key, instance/array token, observed raw type, finite/nonfinite counts, distinct-value summary, and certification status. It must not publish `eid`, raw values, participant predictions, or hashes derived from participants.

Certification is deliberately layered:

- **Infrastructure-certified** means exact source/hash/schema, exact column presence, one-to-one `eid`, successful join, and no conflicting overlap. Failure is a hard infrastructure stop.
- **Coding-certified** means a frozen public coding is available. Only `31-0.0` is coding-certified here: metadata records field 31 as sex, coding 9, with `0=Female` and `1=Male`. No global negative-code recoding is allowed.
- **Temporal-operational** means `53-0.0` and `53-1.0` are parsed as calendar dates only after successful date parsing. The catalog says field-instance/array tokens are not elapsed time and exact dates remain server-only; therefore the protocol records that dates are operationally parsed values, not a claim that instance index is time. Non-parseable dates fail eligibility; they are not imputed.
- **Assay-semantic-certified** would require a frozen field label, unit, missing-value dictionary, and assay/provenance statement for each of `30700-0.0/1.0` and `30720-0.0/1.0`. The available `datasets/ukb/metadata.json` explicitly says per-field labels, units, and missing-value meanings are absent locally. Consequently these four fields receive status `SEMANTIC_UNVERIFIED`, not a guessed unit or assay certificate.

The equations below are a prespecified **protocol transform of numeric encoded columns**. The transform may be run for computational reproducibility if infrastructure and value checks pass, but the output is named `encoded-field eGFRcr/eGFRcys` in all internal artifacts until an external steward supplies the missing field certificate. A value check is limited to finite, positive numeric inputs required by the formula and a logged range audit; it cannot establish units, assay calibration, biological validity, or chronicity. Negative values and other nonfinite values are excluded by the predeclared eligibility rule, never silently recoded.

Define two separate verdict bits:

- `COMPUTATION_STATUS`: PASS only if all infrastructure, join, mask, fold, fit, and numerical checks pass; otherwise FAIL.
- `SEMANTIC_STATUS`: CERTIFIED only if the required field certificate exists; in this snapshot it is `UNVERIFIED`.

If `COMPUTATION_STATUS=FAIL`, stop all outputs. If computation passes but `SEMANTIC_STATUS=UNVERIFIED`, enter **NEUTRAL-STOP**: report aggregate computational quantities only as encoded-field/schedule-marginal diagnostics; do not issue a supportive or adverse biomarker conclusion, do not call the output creatinine or cystatin C measurement evidence, and do not recommend repeat testing. A later steward certificate can release a separately versioned interpretation, but cannot change the frozen computation. If semantic metadata is supplied with conflicting field names, units, missing codes, or assay identity, stop and do not reconcile manually.

## Population, time rule, and exact outcome

Reconstruct the inherited masks jointly, with no relaxation:

1. `B` requires joined intersection, sex `31-0.0` in `{0,1}`, parseable baseline date `53-0.0`, and finite positive baseline age `21003-0.0`, encoded creatinine `30700-0.0`, and encoded cystatin C `30720-0.0`.
2. A qualifying repeat is the observed instance 1 with finite positive repeat age `21003-1.0`, encoded repeat creatinine `30700-1.0`, encoded repeat cystatin C `30720-1.0`, parseable repeat date `53-1.0`, and `t=(date_1-date_0)/365.25` in the inclusive interval `[2,8]`. `R` is applied jointly with `B`.
3. Direction-A target is `T_A=B intersect {eGFRcr_0 >= 60}` and analysis cohort is `S_A=T_A intersect R`.
4. Exposure is `E=1{eGFRcys_0 < 60}`. Outcome is `Y=1{eGFRcr_1 < 60}`. Repeat cystatin C is used only for inherited complete-repeat-biomarker eligibility; it is not a model feature or outcome.

Convert the numeric encoded creatinine by the protocol division `Scr=30700/88.4` and use the 2021 race-free creatinine formula

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/kappa,1)^alpha * max(Scr/kappa,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^age * female_factor`,

with `(kappa,alpha,female_factor)=(0.7,-0.241,1.012)` for sex 0 and `(0.9,-0.302,1)` for sex 1. Use the 2012 cystatin-C-only formula

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^age * female_factor`,

with female factor `0.932` for sex 0 and `1` for sex 1. Use baseline age for baseline values and repeat age for `eGFRcr_1`; use unrounded values. These are computational definitions, not proof that local fields have the assumed units.

## Models and exact leakage boundary

Use precisely the parent algorithms. Both predict `Y` for a participant in `S_A`, without class weights. The baseline-only feature set is `X0=(eGFRcr_0, age_0, sex, t)`, where `t` is included because the estimand is conditional on the observed repeat schedule and is not available at original baseline. State this prominently: the design estimates schedule-marginal performance among people whose qualifying repeat was observed, not deployable baseline triage.

`M0` is ridge logistic regression with unpenalized intercept, sex, five-knot restricted cubic spline of `eGFRcr_0` (knots training quantiles 0.05, 0.275, 0.50, 0.725, 0.95; four basis columns), four-knot RCS of age and `t` (quantiles 0.05, 0.35, 0.65, 0.95; three columns each), all 12 products of the four eGFR basis columns and three time basis columns, and four sex-by-eGFR basis products. Use the standard restricted-cubic truncated-power basis with first/last boundary knots. Estimate knots and center/scale every nonbinary design column only in the applicable training partition; apply unchanged elsewhere. Zero variance or coincident knots fail that fit.

`M1` contains all M0 terms plus exactly `E` and `E*(eGFRcr_0-60)/10`. No continuous cystatin value, repeat biomarker, feature selection, alternative threshold, or outcome-guided transformation is allowed. Penalize every non-intercept coefficient equally. Tune each model independently over lambda `10^(-4),10^(-3.5),...,10^4` by inner-fold unweighted Bernoulli log loss, selecting the largest value within one standard error of the minimum; ties choose larger lambda. Repeat all preprocessing inside inner folds.

Use ten repeated outer five-fold cross-fitting seeds `[260911,260912,260913,260914,260915,260916,260917,260918,260919,260920]`. Within each repeat, assign a seeded permutation within each `E x Y` cell round-robin to five folds. Inner seed is `outer_seed+1000+outer_fold` with the same stratification. Use `Y` only for fold stratification. Make folds before fitting and demonstrate zero `eid` overlap between every outer test fold and all training/tuning rows contributing to its prediction. Keep ten out-of-fold probabilities per participant/model and average them across repeats. Clip only for log loss at `[10^-6,1-10^-6]`; report clipping counts; do not clip Brier, calibration, or ranking probabilities.

No repeat value except the inherited completeness indicator may enter a feature. The observed `t` is not a baseline feature in a clinical deployment; it is a conditioning variable that defines schedule-marginal prediction after a repeat interval has been observed. This distinction is an estimand boundary, not a claim that an operational scheduling policy has been validated.

## Primary estimands: schedule-marginal, not decision utility

Let `p0_i` and `p1_i` be each participant's arithmetic mean of ten out-of-fold predictions, and let `N=|S_A|`. Primary 1 is paired Brier improvement

`Delta_Brier = 1000*N^(-1) * sum_i [(Y_i-p0_i)^2-(Y_i-p1_i)^2]`.

Positive values mean lower squared error for M1 per 1,000 selected observed repeat attenders. Use a paired participant bootstrap interval and null-centered two-sided bootstrap p-value.

Primary 2 is a fixed-capacity scheduling contrast, not decision value. Set `K=ceil(0.10*N)` (expected 1,638). For each model rank by averaged cross-fitted probability, resolving exact ties by ascending SHA-256 of `"direction-A-capacity-v1|"+eid` locally. Define

`Delta_yield10 = 1000*(sum_{i in TopK_1}Y_i - sum_{i in TopK_0}Y_i)/K`.

Report event counts, capture fractions, predicted-risk summaries, number promoted/displaced, and outcomes among promoted/displaced in aggregate only. Interpret it as the additional observed encoded-field threshold events concentrated in a fixed number of repeat slots under two hypothetical ranking lists. It is not an estimate of benefit from ordering cystatin C, net benefit, cost-effectiveness, avoided tests, harm, or causal effect.

Use a fixed sequence: test `Delta_Brier` first; only if it passes test `Delta_yield10`. Each uses a two-sided 95% interval and plus-one symmetric null-centered bootstrap p-value at 0.05. AUC and paired log-loss are descriptive only. The incremental schedule-marginal claim requires both primary steps and all gates.

For transparency, explicitly label the decision-value estimand as **not identified** in these data. A clinical repeat-testing decision value would require potential outcomes under offering/not offering/repeating at each schedule, attendance probabilities under each offer, testing cost/burden/harms, downstream actions, and clinically adjudicated outcomes. None is available in the three bound tables. Do not compute decision curves by assigning arbitrary harms or treating observed attendance as randomized. The protocol's decision-facing output is therefore only a resource-constrained schedule-ranking diagnostic.

## Gates, calibration, uncertainty, and falsification

Before any positive schedule-marginal interpretation, require:

- exact hashes, schemas, one-to-one joins, masks, anchors, and `SEMANTIC_STATUS` recorded;
- all four `E x Y` cells in every outer training/test and inner training partition, at least 50 outcomes in each outer test fold, and at least 8 `E=1,Y=1` in each outer test fold;
- finite converged fits/probabilities and fewer than 1% log-loss clipping;
- for each model, observed/expected event ratio in `[0.90,1.10]` and recalibration slope from `Y~1+logit(p)` in `[0.80,1.20]`, with bootstrap intervals reported; M0 Brier better than prevalence-only predictor;
- positive repeat-specific `Delta_Brier` in at least 8/10 repeats and, if the second step is reached, positive repeat-specific `Delta_yield10` in at least 8/10;
- at least 2,000 successful paired participant-bootstrap replicates, maximum 2,500 attempts, seed 260921, with failed attempts and reasons logged.

Use the parent bootstrap exactly: resample unique participants with replacement, carrying `Y`, exposure, covariates, and all ten paired prediction columns; average predictions within participant; recompute both primary contrasts and calibration. For capacity, rank unique sampled participants with the fixed local tie hash, use multiplicity weights, and fractionally include only the boundary participant so capacity is exactly `ceil(0.10*sum weights)`. Use percentile 95% intervals and

`p=(1+# {b: |Delta_b-Delta_hat| >= |Delta_hat|})/(B+1)`.

Report ten equal-count calibration bins with aggregate N, events, mean prediction, and observed risk; suppress bins below 20 people. Do not publish participant-level predictions, folds, ranks, hashes, coefficients linked to participants, or `eid`.

Falsification is symmetric. The schedule-marginal claim is **supportive** only if computation passes, all statistical/support/calibration gates pass, `Delta_Brier>0` has interval excluding zero and p<0.05, and the fixed-sequence capacity contrast is also positive with interval excluding zero and p<0.05. If those conditions hold while semantic status is unverified, the final result remains NEUTRAL-STOP for biomarker or clinical claims; at most it is a computational result conditional on the encoded-field transform.

It is **adverse** for the computational schedule-marginal hypothesis if a primary interval is wholly below zero, M1 materially loses calibration while M0 passes, or repeat-level signs reproducibly favor M0. Under semantic unavailability, describe this only as an encoded-field model comparison and do not call it evidence against cystatin-C assay information. It is **inconclusive** if intervals include zero, the fixed sequence does not advance, gates fail, signs are unstable, events/swaps are too sparse, bootstrap completion fails, or semantic status remains unverified for any proposed biomarker interpretation. A neutral stop is not evidence for equivalence or absence of information.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

Compared with the parent, this child does not merely add another learner or metric. It turns the parent’s implicit assay and actionability caveats into fail-closed operational states. It tests a clinically relevant but narrower scheduling question against the same strong nonlinear comparator, while explicitly refusing the invalid leap from event concentration among observed repeaters to a recommendation to repeat-test. The design can yield a falsifiable computational result without pretending that unavailable assay metadata or counterfactual testing outcomes exist. Remaining uncertainty is substantial: the estimand conditions on observed complete-biomarker repeat attendance and the observed interval, has only 309 outcomes, uses a protocol-derived threshold rather than an adjudicated endpoint, and has no evidence about effects or value of changing repeat-testing decisions.
