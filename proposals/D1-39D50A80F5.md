# Concordant recorded-care pathways after an HCC-coded admission

## Substantive repair and scientific deliverable

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent correctly solved admission anchoring, early competing observed-care states, and the coarse-timestamp problem with laboratories restricted to [0,12) and a later outcome window [24,72). Its remaining limitation is that a procedure-text family is still a locally interpreted documentary label. A procedure row alone cannot tell whether the record contains a corresponding planned pathway, and an order row alone cannot tell whether a procedure occurred.

This child makes that limitation the estimand. It tests a reproducible, time-linked *recorded pathway documentation state*:

- concordant: a pathway-family order record precedes a same-family procedure record;
- procedure-only: a family procedure has no qualifying preceding order;
- order-only: a family order has no matching procedure before discharge or 72 hours;
- other procedure, discharge, unresolved timing, and no transition remain competing states.

These are documentation states, not treatment states. They do not establish active HCC, indication, intent, scheduling, receipt, completion, appropriateness, response, benefit, or utility.

The future solver must newly fit and lock:

1. an admission-anchored sequential competing-state baseline (B0);
2. a matched early-laboratory model (B1); and
3. an assay-specific irregular latent-state model (B2),

then produce locked patient-level predictions, state probabilities, pathway-specific cumulative incidence/terminal distributions, proper scores, calibration, clustered uncertainty, and all prespecified robustness/falsification outputs. Completion is established by those computed artifacts and audits; a positive result is not required.

## Supported versus unresolved claim

The strongest supported claim is limited to source facts. The HCC snapshot contains admission/discharge times in `encounters`; procedure name and native start/end/source fields in `procedures`; laboratory assay/result/native time fields in `labs`; and non-drug order text, opening/start/end times, duration, status and frequency in `orders`. The schemas and source headers have been inspected. A bounded read-only audit associated 42,720 provisional first-patient HCC admissions with the available source and found 85,337 procedure rows at midnight and 6,518 patients with at least one invalid or missing procedure start. Those figures are feasibility evidence, not locked study estimates. The order source is available as a 2,189,657,512-byte CSV and its header is verified. A bounded streaming audit of the selected encounter keys found 9,030,424 order rows, 307,088 broad family-token rows with parseable `开立时间`, 41,579 procedure-bearing encounter keys, 1,141 order-bearing keys without a procedure key, and 4,411 family-order rows marked `已预约`; these are feasibility counts, not locked window-specific endpoint counts. The audit took about 92 seconds. Exact order/procedure pairing within the primary windows remains a solver output.

The source supports asking whether two independently timed record streams are concordant. It does not support saying that either stream represents a clinical decision or delivered treatment.

The falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults at their first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission, does assay-specific laboratory history observed strictly in [0,12) improve calibrated prediction of the admission-anchored terminal recorded-care documentation state through 72 hours—concordant pathway, procedure-only pathway, order-only pathway, other procedure, discharge, unresolved timing, or no transition—over admission and pre-admission information, and does it distinguish pathway documentation states rather than only any-procedure occurrence?

The clinically consequential advance is a data-quality and pathway-recognition estimand: a stable signal for concordant versus nonconcordant recorded pathways would identify whether early physiology is associated with *how reliably a pathway is documented across record streams*. It could motivate prospective adjudication or better time-linked capture. It cannot be used to select treatment or claim that a patient received beneficial care.

## Population and admission-anchored time design

Use HCC snapshot `[source checksum]`; catalog SHA-256 is `[source checksum]`.

Normalize whitespace and encoding in `患者主索引` and `就诊号`, audit duplicates and unmatched keys, and join `diagnoses` to `encounters` on that pair. Retain adults with age >=18, parseable native `入院时间` and `出院时间`, nonnegative stay, admission in [2011-01-01, 2026-01-01), and discharge before 2026-01-01. Apply and print the frozen HCC-core rule to `诊断名称`: normalized `肝细胞癌` or the explicitly frozen English-equivalent pattern `hepatocellular carcinoma`. Report every matched diagnosis string, row/key counts, overlap cells, and the exact regex before locked evaluation.

Retain the earliest eligible encounter per `患者主索引`, ordered by native `入院时间` then `就诊号`; do not replace an invalid earliest record with a later one. Exclude `姓名`, `身份证号`, `手机号`, `医保/就诊卡号`, and `住院号` from predictors. All source files are read-only.

Set t0 to native `入院时间`. Use half-open elapsed-hour intervals:

- early documentary-state window: [0,24);
- primary laboratory window: [0,12), only parseable `检验时间 < t0+12h);
- primary terminal documentation window: [24,72);
- exact boundary observations belong to the later interval.

Every eligible admission contributes to the all-admission estimand. Construct the first early state in [0,24), retaining competing observed-care states:

- `E_PROC`: valid procedure start in [0,24), before discharge;
- `E_ORDER`: qualifying pathway-family order with parseable `开立时间` in [0,24), when no earlier valid procedure establishes `E_PROC`;
- `E_UPROC`: invalid/missing procedure time that could change early ordering;
- `E_UORDER`: qualifying order with missing/invalid usable order time that could change early ordering;
- `E_DISCH`: discharge at or before 24 hours before a valid earlier procedure/order;
- `R24`: no established early procedure, order, unresolved-time state or discharge.

If multiple states are simultaneous, use a frozen precedence table printed before test prediction; do not silently discard ties. Tabulate starts before admission, starts after discharge, invalid dates, duplicate rows, same-time procedures/orders, procedure-after-discharge, and order status values. An unknown time is never coded as no care. The primary conservative rule assigns an unresolved state whenever the unknown time could alter ordering; an optimistic bound treats it as outside the interval only when logically justified.

The early-state model is baseline-only in every model comparison: B0, B1 and B2 use the same admission/pre-admission inputs for this stage. In particular, the [0,12) laboratory increment is not allowed to predict an overlapping [0,24) early state. Only `R24` receives the later conditional state model. This is not a survivor-only primary conclusion: the all-admission terminal vector is obtained by multiplying the estimated probability of `R24` by its later conditional distribution and retaining all early states.

## Primary terminal documentation estimand

For each `R24` admission, construct the first pathway episode in [24,72) from procedure and order records joined on the same normalized (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`) pair. Procedure text is read from `手术`; order text is read from `医嘱(非药品)`. Use procedure `开始时间` and order `开立时间` as the primary event times; `开始时间` and `结束时间` are retained for audit and sensitivity. A valid procedure after discharge is not an inpatient event. A qualifying order is not evidence of administration.

Use the same frozen auditable family dictionary on both streams:

- E: text contains `TACE`, `TAE`, `栓塞`, or `化疗栓塞`, and also `肝`, `肝动脉`, `hepatic`, `liver`, or `动脉`;
- S: liver/hepatic context and `切除`, `移植`, `resection`, or `transplant`;
- A: liver/hepatic, tumor, or lesion context and `消融`, `射频`, `微波`, `冷冻`, or `ablat`;
- M: more than one family matches;
- O: a valid procedure that matches none of E/S/A/M.

For an order to be qualifying, its text must match E/S/A/M under the identical normalization and token rules. Do not infer order completion from `医嘱状态`; report status distributions and perform a status-stratified sensitivity only. A concordant family episode requires a family-matched order with parseable `开立时间` no later than the procedure `开始时间`, with a gap in [0,48] hours, on the same encounter, and a valid procedure start before discharge. The 48-hour pairing window is frozen before test prediction. A later order does not retroactively turn a procedure-only label into concordant.

The mutually exclusive later states are:

- `C_E,C_S,C_A,C_M`: first valid family procedure with an antecedent same-family order within 48 hours;
- `P_E,P_S,P_A,P_M`: first valid family procedure without such an antecedent order;
- `Q_E,Q_S,Q_A,Q_M`: a qualifying family order with no matching family procedure before discharge or 72 hours;
- `O`: first valid non-family procedure;
- `D`: discharge before any established qualifying order/procedure;
- `U`: unresolved procedure/order timing that could change the state;
- `N72`: no established state through 72 hours.

If an `O` procedure precedes a later family episode, retain `O` as the first competing state. If multiple same-time families are present, retain `M`, not an arbitrary favorable family. If a family order precedes a procedure but its order time or procedure time is unresolved, retain `U` when the classification could change. If a `Q` order occurs and a later procedure is not observed, `Q` is assigned at the end of the observable window; this is a terminal documentary state, not a time-to-receipt claim. If a valid family procedure occurs without an antecedent order, it is `P`, even if an order is entered later.

The primary all-admission estimand is the mean terminal-state probability vector over every eligible admission at t0. Report early-state probabilities, conditional later-state probabilities among `R24`, and their admission-anchored product for B0, B1 and B2. Report the paired B1-minus-B0 changes for every `C_f`, `P_f`, `Q_f`, `O`, `D`, `U`, and `N72`. Sparse states remain visible and are descriptive/inconclusive, never folded into a favorable category.

The parent’s procedure-family endpoint without order corroboration is a prespecified secondary sensitivity: report first E/S/A/M/O procedure, discharge, unresolved and no-transition states under the same time rules. This reveals whether the conclusion is driven by the new corroboration requirement or by procedure text alone.

## Matched alternatives, split, analysis and uncertainty

Use participant hashing so all encounters and modalities for one patient stay in one partition: buckets 0–59 fit, 60–69 preprocessing/regularization selection, 70–79 locked evaluation; buckets 80–99 are inaccessible and are not external validation. Freeze cohort, dictionaries, order/procedure pairing rule, state precedence, assay inclusion, transformations, latent dimension, hyperparameters and outcomes before test predictions.

B0 is an admission/pre-admission transparent sequential multinomial cause-specific hazard model. It uses age, sex, admitting department, calendar era, admission time-of-day, prior encounter count, and pre-admission assay-specific nearest/count/recency/missingness summaries. It predicts all early states and later documentation states. It excludes current-window lab values, diagnosis text/type, procedure/order text, medications, documents, pathology and post-boundary fields.

B1 is the matched model with the identical split, targets, preprocessing, regularization and model family, adding only valid assay-specific lab history in [0,12): first/last result, within-assay change, elapsed time, count, missingness/density, and slope where at least two native times support it. It cannot use order/procedure information as predictors. The primary contrast is B1 versus B0 on the full admission-anchored terminal vector and the conditional `R24` vector.

B2 is the substantive learned alternative: an assay-specific irregular state-space model with assay offsets/noise, irregular observation times, shrinkage, posterior state uncertainty, and coupled competing-risk heads for the identical early/later states. Two low-dimensional latent coordinates may represent reserve/stress-like trajectory structure only as model parameters. B2 can expose nonlinear level/slope coupling and observation-time structure that B1 summaries discard. It is scientifically useful only if it improves held-out multiclass log/Brier score and calibration over B1 and remains stable under time/value and label falsifications.

Evaluate patient-level held-out multiclass log score, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope or reliability bins, state-specific cumulative incidence/terminal probability, observed-versus-predicted vectors, and paired B1-minus-B0/B2-minus-B1 contrasts. Use >=500 patient-clustered paired bootstrap resamples (1,000 if within budget), preserving each patient’s complete sequence; report 95% intervals. Locked-prediction bootstrap is primary and refit-on-bootstrap is sensitivity. Report effective support and missingness for every state; sparse C/P/Q family cells are inconclusive.

## Falsification and interpretation

1. Shuffle lab times within patient and assay, then values within assay. A physiology-linked gain should attenuate; persistence indicates assay identity or observation process.
2. Compare B1 to a count/density/missingness-only model. If the gain is fully explained by testing intensity, it is adverse or inconclusive for laboratory physiology.
3. Permute early/later state labels within calendar era and admitting department. Surviving gains indicate leakage or documentation artifact.
4. Use a forbidden-window sentinel: [12,24) information must not improve a target that is claimed to be predicted using [0,12) information.
5. Repeat exact-boundary, midnight, same-time-tie, conservative/optimistic unresolved-time and order-event-time (`开立时间` versus `开始时间`) rules. Large changes make the result inconclusive.
6. Apply frozen strict and broad family dictionaries to both procedure and order text. Route reversal, high unmatched concentration, or unstable concordance is lexical fragility.
7. Vary the 48-hour order-before-procedure pairing window only as a locked sensitivity (for example 24 and 72 hours); a large change means the concordance estimand is not robust.
8. Report status-stratified order concordance without treating any status as proof of execution; exclusion of obvious cancellation strings is sensitivity only and is never the primary label.
9. Compare the primary concordance endpoint with the parent’s procedure-only endpoint. If B1’s gain appears only for procedure occurrence and not C/P/Q distinctions, the pathway-documentation claim is unsupported.
10. Use examinations, clinical documents, pathology and medications only for secondary text/process concordance. Their missing native times prevent causal ordering or adjudication.

Supportive evidence requires coherent joins, a stable native-time flow, uncertainty-supported B1 improvement over B0 on the full terminal vector, calibration, improvement that includes C/P/Q distinctions rather than only O/D/N72, attenuation under timestamp and label permutations, and no dominance by testing density or unresolved states. B2 is supportive only if it adds reproducible calibrated information over B1.

Adverse evidence includes no B1 gain; gain explained by density; persistence under time/label permutations; dependence on midnight, order-time, status, or unknown-time policy; or gains confined to generic procedure occurrence. This rejects the directional laboratory-to-documentation hypothesis or identifies a process/lexical artifact; it does not show that laboratories lack clinical value.

Inconclusive evidence includes a large unresolved mass, sparse concordant or order-only states, poor family coherence, wide intervals, nonconvergence, few repeated assays, order/procedure key mismatch, or disagreement between strict and broad dictionaries. Do not relabel inconclusive results as support.

A supportive result means only that early recorded assay history predicts locally recorded pathway-documentation states in this snapshot. It does not establish active HCC, diagnosis timing, stage, tumor burden, resectability, indication, intent, receipt, completion, response, toxicity, mortality benefit, external care, causality, transportability or clinical utility. Those require clinical adjudication, time-linked imaging/pathology, treatment administration/completion, outcomes, external validation, and another prospective or causal study.

## Compute, method choice and revisit record

The discovery audit was an ordinary read-only source audit and took about 8 seconds; its counts are not solver results. Plan the future solver at 16 CPUs, 256 GiB, and at most 28,800 seconds under the solver-planning envelope. The 2.19-GB orders CSV requires streaming/chunked aggregation by encounter; B0/B1 and dictionary/pairing construction are CPU-first. B2 fitting and 500–1,000 clustered bootstrap repetitions are unverified; request one allocated A100 only if a bounded implementation demonstrates material need, use `cuda:0`, and never infer GPU absence from an unallocated shell. Discovery and future-solver budgets are separate; current compute limits are 7,200 discovery seconds and the solver envelope is planning-only.

B0 is retained because it makes admission context, prior testing and incremental laboratory information auditable. B2 is retained because the substantive uncertainty is irregular, nonlinear assay trajectories and posterior uncertainty, not a small predictive chase; it may reveal structure B1 loses. A count/density-only model is a diagnostic for observation process, not a scientific replacement. Raw document modeling is deferred because `clinical_documents` has no native time and metadata says extraction is unvalidated for comprehensive temporality/scope. Imaging/pathology-confirmed HCC, treatment-effect, receipt/completion and utility analyses are deferred because required adjudication, imaging, administration and outcomes are unavailable.

The actual deliverable is the newly fitted, locked B0/B1/B2 comparison and the full admission-anchored C/P/Q/O/D/U/N72 state distribution, with the procedure-only sensitivity and claim-to-output evidence boundary. Revisit the design only if order/procedure concordance is too sparse or lexically incoherent; then the evidence for revisiting is the locked support audit, not an uncomputed positive result.
