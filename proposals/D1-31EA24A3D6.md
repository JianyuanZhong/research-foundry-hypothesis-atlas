# EP7 Child: Early Post-Hepatectomy AFP Kinetics as a Relapse-Risk Biomarker

**Parent:** [prior hypothesis] (pre-op AFP + liver reserve; H2 AFP x reserve interaction)
**Status:** deliverable, data-bound, discovery-phase (not yet fitted)

## 1. Unresolved claim and clinical consequence

**Unresolved claim (H3).** After curative hepatectomy for HCC, early post-operative AFP kinetics (AFP level in days 1-42, normalization, delta vs pre-op) independently predict early treatment re-escalation (first TACE/ablation/systemic therapy within 365d, a relapse surrogate) beyond pre-op AFP and static features.

**Clinical importance.** 30-70% of HCC patients recur within 2 years. Current surveillance and adjuvant-therapy decisions use static pre-operative features. Post-op AFP failure to normalize (tumor half-life 5-7d for AFP<400) is a recognized clinical signal of residual/recurrent disease, but hepatic regeneration after resection also transiently elevates AFP. A model using routinely drawn post-op AFP to (a) identify high-risk patients and (b) determine whether the signal is tumor-specific vs regeneration artifact directly informs surveillance intensity and early adjuvant therapy timing.

**Feasibility (executed).** 19,822 hepatectomy patients; 14,921 with >=1 post-op AFP; 13,356 with both pre and post. First post-op AFP median day 2. AFP distribution: median 19.5, p75 298, p99 37890, max 60491 (ng/mL). AFP-L3: 69,771 numeric rows, median 14.5, p75 36.7, max 99.

**Advance over parent.** Parent exposure = pre-op (0-90d before surgery). This child exposure = post-op (days 1-42 after surgery). Different biological question: does the post-op AFP trajectory (what happens after tumor removal) carry independent prognostic information, and is it tumor-specific or a regeneration artifact?

## 2. Rival explanations

**Leading claim (H3):** post-op AFP kinetics carry independent prognostic information beyond pre-op AFP and static features.

**R1 (Tumor-biology):** post-op AFP reflects residual/recurrent tumor. Predictions: (a) post-op AFP effect independent of pre-op AFP (no significant interaction); (b) effect persistent in days 1-7 and 8-42; (c) effect not dependent on liver reserve.

**R2 (Hepatic-regeneration/injury):** post-op AFP reflects hepatocyte turnover after resection. Predictions: (a) post-op AFP effect concentrated in high pre-op AFP stratum (significant interaction); (b) effect stronger in impaired reserve (significant interaction); (c) effect concentrated in days 1-7, attenuated in days 8-42.

**R3 (Surveillance selection):** patients with rising post-op AFP are surveilled more intensively and re-escalated earlier. Partially testable: restrict to same number of post-op encounters; check if association persists. Not fully refutable in this dataset.

**Not fully separable** (no imaging-confirmed recurrence, no randomized surveillance, single-center). Design bounds the claim and reports which rival best fits.

## 3. Population and temporal boundaries

- **Index:** first hepatectomy (手术 contains 肝切除|肝叶切除|肝部分切除|肝段切除|肝楔形切除|肝肿瘤切除, earliest 开始时间)
- **Cohort:** >=1 post-op AFP (检验=甲胎蛋白, 检验时间 in [index+1d, index+42d]) AND >=1 pre-op AFP (检验时间 in [index-90d, index-1d]). n approx 13,356.
- **Exclusions:** (a) unparseable 开始时间; (b) systemic therapy before index; (c) index < 12mo before study end; (d) NEW: re-escalation before first post-op AFP (immortal-time handling).
- **Follow-up:** index to first re-escalation OR last encounter OR study end. Primary 365d; sensitivity 180d, 730d.
- **Join key:** 患者主索引. Time fields: 开始时间 (procedures), 检验时间 (labs), 就诊时间/出院时间 (encounters).

## 4. Estimand, outcome, exposure

**Outcome:** time to first re-escalation (TACE|栓塞|动脉化疗|肝动脉造影|消融|射频|微波|酒精注射|免疫|靶向) after index, within 365d. Surrogate for relapse (no imaging confirmation).

**Primary exposure:** last post-op AFP in days 1-7 (log-transformed). Rationale: median first post-op AFP day 2; AFP half-life 5-7d; last value captures trajectory.

**Secondary exposures:** (a) normalization binary (post-op <20 or <pre-op); (b) delta (post-op - pre-op); (c) ratio (post-op/pre-op); (d) AFP day 8-42 (timing discriminator); (e) AFP slope (>=2 points, n approx 11,554); (f) AFP-L3 (if coverage >=1000 in subcohort).

**Covariates (inherited):** age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior TACE count, pre-op log(AFP), liver reserve (albumin, bilirubin, PLT, PT, A/G, HA, ALT/AST), composite reserve score.

## 5. Pre-specified rival-explanation discriminator

**(a) Post-op AFP by pre-op AFP stratum:** stratify by pre-op AFP (low <= median vs high > median). Wald test on difference in post-op AFP log-HRs. R1: similar HRs (interaction NS). R2: larger HR in high pre-op AFP (interaction significant).

**(b) Post-op AFP x reserve interaction:** Cox with interaction term. R1: interaction NS. R2: larger effect in impaired reserve (interaction significant).

**(c) Timing: day 1-7 vs day 8-42:** separate coefficients, Wald test. R1: similar (persistent). R2: stronger in days 1-7 (early-only).

**Falsification:** R1 supportive = all 3 tests point R1 direction + primary post-op AFP significant. R2 supportive = >=2 tests point R2 direction. Inconclusive = mixed or borderline.

## 6. Baseline vs learned comparison

- **B0:** Cox, static features only.
- **B1:** Cox + pre-op AFP + reserve + post-op AFP kinetics + interactions.
- **B2:** XGBoost/LightGBM survival, same features.
- **Evaluation:** C-index, time-dependent AUC (180/365/730d), calibration, DCA, bootstrap CI (>=1000), patient-level split by 患者主索引. Report B1-B0 and B2-B0 delta with CI. SHAP for B2 (post-op AFP joint contribution).

## 7. Falsification criteria

**H3 (primary):** Supportive = post-op AFP p<0.05 AND C-index improvement >=0.02. Adverse = p>=0.10 OR C-index improvement <0.01. Inconclusive = 0.05<=p<0.10 OR 0.01<=C-index improvement<0.02.

**Discriminator:** R1 supportive = all 3 tests R1 direction + primary significant. R2 supportive = >=2 tests R2 direction. Inconclusive = mixed or borderline.

**AFP-L3 (secondary):** Supportive = p<0.05 AND C-index improvement >=0.01. Adverse = p>=0.10 OR improvement <0.005. Inconclusive = borderline.

**R3 (surveillance):** Supportive = association attenuates >=50% after encounter-count restriction. Adverse = HR ratio >0.7. Inconclusive = partial attenuation or not computable.

## 9. Limitations

1. Surrogate outcome (re-escalation, not imaging-confirmed recurrence).
2. Single center; no external validation.
3. No units on labs; assay-native values.
4. Right-censoring; administrative censoring at last encounter.
5. Surveillance bias (R3) partially testable, not fully refutable.
6. No imaging data; cannot confirm residual/recurrent tumor.
7. AFP-L3 coverage in subcohort needs verification (flagged).
8. Free-text tumor size/MVI parsing is partial.
