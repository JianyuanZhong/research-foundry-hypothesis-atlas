## Unresolved clinical question, existing support, and falsifiable hypothesis

After vasopressor-treated shock has resolved, should a markedly fluid-positive adult who remains invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48 and has adequate MAP, urine output, and potassium follow a policy of starting/restarting an ICU-input-system recorded furosemide or bumetanide delivery in the next 12 hours while ventilation continues, or avoid that recorded delivery during the interval?

This is consequential because clinicians trade possible earlier airway liberation against kidney injury, dialysis, death, and unfavorable disposition. I inspected relevant frozen full-XML passages from Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13476641`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`, source `[source checksum]`), Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13154636`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075`, source `[source checksum]`), and Hansford et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC12927355`, DOI `10.1136/bmjmed-2024-001280`, source `[source checksum]`). The first two support that fluid overload matters and that timing, volume, duration, and optimal diuretic strategy remain uncertain; they do not establish benefit of this hour-48 initiation policy. The third supports temporal alignment in target-trial emulation; it does not make observational results causal. I did not inspect or cite unavailable paper text or supplementary methods.

The strongest supported claim is that the decision is important, evidence is heterogeneous and uncertain, and this record-defined comparison is computable in MIMIC. The unresolved claim remains:

> In the prespecified measured-overlap discovery population, assignment-compatible follow-up under A12 increases the lower bound of five-day same-admission recorded 48-hour airway-event-free extubation by more than 0.05 versus B12, while one-sided upper limits for record-defined 48-hour creatinine rise, observed RRT7, and terminal/hospice/acute-transfer harm remain below +0.03, +0.05, and +0.03.

This is a falsifiable dynamic recorded-policy association, not a pharmacologic effect among recipients, proof of safety, or a recommendation.

## Corrected uncertainty and result branches

Run 1,999 successful subject bootstraps with PCG64 seed 480048, rerunning folds, preprocessing, both fixed-penalty model attempts, overlap, truncation, and every endpoint bound. Attempt at most 2,499 draws; fewer than 1,999 successes or more than 5% failed attempts is computationally infeasible. Pointwise one-sided 95% limits remain the 0.05/0.95 empirical quantiles and are reported for magnitude.

The six adverse-oriented statistics are `-DeltaU_RHA`, `DeltaL_RCR48-0.03`, `min(DeltaL_RRT7_lower,DeltaL_RRT7_upper)-0.05`, `DeltaL_RTHD28-0.03`, `DeltaL_true_mortality-0.03`, and `-0.05-DeltaU_ANHATD28`; positive values favor harm. For each `d_hat_j`, compute centered-bootstrap `p_j=(1+sum I[(d*_bj-d_hat_j)>=d_hat_j])/(B+1)`, counting ties against rejection. Apply Holm step-down across the six at familywise alpha 0.05, with protocol-listed order breaking ties. The exact implementation and fixtures are in `analysis/uncertainty_reference.py`.

Results are classified on separate axes:

1. **Infeasible:** source/state/minimum/model/endpoint/bootstrap computation fails. No directional claim.
2. **Validity-inconclusive:** computation succeeds but any frozen positivity, balance, calibration, negative-control, ambiguity, contamination, timing, observation, endpoint-proxy, process-control, or material-reversal gate fails. Directional estimates are descriptive.
3. **Adverse-FWER:** computation and validity pass and at least one adverse family is Holm-rejected. Name all rejected families and report estimate, bound, pointwise limit, raw and adjusted p. This is an observational concern, not proof of toxicity.
4. **Supportive recorded:** validity passes; no adjusted adverse family; `DeltaL_RHA` lower limit >+0.05; chart-augmented and continuous-ICU contrast lower limits >0; renal/RRT/terminal upper limits remain below margins; disposition is not adverse; and every proxy diagnostic passes.
5. **Margin-falsified:** no adjusted adverse family and the one-sided upper limit for `DeltaU_RHA<=+0.05`. This rejects the prespecified five-point recorded-proxy improvement, not every physiologic benefit.
6. **Statistically inconclusive:** all other feasible, valid results, including wide bounds or safety limits that cross margins without adjusted evidence of harm.

Every run also emits a non-branching safety-alert table for any positive adverse-oriented estimate, raw one-sided `p<0.05`, or Holm rejection. Failed validity gates cannot hide a signal, but the verifier must label it exploratory and cannot promote it to formal adverse evidence.

## Verification, evidence boundary, and remaining uncertainty

The verifier can check source hashes/headers, partition, joins, clocks, item values, washout, event ordering, clone transitions, endpoint bounds, weights, bootstrap accounting, six adverse statistics, centered p-values, Holm ordering, branch axes, and output-linked language. Fixtures must reject: one raw `p<0.05` labeled formal adverse without Holm rejection; a diagnostic failure called computationally infeasible; a material reversal ignored; supportive language from nonsignificant safety tests; or any causal, renal-safety, durable-extubation, equivalence, or recommendation claim unsupported by outputs.

Automation cannot establish actual route/receipt or complete all-route nonreceipt, clinical shock recovery or congestion, extubation intent/readiness, complete ward airway capture, true KDIGO AKI or renal safety, goals of care, exchangeability, causality, transportability, or clinical actionability of an adjusted signal. These require blinded clinical/nephrology review, validated medication/airway linkage, fuller notes or external outcomes, replication, and preferably randomization.

An initial treatment-blind aggregate source-semantic scan included all selected loop rows, joined no outcomes, estimated no effect, and was discarded and transparently retained in the audit sampling history.
