# Compiler-ready exact-source endpoint and documentation-opportunity audit for the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Status and targeted repair

This is a substantive compiler-ready child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It repairs only the remaining weakness identified by the Lead: the frozen respiratory-label design has sparse multicenter overlap, and the label-to-trajectory association can be confounded by whether a hospital and stay had an opportunity to document respiratory or other care data. The strongest available evidence is only that the exact labels are sparse and strongly hospital concentrated in this selected snapshot, while prior structured mappings can be dominated by a chart component and reverse across sites or landmarks. That evidence does not establish documentation convention, SBT delivery, extubation evaluation, readiness, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality. This proposal reports no new results.

## Models, documentation audit, and falsification

Use deterministic leave-one-hospital-out folds with all landmarks from a stay in one fold and no hospital predictor. Fit fixed low-dimensional multinomial logistic pipelines with training-hospital-only imputation, scaling, feature selection, calibration, and cutpoints:

* `M_C`: C only;
* `M_Oresp`: C + O_resp;
* `M_Ojoint`: C + O_joint;
* `M_S`: C + S;
* `M_0`: C + S + O_joint (or fail-closed O_resp if O_joint support fails);
* `M_1`: exactly M_0 plus the current exact two-level label.

Use a fixed regularization grid, solver, tolerance, deterministic tie-break, fixed seed `61061`, and cache one out-of-fold prediction per row. Report held-out K multiclass log loss and Brier score, M_Oresp-minus-M_C, M_Ojoint-minus-M_Oresp, M_0-minus-M_S, and M_1-minus-M_0. The key documentation audit is whether the label increment disappears after adding O_joint and whether O_joint materially improves observability/trajectory prediction over O_resp. A label increment that is present only with O_resp, only in high-opportunity cells, or only after endpoint availability conditioning is adverse to a clean residual-state interpretation.

Construct a value-blind documentation audit target from each pre-D family: `opportunity_present` requires at least two distinct clinical timestamps spanning at least 30 minutes in the specified pre-D window; otherwise use `opportunity_unobservable`. Report family-level and joint opportunity rates by hospital, landmark, and arm only after freezing definitions. A positive current-label increment for the value-blind opportunity target is adverse to the claim that the label carries information beyond documentation process; a null is not proof of semantic validity. Do not use row counts from carePlanGeneral.

Retain the parent negative controls without replacing K: (a) the pre-D respiratory trajectory placebo from `(D-1440,D-720]` to B using the same parser and explicit `pre_unobservable`; (b) a value-blind pre-D timestamp opportunity placebo; (c) the exact +720-minute future-anchor lagged-label placebo with anchor `D_pl=D+720`, separate competing exits and overlap statuses; and (d) opportunity/state-preserving conditional exact-label permutations within held-out hospital and training-derived S/O cells, plus complete-stay label-vector permutations where feasible. At least 1,000 valid arrangements are attempted when feasible, or all unique arrangements when fewer exist; fewer than 20 valid arrangements, fewer than five hospitals, lost support, or any invariant failure is inconclusive, never adverse. A positive pre-D or opportunity placebo is adverse to temporal or documentation-residualized interpretation; a positive lagged-label placebo is adverse to contemporaneous specificity. Placebo outcomes never alter frozen K or its denominators.

## Uncertainty, interpretation, and fail-closed rules

Use a fixed-seed stay-cluster bootstrap (`61061`), minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates, resampling complete stays within hospital with all landmarks, features, labels, K/statuses, and placebo statuses together. Refit held-out pipelines and recompute support, cells, opportunity specifications, nulls, and metrics in each replicate. Do not nest permutations inside bootstrap replicates. If source/schema/artifact checks, parser boundaries, exact labels, D equality, K precedence, stay grouping, fold isolation, support invariants, or bootstrap minimums fail, set `verification.status=invalid` or `inconclusive` as appropriate and make no scientific interpretation.

A computationally supportive exact-label information result requires improvement in both micro and equal-hospital held-out K loss over M_0 in the unbounded all-row analysis and at least two prespecified endpoint/observation sensitivities, supported residual cells, no adverse conditional/pre-D/opportunity/lagged placebo, no concentration or direction reversal beyond the gates, and bootstrap uncertainty compatible with the increment. It must additionally beat `M_Ojoint` rather than merely reproduce generic chart intensity. If O_joint support fails, the claim is limited to O_resp-adjusted diagnostics and cannot be called generic-documentation-adjusted.

Supportive findings establish only incremental prediction of the named structured-chart K states or prioritization of a bounded manual chart-review task. Adverse findings mean the label is not robust for that construct or remains compatible with hospital, state, opportunity, exit, persistence, or repeated-documentation composition. Inconclusive findings mean support, overlap, source availability, calibration, null exchangeability, computation, or uncertainty was insufficient. No result establishes actual bedside evaluation, SBT, extubation, delivered ventilator setting, appropriateness, treatment effect, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.
