# Episode 59: does late lactate retain material decision information beyond a time-indexed 24-hour laboratory state?

## Unresolved question, supported evidence, and hypothesis

At the 24-hour reassessment after an early recorded norepinephrine entry, is the selected late lactate still materially informative once the model is given the early lactate and a broad non-lactate organ-state history whose collection and result-revision offsets are at or before that person's landmark?

The strongest claim currently supported is narrow and must not be upgraded. In the Episode 58 APACHE-IVa-complete subset (1,084 people, 31 hospitals), adding the observed late lactate to retrospective APACHE IVa plus entry lactate produced a positive modeled increment over mortality-review thresholds 0.30–0.50: ΔINB +1.982 per 100 (2,000-refit copied-hospital 95% interval +0.483 to +3.351), with positive proper-score changes and 31/31 positive site deletions. This supports positive modeled information in that selected cohort and utility scenario. It did **not** resolve the locked >=+1-per-100 materiality claim because +1 lies inside the interval. APACHE IVa's `predictedhospitalmortality` has no availability timestamp in EICU, so it is not evidence for what was operationally known at this landmark.

The other parent preserves a different unresolved boundary. In 1,229 people from 33 hospitals, adding continuous trajectory to a model containing late lactate yielded ΔAUROC +0.00479 (95% −0.00849,+0.02286), ΔBrier +0.000756 (−0.00266,+0.00435), and 33/33 positive site deletions. Its +0.02 AUROC materiality claim and even the direction after clustered refitting remained inconclusive. Its later fixed-prediction decision feasibility was also small and uncertain. Neither parent established test-ordering utility, causality, changed care, or patient benefit.

The Episode 59 primary hypothesis is:

> In the frozen 1,229-person/33-hospital EICU 24-hour risk set, adding the **observed late lactate value** to a model containing entry lactate plus the prespecified non-lactate laboratory state available by the landmark increases uniformly threshold-averaged net benefit over hospital-mortality review thresholds 0.30–0.50 by at least **1 net true-positive-equivalent review per 100 people**.

This is a new, harder conditional-information test, not a rescue or repetition of either parent. A late-lactate result that survives this comparator would motivate independent prospective validation of a lactate-assisted risk-review model. An increment absorbed by the richer baseline would show that the prior modeled gain was conditional on a sparse or operationally ambiguous comparator. Because every person was selected for having a late lactate, this experiment does not estimate whether ordering that test helps.

## Clinical importance and literature boundary

The inspected full-text XML of Baysan et al. (2022; DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750; PMCID PMC9444407; frozen source `[source checksum]`; [source checksum]) reports external validation in 4,440 critically ill adults with sepsis: adding 24-hour lactate to recalibrated APACHE IV increased C-statistic from 0.62 to 0.64, and admission plus 24-hour lactate had the smallest AIC. It supports modest serial prognostic information in a different population. It does not test a norepinephrine-entry cohort, a contemporaneous non-lactate laboratory comparator, decision-curve materiality, or test-ordering benefit. The article's supplementary material was not inspected.

The inspected full HTML decision-curve guide by Vickers et al. (PMCID PMC6261531; frozen source `[source checksum]`; [source checksum]) supports comparing net benefit with treat-all and treat-none over clinically reasonable thresholds and emphasizes that thresholds encode benefit/harm preferences. It does not validate this study's 0.30–0.50 range, uniform weighting, review action, or +1-per-100 margin.

The three research-ambition demonstrations and their availability limits were reviewed through `references/research-ambition/README.md`; none supplies evidence for this hypothesis. No unavailable cancer-paper main text or STAR Methods is claimed to have been inspected.

## Frozen population, clocks, and outcome

Reconstruct the complete EICU snapshot `[source checksum]`; do not sample source rows.

1. Parse `patient.age`; map literal `> 89` and numeric values above 89 to 90; retain age >=18.
2. Define `t0` as the earliest `infusionDrug.infusionoffset` in [0,1440] whose case-folded `drugname` contains literal `norepinephrine` or `levophed`. This is a recorded entry, not verified administration or dose.
3. Per `uniquepid`, retain the stay with smallest `(t0,patientunitstayid)`.
4. Define `L24=t0+1440`. Require `hospitaldischargestatus` in {Alive, Expired} and `hospitaldischargeoffset >= L24`.
5. Reproduce the parents' baseline lactate `B`: case-folded trimmed `labname='lactate'`, system unit `mmol/L`, finite `labresult` in [0.2,30], collection `labresultoffset` in [t0−360,t0], and `labresultrevisedoffset <= t0+60`. At a collection retain the greatest eligible revision; invalidate conflicting values at that revision; identical ties use smallest `labid`. Select the latest valid collection and require `B>=2`.
6. Reproduce late lactate `F` with the same analyte/unit/range/revision/conflict rules, collection in [t0+720,t0+1440], revision by L24, selecting nearest t0+1080, then earlier collection, then smallest `labid`.
7. Preserve the trajectory parent's outcome-blind site gate: calculate legacy `X=1[(B−F)/B<0.20]` only to require at least five X=0 and five X=1 people per hospital. Freeze the site list before mapping outcomes. X is never a predictor, estimand, subgroup, or rescue analysis.
8. Only then define `D=1` for Expired and 0 for Alive. The outcome is subsequent hospital discharge status; EICU has no exact death time or cause.

The required flow is 200,859 patient rows; 200,234 adult rows, including 7,081 older-age mappings; 16,299 early-pressor stays; 14,786 selected people; 14,672 known outcomes; 13,023 hospitalized through L24; 5,077 valid B; 3,117 with B>=2; 1,506 with valid F in 96 hospitals; and 1,229 people in the frozen 33 hospitals. The final cohort has 507 deaths and 722 survivors. A complete-source audit found zero people discharged at exactly L24, removing the potential equality ambiguity without changing the parent rule.

Selection requires survival/hospitalization, a recorded pressor entry, repeated lactate measurement, and an outcome-blind site-volume/overlap rule. It is not representative of all shock or all ICU patients.

## Strictly time-indexed non-lactate baseline

The primary comparator deliberately excludes `apachePatientResult`. Its schema `datasets/eicu/table-754bebf64d3d9909.json` lists no temporal column, so neither the compiler nor solver may use APACHE predictions, APS variables, discharge variables, post-L24 rows, notes, diagnosis codes, treatment labels, or hospital identity as predictors.

For every fixed marker below, use case-folded trimmed `labname` and `labmeasurenamesystem`. Missing system unit means genuinely blank/missing, not the literal string “<na>”.

- `potassium|mmol/L`, `sodium|mmol/L`, `chloride|mmol/L`, `bicarbonate|mmol/L`
- `BUN|mg/dL`, `creatinine|mg/dL`, `glucose|mg/dL`
- `Hgb|g/dL`, `platelets x 1000|K/mcL`, `WBC x 1000|K/mcL`
- `calcium|mg/dL`, `magnesium|mg/dL`, `phosphate|mg/dL`
- `anion gap|missing unit`
- `pH|missing unit`, `paCO2|mm Hg`, `paO2|mm Hg`, `base excess|mEq/L`
- `albumin|g/dL`, `total bilirubin|mg/dL`, `ALT (SGPT)|units/L`, `AST (SGOT)|units/L`, `PT - INR|ratio`

A non-lactate row is eligible only if its collection offset is in [t0,L24], its revision offset is finite and <=L24, and its value is finite. At each `(patientunitstayid,marker,labresultoffset)`, retain the greatest eligible revision, invalidate a collection if values conflict at that revision, and resolve identical ties by smallest `labid`. No lactate row may enter these summaries.

For each marker create six frozen features: missing indicator; `log1p(number of valid collections)`; last value; landmark recency `(L24−last_collection)/1440`; minimum; and maximum. Thus the comparator represents current state, extrema, recency, and measurement intensity, not only one value. Within each training fold, median-impute missing last/recency/min/max values, then winsorize continuous nonindicator features at training 1st/99th percentiles and standardize with the training mean and population SD. Apply constants unchanged to held-out data. Zero counts remain zero before `log1p`. No outcome-dependent feature or missingness selection is permitted.

The bounded audit `episode59_availability_audit.py` ([source checksum]) scanned the complete patient, infusion, and lab files. Its aggregate output ([source checksum]) reproduced 1,229/33/507 and found the 23 frozen markers in 756–1,213 people (61.5%–98.7%) under collection-plus-revision cutoffs before final per-collection conflict resolution. Marker selection used coverage and clinical nonredundancy, not outcomes; the death count was cohort QC only. This establishes bounded availability, not predictive gain or hypothesis support. The definitive run must report post-conflict coverage and becomes not computable unless at least 20 of the 23 markers each occur in at least 55% of people and in at least 20 of the 33 hospitals.

`labresultrevisedoffset` is a database availability proxy, not proof of first result release or clinician viewing. This study's baseline is therefore stricter and more auditable than untimestamped APACHE, but it is not a complete reconstruction of the bedside information set. Nurse charting, notes, imaging, exact interventions, and unrecorded knowledge remain absent.

## Models, validation, and estimand

`R0`, the primary comparator, contains:

- age and age squared; Female and Male indicators with Other/Unknown omitted;
- `t0/1440` and its square;
- baseline-lactate lag `(t0−B_offset)/360`, `ln(B)`, and `ln(B)^2`;
- follow-up measurement age `(L24−F_offset)/720`, so R0 knows that a repeat was obtained and when but not its value;
- all 138 frozen non-lactate summary features above.

`R1` adds only `ln(F)` and `ln(F)^2`. The primary contrast is R1−R0.

A prespecified non-label-changing trajectory-shape model `R2` adds `T^2`, where `T=ln(F/B)`, to R1. Linear T is not added because R1 already contains ln(B) and ln(F), making it algebraically redundant; T² tests the remaining nonlinear B-by-F pattern. R2 cannot rescue or reclassify the primary R1−R0 result.

Fit unweighted L2 logistic regression with intercept, `C=1.0`, `lbfgs`, `max_iter=10000`, `tol=1e-8`, no class weighting, and seed `20260924`. Validation is leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO), with all imputation, winsorization, and scaling learned only from training hospitals. Produce exactly one unrecalibrated R0/R1/R2 probability per person. No feature search, coefficient interpretation, pooled recalibration, threshold adjustment, or held-out-outcome tuning is allowed.

At each `p in {0.30,0.31,...,0.50}`, alert when held-out risk is >=p and compute `NB=TP/N−FP/N*p/(1−p)`. Treat-none is zero; treat-all uses pooled prevalence; training-hospital prevalence is a sanity predictor. The primary estimand is

`ΔINB_F = 100/(0.50−0.30) * integral_[0.30,0.50] [NB_R1(p)−NB_R0(p)] dp`

using the trapezoid rule on all 21 points. The material target is +1 per 100. The threshold range, uniform weighting, and margin are inherited working decision scenarios, not elicited preferences.
