# Episode 95 successor: findings-only semantic quarantine for HCC documentary-M2 allocation

Parent anchors: \`[prior hypothesis]\` (patient/whole-accession capacity and structural ledgers) and \`[prior hypothesis]\` (semantic-attribution quarantine and lifecycle-unknown certificate).

## 1. Clinical question and independent hypothesis

The mature HCC design can show a finite-corpus documentary-M2 advantage under patient-slot, accession-unit, raw-component, and component-plus-text budgets. The attribution parent can additionally show that retained semantic features are traceable to exact examination rows, fields, ordinals, and normalization digests. One consequential ambiguity remains: exact provenance does not establish that the semantic signal is independent of the radiologist's conclusion/impression text. A queue whose signal is carried mainly by \`examination diagnosis\` may be prioritizing a stored conclusion that requires report finalization, release, retrieval, and clinician reading; it is not evidence that a preoperative reader could recover the same signal from the imaging findings or that anyone acted on it.

This is clinically important because the unresolved bridge is different in the two cases. A findings-derived signal could motivate a prospective image-reread and workflow validation. A diagnosis/impression-dependent signal first requires report-lifecycle and communication validation; a stored narrative alone cannot establish that it was available or acted upon. Public evidence is consistent with this distinction but does not solve it: the indexed abstract for Gershanik, Lacson and Khorasani (AMIA 2011; PMID 22195100; PMCID PMC3243237) reports pulmonary nodules in 3,401 findings sections but only 2,162 impressions, and explicitly frames the impression as the summarized section physicians often focus on. The report did not measure access, reading, downstream action, or patient outcomes. The full article was not treated as inspected here because the attempted page fetch was blocked; only the frozen indexed abstract and a model-mediated page extraction with that limitation were used.

### Hypothesis

At the inherited frozen threshold \(q^*\), the documentary-M2 advantage of the semantic queue will survive a no-refit, findings-only semantic replay in which every semantic feature sourced wholly or partly from \`examination diagnosis\` (or from an unresolved/mixed source) is replaced by the frozen unavailable value, while features sourced exclusively from \`examination findings\` may be retained. The replay must retain the strict source/content certificate, all three documentary ledgers, every inherited comparator/random/deletion gate, and the untouched 2021 temporal confirmation.

Formally, partition the frozen 2019 semantic feature manifest into:

- \(P_F\): every source field for the feature is exactly \`examinations.examination findings\` and its raw rows/normalization/aggregation are fully certified;
- \(P_D\): every source field is exactly \`examinations.examination diagnosis\` and certified;
- \(P_X\): mixed fields, another field/table, missing manifest, or unresolved provenance.

\`G_F\` is byte-identical fitted \`G\` with only \(P_F\) features retained; every \(P_D\) and \(P_X\) feature is replaced by the exact 2019 unavailable block, without refitting, recalibration, or feature reordering. \`G_D\` is a diagnostic negative/attribution decomposition with only \(P_D\) features retained and all other semantic features masked; it cannot rescue \`G_F\` or select \(q^*\).

The confirmatory new estimand is

\[
 D^F_{C,\ell}(z)=100[H_{G_F,\ell}(z)-H_{C,\ell}(z)]/K,
 \quad C\in\{B,R,G_{\rm mask}\},\quad \ell\in\{U,R,T\},
\]

and

\[
 \Delta_F(q^*)=\min_{z\in Z_{20}\cup Z_{21}}
 \min_{\ell\in\{U,R,T\}}
 \min_{C\in\{B,R,G_{\rm mask}\}}D^F_{C,\ell}(z).
\]

The primary findings-only hypothesis is supported only if \(\Delta_F(q^*)>5\) percentage points under exact integer cross-products, all inherited gates pass, and the result is temporally replicated in 2021. Report the paired displacement from \`G_SA\`/\`G\`, masked feature counts, changed ranks, EMPTY positions, and patient/accession witnesses; these are diagnostics, not replacement estimands. A pass means a source-attributed findings-only shadow retains the finite-corpus queue property. It does not mean the findings were image-derived, released, read, clinically correct, or acted upon.

## 2. Evidence versus unresolved claim

The HCC source and structural audit support that examination narratives have separate findings and diagnosis fields and that this contrast is computable. In the exact examination file, a whole-source read-only audit found 419,996 rows, 392,854 nonblank accession keys, 20,611 accessions with findings but no diagnosis, 39,462 with diagnosis but no findings, 332,653 with both, and 72 rows with blank accession keys. These are not HCC cohort counts, prevalence, review time, or clinical-action measurements. The audit is preserved at \`support/examination_field_presence_audit.json\` with output SHA-256 \`[source checksum]\`.

The strongest supported HCC claim before execution is that a deterministic field-level provenance partition, conservative masking, and fixed-capacity queue replay are computable. The unresolved claim is whether the semantic allocation property is retained without conclusion/impression text. A supportive result cannot establish actual report lifecycle, review burden, semantic correctness, clinician action, treatment effect, safety, fairness, transportability, recurrence, survival, or patient benefit. An adverse findings-only result would show dependence on the quarantined conclusion/mixed block under this frozen design; it would not prove that semantic imaging is clinically useless. An inconclusive result means the source or frozen manifest cannot answer the question.

The demonstration papers in \`references/research-ambition/README.md\` are ambition references, not evidence for this HCC hypothesis. The cancer demonstration's main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not claimed as inspected.

## 3. Population, chronology, and endpoint (inherited and frozen)

Set \(t_{op}\) to the qualifying procedure/anesthesia \`procedures.Start Time\`. Set \(t_{dec}=t_{op}-24\) hours. An examination accession is eligible only when its \`Start Time\` interval is wholly inside \([t_{op}-90d,t_{dec})\). Retain 12-, 48-, and 72-hour onset worlds as descriptive monotone sensitivities; 24 hours is confirmatory. No report release, view, read, or finalization time is imputed from acquisition time.

Keep patient-disjoint roles exactly: 2015–2018 development; 2019 dictionary, preprocessing, feature/provenance manifest, model, book, capacity, tie-key, seed, and rational-grid lock; 2020 threshold selection only; 2021 one untouched temporal replay; 2022 onward audit only. The new replay is evaluated at the inherited \(q^*\) and cannot select or alter it. Retain the inherited \(K=\lfloor0.10N_{ref}\rfloor\), \(C_U,C_R,C_T=\lfloor0.10W^\ell_{ref}\rfloor\), nonvacuity conditions, and longest-prefix no-skip queue. No queue may skip a patient, backfill, rerank, or spend unused capacity.

Retain the complete fixed radiology and pathology books, two independent qualified readers plus adjudicator for each modality, nine radiology-by-pathology coupled pairs, unresolved labels, patient-disjoint deletion tests, and coupled source/onset/reader/pathology worlds. The endpoint is documentary-M2 as defined by the frozen books; it is not specimen-linked biological MVI. The pathology table has no time, specimen, block, slide, or examination accession, so it is an endpoint book only.

## 5. Frozen feature provenance and replay

The compiler must read the inherited 2019 feature manifest and model artifact. For each semantic feature it must record model block/position/hash; exact table and field; accession and row-ordinal set; raw-byte digest; normalization/tokenizer/parser version and output digest; aggregation rule; and missing value. No feature may be assigned to \(P_F\) based on its name, cleaned text, or apparent clinical meaning.

The inherited semantic-attribution certificate \(C^{SA}_{iy}(\omega)\) remains required: every contributing accession must have a nonblank whole-accession key, eligible acquisition interval, unique patient attribution, stable payload digest, exact ordinal/field replay, and no cross-world value. Conflicting duplicate payloads, blank/nonblank mixtures, cross-patient accession collisions, irreconcilable modality/time parses, failed byte replay, or an absent manifest mask the entire semantic block. No favorable accession subset may be selected after looking at endpoint, score, year, reader, pathology, onset, or ledger.

For the new findings-only certificate, a retained semantic feature additionally requires every contributing source field to be exactly \`examinations.Examination findings\`. A feature that uses both \`Examination findings\` and \`Examination diagnosis\`, even if its value is mostly findings text, is \(P_X\) and is masked. \`Check\` can remain in inherited acquisition/report-surface arms only under its original 2019 manifest; it is not silently promoted into the semantic findings block. Clinical documents, pathology, diagnoses, labs, identifiers, reader identity, audit flags, future rows, and outcome-derived values are forbidden semantic sources.

Compute, without refitting:

1. inherited \`B\`, \`R\`, \`G\`, \`G_mask\`, and \`G_SA\` exactly as frozen;
2. \`G_F\` with only \(P_F\) semantic positions retained and all others set to the exact unavailable block;
3. diagnostic \`G_D\` with only \(P_D\) positions retained and all other semantic positions unavailable.

Use identical binary64 coefficients, feature order, score bytes, protected tie keys, patient set, \(K\), \(C_U,C_R,C_T\), longest-prefix queue, EMPTY positions, coupled state, random seeds, 256 hash-random permutations, and anchored deletion positions. \`G_D\` is never used for threshold choice. A failure to partition the frozen manifest is computationally inconclusive, not permission to classify a feature as findings-derived.

## 6. Supportive, adverse, inconclusive, and falsification rules

Apply this precedence:

1. \`computationally_inconclusive\` if any source/catalog/schema hash, encoding/ordinal/byte replay, declared join, field partition, feature manifest, model hash, score byte, tie, fixed-capacity, EMPTY, patientwise-max, no-refit, output-linkage, or threshold-freeze check fails.
2. \`feasibility_inconclusive\` if inherited \(N,K,C_\ell\), event, label-stable patient, reader/pathology-book, or nonvacuity requirements fail.
3. \`selection_adverse_no_subcomplete_threshold\` if inherited 2020 has no valid \(q<1\) or \(q^*=1\); do not use a shadow to rescue it.
4. \`inherited_gate_adverse\` if any inherited comparator, 256-random, deletion, identity/content, structural-ledger, or 2021 temporal gate fails.
5. \`findings_only_adverse\` if inherited selection is valid but \(G_F\) has \(\Delta_F(q^*)\le5\), fails any required comparator/random/deletion gate, or fails locked 2021 replay. Report the first year/world/reader/pathology/cutoff/ledger/comparator and patient/accession/feature witness. If \(G_D\) passes while \(G_F\) fails, label the descriptive pattern \`impression-dependent\`; if both fail, label it \`not-supported-by-either-field-restricted-shadow\`. Neither label is causal.
6. \`findings_only_supportive\` only if all inherited gates and predeclared \(G_F\) gates pass in 2020 and untouched 2021, with exact output-linked witnesses. This supports only a finite-census findings-only robustness statement.
7. Append \`report_lifecycle_unidentified\` and \`clinical_action_unidentified\` to every status. HCC has no authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrieved/view/read timestamps and no report-to-action attribution.

The hypothesis is falsified by a valid, fully replayable result in which any required \(D^F_{C,\ell}(z)\le5\), any required random/deletion gate fails, or the findings-only property does not replicate in 2021. A missing or non-replayable manifest is inconclusive, not falsification. The verifier must reject prose that turns a finite-census margin into a probability, confidence guarantee, causal effect, workflow minutes, treatment effect, survival effect, or patient benefit.

The verifier must inject or recompute these adversaries:

- change only a 2021 finding/diagnosis field, source ordinal, accession, or provenance mapping and verify no 2019 artifact or \(q^*\) changes;
- alter a raw field boundary, HTML tag, encoding, or source ordinal and require inconclusive status rather than favorable masking;
- duplicate an identical row and retain one accession contribution with multiplicity in the ledger;
- introduce a conflicting duplicate or cross-patient \((visit number, examination number)\) collision and require whole-accession conservative masking;
- relabel a \`Examination Diagnosis\` source as \`Examination Findings\` and require the partition hash and \(G_F\) output to change or the run to be inconclusive;
- force a mixed-field feature into \(P_F\), or map a feature to clinical documents/pathology, and require rejection;
- verify \(G_F\) uses exactly inherited coefficients, unavailable block, \(K,C_\ell\), tie key, patient set, coupled states, and no refit;
- verify \(G_D\) cannot select \(q^*\) or rescue failed \(G_F\);
- verify lifecycle-unknown stored text is never described as released, viewed, read, finalized, or available;
- feed a deliberately incorrect supportive/action/benefit conclusion while preserving computed outputs and require verifier failure because the prose is not entailed;
- feed correct adverse and inconclusive conclusions and require acceptance only when linked to exact witness/status precedence.

Automatic verification can establish source lineage, field partition, deterministic masking, queue arithmetic, temporal lock, and whether the reported finite-corpus conclusion follows from outputs. It cannot establish that \`examination findings\` is image-derived, that either field is clinically correct, that a report was available to a clinician, that anyone reviewed it, that a clinician acted, or that patients benefited. Those claims require external evidence and expert adjudication.

## 8. External bridge required for clinical interpretation

If \`findings_only_supportive\`, the next study must capture immutable report-version/accession IDs and hashes, section boundaries, author/finalization/release/ingest/retrieval/read timestamps, image-series and prior-comparison counts, and blinded expert start/stop review time. Radiologists should independently reread corresponding CT/MRI images before \(t_{dec}\), derive the \(P_F\) variables under a predeclared dictionary, and compare image-derived features with stored findings-only features. A calibrated positive association between structural ledgers and observed time/RVU would be needed before any ledger can be called operational burden; weak or non-monotone calibration leaves the HCC result documentary only.

Only a separate silent-mode workflow study can test whether a findings-only queue changes clinician review, acknowledgement, follow-up orders, resection planning, safety, or equity. Treatment/procedure rows and order rows in HCC cannot establish those effects because their intent, attribution to the report, completion, counterfactual, and patient outcomes are unavailable. Specimen-linked pathology and longitudinal recurrence/survival data would be required for biological or patient-benefit claims.
