## Decision and substantive advance

This is a narrow successor to the selected mature proposal. It preserves the hour-48 post-shock A12/B12 recorded-loop question, discovery partition, earliest-opportunity freeze, treatment-before-`C=min(X,V.endtime)` rule, multisource renal guards, bounded outcomes, measured-overlap clone-censor estimand, 1,999 joint bootstraps, eight-family Holm control, result branches, and explicit noncausal limits.

It repairs one upstream clinical-validity defect: parent eligibility requires an accepted `procedureevents` item 225792 `Invasive Ventilation` interval crossing `t0`, but does not establish whether ventilation is through an endotracheal tube or a tracheostomy. The primary RHA-E48-D5 event is item 227194 `Extubation`. Extubation is removal of an endotracheal tube; ventilator liberation in a tracheostomized patient is final disconnection from the ventilator. A baseline tracheostomy therefore creates a different clinical population and a different event process. Merely treating a prior tracheostomy as failure can build structural outcome zeros into an “extubation” estimand.

The source-demonstrated repair is to exclude recorded definite or possible prevalent tracheostomy before all later clinical eligibility gates, without reopening a later stay; label the resulting target as “no recorded definite/possible prevalent tracheostomy,” not “confirmed ETT”; and require full-pipeline 6-hour, late-chart, source-ablation, and ETT-confirmed sensitivities. The governing amendment is `analysis/episode50_airway_route_contract.json`. It overrides the parent only for baseline airway-route eligibility, route diagnostics, related validity gates and verifier fixtures. All unmentioned parent definitions remain controlling.

This defect is clinically consequential despite affecting fewer broad records than the renal repair: it is a category error in the primary endpoint, not merely incomplete capture within a valid endpoint. The treatment-blind audit demonstrates that procedure-only screening misses most record-positive baseline tracheostomies.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by inspected evidence is limited:

1. MIMIC has distinct route evidence in `chartevents` and tracheostomy procedures, whereas item 225792 alone is not an ETT guarantee.
2. The current official MIT-LCP ventilation concept explicitly gives tracheostomy priority over mechanical ventilation, recognizes `Tracheostomy tube` and `Trach mask `, and states that invasive ventilation can be delivered through an oro/nasotracheal tube or tracheostomy.
3. Contemporary clinical guidance distinguishes extubation from ventilator liberation. A focused extraction of the 2024 AARC guideline states that tracheostomy may precede liberation. The PROMIZING protocol defines success as extubation for an ETT patient but final ventilator disconnection for a tracheostomized patient.
4. In a treatment-blind discovery audit of 7,663 earliest broad adult hour-48 item-225792 opportunities, baseline-available records identified 209 with tracheostomy evidence in the last six hours and 235 in the last 24 hours (3.1%). Same-admission procedure evidence identified only 13. There were 6,841 with a recent ETT device record, 631 with neither recent ETT nor tracheostomy route evidence, and 18 with conflicting recent route evidence. These categories overlap as stated. A separate procedure audit found seven same-stay tracheostomies by t0 and none had an extubation task by day five.

Those observations establish source semantics and potential endpoint/population impact. They do not establish actual airway route, final-cohort prevalence, arm imbalance, any outcome rate, or a treatment effect.

The unresolved claim remains:

> In adults in the prespecified measured-overlap discovery population who have no recorded definite or possible prevalent tracheostomy at ICU hour 48, assignment-compatible A12 follow-up increases the lower bound of five-day recorded 48-hour airway-event-free extubation by more than 0.05 versus B12, while one-sided upper limits remain below +0.03 for recorded creatinine rise and creatinine-or-death, below +0.05 for multisource RRT and RRT-or-death, and below +0.03 for recorded terminal/hospice/acute-transfer harm.

A12 must begin strictly before `C=min(X,V.endtime)`. This is falsifiable. It remains an association between recorded policies and bounded record proxies, not a pharmacologic effect, proof of actual extubation or ventilator liberation, renal safety, or a recommendation.

## Falsification and interpretation

Execution, validity, direction and alerts remain separate.

- **Infeasible:** a required source, model, bootstrap or estimator computation fails.
- **Validity-inconclusive:** computation succeeds but any parent gate or new route gate fails, including nonanalyzable ETT confirmation, excessive conflict/unmapped values, wrong gate ordering, or a material route-sensitivity reversal.
- **Adverse-FWER:** validity passes and any parent adverse statistic is Holm-rejected. The route repair adds no outcome family and does not dilute safety monitoring.
- **Supportive-recorded:** validity passes, no adjusted adverse family occurs, every parent airway/renal/terminal/disposition condition passes, and every route analysis passes. The conclusion is limited to the no-recorded-tracheostomy overlap population.
- **Margin-falsified:** no adjusted adverse family and the airway upper limit is at most +0.05. This rejects only the prespecified recorded-proxy margin in the amended population.
- **Statistically inconclusive:** every other feasible, validity-passing result.

Supportive results would strengthen only a recorded association under the amended policy and endpoint. Adverse results would identify a record-defined concern, not prove toxicity. A route-sensitive or nonanalyzable result means MIMIC cannot securely align this extubation endpoint with the intended baseline airway population. Null or wide results are neither equivalence nor safety.
