# Provisional hypothesis: fat distribution separates metabolic from functional/fall risk

Status: provisional design hypothesis, not a computed result and not selected.

## Proposed relationship

Among UK Biobank participants with baseline body-composition MRI and subsequent follow-up, at similar total body fat, higher visceral adiposity will be more specifically associated with subsequent metabolic disease, while higher posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) will be more specifically associated with subsequent functional decline or falls.

The proposed advance is to test whether fat distribution identifies distinct downstream clinical trajectories rather than merely predicting a generic health-risk score. A positive result would motivate targeted body-composition assessment; a null or reversed result would argue against separating these phenotypes for risk stratification.
