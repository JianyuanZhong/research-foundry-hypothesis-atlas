## Clinical question, established evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

The consequential question is:

> Among adults who could have undergone a first eligible HCC liver resection in the 2020–2021 locked test period, does adding semantic information from routinely stored CT/MRI reports allow a fixed-capacity MDT review queue to capture more pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2) than a laboratory-independent structured queue, when all admissible source, clock, linkage, report-visibility and documentary-outcome states are retained?

The output is a historical ranking for possible review, not a treatment recommendation. It cannot justify wider resection, transplantation, systemic or locoregional treatment, neoadjuvant/adjuvant therapy, surveillance, or a claim of improved recurrence, survival, safety, quality of life, cost, or patient benefit.

### Strongest claim supported by current evidence

The inspected HCC catalog, full metadata, table schemas, and source headers establish that:

- `encounters` contains patient/encounter identifiers, age/sex and encounter/admission/discharge time strings;
- `procedures` contains operation labels, start/end strings and a procedure-source field, but not a guaranteed unique operation identifier;
- `examinations` contains accession-like `检查号`, examination label, narrative `检查所见`/`检查诊断`, acquisition-like `开始时间`, and `机器型号`;

- `labs` contains assay and result fields plus one `检验时间` field, but no collection, verification, finalization, release, view, accession, panel or unit field;

The source does not establish that an examination acquisition time equals report finalization, release, or clinician view; that a pathology row belongs to the resection specimen; that the pathology sampling protocol was adequate; or that an M2 lexical interpretation is biologically correct. It contains no raw images/slides, report version history, clinician review/action, recurrence, survival, treatment assignment/effect, harms or costs.

### Hypotheses

The primary capacity hypothesis, **P-Queue**, is tested at fixed review capacities `q in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}` of the state-specific eligible target denominator (with a prespecified integer/tie rule). Let `Top_q(M,s)` be the queue generated by model/action pipeline `M in {S,S0,B}` and `Y_i(s)` the state-specific documentary-M2 indicator. Define capacity-normalized capture `CC_q(M,s)=sum_i a_i^T(s)Y_i(s)I(i in Top_q(M,s))/N_T(s)`. Two paired co-primary contrasts are required in the same state, roster and capacity: semantic attribution `D_q=CC_q(S)-CC_q(S0)` and complete-strategy comparison `C_q=CC_q(S)-CC_q(B)`. The frozen research-relevance margin is five additional documentary-M2 cases per 100 reviews, equivalently `m_q=0.05q` on the target-frame scale: 0.0025, 0.005 and 0.01 at q=0.05, 0.10 and 0.20. P-Queue states that for every terminal state in the certified outer envelope, the simultaneous 97.5% lower confidence limits for both `D_q` and `C_q` exceed `m_q` at all three capacities. Report both scales. This margin is an interpretable research benchmark, not proof that the queue or workload is clinically acceptable; a blinded MDT/workflow panel must endorse capacities and margins before any deployment claim.

A secondary **P-Utility** hypothesis preserves the parent's threshold estimand: the same S model has a lower identified net-benefit endpoint above 0.01 at threshold 0.30 over B, and remains positive at 0.25, 0.275, 0.30, 0.325 and 0.35, with the semantic-null gate passing. This is secondary because no workload or action preference is observed.

A transport hypothesis, **T-Queue**, asks whether at least one prespecified CT-only, MRI-only, or dual-report access vector `(h_C,h_M,h_CM)` supports P-Queue under the same state and uncertainty envelope. This remains an eventual-stored-content/service-level claim until an external report-version final/release/view audit is supplied.

These hypotheses are not conclusions. A high AUC, favorable complete-case estimate, or a positive point estimate cannot establish them.

## Target frame, state construction, and temporal boundaries

The patient identifier is `患者主索引`; all same-encounter joins use `(患者主索引, 就诊号)`. Construct the complete raw ambiguity ledger before viewing pathology grade, report semantics, outcome, model results or period-specific counts. Exact duplicate rows are recorded and removed only by equality over all source columns; all raw row ordinals remain in provenance.

Two hepatobiliary surgeons and one surgical informatician freeze a high-sensitivity procedure-value dictionary and admissible cross-source episode rules before outcome review. The outer dictionary includes partial, segmental, hemihepatic and liver-tumor resections; unanimous explicit exclusions may include transplant-only, biopsy/puncture-only, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ and explicit repeat/recurrence procedures. Disagreements stay in the outer envelope. The adjudicated envelope may remove a clinically impossible linkage only with reviewer identities, blinded reason and raw provenance. No procedure-value frequency is allowed to define eligibility.

Enumerate every defensible source-reconciled operative episode across all procedure rows, including same-day component partitions and adjacent-date alternatives. Do not choose the first raw row. For a linked episode with a valid judged-linked `手麻系统` start, retain that exact start; a case-record `病案手术` date without validated clock is an interval `[d,d+24h)`. Invalid or missing clock states remain unresolved/nonmember alternatives rather than being dropped. The earliest episode is selected separately in each coherent state.

For each state, define `t_op`, `t_dec=t_op-24 hours`, and state-specific eligibility:

- selected episode has an `encounters` row with age `>=18`;
- all pathology rows on the selected index encounter explicitly support HCC under the frozen adjudicated parser;
- the episode lies in the target period;
- no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy occurs in `[t_dec-365 days,t_dec)`, using state-matched `procedures`, `medications`, and `orders`.

Recorded absence is not biological treatment-naivety. Operation and index encounter can change across states, so pathology, report bundle, period, eligibility, treatment history, denominator and outcome must change together.

Use 2015–2018 for development, 2019 for tuning/penalty and calibration decisions, and 2020–2021 as a patient-disjoint locked test. Every patient with any admissible test-period state is quarantined from development and tuning, even if another state would be a nonmember. Rebuild states at locked 12-, 24-, 48- and 72-hour decision buffers. The 24-hour analysis is primary.

The target is the fixed union roster of all patients with at least one outer-envelope test state. In each state, `a_i^T(s)` determines membership and `N_T(s)=sum_i a_i^T(s)` is recomputed. Nonmembers are not coded as negative outcomes. All target-roster patients remain in the uncertainty analysis.

## State-complete report and pathology construction

For examinations, deduplicate exact rows, then group components by `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` before modality classification. A group is one accession-first report unit. Retain all components and their `检查`, `检查所见`, `检查诊断`, `开始时间`, and `机器型号`. Blank accession groups are unresolved and cannot become singleton semantic reports. Exclusive CT, MRI, dual, unresolved and no-report patterns are assigned only by the frozen modality dictionary; conflicting components remain unresolved states rather than being duplicated.

Only accession groups whose acquisition `开始时间` lies in `[t_dec-90 days,t_dec)` enter the eventual-content feature block. This is an acquisition eligibility proxy, not proof of availability. CT/MRI semantic features are prespecified and interpretable, with raw text, negation/uncertainty coding and extraction provenance retained; no unrestricted text or leakage from after `t_dec` is permitted. The missing/no-report state is a real state and contributes to the whole denominator.

For pathology, retain every row on the state-selected index encounter from `病理`, `检查所见`, and `检查诊断`. An anchored parser may assign M0/M1/M2 only when the selected-value expression meets a frozen rule. Conflicting, absent, ungradable or non-anchored records yield `Y=unknown`, not a chosen favorable row. The outer envelope enumerates `Y=0` and `Y=1` where allowed by the pre-outcome ledger; a clinical adjudication envelope may narrow that set only with blinded expert review. The endpoint is documentary M2 on the selected pathology composite, not latent/biological M2.

## Predictor sets and zero-lineage guarantee

`B` is a compact structured, laboratory-independent baseline frozen before outcome access: age and sex from the state-selected encounter; CT/MRI/no-report modality pattern; number of eligible accession-first CT and MRI units; days from the most recent eligible acquisition to `t_dec`; missing acquisition/accession flags; machine-model category; and report-component count. It uses no report-body token, derived lesion measurement, semantic extraction, diagnosis, laboratory value, or post-`t_dec` fact. `S` uses the identical B block plus a prespecified CT/MRI semantic block extracted only from `检查所见` and `检查诊断`: maximum stated lesion diameter, lesion multiplicity, irregular/non-smooth margin, capsule presence/incompleteness, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement, satellite nodules, portal/hepatic vein tumor thrombus or vascular invasion, cirrhosis and ascites, each represented by present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned indicators. Two liver radiologists and a bilingual clinical NLP reviewer must freeze exact Chinese synonyms, negation/uncertainty rules, unit normalization and a stratified blinded validation set before outcomes. Failed semantic validation makes P-Queue inconclusive. `S0` is the same fitted S pipeline with the semantic block masked, not an independently refitted comparator. The primary semantic attribution is `D=S-S0`; complete strategy comparison `C=S-B` is secondary but required.

No field from `labs` enters cohort definition, state generation, predictor values, missingness, calibration, permutation, bootstrap, queue ranking or inference, because `检验时间` does not establish result visibility. A source-lineage test must fail execution if any lab path reaches B, S, S0, outcome, state, queue or uncertainty files. `diagnoses` is corroboration only, never a predictor. Untimed `clinical_documents` is excluded from primary predictors. Identifier-only `vitals`, `transfers` and `front_page` cannot supply payload. ## Fitting, queue rules, and estimands

Fit B, S and the same-fit S0 through the certified state set. Use the parent's exact patient-wise worst-state convex logistic objective for development, tuning and recalibration: an individual contributes the worst admissible state loss, rather than an invented probability over report availability. Normalize by state-specific eligible development membership; no fixed-denominator padding and no complete-case conditioning are permitted. All state transitions used in fitting carry ledger backpointers.

For each locked test state and model, calculate calibrated risk scores. At each capacity q, select exactly the state-specific integer number of eligible patients with highest score. Ties use a frozen hash of `患者主索引` and are reported; tie-breaking cannot use Y or pathology text. For variable denominators, queue capacity and capture are calculated in each state, with a fractional final slot represented explicitly rather than rounded opportunistically. The queue is evaluated on the full target roster with nonmembers contributing zero to both numerator and denominator; the target denominator is state-specific and reported beside every endpoint.

The primary P-Queue endpoints are paired `D_q=S-S0` and `C_q=S-B` documentary-M2 capture differences at each q. S, S0 and B are evaluated on the same state, roster and capacity. S0 scores are produced by masking semantics in the already fitted S pipeline; no S0 refit or recalibration is allowed. Under a state where every semantic feature is masked, S and S0 scores, rankings, tie sets and queues must be identical, so `D_q=0` exactly. Also report precision among reviewed, number needed to review per captured M2, queue overlap, rank correlation, calibration, and absolute score changes, but these are descriptive unless margins are frozen in advance. Compare against flag-all, flag-none, random-with-seed, and a prevalence-ranked/no-feature baseline. A semantic permutation preserves accession, modality, missingness, report length and process strata while breaking semantic content; it is a falsification/null refit, not a rescue.

Use exact state-expanded mixed-integer linear-fractional optimization to obtain lower and upper endpoints for each queue contrast over the outer envelope; binary rank/queue membership, state selection, ties, variable denominators and fractional final slots are represented explicitly. For small fixtures, brute force must equal the optimizer. Do not average state worlds, weight them by an invented availability distribution, or report only the most favorable state.

## Uncertainty, access, and decision-time separation

Use patient-level outer bootstrap with full state regeneration, worst-state refitting, tuning/recalibration, ranking and endpoint recomputation. The family includes all three q values, D, C, default contrasts, four buffer analyses, and prespecified modality/access cells. Control a single simultaneous 97.5% family (or a predeclared alpha-spending equivalent) for the primary envelope; do not combine separately unadjusted model and access confidence statements.

P-Queue requires the simultaneous lower confidence limits for both D_q and C_q to exceed the frozen capacity margin at every q, with no direction reversal in buffer sensitivities, semantic-null rejection, event/extraction/lineage/computation gates passing, and no unresolved state certificate failure. A pattern-access frontier is computed by exact state-expanded optimization over CT-only, MRI-only and dual report availability. It describes the minimum service-level visibility needed for the historical stored-content queue to retain its result; it is not proof that a report was available to clinicians.

An external audit is required for any decision-time statement. It must sample the same version-matched accession-first unit and exact `t_dec` clock, and observe authored/final/release/view timestamps and the body shown before `t_dec`. If the audit fails representativeness, version matching, or joint 97.5% error control, P-Queue may still support eventual stored-content value but all deployability claims are inconclusive.

## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive P-Queue requires both D_q and C_q at all q capacities to pass the simultaneous margin, outer-envelope lower endpoint, same-mask S/S0 identity, null, state-completeness, calibration, event, extraction and queue-capacity gates. The allowed conclusion is that runtime semantic content itself, and the complete semantic strategy relative to the frozen structured baseline, captured more record-defined documentary M2 cases at the same historical review capacity under every certified source reconstruction. It does not establish benefit from review.

Adverse P-Queue requires computation and certificate gates to pass and the simultaneous upper endpoint for either D_q or C_q at a primary capacity to be at or below zero (harmful) or at/below its margin for all admissible states (non-material). A supportive C_q with non-supportive D_q cannot be attributed to runtime semantics and fails P-Queue. A favorable core or adjudicated-only envelope with an adverse outer envelope is adverse/inconclusive for the source-robust claim, not a success. If one capacity is supportive and another adverse, report mixed capacity dependence and reject the all-capacity claim.

Inconclusive results include any envelope interval straddling the required margin, incomplete state reachability, variable-denominator failure, sparse/ungradable outcome strata, failed replay, unavailable exact report version, or a favorable complete-case/AUC result with unfavorable or unidentified queue capture. A positive net-benefit estimate at 0.30 cannot rescue a failed capacity claim; neither can a favorable threshold chosen after seeing outcomes.

A correct computation paired with an unsupported biological-M2, treatment, recurrence, survival, cost, or patient-benefit conclusion must be marked unsupported. The verifier may check state generation, hashes, joins, clock boundaries, no-overlap quarantine, variable denominators, optimizer/brute-force equality, queue capacity, zero lab lineage, null refit, simultaneous bands, and interpretation linkage. It cannot establish operation identity, state completeness beyond the submitted certificate, Chinese semantics, specimen linkage/sampling, report access, audit representativeness, stakeholder margins, clinician action, treatment effects or patient benefit.
