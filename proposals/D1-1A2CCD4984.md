# Fail-closed multicenter transport and documentation-opportunity audit for the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Status, parentage, and substantive advance

This is an independent substantive successor of the two assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It is not a cosmetic restatement. It preserves every Lead-frozen scientific decision: the exact eligible population, first-stay rule, five landmarks, two literal care-plan labels, decision time, inherited exclusions, clinical-time windows, parser, thresholds, all-row competing-exit outcome, repeated-landmark handling, and descriptive noncausal estimand. It does not replace the frozen primary outcome with an observation-weighted or complete-case estimand.

The substantive repair is a stricter multicenter support and audit contract. It separates three questions that can otherwise be conflated:

1. whether the exact label is used with adequate multicenter overlap;
2. whether it adds information about the frozen later structured respiratory-chart trajectory beyond pre-decision state and value-blind documentation opportunity; and
3. whether either result survives held-out-hospital transport, source-specific opportunity residualization, temporal falsification, and exact support gates.

Sparse hospitals are never rescued by smoothing, pooled site intercepts, relabelling, or extrapolation. A pooled result is reported, but it cannot receive a transport or residual-state interpretation unless the prespecified held-out hospital and joint state/opportunity support requirements pass. A failed support or falsification requirement is inconclusive unless a prespecified adverse condition is actually observed. No bedside adjudication is available, and no result can establish SBT delivery, extubation evaluation, readiness, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The completed parent transport audit remains contextual evidence and is not silently re-estimated: it found adverse common-phenotype gates in the frozen cohort, including only 3/74 hospitals with at least ten rows per label, hospital-label dependence, peak-pressure dominance, and site/leave-one-out instability in less dominant proxies. The parent’s independent liberation-marker ascertainment also remains failed because events and adequate observation were sparse. Those are prior observed findings, not new results claimed by this proposal; this experiment tests the narrower residualized structured-chart information question.

## Evidence-supported versus untested claim

The strongest available evidence supports only that the two exact strings are sparse, heterogeneous structured documentation and that apparent label-to-chart associations may be generated by pre-D patient state, source opportunity, hospital/workflow composition, entry-clock behavior, exits, or repeated documentation. The exact strings are not validated bedside actions.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> Among the frozen labelled person-landmarks, after using only pre-D respiratory state proxies and value-blind documentation opportunity learned without hospital identity, does the exact current label add reproducible information about the later independently parsed five-state respiratory-chart trajectory in hospitals held out from fitting and in supported joint state/opportunity cells?

A positive answer would establish only incremental prediction of named structured chart constructs in supported eICU hospitals. A negative answer would be evidence against incremental information beyond the declared proxies. A result that is pooled-positive but unsupported, site-reversing, out-of-range, placebo-positive, or opportunity-process-positive is not evidence of residual patient-state information.

`p_k(a) = N_a^{-1} Σ_i 1{A_i=a,K_i=k}` and `Δ_k = p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`.

`q_c(a) = count(A=a,K=classifiable-c)/count(A=a,K is classifiable)` and `δ_c = q_c(evaluation)-q_c(no_trial)`.

These are fixed-arm-denominator descriptive chart-proxy quantities, not causal effects, treatment effects, or probabilities of clinical benefit. Nested model increments, site summaries, and review-allocation metrics supplement them and cannot replace them.

## Opportunity residualization contract

Every feature has exactly one block in a machine-readable `feature_block_manifest.json`. The primary residualization is cross-fitted leave-one-hospital-out. Each held-out hospital is excluded from all fitting, preprocessing, cutpoint selection, calibration, feature selection, and support decisions. Complete stays, including all retained landmarks, are assigned together; hospital identity and site intercepts are forbidden predictors. Fit deterministic low-dimensional multinomial logistic models with fixed regularization grid, solver, tolerance, and tie-break:

* `M_C`: C;
* `M_O`: C + O, no values and no label;

## Sparse multicenter support and transport envelope

Support is assessed separately for label use, outcome availability, model fitting, each placebo, each landmark, and each joint state/opportunity cell. All thresholds are locked before outcome values are inspected.

This is conditional label mix among the frozen rows, not prevalence among all ventilated patients.
2. **Hospital-level estimability.** A hospital-level arm contrast requires at least 10 outcome-available rows per exact arm and at least 20 total, with at least 10 distinct stays per arm where stay counts are available. Smaller hospitals remain in descriptive counts and all-row accounting, but cannot be pooled into a hospital contrast or treated as negative evidence.
3. **Fold-local range.** For every continuous S/O feature and derived score, record training minimum/maximum, held-out in-range, out-of-range, missing, nonfinite, and parser-invalid counts. Apply training cutpoints without clipping; out-of-range rows remain visible but cannot support transport interpretation. Do not use label, hospital, K, exit, placebo, or effect direction to choose bins.
4. **Joint cells.** From training hospitals only, create fixed empirical tertiles for scalar S and O scores if all have support, otherwise a deterministic median split, otherwise a documented feature-order sparse-cell collapse. A held-out cell is supported only with at least 10 rows in each exact arm, at least 20 rows total, finite training maps, no unresolved block assignment, and at least 10 distinct stays per arm when that count is available. Do not merge, reweight, clip, or impute unsupported cells. Report row-level and stay-level support, exact denominators, and site contribution.
5. **Transport envelope.** A residual-state interpretation requires at least five held-out hospitals with at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows per exact arm, covering at least 50% of supported rows, at least five supported S×O×landmark cells spanning at least five hospitals and both arms, no single hospital or cell contributing more than 25% of supported rows, no more than 25% site directional reversals, and no adverse range gate. A result supported by one hospital, one component, one source, one landmark, an out-of-range group, exits/unobservability, or repeated-landmark persistence alone is not residual patient-state information.

## Falsification suite and fail-closed decision logic

Freeze all tests and code manifests before viewing arm-stratified outcomes. Every test has its own denominator and status. Failure of opportunity or support is inconclusive, not a negative finding.

### Pre-D trajectory placebo

Construct `K_pre` from `P=(D-1440,D-720]` to `B=(D-720,D]` using only respiratoryCharting clinical offsets, respiratoryCare airway corroboration, and the same parser, spacing, and state rules. Use pre-D source fields only; if either state is unsupported, assign `pre_unobservable`, never a classifiable negative. Fit the same held-out M0/M1 definitions and report cell/site metrics. A reproducible label increment on K_pre is adverse to a clean post-D residual-state interpretation; a null is only temporal reassurance.

### Value-blind opportunity placebo

Define `O_pre_shift` from source timing alone: respiratoryCharting has at least two distinct clinical timestamps spanning at least 30 minutes in P, with explicit `unobservable` otherwise. It uses no measurement values, care-plan fields, label semantics, K, exit, or post-D data. A reproducible M1 gain after declared O adjustment is adverse to the claim that the label increment is not documentation-process residual. A null does not validate bedside meaning.

### Future-anchor lagged-label placebo

Set `D_pl = D+720` without changing the primary row, D, K, or Δ. Construct the same parser-based trajectory after D_pl, with competing-exit precedence and explicit unresolved statuses. The original label predates the placebo anchor and is treated as historical exposure. Overlap with the primary endpoint, competing exit, or invalid anchor is recorded, not silently removed. A reproducible lagged-label increment is adverse to temporal-specificity and interpreted as persistence/site process/evolving state, never as validation. Run a first-landmark-only sensitivity and cluster by `patientunitstayid`.

### Conditional label nulls

Use deterministic seed `61061`. First, permute labels only within held-out hospital-by-landmark-by-training-S/O-cell blocks, preserving exact arm counts and stay assignment. Second, for complete stays, permute entire label vectors only among identical label-independent signatures consisting of all retained landmarks, frozen S/O bins, and pre-D availability/status. Signatures contain no K, exit, post-D observability, placebo target, or care-plan field beyond the label being permuted. Assert unchanged stay IDs, signatures, vector lengths, hospital membership, per-landmark/cell arm counts, and no stay splitting. Attempt at least 1,000 valid arrangements where feasible or enumerate all unique arrangements when fewer exist. Fewer than 20 valid global arrangements, fewer than five hospitals, lost supported cells, or any invariant failure is inconclusive. If the observed M1−M0 increment is typical of either conditional null, it is adverse to incremental residual patient-state information.

Retain inherited broad hospital-by-landmark opportunity-preserving permutation, site-balanced envelope, leave-one-hospital-out influence, first-versus-repeated landmark persistence, whole-stay sequence null when exactly feasible, source/component ablations, exit decomposition, and clinical-time versus as-entered sensitivity. These are diagnostics and cannot change the frozen primary estimand.

## Uncertainty, verification, and fail-closed gates

Use fixed-seed `61061` stay-cluster bootstrap, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates, resampling complete retained stays within hospital with all landmarks, labels, predictors, K/H statuses, and placebo statuses together. Refit the full held-out pipeline and recompute support, range, cells, null compatibility, transport, and review metrics. Never use a row bootstrap or nest permutations inside bootstrap replicates. If fewer than 500 valid replicates are obtained, uncertainty-dependent claims are inconclusive.

A run is computationally invalid on source/schema/catalog/member/hash mismatch; missing or divergent inherited artifacts; duplicate or non-one-to-one joins; wrong labels, landmarks, D, parser boundaries, inequalities, thresholds, units, or K/exit precedence; post-D leakage; feature overlap between blocks; fold or stay leakage; placebo anchor leakage; failed permutation invariant; unsupported action tables; or missing machine-readable linkage from any conclusion to its output, denominator, support status, and interval. Stop interpretation and emit `verification.status=invalid`.

Decision gates are mechanically applied in this order:

1. verify source, schema, artifacts, headers, joins, parser, times, and K identity;
2. verify frozen all-row p_k and Δ_k before any model result;
3. classify feasibility and each source/landmark/cell support status;
4. classify transport and range envelope;
5. classify pre-D, opportunity, lagged-label, conditional-null, exit, and persistence falsification statuses;
6. classify uncertainty/calibration status;
7. only then permit a narrative label-information interpretation.

Any invalid prerequisite fail-closes all downstream interpretation. Any unsupported or underpowered gate is `inconclusive`, not adverse. An adverse placebo, conditional null, site reversal, range failure, or opportunity-process gain blocks the corresponding stronger interpretation. Supportive results establish only incremental prediction of named structured chart constructs in supported held-out hospitals. Adverse results mean the label is not robust for that construct or remains compatible with state, opportunity, site, exit, persistence, or repeated documentation. No result establishes actual bedside action or patient outcome.
