# Context transport of acute hemoglobin-decline/transfusion-risk heterogeneity in MIMIC-IV

## Scientific question and deliverable

This is an evolution of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent asks whether a recent acute recorded Hb fall versus stable low Hb changes the observed association between a recorded PRBC start and deterioration under record-defined physiologic stress. The new unresolved question is distinct:

> Is that prespecified observed-risk heterogeneity reproducible across ICU care contexts, after separating patient physiology from care/documentation intensity, or is it primarily a unit-specific measurement and practice signal?

This matters before using an EHR phenotype to enrich a transfusion trial or prioritize prospective adjudication. A context-stable signal would justify a common record-defined hypothesis for multi-unit adjudication; a context interaction would identify where ischemia, bleeding, transfusion intent and observation processes must be separately validated. A context-specific signal is clinically informative even if it prevents a universal rule.

The future solver must newly construct the cohort, fit a pooled context-interaction model and a hierarchical partial-pooling alternative, estimate context-specific and transport-standardized contrasts with uncertainty, and produce a complete not-estimable report if support or ascertainment gates fail. No prevalence, effect, model result or clinical conclusion is claimed here.

## Evidence boundary and demonstrations

The strongest available evidence is availability, not support for the hypothesis: the read-only MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains the required longitudinal Hb, ICU physiology, PRBC input, procedure, transfer, admission, patient and death fields. The natural-history demonstration was read through its local article extraction and methods summary; it verifies that dated structured histories can support bounded chronological adaptation, but its UKB/Danish external validation is unavailable here. The Bayesian demonstration was read through its local article and methods summary; it supports considering a latent longitudinal/partial-pooling model, but verified genetics and the three biobanks are absent. The cancer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; only its supplement was inspected, showing lab-only/image-only/full-model ablations and baseline comparisons. MIMIC-CXR images and raw waveforms are unavailable, so the multimodal demonstration is not reproduced. These are method/context demonstrations, not evidence that this hypothesis is true.

The expert MIMIC seed on transfusion heterogeneity was assessed repairable and developed into this lineage. Other MIMIC seeds remain useful untested inputs but are not silently substituted for this question.

## Population and time zero

Join `icu/icustays` to `hosp/admissions` on `subject_id,hadm_id`, and `hosp/patients` on `subject_id`. Retain adults (`anchor_age >=18`, preserving MIMIC's >89 code 91), order ICU stays by `intime,stay_id`, and use the earliest qualifying ICU episode per subject. A one-episode-per-admission sensitivity is allowed but all subject episodes remain in one partition.

Define `t0` as the first valid item-51222 hematology Hb in the selected ICU interval at least 12 h after `intime`, with finite `valuenum`, accepted g/dL `valueuom`, `storetime <= charttime=t0`, and 6.5 <= Hb <= 8.0. Deduplicate by `specimen_id` using a pre-outcome deterministic rule (charttime, storetime, labevent_id), recording ambiguity. Let `tau=t0+6h`. Exclude death/ICU exit before `tau`, reversed/missing times and unresolved exposure ascertainment; retain all exclusions in the flow.

Exposure T=1 is any administration-compatible PRBC input start in [t0,tau), restricted to items 220996/225168/226368/227070, positive finite mL amount or totalamount, finite starttime, treatment-compatible status and no order-only row. T=0 requires complete ascertainment and no qualifying start. Audit status, amount units, endtime, storetime, open-bag/continuation, order/link identifiers and rewritten/overlapping rows. Exclude PRBC active at t0, a qualifying start in [t0-12h,t0), procedure item 229620 in that interval, and a comparable preceding >=2,000 mL PRBC proxy when measurable. Call this “no observed recent PRBC/massive-transfusion proxy,” never “nonbleeding.” OR/PACU exclusion and item-225168-only are sensitivities.

Trajectory G uses the nearest distinct prior item-51222 specimen in [t0-24h,t0), 6–24 h before t0, with valid g/dL and storetime <=t0. `delta_hb=Hb(t0)-Hb(prior)`: acute-fall if <=-1.0 g/dL, stable-low if |delta|<0.5, otherwise indeterminate. Freeze this rule before using T, outcome or context. Report continuous-slope and 0.5/1.5-g/dL sensitivities and item 50811 sensitivity.

Record-defined stress S is measured in the 12 h before t0, using only chart/store-valid rows and valid interval overlaps: high requires one supply plus one demand/support component or two independent supply components; low requires valid supply and demand/support observations and at least two physiologic observations; otherwise indeterminate. Supply is lactate >=2 (items above), repeated MAP <65, or pressor intervals (221289/229617, 221662, 221749/229630/229631/229632, 221906, 222315). ## New context estimand

Define context C from `hosp/transfers` interval `intime <= t0 < outtime`, joined by `subject_id,hadm_id`, with tie/overlap audit; do not use terminal `last_careunit`. To remove the critique-identified ambiguity, primary context labels are mutually exclusive exact-label groups, frozen before outcomes: medical-only = `Medical Intensive Care Unit (MICU)`; mixed medical-surgical = `Medical/Surgical Intensive Care Unit (MICU/SICU)`; cardiovascular = `Cardiac Vascular Intensive Care Unit (CVICU)` or `Coronary Care Unit (CCU)`; surgical/trauma = `Surgical Intensive Care Unit (SICU)` or `Trauma SICU (TSICU)`; neuro = exact `Neuro Surgical Intensive Care Unit (Neuro SICU)`, `Neuro Intermediate`, or `Neuro Stepdown`. No exact label belongs to two groups. Other labels (including PACU, Medicine, Surgery/Trauma and unresolved overlaps) are reported and excluded from the primary context estimand, never silently merged. If transfer coverage is absent, use `first_careunit` only as a prespecified sensitivity, not primary. Create a separate pre-t0 documentation-intensity vector from counts and time-since-last-observation in the permitted Hb, lactate, MAP, HR, RR, FiO2, PEEP and pressor sources; it is not a physiologic covariate. The context analysis is a secondary conditional deliverable: first establish that the parent pooled Hb-trajectory/transfusion estimand is estimable under its gates (whether its interval supports, nulls or reverses the claim); if not estimable, report context contrasts as unavailable rather than treating context portability as established.

The outcome window is strictly (tau,tau+24h]. Construct first mutually exclusive D (earliest in-hospital death), E (new invasive ventilation, new target pressor, or new renal replacement), X (indexed ICU exit before D/E), N (observed through window without D/E), U (unknown/irreconcilable follow-up), using admissions death fields, icustays, procedureevents and interventions with the parent’s no-pre-existing-event rules. Primary Y=D/E; report D and E separately, X/N/U and later T=0 PRBC starts.

For each context c and stress/trajectory cell (s,g), estimate the held-out model-standardized observed-risk contrast Delta_{c,s,g}: predicted 24 h Y risk under recorded T=1 versus T=0 labels with each episode’s pre-t0 covariates held fixed, standardized to common-support target subjects. This is not a counterfactual treatment effect. Primary context heterogeneity is:
- K_c = Delta_{c,high,acute} - Delta_{c,high,stable}, with cardiovascular, medical and surgical/trauma contrasts prespecified;
- K_transport = max(K_c)-min(K_c) across supported contexts;
- secondary context-by-T-by-G-by-S interaction, plus D/E decompositions.
A context-stable result means all supported K_c retain direction and the simultaneous interval for K_transport lies within [-0.05,0.05]; a context-shift result means the simultaneous interval excludes 0 and at least one context contrast differs from the pooled contrast by >=0.05 with overlap/ascertainment gates met. Thresholds are scientific decision thresholds, not clinical utility claims.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

All methods share identical cohort, source whitelist, timing, split, outcomes, support gates and test sets.

B_context is a transparent penalized four-bin competing-risk discrete-time hazard model for D, E and X. It includes T, G, S, all prespecified T×G×S terms, context C and T×G×S×C, current/prior Hb and gap, pre-t0 physiology/severity, bleeding/operative proxies if frozen, and documentation-intensity masks/counts/time gaps. Training-only imputation, scaling, splines and penalty selection are used. It yields standardized Delta/K estimates, calibration, Brier score, overlap and ESS.

M_context is a substantive hierarchical varying-coefficient competing-risk model: the context-specific T×G×S coefficient vectors receive partial-pooling priors around a common mean, with a separate observation-process/documentation component and the same physiologic inputs. Fit only in development eras; use posterior/sandwich uncertainty and held-out predictions. It can reveal whether apparent unit differences are systematic after accounting for sparse cells and whether physiology and documentation have separable context effects—information a fully pooled or unpooled B_context loses. A small global prediction gain without stable context contrasts is not success. A masked documentation-only model and a physiology-only model are required decompositions, not competing scientific endpoints.

Use subject-level 70/15/15 development split and fit development anchor-year groups 2008–2016; lock 2017–2019 calibration and 2020–2022 transport without refitting. Stratification uses only pre-index T/G/S/C/Y and anchor-year group; all episodes of a subject remain together. Report supported context cells or not-estimable transport rather than pooling unsupported eras/units.

## Support, uncertainty and falsification

Before interpreting any context contrast require each primary context×G×S cell >=50 episodes and >=20 known Y events; each G state/context >=10 D and >=10 E events for components; >=80% known outcomes; exposure common support [0.05,0.95] after only prespecified trimming; ESS >=100 per context×trajectory state; weighted standardized differences <=0.25; <=5% unresolved PRBC status/unit among otherwise eligible exposures; zero post-t0 leakage or impossible time/unit failures. Require context coverage and no context defined by post-t0 fields. Failure is an explicit not-estimable result.

Use 1,000 subject-level bootstrap refits where feasible, report completed/failure counts and simultaneous 95% intervals for pooled K, each K_c and K_transport. Report calibration, Brier, PPV/event capture at q=5/10/20%, missingness and documentation distributions, context overlap and transport drift.

Locked falsifications: permute T within Hb/G/S/context/era bands; permute G within Hb/T/S/context/era; shuffle Hb order in M_context; use documentation-only predictors; predict pre-t0 deterioration using future T; use non-PRBC blood-product rows as a process negative control; compare early placebo trajectories and shifted exposure labels; leave-one-context-out transport; charttime-only availability sensitivity; item-50811, threshold, PRBC-item and OR/PACU sensitivities. Any strong context effect in documentation-only or process controls, or failure to collapse under permutation, undermines physiologic interpretation.

Supportive evidence requires gates, calibrated held-out predictions, concordant B_context/M_context context contrasts, a primary simultaneous interval outside the predeclared null region, permutation collapse, no comparable documentation-only result, and direction retained in at least one locked later era and leave-one-context-out evaluation. It supports prospective adjudication and context-specific trial design, not a transfusion benefit claim.

Adverse evidence includes a reproducible null, reversal, context-specific instability, documentation-only parity, permutation persistence, pre-t0 future-treatment association, poor calibration or loss of overlap. It argues against a portable record-defined stratifier. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse context cells, high indeterminate flow, unknown outcomes, unresolved PRBC units/status, failed ESS/overlap, unstable bootstrap or hierarchical convergence, or temporal transport failure; no clinical action follows.
