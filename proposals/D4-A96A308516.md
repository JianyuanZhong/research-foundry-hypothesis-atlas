## Scientific question and substantive change

The parent asks whether a direction-certified deterioration vector adds fixed-capacity review value beyond current biomarker levels and repeat spacing. Its primary estimand can fail closed because the local catalog does not certify clinical worsening directions for all eight fields. This successor changes the information source, not merely the model: it tests whether direction-free movement between two comparable assessments—overall instability and discordance in which fields moved—changes the composition of a fixed-size review list.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among participants in the parent’s frozen central current-risk band, adding direction-free prior/current biomarker instability and cross-field discordance to current levels and repeat spacing increases held-out capture and yield of the subsequent recorded target event when exactly K review slots are available, and the increment is stable when an early-fitted model is applied without refitting to the 2013 assessment cohort.

The primary signal does not say that a larger or smaller value is worse. For each field, it measures absolute displacement between prior and current percentile positions in a fold-local reference distribution. The clinical decision is conditional review prioritization after a current assessment, not whether ordering a repeat test causes benefit. A supportive result would justify prospective evaluation of whether repeat measurement is useful for identifying people needing review; it would not establish disease progression, treatment benefit, or clinical utility.

The substantive advance over the parent is an estimand that remains defined when worsening directions are unavailable. It asks whether the fact and pattern of change—not an unsupported biological interpretation of its sign—adds information beyond the current panel and elapsed interval. It also separates uniform movement across fields from discordant movement concentrated in only one or a few fields.

## Evidence-supported and unresolved claims

The strongest available local evidence is structural:

- the catalog exposes one-to-one eid-keyed UKB tables for population, assessment, biological samples, and health outcomes;
- all 16 repeated cells for the eight fields at instances -0.0 and -1.0 are present in biological_samples;
- assessment fields 53-0.0 and 53-1.0 are present;
- health_outcomes contains 259 code/date pairs, 41270-0.j with 41280-0.j for every j=0,...,258, and death dates 40000-0.0 and 40000-1.0;
- the source audit and parent design require positional pairing because outcome arrays may be unsorted or contain repeated dates.

These facts do not establish analyte names, units, valid ranges, missing-value meanings, specimen collection timing, cross-instance comparability, measurement error, disease onset, complete follow-up, or clinical worsening directions. The catalog says field-instance encoding does not provide these semantics. No field direction is needed by this successor, but field identity, comparable units/ranges, specimen timing, and missing-value meaning remain essential.

The experiment will test, rather than assume:

1. whether absolute percentile displacement and its cross-field pattern add fixed-K capture/yield beyond current values and L=(T1-T0)/365.2425;
2. whether any increment is distinguishable from current risk, repeat-selection, interval length, regression to the mean, assay drift, treatment, or calendar-practice change;
3. whether transparent and matched nonlinear models show the same signal across early folds and the no-refit 2013 application.

No event count, fitted estimate, causal effect, clinical recommendation, or external-validation claim is made here.

Set H to T1 plus five calendar years with explicit leap-day handling. Exclude a participant with a qualifying target pair on or before T1 or death on or before T1. ## Direction-free representation

Within each training fold, fit an empirical percentile map F_j to eligible training-fold T0 values after approved missing/range rules. Do not use outcomes, late data, held-out fold values, or late quantiles. Clip values outside the training reference range to the corresponding endpoint and record clipping. For each participant define:

u_ij = |F_j(x_i,current) - F_j(x_i,prior)|, for j=1,...,8.

Thus u_ij is a unit-invariant absolute movement measure and contains no worsening sign. Define B_i=mean_j(u_ij), overall instability burden, and H_i=SD_j(u_ij), cross-field discordance of movement magnitudes. B is high when many fields move substantially; H is high when movement is concentrated unevenly. These are summaries, not diagnoses. A secondary field-resolved analysis uses all eight u_ij values without assigning biological meaning to any field.

This still requires comparable measurements and timing. It does not solve regression to the mean, analytical variation, treatment, or specimen-handling change. Missing field identity/units/ranges or incompatible timing is a primary fail-closed condition. Raw unsigned differences, signed worsening directions, and arbitrary field-specific thresholds cannot rescue failure.

## Selection, split, risk band, and fixed capacity

Produce a complete flow from population through age/sex, T0/T1, interval, 16-cell completeness, endpoint eligibility, ascertainment, era, A-band, policy eligibility, and T/D/N outcomes. Report counts for malformed positions, targets, deaths, exclusions, clipping, semantic/timing failures, early/late A sizes, K, selected counts, and support failures.

Among T0-complete participants define S=1 as valid paired T1 plus complete panel and accepted endpoint eligibility. Fit an early-only regularized selection model using pre-T1 T0 panel values, age, sex, and T0 calendar year. Apply it unchanged to late T0-complete participants. Use stabilized weights P(S=1)/p-hat(S=1|X), truncated at early 1st and 99th percentiles. Report overlap, maximum weight, standardized differences, and effective sample size. This is a missing-at-random sensitivity analysis, not causal correction.

Use one deterministic participant-level five-fold hash split on eid within early data. Every transformation, percentile map, imputation decision, regularization choice, hyperparameter, calibration, selection model, and cutpoint is training-fold-only. Early out-of-fold predictions alone define the frozen risk band.

Fit M0 using current eight-field values, age, sex, and T1 calendar year; obtain held-out T/D/N probabilities. Define q20 and q80 from eligible early out-of-fold M0 target probabilities: L is r<q20, A is q20<=r<q80, U is r>=q80. Apply the early cutpoints unchanged to late predictions. No late outcome, instability prediction, or permuted field defines A.

Set K=floor(0.10*n_A,early) before policy-outcome inspection. Require K>=40 and n_A,late>=K. Use exactly this K in both eras, rank by held-out target probability, and break ties by deterministic eid hash. Parent within-Q and equal-quota policies are secondary continuity scenarios. Require at least 100 T events, finite weights, adequate overlap, and late weighted ESS >=200 and at least half the unweighted ESS in each retained descriptive Q cell; failed gates make the affected inference inconclusive.

## Matched models and scientific comparison

All models use the same eligible people, endpoint, split, frozen A, K, held-out evaluation, and uncertainty procedure.

- M0: current panel + age + sex + T1 year; defines A.
- M1a, transparent baseline: M0 inputs plus L, regularized multinomial logistic T/D/N model with a training-only penalty grid.
- M1u, transparent direction-free model: M1a inputs plus B and H; report coefficients and partial effects.
- M2a, matched nonlinear baseline: shallow gradient-boosted multinomial T/D/N model with exactly M1a inputs and a small prespecified grid.
- M2u, matched nonlinear instability model: the identical booster family, grid, split, target, and evaluation with exactly M1u inputs.
- M1U/M2U, secondary field-resolved pair: M1a/M2a inputs plus the eight u_ij values.
- M1b, parent continuity model: M1a plus the eight literal prior levels.

M1u is the primary comparison because it tests the interpretable question of whether direction-free instability/discordance adds value beyond current levels and spacing. M2u can reveal nonlinear thresholds or B/H interactions that an additive model averages away. An improvement in M2u alone is not evidence for worsening or a biological mechanism. Field-resolved results are secondary and cannot override semantic, timing, endpoint, or ascertainment gates.

## Estimands and uncertainty

For model M and era e, let R_Mi be held-out target probability and B_Mi(K) membership among the K largest predictions in A. Report unweighted estimates and separately the frozen selection-weighted sensitivity:

C_A(M,e) = sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T) B_Mi(K) / sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T)

P_A(M,e) = sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T) B_Mi(K) / sum_i w_i B_Mi(K).

The primary contrasts are Delta_C_instability(e)=C_A(M1u,e)-C_A(M1a,e) and Delta_P_instability(e)=P_A(M1u,e)-P_A(M1a,e). The primary hypothesis is Delta_C_instability(late)>0.05, with a two-sided 95% interval whose lower limit exceeds the prespecified five-percentage-point operational margin, no early-to-late direction reversal, and tau_C_instability=Delta_C_instability(late)-Delta_C_instability(early) within [-0.05,0.05]. The margin is a research decision margin, not a clinical-benefit threshold.

Report K, reviewed/non-reviewed T/D/N frequencies, capture/yield numerators and denominators, missed-target rate, D capture/yield, policy movement, and weighted results. Report M2u-M2a and M2U-M2a contrasts, Brier/log loss, calibration, fold stability, and stable B/H or field patterns. Report M0 versus M1a for spacing, M1u versus M0 for total augmentation, and M1b versus M1a for parent continuity.

Use paired participant bootstrap intervals with 2,000 replicates, resampling within fixed era-by-Q cells while retaining split, endpoint, A/Q labels, K, predictions, selections, and weights. Report failed-support replicates and use a declared simultaneous interval or multiplicity adjustment. A secondary full-pipeline bootstrap may refit early processing and recompute cutpoints/K; label it separately because it includes training uncertainty. Report multiclass Brier/log loss, one-vs-rest calibration intercept/slope, reliability, resolution, observed/predicted frequencies, fold contrasts, overlap/ESS, and held-out predictions for all models.

Run: endpoint-label permutations within fold/era; B/H permutations within era and frozen A/Q strata preserving current values, L, missingness, and capacity; u-vector permutations; prior-panel permutations before calculating u; parent-level permutations; early/late label permutations; random-K comparison; deliberately broken same-position pairing with mandatory failure; verification that independent sorting is rejected; complete-case versus fold-local missingness; and calibration, resolution, clipping, fold-instability, tie, overlap, ESS, and policy arithmetic checks.

If the primary increment persists under repeated B/H permutation beyond the margin, the instability attribution is falsified. If a prior/current pairing null retains the gain, the representation or leakage control is invalid. Endpoint or era permutations yielding systematic gains invalidate the implementation. Random-K outperforming the model is an implementation warning.

## Interpretation and limitations

Supportive evidence requires every source, join, endpoint, death, semantic, timing, ascertainment, support, and capacity gate; identical feasible K; calibrated held-out predictions; positive M1u-minus-M1a late capture above 0.05 with the complete interval above the margin; no temporal reversal; consistent weighted direction; stable folds; and null permutations that remove the signal. This supports only a conditional, capacity-normalized association between a verified two-assessment direction-free representation and capture of the specified recorded inpatient circulatory endpoint in the supported UKB A-band.

Adverse evidence includes no increment, reduced capture/yield, gain explained by spacing or prior levels, disappearance under weighting or instability permutation, poor calibration, fold instability, temporal reversal, or invalid null controls. This weakens the case for a repeat-derived signal for this task, not for every population or workflow.

Inconclusive evidence includes missing field/timing certification, malformed arrays, death disagreement, incomplete ascertainment, sparse events, K infeasibility, poor ESS/overlap, broad intervals, unstable percentiles, conflicting missingness sensitivities, or inability to separate movement from regression to the mean, treatment, assay drift, repeat-selection bias, or calendar change.

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema integrity, joins, flow, endpoint construction, event ordering, representation construction, fits, predictions, frozen A/K, list arithmetic, calibration, uncertainty, ESS, and permutation behavior. Clinical review or another study is required for analyte meaning beyond the dictionary, specimen validity, disease onset, outpatient disease, severity, treatment effects, action utility, clinical thresholds, external validity, and prospective policy benefit. Two measurements do not identify a turning point or establish a mechanism. No automatic verifier can establish those claims. The 2013 cohort is internal temporal application, not external validation.

## Alternatives, disposition, and future deliverable

M1a is the direct current-assessment counterfactual. M1u tests the new instability estimand. M2u tests nonlinear thresholds/interactions using the same information. M1U/M2U test field concentration without mechanism claims. M1b, fixed-K, Q strata, equal-quota, and the selection audit are retained for continuity. Signed deterioration is deferred because worsening directions are unverified; raw differences are deferred because units and scales can dominate; latent, mechanistic, sequence, genetic, imaging, kidney, and multimorbidity branches are deferred for missing semantics, chronology, modalities, or adjudicated endpoints.

The solver must verify all bindings/gates; construct fold-local u/B/H; fit M0, M1a, M1u, M2a, M2u, M1U/M2U, and M1b; create held-out early/late predictions and identical-K lists; estimate capture/yield, transport, calibration, uncertainty, weighting, and nulls; and finish with linked estimates or a reproducible fail-closed audit. A source count or isolated fit is not completion.
