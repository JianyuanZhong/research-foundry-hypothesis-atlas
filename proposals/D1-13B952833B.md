# Margin-calibrated frame-uncertainty bounds for preoperative documentary-M2 triage

## Episode-16 advance and clinical question

The clinical question remains narrow: 24 hours before an adult patient's first eligible liver resection for retrospectively pathology-confirmed hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), can routinely stored CT/MRI report semantics materially improve a laboratory-independent flag for multidisciplinary review of pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)?

The flag is historical risk classification. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy, systemic or locoregional therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

This child preserves the Episode-15 source-reconciled operative episodes, interval-valued case-record clocks, accession-first report units, all-row pathology composite, 24-hour decision boundary and 12/48/72-hour stress tests, 2015–2018 development/2019 tuning/2020–2021 locked test, patient-wise robust fitting, zero laboratory lineage, same-fit S0 ablation, independent B baseline, D=(S,S0) and C=(S,B), pattern access frontier, simultaneous >0.01 margin inference, external exact-version audit, and strict claim limits.

It repairs one consequential population/estimand defect. The parent excludes patients whose admissible operation-linkage/clock states change index encounter, analysis period, or prior-treatment eligibility, permits up to 5% such exclusions, and mentions unspecified extreme-denominator sensitivity. A frequency gate is not calibrated to the clinical margin: at thresholds 0.25–0.35, each unknown paired net-benefit contribution can lie between -1 and +1, so even 1% unconstrained exclusions can erase a 0.01 margin. This child makes the stable-frame estimand explicit and adds a prespecified, simultaneously inferred worst-case margin-erosion bound before any conclusion is extended to the broader documentation-union frame.

**Strongest claim supported now:** the data and literature make a historical documentary-M2 information-value experiment important and technically plausible while demonstrating material source-clock ambiguity. They do not show that report semantics add value.

**Unresolved stable-frame hypothesis P-S:** in the locked 2020–2021 period-stable, index-stable, eligibility-stable frame, complete eventual stored CT/MRI semantics produce simultaneous one-sided 95% lower limits above 0.01 net-benefit units at p=0.30 for D and C and for S versus flag-all and flag-none, with D/C point lower bounds positive at p=0.25, 0.275, 0.30, 0.325, and 0.35.

**Unresolved frame-transport hypothesis P-U:** after assigning every otherwise eligible frame-uncertain patient the worst possible paired contribution and retaining that patient in the denominator, the simultaneous lower margin remains above 0.01 for all four required contrasts at p=0.30. P-U asks whether P-S is robust to the unresolved population boundary; it does not pretend to recover true operation identity.

**Unresolved pattern-access hypothesis T-S:** within the stable frame, at least one prespecified minimum CT-only/MRI-only/dual-report access vector preserves the same margins under adversarial report visibility and documentary outcome assignment. It remains a stable-frame service-level requirement, not evidence of actual access.

Resolving P-U matters because a small, outcome- or workflow-dependent excluded fraction can overturn a small net-benefit margin while leaving AUC and the stable-frame point estimate apparently favorable.

## Models, estimands, bounds, and inference

B is elastic-net logistic regression with age, sex, CT/MRI acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category, and—only when an accession is exposed—frozen lesion diameter, lesion-count category and explicit macrovascular thrombus with unknown indicators. S is exactly B plus present/absent/unknown capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout and peritumoral hypointensity. S0 is never refit: use S's coefficients, preprocessing, penalty and recalibration while replacing only the semantic block by its state-zero vector. Require exact `S=S0` whenever semantics are zero. W and A=W+the identical semantic block are secondary workflow diagnostics; unrestricted text is a ceiling only.

Fit/tune/recalibrate each model with the parent's patient-wise maximum logistic loss over each stable patient's documentary outcome, operation-clock and visibility states; rebuild preprocessing and optimization in every resample. Equal-state or observed-eventual-text weighting is forbidden.

At threshold p, `w=p/(1-p)`, action `I_Mi(s,p)=1[score_Mi(s)>=p]`, and paired contribution
`d_i^j(y,s,p)=[y-w(1-y)] [I_M1i(s,p)-I_M0i(s,p)]`
for D=(S,S0), C=(S,B), S-versus-all and S-versus-none. Shared states are mandatory. Stable-frame sharp lower/upper endpoints optimize each sum over feasible clock/visibility/outcome states and divide by N.

For p in 0.25–0.35, `w<1` and every otherwise unconstrained paired contribution is bounded by [-1,1]. Define conservative union-frame endpoints for each contrast:
`L_U=(N*L_S-M)/(N+M)`, `U_U=(N*U_S+M)/(N+M)`.
These keep every uncertain patient in the denominator and assign the worst/best possible contribution. They remain valid when some union states would not truly be target members; a sharper state-specific variable-denominator analysis may be reported only as secondary and cannot rescue P-U. Verify the envelope against exhaustive synthetic enumeration. Report the minimum stable-frame lower endpoint required to survive M: `0.01 + 1.01*M/N`.

Retain the parent's exact C/M/CM stable-frame visibility frontier on h in {0,.05,...,1}, fixed g=0 whole-N contribution, dual-report CT-only/MRI-only/both states, coordinatewise all-higher-coverage closure, and Pareto-minimal support antichain. Pattern-changing clock states are unconstrained worst contributions and do not satisfy access counts. T-S requires at least 95% invariant patterns and each claimed pattern N>=100 with >=20 V=1 M2.

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps stratified by period, rebuilding frames, N, M, preprocessing, robust fits, S0, sharp endpoints, union envelopes and frontiers. In each replicate all rows/states for a sampled patient travel together; any patient with a possible test state is excluded from resampled development/tuning. Construct one frozen one-sided simultaneous family over all P-S endpoints, P-U endpoints, frontier cells and p=0.25–0.35 requirements; lower confidence limits—not point estimates—must exceed 0.01 at p=.30. More than 2.5% failed replicates invalidates support. Use 1,000 process-matched development/tuning semantic-block permutations; all-stored D must exceed the 97.5th null percentile. Label permutation must be null; post-cutoff injection, split-accession and exact-midnight substitutions must trigger controls.

Feasibility gates retain test N>=1,000; >=75 adjudicated V=1 M2; V=1>=85%; complete clock/state enumeration and adjudication; unresolved clinical episode identity explicitly counted in M; nonempty text >=80%; size/count extraction >=70%; extraction reliability thresholds; robust-fit convergence; complete inference; and zero forbidden/lab lineage. No frequency cap on M substitutes for P-U.

## Results and falsification

- **Supportive P-S only:** all gates pass and stable simultaneous lower limits exceed 0.01 for D, C and both defaults at p=.30, D/C point lower bounds are positive across .25–.35, and semantic-null and sensitivity gates pass. Conclude only favorable historical information value in the explicitly stable documentary-M2 frame.
- **Supportive P-U:** P-S passes and every corresponding union-envelope simultaneous lower limit exceeds 0.01. Only then may the stored-content conclusion be extended to the documentation-union first-resection frame; still no report-availability, treatment, or benefit claim.
- **Supportive T-S without audit:** P-S passes and the stable-frame frontier support set is nonempty. Conclude only that the stated access vectors would suffice in the stable frame.
- **Adverse:** with feasibility intact, a simultaneous upper limit for required P-S D/C is <=0.01, semantic D is nonpositive, defaults are not beaten, the semantic null is not exceeded, or the stable frontier is empty. If P-S passes but the P-U upper limit is <=0.01, that specifically falsifies robustness to frame uncertainty.
- **Mixed:** D supportive/C adverse means favorable within-S semantic action changes without superiority to B. C supportive/D adverse cannot be called semantic gain. P-S supportive/P-U inconclusive or adverse means frame selection is consequential and no broader-frame conclusion is permitted.
- **Inconclusive:** confidence limits cross decision boundaries; event, extraction, adjudication, solver, resampling or leakage gates fail; or P-U lower and upper limits straddle 0.01. AUC, point estimates, unrestricted text, naive timestamps, or low M/N cannot rescue a claim.

Actual decision-time value additionally requires representative source-system records linking the same operations and accession IDs to validated operation clocks, exact report versions/bodies and authored/final/release and preferably clinician-view times. Allocate one-sided familywise alpha .025 to Harbor model/frontier support and .025 to simultaneous audit coverage bounds. Conditional deployment language requires the audit access vector inside the 97.5% stable support set, P-U support, and source-clock/linkage discrepancies within frozen gates. The configured snapshot cannot provide this evidence.
