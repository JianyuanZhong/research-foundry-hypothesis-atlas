# Care-transition robustness of fixed-capacity lactate-by-measured-perfusion monitoring

## Status, parent and actual scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's first eligible live ICU-exit cohort, exact one-hour buffer, dual clinical/recording-time feature rule, persistent-lactate by measured-perfusion phenotype, R/D/S/N/U competing endpoint, observation-era transport split, fixed-capacity queue, uncertainty and falsification rules. It does not replace the parent's legacy-versus-contemporary transport question.

The remaining clinical-decision uncertainty is whether the fixed 10% review signal is interpretable during a care transition. A transfer can alter measurement intensity, the meaning of a blood pressure/pressor record, and the operational consequence of a high-risk score. An era comparison alone cannot tell whether an apparent transport failure is a transition/handoff phenomenon or merely a change in documentation practice.

The new, prespecified stress is:

> Among contemporary MIMIC-IV patients at the first eligible live ICU exit, does the phenotype's incremental capture at a fixed 10% review capacity remain within 5 percentage points in patients with a recent documented care-unit transition compared with patients in a stable care unit, when both are scored by the same historical model without contemporary outcome refitting?

The primary new estimand is the contemporary transition-stability contrast
`DeltaStress10 = G10_recent - G10_stable`, where `G10_g = Cap10_C,g(Bdisc)-Cap10_C,g(B1)`. The queue is exactly the top `ceil(0.10 N_{C,g})` within each prespecified context stratum; this represents a policy that reserves the same review fraction for each observed context. A global contemporary 10% queue and stratum-specific capture are reported as secondary operating characteristics. Support for stability requires the 95% interval for `DeltaStress10` to be wholly above -0.05, with both stratum-specific gains estimated and no material calibration degradation.

This is an observed-record prioritization experiment, not an alert trial. The new scientific deliverable is newly fitted historical models and frozen contemporary predictions, a transition/stable transport contrast, a process-only replication test, R/D-specific capture, competing-risk calibration, uncertainty, and a conclusion ledger—or a complete prespecified gate-failure audit. No fitted result, prevalence, event count, or clinical conclusion is claimed here.

## Evidence boundary and clinical importance

The strongest supported claim before computation is source/design-level: the local MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains ICU stay boundaries, repeated lactate and MAP records, vasoactive intervals, organ laboratories, admission outcomes, and transfer intervals with care-unit labels. The bounded probe verified that the transfer archive member is readable and has the required header. The local dictionaries and parent audit verified lactate 50813, MAP 220052/220181/225312, Foley 226559, the 11 target vasoactive IDs, and the relevant table links/units. This establishes feasibility of a bounded retrospective association and ranking study, not proxy validity, event support, or clinical benefit.

The parent tests whether a phenotype-augmented fixed-capacity score transports from earlier to later MIMIC observation eras. It does not test the unresolved claim that a score remains useful around handoff or transfer, nor whether any apparent signal is reproduced by measurement/documentation process variables. The child tests that narrower, policy-relevant stress.

A positive result would support only that the phenotype's incremental observed R/D capture is not materially attenuated in a later-era, recently transferred subgroup under a fixed retrospective queue benchmark. A gain confined to the stable stratum would warn against using the signal unchanged during transitions. A gain reproduced by the process-only model would support documentation-intensity rather than phenotype attribution. A negative or inconclusive result is clinically useful: it prevents promoting an apparently portable physiologic score into a handoff/transition workflow without prospective evidence.

No result can establish microcirculatory dysfunction, lactate etiology, clinician intent, treatment response, preventability, appropriateness of transfer or discharge, monitoring benefit, utility, net benefit, harms, causal effects, or performance at another hospital. Those claims require adjudication, richer prospective measurements, external validation and/or a silent-mode prospective study.

## Population, exact timing and endpoint

Only MIMIC-IV is used for the study. HCC, eICU and UK Biobank remain directly available in their configured read-only dataset areas, including rows and notes, but are not mixed into the MIMIC population.

Use the read-only structured source `[internal dataset path]`, size 10,551,747,784 bytes, [source checksum], snapshot `[source checksum]`. The complete catalog is `[internal dataset path]`, [source checksum].

Join `icu/icustays` to `hosp/admissions` on `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, and `hosp/patients` on `subject_id`. Require a matching adult patient with `anchor_age >= 18`, retaining MIMIC's age value 91, and a valid admission. For each `hadm_id`, sort ICU rows by `(outtime,intime,stay_id)` and select the first row with finite `intime < outtime`, at least 13 hours of history, and known live exit at `t0=outtime`: `deathtime` null with `hospital_expire_flag=0`, or nonmissing `deathtime > t0`. Selection is before era, transfer context, coverage, phenotype, endpoint, split or model. A selected row failing coverage or support is never replaced by a later stay. Record all exclusion reasons, including missing/reversed times, linkage failure, known death by exit, contradictory life flag and insufficient history.

Set `tL=t0-1 hour`. The feature window is exactly `[t0-13h,tL]=[tL-12h,tL]`, with early `[tL-12h,tL-6h)` and late `(tL-6h,tL]`. Every chart/lab feature requires clinical time and recording time no later than `tL`; interval features require `starttime<endtime), clinical overlap with a half, and `storetime<=tL`. The exit boundary, `outtime`, `los`, terminal `last_careunit`, discharge/death fields, future transfers, notes, waveforms and post-`tL` rows are not predictors.

Construct the first transition in `(t0,t0+48h]), unchanged from the parent:

- R: minimum `intime` of a different `stay_id` in the same `(subject_id,hadm_id));
- D: `deathtime` in the interval;
- S: alive `dischtime` in the interval with `hospital_expire_flag=0`;
- N: no R/D/S by 48 hours with known event-free follow-up;
- U: unresolved ascertainment, including untimed death or contradictory/incomplete follow-up.

A different admission is not R; `intime<=t0` is not R. Ties are ordered D before R before S, with all tied candidates recorded. U is never imputed to N. The adverse composite is A=R or D and S remains a competing transition. Use Aalen-Johansen CIFs for R, D and S at 24 and 48 hours and retain the parent's favorable/adverse U bounds.

## Exact transition context and no-leakage rule

The transfer stress is an evaluation context, not a post hoc subgroup. Derive it only from `hosp/transfers` at the fixed landmark `tL`.

Define a valid context interval as a row with nonmissing `careunit` not equal to `UNKNOWN`, finite `intime`, and either finite `outtime>intime` or an open interval when it is the last available row. Sort rows within `(subject_id,hadm_id)` by `(intime,outtime,transfer_id)`. The landmark unit is the unique valid non-UNKNOWN row with `intime<=tL<outtime`. If an endpoint equals `tL`, apply the half-open convention and select the next interval beginning at `tL`; if no unique interval exists, context is unresolved and remains in flow but is excluded from the primary two-stratum contrast.

A patient is **recent-transition** if, among valid non-UNKNOWN rows available through `tL`, the landmark interval began in `(tL-6h,tL]) and its immediately preceding valid non-UNKNOWN interval has a different `careunit`. A patient is **stable** if a unique landmark interval exists, began at or before `tL-6h), and there is no preceding different-unit interval beginning in `(tL-6h,tL]`. All other patterns—same-unit administrative rows, missing/open boundary ambiguity, no preceding unit, or multiple overlapping landmark intervals—are `transition-indeterminate`, retained for the flow and descriptive process audit but never forced into either primary stratum.

This definition uses no `last_careunit`, `outtime`, future transfer, discharge or outcome information. `first_careunit` from `icustays` remains an admission-context audit field; terminal `last_careunit` is prohibited. For every context group report counts, current and preceding careunit, transfer interval length, hours since transition, `eventtype`, unresolved boundaries and measurement intensity. Context is defined before endpoint labels, fitting, predictions and queue ranking.

The primary transition-stability claim requires, in contemporary C, at least 50 strict known-label subjects and at least 10 known A events in each of recent-transition and stable strata, at least 10 subjects in each 10% queue, U<=10% overall and within each stratum, and effective sample size >=50 for any selection-stress weighting. If either stratum fails, report all arithmetic and process differences but call `DeltaStress10` inconclusive. A stricter secondary gate for R/D component interpretation requires at least five R and five D events per stratum; otherwise components are descriptive.

## Era transport, matched models and decision estimands

Use the parent's exact era mapping: L=2008-2013, B=2014-2016, C=2017-2022, based on `anchor_year_group`; C versus L is the primary era stress and B is descriptive. Because MIMIC timestamps are subject-shifted, this is an observation-era proxy, not calendar transportability.

Preserve the deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 split, stratified only by frozen primary phenotype cell, fixed endpoint label and original five-level `anchor_year_group`. Fit the era-transport models on L+B train, choose preprocessing/model settings on L+B validation, and freeze before reading C outcomes. Score historical test, all eligible C, and strict known-label C. Exclude `anchor_year_group` from transport predictors. The parent full-split result is run unchanged and reported separately.

- **B1**, the simple order-blind history baseline, uses the parent's 12 one-hour-bin lactate/MAP/pressor/organ/process summaries, masks and admission context without phenotype interactions.
- **Bdisc**, the selected transparent alternative, adds only the frozen phenotype terms, early/late values and changes, organ/process availability and interactions to the identical B1 whitelist. Its coefficients, cells and process controls make the scientific increment auditable.
- **Bmask**, a process-only negative control, retains demographics/admission context, care-unit context allowed by the parent, missingness masks, test presence, counts, Foley charting and measurement/pressor intensity but removes lactate/MAP/organ physiologic values. If Bmask shows a comparable transition-stratum gain, the phenotype attribution is not supported.
- **M2**, the substantive learned alternative, is a small masked chronological GRU receiving the same 12 bins, values, masks, process indicators, admission context and frozen phenotype flags. It predicts discrete-time R/D/S hazards and is fit only on historical train with validation-only early stopping/regularization. It is evaluated on the same test subjects, C strata, CIFs, calibration and queues. It can reveal nonlinear/order-dependent coupled trajectories lost by B1/Bdisc, but cannot establish mechanism.
- **Bctx**, a secondary policy adaptation, adds only the time-local recent-transition/stable indicator and hours-since-transition to Bdisc, trained historically and frozen before C outcomes. It is not used to define the primary stress or to rescue a failed primary result. If Bctx restores a stable gain while Bmask does not, that supports a future context-aware prospective evaluation; it still does not establish benefit.

The primary comparison is Bdisc versus B1, not a model-complexity contest. For each C stratum g in {recent, stable}, define `Q10_g(M)` as the exact top `ceil(0.10 N_{C,g})` subjects by predicted 48-hour CIF of A=R/D, with deterministic descending score then `subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id` ties. `Cap10_{C,g}(M)` is A events in `Q10_g(M)` divided by queue size, and `G10_g=Cap10(Bdisc)-Cap10(B1)`. Report sensitivity of all A events captured, false negatives, PPV, NNE, R capture, D capture, S/U counts and the random-allocation benchmark. The primary new stress is `DeltaStress10=G10_recent-G10_stable`. Use fixed 5% and 20% within-stratum queues as sensitivities, never as replacements for a failed 10% gate. Also report one global C 10% queue, stratified capture and queue allocation so the within-stratum policy is not mistaken for an overall deployment result.

Support for transition-stable incremental capture requires the context gate, both `G10_g` intervals wholly above +0.05, `DeltaStress10` interval wholly above -0.05, no material calibration degradation, and no comparable Bmask gain. A transition-specific gain alone supports only a context-specific ranking association. Support for the parent physiology interpretation additionally requires the parent's era-specific theta_RD direction and component gates; queue stability cannot substitute for interaction evidence.

## Uncertainty, diagnostics and falsification

Use subject-level bootstrap within fixed historical and contemporary partitions, preserving admission clustering and identical resamples for paired B1/Bdisc/Bmask/Bctx/M2 predictions. Bootstrap recent and stable C strata independently and combine their contrasts. Report 95% percentile intervals, bootstrap failure counts, effective sample sizes, calibration intercept, slope, O:E, Brier score and 24/48-hour CIF calibration. Calibration slope outside [0.80,1.25] or absolute intercept >0.10 is material degradation and blocks a calibrated risk-estimation claim, though ranking results may be reported with limits.

Before outcome inspection, compare recent/stable/indeterminate and L/B/C by lactate/MAP/pressor availability, distinct MAP times, positive pressor minutes, chart-store lag, rejected/delayed units, Foley charting, phenotype cells, U, first/landmark careunit, transfer timing and all eligible-to-gated flow. These are record-process descriptions, not confounding adjustment.

The fixed falsification set is:

- verify source/member, catalog and table schema hashes, dictionary labels/links/units, joins and selected IDs;
- audit selection order, era assignment, transfer boundary convention, no-future clinical/recording times and same-subject split integrity;
- audit MAP duplicate collapse, exact-repeat pressor collapse and interval unions;
- permute early/late bins preserving per-subject counts and availability;
- permute outcomes within era, split and transition stratum; queue gains and theta contrasts should disappear;
- compare Bmask and process-intensity-only models; comparable gains block phenotype attribution;
- repeat MAP-only, pressor-only, arterial-MAP-only, Foley-only and lactate-4.0 sensitivities;
- compare dual-time with chart-time-only features, 24 with 48 hours, and 4/6/8-bin aggregation;
- repeat transition classification with a fixed 3-hour and 12-hour window as descriptive sensitivities, without changing the primary 6-hour definition;
- audit primary cells, careunits, measurement intensity, recent-transfer timing and era strata descriptively;
- compute outcome-free selection q by subject-blocked cross-fitting using only baseline/context, transfer careunit, 12-hour counts/masks and process intensity available by each control time; report common support and stabilized inverse-odds ESS, never call q a causal correction;
- audit exact queue sizes, deterministic ties, random benchmark, R/D-specific capture and favorable/adverse U bounds.

Adverse evidence includes `DeltaStress10<=-0.05), contemporary gain <=0, attenuation beyond 0.05, calibration degradation, comparable Bmask gain, outcome-permutation resistance, reversed R/D interaction, or a result that disappears under the fixed dual-time, coverage, transfer-boundary or endpoint audit. Inconclusive evidence includes any context, coverage, support, event, U, overlap, ESS, bootstrap or calibration gate failure, unresolved units/joins, sparse R/D components, or intervals too wide to distinguish the prespecified margins. Do not call a failed context gate evidence of no robustness.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes/members, schemas, dictionary bindings, joins, timing, transfer-stratum assignment, labels/ties, gates, split integrity, fitted predictions, CIFs, queue arithmetic, uncertainty, calibration and falsifications. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for physiologic mechanism, clinician intent, preventability, treatment or monitoring effects, handoff appropriateness, utility, net benefit, harms, external validation and causal interpretation.

## Method-choice record and compute

The simple B1 baseline is necessary because it isolates incremental phenotype information from generic history. Bdisc is selected as the main scientific alternative because its phenotype terms, transition-stratum contrasts and process controls are transparent and auditable. M2 is retained as a matched learned alternative because chronological coupling and nonlinear change may be information that fixed summaries lose; a small improvement alone is not a clinical advance. Bctx is a bounded policy adaptation, secondary and historical-only, because it asks whether explicit transition context can repair a transport stress rather than merely increasing capacity.

A two-component diagonal-covariance trajectory mixture on the same training-only change vector is deferred if a pre-fit GRU implementation/stability audit fails; revisit requires a documented failure before any contemporary outcomes are inspected. A full transformer is deferred because its extra capacity adds no distinct clinical uncertainty. Fluid-response, treatment-effect and intervention-utility models are deferred because the available `inputevents` lack clinician intent, fluid responsiveness, valid counterfactual treatment assignment and adequate time-varying confounding control. The deferred branches are not selected after target results.

Measured feasibility facts are the catalog/source/member/header/dictionary audits and the approximately seven-second small joined eligibility audit inherited by the parent; the transfer header probe read the 46,185,771-byte compressed member prefix successfully. Unverified are full event-scan time, strict coverage, context prevalence, event support, U, q overlap/ESS, model stability, bootstrap runtime and GPU necessity. The future solver envelope is 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB memory, up to 8 GPUs and 28,800 seconds; discovery is 7,200 science seconds with concurrency 2. B1/Bdisc/Bmask/Bctx are expected to fit on CPU but this is an estimate. M2 may use one explicitly allocated A100 with model/tensors on `cuda:0`; ordinary shell CUDA absence is not evidence against feasibility. The solver must declare inputs/outputs and preserve checkpoints. No full task-specific fit was run in discovery.

Completion outputs are newly fitted historical and contemporary predictions for B1/Bdisc/Bmask/M2 and Bctx if feasible; parent theta/CIF results; transition flow and provenance manifests; `G10_recent`, `G10_stable`, `DeltaStress10`, global queue and 5/20% sensitivities; R/D/S/N/U and calibration tables; bootstrap/q diagnostics; falsification outputs; and a conclusion ledger—or a complete fixed-gate audit showing which claims are not estimable.
