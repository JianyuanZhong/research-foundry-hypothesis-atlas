# HCC MVI: fixed-day history separated from panel identity

Status: substantive measurement/estimand child of [prior hypothesis]. The explicitly graded cohort, first-resection index, M1/M2-versus-M0 outcome, day -90:-1 source window, 2016-2018/2019/2020/2021 temporal split, and 2020 locked clinical question are preserved. A new aggregate audit and a validation-only D2/T2 prototype were executed. No 2020 outcome model was fitted and no 2021 stress outcome model was fitted.

## What is supported, what remains unresolved, and why it matters

The inspected evidence supports three bounded claims. A static AFP/tumor-size/margin score achieved C-statistics near 0.81 in two Chinese validation cohorts and MVI is confirmed pathologically [K1]. M0/M1/M2 have sampling-dependent histopathologic definitions, with adverse prognosis for M2 in one selected resection cohort [K2]. Routine-care test presence, timing, and intensity can encode health and clinician behavior and cannot automatically be called biology [K3]. None of these works establishes that an additional preoperative laboratory day predicts this database's recorded MVI grade beyond the latest available panel.

The unresolved primary hypothesis is:

> Among explicitly graded, untreated, pathology-HCC first resections with at least two numeric preoperative laboratory days, does one additional earlier observed day improve 2020 prediction of recorded M1/M2 beyond the latest observed day after separating numeric-value information from exact-label panel identity and observation process?

This is clinically relevant because reproducible preoperative information could refine MVI-risk discussion and future study enrollment. It cannot establish treatment benefit, biological evolution, pathology adequacy, or transport beyond explicitly graded cases.

## Consequential audit result: the parent did not fix information exposure

The parent's one-value-per-label/day cap fixed duplicate rows but not panel size or analyte identity. On the frozen cohort, the new aggregate audit reproduced 1,918/835 train patients/events, 992/463 validation, 845/325 locked 2020, and 520/185 stress 2021 with at least two lab days.

Across 20 outcome-independent earlier-day draws, the locked 2020 earlier day contained a mean 67.6-69.7 exact labels, while the latest day contained 11.0. Mean shared labels were 4.47-5.42, but 67.7%-69.6% of patient-replicates had zero shared labels. In 2021, 75.6%-78.8% had zero shared labels. The distribution is strongly bimodal: in replicate 0 the 2020 shared-label quartiles were 0/0/2, even though the mean was 5.39. Thus V2/D2/T2 usually compare different panels rather than two measurements of the same analyte. An apparent gain can be caused by which tests were ordered on which day; “fixed information exposure,” “value evolution,” and “two-state trajectory” are unsupported labels for the primary estimand.

The latest day is also not literally a current snapshot. It is the maximum calendar day in [-90,-1] with at least one finite numeric result. In 2020 its median was day -2 (IQR -3 to -1; range -15 to -1). The correct boundary is: include lab timestamps strictly before index procedure start whose calendar-date difference is -90 through -1; exclude day 0 regardless of clock time. All models receive latest-day recency. Conclusions say “latest observed preoperative day,” not “current state.”

## Outcome/template and assay validity

The exact grade parser still yields 534 lexical discordance flags, not adjudicated errors. In the serial cohort the flag rate was 9.0% in 2016-2018, 2.4% in 2019, 2.8% in 2020, and 3.5% in 2021. Among all graded cases, M0 discordance fell from about 15.5% in 2016 to 4.2% in 2020; 2021 has the known grade-ascertainment discontinuity. These era patterns make 2020 the primary locked evaluation and 2021 a separate template stress set. Primary computation excludes flagged records only as a prespecified sensitivity; it cannot relabel them. Blinded hepatopathology review remains essential to verify specimen/index correspondence, grade, and template-section meaning. Failure of <95% overall or <90% per-grade-era agreement makes outcome interpretation inconclusive.

Labs provide exact label, qualitative result, quantitative result, specimen type, and timestamp, but no unit, reference range, or assay platform. Exact-label sparsity is not the main barrier: 100 labels each occurred in at least 100 training patients, and the top labels had broad 2019-2021 coverage. Numeric comparability is the barrier. The aggregate audit found an approximately 1,000-fold prealbumin median shift in 2020/2021 relative to training and other smaller shifts. This directly forbids biological-change claims and exposes temporal prediction to assay/unit discontinuity.

The primary pipeline therefore freezes train-only median/IQR transforms, clips at +/-10 IQR, and defines an outcome-blind measurement-stability manifest before fitting: exact labels with a >10-fold median ratio or >5 training-IQR median shift in any 2016-2020 calendar year are flagged. Report results both with the full train-supported vocabulary and after dropping flagged labels. The 2020 covariate distribution was inspected for this audit, but no 2020 label-outcome association or model metric was opened; this limitation is disclosed. A conclusion is inconclusive if support depends on drift-flagged labels or reverses under the stability sensitivity. Unit harmonization itself requires laboratory metadata unavailable here.

## Revised matched comparison

This child replaces “fixed information” with a **fixed-day addition** and explicitly models panel identity.

For each of 20 frozen seeds, retain the latest eligible numeric day and sample one earlier eligible day uniformly by a deterministic patient/seed hash. Within exact label/day, use the median; retain one value per label/day. Freeze vocabulary, transforms, penalties, architecture, epochs, and early stopping in train/2019 before any outcome evaluation in 2020.

1. **S-latest:** penalized logistic regression on latest-day values/masks, age, sex, service, latest-day recency, total preoperative distinct days, raw row count, and span. The operational covariates are shared by every arm.
2. **O2-label rival:** the identical two selected days and exact label/day masks, recency/process variables, demographics, and service, with all numeric values masked. This is the matched observation/panel-identity rival. A coarse count-only O-process remains descriptive.
3. **V2:** penalized logistic model on the identical selected panels, train-scaled numeric values, masks, and transparent earlier/latest/difference terms only where the same exact label occurs on both days. It tests additional numeric-value prediction beyond O2-label and S-latest; it is not a trajectory model.
4. **D2:** a label-value interaction encoder pooled within each day and averaged across the two days, plus the same static/process inputs. It can capture nonlinear cross-marker patterns but intentionally cannot use day order.
5. **T2:** the same day encoder followed by a two-step GRU in earlier-to-latest order. It can capture nonlinear dependence on panel/value order. Mandatory controls are T2 with all values zeroed, earlier/latest day swap, within-day value-to-label reassignment, and outcome permutation.
6. **C2 shared-label secondary:** for patient-replicates with at least two common exact labels across selected days, hash-select exactly two common labels and compare latest-only with earlier+latest values for those same labels. This is the only analysis that can speak to within-label ordered change, remains secondary because most patient-replicates have no shared label, and must report cohort/event counts before modeling.

The report-free analysis is primary. No claim “beyond tumor burden” is allowed unless the parent's blinded tumor-report parser gate passes and identical validated tumor variables are added to every arm.

## Validation prototype and learned-method decision

A bounded CPU prototype used one deterministic replicate, 1,918/835 train patients/events and 992/463 validation patients/events, a train-frozen 100-label vocabulary, identical two-day matrices, and three neural seeds. The 2020 and 2021 outcomes were not evaluated. Runtime was 134 seconds on 8 CPUs/64 GiB.

V2 validation AUROC/Brier/log loss were 0.630/0.239/0.675. D2 AUROC was 0.572-0.583 and Brier 0.244-0.245. T2 AUROC was 0.567-0.614 and Brier 0.242-0.246. Label-only T2 AUROC was 0.559-0.568; outcome permutation was 0.531. One T2 seed was highly sensitive to value-label reassignment, while other seeds were not. Validation was used for early stopping, so these are optimistic feasibility diagnostics, not scientific findings.

This adverse and unstable prototype selects V2 as the primary value model. D2/T2 are **deferred from locked primary interpretation**, not declared infeasible. The future solver may fit the frozen three-seed learned models on all 20 replicates only if, before opening 2020 outcomes, the 2019 replicate-averaged learned arm beats V2 by both AUROC and Brier, has AUROC seed range <=0.02 and Brier range <=0.005, beats label-only T2, and passes outcome permutation. The current prototype fails that gate. If the solver retains D2/T2 for completeness, label them exploratory and do not let their 2020 result alter the primary V2 conclusion. Revisit a learned primary only with larger stable serial support or scheduled panels.

CPU is selected: the full matrices are about 3,000 fitting/validation patients, 100 labels, two days, and small networks; the measured multi-seed prototype took 134 seconds. Expected full 20-replicate V2/O2 plus bootstrap is 1-3 hours on 8-16 CPUs and <=64 GiB; optional D2/T2 is estimated 1-2 CPU hours from the prototype. GPU allocation is unnecessary for this scale, though it is available and not scientifically disallowed.

## Evaluation, uncertainty, and falsification

Primary estimand: on identical 2020 patients and each frozen draw, V2 minus S-latest and V2 minus O2-label. Average patient predictions across 20 replicates. Report AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, and net benefit at 0.20/0.30/0.40/0.50. Use 2,000 patient bootstraps that recompute metrics from replicate-averaged predictions; separately report all replicate effects and the fraction with positive gain. The bootstrap does not replace model-refit uncertainty, which must be disclosed. Report 2019, locked 2020, and separate 2021 stress; annual drift, discordance exclusion, stability-label exclusion, and observation-count strata.

Support for additional numeric history requires in 2020: V2 improves over both S-latest and O2-label by >=0.02 AUROC and >=0.01 Brier, both paired 95% intervals exclude zero; calibration slope 0.8-1.2; net-benefit gain at two adjacent thresholds without material harm elsewhere; no 2019 direction reversal; positive Brier effect in >=16/20 replicates; and persistence after pathology-discordance and measurement-stability exclusions. This supports fixed-day historical-value prediction only.

A learned nonlinear claim additionally requires the validation gate above and locked D2/T2 improvement over V2 in AUROC and Brier with paired intervals excluding zero. An order-specific value claim requires T2 > D2 and > value-masked T2; day swap must attenuate >=50% of its Brier gain; value-label reassignment must attenuate >=50%; and label-only swap cannot explain the effect. Only C2 can support a limited within-label order claim, with adequate counts and the same gates.

Adverse evidence is precise exclusion of the V2 thresholds; O2-label matching V2; gain confined to drift-flagged labels or discordant outcomes; instability across replicates; reversal in 2019; or failure of calibration/net benefit. This parks the historical-value claim while allowing a panel/workup predictor. Learned-model validation failure, already observed in the prototype, defers D2/T2 without refuting V2.

Inconclusive evidence includes broad intervals, failed pathology adjudication, too few C2 events, poor calibration, major 2021-only failure, or incompatible results between full and measurement-stable vocabularies. Stable 2020 performance with 2021 failure indicates template/assay-era dependence and blocks deployment but does not erase the bounded 2020 association.

Mandatory checks: source hashes/headers, cohort reconciliation, strictly pre-index timestamps, day-0 exclusion, fixed seeds, train-only vocabulary/transforms, patient split isolation, one value per label/day, outcome permutation, O2-label, value-label reassignment, day swap, day -7 sensitivity, discordance exclusion, assay-drift manifest, 2014-2015 era stress, and claims linked to computed outputs.

## Scientific deliverable and claim boundary

Completion requires newly fitted S-latest, O2-label, and 20-replicate V2; frozen patient-level predictions for 2019/2020/2021; aggregate replicate and bootstrap metrics; calibration and decision curves; pathology/measurement manifests; C2 support counts; and `claims.json` mapping every interpretation to an output and rule. D2/T2 completion is conditional on the validation gate and otherwise recorded as a justified deferral.

The verifier can check computation, bindings, clocks, split isolation, sampling, masks, transforms, controls, metrics, and whether conclusions obey support/adverse/inconclusive rules. It cannot establish pathology truth, specimen adequacy, assay equivalence, tumor burden, clinical utility, mechanism, or treatment benefit. Those require expert adjudication, missing metadata, external validation, or another study.
