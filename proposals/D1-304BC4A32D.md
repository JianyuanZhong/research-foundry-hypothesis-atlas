## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
Discharging a patient alive from the ICU is a consequential, weakly standardised decision. The parent
predicts **48 h unplanned ICU readmission** and **7 d post-discharge death** from a fixed pre-discharge
window and added an outcome-specificity (ICD-chapter) negative control. The parent's OWN residual rival
is **genuine residual instability vs care-escalation/monitoring practice common cause**: sicker-looking
charts may reflect clinician concern (more orders, more frequent monitoring) rather than physiology.
This child makes that rival directly discriminable instead of only adjusting for it.

## 2. Hypothesis / strongest rival
- H1 (leading): after conditioning on a full documentation/escalation footprint, an **objectively-measured,
  passively-acquired physiology** footprint (vitals + oxygen requirement + labs, independent of order
  placement) retains held-out incremental discrimination for the two outcomes.
- H0 (rival): any increment is a **measurement/documenation-intensity artifact**; the objective footprint
  adds nothing once the documentation footprint is included.

## 3. Design (fixed from parent unless noted)
- Population: adults (>=18) discharged ALIVE from their FIRST ICU stay, ICU LOS >= 24 h. `icu/icustays`
  join `hosp/patients` (subject_id) and `hosp/admissions` (hadm_id).
- Landmark t0 = `icustays.outtime`; predictor window [t0-24h, t0) (no future leakage).
- Outcomes: (a) 48 h unplanned ICU readmission (next `icustays.intime` after t0 within 48 h);
  (b) 7 d post-discharge death (`admissions.deathtime` in (t0, t0+7d]).
- Locked temporal split: earliest 70% / latest 30% by t0 (inherited boundary).
- Predictor blocks (bindings, archive members in `mimic-iv-3.1.zip`):
  * Objective physiology: `icu/chartevents` (HR/SpO2/MAP/RR/temperature itemids from `icu/d_items`),
    `hosp/labevents`+`hosp/d_labitems` (lactate 50813, WBC/platelet, creatinine 50912), O2 device/
    requirement via `icu/chartevents` + `icu/datetimeevents`.
  * Documentation/escalation footprint D (the rival channel): order events `hosp/poe`+`hosp/poe_detail`,
    MAR `hosp/emar`/`hosp/emar_detail`, and ICU `icu/inputevents`, `icu/procedureevents`, `icu/outputevents`.
- Ladder: M0 demographics/comorbidity -> M1 M0+D (documentation) -> M2 M1+objective physiology.
- **Primary estimand (this child's delta):** Delta_obj = dAUROC(M2 vs M1) on the locked held-out split.
  A permuted-label control and an observation-intensity covariate (charted-event count in the window)
  are included; a **null/negative-control window** (identical features measured in the same stay at
  [t0-48h,t0-24h)) must show Delta_obj_null ~ 0.
- Predeclared decision rule:
  * Supportive of H1: Delta_obj >= 0.02, 95% CI lower bound > 0, >=75% of Delta_obj retained after adding
    the observation-intensity covariate, permuted-label Delta <= 0.005, and Delta_obj_null <= 0.5*Delta_obj.
  * Adverse (H0 favored / valuable negative): Delta_obj <= 0, or CI includes 0, or retention <50%.
  * Inconclusive: point estimate in (0, 0.02) with wide CI, or controls contradictory.
- Baseline vs alternative (method exploration): interpretable nested logistic ladder = baseline;
  a small irregular-time (GRU-D-style) encoder = deferred learned alternative, revisited only if the
  logistic contrast is inconclusive-but-nonzero.

## 4. Falsifiability / what results mean
- Supportive: objective physiology carries decision-relevant residual-instability signal beyond
  documentation -> supports physiology-grounded discharge monitoring; still association, no causal claim.
- Adverse: signal is documentation/monitoring practice -> redirect to process/standardisation studies.
- Inconclusive: underpowered/prevalence unclear -> report, do not claim.

## 5. Honest limits

study: mechanism, unplanned-ness, generalisability. K3 shows chart-event frequency is itself a routine
model feature (observation-intensity entanglement).
