## 1. Unresolved question

After circulatory stabilisation in sepsis/septic shock, does **cross-organ recovery asynchrony** carry prognostic
information beyond same-time snapshot severity? The episode-4 parent answered this with a model ladder
(M0 snapshot severity < M1 asynchrony < M2 shared-latent-recovery-factor < M3 observation-intensity). The remaining,
potentially fatal objection is that the ladder **cannot separate its own rivals**: an *unsigned* asynchrony class is
predicted identically by (a) genuine organ-specific divergence and (b) a single shared latent recovery trajectory
sampled with organ-specific measurement intensity. Episode 5 makes the rivals predict different observable results.

## 2. Observed vs conjectured

Observed (inspected):
- [K1] Inflammatory recovery follows a **universal** trajectory (exponential WBC decay + delayed PLT growth) across
  infection, ischaemia and trauma, with directional deviation associated with mortality (9x RR of sepsis death).
  Recovery behaves as a shared low-dimensional dynamic — the strongest counterevidence for organ-specific divergence.
- [K2] AKI is increasingly described as a **systemic** syndrome with kidney-to-distant-organ cross-talk (inflammation,
  retained metabolites) and clinical association with non-renal organ dysfunction — supports the organ-specific-
  divergence rival but is mechanistic/narrative (abstract inspected only) and gives no effect size.
- [K3] Creatinine-based GFR estimation is biased when muscle mass is changing (critical illness, weight loss);
  cystatin C-inclusive equations are more accurate and more prognostic. Creatinine-defined "renal recovery"
  can therefore be discordant with true kidney function — a measurement rival for renal-vs-respiratory disagreement.

Conjectured (to test): the *direction* of the lag, not only its magnitude, distinguishes organ-specific divergence
from a shared latent trajectory with organ-specific measurement intensity.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, outcome (unchanged from parent)

- Population: MIMIC-IV 3.1 adults (>=18 y) with a sepsis/septic-shock ICU phenotype (suspected infection:
  antibiotic + culture within the standard window, or ICD-coded sepsis; plus SOFA >= 2 rise or vasopressor +
  lactate > 2 mmol/L). Index ICU stay per subject_id; sepsis onset must precede t*.
- t0 = ICU `intime`. t* = first time after t0 with MAP >= 65 mmHg sustained >= 2 h **without** vasopressor-dose
  escalation. Classification landmark = t* + 24 h; only data in (t*, t*+24 h] may be used to classify recovery.
- Outcome window = (t*+24 h, t*+7 d]: composite deterioration (death, new/persistent vasopressor escalation,
  new invasive ventilation, or new RRT initiation), exactly as frozen by the parent.
- Development vs temporal holdout: split by ICU admission year (earliest ~70% admission-years development,
  latest ~30% held out); all thresholds, risk cutoffs and equivalence margins fixed on development only.

## 4. The advance over the parent: rivals that predict differently

### 4a. Signed lag direction (Branch A of this episode)
Recovery classification stays marker-prespecified but becomes **signed**, from each organ's own prespecified
improvement delta in (t*, t*+24 h]:
- states: {both recovering, renal-lagging (respiratory recovered, renal not), respiratory-lagging, both impaired}.
Divergent predictions:
- **Organ-specific divergence**: renal-lagging and respiratory-lagging patients should differ in 7-day
  deterioration risk after severity adjustment, and (if the mechanism is organ-specific) the two directions
  should not be equivalent; direction should carry information beyond the unsigned class.
- **Shared latent trajectory + organ-specific measurement intensity** [K1]: once magnitude of impairment and
  severity are held fixed, the two directions should be *exchangeable*.
Test: coefficient contrast (renal-lagging vs respiratory-lagging) with a prespecified equivalence margin on the
risk-difference scale. Falsification: if the directions are equivalent within the margin in the temporal holdout,
the signed direction adds nothing and the shared-trajectory/measurement rivals survive; if they differ beyond it,
the shared-trajectory rival is weakened (not excluded — still observational).

### 4b. Recovery-marker definition validity (Branch B of this episode)
Renal recovery is defined two ways — (a) creatinine (itemid 50912, `hosp/labevents`, `charttime`) and
(b) urine-output/RRT-informed (`icu/outputevents` 226559/226560/226566/226627/226631/226713 and
`icu/procedureevents` dialysis 225802/225803/225809/225955/225441). Respiratory recovery is defined two ways —
(a) PaO2/FiO2 (PaO2 50821 `hosp/labevents`, FiO2 223835 `icu/chartevents`) and (b) ventilator-free status
(`icu/procedureevents` 225792/225794).
Divergent predictions: if the asynchrony signal is a creatinine-recovery mis-measurement artefact [K3], the
creatinine-defined association should attenuate or vanish under the urine-output/RRT-informed definition while a
better-measured marker keeps any real signal; if divergence is genuine, the association should be directionally
consistent across marker definitions.
Negative controls: (i) marker-mismatch — pair each patient's renal marker with a time-shifted/within-patient
shuffled respiratory marker; the association must null. (ii) same-organ null — renal marker vs renal marker at two
landmarks should be concordant, not "asynchronous".

## 5. Analysis and evaluation (what the solver must fit/report)

Nested models on the prespecified split, reporting delta-AUROC and delta-AUPRC with bootstrap CIs (stratified by
development/holdout and by admission year), calibration-in-the-large and calibration slope, and net benefit at ONE
prespecified escalation-risk threshold (risk cutoffs fixed on development; rationale: the threshold at which a
clinician would escalate monitoring/therapy rather than observe). Primary contrasts: M1 vs M0; M1 vs M2;
M1 vs M3 (attenuation <25% = robust, >=60% = artefact); signed-direction contrast; marker-definition sensitivity.
Negative controls as in 4b, plus the parent's within-severity permuted-label control.

Supportive / adverse / inconclusive (prespecified):
- Supportive of organ-specific divergence: M1 beats M0 with CI excluding zero; M1 >= M2; attenuation under M3 <25%;
  signed directions differ beyond the equivalence margin; association consistent across marker definitions.
- Adverse: M1 does not beat M0 and does not beat M2; or the M1 gain vanishes under M3 (>=60% attenuation); or the
  signed directions are equivalent within the margin; or the association reverses/vanishes under the
  urine-output/RRT definition.
- Inconclusive: CIs span both the null and the substantive threshold; any definition yields < prespecified minimum
  events per class; holdout contradicts development (report both, do not pool).

## 6. Method exploration (baseline vs substantive alternative)

Baseline: snapshot severity logistic model at t* (M0) — interpretable, low variance, loses within-window dynamics.
Substantive alternatives: (M2) a latent-trajectory mixed-effects/state-space model fitted to the same longitudinal
markers — reveals whether one shared recovery factor suffices, which the snapshot baseline cannot express; and
(optional M4) a small sequence model (GRU) over the 24 h marker series for non-monotone recovery, 1 GPU, ~<=1 h.
Deferred: full latent-class trajectory clustering (larger fitting budget). CPU is sufficient for the primary ladder
and the directional/marker contrasts; GPU is optional and not required. Fitting/marker analysis is task-specific
and within the configured budget.

## 8. Essential limitations and claims requiring other evidence

- Single-centre retrospective MIMIC-IV: no causal or mechanistic claim; associations only.
- Measurement selection is *tested* (M3, marker definitions, negative controls) but not randomised.
- Creatinine recovery remains confounded by muscle loss and RRT [K3]; cystatin C is not available in this snapshot,
  so a marker-independent renal truth standard is unavailable and would require another study/assay.
- Distinguishing "genuine organ-specific divergence" from residual unmeasured-severity confounding ultimately needs
  prospective measurement of organ-specific injury (e.g., injury biomarkers) or interventional/expert adjudication;
  an automatic verifier can check computation and whether conclusions follow from the reported numbers, not the
  mechanism.
- Discovery did not analyse cohort rows; class prevalences and power are unresolved until the solver computes them.
