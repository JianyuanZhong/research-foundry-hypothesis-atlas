# Branch C: Is post-TACE loss of hepatic reserve transient injury or cumulative loss - and how much of the "recovery" is intravenous albumin?

Independent alternative branch, HCC island, episode 1. Dataset: single-centre Chinese
tertiary liver-cancer cohort, read-only at `[internal dataset path]`.
All counts below were computed by this worker from the source CSVs; none are estimates.

---

## 1. Scientific opening

### 1.1 Strongest claim the existing evidence supports

Selective TACE, performed under trial conditions in trial-eligible patients, does not
measurably impair *mean* hepatic reserve. In JIVROSG-2001 PRESIDENT-ALBI (secondary
analysis of a randomized trial, 197 patients), mean ALBI score deterioration was **0.06
at 1 month and 0.02 at 3 months**, and the 3-month ALBI *grade* deterioration rate was
11% (DEB-TACE) vs 6% (cTACE), p=0.203; the risk factors for 1-month deterioration were
large tumour diameter, number of treated tumours and number of treated vessels [K1].
Separately, guideline-level consensus holds that preserving liver function matters as
much as tumour response in intermediate-stage disease, that "TACE should not be continued
in patients who develop TACE failure/refractoriness in order to preserve liver function",
and that ALBI grade 1 maximises the benefit of subsequent systemic therapy [K2].

So the **decision** (when to stop TACE to protect reserve) is agreed to be important, and
the **average effect** of one selective TACE on reserve is agreed to be near zero.

### 1.2 The specific unresolved claim

A near-zero group mean is compatible with at least three different mechanisms, which
[K1] cannot separate because it reports a mean at two protocol time points:

- **H0 (no injury):** the procedure does essentially nothing to reserve in most patients.
- **H1 (transient embolisation injury, recoverable):** every course causes an acute
  hepatocellular insult proportional to the embolisation dose, which then resolves; the
  residual is ~0 and is unrelated to how many courses the patient has had.
- **H2 (cumulative, partly irreversible loss):** each course leaves a small persistent
  step down in reserve; the step is weakly related to the acute cytolytic insult, grows
  with the number of prior courses / treated vessels, and predicts hepatic
  decompensation and failure to receive course k+1.

These make **different, testable predictions about the shape of the per-course ALBI
trajectory**, not about its mean. The unresolved claim this branch tests is:

> **Claim C.** In routine care, the per-course ALBI trajectory decomposes into an acute
> transient component and a persistent step; the **persistent step** carries information
> about hepatic decompensation and non-delivery of the next TACE course that the single
> pre-course ALBI value used by current guidance [K2] does not, **and** a material part
> of apparent "recovery" of the albumin term is iatrogenic, because exogenous intravenous
> human albumin is given in a large minority of courses.

### 1.3 The measurement opening (branch C's distinctive angle)

[K1] adopts ALBI as its endpoint on the explicit premise, quoted verbatim from its
Introduction, that the ALBI score "is a continuous value calculated from the total
albumin and bilirubin values, **is not influenced by other factors**, and has few missing
values, making the ALBI score suitable for monitoring the clinical course of liver
function."

In routine Chinese tertiary hepatology practice that premise is false in a specific,
quantifiable way: **exogenous human albumin (人血白蛋白) is infused intravenously**, which
raises measured serum albumin and therefore improves ALBI without any change in hepatic
synthetic capacity. In this cohort I measured 118,116 albumin-infusion medication records
cohort-wide, 52,218 of them in **10,417 of the 19,596 TACE patients (53.2%)**;
**14.8% of landmark courses have an albumin infusion in day 0-7** and **11.5% in day
28-120**, i.e. exactly inside the acute and re-presentation ALBI windows. Independently,
[K3] shows that the albumin term is not a clean hepatic-reserve signal even without
infusion: two ALBI-grade-2 subgroups with the *same* ALBI but opposite component
composition had significantly different survival, PFS, recurrence multiplicity and
serosal invasion, and the authors state that "disentangling the prognostic implications
of hypoalbuminemia - whether stemming from poor liver function or a direct influence on
tumor biology - remains a major challenge" [K3].

This yields a second, measurement-level unresolved claim:

> **Claim M.** Serial ALBI is a biased monitor of hepatic reserve after TACE because its
> albumin term is partly pharmacological (albumin infusion) and partly inflammatory /
> tumour-biological [K3]; the bilirubin term carries the embolisation-specific injury
> signal. A component-wise model of the trajectory therefore outperforms, and is
> interpretable where, the ALBI scalar is not.

### 1.4 What answering this would change

If H2 holds and the persistent step is measurable, the stop-TACE rule in [K2] acquires a
quantitative, serially measurable liver-function criterion that is cheap (two routine
labs) and available before the next scheduled course. If H1 holds, repeated TACE is
reassuring from the reserve standpoint and a transient day 0-7 ALBI excursion should
**not** be used to deny course k+1 - which would be a direct, practice-relevant negative
result. If Claim M holds, every published serial-ALBI monitoring study that does not
record albumin infusion (including [K1], which does not report it) has an upward-biased
recovery estimate, and the field needs infusion-censored serial ALBI.

### 1.5 Prior work actually inspected, and what remains

Closest inspected works: [K1] (the only randomized-trial serial-ALBI-after-selective-TACE
evidence; group means, two time points, 197 patients, no observation model, no infusion
record), [K2] (the consensus that creates the decision but specifies no trajectory
metric), [K3] (component asymmetry of ALBI, cross-sectional, 285 patients, no serial
data, no infusion accounting). This is not an exhaustive novelty search; a full search of
the TACE-refractoriness and ART/ABCR-score literature is an explicit open task. The
remaining question after these three is precisely the trajectory **shape** in routine
care, the **cumulative** dimension across courses, and the **albumin-infusion artefact**.

### 2.2 Cohort entry, course construction, temporal boundaries

Procedure text in `手术` is flagged by regex; `手术来源` duplicates rows between
病案手术 (230,916) and 手麻系统 (107,124), so sessions are deduplicated to
patient-date. Embolisation flag: `栓塞` or `TACE`.

- **Session** = one (`患者主索引`, calendar date) with an embolisation flag, dated by
  `开始时间`.
- **Course** = maximal run of sessions with consecutive gaps <= 14 days. Justified by the
  data: only **2.00%** of within-patient inter-session gaps are <= 14 days, and the
  median gap between courses is 85-90 days, so courses are well separated.
- **Cohort entry**: first embolisation session. **Verified: 19,596 patients with >= 1
  dated embolisation, 39,789 courses.**
- **Observation window per course**: day -30..-1 (baseline), 0..7 (acute), 8..27 (mid),
  28..120 (re-presentation), relative to `t0` = first session date of the course;
  outcomes are counted relative to `t_last` = last session date of the course.
- **Temporal boundaries**: cohort period 2010-07 to 2026-01 as recorded. Analysis is
  restricted to courses with `t0` on or before 2025-07-31 so that every course has a
  potential 180-day outcome window inside the snapshot. Pre-specified temporal split for
  held-out confirmation: **fit on `t0` < 2023-01-01, confirm on `t0` >= 2023-01-01**
  (13,728 vs 15,116 landmark courses). Patients contributing courses on both sides are
  assigned wholly to the earlier side so that patient-level and temporal splits are
  simultaneously respected.

### 2.3 ALBI and never pooling assays

**There is no unit column in the lab table**, so assays are never pooled. Albumin is
taken from `检验` in {`白蛋白`, `白蛋白（急）`} and total bilirubin from
{`总胆红素`, `总胆红素（急）`}, with the STAT variant retained as a separate assay and
flagged (`alb_stat`, `bil_stat`); the primary analysis uses routine assays and the STAT
variants only in a coverage-sensitivity analysis. 31 further named assays were extracted
individually (ALT `丙氨酸氨基转移酶`, AST, platelets, INR, PT, AFP, creatinine, sodium,
CRP, hsCRP, WBC, neutrophils, lymphocytes, haemoglobin, eGFR(CKD-EPI), LDH, ALP, GGT,
AFP-L3, ammonia `血氨`, prealbumin, cholinesterase, procalcitonin, globulin, total
protein) and are each kept under their own `检验` string.

ALBI = `0.66*log10(bilirubin) - 0.085*albumin`, computed only from a **same-day**
albumin+bilirubin pair, after plausibility filters (albumin 10-60, bilirubin 0.5-800).
Units are implied, not asserted: median albumin 39, median bilirubin 17.2, median ALBI
-2.45, grade distribution **37.8% / 59.0% / 3.2%** (g1 / g2 / g3), consistent with g/L
and umol/L and with published HCC distributions. **Verified: 167,337 same-day ALBI pairs
in 19,461 patients, median 7.0 pairs per patient.**

### 2.5 Outcomes

Primary clinical outcome, day 7-180 after `t_last`, a **composite hepatic
decompensation** flag combining four independent ascertainment routes:
diagnosis text (`腹腔积液|腹水|肝性脑病|肝昏迷|肝衰竭|肝功能衰竭|肝肾综合征|食管.*出血|胃底.*出血|上消化道出血`),

lab-based (any ALBI > -1.39, i.e. grade 3, or total bilirubin >= 51 umol/L in day 15-180),
and vasoactive GI therapy. **Verified composite rate 24.2%** on the analysable set.
Secondary outcome: a mutually exclusive **next-decision state at 180 days** -
repeat_TACE 7,099 / decompensation 2,910 / other_locoregional 1,259 /
contact_no_further_rx 540 / switch_systemic 235.
Continuous outcomes: `dA` = acute ALBI change (worst day 0-7 minus last day -30..-1),
`dR` = residual ALBI change (first day 28-120 minus baseline), and their component
decompositions in albumin and in log10 bilirubin separately (motivated by [K3]).

### 2.7 Exploratory observations already made (labelled, not confirmatory)

On the 12,043-course set: `dA` mean **+0.353** (sd 0.354), `dR` mean **+0.112** (sd
0.399); **29.2%** of courses have `dR` > +0.30 and **13.3%** have `dR` < -0.30;
corr(`dA`, log ALT peak) = **0.267** vs corr(`dR`, log ALT peak) = **0.066**; and mean
`dR` by course index k = 1..6 is 0.122, 0.088, 0.104, 0.123, 0.130, 0.130 - i.e. the
acute change tracks the cytolytic injury dose while the residual does not, and the
residual does **not** rise monotonically with course index. Read naively that favours H1
over H2. It is explicitly exploratory, computed on the return-conditioned subset (a
collider: patients who decompensate and die or leave the system cannot contribute a
day-28-120 lab), and it is the thing the pre-specified analysis must confirm or overturn
on the >= 2023 held-out split with the observation model in place.

### 2.8 The observation process, measured

Lab timing is procedure-anchored, not patient-anchored: labs cluster at admission and day
0-3, collapse in day 8-21 (only **2.5-3.1%** of courses have any ALBI pair there), and
resurge at the next admission. Median in-system activity after a course is 94 days, with
p10 = 1 day and p25 = 2 days; only **38.3%** of courses have >= 180 days of subsequent
recorded activity. This is why a per-course "recovery slope" is not estimable
course-by-course and why the design is a **fixed-window landmark design plus a
population-level hierarchical model with an observation-intensity submodel**, not a naive
slope regression.

## 3. Two matched analyses on the same question

Both are fitted on the identical landmark set (28,844 courses, 15,840 patients), the
identical temporal split, and the identical outcomes. The deliverable is a fitted pair,
not a single model.

### 3.1 Baseline (simple, pre-specified)

- **Inputs** (all course-level, available at the day-7 landmark): `albi_base`, its
  components `alb_base` and `log10(bil_base)`, ALBI grade at baseline, `dA`,
  `alt_peak(0-7)` log-transformed, `n_sess`, `course_idx`, cumulative prior sessions,
  age, sex, baseline platelets, INR, AFP, sodium, creatinine, cirrhosis / PVTT /
  prior-decompensation diagnosis flags, pre-course diuretic use, era (`t0` year),
  albumin-infusion indicator in day -30..-1 and 0..7, and number of baseline lab draws in
  day -30..-1 (measurement-intensity covariate).
- **Fitting target**: (a) logistic regression for the composite hepatic decompensation at
  180 days; (b) multinomial logistic regression for the five-state next-decision outcome;
  (c) linear regression for `dR` among courses where it is observed, with inverse
  probability of observation weights from a separate logistic model for
  `observed(albi_rep)` using the same covariates plus calendar time and days of recorded
  activity.
- **Split**: fit on `t0` < 2023-01-01 (13,728 courses), evaluate on `t0` >= 2023-01-01
  (15,116 courses), patients assigned to one side only; within-fit tuning by 5-fold
  patient-grouped CV.
- **Metric with uncertainty**: AUROC and PR-AUC for the binary outcome, multiclass
  log-loss for the state outcome, calibration slope and intercept, with
  **patient-level cluster bootstrap (1,000 resamples)** 95% CIs for every statistic.
- **Reference comparison**: the pre-course ALBI value alone (the current-guidance
  predictor implied by [K2]), and ALBI grade alone, as nested models; the pre-specified
  test is the increment from adding `dA` and the injury-dose term.
- **Budget**: CPU only, 4 cores, under 20 minutes including bootstrap.
- **What it loses**: it treats `dA` and `dR` as two fixed-window contrasts. It cannot
  identify a *decay constant*, cannot separate a patient's own recovery capacity from
  that course's insult, cannot share information across a patient's repeated courses, and
  cannot express "the trajectory returned to baseline by day 40" versus "it was still
  0.1 above baseline at day 110" - because both are a single `dR` number whose timing
  (`dt_rep`) is driven by when the patient happened to come back.

### 3.2 Substantive alternative: hierarchical nonlinear state-space model of reserve with a joint observation submodel

- **Scientific object**: for patient i, model the latent ALBI trajectory as a persistent
  reserve level plus per-course transient perturbations,

  `ALBI_i(t) = b_i + c_i * t + sum_k [ a_ik * exp(-(t - t0_ik)/tau_i) * 1{t >= t0_ik} + s_ik * 1{t >= t0_ik} ] + beta * AlbInf_i(t) + e_i(t)`

  where `a_ik` is the **transient amplitude** of course k, `tau_i` the patient-specific
  **recovery time constant**, `s_ik` the **persistent step**, `c_i` the background
  cirrhosis/tumour drift, and `beta` the **iatrogenic albumin-infusion offset**
  (identified from the 14.8% / 11.5% of courses with infusions inside the windows). Each
  of `a_ik` and `s_ik` has a regression on course-level dose (`n_sess`, treated-vessel
  proxy, `log alt_peak`) and on `course_idx` / cumulative sessions. Fitted both on the
  ALBI scalar and, as the [K3]-motivated variant, **jointly on the two components**
  (albumin and log10 bilirubin as a bivariate outcome with shared latent reserve and
  component-specific loadings), so that a persistent step driven by albumin can be
  distinguished from one driven by bilirubin.
- **Joint observation submodel (the point of the exercise)**: a simultaneous intensity
  model for *when* a lab exists - a discrete-time hazard of having an ALBI pair on day t
  given days since last session, admission status, era and the current latent reserve -
  shared-parameter linked to the trajectory model. This is what lets the model state
  whether apparent recovery is recovery or is the sicker patients disappearing.
- **Inputs**: all 167,337 ALBI pairs in the 19,461 patients, every session date, the
  albumin-infusion event times, plus the baseline covariates of 3.1.
- **Fitting target / estimand**: the posterior (or penalised ML) distribution of
  population `tau`, of `E[s_ik | course_idx]` (the cumulative-loss slope - **the
  H1-vs-H2 discriminator**), of `beta` (the artefact magnitude), and of the predictive
  distribution of the composite outcome from the posterior `s_i` at the day-7 landmark.
- **Split**: identical temporal and patient splits as 3.1. Predictive comparison is made
  at the same day-7 landmark, using only information available by day 7, so the two
  models are decision-matched.
- **Metrics with uncertainty**: same AUROC / PR-AUC / log-loss / calibration against the
  baseline, with the same patient-level cluster bootstrap; plus posterior intervals for
  `tau`, for the `s_ik`-vs-`course_idx` slope and for `beta`.
- **Budget**: this is the one genuinely heavy piece. Plan: Stan/NumPyro-style HMC on a
  stratified subsample (all courses of ~4,000 patients) for the full Bayesian version,
  approximately 8-16 CPU hours, or a GPU variant (NumPyro on one A100, `gpus=1`) for the
  full 19,461-patient fit, estimated 2-6 GPU hours; a penalised-ML / Laplace
  approximation on the full data as the fallback if HMC does not mix. GPU is justified by
  the number of latent parameters (about 4 per patient plus 2 per course, roughly 140k
  latents), not by preference; the estimate is **unverified** - a bounded mixing probe on
  500 patients is the first solver step and the stated go/no-go for the full fit.
- **Selection**: **both are to be delivered**; the baseline is the primary pre-specified
  inference and the hierarchical model is the mechanistic estimator. The alternative is
  not optional decoration: `tau`, the `s`-vs-`k` slope and `beta` are *the* quantities
  that distinguish H1 from H2 and that quantify Claim M, and no fixed-window regression
  can produce them.
- **What the alternative could reveal that the baseline loses**: a recovery time constant
  (does reserve return in 3 weeks or 3 months); whether the persistent step grows with
  cumulative exposure after the observation process is modelled rather than conditioned
  on; how much of measured recovery is intravenous albumin; and whether the persistent
  step lives in the albumin or the bilirubin component, which is the difference between
  "reserve lost" and "inflammation and tumour biology" per [K3].

### 3.3 Alternatives considered and not chosen (recorded for revisiting)

- **Per-course individual slope regression over day 8-21**: rejected on measured
  evidence - only 2.5-3.1% of courses have any ALBI pair in day 8-21.
- **Deep sequence model (RNN/transformer) over the full lab stream**: deferred. It would
  likely add some discrimination but cannot report `tau`, `s_ik` or `beta`, and the
  scientific question is explanatory. Worth revisiting only if the hierarchical model's
  predictive performance is clearly below a flexible benchmark, which would itself be
  informative about unmodelled structure.
- **Child-Pugh-based trajectory**: rejected - the ascites and encephalopathy terms are
  clinician-recorded text in this dataset and INR is affected by anticoagulants, the
  exact criticism [K1] makes of Child-Pugh.

## 4. Pre-specified quantitative falsification criteria

Evaluated on the held-out `t0` >= 2023-01-01 split, with patient-level cluster bootstrap
95% CIs. Direction of ALBI change is positive = worse.

**Claim C, incremental-information arm.**
- **Supportive** if adding the day-0-7 trajectory terms (`dA`, `log alt_peak`) to the
  pre-course-ALBI baseline model raises held-out AUROC for 180-day composite
  decompensation by **>= 0.02 with the whole bootstrap 95% CI above 0**, and the
  hierarchical model's posterior `s_i` adds a further **>= 0.02** over that.
- **Refuted** if the delta AUROC 95% CI is entirely **below +0.01**. That is a clean
  negative result with practice content: a transient day-0-7 ALBI excursion should not be
  used to withhold course k+1.
- **Inconclusive** if the CI straddles +0.01 or +0.02; the pre-specified response is to
  report the interval, not to re-slice the cohort.

**H1 versus H2.**
- **Cumulative loss (H2) supported** if the hierarchical model's regression of the
  persistent step `s_ik` on `course_idx` has a **positive slope >= +0.02 ALBI units per
  course with the 95% interval excluding 0**, after the observation submodel and the
  albumin-infusion offset, and if that holds in the >= 2023 split.
- **Transient injury (H1) supported** if that slope's 95% interval lies **within
  (-0.01, +0.01)** while the transient amplitude `a_ik` retains a clearly positive
  association with `log alt_peak` (standardised coefficient 95% interval excluding 0) and
  the population `tau` posterior median is **< 60 days**.
- **H0 supported** if the population mean `a_ik` 95% interval lies within
  (-0.05, +0.05) ALBI units. Note that the observed `dA` mean of +0.353 already makes H0
  unlikely in this routine-care cohort, which is itself a contrast with [K1]'s
  selective-TACE trial setting and will be reported as such.

**Claim M, the artefact.**
- **Supported** if `beta`, the albumin-infusion offset, corresponds to **>= 0.05 ALBI
  units** (95% interval excluding 0) and if, in the infusion-free stratum, mean `dR` is
  **at least 0.05 ALBI units higher** than in the infusion-exposed stratum (CI excluding
  0) - i.e. recovery looks better when albumin is given.
- **Refuted** if both intervals are within +/-0.02 ALBI units, in which case the
  premise quoted from [K1] survives contact with routine care and serial ALBI needs no
  infusion censoring.

**Observation-process check (mandatory, pre-specified).** The entire trajectory-shape
result is declared **not interpretable** if the inverse-probability-of-observation
weighted estimate of mean `dR` differs from the unweighted estimate by **> 0.10 ALBI
units**, or if the observation-intensity submodel's coefficient on latent reserve implies
that a 1-unit worse reserve changes the daily probability of being measured by more than
a factor of 2. In that case the deliverable becomes the quantified bias itself, reported
as a negative methodological finding about serial-ALBI monitoring studies.

**Minimum-detectable-effect sanity.** With 15,116 held-out courses and a 24% event rate
(about 3,600 events), a delta AUROC of 0.02 is well above the bootstrap noise floor; the
binding constraint is bias, not power, which is why the falsification criteria are
written around the observation process rather than around p-values.

## 5. What this design cannot resolve

1. **Mortality.** No death registry, no national mortality linkage, no out-of-hospital
   follow-up. The composite outcome is in-system morbidity, and disappearance from the
   system is *not* interpretable as death. Every survival claim in [K1] and [K3] is
   therefore outside what this branch can test.
2. **Tumour burden and response.** There is no BCLC stage field, no tumour size or
   number, no RECIST or mRECIST adjudication. The imaging table
   (`data_检查_*.csv`) holds reports, not adjudicated response. The third named rival -
   tumour progression driving both trajectory and outcome - can only be **partially**
   addressed through AFP, PVTT diagnosis flags, treated-session counts and
   subsequent-treatment patterns. It cannot be eliminated. This is the single largest
   residual confounder.
3. **Embolisation dose and selectivity.** `手术` text does not record lobar versus
   superselective, number of treated vessels, or embolic/drug dose. [K1]'s own strongest
   risk factors (tumour diameter, number of treated tumours and vessels) are therefore
   **not measurable here**, so a direct comparison with [K1] is not possible; `n_sess`
   and ALT peak are proxies only. Chart review of a sampled subset would be required.
4. **Albumin dose.** `单次用药计量单位` is empty for albumin products, so infusion is an
   event count, not a gram dose. `beta` is therefore a per-exposure offset, not a
   dose-response.
5. **Text phenotypes need adjudication.** The decompensation, ascites, hepatic
   encephalopathy, PVTT and cirrhosis flags are **my own regex rules** on `诊断名称` and
   `医嘱(非药品)`; the bundled text-extraction note states that only an unvalidated
   cirrhosis lexical flag exists. Hepatic encephalopathy in particular is expected to be
   under-coded. A sampled clinical adjudication (proposed: 200 randomly selected
   course-outcome windows, dual-reviewed, reporting sensitivity and PPV per component) is
   a stated prerequisite before any clinical claim; the quantitative analysis can be run
   without it, but its outcome definition cannot be called validated until it is done.
6. **Causality.** Even if the persistent step predicts decompensation after adjustment,
   that is a predictive and descriptive claim, not proof that TACE *caused* irreversible
   reserve loss - confounding by indication is intrinsic to a single-arm routine-care
   design. A randomized or at minimum an external-control comparison (TACE versus
   first-line systemic therapy in matched TACE-unsuitable patients, per [K2]) is required
   for the causal version, and is another study.
7. **Calendar time.** Systemic therapy availability changed after about 2020; this is
   handled by era covariates and the temporal split, but the >= 2023 confirmation split
   is also the one most truncated by the snapshot. Era and censoring are partly
   entangled, and the honest statement is that any trend across eras is confounded with
   follow-up length.
8. **Single centre.** 85% male, HBV-dominant. External validity to HCV- or
   MASLD-dominant populations is unestablished.

## 6. Deliverable

Newly fitted and estimated: (i) the baseline landmark logistic / multinomial / IPOW
linear models with bootstrap intervals; (ii) the hierarchical nonlinear state-space model
with the joint observation submodel, in scalar-ALBI and bivariate-component forms,
yielding posteriors for `tau`, the `s`-vs-`k` slope and `beta`; (iii) the pre-specified
falsification table with the held-out numbers filled in; (iv) the observation-process
bias check. Completion is established by those four outputs plus the adjudication-sampling
frame; a favourable prediction metric alone does not establish it.
