# Observation-aware 24-hour hypoglycemia after a qualifying insulin episode

Status: episode-9 substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. This is an observational prognostic and observation-process study, not a causal insulin, feeding, AKI, or alert-benefit study.

## Scientific deliverable

The solver must newly construct a non-overlapping eICU cohort of first qualifying recorded-insulin episodes, freeze the glucose/unit/nutrition codebook before outcome analysis, estimate the standardized 24-hour probability of a newly detected low glucose for renal worsening plus a documented nutrition gap versus neither, and report whether that contrast is estimable under a prespecified support gate. It must also report the secondary six-hour risk, glucose-observation model and weights, cohort flow, event support, uncertainty, hospital transport, falsifications, and a matched transparent-versus-temporal model comparison.

Completion is established by: (1) frozen cohort and codebook artifacts; (2) counts of eligible episodes, stays, hospitals, exposure cells, observations, low events and competing events; (3) fitted B0/B1 estimates and standardized risks with uncertainty when the gate passes, or descriptive/inconclusive output when it does not; (4) held-out-hospital predictions/calibration and the temporal ablation results; and (5) an interpretation file whose claims are traceable to those outputs.

## Unresolved question and advance

The strongest available evidence supports only data availability and a bounded outcome-blind audit: the parent found 10,423 insulin-plus-recent-glucose periods across 2,798 stays and 58 hospitals, but only 783 conservative paired-support periods across 465 stays and 32 hospitals, 9 detected <70 events, and one event in the renal-worsening/documented-gap cell. That audit is feasibility evidence, not a study result.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults in their first ICU stay with a recent valid glucose and a numeric exact-label recorded Insulin (units/hr) update, the combination of recorded creatinine worsening and no qualifying documented nutrition delivery in the preceding 12 hours is associated with a higher probability of a first newly observed glucose <70 mg/dL within the next 24 hours, beyond insulin intensity, prior glucose trajectory and glucose-testing history.

The 24-hour primary horizon and one episode per patient address the parent’s sparse, overlapping-window problem without changing the clinically meaningful glucose threshold. The six-hour detected-low risk remains a prespecified secondary near-term endpoint. A positive result would justify prospective chart review and monitoring studies; it cannot justify insulin or feeding orders. The substantive advance is an observation-aware, transport-tested joint marker with an honest test of whether the signal survives glucose ascertainment correction, rather than claiming bedside hypoglycemia from an unobserved EHR event.

## Population, index and temporal rules

Use snapshot `[source checksum]`. Offsets are minutes from ICU admission. The patient schema has no `unitadmitoffset`; native ICU time zero is offset 0. Keep age >=18 (encode age >89 as 90), positive `unitdischargeoffset), and the smallest valid `unitvisitnumber` per `uniquepid`, breaking ties by `patientunitstayid`.

Define a qualifying episode as the earliest numeric exact `drugname = 'Insulin (units/hr)'` update at or after 1,440 minutes and before discharge, with a valid glucose in [t-360,t]. To prevent pseudo-replication, retain only the first qualifying episode per `uniquepid`; a sensitivity analysis may allow later episodes separated by >=1,440 minutes with patient-clustered uncertainty. Use predictors only through t. Stop follow-up at the earliest discharge, death status, EOL save/discussion, or 24 hours after t.

The primary outcome is the first valid glucose in (t,t+1,440] below 70 mg/dL; <54 mg/dL is secondary. The six-hour outcome is the first valid glucose in (t,t+360]. An unobserved glucose is not a negative outcome: distinguish observed non-low, observed low, no glucose observation, and competing discharge/death/EOL. Retain only explicitly supported glucose units after an outcome-blind audit and deduplicate/reconcile revisions using `labresultrevisedoffset`.

Recorded renal change is the latest creatinine in [t-360,t] minus the latest in [t-1,800,t-1,080], requiring both. Primary exposure is continuous change; >=0.3 mg/dL and >=1.5-fold are sensitivity definitions. Call it recorded creatinine worsening, never KDIGO AKI.

Primary documented nutrition requires a positive numeric `intakeOutput` row before t with an audited whitelist of oral/P.O., enteral/tube-feeding, parenteral or TPN labels and no qualifying positive delivery in (t-720,t]. Exclude flushes, medication/insulin volume, dextrose-only rows and ambiguous labels. Keep no-interface, inadequate coverage, and no qualifying delivery as separate categories. Freeze the codebook before inspecting outcome counts. Use `intakeoutputoffset` as clinical time; `intakeoutputentryoffset` is a timestamp sensitivity.

## Baseline, alternative and analysis

First fit a cross-fitted observation model for any glucose in (t,t+1,440] using only history through t: prior glucose density/gaps and interfaces, severity, hospital training-fold indicators, discharge/EOL context, and prespecified covariates. Estimate detected-low risk unweighted and with stabilized inverse-probability-of-observation weights; report positivity, effective sample size, truncation sensitivity, and the secondary six-hour version.

B0 is a transparent pooled-logistic or complementary-log-log cause-specific model for the primary horizon, with prespecified splines for insulin intensity, prior glucose level/trajectory/gaps, demographics, weight, admission context, early severity, vasopressor context, observation coverage, hospital effects, and competing events, excluding renal/nutrition exposures. B1 adds continuous renal change, documented-gap status, coverage categories, and the prespecified interaction. Report standardized joint-versus-neither 24-hour risk difference, risk ratio, uncertainty from patient-clustered bootstrap, hospital-aware intervals, calibration and fixed-alert-burden value beyond B0.

The substantive alternative is a one-layer GRU or temporal convolution over twelve one-hour bins before t, with the same variables plus explicit missingness, measurement-time and observation channels and separate heads for detected low glucose, glucose observation, and competing discharge/EOL. Use approximately 60/20/20 whole-hospital development/validation/transport folds, keeping `uniquepid` together. Evaluate on exactly the same episode set and endpoints using Brier score, calibration, discrimination as secondary, decision curves at a fixed alert burden, and physiology-only versus observation-only ablations. It can reveal whether ordering and co-movement carry information lost by summaries, or whether apparent signal is documentation/testing artifact. It is not inferentially primary if event support is inadequate.

Before fitting B1, require at least 20 primary <70 events overall, at least 5 in each prespecified exposure cell used for standardization, at least 10 hospitals with events and non-events, effective weighted sample size >=100, and stable sign under weight truncation. These are analysis-stability criteria, not clinical thresholds. If the gate fails, report support and descriptive risks, do not fit an unstable interaction, and classify the result inconclusive.

## Falsification and interpretation

Prespecify insulin-time permutation within stay preserving rates; renal/nutrition-history shuffling within hospital; a pre-index placebo glucose window; strict prior-delivery, complete-observable, direct-unit and no-carry-forward definitions; laboratory versus bedside/interface strata; insulin-free analogous episodes; clinical versus revision timestamps; leave-one-hospital-out transport with and without observation channels; and newly observed non-glucose laboratory testing as a negative-control outcome.

Supportive evidence requires the gate, stable weights, a clinically meaningful prespecified joint-versus-neither difference of 2 percentage points, uncertainty excluding zero, incremental calibration/alert value over B0, concordant six-hour direction, no single-hospital dominance, and failed placebo/permutation/negative-control tests. This supports only prospective chart-reviewed monitoring research. Adverse evidence is a null below target, reversal, disappearance after ascertainment correction or strict labels, poor transport calibration, one-interface/hospital confinement, negative-control similarity, or placebo/insulin-free reproduction; it refutes this measurable marker in the snapshot, not the safety of insulin or feeding interruption. Inconclusive evidence is failed support, unstable weights, ambiguous coverage, unsupported transport folds, competing-event miscalibration, or nonconvergence.

Administration, delivered calories/feed-stop time, rescue treatment, clinician intent, symptomatic/confirmed hypoglycemia, adjudicated AKI, and alert or treatment benefit are unavailable and require clinical adjudication, expert review, external validation, or prospective/emulated-intervention data.

## Method-selection and deferred branches

The baseline is selected as primary because it yields an interpretable standardized risk contrast and uncertainty under sparse support. The temporal learner is retained because the question is explicitly about evolving physiology plus observation process; summaries can lose persistence, ordering and measurement gaps. GPU use is optional, not a merit criterion: B0/B1 fit CPU-first; if the gate passes, one allocated A100 using `cuda:0` may fit the compact temporal model, otherwise CPU is acceptable. Future solver planning remains at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, 8 GPUs and 28,800 seconds; the measured parent support audit used 4 CPUs, 16 GiB and 27.2 seconds, while temporal convergence and full 24-hour event support are unverified.

A mechanistic latent-trajectory model is deferred because the snapshot lacks genetic inputs and adjudicated feeding/insulin physiology. The natural-history transformer is adapted only as a bounded temporal comparison; UK Biobank disease histories and Danish validation are unavailable. The multimodal cancer demonstration is not reproduced: only its supplement was inspected, the main paper/STAR Methods remain unavailable, and eICU has no chest-X-ray modality. The hypotension/AKI seed remains a plausible alternative but is deferred because artifact handling and adjudicated AKI are unresolved; it should be revisited if this episode’s support gate fails and a new outcome-blind vital/lab audit shows stronger support.

The Harbor computation can verify source bindings, cohort construction, temporal leakage, codebook application, support/event counts, model fitting, uncertainty, calibration, transport, falsifications, and that conclusions match outputs. It cannot establish clinical truth, administration, calories, symptoms, AKI, causality, or clinical benefit.
