## The defect and substantive clinical advance

The parent asks an important question but makes its primary ARDL7 success almost structurally dependent on remaining in the ICU: a favorable state requires a qualifying extubation task plus uninterrupted ICU respiratory charting through `D7=t0+7d`; ward transfer and live discharge are unknown. This is conservative as partial identification, but it discards exactly the trajectories clinicians hope diuresis might facilitate. The lower success bound can therefore favor prolonged ICU observation over clinically favorable transfer or discharge, and the frozen <=25% unknownness gate is at high risk of failing for source-topology reasons rather than because the treatment question is unresolved.

Two bounded audits used discovery buckets 0–79 and estimated no loop-treatment or A-versus-B outcome contrast.

1. In 5,752 earliest-adult broad stays with exactly one accepted item-225792 interval crossing hour 48, admission/transfer topology at D7 was determinable in 5,745 (99.878%). At D7, 1,748 index admissions had closed, 1,600 open admissions had one non-ICU location, and 2,421 had one ICU location. Only seven open admissions lacked a uniquely known location. Thus MIMIC observes ICU/hospital transition far more completely than continuous respiratory charting.
2. Among 2,118 broad records with a source-qualifying item-227194 task by day 5, only 465 (21.95%) had the index ICU open at D7, 1,382 remained admitted, and 360 had exact death by D7. Even treating every ICU-open record and every exact death as determinate, at least `2118-(465+360)=1293` (61.05%) lacked either audited route; overlap can only increase that lower bound. Recurrent airway events can convert some paths to failure, while chart gaps and state requirements can only reduce definite success. This is a necessary-condition audit, not final-cohort feasibility or an outcome estimate.

The repair makes **alive recorded ICU-free or favorably discharged status at day 7 (AIFD7)** primary. AIFD7 uses exact discharge and transfer topology that the source actually observes.
