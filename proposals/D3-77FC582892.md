# Directional macrophage-to-fibroblast signaling in fibrotic coculture

Seed: D3-M2 ([prior hypothesis])
Stage: hypothesis; no analysis has been executed.

## Question
Does a macrophage-positive fibroblast coculture induce a directional, pathway-specific fibroblast response beyond donor, culture, and macrophage-negative controls? If so, is that response attenuated when the macrophage stimulus or relevant pathway is inhibited?

## Why it matters
Fibrosis is driven in part by macrophage–fibroblast communication. If a macrophage-derived signal can be identified and blocked in this system, the result would support a testable intervention target and help distinguish a specific inflammatory pathway from generic culture or donor effects. A negative result would argue against using this coculture contrast as evidence for that pathway.

## Hypothesis
Macrophage-positive coculture induces a pathway-specific fibroblast response that is not reproduced by macrophage-negative or FLS-only controls and is attenuated by the relevant inhibitor or preLPS contrast.

## Competing explanations
1. Donor or passage differences.
2. Macrophage abundance or viability differences.
3. LPS or inhibitor effects on both cell types.
4. Targeted-panel and pseudobulk artifacts.
5. Reused donors or non-independent samples.

## Analysis
- Baseline: donor-aware pseudobulk differential expression and pathway enrichment for FLS-only versus macrophage-positive, with macrophage-negative and preLPS controls.
- Alternative: a mechanistic ligand/receptor or pathway-state model testing whether macrophage state predicts fibroblast pathway activation beyond donor and culture composition. This could reveal pathway specificity that a generic differential-expression baseline loses.
- Decision: use the donor-aware baseline for the first bounded screening if donor mapping is incomplete or the targeted panel cannot support a robust mechanistic model. Defer the learned/mechanistic alternative until verified donor and condition metadata support it.

## Falsification and interpretation
- Supportive: a reproducible, donor-aware fibroblast pathway response is present in macrophage-positive versus FLS-only and macrophage-negative samples, is enriched for a coherent macrophage-derived pathway, and is attenuated by the relevant inhibitor/preLPS contrast.
- Adverse: no donor-aware fibroblast response, or the response is present in macrophage-negative/culture controls and not enriched for the hypothesized pathway.
- Inconclusive: insufficient independent donor/condition mapping, low power, or conflicting preLPS/inhibitor directions.
