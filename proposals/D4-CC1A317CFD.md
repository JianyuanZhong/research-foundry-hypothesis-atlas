# Sparse CBC trajectories with an ascertainment-safe recorded-case CRC fallback (corrected field semantics)

## Repair and scientific advance

This is a repaired child of `[prior hypothesis]`, using the frozen field evidence and audit associated with `[prior hypothesis]`. The earlier proposal reversed UK Biobank fields 30070 and 30080. This version corrects that error everywhere: **30070 is RDW (percent), secondary only; 30080 is platelet count (10^9 cells/L), primary**. Every platelet landmark, baseline value, slope, model term, completeness check, range check, semantic gate, and falsification test uses 30080; every RDW reference uses 30070. Any prior coefficient or result computed under the reversed aliases is discarded.

The clinically important hypothesis is that, among repeat attenders with protocolized paired CBCs, a prespecified joint trajectory of falling haemoglobin, falling MCV, and rising platelet count predicts first CRC during days 366–1825 beyond age, sex, and repeat-assessment CBC levels, and provides a larger increment for CRC than for first non-CRC malignancy. The unresolved claim is prognostic and CRC-specific; it is not established by the field pages, audit, or the routine-data cancer demonstration. The substantive safe advance when coverage is unresolved is an identifiable recorded-case contrast: among people who do have a valid recorded malignant diagnosis, do the same trajectories discriminate recorded CRC from recorded non-CRC cancer? This does not treat absence of a record as a non-event.

## Exposures, models, and support gates

For m in 30020, 30040, and **30080**, define `slope_m=(m_1-m_0)/((L-B)/365.2425)`. Prespecify exactly three continuous one-degree-of-freedom slopes: falling Hb, falling MCV, rising platelet count. Comparator: age at L, sex, and instance-1 Hb, MCV, and **platelet 30080**. Add the three slopes in the trajectory model. RDW 30070 and its slope are secondary only, requiring four-marker completeness and never substituting for platelets.

If and only if coverage passes, fit locked low-dimensional ridge-penalized cause-specific Cox models with delayed entry at E, Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence with death/alternate malignancy competing events, and Fine–Gray sensitivity. Use identical participant-level repeated outer splits for comparator and trajectory models; all scaling, allowed imputation, penalty selection, censoring estimation/weight truncation, and calibration fitting are training-fold-local. Report paired out-of-fold time-dependent AUC/Uno C-index, IPCW integrated Brier change, and calibration only with event/effective-sample support. Refit identically for first non-CRC and report `D = incremental CRC AUC - incremental non-CRC AUC` with participant-bootstrap intervals. Fixed 1%, 2%, and 5% capacity summaries are descriptive only.

When coverage is unresolved but semantics pass, run only the recorded-case binary case-case model among recorded cases (CRC vs non-CRC): locked comparator and comparator-plus-three-slope models, identical participant-level repeated splits, fold-local preprocessing, paired out-of-fold AUC and log-loss change with participant-bootstrap intervals. Calibration is within recorded cases only and only with class support; never convert it to risk or PPV. A diagnosis-lag (0–365 versus 366–1459 days from E) secondary stratification is descriptive and only if each stratum passes support; it does not establish lead time or incident status.

Certified branch thresholds: >=60 CRC events permits locked prediction/association/descriptive capacity analyses; 30–59 permits parsimonious association and descriptive censoring-aware analyses, not flexible prediction or strong calibration; <30 makes incremental prediction inconclusive and permits only flow, cumulative incidence, and simplest prespecified penalized association. Fallback thresholds: >=50 recorded CRC and >=100 recorded non-CRC cases permits the locked increment; 25–49 CRC or 50–99 non-CRC permits only parsimonious case-case comparison labeled imprecise; below either minimum permits audits and class counts only. Counts never imply coverage validity.

Branch selection is pre-outcome-performance: (1) semantics plus release-matched coverage pass -> certified population analysis primary and fallback sensitivity; (2) semantics pass but coverage unresolved -> recorded-case fallback and raw audit only; (3) semantics fail -> raw audit only.
