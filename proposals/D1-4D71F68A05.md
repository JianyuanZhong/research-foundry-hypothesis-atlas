# Episode 50: a fixed eligible-population estimand for post-shock loop initiation

Parent: `[prior hypothesis]`

## Unresolved decision and substantive advance

After vasopressor-treated shock has resolved, should a markedly fluid-positive adult who remains invasively ventilated, with adequate MAP, urine output and potassium and no record of active RRT, start or restart an ICU-recorded furosemide/bumetanide delivery during the next 12 hours while recorded ventilation remains open, or avoid one? Earlier airway liberation would matter, but creatinine rise, RRT, death, hospice or acute transfer could outweigh it.

The strongest supported claim is narrower than benefit. The inspected full frozen XML abstract of Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13476641; DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`; source `[source checksum]`, file [source checksum]) reports 26 randomized trials/1,652 participants and says the optimal ICU diuretic strategy remains uncertain. The inspected full XML abstract of Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13154636; DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075`; source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) states that timing, volume and duration of fluid removal remain uncertain and individualized. Exact MIMIC streams can compute imperfect loop, airway, renal and terminal proxies. None of this establishes this policy's benefit, renal safety or exchangeability.

The experiment tests:

> In every patient passing the frozen pre-t0 eligibility rules, assignment-compatible A12 follow-up increases the lower bound of five-day recorded 48-hour airway-event-free extubation by more than 0.05 versus B12, while one-sided upper limits remain below +0.03 for recorded creatinine rise and creatinine-or-death, +0.05 for multisource RRT and RRT-or-death, and +0.03 for recorded terminal/hospice/acute-transfer harm.

The parent instead fit a baseline `e0(X0)` response defined as factual initiation before shared closure/G12, retained `e0 in [0.10,0.90]`, and multiplied both clones by `e0(1-e0)`. Shared closure is post-t0 and can be ventilation end/extubation, ICU exit, discharge or death. Thus an early-closure subject contributes identical A/B clones but is coded as a factual noninitiator for the model selecting and weighting the baseline target. Prognostic baseline strata may be downweighted because they predict truncated opportunity, not lack of equipoise.

A namespaced discovery-partition, treatment-blind audit found 7,663 broad first adult hour-48 ventilation opportunities: 983 closed before G12; 6,262 received the inherited eventual-noninitiation label, including 865 with truncated opportunity versus 5,397 with a full 12-hour opportunity. These broad counts omit phenotype gates and effects. They demonstrate label contamination structure, not bias direction or magnitude. Audit `analysis/episode50_postbaseline_overlap_audit.json`, [source checksum].

The inspected full XML abstract of Hansford et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC12927355; DOI `10.1136/bmjmed-2024-001280`; source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) identifies selection/treatment-misclassification bias from misaligned eligibility, assignment and follow-up. Reep et al. 2025 full XML was inspected for its abstract and target-trial framing (PMCID PMC12613904; DOI `10.1186/s13054-025-05723-x`; source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]); it emphasizes fixed trial components, time zero, grace periods, confounding and positivity in dynamic ICU settings.

This child removes e0 selection/tilt. Every eligible subject receives equal A/B clones at t0. Primary adjustment uses no numerator: it is the product of inverse probabilities of the observed compatible action at non-deterministic decisions, then frozen truncation. Deterministic compatibility contributes factor one. An hour-only numerator and no truncation are mandatory material-reversal sensitivities. The resulting estimand is the clinically interpretable frozen eligible population, not an outcome-opportunity-derived “overlap” population.

## Variables, estimand and computation

Frozen X0 includes demographics, admission/care unit/year, prior utilization/diagnosis/loop history, three-bin 12-hour MAP, urine, balance, creatinine, lactate, potassium, FiO2, PEEP, SpO2 and mode trajectories, sedation, pressor cessation, crystalloid/albumin and missingness. Covariates require clinical time and `storetime<=` the decision boundary. Retrospectively finalized treatment/device intervals may classify recorded states but may not leak future values.

At each non-deterministic compatibility decision, cross-fit the observed-action denominator from X0, hour and history available strictly at interval start using the inherited arm-specific L2 logistic models/five subject folds, C=1 then C=0.1. Save raw probabilities; clip only modeled probabilities to [0.01,0.99] for weights. Primary `W=product(1/p_den)`, followed by pooled 1st/99th truncation. Report raw/clipped probabilities, action support, weight factors/quantiles/CV/truncation, arm/hour ESS, X0 and history balance. Do not model deterministic intervals or replace probability one with 0.99. No e0 or numerator enters primary analysis.

Primary RHA-E48-D5 remains the lower/upper recorded airway proxy: earliest accepted extubation X by day 5 linked within two hours to V; alive/admission-open through X+48h; procedure and chart surveillance for recurrent invasive ventilation/tracheostomy. C48/W48/D48/M48 classes and chart-augmented, continuous-ICU, ward-gap-as-unknown variants are mandatory. It is not adjudicated airway independence.

Renal/terminal endpoints remain RCR48, death-composite RCRD48, multisource RRT7_M, death-composite RRTD7, RTHD28, true-mortality bounds and ANHATD28. For each state `[L,U]`, use arm-normalized Hájek weighted risks and `DeltaL=pA_L-pB_U; DeltaU=pA_U-pB_L`; never complete-case delete or midpoint-impute.

Run 1,999 successful subject bootstraps with PCG64 seed 480048, at most 2,499 attempts and ≤5% failures. Rebuild folds, denominator models, state machine, primary/hour-only-stabilized/untruncated weights, endpoints and eight adverse statistics in every draw. Retain centered one-sided p-values and Holm FWER 0.05 across airway, RCR48, RCRD48, RRT7_M, RRTD7, RTHD28, true mortality and ANHATD28.

If valid and feasible:

- Supportive-recorded requires no Holm adverse family, airway lower limit >+0.05, chart and continuous-ICU lower limits >0, all renal/death upper limits below margins, nonadverse disposition and every diagnostic passing.
- Adverse-FWER requires a positive adverse statistic rejected after Holm; it is an observational concern, not toxicity proof.
- Margin-falsified requires no adjusted adverse family and airway upper limit ≤+0.05; it rejects the specified proxy improvement only.
- Every other result is statistically inconclusive. Always report exploratory safety alerts, including under failed validity.

Supportive evidence would prioritize external validation/randomization; adverse evidence could de-prioritize the policy or trigger expert safety review; inconclusive evidence means this dataset cannot resolve the decision.
