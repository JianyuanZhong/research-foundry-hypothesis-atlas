# Joint all-randomized validation of a vasopressor pathway endpoint: rescue does not censor safety events

## Decision question and substantive advance

The locked question is whether an EHR endpoint measures the assignment-indexed risk-difference vector for norepinephrine-first (NE-first) versus vasopressin-first (VP-first) discontinuation when compared with pump-native delivered rates and blinded intent adjudication. This is endpoint validation, not a test of which strategy improves outcomes. The substantive advance is a falsifiable, all-randomized validation in which rescue is retained as an intervention/pathway event but can no longer censor subsequent death or alive ICU exit, and in which every unresolved paired label is handled by one integer-feasible worst completion rather than favorable imputations selected separately for each metric.

The strongest available evidence is frozen retrospective evidence that MIMIC can produce a sparse EHR-side endpoint but cannot establish pump delivery, clinician intent, randomization, or absence of mechanical circulatory support (MCS). Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, 211 were NE-first and 167 VP-first (6.26% action proxy; the 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls). In 137 observed-stop episodes, 73 determinate failures occurred; 59 contained a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 were restart-only. MCS status was unknown in all 137. Five symmetric transaction reconstructions invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, and 23 stop indices; the inherited rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices. These results support endpoint instability and sparse feasibility only. They do not support treatment benefit, causal safety, or a prospective eligibility/accrual rate.

## Prospective population, timing, and reference truth

Enroll the first eligible randomization per person, age at least 18, alive in an adult ICU, with linked pump-delivered NE and VP concurrently for at least six hours. At the beginning of the final 30 minutes NE must be >0 and <=0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 and <=0.04 U/min, neither increased during those 30 minutes; no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the preceding two hours; at least three valid MAPs across three of four preceding 30-minute bins including the final bin, median >=65 and minimum >=55 mmHg; and a timestamped treating-team record that either strategy is permissible. Eligibility cannot depend on later implementation, rescue, death, exit, capture, adherence, or agreement.

Randomize `Z=0` NE-first and `Z=1` VP-first in configuration-stratified permuted blocks at immutable `tr`; the assigned first stop is never substituted for assignment. A configuration is site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, and clock-map method/version. Use at least 12 hospitals, two adult ICUs per hospital where available, and two pump-interface families. Run-in requires 50 wholly new episodes per configuration, 95% unique patient-pump-channel-drug linkage, complete pump export from two hours before assignment through closure with an escrowed clock map, and 90% contemporaneous intent material; one repeat is allowed, then exclusion. Run-in patients enter no estimand.

Reference qualifying cessation `t0_R` is the first linked pump-native delivered-rate time in `[tr,tr+120 min]` below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP for five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Programmed but unlinked rates do not qualify. Nonassigned cessation first is crossover; both within five minutes is simultaneous; later assigned cessation cannot erase a deviation. Record implementation through `min(tr+120 min, death, ICU exit)`. After qualifying cessation, follow pump export, vital status, and ICU location through `min(t0_R+6h, death, ICU exit)`; importantly, after rescue continue observation and retain later death or alive ICU exit.

Use an escrowed affine clock map with offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving rule, fitting rows, residuals, version, and an ambiguity band equal to the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual capped at 60 seconds. Any uncertainty crossing an ordering, five-minute, 120-minute, or six-hour boundary is indeterminate. Two blinded critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators classify deliberate rescue or prevention of worsening hypotension/impaired perfusion; a third resolves disagreement. Technical pauses, bag changes, channel exchange, transfer continuation, mistaken-stop correction, reconciliation, and documentation fragmentation without interrupted delivery are not rescue; unresolved intent is indeterminate.

## Seven components: an exhaustive state representation without rescue censoring

The terminal disposition remains an exhaustive, non-ordinal partition, but it is no longer used to suppress later safety indicators. For every randomized patient derive `I_R` in `{AQ,XO,SIM,NI_OVERRIDE,NI_NO_STOP,EXIT_PRE,DEATH_PRE,IND_I}` and an outcome/flag record. Define the seven prespecified binary components for arm `z` using all randomized patients in the denominator:

1. `H_impl=1{I_R=AQ}`.
2. `H_rescue=1{a reference rescue occurs after AQ}`.
3. `H_death_pre=1{death occurs before AQ}`.
4. `H_death_post=1{death occurs after AQ and by the six-hour closure}`.
5. `H_exit_pre=1{alive ICU exit occurs before AQ}`.
6. `H_exit_post=1{alive ICU exit occurs after AQ and by six-hour closure}`.
7. `H_stable=1{AQ, no rescue, no death, no alive ICU exit, and observation complete through six hours}`.

Death and alive exit are distinct, competing event types at their first occurrence; an exit after death is not an alive exit. Rescue is a flag, not a censoring event. Thus rescue followed by death has both `H_rescue=1` and the appropriate death flag; rescue followed by alive ICU exit has both rescue and exit flag. Death/exit before qualifying cessation remain pre components. The exhaustive state is the cross-product of the implementation disposition, first post-AQ event, and subsequent safety flags, with `INDETERMINATE` when a boundary or intent cannot be resolved. A patient need not occupy only one of the seven components; this is intentional and is what makes the seven-component vector exhaustive for clinically separable pathway and safety information rather than falsely mutually exclusive. Stable is the sole no-event completion state.

For each component report `Delta_R,k=p_1k-p_0k`, arm-specific state distributions, and the paired EHR/reference distortion. Conditional rescue summaries are diagnostics only. Preserve the legacy composite exactly: `Y_R=1` for pre-AQ deviation/exit/death or AQ followed by rescue/death; `Y_R=0` for AQ followed by no event or alive exit; otherwise indeterminate. It is an eighth guardrail, never a replacement for the seven components.

## Joint feasible-state partial information and exact inference

For component `k`, define `f_zk=P(H_Ek=1,H_Rk=0|Z=z)` and `g_zk=P(H_Ek=0,H_Rk=1|Z=z)`, so `b_k=Delta_E,k-Delta_R,k=f_1k-g_1k-f_0k+g_0k`. Use four two-sided Clopper–Pearson intervals at alpha `0.0125` per component and the strict bound `B_Uk=max(|L(f_1k)-U(g_1k)-U(f_0k)+L(g_0k)|, |U(f_1k)-L(g_1k)-L(f_0k)+U(g_0k)|)`. The component passes only if `B_Uk<0.025`; the same construction is used for the legacy guardrail. The global hypothesis is the intersection-union claim that all seven component distortions and the legacy guardrail are below 0.025. Rejection of every component controls the global false all-pass claim at <=0.05 without alpha division across components.

Unknown EHR/reference labels are not assigned a generic percentage. Before lock, publish a component-specific budget table in integer counts and denominator fractions, with budgets tied to the clinically relevant distinctions: implementation (`u_impl`), rescue (`u_rescue`), pre-death (`u_dth_pre`), post-death (`u_dth_post`), pre-exit (`u_exit_pre`), post-exit (`u_exit_post`), stable (`u_stable`), and legacy (`u_Y`), separately by arm and by configuration cell. Each budget is the maximum number of unresolved labels that can alter that component, obtained from the locked data contract and verified material-completeness rules; it is not an assumed indeterminacy percentage and is not pooled across components. A budget failure is reported for that component and is operationally adverse/inconclusive according to the locked protocol, even if another component is precise. The final gate is still the exact feasible-state analysis, not a budget-based favorable imputation.

Construct one feasible integer set `F` jointly over all patients and all seven components plus legacy. Variables represent unresolved state labels and paired EHR/reference component indicators. Constraints enforce observed paired cells and margins, one terminal state per side, component identities from state, rescue/death/exit temporal compatibility (rescue may coexist with later death/exit), no alive exit after death, arm/configuration totals, and every component-specific budget. For each `x in F`, calculate all four error counts and exact bounds. Solve one mixed-integer worst-completion objective
`T*=max_(x in F) max({B_U,Y(x),B_U,1(x),...,B_U,7(x)})`,
returning the maximizing completion and its integer witness; the same witness must be used for all displayed joint tables. Also report each component's maximum as a diagnostic, but never combine metric-specific favorable completions. Sparse convolution/branch-and-bound is allowed; person-level exhaustive enumeration is an oracle only when at most eight labels are unknown. Timeout, no integer witness, violation of any margin/compatibility constraint, or disagreement with exhaustive tiny cases is inconclusive.

Exact CP bounds are patient-level conditional bounds, valid only under the stated independent-patient error model conditional on the realized configuration mixture. They must not be described as exact marginal coverage under configuration clustering. Add a prespecified configuration-robust sensitivity: resample complete configurations (site/ICU/interface/build units), stratified by arm and with within-configuration patient rows retained, at least 10,000 times; solve the same joint integer optimization in each replicate and report the 97.5th percentile of `T*`, configuration-specific estimates, and leave-one-configuration/site/interface-family-out results. This is a transport/cluster sensitivity and percentile uncertainty statement, not an exact coverage guarantee. Material heterogeneity or sensitivity failure is adverse measurement evidence; sparse cells are inconclusive.

## Interpretation and falsification

Support requires all 7,602 confirmation patients retained, `T*<0.025` for every component and legacy guardrail, all component-specific budgets, linkage/export/clock/intent/adjudication/determinacy thresholds, cluster sensitivity, concentration, and leave-out gates passing. It supports only measurement validity for the specified assignment-indexed vector in the enrolled configuration mixture. It does not establish treatment benefit, safety, strategy superiority, exchangeability, or causal effect.

Adverse evidence is any completed required bound `>=0.025`, a joint witness showing failure, rescue-associated death/exit distortion, cancellation, configuration heterogeneity, or a component-specific robustness failure. Poor implementation, crossover, more rescue, death, or exit can be clinically adverse while not being measurement error unless paired labels disagree. Inconclusive evidence includes missing pump/clock/intent/MCS evidence, unresolvable integer optimization, failed governance, failed accrual/completion, or a changed population, clock, interface, mapping, or window. Computation can verify row accounting, state derivation, joint integer feasibility, exact bounds, arithmetic, and interpretation gates; experts and another randomized study are required for clinical intent, physical delivery truth, MCS ascertainment, safety, and treatment-effect claims.
