# Lead working proposal — does MFI add to thigh-muscle distribution for later falls?

## Question and why it matters

A 2026 UK Biobank study reports that posterior/anterior (P/A) thigh muscle-volume ratio is associated with falls cross-sectionally. The parent question—whether MRI posterior-thigh fat infiltration predicts later falls—remains different in exposure and time ordering, but it should now explicitly test whether MFI adds information beyond this closest anatomical comparator. The clinical uncertainty is whether MFI offers distinct muscle-quality information, or merely restates regional muscle-volume distribution/frailty.

**Hypothesis:** among UKB imaging visit-2 participants with a valid visit-3 falls response at least 366 days later, higher bilateral posterior-thigh MFI at visit 2 is positively associated with any fall reported for the year before visit 3 after adjustment for VAT, total thigh fat-free volume, P/A muscle-volume ratio, prior falls, baseline function, adiposity, age, sex, smoking and activity. This is a conditional prognostic association in selected returnees, not a causal or mechanistic effect. A positive conditional MFI association beyond P/A ratio would distinguish composition from mass distribution; a precise null would suggest no additional MFI signal for this endpoint beyond the closest prior MRI phenotype.

**Strong rival:** MFI is a marker of frailty, inactivity/disuse, adiposity, illness or prior falls; the published P/A ratio may capture the relevant functional anatomy. Selection into MRI/return visits, reporting opportunity, recall and measurement error can also induce/attenuate associations. Adjustment/time order cannot eliminate these explanations.

Falls can lead to injury, fracture, disability and loss of independence. A robust distinct MFI signal warrants external validation for functional-risk phenotyping; a precise near-null argues against prioritizing an extra MRI composition metric. Neither changes treatment or screening without validated utility and intervention evidence.

## Estimand, models, and decision rules

Primary estimand: adjusted odds ratio per SD higher visit-2 posterior MFI for visit-3 any-fall report, conditional on VAT, total thigh fat-free volume, P/A ratio and prespecified covariates. Fit a logistic model first without MFI and then with MFI using the same subjects and covariates. The key inferential test is MFI's conditional coefficient/95% CI; report standardized absolute risk contrasts if supported, calibration/Brier and participant-level stratified five-fold out-of-fold prediction. This is not causal.

Scientifically substantive alternative: a shrinkage-controlled generalized additive logistic fit on the exact same cohort, outcome and folds, with smooth MFI and P/A ratio terms plus their prespecified interaction (and smooth VAT). It tests whether the MFI signal is nonlinear or concentrated in a particular muscle-distribution pattern, information a single linear term loses. Compare uncertainty-supported conditional curves/joint test and calibration/log-loss/Brier; do not select it for a small score gain. If event support/denominator quality cannot sustain it, defer the flexible fit and label the comparison inconclusive rather than changing population or endpoint. Small tabular analysis: CPU, no GPU; planned but unmeasured full solver estimate 2–8 CPU-hours extraction/QC/fits/folds/bootstrap, within `inputs.json` solver envelope (16 CPU, 256 GiB, 28,800 s). No outcomes were fitted during discovery.

Supportive: adjusted MFI association is positive, reasonably precise, robust to prespecified coding/missingness/selection checks and remains beyond P/A distribution, VAT and function; this warrants independent validation only. Adverse: sufficiently precise near-null/opposite MFI estimate excludes a clinical-reviewer-defined meaningful margin and no stable GAM pattern remains, supporting not prioritizing MFI for this specific falls-phenotyping use—not refuting all myosteatosis biology. Inconclusive: sparse outcomes, wide intervals, invalid/unstable ratio, coding anomalies, missingness/selection sensitivity or unstable flexible terms. An imprecise null is not falsification.

Computational verifier can test source bindings, filters, keys, code mapping, P/A derivation, counts, models, uncertainty and whether conclusions track computed outputs. It cannot adjudicate falls, decide the meaningful clinical margin, certify MRI quality, remove unmeasured confounding, infer mechanism/causality or establish clinical utility. Those stronger claims need expert review, objective/event-level falls data, external cohort validation and an intervention or mechanistic study.

## Method/reference disposition

The linear nested logistic model is the interpretable baseline, while the GAM addresses nonlinear MFI and MFI×P/A effect-modification uncertainty on identical inputs/validation. The sequence-model demonstrations Delphi and ALADYNOULLI are not selected because this question is sparse imaging/function at two visits rather than disease-order or latent trajectory learning; Oncoformer is not selected because multimodal cancer imaging/EHR inputs are irrelevant and its main text/full methods were unavailable in the demonstration bundle. These are question-specific deferrals, not blanket method bans.
