# Joint stored-content and coverage-frontier queue for documentary M2

## Unresolved clinical question and advance

For adults undergoing a first eligible HCC liver resection, can semantic information in routine CT/MRI reports improve allocation of a fixed, scarce multidisciplinary-review queue for pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2), compared with a laboratory-independent structured queue? The clinically consequential action is review allocation only. It is not a recommendation for resection extent, transplantation, systemic or locoregional therapy, adjuvant therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

Existing evidence supports the importance of MVI severity after resection but does not establish that report semantics improve preoperative review allocation. The inherited full-text inspection of Li et al. (World Journal of Surgical Oncology 2026, DOI 10.1186/s12957-026-04229-2, PMCID PMC13005532) supports prognostic relevance of M0/M1/M2 severity in a different cohort. The inspected systematic review by Neves and Soares (Cancers 2026, DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028, PMCID PMC13360217) supports heterogeneity, sparse external validation and inconsistent clinical-utility evaluation of HCC prediction studies. Neither establishes this institution's result, report visibility, or benefit from review.

The strongest local evidence is documentary: the snapshot contains operation starts, examination acquisition-like times, accession-like CT/MRI records and narrative text, same-encounter pathology narratives, and longitudinal treatment/order timestamps. It does not contain report authored/final/release/view times or version history, pathology time or specimen ID, raw images/slides, sampling adequacy, clinician review/action, treatment assignment/effect, recurrence, survival, harms, costs or patient benefit. Therefore the primary estimand is an eventual stored-content potential, not a deployed model.

The substantive Episode-20 repair is to separate that identifiable potential from transport. Instead of treating a nonempty eventual report as visible or using an invented availability distribution, compute a worst-case, coverage-indexed frontier over report masks. A favorable stored-content result can stand on its own as a falsifiable information-value result; deployment remains a separately auditable conditional claim.

## Hypotheses and estimands

Use capacities q in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} of the eligible target denominator, with a frozen integer and fractional-final-slot rule. In each coherent source state s, let Y_i(s) be documentary M2 (unknown outcomes are enumerated in the outer envelope), and let CC_q(M,s) be the number of selected documentary-M2 cases divided by the state-specific union-frame denominator N_T(s), for model M.

Fit B (age, sex, report-modality/process features and acquisition/accession structure, without report-body semantics, laboratory values or untimed diagnosis text), S (the identical B block plus a prespecified interpretable CT/MRI semantic block), and S0 (S's fitted pipeline with its semantic block masked, never a separately refitted model).

Primary stored-content contrasts are D_q=CC_q(S)-CC_q(S0) (semantic attribution) and C_q=CC_q(S)-CC_q(B) (complete-strategy value), evaluated on identical states, roster and capacity. The research margin is five additional documentary-M2 cases per 100 reviews, m_q=0.05q on the target-frame scale. P_store requires simultaneous 97.5% lower confidence limits for both D_q and C_q to exceed m_q at all three capacities and at the primary 24-hour buffer. This margin is a prespecified research benchmark; it does not establish that a stakeholder accepts the workload.

Transport is a distinct secondary estimand. For each patient, a latent mask selects which eligible CT/MRI accession groups would have been visible at t_dec. For attainable coverage c, minimize and maximize D_q and C_q over all joint patient states and masks with at least cN_T patients having an exposed report, retaining state-specific modality patterns and denominators. Define c* as the smallest attainable c such that for every attainable c' >= c, both contrasts retain the simultaneous margin at every q and locked buffer. If no such c exists, c* is undefined. This is a deterministic partial-identification frontier, not a visibility probability. It may quantify the report coverage that a future service would need, but it cannot establish that the current source met that coverage.

The deployment claim P_deploy is conditional and not identified by the configured data: a representative external audit must show, at the same accession-first unit and t_dec, the body actually shown before t_dec, final/release/view timestamps, version matching, and coverage whose joint lower confidence bound is at least c*. Without that audit, a supportive P_store result remains eventual stored-content evidence and deployment is inconclusive.

## Population, time and state envelope

Build a pre-outcome ambiguity ledger before reading pathology grade, report semantics, outcomes, model results or period counts. Use the fixed union roster of all patients with at least one admissible 2020-2021 test state. Quarantine every patient with any possible test-state membership from development/tuning. Fit on 2015-2018, tune/calibrate on 2019, and evaluate once on the patient-disjoint 2020-2021 test frame. Primary t_dec is 24 hours before a source-reconciled first eligible resection; locked sensitivity buffers are 12, 48 and 72 hours.

A multidisciplinary panel freezes a high-sensitivity procedure dictionary and linkage rules before outcome review. Enumerate source-reconciled operative episodes, including same-day component and adjacent-date alternatives. Keep a valid linked 手麻系统 start when available; a case-record 病案手术 date without a validated clock is the interval [d,d+24h). Do not silently choose the first procedure row, and do not discard ambiguous alternatives. In each state require age >=18, explicit HCC support from the selected index encounter's pathology composite, target period membership, and no recorded prior HCC resection/transplant/TACE/ablation/radiotherapy/targeted or immunotherapy in [t_dec-365 days,t_dec), using state-matched procedures, medications and orders. Recorded absence is not biological treatment-naivety.

The target denominator is state-specific. Nonmembers in a state are not coded as negatives; they contribute zero to the whole union-frame numerator and denominator exactly as declared. Recompute period, eligibility, selected encounter, pathology, report bundle, visibility mask, N_T(s) and Y(s) together in every state.

## Report, pathology and predictors

Deduplicate exact source rows while retaining raw row ordinals and duplicate counts. Group examination components by (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号) before modality assignment. A blank accession is unresolved and cannot become a singleton semantic report. Retain exclusive CT, MRI, dual, unresolved and no-report patterns; conflicting components remain unresolved states. Only accession groups with 开始时间 in [t_dec-90 days,t_dec) enter the eventual-content block. This is an acquisition eligibility proxy, not a visibility clock.

S semantic features are frozen before outcome access and extracted only from 检查所见 and 检查诊断: lesion diameter, multiplicity, irregular margin, capsule, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement, satellite nodules, portal/hepatic-vein invasion, cirrhosis and ascites, each with present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned states. Two liver radiologists and a bilingual clinical NLP reviewer freeze Chinese synonyms, negation/uncertainty and unit rules and review a stratified blinded validation set. Failed semantic validation makes P_store inconclusive. B can use encounter demographics and report process/modality/accession features but no narrative tokens or semantic extraction. No labs, diagnoses, clinical documents or post-t_dec facts enter prediction. A lineage test must fail if a laboratory path reaches cohort, features, states, queues or inference.

Pathology retains every row on the state-selected encounter from 病理, 检查所见 and 检查诊断. A frozen anchored parser assigns M0/M1/M2 only when the expression is selected-value anchored. Conflicting, absent, ungradable and non-anchored records are Y=unknown. The outer envelope enumerates allowed Y=0/1; a separate blinded expert-adjudicated envelope may narrow it. The endpoint is documentary M2, not biological M2.

## Computation, uncertainty and falsification

Use patient-wise worst-admissible-state convex logistic fitting for development, tuning and recalibration, not equal weighting of invented visibility states and not complete-case conditioning. Rebuild states, fit, calibrate, rank queues and recompute endpoints in a patient-level outer bootstrap. Use one simultaneous 97.5% family containing q, D, C, default contrasts, the four buffers, modality/access cells and all frontier endpoints; do not combine separate unadjusted model and access intervals.

At each q, rank eligible patients by calibrated score using a frozen patient-ID hash for ties, never pathology. Represent variable denominators and fractional final slots explicitly. Compute frontier extrema by an exact state-expanded MILP or multiple-choice dynamic program and verify against brute force on small fixtures. Compare flag-all, flag-none, seeded random and structured no-feature queues. Permute the entire semantic block within frozen year, modality/process and report-cardinality strata in development/tuning; observed D must exceed the prespecified null envelope. A semantic-mask fixture must produce exact S/S0 score, rank and queue identity and D=0.

Supportive P_store requires all computational, parser, extraction, state-completeness, semantic-validation, zero-lab-lineage, calibration, null and simultaneous-inference gates plus both D_q and C_q lower limits above m_q for every q and every terminal outer state. The allowed conclusion is that, in this historical source reconstruction, the fitted semantic pipeline selected more documentary-M2 cases at the same review capacities than its same-fit semantic mask and structured baseline. It is not causal, biological-M2, contemporary, deployable or beneficial evidence.

Adverse P_store requires valid computation and an upper bound at or below zero (harmful) or at/below m_q (non-material) for either contrast at any primary capacity across the outer envelope, or failure of the semantic null. A favorable C with adverse D does not establish semantic value; a favorable adjudicated/core envelope with an adverse outer envelope is not support. Capacity-dependent signs are reported as mixed, not collapsed.

Inconclusive results include an interval crossing the margin, incomplete state reachability, failed optimizer/brute-force replay, sparse or ungradable outcomes, semantic validation failure, report-version mismatch, or a favorable AUC/point estimate with non-supportive queue capture. c* is inconclusive when undefined or when a frontier is not jointly computable. P_deploy is inconclusive whenever the external audit is absent, unrepresentative, version-mismatched, or lacks joint uncertainty; a positive P_store cannot rescue that claim.

An automatic verifier can check hashes, headers, row ordinals, duplicate handling, joins, time windows, state masks, no-overlap quarantine, feature lineage, S0 identity, queue capacities, exact frontier arithmetic, bootstrap family and conclusion linkage. It cannot establish operation identity, Chinese semantic validity, specimen linkage or sampling adequacy, report visibility, audit representativeness, stakeholder workload acceptability, clinician action, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, cost or patient benefit. Those require expert adjudication, source-system evidence, stakeholder review, prospective workflow/outcome linkage or another study.
