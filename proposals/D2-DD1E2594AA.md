# Provisional successor hypothesis: macrophage-context-dependent intervention response in RA FLS

Seed: D2-T1. Status: provisional and evidence-bounded; no inferential result has been computed.

Proposed relationship: In rheumatoid-arthritis fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS), the intervention-associated transcript response is not context-independent: its direction or magnitude will vary with macrophage phenotype/coculture context. The prespecified contrast is an intervention-by-macrophage-context interaction, evaluated with donor dependence respected. This is not a claim of direct target action or clinical efficacy.

Evidence-supported versus unresolved claims: The parent’s inspected evidence supports clinical relevance of macrophage-state-dependent synovial inflammation and treatment response, but does not establish this assay interaction. The unresolved claim is whether a reproducible intervention-by-context interaction survives donor-aware analysis and is not explained by donor composition, batch, condition assignment, or targeted-assay coverage. Direct target action, mechanism, clinical benefit, and causal interpretation remain untested.

Falsifiable test: compare a donor-aware additive model (intervention plus macrophage context, no interaction) with a prespecified interaction model on verified samples. Support requires a reproducible interaction with uncertainty excluding the null and stability across donors; adverse evidence is a robust null or reversal; inconclusive evidence includes failed design/key verification, insufficient independent donors, or wide uncertainty. A positive result would motivate microenvironment-specific target evaluation, not prove a drug target or treatment effect.

Missing evidence and limits: exact local source paths, archive members, table names, columns, join keys, and assay-registry contrasts were not re-verified at this checkpoint. The three parent key-reference receipts and abstract-only claim mappings are inherited but must be rechecked against the revised claim before final selection.
