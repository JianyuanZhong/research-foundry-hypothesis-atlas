# Provisional hypothesis — E-MTAB-8873 MerTK-inhibitor context specificity

## Proposed relationship

In the available E-MTAB-8873 RA macrophage–fibroblast coculture assay, the MerTK-inhibitor-associated transcriptional response differs between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage contexts. The primary estimand is a donor-aware, condition-verified context-by-inhibitor interaction, not a generic mean-expression change. A secondary descriptive question is whether this interaction separates a macrophage-linked resolving/repair-associated program from a fibroblast-linked inflammatory or harmful program.

This is a hypothesis, not an executed result. It does not claim direct MerTK action, causality, protection, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The inspected evidence supports biological plausibility: MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets are associated with remission and repair-related fibroblast responses [K1]; macrophage reprogramming can alter inflammatory homeostasis [K2]; and macrophage–fibroblast repair circuits are context-dependent and can become maladaptive [K3]. Those observations do not establish this assay-specific interaction or transfer to patients.

The unresolved claim is whether the interaction remains after composition, donor, and generic-treatment controls. No effect estimate has been computed in this episode.

## Falsifiable test

Fit a donor-aware additive baseline for condition, treatment, age, sex, tissue, cell type, cell count, and contributing-donor structure. Compare it with the prespecified context-by-inhibitor interaction model. Use donor-clustered or permutation uncertainty. Add a composition negative control and a within-context untreated/control negative control. Report sample-level and program-level estimates, independent-donor support, missing/mixed-donor flags, and uncertainty.

Supportive: reproducible prespecified interaction remains directionally consistent after composition and treatment controls, with adequate uncertainty. Adverse: null/reversed interaction, disappearance after composition adjustment, or generic treatment effect across contexts. Inconclusive: unstable contrasts, missing controls, insufficient independent donors, ambiguous feature definitions, or imprecise estimates.
