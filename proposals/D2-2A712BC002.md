## Question (unchanged from parent)
Does MerTK inhibition selectively abolish the protective FLS program (induced by MerTK/CD206pos macrophages) while sparing the pathogenic FLS program (induced by MerTK/CD206neg macrophages) in E-MTAB-8873, and is the protective-program effect replicated in E-MTAB-8316?

## What this execution found (EXECUTED this episode, [research job])

### Classification: PATHOGENIC_ONLY (Rival Hypothesis 3) — main hypothesis NOT supported

### Step 1: Protective FLS program definition
- 104 genes with mean log2FC > 0.5 in Mpos vs FLS_only (5 FLS donors)
- **CRITICAL FINDING**: The pre-specified protective markers COL1A1, COL1A2, TGFBI are NOT in the protective program:
  - COL1A1: log2FC = -0.139 (NOT upregulated by Mpos)
  - COL1A2: log2FC = -0.221 (NOT upregulated by Mpos)
  - TGFBI: log2FC = -0.223 (NOT upregulated by Mpos)
- The "protective program" as computationally defined is dominated by macrophage surface markers (CD14, CD33, CD68-like genes), chemokines, and complement genes — NOT the collagen/TGFβ repair genes hypothesized
- This suggests the BD Rhapsody 445-gene panel may not capture the true repair program, OR the Mpos macrophages in this system do not induce the collagen/TGFβ program in FLS as expected

### Step 2: Pathogenic FLS program definition
- 163 genes with mean log2FC > 0.5 in Mneg vs FLS_only (5 FLS donors)
- All pre-specified pathogenic markers ARE in the program:
  - IL6: log2FC = 2.321 ✓
  - TNF: log2FC = 2.546 ✓
  - CXCL8: log2FC = 2.795 ✓
  - MMP1: log2FC = 1.624 ✓
  - MMP3: log2FC = 1.171 ✓
  - MMP9: log2FC = 3.858 ✓
- The pathogenic program is well-defined and consistent with the hypothesis

### Step 3: MerTK inhibitor effect on protective program (PRIMARY TEST)
- 4 paired Mpos Inhibitor vs Mpos none contrasts (SA256A x2, SA257A, SA266A)
- Mean log2FC (inhibitor - none) = -0.103 (direction: reduced, but very small)
- Per-pair values: [-1.202, +0.167, +0.755, -0.131] — HIGHLY INCONSISTENT
- Paired t-test: t = -0.250, **p = 0.819** (NOT significant)
- Cohen's d = -0.125 (negligible effect size)
- Pre-specified markers:
  - COL1A1: mean = -0.153, p = 0.317 (not significant)
  - COL1A2: mean = -0.190, p = 0.246 (not significant)
  - TGFBI: mean = -0.174, p = 0.305 (not significant)
- **CONCLUSION**: No significant effect of MerTK inhibition on the protective program

### Step 4: MerTK inhibitor effect on pathogenic program (n=1, descriptive)
- 1 paired Mneg Inhibitor vs Mneg none contrast (SA256A/SA259A)
- Mean log2FC (inhibitor - none) = -0.533 (direction: reduced)
- Pre-specified markers:
  - IL6: log2FC = +0.017 (unchanged)
  - TNF: log2FC = -0.681 (reduced)
  - CXCL8: log2FC = -0.816 (reduced)
  - MMP1: log2FC = +0.850 (INCREASED)
  - MMP3: log2FC = +0.712 (INCREASED)
  - MMP9: log2FC = -1.626 (strongly reduced)
- **CONCLUSION**: Mixed effects on pathogenic markers; TNF, CXCL8, MMP9 reduced but MMP1, MMP3 increased. The overall program mean is -0.533, suggesting a net reduction, but n=1 makes this purely descriptive.

### Step 5: Direct FLS control
- R1_SA256A_FLS_Inhibitor vs R1_SA256A_FLS_only
- 133 genes with |log2FC| > 0.5 — this is a LARGE number (30% of panel)
- This suggests the MerTK inhibitor has substantial direct effects on FLS independent of macrophage co-culture
- Pre-specified markers in FLS control:
  - COL1A1: +0.058 (minimal)
  - COL1A2: +0.038 (minimal)
  - TGFBI: +0.100 (minimal)
  - IL6: -0.114 (minimal)
  - TNF: -1.485 (significant reduction)
  - CXCL8: -0.440 (moderate reduction)
  - MMP1: -0.718 (significant reduction)
  - MMP3: -0.490 (moderate reduction)
  - MMP9: +0.087 (minimal)
- **CONCLUSION**: The inhibitor has direct effects on FLS, particularly reducing TNF and MMP1. This is a MAJOR confound for interpreting the co-culture contrasts. The 133 differentially expressed genes suggest the inhibitor is not fully selective for macrophage MerTK in this system.

### Step 6: Validation in E-MTAB-8316
- Only 4 genes testable in both datasets (APOE, IL6, MMP1, TNF) due to Ensembl ID mapping limitations
- The protective and pathogenic programs share these 4 genes (overlap)
- Protective 8316: mean log2FC (UNC106238 - LPS) = +0.356, p = 0.037 (INCREASED, not reduced)
- Pathogenic 8316: mean log2FC = +0.356, p = 0.037 (same genes, same result)
- Gene-level: IL6 +1.036, TNF +0.397, MMP1 +0.144
- **CONCLUSION**: UNC106238 INCREASES expression of these genes in the LPS-stimulated system. This is the OPPOSITE direction from the 8873 inhibitor effect. However, the experimental systems are very different (M-CSF macrophages vs FACS-sorted STMs, bulk vs scRNA-seq, all LPS-stimulated vs unstimulated), so this may not be a valid replication test.

### Step 7: Selectivity classification
- Protective program: NOT significantly reduced (p=0.819)
- Pathogenic program: Descriptively reduced (mean=-0.533, n=1)
- **CLASSIFICATION: PATHOGENIC_ONLY** (Rival Hypothesis 3)
- This is the OPPOSITE of the main hypothesis

## Interpretation against the competing explanations
- **Main hypothesis (selective protective-program abolition)**: NOT supported. The protective program is not significantly reduced by MerTK inhibition (p=0.819), and the pre-specified protective markers (COL1A1, COL1A2, TGFBI) are not even in the protective program.
- **Rival 1 (shared node)**: NOT supported. The protective program is not reduced, so MerTK is not a shared node for both programs.
- **Rival 2 (no effect)**: PARTIALLY supported for the protective program (no significant effect), but the pathogenic program shows a descriptive reduction (n=1).
- **Rival 3 (pathogenic-only)**: DEScriptively supported, but NOT robust given n=1 and the direct FLS confound. The pathogenic-program reduction (mean=-0.533) could be a direct FLS effect rather than a macrophage-mediated MerTK effect.
- **Direct FLS confound**: 133/440 genes (30%) show |log2FC|>0.5 in the FLS-only inhibitor control. The TNF and MMP1 reductions seen in the Mneg inhibitor contrast may be direct FLS effects, not macrophage-mediated. This is the most important limitation of the selectivity test.

## Falsification criteria (unchanged from parent; now evaluated)
- **Supportive**: MerTK inhibition significantly decreases the protective FLS program (COL1A1, COL1A2, TGFBI) in E-MTAB-8873 (p<0.05, 4 paired contrasts), the pathogenic FLS program is NOT significantly decreased in the single Mneg Inhibitor contrast (descriptive), and the protective program effect is replicated in E-MTAB-8316 (p<0.05, 4 paired contrasts). **NOT MET**: protective program not significantly reduced (p=0.819); pre-specified markers not in program; 8316 validation opposite direction.
- **Adverse**: MerTK inhibition does NOT decrease the protective FLS program in E-MTAB-8873, OR both protective and pathogenic programs are decreased (shared node), OR the effect is not replicated in E-MTAB-8316. **PARTIALLY MET**: protective program not decreased (p=0.819); 8316 validation not replicated (opposite direction).
- **Inconclusive**: The effect is inconsistent across FLS donors, the single Mneg Inhibitor contrast shows a large effect on the pathogenic program (suggesting shared node but n=1), or the E-MTAB-8316 validation is inconsistent. **ALSO APPLIES**: per-pair values highly inconsistent [-1.20, +0.17, +0.76, -0.13]; n=1 for pathogenic; 8316 inconsistent.
- **CURRENT RESULT: ADVERSE/INCONCLUSIVE.** The main hypothesis is not supported. The data points toward pathogenic-only (rival 3), but this is not robust given n=1 and the direct FLS confound. The protective-program premise (COL1A1/COL1A2/TGFBI up in Mpos) is not supported by the data.

## What the result would and would not establish
- WOULD establish (not achieved): a selective MerTK effect on the protective program, with the pathogenic program spared.
- DOES establish: (1) the pre-specified protective markers (COL1A1, COL1A2, TGFBI) are NOT upregulated by Mpos macrophages in this dataset; (2) the MerTK inhibitor has substantial direct FLS effects (30% of panel); (3) the protective program (as computationally defined) is NOT significantly affected by MerTK inhibition.
- Does NOT establish: a selective MerTK effect on the pathogenic program (n=1, direct FLS confound); a validated biomarker; or a mechanism.

## Method alternatives (recorded)
- Baseline (used this episode): per-donor log2FC contrasts, paired t-test on program genes, descriptive n=1 pathogenic contrast, direct FLS control, 8316 validation. Simple, transparent, appropriate for the small n.
- Alternative (deferred): subtract the FLS-only inhibitor effect from the co-culture contrasts to control for the direct FLS confound. This would be a more rigorous test of the macrophage-mediated effect. Revisit next.
- Alternative (deferred): use a lower threshold or focus on specific biological pathways (collagen, TGFβ) rather than the generic log2FC>0.5 criterion for the protective program. The current definition captures macrophage surface markers, not the repair program.
- Alternative (deferred): proper Ensembl-to-symbol mapping for the 8316 validation to test more genes. The current mapping only allows 4 genes.

## Honest limitations
1. **Protective program definition failure**: The pre-specified protective markers (COL1A1, COL1A2, TGFBI) are NOT upregulated by Mpos macrophages in this dataset. The "protective program" as computationally defined is not the biological repair program hypothesized. This fundamentally undermines the selectivity test.
2. **Direct FLS confound**: 133/440 genes (30%) show |log2FC|>0.5 in the FLS-only inhibitor control. The MerTK inhibitor has substantial direct effects on FLS, which confounds the co-culture contrasts.
3. **n=1 for pathogenic inhibitor**: The pathogenic program inhibitor effect is based on a single pair. The "reduction" (mean=-0.533) is descriptive and could easily be due to donor-specific effects.
4. **E-MTAB-8316 validation is weak**: Only 4 genes are testable, and the direction is opposite (increase vs decrease). The different experimental systems (M-CSF vs FACS-sorted, bulk vs scRNA-seq, LPS-stimulated vs unstimulated) make direct comparison difficult.
5. **Panel limitation**: The 445-gene BD Rhapsody panel may not capture the true repair program genes. COL1A1/COL1A2/TGFBI being absent from the protective program may reflect assay sensitivity rather than biology.
6. **Cross-sectional**: No causal/temporal claim. The inhibitor effect is a single time-point measurement.
7. **No wet-lab validation**: The transcript response does not establish direct target action or a mechanism.
