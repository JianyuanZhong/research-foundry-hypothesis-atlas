# Early post-TACE reserve decline: corroborated decompensation or ascertainment intensity?

## Scientific deliverable and clinical decision

The deliverable is a newly fitted, leakage-safe prognostic study in the local HCC snapshot. It will estimate whether liver-reserve worsening during days 3–14 after a first recorded high-specificity TACE predicts a new recorded hepatic-decompensation event during days 15–90, and whether that signal survives a stricter same-encounter examination corroboration and explicit measurement-opportunity analysis.

The consequential decision is narrow: should early laboratory deterioration in a patient who was apparently compensated at TACE trigger prospective clinical assessment and reconsideration before further locoregional treatment? This experiment cannot answer whether intensified monitoring or changing treatment improves outcomes. It can determine whether the observed signal is reproducible for a more specific observed-care endpoint, or is plausibly a documentation/measurement-process artifact.

Completion requires more than a schema audit:

- one index row per eligible patient, a flow/eligibility table, and raw-to-normalized procedure, HCC, compensation, assay and outcome dictionaries;
- assay coverage, missingness, timing and observation-opportunity tables;
- a prespecified baseline-reserve-by-early-worsening interaction estimate for diagnosis-only decompensation, the same estimate for the corroborated endpoint when event support permits, their paired contrast, and absolute-risk contrasts;
- locked test predictions from a static regularized baseline and a temporal learned alternative on the same endpoint;
- calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, AUROC, AUPRC, decision-curve net benefit and patient-bootstrap uncertainty;
- mutually exclusive outcome-category, ascertainment-intensity, vocabulary, censoring and negative-control results; and
- a conclusion explicitly classified as supportive, adverse or inconclusive under the rules below.

## Evidence already supported versus unresolved claim

The local HCC files establish that encounter-linked procedures and diagnoses, dated examinations, and repeated dated laboratory records are available. A bounded read-only audit found 45,643 qualifying high-specificity procedure rows in 19,163 patients; after requiring HCC on/before the index and applying the recorded-compensation proxy, 18,218 patients remained. There were 196 patients with a qualifying diagnosis-only outcome in days 15–90. A raw text scan found 218 same-encounter examination hits among 80 patients before conservative context filtering. These counts are feasibility evidence, not adjudicated incidence and not evidence that any clinical hypothesis is true. The early six-assay observations are uneven, so the outcome and model-support gates below are essential.

The strongest claim supported before fitting is therefore only: this snapshot can support an observed-record prognostic analysis of post-procedure laboratory trajectories, with a possible but unvalidated narrative corroboration endpoint.

The unresolved claim is:

> In recorded-compensated HCC patients after a first high-specificity TACE, the interaction between early reserve worsening and baseline reserve is positive for a new diagnosis-plus-same-encounter-examination corroboration during days 15–90, and is not materially explained by measurement opportunity available before day 15.

The alternative outcome-specific claim is that early worsening predicts diagnosis-only events but not corroborated events. That result would falsify the interpretation that the parent signal is independently corroborated as hepatic decompensation in this snapshot, while not proving that biological reserve is irrelevant.

This is an observational prognostic estimand. It is not a causal effect of TACE, an estimate of treatment harm, a treatment rule, a mediation analysis, or a validated decompensation score.

## Exposure and assay dictionary

The six primary assay families are albumin (`白蛋白`), total bilirubin (`总胆红素`), INR (`国际标准化比值`), platelet count (`血小板计数`), creatinine (`肌酐`) and sodium (`钠`). Primary fitting uses exact canonical labels after trimming; acute-labelled variants such as `白蛋白（急）`, `总胆红素（急）`, `肌酐（急）` and `钠（急）` are retained as raw variants and added only in a prespecified sensitivity if their numeric scale is demonstrated equivalent. Do not use substring matching that would incorrectly include `前白蛋白`, `糖化白蛋白`, urine assays, `氨基末端利钠肽前体` or sodium/chloride ratios. The INR/PT sensitivity replaces INR with exact `凝血酶原时间`; it is not pooled with INR, and the catalog warns that lab units have no separate unit column.

Use numeric `定量结果` only after rejecting impossible, ambiguous, qualitative-only or unit-unsupported values. Orient component changes so higher values mean worse reserve change: bilirubin, INR/PT and creatinine upward; albumin, platelets and sodium downward. Standardize directions using training data only. The baseline-reserve score is the mean of available oriented baseline components under the minimum-component rule; the early-worsening score is the corresponding oriented change from the selected baseline observation to the early observation. Retain individual components, recency, delay, availability and counts as separate variables; missingness is never imputed as a clinical value.

## Outcomes and ascertainment contrast

### D: diagnosis-only recorded event

D is the first new, non-uncertain diagnosis in days 15–90 for ascites, encephalopathy, hepatic failure or upper gastrointestinal bleeding. The qualifying vocabulary is frozen before model fitting. Event time is the linked encounter time; no diagnosis row is treated as independently timestamped. Exclude concepts already recorded on or before index+14. Report each component, first event date, encounter, and ambiguity/uncertainty counts. A new encounter alone is not D.

### C: strict same-encounter corroborated proxy

C=1 only if D=1 and the first qualifying D encounter has an examination row with parseable `开始时间` in days 15–90 whose `检查所见` or `检查诊断` contains a positive concept in the same domain:

- ascites: `腹水` or `腹腔积液`;
- encephalopathy: `肝性脑病`;
- gastrointestinal bleeding: `上消化道出血`, `消化道出血`, `呕血` or `黑便`;
- hepatic failure: `肝衰竭` or an exact mapped failure term.

Require the same (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`) and the examination date within the outcome window. Apply the local conservative context parser to reject explicit negation, uncertainty, family-history, hypothetical and historical markers where supported. Ambiguous text is C=0 and is retained for audit. `clinical_documents` is not a primary corroborator because it has no native timestamp; pathology is not used for this temporal endpoint. Examination text is an unvalidated lexical proxy, not clinical adjudication.

Create mutually exclusive categories: no D, D without C, C. A secondary treatment-concordance flag may record a newly started, prespecified medication pattern within diagnosis time−1 to +7 days using `medications`; it is not an independent endpoint because treatment may respond to the diagnosis and drugs have multiple indications. It cannot replace C.

## Estimands and analysis

The primary scientific estimand is the coefficient and absolute-risk interaction in:

`logit P(Y=1) = alpha + beta1*early_worsening + beta2*baseline_reserve + beta3*(early_worsening × baseline_reserve) + covariates`

where Y is first D, and secondarily C if support permits. Report beta3 and model-based risk differences for a one-SD early worsening at training-derived baseline-reserve quartiles. Covariates are age, sex, index department, prior HCC history, six baseline components, baseline recency/availability, early assay delay/counts, and pre-day-15 encounter/examination/order opportunity variables. The primary interaction model must not use post-event counts or outcome-window utilization.

Use patient-level bootstrap resampling (fixed seed; target at least 1,000 successful replicates) to refit paired D and C models and estimate beta3(C)−beta3(D) and paired absolute-risk contrasts. Do not treat C as independent of D. If the C event-support gate fails—fewer than 20 complete-case C events, or fewer than 10 events in a required split—do not fit an unstable C regression; report C incidence, D/C concordance and exact/binomial or bootstrap uncertainty descriptively. If the complete-window negative count or D support fails, stop the corresponding inferential claim and classify it inconclusive.

Fit the mutually exclusive multinomial outcome (no D, D without C, C) with the same predictors when category support permits. This tests whether worsening is preferentially associated with corroborated events or merely with diagnosis recording. It remains descriptive of observed records.

Prespecified process analyses are:

- add only information available by index+14: early assay counts/delays, number of encounters and examinations in index−14 through index+14, index discharge timing/status and dated orders;
- report outcome-window encounter, examination and lab counts by category without conditioning the primary model on post-event counts;
- repeat among patients with a qualifying outcome-window encounter as a selection sensitivity, not as a causal adjustment;
- use one frozen unrelated/non-hepatic diagnosis vocabulary as an ascertainment negative control if its definition can be fixed before looking at results;
- compare primary and broader assay/procedure/text vocabularies, including the PT replacement, without selecting the version that confirms the hypothesis; and
- report D-only, C and treatment-concordance flags separately.

## Baseline versus substantive alternative

The simple baseline is a prespecified regularized logistic model using pre-index clinical context and baseline reserve only, with no early measurements. A delta extension adds the early-worsening score and the prespecified interaction. Both target D (and C only if gated) on identical locked patients.

The substantive alternative is patient-level temporal gradient-boosted trees over the ordered observations in index−14 through index+14. Inputs are the six assay values, assay identity, time-from-index, baseline/early indicator, component availability, missingness, recency, counts/delays, age, sex, prior HCC history and index department. Fit/tune only on a patient-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test split stratified by outcome category; use a later-index holdout only if event support allows. Cap at 500 trees and 10 configurations, calibrate on validation, lock test predictions, and report Brier, calibration intercept/slope, AUROC, AUPRC, decision-curve net benefit and bootstrap intervals for the same endpoint and patients. Fit a prespecified value-only ablation on the identical split using ordered assay values, assay identity and time while removing missingness, counts, delays and encounter/examination/order opportunity. Fit a complementary process-only ablation using those opportunity variables without assay values. These ablations are part of the scientific test, not model shopping.

The temporal model can reveal nonlinear thresholds, asynchronous component changes and patterns in the order of deterioration that a scalar reserve/delta score loses. It can also reveal that apparent performance comes from measurement counts, missingness or encounter intensity. Support for trajectory information requires the value-only model to improve locked-test calibration/Brier or decision-curve performance over the static model with uncertainty excluding a prespecified negligible margin; a gain confined to the process-only or full model is evidence for ascertainment dependence, not biological corroboration. The interaction estimate remains the prespecified regression estimand; feature importance is exploratory and cannot substitute for it.

A latent GP/mixed-effects trajectory model and a transformer/generative sequence model are deferred for this question. The specific dependency is not GPU access: it is insufficiently established corroborated-event support and unvalidated narrative outcome measurement. More latent capacity would add trajectory and local-optimum assumptions without resolving whether the endpoint represents decompensation. Revisit those alternatives only after adequate corroborated events, clinically reviewed text and evidence that trajectory shape—not ascertainment—is the unresolved uncertainty. This is a question-specific deferral, not a blanket ban on neural networks or GPU use.

Approximate compute: two CPU workers, up to 8 GiB memory, preprocessing/model fitting within 30 minutes for the filtered patient-level table, and up to 90 minutes for bootstrap and sensitivity analyses. No GPU is required by the scientific comparison. Preserve fitted artifacts, split seed, bootstrap seed, package versions, row counts and all derived tables. The total plan must remain within the configured 7,200-second science budget; if the C gate fails, skip only the ungated C fits rather than replacing the research question with an easier one.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive: beta3 is directionally positive for D and C; C has adequate support and nontrivial D/C concordance; the paired C-versus-D interaction contrast is not compatible with a clinically negligible corroboration-only signal; process controls do not materially erase the C association; and the temporal model’s locked-test performance/calibration improvement is not primarily attributable to counts, missingness or encounter opportunity. This supports incremental prediction of a stricter observed-care proxy and motivates blinded expert chart review and external validation. It does not establish true hepatic decompensation, causality, treatment harm or clinical utility.

Adverse: C is null or reversed while D is positive; D-without-C dominates; the association attenuates materially after pre-day-15 process controls; the result changes materially with text/procedure/assay vocabulary; or temporal gains are confined to measurement-process variables. This falsifies the claim that early worsening is independently corroborated in this snapshot and supports coding/ascertainment as a plausible explanation. It does not prove absence of biological deterioration.

Inconclusive: fewer than the support threshold of complete-case C events; wide paired intervals; no reliable 90-day observation opportunity; unstable lexical mapping; severe measurement imbalance; assay-unit ambiguity; failed patient-level split or later holdout; or inability to obtain expert review for a clinically meaningful interpretation. Do not collapse D and C or label this a negative biological finding.

Automatic verification can check source hashes, read-only paths, table/column existence, joins, one index per patient, exact windows, pre-day-15 exclusions, censoring, same-encounter corroboration, category exclusivity, splits, fitted artifacts, bootstrap outputs and agreement between conclusions and computed tables. It cannot adjudicate Chinese clinical text, determine treatment indication, recover uncaptured outpatient events/deaths/transplants, establish causal effects, or prove that prospective monitoring improves outcomes. Those require blinded clinician chart review, stronger outcome capture, external validation and a prospective/interventional study.

## Existing evidence, demonstrations and alternatives

The three research demonstrations were used only for method/scientific framing:

- Delphi/natural-history: concrete adaptation—date-ordered structured trajectories are appropriate to test here with a bounded temporal model; its Danish external validation and original UKB cohort are unavailable and are not claimed.
- Oncoformer: explicit missing dependency—images, the main article and complete STAR Methods are unavailable, so no multimodal reproduction is proposed; a lab-only temporal adaptation is already covered by the local alternative.
- ALADYNOULLI: explicit scientific deferral—its genetics-dependent claim is unavailable and its latent GP assumptions do not resolve the current endpoint-ascertainment question; revisit only after corroborated event support and clinical review.

The 30 imported expert seeds are all eICU, MIMIC-IV or UK Biobank problems. They were retrieved and assessed before parent selection; none has an HCC population or a justified cross-dataset participant key. They remain ordinary source candidates, not HCC parents. No cross-dataset merge is used.

Alternatives not chosen are recorded explicitly: an HCC resection/recurrence study was deferred because the snapshot lacks timestamped radiographic response, stage and reliable recurrence/adjudicated mortality; a treatment-effect comparison was deferred because treatment intent, dose, tumor burden and causal assignment are unavailable; and richer latent/transformer models were deferred for the outcome-support dependency above. Evidence that would justify revisiting them is documented: validated procedure intent and response data for treatment/recurrence, clinically adjudicated endpoints and adequate event density for richer sequence models, or external/cross-institutional care capture.

## Unavailable clinical evidence

The snapshot has no validated decompensation adjudication, grade/severity, complete outpatient history, reliable hospital identity, complete death/transplant capture, tumor stage, radiographic response, recurrence, treatment dose/intent or causal treatment assignment. Vitals, transfers and front_page are identifier-only; there are no image or waveform files. Exact dates are not released, so only within-patient local intervals are used. No result may be presented as a clinical diagnosis, mortality benefit, treatment recommendation or deployment-ready score.

The clinical research value is the resolution of a consequential evidentiary ambiguity: whether an apparently actionable post-TACE laboratory pattern survives a more specific outcome and observation-process stress test. The automatic experiment can establish computed prognostic associations and model behavior; clinical truth requires expert adjudication, missing evidence and another study.
