## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, CHI3L1, FABP1, IL-6/CRP family) as early-detection biomarkers of many diseases. Competing explanations for a robust cross-disease protein signal:
- **A (disease-specific):** the protein marks a process preferential to disease *D*.
- **B (shared systemic axis):** it marks a shared allostatic-load / inflammation / multimorbidity state (K2, K3), and the apparent disease association is that shared state.
- **C (treatment/operative correlate):** it changes with surgery / operative management / care intensity rather than disease biology (K1).

The unresolved question: **after removing the shared healthy-vs-diseased and co-occurrence axes, does any individual protein retain a disease-preferential residual that is not attributable to unrelated operative-treatment state?** This is consequential: it decides whether a nominated protein is a disease-biology candidate or only a marker of being co-diagnosed / under operative treatment. The strongest uncovered rival for the parent's transfer-gap is **treatment/operative state (C)**; this child keeps the collider-safe contrast that addresses it.

## Evidence base (closest inspected work; abstract-only — see limits)
- **[K1]** Lewandowska et al. 2026 — inflammatory proteins are frequently severity/pharmacodynamic/prognostic markers whose dynamics dissociate from clinical improvement; roles "should not be interpreted interchangeably". Bounds the branch: an apparent disease-specific signal may be a treatment/severity correlate. No cross-disease estimand.
- **[K2]** Guan et al. 2026 (ProAL50) — a 50-protein UKB score indexes a trans-disease allostatic-load state predicting cardiometabolic/malignant/pulmonary/hepatic/renal disease and mortality (incident T2D HR/SD 2.72). Direct support for a shared axis (B); does not test per-protein disease-preferential residual.
- **[K3]** Luo et al. 2026 — cross-system "protein aging modules" (IL-6, CRP, CXCL9/10, GDF15, IGF-1, VCAM-1, NT-proBNP, NfL, GFAP) track multisystem decline; supports shared modules (B) but not per-protein disease-specificity and does not control treatment state.

**Advance over [K1–K3]:** [K2]/[K3] establish a shared axis but never isolate per-protein disease-preferential residuals in a diseased-only design; [K1] warns markers mix severity/treatment roles but gives no quantitative separation. This study makes the shared-vs-specific distinction **falsifiable per protein** with a held-out cross-disease transfer gap that must survive co-occurrence and unrelated-procedure contrasts plus a permutation null. (Three works tagged distinctly; no exhausted novelty claim.)

## Design
- **Population:** UKB participants in the Olink subset present in the clinical table (`n_eid` = 53,014). Analysis unit: per-protein (2,923) × disease block.
- **Disease labels:** `s_41270_0_*` mapped to ICD-10 3-char blocks; candidate blocks = non-`Z*`, non-`R*`; positive-control pair and V01–Y98 negative-control block as in the parent.
- **Temporal boundary:** prevalent/current ICD-10 and OPCS state in a single frozen snapshot; **no incident/washout contrast, no temporal/causal claim**.
- **Covariates:** `n_31_0_0` (sex), `n_21003_0_0` (age), age-decile, comorbidity count/multimorbidity strata.

### Primary estimand (collider-safe; unchanged from parent)
Per-protein cross-disease **transfer-specificity gap** computed in **DISEASED-ONLY (case-case)** comparisons:
Δ_D = AUC_casecase(D vs D′) on held-out individuals with ≥1 of {D} ∪ {D′}, minus a co-occurrence-matched permutation null.
The treatment-safe version stratifies on **UNRELATED OPCS procedures only** (procedures not plausibly on the causal path of D): Δ_D^treat. Plain OPCS adjustment is **demoted to a non-interpretable diagnostic** (adjusting for a collider/proxy of severity can induce bias), and is reported as `delta_D_opcs_adjusted_diagnostic` — explicitly not the primary contrast.

### Scientific deliverable the future solver must fit/estimate
The solver must newly **fit and estimate** Δ_D (and Δ_D^treat) per protein on a leakage-controlled split, and report the pre-specified outputs. Nothing is precomputed here.
- **Baseline (1-D shared axis):** project the 2,923 proteins onto a single shared axis (PC1 / ProAL50-like 1-D score fit on a held-out training split), then compute Δ_D on the identical split with the identical estimand. Captures the shared-axis explanation (B) with one dimension.
- **Learned alternative (elastic-net / jointly-estimated latent axis):** elastic-net or a jointly-estimated low-rank latent-axis model fit on the same training split, evaluated on Δ_D on the same held-out diseased-only comparisons. **What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses:** the baseline forces all proteins onto one shared axis, so a protein whose disease-preferential residual is orthogonal to that axis is invisible; the learned alternative can estimate a low-rank basis that separates a disease-preferential component from the shared axis and tests whether any protein retains signal after removing the shared component. Edge is credited only if it survives the permutation band and unattributed-OPCS stratification (substantive uncertainty: does a per-protein disease-preferential residual exist at all), not for a small AUC gain.
- **Selections/deferrals:** a single-axis fit is reported for interpretability/leakage control; a deep sequence model is deferred (no added discriminating power for a 2,923-dim tabular NPX matrix within budget, and it would forfeit the collider-safe interpretability). Approximate resource budget: **CPU only** (no GPU required) — tabular per-protein fits over ~2.9k features × ~50k rows; solver envelope per `solver_planning_limits` (≤16 CPU, 256 GB, 28,800 s) is ample.

### Required solver outputs
- `results.json` — per-protein Δ_D, Δ_D^treat, `delta_D_opcs_adjusted_diagnostic`, permutation null bands, case/procedure counts used.
- `split_manifest.json` — frozen train/held-out split ids and held-out diseased-only comparison definitions (seed recorded).
- `code_list.json` — ICD-10 block map and OPCS unrelated-procedure code list actually used (adjudicated list).

### Falsification (numeric; pre-specified)
- **Permutation band:** 1,000 label permutations of D/D′ within joint **age-decile × sex × comorbidity-count** strata of the diseased-only set.
- **Inclusion rule:** a cell is estimated only with **≥200 cases** and **≥50 unrelated-procedure cases**.
- **Supportive:** Δ_D^treat lower bound **> 0** (below the permutation band) for ≥1 pre-specified disease pair — a protein retains a disease-preferential residual after the shared axis and unrelated-procedure contrast.
- **Adverse:** raw / Δ_D^casecase positive but Δ_D^treat collapses to ~null — the signal was shared morbidity / operative-treatment state, not disease-specific.
- **Inconclusive:** permutation band overlaps 0, or too few co-diagnosed cases / sparse strata (including an adjudicated unrelated-procedure stratum too small to meet ≥50).
- Each outcome states what it would and would **not** establish. An imprecise null is not treated as decisive refutation.

### Negative controls
No-ICD block; V01–Y98 external-cause block; procedure-only set (as in the parent).

## Resolved vs unresolved
**Resolved this episode:** the "planned" power statement is now an **observed** cross-tab; Olink∩clinical = 53,014 (100%); ≥200-case / ≥50-procedure attainability shown at the any-OPCS upper bound.

**Unresolved objections (explicit):**
1. **"Unrelated" is not yet operational.** The cross-tab uses *any* OPCS as an upper bound; the fraction surviving as truly *unrelated* after adjudication is unknown. ~89.7% OPCS prevalence means the unexposed stratum is nearly empty, so the treatment contrast may be weak/underpowered even where case counts are large.
2. **No temporal anchor** (no draw/death/cancer date; `eid`-keyed tables namespace-disjoint) → prevalent/cross-sectional only; no incidence, progression or causal claim.
3. **Inpatient ICD-10 under-ascertainment** may bias co-occurrence and the unrelated-procedure strata.
4. **Comorbidity count is a coarse severity proxy.**
5. **ICD-10 block / OPCS code lists require clinical adjudication** before the estimand is runnable as specified.
6. **Key evidence is abstract-only** ([K1]–[K3]); full texts not inspected.

**NO association result is claimed.** This is a design + computed data-resolvability artifact.
