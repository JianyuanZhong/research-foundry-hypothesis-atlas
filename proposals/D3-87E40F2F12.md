# MerTK-positive macrophage exposure and donor-paired FLS reprogramming

## Scientific opening and hypothesis

The strongest available evidence is that MerTK-positive synovial tissue macrophages are associated with remission-associated states and can induce repair-like responses in synovial fibroblasts [K1]. A separate abstract supports directional macrophage-to-fibroblast communication through an EGF-receptor-dependent inflammatory axis [K2]. However, tissue heterogeneity, donor and treatment differences, and composition effects remain serious alternatives [K3]. The unresolved claim is narrower and falsifiable:

> In the same RA fibroblast-like synoviocyte (FLS) donor, exposure to MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages induces a reproducible repair-associated transcriptional shift relative to MerTK/CD206-negative macrophages and FLS-only controls, and at least a prespecified subset of that shift is attenuated when MerTK activity is inhibited.

This is a hypothesis about an ex vivo directional response, not proof that MerTK signaling causes remission in patients. Resolving it matters because a reproducible, perturbable FLS response would justify targeted validation of a macrophage-to-stroma mechanism; a null or inhibitor-insensitive result would redirect attention toward macrophage composition, disease state, or indirect effects.

## Population, temporal boundary, and variables

Primary analysis population: all 33 recorded FLS/coculture samples in E-MTAB-8873, with the biological unit treated as the donor/condition cluster rather than 33 independent donors. The design metadata explicitly marks most samples as requiring a contributing-donor model. The experiment is an ex vivo 48-hour comparison; no longitudinal patient outcome is available.

Join key: `sample_id` in `E-MTAB-8873.design.json` to the corresponding column in `E-MTAB-8873.counts.tsv.gz`. Required metadata fields are `sample_id`, `donor_id`, `donor_status`, `other_donor_id`, `age`, `sex`, `tissue`, `cell_type`, `condition`, `treatment`, and `cells`. The matrix has 445 measured feature rows and 33 sample columns; preserve source feature identifiers, including transcript/version suffixes, and do not infer unmeasured genes.

Primary contrasts are defined only where the same FLS donor has comparable conditions:
- Mpos versus Mneg, using samples whose condition names identify MerTK/CD206-positive versus MerTK/CD206-negative macrophages;
- Mpos versus FLS-only, using the corresponding FLS-only control;
- Mpos versus Mpos+MerTK inhibitor, using the explicit inhibitor/preInhibitor treatment labels;
- Mneg versus Mneg+LPS as a positive inflammatory control, and FLS-only versus FLS+inhibitor as an assay-control comparison where available.

The exact eligible pairs are determined from the frozen design file before looking at expression. Mixed macrophage ratios (1:3 and 3:1) are a secondary dose-response exploratory analysis, not independent donors. LPS and inhibitor samples are not used to define the primary Mpos/Mneg effect.

## Outcomes and analysis

The outcome is the log2 fold change in each measured feature, with a prespecified repair-associated summary based on the intersection of the targeted panel and the study's stated repair markers (COL1A1/COL1A2, TGFBI/TGFB3, and other panel members only if their identifiers are verified in the feature map). The primary estimand is the donor-cluster mean contrast with uncertainty from a mixed-effects or hierarchical model that groups repeated samples by FLS donor and contributing macrophage donor; no ordinary donor t-test is permitted. If the dependency structure is too sparse for a stable random-effects estimate, report a donor-level paired summary with a bootstrap or permutation distribution conditional on donor, and label the result exploratory.

The simple baseline is a fixed, biologically interpretable pathway score: the mean of prespecified repair-associated features, computed after log transformation and sample-size normalization, with the Mpos-Mneg and Mpos-only contrasts. It is transparent and directly tests the stated mechanism.

The substantive alternative is a regularized multivariate response model (for example, elastic-net or a low-rank functional model) fitted within donor groups to predict the condition contrast from all 445 measured features, with donor-clustered cross-validation and a held-out donor/condition split. It can reveal coordinated gene programs and whether the repair signal is distributed across the panel rather than concentrated in a few markers; the baseline loses that structure. The learned model is not selected for a small predictive gain. It is retained only if it improves held-out reconstruction of the condition contrast or identifies a stable, interpretable program that the fixed score misses. A mechanistic alternative, a ligand-receptor/state interaction model, is deferred because the admitted data do not provide a complete macrophage transcriptome or validated ligand measurements for this assay.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive results require: (1) a reproducible Mpos-versus-Mneg repair shift in the primary donor-clustered analysis; (2) attenuation of that shift in the explicit MerTK-inhibitor contrast; (3) concordant direction in at least two independent FLS donor clusters; and (4) no comparable shift in the FLS-only/inhibitor control. The learned model must reproduce the fixed-score direction on held-out donors.

Adverse results include a reproducible Mpos/Mneg difference that is not attenuated by MerTK inhibition, a shift confined to one donor or batch, or an inflammatory rather than repair pattern. These would weaken the proposed MerTK-specific directional mechanism but could support a different macrophage-state effect. Inconclusive results include wide uncertainty, insufficient paired donors, failed feature-map resolution, or conflicting fixed and learned summaries; they justify a new assay or additional donors rather than a mechanistic conclusion.

Computationally checkable claims are the matrix joins, feature counts, contrast definitions, model fits, uncertainty, and held-out behavior. Clinical remission, joint destruction, direct target engagement, and patient-level treatment benefit require clinical adjudication, longitudinal outcomes, protein/phospho-MerTK or ligand measurements, and an independent perturbation study.
