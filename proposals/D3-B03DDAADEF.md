# Do remission-associated macrophage averages reflect captured state representation?

## Scientific question and deliverable

**Question:** Among rheumatoid-arthritis synovial membrane macrophages sampled once in E-MTAB-8322, does the observed remission-versus-treatment-resistant average-expression contrast have a robust, estimable component attributable to differences in captured macrophage expression-state proportions, or is within-state expression change at least as large?

**Falsifiable hypothesis (untested):** Conditional on a technical-overlap gate, more than half of the squared multigene remission/resistant macrophage-expression distance is attributable to state-proportion differences rather than state-conditional expression differences, and that attribution is stable to donor resampling and leave-one-donor-out state fitting. The 50% threshold defines “dominant” before any analysis. Composition and within-state regulation may coexist. Here “state” means a reproducible expression cluster inferred in this assay, not a stable lineage, transition, or mechanism.

**Clinical importance:** An average synovial signature alone can motivate the wrong next experiment. If state representation robustly accounts for most of this cross-sectional contrast, prioritize measuring recruitment/retention and tissue localization in a follow-up study; if within-state expression dominates, prioritize targeted cell perturbation or functional testing. If the decomposition is unidentifiable or unstable, stop and obtain clinical, spatial, or technical evidence before choosing either direction. No result alone selects or changes a patient’s treatment.

## Evidence-supported and unresolved claims

**Strongest evidence-supported claim:** The inspected literature reports synovial macrophage heterogeneity and remission-associated macrophage observations [K1], describes HBEGF+ macrophage–fibroblast invasiveness as a plausible within-state/tissue-crosstalk alternative [K2], and reports a spatial LYVE1+ macrophage network associated with treatment response in another, spatial and longitudinal study [K3]. These works support biological plausibility and important boundary conditions. K1–K3 were inspected at abstract level only; no full text or supplementary methods are claimed here.

**Untested claim:** Whether the average expression contrast in the specifically bound 13 E-MTAB-8322 RA donors is quantitatively dominated by captured-state proportions rather than expression conditional on state, after testing whether donor status is entangled with available technical structure. E-MTAB-8322 provides a one-time macrophage-only assay. This analysis is a descriptive decomposition, not a causal or treatment-effect estimate, not independent replication, and not evidence of cell transition. The source study may overlap the closest remission literature [K1]; this proposal does not claim novelty of remission-associated macrophage subsets or that the decomposition has never been reported in its detailed source article.

**Strongest rival and artifacts:** Within-state transcriptional regulation is the direct biological rival, and can coexist with a composition effect. A potentially more fundamental rival is that status groups differ in current disease severity, therapy class or history, biopsy selection, cell recovery/capture, or sequencing/library structure. These factors could alter both the measured state proportions and mean expression. The prepared annotation has an empty/null `treatment` field and does not provide disease-activity scores or longitudinal clinical records; statistical adjustment cannot repair those absences. Spatial fibroblast/vascular niches are also unmeasured. Even an adjusted or stable association would not identify a mechanism.

## Analysis plan and method comparison

First, reconcile donor/status mappings and inspect overlap between response group and observable technical variables (run count, ENA experiment/assay, platform/library fields if variable), along with age, sex, captured-cell yield, and sample-processing descriptors available in the design/SDRF. Show complete donor-level tables and standardized differences rather than relying on a regression coefficient with n=13. Quantify RNA/cell recovery and library-depth dependence. Technical variables that are constant across groups cannot be adjusted; if status is perfectly confounded with an observable processing/run variable, stop biological attribution and report non-identifiability. Cell yields characterize sampling precision, not population abundance in tissue.

**Baseline:** aggregate all captured macrophage expression per donor from the bound single-cell matrix, then estimate remission-minus-resistant differences for common expressed genes after library-size normalization and a fixed variance-stabilizing transformation. Confirm direction and donor influence with the prepared donor profile as a scale/lineage sensitivity check. Use equal donor weighting; uncertainty comes from donor bootstrap and donor-label permutation (up to all 1,716 assignments of 7 labels among 13), never from treating cells as replicates. The baseline determines whether an all-cell average difference is present but cannot say why.

**Alternative selected:** cross-fitted donor-level state decomposition using the original per-cell profiles. In leave-one-donor-out folds, learn low-dimensional structure and macrophage expression-state clusters on the other donors, map the held-out donor’s cells to learned states, and align fold-specific state labels by marker/centroid similarity. Report held-out state assignment and mapping stability; the hypothesis is not interpretable if state maps do not reproduce. Estimate a donor’s state-proportion vector and state-conditional gene profile, then compute standardized group means and the composition-versus-within-state contribution to squared multigene expression distance using symmetric Shapley allocation (the composition-by-expression interaction split equally). Restrict the primary feature set to genes detected in both groups under a label-blind donor/cell prevalence filter fixed before status comparisons. Use identical donors, genes, normalization and equal-donor group weights for baseline and decomposition. Bootstrap and permute donors; report uncertainty and all leave-one-donor-out results. Do not make cell-level significance claims or use learned states as treatment predictors.

The method-comparison rationale, targets, split, uncertainty approach and CPU budget are recorded separately in `method-comparison.md`. The decomposition is selected because it could attribute an observed average difference to two biologically distinct statistical components; no neural embedding or GPU is necessary for this small, donor-limited question. Cross-fitting limits donor leakage but cannot make expression-defined clusters independent of the biological contrast or eliminate capture confounding.

**Estimand and decision rule:** The primary value is the Shapley-attributed fraction of total squared remission/resistant group-mean expression distance assigned to composition. “Composition-dominant” means a point estimate above 0.50 with donor-level uncertainty and held-out state assignment sufficiently stable to support that direction. This threshold is a descriptive criterion, not a clinical decision threshold. Report the component magnitudes as well as the share, because ratios can be unstable if total distance is near zero. Do not silently redefine the gene set, clustering resolution, or split after seeing the desired direction; report sensitivity as exploratory and require any selected alternative to be identified as exploratory.

## Outcomes, falsification, and next decisions

- **Supportive:** technical/status overlap passes the prespecified gate; held-out state mappings and donor influence are stable; the composition share is above 0.50 with donor-level uncertainty supporting a dominant direction. This supports only a cross-sectional decomposition of selected, captured macrophages. Continue to spatial/cell-count validation and clinical adjudication; do not say state abundance causes remission.
- **Adverse:** a precise and stable result assigns at least as much distance to within-state expression as to composition, or the composition result reverses under donor holdout/state mapping checks. This weakens this cohort’s composition-dominant hypothesis and redirects the next study toward validating within-state programs or other measured alternatives. It does not establish a particular pathway or mechanism.
- **Inconclusive/defer:** wide donor-level intervals, low state-map stability, small/near-zero total distance, status/technical non-overlap, dominant donor leverage, or lack of sufficient state support in one group. This means the dataset cannot separate the explanations reliably; it is not decisive evidence for equality or against a biological effect. Obtain better-resolved clinical metadata, independent donor replication, spatial/whole-tissue measurement or a prospective design before advancing.

**Exact evidence needed beyond this analysis:** Clinician review of remission/resistance definitions, assessment date, disease-activity score, drug class/dose/duration and prior treatment trajectory, biopsy indication/selection, and donor-level covariates to distinguish severity and treatment history; independent pathology or spatial measurement to determine whether captured state proportions reflect tissue localization/abundance; and prospective/perturbational evidence to claim state transitions, mechanism, or treatment causality. Computationally checkable outputs include joins/cohort counts, cell/run yield summaries, state-fit reproducibility, effect/decomposition calculations, donor-level uncertainty and whether conclusions follow those outputs. Clinical label validity, unrecorded severity/treatment confounding, mechanisms, and treatment effects require adjudication or another study; an automatic verifier cannot establish them.
