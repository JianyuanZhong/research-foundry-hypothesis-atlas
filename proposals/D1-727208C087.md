# Episode 20 lead child — landmark selection, observation opportunity, and respiratory-transition provenance

Status: complete design proposal and substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. This is a planned experiment; no cohort count, fitted coefficient, prediction, metric, confidence interval, or scientific result is claimed.

## Scientific opening and hypothesis

The parent asks whether a pre-landmark respiratory-treatment/process signal and a clinical-offset-linked treatment/respiratory pairing remain associated with a later strict respiratoryCare interval-start after adjustment for a strictly pre-landmark physiologic trajectory. That question remains vulnerable to a selection and documentation explanation: the analysis conditions on being alive and observable at six hours, and a respiratoryCare start, treatment row, or respiratoryCharting row close to that start may be entered in a common post-transition documentation episode. Early alive or expired ICU discharge, carePlanEOL activity, sparse observation, and source-specific missingness can make the six-hour risk set a selected subset and can make a post-event signal look like pre-event evidence.

The strongest claim currently supported by the inspected evidence and the parent is narrower: eICU contains linked multi-center recorded ICU events and measurements, and a frozen timing audit can describe associations among recorded offsets. It does not establish that a treatment row is an order, administration, delivered therapy, or bedside action, nor that a respiratoryCare interval start is physiologic onset. The unresolved claim is whether the parent’s treatment concordance and physiologic-trajectory increment are visible using information available before the six-hour landmark and remain after measured observation opportunity and landmark selection are audited. The principal alternatives are:

1. A reproducible pre-landmark operational signal: pre-360 treatment/process features and the pre-360 physiologic trajectory retain a treatment-block increment for the later strict recorded endpoint across hospitals after selection weighting.
2. Selection/observation: the increment is concentrated among stays that remain in the ICU, have dense or source-complete charting, or have early EOL documentation; it attenuates after measured continuation and observation weights.
3. Backfill/common documentation: treatment/respiratory concordance is assembled at or after the endpoint. Pre-360 concordance is weak or absent, while post-landmark or post-start treatment/entry timing is strong and similar to a documentation-only endpoint.

Falsifiable hypothesis: in the parent’s adult first-ICU population, the pre-landmark treatment/process increment and its clinical-offset concordance with a later strict respiratoryCare interval-start will remain directionally and quantitatively reproducible in the S6 observed risk set after a prespecified six-hour continuation/observation audit and stabilized measured observation weighting, and will be present in at least five viable held-out hospitals. The rival predicts that the increment materially attenuates under the weights, is restricted to high source availability or carePlanEOL strata, or is absent when concordance is required to be visible by minute 360. This is an association/provenance hypothesis, not a treatment effect, causal claim, physiologic mechanism, or deployment claim.

“Visible before the landmark” means that the relevant treatment/process feature or treatment-to-`R_start_doc` pair uses a finite treatmentoffset <=360 and is formed without any post-360 row. A post-landmark-before-start pair has treatmentoffset in (360, `R_start_doc`] and is descriptive timing evidence, not a predictor. A post-start pair has treatmentoffset > `R_start_doc`; it can only be used in the backfill audit. RespiratoryCharting is partitioned separately by its clinical `respchartoffset` and entry `respchartentryoffset`. Because treatment.csv has no treatment-entry time, the study cannot determine whether a treatment row was back-entered; it must report that limitation rather than infer it.

A supportive result would change the next decision toward source-level adjudication of treatment order/administration, device state, and bedside time, and would justify an independently validated transition detector. An adverse result would redirect interpretation toward selection or shared documentation workflow. An inconclusive result would justify no stronger interpretation and would identify the missing timing or clinical evidence needed for a new study.

The primary model uses only treatment rows with 0<=treatmentoffset<=360.

For every valid `R_start_doc` at s, retain raw identifiers and create an audit partition for w=10,30,60 minutes:

- `C_pre(w)`: treatmentoffset <=360 and 0 < s-treatmentoffset <=w.
- `C_mid(w)`: 360<treatmentoffset<=s and 0 < s-treatmentoffset <=w; this is post-landmark but pre-start treatment concordance and is descriptive, not a predictor.
- `C_after(w)`: s<treatmentoffset<=s+w; this is post-start treatment recording and is a backfill/common-documentation warning, not evidence of prediction.
- `C_clin(w)`, `C_clinchart(w)`, and `C_entry(w)`: preserve the parent’s treatment/respiratoryCharting comparisons against respiratoryCare clinical offset s, respiratoryCharting clinical offset, and respiratoryCharting entry offset. Keep exact ties, duplicate/backfill flags, lags, and raw row IDs. RespiratoryCharting rows are additionally split by whether respchartoffset and respchartentryoffset are <=360, (360,s], or >s.

The key comparison is the pre-landmark versus post-landmark partition, not a claim that either timestamp is independently valid. A positive `C_pre` increment with no post-start concentration, stable observation weighting, and held-out transport is compatible with a pre-existing operational recorded signal. A signal confined to `C_mid`, `C_after`, entry offsets >s, or generic documentation/non-respiratory controls supports assembly after the landmark or common workflow. Since treatment lacks an entry field, no treatment-side clinical-versus-entry decomposition is possible; this is an essential unavailable dependency. Treatment rows in (360,720] never enter endpoint eligibility or primary predictors.

## Comparable methods, fitting target, split, uncertainty, and resource plan

The methods receive the same patient-level 12-bin six-channel value/mask input, scalar parent state/process features, source availability/density features, hospital covariates, EOL/selection audit features, and exactly the same endpoint, competing-exit labels, `W6` weights, folds, and missingness handling.

- **Transparent baseline:** cause-specific discrete-time elastic-net hazards for R_start_doc, exit, and administrative follow-up. Flatten the 12-bin values/masks and concatenate scalar features; standardize and impute within the training fold; choose penalty/mixing by training-fold likelihood with a one-standard-error rule. Report signed coefficients and grouped trajectory, availability, treatment, EOL, and weight-sensitive contrasts. This is the primary auditable test of whether treatment-specific pre-landmark information remains after observed physiology and observation opportunity.
- **Learned temporal alternative:** a small two-layer one-dimensional temporal-convolution branch over the same 12-bin value/mask channels, global pooling, and a dense branch for the same scalar features, with cause-specific hazard heads. Use the same split, target, weights, early stopping, and fixed parameter budget. It may reveal late oxygenation/respiratory-rate shape and cross-channel interactions that a flattened additive baseline loses; it is not a score-maximization substitute and does not confer semantic validity. If the solver envelope cannot support it after measured profiling, defer it explicitly and retain the elastic-net analysis without claiming that nonlinear structure is absent.

Hospital-held-out evaluation is primary: group all stays from each hospital in one fold, with prespecified viable-hospital minimum five; no hospital contributes to both fit and evaluation. Report per-hospital and stratum estimates, pooled cluster-weighted summaries secondarily, paired 2,000 hospital-cluster bootstrap intervals (or a predeclared equivalent if the number of viable hospitals makes 2,000 unstable), effective sample sizes, weight diagnostics, cause-specific Brier score, calibration-in-the-large/slope, cumulative incidence at 720, incremental held-out log likelihood/deviance for treatment blocks, and AUROC/PR-AUC only as secondary descriptions. A nonlinear predictive gain without a stable pre-landmark treatment-block contrast is not supportive.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted/estimated: (i) all-eligible continuation and observation-opportunity models; (ii) frozen `W6` weights and their ESS/positivity audit; (iii) weighted and unweighted parent elastic-net estimates and same-input temporal-convolution estimates; (iv) pre/mid/after-start concordance and respiratoryCharting clinical/entry timing tables; (v) hospital/stratum/EOL/early-exit results; (vi) falsification nulls; and (vii) a claim-to-output table. ## Falsification and interpretation

Freeze a manifest before fitting containing source/catalog/schema/header hashes, row/ID audits, adult and all-eligible/S6 flows, early alive/expired exits, starting strata, raw vocabulary rules, trajectory and availability construction, continuation/weight specification, hospital folds, endpoint rules, pre/mid/after partition, nulls, and output filenames.

Required falsification checks:

- **Selection attenuation:** compare unweighted S6, stabilized W6, and observation-density/source-complete stratified estimates. If the treatment increment disappears under W6 or is confined to dense/source-complete/EOL strata, favor selection/ascertainment.
- **Early-exit audit:** report unitdischargeoffset <=360 by alive/expired status, hospital, and source state. Do not call expired discharge a known event time or assign it R_start_doc.
- **Pre/post timing:** require any supportive treatment concordance to be visible in C_pre. Concordance confined to C_mid, C_after, post-start respiratoryCharting entries, or exact ties is adverse provenance evidence. Treatment’s missing entry timestamp prevents a stronger backfill claim.
- **Endpoint controls:** repeat the timing audit for R_stop_doc, first respiratoryCharting row after 360, and the inherited non-respiratory treatment control. A matching generic-documentation signal is not respiratory-specific.
- **Schedule/source nulls:** cyclically shift treatment offsets within each stay’s observed opportunity and permute treatment rows within hospital × density strata, preserving counts, labels, and schedule; recompute concordance. Permute complete pre-landmark vital trajectories within hospital × starting-stratum while preserving masks and density. These are provenance/measurement nulls, not proofs of independence.
- **Boundary/tie controls:** repeat [0,330] plus gap, remove exact offsets and duplicate/backfill rows, and compare clinical versus chart-clinical versus entry anchors without selecting a favorable window afterward.
- **Transport:** no pooled result is supportive if one hospital or one starting/observation stratum supplies the direction; require per-hospital held-out estimates and uncertainty.
- **Weight validity:** report truncation, maximum weight, ESS, positivity and influence by hospital. If weights are unstable, the selection-standardized estimand is inconclusive, not silently dropped.

Supportive results require: at least five viable held-out hospitals; adequate pre-landmark source coverage and weight positivity; a nontrivial pre-landmark treatment/process contrast after the W6 and trajectory blocks; concordant direction in elastic-net and temporal models; no single hospital, EOL/early-exit, density, source state, tie, duplicate, schedule-null, documentation-only endpoint, or non-respiratory control dominating; and no concentration in C_mid/C_after. This establishes a reproducible recorded association compatible with an operational transition beyond measured selection/physiology, not treatment delivery or physiologic onset.

Adverse results include strong W6 attenuation, early-discharge/EOL or dense-charting concentration, absent C_pre with only post-landmark/post-start concordance, entry-offset dominance, generic-documentation/non-respiratory replication, schedule-null failure, unstable weights, or hospital non-transport. Interpret these as support for selection, source availability, severity, or shared documentation workflow; do not claim the parent’s treatment signal is corroborated.

Inconclusive results include fewer than five viable hospitals, insufficient pre-landmark positivity or channel coverage, low ESS, incompatible endpoint semantics, unstable vocabulary, broad intervals, or the treatment-side absence of entry time preventing a clinical-versus-entry conclusion. An imprecise null does not refute a physiologic process.

## What is computable and what is not

Computable from the configured fields are: adult first-ICU and all-eligible flows; early alive/expired discharge status and ICU-relative opportunity; strict respiratoryCare interval integrity and R_start_doc; pre-landmark vital trajectories; source counts, occupied bins, offsets, masks and density; carePlanEOL timing; continuation weights under stated assumptions; held-out association/prediction contrasts; C_pre/C_mid/C_after; respiratoryCharting clinical/entry lag partitions; and schedule, trajectory, endpoint and transport falsification outputs.

Not computable or clinically adjudicated here are treatment order versus administration, treatment entry/backfill time, indication/dose/delivery, ventilator/device state, bedside time, physiologic onset, a reliable death time, untreated severity, causality, or mechanism. Stronger conclusions require expert review of raw treatment/respiratory records against orders, administration/device logs and bedside timing, ideally waveform/FiO2/ventilator data, plus independent or prospective validation. The exact treatment vocabulary and strict interval rules also require clinical review.

## Alternatives and deferral record

The elastic-net baseline is retained because it is transparent and directly tests the pre-landmark incremental association. The same-input temporal convolution is retained because it can reveal trajectory shape and cross-channel interactions that flattening loses; it uses matched hospital splits, target, weights and uncertainty. A raw event-sequence transformer is deferred: the available treatment stream has no entry timestamp and the question is provenance separation rather than maximal predictive accuracy. A causal treatment-effect model is deferred because assignment, indication, administration and delivery are unavailable. A mechanistic ventilator model is deferred because validated device state, FiO2 and waveforms are unavailable. These alternatives should be revisited only if treatment-side provenance, richer physiologic data, or expert adjudication becomes available.
