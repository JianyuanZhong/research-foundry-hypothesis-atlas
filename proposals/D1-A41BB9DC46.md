## Repaired availability contract: ARCD48

Let H48=t0+48h be the specimen horizon and R48=H48+24h the result-readout freeze. The extra 24 hours do not extend the physiologic outcome window: only charttime at or before H48 contributes. The source audit motivates this finite freeze: for item 50912, nonnegative lag was at most 6h for 4,060,768 rows, 12h for 4,308,988, and 24h for 4,316,578; only 2,461 had lag over 24h. Median lag was 82 minutes, the 95th percentile 384 minutes, and the 99th 555 minutes. Six rows lacked storetime and 46 had negative lag; both are excluded.

Define C0 as the latest decision-available qualifying item-50912 result with t0-48h < charttime <= t0 and charttime <= storetime <= t0, ordered by (charttime,storetime,labevent_id) descending after correction resolution. This prevents baseline look-ahead.

A qualifying follow-up specimen has t0 < charttime <= H48 and charttime <= storetime <= R48. Partition by specimen/chart time into B1=(t0,t0+24h] and B2=(t0+24h,H48].

Available-result creatinine dysfunction through 48 specimen-hours (ARCD48) is trinary:

1. Definite threshold-positive: C0 exists and any qualifying follow-up result is at least C0+0.3 mg/dL or at least 1.5*C0. A positive can be established with one specimen.
2. Observed no threshold under the two-bin contract: C0 exists, at least one qualifying result exists in each B1 and B2, and every qualifying follow-up result is below both thresholds.
3. Unknown [0,1]: C0 is absent; no positive is seen and either B1 or B2 lacks a qualifying result; a clock/unit/key contradiction prevents classification; or death/discharge occurs before the two-bin contract is met. Silence, survival, discharge, urine output, or no RRT never makes this category negative.

“Observed no threshold” is not “no AKI,” a complete scheduled opportunity, or renal safety. The endpoint omits urine-output KDIGO staging, preadmission baseline kidney function, and a seven-day creatinine window, and does not identify clinical AKI. Timed accepted ICU RRT remains separately reported observed use; date-only ICD RRT retains the parent's brackets.

Repeat state assignment at readouts H48, H48+6h, H48+12h, H48+24h, and all eventual nonnegative-lag results. Repeat separately with item 52024 under its own matrix; never combine it with 50912. Report one-bin and 12-hour/four-bin contracts only as sampling-density sensitivities.

## Estimation, missing observation, and sampling bias

Post-t0 creatinine sampling is an outcome-observation process affected by illness and potentially by loop initiation. It is forbidden from treatment assignment, baseline overlap, or e0; it may enter only the outcome/observation ledger and artificial-censoring history where temporally available. Similar arm-wise sampling rates do not prove exchangeable observation.

## Gates and interpretation

Computational feasibility requires inherited gates plus: at least 100 definite ARCD48 positives overall; at least 100 compatible records in each arm with the two-bin contract; final-arm unknown fraction <=20% and difference <=5 points; baseline C0 difference <=5 points; item-50912 negative-lag fraction <=0.1%; >24h result-lag fraction <=1%; clock/key/unit contradictions <=1%; and no material classification reversal across 6h/12h/24h/eventual readouts. Failure is inconclusive/infeasible, not safety.

Supportive recorded evidence requires every inherited gate, airway lower one-sided limit >+0.05, all-eligible ARCD48 upper one-sided limit for the sharp RD bound <+0.03, and observed-RRT7 upper bound <+0.05. Passing only in the selected two-bin subset supports no all-eligible renal claim. Permitted language: “In the measured-overlap discovery cohort, the recorded-loop strategy was associated with the prespecified airway outcome and the available record excluded the prespecified three-point increase in record-defined 48-hour creatinine dysfunction and five-point increase in observed RRT.” It must immediately state that this is not clinical AKI, renal safety, or a causal effect.

Adverse recorded evidence is present if the all-eligible ARCD48 lower one-sided RD bound is >=+0.03, RRT7 lower bound is >=+0.05 under both date brackets, or an inherited severe-outcome gate is adverse. A two-bin-subset lower limit >=+0.03 with aligned sampling diagnostics is a “measured-contract renal concern,” not an all-eligible or causal toxicity conclusion.

Margin-falsified retains the parent's airway definition. Inconclusive includes any feasibility failure, ARCD48 bounds crossing the margin, wide unknown bounds, >5-point observation imbalance, sampling/readout reversal, or a renal result supported only by the selected subset. Adverse findings are valid; the verifier must not prefer confirmation.
