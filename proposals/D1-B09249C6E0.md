# Proposal: ascertainment-safe AFP/hepatic trajectories and later therapeutic pathways after TACE

## Episode 16 successor and actual deliverable

This is a substantive child of assessed valid parent [prior hypothesis]. It retains the parent’s encounter-constrained procedure identity, HCC-index definition, day-45 risk set, dated AFP/hepatic inputs, capture controls, patient-level temporal locking, competing recorded pathways, and dynamic diagnostic-contact landmark.

The repair is to remove a future-observation condition from the primary outcome. In the parent, “therapeutic TACE with a prior local reassessment opportunity” is useful descriptively but can select on a post-index lab/examination and therefore blend disease-linked pathway information with the chance of being measured before the event. Episode 16 makes the first therapeutic TACE transition from the full day-45 risk set primary. Reassessment opportunity is reported as a time-varying pathway state and as a sensitivity, never as a requirement for the primary event. The first diagnostic-only contact remains a separate dynamic landmark, with predictors frozen immediately before that contact.

The scientific deliverable is newly fitted, leakage-safe B0, Bobs and B2 competing-transition models; a prespecified observation/loss-of-local-record sensitivity; a dynamic post-diagnostic landmark analysis; and, if feasible under the solver envelope, the same-target irregular-time M3. Completion requires reproducible index, episode, landmark and split manifests; locked predictions; competing cumulative-incidence, calibration, alert-curve and uncertainty outputs; episode-identity and ascertainment contrasts; a negative-control analysis; and an interpretation map linking each claim to a computed estimate and interval. Discovery has not fitted these models and claims no clinical result.

## Strongest supported evidence and unresolved claim

The strongest available evidence supports only a linkable local recorded-care substrate. The HCC snapshot has encounter-constrained identifiers, dated encounters, dated procedure rows, dated laboratory rows, dated examinations, diagnosis names without native diagnosis times, and optional dated orders. The directly inspected schemas confirm that all child tables join to encounters by (患者主索引, 就诊号); procedures have 手术, 开始时间, 结束时间, 手术来源; labs have 检验, 定性结果, 定量结果, 标本类型, 检验时间 but no unit column; and examinations have 检查, 检查所见, 检查诊断, 开始时间. The procedure audit in the parent support record found common angiography/embolization labels, TACE-family labels and untimed procedure rows, supporting an episode-identity sensitivity but not proving treatment intent. No available field establishes viable tumour, radiologic response, treatment indication, death, outside care, appropriateness or treatment benefit.

The unresolved clinical question is:

> Among adults with HCC who have a first recorded TACE-like episode and no encounter-constrained therapeutic liver-directed episode through day 45, does the early AFP/hepatic trajectory provide disease-specific information about a later recorded therapeutic TACE pathway beyond who is measured, contacted, and retained in local care, and does that information persist when a later diagnostic contact is represented as a time-ordered gateway?

The primary hypothesis is:

> On the full eligible day-45 risk set, adding early AFP/hepatic values and changes to an observation/pathway baseline will reduce the locked-test alert fraction at 90% sensitivity for first therapeutic TACE by at least 10 percentage points, with a patient-clustered bootstrap interval excluding zero, while the gain is not reproduced for non-liver recorded procedures or a pre-index placebo. The gain will remain at least 5 percentage points at the dynamic landmark of first encounter-constrained diagnostic-only liver contact, relative to its observation-only comparator. If gain occurs only after requiring a future reassessment opportunity, only among high-contact patients, or only under patient-time procedure merging, the disease-specific interpretation is falsified or inconclusive.

This is a prognostic/recorded-pathway hypothesis. A supportive result does not show recurrence, viable tumour, radiologic response, treatment need, benefit, or a causal effect of TACE.

## Estimands and information boundary

The primary estimand is incremental predictive information for the first recorded therapeutic TACE transition from day 45 in the full eligible risk set, with competing recorded states and local-observation loss explicit. Report discrete-time cause-specific hazards, cumulative incidence through days 30, 90 and 320 after day 45, calibration and alert curves. This estimates local recorded pathways, not treatment effects.

Two measurement estimands are required:

- Paired-observed estimand: performance among patients with the prespecified availability pattern.
- Full-index estimand: standardization to every eligible day-45 patient using models that retain availability/missingness indicators. Any observation/transport weight is fit on training data only, with overlap, positivity, truncation and effective-sample-size diagnostics. Weighting is predictive standardization, not causal adjustment.

The dynamic estimand starts at each patient’s first encounter-constrained diagnostic-only liver contact after day 45. At that contact, freeze all predictors strictly before the contact and estimate first subsequent therapeutic TACE over 30, 90 and 320 days, with alternate, mixed, non-liver and end-of-local-observation states competing. Report direct day-45 pathways and diagnostic-contact-mediated pathways separately. No post-landmark lab, examination, treatment episode or reassessment is a predictor.

Trajectory availability A=1 requires at least one parseable numeric AFP in days -30 to -1 and days 7–45, plus at least one of albumin, total bilirubin, the observed coagulation-ratio assay or platelets in each window. The exact assay vocabulary is frozen from the full labs.检验 values and archived in the vocabulary audit; plausible lexical hits are not silently pooled. For each assay retain the last numeric pre-index and first numeric early-post value, log1p AFP where valid, continuous change, direction, assay identity, timing and measurement count. Do not infer units, pool assays, construct ALBI/MELD, or apply unit-dependent thresholds. Partial patterns and missingness are retained.

Day-45 baseline variables may include age, sex, index year, department, pre-index procedure/diagnosis history, encounter intensity, assay availability, examination opportunities, diagnostic-only state, non-liver/unclassified counts, missingness and dated local-observation features. examinations.检查所见 and examinations.检查诊断 are excluded from primary inference because they are unvalidated narrative response labels. orders is an opportunity/capture sensitivity only, never treatment completion. No future outcome, future reassessment or future measurement enters the day-45 model.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

All alternatives use the same cohort, episode ontology, splits, horizons, estimands, standardization target, competing states and patient-clustered uncertainty.

- B0 transparent clinical-history baseline: regularized discrete-time cause-specific transition hazards from pre-index history, day-45 pathway states and capture/process variables, with no post-index numeric trajectory.
- Bobs observation/pathway comparator: B0 plus trajectory availability, measurement pattern, encounter/examination intensity and local-observation variables, but no AFP/hepatic numeric values. It tests whether the apparent signal is explained by being measured, contacted or retained in care.
- B2 trajectory model: Bobs plus the frozen AFP/hepatic values, changes and timing indicators. It is evaluated on both paired-observed and full-index standardization.
- M3 substantive learned/mechanistic alternative: a sparse low-rank irregular-time state-space model with separate latent AFP activity and hepatic-function states, assay-specific observation models, an encounter-constrained diagnostic-contact intensity and post-contact therapeutic hazards. Fit only with history available before each landmark. Evaluate on the identical locked transitions, horizons and patient bootstrap.

B0/Bobs/B2 are selected for auditability, transparent capture decomposition and direct falsification. M3 is scientifically useful because B2’s paired summaries can lose asynchronous sampling, discordant AFP versus hepatic-function movement, observation intensity and trajectory shape; M3 can expose whether those features explain incremental information. M3 is not granted merit for complexity. Defer a generic transformer because extra sequence capacity cannot resolve unvalidated procedure intent or future observation selection and is less interpretable for this uncertainty. Defer causal treatment-effect models, image/text response models, survival/death claims and external validation because eligibility, intent, adjudicated response, mortality/outside-care linkage, images and an independent cohort are unavailable. Revisit those branches only if those dependencies are supplied.

The actual scientific selection is therefore B0/Bobs/B2 plus M3 as a bounded alternative, not a claim that the learned model is clinically superior.

## Uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Use patient-clustered bootstrap intervals with the bootstrap unit fixed before seeing the test outcome. Report event counts, missing-time fractions, mixed/unclassified fractions, overlap, positivity, effective sample size, local-observation censoring, calibration and threshold uncertainty.

Supportive computation requires all of the following:

- On the locked full-index primary target, B2 versus Bobs reduces the alert fraction at 90% sensitivity by >=10 percentage points, with a bootstrap interval excluding zero and acceptable calibration/overlap.
- At the dynamic diagnostic-only landmark, trajectory gain versus its observation-only comparator is >=5 points with an interval excluding zero.
- Gain is not similar for the non-liver capture control or pre-index placebo, is not confined to high-contact/continued-observation strata, and remains directionally consistent under training-only observation weighting.
- The result is stable within the prespecified 14/30-day and encounter-only reassessment descriptions, but does not depend on conditioning the primary outcome on a future reassessment opportunity.
- Encounter-constrained versus patient-time 24-hour grouping changes the gain by no more than 5 points and does not reverse the pathway conclusion.
- If M3 is run, it converges and improves the same locked target by >=5 points or mean log score by >=0.02 with calibrated uncertainty; otherwise M3 is a scientific deferral, not a negative clinical result.

Adverse evidence includes Bobs explaining nearly all B2 gain; a gain confined to measured/high-contact or non-disappearing patients; similar gain for non-liver procedures or placebo; gain appearing only in the future-reassessment-conditioned outcome; reversal under encounter-constrained episodes; poor calibration or overlap; or M3 nonconvergence. These findings support a capture, coding or ascertainment explanation rather than disease-specific trajectory information.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few therapeutic or diagnostic transitions, sparse paired trajectories, excessive untimed procedures, unresolved composites, inadequate one-year support, temporal-split failure, positivity failure, bootstrap intervals spanning prespecified margins, or a dynamic landmark too sparse for stable estimates. Rules and thresholds cannot be relaxed after inspecting locked outcomes.

A supportive result establishes only reproducible local recorded-care stratification and motivates expert review. Clinical adjudication is still required for TACE indication, treatment intent, procedure bundles, radiologic response, eligibility and whether a recorded pathway reflects active tumour. A further prospective or independently linked study would be required for treatment benefit, survival, mortality, outside-care completeness, clinical utility, causal effects, appropriateness, recurrence, external validity or treatment recommendations. The verifier can check computation, uncertainty and whether interpretations follow outputs; it cannot establish these clinical claims.
