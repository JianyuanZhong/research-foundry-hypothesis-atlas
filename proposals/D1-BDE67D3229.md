FinishedRunning-only, amount-only, rate-only, on-time, ≤1-hour storage lag, and no-negative-lag sensitivities remain mandatory. Because public documentation suggests a positive `Paused` interval may contain recorded delivery before the pause, report it separately; actual receipt/nonreceipt still requires clinical adjudication, and this child does not silently relabel it.

Let `G12=t0+12h`. For accepted extubation task X and its deterministically selected same-stay ventilation interval V, set `C=min(X,V.endtime)`. A12 requires the first qualifying start strictly in `(t0,min(G12,C))`; B12 requires avoidance through that boundary.

At `t0`, clone every eligible subject once into A12 and once into B12 with base weight 1. Event precedence remains death; exact loop-C or unresolved V-X tie; positive-gap merged-ICU exit; strictly ordered loop versus C; then G12.

- A qualifying loop before C/G12 satisfies A and artificially censors B at that instant.
- At G12, avoidance satisfies B and artificially censors an unsatisfied, still-open A clone.
- If C occurs untreated first, both strategies remain compatible because their actions have not diverged; freeze treatment assignment for both. Accepted X can define the recorded airway attempt, while an unlinked verified V end makes the respiratory endpoint unknown.
- Death or positive-gap exit before divergence closes both clones under inherited endpoint states; it is not treated as B assignment. Death enters creatinine-or-death, RRT-or-death, airway/terminal, and mortality bounds as specified.
- Stop compatibility-weight updating at A satisfaction, B completion, shared closure, artificial censoring, or terminal event. Never use later `storetime`, status, end time, outcomes, or closure to construct a baseline weight.

These are dynamic strategies: A means initiate before the earlier of hour 12 and recorded airway closure if that opportunity remains open; B means avoid over the same available opportunity. Shared early paths are not effects of loop receipt.
