# Depth-conditioned hypotension DURATION and ICU AKI: is it an ischemic dose, or a vasoactive/fluid treatment-intensity proxy?

## 1. Unresolved question and why it matters
ICU teams titrate fluids and vasopressors to a mean-arterial-pressure (MAP) target. A time-weighted MAP<65
burden predicts creatinine-based KDIGO AKI (base-model AUC ~0.725), and graded MAP<65 duration tracks
downstream organ outcomes [K2]. The parent already asks whether cumulative time-below-65 (duration) adds
risk **conditional on the deepest MAP (depth)**, and controls BP-charting (n_map) and creatinine-draw (n_cr)
intensity. It leaves one consequential rival unresolved: **confounding by indication / treatment intensity.**

Longer and deeper hypotension mechanically draws **more norepinephrine and more fluid**, and vasopressor/
fluid exposure itself associates with AKI (shock severity, renal perfusion). So a "duration" coefficient may be
a **proxy for the vasoactive/fluid treatment dose**, not an independent ischemic dose. The decision-facing
distinction: if the duration effect survives treatment adjustment, "time below target" is a monitoring target
beyond a single worst MAP and beyond the drug titration itself; if it collapses, duration adds no information
beyond how much vasopressor the patient is already receiving. The permissive-hypotension trial question [K1]
(target MAP 60-65 to *reduce vasopressor exposure*, end-organ support as a secondary outcome) makes this
separation directly decision-relevant.

## 2. Evidence structure: leading claim, strongest rival, discriminating comparison
- **Leading claim (H1):** depth-conditioned cumulative MAP<65 duration carries *independent* information
  about creatinine-AKI risk (a genuine exposure/time-under-target effect).
- **Strongest rival (R4, new this episode):** the conditional duration effect is a **treatment-intensity
  proxy**; once concurrent vasoactive dose and fluid intake are modelled, the duration OR attenuates to the null.
  Treatment-exposure associations in ICU EHR are routinely confounded by indication and require explicit
  adjustment [K3].
- **Rivals carried from the parent:** R1 severity confounding (APACHE score adjustment + fixed effects),
  R2 measurement/charting-intensity artifact (n_map, n_cr, frac_below negative control), R3 outcome-timing
  leakage (hour-24 landmark; AKI window (24,168] h *after* the exposure window; hour-12 placebo landmark).
- **Discriminating comparison:** the *same* depth-conditioned duration estimand fitted (a) without and (b) with
  the treatment channel, plus an IPTW marginal-structural-model (MSM) alternative that handles time-varying
  treatment-confounder feedback the scalar baseline cannot (see §5).

## 3. Design (population, time, variables, outcome)
- **Population:** adult eICU 2.0 stays, age>=16 years (age field `'>89'` treated as >=90), ICU stay surviving to a
  **24 h landmark** (`unitdischargeoffset >= 1440`), with a baseline creatinine in (0,24] h and >=1 follow-up
  creatinine in (24,72] h. (Frozen from the parent.)
- **Exposure (0,24] h:** cumulative minutes (and depth, the minimum) with `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean` < 65 mmHg;
  `observationoffset` is in MINUTES. Depth-conditioned parametrization: duration with min-MAP as a covariate and
  a duration x min-MAP interaction.
- **NEW treatment channel (0,24] h), the addition:**
  - *Vasoactive intensity:* time-weighted **norepinephrine-equivalent (NEE) dose (mcg/min)** from
    `infusionDrug` (`drugname` like `Norepinephrine (ml/hr)` / `Norepinephrine (mcg/min)`; `drugrate`,
    `infusionoffset`) plus `medication` (`drugname`, `dosage`, `drugstartoffset`/`drugstopoffset`, `routeadmin`)
    for epinephrine/dopamine/phenylephrine/vasopressin; NEE conversion factors stated in code. NEE is a
    standard computable treatment-intensity construct [K2].
  - *Fluid intake:* cumulative `intakeOutput` intake volume in (0,24] h (`cellpath`/`celllabel`,
    `cellvaluenumeric`, `intakeoutputoffset`; `intaketotal` where present).
  - *Duration on vasopressor* and its timing relative to the exposure window.
- **Outcome:** creatinine-KDIGO AKI in (24,168] h: follow-up creatinine >= 1.5x baseline OR >= 0.3 mg/dL rise
  (`lab`: `labname`='creatinine', `labresult`, `labresultoffset`, MINUTES).
- **Severity / case-mix:** `apachePatientResult.apachescore`, age, gender, hospitalid/unittype fixed effects.

## 4. Estimand and falsification criteria (pre-specified)
Primary: OR for the **depth-conditioned duration** term on creatinine-AKI, and the **attenuation fraction**
`delta = 1 - OR_adjusted/OR_unadjusted` after adding the treatment channel.
- **Supportive (H1, independent effect):** conditional duration OR>1 with 95% CI excluding 1 **and**
  attenuation <25% after treatment adjustment **and** preserved under leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO) **and**
  hour-12 placebo-landmark null **and** the channel-disjoint sedative-duration negative control null.
- **Adverse (R4, treatment proxy):** duration OR CI includes 1 after treatment adjustment, **or**
  >=50% attenuation, **or** the IPTW-MSM point estimate collapses toward the null.
- **Inconclusive:** CI spans the pre-specified minimum detectable OR (MDE not exceeded), or the treatment
  channel is unrecorded for too many eligible stays, or LOHO/placebo disagree with the full-sample estimate.

## 5. Baseline vs substantive alternative (method exploration)
- **Baseline:** scalar logistic regression (the parent's), `AKI ~ duration + minMAP + duration:minMAP + severity + n_map + n_cr`,
  with hospital fixed effects. Simple, transparent, and the frozen comparator.
- **Alternative (mechanistic):** **IPTW marginal structural model** over a discretized 24 h treatment path
  (vasopressor on/off x dose strata + fluid volume), estimating the marginal effect of cumulative duration
  under time-varying confounding. Clinically, vasopressor dose is *itself* driven by prior MAP and prior dose,
  i.e. a time-varying confounder affected by prior exposure; the scalar baseline conditions on a downstream
  collider-like variable and can yield a biased coefficient. The MSM is the smallest model that could reveal
  what the baseline loses. Selection rationale: prefer the MSM only if its coefficients separate H1 from R4;
  otherwise report the baseline as primary.
- **Resource/time budget:** whole-cohort tabular fit, CPU, well within the solver envelope
  (solver_planning_limits: 16 CPU, 256 GB, 28800 s). vitalPeriodic.csv.gz (1.78 GB) MAP aggregation is the
  dominant cost; a streaming pass over selected columns is sufficient.

## 8. Honest limits and what stronger claims require
eICU-internal LOHO transport only (**no external validation**); invasive-vs-cuff MAP not recorded;
`labname` not harmonized; NEE conversion is approximate; treatment-channel charting may be incomplete at some
sites; observation not intervention => **association, not causation, no threshold claim**. A causal or
target-titration conclusion, or external generalization, would require adjudicated exposure data and an
external/held-out cohort - not this design.
