## Unresolved question, importance, and substantive advance

At ICU hour 48, among adults still recorded as invasively ventilated after recent vasopressor support, with at least +50 mL/kg unit-safe recorded balance, adequate recorded MAP, urine output and potassium, and no active RRT/ECMO or recent loop course, is starting or restarting a qualifying ICU-input-system furosemide/bumetanide record during the next 12 hours associated with at least a five-percentage-point increase in the lower partial-identification probability of being alive and directly observed outside a recorded MIMIC ICU at ICU day 7, without crossing the parent's recorded renal, RRT, death or terminal-disposition harm margins?

The decision is consequential because active fluid removal may facilitate ICU de-escalation but may also worsen perfusion or kidney injury; treatment selection can also mark patients already improving. Current evidence does not resolve this initiation-versus-avoidance decision in the parent’s phenotype. The inspected full XML of Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13476641`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`, frozen source `[source checksum]`) reports 26 randomized trials and 1,652 participants, mostly low/very-low-certainty and clinically heterogeneous evidence; it explicitly excluded diuretic-versus-no-diuretic/placebo/usual-care studies and could not consistently determine initiation timing. The inspected Bircher et al. 2026 full XML (PMCID `PMC13154636`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075`, source `[source checksum]`) states that optimal timing, volume and duration of fluid removal remain uncertain and practice relies heavily on clinical judgment. These sources support equipoise and importance, not benefit.

The parent is also vulnerable to secular confounding. MIMIC’s configured metadata states that dates are shifted independently by subject, preserving within-person intervals but invalidating cross-subject calendar alignment. The inspected MIMIC data descriptor (Johnson et al., 2023; PMCID `PMC9810617`, DOI `10.1038/s41597-022-01899-x`, frozen source `[source checksum]`) describes a dataset spanning changing clinical practice. The available `hosp/patients.anchor_year_group` is the coarse original-era variable. Loop initiation, ventilation management, ICU transfer thresholds and documentation can all change over eras, affecting both A12 and H120-RINO. A shifted year such as 2150 has no common real-world meaning across subjects.

The substantive advance is therefore not a new outcome or a more complex model. It makes the observed-era target population explicit, forces the valid real-era field into every compatibility model and balance audit, and adds prespecified era-standardized and early/late stability analyses. A pooled association that disappears or reverses after valid era control becomes inconclusive rather than “supportive.”

## Falsification and interpretation

After source/computation validity, branch in this order:

1. **Primary infeasible:** any parent source, population, arm, event, ESS, unknownness, model or bootstrap minimum fails. Report no effect direction.
2. **Temporal-invalid:** shifted cross-subject calendar fields are used as era, G is inferred, missing/unseen G is silently coerced, or G is omitted from a required compatibility model/bootstrap rebuild. No directional result is valid.
3. **Adverse-FWER:** a feasible valid analysis has at least one prespecified harm family Holm-rejected. This is a recorded safety concern, not causal toxicity.
4. **Era-inconclusive:** the era-standardized or early/late sensitivity is infeasible, G balance fails, or a material era sensitivity occurs. Report that secular confounding/heterogeneity prevents a stable pooled interpretation.
5. Preserve the parent’s exposure-semantics and endpoint/horizon inconclusive branches.
6. **Five-point margin falsified:** with no higher-priority branch, the one-sided 95% upper limit for era-adjusted `DeltaU_H120<=+0.05`. This rejects only the prespecified recorded association.
7. **Supportive recorded topology:** all parent and era gates pass, no adjusted harm or material sensitivity conflict exists, and the one-sided 95% lower limits for both pooled `DeltaL_H120` and era-standardized `DeltaL_H120^G` exceed +0.05.
8. **Statistically inconclusive:** valid feasible computation meets no directional branch.

Support prioritizes external replication or randomization; it does not recommend diuresis. An adverse result is a recorded concern; a falsified result is not equivalence or proof of no subgroup benefit. Era-inconclusive means the available single-center eras cannot support one stable pooled claim. Infeasible means this snapshot cannot execute the prespecified test.
