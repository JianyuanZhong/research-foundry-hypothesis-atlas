# Bundle-first, pattern-exact access frontier for documentary M2 triage

## Lead decision and substantive child

This child combines the clock- and bundle-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]` with the CT-only/MRI-only/dual-pattern transport parent `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the first-resection frame, 24-hour temporal lock, whole-frame outcome uncertainty in fitting and evaluation, same-fit S0 attribution, simultaneous clinical-margin inference, zero laboratory lineage, and bounded non-treatment claims.

It repairs six defects in the combination:

1. Report components are bundled before modality classification or text filtering. Blank examination IDs never become primary singleton semantic reports.
2. One accession with conflicting CT/MRI evidence is never duplicated into two independently visible bundles. It is adjudicated to one modality or kept as an ambiguous indivisible bundle with no primary body-derived features.
3. Net benefit always uses the whole target-frame denominator `N`. Pattern access uses conditional patient denominators `N_C,N_M,N_CM`; patients with no nonempty selected report contribute a fixed no-report term and are never dropped.
4. Empty selected report bodies are acquisition facts, not visibility opportunities. In dual acquisition, the nonempty-body pattern may therefore be C, M, CM, or empty.
5. The exact point lower frontier is coordinatewise monotone, but a raw bootstrap band need not be. A valid monotone closure is applied before defining the upward-closed support set.
6. Model and external-audit uncertainty receive prespecified 2.5% one-sided familywise error budgets. Bonferroni control is valid whether samples overlap or not.

## Unresolved question, importance, and hypotheses

M2 microvascular invasion is a postoperative pathological risk marker, while preoperative CT/MRI risk information may affect whether a patient is flagged for multidisciplinary review. Existing evidence supports neither this stored-report parser nor a treatment decision. The practical unresolved question is narrower: does routine report semantic content make clinically material documentary-M2 triage changes, and would that value survive the actual CT/MRI access mix rather than only a pooled “some report visible” assumption?

The strongest claim currently supported is that the frozen HCC files contain resection acquisition clocks, examination acquisition clocks, eventual examination text, and untimed pathology text. They do not contain report authored/final/release/view timestamps, report version history, raw images/slides, pathology specimen IDs or sampling adequacy, recurrence, survival, clinician action, or treatment benefit.

**Stored-content hypothesis P.** In the locked 2020–2021 whole first-resection test frame, with every selected nonempty eventual report bundle exposed, the one-sided simultaneous 95% lower confidence limits for both semantic attribution `D=S-S0` and complete strategy value `C=S-B` exceed net benefit 0.01 at threshold 0.30; both point lower bounds remain positive at 0.25, 0.275, 0.30, 0.325, and 0.35; and S exceeds flag-all and flag-none by 0.01 at 0.30.

**Pattern-access hypothesis T.** There is at least one prespecified vector `(h_C,h_M,h_CM)` of minimum patient access fractions for the CT-only, MRI-only, and dual-nonempty stored patterns for which the same simultaneous clinical-margin conditions hold under adversarial patient-level report visibility and unresolved documentary outcomes. For dual patients, “accessible” means at least one of CT or MRI; the lower frontier adversarially chooses CT-only, MRI-only, or both-visible content, so pooled access within CM cannot conceal which modality is exposed.

P and T are hypotheses, not present findings. T identifies a sufficient service-level region conditional on eventual stored pattern; it does not establish that any access vector occurred. A decision-time claim requires external, exact-version timing evidence.

The advance over a pooled frontier is falsifiable transport resolution. Two workflows can have the same overall patient-any coverage but different `(h_C,h_M,h_CM)`; one may lie in the support set while the other does not. The experiment can therefore reveal MRI dependence, CT dependence, dual-pattern fragility, robustness to access mix, or insufficient information.

## Population and temporal lock

The unit is one patient (`patient master index`) at the earliest eligible liver resection found across all available procedure years, before applying analysis-year restrictions.

- Freeze a clinically reviewed exact-`Surgery` dictionary for partial, segmental, hemihepatic, and liver-tumor resections. Exclude transplant, biopsy/puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and explicit repeat/recurrence operations. Require valid `Start time`; break exact-time ties by `Visit number` and normalized `Surgery`. Emit every raw value, frequency, inclusion/exclusion, invalid time, and tie before outcome access.
- Require age at least 18 from the same-encounter encounters row and same-encounter pathology text explicitly identifying HCC. This is retrospective pathology-confirmed resection, not proof of preoperative diagnostic certainty or curative intent.

## Bundle-first construction and report states

Deduplicate exact examination rows across all eight source columns. For nonblank `examination number`, a source bundle is all rows sharing `(patient master index,encounter number,examination number,start time)`. Eligibility and modality are decided from all component `examination` values plus `machine model` before any component is discarded. Retain all component rows of an eligible bundle and concatenate deterministically with field and component boundaries before feature extraction.

A frozen reviewed mapping assigns each bundle exactly one of CT, MRI, nonliver/ineligible, or X (mixed/ambiguous). A bundle matching both CT and MRI is adjudicated using accession and machine provenance; if one modality cannot be established, it remains one indivisible X bundle, is logged, and supplies no primary body-derived feature. It is never copied into both modalities. Blank-`examination number` rows are audited but cannot identify report-level grouping and supply no primary semantic state; a singleton-row sensitivity is labeled non-report-level and cannot support P, T, or deployment.

Among eligible nonblank bundles, select at most the latest CT and latest MRI per patient using acquisition `Start Time`, then nonblank `Examination Number`, then normalized bundle-label tuple and stable row hash as frozen tie-breakers. Selection occurs before checking text. Record selected/discarded bundles, ties, component counts, machine values, X bundles, and blank IDs.

For selected bundle j, `E_ij=1` only if normalized `Examination findings` or `Examination diagnosis` contains at least one non-whitespace character. No clinically meaningful token such as “none” is an empty marker. An `E=0` bundle retains acquisition-only facts but has no exposable body.

Let `J_i` contain selected CT/MRI bundles, including empty bodies; `q_i=sum_j E_ij`. The state space is
`Omega_i={a in {0,1}^{|J_i|}: a_j=0 if E_ij=0}`
in CT-then-MRI order. Exposing a bundle exposes all its component findings and diagnosis fields; masking it masks all size, count, thrombus, formatting, and semantic features together. Acquisition modality/time/count and development-frequency machine category remain available. No MAR, independence, or benefit-from-visibility assumption is made.

Define eventual nonempty stored pattern:

- `g=0`: q=0;
- `g=C`: nonempty selected CT only;
- `g=M`: nonempty selected MRI only;
- `g=CM`: both selected bodies nonempty.

Thus a patient with acquired CT and MRI but empty CT text and nonempty MRI text is in M, while both acquisition indicators remain in the model. `N=N_0+N_C+N_M+N_CM`.

## Documentary outcome and uncertainty in fit and evaluation

For index-resection pathology, `Y_i={1}` only for an explicit, position-anchored, clinically adjudicated M2 assignment; `Y_i={0}` for explicit M0/M1; and `Y_i={0,1}` for missing grade, binary positive MVI, conflict, uncertain specimen linkage, or documented inadequate/unknown sampling. `V=1` denotes a trustworthy singleton assignment; `V=0` is retained as an interval in every denominator.

Only an immediate selected value after a frozen MVI-grade anchor may assign grade; definitions, histories, other-specimen sections, unanchored M2, and conflicts remain uncertain. Two independent parsers must agree. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors and scores review every parsed M2, every conflict, every test V=0, and at least 150 M0 plus 150 M1 records stratified by year/template; a third resolves disagreements. Require class PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95, kappa at least 0.90, and parser disagreement at most 5%.

Every development/tuning fit uses patient-wise worst-case label and report-state logistic loss:
`sum_i max_{y in Y_i,r in Omega_i} [log(1+exp(z_theta(x_i(r))))-y z_theta(x_i(r))] + elastic-net penalty`.
The preprocessing, tuning, nonnegative-slope robust recalibration, and optimizer are rebuilt inside each resample. Evaluation separately enumerates `Y_i` and `Omega_i`; no complete-case outcome is primary.

Raw slides, specimen IDs, block counts, vessel measurements, and sampling protocols are unavailable. The endpoint is documentary M2, not latent biological M2.

## Models, same-fit attribution, and clinical estimands

Fit interpretable robust elastic-net logistic pipelines:

- B: age, sex, acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category, and—only for exposed bundles—frozen maximum lesion diameter, lesion-count category, and explicit macrovascular thrombus, with unknown indicators.
- S: exactly B plus present/absent/unknown capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity.
- S0: never refit. Use S's fitted coefficients, intercept, preprocessing, selected penalty, and recalibration, replacing only the semantic block with its frozen state-zero vector. Assert bitwise-equal `score_S=score_S0` whenever no semantic report is exposed.
- W and A=W+the identical semantic block are secondary provenance/workflow diagnostics. Unrestricted text is a ceiling diagnostic only.

B is independently optimized. Therefore `D=S-S0` attributes action changes to runtime semantic content within the same fitted S, while `C=S-B` asks whether the complete strategy beats an optimized structured baseline.

At threshold p, `w=p/(1-p)`, `I_Mi(r,p)=1[score_Mi(r)>=p]`, and for contrast `j=(M1,M0)`:
`d_i^j(y,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)] [I_M1i(r,p)-I_M0i(r,p)]`.
Primary contrasts are D and C; `F_all=(S,flag-all)` and `F_none=(S,flag-none)`. All divide by whole-frame N.

## Exact pattern frontier

Retain the parent's pooled patient-any frontier as a benchmark only. It cannot identify the access vector.

For `g in {C,M,CM}`, let `h_g in {0,.05,...,1}`, `k_g=ceil(N_g h_g)`, and `a_i(r)=1` if at least one nonempty selected bundle is exposed. In CM, visible states are CT-only, MRI-only, and both. For each contrast and p define
`ell_i(r)=min_{y in Y_i} d_i(y,r,p)` and `u_i(r)=max_{y in Y_i} d_i(y,r,p)`.
Set
`m_i0=ell_i(all-masked)`, `m_i1=min_{r:a_i(r)=1} ell_i(r)`,
and analogously `M_i0=u_i(all-masked)`, `M_i1=max_{r:a_i(r)=1}u_i(r)`.

The exact stratum minimum under `sum a_i>=k_g` starts with the cheaper of `m_i0,m_i1` for each patient, choosing exposed on ties. If fewer than `k_g` are exposed, switch the required number of remaining patients using the smallest positive `m_i1-m_i0`. The exact maximum starts with the larger of `M_i0,M_i1`, choosing exposed on ties; if needed, switch using negative increments closest to zero. Brute-force equality is required for every fixture through `N_g<=12`, including CM patients whose worst visible state differs by contrast.

Crucially, the whole-frame bounds are
`L_j(h,p)=N^-1 [sum_{i:g_i=0} ell_i(all-masked)+L_jC(k_C,p)+L_jM(k_M,p)+L_jCM(k_CM,p)]`
and similarly for U with the fixed `g=0` upper costs. The zero-pattern term may not be omitted. Each bootstrap uses its resampled `N,N_g` and `ceil(N_g h_g)`.

The nested “at least” feasible sets make the point lower frontier nondecreasing coordinatewise. Let `b_raw(h)` be a simultaneously valid raw lower band. Define the valid monotone closure
`b_up(h)=max_{u<=h} b_raw(u)`.
Because `L(h)>=L(u)` for every dominated u, this retains simultaneous coverage and produces a coordinatewise nondecreasing lower band. Apply it separately to each endpoint/threshold. Numerical nonmonotonicity in point L is an implementation failure. Report the Pareto-minimal antichain of the support set; never select an isolated favorable cell.

A grid cell supports T only when at p=.30 the monotone simultaneous lower bands for D, C, F_all, and F_none exceed .01 and the D/C point lower bounds are positive at all five thresholds. An absent pattern (`N_g=0`) makes a three-pattern transport claim unidentifiable, not “100% covered.” Duplicate integer k values are collapsed transparently.

The all-stored state is feasible under every at-least constraint, so its contrast is an upper benchmark for the worst-case lower frontier; this is not an assumption that visibility helps each patient. P failure on a required contrast precludes robust T support, but partial visibility may still change descriptive point performance.

## Result meanings and falsification

- **Supportive P:** all global gates pass and every stored-content margin/plateau/default/null condition passes. Conclude only that eventual stored CT/MRI report semantics made materially favorable actions within fixed S and that complete S exceeded B for historical documentary M2 under maximal stored exposure.
- **Supportive T, timing absent:** P passes, every pattern is adequately populated, and the support set is nonempty. Conclude only that the reported Pareto-minimal access vectors would be sufficient under adversarial outcome and visibility assignment. Deployment remains inconclusive.
- **Adverse:** with gates passed, a required all-stored upper confidence limit is below .01, D is nonpositive, defaults or null control fail, or the exact pattern support set is empty. AUC cannot override adverse net benefit.
- **Mixed:** D supportive/C adverse means semantics alter S actions but S does not beat optimized B; C supportive/D adverse does not demonstrate runtime semantic value. Do not recommend S.
- **Inconclusive:** any adjudication, sample/event, sparse-pattern, optimization, resampling, extraction, or lineage gate fails, or intervals cross a boundary. Missing report-version clocks always make P_dec inconclusive, even when P and T support.
- **Decision-time conditional:** only the jointly controlled exact-version audit vector lies in the support set. This supports conditional historical availability in the audited workflow, not clinician use, biological M2, treatment choice, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, or patient benefit.
