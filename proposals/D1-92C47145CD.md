# Hypotension Duration × Inflammatory Burden × Vasopressor Resistance Interaction

## Hypothesis Statement

In patients with septic shock requiring vasopressor therapy in the eICU, the **interaction between hypotension duration and systemic inflammatory burden** is independently associated with **vasopressor resistance** and subsequent in-hospital mortality, beyond the effects of baseline severity and inflammatory markers alone.

**Formal Statement:** After adjusting for age, APACHE II score, comorbidities, hospital characteristics, and baseline inflammatory markers (CRP/hs-CRP), the multiplicative interaction term between:
- Hypotension duration (time-weighted burden of systolic BP < 70 mmHg)
- Inflammatory burden (time-weighted CRP/hs-CRP levels)

...will be significantly positive (β_interaction > 0, p < 0.05), indicating that patients experiencing BOTH prolonged hypotension AND high inflammation have synergistically increased risk of vasopressor resistance.

## Clinical Importance

1. **Personalized Vasopressor Management**: Identify patients at high risk of vasopressor resistance who may benefit from earlier escalation or alternative strategies (e.g., corticosteroids, terlipressin).

2. **Improved Risk Stratification**: Combined hypotension-inflammation metric improves risk prediction beyond minimum BP targets alone, addressing a critical gap in current sepsis guidelines.

3. **Understanding Mechanisms**: The interaction suggests that inflammatory burden modulates vascular responsiveness to vasopressors, potentially through cortisol resistance or endothelial dysfunction.

## Scientific Advance Over Existing Knowledge

Current research focuses on:
- Minimum blood pressure targets (Sepsis-3 guidelines)
- Binary hypotension outcomes (yes/no)
- Single inflammatory markers (CRP, PCT)

This study advances by:
1. **Temporal Dynamics**: Using duration and time-weighted burden rather than occurrence
2. **Interaction Effects**: Testing multiplicative (not additive) interaction between hypotension and inflammation
3. **Actionable Outcome**: Vasopressor resistance as a more directly actionable phenotype than AKI or mortality
4. **Novel Combined Risk Metric**: Hypotension Duration × Inflammatory Burden product

## Data Bindings

### Primary Tables
- **vitalPeriodic**: Blood pressure measurements (systemicsystolic, systemicdiastolic, systemicmean)
- **infusionDrug**: Vasopressor medications (drugname, infusionrate, drugamount)
- **lab**: Inflammatory markers (CRP, hs-CRP)
- **apachePatientResult**: Baseline severity (apachescore, acutephysiologyscore)
- **patient**: Demographics and comorbidities
- **hospital**: Hospital characteristics

### Key Variables
- **Hypotension Duration**: Time (hours) spent with systolic BP < 70 mmHg
- **Inflammatory Burden**: Time-weighted average of CRP/hs-CRP
- **Vasopressor Resistance**: Defined as requiring vasopressor dose escalation or inability to maintain MAP ≥ 65 mmHg despite adequate fluids

### Population
- Patients with septic shock requiring vasopressors in eICU
- Minimum 24 hours of continuous BP monitoring
- Complete lab measurements for CRP/hs-CRP
- Follow-up for vasopressor resistance outcome (within 48-72 hours)

## Statistical Analysis Plan

### Baseline Model
```
logit(P(vasopressor_resistance)) = β0 + β1*age + β2*APACHE_II + β3*comorbidities + β4*hospital + β5*hypotension_duration + β6*inflammatory_burden
```

### Substantive Alternative (Interaction Model)
```
logit(P(vasopressor_resistance)) = β0 + β1*age + β2*APACHE_II + β3*comorbidities + β4*hospital +
                                   β5*hypotension_duration + β6*inflammatory_burden +
                                   β7*(hypotension_duration × inflammatory_burden)
```

### Falsification Criteria
- **Supportive**: β_interaction > 0, p < 0.05, with β5 and β6 individually significant
- **Adverse**: β_interaction < 0 or p ≥ 0.05, indicating no interaction effect
- **Inconclusive**: β_interaction significant but p ≥ 0.10, or β5 and β6 not significant
