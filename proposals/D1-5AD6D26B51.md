# Discordant renal recovery after creatinine-based AKI: a multi-hospital eICU-CRD 2.0 cohort study

**Candidate type:** experiment specification (adaptation of expert seed `[starting question]` to eICU-CRD 2.0)
**Scientific parent:** [prior hypothesis] (seed import). Context candidates (transition provenance only): [prior hypothesis] (MIMIC-IV development of the same seed, board leader), [prior hypothesis].

## 1. Scientific question, why it matters, and rival explanations

### Question
In adult ICU patients with creatinine-based KDIGO AKI (stage 1-3), does **discordant recovery** — a fall in serum creatinine without a matching recovery of urine output — identify higher risk of (a) in-hospital death and (b) persistent AKI at ICU discharge, compared with **concordant recovery** (creatinine fall AND urine-output recovery) and **non-recovery**, after adjustment for illness severity (APACHE IV predicted ICU mortality) and hospital?

### Why it matters
KDIGO recovery is operationally defined by creatinine and/or urine output, but in practice "recovery" is usually read from the creatinine trajectory alone. If a creatinine fall can occur while the kidney is still oliguric — either because creatinine generation has fallen (muscle wasting, catabolism [K1]) or because true GFR has not recovered — then creatinine-only recovery is an overestimate of renal recovery and such patients may be discharged or de-escalated inappropriately. A multi-hospital test matters because the single-center MIMIC-IV development of this seed (board leader candidate-51d1786c) cannot distinguish a transportable within-hospital signal from a single-site practice artifact; eICU's 208 hospitals and per-stay APACHE IV scores make that distinction testable.

### What this proposal adds over the closest inspected work
- [K1] Haines 2023 (full text inspected) established that creatinine-based estimates systematically overestimate GFR after prolonged critical illness because muscle wasting lowers creatinine generation (posterior mean eGFRcreat overestimation 59 mL/min/1.73m² vs iohexol). It does not test whether the *pattern* of creatinine-only recovery (vs creatinine+UO recovery) predicts outcomes. This proposal tests exactly that pattern against hard outcomes.
- [K2] Abdel-Nabey 2021 (abstract only) defined renal recovery as normalization of *both* creatinine and UO criteria and found recovery trajectories associated with outcomes in a monocenter cohort (n=350, 166 AKI). It does not test the *discordant* (Cr-only) subgroup, does not use multi-hospital data, and did not have a validated per-stay severity covariate. This proposal isolates the discordant group and adds severity + hospital adjustment.
- [K3] eICU-CRD 2.0 documentation bounds the data: 200,000+ stays, 208 hospitals, APACHE severity measures, I&O flowsheet data; it also warns that interface availability differs by unit (missing data is structural, not random).

### Rival explanations and the design feature that separates each
- **R1 — Severity confounding.** Discordant-recovery patients are simply sicker (higher APACHE, more organ failure); the death signal is severity, not discordance. *Separation:* APACHE IV `predictedicumortality` (validated per-stay covariate, unavailable in the MIMIC branch) plus the prespecified unadjusted-vs-adjusted gap readout (F2). If the discordant-vs-concordant odds ratio collapses after adjustment, R1 is supported.
- **R2 — Urine-output documentation/measurement artifact (incl. catheter practice).** "No UO recovery" may reflect under-documentation of UO (no catheter, no charting) rather than true oliguria, concentrated in sicker or differently managed patients. *Separation:* (i) a hard documentation guard (>=12 h of UO documented in the window) so only well-documented stays are classifiable; (ii) hospital random effects absorb hospital-level charting culture (F2 + R4); (iii) sensitivity S3 (include unclassifiable-UO stays, Cr-only phenotyping) shows how much the signal depends on the UO documentation gate; (iv) sensitivity S4 (exclude dialysis/RRT stays in window) removes anuric-on-RRT patients whose UO is mechanically zero.
- **R3 — Creatinine-generation/muscle-wasting direction (per [K1]).** The creatinine fall in discordant patients may be a *false* recovery: falling generation rather than improving GFR. This is the mechanistic reading that makes discordance genuinely informative (a marker of catabolism + persistent dysfunction). *Separation:* the secondary outcome (persistent AKI at ICU discharge) is the discriminator — if discordant patients have higher persistent-AKI rates than concordant patients, the creatinine fall was not a true GFR recovery, supporting R3; if persistent AKI is equal, the discordance is more likely an artifact (R2) or severity (R1). This is a descriptive/mechanistic boundary test, not a proof of mechanism: eICU has no measured GFR or cystatin C, so R3 can be supported but not established (see §9).
- **R4 — Between-hospital practice differences.** The discordant group may be an artifact of a few hospitals with aggressive diuresis, different catheter policy, or different lab frequency. *Separation:* hospital random effects (primary) with fixed-effects fallback; report between-hospital variance and the distribution of discordant stays across hospitals (how many hospitals contribute >=10 discordant stays). If the signal is carried by 1-2 hospitals, R4 is supported.

## 2. Population definition (exact table/column bindings)

All tables join on `patientunitstayid`. Offsets are in **minutes relative to ICU admission** (per `datasets/eicu/metadata.json` conventions). Source base dir: `[internal dataset path]`.

**Inclusion (measured anchors in parentheses):**
1. `patient.csv.gz`: `unitvisitnumber = 1` (first ICU stay of the unit admission).
2. `patient.csv.gz`: `age >= 18` (adults; `age` is an integer year column).
3. `patient.csv.gz`: `unitdischargeoffset >= 720` (>=12 h ICU stay; measured 95,973 of 200,859 stays are >=40 h, so the >=12 h gate retains a large cohort).
4. `lab.csv.gz`: at least one row with `labname = 'creatinine'` (measured 1,277,760 creatinine rows in 190,464 stays).
5. **Baseline creatinine** = first creatinine row in the stay by `labresultoffset` (earliest; ties broken by `labid` ascending). Parse `labresult` (numeric string) to float; exclude rows that do not parse or are <= 0.
6. **t0 (creatinine peak)** = the creatinine row with maximum parsed `labresult`; ties -> earliest `labresultoffset`. Require peak > baseline (otherwise no AKI).
7. **KDIGO creatinine AKI stage 1-3 vs baseline** (creatinine criteria only; UO criteria not used for AKI definition, to keep the phenotype on a single modality):
   - Stage 1: peak >= 1.5 x baseline AND peak - baseline < 0.3 mg/dL; or peak - baseline >= 0.3 mg/dL (with ratio < 1.5).
   - Stage 2: peak >= 2.0 x baseline AND < 3.0 x baseline.
   - Stage 3: peak >= 3.0 x baseline (or peak >= 4.0 mg/dL).
   - Stage = max satisfied. Require stage >= 1.
8. **Assessment window** = [t0 + 2880 min, t0 + 4320 min] (48-72 h after peak). Require the window to end before ICU discharge: `t0 + 4320 <= unitdischargeoffset`. Stays discharging before window end are excluded from the primary analysis (prespecified; reported as a flow count).
9. **In-ICU death before window end**: if `unitdischargestatus = 'Expired'` and the stay ends before the window end, the stay is excluded from the primary (no complete follow-up); reported in the flow diagram. (Measured: 10,907 unit Expired, 18,004 hospital Expired overall.)

**Excluded / sensitivity-only:**
- Stays without classifiable UO (see §3) are excluded from the **primary** analysis and retained for sensitivity S3 (prespecified).

## 3. Phenotype formulas (exact, with edge cases)

**Creatinine recovery (CrRec):** within the assessment window, minimum parsed creatinine `cr_min` over rows with `labresultoffset` in [t0+2880, t0+4320].
- CrRec = (peak - cr_min) / peak >= 0.25.
- Edge: no creatinine row in window -> CrRec = 0 (no documented fall; stay remains classifiable for UO but is Cr-non-recovering).
- Edge: creatinine rows with `labresultrevisedoffset` — use the original `labresult`/`labresultoffset` (revisions are not modeled; documented limitation).

**Urine output (UO) recovery (UORec):**
- **UO source rows:** `intakeOutput.csv.gz` rows where `cellpath` starts with `flowsheet|Flowsheet Cell Labels|I&O|Output (ml)|` AND `lower(celllabel)` contains `'urin'`. This path guard + substring rule includes the measured top labels ('Urine' 3,300,489 rows; 'URINE CATHETER' 406,364; 'Urinary Catheter Output: Indwelling/Continuous Ure...' 261,045) **and** hospital-specific variants, without an exhaustive allowlist. Documented caveat: the substring rule could in principle capture a non-urine label containing 'urin' under the Output (ml) path (e.g., a 'urinary bag' flush entry); the solver must report the distinct matched labels and their row counts as a feasibility output, and any label that is clearly not urine output (judged from the label text) is removed in a documented post-hoc exclusion (sensitivity, not primary).
- **Hourly UO:** for each hour h (integer minutes) in the window, UO_ml(h) = sum of `cellvaluenumeric` over matched rows with `intakeoutputoffset` in [h, h+60). Rows with non-numeric or missing `cellvaluenumeric` are dropped (counted).
- **Weight:** `dischargeweight` (kg) if present and 30 <= w <= 250; else `admissionweight` under the same range; else the stay is **unclassifiable for UO** (excluded from primary; sensitivity S3). Rationale: dischargeweight is the weight at the time the UO window is assessed; the 30-250 kg range guards against missing/placeholder values.
- **UO rate:** rate(h) = UO_ml(h) / (weight_kg) [mL/kg/h].
- **UORec** = (median over hours with documented UO of rate(h)) >= 0.5 mL/kg/h, **and** >= 12 distinct hours with documented UO (UO_ml(h) > 0 or a matched row exists) in the window.
- Edge: hours with matched rows but `cellvaluenumeric` missing/zero are treated as 0 mL (documented oliguria/zero output), not as missing.
- Edge: window shorter than 12 h of documentation -> unclassifiable (primary exclusion, S3 retention).

**Groups (primary, mutually exclusive):**
- **Concordant:** CrRec AND UORec.
- **Discordant:** CrRec AND NOT UORec.
- **Non-recovery:** NOT CrRec AND NOT UORec (UO classifiable).
- **Cr-only-recovery-without-UO-data** (CrRec, unclassifiable UO): excluded from primary; S3.
- **Non-recovery-without-UO-data** (NOT CrRec, unclassifiable UO): excluded from primary; S3.
- UORec AND NOT CrRec (UO recovered but creatinine did not fall >=25%): this is not "discordant recovery" as defined; it is assigned to **non-recovery** for the primary 3-group comparison (the hypothesis concerns creatinine-only recovery, and a creatinine non-fall means the creatinine criterion for recovery is not met). Reported as a 4th descriptive category in the flow table.

## 4. Outcomes

- **Primary: in-hospital death** = `patient.hospitaldischargestatus = 'Expired'` (measured 18,004/200,859 overall). Censoring: none (status is terminal per stay).
- **Secondary: persistent AKI at ICU discharge.** Discharge creatinine = last parsed creatinine with `labresultoffset` in [unitdischargeoffset - 240, unitdischargeoffset]. Persistent AKI = discharge creatinine still meeting KDIGO creatinine criteria vs baseline (ratio >= 1.5 or >= 2.0/3.0 thresholds, or absolute >= 0.3 mg/dL above baseline). Stays without a creatinine in the 4-h pre-discharge window: reported as a **fill-rate feasibility output** (UNVERIFIED), excluded from the secondary analysis (not imputed).
- **Descriptive: ICU length of stay** = `unitdischargeoffset` (minutes), reported per group (median, IQR).

## 5. Analysis

**(a) Unadjusted:** per-group rates of in-hospital death and persistent AKI with 95% CIs (Wilson), plus group sizes, baseline characteristics (age, gender, `unitadmitsource`, baseline creatinine, peak creatinine, AKI stage, APACHE IV predicted ICU mortality where available).

**(b) Adjusted logistic regression for in-hospital death:**
- Model: `logit(death) = group (3-level, ref = concordant) + predictedicumortality + age + gender + unitadmitsource (categorical) + baseline creatinine + (1 | hospitalid)`.
- `predictedicumortality` from `apachePatientResult.csv.gz` (join on `patientunitstayid`; one row per stay expected). Missing APACHE: indicator + mean imputation (fill rate reported; UNVERIFIED).
- Hospital random intercept (`hospitalid`, 208 levels). **Prespecified fallback:** if the random-effects model fails to converge (boundary variance, singular fit), refit with hospital fixed effects (or, if >200 parameters destabilize, drop fixed effects and report the unadjusted + covariate-adjusted model, labeled as such).
- Report: adjusted ORs (95% CI) for discordant vs concordant and non-recovery vs concordant; between-hospital variance estimate.

**(c) Same model for persistent AKI** (secondary), restricted to stays with a discharge creatinine.

**(d) Severity-confounding readout:** the gap between the unadjusted (a) and adjusted (b) discordant-vs-concordant OR is the prespecified R1 readout (F2).

**Prespecified sensitivities:**
- **S1:** assessment window 24-72 h after peak (instead of 48-72 h).
- **S2:** UO recovery threshold 0.4 and 0.6 mL/kg/h (instead of 0.5).
- **S3:** include unclassifiable-UO stays with Cr-only phenotyping (CrRec vs NOT CrRec, 2-group), to test dependence on the UO documentation gate (R2).
- **S4:** exclude stays with dialysis/RRT in the window (RRT identified from `treatment.csv.gz` `treatmentstring` containing dialysis/RRT terms — string set UNVERIFIED, solver must enumerate and report; plus `intakeOutput.dialysistotal > 0` in window as a secondary RRT flag).
- **S5:** exclude stays mechanically ventilated at t0 (ventilation status from `respiratoryCharting`/`respiratoryCare` or `apachePatientResult.actualventdays` proxy — solver to document the chosen source; flagged as a feasibility item).
- **S6:** restrict to stays with `unitdischargeoffset >= 4320` (>=72 h ICU stay).

**Falsification criteria (prespecified readings):**
- **F1 (adverse):** adjusted OR for discordant vs concordant in-hospital death < 1.0 (95% CI excluding 1.0) -> the discordant group does *not* have higher mortality; the hypothesis is adverse.
- **F2 (severity artifact):** unadjusted OR > 1.3 but adjusted OR crosses 1.0 (CI includes 1.0) -> the signal is explained by severity (R1 supported).
- **F3 (underpowered/inconclusive):** discordant group < 200 stays OR < 20 in-hospital deaths -> primary result inconclusive; report only.
- **F4 (measurement artifact):** the direction of the discordant-vs-concordant association is inconsistent between the primary (death) and secondary (persistent AKI) outcomes (e.g., discordant worse for death but not for persistent AKI, or vice versa) -> suggests the UO phenotype is a documentation artifact rather than a true renal signal (R2 supported); R3 not supported.
- **Supportive pattern:** adjusted discordant-vs-concordant death OR > 1.0 (CI excluding 1.0) AND discordant-vs-concordant persistent-AKI OR > 1.0, with the unadjusted->adjusted gap modest (F2 not met) and the signal spread across many hospitals (R4 not supported). This supports the interpretation that creatinine-only recovery identifies patients with persistent dysfunction and higher mortality, consistent with R3, **without establishing the muscle-wasting mechanism** (which would require measured GFR/cystatin C, unavailable here).

## 6. Method-alternatives record

- **Baseline (retained):** unadjusted per-group comparison (a). This is the MIMIC-branch approach; it is the reference for the severity-confounding gap.
- **Chosen learned/mechanistic alternative:** hospital random-effects logistic regression with APACHE IV predicted ICU mortality (b/c). **What it reveals that the baseline loses:** (i) whether the discordant-recovery signal survives a *validated* severity covariate (the MIMIC single-center design had to use a computed severity proxy because charted APACHE was unusable; eICU has `predictedicumortality` per stay); (ii) the within-hospital vs between-hospital decomposition (random-effect variance + hospital spread) — a transportability check the single-center MIMIC design structurally cannot provide.
- **Deferred: machine-learning / Fine-Gray competing-risk models.** Justification: (i) the primary outcome is binary in-hospital death, not a time-to-event with a competing risk that would demand Fine-Gray; (ii) expected event counts per group (especially discordant deaths) are modest relative to a flexible model's parameter budget, so ML would overfit and add no interpretability; (iii) the scientific question is about a *pre-specified 3-group comparison*, which logistic regression answers directly and transparently. Revisit condition: if the discordant group has >500 deaths and the adjusted logistic model shows strong non-proportionality or effect modification by hospital, a hospital-stratified or ML model becomes justifiable.

## 7. Solver envelope and exact steps

**Resources:** CPU-only (solver planning limits: 16 CPUs / 256 GB / 28,800 s; gpus=0). Expected: 4-8 CPUs / 16-32 GB / 2-6 h. Base image `ehr-campaign-cpu:[internal campaign]` (or python:3.11-slim + pip install pandas, statsmodels, scipy).

**Steps (in order):**
1. **Cohort build:** load `patient.csv.gz` (200,859 rows, small); apply §2 filters; join `lab.csv.gz` filtered to `labname='creatinine'` (1.28M rows — stream, groupby `patientunitstayid`); compute baseline, peak/t0, KDIGO stage, window eligibility. Output: cohort table + flow counts.
2. **UO phenotype:** stream `intakeOutput.csv.gz` (12M rows) filtered to the path/label rule; aggregate hourly UO per stay within each stay's window; compute UORec with weight + 12-h guard. Output: phenotype table + matched-label census (distinct labels, row counts).
3. **Outcomes:** in-hospital death from `patient.hospitaldischargestatus`; persistent AKI from the 4-h pre-discharge creatinine (fill rate reported); ICU LOS.
4. **Covariates:** join `apachePatientResult.csv.gz` (fill rate for `predictedicumortality` reported); `unitadmitsource`, age, gender, baseline creatinine from `patient`.
5. **Models:** unadjusted rates + CIs; adjusted logistic (random effects via statsmodels GLM with hospital dummies as fallback; or `statsmodels` mixed model if available, else fixed effects); secondary model; sensitivities S1-S6.
6. **Feasibility outputs to report (UNVERIFIED items):** classifiable N per group; baseline-creatinine fill; weight fill (discharge/admission); APACHE IV fill; RRT `treatmentstring` enumeration (distinct strings containing dialysis/rrt/hemodialysis keywords); discharge-creatinine fill; UO matched-label census; number of hospitals contributing >=10 discordant stays.

## 8. Key references (three inspected works)

- **[K1] Haines et al. 2023** — "Comparison of Cystatin C and Creatinine in the Assessment of Measured Kidney Function during Critical Illness." *Clin J Am Soc Nephrol* (PMC10564373). **Full text inspected** (frozen: `work/support-eicu/ref-haines-2023.readable.txt`, [source checksum]). Supports R3: eGFRcreat systematically overestimates measured GFR after prolonged critical illness (posterior mean +59 mL/min/1.73m² vs iohexol; 95% CrI 49-69) because muscle wasting (2%/day decrease in rectus femoris area) lowers creatinine generation; cystatin C is less affected. **Bounds:** it is a single-center study without outcome analysis of recovery *patterns*; it does not test whether creatinine-only recovery predicts death or persistent AKI. **This proposal adds:** a multi-hospital test of the discordant (Cr-only) recovery pattern against hard outcomes.
- **[K2] Abdel-Nabey et al. 2021** — "Acute Kidney Injury Recovery Patterns in Critically Ill Patients: Results of a Retrospective Cohort Study." *Crit Care Med* 49(7):e683-e692 (PMID 33826581). **Abstract only inspected** (frozen: `work/support-eicu/ref-abdel-nabey-2021.epmc.json`, [source checksum]; full text subscription-required, no PMC). Supports the framing: renal recovery defined as normalization of *both* creatinine and UO criteria; recovery trajectories (group-based modeling) associated with outcomes; AKI stage and ventilation associated with lack of recovery. **Bounds:** monocenter (n=350, 166 AKI), 5-month window, no per-stay validated severity covariate, does not isolate the discordant (Cr-only) subgroup. **This proposal adds:** the discordant subgroup, multi-hospital (208) design, and APACHE IV severity adjustment.
- **[K3] eICU-CRD 2.0 documentation** — Pollard, Johnson, Raffa, Celi, Badawi, Mark. "eICU Collaborative Research Database (version 2.0)." PhysioNet, 2019 (https://physionet.org/content/eicu-crd/2.0/). **Documentation page inspected** (frozen: `work/support-eicu/ref-eicu-crd-2.0.readable.txt`, [source checksum]). Bounds the data: 200,000+ stays, 139,000+ unique patients, 335 units at 208 US hospitals, 2014-2015; includes APACHE severity measures, labs, medications, I&O; **warns that interface availability differs by unit, so missing data is structural** (relevant to the UO documentation gate and APACHE fill). **This proposal adds:** the specific AKI-recovery phenotype and outcome analysis on this platform.
