# Identity-robust patient-coverage frontier for monotone-onset HCC documentary-M2 review

## Question, hypothesis, and advance

The consequential decision is whether a scarce preoperative multidisciplinary review queue could prioritize patients whose eventual first liver-resection encounter contains documentary evidence classified as high-grade microvascular invasion (M2), using routine CT/MRI report information. The tested action is allocation of fixed historical review positions. It is not a recommendation about resection extent, transplant, locoregional or systemic treatment, surveillance, or any other therapy.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is narrower: the HCC snapshot contains patient/encounter-linked procedures, examination rows with acquisition-like times and report narratives, and same-encounter pathology narratives. It also contains dated medication/order records and encounter demographics. Full source inspection demonstrates examination multiplicity: 419,996 examination rows, 419,924 nonblank accession values, 392,854 unique nonblank whole-accession keys, and 16,527 multirow accession keys with nonidentical component payloads. This supports a historical information-ranking experiment, not a clinical deployment claim.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> In separate held-out 2020 and 2021 cohorts, at the 24-hour preoperative cutoff and fixed 10% review capacity, there is an attainable patient-level report-coverage contract under which a locked semantic ranking G has strictly greater than 5 documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered positions than each of an acquisition-only model B, a report-surface model R, a same-fit semantic mask G0, and capacity-matched random review, uniformly over every compatible operation/source/onset/identity/reader/pathology world and whole-patient deletion.

The primary experiment does not select one visibility percentage in advance. It computes the complete finite nondominated frontier of attainable patient-coverage curves, worst-case documentary-M2 yield, false-positive burden, and the minimum outcome-blind exchange rate at which review would be preferred. A cell is clinically action-compatible only after an external report-version/readability audit and outcome-blind stakeholder preference elicitation identify the cell’s real coverage and acceptable exchange rate.

This advances ordinary retrospective MVI prediction by testing whether report semantics remain useful for a fixed-capacity review decision when report identity and time of availability are uncertain, and whether apparent aggregate coverage can be trusted when it may be concentrated in repeat studies from only some patients. It distinguishes stored-content potential from actual preoperative access. It does not claim biological MVI prediction, clinician viewing, workflow benefit, treatment benefit, safety, or patient benefit.

## Population and temporal design

Construct the population separately in every admissible source world.

* Include adults with age `年龄 >= 18` at the indexed encounter, an earliest source-documented eligible hepatobiliary resection under a preregistered expert-reviewed procedure dictionary, and same-patient/same-encounter pathology supporting the HCC frame.
* Exclude a patient-world if it contains an earlier qualifying resection, transplant, TACE/embolization, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in the patient-wide interval `[operation - 365 days, operation)`. Do not search only the index encounter for prior treatment.
* Use a validated point operation time only. A midnight/date-like case-record time is an interval `[date,date+24 hours)`; competing operation clocks remain coupled alternatives. Do not silently convert a date proxy to an exact instant.
* Define decision time as operation time minus 24 hours. The 12-, 48-, and 72-hour cutoffs are locked sensitivity trajectories; they cannot rescue or veto the 24-hour confirmatory result.
* Every retained CT/MRI acquisition interval must lie wholly in `[decision time - 90 days, decision time)`. Examination `开始时间` is acquisition-like only, never release, finalization, ingestion, retrieval, readability, or clinician-view time.
* Use chronology quarantine: development is 2015–2018, 2019 is the independent tuning/K-lock year, and 2020 and 2021 are separate patient-disjoint held-out tests. Possible 2019 cases cannot enter development; possible 2020/2021 cases cannot enter 2019 or development. 2022 onward is audit-only. No held-out outcome, visibility state, coverage curve, rank, or subgroup result may enter preprocessing, fitting, dictionary selection, tuning, or K selection.

The documentary endpoint is a same-encounter pathology-form terminal, not specimen-linked biological MVI. Two independent qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists and an adjudicator retain every pathology row and raw field boundary and assign HCC-frame/M2 terminals `OUT`, `IN0-A`, `IN1-A`, `IN0-U`, or `IN1-U`. Unresolved rows remain outer states; do not mix fields across readers or perform favorable complete-case deletion. Each global radiology-pathology pair applies unchanged to all patients, years, cutoffs, models, masks, and deletions.

## Coupled report-onset states

For each operation world and whole accession, separate acquisition eligibility from stored-report exposure. Let `E_{i,d,u}=1` only when the accession is nonblank, source-clean, valid CT/MRI, attached to the indexed patient/encounter, and its acquisition interval lies wholly in the 90-day window at cutoff `d`.

Conditional on `E=1`, assign one whole-version onset category:

* `O=0`: final/released/ingested/readable no later than the 72-hour cutoff;
* `O=1`: first eligible readability in (72,48] hours;
* `O=2`: first eligible readability in (48,24] hours;
* `O=3`: first eligible readability in (24,12] hours;
* `O=4`: not eligible/readable by 12 hours, or only later/never evidenced.

The exposure vector is `V(O)=(1{O=0},1{O<=1},1{O<=2},1{O<=3})` in 72/48/24/12 order, and `M_{i,d,u}=E_{i,d,u}V_d(O)`. Exposure is therefore monotone across cutoffs and acquisition cannot occur after the cutoff. If an external audit provides an interval, retain every compatible contiguous category and never use a midpoint. Without that audit, HCC-only sensitivity uses all five categories for every otherwise source-clean accession; this is interval-censored stored-content sensitivity, not observed real-time availability.

A required external audit must map each accession to exactly one whole report version using patient/encounter/accession key, source row ordinals, complete source-text hash, version and predecessor/amendment IDs, version hash, authored/final/release/ingestion/readability times, retraction status, timezone provenance, operation-clock validation, and exact pipeline-input hash. A valid first usable time is the maximum of finalization, release, ingestion, and technical readability after timezone normalization, subject to acquisition ordering and non-retraction. Missing mapping remains uncertain; a contradictory hash or invalid lineage is unavailable. Technical readability is not clinician viewing.

## Locked information sets and capacity

Fit and freeze four outcome-blind additive logistic pipelines:

* `B(Z)`: acquisition-only comparator;
* `R(Z,S,Q)`: acquisition plus report-surface/availability comparator;
* `G(Z,S,Q,X)`: semantic model using clean, expert-defined report meaning;
* `G0`: the same fitted G coefficients with every report-semantic block replaced by the training-defined unavailable block, without refitting.

`Z` includes age, sex, acquisition facts, eligible-unit count/recency, accession ambiguity, and development-grouped machine. `S/Q` include report surface and availability information; `X` contains only clean radiology-reader semantic features. Pathology, endpoint labels, identifiers, reader identity, untimed clinical narratives, labs, and test-derived variables are excluded. Use the preregistered regularization grid, outcome-blind patient-balanced preprocessing, robust development fitting across nine fixed radiology-pathology pairs, and independent 2019 tuning and K lock.

At each fixed state, compute `N_ref` as the minimum eligible patient count across 2015–2019, all cutoffs, and all retained operation/source states. Freeze `K=floor(rho N_ref)` for `rho in {0.05,0.10,0.20}`, requiring `N_ref>=500`, `K_.05>=25`, `K_.10>=50`, `K_.20>=100`, and at least 50 documentary-positive events. The 10%/24-hour result in each separate 2020 and 2021 test is confirmatory; other capacities and cutoffs are sensitivities. Exact binary64 score bits and a protected SHA-256 patient-key tie break freeze top-K order. Whole-patient deletion removes all records but never refits, re-quota, backfills, changes surviving ranks, or changes onset bits; a deleted selected position becomes an empty offered slot.

## Primary outcome and complete frontier

For each full-roster visibility/onset state, let `A_i` be all source-clean eligible whole accessions before the visibility mask. Patients with `A_i` empty remain in the denominator. For a complete accession mask `e`, define patient coverage `g_i(e)=sum_u e_u/|A_i|` when `|A_i|>0`, otherwise zero. Store the full ordered quantile curve `Q_e(r)`, including zero-coverage patients, rather than only the inherited 80% point.

For each 24-hour state, retain an identity-robust lower-orthant representation: a contract `L` is a set of rational threshold cells `(alpha,q)` requiring at least fraction `q` of eligible patients to have at least fraction `alpha` of their eligible accessions exposed. The state set `Z(L)` contains every coupled operation/source/onset/accession-identity state satisfying all cells in `L`. Thresholds are exact rationals derived from attainable accession and patient denominators; equal thresholds are deduplicated. No aggregate audit may choose favorable accession identities.

For every `L`, compute on the same state `z in Z(L)`: `H_M(z)`, the documentary-M2 patients in model M's exact top K; `D_{G,C}(z)=100(H_G-H_C)/K` for `C in {B,R,G0}`; `D_{G,rand}(z)=100H_G/K - 100Y/N`, where `Y` is the documentary-positive count before queueing; the exact false-positive burden; all attained worst-state witnesses; and `p_safe(L)=min_z H_G(z)/K`, an exact rational minimum documentary capture per offered position.

The computational output is the complete nondominated finite set over patient-coverage curves, `p_safe`, false-positive burden, and exact comparator/random/deletion witnesses. Dominance requires no worse value in every coordinate and strict improvement in at least one. Generate it by exact threshold-equivalence cells and a monotone feasibility/boundary oracle or complete finite branch-and-bound enumeration. Retain raw primal witnesses. No arbitrary 80% coverage threshold, sampled mask family, or investigator-selected identity arrangement is primary; the inherited 80% result is a regression landmark only.

A primary frontier cell passes documentary superiority gates only when, separately in 2020 and 2021, at 24 hours, rho=.10, every retained operation/source/onset/identity state, all nine reader/pathology pairs, and every whole-patient deletion satisfy all three `D_{G,C}>5` and `D_{G,rand}>5` predicates. Strict inequality uses exact integer cross-products; equality fails. The same state must supply all terms of each contrast. The cell must have `p_safe>0`. The full frontier reports cells failing one or more gates, with adverse witnesses.

The external preference variable `p` is not assigned by Harbor. It is an outcome-blind stakeholder exchange rate for documentary-M2 captures per review position, including burden/cost/harm context. A cell is preference-compatible only if an external interval `[p_L,p_U]` satisfies `p_U < p_safe(L)`; overlap is preference-heterogeneous and `p_L >= p_safe(L)` is preference-adverse. Without elicitation, the frontier is a documentary-computational result and remains bridge-pending.

## Falsification, verification, and interpretation

Create workspace-only ledgers for source/filter provenance, patients and operation states, accessions and component bytes, report-version/onset assignments, pathology and reader books, model hashes, K/ties, masks, coverage curves, frontier cells, and exact witnesses. The manifest must report every row read, all filters and exclusions, actual no-sampling processing, source/schema hashes, parser and dictionary versions, and source-to-derived backpointers.

Use exact enumeration or certified branch-and-bound/MIP over every finite state. A state cap, sampled onset family, omitted branch, unresolved incumbent, timeout, rank inversion, missing witness, floating-point-only breakpoint, nonmonotone onset vector, quotient replay failure, or fixture mismatch sets the computation label to `computationally_inconclusive`. Require transformed-loss residual <=1e-8, complete tie accounting, raw/quotient/hash replay, synthetic brute force through 12 patients, real shards through 20 patients, and regression equality with parent 24-hour acquisition, maximum-content, and 80%-landmark outputs.

Emit exactly one overall interpretation:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: exact computation or replay is incomplete.
2. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: even maximum-content G fails a primary comparator/random/singleton gate.
3. `availability_frontier_adverse`: maximum content passes, but no attainable patient-coverage cell passes strict gates.
4. `frontier_estimable_documentary_supportive`: a nonempty exact frontier cell passes all documentary gates, with no external action claim.
5. `frontier_preference_heterogeneous`: the documentary frontier is estimable but the external preference interval crosses relevant `p_safe` values.

A strict-gate failure with an attained witness falsifies the locked documentary-ranking claim for that state and contrast. A favorable 12-, 48-, or 72-hour sensitivity cannot rescue an adverse 24-hour result. A missing report-version/onset bridge is not adverse evidence; it prevents an action-compatible conclusion. Inconclusive never supports.

The verifier must link each reported conclusion to stored computed outputs and exact uncertainty witnesses. It must test that correct computation is not treated as scientific truth, and that adverse or inconclusive results are not misreported as supportive. It can check source binding, state completeness, arithmetic, replay, and whether documentary conclusions follow. It cannot establish pathology biology, sampling adequacy, Mandarin validity, clinician viewing, workflow, actual capacity, causal equity, treatment effect, safety, recurrence, survival, or patient benefit.
