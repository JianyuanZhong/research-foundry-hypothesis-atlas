## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
When culture/susceptibility results return for a critically ill patient on empiric broad-spectrum
antimicrobials, is **early de-escalation** (narrowing coverage) non-harmful **on average**, and
**specifically** non-harmful in patients whose organ function is **not yet improving**? Continuing
broad coverage drives resistance/toxicity; premature narrowing in a still-deteriorating patient could
delay adequate therapy. This is a guideline-relevant ICU decision.

## 2. Existing evidence vs the remaining question (three inspected works)
- **[K1] Zhang 2026** (MIMIC-IV v3.1 + eICU, target-trial emulation): *fixed day-4* antipseudomonal
  de-escalation was associated with **lower** 30-day mortality (RD -4.91 pp, 95% CI -7.27 to -2.23);
  anti-MRSA non-inferior. It did **not** use a culture-result landmark nor separate a pre-landmark
  organ-trajectory subgroup. — closest prior work.
- **[K2] Sava 2026** (scoping review): de-escalation is the principal stewardship strategy but its
  evidence base is fragmented and practice inconsistent — motivates an effect-modification test rather
  than another average-effect estimate.
- **[K3] Li 2026** (MIMIC-IV sepsis): TTE-with-cloning is an appropriate method for antibiotic-strategy
  effects, countering immortal-time/confounding artifacts — methodological precedent for A1.
Advance beyond prior work: re-anchor the decision at the **culture-result landmark** and test
**effect modification by pre-landmark SOFA trajectory** (the subgroup where safety is most in doubt).

## 3. Leading claim vs strongest rival
- Leading claim **H1**: de-escalation is non-harmful overall AND in the non-improving stratum (Delta ~ 0).
- Strongest rival **H2** (confounding by indication / care-trajectory): clinicians de-escalate patients
  they judge already improving, so apparent safety is created by prognosis; harm hides in non-improvers.
  Observed in K1's average effect not being separable from this structure.
- Observation separating them: the **sign of the interaction** `Delta = RD(non-improving) - RD(improving)`
  (H1: ~0; H2: >0), corroborated by a **negative-control exposure** (non-antimicrobial chronic-med
  discontinuation at the same landmark) and by whether the B0 protective RD **attenuates under A1**.

## 4. Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcome (frozen)
- Adults >=18; first `icu/icustays.stay_id` per `hosp/patients.subject_id`; non-null `hadm_id`.
- Landmark `t_R` = earliest qualifying `hosp/microbiologyevents.storetime` (non-null `org_name` OR
  `interpretation` in {S,I,R}) within `[icustays.intime, intime+72h]`.
- Exposure E=1: antimicrobial spectrum-breadth **decreases** within `(t_R, t_R+48h]` with no new
  broad agent; E=0: unchanged/increased — from `prescriptions.starttime/stoptime` (cross-check
  `emar.medication`, `pharmacy.medication`).
- Modifier M: partial-SOFA trajectory over `(t_R-48h,t_R-24h]` vs `(t_R-24h,t_R]`, dichotomized
  improving / non-improving.
- Outcome Y: 28-day all-cause mortality from `t_R` (`admissions.deathtime` else `patients.dod`).
- Estimand: adjusted RD(E vs not) overall and by M; `Delta = RD(non-improving) - RD(improving)`.
- One locked 70/30 temporal split by `icustays.intime`; bootstrap 95% CIs resampling by `subject_id`.

## 5. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative) — from Generation-B
- **B0** (interpretable, guideline-legible): pre-`t_R`-only covariate-ladder propensity-adjusted
  logistic/Cox; no post-landmark leakage. **Limitation it cannot remove:** immortal time from defining
  E over `(t_R,t_R+48h]` while follow-up starts at `t_R`, and time-varying confounding by indication.
- **A1** (substantive alternative; the scientific point of the comparison): clone-censor-weight
  target-trial emulation with time-varying IPTW/AIPW (+ g-computation cross-check) that uses evolving
  post-landmark covariates (SOFA components, vasopressors, new cultures) as **weight inputs, never as
  plain adjusters**. What A1 reveals that B0 loses: whether the B0 "safety" attenuates toward/past the
  null under correct treatment of immortal time and time-varying confounding (H2 diagnosis).
- **A2** (latent pre-landmark organ trajectory, state-space/growth-mixture): **deferred** with a stated
  reveal-gain (separation of true latent trajectory from charting noise; transportable "non-improving"
  phenotype) and revisit trigger (A1 residual within-stratum heterogeneity, or severe M misclassification).
- A discordant B0-vs-A1 band classification is itself the informative methodological result.

## 6. Falsification (frozen bands)
- Supportive (non-harmful): RD <= +0.02 AND RD upper CI <= +0.05 AND Delta <= +0.02 AND neg-control RD <= +0.01.
- Adverse: RD >= +0.03 with lower CI > 0 **OR** Delta >= +0.03; **OR** negative-control RD <= -0.02 (residual confounding).
- Otherwise **inconclusive** — a genuine outcome, especially with sparse strata.
Adverse means the safety claim fails (de-escalation harmful in the stratum that matters). Supportive in
both B0 and A1, with a null negative control, is the strongest claim the data can support (an adjusted
association, not causation). Inconclusive from sparse strata means the design must be revised/deferred.

## 8. Honest limits and what remains for another study
- Single-centre retrospective; **association, not causation** (a causal claim would need an RCT).
- `storetime` is a proxy for when results became actionable; the drug-spectrum dictionary is a crude proxy
  for true coverage; source control is incompletely observable.
- Partial-SOFA trajectory missingness may be differential (monitoring intensity).
- Prevalence/power **NOT observed** this episode; sparse strata -> inconclusive is a real possibility.
- Items requiring clinical adjudication or another study: mechanistic interpretation, drug-spectrum
  dictionary validity, `storetime` as true result-availability time, and any causal/mechanistic claim.
- Blinded clinical critique not completed this episode (budget). Recommended before compilation.

## Dispositions for the three research demonstrations
- **Delphi (learned disease sequences, Nature 2025)**: not pursued — requires dated, tokenized
  longitudinal event histories across a broad diagnosis space; the de-escalation question is a
  narrow landmark effect-modification test where a transparent TTE is scientifically adequate, and no
  bounded Delphi adaptation adds information the B0/A1 contrast does not already target. (Missing

- **Oncoformer (multimodal EHR + chest X-ray, Cell 2026)**: not pursued — the full multimodal experiment
  needs chest-X-ray image payloads that are not part of this question; a lab-only adaptation would not
  test anything the de-escalation estimand needs. (Explicit missing dependency: images; main article
  full text unavailable in the bundle.)
- **ALADYNOULLI (Bayesian longitudinal latent discovery, Nature 2026)**: partially relevant and

  revisit trigger, rather than adopted now because the dominant threat is time-varying confounding,
  which A1 targets directly and A2 does not.
