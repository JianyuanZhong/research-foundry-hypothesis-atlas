## Proposed relationship
Within the available E-MTAB-8873 dataset, a biologically interpretable intervention or perturbation contrast will show a context-dependent response: the same molecular response is protective in one measured context and pathogenic or adverse in another. The working estimand is the donor-aware difference in response between the two contexts for a prespecified target/pathway, not an unadjusted association between expression and phenotype.

The hypothesis is deliberately narrower than “the target is protective” or “the target is pathogenic.” It asks whether the direction and magnitude of the response depend on a defined context, and whether that dependence is reproducible across donors and the available assay contrasts.

## Competing explanations and falsification
1. Context-specific biological response: the prespecified contrast changes direction or magnitude across contexts in a donor-aware, reproducible pattern.
2. Global intervention effect: the apparent context difference is a shared shift across features or contexts and disappears after donor/batch-aware adjustment.
3. Donor or selection artifact: the result is driven by donor composition, sample selection, missingness, or technical batch.
4. Measurement/assay artifact: the result is not reproducible across the available assay contrasts or is explained by feature-level technical variation.

A supportive result requires a prespecified context-by-response contrast with uncertainty, donor-aware reproducibility, and a pattern not explained by the global or technical controls. An adverse result is a null or reversed pattern that is precise enough to challenge the proposed context dependence, or evidence that the apparent contrast is fully explained by a control. An inconclusive result is imprecision, insufficient donor replication, unavailable context labels, or failure of the assay registry to support the required contrast; it does not refute the hypothesis.

## Baseline versus substantive alternative
The simple baseline is a prespecified donor-aware difference-in-response estimate with uncertainty and explicit controls for donor and technical context. The substantive alternative is a context-conditioned model or mechanistic contrast on the same target/pathway and features, evaluated on the same split and outcome. It can reveal whether the response is conditional on a biological state or pathway context that the baseline averages away. It cannot identify a direct causal target mechanism without perturbation evidence, biological validation, or stronger longitudinal/phenotype data.

## Non-causal limits
Even a supportive computational result would establish a context-dependent association or response pattern in this assay. It would not establish that the target is directly protective or pathogenic, that an intervention causes the observed response, or that the result generalizes to patients. Those stronger conclusions require perturbation evidence, clinical adjudication, longitudinal phenotype data, and/or independent biological validation.
