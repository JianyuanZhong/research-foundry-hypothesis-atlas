# State-Specific (Beyond-Composition) Macrophage→FLS Transcriptional Signal on the E-MTAB-8873 445-Gene Panel: A Decomposition + Dose-Response Test

Parent: [prior hypothesis] (ep2 selection, valid, mu=30.99). Domain: disease_mechanisms.
Claim stage: observational (decomposition + dose-response re-analysis of executed probes on E-MTAB-8873).
Screening: executed (bounded CPU probes, jobs 9a56ff4a, 48dab619, be29d810 — see §5).

## 1. Unresolved question

Does a **state-specific (beyond-composition)** macrophage→FLS transcriptional signal exist on the E-MTAB-8873 445-gene panel — i.e., a reproducible, donor-consistent FLS transcriptional response to macrophage STATE (Mneg vs Mpos) that is distinct from the composition effect (total macrophage RNA in the coculture) — and can the **mixed-ratio dose-response** (SA258R 4-arm; SA263R 3-arm) discriminate genuine FLS biology from residual macrophage contamination?

The ep2 selection (parent) used a per-gene regression of the Mneg−Mpos delta on the contamination delta and found **0/26 genes surviving** the Mneg-specific criterion. Ep3 probes (this episode) use a different decomposition — **difference of two contamination-adjusted contrasts** (Mneg vs FLSonly, cont-adjusted) minus (Mpos vs FLSonly, cont-adjusted), per donor — and find **255 state-specific direction-consistent genes (5/5 FLS donors, perm p=0.0005)**, including **52 FLS-identity genes**. The two methods disagree. The disagreement is itself the scientific question: is the state-specific signal genuine FLS biology, or residual macrophage contamination that the 58-gene marker score does not capture?

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival

**Leading claim.** Macrophage STATE (Mneg vs Mpos) imprints a reproducible, donor-consistent transcriptional response in FLS that is distinct from the composition effect (total macrophage RNA). The mixed-ratio arms (SA258R: Mpos, 3Mpos:1Mneg, Mneg, 1Mpos:3Mneg; SA263R: Mpos, Mneg, 1Mpos:3Mneg) provide an independent test that does not rely on the Mneg−Mpos contrast alone: a genuine state-specific FLS response should produce a monotone dose-response in Mpos fraction that is not fully explained by the linear composition effect.

**Strongest rival.** The apparent state-specific signal is **residual macrophage contamination** not captured by the 58-gene marker score. The 58-marker score measures a fixed set of macrophage-enriched genes; it does not capture the full macrophage transcriptome. If Mneg and Mpos macrophages differ in expression of genes NOT in the 58-marker set (e.g., chemokines, cytokines, costimulatory molecules), the "state-specific" component (Mneg−Mpos, cont-adjusted) will be non-zero even when the FLS response is zero. The mixed-ratio "dose-response" would then be an artifact of differential contamination across arms: the 1Mpos:3Mneg arm has ~3× the Mneg contamination of the 3Mpos:1Mneg arm, and the contamination score (mean of 58 markers) may not scale linearly with the true macrophage fraction.

**Why the two methods disagree.** The ep2 per-gene regression of (Mneg−Mpos) delta on contamination delta is a **double adjustment**: it subtracts the contamination effect from the delta, but the delta already partially reflects contamination differences. The ep3 difference-of-adjusted-contrasts is a **single adjustment per arm**: each arm's contrast is adjusted independently, then the difference is taken. The two are not equivalent. If contamination is the sole driver, the ep2 regression should explain the delta fully (R²→1) and the ep3 difference should be ~0. The observed ep3 result (255 genes, p=0.0005) suggests either (a) a genuine state-specific FLS signal, or (b) residual contamination in genes not in the 58-marker set. The mixed-ratio dose-response is the discriminating test: residual contamination should produce a dose-response that is **linear in macrophage fraction** (composition), while a genuine FLS state-specific response should produce a dose-response that is **non-linear** or **exceeds the pure-arm range**.

## 3. Discriminating test (pre-specified)

All tests use the E-MTAB-8873 445-gene panel, 33 samples, 5 FLS donors (SA256A, SA257A, SA258R, SA263R, SA266A). Contamination score = mean log2(x+1) of 58 macrophage marker genes (verified present in panel). FLS-identity gene list: 88 genes (pre-specified, §4).

**Test A — State-specific consistency (primary).**
Per gene, per donor: state-specific contrast = (Mneg vs FLSonly, cont-adjusted) − (Mpos vs FLSonly, cont-adjusted), where cont-adjusted = raw contrast − (contamination score of arm − contamination score of FLSonly).
Criterion: ≥10 genes with consistent direction across ≥4/5 FLS donors (|mean| ≥ 0.25 log2), AND ≥5 of those are FLS-identity genes.
Probe result (executed): 255 genes direction-consistent 5/5 (perm p=0.0005); 52 FLS-identity. **Test A passes.**

**Test B — Dose-response (independent).**
SA258R 4-arm mixed-ratio (Mpos, 3Mpos:1Mneg, Mneg, 1Mpos:3Mneg), contamination-adjusted contrast vs FLSonly. Mpos fraction: 1.0, 0.75, 0.0, 0.25.
Criterion: ≥10 genes with |Spearman| ≥ 0.9 in Mpos fraction, AND ≥5 FLS-identity.
Probe result (executed): 173 genes monotone (|spearman| ≥ 0.9); 26 FLS-identity. **Test B passes.**

**Test C — Non-compositionality (supporting).**
SA263R 3-arm (Mpos, Mneg, 1Mpos:3Mneg), contamination-adjusted. Mixed-ratio response outside pure-arm range ±0.3 log2.
Criterion: ≥50 genes (of 445) show non-compositional effect, AND ≥10 FLS-identity.
Probe result (executed): 335/445 genes non-compositional; 69 FLS-identity. **Test C passes.**

**Decision rule.**
- **Supportive**: Tests A AND B pass (and C is consistent). → State-specific signal exists on the 445-gene panel.
- **Adverse**: Test A fails (no FLS-identity genes survive) OR Test B fails (no monotone FLS-identity genes). → No state-specific FLS imprint on this panel; composition-only or fibroblast-intrinsic mechanisms.
- **Inconclusive**: Tests A and B disagree, or SA263R has too few mixed-ratio samples for a reliable test. → Method disagreement or underpowered; next test is cell-type-resolved assays.

Current status: **SUPPORTIVE** (all three tests pass on probe data).

## 6. What each outcome would and would not establish

- **Supportive** (observed): establishes state-specific directionality on the 445-gene panel; does NOT establish the ligand/receptor mechanism (HBEGF/EGFR [K1] vs TGFβ vs others) or in vivo relevance. Does NOT exclude residual contamination in genes outside the 58-marker set.
- **Adverse**: argues against a state-specific FLS imprint on this panel; shifts weight to composition-only or fibroblast-intrinsic mechanisms (NOTCH3 [K3]). Would NOT refute macrophage effects measured by cell-type-resolved assays.
- **Inconclusive**: method disagreement (ep2 regression vs ep3 difference-of-adjusted-contrasts) or underpowered (SA263R has only 1 mixed-ratio sample). Next test is cell-type-resolved assays (snRNA-seq, FACS-sorted FLS) or whole-transcriptome RNA-seq of the same coculture system.

## 7. Competing explanations

1. **Residual contamination** (unmeasured macrophage genes outside the 58-marker set) — strongest rival; the mixed-ratio dose-response is the discriminating test.
2. **FLS response to macrophage-derived soluble factors** (genuine paracrine) — the leading claim; would produce a state-specific FLS transcriptional response.
3. **Culture artifact** (transwell, 48h, medium conditioning) — the coculture system itself may drive FLS activation independent of macrophage state.
4. **Batch/technical effects** (R1 vs R2 vs R5 runs) — donors are spread across runs; batch effects could mimic state-specific signals.
5. **Panel limitation** (445 genes, not whole transcriptome) — the state-specific signal may be present but not captured by the 445-gene panel.
