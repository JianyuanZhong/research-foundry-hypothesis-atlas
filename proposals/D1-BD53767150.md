# Semantics-robust calendar transport of assay information for the next recorded HCC transition

## Scientific deliverable and substantive advance

This is a substantive evolution of [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the incumbent's clinically bounded question—whether strictly prior assay results contain information beyond capture intensity and assay selection—and its fixed calendar-shift evaluation. It repairs the primary outcome identification problem exposed by the verified source audit: 243,950 of 322,166 procedure rows with a populated start time occur at minute 00, 15,874 procedure starts are missing or invalid, and the local procedure strings have no validated ontology. Therefore, the primary estimand no longer depends on a clinical procedure-family dictionary. Named families are retained only as a secondary, expert-adjudication-gated characterization.

The future solver must newly produce:

2. a semantics-independent, exhaustive recorded-transition label using native procedure-table presence/start and discharge timing, including explicit early discharge, procedure-time bins, no-procedure observation and unresolved timing states;
3. frozen A/L/Q/I/V feature manifests, where V adds assay-specific prior values/results, I adds assay identity/occurrence, and Q contains capture intensity but no assay identity or payload;
4. fitted transparent multinomial/discrete-time baseline models and a matched irregular-time marked encoder S, with S_I and S_Q ablations, all predicting the same joint transition-time target;

6. a secondary frozen lexical family audit and a random expert-adjudication sample, reported only if coverage, inter-rater agreement and timing support pass prespecified gates;
7. result/value, assay-identity, process-only, time-permutation, history-time and label/timing falsifications;
8. the incumbent's seven-state P0/D0/U0/R24 then P1/D1/U1/N72 sensitivity for direct comparability; and
9. a claim-to-output table separating computationally checkable findings from claims requiring clinical adjudication, linkage or another study.

Completion is the frozen data-flow/label audit, fitted models, locked predictions, proper-score/calibration/uncertainty outputs, falsification reports, and interpretation table. Completion does not require a positive result.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

Available evidence supports only that routine measurements and documentation/capture patterns may be associated with subsequent locally recorded care. It does not establish active HCC, tumor burden, stage, liver reserve, treatment indication, clinician intent, treatment receipt or completion, response, toxicity, survival, benefit, causal effects or clinical utility. The demonstrations were methodological context, not evidence for this HCC claim: Delphi supports evaluating dated structured histories and calibration; the Bayesian latent-trajectory work motivates an EHR-only irregular adaptation but its genetic inputs are unavailable; the cancer demonstration supports modality ablations, but its main article/full STAR Methods and local HCC images are unavailable. No reproduction is claimed. The imported expert seeds are UKB/MIMIC/eICU problems; there is no HCC seed, so none is a scientific parent.

The unresolved information question is:

> Among adults at their first locally recorded HCC-coded inpatient admission, do strictly pre-admission, assay-specific laboratory results and trajectories improve calibrated prediction of the next locally recorded procedure-versus-discharge transition and its coarse native-recorded timing beyond admission context, current-admission laboratories, prior capture intensity and prior assay identity/occurrence; does the result-content increment persist in the later calendar era?

The primary estimand is the paired held-out difference V minus I in multiclass log loss and Brier score for the semantics-independent joint label. Lower scores are better. The directional hypothesis is that V improves both scores in the locked 2021–2025 calendar evaluation, without material calibration deterioration, while the improvement attenuates after within-assay result/value permutation. A random-split gain alone is not confirmatory. I minus Q separately tests assay selection/occurrence; V minus Q is secondary parent comparability. The clinically relevant interpretation is operational only: whether the record suggests an imminent recorded procedure, early discharge, continued observation without a recorded procedure, or unresolved procedure timing, which could prioritize record review or coordination. It is not a recommendation or treatment prediction.

## Primary semantics-independent outcome

For every eligible admission, inspect procedures with valid native 开始时间 in [t0,min(t0+72h,出院时间)].

- If a valid procedure precedes discharge and is before 72h, assign the earliest resolved procedure to one of five time bins [0,6), [6,12), [12,24), [24,48), [48,72), with a single generic mark procedure_present. Procedure-table occurrence is not equated with a clinically completed treatment.
- If no valid procedure precedes discharge within 72h and discharge is by 72h, assign early_discharge.
- If no valid procedure occurs by 72h and discharge is after 72h, assign no_procedure_observed_72h.
- If missing/invalid procedure timing could change whether a procedure is first or its bin, assign unresolved_timing.
- Procedure/discharge exact ties use procedure-wins, with discharge-wins as a sensitivity. Procedures before admission, after discharge, exactly at 72h, and multiple earliest rows remain in audits.

The primary outcome thus tests information about a generic recorded transition and timing, not semantic treatment choice. As a secondary, freeze the incumbent's lexical labels (resection/transplant, locoregional, ablation, systemic infusion, diagnostic/access, other, combined/ambiguous) and report them only if a blinded expert sample establishes adequate recognizability and inter-rater agreement, with coverage and timing gates. Otherwise the semantic layer is explicitly inconclusive and cannot overturn the generic primary result. Rerun the incumbent seven-state P0/D0/U0/R24 then P1/D1/U1/N72 endpoint unchanged as a sensitivity.

## Nested predictors, baseline and alternative

A contains age, sex, admitting 就诊科室, calendar era, admission time-of-day, missingness indicators and strict-history availability. L adds assay-specific current [0,12h) summaries: first/last valid numeric result, change, elapsed time, count, density/missingness, and slope only with two distinct valid times, plus qualitative results. Q adds only prior capture intensity: row and distinct-encounter counts, active days/span, recency, source-specific counts for labs/procedures/orders/medications, missing-time counts, left-truncation and no-history flags. Q contains no names, assay identities, values, results, doses, routes, frequencies or statuses. I adds prior assay identity/occurrence and assay-specific count/recency/span/missingness, but no payload. V adds assay-specific qualitative and quantitative prior values/results and dated within-assay change/slope. All scaling is fit-only within assay; no unit-free physiologic score is invented. Outcome-window fields, procedure/order names, and future process rows are forbidden predictors.

The transparent baseline is regularized multinomial logistic regression over the generic mark-time cells, fit separately for A/L/Q/I/V on the identical split/labels. A discrete-time cause-specific hazard factorization is allowed only if it emits the same joint probabilities and is scored identically. Penalty, imputation, missingness indicators and iteration limits are frozen after selection-partition tuning. This baseline exposes feature nesting and supports direct paired score differences.

The substantive alternative is a fixed low-dimensional continuous-time marked event encoder S. It consumes the same strict-prior event rows and current-lab rows with source/table type, elapsed time, assay identity only where allowed, payload mask and assay-specific payload, then predicts the same generic mark-time cells, no-procedure censoring and unresolved-quality state. S_I removes prior lab payload but retains assay identity; S_Q removes identity and payload but retains process/timing. Fix latent dimension (suggested 64), decay/update family, regularization, optimizer, seed count and early stopping before locked evaluation. It can reveal nonlinear interactions, order-sensitive irregular trajectories and missingness uncertainty lost by first/last/count summaries. It is not selected for a small predictive gain: require seed stability, proper-score/calibration adequacy, and the same V-minus-I direction. CPU-first is reasonable for aggregation, sparse multinomial/discrete hazards, bootstrap and a low-dimensional alternative; an allocated A100 is optional for the sequence encoder only after an environment probe. These are planning estimates, not measured full-solver runtimes; the future envelope is 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, up to 8 GPUs and 28,800 seconds.

## Evaluation, falsification and interpretation

Report paired V-I, V-Q and I-Q differences in log loss and Brier score for random and calendar-locked evaluations, 95% patient-clustered paired bootstrap intervals with at least 1,000 resamples, calibration by state/time bin, and cumulative incidence/discrete probabilities at 24/48/72h. Report results by calendar era, admitting department and procedure source only when support gates pass. Use identical labels and partitions for baseline and S.

Falsifications are: (1) permute prior result/value within assay and fit strata while preserving assay identity/timing; (2) compare result-free process-only Q/S_Q; (3) permute history time/order while preserving payload; (4) compare the [t0−730,t0−30d) negative-control history window; (5) repeat anchor-linked pre-t0 history as sensitivity; (6) vary tie precedence and missing-time bounds; and (7) audit forbidden future-window fields and patient overlap. A meaningful V-minus-I increment should attenuate under value permutation, not be reproduced by Q alone, and not depend on a single coarse-time convention.

Supportive evidence requires a later-era V-minus-I improvement on both primary proper scores with a 95% interval excluding zero in the favorable direction, no material calibration harm, adequate support, attenuation under value permutation, and consistency across supported strata. Adverse evidence is a null/worse later-era increment, process-only parity, persistence after result permutation, strong time-permutation persistence, calibration failure, or instability across tie/missing-time rules. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse states, over 50% unresolved labels, insufficient strata, wide intervals, failed model convergence, semantic adjudication failure, or timing sensitivity too large to identify the target. A random-split improvement alone is not supportive of calendar transport.

Computation can establish data-flow correctness, split/temporal safety, feature nesting, label counts, predictions, scores, calibration, uncertainty, heterogeneity and falsification behavior. It cannot establish that a generic procedure row represents treatment receipt, intent, indication, completion or benefit; expert adjudication is needed for semantic recognizability. Active HCC, stage, tumor burden, resectability, imaging/pathology timing, laboratory units/reference ranges, causal treatment effects, mortality/recurrence, outside-hospital capture, external transportability, patient utility and implementation value require additional clinical data, external validation, and prospective/causal or implementation studies.

## Alternatives retained or deferred

The incumbent's named-family primary was not discarded because semantic pathways are unimportant; it was demoted because the verified dataset has no validated procedure ontology and substantial missing/coarse timestamps. The family layer should be revisited if an expert-adjudicated sample shows stable mapping and adequate event support. A full Delphi-style transformer remains deferred because the primary scientific uncertainty is whether assay payload survives capture and calendar shift, and the transparent plus irregular marked comparison tests that uncertainty more audibly within the planning envelope; revisit with strong residual order-sensitive signal or a new external validation question. A Bayesian genetic adaptation is deferred because genetic inputs are not in the HCC catalog; an EHR-only latent model is retained above. The full Oncoformer multimodal adaptation is deferred because local images and the main article/STAR Methods are unavailable; a lab/EHR ablation is represented by V/I/Q and S/S_I/S_Q. None of these deferrals is a blanket neural-network or GPU ban.
