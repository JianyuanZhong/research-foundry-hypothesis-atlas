# Source-rebuilt, leakage-safe observability design for the persistent respiratory-state question

## Repair target and substantive change

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen 540 person-landmark/443-stay denominator, first qualifying stay, five landmarks, exact two care-plan labels, label decision time `D`, inherited exclusions, eICU snapshot, and all semantic limits. It does not repeat the completed label-vocabulary or transportability audits.

The most consequential remaining validity problem is post-decision observability conditioning. In the parent design, the primary event requires a `respiratoryCare` record during the post-D window and the main risk difference is calculated among complete, non-competing endpoint observations. Both requirements are downstream of D and can be label-dependent: a care-plan label may change what is charted, and conditioning on a post-D respiratory-care record can select a different subset of stays. The proposed state endpoint can therefore produce an apparent label association that is an ascertainment contrast rather than a patient-state contrast, even though its numeric parsing and competing-exit rules are otherwise sound. The earlier inverse-observation weighting, estimated only from pre-D variables, cannot generally correct label-induced post-D observation.

This child repairs that flaw in two ways:

1. **Primary estimand:** a four-state/multinomial outcome is defined for all 540 frozen rows, with persistent improvement, observed non-improvement, endpoint missing, and competing exit as mutually exclusive states. Missingness and competing exit are not discarded from the primary prediction comparison or silently coded as no improvement. The airway record is removed from the primary event definition and becomes a prespecified secondary corroboration/stratification variable.
2. **Cohort reproducibility:** parent row-artifact presence is no longer a prerequisite for source access. The compiler must deterministically rebuild the frozen cohort and exposure from the read-only eICU sources using an outcome-blind, versioned eligibility manifest; if a supplied parent artifact exists it is compared byte-for-byte and any mismatch is a hard stop. A missing parent row artifact therefore triggers a rebuild-and-identity check rather than an automatic compilation failure, while a mismatch still prevents any endpoint analysis.

A positive result would support only incremental prediction of a structured respiratory-chart trajectory, not bedside evaluation, SBT, extubation readiness, treatment effect, quality, benefit, or harm.

## Evidence boundary and falsifiable question

The strongest supported claim from the lineage is limited to exported structured documentation: the exact labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated, their earlier chart mappings do not meet an unqualified multicenter transportability criterion, peak-pressure charting dominated the earlier composite, and durable liberation cannot be ascertained from available independent markers. None of this establishes what clinicians did at the bedside.

The unresolved claim is: among the locked labelled person-landmarks, does the evaluation label add reproducible information about a subsequent persistent, safety-preserving, non-peak respiratory trajectory beyond pre-D state and pre-D documentation opportunity, when every row—including rows with no post-D observation—is retained in the estimand?

The primary hypothesis is that, relative to `no_trial`, `evaluation` increases the marginal probability of the persistent-improvement state and improves held-out prediction of the complete four-state outcome over the same pre-D state/opportunity model without the label. Falsifiers are no improvement in the four-state distribution, a gain confined to post-D documentation availability, a pre-D or lead-time placebo of similar size, reversal under hospital holdout, component/site dependence, or inadequate support. This remains a descriptive conditional association/information question, not a causal effect.

## Frozen population and deterministic source rebuild

### Required frozen identity

The target population is exactly 540 person-landmarks from 443 unique first qualifying ICU stays and 74 hospitals, with landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission. Required row identity is `(patientunitstayid, hospitalid, uniquepid, landmark)` (the parent aliases `patientunitstayid` as `sid` in some artifacts). Retain repeated landmarks within a retained first stay and cluster them by `patientunitstayid`; do not collapse them into independent persons or select landmarks based on outcomes.

The exposure is the first qualifying exact `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue` in `[L,L+360]`, with `D = cplitemoffset`:

* `evaluation`: `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`
* `no_trial`: `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`

Contradictory first target labels at the same qualifying time follow the inherited exclusion. No care-plan row after D may affect eligibility, baseline, or the endpoint.

### Rebuild protocol, independent of endpoint rows

The compiler must write `cohort_rebuild_manifest.json` before reading respiratory outcome rows. It contains the exact source hashes, schema hashes, code hash, normalized-string function, numeric parser, sort keys, filter counts, and the frozen inherited eligibility predicate transcribed from the parent lineage. It must be created from the parent proposals/support, not inferred from respiratory outcomes. Every deterministic operation uses stable sorting by source primary identifier and `patientunitstayid`; ties are resolved by the source row identifier, never input-file order.

Rebuild in the following stages.

1. Read only `[internal dataset path]` for patient identity, age, `uniquepid`, `patientunitstayid`, `hospitalid`, `unitvisitnumber`, and discharge fields. Apply the inherited adult definition, first-qualifying-stay rule, ICU continuation and all pre-landmark eligibility exclusions exactly as frozen in the parent specification. Do not use any field whose timestamp is after the relevant landmark. Produce one immutable stay table with a deterministic row hash.
2. Generate the five landmark rows from each retained stay, retaining only the inherited landmark-continuation criteria. This stage must report counts after each inherited filter and the final expected 540 rows/443 stays/74 hospitals. A different count is a rebuild failure, not permission to alter thresholds.
3. Verify the inherited pre-L invasive/physiologic/end-of-life and prior `Spontaneous - adequate` exclusions from the exact parent source bindings and frozen rules. The rebuild manifest records source table, key, clinical offset, field, normalized values, open/closed boundary, and exclusion count for each predicate. Any predicate for which the parent rule or source binding cannot be recovered is a hard feasibility stop; no analyst may replace it with an outcome-derived approximation.
4. Read `carePlanGeneral.csv.gz` only after the target-independent row set is frozen. Filter exact target values by the literal strings above, retain rows in `[L,L+360]`, sort by `(patientunitstayid, landmark, cplitemoffset, cplgeneralid)`, exclude inherited same-time contradictions, and select the first qualifying target row. Verify one exposure row per retained person-landmark, exact label, and `D == cplitemoffset`.
5. If the parent byte artifacts are supplied, compare the rebuilt population and action rows at byte level and by row identity/hash to the required parent hashes (`[source checksum]` and `[source checksum]`). If absent, the rebuilt artifacts and manifest become the frozen inputs, but the compiler must state that byte identity could not be externally confirmed and must not silently call the result a parent replication. If the rebuilt counts, identity, labels, D values, or hashes disagree, stop before outcome rows and report inconclusive feasibility.

This fallback is deterministic and source-bound, but it cannot rescue an unavailable or underspecified inherited predicate. The correct response to an unresolvable parent rule remains a hard stop, not a relaxed cohort.

## Leakage-safe temporal rules and endpoint construction

For every frozen row, use the following disjoint windows, with offsets measured from ICU admission.

* Strict pre-decision baseline `B = (D-720,D)`; the upper boundary is **open**. A respiratory or vital row at exactly D is not baseline because its clinical timestamp may coincide with, or follow, the care-plan decision. The prior trajectory window is `P = (D-1440,D-720]`. The boundary at `D-720` belongs only to P.
* Post-decision observation `O = (D+360,D+1800]`; the six-hour washout remains locked. Horizon sensitivity is `O12 = (D+360,D+1080]`.
* `respchartentryoffset` is never substituted for clinical time. All equality/open/closed boundaries are unit-tested in the verification artifact.

Parse only finite numeric values after trimming the eICU representation and applying field-specific, outcome-blind physiologic bounds frozen before arm-specific counts. A component window requires at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes; summarize with the median and retain counts, unique times, first/last offset, gap, and parse/missing reason. Primary support components are FiO2 decrease >=0.05, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP decrease >=2 cm H2O, pressure-support decrease >=2, or set-rate decrease >=2. Peak inspiratory pressure is excluded from the primary.

Safety uses `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `respiration` with the same observation rule. When observed in both B and O, safety passes if median Sao2 does not fall by >2 percentage points and median respiration does not rise by >4 breaths/min. If a safety signal is absent in both windows, it is missing—not safe. Predeclare both-signal-required and at-least-one-signal sensitivities.

### Primary four-state outcome

Each frozen row receives exactly one state, in this order.

1. **Competing exit:** discharge/death/truncation makes the end of O unavailable, based on `patient.unitdischargeoffset` and discharge status/location. Discharge at or before `D+1800` is competing for the primary horizon; report no post-washout opportunity separately for discharge at or before `D+360`.
2. **Endpoint missing:** no competing exit but the minimum B/O observations or required safety availability fail.
3. **Persistent improvement:** no competing exit, complete support and safety domains, and at least one primary support reduction with safety preserved. No airway record is required.
4. **Observed non-improvement:** no competing exit, complete endpoint observation, and persistent-improvement criteria are false, including support reduction with safety failure.

The primary analysis predicts this nominal four-state outcome for all 540 rows. Report the full state distribution by arm, hospital, and landmark, along with the evaluation-minus-no-trial difference for each state. Use a multinomial or one-vs-rest model whose outcome categories include missingness and competing exit; the label-added and label-free models must use identical pre-D covariates, preprocessing, and training-only fitting. This is the primary observability-safe estimand because it does not condition on a post-D respiratory-care record or discard rows with no endpoint.

`respiratoryCare` is not required for the primary state. During O, record whether a nonmissing `airwaytype` or ventilator interval exists, its timing, and observation density. A **secondary corroborated event** adds the airway record requirement; a separate secondary analysis stratifies the primary four-state outcome by pre-D airway opportunity. These are explicitly ascertainment-sensitive and cannot replace the primary.

A complete/non-competing binary risk difference may be reported only as a secondary, selection-sensitive estimand with its denominator and all four states shown. Pre-D-only inverse-observation weights may be used as a sensitivity, but cannot be described as correcting label-induced post-D observation. If label-specific post-D opportunity differs, the primary four-state result is the only transportable computational result available from this design.

## Covariates, held-out analysis, and clustering

The state-only baseline contains only pre-D respiratory/vital values, trends, source presence, counts, distinct timestamps, median gaps, and entry-lag summaries from B and P. An opportunity-only model contains pre-D source presence/counts/timestamps/lag but no measured respiratory values or care-plan values. The label-added model adds only the binary exact label. No post-D feature, care-plan count/value, note, treatment string, or endpoint-derived variable enters baseline.

The primary transport estimand is leave-one-hospital-out prediction. For every held-out hospital, fit parsing decisions, imputation, standardization, regularization, missingness weights (if used), and model parameters using training hospitals only. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro multiclass log loss and Brier score, classwise calibration, and hospital-specific state probabilities. A label contrast is not claimed unless at least five hospitals have >=10 rows in each label arm with primary four-state observations represented and at least 50% of rows arise in those hospitals; otherwise classify the arm contrast as inconclusive. Folds with one observed label do not receive an artificial contrast.

Repeated landmarks are clustered by `patientunitstayid`. Bootstrap resamples stays within hospital strata and preserves all retained landmarks of each sampled stay; no row bootstrap is allowed. Use fixed seed `61061`, 1,000 complete pipeline refits if feasible or at least 500 with the shortfall reported before inspecting results. A label permutation is valid only within hospital-by-landmark strata with both labels and uses the same frozen four-state outcome; if fewer than 20 valid strata exist, report it unavailable rather than manufacture a null.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

1. **Pre-D placebo:** apply the same support/safety rules to P versus B, with strict boundaries. A comparable label-added increment is adverse to a newly emerging post-label interpretation.
2. **Lead-time placebo:** move exposure time to `D-720` while retaining the D-relative O window. Similar gain suggests pre-existing trajectory or leakage.
3. **Opportunity negative control:** predict post-D respiratoryCare/respiratoryCharting/vital source density and entry lag. Documentation gain without a corresponding four-state value gain supports ascertainment, not patient-state information.
4. **Component ablations:** remove each support and safety component; separately report the airway-corroborated secondary endpoint. A gain surviving only one field, peak pressure, airway presence, or one hospital is not supportive.
5. **Strict copied-value sensitivity:** repeat with 60-minute separation and first/last summaries. Large instability is chart-persistence sensitivity.
6. **Source-bound robustness:** repeat O12 and the inherited 24-, 12-, and 6-hour pre-L airway variants without redefining the frozen person-landmark population; publish attrition and state availability for each.
7. **Disjoint-time audit:** verify no timestamp-D row enters B/P, no O row enters baseline, and no care-plan row after D enters any predictor. This specifically tests the repaired leakage boundary.

Call the result **computationally supportive** only if the primary four-state label-added model improves both micro and equal-hospital macro log loss over the identical pre-D state/opportunity model by >=0.01, the stay-cluster interval excludes zero favorably, at least five hospitals satisfy the support rule, the marginal persistent-improvement contrast is positive in at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions, and the gain survives the pre-D/lead-time placebos, component ablations, opportunity negative control, and at least two horizon/airway-bound sensitivities. Even then, the claim is only incremental information in this structured chart construct.

Call it **adverse** only when four-state support is adequate and a prespecified failure reproduces in a bounded sensitivity: no label increment, reversal across held-out hospitals, placebo/opportunity explanation, or component dependence. Call it **inconclusive** when the rebuild cannot verify the inherited predicate or identity, fewer than five hospitals support both arms, competing/missing states dominate or are arm-imbalanced, permutation/cluster uncertainty is unavailable, or bounded definitions disagree. Do not turn endpoint missingness into no event, require post-D airway documentation for a positive result, relax the exact labels, or use a row-level bootstrap.

## Required machine-readable outputs and limits

Write `source_manifest.json`, `cohort_rebuild_manifest.json`, `frozen_population_verified.csv`, `exposure_verified.csv`, `trajectory_rows.parquet`/CSV, `site_support.csv`, `heldout_metrics.json`, `bootstrap_summary.json`, `permutation_summary.json`, and `verification.json`. The verification file must include source/schema hashes, rebuild filter counts, parent identity/hash comparison or explicit absence, duplicate/one-to-one checks, boundary tests, no-post-D-baseline audit, four-state exhaustiveness, held-out split isolation, cluster unit, and mechanically derived gate decisions.

The verifier can check all computation, leakage boundaries, source joins, state exhaustiveness, held-out metrics, uncertainty, and whether conclusions follow the gates. It cannot determine whether chart values were delivered settings, whether `airwaytype` proves device state, whether either label expresses SBT intent, or whether the proxy equals readiness/liberation. Those require device-linked data, local workflow/interface metadata, blinded clinical adjudication, validated extubation/reintubation outcomes, and external/prospective replication.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Compared with `[prior hypothesis]`, the primary endpoint no longer conditions on a post-D `respiratoryCare` record or complete-case availability; it retains all rows in a mutually exclusive four-state outcome and moves airway corroboration to a secondary ascertainment analysis. The baseline upper boundary is changed from `(D-720,D]` to strict `(D-720,D)`, eliminating same-offset decision leakage. The missing parent row-artifact dependency is repaired by a deterministic, outcome-blind rebuild from the specified read-only eICU tables, with byte/hash comparison when artifacts exist and a hard identity stop on disagreement. The clinical question, frozen cohort target, labels, landmarks, decision time, and evidence limits are unchanged.

The rebuild remains conditional on recovering the inherited parent eligibility predicate exactly; if that predicate is not available in the parent support, no source-only approximation is valid. The data still lack bedside/device truth and the endpoint may be sparse or documentation-dependent. Those are explicit feasibility and interpretation limits, not reasons to overstate a negative or positive result.
