# Fixed-capacity HCC documentary-M2 review with subgroup safety and availability frontiers

## Question and clinically consequential advance

The clinical decision under study is whether a scarce, retrospective multidisciplinary review queue could identify more patients whose eventual first-resection pathology documentation records high-grade microvascular invasion (documentary M2) from preoperative CT/MRI report material than fixed-capacity alternatives, while avoiding a demonstrable loss of documentary-M2 capture in the sex or age groups represented in this corpus. The action is allocation of a fixed number of historical review positions only. This experiment does not recommend resection extent, transplantation, locoregional or systemic treatment, surveillance, or any treatment change.

The selected parent already repairs an arbitrary global 80%-visibility contract by computing the complete patient report-coverage curve and a finite availability–preference frontier. This successor preserves that design and adds one clinically important bridge: a high-performing queue that systematically fails to capture documentary-M2 cases in a represented subgroup could worsen access to review even if its overall capture is favorable. The subgroup analysis therefore becomes a locked safety screen and a reported equity profile, not a claim of causal fairness or deployment benefit.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is only that the HCC snapshot contains linked demographics, encounters, procedure records with possible operation clocks, acquisition-like examination timestamps and report narratives, and same-encounter pathology narratives. The source supports a historical ranking experiment over a documentary endpoint. It does not establish report release or viewing before a decision, biological/specimen-linked MVI, clinical workflow, actual review capacity, clinician response, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or patient benefit.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> In held-out 2020 and 2021, at each retained cutoff, reader/pathology state, chronology state, whole-accession availability mask, and whole-patient deletion projection, there is a nonempty patient-coverage/availability frontier on which the locked semantic ranking G captures more documentary-M2 cases than both the independently fitted acquisition-only comparator B and capacity-matched random review, while no estimable prespecified sex or age subgroup has a negative G-versus-random documentary-M2 capture excess or a worse G-versus-B excess than the locked comparator direction.

This is a finite-corpus, documentary hypothesis. “No subgroup harm” means no negative exact robustness contrast under the specified states and deletion projections; it does not mean equal clinical opportunity, absence of historical inequity, or safety in practice. The experiment can falsify the locked ranking and its subgroup-safety claim, but cannot prove biological MVI prediction or patient benefit.

- R(Z,S,Q): report-surface/availability comparator;

- G-mask: the same G fit with semantic blocks replaced by the training-defined unavailable block and no refit.

## New subgroup-safety estimand

The subgroup set is locked before model fitting:

- sex: `性别=男` and `性别=女`;
- age at the indexed encounter: <50, 50–64, 65–74, and >=75 years, using `年龄`.

No race/ethnicity, language, socioeconomic, disability, geography, insurance, or other protected attribute is available in the HCC source and none is inferred.

For every held-out year, cutoff, state, reader pair, deletion projection, model, K, and subgroup s, let N_{s} be the subgroup denominator, E_s be the number of documentary-Y=1 patients, and H_{M,s} be the number of those patients in M’s exact top-K roster. If E_s>0, define the exact subgroup capture rate C_{M,s}=H_{M,s}/E_s. The capacity-matched random-review expectation is B_{rand,s}=K E_s/N_s; its exact excess is

[
D_{M,s}=H_{M,s}-K E_s/N_s.
]

All comparisons use integer numerator and rational denominator; no rounded rate determines a gate. Report H, E, N, C, D, and the attained year/state/reader/deletion witness.

The subgroup safety screen is estimable only for strata meeting locked development-only support rules: at least 25 documentary-Y=1 patients and at least 100 eligible patients in the pooled 2015–2018 development roster at the primary cutoff, under the robust outer label state. These rules are fixed before held-out evaluation and do not discard a sparsely observed held-out stratum; a held-out stratum with E_s=0, or with fewer than 10 documentary-Y=1 patients, is labelled subgroup_inconclusive and is still reported. If a subgroup fails the development support rule, its scientific subgroup conclusion is subgroup_inconclusive, not subgroup-safe.

For each estimable subgroup, the locked no-harm predicate is universal over the retained held-out years, cutoffs, states, reader pairs, deletion projections, and K values:

1. D_{G,s} >= 0, so semantic review does not have a negative documentary-M2 capture excess versus capacity-matched random review;
2. H_{G,s} >= H_{B,s}, with ties not called superiority;
3. the primary overall strict >5-capture margins and all parent comparator/random/singleton gates still pass.

The subgroup result is subgroup_supportive only if every estimable subgroup satisfies both no-harm predicates in every required state and every required subgroup has the locked support. Any negative D_{G,s} or H_{G,s}<H_{B,s} in an estimable state is subgroup_adverse, with its exact witness. A subgroup failure does not get hidden by favorable overall performance. A sparse or unsupported subgroup is subgroup_inconclusive and blocks any universal subgroup-safety claim; it does not falsify the overall documentary frontier by itself.

Alongside the safety screen, compute the complete subgroup report-coverage quantile curve Q_{e,s}(r), subgroup event share, top-K capture share, and the maximum and minimum subgroup D contrasts for every nondominated overall availability cell. This separates three phenomena: source/report availability concentration, model ranking harm conditional on availability, and overall capacity scarcity.

The parent’s joint availability frontier remains primary. A cell is on the successor frontier only if it is nondominated by (overall patient-coverage curve, every estimable subgroup coverage curve, p_safe, worst-state false-positive burden, and the recorded subgroup D/H witnesses). Dominance requires no worse value in every coordinate and strict improvement in at least one. Subgroup coverage is reported as an equity profile even when a cell is not dominated on the overall curve; it is not converted into an unsupported causal access claim.

## Availability and preference bridge

For each full-roster mask e, retain the parent’s patient coverage curve and compute h_G, the minimum integer G documentary-M2 captures over both test years, all admissible states, and whole-patient deletions. Let p_safe=h_G/K. Review versus no review is favorable only under the exact rational predicate h_G>pK, where p is an externally elicited, outcome-blind exchange rate for documentary-M2 captures per review position. Harbor does not assign p. Equality is not favorable. Require each comparator/random contrast and each singleton coordinate to exceed the parent’s strict >5-capture margin.

The computational result is supportive only if maximum-content G is not adverse, every exact overall comparator/random/singleton gate passes, all estimable subgroup no-harm predicates pass, and a nonempty nondominated frontier cell is completely enumerated. If maximum content is adverse, label maximum_content_signal_adverse. If maximum content passes but no availability cell passes, label availability_frontier_adverse. If a required subgroup is unsupported or any exact computation gate fails, label computationally_inconclusive or subgroup_inconclusive as applicable; inconclusive never supports.

Action-compatible status requires a separate external bridge:

1. a protected archive audit with immutable report version/predecessor IDs or hashes; authored, finalized, released, amended, retracted, ingested, and readable/view timestamps; timezone provenance; exact whole-pipeline hash crosswalk; validated operation clock; and the eligible denominator;
2. observed patient and subgroup report-coverage lower curves from that audit;
3. an outcome-blind stakeholder elicitation yielding [p_L,p_U] and burden/cost/harm values, with every audited cell satisfying p_U<p_safe;
4. subgroup-specific archive completeness sufficient to distinguish missing reports from genuinely unavailable reports.

The current HCC source has none of those report-version/release/view, pathology specimen/linkage, workflow, capacity, preference, burden, cost, or harm fields. Experts must adjudicate the procedure dictionary, Mandarin semantic rules, reader qualifications, pathology sampling/specimen linkage, and whether documentary M2 corresponds to biological MVI. A prospective silent-mode multicentre study is required for clinician action, transportability, treatment effects, safety, and patient benefit.
