# Observation-aware renal/nutrition safety signal after recorded insulin

Status: substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`; proposed for Lead selection. This is a prognostic and observation-process study of a recorded safety marker, not a causal insulin, feeding, AKI, or alert-benefit study. No model has been fitted and no clinical result is claimed.

## Scientific deliverable

The solver must newly:

1. freeze an outcome-blind glucose-unit, creatinine-label, insulin-rate, nutrition-label and coverage codebook;
2. construct one non-overlapping qualifying insulin episode per adult patient;
3. estimate the 24-hour detected-low risk contrast for recorded creatinine worsening plus a documented nutrition-delivery gap versus neither, at comparable recorded insulin intensity;
4. fit and audit a glucose-observation model, reporting the observation probability separately from low-glucose risk;
5. compare a transparent discrete-time baseline with a temporal multi-task alternative on the identical episodes, hospital splits and endpoints; and
6. return a support-gated interpretation traceable to counts, estimates, uncertainty, calibration, transport and falsification outputs.

Completion requires cohort-flow and codebook artifacts; counts by exposure cell, hospital, observation status, outcome and competing event; baseline and alternative estimates with uncertainty when supported; observation positivity/effective sample size; held-out-hospital calibration and alert-burden value; falsification results; and a limitations/interpretation file. If the event/support gate fails, completion is a documented feasibility/inconclusive report rather than an unstable fitted interaction.

## Unresolved claim and clinical importance

The strongest available evidence is limited to an outcome-blind feasibility audit already recorded by the parent: 10,423 insulin-plus-recent-glucose periods across 2,798 stays and 58 hospitals, narrowed to 783 conservative paired-support periods across 465 stays and 32 hospitals, with 9 detected <70 mg/dL events and one event in the renal-worsening/documented-gap cell. These are not estimates of clinical hypoglycemia and do not establish the hypothesis.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> In adults’ first ICU stay, after a valid recent glucose and a numeric exact-label recorded insulin-rate update, the joint marker of recorded creatinine worsening and no qualifying documented nutrition delivery in the preceding 12 hours is associated with a clinically meaningful higher probability of a first newly detected glucose <70 mg/dL during the next 24 hours, at comparable recorded insulin intensity and prior glucose-testing history; the signal is not explained entirely by whether glucose was checked.

This matters because a reproducible marker could prioritize bedside glucose review and prospective chart adjudication during a high-risk insulin period. A supportive result would justify a prospective monitoring/chart-review study, not an insulin or feeding order. The substantive advance over the seed and parent is an explicitly two-part ascertainment analysis: it tests whether the joint marker predicts a detected safety event beyond documentation opportunity, while retaining the clinically relevant detected-low endpoint. It does not claim to identify unrecorded hypoglycemia.

## Population, index and time

Use eICU snapshot `[source checksum]`. Offsets are minutes from ICU admission; `unitadmitoffset` is unavailable, so ICU offset 0 is the time origin.

Include age >=18 (encode age >89 as 90), positive `unitdischargeoffset`, and the smallest valid `unitvisitnumber` per `uniquepid`, breaking ties by `patientunitstayid`. The index t is the earliest numeric exact `drugname = 'Insulin (units/hr)'` row at or after 1,440 minutes and before ICU discharge, with a valid recent glucose in [t-360,t]. Primary rate is the parseable `drugrate`; `infusionrate` is audited and used as a prespecified sensitivity, not silently substituted. Keep only the first qualifying episode per `uniquepid`; an optional sensitivity permits later episodes separated by >=1,440 minutes with patient-clustered uncertainty.

Predictors stop at t. Follow-up stops at the earliest ICU discharge, documented death/discharge status, care-plan EOL save or discussion, or t+1,440 minutes. Discharge/death/EOL before an observed outcome is a competing event, not a negative glucose.

### Outcome and estimand

Let O24 indicate at least one valid post-index glucose observation in (t,t+1,440] and let Y24 indicate that the first valid post-index glucose in that interval is <70 mg/dL (if multiple same-time/revised results exist, retain the latest valid result by `labresultrevisedoffset) under the frozen reconciliation rule). The primary estimand is the standardized detected-low risk difference and risk ratio between:

- joint marker: continuous recorded creatinine change plus documented nutrition-gap category; and
- reference: no recorded creatinine worsening plus documented delivery,

evaluated at the empirical covariate distribution and comparable recorded insulin intensity.

The primary detected-low endpoint is Y24 among all eligible episodes, with O24 reported alongside it. Secondary quantities are (a) O24, (b) P(Y24=1 | O24=1), (c) <54 mg/dL among observed readings, and (d) six-hour detected-low risk. “No glucose observed” is a separate outcome state. An observation-weighted analysis is a sensitivity under an explicit missing-at-random-given-history assumption; it may estimate a documentation-adjusted detected-low quantity, not latent true hypoglycemia.

Recorded renal worsening is the latest valid creatinine in [t-360,t] minus the latest in [t-1,800,t-1,080], requiring both. Primary exposure is continuous change; >=0.3 mg/dL and >=1.5-fold changes are sensitivity definitions. Use “recorded creatinine worsening,” never KDIGO AKI. Nutrition gap requires a positive numeric `intakeOutput.cellvaluenumeric` before t and no qualifying positive delivery in (t-720,t]. The whitelist must be frozen from `cellpath`, `celllabel` and `cellvaluetext` review; include oral/P.O., enteral/tube and parenteral/TPN only, and exclude flushes, medication/insulin volume, dextrose-only and ambiguous labels. Report “no interface,” “inadequate coverage,” and “no qualifying documented delivery” separately. Primary clinical time is `intakeoutputoffset`; `intakeoutputentryoffset` is a timestamp sensitivity.

## Analysis and alternatives

First build a cross-fitted observation model for O24 using only through-t history: prior glucose values/gaps, number and interface of prior glucose tests, other-lab testing density, severity/physiology history, admission context, hospital training-fold indicators and EOL/discharge context. Report calibration, positivity, stabilized weights, truncation, and effective sample size. Never label O24=0 as Y24=0.

### Selected transparent baseline

B0 is a pooled discrete-time logistic or complementary-log-log cause-specific model for Y24, O24 and competing discharge/death/EOL, with prespecified splines for insulin rate, prior glucose level/slope/gaps, age, sex if available, weight, admission source/type, early severity/vasopressor context, testing/coverage history and hospital effects, excluding renal/nutrition exposures. B1 adds continuous renal change, nutrition-gap category and their interaction. Standardize B0/B1 predictions to the reference and joint marker while retaining all other covariates at their empirical values. Report risk difference, risk ratio, calibration, Brier score, fixed-alert-burden decision value and patient-clustered uncertainty. Use hospital-cluster bootstrap or a hierarchical hospital-aware interval; do not call the coefficient a causal effect.

### Scientifically substantive alternative

A compact one-layer GRU or temporal convolution consumes twelve one-hour bins before t. Each bin contains insulin-rate summaries, creatinine/glucose values and slopes, nutrition-delivery indicators, vitals/severity/vasopressor context, prior laboratory-testing density, interface indicators, explicit missingness and time-since-measurement channels. It has separate multi-task heads for Y24, O24 and competing discharge/death/EOL. The fit target is the same episode-level observed outcomes, with censor/competing-event handling fixed before training. For the same standardized joint/reference inputs, generate predicted detected-low risks and observation risks; use physiology-only and observation-only ablations.

Use a fixed whole-hospital 60/20/20 development/validation/transport split, keeping `uniquepid` together; if the number of eligible hospitals makes that partition impossible, predeclare the nearest valid hospital-stratified split and report it. Evaluate on exactly the B0/B1 episode set with Brier score, calibration slope/intercept, discrimination only as secondary, risk-contrast agreement, decision curves at a fixed alert burden, and hospital-wise transport. Uncertainty is obtained by resampling hospitals within development/transport evaluation and repeating learner seeds; report intervals and calibration dispersion, not only a point estimate. The alternative can reveal persistence, ordering and co-movement that one-window summaries lose, and can quantify whether apparent value lives in observation channels. It is not promoted to inferential primary status by predictive improvement alone.

Choose the transparent model for the primary estimand because it provides a traceable standardized contrast and is defensible under sparse events. Retain the temporal model as a prespecified scientific probe of the mechanism/observation representation; defer it if the support gate fails or it cannot converge/calibrate, while retaining the failure as evidence about feasibility rather than replacing the question.

## Support, falsification and interpretation

Before fitting the exposure interaction, require >=20 primary detected-low events overall, >=5 per exposure cell used for standardization, >=10 hospitals containing both events and non-events, observation-model positivity with stabilized-weight effective sample size >=100, and stable contrast sign under prespecified weight truncation. These are stability criteria, not clinical thresholds. The parent’s 9-event audit means this gate is uncertain and may fail; the solver must recompute it for the primary 24-hour endpoint and stop inferential interaction fitting if it fails.

Falsifications are: permute insulin index times within stay while preserving rates; shuffle renal/nutrition histories within hospital; use a pre-index placebo glucose window; repeat strict prior-delivery, complete-observable, direct-unit and no-carry-forward definitions; stratify laboratory versus bedside/interface glucose; run insulin-free analogous episodes; compare clinical versus revision timestamps; leave one hospital out with and without observation channels; and use newly observed non-glucose laboratory testing as a negative-control outcome. A meaningful signal should not be reproduced by placebo timing, shuffled exposures or the negative-control testing outcome.

Supportive evidence requires the gate, adequate observation positivity, a prespecified joint-versus-reference detected-low difference >=2 percentage points with uncertainty excluding zero, concordant six-hour direction, incremental calibration/alert value beyond B0, no single-hospital dominance, and failed falsification tests. This supports only prospective chart adjudication and monitoring research. Adverse evidence is a null below target, reversal, disappearance under strict labels/ascertainment adjustment, poor held-out-hospital calibration, one-interface/hospital confinement, or falsification/negative-control reproduction; it refutes this measurable marker in this snapshot, not insulin safety or nutrition practice. Inconclusive evidence is failed support, unstable weights, ambiguous labels, unsupported transport folds, nonconvergence, or competing-event miscalibration.

Unavailable evidence requiring clinical adjudication or another study includes actual insulin administration and dose received, delivered calories or true feed-stop time, rescue dextrose, clinician intent, symptoms/confirmed hypoglycemia, adjudicated KDIGO AKI, and benefit or harm of an alert/order. The public eICU release also lacks narrative notes, images and raw waveforms; structured note fields cannot supply those claims. The reserved catalog partition is inaccessible and is not external validation. No causal or bedside-treatment conclusion is permitted.
