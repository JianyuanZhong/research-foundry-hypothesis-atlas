# Episode 86 successor: a same-threshold certified and uncertainty-qualified HCC review reserve

## 1. Targeted repair and decision question

This is a child of [prior hypothesis] and [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the mature first-resection, documentary-M2, fixed-capacity HCC experiment and every inherited population, chronology, reader-book, queue, coverage, deletion, comparator, q_req, q_LS, q_M2 and q_dec contract.

The two parents add complementary safeguards. candidate-9f8ba5 adds q_LS_U05, an exact family-wise uncertainty reserve for loss of book-specific label-stable positives. candidate-eab693 adds patientwise source-content certification q_DOC and correctly fails closed on absent report lifecycle/version/view evidence. They do not answer one operational question: can one fixed coverage threshold support both safeguards simultaneously? Separate minima are not enough because the state registry and uncertainty pass are evaluated at every threshold, uncertainty pass is not assumed monotone, and a patientwise-certified queue can be full at a different threshold from the uncertainty-qualified queue.

The only new frontier is therefore q_JOINT, a same-threshold conjunction:

- the unchanged inherited PASS(q), q_LS conditions and q_LS_U05 exact reserve pass in every required state;
- every required P_LS patient has all eligible source-clean accessions exposed in that state;
- the non-EMPTY G queue contains K source-content-certified positions in every required state; and
- all of these conditions hold at the same q.

This is a new falsifiable decision question, not a new model or label. It asks whether the site has enough evidence to fund a prospective silent-mode archive/version capture and queue validation under fixed review capacity. It never authorizes deployment or changes care. q_JOINT is evaluated directly over R and must not be replaced by max(q_DOC,q_LS_U05): their pass sets can be disjoint.

I considered adding a new clinician semantic rule. It would be materially redundant with the locked R versus G/Gmask semantic-content comparisons and would require another unvalidated text interpretation. The joint reserve is the narrower missing decision safeguard.

## 2. Clinical importance, evidence, and hypothesis

A service with fewer review slots than eligible imaging content needs to know not only whether a documentary-positive patient can be retained, but whether the retained queue is source-complete at patient level and whether the observed loss estimate has a defensible uncertainty reserve. A high aggregate Q can coexist with one protected patient missing one accession. Conversely, zero observed losses can coexist with a wide exact upper bound when the protected set is small. Either failure matters before investing in an archive-linked prospective validation.

The endpoint is documentary only: a patient is positive when a frozen pathology reader book supports the prespecified encounter-documentary M2 terminal. It is not specimen-linked biological microvascular invasion, treatment response, clinician action, recurrence, survival, safety, utility or patient benefit.

The strongest claim supported before execution is structural. The read-only HCC catalog contains a linked set of ordinary CSV sources with encounter keys, procedure clocks, examination text/metadata and whole-examination accessions, same-encounter pathology text, and dated medication/order fields. The inherited full scan recorded 105,044 encounter rows, 338,040 procedure rows, 419,996 examination rows, 46,395 pathology rows, 4,097,517 medication rows and 16,730,319 order rows, including 392,854 nonblank whole-accession keys, 16,534 multirow accessions and 8,932 encounters with multiple pathology rows. These are structural feasibility observations, not evidence of eligibility, semantic validity, biological MVI or actual report availability.

The research-ambition README was read. I also inspected the accessible article passages for the natural-history demonstration and the Bayesian EHR demonstration, which use patient-disjoint/temporal validation and explicitly distinguish retrospective discovery from clinically timed prediction. I inspected only the cancer supplement's cohort-flowchart material; the README says the cancer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable, so no claim about them is made.

The primary hypothesis is:

> Among adults undergoing a first eligible source-documented hepatobiliary resection in the fixed 2015–2021 HCC cohort, there exists a rational stored-content coverage threshold q<1 at which a fixed-capacity incomplete-content queue simultaneously retains every required book-specific label-stable documentary-M2-positive patient, has a simultaneous exact 95% upper loss bound no greater than 0.05 under a declared conditional iid/exchangeability target, and has K patientwise source-content-certified queue positions in every required 2020/2021 state.

The finite-corpus component is exact and does not require a population generalization. The uncertainty component is a conditional target; mere census language does not justify a binomial probability claim. A fixed-corpus report without an accepted sampling/conditional iid target is descriptive and operationally inconclusive.

The unresolved claim is this same-threshold conjunction. Existing evidence does not establish it, and neither a favorable q_LS nor a favorable q_DOC alone establishes it.

## 5. Unchanged documentary frontiers

For fixed nonpathology state a, let B_path be the three pathology books and define:

U_LS(a)={i: Y_i(a,b')=1 for every b' in B_path}.

Retain the diagnostic:
P_common(a)=intersection over b' of {i: i in S_max(a,b') and Y_i(a,b')=1}.

For current pathology book b retain exactly:
P_LS(a,b)=S_max(a,b) intersect U_LS(a),
P_M2(a,b)={i: i in S_max(a,b) and Y_i(a,b)=1}.

Thus P_common subseteq P_LS subseteq P_M2 subseteq S_max. Define J_common, J_LS, J_M2 and J_dec exactly as in the parents. Retain:

- q_req, the minimum q in R with inherited PASS(q);
- q_LS, the minimum q with inherited PASS(q), nonempty required P_LS sets and J_LS=1 in every eligible 2020/2021 state with Q>=q;
- q_M2, the unchanged all-current-book documentary-M2 retention frontier; and
- q_dec, the unchanged frontier requiring S_G=S_max in every eligible state.

Undefined or equal-to-one frontiers fail their claims. Preserve q_req <= q_common <= q_LS <= q_M2 <= q_dec whenever the corresponding nonvacuity conditions hold. An empty P_common is diagnostic-only and cannot veto nonempty P_LS.

## 6. Preserved uncertainty reserve

At every q and eligible state c=(a,b), set
m_c(q)=|P_LS(c)| and x_c(q)=|P_LS(c) minus S_G(c,q)|.

Let C(q) be all required eligible state cells, including both test years and every permitted source/onset/reader/pathology/deletion state. Freeze M=|R|*max_q |C(q)| after the state registry is frozen and use alpha_cell=.05/M. Compute the exact one-sided Clopper–Pearson upper bound
U_c(q)=Beta-inverse(1-alpha_cell; x_c+1, m_c-x_c).
For x=0 use 1-alpha_cell^(1/m); for x=m use 1. Store integer x,m, decimal U, library and version. Do not use normal approximations.

Retain q_LS_U05 exactly:
U_PASS(q)=1 iff inherited PASS(q), all required P_LS are nonempty, and U_c(q)<=delta=.05 for every required c in C(q);
q_LS_U05=min{q in R: U_PASS(q)=1}.

Evaluate every q directly; do not assume monotonicity. Preserve lower-boundary witnesses and exact finite-corpus counts. The numerical reserve is not changed by this child.

For the inferential statement, the verifier must require a declared conditional iid Bernoulli loss/sampling target (or an explicitly documented design that implies the binomial model). Exchangeability language alone is insufficient to establish independent-binomial coverage. If the HCC snapshot is treated only as a fixed census, U_c is descriptive and q_LS_U05 cannot support a future-episode probability claim; classify that operational uncertainty interpretation as inconclusive.

## 7. Preserved patientwise source certification

For every state s, q and patient i define:
A_i(s,q)=1{every eligible nonblank accession of i has M=1 at q}.

A patient with no eligible accession is not assigned A_i=1 and is excluded by n_A feasibility. Any blank-ID, contradictory or incomplete accession makes A_i=0. This is source-content completeness, not report finalization or clinician access.

Retain:
J_DOC(s,q)=1[P_LS(a,b) subseteq {i:A_i(s,q)=1}] AND J_LS(s,q).

For every nonvacuous eligible state, DOC_PASS(q)=1 only when inherited PASS(q), all required P_LS are nonempty, J_LS=1, J_DOC=1 and
K_cert(s,q)=|S_G(s,q) intersect {i:A_i(s,q)=1}|=K
in every required 2020/2021 state with Q(s,q)>=q. Retain q_DOC=min{q in R:DOC_PASS(q)=1}. q_DOC is subordinate and cannot be described as report availability.

At every q and lower boundary emit Q,n_A,N,e,K, exposed-accession counts, |P_LS|, certified protected count, protected uncovered count, hashes of P_LS/certified/uncovered sets, each protected patient's accession and exposure counts, queue rank/slot and EMPTY status, S_max membership, cross-book vectors, q_LS, q_DOC, score/model/queue hashes and deletion results.

## 8. The new same-threshold frontier

Define:
JOINT_PASS(q)=1 iff, for every required state cell at q,

1. all inherited computational, feasibility, comparator, PASS(q), nonvacuity, q_LS and q_M2/q_dec prerequisites required by the inherited interpretation pass;
2. U_c(q)<=.05 under the declared conditional iid/sampling target;
3. every protected P_LS patient has A_i=1; and
4. K_cert(s,q)=K with no backfill or substitution.

Equivalently, compute the conjunction of the unchanged U_PASS(q) and DOC_PASS(q), but evaluate it as a single same-q state registry and retain both component ledgers. Define:
q_JOINT=min{q in R:JOINT_PASS(q)=1}.

Do not infer q_JOINT=max(q_LS_U05,q_DOC). The pass sets can be nonmonotone or disjoint. A q_JOINT<1 is a supportive result for the bounded prospective-validation decision only when all inherited q frontiers and exact witnesses pass. q_JOINT=1 or undefined falsifies the combined reserve claim; it does not negate a favorable individual q_LS_U05 or q_DOC. Report the first failing component and the immediate lower-threshold witness.

The prospective bridge remains conditional. A future archive-linked replication would require, for each whole accession, an immutable report/version key, predecessor/amendment/retraction lineage, authored/final/release/ingestion/retrieval/parse-readable/view timestamps with timezone provenance, exact as-of-t_dec payload hashes, complete nonresponder accounting and patient/year bounds showing the unchanged N,n_A frame was not selectively lost. No such evidence is imported into current HCC computation.

7. uncertainty_reserve_inconclusive: q_LS<1 but no accepted conditional iid/sampling target exists, or q_LS_U05 cannot be defined for nonvacuity;
8. uncertainty_reserve_adverse: the conditional target is accepted but q_LS_U05 is undefined or equals 1;

13. full_documentary_queue_supportive_bridge_pending: q_JOINT<1, all inherited exact minimality/boundary witnesses pass, K_cert=K in every required state, and no preceding label applies.

Supportive means only a finite HCC stored-content queue passed the stated conditional reserve and source-content certification at one common threshold. Adverse means a specified documentary or reserve claim failed in at least one required state. Inconclusive means provenance, feasibility, nonvacuity or the inferential target prevented the claim. Append operational_metadata_unavailable to every current HCC result. No result may be called operationally available, biologically MVI-valid, clinician-action validated, causal, beneficial, safe, fair or deployable.

A q_JOINT failure with individually favorable q_DOC and q_LS_U05 is a substantive adverse result for the combined prospective-validation decision, not a computational failure. It does not erase either component's finite-corpus result. Twelve/48/72-hour, rho, pooled-year, average-book, favorable-state and favorable-deletion analyses cannot rescue a failed required state. Always emit the diagnostic parent_common_intersection_optimistic when q_common<1 but q_LS is 1/undefined or q_LS>q_common.
