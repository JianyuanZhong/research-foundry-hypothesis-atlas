## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Multimorbidity instruments (comorbidity counts, multimorbidity indices, CKM stage) render burden as a set/count; clinicians reason about sequence (diabetes-then-hypertension vs hypertension-then-diabetes). Whether within-person first-diagnosis ORDER of a co-present pair carries information about downstream diagnosis accrual **beyond co-presence** is unresolved, and the strongest rival (B1) is that any apparent ORDER signal is care-intensity / ascertainment coupling (patients coded for one chronic disease are coded for others) rather than disease biology.

## Substantive advance over parent (the parent's explicitly named unfinished item)
The parent pre-registered a documentation-breadth proxy but measured it **contemporaneously** with the index diagnoses, leaving it on the causal path and unable to separate B1. This child:
1. Redefines the proxy as **temporally ANTECEDENT**: distinct 3-char ICD-10 blocks with a `s_41280` date strictly **before** the earlier of the two index diagnoses. An antecedent proxy cannot be caused by the ORDER itself, so it can act as a genuine separator between claim A (biology) and rival B1 (care coupling).
2. Pre-registers a **breadth-stratified Δ_order contrast** and a **generic-accrued-record-volume NEGATIVE CONTROL** outcome.
3. Adds a computed feasibility diagnostic (below) showing a material, previously unquantified limitation.

## Pre-registered estimand and falsification (design; solver computes)
- Population: UKB participants with dated E11 and I10 records in `s_41270_0_*` (≥1 each, distinct non-tie first dates). Sex `n_31_0_0`, age `n_21003_0_0`. Disjoint `eid`/`n_eid` namespaces are NOT joined.
- Outcome: downstream disease-specific diagnosis accrual after the later index diagnosis.
- Estimand: Δ_order = held-out ΔAUC / proper-score of {set/count of co-coded blocks + age + sex + interval + co-coding intensity} vs same model + ORDER. Permutation null; tie exclusion; ≥365-day subgroup; E11/E78 control pair.
- Breadth-stratified contrast: Δ_order within antecedent-breadth tertiles, with the empty-window stratum reported separately.
- Negative control: generic accrued record volume (not disease-specific).
- **Supportive:** Δ_order>0 beyond permutation null AND Δ_order for the disease-specific outcome exceeds Δ_order for the negative control by a pre-set margin.
- **Adverse (favours B1):** Δ_order predicts the negative control as strongly as the disease-specific outcome.
- **Inconclusive:** Δ_order CI includes 0, or the empty-window stratum dominates power.

## Honest limits (unchanged from parent)
No draw/death/censoring dates → no causal claim; 3-char ICD-10 blocks unadjudicated (need clinical code-list adjudication); ~40% same-day ties limit power; documentation breadth is an imperfect, possibly disease-driven care-intensity proxy; inpatient/records-based ascertainment.

## Key works (inspected; abstract-only) and claim mapping
Exactly three distinct inspected works are highlighted; receipts and hashed snapshots in `work/lead-ep32/key-references.json` and `work/lead-ep32/refs/`.
- **[K1]** Stow D, et al. *PLoS Medicine* 2026;23(7):e1004844 (doi:10.1371/journal.pmed.1004844). Supports that ORDER is not inert: in a UK South Asian cohort the cardiometabolic->internalising trajectory direction carried the highest 10-year cardiovascular/renal risk. Limitation: different cohort, multistate primary/secondary-care design; does not test pairwise order against a co-presence/count baseline nor an ascertainment control. This proposal tests whether pairwise ORDER adds out-of-sample information once the pair is held fixed, and whether any gain is separable from an ANTECEDENT care-intensity proxy and a negative control.
- **[K2]** Huang D, et al. *BMC Geriatrics* 2026;26(1):1104 (doi:10.1186/s12877-026-07765-4). Bounds the strongest structural rival (B3, hub-centrality/co-occurrence): diabetes and hypertension are the highest-betweenness nodes in an indicator co-occurrence network, so pairwise structure may reflect hub prominence rather than sequence. This proposal holds the pair fixed and asks whether ORDER adds beyond the set/count representation. Limitation: health-examination indicators, n=4,195, cross-sectional, no order/prognosis.
- **[K3]** Deng P, et al. *Acta Dermato-Venereologica* 2026;106:adv-2025-0181 (doi:10.2340/actadv.v106.adv-2025-0181). Bounds the shared-axis rival (B2, cumulative nonspecific burden): proteomics prioritised nonspecific inflammatory proteins (HLA-DRA, MMP12, TNFRSF14, VCAM1) shared across conditions. This proposal asks whether a pairwise ORDER feature survives adjustment for coded burden, antecedent documentation breadth and same-encounter co-coding. Limitation: atopic-dermatitis/psychiatric pair, cross-sectional, no diagnosis order.
