clinical_question_and_importance: >-
  Among UK Biobank repeat-assessment participants aged 40–75 without prior malignant cancer,
  do annualized falls in haemoglobin and MCV and rise in platelet count between assessment
  instances 0 and 1 add 1–5-year colorectal-cancer (CRC) prognostic information beyond age,
  sex, and those markers' current values? Anaemia-like CBC deterioration can precede CRC, but
  acting on nonspecific changes could also generate burdensome investigation. Establishing whether
  the signal survives sparse protocolized measurement and is CRC-specific would determine whether
  prospective clinical validation is justified. This observational study does not estimate benefit
  from referral, FIT, colonoscopy, screening, stage shift, or treatment.

supported_vs_untested:
  strongest_evidence_supported_claim: >-
    The inspected full text of BLOODTRACC (Virdee et al., BMC Cancer 2026; PMCID PMC13295383;
    DOI 10.1186/s12885-026-16179-9) externally validated sex-specific models using age plus up to
    five years of clinically ordered haemoglobin, MCV, and platelet histories for two-year CRC
    prediction in 2,956,977 men (12,578 CRCs) and 3,561,349 women (11,939 CRCs), with reported
    c-statistics 0.73 and 0.74 and calibration slopes 0.92 and 0.95. Thus longitudinal FBC
    prediction in indication-driven primary-care records is already supported. The inspected
    BLOTTED protocol (PMCID PMC9830700) motivates investigation selection but does not establish
    implementation benefit.
  unresolved_testable_claim: >-
    In sparse, protocolized UKB repeats, the three prespecified annualized changes have incremental
    association and prediction beyond the current marker values over days 366–1825, and any signal
    is stronger or more coherent for CRC than for incident non-CRC malignant cancer.
  not_identifiable_here: >-
    Two protocolized observations cannot isolate test indication, visit density, occult bleeding,
    symptoms, or iron deficiency, and cannot prove why BLOODTRACC transports or fails to transport.

predictors_and_comparators:
  annualized_changes: >-
    For marker m, Δm/year = (m at instance 1 − m at instance 0) / ((53-1.0 − 53-0.0)/365.2425).
    Prespecified adverse directions are falling haemoglobin, falling MCV, and rising platelet count.
    Retain each as a separate continuous term with one degree of freedom after training-fold-only
    standardization. Do not average them or choose among them by observed performance.
  baseline_model: >-
    Landmark age, sex, and current instance-1 haemoglobin, MCV, and platelet count, using linear
    terms in the event-constrained primary model. Nonlinearity may be shown descriptively but is not
    added unless effective sample size permits a predeclared sensitivity analysis.
  trajectory_model: "Baseline model plus the three continuous direction-coded annualized changes."
  secondary_direction_concordant_pattern: >-
    Report four mutually exclusive patterns: no adverse-direction changes, haemoglobin-only/MCV-only/
    platelet-only or other one-marker change, any two adverse-direction changes, and all three adverse-
    direction changes. Also report an ordinal count from zero to three. Define direction solely by sign
    after confirming field orientation, not an arbitrary magnitude or clinical threshold. Call this an
    iron-deficiency-like or direction-concordant CBC pattern only; without ferritin, transferrin saturation,
    symptoms, bleeding source, and adjudication it is not diagnosed iron deficiency.
  exploratory_rdw: >-
    Add current RDW and annualized RDW rise only as a separately reported exploratory model, never as
    a performance-selected primary component.

analysis:
  descriptive: >-
    Report exact cohort flow, missingness, marker distributions and elapsed time; CRC, competing-death,
    non-CRC cancer and post-death-inconsistency counts; and Aalen–Johansen CRC cumulative incidence.
    Compare included repeat attenders with instance-0 participants lacking an instance-1 visit to
    characterize selection without claiming it is corrected.
  association: >-
    Fit a ridge-penalized cause-specific Cox model from day 366 with the fixed six predictor terms,
    reporting direction-coded HRs per training-set SD, a single three-degree-of-freedom incremental
    test, proportional-hazards checks, and bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. Use Fine–Gray estimates
    only as sensitivity evidence. If software-validated Firth Cox is available, use it as a sensitivity
    analysis rather than changing the estimand.
  prediction: >-
    Compare baseline and trajectory models under identical repeated stratified cross-validation or
    bootstrap optimism correction. Put every standardization and penalty choice inside training data;
    split by participant. Report paired changes with uncertainty in Uno C-index, five-year time-dependent
    AUC, integrated Brier score, calibration-in-the-large and calibration slope. Report sensitivity,
    specificity, PPV and event yield at fixed top 1%, 2% and 5% capacity solely as descriptive triage
    characteristics, not referral thresholds or clinical utility.
  event_count_rule: >-
    With 81 feasibility events, keep the fixed parsimonious models and penalization. If final coverage-
    valid eligible events or resample event allocation are insufficient for stable estimates, report
    descriptive cumulative incidence and the simplest penalized association only. Do not escalate to
    flexible models, select markers, or suppress uncertainty.
  uncertainty: >-
    Bootstrap participants, not rows; use confidence intervals for all contrasts and show fold/bootstrap
    distributions. Treat calibration estimates as low precision at this event count.

falsification_and_interpretation:
  pipeline_checks:
    - >-
      Permute outcome labels within sex and 5-year age bands and rerun the full resampling pipeline;
      non-null incremental performance suggests leakage or implementation error.
    - >-
      Apply the fitted construction to CRC dated before the landmark as an intentionally impossible
      temporal prediction task; apparent prospective incremental performance suggests cohort leakage.
    - "Audit exact suffix pairing and exclude post-death diagnoses from observable events."
    - "Do not reverse visit order: it deterministically changes slope signs and is not a valid negative control."
  supportive_result: >-
    Evidence is supportive if the jointly estimated change effects are coherent with the three
    prespecified directions and the trajectory-versus-current-value contrast consistently improves
    held-out discrimination or event concentration without evidence of degraded calibration or Brier
    performance, with uncertainty sufficiently narrow to distinguish the finding from no meaningful
    incremental information. A substantially weaker non-CRC cancer association strengthens a CRC-
    specific interpretation. No single p-value, hazard ratio, AUC increment, or cutoff defines truth.
  adverse_result: >-
    Evidence is adverse if estimates are precise enough to rule out a clinically consequential
    incremental signal, point coherently opposite to the hypothesis, or show worse held-out calibration/
    error, especially if any association is equally or more evident for non-CRC cancer. This argues
    against prioritizing this sparse trajectory for CRC triage; it does not refute BLOODTRACC in
    clinically ordered primary-care data.
  inconclusive_result: >-
    Wide intervals compatible with both consequential benefit and no benefit/harm, unstable resampling,
    too few coverage-valid events, unresolved field semantics, unreliable coverage, or discordant metrics
    are inconclusive. Report what is estimable and stop rather than adding flexibility.
  strongest_possible_claim: >-
    Support would establish only incremental prognostic information in selected UKB repeat attenders
    under protocolized sparse measurement. Adverse evidence would establish lack of useful transport in
    this setting only. Neither establishes mechanism or a clinical action strategy.
