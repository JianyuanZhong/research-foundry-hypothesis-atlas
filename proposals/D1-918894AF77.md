# Episode 87 successor: does repeat-status recalibration remain valid for the patients occupying a fixed-capacity queue?

## Unresolved clinical question and substantive advance

A receiving ICU may have a fixed number of review slots. It therefore needs two separable properties from a transported repeat-lactate score: (1) a ranking that fills those slots with patients at the intended priority, and (2) a probability display that is honest for the patients actually placed in the queue. The available EICU evidence and the two parent designs do not resolve whether a local recalibration remedy that looks acceptable in the whole target population remains valid in the operational queue, especially when repeat-lactate availability is an observation-process state.

The substantive advance is a prespecified, F_available-specific intercept recalibration evaluated on the fixed-capacity queue selected by the unchanged source-trained R_full ranking. It tests the clinically actionable contract “use the source score to rank, and use a locally validated probability display within each F_available state” across both directed hospital transfers and every fixed-capacity level. This goes beyond whole-population threshold-cell calibration: a remedy can pass calibration-in-the-large while being wrong among the patients actually reviewed, or it can improve displayed risk while leaving ranking unchanged.

The strongest available evidence supports only the narrower claims that repeat lactate may add prognostic information in selected measured cohorts and that EHR observation processes can be informative. The inspected demonstration papers also show the importance of external validation and calibration in longitudinal EHR prediction, but neither establishes this EICU transport question. No cited evidence establishes that repeat-lactate availability means a clinician ordered, received, viewed, or acted on a result. The unavailable cancer demonstration main article and full STAR Methods were not inspected; only its metadata and supplement are available according to the research-ambition README.

This remains a prognostic validation experiment. It does not estimate a treatment effect, the effect of ordering repeat lactate, or the effect of operating a review queue.

## Primary hypothesis and single primary estimand

The primary remedy is a target-label, hospital-held-out, F_available-specific intercept shift applied to the frozen source-trained R_full linear predictor. For target person i in hospital h with status s_i=F_available_i, define

p_state,i = expit(eta_full,i + delta_{h,s_i}).

The queue is formed only from the raw eta_full score, never from p_state. Thus the remedy is a probability-display remedy and cannot change fixed-capacity ranking or membership.

For direction d (low-repeat source to high-repeat target, or high-repeat source to low-repeat target), hospital h in the target stratum, capacity c in C={0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20}, let k_h(c)=ceil(c n_h), and Q_full,h(c) be the top k_h(c) target people by eta_full, breaking exact score ties by patientunitstayid. Define the status-specific queue calibration error

e_Q(d,s,c) =
  mean over i in union_h Q_full,h(c), F_available_i=s of (D7_i - p_state,i).

The one primary estimand is the worst absolute queue calibration error

Q_state = max over d in {low->high, high->low}, s in {0,1}, c in C of |e_Q(d,s,c)|.

The primary falsifiable claim is that the state-specific recalibration is operationally valid: Q_state <= 0.05. Support requires the upper endpoint of the two-sided, copied-hospital, joint 95% percentile interval for Q_state to be <=0.05, with every required status-by-direction-by-capacity cell defined and at least 1,900 of 2,000 joint replicates finite. Adverse evidence is a lower endpoint >0.05. Any other computable result is inconclusive. A point estimate below 0.05 is not support.

The primary estimand is not changed to a favorable capacity, direction, or availability state. The capacity grid is fixed in advance. If a required cell has fewer than 30 selected people in the original data, report it and classify the primary remedy as inconclusive for precision/feasibility; do not pool statuses or silently drop that cell.

Secondary remedy comparison: fit a pooled intercept shift delta_h using all F_available states, with the same held-out-hospital rule, and report Q_global over the same queue cells. Report I_Q=Q_global-Q_state with a joint interval, but do not use I_Q to rescue a state-specific failure or to select a remedy after seeing outcomes. Also report raw source-score queue calibration using p_source=expit(eta_full). These baselines distinguish absolute transport failure, improvement from local labels, and the incremental value of conditioning the remedy on repeat availability.

## Held-out recalibration remedy

For each direction and each target hospital h:

- Global comparator: solve for delta_h,global using all target people outside h:
  sum over i not in h of (D7_i-expit(eta_full,i+delta_h,global))-delta_h,global=0.
- Primary state-specific remedy: separately for s=0 and s=1, solve
  sum over i not in h,F_available=s of (D7_i-expit(eta_full,i+delta_h,s))-delta_h,s=0.

Use deterministic bisection on [-20,20], tolerance 1e-12. The -delta penalty makes each root unique under separation. An empty status-specific calibration set, nonfinite root, or root outside the bracket is noncomputable; never pool statuses to repair a missing state. For held-out hospital h, compute p_state for its patients using delta_h,F_available; no D7 label from h enters its offset. This is historical target-label validation, not an unsupervised prospective deployment procedure. The target labels never alter eta, ranks, queue membership, or fitted model coefficients.

The state-specific remedy is a calibration/display intervention only. If a future implementation instead ranks on p_state, that is a different experiment and is not licensed by this proposal.

Run 2,000 copied-hospital refit bootstrap replicates with seed 20260973. Independently sample source and target hospitals with replacement within the frozen low/high practice strata. In each replicate:

1. Refit source-only preprocessing and R_full/R_process.
2. Recompute target eta scores.
3. Recompute global and status-specific held-out-hospital offsets.
4. Rebuild raw-score Q_full and Q_process at every c.
5. Recompute all primary e_Q cells, Q_state, Q_global, Q_source, I_Q, and inherited queue functionals.

A copied version of a held-out original hospital is excluded from its offset calibration set, so no target label from that original hospital or any copy of it can leak into its predictions. A replicate is finite only if all required fits, scores, roots, queue cells and functionals are finite and all primary cells have at least 30 people. Require at least 1,900 finite joint replicates; report finite counts and two-sided percentile intervals from the same replicate records. Q_state is the replicate-level maximum over both directions, both F states and all seven capacities, never a maximum of separately reported marginal interval endpoints.

Run fixed-design leave-one-original-hospital-out deletion using the frozen original-hospital scores and capacities. Recompute Q_state, Q_global, I_Q and queue functionals. Report whether support depends on one hospital; if more than 30% of represented deletions violate the 0.05 Q_state screen, classify support as inconclusive even if the full-data interval passes. This deletion rule is a stability diagnostic, not a substitute for the joint confidence interval.

Retain the parent’s mixed-stratum and source-only sensitivity analyses as secondary. They cannot rescue a failed directed primary transfer and a failed sensitivity cannot negate the directed result.

Otherwise classify the primary remedy independently:

- Supportive: Q_state upper 95% joint bound <=0.05, all primary cells pass n>=30, finite-replicate and deletion requirements pass.
- Adverse: Q_state lower 95% joint bound >0.05, identifying the observed worst cells without claiming every cell fails.
- Inconclusive: the interval crosses 0.05, cell precision is insufficient, deletion instability triggers, or any other computable support gate fails without an adverse bound.

Report the secondary comparison separately: state-specific improvement over the global remedy is supported only if the lower joint bound for I_Q is >=0.02; it is adverse if the upper bound is <0.02; otherwise inconclusive. This comparison cannot change the primary state-validity state. If the unmodified source score already passes, state that recalibration was unnecessary; do not convert that into evidence that local labels are beneficial.

The ranking result is separate. A supportive Q_state does not support R_full queue superiority. A supportive tau7 result does not support probability-display validity. Intercept recalibration is expected to preserve raw-score queue membership exactly; any changed queue indicates an implementation error or an unauthorized ranking rule.

## Interpretation and unavailable evidence

Supportive Q_state means only that a historically target-label-trained, hospital-held-out, F_available-specific intercept display met the prespecified mean-risk screen among the selected R_full queue patients in these EICU hospitals. It does not establish individual calibration, calibration slope, assay or specimen validity, true norepinephrine administration or indication, whether lactate was ordered/collected/released/visible/reviewed by L24, clinical review, staffing, actual fixed capacity, alert burden, actionability, utility, fairness, safety, preventability, causal treatment benefit, external or temporal transport, or patient benefit.

Adverse Q_state means the proposed minimal recalibration is not adequate for the specified queue-relevant probability screen; it does not refute lactate prognosis and does not prove another remedy cannot work. Inconclusive means the deployment question remains unresolved. A rank-supportive but Q_state-adverse result permits at most a research claim about ordering under the tested data; it does not justify displaying probabilities or using a probability threshold. A Q_state-supportive but rank-adverse result permits a calibration statement only and does not justify queue deployment.

The EICU snapshot lacks calendar time for temporal validation, post-discharge deaths, adjudicated medication administration, laboratory order/specimen/result lineage, clinician viewing or action logs, prospective workload/capacity, stakeholder utility, and causal treatment outcomes. HCC, MIMIC and UKB remain configured and accessible but are not pooled or called external validation because this revision-aware EICU estimand has no validated harmonization in the catalog. Required next evidence is laboratory and medication adjudication, prospective workflow/capacity measurement, independent temporal and external-site silent validation of the selected remedy, subgroup/fairness review, and only then an impact study or randomized evaluation.
