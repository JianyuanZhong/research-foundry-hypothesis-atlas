# Observed renal-recovery discordance at a fixed 72-hour ICU landmark — episode 7

## Scientific deliverable

Newly derive and freeze a one-row-per-hospitalization cohort from the read-only MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot, then estimate whether an explicitly observed discordance between creatinine recovery and Foley-recorded urine-output recovery is associated with subsequent mortality. Completion requires:

1. a provenance manifest, cohort-flow counts, exact item/label/unit support, duplicate and storage-time audit, coverage/missingness by phenotype cell, and post-landmark event counts;
2. an unadjusted and pre-recovery-adjusted standardized mortality contrast with 95% uncertainty and a cause-specific time-to-event sensitivity;
3. two newly fitted models on the same subject-held-out population and split: a transparent ridge-logistic baseline without urine/fluid channels and a nonlinear six-hour temporal tree model, plus urine/fluid ablation;
4. held-out AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and subject-bootstrap uncertainty, with grouped feature-family contributions;
5. prespecified threshold, creatinine-source, chart-time versus storage-available, coverage-density, weight/unweighted, and pre-landmark-RRT sensitivities, plus within-stay time-permutation and care-process falsification;
6. a conclusion labeled supportive, adverse, or inconclusive and linked to computed outputs.

No cohort count, effect, model metric, or clinical conclusion is asserted here.

## Evidence-supported claim and unresolved hypothesis

The inspected kidney-function reference supports only a measurement concern: in a small selected critical-illness study, creatinine- and cystatin-C-based assessments could be discordant near ICU discharge. It does not establish that an observed creatinine/Foley discordance is prevalent, prognostic, mechanistically renal, or actionable in this MIMIC snapshot. Cystatin C, measured GFR, muscle mass, adjudicated volume status and clinician adjudication are unavailable.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults alive at exactly 72 hours in the earliest valid ICU stay of a hospitalization, with an observed early creatinine rise, apparent creatinine recovery, documented early oliguria, dense paired Foley observation and storage-available weight, creatinine recovery without late Foley-output recovery has higher subsequent same-admission mortality than concordant creatinine and Foley recovery.

This is a noncausal association between observed chart phenotypes. It does not test whether creatinine recovery is harmful, whether oliguria is the mechanism, or whether treatment should change.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The simple baseline is ridge logistic regression for post-`t0` death using age, sex, first-care-unit/context, creatinine summaries/slopes, pre-48-hour physiology and treatment indicators, CKD/ESRD proxy, measurement counts and missingness, but no urine/output/fluid channels. Standardize using training data only.

The substantive alternative is a CPU-fitted HistGradientBoostingClassifier or equivalent nonlinear temporal tree on the identical population, outcome, `t0`, subject-level 60/20/20 train/validation/test split and predictor boundary. Build six-hour bins over `[0,72)` for creatinine, Foley output, weight-standardized output, input-minus-output, MAP, heart rate, respiratory rate, SpO2, lactate, vasoactive exposure, ventilation and RRT, retaining last/mean/min/max, slope/variation where identifiable, count and missingness. Tune only on grouped validation, evaluate once on the held-out test set, and fit an ablation removing urine/fluid features.

The alternative is scientifically useful because the baseline compresses time into slopes and totals: the temporal model can test whether persistence, timing and interactions distinguish discordant recovery from a measurement-density artifact, and whether urine/fluid trajectories add calibrated information beyond creatinine. A small predictive gain alone is not evidence for renal mechanism or clinical utility. A sequence transformer or latent GP model is deferred: it adds assumptions and compute without resolving the central missing-GFR/observation-process question; revisit only if support is strong and a distinct temporal scientific claim is justified. Approximate budget is <=90 minutes, 2 CPUs, 8 GiB RAM, no GPU; no mandatory GPU or expensive pilot is needed.

## Gates, falsification and interpretation

Before fitting, independently reproduce the support audit from prior support `mimic_renal_support_audit_episode6.py` and write a new audit manifest. Hard inconclusive/no-go conditions are unavailable member/schema, failed joins or timestamp containment, either primary cell <50, fewer than 10 deaths in either cell, no storage-available weight, inability to distinguish missing output from zero, or unavailable follow-up ascertainment. Model results are inconclusive if train/test lack 20 events and 20 non-events, calibration cannot be estimated, or bootstrap uncertainty is unstable.

Falsification: permute phenotype labels within eligible subjects; permute event times within stay; compare chart-time-only with storage-available analyses; use each creatinine source separately; stratify coverage density; fit output/chart-density-only predictors; use unweighted and alternative-threshold analyses; and remove pre-landmark RRT. A strong contrast reproduced by density/source indicators alone is adverse to the renal interpretation.

Supportive means higher discordant mortality with a 95% interval excluding the null, persistence across source/storage/coverage/threshold checks, no process-only explanation, and incremental urine/fluid information with calibrated held-out performance. Adverse means near-null/reversed contrast, loss under dense or storage-available checks, or a process-only explanation; nonlinear performance alone cannot rescue it. Inconclusive means failed support/event/calibration gates, extreme differential observability, threshold instability, leakage, or irreproducible computation.

Computation can establish support, joins, timing, observed phenotype prevalence, fitted associations, prediction metrics, uncertainty and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish true GFR, complete urine capture, mechanism, causality, treatment benefit, bedside thresholds, clinical utility or external validity. Those require cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle and volume assessment, indication data, clinical adjudication and prospective/external validation.
