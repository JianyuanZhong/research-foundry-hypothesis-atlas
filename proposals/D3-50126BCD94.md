# Provisional hypothesis: donor-aware macrophage-to-FLS communication in E-MTAB-8873

## Proposed relationship

In the available E-MTAB-8873 synovial coculture experiment, exposure of synovial fibroblast-like cells (FLS) to macrophages may induce a reproducible, condition-specific transcriptional response that is distinguishable from FLS-only and macrophage-negative controls after accounting for donor and repeated context. This is a hypothesis, not a computed result.

## Concrete opening and evidence status

The dataset guide identifies E-MTAB-8873 as a targeted 445-feature assay with 33 condition samples, including FLS-only, macrophage-positive, macrophage-negative, pre-LPS, and inhibitor-associated conditions, with repeated context measurements and reused or contributing donors. The exact source is the prepared count matrix and design JSON listed in datasets/disease_mechanisms/README.md. The guide warns that pseudobulk does not make samples independent, that the panel is targeted, and that ordinary donor t-tests are disabled.

No numerical result, donor mapping, feature identity check, or biological conclusion has been computed in this draft. The closest prior work and seed question motivate directional cell communication, but do not establish this assay's response or direct ligand-receptor causality.

## Unresolved claim and falsifiable test

Test whether the macrophage-positive versus FLS-only contrast, and the macrophage-positive versus macrophage-negative contrast, show reproducible condition-specific changes in the targeted expression features after donor-aware and repeated-measurement handling. The primary comparison is within-donor where the design permits it; otherwise, the analysis must explicitly treat donor as a limitation rather than pretending samples are independent.

A supportive result would be a reproducible contrast with uncertainty that excludes a prespecified null and is not explained by donor, batch, or condition composition alone. An adverse result would be no reproducible contrast, or a contrast that disappears under donor-aware controls, weakening the directional communication interpretation. An inconclusive result would arise from insufficient independent donors, missing metadata, unstable feature mappings, or wide uncertainty; it would not be interpreted as evidence for or against the mechanism.

## Method comparison and scientific deliverable

A simple baseline is a donor-aware, condition-specific contrast with permutation or bootstrap uncertainty over the available independent donor/context units. A substantive alternative is a hierarchical or mechanistic model that estimates donor-specific effects and condition interactions while preserving the same contrasts and uncertainty target. The alternative could reveal whether an apparent response is a stable within-donor pattern or driven by a few donors/conditions; the baseline loses that decomposition. Neither method has been fitted here.

The actual future deliverable is a newly fitted donor-aware contrast estimate with uncertainty, condition/feature-level outputs, a donor-composition sensitivity analysis, and a prespecified supportive/adverse/inconclusive interpretation. No result is claimed in this provisional draft.
