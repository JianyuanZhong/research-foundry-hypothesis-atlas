# Target-cell actionability and clinical-anchoring repair with independent evidence panels

## Purpose, parentage, and what remains unproved

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It addresses the remaining decision-validity gap: an exact atom in a canonical discharge summary is still only stored text. The current protocol can establish reproducible target-linked documentation, but cannot tell whether the recommendation remained active at discharge, was explicitly superseded, referred to the intended finding, was clinically appropriate, was seen by anyone, or led to care.

The child preserves the assigned frozen schema and does not replace or pool its estimand. It preserves:

- the first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity pulmonary denominator;
- reciprocal pre-discharge radiology report/addendum chronology and `D = admissions.dischtime`;
- one canonical pre-D discharge summary;
- exact target atom/route and all-unit adverse precedence;
- label-independent target and matched-control selection;
- positive-probability two-phase whole-admission sampling, finite-population HT/Hájek inference, nested design uncertainty, and the <=900 phase-1 master;
- the fixed 120--300 distinct-admission and 84,000 charged-minute/1,400 reviewer-hour ceilings, including the single 15% reserve; and
- the strict retrospective ceiling: at most a stored-text/actionability-anchoring result that can nominate a separately governed prospective **silent** bridge.

No MIMIC result under this proposal establishes clinical truth, guideline appropriateness, workflow delivery, viewing, responsibility, order, referral, scheduling, completion, safety, benefit, causality, or transport.

## Falsifiable question and substantive advance

After the inherited exact target atom `g*=(E*, M*, W*)` and route are frozen, is the target recommendation (a) independently judged to remain active or to have an explicit superseding/resolving plan at `D`, and (b) preserved in canonical DS with exact atom identity, more often than the matched non-target recommendation control, without a comparable signal in route-scrambled and downstream-documentation negative controls?

The new claim is deliberately narrower than clinical actionability. A supportive result means that independent panels consistently classify a target-linked pre-D state and that the canonical DS preserves the same atom in a way that is more specific than the frozen control. It does **not** mean that the recommendation was correct or acted upon. An adverse result means the exact target-cell construct is not reliably anchored or is not more specific than controls. Missing or conflicting evidence is `DEFER`, not evidence that care failed.

The substantive advance is separation of three previously conflated constructs:

1. **State at discharge:** active, explicitly superseded/resolved, reconciliation-needed, or unresolved.
2. **Documentary handoff:** exact atom-preserving canonical-DS text, independently adjudicated.
3. **Downstream documentation concordance:** later MIMIC text that is target-linked under frozen source rules, analyzed only as a documentation control and never as completion.

## Pre-label state hierarchy and freeze

The hierarchy and all lexical/context rules are sealed before any phase-1 or panel label is exposed. It is a classification contract, not a clinical gold standard. The hierarchy is applied independently to every frozen target and control unit, and admission-level state uses all-unit adverse precedence.

For each unit, the source-only eligibility parser first assigns `E0=NOT_ELIGIBLE` or an eligible exact atom. An eligible unit then receives one mutually exclusive state, in this order:

- `S_U`: unresolved/unavailable. Missing or conflicting keys/times, ambiguous atom identity, inseparable target/control span, unreadable/truncated source, absent required panel, or irreconcilable evidence. `S_U` has precedence over every positive state.
- `S_X`: explicitly superseded/resolved before `D`. A later same-admission pre-D source text explicitly states that the finding/recommendation was completed, cancelled, no longer needed, replaced by another modality/interval, or otherwise resolved. A generic later note, absence, or a different service alone cannot produce `S_X`.
- `S_A`: active at `D`. Independent evidence identifies the same object cluster and affirmative atom as still intended, with no scoped negation, supersession, completion, or conditionality. `S_A` is a documentary state, not an appropriateness judgment.
- `S_R`: reconciliation-needed/conditional. The recommendation is present but a clinician would need to reconcile ambiguous ownership, competing plan, conditional wording, discordant interval/modality, or incomplete target linkage; it is not promoted to active.
- `S_N`: not clinically interpretable from available text. The unit is atom-eligible but the available structured/text evidence cannot distinguish active, superseded, or reconciliation-needed. This is a descriptive unknown lane and is never pooled into `S_A`.

The state precedence is `S_U > S_X > S_R > S_A > S_N` only when multiple permissible findings coexist; the compiler must retain all underlying findings and the exact reason code. `S_X` does not mean care was inappropriate, and `S_A` does not mean care was indicated. A target admission is positive for the conservative state gate only if every target unit is independently `S_A` and no target unit is `S_X`, `S_R`, `S_N`, or `S_U`; any adverse/uncertain unit makes the admission fail the state gate. For descriptive analyses, each state and its design-weighted total is reported separately; no unknown is complete-case deleted.

The state hierarchy, dictionary, span windows, prohibited inference list, and panel codebooks are hashed before `FRAME_SUPPORT_SEALED`. Phase-1 label permutation must not change target/control membership, state-support counts, packet costs, or the selected target. The first inherited target cell failing state/control support or worst-case budget is `NO_SELECTION`; no later favorable cell is searched.

## Independent evidence panels and shared-error controls

The panel design separates source eligibility, discharge documentation, anchoring state, and downstream documentation. Rosters, packet IDs, codebooks, random seeds, and text exposure are disjoint. No panel sees another panel's labels, route, target/control role, H outcomes, or favorable result.

**Panel A — atom and unit panel.** Two independent trained reviewers see only the reciprocal pre-D radiology report/addendum and radiology-detail fields needed to identify the frozen unit. They assign atom components, affirmative/negated/conditional status, target object span, and source provenance. They do not see canonical DS, downstream notes, service, ICU, transfers, or the clinical state rubric. A deterministic source parser supplies navigation but never overrides a reviewer. Disagreement is `A_U`, not majority-rescued.

**Panel B — independent clinical-anchoring panel.** A fresh roster receives the original pre-D report with the target recommendation span replaced by an atom card containing only the frozen `E/M/W` IDs, plus a pre-registered same-admission context window drawn from non-report structured fields and non-target text. It does not receive the canonical DS, downstream radiology, Panel A labels, route, or H packets. Each reviewer assigns the state hierarchy (`S_A/S_X/S_R/S_N/S_U`) and marks the minimal text span/reason code. The panel may say that evidence is insufficient; it may not infer from missing documentation, service identity, ICU stay, discharge location, death flag, or transfers that a plan was active or completed. If the context window cannot support target linkage without the hidden span, the result is `S_N` or `S_U`, never `S_A`.

**Panel C — discharge documentary panel.** Fresh readers see the atom card, canonical DS text/detail fields, and fixed provenance, but not the original radiology text, Panel A/B labels, terminal service, route, or downstream notes. They assign `H+`, `H-`, or `H_U` using the inherited exact cluster/modality/interval/affirmative-scope/span rules. `H+` requires an attributable DS span; generic follow-up, a related modality, a different interval, a conditional phrase, or cross-sentence clinical inference is not H+.

**Panel D — downstream-documentation panel.** Fresh readers see only post-D radiology notes/detail fields and an atom card, with pre-D report and canonical DS hidden. They assign `K+` only when the same frozen atom and target-linked object are explicitly documented in a post-D report under a fixed same-admission linkage rule; otherwise `K-` or `K_U`. `K+` is a later documentation occurrence, not proof of imaging completion, result review, delivery, or benefit. If no eligible post-D note can be observed in the frozen window, code `K_0=structurally unobserved`, not `K-`.

For a sampled target/control admission, Panel A and B process every frozen target/control unit; Panel C processes the target and the deterministic matched control; Panel D processes all downstream candidates in a fixed target-linked window and one pre-registered negative-control window. No unit or favorable state may be sampled after labels. A third adjudicator may document disagreement only under a pre-registered roster and cannot rewrite primary statuses or convert `U` to positive.

Shared-text controls are mandatory. The compiler creates (i) route-scrambled atom cards paired with unchanged DS/source provenance, (ii) target-span deletion/addition fixtures using synthetic text only, (iii) same-admission non-target controls matched exactly on modality and interval, and (iv) downstream placebo windows with an atom not present in the pre-D opportunity. These controls are fixed before labels; they are not clinical negative controls. Any leakage of original target text, panel status, route, or role causes invalidation.

## Downstream documentation controls and estimands

The primary inherited target/control handoff estimand remains unchanged. The new state and downstream quantities are secondary, target-cell-specific, and defined on frozen domains:

- `S_TC`: full-frame admissions with the target and deterministic matched control, before any panel label;
- `theta_A`: finite-population proportion of target units/admissions passing the conservative `S_A` gate;
- `theta_X`, `theta_R`, `theta_N`, `theta_U`: mutually exclusive state proportions on the same frozen target domain;
- `theta_H_T` and `theta_H_C`: exact DS H+ rates for target and control on `S_TC`;
- `Delta_H = theta_H_T - theta_H_C`;
- `theta_K_T`: proportion with a `K+` downstream documentation occurrence among target opportunities with an observable post-D window;
- `theta_K_C`: the analogous control rate where the control's frozen atom has a valid post-D window; and
- `Delta_K`, reported only when both domains have positive design support.

The denominator for `K` is not silently all target opportunities. For each atom, define a pre-label observability state: `K_O` if the due/post-D window is locally within available subject/admission observation and the required clocks/text linkage are present; `K_0` if no eligible downstream note is observable; `K_U` if dates or linkage are unresolved. Report `K_0` and `K_U` separately. `K_0` is not no completion and `K_U` is not failure. No exact-calendar assumption is made from `anchor_year` or `anchor_year_group`.

Use the inherited sequential admission weight `1/(pi1_i q_i)` and retain `pi1`, conditional `q`, and second-order factors separately. H_U, S_U, K_U, nonresponse, and structural no-note states remain on their original path weights. A pairwise interval is undefined if any requested support denominator or second-order probability is zero. No complete-case deletion, imputation, adjacent-atom borrowing, or control replacement is allowed.

The downstream negative controls are: (1) a pre-D placebo interval with the same duration rule where a post-D occurrence is impossible by construction, (2) a route-scrambled atom not present in the source opportunity, and (3) the frozen same-admission non-target control. Their expected role is to expose linkage/parser/common-documentation artifacts, not to prove absence of care. Any positive rate above the pre-registered upper specificity ceiling is adverse for the documentary construct; it does not imply clinical harm.

## Budget, positivity, and selection gates

All added panel work is charged to the inherited whole-admission ledger before labels. The compiler must enumerate every allowed post-label band partition and every occupied role subcell using only sealed frame fields, target/control hashes, state-support fields, and fixed band definitions. For each partition it stores maximum target, control, state, downstream, negative-control, calibration, duplicate, and re-read packet counts; two-reader minutes; fixed panel charges; reviewer-hours; weekly minutes; roster sizes; and the exact `ceil` operations.

The governing cost is the componentwise maximum, not a mean or observed time. The ledger remains

`T = T_micro + T_floor + T_target_extra + T_non_target_extra + T_control_only`,

with `120 <= T <= 300`, target quota at most 180, no unit sampling or replacement, and

`reserve_minutes = ceil(0.15 * (phase2_reading_minutes + fixed_controls_minutes))`;

`charged_minutes = phase2_reading_minutes + fixed_controls_minutes + reserve_minutes <= 84,000`.

Total reviewer-hours, including all independent panels, fixed controls, calibration, duplicate packets, and registered nonresponse/re-read charges, must remain `<=1,400`; the compiler must show the additive decomposition and must not count one admission twice when it carries multiple roles. A synthetic-only packet rehearsal fixes finite per-unit maximum times and identifies layout failures without using MIMIC text, labels, or target-cell outcomes. Missing or unbounded timing constants, an infeasible panel roster, or any partition exceeding a ceiling yields `NO_SELECTION`.

Every occupied target, control, state, downstream, and negative-control subcell must have positive first-stage and conditional phase-2 inclusion probability under every allowed partition. A zero/undefined `q`, zero pair support, or a panel only reachable after observing a favorable label is a fail-closed invalid design. The target cannot be selected merely because an ancillary panel is cheap or because downstream support is favorable. If the added state/anchoring layer lacks its fixed support floor, the inherited stored-text result may still be reported only under the parent's original ceiling, but this child’s actionability nomination is `DEFER` and cannot be upgraded by pooling.

Before labels, require at least 120 full-frame `S_TC` admissions, at least 60 in the phase-1 master, representation in at least two inherited strata, and positive worst-case support for every required panel role. These are feasibility thresholds, not clinical thresholds. Margins are fixed before labels: target state-gate failure upper bound <=0.10 for a supportive state result; simultaneous lower bound for `Delta_H` >=0.20; route-scrambled and downstream placebo upper bounds <=0.05; and finite, computable intervals for all nominated quantities. A positive point estimate without its prespecified bound is inconclusive.

## Interpretation, falsification, and verification

**Supportive:** all inherited primary gates pass; independent Panel A/B state support is positive; target `S_A` and exact H+ are sufficiently supported; `Delta_H` lower bound meets 0.20; route-scrambled and placebo controls meet their ceilings; downstream results, if reported, have positive support and do not show a documentary-specificity failure; and all seals, rosters, chronology, ledger, and nonresponse invariants pass. This supports only target-linked stored-text anchoring and documentary specificity in the selected MIMIC cell. It permits consideration of a separately approved silent bridge, never a live alert or order.

**Adverse:** a sufficiently supported target has any conservative active-state failure above the frozen margin; explicit supersession/reconciliation is common; target H+ is not better than the matched control; a negative control is spuriously positive above its ceiling; independent panels fail to reproduce the atom/state; or any inherited primary route fails. The result excludes that exact cell from bridge priority but does not show unsafe care, missed follow-up, or inappropriate recommendation.

**Inconclusive:** `NO_SELECTION`, sparse or zero positivity, undefined pair support, broad intervals, panel nonresponse, `S_U/S_N/K_U` excess that prevents the prespecified margin, source or chronology ambiguity, failed synthetic rehearsal, roster overlap, leakage, or budget failure. `K_0` alone is not adverse; it is a structural observation state. Inconclusive means additional evidence or a new governed design is needed.

The verifier must independently test source hashes and exact archive members; joins and null-key handling; first-eligible ordering; reciprocal chronology; terminal service and canonical DS tie rules; target/control immutability under label permutation; state hierarchy precedence; panel masking and roster disjointness; target/control atom matching; downstream boundary and no-subject-only linkage; positive `pi1/q` and zero-pair behavior; all-unit adverse precedence; exact H/K spans; placebo and derangement construction; finite ledger and one reserve; simultaneous margins; and nested variance replay. It must test that a mechanically correct run does not justify claims of clinical appropriateness, delivery, viewing, responsibility, completion, safety, benefit, causality, or transport.

## Prospective bridge requirements and irreducible evidence gap

A supportive retrospective result can only nominate a target-cell-specific **silent** prospective bridge. Before exposure, governance must freeze the exact atom, eligibility, primary feasibility estimand, sample/precision, stopping rules, safety review, and progression criteria. The shadow output must be suppressed from clinicians, patients, orders, referrals, scheduling, copied text, and UI actions.

The bridge must collect actual-calendar timestamps and immutable audit evidence for report availability, delivery, receipt/view, user role, accountable service/clinician, target-linked order/referral, scheduling, completion, result review, UI edits/copy/override, patient preference/refusal, outside-care linkage, loss to follow-up, and censoring. It must independently adjudicate whether the finding was real, whether the recommendation was appropriate, whether the active/superseded state was correct, and whether timing/action was safe. Images or a clinically governed reference standard are required for claims about the finding itself. Retrospective canonical-DS H+ and post-D radiology text must not be used as surrogates for delivery, completion, or benefit.

Irreducibly unavailable in this MIMIC snapshot are actual discharge/departure and report delivery/view logs; named accountability; order/referral/scheduling/completion provenance; patient preference and refusal; outside-care and loss-to-follow-up ascertainment; UI presentation/edit/override; contemporaneous clinician intent; images or a validated clinical gold standard; actual-calendar transport; and patient-level safety, clinical, or causal outcomes. `hosp/transfers`, `discharge_location`, `hospital_expire_flag`, ICU stays, services, prescriptions, POE records, and post-D notes cannot repair those absences and must not be relabeled as workflow or clinical truth. Only a prospective, silent, target-cell-specific bridge with these measurements can test feasibility; only a later governed comparative study can test utility, safety, equity, or benefit.

## Substantive change and remaining uncertainty

Compared with the parents, this child adds a pre-label mutually exclusive active/superseded/reconciliation/unknown hierarchy, separates source atomization from independent clinical-anchoring and discharge-document panels, and adds downstream documentation and placebo controls without changing the frozen denominator, target, route, primary estimand, or sampling design. It makes every new state and panel role positive-probability and worst-case budget-constrained, and it prevents a reproducible DS phrase from being mislabeled as active care or clinical actionability.

The remaining uncertainty is not a wording defect: expert panels can share conceptual error even with disjoint packets, text can omit intent, post-D documentation can reflect only a new note, and MIMIC cannot observe workflow or clinical outcomes. Therefore even a fully supportive run remains a documentation/anchoring result and a silent-bridge nomination, never clinical truth or a claim that any patient benefited.
