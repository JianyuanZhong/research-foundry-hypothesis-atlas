# A bounded longitudinal documentation falsification layer for the frozen pulmonary routing audit

## Decision question and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent asks whether one exact support-atom/inherited-route cell can be measured reproducibly and precisely enough to warrant a separately governed prospective silent actual-workflow bridge. It deliberately does not claim guideline appropriateness, clinical truth, communication, completion of recommended imaging, safety, benefit, or causality.

The parent's remaining identification gap is important: agreement about a stored discharge-text construct does not establish that the construct is clinically anchored to any subsequent documentation pattern. This child adds a separately analyzed longitudinal **documentation audit**, not a follow-up-completion endpoint. It tests whether the frozen discharge opportunity is followed by a pre-specified, recommendation-compatible radiology-documentation signal in the same subject and whether that signal is distinguishable from a negative-control documentation signal. The audit is designed to falsify overinterpretation of the text construct, not to prove that care occurred.

The strongest supported claim remains source feasibility: the MIMIC snapshot has pre-departure radiology/discharge text, admission and service chronology, and some later same-subject admissions and radiology reports. The new unresolved claim is narrower and falsifiable: among frozen opportunities and under an explicitly tiered linkage/ascertainment rule, is there reproducible post-departure target-compatible radiology documentation, and is its apparent rate materially different from a registered non-target radiology documentation control? A positive result would only show a within-source documentation association. It would not show that the recommendation was delivered, viewed, ordered, completed, clinically appropriate, or beneficial.

A required preliminary audit makes the limitation concrete. I read the immutable source headers and sampled the first 5,000 rows of `hosp/admissions` in archive order, retained the first admission per subject, then retained the first 40 such subjects with nonmissing `dischtime` and scanned all `note/radiology` rows for those subjects. There were 5,000 admissions, 2,153 subjects, 40 audited subjects with a discharge time, 145 later radiology rows by `charttime>D`, and 16 subjects with at least one such row. In the same 5,000-row admission slice, 29 later admissions occurred for 13 audited subjects. The first later reports include blank `hadm_id` values, while others have an exact admission key. Therefore absence of an exact post-D report is not noncompletion, and subject-only linkage cannot be treated as an encounter or care link. This child retains the longitudinal layer only as a bounded, tiered documentation audit; it abandons any claim of a valid completion endpoint.

## Frozen inherited design, unchanged

All parent invariants are copied without modification.

* Population is the complete first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity population. The first eligible admission is ordered by `admittime, hadm_id` after all inherited exclusions. The opportunity is an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal opacity with a patient-specific unconditional CT recommendation at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months.
* Chronology is reciprocal pre-departure evidence only. Radiology report/addendum chains require subject/admission consistency, `charttime < D`, `storetime < D`, and the inherited retrieval rules. A single canonical discharge artifact is selected only from `note/discharge` rows with `note_type == "DS"`, keyed by `(subject_id, hadm_id)` under the parent's exact canonical rule. Post-D material is prohibited from eligibility, unitization, phase-1 labels, route derivation, target selection, sampling, rescue, and the primary estimand.
* Recommendation units remain the smallest `(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity)` units, nested in whole admissions. Any adverse or unresolved minority unit controls the admission.
* The exact support atom remains `g=(E, terminal curr_service, W)`, with the inherited `E`, terminal service, and `W` construction, support atlas, fixed route order, and no pooling, adjacency, borrowing, shrinkage, or return. The route remains the parent's concealed compatibility-map route with all-unit adverse precedence.
* The parent's independent two-phase design remains intact: outcome-blind five-stratum phase 1, at most 900 distinct admissions, sealed screens/bands, and a single exact target selected in fixed atlas order before any phase-1 clinical label or band is exposed. Phase 2 remains whole-admission sampling with positive first- and second-stage probabilities, fresh independent C/S readings for every unit, path weight `1/(pi1_i*q_i)`, nested design uncertainty, and no replacement or adaptive allocation.
* The fixed phase-2 range is 120–300 admissions, with the 1,400 physician-hour, 96 reviewer-hour, 6 reviewer-hour/week, and 32-week ceilings. Tiny strata are censused and all target/non-target floors are counted. The parent's executable allocation, probability, precision, shared-error, and control fixtures remain supporting code and are not replaced by this child.
* The conclusion ceiling is unchanged: a supportive result can warrant only a separately approved prospective silent actual-workflow bridge, with outputs suppressed or `DEFER`; it cannot authorize `AUTO`, `RECON`, a live alert, clinical responsibility, safety, benefit, or causal interpretation.

## Longitudinal audit: estimands and linkage hierarchy

The longitudinal layer is a prespecified secondary analysis performed only after the frozen primary frame, target, allocation, phase-2 packets, and all primary labels are sealed. It cannot change a primary label or route. It is reported for the entire frozen opportunity frame when computationally feasible and for the probability-sampled phase-2 admissions with design weights as a prespecified verification; the two are not silently pooled. The phase-2 estimate is descriptive/verification-only and does not become a clinical reference endpoint.

For each frozen opportunity `i`, let `D_i` be the inherited discharge time and let each post-D radiology row have subject `s`, optional `hadm_id`, `charttime`, `storetime`, `note_id`, `note_type`, `note_seq`, `text`. Define windows by within-subject elapsed time, never by deidentified cross-subject calendar year:

* `W3 = (D_i, D_i + 120 days]`, `W6 = (D_i + 120 days, D_i + 240 days]`, and `W12 = (D_i + 240 days, D_i + 480 days]`. These broad windows are fixed before inspection, cover the inherited 3/6/12-month recommendation intervals with a tolerance that is explicit rather than hidden, and are used only for documentation timing. The exact due interval/date remains frozen in the primary unit and is not overwritten by these analysis windows.
* A row must have `charttime > D_i`; a row with missing or unparsable `charttime` is `K_U` for this audit and is never silently excluded. `storetime` is retained as a separate clock. Rows with `storetime <= D_i` are pre-departure stored artifacts and cannot be counted as post-departure documentation; if `charttime>D_i` but `storetime<=D_i`, the row is an explicit clock-conflict category and is adverse/inconclusive for the audit, not evidence of subsequent care.
* `L0` (exact encounter linkage) requires the same `(subject_id, hadm_id)` as a later `hosp/admissions` row and a nonmissing `hadm_id`, with the report `charttime` inside that later admission's `[admittime, dischtime]` interval or with the parent's explicit diagnostic transfer exception. `L1` (subject-linked/encounter-ambiguous) requires the same `subject_id` and a post-D report but either a blank `hadm_id` or no uniquely matching later admission. `L2` is an unlinked/invalid or clock-conflict row. L0 is the only tier that can be called an exact same-encounter documentation signal. L1 is a lower-bound signal for source-visible same-subject documentation, not an encounter or completion measure. L2 is never counted as a positive signal.
* If one row matches multiple later admissions because of overlapping or missing intervals, it is `L1` and not assigned to either admission. If the row has a nonmissing `hadm_id` that does not occur in the admission table, it is not repaired by subject matching; it is `L1` or `L2` according to the clock and ambiguity rules. This prevents false certainty from a partially missing join.
* Death, a later admission with `hospital_expire_flag==1`, and later `deathtime`/`dod` are competing observable events, not evidence of absent imaging. For each opportunity report risk sets as `observable-source`, `death/terminal-event-observed`, `later-admission-observed`, `no-link-found`, and `administrative/censoring-limited`. A subject who dies before a window's end is censored at the observed death time for documentation timing; no no-event label is imputed. If death is unavailable or inconsistent, status is `K_U`.
* Outside care, care at another institution, missing notes, note under-coverage, and the deidentified calendar shift are always treated as unobserved. The source contains no reliable capture denominator for completed imaging. No inverse-probability correction for outside care is attempted.

The primary audit quantity is therefore an **exact-source documentation signal**, not completion:

`Y_doc0(i,w)=1` only if at least one L0 report in window `w` is compatible with the frozen unit's target modality/action and contains a pre-registered pulmonary-target textual or structured marker. `Y_doc1(i,w)=1` is the corresponding L1 signal. `Y_doc0=0` means only that no exact-source row meeting the rule was found, never that the recommendation was not completed. A row can be positive for at most one window; duplicate reports within a single episode are collapsed to one signal after retaining counts and note IDs.

## Target-compatible documentation marker and negative control

The matching procedure is sealed before any phase-2 labels or results are viewed. The protocol stores the exact versioned matcher, token patterns, structured `exam_code/exam_name` fields, and all candidate rows, rather than publishing only positives. The matcher has two independent components:

1. **Modality compatibility.** Use `note/radiology_detail.field_name in {"exam_code","exam_name"}` and the corresponding `field_value`, joined by `(subject_id,note_id)`, plus the report text when detail is absent. The compatible set is fixed from the frozen recommendation action/modality; unknown or absent detail is `K_U`, not a negative. A clinical adjudicator may review a fixed, route-blinded sample of marker construction, but adjudication cannot revise the computational endpoint after looking at outcomes.
2. **Pulmonary-target marker.** Use a frozen lexical/section rule over `note/radiology.text` that requires a pulmonary nodule/opacity/lesion/follow-up concept and excludes explicit negation/irrelevant history according to a versioned codebook. The rule is an operational documentation marker only. It is not a validated diagnosis or proof that the later study addressed the original lesion. All raw text remains source data and is not sent to public search.

A negative-control endpoint is defined in parallel: a later L0/L1 radiology report in the same window with a non-thoracic compatible examination and no pulmonary-target marker, using the same row, time, storage, linkage, and censoring rules. The negative control is not expected to be zero; it measures general source-visible radiology documentation and helps expose whether a putative target signal is merely a generic tendency to generate later reports. A second, stricter negative control is a pulmonary-compatible report whose text is temporally pre-existing or explicitly states that the finding is historical/stable and does not describe interval follow-up; this is a documentation-negative control, not a clinical truth label. The negative controls cannot validate the primary route and cannot be used to rescue a failure.

The audit reports separately by linkage tier (`L0`, `L1`, `L2/K_U`), window, exact support atom-route target versus all other frozen opportunities, terminal service, inherited phase-1 stratum, and death/terminal-event status. No standardization, neighboring-cell pooling, or outcome-defined subgroup is permitted. If the selected exact target has fewer than the registered minimum of observable opportunities, the longitudinal layer is inconclusive; it cannot borrow from another atom or route.

## Identification contrasts and falsification

The principal contrast is a documentation-only risk difference between target-compatible and negative-control signals within the same frozen opportunity and window, reported separately for L0 and L1. The analysis uses paired subject/opportunity indicators where possible, exact finite-population counts for the full frame, and design-weighted Horvitz–Thompson/Hájek estimates for the phase-2 verification. Confidence intervals use the parent's nested first-/second-stage uncertainty for the weighted verification, with a simultaneous family covering all windows, linkage tiers, target/non-target atom-route displays, negative controls, censoring/competing-event summaries, and primary gates.

A nominally supportive longitudinal result requires all of the following, fixed before analysis:

* the target-compatible marker, linkage, window, and censoring code pass source-integrity and reproducibility checks;
* at least the registered minimum number of L0 target observations and positive second-order probabilities in the sampled verification, with no dependence on post-D outcomes for selection;
* the target signal is present in the exact target cell and its L0 lower confidence bound exceeds the corresponding negative-control signal by the registered documentation margin, while the L1 estimate is reported separately and does not substitute for L0;
* shifted-time and report-order negative controls do not reproduce the same contrast, and clock-conflict, duplicate, and missing-link rates are below registered feasibility bounds;
* primary parent gates also pass. A longitudinal association cannot upgrade a primary failure.

An adverse result is any of the following: no exact L0 target signal, target and negative-control signals indistinguishable or reversed, a strong shifted-time/negative-control signal suggesting a generic documentation artifact, excessive missing/ambiguous links, post-D leakage into eligibility or route derivation, or a target-cell adverse primary endpoint. This makes the proposed clinical anchoring claim fail or remain unsupported; it does not prove noncompletion.

An inconclusive result includes insufficient L0 support, sparse target or negative-control events, undefined pair probabilities, death/outside-care/censoring dominance, clock conflicts, matcher instability, inability to distinguish duplicate reports, or an unavailable exact linkage. It maps to `DEFER` and a request for actual workflow logs or prospective data, never to safety or route correctness.

The following falsification controls are compulsory:

* **Shifted-time control:** rerun the frozen matcher in a pre-D window of equal duration and in a deliberately shifted post-D window that cannot correspond to the unit's due interval. A similar positive contrast in the pre-D or shifted window falsifies temporal anchoring of the documentation signal.
* **Permutation control:** permute target-unit labels within the sealed frame while preserving subject, report, and window structure. Any material target-vs-control signal after permutation indicates leakage or matcher/selection error.
* **Route concealment:** longitudinal extraction sees no phase-1/phase-2 route, route cell, or adjudicator label until its raw candidate row file and linkage tier are sealed. Route-concealed reviewers, if used, cannot see the discharge recommendation or primary result.
* **Storage-clock control:** charttime and storetime are analyzed separately. Counting a row because of charttime when its storetime precedes departure is prohibited; changing that rule after results is falsification.
* **Negative-control invariance:** the same machinery must produce the declared negative control; omission of a high-volume control, non-thoracic report, or ambiguous row is a protocol failure.
* **Competing-event audit:** deaths, terminal admissions, later admissions, and source-observable windows are displayed as mutually exclusive status categories. Complete-case or survivor-only analyses are descriptive sensitivity analyses, never the primary result.

## Workload and analysis separation

The added extraction is computational and does not increase the parent's physician/reviewer-hour contract. Any expert review of marker validity is a fixed, route-blinded, outcome-blind calibration sub-sample drawn before results, charged inside the existing 96 reviewer-hour ceiling, and cannot be expanded after observing event rates. If that calibration cannot fit the frozen roster/week/deadline contract, omit human review and classify clinical semantic validity as unresolved; do not substitute an unvalidated matcher for clinical truth. No GPU, model training, publication, or public search is needed.

The parent phase-2 primary analysis runs first and is frozen. The longitudinal file is then joined by frozen opportunity key `(subject_id,hadm_id)` and not by subject alone. Subject-level rows with blank or ambiguous `hadm_id` remain in the L1 descriptive layer only. A later report that exactly matches an admission key is not automatically considered the recommended examination; it still needs the pre-registered modality/marker rule. No later record can be used to select the target, alter a route, reclassify a primary adverse unit, or rescue a failed precision certificate.

## Interpretation ceiling

Supportive primary plus supportive documentation audit means only that the stored text was precise in the parent construct and has a detectable source-visible, temporally ordered documentation association under exact linkage. It may justify prioritizing a prospective silent bridge that logs recommendation generation, UI exposure, acknowledgement, order/referral, scheduling, completed study identity, outside-care capture, and clinician/patient context. It does not establish any of those events.

An adverse documentation audit warns that the stored-text construct lacks observable longitudinal anchoring or is indistinguishable from a generic documentation process. It excludes that cell from bridge priority, but does not prove that patients lacked care. An inconclusive audit preserves `DEFER` and identifies the missing evidence: actual orders and scheduling, radiology accession/completion logs, external-care records, timestamps in a non-shifted calendar, patient preference, named responsibility, and clinical expert adjudication of whether later imaging addressed the same lesion. A future prospective, probability-ascertained silent workflow study is required before any operational or clinical claim. A later comparative governed study is required for safety, workload, patient-reported outcomes, diagnostic accuracy, benefit, or causality.

This child therefore strengthens clinical anchoring without converting a sparse, shifted, incompletely linked EHR into a false completion endpoint. The exact atom, nested probability design, ceilings, all-unit adverse precedence, and silent-bridge-only conclusion are unchanged.
