## 1. The scientific opening

Synovial tissue macrophages (STMs) in rheumatoid arthritis separate into a MerTK⁺CD206⁺ compartment
enriched in sustained, drug-free remission and a MerTK⁻ compartment enriched in active disease; the
MerTK⁺ compartment is reported to instruct synovial fibroblasts (FLS) toward a repair-like rather than
inflammatory state, and the MerTK⁺ proportion (<47.5%, or MerTK⁺:MerTK⁻ ratio <2.5) is proposed as a
flare-risk readout after treatment cessation [K1]. A second, independent transwell system established
the opposite arm of the same circuit: fibroblast-entrained macrophages acquire an HBEGF⁺/EREG⁺ state
whose output alters 855 FLS genes and increases Matrigel invasiveness in an EGFR-inhibitor-sensitive
manner, and the reciprocal fibroblast→macrophage arm is transwell-separated and PGE2-dominant [K2].
Both arms are reasonably well supported.

**What is not established is the role of MerTK itself.** "MerTK⁺" is used in two logically different
ways that are rarely separated:

- as a **surface marker of a lineage/phenotype** whose *other* properties (lipid-mediator biosynthesis,
  transcriptional program, residency origin) deliver the fibroblast instruction; or
- as the **effector receptor** — a signalling node whose activity is the proximal brake on the
  macrophage's fibroblast-activating output.

Sorting cannot distinguish them: everything co-varying with the sort gate is a candidate cause.
Episode 1 measured on E-MTAB-8873 that the published MerTK⁺-vs-MerTK⁻ direction replicates in 9/9
within-run contrasts but with 64–88% per-cell overlap, donor-matched effect sizes d = 0.028/0.217/0.042
(one of four reaching ~0.5), against a between-macrophage-donor σ of 0.2226 on the same axis — i.e. the
sort **label** is a weak, donor-contaminated descriptor of instructing capacity. That sharpens the
present question rather than answering it: if the label is weak, is the **pathway** strong?

Exactly one admitted experiment in this island perturbs the pathway instead of sorting on it, and no
candidate has ever bound it: **E-MTAB-8316**.

## 2. What the evidence already supports (distinguished from what we test)

1. MerTK⁺CD206⁺ STM abundance is associated with sustained remission and with flare risk after therapy
   cessation; sorted MerTK⁺ vs MerTK⁻ STMs induce divergent FLS programs [K1]. **Limits of that
   evidence [OBSERVED from the inspected landing page]:** observational, n = 11 flared vs 11 sustained,
   ROC cut-offs fitted in the same sample; and the narrative explicitly calls the MerTK⁻→FLS arm
   **"contact-dependent"**, which is a direct constraint on our transwell design (§9).
2. A **soluble/paracrine** macrophage→fibroblast activating arm exists and is EGFR-ligand driven [K2].
   Our proposal must add MerTK-dependence, **not rediscover this arm**.
3. TAM-receptor pharmacology: UNC1062 inhibits Mer with Ki 0.33 nM and 78×/36× selectivity over Axl and
   Tyro3, but **adherent** cells need >300 nM (250–500 nM) for target engagement, and no kinome-wide
   selectivity panel is reported [K3]. This grounds the off-target rival in measured pharmacology.
4. **Novelty scoping, stated plainly [OBSERVED, K1 data-availability statement]:** the original authors
   deposited E-MTAB-8316 and refer to "DEGs from FLS-MoM cocultured with MerTK inhibitor" in a
   **paywalled supplementary dataset we could not inspect**. We therefore do **not** claim to be first
   to contrast these 12 libraries. What is unpublished and unresolved is the *interpretation*: whether
   the response lies on the resolving-instruction axis, on the generic inflammation axis, or on the
   measured inhibitor off-target axis — a decomposition that requires the E-MTAB-8873 axes and the
   macrophage-free control, and which no inspected text performs. [UNRESOLVED] If the paywalled
   supplement turns out to contain this decomposition, the contribution reduces to an independent

## 3. The unresolved claim and its rivals

> **H-effector.** MerTK kinase signalling in the macrophage is a causal brake on the macrophage's
> **soluble** fibroblast-instructing output: blocking it moves the receiving RA fibroblast along the
> resolving-instruction axis in the direction opposite to the MerTK⁺CD206⁺ instruction, by an amount
> not explained by generic inflammatory amplification or by direct drug action on the fibroblast.

> **H-generic.** MerTK is a marker; blockade de-represses TLR output, so the fibroblast response is
> collinear with the **LPS-instruction** axis and has no residual component on the resolving axis.

> **H-offtarget.** The response is collinear with the measured **fibroblast-autonomous** response to the
> inhibitor (episode 1's documented confound) and says nothing about macrophage MerTK.
> **[OBSERVED] This rival is not closed by target absence**: the receiver FLS express MERTK at only
> 0.15–2.72 CPM but AXL at 110–302 CPM and TYRO3 at 9–22 CPM, so carried-over pan-TAM compound has an
> abundant direct fibroblast target.

> **H-novel** (pre-specified, not discovered post hoc). The response is a coordinated program orthogonal
> to all three axes — the outcome a projection-only analysis discards as noise, and the reason a
> latent-factor alternative is carried alongside the projection baseline (§8).

### 3.1 The pharmacokinetic coupling between H-effector and H-offtarget (the design's real ceiling)
**[OBSERVED from the protocol, `rivals.md` §1 — this is the strongest objection and I state it before
the design, not after.]** The inhibitor is applied for the **last 2 h of the 4 h LPS prime and then
washed off with PBS** before the 48 h co-culture. Only two routes can carry an effect to the fibroblast:

- **route (i) durable reprogramming** — 2 h of kinase block leaves a 48 h-stable imprint on the
  macrophage secretome, with no drug present during co-culture;
- **route (ii) carryover** — enough compound survives the wash to act during the co-culture.

H-effector in its *natural* form (MerTK restrains the output while the circuit runs, ligated by the
FLS-derived GAS6/PROS1 that cross the 0.4 µm membrane — ligands we measured at 295–948 and 36–117 CPM)
**requires route (ii)**, which is exactly H-offtarget's premise. So the two leading hypotheses share a
pharmacokinetic assumption and differ only in **which cell** the surviving drug acts on, and E-MTAB-8316
contains no arm where drug meets fibroblasts without macrophages (§4.3). Three consequences, all carried
into the decision rule:
1. The A3 projection is not a nicety; it is the only in-data adjudicator of route (ii), and it is the
   best-**precision** test in the study (MDE 0.081) — but a dedicated episode-2 diagnostic
   (§4.2a, `[research job]`) measured that its yardstick is **62.4% pure cell-sampling
   noise**, so it is *not* the most informative test, and its precision buys a much weaker bound on the
   true off-target content than the raw MDE suggests. The decision rule in §6 is rewritten accordingly.
2. A null is ambiguous between "no MerTK brake" and "route (i) is weak". Independent mechanistic reason
   to expect route (i) to be weak: LPS drives ADAM17-dependent MerTK ectodomain **shedding** in primary
   macrophages, i.e. surface MerTK is being removed during the very 4 h window in which the kinase
   inhibitor acts (Duprez et al., mAbs 2026, doi:10.1080/19420862.2026.2704449, **abstract inspected
   only**; the quantitative extent at 1 ng/ml LPS over 4 h is UNRESOLVED).
3. Even a clean positive with β₃ ≈ 0 does **not** identify MerTK: a 2 h exposure at the deposited dose
   could act on other macrophage kinases (`rivals.md` §2.A2), a rival that survives a *perfect* wash and
   that no within-8316 comparison can touch. See the claim ladder in §9.1.

### Clinical consequence
The MerTK⁺ proportion/ratio biomarker is used to frame **therapy-withdrawal decisions** in RA remission
[K1], and the TAM/efferocytosis axis is an active therapeutic target class. **H-effector** ⇒ MerTK is a
modifiable signalling state, MerTK agonism/efferocytosis enhancement is a mechanistically plausible
fibroblast-directed strategy, and the biomarker plausibly indexes something restorable. **H-generic** ⇒
MerTK agonism would blunt generic inflammation rather than restore a repair program, the biomarker
indexes lineage availability, and development effort should move to what else distinguishes the MerTK⁺
lineage (e.g. its lipid-mediator machinery). **H-offtarget** ⇒ existing inhibitor-based evidence in this
experimental family cannot separate sender from receiver drug effects, and the next step is genetic
rather than pharmacological perturbation. These are three different research decisions.

## 6. Estimand, pre-registered decision rule, and measured power

For pair *i*, `d_i` = within-pair difference (UNC − none) on log2(CPM+1) over the 429 axis genes;
`w = A1⊥/(A1⊥·A1⊥)` with A1 orthogonalised against A2 and A3 (ordering fixed in `axes.md`);
`b_i = w′d_i`; **β₁ = mean(b)** by paired t-test on 5 df with FLS donor as a block, plus a
within-specimen sign-flip permutation reference (2⁶ = 64 flips; two-sided exact p floor 1/32 ≈ 0.031 —
declared in advance). Secondary: **β₂** (A2⊥), **β₃** (A3⊥), residual fraction, and the named-gene panel
of §4.1 with a pre-declared Holm correction within the 16 usable genes. `b = 1.0` means one full unit of
the MerTK⁺-vs-MerTK⁻ instruction.

### 6.1 A normalisation mismatch I found while auditing the feasibility code, and how it is pre-handled
**[OBSERVED, `run_bindings.py` lines 60/146/272; this is a Lead audit finding, not a worker claim.]**
The two sides of the projection are normalised on different bases: the 8873 axes are built on
**panel-CPM** (library = the sum over the 445 panel features, a closed composition), while the 8316
response uses **whole-transcriptome CPM** restricted to the same 429 genes. A closed-composition
denominator shift adds an approximately **constant** offset *c* to every gene's log difference, and
such an offset does **not** cancel in `w′d` unless the weights sum to zero — so the absolute scale of
β₁ ("one unit of A1") is only approximately transferable between the two platforms.

Pre-specified handling, fixed now rather than after seeing the result: (i) the **primary** estimand uses
a **mean-centred** weight vector (`w ← w − mean(w)` after orthogonalisation), so that any uniform
composition/normalisation offset contributes exactly zero; (ii) the uncentred weights and an 8316
variant normalised *within* the 429-gene subspace are both reported as pre-declared sensitivity
analyses; (iii) if the primary and sensitivity β₁ differ by more than the primary CI half-width, the
result is declared **normalisation-dependent** and reported as inconclusive for H-effector regardless of
its sign.

**The recomputation is done, not deferred [OBSERVED, `[research job]`, 3.26 s, 2 CPUs,
`work/lead/centred_weights.json`, sign-free: per-gene condition means formed in memory and discarded].**
The mismatch is material, not pedantic: the mean weight carries **32.4% of ‖A1⊥‖** and
cos(centred, uncentred) = 0.946, so a uniform composition offset is not a negligible contaminant of the
uncentred estimand. Under the primary (centred) definition, σ_proj = **0.1561** (vs 0.1802 uncentred),
MDE80 = **0.2243** (vs 0.2589), expected 95% CI half-width **0.164** (vs 0.189), inter-gene correlation
inflation 3.23×. Under the within-429-gene normalisation sensitivity: σ_proj 0.1841, MDE80 0.2645.
Centring therefore *improves* precision slightly as well as removing the artefact.

**Thresholds are deliberately NOT moved to match**: the supportive threshold stays at β₁ ≤ −0.26, which
under the primary definition now sits *above* the measured MDE (0.224) and is therefore conservative.
Threshold-shopping after seeing a more favourable σ is exactly the move a pre-registration exists to
prevent. [UNRESOLVED and carried: my job computed the A2/A3 columns with **non-orthogonalised** axes
(A2 MDE 0.167, A3 MDE 0.168 uncentred), whereas `power.md`'s A2/A3 figures (0.169 / 0.081)
orthogonalise each axis against the other two; the two conventions are different estimands and the
solver must state which it uses. The §6 β₂/β₃ thresholds refer to the orthogonalised convention.]

**Measured operating characteristics [OBSERVED, 20 000-draw simulation, `power.md`]:**
σ_proj = 0.1802 (the independent-gene calculation would give 0.0501 — a **3.6× inter-gene correlation
inflation** that a naive power calculation would miss); MDE at 80% power = **0.259 units of A1**
(ICC 0–0.6 changes it by <1%), **0.380 after correcting for A1's 0.683 reliability**; expected 95% CI
half-width 0.189 (5th–95th percentile 0.091–0.281). For the controls: A2 MDE 0.169, A3 MDE **0.081**
(CI half-width 0.059). FLS-donor ICC is not estimable and shows no positive clustering (implied
component −0.043 with 2 duplicated donors) — blocking is retained as correct practice, **not** advertised
as a power gain.

**Therefore this is a rival-elimination design, not an effect-estimation design**, and the decision rule
is written accordingly:

- **Supportive of H-effector**: β₁ ≤ −0.26 (i.e. beyond the measured MDE), permutation p ≤ 0.05, sign
  consistent in ≥5/6 specimens, and |β₃| < 0.15. Report both β₁ and the attenuation-corrected β₁/0.683
  with the reliability caveat.
- **Supportive of H-generic**: β₂ ≥ **+0.17** (directional: de-repression must *raise* the inflammatory
  instruction; the threshold is signed, not absolute) with the same evidence standard, while the 95%
  interval on β₁ excludes −0.26 — i.e. a well-measured inflammatory shift without a resolving-axis
  component. **β₂ ≤ −0.17 is a fourth, unanticipated outcome** (blockade *reducing* the inflammatory
  instruction), which neither supports H-generic nor H-effector and must be reported as such rather
  than folded into an absolute-value rule.
- **Supportive of H-offtarget**: β₃ ≥ **+0.15** (directional: carried-over compound should move the
  fibroblast *along* the measured macrophage-free inhibitor response, not against it), or
  r(d̄, A3) > 0.5. **This voids the MerTK interpretation of both other branches** and is the
  best-**precision** determination in the study and, per §4.2a, is attenuation-conservative so it
  cannot fire spuriously from A3's measurement error. **Caveat now measured:** r > 0.5 is only ~1.1×
  the background |cos| of 0.183 (max 0.453) between unrelated difference vectors in this closed panel,
  so the correlation trigger is weaker than it looks and the β₃ magnitude trigger is primary. A large
  *negative* β₃ is not carryover and is reported as an anomaly requiring the A3 sign convention and
  axis provenance to be re-checked before interpretation.
- **H-offtarget bounded, NOT falsified** (rewritten after the §4.2a measurement, before any outcome was
  seen). With A3's measured attenuation (0.376 variance-fraction, or 0.346 Spearman–Brown), a measured
  β₃ interval inside ±0.15 certifies only that the **true** off-target content is below ≈**0.40–0.43**
  units. Certifying the originally intended ±0.15 would require a β₃ interval of ±0.056, which is
  **narrower than the attainable 0.0594 half-width**, so the strong falsification clause **cannot be
  satisfied even if β₃ comes out exactly zero** (best attainable bound t ≤ 0.158–0.171). The clause is
  therefore replaced by: *"β₃ interval within ±0.15 ⇒ off-target action is not supported at the
  ≥0.40-unit level"*, and the ±0.15-unit falsification is marked **not adjudicable in-data**. Note the
  asymmetry, which is why the positive trigger above is retained unchanged: the same attenuation makes
  `|β₃| ≥ 0.15` **conservative** (it needs t ≳ 0.40 to fire, so attenuation cannot create a spurious
  off-target verdict), while it makes the falsification clause anti-conservative by the same 2.7×.
- **Supportive of H-novel**: residual fraction > 0.5 with a residual factor surviving the permutation
  null in `work/.../rivals/methods.md`.
- **Inconclusive** — pre-committed as the most likely single outcome: intervals spanning the thresholds.
  It licenses only a bound plus the required-replication calculation (MDE scales ~1/√n: 12 pairs → ~0.17,
  24 pairs → ~0.12; no further pairs exist in any admitted source).

**Falsification.** H-effector is falsified *in this system* if β₁ ≥ 0 with a tight interval, or if the
response is adequately explained by A2/A3 alone. The falsification is bounded to the **soluble** arm, to
**monocyte-derived** macrophages, to a **2 h** pre-treatment, and to an inhibitor whose engagement in
adherent cells requires >300 nM [K3].

## 7. Pre-registration integrity and the unblinding disclosure (honest, not concealed)

The 8316 none-vs-UNC106238 contrast was **not computed**. Dispersion was obtained by forming the
within-pair differences in memory, subtracting and **discarding** the per-gene condition mean, and
retaining only zero-mean residual quantities (`gene_residual_sd.tsv`, σ_proj). No fold change, no
per-gene condition mean, no signed within-pair difference, no condition-labelled ordination was stored.

**Disclosure [OBSERVED]:** three per-sample tables were written — `qc_libsize.tsv`, `qc_marker_cpm.tsv`,
`gene_raw_counts.tsv` (and `work/.../rivals/qc_raw.json`) — and because the pairing is public in
design.json, a reader of those files can *mentally* form the forbidden contrast for the genes listed
there. I inspected `qc_raw.json` and therefore know the apparent direction of several high-expression
inflammatory genes (CXCL8 among them) across the six pairs. I record this rather than hide it, and
respond by **removing those genes from any confirmatory role**: the single-gene panel is explicitly
demoted to secondary/supportive, and the confirmatory estimand is the 429-gene orthogonalised projection
β₁, which cannot be eyeballed from a handful of genes and which no worker computed. A reviewer should
treat the named-gene results as exploratory for this reason.

## 8. Baseline and substantive alternative on the same question
(Developed in `work/.../rivals/methods.md`; the selection reason is mine.)

- **Baseline** — paired limma-voom/DESeq2 with FLS-donor blocking, then projection onto A1⊥/A2/A3.
  Transparent, minutes of CPU, yields the pre-registered estimands directly. **What it loses:** it
  assumes the response lies in the span of three pre-specified axes built in a 429-gene panel space; a
  coordinated MerTK-specific program orthogonal to all three — or living in the 57 873 transcriptome
  genes carrying no axis weight — is discarded as residual noise, and gene–gene correlation is not
  modelled (we measured that correlation to inflate the null scale 3.6×, so it is not a nuisance detail).
- **Substantive alternative** — a hierarchical/latent-factor decomposition of the paired response with
  specimen pairing and FLS-donor random effects, estimating A1/A2/A3 loadings **and** a residual
  coordinated factor whose reality is tested against the within-specimen sign-flip null. **What it can
  reveal that the baseline cannot:** whether H-novel holds, i.e. whether MerTK blockade creates a
  fibroblast state that is a *third thing* — which would redirect the next experiment away from both the
  resolving-axis and the inflammation-axis framings. **Identifiability at 6 pairs is the open question
  and is part of the deliverable, not an assumption**; if the residual factor is not separable from noise
  under the permutation null, that negative methodological result is itself reportable.
- **Device: CPU.** 12 samples × ~11 447 robustly expressed genes; the executed discovery jobs took 2.7 s
  and 3.9 s at ~150 MiB. GPU is unjustified here as a measured workload judgement, not a prohibition.
  **Revisit trigger:** joint modelling of the 8873 per-cell matrices together with 8316 in one latent
  space would change the costing.
- **Not selected, with revisit evidence:** cross-platform generative integration of 8873 single-cell and
  8316 bulk. Deferred because the limiting quantity is the 429-gene shared space and A1's measured
  reliability of 0.466, not model capacity; revisit if a whole-transcriptome single-cell arm of the same
  system, or more matched 8873 pairs, are admitted.

## 9. What each outcome does and does not establish
- **Supportive**: establishes that an acute pharmacological block of macrophage MerTK shifts the
  **soluble** fibroblast-instructing output along the resolving axis **in this system**. It does not
  establish that this happens in human synovium, that MerTK agonism would reverse it, or that the
  MerTK⁺ proportion biomarker is causal. Next steps required: genetic perturbation; synovial tissue
  rather than monocyte-derived macrophages; a sender-side transcriptome for mediation; medium
  proteomics for the actual mediator.
- **Adverse**: bounds the MerTK-signalling brake in the soluble arm at ≤ the measured effect. **Bounded
  explicitly by [K1]'s own description of the MerTK⁻→FLS arm as "contact-dependent"**: a transwell null
  does not refute a contact-mediated or juxtacrine MerTK dependence (HBEGF is a shed membrane ligand
  [K2]), nor chronic dependence beyond a 2 h block.
- **Inconclusive**: the most likely single outcome; yields a bound plus the required-replication number.
  It must not be reported as evidence for H-generic.
- **Off-target dominant**: a genuinely useful negative — it would mean inhibitor-based evidence in this
  experimental family cannot separate sender from receiver drug action, and would make a macrophage-free
  fibroblast+inhibitor control mandatory in every future design of this kind.

**Not computationally checkable by any verifier** and requiring clinical/expert adjudication or new
data: whether any of this transfers to synovial tissue in patients; whether MerTK agonism changes flare
risk; whether the 47.5%/2.5 cut-offs are valid outside their derivation sample; and the true identity
and dose of the deposited compound (§10).

## 10. Rival explanations carried (detail in `work/.../rivals/rivals.md`)
1. **Dose/compound [UNRESOLVED, OBSERVED discrepancy]**: SDRF `Factor Value[dose] = 250` with IDF text
   "250 µM", versus 250 nM UNC1062 in E-MTAB-8873; the compound string `UNC106238` is not a catalogue
   identifier and looks like a conflation of UNC1062. [K3] measures that adherent cells need >300 nM for
   engagement and reports selectivity only within the TAM family, so a µM-range exposure is exactly the
   regime where off-target kinase action and post-wash carryover are plausible. We do not assert the
   identity or the true concentration.
2. **Off-target on the receiver** — not closed by target absence (FLS AXL 110–302 CPM, TYRO3 9–22 CPM,
   MERTK <3 CPM). Adjudicated only by the A3 projection, whose axis rests on one cross-platform pair.
3. **Cell source / transportability** — healthy-donor monocyte-derived macrophages, not synovial tissue
   macrophages; MerTK engagement normally requires GAS6/PROS1 plus phosphatidylserine, and apoptotic
   cells are not deliberately supplied in this culture, so the brake may be weakly engaged and the
   effect correspondingly small (consistent with the demanding MDE). **Partially rebutted by external
   measurement (§4.4):** in GSE95588 monocyte-derived macrophages express MERTK at 71–109 CPM and
   up-regulate it, together with GAS6 and PROS1, on fibroblast transwell co-culture. The residual form
   of the rival — that *synovial-tissue* macrophages would show a larger or qualitatively different
   brake — is untouched and remains a reason an adverse result here is bounded rather than general.
4. **Soluble-only readout** — the transwell bounds every conclusion to the soluble arm (§9).
5. **FLS donor heterogeneity and reuse** — SA049 and SA114 each contribute two specimens; handled by
   pairing and blocking; measured ICC not estimable, no positive clustering detected.
6. **Batch structure** — inferable from the MK numbering; reported in `rivals.md` with whether any run
   or date field is confounded with condition.
7. **Composition rival (D3-M1) is structurally excluded here**, which is a genuine advantage of this
   deposit: the readout is a single purified cell type in a transwell, so a "shift in cell-type
   proportions" cannot generate the signal — only within-cell regulation can.
