# Provisional HCC trajectory repair: frozen procedure endpoint and support audit

## Proposed relationship

Among adults with an exact documented diagnosis of 肝细胞癌 (HCC), an ordered 7-day within-assay laboratory trajectory from admission through day 7 will add held-out information about a prespecified non-routine post-window procedure beyond an admission snapshot that includes treatment and documentation-process variables. The primary claim is predictive, not causal: the endpoint is a computable procedure proxy, not death, ICU admission, or clinical harm.

The strongest currently supported claim is only that the HCC source contains joinable encounter, diagnosis, laboratory, and procedure records with substantial timed procedure support. The unresolved claim is whether temporal laboratory information predicts a clinically meaningful non-routine escalation proxy after accounting for routine HCC treatment and care-process intensity.

## Frozen endpoint dictionary

The endpoint dictionary is version `hcc-nonroutine-v1`. Normalize only by trimming whitespace and lower-casing for matching; retain the original label in the audit output. A procedure row is eligible only when it has a parseable `开始时间), is strictly after `入院时间 + 7 days`, and is not after `出院时间` when discharge is available. Untimed procedures are excluded from the primary endpoint and reported as ascertainment loss.

### Routine dictionary

Classify as routine HCC treatment when the normalized label contains any of these exact semantic tokens or documented variants:

`tace`, `经导管肝动脉栓塞`, `肝动脉栓塞`, `动脉化疗栓塞`, `肝动脉造影化疗栓塞`, `肝移植`, `供肝`, `肝切除`, `肝叶切除`, `肝段切除`, `肝部分切除`, `肝肿瘤切除`, `射频消融`, `微波消融`, `无水酒精注射`, `抗肿瘤免疫治疗`, `分子靶向治疗`, `静脉注射化疗药物`.

This is intentionally a conservative treatment dictionary, not a clinical taxonomy. It includes only labels that are plausibly planned HCC-directed treatment in the audited label space. The solver must emit the matched token, original label, and row count for every routine classification.

### Non-routine escalation proxy

The primary endpoint is the first eligible procedure not classified routine by the dictionary. To make the proxy auditable, the solver must also assign one of these mutually exclusive support categories, in this precedence order:

1. `vascular_access`: `中心静脉置管`, `颈内静脉穿刺`, `颈内静脉插管`, `输液港`.
2. `thoracic_abdominal_drainage`: `胸腔穿刺`, `胸腔闭式引流`, `胸腔穿刺抽液`, `腹腔穿刺引流`, `肝脓肿穿刺引流`, `经皮胆管置管引流`, `PTCD`.
3. `surgical_reintervention`: `肠粘连松解`, `膈肌修补`, `胆囊切除`, `腹腔淋巴结清扫`, `ALPPS`.
4. `other_nonroutine`: all remaining eligible non-routine labels.

The primary binary outcome is any first eligible non-routine procedure; the four categories are secondary descriptive outcomes and sensitivity analyses. The solver must not silently merge categories or treat the proxy as adjudicated deterioration. A procedure may be routine in one context and non-routine in another; the dictionary is therefore a reproducibility rule, not clinical truth.

## Support audit and timing check

The inherited audit, which must be rerun and frozen by the solver from the exact source files, found 105,044 encounter rows, 101,457 valid admission times, 104,554 exact HCC patient/admission keys, 338,040 procedure rows, 321,207 timed HCC procedure rows, 15,774 untimed HCC procedure rows, and 20,052 timed HCC procedures after day 7 and before discharge (6,779 keys). Under the inherited lexical routine pattern, 10,590 rows/5,090 keys were routine and 9,462 rows/4,379 keys were non-routine. These are support counts, not validity evidence.

The audit must additionally report, for the frozen dictionary: total eligible rows and keys; routine/non-routine rows and keys; category counts; top 20 original labels per category; timing distributions relative to admission; duplicate procedure labels within a key; and the number of labels that match more than one category or remain ambiguous. It must report the number of procedures whose `开始时间` is before admission, equal to the day-7 boundary, or after discharge, rather than silently discarding them. The primary endpoint uses strict `> admission + 7 days` and `<= discharge`; boundary and invalid-time counts are audit outputs.

A timing-shifted negative control shifts each patient's procedure labels by a fixed 14-day offset while preserving labels and patient-level counts, or equivalently evaluates a pre-window procedure outcome using the same feature window. This tests whether any apparent trajectory signal is merely a generic procedure/documentation process. A within-patient order-permutation control for laboratory features tests whether ordering, rather than mere measurement count, matters.

## Experiment

Cohort: exact HCC diagnosis, nonempty valid admission time, and one of `肝肿瘤内科, 肝胆肿瘤与肝移植外科, 肝胆肿瘤内科, 肝肿瘤外科`; the unrestricted exact-diagnosis cohort is a sensitivity analysis. Feature window is admission through admission plus 7 days and no later than discharge. Use only the nine exact assay labels `白蛋白, 总胆红素, 国际标准化比值, 甲胎蛋白, 肌酐, 丙氨酸氨基转移酶, 血小板计数, 凝血酶原时间, 尿素`. Preserve specimen type, time since admission, missingness, and the raw numeric `定量结果` when parseable. Do not infer units.

Baseline: penalized logistic regression with age, sex, department, first available assay values, time-zero routine-treatment/medication indicators, and documentation counts. Alternative: a patient-level ordered sequence/state-space model with the same covariates and within-assay temporal features. Split by `患者主索引`, use nested time-aware validation and repeated bootstrap uncertainty. Evaluate held-out discrimination, calibration, and incremental performance over the baseline. The sequence model may reveal persistence, ordering, and cross-marker interactions that the snapshot loses; it is not selected for complexity.

Supportive results require a nontrivial held-out trajectory advantage for the non-routine endpoint, uncertainty excluding a prespecified trivial margin, stability across department/documentation/missingness/assay sensitivities, and absence of the advantage in the timing-shifted control. Adverse results include no improvement, degradation, an advantage confined to routine procedures, disappearance after process adjustment, or a positive timing control. Inconclusive results include sparse events, unstable dictionary categories, excessive untimed records, imprecise intervals, or inability to adjudicate clinical meaning.

## Evidence and limits

The inherited candidate's three inspected abstract-only works remain the evidence context: Yang et al. (2026) supports a static laboratory response comparator in HBV cirrhosis; Bui et al. (2026) supports the importance of static HCC resection factors; Millarch et al. (2026) motivates a temporal-versus-static comparison in a different population. None establishes HCC procedure semantics, causal deterioration, or transferability. The exact three-reference bundle and frozen source receipts must be carried into the final child.

No model has been fitted and no clinical effect has been estimated. The source lacks laboratory units, validated death/ICU fields, independent clinical adjudication, and a validated procedure ontology. The solver must newly fit both models, rerun the dictionary audit, estimate held-out performance and uncertainty, and link conclusions to computed outputs. CPU is appropriate for the tabular baseline and bounded sequence fit; GPU is not required. A larger neural sequence model is deferred unless event support and runtime justify it.

## Method alternatives and deliverable

The simple baseline is scientifically adequate as a snapshot/process comparator. The ordered sequence/state-space alternative is retained because it can test persistence and ordering on the same endpoint with the same inputs and split. A larger neural sequence model is deferred: it would add capacity without resolving the endpoint-semantics uncertainty and is not needed for the smallest feasible test. The actual deliverable is a frozen endpoint dictionary plus audit, fitted baseline and temporal alternative, held-out estimates with uncertainty, negative-control results, and an interpretation classified as supportive, adverse, or inconclusive. No fitted result is claimed here.
