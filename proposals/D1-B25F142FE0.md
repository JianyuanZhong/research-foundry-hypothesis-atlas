## 0. Hypothesis vs already-supported conclusion

Already supported (NOT the novel claim): absolute/stress hyperglycemia is *associated* with
short-term ICU mortality (descriptive regularity across many cohorts). Relative/acute-on-chronic
glycemia (stress hyperglycemia ratio, SHR = admission glucose / HbA1c-implied chronic glucose) is
reported to add prognostic information in single-centre MIMIC-IV cohorts [K1], while other
MIMIC-IV analyses find the relative-glycemia increment attenuates or vanishes under full adjustment
and in non-sepsis subgroups [K2], and at least one study finds SHR predicts a *non-mortality* acute
outcome with no direct mortality effect after mediation [K3].

Novel/unresolved claim this child tests: **conditional on measured severity and on the
HbA1c-testing process, does a stay-level acute relative-glycemia signal carry information about
28-day mortality that is separable from (i) absolute admission glucose and (ii) a generic
severity/testing-propensity common cause?** This is an identifiability question, not a new
association estimate.

## 1. Leading claim vs strongest rival

- **Leading claim (H-relative):** the SHR term reflects a stay-level acute deviation of glucose
  from the patient's own chronic set-point and adds mortality information beyond absolute glucose.
- **Strongest rival (R-severity/process):** SHR is a non-specific correlate of severity and of the
  HbA1c-testing/observation process; the apparent independent increment is a common-cause artefact
  driven by which patients are sick enough to be tested (and by repeated-stay selection).

They differ on an observable: after decoupling the measurement process, the within-stay SHR
coefficient should remain (H-relative) or collapse toward the equivalence band (R-severity/process).

**Defect this child repairs (vs the parent).** The parent's decisive within-subject contrast on the
16,242 multi-stay subjects (COMPUTED by parent job `f0bd0cbdc27f6cf0e5f65fea`) is itself exposed to
two rival-generating selection mechanisms the parent did not formally separate: (a) **repeated-stay
/ immortal-time selection** — later stays belong to patients who survived to be readmitted, so
within-subject differencing can induce a stay-order artefact; and (b) **stratum-restricted
measurement** — HbA1c-implied chronic glycemia is only observed in patients selected for HbA1c
testing, and the SHR formula inherits that selection non-linearly (through the denominator). This
child adds explicit, predeclared rules that force these two mechanisms to make *distinguishable*
predictions rather than merely be "adjusted away".

## 3. Added predeclared falsifiers / rival-separating predictions (the advance)

- **S5 (stay-order placebo).** Within-subject contrast restricted to the *first two* ICU stays only,
  with `stay_index` as the lone regressor on a permuted outcome: the stay-order coefficient must be
  within the equivalence band |b| <= 0.10 on log-odds. If a stay-order "effect" survives the
  placebo, the within-subject contrast is contaminated by repeated-stay selection and cannot
  adjudicate H-relative vs R-severity/process.
- **S6 (denominator-validity stratification).** Pre-specify three HbA1c strata (normal <5.7%,
  pre-diabetic 5.7-6.4%, diabetic >=6.5%). R-severity/process predicts a *non-differential or
  inverse* SHR-mortality association across strata; H-relative predicts a *monotone-increasing*
  association restricted to the normal/pre-diabetic strata (where Gc genuinely estimates the
  pre-morbid set-point). A flat/inverse pattern is adverse for H-relative.
- **S7 (measurement-process placebo exposure).** Apply the SHR functional form to a
  non-glycemic, measurement-propensity-matched lab ratio (creatinine 50912 paired with a
  same-window chronic-ish anchor), with the same propensity-to-anchor weighting. If the placebo
  ratio reproduces the SHR increment, the increment is a measurement/severity artefact.

Inherited falsifiers retained: S1 label-permutation, S2 value-free measurement-only negative
control at equivalence margin 0.005, S3 negative-control outcome, S4 within-subject discordance.

## 4. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative, same question)

- **B0 (baseline):** penalized logistic regression on absolute admission glucose + standard
  severity covariates (interpretable, CPU).
- **B1 (platform):** B0 + SHR term; estimand = incremental discrimination (ΔAUROC, ΔNB at 5/10/20%).
- **A1 (substantive alternative):** joint measurement-selection + outcome latent-factor model —
  models the probability of HbA1c measurement (anchor propensity) jointly with the mortality
  outcome so that the SHR coefficient is estimated conditional on the observation process.
  **What A1 reveals that B0/B1 cannot:** whether the SHR increment is identified once the
  selection into HbA1c testing is modelled, i.e. whether "independent SHR effect" survives an
  explicit selection model rather than an IPW adjustment. Approximate budget: CPU, EM/IRLS, <1 h.
- **Branch B (deferred alternative):** within-stay serial-glucose trajectory with a learned
  irregular-time model (GA/transformer), which would test whether the *dynamics* rather than the
  admission ratio carry the signal. Deferred this episode only for time; retained as a recorded
  alternative.

Decision used B0 vs B1 vs A1 as the primary contrast; not selected this episode for compute.

## 5. Supportive / adverse / inconclusive

- **Supportive of H-relative:** S5 passes (stay-order placebo in band) AND a positive S6 monotone
  association in normal/pre-diabetic strata AND S2 value-free control within the 0.005 margin.
- **Adverse for H-relative:** S5 fails, or S6 flat/inverse across strata, or S7 placebo ratio
  reproduces the increment — any one bounds the SHR increment as non-identifiable from the
  severity/measurement process.
- **Inconclusive:** underpowered HbA1c stratum (small n in the locked test split → predeclared
  power gate), or CI spanning both the null and the equivalence margin.
