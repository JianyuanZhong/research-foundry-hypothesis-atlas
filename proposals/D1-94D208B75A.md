# MIMIC AKI discordance: all-eligible landmark-survival and ascertainment repair

Status: substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. Design only. No
MIMIC cohort, outcome, model, or clinical result has been computed. The bounded
read-only availability check is recorded in `mimic-feasibility-check.md`; it found
the required archive members, headers, and dictionary bindings but did not estimate
row support.

## Scientific opening and claim boundary

The unresolved clinical question is whether a creatinine/urine/recorded-input
discordance in an AKI episode carries component-specific prognostic information
beyond pre-exposure severity and the processes that determine who remains alive,
remains in the index ICU, and receives qualifying measurements at a landmark. This
matters because an apparent renal signal could change how clinicians interpret
discordant creatinine and urine trajectories, but it could also be a severity or
surveillance artifact. The distinction affects whether the pattern is worth
prospective measurement validation or should instead be treated as a marker of
critical-illness workflow.

The strongest evidence-supported claims are bounded. [K1] reports, in its
abstract, that cystatin-C-inclusive equations can outperform creatinine-only
equations and may improve GFR estimation during muscle-mass change or critical
illness; MIMIC here has neither cystatin C nor adjudicated GFR. [K2] describes AKI
as a systemic syndrome associated with remote-organ dysfunction, so hospital death
and an ICU-recorded RRT transition should not be treated as a kidney-only endpoint.
[K3] provides a protocol example of comparing dynamic landmark survival with a
temporal sequence model under patient-level/temporal separation and calibration
evaluation. None establishes the proposed MIMIC association, a complete RRT
endpoint, a true fluid state, a mechanism, or a causal effect.

The falsifiable unresolved claim is narrower:

> At each L in {24,48,72} hours after a creatinine-defined t0, the observed
> component-specific discordance pattern changes the all-eligible probability of
> reaching that landmark state and then experiencing seven-day hospital death or
> ICU-recorded RRT, after the design explicitly records pre-landmark death, RRT,
> ICU exit, discharge, and missing observation opportunity. Among those who do
> reach a qualifying observed state, the parent’s conditional component contrast
> should remain directionally reproducible if it is not primarily an ascertainment
> artifact.

This is descriptive/prognostic and noncausal. Adjustment for recorded context does
not identify a treatment effect or mechanism.

## What changed from the parent

The parent’s main risk set began with patients alive in the index ICU at L who had a
qualifying observation opportunity. That is appropriate for a conditional landmark
contrast but makes survival, ICU exit, support, and observation selection secondary.
This child adds a pre-landmark flow from every t0-eligible episode and makes an
explicit `unknown` state for patients alive and still in the index ICU at L but
without the frozen qualifying opportunity. It does not impute a discordance to that
state, reassign patients after ICU exit, or silently replace ICU-RRT with hospital
RRT.

The new primary estimand incorporates gate probability; the parent conditional
component-specific estimands, exposure windows, states, outcomes, item sets, and
noncausal interpretation remain secondary and unchanged.

## Population and temporal boundaries

The index population E0 is MIMIC-IV adults with `hosp/patients.anchor_age >= 18`,
joined by `subject_id`, and an eligible first ICU stay joined to `hosp/admissions`
on `(subject_id,hadm_id)`.  Require finite `intime < outtime`,
`admittime <= intime`, `intime < dischtime`, and `outtime > admittime`. Clip
observable ICU follow-up at valid `dischtime` and retain an `outtime > dischtime`
exception flag. Within each admission, sort by `intime`, then `stay_id`, and keep
the first eligible stay; do not substitute a later stay when the first has no t0.
All admissions for a subject stay in one deterministic split.

Define t0 exactly with the parent’s frozen primary blood creatinine tuple
`hosp/labevents.itemid=50912`: finite `valuenum`, compatible declared
`valueuom`, clinical `charttime`, same-admission comparators, same-time duplicate
median, bounded blank-`hadm_id` assignment, and the prespecified pre-t0 RRT
exclusion set. `itemid=52546` remains sensitivity-only urine creatinine and is
never merged with 50912.

For L in {24,48,72}, let `Ltime=t0+L`,
`A_L=[max(t0,Ltime-24h),Ltime-6h)`, and
`W_L=[Ltime-6h,Ltime)`.  The three six-hour A bins, renal/urine state definitions,
support/order definitions, input precedence, and valid-opportunity rules are copied
from the parent’s frozen manifest without changing thresholds. Define the new
strictly pre-exposure window
`B_L=[max(t0,Ltime-24h)-6h,max(t0,Ltime-24h))`; it is truncated at the
index boundary and marked incomplete when necessary. No A, W, post-L, or storetime
value enters B.

## Primary all-eligible flow and estimand

For every E0 episode and each L, construct one mutually exclusive gate `G_L` by
the first event in `[t0,Ltime)`, using the parent’s half-open intervals and exact
tie precedence:

1. `death_preL`: valid hospital death before L;
2. `RRT_preL`: first exact-set ICU procedure RRT before L while alive;
3. `ICU_exit_preL`: index ICU exit before L while alive;
4. `discharge_preL`: hospital discharge before L without an earlier gate;
5. `administrative_invalid`: invalid/inconsistent boundary preventing a valid gate;
6. if none occurs, `alive_in_index_ICU_at_L` is split into:
   `observed_concordant`, each parent-defined observed discordance component,
   and `unknown_no_qualifying_observation`.

The unknown state includes a patient alive at L and still in the index ICU when the
A/W opportunity rule fails, including incomplete observation; it does not mean
normal physiology. A patient who exits the ICU, dies, has pre-L RRT, or is
discharged is not put in unknown and is not given a later state. Report all gate
counts and proportions over E0, including every unknown reason and the timing of
each pre-L transition.

For a component state s (concordant or one prespecified discordance component), let
`D_L(s)` be hospital death in `[Ltime,Ltime+7d)` and `R_L(s)` be the first
exact-set ICU-recorded RRT in the same window, strictly before clipped ICU exit and
while alive. Death, ICU exit, RRT, alive discharge, administrative censoring, and
exact ties follow the parent’s frozen precedence. Ward/post-ICU RRT is not imputed.

The primary all-eligible quantities are the joint pathway probabilities

`Q_D(L,s)=P_E0(G_L=s and D_L(s))` and
`Q_R(L,s)=P_E0(G_L=s and R_L(s))`.

The primary component contrasts are
`RD_D^all(L)=Q_D(L,discordant)-Q_D(L,concordant)` and
`RD_R^all(L)=Q_R(L,discordant)-Q_R(L,concordant)`, reported for each component
and L. They include the probability of surviving/remain-in-ICU/reaching observation
opportunity, so they answer a landmark-survival-and-ascertainment question rather
than hiding those gates in the denominator. Also report the factorization
`P_E0(G_L=s) x F_D(7d|G_L=s)` and its RRT analogue, with separate uncertainty;
this distinguishes a gate-selection difference from a post-landmark conditional
difference. The all-eligible contrast is not a causal effect.

The parent’s conditional estimands remain secondary and exact:
`RD_D(L,s)=F_D(7d|discordant,s)-F_D(7d|concordant,s)` and
`RD_R(L,s)=F_R(7d|discordant,s)-F_R(7d|concordant,s)`, where F is the observed
Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence within the same landmark risk set and frozen
support/order stratum. Report them only with the E0 gate table beside them. A
conditional contrast without a corresponding gate/unknown report is incomplete.

Estimate pre-L gates and post-L outcomes with cause-specific six-hour flow tables and
Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence, using the index admission/stay as the unit and
subject-cluster bootstrap intervals. For sparse cells (<50 E0 or risk-set subjects,
or <10 component events), label the estimate inconclusive and do not adaptively merge
cells. The solver must not infer the unknown component label or use outcome-derived
gate definitions.

## Pre-exposure severity and observation audit

In `B_L`, use the exact literal `icu/chartevents` six-vital panel
220045/220210/220277/220052/220181/223762 and the exact `hosp/d_labitems`
tuples in the binding audit: primary creatinine 50912 plus lactate 50813,
bicarbonate 50882, sodium 50983, bilirubin 50885, platelet 51265, WBC 51301,
and urea nitrogen 51006. Resolve labels, `linksto`, fluid/category, and declared
units fail-closed. Add the parent’s administered-input, procedure/support, renal
opportunity, and nonrenal density rules, using clinical times. Summaries are last,
valid count, physiologic range/worst direction where prespecified, duration and
missingness masks; they are observed context, not SOFA, true GFR, or total fluid
balance.

Report B severity/opportunity distributions by E0 gate, state, component, landmark,
and support/order category. Fit a fixed-context sensitivity with B summaries and
B opportunity added only from training subjects; never treat A/W density as
pre-exposure. A process-only B model with no renal discordance input predicts
`G_L` and the post-L component outcomes descriptively. A similar gate/unknown
gradient in this process-only model is evidence for ascertainment, not a negative
control outcome.

## Baseline and learned alternative

Both alternatives use exactly the same E0 rows, t0, L values, A/W/B windows,
gate labels, outcomes, masks, and deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 split seeded
`20261002); no subject’s admissions cross partitions. Training-derived cut points,
scalers, and missingness summaries are frozen before test evaluation. The required
scientific deliverable is newly estimated all-eligible gate probabilities,
component-specific Q contrasts and parent conditional contrasts, with held-out
calibration and uncertainty—not a completed result in this proposal.

The transparent fixed-summary baseline is primary. It uses prespecified t0/A summaries
(creatinine level/peak/slope, three urine bins and masks, accepted input and
composition, support/order, coverage and missingness) plus B context, opportunity,
demographics, and fixed ICU context. It fits separate pooled-logistic cause-specific
six-hour hazards for the pre-L gates and post-L death/RRT/exit transitions, recursively
produces gate probabilities and seven-day CIFs, and reports E0 flow calibration,
component calibration, integrated/time-dependent Brier score, predicted-versus-
observed competing-event counts, Q/RD contrasts, and 1,000 subject-cluster bootstrap
percentile intervals.

The matched learned alternative is a small two-stream temporal competing-flow model:
a renal stream for the A bins (creatinine, direct urine, masks, coverage and item
composition), and a support/observation stream for input, organ support, nonrenal
density, missingness, and ordering. B summaries and fixed demographic/ICU context
are static covariates. Discrete-time heads predict the pre-L gate states and,
conditional on each observed/unknown gate, the post-L death, ICU-RRT, ICU-exit,
alive-discharge and administrative transitions. It sees no W value, outcome,
post-L value, or future ICU movement. Required ablations are fixed-summary, B-only,
renal-only, support/observation-only, and full two-stream.

The temporal model can reveal within-A persistence and renal-versus-support ordering
that a fixed summary loses; the all-eligible gate head can reveal whether that
information predicts survival/ICU retention/observation itself rather than only the
conditional renal contrast. It cannot reveal a latent mechanism. A latent
state-space/GFR model is deferred because the available tables lack measured GFR,
cystatin C, muscle mass, tubular injury markers, adjudicated fluid state, treatment
intent, and complete ward/post-ICU RRT.

Retain the temporal model for scientific interpretation only if held-out gate and
component CIF calibration and Brier performance are stable, it changes a
component/order/B-context interpretation reproducibly at L=48 with directional
support at L=24 or 72, and the change is not solely an ICU-exit coding gain. A small
AUROC gain or opaque embedding alone is not information gain. CPU is the default:
archive scans, fixed summaries, flow estimates and bootstrap fit the planned
16-CPU/262,144-MiB/28,800-second solver envelope. The small temporal fit is planned
CPU-first; one allocated A100 is optional if readiness shows repeated sequence fits
materially benefit. These are planning estimates, not measured runtimes; eligible
counts, event support, sparse cells, feature size, convergence, and bootstrap time
remain unverified.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires: (i) a directionally consistent all-eligible component
contrast at L=48 with directional replication at L=24 or 72; (ii) the gate table
shows the result is not only a large discordance-associated death/exit/unknown
gradient; (iii) the conditional parent contrast persists in adequately supported
B-context strata; (iv) the process-only B opportunity diagnostic does not reproduce
the component pattern; (v) results are stable to the parent’s boundary/current-window,
storetime, excluded-item, support/order, and ICU-exit composition sensitivities; and
(vi) held-out gate/component calibration and uncertainty are acceptable. This
supports a reproducible prognostic association conditional on observed context and
an explicit landmark pathway. It does not support mechanism, true GFR, a treatment
effect, or causal inference.

Adverse evidence is a dominant pre-L death/ICU-exit/RRT/discharge or unknown gradient,
a joint contrast that vanishes after showing the gate factor, a conditional contrast
that disappears after B context, a comparable process-only opportunity gradient,
reversal across B completeness, instability across dictionary/temporal sensitivities,
or an ICU-exit gradient accounting for the RRT contrast. This redirects the
interpretation to severity or ascertainment; it does not prove normal filtration or
absence of benefit from RRT.

Inconclusive evidence is sparse E0 or landmark support, <10 component events,
high B incompleteness, inconsistent times, large administrative loss, wide
intervals, contradictory landmarks, or a learned improvement without stable
component-specific calibration and replication. An imprecise null is not a decisive
refutation.

Computationally checkable claims are source/member/schema binding, joins, split
integrity, t0/A/W/B ordering, gate precedence, flow counts, AJ/Q estimates, model
metrics, calibration, and uncertainty. Clinical adjudication or another study is
required for true GFR, total-body fluid balance, treatment intent, complete
hospital-wide RRT, goals of care, mechanism, causal effects, and transportability.

## Selection, deferral and completion

This repair is selected because it changes the scientific estimand in response to
the strongest rival—survival and observation selection—while preserving the
component-specific renal question. The fixed-summary model is primary because its
gate and Q decomposition is auditable. The temporal model is a matched alternative
because it can expose within-window ordering and incremental information; it is not
retained for a small predictive gain alone. A causal treatment model is deferred
because treatment intent and complete exposure are unavailable. A latent GFR/fluid
model is deferred because validating markers are absent. Revisit either only with
adjudicated physiologic markers, complete follow-up, or an external cohort.

Completion requires the solver to emit: the E0-to-gate flow table; unknown reasons
and observation-opportunity manifests; exact resolved item/unit lists; primary
all-eligible Q and RD contrasts at L={24,48,72}; parent conditional AJ contrasts;
pre-exposure severity/opportunity audit; ICU-exit/RRT transition table; baseline and
learned ablation predictions; held-out gate/component calibration, Brier scores,
competing-event counts, and subject-cluster uncertainty; and a conclusion explicitly
labeled supportive, adverse, or inconclusive and linked to those outputs.

This is an adaptation, not a reproduction. The three inspected works and their
claims/limits are frozen in [key-references.json](key-references.json), with attached
UTF-8 evidence excerpts [evidence-k1.txt](evidence-k1.txt),
[evidence-k2.txt](evidence-k2.txt), and [evidence-k3.txt](evidence-k3.txt).
