## Three-way interpretation and decisions

- **Supportive for internal validity only:** exact sample/assay mappings and six pairs agree, and a source team independently confirms reagent identity, dose/unit, schedule, cell compartment and FLS harvest. This validates the annotation chain, not MERTK specificity or therapeutic benefit. Continue only to the bounded, orthogonal functional test if an expert confirms a plausible non-toxic engagement range; retain the prior mixed RNA result.
- **Adverse to use of the archive as MERTK-target evidence:** reproducible matrix/design/SDRF key or pair mismatch, unresolved cell/readout conflict, or source adjudication finds dose/identity/protocol error. Repair data binding if mechanically resolvable; otherwise exclude the archival contrast from target-specific evidence and park the compound-based MERTK rationale pending independent perturbation. This does not refute MERTK biology.
- **Inconclusive:** internal metadata agree but no original lab records/author response, actual exposure, engagement, selectivity, wash/carry-over, or functional data exist; or donor-level functional uncertainty is wide. Keep “as annotated” wording and defer the target decision. A null imprecise functional result is not refutation.

Computationally checkable: column/sample joins, source field values, duplicate rows/mates, pairing and donor structure, protocol text concordance, and output-linked reporting. Not computationally established: actual reagent/dose used, cell purity, target engagement, mechanism, therapeutic window, patient benefit/safety. Those need source-team/expert adjudication, fresh orthogonal loss/rescue and functional studies, then independent translational/clinical evidence. The exact advance over prior association/expression knowledge [K1]–[K3] is a falsifiable barrier test preventing an unresolved annotation from being promoted as target-specific pharmacology; it does not claim a new biological mechanism.
