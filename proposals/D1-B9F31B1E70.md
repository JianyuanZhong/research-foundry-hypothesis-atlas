# Episode 78 successor: capacity-robust seven-day optional-lactate prioritization over the inherited 10%–20% workload range

## Lead audit, unresolved decision, and substantive advance

The frozen parent is internally valid: it uses the correct landmark-relative horizon `H7=L24+10080=t0+11520`, keeps every member of the audited all-baseline/L24 cohort, does not treat discharge after H7 as censoring, excludes post-L24 predictors, compares equal within-hospital queues, and uses the correct paired substitution denominator. Its strongest remaining clinical decision limitation is capacity dependence. Ten percent is an unelicited operational anchor, while the parent's 20% analysis is only a weak stress: a 20% result whose point estimate is adverse and whose interval is wide could still pass because support requires only that its upper bound be nonnegative. A hospital therefore cannot tell whether the prognostic ranking signal is stable when review capacity expands within the already contemplated 10%–20% range.

This successor changes exactly that decision boundary. It freezes three capacity regimes, `Q={0.10,0.15,0.20}`, before outcome modeling; uses one set of hospital-held-out D7 scores at all three regimes; makes the equal-weight capacity-standardized contrast the sole primary estimand; and requires joint simultaneous nonnegative lower bounds at every regime. The 10% and 20% endpoints are inherited, and 15% is the prespecified midpoint. No horizon, capacity, model, stratum, or swap yield may be selected after seeing outcomes or used to rescue the primary result.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the frozen 2,302-person, 30-hospital EICU all-baseline/L24 cohort, the equal-weight average of the D7 `R_full-R_attention` fixed-slot contrasts at 10%, 15%, and 20% within-hospital capacity is at least +0.03 deaths per slot: its copied-hospital 95% lower bound is at least +0.03; simultaneous lower bounds are nonnegative at all three capacities; capacity-standardized low-availability, high-availability, and equal-hospital macro lower bounds are nonnegative; deletion stability passes; and the same D7-fitted queue substitutions have nonnegative capacity-standardized and simultaneous eventual hospital-death capture lower bounds.

The +0.03 margin means three additional source-defined D7 in-hospital deaths captured per 100 queued positions under the frozen three-regime standardization. It is inherited, not a stakeholder utility. The result would be prognostic and comparative only. It cannot show that a review occurred, that a death was preventable, that lactate was visible at bedside, or that any patient benefited.

## Capacity-standardized fixed-slot estimand

Freeze `Q={0.10,0.15,0.20}`. For hospital h with frozen size `n_h`, capacity q has `k_h(q)=ceil(q*n_h)`. The top `k_h(q)` ranks form `Q_m,h(q)`; queues are nested within model and hospital because ranking is fixed. Let `K(q)=sum_h k_h(q)`.

The outcome-blind capacity audit gives:

- q=0.10: K=243, realized pooled fraction 10.56%, hospital quotas 4–17;
- q=0.15: K=359, realized pooled fraction 15.60%, hospital quotas 6–26;
- q=0.20: K=472, realized pooled fraction 20.50%, hospital quotas 8–34.

For outcome `o in {7,inf}`, define `S_m,h^o(q)=sum_{i in Q_m,h(q)} D_i^o`, `P_m,h^o(q)=S_m,h^o(q)/k_h(q)`, and

`Delta_o(q) = [sum_h S_full,h^o(q)-sum_h S_attention,h^o(q)]/K(q)`.

The sole primary estimand is the equal-regime capacity-standardized D7 contrast

`A7 = [Delta_7(0.10)+Delta_7(0.15)+Delta_7(0.20)]/3`.

Report `100*A7` as additional D7 in-hospital deaths captured per 100 queued positions under this three-regime standardization. Equal weighting is a frozen analytical standardization, not an estimate of real hospitals' capacity distribution. Never pool numerators and denominators across q as a substitute; doing so would overweight larger capacities.

The same-queue eventual guard is

`Ainf = [Delta_inf(0.10)+Delta_inf(0.15)+Delta_inf(0.20)]/3`.

These are the identical D7-fitted rankings and assignments evaluated with Dinf, not eventual-outcome-trained policies.

For portability define at each q:

`Delta_macro,7(q)=mean_h[P_full,h^7(q)-P_attention,h^7(q)]`

and pooled slot contrasts restricted to frozen low- and high-F-availability hospitals. Their equal-q averages are `A7_macro`, `A7_low`, and `A7_high`. These distinguish equal-hospital and process-context transport within the snapshot; they do not establish external transportability.

## Paired substitution algebra at every capacity

For each q and h define `Add_h(q)=Q_full,h(q)\Q_attention,h(q)`, `Remove_h(q)=Q_attention,h(q)\Q_full,h(q)`, `s_h(q)=|Add_h(q)|=|Remove_h(q)|`, `M(q)=sum_h s_h(q)`, and `rho(q)=M(q)/K(q)`.

For outcome o define

`G_o(q)=sum_h[sum_{i in Add_h(q)}D_i^o-sum_{i in Remove_h(q)}D_i^o]`

and, if `M(q)>0`, `Psi_o(q)=G_o(q)/M(q)`. Enforce separately at every q and outcome:

`Delta_o(q)=G_o(q)/K(q)=rho(q)*Psi_o(q)`, with `|Delta_o(q)|<=rho(q)`.

If `M(q)=0`, G and Delta must be zero and Psi missing. Save K, M, rho, G7, Delta7, Psi7, Ginf, Deltainf, Psiinf, addition/removal event proportions, hospitals with substitutions, and `s_h/k_h` distributions at each q. A denominator of `2M` is wrong. There is deliberately no single integrated swap yield for A7 or Ainf: averaging G or Psi across capacities does not reproduce the equal-q average of G/K. Swap yields are descriptive and cannot rescue or veto the fixed-slot decision.
