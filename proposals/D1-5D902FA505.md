# Outcome-independent contact anchoring for postoperative HCC surveillance selection

Status: substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. A bounded aggregate audit repaired the parent’s outcome-derived opportunity definition and verified support for encounter-only anchors, target-only weeks, and common target/control risk sets. No hypothesis model has been fitted. The biological recurrence route remains frozen.

## Scientific opening, supported claim, unresolved claim, and advance

The strongest evidence-supported clinical claim is that, in 549 selected resected HCC patients with preoperative AFP >400 ng/mL, incomplete AFP response measured at a 4–6-week postoperative visit was associated with worse recurrence-free and overall survival [K1]. This does not establish that persistent AFP is residual tumor, that follow-up AFP availability is ignorable, or that changing surveillance improves outcomes.

Routine EHR observation is generated by care: in 669,452 patients at two hospitals, the presence and timing of many common laboratory orders were strongly associated with survival independently of recorded values [K2]. A methodological review distinguishes patient presence from longitudinal observation, describes explicit observation-process and latent-structure methods, and warns that prediction and explanation require different interpretations [K3]. These works support treating observation as data; they do not identify why a specific HCC patient was measured.

The unresolved claim tested here is: among adults at a day-60 landmark after first observed HCC resection, pre-landmark marker history is associated with subsequent HCC-targeted measurement or imaging beyond an independently recorded institutional encounter process and measured severity, and that association is stronger than the same marker-history association with comparable generic laboratory and nonhepatic-imaging outcomes. The leading explanation is residual target-specific surveillance selection. The strongest rival is ordinary local contact/severity: patients with more recorded encounters or greater illness burden receive all tests, without a distinct HCC-targeting process.

This matters because biomarker studies conditioned on observed follow-up can select a highly monitored subgroup and overstate transportability. The parent attempted to address this but defined generic opportunity G partly from non-marker labs and nonhepatic imaging while also using those same events as negative-control outcomes. Conditioning on G could therefore select deterministically on modeled outcomes, induce collider bias, and omit target-only weeks. This child’s substantive advance is an outcome-independent at-risk process: every eligible patient contributes the same 44-week grid; encounter contact comes only from the encounters table; no target or control event creates eligibility or contact; and targets and controls use identical denominators and feature bases.

This is an ascertainment study. It does not test biological recurrence or a treatment effect.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Primary hypothesis: on the fixed day-61:365 patient-week grid, high/persistently elevated versus low/clearing pre-landmark marker trajectory is associated with greater standardized cumulative incidence of:

1. AFP/PIVKA-II/AFP-L3 measurement (L-M) relative to sodium/creatinine/hemoglobin measurement (N-L); and/or
2. hepatic/upper-abdominal CT/MR (S) relative to nonhepatic CT/MR (N-I),

after adjustment for an outcome-independent encounter anchor A, lagged encounter history, calendar and measured severity.

A is not a restriction. For every patient-week, A(t)=1 only if an encounters-table interval from admission/visit/start through valid discharge overlaps the week; when an interval cannot be formed, the admission, else visit, else discharge fallback date lies in the week. A start-date-only version is prespecified sensitivity A0. Labs, examinations, procedures, medications, L-M, S, treatment T, N-L and N-I never define A.

All eligible patients are operationally at risk for a *recorded local-system mark* in every one of 44 weeks from day 61 through day 365, regardless of A and regardless of previous marks. There is no death field, complete outside-care capture, or enrollment-end field, so “no mark” means no mark in these institutional sources—not alive, disease-free, or absent from care. This fixed local-capture estimand is preferable to censoring at last contact, which would again make follow-up depend on observed care.

Let H be the train-scaled marker-history exposure among marker-observed patients: persistent/high versus low/clearing, prespecified from preoperative rank and latest day-15:60/preoperative log ratio; continuous rank and ratio are primary model inputs, and the dichotomy is used for standardized contrasts. Do not pool raw values across labels or eras because lab units and assay platform are unavailable. Marker-observed versus marker-absent is a separate full-cohort ascertainment-state contrast; no biomarker value is imputed for absent patients.

For mark k, estimate standardized 365-day risk (R_k(H)) by g-computation over the empirical covariate and observed A histories, using history through t− only except contemporaneous A. The two primary specificity estimands are:

- laboratory specificity: Δ_lab = [R_L-M(high)−R_L-M(low)] − [R_N-L(high)−R_N-L(low)];
- imaging specificity: Δ_img = [R_S(high)−R_S(low)] − [R_N-I(high)−R_N-I(low)].

Also report ratio-of-risk-ratios, each component risk and cluster-bootstrap 95% intervals. These are standardized descriptive associations, not causal effects of marker level or testing. Treatment T and injury-panel measurement L-I are secondary marks; T intent is not inferred from occurrence.

## Frozen split and common preprocessing

Assign patients by `int(SHA256(患者主索引)[:8],16) mod 100`: 0–59 train, 60–69 validation, 70–79 locked test, 80–99 untouched reserve. Freeze cohort, event dictionaries, A construction, transformations, exposure thresholds, gates and model-selection rules before test access. Reserve is not used for fitting or selection. Both methods receive the identical 44-week grid, patients, dated marks, covariates and split.

Every mark is binary within week; multiple same-mark rows collapse to one, while different marks may co-occur. Features use information through t−: lagged A counts and recency, lagged mark counts/recency, baseline pathology/severity, age, sex, year, service, index length of stay, prior treatment, pre-landmark injury trajectory, marker-observation state, exact marker label/specimen, and medication flags. Contemporaneous A is allowed because it comes from independent encounter fields, but results must be repeated with lagged-A-only and start-date-only A0. No N-L or N-I outcome enters another outcome’s feature set at t.

## Matched transparent alternative

Fit one stacked discrete-time recurrent-mark model on the full grid with mark-type indicators for L-M, S, N-L and N-I, a common spline of week, common covariate basis, mark-specific intercepts, A/current and lagged-A interactions, and marker-history × mark-type interactions. Use elastic-net regularization chosen in validation, then refit the selected basis with GEE or cluster-robust estimation; use patient-cluster bootstrap for standardized risks and paired specificity intervals. Fit A itself in a separate pooled-logistic model using only baseline variables and lagged A; this describes institutional contact and is not used for inverse-probability selection.

Report Δ_lab, Δ_img, ratio-of-risk-ratios, all component risks, calibration-in-the-large, calibration slope, Brier score and log loss by mark and split. Secondary models add L-I and T with the same grid. Also report the encounter-choice estimand among A=1 weeks, but never substitute it for the primary grid result.

This baseline directly exposes whether the same marker-history contrast differs between target and control outcomes. It loses irregular within-week ordering, continuous coupling of evolving values and observation, and residual patient-level contact heterogeneity.

## Matched learned/mechanistic alternative

Fit an anchored discrete-time multivariate state-space marked-process model on the identical grid:

- observed A is an emission anchoring latent generic-contact state U with fixed positive loading +1;
- U may affect every mark; its transition uses baseline variables and lagged A only;
- target-selection state V has loading +1 on L-M, exactly zero on A, N-L and N-I, and estimated loadings on S and secondary T;
- injury-recovery state W uses dated ALT/AST/bilirubin/INR positively and albumin/platelets negatively, with label/era offsets;
- marker state M uses within-label ranks/ratios for AFP and optional PIVKA-II/AFP-L3 and is never called tumor;
- the weekly mark vector is multilabel, so A=1 target-only, control-only, co-marked and A=0 target-only weeks all contribute without deterministic conditioning;
- all transitions and emissions use only t− history except observed contemporaneous A.

Compare U-only with U+V. The alternative can reveal whether residual covariance is concentrated in HCC-targeted marks and preserve evolving irregular histories that the baseline compresses. It cannot identify clinician intent, outside care, or tumor biology. Evaluate held-out marked-process log likelihood, target/control Brier and calibration, posterior predictive counts in all six observed week states, loading/seed stability and simulation recovery. Require three seeds, U/V recovery correlation ≥0.8, no factor swap, and 90–98% interval coverage in prespecified null, target-specific and shared-contact simulations. Select U+V only if it reduces validation targeted-mark deviance by ≥1% relative to U-only without reducing control-mark deviance by ≥1%, remains calibrated, and passes simulation/seed gates. Otherwise prefer the transparent baseline.

Planned future-solver resources: transparent model 8–16 CPUs, 64 GiB, 1–2 hours; state-space model one allocated A100, 16 CPUs, 128 GiB, 4–6 hours, with checkpoints. These are unverified planning estimates within the 16-CPU/8-GPU/256-GiB/8-hour solver envelope. The measured discovery audit used 4 CPUs/32 GiB for 50.3 seconds. A CPU hierarchical recurrent-event model is the fallback if GPU dependencies fail; infrastructure failure is not scientific evidence.

A generic transformer is deferred because prediction gain alone would not distinguish shared contact from target selection. Inverse-intensity weighting is deferred as primary because no model can recover outside care or death absent from the sources.

## Gates, falsification, and interpretation

Before final fitting:

- retain ≥20 patient-level occurrences of each primary mark in validation and test (observed patient support passes: L-M 145/145, S 48/43, N-L 154/155, N-I 47/35);
- report all A/mark cross-cells by split, including the 14 audited A=0 target-only weeks; never drop or recode them;
- require ≥100 effective patients per exposure contrast after standardization; otherwise narrow to continuous association or declare the dichotomous contrast inconclusive;
- audit ≥100 sampled laboratory labels and ≥100 examination titles, blinded to marker exposure, with PPV/NPV ≥0.90 for each target/control dictionary;
- for T interpretation, require ≥100 medication-positive and ≥100 oncology-like dictionary-negative snippets reviewed by pharmacy/hepatobiliary experts with PPV/NPV ≥0.85; otherwise T remains unclassified treatment occurrence;
- verify no target/control field contributes to A and no current/future outcome leaks into predictors.

A clinically material specificity signal is prespecified as Δ ≥2 absolute percentage points in standardized 365-day risk and ratio-of-risk-ratios ≥1.25.

**Supportive:** at least one Holm-adjusted target/control pair has lower 95% bounds above zero for Δ and above 1 for the ratio-of-risk-ratios, point estimates meet both materiality thresholds, direction replicates in locked test, calibration is acceptable (absolute calibration error ≤0.02), and permutation of marker history within era/observation stratum removes the specificity. This supports a residual target-specific *recording/selection association* in this institution. Stable U+V improvement adds evidence that the dependence is longitudinally concentrated in target marks; it is not required to call the transparent association supportive and never makes it causal.

**Adverse:** for both pairs, upper 95% bounds exclude Δ=0.02 and ratio-of-risk-ratios=1.25, marker-history associations are no greater for targets than controls, and U-only predicts held-out target marks as well as U+V within the 1% deviance threshold. This supports ordinary contact/severity as the adequate observed explanation and argues against a clinically material target-specific selection signal.

**Inconclusive:** intervals include clinically material positive specificity; dictionaries fail review; effective support fails; test direction reverses; A construction is materially inconsistent with linked timestamps; assay-era sensitivity reverses results; calibration fails; U/V simulation or stability fails when making latent claims; or A=0 marks are too sparse for the lagged-A/start-only sensitivities. An imprecise null is not adverse evidence. Baseline support with failed U/V recovery permits only the transparent association claim.

Negative-control falsification is built into the paired estimands. Additional train-only marker-history permutation must erase target specificity while preserving generic contact structure. A V loading on A, N-L or N-I is fixed at zero by design; posterior predictive excess concentrated in controls, or comparable U+V control improvement, falsifies the intended target-specific interpretation.

## Alternatives retained and revisit triggers

- Parent outcome-derived G: rejected because N-L/N-I both created opportunity and served as outcomes.
- A=1-only choice model: retained as secondary; rejected as primary because it omits 14 A=0 target-only weeks and changes the estimand to within-recorded-encounter choice.
- Fixed grid plus encounter-only A: selected because opportunity is independent of all outcomes and target/control denominators are identical.
- Transparent stacked model: selected as the primary completed analysis if learned recovery fails.
- Anchored U+V model: selected as the substantive alternative only after simulation recovery and target-specific held-out improvement.
- Orders-based opportunity: revisit only after order-status and order-purpose adjudication demonstrates a generic, outcome-independent scheduled-contact signal.
- Four-state measurement multinomial: remains rejected for nonpositivity (marker-only 0; neither 54).
- Biological recurrence route: remains frozen until the inherited adjudicated-report gate or prospective surveillance data is available.
