## Question and clinical importance

Among adults recorded as invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48, with recent but no immediate vasopressor support, positive recorded fluid balance, adequate recorded MAP/urine/potassium, and no active RRT/ECMO or recent loop course, is initiation or restart of a qualifying furosemide/bumetanide input record during the next 12 hours associated with a higher probability of being alive and not occupying a recorded MIMIC ICU at day 7, without exceeding prespecified recorded renal, RRT, death, or terminal-disposition harm margins?

This matters because de-resuscitation could facilitate transition out of intensive care, but treatment selection may also mark patients already improving; excessive removal could accompany renal injury, dialysis, or death. A day-7 ICU-topology milestone is consequential for resource use and patient flow while avoiding an invented utility ordering among home, rehabilitation, skilled nursing, hospice, LTAC, or acute-hospital destinations.

The strongest available evidence supports only that this MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains sufficiently structured ICU stays, admissions, transfers, and death/discharge fields to attempt a recorded-topology reconstruction, and that broad outcome-blind audits found substantial destination heterogeneity and near-complete determinable day-7 topology in the broad V0 cohort (5,745/5,752 in the inherited audit). The paused-loop audit found 2,741 positive typed loop segments ending in `Paused` among 117,354 discovery-partition rows. These are feasibility and semantic observations, not treatment-effect evidence.

Current public evidence is context only. Gisca et al. (2026, PMCID `PMC13007817`, acquired Europe PMC source [source checksum]) reports observational associations of cumulative fluid balance with ICU mortality and respiratory/renal outcomes but says causal inference cannot be established. Boyle et al. (2026, PMCID `PMC13424680`, acquired source [source checksum]) discusses endpoint tradeoffs and the need to distinguish mortality from morbidity. Neither validates this exposure, population, MIMIC coding, or endpoint.

The unresolved hypothesis is deliberately narrower:

> In the repaired primary population, the equal-clone, pause-aware recorded-loop initiation contrast A12 minus B12 has a 95% bootstrap lower confidence limit above +0.05 for the lower partial-identification contrast in D7-RINO, with all validity gates, exposure-semantic checks, endpoint sensitivities, component-coherence checks, and prespecified harm margins passing.

This is an association under assignment-compatible recorded follow-up, not an intervention effect. The substantive advance is endpoint interpretability: D7-RINO asks only about death-penalized recorded ICU nonoccupancy at a fixed clock time. Destination labels cannot change its value. They remain transparent overlays and components, so hospice or rehabilitation is not silently treated as equivalent to home, and a favorable aggregate cannot be narrated as recovery.

## D7-RINO v2 state machine

Set `D7=t0+7 days`. Scan all `hosp/admissions` for the same `subject_id` through D7; later admissions affect outcomes only. Join each admission to `hosp/transfers` on `subject_id,hadm_id`, using `intime <= D7 < outtime`; corroborate ICU status using `icu/icustays` on `subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id` with `intime <= D7 < outtime`. Invalid times and unseen careunits are never inferred.

Death precedence is explicit. Any exact admission `deathtime <= D7` is `[0,0]`. A consistent `hospital_expire_flag=1` or `discharge_location=DIED` on an admission closed by D7 is also `[0,0]`. A patients `dod` date is the interval [local date 00:00, next local date 00:00): wholly before D7 is failure, straddling D7 is unknown, and beginning after D7 is not death by D7. A conflicting death indicator is unknown unless exact `deathtime <= D7` exists.

After death resolution, `[1,1]` (RINO success) requires either (a) exactly one active same-subject admission with exactly one valid active transfer whose careunit is in the frozen non-ICU list and exactly zero corroborating active ICU stays, or (b) no active same-subject admission, the index admission closed by D7, and no death/boundary-compatible death. This intentionally treats out-of-system nonfatal closure as “no recorded ICU occupancy,” not as recovery. `[0,0]` requires one active admission with one valid ICU transfer and exactly one corroborating active ICU stay, with no conflict. All other paths are `[0,1]): multiple active admissions or transfers, absent/overlapping/malformed topology, transfer–ICU disagreement, unknown/PACU/Nursery careunit, unresolved death/disposition conflict, or date-only DOD straddling D7.

Report mutually exclusive state counts: definite death, in-system ICU, in-system non-ICU, out-of-system after nonfatal closure, and unknown with reasons. Report every exact index `discharge_location` separately and destination overlays (community; facility/rehabilitation; hospice/LTAC/acute hospital; unresolved) only as descriptive components. Destination is prohibited from the primary endpoint. Retain AIFD7 as a subordinate composition diagnostic, not a co-primary: it is reported with its old frozen categories, but any destination-dependent shift is endpoint-composition evidence, never evidence for D7-RINO. Retain the sustained-topology sensitivity requiring continuous non-ICU coverage and no ICU interval during `(D7-48h,D7]`, plus parent ARDL7 and RHA-E48-D5 as orthogonal airway comparators.

## Analysis, baselines, and falsification

The estimand is the E_R equal-clone A12-minus-B12 bounded D7-RINO recorded association interval. Fit the inherited five subject-fold cross-fitted arm-specific L2 logistic compatibility models at nondeterministic clone-hours, C=1 with frozen C=0.1 fallback. Use only frozen pre-hour demographics, unit/era, completed-admission history, repaired loop/pressor history, MAP/urine/balance, creatinine/lactate/potassium, FiO2/PEEP/SpO2/mode/sedation, crystalloid/albumin, and missingness. Forbid `outtime, dischtime, deathtime, last_careunit`, post-t0 transfers/location, X, endpoint components, and any outcome. Use numerator-free inverse compatibility weights, clipping [0.01,0.99], pooled first/99th percentile truncation, deterministic weight 1, and Hajeck bounds:
`p_aL=sum(wL)/sum(w)`, `p_aU=sum(wU)/sum(w)`, `DeltaL=p_A,L-p_B,U`, `DeltaU=p_A,U-p_B,L`.

Run 1,999 successful PCG64 seed 480048 subject bootstraps, at most 2,499 attempts and <=5% failures, rebuilding the entire pipeline. Apply centered one-sided bootstrap p-values and Holm FWER 0.05 across D7-RINO, RCR48, RCRD48, RRT7, RRTD7, RTHD28, true mortality, and ANHATD28. Apply minima independently to E_R and E_M_minus_V0: N>=200, A12 satisfaction>=100, B12 completion/shared closure>=100, ESS>=75 per arm, at least 100 definite RINO successes and 100 definite failures, weighted unknown<=0.10 in each arm and absolute arm unknown difference<=0.05.

Mandatory baselines/falsifiers are index-admission-only RINO, sustained-topology RINO, subordinate AIFD7, full legacy Paused-ambiguous exposure reconstruction, and frozen balance/pressor variants. A material endpoint conflict is a bound shift >=0.05, midpoint sign reversal with both absolute midpoints >=0.02, a favorable component contrast <=-0.03 while the primary is supportive, or adverse-transfer disposition exceeding its frozen +0.03 margin. A material exposure conflict is a category change, bound shift >=0.05, or similarly defined sign reversal. Also report positivity, calibration, balance, all ambiguity/attrition reasons, later-admission linkage, raw and weighted states/destinations, bounds, ESS, confidence limits, p-values, Holm decisions, and bootstrap failures.

## Evidence limits and verification

Computationally checkable claims include archive/catalog/schema hashes, actual sampling and filtering, all joins and half-open clocks, V0 uniqueness/C0, Paused handling, clone equality, predictor exclusion, death precedence, RINO state reconciliation, destination neutrality, bounds, bootstrap/Holm arithmetic, minima, sensitivity branches, and conclusion-to-output consistency. Adversarial fixtures must include identical primary RINO values with HOME/SNF/REHAB/HOSPICE/LTAC/ACUTE HOSPITAL overlays; index discharge followed by same-subject ICU readmission active at D7; open ward versus ICU; exact death versus same-date DOD; missing/unseen/overlapping topology; Paused end-time and positive-gap cases; and correct numerical outputs paired with unsupported recovery, survival-after-discharge, destination-quality, airway, receipt, safety, causal, equivalence, policy, or recommendation claims.

Automation cannot establish actual medication receipt/route, clinician intent, true congestion or shock resolution, functional recovery, destination appropriateness, actual ventilator/extubation state, KDIGO AKI or renal safety, goals of care, ICU use/death outside linked MIMIC, exchangeability, causality, transportability, or actionability. No treatment effect or A-versus-B outcome contrast is estimated in this proposal.
