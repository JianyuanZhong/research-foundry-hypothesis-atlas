# Episode 19 provisional child: discordant AKI recovery after a severity-and-observation stress test

Status: provisional, evidence-bounded; no cohort counts, model estimates, or clinical conclusions are claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adults with an ICU stay and a prespecified creatinine-defined AKI episode who survive and remain observed at a fixed 48-hour landmark, persistent creatinine improvement without concurrent urine-output improvement will predict subsequent renal deterioration or in-hospital death beyond a deliberately strong current-state model. The key unresolved claim is whether the discordance label retains incremental prognostic information after matching/adjusting for current severity, treatment state, and observation intensity.

This is an incremental prognostic hypothesis, not a causal treatment effect, mechanism, or proof of true renal recovery. The strongest rival is that discordance is a severity/monitoring artifact: sicker patients have more measurements, more diuretics/vasopressors/RRT, and more clinician-driven urine documentation, while creatinine itself can be distorted by changing muscle mass, perfusion, and fluid balance.

## Evidence boundary

The inherited branch’s inspected works support the measurement concern and an operational creatinine/urine-output definition, not the proposed association. [K1] supports the concern that creatinine-based kidney-function assessment may be inaccurate in critical illness; [K2] supports a prespecified creatinine/urine-output operational definition while highlighting implementation gaps; [K3] bounds interpretation of subsequent CRRT initiation as potentially clinician-driven rather than an objective indication. None establishes the discordance association, prevalence, measured GFR/cystatin C, fluid-status adjudication, or treatment benefit.

## The substantive Episode 19 advance

The parent’s baseline and trajectory comparison are retained, but the decisive comparison is now a severity/observation stress test rather than a generic adjustment:

1. Baseline B0: a landmark model using current creatinine, creatinine slope, urine-output summary, AKI stage, age/sex, severity, treatment, and measurement intensity.
2. Strong rival B1: B0 plus a prespecified observation/severity nuisance model. Fit the probability and density of urine and creatinine measurements from pre-landmark chart/output/lab timestamps, and include measurement counts, gaps, time since last measurement, ICU length of stay, organ-support proxies, and treatment intensity. Use inverse-observation weighting or a missingness/observation-intensity sensitivity analysis, not a post-landmark variable.
3. Substantive alternative A1: a compact trajectory model using pre-landmark creatinine and urine-output sequences (slopes, nadir, persistence, and co-movement), with identical patient splits, held-out temporal evaluation, calibration, discrimination, uncertainty, and the same observation/severity nuisance adjustment.

The alternative can reveal whether persistence and ordering contain information lost by a cross-sectional mismatch label. It is deferred if sequence support is inadequate. CPU is adequate for both; GPU use is not required unless the trajectory implementation becomes matrix-intensive.

## Falsification and interpretation

Support requires a prespecified directionally consistent association and reproducible incremental calibration/discrimination in held-out data, with uncertainty and sensitivity analyses showing it is not explained solely by missing urine measurements, diuretic/RRT exposure, or current severity. Adverse results are no incremental signal, reversal, or instability after these adjustments. Inconclusive results include sparse discordant cells, inadequate follow-up, or wide uncertainty. None supports mechanism or treatment benefit.

## Missing evidence and next checks

The association, eligible cohort size, and measurement density remain untested. Measured GFR/cystatin C, reliable fluid-status adjudication, complete urine measurement, and clinical adjudication of AKI/recovery and RRT indication are unavailable or incomplete. Cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid because timestamps are subject-specific shifts. No numerical result is claimed.
