# Pre-repeat-TACE hepatic state: an exact-clock, event-selected laboratory-trajectory experiment

## Decision and substantive advance

A complete-source audit falsifies a clinically clean post-TACE2 hepatic laboratory window as computable in this snapshot. After preserving the frozen common-TACE2 cohort and requiring paired pre-TACE2 exact albumin/total-bilirubin values, a candidate follow-up had to be strictly before the first later strict TACE or non-bevacizumab systemic record, more than seven calendar days from every strict TACE, and in an encounter containing neither any procedure nor an ontology-matched systemic record. Joint exact-assay support was 0 systemic-record versus 4 comparator patients on days 15–29, 1 versus 8 on days 15–45, 1 versus 12 on days 15–60, and 1 versus 16 on days 30–90. No clean-window arm comparison is estimable; widening these windows after seeing attrition would not repair the observation process.

The strongest distinct computable alternative is therefore an explicitly event-anchored question: **among patients selected for a first recorded repeat TACE on TACE2 days 15–90, do the frozen days-1–14 systemic-record and TACE2-only pathways differ in within-person hepatic laboratory trajectory from pre-TACE2 baseline to the latest joint albumin/total-bilirubin panel strictly before that repeat procedure?** The primary pre-event panel must be in the repeat-procedure encounter, at one common timestamp, and within 72 hours before the exact procedure clock. Complete-source support is 55 systemic versus 186 comparator patients; a seven-day sensitivity adds no systemic patients and 10 comparator patients (55 versus 196).

This differs from the existing repeat-TACE acute-perturbation candidate `[prior hypothesis]`: that experiment contrasts pre- with post-repeat-procedure assays to study immediate admission-episode perturbation. This child stops strictly before the repeat procedure and tests the hepatic state in which an observed retreatment proceeds. It addresses the parents' temporal-ordering defect but does not pretend to recover routine outpatient reserve: the measurement is admission-driven, and both repeat-TACE selection and panel observation are post-baseline selection events. The result can inform whether a pre-retreatment laboratory trajectory is worth prospective monitoring; it cannot estimate medication effects, TACE toxicity, treatment eligibility, or comparative benefit.

The feasibility audit emitted descriptive raw values as a quality check, so design selection was not outcome-blinded. The endpoint was chosen for temporal interpretability and prespecified support, not direction. Unadjusted medians were mildly adverse for the systemic-record arm (albumin change -1 versus 0; bilirubin change +0.8 versus -0.15 in the 72-hour joint cohort), but these are selected, unadjusted diagnostics and are not evidence for the hypothesis.

## Clinical question, supported evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

### Clinical importance

Hepatic laboratory state at the time another TACE is undertaken is consequential for procedural planning and surveillance. The inherited day-30–90 outcome cannot represent a clean longitudinal state because every paired panel is admission-linked and most are near or after subsequent care events. A strictly preprocedure event anchor provides an honest alternative: it can determine whether patients returning for repeat TACE have measurably different albumin and bilirubin trajectories according to their frozen early recorded pathway, while preserving exact temporal ordering.

A useful signal would motivate prospective validation of pre-retreatment monitoring and adjudication of why patients with particular trajectories proceeded to repeat TACE. An adverse or null result is equally useful. This database alone cannot determine whether the repeat procedure was clinically appropriate, whether medication was administered, or whether laboratory differences influenced the decision.

### Strongest claim already supported

Using HCC snapshot `[source checksum]`, the audit scanned every row of the five required sources without sampling: 105,044 encounters, 1,810,646 diagnoses, 338,040 procedures, 4,097,517 medications, and 28,159,928 laboratory rows. It reproduced 78,067 strict textual TACE matches, 72,273 with valid procedure time, 38,742 patient-day episodes, 6,589 earliest adjacent qualifying pairs, 6,586 HCC/adult pairs, 2,992 era/day-14-observation-qualified patients, 1,107 prior-systemic exclusions, 319 systemic-record patients, 1,491 ambiguity-excluded TACE2-only patients, 8 bevacizumab-only ambiguities, and 67 generic-procedure ambiguities. There were joint pre-TACE2 albumin/bilirubin baselines in 172 versus 805 patients.

A first later strict TACE on TACE2 days 15–90 was recorded in 159/319 systemic versus 526/1,491 comparator patients (49.84% versus 35.28%). Among those events, 55 versus 186 patients had a paired pre-TACE2 baseline and a same-encounter, same-timestamp joint panel within 72 hours strictly before the event. The selected panel preceded the procedure by a median 11.46 hours (IQR 6.55–25.07) versus 7.51 hours (IQR 5.79–21.34); event days had medians 54 versus 55.5. Under a seven-day boundary, support was 55 versus 196; all systemic and 186/196 comparator panels were within 72 hours. These are feasibility, timing, and selection facts, not estimates of treatment effect.

The same audit showed why alternatives must remain contextual. The first post-landmark admission-presentation panel in the first 24 hours and before any timestamped in-encounter procedure or matched systemic record was available in 67 versus 274 patients. A pre-later-systemic-record panel within 72 hours was available in 59 versus 40. These anchors can test event specificity, but neither represents routine surveillance.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

Conditional on a recorded first strict repeat TACE on days 15–90 and observation of the locked pre-event joint panel, the frozen days-1–14 systemic-record pathway is associated with a different within-person change in exact albumin and total bilirubin from pre-TACE2 baseline to immediately before repeat TACE than the frozen TACE2-only pathway.

A clinically favorable direction is a smaller albumin decline without a larger bilirubin increase; a larger albumin decline and/or bilirubin increase is adverse. Because source units and clinically validated margins are unavailable, “favorable,” “adverse,” and “precise null” require unit validation and clinical review. Computationally, the experiment can estimate raw-scale directions, intervals, robustness, observation support, and whether the event-context controls explain the contrast.

This is a fixed-baseline pathway association in an event- and observation-selected population. It is not a causal effect of systemic therapy, an effect of repeat TACE, a direct or per-protocol effect, or a comparison of treatment strategies.

## Temporal boundaries, laboratory parsing, and deterministic selectors

### Pre-TACE2 baseline

For each exact assay separately, parse `检验时间` and normalize only for window membership. Baseline is the latest valid value on TACE2 days -30 through -1 inclusive, breaking ties by latest exact timestamp. TACE2 day 0 is excluded. The primary joint cohort requires both baseline assays but they need not share timestamp; report whether they share encounter/day/time. A same-timestamp baseline sensitivity is required.

Trim `定量结果`, remove commas, retain an optional leading `<`, `>`, `≤`, or `≥`, and accept only if the entire remaining string is a signed decimal or scientific-notation number. Reject partial numeric strings and all other text. Preserve the inequality marker rather than claiming an exact uncensored value. Report parsing attrition and specimen types. Do not harmonize other labels, infer units, calculate ALBI, or apply external thresholds.

### First repeat-TACE event

Within the frozen arms, select the first strict TACE patient-day episode whose normalized delta from TACE2 is 15–90 inclusive. The event clock is the earliest valid `开始时间` on that episode, with encounter string as deterministic tie-break if needed. Event selection must not depend on laboratory availability or values. A repeat TACE may occur in calendar 2025 after a 2024 TACE2; retain it because entry era, not event era, is frozen.

### Primary pre-event panel

Within the selected event's exact (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`), find timestamps in `[event time - 72 hours, event time)`. The event timestamp is excluded. Pivot exact assays by timestamp and retain the latest timestamp containing valid numeric values for both `白蛋白` and `总胆红素`; do not pair assays across timestamps. If duplicate same-assay values share the timestamp, retain the final source-order value only after reporting duplication; discordant duplicates trigger a sensitivity excluding that panel.

Outcomes are assay-specific within-person changes:

- `Δalbumin_pre_repeat = albumin_pre_repeat - albumin_pre_TACE2`
- `Δbilirubin_pre_repeat = total_bilirubin_pre_repeat - total_bilirubin_pre_TACE2`

Also model each pre-repeat level adjusted for its corresponding baseline; this is the preferred regression parameterization because raw change can couple baseline measurement error to outcome. Report both. Albumin and bilirubin are co-primary, not combined into an unvalidated score.

### Locked timing and context alternatives

Report without outcome-driven promotion:

- seven-day same-encounter/same-timestamp pre-event panel (audited support 55/196);
- 24-hour and 48-hour boundaries;
- first rather than latest eligible joint panel;
- assay-specific panels that need not occur at one joint timestamp;
- same-timestamp paired pre-TACE2 baseline;
- strict event encounter only versus any encounter strictly before the event, clearly labeling the latter as broader and less event-specific;
- exclusion of any matched non-bevacizumab systemic record on TACE2 days 15 through the instant before repeat TACE;
- exclusion of repeat-TACE events after 2024;
- event day 15–59 versus 60–90 only if each reported systemic stratum has at least 20 patients and effective sample size at least 15.

The primary cannot be replaced by whichever selector gives a favorable estimate.

- first repeat TACE days 15–90: 159, 526, reported both in the full arm and among baseline-paired patients;

- seven-day joint cohort: 55, 196;
- exclusions for missing/invalid time, nonnumeric result, absent encounter match, no common timestamp, outside boundary, same-as-event timestamp, and discordant duplicates.

Report fractions relative to each preceding denominator with Wilson intervals. Compare panel-observed and unobserved selected-event patients by arm, year, age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 gap, baseline exact assays and missingness, baseline severity proxies, utilization, event day, admission timing, and later systemic context. Report time-to-event and hours from panel to procedure distributions. Observation equality among repeat-event patients must be computed rather than assumed; the audited joint fraction is 34.6% (55/159) versus 35.4% (186/526), but baseline availability and event selection remain differential.

Fit two separate five-fold cross-fitted logistic models using only information temporally available before each selection:

1. first repeat TACE by day 90 among all 1,810 frozen-arm patients, using pre-TACE2 covariates and arm;
2. primary joint-panel observation among selected repeat-event patients, using pre-TACE2 covariates, arm, event day/year/month, and encounter/admission facts known before the panel, but no panel value or later information.

Report arm-specific calibration, AUC, predicted-probability distributions, positivity, and influential predictors. These models diagnose and, in sensitivity analyses, reweight measured selection; they do not identify the effect in all frozen patients or repair unmeasured retreatment rationale.

## Estimands and analysis

### Full-cohort recorded-event estimand

As a required selection diagnostic, estimate the crude and pre-TACE2 overlap-weighted arm difference and ratio in institutional recording of a first strict repeat TACE by day 90. With unavailable vital status, outside care, and reliable censoring, this is recorded-event incidence, not biological incidence or a beneficial/adverse outcome.

### Primary event-selected estimand

For assay `a`, among patients with a selected first repeat TACE and the primary joint panel, estimate:

`E_w[a_pre_repeat - a_pre_TACE2 | systemic-record arm, repeat TACE, joint observed] - E_w[a_pre_repeat - a_pre_TACE2 | TACE2-only arm, repeat TACE, joint observed]`.

The target is the measured-confounder-adjusted association between frozen pathway and pre-retreatment hepatic laboratory trajectory in the event- and observation-selected population.

Fit the inherited pre-TACE2 propensity model in all 1,810 frozen-arm patients. Prespecified variables are age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, entry year/month, latest pre-index department, prior 30/90/180-day encounter counts, prior 90/180-day procedure counts, prior resection/ablation/transplant indicators, encounter-timed cirrhosis/portal hypertension/ascites/encephalopathy/variceal bleeding/portal-vein invasion/metastasis proxies, and closest pre-TACE2 exact albumin, bilirubin, INR, platelet count, creatinine, and sodium values plus missingness indicators. No grace-period detail, later event, event-admission context, or post-TACE2 laboratory enters this baseline model. Use overlap weights (`1-p` in systemic, `p` in comparator), and report pooled/yearly propensity support, weights, effective sample size, and every weighted standardized difference.

For each assay, fit a weighted robust linear model of pre-repeat level including arm, the corresponding pre-TACE2 level, baseline-to-panel elapsed days, event year/month, hours from panel to event, and only prespecified baseline covariates remaining above absolute weighted standardized difference 0.10. Report the arm coefficient together with weighted arm means and raw within-person changes. Use patient-level heteroskedasticity-robust 95% intervals. Because there are only 55 systemic joint panels, keep models parsimonious and do not fit machine-learned outcome models.

The primary uses baseline overlap weights in the selected observed cohort. Locked sensitivity multiplies overlap weights by inverse predicted repeat-event probability and inverse predicted joint-observation probability, reported untruncated and truncated at the 1st/99th percentiles. Report weight distributions, systemic and total effective sample sizes, influence diagnostics, and the difference from the primary estimate. This remains a measured-selection sensitivity, not a causal all-cohort estimand.

Raw-scale estimates are primary. Rank-based and 20% winsorized analyses are robustness checks because extreme values are present. Inequality-bearing values are reported separately and excluded in one sensitivity. No standardized composite can replace assay-specific estimates.

## Event-context specificity checks

Two locked analyses determine how narrowly a result can be interpreted.

1. **First post-landmark admission presentation:** select the first admission on TACE2 days 15–90 with a common-timestamp joint panel from admission through 24 hours, strictly before any timestamped procedure or nonplacebo ontology-matched systemic record in that encounter. Audited support is 67 systemic versus 274 comparator. Repeat the baseline-adjusted level/change analysis as a contextual negative control. A similar contrast here means the primary signal is a general selected return-admission trajectory, not specific to proceeding with repeat TACE.
2. **Before first later systemic record:** among patients with a first nonplacebo non-bevacizumab record on days 15–90, select the latest same-encounter/same-timestamp joint panel within 72 hours strictly before that record. Audited support is 59 versus 40. Treat this as descriptive because comparator support is small and later records represent continuation/escalation in one arm but initiation/crossover in the other. Concordance across this and repeat-TACE anchors supports only a general later-care selection pattern; it does not support drug readiness or administration.

These are not alternative primaries. Discordance is informative about event context; concordance narrows, rather than strengthens causality.

## Gates, falsification criteria, and interpretation branches

1. **Frozen reconstruction:** failure to reproduce the complete flow and 319/1,491 arms invalidates the experiment.
2. **Clean-window negative feasibility:** reproduce the exact locked clean-window counts (0/4, 1/8, 1/12, 1/16, systemic/comparator). Any discrepancy requires code reconciliation; no clean-window efficacy or safety estimate may be reported from these counts.
3. **Clock integrity:** all primary panels must share the event encounter and timestamp, be strictly earlier than the procedure, and satisfy the 72-hour boundary. Same-event-time assays, cross-time pseudo-panels, or unresolved duplicate ordering falsify execution.
4. **Support:** fewer than 50 systemic primary joint panels or systemic baseline-overlap effective sample size below 40 makes adjusted assay contrasts descriptive/inconclusive. The audit gives 55 systemic panels, so precision remains borderline.
5. **Overlap/balance:** absent common propensity support, domination by one year, any core post-weight baseline absolute standardized difference above 0.10, or unstable extreme weights makes adjusted interpretation inconclusive.
6. **Selection sensitivity:** poor repeat-event/panel-model calibration, combined-weight systemic effective sample size below 35, more than 20% estimate change under locked truncation, or direction reversal between baseline-overlap and combined-weight estimates requires a selection-sensitive conclusion. Passing does not eliminate collider bias.
7. **Timing stability:** reversal across locked 24/48/72-hour, seven-day, first/latest-panel, or same-timestamp-baseline checks falsifies a stable pre-retreatment trajectory claim.
8. **Context specificity:** a comparable contrast at first general admission presentation or before later systemic records falsifies a repeat-TACE-specific interpretation; it supports only a general selected later-care/admission trajectory.
9. **Outcome coherence:** smaller albumin decline with clearly larger bilirubin rise, or vice versa, is mixed rather than favorable. Unknown units prevent automatic clinical importance or equivalence thresholds.
10. **Influence/extremes:** conclusions driven by a few observations, inequality-bearing values, discordant duplicate panels, or reversal under rank/winsorized checks are unstable/inconclusive.
11. **Temporal and claim boundary:** no result can establish why repeat TACE occurred, that the panel caused or prevented it, that systemic medication was administered, or that either pathway improved safety, survival, response, or benefit.

Result branches are locked as follows:

- **Supportive but bounded:** reconstruction, support, balance, clock, selection, timing, and influence gates pass; the systemic-record arm has a smaller albumin decline without a larger bilirubin rise; and the contrast is not reproduced at general admission or later-systemic anchors. Conclude only that a more favorable pre-repeat-TACE laboratory trajectory is associated with the frozen pathway among selected and observed repeat-TACE patients.
- **Adverse:** gates pass and the systemic-record arm has a larger albumin decline and/or bilirubin increase with coherent timing checks. Conclude an adverse pre-retreatment association warranting prospective monitoring research, not systemic-treatment toxicity or contraindication to TACE.
- **Precise null:** verified source units and clinician-defined margins show narrow intervals excluding meaningful differences for both assays, with all computational gates passed. Conclude no detectable pre-retreatment difference in this selected cohort, not equivalence or equal safety. Without unit validation, the automated result can only be a statistically precise raw-scale null.
- **General later-care trajectory:** repeat-TACE and admission/later-systemic anchors show comparable contrasts. Reject repeat-TACE specificity and report a selected return-care/admission pattern.
- **Selection-sensitive:** event/panel probabilities, combined weights, calendar support, or estimates materially differ. Report that post-baseline selection governs inference; do not choose the favorable estimator.
- **Methodologically falsified:** reconstruction or clocks fail, common-timestamp pairing is violated, or direction reverses under locked timing selectors. Reject the proposed interpretation.
- **Inconclusive:** sparse systemic support, low effective sample size, poor overlap, wide intervals spanning favorable and adverse directions, discordant assays, influential extremes, unknown units, or event-context controls prevent a stable conclusion.
- **Clean-window unavailable:** this is already the expected evidence-supported branch. State that routine/event-free hepatic change is not computable in this snapshot; do not broaden the endpoint after results.

## Evidence limits, required adjudication, and verifier scope

The snapshot lacks a laboratory-unit field, analyzer/reference-range validation, a reliable outpatient/inpatient assay indicator, complete imaging/tumor burden and response, performance status, complete Child-Pugh components, repeat-TACE rationale, technical selectivity and embolized territory, drug/contrast dose, adjudicated complications, medication administration/adherence/intent, fluid balance and exogenous albumin adjudication, outside-care capture, and reliable mortality. Encounter linkage and preprocedure ordering do not prove elective status, stable reserve, treatment eligibility, or absence of acute illness. Institutional observation does not prove survival.

Stronger clinical conclusions require laboratory metadata and unit validation, clinical review of censored/duplicate values, admission reason and acuity, interventional-radiology notes, medication administration and fluid/albumin records, imaging and tumor response, reasons for retreatment, longitudinal outpatient assays, outside-care linkage, and mortality. A causal strategy comparison additionally requires time-varying disease severity and decision covariates with a separately specified longitudinal estimand.

An automatic verifier can check source hashes and row counts, joins, duplicate handling, frozen cohort flow, event selection, exact timestamps, assay parsing and selectors, all attrition, observation models, propensity/selection weights, balance, effective sample sizes, intervals, context controls, locked sensitivities, and whether a submitted conclusion follows the computed branch. It cannot establish clinical importance on missing units, elective versus acute admission, treatment administration, retreatment rationale, hepatic toxicity, response, survival, safety, causality, or benefit.
