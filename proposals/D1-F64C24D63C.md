# HCC assay trajectory versus day-42 snapshot for repeat-TACE endpoint validity

Status: substantive Episode 12 child of `[prior hypothesis]`. Design only; no fitted result, prevalence estimate, clinical-intent claim, treatment-response claim, or causal conclusion is made.

## Scientific opening, hypothesis, and advance

The parent established a clinically useful endpoint hierarchy: O0 is a literal later TACE record, O1 is a semantically broader procedure record with a dated same-visit examination/order corroborator, and O2 adds same-visit medication corroboration. It also made workflow and lead-time controls decision-facing. The remaining scientific uncertainty is the predictor estimand. A single late assay snapshot can be a marker of who was measured late, who had intensive follow-up, or who remained in care long enough to be documented. A trajectory from before index through day 42 could instead encode an evolving physiologic state that is reproducible beyond baseline severity and the opportunity to measure it.

The strongest available evidence supports only that locally recorded assay information may predict a later locally recorded operational repeat-TACE event. It does not establish treatment intent, imaging response, viable tumor, benefit, or mechanism. [K1] supports treatment-adjacent prediction in a selected repeat-TACE cohort but does not validate this site's EHR endpoint or assay trajectory. [K2] bounds HCC response as imaging/necrosis-aware rather than a procedure label. [K3] illustrates selection and ascertainment limits of documented treatment-adjacent comparisons. The parent’s evidence and field audit show that this HCC snapshot has dated laboratory observations but no laboratory units/reference ranges, no reliable scheduling/intent field, no actual images, no outside-care capture, and untimed pathology/diagnosis/document narrative.

Hypothesis H is:

> Among the inherited eligible HCC patients, a pre-index-to-day-42 trajectory of the parent’s eight exact assay labels will provide reproducible held-out information for later O0 and boundary-validating O1 beyond a day-42 snapshot, after accounting for pre-index baseline severity, laboratory measurement opportunity, and care workflow. If the apparent trajectory increment disappears under workflow matching, late-measurement truncation, or timestamp/order controls, the leading explanation is selective measurement/late care intensity rather than a distinct assay state.

This is a descriptive/predictive state-information test. A positive result would justify collecting time-stamped clinical adjudication data; it would not justify repeat-TACE selection or claim that the trajectory causes treatment.

The substantive advance over the parent is a pre-specified nested estimand that separates: (i) baseline severity, (ii) opportunity/care workflow, (iii) the day-42 snapshot, and (iv) within-person assay change and ordering. It tests whether trajectory adds information that the snapshot loses, rather than rewarding a more complex model for a small predictive gain.

## Population and endpoint hierarchy

All parent population and outcome rules are frozen.

* Index is each patient’s first procedures row whose Unicode-NFKC-normalized, trimmed, case-folded `手术` equals `TACE`, with nonmissing `患者主索引`, `就诊号`, and `开始时间`. Collapse exact duplicate patient/start rows for counting but retain duplicate counts and raw-label hashes.
* Require all-source observation end at least 42 days after index start. Exclude any later literal-code TACE with 0 < elapsed days < 43. Follow-up ends at the first qualifying event, all-source `obs_end`, or day 181.
* O0 remains the first later unique literal-code TACE with 43 <= elapsed days < 181. [43,90] and [91,181) remain fixed sensitivities.
* O1 remains the first later procedure satisfying the parent semantic procedure predicate P, a valid procedure start, a same-patient/same-visit encounter link, and at least one dated same-visit examination/order corroborator. The encounter rule is encounter `就诊时间` within two elapsed days of procedure start, or valid `入院时间 <= t <= 出院时间`; missing discharge makes the second route false. The examination/order window is [t-42,t+7].
* O2 remains O1 plus same-visit medication predicate M in [t-1,t+1].
* Untimed procedures are flagged, never imputed as events or timed negatives. A dated procedure with missing end time can define an event; its censoring/observation deficiency is audited. A qualifying procedure lacking O1 corroboration is a timed O1/O2 near-miss/negative only when its follow-up is observed.
* O0 is the primary operational outcome, O1 is the boundary-validation outcome, and O2 is secondary. O2 cannot rescue an unsupported O1. All post-cutoff endpoint evidence is outcome-only and never enters a predictor.

No endpoint is called treatment intent, response, benefit, or clinical deterioration. The inherited pre-procedure corroborator, process/timestamp, early-alias, and lead-time controls remain required.

## Analysis and uncertainty

Use the inherited chronological split: earliest 80% of eligible index dates for development and latest 20% untouched test, patient-disjoint; five grouped development folds; all dictionaries, assay encoding, imputation, scaling, weighting, and feature selection are frozen in development. No outcome-tuned binning, assay selection, or window change is permitted.

The confirmatory baseline is a pooled-logistic discrete-time hazard with elastic-net regularization, fit separately for O0, O1, and O2 and for M0, M0+Spre, A0, AS42, and AT plus the inherited ablations. Report ordinary and all-source-observation/censoring-weighted held-out log loss, Brier/integrated Brier, calibration, AUROC/AUPRC, risk-set/event counts, effective sample size (ESS), and weight tails. The primary trajectory increment is:

`Delta_T|S = L(AS42) - L(AT)`

where positive values mean the trajectory has lower held-out loss than the day-42 snapshot at the same baseline/workflow information. Report the paired trajectory increment for O0 and O1; O2 is secondary. Also report `Delta_S = L(A)-L(B)`, `Delta_W = L(M0+Pobs)-L(A)`, and `G = Delta_S-Delta_W` from the inherited analysis. These are predictive/descriptive contrasts, not causal effects.

Use one pre-specified simultaneous max-|t| patient-bootstrap family across O0/O1/O2, S42-versus-T, state-versus-workflow, and the fixed falsification axes. Preserve paired patients, risk sets, censoring weights, event counts and ESS in every resample. The inherited 0.005 weighted-log-loss margin remains an operational resolution margin, not a clinical MCID. Do not interpret a small predictive difference as clinical benefit.

The learned alternative answers exactly the same question on the same split, outcomes and uncertainty. It is a compact time-aware GRU over the allowed event stream from day -365 through +42, with separate O0/O1/O2 heads and channel ablations for M0, baseline severity, measurement opportunity, care workflow, S42, and T. Three fixed development seeds are required. It could reveal nonlinear ordering, delayed change, and interactions among assay missingness and care events that engineered bin summaries lose; it cannot recover intent, imaging response, units, or outside care. ## Falsification and the next clinical data decision

The following controls are frozen before outcome inspection:

1. **Baseline-severity control:** compare AS42 with AT after M0+Spre, and report the unadjusted/inherited comparison separately. If the apparent gain is already present in Spre and T adds nothing, the result is baseline severity, not a day-42 trajectory.
2. **Measurement/care matching:** compare AS42 and AT after Pobs and Pcare; stratify/report by prespecified observation-intensity strata and require nondegenerate support. If the gain collapses only after these channels, selective measurement or care intensity is the leading explanation.
3. **Late-lead-time controls:** refit with labs and workflow truncated at day 28, and with all [35,42] assay/process records removed. A trajectory that depends only on the last week is compatible with late measurement/lead-time, not a stable trajectory state.
4. **Order/timing controls:** preserve patient assay values and counts while shuffling valid assay timestamps/order within development strata (and separately permuting patient labels for the trajectory channel); preserve workflow counts. If the shuffled process reproduces the gain, the ordered trajectory interpretation is adverse.
5. **Pre-index negative-control comparison:** fit an otherwise matched pre-index-only trajectory. If post-index T is no better than pre-index history after Spre, the result is consistent with persistent severity/history rather than an evolving state.
6. **Endpoint workflow controls:** retain the parent’s [-42,-1], [-28,-1], and [-7,-1] pre-procedure corroborator restrictions, process/timestamp permutations, early semantic-alias exclusion, ordinary versus development-fold observation weighting, and exact O0/O1/O2 comparisons. These test late documentation of the endpoint separately from assay timing.

Support requires, for O0 and O1, at least 50 overall events, at least 15 development and 15 test events per reported stratum, weighted ESS at least 100, nondegenerate observation/censoring support, no frozen weight-tail failure, and complete required timing audits. O1 remains unsupported/inconclusive if its conditions fail; O2 cannot rescue it. Report event/risk-set, missing-time, near-miss, measurement-intensity, and untimed documentation counts before interpreting increments.

The frozen three-way rule is:

* **SUPPORTIVE trajectory/state:** O0 and O1 pass support gates; the simultaneous lower bound for Delta_T|S is above zero for O1 (with O0 directionally consistent); the gain persists after Spre, Pobs, and Pcare; it is not reproduced by timestamp/process permutations or late-week truncation; and the inherited endpoint state-versus-workflow and pre-procedure controls do not indicate workflow domination. This supports only reproducible recorded trajectory information for an operational event. The next clinical data decision is a blinded validation study linking the trajectory to time-stamped imaging reports/images, treatment scheduling/intent, and outside-care capture, with expert episode adjudication. It does not support a treatment rule.
* **ADVERSE workflow/lead-time:** the trajectory gain disappears after Pobs/Pcare matching, late truncation, timestamp shuffling, or the pre-index-only comparison is equivalent; or a workflow/timestamp permutation matches it. Retain O0/O1 only as operational recorded-event estimands, stop describing T as independent state information, and prioritize acquisition of complete laboratory ordering/collection timestamps, scheduling/intent, outside-care records, standardized imaging/report timestamps, and validated medication indications. This does not prove the assay has no prognostic value.
* **INCONCLUSIVE:** event, ESS, overlap, timing, or weight gates fail; uncertainty is wide or unstable; or the controls cannot distinguish trajectory from workflow. Do not enlarge the model or select a favorable subgroup. The next decision is a bounded source/timing audit and independent validation; the underlying assay question is deferred rather than accepted or rejected.

The observed HCC files cannot establish the stronger clinical claims. Needed evidence includes assay units/reference ranges and collection context, reliable ordering/collection timestamps, treatment intent/scheduling and outside-care capture, actual imaging or validated report-level response adjudication, expert review of discordant O0/O1/O2 episodes, and external validation. A computational verifier can check source bindings, windowing, split, endpoint definitions, predictions, losses, support, uncertainty, and whether conclusions match those outputs. It cannot adjudicate treatment intent, imaging response, or clinical benefit.

## Alternatives and selection record

The simple baseline and learned alternative address the same scientific uncertainty with identical population, cutoff, outcomes, split, weighting and uncertainty:

* Selected primary: interpretable elastic-net discrete-time hazards with nested AS42 versus AT and explicit baseline/workflow ablations. It is adequate because the claim is an incremental trajectory estimand that must be auditable by assay, bin, missingness, and workflow stratum.
* Retained learned sensitivity: compact GRU over the same dated stream. It may reveal nonlinear order and delayed patterns that fixed bins lose, but complexity cannot rescue poor support or identify intent. It is deferred to the future solver envelope and no GPU is required for the primary.
* Not chosen: larger transformer, automated NLP of pathology/findings/documents, latent causal/intent model, and mechanistic response model. They require reliable narrative temporality/coreference, event-time pathology/diagnosis, treatment intent, images, assay units/reference ranges, or external labels that are not available. Revisit only after a timed, expert-labeled intent/response corpus or validated image/report linkage exists.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted, held-out AS42-versus-AT and inherited endpoint estimates with joint uncertainty, not a readiness report. Completion requires:
