## Falsification, uncertainty, and decision rules

Required checks: non-renal observation-density quartiles as a process-only exposure; urine/input opportunity and all four support statuses; support-first/renal-first/mixed cells; amount-only, totalamount-only, rate-only, complete-interval, excluded-urine, all-three-bin, current-window, storetime, discharge-clipping, and timing-exception sensitivities. A negative temporal control must show that records after Ltime cannot change the primary exposure. A boundary-shift fixture that deliberately moves W_L into exposure must be labeled leakage-positive and may change only the boundary-sensitive estimate.

Supportive results: directionally consistent component contrasts at >=2 landmarks, similar direction in adequately supported and renal-first cells, no comparable process-only gradient, and no dependence on point allocation, storetime, excluded items, or current-window records. Adverse results: disappearance after support/order stratification, concentration in high-density/support-first cells, a comparable process-only gradient, boundary dependence, or RRT differences explained by ICU exit/surveillance. Inconclusive: sparse cells, <10 events, clinically important intervals too wide, high unknown opportunity, invalid timing, or sequence improvement without reproducible component contrast/calibration. These outcomes support revision/deferral/abandonment of the renal interpretation as appropriate; they do not prove mechanism or causality. True GFR, total-body balance, intent, complete ward RRT, and clinical adjudication require another study/expert review.
