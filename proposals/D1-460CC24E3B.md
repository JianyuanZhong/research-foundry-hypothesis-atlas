# Documentary-positive preservation frontier for the fixed-capacity HCC review queue

## 1. Successor rationale

This proposal is a child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It retains that candidate’s mature HCC population, 2015–2022 role boundaries, 24-hour confirmatory frame, whole-accession report exposure, coupled uncertainty worlds, patient-complete conditional coverage contract, fixed-capacity score, inherited comparator/random/deletion gates, exact arithmetic, and fail-closed evidence limits.

The parent closes an important gap left by aggregate capture-count gates: equal documentary-M2 counts do not imply that the same patients enter review. Its new `q_dec` requires equality of the entire offered queue against the same-world maximum-stored-content oracle. That is a strong and useful safety condition, but it treats every queue substitution as equally consequential. Swapping two patients who are both documentary-M2-negative is not the same documentary risk as omitting a patient who is M2-positive under the locked reader books.

The successor adds one targeted endpoint, with no new model, feature, refit, clinical label, or data source: preservation of the set of documentary-M2-positive patients offered in the fixed-K queue. The parent’s whole-queue `q_dec` remains mandatory and is reported as a stricter secondary endpoint. The new frontier asks the more decision-proximal documentary question: does report-bundle missingness cause the queue to lose or replace a patient who the maximum stored-content oracle would have offered and whose locked radiology/pathology books classify as documentary M2-positive?

This is a principled refinement, not a claim that documentary M2 is biological truth or that queue membership improves care.

## 2. Clinical question, evidence boundary and falsifiable hypothesis

### Unresolved question

At 24 hours before an adult’s first eligible HCC resection, can a fixed-capacity review queue remain robust to report-bundle exposure uncertainty with respect to the documentary-positive patients it offers, even when exact identity of every offered patient is not preserved?

This matters when expert review is scarce. A count-only guarantee can conceal the omission of a high-priority documentary-positive case, while an all-patient queue-equality requirement can fail because of clinically irrelevant substitutions among documentary-negative cases. The new endpoint separates those two failures.

### Strongest supported evidence

The inspected HCC snapshot demonstrably contains encounter-linked procedures, acquisition-like examination times, multirow examination reports with narrative fields, same-encounter pathology narratives, and temporally labelled medications/orders. The raw examination source has a whole-accession identifier and can be grouped without discarding component rows. The source also contains no report authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrievable/readable/viewed clock, no raw images, no specimen/slide/accession linkage for pathology, and no clinical-action, treatment, recurrence, survival, harm, cost or utility endpoint.

The expert-selected natural-history demonstration was read from its local article extraction and reports that source-specific missingness can itself be learned as predictive signal; it cautions against treating such associations as causal. The Bayesian EHR demonstration was read from its local article extraction and emphasizes temporal validation, calibration, selection-bias concerns, and diagnostic-date uncertainty. A current oncology prediction review was acquired and read from DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1798803`; it argues that discrimination alone is insufficient and that external/temporal validation, calibration, clinical utility and generalizability must be evaluated together. These sources support the importance of evaluating an operational decision contract under missingness; they do not establish the HCC hypothesis.

### Untested claim

The untested claim is that, among all compatible source, operation, onset, reader, pathology and deletion worlds, the report-bundle reserve can preserve the documentary-positive patients offered by the locked G queue at 24 hours before surgery.

### Confirmatory hypothesis

At rho = 0.10 and d = 24 hours, separately in 2020 and 2021:

1. maximum stored content passes every inherited comparator, random, and anchored singleton-deletion gate;
2. the parent’s conditional-coverage frontier is positive (`q_req < 1`);
3. the new documentary-positive frontier is positive (`q_M2 < 1`);
4. every primary complete state has a nonempty maximum-content documentary-positive queue target, so the new equality test is not vacuous; and
5. the parent’s exact whole-queue result (`q_dec`) is reported without being silently replaced by `q_M2`.

Here `q_M2` is the smallest exact rational patient-complete conditional bundle-coverage threshold for which all inherited gates and all same-world maximum-content documentary-positive queue-set equalities hold in every informative compatible state. The required logical check is `q_M2 >= q_req`. Because whole-queue equality is stricter, `q_M2 <= q_dec) is expected whenever both are defined, and the computation must verify rather than assume that relation.

A positive result is only a finite-corpus documentary robustness certificate. It does not establish report release, clinician viewing, biological MVI, treatment selection, benefit, safety, or patient utility.

## 5. Coupled exposure and uncertainty worlds

Each source-clean eligible accession receives one coupled onset state:

- O=0: eligible by 72 hours;

Source, operation, identity, onset, reader, pathology, year, deletion and model inputs remain coupled.

## 7. New patient-level documentary-positive estimand

Let pi_G(z) be the length-K fixed-position offered queue from the fitted G scores in state z, with protected patient hashes or EMPTY. Let pi_G^max(z) be the same fitted G pipeline in the same source/operation/year/book/deletion state with every source-clean eligible accession exposed (M=1). It is a maximum-stored-content oracle, not ground truth.

Let Y_i(z) be the inherited documentary-M2 indicator for patient i in the fixed radiology/pathology book pair and source world. It is defined only by the locked reader books; it is not pathological truth. Define:

- P_G(z) = {i: i is in pi_G(z) and Y_i(z)=1};
- P_G^max(z) = {i: i is in pi_G^max(z) and Y_i(z)=1};
- J_M2(z) = 1[P_G(z) = P_G^max(z)];
- L_M2(z) = |P_G^max(z) minus P_G(z)|, the number of oracle-positive patients omitted by visibility;
- S_M2(z) = |P_G(z) symmetric-difference P_G^max(z)|, the positive-target substitution count;
- R_M2(z) = |P_G(z) intersect P_G^max(z)| / |P_G^max(z)| when the denominator is nonzero.

The primary new predicate is exact positive-target set equality, not a floating-point tolerance. It permits substitutions among patients who are negative in both queues while detecting omission or replacement of a documentary-positive target. `L_M2=0` is reported as a secondary loss count; `S_M2` and `R_M2` are descriptive and do not replace the equality predicate.

For every exact rational q in the inherited threshold set, define M2_PASS(q)=1 only if:

1. the parent’s PASS(q)=1 in every complete primary state with Q(omega,2020) >= q and Q(omega,2021) >= q;
2. J_M2(z)=1 in every such state; and
3. every such state has |P_G^max(z)| >= 1.

If maximum content passes, feasibility gates are valid, and all primary states are informative, define q_M2 = min{q in R: M2_PASS(q)=1}. Report q_M2, q_req, q_dec, q_M2-q_req, q_dec-q_M2, all lower-boundary states, the protected positive-set signatures, L_M2, S_M2 and R_M2. Check q_M2 >= q_req and q_M2 <= q_dec when both are defined. No visibility state may be compared with a maximum oracle from another world.

The computational advance is decision validity at the target-event level: the analysis can distinguish “the same number of documentary positives” from “the same documentary-positive patients were offered.” It does not assign biological severity, urgency or utility to M2-positive patients.

## 8. Supportive, adverse and inconclusive outcomes

Use mutually exclusive one-hot labels.

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: source replay, headers/hashes, complete accession grouping, interval-valued operation audit, coupled-state generation, exact ranking/tie replay, patient-complete contract, positive-target oracle, threshold frontier, boundary witness or verifier replay fails. Any sampling, state cap, unresolved incumbent, heuristic, floating-only threshold, quotient collision, denominator substitution, world stitching or accession-row sampling is computational failure.

2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: any locked N/K/event/n_A/structural gate fails, or any primary state has |P_G^max|=0 so the positive-target test would be vacuous. Report the parent’s result if it remains computable, but do not call the new hypothesis supported.

3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: maximum stored content fails an inherited comparator, random or anchored-deletion gate in 2020 or 2021. This rejects the locked semantic advantage regardless of q_M2.

4. `conditional_coverage_adverse`: maximum content passes but q_req is undefined or q_req=1. No positive missing-bundle reserve is certified.

5. `positive_target_adverse`: maximum content passes, q_req<1, but q_M2 is undefined or q_M2=1. The aggregate contract and possibly whole-queue contract may survive, but report-bundle exposure cannot guarantee preservation of the documentary-positive patients offered by the oracle.

6. `target_supportive_full_queue_adverse`: q_M2<1 but q_dec is undefined or q_dec=1. The documentary-positive target set is preserved, but irrelevant-to-M2 queue substitutions remain; this supports only target-level documentary robustness, not exact patient-queue identity.

7. `documentary_decision_supportive`: maximum content passes, q_M2<1, q_dec<1, the immediately lower thresholds fail where defined, all exact witnesses verify, all primary states are informative, and all N/n_A/e certificates are complete. This supports both documentary-positive target preservation and full queue identity in the finite HCC corpus.

8. `external_bridge_pending`: the HCC computation is valid but no external archive can establish that the stored content corresponds to the same report accession/version and actual visibility process. This is inconclusive for deployment, not adverse evidence about G.

A favorable 12/48/72-hour sensitivity cannot rescue or veto the 24-hour label. Report all structural e, N, n_A, strata, H_G, K-H_G, inherited margins, q_req, q_M2, q_dec, target-set differences, slot differences and raw worst witnesses.

## 11. What this advances and what remains uncertain

The parent establishes that aggregate documentary-M2 capture and, potentially, the identity of every fixed-capacity queue patient can be robust to a conditional stored-content contract. This successor adds a clinically more targeted intermediate claim: preservation of the documentary-positive patients, while retaining the parent’s exact queue identity as a stricter endpoint. It can therefore produce three scientifically distinct outcomes: no capture reserve, positive-target loss despite capture reserve, or target preservation with residual negative-patient queue churn.

The result remains a finite-corpus, stored-content/documentary claim. HCC lacks report-release and view timestamps, image files, pathology specimen linkage/time, actual review actions and patient outcomes. MIMIC, eICU and UKB cannot repair those gaps because no HCC crosswalk or compatible documentary-M2 endpoint is established. The experiment can falsify the positive-target robustness contract and identify the next archive/workflow/specimen study; it cannot establish clinical benefit or biological truth.
