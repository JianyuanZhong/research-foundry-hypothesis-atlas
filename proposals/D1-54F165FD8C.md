## Hypothesis to test

At similar illness severity, differences between hospitals in the speed of liberation from organ support are associated with treatment practices.

## Study population and main data

Patients receiving organ support; sedation, fluid, and ventilation records.

## Suggested approach

Model liberation from support, death, and discharge/transfer separately and compare hospitals using hierarchical models.

## Scientific significance

Identify practice differences that warrant further investigation.

## Main limitations

Residual differences cannot be interpreted directly as quality of care and must not be used for simplistic hospital rankings.
