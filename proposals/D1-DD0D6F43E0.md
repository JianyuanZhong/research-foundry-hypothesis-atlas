# Episode 42 (eICU) — Dose, detection, or treatment intensity? Binding the vasoactive/fluid channel to the (0,24]h MAP<65 burden → (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI question

Child of the Lead-assigned board leader `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 32.48, valid, delivery-complete). Operation: **evolve**. No parent rating is inherited.

Branch: eICU 2.0 `(0,24]h MAP<65 burden → (24,72]h creatinine-rise KDIGO-AKI`, dose vs observation (episodes 28–41).
Content marked **planned** unless sourced to an executed probe; the parent's executed probe (`[research job]`) is reused as saved evidence and is labelled as the parent's.

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance

An ICU stay with more time below MAP 65 mmHg is often reported to carry higher AKI risk. The parent compares two rivals:
- **H-dose:** (0,24]h MAP<65 burden is a physiologic ischemic dose that independently raises (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI risk.
- **H-observation:** hypotension triggers more monitoring/lab testing, so AKI is *detected* more, inflating the association without proportionate injury.

Episode 41 executed an estimability/negative-control diagnostic (parent job `[research job]`) and observed that its **raw** contrast leant toward H-observation: among 32,889 stays with ≥6 MAP observations, Spearman(burden, creatinine-draws)=+0.121 vs Spearman(burden, non-renal-draws)=+0.185 (contrast −0.063), i.e. broad monitoring rose at least as fast as the renal channel. The parent explicitly recorded that this was **raw, not the fitted estimand**, and left the third channel **unbound**.

**This episode's substantive change:** bind and use the **treatment-intensity channel** (vasoactive infusion dose + fluid) that the parent left unbound, and fit the estimand rather than reading raw correlations. This introduces a third named rival:
- **H-treatment:** the burden→AKI association is a marker of vasoactive/fluid treatment intensity (confounding by indication / treatment itself), which the parent's design could not condition on.

H-dose, H-observation and H-treatment imply different clinical decisions: under H-dose, minimising time below MAP 65 is a treatment target; under H-observation the "target" partly reflects who was watched; under H-treatment the apparent target partly reflects shock treatment itself. Separating them changes whether a MAP threshold should be acted on or whether monitoring/treatment intensity must be modelled first.

## 2. Closest inspected work and remaining question

- [K1] (abstract_only) Time-weighted MAP<65 burden is an established correlate of creatinine-based KDIGO AKI and a base logistic model on that burden reaches AUC 0.725 in a surgical cohort; arterial waveform features add nothing. So a burden→creatinine-AKI **signal is plausible but has limited confirmatory specificity**, and no monitoring/treatment channel was conditioned on.
- [K2] (abstract_only) A lower-MAP/renal-composite association (OR 0.217 per 5 mmHg) was present **while MAP was not clearly associated with subsequent creatinine summaries** — bounding any strong MAP<65→creatinine-rise causal claim and motivating a creatinine-specific, detection-aware, treatment-aware estimand.
- [K3] (metadata/keywords only) Real-world EHR phenotyping constrains causal/estimand claims — supporting framing the question as **discrimination between rivals**, not mechanism.

**Unresolved claim this experiment tests:** In eICU 2.0, is the (0,24]h MAP<65 burden → (24,72]h creatinine-rise KDIGO-AKI association attenuated to ~null once the **observation process** is modelled, or once **vasoactive/fluid treatment intensity** is added, or does it persist under both?

## 3. Design (bounds to exact eICU 2.0 sources)

All source paths under `[internal dataset path]`; snapshots & SHA-256 in `datasets/eicu/README.md` (snapshot `[source checksum]`).

- **Population (planned):** adult ICU stays in `patient.csv.gz` (`patientunitstayid`, `age`, `gender`, `hospitalid`, `unitadmitsource`, `unitdischargeoffset`), age ≥ 18 (age is top-coded 90+), ICU length ≥ 1440 min, and ≥ 1 MAP observation in (0,24]h. Join key `patientunitstayid`.
- **Exposure (planned):** `vitalPeriodic.csv.gz`, column `observationoffset` (min from ICU admission), MAP from `systemicmean` (fall back to (`systemicsystolic`+2·`systemicdiastolic`)/3 when `systemicmean` is null). Burden = number of MAP observations with MAP<65 in (0,24]h (primary), and a time-weighted burdorder sensitivity.
- **Outcome (planned):** `lab.csv.gz` (`labresultoffset`, `labname`, `labresult`), `labname='creatinine'`; (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI = ≥ 1.5× the lowest (0,24]h creatinine, or a ≥ 0.3 mg/dL absolute rise across the follow-up window. Requires ≥ 2 creatinine draws.
- **H-observation channel (planned, reused from parent):** count of **non-renal** `lab.csv.gz` draws in (0,24]h (draws whose `labname` ∉ {creatinine, BUN/urea, eGFR}).
- **H-treatment channel (planned, NEW):** `infusionDrug.csv.gz` (`infusionoffset`, `drugname`, `patientweight`): vasoactive intensity = count and duration of vasopressor infusions in (0,24]h, restricted to observed eICU labels `Norepinephrine (mcg/min)`, `Phenylephrine (mcg/min)`, `Vasopressin (units/min)`, `Dopamine (mcg/kg/min)`, `Epinephrine (mcg/min)`, `Dobutamine (mcg/kg/min)`, `Milrinone (mcg/kg/min)` (probe `work/lead-ep42/probe_treatment_channel.json`). Fluid channel = `intakeOutput.csv.gz` (`intakeoutputoffset`, `cellpath`, `cellvaluenumeric`) sustained-intake/fluid totals in (0,24]h.
- **Baseline covariates (planned):** age, gender, `unitadmitsource`, APACHE severity from `apachePatientResult.csv.gz` (`apachescore`, `predictedicumortality`), admission diagnosis (`apacheadmissiondx`); hospital fixed effects via `hospitalid` (and `hospital.csv.gz` teachingstatus/region for transport).

## 4. Estimands, baselines and learned/mechanistic alternative

- **B1 (baseline):** logistic regression of KDIGO-AKI on burden + age/sex/severity (the parent's model form) — unadjusted for channels.
- **A1 (primary, doubly-robust):** AIPW/doubly-robust estimate of the mean difference in AKI risk across burden strata, after (i) conditioning on the H-observation channel only, (ii) the H-treatment channel only, (iii) both. Fitted with cross-fitting and hospital stratification. This is the substantive alternative the baseline loses: B1 cannot say *which* measured process (detection vs treatment) explains the association.
- **A1' negative-control outcome:** the same models applied to a non-renal lab change (e.g. bilirubin/albumin) that should not be a hypotension dose effect; a burden→negative-control-outcome association under the monitoring channel falsifies a purely physiologic reading.
- **A2 (deferred):** gradient-boosted / spline flexible burden-response for non-linearity; not required for the primary falsification and deferred to keep the discovery episode bounded.
- **Reproducibility bound:** naive fits on ~130k stays with ≤ ~15 covariates are CPU-feasible; the full `lab.csv.gz` (517 MB) and `nurseCharting.csv.gz` (1.7 GB) streaming pass dominates cost, so chunked streaming is specified. This is a bounded diagnostic, not a full study.

## 5. Falsification criteria (pre-specified; the experiment's job is to discriminate)

Let the AIPW risk difference be RD_dose (burden effect), RD_obs (after conditioning on the non-renal monitoring channel), RD_tx (after also conditioning on vasoactive/fluid intensity).

- **Supportive H-dose:** RD_dose ≠ 0 and RD_obs and RD_tx remain materially non-null (e.g. attenuation < ~25% of RD_dose) and the negative-control outcome is null while creatinine-AKI is not.
- **Adverse (H-observation):** RD_obs ≈ 0 (≥ ~75% attenuation) while RD_dose ≠ 0, and the negative-control outcome moves with burden as much as creatinine-AKI does.
- **Adverse (H-treatment):** RD_obs remains non-null but RD_tx ≈ 0.
- **Inconclusive/non-estimable:** exposure too sparse (parent saw only 19.3% of monitored stays with any MAP<65 in (0,24]h), or too few stays with ≥2 creatinine draws and adequate channel variation, or overlapping-CI results underpowered to separate channels → park the branch, do not claim the burden is causally benign.

## 6. What each outcome would and would not establish

Under any outcome the design cannot establish mechanism or a causal treatment target: it can only **rank** the three named measured rivals by which conditioning set removes the association. A non-null RD_tx does not prove treatment causes AKI (dose and severity co-vary); a null RD_obs does not prove no injury occurred (undetected injury is unmeasured). Clinical adjudication of a target MAP, external validation and (ideally) a within-hospital or quasi-randomised design would be required for a treatment claim, and are explicitly **not** provided here.
