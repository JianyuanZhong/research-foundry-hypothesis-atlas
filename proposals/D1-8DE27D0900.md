## Observed creatinine vocabulary and scale audit

A managed CPU scan read all 39,132,531 lab rows and wrote the bounded derived summary `lab-audit.json` (job output [source checksum]). Independent bounded shell summaries checked the exact serum rows and displayed nonnumeric examples.

Observed labels containing creatinine were:

- `creatinine`: 1,277,760 rows.
- `urinary creatinine`: 13,282 rows.

For exact `labname=creatinine`, all 1,277,760 rows had `labmeasurenamesystem=mg/dL`. The interface counts were `mg/dL` 1,159,507; `MG/DL` 78,742; `mg/dl` 39,509; and blank 2. No serum row had a micromolar interface in this exact-label audit. Urinary creatinine is also reported with `mg/dL` system units but has a different biological quantity and is excluded.

Among exact serum-creatinine rows, 1,275,796 had a nonnegative numeric `labresult`; 1,964 had no numeric `labresult`. Examples have blank `labresult` and `labresulttext=<0.20`, so the primary label will not parse text or silently turn a detection limit into a number. Numeric serum values in this source scan ranged from 0.06 to 129 (mg/dL); this is a source-scale audit, not an assertion that extreme values are clinically correct. Duplicate clinical timestamps are possible; the latest `labresultrevisedoffset` is the deterministic tie-breaker, while `labresultoffset` remains the clinical measurement time.

The full scan also found interface variants such as `umol/L` when aggregating all strings containing “creatinine”; these are attributable to other creatinine-related rows (not exact serum `labname=creatinine` in the exact-label cross-tab) and are a reason to require the exact-label/system-unit rule rather than case-insensitive substring matching. There is no separate units column: unit evidence is encoded in the two measure-name fields and needs external clinical validation.

## Frozen label gate

The source-level vocabulary/scale gate passes conditionally for a reproducible creatinine-only label:

1. exact `labname=creatinine`;
2. nonempty numeric `labresult` matching a frozen decimal parser;
3. case-folded `labmeasurenamesystem=mg/dl`;
4. `labmeasurenameinterface` blank or case-folded `mg/dl`;
5. exclude `urinary creatinine`, text-only values, and all other lab names;
6. for identical `patientunitstayid` and `labresultoffset`, retain the maximum `labresultrevisedoffset` (and document ties);
7. use `labresultoffset` for all windowing.

This is not full KDIGO adjudication. The primary planned outcome is a new numeric serum-creatinine rise of at least 0.3 mg/dL from the last eligible baseline in minutes -24 to 0, evaluated in minutes 360 to 2,880. The experiment must report how many rows/patients fail the label gate, the text-only/detection-limit count, duplicate/tie count, baseline availability, and follow-up observation. If the vocabulary/system mapping is not accepted by clinical review or the event support is inadequate, stop the primary outcome fit rather than substitute `labresulttext`, BUN, `apacheApsVar.creatinine`, urine output, or dialysis.

## Population, estimand, censoring and competing events

Preserve the inherited noncausal prognostic estimand: among adult first ICU stays with adequate early observation and common support, compare early cumulative systemic MAP time below 65 mmHg in minutes 0–360 against nadir and early severity for a creatinine-only event in minutes 360–2,880. Require survival/in-ICU presence through minute 360, at least 12 valid five-minute BP bins and 240 minutes between first and last covered bin, plus the frozen creatinine baseline. Do not interpolate gaps over 10 minutes or treat missingness as normal.

B0 is a regularized logistic/discrete-time cause-specific regression with baseline severity, baseline creatinine, pre-index lab coverage, BP coverage/modality/missingness and initial vitals. B1 adds duration, nadir, episodes and restricted-spline duration at fixed-nadir/common-support strata. The substantive temporal alternative is a small GRU or temporal convolution over the same 72 five-minute bins, with MAP, heart rate, respiration, modality, masks, time since last observation, conservative recorded-pressor features, and pre-index severity. It is tested for reproducible ordering/recovery information, not for a small accuracy gain. Fit only in training hospitals, calibrate in validation hospitals and evaluate once in held-out hospitals (whole-hospital 60/20/20 or prespecified grouped-hospital CV if necessary). Report held-out log loss, Brier score, calibration, risk curves, hospital-clustered uncertainty and event counts.

Early discharge/transfer without a qualifying follow-up result is right-censored, not a negative. Death before 2,880 without an event is a competing event; `unitdischargeoffset` and `unitdischargestatus` are the available endpoint fields, not a precise independent death timestamp. Report censoring and competing-event counts and do not use post-index outcome or EOL information as predictors. The data do not supply clinician-confirmed AKI, pre-ICU baseline kidney function, urine-output adjudication, validated renal-replacement timing, or verified lab-unit provenance.

## Process-readiness and falsification criteria

Before fitting B1 or the temporal model, fit a separate observation-process endpoint using the same frozen vocabulary: any valid follow-up creatinine and time to first valid follow-up result. Compare its duration association with the clinical-event association; apply only training-hospital observation weights using baseline severity, early BP coverage/modality, lab coverage, recorded treatment and early endpoint/process features. Run the prespecified pre-index negative-control rise check. Treatment features are included only after a drug-name/field-unit audit; otherwise treatment is marked unknown, not dose-converted.

Support requires the label gate, event support, common support and held-out calibration; a duration gain should persist after severity/observation and conservative treatment/process adjustment, density thinning and fixed-nadir strata, without a comparable pre-index control. Adverse evidence includes label failure, strong duration prediction of observation with disappearance after weighting, a comparable pre-index association, one-hospital/low-density dependence, poor support, or disappearance after recorded-treatment adjustment. Wide intervals, unresolved unit provenance, exposure-dependent follow-up or too few events are inconclusive, not refutations. Positive results remain transportable prognostic associations, not causal effects, mechanisms, BP targets or treatment recommendations.

If the gate fails, the exact remediation is (a) an eICU data dictionary or source-interface mapping that validates the exact serum test and units, including how censored `<x` values should be handled; (b) clinical adjudication of a sample against laboratory records; (c) reliable event and censoring timestamps plus clinician-adjudicated AKI/urine/dialysis labels if a full KDIGO outcome is desired; and (d) an external hospital cohort for transportability. Without these, report only label/observation-process feasibility and defer the clinical-event claim.
