# Provisional hypothesis: fat distribution separates metabolic from functional/fall risk

Status: provisional and not selected. This is a design hypothesis, not an observed result.

## Proposed relationship

Among UK Biobank participants with baseline body-composition MRI and subsequent follow-up, at similar total body fat, higher visceral adiposity will be more specifically associated with subsequent metabolic disease, while higher posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) will be more specifically associated with subsequent functional decline or falls. ## Method comparison

The baseline is a simple total-adiposity model. The substantive alternative is a prespecified composition model that retains visceral fat and MFI as separate biological axes, allowing the data to reveal whether they carry distinct downstream information lost by total fat. A more flexible learned model is deferred unless the composition model shows a scientifically meaningful residual pattern and event support is adequate; complexity alone is not a reason to select it.
