# Provisional hypothesis: context-specific MerTK-inhibitor response in E-MTAB-8873

## Status

This is a provisional, planned research hypothesis for episode 16. It is not an executed result and does not claim direct MerTK action, causality, protection, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.

## Proposed relationship

In the available E-MTAB-8873 RA macrophage–fibroblast coculture assay, the transcriptional response associated with a MerTK inhibitor will differ between macrophage contexts defined by MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophages. The primary estimand is a donor-aware, condition-verified context-by-inhibitor interaction, not a generic mean-expression change. A secondary descriptive question is whether that interaction separates a macrophage-linked resolving/repair-associated program from a fibroblast-linked inflammatory or harmful program.

## Why it matters

If the response is context-specific, it would justify targeted mechanistic follow-up and distinguish a potentially selective therapeutic context from a broad treatment effect. If it is generic, donor-dependent, or composition-dependent, the result would redirect interpretation toward assay or treatment artifacts. This is clinically important because the available literature supports biological plausibility for MerTK-positive macrophage subsets and macrophage–fibroblast repair circuits, but does not establish this inhibitor’s action or benefit in this assay or in patients.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The closest inspected evidence supports that MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets are associated with remission and can induce repair responses in synovial fibroblasts; macrophage reprogramming can alter inflammatory mediator production; and macrophage–fibroblast repair circuits are context-, timing-, and niche-dependent. These observations are biological motivation, not evidence for the proposed E-MTAB-8873 effect. The unresolved claim is whether the intervention-associated response is genuinely context-specific after accounting for donor, condition, pseudobulk composition, and generic treatment effects.

The substantive alternative adds a context-by-inhibitor interaction and tests whether the interaction separates resolving/repair-associated from inflammatory/harmful programs.
