title: "Does worsening complete-blood-count trajectory identify colorectal cancer early enough to change diagnostic testing?"
clinical_question: >-
  Among cancer-free UK Biobank participants who had two protocolized assessment visits,
  does a multi-year trajectory toward falling haemoglobin/MCV and rising RDW/platelets
  identify colorectal cancer diagnosed 1-5 years later better than the same biomarkers'
  latest values plus established demographic and lifestyle risk factors? A positive result
  could justify prospective evaluation of trajectory-triggered FIT/colonoscopy; this study
  itself cannot establish that such testing improves outcomes.

supported_vs_unresolved:
  strongest_current_support: >-
    The available Cell cancer supplement (not the unavailable main article) reports that
    longitudinal laboratory models predicted several cancers under a 90-day washout and
    that performance declined as progressively more prediagnostic data were removed. It
    reports a UKB reference model based on age, sex and available routine laboratory
    variables, but does not establish that within-person CBC change has clinically useful
    incremental value for colorectal cancer or that acting on it benefits patients.
  unresolved_hypothesis: >-
    In participants without prior cancer, a prespecified adverse CBC trajectory between
    assessment instances 0 and 1 is associated with incident registry-confirmed colorectal
    cancer during days 366-1825 after instance 1, and adds materially to a model containing
    latest CBC levels and baseline risk factors.
  estimand: >-
    Prognostic association and incremental prediction, not a causal effect: cause-specific
    hazard ratio for the adverse-trajectory score and 5-year landmark risk discrimination,
    calibration and net benefit.

  primary_followup: "Day 366 through day 1825 after 53-1.0."
  outcome: >-
    First cancer-registry C18, C19 or C20 diagnosis whose paired 40005 date lies in the
    primary follow-up. Death before CRC is a competing event. Administrative censoring is
    the earlier of 1825 days and the empirically observed registry/death coverage end in
    this snapshot; the derived coverage date and its rule must be reported.
  observed_feasibility: >-
    Direct full-row scan found 20,343 participants with instance-1 visit dates; 18,379 had
    complete paired haemoglobin/MCV/RDW/platelet measurements. Of these, 1,801 had a prior
    registry cancer, leaving 16,578 cancer-free participants; 81 first post-landmark CRCs
    occurred during days 366-1825 (40 during days 366-1095). These are feasibility counts,
    not study results, and eligibility may shrink after all date/range checks.

predictors_and_comparators:
  primary_trajectory_score: >-
    For each marker calculate annualized change (instance1-instance0)/elapsed years.
    Standardize within sex and 5-year landmark-age bands using controls only inside each
    training fold. Adverse score is mean[z(-haemoglobin slope), z(-MCV slope), z(+RDW slope),
    z(+platelet slope)]. No data-driven marker selection.
  baseline_model: >-
    Restricted-cubic-spline landmark age, sex, validated ethnicity coding, latest smoking
    status, latest BMI, and the four instance-1 CBC values. This is the clinically relevant
    comparator because a clinician can already see current values.
  trajectory_model: "Baseline model plus four annualized slopes, represented by the prespecified score (primary) and separate slopes only as secondary."
  simple_control: "Age+sex model, reported but not used to claim added clinical value."

analysis:
  primary_association: >-
    Cause-specific Cox model from day 366, with death and non-CRC cancer censoring; report
    HR per SD score and top versus bottom quintile with 95% bootstrap CIs. Check proportional
    hazards. Fine-Gray cumulative incidence is a sensitivity analysis.
  prediction: >-
    Because only about 81 primary events were observed, use repeated nested 5-fold cross-validation,
    ridge-penalized Cox models, identical folds for comparators, all preprocessing inside folds.
    Report optimism-controlled 5-year Uno C-index/time-dependent AUC, integrated Brier score,
    calibration intercept/slope and calibration-in-the-large. Primary incremental metric is
    paired change in 5-year time-dependent AUC; bootstrap participants for 95% CIs.
  clinical_utility: >-
    Report sensitivity, specificity, PPV and number flagged at fixed top 1%, 2% and 5% risk
    thresholds, and decision-curve net benefit across plausible referral thresholds. Do not
    call net benefit clinical benefit without external prospective validation.
  missingness: >-
    Complete-case analysis is intrinsic for paired CBC predictors; compare included versus
    excluded repeat-attenders. Multiple-impute missing covariates within training folds only.
  sensitivity:
    - "Alternative washouts of 180 and 730 days; horizons 3 and 5 years"
    - "Exclude any diagnosis of anaemia/bleeding/inflammatory disease in hospital ICD-10 41270/41280 during the year before landmark, after explicit code-list review"
    - "Sex-stratified estimates and proximal C18-C19 versus rectal C20 outcomes, labelled underpowered"
    - "Restrict visit interval to 2-6 years"
    - "Compare raw change, annualized change and regression-to-the-mean-residualized change without selecting the best result"
  leakage_controls:
    - "No feature dated after 53-1.0"
    - "One-year outcome washout"
    - "Fold-specific standardization/imputation and participant-level splits"
    - "Do not use cancer registry, death, post-landmark hospital, procedure, medication or diagnostic-test fields as predictors"

falsification_and_interpretation:
  supportive_result: >-
    Prespecified support requires HR >=2.0 for top versus bottom score quintile with 95% CI
    excluding 1, AND cross-validated AUC improvement >=0.03 with 95% CI excluding 0, AND no
    major calibration deterioration (absolute calibration-in-the-large <=0.01 at 5 years).
    This would support prospective external validation of a CBC-trajectory trigger, not immediate screening.
  adverse_result: >-
    HR direction opposite to prediction, AUC change <=0, or materially worse calibration/net
    benefit would argue against using this trajectory for CRC triage and would be clinically useful negative evidence.
  inconclusive_result: >-
    Directionally positive association but CI crossing 1, AUC gain below 0.03, unstable folds,
    or fewer than 60 eligible events is inconclusive rather than negative; no flexible model escalation.
  negative_controls:
    - "Reverse the two visits when calculating slopes; a genuine forward trajectory should attenuate or reverse, not strengthen."
    - "Test the score against incident non-gastrointestinal cancers (excluding C15-C26) over the same window; a similar association suggests nonspecific frailty/inflammation."
    - "Permutation of outcome labels within sex/age bands must yield null incremental performance."
