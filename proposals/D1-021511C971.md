# Positive identification contrast: does the AFP-slope → progression association survive in interval-scheduled (non-triggered) imaging?

## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE/resection/ablation) for HCC, surveillance repeats serum AFP,
albumin and bilirubin and periodic abdominal imaging. Question: **does the within-patient AFP
slope add prognostic information beyond baseline AFP and beyond same-draw liver-reserve (ALBI)
kinetics for time to radiologic progression?**

The parent bounded (did not identify) informative observation: its E_early/E_late detection-timing
strata and its negative-control imaging-intensity hazard are both defined from the same
`检查` table, so both measure the same reporting process. [K1] shows a dated EHR exposure can flip
sign with time alignment; [K2] shows landmark estimates are biased toward the null while
time-dependent regression is more robust; [K3] shows AFP *trajectories* associate with HCC
outcomes after locoregional/HAIC therapy. **The still-open objection:** no analysis yet separates
"a rising AFP *prompts* the next scan and thereby the progression report" from "AFP tracks tumour
activity that would be reported at the next scheduled scan anyway." That is a positive
identification question, not a negative one.

**Clinical importance.** If the AFP→progression association lives only in lab-triggered imaging, the
actionable change is **surveillance-interval policy** (mandated interval imaging, not marker-triggered).
If it survives in interval-scheduled imaging, it supports marker-informed escalation and earlier
scheduled reassessment. A precise null (liver reserve alone) argues for de-emphasising AFP-only
monitoring. These imply different decisions.

## 3. THE ONE ADDED ELEMENT — positive identification contrast

Classify **each post-landmark abdominal imaging study** as **lab-triggered** vs **interval-scheduled**
using `examinations.开始时间` and `labs.检验时间`:

- **Triggered** if the study occurs within **w = 14 days** after a prior `甲胎蛋白` OR ALBI-component draw,
  **AND** the gap since the previous abdominal study exceeds the institutional surveillance interval
  **I = 60 days**. **Interval-scheduled** otherwise (i.e. the study was not preceded by a recent lab
  trigger, or followed the previous study within the scheduled interval).
- **Pre-specified justification (not tuned):** w = 14 d spans typical order-to-perform latency for an
  abnormal tumour-marker result; I = 60 d is the lower bound of post-treatment q2–3-month cross-sectional
  surveillance at one Chinese hospital. Report sensitivities w ∈ {7, 28} d and I ∈ {45, 90} d; the
  primary classification is fixed at (w=14, I=60) **before** any outcome is seen.
- **Estimated quantity:** fit the AFP-slope → progression-report hazard (same modality-restricted
  primary endpoint) **separately** in the interval-scheduled and triggered strata, on a standardized
  per-1-SD slope scale, adjusted for baseline AFP, ALBI slope, age, sex, index year; with a formal
  **slope × stratum interaction** Wald test (and interaction HR CI). Primary discriminating statistic:
  `Δ = log HR_prog(triggered) − log HR_prog(interval-scheduled)` for the AFP slope.

## 4. Pre-declared outcome branches (mapped to distinct numeric results)

- **Supportive** (identification survives): interval-scheduled AFP-slope HR (95% CI) excludes 1 and
  HR > 1, **AND** interaction p ≥ 0.05 (or interaction HR CI includes 1), **AND** interval-scheduled
  stratum has ≥ 30 progression events.
- **Adverse** (signal is a scan-triggering artefact): AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 in the **triggered**
  stratum only, **OR** a significant interaction (p < 0.05) with triggered HR materially larger than the
  interval-scheduled HR.
- **Precise null / de-emphasis**: interval-scheduled AFP-slope HR CI entirely within a pre-set
  equivalence band (0.8–1.25) with ≥ 30 events → AFP-only monitoring de-emphasised.
- **Inconclusive**: interval-scheduled stratum < 30 progression events, or slope CI width too wide to
  exclude the equivalence band (underpowered interaction).
Every branch is reported as computed; the solver must not pick the branch after seeing numbers.

**The contrast REDUCES but cannot eliminate informative observation.** A lab can also trigger imaging
outside w; scheduled imaging can itself be shifted by clinical gestalt; the progression descriptor is a
lexical flag on unadjudicated reports. **Radiologist-adjudicated mRECIST progression remains required**
and is not computed here though it is the flag finally used.

## 5. Method comparison (recorded before selection)

- **M0 (baseline, simple):** landmark Cox on baseline AFP + ALBI value (no trajectory), age, sex,
  index year; complete-case; naive time-to-event. Falsification anchor: is there ANY trajectory signal?
- **M4 (mechanistic/learned alternative):** joint linear mixed-effects model of the AFP and ALBI
  longitudinal processes with patient-level random slopes; fitted slopes (BLUPs) enter the stratified
  Cox. **What M4 reveals beyond M0:** M0 collapses each patient to one noisy time point and cannot
  separate within-patient slope from between-patient level; M4 borrows strength across draws, shrinks
  per-patient slope noise, and yields shrinkage-corrected slopes directly comparable across the two
  imaging strata — essential because interrupted follow-up (informed observation) biases the number and
  timing of draws. **Budget:** CPU-feasible; nlme/lme4 + survival on ≤ a few thousand subjects and
  ≤ ~10 draws each is a minutes-scale fit; no GPU required. Model-based SEs via profile/nonparametric
  bootstrap (≥ 500 resamples, bounded).

## 6. Rival explanations → distinct tests

1. **Detection-timing artefact** → stratified slope estimates + slope×stratum interaction (§3–4).
2. **Same-draw liver reserve** → AFP slope vs ALBI slope entered jointly (M0 vs M4).
3. **Scan-triggering / informative observation** → the interval-scheduled vs triggered contrast (§3),
   backed by the parent's imaging-intensity hazard.
4. **Baseline severity confounding** → adjust for baseline AFP, ALBI value, index year, age, sex.
