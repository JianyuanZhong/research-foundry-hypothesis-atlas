## Frozen population, temporal rules, variables, and outcome

The diagnostic exactly preserves the parent’s primary construction:

- Parse ISO dates and finite numbers.
- Require `365 <= L-B <= 2922` calendar days.
- Impute date of birth to the 15th of recorded birth month and require completed age 40–75 inclusive at `L`.
- Require complete finite instance-0 and instance-1 values for haemoglobin 30020, MCV 30040, and platelet count 30080. No CBC imputation is allowed.
- Define entry `E=L+366 days`, horizon `H=L+1825 days`, washout `[L,E)`, and follow-up `[E,H]`. An event exactly on `E` is included in follow-up.
- Normalize ICD-10 by trimming, uppercasing, and removing periods. Malignancy is C00–C97; CRC is C18–C20 including descendants.
- Preserve exact suffix provenance and collapse exact participant/date/code duplicates only after same-suffix construction.
- Exclude a participant with first recorded malignancy or death before `L`, then exclude first recorded malignancy or death in `[L,E)`.
- Include in the fallback only a first recorded malignancy in `[E,H]` that strictly precedes death. Under the primary death-first rule, cancer on the same date as death is excluded.
- Define `Y=1` if any CRC code is present on the first malignant date and `Y=0` if the first malignant date has only non-CRC malignancy. A same-first-day CRC/non-CRC event would be classified CRC primarily and audited. Participants with no recorded malignant diagnosis are absent from this case-type population, never assigned `Y=0`.
- Define diagnosis lag as `first_malignant_date-E` in days and freeze the nuisance category as early 0–365 versus late 366–1459.
- For marker `m`, define `slope_m=(m_1-m_0)/((L-B)/365.2425)`. The three adverse-oriented primary trajectories are `-slope_Hb`, `-slope_MCV`, and `+slope_platelet30080`.

The conditional estimand remains:

`P(recorded CRC | first recorded malignant diagnosis in [E,H], eligible recorded history, complete protocolized primary CBC pairs, diagnosis before death)`.

It is not a population CRC-risk estimand.

## Falsification and interpretation

Implementation must fail if it aliases 30070 as platelet or 30080 as RDW; cross-pairs cancer date/code suffixes; moves exact day `E` into washout; includes cancer on or after death under the primary death-first rule; assigns a no-record participant to non-CRC; leaks held-out information into scaling or penalty selection; changes paired outer splits between models; or evaluates repeated held-out predictions as independent rows rather than averaging by participant.

The diagnostic’s own reconciliation criteria were prespecified and passed: 502,370 joined eids; 16,332 washout-pass participants; 1,001 first malignancies before death ordering; and 998 death-first cases, 75 CRC and 923 non-CRC. A mismatch would have made the diagnostic repairable or rejected pending explanation.

Supportive diagnostic evidence means that exact cohort reconciliation succeeds, required label/lag cells exist, nested splitting is executable, and no design-breaking covariate or field failure appears. Those conditions were met. This supports proceeding to the locked pipeline only; it does not support the CBC hypothesis.

Adverse diagnostic evidence would have included irreconcilable cohort counts, absent or tiny required strata causing split failure, systematic missingness of a primary field after cohort construction, or a semantic/event-order violation. No such failure was observed. The comparatively sparse early-CRC and early-female-CRC cells remain precision warnings rather than failures.

An inconclusive diagnostic result would include unexplained count disagreement, failed split construction, or insufficient aggregate detail to distinguish field or temporal errors. The current diagnostic is conclusive for computational support and descriptive composition, while the scientific trajectory hypothesis remains unresolved.

For the later model analysis, supportive evidence requires the parent’s hierarchy: `Delta_logloss<0` with a 95% interval excluding zero and stratified-permutation `p<0.05`; only then may positive `Delta_AUC` with an interval excluding zero be interpreted as supportive discrimination. Even then the conclusion is limited to recorded CRC versus the observed recorded non-CRC case mixture conditional on diagnosis lag and selection. Adverse evidence is precise deterioration or non-improvement under the hierarchy, or reproducibly opposite trajectory behavior. Inconclusive evidence includes broad intervals, metric discordance, unstable resampling, failed permutation/temporal falsification, or sensitivity to unresolved same-day order. `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED` makes population prognosis inconclusive regardless of fallback findings.

## Ascertainment, collider, missing evidence, and verifier boundary

Every contrast conditions on selected repeat attendance, complete paired protocolized CBCs, survival to the first recorded malignant diagnosis, and the occurrence/recording of a malignant diagnosis during a fixed interval. CBC trajectories, health-care contact, diagnosis timing, cancer site, survival, and recording processes may jointly affect inclusion. Conditioning on this selected case set can induce ascertainment and collider bias. Consequently, no-record participants cannot serve as controls, and a positive case-type contrast cannot be translated to population incidence, sensitivity, PPV, calibration, screening performance, lead time, referral policy, or benefit.

Symptoms, FIT, colonoscopy, pathology, stage, treatment, screening status, referral actions, harms, costs, and clinical adjudication are unavailable in the four bound tables. Registry completeness, exact-release applicability, delayed registration, national-source membership, and within-day event order remain uncertified. Stronger conclusions require a release-matched coverage certificate, clinical/registry adjudication, validation in clinically timed routine-care CBC data with appropriate non-case follow-up, and prospective impact evaluation.

An automatic verifier can check exact paths and hashes, required columns, complete-row scans, key uniqueness, one-to-one joins, exact suffix pairing, corrected 30080/30070 semantics, cohort flow, temporal boundaries, class and lag counts, split construction, feature summaries, aggregate site composition, absence-as-control prohibition, and whether later numerical conclusions obey the frozen hierarchy. It cannot establish registry completeness, adjudicate cancer type or same-day ordering, prove incident disease or mechanism, remove collider bias, establish transportability, or infer patient benefit.
