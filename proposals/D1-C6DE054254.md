# Early post-TACE reserve decline and independently corroborated hepatic decompensation in recorded-compensated HCC

## Scientific deliverable and clinical decision

This is a substantive successor to the selected HCC proposal. It preserves its exact first high-specificity TACE index and recorded-compensated cohort, its baseline window, its days 3–14 early window, and its days 15–90 follow-up. The repair is to separate a broad diagnosis-record signal from a stricter, timed two-source corroboration and to measure ascertainment intensity before the outcome begins.

The new scientific deliverable is a prespecified estimate of whether early post-TACE liver-reserve worsening predicts a two-source corroborated recorded hepatic-decompensation event in the subsequent 15–90 days, and whether that association is distinguishable from a diagnosis-only signal that may be more sensitive to care and documentation intensity. The primary effect-modification estimand remains the baseline-reserve-by-early-worsening interaction, now applied to the corroborated endpoint. A competing-endpoint analysis estimates the corresponding risk for diagnosis-only events. This is an observational prognostic study; it does not estimate the causal effect of TACE, surveillance, documentation, or repeat treatment.

Completion requires:

- a versioned cohort-flow and ascertainment-opportunity table with one row per patient-index;
- exact normalized TACE, HCC, decompensation and examination-concept dictionaries, with raw-label counts and uncertain/negated exclusions;
- baseline and early six-marker mappings, missingness, timing delay and measurement-intensity tables;
- a primary fitted model for the corroborated endpoint with the early-worsening-by-baseline-reserve interaction, standardized absolute-risk contrasts and patient-bootstrap 95% intervals;
- a competing-risk/multinomial table separating no qualifying diagnosis, diagnosis-only, and diagnosis-plus-timed-examination events;
- locked-test predictions from a static pre-index baseline and a temporal learned alternative for the same corroborated endpoint, with Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, AUROC, AUPRC and decision-curve net benefit when event support permits;
- early-intensity-stratified, intensity-adjusted, vocabulary, assay, horizon and follow-up sensitivity outputs;
- a conclusion table whose claims point to computed output paths and label clinical-adjudication requirements.

A schema audit or a model fit without the corroboration and ascertainment decomposition is not completion.

The clinical decision relevance is deliberately narrow. If laboratory worsening identifies patients who subsequently have concordant diagnostic and examination records, clinicians would have a stronger basis for prospective adjudication and intensified observation after TACE than a diagnosis-only association provides. If it predicts only diagnosis-only events or is confined to high-intensity measurement strata, the appropriate conclusion is that the snapshot does not validate a biological reserve-warning signal. Neither result establishes that monitoring or changing subsequent treatment improves outcomes.

## Hypothesis, existing evidence and unresolved claim

Primary hypothesis: among patients with HCC undergoing their first recorded high-specificity TACE who have no previously recorded hepatic decompensation, greater worsening of the six-marker liver-reserve trajectory during index+3 through index+14 is associated with a larger increase in the probability of a new two-source corroborated hepatic-decompensation record during index+15 through index+90 among patients with higher recorded baseline reserve than among those with lower recorded baseline reserve.

The corroborated event is not called adjudicated decompensation. For a component c, it requires both:

1. a qualifying diagnosis label for c in diagnoses, linked to a patient and encounter whose encounter-derived time is in index+15 through index+90; and
2. a row in examinations for the same patient and encounter key, with 开始时间 in the same outcome window and 检查所见 or 检查诊断 containing the same prespecified concept c.

The primary composite uses ascites (腹水), hepatic encephalopathy (肝性脑病), liver failure (肝衰竭, including only explicitly mapped acute/chronic variants), and gastrointestinal bleeding (上消化道出血/消化道出血). Concept matching is component-specific: a diagnosis of ascites paired with an examination mention of ascites qualifies; an unrelated examination mention does not. Uncertain labels such as 疑似 and 待查, and explicit negative/history-only mentions when reliably identified by the prespecified lexical rules, are excluded from the primary positive flag and retained in an audit table. The lexical detector is not a clinical adjudicator.

The broad endpoint is a new qualifying diagnosis in the same window, using the parent’s exact diagnosis dictionary. Each patient is classified as no qualifying diagnosis, diagnosis-only (broad diagnosis without same-concept timed examination corroboration), or two-source corroborated. A diagnosis-only event is not silently treated as evidence against disease: the competing-endpoint analysis asks whether the early signal preferentially predicts corroborated records or only records whose ascertainment may be more intense.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is that the local HCC snapshot has linked encounters, procedures, diagnoses, timed examination records and repeated laboratory records that can support a bounded observed-care prognostic analysis. A bounded read-only feasibility scan using the inherited procedure predicate found broad events and same-encounter examination mentions in nonzero numbers, but it did not establish the final assay-complete cohort or clinical validity. The examination and document fields are unvalidated narrative proxies. The snapshot does not establish complete treatment capture, complete outpatient capture, or adjudicated decompensation.

The unresolved claim is therefore narrower and falsifiable: early laboratory worsening has reproducible prognostic information for a more independently corroborated recorded event, beyond what can be explained by baseline covariates and measured pre-outcome care intensity. It is not a claim that TACE caused the worsening, that the event reflects a biological state rather than another illness, or that the signal improves treatment decisions.

## Primary estimand and ascertainment analysis

Let B be the continuous training-derived baseline reserve score, W the early-worsening score, X the pre-index covariates and A the process-intensity vector known by index+14:

- number of linked encounter keys in index+3 through index+14;
- number of all valid lab rows and number of reserve-assay rows in that window;
- number of distinct lab timestamps/dates and reserve-assay availability indicators;
- number of linked examination rows and distinct examination encounter keys in that window;
- baseline and early assay delays and missingness.

The primary model is a prespecified penalized logistic regression for the binary two-source corroborated outcome, with W, B, W×B, X, baseline assay values/availability/recency, and A. The scientific interaction is the coefficient and standardized absolute-risk contrast for W×B, not AUROC. Use training-derived centering and scaling only. Report standardized 90-day corroborated risk at W=P10 and W=P90 at B=P10, P50 and P90, plus the risk difference and bootstrap 95% interval. The primary null is no interaction on the model scale and no prespecified clinically meaningful absolute-risk difference; the margin must be declared before fitting and not chosen from the test set.

Because A is partly downstream of early worsening, its adjusted analysis is explicitly an ascertainment robustness estimand, not a causal effect. Report two versions:

- the process-agnostic prognostic association adjusted for pre-index covariates and baseline measurement opportunity; and
- the ascertainment-robust standardized association additionally adjusted for A, with a complete-case and missingness-indicator implementation.

Do not present the second as “the biological effect.” Compare the W association and absolute risks across A quartiles and test W×A descriptively. If the association disappears only after A adjustment, or exists only among high-A patients, that is evidence of unresolved measurement/ascertainment dependence.

Fit the broad diagnosis-only outcome in the same complete-window cohort. A prespecified multinomial or competing-risk table reports cumulative probability of no diagnosis, diagnosis-only, and corroborated diagnosis by day 90. Cause-specific models use the same W, B, X and A definitions. The key contrast is whether W predicts corroborated events more strongly or consistently than diagnosis-only events, with uncertainty. The two-source endpoint is not assumed to be more sensitive; discordant diagnosis-only events remain an explicit adverse or inconclusive finding.

The clinical-document source is a secondary three-source audit: among diagnosis-plus-examination events, flag whether a same-encounter clinical_documents field contains the matching concept. Because clinical documents lack a native time and may repeat diagnoses or describe history, this audit is descriptive and cannot upgrade the endpoint to adjudicated decompensation.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The simple baseline is a regularized logistic model for the corroborated outcome using only information available through index: age, sex, department, prior HCC/decompensation history, baseline six-marker values, baseline reserve score, assay availability/recency, and index follow-up eligibility. It excludes early labs, examination text, procedure-label text as an outcome proxy, and all future encounters. The same static model is also fit to the broad diagnosis outcome so that endpoint-specific calibration is visible.

The substantive learned alternative uses patient-level temporal gradient-boosted trees for the same corroborated endpoint. Inputs are ordered reserve-assay observations from index−14 through index+14, with assay identity, numeric value, time-from-index, baseline/early indicator, within-assay delay, measurement count/missingness, age, sex, prior history, index department, early encounter/examination counts, and no outcome-window records. Train/validation/test are patient-level 70/15/15 splits, stratified by the corroborated outcome; use a later-index holdout if the event count supports it. Fit/tune only on training/validation, calibrate on validation, and lock test predictions before endpoint comparisons. Cap the search at 500 trees and 10 configurations. Feature-group permutation summaries are exploratory; they do not replace the prespecified interaction estimate.

This alternative can reveal nonlinear reserve thresholds, whether timing/asynchronous assays carry information lost by one composite change score, and whether measurement-process variables dominate apparent signal. The baseline preserves transparent calibration and makes the endpoint/ascertainment distinction inspectable. Both models therefore answer the same prognostic question; the learned model is not justified by a small predictive gain alone.

The learned model is a feasibility-gated analysis. If final corroborated events are fewer than 50, or any test split has fewer than 10 events, report the event-support failure and do not make a comparative utility claim; the primary interaction and multinomial ascertainment estimates remain the scientific deliverable with wide uncertainty. A latent mixed-effects/GP reserve-state model is deferred because local laboratory units are not supplied and the independent corroborated event is sparse; revisit only if assay equivalence, repeated trajectories and event support are documented. A transformer/generative model is deferred because it would add sequence capacity without resolving endpoint validity. Revisit either model only if a later audit shows adequate event density and a specific scientific need for individualized latent trajectories.

## Uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Use patient bootstrap resampling for the primary interaction, standardized risk contrasts, multinomial contrasts, calibration and performance metrics. If bootstrap samples have no event or no corroborated event, use a documented stratified bootstrap rule or report the metric as undefined; do not silently impute stability. Report event counts, effective complete-case size, follow-up completeness, missingness, assay-mapping uncertainty and split seed.

Prespecified falsification and sensitivity analyses are:

- inherited high-specificity versus broader TACE vocabulary, without changing the primary index after results;
- exact primary assays versus only validated acute-labelled equivalents;
- INR versus PT sensitivity;
- complete-case versus missingness-indicator exposure models;
- no index-encounter labs in the early window;
- 30-day and 90-day outcome horizons and an early 0–30-day descriptive window, keeping the primary 15–90 separation;
- diagnosis-only versus diagnosis-plus-timed-examination endpoint, each component separately;
- A-stratified and A-adjusted analyses, with early examination/lab count definitions frozen before outcome extraction;
- censored time-to-recorded-event sensitivity among patients without a complete day-90 encounter;
- secondary diagnosis-plus-examination-plus-document concordance;
- an examination-count/process endpoint to show whether W predicts observation intensity itself.

Supportive results are a directionally positive W×B interaction for the two-source endpoint with a reasonably bounded interval, consistent absolute-risk contrasts across the prespecified baseline-reserve values, and similar direction after ascertainment-intensity adjustment. Reproducible improvement over the static baseline in locked-test calibration/Brier or decision-curve net benefit is supportive only of incremental prediction. This would support a prospective adjudication study of an early reserve-warning phenotype in observed-care HCC.

Adverse results are a null or reverse interaction for corroborated events, a diagnosis-only association with no corroborated counterpart, collapse after intensity adjustment or low-A restriction, strong dependence on a broad procedure/outcome vocabulary, or a learned model that uses encounter/examination frequency while adding no calibrated corroborated-event information. These findings refute the local claim that early reserve worsening is a robustly corroborated prognostic signal. They do not prove that post-TACE biology is irrelevant.

Inconclusive results are too few corroborated events, wide intervals, inability to identify complete-window negatives, strong assay ambiguity, missing examination coverage, dependence on document timing assumptions, unstable component-specific results, or lack of a valid patient-level locked test split. This means the snapshot cannot resolve biological corroboration versus ascertainment intensity. It is not a license to report the broad diagnosis result as confirmation.

Automatic verification can check the snapshot and schema hashes, read-only source access, raw-label dictionaries, first qualifying TACE logic, HCC and recorded-compensation exclusions, join keys, parsed times, strict exposure/outcome separation, same-concept same-encounter corroboration, complete-window negative rules, process-variable cutoff, patient-level split, model artifacts, bootstrap calculations, and conclusion-to-output links. It cannot establish that examination language denotes active decompensation, that diagnosis/examination sources are clinically independent, that unrecorded events or deaths are absent, or that the signal is causal or clinically useful. Those require expert chart adjudication, complete longitudinal/death data, tumor stage and response data, external validation, and ultimately prospective evaluation.

## Clinical evidence limits and provenance

Unavailable evidence remains: validated hepatic-decompensation adjudication and grade; clinician-confirmed active-versus-history/negative context for all narrative text; complete death, transplant and outpatient capture; transplant-free survival; tumor stage, burden, radiographic response or recurrence; TACE dose, intent and technical completeness; reliable hospital identity; and treatment or monitoring decisions after the index. No claim about death, staging, treatment response, causality, repeat-treatment benefit or deployment is allowed.

The natural-history demonstration was inspected through its locally available article/method material and supports the general idea that dated structured histories can be modeled; its UK Biobank cohort and Danish validation are unavailable, so this is not a reproduction. The cancer Oncoformer main article and complete STAR Methods remain unavailable; only the accessible supplement supports a bounded lab-only adaptation, not a multimodal reproduction, and HCC images are unavailable. The Bayesian ALADYNOULLI paper materials support consideration of longitudinal latent-state modeling, but missing genetic inputs and the sparse corroborated endpoint make that a deferred alternative here. These references do not establish this HCC hypothesis.
