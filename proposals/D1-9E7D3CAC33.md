# Clinically anchored pre-label verification without a clinical gold standard

## Purpose and substantive advance

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent correctly adds roster-disjoint holistic reviewers and counterfactual controls, but it still asks several independent readers to infer route-defining facts from the same artifact. Agreement therefore cannot distinguish a clinically meaningful routing error from a shared interpretation of a misleading or incomplete note. It also places a large three-panel/control burden on a fixed sample of at most 900 admissions.

This child adds a **pre-label, clinically anchored verification layer** for a small, design-based subset and simplifies the holistic challenge. The verifier does not decide whether a recommendation is guideline-concordant, whether the patient needed follow-up, whether a clinician was responsible, or whether care was safe. It verifies only narrowly stated, observable propositions against a frozen evidence packet and records the provenance and temporal sufficiency of each proposition. The propositions are deliberately closer to facts that can be checked than to the downstream route label:

* whether the radiology chain contains an explicit pulmonary-nodule/lesion finding rather than a lexical mention alone;
* whether an explicit action/recommendation is present, and what action, polarity, and target it names;
* whether timing language is explicit and interpretable as a relative interval or only vague/absent;
* whether an intended recipient/team is explicitly named, or is unknown;
* whether the same target has an explicit replacement, contradiction, or unresolved duplicate recommendation; and
* whether the candidate evidence was stored before or after the canonical discharge artifact and before physical discharge as represented by `D`.

Each proposition is independently verified by two clinically trained reviewers using separate, independently written anchor codebooks, with an optional third verifier only for pre-specified high-impact discordance. Their outputs remain measurements, not truth. A source-provenance audit and a pre-label “clinically anchored” review make shared codebook error visible as a failure to cite a qualifying span, an unresolvable temporal mismatch, or a mismatch between the anchor proposition and the route-defining atomic state. The design therefore tests a stronger and more clinically relevant claim than agreement: **a route can be nominated only when its atomic inputs are both reproducible and traceably supported by a narrowly defined observable anchor, under adverse-compatible uncertainty**.

The workload reduction is material. Every inherited sampled admission still receives the parent's component extraction, exhaustive recommendation-unit states, seals, all-route audit, worst-unit routing, and support-atlas accounting. Only a deterministic, pre-label subset of at most 180 admissions receives the paired holistic challenge and anchor verification. A further maximum of 60 discordant/high-impact cases may receive a third verifier, selected by rules fixed before labels, not by favorable route results. No admission is added, replaced, adaptively enriched, or selected because a model or reviewer has already indicated a preferred route.

## Evidence boundary, hypothesis, and decision

The available records support construction of a reproducible first-eligible admission frame, linking radiology text to admissions, deriving within-admission storage and discharge intervals, and observing service/ICU proxies. They do not establish that a note was visible at physical departure, that the named or coded team owned the task, that a recommendation was clinically appropriate, that an order or referral was executed, or that follow-up or outcome occurred. Multiple reviewer agreements do not change those limitations.

The unresolved claim is whether the parent's support-qualified atoms have route-defining states that are sufficiently anchored in observable text and chronology to justify a **prospective silent validation nomination**, rather than merely agreeing across text readers. The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> For every atom placed on an `AUTO`, `RECON`, or `TIMING` allow-list (and for any `DEFER` atom claimed to have a specific reason), the route-defining atomic propositions will meet the pre-label anchor protocol, the two independent anchor verifiers will be compatible with one another and with the sealed component states, and any clinically consequential unresolved proposition will be conservatively routed to `RECON` or `DEFER`. The result will remain valid when the small anchor subset is analyzed with adverse-compatible missingness and finite-population design weights.

A failure of anchor sufficiency excludes the affected atom even if H1/H2 agreement is high. A passing result supports only a within-site, silent validation of the exact observable signatures. It does not support a claim of correct clinical management or benefit.

## Frozen population, chronology, artifacts, and route semantics

All inherited definitions from `[prior hypothesis]` are immutable:

1. Use the complete first-eligible admission per subject in the parent's all-opportunity pulmonary-nodule population, the parent's adult rule, and the parent's exact inclusion/exclusion chronology. Do not replace it with a convenient radiology-positive cohort or a patient-level sample.
2. Preserve the reciprocal pre-departure radiology chain, including every relevant restatement/addendum relationship and every recommendation unit. Preserve canonical discharge selection and the discharge artifact used by the parent. Let `D = admissions.dischtime`; retain missing and conflicting times as explicit states rather than silently imputing them.
3. Preserve the parent's unit states `Y,V,H,Z`, split-view seals, independent packet hashes, route precedence, support atlas and exact atom `g=(possible-calendar class, terminal curr_service, workflow class)`. Preserve all-route, route-independent auditing and worst-unit admission routing. A single safe-looking unit cannot mask a conflicting unit in the same admission.
4. Preserve `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` precedence, finite-population estimands, design weights, default-`DEFER` handling, and every domain-specific no-rescue rule. Unknown, rare, unsupported, and novel signatures remain `DEFER`; anchor review never turns unknown into negative evidence.

The new anchor layer may change only the certification/verification evidence and the allocation of the already sampled challenge workload. It may not redefine the population, time boundary, artifact, unit states, route table, or estimand.

## Pre-label anchor protocol

### Anchor universe and allocation

Construct the full inherited `N_A`, five inherited outcome-blind strata, support atlas, exact atoms, residual envelope, and the inherited sample draw before opening any labels or challenge outputs. Keep sentinel census and every inherited finite-population sample size and inclusion probability unchanged. The complete selection must remain at most 900 admissions and must satisfy the inherited `NO_SELECTION` rule if the formula draw cannot fit.

From the already selected admissions, create a deterministic **anchor frame** before labels using protocol hash, admission key, and frozen snapshot. Allocate at most 180 admissions, with positive conditional probability within every occupied final atlas atom whenever feasible. Use a minimum of 2 per occupied atom only when this is feasible under the 180 ceiling; if not, record the atom as anchor-infeasible and do not certify it. Reserve at most 60 of the 180 for the third-verifier rule below. The selection is independent of component labels, routes, disagreement, text lexical score, and outcomes. Publish the realized conditional inclusion probability for every sampled admission and use inherited design weights; never use a complete-case or unweighted shortcut.

Every inherited admission still gets the component labels and all-unit audit. The anchor frame receives two holistic packets and two anchor-verifier packets. To reduce review burden, H1 and H2 are performed only on this frame, rather than on every inherited sampled admission. The parent's component audit remains the population-level reproducibility screen; holistic and anchor estimates are explicitly conditional on anchor eligibility and cannot be generalized as if all 900 were challenged.

### Evidence packet and blinding

Generate packets from a sealed raw extract before any human label. Each anchor packet contains the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, the canonical discharge artifact and permitted detail fields, admission/disposition/death fields permitted by the parent, exact missingness indicators, and source note IDs/times. It excludes component labels, routes, stratum, atom identity, weights, post-`D` notes, POE/POE-detail, and any outcome. H1 and H2 receive roster-disjoint admissions, independent pseudonyms and orderings, no shared training cases, and independently written/hashes of codebooks. Anchor reviewers receive the same source artifact in a separate packet but do not see H1/H2 outputs, component states, route, or atom.

The anchor codebooks are frozen and hashed before packet construction. `AV1` and `AV2` each provide, for every proposed atomic proposition, one of `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `INDETERMINATE`, or `NOT-OBSERVABLE`, plus exact note ID, field/ordinal or text-span offsets, temporal relation, and a one-sentence reason. `NOT-OBSERVABLE` is distinct from `CONTRADICTED`. A reviewer may cite more than one span, but cannot create a fact by paraphrase. The codebooks use independently worded definitions and examples and are not derived by copying the parent's route labels.

### Clinically anchored, but not a gold standard

The anchor verifier is clinically trained in radiology or care-transition review and receives a short operational charter. The charter asks whether the artifact contains sufficient explicit evidence for the narrow proposition, not whether the proposition is clinically correct in the real world. For example, an explicit sentence such as “follow-up CT in 12 months” can support an observable timing proposition; it does not prove that 12 months is appropriate, that a referral was sent, or that follow-up happened. A named service in prose can support “the text names service X”; it does not prove service X accepted responsibility. If a note's meaning depends on unavailable images, outside records, implicit coreference, or clinical judgment not stated in the artifact, the result is `NOT-OBSERVABLE` or `INDETERMINATE`.

For each admission, anchor propositions are generated from the parent’s already sealed atomic units but the verifier does not see the route label. The pre-label proposition schema is:

`A1`: target finding is explicit and attributable to the current study/report, versus negated, historical, family, hypothetical, or unresolved;

`A2`: action/recommendation is explicitly present, absent, or contradicted, with action class and polarity;

`A3`: target/action linkage is explicit, versus only inferred by proximity or unsupported coreference;

`A4`: timing is explicit relative/absolute text, vague, conflicting, or absent; no invented calendar date is allowed;

`A5`: recipient/responsibility is explicitly named, not named, or only inferred from service/author metadata;

`A6`: replacement, duplicate, addendum, or conflict relation is explicit in the reciprocal chain;

`A7`: the cited text is in an artifact with a defensible relation to `D` under the parent's `charttime/storetime` rules; missing or contradictory clocks are uncertainty states, not positive evidence.

The reviewer must verify the evidence trail before giving the state. The analysis creates an `anchor_compatibility` relation between each anchor result and the parent's unit state; it does not overwrite the parent state. An atomic route input is **anchor-supported** only if both AV1 and AV2 cite compatible evidence and neither has `CONTRADICTED`/`NOT-OBSERVABLE` on a route-critical proposition. Any disagreement on target identity, polarity, timing sufficiency, replacement/conflict, or temporal eligibility is adverse-compatible. The two outputs are retained separately.

### Third verifier and escalation

A third verifier `AV3` is used only when fixed rules identify a high-impact discordance before opening AV3 labels: (i) AV1 and AV2 disagree on a route-critical proposition; (ii) either reports an explicit contradiction or missing/invalid time for a route-critical proposition; or (iii) the admission's parent unit-level worst-case route would differ between the two adverse-compatible interpretations. The cap is 60 admissions. AV3 is roster-disjoint from AV1/AV2 where feasible, sees the same packet and both prior outputs only as blinded disagreement exhibits, and records a fresh proposition-level decision with cited evidence. AV3 is a tie-breaking measurement and audit, not a consensus truth procedure. If AV3 disagrees or cannot observe the proposition, preserve the compatible set and route conservatively; do not majority-vote it into safety.

## Simplified holistic challenge and workload accounting

H1 and H2 retain the parent's independent whole-admission challenge and all locked controls, but only within the pre-label anchor frame. Each challenge case receives:

* one route-irrelevant negative pair preserving all route-relevant spans;
* one route-decisive positive control changing one locked atomic field;
* one omission/addition pair only if the requisite span is unambiguous under the pre-label parser; and
* one nuisance-format/author-token pair where semantics are preserved.

Controls are generated from a locked transformation manifest before any labels and are stored separately from source text. H1/H2 are blinded to pair type and expected transition. Control-infeasible cases remain explicit and count against the adverse-compatible upper bound; they are never hand-edited or silently removed. H1/H2 must reproduce the expected invariant/locked transition, but control performance is a test of internal measurement fidelity, not clinical correctness.

The maximum work is 900 admission-level inherited audits plus 180 admissions x (two holistic readers + two anchor verifiers), with at most 60 of those receiving AV3. Duplicate units and duplicate restatements do not create additional admissions. The pre-label workload ledger reports admissions, packet count, reviewer-hours, control count, and conditional inclusion probabilities. If the governance-approved physician-hour budget or the 180-case anchor cap is exceeded, the design returns `NO_SELECTION` or leaves the affected atom inconclusive; it must not reduce the inherited sample or selectively omit difficult cases. The 180 frame is a workload optimization, not a new population.

## Estimands, baselines, and primary analysis

The primary population-level estimands remain exactly the parent's finite-population estimates and simultaneous adverse bounds for unit-state reproducibility, route capture, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `leak_AUTO`, timing validity, workload, and all-route worst-unit outcomes. Compute them using the inherited admission-level draw, design weights, sentinel census handling, and Rao-Wu finite-population replicates (20,000 as inherited), retaining all units and all missingness states.

New estimands are explicitly conditional on the pre-label anchor frame and reported by atom and route:

* proportion with both AV1/AV2 anchor-compatible for each route-critical proposition;
* upper adverse-compatible rate of anchor-versus-component incompatibility;
* upper rate at which a route-critical unresolved anchor would change the all-unit worst-case route;
* H1/H2 route discordance conditional on anchor eligibility;
* route-invariant control flips, locked decisive-control violations, and packet/control infeasibility; and
* the fraction of admissions/atoms for which AV3 was required and remained unresolved.

Use a compatible-set analysis rather than a latent-class truth model. For each admission, retain all states consistent with the observed AV1/AV2/AV3 outputs, cited evidence, and missingness. The lower endpoint counts only jointly compatible favorable states; the upper endpoint assigns every unresolved, nonresponse, missing-clock, and control-infeasible state to the adverse route-compatible state. No reviewer consensus is privileged. A latent-class model is diagnostic only and cannot narrow the primary interval without an external, independently justified identifiability constraint established before labels.

The primary certification rule is atom-specific and conjunction-based. A support-qualified atom can be nominated only if every inherited gate passes and, within the anchor frame when feasible:

* at least 20 anchor admissions and at least 10 with each required route-critical proposition observed; if fewer are available, the atom is `INCONCLUSIVE/DEFER` rather than padded;
* the simultaneous lower bound for AV1/AV2 compatibility is at least 0.90 for an `AUTO` or `TIMING` nomination and at least 0.85 for `RECON`; this is an operational verification gate, not a correctness probability;
* the simultaneous upper bound for anchor-versus-component incompatibility is at most 0.05 for `AUTO`/`TIMING` and 0.10 for `RECON`;
* no unresolved anchor proposition can make an `AUTO` admission compatible with `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`; if it can, the admission is conservatively `RECON` or `DEFER` under inherited precedence;
* route-invariant control flips have simultaneous upper bound <=0.02, decisive-control violations <=0.05, and all missing/nonresponse/control-infeasible states are included adversely; and
* the anchor-frame conditional workload and AV3 escalation upper bounds remain within the preapproved budget.

For `DEFER`, the anchor layer may document a specific observable reason but may not certify safety. For atoms with fewer than 20 anchor cases, with zero/rare events, or with wide bounds, report `INCONCLUSIVE` and retain default `DEFER`. There are no top-ups, atom merging, favorable consensus rescue, pooled rescue, neighboring-domain rescue, macro-service rescue, model imputation, or hierarchical shrinkage rescue.

Baselines are run on identical sealed source extracts: the parent-only component audit; parent plus H1/H2 without anchor gating; AV1/AV2 compatibility without H1/H2; majority/favorable-consensus diagnostics; a single-codebook duplicate diagnostic; pooled versus atom-specific summaries; and a latent-class sensitivity. Only the adverse-compatible anchor-plus-component analysis can nominate a route. The baseline comparison is diagnostic and must not be presented as evidence that consensus is true.

## Falsification and verification fixtures

The experiment falsifies the route-nomination hypothesis when any of the following occurs for a support-qualified atom:

1. AV1/AV2 cannot cite a qualifying span for a route-critical state, or one explicitly contradicts the state, at a rate crossing its adverse gate.
2. A route-critical disagreement remains unresolved after AV3, and its adverse-compatible interpretation changes the all-route worst-unit route.
3. A high `AUTO`/`TIMING` component agreement coexists with an anchor incompatibility bound above its gate.
4. A missing or contradictory `storetime/charttime` relation is treated as positive temporal evidence, or time uncertainty is deleted from the denominator.
5. A route-invariant formatting/author-token control changes route, or a one-field decisive control changes an unrelated route state.
6. An atom-specific failure is rescued using pooled performance, a neighboring atom, consensus majority, an inferred service owner, a latent-class point estimate, or a favorable complete-case analysis.
7. Any packet contains post-`D` text, parent labels/routes, atom/stratum/weight identity, POE information, or unsealed transformed text; any roster/codebook overlap or post-label selection invalidates the affected run.
8. The anchor sample is not selected before labels, conditional probabilities are not recorded, or the fixed 900-admission inherited sample is changed.

Mandatory computational fixtures include: a planted shared-wrong label with a syntactically convincing but uncited proposition (agreement may be high, but anchor compatibility must fail or remain adverse-compatible); pooled-safe/atom-unsafe data; explicit negation and historical mentions; duplicate versus replacement units; missing discharge `storetime`; contradictory reciprocal addendum ordering; route-invariant formatting changes; one-field timing/replacement changes; unsupported novel service/workflow signatures; missing high-weight anchor labels; and latent-class nonidentifiability. These fixtures test computation and logic, not clinical truth.

## Interpretation

**Supportive.** All inherited gates and atom-specific anchor/control gates pass, the anchor frame was feasible and pre-label, adverse-compatible bounds are sufficiently narrow, and no worst-unit route is changed by unresolved anchor states. This supports only nomination of the exact observable atom for prospective within-site silent validation. It does not establish that the recommendation is appropriate, that a team will act, or that a patient will benefit.

**Adverse.** Any atom-level anchor failure, route-critical unresolved proposition affecting worst-unit routing, invariant-control flip, decisive-control violation, broken seal, or workload infeasibility blocks nomination of that atom. A shared wrong interpretation is specifically adverse-compatible even if all readers agree. An inherited global failure blocks the corresponding global claim. Adverse results are not estimates of clinical harm; they show that the proposed retrospective verification is insufficient for the intended nomination.

**Inconclusive.** Sparse anchor atoms, failure to achieve positive conditional inclusion in an occupied atom, too few observed route-critical propositions, wide compatible bounds, missing/nonresponse, infeasible controls, missing clocks, or AV3 residual discordance yield `INCONCLUSIVE` and default `DEFER`. Inconclusive is neither safety nor failure of the underlying clinical workflow.

## Unavailable evidence and next study

These files contain text reports and administrative/temporal proxies, not radiology images (MIMIC-CXR images are not present), complete clinical context, verified reviewer credentials, actual note viewing time, finalization/signature or transmission semantics, responsible-team acceptance, order/referral placement, scheduling, patient communication, outside-plan execution, follow-up completion, malignancy/pathology, appropriateness, patient preferences, harm, utility, or causal effects. `admit_provider_id`, note author-like fields, service transitions, transfers, and ICU locations cannot establish responsibility. Deidentified dates cannot establish forward-calendar transport. No claim about workflow completion or patient outcome may be made.

A passing retrospective anchor audit must therefore be followed by a separately approved prospective silent bridge that logs actual artifact visibility, edits/signatures, communication, order/referral and scheduling events, responsibility acceptance, and patient-level follow-up. Clinical appropriateness and benefit require expert adjudication and a prospective or otherwise independently designed outcomes study. The present design is valuable because it can falsify a routing nomination using traceability and conservative observable evidence while honestly refusing to label an unavailable clinical gold standard.
