## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by available evidence is only that dated local procedures, encounters and laboratory rows can be linked using the two stated identifiers, and that the source contains many relevant labels and exact serum assay names. The data do not establish tumor burden, viable tumor, radiologic response, treatment indication, intent, technical completion, hepatic reserve in clinical units, death, or care outside this institution.

The unresolved question is whether the early joint component pattern contains reproducible information about a later *coherent recorded retreatment pathway*, beyond pre-index history and observation/capture intensity, rather than merely predicting diagnostic contact or coding density.

Primary hypothesis: among adults with an eligible first recorded TACE-like episode, HCC diagnosis ascertainment near the index, and no primary-ontology liver-directed therapeutic episode through day 45, an AFP decrease with non-worsening assay-standardized albumin and total bilirubin has a different cause-specific probability and cumulative incidence of a subsequent strict TACE-containing retreatment episode than an AFP decrease with worsening of either hepatic component. This contrast should persist on the strict endpoint and remain directionally specific to liver-directed therapeutic records, not to diagnostic angiography, non-liver procedures, or a pre-index placebo trajectory.

The result falsifies the hypothesis if the component interaction is null/reversed on the strict endpoint, is erased by the coding/observation baseline, appears similarly for diagnostic-only or non-liver capture outcomes, is driven by ambiguous compound strings, or fails under encounter-only versus 24-hour episode construction. A null or adverse result is informative about the recorded-pathway hypothesis; it is not evidence of absent biological response.

## Outcomes and endpoint ladder

Create mutually exclusive first recorded states from days 46-365, with administrative observation loss handled as a competing/censoring state and reported separately:

- `TACE_strict` (primary): a timed episode containing literal `TACE`, or an explicitly frozen training-only list of labels containing arterial chemoembolization/embolization terms that do not consist solely of angiography and have no unresolved mixed-only interpretation. The exact vocabulary, exclusions and ambiguous labels are published before locked evaluation.
- `Liver_therapeutic_broad` (secondary): the parent broad liver-directed therapeutic ontology, including TACE, ablation, resection, transplant and other frozen therapeutic families, but never angiography alone.
- `Liver_diagnostic`: hepatic angiography, liver imaging/procedure work-up, or diagnostic-only liver contact under a frozen lexical procedure screen.
- `Mixed_ambiguous`: compound or unresolved strings not safely classifiable as strict therapy.
- `Nonliver_procedure`: first timed procedure outside the liver-directed therapeutic/diagnostic ontology, used as a capture control.
- `No recorded transition` or administrative end of local observation.

Use a strict-versus-broad endpoint ladder as a scientific robustness output, not as post hoc endpoint shopping. If a label cannot be classified from the frozen screen, retain it as ambiguous and report it; do not infer intent from `手术来源`. Diagnostic procedures are not therapeutic outcomes.

## Matched methods and alternatives

All methods share the cohort, index, day-45 information boundary, endpoint states, patient-level temporal split, uncertainty and locked test.

- B0: regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazards using age, sex, index year/department, pre-index diagnosis/procedure counts and prior local contact.
- Bobs/code: B0 plus testing/contact intensity, assay availability, examination/order opportunity, procedure-source mix, episode/coding density, and ambiguous-label indicators.
- Bmain: Bobs plus separate AFP, albumin and bilirubin standardized changes, timing and missingness.
- Bint (primary transparent test): Bmain plus the prespecified AFP-by-albumin-by-bilirubin interaction and component-specific contrasts, evaluated on strict and broad endpoints.
- Mstate (substantive alternative): an irregular-time multi-output state-space model with separate latent AFP, albumin and total-bilirubin states, assay-specific observation models, contact intensity and competing transition hazards. It can reveal asynchronous sampling, nonlinear change, uncertainty in components, and whether strict/broad pathway differences are driven by a single assay or a latent trajectory that endpoint categories lose. It must use only information through day 45 and the same outcomes/split; report posterior contrasts, calibration, log score, convergence and observation-model sensitivity.

The baseline is scientifically necessary because it quantifies incremental component/pathway information over prior history and coding/capture. Mstate is retained because the substantive uncertainty is asynchronous latent component evolution and informative observation, not a small discrimination gain. A generic transformer is deferred: the limiting dependencies are clinical intent, response adjudication, validated coding semantics, units, outside-care linkage and death—not sequence-model capacity. Revisit if those dependencies become available.

## Split, uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Use 2010-2022 for fitting, 2023 for tuning, and 2024 through the cutoff as locked test when event support permits; otherwise use a deterministic patient-hash split declared before fitting. No patient crosses splits. Freeze vocabulary, episode rules, threshold/tolerance, sparse-cell rule, endpoint ladder, weighting, model tuning and bootstrap count before locked evaluation.

Use patient-level bootstrap or an equivalent patient-clustered procedure; never row-bootstrap. Report cause-specific hazards, cumulative incidence at 30, 90 and 320 post-landmark days, calibration, overlap, log score, and absolute contrasts with intervals.

Supportive computation requires a non-null, materially relevant strict-pathway component interaction with adequate overlap/calibration and patient-level interval excluding the prespecified margin; directionally compatible strict and broad estimates; specificity stronger for therapeutic than diagnostic/non-liver outcomes; persistence after Bobs/code and training-only observation weighting; no pre-index placebo signal; and convergent Mstate with compatible component contrasts. This supports a robust association with a local recorded pathway only.

Adverse computation is null/reversal after coding/observation adjustment, similar diagnostic/non-liver/placebo associations, concentration in ambiguous bundles, dependence on one assay variant, endpoint or episode instability, poor overlap/calibration, or Mstate nonconvergence/observation-model sensitivity. These favor capture, coding, measurement or episode explanations.

Inconclusive computation is insufficient paired support, unstable assay vocabulary, sparse strict events, positivity failure, inadequate follow-up, temporal-split failure, intervals crossing the margin, or inability to separate diagnostic from therapeutic records. Null/adverse/inconclusive findings do not establish absence of biological response.

The verifier can check paths, headers, joins, time boundaries, no leakage, episode construction, vocabulary, competing-risk calculations, predictions, uncertainty, calibration and conclusion-to-output mapping. It cannot adjudicate indication, intent, completion, radiologic response, viable tumor, liver failure, death, survival, appropriateness, treatment benefit, utility, or outside care. Those require expert review and prospective/external outcome ascertainment.
