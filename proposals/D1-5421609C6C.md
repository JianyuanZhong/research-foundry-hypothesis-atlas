# A common-mode adjudication stress test for the minimax pulmonary-routing support atlas

## Decision bottleneck and substantive child change

The frozen clinical estimand is unchanged. In the first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity population, can the admission-level `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes be reproduced with sufficiently low **text-defined unsafe cross-route contamination** to justify only a prospective silent validation? The observation remains one first eligible admission per subject; every recommendation unit in a sampled admission is reviewed; one unsafe minority unit controls the admission; and no result is a claim about guideline correctness, clinician fault, patient safety, benefit, follow-up completion, or outcomes.

The parent candidate `[prior hypothesis]` materially repaired pooled domain masking by pre-label possible-calendar/exact-service/workflow atoms, a deterministic support atlas, maximin allocation, simultaneous atom-specific adverse finite-population bounds, an explicit allow-list, and default-`DEFER` treatment of unsupported signatures. Its strongest remaining threat is correlated adjudication error. Role separation and reciprocal views do not make physicians independent when all lanes ultimately inspect the same imperfect, templated, or copied clinical artifacts. Agreement, AC1, and a consensus-like cross-product can therefore be high while a shared misreading remains.

This child adds a pre-label, route-blind **common-mode error stress test** without changing the population, target unit, chronology, canonical discharge artifact, unit clocks, route precedence, sample-size ceiling, support atlas, estimands, or primary source files. Each sampled admission receives a fourth, independently rostered challenge panel that must reproduce atomic evidence from an explicitly different evidence slice, and every primary gate is re-evaluated under a prespecified bounded adversarial perturbation of agreements that rely on shared evidence. The perturbation is not presented as a validated probability of reviewer error. It is a finite-population sensitivity envelope: if a route is certifiable only when common-mode agreements are assumed correct, it is not certifiable here.

The child therefore repairs inference about reviewer reliability, not clinical truth. It makes the evidence ceiling narrower and explicit: even a pass can nominate only a silent bridge for the exact allow-list; no retrospective route is released.

## Supported claim, unresolved claim, and falsifiable hypothesis

### Strongest claim currently supported

The available snapshot contains the fields required to define the inherited population, split evidence views, exact recommendation units, pre-departure clocks, support atoms, and route-independent audits. In particular, the source catalog and inspected schemas provide admission times and disposition (`hosp/admissions`), subject age/year representation (`hosp/patients`), pre-departure service chronology (`hosp/services`), ICU exposure (`icu/icustays`), radiology narrative and chart/storage times (`note/radiology`), radiology detail rows (`note/radiology_detail`), and one canonical discharge-note artifact plus its detail rows (`note/discharge`, `note/discharge_detail`). These sources support reproducible documentation and reviewer-agreement experiments. They do not provide a gold-standard guideline decision, verified responsible clinician, actual report viewing, order/referral completion, scheduling, outside care, patient preference, or clinical outcome.

The parent also supports an outcome-blind, pre-label support atlas that prevents a pooled average or shrinkage model from certifying a failing observable era/service/workflow signature. That is a design property, not evidence that any signature is safe or that the route is clinically correct.

### Unresolved claim

Does a supported atom remain certifiable when agreement that is dependent on shared copied/template evidence is treated as potentially wrong, rather than treating nominally independent reviewer agreement as a gold standard? Can the route's text-defined contamination and capture gates survive an explicitly bounded common-mode perturbation while retaining the fixed <=900-admission workload and positive-probability design?

### Falsifiable hypothesis

For at least one pre-label support-atlas atom, and for every atom placed on the final allow-list, the inherited pooled and atom-specific adverse gates will hold simultaneously after adding the challenge-panel evidence and applying the fixed common-mode sensitivity envelope. In particular, `AUTO` must satisfy the inherited simultaneous upper bounds `U95(C_AUTO)<=0.05`, `U95(leak_AUTO)<=0.02`, `U95(D_MIX)<=0.10`, lower confirmation/AC1 thresholds, no adverse-compatible unresolved minority unit, and workload limits **at every approved common-mode budget** `kappa in {0.00, 0.05, 0.10}` of the relevant candidate-`AUTO` denominator. The `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` gates receive the analogous adverse perturbation to confirmation, capture, PPV, and leakage.

`kappa` is a governance parameter fixed before real labels are viewed; it is not estimated from favorable results and is not interpreted as a clinical error rate. A supported atom failing at any kappa falsifies nomination for that atom. If challenge evidence is unavailable, if the common-mode ambiguity set is too wide to support a gate, or if the fixed burden cannot accommodate the panel, the result is `NO_SELECTION` or `INCONCLUSIVE`, never a pass by assuming independence. Unsupported/unknown/novel atoms remain `DEFER` and are not evidence for or against the hypothesis.

## What is preserved exactly

The following are inherited without alteration and must be hashed into the protocol manifest.

1. **Population and temporal boundaries.** Stream all radiology and detail rows before unredacted discharge-plan review. Candidate reports are exact admission-linked and have `charttime` in `[admittime-12 hours,D]`, where `D=dischtime`; inherited high-recall pulmonary/nodule/opacity, recommendation, interval, thoracic-CT, or exam-code/name rules are frozen. Use one first eligible adult admission per subject sorted by `admittime`, then `hadm_id`. Exclusions remain screening, established surveillance, staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional/optional plans, explicit no follow-up, in-hospital death, locked hospice/comfort-only transition, and recommendations first introduced after `D`.
2. **Reciprocal chronology.** Require subject- and admission-consistent `AR` parent and corresponding `RR` addendum relations. Every operative component has `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`; missing, one-way, contradictory, unresolved, or untimed nodes remain `U_R`.
3. **Canonical artifact.** Require exactly one `note_type==DS` row per eligible admission, keyed by `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. Missing, duplicate, unreadable, unlinked, missing-storetime, or contradictory canonical artifacts remain unknown and stop the relevant run; `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.
4. **Exhaustive states and unit-specific clocks.** Preserve `Y={F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}`, `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}`, and all cells of `J_A`. For each operative unit retain `R_store,u`, `DS_store`, `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u`, and check `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`. Only unit-specific `G_u>=24 hours` permits content routing; under-24, stale, post-departure, and unknown clocks cannot become content failures.
5. **Units and route.** A unit is the smallest operative `(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity)` tuple. Different actions/windows remain separate; reciprocal restatements deduplicate; material addenda revise. Unresolved splitting is `U_BOUNDARY`. Preserve `U_RESOLVED,U_OPEN,U_TIMING,U_CLOSED,U_DEFER`, with `U_DEFER` then `U_TIMING` then complete closed/no-action precedence; `AUTO` requires every open unit wholly omitted, ample clocks, reproducible closure elsewhere, and no conflict; incomplete/conflicting/mixed admissions are `RECON`. One unsafe minority unit controls the admission.
6. **Design and support atlas.** Preserve the five outcome-blind strata `S_SENTINEL,S_TIMECLOCK,S_ZERO,S_PARTIAL,S_COMPLETE`, parent sample formulas, census sentinel, total ceiling of at most 900 admissions, fixed possible-calendar classes derived from widened `anchor_year_group` intervals, exact terminal `curr_service`, workflow classes, pre-label atlas pruning, residual envelope, maximin allocation, SRS without replacement, and positive first- and second-order inclusion probabilities. No route-, event-, response-, workload-, or result-based replacement, top-up, regrouping, or reallocation is allowed.
7. **Evidence firewalls.** Preserve disjoint eligibility, redaction, recommendation, context, departure-plan, and safety roles, two prespecified lanes for the inherited index and audit components, sealed label/code/access manifests, no communication, no real-case training or rehearsal, all-route audit for every sampled admission, and prohibition on using POE as a primary route, domain, label, or outcome source.
8. **Estimands and margins.** Preserve HT totals and Hájek ratios for route burden, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, `leak_AUTO`, confirmation, reproducibility, additional-unit rate, and workload. Preserve adverse compatible-assignment treatment of unknowns, missing audits, nonresponse, redaction/boundary failures, and route ambiguity, plus >=20,000 Rao–Wu replicates, exact finite-population inversion where applicable, and one max-statistic simultaneous family. The child adds terms to these same estimands; it does not invent a patient-level endpoint.
9. **Evidence ceiling.** A favorable result can nominate only a separately approved prospective silent bridge, with actual dates, report availability/viewing, finalization/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, preferences/competing risks, and final transmitted artifact measured prospectively. The current retrospective data cannot establish these.

## New common-mode module

### A. Pre-label challenge packet construction

After `N_A`, canonical keys, clocks, units, inherited strata, and the final support atlas/allocation are frozen, but before any component label is read or any route is computed, generate a challenge packet for every sampled admission. The challenge packet is not allowed to change the inherited index or audit labels. It is an additional diagnostic and robustness component that participates in the adverse certification envelope.

The challenge panel is roster-disjoint from eligibility, all inherited lanes, packet construction, and safety review. It has two independent readers with separately randomized admission order, role-specific pseudonyms, and no access to routes, domains, sampling cells, weights, other labels, or outcomes. Their packet contains only:

* the reciprocal admission-linked radiology chain, with `note_id`, subject identifiers, note sequence, and raw timestamps replaced by opaque within-packet tokens;
* a separately rendered, deterministic reconstruction of the same chain from all retained `note/radiology_detail` rows, preserving every `field_ordinal`, `field_name`, and `field_value` after the predeclared redaction rule;
* the canonical `DS` text only where the challenge task is the discharge-plan atomic field, rendered from `note/discharge` and not from any derived route packet; and
* a machine-readable time ledger showing only permitted relative relations (`charttime<=D`, `storetime<=D`, and unit-specific lag category), never the inherited domain category or route.

The challenge panel must independently enumerate the same atomic fields: target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity, recipient/responsibility, replacement/conflict, evidence citation, and uncertainty. It also records whether each asserted fact is supported by (i) a single exact source span, (ii) concordant spans in distinct notes within the reciprocal chain, (iii) the independently rendered detail record, or (iv) no retrievable source. A reviewer cannot mark a fact certain solely because it appears in a familiar template; the citation token must resolve to an immutable source row and ordinal.

The challenge packet deliberately does not claim to be an independent clinical gold standard. `radiology_detail` is a rendering of radiology documentation, and the discharge note is not an independent observation of guideline correctness. Their value is to reveal shared-artifact dependence and missing citation support. If the supposedly distinct rendering cannot be reconstructed without the same source span, that fact is coded `SHARED_SOURCE`, not counted as independent replication.

### B. Evidence-dependence variables

For every atomic fact and every admission, sealed code compares the two challenge readers and the inherited lanes only after all labels are immutable. It creates:

* `Q_shared`: the fraction of apparently agreeing route-critical facts whose cited source-row/ordinal set overlaps across readers or whose only support is one shared copied span;
* `Q_independent`: the fraction with agreement supported by nonoverlapping source spans and the detail reconstruction, without asserting clinical independence;
* `Q_missing`: unresolved or uncitable facts;
* `Q_template`: a predeclared deterministic flag for repeated identical sentence/field templates within an admission chain; and
* `Q_cross`: disagreement specifically between note narrative and detail-rendered reconstruction or between radiology and canonical discharge atomic fields.

These are secondary explanatory measurements, never substitutes for the inherited atomic labels. An empty citation, malformed ordinal, or source row unavailable at packet construction is adverse unknown, not a negative citation.

### C. Bounded common-mode ambiguity set

For each sampled admission, retain all inherited compatible route/event assignments. In addition, define a set of assignments in which up to `kappa` of the relevant route denominator's apparently agreeing, route-critical atomic facts may be wrong in a common direction if they satisfy `Q_shared=1` or `Q_template=1`, subject only to the following conservative constraints:

* the perturbed fact must be an observable atomic field; no invented patient fact is allowed;
* a perturbation may move an admission only to an already compatible inherited state (`RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` for an `AUTO` candidate, and the analogous adverse state for other routes);
* one admission remains one observation, and all affected units in that admission retain their dependency;
* unit precedence, `G_u`, departure state `A`, and the frozen `N_A` are not recomputed or favorably repaired; and
* the same perturbation assignment is used for every estimand in a bootstrap replicate, so contamination, capture, workload, and route totals cannot be optimized independently.

Use `kappa={0.00,0.05,0.10}` exactly, fixed before real labels. The `kappa=0` result is the inherited analysis. The `kappa=0.05` and `0.10` results are bounded sensitivity analyses, not probability statements. For each kappa, solve the adverse linear-fractional optimization over shared-citation perturbations jointly with the inherited missingness/unknown assignment. Report the widest valid endpoint across the exact finite-population calculation, Rao–Wu max-statistic replicates, and this ambiguity set. The allow-list is valid only if all relevant gates pass at all three kappa values.

The design does not assert that 10% is the true common-mode error rate. Governance must approve this stress envelope before packet release. If governance will not approve it, the protocol has no correlated-error certification and must return `NO_SELECTION` rather than silently reverting to an independence interpretation.

### D. Correlated-error certification gates

Add the following gates to the parent's atom-level gates.

1. At least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 non-sentinel admissions remain required; challenge response must be present for every admission contributing to a route denominator, or the missing challenge is assigned adverse-compatible states.
2. The simultaneous upper bound for each adverse route quantity must pass at `kappa=0.00,0.05,0.10`. In particular, `AUTO` requires `U95(C_AUTO)<=0.05`, `U95(leak_AUTO)<=0.02`, `U95(D_MIX)<=0.10`, lower confirmation and AC1 thresholds, and no common-mode-compatible unresolved minority unit. `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` retain all inherited capture, PPV, confirmation, leakage, and workload limits under the same envelope.
3. No route may claim challenge agreement as an independent gold-standard confirmation. Report `Q_shared`, `Q_independent`, `Q_missing`, `Q_template`, and `Q_cross` with design weights and uncertainty, but do not convert any into a clinical validity probability.
4. A high `Q_shared` or `Q_template` is not automatically failure; it becomes failure when the route's adverse bound fails under the corresponding kappa assignment. Conversely, a low shared-citation fraction cannot rescue a route whose ordinary adverse bounds fail.
5. A challenge packet showing that all available evidence is one shared rendering, or a challenge panel whose role/access separation is broken, invalidates the affected route/atom and blocks nomination.

The pooled parent gates, residual-envelope burden, support-atlas minimax gates, maximum normalized weight, ESS, retrieval-negative undercoverage, and <=900 distinct-admission ceiling remain mandatory. Challenge readings count toward physician-hours and the preapproved absolute workload budget; they do not increase the admission ceiling or permit fewer inherited reviews. If the added panel makes the workload budget infeasible, the result is a design failure, not a justification to sample fewer admissions.

## Analysis and executable workflow

Freeze a protocol hash over source IDs and schema hashes, parser/dictionary versions, inherited population/unit/route code, support-atlas code, challenge rendering code, kappa grid, role rosters, access permissions, workload budget, seeds, and output schemas.

1. Stream source rows in declared source-row order. Construct `N_A`, reciprocal chains, canonical `DS`, exhaustive inherited states, unit inventories, unit clocks, five inherited strata, and final pre-label support atlas. Assert all inherited stop conditions.
2. Freeze fixed sample cells, ordered IDs, `pi_i`, `pi_ij`, source-row manifests, packet hashes, and no-replacement lists. No real labels are read before this checkpoint.
3. Build inherited `R-I/K-I/S-I` and `R-A/K-A/S-A` packets and the common-mode challenge packets. Check role-disjoint access and canary isolation. Record all response/nonresponse; do not replace a selected admission.
4. Obtain labels independently. Seal initial labels, challenge atomic fields, source citations, and timestamps. A challenge reader may mark `UNCITABLE` or `SHARED_SOURCE`; neither is deleted.
5. Form the locked cross-product route states and all compatible adverse assignments. Keep all admissions and all units coupled within each assignment.
6. Compute weighted pooled, atom, residual, and diagnostic quantities. For each Rao–Wu replicate, jointly draw within every noncensus cell, retain an admission's complete label bundle, and optimize the same assignment across all outcomes.
7. For each `kappa`, constrain common-mode perturbations to shared/template-supported route-critical facts and to already compatible adverse states. Calculate exact/finite-population, bootstrap max-statistic, and adverse partial-identification endpoints; retain the widest. Use one simultaneous family spanning pooled gates, every final atlas atom, minimax endpoints, and all kappa values. Secondary citation-dependence summaries use a separately adjusted family.
8. Execute the inherited baselines under the same sample, weights, labels, and challenge availability: pooled gate, macro-domain pooling, majority routes, sentinel-only, favorable consensus, complete-case, unweighted, and hierarchical shrinkage diagnostics. None can nominate.
9. Apply inherited gates plus the kappa-robust gates. Produce either an explicit atom allow-list, `NO_SELECTION`, or `INCONCLUSIVE`; map every residual/unknown/novel prospective signature to `DEFER`.
10. Run a claim checker rejecting independence-as-truth, clinical safety, guideline correctness, live use, benefit, follow-up completion, exact-calendar trend, and external transport language.

A minimal implementation contract is:

```text
for each sampled admission i:
    bundle[i] = inherited_labels(i) + challenge_labels(i) + citations(i)
    assert all_units_coupled(bundle[i])
for kappa in [0.00, 0.05, 0.10]:
    for replicate in design_bootstrap:
        assignments = compatible_inherited_states(bundle)
        assignments += shared_or_template_supported_flips(bundle, kappa)
        y = joint_adverse_assignment(assignments, replicate)
        estimate_all_HT_and_Hajek(y, same_assignment=True)
    endpoint[kappa] = widest_exact_bootstrap_partial_id_endpoint()
allow_atom_g only if every inherited gate and every endpoint[kappa] gate passes
```

The implementation must not use a language model or external service on clinical text, must not infer a latent gold label by consensus, and must not use challenge labels to edit the inherited population, unit definitions, or frozen routes.

## Outcomes, baselines, and falsification

Primary outcomes remain the inherited text-defined quantities: route burden, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `leak_AUTO`, confirmation, AC1/reproducibility, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, workload, and their atom/minimax versions. New prespecified robustness outcomes are weighted `Q_shared`, `Q_independent`, `Q_missing`, `Q_template`, `Q_cross`, challenge response, and the change in each primary adverse endpoint from kappa 0 to kappa 0.10. These are measurements of evidence dependence and robustness, not clinical error probabilities.

The repair is falsified by synthetic fixtures and sealed computational tests if a shared-source agreement can pass the kappa envelope without permitting the planted compatible adverse flip; if a failing atom is rescued by pooled, macro-domain, hierarchical, or favorable-consensus estimates; if challenge missingness disappears through complete-case deletion; if coupled admission assignments split units or outcomes; if a novel signature is mapped to a neighboring atom; or if kappa selection changes after labels/results. A synthetic fixture is a code test, not clinical validation.

Additional traps are required: copied template spans must be `Q_template=1`; different renderings of the same detail row must be `SHARED_SOURCE`; conflicting narrative/detail facts must remain adverse-compatible; source ordinals must be immutable; all-route challenge review must occur even for apparent `DEFER`; and role/access canaries must invalidate rather than repair a run. Unequal cell fractions must recover HT totals and finite-population coverage; zero events must retain a positive noncensus bound; and the max-statistic family must prevent nominal per-kappa or per-atom intervals from licensing a failed minimax endpoint.

## Interpretation and evidence ceiling

**Supportive.** The inherited pooled gates and every claimed atom gate pass under the fixed support atlas and simultaneously at kappa 0, 0.05, and 0.10, with adequate challenge response, no firewall breach, and workload within budget. This supports only a prospective silent bridge for the exact allow-list and means the route is robust to this declared documentation-dependence stress envelope. It does not demonstrate that reviewers are clinically correct or that `AUTO` is safe.

**Adverse.** A supported atom fails under the common-mode envelope, has uncitable/shared evidence that permits an adverse endpoint over a gate, or shows a minority-unit failure. The atom is excluded even if nominal agreement, pooled purity, or a model-based latent-class estimate looks favorable. A failure is evidence against release robustness for that observable atom, not proof of guideline error or patient harm.

**Inconclusive.** The challenge panel cannot be staffed within the fixed workload, response or citation completeness is inadequate, atom ESS is insufficient, all available evidence is shared, the atlas has no support-qualified atom, governance does not approve kappa, or simultaneous bounds remain wide. Inconclusive is not a negative clinical finding and cannot be converted into a pooled pass.

Even a complete retrospective pass cannot establish exact calendar transport, actual report viewing, transmission, responsibility, order completion, follow-up, patient preference, appropriateness, harms, utility, or benefit. Those require a new prospective silent bridge and then expert-adjudicated workflow/outcome study. The default behavior outside the certified allow-list remains `DEFER`.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The parent prevented a large pooled domain from validating a failing small domain. This child prevents nominally independent agreement from being treated as a gold standard when reviewers may share the same copied or templated evidence. It adds an executable, outcome-blind measurement of citation dependence and a bounded, predeclared adverse common-mode envelope while preserving the parent's exact estimand and finite-population design. The result is a clinically more interpretable decision boundary: a route may be reproducible enough for a silent bridge only if its certification survives plausible shared-artifact disagreement, not merely if two people agree.

The repair cannot identify the true clinical label because MIMIC supplies no adjudicated guideline gold standard or external outcome. The kappa grid is a governance sensitivity envelope, not a validated error model; a pass at 10% does not prove less than 10% error, and a failure does not prove harm. Detail rows are not an independent source, discharge text is not proof of action, provider IDs do not reveal responsibility, and prospective evidence is unavailable. These limitations are deliberately fail-closed: no common-mode assumption, pooled result, longitudinal note, or model can license retrospective automation or a claim of benefit.
