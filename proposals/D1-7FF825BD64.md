## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats serum AFP,
albumin and bilirubin. The branch's open question: does the *within-patient AFP trajectory* add
prognostic information beyond **baseline AFP** and beyond **liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics measured in
the SAME draws**, for time to radiologic progression?

The branch's parents fixed endpoint misattribution (modality-restricted abdominal imaging; local
progression vs `转移` as competing events) and lead-time/informative observation (E_early/E_late
stratum + dual landmark and time-dependent Cox). **The worst remaining objection is not yet
falsified**: a single-window fitted slope can be a *transient peri-procedural AFP surge* or a
*regression-to-the-mean* artifact, and the enrolled rivals are asserted rather than tested against a
pre-specified alternative exposure definition.

**Clinical importance.** If the AFP-trajectory signal is a durable, replicating within-patient trend
it supports trajectory-informed re-imaging/escalation timing; if it appears only in one half of the
window it is an artifact and AFP-only trajectory monitoring should be de-emphasised. These are
different decisions.

## 3. Added by this child (ONE pre-specified element)

**Split-window exposure replication.** Replace the single fitted exposure window with two
non-overlapping, equal-length sub-windows inside the parent's already-frozen exposure period
`(index, t*]`:
- **E1** = draws in `(index, index+90 d]`  — primary exposure (AFP slope + ALBI slope).
- **E2** = draws in `(index+90 d, index+180 d]` — independent replication window, same formula.

A supportive claim of *durable* trajectory information requires BOTH:
1. the E1 AFP-slope hazard CI excludes 1 in the primary model (landmark Cox on E1), **and**
2. the E2 AFP-slope shows the same sign, with a pre-specified concordance floor (per-patient sign
   agreement rate >> 0.5) and an E2 hazard whose CI excludes 1 or lies within a pre-declared
   equivalence band of the E1 hazard.

This is a genuine replication/estimand requirement (durable vs transient), distinct from the parent's
E_early/E_late *event-detection* stratum, and it directly falsifies the transient peri-procedural and
regression-to-the-mean rivals.

## 5. Method comparison (recorded before selection)

- **M0 (baseline):** landmark Cox on baseline AFP + ALBI grade + age/sex/treatment + draw intensity.
- **M4 (learned/mechanistic alternative):** joint linear-mixed-effects model of AFP and ALBI slopes
  (random intercepts + slopes) entered into the Cox hazard; reveals *change-vs-level* and separates
  AFP trajectory from same-draw liver reserve, which M0 cannot.
- **M4' (this child's extension):** the same joint model fit separately on E1 and E2 with the
  pre-specified concordance requirement — the durability test.
- **Deferred:** full shared-parameter joint model with correlated random effects/measurement error;
  Fine-Gray competing-risks model. Revisit if E1/E2 concordance is borderline or if the competing
  event rate is non-trivial.

## 6. Analysis, falsification, and outcome meanings

Falsification is conjunctive with a pre-specified M0 equivalence null. Branches:
- **Supportive:** E1 AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 AND E2 concordance floor met AND E_late stratum holds
  AND both landmark and time-dependent Cox hold AND MRI/CT stratum directions agree.
- **Adverse:** E1-only signal, OR sign reversal in E2, OR E_early-only, OR time-dependent attenuation,
  OR modality-stratum disagreement -> the durable-trajectory claim is not supported.
- **Inconclusive/null:** conjunctive equivalence vs M0 (both slope HR CIs include 1 AND held-out
  delta-C-index bootstrap CI within +/-0.01) -> AFP trajectory adds no durable information.

Supportive => trajectory-informed re-imaging timing is worth prospective study. Adverse => the signal
is transient/artifact and AFP-only monitoring should be de-emphasised. Inconclusive => no change;
the null must be interpreted as a bounded negative, not proof.

**Additional data required for stronger claims:** radiologist-adjudicated mRECIST progression
(not available), mortality/vital status (absent), and multi-institution replication. A causal claim
requires expert adjudication or another study; a QA verifier cannot establish it.
