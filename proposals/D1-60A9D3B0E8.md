# Dynamic Ventilation care-plan states are too sparse and concentrated for a transportable longitudinal feature, with predictive fitness inconclusive

## Substantive question and advance

Parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]` established that static exact-label meaning is hospital-dependent and that exhaustive outcome-blind expansion from the two exact values to all four literal `Ventilated -` values adds only 0.10–0.13 percentage points of bounded coverage and does not improve hospital-held-out prediction. They did not resolve the last plausible longitudinal use: perhaps a value newly received within a stay, or a change relative to the patient's own recent label history, is more informative than static receipt despite site workflow differences.

This child tests that unresolved dynamic-fitness hypothesis. The strongest new evidence is that exact and broader new/change/persistent states can be computed without outcome leakage, but current receipt and within-hospital dynamic variation are sharply concentrated. Neither exact nor broader dynamics meets prespecified support or predictive gates in any airway definition. Point losses are small and inconsistent, and the completed 20-replicate nested execution does not meet the deliberately stronger adverse-loss gate. The honest result is therefore **inconclusive dynamic predictive fitness, but adverse measurement/support evidence for deploying this field as a generic multicenter longitudinal feature**. This closes a substantive loophole without repeating synonym enumeration or durable-liberation analyses.

## Repeated rows, hospital transport, baselines, missingness, and analysis

Repeated landmarks are retained because the deployment estimand is a landmark prediction. Every uncertainty resample clusters all rows from the same `sid`; no stay crosses train/test hospital folds. Leave-one-hospital-out evaluation is restricted to held-out hospitals with at least 20 observed outcomes and both classes, while training uses all other observed frozen rows. This yields 28, 18, 16, and 11 held-out hospitals for unbounded, 24-hour, 12-hour, and 6-hour definitions. Micro log loss and equal-hospital macro log loss are primary; Brier score and AUROC are descriptive.

Five regularized logistic models use fold-specific one-hot encoding, unknown-category handling, median imputation plus missing indicators, scaling, `C=1`, and no class weighting: process/history baseline; exact static current identity; exact dynamic state; broader static current identity; broader dynamic state. The key contrasts are dynamic versus same-vocabulary static identity and dynamic versus process/history baseline. This prevents apparent gain from merely identifying current receipt or prior documentation.

The executed nested uncertainty run has 20 complete replicates and zero failed folds. Within every training and held-out hospital, stays are sampled with replacement, all their landmarks move together, and every preprocessing/model pipeline is refit. A planned 100-replicate resource-managed run timed out without producing a receipt; it is not represented as completed. The 20-replicate intervals are exploratory and make the result more, not less, conservative/inconclusive. Hospitals are fixed, so uncertainty does not represent a random population of unseen hospitals.

Supportive fitness was prespecified to require, for exact or broader encoding, unbounded plus at least two bounded variants each with at least 20 new/transition rows, new and transition represented in at least three hospitals each, at least three hospitals with at least five receipts and two state classes, largest hospital share at most 60%, and dynamic models beating both static and process/history baselines in micro and macro log loss with all nested upper 97.5% bounds below zero. Adverse dynamic prediction requires inadequate transportable support in at least two bounded variants, no bounded predictive support, and for both vocabularies nested probability at least 0.75 of worse dynamic-versus-static macro loss in at least two bounded variants. Otherwise predictive fitness is inconclusive.

## Executed results

Analysis sizes were 3,296, 2,273, 1,555, and 1,018 rows. Exact current receipt occurred in 98/82/65/38 rows across 15/8/7/6 hospitals. Broader receipt occurred in 101/85/67/39 rows in the same 15/8/7/6 hospitals. The largest hospital supplied 70.4%, 78.0%, 80.0%, and 68.4% of exact receipts, and 71.3%, 78.8%, 80.6%, and 69.2% of broader receipts. Only 3/2/2/2 hospitals per definition had at least five exact receipts and two dynamic state classes; broader counts were also 3/2/2/2. No encoding/definition passed transportable-support criteria.

Exact new/persistent/transition counts were respectively 54/24/20 unbounded, 40/24/18 at 24 hours, 30/22/13 at 12 hours, and 17/11/10 at 6 hours. Broader counts were 52/23/26, 38/23/24, 28/21/18, and 16/10/13. Thus transitions are not nonexistent, but their effective multicenter overlap is poor and dominated by one hospital.

Against the stronger process/history baseline, exact dynamic micro/macro deltas were -0.00081/+0.00009, -0.00136/+0.00017, -0.00084/+0.00151, and +0.00201/+0.00362; broader deltas were -0.00054/+0.00089, -0.00122/+0.00097, -0.00132/+0.00155, and +0.00171/+0.00328. No bounded variant passed predictive support because equal-hospital results did not improve and uncertainty crossed zero.

In 20 nested refits, exact dynamic-versus-static macro P(worse) was 0.60, 0.40, 0.70, and 0.40; broader was 0.75, 0.55, 0.70, and 0.35. All corresponding 95% intervals crossed zero (for example exact: unbounded -0.00219 to +0.00282; 24 h -0.00278 to +0.00133; 12 h -0.00145 to +0.00783; 6 h -0.00501 to +0.00430). Therefore the adverse predictive-loss gate is not met. The outcome is `inconclusive_dynamic_fitness`, alongside clearly adverse measurement support for generic multicenter deployment.

## Meaning and limits

Supportive results would have justified prospective local workflow and semantic adjudication of dynamic states, not bedside use. The observed sparse, concentrated and non-reproducible result argues against treating new/changed/persistent exact or broader receipt as a generic hospital-transportable feature for this later chart trajectory. It does not establish that the feature is harmful, useless within the dominant hospital, or useless for another endpoint. It also cannot distinguish true workflow persistence from interface behavior, periodic copying, asynchronous entry, missing exports, or clinical changes.

Care-plan values do not prove invasive ventilation, evaluation, SBT, readiness, extubation, liberation, quality, benefit, harm, or causal care. The outcome is not a monotone clinical improvement endpoint. Stronger claims require local interface metadata, blinded chart review, direct device/airway/SBT/extubation timestamps, prospective multisite validation, and a larger independently sampled hospital population.

An automatic verifier can check hashes, row preservation, source columns, joins, windows, first/latest rules, transition counts, overlap/concentration, model nesting, hospital separation, stay clustering, point metrics, 20 completed resamples, and gate logic. It cannot adjudicate bedside semantics, workflow equivalence, care quality, causality, or unseen-hospital transport.
