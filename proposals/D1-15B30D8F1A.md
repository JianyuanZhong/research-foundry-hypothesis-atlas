# Episode 16 successor: delivery- and route-limited post-shock loop initiation

Parent: [prior hypothesis]

## Substantive repair

This successor preserves the parent's corrected SHA256 discovery partition, ICU-hour-48 landmark, positive-balance/vasopressor-recovery population, two-clock rule, unit-safe fluid phenotype, measured-overlap clone-censor analysis, destination-aware competing-state discharge outcome, and provenance-aware observed post-landmark RRT outcome.

It repairs a clinically material exposure-label and time-varying-indication defect. MIMIC `icu/inputevents` records delivered ICU inputs, drug item IDs, amount/rate, status and time, but has no route column. Therefore an ICU inputevent can operationally identify an ICU-recorded delivered loop exposure, but cannot by itself prove intravenous administration. Conversely, `hosp/prescriptions` and `hosp/pharmacy` identify orders/dispensing, not administration. This proposal will not call the primary intervention “IV” or treat an order as a dose. It will use eMAR administration details only for route-explicit and concordance sensitivities.

The parent has hourly censoring weights but leaves the time-varying indication contract underspecified. During the 24-hour decision window, new hypotension, urine change, ventilation change, renal/electrolyte results, and other treatment can both predict loop delivery and outcomes. The primary analysis therefore freezes a sequential, hour-by-hour treatment and censoring model using measurements known by each hour. No post-hour clinical value, final procedure status, future endpoint, or future stored covariate may enter that hour's risk set. This does not recover unmeasured bedside congestion, intent, or exchangeability; it makes the estimand and residual limitation explicit and falsifiable.

## Clinical question and evidence boundary

In an adult who is invasively ventilated 48 hours into an ICU stay, has survived vasopressor-treated shock, remains substantially fluid-positive, and is currently off vasopressors, should clinicians initiate a loop diuretic during the next day? The decision may affect transition out of the hospital and respiratory support, but could also worsen hypotension, electrolytes, or renal-support burden.

Evidence supports only a narrower claim: conservative-fluid/active-deresuscitation bundles can lower recorded fluid balance; modest randomized and secondary analyses have not established a patient-centered benefit or renal safety, and they do not isolate this delivery-defined decision at this landmark. The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among the measured-overlap population defined below, assignment at ICU hour 48 to the policy “receive the first qualifying ICU-recorded delivered furosemide/bumetanide loop during the following 24 hours, with no non-ICU loop before that initiation” increases the probability of recorded live index-hospital discharge by day 28 to a non-hospice, non-acute-hospital destination (ANHATD28) by more than 5 percentage points compared with the policy “receive no actual loop by any route during that 24-hour window,” without an adverse difference in alive documented durable liberation by day 7, recorded day-28 mortality, or observed post-landmark RRT use through day 7.

The intervention is deliberately named “ICU-recorded delivered loop,” not IV loop. A route-explicit eMAR sensitivity can address an IV-only version if enough observations have positive route ascertainment. The result is an association unless exchangeability and consistency are justified by clinical review or randomization.

A positive result would prioritize a pragmatic randomized trial or external validation of a specific post-shock initiation policy. It would not recommend loop use at the bedside. The advance over existing evidence is a decision-time, delivery-coherent estimate with dynamic indication handling and separate discharge destination, death, respiratory observation, and RRT provenance rather than a fluid-balance surrogate or a bundle-level inference.

## Exposure definitions and route contract

Primary A is the first positive, qualifying ICU-recorded delivered furosemide or bumetanide segment during `(t0,t0+24h]): furosemide items 221794/228340 and bumetanide item 229639 in `icu/inputevents`, with valid subject/hadm/stay keys and start/end times, accepted delivered statuses `FinishedRunning`, `ChangeDose/Rate`, `Stopped` or `Paused` (the last only in sensitivity), and a positive dose convertible to mg or a positive mg/hour rate over positive duration. Normalize dose units only when `amountuom`/`rateuom` is recognized; unknown, mass-incompatible, nonpositive, order-only, canceled and not-started rows are not delivery. The primary exposure is not asserted to be IV because inputevents has no route column.

Primary B is no actual furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide or ethacrynic-acid administration by any route in the window. Detect hospital administrations by joining `hosp/emar` to `hosp/emar_detail` on `subject_id, emar_id, emar_seq`; retain each `parent_field_ordinal`, then aggregate detail to one administration event before exposure classification. Require medication/product identity, actual-administration `event_txt`/administration fields, and positive `dose_given` or `product_amount_given`; an order in `prescriptions` or `pharmacy` alone never counts. Any positive actual administration with missing route is still a B deviation, but is labeled route-unknown. A route-explicit IV sensitivity requires an eMAR route whose normalized value is IV and positive administration evidence. Same-drug ICU/eMAR records within 15 minutes are concordance evidence, not additive doses.

A non-ICU actual loop before qualifying A initiation deviates A. A qualifying ICU loop initiates A. Death or explicit discharge/transfer before differentiation is an outcome in both clones. A remains compatible until qualifying initiation, then follows; censor unsatisfied A at hour 24. B is censored at the first actual loop by any route. At ties, death/discharge precedes artificial censoring, actual delivery precedes the hour-24 check; reverse precedence is sensitivity. Treatment after hour 24 is unrestricted. This is a 24-hour initiation-policy estimand, not a dose-response, sustained-dose, class-wide biological, or proven-IV effect.

## Time-varying indication adjustment

Clone subjects to A and B at `t0). At each completed hourly interval before differentiation or censoring, form treatment and censoring risk sets using only information with event/chart/clinical time and storetime no later than that hour. Candidate time-varying predictors are:

- MAP and hypotension, pressor presence/dose and time since cessation from `icu/chartevents` and `icu/inputevents`;
- urine output and cumulative fluid components from `icu/outputevents` and `icu/inputevents`;
- FiO2, PEEP, oxygenation and ventilation status from `icu/chartevents` and `icu/procedureevents`;
- creatinine, potassium, lactate and their lagged values/slopes from `hosp/labevents`;
- newly delivered crystalloid/albumin/pressor and other procedure-support indicators from `icu/inputevents` and `icu/procedureevents`;
- ICU location/transfer state from `hosp/transfers` where available.

Use prior-hour values, missingness indicators, and pre-specified slopes. No post-hour values, future final status, discharge destination, response, death, or later diagnosis enters a risk set. Fit cross-fitted pooled logistic treatment-deviation and censoring models separately by hour, stabilized by baseline history, with truncation at the 1st/99th percentiles. The primary baseline target is the empirical overlap restriction `Pr(A|X_t0) in [0.10,0.90]`; apply common `e(1-e)` tilt to both clones, never an arm-specific baseline weight. Report crude, baseline-overlap, and sequentially weighted estimates, calibration, standardized differences, weight tails, positivity, arm counts and ESS. A stochastic odds-shift analysis over 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.33 and 2.0 times the observed hourly odds assesses how much unmeasured readiness would be needed to change the conclusion. Pre-`t0` slopes remain negative-control outcomes.

## Outcomes

The primary ANHATD28 state is evaluated at `H28=t0+28 days` using `hosp/admissions`:

1. flag-confirmed in-hospital death with valid `deathtime` in `(t0,H28]`;
2. with death flag 0 and `dischtime <= H28`, exact `HOSPICE`;
3. exact `ACUTE HOSPITAL`;
4. missing/`DIED`/discordant destinations as invalid-state failure;
5. every other exact destination as recorded alive non-hospice/non-acute discharge;
6. `dischtime>H28` as prolonged hospitalization;
7. remaining temporal contradictions as invalid.

Report all state probabilities and an Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence with death, hospice, acute transfer, prolonged hospitalization and invalid state as competing states. Reconcile it to the binary primary risk. Sensitivities are any-location live discharge, exact HOME/HOME HEALTH CARE, each destination, day 14/21, era/service/first-careunit strata, anomaly exclusion, and subsequent same-system admission by H28. A sign reversal among primary, community, or any-discharge estimates when both arms have at least 20 events and ESS at least 75 blocks support.

Secondary outcomes are alive documented durable liberation by day 7 (ALDL7), mortality, and observed post-landmark RRT. ALDL7 requires accepted extubation (procedureevents 227194/225468/225477, status `FinishedRunning`) by day 5, continuous linked-ICU observation through day 7, survival, and no recurrent invasive ventilation (225792; item 224385 for intubation). Death is failure; complete observation without liberation is failure; observation gaps are lower 0/upper 1. Report lower/upper risks and risk differences.

Observed post-landmark RRT is not “new RRT,” AKI, or renal safety. ICU evidence is procedureevents items 225441/225802/225803/225805/225809/225955, starttime in `(t0,t0+7d]`, accepted status `FinishedRunning` or `Stopped`. Hospital delivered dialysis evidence is procedures_icd codes ICD-9 3995/5498 or ICD-10 5A1D00Z/5A1D60Z/5A1D70Z/5A1D80Z/5A1D90Z, dictionary-confirmed through `hosp/d_icd_procedures`; access/imaging codes are excluded. A chartdate is `[00:00,+1d)`: full containment is the lower indicator and any intersection is the upper indicator. Duplicate ICU/hospital evidence is one event, and death is competing. Report prior timed RRT, strictly earlier date-only dialysis, same-date indeterminate, no-prior strata, incident sensitivity among no-definite-prior participants, and ambiguity counts.

Also report 24/48-hour balance, urine, potassium/creatinine, MAP<55 and new pressor as descriptive/safety signals. No fluid change can override the patient-centered branches.

## Estimand, uncertainty, and falsification

The primary estimand is the weighted 28-day ANHATD28 risk difference A minus B in the baseline empirical-overlap population under the delivery-defined policies. Under consistency, positivity and conditional exchangeability it could be interpreted as a per-protocol effect. MIMIC cannot measure bedside congestion, clinician intent, goals of care, complete route/adherence, or all post-ICU outcomes; the default conclusion is an adjusted association.

Use cross-fitted doubly robust arm risks and risk difference, at least 500 subject bootstraps, and report 95% intervals. Declare the primary analysis infeasible/inconclusive if either arm has fewer than 100 adherent subjects, ESS<75, >10% of person-hours with treatment/censor probability <.01 or >.99, >1% temporal/state contradictions, >10 percentage-point arm imbalance in anomaly rates, material late-entry differential, failed negative controls, nonreconciliation of state/binary/RRT bounds, or route-explicit eMAR coverage is too sparse to interpret its sensitivity. The route sensitivity cannot invalidate the primary if it is unavailable; it must be labeled unavailable rather than silently redefining exposure.

Magnitude status is supported only if the lower 95% limit for ANHATD28 RD exceeds +0.05; falsified at the stated margin if the upper limit is at most +0.05; otherwise unresolved.

Clinical branches are:

- Supportive: magnitude supported; upper 95% limit for mortality lower-bound RD is <+0.03; upper 95% limit for observed post-landmark RRT upper RD is <+0.05; upper 95% limit for ALDL7 upper RD is >0; no eligible destination estimate reverses with required event/ESS; sequential weighting, overlap, temporal, missingness, negative-control, state-reconciliation, route-concordance and stochastic-bias checks pass. This supports external validation/trial prioritization only.
- Adverse: primary RD upper limit <=0, or ALDL7 upper RD <=0, or mortality lower-bound RD lower limit >=+0.03, or post-landmark RRT lower-bound RD lower limit >=+0.05 under both date brackets.
- Inconclusive: all other results, including a margin-falsified but clinically non-harmful result, sparse route evidence, a small positive effect, sensitivity sign conflict, or a stochastic unmeasured-indication shift that crosses 0 or +0.05.

A positive fluid-balance change is not a patient benefit. A null observed RRT contrast is not renal safety. A margin-falsified hypothesis is not proof of harm. No branch establishes causality, functional recovery, survival after discharge, or a bedside recommendation.
