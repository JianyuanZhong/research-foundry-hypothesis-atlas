# Hypotension duration beyond nadir: independent prognostic information for early AKI in eICU

Parent: [prior hypothesis] (expert seed [internal campaign], "Duration of hypotension").
Dataset: eICU-CRD 2.0 snapshot [source checksum].

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance

In sepsis and shock, perfusion pressure below renal autoregulation is a leading
mechanistic explanation for acute kidney injury (AKI). Existing evidence establishes
that *delays* in treatment after hypotension onset increase AKI odds (Bagshaw 2009,
OR 1.14 per log-hour) and that in the intraoperative setting both *magnitude and
duration* of hypotension below threshold show dose-dependent AKI risk (Penev 2024
systematic review). However, in general ICU populations it remains unresolved whether
the **duration** of hypotension carries prognostic information for AKI **beyond the
nadir (depth) of the blood pressure** once vasopressor exposure and illness severity
are accounted for. Clinically this matters because nadir-only summaries (e.g., a
single lowest SBP) are what most EHR-derived risk tools capture; if duration adds
independent, hospital-stable information, time-weighted hypotension metrics become a
more stable and actionable risk measure for early renal protection decisions (fluids,
vasopressor titration, nephrotoxin avoidance).

Leading claim: time below threshold (duration) is an independent AKI risk factor
beyond nadir. Strongest rival: duration is a proxy for (a) illness severity, (b)
vasopressor requirement, or (c) measurement density (sicker patients are monitored
more often, so more below-threshold readings are recorded). The discriminating
comparison is the within-nadir-stratum gradient in duration after adjustment for
vasopressor time, baseline creatinine and age, tested for consistency across
hospitals.

## 3. Population and temporal boundaries

Inclusion (prespecified):
1. unitvisitnumber = 1 (index ICU stay).
2. ≥10 non-missing systemicsystolic readings with observationoffset in [0, 2880) min
   (first 48 h).
3. ≥1 creatinine (labname = 'creatinine') with labresultoffset in [0, 1440) min
   (first 24 h) — baseline creatinine = value at the first such draw.
4. unitdischargeoffset > 1440 min (observed beyond 24 h).
5. Age 18+.

Exclusions: stays with >6 h gaps in the first 48 h are retained but gaps are treated
as unmeasured (excluded from the duration denominator); sensitivity analysis
requiring ≥50% of the 48 h window covered by measurements (gap rule).

## 4. Variables

Exposures (first 48 h, from vitalPeriodic):
- nadir_sbp = minimum systemicsystolic.
- hypotension_duration_frac = fraction of the 48 h window (excluding unmeasured gap
  time) during which linearly interpolated SBP < 90 mmHg. Primary threshold 90;
  sensitivity thresholds 85 mmHg and MAP < 65 mmHg.
- bp_density = number of systolic readings per hour of coverage (measurement-density
  covariate).

Covariates:
- age, gender (patient).
- baseline_creatinine (lab, first draw in first 24 h).
- vasopressor_time_24h = total minutes in first 24 h with an active
  norepinephrine infusion (infusionDrug rows with drugname containing
  'norepinephrine', infusionoffset < 1440; active interval approximated as
  [infusionoffset, next infusionoffset for same patient or +60 min if none),
  plus total norepinephrine dose (sum drugrate × duration, mcg/kg/min units as
  recorded).
- hospitalid (random effect).

Outcome:
- AKI_72h = 1 if any creatinine with labresultoffset in (baseline_draw_offset,
  4320) min is ≥ 1.5 × baseline_creatinine (KDIGO-like stage ≥1). Secondary:
  ≥2.0× (stage ≥2) and need for renal replacement therapy (treatment.csv /
  diagnosis codes) as a sensitivity outcome.

## 5. Analysis plan

All models use hospital random intercepts (mixed-effects logistic, GLMM) and are
fitted on the full eligible cohort; evaluation uses a pre-specified hospital-level
holdout (leave 2 hospitals out, chosen by size, fixed before fitting).

1. Baseline model: AKI_72h ~ nadir_sbp (per 10 mmHg) + age + baseline_creatinine +
   vasopressor_time_24h + bp_density + (1|hospital).
2. Alternative model: baseline + hypotension_duration_frac (per 10% of window).
   Primary test: Wald/LR test for the duration term; report OR with 95% CI,
   marginal AUC delta and calibration slope on the holdout.
3. Nadir-stratum check: within nadir tertiles, compare AKI rates across duration
   quartiles (adjusted) — the direct test of "at the same minimum BP, longer
   hypotension → more AKI".
4. Learned alternative (same scientific question, flexible learner):
   histogram gradient-boosted trees (LightGBM or sklearn HistGradientBoosting,
   CPU, 16 cores) with the same covariates, with and without
   hypotension_duration_frac; compare holdout AUC, calibration and the SHAP/
   permutation importance of the duration feature. This tests whether the duration
   signal is non-linear or threshold-shaped, which the linear GLMM cannot show.
   GPU is not required; fits are minutes-scale on tabular data.
5. Cross-hospital consistency: hospital-level random-effect distribution for the
   duration term; fraction of hospitals with positive duration OR.

## 6. Falsification and interpretation

- Supportive: duration term significant (p < 0.05) with positive OR in the GLMM,
  AUC delta ≥ 0.005 on holdout, positive direction in ≥80% of hospitals, and
  duration importance among top-5 features in the GBM.
- Adverse (hypothesis refuted): duration term non-significant (p > 0.05) AND AUC
  delta < 0.005 in both model families across both holdout splits → duration adds no
  information beyond nadir + vasopressors + severity; nadir-only summaries suffice.
- Inconclusive: significant in only one model family, or direction inconsistent
  across hospitals (<80% concordant), or AUC delta between 0.005 and 0.02 with wide
  CI → measurement-density or severity confounding not fully excluded; requires
  arterial-line data or a different cohort.

What each result would/would not establish: a supportive result establishes
association and cross-hospital stability of a duration effect, not causality (no
randomization; vasopressor timing is confounded by indication). An adverse result
would argue against duration-based monitoring targets in this population. Neither
result establishes a treatment threshold; that requires a trial (cf. Lamontagne
2020 permissive-hypotension RCT, in which acute renal failure was the most common
serious adverse event).

## 9. Limitations and honest gaps

- eICU vitals are periodic and interface-dependent; duration is estimated from
  interpolated periodic readings, not continuous arterial waveforms.
- AKI definition uses creatinine only (no urine output in eICU lab table); RRT
  outcome is a sensitivity only.
- Vasopressor "active time" is approximated from infusion start rows without stop
  times; dose uses recorded drugrate.
- No arterial-line flag; measurement-density covariate partially addresses
  monitoring-confounding but cannot fully remove it.
- Abstract-only inspection for K2/K3; full texts not read.
