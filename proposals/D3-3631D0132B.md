# D3-M2: MerTK-Dependent Macrophage-to-FLS Signaling (EXECUTED)

## Scientific Question
In RA synovial FLS, does MerTK inhibition specifically block pro-inflammatory signaling from MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages, or does the inhibitor have off-target effects on FLS independent of macrophage contact?

## Hypothesis
MerTK-positive (Mpos) macrophages activate FLS through a MerTK-dependent pathway. Inhibiting MerTK on macrophages should reduce FLS activation markers in co-culture but should NOT affect FLS in the absence of macrophages.

## Data
- **Dataset**: E-MTAB-8873 (targeted 445-gene assay, 33 samples, 5 FLS donors)
- **Source**: `[internal dataset path]`
- **Design**: `[internal dataset path]`
- **FLS donors**: SA256A, SA257A, SA258R, SA263R, SA266A
- **Groups**: FLS_only (6), Mpos (8), Mpos_Inhibitor (4), Mneg (6), Mneg_Inhibitor (1), Mneg_LPS (4), Mpos_LPS (1), mixed (3)
- **Key genes**: 38 inflammatory/activation genes (IL6, TNF, IL1B, CCL2, CXCL8, TGFB1, MERTK, MMP1-13, COL1A1/2, FN1, VEGFA, ICAM1, etc.)

## Analysis (EXECUTED - [research job])

### Method
1. **Baseline**: Donor-aware pseudobulk comparison (Wilcoxon rank-sum per gene, BH correction)
2. **Alternative**: Mixed-effects model (donor as random effect, condition as fixed effect)
3. **Interaction test**: (delta_Mpos_inhibitor - delta_Mneg_inhibitor) per gene

### Results

| Contrast | Significant (BH<0.05) | Key Finding |

## Interpretation: ADVERSE/INCONCLUSIVE

The results are **adverse** for the MerTK-specificity hypothesis:
1. MerTK inhibitor does NOT significantly reduce FLS activation in Mpos co-culture
2. The inhibitor effect is actually LARGER in Mneg (MerTK-negative) co-culture
3. This is the OPPOSITE of what MerTK-specificity would predict

However, the result is also **inconclusive** because:
- Only 1 Mneg_Inhibitor sample exists (SA256A) - no statistical power
- No true FLS-only+Inhibitor control exists to rule out off-target effects
- The targeted 445-gene assay may miss key MerTK pathway mediators

## Competing Explanations
1. **Off-target effects**: The inhibitor may affect FLS or Mneg macrophages directly (cannot be tested without FLS-only+Inhibitor control)
2. **MerTK not primary mediator**: Other TAM family receptors (AXL, TYRO3) or different signaling pathways may mediate Mpos-to-FLS communication
3. **Assay limitation**: Key genes (NFKBIA, PTGS2, VCAM1, IFIT1, OAS1) are not measured
4. **Single-sample artifact**: The Mneg_Inhibitor result is driven by one sample from one donor
