# Fixed-lock validation that an EHR vasopressor-restart endpoint preserves consequential randomized contrasts

## Decision, evidence boundary, and falsifiable claim

The clinically consequential prerequisite is no longer whether an EHR restart label has a prevalence-dependent PPV above a generic threshold. It is whether using the label in a later randomized norepinephrine-versus-vasopressin stopping-order trial could change the clinical decision that would have been reached using intent-qualified pump rescue.

All inherited MIMIC findings are frozen. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, assigned actions were rare: 211 norepinephrine-first (NE-first; 3.49%) and 167 vasopressin-first (VP-first; 2.76%), both below the 5% gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort has 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate composite failures. Arms are neither randomized nor exchangeable and are too small for causal comparison. Of 73 failures among 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvement (about 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, below 0.01) and adverse later transport; no score or subgroup advances.

The complete transaction audit used all 137 inherited episodes and all rows remaining after declared stay, item, and time filters. It recovered every boundary and all 59 raw restarts. Five symmetric reconstruction rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries. These are findings of EHR-label instability, not pump truth. Status, chain, dose, caregiver, `storetime`, open-bag/transfer fields, and intermittent MAP cannot establish delivered drug, genuine cessation, rescue intent, false-positive rate, or MCS absence.

Public evidence makes a clinically anchored error tolerance necessary but does not determine one uniquely. RENOVA, a single-center open-label randomized trial (78 analyzed, 39 per group), reported 24-hour hypotension in 43.6% after initial NE reduction versus 25.6% after initial VP reduction: an absolute difference of 18 percentage points, RR 1.7 (95% CI 0.9–3.2), without detected differences in downstream clinical outcomes (PMID 39890531; DOI 10.1016/j.medine.2025.502147). It is small, uses hypotension rather than this intent-qualified rescue endpoint, and predominantly titrated VP, so it justifies neither efficacy nor a transport assumption. Retrospective reports span approximately 14%–82% hypotension depending on arm and definition, reinforcing broad uncertainty rather than a single expected rate. Modern outcome-misclassification work shows that ignoring binary endpoint error can materially bias effects and interval coverage; validation must therefore target sensitivity and specificity, which map true arm risks to observed arm risks, rather than PPV, which changes with prevalence.

The prospective hypothesis is:

> Among first qualifying patients in each randomized-order analogue stratum and every supported pump–EHR configuration, the locked EHR six-hour restart endpoint has arm/configuration-specific sensitivity and specificity sufficiently high that a simultaneous 95% confidence set implies less than 2.5 percentage points absolute distortion of the NE-minus-VP risk difference for every pair of true rescue risks between 15% and 60% with absolute difference at most 25 percentage points.

This is deliberately stringent and falsifiable. The 15%–60% range brackets the randomized RENOVA arm rates and the more relevant central range of published discontinuation cohorts while avoiding claiming that incomparable hypotension definitions estimate rescue prevalence. The 25-point effect envelope includes RENOVA's imprecise 18-point signal. The strict 2.5-point bias tolerance is one half of a prospectively declared 5-point future noninferiority margin and is small enough to preserve the sign of a true superiority contrast of at least 5 points. For a future harmful-outcome estimand `RD=true risk_NE − true risk_VP`, it also protects an NI conclusion when true RD is at most 2.5 points and protects rejection when true RD is at least 7.5 points; `[0.025,0.075]` is an explicitly unprotected decision gray zone. No validation study can guarantee the same finite-trial p-value or confidence-limit decision near a boundary.

A supportive result validates only endpoint-based treatment-contrast preservation in the listed configurations, risk/effect region, first-patient unit, and six-hour window. It does not establish which stopping order benefits patients, endpoint clinical importance, safety, equipoise, or a noninferiority margin acceptable to clinicians/regulators.

## Consecutive population and estimands

Recruit at least six adult ICUs with synchronized smart-pump, monitor, randomization/assigned-order analogue, and EHR exports. Each configuration completes a fresh 50-episode technical run-in after all definitions are locked. At least 95% must have unique patient–pump–channel–drug linkage, measured clock maps, and complete pump export from two hours before index through the observable six-hour window; at least 90% must have contemporaneous intent evidence. Infrastructure may be repaired, but thresholds, windows, labels, estimands, risk region, bias tolerance, and adjudication categories may not change. A failed configuration repeats with 50 new episodes; a second failure makes it feasibility-only. Run-in episodes are excluded.

After run-in, capture and fully verify every consecutive qualifying cessation, regardless of EHR or reference status. Qualification uses only information available at cessation:

1. age at least 18 years, alive in an adult ICU;
2. linkable pump-delivered concurrent NE and VP for at least six continuous hours immediately before cessation;
3. at the start of the final 30 pre-index minutes, NE >0 and <=0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 and <=0.04 U/min, with neither increased during that interval;
4. no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours;
5. at least three valid MAP observations spanning at least three of four prior 30-minute bins, including the final bin, with median MAP >=65 and minimum MAP >=55 mmHg; and
6. a non-simultaneous first cessation of one study drug while the comparator continues pump-native delivery.

Both drugs crossing below threshold within five minutes is excluded and counted as simultaneous. A procedure/transport pause known by `t0` is excluded and counted. Actual MCS type, active/standby state, and support setting are captured; absence is never inferred from silence.

A person contributes only their first qualifying cessation to confirmation, matching a possible patient-randomized trial. No later event, outcome, capture state, or arm selects that episode. Every later qualifying episode is nevertheless fully verified. It requires both drugs to re-satisfy the full six-hour baseline and `t0` at least 24 hours after prior `t0`; closer transitions remain in the prior episode. Recurrent performance is secondary, analyzed with patient-cluster bootstrap (>=10,000 resamples) stratified by arm/configuration and marginal GEE. It cannot rescue the primary claim; a >0.10 first-versus-recurrent sensitivity or specificity difference is adverse for extension to repeated events.

For arm `a`, reference `R`, and locked EHR endpoint `E`, primary diagnostic estimands are `Se_a=P(E=1|R=1,a)` and `Sp_a=P(E=0|R=0,a)`. PPV, NPV, reference prevalence, and EHR positivity are descriptive. The future true arm risk is `p_a=P(R=1|a)` and the EHR risk implied by validation parameters is

`q_a(p_a)=Se_a*p_a + (1-Sp_a)*(1-p_a)`.

The true trial contrast is `D=p_NE-p_VP`; the EHR contrast is `D_E=q_NE-q_VP`; distortion is `B=D_E-D`. Primary validation asks whether `sup |B| <0.025` over `p_NE,p_VP in [0.15,0.60]`, `|D|<=0.25`, and the full simultaneous confidence set for all four Se/Sp values. Differential error is allowed; no arm symmetry assumption is made.

## Locked clocks, index test, and reference

`t0` is the first pump timestamp at which the first-stopped drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below for at least five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Drug-library identity, concentration, patient, channel, weight, and delivered-rate stream must be linkable; a programmed rate alone is insufficient.

The target window is `(t0,min[t0+6 hours,death,ICU exit]]`. Death or ICU exit is determinate closure only when pump and EHR capture are complete through closure. A technical/export/linkage gap before closure makes `R` indeterminate, never negative. Eligibility cannot depend on future capture, rescue, EHR restart, physiology, death, or exit.

Before validation, each source clock has an escrowed affine mapping to pump time, including offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving handling, fitting rows, residuals, immutable version, and a configuration-specific boundary band equal to the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds. A wider required band fails qualification.

The EHR algorithm is escrowed before reference adjudication and retains the exact MIMIC-compatible mapping: NE item 221906 retains mcg/kg/min or converts mcg/min using contemporaneous valid weight 30–300 kg, rejecting normalized rates >5 mcg/kg/min; VP item 222315 divides units/hour by 60, retains units/min, and rejects normalized rates >0.2 U/min. `E=1` when a new positive stopped-drug EHR interval has `endtime>starttime` and deterministically mapped `starttime` in `(t0,t0+6h]`, before earlier death/exit. A row spanning `t0` is not new; pump rescue concealed within it remains `E=0`. Chain, status, `storetime`, caregiver, open-bag, transfer, and order fields never repair the primary label. `E=?` when a prespecified mapped 95% timestamp uncertainty set crosses a boundary; deterministic-time classification and excluding boundary cases are secondary only.

`R=1` requires (1) linked delivered pump rate for the stopped drug again reaches its index threshold continuously for at least five minutes within the observable window after the qualifying off interval and (2) two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators conclude from contemporaneous timestamped evidence that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion after cessation. Bag replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, procedural pause, mistaken-stop correction, and uninterrupted delivery segmented only in documentation are reference-negative. The pump algorithm precedes adjudication. Adjudicators see pump/monitor traces, workflow, cointerventions, and contemporaneous intent evidence, but not `E`, EHR transaction diagnostics, aggregate accuracy, or each other's label. A third blinded adjudicator resolves disagreement.

After the first 100 calibration adjudications, raw agreement <85% or both Gwet AC1 and kappa <0.70 suspends accrual. The manual may be clarified without viewing `E x R`; reliability restarts on 100 new calibration patients, prior patients remain feasibility-only, and a second failure is operationally inconclusive. Report all agreement measures. Pump-only performance is secondary and cannot replace intent-qualified rescue.

## Full verification, indeterminates, events, and recurrence

Every screened cessation receives exactly one disposition: eligible first patient, eligible recurrent episode, simultaneous stop, known procedure/transport pause, other ineligibility, capture/linkage failure, or duplicate transition. Every eligible episode has `E in {0,1,?}` and `R in {0,1,?}`; no complete-case deletion. Report the full 3x3 table by arm, phase, site, configuration, interface family, person, and first/recurrent status.

For confirmation, enumerate every joint binary completion of every `?`, subject only to determinate data. For each completion compute all four Se/Sp bounds and endpoint-distortion optimization. The conservative claim uses the union over joint completions: if any admissible completion yields `sup|B|>=0.025`, support fails. This prevents incompatible metric-specific assignments from manufacturing validity. An exact integer/corner optimizer may replace literal person enumeration only after unit-test equality against exhaustive enumeration for every small 3x3 pair with 0–8 indeterminate patients. Determinate-only estimates and identification intervals are secondary. Indeterminacy >5% overall, >7.5% in any arm-by-configuration cell with >=20 first patients, or an arm difference >5 points is adverse and prevents support.

Secondary event analysis enumerates all pump resumptions after qualifying five-minute off spells and all new EHR intervals, maps times with escrowed clocks, and performs one-to-one maximum-cardinality/minimum-total-absolute-lag bipartite matching within patient, episode, drug, and window under locked +/-10 minutes. Ties resolve by earlier pump time, earlier EHR time, then immutable row ID. Matches cannot cross any patient, episode, drug, `t0`, death/exit, or six-hour boundary. Matching never changes `E` or `R`.

## Independent calibration and exact common fixed lock

The inherited separation of calibration and confirmation is retained so that prevalence planning cannot inspect E-by-R accuracy. After run-in, fully verify exactly 1,200 consecutive first qualifying patients. They remain in feasibility, pump-only, recurrent, and transport descriptions but are excluded from confirmatory estimates.

A firewall statistician sees only arm, membership in R-positive and R-negative denominators, indeterminate flags, configuration, first/recurrent flag, and calendar accrual—not `E`, any E-by-R cross-tab, correct/error counts, metric estimates, or narratives. For each of four denominator probabilities (NE/R-positive, NE/R-negative, VP/R-positive, VP/R-negative), calculate the Jeffreys Beta(0.5,0.5) posterior 1.25th percentile from calibration counts. For each lower probability, choose the smallest confirmatory total N giving >=0.975 binomial probability of at least 220 denominator observations. Lock `Nconfirm` to their maximum. This targets at least 220 true-positive-status and 220 true-negative-status observations per arm without outcome-dependent stopping and gives a Bonferroni planning lower bound of 0.90 for attaining all four targets. E-positive prevalence is not used because PPV is not primary.

Before confirmation, escrow configurations, common start, and exact `Nconfirm`. Enroll every consecutive first patient until exactly `Nconfirm` overall or 48 months, whichever comes first; all sites continue to the same lock. There is no denominator-triggered or arm-specific stopping, enrichment, negative subsampling, early success/futility, unblinded look, or post-lock extension. Hard cap remains 6,000 confirmatory first patients (7,200 including calibration), plus fully verified recurrent episodes. If calibration implies >6,000, fewer than six qualified sites/two families exist, expected accrual exceeds 48 months, or any arm/reference-status denominator is <220 at lock, confirmation is inconclusive. One entirely new 1,200-patient calibration is permitted only after documented configuration expansion; old calibration remains feasibility-only.

The exact fixed lock is intentionally conservative but feasible across the declared region: ignoring calibration uncertainty, a status with prevalence 0.15 needs 1,651 total patients for >=0.975 probability of 220 observations; prevalence 0.10 would need 2,485. These are planning calculations, not observed event-rate claims.

## Confirmatory inference and clinical-decision gate

For each of `Se_NE, Sp_NE, Se_VP, Sp_VP`, compute a one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson lower confidence limit at alpha 0.0125. Bonferroni gives a simultaneous confidence set with coverage at least 95%; each upper limit is conservatively 1. This uses exactly the four metrics that map true risks to EHR risks. PPV/NPV have no confirmatory threshold.

For every joint indeterminate completion, maximize `|B|` over the Cartesian product `[L_Se_NE,1] x [L_Sp_NE,1] x [L_Se_VP,1] x [L_Sp_VP,1]` and the six-vertex polygon defined by `p_NE,p_VP in [0.15,0.60]` and `|p_NE-p_VP|<=0.25`. Because `B` is multi-affine, checking all metric-box corners and risk-polygon vertices is exact, not a grid approximation. The global result is supportive only if the largest value over all completions is strictly <0.025 and every configuration transport gate passes. Equality fails.

The inherited target of 220 per arm/reference-status metric now has a transparent consequence. With zero errors in all four 220-person denominators, each exact lower limit is 0.9802788 and worst-case absolute RD bias over the declared region is 0.0246516, so the gate barely passes. At denominator 220, any error in any primary metric may make the global region fail depending on the other bounds. Larger attained denominators permit some errors: if all four denominators and accuracies were equal, the maximum error counts still passing are 0/220, 0/300, 1/400, 3/500, and 4/600. These are diagnostics, not alternate stopping rules. This severity is a consequence of protecting a 2.5-point contrast tolerance, not an arbitrary demand for near-perfect PPV.

Report plugin estimates and exact bounds, `sup|B|`, maximizing risk/metric corner, attenuation/amplification/sign behavior, and decision maps for: superiority sign when `|D|>=0.05`; NI margin 0.05 with robust-accept region `D<=0.025`, robust-reject region `D>=0.075`, and gray zone; plus broader descriptive stress tests for risks 0.10–0.80 and effects up to 0.30. A sensitivity allowing the future trial to correct its arm risks using validation estimates is descriptive; the primary gate addresses the safer use case of an uncorrected locked endpoint and does not assume future error transport beyond enumerated configurations.

A configuration-stratified patient bootstrap (>=10,000 samples) is a dependence/mixture sensitivity; reversal of the pass is inconclusive. Clock-band/off-duration sensitivities cannot redefine the test.

## Configuration transport gate

A configuration is site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, and clock-map method/version. Any change creates a new configuration and fresh run-in; pre/post-change data are not silently pooled.

Transportable support requires at least six sites and two families; both arms at >=4 sites and two families; no site/configuration/family >30% of any of the four R-status denominators; and, for every arm metric, denominator >=40 in at least three configurations and >=20 in at least four. Report configuration/family estimates, leave-one-site/configuration/family-out analyses, hierarchical binomial estimates, heterogeneity variance, and 95% prediction intervals. For every supported configuration (denominator >=40), each conservative Se/Sp lower bound must exceed 0.70. Prediction below 0.70, adjusted configuration range >0.15, or Holm-significant interaction with absolute difference >0.10 is adverse. Every leave-one-out analysis must retain the global 2.5-point distortion gate. Sparse support is inconclusive. Stop a configuration after >5% linkage/clock/export failure in its latest 50 episodes; repaired configurations cannot re-enter confirmation. Two reliability failures or inability to maintain four active validation sites is inconclusive.

## Results and interpretation

**Supportive:** every joint completion and every required leave-out/configuration analysis has simultaneous worst-case absolute RD distortion strictly below 0.025; denominator, fixed-lock, full-verification, clock, indeterminate, recurrence, adjudication, and transport gates pass. This supports using the locked EHR endpoint to preserve contrasts only within the declared region/configurations. It does not support either treatment strategy.

**Adverse:** the completed fixed sample gives worst-case distortion >=0.025; any arm/configuration Se or Sp pattern permits sign reversal or clinically material attenuation/amplification in the region; error is differential; indeterminacy or recurrence error is excessive; matching reveals systematic missing/spurious events; or supported configurations violate transport gates. High PPV cannot compensate for low specificity or sensitivity.

**Inconclusive:** calibration projects >6,000 or >48 months; any denominator is <220 at common lock; a bound equals 0.025; joint indeterminate completions straddle the gate; capture, clocks, linkage, adjudication, representation, or reliability fail; or validation supports only risks/configurations narrower than the declared target. Inconclusive never supports endpoint validity, NI, treatment benefit, or post-lock extension.
