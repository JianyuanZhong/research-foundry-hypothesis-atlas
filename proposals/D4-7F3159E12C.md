## Scientific question and substantive advance

The parent asks whether an earlier biochemical panel adds recorded-risk information beyond a current panel and repeat-assessment timing, including calendar-era transport. This successor changes the estimand and does not use the parent’s repeated panel, ambiguity band, repeat spacing, or no-refit era test.

The clinical question is:

> At a common baseline BMI, do waist circumference and grip strength identify different prospective recorded disease vulnerabilities—waist for cardiovascular/renal events and grip for serious infection?

BMI is common in clinical triage but does not distinguish body shape or physical reserve. A positive result could motivate a prospective study of whether a single anthropometric/strength assessment should route otherwise similar people toward different prevention or vulnerability review pathways. The substantive advance is an outcome-family-specific complement-versus-proxy estimand: it tests whether waist and grip add distinct information conditional on BMI, rather than producing another predictor ranking or asserting that either measurement represents visceral fat, muscle mass, frailty, or a causal treatment target. A null or reversed result would be clinically useful evidence against adding these measurements for this recorded-risk purpose.

The expert seed proposed imaging-defined fat distribution, BMI, grip, cardiovascular/kidney outcomes, infection, and disability. The configured UKB data have no raw imaging bundle and no verified disability outcome field. This is therefore a non-imaging adaptation: waist is a field-level anthropometric measure only if its dictionary meaning and units pass; grip is a measured strength field only if both field semantics and comparability pass. Disability, disease severity, raw body composition, visceral fat, muscle mass, and mechanism are excluded.

The strongest evidence currently supports:

- the presence of the required participant key and fields in the local headers/catalog: BMI `21001-0.0`, waist-like `48-0.0`, strength-like `46-0.0` and `47-0.0`, assessment date `53-0.0`, age `21022-0.0`, sex `31-0.0`, death fields `40000-0.0/1.0`, and the complete outcome code/date arrays;
- one-to-one horizontal `eid` joins as specified by the catalog.

It does not yet establish the field-level meanings, units, valid ranges, measurement protocol, exact code domain, disease onset, specimen timing, or complete follow-up. The local metadata explicitly says that per-field coding/units are absent except for frozen public metadata for age and sex, and that the UKB field-instance index is not elapsed time. Those facts are hard gates, not details to infer from numeric values.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In adults with a valid baseline BMI, waist and grip assessment, adding waist to age/sex/calendar-year/BMI will reduce held-out five-year recorded cardiovascular/renal outcome Brier loss by at least 0.002 relative to the BMI baseline, while adding grip will reduce held-out five-year recorded infection outcome Brier loss by at least 0.002. Moreover, the waist increment will exceed the grip increment for cardiovascular/renal outcome and the grip increment will exceed the waist increment for infection, each by at least 0.001 Brier units after BMI-standardized prediction and the prespecified selection sensitivity.

This is a conditional prognostic-information hypothesis. It is not a causal effect of measuring waist or grip, a recommendation to change body size or strength, proof of visceral adiposity or sarcopenia, a treatment effect, or evidence that a recorded diagnosis marks biological onset.

## Prespecified endpoint families

The health-outcome columns are position-matched: pair `41270-j` with `41280-j` for every j in 0,...,258. Never sort the code and date arrays independently, discard a code with a missing paired date as a negative, or use only positions 0–29. For each endpoint family k, define the first qualifying recorded event strictly after T0 and no later than H:

1. Cardiovascular/renal (C/R): ICD-coded cardiovascular domain I00–I99 OR renal domain N00–N29, but only if the field dictionary confirms a diagnosis-code domain and clinical review accepts these family boundaries.
2. Infection (INF): ICD-coded infection domain A00–B99, only if the same code-domain and clinical review gates pass.

The exact code list, code normalization and handling of decimal suffixes must be frozen in the manifest before outcome labels or model selection are inspected. If the code system is not confirmed as ICD diagnosis data, or the codebook review cannot justify these families, fail closed rather than treating arbitrary numbers as diagnoses.

For each k, construct a three-category five-year recorded outcome:

- T: first qualifying endpoint-k code/date after T0 and by H;
- D: verified death by H before a qualifying endpoint-k event;
- N: neither a qualifying recorded endpoint-k code nor death by H.

If T and D share a verified date, assign D precedence and record the tie count. If death semantics or complete follow-up fail, use only a separately labelled T versus “no qualifying record by H” recorded-category output; never call N disease-free. This fallback does not support the prospective clinical claim.

The primary estimand is the population-average held-out difference in proper predictive loss for a fixed measured-assessment cohort:

- B: age + sex + baseline calendar year + BMI;
- W: B + waist;
- G: B + strength;
- WG: B + waist + strength.

For endpoint k, let \\(Δ_{W,k}=BS(W_k)-BS(B_k)\\) and \\(Δ_{G,k}=BS(G_k)-BS(B_k)\\), where negative values favor the added feature family. Also report \\(Δ_{WG,k}\\), the joint gain, and the interaction/complementarity contrast \\(Δ_{WG,k}-(Δ_{W,k}+Δ_{G,k})\\). The primary directional contrasts are:

- \\(Δ_{W,C/R} \\le -0.002\\) and \\(Δ_{G,INF} \\le -0.002\\);
- \\(Δ_{W,C/R}-Δ_{G,C/R} \\le -0.001\\);
- \\(Δ_{G,INF}-Δ_{W,INF} \\le -0.001\\).

All values are computed at the same participants and outcome labels. These are practical information margins, not clinical utility thresholds.

To make “same BMI” operational, standardize each model’s predicted probabilities to the pooled eligible empirical distribution of age, sex, calendar year and BMI; report the standardized contrast as the primary presentation and the unstandardized held-out contrast as a sensitivity. This is covariate standardization of predictions, not a causal intervention on BMI. A secondary BMI-by-waist and BMI-by-strength interaction analysis uses only predeclared regularized terms and is interpreted only as heterogeneity of recorded prognostic information.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The transparent primary baseline is regularized multinomial logistic regression for T/D/N with exactly B, W, G and WG feature sets. Use field values on their verified native scale after fold-local imputation only if missingness is permitted by the predeclared analysis; the primary cohort is complete on BMI/waist/strength. Include missingness indicators in the complete-case sensitivity, not as an outcome-driven rescue. Standardize continuous predictors fold-locally and use a fixed regularization grid selected inside training data.

The matched learned alternative is CPU HistGradientBoostingClassifier (multinomial classification) fit separately for B, W, G and WG on exactly the same participants, labels, feature sets, five outer folds, standardization distribution, and held-out metrics. Use a small training-only grid: max leaf nodes {7, 15}, learning rate {0.03, 0.10}, max iterations {100, 300}, and L2 regularization {0, 1}; select by inner four-fold multiclass log loss, with deterministic tie-breaking. Calibrate only within the outer training data using an inner split; never use outer-fold outcomes for tuning or calibration.

The baseline estimates whether an inspectable additive increment exists after BMI adjustment. It can miss clinically meaningful thresholds, U-shapes, BMI-by-waist interactions, and discordance patterns such as high waist with preserved grip or low grip at ordinary BMI. The learned alternative can reveal those patterns through predeclared two-dimensional partial-dependence/standardized-risk summaries and subgroup contrasts, but only if they are stable across outer folds and the held-out late/temporal sensitivity where available. A small learned gain without stable calibration or replicated shape is not a substantive discovery.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted outer-fold B/W/G/WG baseline and learned models for both endpoint families, one held-out prediction record per eligible `eid`, the standardized feature-family estimands and confidence intervals, the frozen endpoint/gate manifest, support and selection diagnostics, and reproducible falsification outputs. A complete fail-closed gate certificate is also a valid computational deliverable if the required semantic or observation evidence is absent; it is not a positive or negative clinical result.

## Measurement selection and sensitivity

Complete BMI/waist/grip measurement is not random. If a valid, semantically approved pre-T0 covariate set is available, fit an outcome-blind propensity for complete physical measurement using only age, sex, calendar year, baseline assessment indicators and other verified pre-T0 variables; estimate stabilized inverse-probability weights, truncate at the 1st/99th percentiles determined within training data, and apply them to held-out contrasts. This is a measured-feature transport sensitivity under an observation model, not a causal correction for health-seeking behavior or confounding.

If no additional pre-T0 variables have approved semantics, use age, sex and calendar year only and explicitly report that limited adjustment. Do not use future outcomes, endpoint codes, death dates, or post-T0 fields in the selection model. A complete-case result that reverses under a supported selection sensitivity is adverse for the robustness claim; unsupported weighting is inconclusive, not evidence of reversal.

## Interpretation rules

Supportive evidence for the primary divergence hypothesis requires all relevant field-semantic, timing, code/date, prior-record and ascertainment gates to pass; at least 100 endpoint events for both C/R and INF; no key, pairing, leakage or permutation failure; primary 95% upper confidence bounds at or below the stated margins for \\(Δ_{W,C/R}\\) and \\(Δ_{G,INF}\\); the two family-direction contrasts meeting their \\(-0.001\\) margins; calibrated probabilities without a material deterioration of W/G/WG relative to B (absolute calibration-intercept shift <=0.05 and slope shift <=0.10 for every outcome category); and consistent direction in the complete-case and supported measurement-selection analyses. The learned model strengthens, but is not required for, the transparent family-level claim; it adds scientific support only when its held-out calibration is acceptable and its predeclared BMI-by-feature/discordance patterns replicate across folds.

Adverse evidence is a reversed family direction, a confidence interval excluding the prespecified practical gain in the wrong direction, a gain explained by BMI standardization or measurement selection, disappearance under the within-stratum permutation, material calibration deterioration, fold instability, or a learned pattern that does not replicate. This weakens the claim that waist and grip provide distinct information for these recorded endpoints; it does not show that the measurements have no value in another population or clinical setting.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed or unavailable field semantics/units, inability to confirm assessment timing, ambiguous code domains, missing paired dates, unverified death or five-year ascertainment, fewer than 100 events in either endpoint family, sparse support, unsupported weights, endpoint-list changes after inspection, broken pairing, leakage, unstable folds, or intervals too wide to classify the practical margins. Inconclusive is not evidence for or against the biological hypothesis. A learned-model improvement alone, especially a small discrimination gain without calibration and replicated shape, is also inconclusive.

Computationally checkable claims are source integrity, eligibility flow, exact endpoint construction, model fitting, held-out prediction, score/contrast arithmetic, standardization, calibration, ESS and permutation behavior. Expert clinical adjudication is required for the validity of BMI/waist/grip semantics, endpoint-domain mapping, disease onset, complete ascertainment, action utility and any biological interpretation. Another prospective or externally validated study is required before recommending measurement policy, treatment, screening, or causal monitoring.
