# Does one leakage-safe intermediate hepatic panel improve prediction of an event-aligned early albumin/bilirubin deterioration state after repeat TACE?

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and falsifiable claim

For an adult already selected for a recorded first repeat TACE, can a prior leakage-safe hepatic panel add prognostic information about a coordinated early post-procedure laboratory deterioration state beyond the contemporaneous pre-repeat panel, a remote pre-TACE2 anchor, compact covariates, and the prior observation process?

This replaces the parent's two clinician-timed “next observed assay value” outcomes with one prespecified, event-aligned state: at one common albumin/total-bilirubin timestamp nearest nominal +24 hours inside `[event_time+12h,event_time+36h]`, albumin is lower and total bilirubin is higher than their respective contemporaneous pre-repeat values `P`. The two within-assay directions are clinically coherent with early worsening hepatic laboratory state and do not require cross-assay arithmetic or units. They are not an adjudicated toxicity, decompensation, or indication to stop TACE.

A 2025 retrospective HCC study reported that persistent ALBI-grade deterioration after initial TACE, considered with baseline liver condition and tumour burden, identified subgroups without significant survival benefit from repeat TACE (Chen et al., *Hong Kong Medical Journal*, DOI `10.12809/hkmj2311208`; frozen full HTML source inspected by the parent). That evidence supports the clinical importance of longitudinal liver-function state, not this short-window endpoint, this dataset's unit-free comparisons, or incremental value of an intermediate panel.

The strongest claim currently supported is feasibility. A fresh no-sampling scan of all five sources reconstructed 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator pathway patients and 159/526 selected repeat events. Requiring both `B` assays, both `P` assays, and a same-timestamp post panel in the locked 12–36-hour window yields 34 systemic and 97 comparator episodes. The endpoint occurs in 23/34 and 63/97; every deterministic held-out fold contains events and non-events. Both intermediate assays exist for 32/34 and 93/97. This feasibility audit inspected endpoint prevalence and timing but no predictor–endpoint association or model performance.

The unresolved claim is:

> Adding the two prespecified latest leakage-safe intermediate-to-current within-assay directions improves cross-fitted Brier score for the locked joint early deterioration state by at least 0.02 beyond current/remote within-assay state, compact pre-event covariates, and matched intermediate-panel observation variables.

This is a prognostic-information experiment. It does not estimate treatment effect, toxicity, monitoring benefit, or clinical utility.

## Parsimonious unit-robust nested experiment

Because units/reference ranges are absent, do not use ALBI, native magnitudes across assays, ratios, percent changes, standardized clinical thresholds, or cross-assay arithmetic. Encode equality as reference and define:

- `BP_a_up=1[P_a>B_a]`, `BP_a_down=1[P_a<B_a]` for each assay `a`.
- `LP_a_up=1[H_any_a=1 and P_a>L_a]`, `LP_a_down=1[H_any_a=1 and P_a<L_a]`.

The assays were co-observed for all history-positive endpoint episodes in the feasibility scan. To avoid duplicate collinear observation terms, use albumin's shared-panel `H_any`, `logN`, and `R_hours`; execution must verify albumin/bilirubin equality for `H_any`, `N_H`, and `L_time` in every evaluation patient. A mismatch is computationally inconclusive rather than silently collapsed.

Use two nested unpenalized logistic models:

`M_obs: Y ~ BP_albumin_up + BP_albumin_down + BP_bilirubin_up + BP_bilirubin_down + arm + age + sex + TACE2_to_repeat_days + P_recent_lead + P_old_lead + H_any + logN + R_hours`

`M_value: M_obs + LP_albumin_up + LP_albumin_down + LP_bilirubin_up + LP_bilirubin_down`

where `P_recent_lead` and `P_old_lead` are the smaller/larger positive leads of the two selected `P` timestamps. Thus both models know whether/how often/how recently the intermediate panel was observed; only `M_value` knows its directional numeric relation to `P`.

Assign five arm-balanced folds by SHA-256 sorting of `hcc-repeat-tace-fixed-joint-24h-v1|患者主索引`, rank modulo five. In each fold: train on four folds; training-median-impute age and `R_hours`; impute direction indicators to zero when `H_any=0`; scale continuous predictors using training only; drop zero-variance sex/age-missing indicators identically from the nested models; fit logistic regression with an unpenalized intercept and fixed L2 coefficient penalty `lambda=1` to every retained slope (equivalently minimize mean binomial log loss plus `0.5*sum(beta^2)`, after training-only scaling). Use a deterministic Newton or LBFGS implementation with gradient tolerance `1e-8`, at most 10,000 iterations, and no data-dependent penalty tuning. Require both outcome classes, finite nonzero variance for every retained continuous predictor, optimizer convergence, finite coefficients, and finite held-out probabilities in every fold. The fixed penalty is necessary because the outcome-blind feasibility audit found deterministic exact dependencies in the unpenalized indicator design; it is a stabilization convention, not a tuned claim. No penalty fallback, feature selection, interaction, arm-specific model, nonlinear learner, or threshold optimization.

Primary estimand:

`Delta_Brier = Brier(M_obs) − Brier(M_value)`

from pooled cross-fitted probabilities; positive favors intermediate numeric state. The design-sized margin is `0.02` absolute Brier units, explicitly a forecasting convention rather than a validated clinical benefit. Report both Brier scores, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, prediction range/variance, arm-stratified descriptive Brier scores, observed endpoint prevalence, and each patient's paired Brier contribution. AUROC is descriptive only because discrimination can be unstable at this size.

## Uncertainty, observation selection, and falsification

1. **Paired uncertainty.** Bootstrap 2,000 fixed-seed (`20260911`) patient-level cross-fitted tuples within arm; recompute both Brier scores and `Delta_Brier`. Require 95% estimable. Report percentile 95% interval and one-sided tail `(1+#Delta<=0)/(1+#estimable)`. It is conditional on fitted cross-validation models.
2. **Linkage destruction.** Among `H_any=1`, jointly permute the paired `(L_albumin,L_bilirubin)` panel across patients within arm 500 times, preserving observation variables, `B/P/Y`, covariates, and folds; rederive LP directions and refit. Support requires observed `Delta_Brier` above the 95th permutation percentile.
3. **Missing-history sensitivity.** Repeat identical cross-fitting among `H_any=1` only. A favorable estimate that reverses sign is missing-history-sensitive.
4. **Observation selection.** Among all 159/526 selected events, separately report sequential gates: both `B/P`; any common post panel in 12–36h; complete endpoint. Fit five-fold cross-fitted logistic models for (a) both `B/P`, then (b) common 12–36h panel conditional on both `B/P`, using only arm, age, sex, event year, TACE2-to-repeat days, and pre-panel lead where available. Multiply stabilized stage weights, truncate at fixed 1st/99th percentiles, and recompute weighted paired Brier scores among endpoint-observed episodes. Report positivity, concentration, arm ESS. Favorable evidence is selection-sensitive if systemic ESS<25, comparator ESS<70, one patient has >10% weight, weighted gain reverses, or differs by >0.02. This MAR diagnostic cannot recover unmeasured outcomes or remove collider bias.
5. **Window sensitivity only.** Repeat endpoint construction at common timestamps in `(24h,48h]`, selecting nearest +36h, fixed before modeling. It retained 36/99 in the audit. Recompute `B/P/H`. If at least 75% of primary patients are retained and a favorable gain reverses, call timing-sensitive. Do not use infeasible 0–24h (4/5) or 48–72h (3/15) as inferential alternatives.
6. **Endpoint-component falsification.** Report separate cross-fitted performance for albumin decrease and bilirubin increase using the identical models, secondary only. Because bilirubin increase is 30/34 and 91/97 and “any adverse” is 34/34 and 96/97, neither bilirubin alone nor “either direction” may replace the primary endpoint. A favorable primary gain with neither component's Brier gain positive is endpoint-combination-sensitive.
7. **Influence.** Delete each frozen paired prediction tuple in turn. If any deletion reverses a favorable sign or changes `Delta_Brier` by >0.02, call influence-sensitive.
8. **Repeat-event selection.** Report 319/1,491 to 159/526. Do not weight patients lacking a repeat event because their event-centred outcome is undefined.

## Deterministic interpretation

Apply in order:

1. **Reconstruction/negative-feasibility:** source/snapshot mismatch; unexplained failure to reproduce frozen counts, parser/duplicates, fixed-window support 34/97, endpoint events 23/63, or history support 32/93; join/clock violation. Stop; never widen the endpoint.
2. **Computationally inconclusive:** any fold lacks classes, an included continuous feature has zero/nonfinite training variance, fixed-penalty optimization fails or yields nonfinite estimates/predictions, prediction degeneracy occurs, or <95% bootstrap estimability.
3. **Selection/timing/missing-history/component/influence-sensitive:** a favorable estimate fails its locked diagnostic. Report the estimate but no robust increment.
4. **Adverse degradation:** computational gates pass and the 95% interval lies wholly below zero. Adding intermediate numeric directions worsened generalization.
5. **Supportive evidence:** `Delta_Brier>=0.02`, 95% interval excludes zero, one-sided tail <0.05, log loss does not worsen, observed gain exceeds the 95th linkage-permutation percentile, and no sensitivity destabilizes it. Conclude only that the prespecified leakage-safe intermediate panel directions add reproducible prognostic information for this selected observed state.
6. **Precise null:** gates pass and interval upper bound is below +0.02. Rule out the design-sized gain under the locked model/conditional uncertainty; favor parsimony for this task only.
7. **No demonstrated increment:** point estimate <0.02 or linkage result is comparable, without wholly adverse or precise-null interval.
8. **Inconclusive:** interval spans meaningful degradation and +0.02 or another prespecified ambiguity remains.

Arm coefficients and arm-stratified errors are descriptive. No treatment comparison or interaction claim is permitted.

## Limits and verifier boundary

HCC lacks lab units/reference ranges/analyzer harmonization, adjudicated decompensation, Child–Pugh/MELD, tumour burden/radiographic response, technical TACE success/intent, albumin infusion/transfusion/fluid balance, infection/bleeding, verified drug administration/adherence/dose, outside testing, and reliable mortality. The endpoint can reflect expected peri-procedural physiology or supportive care as well as injury. Conditioning on repeat TACE, pre-panels, and post-panel observation can induce selection/collider bias. Mathematical coupling to `P` and measurement error remain.

A verifier can check hashes/full scans, paths/columns/keys, cohort/event reconstruction, parsing/duplicates, clocks, common timestamps, directional variables, folds/training-only preprocessing, convergence, paired metrics, bootstrap/permutation/weight arithmetic, sensitivities, and branch precedence. It cannot establish hepatic toxicity, decompensation, causal effect, decision utility, or clinical thresholds. Stronger conclusions require unit-validated panels, relevant treatments/supportive care, adjudicated outcomes, tumour/liver context, complete follow-up, external/prospective validation, and expert clinical review.
