# Successor proposal: uncertainty-qualified, book-specific HCC documentary retention under fixed review capacity

## 1. Assigned parent and one substantive repair

This is a successor to assessed-valid [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the parent's documentary-only, fixed-capacity experiment, exact HCC source bindings, complete immutable radiology/pathology reader books, coherent source/onset/reader/pathology states, patient-disjoint calendar roles, locked B/R/G/Gmask scores, K_rho queues, comparator/random-capture margins, anchored deletion, and the exact frontiers q_req, q_LS, q_M2, and q_dec.

The single repair is an uncertainty and operational-decision repair. The parent establishes zero observed loss of a protected documentary-positive set at a threshold, but its binary q_LS<1 result does not quantify how much evidence supports a review-capacity reserve for future eligible episodes. Its external operational bridge correctly says that actual report release, retrieval, readability and clinician viewing are unavailable, but it does not provide a prespecified, fail-closed capacity-screen rule for uncertainty around the observed patient-level loss rate.

I add one secondary frontier, q_LS_U05, without changing any inherited frontier or endpoint. At each allowed threshold it computes an exact one-sided 95% family-wise upper bound on the proportion of all-book label-stable, maximum-content-prioritized patients lost from the incomplete-content queue. The primary tolerance is delta=0.05: at most 5% loss as a capacity-screen tolerance, not a clinical-risk or benefit threshold. q_LS_U05 is defined only if every required protected set is nonempty and the exchangeability interpretation is explicitly accepted as a conditional assumption. If its gate fails, the result is uncertainty-inconclusive or uncertainty-adverse for the reserve claim; the exact finite-corpus q_LS result remains separately reported.

This repairs the bridge between a retrospective zero-loss observation and a consequential but bounded decision: whether the evidence is strong enough to justify a prospective silent-mode archive/queue validation that might reserve fixed review slots. It does not authorize deployment or change care.

## 2. Clinical question, evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

### Clinical importance

For patients undergoing a first eligible hepatobiliary resection, a fixed review service may have fewer report-review slots than eligible imaging content. Missing or incomplete stored report content could alter a maximum-content queue and cause a documentary-M2-positive patient to leave the review set. A capacity policy that preserves a predefined documentary-prioritized set is useful only if the result is reproducible under pathology-label ambiguity and has an explicit uncertainty reserve. This can inform whether a site should fund prospective archive/version capture and silent-mode validation before any clinical workflow decision.

The endpoint is deliberately documentary. It is not biological microvascular invasion (MVI), a specimen-linked diagnosis, a clinician action, recurrence, survival, safety, utility, or patient benefit.

### Strongest supported claim before execution

The HCC snapshot and full catalog establish a linked, read-only set of 12 ordinary CSV sources with encounter keys, dated procedure and examination fields, whole-examination accession identifiers, examination text/metadata, same-encounter pathology text, and dated medication/order rows. Prior full-scan support recorded 105,044 encounter rows, 338,040 procedure rows, 419,996 examination rows, 46,395 pathology rows, 4,097,517 medication rows and 16,730,319 order rows; it found 392,854 nonblank whole-accession examination keys, 16,534 multirow accessions (maximum 12 rows), and 8,932 encounters with multiple pathology rows (maximum 14 rows). These establish structural feasibility only. They do not establish eligibility, Chinese semantic validity, actual report availability by the decision deadline, biological MVI, clinician action or benefit.

The research-ambition README was inspected. Its demonstrations support the value of temporal validation and explicit uncertainty, but they do not validate this HCC population, endpoint or method. The cancer demonstration's main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; only its supplement and metadata are available, so no claim about the unavailable main text is used here.

### Existing evidence versus tested claim

The parent's corrected estimand fixes an important defect: a patient may be documentary-M2-positive under every pathology book but selected by only one book-specific maximum-content model. Requiring membership in every book-specific S_max would misattribute this loss to pathology-label instability. The corrected set is book-specific and satisfies P_common subseteq P_LS(a,b) subseteq P_M2(a,b) subseteq S_max(a,b).

The primary unresolved claim is:

On the fixed 2015–2021 HCC cohort, after all inherited provenance, feasibility, comparator, deletion and nonvacuity gates pass, there is a threshold below full stored-content coverage at which every required current-book, book-stable documentary-positive patient is retained by the incomplete-content queue in every allowed state.

The added uncertainty claim is:

At some allowed threshold, the patient-level loss probability for each required protected set is at most 5%, with a simultaneous one-sided 95% exact upper bound, conditional on exchangeability of eligible patient episodes within each frozen state and on the fixed reader books being the target documentary definitions.

Neither claim concerns biological MVI or an actual clinical intervention.

## 3. Population and temporal design

The analysis unit is one patient and their first eligible source-documented hepatobiliary resection episode. Include age >=18 from encounters age at the earliest eligible resection. Eligibility uses a preregistered hepatobiliary procedure dictionary independently reviewed by a hepatobiliary surgeon before lock. Exclude an earlier qualifying resection and recorded transplant, TACE/embolization, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy in [t_op-365d,t_op), using procedures and patient-wide medication/order temporal joins. Ambiguous dictionary membership is an outer coherent state, never silently resolved.

Join encounter-level tables on (patient master index, encounter number). Treat an explicitly supported anesthesia-system procedure start as a point. Treat a date-like case-record start as [date,date+24h). Preserve missing, contradictory, invalid and competing clocks as separate coherent source/operation states; never substitute admission or discharge time.

Set t_dec=t_op-24h. A CT/MRI acquisition interval is eligible only when wholly inside [t_op-90d,t_dec). The locked 12/48/72-hour onset cutoffs are sensitivity states, not rescues.

Calendar roles are patient-disjoint: 2015–2018 development; 2019 preprocessing, hyperparameter and K lock; 2020 and 2021 separate tests; 2022+ audit only. Any patient possibly appearing in a later role is quarantined from earlier roles. Development labels and score fitting never use test patients.

The fixed target frame is the union-defined eligible frame under inherited all-row pathology rules. Do not condition the frame on consensus or on a successful M2 label. At each state report N, Y, K, number with at least one eligible accession n_A, entry fraction e=n_A/N, zero-acquisition count, incomplete/complete counts, and Q, the proportion of fixed n_A whose every eligible accession is exposed. If n_A=0, feasibility is inconclusive.

## 4. Frozen predictors, queues and documentary outcome

The inherited frozen models are unchanged:

- B(Z): age, recorded sex, eligible acquisition count/recency, acquisition modality/ambiguity and development-grouped machine.
- R(Z,S,Q): B plus report surface/availability fields such as length, component count, field presence and availability.
- G(Z,S,Q,X): B/R plus frozen radiologist-book clinical-content fields.
- Gmask: the identical fitted G object with every semantic block replaced by the training-defined unavailable block, without refitting.
- Capacity-matched random capture: exact expected documentary-positive capture K*Y/N, checked by integer cross-products.

Patient IDs, test labels, pathology, labs, untimed clinical notes, future rows, reader identity and test-derived dictionaries are forbidden predictors. Freeze patient-balanced additive fitting, preprocessing, feature order, coefficients, penalty, binary64 score bytes, tie keys and K_rho=floor(rho*N_ref) in 2019. Require N_ref>=500, K_.05>=25, K_.10>=50, K_.20>=100; each test state requires N>=K_.10 and at least 50 documentary-positive events. Report rho=.10 as primary and .05/.20 as locked sensitivities; use d=24h primarily.

Two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists plus an adjudicator create three complete immutable radiology books. Two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists plus an adjudicator create three complete immutable pathology books. Reader books are global and fixed across patients, years, cutoffs, source/onset worlds, models, thresholds and deletions. No favorable row-level mixing is permitted.

A pathology terminal is one of OUT, IN0-A, IN1-A, IN0-U, IN1-U. Complete states map each terminal to Y_i(a,b) in {0,1}; unresolved alternatives remain separate states. Pathology is never a predictor.

A whole examination accession is (patient master index, encounter number, examination number) with nonblank examination number. Preserve every raw row and field boundary in ordinal order. Never choose a representative or deduplicate text. Blank accession ID, component disagreement, ambiguous modality or irreversible parse failure makes that accession unavailable in that state.

For each source-clean eligible accession, retain one coupled monotone onset state: O=0 eligible by 72h; O=1 first eligible in (72,48]h; O=2 in (48,24]h; O=3 in (24,12]h; O=4 later/never. The hypothetical exposure M=E*V(O) means exposure of eventual stored content, not authored, final, release, retrieval or clinician-view evidence.

For non-EMPTY incomplete-content queue S_G(a,b), define S_max(a,b) by running the same frozen G pipeline with all source-clean eligible accessions exposed. Use exact fixed-K queues. Anchored deletion removes all records of one patient without refit, rerank, backfill or requota; the deleted offered slot remains EMPTY.

Retain strict inherited margins 100*(H_G-H_C)/K>5 for C in {B,R,Gmask}, plus the capacity-matched random-K margin, separately in 2020 and 2021 and every allowed state/deletion. Preserve exact rational threshold set R, monotone PASS(q), q_req, q_M2, q_dec, immediate lower-threshold witnesses and all score/queue hashes.

## 5. Preserved q frontiers and added uncertainty reserve

For fixed nonpathology state a, let B_path be the three pathology books and define:

U_LS(a)={i: Y_i(a,b')=1 for every b' in B_path}.

Retain the diagnostic P_common(a)=intersection over b' of {i: i in S_max(a,b') and Y_i(a,b')=1}.

For current book b, retain exactly:

P_LS(a,b)=S_max(a,b) intersect U_LS(a).

P_M2(a,b)={i: i in S_max(a,b) and Y_i(a,b)=1}.

Thus, for every defined state:
P_common(a) subseteq P_LS(a,b) subseteq P_M2(a,b) subseteq S_max(a,b).

Define J_common=1[P_common subseteq S_G], J_LS=1[P_LS subseteq S_G], J_M2=1[P_M2 subseteq S_G], and J_dec=1[S_G=S_max].

The inherited frontiers remain:

- q_req=min{q in R: PASS(q)=1}, with exact inherited comparator and feasibility gates.
- q_LS=min{q in R: PASS(q)=1 and J_LS=1 for every eligible 2020/2021 state with Q>=q}, requiring every required P_LS nonempty.
- q_M2: the inherited analogous all-current-book documentary-M2 retention frontier.
- q_dec=min{q in R: PASS(q)=1 and J_dec=1 for every eligible state with Q>=q}.

Undefined or equal-to-one frontiers fail their corresponding claims. Preserve the required nesting when defined: q_req <= q_common <= q_LS <= q_M2 <= q_dec. If P_common is empty, q_common is diagnostic-undefined and cannot veto a nonempty q_LS.

### Added exact uncertainty quantity

At each q in R and each eligible state c=(a,b) with Q>=q, set m_c(q)=|P_LS(c)|, x_c(q)=|P_LS(c) minus S_G(c)|, and p_c(q)=x_c(q)/m_c(q).

If m_c(q)=0, the uncertainty reserve is undefined and cannot pass by vacuity. Patient episodes, not rows, are the independent units. Each state uses the complete fixed cohort; no sampling or downsampling is done.

Let C(q) be all required eligible state cells at threshold q, including both test years and every permitted source/onset/reader/pathology/deletion state. Let M=|R|*max_q |C(q)| after the complete state registry is frozen. Use alpha_cell=.05/M. For x=x_c(q), m=m_c(q), calculate the one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson upper bound U_c(q)=Beta-inverse(1-alpha_cell; x+1, m-x). For x=0 it is 1-alpha_cell^(1/m); for x=m it is 1. Store the calculation library/version, decimal result and the integer x,m that determines it. Do not use a normal approximation.

The added reserve pass is U_PASS(q)=1 iff inherited PASS(q)=1, all required P_LS(c) are nonempty, and U_c(q)<=delta=.05 for every required c in C(q).

Define q_LS_U05=min{q in R: U_PASS(q)=1}. Evaluate every threshold directly; do not assume or enforce monotonicity of uncertainty bounds. The immediate lower-threshold witness must show which exact gate fails. By construction, if both are defined, q_LS<=q_LS_U05; if not, report the violated definition rather than assert nesting.

Interpretation of q_LS_U05 is conditional: it is an exact finite-sample uncertainty certificate for patient-episode loss under the declared exchangeability model within each frozen state and for the documentary books as fixed labels. It is not a confidence statement about biological MVI, a future hospital, an unobserved archive process, or patient benefit. If the source is treated as a fixed census with no exchangeability target, report the bound descriptively and classify the operational uncertainty claim as inconclusive.

Report at every q and lower boundary: U_LS, P_common, P_LS, P_M2, S_max minus S_G hashes and counts; cross-book S_max and Y bit-vectors; x,m,U_c; H captures; N/Y/K/n_A/e/Q; EMPTY positions; ordered-slot disagreements; model/score hashes; and attained witnesses. Split losses into (1) label-stable and common-queued, (2) label-stable but not common-queued, (3) book-variable positive, and (4) current-book documentary negative.

## 6. Fail-closed primary interpretation

Use one primary label in this order:

1. computationally_inconclusive: source replay, schema/header/hash, raw ordinal, accession grouping, clock, state, book completeness, score/tie, fixed K, deletion, threshold, set/hash, interval, boundary or invariant checks fail.
2. feasibility_inconclusive: N/K/event/n_A/fixed-frame requirements fail.
3. maximum_content_signal_adverse: any inherited maximum-content comparator, random, year, reader, source or deletion gate fails.
4. aggregate_capture_reserve_adverse: maximum content passes but q_req is undefined or equals 1.
5. label_stability_attribution_inconclusive: any required P_LS is empty.
6. book_specific_label_stable_retention_adverse: q_req<1 but q_LS is undefined or equals 1.
7. uncertainty_reserve_inconclusive: exact q_LS<1, but exchangeability/census target is not declared or q_LS_U05 is undefined because a required set is empty.
8. uncertainty_reserve_adverse: exact q_LS<1, declared conditional target, but q_LS_U05 is undefined or equals 1.
9. label_stable_reserve_supportive_any_book_adverse: q_LS_U05<1 but inherited q_M2 is undefined or equals 1. Stable positives meet the uncertainty reserve, but book-variable positives do not.
10. dual_M2_reserve_supportive_queue_fragile: q_M2<1 but q_dec is undefined or equals 1.
11. full_documentary_queue_supportive_bridge_pending: q_dec<1, all exact minimality/boundary/uncertainty witnesses pass, and no preceding gate fails.

A supportive result means only that the finite HCC documentary experiment and its stated conditional reserve passed. An adverse result means the stated stored-content or uncertainty-reserve claim was falsified in at least one required state. An inconclusive result means computation, nonvacuity or the inferential target prevented the claim; it is neither supportive nor adverse biological evidence.

Always emit diagnostic parent_common_intersection_optimistic when q_common<1 but q_LS is 1/undefined, or q_LS>q_common. Equality to 1 fails. Twelve/48/72-hour, rho, pooled-year, average-book, favorable-state and favorable-deletion results cannot rescue a primary failure.

## 9. Essential missing evidence and limits

Clinical adjudication is essential for the procedure/prior-treatment dictionaries, Chinese radiology and pathology books, terminal mapping, and any claim that documentary agreement means anything biological. HCC has no pathology time, specimen/accession linkage, sampling protocol, blocks/slides or raw imaging. Biological MVI requires specimen-linked pathology and sampling review.

The archive/operational bridge is explicitly unavailable. A future validation dataset must add immutable report/version IDs; amendment/retraction lineage; authored/final/release/ingestion/retrieval/parse-readable/clinician-view timestamps with timezone provenance; exact as-of-cutoff payload hashes; complete nonresponder accounting; and patient-level, year-specific lower bounds on the unchanged N/n_A frame. Only then can Q be interpreted as observed decision-time availability. In this HCC experiment, M is hypothetical eventual stored-content exposure.

Queue-to-action, review workload, clinician behavior, utilities, harms, cost, fairness, transportability, recurrence, survival, quality of life and patient benefit require workflow observation, linked outcomes, prospective silent-mode validation and, for benefit, a decision-appropriate prospective/interventional study.

Reference execution establishes computational feasibility and linkage of conclusions to outputs, not scientific truth. The automatic verifier can establish only the computational claims listed above. It cannot establish clinical semantics, pathology sampling adequacy, biological MVI, actual report access/viewing, clinical action, utility, causality, safety, transportability or patient outcomes.
