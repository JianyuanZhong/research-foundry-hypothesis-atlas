## Falsification and interpretation

A sensitivity materially reverses the primary if it changes the result branch, moves either airway bound by at least 0.05, or reverses midpoint sign with both absolute midpoints at least 0.02. Output recording-lag robustness requires the recorded-by-t0 branch to meet minimums and avoid material reversal; otherwise even a favorable clinical-time result is validity-inconclusive for the combined hypothesis.

Supportive recorded evidence requires the one-sided 95% lower limit of the lower-bound airway contrast above +0.05; chart-augmented and continuous-ICU lower limits above zero; output-recorded-by-t0 and principal recent-course variants feasible and non-reversing; ESS and diagnostics passing; and renal, RRT, death, and adverse-disposition upper limits below their frozen harm margins. The only allowed conclusion is that in this measured retrospective MIMIC cohort, assignment-compatible follow-up under the ICU-input-system recorded A12 policy was associated with at least a five-point improvement in the prespecified recorded airway proxy and was robust to the recorded-by-t0 output definition. It may motivate adjudication, external validation, or a trial; it is not a recommendation.

Adverse is an adjusted airway upper limit at or below zero, renal-harm lower limit at least +0.03, RRT lower limit at least +0.05 under both date brackets, or a frozen death/adverse-disposition margin crossing, provided validity gates pass. Call this an observational signal, not pharmacologic toxicity. Margin-falsified is an airway upper limit at or below +0.05 without an adverse branch. Feasible, validity-passing results that meet none of those are statistically inconclusive. Failed source, ambiguity, positivity, balance, calibration, observation, output-recording, recent-course, or uncertainty gates are validity-inconclusive/infeasible. Null or wide bounds never establish equivalence, safety, or no effect.
