## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, CHI3L1, FABP1, IL-6/CRP family) as disease-specific preclinical biomarkers. It is unresolved whether a protein–diagnosis association marks a disease-specific process (claim A) or a shared systemic morbidity/inflammation/aging axis elevated across many diagnoses (claim B) [K3]. Prediction is not specificity [K1]; measured plasma protein abundances are imperfect proxies [K2]. Resolving this changes whether a nominated protein is a tractable disease target or a marker of general burden.

## Strongest claim supported vs the unresolved test
- Supported by evidence: some circulating proteins associate with multiple diseases and mortality ([K3]; abstract-level only).
- The experiment tests: on a held-out half, is a multi-protein signature's discrimination for disease D disease-specific (claim A) or largely transferable to unrelated diagnoses (claim B)?

## Design (frozen)
- Population: UKB participants with Olink plasma proteomics and an in-patient ICD10 record; prevalent-state (no temporal anchor — see limits). N=53,014.
- Estimand: primary Δ_D = AUC(signature→D) − mean AUC(signature→D') over unrelated diagnoses D', on a held-out half, with a 1,000-permutation null within age-decile × sex strata. Secondary: attenuation fraction after residualizing age/sex and a shared-axis score.
- Variables: exposures = 2,923 Olink NPX proteins; D = a pre-specified diagnosis block; D' = unrelated blocks; covariates = age (n_21003_0_0), sex (n_31_0_0).
- Baselines: age+sex; flexible-age spline; 1-D shared-axis score. Learned alternative: elastic-net / latent-axis signature; the alternative can reveal whether transfer is carried by a coherent module rather than diffuse weights.
- Falsification: supportive = Δ_D lower permutation band bound > 0; adverse = large AUC(D) but Δ_D ≈ 0 (signal fully transferable → claim B); inconclusive otherwise. Negative-control protein set and a V01-Y98 block retained.
