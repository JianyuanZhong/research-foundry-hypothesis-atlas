# State-robust, non-causal fixed-K ranking potential for documentary M2 in HCC

## Successor and repair

This is the Episode-29 successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that candidate's retrospective fixed-K question and exact HCC bindings, but repairs four coupled weaknesses:

1. the estimand is now an identified set over one predeclared uncertainty space rather than a collection of separately favorable clock/access/outcome analyses;
2. the operative clock is explicitly an anchor convention, not an observed clinical decision time;
3. report access and stored-report content are separated: hypothetical exposure is never called observed availability, and absent report versions cannot be imputed;
4. the independently refit, same-information nonsemantic model is the primary baseline; the parent's same-fit semantic masking contrast is retained only as a diagnostic attribution contrast.

The resulting claim is deliberately non-causal:

> In the source-defined retrospective cohort of adult, recorded-treatment-naive HCC patients with a first eligible completed resection, and conditional on the complete HCC snapshot's completed-case index and stored examination content, does a locked semantic-plus-acquisition score capture more documentary-M2 cases in a fixed K-position ranking list than an independently fitted nonsemantic score given the same prespecified information state, with the advantage surviving every prespecified operative-clock, examination-unit/access, semantic-content, and pathology-outcome state?

This is a question about retrospective ranking potential per fixed ranking position. It is not a claim about referral, report release or viewing, service capacity, clinical prioritization, treatment, biological M2, or patient benefit.

## Evidence boundary and unresolved claim

The strongest claims supported before this experiment are source/provenance claims:

- HCC snapshot `[source checksum]` contains linked encounter, procedure, examination, pathology, medication, order and diagnosis tables. Examination rows include acquisition-like start time, accession-like `Examination Number`, modality/name and report text. Pathology contains narrative text but no time, specimen, slide, block or sampling fields.
- The snapshot supports a retrospective completed-resection index and chronological feature-construction boundaries. It does not identify referral, presentation, roster entry, planned operation, cancellation, offered/completed review slots, report authored/final/release/view/version times, or noncompleters.
- The configured research-ambition README identifies the downloaded demonstrations and their availability limits. The cancer demonstration's main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; this proposal does not claim to have inspected them or use them as evidence. The demonstrations motivate temporal separation and explicit uncertainty, but do not validate this endpoint or model.
- The HCC catalog says source files are ordinary read-only CSVs, and the live header audit agrees with the catalog schemas. No images are available; vitals and transfers are identifier-only, and the front-page table is nominal/header-only.

The unresolved claim is narrower than any clinical-effect claim: under a frozen retrospective operation on the source-defined completed-resection index, report-semantic features have a robust incremental association with documentary M2 ranking capture per fixed K positions. The experiment cannot resolve whether the score would improve care.

## Target population and estimand

The target is not all people with liver lesions and not a service's referral population. It is:

- age at least 18 on the selected encounter;
- pathology rows linked to the same patient and encounter explicitly support HCC;
- one first eligible resection episode, after applying the frozen operative dictionary;
- no *recorded* HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy in the preceding 365 days, using only state-matched dated procedures, medications and orders.

This is recorded-treatment-naive, not biologically treatment-naive. Untimed diagnoses are corroboration/audit only and cannot exclude, predict, or establish chronology. Completion and pathology create a retrospective selection on completed cases. Nonmembers, noncompleters, and non-HCC cases are not controls or negatives. The selection limitation is part of the estimand, not something the analysis may silently correct.

## Operative clock and hidden-selection safeguards

The source does not contain an observed decision time. For each eligible operation, define the anchor (t_{
m anchor}=t_{
m op}-d) solely to enforce a pre-anchor information boundary. It must be called an operative anchor, never a referral, presentation, report-release, or decision timestamp.

The procedure dictionary and episode reconciliation are frozen before model fitting by two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician, including `Surgery`, `Surgery Source`, and any validated anesthesia start field if such a field is actually present. Missing, competing, same-day, adjacent-day and midnight/date-only times produce coupled states. A date-only time is the interval [date,date+24 hours), not an exact instant. The primary (d=24) state and 12/48/72-hour sensitivity states are all retained; none is chosen after seeing results.

Features for a member at anchor (t_{

m anchor}-90	ext{ days},t_{
m anchor})). No postoperative or future row may enter membership, report selection, feature construction, calibration or score. The fixed-K list is formed after the complete source-defined test-year index is constructed; its ordering is not called arrival, referral, availability or queue order.

The following checks are mandatory: append-later-row invariance, patient quarantine across periods, no pathology/outcome lineage in features, no future-row lineage, exact join audit, and a coupled-state backpointer for every member and every score.

## Examination identity, access and content uncertainty

An examination source unit is formed before modality filtering or semantic parsing:

[
(\text{patient master index},\text{visit number},\text{examination number}),
]

retaining every component row and raw row ordinal. Blank accession rows remain in the acquisition audit but have identity-unknown text and are masked. Conflicting modalities, component clocks differing by more than 24 hours, or accession ambiguity create an (a)-state containing both the indivisible accession partition and the deterministic normalized ((examination, start-time calendar date)) partition, with raw backpointers. A unit is eligible only if its component clock can be placed in the 90-day look-back; the conservative state uses the maximum component start. At most the latest eligible CT and latest eligible MRI unit are selected, with frozen raw ordinal/hash ties. Two blinded abdominal radiologists classify `examination` as CT, MRI, non-target or unresolved; a third resolves disagreement.

The source has no report-authored, finalized, released, viewed or version field. Therefore no state may be described as an observed access probability or report availability. Exposure (e) is a hypothetical information-mask sensitivity only:

- (e_0): all semantic text masked;
- (e_C): complete stored CT units exposed, MRI masked;
- (e_M): complete stored MRI units exposed, CT masked;
- (e_{CM}): both complete stored units exposed;
- optional prespecified hash-stratified fractions (e_{C,h}), (e_{M,h}), (e_{A,h}), (e_{B,h}), (hin{0.05,ldots,1.00}), where complete units are exposed by frozen patient-hash rank within the relevant modality/pattern stratum.

If fractional states are used, assignment is made before outcomes by the frozen hash and is identical for S and B; it is not a model-selected or outcome-selected sample and is not an estimate of release. A state is invalid if it exposes a phrase or component row without its complete source unit.

The stored report text is held fixed to the snapshot. The unavailable earlier/final/version history is not imputed or averaged. Semantic content uncertainty is represented only by frozen, blinded interpretations of the actually stored text: strict anchored positive/negative/uncertain parsing and expert-adjudicated presence/absence/uncertainty for each feature. If a report version or its content could differ in reality, that is an external limitation requiring versioned report data; it cannot be converted into a stronger internal result. This distinction prevents "eventual stored text" from being mislabeled as content available at the anchor.

## Features and fair baselines

S uses only prespecified interpretable flags from selected complete CT/MRI text: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis and ascites, each represented as present, absent, uncertain or not mentioned. No pathology, diagnosis, laboratory, clinical-document, vital, transfer, identifier, year or post-anchor field is allowed.

B uses the same state and patient set, but no report text or semantic flags. It may use age, sex, CT/MRI/other acquisition indicators, eligible source-unit count, acquisition-to-anchor recency, component count, report-identity ambiguity and development-frequency `machine model` category. This is an acquisition/context baseline, not a clinical standard and not a causal control. A secondary age/sex-only baseline and a deterministic first-index/random fixed-K diagnostic are also reported. An outcome-aware top-K oracle is an unattainable arithmetic ceiling only.

For each (omega), S and B are independently fit on the same 2015–2018 state-matched training rows, with identical patient-level folds, preprocessing lock rules, elastic-net family, tuning grid, missingness handling, calibration procedure and score direction. If (e) masks a modality or fraction in test, the identical mask is applied in development and calibration for B and S. 2019 may lock calibration and a threshold-independent ranking score but cannot alter primary ranking based on test outcomes. A secondary threshold, if reported, is chosen only in 2019 from the frozen grid {0, .005, ..., 1} and is reporting-only.

S0 is generated from S by masking semantic inputs after the S fit. It is deliberately a same-fit diagnostic, not a refit baseline. C is the fair primary comparison; D is a sensitivity/attribution comparison. Neither is a treatment contrast.

## Pathology endpoint and outcome uncertainty

For every exact-deduplicated pathology row on the selected encounter, compose `Pathology`, `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis` in frozen raw order. A frozen position-aware parser sets (Y=1) only for anchored selected M2 and (Y=0) only for anchored M0/M1. Boilerplate, unanchored mentions, missing or uncertain grade, M0/M1 conflict, uncertain linkage or inadequate documented sampling are not silently assigned.

Two Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to all predictors adjudicate every test unknown/conflict and parsed M2; a third resolves disagreement. Retain an adjudicated label where the adjudication is valid and an unresolved interval (Y_iin{0,1}) otherwise. The endpoint is documentary M2 in the linked pathology text, not biological M2, specimen-confirmed microvascular invasion, clinical diagnosis, recurrence, survival or treatment response. There is no pathology time, specimen identifier, slide, block or sampling field; experts may adjudicate text but cannot recover these absent facts.

For a ranking contrast (C=T_S-T_B), let (r_i) be the difference between the two policies' K-list inclusion indicators divided by K. The exact outcome-uncertainty bounds are
(C^- = 100sum_i[r_i>0,r_iY_i^-+r_i<0,r_iY_i^+]) and
(C^+ = 100sum_i[r_i>0,r_iY_i^++r_i<0,r_iY_i^-]), with the analogous formula for D. This preserves shared outcomes for overlapping lists and avoids the invalid practice of independently choosing the most favorable endpoint for each model. If an adjudicated label is fixed, (Y_i^-=Y_i^+).

## Inference and falsification

Refit 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates. Each replicate rebuilds the same coupled state space, development fits, 2019 locks, test rankings and outcome bounds; K, Ω, dictionaries, access masks, margins and decision rules remain frozen. Construct one max-centered simultaneous 97.5% family over C and D, both test years, q values, clock/access/content states, secondary baselines, outcome bounds and prespecified null checks. Every row contains the estimate, uncertainty interval, state-wise lower/upper extrema, K, list M2 bounds, cohort counts, gate vector and raw result locator.

Run 1,000 development-only semantic-block permutations, stratified by year, modality pattern, source-unit cardinality, identity ambiguity and formatting family, with full refits. A semantic result must clear the simultaneous one-sided 0.025 null envelope. Complete semantic masking must make S0's features and scores exactly reproducible under its defined masking operation. The experiment fails if any state can combine incompatible membership, clock, access, content or outcome backpointers.

Required gates are:

- (N_{
m ref}ge500), each claimed (K_qge50), and every required test state has at least K members;
- at least 50 adjudicated M2 events in each claimed year/state envelope, or otherwise no year/state-level claim;
- anchored-grade parsing >=85%, parser kappa >=0.90, adjudicated M2 PPV >=0.98 and sensitivity >=0.95;
- modality agreement >=0.95 and unresolved modality <=1%;
- every claimed O/C/M/CM pattern has >=100 members and >=20 M2 events;
- semantic feature PPV/sensitivity >=0.85 and no required-feature/pattern shift >15 percentage points from 2019;
- calibration slope 0.80–1.20 and intercept -0.10–0.10 for each claimed information regime;
- exact joins, patient quarantine, future-row invariance, no forbidden predictor lineage, coupled-state completeness, optimizer/brute-force equality on N<=12 fixtures, and <=2.5% failed bootstrap replicates.

Falsification is prespecified: C's simultaneous state/outcome lower bound not exceeding 5, C's upper bound at or below 5, semantic-block permutation compatibility, a positive result confined to a single access/content/clock state, or a failed data/adjudication gate. A positive result in an outcome-aware oracle cannot rescue S or B.

## Decision rules

The threshold is five additional documentary-M2 cases per 100 fixed ranking positions. Equality at five is not supportive.

Supportive semantic ranking potential requires all gates, complete state enumeration, and the simultaneous lower confidence bound of C to be >5 across every claimed year, q, clock, identity, access and semantic-content state, after applying the shared-outcome envelope. A corroborative same-fit semantic-reliance result may additionally require D's lower bound >5, but D alone can never establish the primary claim. A supportive result means only that the observed stored-report semantic score has robust retrospective ranking potential within the source-defined completed-case estimand.

Adverse requires an otherwise valid computation whose simultaneous upper bound for C is <=5 in the prespecified primary scope. If the upper bound is <0, report the bounded finding that S has lower documentary-M2 ranking potential than B in that scope. A favorable S0/B result with adverse D means masking did not show a robust semantic reliance signal; a favorable oracle with adverse C means the fitted model did not approach the retrospective ceiling. None implies clinical harm.

Inconclusive includes intervals crossing 5, incompatible state envelopes, a result confined to non-primary sensitivity states, insufficient events, failed identity/content/clock/adjudication/calibration/permutation/temporal gates, undefined/non-K lists, unresolved backpointers, or missing conclusion-to-result linkage. Gate failure is not evidence against a biological association. Supportive, adverse and inconclusive status is reported separately for total C, diagnostic D, and each baseline comparison. No favorable state may be promoted to the primary result, and no failed state may be omitted.

These rules are non-causal. They do not estimate what would happen if a clinician received, trusted, or acted on a report. They do not establish a treatment effect, benefit, fairness, workflow utilization, or clinical utility.

## What can and cannot be verified

An automatic verifier can check source hashes/headers, exact joins and filters, frozen K, patient quarantine, temporal feature boundaries, state/backpointer coupling, complete-unit masking, identical information masks for S and B, independent fitting, S0 masking identity, fixed-K arithmetic, shared-outcome interval arithmetic, bootstrap/permutation execution, decomposition identities, all-state result coverage and conclusion-to-result locators.

It cannot establish true referral identity, report authorship/release/view/version, real capacity or workload, specimen linkage/sampling, biological M2, acceptable clinical tradeoffs, action, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, fairness or transportability. Those require a timestamped preoperative roster with noncompleters/non-HCC cases, versioned report/access logs, specimen-linked pathology and prospective silent-mode/impact evaluation. Clinical adjudication is required for operation dictionaries, modality/content interpretation and uncertain pathology text; it cannot recover unavailable timestamps or specimen fields.

The following fixtures are mandatory:

1. Fixed-K arithmetic: K=100, S has 40 and B 27 documentary M2 cases, so C=13 cases/100; shared outcome bounds must reproduce this when labels are fixed.
2. Equal lists: C=D=0 and all decomposition terms are zero.
3. Swap symmetry: swapping S and B negates C exactly.
4. Shared-outcome uncertainty: a patient appearing only in B uses the upper label for the lower contrast bound and the lower label for the upper bound; independent favorable endpoint choices are rejected.
5. Non-K/undefined lists never receive imputed negatives; the state is inconclusive.
6. S0 masking is deterministic; its output is reproducible, while D is never mislabeled as the independent baseline comparison.
7. Future isolation and patient quarantine hold after appending later rows.
8. Clock/access/content/pathology coupling rejects mixed-state composites.
9. A positive C must be rejected if labeled referral performance, report-release benefit, workflow utilization, causal prioritization, biological M2, treatment effect or patient benefit.
10. Correct adverse and inconclusive conclusions must be accepted, and every accepted conclusion must point to a computed row containing state extrema, K, counts, gates, source hashes, code version and raw locator.

Reference execution establishes computational feasibility and linkage only; it does not establish clinical truth.
