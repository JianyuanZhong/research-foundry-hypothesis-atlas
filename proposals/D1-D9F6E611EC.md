# Episode 11: a restricted-mean oxygenation-curve test of the pH-conditioned SpO2 mapping

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and advance

When released monitor SpO2 is 96–97%, does acidemia correspond to a clinically material upward displacement of the observed PaO2 distribution among observations sharing the same hospital and similar measured oxygenation context, even when the sparse PaO2 >120 mm Hg tail is prevented from dominating?

This is a different and more information-efficient question than the episode-10 binary endpoint. Episode 10 asked whether acidemia raised the probability of PaO2 >120 mm Hg by at least 10 percentage points; only ten events occurred in the 237-row measured-overlap population, yielding +2.955 points (95% interval −7.875 to +15.535) and an inconclusive result. The successor asks whether the *whole PaO2 mapping up to that same 120-mm Hg boundary* is shifted upward by at least 10 mm Hg. Every retained PaO2 contributes, while values above 120 are tied at 120 so a few extreme values cannot manufacture support.

A 10-mm Hg distributional displacement is prespecified as the material threshold. It is large relative to routine PaO2 target separation: the inspected 2026 OXY-BREATHES review categorized conservative oxygen targets as PaO2 <80 and liberal targets as PaO2 >=90 mm Hg. It is also one third of the distance from 90 to the parent's 120-mm Hg hyperoxemia boundary. This is a clinical anchor, not a validated individual-level minimum clinically important difference.

The clinical decision is whether the pH-conditioned SpO2-to-PaO2 mapping is discrepant enough to justify a prospective, synchronized validation study and possibly test pH-aware oxygen assessment. This retrospective experiment cannot justify extra arterial blood gases, lower FiO2, an oxygen target, or any treatment change.

## Evidence already supported versus claim to be tested

The inspected full-text XML of Delgado et al. (Antioxidants 2026; DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712) describes a retrospective single-tertiary-center study of 21,406 adults and 717,064 paired measurements. It reports higher PaO2 in acidotic states at comparable SpO2, modification by FiO2, and generally physiologic PaO2 at SpO2 96–97%. This supports physiologic plausibility and a large single-center concurrent association; it does not establish the EICU overlap contrast, a causal pH effect, multi-hospital transportability, toxicity, or treatment benefit.

The inspected full-text XML of Nguyen et al. (Critical Care Medicine 2026; DOI 10.1097/CCM.0000000000007031; PMCID PMC13134654) reports a systematic review of nine randomized trials with 20,447 mechanically ventilated adults. Conservative and liberal targets had no substantial difference in 90-day mortality (RR 1.01, 95% CI 0.94–1.09), with subgroup uncertainty. This establishes that target selection is consequential and unresolved; it does not test pH-conditioned measurement mapping and cannot turn the present association into treatment evidence.

The strongest EICU claim supported before the proposed inferential execution is computational: complete-source reconstruction yields 822 SpO2-band rows, 705 acidemic/normal rows, and the frozen 237-row, 57-cell, nine-hospital overlap population. A bounded feasibility run on all source rows found:

- overlap-weighted mean PaO2 91.34 versus 80.57 mm Hg, but the acidemic maximum was 556.4 versus 182.0 mm Hg and the raw-mean contrast interval was −2.11 to +39.02;
- overlap-weighted restricted means E[min(PaO2,120)] of 85.08 versus 79.58 mm Hg, contrast +5.497 mm Hg;
- a 5,000-replicate copied-hospital/person interval of −2.673 to +11.852 mm Hg, with 5,000/5,000 replicates defined;
- a weighted median contrast of +8.4 mm Hg; and
- leave-one-site-out restricted-mean contrasts from +1.926 to +6.613 mm Hg.

These are feasibility/reference-QC outputs from the same snapshot, not independent confirmation. They show that the restricted mean is much less tail-sensitive than the raw mean, but the reference result is still **inconclusive** about +10 because its interval contains +10 and 0.

**Primary hypothesis (frozen before compilation):** in the prespecified measured-overlap population, the symmetric overlap-weighted acidemic-minus-normal difference in E[min(observed PaO2,120 mm Hg)] is at least +10 mm Hg.

The estimand, cap, threshold, cells, weights, bootstrap, and interpretation states must not change after execution.

## Measured-overlap population and primary estimand

Before reading PaO2, create a typed tuple cell containing:

- integer `hospitalid`;
- numeric released median SpO2 (96, 96.5, or 97);
- integer `floor(FiO2_percent/10)`;
- integer `floor(PaCO2/20)`, with missing PaCO2 represented by a separate typed missing level; and
- integer `floor(draw/1440)` for ICU day 0, 1, or 2.

Do not use concatenated strings as cell identity. Retain cells containing at least one acidemic and one normal observation. Reconcile exactly 237 rows, 57 cells, 198 stays, 197 people, and nine hospitals: 110 acidemic and 127 normal rows. The hospital IDs are 122, 183, 184, 318, 394, 420, 443, 458, and 459.

For cell s with counts n1s and n0s, assign each acidemic row `w=n0s/(n1s+n0s)` and each normal row `w=n1s/(n1s+n0s)`. Normalize weights separately within exposure group.

For each row define `Y120=min(PaO2,120)` in mm Hg. The group restricted mean is

`mu_a = sum_i I(A_i=a) w_i Y120_i / sum_i I(A_i=a) w_i`.

The primary contrast is `Delta_RM120 = mu_1 - mu_0`.

Because valid PaO2 is at least 20, `mu_a = 20 + integral from 20 to 120 of P_a(PaO2>t) dt`. Thus Delta is the signed area between the two overlap-weighted PaO2 survival curves from 20 through 120, expressed in mm Hg. It is a broad oxygenation-curve displacement, not the probability of crossing 120, not the uncapped mean, and not causal.

Report both group restricted means, Delta, group weight sums, ESS `(sum w)^2/sum(w^2)`, maximum normalized row weights, each site's weight fraction, and weighted empirical survival probabilities at fixed PaO2 values 60, 70, 80, 90, 100, 110, and 120 mm Hg. Plot the two weighted empirical curves only as a descriptive rendering of these computed values.

## Uncertainty and diagnostics

Use 5,000 deterministic hierarchical bootstrap replicates, seed 20260910, conditional on the frozen 237-row source:

1. sample its nine hospitals with replacement, taking nine draws;
2. within each sampled hospital copy, sample its `uniquepid` clusters with replacement, with draw count equal to that original hospital's observed person count, carrying all rows;
3. assign every hospital copy and person copy a new identifier;
4. rebuild typed overlap cells using the *new hospital-copy ID*, discard replicate cells lacking one exposure group, recompute symmetric weights, both restricted means, and Delta.

Report defined/failed replicates and the percentile 95% interval. Pooling repeated hospital copies under an original ID is a failure.

The primary interpretation is forced to inconclusive if more than 10% of replicates are undefined, either observed group ESS is <50, one original site contributes >50% of observed total overlap weight, or deleting any site with at least ten overlap rows reverses the observed primary sign. Report all other prespecified sensitivity estimates and intervals; a sign reversal in earliest-person, tighter-cell, 12-hour-block, or any outcome-blind timing sensitivity is a robustness failure and forces inconclusive language. Do not replace a failed analysis with a favorable baseline.

The reference execution has ESS 87.7 and 105.7, maximum site weight 45.3%, no leave-site-out sign reversal, and 5,000/5,000 defined replicates. Its Delta +5.497 mm Hg (95% interval −2.673 to +11.852) is inconclusive about +10.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive for a material broad mapping shift** requires Delta's lower 95% bound >=+10 mm Hg and every primary diagnostic to pass. The strongest allowed conclusion is: "Within this retrospective measured-overlap construction, the PaO2 distribution through 120 mm Hg was displaced upward by at least 10 mm Hg in acidemic versus normal-pH observations." This does not establish a mechanism or action.

**Adverse to +10** means the upper 95% bound is <+10. If the interval is wholly above 0 but below 10, report a smaller positive displacement that is adverse to the material threshold. If the upper bound is <0, report an opposite-direction association in this estimand.

**Inconclusive** includes any interval containing +10, any mandatory diagnostic failure, or a required sign reversal. A point estimate +11 with interval −1 to +20 is inconclusive. The reference +5.497 (−2.673 to +11.852) is inconclusive: it supports neither "at least +10," "no association," nor "adverse to +10."

Discordant endpoints have fixed meanings:

- restricted-mean support with binary PaO2 >120 adversity supports a broad sub-threshold shift, not increased hyperoxemia probability;
- binary support with restricted-mean adversity supports a tail-specific difference, not a broad curve displacement;
- a supportive uncapped mean with an inconclusive/adverse primary is tail sensitivity, not primary support;
- full-cohort B2 support with overlap adversity means the broader association did not survive the measured-overlap challenge; differing target populations prevent attributing the discrepancy uniquely to confounding.

No result establishes tissue hyperoxia, oxidative injury, benefit or harm from oxygen, or a treatment rule.

## Evidence unavailable and claims requiring another study

EICU cannot establish arterial specimen identity, co-oximeter arterial saturation, waveform synchronization or artifact, monitor/device make or calibration, delivered FiO2, temperature, 2,3-DPG, exact ventilation persistence, clinician intent, or missing-at-random measurement. Clinical adjudication is required to validate specimen and ventilation status and interpret implausible/extreme PaO2 values. Prospective synchronized measurement is required to validate the pH-conditioned curve and decide whether it can inform a pre-draw alert. External validation is required for transportability. An intervention study is required for any claim about extra ABGs, oxygen titration, toxicity, clinical utility, benefit, or harm.
