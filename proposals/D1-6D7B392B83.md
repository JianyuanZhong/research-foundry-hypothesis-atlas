# A route-blinded clinical-actionability and superseding-plan falsification layer for pulmonary-nodule discharge routing

## Decision question and targeted repair

The frozen decision is whether any exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity domain atom may be placed on a retrospective `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` allow-list for a **prospective silent** validation. The experiment remains noncausal and all-unit adverse: one unsafe or unresolved recommendation unit controls the admission. No retrospective output authorizes a live alert, copied recommendation, order, referral, or patient-facing action.

Parent `[prior hypothesis]` makes text-route reproducibility difficult to fake. It preserves a complete first-eligible all-opportunity population, reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum chronology, one canonical discharge artifact, exhaustive departure/availability/lag/eventual states, unit-specific opportunity clocks, split evidence views, all-route audit, exact era/service/workflow support atoms, minimax release, and at most 900 distinct reviewed admissions. It also adds roster-disjoint holistic panels, separately written codebooks, counterfactual text controls, and adverse compatible-set inference. Its remaining decision-validity weakness is fundamental: H1, H2, H3, and the component panels can all reproduce the same clinically wrong ontology. They can agree that a recommendation was omitted or replaced without establishing that it remained clinically actionable at departure, that an apparent replacement truly superseded it, or that copying it would be preferable to clinician reconciliation. Expert agreement is another observation, not truth.

This targeted successor preserves every frozen population, chronology, artifact, unit, route, domain, sample-size, inference, workload, no-rescue, and noncausal rule. It adds a separately blinded **clinical-actionability/superseding-plan decision reference**, route-reveal and route-swap controls that specifically detect route-informed clinical inference, and a partial-identification analysis that permits all clinical reviewers to share correlated error. The reference layer can falsify a route; it cannot prove clinical correctness.

The clinically consequential unresolved claim is:

> Among admissions that are textually reproducible and support-qualified, are the units proposed for `AUTO` distinguishable from units requiring clinician reconciliation or no intervention when clinicians judge actionability without seeing the derived route or the discharge-text realization used to create it, and does that distinction survive adverse reference uncertainty and route-inference controls?

The falsifiable hypothesis is that every atom proposed for `AUTO` will have a simultaneous adverse upper bound of at most 0.05 for units compatible with clinician choice, no action, supersession, or unknown; every atom proposed for `RECON` will have a lower bound of at least 0.90 for a still-active but clinician-choice-compatible unit and an upper bound of at most 0.05 for no-action/superseded units; route-invariant controls will have an upper flip bound of at most 0.02; and these gates will hold under a preregistered correlated-reference-error budget. A supported atom that fails is excluded even if all text panels agree. An atom without a defensible shared-error budget is reported only as conditionally classifiable and prospectively defaults to `DEFER`.

## What evidence currently supports, what remains untested, and why it matters

### Strongest supported claim

The configured MIMIC snapshot contains enough admission-linked radiology and discharge text, within-subject storage times, reciprocal report/addendum metadata, service histories, and ICU-workflow proxies to construct the frozen retrospective audit. Complete-source profiles already performed for the lineage found:

* `anchor_year_group` patient counts of 101,607 (`2008 - 2010`), 76,266 (`2011 - 2013`), 71,640 (`2014 - 2016`), 65,941 (`2017 - 2019`), and 49,173 (`2020 - 2022`).

These are source-feasibility facts. No clinical-actionability labels, final `N_A`, sampled admission IDs, route prevalences, or route-validity results have yet been produced in this episode. The proposed clinical review is prospective work within the experiment, not a completed analysis. No content prevalence is invented.

The parent supports, at most, the proposition that route-defining assertions can be tested for reproducibility against several independent text measurements. It does not support clinical actionability, appropriateness, responsibility, awareness, actual discharge visibility, completed follow-up, safety, or benefit.

### Untested claim

The experiment tests whether textually candidate `AUTO` units are, on the supplied record, robustly compatible only with an active and portable follow-up action; whether candidate `RECON` units are robustly compatible with an active action that needs a clinical choice; and whether explicit target-linked text supports a genuine superseding-plan interpretation rather than mere omission, conflict, restatement, or route-shaped inference.

This distinction matters because the intervention is different. Propagating an inactive or genuinely superseded recommendation can cause duplicate imaging, anxiety, incidental cascades, and workload. Sending an active but clinically conditional recommendation directly to structured propagation can suppress needed clinician judgment. Conversely, treating a simple active omission as generic reconciliation creates avoidable workload and delay. Published workflow reports show that closed-loop incidental-finding systems expose clinicians to acknowledgements and follow-up-order decisions; for example, the abstract of Fu et al. reports 12,919 alerts, 93.9% acknowledgement within 73 hours, and follow-up orders for 62.3% of patients in one implementation (DOI `10.1007/s10278-024-01357-7`; frozen Europe PMC search source `[source checksum]`). Those implementation observations do not establish benefit and are not assumed transportable to MIMIC. The research-ambition guide was inspected; none of its demonstration papers is treated as a source for this pulmonary-nodule design.

## Independent clinical-actionability and superseding-plan reference layer

### Governance and role independence

Before packet generation, a multidisciplinary governance group freezes and hashes: the clinical question, state definitions, evidence hierarchy, packet schemas, allowed and prohibited fields, roster qualifications, control transformations, correlated-error sensitivity grid, margins, workload budget, output shell, and decision logic. At minimum, the actionability roster contains pulmonary clinicians and care-transition/generalist clinicians; the superseding-plan roster is separately staffed. No reviewer may have served in eligibility, redaction, R/K/S, H1/H2/H3, minority-safety, packet-construction, or route-analysis roles. Expertise diversity is intended to expose different decision assumptions, not to manufacture a consensus truth.

No real `N_A` case is used for training or ontology tuning. Use only synthetic cases not derived from MIMIC for role rehearsal. All initial individual labels, rationales, cited spans, missing-evidence fields, response status, and review times are append-only sealed. There is no consensus meeting and no majority-vote replacement. Disagreement is scientifically retained.

### Actionability packet `C-P`: route and discharge realization concealed

For each selected admission/unit, two independently staffed clinical-actionability reviewers receive:

1. the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, including the operative finding and recommendation payload;
2. permitted admission/death/disposition fields and relative within-admission chronology;
3. the canonical DS context after independently redacting every pulmonary target, action, timing, recipient, responsibility, replacement, completion, conflict, and recommendation-status span; and
4. an explicit checklist of unavailable evidence.

They do **not** receive the canonical DS target-plan realization, derived route, component/holistic labels, unit match, atom, stratum, weights, raw service labels as ownership claims, categorical opportunity clock, POE, post-`D` evidence, or outcomes. Exact times are retained for chronology validation by locked code but displayed only when clinically necessary and never as route categories. Packet redaction is independently audited; any residual route cue or material context removal is adverse unknown.

Each reviewer labels the unit independently:

* `C_PORTABLE_ACTIVE`: on the supplied evidence, the recommended target/action/window remains active at departure and could be copied into a structured pending-action field without choosing among clinically material alternatives;
* `C_CHOICE_ACTIVE`: some target-specific follow-up appears active, but modality/window/priority/recipient or competing context requires clinician reconciliation before propagation;
* `C_NO_ACTION`: the supplied pre-departure context explicitly makes the recommendation inactive, completed, contraindicated, declined, or inconsistent with a documented care goal;
* `C_UNDETERMINED`: the supplied record cannot distinguish the above; and
* `C_PACKET_INVALID` for leakage, missing required artifact, or unreadability.

A reviewer must cite source spans and record which missing facts could change the decision. `C_PORTABLE_ACTIVE` is not a declaration that the recommendation is guideline-correct. It means only that this reviewer found no supplied-record reason requiring a new clinical choice.

### Superseding-plan packet `S-P`: replacement extraction separated from actionability

Two different reviewers receive only the reciprocal radiology recommendation, every pre-`D` target-linked statement in the canonical DS with limited local context, source identity, and within-admission ordering. They do not see `C-P` labels, routes, atoms, clocks as categories, non-target contextual conclusions, POE, post-`D` notes, or outcomes.

For each radiology unit they enumerate rather than vote:

* target linkage and whether linkage is exact, possible, conflicting, or absent;
* action/modality, timing/window, polarity, recipient/responsibility assertion, and status;
* whether a later statement explicitly says it **replaces**, **completes**, **cancels/declines**, **modifies**, **coexists with**, or is merely a **restatement** of the radiology recommendation; and
* quoted evidence and unresolved coreference.

The locked hierarchy yields `S_EXACT_SUPERSESSION` only when a later pre-`D` statement is target-linked and explicitly replaces/completes/cancels the original with no competing compatible interpretation. `S_CONFLICT_OR_MODIFICATION` covers incomplete replacement, materially different modality/window, unresolved coexistence, or conflicting plans. `S_NONE` means no observed target-linked superseding statement; absence is not proof that no outside plan existed. `S_UNKNOWN` includes ambiguous linkage, missingness, nonresponse, or packet invalidity.

A discharge sentence that simply omits or paraphrases the recommendation is never a superseding plan. A service code, author field, billing code, order-like row, or reviewer intuition cannot establish supersession.

### Clinical decision-compatible set

For unit `u`, locked code forms the Cartesian product of every individual `C-P` and `S-P` label, redaction-audit result, packet validity state, and inherited boundary uncertainty. It maps each compatible combination to latent **decision need**:

* `K_P` (portable active) only when `C_PORTABLE_ACTIVE` and `S_NONE` are the sole compatible clinical/supersession states;
* `K_R` (active clinical choice) when every compatible state remains active but at least one is `C_CHOICE_ACTIVE` or `S_CONFLICT_OR_MODIFICATION`;
* `K_N` (no new target action) when any compatible state includes `C_NO_ACTION` or `S_EXACT_SUPERSESSION`;
* `K_U` when any state is undetermined/invalid, action status conflicts across reviewers, or active versus no-action cannot be distinguished.

`K_N` and `K_U` dominate `K_P` for safety; `K_U` is not repaired by H1/H2/H3 agreement. At admission level, all unit-level decision needs are retained. One `K_N` or `K_U` unit blocks admission-level `AUTO`; coexistence of `K_P` and `K_R` gives `RECON`; all units `K_N` gives `NOACTION/DEFER` under the inherited route framework. These clinical states do not rewrite inherited routes. They are a second, adverse release filter.

## Controls targeted to route-informed clinical inference

Controls are generated from the already selected admissions before labels under a sealed transformation manifest. They are duplicate readings, not new sampled admissions and not primary MIMIC labels. Transformed text is stored separately with immutable provenance. No hand editing is permitted when a locked parser cannot identify an unambiguous span; infeasibility is adverse.

### 1. Route-reveal anchoring control

After the primary `C-P` labels are sealed, a separate roster-disjoint control reviewer sees the identical clinical packet plus the actual target-plan realization from the canonical DS. The clinical facts are otherwise unchanged. The expected actionability state is invariant: DS omission, faithful copy, or incompleteness by itself must not decide whether the recommendation is clinically active. Any switch between `C_PORTABLE_ACTIVE/C_CHOICE_ACTIVE` and `C_NO_ACTION`, or a confidence increase unsupported by a newly revealed clinical fact, is a route-anchoring signal. This control estimates susceptibility to route realization; it does not identify which label is true.

### 2. Route-swap negative control

When locked spans permit it, replace only the DS route realization with a provenance-matched synthetic realization representing another inherited route while preserving the radiology recommendation and all clinical-context facts: omission versus faithful copy, formatting-only incompleteness, or responsibility wording without a clinical replacement. Reviewers are blinded to pair status and order. `C-P` decision need must remain invariant. A change detects route-informed inference. No transformed result enters the real-case route label.

### 3. Non-target and nuisance controls

Insert or remove an unrelated nonpulmonary recommendation sentence, permute non-target section order, or alter author/signature/formatting tokens while preserving semantics. Clinical decision need and superseding status for the pulmonary unit must not change. These controls detect generic discharge-route and provenance shortcuts.

### 4. Superseding-plan decisive controls

For cases with unambiguous target spans, create one locked pair that adds an explicit, target-linked replacement statement and one that changes the same statement into a coexistence or uncertain-modification statement. `S_NONE` must transition to `S_EXACT_SUPERSESSION` only in the explicit replacement version; coexistence must map to `S_CONFLICT_OR_MODIFICATION`. This is a sensitivity/logic control, not evidence that reviewers can recognize true real-world supersession.

### 5. Clinical-context decisive controls

Only in synthetic fixtures, hold the route realization fixed while changing one explicit clinical fact, such as documented completion of the same target action versus a still-pending action, or explicit patient decline versus no preference evidence. The actionability state must change according to the locked table. Failure shows insensitivity to a clinically decisive fact. Success does not validate the ontology.

### 6. Wrong-ontology agreement fixture

Plant a synthetic case in which every text panel and both clinical codebooks can agree on a portable action because of a shared phrasing convention, while the sealed clinical fact table includes an explicit target-linked completion or replacement. The implementation must show high agreement yet assign `K_N/K_U` and block `AUTO`. A verifier that treats agreement as truth fails this fixture.

Pair order, pseudonyms, and transformation type are concealed. Missing control labels, parser-infeasible transformations, pair unblinding, or post-label construction widen adverse bounds and cannot rescue an atom.

## Partial identification with correlated reference error

### Why no gold standard or primary latent-class point estimate is allowed

Let the unobserved true departure decision need be `T_i in {P,R,N,U}`. Individual C and S labels, inherited text panels, and controls are fallible measurements. Conditional independence is implausible because reviewers share the same limited notes, medical culture, and possibly the same implicit assumptions. More independent codebooks do not make a latent-class model identifiable. No reviewer panel, senior adjudicator, or majority vote is called a gold standard.

The primary analysis therefore reports bounds, not a point estimate of truth. A latent-class model may be shown only as a diagnostic sensitivity if its identifying restrictions were externally justified before labels; it cannot narrow primary bounds or certify an atom.

### Observable compatible-set bounds

First compute sharp design-weighted lower and upper bounds over all individual observed labels, nonresponse, packet defects, redaction uncertainty, boundary ambiguity, and control outcomes. For each atom and inherited route, solve the finite-population linear or linear-fractional program over admission-level assignments; retain all units from an admission together. Report bounds for:

* `Q_AUTO,g`: proportion of candidate-`AUTO` admissions with any unit compatible with `K_R`, `K_N`, or `K_U`;
* `Q_NOACTION_AUTO,g`: candidate-`AUTO` proportion compatible with `K_N`;
* `A_RECON,g`: candidate-`RECON` proportion whose every compatible unit remains active and at least one requires `K_R`;
* `Q_NOACTION_RECON,g`: candidate-`RECON` proportion compatible with `K_N`;
* target-linked exact-supersession and conflict burdens per 100 `N_A` admissions;
* route-reveal, route-swap, non-target, nuisance, and decisive-control violation rates; and
* actionability/supersession review hours per 100 atom admissions.

Unknowns are assigned adversely: for `AUTO`, to `K_R/K_N/K_U`; for `RECON`, to no-action or unresolvable; for capture, to the missed state; for workload, to the upper compatible time. There is no complete-case or favorable-consensus primary estimate.

### Correlated shared-error envelope

Observed disagreement cannot bound a shared mistake. For each atom `g`, introduce `kappa_g`, the finite-population fraction on which **all** clinical-reference measurements may be jointly wrong in the same direction and `T_i` may lie outside the observed compatible set. For a specified `kappa`, permit adversarial reassignment of up to `floor(kappa*N_g)` atom admissions, prioritizing high-weight admissions and keeping all units together. Recompute sharp lower/upper endpoints. This is a contamination neighborhood, not an estimated error rate.

Report:

1. the unrestricted envelope `kappa in [0,1]`, which cannot establish a clinically correct route and will ordinarily imply only `DEFER`;
2. a prespecified sensitivity grid `kappa={0,0.01,0.025,0.05,0.10}`;
3. each atom-route **breakdown point** `kappa*_gr`, the smallest shared-error fraction for which any release gate fails; and
4. a two-component envelope `kappa_total <= kappa_clinical + q_route`, where `q_route` is bounded by the simultaneous upper route-invariant control-flip rate and `kappa_clinical` remains unidentifiable from MIMIC.

Controls can falsify a small `q_route`; they cannot identify `kappa_clinical`. A governance-selected `kappa_max` may govern a conditional silent-validation nomination only if it was frozen before labels and justified by an external, ascertainment-described validation source independent of these MIMIC text artifacts. If no such justification exists, report conditional curves and force `DEFER` prospectively. Expert agreement alone is never justification for `kappa_max=0`.

## Design-based inference

Retain the parent's HT totals, Hájek ratios, finite-population variance, hypergeometric/multivariate-hypergeometric inversion, and at least 20,000 Rao-Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates within every noncensus design cell. Every admission's inherited labels, clinical labels, controls, compatible states, and workload remain together in each replicate; census admissions remain fixed.

Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over all inherited pooled and atom gates, H1/H2/H3 and control gates, the new clinical-actionability/supersession quantities, every final atlas atom proposed for a route, minimax endpoints, and correlated-error sensitivity endpoints at the policy `kappa_max`. Report numerator and denominator totals and do not report a ratio with zero estimated denominator. For rare events use the widest valid simultaneous endpoint from exact inversion, bootstrap, and compatible-set optimization. Zero observed adverse events in a noncensus atom never imply zero upper risk.

The policy sensitivity grid and breakdown points are required descriptive outputs. Only the preregistered policy `kappa_max` can govern a conditional nomination; selecting a favorable `kappa` after labels invalidates selection.

## Atom-level release logic

The new layer can only remove an atom from a route allow-list; it cannot rescue one.

Governance must approve the new margins, `kappa_max`, and absolute review budget before labels. The defaults below are operational prerequisites for a silent study, not clinical harm thresholds.

For every route/atom, require at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions, at least 10 outside the sentinel census, route-denominator ESS at least 50, normalized maximum weight at most 0.10, positive simultaneous lower route burden, clinical packet/nonresponse/control-infeasibility upper bound below 0.05, and workload upper bound below the approved atom-specific absolute budget. Failure of support is inconclusive/unsupported, not a pass.

### `AUTO` atom

An atom enters a conditional `AUTO` allow-list only if:

1. every inherited `AUTO` and holistic gate passes;
2. under all observed compatible labels and every correlated reassignment up to `kappa_max`, simultaneous `U95(Q_AUTO,g)<=0.05` and `U95(Q_NOACTION_AUTO,g)<=0.025`;
3. no sampled candidate-`AUTO` admission has a reviewer-confirmed explicit exact supersession, completion, cancellation/decline, or unresolved active-versus-no-action conflict;
4. `U95(route-reveal or route-swap actionability flip)<=0.02`, `U95(non-target/nuisance flip)<=0.02`, and `U95(decisive-control violation)<=0.05`;
5. every unit in every adverse-compatible state is `K_P`; one `K_R`, `K_N`, or `K_U` unit blocks the admission and atom's purity bound; and
6. the atom's shared-error breakdown point exceeds `kappa_max` under the simultaneous family.

Passing supports only silent observation of what the frozen route would have proposed. It does not authorize copying text or ordering imaging.

### `RECON` atom

An atom enters a conditional `RECON` allow-list only if:

1. every inherited `RECON` and holistic gate passes;
2. under all compatible labels and correlated reassignment up to `kappa_max`, simultaneous `L95(A_RECON,g)>=0.90` and `U95(Q_NOACTION_RECON,g)<=0.05`;
3. the reason for reconciliation is an active clinical choice, conflict, or incomplete supersession, not merely reviewer uncertainty, packet leakage, no-action, or missing evidence;
4. route/control and workload gates above pass; and
5. the breakdown point exceeds `kappa_max`.

If actionability could be either active or no-action, map to `DEFER`, not `RECON`; a clinician-review queue is not a harmless receptacle for reference uncertainty.

### `TIMING` atom

Preserve `TIMING` only as a silent chronology-study route when the inherited under-24-hour/unknown-opportunity definition passes. The actionability layer may describe clinical states but cannot convert insufficient opportunity into content failure. A `TIMING` atom cannot be described as clinically actionable unless its separate clinical compatible-set gate passes. Any attempt to infer actionability from later DS realization is prohibited.

### `DEFER` and `NOACTION`

`K_N`, `K_U`, unsupported/rare/residual/unknown/novel atoms, no externally defensible `kappa_max`, broken controls, or wide bounds map to prospective `DEFER`. `NOACTION` is a descriptive compatible state, not proof that no follow-up was medically needed. The silent mapper must retain `DEFER` for every signature not exactly on a passed allow-list.

The allow-list is exactly the set of atoms independently passing all inherited and new gates. Minimax simultaneous endpoints across the stated allow-list must also pass. Pooled performance, H1/H2/H3 unanimity, a senior opinion, neighboring atoms, macro-services, early/late similarity, sentinel-only results, consensus, imputation, standardization, shrinkage, or latent-class estimates cannot rescue a failing or unsupported atom.

## Baselines and falsification tests

Apply the identical sample, weights, labels, and uncertainty to these diagnostics, none of which may nominate a route:

1. inherited text-only route and H1/H2/H3 agreement;
2. clinical majority vote and senior favorable consensus;
3. one shared clinical codebook versus the separated decision tasks;
4. a model treating expert agreement as truth;
5. `kappa=0` versus the full sensitivity grid and unrestricted envelope;
6. route-revealed versus route-concealed clinical review;
7. pooled, macro-service, complete-case, unweighted, and hierarchical-shrinkage analyses; and
8. a latent-class diagnostic with conditional-independence assumptions explicitly displayed.

Mandatory executable fixtures include:

* **shared-wrong-ontology:** all text and clinical panels agree on `K_P`, but an explicit target-linked completion/replacement is present; agreement may pass while `AUTO` fails;
* **route-anchoring:** changing only omission/faithful/incomplete realization changes a clinical label; route-invariance gate fails;
* **clinical-decisive:** holding route realization fixed while changing explicit completion or decline changes actionability exactly as locked;
* **supersession-linkage:** an unrelated plan cannot supersede the pulmonary unit, an exact replacement can, and coexistence maps to conflict;
* **correlated-error:** all reviewers agree, but increasing `kappa` widens bounds monotonically and eventually crosses the gate; no latent-class result may narrow it;
* **high-weight unknown:** nonresponse and packet defects on high-weight cases worsen adverse endpoints;
* **minority-unit:** one `K_N/K_U` unit among several `K_P` units blocks `AUTO`; duplicating safe restatements changes neither route nor weight;
* **domain masking:** pooled clinical-actionability purity passes while one supported atom fails; only that atom and any unrestricted claim fail;
* **unsupported signature:** a rare, unknown, or novel atom maps to `DEFER` even if a neighboring atom passes;
* **zero event:** noncensus zero adverse events retain a positive upper bound;
* **seal/leakage:** route access before clinical seals, roster overlap, transformed-pair disclosure, post-label control creation, or altered append-only labels invalidates the affected run; and
* **claim checker:** rejects statements that expert agreement is truth or that retrospective passing establishes appropriateness, responsibility, actual communication, safety, benefit, follow-up completion, or causal effect.

The verifier can check source and schema hashes, row counts, joins, within-subject times, first-eligible selection, reciprocal chains, canonical DS, unit clocks, atoms, support pruning, fixed sample and probabilities, packet fields, redaction and access logs, roster disjointness, seals, transformation manifests, compatible-set mappings, survey estimators, partial-identification programs, monotonic widening in `kappa`, simultaneous gates, minimax allow-lists, workload, and whether the reported interpretation follows from computed outputs. It cannot establish clinical truth, appropriateness, real responsibility, utility thresholds, or benefit.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results

### Supportive

A supportive retrospective result requires all inherited gates, at least one support-qualified atom, all new clinical/actionability/control/workload gates, and minimax passage for every atom in the stated allow-list under the preregistered externally justified `kappa_max`. It supports only a prospective within-site **silent** evaluation of those exact observable signatures, with every other signature forced to `DEFER`. It supports the narrow claim that text route and route-concealed clinical decision measurements are distinguishable with prespecified robustness; it does not show that either is true.

### Adverse

An adverse result occurs when a supported atom has sufficiently precise evidence that candidate `AUTO` cases include active-choice, no-action, superseded, or unknown units above a gate; candidate `RECON` contains excessive no-action/superseded cases; route reveal/swap changes actionability; decisive clinical facts are ignored; workload exceeds budget; or the breakdown point is at or below `kappa_max`. The atom is excluded even if all text reviewers agree. Such a negative result is clinically useful because it blocks propagation or review workload in a distinguishably unsuitable workflow.

### Inconclusive

Inconclusive includes no feasible support atlas within 900 admissions, sparse route events, low ESS, wide simultaneous or partial-identification bounds, no externally defensible `kappa_max`, high residual/unknown burden, route-redaction failure, control infeasibility, nonresponse, broken seals, excessive review workload, or unavailable evidence needed to distinguish active from no-action. Inconclusive does not imply safety, stability, or transport. Under unrestricted shared clinical-reference error, the honest retrospective action is `DEFER`.

## Prospective evidence required for actual workflow, safety, utility, or benefit

MIMIC lacks the evidence needed to establish actual actionability. It does not provide a validated final artifact at physical departure; immutable draft/edit/sign/transmit history; report notification, inbox delivery, viewing, acknowledgement, or discussion; target-linked clinician responsibility; patient understanding or preference; outside-system plans; target-linked orders/referrals/scheduling; actual imaging completion; radiation/contrast burden; incidental cascades; diagnostic resolution or malignancy; anxiety; workload displacement; or causal outcomes. `storetime` is a storage proxy, service code is a workflow proxy, and observed author is not ownership.

Before any live exposure, conduct a separately approved prospective silent bridge on consecutive adult discharges. Freeze the retrospective extractor, exact allow-list, default-`DEFER` mapper, unit schema, and routes. Capture actual calendar dates and immutable event logs for radiology finalization/addenda, report delivery/viewing/acknowledgement, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, named accountable clinician/team, target-linked replacement rationale, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, and patient preference/competing-risk context. Blind prospective clinical adjudicators to silent routes and future outcomes. Require at least 100 route-positive admissions overall and 30 per claimed atom or stop accrual; repeat all-unit adverse actionability, contamination, capture, route-anchoring, and workload gates. Novel/changed signatures remain `DEFER`.

Only after a successful silent bridge should a governed comparative study expose an intervention. A route-stratified design could compare usual care with clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation for validated `AUTO` candidates and side-by-side reconciliation for validated `RECON` candidates. Prespecify intervention receipt, correction/dismissal, inappropriate insertion, alert burden, time to acknowledged responsible clinician, target-linked order/referral, scheduled and completed follow-up within an allowed window, duplicate/unnecessary imaging, downstream procedures, patient-reported understanding/anxiety, diagnostic resolution, and adverse events. Safety monitoring, equipoise, utility weights, sample size, and causal estimands require clinical governance and a new protocol. Retrospective expert agreement cannot substitute for these data.

## Substantive advance and residual uncertainty

The parent asks whether route-defining text can be reproduced without leakage, pooled masking, or obvious shared-codebook artifacts. This child asks the next decision-relevant question: whether a route-concealed clinical decision layer distinguishes portable active work from clinician-choice work and true no-action/supersession, while allowing all experts to share the same mistake. It converts agreement from an implicit validation target into one fallible measurement, adds direct falsification of route-anchored inference, and makes the unidentified shared-error budget visible through bounds and breakdown points.

The result may be deliberately restrictive. Without an external basis for bounding correlated clinical-reference error, no atom receives an unconditional clinical-validity claim and the operational prospective mapper remains `DEFER`. That is not failure of the experiment. It is the scientifically correct boundary between reproducible retrospective text and evidence adequate to test real workflow, safety, or benefit.
