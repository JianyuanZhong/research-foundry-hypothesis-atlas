# Joint patient-balanced availability–preference frontier for fixed-quota documentary-M2 review

## 1. Successor and clinical decision

This substantive child combines assessed-valid [prior hypothesis] (patient-anchored coverage) and assessed-valid [prior hypothesis] (preference frontier). It preserves the inherited frozen first eligible liver-resection population; 24-hour primary cutoff with locked 12/48/72-hour diagnostics; 90-day CT/MRI acquisition window; 365-day prior-treatment lookback; chronology possibility quarantine; whole-accession/version-faithful masks; documentary-M2 endpoint; six frozen reader books and exactly nine reader-pair worlds; locked patient-balanced B, R_clean, G_clean and same-fit Gclean_mask models; fixed K; strict >5-per-100-position margins; whole-patient singleton deletion; exact finite-state computation; and non-causal limits.

The single consequential remaining decision defect is the use of an arbitrary point contract. The patient-anchored parent requires 80% of each patient’s clean accessions to be effectively visible, while the preference parent maps thresholds under that point. Neither describes the complete boundary when both operational availability and stakeholder willingness to trade non-M2 reviews against documentary-M2 reviews are unknown.

The decision is whether a retrospective result justifies obtaining a same-snapshot report-version/access audit and then conducting a prospective silent-mode study of a scarce preoperative review queue. The retrospective action is allocating exactly K review positions to patients. A selected patient is referred for expert review of possible M2 risk. This is not a treatment, resection, transplant, surveillance, diagnosis, or patient-benefit rule.

The hypothesis is:

> In the frozen HCC first-resection frame, there is an exactly estimable, nonempty region of pairs (c,p) in which the semantic top-K queue beats each locked comparator by strictly more than five documentary-M2 captures per 100 positions and has positive net benefit over no review, simultaneously across 2020 and 2021, all admissible source/pathology/reader states, every allowed patient-balanced whole-accession mask, and every anchored whole-patient deletion. The result is reported as the full joint frontier, not as a claim at 80% visibility or 20% preference.

Here c is a contractual minimum effective-visibility fraction for every eligible patient who has at least one clean accession; p is an externally supplied preference threshold. A stakeholder using p treats one documentary-M2 review as offsetting p/(1-p) non-M2 reviews. Neither c nor p is observed or validated by HCC.

A claimed pair is falsified by one attained state with a comparator margin at or below 5, random-K dominance at or below zero, or h_G <= pK. A closed frontier can be clinically unattractive; that is an adverse result, not a reason to select a more favorable point.

## 5. One coherent joint frontier

For each state omega, year a, cutoff d and deletion projection j, define A_i(a,omega,d,j) as the source-clean, nonblank, whole CT/MRI accessions attached to patient i that satisfy the inherited acquisition interval. It is created after all state choices and exact joins. A patient with A_i empty remains in the eligible roster and receives unavailable report blocks; it is never dropped.

For rational c in [0,1], the patient-balanced availability family is:

E_P(c) = { binary e over whole units :
  sum over u in A_i of e_u >= ceil(c |A_i|)
  for every eligible i with |A_i|>0 }.

e_u=1 exposes the complete eventual unit exactly as stored. e_u=0 replaces all report-body-derived S, Q and X blocks by the development-defined unavailable block in R_clean and G_clean; the same surface/availability mask is applied to Gclean_mask. B and Z stay fixed. The same e must be used in every arm, reader pair, year, capacity and paired deletion comparison. An accession cannot be split.

This is an adversarial, patient-balanced counterfactual contract. At c=0 the adversary may mask every clean unit; at c=1 every clean unit must be exposed. The ceiling is applied separately per patient, so a one-accession patient requires that accession at every positive breakpoint. The inherited global accession family is retained only as a diagnostic:

E_A(c) = { e : sum over all u of e_u >= ceil(c |A|) }.

A global quota can be satisfied by repeat-heavy patients while another patient has no exposed unit; E_A therefore cannot substitute for E_P.

For p in [0,1), define the no-review net-benefit numerator:

NB_G(p; a,omega,e,j)
  = h_G(a,omega,e,j) - [p/(1-p)] [K-h_G(a,omega,e,j)].

Exactly, NB_G>0 if and only if h_G>pK. Equality fails strict support. This is a threshold utility for the documentary endpoint, not a calibrated risk or expected patient benefit.

For each availability breakpoint c, calculate three worst-state quantities over one shared state domain containing both test years, all four cutoffs, all admissible source/pathology/reader states, all e in E_P(c), the undeleted projection and every singleton deletion:

- D(c) = minimum over C in {R_clean, B, Gclean_mask} of (Delta_C - 5);
- R(c) = minimum of (N h_G - K E)/(K N);
- p*(c) = minimum of h_G/K.

The minima include the six undeleted and six singleton coordinates and retain their attained state witnesses. The exact joint support set is:

F_P = { (c,p) : c is an availability breakpoint, 0 <= p < 1,
                    D(c) > 0, R(c) > 0, and p < p*(c) }.

Thus the same patient roster, state, readers, e, K, scores and top-K queue determine comparator, random and no-review terms. No state can be favorable for one term and unfavorable terms evaluated elsewhere. Equality on any boundary is not support.

The availability breakpoint set C is the sorted union of 0, 1, every m/|A_i|, and every m/|A| for all m in the corresponding integer ranges, across all reachable worlds, years, cutoffs and deletion projections. Store exact reduced rationals. The preference crossing set is P={0,1} union {h/K for every attained h_G}. For p=a/b, verify b h_G > aK with integer arithmetic. There is no arbitrary 80% or 20% primary point.

Report the exact step frontier p*(c), D(c), R(c), attained witnesses and equivalent maximum non-M2 burden b*(c)=(1-p*(c))/p*(c), with +infinity when p*(c)=0. For an externally supplied minimum availability q, report the full slice {p:(q,p) is in F_P}. For an externally supplied preference p, report:

c*(p) = minimum c in C such that D(c)>0, R(c)>0 and p<p*(c),

or empty if no such cell exists. These inverse slices are the decision-relevant output.

A stakeholder interval [p_L,p_U] must be elicited and frozen independently of test outcomes. It is compatible at c only when D(c)>0, R(c)>0 and p_U<p*(c). It is preference-heterogeneous if it straddles p*(c), and range-adverse if p_L >= p*(c). Without a valid interval, action status is bridge_pending even when F_P is nonempty.

## 8. Disjoint conclusions and limits

Emit exactly one computational label in this order:

1. computationally_inconclusive: any source, header, chronology, roster, leakage, reader/pathology, fit, parser/hash, fixed-K, deletion, exact optimization, frontier or replay gate fails.
2. patient_balanced_joint_frontier_supportive: all inherited full-content and singleton comparator/random gates pass; the exact patient-balanced F_P is nonempty, closed and internally replayable.
3. patient_balanced_frontier_adverse: computation completes but F_P is empty or the patient-balanced domain is infeasible.
4. accession_global_only: the global accession result passes but the patient-balanced result is adverse.
5. semantic_evidence_only: semantic contrasts pass the required coordinates but at least one acquisition baseline Delta_B coordinate fails.
6. policy_only: only the policy/comparator contrast passes.
7. falsified_localized: computation completes with an attained localized witness and no preceding label applies.

Supportive means only that the locked model’s documentary-M2 top-K capture is robust over the reported finite patient-balanced availability region and that its conditional no-review boundary is exactly estimable. It does not mean that the region was historically achieved, that p is clinically reasonable, that K is actual capacity, that clinicians would act, or that patients benefit.

External preference interpretation is separate and one-hot: supportive requires outcome-blind preregistered [p_L,p_U] with p_U strictly below p*(c); adverse requires p_L at or above the frontier; heterogeneous means the interval straddles it; bridge_pending is mandatory without preference elicitation and the external archive audit. A computationally supportive frontier with bridge_pending is not a clinical action recommendation.

Automatic verification can check hashes, headers, joins, time roles, forbidden predictors, finite-state reachability, fixed models/K/ties, whole-unit masks, patient-specific ceilings, rational arithmetic, integer witnesses, deletion invariance, solver certificates, replay, labels and conclusion linkage. It cannot establish Chinese interpretation correctness, reader qualifications beyond records, operative completion, specimen linkage or pathology sampling, enterprise final/readable meaning, archive completeness without source-owner attestation, stakeholder preferences, capacity, workflow, fairness, transportability, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harm, cost or patient benefit. These require expert adjudication, source-owner review, a complete version archive, outcome-blind stakeholder elicitation, a prospective silent-mode workflow study, or a controlled outcomes study.
