## Scientific opening, supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

The selected parent establishes a sharper provenance question: a pre-360 respiratory observation block is compared with a strict recorded respiratoryCare start after outcome-blind generic process and state controls. Its non-respiratory nurseCharting endpoint is useful as a negative control, but its measurement validity remains unresolved. A row can be present because a nurse charting workflow was active, because the row has only a free-text value, or because a respiratory concept was not captured by the frozen token rule. Treating those possibilities as one endpoint could make a generic-workflow result look respiratory-specific or make a lexical artifact look like a clinical source contrast.

The strongest claim supported by the inspected evidence and the parent design is limited to this: the configured eICU snapshot contains ICU-relative, multi-center patient, respiratory, nursing, vital, treatment, care-plan, hospital, and laboratory tables with the headers recorded in the attached header audit; the rows are documented records, not validated treatment delivery or physiologic onset [K1]. Temporally structured EHR comparisons and reproducible structured preprocessing are feasible precedents [K2, K3]. No inspected work establishes the proposed endpoint semantics or result.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adult first-ICU stays that reach minute 360 and are in the parent’s respiratoryCare-negative B/C strata, the pre-360 respiratory opportunity residual (D^R) has a larger held-out association with a strict recorded respiratoryCare interval-start than with each measurement-controlled non-respiratory nursing endpoint, after the same generic workflow, measured state, hospital-regime, continuation-weight, and timing controls. The non-respiratory endpoint comparison is valid only if the association is reproducible for both the parent broad endpoint and a structured-label endpoint, while a value-ignored lexical stress test is treated as measurement sensitivity rather than a clean negative control.

The leading rival remains generic documentation/monitoring: (D^R) should predict any nursing workflow that occurs when charting intensity rises, including both structured-label and broad endpoints. A second rival is lexical misclassification: differences between broad and structured endpoints arise from value-only rows or respiratory terms occurring outside the frozen structured labels. A result that survives the structured comparison supports respiratory-specific recorded provenance only. It does not establish deterioration, treatment delivery, indication, physiologic onset, mechanism, causality, or clinical utility.

This is a substantive advance over the parent because it converts an implicit endpoint caveat into a predeclared measurement-validity experiment. The same source block, target window, competing exits, folds, weights, and models are used for endpoint variants; the endpoint eligibility disagreement matrix and label/value-only audit identify whether a negative-control result is workflow evidence or lexical/field-availability evidence.

## Sparse-endpoint, transport, and interpretation gates

Before any model fit, compute endpoint support from the parent B/C eligible cohort and freeze it. An endpoint is *analysable for a specificity claim* only if it has at least 200 endpoint-positive eligible stays and at least 200 eligible stays without that endpoint before administrative/ICU exit, positive and non-positive support in at least five hospitals with at least 20 of each in each such hospital, no single hospital supplies >50% of endpoint-positive stays, and all inherited H-cell, positivity, and ESS gates pass. Otherwise produce its audit and predictions only as descriptive/readiness outputs and label its specificity comparison inconclusive; do not change the endpoint, window, cohort, or gate. The same gate is applied separately to N_any, N_label_safe, and N_label_ignored_value. A pooled result from fewer than five viable held-out hospitals cannot support transport or specificity.

Supportive evidence would require: the three-way nurse eligibility audit showing N_label_safe is not dominated by value-only or token-disagreement rows; the primary (Delta_R-Delta_label) and (Delta_R-Delta_any) intervals both exclude zero in the respiratory-specific direction; no matching positive increment for N_label_safe; similar direction and uncertainty in elastic-net and temporal convolution; stable direction across at least two lead windows and held-out hospitals; no persistence under schedule/source-label/dynamic-order nulls; and no concentration in one hospital, EOL/process stratum, entry-lag/tie class, or weight-sensitive subset. This supports reproducible respiratory-specific recorded provenance conditional on measured controls.

Generic-workflow evidence would be similar (Delta_R,Delta_any,Delta_label) with overlapping paired uncertainty, attenuation after G or W6^G, or the same lead/lag pattern for all nurse endpoints. Lexical-misclassification evidence would be a material N_any versus N_label_safe discrepancy explained by value-only or token-only disagreement, or a change confined to N_label_ignored_value. These outcomes revise the interpretation toward workflow or measurement rather than respiratory physiology.

Adverse evidence includes equality across controls, disappearance after generic adjustment, persistence under schedule/source permutations, strong value-field sensitivity, post-landmark/backfill/tie concentration, W6^{G+R} collapse, low ESS/positivity, or one-hospital domination. It does not prove absence of a true clinical signal. Inconclusive evidence includes any sparse endpoint gate failure, fewer than five viable hospitals, unresolved endpoint semantics, broad paired intervals, unstable weights, or inability to separate structured labels from free-text values. An imprecise null is not refutation.

The claims that can be computed are header/source integrity, cohort flow, endpoint row eligibility and disagreement, ICU-relative H/D features, weights under stated assumptions, held-out recorded-endpoint associations, lead/lag contrasts, uncertainty, and falsification outputs. Essential unavailable or adjudication-dependent evidence remains treatment order/administration/delivery/indication/dose, validated device identity/state, FiO2/waveforms, bedside time, physiologic onset, narrative context, latent severity, reliable death time, and causal effects. Stronger clinical claims require expert review against orders/device logs/bedside timing and an independent cohort or prospective validation.
