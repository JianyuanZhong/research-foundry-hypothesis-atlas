# Episode 48: validated record-proxy semantics for the extubation-ordered policy

Parent: [prior hypothesis]

## Unresolved question, evidence boundary, and falsifiable hypothesis

After vasopressor-treated shock has resolved, should a markedly fluid-positive, invasively ventilated adult with adequate blood pressure, urine output, and potassium follow a policy of starting or restarting an ICU-input-system recorded furosemide or bumetanide delivery during the next ICU shift, while the patient remains invasively ventilated, versus avoiding such recorded delivery during that interval? The potential benefit is earlier durable liberation from an endotracheal airway; countervailing harms are early recorded creatinine rise, renal replacement therapy (RRT), or death.

The strongest available evidence supports narrower claims. The inspected full XML of Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13476641; DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120; frozen source [source checksum]) reports 26 randomized trials with 1,652 participants comparing diuretic strategies; most outcome evidence was low or very-low certainty and clinically heterogeneous, largely from heart-failure and post-cardiovascular-surgery populations. It does not establish the effect of initiating a loop after resolved shock. The inspected full XML of Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13154636; DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075; frozen source [source checksum]) states that optimal timing, volume, and duration of fluid removal remain uncertain and care is individualized. The inspected full XML of Hansford et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC12927355; DOI 10.1136/bmjmed-2024-001280; frozen source [source checksum]) supports aligning eligibility, strategy assignment, and follow-up and explicitly notes that target-trial emulation does not remove confounding. These sources support the importance and uncertainty of the question, not benefit, renal safety, a 12-hour biologic threshold, or causality. The previously inspected Chanda et al. full XML supports separating record-defined creatinine change from clinical AKI, not a loop effect. The ambition papers remain methodological demonstrations and are not clinical evidence for this question; no unavailable text is claimed as read.

The unresolved primary hypothesis is: in the frozen measured-overlap population, assignment-compatible follow-up under a policy of recorded loop initiation by 12 hours while still invasively ventilated increases the lower bound of the five-day, same-admission, 48-hour recorded airway-event-free extubation proxy (RHA-E48-D5) by more than 5 percentage points versus recorded avoidance through 12 hours. Separate adverse hypotheses are an increase of at least 3 points in all-eligible record-defined 48-hour creatinine rise or at least 5 points in observed RRT7. These are associations between record-defined dynamic policies and record-defined outcomes. They do not identify a pharmacologic effect among recipients.

## Falsification and result interpretation

Supportive evidence for the record proxy requires the one-sided 95% lower limit for the RHA-E48-D5 lower-bound contrast to exceed +0.05; ESS at least 75 per arm; at least 100 A12 satisfactions and 100 B12 compatible completions; no A12 treatment satisfaction after or tied with airway cessation; shared pre-initiation extubation reported and no more than 15% of eligible participants; and all inherited source, partition, state, overlap, balance, negative-control, ambiguity, contamination, renal-observation, and timing gates. The chart-augmented and continuous-ICU contrasts must have lower limits above 0, neither exact/12-hour linkage nor 6/24-hour timing may materially reverse direction, weighted W48 membership may differ by no more than five points between arms, and adding the chart-status failure stream may change neither arm risk nor the contrast by more than two points without forcing an inconclusive endpoint-validity branch.

If those gates pass, the only permitted respiratory conclusion is: “In the measured-overlap discovery cohort, assignment-compatible follow-up under a policy of ICU-input-system recorded loop initiation by 12 hours while invasive ventilation continued was associated with at least a five-point increase in the prespecified recorded airway-event-free extubation proxy versus recorded avoidance.” It must state that this is not evidence of clinically adjudicated durable extubation, that shared early extubations were not effects of receipt, and that causality is not established. Even a supportive result can motivate endpoint adjudication or a trial; it cannot by itself recommend loop initiation.

If RCR48 or RRT lower bounds cross +0.03 or +0.05 respectively, report a record-defined renal-harm signal and leave the clinical decision adverse or unresolved. If their lower bounds do not cross, say only that no prespecified recorded harm margin was detected; if upper bounds cross, renal safety and the combined decision remain unresolved.

Adverse evidence is RHA-E48-D5 upper limit <=0, all-eligible RCR48 lower limit >=+0.03, observed-RRT7 lower limit >=+0.05 under both date brackets, or inherited death/alive-discharge harm. Margin-falsified is airway upper limit <=+0.05. Inconclusive/infeasible includes inherited sample, positivity, overlap, balance, control, contamination, or weight failures; early common extubation >15%; any treatment-satisfied A12 airway event not strictly after loop onset; fewer than 100 A12 satisfactions; wide bounds; renal unknown >15% or >5-point arm imbalance; weighted W48 imbalance >5 points; chart augmentation changing an arm risk or contrast by >2 points; contradictory or unlinked X states >5% of final candidates; or material reversal under timing, linkage, continuous-ICU, ward-gap-as-unknown, chart-corroborated, procedure-only, store-lag, status-conflict, loop-item/version, amount/rate, creatinine-readout, or RRT-date sensitivities. Failure of the ward-gap-as-unknown lower bound to exceed +0.05 does not falsify the record-proxy hypothesis; it explicitly means clinically durable benefit remains unresolved. Null, adverse, and inconclusive outputs must never be narrated as benefit, safety, equivalence, or proof that the policy has no effect.
