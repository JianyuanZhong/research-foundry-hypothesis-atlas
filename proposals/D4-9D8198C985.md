## Neutral-only audit lane

When certification is unavailable, the only permitted lane is `ASSUMPTION_CONDITIONED_AUDIT`, with an immutable assumption manifest naming each assumption, its origin, scope, and failure action. It may read raw values solely to test:

- exact headers, source/schema/catalog hashes, row counts, duplicate/non-null `eid` keys, one-to-one join and overlapping-field equality;
- parser behavior and date arithmetic on raw fields without calling dates clinically valid;
- finite-value, sign, missing-code, and mask bookkeeping diagnostics;
- deterministic equation arithmetic on synthetic fixtures or unlabeled `raw_*`/`assumed_*` values;
- feature dimensions, fold construction, held-out provenance, leakage isolation, and algebraic identities;
- reproducibility of the state machine itself, including deliberately malformed and contradictory certificates.

It must not emit participant identifiers, row-level values, lab distributions, converted analytes, eGFR names or values, cohort/event/outcome counts, attendance or timing summaries, model fits, predictions, discrimination/calibration, capture, PPV, net benefit, bootstrap intervals, permutation tails, biological verdicts, or supportive/adverse conclusions. In particular, a neutral audit may report a count of parser failures or a hash of a private diagnostic object, but may not report how many participants qualify for the scientific cohort or outcome. All derived names must be prefixed `raw_`, `assumed_`, or `qa_`; an audit manifest cannot change the estimand or equation and cannot upgrade a status. Missing, contradictory, or violated assumptions yield `AUDIT_FAIL`, not a partial biological result. No manifest yields a hard stop rather than an implicit default.

A separate certified lane may emit the scientific analysis only after the certificate gate. This separation is enforced in code by requiring a signed/hashed state token as an input to every biological-output function; the token is issued only by the certificate validator and is never issued by the audit lane. Thus a successful parser or a high-performing assumption-conditioned model cannot bypass certification.

## Falsification and limits of inference

The design is falsified as an incremental prioritization claim if M1 fails to outperform M0 under the prespecified practical and uncertainty gates, if gains disappear under the Z predictor-null or reversed labels, if the elapsed-time sentinel reproduces the gain, if calibration harm is material, or if apparent superiority is an artifact of leakage, fold reconstruction, quota arithmetic, or undocumented field transformations. A negative certified result is scientifically useful; a technical stop is not evidence for or against the biology.

Computation can establish source hashes, exact schemas and headers, join integrity, certificate validity/deficiency behavior, state transitions, equation arithmetic, dimensions, baseline-only provenance, folds, quota and timing identities, uncertainty calculations, null completion, and gate consistency. It cannot establish assay validity beyond independently supplied certification evidence, measured-GFR accuracy, persistent CKD/chronicity, true first clinical testing, outcomes for non-attenders, causal benefit or harm, threshold appropriateness, cost-effectiveness, patient preference, treatment impact, or transportability. Those stronger claims require clinical/laboratory adjudication and a separate study with appropriate measurements and outcomes. Raw rows, eids, predictions, folds, weights, and resampling arrays remain private; only aggregate diagnostics and the certificate/stop deficiencies are publishable.
