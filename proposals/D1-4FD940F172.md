# Assay content and a decision-relevant recorded-care commitment after an HCC-coded admission

## Decision, hypothesis and substantive advance

This is a substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that candidate's strongest estimand: the incremental value of strictly pre-admission assay content over admission/current-lab context plus a capture-intensity-only comparator, evaluated on the complete all-admission seven-state recorded transition. It repairs the remaining clinical limitation by testing whether the transition has a more decision-relevant, two-source recorded signature—an auditable non-drug order preceding a procedure—while retaining the semantics-independent endpoint as the compulsory fallback.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among all eligible adult first-observed HCC-coded inpatient admissions, prior assay identity, result/value and dated trajectory improve calibrated prediction of the complete recorded procedure/discharge transition beyond admission context, first-12-hour current labs and pre-admission capture intensity; the increment is also present for a prespecified order–procedure concordance transition when that transition is sufficiently observed and temporally resolvable.

The primary target is still the local recorded-care distribution, not active HCC, physiology, treatment intent or benefit. The concordance result is a stronger documentation signal, not a validated clinical-decision label.

The clinical advance is a falsifiable separation of three propositions that the parent could not fully distinguish:

1. prior assay content predicts any locally recorded transition;
2. that increment survives adjustment for prior observation/capture intensity;
3. that increment is visible for a temporally ordered, two-source record of procedural commitment rather than only a procedure row.

A failure of (3) does not erase a valid (1)/(2) result; it limits the claim to procedural recording. A failure of family concordance does not discard the primary endpoint: the exact generic endpoint is the prespecified computable fallback.

## Required scientific deliverable

The solver must newly produce, before locked evaluation:

- a frozen cohort, patient split, all-row key/time audit and feature manifest;
- the unchanged seven-state primary labels `P0,D0,U0,P1,D1,U1,N72` for every eligible admission;
- a deterministic concordance audit and, if its gate passes, secondary mutually exclusive labels `C0,P0only,O0only,D0,U0,R24), followed by `C1,P1only,O1only,D1,U1,N72`; these secondary labels never replace the primary seven-state labels;
- locked predictions for the matched transparent models A, L, Q, E and H and the irregular learned models S and `S_Q), for both endpoints when the secondary endpoint is eligible;
- the primary paired held-out E-minus-Q log-loss and Brier contrasts for the complete seven-state vector, plus the same contrast for the concordance endpoint only when its predeclared gate passes;
- calibration, state probabilities, source/family coverage, timestamp resolution, missing-history diagnostics and at least 1,000 patient-cluster bootstrap intervals;
- all falsification outputs, the coverage/temporal-coherence gate result, and a claim-to-evidence table distinguishing computation from clinical adjudication.

Completion is the frozen audit, fitted models, locked predictions, uncertainty, falsification and interpretation. It is not a positive finding.

## Evidence-supported versus untested claims

Verified source evidence supports native encounter admission/discharge times; native lab, procedure, order and medication time/payload columns; and joins by (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). The procedures source has been directly scanned: 338,040 rows, 7,781 distinct `手术` strings, 179 blank names and two `手术来源` values. These are availability facts, not outcome prevalence or model results. Parent provisional counts remain provisional and must be recomputed.

The strongest supported statement is that this snapshot can describe local recorded assays, event capture and later recorded procedure/discharge transitions. Diagnoses have no diagnosis time. No available field establishes active HCC, stage, burden, resectability, indication, scheduling, consent, intent, receipt, completion, response, toxicity, mortality, outside-care completeness or benefit.

The untested claim is the E-versus-Q predictive increment and, conditionally, whether it is present for the concordant two-source recorded transition. No solver result is being claimed.

## Population and exact temporal design

Use HCC snapshot `[source checksum]`, catalog [source checksum]. All listed HCC sources are ordinary read-only files; no archive member is used.

Normalize Unicode/whitespace in keys and diagnosis text. Join `diagnoses` to `encounters` exactly on (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). HCC-coded means normalized `肝细胞癌` or case-insensitive substring `hepatocellular carcinoma` in `诊断名称`; print every matched string, `诊断类型` overlap, duplicate keys and unmatched keys. This is record coding, not active-disease adjudication.

Require age >=18, parseable native `入院时间` and `出院时间`, nonnegative stay, and encounter date in [2011-01-01, 2026-01-01). Retain the earliest eligible HCC-coded encounter per `患者主索引`, ordered by native `入院时间` then `就诊号`; do not replace an invalid earliest record with a later one. Let `t0 = 入院时间`. Keep early discharges, early procedures and unresolved-time admissions.

Use these half-open windows exactly:

- prior history: [`t0 - 730 days`, `t0`);
- current labs: [`t0`, `t0 + 12 hours`);
- early outcome: [`t0`, `t0 + 24 hours`);
- later outcome: [`t0 + 24 hours`, `t0 + 72 hours`).

A history row must have a valid native event time strictly less than `t0`, even when linked to the anchor encounter. Prefer rows linked to encounters with native `入院时间 < t0`; audit anchor-linked pre-anchor rows. Count left truncation, invalid times, exact boundary/tie rows, duplicates/multiplicity, unmatched keys, after-discharge rows and patients with no prior record. Invalid time is unresolved/excluded from timed features, never absence.

### Primary endpoint preserved exactly

Using only procedure presence and native `procedures.开始时间`, label every admission:

- `P0`: valid procedure in [0,24h) and before discharge;
- `D0`: discharge at or before 24h before any valid early procedure;
- `U0`: missing/invalid procedure timing whose possible position could change procedure/discharge or boundary ordering;
- `R24`: none of those established;
- among `R24`, `P1`, `D1`, `U1), or `N72) are assigned analogously in [24h,72h).

Procedure wins exact ties as in the parent, but report discharge/procedure-tie sensitivity. Report the complete seven-state mean terminal probability vector over all admissions; R24-conditional results are secondary. Missing procedure timing never becomes no procedure. Audit midnight starts, before-admission procedures, after-discharge rows, multiple procedures, exact boundaries and optimistic/conservative unresolved rules.

The primary E-Q estimand remains the paired change in held-out proper scores for this all-admission vector. It remains the principal conclusion even when the concordance layer is gated off.

### Decision-relevance/concordance endpoint and empirical gate

Use only a frozen deterministic lexical map over `procedures.手术`:

- embolization/TACE: `TACE`, `栓塞`, `化疗栓塞`;
- resection/transplant: `切除`, `移植`;
- ablation: `消融`, `射频`, `微波`, `无水酒精`;
- systemic/infusion: `化疗`, `靶向`, `免疫治疗`, `灌注`;
- diagnostic/access: `造影`, `穿刺`, `活检`, `引流`, `置管`;
- otherwise `other`; preserve every multi-match, blank and ambiguous string.

For each procedure row eligible for P0/P1, search `orders` joined by the same (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). Define an order as temporally preceding only when its valid `开立时间` is strictly less than the procedure `开始时间`; if `开立时间` is invalid, use valid `开始时间` only as a separately reported lower-confidence timing tier, never silently pool it with the primary tier. An exact tie, order after procedure, missing times or conflicting order start/end is not concordant. A candidate order must be a non-drug order with a nonblank `医嘱(非药品)`. Its family match is deterministic: apply the same frozen token sets listed above to the exact normalized `医嘱(非药品)` string (substring matching, multi-labels retained); do not infer synonyms or use a language model. No outcome row is used as a predictor. The generic any-order fallback is the prespecified option if this exact shared-token map is not supportable.

The family-specific concordance gate is frozen before seeing locked labels:

- at least 100 primary-window procedure admissions with a valid uniquely assigned family and a valid pre-procedure order time;
- at least 80% of timed procedure admissions have a nonblank procedure string and exactly one family label (all remaining rows are explicitly counted as ambiguous/unmatched);
- at least 70% of candidate order rows have a resolvable `开立时间`, and every fallback to `开始时间` is reported;
- no family accounts for >90% of concordant events, and no more than 25% of concordant candidates depend on exact-midnight ties or unresolved timing.

If these criteria fail, declare family concordance inconclusive before model comparison and run only the generic order-before-procedure fallback if it has at least 100 temporally resolvable events; otherwise retain only the primary seven-state endpoint. These thresholds determine feasibility, not significance and not a post hoc favorable subset.

For the eligible secondary analysis, assign the first event with this precedence within each early/later window:

- `C0/C1): a qualifying same-family order strictly precedes a same-family procedure;
- `P0only/P1only`: a qualifying procedure without a qualifying preceding order;
- `O0only/O1only`: a qualifying non-drug order without a qualifying procedure;
- `D0/D1`: discharge before any qualifying event;
- `U0/U1`: missing/ambiguous timing could change the order;
- `R24/N72`: no established event.

For the generic fallback, replace “same-family” with any non-drug order and any procedure, preserving procedure-only and order-only states. Procedures with no family remain procedure-only/other and are not forced into a family. Exact ties and unresolved events remain U. The primary and secondary state labels are emitted side by side so a failed lexical layer cannot contaminate the parent estimand.

This endpoint is a recorded order–procedure sequence, not a clinician decision, indication, intended treatment, administration, completion or benefit. Clinical adjudication would require chart review of order meaning, scheduling and intent.

## Information sets

All models use the identical cohort, endpoint, split, fit-only preprocessing and scoring rules.

- **A**: age, sex, admitting department `就诊科室`, calendar era, admission time-of-day and admission missingness.
- **L**: A plus current labs in [0,12h), using `labs.检验`, `定性结果`, `定量结果`, `标本类型`, `检验时间`; per assay first/last valid numeric value, change, elapsed time, count, density/missingness and slope only with two distinct times; qualitative results remain assay-specific.
- **Q**: L plus prior 730-day capture/process intensity only: prior encounter counts/durations and days active; days since last event/encounter; source-specific row and distinct-encounter counts for labs/procedures/orders/medications; observed source-type count; missing-time counts; left-truncation and no-history flags. Q contains no assay identity/value/result and no procedure/order/medication names, dose, route, frequency or status.
- **E**: Q plus prior lab content and dated trajectory from the exact lab fields above: assay identity, numeric/qualitative result, fit-only within-assay scaling, change/slope where supported, recency, span, count and missingness. Units are absent; never pool numeric values across assays or create a physiologic score.
- **H**: the incumbent transparent all-history summary, rerun exactly: Q-like encounter/source features, assay-specific prior-lab summaries, prior procedure occurrence/recency/gaps, and order/medication process counts/recency/gaps. Names are not interpreted in H.
- **S**: a two-coordinate continuous-time latent state model over the same prior rows as E/H. Each event has source/type, elapsed time, payload mask and, only for labs, assay-specific numeric/qualitative content. Generic procedures/orders/medications are process/type tokens; no treatment interpretation. Latent coordinates evolve with elapsed-time decay, assay emissions are assay-specific, and a supervised early/later competing-state head predicts either endpoint.
- **S_Q**: the same S with assay values/results removed, retaining event source/type/time and capture information.

Fit A/L/Q/E/H with identical regularized sequential multinomial-logistic heads for early and R24-conditional later distributions, then marginalize to the complete target. Fit S/S_Q with supervised terminal-state negative log likelihood; dimensions, decay, masking/reconstruction term and shrinkage are fixed before locked evaluation and tuned only on selection data. S is informative only if it adds calibrated held-out information beyond E/H and S_Q does not reproduce the gain.

The primary contrast is E minus Q. Secondary contrasts are Q minus L, E minus L, H minus L, S minus E and S_Q minus Q. For the concordance endpoint, report E-Q only after the gate; do not reinterpret a change in event definition as a model improvement.

## Split, analysis and uncertainty

Assign patients using SHA-256(normalized `患者主索引`) modulo 100: 0-59 fit, 60-69 selection/preprocessing/calibration, 70-79 locked evaluation, 80-99 inaccessible. All rows for a patient follow one bucket. Freeze dictionaries, gate counts, windows, label precedence, features, dimensions, hyperparameters and calibration before using locked labels.

Primary score is paired held-out multiclass log loss and multiclass Brier for the complete seven-state vector on all eligible admissions. Report state-specific calibration intercept/slope, reliability, observed-versus-predicted probabilities and probability contrasts. Use patient-clustered paired bootstrap with >=1,000 resamples, preserving all admissions from each patient, for 95% intervals. Report no-history/1-2/>=3 prior-encounter and fit-derived capture-intensity strata; do not call them causal subgroups or external validation.

Supportive evidence requires the primary E-Q interval to be below zero for log loss and/or Brier without material calibration deterioration, Q not reproducing the parent H/L gain, attenuation after assay-value/history permutation, stability across capture strata and 30/180/730-day windows, and no forbidden-window or split artifact. For the secondary endpoint additionally require the concordance gate, adequate state support, a primary-direction E-Q improvement, and no material reversal under generic fallback or timestamp-tier sensitivity. S is supportive only when calibrated, seed-stable, beyond E/H, and not reproduced by S_Q.

Adverse evidence is no primary E-Q improvement, worsening calibration, Q reproducing E/H, persistence after content/history permutations, forbidden-window or patient-split leakage, or a failed gate with no adequately supported fallback. Concordance-specific adverse evidence is disappearance under order-time scrambling, dominance by procedure-only/order-only states, or instability between `开立时间` and `开始时间` tiers.

Inconclusive evidence includes dominant U states, wide cluster intervals, sparse assay channels, severe left truncation, unresolved/coarse timestamps, insufficient concordance coverage, ambiguous lexical strings, source-specific reversal, or a secondary result present only in a low-confidence timing tier. Inconclusive does not confirm the hypothesis.

## Alternatives, selection and compute

The transparent model is scientifically necessary because it makes the E-Q estimand auditable: exact assay channels and dated summaries are visible, and Q's process-only contribution can be inspected. The learned alternative is useful for a different reason: irregular event spacing, order-sensitive assay trajectories, nonlinear interactions and posterior uncertainty are discarded by first/last/count summaries. S_Q tests whether those advantages are merely capture-process structure.

A large transformer is deferred: no evidence yet requires long-context representation, and its marginal predictive gain would not resolve the clinical meaning problem. Raw clinical-document/pathology/examination text is deferred because documents/pathology have no native time and narrative extraction is explicitly unvalidated; adding them would destroy the pre-admission temporal interpretation. Causal treatment-effect and bedside-utility analyses are deferred because indication, intent, receipt, completion, counterfactual treatment and outcomes are unavailable. Family concordance is selected as a secondary, gated adaptation because orders and native order/procedure times are present; the generic any-order fallback is retained if the family dictionary is not supportable.

Future solver planning, not discovery execution: chunked scans of the 2.2 GB labs and 2.19 GB orders plus smaller sources; compact patient-level matrices; 8-16 CPUs and 64-192 GiB RAM, expected but unverified 1-4 hours for aggregation/transparent fits and 1-4 more hours for bootstrap. S is CPU-first; if an allocated A100 is materially useful, request one via `ehr_job_submit` using `ehr-campaign-gpu:20260908`, put model/tensors on `cuda:0`, and measure that separately. The separate solver planning envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, 8 GPUs and 28,800 seconds; discovery's 7,200-second science budget is not silently used as the study limit. No solver fit has been run and these are not measured results.

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema/key/time audits, cohort and endpoint counts, concordance coverage/gate status, locked predictions, scores, calibration, bootstrap intervals, ablations and permutations. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for active HCC, diagnosis timing, stage, burden, resectability, order meaning, intent, indication, scheduling, receipt, completion, appropriateness, response, toxicity, mortality, causal effects, bedside utility and transportability. The procedure-family map is lexical and cannot establish treatment identity or benefit.

The actual deliverable is therefore a newly fitted, locked comparison of E versus Q on the unchanged complete all-admission endpoint, with a predeclared attempt to show whether the result transfers to a resolvable order–procedure record. The explicit revisit rule is to revisit richer sequence models or clinical-text adjudication only if S adds stable information beyond E/H or if expert chart review establishes time-valid order semantics; neither is assumed.
