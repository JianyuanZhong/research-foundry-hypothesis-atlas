## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the executed evidence is that the inherited airway-lookback classes have sharply different contemporaneous structured documentation states under a prespecified value-aware proxy, and that these states change substantially when the same six-hour algorithm is shifted after the landmark. The long-lookback classes contain many rows with a carried invasive-airway value but no qualifying contemporaneous positive support. This demonstrates sensitivity of denominator interpretation to documentation recency and proxy construction.

The unresolved clinical claim is whether those unsupported or ambiguous rows were actually not invasively ventilated, whether the structured documentation is incomplete, or whether source semantics create apparent discordance. The available tables cannot resolve that claim without expert episode adjudication.

The operational hypothesis tested here was: compared with `retained_24h_only` and `retained_6h`, `unbounded_only` rows would have a lower present positive-proxy fraction and higher ambiguous/unsupported-carried fractions, with differences persisting across landmarks and hospitals; a future window would not simply reproduce the present ordering if the present signal genuinely reflected landmark-timed documentation rather than ubiquitous chart presence. Falsification or qualification occurs if group differences disappear with clustered uncertainty, reverse across landmarks/hospitals, are dominated by one component, or are reproduced indiscriminately in the future window.

## Executable value-aware state machine

The primary state is one of positive, negative, ambiguous, or conflicting.

A decisive positive event is the latest of:

1. An exact value-aware chart signal in the window: `RT Vent On/Off` equal to `start` or `continued`; an observed invasive-compatible value for `Mechanical Ventilator Mode` or `Ventilator Support Mode`; or `Ventilator Population=1`.
2. `notetype=Intubation`.
3. A recent invasive-airway event plus at least two distinct plausible ventilator-setting families.
4. At least two distinct plausible setting families plus contemporaneous treatment containing `mechanical ventilation` but not `non-invasive ventilation`.

Plausible setting families are bounded observed values for set respiratory rate, set tidal volume, machine minute ventilation, machine tidal volume, and machine pressure. Isolated setting-label presence is insufficient. An old carried invasive-airway value cannot independently create a positive primary state.

A decisive negative event is the latest exact `RT Vent On/Off=off`, trach-mask/T-piece mode, `No Artificial Airway`/`none`, or `notetype=Extubation` in the same window. If positive and negative events occur within 30 minutes, state is conflicting. If they are more than 30 minutes apart, the later event wins. If neither exists, state is ambiguous. `RT Suspended`, CPAP, BiPAP, NIV, pressure-support, and spontaneous values are retained as alternative-modality components and are not forced into either primary state.

`ventstartoffset`/`ventendoffset` activity is reported only as sensitivity because row-time, null, and zero end semantics have not been clinically validated. `priorventstartoffset` and `priorventendoffset` are not treated as current ventilation.

Component discordance is retained explicitly as: any positive and negative decisive events; carried invasive airway without positive support; carried invasive airway with a negative event; carried no-airway with a positive event; alternative modality with a positive event; and interval-only sensitivity.

## Interpretation, adverse results, and falsification

Supportive operational result: `retained_6h` has consistently more positive contemporaneous proxy support and much less unsupported carry-forward than the longer-lookback classes across landmarks and common hospitals.

Adverse or qualifying result: the `unbounded_only` versus `retained_24h_only` difference crosses zero under hospital-cluster resampling and is small under common-hospital equal weighting. The large 6-hour contrast is partially structural because recent airway is both a group-defining variable and a positive-rule component. The future shifts are large and group-specific rather than a neutral repetition of present state. Consequently, the evidence supports measurement fragility and recency-dependent documentation coherence, but it does not establish that unbounded-only patients were extubated, that 6-hour patients were truly ventilated, or that one lookback is clinically correct.

The inherited stale-carry-forward hypothesis would be strengthened only if clinical adjudication showed that ambiguous/unsupported long-lookback rows were no longer invasively ventilated and that positive 6-hour rows represented active invasive ventilation. It would be weakened if adjudication showed systematic missing structured documentation during true ventilation or misinterpreted airway/setting semantics. A stratified adjudication sample must include each group, present/future transition type, hospital documentation pattern, landmark, and discordance class.

## Exact data required for stronger conclusions

A stronger ventilation-validity claim requires clinician-adjudicated invasive ventilation episodes with tube placement/removal, ventilator connection/disconnection, NIV distinction, and continuity over each six-hour window; validation of `airwaytype`, `ventstartoffset`, and `ventendoffset` semantics; and review of respiratory flowsheets and source-system copying/late-entry behavior. Actual SBT delivery requires an independently validated SBT record or adjudicated respiratory/clinical notes. Care quality, causal effects, and clinical benefit require a separate outcome study with confounding control and clinically meaningful outcomes.

This automatic computation can verify cohort identity, windows, mappings, event ordering, counts, transitions, parser accounting, and uncertainty calculations. It cannot verify bedside truth, source semantics, clinician intent, SBT delivery, quality, causality, benefit, or transportability beyond observed hospitals.
