# POD2 coupled recovery and context-supported invasive fluid intervention after first HCC resection

Status: substantive endpoint-validation child of [prior hypothesis]. A full-census context audit has been executed; no outcome model has been fitted and no predictive result is claimed.

## Clinical opening, supported claim, and falsifiable hypothesis

At postoperative hour 48 after a first recorded liver resection for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), clinicians must decide who merits intensified respiratory/fluid review and imaging rather than routine recovery. Intervention-requiring pleural or abdominal fluid collections are consequential after hepatectomy: a recent primary-liver-cancer study included thoracentesis and abdominal drainage in an adjudicated 30-day complication outcome [K1], a hepatectomy study defined clinically relevant pleural effusion by thoracentesis or thoracic drainage [K2], and treatment-based complication grading treats invasive intervention as grade III [K3]. None validates this extract's codes as unplanned complications.

**Strongest claim supported by the executed audit.** In the 2018–2025 modeling-era risk set, 326 patients had the parent's candidate procedure endpoint. Of these, 300 (92.0%) also had independent postoperative context: a timestamped non-negated fluid finding between T0 and event+12 hours, a procedure-specific non-drug order from event−48 to event+12 hours, or explicit postoperative drainage context in the encounter's treatment-course document. Seven were generic/explicit biliary transhepatic drainage, three had pre-existing-fluid evidence without new intervention context, and 16 had procedure evidence only. This supports a **context-supported recorded postoperative invasive fluid intervention** phenotype. It does not establish that interventions were unplanned, caused by the hepatectomy, or Clavien–Dindo complications.

**Unresolved hypothesis to test.** Among adults still hospitalized and free of recorded pleural/abdominal fluid intervention at 48 hours after their first recorded HCC liver resection, ordered coupled 0–48-hour recovery carries prognostic information for a first **context-supported pleural or abdominal invasive fluid intervention** in (48,336] hours beyond the latest POD2 state, operation burden, treatment indicators, and observation intensity.

This is a prognostic temporal-information hypothesis. It is not a causal claim about albumin, diuretics, inflammation, fluid management, or a biological mechanism.

**Clinical importance and advance.** A supported result would justify prospective clinician adjudication of a POD2 monitoring phenotype for targeted respiratory/fluid assessment. An adverse result would favor a simpler POD2 snapshot. An inconclusive result would identify endpoint adjudication, observation practice, or era drift—not model capacity—as the next bottleneck. The advance over [K1–K3] is a temporally locked, context-corroborated EHR outcome and a direct test of ordering/coupling against current state; it is not another static nomogram or a claim that local records reproduce adjudicated complications.

Observed evidence among the 326 candidates:

- 221 had a procedure-specific near-event order; 39 had explicit postoperative intervention in the treatment-course document.
- 18 had preoperative imaging fluid evidence and 23 had admission/history fluid evidence.
- Two index-operation documents mentioned planned drain placement and 50 candidates had early drain-care orders. These signals usually co-occurred with later intervention evidence, so automation could not reliably decide prophylactic versus new rescue.
- 244 candidates had multiple matching procedure rows within 24 hours; 233 had both procedure sources and there were 639 candidate rows around 326 first events. Patient-level first-event collapse prevents event-count inflation; cross-source records are corroboration, not separate outcomes.
- The narrowed primary has 300 events: pleural 231, abdominal 66, and combined-name 3. The 26 nonprimary candidates are retained for sensitivity and adjudication, not deleted.

The audit is descriptive validation of record context, not clinical adjudication. Examinations have a usable `开始时间`; orders have `开立时间`, `开始时间`, and `结束时间`. Clinical documents have no timestamp, so admission/history fields can support pre-existing disease and treatment-course fields can support encounter context, but row order cannot establish chronology. Lexical negation and temporality are incomplete, clinical intent is absent, and no automated verifier may certify “unplanned complication.”

## Frozen population, temporal boundaries, and revised outcome

2. For each patient choose the chronologically first encounter containing a procedure name with `肝` and `切除`, excluding `移植|供体|供肝|活体供`. Require that chosen encounter to have a `手麻系统` qualifying resection and define T0 as its earliest qualifying `开始时间`.
3. Require T0 within one day of admission/discharge and recorded discharge after T0+48 hours.
4. Exclude a primary-matching fluid procedure in [T0,T0+48h].
5. Restrict primary modeling to 2018–2025.

**Candidate procedure:** first `开始时间` in (T0+48h,T0+336h], no later than discharge+2h, matching:

- pleural: `胸腔.*(穿刺|抽液|置管|闭式)`, excluding `活检`;
- abdominal/operative-bed: `腹腔.*(穿刺|置管).*引流|腹腔穿刺引流|腹腔置管引流|手术区积液.*引流|膈下积液.*引流|经皮.*肝.*引流|肝脓肿.*引流`.

Collapse all matching rows/sources to the patient-level first timestamp.

**Narrowed primary outcome:** candidate procedure plus at least one independent context criterion:

1. timestamped examination between T0 and event+12h with a non-negated mention of `胸腔积液|胸水|腹水|腹腔积液|腹盆腔积液|肝周积液|手术区积液|膈下积液|肝脓肿`; or

## Rival explanations and expected observations

1. **Ordered-recovery signal.** A coupled early state linking stalled oncotic/synthetic recovery, inflammatory activation, and renal/fluid-handling stress precedes later intervention. T1 should improve proper scores beyond latest state, and destroying 0–24-hour value order should remove much of the gain.
2. **Current-severity/treatment-indication rival.** Latest POD2 values and early albumin, loop-diuretic, antibiotic, vasopressor, or hemostatic orders contain all usable information. B0 or a nonlinear latest-state control should match T1. Medication associations are clinician concern, not treatment effects.
3. **Observation/documentation rival.** Imaging and order intensity determine whether a procedure receives corroborating context; calendar/source changes drive apparent prediction. A timing-only model should match T1, gains should vary across examination-free/order-free endpoints, or era/source calibration should fail.
4. **Planned/chronic-care rival.** Some procedures manage prophylactic drains or pre-existing effusion/ascites rather than a new postoperative complication. Available text cannot resolve this reliably. Clinician adjudication should show lower PPV or disagreement concentrated among early-drain-care/pre-existing-fluid strata.

Association surviving adjustment cannot distinguish these explanations mechanistically.

## Baseline and substantive learned alternative

**B0 transparent POD2 baseline.** Elastic-net logistic regression with restricted cubic splines for age, operation duration, and each latest 24–48-hour lab value; missingness, time since latest value, 0–48-hour measurement count, operation year/flags, and whether/when each medication group was ordered. Prespecified interactions: albumin×diuretic, albumin×creatinine, INR×bilirubin, and neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio×procalcitonin. Select penalty on 2022 Brier score, refit 2018–2022, recalibrate intercept/slope only in 2023.

**T1 coupled irregular-time state model.** Fit a masked sparse dynamic factor/state-space model to eight 6-hour bins over [0,48h], using last exact-assay value, missingness, time-since-observation, and medication starts. Prespecify oncotic/synthetic, inflammatory/injury, and renal/fluid-handling domains with sparse cross-loadings; choose two to five factors by 2022 masked likelihood plus downstream Brier score. Fit a penalized outcome head on POD2 state, state change, innovation magnitude, and common predictors. Report loadings/transitions and five-seed stability.

T1 can reveal whether coupled transition information discarded by B0 is prognostically useful; it cannot establish physiology. A gradient-boosted latest-state control tests nonlinearity. A supervised transformer/GRU-D remains deferred because only 106 primary events are available through tuning, making seed instability and overfitting more consequential than representational capacity. Revisit only with stable order-dependent signal or more adjudicated events.

## Falsification, evaluation, and interpretation

Primary estimand: paired locked-test Brier difference T1−B0. Also report log loss, AUROC, AUPRC, calibration intercept/slope, decision curves at 1%, 2%, 3%, 5%, 2,000 paired patient bootstraps, and ≥200 end-to-end bootstrap refits. Freeze all lexicons/transforms before opening 2024–2025 outcomes.

Required controls: 100 within-patient order ablations preserving latest state/timestamps; timing/missingness-only model; gradient-boosted latest-state control; year/source analyses; S1–S6 outcome sensitivities; model with/without medications; complete-value and values-only analyses; label permutation within year×source; and +28-day within-encounter timestamp shift where possible.

**Supportive** requires T1−B0 Brier ≤−0.0010 with paired 95% CI below zero; AUPRC gain ≥0.02 with CI above zero; recalibrated T1 slope 0.8–1.2 and intercept −0.20–0.20; ≥50% gain loss under order ablation; timing-only and nonlinear-latest-state controls do not explain the gain; direction agrees for S1/S2 and examination-free/order-free definitions without catastrophic era/source instability. This supports prognostic temporal information for a recorded context-supported intervention, not an unplanned complication or mechanism.

**Adverse** requires adequate event/source support but failure of the proper-score/effect-size gates with intervals excluding meaningful benefit, or preservation of gain after order destruction. Prefer B0 and abandon the ordering claim for this endpoint.

**Inconclusive** includes wide intervals, failed calibration, seed instability, source/era reversal, gain limited to observation intensity, endpoint-definition reversal, or poor clinician adjudication. Revise measurement or obtain new data; do not call an imprecise null refutation.

## Clinical adjudication gate

Before full model fitting, two hepatobiliary clinicians independently review a deidentified stratified set: 100 narrowed-primary events across era/component/source and baseline-fluid/early-drain-care strata, all 26 excluded/unresolved candidates, and 24 candidate-negative encounters enriched for postoperative fluid imaging/orders to probe obvious lexical misses. They label: unplanned post-hepatectomy complication treatment; planned/prophylactic drain care; chronic pre-existing fluid management; duplicate-only record; unrelated procedure; or insufficient evidence. Adjudicators are blinded to 0–48-hour predictors and future model scores.

Proceed with the narrowed endpoint only if strict-positive observed PPV is ≥0.80, its Wilson lower bound is ≥0.70, Cohen's κ for the binary unplanned-complication label is ≥0.70, and no reviewed excluded stratum has ≥0.50 unplanned-complication yield. Revise if PPV 0.60–0.79, κ 0.40–0.69, or excluded-stratum yield is high. Defer/abandon if PPV <0.60 or κ <0.40. The enriched negative sample cannot estimate population sensitivity; estimating sensitivity requires broader random negative review or prospective adjudication.
