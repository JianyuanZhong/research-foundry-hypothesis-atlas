# Does mechanics-aware ventilatory burden predict delayed documented liberation beyond tidal volume?

## Status, scientific deliverable, and evidence boundary

This is a data-bound adaptation of expert seed `[starting question]`, not a reproduction of a paper. The future solver must newly construct an auditable mechanically ventilated adult ICU cohort, fit a transparent tidal-volume baseline and a matched mechanics-aware dynamic alternative, and estimate whether the alternative changes the clinically scaled interpretation of delayed ventilator liberation across held-out hospitals. Completion requires a cohort-flow table, frozen respiratory-label and unit dictionaries, a stay-level event ledger, split manifest, fitted model parameters/predictions, standardized contrasts with uncertainty, hospital-held-out calibration/transport, and all prespecified falsification outputs. Discovery has not fitted these models and asserts no clinical result.

The strongest claim supported now is data availability: the frozen eICU 2.0 snapshot contains adult patient/disposition records and structured respiratory chart rows with recorded TV/kg IBW, set and observed tidal volume, plateau pressure, PEEP, pressure support, FiO2, respiratory rate, and explicit RT Vent On/Off values. A bounded full-file audit measured 20,168,176 respiratoryCharting rows across 124,598 stays, including 1,024,017 TV/kg IBW, 353,455 plateau-pressure, 1,464,808 PEEP, 371,161 pressure-support, and 60,101 RT Vent On/Off rows. It also found 865,381 respiratoryCare rows, but only 157 nonzero ventendoffset values; therefore respiratoryCare end offsets cannot be treated as a complete liberation endpoint.

The unresolved claim is whether early recorded ventilatory burden that incorporates body size and respiratory mechanics distinguishes patients who achieve a documented ventilator-off transition from those who remain documented on support, beyond tidal volume alone, and whether this relationship transports across hospitals. This is a prognostic/measurement-process claim about recorded support, not a causal claim that a setting caused harm or that ventilator-off means successful extubation.

## Hypothesis and clinical importance

Among adult ICU stays with a newly documented ventilator start and still-recorded ventilator support at a six-hour landmark, a higher early mechanics-aware burden—especially high TV/kg IBW together with high plateau pressure, PEEP, pressure-support requirement, or low observed dynamic compliance proxies—will be associated with a lower 48-hour cumulative incidence of a first documented ventilator-off transition than an otherwise similar tidal-volume-only profile. The alternative prediction is that tidal volume alone explains the association, or that apparent burden is mostly a respiratory documentation-density artifact.

A supportive result would identify a reproducible, transportable recorded trajectory that could help select patients for a standardized spontaneous-breathing/lung-protective review. A null result would argue against treating a composite mechanics measure as clinically useful beyond TV/kg IBW. An adverse result could show that the composite is less transportable or reverses after accounting for charting opportunity. None of these results establishes an optimal ventilator setting, extubation readiness, quality ranking, or treatment effect.

Recent public-search evidence indicates prior retrospective MIMIC/eICU prediction work using PEEP reduction and prior mechanical-power studies. This proposal advances that literature only if it tests a pre-specified, hospital-held-out, process-aware comparison in this snapshot: mechanics/shape versus tidal volume, with the endpoint restricted to an explicitly recorded ventilator-off transition and with charting opportunity separated from physiology. A small AUROC improvement without a stable clinical contrast is not a substantive advance.

## Estimands and matched methods

The primary estimand is the training-standardized difference in 48-hour cumulative incidence of first recorded Off between a prespecified 25th-versus-75th mechanics-aware burden profile, at fixed TV/kg IBW, baseline severity, support coverage, and admission descriptors. A clinically meaningful target is 2 absolute percentage points; an interval spanning that margin is inconclusive. Report a secondary restart-after-Off estimand only in the Off-positive subset.

The transparent baseline is a regularized discrete-time competing-risk pooled-logistic model in 30-minute bins from the landmark to 48 hours. It includes TV/kg IBW (or a validated normalized equivalent), recent set/observed tidal volume, FiO2, PEEP, respiratory rate, exposure coverage, age/sex/ethnicity, admission source/type, unit type, APACHE sensitivity variables, and time-bin indicators. It does not include any row after the current bin or any Off-related field. A pre-specified TV-only nested model isolates what the expert seed asks: baseline versus adding mechanics.

The substantive mechanistic alternative is a piecewise-exponential multi-state model on the same bins and cohort, with states support-at-landmark, Off, unit death, and alive observation loss. It uses time-varying trajectories rather than one summary: TV/kg IBW level and slope, plateau pressure, PEEP, pressure support, FiO2, spontaneous/total respiratory rate, and valid paired mechanics. When plateau pressure and PEEP are paired within 10 minutes, derive a driving-pressure proxy only after numeric/unit checks; if not, leave it missing and report support. Use observed compliance only as a separate sensitivity, not as a silently substituted definition. Model charting opportunity (number of respiratory rows, time since last support setting, and whether a care-status row was present) as a companion observation process, with past-only inverse-observation weighting or joint-process sensitivity. The alternative reveals whether delayed Off follows sustained mechanics/shape and recovery trajectories, versus the baseline's scalar TV signal, and whether the apparent result is just documentation intensity. It is selected for this scientific information, not for a small predictive gain.

A neural sequence model is deferred, not prohibited: the public literature and this audit make the mechanism/endpoint-interpretability question more important than a generic sequence leaderboard. Revisit only if the mechanistic model passes label stability and event-density gates and a pre-registered learned sequence adds an interpretable trajectory contrast on the identical split/outcome. GPU use is not required for the primary fits.

Split whole hospitals before label-map freezing, unit validation, imputation, scaling, or model fitting; keep every `uniquepid` in one fold. Use grouped inner validation by hospital in development and a final held-out-hospital evaluation. Report hospital-cluster bootstrap intervals plus a uniquepid-clustered sensitivity, calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, AUROC/AUPRC only as secondary summaries, and decision-curve/net-benefit summaries only if a threshold can be clinically justified.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires a >=2-point standardized Off-incidence contrast for mechanics-aware versus TV-only profiles, uncertainty excluding zero and compatible with the margin, stable direction in held-out hospitals, and persistence after coverage adjustment and the nonzero-care-endpoint sensitivity. A secondary restart pattern can support clinical concern only if its endpoint dictionary is independently stable. This supports a recorded trajectory association, not a treatment recommendation.

Adverse evidence is a precise null/reversal of the mechanics contrast, loss of transport, or disappearance after charting-opportunity adjustment; it rejects the corresponding portable prognostic interpretation. A signal confined to rows labeled Continued or to hospitals with dense respiratory interfaces is evidence of documentation dependence.

Inconclusive evidence includes unstable label/value maps, too few eligible hospitals or events, no valid paired mechanics, placeholder zero offsets, poor numeric/unit validation, domination by unit exits, nonconvergence, or intervals spanning the 2-point margin. Do not replace the endpoint after seeing results.

Falsification/sensitivity checks are: no interpolation versus short-gap interpolation; result/entry-offset ordering; exclude rows after unit discharge; alternative Start definitions; RT Off versus corroborated respiratoryCare endpoint; invasive-airway-only versus all documented support; exclude tracheostomy and analyze it separately; pressure/PEEP pairing windows of 5/10/30 minutes; no-compliance versus compliance proxy; chart-density strata; time-shuffled exposure trajectories within stay; and a negative-control outcome of documentation of a non-respiratory structured care row after the landmark. A strong association under time shuffling or a large effect only in an observation-density stratum fails the intended physiologic interpretation.

Computationally checkable claims are headers/schema hashes, row counts, label maps, units, cohort flow, joins, event ordering, coverage, split integrity, model parameters, standardized contrasts, uncertainty, calibration, transport and sensitivity outputs. Expert adjudication or another study is required for true extubation, successful spontaneous breathing trial, reintubation, ventilator-associated injury, delivered versus ordered settings, clinical intent, and causal effects of ventilator settings. The data cannot establish that a recorded Off means extubation or that a burden target should be changed at bedside.
