## Union rosters and temporal ownership

Construct rosters only from admissible states, never from a favored state:

1. `R_test={i: max_z E_i^test(z)=1}`.
2. `R_tune={i not in R_test: max_z E_i^2019(z)=1}`.

## Models and robust fitting with variable eligibility

B uses age, sex, acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category, and—only for exposed bodies—frozen lesion diameter, lesion-count category, and explicit macrovascular thrombus with unknown indicators. S is B plus present/absent/unknown capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity. S0 is never refit: use S coefficients, preprocessing, penalty, and recalibration while replacing only the semantic block by its frozen state-zero vector. Assert bitwise `S=S0` whenever semantics are zero. W and A=W plus the same semantic block remain secondary workflow diagnostics; unrestricted text remains a ceiling diagnostic.

For model M and block b, use the state-normalized robust risk

`R_b(theta)=max_{z_i,y_i,r_i} [sum_i E_i^b(z_i) ell(y_i,M_theta(x_i(z_i,r_i)))] / [sum_i E_i^b(z_i)]`.

The max is joint over each patient's admissible history, documentary label, and visibility state; absent states contribute neither numerator nor denominator. Fit development models by minimizing `R_dev + elastic-net penalty`. Select penalties and preprocessing on worst-case normalized 2019 risk; fit the frozen robust recalibration on 2019 only. No test state or outcome is used for selection.

This is computable. For fixed theta and candidate ratio lambda, the separation oracle is the sum of patientwise maxima of `E_i[ell_i-lambda]`; Dinkelbach/bisection finds the exact worst ratio because the finite state set is patient-separable and `N_b^->0`. The outer robust logistic objective is convex; use deterministic epigraph cutting planes with a frozen convergence tolerance and compare against exhaustive enumeration on all fixtures through N<=12. Every bootstrap and semantic-null repetition rebuilds state sets, preprocessing, robust fits, tuning, and recalibration. No outcome-dependent state pruning is permitted.

## Partially identified test estimand

At threshold p, `w=p/(1-p)`, `I_Mi(z,r,p)=1[score_Mi(z,r)>=p]`, and for contrast j=(M1,M0),

`d_i^j(y,z,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)] [I_M1i(z,r,p)-I_M0i(z,r,p)]`.

Primary contrasts are D=(S,S0), C=(S,B), S-versus-flag-all, and S-versus-flag-none. For complete stored content, r exposes every eligible nonempty selected body. In `R_test`, compute sharp conditional-on-record bounds

`Delta_j^- = min sum_i E_i^test(z_i)d_i^j / sum_i E_i^test(z_i)`,
`Delta_j^+ = max sum_i E_i^test(z_i)d_i^j / sum_i E_i^test(z_i)`.

The optimization jointly changes membership, index encounter/pathology, clock, selected accession, score, and label. It never assigns a contribution from one state and eligibility from another. The same fractional separation oracle, with patientwise minima for the lower endpoint and maxima for the upper endpoint, yields exact all-stored bounds. Report extremizing state manifests and reason counts, not patient records.

These bounds target average net-benefit contrast among the latent eligible test-period population, not per union-roster member and not the invariant complete-case subset. `N_test^-` and `N_test^+` accompany every result.

## Supportive, adverse, mixed, and inconclusive interpretation

**Supportive P-Frame:** every gate passes; the simultaneous lower confidence limit for the sharp lower endpoint exceeds .01 at p=.30 for D, C, and both default contrasts; D/C sharp lower point endpoints are positive at all five thresholds; the semantic-null gate passes; and raw-row/midnight and invariant-frame diagnostics do not expose a code or provenance failure. The allowed conclusion is only that, in this historical record-defined latent eligible first-resection population, complete eventual report semantics robustly changed frozen-model review flags favorably and S robustly exceeded B and default policies despite admissible frame uncertainty.

**Supportive T-Frame without audit:** P-Frame passes, all pattern-size gates pass, and at least one access cell meets the same simultaneous lower-bound criteria. The allowed conclusion is only that the stated eventual-content access fractions would suffice under the enumerated uncertainty. Actual decision-time access remains unresolved.

**Adverse:** with feasibility and computation gates passed, the simultaneous upper confidence limit for the sharp upper endpoint of D or C is <=.01 at p=.30, meaning even the most favorable admissible frame state fails the materiality margin; or it is <=0, supporting harmful rather than merely non-material action changes. For T-Frame, adverse transport evidence requires that every cell's relevant sharp upper confidence bound fails the margin, not merely that the lower-bound support set is empty.

**Mixed:** one of D and C is supportive while the other is adverse; stored-content P-Frame is supportive but no transport cell is supportive; default-policy contrasts disagree with D/C; or effects are supportive at .30 but reverse or become adverse at a prespecified neighboring threshold. State-dependent sign with neither side excluded by simultaneous confidence is reported as provenance-sensitive and inconclusive, not averaged into “mixed benefit.”

**Inconclusive:** any sharp identified interval or simultaneous band straddles a required boundary; only complete-case or point estimates are favorable; denominator/event/pattern, review, extraction, null, optimization, bootstrap, sensitivity, or lineage gates fail; or external audit is absent for a decision-time statement. Favorable AUC, unrestricted text, or a naive exact-midnight analysis cannot rescue the claim.

No result supports biological M2 detection, treatment choice, wider margins, transplant, systemic/locoregional therapy, surveillance, recurrence reduction, survival, harms, costs, or patient benefit.
