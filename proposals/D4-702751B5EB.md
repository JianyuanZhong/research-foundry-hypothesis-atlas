## Evidence boundary and unresolved claim

The strongest supported claim before this child remains limited. The parent’s inspected evidence supports that creatinine–cystatin eGFR discordance is common and internally predictable in UK Biobank (Chen et al., Kidney Medicine 2024, DOI `10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796`, frozen full-text XML source `[source checksum]`, retrieved-file [source checksum]). The parent also inspected Lees et al. (JAMA Network Open 2022, DOI `10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.38300`, PMCID `PMC9597396`, frozen XML source [source checksum]) and Liu et al. (Clinical Kidney Journal 2025, DOI `10.1093/ckj/sfaf003`, PMCID `PMC11997436`, frozen XML source [source checksum]). Those sources motivate cystatin-related prognostic investigation but do not establish incremental grip value, this fixed-capacity policy, subgroup consistency, measured-GFR accuracy, or clinical benefit. The research-ambition demonstrations are references rather than evidence for this hypothesis; the cancer demonstration’s main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not claimed to have been read.

The strongest parent-supported claim is therefore only that the frozen AG1-versus-AF1 experiment is executable and can test whether AG1 enriches a recorded inpatient N18-before-death endpoint at a fixed 466-person assay capacity. The parent does not establish that a pooled result applies similarly across recorded-sex strata.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> In the exact frozen O1_hold population, AG1 is not materially worse than AF1 for recorded inpatient N18-before-death contribution in either recorded-sex stratum, when each stratum’s policy yield uses the same total 466-assay denominator and both strata are evaluated with simultaneous uncertainty.

The operational non-inferiority margin is one net recorded event per 466-slot list, `m = 1000/466 = 2.145922746781116` per 1,000 assay slots. This is the smallest nonzero resolution at the fixed quota, not a validated clinical-equivalence, utility, cost, or safety threshold. A result passing this qualification means only that the data do not show a stratum-specific loss larger than this operating margin under the frozen recorded endpoint.

## Child estimands and baselines

The parent’s primary baseline remains the overall fixed-denominator contrast:

`Delta18 = (1000/466)[sum_{i in S_AG1} E18_i - sum_{i in S_AF1} E18_i]`.

For each recorded-sex stratum `s in {0,1}`, define the global-slot policy contribution and contrast:

`Q_AG1,s = (1000/466) sum_{i in S_AG1 intersect G_s} E18_i`

`Q_AF1,s = (1000/466) sum_{i in S_AF1 intersect G_s} E18_i`

`Delta18,s = Q_AG1,s - Q_AF1,s`.

The denominator is 466 for every stratum. Do not divide by the number of people of sex `s`, the number selected in that stratum, the number of swaps, survivors, events, or complete cases. This answers whether a global fixed-capacity policy loses outcome contribution in one stratum, while preserving the policy’s actual total assay-slot resource.

Because `G_0) and `G_1) partition O1_hold, require the exact decomposition `Delta18,0 + Delta18,1 = Delta18) within declared arithmetic tolerance. Report stratum sizes, each list’s stratum counts, intersection and directional swaps by stratum, every numerator and yield, the two component contrasts, and the decomposition residual. Report the 435-person overlap as a no-policy-change diagnostic and the 31-person directional swap event counts as the mechanistic source of any contrast; do not treat the overlap as a second comparator or refit a model.

AF1 is the sole clinical policy baseline. AG1-versus-AF1 is a fixed-roster paired policy comparison, not an adjusted regression and not an estimate of causal treatment effect. The inherited overall `Delta18` is required output and cannot be replaced by a favorable subgroup result.
