It repairs one consequential defect. Those exclusions are not missing completely at random: the reason for exclusion can also change the pathology encounter, selected reports, report pattern and treatment history. Extreme-denominator padding after fitting cannot recover an estimand for the omitted patients, and a favorable “identifiable-frame” result could therefore be created by documentary stability itself.

The repair defines the clinical target before source resolution, retains every patient who could belong to the 2020–2021 target, and treats target membership, index encounter, clock, report selection, pattern, prior-treatment status and documentary outcome as parts of one patient state. It reports the identified set of net benefit over all admissible global reconstructions, with the denominator recomputed in each reconstruction. It never treats a nonmember as an outcome-negative patient and never fixes a convenient denominator. A chronology-safe superframe assignment prevents a patient who could be in the test target from contributing to fitting or tuning.

## Clinical question, evidence and unresolved hypothesis

The bounded clinical question is whether, 24 hours before an adult patient's first eligible liver resection for retrospectively pathology-confirmed HCC, semantic features in routinely stored CT/MRI reports add clinically material information to a laboratory-independent flag for multidisciplinary review of pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2).

The flag is historical risk stratification. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy, locoregional or systemic treatment, or surveillance.

### Strongest claim currently supported

It does not establish which cross-source procedure rows are the same operation; that a case-record midnight is incision time; report authored/final/release/view times or version history; pathology specimen identity, sampling adequacy or time; latent biological M2; recurrence; survival; clinician action; treatment effect; harms; costs; or patient benefit.

The inspected full XML of Li et al. (2025, DOI `10.1111/jcmm.70746`, PMCID `PMC12309289`; frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) supports that MVI is pathology determined, prognostically relevant, and unavailable directly for preoperative planning, while MVI-directed management remains controversial. The inspected full HTML of Huang et al. (2026, DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`; frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports a retrospective CT/AFP binary-MVI model in 487 development and 256 external-validation patients, with blinded raw-image review, seven-point pathology sampling and AUC 0.740/0.781. It does not validate M2, routine report text, source-reconciled episode reconstruction, report availability, this institution, or a treatment effect.

Episode 15's full procedure audit found 336,907 exact-deduplicated rows and pervasive cross-source clock multiplicity. The Episode-16 broad feasibility proxy—not an analytic dictionary or cohort—additionally found 537 patients with valid proxy rows in more than one of the 2015–2018/2019/2020–2021 periods, 261 encounters with more than one proxy calendar date, and 33 adjacent-day within-encounter pairs. Among 24,568 proxy patients represented in both procedure sources, the source-specific earliest proxy row differed by encounter for 61, by calendar date for 268, and by analysis period for 34. These counts do not establish alternative index states, but they show that stability-based exclusion cannot be assumed ignorable.

An **operative reconstruction** `e` selects one admissible partition/linkage of the patient's qualifying rows, orders all resulting episodes over all available years before filtering, and identifies the earliest episode. A **clock state** `k` selects an exact anesthesia time or one finite partition of the date interval. For `t_op(e,k)`, define `t_dec=t_op-24h`. Partition a date interval at every boundary at which an examination accession enters or leaves `[t_dec-90d,t_dec)`, or a recorded prior-treatment row enters or leaves `[t_dec-365d,t_dec)`; evaluate representatives and required open/closed boundary limits. The 12/48/72-hour analyses rebuild every state and result.

For each patient, an admissible state
`s=(e,k,a,period,prior,J,g,Y,V)`
is generated coherently from one reconstruction and clock:

- `a=1` only if the selected earliest episode has an encounter row with age at least 18, its all-row same-encounter pathology composite explicitly supports HCC, it is in the relevant calendar period, and no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy occurs in `[t_dec-365d,t_dec)`. “No recorded prior treatment” is not biological treatment-naivety.
- `period` is determined by the selected index operation: development 2015–2018, tuning 2019, test 2020–2021, or outside analysis.
- `J` and `g` are the state-specific selected accession set and 0/C/M/CM eventual pattern.
- `Y,V` come from the state-selected index encounter's complete pathology composite. If index encounter changes, the outcome composite changes with it; no outcome is borrowed across encounters.
- A patient's state may be a nonmember (`a=0`). Nonmembership is retained as a possibility, not recoded as `Y=0`.

No Cartesian product of independently chosen episode, clock, report, treatment and outcome states is permitted. Every emitted state includes its originating procedure rows, index encounter, breakpoints, treatment rows, accession components and pathology rows. Clinician-adjudicated impossible combinations are removed with a reason code; unresolved combinations remain.

## Target population, temporal quarantine and estimand

Define `a_i^T(s)=1` when state `s` satisfies all target criteria and its index operation is in 2020–2021. The **test superframe**
`U_T={i: exists s in S_i with a_i^T(s)=1}`
is frozen before model fitting. Every patient in `U_T` is reserved from development and tuning, even if another admissible state places that patient in 2015–2019 or outside the analysis years. Thus no possible test patient can leak into fitting.

Among remaining patients, define tuning superframe `U_V` by any admissible eligible 2019 state and reserve those patients from development. Define development superframe `U_D` among the remainder by any admissible eligible 2015–2018 state. Patients with only pre-2015 or post-2021 states are extraction audits. This priority assignment is an algorithmic chronology safeguard, not a claim that the latest period is true.

A global reconstruction `q=(s_i:i in U_R)` chooses exactly one admissible state for each patient in role superframe `R`. For test world `q`,
`P_T(q)={i in U_T:a_i^T(s_i)=1}`
is the conceptual adult, no-recorded-prior-treatment, first-resection, pathology-documented HCC target under that reconstruction. Its size `n_T(q)` is allowed to vary.

For contrast `j=(M_1,M_0)`, threshold `p`, and access cell `h`, let
`u_i^j=[Y_i-w(1-Y_i)] [I(M_1,p)-I(M_0,p)]`,
where `w=p/(1-p)`. The state-specific estimand is
`Delta_j(q,h,p)=sum_i a_i^T(s_i) u_i^j(s_i,h,p) / n_T(q)`.
The reported identification interval is the sharp minimum and maximum of `Delta_j` over all admissible q, documentary outcomes and access assignments.

This is a mean among target members in each possible source world, not a mean over a selected stable subset. A nonmember contributes to neither numerator nor denominator. Report `|U_T|`, the certainly-member core, minimum/maximum `n_T(q)`, counts whose index encounter/period/prior status varies, and the characteristics of certain versus possible members. The former Episode-15 identifiable-frame estimate is a labeled secondary comparison only and cannot rescue P-Frame or T-Frame.

## Models and jointly robust fitting

B uses age, sex, acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category and—only for exposed accessions—frozen lesion diameter, lesion-count category and explicit macrovascular thrombus with unknown indicators. S is B plus present/absent/unknown capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout and peritumoral hypointensity. No unrestricted text enters the primary models.

S0 is never fitted or recalibrated separately: apply the fitted S preprocessing, coefficients, penalty and calibration with only the semantic block replaced by its frozen state-zero vector. Assert bitwise `S=S0` whenever semantics are zero. W and A=W plus the identical semantic block remain secondary workflow diagnostics; unrestricted text remains a ceiling diagnostic.

For model M in role R, robust fitting minimizes normalized worst-world logistic risk:
`min_theta max_{q in Q_R:n_R(q)>0} {sum_i a_i^R(s_i) ell(y_i,z_theta(x_i(s_i))) / n_R(q)} + lambda P(theta)`.
The state choice jointly determines membership, index encounter, clock, report, features and Y; one patient cannot supply the loss from one state and membership from another. Development hyperparameter fitting, 2019 selection/recalibration and semantic-null refits use the same construction. Report worst-world and best-world effective sample sizes.

The inner maximum is computable exactly with a fractional separation oracle: for a trial value c, maximize `sum_i a_i(s_i)[ell_i(s_i)-c]` by choosing one allowed state per patient; use Dinkelbach iteration to find the maximum ratio. A cutting-plane epigraph solver alternates convex elastic-net master fits and exact state-separation until no violation exceeds `1e-8`. An explicit global mixed-integer/reference implementation must match exhaustive enumeration for all fixtures through 12 patients and all real-data shards through 20 patients. Approximate state sampling, independent marginal maxima or dropping hard patients cannot support a primary claim.

## Sharp test bounds, access frontier and simultaneous inference

For a fixed bootstrap fit, sharp lower/upper test endpoints solve the linear-fractional optimization over one coherent state per test-superframe patient. Dinkelbach subproblems are mixed-integer linear programs after precomputing each state's score/action contribution. Access constraints use the same state's membership and pattern indicators. Enumerating a denominator is an acceptable independent reference. Require optimized and brute-force equality through 12-patient fixtures, primal/dual gap at most `1e-8`, and identical answers under state ordering permutations.

Primary contrasts are D=(S,S0) and C=(S,B); S versus flag-all and flag-none are required. Every contrast in a world uses the same member denominator. P-Frame uses complete exposure. T-Frame uses the exact access optimizer, coordinatewise coverage-preserving monotone closure, and reports the upward-closed support set and Pareto-minimal antichain.

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps stratified by chronology-safe superframe. A resampled patient carries all procedure rows, admissible reconstructions, clock breakpoints, treatment rows, accession components, pathology rows and state-dependent role memberships. Rebuild robust B/S fits, S0, tuning/recalibration, sharp test bounds and the entire access frontier in every replicate. More than 2.5% failed refits or bound solves makes the result inconclusive.

Construct one frozen simultaneous one-sided family over the lower identified endpoints required for P/T support, all five thresholds, D/C/default contrasts and all frontier cells. The model family uses familywise alpha 0.025; the external audit family, if later available, uses alpha 0.025, so a combined decision-time claim has familywise error at most 0.05. Support requires the confidence limit for the *lower identification endpoint*, not a point estimate or stable-subset estimate, to exceed its margin. Adverse evidence uses the simultaneous upper confidence limit for the *upper identification endpoint*.

Run 1,000 development/tuning semantic-null repetitions, permuting each patient's complete semantic state collection—not individual rows—within frozen chronology role, possible-period signature, pattern-support signature, state cardinality and formatting family; refit the entire pipeline. All-stored D at 0.30 must exceed the 97.5th null percentile. Label permutation must be null; post-cutoff text injection, independent-state recombination, denominator fixing, dropping optional members and midnight-as-exact substitution must trigger controls.

## Feasibility and falsification gates

Before interpretation require:

- the minimum `n_T(q)` over all admissible worlds is at least 1,000; minimum adjudicated `V=1` M2 count is at least 75; and minimum V=1 fraction is at least 85%;
- every potential test patient and every admissible state is enumerated or represented exactly by verified constraints; report certain/possible membership, index, period, prior-treatment and pattern counts without a maximum “uncertain fraction” exclusion;
- all dictionaries and required clinical reviews are complete; all state provenance and join multiplicities are emitted;
- nonempty text occurs in at least 80% of selected CT/MRI accessions and size/count extraction succeeds in at least 70% of nonempty accessions;
- optimization, inference and null procedures meet their tolerances, with zero laboratory or forbidden-feature lineage;
- modality-specific T claims additionally have at least 100 target members and 20 V=1 M2 in that state-specific pattern in every world used to support the cell.

The primary 24-hour analysis is repeated from scratch at 12, 48 and 72 hours. P-Frame requires D/C lower endpoints positive at every prespecified threshold and no reversal of the 0.01 margin conclusion at the buffer stress tests. The raw-earliest-procedure and split-key analyses remain falsifying sensitivities only.

The repair is specifically falsified if the Episode-15 identifiable-frame analysis is favorable but the simultaneous upper limit for the repaired all-frame upper endpoint is at most 0.01, or if dropping variable-membership patients changes any required D/C point by at least 0.005 or changes a P/T conclusion. Such a result shows that apparent value is conditional on documentary identifiability.

## Interpretation zones

**Supportive P-Frame:** every feasibility gate passes; simultaneous lower limits for the lower identified endpoints of D, C and both default contrasts exceed 0.01 at 0.30; D/C lower endpoints are positive across the threshold plateau and buffer analyses; and the null and source-grain controls pass. The allowed conclusion is that, for every admissible reconstruction of this historical 2020–2021 first-eligible-resection target, complete eventual CT/MRI report semantics produced materially favorable action changes within fitted S and complete S beat B for documentary M2.

**Supportive T-Frame without audit:** P-Frame passes and at least one access cell passes the same lower-endpoint gates with adequate state-specific pattern counts. The allowed conclusion is only that the stated access vector would suffice under the enumerated source, membership, clock, outcome and access uncertainty. Actual decision-time availability remains unresolved.

**Adverse:** with feasibility established, a simultaneous upper limit for the upper identified endpoint of D or C is at most 0.01 at 0.30, meaning even the most favorable admissible reconstruction does not achieve the clinical margin. An upper limit at most zero is evidence that the action changes are nonbeneficial or harmful on the net-benefit scale. A favorable stable-subset result paired with such an all-frame result is adverse evidence against the Episode-15 identifiability-conditioned claim.

**Mixed:** D is supportive while C is adverse, so semantics alter actions favorably within fitted S but S does not beat optimized B; C is supportive while D is adverse, so complete S may beat B without attributing value to runtime semantics; or P is supportive while T is not, so stored-content value does not establish a workable access level. A frontier supported for one modality pattern but not another is pattern-specific, not overall support.

**Inconclusive:** identification or confidence intervals cross a decision boundary; lower and upper endpoints permit both material and nonmaterial worlds; a support set is empty without an adverse upper bound; any population, adjudication, state-enumeration, sample-size, pathology, extraction, solver, resampling, null or sensitivity gate fails; or a decision-time claim lacks the exact-version audit. Favorable AUC, unrestricted text, point estimates, a stable subset or a naive midnight clock cannot rescue the primary claim.
