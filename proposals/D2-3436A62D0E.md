# Does the MerTK/CD206pos macrophage brake on the RA FLS effector program replicate across independent FLS donors, with macrophage donor held fixed? A specificity- and ambient-controlled per-cell test in E-MTAB-8873

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (island leader, valid, mu 33.12). Key works [K1],[K2],[K3]. Branch: work/lead/. Screening status: **planned, not executed** (only bounded read-only inspection of prepared files and design; no managed compute job).

## 1. Unresolved question (the opening)

The island's leader established that the per-FLS-cell effector readout of a MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) macrophage "brake" is better than pseudobulk means, and added a matched non-effector specificity control (F1), a per-cell ambient-RNA control (F2) and a shift-vs-mixture decomposition (F3). Its one recorded weakness is that the discriminating contrast is estimated inside a **single FLS donor ladder** (`R2_SA258R`). A donor-idiosyncratic effect cannot be separated from a general, target-worthy brake. The smallest check whose result would change the next decision is therefore **replication of the same specificity-controlled contrast across independent FLS donors while holding the macrophage donor fixed**.

## 2. Clinical importance

If a MerTK/CD206pos subset dose- and effector-specifically lowers the FLS matrix-degrading program across donors, then restoring that subset or its efferocytic signalling is a target-prioritisation decision distinct from generic immunosuppression [K3]. If the effect is donor-idiosyncratic, or explained by composition/ambient macrophage RNA or a global technical effect [K2], then MerTK positivity is a state marker and a poor standalone target. The result is decision-relevant as a **supportive, adverse or inconclusive replication outcome** — not a clinical-efficacy claim.

## 3. Hypotheses and the observation that separates them

- **H_R (reproducible effector-specific brake).** Within each FLS donor, raising MerTK/CD206pos macrophage fraction lowers the per-FLS-cell effector program, and the pos-vs-neg drop (i) exceeds the matched non-effector control (F1), (ii) survives adjustment for per-cell macrophage-restricted ambient reads (F2), and (iii) is distribution-wide (F3), **in the same direction in every donor**.
- **H_C (artifact / donor-idiosyncratic / fixed step).** The drop is not reproducible across donors, or is matched by the control gradient, or is absorbed by ambient adjustment, or is a two-state step.

**Pre-registered donor-replicated contrasts (macrophage donor held fixed within each pair; verified member names):**
- FLS `SA257A`: `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mneg` (396 cells) vs `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mpos` (383) — macrophage donor `SA266A` fixed.
- FLS `SA263R`: `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mneg` (440) vs `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mpos` (455) — macrophage donor `SA278R` fixed.
- FLS `SA266A`: `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` (1144) vs `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos` (1060) — macrophage donor `SA277A` fixed.
- Graded ladder (single donor, gradedness only): `R2_SA258R` `{FLS_only 1332, 1Mpos/3Mneg 1364, 3Mpos/1Mneg 1145, Mpos 1207}`; plus `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_1Mpos_SA277A_3Mneg` (538) as a partial second-ladder anchor.
- Not used as a clean contrast (macrophage donor not held fixed): `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_Mpos` (adds SA267R).

H_R predicts a same-signed, CI-separated pos-vs-neg effector drop with F1/F2 passed in all three donors. H_C predicts reversal, non-separation, control-gradient matching, or ambient-absorption in at least one donor (pre-registered as adverse).

## 4. Estimand and statistics

- **Primary estimand.** Per-FLS-donor difference in mean per-cell log-normalised effector-program score, Mpos minus Mneg, with each donor contributing one difference; pooled across the three macrophage-donor-matched donors with a **donor-level random effect (n=3)** and an explicit statement that three donors cannot support a confident cross-donor variance estimate (uncertainty reported, not over-claimed).
- **Effector score (verified present, `Reference_end` rows):** MMP1|NM_002421.3, MMP3|NM_002422.4, MMP9|NM_004994.2, MMP13|NM_002427.3, MMP14|NM_004995.3, IL6|NM_000600.4, CXCL8|NM_000584.3, CXCL10|NM_001565.3, CCL2|NM_002982.3, CCL5|NM_002985.2.
- **F1 non-effector control:** GAPDH|NM_002046.5, PGK1|NM_000291.3, FTH1, COL1A1, COL1A2, COL3A1. Adverse if control gradient >= effector gradient in a donor.
- **F2 ambient control:** per-FLS-cell fraction of counts from MERTK|NM_006343.2, MRC1|NM_002438.3, CD163|NM_004244.5; adverse if the effector difference loses CI-separation after adjustment.
- **F3 shift-vs-mixture:** distribution-wide shift vs a fitted two-component step. Secondary in this child (gradedness is design-limited to the SA258R ladder); reported as supporting/inconclusive, not primary.
- **Method comparison.** Baseline: pseudobulk sample-mean effector score. Substantive alternative (chosen): per-cell distribution analysis of the 445-feature matrices, quantifying per-cell shift and effector-high fraction; the baseline loses the shape distinction (a responding subpopulation vs a global shift), which is exactly the H_R/H_C separator. Heavier models rejected as uninformative for this estimand. CPU-only, ~33 members x <=1500 cells x 445 features (<2 GB); discovery inspection was read-only/unallocated, so this is a solver-envelope estimate.

## 6. Limits (must be stated, not hidden)

Targeted 445-feature panel (unmeasured genes unavailable); cell-level members are FLS-only (MERTK+ ~1-3%, so MerTK-intrinsic signalling is unobservable per cell); n=3 replicated donors is underpowered for a confident cross-donor variance estimate; cross-sectional/coculture, no protein or wet-lab causality; not a clinical-efficacy claim. MerTK-inhibitor arms are direction-only (n=1) and remain exploratory.

## 7. What would change the decision; what needs other evidence

Supportive (same-signed, CI-separated effector drop passing F1/F2 in all three donors) justifies continuing the MerTK/CD206pos resolution-target branch. Adverse (reversal or ambient-absorption in any donor) parks it as a donor-specific/state phenomenon. Inconclusive keeps it open with a larger donor panel. MerTK-intrinsic causality, clinical efficacy and independent replication require protein/functional readouts, expert review, or another study — not this computational design.
