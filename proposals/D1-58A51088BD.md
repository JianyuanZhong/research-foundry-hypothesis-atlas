## 1. Unresolved question
After circulatory stabilisation in sepsis/septic shock, the parent tests whether renal-vs-respiratory recovery
asynchrony at a decision time t* adds information beyond (M0) same-time snapshot severity, (M2*) an independent
acute-inflammatory recovery axis, and (M3) organ-specific observation intensity, for a **7-day composite**
deterioration outcome. The residual unresolved objection (parent's own limit) is that a *composite* outcome cannot
separate **genuine organ-specific divergence** from a **single common severity/observation trajectory that is
sampled unevenly** across the two limbs. A common-trajectory rival and true divergence make nearly identical
predictions for a composite non-specific outcome.

## 2. The discriminating test (the advance)
Replace the composite outcome with an **organ-matched vs cross-matched** contrast. If the slower-recovering limb
carries genuine organ-specific information, its recovery should predict failure of **its own** organ system more
strongly than failure of the other (asymmetry > 0). If asynchrony merely indexes a shared severity/trajectory
axis, the slow limb predicts both organ systems about equally (asymmetry ~ 0). This asymmetry is a prediction on
which the leading claim and its strongest rival differ [K1].

- Leading claim H1: organ-matched coupling > cross-matched coupling (asymmetry positive, CI excludes 0).
- Rival H0: matched ~ cross-matched (asymmetry CI includes 0) => asynchrony reduces to a non-specific axis.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, variables
- Population: MIMIC-IV adults (>=18) with sepsis/septic shock (Sepsis-3: suspected infection + SOFA>=2) and both a
  measurable renal and a measurable respiratory limb in the t*-24h window.
- t*: first time with sustained MAP>=65 mmHg [K] **without** vasopressor-escalation, requiring >=2h stability.
- Predictor window: (t*-24h, t*]; limbs classified by change across that window (no future information).
- Renal limb recovery: creatinine decline (labevents 50912) **and** urine-output improvement (outputevents
  226559/226560/226566/226627/226631/226713), with RRT status; discordance handled per [K2].
- Respiratory limb recovery: P/F-ratio improvement (pO2 50821, FiO2 223835) and ventilation status
  (procedureevents 225792/225794); MAP/vasopressors FiO2 223835, MAP 220052/220181, vasopressors
  221906/221289/222315/221749/221662/221653.
- Asynchrony at t*: |z(renal recovery) - z(respiratory recovery)| (M1 predictor; parent definition preserved).
- M2* (independent axis): acute inflammatory recovery from WBC 51301 decay, platelet 51265, absolute
  lymphocytes 51133 (labevents), kept as a covariate, not a limb.

## 4. Outcomes (7-day post-t*), organ-matched
- Renal event: new or continued RRT escalation (procedureevents 225441/225802/225803/225809/225955) by t*+7d.
- Respiratory event: reintubation / invasive-ventilation escalation or new NIV (procedureevents 225792/225794,
  extubation 227194) by t*+7d.
- Death (non-specific anchor): hosp/admissions `hospital_expire_flag`/`deathtime` within 7d.
- Composite (secondary): renal OR respiratory OR death within 7d (parent outcome, retained for continuity).

## 5. Baselines and models (method exploration on one question)
- Baseline B0 (interpretable): logistic regression, M0 snapshot severity + asynchrony, organ-matched outcome.
- Learned/structured alternative A1: a joint latent-variable / dynamic-factor model that decomposes the two organ
  time series into a shared recovery factor + organ-specific residuals, then tests whether the **organ-specific
  residual** (not the shared factor) drives the matched-vs-cross-matched asymmetry. A1 can reveal whether the
  asymmetry lives in organ-specific dynamics that B0's marginal regression cannot separate from the shared factor.
- Both fit on ONE locked temporal split (earliest 70% development / latest 30% holdout by `icustays.intime`).
- Report matched OR, cross-matched OR, and their bootstrap-CI asymmetry; A1 reports the residual-attributable
  component of the asymmetry. Selection reason: A1 is the only arm that can attribute asymmetry to organ-specific
  vs shared variance; B0 alone cannot. Approx budget: CPU, tabular + small EM/factor fit, < 1 h; no GPU required
  (defer A1 GPU escalation unless the factor model is refit across many seeds).

## 6. Falsification / interpretation
- Supportive H1: asymmetry CI excludes 0 and matched>cross-matched for both limbs => organ-specific information
  beyond a shared trajectory. Does NOT establish causation or a mechanism.
- Adverse H0: asymmetry CI includes 0 => asynchrony reduces to a non-specific severity/trajectory axis; the
  "genuine divergence" claim is falsified and the parent's composite result is reinterpreted.
- Inconclusive: wide CIs / low event counts (discovery did not establish prevalence) => defer, do not claim.
- Negative control: permuted limb labels within the holdout must yield asymmetry CI covering 0.
- Claims the data CANNOT settle (need clinical adjudication / other data): whether any asymmetry reflects a
  mechanism vs muscle-mass/creatinine artefact [K3] vs observation practice; cystatin C is unavailable.

## 8. Essential adjudication / unavailable evidence
Single-centre retrospective; no causal/mechanistic claim. Creatinine renal limb confounded by muscle mass and RRT
