# Audited persistent respiratory-setting endpoint: corrected feasibility and validity child

## Parentage and decision

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the assigned episode-58 goal, the adult first-qualifying-stay denominator, landmarks `1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200`, the four exact airway definitions, post-freeze exposure windows, disjoint time windows, hospital-held-out row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro estimands, stay-level clustering, and strict documentation-proxy limits.

The substantive change is an implementation and validity audit rather than a change in population or estimand. The inherited denominator and care-plan artifact were directly accessible and byte-verified. The numeric endpoint was corrected so a component must have a valid paired pre/post observation design to contribute an observed endpoint state; unsupported rows are missing rather than being coded as non-events. The pre-window separation rule now permits any two observations separated by at least 30 minutes, while retaining the median of the last three valid pre-window values. Corroboration processing was de-duplicated across airway definitions and the airway transition requires an artificial-airway status before a later `No Artificial Airway` status. The broader model uses the four identity-preserving `broad_0`–`broad_3` indicators.

The corrected computation is a meaningful full-cohort feasibility and point-estimate audit, but not a fully confirmatory causal or clinical validation study. Placebo timing controls, unrelated-treatment controls, and nested stay-cluster refitting were specified by the parent goal but were not completed in this execution. Accordingly, the result is classified as **inconclusive feasibility/validity**, not supportive evidence for bedside liberation or a clinical decision.

## Evidence-supported and untested claims

The inherited evidence supports that exact care-plan labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated, and that broader Ventilated-string documentation does not reliably improve transportable prediction of the inherited chart-proxy endpoint. The corrected execution additionally establishes computational feasibility of a repeated numeric-setting endpoint on the frozen cohort, quantifies component support and missingness, and demonstrates sparse independent corroboration.

The untested claim remains narrow and falsifiable: conditional on the frozen target-independent adult first-stay landmark and pre-landmark respiratory state, does an exact or outcome-blind broader care-plan indicator improve hospital-held-out prediction of a persistent, repeated numeric respiratory-setting decrease? These data do not test whether a patient was invasively ventilated, whether a spontaneous breathing trial or extubation evaluation occurred, whether liberation was clinically appropriate or successful, or whether treatment caused benefit or harm.

## Frozen population, temporal rules, and endpoint

The denominator is inherited from the byte-frozen artifact below and is filtered without using post-window endpoint values or care-plan labels for cohort selection. Rows require one of the four definitions, `full_window == True`, `complete_outcome_window == True`, and `unitdischargeoffset > landmark + 1080`. The baseline is `[L-360,L)`, the inherited classification/exposure window is `[L,L+360]`, and the numeric endpoint window is `(L+360,L+1080]`.

From `respiratoryCharting`, the implementation uses only the fixed canonical labels: FiO2 (`FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`), PEEP (`PEEP`), pressure support (`Pressure Support`), and set rate (`Vent Rate`). FiO2 percentages are normalized to fractions; accepted ranges are 0.21–1.00, 0–40, 0–60, and 0–80 respectively. For each component, the pre-reference is the median of the last three valid observations in the baseline window, provided at least two pre observations span 30 minutes. A component is endpoint-observed only if its post window has at least two valid observations spanning 120 minutes. A persistent component reduction requires the first and last qualifying post observations to be below the component threshold, the later qualifying value to be no higher than the earlier one, and the complete post-window median to confirm the threshold reduction. Thresholds are 0.05 FiO2, 2 cm H2O PEEP, 2 cm H2O pressure support, and 2 breaths/min set rate. The composite is one if any observed component meets the rule; it is missing when no component is paired. A two-component composite and minimum-value sensitivity are retained for audit only.

The independent corroborator sensitivity scans `respiratoryCare` for an ordered artificial-airway to `No Artificial Airway` transition in the endpoint window, and `treatment` for the exact literal `pulmonary|ventilation and oxygenation|ventilator weaning`. Both are documentation proxies, not gold standards. The corrected scan finds only one persistent event with airway-transition corroboration in the unbounded definition and 31 with the treatment-string corroborator; corresponding totals are 1/27, 0/17, and 0/10 for airway transition/treatment-correlated persistent events in the 24-hour, 12-hour, and 6-hour definitions (the treatment counts are shown as persistent-and-weaning; the combined corroborated event counts are 27, 17, and 10).

## Point-estimate modeling and interpretation

The executable fits baseline, process, exact, and broader models with hospital-held-out preprocessing and fitting, median imputation with indicators, standardized numeric variables, one-hot landmark, and four identity-preserving broader indicators. Primary model rows are endpoint-available only. Hospitals require at least 20 endpoint-available rows and both endpoint classes. The resulting held-out sample sizes were 2,215/1,503/1,066/676 for unbounded/24h/12h/6h, with event counts 370/281/184/119.

Broad minus process log-loss deltas (positive is worse) were respectively:

- Unbounded: micro `+0.00520`, macro `+0.00062`.
- Airway-24h: micro `+0.00521`, macro `+0.00087`.
- Airway-12h: micro `+0.00729`, macro `+0.00292`.
- Airway-6h: micro `+0.00992`, macro `+0.00323`.

Broad minus exact deltas were `+0.00467/+0.00103`, `+0.00451/+0.00116`, `+0.00635/+0.00212`, and `+0.00894/+0.00295` for micro/macro across the same four definitions. These corrected point estimates are adverse to a broader-label predictive improvement, but they are not promoted to the prespecified final adverse gate because nested refit intervals and the placebo/unrelated-treatment controls were not executed. They also do not establish that care was absent or inappropriate.

## Falsification and validity status

The parent-prespecified prior-window placebo and three unrelated-treatment outcomes were not run in this bounded execution. Nested stay-cluster refitting was also not run; therefore no interval-based gate is claimed. A future confirmatory child should run those controls and refit preprocessing/model parameters within every LOHO training fold, resampling complete stays with all landmarks together. It should report whether the broader-minus-process signal is specific to the respiratory endpoint and temporally prospective.

Automatic checks supported by this child include source and inherited-artifact hashes, exact joins, temporal inequalities, endpoint non-overlap, repeated-observation spacing, missing endpoint states, component-level support, ordered airway transition logic, and arithmetic of point estimates. Clinical adjudication remains essential for determining whether charted settings correspond to actual device settings, whether an airway transition is complete, and whether a treatment string records an executed intervention. Direct device/ventilator data, SBT/extubation records, workflow metadata, blinded clinician review, and external validation would be required for stronger clinical conclusions.
