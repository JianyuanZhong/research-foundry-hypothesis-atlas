# 72-hour observed creatinine/Foley recovery discordance — episode 9 executable successor

## Decision and scientific deliverable

This successor preserves the fixed, noncausal clinical question:

> Among adults in an index ICU stay who are alive and still in that ICU at 72 hours, have an observed early creatinine rise, documented early Foley oliguria, apparent creatinine recovery by 72 hours, and adequate paired measurement support, is failure of recorded Foley output to recover by 72 hours associated with higher subsequent same-admission in-hospital mortality than recovery of recorded Foley output?

The population, landmark, observed phenotype, and primary outcome remain fixed unless a declared source-integrity failure makes them impossible. This is an association study of a chart-observed phenotype, not a causal treatment analysis and not a claim about true GFR.

The clinical problem is consequential: a falling creatinine may be read as reassurance even when recorded urine output remains low. If this discordance is reproducibly associated with later death after the 72-hour landmark, it would justify prospective validation of a warning phenotype using complete urine capture, cystatin C or measured GFR, muscle/volume assessment, and treatment context. It would not show that low urine output is complete, that creatinine recovery caused harm, or that an alert should change care.

The actual scientific deliverable must contain newly computed outputs:

1. provenance.json, including the exact source path, archive SHA-256, snapshot, member names and sizes, catalog/schema hashes, dictionary audit, software versions, stage timings, and checkpoint status.
2. cohort_flow.csv, item_support.csv, and measurement_process.csv, with raw, valid, duplicate, late-storage, incompatible-unit, eligible-key, and window counts for every required source and every support gate.
3. phenotype_outcomes.csv, one frozen row per selected hospitalization, with all continuous components, density/process variables, phenotype labels, outcome dates, and missingness flags.
4. A primary risk table estimating unadjusted discordant-versus-concordant mortality risks, risk difference, risk ratio, and subject-clustered 95% intervals; and a prespecified standardized associational contrast adjusted only with pre-48-hour covariates.
5. First observed post-landmark RRT, later same-admission ICU readmission, death-or-RRT, and length-of-stay summaries.
6. A newly fitted transparent ridge-logistic baseline, a newly fitted six-hour nonlinear temporal model, and a prespecified urine/output ablation on the same support-defined population and locked subject-level split.
7. Held-out discrimination, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, uncertainty, grouped feature-family ablations, density/process controls, and timestamp/label negative controls.
8. decision.json linking every supportive, adverse, or inconclusive conclusion to named computed outputs and applying the frozen rules below.

No cohort size, event count, effect estimate, or model result is asserted in this proposal. The prior broad row-level audit was canceled without output; that cancellation is not evidence about support or the hypothesis.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The expert card references/expert-seeds/cards/mimic-03.md is explicitly an untested proposal about creatinine falling without urine-output or fluid-status improvement. It warns that measured kidney function is unavailable. The local Haines et al. paper, references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt and article.pdf, studied 38 mechanically ventilated patients and reported divergence between creatinine-based and cystatin-C/measured-GFR estimates during prolonged critical illness, with concurrent muscle loss. That supports biological plausibility that creatinine can misrepresent renal recovery in selected critical illness. It does not establish the prevalence, observation completeness, prognosis, or mechanism of Foley/creatinine discordance in MIMIC-IV.

The strongest claim available before this experiment is therefore: creatinine and measured kidney function can diverge during prolonged critical illness, and the divergence may relate to muscle loss in a small selected study. The unresolved claim tested here is narrower and falsifiable: within a precisely observed MIMIC population at a fixed 72-hour landmark, does a documented creatinine-recovery/recorded-Foley-nonrecovery phenotype predict later in-hospital death relative to documented recovery of both?

The computable claim, if supported, is only a conditional observed prognostic association in the support-defined MIMIC population. True GFR, muscle loss, complete Foley capture, catheter placement or recording intent, volume status, complete intake, KDIGO adjudication, treatment indication, causality, bedside utility, and external validity require other data, clinical review, or another cohort.

## Population, phenotype and outcomes

The unit is one row per hospitalization’s earliest valid ICU stay after Stage 1. Require adult age, valid interval, survival to t0, ICU presence at t0, and discharge/outcome support as specified above. Multiple hospitalizations can contribute for one subject, but all rows for a subject remain in one model split and bootstrap clusters by subject_id.

### Creatinine phenotype

Require at least one primary storage-available creatinine in every W0, W1, and W2 window. Define C0 as minimum W0 creatinine, C1 as maximum W1 creatinine, Cpeak as maximum of W0 and W1, and Clate as minimum W2 creatinine.

An observed early rise is C1 >= C0 + 0.3 mg/dL OR C1/C0 >= 1.5. Apparent recovery is Clate <= Cpeak - 0.3 mg/dL OR Clate/Cpeak <= 0.75. Preserve both absolute and relative components and continuous values. These rules are observed screens, not KDIGO AKI staging.

### Foley phenotype

Require a positive W0 weight from 226512, otherwise 224639, with charttime in W0 and primary storage support. If both are eligible at the same time, 226512 has priority; preserve source and timing. Weight normalizes urine but does not establish stable volume status.

Require in both W0 and W2: at least 18 distinct observed chart hours, first-to-last observed-hour span at least 20 hours, and maximum gap no more than 4 hours. Divide recorded Foley volume by the full 24-hour window and W0 weight, never by observed hours. Define early observed oliguria as W0 recorded volume < 0.5 mL/kg/hour; define W2 recorded-output recovery as >= 0.5 mL/kg/hour and nonrecovery as < 0.5. Dense observation is an auditability criterion, not evidence of complete Foley capture.

The primary support population requires early creatinine rise, apparent creatinine recovery, early observed oliguria, weight support, dense W0/W2 Foley support, and an observable post-landmark outcome. The two primary groups are discordant (creatinine recovered and W2 Foley output did not recover) and concordant (creatinine recovered and W2 Foley output recovered).

The census also reports all creatinine rise/recovery cells, lower-density support, no-early-oliguria rows, missing/late-weight rows, charttime-only rows, source-specific creatinine rows, and storage-lag strata. These are audit outputs, not post hoc replacements for the primary phenotype.

### Outcome and estimand

The primary outcome is all-cause in-hospital death with t0 < deathtime <= dischtime. deathtime supplies event timing; hospital_expire_flag is a consistency check. If deathtime is missing with flag 1, or dates are impossible or irreconcilable, the row is outcome-unknown for the primary analysis and the discrepancy is reported. No outcome is imputed from discharge disposition.

The primary estimand is the observed difference and ratio in probability of death before discharge between discordant and concordant groups in the support-defined survivor population. Report group risks, risk difference, and risk ratio with percentile bootstrap intervals clustered at subject level. The adjusted estimand is a standardized associational contrast from a ridge-logistic model containing group plus only covariates available through 48 hours: age, sex, first care unit/admission context, W0 weight, C0/C1/rise severity, W0 Foley amount and coverage, and pre-48 summaries, counts, and missingness for MAP, HR, RR, SpO2, and lactate. Do not adjust for W2 Foley, W2 density, Clate, post-t0 treatment, or outcome-derived variables. This is not a causal effect.

Secondary descriptive outcomes are first new qualifying RRT after t0 and before dischtime, using procedure items 225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809, and 225955 with starttime > t0; procedures beginning before t0 are not new. Later same-admission ICU readmission is a later icustays row for the same (subject_id, hadm_id) with later.intime > index.outtime and before discharge. Report death-or-RRT, readmission, and length of stay descriptively; none replaces the primary mortality estimand.

## Frozen baseline and substantive alternative

The method comparison addresses a separate linked scientific uncertainty: whether temporal output behavior and the output-observation process add clinically relevant prognostic information beyond a transparent creatinine-centered summary. It cannot rescue a null primary association.

The prediction population is frozen before group labels: all support-defined stays with observable post-t0 death outcome, including both eventual groups. Target: post-t0 in-hospital death. Split by SHA-256 of subject_id concatenated with renal-discordance-episode9 into 60% train, 20% validation, and 20% locked test using the first two hexadecimal digits (00–98, 99–cc, cd–ff); all admissions of a subject share a partition. Preprocessing and lambda/tree settings use training and validation only; the test set is read once.

Baseline: ridge logistic regression with one-hot categorical encoding and standardized continuous features. Inputs are age, sex, care/admission context, W0 weight, C0/C1/Cpeak/Clate, and pre-48 physiology summaries plus counts and missingness. It excludes all Foley amount, normalized output, output-density, storage-lag, and other urine/fluid-channel features. A fixed small lambda grid is selected by validation Brier score. This baseline is transparent and tests whether an output channel adds beyond creatinine and routine physiology.

Alternative: CPU histogram gradient-boosted trees on six-hour bins over [0,72h). For creatinine, Foley mL/hour, Foley mL/kg/hour, MAP, HR, RR, SpO2, and lactate, compute last, mean, minimum, maximum, within-bin slope when at least two times exist, count, missingness, and storage-lag summaries. Add ventilation flags from 225792/225794 and pre-landmark RRT flags. The full model includes output trajectory, normalized output, output density, and lag; the primary ablation removes all Foley amount/normalized-output features while retaining general physiology and generic observation counts. A process-only model uses Foley density, maximum gap, and storage lag without urine amount.

The alternative can reveal persistence, timing, nonlinear thresholds, within-window trajectory, and interactions that terminal creatinine summaries lose. Its scientific success is not a small AUROC increase: it must show calibrated, stable incremental information from the output channel and clarify whether the signal is biological-looking output behavior or documentation process. A sequence transformer, latent Gaussian process, and mechanistic fluid-balance model are deferred. A transformer/GP would add capacity without solving missing catheter intent and outcome adjudication; a fluid model would require complete intake/output, volume status, treatment indications, and reliable catheter capture, none of which the configured evidence establishes. Revisit only if the census shows adequate support/events and the tree’s residual temporal structure remains clinically important after process controls. The deferred branches and revisit evidence are recorded in method_decision.md.

For each fitted model report test AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, reliability bins, and subject-cluster bootstrap intervals, plus grouped output/creatinine/physiology/process ablations. Require at least 20 deaths and 20 nondeaths in both train and locked test and estimable calibration; otherwise the model comparison is inconclusive. Do not interpret a small predictive difference as clinical utility.

## Process controls, falsification and uncertainty

The measurement process is part of the scientific threat model. The primary analysis never adjusts for W2 density, because doing so could condition on the observed exposure process. Instead:

- report density, maximum gap, storage lag, source composition, and missingness by phenotype group;
- repeat the association under the prespecified 18-hour support threshold and the primary dense threshold, with density-stratified estimates where overlap exists;
- compare storage-available versus charttime-only support;
- compare combined, ICU-chart-only, and hospital-lab-only creatinine sources;
- report absolute-only, relative-only, combined, 0.5-threshold, and unweighted mL/hour sensitivities;
- compare admission versus daily weight and exclude any pre-t0 RRT;
- fit the process-only model and compare it with the output-amount model;
- permute Foley timestamps within stay while preserving values, counts, density, and windows;
- permute phenotype labels within prespecified creatinine-recovery and density strata as an implementation negative control;
- use pre-landmark Foley density and storage lag as process negative controls rather than treating them as biology;
- verify no feature, label, or outcome timestamp crosses the split or t0 boundary.

Supportive classification requires all of the following: at least 50 stays and 10 deaths per primary cell; valid outcome reconciliation; adjusted and unadjusted mortality contrasts higher in discordant stays with a 95% interval excluding the null; direction retained across dense/storage/source/threshold checks; the process-only and timestamp-permutation controls do not explain the contrast; and the temporal model shows stable calibrated information from urine amount beyond the baseline and beyond density-only features. The conclusion remains a conditional observed prognostic signal.

Adverse classification is a precise null or reversal under adequate paired support and events, especially if it persists under dense, storage-available support. Attenuation explained by source, coverage, or process controls is adverse to the biological discordance interpretation. A temporal model improvement without the prespecified discordant-versus-concordant mortality contrast is adverse for the renal-discordance hypothesis, not a rescue.

Inconclusive classification applies to inadequate paired support/events, irreconcilable outcome dates, differential observability that cannot be separated from phenotype, failed negative controls, leakage, unstable bootstrap/calibration, schema/item mismatch, or failure of the no-sampling resource contract. No threshold, cell minimum, or model rule can be relaxed after results are seen.

The verifier can check archive/member/schema/item bindings, joins, time bounds, duplicate rules, flow counts, feature construction, split, fit, uncertainty, falsification outputs, and whether conclusions cite computed files. It cannot establish true GFR, complete Foley capture, muscle loss, volume status, catheter intent, treatment indication, causality, bedside utility, or external validity. Those require cystatin C/measured GFR, clinical adjudication, richer intake/catheter data, prospective validation, or another cohort.

A reference execution that only establishes parser feasibility is not a completed scientific result.
