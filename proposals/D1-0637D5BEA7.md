## Prespecified falsification and interpretation gates

**Supportive for rescue:** adequate overlap and ESS; IPW broader encoding beats process in micro and macro log loss with macro upper 95% bound below zero in unbounded and at least two bounded variants. This did not occur.

**Further adverse:** no rescue, and bounded variants show worse broader-versus-process loss with clustered intervals excluding zero. This occurred for 12- and 6-hour variants, but not uniformly across all definitions.

**Unchanged:** no supportive variant and every absolute IPW-minus-unweighted macro shift is at most 0.005. This failed narrowly at 6 hours (+0.00563).

**Inconclusive:** clipping/overlap limitations, a material selection shift in any required definition, or discordance across definitions. This is the overall gate because selection is associated with receipt/hospital and the strictest bound crosses the shift threshold, although it does not reverse direction.

The evidence-supported advance is therefore precise: complete-case ascertainment is informative, yet measured weighting does not rescue exact or exhaustive broader care-plan encodings and strengthens the adverse direction in the stricter bounded cohorts. It does not prove missingness at random, absence of a clinical signal, or fitness failure for every local use.
