# Value-aware temporal documentation-state audit with placebo transitions and clustered robustness

## Purpose and substantive advance

This proposal is a substantive repair of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that candidate's frozen, target-independent denominator, strict physiology and exclusions, variant-specific first-stay freezing, five fixed landmarks, mutually exclusive overlap groups, and post-freeze care-plan classification. It does not reselect the population or use a target to define ventilation evidence.

The unresolved measurement question is whether the operational documentation state is less often positively supported, and more often ambiguous, in the `unbounded_only` group than in the groups retained by a shorter airway lookback. This is clinically important because denominator misclassification could distort any subsequent study of daily evaluation, SBT documentation, or care-plan receipt. The advance over the parent is a reproducible value-aware state machine, explicit negative/conflicting evidence, a future-window placebo transition analysis, component-level discordance, clustered uncertainty, hospital and landmark robustness, and quantified parser exclusions.

The strongest supported claim is limited: within this frozen population, shorter-lookback groups more often satisfy the specified structured documentation-state proxy than `unbounded_only` rows. The untested clinical claim is whether that proxy corresponds to actual active invasive ventilation at the bedside. These data cannot establish true ventilation, SBT delivery, care quality, causality, or benefit.

## Operational state machine

All timing uses clinical event offsets, not chart-entry timestamps.

* `respiratoryCare.csv.gz` supplies the last pre-L carried `airwaytype`, recent airway counts, and `ventstartoffset`/`ventendoffset` interval fields. Positive airway values are `oral ett`, `nasal ett`, `tracheostomy`, `double-lumen tube`, and `cricothyrotomy`; `no artificial airway` and `none` are negative; blanks and other values are ambiguous. The prior-vent fields are retained in the source scan but are not silently substituted for current interval status.
* `respiratoryCharting.csv.gz` treats `RT Vent On/Off` values `continued` and `start` as positive, `off` as negative, and other values as ambiguous. Separately, a value-bearing device row is recognized only when its label exactly (case-insensitively) equals one of the 18 labels locked by the parent: `Tidal Volume (set)`, `Mean Airway Pressure`, `Exhaled MV`, `Exhaled TV (machine)`, `Exhaled TV (patient)`, `Tidal Volume Observed (VT)`, `Exhaled Vt`, `Mechanical Ventilator Mode`, `Ventilator Support Mode`, `Ventilator Population`, `Pressure Support`, `Pressure Control`, `Adult Con Setting Set Vt`, `Adult Con Setting Set RR`, `Adult Con Pt/Vent MinuteVentil`, `Adult Con Pt/Vent Spont Rate`, `Adult Con Pt/Vent InspiratorTV`, and `Ventilator Checks`. Values blank, `none`, `null`, `na`, `n/a`, or `nan` are not value-bearing. `respchartoffset` is the clinical event time; `respchartentryoffset` is retained but not used as event time.
* `treatment.csv.gz` supplies invasive corroboration when `treatmentstring` contains mechanical ventilation but not non-invasive/noninvasive ventilation. NIV is retained as an alternative modality signal and is not invasive evidence.
* `note.csv.gz` supplies only timestamped `Intubation` and `Extubation` note-type components; note text is not read into the result or exported.

Present positive means an active interval at L, or value-bearing RT/device evidence plus invasive treatment, or an invasive carried airway plus value-bearing RT/device evidence. Present negative means RT `off` or a carried negative airway when no positive rule fires. Positive and negative evidence together is `conflicting`; otherwise neither is `ambiguous`.

The future placebo uses the same value-aware combinations, but all airway evidence is window-local to `[L,L+360)` rather than carrying a pre-L airway label. The interval component is evaluated at the endpoint `L+360`, matching the operational interval-at-time definition. This is a documentation placebo, not an outcome or a claim of continued ventilation. An additional clinical validation study should test interval coverage throughout the future window because endpoint status alone cannot establish duration.

## Results

### Present states

* `unbounded_only`: 579/1,148 positive (50.44%), 0 negative, 569 ambiguous (49.56%), 0 conflicting.
* `retained_24h_only`: 913/1,462 positive (62.45%), 4 negative (0.27%), 545 ambiguous (37.28%), 0 conflicting.
* `retained_6h`: 961/1,178 positive (81.58%), 7 negative (0.59%), 210 ambiguous (17.83%), 0 conflicting.

The positive risk differences are +12.01 percentage points for 24-hour-only versus unbounded-only and +31.14 points for 6-hour-retained versus unbounded-only. The corresponding ambiguous-state differences are -12.29 and -31.74 points.

### Future-window placebo and transitions

Future-local positive proportions were 142/1,148 (12.37%) for unbounded-only, 453/1,462 (30.98%) for 24-hour-only, and 528/1,178 (44.82%) for 6-hour-retained. Present-to-future transitions were:

* Unbounded-only: present positive to future positive 104 (9.06%), positive to ambiguous 474 (41.29%), positive to negative 1 (0.09%); present ambiguous to future positive 38 (3.31%), with 531 remaining ambiguous.
* Retained-24h-only: positive to future positive 348 (23.80%), positive to ambiguous 559 (38.24%), positive to negative 6 (0.41%); present ambiguous to future positive 105 (7.18%), with 438 remaining ambiguous.
* Retained-6h: positive to future positive 463 (39.30%), positive to ambiguous 489 (41.51%), positive to negative 8 (0.68%); present ambiguous to future positive 61 (5.18%), with 146 remaining ambiguous.

The future-minus-present positive differences are -31.46 points, -36.76 points, and -38.07 points for unbounded-only, 24-hour-only, and 6-hour-retained respectively. These declines primarily show the window-local and endpoint nature of the placebo documentation signal; they do not measure clinical discontinuation.

### Component discordance

Rows with at least one positive and at least one negative component were 4/1,148 (0.35%) in unbounded-only, 18/1,462 (1.23%) in 24-hour-only, and 39/1,178 (3.31%) in 6-hour-retained. Device-value components were present in 1,143 (99.56%), 1,457 (99.66%), and 1,171 (99.41%) rows respectively, while invasive-treatment corroboration was present in 128 (11.15%), 231 (15.80%), and 173 (14.69%). This contrast is why device-label presence alone is not treated as ventilation.

The component table retains carried airway, recent airway, interval, RT, device, invasive treatment, NIV, and intubation/extubation-note components separately. In target stays, no RT Vent On/Off record was observed; airway values and note-type counts are inventoried in the aggregate artifact. Sparse corroboration and absent RT values require semantic validation rather than optimistic interpretation.

### Uncertainty and robustness

Two-thousand-replicate bootstrap intervals were computed for stay-clustered and hospital-clustered risk differences. For present positivity:

* 24-hour-only minus unbounded-only: +12.01 points; stay-cluster 95% interval +7.53 to +16.49; hospital-cluster interval -20.01 to +28.91.
* 6-hour-retained minus unbounded-only: +31.14 points; stay-cluster interval +26.48 to +35.67; hospital-cluster interval +1.40 to +51.79.

For future-local positivity, the corresponding stay-cluster differences were +18.62 points (24-hour-only) and +32.45 points (6-hour-retained), with intervals +15.42 to +21.73 and +28.62 to +36.29; hospital-cluster intervals were +7.67 to +22.51 and +22.25 to +42.58 points.

At landmarks, the 6-hour-retained minus unbounded-only present-positive differences were +38.49, +29.23, +23.83, +34.90, and +33.58 points at 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes. The 1,440-minute unbounded-only cell is small (24 rows), so that stratum is unstable.

With at least 10 rows per group within a hospital, the equal-hospital mean differences were +3.07 points for 24-hour-only and +21.97 points for 6-hour-retained. Hospital-specific ranges were -16.67 to +23.08 and -13.70 to +55.53 points. Leave-one-hospital-out pooled ranges were -2.62 to +16.39 points for 24-hour-only and +14.55 to +36.65 points for 6-hour-retained. Thus the 24-hour contrast is not robust to hospital clustering or hospital omission, whereas the 6-hour direction persists in this sensitivity but remains heterogeneous.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

Supportive evidence for the stale-carry-forward measurement concern would be a lower positive-state and higher ambiguous-state proportion in unbounded-only, replicated across landmarks, with the 6-hour contrast persisting under hospital sensitivity and not being explained solely by a single site. The current computation is directionally supportive only for the operational proxy.

Adverse evidence would be similar positive-state proportions across variants, frequent explicit active intervals in unbounded-only, low ambiguity, or a reversal after exact value-aware classification. Frequent temporally adjacent positive and negative components would also challenge a simple carried-forward interpretation and should be retained as discordance, not deleted.

Results are inconclusive for clinical ventilation if hospital-cluster intervals are wide, effects are site-dependent, parser rejection is differential, or expert review shows that positive rules mainly capture device documentation. A result cannot be upgraded to clinical truth without review against respiratory flowsheets, tube/device placement and removal, validated event semantics, and an adjudicated ventilation episode gold standard. SBT receipt, care quality, causal effects, and patient benefit require separate outcomes, confounding control, and clinical adjudication.
