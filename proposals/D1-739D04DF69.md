# Result: locked 12–24-hour lactate-nonclearance landmark experiment is inconclusive because one overlap gate fails

## Locked question and strongest supported claim

This successor executes [prior hypothesis] without changing its population, deterministic t0/person selection, 24-hour landmark, lactate windows or tie rules, exposure, measured-overlap estimand, +10-percentage-point margin, outcome-blind site gate, covariates, overlap weighting, bootstrap, leave-site-out analysis, or falsification rules.

The available EICU evidence supports this bounded claim: in the frozen measured cohort of adults with an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed infusion row, baseline lactate at least 2 mmol/L, a qualifying 12–24-hour lactate, and hospital follow-up beyond t0+24 hours, late lactate nonclearance had a large positive overlap-weighted association with hospital mortality. The estimate was +27.15 percentage points (95% copied-hospital-then-copied-person percentile-bootstrap interval +20.13 to +33.91 points). However, the locked primary result is **inconclusive**, not supportive, because one fitted propensity was 0.01920, below the required 0.02 minimum. All other gates passed.

The unresolved tested claim therefore remains whether this association clears every prespecified reliability gate while excluding a risk difference smaller than +10 points. The experiment did not yield adverse or opposite evidence: its interval is wholly above +10 and zero, but a favorable estimate cannot override the failed gate. The stronger clinical claims—that nonclearance represents persistent tissue hypoperfusion, that it should trigger an intervention, or that acting on it improves outcome—were not tested.

## Population audit and outcome-blind site gate

The complete eICU snapshot was scanned without sampling. The frozen design says to map eICU age `"> 89"` to 90. The parent feasibility script instead attempted to parse that string as a float and excluded it. Correct execution found 1,506 measured people in 96 hospitals (983 clearance, 523 nonclearance). The unchanged exposure-count-only gate of at least five people in each group retained the same 33 hospitals and 1,229 people (788 clearance, 441 nonclearance), including 31 correctly mapped older adults.

As a code audit, excluding those age rows reproduces every parent artifact count exactly: 1,468 measured (957/511), 96 hospitals, 33 eligible hospitals, and 1,198 retained (764/434). This establishes that the difference is the parent parser defect rather than drift in source data or lactate logic. Hospital-discharge status was used before the gate only to enforce the specified set `{Alive, Expired}`; no death indicator or death count entered site eligibility. After the eligible-hospital list was serialized, there were 253 deaths among clearance patients and 254 among nonclearance patients.

Crude mortality was 32.11% for clearance and 57.60% for nonclearance (difference +25.49 points). These are descriptions of a measurement-selected landmark cohort, not population incidence.

These subgroup descriptions have no multiplicity-controlled inferential claim and do not alter the primary classification.

## What is and is not established

Computationally checkable and independently audited claims are source hashes/headers, exact IDs and one-person selection in the derived cohort, landmark and lactate windows, exposure recalculation, exposure-only site counts, outcome mapping, crude and weighted risks, ESS and weight shares, balance, bootstrap quantiles, leave-site-out summaries, gates, and classification.

The strongest justified conclusion is: a large positive adjusted prognostic association was estimated, but the locked experiment is formally inconclusive because overlap missed its lower bound by 0.000802 for one person. This does not justify trimming that person, rounding the propensity to 0.02, relaxing the gate, or promoting the aperiodic sensitivity.

EICU cannot adjudicate true norepinephrine administration or dose, treatment intent, arterial versus venous lactate, assay indication, fluids, beta-agonists, hepatic clearance, source control, organ support, treatment limitations, exact death time/cause, tissue perfusion, or whether acting on the marker helps. Those claims require clinical adjudication and a prospective decision-impact study or randomized/credible target-trial evidence with the missing treatment and physiologic variables.
