### Adverse

A supported atom is adverse if phase 2 finds candidate-`AUTO` compatibility with choice, completion/stop/replacement, unknown, or packet failure; candidate-`RECON` compatibility with no-action or prohibited uncertainty; a phase-1 false-safe pattern; route anchoring; decisive controls indistinguishable from nuisance; atom-specific adverse bounds beyond inherited margins; zero breakdown; or workload beyond capacity. A negative result is clinically useful because it prevents premature automation/reconciliation work and identifies where stored-text measurement fails.

### Inconclusive and fail closed
