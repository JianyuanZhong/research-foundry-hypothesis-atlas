## Decision, evidence boundary, and unresolved hypothesis

The decision remains whether an adult who is still invasively ventilated and markedly fluid-positive at ICU hour 48 after recent recorded vasopressor support and six recorded pressor-free hours should start or restart an ICU-recorded furosemide or bumetanide delivery during the next 12 hours while the linked ventilation interval remains open, versus avoid it. Earlier recorded airway liberation may matter, but creatinine rise, RRT, death, hospice discharge, or acute transfer could outweigh it.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is narrower. The inspected full XML of Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13154636; DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075; frozen source [source checksum]; [source checksum]) states that optimal timing, volume, duration, and phenotype-guided fluid removal remain uncertain. The inspected Europe PMC record for the 2025 ESICM guideline (PMID 40828463; DOI 10.1007/s00134-025-08058-x; source [source checksum]; [source checksum]) contains an abstract-level conditional recommendation for protocolized diuretic fluid removal after acute resuscitation and says evidence in specific cohorts is limited. The inspected full public HTML of the 65 Trial (PMCID PMC7064880; DOI 10.1001/jama.2020.0930; source [source checksum]; [source checksum]) used expected vasopressor duration of at least six hours in an older vasodilatory-hypotension trial, but that prospective judgment is unavailable here. These sources support importance and uncertainty, not efficacy, a validated six-hour retrospective phenotype, or exchangeability.

Episode 57 intended to test whether support survives restriction from any recent positive-duration pressor record to sustained recent support. Its principal duration rule instead clips all valid intervals to `[icu.intime,t0-6h]`, not the phenotype-defining recent window `(t0-24h,t0-6h]`. Its own treatment/outcome-blind audit shows this is material: among 764 broad recent/stable opportunities, 645 have at least six all-prior union hours but only 367 have six hours inside the recent window. Thus 278 (36.4% of all broad opportunities; 43.1% of all-prior six-hour passes) can pass the intended robustness test without six recent-window hours. This supports a population-definition defect only; it says nothing about treatment or outcomes.

The unresolved falsifiable hypothesis is: among every subject passing the Episode-56 primary eligibility, the A12 versus B12 recorded-policy association satisfies the inherited five-point airway-improvement and renal/terminal-harm criteria, and this conclusion is not materially reversed when the complete pipeline is rebuilt among subjects with at least six union hours of valid recorded pressor support within `(t0-24h,t0-6h]`.
