# Incremental preoperative value of routine HCC reports and laboratories for pathology-defined M2

## Clinical question, claims, and hypothesis

The decision target is a **preoperative M2-risk flag for multidisciplinary review**, not a treatment recommendation. The strongest evidence-supported claim is that microvascular invasion (MVI), particularly M2, is associated with postoperative recurrence, is definitively ascertained in the available record only from resection pathology, and that many preoperative models have weak external validation (Li et al., 2025, DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746; acquired full-text XML was inspected and its provenance is frozen in the parent support file). The HCC data have not yet shown that a model adds value.

The unresolved claim is: among adults proceeding to first liver resection for untreated HCC, does information in routine preoperative CT/MRI reports plus routine laboratories add clinically material M2 triage value beyond a compact baseline representing tumor burden and liver reserve under calendar-time shift?

**Primary hypothesis.** In a patient-disjoint 2023–2025 locked test cohort, the report-plus-lab extension will exceed the structured baseline by at least 0.01 net-benefit units at the prespecified risk threshold (p_t=0.30) (one additional net true-positive flag per 100 patients), with a paired 95% CI excluding zero; it will also be calibrated and outperform “flag all” and “flag none.” Pathology-defined M2 is the reference outcome. No recurrence, survival, or treatment-benefit claim is tested.

The substantive advance over the parent is an exact incremental estimand. Both models face the same patients, cutoff, outcome, model family, and threshold; the extension must improve decision utility beyond information clinicians routinely use to summarize tumor burden and hepatic reserve, rather than merely outperform prevalence.

## Target population and temporal lock

Unit: one patient (`Patient Master Index`), one index operation.

1. Identify the patient's first procedure with valid `Start Time` and a reviewed normalized `Surgery` value indicating partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resection. Freeze and emit all included and excluded procedure strings before model fitting. Exclude transplant, biopsy, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ resection, and obvious non-HCC operations.
2. Require age >=18 and same-encounter resection pathology that identifies HCC and exactly one MVI grade (M0, M1, or M2). Exclude positive-ungraded MVI, unknown grade, conflicting rows, mixed/non-HCC primaries, and missing pathology.
3. Define the computable preoperative decision time (t_0) as `index procedure start time - 24 hours`. Require at least one eligible liver CT or MRI with `examination.start time` in ([t_0-90 days,t_0)). Laboratories must have `test time` in ([t_0-30 days,t_0)). No record timestamped at or after (t_0) may be a predictor.
4. The primary untreated cohort excludes HCC-directed resection, transplantation, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, molecular therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during ([t_0-365 days,t_0)). A separately reported sensitivity cohort retains these patients and adds prespecified treatment-history indicators.
5. Development is index year <=2021; 2022 is used only to select penalties and fit a logistic recalibration map; 2023 through snapshot end 2025-11-11 is touched once as the locked test. Patients, including all their encounters, belong to one period only. Coefficients are estimated on development, preprocessing is development-only, and neither model, threshold, missingness strategy, vocabulary, nor extraction rules may change after 2022.
6. The primary analysis includes every eligible patient despite missing predictors, with development-median imputation plus explicit missingness indicators. Both models must produce predictions for the identical locked-test rows.

(t_0) is a conservative operational surrogate: actual tumor-board, consent, report-finalization, and operative-planning times are unavailable. The examination table timestamps acquisition, not report sign-off. Therefore automatic checks can establish that source timestamps precede (t_0), but only chart audit can establish that report content was truly available at the clinical decision.

## Outcome and adjudication

The sole primary outcome is (Y=1) for explicit same-encounter pathology-defined **M2** and (Y=0) for explicit M0 or M1. M2 phrases include `MVI risk grade: M2`, `M2 (high-risk group)`, and equivalents frozen in the label dictionary. Positive ungraded MVI is never a control. Pathology columns are used only after prediction and never enter features. There is no pathology timestamp, so linkage must use both encounter keys, and discordant same-encounter rows are excluded.

Before fitting, two clinically qualified Chinese-reading reviewers blinded to predictors independently adjudicate random samples of at least 100 each of parser-assigned M0, M1, and M2 plus every conflict; a third resolves disagreements. Each class must have PPV >=0.95 and sensitivity >=0.90, with Cohen's kappa reported. Two reviewers also adjudicate 200 imaging reports: maximum-diameter agreement within 5 mm or 10% (whichever is larger) in >=90%, tumor-count-category agreement >=90% and kappa >=0.80, and each added binary report feature PPV and sensitivity >=0.85. These gates require clinical review and cannot be certified from code alone.

## Baseline, extension, and operating rule

The two prespecified elastic-net logistic models are nested and use the same development/tuning protocol.

**Structured baseline (B):**

- case mix: age (`age`) and sex (`sex`);
- routine tumor burden from the latest eligible CT and latest eligible MRI reports: maximum lesion diameter (continuous), tumor count (1, 2, >=3), modality (CT only, MRI only, both), and explicit macrovascular tumor-thrombus language, each with unknown indicators;
- liver reserve/portal-hypertension proxies: last exact-assay albumin (`albumin`), total bilirubin (`total bilirubin`), INR (`international normalized ratio`), and platelet count (`platelet count`) before (t_0), each continuous with a missing indicator.

This is not called Child-Pugh or ALBI because ascites/encephalopathy reliability and laboratory units are insufficient.

**Report-plus-lab extension (E):** all (B) variables plus rule-extracted capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, and enhancement-pattern indicators; development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF from `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis`; and last exact-assay AFP (`Alpha-fetoprotein`), ALT (`Alanine aminotransferase`), AST (`Aspartate aminotransferase`), GGT (`γ-Glutamyl transferase`), ALP (`Alkaline phosphatase`), and creatinine (`Creatinine`), with missing indicators.

For imaging, combine latest eligible CT and MRI deterministically: maximum diameter is the maximum reported across modalities, count is the maximum category, and a binary finding is present if either report is positive, absent only if at least one report explicitly negates it and neither is positive, otherwise unknown. Strip identifiers, dates, accession numbers, pathology/specimen language, explicit `MVI`/`microvascular invasion`, and copied-pathology segments. Exclude examinations labeled pathology/specimen. Audit the 100 largest-magnitude extension n-grams before test unblinding; any postoperative or label proxy invalidates the run.

All continuous transforms, winsorization bounds, medians, TF-IDF vocabulary, penalties, and the 2022 calibration map are frozen. Because lab units are absent, do not use clinical cutoffs or combine differently named assays; report exact-assay/year distributions and repeat with within-assay/year ranks.

The operating rule is fixed before fitting: **flag if predicted M2 risk >=0.30**. At (p_t=0.30),
[
NB = TP/N - (FP/N){0.30/(1-0.30)}.
]
This threshold expresses a false-positive:false-negative harm ratio of 3:7 only for evaluation. Its acceptability requires multidisciplinary elicitation before clinical use. Sensitivity decision curves cover 0.20–0.40 but cannot replace the primary threshold.

## Estimands, analysis, and gates

The primary estimand is paired
[
Delta NB_{0.30}=NB_E(0.30)-NB_B(0.30)
]
in all eligible locked-test patients. Report 2,000 patient bootstrap 95% CIs for this and for each model versus flag-all and flag-none. Key secondary quantities are paired delta-AUPRC, AUROC, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, Brier score, calibration-in-the-large (intercept from (Y\sim1+offset(logit(p)))), and calibration slope (from (Y\sim1+logit(p))). Report calibration curves and threshold-specific observed versus predicted risk. Preserve the parent's delta-AUPRC target of >=0.08 and fixed-threshold PPV >=0.50 with sensitivity >=0.70 as supporting clinical-performance criteria.

The result is eligible for a supportive interpretation only if all gates pass:

- >=500 locked-test M2 outcomes and >=100 outcomes in each prespecified modality stratum;
- nonempty eligible report text >=80%, jointly extractable size/count >=70%, each baseline liver-reserve assay observed >=70%, and each extension assay observed >=60% in every period; no variable's missingness changes by >15 percentage points from development to test;
- >=90% of test patients have no more than one observed continuous predictor outside its development 0.5th–99.5th percentile; every test modality category has >=100 development patients; median fraction of report character 3–5-grams represented in the development vocabulary >=80%;
- label/report adjudication passes, every eligible row receives both predictions, leakage audits pass, and no single test year or modality has (Delta NB_{0.30}<0);
- extension calibration-slope 95% CI lies within 0.80–1.20 and calibration-intercept 95% CI within -0.10–0.10.

Also report results by sex, age <65/>=65, AFP observed/missing, CT only/MRI only/both, M0 versus M1 controls, and test year; these are precision/transport checks, not separate efficacy claims. Sensitivities are M2 versus M0, exact-template labels only, CT only, MRI only, no free text, within-assay/year rank labs, and the previously treated cohort.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** every gate passes; (Delta NB_{0.30}) is >=0.01 with paired lower 95% bound >0; extension net benefit exceeds flag-all and flag-none with lower bounds >0; delta-AUPRC is >=0.08 with lower bound >0; and PPV/sensitivity and calibration criteria pass. The strongest allowed conclusion is that timestamp-eligible routine reports and labs add temporally transportable M2-risk stratification beyond structured tumor burden/liver reserve and justify independent-center prospective validation.

**Adverse:** gates pass but the upper 95% bound for (Delta NB_{0.30}) is <0.01 (falsifying the prespecified material increment), extension net benefit is <= baseline or either default strategy, the upper bound for delta-AUPRC is <=0, or extension produces net harm in a prespecified modality/year. Conclude that the extension is not suitable for the proposed triage use; do not rescue it with development metrics or alternate thresholds.

**Inconclusive:** a gate fails, uncertainty spans material benefit and no benefit, calibration equivalence is not demonstrated, label/report audit fails, report availability cannot be substantiated, or drift/missingness prevents a transportable estimate. Report the estimates and reason without calling the hypothesis confirmed or refuted.

Even a supportive result does not establish that changing resection extent, adding neoadjuvant/adjuvant therapy, or issuing the flag improves recurrence, survival, quality of life, or harms. Those claims require threshold-preference elicitation, independent-center validation with report-finalization times, raw-image and pathology-protocol review, and a prospective impact or randomized treatment study. The dataset lacks raw images/slides, pathology sampling protocols, actual decision times, treatment intent, tumor-board decisions, reliable complete surveillance, death registry, patient-reported outcomes, and costs.

## Harbor verification contract

Computationally checkable: paths/hashes/columns; frozen dictionaries; joins and one-row-per-patient invariant; attrition and actual filtering; patient-disjoint splits; every feature timestamp before (t_0); absence of pathology/untimed/postoperative features; identical evaluation rows; frozen transformations/models/threshold; all gates, metrics, paired CIs, calibration and decision curves; and links from every numeric conclusion to machine-readable output.

Adversarial conclusion tests must reject: (1) correct calculations followed by a treatment or survival claim; (2) a supportive claim when a gate or criterion fails; (3) cherry-picking another threshold after adverse (p_t=0.30); and (4) calling an imprecise or adjudication-failed result negative. Injected pathology/post-(t_0) text must fail leakage tests, and an independent implementation must recompute labels, predictions, net benefit, and uncertainty from frozen row-level outputs.

Not automatically checkable: clinical truth of Chinese phrase interpretation, pathology sampling adequacy, actual report availability at the decision, acceptability of the 0.30 tradeoff, or benefit from model-guided care. These require the stated adjudication or another study.
