# A deterministic source-to-discharge anchor certificate for pulmonary handoff documentation

## Successor scope and unresolved question

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]`, with `[prior hypothesis]` retained as the executable parent protocol. It changes only the secondary documentary-anchor/actionability layer. The first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity denominator; reciprocal pre-discharge radiology chronology; canonical discharge summary; exact atom and route; sealed route-concealed C/S/H panels; label-independent matched controls; all-unit adverse precedence; positive-support two-phase whole-admission sampling; additive workload ledger; finite-population inference; and silent-validation-only conclusion ceiling remain frozen.

The unresolved question is not whether care occurred. It is whether a stored target instruction has a reproducible, source-linked documentary anchor that preserves the finding identity and exact temporal instruction in the canonical pre-discharge discharge summary, beyond what is seen for an atom-matched non-target control. The substantive repair is to make anchor eligibility and temporal adjacency computable before labels and to reserve human review for blinded verification of a finite candidate span, rather than asking readers to invent anchors or interpret document sections.

> **Hypothesis.** Within the frozen paired target/control domain, the target has a higher rate of jointly verified source-linked, finding-specific, temporally explicit discharge documentation than the matched control, and source/DS-span permutation controls do not reproduce that contrast.

A supportive result means only that the exact target is more specifically represented and traceable in stored text under this codebook. It is not delivery, viewing, responsibility, an order, referral, scheduling, completion, appropriateness, safety, benefit, causality, or clinical truth. A negative result blocks a silent-bridge nomination for this exact atom/route. An under-supported, ambiguous, or infeasible result is `DEFER`.

## Label-independent documentary-anchor candidate construction

### Global dictionary freeze

Before target selection and before labels, scan only eligible pre-D radiology and canonical-DS source text to create a finite, hashed dictionary of **literal** normalized token sequences. The dictionary is not fitted to H/K labels and does not use a language model, embeddings, spelling correction, external terminology, reader edits, or clinical synonym expansion. Normalize Unicode NFKC, lowercase, collapse whitespace, preserve original character offsets, and use a fixed punctuation/date policy. The dictionary contains:

* the inherited exact cluster, modality, recommendation, negation, uncertainty, historical, hypothetical, optional/conditional, screening/surveillance/staging, and no-follow-up lexicons;
* finite descriptor classes: anatomic/location, laterality, size/measurement, multiplicity, and finding qualifiers, each with exact token IDs and fixed maximum span length;
* action-verb and temporal-expression lexicons, with exact canonical `M` and `W` IDs; and
* a version/hash, row order, tie rules, and parser implementation identifier.

A descriptor is not inferred to be anatomically correct or clinically important. Generic tokens (`finding`, `lesion`, `nodule`, `opacity`, `follow-up`) alone never qualify as a descriptor. A candidate containing incompatible descriptor, modality, or interval parses is `ANCHOR_U`, not resolved by reader preference.

### Source candidate `S*`

For every frozen target or first-control unit, the parser emits zero or more candidate source anchors before labels. A candidate is a contiguous original-text span within that unit or its fixed linked report/addendum unit and has:

1. the packet's exact cluster `E` with a positive scope;
2. at least one descriptor token sequence from the frozen descriptor dictionary within the fixed window; and
3. no negation, historical, hypothetical, uncertain, optional/conditional, screening/surveillance/staging, or explicit no-follow-up scope over the cluster or descriptor.

The candidate key is `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id,field_ordinal,unit_ordinal,span_start,span_end)`. Report/addendum representations are deduplicated by the inherited unit key. Choose the first candidate by `(note charttime, note storetime, note_seq, note_id, field_ordinal, unit_ordinal, span_start, span_end)`; retain the full candidate list and a deterministic `source_candidate_count`. If no unique candidate remains, source status is `S_U` and no reader may create one. A target and control candidate cannot share a source span or inseparable unit.

### Discharge candidate `D*` and temporal candidate `T*`

Within the unique canonical DS, the parser emits a candidate discharge anchor only when one contiguous DS sentence/span, or adjacent detail fields joined solely by the fixed `field_ordinal` adjacency rule, contains:

1. the exact positive cluster `E`;
2. the exact modality ID `M`;
3. the exact interval/date ID `W`;
4. a fixed action verb in the same sentence/span or registered adjacent fields; and
5. the same literal descriptor token sequence as the selected `S*`, or a fixed literal cross-reference token sequence explicitly in the dictionary.

No pronoun/coreference, synonym, inferred section meaning, or cross-sentence completion is permitted. A temporal candidate is valid only if the exact `W` expression is in the same instruction span as the action verb and cluster; “follow up,” “as needed,” “outpatient evaluation,” and other vague expressions cannot qualify. For an exact date, normalized ISO identity is required; no date is inferred from admission or discharge time. The candidate key includes `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id,field_ordinal,ds_span_start,ds_span_end)`. If multiple candidates tie, choose the fixed minimum key only when the tied text and atom are identical; conflicting candidates yield `D_U/T_U`, not file-order selection.

The parser writes candidate reason codes before labels: `NO_SOURCE_CANDIDATE`, `MULTI_SOURCE_CONFLICT`, `NO_DS_CANDIDATE`, `MULTI_DS_CONFLICT`, `TEMPORAL_SCOPE_FAIL`, `DESCRIPTOR_SCOPE_FAIL`, `LINK_FAIL`, `POST_D`, and `VALID_CANDIDATE`. These are documentary states, not claims that care was absent or unsafe.

### Blinded verification outcome

Two fresh, roster-disjoint readers receive identical target/control packet layouts containing the fixed source span, fixed DS candidate span/context, frozen atom IDs, original provenance keys, and reason-code-independent text. The packet conceals target/control role, route, phase-1 labels, other outcomes, and all post-D fields. Readers verify, without searching outside the fixed windows:

* `K_S`: the displayed source span contains the exact positive `E` and descriptor under the frozen scope rule;
* `K_D`: the displayed DS span contains exact positive `E,M,W` plus the same permitted literal descriptor/cross-reference;
* `K_T`: the displayed action has exact `W` in the same instruction scope;
* `K_L`: both spans carry valid same-admission note identity and strict pre-D clocks; and
* `K_P`: optional literal `field_name`/`field_ordinal` availability, reported descriptively and never interpreted as a clinical section or workflow owner.

A reader may mark only `V+`, `V-`, or `V_U` per required component. `V+` requires agreement with the fixed candidate span; readers cannot expand, replace, or semantically reinterpret it. Any reader disagreement, missing span, unreadability, unresolved key/time, or ambiguity is `K_U`; a readable linked failure is `K-`. `K+` requires both readers to assign all required `K_S,K_D,K_T,K_L=V+` and the inherited jointly positive exact-handoff status `A=1`. `K_P=UNAVAILABLE` does not itself make K negative. For multiple target units, one K- or K_U controls the admission under inherited all-unit adverse precedence. H/K nonresponse is retained at original path weight and never complete-case deleted.

This split makes the actionability claim testable: code determines what can be verified and readers judge only the displayed evidence, not whether a clinical finding is true or whether a plan was appropriate.

## Paired estimand, controls, and falsification

Retain the parent's pre-label control universe: affirmative patient-specific non-target units from the same admission, exact canonical modality `M*` and exact interval/date `W*`, source-unit and cluster disjointness, fixed first-control ordering, no post-label replacement, and no control-only substitution. Let `S_TC` be the full-frame target-plus-control domain, fixed before labels. Report, without pooling:

* target and control exact-handoff rates `theta_T^A`, `theta_C^A` and paired `Delta_A`;
* target and control verified-anchor rates `theta_T^K`, `theta_C^K` and paired `Delta_K=theta_T^K-theta_C^K`;
* component rates and joint `(A,K)` states, including K_U/nonresponse and `K_P=UNAVAILABLE`; and
* all inherited route estimands and adverse all-unit indicators.

Use sequential inclusion probability `p_i=pi1_i q_i` with stored first- and second-order probabilities and paired HT/Hájek inference on `S_TC`. Undefined support or zero pair probability is `DEFER`; it cannot be repaired by another stratum, control, subgroup, or cross-admission join.

Before labels, generate two fixed, cryptographically seeded falsifiers from source-only pools. (1) **Source-descriptor permutation:** permute selected literal source descriptor spans among eligible same-admission units with a nonidentity derangement, leaving original DS and atom metadata fixed and marking packets non-clinical. (2) **DS-candidate permutation:** permute fixed DS candidate spans among eligible canonical DS fields from the same admission, requiring identical packet shape and a nonidentity derangement. The parser first certifies pool size, identity, and positive inclusion support. If a required pool has no valid derangement, its estimand is undefined and the anchor result is `DEFER`; no cross-admission permutation is allowed. Any K+ on a falsified packet is a documentary-specificity failure, not a clinical event.

Fix margins before labels: lower simultaneous one-sided 95% bound for `Delta_A >= 0.20`, lower simultaneous bound for `Delta_K >= 0.20`, upper simultaneous bound for each falsifier K+ rate `<= 0.05`, and upper simultaneous bound for target all-unit H/K failure `<= 0.10`. These are operational text-audit margins, not safety thresholds. The multiplicity family and max-statistic procedure are sealed before labels. A positive point estimate without its required bound is not supportive.

## Pre-label feasibility and workload certificate

The anchor layer must not be added after seeing labels or by average-time optimism. Before `PHASE1_LABELS_SEALED`, enumerate every finite phase-1 band partition and every allowed phase-2 occupied role subcell under the inherited allocation state machine. Using only source-derived target/control/candidate hashes and fixed strata, calculate componentwise maxima for:

* target units, controls, candidate verification packets, source/DS permutation packets, duplicate and calibration packets;
* two-reader active minutes, codebook and roster charges, reread/nonresponse charge, reviewer-hours, weekly minutes, and 32-week totals;
* distinct admissions, with overlap counted once; and
* `reserve_minutes = ceil(0.15 * (phase2_reading_minutes + fixed_controls_minutes))` and `charged_minutes`.

Use a synthetic-only fixture with generated texts and known candidate statuses to establish a finite `t_anchor_max_minutes_per_packet`, layout integrity, disagreement/re-read charge, and reader roster capacity. It must not use MIMIC text, real labels, observed times, or target outcomes. The target is selected only if every allowed partition has positive support for target, control, and required falsifier packets, `120 <= T <= 300`, target quota is within the inherited ceiling, `charged_minutes <= 84,000`, reviewer-hours `<=1,400`, and all inherited weekly/roster/deadline limits pass. Missing or unbounded constants, packet-layout failure, pool/derangement failure, or any failed partition yields `NO_SELECTION` or `DEFER` as pre-registered, never post-label rescue. The complete integer certificate and every partition result are retained in the manifest.

The added packets are charged once per distinct admission even when roles overlap. No unit sampling, replacement, post-label top-up, mean-time substitution, favorable subgroup, or additional target is permitted. The parent’s single charged 15% reserve is retained exactly.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive** requires all inherited gates, positive paired support, valid independent seals, exact target/control H and K processing, target-failure bound `<=0.10`, `Delta_A` and `Delta_K` lower bounds `>=0.20`, both falsifier upper bounds `<=0.05`, and a passing worst-case ledger. It supports only a target-cell-specific nomination for a separately approved prospective silent validation.

**Adverse** includes target H/K failure above the registered bound, `Delta_A` or `Delta_K` bound below zero, target no better than control, a falsifier ceiling failure, any target-unit minority omission, or any inherited route failure. It blocks that cell’s bridge priority but does not imply unsafe care, inappropriate care, or absent follow-up.

**Inconclusive/DEFER** includes `NO_SELECTION`, insufficient source-only support, undefined derangement or pair support, K_U/nonresponse, conflicting candidate spans, missing workflow evidence, unbounded intervals, failed synthetic rehearsal, roster/ledger/seal failure, or any parser/chronology/link invariant failure. K_U and missing detail placement are retained as uncertainty/availability states, never silently made K- or clinically adverse.

## Prospective evidence ceiling

A supportive result may nominate only a silent study whose outputs suppress alerts, orders, referrals, copied text, and patient-facing communication. Before exposure, that study must freeze target-cell-specific estimands and collect actual-calendar report availability/delivery/view logs with user role and time; accountable service/clinician; order, referral, scheduling, completion, and result-review events; outside care and loss-to-follow-up; patient preference/refusal; UI display/edit/copy/override; and independent adjudication of finding identity, recommendation appropriateness, timing, safety, and patient outcomes. Report availability, action, completion, safety, and clinical outcomes separately with right-censoring and unknown categories. Retrospective A, H, or K is never a surrogate. Missing responsibility, delivery/view ascertainment, outside care, or expert adjudication forces `DEFER`. Only a later governed comparative intervention study could assess utility, safety, equity, benefit, or causality.

## Falsification and verification contract

Fail closed on any post-label target/control/candidate modification; source-only dictionary drift; use of synonyms or inferred coreference; H/K reader-created spans; generic or vague temporal language scored positive; target/control source overlap; cross-admission permutations; missing or post-D timestamps; detail joins by field name alone; route or phase-1 leakage; reader roster overlap; omitted uncertain target units; complete-case deletion; undefined probabilities or zero denominators; omitted fixed controls or 15% reserve; duplicate admission counts; or conclusions about delivery, viewing, responsibility, orders, scheduling, completion, appropriateness, safety, benefit, or causality.

The compiler must emit per packet: source and DS provenance `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id,field_ordinal,charttime,storetime,D)`, original and normalized span offsets, exact `E/M/W` IDs, dictionary/parser hashes, candidate reason codes and counts, target/control/falsifier role hash, reader statuses and disagreement reasons, K component statuses, field-placement availability, inclusion probabilities, seed/permutation ID, and ledger role. The verifier independently reconstructs joins, chronology, canonical DS, target/control/candidate invariance under label permutation, exact span and scope rules, derangement support, all-unit precedence, HT/Hájek weights, simultaneous bounds, and worst-case workload partitions. Synthetic fixtures establish mechanics only; they cannot establish clinical truth or prospective safety.

Computationally checkable claims are source integrity, deterministic candidate membership, exact spans, chronology, blinding, support, probabilities, falsifier construction, margins, and ledger feasibility. Delivery, action, completion, appropriateness, safety, benefit, and causality remain prospective or expert-adjudication claims.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

This successor preserves the leading protocol while repairing its remaining documentary-actionability ambiguity. It freezes a global literal dictionary before target/labels, deterministically emits source and DS candidate spans with explicit temporal scope and conflict states, and uses blinded readers only to verify fixed candidates. It therefore distinguishes “exact text reproduced” from “source-linked and temporally anchored text” without turning document placement into workflow or responsibility. Same-admission permutation controls, finite pre-label support/workload certificates, and fail-closed missingness make the added claim falsifiable and executable. The unavoidable limitation remains that stored MIMIC text cannot establish who received, viewed, owned, ordered, scheduled, completed, or benefited from a recommendation; the only legitimate bridge is a separately governed prospective silent study with actual logs and independent clinical adjudication.
