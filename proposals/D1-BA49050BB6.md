# Joint patient-balanced availability–preference frontier for fixed-K documentary-M2 review

## 1. Successor scope and clinically important question

This is a targeted successor of [prior hypothesis] and [prior hypothesis]. It keeps the frozen HCC first-resection population, chronology-safe 12/24/48/72-hour states, documentary-M2 endpoint, whole-accession masking, source/pathology/reader uncertainty, locked comparator-closed models, fixed-K margins, singleton deletion, exact source bindings, and non-causal limits.

The remaining decision defect is that a single patient-visibility value (the inherited 80% contract) is not clinically identified by HCC. A useful decision question is not whether an arbitrary 80% cell passes. It is:

> Across every attainable patient-balanced level of effective report availability, for which stakeholder tradeoffs between a true documentary-M2 capture and an unnecessary review does the fixed-K semantic queue remain superior to its locked equal-capacity comparators and to no review?

The study will compute the complete finite-corpus availability–preference frontier. Availability is the minimum fraction of each patient’s eligible, source-clean whole accessions that are effectively visible; preference is an outcome-blind threshold p at which one false-positive review is exchanged against one true documentary-M2 capture. Neither is claimed to be observed in HCC. The frontier is the primary result; 80% is only a reported landmark.

This matters because a patient-level review queue can appear robust under a global accession quota while leaving one patient with no usable report. Conversely, a queue that works only at nearly complete availability or only for an extremely low burden tolerance is not ready for workflow evaluation. The experiment turns both deficiencies into explicit, falsifiable boundaries without pretending that retrospective data measure report release, workflow, treatment, or benefit.

## 2. Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and advance

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is documentary and structural: the frozen HCC snapshot contains patient/encounter-linked operative, examination, pathology, treatment-history, and narrative rows; examinations provide an acquisition-like start time, modality-like examination, narrative fields, machine field and accession-like examination number; same-encounter pathology provides narrative composites. The catalog and live headers contain no radiology authored/final/released/view times, report-version or predecessor chain, target-service readability flag, version-body hash, pathology specimen/slide/block identity, treatment assignment, review capacity, stakeholder preferences, or patient outcomes.

That evidence supports constructing a finite retrospective ranking experiment and auditing the missing operational evidence. It does not support that any eventual report was visible before a cutoff, that it was final or readable by a clinician, that an IN1 pathology terminal is biological M2, that a clinician acted, or that a patient benefited.

The unresolved Harbor claim is whether the locked semantic model has a nonempty, exact joint region in which:

1. all comparator-closed fixed-K margins exceed the prespecified 5-per-100-slot margin in both test years and after every allowed whole-patient singleton deletion;
2. the semantic top-K set beats capacity-matched random allocation; and
3. it has positive net benefit over no review for a nonzero, explicitly reported range of stakeholder preferences at some attainable patient-balanced availability level.

The substantive advance is a two-dimensional, threshold-independent decision boundary. It reports both the worst preference tolerated at each patient-balanced visibility level and the minimum visibility required for each preference. It therefore distinguishes maximum-content signal, availability brittleness, and preference brittleness, rather than selecting either a visibility threshold or a preference threshold after seeing outcomes.

The research-ambition materials were inspected only within their stated availability limits. The README identifies full local materials for the natural-history and Bayesian demonstrations and only a supplement for the cancer demonstration; the cancer main article and full STAR Methods are unavailable and are not treated as read. These demonstrations motivate longitudinal leakage control and uncertainty-aware validation, but they do not establish HCC report timing, biological M2, stakeholder preferences, or clinical benefit.

## 3. Hypothesis and one coherent finite-state estimand

### Hypothesis

For each of 2020 and 2021, the locked semantic model G has an exactly estimable joint support region
J_y = {(c,p): all required fixed-K margins and random-allocation gaps pass, and G has positive net benefit over no review at p}
over the full attainable patient-balanced availability lattice 0 <= c <= 1, and J_y contains at least one pair with p > 0. The study does not assert that either a particular c or a particular p is clinically acceptable. It tests whether this region exists, maps its exact boundary, and shows whether it collapses as availability falls or stakeholder burden tolerance becomes stricter.

A joint region is adverse if it is empty, if the all-content endpoint has no positive preference interval, or if the semantic model fails its locked comparator/random criteria even when all eligible content is exposed. A frontier with support only at high c is a valid result but is adverse for low-availability deployment; it is not silently converted into a pass/fail at 80%.

### One coherent state

Let a coherent finite state be
omega = (a,d, operation clock, imaging membership, prior-treatment membership, source state, pathology/form terminal, reader pair),
where a is 2020 or 2021, d is 12, 24, 48 or 72, source and pathology alternatives are jointly consistent, and one fixed radiologist/pathologist reader pair is used. A state includes all membership, chronology, source-cleanliness, pathology-terminal and reader choices needed to construct the same roster, outcomes, features and masks. No component of the state may be selected using test scores or the outcome.

For patient i in omega, let A_i(omega) be the set of eligible, nonblank, source-clean, whole CT/MRI accessions attached to that patient whose acquisition interval lies wholly in the inherited 90-day pre-cutoff window. A whole unit is
u = (patient master index, encounter number, examination number);
all exact-deduplicated component rows, source ordinals, raw bytes, field boundaries and reversible parser lineage stay indivisible. A blank accession is never assigned accession semantics. Source-unclean, contaminated, nonreversible and forced-unavailable units cannot be chosen visible.

For 0 <= c <= 1, define the patient-balanced availability family
E_P(c,omega) = {e:
sum over u in A_i(omega) of e_u >= ceil(c times |A_i(omega)|)
for every i with |A_i(omega)| > 0, and e_u in {0,1}}.
Patients with A_i empty remain in the outer eligible roster and receive the operationally unavailable report block; they are not dropped and impose no artificial report unit. The ceiling is applied separately for every patient. Thus a one-accession patient must have that unit visible for every c > 0, while c = 0 is a genuine no-availability endpoint. The mask is identical across B, R_clean, G_clean, G_clean_mask, reader pairs, contrast arms, and the paired undeleted/deleted analysis.

Effective visibility is a stored-content intervention, not an observed access event. e_u = 1 exposes the complete eventual unit; e_u = 0 replaces every report-body-derived surface, availability and semantic block with the development-defined operational-unavailable block, without refitting, recalibration or changing the score threshold. B is unchanged. The inherited version-faithful hash is computed from schema/parser version and length-delimited ordered tuples of source row ordinal, field name, exact raw bytes, and V/A/E parser lineage. An external audit may certify e_u = 1 only with a unique all-component crosswalk, pre-cutoff authenticated final/released/readable version, complete correction/addendum/retraction/merge/split chain, exact body-hash identity, timezone/clock provenance and archive-completeness evidence. HCC cannot observe these conditions.

For model M, year a, state omega, mask e, and deletion j in {none, each eligible patient}, let N_(a,omega,e,j) be the post-deletion binary-outcome roster, E_(a,omega,e,j) the count of documentary-M2 positives, K the frozen absolute review quota, and h_M(a,omega,e,j) the number of documentary-M2 positives in the exact top K under M. Require N >= K+1 in every confirmatory state. The fixed-K signal margins are
D_C(a,omega,e,j) = 100 times (h_G - h_C)/K, C in {B,R_clean,G_clean_mask}.
The random-allocation excess is
R(a,omega,e,j) = (N times h_G - K times E)/(K times N).
For a stakeholder preference p in [0,1), where p is a supplied harm-to-benefit exchange threshold and not a calibrated risk, the normalized net benefit of reviewing the G top K is
NB_G(p) = (h_G - K times p)/(N times (1-p)).
It is positive exactly when p < h_G/K.

Define the single finite-state lower envelopes:
D_C^y(c) = minimum over omega,j,e in E_P(c,omega) of D_C(a=y,omega,e,j),
R^y(c) = minimum over omega,j,e in E_P(c,omega) of R(a=y,omega,e,j),
P^y(c) = minimum over omega,j,e in E_P(c,omega) of h_G(a=y,omega,e,j)/K.

The primary joint estimand is the set
J_y = {(c,p): D_C^y(c) > 5 for every C, R^y(c) > 0, and 0 <= p < P^y(c)}.
The strict inequalities matter: equality at 5, equality at zero random excess, or p = P^y(c) does not support the claim. The joint result is the pair of boundary functions
P^y(c) and C^y(p) = minimum c such that (c,p) is in J_y,
with C^y(p) = infinity if no availability breakpoint supports p. Strict common two-year support uses the minimum across 2020 and 2021. Report attained witnesses, not only rounded curves.

Because E_P(c) is nested, the lower envelopes are monotone nondecreasing in c, but this is a property to verify, not a modeling assumption. The exact c lattice is the sorted union of 0, 1, and all attainable m/|A_i| across every retained state and deletion projection; the exact p boundary values are attainable h_G/K fractions. A global accession quota is retained only as a secondary diagnostic and must not define the joint estimand.

## 6. Exact computation, frontier construction, and falsification

Create a lossless finite ledger with protected patient hashes, year, cutoff, membership states, documentary outcome terminal, Z/S/Q/X blocks, source/pathology/form/terminal hashes, whole-unit component ordinals, HCC version-faithful hashes and raw backpointers. Retain the full outer roster, including patients with no clean eligible unit. Preserve all source rows and notes read-only.

For every year, cutoff, coherent source/pathology/reader world, fixed K, contrast and singleton deletion:

1. Fit only on the sealed development artifacts and score the sealed test ledger.
2. Verify the all-content endpoint e = 1, then enumerate or solve all masks in E_P(c,omega) at every exact availability breakpoint.
3. Use a certified exhaustive integer method (or a formally equivalent complete enumeration) to optimize h_G-h_C, N h_G-K E, and h_G under the same world, mask, roster and deletion for every arm.
4. Store integer numerator/denominator arithmetic, top-K ties resolved by the frozen SHA-256 protected patient key, the attained extremal mask/world/deletion/reader witness, and a solver certificate.
5. Replay every frontier crossing from raw integer counts: p < P^y(c) iff Kp < h_G, with equality failing; verify the inverse C^y(p) from the same lattice.

The computation must not use sampling, state caps, beam search, favorable incumbents, time-limited bounds, omitted reader books/worlds, post-outcome mask construction, global rather than per-patient ceilings, accession splitting, re-fitting after masking, pooled years, or rounded frontier decisions. Synthetic brute-force fixtures must cover zero units, one-accession patients, repeat-heavy concentration, equality at 5, equality at p = h/K, random equality N h_G = K E, empty/all-content regions, singleton deletion and every conclusion label. Real-data shard tests can validate code, but only the complete finite run decides the hypothesis.

Falsification is attained and local. Any one coordinate with D_C <= 5, R <= 0, or frontier P^y(c) = 0 at the relevant all-content or tested availability cell is reported with its year, cutoff, comparator, state, reader pair, mask and deletion witness. A complete run with all-content comparator/random failure falsifies the maximum-content signal. A complete run with all-content success but an empty or sharply restricted J_y falsifies the claim of a useful joint region and identifies availability/preference brittleness. A failure at low c does not erase support at higher c; the complete boundary is the scientific output.

## 7. Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

Emit one computational label in this order:

1. computationally_inconclusive: any source hash/header, roster/form/book, chronology, leakage, N/K/event/anchoring, fit, parser/hash, exact-optimization, deletion, or frontier-replay gate fails; any required state is missing or only bounded by a timeout.
2. maximum-content-signal-adverse: computation is complete, but all-content D_C or R fails in a required year/deletion state.
3. joint-frontier-empty: computation is complete and all-content signal gates pass, but no (c,p) with p > 0 satisfies the joint estimand.
4. joint-frontier-supportive: computation is complete, both years’ required all-content and frontier gates pass, and the common joint region contains a documented p > 0 interval at one or more exact patient-balanced availability levels.
5. joint-frontier-availability-brittle: the supportive region exists but only above disclosed high-availability breakpoints; this is a qualified secondary interpretation, not a new binary cutoff.

Supportive means only that the locked semantic ranking’s documentary-M2 capture survives the specified finite source/pathology/reader worlds, equal-K margins, patient-balanced stored-content masks and singleton deletions over the reported (c,p) region. It supports stakeholder preference elicitation and a same-snapshot operational archive audit. It does not establish calibrated risk, a clinically acceptable preference interval, report finality/timing, actual patient-level access, review capacity, workflow feasibility, clinician action, treatment effect, safety, recurrence, survival, cost, fairness, transportability or patient benefit.

Adverse means the stated joint claim is falsified at an attained witness or the useful region is empty/restricted. It does not prove that radiology semantics have no biological value, that reports were late, that another model would not work, or that no workflow could benefit. Inconclusive means the evidence or computation could not decide and must not be labeled negative or supportive.

A later external, outcome-blind stakeholder panel may supply a rational preference interval [p_L,p_U] tied to the actual action, review burden, consequences and decision population. It clears the computational frontier only if the exact interval lies strictly within the common P^y(c) boundary at a certified availability c, with no equality. If it straddles the boundary, report preference_heterogeneous; if p_L is at or above the boundary, report preference_range_adverse; if absent or methodologically incomplete, report preference_bridge_pending. HCC cannot populate or validate this interval.
