# Provisional hypothesis: fat distribution separates metabolic from functional/fall risk trajectories

Status: provisional and not yet validated. This is a research design, not an observed result.

## Proposed relationship
Among UK Biobank participants with a second assessment and complete body-composition measures, at similar total adiposity, higher visceral fat will be more specifically associated with subsequent metabolic outcomes, while higher posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) will be more specifically associated with subsequent functional decline or falls. The contrast is between a simple total-adiposity baseline and a prespecified composition-specific model.

## Falsifiable test
Define an incident-outcome window after the second assessment, excluding prevalent outcomes. Compare:
1. baseline: age, sex, total body fat/adiposity and measured confounders;
2. composition model: baseline plus visceral fat and posterior-thigh MFI, with prespecified interaction or separate outcome-specific contrasts.
Use held-out evaluation and uncertainty estimates. Support requires a reproducible, clinically meaningful divergence of component-specific associations beyond the baseline in the prespecified direction. Adverse evidence is no divergence, reversal, or instability across outcome definitions and held-out splits. Inconclusive evidence is imprecision, inadequate overlap, or insufficient incident events.
