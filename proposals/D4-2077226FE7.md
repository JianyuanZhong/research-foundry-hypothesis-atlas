For each repeat `r`, calculate participant-level paired loss differences `d_i,r = loss(M1)-loss(M0)` and average over participants; the primary estimate is the arithmetic mean of the ten repeat means. Never treat the 50 prediction records as 50 independent people.

Resample the `S_A` participants as clusters and carry all ten paired predictions for each sampled participant. For loss and mean-risk summaries, bootstrap the participant-level ten-repeat average. ## Gates and interpretation

A primary supportive result requires both Brier and log-loss differences to be negative, both Holm-adjusted p-values below .05 with finite successful uncertainty, no directional deterioration in held-out calibration that is statistically clear under the predeclared calibration comparisons, and a positive 10% selected-risk difference whose 95% interval excludes zero. Since this dataset contains no validated clinical minimum-loss or net-benefit margin, do not invent a claim of clinical importance: a result meeting these gates supports only protocol-specific incremental information in selected observed repeat attenders. Any calibration deterioration that is statistically clear but lacks a validated clinical margin is non-supportive; ambiguous calibration is inconclusive rather than silently acceptable.

An adverse result is a worsened primary loss, repeat-inconsistent sign, clear calibration deterioration, or failure of fixed-capacity concentration despite convergence. It falsifies the claim that `D` adds reproducible information under this model class, even if the parent coefficient or an in-sample likelihood-ratio test is positive. An inconclusive result is any source/count mismatch, invalid spline or solver failure, leakage assertion failure, insufficient bootstrap, extreme support/finite-prediction failure, or discordance that cannot distinguish a null from a practically relevant effect. A small non-significant improvement is not evidence of equivalence.

Computational verification can establish source hashes, joins, masks, equations, folds, training-only transformations, nesting, convergence, held-out losses, calibration arithmetic, capacity arithmetic, bootstrap behavior, and aggregate privacy. It cannot establish assay traceability, biological meaning of cystatin C, measured GFR, CKD or chronicity, missing-outcome mechanisms, causality, treatment benefit, clinical utility, or transport beyond the selected repeat-attender cohort. Those require adjudication, additional variables, external validation, or a prospective study.
