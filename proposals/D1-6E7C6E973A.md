# Route-concealed pulmonary-opportunity audit with a preregistered governance state machine

## Decision question, evidence ceiling, and substantive repair

The frozen decision remains whether an exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated only for a prospective, within-site **silent validation** of the inherited admission routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER`. No retrospective result authorizes an alert, copied recommendation, order, referral, scheduling, patient-facing communication, or other clinical action.

The available evidence supports construction of a first-eligible, all-opportunity admission frame from linked radiology/discharge artifacts, reciprocal chronology, service/ICU proxies, and stored timestamps. It cannot establish physical departure visibility, clinical appropriateness, responsibility, communication, patient preference, actual viewing, execution, follow-up, benefit, harm, or causality. The falsifiable claim is narrower: among support-qualified atoms, do independent route-concealed readings reproducibly identify an explicit target-linked discharge action, and do they reproducibly identify an explicit target-linked plan that supersedes it? This is a nomination-screen measurement, not a clinical gold standard.

This successor makes two implementation decisions explicit. (1) One challenged admission is the sole sampling unit at the nested stage; allocation is deterministic conditional on the already drawn admission sample, with no route- or label-dependent top-up. (2) Nomination and supersession are emitted by a frozen finite-state governance machine with published reason codes. Numeric tolerances below are measurement and quality-control operating tolerances only; they are not clinical harm thresholds and cannot be interpreted as acceptable risk.

## Fixed inherited draw and exact nested allocation

Construct `N_A`, the five inherited outcome-blind strata, and final pre-label atom `g=(possible-calendar class, terminal curr_service, workflow class)` before any packet or label. Preserve the inherited draw exactly: sentinel census; `S_ZERO` is the larger of 300 and the parent finite-population ±4-point formula; every other stratum is the larger of 120 and its parent ±7-point formula, capped at `N_h`; if the total exceeds 900, return `NO_SELECTION`. No top-up, replacement, adaptive allocation, atom merging, route-triggered verification, or complete-case substitution is allowed.

Let `I` be the set of distinct admissions in that frozen inherited sample and let `K` be the number of occupied final atoms in `I`. Set `m=min(300,|I|)`. The challenge is feasible only if `m>=2K`; otherwise write `challenge_status=INFEASIBLE_COVERAGE`, select no challenged admission, and force every actionability nomination to `DEFER`. If feasible, form proportional real-valued quotas `q_g=mN_g/sum_h N_h` from the frozen pre-label atom sizes (not from labels or routes), then impose the deterministic lower bound `l_g=2` for every occupied atom. Allocate the residual budget `m-sum_g l_g` in proportion to the residual demands `max(q_g-l_g,0)` by largest remainder; if all residual demands are zero, allocate the residual by largest remainder of the original `q_g`. Ties use fixed lexical atom key `g`. Set `a_g=l_g` plus its residual allocation. Record `N_g`, `q_g`, `l_g`, residual quotas, remainders, and final `a_g`. This gives exactly `sum_g a_g=m`, at least two admissions per occupied atom, and a fully deterministic proportional allocation subject to coverage. If `m<2K`, the preceding infeasibility rule applies. The allocation depends only on frozen pre-label frame sizes, never route, label, disagreement, or outcome.

Within each atom, use the first `a_g` admissions in a uniform permutation generated by sole protocol seed `ACTSUP-v1` (seed derivation includes snapshot hash, atom key, and admission key, not clinical text). The conditional challenge inclusion probability is `pi_chal(i|I)=a_g/m_g` for `i in g`; the unconditional probability is `pi_inh(i)*pi_chal(i|I)`, with inherited `pi_inh` retained rather than recomputed. Record first- and second-order probabilities (zero only for pairs made impossible by the fixed without-replacement design). If `|I|<300`, challenge all inherited admissions and report the power limitation; if any required probability is nonpositive or unrecordable, status is `INFEASIBLE_POSITIVITY` and all nominations are `DEFER`.

Route support is not manufactured by the atom allocation. For every atom-route claim, count candidate-route admissions and challenged-route admissions after all inherited labels and added labels are sealed. An atom-route may be nominated only if it has at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 challenged admissions for that route; if fewer than 10 challenged cases exist, the route-specific result is `INSUFFICIENT_ROUTE_CHALLENGE`, not a zero or a favorable estimate. The challenge remains valid for other routes and atoms. A support-qualified atom with no route meeting this requirement is `DEFER`.

## Auditable packet and workload accounting

Before labels, generate sealed, pseudonymized views from only the permitted raw extracts. Views contain no route, component abstraction, stratum, atom, weights, outcomes, post-`D` text, POE, or other reviewer output. Freeze packet hashes, codebook hashes, random order, roster membership, and seal order before review.

* `A1`: reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, permitted admission/death/disposition fields, canonical DS and permitted DS detail, explicit missingness and timestamps; records target, requested action, timing, recipient, responsibility language, polarity, and departure-artifact status.
* `A2`: independently rendered and ordered copy of the same evidence, read by a disjoint roster with independently written `CB-A2`; it never exposes A1 fields or parent labels.
* `S1`: the same allowed evidence in a disjoint rendering, retaining recommendation wording and target-linked plan spans but no route, abstraction, or A1/A2 output; its disjoint roster records the target-linked supersession hierarchy.
* `H3`: after append-only sealing of A1/A2/S1, a third disjoint roster reads the whole packet and records whether each asserted actionability or supersession field is supported, contradicted, or indeterminate by the supplied artifact. H3 is a measurement system, not an oracle.

A **whole-admission reading** is one reviewer opening one H3-style packet and completing all units for that admission in one uninterrupted assignment; it is counted once regardless of the number of radiology notes, addenda, DS fields, or recommendation units. The fixed primary workload is exactly `3m` whole-admission readings: one A1 assignment, one A2 assignment, and one H3 assignment per challenged admission. S1 is deliberately counted as a **control-packet/unit reading**, not silently hidden in the primary total: one S1 assignment per challenged admission contributes `m` to the control-packet total. Each locked control has one assignment per applicable disjoint roster, and each assignment is one control-packet reading even when its paired real packet is also read. The protocol is feasible only when `3m<=900` and the complete predeclared control roster has `C<=1,800` control-packet readings; all control assignments and any nonresponse re-reads are included in `C` before labels. For `m=300`, primary workload is exactly 900 and the control budget is 1,800 minus the measured S1 contribution and all other prespecified assignments. If either bound fails, write `workload_status=INFEASIBLE_PRELABEL`, do not omit S1 or controls, and force all actionability nominations to `DEFER`; no reviewer or case may be added after seeing labels. Record packet open/close times and physician-hour totals by atom as descriptive operational feasibility outputs, never as evidence of safety or appropriateness.

The three component rosters (A1, A2, S1) and H3 are pairwise disjoint. A reviewer may have no access to another roster’s packet output. A1/A2/S1 are sealed append-only before H3; routes are derived only after all labels are sealed by the inherited locked Cartesian-product compatibility table. Added labels can only block an atom or map to `RECON`/`DEFER` under inherited precedence.

## Exhaustive labels and the governance state machine

Actionability per recommendation unit is mutually exclusive and exhaustive: `A1_ACTIVE_PORTABLE` (explicit target-linked action plus timing/recipient, no explicit superseder, no contradiction); `A2_ACTIVE_RECONCILE` (active target-linked need but timing, recipient, responsibility, or plan incomplete/ambiguous); `A3_SUPERSEDED_EXPLICIT` (explicit target-linked alternative/replacement/changed interval supersedes the recommendation); `A4_RESOLVED_OR_INACTIVE` (explicitly resolved, cancelled, negative, or no longer applicable); `A5_NO_TARGETED_ACTION` (informational/non-actionable for discharge); and `A6_UNKNOWN` (missing, contradictory, or indeterminate). Supersession is `S0_NONE`, `S1_EXPLICIT_REPLACEMENT` (target-linked alternative plus replacement relation and timing/recipient), `S2_EXPLICIT_ALTERNATIVE_INCOMPLETE`, `S3_COMPETING_CONTRADICTORY_PLANS`, or `S4_UNKNOWN`. A3 dominates all other categories; S3 and S4 are adverse. Duplicate restatements are one unit; distinct target/action/timing units remain distinct. Uncertainty is a compatible set, never forced to a favorable label.

For each atom and route, execute this frozen state machine after labels and before any release decision.

1. `INPUT_CHECK`: if source, join, packet hash, seal-order, roster-disjointness, positivity, or workload checks fail, emit `DEFER/INPUT_INVALID`.
2. `COVERAGE_CHECK`: if challenge coverage or route support is insufficient, emit `DEFER/COVERAGE_INSUFFICIENT`.
3. `CONTROL_CHECK`: any invariant-control flip or unlisted decisive-control transition emits `DEFER/CONTROL_FAIL` for the affected run; no real-case result can rescue it.
4. `EVIDENCE_CHECK`: if any adverse-compatible panel output is A3, S2, S3, S4, or A6, emit `DEFER/ADVERSE_COMPATIBLE_UNCERTAINTY`; if every compatible output is A4 or A5, emit `DEFER/NO_ACTIVE_TARGET`; otherwise continue.
5. `GOVERNANCE_CHECK`: emit `NOMINATE_SILENT_VALIDATION` only if the inherited route is `AUTO`, every compatible output is `A1_ACTIVE_PORTABLE`, and every compatible supersession output is `S0_NONE`. An active but incomplete unit emits `DEFER/RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`; an explicit replacement emits `DEFER/EXPLICIT_SUPERSESSION`; any inherited `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` route emits `DEFER/ROUTE_NOT_AUTO` unless a separately preapproved silent-validation protocol explicitly accepts that route. This is a governance scope rule, not a harm or benefit threshold.
6. `RELEASE_CHECK`: even a nominal pass is released only as a versioned list of exact atom signatures and the statement “eligible for prospective silent validation review.” Any missing gate, unresolved compatible set, or disagreement emits `DEFER`.

The state machine is auditable because every non-nomination has exactly one first-failing reason code, plus all later failing codes; precedence is frozen as above. It is conservative and monotone: adding contradictory or missing evidence cannot turn a `DEFER` into a nomination. Supersession is not a numerical “harm” criterion: an explicit replacement always blocks the earlier unit because it changes the observable target-plan state, while the protocol makes no claim about whether either plan was medically right.

## Inference, controls, gates, and falsification

For each final atom and route, report design-weighted unit and admission estimates, all-unit worst-admission estimates, capture, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, route discordance, actionability state, explicit supersession, workload, missingness, and first-failing reason codes. Use exact finite-population inversion for binary outcomes and 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates with all admission-level labels retained together. Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over inherited gates, atom gates, panel discordance, actionability, supersession, controls, workload, and minimax endpoints. Primary intervals are adverse-compatible: only jointly observed support is in a lower endpoint; every unresolved/disagreement pattern is assigned to the adverse state in the upper endpoint. This is observed-label partial identification, not latent shared-error estimation. Latent-class or kappa analyses are diagnostics only and cannot narrow bounds or certify.

Create before labels, for every feasible challenged case: a route-irrelevant formatting/section-order/nuisance-token pair; a one-field route-decisive replacement or timing pair; a masked/inserted recommendation or plan pair with a frozen compatibility-set transition; and a target-linked versus non-target-linked plan pair matched for wording and provenance. Reviewers are blinded to control type and expected answer. Any invariant flip or unlisted decisive change falsifies the affected run; controls cannot rescue a real-case failure.

The following are frozen nomination-screen operating gates, not clinical harm thresholds: inherited support/event/effective-sample-size/weight/simultaneous-bound/route/workload gates; at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 challenged admissions for a route when feasible; lower simultaneous A1/A2/H3 agreement at least 0.90; upper parent-versus-actionability-route discordance at most 0.05 for AUTO/TIMING and 0.10 for RECON/DEFER; upper adverse-compatible active-but-superseded-or-unknown mass at most 0.05 for AUTO and 0.10 for RECON; upper invariant-control flip at most 0.02 and decisive-control violation at most 0.05; packet/nonresponse/control-infeasibility upper bounds below 0.05 unless the atom is censused; and workload pass. Because this successor’s state machine scopes nomination to AUTO, the RECON tolerance is reported as a diagnostic only and can never authorize nomination. A supported AUTO atom with any adverse-compatible unresolved or superseded unit is blocked. Rare, novel, unknown, and residual atoms default to DEFER. No failing atom can be rescued by pooled or neighboring atoms, macro-service grouping, sentinel performance, favorable consensus, imputation, shrinkage, latent-class estimates, or a negative control.

Mandatory fixtures include shared-wrong labels (high agreement must not narrow adverse-compatible actionability), pooled-safe/domain-unsafe, route-invariant nuisance perturbation, one-field decisive perturbation, missing high-weight labels widening bounds, unsupported signatures mapping to DEFER, latent-class nonidentifiability leaving primary bounds unchanged, duplicate-restatement versus distinct-unit precedence, allocation with `K=1`, `K=150`, `K>150`, `|I|<300`, and tied largest remainders, plus a control roster whose exact count is 1,800 or 1,801. Verify source/schema hashes, catalog counts, joins, reciprocal links, `D`, clocks, Y/V/H/Z, atom construction, fixed draws, inclusion probabilities, packet/codebook hashes, roster disjointness, seal order, exact `3m` primary count, exact `C` control count, and first-failing reason-code precedence. Route-before-seal execution, packet leakage, roster overlap, post-label control creation, favorable unknown assignment, prohibited POE use, omitted S1 accounting, or workload overflow invalidates the run.

## Interpretation and remaining uncertainty

Supportive results require every inherited gate and every applicable state-machine gate for each stated atom and support only prospective silent-validation review of those exact observable signatures, with all others forced to DEFER. Adverse results exclude the affected atom from nomination when a measurement gate crosses its preregistered operating tolerance or a control falsifies invariance; they do not prove clinical harm. Inconclusive results include sparse challenge coverage, infeasible allocation, infeasible controls, nonresponse, broken seals, wide bounds, unmet positivity, or workload overflow; they are not evidence of safety.

The substantive advance is a reproducible governance audit that separates “textually active and non-superseded” from any claim of medical correctness, and that makes the nomination decision traceable to a finite state machine rather than an informal threshold interpretation. It also makes the nested sample and workload exactly computable: one admission is one nested unit; `sum a_g=m`; `3m` is the explicit whole-admission primary count; S1 and every control assignment are included in a separately auditable control-packet count; and infeasibility is a prespecified DEFER outcome rather than an adaptive relaxation.

MIMIC cannot establish actual viewing, signature/finalization semantics, physical departure visibility, communication, responsibility, patient preference, outside plans, order/referral execution, scheduling, appropriateness, follow-up, malignancy, harm, utility, benefit, or forward/external transport. A passing retrospective audit therefore requires a separately approved prospective actual-calendar silent bridge measuring viewing, edits, signatures, transmission, responsible team, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside plans, and outcomes before any live, safety, utility, or causal claim.
