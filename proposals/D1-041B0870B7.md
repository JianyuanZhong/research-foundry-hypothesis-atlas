# Residual instability before ICU discharge (MIMIC-IV, episode 20)

## Unresolved question
Among adults clinically judged ready for ICU discharge (stable first ICU stay ending in ward transfer, not death), does residual physiologic instability over a prespecified pre-discharge window — persistently high oxygen requirement (FiO2/O2 flow) and/or vital-sign variability (HR/RR/MAP/SpO2) — predict **unplanned ICU readmission or ward deterioration/death** *beyond* a snapshot severity score at the discharge decision, or is any signal explained by (R2) underlying severity/ICU-LOS or (R3) observation/charting density?

## Evidence vs. untested claim
- Supported (K1, Kramer 2012): readmission is common (~6.1%/229,375 first admissions) and snapshot "physiologic abnormalities at ICU discharge" + ICU LOS are high-OR risk factors; readmission ~4x mortality. => snapshot physiology carries risk. **This is the baseline to beat.**
- Supported (K2, Aksu 2026): aggregate physiology (NEWS) carries prognostic signal for 30-day adverse outcomes [K2].
- Conjecture to test: a **within-window dynamic** instability signal adds discrimination beyond the snapshot score at discharge, and is not merely severity or charting density.
- Rival R3 grounded by K3 (Shaw 2026): monitoring/charting intensity is workflow-/documentation-linked, so measured instability may partly reflect how often vitals were charted.

## Rivals & observable predictions
- **R1 (causal residual instability):** dynamic instability adds AUC/AIC beyond snapshot score; remains ~unchanged after adjusting for charting density; dose-response with severity of instability.
- **R2 (severity/LOS only):** increment vanishes after adding last-24h snapshot score + ICU LOS.
- **R3 (charting-density artifact):** increment shrinks/vanishes once events-per-hour (observation density) is included; instability correlates with number of charted measurements.
Compare: all models share the same split and the same outcome; only the predictor set changes.

## Design (computable Harbor experiment)
**Population:** adult (age>=18) first ICU stay per hadm_id in `icu/icustays`, discharged **alive** to a ward (last_careunit != death), via `hosp/transfers`. Exclude stays ending in death, second+ stays of a hospitalization, and stays <6h. Episode time anchor = `icustays.outtime` (ICU discharge decision). Need `hosp/patients` (anchor_age/dod) for age.

**Prespecified window:** last 24h before outtime: `[outtime-24h, outtime]`.

**Variables (exact itemids verified from `mimic-iv-3.1/icu/d_items.csv.gz`):**
- HR `220045`; RR `220210` (also `224690` total); SpO2 `220277`; MAP `220052` (arterial) and `220181` (NBP mean); FiO2 `223835` (Inspired O2 Fraction); O2 flow `223834`, plus `227287` (additional cannula), `227582` (BiPap O2 flow). Values from `icu/chartevents` (`charttime`,`valuenum`,`stay_id`).
- Snapshot severity baseline: last-24h SOFA-style components (RR/SpO2/MAP + labs) + ICU LOS. Labs from `hosp/labevents` (`itemid`,`charttime`,`valuenum`,`hadm_id`).

**Outcome (primary):** unplanned ICU readmission = a subsequent `icu/icustays` (or ICU `hosp/transfers` eventtype ICU) after ward discharge within the same hadm_id; secondary = ward deterioration/death (post-ICU in-hospital death via `hosp/admissions.discharge_location`/`deathtime`). **Prevalence NOT measured in discovery (unobserved).**

## Baseline vs. substantive alternative (same split, same outcome)
- **Baseline (simple):** logistic model on snapshot score (last-24h SOFA-style + LOS) only.
- **Alternative (dynamic trajectory/variability):** per-signal pre-discharge features from the same vitals/O2 rows — mean/trend slope, coefficient of variation, and O2-requirement trajectory (FiO2, O2 flow) — added to the baseline. The snapshot loses *within-window dynamics*: how unstable/fluctuating and how fast O2 need is rising; the dynamic model reveals whether fluctuation per se, not just level, predicts deterioration.
- **R3 control:** observation density = charted events/hour per stay in the window, added as a covariate; refit and compare increment.

**Split/eval:** patient-level (subject_id) train/test split (e.g., 70/30) to prevent leakage; compare AUC (DeLong), calibration, and AIC; report bias-corrected CIs.

## Falsification (prespecified)
- **Supportive:** dynamic features add discrimination/association beyond baseline (ΔAUC>0 with CI excluding 0, or OR>1 significant) AND increment robust to observation-density adjustment.
- **Adverse:** no increment over baseline, OR increment explained by charting density (collapses after adjustment).
- **Inconclusive:** wide CI / power gate failure (event count below prespecified minimum), i.e. cannot distinguish R1 from R2/R3.

## Honest limitations
No cohort outcome rows analysed in discovery; prevalence/power unobserved. Faltering: unverified items (e.g. exact ventilator-mode itemids) recorded as limitations. Ward-deterioration/death needs an external definition. Causal (R1) claims require design beyond this observational model (confounding, treatment limitations, competing risk of death) and expert/clinical adjudication.
