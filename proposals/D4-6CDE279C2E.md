# Episode 29: a muscle-mass challenger for the frozen incremental-grip experiment

## Scope and clinical decision

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid, executable
`[prior hypothesis]`. The parent is controlling for every
primary design object: O1_hold=2,334, n1=466, the baseline-development-fitted
A1 and AG1 scores and rosters, visit-1 H1 primary estimand, D1 concordance
gate, low-UACR nine-year N18 secondary layer, shared-person missing-label
bounds, bootstrap and multiplicity, hierarchy, and all noncausal limits.

The only new object is a prespecified, non-rescuing clinical qualification:
whether the apparent grip increment is unique relative to a direct muscle-mass
alternative that is physically present in UK Biobank. This is important because
grip is being justified as a cheap proxy for altered creatinine generation. If
an equal-capacity rule using whole-body fat-free mass performs at least as well,
a positive AG1:A1 result would not identify a grip-specific reason for
prospective evaluation. If AG1 materially outperforms the fat-free-mass
challenger, the case for evaluating grip as the simpler bedside measurement is
stronger, while still not proving benefit.

The new question is therefore:

> After the frozen parent AG1 and A1 rules have been evaluated, does the frozen
> AG1 rule have a material H1 and directionally concordant D1 advantage over an
> independently baseline-developed A1-plus-fat-free-mass rule (AF1), at the
> same n1=466 capacity and in the same O1_hold population?

This does not alter or replace the primary AG1-versus-A1 hypothesis. It tests
whether any parent-positive increment is specifically attributable to grip
rather than to a more direct body-composition signal.

## What is supported and what remains unresolved

The parent evidence supports that creatinine-cystatin discordance is
predictable from participant characteristics, that cystatin-based
reclassification can carry prognostic information in low albuminuria, and
that the exact UKB join, holdout, frozen A1 comparator, AG1/A1 rosters and
near-complete visit-1 cystatin labels are computationally feasible. It does
not support a result for AG1 versus A1, a result for AF1, measured-GFR
accuracy, a muscle mechanism, or clinical benefit.

The unresolved parent claim is that adding grip to age, sex, BMI, eGFRcr and
UACR materially improves fixed-capacity targeting of visit-1 combined-eGFR
reclassification. The new unresolved qualification is whether that improvement
is unique to grip when the same information set instead contains whole-body
fat-free mass. No selected-list H1, D1, N18 or death outcome has been opened in
this investigation.

Public UK Biobank Showcase metadata describes field 23101 as whole-body
fat-free mass, a continuous impedance-derived kilogram measure. This
description is a rationale for the challenger, not evidence that fat-free
mass is a validated clinical kidney-allocation rule. The three demonstration
papers were treated as references for ambition and rigor; the cancer paper's
main article and full STAR Methods were not available and are not claimed to
have been inspected.

## New AF1 challenger

AF1 is fit only after locking the parent objects, and cannot feed back into
population construction, A1/AG1 fitting, parent scores, parent rosters,
primary outcomes, or parent labels.

1. Use the exact parent baseline development participants and baseline H labels.
   AF1 has the parent A feature vector plus one and only one additional
   covariate: whole-body fat-free mass `23101-0.0), positive finite in kg.
   Standardize this covariate using moments calculated within baseline
   development after a prespecified development-only fallback: if unavailable,
   replace it by the positive-finite development median. No H, cystatin,
   visit-1 outcome, event, death or roster membership may determine this
   fallback. Emit the raw availability and fallback counts.

2. Apply the same transform convention as grip's single added term: the
   development-standardized AF1 fat-free-mass covariate is linear, with no
   interaction, missingness indicator, cystatin, future field or new
   subgroup. Use the same five eid-hash folds, 21-value ridge grid,
   one-standard-error choice, binary64 L-BFGS settings and tie rule as the
   parent. This is a new fit, not a claim that AF1 is clinically validated.

3. Apply AF1 to every O1_hold participant using `23101-1.0), with unavailable
   or nonpositive/nonfinite values replaced by the locked development median.
   Rank descending AF1 probability, then ascending full SHA-256 of
   `"ukb-grip-cys-tie-v3|"+eid`, then eid only on digest collision, and
   select exactly 466. Generate and authenticate a new AF1 ranked roster; do
   not change the parent A1 or AG1 files. If the field is absent, duplicated,
   nonnumeric, or its fit/roster is not reproducible, the qualification is
   inconclusive/noncomputable, never supportive or adverse.

4. With fixed rosters and fixed denominator 466, define
   Q_H=Y_H(AG1)-Y_H(AF1) and Q_D=Y_D(AG1)-Y_D(AF1), using exactly the
   parent's H1/D1 definitions and the same 2,000 bootstrap draws. Use a
   shared unknown label for each person missing H1 or D1 in either compared
   list; for c_i=I(AG1)-I(AF1), use missing contributions
   min(0,c_i), max(0,c_i), and exact ratio-free bounds. Never independently
   assign a shared missing person. Use a new multiplicity family containing
   Q_H and Q_D only, with the parent's studentized max-t and its declared
   Bonferroni percentile fallback. This family does not silently change any
   parent family.

The qualification is labelled only after all source, fit, roster, missingness,
count, bootstrap and integrity gates pass:

- `grip_unique_over_FFM_supportive`: the one-sided simultaneous lower limit
  for Q_H is strictly > +2/1,000 and the one-sided simultaneous lower limit
  for Q_D is strictly >0. This is evidence of a material advantage over this
  fixed, simple FFM challenger, not proof of patient benefit or mechanism.

- `grip_not_materially_unique_over_FFM_adverse`: the one-sided simultaneous
  upper limit for Q_H is <= +2/1,000 and the one-sided simultaneous upper limit
  for Q_D is <=0. This means the study cannot support a material AG1 advantage
  over AF1 within the prespecified margins; it does not prove AF1 is
  clinically superior. Equality is adverse here only because the qualification
  asks whether a material unique advantage has been demonstrated; it does not
  alter the parent equality-as-inconclusive rules.

- `grip_FFM_qualification_inconclusive`: every other result, including
  sparse labels, excess missingness, unstable fit or roster, a limit crossing
  either boundary, or any noncomputability. Inconclusive does not mean
  equivalence.

Use the parent anti-vacuity checks (at least 40 observed H1 labels in each
parent list, at least 20 labeled directional swaps where applicable, <1%
missing H1 labels, n1>=400, finite positive uncertainty, and no feasible
zero denominator) for the new AG1-AF1 comparison, and report AF1/AG1 overlap,
directional swaps, observed labels, fallback counts and all bounds. A large
AF1 missingness rate is a feasibility limitation, not evidence against grip.

## Hierarchy and interpretation

The parent visit-1 AG1:A1 label remains the primary decision. The parent
low-UACR N18 layer remains secondary and non-rescuing. The new AF1 label is a
qualification after the parent biochemical hierarchy: AF1 cannot rescue a
parent adverse or inconclusive result, and a parent-positive result is not
called grip-specific unless the new qualification is supportive. A parent
supportive plus AF1-adverse result means that the incremental target yield is
not materially unique to grip; a prospective study could instead evaluate
the body-composition alternative, or compare both, subject to workflow review.
A parent and AF1 supportive result strengthens the case for a prospective
head-to-head testing-strategy study of grip versus AF1, but not for routine
adoption. All other parent/AF1 combinations remain unresolved rather than
manufacturing a decision.

Supportive, adverse and inconclusive outputs are linked to computed numerators,
fixed-denominator yields, bounds, point estimates, confidence limits, fit
diagnostics, and authenticated roster hashes. Automatic verification can check
source identities, field presence, joins, development-only fitting, transforms,
fallback isolation, AF1 scores/roster, fixed-denominator arithmetic, shared
missing-label bounds, bootstrap seeds, multiplicity and whether the text
follows the output. It cannot establish measured GFR, persistent CKD,
sarcopenia, muscle mechanism, clinical superiority of AF1, complete outcome
capture, specimen chronology, clinical actions, cost, safety, fairness,
transportability or patient benefit. Those require repeated clinically timed
biomarkers, measured GFR, complete outpatient/kidney-replacement and censoring
data, chart/nephrologist adjudication, workflow/cost/harms/equity review,
external validation and a prospective implementation or randomized
testing-strategy study.
