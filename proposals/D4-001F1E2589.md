# UKB 23: Do changes in blood cells indicate occult blood loss or hematopoietic abnormalities?

Admission: feasibility_required. Verify participant overlap, measurement dates, missingness and event support before committing this design.

## Hypotheses to Be Tested

A hemoglobin decrease accompanied by elevated platelets and a hemoglobin decrease accompanied by abnormalities in multiple cell lines are more strongly associated with subsequent colorectal cancer and hematologic malignancies, respectively.

## Study population and key data

Individuals with repeat blood counts and no cancer at the second blood draw; cancer registry and inflammatory markers.

## Basic Approach

Prespecify a small number of blood cell change patterns, compare different cancer types and time windows starting from the second blood draw, and account for infection and known causes of anemia.

## Significance of the Research

Use disease divergence to test different explanations for the same anemia phenotype.

## Main Challenges

Iron deficiency or clonal abnormalities cannot be diagnosed based on routine blood counts; hematologic malignancy events may be less frequent.

## Life Sciences

Disease prodrome and state transitions

## Competing Explanations

Chronic inflammation, nutritional deficiency, or selection of tests near diagnosis may produce differences in patterns.

## Minimal validation and falsification criteria

First confirm the timing of the two measurements and the reproducibility of abnormalities across multiple systems; if the findings disappear after excluding recent diagnoses, restrict interpretation to a prodromal marker.
