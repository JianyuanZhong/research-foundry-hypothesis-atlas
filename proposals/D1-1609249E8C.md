# Surface-adjusted fixed-capacity documentary-M2 HCC experiment

## 1. Assigned successor and scientific claim

This proposal is a targeted successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's identifiable retrospective eventual-stored-corpus estimand, fixed capacity, temporal quarantine, coupled source/clock/report/pathology/access uncertainty, exact whole-patient access bounds, and strict non-causal limits. It repairs one flaw: documentation surface is no longer an external negative-control veto that can invalidate a content result merely because surface itself predicts the endpoint.

The clinically consequential downstream question is whether it is worth obtaining timestamped report-version/access data and testing a future fixed-capacity multidisciplinary documentary-review strategy. The present experiment is only a prerequisite. It is not a triage, referral, treatment, workflow, or benefit study.

The strongest claim already supported by available evidence is documentary. It permits a retrospective future-year cohort and an encounter-documentary M2 endpoint. Current literature motivates rigor but does not answer this question. The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults in a future-defined, source-documented first eligible HCC resection episode with same-encounter documentary HCC pathology and no recorded qualifying HCC treatment in the preceding year, an independently optimized locked full model H containing nonsemantic acquisition information, documentation-surface information, and adjudicated eventual CT/MRI report content will capture more encounter-documentary M2 cases in an identical fixed top-K list than (i) an independently optimized surface-adjusted baseline G containing exactly the same nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface information but no semantics and (ii) the same fitted H pipeline H0 with semantic content masked while its surface inputs are retained. The substantive advance is not another discrimination model. A supportive result would justify collecting real report timestamps/access logs and prospectively testing a silent fixed-capacity process. 5. same-encounter pathology-row composition and set-valued documentary outcome; and

Only source-compatible combinations are admissible. An extremum is over complete states; it may not splice a favorable operation date from one state to an examination, pathology completion, annotation, or access allocation from another. State construction is deterministic and frozen before test outcomes are inspected.

A validated non-midnight timestamp is a point. A date-only or unvalidated midnight value is `[date,date+24 hours)`. Missing, competing, adjacent-day, duplicate, and source-discordant rows generate explicit alternatives. No procedure row proves scheduling status, anesthesia, completion, or resection.

For `d in {12,24,48,72}` hours, define analytical cutoff `c_d=t_op-d`. The primary offset is 24 hours; 12, 48, and 72 hours are locked sensitivity offsets. `c_d` is not a clinical decision, report-finalization, release, viewing, or referral time.

Development is 2015-2018. The entire score specification, dictionaries, preprocessing, hyperparameters, calibration map, tie rule, K rule, and conclusion rule are locked with 2019. Calendar years 2020 and 2021 are separate locked tests. A patient whose admissible state can enter a later period is quarantined from every earlier fit; a patient ambiguous between test years is one coupled alternative, never two observations. Rows from 2022 onward are ascertainment/drift audit only and cannot change a prior score or conclusion.

## 3. Population and temporal boundaries

The index population is intentionally conditional and selected. For each state and cutoff it includes one row per patient satisfying all of:

1. age at least 18 on the linked encounter;
2. earliest eligible source-documented hepatobiliary procedure episode according to a frozen dual-surgeon-reviewed Chinese procedure dictionary;
3. at least one exact-deduplicated same-encounter pathology row whose composed narrative explicitly supports HCC after blinded pathology-text adjudication; and
4. no recorded earlier HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy during `[c_d-365 days,c_d)`, searched patient-wide in dated procedures, medications, and non-drug orders using frozen dual-clinician dictionaries.

This is recorded-treatment-naive, not biologically treatment-naive. Untimed diagnoses can corroborate wording in an audit but cannot date treatment, establish negative history, define membership, or enter a predictor. Patients outside the source-documented procedure/pathology frame and episodes indistinguishable from planned or cancelled procedures are not controls and are not outcome negatives.

Index size is determined before pathology M2 grade, test report semantics, or any test outcome is read. Every exclusion is written with patient hash, raw row ordinal, reason code, cutoff interval, and state id. Report actual counts at every filter and the number of patients whose inclusion/year changes across states.

## 4. Documentary endpoint and required clinical adjudication

The endpoint is encounter-documentary M2, not biological MVI. Within the selected `(患者主索引, 就诊号)`, exact-deduplicate and concatenate every pathology row's `病理`, `检查所见`, and `检查诊断` in raw order. Two independent Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to examination text, scores, year, and access state, apply a frozen context rubric; a third resolves disagreements.

- `Y=1` only for an M2-like statement textually anchored to microvascular invasion or an HCC MVI-grade context.
- `Y=0` only for an anchored M0/M1 statement that excludes M2 in that context.
- `Y={0,1}` for absent grade, unanchored mention, boilerplate, conflict, uncertain attribution, inadequate text, or unresolved disagreement.

Untimed pathology cannot be made postoperative by adjudication. The extract has no pathology time, specimen/accession, slide/block, sampling, or chain-of-custody key. Those facts cannot be reconstructed. Parser-only, adjudicated, and all-row envelopes are reported separately, and every primary result preserves set-valued outcomes. Feasibility requires at least 85% anchored adjudicated grade and at least 50 lower-envelope M2 events in each claimed test year; this gate is not evidence of biological truth.

This adjudicates text meaning only. There are no images, so it supplies no feature PPV, sensitivity, specificity, imaging gold standard, biological truth, or clinical diagnosis. The complete stored unit is processed, never a selected phrase.

For each patient retain at most the latest eligible whole CT and latest eligible whole MRI whose full admissible acquisition interval lies in `[c_d-90 days,c_d)`. Boundary-overlap alternatives remain in `theta`. Choose by maximum admissible component start, then raw ordinal, then frozen patient hash. ## 6. Nested information sets and locked models

All models use the identical population, outcome state, cutoff, K, candidate rows, and tie key. Predictors are frozen into three disjoint blocks.

`B` is the nonsemantic acquisition/demographic block: age, sex, CT/MRI/other indicators, eligible-unit count, acquisition-to-cutoff recency, component count, identity ambiguity, source-text-missing indicator, and development-frequency category for `机器型号`. It excludes narrative form beyond a missing indicator and excludes all semantics, pathology, diagnoses, labs, clinical documents, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff information.

`S` is the documentation-surface block, computed separately by modality on complete whole units: total character count; `检查所见` and `检查诊断` field-presence indicators and character counts; digit, punctuation, whitespace, Latin, and Chinese-character proportions using a frozen Unicode table; line count; component-row count; exact-duplicate-row count; and accession/component ambiguity indicators. It contains no token identity, n-gram, embedding, selected phrase, clinical concept, negation flag, pathology, diagnosis, or outcome-derived quantity. Variables already in B are not duplicated in S.

`X` is only the radiologist-adjudicated semantic concept block, with prespecified CT and MRI slots and their present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned/source-text-unavailable codes.

Fit four named score pipelines:

- `B`: independently fitted on B alone, for descriptive benchmarking.
- `G`: the independently optimized surface-adjusted baseline fitted on `B+S`.
- `H`: the independently optimized full model fitted on `B+S+X`.
- `H0`: no refit. Apply the exact fitted H preprocessing, imputation, coefficients, intercept, and calibration, replacing every X value and semantic missingness code with H's training-defined source-text-unavailable code. Every B and S input, including surface missingness, must be byte-identical between H and H0.

Thus H versus G is the strategy comparison against an independently optimized model with the same nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface information. H versus H0 is the within-pipeline diagnostic that the fitted H ranking actually uses semantic content. H0 is not a substitute for G: because H and G are optimized independently, H0 need not equal G.

Use minimax patient-clustered elastic-net logistic loss over the finite coupled development states. The penalty and mixing grids, preprocessing, and optimization tolerances are identical for B, G, and H, but each is independently optimized. Use 2019 only to choose among the frozen grid by worst-state fixed-K capture, fit the frozen monotone calibration map, and lock scores. Calibration never changes ranking and cannot rescue a failed capture contrast. No test outcome, test pathology text, test annotation, access allocation, or post-cutoff row may affect selection or tuning.

Descriptive contrasts are `T_H-T_B` and the surface increment `T_G-T_B`. The latter is labeled surface-positive when its simultaneous lower bound exceeds 5, surface-negative when its simultaneous upper bound is at most 5, and surface-uncertain otherwise. This label is diagnostic and never changes the primary H-versus-G/H0 classification. A positive surface signal is reported and investigated as documentation-process information; it is not automatic falsification of semantic incremental value.

## 7. Fixed-capacity estimands

For complete state `theta`, offset `d`, year `a`, and `q in {.05,.10,.20}`, let `N_a(theta,d)` be eligible index size before M2 grade, test semantics, or test outcomes. Freeze

`N_ref = min_{theta,d,a in 2015..2019} N_a(theta,d)` and `K_q=floor(q*N_ref)`.

The primary capacity is `q=.10`; `.05` and `.20` are locked sensitivities. Require `N_ref>=500`, `K_q>=50` for every reported q, and at least `K_q` candidates in every required test state; because `q=.05` is declared, its `K_q>=50` condition may be the binding requirement.

Rank descending by locked score, then frozen patient hash, then raw episode backpointer. Select exactly K, without threshold padding. For `M in {B,G,H,H0}` define

`T_M(theta,d,a,q) = (100/K_q) * sum_{i in TopK_M} Y_i(theta)`.

With set-valued Y, preserve the attainable lower/upper top-K sum inside the same complete state. The unit is documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed ranking positions. It is not prevalence, recall, PPV, service utilization, opportunity to review, or utility. Since every strategy selects exactly K, capacity use is identically equal and is not an outcome.

The two co-primary contrasts are:

- `Delta_HG = T_H-T_G`, incremental fixed-K capture over the independently optimized same-information surface-adjusted baseline;
- `Delta_H0 = T_H-T_H0`, same-fitted within-pipeline content-use contrast.

The hypothesis margin is strictly greater than 5 captures per 100 positions for both contrasts. An outcome-aware top-K oracle may be reported only as a retrospective documentary ceiling.

## 8. Whole-patient report-access bounds and exact endpoints

Partition patients into CT-only, MRI-only, and CT+MRI strata under the same `theta`. For each stratum/modality choose marginal fractions from `{0,.25,.50,.75,1}`, convert to integer counts by a frozen largest-remainder rule, and enumerate every whole-patient assignment meeting the margins. CT/MRI overlap in dual-modality patients is unconstrained beyond those margins and its attainable range is reported. No phrase, row, or concept can be selectively exposed.

For an allocation `alpha`, inaccessible whole units have both S and X report-derived blocks replaced by the respective model's locked source-unavailable encoding; B acquisition facts remain because the source records an examination even when report access is hypothetical. Define `G_alpha` and `H_alpha` by applying the already fitted G and H pipelines without refitting. Define `H0_alpha` by applying the same fitted H pipeline to the identical B and alpha-specific S vector used by H_alpha while masking X for every unit. Therefore H_alpha and H0_alpha always have byte-identical B+S inputs.

For every access cell compute exact joint extrema of `T_Halpha-T_Galpha` and `T_Halpha-T_H0alpha` over whole-patient allocations, source states, and outcome completions. A certified mixed-integer program may use one four-state access variable per patient, precomputed scores for each allowed visibility state, exact top-K membership with frozen ties, modality-count constraints, and set-valued outcome bounds. Any nonzero optimality gap, timeout, infeasible required state, or fixture disagreement makes the cell inconclusive.

Mandatory endpoints are:

- all reports visible: `H_alpha=H`, `G_alpha=G`, and `H0_alpha=H0` bit-for-bit;
- all reports inaccessible: H_alpha and H0_alpha are bit-for-bit identical, so `Delta_H0=0`; G_alpha uses its own locked all-unavailable surface encoding;

## 9. Inference, falsification, and decision rules

Source uncertainty is the coupled `theta`; it is not sampling uncertainty. Add sampling uncertainty with 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates. Each replicate resamples whole patients, rebuilds coupled procedure/examination/pathology states, refits B/G/H, repeats 2019 tuning/calibration, derives H0 without refitting, and reconstructs exact top-K and access extrema. It may not independently combine state components.

Construct a single max-T simultaneous one-sided 97.5% family for both co-primary contrasts, both test years, every declared q and d, every finite admissible source/outcome state, and every claimed access cell. Include descriptive H-B and G-B contrasts in the same family so their labels cannot exploit multiplicity. Report point estimate, simultaneous lower and upper bounds, K, N_ref, year/q/d, complete state and access ids, top-K outcome envelope, event counts, gate vector, model/data/code hashes, and raw-result locator. A universal result uses the worst coordinate only after the family is formed.

As a semantic-alignment falsification diagnostic, run 1,000 development-only permutations moving each patient's entire X block within frozen strata of year, modality pattern, unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and S quantile bins while leaving B, S, and outcome rows fixed. Refit H/H0 and rerun the lock. The maximum statistic is the minimum of the standardized `Delta_HG` and `Delta_H0`; its familywise permutation p-value must be at most .025 for a supportive label. This tests whether the observed semantic block adds beyond preserved surface structure; it does not validate semantic truth.

Required gates are: matching catalog/snapshot/file hashes and headers; BOM/CRLF-safe parsing; exact joins and raw ordinals; one index row per patient; complete state ledger and temporal quarantine; frozen dictionaries; N_ref/K/event feasibility; no forbidden lineage; whole-unit construction; independently optimized G and H; identical B+S bytes for H/H0; H0 coefficient/preprocessing identity with H; blinded dual-reader/third-reader adjudication and unresolved accounting; set-valued pathology handling; reproducible max-T and permutation seeds; exact access certificates and brute-force fixtures; no more than 2.5% failed bootstrap replicates; and no required content-code prevalence shift greater than 15 percentage points from 2019. A shift failure means nontransportable/inconclusive, not adverse biology.

For each required coordinate classify `P` if both co-primary simultaneous lower bounds are strictly greater than 5; `N` if either co-primary simultaneous upper bound is at most 5; otherwise classify `I`. Let R contain both years, all declared q/d values, and every required source/outcome state for the eventual corpus; access cells are labeled separately by the same rule.

- **Supportive:** all gates, including permutation, pass and every coordinate in R is P. Report separately whether surface is positive, negative, or uncertain. This supports robust retrospective incremental semantic fixed-K capture only.
- **Adverse:** all computation gates pass, every coordinate in R is N, and none is P. This falsifies robust added content value in the declared scope. If an upper bound is below zero, H has lower documentary capture in that coordinate.
- **Mixed:** all computation gates pass and R contains at least one P and at least one N, or the two test years/contrasts resolve in opposite directions. The universal hypothesis is not supported; report the prespecified heterogeneity without averaging it away. Surface status remains orthogonal.
- **Inconclusive:** any indispensable gate fails, any required coordinate is I without the P/N pattern needed above, bounds cross 5, the state family is incomplete, or an optimizer/access cell is uncertified. Gate failure is not a biological negative.

A surface-positive supportive result means semantics add beyond a real documentation-process signal. Surface-positive with `Delta_HG` N means the independently optimized surface model accounts for the apparent strategy gain. `Delta_HG` P with `Delta_H0` N means H ranks differently for reasons not demonstrably attributable to its semantic inputs. Neither pattern may be called supportive.
