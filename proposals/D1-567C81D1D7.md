## Unresolved question, endpoint defect, and advance

The clinically important question remains whether, among adults still recorded as invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48 after recent vasopressor support and with marked positive recorded balance, starting or restarting a qualifying furosemide/bumetanide input record in the next 12 hours (A12) rather than avoiding initiation (B12) is associated with better day-7 recorded patient flow without crossing renal, RRT, death, terminal-disposition, or unfavorable-disposition harm margins.

AIFD7 is observable but not clinically homogeneous. It calls open non-ICU occupancy, home, home health, rehabilitation, skilled nursing, psychiatric facility, assisted living, and generic healthcare-facility discharge the same “favorable” outcome; it calls ICU occupancy, death, hospice, LTAC, and acute-hospital transfer the same “unfavorable” outcome. These states differ in survival, dependency, resource use, goals of care, and discharge practice. MIMIC has no functional status, destination appropriateness, or complete post-discharge outcomes with which to validate that ordering. Thus AIFD7 can support only a recorded topology/disposition claim, not recovery.

A new treatment- and outcome-blind audit used discovery buckets 0–79, each subject’s earliest adult ICU stay, `t0=intime+48h`, admission/index-ICU open at t0, and exactly one accepted same-stay procedureevents item 225792 interval crossing t0. It read no inputevents, A12/B12 assignment, renal/airway outcome, or arm contrast. Among 5,752 broad records, 1,748 index admissions closed by D7. Their exact `discharge_location` values included DIED 935, HOME 184, HOME HEALTH CARE 199, REHAB 82, SKILLED NURSING FACILITY 162, HOSPICE 34, LTAC 84, ACUTE HOSPITAL 34, PSYCH FACILITY 15, and smaller unresolved categories. At D7, 2,404 open admissions had one ICU location and 1,593 had one non-ICU location; only seven lacked active transfer coverage and none overlapped. The inherited “favorable” discharge group contained 643 heterogeneous records. This establishes excellent topology observability and substantial composition heterogeneity, not outcome validity or any recorded-loop association.

The repair makes **D7-RINO: death-penalized recorded in-system ICU nonoccupancy at day 7** primary. Discharge destination never changes D7-RINO. Every mutually exclusive patient-flow state and every exact destination category is reported separately. The construct is intentionally modest: it asks whether the patient is recorded outside a MIMIC ICU at D7, with death assigned failure. It is not called recovery, airway liberation, alive status after discharge, or freedom from ICU use elsewhere.

The substantive advance over the parents is threefold:

1. destination labels no longer encode an unvalidated recovery utility in the primary endpoint;
2. later MIMIC admissions for the same `subject_id` are linked through D7, so an observed ICU readmission cannot be misclassified as favorable merely because the index `hadm_id` closed;
3. component and sensitivity discordance can make the endpoint construct inconclusive even when the aggregate is numerically supportive.

## D7-RINO state machine and decomposition

Set `D7=t0+7d`. Scan all `hosp/admissions` rows for the same `subject_id` through D7; later admissions affect outcomes only and never eligibility, treatment, covariates, or weights. An admission is active iff `admittime<=D7<dischtime`; join its transfers by `subject_id,hadm_id` and corroborate ICU location with any same-admission `icustays` interval covering D7.

Death has precedence. Any exact `deathtime<=D7` is failure. A consistent `hospital_expire_flag=1` or `discharge_location='DIED'` with `dischtime<=D7` is failure; a clearly alive conflicting field is unknown unless `deathtime` is exact. Treat `patients.dod` as interval `[date 00:00,date+1d)`: an interval wholly before D7 is failure; one straddling D7 is unknown.

D7-RINO is `[1,1]` if:

- exactly one same-subject admission is active at D7, exactly one valid active transfer is in the frozen non-ICU list, and no ICU-stay interval covers D7; or
- no same-subject admission is active, the index admission closed nonfatally by D7, and death is neither definite nor boundary-compatible by D7.

It is `[0,0]` for definite death or for one active admission consistently located in a frozen ICU. It is `[0,1]` for multiple active admissions, malformed/overlapping/absent topology, unseen units, PACU/Nursery, transfer/ICU disagreement, unresolved death/disposition conflicts, a DOD interval straddling D7, or an index admission not closed without a unique location. Exact frozen ICU and non-ICU careunit lists are in the contract; unseen values are never inferred.

Report mutually exclusive definite states: death, in-system ICU, in-system non-ICU, out of the MIMIC hospital system after nonfatal closure, and unknown. Separately report each index `discharge_location`, grouped only for audit as community (HOME/HOME HEALTH CARE/ASSISTED LIVING), facility/rehabilitation (REHAB/SNF/HEALTHCARE FACILITY/PSYCH FACILITY), adverse transfer (HOSPICE/LTAC/ACUTE HOSPITAL), and unresolved (AGAINST ADVICE/OTHER FACILITY/missing). These overlays never alter D7-RINO and are never called recovery.
