## Question and bounded advance

The parent tests whether a respiratory-specific treatment record is concordant with a separately stored respiratoryCare start beyond generic treatment opportunity, matched non-respiratory workflow, scheduling, availability, duplicates, and end-of-stay artifacts. A remaining clinically consequential ambiguity is whether the two apparent event times are independently timed. The treatment table contains one offset and a raw label. respiratoryCare contains status and vent interval offsets, while respiratoryCharting contains both a clinical offset and a documentation-entry offset. Concordance can therefore arise from a shared interface, copied timestamps, scheduled charting, or backfill even if no independently timed therapy event is represented.

The strongest evidence supports only that linked eICU tables can support reproducible multi-center analyses of recorded events and that temporal EHR representations and preprocessing need transport and measurement audits [K1,K2,K3]. It does not establish that treatmentoffset is order, administration, delivery, or bedside time; that respiratoryCare ventstartoffset is physiologic onset; or that respiratoryCharting entry time is reliable provenance.

Falsifiable hypothesis: in the parent S6 operational risk set with no auditable active respiratoryCare interval at minute 360, treatment/respiratory concordance is more reproducible when anchored to the respiratory event's clinical offset than when anchored only to chart-entry or shared-schedule timing, and its pre-landmark state/process increment persists in held-out hospitals after source-timing and availability adjustment.

The strongest rival is shared documentation/interface timing. It predicts that treatment rows align as well or better with respiratoryCharting entry offsets than with respiratoryCare clinical offsets, are enriched at exact/scheduled offsets, show same-stay duplicate/backfill coupling, and lose their process increment under timing-preserving or source-specific nulls. A second rival is selection: the event marks prolonged stay, end-of-life, or dense observation. Parent controls plus this child's exit/EOL and observation audits address that rival but cannot remove all unmeasured selection.

This advances the parent by testing the provenance boundary of cross-table concordance, not by adding another predictive modality. Support justifies blinded clinical adjudication and order/administration/device linkage. Adverse evidence warns against treating cross-table agreement as treatment corroboration. Neither outcome supports treatment effectiveness, causality, physiologic deterioration, or deployment.

### New source-timing outcomes

For every pair retain raw row identifiers, offsets, class, and lag rather than only a binary result.

1. C_clin(w) is the parent C_RT_doc(w), anchored at respiratoryCare ventstartoffset.
2. C_entry(w) is T_resp_any within [e-w,e+w] of a respiratoryCharting documentation-entry anchor e, where the chart row is within a prespecified 30-minute window of the same stay's R_start_doc and has finite respchartentryoffset. It is a timing diagnostic, not a replacement endpoint.
3. C_clinchart(w) is treatment within w of the corresponding respiratoryCharting clinical offset, with identical eligibility and tie rules.
4. Entry-dominance compares absolute treatment lag to respiratoryCare ventstartoffset, respiratoryCharting clinical offset, and respiratoryCharting entry offset; report paired within-stay differences and the proportion positive under entry but negative under clinical chart time. This is evidence for documentation timing, not proof of an interface.
5. Tie/backfill strata include exact same-offset ties, shared integer offsets, chart-entry minus clinical lag bins, duplicate treatment IDs/strings/offsets, and post-discharge offsets. These are audits/effect strata, never semantic labels.
6. Schedule/source-coupling nulls retain each stay's treatment count, raw class, offset distribution, and hospital, then cyclically shift treatment offsets within each stay's observed (360,720] opportunity and separately permute treatment rows within hospital and offset-density strata. Recompute C_clin, C_entry, C_clinchart, T_any_record, and T_nonresp_control. These break patient event timing but do not establish independence.
7. Repeat with a strict clinical-time requirement and with entry-aligned pairs excluded. Treatment has no entry timestamp, so treatment-side clinical/documentation separation is unavailable and explicitly reported as unavailable.

The primary estimand is the held-out-hospital difference in the pre-landmark process increment for C_clin(30) versus T_any_record and T_nonresp_control, with C_entry/C_clinchart and timing strata as secondary decomposition. Compare value-only state, state-plus-generic availability, and state-plus-treatment-specific timing models on identical risk sets, folds, and outcomes. The estimand is a recorded-event association.

## Interpretations and limits

Support independent recorded timing only if at least five viable hospitals have positive clinical opportunity; classes and timing strata are frozen; C_clin and its process increment are coherent in both baseline and boosting; it survives availability, ±10/30/60, duplicate/backfill, discharge/EOL, schedule/source-coupling, entry-exclusion, and hospital-held-out checks; C_entry does not dominate; generic and non-respiratory controls do not reproduce it; and no single hospital, stratum, tie pattern, or model explains it. This establishes only a reproducible clinical-offset-linked recorded association.

Adverse evidence is entry-aligned dominance, exact-offset/duplicate/backfill or schedule enrichment, loss after nulls/weighting/holdout, matching non-respiratory control, or exit/EOL/hospital dominance. Preserve any narrower respiratoryCare association but conclude treatment adds no independent recorded evidence. Inconclusive evidence is fewer than five hospitals, inadequate positivity/ESS, unstable vocabulary, wide intervals, no reliable clinical-entry pairing, or dependence on unavailable treatment-side timestamps. A low-precision null is not a refutation.

Completion requires source audits, frozen manifests/classes, S6 and starting-stratum manifests, interval and chart lag audit, pair lags, C outcomes at all windows, tie/backfill/null outputs, fold-isolated predictions, uncertainty, and a claim-to-output conclusion table. Readiness or a positive association does not complete the objective.

A verifier can check paths, hashes, headers, columns, joins, time boundaries, endpoint/exit logic, outcome-blind classes, lag/tie/null definitions, fold isolation, metrics, uncertainty, and claim-to-output links. Clinical adjudication is required to establish order, administration, delivery, bedside action, or physiologic meaning. Sources lack treatment order/administration/device provenance, indication, assignment, reliable death timestamps, interface IDs and raw waveforms. No model fit resolves these limits. The other three configured dataset islands remain available; they are not cross-joined.
