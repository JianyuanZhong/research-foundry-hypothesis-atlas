Status: planned design only. No patient rows were read, no cohort was fitted, and no clinical result is claimed.

Parent: [prior hypothesis]. This child preserves the parent population, first-ICU-discharge landmark, non-causal 48-hour estimand, exact verified MIMIC bindings, and the inherited three-work evidence package. ## Scientific opening and claim boundary

Creatinine and urine output are complementary renal observations, but the parent question remains unresolved: does a fall in creatinine without adequately observed urine recovery immediately before ICU discharge identify a reproducible short-horizon prognostic phenotype, and does recurrence across an earlier period distinguish persistent observed physiology from a transient threshold or documentation episode?

[K1] supports the complementary creatinine/urine assessment premise but does not test this landmark phenotype. [K2] bounds interpretation by reporting measured-clearance evidence that creatinine-based estimates can be misleading during critical illness and muscle loss; it does not establish MIMIC urine ascertainment or prognosis. [K3] supports dynamic renal assessment as a risk-stratification idea while limiting claims about incremental biomarkers; it does not distinguish persistent EHR discordance from transient charting noise. These works do not establish the unresolved MIMIC claim.

The falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults surviving a first ICU stay of at least 48 hours, with dense and valid urine observation, creatinine improvement without observed urine-output recovery in [t0-24 hours,t0) is associated with higher first-48-hour post-discharge risk of ICU readmission or in-hospital death beyond measured pre-t0 severity and discharge-selection proxies; recurrence in the immediately preceding non-overlapping 24-hour window supplies additional held-out prognostic information beyond the final-window phenotype, and a masked HMM supplies further information only if its transition/persistence summaries outperform that transparent recurrence comparator.

This is a standardized observational prognostic comparison, not an intervention effect. It cannot establish true GFR, complete urine collection, CKD, renal reserve, fluid balance, tubular injury, a renal mechanism, causality, a discharge decision effect, or transportability.

The clinical advance is a boundary test. A positive final-window association that disappears with coverage or support adjustment is evidence for ascertainment or selection. A stable repeated-versus-transient contrast would show that the observed temporal pattern has prognostic information beyond a single discharge snapshot. If the HMM adds nothing beyond a simple prior-window indicator and coverage summaries, the clinically useful conclusion is that latent-state complexity is unnecessary for this question.

## Population, landmark and outcome

Use only the frozen MIMIC-IV 3.1 source. Include adults with patients.anchor_age >= 18; a valid first ICU stay per subject_id with intime < outtime and los >= 2 days; a linked admission; outtime <= admissions.dischtime; and survival to t0 = icustays.outtime. A death time earlier than t0 is an exclusion/data-quality failure, not a post hoc outcome assignment.

The primary outcome is the first event in (t0, t0 + 48 hours] and before or at admissions.dischtime:

1. admissions.deathtime, or
2. a later icustays.intime for the same subject_id and hadm_id with intime > t0.

Keep ICU readmission and death separate. Secondary outcomes are the component events, 7-day in-hospital composite, and time to the primary component. Do not infer an event time from hospital_expire_flag when deathtime is unusable; report that exception and apply the frozen time-to-event rule.

All predictors precede t0. The primary final window is [t0-24 hours,t0). Longitudinal bins are non-overlapping 6-hour bins from icustays.intime through t0, with partial first-bin duration retained. MIMIC timestamps are subject-shifted; no cross-subject calendar alignment is used.

## Exact measurements and discordance definition

Creatinine is restricted to hosp/labevents itemid=50912, linked to hosp/d_labitems. Retain finite, positive valuenum values with verified compatible valueuom (canonical mg/dL); exclude and report incompatible or unitless values rather than silently converting. In a 24-hour window, require at least two observations at least 6 hours apart, with the final valid observation inside the window. Let C_peak be the maximum and C_last the final valid value; creatinine improvement is C_peak-C_last >= 0.3 mg/dL. Retain count, observation span and time since peak.

Urine is restricted to icu/outputevents itemid 226559 (Foley), 226560 (Void), or 226561 (Condom Cath), with finite nonnegative value, valueuom=mL, and charttime inside the selected stay/window. Sum valid values; absent rows are not zero. Retain row count, source indicators, zero fraction, earliest/latest charttime and observed-span. Span is documentation timing, not proof of complete collection.

The dense primary ascertainment rule is at least two valid urine rows in each of the four final 6-hour bins and at least one verified source type. The full eligible cohort and a frozen ladder (any valid row; at least two final-window rows; at least one row in every final bin; strict two rows per final bin) are reported separately. Low output is total observed urine <400 mL/24 hours, deliberately unweighted because a validated, time-aligned weight field is not bound. Continuous volume and documentation measures are retained.

Final-window groups remain improved/high-output, improved/low-output (discordant), not-improved/high-output, and not-improved/low-output. These are descriptive observed groups, not diagnoses.

## The repaired method comparison

### B0/B1 final-window baseline

B0 is penalized logistic regression for the primary composite. Its verified inputs are age, gender, admission_type, ICU LOS, admission-to-t0 duration, first_careunit and last_careunit, creatinine level/trajectory and observation intensity, final-window urine volume/low-output/row count/observed span/source/zero fraction, MAP summaries and measurement indicators, vasopressor exposure and duration/last-active status, invasive-ventilation exposure and duration/status, and explicit missingness indicators.

MAP is icu/chartevents itemid 220052. Vasopressors are icu/inputevents itemids 221906, 221289, 221749 and 222315, using starttime, endtime and statusdescription. Ventilation is icu/procedureevents itemid 225792, using starttime, endtime and statusdescription. Transfer context from hosp/transfers is a pre-t0 selection diagnostic.

B1 adds the four-level final-window group while retaining continuous component measurements and documentation fields. B1 versus B0 asks whether the thresholded discordance grouping adds information beyond its measured ingredients. B0/B1 do not contain any post-t0 value.

### B2: transparent longitudinal baseline

B2 adds one pre-specified longitudinal comparison to B1. Define the immediately preceding, non-overlapping window P=[t0-48 hours,t0-24 hours) using exactly the same creatinine, urine, units, and coverage rules as the final window. Define prior discordance D_P=1 only when P has the required creatinine improvement and observed urine <400 mL with strict four-bin urine coverage. The final discordance D_F is defined identically in [t0-24,t0).

B2 includes D_P, a prior-window-valid indicator, prior urine row count/span/source/zero-fraction, prior creatinine count/span/time-since-peak, and prior continuous urine volume and creatinine change. Its focal descriptive contrast is:
- repeated: D_F=1 and D_P=1;
- transient: D_F=1 and D_P=0 with prior-window validity;
- not-final-discordant: D_F=0.

No missing prior window is called transient. B2 is the simple baseline because it makes recurrence explicit, uses a non-overlapping historical window, and preserves documentation information. It is fitted with the same penalized logistic family and same outcome as B0/B1. Thus B1-to-B2 measures the information in a transparent recurrence summary.

### L1: masked HMM, tested beyond B2

L1 is a three-state masked HMM over the 6-hour sequence from ICU intime to t0. Per-bin channels are creatinine last value and adjacent observed change, urine volume, urine row count, observed span, source indicators, zero fraction, MAP, vasopressor/ventilation support, and explicit masks. Unobserved urine is a missing emission, never zero. A partial first bin retains duration. Only verified item IDs and fields listed above enter the primary model; unresolved lactate, platelet and renal-replacement mappings are deferred and cannot appear by assumption.

Fit the HMM on training subjects only. Tune emission family/regularization on the tuning subjects using unsupervised sequence likelihood and state-stability criteria; the test outcomes remain untouched. State labels are not biological labels. For each subject, export only pre-specified, label-invariant summaries: expected adjacent transition count; posterior probability that adjacent latent states remain equal through the final four bins; posterior probability of remaining in the same latent state across the boundary between P and the final window; occupancy/observation-mask summaries; and time since the last inferred transition. These are trajectory descriptors, not claims about renal mechanisms.

The outcome model for the HMM comparison is B2 plus the limited HMM summaries, with penalty selected on the tuning split. This nested comparison is essential: L1 must beat B2 to demonstrate information beyond final-window measurements, transparent recurrence, and documentation. An HMM improvement over B1 alone is not sufficient.

The charting-noise/discharge-selection rival predicts that B2 and L1 recurrence is concentrated in low-coverage or high-support records, that latent transitions are unstable, and that held-out risk separation attenuates after documentation and support adjustment. A persistent-observed-pattern explanation predicts stable recurrence and a transition/persistence contrast that remains in dense and support-restricted analyses. Neither explanation proves biological renal recovery.

A deterministic filtration/fluid-balance model is deferred because the bound data do not provide measured GFR, complete urine collection, fluid composition, validated creatinine-generation parameters, reliable time-aligned weight, or complete diuretic exposure. A transformer/LSTM is deferred because added opaque capacity would not supply a more direct observable than B2/HMM transition summaries. These are question-specific decisions, not blanket exclusions of neural networks or GPU use.

## Split, fitting, evaluation and uncertainty

Create one patient-level 70/15/15 train/tune/test split before fitting, using the first-ICU subject population and a recorded fixed seed/hash rule. Reuse this membership for B0, B1, B2 and L1. Fit all preprocessing on train only, select logistic penalties and any HMM hyperparameters on tune only, and inspect test outcomes once.

Report held-out standardized risks and risk difference/risk ratio for final discordance versus improved/high-output, and for repeated versus transient among D_F=1 with valid P. Standardize comparisons over fixed B1-risk strata whose cutpoints are learned from training predictions, plus the pre-specified dense/full and no-support restrictions. Report Brier score, log loss, calibration slope/intercept and calibration plots; AUROC/AUPRC are descriptive only. Report paired held-out deltas B1-B0, B2-B1 and L1-B2, not a leaderboard.

Use 200 patient-level bootstrap resamples of held-out predictions for uncertainty in all risks, contrasts and proper-score deltas. Report test size, event count, and repeated/transient group sizes. For HMM state stability, use label-invariant summaries in bounded train-resampling diagnostics; failure to converge or unstable summaries is inconclusive, not evidence for either rival.

The HMM must not be fit or interpreted if the test set has fewer than 200 subjects or 30 primary events, if masks require imputation, if either repeated or transient focal group has fewer than 20 test subjects, or if required train/tune support is absent. A fit is inconclusive if a state occupies <5% of training bins, median maximum posterior state probability is <0.60, convergence fails, or label-invariant persistence/transition summaries are directionally stable in fewer than 80% of the pre-specified stability refits.

L1 is scientifically retained only when it shows a reproducible transition/persistence pattern, an incremental held-out proper-score result over B2 whose paired bootstrap interval excludes zero, and a repeated-versus-transient risk contrast that remains directionally consistent across dense/full and support-restricted checks. A small score improvement without a distinct stable temporal contrast is prediction complexity, not a clinical advance; defer the HMM and retain B0-B2. No clinical utility or treatment threshold is claimed.

## Falsification and interpretation

Freeze before test-outcome review: archive/member/schema/item checks; cohort flow; coverage ladder; 12-hour and 48-hour follow-up; continuous creatinine change; measurement-density and ICU-care-unit strata; no-support restriction; permutation of final discordance within ICU-LOS/admission/B1-risk strata; creatinine-only and urine-only models; and a pseudo-landmark 24 hours before t0 constructed only from data before that pseudo-landmark. A pseudo-landmark is a timing diagnostic, not a second causal analysis.

Supportive evidence is a calibrated dense-cohort final-window contrast after measured severity/support adjustment, a stable repeated-versus-transient contrast in B2, and an additional stable, held-out L1 transition/persistence contrast beyond B2. This supports an observable prognostic regularity and justifies external validation or clinical adjudication of urine completeness.

Adverse evidence is attenuation/reversal after coverage or support restriction, concentration in sparse documentation, a permutation contrast comparable to the observed contrast, or L1 transitions dominated by low-density bins with no incremental evidence over B2. This supports ascertainment, residual severity or discharge selection and argues against paying the complexity cost of HMM.

Inconclusive evidence includes invalid units or times, hash/schema/item mismatch, unresolved outcome timestamps, too few events/groups, calibration failure, HMM instability, or wide uncertainty. Report the exact limitation without converting it to a null or favorable subgroup.

Computationally checkable claims are source/member/schema availability, leakage-free cohort/split construction, model fitting, held-out predictions, uncertainty, transition summaries and whether interpretations match those outputs. Complete urine collection, true filtration, CKD/renal reserve, mechanism, discharge rationale, causality, clinical utility and transportability require clinical adjudication, richer measurements, external validation or another study.

## Alternatives not chosen and revisit evidence

B2 is selected as the necessary simple comparator because it is transparent, uses the same measurements, and gives a direct repeated-versus-transient estimand. L1 is retained conditionally because mask-aware transitions are information that B2’s count/indicator loses. Revisit L1 only if held-out results show stable incremental transition information beyond B2; defer it if it merely improves a score without a distinct temporal contrast or if state instability/coverage dependence dominates. Revisit a mechanistic model only if verified time-aligned weight, fluid composition, complete diuretic exposure and a validated filtration target become available. Revisit a neural sequence model only if a concrete clinical observable unavailable to B2/L1 is defined and its added information can be tested on the same held-out split.
