# Fail-closed executable persistent respiratory-state experiment

## Substantive advance and preserved estimand

This child combines the endpoint-validity repair with the persistent-state requirement. It preserves the frozen eICU denominator and the descriptive question: among the pre-specified person-landmarks in the first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`, is the first exact care-plan label associated with a subsequent respiratory-state trajectory, beyond pre-label respiratory state and documentation opportunity? This is an association/prediction estimand, not a treatment effect, causal effect, clinical recommendation, or probability of benefit.

The frozen population is exactly 540 person-landmarks, 443 stays, and 74 hospitals, with landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after unit admission. For each landmark, the decision time `D` is the `cplitemoffset` of the first qualifying exact label in `[L,L+360]`, with the inherited parent exclusions, first qualifying stay rule, and arm construction unchanged. The two arms are the literal, case-sensitive `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue` values `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`; no synonym, substring, or semantic relabeling is permitted. The frozen action table, if recovered, remains authoritative; it must not be redefined by endpoint availability.

The clinical advance is a testable distinction between a transient charted setting and a reproducible, multi-domain respiratory-state trajectory. A label is informative only if the prespecified change is present in both post-decision subwindows and is not accompanied by deterioration in either available safety signal. This remains a structured-chart proxy. It cannot establish delivered ventilator settings, clinician intent, spontaneous breathing trial performance, extubation readiness, liberation, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Parser freeze and endpoint definition

Create and hash a parser manifest before opening arm labels for endpoint comparison. It contains the exact label lists, regular expression, bounds, windows, tie rules, missingness rules, and code version. No threshold, unit conversion, synonym, or label selection may be changed after arm-level endpoint counts are viewed.

For `respiratoryCharting`, exact trimmed, case-sensitive labels are:

* FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`;
* PEEP: `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`;
* pressure support: `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`;
* set rate: `Vent Rate`, `VS RESP RATE`.

Parse a value only when the entire trimmed token matches `^[+-]?(?:\\d+(?:\\.\\d*)?|\\.\\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\\d+)?$`. Reject blanks, embedded units, ranges, inequalities, and all other strings while recording label-specific rejection reasons. Retain FiO2 only in `[21,100]` for all three FiO2 labels; values such as `0.4`, `2`, and `3` are `unit_ambiguous_or_invalid`, not multiplied by 100. Retain PEEP and pressure support in `[0,60]`, set rate in `[0,100]`, `sao2` in `[0,100]`, and `respiration` in `[0,200]`. No interpolation, carry-forward, or post hoc fraction conversion is permitted. Numeric source fields are parsed with the same full-token rule.

Use half-open clinical-time windows relative to D. Pre-state windows are `(D-1440,D-720]` and `(D-720,D]`. The post period is split into an early window `(D+360,D+1080]` and a late window `(D+1080,D+1800]`, preserving the six-hour gap after the decision. For each component and period require at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. Collapse exact duplicate clinical-time/value rows after retaining source IDs and deterministic sort `(clinical_offset, entry_offset, source_id)`. If distinct valid values share one clinical offset, mark that component-period `time_tie_conflict` and do not choose one by entry time. Summarize each available component-period by the median.

For each of FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, and set rate, define a component reduction only when the pre-state summary and both early and late post summaries exist and the late and early summaries are each no greater than the pre summary. This is a deliberately conservative persistence criterion: a transient early change cannot qualify. The respiratory trajectory endpoint is `persistent_improvement` when at least one component meets this reduction criterion and both post windows have valid safety signals with no adverse change. Define safety using only `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration`: in each post window, median SpO2 must not be more than 2 points below the pre-state median and median respiration must not be more than 4 breaths/min above the pre-state median. Require at least two valid observations in each safety period, with the same 30-minute separation and tie rules. Missing either signal in any required period is missing, not a non-event.

A secondary, explicitly non-persistent endpoint reports the earlier parent-compatible single-post-window result using `(D+360,D+1800]`; it is descriptive only and cannot replace the primary persistent endpoint. This separates feasibility loss caused by persistence from disagreement about the label or parser. It must be declared before counts are inspected.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

Run a same-parser pre-D placebo, a D-720 lead-time placebo, leave-one-component-out analyses, the single-post-window secondary endpoint, chart-only versus care-source sensitivities, all three airway recencies, and a documentation-density negative control. Perform 1,000 fixed-seed within-hospital-by-landmark arm permutations preserving arm counts and the frozen row set. Keep the permutation seed and assignment manifest.

Supportive evidence requires the primary persistent endpoint and at least two of the three airway-recency variants to have the same direction, at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions to agree, and the label increment over baseline/opportunity to exceed 0.01 in both micro and macro held-out log loss with a stay-clustered 95% interval excluding zero. It must survive leave-one-component-out and source ablations, while placebos are absent or smaller and the observed metric exceeds the permutation null. This supports only reproducible incremental information in the structured chart construct.

Adverse evidence is no label advantage over opportunity-only, placebo/permutation-like performance, reversal across hospitals, or a documentation-only signal; this is a useful negative result against transportable label information. Inconclusive evidence includes failure of cohort reconstruction, insufficient persistent endpoint support or overlap, intervals spanning zero and the predeclared meaningful increment, competing exits/missingness concentrated by arm, or disagreement across airway-recency variants. A single-window positive result with persistent failure is explicitly classified as transient/non-persistent association, not supportive of the persistent hypothesis.
