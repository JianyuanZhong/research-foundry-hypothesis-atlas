# Early post-TACE hepatic trajectory and short-term repeat admission in HCC

## Candidate status and scientific question

This is a new HCC proposal for episode 1. It is an observational, evidence-bounded hypothesis, not a treatment recommendation.

**Hypothesis.** Among adults with hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) receiving a first observed transarterial chemoembolization (TACE), deterioration in hepatic/injury laboratory measures during the first 0–3 days after the procedure, relative to the patient’s own pre-procedure values, is associated with a higher risk of a subsequent inpatient admission within 30 days after discharge, beyond baseline hepatic reserve, demographics, prior utilization, and recorded disease burden.

The estimand is the adjusted association and incremental prognostic information of the early trajectory block. It is not the causal effect of TACE, and it cannot establish that changing treatment or monitoring the laboratory values improves outcomes.

## Unresolved claim and clinical importance

The strongest claim supported by the accessible evidence is narrower: baseline hepatic reserve is clinically relevant to tolerance and outcomes after locoregional HCC treatment. A current open-access cohort of patients undergoing TACE/TARE reported that baseline ALBI score was associated with post-locoregional-therapy hepatic decompensation and that decompensation was associated with shorter survival (Sanai et al., DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-54466-4; the abstract and relevant introduction were read from the acquired full-text XML). That evidence does not establish that an early within-person change after TACE adds information beyond baseline reserve, nor that it identifies patients likely to return for inpatient care in this HCC service.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is therefore:

> Conditional on baseline measurements and observed case mix, does an early post-TACE laboratory trajectory provide reproducible short-term risk information that is absent from baseline values alone?

This matters because post-TACE monitoring and discharge planning are consequential decisions. If the trajectory adds reliable information, it could motivate a prospective study of intensified follow-up or earlier evaluation for selected patients. If it adds no information beyond baseline, routine post-procedure laboratory escalation based on these changes would have weak empirical support. Either result is useful; neither result alone justifies a clinical intervention.

The substantive advance over existing baseline-risk work is a time-anchored test of dynamic hepatic tolerance using routinely collected post-procedure data, with a baseline-only comparator and a clinically observable near-term utilization outcome. It does not pretend to replace imaging-based response assessment or validated hepatic-decompensation adjudication.

## Available-data audit and feasibility

The local HCC snapshot is [source checksum]. Dates span 2010-07-27 through 2026-01-01 in encounters. A full-file, non-sampled audit found:

- encounters: 105,044 rows and 43,815 patients.
- diagnoses: 1,810,646 rows and 105,044 encounter keys; 肝细胞癌 occurs among the recorded diagnosis names.
- procedures: 338,040 rows; TACE was identified only by the prespecified strings TACE, 肝动脉化疗栓塞, or 动脉化疗栓塞.
- There were 17,671 patients with a dated first observed TACE. After requiring an adult index encounter with a discharge time and allowing complete 30-day follow-up through 2026-01-01 (index procedure no later than 2025-12-02), 17,359 remained before laboratory availability filtering.
- In that audit, 1,408/17,359 (8.1%) had a subsequent inpatient admission within 30 days after the index encounter’s discharge. This is a feasibility count, not a prespecified adjusted estimate.
- The full labs file has 28,159,928 rows. Relevant assay labels are present with numeric results: 总胆红素 238,553 rows, 白蛋白 238,293, 丙氨酸氨基转移酶 308,671, 门冬氨酸氨基转移酶 238,302, 国际标准化比值 297,735, 肌酐 228,128, and 血小板计数 253,588.
- Among dated first-TACE patients, a baseline (-14 to <0 day) and early (0–3 day) measurement was found for all seven assays in 5,385 patients before the final adult/discharge/follow-up restrictions and in 5,325 after those restrictions; 453/5,325 had the feasibility outcome (8.5%). These counts were obtained by a complete scan, with no row sampling and no outcome-model fitting.

The catalog explicitly states that laboratory rows have no separate unit column. Therefore the primary analysis must use assay-specific within-assay change, not a pooled biochemical score. An ALBI calculation is not part of the primary experiment unless a domain expert independently validates that albumin and bilirubin units are stable and clinically interpretable. The feasibility audit does not establish clinical validity of any laboratory cutoff.

## Population and temporal boundaries

The Harbor experiment will use only the accessible HCC discovery partition defined by the catalog’s participant hash policy (buckets 0–79 of 0–99). The reserved buckets 80–99 are inaccessible and are not an external validation set.

An index is the earliest observed procedure for a patient whose procedures.手术 contains one of the three prespecified TACE strings and whose procedures.开始时间 parses as a timestamp. The index procedure must join to one encounters row on (患者主索引, 就诊号), have 年龄 >= 18, have a nonmissing 出院时间, and occur from the earliest available procedure date through 2025-12-02 23:59:59. The patient must have an HCC diagnosis (诊断名称 containing 肝细胞癌) on the index encounter or an earlier dated encounter. If multiple procedure rows share the same patient, encounter, time, and text, they are collapsed to one index event; later TACE procedures are not additional observations in the primary cohort.

The baseline laboratory window is 14 calendar days before the TACE start, excluding the procedure time. The early window is procedure start through 3 calendar days after it, inclusive. For each assay and window, use the median valid numeric 定量结果 after conservative numeric parsing; retain qualitative results only for an availability audit, not as numeric exposure values. A primary core cohort requires paired baseline and early values for 总胆红素, 白蛋白, and 国际标准化比值. The expanded secondary cohort additionally requires 丙氨酸氨基转移酶, 门冬氨酸氨基转移酶, 肌酐, and 血小板计数.

Follow-up starts at the index encounter’s 出院时间, not at the procedure time. The primary outcome is any later encounter for the same patient with a nonmissing 入院时间 strictly after that discharge and no later than 30 days after discharge, excluding the index 就诊号. A 90-day subsequent inpatient admission is a secondary outcome, restricted to indices no later than 2025-10-03 23:59:59. The outcome is deliberately named “subsequent inpatient admission,” not “unplanned readmission,” because the source does not reliably encode scheduling intent.

Patients are not treated as event-free beyond the last observed encounter; lack of further records is administratively censored. The primary 30-day cohort excludes index encounters too close to the snapshot end to have a complete calendar window.

## Exposure representation and models

For each assay, calculate the within-person early change as the early-window median minus the baseline-window median. Because units are absent, analyze each assay separately after fold-specific robust standardization (median and IQR learned in the training fold). Do not form an unvalidated ALBI score or sum heterogeneous assays. Prespecify the expected direction for interpretation: increasing bilirubin, INR, ALT, AST, or creatinine and decreasing albumin or platelets are adverse directions, but the direction is not imposed during fitting.

The primary inferential analysis is a logistic regression for the 30-day admission outcome:

1. Baseline-only model: age, sex, index department, baseline assay medians, prior encounter count in the preceding 365 days, and the prespecified diagnosis/procedure history indicators.
2. Trajectory model: all baseline-only variables plus the seven assay-specific early changes, with missingness indicators in a sensitivity model.
3. A prespecified global likelihood-ratio test evaluates whether the trajectory block improves fit conditional on baseline variables. Report adjusted odds ratios per assay IQR, 95% confidence intervals, absolute risk differences at representative covariate patterns, Brier score, calibration slope/intercept, AUROC, AUPRC, and patient-level bootstrap confidence intervals.
4. Repeat in the core three-assay cohort and expanded seven-assay cohort; compare complete-case, missingness-indicator, and inverse-observation-weighted sensitivity analyses. If laboratory units or assay semantics fail expert validation, retain only the direction-free statistical result and label the clinical interpretation unavailable.

A bounded model-learning alternative uses elastic-net logistic regression and a shallow gradient-boosted tree model with the same baseline and trajectory feature blocks. It is a robustness/comparative-prediction analysis, not evidence that a complex model is clinically superior. All preprocessing and tuning occur inside patient-level folds.

Use patient-level five-fold cross-validation within discovery buckets 0–79, with no patient appearing in both training and test folds. Add a chronological sensitivity split using index dates 2010–2022 for derivation and 2023–2025-12-02 for testing, while preserving patient-level separation. The inaccessible reserved partition must not be described as validation.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

The hypothesis is supportive only if the trajectory block has a prespecified global evidence of incremental association (likelihood-ratio p < 0.05), its optimism-corrected out-of-fold Brier improvement over baseline has a 95% bootstrap interval excluding 0, and the direction/calibration result is not reversed in the chronological sensitivity split. This is still prognostic evidence, not a clinical treatment effect.

The hypothesis is adverse/falsified if the trajectory block has no evidence of incremental association and its out-of-fold calibration/Brier performance is no better than baseline, or if the apparent gain disappears or reverses in the chronological split. A finding that only a high-capacity model improves in-sample discrimination but fails calibration or held-out performance is a model failure, not support.

Results are inconclusive if the assay-unit audit fails, the event definition cannot be reproduced from discharge/admission timestamps, complete-case availability differs materially by calendar period or department, the trajectory direction is unstable across core/expanded cohorts, or uncertainty is too wide to distinguish no incremental information from a clinically meaningful gain. Inconclusive does not become supportive through a more complex model.

A supportive result would justify a prospective, clinically adjudicated study of post-TACE monitoring and discharge pathways. An adverse result would argue against using these routinely recorded early changes as a stand-alone escalation signal. Neither result establishes hepatic decompensation, recurrence, treatment response, mortality benefit, or a safe clinical threshold.

## Evidence limits requiring adjudication or another study

The source does not reliably distinguish scheduled repeat TACE from unplanned readmission, and there is no dependable outpatient capture. 诊断类型 includes a small number of death-coded rows, but the HCC catalog provides no mortality table and only 29 death-coded patient/encounter keys in the audit; death is therefore exploratory and not a primary outcome.

There is no validated structured BCLC stage, tumor number/size, vascular invasion measure, performance status, Child–Pugh score, procedure technical dose/territory, or adjudicated hepatic decompensation endpoint. The text fields in examinations and clinical documents require expert review; the local lexical detector is explicitly not a diagnostic or comprehensive temporal NLP system. The lack of laboratory units prevents a validated ALBI calculation unless independently resolved. The snapshot is single-institution/source release with no external validation, and the reserved hash partition is inaccessible rather than an external cohort.

Consequently, Harbor can check cohort construction, timestamps, reproducible feature computation, model fitting, held-out uncertainty, and whether conclusions follow from computed outputs. It cannot establish clinical adjudication, causal treatment benefit, transportability, or that a lab-triggered intervention improves outcomes.

## Demonstration dispositions

- **Learning the natural history of human disease with generative transformers**: concrete adaptation is the interpretable paired baseline/early trajectory representation above. The HCC snapshot has longitudinal encounters, procedures, labs, medications, and diagnoses, but the proposed experiment does not claim to reproduce the demonstration’s generative model; its clinical question is tested with transparent landmark models and a bounded model-learning sensitivity.
- **Advancing cancer detection and treatment using longitudinal routine clinical data**: not pursued as a reproduction. The local README states that the main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; only the supplement was inspected. Its multi-cohort/validation and missingness-control ideas motivate patient-level held-out evaluation and missingness sensitivity, but HCC lacks the stated external cohorts and cannot support those stronger claims.
- **A Bayesian framework for longitudinal EHR and genetic discovery**: not pursued as the primary method. The readable article/supplement supports the value of longitudinal disease trajectories, but HCC has no germline genetic/PRS modality in its configured catalog and has limited temporal detail for the richer generative framework. The simpler trajectory question preserves the clinically important time-varying claim while respecting the available data.

The 30 imported expert seeds were retrieved and assessed before this no-parent design. Every seed is for eICU, MIMIC-IV, or UK Biobank; the expert-seed README explicitly supplies no HCC seed. They were rejected as HCC parents because using them would require inventing cross-dataset rows or modalities. Their questions remain preserved as non-use dispositions, not treated as scientifically disproven.

The feasibility audit read the complete local HCC encounter, procedure, diagnosis, and laboratory files without sampling. It filtered only on the explicit rules above: dated first observed TACE string, adult age, index encounter/discharge join, calendar cutoff, HCC diagnosis, assay name, parseable numeric result, and prespecified time windows.
