TITLE
Selected-first-repeat-attender cross-marker eGFR incident threshold experiment

PARENT AND SUBSTANTIVE REPAIR
This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis] under the frozen goal of making the cross-marker threshold experiment executable. The parent correctly imposed true baseline target-marker non-event restrictions, q=60, two directions, the exact 2–8-year first-repeat window, source bindings, multiplicity, support gates, and strict protocol-biomarker limits. Its remaining central defect was a primary estimand averaged over baseline nonattenders even though first-repeat elapsed time and the first-repeat outcome are undefined for them. This child removes that nonidentified transport entirely: primary inference is restricted to eligible first-repeat attenders, and attendance is reported only as selection diagnostics or as an explicitly assumption-indexed sensitivity, never as an identified baseline-population risk contrast.

CLINICAL QUESTION, EVIDENCE, AND UNRESOLVED CLAIM
The clinically consequential unresolved question is whether baseline discordance of one protocol filtration marker identifies an observable incident threshold crossing of the other marker at the first repeat, among people who actually return for an eligible first-repeat assessment. This can inform whether the discordant marker merits external validation as a monitoring signal, while not claiming that ordering it improves care. Existing local evidence supports the assay-field identities/units and computable eGFR protocol equations, and supports only an observable two-visit threshold-status experiment. The untested claim is a direction-specific association between baseline cross-marker exposure and a subsequent first-repeat threshold crossing after adjustment for the target marker, age, sex, and elapsed follow-up. No result can establish CKD, chronicity, true GFR decline, assay-reference traceability, causal prediction, treatment benefit, clinical utility, or transportability to people who do not return.

PRIMARY SELECTED-ATTENDER ESTIMAND
The primary estimand is entirely conditional on eligible first-repeat attendance. For d∈{A,B}, let Z_d=(eGFRtarget_0, age_0, sex, years), where years=(53-1.0-53-0.0)/365.25. Let m_d(e,z)=Pr(Y_d=1 | E_d=e,Z_d=z,R=1,T_d). Fit the frozen outcome model below on S_d and define
 RD_d^S = |S_d|^-1 Σ_{i∈S_d}[m_d(1,Z_i)-m_d(0,Z_i)].
The reported risks are p_d^S(e)=|S_d|^-1Σ_{i∈S_d}m_d(e,Z_i), and RD_d^S=p_d^S(1)-p_d^S(0), with the primary contrast in risk units, not an odds ratio. This is a descriptive model-standardized contrast among eligible first-repeat attenders; the notation m_d(e,z) does not assert causal intervention or transport to nonattenders.

The baseline B, T_d, and R counts are selection diagnostics only. We will not report, name, or interpret an identified T_d-population risk contrast. In particular, no inverse-attendance weighting, standardization over R=0, or prediction of a missing first-repeat outcome is part of primary inference. An assumption-indexed sensitivity may report how RD_d^S would change under a stated selection model, but it must be labeled nonidentified without that assumption, cannot be a confirmatory test, and cannot be called transport or a baseline-population risk estimate.

FROZEN MODEL TERMS, BASIS, AND INTERACTIONS
The primary outcome model contains no interactions. For each direction d, fit exactly
 logit Pr(Y_d=1 | S_d,E_d,Z_d)=α_d+β_d E_d+f_d(eGFRtarget_0)+g_d(age_0)+h_d(years)+γ_d sex,
where f_d, g_d, and h_d are natural restricted-cubic-spline bases with exactly four knots each and no other terms. To make the basis reproducible and avoid outcome-driven selection, compute knots once from the complete eligible-attender analysis set S_d before any model fitting or bootstrap: for baseline target eGFR use fixed empirical quantiles (5%, 35%, 65%, 95%) of eGFRtarget_0 in S_d; for age use (5%,35%,65%,95%) of age_0 in S_d; for years use (2,4,6,8) years. The lower and upper boundary knots are the first and fourth listed knots for each variable. If an empirical 5% or 95% knot is tied, retain the tied value and apply the deterministic rank-revealing check; a direction with a non-full-rank basis is inconclusive. Values outside the boundary are evaluated by the natural-spline linear tail, not clipped. Knots and basis construction are frozen from the original S_d and reused unchanged in every bootstrap replicate; no knot, degree, interaction, penalty, or covariate is selected after seeing outcomes.

For exact implementation, define the natural cubic truncated-power basis for variable x with boundary knots κ_1<κ_4 and internal knots κ_2,κ_3 by the intercept-free columns
 B_j(x)=(x-κ_j)_+^3-(x-κ_4)_+^3(κ_4-κ_j)/(κ_4-κ_1)+(x-κ_1)_+^3(κ_j-κ_1)/(κ_4-κ_1), j=2,3,
then use the two columns B_2,B_3 together with the ordinary linear x column; equivalently use one standard natural cubic spline implementation whose columns are numerically identical up to a full-rank reparameterization. The intercept is α_d and the exposure coefficient is β_d. Sex is included only after the field-31 coding certificate passes; otherwise the direction is inconclusive rather than silently recoded.

UNCERTAINTY AND BOOTSTRAP
Use a participant-level nonparametric bootstrap of S_d with replacement, preserving duplicate draws as frequency multiplicities. In each replicate, refit the same unpenalized logistic model with the original frozen knots/basis and no interactions, then standardize predictions over the replicate’s S_d empirical distribution with multiplicity. Do not resample B and manufacture repeat outcomes for nonattenders. Require at least 2,000 successful replicates per direction, report all failures and deterministic reasons, and use the same two-sided percentile 95% interval for both primary RDs. The bootstrap p-value is the two-sided plus-one tail proportion of replicate contrasts relative to zero under the predeclared sign convention; alternatively, if the implementation uses a Wald p-value, it must use that same method for both directions and state it before execution. The executable default is percentile interval plus two-sided normal/Wald test based on the bootstrap standard error, with the method frozen before reading results. A materially unstable or failed bootstrap is inconclusive, not evidence.

ATTENDANCE SELECTION DIAGNOSTICS AND ASSUMPTION-INDEXED SENSITIVITY
Attendance is not an estimand modifier. Report, by direction and exposure, R proportions within T_d, baseline covariate summaries for R=1 versus R=0, standardized mean differences, and aggregate missingness/eligibility reasons. These diagnose selection and cannot establish what would have happened at an unobserved repeat. If an assumption-indexed sensitivity is run, predeclare a logistic selection model R~E+the same frozen baseline spline terms (target eGFR, age, and sex; years is excluded because undefined for R=0), a fixed probability floor of .005, and a scalar selection-bias parameter δ that shifts the repeat-outcome logit for R=0 by δ conditional on (E,Z); report a prespecified grid δ∈{-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1}. Label every resulting quantity as assumption-indexed and nonidentified. It is not included in the primary family, cannot support transport, and cannot convert R=0 into observed first-repeat evidence.

MULTIPLICITY
The sole confirmatory family is the two selected-attender primary risk differences RD_A^S and RD_B^S. Compute two-sided raw p-values using the frozen bootstrap/Wald procedure, sort p_(1)≤p_(2), and apply the prespecified Holm step-down: adjusted values min(1,2p_(1)) and min(1,p_(2)); reject the first only if p_(1)≤.025 and the second only if p_(2)≤.05. Cells, attendance diagnostics, calibration, equation checks, sensitivity values, and model diagnostics are not additional confirmatory hypotheses. The two directions are not claimed to be reciprocal or renal-specific merely because both are analyzed.

FALSIFICATION AND INTERPRETATION
Support for a direction requires estimability, a positive prespecified direction, Holm-family significance, a 95% interval excluding zero, acceptable cell support/rank/separation/calibration, and concordance with the unweighted selected-attender descriptive tabulation and predeclared diagnostics. Support means only that baseline cross-marker threshold discordance is associated with first-repeat target-marker threshold status among eligible first-repeat attenders in this UKB snapshot and protocol. It does not establish a clinical threshold, CKD, chronicity, true GFR decline, causal effect, utility, benefit, or transport.

Adverse evidence is a null or reversed direction, loss after adjustment for the target-marker spline, strong dependence on the predeclared selection sensitivity, poor calibration, or support failure; this weakens or falsifies the selected-attender protocol-biomarker reclassification claim for that direction but does not prove absence of renal biology. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse cells, separation, rank failure, unstable bootstrap, failed equation/unit/sex certification, unresolved assay traceability, or failure to meet the declared model gates. A result in one direction alone is direction-specific. Any stronger conclusion about nonattenders, baseline-population risk, clinical outcomes, intervention, or patient benefit requires external follow-up with outcomes for nonattenders, clinical adjudication, measured GFR/CKD evidence, or another study.
