# MerTK-instructed brake vs composition/state-marker: dose dosimetry plus an orthogonal monocyte-intrinsic perturbation line (episode 12)

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (valid, mu 30.99). Key works [K1],[K2],[K3]. Screening status: **planned, not executed**.

## Unresolved question
Does the MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) synovial macrophage state **actively brake** the FLS effector program, or is the pos-vs-neg effector difference a **composition/admixture** artifact — total macrophage load, resident-like vs SPP1+ state, or FLS donor identity? The parent tested a within-donor pos-vs-neg score and a single 1:3 / 3:1 ratio pair; with n=1 per arm and one dataset it could not separate a graded causal brake from load/composition. This child adds (A) a formal **macrophage-dose dosimetry model** over the ratio-titration wells with a pre-registered effector score, and (B) an **orthogonal, macrophage-intrinsic perturbation line** in a distinct dataset (E-MTAB-8316) and readout compartment.

## Clinical importance
~1/3 of RA patients do not sustain remission. If MerTK+CD206+ macrophages causally brake matrix-degrading/inflammatory FLS output in a **dose-dependent** way, MerTK-directed resolution therapy is warranted. If the association is load/composition or donor context, enriching the marker would not restore resolution [K3], and MerTK-family inhibition would not predictably worsen effector output. [K2] shows MerTK+CD206+ cells can acquire pro-arthritogenic function, so MerTK status is not sufficient for protection.

## Leading claim vs strongest rival
- **Leading (causal brake):** effector score falls monotonically as the MerTKpos fraction rises; the sign reverses with MerTK-inhibitor pre-treatment; a direction is reproduced by a macrophage-intrinsic MerTK-family perturbation.
- **Rival (composition/state/donor):** the pos-vs-neg difference is absorbed by total macrophage content, an SPP1+ vs FOLR2/TREM2 state covariate, and FLS donor identity; titration is flat/non-monotone [K1 supports a remission-associated subset — an association, not a brake; K2 supplies the pro-arthritogenic rival].

## Pre-registered effector score
`FLS_effector_score` = mean of per-gene z-scores (across the 33 E-MTAB-8873 columns, on log2(CPM+1)) of the **exact symbols** MMP1, MMP3, MMP9, MMP13, IL6, CXCL8, CXCL10, CCL2, CCL5 — all verified present. Summary rule: single unweighted mean of the 9 gene z-scores; no data-driven gene selection.

## Macrophage-dose variable
For titration wells, `pos_fraction = #Mpos / (#Mpos + #Mneg)` from the well name (0.25 for `_1Mpos_3Mneg`, 0.75 for `_3Mpos_1Mneg`); `total_macrophage_load` recorded as well. Single-Mpos/single-Mneg wells give pos_fraction 1.0 / 0.0.

## Baseline vs substantive alternative
- **Baseline (simple):** within-FLS-donor paired effector-score difference (Mpos − Mneg); exact paired sign/permutation statistic over the FLS-donor unit (macrophage donors nested); no ordinary donor t-test (design `donor_status = requires_contributing_donor_model`).
- **Substantive alternative A (dosimetry):** `effector_score ~ β1·pos_fraction + β2·total_macrophage_load + β3·FLS_donor` with a **monotonicity test** across pos_fraction ∈ {0.25, 0.75} and inhibitor reversal. Information the baseline loses: whether response is graded in dose and reversible by MerTK inhibition (a causal brake) rather than a step explained by load.
- **Substantive alternative B (composition/covariate rival):** add an SPP1+ vs FOLR2/TREM2 state covariate and FLS-donor fixed effects; ask whether β1 survives (brake) or is absorbed (composition/state/donor).
- **Learned/neural model:** not justified — a 445-gene targeted panel with 33 samples and no independent test set cannot support a generalisable learned model; documented non-use.
- **Orthogonal line (E-MTAB-8316):** paired donor-aware sign test of the resolution/MerTK-program score (e.g. MERTK, MRC1, AXL, CD163, FOLR2 mapped from ENSG) between UNC106238 and none, controlling donor. Aggregate repeated specimens within donor first. **4 donors → underpowered; labelled exploratory.**

## Two models make different predictions
- Causal brake predicts: negative slope on pos_fraction for pos wells; graded monotone shift (pos_fraction 0.25 > 0.75 suppression); inhibitor pre-treatment **increases** effector score relative to untreated pos (reversal).
- Composition/donor predicts: slope vanishes once total load and SPP1/FOLR2 state and FLS donor are modelled; titration flat or non-monotone; inhibitor has no consistent direction; pos-vs-neg difference tracks FLS donor.

## Split / contrast structure
Unit of inference = FLS donor; paired contrasts within donor; macrophage donor nested. Titration is a **within-donor dose contrast** (SA258R, SA263R), not independent replication. E-MTAB-8316 is a separate dataset and readout compartment (no cross-dataset join).

## Falsification criteria
- **Supportive:** consistent within-donor pos<neg; negative monotone pos_fraction slope; inhibitor raises effector score; orthogonal line shows compound-directional shift of the MerTK/resolution program.
- **Adverse:** no within-donor difference; flat/non-monotone titration; effect fully absorbed by total load, SPP1/state, or FLS donor; inhibitor direction absent/opposite; orthogonal line null/opposite.
- **Inconclusive:** n=1 per titration arm; GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3 ligand-receptor genes unmeasured; donor imbalance; ENSG unmapped genes; cross-sectional anchor only → cannot separate; needs targeted ligand/protein assay or wet-lab perturbation.
