## 13. Clinical adjudication and claims requiring another study

Clinical experts must adjudicate the procedure/prior-treatment dictionary, stored report meanings, modality conflicts, and pathology wording. An automatic verifier can check that those signed artifacts were used as frozen, but cannot establish their clinical correctness.

Specimen-linked pathology with timestamps and sampling protocol is required to test biological M2. Raw images and an image-reference process are required for imaging-feature accuracy. Report author/final/release/version/view logs are required for actual pre-decision availability. A prospective silent-mode study with a real candidate stream, review capacity, actions, and monitored subgroup performance is required for operational validity and fairness. A controlled impact study with treatment, harms, recurrence, survival, resource use, and costs is required for clinical utility or benefit. External sites are required for transportability.

Accordingly, the strongest possible supportive conclusion is: “In this frozen retrospective HCC eventual-report corpus, adjudicated report meanings added more than the prespecified fixed-K documentary-M2 capture margin beyond the same acquisition/documentation surface, robust to declared source/outcome states.” Nothing causal or prospective follows automatically.
