# Working Proposal — Episode 5 (HCC island)

## Scientific question
After TACE for hepatocellular carcinoma, do the EARLY (first post-procedure) laboratory
changes — relative to the pre-procedure baseline — predict 90-day readmission beyond what
is explained by baseline MELD score?

## Rationale and opening
- TACE is the most common treatment in this cohort (19,610 index TACE encounters, 16,438
  patients with pre+post labs). Post-embolization syndrome (PES) is common (55-68% in
  published studies) but prior work has focused on symptom-based definitions and procedural
  predictors (tumor size, number, embolization technique).
- The unresolved question: does the early laboratory trajectory carry independent prognostic
  information for 90-day readmission beyond baseline MELD? This would identify patients at
  risk of hepatic decompensation who should be monitored more closely or considered for
  alternative treatment strategies.

## Discovery probes (this episode, verified)
- n=16,438 patients with both pre- and post-TACE labs in the index encounter
- 90-day readmission: 5,331 events (32.4%)
- MELD available for 9,145 (55.6%); MELD-only AUC test=0.504
- MELD + 6 lab deltas + age + sex (logistic, imputed): AUC test=0.541 (+0.037)
- HistGBM (MELD+deltas+age+sex): AUC test=0.554
- Deltas-only GBM (full cohort, no MELD): AUC test=0.567
- Deltas-only LR (full cohort): AUC test=0.530
- Key logistic coefficients (MELD sub-cohort): d_hemoglobin (+0.395), d_albumin (+0.371),
  d_fibrinogen (+0.230), d_platelet (−0.145) — all in the expected direction
- Quartile analysis (test set): d_ALT graded (Q1=27.9% → Q4=36.4%); d_bili non-monotonic
- These are exploratory; the formal experiment requires proper train/test split,
  calibration, and comparison against a well-specified baseline.

## Hypothesis
In HCC patients undergoing TACE, the relative change in total bilirubin, ALT, platelet
count, and fibrinogen between the last pre-procedure lab and the first post-procedure lab
is independently associated with 90-day readmission, after adjusting for baseline MELD,
age, and sex.

## Population
- All patients in the HCC dataset with:
  * At least one TACE-related procedure (手术 contains 'TACE' or '栓塞')
  * A TACE-containing index encounter (first TACE per patient)
  * At least one lab measurement in the index encounter before TACE
  * At least one lab measurement in the index encounter on/after TACE
  * 90-day follow-up: any new admission within 90 days of t0
- Exclusions: missing t0, missing encounter boundaries, no labs in either window

## Temporal boundaries
- t0 = first TACE procedure time (开始时间) for the patient
- Pre-window: admit ≤ t < t0 (last lab before TACE)
- Post-window: t0 ≤ t ≤ disch (first lab on/after TACE)
- Outcome window: t0 < admit_next ≤ t0 + 90 days

## Variables
### Baseline (pre-TACE, last measurement in pre-window)
- 总胆红素 (total bilirubin), 肌酐 (creatinine), 国际标准化比值 (INR) → MELD
- 白蛋白 (albumin), 血小板计数 (platelet count), 丙氨酸氨基转移酶 (ALT)
- 甲胎蛋白 (AFP), 血红蛋白 (hemoglobin), 纤维蛋白原 (fibrinogen)
- 年龄 (age), 性别 (sex) from encounters

### Early post-TACE (first measurement in post-window)
- Same analytes → compute relative deltas:
  d_analyte = (post − pre) / max(pre, ε) where ε is a small constant for stability

### Outcome
- y90 = 1 if any new admission (入院时间) occurs in (t0, t0+90d], else 0

## Analysis plan
1. **Cohort construction**: Build the index cohort as described above.
2. **Baseline model (MELD only)**: Logistic regression with MELD as sole predictor
   (sub-cohort with complete MELD components, n≈9,145).
3. **Extended model (MELD + deltas)**: Logistic regression with MELD + 6 lab deltas +
   age + sex; median imputation for missing deltas.
4. **Gradient boosting alternative**: HistGradientBoostingClassifier (handles NaN natively)
   with the same features; compare AUC and Brier score.
5. **Full-cohort deltas-only model**: HistGBM with 6 deltas only (no MELD requirement),
   n≈16,438.
6. **Train/test split**: 70/30 by patient (no patient appears in both).
7. **Evaluation**: AUC, Brier score, logistic coefficients with 95% CIs, quartile
   readmission rates for key deltas, calibration (mean predicted vs actual).
8. **Falsification criteria**:
   - SUPPORTIVE: Extended model AUC > baseline AUC + 0.02 in test set, with at least
     one lab delta having a significant coefficient (p<0.05) in the logistic model.
   - ADVERSE: Extended model AUC ≤ baseline AUC + 0.02, or no lab delta is significant
     after adjusting for MELD.
   - INCONCLUSIVE: AUC difference between 0.01-0.02, or significant deltas but poor
     calibration, or event rate in test set too low for stable estimation.

## Baseline vs learned alternative
- **Baseline**: MELD score (established, interpretable, clinically used).
- **Learned alternative**: HistGBM with lab deltas. This can capture non-linear
  interactions (e.g., high baseline bilirubin + large relative rise may be more dangerous
  than either alone) and heterogeneous effects that a linear model would miss.
- **What the alternative reveals**: Whether the prognostic signal in early lab changes is
  linear or has threshold/interaction effects; whether the signal is driven by a few
  extreme values or is a graded phenomenon.

## Key references
- [K1] Hund et al. 2023 (PMID 36481322, JVIR): Six-year same-day discharge after TACE;
  30-day readmission tracked; baseline Child-Pugh, encephalopathy, ascites associated
  with readmission; no post-procedure lab trajectory tested.
- [K2] Fritsche et al. 2018 (PMID 29478795, JVIR): Same-day discharge after TACE;
  30-day readmission; Child-Pugh B/C, encephalopathy history, ascites predictive;
  age, tumor number, embolization level not predictive; no lab trajectory or MELD model.
- [K3] Hu et al. 2021 (PMID 33038139, AJG): ML models (LR, SVM, RFC) for 90-day
  readmission in cirrhosis achieve AUC ~0.62, equivalent to MELD-Na; additional
  biomarkers needed.

## Limitations
- Single-center (HCC dataset is one hospital); generalizability uncertain.
- Readmission as outcome is a proxy for hepatic decompensation; not all readmissions
  are due to liver-related causes (no ICD-coded cause of readmission available).
- No mortality data (no death records in diagnoses table).
- Lab timing: "first post-procedure lab" may be 1-5 days after TACE depending on
  hospital practice; the day-1 window is approximate.
- No imaging data to confirm tumor response or progression as competing risk.
- PES is not directly measured (no symptom scores); the lab trajectory is a surrogate.
- MELD available for only 55.6% of cohort (missing INR or bilirubin); the full-cohort
  deltas-only model addresses this but lacks the MELD baseline comparison.

## What a supportive result would establish
- Early laboratory changes after TACE carry independent prognostic information for
  90-day readmission beyond baseline MELD.
- This would support using early post-TACE labs as a triage tool for closer follow-up.

## What an adverse result would establish
- Baseline MELD captures essentially all the prognostic information available from
  early post-TACE labs; the lab trajectory is not an independent predictor.
- This would argue against adding early post-TACE labs to risk stratification.

## What an inconclusive result would mean
- The signal is weak or confounded; further work would need a larger cohort,
  cause-specific readmission coding, or a different outcome (e.g., mortality with
  external linkage).
