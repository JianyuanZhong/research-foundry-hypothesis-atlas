# An independently anchored departure-status ledger for copied, tentative, completed, and superseded recommendations

## Decision question and targeted validity repair

The frozen decision remains whether any exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated only for a prospective **within-site silent validation** of the inherited `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes. This child preserves the first-eligible all-opportunity population, reciprocal pre-departure opportunity chain, canonical discharge artifact, exhaustive `Y,V,H,Z` states, exact support atoms, all-unit worst-case admission routing, fixed positive-probability at-most-900-admission design, adverse-compatible finite-population inference, and strict noncausal ceiling of parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`.

The parents establish independent textual reproducibility and route-concealed actionability/supersession measurements. Their consequential residual gap is **reference validity**. Panels can agree that a sentence is an active plan while it is copied boilerplate, merely tentative, documents an action already performed, or is superseded elsewhere in the same available admission artifacts. More panel agreement does not make the recommendation a clinically live obligation.

Available MIMIC evidence can support a narrower statement: an admission contains an explicit, target-grounded, future-directed textual action that is not contradicted by an explicit target-linked completion, cancellation, or replacement in the canonical discharge and admission-linked pre-departure radiology artifacts. MIMIC cannot prove that the action was clinically appropriate, still required at physical departure, visible, assigned, communicated, ordered, scheduled, executed, beneficial, or safe. The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is that at least one inherited support-qualified `AUTO` or `RECON` atom remains eligible after an independently rendered and independently adjudicated **departure-status ledger** assigns every challenged unit an adverse-compatible status and after high-specificity source anchors and copy-provenance controls pass. Failure excludes the affected atom; absence of identifying evidence is `UNKNOWN/DEFER`, not a favorable reference label.

## Bounded source check motivating the ledger

A read-only lexical feasibility profile was run before proposal publication, solely to verify that the proposed reference threats occur in available fields. It read the first 100,000 physical discharge rows and first 300,000 physical radiology rows in gzip order, without clinical-label or population filtering. Among those nonrandom rows, 7,461 discharge texts contained a broad pulmonary/lung-nodule phrase; their normalized target/follow-up sentences included 7,492 occurrences belonging to fingerprints repeated at least twice. Of 1,781 sampled admission keys with target text in both files, 538 had an exact normalized target/follow-up sentence overlap. Tentative, completion, and replacement lexical patterns were also observable. These are deliberately labeled unvalidated lexical counts, not prevalences, patient states, or evidence that any repeated text was inappropriate. The executable method and frozen aggregate output are supporting files.

## Presealed provenance ledger: objective facts, not clinical labels

Before any A1/A2/S1/H3 or new reference label is revealed, tokenize and hash every candidate recommendation span and its DS sentence using a frozen normalizer that changes only Unicode form, case, whitespace, punctuation spacing, and deidentification placeholders. Measurements, anatomy, laterality, modality, timing, negation, and modal verbs are preserved. Store no source text in public outputs. For each unit record:

1. exact and token-longest-common-subsequence match to its own reciprocal radiology recommendation span;
2. the number of distinct admissions and subjects containing the exact normalized DS sentence; `RECURRENT_5` means at least five distinct subjects;
3. whether the DS span has a target-specific grounding token matching the source chain: anatomy/laterality, measurement, modality, comparison, or explicitly linked interval;
4. DS section coordinates from a frozen section parser, with `SECTION_UNKNOWN` when headings are absent or ambiguous;
5. every same-admission radiology report after the source recommendation but before `D` and stored before `D`, with timestamps and target/action candidate links; and
6. every target-linked firm, tentative, completion, cancellation, replacement, and contradiction candidate span anywhere in the canonical DS, including spans outside the apparent follow-up section.

`RECURRENT_5`, source-copy similarity, or a section heading is never itself a favorable or adverse clinical label. Recurrent wording may be an appropriate template; verbatim source propagation may be faithful. These flags calibrate whether reviewers rely on generic language without target grounding and make copying visible rather than assuming it away.

## Independent departure-status reference packets and labels

Every admission in the inherited 300-admission actionability challenge receives this layer; no new admission is sampled. If the inherited challenge uses fewer than 300, use all of it and report the limitation. There are exactly two additional compact whole-admission reference readings per challenged admission, at most 600 readings total. Exceeding 600, roster insufficiency, or inability to include every unit is `REFERENCE_INFEASIBLE`; no atom passes. Packet creation, rosters, codebooks, parser, hashes, and order are frozen before labels.

Reference panels R1 and R2 are roster-disjoint from each other and from all inherited index, audit, A1/A2/S1/H1/H2/H3 panels. They receive independent pseudonyms, orderings, and independently written codebooks with no shared examples or training cases. Neither sees atoms, strata, weights, routes, inherited labels, other-panel labels, or expected control answers. R1 receives an **assertion-centered view**: each source recommendation, all DS target/action spans in original local context, section coordinates, and explicit timestamps. R2 receives a **trajectory-centered view**: the chronological reciprocal chain, all other admission-linked pre-`D`/pre-storage radiology reports, and the entire canonical DS with candidate spans unhighlighted. Both views display missingness and distinguish `charttime`, `storetime`, and `D`; neither implies that storage equals visibility.

For each inherited recommendation unit, each panel independently records an atomic tuple:

* target identity and whether a DS action is target-grounded (`EXACT`, `COMPATIBLE`, `MISMATCH`, `UNKNOWN`);
* force: `FIRM_FUTURE`, `TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `INFORMATIONAL`, `NEGATED_OR_CANCELLED`, `CONFLICT`, `UNKNOWN`;
* action, interval/timing, named recipient, and responsibility wording, each as observed/absent/contradicted/unknown;
* documented action status: `NOT_DOCUMENTED_COMPLETED`, `MATCHING_ACTION_PERFORMED_PRE_D`, `PERFORMED_BUT_NEXT_ACTION_REMAINS`, `COMPLETION_AMBIGUOUS`, `UNKNOWN`;
* replacement status: `NONE_EXPLICIT`, `EXPLICIT_REPLACEMENT`, `EXPLICIT_CHANGED_INTERVAL`, `COMPETING_PLANS`, `UNKNOWN`;
* provenance: `CASE_GROUNDED`, `RECURRENT_BUT_GROUNDED`, `RECURRENT_UNGROUNDED`, `SOURCE_COPY_GROUNDED`, `SOURCE_COPY_UNGROUNDED`, `PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN`; and
* one compatible set over `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`, `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, `R_TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `R_COMPLETED_NO_REMAINING_ACTION`, `R_SUPERSEDED`, `R_INACTIVE_INFORMATIONAL`, `R_CONFLICT`, and `R_UNKNOWN`.

`R_FIRM_ACTIVE` requires an explicit target-grounded future action, firm rather than solely tentative language, and no explicit matching completion without a remaining future action, cancellation, replacement, or competing plan in the supplied artifacts. It does not require that a clinician should act. `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE` requires a target-grounded firm future action but incomplete timing/recipient/responsibility or unresolved plan detail. A recommendation copied from radiology can enter either firm state only if target grounding is independently supported; recurrent ungrouded text cannot. `TENTATIVE_ONLY`, completion without an explicit remaining action, supersession, inactivity, conflict, and unknown are never converted to active by consensus.

No senior consensus collapses R1/R2. The primary reference compatible set is their union plus every state compatible with missing/corrupt source evidence. Favorable intersection is reported only diagnostically. R1/R2 are measurements, not a gold standard.

## High-specificity anchors and falsification controls

The following anchors use observable source relations and are deliberately narrower than clinical truth:

* **explicit negative anchor:** a target-linked cancellation/no-further-action statement, or an explicit replacement relation naming old and new action/interval, present verbatim in the DS;
* **documented completion anchor:** a later target-linked pre-`D`, pre-storage radiology report documents the matching recommended imaging action, and the DS contains no explicit next future action; this anchors documented completion, not clinical resolution;
* **tentative-only anchor:** every target-linked action clause uses only conditional/optional language and no firm future clause is present anywhere in the permitted artifacts;
* **firm textual anchor:** a dedicated plan/instruction span contains target, future action, firm modal/imperative, and timing or recipient, is target-grounded to the source chain, and no permitted artifact contains an explicit completion, cancellation, replacement, or contradiction; and
* **copy-provenance anchor:** exact cross-subject recurrence or exact source-to-DS copying as defined above; it anchors textual provenance only.

R1/R2 must classify anchor components without seeing anchor names. Locked controls are inserted into the reference packet queue before review: a copied firm sentence with target grounding preserved; the same sentence with target attributes swapped to an incompatible target; a firm-to-tentative modal change; an inserted explicit completion; an inserted explicit replacement; and a section/format-only perturbation. The copied grounded sentence must retain textual status, the target swap must lose grounding, firm-to-tentative must change force only, completion/replacement must change status only, and formatting must change nothing. Controls do not rescue failure on real cases and do not establish clinical correctness.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

A supportive result requires all inherited and new atom-specific gates and at least one fully supported exact atom. It supports only this statement: for the available MIMIC textual artifacts, the atom's route is reproducible and its candidate action units are explicitly target-grounded, firm and future-directed, without observed target-linked completion, cancellation, replacement, or contradiction under adverse-compatible reference review. That is sufficient only to nominate a prospective within-site **silent** workflow study; all unlisted signatures remain `DEFER`.

An adverse result is an atom-specific excess of tentative-only, completed, superseded, inactive, recurrent-ungrounded, mismatched, conflicting, or unknown units; a reference/actionability discordance bound crossing a gate; or an anchor/control falsification. It excludes that atom from nomination but does not prove clinical harm, negligence, or that the recommendation was inappropriate.

An inconclusive result includes sparse route or anchor classes, inability to inspect every unit, infeasible 600-reading workload, nonresponse, broken seals, ambiguous target matching, missing pre-`D` evidence, or wide compatible bounds. Inconclusive is not a pass and defaults to `DEFER`.

Even a perfect result cannot establish that a recommendation was a **clinically live departure obligation**. MIMIC lacks actual viewing, edit/sign/finalization semantics, responsibility assignment, conversations, patient preferences, external records, target-linked orders/referrals, scheduling, execution, appropriateness, malignancy, benefit, harm, or forward/external transport. The required next study is a separately approved actual-calendar prospective silent bridge capturing those workflow and clinical facts. Only expert-governed prospective evidence could support live use, safety, utility, or causal benefit.
