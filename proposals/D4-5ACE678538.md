# Case-only cancer-mechanism decomposition: CRC, other gastrointestinal, and non-GI malignancy

## Parent and unresolved question

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's selected recorded-case population, landmark, horizon, washout, CBC semantics, outcome ascertainment safeguard, and `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED` gate, while replacing the broad CRC-versus-all-other-malignancy contrast with a prespecified, mechanism-proximal decomposition.

The strongest supported prior claim is limited: in 998 people selected because a first qualifying malignancy was recorded during `[E,H]` (75 CRC and 923 non-CRC), the broad adjusted CBC residual-moment analysis did not reject its null, but was inconclusive rather than evidence of equivalence. The unresolved claim is whether that concordant non-rejection masked heterogeneity because non-CRC malignancies combine cancers with different relationships to occult blood loss/inflammation. The falsifiable question is whether the adverse-oriented CBC trajectory block has a different adjusted association across three exhaustive first-diagnosis groups: CRC, other gastrointestinal (GI) malignancy, and non-GI malignancy.

This remains a case-only association analysis. It does not compare people without a recorded cancer and does not estimate cancer incidence or prospective risk.

## Estimand and analysis

Let `G=(G_CRC,G_otherGI)` be two indicators, with non-GI as the mutually exclusive reference category. Let `C` be exactly age at `L`, sex, repeat-visit Hb, repeat-visit MCV, repeat-visit platelet count, and `lag_late`. Let `S=(S_Hb,S_MCV,S_PLT)`. The primary global null is the six-dimensional residual-moment null

`E[(G - pi(C)) tensor (S - g(C))] = 0`,

where `pi(C)=E[G|C]` is estimated separately for each group indicator using the prespecified binary ridge-logistic nuisance model, and `g(C)=E[S|C]` is estimated separately for each trajectory using ridge-linear nuisance models. The six components test whether each trajectory's adjusted association differs between CRC or other-GI and non-GI. This is a low-dimensional, mechanism-anchored heterogeneity target, not conditional independence of all possible nonlinear functions.

Use the existing 20 repetitions of five-fold cross-fitting with seeds `20260909` through `20260928`. Outer folds are jointly stratified by the three-level group, `lag_late`, and sex; each stratum must have support in every fold, with no zero group in any training or test fold. The aggregate feasibility minimum of 5 is a prerequisite, not evidence that estimates are precise. All fold assignments are fixed before fitting and shared across nuisance and score calculations. Standardize only continuous variables using training-fold means and standard deviations; leave sex, lag, and group indicators on their defined scales. Inner five-fold tuning is training-only over ridge penalties `10^-4,...,10^4`, selecting the largest penalty tied at minimum loss. The intercept and `lag_late` are unpenalized in each group nuisance model.

For each held-out person, form `u_k=G_k-pi_hat_k^{-fold}(C)` and `v=S-g_hat^{-fold}(C)`, and stack `psi=u tensor v` in the fixed order `(CRC-Hb, CRC-MCV, CRC-PLT, otherGI-Hb, otherGI-MCV, otherGI-PLT)`. Average each participant's score vector over the 20 repetitions before inference, so repeated appearances are not treated as independent. Estimate the participant-level robust covariance and use the six-dimensional quadratic Wald statistic. Calibrate it with at least 20,000 independent standard-normal participant multipliers applied to centered fixed cross-fitted score contributions, using a plus-one p-value. This is multiplier calibration, not an exact permutation test.

The primary global test is two-sided at alpha 0.05. Report all six estimates, robust standard errors, the simultaneous six-dimensional 95% ellipsoid, covariance conditioning, and the multiplier distribution. Component intervals are secondary and must use a prespecified simultaneous adjustment (max-statistic multiplier intervals or Bonferroni alpha/6). Do not interpret a component before the global gate.

Gatekeeping is closed and prespecified. If the global six-dimensional test rejects, test the two mechanism contrasts jointly as a two-dimensional family: (i) CRC versus other-GI, and (ii) CRC versus non-GI, each using the corresponding residual-moment contrast and the same cross-fitted score construction. Reject a contrast only under the closed-testing rule: its local two-sided p-value and the global p-value must both be below 0.05, with max-statistic familywise adjustment across the two contrasts. A CRC-versus-other-GI result is the clinically focused test of whether CRC resembles other luminal GI malignancy more than the aggregated non-GI comparator; it cannot be reported if the global gate fails. The other-GI group is not a negative control; it is the prespecified mechanistic comparator. RDW replacement (`30070`) is an exploratory negative-control analysis only and cannot open any gate.

As a non-primary benchmark, fit ordinary unpenalized multinomial logistic regression of the three-level group on `C` versus `C+S`, and calibrate the added slope block with at least 5,000 parametric bootstrap samples generated under the comparator-only model. Require concordance of global direction and interpretation with the orthogonal score. A failed or discordant benchmark makes the result inconclusive rather than motivating method selection. Because CRC has only 75 cases, no site-specific model, interaction fishing, or high-dimensional subtype search is permitted.

## Feasibility, audit, and falsification criteria

The experiment is computationally invalid if source hashes or frozen-cohort hash change; any join duplicates or loses a frozen `eid`; same-suffix date/code pairing is violated; the recovered CRC status or first date disagrees with the frozen cohort; a death or washout event is qualified; day `E` is assigned to washout; any unrecorded-cancer person enters; group definitions are changed after inspecting outcomes; a fold lacks every group/lag/sex support requirement; held-out values influence scaling, tuning, nuisance fitting, or group labels; nuisance predictions are nonfinite; or score and bootstrap statistics differ in definition.

Report full cohort and event counts, group-by-lag and group-by-lag-by-sex counts, every fold's group support, nuisance convergence and selected penalties, cross-fitted log loss/Brier diagnostics, slope-nuisance error, score finiteness, covariance eigenvalues, and multiplier tail behavior. Severe lack of overlap, nonfinite calibration, repeated fold failure, covariance conditioning above `10^10`, or fewer than 20,000 finite multiplier draws forces `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED`/inconclusive interpretation for this experiment.

Use two falsifications that are independent of the scientific conclusion. First, jointly permute all three slope columns within lag-by-sex strata while holding group and `C` fixed, repeating under 20 prespecified seeds; this is an implementation/calibration diagnostic only and should not show repeated extreme global statistics. Second, replace `S` with the RDW trajectory from `30070` and label it exploratory; it cannot rescue a failed primary test. These falsifications do not prove exchangeability or biological absence.

## Interpretation and claim limits

Supportive evidence requires global rejection, at least one closed-testing contrast rejection, coherent mechanism-compatible direction, stable nuisance/covariance/calibration diagnostics, and concordance with the multinomial benchmark. It would support only a selected-recorded-case association pattern in which the broad non-CRC aggregate masks group heterogeneity. It would not establish occult bleeding, inflammation, pathology, stage, or mechanism without clinical adjudication.

Adverse evidence requires stable calibration and a prespecified equivalence margin, which is not available in this dataset. Therefore a non-rejection is not equivalence. A precise result that is incompatible with the hypothesized CRC/other-GI distinction could weaken that selected-case heterogeneity hypothesis, but cannot prove no heterogeneity elsewhere.

Inconclusive evidence includes a non-rejecting global gate, broad intervals, sparse-fold or nuisance instability, score/benchmark disagreement, failed falsification calibration, or dependence on exploratory RDW or an alternative grouping. The likely conclusion for a non-rejecting global test is `INCONCLUSIVE_WITHOUT_EVIDENCE_OF_GROUP_HETEROGENEITY`, not evidence that groups are biologically identical.

Selection conditions on a future recorded malignancy and can induce collider bias; lag adjustment is post-landmark. The release-matched registry completeness/prognosis gate remains `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED`. These files lack certified ascertainment completeness, pathology and staging, symptoms, FIT, endoscopy, treatment, routine-care CBC timing, outcomes in people without recorded malignancy, harms, costs, and external validation. No result may claim incidence, prospective risk, causality, screening performance, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, lead time, referral utility, transportability, completeness, or patient benefit. Stronger biological or clinical conclusions require pathology/registry adjudication and an independent population-based cohort with protocolized repeat CBC measurements.

The substantive advance over the parent is not another broad null test: it directly tests, with exhaustive and feasibility-verified cancer grouping, whether aggregation of heterogeneous non-CRC malignancies obscured a biologically interpretable GI-versus-non-GI pattern while preserving the exact selected-case design and its evidence limits.
