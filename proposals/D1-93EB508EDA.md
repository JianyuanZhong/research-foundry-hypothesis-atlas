# Route-concealed actionability and target-linked supersession audit

## Decision question, supported evidence, and hypothesis

The frozen decision is whether any exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated for a prospective **within-site silent validation** of the inherited admission routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER`. No retrospective result authorizes an alert, copied recommendation, order, referral, or patient-facing action. The estimand remains text-defined reproducibility and contamination, not guideline correctness, clinician performance, safety, benefit, follow-up, or causal effect.

The available evidence supports construction of a first-eligible, all-opportunity admission frame from linked radiology/discharge artifacts, reciprocal chronology, service/ICU proxies, and stored timestamps. It does not establish physical departure visibility, clinical appropriateness, responsibility, communication, patient preference, or execution. The unresolved, falsifiable claim is narrower: among support-qualified atoms that pass the parent’s split-view and independent holistic reproducibility audit, do route-concealed reviewers find an explicit, target-linked, still-active discharge action need, and do they find that no explicit target-linked plan superseded it? If not, apparent reproducibility is not actionable even when panels agree.

The primary hypothesis is that at least one support-qualified `AUTO` or `RECON` atom will pass both inherited adverse gates and the frozen actionability/supersession gates below. Falsification is an adverse-compatible atom-level bound crossing any gate, a route-invariant control flip, or a route-decisive control violation. Agreement is a measurement result, never a clinical gold standard. No latent-class model, shared-error point prevalence, or assumed nonzero common-error bound is used.

## Route-concealed packet views and frozen categories

Generate all packets from sealed raw extracts before labels. Each challenged admission has separate, pseudonymized packet views with independent ordering and no component labels, routes, strata, atom, weights, post-`D` text, POE, or outcomes:

* **A1 active-action packet:** reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, permitted admission/death/disposition fields, canonical DS and permitted DS detail, with explicit missingness and timestamps. It records only target, requested action, timing, recipient, responsibility language, polarity, and departure-artifact status.
* **A2 independent active-action packet:** the same raw evidence, independently rendered and ordered, read by a disjoint roster using independently written `CB-A2`; it never displays A1 fields or parent labels.
* **S1 supersession packet:** the same allowed evidence rendered with all recommendation wording and target-linked plan spans retained but without any route, component abstraction, or A1/A2 output. A disjoint roster records the target-linked superseding-plan hierarchy.
* **H3 holistic challenge packet:** after A1/A2/S1 append-only sealing, a third disjoint roster receives the whole packet and records whether each asserted actionability or supersession field is supported, contradicted, or indeterminate by the supplied artifact. H3 is another measurement system, not an oracle.

All rosters, packet hashes, codebook hashes, packet order, and seal order are frozen before review. No reviewer assigns a route. Parent routes are derived only after all component labels are sealed by the inherited locked Cartesian-product compatibility table; the added labels may only block an atom or map to `RECON`/`DEFER` under the inherited precedence.

The mutually exclusive, exhaustive **actionability categories** per recommendation unit are fixed: `A1_ACTIVE_PORTABLE` (explicit target-linked action and timing/recipient are present, no explicit superseder, and no contradiction); `A2_ACTIVE_RECONCILE` (active target-linked need is present but timing, recipient, responsibility, or plan is incomplete/ambiguous); `A3_SUPERSEDED_EXPLICIT` (an explicit target-linked alternative/replacement/changed interval supersedes the recommendation); `A4_RESOLVED_OR_INACTIVE` (explicitly resolved, cancelled, negative, or no longer applicable); `A5_NO_TARGETED_ACTION` (recommendation is informational/non-actionable for discharge); and `A6_UNKNOWN` (missing, contradictory, or indeterminate evidence). The **supersession categories** are fixed: `S0_NONE`, `S1_EXPLICIT_REPLACEMENT` (target-linked alternative plus replacement relation and timing/recipient), `S2_EXPLICIT_ALTERNATIVE_INCOMPLETE`, `S3_COMPETING_CONTRADICTORY_PLANS`, and `S4_UNKNOWN`. A3 dominates all other categories; S3 and S4 are adverse. Duplicate restatements are one unit; distinct target/action/timing units remain distinct. Any uncertainty is represented as a compatible set, not forced to a favorable label.

A unit may enter `AUTO` only if the inherited route is AUTO and every adverse-compatible panel output is `A1_ACTIVE_PORTABLE` with `S0_NONE`; `A2_ACTIVE_RECONCILE`, any A3/S2/S3/S4, or any unresolved minority unit maps to `RECON` or `DEFER` under inherited precedence. A unit with explicit replacement is never AUTO. These are operational text-route rules, not assertions that propagation is clinically right.

## Inference, controls, release, and falsification

For each final atom and route, report design-weighted unit and admission estimates, all-unit worst-admission estimates, capture, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, route discordance, actionability state, explicit supersession, workload, and missingness. Use exact finite-population inversion for binary outcomes and 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates with all admission-level labels retained together; use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over inherited gates, atom gates, panel discordance, actionability, supersession, controls, workload, and minimax endpoints. Primary intervals are adverse-compatible: only jointly observed support is in a lower endpoint, while every unresolved/disagreement pattern is assigned to the adverse state in the upper endpoint. This is identifiable partial identification from observed labels, not an estimate of latent shared error. Latent-class or kappa analyses are diagnostics only and cannot narrow bounds or certify.

Before labels, create the locked controls for each feasible challenged case: (i) route-irrelevant formatting/section-order/nuisance-token pair, which must preserve unit inventory and route; (ii) one-field route-decisive replacement or timing pair, whose route may change only according to the inherited table; (iii) masked/inserted recommendation or plan pair with a frozen compatibility-set transition; and (iv) target-linked plan versus non-target plan pair matched for wording and provenance. Reviewers are blinded to control type and expected answer. Any invariant flip or unlisted decisive change falsifies the affected run; control performance cannot rescue a real-case failure.

The following gates are frozen operational nomination rules and are not clinical harm thresholds. A support-qualified atom needs inherited support/event/effective-sample-size/weight/simultaneous-bound/route/workload gates, at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 challenged admissions for that route when feasible, lower simultaneous A1/A2/H3 agreement at least 0.90, and upper parent-versus-actionability-route discordance at most 0.05 for AUTO/TIMING and 0.10 for RECON/DEFER. Upper adverse-compatible active-but-superseded-or-unknown mass must be at most 0.05 for AUTO and 0.10 for RECON; upper invariant-control flip must be at most 0.02 and decisive-control violation at most 0.05; packet/nonresponse/control-infeasibility upper bounds must be below 0.05 unless the atom is censused; and fixed packet/control workload limits must pass. A supported AUTO atom with any adverse-compatible unresolved or superseded unit is blocked. No failing atom can be rescued by pooled or neighboring atoms, macro-service grouping, sentinel performance, favorable consensus, imputation, shrinkage, latent-class estimates, or a negative control. Rare, novel, unknown, and residual atoms default to DEFER.

Mandatory computation fixtures are shared-wrong labels (agreement may be high but adverse-compatible actionability must not narrow), pooled-safe/domain-unsafe, route-invariant nuisance perturbation, one-field decisive perturbation, missing high-weight labels widening bounds, unsupported signatures mapping to DEFER, latent-class nonidentifiability leaving primary bounds unchanged, and duplicate-restatement versus distinct-unit precedence. Verify source/schema hashes, counts, joins, reciprocal links, `D`, clocks, Y/V/H/Z, atom construction, fixed draws, inclusion probabilities, packet/codebook hashes, roster disjointness, and seal order. Any route-before-seal execution, packet leakage, roster overlap, post-label control creation, favorable unknown assignment, or prohibited POE use invalidates the run.

## Interpretation and evidence ceiling

Supportive results require every inherited gate and every actionability/supersession gate for each stated atom and support only a prospective silent validation of those exact observable signatures, with all others forced to DEFER. Adverse results exclude the affected atom when a bound crosses a gate or a control falsifies route invariance; they do not prove clinical harm. Inconclusive results include sparse challenge coverage, infeasible controls, nonresponse, broken seals, wide bounds, or unmet positivity/workload; they are not evidence of safety.

MIMIC cannot establish actual viewing, signature/finalization semantics, physical departure visibility, communication, responsibility, patient preference, outside plans, order/referral execution, scheduling, appropriateness, follow-up, malignancy, harm, utility, benefit, or forward/external transport. The actionability labels test observable textual action structure and target-linked supersession, not clinical truth. A passing retrospective audit therefore requires a separately approved prospective actual-calendar silent bridge measuring viewing, edits, signatures, transmission, responsible team, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside plans, and outcomes before any live, safety, utility, or causal claim.
