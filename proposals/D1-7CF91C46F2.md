# Proposal: diagnostic-contact-to-concordant therapeutic pathways after an HCC index episode

## Episode, parent, weakness, and deliverable

This Episode-31 child evolves assessed-valid parent [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the parent's one-first-index HCC cohort, encounter-constrained timed TACE-like index, all-index accounting through day 45, L=14/L=45 component-resolved AFP/albumin/total-bilirubin profile, procedure-plus-same-encounter-order C2 tier, procedure-only/broad/diagnostic/non-liver/observation-loss states, patient-level temporal locking, clustered uncertainty, and noncausal recorded-care boundary.

The strongest remaining weakness is endpoint sequence validity. C2 is stricter than procedure-only, but a procedure and order on one encounter can still be copied, bundled, or administrative documentation. The parent adjusts for history and capture and compares C2 with non-liver procedures, but does not require that a liver-directed diagnostic/reassessment contact precede a later therapeutic-looking record. A same-day C2 cannot distinguish reassessment-to-action workflow from a bundle.

The substantive repair is to make that temporal transition the estimand, while retaining the parent one-step endpoint as a required comparator and continuity check. The future solver must newly construct/freeze:

1. A row-level provenance/index manifest for every eligible patient, including early events, no paired profile, invalid times, and no later local record.
2. Procedure/examination/order dictionaries; 24-hour episodes; dated diagnostic-contact state Q; separated Q-to-C2 evidence; direct C2, C1/C0, diagnostic-only, non-liver, and observation-loss paths; and candidate-match audits.
3. L=14 and L=45 trajectory and risk-set manifests. The all-index analysis retains early transitions rather than silently conditioning them away; profile-specific post-L contrasts state their risk set and missing-profile category.
4. Standardized CIFs at 30, 90, and 320 days after t0, post-L estimates through 365 days, profile-by-history interaction, and observation-weight sensitivity.
5. A transparent regularized competing-transition baseline and an irregular-time latent-state/marked-history alternative on the same patients, boundaries, states, split, and estimand.
6. Locked predictions, calibration/dynamic scores, patient-clustered intervals, overlap/positivity, falsification outputs, and interpretation.json linking every statement to an estimate, interval, model, endpoint, boundary, stratum, split, and frozen rule.

Completion is computed or explicitly infeasible path probabilities and diagnostics. A supportive result is not required.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved question, and hypothesis

The available evidence supports only that this snapshot contains dated local encounters, procedures, examinations, non-drug orders, medications, and laboratory rows linked by patient and encounter identifiers. It supports an operational same-encounter C2 record and, subject to a training-frozen dictionary, a dated diagnostic/procedure sequence. No association or clinical truth is established. There is no validated treatment intent, completion, indication, radiologic response, viable-tumor endpoint, mortality, or outside-care field.

The unresolved question is:

> Among adults with a first recorded TACE-like index and an observed L-boundary profile, does a favorable early AFP/albumin/total-bilirubin trajectory have a different association with a later, temporally separated diagnostic-contact-to-C2 recorded pathway than with a direct same-encounter C2 record or generic non-liver procedure, after standardizing observed pre-index history and local observation opportunity?

Favorable is inherited exactly: AFP (alpha-fetoprotein) decreases, albumin is non-worsening, and total bilirubin is non-worsening, with exact component pairing, no cross-assay pooling, and explicit discordant, missing, qualitative, and inequality categories. Inherit any parent numeric tolerance; otherwise freeze signs/tolerance on training. There is no lab-unit, ALBI/MELD, liver-failure, or biological-response claim.

The primary hypothesis is directional and noncausal: favorable versus discordant profile will have a lower standardized 320-day CIF for a separated diagnostic-contact-to-C2 path, while the corresponding contrast for N, a generic recorded non-liver procedure, will be smaller. Define Delta_k^L(h) as the standardized favorable-minus-discordant absolute CIF contrast for path k, and:

S_QC2^L(h) = Delta_QC2^L(h) - Delta_N^L(h).

The primary decision margin is S_QC2^45(320) <= -0.03 with a patient-clustered 95% interval excluding zero. This is a design margin, not an expected or observed result. A secondary two-sided comparison is separated QC2 versus direct C2; neither path is called treatment or response.

Clinical importance: a profile associated specifically with a later separated diagnostic-to-concordant workflow, rather than a same-day bundle or generic local procedure, identifies a reproducible recorded-care pattern that could motivate prospective review of reassessment timing and clinical indication. A signal confined to direct bundles, non-liver contact, or dense history warns of documentation/capture intensity. Even supportive evidence establishes only a local recorded workflow association.

## Frozen outcome and transition ontology

Aggregate each child before the exact two-key join (patient master index, encounter number), preserving source row IDs/labels in source_concordance_audit.csv.

Parent states are mandatory: C2 is a timed TACE-like procedure episode plus a qualifying same-encounter order; C1 is timed TACE-like without that order; C0 is broad/ambiguous/mixed liver-directed not C1/C2; D is diagnostic/technical-only; N is timed non-liver procedure; O_loss is the observation-loss state. Unknown/untimed records remain audit-only unless a training rule says otherwise.

New sequence states:

- Q: timed liver/upper-abdominal diagnostic/reassessment contact from a training-frozen positive dictionary over examinations.test with valid start time, or a procedure episode classified as diagnostic hepatic angiography without a therapeutic label. examination findings and examination diagnosis are narrative sensitivity fields only, never radiologic adjudication.
- QC2: Q at q followed by C2 at p with p > q+24 hours and p <= q+30 days. C2 still requires the same-key procedure/order match and primary +/-24-hour procedure/order window. This separates the primary sequence from same-day bundles.
- QC1, QC0, QN: Q followed within 30 days by C1, C0, or N under the same gap rule.
- direct_C2/direct_C1/direct_C0: first therapeutic-looking episode after L without a qualifying Q in the preceding 30 days. Same-day Q is direct for the primary sequence and a bundle sensitivity.
- Q_only: Q without qualifying downstream therapeutic/N path by 30 days.
- D, N, and O_loss: first diagnostic-only, non-liver, or observation-loss path not already represented by a Q transition.

Primary timing uses examinations.start time, procedures.start time, and orders.order time or orders.start time. Sensitivities use q-to-p gaps >6/>24/>72 hours, Q-to-action windows 14/30/90 days, order time fields separately, exact observed order-status and non-canceled subsets, and same-patient cross-encounter matching. An order defining C2 never enters its own predictors or capture covariates.

## Estimands and analyses

For k in {QC2,direct_C2,QC1,QC0,QN,N,D,Q_only,O_loss}, estimate:
Delta_k^L(h) = CIF_k(h | favorable, standardized to the observed joint distribution of H,O in the eligible L risk set) minus CIF_k(h | discordant, same distribution).

Primary is S_QC2^45(320)=Delta_QC2^45(320)-Delta_N^45(320). Report every component, event counts, risk-set denominators, profile-missing category, and history-stratified estimates. Report ratios only with stable denominators. Estimate the all-index joint path probability from t0 through Q then QC2, with direct C2, QC1/QC0, QN, Q_only, and O_loss competing; do not condition away early events. The parent day-45 continuity analysis remains separate.

History heterogeneity is I_H^L(h)=Delta_QC2^L(h|H1)-Delta_QC2^L(h|H0), plus the difference-in-differences for QC2-minus-N. H2 stays explicit; sparse strata are inconclusive. Common support is frozen on training.

Use the parent's split: 2010-2022 fit, 2023 tuning, and 2024-01-01 through 2025-01-01 locked test when support permits; otherwise declare a deterministic patient-hash split before fitting. No patient crosses splits. Fit dictionaries, assay/profile rules, history bins, status rules, margins, weights and support restrictions on training. Use at least 500 patient-clustered bootstrap resamples or a declared equivalent.

### B_transition: transparent baseline

Fit nested regularized discrete-time cause-specific multinomial hazards/semi-Markov transitions:

- B0: age, sex, index date/department, diagnosis ascertainment, H, prior episode count/days, pre-index source-specific capture, and time-varying observation opportunity.
- B1: B0 plus L-boundary component values/changes, assay/specimen and delays, qualitative/inequality/missing flags, and assay counts.
- B2: B1 plus Q and elapsed time since Q for post-Q transitions; Q/C2 defining rows are excluded from their own endpoint predictors.

Fit the full-index t0-to-L state audit without profile values before L and the dynamic-landmark models. Standardize CIFs over the same H/O distribution. Report coefficients, transition matrices, CIFs, calibration, Brier/dynamic log score, overlap/positivity, weight diagnostics, history interactions, and intervals. B0 is essential: similar QC2 and N contrasts mean no liver-directed specificity.

### M_latent_sequence: substantive alternative

Fit a joint irregular-time hidden semi-Markov/marked point-process model on the same patients, L boundary, states, split, and uncertainty process. It has latent reassessment and workflow states, without calling either treatment or response. Observed streams are procedure marks/source/start/end, examination marks/name/start (narrative-presence only), order marks/name/all three times/status/frequency, and assay-specific irregular lab observations. Include H, shared observation frailty, source emissions/timing kernels, profile effects and profile-by-history interaction, and O_loss.

Target is the joint training likelihood of dated source streams and first-transition/path marks. Evaluate held-out source/mark log score, CIF Brier/calibration for QC2/direct/N, convergence, posterior/predictive checks, prior sensitivity, and stability of QC2-vs-N. Fixed priors/penalties and initialization precede test access.

M can reveal whether asynchronous source emissions and a shared latent reassessment/capture process explain a hard C2 label, information B_transition loses. It is retained for scientific evaluation, not complexity. If nonconvergent, nonidentified, prior-dominated, unsupported, or not predictively/calibrationally useful, B1/B2 is primary and M is deferred/inconclusive. GPU is optional infrastructure, not evidence.

## Falsification and interpretation

1. Reproduce parent index, profile categories, all-index early transitions, C2/C1/C0/D/N/O_loss counts and day-45 continuity. Failure is packaging/definition failure.
2. Repeat separation gaps >6/>24/>72 hours and windows 14/30/90. QC2 appearing only when same-day bundling is allowed falsifies the primary sequence claim.
3. Construct analogous non-liver examination-to-N and generic source-contact paths. Similar/larger contrasts favor capture/workflow.
4. Report direct C2 separately. A signal only in direct C2 or C1/C0/D, not separated QC2, does not support reassessment-to-concordant interpretation.
5. Permute examination times within patient while preserving marks/encounters and use pre-index pseudo-Q. Persistent QC2 specificity is adverse to a temporal interpretation.
6. Freeze and audit Q dictionary; report exact matches/unknowns and narrow/broad non-narrative inspection sensitivity. Sparse or unstable Q is inconclusive.
7. Vary order field, +/-6/24/72-hour window, same/cross-encounter, status/non-canceled rule. Collapse is adverse/inconclusive, not evidence of no care.
8. Report QC2, direct C2, N, and specificity in H0/H1/H2. Reversal or dense-H1-only signal favors history/capture; unsupported strata are inconclusive.
9. Compare unweighted, observation-weighted, and complete-pair analyses; vary O_loss gap 30/90/180 days and inspect overlap/weights. Never treat O_loss as death.
10. Show all-index t0-to-L paths and L=14 versus L=45. Any profile result is labelled landmark, not all-index or causal.
11. Verify aggregation, invalid times, temporal split, lexical stability, M convergence/identification, predictive checks, and prior sensitivity.

Supportive requires parent continuity; negative, practically meaningful S_QC2 at L=45 with interval excluding zero; smaller N/generic contrast; stable timing/status/dictionary sensitivities; adequate Q/C2 support and overlap; no permutation/placebo signal; interpretable histories; and, if reported, a converged non-prior-dominated M with calibrated stable paths. This supports only a specifically timed local recorded workflow association.

Adverse includes S near zero/reversed, similar QC2/N, time-permutation persistence, same-day dependence, C1/C0/D/direct-only concentration, dictionary/status collapse, dense-history-only signal, poor overlap, unstable weights, or prior-dominated M. Inconclusive includes sparse Q/QC2/N, profile missingness, invalid timestamps, no valid two-key matches, unstable status/dictionary, unsupported history, positivity/split/follow-up failure, nonconvergent M, or intervals crossing the margin. None establishes absence of recurrence, progression, response, benefit, mortality, or treatment effect.
