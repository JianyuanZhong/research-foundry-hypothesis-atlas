# Executed +72-hour disposition-information experiment in the frozen repeat-TACE cohort

## Substantive advance and unresolved question

This child combines the two assigned parent branches: the exact, temporally ordered +72-hour nested prediction experiment from `[prior hypothesis]` and the explicit observation-process safeguard from `[prior hypothesis]`.

The unresolved question is whether the frozen common early albumin/bilirubin panel adds reproducible out-of-fold information, beyond pre-panel trajectory, measurement-process, workflow-timing, calendar, demographic, and pathway variables, for whether the linked repeat-TACE encounter remains administratively open strictly after 72 hours. The endpoint is `D72 = 1[encounters.出院时间 > event_time + 72h]`, with equality coded zero. It is a recorded administrative encounter-open indicator only. It is not morbidity, toxicity, hepatic decompensation, medical necessity, discharge readiness, treatment effect, causality, utility, or a recommendation to retain or discharge.

The substantive advance is an executed test of incremental information at a fixed early-panel landmark, rather than an association analysis or a causal claim. The observation-process diagnostic characterizes inclusion in the observed 131-episode panel cohort against the broader 242-episode denominator with both B and P assays. It does not weight observations, assert MAR or exchangeability, remove selection bias, or recover unobserved outcomes.

## Evidence established before predictor-outcome analysis

The inherited reconstruction rescanned every row of the five frozen HCC source files without sampling. It reproduced 319 systemic and 1,491 comparator pathways, then 159 and 526 selected repeat-TACE events. The laboratory audit found 28,159,928 source rows, 476,846 exact target-assay rows, 476,820 valid uncensored numeric-time rows, seven duplicate assay-timestamp groups, and zero discordant duplicate groups.

The frozen both-B/both-P denominator was 55 systemic and 187 comparator episodes (242 total). The frozen common early panel yielded 34 systemic and 97 comparator episodes (131 total), with 48 D72-positive and 83 D72-negative episodes. All 131 had the required shared history clock, valid linked encounter chronology, and no selected panel after discharge. The reconstruction found one assay-process mismatch among the 242 B/P episodes; this diagnostic was reported and did not alter the frozen 131 cohort.

These are feasibility and availability facts, not evidence that early directions predict D72.

The inherited exact event, medication, diagnosis, encounter, and laboratory filters were not rewritten. TACE selection, the 14–180 calendar-day pair, the TACE2 eligibility and 14-day institutional-record requirement, the exact systemic-record exclusions, and the first repeat-TACE patient-day in TACE2 days 15–90 are preserved.

For exact assays `白蛋白` and `总胆红素`, the strict signed decimal/scientific parser with optional inequality marker was retained. Primary models use uncensored numeric values; duplicate patient/encounter/assay/exact-time groups retain the final source-order row only when value and censor marker agree, otherwise the group is rejected. B is the latest assay value on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1. P is the latest value in the repeat-event encounter during `[event_time−72h,event_time)`. H is restricted to times on/after TACE2, before `event_time−72h`, strictly before P, and outside the repeat-event encounter; its shared H clock is summarized by H_any, N_H, and recency. The early common panel is the same-encounter exact albumin/bilirubin timestamp in inclusive `[12h,36h]`, closest to +24h, earlier on ties.

The two early indicators are `E_alb_down = 1[early_albumin < P_albumin]` and `E_bili_up = 1[early_bilirubin > P_bilirubin]`. Equality and the opposite direction are zero. No direction is called deterioration or toxicity.

## Locked prediction design

The primary pre-panel model contains `BP_alb_down`, `BP_bili_up`, arm, age, sex indicators, event year, TACE2-to-repeat interval, `P_old_lead_hours`, `panel_hours`, shared `H_any`, `log(1+N_H)`, and `H_any*log(1+R_hours/24)`. The early model adds only `E_alb_down` and `E_bili_up`.

Five deterministic SHA-256 arm-and-D72-balanced folds were used, with held-out sizes 27, 27, 27, 26, and 24 and both arms/outcome classes represented. Continuous variables were standardized within training folds only. A single pooled logistic model was fitted with unpenalized intercept, fixed slope ridge penalty lambda=1, no tuning, interactions, feature selection, nonlinear learner, or outcome-driven fallback. All ten fits converged, had finite predictions, no zero-variance continuous feature, and maximum absolute gradients below approximately 4.1e-8.

The estimand was pooled paired out-of-fold Brier improvement, `Delta_D72 = Brier(Q_pre) - Brier(Q_early)`, so positive values favor the early block. The predeclared 0.02 threshold is a design-sized forecasting increment, not a clinical-utility threshold.

## Executed results

Primary D72 results in the 131 frozen episodes:

- pre-panel Brier: 0.229519737
- early-panel Brier: 0.229495352
- Delta_D72: +0.000024385
- training-prevalence null Brier: 0.232212060
- pre-panel log loss: 0.651222324
- early-panel log loss: 0.651179279
- early prediction range: 0.251623 to 0.427730; variance 0.00111628
- full-refit conditional bootstrap: 1,000/1,000 estimable; percentile 95% interval −0.000485606 to +0.000828495; median −0.0000130715; fraction at or below zero 0.521

The early model beat the null benchmark, but the incremental gain was effectively zero and far below 0.02. The 500 joint early-panel linkage-destruction permutations had 5th, median, and 95th percentiles −0.000427596, +0.000034661, and +0.000943926; the observed gain was not above the permutation 95th percentile (upper permutation p-value 0.52096).

Robustness diagnostics were unfavorable to a positive increment: partition gains for salts v1–v5 were +0.000024385, −0.000144563, +0.000021287, −0.000156340, and −0.000192841, with only 2/5 positive and median −0.000144563. Delete-one-out gains ranged from −0.000018617 to +0.000060456, with six sign changes and maximum absolute shift 0.000043002. Albumin-only gain was −0.000098210; bilirubin-only gain was +0.000123362. The +96-hour sensitivity gain was −0.000085100 (26 positive, 105 negative outcomes), so the direction did not persist at the secondary clock.

The deterministic interpretation branch is `precise_null_for_design_sized_increment`: the interval is not wholly below zero and its upper bound is below +0.02. Under this locked, conditional design, the data rule out a design-sized 0.02 Brier improvement and favor retaining the simpler pre-panel representation for this narrowly defined forecast. This does not justify reduced monitoring or any clinical policy.

## Observation-process diagnostic

In the 242-episode both-B/both-P denominator, the common early panel was observed in 131/242 (54.132%; Wilson 95% interval 47.839%–60.297%), including 34/55 systemic (61.818%) and 97/187 comparator (51.872%). Prior common history was strongly associated with panel observation: 32/37 systemic and 93/108 comparator episodes with panel history were observed, compared with 2/18 and 4/79 without it. Event year and history-process variables also showed marked descriptive differences.

A five-fold process-only versus numeric-history-value model yielded Brier process 0.132566914, value 0.132600232, and Delta_O −0.000033318. In 500 history-linkage permutations, the 5th, median, and 95th percentiles were −0.000074079, +0.000068657, and +0.000273841; observed Delta_O was not above the 95th percentile (upper p-value 0.88224). The diagnostic therefore passed its prespecified descriptive criterion, indicating no detectable extra predictive value of the numeric history values beyond the process variables for O in this exercise. It does not establish ignorability, MAR, exchangeability, absence of selection bias, or recover the 111 unobserved panels, and no inverse-probability weighting was used.

## Falsification and interpretation branches

Supportive results would require a positive gain at least 0.02, a bootstrap lower bound above zero, superiority to the null and linkage-permutation threshold, and no partition or influence sensitivity. Those conditions were not met. An adverse result would require a wholly negative bootstrap interval; that was also not met. The observed result is a precise null for the design-sized increment, not proof that no biological or clinical relationship exists.

A verifier can recompute source scans, joins, clocks, folds, fits, Brier gains, bootstrap, permutations, and branch logic, and can check that conclusions match those outputs. Clinical interpretation requires unavailable adjudication and external validation. The snapshot lacks documented readiness, reasons for prolonged stay, bed or scheduling context, complications, mortality, outside encounters, TACE technical details, verified administration, laboratory units/reference ranges/analyzer harmonization, supportive-care interventions, liver severity measures, tumor burden, and radiographic response. Conditioning on repeat TACE, B/P availability, and observed panel can induce selection or collider bias. P is reused in defining directions, although it is identically included in both nested models.
