# E-MTAB-8873 donor-aware repair (provisional hypothesis)

## Proposed relationship

In the frozen E-MTAB-8873 targeted assay, FLS cells co-cultured with MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages have a reproducible, donor-set-aware difference in the measured transcript state from FLS cells co-cultured with MerTK/CD206-negative macrophages, beyond the recorded cell count and the complete set of contributing donors. This is a context-associated hypothesis only; it is not a causal macrophage-mechanism claim. No expression contrast or model has been computed.

## Exact primary contrasts and donor dependence

The primary comparison is restricted to `treatment == none` and condition exactly equal to `co-cultured with MerTK/CD206pos macrophages` or `co-cultured with MerTK/CD206neg macrophages`. The verified candidate pairs are:

1. `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mneg` versus `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos` (491 versus 368 cells).
2. `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mneg` versus `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mpos` (396 versus 383 cells).
3. `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA266A_Mneg` versus `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_Mpos` (1469 versus 1207 cells; macrophage donor composition differs).
4. `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` versus `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mpos` (540 versus 455 cells; macrophage donor composition differs).
5. `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` versus `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos` (1144 versus 1060 cells).

These are not independent donors. The analysis unit is the complete contributing-donor set, not a row. A stricter sensitivity analysis retains only pairs with identical contributing-donor sets and the same FLS donor; if none remain, the donor-specific interpretation is not identified and the result must be downgraded to context-associated. The malformed/ambiguous `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA260R_Mpos` donor record (`donor_id=SA256A | SA256A, SA260R` with conflicting source metadata) is excluded from the primary matched set and reported as a data-quality limitation. Mixed Mpos/Mneg samples and LPS/inhibitor samples are not substitutes for the primary none-treatment contrast.

## Falsifiable analysis

1. Parse the 33 design records and 445 feature-instance rows using exact `sample_id` and exact feature keys. Preserve the bare `TNFRSF11B` and all duplicate-symbol instances.
2. For each of the five primary pairs, calculate Mpos-minus-Mneg on log(count + 1) with `cells` as a prespecified offset (or an equivalent count model using `cells`); do not collapse feature instances.
3. Fit the simple baseline: a regularized linear model of log count with condition, cell-count offset, and complete contributing-donor-set indicators. Fit the structured alternative: a hierarchical model with a fixed context effect and random effects for the complete contributing-donor set (and, if estimable, FLS donor), with feature-level uncertainty. The alternative can reveal whether direction is reproducible across donor sets and how much variation is donor-set/sampling variation, which the fixed-indicator baseline loses.
4. Evaluate paired contrast direction, feature-level uncertainty, held-out donor-set generalization, and sensitivity to excluding the malformed record and restricting to identical-donor-set pairs. The target is a newly fitted contrast/decomposition with uncertainty and a sensitivity report, not a claimed biological result.

Supportive evidence would be a stable context direction with uncertainty that survives donor-aware fitting, cell-count adjustment, and the stricter sensitivity subset. Adverse evidence would be disappearance after donor-set matching or cell-count adjustment, a result driven by one donor set, or reversal in the matched subset. Inconclusive evidence includes too few independent donor sets, unstable estimates, failed feature reconciliation, or inability to separate context from treatment/batch. Neither outcome establishes causality, a pathway, whole-tissue composition, effects on unmeasured genes, or clinical benefit.

## Missing evidence and limits

The available data are a targeted 445-feature assay and recorded pseudobulk samples. They do not provide complete batch/processing-order covariates, independent biological replication beyond these donor sets, whole-tissue cell abundances, or direct perturbation validation. The three required inspected key works and frozen receipts are not yet available in this repair and must not be claimed. The next test is execution of the above donor-aware analysis; until then, all biological statements remain planned.
