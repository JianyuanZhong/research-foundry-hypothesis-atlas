# Hemoglobin trajectory as a distinct modifier of observed transfusion-associated risk

## Scientific deliverable and unresolved claim

This is a substantive successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It retains the parent’s first qualifying low-hemoglobin ICU landmark, six-hour recorded-PRBC exposure, non-overlapping 24-hour deterioration clock, fixed-era evaluation, and strictly observed-risk interpretation. It tests a different uncertainty: whether *recent hemoglobin trajectory*—an acute recorded fall versus a stable low value—modifies the recorded PRBC–deterioration contrast after accounting for the parent’s physiologic-stress phenotype.

Before fitting, the strongest supported claim is only that the read-only MIMIC-IV snapshot contains serial hemoglobin, temporally indexed PRBC input, physiologic, procedure, admission, ICU and death fields from which this question can be attempted. The dictionary and schema audit did not establish cohort size, trajectory-cell support, units/status quality, or estimability. No fitted result is claimed.

The falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults reaching a first ICU hemoglobin of 6.5–8.0 g/dL after at least 12 ICU hours, with no observed recent PRBC or massive-transfusion proxy, does a prespecified recent hemoglobin fall (versus stable low hemoglobin) produce a different held-out 24-hour observed-risk contrast between recorded PRBC in the next six hours and no recorded PRBC, beyond the parent’s record-defined stress interaction, with calibration and locked later-era stability?

This matters because a clinically important transfusion decision uncertainty is whether the same current hemoglobin means something different when it follows a rapid fall than when it is persistently low. A positive result would motivate prospective adjudication of acute-anemia/bleeding context and possible trial enrichment; a null result would argue against treating a simple Hb trajectory as a transfusion-stratification signal. The trajectory is only a record phenotype: it is not bleeding, blood loss, ischemia, oxygen debt, chronic anemia, or transfusion indication.

The substantive advance over the parent is a new modifier and estimand, not another transport or capacity refinement. The study asks whether temporal change in the measured hemoglobin itself explains clinically relevant heterogeneity that a single landmark value plus contemporaneous stress can miss. It will also test whether any apparent trajectory signal is merely intensified measurement or care-process documentation.

From `hosp/admissions.deathtime), `icu/procedureevents.starttime/endtime/itemid`, and `icu/icustays.outtime), assign the first mutually exclusive state: D=death in the window; E=new ventilation procedure 224385 or 225792, a new listed pressor start when none is active at tau, or new renal-replacement procedure 225441,225802,225803,225805,225809,225955; X=ICU exit before D/E; N=no event; U=irreconcilable or missing follow-up. For trajectory state t, define `Delta_t = standardized Risk(Y=1 | A=1,T=t) - standardized Risk(Y=1 | A=0,T=t)`, using the same eligible target distribution of prespecified pre-t0 covariates and S for both exposure labels. The primary trajectory heterogeneity contrast is `H_T = Delta_acute-fall - Delta_stable-low`. This is a contrast of observed conditional risks under recorded labels, not an individual treatment effect. The model must also report `H_S), the parent’s stress contrast, the joint T-by-S decomposition, D/E components, label-specific calibration, exposure overlap and observation-process summaries.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The simple, clinically interpretable baseline B-T is a cause-specific discrete-time or penalized hazards model for D, E and X. It uses A, T, S, current hemoglobin, pre-t0 physiology/support/context, missingness indicators and measurement counts/recency; includes A×S and the single prespecified A×T interaction; and contains no post-t0 variables. It estimates `H_T) by standardization. Hemoglobin-only and stress-only selectors are secondary simple comparators. B-mask retains the same counts, masks, recency and context but masks hemoglobin values and trajectory, testing whether apparent trajectory heterogeneity is documentation intensity rather than Hb information.

The substantive alternative M-T is an irregularly sampled latent hemoglobin local-linear-trend model coupled to the same cause-specific outcome hazards. It treats the observed item-51222 values as noisy measurements of latent level and slope, with observation times and measurement-error parameters fitted only in development data; the latent level and slope at t0 enter the identical A, S, covariate and outcome-hazard structure, including A×latent-slope. It can reveal whether the acute-fall signal persists after accounting for irregular sampling and measurement noise—information that the baseline’s nearest-pair threshold discards. It is a mechanistic measurement model, not a claim that its latent slope equals blood loss. It must be compared on exactly the same episodes, outcomes, split, covariates and uncertainty procedure; it is not selected for a small predictive gain.

Use a deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 split within development-era subjects. Fit thresholds, imputation, standardization, penalties and latent measurement parameters using development training only; use development validation for model choices. Fit only on anchor-year groups 2008–2010, 2011–2013 and 2014–2016; use 2017–2019 as locked calibration and 2020–2022 as untouched temporal transport. Do not refit on later-era outcomes or change T/S/A definitions. Evaluate standardized `Delta_t), `H_T), stress-stratified components, D/E calibration intercept/slope and Brier score, overlap/ESS, and the signed subject-level risk contrast. At fixed q=5%, 10% primary and 20%, compare event capture and PPV for the trajectory selector against the parent Bhet selector, hemoglobin-only and stress-only selectors. Report 1,000 subject-bootstrap intervals on the locked test set, simultaneous intervals for `H_T) and its D/E components, and actual versus planned refit-bootstrap counts.

The future solver’s actual scientific deliverable is a complete audited cohort/flow and a newly fitted comparison of B-T and M-T that outputs `H_T), D/E decomposition, calibration, support/overlap, fixed-capacity operating characteristics, trajectory missingness/observation-process diagnostics, later-era transport, falsification results, and a conclusion ledger. Completion is either a valid gated estimate or a complete not-estimable report; no fitted result is supplied here.

## Gates, falsification and interpretation

Report all index, prior-measurement, T, S, A, follow-up, D/E/X/N/U, care-unit and era counts; selected and excluded specimen duplicates; Hb units, time gaps, missingness and measurement intensity; PRBC amount/status ambiguity; and any row with reversed or unresolved times. The primary estimand is not estimable if any A×T cell has fewer than 50 subjects or 20 Y events, either T state has fewer than 10 D or 10 E events where those components are claimed, common A support fails [0.05,0.95], weighted ESS is <100 within either T state, or prespecified weighted standardized mean differences exceed 0.25. Do not merge T states, retune thresholds, pool eras, or replace missing trajectory with a favorable category to rescue support.

Falsifications are locked before outcome inspection:

1. Permute A within t0-hemoglobin, T, S, care-unit and anchor-year-group strata; `H_T) should collapse toward zero.
2. Permute T within t0-hemoglobin, A, S, care-unit and anchor-year-group strata; a persistent contrast indicates process or model artifact.
3. Compare B-T with B-mask and an observation-only model using counts, time gaps, masks and care context but no Hb values. Comparable `H_T) means the phenotype is likely documentation/attention rather than hemoglobin trajectory.
4. Test recorded future A against a pre-t0 deterioration composite in (t0-12h,t0]; a strong association quantifies residual indication/severity and forbids treatment-effect language.
5. Repeat with blood-gas Hb item 50811, continuous slope, 0.5/1.5 g/dL fall thresholds, 12–24h prior intervals, and item 225168 alone. Keep primary definitions unchanged.
6. Repeat after excluding rows with ambiguous status/open-bag or specimen duplication and after stratifying by observation-intensity quintile, care unit, hospital day and anchor-year group.
7. Use a pre-t0 time-shifted placebo trajectory from an earlier non-overlapping window, where available, to determine whether any apparent A×T contrast is specific to the landmark trajectory.
8. Scan all model matrices for post-t0 chart/store/start/end times, terminal/death/dod fields used as predictors, and post-exposure physiology.

Supportive evidence requires all source, leakage, support and calibration gates; a simultaneous 95% interval for `H_T) wholly outside [-0.05,0.05]; compatible direction and material magnitude in B-T and M-T; collapse under both label permutations; no comparable B-mask/observation-only result; and the direction retained in 2017–2019 and 2020–2022 without material overlap or ascertainment loss. Fixed-capacity trajectory selection must additionally improve event capture/PPV over the parent Bhet and both simple selectors at q=0.10 while retaining calibration. This supports reproducible observed-risk heterogeneity associated with a record-defined Hb trajectory and justifies prospective adjudication—not causal benefit, harm or a transfusion rule.

Adverse evidence includes a null or reversed `H_T), model disagreement, B-mask parity, permutation persistence, strong pre-outcome association, concentration in one care context, miscalibration, or transport reversal. That challenges Hb trajectory as a useful transfusion-stratification proxy but does not show homogeneous causal transfusion effects. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse acute-fall/stable cells, high indeterminate flow, failed overlap/ESS, unresolved units or status, unstable latent model, wide intervals spanning material directions, or transport cells that are not estimable. The correct conclusion is “not estimable with this snapshot,” with no clinical action.

MIMIC lacks adjudicated bleeding or blood loss, transfusion intent/rationale, completed-unit and compatibility certainty, ischemic symptoms/ECG, oxygen delivery/consumption, cardiac output, chronic baseline hemoglobin, clinician appropriateness, rescue completeness, randomized assignment and external validation. Notes and diagnosis/procedure codes do not replace validated adjudication. A supportive result cannot establish that acute blood loss is the mechanism, that transfusion benefits or harms patients, that a hemoglobin threshold is safe, or that the signal transports outside this snapshot. Those claims require expert adjudication, temporally external/other-site validation and causal or randomized study. A causal redesign changes the estimand and must return to the Lead.

## Method selection, alternatives and compute

The parent’s chronological GRU is retained as a deferred alternative, not silently reused as the new primary: it may be revisited if M-T cannot converge or if irregular physiologic trajectories beyond hemoglobin become scientifically necessary, but a second sequence learner would not by itself resolve the new measurement-error uncertainty. A genetic latent model is unavailable because no verified germline/PRS dependency exists in the MIMIC catalog. Image/multimodal methods are deferred because MIMIC-CXR images and raw waveforms are not in the configured snapshot. A causal g-formula/TMLE is deferred because treatment intent, bleeding and time-varying confounding needed for a causal estimand are not observed. These are evidence-bound deferrals, not blanket method bans.

B-T should fit CPU-first within the future planning envelope of up to 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds; the actual runtime is unmeasured. M-T requires repeated latent-trajectory likelihood optimization and bootstrap refits; its runtime and convergence are unverified. Begin CPU-first with a bounded convergence/runtime check. If repeated fits materially exceed the envelope, use one allocated A100-SXM4-80GB with `cuda:0` in the cached GPU image, explicitly moving model tensors to that device; ordinary shell CUDA absence is not evidence against feasibility. The eight A100 capacity and 7,200-second discovery budget are deployment facts, not a claim that the full future study has been run. No discovery fitting or proposer/solver training is authorized here. The future solver must report measured runtime, convergence failures, resource use and any prespecified fallback; it must not replace M-T with an easier scientific question without Lead approval.
