## Canonical evidence ledger

Every derived assertion, bridge state, gate, and charge is represented by one append-only JSONL record in `evidence_ledger.jsonl`. The ledger is keyed by

```text
record_key = (admission_key, source_unit_U_or_NULL, artifact_role, assertion_id,
              panel_or_algorithm_id, gate_id_or_NULL)
```

The canonical source unit remains exactly

```text
U=(subject_id,hadm_id,source_note_id,reciprocal_chain_id,unit_ordinal)
source_note_id=(subject_id,note_id)
```

A source unit is emitted only by the locked extractor over the reciprocal pre-`D` chain. It is sorted by `(storetime, charttime, note_seq, note_id)` with null times after non-null times and the literal serialized note key as final tie-break. Equal text never merges distinct source identities. Failure to produce a unique chain or ordinal emits `U_BOUNDARY`; it does not become a favorable empty unit.

Each ledger row has the following mandatory fields, serialized with UTF-8, Unicode NFC, ASCII LF, sorted object keys, explicit JSON null, strings for identifiers, six fractional digits for present UTC-naive timestamps, no NaN/infinity, and SHA-256 over the canonical row.

```text
{
  protocol_hash, snapshot_id, source_registry_ids[], schema_hashes[], source_hashes[],
  admission_key, source_unit_U_or_NULL, artifact_role, assertion_id,
  panel_or_algorithm_id, parent_record_keys[], raw_condition_flags[],
  field_state_vector, workflow_fact_vector, availability_provenance,
  eligibility_code, actionability_code, route, compatible_member_hashes[],
  gate_id_or_NULL, gate_state, charge_class, charge_units, row_sha256
}
```

`availability_provenance` is a nested object with `source_level`, `record_level`, `source_registry_ids`, `column_ids`, `join_keys`, `time_rule`, and `reason_code`. `source_level` is one of `PRESENT_CAPABLE`, `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`, or `PROHIBITED_BY_PROTOCOL`; `record_level` is one of `OBSERVED_PROXY`, `MISSING`, `NULL`, `CONTRADICTORY`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `OUTSIDE_WINDOW`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. A source-level unavailability never becomes a patient-level negative. A potentially capable source with a missing, null, contradictory, ambiguous, or temporally incompatible record is `UNKNOWN` and adverse-compatible. `OBSERVED_PROXY` is never promoted to a workflow fact.

The ledger contains exactly these artifact roles.

1. `POPULATION_FRAME`: complete adult/first-eligible all-opportunity denominator and every rejection reason before sampling.
2. `CHRONOLOGY`: source rows, chain identity, `R_store`, `D`, `DS_store`, `G_u`, and `Y,V,H,Z`; no label output is allowed as an input.
3. `ATOM_ROUTE_INPUT`: calendar class, terminal service proxy, workflow class, unit field states, and route input frame.
4. `PANEL_ASSERTION`: H1/H2/H3 or bridge-reader append-only outputs keyed to `U` or `NO_UNIT_CLAIM`.
5. `MATCH`: exact field-wise match states, ambiguity branches, duplicate-restatement decisions, and cross-admission/malformed reasons.
6. `ACTIONABILITY`: the parent's complete mutually exclusive eligibility/actionability table and raw condition vector.
7. `COMPATIBLE_MEMBER`: every retained `(x,error_flags)` member, including shared-error and UNKNOWN-wildcard alternatives.
8. `GATE_INPUT` and `GATE_DECISION`: preconditions and final state for each atom-level nomination gate.
9. `CHARGE`: one ledger row per required activity, with `activity_id`, `admission_count`, `challenge_count`, physician minutes, packet minutes, and whether it is base, challenge, setup, or closeout.
10. `SOURCE_AUDIT`: hashes, headers, keys, nulls, duplicate counts, unavailable-fact reasons, and source-to-derived conservation checks.

Ledger rows are append-only. Corrections create a new row with `supersedes_row_sha256`; no prior row is deleted. A verifier rejects duplicate record keys without a declared supersession, orphan parent references, a source field absent from the registry, a bridge charge lacking an activity ID, a gate decision without all required inputs, or any row whose hash does not recompute.

## Closed eight-fact truth and availability map

The eight workflow facts remain availability-limited and are carried in every eligibility audit, panel manifest, compatible member, gate input, and final summary. They are not routes or outcomes.

* `WF01_DEPARTURE_VISIBILITY`: always `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` for physical visibility. `SRC_ADM.dischtime` and note `storetime` can yield an `OBSERVED_PROXY` interval only.
* `WF02_FINALIZATION_SIGNATURE`: always `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`; note presence, `charttime`, or `storetime` is not signature/finalization.
* `WF03_UI_EXPOSURE`: always `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`; no permitted UI/audit table exists.
* `WF04_CONTEMPORANEOUS_AWARENESS`: always `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`; author/service/timestamp is not awareness.
* `WF05_RESPONSIBLE_TEAM`: fact code remains `UNKNOWN` because `SRC_SVC.curr_service/prev_service` and `SRC_XFER.careunit` are proxies, even when a unique temporally compatible proxy is present. Missing, duplicate, contradictory, or unjoinable proxy adds an adverse record-level reason.
* `WF06_COMMUNICATION`: always `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`; text co-occurrence is not communication.
* `WF07_ORDER_REFERRAL_EXECUTION`: always `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`; POE is prohibited and DS text/prescriptions cannot establish execution.
* `WF08_SCHEDULING_OUTSIDE_PLAN`: always `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`; no permitted scheduling, outside-care, or completion event exists.

For each fact, the ledger records both the fixed source-level code and the record-level reason. Thus a source-unavailable fact cannot accidentally enter the compatible set as `UNKNOWN` patient evidence, while a missing service proxy cannot be mistaken for a source-level absence. Neither class authorizes favorable imputation. Any stronger workflow claim is `UNTESTABLE_REQUIRED_EVIDENCE` at the gate and blocks promotion.

## Closed actionability and gate state machines

The inherited actionability state machine is retained verbatim: structural failure maps to `(NOT_ELIGIBLE,NA_NOT_ELIGIBLE)`; explicit absent target to `NA_NO_TARGET`; explicit outside-construct no action to `NA_NO_ACTION`; exact linked target/action/due with no conflict to `ACTIVE_EXACT`; partial, replacement, contradiction, unresolved timing, unreadable, nonresponse, detail ambiguity, and unresolved mixtures map to their corresponding adverse/inconclusive states. No missing reader or unknown field can generate an NA code. Raw coexisting conditions remain in the ledger even when one precedence code is selected.

The new nomination state machine is separate from actionability and route. For each atom `g`, define these ordered states:

```text
G0_UNASSESSABLE       := missing source audit, broken snapshot/schema/hash, or population/chronology seal failure
G1_FRAME_VALID        := G0 false and complete frame, chronology, atom, sample, weights, and ledger conservation pass
G2_BRIDGE_EXECUTABLE   := G1 and canonical U/matcher/H3 serializer fixtures pass,
                           every eligible record has a closed actionability code,
                           and all compatible sets are nonempty
G3_EVIDENCE_BOUNDED    := G2 and the declared exact/adverse-compatible bridge estimands,
                           missingness, and control bounds are finite/non-undefined,
                           with no source-unavailable fact promoted to observed workflow truth
G4_SILENT_NOMINABLE    := G3 and every inherited support/event/ESS/weight/route/workload/
                           simultaneous-bound gate passes for g, plus bridge gates below
```

States are monotone only forward during a sealed run. Any failed or later-invalidated prerequisite transitions to `BLOCKED_<reason>` and cannot be repaired by deleting a row, changing a label, pooling atoms, or reallocating work. `G4_SILENT_NOMINABLE` is a protocol nomination state, not a clinical recommendation. The allowable output is `NOMINATE_PROSPECTIVE_SILENT_VALIDATION` for the exact atom/signature; live action remains prohibited. `NO_SELECTION` is emitted for a global failure; `DEFER_UNSUPPORTED`, `BLOCKED_BRIDGE`, or `BLOCKED_INHERITED_GATE` is emitted for atom-specific failure. Sparse or zero-denominator atoms are `INCONCLUSIVE`, never zero success.

Bridge gates are fixed as follows and are evaluated with exact rational arithmetic and the widest compatible/design/simultaneous interval.

1. `LEDGER_CONSERVATION`: every selected admission has exactly one base charge per inherited activity; every challenged admission has exactly one H1, H2, H3, and controls charge; setup/closeout appears once globally; bridge activities have IDs and are included in the parent activity rows or explicitly charged within their fixed rows, never both.
2. `UNIT_CERTIFICATE`: source and panel manifests have equal canonical bytes under repeated/reordered execution where required; all duplicate restatements and distinct units follow the exact matcher; no `CROSS_ADMISSION_NONMATCH`, `MALFORMED_MATCH`, or unresolved `U_BOUNDARY` is favorable.
3. `ACTIONABILITY_CLOSURE`: eligible records have exactly one code; all adverse/inconclusive and unavailable-evidence flags are retained; `NA_NO_ACTION` is not generated by route `NOACTION` or missingness.
4. `COMPATIBLE_SET`: every admission has nonempty `Omega_i`; shared-error unanimous support retains adverse-compatible alternatives when permitted; joint numerator/denominator optimization is used; zero feasible denominator is undefined.
5. `BRIDGE_SUPPORT`: the exact-preservation lower bound and adverse-union upper bound satisfy the inherited bridge gate, with minimum support and ESS requirements unchanged. The result is an observable stored-text functional only.
6. `CONTROL_INVARIANCE`: route-irrelevant transformations preserve unit identity, eligibility, actionability, and route; route-decisive transformations change only the locked route field; infeasible transformations do not count as passing controls.
7. `WORKLOAD`: the additive parent-plus-bridge totals remain at most 2,370 physician-hours and 84,000 packet minutes at `A=900,C=300`, with atom-specific weighted workload gates; no retry or top-up is permitted.
8. `EVIDENCE_CEILING`: if a proposed interpretation requires visibility, signature, awareness, responsibility, communication, execution, scheduling, outside care, outcome, image, or clinical-truth evidence, the gate is `BLOCKED_UNAVAILABLE_EVIDENCE`, not pass.

The verifier must print each gate's input row hashes, interval endpoints, exact rational arithmetic, and decision reason. A gate without a complete input set is blocked, not passed.

## Sampling, chronology, analysis, and falsification

The inherited pre-label procedure is unchanged. Freeze the complete first-eligible adult one-admission-per-subject frame; construct the reciprocal pre-`D` report/addendum chronology using only permitted columns and explicit parser relations; select exactly one canonical DS by the inherited independent tie-break; compute `G_u`, `Y,V,H,Z`; derive the exact atom; freeze five strata, positive inclusion probabilities, sentinel census, hash-ranked draws, challenge reservation, packets, and the ledger before labels. The bridge cannot alter frame, atom, route input, sample, weight, denominator, or primary estimand.

For each eligible source unit, H1/H2/H3 outputs use the parent's exact JSON contract and canonical `U`. `UNKNOWN` fields branch to both match and nonmatch where allowed; `ABSENT` versus coded is a nonmatch; same complete `U` and assertion is `DUPLICATE_RESTATEMENT`; equal text with a distinct note identity or ordinal is `DISTINCT_UNIT`; cross-admission and malformed claims remain adverse-compatible. H3 independently reproduces the source manifest and assertion tuple under reordered physical rows and fresh process state; byte/hash mismatch blocks `G2`.

Primary and bridge estimands use the inherited design weights and admission clustering. For every compatible admission member retain route, actionability, numerator, denominator, workflow vectors, and error flags. Optimize ratio numerator and denominator jointly over the Cartesian product of admission compatible sets; do not combine separately optimized extrema. Report the sharp compatible interval, exact finite-population inversion, and the inherited Rao-Wu max-statistic family. No midpoint, consensus, latent-class estimate, favorable complete-case analysis, or unvalidated proxy may narrow certification.

Predeclared falsification fixtures must include all parent T0--T13 and O0--O4 plus these ledger-specific cases.

* A schema mutation that inserts a nonexistent `parent_note_id` reference must fail source-registry validation; it cannot be silently read or approximated from row order.
* The same admission with one source-unavailable workflow fact and one missing service proxy must produce distinct availability provenance (`NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` versus `UNKNOWN`) and neither may become favorable observed truth.
* Duplicate charge rows, an omitted H3 charge, and a bridge charge appearing both inside and outside an inherited row must fail `LEDGER_CONSERVATION`.
* A gate input with one missing row hash, an orphan compatible member, or a superseded record lacking its predecessor must block rather than default pass.
* Reordered source rows and repeated H3 extraction must preserve canonical manifest bytes; a changed code version or schema hash must produce a new protocol/run identity, not a silent overwrite.
* An exact stored-text pass paired with a proposed claim of communication, order execution, or safety must be rejected by `EVIDENCE_CEILING`.
* Atom-specific workload failure while pooled workload passes excludes that atom only; a global snapshot/hash or ledger failure yields `NO_SELECTION`.
* An empty eligible target-bearing set, zero denominator, wide shared-error interval, or unavailable required evidence is `INCONCLUSIVE`/blocked and never interpreted as safety or clinical failure.

Supportive results therefore mean only: the exact atom has a valid source and ledger certificate, closed compatible states, passing observable bridge bounds and controls, and all inherited gates, so it may be nominated for prospective silent validation. Adverse results include any adverse-compatible non-`AUTO` state where `AUTO` certification is sought, failed deterministic fixture, control flip, ledger overflow, failed inherited gate, or bound crossing. Inconclusive results include sparse/empty support, undefined denominator, broad compatible interval, nonresponse, unresolved chain, control infeasibility, or required workflow evidence unavailable. None is a clinical-truth result.

Radiology and DS text are not images. `curr_service`, `careunit`, provider identifiers, disposition, prescriptions, and stored times are proxies, not validated responsibility, viewing, communication, execution, or scheduling events. A successful retrospective certificate would justify only a prospective within-site silent study that records actual report/DS viewing and finalization, responsible recipient, communication, orders/referrals, scheduling and outside pathways, protocol adherence, and patient-centered outcomes. Expert adjudication with actual images and intended context is required for clinical appropriateness. A later governed interventional study is required for safety, utility, benefit, or causal effects. No result from this protocol authorizes retrospective automation or live care.
