# Episode 6: strictly time-safe multi-hospital pH-conditioned hidden hyperoxemia

## Question, evidence boundary, and advance

At monitor SpO2 96–97%, clinicians may infer that arterial oxygen tension is not markedly excessive. Acidemia shifts the oxygen–hemoglobin dissociation relation, but it is unresolved whether this creates a clinically material and reproducible blind spot across heterogeneous ICUs.

**Primary hypothesis:** among the eligible EICU concurrent observations below, the acidemic-minus-normal-pH marginal standardized risk difference (RD) for observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg is at least +10 percentage points. Acidemia is pH <7.35; normal pH is 7.35–7.45. Full support additionally requires positive within-hospital RDs in at least five of six prespecified exposure/documentation-adequate hospitals. This is a concurrent measurement association, not an effect of changing pH, FiO2, SpO2, or oxygen treatment.

The clinical decision is whether a fixed SpO2 interpretation has a large enough acid-base blind spot to justify a prospective, synchronized selective-ABG study. The advance over the parents is one coherent protocol bound to the exact audited cohort: strictly nonfuture FiO2 entry, exact median SpO2, deterministic lab revision and ventilation-sentinel rules, outcome-blind site selection, a sparse-data primary model, and a confidence interval that must itself clear the +10-point threshold.

The inspected full-text Europe PMC XML of Delgado et al. (Antioxidants 2026; DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712; frozen source [source checksum], [source checksum]) supports only a narrower claim: one 2017–2022 tertiary center aligned ABGs with preceding SpO2/FiO2 and found that SpO2 ≥98% often accompanied elevated PaO2, 96–97% was generally more physiologic, and pH/FiO2 modified the mapping. It does not establish a +10-point EICU contrast, cross-hospital consistency, a pre-ABG alert, or treatment benefit. The research-ambition README was inspected; its demonstrations motivate rigorous temporal/external validation but are not evidence for this hypothesis. The cancer main article and STAR Methods are unavailable and are not claimed as read.

## Audited feasibility and analysis

A complete-file audit under these exact rules found 169,684 valid paired panels; 20,931 probable-ventilation panels; 7,226 strict nonfuture-FiO2 panels; 6,085 with preceding SpO2; and 5,301 after block thinning. The target band has 728 observations from 590 stays, 583 people, and 45 hospitals: 275 acidemic, 343 normal, 110 alkalemic; 725/728 have PaCO2. The primary denominator is 618. Recompute exactly and stop with a discrepancy report if unmatched. These are feasibility/QC, not inferential support.

Freeze six hospitals meeting, before PaO2 inspection, ≥30 target-band observations, ≥5 acidemic, and ≥10 normal observations. No outcome/event count may select or remove a site.

Outcome is observed PaO2 >120. Secondary outcomes are >150, continuous PaO2, and <60 (a gas state, not tissue hypoxia). The estimand is the observation-weighted marginal standardized RD among all 618 observations. Fixed models are B0: intercept + SpO2 centered at 96.5; B1: B0 + linear FiO2/10 + PaCO2/10 + PaCO2-missing + categorical six-hour block; B2: B1 + acidemia + acidemia×linear FiO2. Standardize by setting acidemia to 1 and 0 for every row while preserving other observed variables, average predictions, and subtract. This is descriptive, not causal. Age, sex, unit type, and admission source are a case-mix sensitivity; fixed FiO2 bands 21–39/40–59/60–100 are a functional-form sensitivity. No outcome-selected terms or hospital fixed effect.

Fit logistic GEE with exchangeable correlation by `uniquepid`. Primary uncertainty is a 1,000-resample hospital-cluster bootstrap; copied hospitals receive new copied hospital and person IDs. Report failed fits and person-cluster sandwich interval. Report equal-stay weighting and one-observation-per-person sensitivities.

In each frozen site, fit `outcome ~ acidemia + I(SpO2=97) + linear FiO2` using prespecified Firth/bias-reduced logistic estimation for separation, standardize within site, and report all denominators/events. Pool site RDs with random-effects meta-analysis, tau², I², and 95% prediction interval. Six sites support only limited consistency, not national transportability.

Other prespecified sensitivities: first 24 hours; last rather than median SpO2; 10-minute SpO2; FiO2 lookbacks 5/10/60 minutes; exact alternate FiO2 labels; late-entered FiO2 as documentation-bias contrast; alternate revision rule; closed nonzero and broader airway proxies; PaO2>150/continuous; delete each adequate site; and stabilized inverse-linkage weights using only pre-draw descriptors/block/already charted respiratory data, truncated at 1st/99th percentiles. Weighting cannot prove missing at random.

## Falsification, conclusions, and verifier boundary

**Full support** requires the primary RD lower two-sided 95% bound ≥+10 points, positive site RDs in at least five of six, no site deletion reversing pooled direction, site prediction interval not below −5, and no primary timing/ventilation sensitivity reversing direction or changing RD by >5. The strongest conclusion is a reproducible, clinically material concurrent measurement association warranting prospective synchronized validation.

**Adverse:** upper bound <+10 falsifies the claimed magnitude even if a smaller association remains; upper bound <0 supports an opposite association; ≥4/6 nonpositive sites is adverse to limited consistency. A precise +6 is adverse to +10, not “no association.”

**Inconclusive:** interval includes +10; cohort mismatch; >10% bootstrap failure; all six site contrasts cannot be reported; prediction interval spans ≤−5 and ≥+10; sensitivity changes >5 or reverses sign; severe FiO2 nonoverlap; or strict linkage retains <20% of probable-airway panels with both monitors. Pooled support with failed site criteria is “pooled association supported; transportability inconclusive/adverse,” never full support.

The verifier can check source hashes/headers, joins, ranges, revisions, timing, ventilation inequalities, nonfuture entry, medians, block thinning, attrition, site gates, formulas, standardization, bootstrap, and deterministic interpretation. It must reject correct computation paired with claims of tissue hyperoxia, toxicity, causal benefit, deployable alert, broad transportability, or treatment recommendation. It cannot establish specimen identity, arterial provenance, ventilation truth, delivered FiO2, device validity, ignorable measurement selection, clinical utility, causality, or treatment effect.

Those claims require clinical informatics/device adjudication, prospectively synchronized specimen/airway/delivered-FiO2/waveform/device data, external validation, and for treatment benefit a randomized study with hypoxemia and patient-centered outcomes. All four configured datasets remain directly available and read-only; this experiment uses EICU only.
