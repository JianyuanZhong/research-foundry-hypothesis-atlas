# Child proposal: observation-aware hepatic-reserve study with a nested adjudicated imaging-course substudy after common TACE2

## Clinical question and substantive advance

Among adults with recorded hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) who reach a second qualifying TACE (TACE2), does a recorded systemic HCC-medication pathway during TACE2 calendar days 1–14 associate with a different subsequent hepatic biochemical trajectory than the ambiguity-excluded TACE2-only recorded pathway, and is any observed contrast interpretable across the selectively observed post-TACE2 imaging pathway?

This child preserves the parent's frozen common-TACE2 cohort, time zero, medication ontology, day-14 institutional-observation landmark, 2018–2024 era, exact assay rules, and primary hepatic outcomes. It adds a clinically consequential observation-process layer: imaging is not treated as an automatically available tumor-response endpoint, and the degree and pattern of imaging observation are themselves measured. A nested, blinded adjudication substudy will determine whether radiologist-stated tumor course can be recovered from reports at all, without claiming mRECIST/RECIST validity from text alone. This is a substantive advance over the parent because it tests whether hepatic biochemical associations can be interpreted alongside tumor-course evidence and explicitly quantifies informative imaging ascertainment rather than silently conditioning on it.

### Strongest claim supported by available evidence

The complete HCC snapshot and frozen reconstruction support feasibility and selection findings only. The frozen cohort contains 319 systemic-record and 1,491 TACE2-only patients. Exact albumin and total-bilirubin baseline/follow-up pairs are available for 99/319 and 387/1,491, respectively. The complete examination audit found 419,996 examination rows overall and 31,783 rows for cohort patients; 31,767 had valid native examination timestamps and all 31,783 matched the encounter composite key. In the principal days 30–90 window, contrast-liver examination reports were available for 37 systemic and 132 comparator patients; in days 30–120, for 55 and 186. These reports were overwhelmingly MRI (day-60 selection: 35/37 systemic and 114/132 comparator; day-75 selection: 52/55 and 167/186), with a small number of PET/CT and CT/CTA records.

The evidence does not support an automated response/progression endpoint. In days 30–90, only 4 systemic and 9 comparator patients met the narrow lexical rule requiring contrast-liver imaging, comparison language, lesion language, and an explicit direction phrase; only 0 systemic and 1 comparator patient had both baseline and follow-up reports satisfying that rule. Numeric measurements were common, but target lesions were not linked across time and viable enhancing diameter was not structured. Broader bounded triage categories showed mixed progression/response language in 1 systemic and 9 comparator selected reports at day 60, and 6 and 14 at day 75; 19/37 systemic and 65/132 comparator day-60 reports remained unclassifiable by the bounded text rule. These findings support a small adjudication substudy and an informative-observation analysis, not a powered comparative imaging-effect estimate.

Clinical documents do not repair temporal validity: all 12,317 cohort document rows joined to encounters and received a proxy time, but the document table has no native document timestamp and its text can refer to imaging performed at another time. Documents therefore remain secondary contextual evidence, not the primary imaging endpoint source.

### Untested hypothesis

Conditional on the frozen day-14 institutional-observation population and measured pre-TACE2 covariates, the systemic-record pathway is associated with less albumin decline or greater albumin increase from the selected baseline to target-nearest day 60, without a larger total-bilirubin increase, than the TACE2-only recorded pathway. Among patients with a valid, native-timestamped post-TACE2 contrast-liver report, the distribution of clinician-adjudicated overall tumor course (response, stable, progression, mixed response/progression, or indeterminate) will provide clinically interpretable context for this hepatic association, but the study will not assume that imaging is missing at random or that report availability represents tumor response.

This is an association between recorded pathways and observed outcomes. It is not a claim of medication administration, adherence, treatment efficacy, toxicity causation, survival, or comparative effectiveness.

## Imaging ascertainment and nested endpoint

### Computable report eligibility

For each cohort examination row, define a cross-sectional liver report if:

1. `检查` contains CT/计算机断层 or MR/MRI/磁共振;
2. the concatenated `检查`, `检查所见`, and `检查诊断` contains a liver/HCC cue (`肝`, `HCC`, or `hepat`); and
3. at least one of `检查所见` or `检查诊断` is nonempty.

Define contrast-liver imaging by additionally requiring a contrast cue in the concatenated report: `增强`, `强化`, `动脉期`, `门静脉期`, `延迟期`, or `造影`. This is a reproducible text-based eligibility screen, not proof of a technically adequate multiphasic study. Classify modality descriptively as MRI, CT/CTA, PET/CT, or other CT/MR from `检查`; do not pool modalities as equivalent without adjudicator review.

The principal report is the contrast-liver report nearest calendar day 60 in days 30–90, with ties resolved by earlier day and then latest timestamp. A secondary feasibility window selects the analogous report nearest day 75 in days 30–120. Early days 1–29 are not a tumor-response endpoint and are used only as a surveillance/complication observation control. Baseline availability is summarized in days -90 to -1 and is not treated as a valid paired response measure unless an adjudicator confirms the report-referenced comparison study and target lesion continuity.

### Adjudicated course endpoint

The nested endpoint is the overall tumor course stated or supportable from the selected report, coded by two independent liver radiologists or appropriately qualified clinicians blinded to arm, hepatic laboratory values, and later outcomes. Reviewers receive the selected report and, where available, the report's explicit comparison date or referenced prior examination; they do not receive patient identifiers or exact treatment labels. Categories are:

- response/improvement of intrahepatic viable tumor;
- stable/no meaningful change;
- progression of intrahepatic tumor;
- extrahepatic progression;
- mixed response/progression, retaining both favorable and unfavorable directions;
- indeterminate or insufficient comparison.

A report cannot be coded as mRECIST or RECIST response from text alone. Such a claim requires image-level review, lesion linkage, target-lesion measurements, enhancement-phase adequacy, and prespecified criteria. If images are unavailable, the report-level category is descriptive and clinician-adjudicated. Reviewers record whether the report explicitly compares with a prior study, whether a parseable prior date is present, whether that date is pre-TACE2 or post-TACE2, whether the report supplies lesion continuity/measurements, and whether the direction is overall versus lesion-specific.

Resolve disagreements with a third blinded reader. Report per-category counts, the indeterminate fraction, pairwise agreement and a prespecified agreement statistic with confidence interval, and adjudicator reasons for indeterminacy. Do not force an overall favorable/unfavorable binary. The automatically detected course categories in the deep audit are triage only and cannot replace this review.

The adjudication sample consists of all days 30–120 narrow lexical candidates when at most 200, otherwise a seeded random 200, plus a seeded random 25 selected day-60 contrast-liver reports per arm (`random_state=20260909`). The private all-selected day-60 and day-75 report files are for adjudication only and are not public support. If resources permit, adjudicate all 169 principal day-60 selected reports as a definitive nested feasibility set; otherwise report the sampling fraction and treat prevalence estimates as exploratory.

## Negative controls and locked sensitivities

Prespecify the following controls:

1. The parent's same-assay pre-exposure change negative control, using the locked early/late pre-TACE2 laboratory selectors.
2. Days 1–29 cross-sectional imaging observation, interpreted as surveillance/complication ascertainment rather than tumor response; a large arm imbalance tests differential surveillance rather than benefit.
3. Pre-TACE2 cross-sectional/contrast-liver report availability in days -90 to -1, testing whether pathway groups already differ in imaging capture.
4. A report-reference-date control: among selected reports with parseable dates, distinguish references on or before TACE2 from references after TACE2. A high fraction of post-TACE2 references means the report cannot serve as an independent pre-index comparator.
5. Non-liver or noncontrast imaging observation, which should not be interpreted as tumor response but can reveal general utilization imbalance.
6. Document-based imaging mentions, assigned only by encounter proxy time, as a concordance/ascertainment sensitivity and not a primary endpoint.

Lock timing sensitivities at follow-up days 30–120 with target day 75, days 45–75 with target day 60, and days 60–120 with target day 90. Lock strict versus prespecified broad TACE text, the inherited timestamp baseline as a boundary sensitivity, and the parent's bevacizumab-partner sensitivity. Never promote a window or endpoint because it is more favorable.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

- **Reconstruction failure:** any mismatch in source counts, frozen arm counts, TACE2 selection, or exact hepatic pair counts invalidates execution until repaired.
- **Timing failure:** dependence on day-0 baseline tests, post-TACE2 comparison references, or reversal across locked report selectors blocks a stable interpretation.
- **Observation failure:** extreme observation weights, poor calibration, severe ESS collapse, or materially conflicting complete-case and observation-weighted hepatic results makes the hepatic association inconclusive.
- **Positivity failure:** absent arm overlap, an arm/year cell with no support, or residual core-covariate imbalance above the locked threshold yields descriptive/inconclusive results rather than extrapolation.
- **Imaging validity failure:** low adjudicator agreement, high indeterminate fraction, inadequate pre-TACE2 reference continuity, or disagreement concentrated by modality prevents a stable imaging-course interpretation. This is a valid negative feasibility result.
- **Sparse imaging failure:** if principal report counts remain 37 versus 132 and adjudicated course categories are too sparse for uncertainty that distinguishes clinically meaningful directions, imaging is retained as an observation/adjudication feasibility outcome and is not used to estimate comparative tumor response.
- **Negative-control failure:** a pre-exposure contrast capable of explaining the post-index hepatic finding falsifies the claim that the difference newly emerged after TACE2. A null negative control does not establish exchangeability.
- **Outcome coherence:** albumin and bilirubin are separate. Favorable movement in one with clearly adverse movement in the other is mixed/adverse, never a composite success.

Interpretation branches are locked. Supportive results require successful reconstruction, adequate balance and observation diagnostics, no plausible pre-exposure explanation, and compatible hepatic directions across locked sensitivities; they support only a more favorable recorded-pathway association among observed patients. Adverse results argue against assuming a liver-preserving association but do not establish drug toxicity. A narrow null supports no detectable recorded-pathway difference, not equivalence. Inconclusive results arise from sparse pairs, unstable weights, poor overlap, discordant assays, timing dependence, or inadequate adjudication. Imaging remains negative/indeterminate if it cannot meet its adjudication gates.

## Evidence limits and verification boundary

The sources lack medication administration and adherence, dose delivered, treatment indication and rationale, validated laboratory units, raw images, reliable performance status and complete Child-Pugh components, adjudicated TACE refractoriness, complete outside-care capture, and linked vital status. Clinical documents have no native timestamp. Reports may contain mixed lesion directions and references to scans at heterogeneous dates. Therefore do not claim administration, causation, toxicity, survival, treatment superiority, mRECIST/RECIST response, or clinical benefit.

Automatic verification can check source scans, table columns, composite joins, patient-level alignment, timestamp windows, modality flags, deterministic report selection, aggregate yields, seeded sampling, adjudication masking fields, weighting, balance, ESS, uncertainty, and whether conclusions follow the computed branches. It cannot establish whether report text reflects true radiologic response, whether an imaging course is clinically important, whether a treatment was delivered, or whether a recorded-pathway association is causal. Those claims require blinded expert review, raw image access, administration data, validated units, vital-status linkage, outside-care capture, and ideally external or prospective validation.
