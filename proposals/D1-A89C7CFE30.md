# Source-native eICU reconstruction and observation-aware endpoint robustness for the care-plan encoding

## Assigned lineage and substantive advance

This proposal is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It retains the lineage's target-independent adult ICU denominator, first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`, landmarks at 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes, four latest-explicit-airway variants (unbounded, 24 h, 12 h, and 6 h), inclusive post-landmark classification window `[L,L+360]`, exhaustive eight-value `Ventilation` vocabulary, exact and broader indicators, pre-landmark respiratory summaries, hospital-held-out/stay-clustered evaluation, and the non-peak structured respiratory-chart proxy limits.

The selected parent results support a narrow documentation-level negative: the two exact care-plan values are sparse and concentrated, and adding the only two source-exported `Ventilated -` alternatives does not rescue held-out prediction of a later charted respiratory-setting reduction. The observation-state parent additionally shows that complete-case selection is informative by receipt and hospital; its strictest-airway IPW sensitivity is therefore appropriately inconclusive rather than a blanket missing-at-random correction.

The main reproducibility defect is that both parents' frozen `parent_rows.csv` is outside compiler-visible workspace inputs. This child removes that dependency. The executable reconstructs the frozen population and every landmark row directly from the read-only eICU sources, then performs the endpoint and observation-state checks from the reconstructed rows. It also adds a prespecified alternative endpoint based on a non-peak, setting-specific chart reduction requiring two independently timed post-freeze respiratory observations: the inherited `robust_reduction` (any qualifying reduction) is compared with `sustained_reduction_2obs`, which requires a qualifying reduction at two distinct `respchartoffset` values. This tests whether the adverse encoding result is driven by a single possibly transient chart entry. Neither endpoint is an extubation, liberation, SBT, quality, benefit, harm, or causal endpoint.

## Hypothesis and evidence boundary

**Hypothesis.** If the adverse exact/broader result is an artifact of a single transient respiratory-setting entry, requiring a repeated non-peak chart reduction should materially change the exact-versus-broader contrast or produce a reproducible broader rescue across hospitals. Conversely, a similar adverse or null contrast under the repeated-observation endpoint would strengthen the conclusion that exported care-plan vocabulary has no demonstrated transportable incremental information for these chart proxies.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is only that the exported labels and alternatives are sparse, hospital-concentrated documentation states and that their incremental predictive fitness was not demonstrated under the inherited single-event proxy. The unresolved claim tested here is endpoint ascertainment robustness, not clinical meaning. A supportive result means the original endpoint is sensitive to transient charting and motivates adjudicated endpoint work; an adverse result means the documentation-level negative is robust to this particular repeated-observation definition; an inconclusive result follows sparse endpoint cells, inadequate hospitals, disagreement across airway bounds, or materially changed observation selection.

## Endpoint robustness estimand and analysis

For each airway definition, retain the reconstructed classification-complete population for descriptive state tables. For outcome models, use only rows still in ICU through `L+1080` with computable endpoint and fixed hospitals having at least 20 endpoint-observed rows, both endpoint classes, and at least three noncomputable rows. Compare baseline plus generic classification-window process controls against process plus exact indicators and process plus broader indicators. Fit leave-one-hospital-out logistic models, report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss, Brier score, AUROC, calibration, and hospital directions. Repeated landmarks remain clustered by `sid`; the primary uncertainty is a 100-replicate stay-cluster bootstrap within fixed held-out hospitals. Repeat the observation-aware IPW sensitivity using the same cross-fitted computability model, 0.05/0.95 probability clipping, weight cap 10, held-out-hospital normalization, ESS, and the second parent's explicit identification limits.

The primary robustness contrast is broader minus process and broader minus exact under `sustained_reduction_2obs` versus `robust_reduction`, with endpoint prevalence, hospital support, and selection-state changes reported before model interpretation. Supportive evidence for transient-endpoint sensitivity requires a prespecified absolute change of at least 5 percentage points in the broader-minus-process macro contrast or a direction reversal in at least two airway definitions, with a 97.5% nested upper bound below zero for the repeated endpoint's broader-minus-process loss. Adverse evidence requires no such change, no broader rescue, and adverse or near-null contrasts in at least two bounded definitions. Otherwise classify as inconclusive. This threshold is a design gate, not a significance claim.

## Interpretation limits and expected outcomes

A repeated-observation adverse result would support robustness only for this exported chart proxy. A supportive result would identify endpoint transientness as an unresolved measurement problem and require blinded chart adjudication before any clinical interpretation. A discrepancy or sparse repeated endpoint is itself valuable feasibility evidence and blocks claims of robustness. None of the analyses establishes invasive ventilation, a bedside evaluation, SBT, extubation, readiness, liberation, quality, benefit, harm, or causality. Live ICU discharge and death remain competing observation states and are never assigned a favorable or adverse chart outcome. Missingness is not proven MAR by IPW. Clinical meaning, local interface configuration, and transport to unseen hospitals require expert review, direct device/SBT/extubation evidence, workflow metadata, and prospective or external validation.
