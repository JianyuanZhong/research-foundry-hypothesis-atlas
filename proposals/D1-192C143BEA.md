# Target-independent ventilation-marker concordance across airway lookbacks

## Question and falsifiable hypothesis

The parent candidate showed that explicit invasive-airway qualification yields 3,722 person-landmark rows when the last airway status is carried forward without a bound, versus 2,612 and 1,178 rows when airway status must occur within 24 hours or 6 hours. Exact target receipt remained approximately 3%, but the eligible populations overlapped imperfectly. The unresolved measurement question is whether the rows excluded by short recency are stale-airway artifacts or remain clinically plausible active-ventilation observations.

The falsifiable hypothesis is that rows retained only by unbounded carry-forward will have less contemporaneous, target-independent ventilation evidence than rows retained by a 24-hour or 6-hour airway rule, and will show temporal placebo patterns (future markers as common as contemporaneous markers) compatible with documentation persistence rather than active state. Conversely, comparable contemporaneous marker coverage, concordant marker types, and lower future than present coverage would support (but not prove) the plausibility of the older airway status. This is a measurement validation study, not a causal or quality study.

## Frozen population and exact source bindings

The inherited population and care-plan logic are preserved. Landmark times are L = 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes. Adults are identified from `patient.csv.gz` (`patientunitstayid`, `uniquepid`, `hospitalid`, `age`, `unitvisitnumber`, `unitdischargeoffset`), with `> 89` mapped to 90 and `unitdischargeoffset > L`. Care-plan receipt is attached only after selection and is retained as a descriptive downstream field.

The parent membership artifact supplies exact `(patientunitstayid, landmark)` rows for `exact_unbounded`, `exact_airway_24h`, and `exact_airway_6h`. The source snapshot is eICU 2.0 `[source checksum]`; catalog SHA-256 is `[source checksum]`.

Marker sources were audited against the catalog and actual values:

* `respiratoryCharting.csv.gz`, table `respiratoryCharting`, columns `patientunitstayid`, `respchartoffset`, `respchartvaluelabel`, `respchartvalue`. A structured-chart marker is present in `[L-360,L)` if any of the following target-independent labels occurs: `Tidal Volume (set)`, `Mean Airway Pressure`, `Exhaled MV`, `Exhaled TV (machine)`, `Exhaled TV (patient)`, `Tidal Volume Observed (VT)`, `Exhaled Vt`, `Mechanical Ventilator Mode`, `Ventilator Support Mode`, `Ventilator Population`, `Pressure Support`, `Pressure Control`, `Adult Con Setting Set Vt`, `Adult Con Setting Set RR`, `Adult Con Pt/Vent MinuteVentil`, `Adult Con Pt/Vent Spont Rate`, `Adult Con Pt/Vent InspiratorTV`, or `Ventilator Checks`. `RT Vent On/Off` is separately tabulated and is not included in the composite because it is an airway/ventilation documentation state closely related to the parent ascertainment.
* `treatment.csv.gz`, table `treatment`, columns `patientunitstayid`, `treatmentoffset`, `treatmentstring`. A treatment marker is a contemporaneous `mechanical ventilation` substring excluding rows containing `non-invasive ventilation`; this retains invasive/mechanical documentation descendants without claiming invasiveness as truth. * `note.csv.gz`, table `note`, columns `patientunitstayid`, `noteoffset`, `notetype`. `Intubation` and `Extubation` note types are separately counted. Notes are not exported or sent to public search.

All three sources were scanned with DuckDB `read_csv_auto(..., strict_mode=false, ignore_errors=true)` because the frozen source files contain known malformed rows. The marker audit therefore records the parser choice; a production analysis should quantify and inspect malformed-row exclusions. Present windows are `[L-360,L)` and future placebo windows are `[L,L+360)`.

## Overlap groups and analysis

Rows are grouped on `(sid, landmark)` after variant-specific first-stay freezing:

* `unbounded_only`: present in unbounded but neither 24-hour nor 6-hour membership;
* `retained_24h_only`: present in 24-hour membership but not 6-hour membership (whether or not also unbounded);
* `retained_6h`: present in 6-hour membership.

For each group and each hospital and landmark, report denominator size, marker coverage, marker-type count, pairwise disagreement, and target receipt only as a descriptive stratification. The primary concordance variables are structured chart marker, non-NIV mechanical-ventilation treatment, and intubation/extubation note markers. The composite is any of these markers. Report 0, 1, and at least 2 marker types. Report the future composite and each future marker as placebo checks. A documentation-intensity concern is raised if future coverage approaches or exceeds contemporaneous coverage, or if apparent group differences are driven by note/treatment presence rather than physiologic/device traces.

The executed artifact also reports hospital-by-group and landmark-by-group tables. No pooled percentage is treated as clinical truth; hospitals with sparse rows and landmark cells are shown rather than silently excluded.

## Executed evidence

The audit analyzed 5,518 distinct row/landmark keys across the three membership sets. Results were:

* `unbounded_only`: 1,148 rows, 744 stays, 65 hospitals. Structured-chart marker coverage was 1,143/1,148 (99.56%); non-NIV mechanical-ventilation treatment 128/1,148 (11.15%); intubation notes 0 and extubation notes 1 (0.09%). The composite contemporaneous marker was present in 1,143/1,148 (99.56%); 1,014 rows had exactly one of the primary marker types and 129 had at least two. Future composite coverage was 1,045/1,148 (91.03%), lower by 8.54 percentage points.
* `retained_24h_only`: 1,462 rows, 1,131 stays, 63 hospitals. Structured-chart coverage was 1,457/1,462 (99.66%); treatment 231/1,462 (15.80%); intubation notes 0 and extubation notes 6 (0.41%). Composite present coverage was 1,458/1,462 (99.73%); 1,224 had exactly one type and 235 had at least two. Future composite coverage was 1,317/1,462 (90.08%), 9.64 percentage points lower.
* `retained_6h`: 1,178 rows, 819 stays, 35 hospitals. Structured-chart coverage was 1,171/1,178 (99.41%); treatment 173/1,178 (14.69%); intubation notes 6 (0.51%) and extubation notes 21 (1.78%). Composite present coverage was 1,171/1,178 (99.41%); 982 had exactly one type and 190 at least two. Future composite coverage was 1,073/1,178 (91.09%), 8.32 percentage points lower.

The apparent result is adverse to the stated stale-carry-forward hypothesis: the available contemporaneous structured chart marker is nearly ubiquitous in all groups and differs by less than 0.3 percentage points between unbounded-only and 6-hour-retained rows. Future coverage is consistently lower, not higher. However, this is not supportive evidence that the airway state is clinically true. The dominant chart marker may reflect repeated device-oriented documentation, and treatment/note markers are sparse and discordant. Treatment coverage is modestly higher in 24-hour-retained rows than unbounded-only, but the difference is not an adjudicated state contrast. The marker-type count is mostly one type in every group, so multi-marker concordance is limited.

Care-plan target receipt remains sparse in the same rows: among the exact-unbounded membership rows, 108/3,722 (2.90%) receive either exact target; among exact-24-hour rows, 87/2,612 (3.33%); and among exact-6-hour rows, 39/1,178 (3.31%). These are documentation outcomes only and do not validate active ventilation, daily evaluation, SBT delivery, or bedside action.

## Interpretation gates and falsification

Supportive evidence for old carry-forward plausibility would require comparable or higher present multi-marker coverage in unbounded-only rows, a meaningful fraction with at least two independent marker types, and present coverage clearly exceeding future placebo coverage across hospitals and landmarks. The observed chart-marker result meets the first condition but not the stronger independent-concordance requirement because the chart signal is nearly universal while treatment and note signals are sparse and discordant; therefore the joint validation verdict is inconclusive.

Adverse evidence for carry-forward would be lower present coverage in unbounded-only rows, materially higher future coverage, or hospital/landmark patterns showing that the unbounded-only group follows documentation intensity rather than contemporaneous ventilation traces. These were not observed in the composite. An adverse conclusion cannot be ruled out because the primary structured chart family may itself be a repeated documentation process.

A definitive supportive or adverse finding requires expert review of a sampled set of unbounded-only and short-lookback-retained episodes, including respiratory flowsheet context, tube/device status, ventilator settings and timestamps, extubation/removal events, and note semantics. Required unavailable or nonvalidated evidence includes a clinically adjudicated continuous invasive-ventilation episode, reliable interval semantics, and explicit distinction between invasive ventilation, NIV, and device documentation. The present computation cannot establish causality, quality, benefit, transportability, SBT performance, or actual daily evaluation.
