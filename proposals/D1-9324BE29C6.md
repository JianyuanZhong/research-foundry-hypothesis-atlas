# Counterfactual stored-content offered-slot potential for HCC documentary-M2 review

## Targeted repair and clinical question

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that parent’s source-coupled cohort, operative-clock alternatives, accession-first identity, all-row pathology uncertainty, temporal quarantine, same-fit semantic ablation S0, independently fitted baseline B, fixed pre-2020 K and release curve, arrival-only no-recall replay, simultaneous uncertainty, and strict non-treatment boundary.

The unresolved question is narrower than a delivered-service question:

> Conditional on a source-observable, completed-resection HCC episode entering a counterfactual chronological replay, does access to interpretable semantics in its already stored CT/MRI text increase the number of eventual documentary-M2 cases selected per fixed offered-slot budget, compared with the same fitted model with semantic inputs masked and with an independently fitted nonsemantic baseline?

The repaired estimand is **counterfactual stored-content offered-slot potential**, not service yield, referral yield, slot completion, or clinical utility. It asks what a hypothetical reviewer could have selected from this completed-resection source stream if a fixed number of offers were available and the specified stored report content were made available under an explicit access guarantee. It does not say that any person was referred, that any report was released or read before surgery, that a slot existed, or that a review occurred.

This distinction is the substantive repair. The parent’s arithmetic correctly normalized by fixed offered slots, but “pseudo-roster,” “arrival,” and “service yield” could still be read as observed workflow events. The child makes the data-supported claim conditional and names the unavailable bridge to a real service question.

## Evidence boundary and what is actually unresolved

The strongest supported claim remains descriptive: the HCC snapshot can replay a chronologically ordered cohort of selected completed resections using encounter, procedure, examination-accession, stored examination text, and pathology rows. It can compare frozen allocation policies under a fixed counterfactual budget. It cannot identify the real opportunity set or decision-time content availability.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is therefore a stored-content allocation claim. It is not established by the presence of predictive signal in report text, by the pathology association, or by the existence of a simulated capacity rule.

The research-ambition demonstrations were read at the available level. The natural-history article shows the value and risk of temporally structured longitudinal prediction; its full article and extracted text were available. The Bayesian article and extracted text show the importance of explicit longitudinal/selection structure; its full article and supplement were available. For the cancer demonstration, only the supplementary PDF/text and metadata are available; the main Cell article and full STAR Methods were not read and are not cited as inspected evidence. These demonstrations motivate temporal and missingness discipline, not a claim that their methods or results validate this HCC experiment.

## Pseudo-roster and pseudo-arrival contract

For each coherent state and historical year a, construct the roster from the completed-resection definition without using report-body semantics, pathology grade, or the outcome Y. Sort members by (t_dec, frozen hash of 患者主索引, raw episode backpointer). Call this an **index stream**, not an arrival stream. A member’s replay index is the first time at which the hypothetical allocator is allowed to process that source-defined episode.

The word arrival must not appear in a conclusion without the qualifier “pseudo-.” No row in the HCC source records referral, presentation to a review service, planned operation, cancellation, deadline, queue entry, or reviewer completion. Consequently:

- no member is asserted to have been available to a real service;
- no nonmember is assumed to be a negative or an eligible referral;
- no policy comparison is a common-opportunity causal contrast;
- no unused slot is interpreted as a missed real appointment;
- the index stream is only a fixed replay ordering conditional on eventual observed resection.

Before modeling, report a provenance table for each year/state: all encounter-linked episodes, procedure-source alternatives, exclusions by reason, count with valid t_dec intervals, count with each examination pattern, count with documentary Y=0/1/unknown, and the number of members in the final index stream. This is required to expose selection into the completed-case source cohort.

The mechanism is falsifiable as a replay mechanism. Every allocation for index i must be invariant to appending future rows, must use only information allowed at i, and must reproduce exactly under a second implementation on small fixtures. A synthetic future-row injection, order-preservation test, and patient-hash tie test must be included. If an implementation uses a later procedure, a pathology string, a final cohort size, or a future index, the result is invalid rather than evidence against the hypothesis.

## Fixed counterfactual offered-slot budget

For each state, let N_a be the size of the completed-resection index stream in 2015–2019. Set N_ref=min_s min_a N_a(s), K_q=floor(qN_ref) for q in {0.05,0.10,0.20}, and J_q=ceil(0.90K_q). K_q is frozen using only information available by 31 December 2019 and is the same absolute number in 2020 and 2021. A confirmatory q requires K_q>=50, J_q>=45, and every coherent 2020/2021 state has at least K_q pseudo-arrivals. K and J cannot depend on test N, outcome, visibility, policy or bootstrap refit.

The fixed budget is a **counterfactual offer budget**, not an observed capacity log. From 2015–2019 pseudo-index times only, freeze the monotonized 366-bin release fraction F_ref(u), where u is the scaled position of t_dec within the calendar year, and L_q(u)=floor(K_qF_ref(u)) with L_q(1)=K_q. A released offer may be used later in the same index stream; rejected or blocked members are not queued, recalled, replaced or backfilled. There is no end-of-year fill. The release curve is a prespecified mathematical budget schedule, not a claim that the institution released slots on those dates.

For policy M in {S,S0,B}, A_i(M) may use the allowed member features, u, current released/used budget and objects frozen by 31 December 2019. It may not use future index members, final N, Y, future rank or another policy’s action.

## Stored-content intervention and report identity

A nonblank examination source unit is first formed as (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号), before time filtering, text parsing, modality labeling or visibility masking. All component rows are retained. Conflicting component modalities, clocks differing by more than 24 hours, and identity ambiguity produce coupled source states containing both indivisible-accession and deterministic normalized (检查, 开始时间 calendar-date) partitions with raw backpointers. Blank accessions remain in the acquisition audit; their text is identity-unknown and masked.

A unit is eligible when its component clocks can place it in [t_dec-90d,t_dec). The conservative selection uses the maximum component start. Select at most the latest eligible CT and MRI unit; ties use frozen raw ordinal/hash. Two blinded abdominal radiologists map 检查 to CT, MRI, non-target or unresolved. Concatenate 检查所见 and 检查诊断 in frozen raw order. Visibility exposes a complete source unit, never selected phrases or rows.

Because the source has no report authored, final, release, view or immutable-version field, the semantic input is not observed preoperative availability. Define an explicit intervention label:

- H0: no report-body semantics; only nonsemantic B variables and fitted S masks;
- H1(h): the eventual stored body of an eligible unit is hypothetically available under exact access guarantee h;
- H2(h): the same H1(h) content is made available to S, while S0 masks only semantic inputs within S’s already fitted preprocessing, coefficients, calibration, threshold and state machine.

Thus S versus S0 is a stored-content intervention inside a replay, not an observed “review received report” contrast. The access frontier is the coordinatewise-minimal h on a 0.05 grid (h_C, h_M, h_A, h_B with h_B<=h_A) for which the hypothesis passes. It is a requirement on hypothetical content access; it is not an estimate of report-release probability. O/C/M/CM patterns remain in denominators. No claim may use “available at decision time” unless it says “hypothetically exposed under H1(h).”

S and B are fit in 2015–2018; 2019 chooses one threshold per S/q and B/q from {0,0.005,…,1}, maximizing documentary-M2 captures subject to utilization at least 0.90 under F_ref, with ties by fewer false reviews then larger threshold. S0 inherits S’s threshold. All policy rules are frozen before 2020.

## Outcome and linkage boundary

Compose every exact-deduplicated pathology row on the selected encounter from 病理, 检查所见 and 检查诊断. A frozen position-aware parser assigns Y=1 only for anchored selected M2 and Y=0 only for anchored M0/M1. Boilerplate, unanchored mention, missing/uncertain grade, M0/M1/M2 conflict, uncertain specimen linkage or inadequate documented sampling leaves Y in {0,1}; it is never silently discarded. Two Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors adjudicate every test unknown/conflict and parsed M2; a third resolves disagreement.

The computable endpoint is **documentary M2 in all pathology rows linked only by the source encounter key**. It is not biological M2, histologic MVI prevalence, specimen-confirmed resection pathology, or a clinical diagnosis. The absent pathology time, specimen, slide, block and sampling fields are an irreducible linkage threat. Clinical experts may adjudicate documentary adequacy, but they cannot reconstruct absent timestamps or specimen identifiers from this snapshot.

The primary analysis reports both the adjudicated-result envelope and the unresolved-outcome envelope. In the latter, each coherent state keeps Y∈{0,1} for unresolved rows and propagates the state-wise extrema; it does not treat unknown as negative or positive. A clinical translation claim requires a separate pathology source with specimen/procedure linkage, pathology date, sampling protocol and report version. Until then, all conclusions remain documentary and source-conditional.

## Policies, estimands and decomposition

For fixed year, q, buffer, h and coherent state, define
n_M=Σ_i A_i(M), z_M=Σ_i A_i(M)Y_i, U_M=n_M/K_q, P_M=z_M/n_M, and
V_M^pot=100z_M/K_q=100U_MP_M.

V_M^pot is documentary-M2 captures per 100 counterfactual offered slots in the completed-resection index stream. An unused budget unit contributes zero. The primary contrasts are
Δ_S0=V_S^pot−V_S0^pot and Δ_B=V_S^pot−V_B^pot.
They compare identical K, F_ref, index horizon, outcome rule and access state. They do not estimate treatment, referral, reviewer workload, real utilization, or patient benefit.

For R in {S0,B}, preserve the exact noncausal decomposition:
Ψ_util(S,R)=50(U_S−U_R)(P_S+P_R),
Ψ_case(S,R)=50(P_S−P_R)(U_S+U_R),
so Δ_R=Ψ_util+Ψ_case. “Case-composition” is the preferred label; “selection effect” is forbidden unless explicitly qualified as a descriptive policy decomposition. It compares policies’ own accepted index members and is not a controlled direct effect or common-opportunity contrast.

First-J remains a policy-specific prefix diagnostic only. Report its pseudo-index time, index number, completion state, and exact patient backpointers. It cannot be co-primary or described as equal opportunity. The outcome-aware top-K, first-come, frozen seeded lottery, none, age/sex-only controls and an offline oracle remain diagnostics; the oracle is never online and never a deployment comparator. Report state-matched z_S/z_oracle only when the oracle numerator is positive.

## Hypothesis and interpretation zones

The confirmatory hypothesis is:

**H1-potential:** There is a nonempty exact-access frontier h such that, for every claimed h, q, 2020/2021 test year, 12/24/48/72-hour buffer state, and coherent source state, the max-centered simultaneous 97.5% lower limits for both Δ_S0 and Δ_B exceed 5 documentary-M2 captures per 100 counterfactual offered slots; all policies reach J_q and use at least 90% of K_q; S’s state-matched oracle attainability lower limit exceeds 0.80; and every data, identity, adjudication, chronology, calibration, source-state, utilization, optimizer and permutation gate passes.

Supportive H1-potential means only that, within this retrospectively selected completed-resection index stream, hypothetical exposure to eventual stored report semantics would improve capacity-normalized documentary-M2 allocation potential under the frozen replay. If the external workflow audit later confirms referral opportunity, actual report release/version timing, reviewer capacity and completion, the result could motivate a silent-mode workflow study. It does not itself justify deployment.

Positive component labels are separate:

- composition-supported if simultaneous lower limits for both Ψ_case(S,S0) and Ψ_case(S,B) exceed zero;
- utilization-supported if the corresponding Ψ_util lower limit exceeds zero;
- mixed if either component interval crosses zero;
- utilization-explained if H1-potential passes, Ψ_util is positive, and the relevant Ψ_case upper limit is at or below zero.

A positive component never becomes a causal clinical effect.

Adverse results include a simultaneous upper limit at or below 5 for either primary contrast at the maximal exact-access cell, oracle attainability below 0.80, a valid fully enumerated finding that no access cell passes, failure of chronology or index-stream replay, or a result that disappears when the outcome-linkage envelope is propagated. A favorable composition component with adverse V^pot means better documentary composition did not produce useful fixed-budget potential. A favorable V^pot with adverse composition is utilization/policy-accounting evidence only. If the oracle is favorable but S is adverse, retrospective ranking did not survive the chronological fixed-budget replay.

Inconclusive results include any primary interval crossing 5, a component interval crossing zero when attribution is attempted, incompatible years/q/buffers, a frontier empty because uncertainty—not an adverse bound—prevents qualification, fewer than 50 adjudicated M2 events per claimed year envelope, failure to adjudicate test unknowns, unresolved accession or modality thresholds, or any missing external evidence needed to relabel the endpoint as a clinical event.

## Computation, falsification and uncertainty

Rebuild coherent source states, models, 2019 tuning, thresholds, policies, outcomes, V^pot, decomposition and diagnostics in 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits. Keep K, J, release bins, feature dictionaries, margin 5, oracle threshold, interpretation rules and access grid fixed. Use one max-centered simultaneous family over both primary contrasts, invoked components, years, q values, buffers, access cells, attainability and null tests. Preserve state-wise extrema within each replicate; never combine table-wise extrema.

Run 1,000 development semantic-block permutations, stratified by year, modality pattern, source-unit cardinality, identity ambiguity and formatting family, with complete refit. Required gates retain the parent’s thresholds: K/J feasibility; at least 50 adjudicated M2 events; anchored-grade fraction at least 85%; all test unknowns adjudicated; parser kappa at least 0.90, M2 PPV at least 0.98 and sensitivity at least 0.95; modality agreement at least 0.95 and unresolved at most 1%; each claimed pattern at least 100 members and 20 M2; semantic PPV/sensitivity at least 0.85; no required-feature/pattern shift over 15 percentage points from 2019; all/no-report calibration slope 0.80–1.20 and intercept −0.10–0.10; each policy reaches J and worst-state U≥0.90; exact release accounting; backpointer replay; optimizer/brute-force equality on N≤12 fixtures; coordinatewise access monotonicity; zero lab/forbidden lineage; and at most 2.5% failed bootstrap replicates.

Add the following targeted mechanism checks:

1. **Index-stream provenance:** every included member must have a trace to one selected encounter, one operative episode alternative, and one t_dec; every excluded completed-resection candidate gets one recorded exclusion reason. No referral or capacity field may enter the index.
2. **Future-isolation:** append arbitrary later rows and verify all prior A_i, scores and budget counters are unchanged; run a forbidden-lineage scan for pathology, future N, test outcomes, release/view fields and another policy’s actions.
3. **Pseudo-time sensitivity:** repeat the fixed 12/24/48/72-hour operative buffers and report whether the sign/frontier changes; a change is a source-clock sensitivity, not evidence of workflow effect.
4. **Budget conservation:** for every policy and state, used offers never exceed L_q(u), n_M/K_q is reported, unused offers remain zero-yield, and no rejected member re-enters.
5. **Content-access monotonicity:** enlarging h cannot reduce the stored-content opportunity set; any nonmonotone result fails. This checks the hypothetical access intervention, not a release probability.
6. **Outcome-linkage audit:** report the fraction of rows with unknown/conflict/specimen uncertainty and the extremal V^pot under both adjudicated and unresolved envelopes. A positive point estimate without a passing linkage gate is not supportive.
7. **Negative-control lineage:** labs, clinical_documents, diagnoses, vitals, transfers and front_page must either be excluded as specified or appear only in the availability/audit ledger; use no laboratory values or untimed narratives as predictors or timed exclusions.
8. **Arithmetic/conclusion fixtures:** verifier fixtures must reject numerically correct output paired with claims of real utilization, observed referrals, report release before t_dec, biological M2, treatment benefit, recurrence/survival benefit, fairness, cost-effectiveness or deployment readiness. They must accept bounded supportive, adverse, utilization-explained, composition-supported, mixed and inconclusive interpretations.

## Missing evidence and claims requiring another study

To interpret V^pot as a real service quantity, an external representative roster audit must include every considered referral, cancellation, non-HCC pathology and noncompleter with referral/presentation time, planned operation and changes, deadline, exclusion and HCC linkage. A capacity audit must record offered slots, release times, reviewer roles/hours, queue/overflow/replacement rules and actual completions. A report audit must link accession to report identity and immutable versions with authored/final/release/view times and body hashes. A pathology audit must provide specimen/procedure linkage, pathology time, sampling protocol and report versions.

Clinical experts must adjudicate operative identity, modality labels, report concepts, documentary adequacy, pathology linkage and acceptable capacity/margin. Biological M2, actionability, treatment choice, recurrence, survival, harms, cost, fairness and benefit require other data, prospective silent-mode validation and ultimately a randomized or pragmatic impact study. No result from this experiment can establish them.

## Compiler and verifier contract

Compilation must preserve the parent’s population, all operative-clock alternatives, accession-first coupled states, all-row Y uncertainty, t_dec buffers, quarantine, pre-2020 absolute K/J and release curve, online no-recall rule, same-fit S0, independent B, primary Δ contrasts, noncausal decomposition, access frontier, bootstrap/permutation family and clinical limits. A verifier must separately check:

- conclusion linkage: any supportive statement must cite computed Δ intervals, gates, state/year/q/buffer/access cell and outcome envelope;

The child’s substantive advance is an interpretable estimand contract: the available HCC data can falsify a counterfactual allocation-potential claim, while the proposal explicitly refuses to convert that result into a real service or clinical-benefit claim without the missing workflow, versioning and pathology-linkage evidence.
