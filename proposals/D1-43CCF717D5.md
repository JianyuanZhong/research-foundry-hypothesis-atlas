# Is historical M2 triage value semantic or procedural? A process-matched, provenance-falsified experiment

## Clinical decision, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The intended decision remains narrow: before a first eligible liver resection, should an adult with HCC be flagged for multidisciplinary review because the resection specimen may show pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)? The retrospective source identifies people who ultimately underwent resection; it does not prove preoperative resectability, curative intent, or that a flag would change care. A flag is not a recommendation for wider resection or systemic, neoadjuvant, or adjuvant treatment.

**Strongest claim the available evidence supports now.** The read-only HCC source contains procedure start times, CT/MRI acquisition times and report text, assay-specific laboratory values and times, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. Complete inherited streaming audits found 338,040 procedure and 46,395 pathology rows; a broad first-resection audit found 75 preliminary anchored M2 labels in 2020 and 24 in 2021 before final exclusions and adjudication. It also showed that an anywhere-in-document M2 search is invalid because an M0 selected value can be followed by boilerplate defining M2, and that explicit selected-grade recovery collapsed after 2021. These are feasibility and construct-validity findings, not M2 prevalence or model performance.

Inspected full-text public evidence supports that MVI/M2 is pathology-defined and prognostically important, that the Chinese M0/M1/M2 construct depends on number and distance of invaded microvessels, and that inadequate non-tumoral sampling can underestimate grade (Li et al., DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746, frozen XML source `[source checksum]`; Chen et al., DOI 10.3350/cmh.2025.0167, frozen XML source `[source checksum]`). Huang et al. used irregular margin and AFP in an externally validated 2026 model for **binary MVI**, not M2 (DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034, frozen XML source `[source checksum]`). None of this evidence establishes performance in HCC, a 0.30 review threshold, or treatment benefit.

**Unresolved claim.** A historical gain from report text or extra laboratory fields could be generated by report templates, machine/modality mix, report volume, or selective assay ordering. It could also be confined to CT or MRI provenance. Such a result would not establish transportable clinical content even if AUPRC increased.

**Single primary hypothesis.** In the locked 2020–2021 all-patient target frame, adding prespecified, clinically semantic imaging findings and laboratory **values** to a comparator that already contains the unchanged structured baseline plus documentation, assay-ordering, template, machine, and CT/MRI provenance variables will yield material, threshold-robust M2 classification value. Specifically, for `S-W` at `p_t=0.30`, the sharp worst-case lower bound must be at least 0.01 and its patient-bootstrap lower 95% confidence limit must exceed zero; the simultaneous lower band must exceed zero throughout 0.25–0.35; the gain must remain positive in estimable CT-only and MRI-only test strata and non-adverse in an estimable dual-modality stratum; and the observed gain must exceed a workflow/provenance-matched semantic-permutation null. Failure under passed validity gates falsifies the stated semantic, provenance-robust claim. Failed gates or bounds too wide to distinguish benefit from harm are inconclusive, not negative.

This child combines the parents only where coherent. It preserves the semantic/workflow parent's population, 24-hour information safety clock, windows, temporal split, endpoint, denominator, and exact missing-outcome bounds. It imports the other parent's CT/MRI transport challenge, but not its incompatible operation-time clock or broader population. The substantive advance is one direct, nested estimand: semantic content conditional on workflow and provenance, rather than separate comparisons that leave attribution ambiguous.

## Locked population and information clocks

The unit is one patient (`患者主索引`) and one first eligible index resection.

1. In `procedures`, select the patient's earliest row with a valid `开始时间` whose normalized exact `手术` value matches a frozen, clinically reviewed dictionary for partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resection. Break exact time ties deterministically by `就诊号` and normalized operation string. Exclude transplant, biopsy, puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and explicit repeat/recurrence operations. Emit the complete raw-value dictionary, included/excluded frequencies, tie counts, and one-index-per-patient assertion before outcome access.
2. Require age at least 18 and same-`(患者主索引,就诊号)` pathology that explicitly identifies HCC and is adjudicated as belonging to the index surgical resection. Do **not** require an MVI grade. Pathology has neither a timestamp nor specimen identifier; a composite-key match is necessary but not sufficient. Ambiguous specimen linkage sets `V=0` and never removes the patient.
3. Preserve `t0 = procedures.开始时间 - 24 hours`. Require at least one liver CT or MRI with `examinations.开始时间` in `[t0-90 days,t0)`. Eligible laboratories have `labs.检验时间` in `[t0-30 days,t0)`. Nothing at or after `t0` is a predictor. Examination start is acquisition time, not report-finalization or actual decision availability.
4. Preserve the primary no-recorded-prior-treatment cohort: exclude a patient with recorded prior HCC-directed resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during `[t0-365 days,t0)`, using frozen clinically reviewed dictionaries and valid event times. Report separately all exclusions due to each table and invalid/implausible times. This operational filter cannot prove biological treatment-naivety. Previously treated patients form the locked sensitivity cohort with the same endpoint and clocks.
5. Development is 2015–2018; hyperparameter selection and logistic recalibration use 2019 only; the untouched test is 2020–2021. Every record for a patient stays in one period based on index date. The 2013–2014 records remain excluded. Frozen models may score 2022 through snapshot end 2025-11-11 only for predictor/template drift and endpoint identifiability; no contemporary performance claim is allowed unless at least 85% of grades and 75 M2 outcomes are recovered under the adjudication protocol.
6. Freeze operation, HCC, therapy, modality and assay dictionaries; text masks; semantic rules; template construction; rare-category handling; imputation; scaling; winsorization; vocabulary; model penalties; calibration; thresholds; and adjudication rules before the locked test is read. Report the accessible discovery/reserved partition counts; the unavailable reserved partition is not external validation.

The Harbor output must give actual row and patient counts at every filter, missing/invalid time counts, duplicate removal, candidate many-to-one join multiplicity, and reasons for exclusion. Source rows remain read-only; private derived patient-level files remain in the workspace and never enter public searches.

## Locked pathology-only endpoint and verification state

The endpoint is unchanged: `Y=1` only for pathology-defined M2; `Y=0` for M0 or M1. Let `V=1` mean that an index-specimen M0/M1/M2 assignment is trustworthy under the frozen parser/adjudication protocol and `V=0` otherwise.

A state-machine parser may assign a grade only from the immediate selected value following an anchored assigned-result field such as `MVI提示风险分级[:：]`, stopping before explanatory bullets, definitions, historical clauses, or a new specimen section, or from a prespecified synonymous selected-value construction accepted during blinded review. It must handle negation, `未见`, multiple specimens, and discordant fields. Unanchored M2 text, binary `微脉管侵犯：有/无`, positive-ungraded MVI, blank number/distance fields, mere absence of MVI language, explanatory definitions, and conflicting clauses cannot assign M0/M1/M2. Pathology never enters a predictor.

Two independently implemented parsers must agree on the locked test. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, random samples of at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 stratified by year/template, every `V=0` test record, and at least 100 contemporary `V=0` records per year; a third resolves disagreement. Require class-specific PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95, kappa at least 0.90, and dual-parser disagreement no more than 5%. Reviewers may derive grade from explicit number/distance statements under the frozen definition. Inadequate text, uncertain specimen linkage, or uncertain sampling remains `V=0`.

This adjudication can validate interpretation of available text, not biological ground truth. Raw slides, block counts, distance measurements, and the sampling protocol are unavailable, so undersampling can remain hidden.

## One nested semantic-versus-process experiment

Fit elastic-net logistic regressions on the same `V=1` development/calibration patients, with the same preprocessing, penalty search, and 2019 recalibration. Every frozen model must score every 2020–2021 target-frame patient, including `V=0`.

**B: unchanged structured clinical baseline**

- age and sex;
- from the latest eligible liver CT/MRI bundle: maximum lesion diameter, count category (1, 2, at least 3), modality (CT only, MRI only, both), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with an unknown indicator;
- last eligible exact-assay albumin, total bilirubin, INR, and platelet count, represented continuously plus missing indicators.

**W: process/provenance-matched comparator**

W contains every B variable plus only information about documentation and ordering process:

- number of qualifying reports, total and latest-report character count, section/line count, and number of distinct `检查号` values, but never the identifier itself;
- CT-only, MRI-only, or dual-modality provenance and exact `检查` categories, with levels and rare-level pooling learned without outcomes in 2015–2018;
- `机器型号` availability, number of distinct labels, and outcome-blind development-frequency categories with rare labels pooled; no nearest outcome-associated mapping;
- an outcome-blind template family built only from punctuation, line breaks, section order, masked boilerplate spans, and length after clinical words, numbers, dates, identifiers, and values are replaced; unseen formats map to `unseen`;
- presence/absence, count, and time-since-last measurement for each extension assay named below, but not its result.

W contains no added semantic imaging feature and no extension-assay value. It is a negative-control/comparator, not a deployable model.

**S: the primary semantic extension**

S is exactly W plus:

- frozen rule extractions from `examinations.检查所见` and `检查诊断`: complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular or non-smooth margin, satellite or peritumoral lesion/enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity, with explicit present/absent/unknown states;
- last eligible exact-assay AFP, ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, and creatinine numeric values.

Because no lab unit column exists, differently named assays are never pooled merely by apparent meaning and no external cutoff is applied. Numeric transforms are fit within exact `检验` name and `标本类型`; the primary representation uses development-only winsorization and standardization, with within-assay/year rank as a prespecified sensitivity. Missingness and measurement timing for extension assays already reside in W, so `S-W` isolates their values plus semantic report content conditional on ordering.

No character n-gram, year, report code, raw examination identifier, raw machine identifier, template label, report length, pathology/MVI term, diagnosis, procedure name, clinical-document text, or post-`t0` content may enter S except through the W process block shared identically by both models.

**U: diagnostic unrestricted-text ceiling, not another hypothesis**

U is W plus development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after removal of identifiers, dates, accession strings, pathology/MVI terms, and post-`t0` text. Audit its 100 largest coefficients before unblinding. U asks whether unspecified report text contains signal missed by the semantic rules. U performance cannot establish semantic validity and cannot rescue failure of the S-W primary contrast.

Two blinded radiologists review core size/count/macrovascular-thrombus extraction and each added semantic feature using samples stratified across development, 2019, 2020, 2021, CT-only, MRI-only, parser-positive and parser-negative reports. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in at least 90%, count-category agreement at least 90% with kappa at least 0.80, and per-test-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity at least 0.85 for each added binary feature. At least 150 reports per test year and 75 per available year-modality cell are reviewed, enriching rare positives but reweighting accuracy to the frame. Separately audit 100 reports per period for whether content was finalized and actually available before `t0`; inability to establish availability makes clinical interpretation inconclusive.

## Primary estimand and exact all-frame bounds

For threshold `p`, let `w=p/(1-p)`, `A_S(p)=1[score_S>=p]`, and `A_W(p)=1[score_W>=p]`. Net benefit on the **entire** target frame of size N is

`NB_M(p) = {sum_i [Y_i A_Mi - w(1-Y_i)A_Mi]}/N`.

The primary estimand is paired `ΔNB(S-W,p)`; `p_t=0.30` is fixed. Evaluate `p=0.20,0.21,...,0.40` only for the prespecified robustness analysis. No threshold may be chosen after seeing test outcomes.

For every `V=0` patient, use the exact outcome-free contribution interval:

- if `A_S=A_W`: `[0,0]`;
- if `A_S=1,A_W=0`: `[-w,+1]`;
- if `A_S=0,A_W=1`: `[-1,+w]`.

Add the observed paired contributions for `V=1`, choose each `V=0` patient's adverse endpoint for the sharp lower bound `L(p)` and favorable endpoint for the sharp upper bound `U(p)`, then divide by the same all-frame N. Do not impute unknown outcomes and do not complete-case the primary analysis. Apply identical sharp-bound arithmetic to S versus B, W, U, flag-all, and flag-none.

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps of the whole target frame, preserving all rows belonging to a sampled patient. Report pointwise percentile intervals for L and U and simultaneous 95% bands across the 21-threshold grid using the bootstrap maximum absolute deviation from each bound. Define `R` as thresholds where the simultaneous lower band for `L_ΔNB(S-W)` exceeds zero and S's lower-bound NB exceeds both flag-all and flag-none. R's width and components are outputs, not tuning targets.

At 0.30 report bounds by test year, sex, age below/at least 65, AFP availability, template family, and mutually exclusive provenance:

- CT-only: at least one eligible CT and no eligible MRI;
- MRI-only: at least one eligible MRI and no eligible CT;
- dual: at least one of each.

A provenance stratum is estimable only with at least 100 target-frame patients and 20 `V=1,Y=1` M2 outcomes. Suppress inferential claims otherwise. Among V=1 only, report paired AUPRC, AUROC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV as selection-sensitive diagnostics. Model V from pre-`t0` B plus year/template variables and report standardized differences; neither inverse weighting nor these diagnostics identify missing outcomes.

## Process-matched falsification

Run 1,000 semantic-null repetitions. Within each temporal period, permute the entire added semantic block (report feature states and extension-lab values) across patients within predeclared process blocks defined by test year, CT-only/MRI-only/dual provenance, template family, and extension-assay availability pattern, while leaving B, W, Y/V, and patient grouping fixed. Refit, recalibrate, and rescore the complete pipeline each repetition. Blocks with fewer than 10 patients follow a frozen hierarchy that drops assay pattern and then template family; if more than 10% of patients require the final year-by-modality fallback or cannot be permuted, the control is inconclusive. This preserves observed workflow/provenance structure while breaking patient-level semantic-outcome correspondence.

The observed `L_ΔNB(S-W,0.30)` must exceed the 97.5th percentile of the permutation distribution. Label permutation and a full patient-level text shuffle are implementation controls and should be compatible with zero. A future-information model using post-`t0` data is a leakage demonstration only and can never enter the clinical estimate.

## Prespecified resolution and falsification

The experiment is interpretable only if all inherited gates pass: at least 1,000 test patients; at least 75 adjudicated M2 outcomes; V=1 for at least 85% of the all-patient frame; every test V=0 reviewed; pathology and radiology reliability gates met; both parsers pass; all models score identical rows; eligible report text is nonempty in at least 80%; size/count are jointly extractable in at least 70%; each B laboratory is available in at least 70%; no required variable changes missingness by more than 15 percentage points between 2019 and either test year; no forbidden feature enters S; and S has calibration slope 0.80–1.20, intercept -0.10–0.10, sensitivity at least 0.70 and PPV at least 0.50 among V=1. CT-only and MRI-only must each meet the provenance estimability minimum; dual is gated only if it meets that minimum.

**Supportive result.** All gates pass; `L_ΔNB(S-W,0.30)>=0.01` and its lower 95% confidence limit is above zero; `[0.25,0.35]` is contained in R; CT-only and MRI-only each have point `L_ΔNB>0` with lower 95% confidence limit above zero; an estimable dual stratum has a lower confidence limit no worse than -0.01; neither 2020 nor 2021 has a negative point L; and the observed primary statistic exceeds the 97.5th permutation percentile. The strongest conclusion allowed is: prespecified semantic imaging/lab content added historically threshold-robust weighted M2 classification value beyond measured documentation, assay-ordering and provenance variables in this institution's 2020–2021 resection frame, even under adverse assignment of unrecoverable grades. “Arises from” means conditional incremental evidence under this design, not causal proof that workflow is irrelevant.

**Adverse result.** With all relevant gates passed, the upper 95% confidence limit for the sharp upper bound `U_ΔNB(S-W,0.30)` is below 0.01, falsifying clinically material semantic gain; or it is below zero, indicating harm. A pooled positive result is adverse to the modality-robust hypothesis if an estimable CT-only or MRI-only stratum has an upper confidence limit at or below zero. A positive W-B contrast with failed S-W, or an observed S-W statistic not exceeding the matched-permutation null, supports the narrower explanation that measured workflow/provenance can reproduce the apparent gain. Positive U-W with failed S-W means only that unspecified text contains signal; it does not validate the prespecified semantic construct. Better AUPRC cannot rescue an adverse decision result.

**Inconclusive result.** Any endpoint, availability, reliability, report-finalization, cohort-size, provenance-size, overlap, independence, or permutation gate fails; sharp bounds or confidence bands include both material benefit and no benefit; or one required modality stratum is too sparse. Inconclusive means the available evidence cannot resolve semantic value—it is neither evidence of no association nor evidence of utility. The output must identify whether uncertainty came from ungradable pathology, CT/MRI sparsity, parser disagreement, report availability, assay drift, or statistical width.

The 0.25–0.35 interval is a robustness stress test, not an elicited clinical preference. Even support cannot establish that 0.30 is acceptable, that clinicians act on flags, or that care improves.

## Harbor compiler and verifier contract

The compiler must preserve population, first-resection rule, no-recorded-prior-treatment primary cohort, `t0=operation start-24h`, 90/30-day windows, 2015–2018/2019/2020–2021 split, pathology-only M2-versus-M0/M1 endpoint, V rules, all-frame denominator, B/W/S definitions, fixed 0.30 threshold and 0.20–0.40 grid, exact contribution intervals, 2,000 patient bootstraps, simultaneous band/R definition, process-matched permutation, modality gates, and resolution zones. Changing any requires a proposed scientific child.

Computationally checkable claims include source hashes/headers; dictionary freezing; joins, deduplication and one-index-per-patient; clocks and period disjointness; forbidden-feature exclusion; identical scoring rows; anchored parser unit tests, including selected M0 followed by M2 boilerplate remaining M0; model nesting; no outcome use in template construction; W containing no extension semantic values; S and W sharing process features exactly; sharp-bound arithmetic for all three flag patterns; bootstrap and permutation implementation; stratum suppression; and one-to-one links from each reported conclusion to machine-readable outputs.

The verifier must reject:

- a supportive conclusion from complete cases when the sharp lower bound fails;
- “semantic” attribution from S-B without the primary S-W result;
- a pooled supportive claim when CT-only or MRI-only is harmful or unestimable;
- threshold cherry-picking or pointwise intervals called simultaneous;
- treatment, recurrence, survival, cost-effectiveness, external-validity, or contemporary-performance claims;
- calling failed pathology recovery model failure, or calling imprecision adverse;
- correct arithmetic followed by a conclusion outside the predeclared supportive/adverse/inconclusive zone.

It must accept correctly computed adverse and inconclusive outcomes as scientifically valid. Verification establishes computation and conclusion/result consistency, not clinical truth.

Not automatically checkable are Chinese clinical semantics, adequacy of tissue sampling, correct specimen linkage, report availability before the real decision, reader qualifications, acceptability/workload of the threshold, clinician response to a flag, or patient benefit. Those require pathologist/radiologist adjudication, stakeholder review, unavailable evidence, prospective external validation, and an impact study with treatment, recurrence, survival, harms, capacity, cost and patient-reported outcomes.

## What resolution would change

Support would justify only a prospective, clinically adjudicated validation of a local semantic M2 review flag with explicit modality/template monitoring. Adverse evidence would prevent claiming that historical text/lab gains are clinically semantic or modality-transportable and would redirect work toward provenance-specific redesign or abandonment. Inconclusive evidence would identify the missing ingredient—most likely pathology ascertainment, report-finalization evidence, or modality-specific events—needed before another model study is warranted.
