# State–label discordance as an early warning phenotype for respiratory instability in eICU

## Parent, preserved lineage, and substantive new question

This proposal is a successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`, with `[prior hypothesis]` retained as a supporting parent. It preserves the parent’s frozen eICU person-landmark lineage rather than selecting a new cohort: the first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid`, the five landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after unit admission, the two exact case-sensitive `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue` arms, the first qualifying label in `[L,L+360]`, decision time `D`, and every inherited pre-landmark invasive/physiologic/end-of-life exclusion. The inherited action/cohort artifacts remain authoritative; if their exact content cannot be recovered, execution stops and does not substitute a newly reconstructed denominator.

The previous lineage has already tested whether the label is associated with a persistent respiratory-state improvement trajectory and has established substantial transportability and ascertainment limitations. Repeating that feasibility question is not the aim here. The new clinical question is:

> **Does the meaning of the exact care-plan choice depend on the patient’s objective respiratory state immediately before the label, such that state–label discordance identifies a subgroup at elevated short-term risk of respiratory instability?**

The substantive advance is an interaction/phenotype test, not a new vocabulary audit or a looser endpoint. It asks whether a label that appears to express an extubation-related plan is clinically interpretable only when concordant with measured support, and whether discordance can identify a review-worthy high-risk state. This could inform whether such a structured field is suitable for risk stratification or should be ignored unless reconciled with current physiology. A positive result would support only a reproducible, structured-chart early-warning association. It would not show that the label caused deterioration, that clinicians made an error, that an extubation trial occurred, or that a bedside alert would improve outcomes.

## Evidence boundary

The strongest claim already supported by the parent lineage is limited to the following: the exact labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated; earlier label-to-setting associations were unstable and partly driven by peak-pressure documentation; durable liberation is not validly ascertainable from the available independent fields; and a conservative persistent respiratory trajectory can be specified only as a chart proxy with potentially sparse support. These findings do not establish whether the label’s information, if any, is modified by the pre-label physiologic state.

The unresolved claim tested here is specifically the interaction claim: after accounting for pre-D respiratory state and documentation opportunity, the label-by-state interaction will predict a subsequent respiratory-instability proxy, with greater risk among discordant state–label combinations than among concordant combinations. The experiment is falsifiable because it predefines the state strata, label mapping, endpoint, baselines, held-out evaluation, and null/permutation criteria before arm-level outcomes are inspected.

## Pre-D state, state–label concordance, and estimand

Use the same outcome-blind, full-token parser as the parent for exact trimmed case-sensitive respiratory labels `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`, `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`, `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`, `Vent Rate`, and `VS RESP RATE`. Parse only tokens matching `^[+-]?(?:\\d+(?:\\.\\d*)?|\\.\\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\\d+)?$`; reject blanks, ranges, inequalities, embedded units, and other strings. Retain FiO2 only in `[21,100]`, PEEP and pressure support in `[0,60]`, and set rate in `[0,100]`; do not convert fractions such as `0.4`.

Define the pre-D state using the latest valid median in `(D-720,D]` for FiO2 and PEEP, requiring at least two valid observations at distinct `respchartoffset`s separated by at least 30 minutes for each component. If multiple valid values at one clinical offset disagree, mark a time-tie conflict rather than selecting by entry time. The pre-D respiratory state is:

* `low_support`: FiO2 `<=40` and PEEP `<=5`;
* `high_support`: FiO2 `>=50` or PEEP `>=8`;
* `intermediate`: all remaining observed states;
* `state_missing`: either component lacks the required valid repeated observations.

These thresholds are frozen clinical-chart thresholds, not data-derived cut-points. Pressure-support and set-rate summaries, plus `sao2` and `respiration`, are included as continuous pre-D adjustment variables when available and with missingness indicators; they do not redefine the three primary state strata. All predictors have clinical time `<=D`, and no post-D field, care-plan count/value, treatment, endpoint availability, or respiratory-care row is allowed in the baseline.

Define concordance before observing post-D outcomes. The daily-evaluation arm is state-concordant only in `low_support`, and the no-daily-trial arm is state-concordant only in `high_support`; the opposite label-state pairs are `discordant`. `intermediate` and `state_missing` are not forced into either category and are retained for descriptive reporting and a predeclared secondary model with a three-way state interaction. This operationalization tests whether labels have conditional meaning at the extremes; it does not claim that low support equals extubation readiness or that high support equals contraindication.

The primary estimand is the hospital-held-out, landmark-standardized risk difference in 18-hour respiratory instability between discordant and concordant label-state combinations among rows with an observed primary endpoint, adjusted for pre-D state severity and documentation opportunity. The principal model is a predeclared logistic model with label, state, label-by-state interaction, pre-D continuous summaries, missingness indicators, landmark fixed effects, and no post-D variables. Report the interaction contrast and standardized risk differences for low, intermediate, and high support. Because the frozen cohort is small and sparse, the model is descriptive and regularized only using training-fold information; no causal language or recommendation is allowed.

The predictive increment estimand compares hospital-held-out log loss and Brier score for three models: (A) pre-D physiology plus documentation opportunity; (B) A plus the two-label exposure; and (C) A plus label, state strata, and label-by-state interaction. Compare C versus B as the specific value of state dependence, and B versus A as the label’s overall incremental information. Use both micro averaging and equal-hospital macro averaging. Hospital is the held-out unit; all preprocessing, calibration, regularization and standardization are learned within training hospitals only. Bootstrap uncertainty resamples whole stays (and their landmarks together) within the held-out prediction aggregation, with a `uniquepid` cluster sensitivity. Do not report AUROC without a defined outcome and adequate support.

## Primary outcome and competing exits

The primary outcome is `respiratory_instability_18h`, assessed in the clinical interval `(D+360,D+1440]`, with a six-hour post-decision washout preserved from the parent. It is positive if either of the following independently defined signals is present.

1. **Confirmed support escalation.** At least one of FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, or set rate has valid summaries in both `(D+360,D+900]` and `(D+900,D+1440]`, with at least two observations separated by 30 minutes in each window, and the later-window median exceeds the pre-D median by at least 10 percentage points for FiO2 or at least 2 units for PEEP, pressure support, or set rate. The increase must be present in both post windows. This is a charted escalation proxy, not delivered-setting escalation.
2. **Confirmed physiologic instability.** In both post windows, the median SpO2 is at least 3 points below the pre-D median or median respiration is at least 6 breaths/min above the pre-D median, with at least two valid observations separated by 30 minutes per signal and no time-tie conflict. The criterion is evaluated separately for SpO2 and respiration; either qualifies. Values are bounded to SpO2 `[0,100]` and respiration `[0,200]`.

A row is `endpoint_negative_observed` only if it has sufficient valid observations to evaluate all four support components and both vital signals in both post windows, or, in a prespecified component-specific analysis, sufficient support observations plus at least one valid vital signal. The primary complete-case endpoint uses the stricter all-signal rule; a secondary `any-signal-observed` endpoint reports the less restrictive ascertainment without replacing the primary. Missingness is never coded as stability.

If `unitdischargeoffset < D+1440`, classify an exit as death only when trimmed `unitdischargestatus` equals `Expired` case-insensitively; otherwise classify `discharge_transfer`. Unclassifiable status/location is `truncation`. An exit before the endpoint can neither be an event nor a non-event. Report endpoint status as mutually exclusive `instability_event`, `stable_observed`, `death_before_window_complete`, `discharge_transfer_before_window_complete`, `truncation`, `no_pre_state`, `no_post_support`, `no_post_vital`, `time_tie_conflict`, `parser_rejection_only`, `no_chart_source`, or `source_error`, with counts by arm, state, landmark and hospital. Retain all 540 rows in the denominator and display informative missingness separately.

## Sensitivity and negative controls

`respiratoryCare` is used only in predeclared sensitivities. Repeat the instability endpoint with (i) any post-window airway row having nonempty `airwaytype` or `airwayposition`, (ii) a ventilator interval overlapping `(D+360,D+1440]`, requiring `ventstartoffset < D+1440` and `ventendoffset > D+360`, and (iii) exact pre-D artificial-airway recency windows of 360, 1,440 and 4,320 minutes using `respcarestatusoffset`, with ties resolved by `respcareid` only at identical clinical time. These analyses test ascertainment robustness and not delivered ventilation.

Run the following falsification analyses, all fixed before outcomes are viewed.

* A lead-time placebo shifts the putative endpoint to `(D-1440,D-360]`; the label-by-state interaction should not reproduce there if it represents prospective instability information rather than pre-existing documentation differences.
* A same-parser pre-D placebo uses two non-overlapping pre-D windows and asks whether the nominal post-D rule is already present before D.
* A documentation-density negative control uses pre-D respiratoryCharting row counts and distinct clinical offsets as outcomes; a label effect that appears only for documentation density is evidence of recording propensity, not respiratory instability.
* A label-permutation null permutes arm labels within hospital-by-landmark strata while preserving the frozen row set and arm counts, with 1,000 fixed-seed permutations. If a stratum has only one arm, it is not permuted and is explicitly excluded from the permutation estimand.
* Leave-one-component-out analyses remove each support component and each vital signal in turn. Directional conclusions cannot rely on one chart field.
* Compare the acute 18-hour instability endpoint with the parent’s declared persistent-improvement endpoint as a cross-endpoint diagnostic, not as a replacement or post hoc selection.

## Feasibility gates and interpretation

Before fitting the interaction model, require at least five held-out-capable hospitals, at least 10 observed primary-endpoint rows in each of the two extreme concordance/discordance groups when those groups are compared, at least 50% of analyzed rows contributed by hospitals meeting those support requirements, and no hospital contributing more than 40% of either comparison group. If state strata or endpoint support fail these gates, report `inconclusive_endpoint_support` and do not fit or interpret the interaction. Sparse intermediate/state-missing cells are reported descriptively.

Supportive evidence requires all of the following: (1) the prespecified interaction contrast is directionally consistent with higher instability in discordant pairs; (2) the stay-clustered 95% interval for the discordant-versus-concordant risk difference excludes zero and the 1,000-stratum permutation distribution does not explain the observed interaction; (3) model C improves over model B by more than 0.01 in both micro and equal-hospital-macro held-out log loss or Brier score, with the interval excluding zero; (4) the direction is retained in at least four of five estimable hospital-direction summaries; (5) the result survives each single-component and respiratory-care sensitivity; and (6) the lead-time and documentation-density placebos are absent or materially smaller. This supports only conditional incremental information in a structured respiratory-chart phenotype.

Adverse evidence is a null or reversed interaction, no predictive increment over pre-D state/opportunity, performance indistinguishable from the permutation null, a signal confined to documentation density, reversal across hospitals, or disappearance when one dominant component is removed. Such a result is evidence against using the exact label-state combination as a transportable early-warning phenotype, not evidence that either care plan is clinically ineffective.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed exact cohort reconstruction, fewer than the support gates, concentrated competing exits or missingness, non-estimable interaction contrasts, disagreement across endpoint definitions or airway recencies, or a positive result confined to one hospital/landmark. A positive association in the parent persistent-improvement endpoint but not in acute instability, or vice versa, is interpreted as endpoint-specific structured-chart association rather than a contradiction about bedside care.

## Relation to the research ambition

The available demonstrations motivate longitudinal, state-aware modelling and explicit handling of temporal dependence and missingness, but they do not establish this eICU hypothesis. The natural-history demonstration emphasizes future trajectory prediction and warns that learned records can encode bias; the Bayesian demonstration emphasizes joint longitudinal modelling, explicit likelihoods and selection adjustment; and the cancer demonstration’s supplement illustrates multicenter validation and missingness-aware architectures, while its main article and full methods were unavailable locally. This proposal adopts the transferable scientific principles—time ordering, transportability checks, calibration and explicit uncertainty—without claiming to reproduce their unavailable modalities, cohorts, genetic data, imaging, or model results.
