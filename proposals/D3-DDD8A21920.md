## 1. Unresolved question and what changed this episode

The parent narrowed the unresolved claim to: does macrophage MerTK-pathway state carry ANY FLS-output effect detectable transcriptome-wide (H2'), with a composition-artifact question (H1') and an in-sample-refuted FLS-intrinsic arm (H3')? This episode **executed H2' as managed compute** (job `[research job]`, state=succeeded, 11.2 s; result `work/lead/emtab8316_transcriptome_result.json`, [source checksum]) — the analysis episode 3 prepared but could not run.

**Observed results (E-MTAB-8316, 12 samples, 6 specimen pairs, 12,757 expressed genes tested of 58,302 features):**

1. **Zero differentially expressed genes at BH FDR < 0.05** for the paired none-vs-UNC106238 (dose 250) contrast — by paired t-test, Wilcoxon signed-rank, and their union. The smallest q-value across 12,757 genes is ≈0.24. The strongest raw effects (|mean log2FC| 0.2–0.67, e.g. ENSG00000176472 −0.46) do not survive multiple testing.
2. **Both gene classes are null**: putative FLS-response genes (n=1,553 strict classification) and macrophage-intrinsic genes (n=11,204) each show 0 DE genes; class medians of mean log2FC are −0.029 and −0.015 respectively. The lenient classification (1,065 / 11,692) is identical in outcome. Fisher exact enrichment of DE by class is undefined (no DE events; p=1.0).
3. **Donor-aware individual-level inference (n=4 individuals, averaging SA049's and SA114's two pairs each) is also null**: 0 DE genes at FDR<0.05.
4. **Order-independent per-gene variance partition**: median unique R² is 4.3% for treatment vs 75.2% for the specimen (pairing-unit) context; full model median R² 84.3%. In this dataset the pairing unit (FLS donor cluster specimen with nested monocyte donor) — not acute MerTK inhibition — carries almost all transcriptome variance. Caveat: the specimen factor bundles FLS-donor, monocyte-donor and pair-level context effects and cannot decompose them (nested design; see §5).

**Adjudication per the parent's pre-specified criteria**: the outcome is the **inconclusive (no-shift) category** — "no shift anywhere". It is NOT the adverse category, which required macrophage-intrinsic transcripts to shift while FLS-response genes stayed unchanged; nothing shifted in either class.

**Refined unresolved claim.** Across two executed screens (episode 2: targeted panel, n=33 wells, adverse-direction pattern; episode 4: transcriptome-wide, n=6 specimen pairs/4 individuals, exact null with class medians |log2FC| ≤ 0.03), acute pharmacological MerTK-pathway inhibition (UNC106238) produces no detectable stromal-output change at either scale. The unresolved question is now: **is this a genuinely negligible stromal-output effect of MerTK-pathway modulation, or a power-bounded null?** (6 pairs, 4 individuals; no formal detectable-effect-size analysis has yet been executed.) The remaining live mechanistic axis from the episode-2 pilot is macrophage inflammatory-state pretreatment (LPS), not pathway blockade.

**Clinical consequence.** The failed-resolution framework proposes resolution-restoring strategies whose single-arm versions include restoring MerTK-dependent efferocytic signaling [K1], harnessing efferocytosis-driven macrophage reprogramming [K2], and manipulating pro-resolving subsets [K3]. Both executed screens find no measurable stromal-output consequence of acute MerTK inhibition; if the null survives a formal power-bounded analysis, single-arm MerTK-pathway modulation would be deprioritized at the stromal-output level relative to macrophage inflammatory-state reprogramming — a concrete prioritization decision for preclinical resolution-therapy programs in persistent-inflammatory difficult-to-treat RA [K1]. If the power analysis shows the design could not have detected effects of the magnitude the reviews imply, the correct next step is a higher-powered perturbation, not abandonment of the arm.

## 5. Competing explanations and limitations

- **Power**: n=6 pairs / 4 individuals; min q≈0.24. The null is bounded, not proof of absence; no detectable-effect-size analysis has been executed yet (named as the solver's first task).
- **Nested design**: the specimen factor bundles FLS-donor, monocyte-donor and pair-level context — the 75.2% specimen R² cannot be read as "FLS-intrinsic dominance" (the episode-2 module-level result contradicts that reading; different datasets, different structures — no cross-dataset transitivity claimed).
- **Classification error**: E-MTAB-8322 is a different cohort/modality (scRNA-derived macrophage-only pseudobulk); scRNA dropout can misclassify lowly expressed macrophage genes as putative FLS-response. Strict and lenient thresholds agree in outcome (0 DE in both classes either way).
- **Pharmacology**: UNC106238 off-target effects and the possibility that dose 250 does not fully inhibit MerTK in these cultures are unmeasured; a null is also consistent with insufficient target engagement (declared).
- **Ensembl-only feature space** (no symbol map in configured data): DE genes, if any had appeared, would be reported as Ensembl IDs; pathway-level interpretation tools requiring symbols are out of scope.
- **Single-stimulus context**: all samples under LPS 1 ng/mL; the null is conditional on an inflammatory state in which MerTK signaling may already be suppressed.
- No wet-lab validation, no in-vivo claims; known NOTCH3/HBEGF/PGDH findings untouched.

## 6. Falsification criteria for the refined claim (pre-specified for the solver)

- **Claim**: acute MerTK-pathway inhibition has no detectable stromal-output effect at the transcriptome-wide scale in RA synovial coculture under LPS.
- **Supportive of the null being power-limited**: the detectable-effect analysis shows the design could not reliably detect |log2FC| ≤ X at the class level, where X covers effect sizes implied by the resolution-therapy literature → outcome "inconclusive, redesign required" (higher-powered perturbation named as missing evidence).
- **Refuting the null**: label-permutation calibration or a higher-powered replication finds FDR-significant shifts in putative FLS-response genes with donor-aware intervals excluding zero → the MerTK-pathway arm reopens.
- **Confirming the null as biological**: power analysis shows the design WOULD detect literature-implied effect sizes, and the null persists → single-arm MerTK-pathway modulation is deprioritized at the stromal-output level; macrophage inflammatory-state reprogramming becomes the prioritized axis.
