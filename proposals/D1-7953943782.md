# Prospective validation and governed trial of an admission-level pulmonary-nodule handoff gate

## Decision question and targeted advance

The decision is whether the departure-frozen, admission-level gate in `[prior hypothesis]` is accurate and safe enough in a live hospital to test two distinct workflow actions: clinician-confirmed prepopulation for reproducible pure omissions and clinician reconciliation for incomplete, conflicting, or mixed plans. Retrospective MIMIC storage chronology cannot answer that question. It has no true report-finalization event, role-specific UI availability, clinician awareness, live discharge-workspace version, assignment event, or intervention outcome.

This child therefore does not add another retrospective classifier contrast. It preserves the parent's retrospective denominator, recommendation units, corrected storage clock, conservative admission aggregation, uncertainty states, and noncausal limits, and freezes a prospectively executable two-stage protocol:

1. **P1 silent validation** of the unchanged route code against an independent, time-matched clinical reference using a new event/UI data contract; then
2. **P2 route-stratified cluster-randomized workflow evaluation**, entered only by routes that pass governance-locked P1 thresholds.

The useful timing synthesis from `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]` is retained narrowly. Ordinary institutional storage calibration and lead-time strata remain necessary shadow diagnostics, but they cannot stand in for prospective UI availability or awareness. Prospective clocks are defined from source finalization, interface ingestion, role-authorized rendering, explicit clinician confirmation, discharge-artifact versions, signing/transmission, and actual departure. This materially improves decision validity without changing the route or introducing a causal interpretation of MIMIC.

The strongest claim currently supported is only that the MIMIC sources and chronology needed for the retrospective gate are available and that generic discharge storage delay is substantial. The inherited complete-source scan found 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 unique canonical `DS` admission keys, 17 missing `DS.storetime` values, and 3,764 pulmonary retrieval-proxy reports. These are feasibility and retrieval counts, not adjudicated pulmonary opportunities or route outcomes. The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among consecutive first-eligible adult admissions at prospective sites, a frozen admission-level gate evaluated only after every active unresolved recommendation unit has at least 24 hours of verified role-specific UI availability will identify pure-omission admissions with at least 90% positive predictive value and no more than 10% mixed/incompatible admissions, while separately identifying a clinically useful reconciliation route. If those validation gates pass, route-matched workflow support will improve the route-specific final patient-facing plan without increasing independently adjudicated unsupported-plan insertion by 2 percentage points or more.

P1 falsifies progression for a route if its locked accuracy, temporal-integrity, event-completeness, or precision gates fail. P2 falsifies workflow benefit for a route if the simultaneous interval does not exclude no improvement, if the safety margin fails, or if contamination or event capture invalidates assignment. Failure in one route does not rescue or condemn the other.

## Prospective population and immutable admission unit

At each participating site enroll consecutive adult inpatient encounters during a fixed accrual window that meet the same pulmonary recommendation eligibility and exclusions, using the first eligible admission per patient during the study. The eligibility event is a newly finalized or materially amended, patient-specific, unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months for an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular opacity, with the patient still admitted. Screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional/optional plans, explicit no-follow-up, death before discharge, and hospice/comfort-only transitions remain excluded under the locked rubric. Eligibility reviewers and the live extractor may use only information available by their evaluation time.

The admission remains the enrollment, randomization, analysis, resampling, and safety unit. Recommendation units are nested within it. Repeated wording is deduplicated; materially changed addenda update the same unit version; distinct targets/actions/due windows remain separate. Any unresolved unit boundary is uncertainty. No admission enters `AUTO` if any unit is mixed, incomplete, conflicting, timing-limited, withdrawn without a clear replacement, or uncertain.

P1 and P2 retain every enrolled admission in an all-admission flow table, including `NOACTION`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, missing events, withdrawal, death before route, and no route-positive state. Route-specific estimands condition only on the route assigned by the frozen live gate, not on later acceptance, clinician response, artifact completion, or follow-up.

## Prospective clocks and route epoch

Define the following separately for every active recommendation version `u`:

* `T_final,u`: source-author finalization/commit of the operative report or material addendum.
* `T_ingest,u`: receiving interface ingestion. This is transport, not visibility.
* `T_UI,u`: first verified role-authorized successful rendering time of the exact active version to the assigned discharge-team role. This is UI availability, not a view or awareness.
* `T_view,u`: first qualifying foreground display by an assigned clinician, if any. This is exposure, not awareness.
* `T_ack,u`: first explicit content-linked confirmation, if any. This is the only awareness measure.
* `T_draft`, `T_save`, `T_sign`, and `T_transmit`: distinct discharge-artifact events.
* `T_D`: physical departure; `T_ADT` is reported separately.

Report the transport interval `T_ingest-T_final`, UI-release interval `T_UI-T_ingest`, observed view interval `T_view-T_UI`, explicit-confirmation interval `T_ack-T_UI`, and artifact intervals without collapsing them. Missing `T_view` does not prove no viewing; missing `T_ack` does not prove no awareness. Conversely, neither storage nor viewing proves comprehension.

The live scheduler evaluates the gate at every locked discharge milestone and at `T_UI,u+24h`. The **route epoch `T*`** is the first time at which all of the following are true: the patient is still admitted; a classifiable current discharge-artifact draft exists; all active recommendation versions and artifact versions are frozen in an immutable snapshot; and every observably open unresolved unit has `T*-T_UI,u >=24h`. If any open unit has less than 24 hours of verified UI availability, the admission is `TIMING`; if UI availability, boundary, context, or artifact state is unknown, it is `DEFER`. A later eligible epoch may update `TIMING` to another route before assignment, but once randomized the analysis route and assignment are immutable. No post-assignment unit can be silently ignored: a material addendum creates a safety reevaluation and either preserves the compatible route or suspends the intervention to `DEFER`.

This is deliberately stricter than using `R_store` or `DS_store`. The retrospective shadow clocks `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u`, `D-R_store,u`, and the 0/24/72/168-hour institutional horizons are computed only to quantify transport from the MIMIC-era proxy to live event semantics. They never determine prospective eligibility or route.

## Unchanged conservative admission routing

At `T*`, independently classify every unit using only information present in the snapshot. The parent's evidence hierarchy remains: explicit target-linked non-pursuit (`S_DEC`), definitive replacement (`S_DEF`), executable same-or-more-protective plan (`S_EQ`), conflict (`S_CON`), partial plan (`S_PART`), no target-specific plan (`S_0`), and unknown (`S_UN`). Observable context remains `K_OPEN/K_CLOSED/K_UNKNOWN`; it is not a claim of clinical appropriateness.

The admission route is:

* `AUTO` only when every active open unit has at least 24 hours of verified UI availability, is reproducibly `S_0` and wholly omitted from the current artifact, every other unit is resolved/closed, and there is no conflict, interaction, boundary uncertainty, or unknown. The intervention may prepopulate the exact source payload but always requires clinician confirmation; it never silently inserts text.
* `RECON` when at least one active open unit is partial, conflicting, or interaction-dependent.
* `MIXED` is a named subset of `RECON` containing both a pure omission and a reconciliation unit. It is never `AUTO`.
* `TIMING` when any active open unresolved unit has less than 24 hours of verified UI availability and none is `DEFER`.
* `DEFER` for decision-relevant uncertainty, unknown UI availability, unknown context/plan/content, unresolved boundary, absent classifiable current artifact, or route-changing reviewer-compatible assignments.
* `NOACTION` when all units are reproducibly resolved or closed.

Worst-case admission aggregation is mandatory. Duplicating or splitting a unit cannot make an admission more eligible for `AUTO`. A one-unit conflict defeats an otherwise pure-omission admission. Route code is deterministic and versioned; clinicians and adjudicators never vote directly for a route.

## P1: consecutive silent validation

Run the frozen extractor and scheduler silently. It must not alter the artifact, notify clinicians, solicit a study-specific acknowledgement, expose a route, or change ordinary care. Existing UI view and confirmation events may be observed, but study-created awareness is prohibited. Consecutive enrollment continues until 100 reference route-positive admissions (`AUTO+RECON`, with `MIXED` inside `RECON`) and at least 30 admissions in every route for which progression is claimed, or 12 months, whichever occurs first. All admissions accrued by the stopping boundary are analyzed; no outcome-triggered extension is allowed.

Independent dual clinicians, blinded to system route and later final-artifact outcomes, adjudicate the same information available at `T*` and construct the reference recommendation units and admission route. A third reviewer adds but does not erase disputed labels. Primary P1 outputs are the complete system-by-reference route confusion matrix; route PPV and sensitivity; `P(reference RECON/MIXED/TIMING/DEFER | system AUTO)`; mixed-admission auto-miss rate; defer and timing rates; event/clock completeness; and route stability across site and prespecified service families. All are admission-level with site and patient clustering and simultaneous 95% intervals. Reviewer-compatible and unknown-adverse bounds accompany consensus estimates.

Before P1 labels are opened, clinical governance must sign and hash the following thresholds. If it does not, no progression decision is made:

1. at least 99% of enrolled admissions have reconciled finalization, UI-availability, artifact-version, route-snapshot, and physical-departure fields, with no temporal leakage and no route computed from ingestion/storage alone;
2. eligibility, unit boundary, evidence state, and derived-route raw agreement are at least 90%, with kappa or Gwet AC1 at least 0.70 and no leave-one-reviewer-out change from `AUTO` to an incompatible route;
3. for `AUTO`, the simultaneous lower 95% bound for PPV is at least 0.90, the upper bound for reference `RECON/MIXED/TIMING/DEFER` among system `AUTO` is at most 0.10, and the upper bound for auto-routing a reference `MIXED` admission is at most 0.10;
4. for `RECON`, the simultaneous lower 95% bound for PPV is at least 0.80 and the upper bound for reference `TIMING/DEFER/NOACTION` among system `RECON` is at most 0.10;
5. every claimed route has at least 30 reference events and simultaneous interval half-width no greater than 0.10; and
6. performance does not depend on a single site, service, reviewer, unresolved clock class, or materially stale version. A route whose compatible bounds cross its threshold does not progress.

These are prospective operating thresholds, not estimates of clinical utility. The inherited 0.10 purity margin is retained for `AUTO`; P1 supplies the evidence MIMIC lacks. `RECON` may progress while `AUTO` fails. `AUTO` may not progress by pooling it with `RECON`.

## P2: route-stratified governed workflow trial

Only validated routes enter P2. Before randomization, freeze the extractor, route rule, UI component, outcome codebook, cluster map, analysis code, safety margins, and sample size. Use parallel service-team clusters stratified by site, service family, and P1 route volume, randomized 1:1 to usual care or active support for the full trial. At least 20 independent clusters and at least 10 clusters per arm are required; if cross-cover prevents a stable team-cluster mapping, P2 does not start under this protocol. All route-eligible admissions in a cluster receive that cluster's assignment, and analysis is intention-to-treat at the admission level with cluster-robust or randomization-based inference.

In active clusters:

* validated `AUTO` admissions receive a source-linked, version-locked prepopulation panel containing the exact target/action/due payload. The clinician must accept, edit, or reject each unit before insertion. No default acceptance, silent insertion, or automatic transmission is permitted;
* validated `RECON`, including every `MIXED` admission, receives a side-by-side source/artifact reconciliation panel. The clinician must choose a resolved plan, document a valid replacement/non-pursuit, or explicitly defer with reason. It never receives bulk prepopulation.

Control clusters run the gate silently and receive ordinary care. Intervention payloads cannot be exported as reusable templates; control interfaces contain no hidden active component. Technical exposure in a control admission is a contamination event. Clinicians' general learning cannot be assumed absent, so staff cross-cover, prior active-cluster exposure, source-text copying, and secular control-route fidelity are measured prospectively.

### Route-specific estimands

All outcomes are worst-case admission-level outcomes across every eligible unit, adjudicated from the final patient-facing artifact at `min(T_transmit,T_D)` and from protected event logs. A unit documented only after departure cannot make the primary endpoint successful.

For system-`AUTO` admissions, the primary efficacy estimand is the cluster-randomized intention-to-treat risk difference:

`RD_AUTO = P(all active units have a faithful executable target/action/due/recipient plan in the final patient-facing artifact | active cluster) - P(... | control cluster)`.

For system-`RECON` admissions, the primary efficacy estimand is:

`RD_RECON = P(all conflicts/partial/mixed units are resolved into one explicit executable plan or a valid documented resolution by departure | active) - P(... | control)`.

For each route, the co-primary safety estimand is the risk difference in **new unsupported plan insertion**, defined as any final patient-facing target/action/timing/recipient statement not supported by the operative source recommendation or an explicit clinician-documented replacement. Wrong-patient or wrong-target insertion is a named serious subtype. Missing or disputed outcome adjudication is adverse in the primary safety analysis and receives compatible bounds.

Secondary route-specific outcomes are clinician acceptance/edit/rejection; explicit awareness responses without imputing awareness from views; active EHR interaction time; number of interruptive and noninterruptive exposures; artifact signing and physical-departure intervals; responsible-recipient assignment; target-linked order/referral/scheduling by 7 days; documented patient communication/receipt; and route crossover or technical failure. Imaging completion at the due window and clinical outcomes are exploratory unless a separately powered, externally linked follow-up protocol is approved; they are not used to declare this workflow effective.

A statistician blinded to arm outcomes uses P1 pooled route-specific baseline rates and cluster intraclass correlation to freeze a common fixed accrual target before P2. The target provides 90% power for a 0.10 absolute route-specific improvement with two-sided familywise 0.05, inflated for clustering and the observed missing-outcome rate. Require at least 100 assigned admissions per arm in each tested route, 20 clusters overall, and no more than 24 months of accrual. If the calculated target exceeds feasible accrual, that route does not start; the target is not reduced after outcomes are seen.

Use one simultaneous 95% family across `RD_AUTO`, `RD_RECON`, and their two route-specific safety risk differences for routes actually opened. A route succeeds only if its efficacy lower bound is above zero, the upper bound for increased unsupported-plan insertion is below the governance-locked absolute margin of `+0.02`, event completeness is at least 99%, and the upper 95% bound for additional active EHR time is below 5 minutes per admission. The 0.10 efficacy planning difference, 0.02 safety margin, and 5-minute burden limit are operating criteria requiring governance approval before P1 unblinding; they are not learned from MIMIC or claimed to encode patient utilities.

An independent safety group reviews blinded aggregate capture monthly and unblinded serious events after every 100 active-route admissions. Any confirmed wrong-patient payload, any silent insertion/transmission without clinician confirmation, source-version overwrite, or control-arm UI exposure pauses the affected route immediately. Three independently confirmed serious unsupported-plan events in an active route before its matched control count reaches the same information fraction stop that route for safety review; no efficacy analysis can override the pause.

## Contamination, drift, and transport safeguards

Cluster assignments, UI feature flags, rule versions, and payload hashes are escrowed before accrual. Staff-team membership and cross-cover are captured at every route epoch. Technical contamination must be no greater than 1% of control route-positive admissions; a higher upper one-sided 95% bound stops interpretation. Report control-arm plan fidelity by calendar block and prior clinician exposure to active clusters. A material control improvement associated with prior active exposure is behavioral contamination and makes the efficacy estimate transport-limited even if the ITT contrast is null.

Before P2, repeat one silent month after any source-system, interface, UI, discharge-template, report-template, or route-code change. Revalidate content hashes, UI rendering, route confusion, and timing completeness. No model recalibration or threshold change is permitted during a trial version. Site-specific route prevalence is descriptive; pooling cannot conceal a site whose `AUTO` incompatible-route bound exceeds 0.10. A failed site is paused, not averaged into success.

The institutional timing concepts are used as diagnostics rather than duplicated as a second decision system. At P1 and P2, report the 0/24/72/168-hour distributions for technical storage, true `T_UI`, `T_view`, and `T_ack`; the legacy `R_store`/`DS_store` shadow clocks where available; ordinary discharge-artifact timing; and non-nodule chest-report interface lag. A large storage-to-UI discrepancy, generic institutional delay, or lead-time sign reversal falsifies transport of a storage-clock interpretation but does not alter the true prospective route clock. Conversely, a clean UI clock does not prove awareness.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**P1 supportive for `AUTO`** means the frozen admission-level gate meets every AUTO accuracy, purity, temporal-integrity, event, reviewer, and precision threshold under consensus and compatible bounds. It supports only opening the clinician-confirmed AUTO trial lane. It does not support deployment, silent automation, clinician fault, or benefit.

**P1 supportive for `RECON`** means the separately evaluated reconciliation route meets its thresholds. It supports opening only the reconciliation lane. A high `MIXED` rate is supportive of admission-level reconciliation over recommendation-unit copying, not evidence of clinical benefit.

**P1 adverse** includes an AUTO incompatible/mixed rate above 0.10, inadequate PPV, unstable route under reviewer or unit-boundary assignments, or a route that relies on storage/ingestion rather than verified UI availability. It falsifies progression of that route under this rule. A failed AUTO lane may coexist with a valid RECON lane. Excess `TIMING` indicates a timing-limited workflow burden and motivates a separately designed earlier-trigger study; it cannot be relabeled content failure.

**P1 inconclusive** includes sparse routes, wide intervals, incomplete physical-departure or UI events, clock drift, poor reviewer reliability, unresolved version chains, site drift, or failure to obtain governance approval. Extending accrual or relaxing thresholds after seeing route outcomes is prohibited.

**P2 supportive for a route** requires positive route-specific ITT efficacy, safety below the +0.02 margin, acceptable burden, valid capture, and contamination control in the simultaneous family. This supports the tested clinician-mediated workflow in the enrolled sites and route, not silent automation or improved imaging/cancer outcomes.

**P2 adverse** includes no positive efficacy interval, failure of the unsupported-plan safety margin, wrong-patient/target events, increased unresolved plans, or excessive workflow burden. A null effect with high acceptance but unchanged final fidelity is evidence against the UI component as implemented. A fidelity gain with failed safety is not a success.

**P2 inconclusive** includes insufficient clusters/accrual, contamination above threshold, event loss, route drift, cluster imbalance that defeats randomization-based inference, or bounds crossing either efficacy or safety criteria. Inconclusive is not proof that pulmonary follow-up is unimportant.

## Verification and evidence boundary

A computational verifier can check MIMIC hashes, schemas, members, keys, frozen retrospective denominators, complete state accounting, clock identities, recommendation-unit aggregation, prospective table constraints, event supersession, timestamp-source separation, route reproducibility from immutable snapshots, randomization, all-assigned retention, cluster-level inference, contamination counts, simultaneous intervals, and whether each conclusion follows from computed outputs. Adversarial tests must include: ingestion without UI rendering; a UI view without confirmation; explicit confirmation without a prior logged view; a late material addendum; one pure omission plus one conflict; an absent artifact draft; one unit with only 23.9 hours of UI availability; wrong-patient payload; control-arm UI exposure; post-departure signing; and a favorable fidelity result with safety above +0.02. The required outputs are respectively unknown/defer or timing, awareness only when explicitly confirmed, addendum reevaluation, `MIXED/RECON`, `DEFER`, `TIMING`, safety pause, contamination flag, primary failure, and no-go despite efficacy.

Automatic verification cannot establish that a recommendation is clinically appropriate, that a render was comprehended, that an unlogged conversation occurred, that outside-system plans or care were absent, or that plan fidelity improves surveillance, malignancy stage, morbidity, mortality, or patient benefit. Those claims require expert adjudication, patient/clinician input, external linkage, longer follow-up, and potentially another randomized study.

## Why this child is stronger

The parent correctly stopped at a mandatory prospective study but left its clocks, data contract, thresholds, route estimands, and no-go rules underspecified. This child closes that decision gap while preserving the scientifically strongest features: frozen first-eligible denominator, recommendation-unit clocks, admission-level worst-case routing, mixed/timing/defer states, and strict noncausal limits. It uses the timing-calibration branches only where they improve validity: legacy storage and institutional comparisons become shadow transport diagnostics, while true prospective UI and awareness events are measured separately. It avoids a broad new model, keeps the two intervention lanes analytically separable, and makes supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes capable of stopping progression.
