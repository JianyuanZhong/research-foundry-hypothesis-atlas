# Is the post-LDT AFP-slope → local-progression hazard a biological effect or a follow-up-intensity artifact? A pre-specified detection-bias discrimination test

Island: hcc (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records; snapshot 3f97e661...). Episode 24, operation = evolve.
Scientific parent (fixed by the Lead): `[prior hypothesis]` (valid; post-LDT AFP/ALBI landmark design +
first-modality effect-modification test). The parent's frozen design (Section 3) is inherited **verbatim**. This child
adds exactly ONE pre-specified, falsifiable element (Section 4): a **surveillance-intensity / detection-bias
discrimination test**. Status: design + this-episode bounded feasibility probe; **no outcome model, HR or interaction
test is computed here** (discovery).

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
The parent branch asks whether the per-1-SD within-patient AFP slope after a 180-day post-treatment landmark predicts
local progression, and whether that hazard is transportable across first liver-directed modality (TACE / ablation /
resection). It does not yet distinguish two explanations for a modality-dependent hazard:
- **H_bio:** the AFP-slope→progression hazard differs by modality (effect modification of tumour biology / treatment context).
- **R_detect (strongest rival):** patients who are monitored more intensively (more AFP draws, more abdominal imaging)
  are *both* more likely to have an estimable AFP slope and more likely to have a progression event recorded earlier.
  Apparent modality differences could then be a **follow-up-intensity / surveillance artifact**, not biology.
These have opposite consequences: H_bio supports modality-specific AFP-guided imaging; R_detect means the "AFP-guided
surveillance" lever is confounded by the very surveillance it is meant to guide, and the pooled recommendation should
be suspended pending a marker whose measurement is not itself the exposure.

## 2. Claims: supported evidence vs the claim this experiment tests
- Strongest claim the already-available evidence supports: baseline ALBI grade stratifies TACE-treated uHCC prognosis
  [K1]; serial AFP trajectories are heterogeneous and modellable, with a dominant non-rising class [K3]; lead-time,
  time-dependent sensitivity/specificity and immortal-time bias are named, real pitfalls of serial-biomarker analysis [K2].
- Unresolved claim this experiment tests: after adjusting for post-landmark surveillance intensity, is the AFP-slope
  hazard (a) attenuated to the null (detection artifact), (b) preserved and modality-homogeneous, or (c) preserved but
  still modality-heterogeneous (H_bio survives)?
- None of [K1][K2][K3] isolates a post-treatment AFP-slope hazard by first modality, nor separates it from
  measurement/follow-up intensity.

## 3. Frozen inherited design (VERBATIM from parent; unchanged)
- Population: HCC diagnosis (诊断名称 contains 肝细胞癌/肝癌) AND ≥1 liver-directed procedure; join key 患者主索引 + 就诊号.
- Index = earliest qualifying liver-directed procedure; landmark t* = index + 180 d (90 d sensitivity). Exposure
  (AFP and ALBI slopes, per 1 SD of within-patient slope) fit **only** on draws in (index, t*].
- Same-draw AFP + albumin + total-bilirubin pairing within ±2 d on 检验时间; ALBI = 0.66·log10(bilirubin) − 0.085·albumin.
- Endpoint: modality-restricted abdominal local progression {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多} from 检查 reports; 转移 =
  competing event; retreatment proxy = secondary; censor via 出院时间. No survival / mRECIST claim.
- Look-alike analyte exclusions (前白蛋白 2422, 糖化白蛋白 287, 白蛋白（急）188, 总胆红素（急）188, 体液白蛋白 17,
  尿白蛋白 1); diagnostic 肝动脉造影 excluded from the procedure set.
- Four rivals each with a distinct test; conjunctive falsification; no causal claim.

## 4. THIS EPISODE'S ONE ADDED ELEMENT: surveillance-intensity / detection-bias discrimination
Pre-specify, before any outcome is computed:
(a) **Surveillance-intensity measures (new, measurable covariates).** Per patient, within (t*, censor]: `n_abd_exam`
    (abdominal cross-sectional studies), `n_afp_draw`, and `first_abd_exam_days` (days from t* to first abdominal exam).
    Reported as *descriptive* by first modality (this is the measured part of this proposal).
(b) **Negative-control marker test.** Re-fit the primary landmark Cox with **ALBI slope alone** (an AFP-independent
    liver-reserve marker) replacing the AFP slope, adjusted for the same covariates. If the ALBI-slope HR shows the same
    modality-dependent pattern as the AFP-slope HR, the pattern is non-specific to AFP and consistent with R_detect.
(c) **Intensity-adjusted re-test.** Re-fit the primary model adding `n_abd_exam` and `n_afp_draw` (and, secondarily,
    inverse-intensity-of-observation weighting). Re-run the parent's 2-df AFP-slope×modality LRT and report the
    attenuation of the AFP-slope HR (ΔHR) with 95% CI.
**Pre-declared branches (fixed before outcomes):**
- *Supportive of H_bio:* intensity-adjusted AFP-slope HR remains >1 with CI excluding 1, the modality interaction LRT
  stays significant (p<0.05), and the ALBI-slope negative control is flat (HR≈1).
- *Adverse (R_detect):* the AFP-slope HR attenuates by ≥50% toward the null after adjustment, OR the ALBI-slope
  negative control reproduces the same modality-dependent pattern (both → detection/observation artifact).
- *Inconclusive:* adjustment moves the HR by <50% but the interaction LRT loses significance, or event counts are too
  sparse per modality to estimate the interaction (pre-declared non-estimability boundary).
- *Precise null:* adjusted AFP-slope HR≈1 with a tight CI and a flat negative control.

- Per first modality (patients with >=1 post-landmark abdominal exam in the censoring window / progression descriptor
  post-landmark / metastasis descriptor / >=1 post-landmark AFP draw / any abdominal exam):
  TACE 2,050 / **1,267** / 377 / 2,934 / 7,718; ablation 726 / **344** / 49 / 1,028 / 2,678;
  resection 3,878 / **1,952** / 355 / 5,236 / 22,485.
Interpretation the solver must confirm, not assumed here: (i) post-landmark abdominal-exam coverage differs by modality
(resection 17.2% vs TACE 26.6%, ablation 27.1%) - **follow-up intensity is not exchangeable across modality**, the exact
premise of the detection-bias rival; (ii) progression-descriptor prevalence among examined patients also differs
(TACE 61.8% vs ablation 47.4% vs resection 50.3%), consistent with ascertainment heterogeneity; (iii) progression event
counts per stratum (1,267 / 344 / 1,952) are non-zero, so the stratified hazard and interaction LRT are estimable
(ablation is the smallest stratum - a pre-declared non-estimability boundary).
Honest limits: this probe measures cohort/label/adherence bindings only. It computes **no** HR, interaction p-value or
adjusted estimate; `afp_after` counts >=1 draw (not slope estimability, which needs >=2 paired draws); the lexical
endpoint still needs radiologist mRECIST adjudication.

## 6. Method comparison (recorded before selection)
- **M0 (baseline):** standard landmark Cox with AFP_slope_SD (+ covariates) and a 2-df modality interaction LRT — the
  parent's analysis. Cheap, interpretable, but assumes no measurement-intensity confounding.
- **M4 (chosen addition):** M0 + surveillance-intensity covariates and an ALBI-slope negative control (and, secondarily,
  inverse-intensity-of-observation weighting). Fitting target: partial-likelihood Cox coefficients and the LRT statistic;
  same split/population; evaluation by HR+95% CI, ΔHR, LRT p and negative-control flatness. What M4 reveals that M0
  loses: whether an apparent AFP/progression association is separable from the surveillance process that generates both
  the AFP measurements and the detected event. CPU-only, minutes-scale, well inside budget.
- Deferred: shared-parameter joint observation–event model (would model the draw process directly) — revisit if the
  negative control or IOO weighting indicates non-ignorable observation; not needed for the first discrimination test.

## 7. Estimand, falsification and what each result would/would not establish
Primary estimand: the intensity-adjusted per-1-SD AFP-slope hazard ratio for local progression within first-modality
strata, and its modality interaction. Computationally checkable: the HR/CI, ΔHR, LRT p, negative-control pattern, and
branch consistency with the pre-declared bands. NOT computationally checkable (needs external evidence / expert review):
the mRECIST validity of the lexical endpoint, causal "why modalities differ", and clinical truth of any surveillance
recommendation. A supportive result would not by itself establish a mechanism; an adverse result would show the pooled
AFP-slope association is not separable from follow-up intensity in this cohort.
