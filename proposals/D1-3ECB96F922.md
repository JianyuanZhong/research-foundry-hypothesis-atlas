## Evidence boundary and question

Inspected evidence supports that a static AFP/tumor-size/margin score achieved C-statistics near 0.81 in two Chinese validation cohorts and MVI is pathologically confirmed [K1]; M0/M1/M2 are sampling-dependent histopathologic grades and M2 was prognostic in one selected cohort [K2]; and routine-care observation presence and timing can encode clinician behavior rather than biology [K3]. None establishes that one additional preoperative laboratory day predicts this database's recorded MVI grade beyond the latest observed day.

The frozen tested hypothesis was: among explicitly graded, untreated, pathology-HCC first resections with at least two numeric preoperative laboratory days, does one additional earlier observed day improve 2020 prediction of recorded M1/M2 beyond the latest observed day after separating numeric values from exact-label panel identity and shared observation process?

## Computed results

In 2020, S-latest AUROC/Brier were 0.5435/0.2390, O2-label 0.5494/0.2429, and V2 0.6749/0.2139. V2 versus S improved AUROC by +0.1314 (95% patient-bootstrap CI +0.0902,+0.1708) and Brier by +0.0251 (+0.0182,+0.0320). V2 versus O2 improved AUROC by +0.1255 (+0.0807,+0.1688) and Brier by +0.0290 (+0.0208,+0.0371). All 20 replicates had positive AUROC and Brier effects against both comparators.

The direction was already present in 2019: V2 versus S AUROC +0.0916 and Brier gain +0.0165, with intervals excluding zero. In separate 2021 stress, V2 AUROC/Brier were 0.6125/0.2202 versus S 0.5623/0.2382 and O2 0.5543/0.2457; 2021 is not pooled with 2020.

V2's 2020 calibration slope was 1.3187, outside the frozen 0.8–1.2 gate. Net benefit exceeded both comparators at thresholds 0.30, 0.40, and 0.50, but was slightly lower than O2 at 0.20. Therefore the complete support rule fails even though the discrimination and Brier components pass.

## Attribution stress tests

Panel identity alone did not explain the fitted gain. O2 versus S had AUROC delta +0.0059 (95% CI −0.0357,+0.0477) and Brier gain −0.0039 (−0.0101,+0.0023). Reassigning observed values among exact labels within each day attenuated 86.4% of V2's Brier gain versus S; swapping earlier/latest days attenuated 51.6%; outcome permutation was below chance (AUROC 0.468) and worse in Brier.

The assay-drift manifest flagged only prealbumin under the frozen >10-fold median-ratio or >5 training-IQR rule. Dropping it preserved the 2020 V2-S AUROC delta (+0.1369; CI +0.0981,+0.1741) and Brier gain (+0.0222; +0.0159,+0.0282). Excluding 24 lexical-discordance-flagged 2020 pathology records also preserved or strengthened the result (+0.1370 AUROC; +0.0307 Brier). These are sensitivity analyses, not unit harmonization or pathology adjudication.

The C2 same-two-label secondary included 233–248 2020 patients and 95–107 events per replicate. Earlier+latest values versus latest-only had mean AUROC delta −0.0008 and mean Brier gain −0.0002. Thus C2 does not support a within-label ordered-change claim. The day −7 analysis retained only 86 2020 patients/36 events and was imprecise against S; it does not overturn the main association or establish robustness at that earlier boundary.

## Frozen-rule interpretation

The strongest computed claim is that, in this selected recorded-grade cohort, V2 captured predictive information beyond latest-day values and beyond exact panel identity, and this association survived the prespecified drift and pathology-template exclusions. The experiment does **not** meet the frozen supportive definition because calibration slope failed; C2 also gives no evidence for within-label ordered change. The honest overall classification is inconclusive, not supportive and not a null result.

The fixed-day numeric association is worth independent confirmation only if a future study provides a genuinely unopened later/external cohort, explicit units/platforms, and blinded pathology adjudication.
