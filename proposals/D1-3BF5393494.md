# HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B validation with an explicit assay-observation, specimen/result-validity, and clinical-interpretation contract

## Successor, preserved design, and narrow repair

This is a targeted two-parent successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the mature frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B design: the selected cohort and repeat-event rule; the exact B/P/event/Y clocks; annual forward validation; locked nested ridge models; the primary observed-complete-triplet estimand; assay-separated all-BP missing-Y partial-identification bounds; patient-frequency bootstrap; source-, window-, and patient-event-specific observability horizons; and the strict noncausal evidence boundary. It does not change the scientific schema, cohort, temporal boundaries, estimand, outcome, model, denominator, or interpretation of the inherited primary result.

The remaining clinically material weakness is semantic rather than predictive. A row in `labs` proves that an assay-like record was written, but does not by itself prove that a specimen was collected and acceptable, that the numeric result is analytically valid, or that a longitudinal change is clinically interpretable. This proposal adds a compiler-ready, fail-closed three-layer assay contract around the frozen analysis:

1. **Recorded assay observation**: an exact target-assay row with a linked patient/encounter key and a valid `test time`, irrespective of whether a usable result payload exists.
2. **Machine-checkable specimen/result usability proxy**: a stricter, observable classification requiring a nonempty `Specimen type`, a finite uncensored numeric `Quantitative result`, and no explicit invalidity/cancellation/rejection token in the available result/specimen fields. This is a reproducible data-quality proxy, not proof of true specimen validity.
3. **Clinically interpretable HCC laboratory trajectory**: not available from this snapshot. The schema has no unit, reference-range, assay-method, collection/accession, quality-control, rejection/status, treatment-response, symptom, imaging-response, or adjudicated clinical-outcome fields. Therefore no row, change, slope, threshold crossing, or model prediction may be called clinically interpretable, treatment response, hepatic deterioration, or patient benefit.

The primary clocks retain the parent numeric parser and use layer 2 only as the explicit name for the existing `finite uncensored numeric` requirement. Layer 1 is reported separately and never substitutes for B, P, or Y. Layer 3 is globally `unavailable`, not silently inferred. No new empirical HCC finding is claimed; the assay-contract outputs remain downstream computations.

## Question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The frozen falsifiable question remains:

> Among selected adult HCC repeat-TACE episodes with remote baseline assay B and pre-repeat assay P, does P improve held-out prediction of the subsequent same-assay laboratory value Y beyond B and the locked pre-event core?

The observation-process question is now more precise:

> Among BP-eligible episodes and all target-assay rows in the relevant windows, how often is an assay merely recorded, how often does it meet the strict observable specimen/result-usability proxy, and how often can a longitudinal result be classified as clinically interpretable from the available data?

The strongest claim any successful run can support is limited to a selected, assay-specific, short-horizon prognostic association using recorded numeric values that pass the declared parser, conditional on the observed complete-triplet and observation process. A favorable result cannot establish specimen collection quality, laboratory accuracy, units, reference-normalized change, HCC trajectory, treatment response, causal benefit, safety, utility, or an action rule. A result that fails the stricter assay contract is not evidence of a normal or abnormal clinical state; it is `unknown/invalid-for-analysis` under the declared contract.

The substantive advance over the parents is that every assay-related state has an explicit meaning and an executable transition. `recorded` is no longer conflated with `result-valid`; `result-valid proxy` is no longer conflated with `clinical interpretation`; and absent metadata produces an adjudication-needed/unknown branch rather than a favorable or adverse clinical label. The layer uses only structured fields available in HCC and cannot manufacture missing laboratory metadata.

## Executable assay-state ladder

The compiler must retain one row per source laboratory row before target-clock selection, with stable within-source row identity or a deterministic hash of all source fields. It must preserve raw non-identifying assay fields only as needed for aggregate audit; no names or direct identifiers may be emitted. Normalize whitespace only for parsing flags and preserve the raw value for audit counts. Do not silently coerce an empty string to zero.

### Layer A: recorded assay observation

For a target assay `a` in `{Albumin, Total Bilirubin}`, define:

```
recorded_assay_observation =
    (Test == a)
    AND nonempty linked (patient master index, encounter number)
    AND valid parsed test time
```

This is a source-record presence state. It does not require `quantitative result`, `qualitative result`, or `specimen type`. Report counts by assay, arm, origin/block, event-relative window, and whether the row is linked to selected TACE2, repeat event, B, P, Y window, D1, or D2. Rows with a target label but missing/invalid time are `recorded_label_without_valid_time`, not timed observations. A row with a key but no exact target label is not a target assay observation.

### Layer B: specimen/result usability proxy

Define deterministic parser flags for each target row:

* `specimen_type_present = nonempty(trim(Specimen Type))`.
* `quantitative_value_finite = quantitative result parses as one finite real number`; reject empty, NaN, infinity, and censored/inequality forms such as leading `<`, `>`, `≤`, `≥`, or nonnumeric mixed result strings. Do not substitute `qualitative result` for a numeric value.
* `explicit_invalidity_token = 1` if the normalized available `specimen type`, `qualitative result`, or `quantitative result` contains an explicit specimen/result failure token from a frozen, versioned dictionary, including rejection/cancellation/insufficient/hemolysis/contamination markers; otherwise `0`. The dictionary must be declared in the output and may only produce a conservative flag. An unrecognized string is not evidence of validity and is `unknown`, not automatically valid.
* `result_payload_present = quantitative_value_finite OR nonempty(trim(Qualitative Result))`.

The strict machine-checkable proxy is:

```
specimen_result_usable_proxy =
    recorded_assay_observation
    AND specimen_type_present
    AND quantitative_value_finite
    AND explicit_invalidity_token == 0
```

A row satisfying this proxy is named `numeric_result_usable_proxy`, never `true_valid_specimen`, `clinically_valid`, or `normal/abnormal`. The frozen B/P/Y clocks use this proxy exactly; this is the inherited finite-uncensored parser plus the newly explicit specimen/status guard. If the parent frozen denominators do not reproduce after applying this declared proxy, stop and classify the run `unsupported` rather than silently reverting to source presence or changing the cohort. A conservative sensitivity report may show the inherited numeric-only parser side by side, but it cannot replace the primary contract or be pooled with it.

Use mutually exclusive row states in this order:

1. `usable_numeric_proxy` — all proxy conditions pass;
2. `explicit_invalid_or_rejected` — a frozen invalidity token is present;
3. `missing_or_censored_numeric` — target row and valid time exist, but numeric result is missing, nonfinite, censored, or nonnumeric;
4. `missing_specimen_type` — target row and valid time exist but specimen type is empty, with the numeric substate retained;
5. `recorded_only_payload_unknown` — target row/time exist, payload cannot be classified by the frozen parser;
6. `label_without_valid_time` — target label exists but time is invalid;
7. `not_target_assay`.

The implementation must report the joint cross-tab of `recorded_assay_observation` and the proxy, not only the proxy denominator. A recorded assay with unusable result is not a negative laboratory result and cannot create a missing-Y completion event.

### Layer C: clinically interpretable trajectory

Set `clinical_interpretability_status = unavailable_missing_metadata` for every target assay and every derived B/P/Y trajectory unless an external adjudication file is explicitly supplied and separately frozen. Do not infer interpretability from:

* a finite number;
* a nonempty specimen type;
* a repeated same-assay result;
* a monotone or nonmonotone change;
* a value above or below an empirically observed range;
* a discharge timestamp, diagnosis, procedure, medication, examination, or document;
* agreement between albumin and bilirubin; or
* model prediction or RMSE gain.

A permitted intermediate label is `recorded_numeric_trajectory_candidate`: at least two `usable_numeric_proxy` rows for the same patient and exact assay with strict time ordering, each within a declared episode/window. This label describes a reproducible sequence of source numbers only. It is not a clinical trajectory and must carry `adjudication_needed=1`, `units_available=0`, `reference_range_available=0`, `assay_method_available=0`, `specimen_quality_status_available=0`, and `patient_outcome_link_available=0`. Do not compute clinically named slopes, “improvement,” “worsening,” threshold crossings, or response categories. If a downstream consumer requests a clinical interpretation, return `not_estimable_from_HCC_snapshot` rather than a guessed category.

### Validity/adjudication output contract

For each assay and each B/P/Y candidate row, emit separate fields:

`recorded_assay_observation`, `recorded_time_valid`, `specimen_type_present`, `quantitative_value_finite`, `explicit_invalidity_token`, `numeric_result_usable_proxy`, `true_specimen_validity_status`, `clinical_interpretability_status`, `adjudication_needed`, and `eligibility_role`.

`true_specimen_validity_status` is always one of `unknown_not_encoded`, `explicitly_failed`, or `not_applicable`; it must never be set to `valid` merely because the proxy passes. `clinical_interpretability_status` is always `unavailable_missing_metadata` in this snapshot. For primary clock rows, `eligibility_role` is `B`, `P`, `Y`, or `none`; a layer-A-only row has `eligibility_role=none` even when it is in the event window. This prevents source presence from being mistaken for a usable endpoint.

A required aggregate field-availability table must include, for `labs`: source path, table/schema ID and hash, columns used, target assay, nonempty counts for `test`, `qualitative result`, `quantitative result`, `specimen type`, `test time`, valid-time count, finite-numeric count, censored/nonfinite count, explicit-invalidity-token count, duplicate composite-key count, and status of each missing metadata field. It must explicitly state `unit_available=0`, `reference_range_available=0`, `assay_method_available=0`, `collection_time_available=0`, `specimen_quality_status_available=0`, and `clinical_interpretation_available=0`. Missingness of these fields is a reason for adjudication-needed, not a reason to discard all source records or to assign a clinical class.

## Outcome-blind observation, horizons, and downstream recording

Retain the parent event-specific, source-specific, window-specific horizon design unchanged. For every BP row define `D_y = event_time +72 hours`, and freeze opportunity strata before reading Y or any source row after `D_y`:

1. `exam+followup`: valid examination `Start Time` in `(event,D_y]` plus an additional distinct encounter with valid `Encounter Time` in the same interval;
2. `exam_only`;
3. `followup_only`;
4. `document_only`: nonempty linked document for selected encounter with neither timed indicator;
5. `none`.

The strata are recording opportunities, not completed testing or care. A document has no native timestamp and is only `encounter_timed_document_presence` when inherited from an additional encounter time. Do not read narrative text.

For the selected event encounter, parse `Discharge Time` only if finite and not earlier than valid `Admission Time`. Classify `< event_time`, `== event_time`, `(event_time,D_y]`, `>D_y`, or `discharge_missing_or_invalid`. It is not disposition, death, transfer, completed follow-up, or proof of nonmeasurement. Emit `death_status=unavailable` globally; never infer death from absent rows. A transfer key match remains identifier-only.

For each BP event, assay, source, and requested window construct the patient-event-specific horizon from independent valid timestamps, never from a dataset-wide maximum. Exclude the selected event encounter for additional-encounter capture and exclude target-Y/clock-defining lab rows as coverage anchors. Eligible timestamp expressions are encounters `Visit Time`, labs `Test Time` (exact assays separate), examinations `Start Time`, orders under the fixed precedence, medications valid start/end, procedures valid start/end, and encounter-timed documents inherited from encounters. Transfers, vitals, and front_page have no usable temporal horizon. For D1 use `(event+72h,event+7d)` and require a source anchor at or beyond event+7d; for D2 use `[event+7d,event+30d]` and require an anchor at or beyond event+30d. Missing independent anchors are `administratively_incomplete`, not no-care.

Only after freezing all BP rows, layer-A/B assay states, opportunity strata, discharge states, and Y attachment may downstream audit inspect D1/D2. Retain source components `recorded_encounter_capture`, `recorded_lab_capture` with generic and exact-assay flags, `recorded_exam_capture`, `recorded_order_capture`, `recorded_medication_capture`, `recorded_procedure_capture`, `recorded_document_capture`, and `recorded_any_capture`. None is a clinical endpoint or proof of assay completion. For exact target lab capture, report both `target_assay_recorded` (layer A) and `target_assay_usable_numeric_proxy` (layer B); a generic lab row cannot be substituted for either.

## Falsifiable negative-control and adjudication-needed layer

The new layer must include the following prespecified checks, all descriptive and outside the primary models, bounds, event rules, Y selection, and bootstrap.

### 1. Presence-versus-usability discordance check

Among exact target rows with valid time, compare the fraction that is layer A recorded to the fraction that is layer B usable proxy, separately by assay, arm, origin/block, event-relative window, and opportunity/discharge state. A nonzero discordance is expected as a possibility, not a finding. If any implementation reports identical counts without showing the underlying payload-state cross-tab, mark the assay layer `unsupported`. If a favorable prognostic interpretation is based on layer-A rows that fail layer B, mark `selection_sensitive` or `inconclusive`, never supportive.

### 2. Pre-specified recording-process negative control

For each BP episode, define an outcome-blind `non_target_lab_recorded` indicator from `labs` rows with a valid `Test Time` and `Test` not equal to either exact target assay, in the same pre-Y or D1/D2 window. Keep exact target rows out of this negative-control flag. Report its association with (a) layer-A Y presence and (b) layer-B Y usability, using descriptive rates and Wilson intervals only, stratified by assay and block. A gradient is evidence of recording opportunity/selection, not biological HCC trajectory. If the negative-control flag is accidentally allowed to use Y rows or target assay rows, fail closed as `unsupported`.

A second implementation check uses a **payload-invalid target-row control**: target rows that are layer A but not layer B, identified before Y attachment, must not count as valid B/P/Y or completed testing. If their presence is used to claim an abnormal or normal laboratory state, emit `adjudication_needed`, and the verifier must reject any supportive clinical interpretation.

### 3. Time-shift placebo

Repeat the outcome-blind non-target-lab recording diagnostic in a pre-event placebo window of equal duration shifted before `event_time`, and in a delayed post-Y window only after primary state freezing. These are recording-intensity controls. Any discrepancy is not a causal null test. The implementation must report window endpoints and prevent a shifted-window row from entering B/P/Y or a source horizon.

### 4. Assay-separation control

Albumin and total bilirubin must have separate layer-A/B/C states, denominators, parser ranges, predictions, bounds, and Wilson intervals. A bilirubin row cannot establish albumin observation, validity, or interpretation. If assays are pooled, if one assay's unit/reference status is used for the other, or if a generic lab row fills either target, fail `unsupported`.

### 5. Adjudication-needed sentinel tests

A synthetic oracle must include rows with: valid exact assay and time but empty specimen type; specimen type and finite number but explicit rejection token; finite numeric result with a censored token; nonempty qualitative result but no quantitative result; repeated values with no units/reference range; target row at each endpoint boundary; generic non-target lab; and target row after the Y window. Expected states must be exact. Every repeated numeric sequence must retain `clinical_interpretability_status=unavailable_missing_metadata`; no numeric pattern may produce a clinical trajectory label. A failed sentinel test is `unsupported`, not a data-dependent inconclusive result.

## Missing-Y partial identification and uncertainty, retained unchanged

Run the inherited all-BP bounds for every assay, origin, and future block. Fit M0/M1 only on pre-origin observed complete triplets and freeze finite predictions for every BP row. Let `N` be all BP-eligible rows and `n_mis` the missing-Y count. Define `L_train` and `U_train` from the exact finite training parser. An empty/degenerate range or any future observed Y outside the training range is `unsupported`; do not widen the range or impute Y.

For observed rows use `e0=(Y-p0)^2`, `e1=(Y-p1)^2`, `d=e0-e1`. For missing rows, model-specific endpoint errors are:

```
e_j,min = (clip(p_j,L_train,U_train)-p_j)^2
 e_j,max = max((L_train-p_j)^2,(U_train-p_j)^2)
```

Use the affine contrast `d(y)=2*y*(p1-p0)+p0^2-p1^2`, with missing-row extrema `min(d(L),d(U))` and `max(d(L),d(U))`, then add observed terms and divide by fixed all-BP `N`. Report global and arm-specific endpoints, all-missing-at-L, all-missing-at-U, and least-favorable assignments. These are deterministic identification regions, not confidence intervals. Preserve the fixed-prediction, arm-stratified, patient-frequency bootstrap with B=2000 when estimable; zero/nonfinite SD, no observed triplets, or fewer than two observed patient clusters is non-estimable, not zero. Preserve the piecewise-linear common-displacement tipping calculation and its root rules. The assay-state ladder may annotate rows but cannot change the all-BP denominator, the primary complete-case estimand, or the bound arithmetic.

## Interpretation precedence and fail-closed branches

Apply these labels in order:

1. **unsupported** — source/schema/hash/header mismatch; duplicate-unsafe join; clock or endpoint error; target rows used as horizon anchors; assay pooling; invalid state parser; failure to distinguish layer A from B; true validity or clinical interpretation silently inferred; negative-control leakage; or failed synthetic sentinel.
2. **adjudication_needed** — any request to call a specimen truly valid, a result clinically abnormal/normal, or a numeric sequence an HCC trajectory when units, reference range, method, collection/quality status, or patient-important outcome is unavailable. This label is a data-meaning limitation, not evidence against P or M1.
3. **inconclusive** — failed BP/arm gates; sparse cells; nonfinite predictions; empty/degenerate training range; future Y outside training range; non-estimable SD/bootstrap; incomplete event/source horizon; unknown payload state affecting the requested contrast; or an observation cell below the declared threshold.
4. **adverse_primary** — a nonpositive adequately supported primary observed gain, repeated M1 degradation, or an all-BP global bound wholly nonpositive. Downstream recording patterns or a layer-A count cannot rescue it.
5. **selection_sensitive** — favorable complete-case gain with a global bound crossing zero; material layer-A versus layer-B discordance; strong assay/opportunity/discharge/calendar/arm gradients; negative-control recording warning; source disagreement; or dependence on unadjudicated payload states.
6. **supported_recording_description** — only a finite, reproducible, gate-passing administrative recording vector with complete relevant horizons. This describes recorded data, not completed assay, true specimen validity, clinical trajectory, benefit, or actionability.
7. **supportive prognostic association** — permitted only if the inherited estimand is estimable, support gates pass, no material observation or assay-state warning exists, the global all-BP Delta interval is strictly positive throughout the declared training-range scenario, and the claim is explicitly limited to selected short-horizon association in the tested calendar mixture. It is never a clinical interpretation.

If the layer-A observation rate is high but layer-B usability is low, this is not a high assay-completion rate; report discordance and use `selection_sensitive`, `inconclusive`, or `adjudication_needed` as appropriate. If layer B is high but metadata remain absent, the numeric prediction may be computed under the frozen estimand, but clinical interpretation remains unavailable. If a result is absent, do not call it a failed assay or clinical deterioration. If a source horizon is incomplete, do not call absence of a record no care. If a negative control is positive, interpret it as recording-process evidence only.

## Verification contract and limits

The compiler/verifier can check exact hashes and headers; deduplicated joins; inherited cohort/event/triplet counts; B/P/event/Y clocks; layer-A/B state transitions; explicit invalidity sentinels; assay separation; no target leakage; opportunity strata; discharge validity; source-specific horizons; D1/D2 boundaries; Wilson arithmetic; fixed predictions; all-BP endpoint arithmetic; piecewise-linear roots; bootstrap bookkeeping; and precedence-ordered labels. It can verify that no clinical interpretation was emitted when required metadata are unavailable.

It cannot establish true specimen validity, assay calibration, units, reference ranges, analytic method, collection quality, laboratory error, biological meaning of a change, HCC burden, imaging response, treatment response, hepatic failure, death, readmission, completed follow-up, external-care capture, treatment administration, causality, transport, utility, safety, or patient benefit. Those require laboratory metadata and expert adjudication, linked claims/mortality and imaging or clinical outcomes, and independent temporal/prospective validation. A verifier must reject a numerically correct computation if its conclusion calls a recorded assay a completed valid specimen or a numeric sequence a clinically meaningful HCC trajectory.

No source rows are modified. No empirical HCC result is claimed in this proposal. The requested executable layer is a conservative semantic contract around the frozen design; full source reconstruction, model fitting, state counts, negative controls, and bounds remain downstream work.
