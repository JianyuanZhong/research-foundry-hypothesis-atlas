# Source-local benefit–harm gate for context-selective MerTK prioritization

Status: substantive successor of `[prior hypothesis]`; planned Harbor experiment only. No screening or scientific fitting has been executed. “Observed” below means registry, schema and source-file inspection, not biological results.

## Scientific opening, hypothesis and decision

The three inspected works establish a bounded prior, not the proposed conclusion. MerTK-positive synovial macrophage states are associated with remission biology and can induce fibroblast repair [K1]. An inflammatory macrophage state can drive fibroblast invasiveness through tissue cross-talk [K2]. Fibroblast inflammatory programs are heterogeneous and niche-linked [K3]. The unresolved decision is whether the configured data can support a next-stage target-prioritization decision that weighs a remission-associated macrophage benefit proxy against MerTK-perturbation FLS harm.

Falsifiable hypothesis:

> A state-stratified MerTK follow-up is justified only if two source-local findings jointly pass independent identifiability gates: (1) E-MTAB-8322 remission macrophages have a stable, preregistered MerTK-associated benefit proxy above the naive/resistant reference; and (2) the exact E-MTAB-8873 MerTK-inhibitor interaction remains materially harmful in the Mpos context relative to Mneg after membership-aware controls and sensitivities. If either axis fails, these data do not support a context-selective MerTK strategy.

This is a conjunctive advancement gate, not a patient-level net-benefit estimand. The sources have no shared donor, perturbation, time field, cell composition, endpoint or verified common biological unit. No `benefit - harm` arithmetic, cross-source pooling or donor-ID matching is allowed.

The clinically important decision is whether to invest in state-stratified MerTK preservation/activation, balanced loss-of-function and orthogonal functional assays, or abandon selective MerTK reasoning in favor of generic inflammation/composition explanations. Supportive results justify follow-up only; they do not establish clinical efficacy, direct target engagement, a therapeutic window or a patient-selection rule.

## Evidence, leading explanation and rivals

[K1] supports remission-associated MerTK-positive macrophages and fibroblast repair, but not the configured benefit proxy, inhibitor interaction or clinical efficacy. [K2] is the consequential rival: inflammatory macrophage–fibroblast cross-talk could explain FLS harm without MerTK selectivity. [K3] limits any four-transcript FLS harm score because fibroblast programs are niche-linked and heterogeneous.

The leading explanation is that a remission-associated MerTK program identifies a protective macrophage context whose perturbation exposes FLS harm. Plausible alternatives are:

- Disease-state selection, treatment history, cell composition, capture depth or donor biology may generate the 8322 state score without protective MerTK biology.
- Generic stress, LPS response, assay round, composition, exposure tier or FLS baseline may generate the 8873 interaction; HBEGF-like cross-talk is a substantive rival [K2].
- The targeted FLS panel may miss invasion, matrix degradation, viability and other niche-specific functions [K3].
- Concordant source-local axes would remain descriptive and non-causal; they cannot establish direct MerTK action or a shared patient-level benefit-harm effect.

## Baseline versus substantive alternative

Both methods use the same exact source-local inputs, targets, split and uncertainty criteria.

The transparent baseline computes fixed `B) and its state contrasts in 8322, and the parent’s paired score/interaction model for `Delta) in 8873 using the incidence matrix only when rank permits. It reports component effects, controls, exact versus preInhibitor tiers, leave-round-out and leave-membership-out results. Its advantage is auditable behavior under sparse designs; it loses correlated marker structure and uncertainty sharing.

The substantive alternative is a source-stratified multivariate/mechanistic model. In 8322, fit the same seven benefit transcripts with a shared latent macrophage-state factor, gene-specific loadings, state effects and donor-level residual covariance. In 8873, fit the same 11 measured target/context/harm transcripts with feature-specific exposure/context/interaction effects, crossed FLS/macrophage membership terms and a shared harm factor. It must output the same `B) contrasts and `Delta), components, controls and uncertainty—not a surrogate prediction. The alternative can reveal whether each signal is a coherent program or a one-marker artifact; it cannot establish direct mechanism. If either source gate fails, defer that multivariate fit and report why.

The fixed split is generated once from frozen IDs: five-fold donor/sample holdout stratified by state in 8322 and by admissible membership cluster/round in 8873, reused by both methods. In 8322, this is a stability diagnostic because groups are small, not external validation. Fit any learned loadings or nuisance adjustments inside training folds; evaluate held-out contrasts/rank stability. Primary uncertainty is a cluster bootstrap over 8322 donor IDs and 8873 contributing membership clusters, with bounded sign/permutation and leave-cluster-out sensitivity. Cells and matrix entries are never replicates.

CPU is selected: planned future solver use is 4–8 cores, <16 GiB and minutes for 19 samples/33 samples and 7/11 features; this is an estimate, not measured runtime. GPU is deferred because the bottleneck is identifiability, not matrix scale. A neural predictor is not selected because predictive gain would not distinguish donor/composition selection from MerTK biology or answer the target decision.

## Falsification and interpretation

Freeze a material threshold of 0.5 SD of `B) and 0.5 SD of `H) before fitting. These are within-source thresholds and must not be subtracted.

Supportive requires the 8322 feature/state gate to pass, remission `B) contrast above its threshold with uncertainty supporting it, stable held-out/leave-donor signs and no single marker/donor driving it; the 8873 exact gate to pass, `Delta) above its threshold with uncertainty supporting it, coherent components and stable admissible sensitivities; and no Mpos-selective LPS/FLS-only mimicry. This supports orthogonal MerTK perturbation, protein/phospho-MerTK, viability, efferocytosis, secreted mediators and direct FLS invasion/matrix-degradation testing, not clinical efficacy.

Adverse means the 8322 proxy is nonpositive/unstable, the 8873 `Delta) is nonpositive or at least as large in Mneg, a control reproduces selectivity, or the multivariate model reveals a single-marker/membership artifact. This favors generic inflammation, composition, treatment history or assay-specific explanations.

Inconclusive means a feature, state, rank, overlap or holdout gate fails; uncertainty spans zero or threshold; an admissible mapping/tier changes sign; one cluster drives the result; or state selection cannot be separated from protective biology. Do not interpret an imprecise null as refutation. The next study needs balanced matched Mpos/Mneg FLS–macrophage donors, dose/exposure time, viability, protein/phospho-MerTK, efferocytosis, secreted mediators, spatial localization and direct FLS functional endpoints.

Harbor can check joins, exact feature presence, state counts, rank, split reproducibility, uncertainty, controls and conclusion-to-output linkage. It cannot establish clinical benefit, direct mechanism, viability, spatial localization, therapeutic window or biological validity of labels without expert review and another study.
