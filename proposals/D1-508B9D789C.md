# Residual instability before ICU-to-ward transfer: one value-over-process result contract

Status: substantive crossover of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen cohort, landmark, outcomes, measured feasibility, and scientific question. ## Decision and scientific boundary

The measured evidence supports feasibility only. The frozen cohort has 49,157 first eligible transfers among 40,014 subjects; its deterministic subject split has no role crossing. Among raw-G rows, the endpoint-fixed perturbation is effective in 99.43%–99.57% of every required role, median null support is 133–135 distinct G values, median scaled permutation SD is 0.596–0.624, median materially moved fraction is 0.955, and G-pre coverage is 74.99%–84.48%. This licenses the outcome experiment. It does not establish outcome association, clinical utility, order attribution, physiology, preventability, or benefit from changing transfer or monitoring.

Existing work shows that longitudinal ICU-discharge models may outperform snapshot scores, while operational factors, treatment limitation, and composite endpoints complicate interpretation [K1]. A systematic review finds potential value in longitudinal predictors but heterogeneous outcomes, populations, and bias [K2]. Observation timing and frequency can themselves carry outcome information because EHR trajectories encode healthcare processes [K3]. These works motivate the question and its main rival; none answers it.

The unresolved hypothesis remains: among eligible ICU-to-general-ward transfers with the same measured endpoint state at t0, unusually worsening rather than improving recent measured-value history adds calibrated information about 48-hour ICU return/death and materially changes a fixed-capacity review queue, including when endpoint state and recent direction disagree.

The strongest rivals are: (1) mathematical coupling or regression to the mean caused by sharing the final measurement between endpoint and direction summaries; and (2) treatment, observation, documentation, clinician-concern, and transfer-selection processes. The experiment can distinguish predictive value over recorded process from these measured rivals. It cannot identify physiology, clinician intent, a treatment effect, unrecorded selection, or intervention benefit.

## Identifiability repair

Residualization and joint adjustment are not two independent confirmations. `G_perp=G-m_hat(Z)` is an outcome-blind feature definition: it removes the component of G predictable from endpoint/context/recorded-process Z. Adding the same process block to the outcome model then estimates the incremental prognostic value of that residual component conditional on recorded process. This is not a causal direct effect, and nonlinear splines/interactions mean exact Frisch-Waugh equivalence is not claimed.

Accordingly, the confirmatory transparent ladder is only:

- `E0`: endpoint state and static context;
- `P1=E0+P`: endpoint plus the recorded process block;
- `J1=P1+RCS(G_perp)+C×G_perp`: process-adjusted endpoint plus residual ordered value.

The sole confirmatory value-over-process estimand is J1 versus P1. A separate E0-plus-G_perp outcome model is removed because it reuses the same residualization without conditioning the outcome model on P and would be wrongly countable as independent evidence. Raw `D1=E0+RCS(G)+C×G` remains only a coupling diagnostic. E0 versus P1 reports recorded-process prediction, not causation. G-pre, reversal, 2%/10% queues, process strata, AUROC, and component endpoints are diagnostics or sensitivities and cannot rescue the primary result.

## Matched transparent order null: 200 complete refits

The inherited PCG64 seed integers and source/target ordering are frozen. Within each transfer/domain, permute nonfinal observed bins while preserving the final observed bin, complete observed value multiset, missing positions, chart/store schedule, caregivers, treatment/process sequence, split, and role.

E0 and P1 are invariant and are fit once. For each null seed k:

1. Build `G_k` from its frozen permutation before any outcome is used.
2. Reuse the observed-selected nuisance family, hyperparameters, folds, transforms, columns, and category levels, but refit all nuisance coefficients from scratch on development with target `G_k`. Outcome labels are never a nuisance input.
3. Generate development OOF and unopened-role nuisance predictions; standardize `G_perp_k` by that seed’s development OOF residual SD; save all balance diagnostics.
4. Fit raw D1_k and joint J1_k from scratch on development labels. Apply the same frozen alpha-selection and calibration algorithms independently on validation labels. No pooled-test or temporal label may select a model, knot, seed, threshold, or calibration parameter.
5. Evaluate the matching frozen predictions in pooled test and temporal roles. Save coefficients, selected alpha, calibration, predictions, and metrics.

Reusing observed nuisance coefficients on G_k is diagnostic only. Reusing an observed J1 outcome fit is invalid. The bootstrap resamples sealed predictions and outcomes; it does not refit models. Within each replicate, one subject draw is shared by observed and all 200 null metrics before subtracting the median null. Monte Carlo tail probability is `(1 + number of nulls at least as favorable as observed)/201`.

This null asks whether observed order adds more outcome information than histories with the same endpoint, values, missingness, and recorded process. It does not identify a biological mechanism.

## Smallest learned alternative and fair learned null

The learned alternative asks whether scalar G_perp loses clinically material nonlinear lag, opposing-domain, or cross-domain order information. It uses the same raw-G rows, labels, role map, and static E0 context.

Inputs are fixed: a value stream of 12 two-hour bins over `[t0-24h,t0)` for the six signed margins; a process stream of masks, time since observation, counts, store lag, caregiver counts, and ventilation/vasopressor/oxygen transitions; and the E0 static stream. No measured value enters the process stream. Permutation occurs before causal forward fill. Leading missing values use pooled-development medians; later missing values are forward-filled only after the first observation.

Fit only:

- full models for the first 20 frozen 12-bin order-null mappings, all with training seed 20260923: 20 fits.

The learned order comparison uses observed full seed 20260923 against the 20 same-initialization null fits; five observed seeds quantify stability. The observed ensemble full-versus-process contrast and full-versus-J1 contrast use mean calibrated probabilities across five seeds. ## One primary result and multiplicity control

All gains are oriented positive. The pooled-test primary metric is
`Delta_Brier = Brier(P1)-Brier(J1)` for binary R+D probability. The fixed-capacity co-gate is
`Delta_Capture5`, additional R+D events captured by J1 versus P1 per 1,000 records reviewed in the prespecified top 5% queue. The explanatory estimand is the frozen C×G_perp interaction and its 30th/70th risk-grid contrast. Raw D1, null contrasts, and reversal determine attribution; they are gates, not separate opportunities to declare success. The 2% and 10% queues, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, G-pre, component endpoints, process strata, and learned comparison are labeled secondary.

No multiplicity correction is needed across pooled and temporal roles because only pooled test decides the primary state and every supportive gate must pass; the temporal roles cannot upgrade it. Within pooled test, success is an intersection-union rule, not “any significant endpoint.” No quota, endpoint, role, seed, or subgroup may be selected after opening.

A transport modifier is appended after the pooled state:

- `transport_consistent`: both era point estimates for Delta_Brier and Delta_Capture5 are concordant, calibration/support gates pass, and neither 95% interval includes a clinically important reversal worse than -0.002 Brier or -2 events/1,000;
- `transport_adverse`: either era has a calibrated 95% upper bound below zero for both primary gains or a clinically important opposite effect;
- `transport_inconclusive`: all other cases, including poor support or calibration.

Because roles may overlap, these are generalization stress tests, not independent evidence.

## Exhaustive pooled interpretation

Apply the first matching state after structural, leakage, prediction-coverage, nuisance-balance, endpoint, and calibration gates. Calibration is locked before outcome opening: for P1 and J1 in pooled test, R+D calibration intercept must be within [-0.10,0.10], slope within [0.80,1.20], and the top-5% observed-to-predicted R+D ratio within [0.80,1.25]; failure is inconclusive rather than adverse.

**Supportive ordered-value information.** J1 versus P1 has a pooled-test 95% lower bound above zero for Delta_Brier; the 95% lower bound for Delta_Capture5 is at least 2 events/1,000; the frozen interaction/risk-grid contrast is concordant with a 95% interval excluding no interaction; raw D1 is concordant; reversal attenuates or opposes it; and observed J1-minus-P1 interaction and Brier gains exceed their matched nulls with Monte Carlo p<=0.05 and paired observed-minus-median-null 95% lower bounds above zero. This supports calibrated ordered-value information and consequential re-ranking beyond recorded process in the pooled cohort, not mechanism or benefit.

**Coupling-consistent adverse.** Raw D1 is favorable but lies within the central 95% raw-null distribution, reversal does not attenuate it, and observed-minus-median-null intervals include zero while J1 precision excludes material benefit: the 95% upper bound for Delta_Brier is <0.002 and for Delta_Capture5 is <2/1,000. Conclude that the favorable raw pattern is adequately reproduced by unordered repeated values plus endpoint/process-preserved coupling under this design. Do not claim a biological cause.

**Endpoint/process sufficient adverse.** Raw D1 is not favorable, and J1’s 95% upper bounds are <0.002 for Delta_Brier and <2/1,000 for Delta_Capture5. Prefer E0 if P1 also excludes material gain; prefer P1 if recorded process adds reproducible gain. If P1 improves but J1 does not, call this recorded-process-dominant prediction, not process causation. A precise opposite J1 effect is ordered-value adverse and must be reported, not folded into a null.

**Inconclusive.** Any mandatory gate fails; either primary interval contains both material benefit and material absence/harm; Brier and queue conclusions conflict; observed/null contrasts are imprecise; raw and residual/joint directions conflict without null explanation; raw versus adjudicated endpoint conclusions materially disagree; numeric oxygen sensitivity reverses the conclusion; or learned seeds/nulls are unstable for a learned claim. A G-pre null, failed learned extension, or temporal inconsistency alone does not erase a valid pooled transparent result; it limits the corresponding secondary or transport claim.

The learned axis is separately `learned_additional`, `learned_no_material_increment`, or `learned_inconclusive` under the exact rule above. It never changes the pooled transparent state.
