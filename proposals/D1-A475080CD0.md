## 0. Hypothesis vs. already-supported conclusion

**Already supported (NOT this child's claim).** Relative glycemia (stress hyperglycemia ratio,
SHR = admission glucose / HbA1c-derived chronic glucose) is *associated* with short-term ICU
mortality [K1]; the effect attenuates under full adjustment and is null in non-sepsis subgroups
[K2]; SHR also predicts a non-mortality acute outcome (72-h delirium) with no significant direct
mortality effect after mediation [K3]. So "SHR associates with mortality" is evidence-backed and
is the parent's retained platform claim.

**Unresolved question this child tests (the substantive advance).** The parent leaves its
strongest rival — a *patient-level* chronic-frailty + testing-propensity common cause — only
"bounded, not eliminated" by cross-sectional (between-subject) adjustment, and it never computed
its two estimands. The clinically consequential decision is: is the SHR–mortality increment a
*stay-level acute-severity* signal that survives the leading measurement/selection and frailty
rivals, and is it large enough to change a decision at plausible risk thresholds? A between-
patient common cause and a stay-level signal make *different predictions* on a within-subject
contrast and on a decision-curve analysis; that difference is what this child measures.

**Leading claim (H-acute).** The SHR–mortality increment carries stay-level information that
persists when each patient serves as their own control.

**Strongest rival (R-common-cause), three sub-rivals that must be separated:**
- **R-meas (measurement/observation process).** Sicker or more-monitored patients get more labs;
  SHR is partly a proxy for testing intensity/prior lab draw, so the signal is manufactured by the
  observation process, not the glucose value.
- **R-frailty (patient-level common cause).** Time-invariant frailty/comorbidity + each patient's
  testing propensity jointly drive SHR and mortality; the cross-sectional coefficient is a
  between-patient marker, not an acute signal.
- **R-timing (timing artifact).** HbA1c validity (CKD/anaemia/transfusion) and the admittance-time
  window contaminate the anchor glucose used in the ratio.

These are separable from H-acute on the Q-A contrast (within vs between coefficient) and on the
predeclared falsifiers S1–S4.

## 3. Estimands, baselines, analysis

- **Q-A (within vs between SHR coefficient).**
  - `β_between`: SHR coefficient from a penalized logistic on the primary (first-stay) cohort.
  - `β_within`: SHR coefficient from a conditional (fixed-effects) logistic stratified by
    `subject_id` among multi-stay subjects (each patient is their own control), with a
    within-subject demeaned SHR predictor as the sensitivity form.
  - **`dCoef = β_within − β_between`**, with patient-clustered bootstrap CIs.
  - **Predeclared equivalence/informative-null band: `|dCoef| ≤ 0.10` on the log-odds scale**
    (i.e. the within and between coefficients are practically the same magnitude → H-acute
    supported). Any change larger than the band that reverses the within-subject sign is adverse.
- **Q-B (decision relevance).** Decision-curve net benefit `NB(threshold)` for M0 (severity) vs
  M1 (M0 + SHR) at thresholds **5% / 10% / 20%**; `dNB = NB(M1) − NB(M0)` with patient-clustered
  bootstrap CIs. **Predeclared decision-relevance margin: `dNB ≤ 0.005` at a threshold is an
  informative null** (design-relevant only if the CI lower bound > 0 at ≥1 prespecified threshold
  with consistent sign).

Model ladder (retained from parent, per the method-exploration requirement):
- **M0 severity baseline:** age, sex, admission type, first-window SOFA-proxy items
  (GCS/MAP/lactate/creatinine via `icu/d_items` itemids) — no glycemia term.
- **M1 = M0 + SHR.**

### Method comparison (baseline vs. substantive alternative — required record)

- **B0 — simple interpretable baseline: penalized (L2) logistic regression** for 28-day mortality
  on {M0, M1}, split by the locked 70/30 temporal boundary; report AUC, `ΔAUC`, and the M1 SHR
  coefficient. *What B0 loses:* it treats the SHR value as given and only *adjusts* for
  covariates; it cannot represent that the glucose measurement itself is generated by the
  observation process, nor remove time-invariant patient effects.
- **A1 — substantive alternative: joint measurement-selection + outcome model.** Fit the
  testing/observation process (probability and count of glucose/HbA1c orders and first-draw
  timing, from `labevents` order-presence and `order_provider_id`/`priority`) jointly with the
  28-day mortality outcome (shared latent severity / Heckman-style selection or a joint
  logit with an observation-intensity latent). *What A1 reveals that B0 loses:* whether the SHR
  coefficient survives once the *generating process* of the measurement is modelled rather than
  adjusted — the direct test of R-meas. Target: sign/significance of the SHR coefficient in the
  outcome equation and the change vs. B0.
- **A2 — Q-A conditional within-subject model** (conditional logistic / within-subject demeaning)
  on the 16,242-subject multi-stay cohort, `β_within`, `dCoef`. *What A2 reveals that B0 loses:*
  removal of all time-invariant patient-level common causes (frailty, chronic comorbidity,
  baseline testing propensity) — the direct test of R-frailty.

## 4. Falsifiers

- **S1 label-permutation:** permute the 28-day mortality label within the split; the M1−M0
  `ΔAUC` and the SHR coefficient must collapse toward the informative-null band.
- **S2 value-free measurement-intensity-only negative control:** replace SHR with the
  value-free testing-intensity exposure (§2). If the M1 increment reproduces under an exposure
  that cannot carry glycemic information, R-meas is supported (adverse).
- **S3 negative-control outcome:** a plausibly SHR-independent outcome; if SHR "predicts" it with
  the same magnitude, SHR behaves as a non-specific severity/monitoring correlate (adverse).
- **S4 within-subject discordance:** among multi-stay patients whose SHR moves in opposite
  directions, does mortality track the SHR change (H-acute) or not (R-frailty)?

## 5. Supportive / adverse / inconclusive outcome rules

- **Q-A supportive (H-acute):** `|dCoef| ≤ 0.10` (CI within band) and `β_within > 0` with the
  within-subject increment preserved.
- **Q-A adverse (R-frailty/R-common-cause):** `|dCoef| > 0.10` with `β_within` ≈ 0 or reversed and
  a CI excluding `β_between` ⇒ signal is dominantly patient-level. Valuable NEGATIVE finding.
- **Q-A inconclusive:** CI spans both alternatives, or too few multi-stay subjects with events
  in-window (report exact `N_anchor`/`events_anchor`).
- **Q-B supportive (decision-relevant):** `dNB > 0` with CI lower > 0 at ≥1 prespecified
  threshold and consistent sign across thresholds.
- **Q-B adverse (decision-irrelevant):** `dNB ≤ 0`, or CI includes 0 at all thresholds
  (informative null).
- **Q-B inconclusive:** sign disagreement across thresholds with wide CIs.
- **Overall:** support requires the M1 increment surviving S1–S4 **AND** (preserved within-subject
  coefficient **OR** positive `dNB`). Otherwise report adverse or inconclusive. A null does not
  refute the platform association.

## 6. Honest limits / missing evidence

- Design stage + bounded binding probe only. `N_anchor`, `events_anchor`, `ΔAUC`, `β_within`,
  `β_between`, `dCoef`, `dNB` are **NOT-COMPUTED**.
- HbA1c validity in CKD/anaemia/transfusion (R-timing) and the "no glycemia mechanism" assumption
  behind negative-control outcomes require **clinical adjudication**; they cannot be established
  by this computation.
- Lab→stay linkage goes through `hadm_id` + time window because `labevents` has no `stay_id`;
  multi-ICU-stay admissions are a residual ambiguity mitigated (not eliminated) by the window.
- Single-centre retrospective; association / incremental-prognostic-information only; no causal
  or mechanistic claim. Measurement selection is *bounded by predeclared tests*, not eliminated.
- R-meas's structural assumption (that the joint model identifies the observation process) is a
  modelling assumption, not a physical guarantee; A1's result is conditional on it.
