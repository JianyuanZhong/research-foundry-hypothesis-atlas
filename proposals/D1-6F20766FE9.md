# Recognition-mode-stratified escalation: does a within-patient drop in ICU lab-draw intensity mark unmet monitoring need or a hospital draw-practice fingerprint?

Evolved working proposal — eICU 2.0 island, **episode 20**. Operation: **evolve** of true parent
`[prior hypothesis]` (valid, delivery-complete, mu ~32.05; rescue-sensitive
failure-to-rescue branch; seed origin `[starting question]`, "Reduced testing and subsequent
deterioration"). Status: **design frozen by the Lead for compilation**. The frozen parent population /
landmark / exposure / severity adjustment are kept verbatim; the **single substantive change** is that
the parent's #1 stated limit is converted into a new pre-specified, computable, directional test by
re-specifying the outcome as **recognition-mode-stratified escalation**. No reference/solver solution
was executed at discovery — this is design work, not a solved result.

## 1. The unresolved question and why it matters

ICU lab draws are a monitoring decision made by clinicians, and their *pattern* — not only their
values — encodes a care process. [K1] Agniel 2018 showed, across 272 lab tests in 669,452 patients,
that the **presence and timing of a test order** carry survival signal independent of the value
(order presence significant in 233/272 tests; timing more accurate than the value in 118/174). [K2]
Yeo 2026 reports a surgical-ICU de-implementation programme that reduced routine testing using
mortality/LOS as safety counterbalances, and [K3] the WICH-ICU stepped-wedge cluster RCT is now
trialling testing intensity against organ-support endpoints (duration of mechanical ventilation,
CRRT, ICU LOS). So the consequential, live, unresolved question is:

> When a patient's own clinical-panel draw intensity falls while they remain physiologically
> unstable, does that fall mark **unmet monitoring need that precedes a rescue event that was
> recognised late** (organ-support escalation **without a preceding charted trigger**), or is it a
> **between-hospital draw-practice fingerprint** that carries no recognition content?

The parent proposal established the rescue-sensitive **outcome** (new vasopressor/mechanical-VM
escalation in (24,48]h) and a **within-patient** exposure contrast, and correctly named its own
top unresolved limit: eICU lacks order-set/staffing/bedside-decision data, so the harmful
within-patient draw-rate drop could not be separated from a pure between-hospital practice process.
This evolution does not add a covariate; it **re-specifies the outcome into two recognition modes**
and pre-specifies a directional differential test that can, within the limits stated in §8, partially
separate the two mechanisms.

## 2. Claims: leading explanation and strongest rivals

- **H1 — unmet monitoring need / delayed recognition.** Among rescue-eligible patients, a
  **within-patient drop** in the clinical-panel draw rate after the hour-24 landmark predicts new
  organ-support escalation in (24,48]h, and — the discriminating prediction — that harmful association
  **concentrates in UNHERALDED escalations** (no charted abnormal vital/lab in the 4 h before onset)
  rather than in TRIGGERED ones. A patient whose monitoring was reduced is more likely to escalate
  without the charted warning that would normally have preceded it.
- **R1 — confounding by indication / recovery.** The drop marks a *recovering* patient; the harmful
  association is confined to the improving trajectory stratum or is null there. Kept as a secondary
  directional control so R1 stays testable.
- **R2 — pure draw-practice fingerprint.** The apparent association is a between-hospital drawing
  style. Its distinctive prediction: **no differential** — a draw-rate drop predicts triggered and
  unheralded escalation **equally, or neither** — because the exposure is a hospital-level property
  with no recognition content.
- **R3 — end-of-life / withdrawal.** The drop tracks a comfort-care transition; excluded by design
  (active carePlanEOL before the landmark and patients already on maximal support are removed).

H1 and R2 differ observably **on the differential between recognition modes** (§3). R1 differs on the
trajectory stratum. R3 is removed by the cohort definition.

## 3. The discriminating observation: what each result would and would not establish

The pre-specified discriminating comparison is the **recognition-mode differential**:

> Does a within-patient drop in `logR` (post- vs pre-hour-24 draw rate) predict **unheralded**
> escalation more strongly than **triggered** escalation, on a **shared cohort**?

Implement as two cause-specific logistic contrasts on the shared cohort — `unheralded` vs the
`no-escalation` set, and `triggered` vs the `no-escalation` set — and compare the `logR` coefficients;
equivalently a multinomial (3-level outcome: none / triggered / unheralded) logit. The primary
statistic is the **differential** Δ = β_unheralded − β_triggered on the log-odds scale, reported as the
ratio OR_drop,U / OR_drop,T.

- **If the differential favours unheralded (Δ < 0, ratio > 1, CI excluding 1) with triggered null:**
  *supports* H1's delayed-recognition signature. It does **not** prove delayed recognition, and it does
  **not** establish causation — uncharted triggers and unmeasured practice remain.
- **If there is no differential (ratio CI includes 1) while both modes are equally predicted, or
  neither:** *adverse to H1 and consistent with R2* (practice fingerprint). It does **not** prove the
  effect is practice rather than a common severity mechanism — it only removes the recognition-mode
  signature H1 requires.
- **If the differential favours triggered:** *adverse to H1* and consistent with practice/detection
  artefacts.
- **If CIs are too wide to separate modes, or hospital variance dominates:** *inconclusive*; an
  imprecise null does not refute H1.

Neither outcome of this computable test can establish causality; the parsimonious mechanistic claim
requires the external evidence named in §8 and §9.

## 4. Frozen design (parent verbatim unless noted)

**Population.** Adult eICU 2.0 first unit stays; age ≥ 16; ICU stay `unitdischargeoffset` ≥ 1440 MINUTES;
decisive `unitdischargestatus`; **rescue-eligible** at the hour-24 landmark = alive at 24 h, no active
EOL order (`carePlanEOL.cpleoldiscussionoffset` ≤ 1440 **or** `cpleolsaveoffset` ≤ 1440), and **not
already on maximal support** (no vasopressor and no invasive-MV treatment string before offset 1440).
Hospitals with ≥ 200 eligible stays retained for multilevel / leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO).

**Join key.** `patientunitstayid`. All `*offset` fields are **MINUTES** from unit admission.
MAP = `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean`. Treatment strings live in `treatment.treatmentstring`
(`treatmentoffset`).

**Landmark = hour 24.** Exposure = within-patient clinical-panel log draw-rate ratio
`logR = log((draws in (24,48]h / 24 + 0.5) / (draws in (0,24]h / 24 + 0.5))`; clinical panel =
`lab.labname ∈ {creatinine, bicarbonate, pH, paO2, FiO2, lactate, WBC, hemoglobin/hematocrit,
platelets, sodium, BUN, glucose}`. Model `logR` continuously and as an RCS(3 df) main effect.

**Trajectory stratum (secondary control).** `not_improving` vs `improving` at hour 24 from
`apacheApsVar` + `vitalPeriodic` (worsening vs stable/improving MAP; rising vs falling lactate/
creatinine across (0,24]). Kept so R1 stays testable.

**Outcome (failure to rescue) — RE-SPECIFIED (the evolution).** Escalation event = first appearance
in (24,48]h of a **vasopressor** treatment string (norepinephrine / phenylephrine / vasopressin /
dopamine / dobutamine / epinephrine) or an **invasive-MV** string, absent before the landmark;
observability rule requires ≥ 6 h further in-unit observation or a documented escalation/death.

Recognition mode is assigned from charting **within the 4 h window preceding onset**,
`[onset_offset − 240, onset_offset)` (minutes):

- **TRIGGERED** = ≥ 1 charted trigger in the window: MAP < 65 (`vitalPeriodic.systemicmean`),
  HR > 110 (`heartrate`), respiration > 22 (`respiration`), SpO2 < 90 (`sao2`), **or** the last
  clinical-panel lab value before onset in a pre-specified abnormal range.
- **UNHERALDED** = same escalation with **no** such preceding charted trigger.

**Primary estimand (new).** The recognition-mode differential Δ = β_unheralded − β_triggered of the
`logR` coefficient, estimated inside a hospital random-intercept model adjusted for hour-24
APACHE-IVa severity (`apachePatientResult.apachescore` / `predictedicumortality`) and the
RCS(`logR`) main effect; trajectory stratum is a **secondary** control, reported as
`logR × trajectory_stratum`.

**Negative control (new, computable).** A **placebo landmark at hour 12**: recompute
`logR12 = log((draws (720,1440]/720 + 0.5)/(draws (0,720]/720 + 0.5))` and the escalation outcome in
(720,1440] with the same observability and recognition-mode logic. Pre-specified expectation: the
hour-12 differential should **not** reproduce the (24,48] signal (its ratio CI includes 1). Excluding
patients already escalated before the landmark removes prevalent-escalation anticipation.

**Baseline vs substantive alternative (method comparison kept intact).**
- *Baseline:* fixed-effect cause-specific/multinomial logistic, hour-24 severity + linear `logR`
  (recognition-mode-stratified) — the simple, interpretable comparator.
- *Alternative:* within-patient inhomogeneous marked point-process **draw model** yielding a
  **physiology-adjusted draw deficit** (expected draws given the hour-24 physiology vs observed).
  Compared to the baseline by ΔAUROC, calibration slope, O:E ratio, Brier score and net benefit, with
  LOHO transport. **Information the alternative reveals that the baseline loses:** whether the signal
  is a within-patient deficit in *expected* draws given physiology (H1) or a between-hospital
  level/practice property (R2). A single fixed-effect linear term on `logR` cannot distinguish a
  physiology-adjusted deficit from a level shift, so this is a substantive alternative, not a small
  predictive gain.

## 6. Falsification criteria (pre-specified, directional)

Let OR_drop = odds ratio per **1-unit LOWER** `logR` (equivalently exp(−β)); subscripts U = unheralded,
T = triggered; differential ratio R = OR_drop,U / OR_drop,T = exp(−Δ).

- **SUPPORTIVE of H1 (delayed recognition):** OR_drop,U > 1 with 95% CI excluding 1 **AND**
  OR_drop,T 95% CI includes 1 **AND** the differential R > 1 with 95% CI excluding 1 **AND** the
  unheralded effect keeps the same sign after (i) the R3 restriction, (ii) within-patient centering
  and hospital random-slope, and (iii) LOHO transport **AND** the hour-12 placebo-landmark differential
  CI includes 1 (negative control passes).
- **ADVERSE to H1 (favours R2 or R1):** OR_drop,U ≤ 1; **OR** OR_drop,T > 1 with CI excluding 1 while
  OR_drop,U CI includes 1 (reverse differential); **OR** differential R CI includes 1 with both modes
  predicted equally (null differential → R2 practice signature); **OR** the unheralded effect
  disappears under within-patient centering / LOHO (between-hospital structure → R2).
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** differential CI spans 1 AND (either stratum CI includes 1 OR the log-scale CI width
  exceeds 1.5 log-odds OR unheralded event count < 100), or hospital-level variance dominates the
  within-patient contrast. An imprecise null does **not** refute H1.

## 8. Honest limits

eICU lacks order-set, staffing and bedside-decision data, so **H1 vs a pure practice mechanism is only
PARTIALLY separable**. Recognition mode is **inferred from charted vitals/labs**, so a true clinical
trigger may be **uncharted** (an "unheralded" case may have had an unrecorded warning) — this biases
toward the null differential, not toward a false H1. Vasopressor strings are bound at the string level
and are **not dose-normalized**. The outcome is a **failure-to-rescue proxy**, not an adjudicated
rescue event. The draw-rate `logR` is a documentation-derived intensity, not a direct staffing/care
measure. **Causal claims require external data and expert review**; a supportive computable result
establishes only the recognition-mode differential pattern consistent with H1, not causation.

## 9. Requires clinical adjudication / unavailable evidence

1. Whether a charted trigger reflects true clinical *recognition* vs a coincidental charted value.
2. Whether an "unheralded" escalation is genuine **delayed recognition** vs an escalation actually
   prompted by an **uncharted** trigger (order-set/staffing records needed).
3. Whether the draw-intensity drop is a **clinical monitoring decision** vs a documentation artefact.
4. Whether an escalation is a **rescue** vs a planned/elective intervention.
5. Dose-normalization of vasopressors (unavailable as a normalized field).

## 10. Method alternatives not chosen (and evidence that would justify revisiting)

- **Marginal structural model / g-computation** for time-varying confounding by indication —
  revisit if the trajectory stratum demonstrably fails to control indication and treatment-confounder
  feedback is shown present.
- **Competing-risks (cause-specific vs subdistribution) hazard model with time-to-escalation** —
  revisit if differential censoring (discharge/death) materially changes the estimated contrasts.
- **Instrumental-variable / between-vs-within decomposition using hospital draw practice as an
  instrument** — only if the exclusion and monotonicity assumptions are defensible (they are not
  assumed here).
- **Joint model of longitudinal draws and escalation timing** — revisit if a single landmark is judged
  too coarse for the recognition-window claim.
