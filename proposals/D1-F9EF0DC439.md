# Exact co-primary pathway validation with a new fixed 7,602-patient confirmation

## Decision question and the repair

The decision remains whether a locked EHR endpoint can reproduce the randomized arm risk differences that would be obtained from prospective pump-native and adjudicated reference data for norepinephrine-first (NE-first) versus vasopressin-first (VP-first) discontinuation. The selected parent made the necessary clinical advance: it retained every randomized first patient and separated assigned implementation, rescue, death, alive ICU exit, and stable completion instead of trading them in an unratified scalar. Its principal unresolved flaw was operating, not conceptual: the inherited 5,920-patient confirmation had exact assurance only for the legacy composite, while the seven-component family divided 0.05 across 28 error probabilities and could be too imprecise to support its simultaneous claim.

This successor preserves the parent's seven all-randomized components and replaces that unsupported multiplicity/sample combination with a prospectively fixed **co-primary intersection-union family** and a newly justified confirmation of exactly **7,602 patients, 3,801 per arm**. Each component is tested with its own exact 95% simultaneous four-probability construction; the single program-level claim is made only if all seven components pass. This does not inflate familywise type-I error: when the global null is the union that at least one component has absolute RD distortion at least 0.025, rejection requires rejection of every component null, so the probability of a false global claim is bounded by the level of any true component test, at most 0.05, without dividing alpha across clinically distinct components. No component can compensate for another, no post hoc component is selected, and no scalar weighting is introduced.

The unchanged legacy composite remains an additional guardrail, not a clinical estimand. Confirmation is supportive only if the legacy guardrail and all seven co-primary component gates pass. Thus the new logic solves simultaneous precision while preserving the clinically separable pathway.

## Supported evidence versus the untested claim

All inherited MIMIC findings are frozen adverse feasibility evidence. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, observed actions were 211 NE-first (3.49%) and 167 VP-first (2.76%), below the frozen 5% positivity gate; the 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort had 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate failures. Of 73 failures in 136 determinate episodes, 59 included a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 were restart-only. MCS status was unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvement (about 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1), below the 0.01 gate, with adverse transport.

The frozen complete transaction audit retained all 137 inherited episodes and all rows after declared stay, item, and time filters, reproduced all endpoint boundaries and all 59 raw restarts, and found that five symmetric reconstructions invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited shared-chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries. These findings establish EHR transaction instability only, not interrupted physical delivery, clinician intent, or rescue.

The strongest supported claim is therefore that the retrospective EHR-side endpoint is computable, sparse, restart-dominated, and sensitive to transaction rules. MIMIC lacks randomization, assignment, protocol permissibility, pump-native delivered-rate truth, an escrowed cross-system clock map, adjudicated rescue intent, and reliable evidence that MCS is absent. The unresolved prospective claim is whether the locked EHR system measures **each** clinically distinct all-randomized component RD, and the legacy-composite RD guardrail, with absolute distortion below 0.025 in the enrolled configuration mixture.

## Population, assignment, clocks, and immutable pathway states

All parent rules are retained except the confirmatory sample size and its exact component testing allocation.

The program includes at least 12 hospitals, at least two adult ICUs per hospital where available, and at least two pump-EHR interface families. Contracted expansion to at most 24 hospitals is allowed only before confirmation. A configuration is `(site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, clock-map method/version)`; changes after first confirmatory enrollment are separately versioned and cannot silently pool.

Each configuration completes a 50-episode technical run-in: at least 95% unique patient-pump-channel-drug linkage and complete pump export from two hours before candidate assignment through closure with an escrowed clock map, and at least 90% contemporaneous material for blinded intent adjudication. One repeat with 50 wholly new episodes is allowed after repair; a second failure excludes the configuration. Run-in patients enter no estimand.

Eligibility is locked before randomization and cannot depend on later implementation, actual first stop, rescue, death, exit, capture, adherence, or agreement: age at least 18; alive in an adult ICU; linked pump-delivered NE and VP concurrently for at least six hours; at the beginning of the final 30 minutes NE greater than 0 and at most 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP greater than 0 and at most 0.04 U/min, neither increased during those 30 minutes; no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the preceding two hours; at least three valid MAPs spanning three of four preceding 30-minute bins including the final bin, median at least 65 and minimum at least 55 mmHg; and a prereandomization treating-team record that either strategy is permissible.

The first eligible randomization per person is the sole primary unit. Immutable randomization time is `tr`; `Z=0` assigns NE-first and `Z=1` VP-first in configuration-stratified permuted blocks. Actual first-stopped drug never replaces assignment. The assigned drug should cease within 120 minutes. Reference qualifying cessation `t0_R` is the first linked pump-native delivered-rate time in `[tr,tr+120 min]` below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP for five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. A programmed but unlinked rate is insufficient. Nonassigned cessation first is crossover; both within five minutes are simultaneous; later assigned cessation cannot erase either deviation.

Implementation is observed to `min(tr+120 min,death,ICU exit)`. After qualifying cessation, follow `(t0_R,min[t0_R+6 h,death,ICU exit]]`; pump export, vital status, and ICU location continue to six hours after earlier rescue so later death or exit is retained. Each source uses a prospectively escrowed affine clock map with offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving rule, fitting rows, residuals, and version. The ambiguity band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds. Uncertainty crossing an ordering, five-minute, 120-minute, or six-hour boundary is indeterminate, never rounded toward agreement.

Every randomized patient has immutable reference and EHR implementation state `I`: `AQ`, `XO`, `SIM`, `NI_OVERRIDE`, `NI_NO_STOP`, `EXIT_PRE`, `DEATH_PRE`, or `IND_I`. `NI_OVERRIDE` requires a prospectively timestamped reason code. For source-specific `AQ`, post-cessation `O` is `RESCUE`, `DEATH_POST`, `EXIT_POST_OFF`, `NO_EVENT_6H`, or `IND_O`; otherwise `O=SNA`. Reference rescue requires return of the assigned drug to threshold for at least five minutes before closure plus two blinded adjudicators classifying it as deliberate treatment or prevention of worsening hypotension/impaired perfusion. Rescue followed by death remains first-disposition rescue and also records subsequent death.

The corresponding mutually exclusive `C` state is `IMPLEMENTATION_DEVIATION`, `DEATH_PRE`, `EXIT_PRE`, `RESCUE_AFTER_AQ`, `DEATH_POST_AQ`, `EXIT_POST_AQ_OFF`, `STABLE_OFF_6H`, or `INDETERMINATE`. This is a partition, not an ordinal outcome. Alive exit is not silently declared success or failure, and rescue, death, exit, implementation, and stable completion are never exchanged.

## Seven co-primary all-randomized components

For arm `z` and component `k`, `p_zk=P(H_Rk=1|Z=z)` uses every randomized patient in the arm denominator:

1. `H_impl=1{I_R=AQ}`;
2. `H_rescue=1{C_R=RESCUE_AFTER_AQ}`;
3. `H_death_pre=1{C_R=DEATH_PRE}`;
4. `H_death_post=1{C_R=DEATH_POST_AQ}`;
5. `H_exit_pre=1{C_R=EXIT_PRE}`;
6. `H_exit_post=1{C_R=EXIT_POST_AQ_OFF}`;
7. `H_stable=1{C_R=STABLE_OFF_6H}`.

The clinical result is the vector `Delta_R,k=p_1k-p_0k`, with exact/randomization-compatible intervals, arm-specific pathway distributions, and separate subsequent-death and subsequent-exit flags through six hours. Conditional rescue among `I_R=AQ` is a selected post-randomization diagnostic only and must be displayed beside the all-randomized implementation rate. There is no primary scalar treatment ranking.

The unchanged legacy guardrail maps `Y_R=1` for `I_R in {XO,SIM,NI_OVERRIDE,NI_NO_STOP,EXIT_PRE,DEATH_PRE}` or `I_R=AQ,O_R in {RESCUE,DEATH_POST}`; `Y_R=0` for `I_R=AQ,O_R in {NO_EVENT_6H,EXIT_POST_OFF}`; otherwise indeterminate, with identical EHR mapping. It requires clinical panel ratification before launch and cannot establish exchangeability or treatment benefit.

## Paired distortion identity and the exact co-primary family

For each component and arm, over all randomized patients, define `f_zk=P(H_Ek=1,H_Rk=0|Z=z)` and `g_zk=P(H_Ek=0,H_Rk=1|Z=z)`. Then `d_zk=q_zk-p_zk=f_zk-g_zk` and the EHR-versus-reference RD distortion is

`b_k=(Delta_E,k-Delta_R,k)=f_1k-g_1k-f_0k+g_0k`.

For each component separately, construct four two-sided Clopper-Pearson intervals, one for each of `f_1k,g_1k,f_0k,g_0k`, at interval `alpha=0.0125`. Their Cartesian product has at least 95% simultaneous Bonferroni coverage for that component. Exact conservative endpoints are

`b_Lk=L(f_1k)-U(g_1k)-U(f_0k)+L(g_0k)` and

`b_Uk=U(f_1k)-L(g_1k)-L(f_0k)+U(g_0k)`;

`B_Uk=max(|b_Lk|,|b_Uk|)`. Component null `H0k: |b_k|>=0.025` is rejected only when `B_Uk<0.025`; equality fails.

The confirmatory family is prospectively fixed as a co-primary intersection-union test:

`H0_global = union over k H0k` versus `H1_global = intersection over k {|b_k|<0.025}`.

A global separable measurement claim requires rejection of all seven component nulls. If any `H0k` is true, false rejection of the global null implies false rejection of that true component null, whose probability is at most 0.05. Therefore the global type-I error is at most 0.05 under arbitrary dependence among components. Alpha is not divided across components because this is not a claim that any selected component succeeds; all seven must succeed. Component-specific claims may be reported only with their exact 95% construction and prespecified status, never promoted post hoc to the global claim.

The legacy `Y` guardrail retains four two-sided Clopper-Pearson intervals at `alpha=0.0125`, the same exact linear bound, and strict `B_U<0.025`. It is an eighth required gate, but not part of the seven-component clinical vector and cannot rescue a component failure.

## Exact fixed-sample operating characteristics

Confirmation enrolls exactly **7,602 new first randomized patients, 3,801 per arm**, under one common start and database lock, or closes unsuccessfully at 60 months. There is no outcome-, component-, arm-, accuracy-, adherence-, denominator-, or event-dependent stopping; no sample-size re-estimation; and no post-lock extension.

The fixed-N calculation is exact and conservative. The planning alternative is `f=g=0.005` for all 32 required arm-endpoint error probabilities: 28 for the seven components and four for the legacy guardrail. To provide dependence-robust assurance of at least 0.90 that all eight required exact bounds are below 0.025, allocate the 0.10 assurance-failure budget across 64 binomial tails: `0.10/64=0.0015625` per tail. At `n=3,801`, the exact count envelope is 8 through 33 for every one of the 32 counts: lower-tail probability `P(X<=7)=0.0014788317926379508`, upper-tail probability `P(X>=34)=0.0011763255720924528`. Each count lies in the envelope with probability 0.9973448426352696, so the union-bound assurance across all 32, requiring no independence, is 0.9150349643286262. At the worst compatible component combination, the exact bound is `2[U_CP(33;3801)-L_CP(8;3801)]=0.024997364191007024`, strictly below 0.025.

This `N` is the smallest integer per-arm sample found by exhaustive enumeration from 1,000 through 20,000 under the fixed 32-count assurance rule. At `n=3,800`, the same 8-through-33 envelope gives a worst component bound of 0.025003926365504702, which fails despite assurance above 0.90. At the inherited `n=2,960`, the analogous family envelope is 5 through 27 and the worst component bound is 0.028170663608459844, explicitly demonstrating the parent's unresolved precision defect. These values are planning operating characteristics, not observed evidence or acceptance counts; confirmation always uses actual exact intervals.

For the unchanged legacy planning construction at `n=3,801`, its four-count envelope under the inherited legacy assurance allocation is 10 through 29 and its worst exact bound is 0.021819501849321583; thus the component-driven sample also supports the guardrail. The frozen calculation is in `component_iut_design.py` and `component_iut_design.json`.

Confirmation requires 126.7 patients/month over 60 months. Before launch, signed contracts must project at least 135/month and the final three pilot months must average at least 100/month; the lower pilot accrual gate alone is not proof of confirmatory feasibility. Failure to reach 7,602 by 60 months is inconclusive, not a smaller completed study.

## Indeterminacy, implementation, and adverse rules

Every patient remains in paired `C_E x C_R` tables including `INDETERMINATE`. Component and legacy 3x3 tables are completed jointly: allocate unknown EHR/reference labels as nonnegative integer counts compatible with observed aggregate cells; for every feasible joint completion compute all `f`, `g`, intervals, and bounds. Report the maximum legacy `B_U` and every maximum `B_Uk` plus a single compatible integer witness. Metric-specific favorable imputations cannot be combined. Aggregate sparse convolution/branch-and-bound is required, with person-level enumeration only as an oracle when at most eight labels are unknown. Timeout, absent witness, disagreement with exhaustive tiny cases, noninteger or marginally incompatible imputations, or failure to optimize the joint global pass is inconclusive and cannot validate.

Indeterminacy above 2% overall, above 3% in an arm-by-configuration cell with at least 50 patients, or an arm difference over two percentage points is operationally adverse even if measurement bounds pass. Missing verification of EHR negatives or coding lost capture as no event invalidates the analysis.

Reference implementation is strategy performance, not measurement error. Report by assignment every `I_R` and `C_R` category, time to first attempt, actual first-stopped drug, reason, `t0_R`, subsequent death/exit, and all component RDs. Assigned cessation below 60% in either arm, crossover/simultaneous cessation above 15%, arm difference in assigned cessation above 10 percentage points, or safety-board concern is adverse clinical/implementation evidence but is measurement error only when EHR and reference disagree.

Even if all exact bounds pass, the inherited cancellation diagnostics remain adverse: a component with at least 100 reference events per arm and sensitivity below 0.80; any state contribution with absolute arm-differential error above 0.010; sign reversal of estimated legacy distortion after removing a component with more than 20 discordances; or pilot absolute arm-differential error at least 0.025 in rescue, death, or exit. Pooled accuracy, PPV, NPV, or composite agreement never substitutes for a co-primary component gate.

## Transport, recurrence, and interpretation

Later eligible episodes never enlarge the primary first-patient denominator. Recurrence requires a new six-hour dual-drug baseline and starts at least 24 hours after prior `t0` (or randomization if no `t0`); it retains original assignment absent separately approved rerandomization before a new lock. Recurrent vectors/errors use assignment/configuration-stratified patient-cluster bootstrap and diagnostic marginal GEE only.

No hospital, configuration, or interface family supplies more than 20% of either arm. At least eight hospitals and both families each contribute at least 100 per arm. Report configuration transitions, hierarchical multinomial summaries, prediction intervals, and leave-one-hospital/family-out exact legacy and component bounds. Every leave-out mixture must pass legacy and all seven co-primary bounds; a configuration with at least 500 per arm must have legacy `B_U<0.05`; sparse configurations are inconclusive. Material assignment-by-configuration interaction or adjusted state-error range above 0.10 is adverse. Support is restricted to the locked mixture and rules.

**Supportive:** all 7,602 confirmation patients are retained; the worst joint indeterminate completion has legacy `B_U<0.025` and every co-primary `B_Uk<0.025`; implementation, cancellation, determinacy, adjudication, clocks, configuration concentration, recurrence, and leave-out gates pass. This supports only RD measurement validity for every specified component and the guardrail in the enrolled mixture. It does not establish strategy benefit, safety, efficacy, noninferiority, or component exchangeability.

**Adverse:** completed confirmation has legacy `B_U>=0.025` or any component `B_Uk>=0.025`; the global separable claim fails, with each component status reported. Arm-differential rescue/death/exit error, cancellation, or harmful heterogeneity is also adverse measurement evidence. Separately, poor assigned implementation, excessive crossover/simultaneous stopping, imbalance, more rescue, more death, delayed exit, or safety-board concern is adverse clinical/strategy evidence, not measurement error unless paired labels disagree. A component may have a supportive component-specific result while the global claim remains adverse, but no subset is promoted as the endpoint-validation conclusion.

**Inconclusive:** governance does not ratify definitions/guardrail; randomization is impermissible; run-in, pump linkage, clocks, intent adjudication, MCS ascertainment, determinacy, exact optimization, or contracts fail; 7,602 are not completed by 60 months; or population, pathway, death/exit handling, configuration mixture, modalities, or clocks change. Completed confirmation with a required bound at or above 0.025 is adverse, not relabeled as merely underpowered.

The verifier must reproduce all eligibility/state/full-denominator rules, paired identities, integer-compatible joint completions and witnesses, each component's four `alpha=0.0125` exact intervals, intersection-union logic, legacy gate, pilot exclusion, common lock, and the exact `n=3,801` versus `3,800` arithmetic. Adversarial cases must show that success in six components cannot rescue the seventh; less rescue with more death cannot become benefit; early alive exit remains separate; rescue followed by death preserves both; crossover remains crossover; perfect agreement with poor implementation can pass measurement yet remain clinically adverse; favorable metric-specific imputations cannot form a joint pass; and no valid measurement output can be described as treatment benefit.

Computation can establish row accounting, state derivation, exact bounds, fixed-N arithmetic, multiplicity logic, and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish clinical permissibility, physical pump truth from EHR, rescue intent without experts, MCS absence, safety/equipoise, transport to changed systems, or which stopping strategy should be used. Those require the missing prospective modalities, clinical governance, safety review, and a separately powered randomized clinical-effectiveness study.
