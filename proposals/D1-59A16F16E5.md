# Episode 16: boundary-free acidemic PaO2 rank shift, and a stopping rule for EICU refinement

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and advance

In invasively ventilated adults with concurrent acidemia, does a released monitor SpO2 of 94–95%, rather than 96–97%, identify a materially downward-shifted PaO2 distribution after comparing observations in the same outcome-blind hospital/FiO2/PaCO2/pH/ICU-day cells?

This is a concurrent mapping question, not a target-assignment, treatment-effect, calibration, safety, harm, or benefit question. The episode-15 parent already falsified the proposed +10-point relocation into PaO2 70–89: (M_{70}=-11.866) points (95% interval −35.168 to +6.859), while (L_{70}=+29.027) points (+2.697 to +45.321) established more <70 mapping in the lower band but did not resolve its magnitude against a +10-point cap. The 70-mm Hg categorical negative stands and cannot be rescued here.

What remained unresolved was whether that result reflected a boundary-independent downward ordering of PaO2 or merely a favorable/unfavorable placement of category edges. The successor removes every PaO2 outcome cutpoint from the primary estimand. It uses the same 556-row source construction and frozen 199-row measured-overlap population. A separate design lock, written before inspecting the rank result, fixed a 0.60 probability-of-superiority anchor and a −5 mm Hg scale-consistency anchor. Thus neither the estimand nor its margins were chosen to fit the resulting point estimate.

The clinical value is discriminating two next steps. A robust, materially downward distributional shift would justify prospective synchronized validation of continuous SpO2–PaO2 mapping in acidemia. An adverse or materially inconclusive result means that no further PaO2 boundary search in this EICU snapshot is warranted. It still cannot choose an oxygen target. Nguyen et al. (Critical Care Medicine 2026; DOI 10.1097/CCM.0000000000007031; PMCID PMC13134654) found similar overall 90-day mortality across heterogeneous conservative and liberal oxygen strategies in nine RCTs/20,447 ventilated adults; this leaves condition-specific targeting unresolved. Delgado et al. (Antioxidants 2026; DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712) reported pH- and FiO2-dependent SpO2–PaO2 mapping in a large single-center retrospective cohort, supporting physiologic plausibility but not this contrast or a treatment claim. Hohmann et al. (Respiration 2025; DOI 10.1159/000549732; PMCID PMC12885501) provides the inherited weak, very-low-certainty 92–96%/70–90 mm Hg guidance; it is context only and supplies no boundary to the new primary.

### Audited alternatives rejected

A pH-interaction successor was audited by extending the otherwise identical extraction to pH 7.35–7.45 and requiring four-way support for acidemia/normal-pH by lower/higher SpO2. Its nominal primary construction retained only 257 rows in 20 cells across five hospitals; group ESS ranged from 26.76 to 54.80, and reasonable PaCO2/cell variants left only four to five hospitals with minima near 19–32. It would change the population and estimand while remaining sensitive to a <70 category or truncation choice, so it is not an appropriate repair of the parent.

A within-person switching analysis was also audited. Only 14 of the 171 overlap people had both bands (31 rows); only eight nearest pairs were within six hours. That cannot sustain clustered uncertainty or distinguish mapping from time-varying FiO2/illness changes. Neither rejected branch contributes to the selected result.

### Evidence-supported claim and hypothesis tested

The strongest evidence already supports that the exact EICU extraction is computable, that the parent’s favorable 60–89 result was boundary-sensitive, and that its clinically anchored 70–89 relocation claim was adverse. It does not support an oxygen target or causal safety claim.

For each retained overlap cell (s), let (n_{Ls}) and (n_{Hs}) be lower- and higher-band counts and (m_s=n_{Ls}n_{Hs}/(n_{Ls}+n_{Hs})). Select a cell with probability proportional to (m_s), then independently select one lower-band and one higher-band observation uniformly within that cell. The primary estimand is θ = P(PaO2_L < PaO2_H) + 0.5 × P(PaO2_L = PaO2_H).

The falsifiable materiality hypothesis is (θ >= 0.60). The natural no-ordering value is 0.50. The prespecified scale diagnostic is the overlap-mass-weighted Hodges–Lehmann median of all within-cell differences (PaO2_L-PaO2_H), with consistency anchor <=−5 mm Hg. The 0.60 and −5 anchors are transparent decision anchors, not MCIDs, safety thresholds, or validated clinical utility values.

## Analysis, baselines, uncertainty, and gates

Within each cell, enumerate all lower-by-higher PaO2 pairs. Give every pair weight (m_s/(n_{Ls}n_{Hs})=1/(n_{Ls}+n_{Hs})). Report (θ), tie probability, total pairs, common mass, and weighted 25th/50th/75th percentiles of (PaO2_L-PaO2_H). Pair enumeration is an estimand construction, not 209 independent observations; uncertainty remains clustered.

Fixed baselines are the parent’s (L_{70}), (M_{70}), and (U_{90}) category contrasts, plus the inherited overlap-weighted raw-mean PaO2 difference (−5.099 mm Hg) and (E[min(PaO2,120)]) difference (−9.499 mm Hg). These show consistency or inconsistency with prior summaries but cannot rescue the primary. No alternate PaO2 boundary is permitted.

Use 5,000 deterministic hierarchical bootstrap replicates, seed 20260916, conditional on the frozen 199-row overlap source:

1. sample its 11 hospitals with replacement, creating 11 distinct hospital-copy IDs;
2. within each copy, sample that original hospital’s number of `uniquepid` clusters with replacement and carry all their rows;
3. rebuild copied cells using hospital-copy ID, dropping cells that no longer contain both bands;
4. recompute pair weights, (θ), and the Hodges–Lehmann median.

Report defined/failed replicates and separate percentile 95% intervals. Pooling duplicate hospital or person copies under original IDs is an error.

Support requires all of: <=10% failed replicates; inherited band ESS >=50; no original hospital >50% of total overlap weight; (θ)’s lower interval bound >=0.60; the median-difference upper bound <=−5 mm Hg; every mandatory outcome-blind sensitivity has (θ>0.50) and median difference <0; and every deletion of a site with >=10 rows preserves those directions. The two magnitude components are joint and cannot rescue one another.

Mandatory rebuilt sensitivities are earliest row/person, earliest row/stay, first 24 hours, complete PaCO2, 12-hour time cells, five-point FiO2/ten-mm Hg PaCO2 cells, no pH cells, 0.10-pH cells, 0.05-pH cells, and each-site deletion. Retain the parent’s six extraction diagnostics—last rather than median SpO2, 60-minute FiO2 lookback, chart-offset FiO2 without entry restriction, closed nonzero ventilation intervals, alternate FiO2 labels, and broader airway proxy—as required compiler analyses; each rebuilds eligibility and overlap before estimating rank shift. The current reference run verifies the primary, nine outcome-blind rebuilds, and all site deletions, not the six extraction variants.

## Full-source result and stopping conclusion

Full-source reconstruction reproduced 556/199 rows and all inherited weights. The 55 cells create 209 distinct lower-by-higher row pairs under the defined enumeration (not an effective sample size). The primary estimate is (θ=0.6974), tie probability 0.01965, with 95% interval 0.5278–0.8243. The Hodges–Lehmann median difference is −10 mm Hg, interquartile pair-difference range −24 to +4, with 95% interval −18 to −1. All 5,000 replicates were defined.

All nine outcome-blind sensitivity points retained downward ordering: (θ=0.6639) to 0.7387 and median difference −7 to −11 mm Hg. Every site deletion retained it: (θ=0.6653) to 0.7092 and median difference −6 to −12 mm Hg. These are same-snapshot robustness checks, not independent validation.

The result supports a boundary-free directional statement: within the measured overlap cells, lower-band observations were more likely to have lower concurrent PaO2, and both intervals exclude their no-direction values (0.50 and 0). It is **inconclusive for prespecified materiality**, because the (θ) lower bound is below 0.60 and the median-difference upper bound is above −5 mm Hg. It does not support a target, safety claim, treatment effect, device calibration claim, or patient outcome effect.

This is the stopping result for this snapshot. The parent’s adverse (M_{70}) result stands. The directional rank finding is not permission to choose another PaO2 cutpoint, relax a margin, or call 94–95 unsafe. Further within-EICU boundary refinement would reuse the same selected data without resolving specimen identity, waveform timing, delivered FiO2, ABG selection, or clinical outcome relevance. The justified next study is prospective synchronized validation; otherwise the lineage should stop.
