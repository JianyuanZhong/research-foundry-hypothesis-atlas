# Can preoperative M2 triage survive pathology-template drift? A verification-aware, partially identified validation

## Clinical decision, supported evidence, and unresolved claim

The decision is whether to use a preoperative risk flag to send an adult with apparently resectable hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) for multidisciplinary review because high-grade microvascular invasion (M2) may be present. The flag is not a recommendation for wider resection, neoadjuvant treatment, or adjuvant treatment.

**Strongest supported claim.** Inspected literature supports that M2 is a pathology-defined high-risk phenotype associated with worse recurrence outcomes and that preoperative models have weak external validation. The inspected Chen et al. full text defines the Chinese three-tier system by number and distance of invaded microvessels and warns that inadequate non-tumoral parenchymal sampling can underestimate grade (DOI 10.3350/cmh.2025.0167; frozen full-text XML source `[source checksum]`). These are literature claims, not HCC-dataset model findings.

The selected parent hypothesizes that routine preoperative reports and laboratories add M2 triage value beyond a compact tumor-burden/liver-reserve baseline. A new aggregate source audit exposes a decisive unresolved problem: many pathology documents contain an immediate selected grade followed by boilerplate definitions of all three grades. Thus an anywhere-in-document occurrence such as `M2（高危组）` can be a definition inside an M0 report. Under a transparent broad resection audit, immediate values after `MVI提示风险分级：` were common in 2015–2021 but nearly absent in 2022–2023 and returned mostly as M0 in 2024–2025. The inherited count of 2,482 lexical M2 records is therefore feasibility evidence only and cannot be treated as 2,482 true selected M2 outcomes.

**Unresolved claim.** After position-aware pathology parsing, clinician adjudication, and worst-case accounting for patients whose M2 status is not recoverable, do report-derived features plus routine laboratories still add clinically material net benefit over the same structured baseline in an independent temporal test?

**Primary hypothesis.** In the 2020–2021 target-frame test cohort, the report-plus-lab extension (E) will exceed the structured baseline (B) by at least 0.01 net-benefit units at the fixed threshold (p_t=0.30), and the patient-bootstrap lower 95% confidence bound for the **worst-case partial-identification lower bound** of (Delta NB_{0.30}) will exceed zero. This means the incremental utility remains positive even if every ungradable test outcome is assigned adversarially. A separate contemporary-transport claim for 2022–2025 is not identifiable from this snapshot unless adequate three-tier outcomes can be clinically recovered; failure to recover them prohibits a current-use claim rather than being relabeled as poor model performance.

The substantive advance over the parent is not a more complex predictor. It repairs outcome-definition contamination, moves the locked test to a period with demonstrable three-tier ascertainment, includes ungradable patients in the estimand denominator, and makes the effect of missing reference outcomes exactly computable rather than assuming complete-case transportability.

## Population, clocks, and frozen temporal design

Unit: one patient (`患者主索引`), one first eligible index resection.

1. Start with adults age >=18. From `procedures`, identify the patient's first valid-`开始时间` partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resection using a clinically reviewed dictionary of exact `手术` strings. Emit all included/excluded strings and frequencies before modeling. Exclude transplant, biopsy, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and repeat/recurrence resection.
2. Require same-encounter pathology that explicitly identifies HCC. The **target frame does not require an MVI grade**. This is essential: grade availability is an observed post-resection variable, not an eligibility condition.
3. Define (t_0=) index `procedures.开始时间 - 24 hours`. Require at least one liver CT or MRI acquisition in ([t_0-90	ext{ days},t_0)). Eligible labs are in ([t_0-30	ext{ days},t_0)). Predictors at or after (t_0) are forbidden.
4. The primary untreated cohort excludes HCC-directed resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during ([t_0-365	ext{ days},t_0)). Previously treated patients form a sensitivity cohort with treatment-history indicators.
5. Development: index years 2015–2018. Tuning and logistic recalibration: 2019 only. Locked target-frame test: 2020–2021. Years 2013–2014 are excluded because the audit found almost no selected three-tier labels. Years 2022 through snapshot end 2025-11-11 are a **contemporary drift/ascertainment audit only**: frozen models may generate predictions, but no discrimination, calibration, or net-benefit claim may use those years without adjudicated M2 outcomes.
6. A patient and all encounters belong to one period only. Development-only preprocessing, vocabulary, imputation, winsorization, and scaling are frozen before 2019 tuning; models, threshold, dictionaries, and adjudication rules are frozen before test access.

The acquisition timestamp is not report-finalization time. Code can verify that acquisition predates (t_0), but only chart audit can establish that report content was available for the actual clinical decision.

## Reference outcome and required adjudication

The primary outcome is (Y=1) for pathology-defined M2 and (Y=0) for M0 or M1. Define (V=1) when a trustworthy grade is recoverable and (V=0) otherwise.

The parser may assign a grade only from:

- the immediate selected value after an anchored field such as `MVI提示风险分级[:：]`, stopping before explanatory bullets or later definitions; or
- a prespecified synonymous selected-value construction approved during blinded adjudication.

An unanchored `M2（高危组）`, binary `微脉管侵犯：有/无`, positive-ungraded MVI, blank vessel-count/distance fields, and absence of MVI language cannot by themselves assign M2, M1, or M0. Multiple discordant selected fields are conflicts. Pathology is never a predictor.

Before fitting, two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and model output, independently review:

- every parser-assigned M2 in all periods;
- every conflict;
- random samples of at least 150 parser M0 and 150 parser M1, stratified by year/template;
- every (V=0) patient in the 2020–2021 locked target frame; and
- at least 100 (V=0) records per year in 2022–2025 to measure contemporary recoverability.

A third reviewer resolves disagreement. Require class-specific PPV >=0.98 and sensitivity >=0.95, kappa >=0.90, and documented reasons for ungradability. If narrative number/location fields allow a grade, reviewers apply the prespecified M0/M1/M2 definitions; if sampling or text is insufficient, (V) remains 0. Raw slides and the pathology sampling protocol are unavailable, so even reviewer agreement cannot establish that tissue sampling captured the true grade.

Two qualified reviewers also audit 200 imaging reports for tumor diameter/count/macrovascular thrombus and 100 reports per period for feature timestamp/availability. The inherited agreement gates remain: size within 5 mm or 10% in >=90%, count-category agreement >=90% with kappa >=0.80, and each added binary feature PPV and sensitivity >=0.85.

## Models and fixed operating rule

Fit the same nested elastic-net logistic models under identical development/tuning procedures.

**Structured baseline B**

- age (`encounters.年龄`) and sex (`性别`);
- from the latest eligible CT and MRI: maximum lesion diameter, tumor count (1, 2, >=3), modality (CT only, MRI only, both), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown indicators;
- last exact-assay albumin (`白蛋白`), total bilirubin (`总胆红素`), INR (`国际标准化比值`), and platelet count (`血小板计数`) before (t_0), continuous plus missing indicators.

**Extension E**

All B variables plus rule-extracted capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, and enhancement-pattern indicators; development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF from `检查所见` and `检查诊断`; and last exact-assay AFP, ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, and creatinine with missing indicators.

Strip identifiers, dates, accession numbers, specimen/pathology language, explicit MVI terms, and copied pathology segments. An examination labeled pathology/specimen is ineligible. Audit the 100 largest-magnitude extension n-grams before unblinding. A postoperative or outcome proxy invalidates the run.

The flag is fixed at predicted M2 risk >=0.30. With (w=p_t/(1-p_t)=3/7),
[
NB=TP/N-w(FP/N).
]
The threshold is an evaluative false-positive:false-negative tradeoff; its clinical acceptability still requires stakeholder elicitation.

## Primary estimand: utility bounds over the whole target frame

Both models must predict every 2020–2021 target-frame patient, including (V=0). Let (a_E,a_B) be their binary flags. The primary denominator (N) is the entire target frame, not only graded patients.

For (V=1), compute the observed per-patient contribution to (NB_E-NB_B). For (V=0), use exact outcome-free bounds:

| flags | contribution if M2 | contribution if not M2 | lower/upper contribution before division by N |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| E=0, B=0 or E=1, B=1 | 0 | 0 | [0, 0] |
| E=1, B=0 | +1 | (-w) | [(-w), +1] |
| E=0, B=1 | -1 | (+w) | [-1, (+w)] |

Sum observed contributions and the corresponding lower or upper value for every (V=0), then divide by (N). This yields ([L_{Delta NB},U_{Delta NB}]) without missing-at-random assumptions. Use 2,000 patient-level bootstrap samples of the entire target frame to obtain uncertainty for both bounds. Apply analogous bounds to E versus flag-all and flag-none.

Secondary, explicitly selection-sensitive analyses among (V=1) report paired delta-AUPRC, AUROC, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, and decision curves from 0.20–0.40. Report grade recoverability (P(V=1)) and model it using only pre-(t_0) B variables plus year/template; report standardized differences between (V=1) and (V=0), but do not use inverse-probability weighting as if it recovered unknown outcomes. Report results by 2020/2021, sex, age <65/>=65, modality, AFP missingness, and M0 versus M1 controls.

## Feasibility, gates, and falsification

The aggregate audit scanned 338,040 procedure and 46,395 pathology rows. Its broad first-resection rule yielded 25,613 patients; immediate selected-value parsing yielded 75 M2 in 2020 and 24 in 2021 before final HCC/imaging/treatment exclusions. These are actual preliminary counts, not final attrition. They make precision a genuine risk.

A test is interpretable only if:

- final 2020–2021 target frame has >=1,000 patients, >=75 adjudicated M2, and (V=1) for >=85%; every (V=0) record is reviewed;
- parser and imaging adjudication gates pass;
- both models predict all target-frame rows and no forbidden feature or test-informed preprocessing is present;
- report text is nonempty in >=80%, structured size/count are jointly extractable in >=70%, each B lab in >=70%, and no required variable changes missingness by >15 percentage points across development/test;
- test-set vocabulary coverage and continuous-feature support pass the parent's prespecified overlap audit; and
- among (V=1), E calibration slope is 0.80–1.20, calibration intercept is -0.10–0.10, sensitivity >=0.70, and PPV >=0.50, with uncertainty reported.

**Supportive historical result:** all gates pass; (L_{Delta NB}ge0.01); the bootstrap lower 95% bound for (L_{Delta NB}) is >0; lower-bound E net benefit exceeds flag-all and flag-none; and no individual test year has an observed complete-case (Delta NB<0). The strongest conclusion is that timestamp-eligible reports/labs add M2 triage utility beyond B in the 2020–2021 institutional target frame despite adversarial assignment of ungradable outcomes.

**Adverse historical result:** all gates pass but the bootstrap upper 95% bound for (U_{Delta NB}) is <0.01, or E's upper-bound net benefit cannot exceed B or a default strategy. This falsifies clinically material incremental utility in the tested era. A favorable AUPRC cannot rescue it.

**Inconclusive historical result:** gates fail; fewer than 75 M2 remain; adjudication cannot recover enough grades; or the partial-identification interval/uncertainty spans material benefit and no benefit. This is not evidence of no effect.

**Contemporary interpretation:** report the 2022–2025 target-frame (V) rate, selected-value distributions, predictor drift, and flag rates by year. If >=85% can be assigned M0/M1/M2 by the prespecified clinical review and >=75 M2 exist, repeat the frozen bounded analysis as a secondary temporal transport test. Otherwise current-era M2 utility is **not identifiable from this snapshot** and clinical use is unsupported. This is an ascertainment failure, not an adverse performance result. A prospective registry with report-finalization time, standardized pathology sampling, and complete three-tier outcomes is then required.

Even a supportive historical and contemporary result cannot show that changing treatment improves recurrence, survival, quality of life, harms, or cost. Raw images, slides, sampling protocol, pathologist/radiologist identity, actual decision time, tumor-board decisions, reliable complete follow-up, death registry, patient-reported outcomes, and costs are unavailable.

## Harbor compiler and verifier contract

The compiler must preserve the target frame (including (V=0)), 2015–2018/2019/2020–2021 temporal boundaries, (t_0), outcome/parser rules, nested B/E models, (p_t=0.30), whole-frame denominator, exact lower/upper bound formulas, gates, and falsification zones. Changing any is a scientific child, not a mechanical repair.

Computationally checkable claims include source paths/hashes/columns; operation dictionary; composite-key joins; one-patient-one-index; attrition; patient-disjoint periods; anchored selected-value parsing; a unit test in which selected M0 followed by an explanatory M2 definition remains M0; (V) counts; pre-(t_0) feature timestamps; absence of pathology/postoperative features; identical prediction rows; frozen preprocessing/models; bound arithmetic; bootstrap intervals; secondary metrics; drift tables; and links from every numeric conclusion to machine-readable outputs.

Adversarial verification must reject: correct metrics followed by a treatment/survival claim; a complete-case supportive claim when worst-case bounds fail; an M2 label based only on an unanchored boilerplate definition; calling current-era non-identifiability adverse model performance; cherry-picking a threshold after (p_t=0.30) fails; and calling imprecision negative. It must accept appropriate bounded interpretations of supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outputs.

Not automatically checkable are Chinese clinical semantics, adequacy of tissue sampling, true report availability at the decision, acceptability of the 0.30 tradeoff, or benefit of model-guided care. These require qualified adjudication, missing pathology evidence, stakeholder review, or another study.
