## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats serum AFP,
albumin and bilirubin. The open question: does the *within-patient AFP trajectory* add prognostic
information beyond **baseline AFP** and beyond **liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics measured in the SAME
draws**, for time to radiologic progression?

Two independent threats to this question remain only half-fixed by either parent:
1. **Endpoint misattribution** (parent 1448fca4's contribution): a progression flag pooled across all
   `examinations` reports mixes non-hepatic studies and is dominated by `转移` (metastasis, 20,762)
   over local descriptors (`增大` 9,108 / `复发` 4,026 / `进展` 2,921), so the AFP contrast can be
   attenuated or misattributed. Fix: restrict primary studies to abdominal liver-directed cross-sectional
   imaging and model **local progression vs metastasis as competing events**.
2. **Informative observation / lead-time** (parent 9a1e0273's contribution): imaging is not scheduled at
   random, so a rising AFP slope can stochastically precede a report merely by triggering the scan. Fix:
   a **detection-timing stratum** (E_early = event at the first post-landmark study; E_late = event only
   at a study >=90 d after t*) plus a **dual-model requirement** (landmark Cox AND time-dependent Cox).

Neither parent fixes both. The crossover carries both, which yields a result neither parent can produce.

**Clinical importance:** if AFP kinetics carry *local* progression information (independent of same-draw
liver reserve) and survive the late-detection stratum, that supports AFP-informed re-imaging / treatment
escalation timing. If the signal appears only on a metastasis-inclusive composite, AFP is tracking
systemic spread and the imaging/monitoring policy differs. A clean null (liver reserve alone) argues for
de-emphasising AFP-only trajectory monitoring. These are distinct decisions.

## 3. Added by this child (pre-specified, one coherent combination)

1. Primary studies = examinations whose `检查` matches an abdominal liver cross-sectional include-list
   (`上腹|肝|腹部` AND modality CT/MRI/增强/DWI, EXCLUDING `胸部`,`正位`, plain-film, endoscopy and
   ultrasound-only rooms) discovered at run time by explicit regex over `检查`.
2. Primary event = first post-t* restricted study whose `检查诊断` contains a local descriptor
   {`增大`,`进展`,`复发`,`新发`,`增多`}; a separately modelled **competing event** = first `转移`
   descriptor. A pooled composite is kept only as a pre-declared sensitivity, never primary.
3. Detection-timing stratum on the restricted primary event: E_early (first post-t* study) vs E_late
   (>=90 d after t*); supportive requires the AFP-slope hazard to hold for E_late.
4. Dual-model robustness: any supportive claim must hold in BOTH the landmark Cox and a time-dependent
   Cox (mirroring the disagreement [K3] shows is possible).
5. Modality-stratified robustness: supportive claim must hold within the MRI subset and the CT subset
   separately, or the instability is reported as adverse.

## 5. Method comparison (recorded before selection)

- **M0 baseline** (landmark Cox): baseline AFP + ALBI grade + age/sex + treatment type + draw intensity.
- **M4 learned/mechanistic alternative** (joint mixed-effects AFP and ALBI slopes entered into the Cox):
  reveals change-vs-level and AFP-vs-same-draw-liver-reserve separation that M0 cannot.
  A full shared-parameter joint model and a Fine-Gray competing-risks model are recorded as **DEFERRED**;
  the evidence that would justify revisiting them is a non-trivial cause-specific vs subdistribution
  divergence or a strong within-patient AFP/ALBI correlation that destabilises M4.
- Approx budget: M0+M4 on ~38k patients, CPU-feasible within the solver envelope (no GPU required for
  the fitting; the discovery episode computed no outcomes). Exact available inputs, split (temporal
  hold-out by index year for the concordance floor), outcome evaluation (HR CI + held-out delta-C-index
  bootstrap CI) as in the parents.

## 6. Falsification (conjunctive, with regime interpretation)

- **Supportive:** local-progression AFP-slope HR (per 1 log10 unit/180 d) CI excludes 1, survives E_late,
  survives the time-dependent Cox, meets the concordance floor, and is consistent across MRI/CT strata.
- **Adverse:** signal only for E_early, only for the metastasis competing event, or attenuated to the null
  in the time-dependent Cox -> the reading is scan-triggering or systemic spread, not local tumour activity.
- **Null (equivalence):** both slope HR CIs include 1 AND held-out delta-C-index bootstrap CI within
  +/-0.01 of M0 -> AFP kinetics add no prognostic information beyond same-draw liver reserve.
- **Inconclusive:** reduced event counts after modality restriction/modality imbalance -> pre-declared
  fallback to the pooled composite reported as a sensitivity, flagged as lower validity.
