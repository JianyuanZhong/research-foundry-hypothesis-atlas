# Baseline-deployable renal successor: endpoint-certification gate and no-go rule

## Purpose and substantive advance

This proposal is a substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent found a reproducible held-out log-loss reduction for continuous baseline creatinine--cystatin-C discordance, but its cohort required future repeat attendance and its comparator used observed future repeat time. It therefore did not answer a baseline-deployable clinical question. This successor removes both sources of future information and tests whether a fixed-horizon consequential outcome can be certified before any model is fit.

The clinically important unresolved question is whether baseline discordance can improve prediction of a consequential event that is observable without requiring a later research visit. The strongest claim supported by existing evidence is only that the continuous log ratio contains incremental internal information for a repeat creatinine-derived threshold in a selected first-repeat-attender cohort. That does not establish prediction of mortality, severe kidney disease, CKD, measured GFR, utility, or clinical benefit. The proposed experiment will not make any of those stronger claims unless its endpoint and censoring coverage are first certified.

## Proposed clinical estimand if, and only if, certification passes

The only candidate primary outcome is all-cause mortality by a fixed five-year horizon after baseline assessment date `53-0.0`. Define the baseline-only eligible population as participants with valid baseline date, positive finite creatinine and cystatin C, valid age and sex, and baseline measurements obtained before the certified outcome-ascertainment cutoff. Exclude anyone with a certified death date on or before baseline. Define `Y5=1` if certified death date is in `(baseline_date, baseline_date + 5 years]`; define `Y5=0` only when the certified coverage rule proves the participant alive through baseline + 5 years. Participants lacking that coverage are excluded before outcome labeling and reported in a coverage table. No repeat attendance, repeat biomarker, future assessment date, or observed future elapsed time may enter eligibility, predictors, or censoring.

A renal clinical endpoint is not currently certifiable from the locally available metadata. A secondary hospital-coded severe-kidney composite may be considered only if the same certification gate succeeds: a prespecified ICD-10 list for acute kidney failure and kidney replacement/transplant-level events, the exact diagnosis/admission date fields, and complete ascertainment through five years are all required. Do not call a code-derived composite CKD, progressive kidney disease, measured GFR decline, or adjudicated AKI without clinical validation. If the mortality gate passes but the renal gate does not, mortality remains the sole primary outcome and the renal endpoint is not analyzed.

## Exposure, comparator, and one frozen extension

Use race-free equations inherited from the parent to calculate baseline `eGFRcr_0` from `30700-0.0` and baseline `eGFRcys_0` from `30720-0.0`; retain only positive finite assay values and valid baseline covariates. Define one prespecified continuous discordance feature

`Z = log(eGFRcys_0 / eGFRcr_0)`.

M0 is a strong baseline creatinine-only comparator: intercept, natural-cubic spline for baseline eGFRcr, natural-cubic spline for baseline age, and sex. Splines use training-fold quantile knots, training boundaries, centered constraints, and training-only scaling. Mc is exactly M0 plus one training-standardized linear Z term. No repeat-time term, repeat biomarker, attendance indicator, health-outcome field, or post-baseline variable is permitted as a predictor. The equations and feature definition are fixed before inspecting outcome performance.

## Analysis and uncertainty

Use outcome-stratified round-robin five-fold cross-fitting with ten fixed repeats, the inherited logistic-regression implementation (`lbfgs`, L2, `C=1`, unpenalized intercept, `tol=1e-10`, `max_iter=2000`, no class weighting, no tuning). Primary estimands are paired held-out log-loss difference and, as a secondary capacity estimand, the difference in events captured at a fixed 10% capacity. Report calibration intercept, calibration slope, and observed-minus-predicted risk for both models. Use 4,000 participant-level paired bootstrap resamples with a frozen seed, carrying all repeated predictions and selection indicators together without refitting; label this conditional uncertainty and do not imply it includes split or refit uncertainty.

The analysis must first report a CONSORT-like ascertainment table: joined population, valid baseline date, valid biomarkers/covariates, pre-baseline death exclusions, certified five-year coverage, events, non-events, and exclusions due to unknown coverage. If the certified covered cohort has fewer than 100 primary events, or fewer than 90% of biomarker-complete participants have certified five-year outcome status, stop as infeasible and do not interpret model comparison as clinical evidence. These are feasibility/coverage gates, not claims of clinical utility.

## Frozen interpretation and falsification criteria

Supportive classification requires all of the following: (1) the endpoint-certification gate passes; (2) the primary covered cohort and event-count gates pass; (3) Mc improves held-out log loss with a 98% paired interval wholly below zero and improvement in at least 8/10 repeats; (4) the fixed 10% capacity difference has a 98% interval wholly above zero, a prespecified minimum of +5 events per 10% capacity, and is positive in at least 8/10 repeats; (5) Mc-minus-M0 calibration degradation remains within operational margins of 0.10 for intercept and slope and 0.002 for calibration-in-the-large; and (6) all fits converge with complete held-out predictions and exact capacity quotas.

Adverse classification is triggered by any of: failure of endpoint semantics or coverage certification; evidence that missing death values do not establish survival through the horizon; a primary endpoint assembled from unverified codes; a capacity interval wholly below zero with adverse direction in at least 8/10 repeats; or a calibration degradation interval exceeding an operational margin. A failed endpoint-certification gate is a definitive no-go for this child, not an adverse biological finding.

Inconclusive classification applies when certification and computation pass but one or more supportive performance gates fail without an adverse criterion. A favorable log-loss result without capacity success remains inconclusive. A model cannot be described as clinically useful, CKD-predictive, causal, mechanistic, or transportable from this experiment.

## Why the no-go branch is currently required

The local snapshot proves that candidate mortality and hospital field columns exist, but it does not prove their exact labels, code meanings, date semantics, or non-event coverage. Public concept searches and generic web pages are not adequate substitutes for authoritative field metadata, and no private participant rows may be sent to public search. Because a fixed-horizon mortality analysis would be invalid if missing death values were mistaken for survival, and because an unverified hospital-code composite would not be a certifiable renal endpoint, the scientifically correct current branch is no-go unless the endpoint-certification gate is independently satisfied. This preserves a falsifiable, baseline-deployable design without manufacturing an outcome.

## Evidence limits and required future work

Computationally checkable claims are source paths, snapshot/schema identifiers, header availability, key uniqueness, preprocessing, model construction, cross-fitting, convergence, prediction coverage, arithmetic, and gate consistency. Semantic validity of mortality and hospital fields, completeness of death registration, clinical validity of an ICD-10 severe-kidney composite, assay validity, CKD chronicity, measured filtration, net benefit, patient consequences, causal effects, and external transport require authoritative metadata, expert adjudication, or another study. The parent's same-snapshot result is internal motivating evidence only and is not prospective confirmation.
