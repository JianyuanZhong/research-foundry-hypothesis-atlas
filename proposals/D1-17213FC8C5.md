# An independently anchored departure-status ledger for copied, tentative, completed, and superseded recommendations

## Decision question and targeted validity repair

The frozen decision remains whether any exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated only for a prospective **within-site silent validation** of the inherited `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes. This child preserves the first-eligible all-opportunity population, reciprocal pre-departure opportunity chain, canonical discharge artifact, exhaustive `Y,V,H,Z` states, exact support atoms, all-unit worst-case admission routing, fixed positive-probability at-most-900-admission design, adverse-compatible finite-population inference, and strict noncausal ceiling of parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`.

The parents establish independent textual reproducibility and route-concealed actionability/supersession measurements. Their consequential residual gap is **reference validity**. Panels can agree that a sentence is an active plan while it is copied boilerplate, merely tentative, documents an action already performed, or is superseded elsewhere in the same available admission artifacts. More panel agreement does not make the recommendation a clinically live obligation.

Available MIMIC evidence can support a narrower statement: an admission contains an explicit, target-grounded, future-directed textual action that is not contradicted by an explicit target-linked completion, cancellation, or replacement in the canonical discharge and admission-linked pre-departure radiology artifacts. MIMIC cannot prove that the action was clinically appropriate, still required at physical departure, visible, assigned, communicated, ordered, scheduled, executed, beneficial, or safe. The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is that at least one inherited support-qualified `AUTO` or `RECON` atom remains eligible after an independently rendered and independently adjudicated **departure-status ledger** assigns every challenged unit an adverse-compatible status and after high-specificity source anchors and copy-provenance controls pass. Failure excludes the affected atom; absence of identifying evidence is `UNKNOWN/DEFER`, not a favorable reference label.

## Bounded source check motivating the ledger

A read-only lexical feasibility profile was run before proposal publication, solely to verify that the proposed reference threats occur in available fields. It read the first 100,000 physical discharge rows and first 300,000 physical radiology rows in gzip order, without clinical-label or population filtering. Among those nonrandom rows, 7,461 discharge texts contained a broad pulmonary/lung-nodule phrase; their normalized target/follow-up sentences included 7,492 occurrences belonging to fingerprints repeated at least twice. Of 1,781 sampled admission keys with target text in both files, 538 had an exact normalized target/follow-up sentence overlap. Tentative, completion, and replacement lexical patterns were also observable. These are deliberately labeled unvalidated lexical counts, not prevalences, patient states, or evidence that any repeated text was inappropriate. The executable method and frozen aggregate output are supporting files.

The bounded profile also tests whether longitudinal separation is computationally plausible without pretending to estimate it. A read-only job scanned all 331,794 physical discharge rows and 2,321,355 physical radiology rows. It indexed the first 20,000 physical discharge rows in gzip order using nonmissing `hadm_id`, `charttime`, and `storetime`, exact `note_type==DS`, and broad pulmonary target plus follow-up regexes, yielding 1,485 nonrandom index rows. Using index `charttime` only as a departure proxy, and scanning same-subject rows with `0 < charttime-index_charttime <=365` days and `storetime>index_charttime`, 1,110 had any future note, 704 any future target text, 555 future target/follow-up text, 468 future target text with a broad firm marker, 161 with a broad tentative marker, 576 with a broad completion marker, and 207 with a broad supersession marker. These overlapping, regex-defined counts are feasibility signals only; they are not prevalence, outcomes, or evidence of persistence. The exact script and JSON output are supporting artifacts, and the final experiment must rerun the method on the frozen 300-admission challenge roster using `D`, not this proxy.

## Presealed provenance ledger: objective facts, not clinical labels

Before any A1/A2/S1/H3 or new reference label is revealed, tokenize and hash every candidate recommendation span and its DS sentence using a frozen normalizer that changes only Unicode form, case, whitespace, punctuation spacing, and deidentification placeholders. Measurements, anatomy, laterality, modality, timing, negation, and modal verbs are preserved. Store no source text in public outputs. For each unit record:

1. exact and token-longest-common-subsequence match to its own reciprocal radiology recommendation span;
2. the number of distinct admissions and subjects containing the exact normalized DS sentence; `RECURRENT_5` means at least five distinct subjects;
3. whether the DS span has a target-specific grounding token matching the source chain: anatomy/laterality, measurement, modality, comparison, or explicitly linked interval;
4. DS section coordinates from a frozen section parser, with `SECTION_UNKNOWN` when headings are absent or ambiguous;
5. every same-admission radiology report after the source recommendation but before `D` and stored before `D`, with timestamps and target/action candidate links; and
6. every target-linked firm, tentative, completion, cancellation, replacement, and contradiction candidate span anywhere in the canonical DS, including spans outside the apparent follow-up section.

`RECURRENT_5`, source-copy similarity, or a section heading is never itself a favorable or adverse clinical label. Recurrent wording may be an appropriate template; verbatim source propagation may be faithful. These flags calibrate whether reviewers rely on generic language without target grounding and make copying visible rather than assuming it away.

## Route-concealed longitudinal persistence challenge

The strongest remaining weakness is not merely panel error. Even an accurate departure ledger can mistake a transient, copied, or already-resolved sentence for a durable target-linked future action because it examines one admission's artifacts. A later mention can challenge that interpretation, but later care is not a clinical gold standard: documentation may be missing, delayed, copied, generated for a different reason, or reflect care that was inappropriate. The falsifiable added hypothesis is therefore deliberately narrower: among the *same pre-specified inherited challenge units*, firm target-grounded departure recommendations that survive the ledger will have more temporally separated, target/action-consistent future documentation and fewer explicit revisions/completions than units classified tentative, completed, superseded, inactive, or unknown. This is a challenge of longitudinal textual durability, not validation of execution, necessity, resolution, safety, or benefit.

After the two departure panels and all inherited route labels are sealed, a deterministic extractor scans the complete available note files for every challenged admission's subject through `D+365` days. No unit, route, stratum, panel label, or observed future evidence is used to choose who is scanned; every challenged unit remains in the denominator, including units with no later record. The primary separated window excludes the index admission (`hadm_id` equal to the index `hadm_id`) and requires a later note's `charttime>D` and `storetime>D`; windows are `(D,D+30 days]`, `(D+30,D+90 days]`, and `(D+90,D+365 days]`. Later records with missing `hadm_id` but known `subject_id` are retained in a separately identified `UNLINKED_FUTURE` category, never silently discarded. Same-admission post-`D` notes are enumerated in a leakage diagnostic, not counted as independent persistence. A note with only one missing time is `TEMPORALLY_UNRESOLVED`, not absent.

The extractor records only auditable, hashed features: note identity, subject/admission linkage status, chart/store times and window, target-component match (anatomy/laterality, measurement, modality, comparison, interval), action/interval match, explicit firm/tentative/completion/replacement/contradiction markers, exact-source or cross-subject recurrence, and whether the text is a new target/action combination versus a verbatim recurrence. It does not infer that an imaging study occurred from a later recommendation, and it does not use medications, procedures, encounters, or later utilization as outcomes. A blinded longitudinal reader, roster-disjoint from R1/R2 and all prior panels, sees only hashed unit IDs, the departure target/action abstraction, and chronological future feature packets; it sees no route, atom, stratum, weights, departure labels, or expected class. The reader assigns a compatible challenge tuple: `PERSISTENT_FUTURE_TEXT`, `FUTURE_REVISED_OR_SUPERSEDED`, `FUTURE_DOCUMENTED_COMPLETION`, `FUTURE_TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `FUTURE_CONTRADICTION`, `FUTURE_NO_OBSERVED_TEXT`, `FUTURE_UNLINKED_OR_TIME_UNKNOWN`, or `FUTURE_TARGET_UNKNOWN`. The last three are adverse-compatible missingness states, not negative clinical findings. If the reader cannot distinguish copied recurrence from an independently target-grounded later mention, both states remain compatible and no favorable persistence is counted.

This is not an additional clinical-reading workload: feature extraction is a presealed, full-file computation and the one longitudinal reader receives at most the same 300 challenged admissions in one compact pass. If the roster, packet, or full scan cannot include every challenged unit, the persistence layer is `LONGITUDINAL_INFEASIBLE` and cannot support any release. The reader's labels are never a gold standard. Primary results report, by inherited atom and route, design-weighted compatible-set bounds for persistence, revision/completion, and all missingness categories; every disagreement, unlinked note, time ambiguity, packet failure, and nonresponse is assigned to the adverse state. The persistence layer is a falsification/challenge covariate only: it cannot rescue an atom that fails the departure gates, and a later text match cannot upgrade a tentative, completed, superseded, or unknown departure unit to `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`.

The longitudinal scan is bound exactly to `[internal dataset path]` (`note/discharge`, columns `note_id,subject_id,hadm_id,note_type,note_seq,charttime,storetime,text`, identity `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id)`) and `[internal dataset path]` (`note/radiology`, the same columns and identity). It joins future rows to the frozen index through `subject_id` and the index admission through `hadm_id` only when present; it never joins on `note_id` across tables. `[internal dataset path]` and `note/discharge_detail`, columns `note_id,subject_id,field_name,field_value,field_ordinal`, and `[internal dataset path]` and `note/radiology_detail` with the same columns are used only to preserve note identity/field provenance, joined on `(subject_id,note_id)`; detail cannot create a future event or supply absent timing. The index `D` and first-eligible population remain bound to `hosp/admissions` archive member `mimic-iv-3.1/hosp/admissions.csv.gz`, table `hosp/admissions`, key `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, `dischtime`; patients/services/transfers/ICU bindings and the inherited reciprocal chain are unchanged. The deidentified within-subject timestamps are used only for ordered separation; no cross-subject calendar comparison is made.

## Independent departure-status reference packets and labels

Every admission in the inherited 300-admission actionability challenge receives this layer; no new admission is sampled. If the inherited challenge uses fewer than 300, use all of it and report the limitation. There are exactly two additional compact whole-admission reference readings per challenged admission, at most 600 readings total. Exceeding 600, roster insufficiency, or inability to include every unit is `REFERENCE_INFEASIBLE`; no atom passes. Packet creation, rosters, codebooks, parser, hashes, and order are frozen before labels.

Reference panels R1 and R2 are roster-disjoint from each other and from all inherited index, audit, A1/A2/S1/H1/H2/H3 panels. They receive independent pseudonyms, orderings, and independently written codebooks with no shared examples or training cases. Neither sees atoms, strata, weights, routes, inherited labels, other-panel labels, or expected control answers. R1 receives an **assertion-centered view**: each source recommendation, all DS target/action spans in original local context, section coordinates, and explicit timestamps. R2 receives a **trajectory-centered view**: the chronological reciprocal chain, all other admission-linked pre-`D`/pre-storage radiology reports, and the entire canonical DS with candidate spans unhighlighted. Both views display missingness and distinguish `charttime`, `storetime`, and `D`; neither implies that storage equals visibility.

For each inherited recommendation unit, each panel independently records an atomic tuple:

* target identity and whether a DS action is target-grounded (`EXACT`, `COMPATIBLE`, `MISMATCH`, `UNKNOWN`);
* force: `FIRM_FUTURE`, `TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `INFORMATIONAL`, `NEGATED_OR_CANCELLED`, `CONFLICT`, `UNKNOWN`;
* action, interval/timing, named recipient, and responsibility wording, each as observed/absent/contradicted/unknown;
* documented action status: `NOT_DOCUMENTED_COMPLETED`, `MATCHING_ACTION_PERFORMED_PRE_D`, `PERFORMED_BUT_NEXT_ACTION_REMAINS`, `COMPLETION_AMBIGUOUS`, `UNKNOWN`;
* replacement status: `NONE_EXPLICIT`, `EXPLICIT_REPLACEMENT`, `EXPLICIT_CHANGED_INTERVAL`, `COMPETING_PLANS`, `UNKNOWN`;
* provenance: `CASE_GROUNDED`, `RECURRENT_BUT_GROUNDED`, `RECURRENT_UNGROUNDED`, `SOURCE_COPY_GROUNDED`, `SOURCE_COPY_UNGROUNDED`, `PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN`; and
* one compatible set over `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`, `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, `R_TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `R_COMPLETED_NO_REMAINING_ACTION`, `R_SUPERSEDED`, `R_INACTIVE_INFORMATIONAL`, `R_CONFLICT`, and `R_UNKNOWN`.

`R_FIRM_ACTIVE` requires an explicit target-grounded future action, firm rather than solely tentative language, and no explicit matching completion without a remaining future action, cancellation, replacement, or competing plan in the supplied artifacts. It does not require that a clinician should act. `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE` requires a target-grounded firm future action but incomplete timing/recipient/responsibility or unresolved plan detail. A recommendation copied from radiology can enter either firm state only if target grounding is independently supported; recurrent ungrouded text cannot. `TENTATIVE_ONLY`, completion without an explicit remaining action, supersession, inactivity, conflict, and unknown are never converted to active by consensus.

No senior consensus collapses R1/R2. The primary reference compatible set is their union plus every state compatible with missing/corrupt source evidence. Favorable intersection is reported only diagnostically. R1/R2 are measurements, not a gold standard.

## High-specificity anchors and falsification controls

The following anchors use observable source relations and are deliberately narrower than clinical truth:

* **explicit negative anchor:** a target-linked cancellation/no-further-action statement, or an explicit replacement relation naming old and new action/interval, present verbatim in the DS;
* **documented completion anchor:** a later target-linked pre-`D`, pre-storage radiology report documents the matching recommended imaging action, and the DS contains no explicit next future action; this anchors documented completion, not clinical resolution;
* **tentative-only anchor:** every target-linked action clause uses only conditional/optional language and no firm future clause is present anywhere in the permitted artifacts;
* **firm textual anchor:** a dedicated plan/instruction span contains target, future action, firm modal/imperative, and timing or recipient, is target-grounded to the source chain, and no permitted artifact contains an explicit completion, cancellation, replacement, or contradiction; and
* **copy-provenance anchor:** exact cross-subject recurrence or exact source-to-DS copying as defined above; it anchors textual provenance only.

R1/R2 must classify anchor components without seeing anchor names. Locked controls are inserted into the reference packet queue before review: a copied firm sentence with target grounding preserved; the same sentence with target attributes swapped to an incompatible target; a firm-to-tentative modal change; an inserted explicit completion; an inserted explicit replacement; and a section/format-only perturbation. The copied grounded sentence must retain textual status, the target swap must lose grounding, firm-to-tentative must change force only, completion/replacement must change status only, and formatting must change nothing. Controls do not rescue failure on real cases and do not establish clinical correctness.

## Estimands, adverse-compatible inference, and release gates

Retain all inherited estimands and gates. Add, by exact atom and inherited route, design-weighted unit- and worst-unit admission-level estimates of: reference-firm-active mass; firm-incomplete mass; tentative-only, documented-completed, superseded, inactive, conflict, and unknown mass; recurrent/source-copy provenance; ungrounded recurrent mass; inherited-actionability versus reference discordance; anchor recognition; invariant and decisive-control violations; packet/nonresponse; and reference workload. Add longitudinal challenge estimands, always conditional on the fixed inherited challenge sample: first and last temporally separated target/action-consistent future-text window, persistence compatible set, explicit future revision/completion/contradiction, and `NO_OBSERVED_TEXT`, `UNLINKED_FUTURE`, and `TEMPORALLY_UNRESOLVED` mass. Report these separately for departure `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`/`R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE` versus each adverse-compatible departure state, without conditioning on having a future note. The prespecified falsification contrast is the adverse-compatible lower/upper-bound difference in durable future-text support between firm-active and non-active strata; no minimum effect is assumed. If compatible bounds include zero or the class ordering is reversed, the challenge is non-supportive/inconclusive, not evidence of durability.

Use inherited sampling weights and joint admission clusters. The reference layer is conditional on inherited actionability-challenge inclusion and reports those conditional probabilities; it does not masquerade as a new simple random sample. Compute exact finite-population inversion for binary outcomes and 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates, retaining every unit and panel label from an admission together. Extend the single inherited max-statistic simultaneous 95% family to these estimands and controls. Lower bounds count only jointly observed favorable support. Upper adverse bounds assign every R1/R2 disagreement, unknown, missing report, ambiguous target link, parser failure, packet corruption, and nonresponse to the route-adverse state. For the longitudinal layer additionally assign every absent later note, unlinked later note, missing timestamp, copied-versus-new ambiguity, and reader disagreement to the adverse/missing state; never condition the primary contrast on future-note availability. Kappa, consensus, majority vote, latent-class estimates, semantic models, and favorable imputation are diagnostics only.

An atom may remain on an `AUTO` allow-list only if it passes every inherited gate and, among its challenged candidate-route admissions, (i) every adverse-compatible unit is `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`; (ii) the simultaneous upper bound on inherited active/AUTO units incompatible with the reference is at most 0.05; (iii) the upper bound on recurrent-ungrounded or target-mismatched units is at most 0.02; (iv) the lower bound for recognition of each occupied explicit-negative/completion/tentative anchor class is at least 0.95; (v) the upper invariant-control violation is at most 0.02 and each decisive-control violation at most 0.05; and (vi) reference missingness/nonresponse upper bound is below 0.05 unless censused. Any single adverse-compatible completion, supersession, tentative-only, inactivity, conflict, or unknown unit blocks AUTO under inherited worst-unit precedence.

An atom may remain on a `RECON` allow-list only if every unit is adverse-compatible with `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` or `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, the upper inherited-versus-reference discordance bound is at most 0.10, the upper completed/superseded/tentative/inactive/unknown mass is at most 0.10, and the same anchor, control, missingness, support, event, effective-sample-size, weight, and workload gates pass. `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE` can require reconciliation but never AUTO. `TIMING` retains inherited chronology precedence but cannot override a non-active reference state. All other signatures and every sparse, unsupported, failed, or inconclusive atom are `DEFER`.

At least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 reference-challenged admissions for that route in the atom are required when feasible, exactly as inherited. No passing pooled estimate, neighboring atom, macro-service group, favorable consensus, source copying, negative-control performance, shrinkage, or model can rescue an atom. A missing anchor class is reported as not calibratable; it neither fails nor passes that class, but if the corresponding adverse state remains possible for route candidates, bounds remain wide and prevent nomination.

## Mandatory verification fixtures

Verification must reproduce source/schema hashes, joins, reciprocal links, canonical DS, `D`, unit clocks, `Y,V,H,Z`, atoms, inherited draws/probabilities, packet/codebook hashes, roster disjointness, seal ordering, and the provenance ledger. It must additionally reproduce the complete-challenge longitudinal roster, exclusion of index-admission post-`D` notes from the primary separated window, exact `(D,D+30]`, `(D+30,D+90]`, `(D+90,D+365]` boundaries, subject-only handling of missing `hadm_id`, `UNLINKED_FUTURE` and `TEMPORALLY_UNRESOLVED` states, and the no-future-note denominator. Required fixtures are: (1) exact copied but target-grounded text retained as copy without automatically becoming inactive; (2) recurrent text with a swapped target blocked as ungrounded; (3) tentative-only wording not upgraded by panel agreement; (4) a later matching pre-`D` report classified as documented completion while an explicit next-step clause preserves the next action; (5) an explicit changed interval superseding the old interval; (6) a competing plan widening the compatible set; (7) a target-linked replacement outside the apparent follow-up section still detected; (8) missing high-weight R2 labels widening adverse bounds; (9) pooled-safe/atom-unsafe behavior; (10) unknown/novel signatures mapping to `DEFER`; (11) an index-admission post-`D` note appears in leakage diagnostics but not separated persistence; (12) a later same-subject, missing-`hadm_id` note is `UNLINKED_FUTURE`, never silently absent; (13) missing charttime or storetime is `TEMPORALLY_UNRESOLVED`; (14) copied later wording cannot count as independently persistent without target/action grounding; and (15) a zero-future-note case remains in the denominator and widens adverse bounds. Route revelation before seals, roster overlap, post-label anchor construction, omitted units, POE use, favorable unknown assignment, future-note conditioning, or source-text export invalidates the affected run.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

A supportive result requires all inherited and new atom-specific gates and at least one fully supported exact atom. It supports only this statement: for the available MIMIC textual artifacts, the atom's route is reproducible and its candidate action units are explicitly target-grounded, firm and future-directed, without observed target-linked completion, cancellation, replacement, or contradiction under adverse-compatible reference review, and the temporally separated challenge does not falsify a greater persistence-compatible mass in the firm-active class than in adverse departure classes. That is sufficient only to nominate a prospective within-site **silent** workflow study; all unlisted signatures remain `DEFER`. A supportive longitudinal contrast is not evidence that anyone acted, that the action was needed, or that the target persisted clinically.

An adverse result is an atom-specific excess of tentative-only, completed, superseded, inactive, recurrent-ungrounded, mismatched, conflicting, or unknown units; a reference/actionability discordance bound crossing a gate; an anchor/control falsification; or a longitudinal challenge showing no separation, reversed ordering, or materially larger adverse-compatible future revision/completion/missingness among firm-active units. It excludes that atom from nomination but does not prove clinical harm, negligence, inappropriate care, failure to follow up, or that later text is a clinical outcome.

An inconclusive result includes sparse route or anchor classes, inability to inspect every unit, infeasible 600-reading workload, nonresponse, broken seals, ambiguous target matching, missing pre-`D` evidence, or wide compatible bounds. Inconclusive is not a pass and defaults to `DEFER`.

Even a perfect result cannot establish that a recommendation was a **clinically live departure obligation**. MIMIC lacks actual viewing, edit/sign/finalization semantics, responsibility assignment, conversations, patient preferences, external records, target-linked orders/referrals, scheduling, execution, appropriateness, malignancy, benefit, harm, or forward/external transport. The required next study is a separately approved actual-calendar prospective silent bridge capturing those workflow and clinical facts. Only expert-governed prospective evidence could support live use, safety, utility, or causal benefit.
