# UACR-conditioned test of raw-grip cystatin-C allocation

## Decision, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

The inherited experiment asks whether raw grip improves allocation of a fixed cystatin-C budget among UK Biobank adults with baseline eGFRcr 60–89. It did not compare grip with urine albumin/creatinine information, although current kidney evaluation uses both GFR and albuminuria. That omission could make grip appear useful only because the clinical information set was incomplete.

The strongest claim supported before opening any locked cystatin label is feasibility: in the unchanged inherited O (134,118 participants), baseline UACR can be constructed for 130,257 (97.12%) using numeric urine microalbumin or its documented low-result flag plus positive urine creatinine. Coverage is 97.18% in locked test and 97.14% in locked replication. This does not establish UACR prediction, grip increment, true GFR, CKD, or benefit.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> In each locked partition, adding the inherited single raw-grip scalar to a UACR-informed policy (AG versus A) will identify at least 2 additional combined-equation eGFR<60 reclassifications per 1,000 cystatin assays, with yield ratio at least 1.10, while the original grip-only G policy is noninferior to A at -1 reclassification per 1,000. All inherited G:B/G:F/G:T, discordance, placebo, missing-label, calibration, sex-stability and N18 rules must still pass. If N18 sufficiency passes, AG must beat A by at least 1 H×K18 event per 1,000 and G must be noninferior to A at -1 per 1,000.

AG:A tests whether raw grip contributes after albuminuria. G:A separately tests whether raw grip alone can substitute for an already available UACR benchmark. UACR is itself an assay; equal cystatin budgets do not imply equal total collection cost.

## UACR construction and policies

Use baseline instance-0 fields only. Let albumin be positive numeric 30500-0.0 mg/L and urine creatinine be positive numeric 30510-0.0 µmol/L. For numeric albumin:

UACR = 1000 × albumin / urine_creatinine mg/mmol.

If albumin is blank and trimmed 30505-0.0 is exactly <6.7, define the primary censored value as albumin=3.35 mg/L. Any contradictory numeric-plus-flag row is an integrity failure and is reported. A missing/nonpositive urine creatinine, missing albumin without that flag, any other flag, or nonfinite ratio is unavailable UACR. Field 30515-0.0 is the urine-creatinine result flag and is audited, never parsed as albumin. Instance 0 denotes baseline collection; there is no separate urine collection date in the bound design, so co-temporality beyond UKB instance assignment is not adjudicable.

In development O only, form Z_A by log1p(UACR), winsorizing at the development 1st/99th percentiles, standardizing by development mean/SD, and imputing unavailable values to the pre-winsorization development median before applying the frozen transform. No missing indicator, category threshold, spline, interaction, future value, cystatin value, outcome, or locked statistic enters primary Z_A.

A uses the identical ridge-logistic H model and B columns plus exactly Z_A. AG is bit-for-bit A plus exactly the inherited frozen Z_R raw-grip column. Thus AG:A isolates one-column grip increment after UACR; A and AG rank every O participant. Fit and tune only in development under the inherited procedure, then freeze models and rank each partition to exactly n=floor(0.20×|O_partition|) with the inherited tie hash.

Before opening locks, freeze six UACR variants: censored albumin 0, 3.35, or 6.7 mg/L crossed with unavailable UACR imputed to the development 1st or 99th percentile. The 3.35/development-median version is primary; extreme variants are missingness/LOD stress tests and cannot rescue it. UACR unavailability above 5% in either lock is inconclusive.

C is a non-rescuing routine-risk stress policy: A plus baseline doctor-diagnosed diabetes 2443-0.0 (known 0/1), mean of available positive automated systolic readings 4080-0.0/0.1, and sex-specific self-reported BP-medication indicator (code 2 in any 6153-0.0…0.3 when sex=0 or 6177-0.0…0.2 when sex=1). CG=C plus exactly Z_R. Development medians/modes and explicit missing indicators are frozen before locks. A second non-rescuing variant adds positive HbA1c 30750-0.0. C/CG cannot establish primary support but can show whether AG:A was explained by common kidney-risk information.

## Estimands, inference, and gates

Retain inherited yields Y_H, Y_D, and Y_H18, all per the same exact cystatin-assay denominator n. Add paired contrasts and ratios for AG:A and G:A, with overlaps and AG-minus-A/A-minus-AG and G-minus-A/A-minus-G swap-set decompositions.

Extend the inherited 2,000 participant-paired, no-refit bootstrap max-|t| H family to AG:A and G:A. In each lock, primary AG:A must have adverse joint-bound Delta_H>=2/1000, ratio >=1.10, simultaneous 95% intervals above 0 and 1, and positive AG-minus-A versus A-minus-AG H yield. G:A must have a one-sided simultaneous 95% lower bound above -1/1000; claim G superiority only if its two-sided interval is above 0. Extend D gates: AG:A must be positive in both locks with pooled partition-stratified paired 95% interval above 0; G:A must have a one-sided lower bound above -1/1000. All inherited G gates remain mandatory.

The primary grip-beyond-UACR claim additionally requires Delta_H(AG:A)>0 and Delta_D(AG:A)>0 in every frozen UACR extreme variant in both locks; variants need not meet primary materiality or ratio thresholds. Any sign reversal is inconclusive. Report C/CG and HbA1c stress results without rescue.

For N18, extend inherited event sufficiency to require at least 20 observed-label H×K18 events in each pooled AG-minus-A and A-minus-AG swap set and, for G:A noninferiority, each G-minus-A and A-minus-G set. If sufficient, require adverse-bound Delta_H18(AG:A)>=1/1000 with pooled two-sided 95% interval above 0 and one-sided lower bound for G:A above -1/1000. Positive N17/Kany cannot rescue N18. Participant-level joint missing-H bounds, the >1% missing-cystatin rule, death handling and all inherited uncertainty rules apply unchanged to every added comparison.

Fully supportive requires every inherited gate, primary AG:A biochemical gates in both locks, G:A biochemical noninferiority, UACR robustness, and sufficient supportive N18 gates. This supports prospective comparison of allocation strategies only.

If AG beats A but G is inferior to A, grip may complement UACR but the original grip-only policy is operationally adverse where UACR is available. If A matches or beats AG with adequate precision, raw grip adds no decision-relevant information beyond UACR under this design. If G matches A but AG does not beat A, grip may be a lower-burden substitute candidate but not an incremental marker. Sparse swaps/events, UACR variant sign reversal, >5% UACR unavailability, partition disagreement, crossed intervals, inherited failures, or chronology/integrity failure are inconclusive. No sensitivity result rescues a failed primary gate.

## Falsification, verification, and limits

Add fixtures for confusing 30505 with 30515; wrong UACR units; using 6.7 as if measured; complete-case restriction that changes O or n; fitting imputation/winsorization on locks; A/AG unequal base matrices; AG containing anything beyond A+Z_R; opening locked labels before all variants freeze; AG passing while G is wrongly called a UACR substitute; G noninferiority wrongly called incremental value; primary failure rescued by C/CG; and supportive arithmetic followed by claims of CKD, muscle mechanism, benefit, cost-effectiveness, or guideline endorsement.

The verifier can check headers/hashes, O, transforms, policy membership, exact budgets, leakage, bounds, uncertainty, gates and output-linked conclusions. It cannot verify that one baseline UACR is persistent albuminuria, combined eGFR is true GFR, N18 is adjudicated CKD, grip measures muscle mass, UACR is already available or costless in deployment, or testing changes treatment or outcomes. These need repeat urine testing, measured GFR, outpatient/chart data, nephrology and equity review, costs/actions, external transport validation, and a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.
