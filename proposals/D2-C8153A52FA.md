# MerTK-efferocytic brake vs SPP1+/LAM state-marker: an independent human disease-spectrum falsification test (episode 14)

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2 (repaired/evolved). Operation: **evolve**.
Parent: `[prior hypothesis]` (valid, reference-complete, mu ~31.6).
Key works: [K1],[K2],[K3]. Screening status: **planned, not executed**.

## Unresolved question
The parent branch argues that the MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) synovial macrophage state **actively brakes**
the pathogenic FLS effector programme, resting that claim on a single in-vitro dataset (E-MTAB-8873,
445-feature panel, n=1 titration arms) plus a macrophage-only perturbation (E-MTAB-8316). Neither tests
the brake hypothesis **in human disease across clinical states**. The unresolved, still falsifiable claim:
*is loss of the MerTK-efferocytosis axis associated with more severe / treatment-resistant RA in vivo
(causal-brake prediction), or is MerTK simply a bystander whose level is set by a generic
SPP1+/TREM2+ lipid-associated macrophage (LAM) state that itself tracks inflammation (state-marker rival)?*

## Clinical importance
~1/3 of RA patients do not sustain remission on current DMARDs. If the MerTK-efferocytic axis is
specifically depleted in severe/resistant disease, resolution-restoring (MerTK-agonism) strategies gain
mechanistic support in the very population that fails current therapy. If instead the axis merely
co-varies with a generic inflammatory/LAM state, targeting it is likely misguided and effort should shift
to the TF-driven effector programmes [K3]. Both outcomes change the next research decision.

## Population, variables, outcomes
- Population: 25 independent-donor synovial macrophages (exclude the 2 flagged donors), pseudobulk.
- Exposure (ordinal disease rank): Healthy(0) < Undifferentiated arthritis(1) < Na\u00efve RA(2) < Remission RA(3) < Resistant RA(4).
  Remission vs resistant is the clinically decisive contrast.
- Outcome axes (pre-registered, gene lists frozen before fitting):
  - **MerTK-efferocytosis axis** (primary): MERTK, GAS6, PROS1, AXL, TYRO3, MRC1 (mean z-score of log2 CPM+1, library-size normalised).
  - **LAM/state rival axis**: SPP1, TREM2, FOLR2.
  - **Specificity control**: pan-macrophage markers CD68/CSF1R/ITGAM (expected to be roughly flat).
- Covariates: donor age, sex, and `log10(cells)` (composition/quality).

## Estimand and analysis
1. Baseline: Spearman rho between MerTK-axis score and disease rank; and a donor-aware robust regression
   (axis ~ rank + age + sex + log cells) with rank as ordinal.
2. **Partial-association discriminator**: partial Spearman between MerTK-axis and disease rank controlling for the LAM/state axis.
3. Learned alternative: cross-validated (leave-one-donor-out) classifier of resistant vs remission using
   MerTK-axis-only vs LAM-axis-only vs combined feature sets; report AUC and whether MerTK-axis adds over LAM-axis.
4. Sensitivity: repeat excluding each disease group in turn and with/without the 2 flagged donors (secondary only).

## Supportive / adverse / inconclusive
- **Supportive (causal-brake)**: MerTK-axis score decreases monotonically with disease severity and its
  partial association with severe/resistant RA survives LAM adjustment (point estimate, CI excluding 0),
  and MerTK-axis adds incremental discrimination of resistant vs remission over LAM features.
- **Adverse (state-marker)**: MerTK-axis is flat/higher in resistant RA, or its association is fully absorbed
  by the LAM axis (partial rho ~ 0), or it is driven by the 2 flagged donors.
- **Inconclusive**: direction consistent but CI wide / not significant at n=25, or the axis is dominated by
  a single gene; would justify new donors, not a directional claim.

## Falsification criteria
Pre-register directional sign and the LAM-adjustment test: the causal-brake reading is falsified if the
partial association with severe/resistant RA is null or opposite after LAM adjustment.

tissue); small n and 2 related donors; no protein/wet-lab causality. Not a clinical-efficacy claim.
