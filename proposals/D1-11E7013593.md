## Deterministic outcome construction

Define `B=(D-720,D]`, pre-D placebo interval `P=(D-1440,D-720]`, primary outcome `O=(D+360,D+1800]`, and fixed horizon sensitivity `O12=(D+360,D+1080]`. All boundaries are open/closed exactly as written and all clocks are minutes from ICU admission. Require `unitdischargeoffset > D+1800` for an ordinary primary endpoint observation. If discharge/death/truncation occurs by the horizon, classify a competing exit with exit offset, `unitdischargestatus`, and `unitdischargelocation`; discharge at or before `D+360` is separately marked no post-washout opportunity. Never recode an exit as improvement or ordinary non-improvement.

### Frozen value parser

Freeze this parser and label map before reading arm-specific counts. Normalize labels only by Unicode whitespace collapse, trim, and ASCII case-folding; no substring matching or post-hoc synonyms. The primary exact maps are:

* `fio2`: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`; values for `FiO2` and `Set Fraction...` must parse finite numbers in `[0,1]`; `FIO2 (%)` must parse finite numbers in `[0,100]` and is divided by 100 under the eICU percent convention.
* `peep`: `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`; finite numeric values in `[0,50]` cm H2O.
* `pressure_support`: `Pressure Support`, `PS`, `PS above PEEP`; finite numeric values in `[0,60]` cm H2O.
* `set_rate`: `Adult Con Setting Set RR`, `NIV Setting Set_RR`, `Backup RR (Set)`; finite numeric values in `[0,80]` breaths/min.
* `peak_pressure` is enumerated and parsed only for an ablation, never the primary endpoint.

Empty, nonnumeric, nonfinite, and out-of-bound values are missing with a recorded reason. No unit conversion other than the explicitly defined percent convention is permitted. In a full scan, enumerate every distinct `respchartvaluelabel`, its normalized form, row count, matched family, and unmatched reason; fail if duplicate normalized labels map to multiple primary families. For every `(sid,landmark,component,window)`, retain valid-row count, distinct offsets, first/last offset, median, median gap, and parser counts. A component window is available only with at least two valid observations at distinct `respchartoffset` values separated by at least 30 minutes.

Support improvement is a reduction from B median to O median of at least 0.05 FiO2, 2 PEEP, 2 pressure support, or 2 set-rate units in at least one available non-peak component. Safety is preserved when both `sao2` and `respiration` are available in both windows, with O median Sao2 no more than 2 percentage points below B and O median respiration no more than 4 breaths/min above B. If either safety signal is observed in both windows and fails, safety fails; if neither signal is available in both windows, endpoint is missing. The primary event requires support improvement, safety preservation, and at least one O respiratoryCare record with nonmissing `airwaytype` or one of the four ventilator interval fields. This is corroboration, not bedside proof.

Assign exactly one primary state in this order: `competing_exit`; otherwise `endpoint_missing` if minimum observations/safety availability fail; otherwise `persistent_improvement` if all primary conditions pass; otherwise `observed_non_improvement`. Emit component-level endpoints, support-only endpoint, setting-only/no-airway endpoint, safety-preserving FiO2/PEEP endpoint, and O observation density as secondary outputs. Airway sensitivity is unavailable rather than estimated if either arm has fewer than 10% observed rows: exact `airwaytype` transition to `No Artificial Airway`, or a pre-frozen exact normalized ETT-removal treatment string followed by no invasive-airway respiratoryCare record for 48 hours. These proxies are not extubation or liberation.

## Held-out estimands and uncertainty

Primary transport evaluation is leave-one-hospital-out. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss/Brier score, calibration slope/intercept, AUROC only when both classes exist, four-state confusion/availability, observed and predicted evaluation-minus-no-trial risk differences, and fold-specific directions. The principal comparison is state-plus-label versus state-plus-opportunity; opportunity-only is a negative-control comparison. Do not claim label information from a gain that only predicts missingness/competing exits.

Before fitting, publish per-hospital and per-landmark counts of each arm, complete endpoint observations, missingness, exits, and four states. A transport risk-contrast gate requires at least five hospitals with at least 10 complete endpoint-observed rows in each arm, at least 50% of complete rows in those hospitals, and no hospital supplying over 40% of either arm. Folds with one arm contribute only metrics that are defined for their observed distribution, never an arm contrast.

Use stay-cluster bootstrap, not row bootstrap: resample complete `sid` clusters within hospital strata, preserving all landmarks of each stay, refit the entire pipeline, fixed seed `61061`, 1,000 replicates (or at least 500 with shortfall recorded before results); report 95% percentile intervals and failures. Permute labels within hospital-by-landmark strata only where both arms exist, preserving arm counts and all row features; run 1,000 permutations if at least 20 valid strata, else record the exact number and mark the permutation gate unavailable/inconclusive.

Call the result computationally supportive only if the primary endpoint and at least two airway-recency variants pass the support/lineage gates, at least five hospitals meet the arm support rule, the positive risk difference has at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions, both micro and macro held-out log-loss increments over state-plus-opportunity are at least 0.01 better for the label model with the stay-cluster interval excluding zero, no held-out reversal occurs, placebos/permutation/ablations do not reproduce the gain, missingness/exits do not explain it, and independent airway corroboration is at least 10% in both arms. These are not clinical-truth or causal gates.
