# Does early ventilatory burden predict a documented failed liberation transition?

## Scientific deliverable and advance

This is a data-bound adaptation of expert seed [starting question], “Ventilatory burden and liberation from ventilation.” The seed is an untested hypothesis, not evidence for the result.

The future solver must newly construct an auditable adult eICU invasive-ventilation landmark cohort and timestamped respiratory-state ledger, then estimate whether early ventilatory burden—body-size-adjusted tidal volume together with documented mechanics—predicts a subsequent adverse liberation transition beyond tidal volume alone.

Completion requires: source/header/schema/value audits; a leakage-safe six-hour landmark cohort; a 30-minute respiratory-state ledger through 48 hours; matched tidal-volume-only and full-burden transparent models plus a mechanistic state-transition model on the same cohort and endpoint; standardized competing-risk contrasts, calibration, scoring, hospital-held-out transport and cluster uncertainty; documentation-process, timing, airway and endpoint-specificity falsifications; and an evidence-linked supportive/adverse/inconclusive conclusion. Discovery fitted no target-cohort model and makes no clinical conclusion.

The substantive advance is a falsifiable decomposition of the seed’s broad burden idea into body-size-adjusted tidal volume, pressure/load and compliance information, and the ordered pathway active ventilation → documented off → documented restart or durable off. It tests whether these fields add transportable prognostic information and whether the signal is specific to a liberation pathway rather than generic documentation intensity. It does not reproduce any demonstration paper. Systematic novelty review remains future work.

## Evidence boundary and hypothesis

The strongest claim supported before fitting is only data availability and joinability. The local eICU 2.0 snapshot contains ICU-relative respiratory charting with explicit ventilator status values and repeated settings; observed labels include TV/kg IBW, Plateau Pressure, PEEP, Pressure Support and Compliance; patient rows contain height, demographics, hospital/unit identifiers and disposition; and tables join by patientunitstayid, with hospitalid and uniquepid available for transport and clustering.

A bounded scan found 200,234 adult patient-unit stays; 195,536 adults with height 100–250 cm; 29,975 adult stays with TV/kg IBW in minutes 0–360; 17,012 with Plateau Pressure; 9,139 with Pressure Support; 36,012 with PEEP; 8,575 with Compliance; 2,382 stays with RT Vent On/Off=Start; and 953 with an Off record. These are availability counts, not eligibility or event-rate results.

Unresolved claim: among adult ICU stays with explicitly documented invasive ventilation active at a six-hour ICU landmark, does a prespecified early burden vector containing body-size-adjusted tidal volume, plateau pressure, PEEP, pressure support and inverse compliance predict a higher 48-hour cumulative incidence of a documented failed liberation transition than a matched tidal-volume-only baseline, after accounting for coverage and measured admission characteristics?

Primary falsifiable hypothesis: the full-burden model has a higher held-out-hospital standardized 48-hour cumulative incidence for documented failure for its prespecified 25th-to-75th burden contrast, with an absolute contrast of at least 2 percentage points; the direction is concordant in the mechanistic model and primary airway/time sensitivities. A null, reversal, calibration harm, non-transport, or persistence after within-stratum burden permutation falsifies or materially weakens the claim.

This is observational prognostic research about a recorded respiratory process. It is not a causal claim about settings, pressure injury, extubation, reintubation or clinical benefit.

## Population, timing and eligibility

Use one row per patientunitstayid. Time zero is ICU admission; all respiratory and disposition offsets are minutes relative to ICU admission.

Primary population: age at least 18, parsing age='> 89' as 90 while retaining an age-censored indicator; valid admissionheight 100–250 cm; unit observation reaches minute 360, meaning unitdischargeoffset is missing or at least 360; at least one first-six-hour O2 Device=Ventilator or Oxygen Delivery Method=Mechanical ventilator row; at least two valid numeric respiratory setting/mechanics observations in minutes 0–360; and explicit ventilation-active status at minute 360. For the latter, sort RT Vent On/Off rows by respchartoffset, respchartentryoffset, respchartid and require the latest status at or before 360 to be Start or Continued, with no later Off/off/Suspended before 360.

A device row without explicit active status is excluded from primary analysis and retained for permissive sensitivity. Tracheostomy is retained in the primary device-status cohort but repeated in an ETT-restricted cohort. Repeated ICU stays are separate observations; stays sharing uniquepid remain in one split, with a uniquepid-clustered sensitivity.

Do not use respiratoryCare ventendoffset=0 as an end event. The scan found only one stay with a nonzero ventendoffset, so respiratoryCare is an airway/audit source, not the primary endpoint source.

## Exposure construction

Use ICU minutes [0,360] and a 30-minute grid. Numeric validity rules are: TV/kg IBW >0 and ≤20 mL/kg; Tidal Volume (set) >0 and ≤2,000 mL; Plateau Pressure >0 and ≤60 cmH2O; PEEP 0–40 cmH2O; Pressure Support 0–50 cmH2O; Compliance >0 and ≤200 mL/cmH2O. Blank, nonnumeric, 0, 9999 and out-of-range values are missing.

Within patientunitstayid and respchartoffset, take the median of valid same-time rows; carry forward at most 30 minutes and never bridge longer gaps. Record rows, unique times, coverage, missingness and longest gap. respchartoffset is primary clinical time; respchartentryoffset is a revised-entry sensitivity.

The tidal-volume-only feature is coverage-weighted mean TV/kg IBW, IQR, slope and coverage indicators. Secondary reconstruction divides Tidal Volume (set) by predicted body weight from admissionheight and gender: male 50 + 0.91(height−152.4), female 45.5 + 0.91(height−152.4) kg; unknown sex is separate.

The full transparent burden vector adds coverage-weighted mean, IQR and early-to-late slope for Plateau Pressure, PEEP, Pressure Support and inverse Compliance, plus component coverage indicators. Standardize continuous features using development-hospital median/IQR only. The equal-weight mean of five standardized components is a reproducible profile-contrast device, not a clinical score.

Both models share age, age-censored indicator, sex, ethnicity, height, admission source, unit type, unit stay type, unit visit number, respiratory-device coverage/status-row count, and first-six-hour heart rate, respiration and SaO2 summaries and coverage from vitalPeriodic. Hospital is split/cluster metadata, not a patient feature. APACHE is sensitivity-only because its timing can overlap exposure.

## Endpoint and competing exits

Primary endpoint: documented ventilator-off followed by restart within 24 hours. Let Off be the first RT Vent On/Off value Off or off at t_off≥360. Failure is the first later Start with 0<t_start−t_off≤1,440 minutes, with the fixed ledger’s intervening-status rules. The report must use the label “documented restart within 24 hours of documented ventilator-off status.” Do not call it extubation failure, reintubation or successful liberation.

A durable-off transition is off plus 1,440 minutes without Start/restart and with a subsequent respiratory status row at or after that time, or nonfatal unit discharge at or after that time. It is labelled “documented ventilator-off interval without recorded restart.” No qualifying Off means neither success nor failure; report this censoring and a secondary time-to-first-Off estimand.

Other exits are recorded unit death; alive unit discharge/transfer; and respiratory-status observation loss, defined by no subsequent RT Vent On/Off row within the ICU observation boundary. These are not negative liberation outcomes. Use end_i=min(2880, unitdischargeoffset) for valid nonnegative unit discharge offsets. Missing/negative disposition never implies 48-hour observation; use the last defensible respiratory timestamp and label the remainder unknown. Death wins ties.

The primary estimand is the development-fitted, standardized 48-hour cumulative incidence of documented restart before durable off, death, alive exit or respiratory censoring, contrasting 25th versus 75th burden profiles at the common adjustment distribution. Report cause-specific and Aalen–Johansen sensitivities.

## Analysis, uncertainty and falsification

Fix a deterministic hospital split before fitting: SHA-256 of salt eicu-vent-20260914 plus hospitalid, first 8 hex characters, 80% development and 20% held-out transport. Require at least 10 hospitals in each role and 100 landmark stays/20 endpoint events in each evaluation role; otherwise report inconclusive feasibility.

Fit B_TV, a transparent 30-minute discrete-time competing-risk pooled-logistic model with tidal-volume-only features and common adjustment; B_FULL, the identical model adding the full mechanics vector and burden profile; and M_STATE, a piecewise-exponential multi-state model on the identical cohort/ledger with active ventilation, documented off, durable off, restart, death, alive exit and unknown censoring. M_STATE has separate active→off and off→restart hazards and reveals whether burden changes reaching off, restarting after off, or competing exits.

Use nested development cross-fitting; held-out hospitals are never used for fitting, recalibration, threshold or dictionary changes. Report held-out and out-of-fold time-dependent Brier/integrated Brier, log score where estimable, calibration slope/intercept, calibration-in-the-large, 48-hour CIF and absolute profile contrasts. Use 500 hospital-cluster bootstrap replicates, refitting paired models; repeat uniquepid clustering where feasible. Preserve cohort, ledger, predictions, fits, split and bootstrap checkpoints with hashes.

Supportive evidence requires a held-out full-minus-TV contrast ≥2 percentage points with a two-sided hospital-cluster interval excluding zero; no material calibration deterioration; concordant M_STATE pathway direction; stability in ETT, revised-entry, noninvasive-device exclusion and second-split analyses; placebo permutation removes the contrast; and no one label/hospital dominates.

Adverse evidence includes null/reversed contrast, calibration harm, non-transport, model disagreement, disappearance after coverage adjustment, one-source dependence, similar gain after within-stratum permutation, or association confined to time-to-Off. Inconclusive means sparse off/restart sequences, excessive censoring, poor overlap, unstable status semantics, convergence failure or wide intervals. Neither adverse nor inconclusive proves safety or ineffectiveness.

Falsification sensitivities: restart windows 6 and 48 hours; durable-off windows 12 and 48 hours; Suspended excluded versus treated as off; ETT restriction; explicit O2 Device=Ventilator only; revised-entry clock; and a negative-control respiratory transition. Preserve the six-hour information boundary.

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema/header hashes, joins, values, eligibility, feature validity, ordering, split, fits, predictions, scores, calibration, CIFs, bootstrap intervals and permutations. Clinical adjudication or another study is required to determine whether Off means extubation or pause, Start means reintubation or another device transition, charting is complete, an SBT occurred, or a risk estimate changes care. Missing are validated extubation/reintubation, mode continuity, SBT, sedation/delirium, cough/secretions, blood gases, clinician intent, preferences and post-ICU outcomes.
