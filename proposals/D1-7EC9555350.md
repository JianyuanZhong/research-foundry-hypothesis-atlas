# Episode 20 evolution: assay-result content versus observation and capture clocks

Status: design only. No cohort count, fitted result, prevalence, effect estimate, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

Parent: `[prior hypothesis]`. This child keeps the parent’s HCC population, fixed `[t0+43,t0+181)` first-event estimand, O0/O1/O2/O3/O4 provenance classes, `O_planproxy_cap`, `unknown_provenance`, R42/R28 arms, temporal patient-held-out split, uncertainty family, and non-adjudication limits. ## Scientific opening and clinical importance

The strongest claim supported by the inherited evidence is narrow: among patients with a first dated exact-code TACE record and observation through day 42, a pre-index-to-day-42 laboratory trajectory may predict a later operational TACE record better than a day-42 snapshot after measured baseline, assay opportunity, observation, care workflow, and endpoint provenance are considered. O0–O4 and `O_planproxy_cap` are records and capture classes. They are not adjudicated TACE intent, completion, indication, radiographic response, progression, failure, survival, benefit, or utility.

The remaining rival is not only “planned care.” Even a closed-coherent O4 may be easier to capture in patients with dense, promptly timestamped documentation. A GRU can exploit the fact that a test was ordered, which label was ordered, and when rows appeared, even if its apparent gain is called an assay trajectory. Conversely, a result-value pattern could add information beyond the same observation schedule. The available HCC schema permits this distinction at the record level: labs contain result fields and a lab time; encounters, examinations, orders, and medications contain separate observation/order times; procedures contain start and end times. The schema does not provide units/reference ranges, imaging, intent, or response.

This changes the next clinical decision. If result content adds held-out information after the identical observation clock is supplied, the next useful study is blinded adjudication of indication/completion and imaging response. If the gain is carried by the observation clock or is concentrated in one procedure-capture-latency regime, the assay/biology interpretation should be abandoned in favor of workflow and data-acquisition research. Either outcome is informative; neither establishes mechanism or benefit.

The closest inspected works bound this question. [K1] shows that ordered health-record histories can predict future disease but explicitly reports learned bias from training data; this proposal isolates the ordering/collection channel rather than treating sequence gain as biology. [K2] motivates explicit longitudinal selection modeling but does not make weighting causal or provide HCC labels. [K3] distinguishes recorded/calendar, biological, and decision time and warns that temporal architecture does not establish a beneficial clinical action. The present advance is a computable content-versus-clock decomposition and an unchanged-endpoint capture-latency audit.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Primary hypothesis:

> In the inherited HCC index population, assay-result content in the trajectory improves held-out prediction of the fixed O4 closed-coherent TACE proxy beyond the same assay-event timing, test-label, sample-type, missingness, observation, and care-documentation clock; this content increment is not reproduced for the capture-adequate pre-opened-order/no-strict-coherence process pattern `O_planproxy_cap`, and is not confined to one post-procedure capture-latency band.

This is a predictive/descriptive hypothesis about records. “Assay-result content” is deliberately not called biological state because lab units and reference ranges are unavailable. No causal effect, mechanism, treatment response, or clinical utility is claimed.

Use the inherited feature constructions:

- `A0=M0+B0+P_obs+P_care`;
- `A_snap42=A0+S42`;
- `A_traj42=A0+T42`;
- inherited primary trajectory contrast `D_j=L_w(A_snap42,j)-L_w(A_traj42,j)`, positive in the parent’s direction;
- inherited secondary `L_w(A0,j)-L_w(A_traj42,j)`.

Add, without changing those estimands:

- `C42): the observation/documentation clock through the day-42 landmark. For every lab event it contains event presence, `test` label, `specimen type`, parsed event time, inter-event/relative time, value-present/missingness indicators, and assay opportunity. It also contains pre-landmark row counts, distinct source-family counts, encounter-day coverage, and timestamp/status completeness from encounters, examinations, orders, and medications. It contains no `qualitative result` or `quantitative result` value.
- `R42): the matched assay-result-content channel at the same lab events: `Qualitative Result` and `Quantitative Result`, with assay-specific parsing and missingness retained. Numeric values are never pooled across assays because the catalog has no unit/reference-range field; raw qualitative strings are not interpreted as clinical response.
- `A_clock=A0+C42`; `A_full=A0+C42+R42`. The two models see identical patients, lab event times, opportunity, missingness, encounter/source coverage, folds, weights, masks, and endpoint classes. Only result content differs.
- `G_{j}=L_w(A_clock,j)-L_w(A_full,j)), a result-content increment for endpoint (j). Positive (G_j) means content improves loss beyond the same observation clock. Report `G_O4`, `G_{O_planproxy_cap}`, and the inherited `D_O4) jointly. The process-control contrast is not a clinical outcome.

The timing repair is an outcome audit, not a new endpoint. For each O4/O3 candidate, calculate `u_min-tp`, where `u_min` is the earliest valid qualifying post-procedure examination/order/medication time already allowed by O3, and calculate `te-tp` from the same procedure row. Report the unchanged O4 label in prespecified capture-latency bands ([0,24]) hours and ((24,72]) hours, plus closure-invalid/missing and unknown-provenance reasons. These bands are descriptive strata for the same O4 first-event target; they never relabel a candidate, change censoring, or enter predictors. A positive result confined to one supported band is a capture-timing warning, not biological support.

## Exact inherited provenance classes

The following classes are frozen from the parent and are not proliferated or redefined.

- **O0:** first later unique literal-code TACE.
- **O1:** O0 satisfying the frozen semantic hepatic-arterial procedure rule plus valid same-patient/same-visit timed examination or order corroboration.
- **O2:** O1 plus frozen same-visit medication corroboration.
- **O3:** O2 plus at least one qualifying examination, order, or medication row on the exact visit key with nonmissing event/start time `u` strictly after `tp) and no later than `tp+72 hours`. An order must have nonblank `order_status` and must not contain normalized cancellation tokens `cancel`, `void`, `revoke`, or `discontinue`.
- **O4 closed-coherent:** O3 plus the same later `procedures` row has parseable `end time=te` and (0 le te-tp le 72) hours. Zero duration is allowed for timestamp granularity. Missing, negative, malformed, or longer closure is `closure_unknown`, not a negative clinical event.
- **`O_planproxy_cap`:** a later literal TACE candidate with a same-visit order whose `order time` is valid and in ([tp-7,tp)), with parent-defined post-window capture-adequate=1, complete provenance audit, no O4 classification, and no qualifying O3 post-procedure examination/order/medication. Required post-source time and order-status fields must be present and valid; otherwise the candidate is `unknown_provenance), never planned-only.
- **capture-adequate:** unchanged parent rule: valid `encounters.Discharge Time ge tp+72h), or at least one non-procedure row with valid time in ((tp,tp+72h]) in examinations/orders/medications/labs. This is an observable capture indicator, not proof of complete capture.
- If O4 and the pre-opened-order rule overlap, retain O4 and an overlap flag. O3-only, closure-unknown, and capture-unknown remain visible. No absence of a downstream row is treated as evidence of intent.

The new `u_min-tp) latency audit uses only rows already eligible for O3. It cannot upgrade O0/O1/O2, turn a missing time into a negative event, or be used as a predictor.

## Baseline versus learned alternative

Both methods use the exact same eligible patients, O0–O4/`O_planproxy_cap` labels, risk grid, right-censoring masks, R42/R28 weights, latency audit, and temporal split. Endpoint labels and post-`tp) capture fields never enter predictors.

Transparent baseline: fit the inherited elastic-net pooled-logistic discrete-time hazard with intercept and risk-bin indicators. Fit, only inside grouped development folds, alpha=0.5, lambda, assay-specific robust parsing/scaling, imputation, missingness indicators, and R42 selection weights. Fit matched variants `A0`, `A_snap42`, `A_traj42`, `A_clock`, and `A_full). The scientific baseline comparison is `A_clock) versus `A_full): it holds the observation clock fixed and adds only result content. The inherited snapshot-versus-trajectory contrast remains reported, not replaced.

Learned alternative: use the parent’s compact time-aware GRU with explicit two-channel inputs. The clock channel receives lab label, sample type, relative/inter-event time, opportunity, event/missingness flags, and pre-landmark observation/care summaries. The result channel receives only parsed qualitative/numeric assay content and an assay-specific missing-content flag. One GRU layer, hidden size 32, dropout 0.10, head 32/16, Adam 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, at most 50 epochs, eight-epoch early stopping, and fixed seeds 17/29/41 are retained. Fit clock-masked and full-content variants on identical folds and weights. A full-model gain without a clock-matched content increment is representation-sensitive workflow prediction, not assay evidence.

The baseline is selected for transparent source-family, missingness, weight, and content-block auditability. The GRU is retained because irregular ordering and nonlinear interactions could reveal information lost by pooled summaries; its ablation makes that alternative scientifically interpretable. A transformer, narrative NLP, mechanistic response model, or latent biological model is deferred: the HCC snapshot lacks validated response/intent labels, images, assay units/reference ranges, and externally adjudicated biology. A cross-fitted nuisance-residualization analysis is also deferred as a primary method: without a validated observation model it risks converting extrapolation into apparent “residual biology”; the matched clock/content ablation is more directly auditable. These are recorded deferrals, not claims of infeasibility.

Approximate future solver workload: source materialization and deterministic audits 4 CPUs/16 GiB for 1–3 hours; elastic-net and paired bootstrap/permutations 4 CPUs/16 GiB for 1–3 hours; three-seed GRU 4 CPUs/16 GiB for 2–5 hours. These are planning estimates, not measured results. CPU is adequate for the transparent fit; the GRU may use one allocated A100 only if the solver requests it through `job_submit`, explicitly moves tensors to `cuda:0`, and records the allocation. Ordinary-shell CUDA visibility is not a hardware test. The planning envelope is 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, up to 8 GPUs, and 28,800 seconds; no discovery fit or solver/model training has been run here.

Frozen falsifiers:

1. Remove all assay result content but retain exact lab event timing, labels, sample types, opportunity, missingness, and observation/care clock; this is the clock-only model, not a post-hoc sensitivity.
2. Within patient, permute result content among lab events while preserving event times, label, sample type, missingness, and availability. A persisting `G_O4) falsifies content specificity.
3. Remove late assay rows in ((t0+35,t0+42]) while retaining earlier content and the clock.
4. Compare R42 with R28 and ordinary weighting.
5. Compare the unchanged O4 label with inherited O0/O1/O2/O3 and `O_planproxy_cap`; do not create latency-specific endpoints.
6. Report the same `G) increment in the two fixed capture-latency bands. If only one supported band has events, the latency distinction is unresolved, not supportive.
7. Fit a timing/source-only model using no lab result values. If it matches the full model, the assay claim fails even if the parent `D_O4) is positive.

## Decision gates and interpretation

**Supportive for a record-level assay-content increment:** all of the following must hold in the untouched test set: (a) `G_O4) has a positive simultaneous lower 95% bound in both elastic-net and GRU, with direction retained in ordinary and R42-weighted analyses and the R28/late-window controls; (b) O4 counts, closure support, paired availability, ESS, and weight tails pass the inherited support gates; (c) `G_{O_planproxy_cap}) has an upper simultaneous 95% bound at or below zero, and the same clock/content comparison does not show a positive result for the process pattern; (d) the O4 result is not confined to one supported `u_min-tp) band; and (e) the within-patient content permutation and timing-only falsifiers do not reproduce the increment. The inherited `D_O4) may be concordant, but a snapshot-versus-trajectory gain without the content increment is not supportive of assay-result information.

A supportive result establishes only that recorded assay-result content adds held-out information for a narrower operational O4 record after the measured observation clock, and is not similarly predictive of the capture-adequate pre-opened-order process pattern. It does not establish that the assay is biological, clinically interpreted, treatment-related, causal, responsive, beneficial, or actionable.

**Adverse:** `G_{O_planproxy_cap}) has a positive simultaneous lower bound; the timing-only model matches full content; the content permutation survives; O4 `G) disappears when closure or post-source timing is audited; the gain appears in only one method/weight/seed; or it is concentrated in one supported capture-latency band. These results favor documentation intensity, procedure-capture timing, or representation sensitivity. They do not identify which unmeasured workflow mechanism caused the pattern.

**Inconclusive:** O4 or process-control events/ESS are inadequate; closure or capture unknowns dominate; latency strata are unsupported; paired test availability is unstable; simultaneous intervals are wide; R42/R28 disagree; weight tails are extreme; methods disagree; or available fields cannot support reliable result parsing. Do not alter the population, time window, provenance rules, latency bands, endpoint classes, or model after test scoring. The next step is better capture or blinded clinical adjudication, not another endpoint variant.

## Alternatives and revisit rule

The unchosen alternatives are explicit. A cross-fitted observation-model residualization could be revisited if a validated capture model or external adjudicated outcome becomes available; revisit only if it improves separation without changing the fixed estimand. A transformer or latent biological model could be revisited after validated assay units, sufficient repeated measures, and clinical outcome labels are added. Narrative NLP and imaging models require unavailable full text/images and are deferred. If the clock/content increment is adverse or inconclusive, do not rescue the direction with these models; acquire adjudication/capture evidence first.
