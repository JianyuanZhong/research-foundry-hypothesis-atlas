# Route-concealed, target-linked actionability challenge with a pre-registered futility protocol

## Purpose and substantive advance

This is an independent targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`, using repairable `[prior hypothesis]` as the actionability anchor. It preserves the leader's frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity population, exact chronology and source bindings, support atlas, all-unit worst-case routing, finite-population audit, independent holistic challenge, adverse-compatible inference, default-DEFER/no-rescue rules, and noncausal ceiling. It does not redefine the population or the inherited `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER` route precedence.

The clinically consequential unresolved question is narrower than clinical safety: does a reproducible route contain an explicit, target-linked, still-active discharge plan that a prospective silent workflow could present for reconciliation, rather than merely repeating a radiology finding or an already superseded/indeterminate plan? Existing panels can agree on the same wrong abstraction. The new test therefore conceals the candidate route and all component labels from an independently rostered actionability panel, and tests whether a route-defining plan survives a locked target/action/timing/recipient/supersession crosswalk and prespecified counterfactuals.

The strongest claim supported by the records is only that observable text and structured time proxies can be assembled reproducibly. The hypothesis tested here is: among support-qualified atoms that pass all inherited gates, the candidate route will have an explicit active target-linked plan, and no route-defining plan will be contradicted by a later-in-chain superseding or replacement statement under the frozen protocol. A failure excludes the affected atom from silent-validation nomination even if component agreement is high. Agreement is not clinical truth, and the experiment cannot establish that a plan is appropriate, that a responsible clinician endorsed it, or that an action would benefit a patient.

## Immutable pre-execution governance packet

Before reading, sampling, packet construction, transformation, or labels, an independent governance group must accept one complete protocol packet containing the population SQL, route table, annotation manual, every category below, every numerical constant below, sample allocation, reviewer roster and workload, packet mask, control transformation manifest, analysis code hash, and release rules. The packet is signed and hashed as `protocol_hash`; the acceptance record and timestamp are inputs to execution. A run without recorded acceptance is `NO_SELECTION` and cannot be repaired by relabeling.

All categories, labels, thresholds, workload limits, and tie/default rules are immutable after the first packet is opened. No post-label editing, governance-selected threshold, category merging, reviewer retraining, sample top-up, replacement, adaptive allocation, or route-triggered verification is allowed. A clerical correction may append an audit record but cannot alter the frozen ontology, labels, raw text, sampling draw, or release decision. Any attempted substantive edit invalidates the run. The constants are conservative nomination/futility constants chosen to make a retrospective text audit fail closed; they are not estimates of harm, utility, acceptable patient risk, or clinical equivalence, and no utility interpretation may be attached to them.

### Frozen actionability ontology

Each atomic plan mention is coded independently by H1 and H2 with exactly one value in each field; `UNKNOWN` is a valid value and is adverse-compatible. No free-text category is allowed.

* `TARGET`: `PULMONARY_NODULE`, `OTHER_PULMONARY_FINDING`, `NONPULMONARY_FINDING`, `NO_CLINICAL_TARGET`, `MULTIPLE_TARGETS`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `ACTION`: `REPEAT_IMAGING`, `SPECIALIST_REFERRAL`, `PRIMARY_CARE_FOLLOWUP`, `PROCEDURE_OR_BIOPSY`, `TREATMENT`, `MONITOR_WITHOUT_SPECIFIED_ACTION`, `NO_ACTION`, `OTHER_ACTION`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `TIMING`: `WITHIN_24H`, `DAYS_2_7`, `DAYS_8_30`, `DAYS_31_90`, `AFTER_90_DAYS`, `UNSPECIFIED`, `UNKNOWN`. These are text/time bins, not clinical urgency classes.
* `RECIPIENT`: `PATIENT_OR_CAREGIVER`, `PRIMARY_CARE`, `PULMONOLOGY`, `OTHER_SPECIALIST`, `INPATIENT_TEAM`, `RADIOLOGY`, `OTHER_EXPLICIT_ROLE`, `NO_RECIPIENT`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `RESPONSIBILITY`: `EXPLICIT_NAMED_ROLE`, `EXPLICIT_SERVICE`, `PATIENT_SELF`, `SHARED_OR_UNSPECIFIED`, `NONE`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `POLARITY`: `RECOMMEND`, `CONDITIONAL_RECOMMEND`, `COMPLETED`, `NEGATED`, `UNCERTAIN`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `PLAN_STATE_AT_D`: `ACTIVE_AT_DEPARTURE`, `COMPLETED_BEFORE_D`, `SUPERSEDED_BEFORE_D`, `EXPIRED_BY_STATED_TIME`, `ABSENT`, `INDETERMINATE`.
* `RELATION_TO_PRIOR_PLAN`: `SUPERSEDES`, `SUPPLEMENTS`, `REPEATS`, `CONFLICTS_WITH`, `NO_PRIOR_PLAN`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `EVIDENCE_STATUS`: `EXPLICIT_TEXT`, `STRUCTURED_TIME_ONLY`, `INFERRED`, `MISSING`. Only `EXPLICIT_TEXT` can satisfy an actionability nomination gate; structured time is used for chronology and never upgrades text.

The complete route-concealed panel output is one of `ACTIONABLE`, `NOT_ACTIONABLE`, `SUPERSEDED_OR_CONFLICTING`, or `INDETERMINATE`, plus the atomic fields and a confidence field with exactly `HIGH`, `LOW`, or `UNKNOWN`. `ACTIONABLE` requires an explicit target-linked plan with `TARGET=PULMONARY_NODULE`, an action other than `NO_ACTION`/`UNKNOWN`, a stated timing bin other than `UNSPECIFIED`/`UNKNOWN`, an explicit recipient or responsibility, `POLARITY=RECOMMEND` or `CONDITIONAL_RECOMMEND`, and `PLAN_STATE_AT_D=ACTIVE_AT_DEPARTURE`; a prior plan marked `SUPERSEDES`, `CONFLICTS_WITH`, or otherwise unresolved makes the case `SUPERSEDED_OR_CONFLICTING` or `INDETERMINATE`, never actionable. A completed plan is not an active discharge plan. These are reproducible nomination definitions, not judgments that the action was medically indicated.

### Frozen numerical gates and their interpretation

The following constants are locked in the governance packet. They are conservative nomination/futility cutoffs, not estimated harm utilities or probabilities of clinical benefit.

1. An atom must have at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 actionability-panel admissions in that route when feasible; otherwise it is `INCONCLUSIVE/DEFER`.
2. The design-based, simultaneous 95% lower bound for exact H1/H2 actionability-category agreement must be at least `0.90` for any route allow-list nomination.
3. The simultaneous 95% upper bound for panel-to-parent route-defining discordance is at most `0.05` for `AUTO` and `TIMING`, and at most `0.10` for `RECON` and `DEFER`.
4. The simultaneous 95% upper bound for a route-invariant control flip is at most `0.02`; for a route-decisive control violation it is at most `0.05`.
5. The simultaneous 95% upper bound for packet missingness, nonresponse, or control infeasibility is at most `0.05`; any unknown or missing label is adverse-compatible rather than complete-case deleted.
6. For `AUTO`, every adverse-compatible actionability label for every unit must remain `ACTIONABLE`; one `NOT_ACTIONABLE`, `SUPERSEDED_OR_CONFLICTING`, or `INDETERMINATE` unit forces `RECON` or `DEFER` under the inherited all-unit precedence. For `RECON`, an adverse-compatible unresolved unit may remain `RECON` but cannot be promoted to `AUTO`.
7. The fixed actionability review budget is 1,980 packet-units: 180 primary whole-admission packets read by H1, 180 by H2, 180 by H3, and four locked control packets per primary case read by H1 and H2 (900 packet-units per panel). No panel may exceed this allocation; unused capacity cannot be reallocated.

The `0.90`, `0.10`, `0.05`, `0.02`, 30, 10, 180, four controls, 95%, and 1,980 values are deliberately conservative nomination/futility constants: they define when this retrospective audit is too unreliable to nominate a silent test. They must not be described as a tolerated injury rate, utility tradeoff, or validated safety margin. No kappa, `kappa_max`, shared-error point estimate, latent-class estimate, or nonzero common-error bound is required or used for certification. With no external gold standard, arbitrary common-mode error remains unidentified; unanimous panel labels are therefore compatible with a shared wrong inference and cannot establish clinical correctness.

## Fixed panel workload and locked controls

H1 and H2 each review exactly 180 primary packets plus four controls per case: (i) route-irrelevant section-order/provenance-matched nuisance change, (ii) one-field route-decisive replacement or timing change, (iii) prespecified omission/addition of a target-linked plan, and (iv) formatting/author-token nuisance change. Thus each reads exactly 900 packet-units. H3 reads exactly the 180 untransformed primary packets. The three panels use disjoint rosters, and a fixed three-reviewer roster per panel receives exactly 60 primary cases; H1/H2 each receive 300 packet-units (60 primary plus 240 controls), and H3 receives 60 primary cases. No cases are moved between reviewers, and there is no adaptive second read. Transformations are generated by a locked pre-label manifest only when an unambiguous eligible span exists; otherwise the control is marked infeasible and is adverse-compatible, never manually edited.

For route-irrelevant pairs, target/action/timing/polarity and all route-relevant spans must be unchanged and the expected route and unit inventory must be unchanged. For route-decisive pairs, exactly one frozen atomic field changes and the expected route transition is stated in the manifest. For omission/addition pairs, the compatibility-set transition is frozen before review. Any invariant flip, an unallowed decisive transition, or an actionability change under a nuisance change falsifies the affected atom. Controls test internal route validity and sensitivity, not clinical appropriateness and not a gold standard.

## Analysis without a shared-error bound

The primary estimand is the design-weighted, atom-specific proportion of observed plan units/cases meeting the frozen textual `ACTIONABLE` definition, and the design-weighted proportions of supersession, conflict, indeterminate status, panel discordance, and control violations. Use exact finite-population inversion for binary outcomes and 20,000 Rao-Wu resamples with the inherited weights, retaining all admission-level labels together. Use one simultaneous 95% max-statistic family across inherited gates, atom gates, actionability categories, supersession outcomes, control invariance, missingness, and workload.

For each atom and route, retain the full compatible set of H1/H2/H3 outputs and report adverse-compatible lower and upper endpoints. The lower endpoint treats unresolved and disagreement patterns as failures; the upper endpoint treats every unresolved pattern as adverse-compatible for release. Do not infer truth from majority, consensus, pooled or macro-domain estimates. A latent-class model, if displayed, is diagnostic only and cannot narrow bounds or certify an atom. There is no kappa threshold and no assumed shared-error parameter; because no external reference is available, the report explicitly leaves common-mode error unidentified. This is a deliberate limit, not a missing numerical tuning choice.

## Release, falsification, and interpretation

An atom can be nominated only if all inherited support/event/ESS/weight/simultaneous-bound/workload gates pass, the frozen actionability gates pass, every candidate `AUTO` unit is actionably compatible under the adverse-compatible set, and no route-invariant or route-decisive control fails. Unsupported, rare, unknown, novel, infeasible, or unresolved signatures remain `DEFER`. No pooled, neighboring, sentinel-only, favorable-consensus, imputed, shrinkage, or model-based result rescues a failing atom.

Supportive results mean only that the exact observable MIMIC signature has a reproducible, text-explicit active target-linked plan under this pre-execution protocol and is eligible for a separately approved prospective within-site **silent** validation. Adverse results include a target-linked plan that is absent/indeterminate, a superseding or conflicting plan, a route-invariant flip, a route-decisive violation, or any adverse-compatible label that breaks `AUTO`; the affected atom is excluded. Inconclusive results include sparse strata/atoms, infeasible fixed allocation, missing timestamps, nonresponse, packet/seal failure, wide compatible bounds, or workload failure; inconclusive is neither safety nor transport evidence.

Computationally checkable claims are the population construction, joins, note ordering, canonical artifact selection, route and atom assignment, fixed draw, packet masking, codebook/roster hashes, workload, control transformations, and the reported weighted bounds. Clinical adjudication is still required to decide whether a textual plan is medically appropriate, whether responsibility is real, whether a recommendation was actually intended or superseded, and whether a copied action is desirable. MIMIC cannot establish physical departure visibility, actual viewing/finalization/signature, communication, clinician ownership, patient preferences, outside plans or care, order/referral execution, scheduling, follow-up, harm, utility, causal benefit, or prospective transport. A subsequent prospective study with actual-calendar workflow logs, viewing/edit/sign/transmission, responsibility, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside-plan and patient-outcome evidence is required before any live action, safety, utility, causal, or clinical-benefit claim.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Compared with the valid parent, this child adds a route-concealed actionability/supersession measurement layer but fixes the prior child's validity defects: the full ontology and all operational constants are frozen and governance-accepted before labels; constants are explicitly nomination/futility conventions rather than harm utilities; the panel workload and 180-case stratum allocation are fixed with no adaptive rescue; and certification uses no unjustified kappa or shared-error bound. The target-linked superseding-plan and counterfactual controls remain falsification tests rather than truth claims. The unresolved common-mode clinical error, actual workflow behavior, appropriateness, and benefit remain outside what retrospective MIMIC can identify.
