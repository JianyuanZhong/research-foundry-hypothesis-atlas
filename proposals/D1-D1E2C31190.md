# Exact eICU ventilation care-plan labels and a persistent, independently defined respiratory patient-state trajectory

## Status and substantive advance

This is a targeted child of the assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen parent denominator, first qualifying stay rule, five landmarks, exact two labels, label timing, inherited pre-landmark invasive/physiologic/end-of-life exclusions, and the completed adverse transportability findings. It does not reinterpret those findings as bedside truth.

The parents establish that the two literal `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue` strings are sparse and hospital-concentrated, that a previously used 12-hour chart-setting association was dominated by peak-pressure charting, that non-peak and sedation mappings reverse across hospitals, and that durable liberation cannot be ascertained from the available independent markers. The unresolved clinically important question is narrower: **within the frozen labelled person-landmarks, does receipt of either exact label contain reproducible information about a subsequent persistent respiratory patient-state trajectory, beyond the state and documentation opportunity already visible before the label?**

The substantive advance is a care-plan-blinded, temporally separated, multi-domain endpoint with an explicit observation/missingness estimand, competing-exit accounting, and a predeclared hospital-held-out analysis. A transient or copied single setting is not allowed to define improvement. A positive computation would support only incremental information in this structured chart construct. It would not establish a daily extubation evaluation, spontaneous breathing trial, extubation readiness, treatment effect, quality, benefit, or harm.

## Evidence boundary and hypothesis

The strongest claims already supported by the lineage are limited to exported structured documentation: conditional label use and its available chart mappings do not meet unqualified multicenter transportability criteria in this eICU snapshot; peak pressure drives most of the earlier composite; and independent liberation validation is sparse and failed its prespecified ascertainment requirements. Those claims are not evidence that clinicians did or did not perform an evaluation.

The untested claim is that, conditional on the locked labelled denominator and pre-label state/opportunity, the `evaluation` label is associated with a higher probability of a persistent, safety-preserving, non-peak respiratory support de-escalation than the `no_trial` label, and that the label improves hospital-held-out prediction over a model that has the same pre-label state and opportunity variables but no label. The falsifying alternatives are that the contrast is absent after baseline adjustment, is already present before the label, is explained by observation opportunity, is driven by one component or site, reverses under hospital holdout, or is not estimable because support is too sparse.

This is a descriptive conditional association/information estimand, not a causal treatment effect. No exchangeability, positivity, consistency, or bedside semantic assumption is asserted.

## Primary endpoint: persistent safety-preserving non-peak support de-escalation

The primary endpoint uses no `carePlanGeneral` values, care-plan counts, care-plan group strings, notes, treatment rows, or post-D exposure features. It is assembled from `respiratoryCharting`, `respiratoryCare`, and `vitalPeriodic`.

### Baseline and outcome windows

For each row, define:

* baseline state window `B = (D-720,D]`;
* pre-D trajectory window `P = (D-1440,D-720]` versus B;
* post-label observation window `O = (D+360,D+1800]` (24 hours after a six-hour washout from D);
* prespecified horizon sensitivity `O12 = (D+360,D+1080]`.

The six-hour washout prevents the classification-time chart from being counted as the response. `respchartoffset`, `respcarestatusoffset`, and `observationoffset` are clinical offsets; `respchartentryoffset` is never substituted for clinical time.

For each numeric component, parse only finite numeric values after trimming the eICU value representation. Apply fixed, field-specific physiologic bounds frozen before looking at label contrasts. Values outside bounds, empty/non-numeric values, and ambiguous textual values become missing; they cannot be imputed from a different component. Preserve the exact bounds and parse counts in an artifact. Do not infer unit conversions beyond the eICU published convention.

A component is eligible for a window only if it has at least two valid observations with distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. For an eligible window use the median of all valid observations. Thus one copied value cannot create a transition. Record count, unique timestamp count, first/last offset, median, inter-record gap, and parse/missing reason for every component-window.

### Support domain

Peak inspiratory pressure is excluded from the primary endpoint because the completed parent audit found it was the dominant and semantically uncertain contributor to the earlier composite. Define support de-escalation as at least one of the following baseline-to-post median reductions, with no requirement that the other support components be observed:

* FiO2 decreases by at least 0.05;
* PEEP or PEEP-CPAP decreases by at least 2 cm H2O;
* pressure support decreases by at least 2 cm H2O;
* set respiratory rate decreases by at least 2 breaths/min.

The component family mapping (including exact case-insensitive/whitespace-normalized labels) must be outcome-blind and frozen before arm-specific counts. The executable must enumerate every respiratoryCharting `respchartvaluelabel` encountered in a full scan, record matched and unmatched labels, and fail verification if a claimed family is not reproducibly mapped. `peak inspiratory pressure` may be calculated only as a secondary ablation, never silently included in the primary.

### Safety domain

Use `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration`, with the same two-observation/separation rule and medians in B and O. Among signals available in both windows, safety is preserved when:

* median `sao2` does not fall by more than 2 percentage points; and
* median `respiration` does not rise by more than 4 breaths/min.

If either signal is observed in both windows and violates its threshold, the row fails the safety domain. If one signal is entirely unavailable in both windows, classify the row as endpoint-missing rather than as a safe non-event; a safety-preserving claim cannot be made from no safety measurement. A sensitivity uses both signals required and a second sensitivity uses at least one signal required, with both frozen in advance.

### Airway corroboration and outcome states

During O, require at least one `respiratoryCare` record with a nonmissing `airwaytype` or a nonmissing ventilator interval among `ventstartoffset`, `ventendoffset`, `priorventstartoffset`, and `priorventendoffset`. This is a source-availability/corroboration requirement, not proof of device delivery. The primary state is:

1. **persistent improvement**: support domain and safety domain pass, with airway corroboration;
2. **observed non-improvement**: the complete-window criteria are met but the primary improvement condition is false, including a support reduction with safety failure;
3. **endpoint missing**: minimum observations or safety availability fail;
4. **competing exit**: ICU discharge/death/truncation occurs before the end of O, including discharge at or before `D+1800` for the primary horizon.

Do not recode discharge, death, or truncation as respiratory improvement or ordinary non-improvement. A row with no complete O but a competing exit is competing exit, not generic missingness. Use `patient.unitdischargeoffset` and `unitdischargestatus`/`unitdischargelocation` to report exit type. `unitdischargeoffset <= D+360` is additionally reported as no post-washout opportunity.

The primary risk contrast is the evaluation-minus-no-trial risk difference for persistent improvement among complete, non-competing endpoint observations. This conditional contrast is accompanied by a joint four-state distribution over all 540 rows and an observation/competing-exit contrast; no complete-case result may be presented without those denominators. The predictive estimand uses all rows with missingness and competing exits explicitly modeled as non-target outcome categories or, in the binary sensitivity, inverse-observation weights estimated only from pre-D information.

A secondary “support-only” endpoint removes the safety requirement, and a secondary “setting-only/no airway corroboration” endpoint removes the airway requirement. These cannot replace the primary endpoint. An airway transition sensitivity uses only a pre-frozen, exact literal rule: a time-ordered `respiratoryCare.airwaytype` change to the exact value `No Artificial Airway`, or a pre-frozen exact normalized `treatment.treatmentstring` ETT-removal candidate followed by no invasive-airway respiratoryCare record for 48 hours. It is validation context only; if fewer than 10% of rows in each arm have the sensitivity observed, it is unavailable/inconclusive and no association is estimated.

## Hospital-held-out estimand, clustering, and model specification

The primary transport estimand is leave-one-hospital-out prediction of the primary endpoint and its observation/competing-exit states. For each held-out hospital h, fit preprocessing, imputation, standardization, regularization, and any observation weights using training hospitals only. Do not fit a target rate, label rate, or calibration intercept using held-out outcomes. Report:

* row-weighted micro log loss, Brier score, and calibration slope/intercept;
* equal-hospital macro log loss and Brier score, giving each held-out hospital equal weight;
* AUROC only where both outcome classes occur and with an explicit availability count;
* observed risk difference and predicted risk difference for evaluation versus no-trial;
* hospital-specific directions, endpoint availability, label-arm counts, and competing/missingness rates.

The primary comparison is state-plus-label versus the identical state-plus-opportunity model without label. The secondary comparison is versus opportunity-only. A model cannot be credited for improvement when its apparent gain is solely a different missingness prediction; report separate discrimination/calibration for the four-state observation process.

Because the frozen denominator has repeated landmarks within a stay, uncertainty is obtained by a stay-cluster bootstrap, not row bootstrap. Resample `sid` clusters within training and held-out hospital strata, preserving all landmarks of a sampled stay. Refit the complete pipeline in each replicate for the primary comparison; use 1,000 replicates if feasible, otherwise at least 500 with the shortfall reported before inspecting results. Use fixed seed `61061`. A sensitivity uses a stay-cluster permutation of labels within hospital-by-landmark strata, only where both labels are present; preserve arm counts and all outcome/opportunity rows. Run 1,000 permutations if at least 20 valid strata exist; otherwise report the exact number and mark the permutation gate inconclusive rather than manufacturing a null distribution.

### Feasibility rules for hospital holdout

Do not call an analysis “hospital-held-out” merely because a hospital ID was placed in a test column. Before fitting, publish a support table for every hospital with counts of each label, complete primary endpoint observations, competing exits, endpoint missingness, and each four-state outcome. The primary label-contrast transport gate requires at least five hospitals with at least 10 complete endpoint-observed rows under each label and at least 50% of complete endpoint-observed rows in those hospitals. If fewer than five hospitals meet this requirement, the label-contrast transport estimand is **inconclusive**, not a negative clinical result; the earlier parent transportability adverse result may still be reported separately as inherited evidence.

For folds with only one label in training or testing, do not use artificial relabeling or a forced contrast. Such folds contribute to broad prediction metrics only if the comparison is defined for the observed test distribution; they do not contribute a within-hospital arm contrast. If no held-out hospital has both arms with complete endpoint support, report no risk contrast and no label-added transport claim.

## Falsification and robustness tests

1. **Pre-D placebo trajectory.** Apply the same component thresholds, two-observation requirement, median rule, safety rule, and airway corroboration to P versus B. The label is not permitted to define this outcome. A pre-D evaluation/no-trial contrast comparable to the post-D contrast is adverse to a newly emerging post-label interpretation.
2. **Lead-time placebo.** Reclassify the model exposure time as `D-720` while retaining the O window relative to D. A similar held-out gain indicates pre-existing trajectory or timing leakage.
3. **Component ablation.** Remove FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, set rate, sao2, and respiration one at a time; separately remove the airway corroboration requirement. A result that survives only with peak pressure, one field, one source family, or one site is not supportive.
4. **Opportunity negative control.** Predict post-window source density, unique times, and entry lag from the label and pre-D variables. A label effect on opportunity without a corresponding value-based trajectory gain supports documentation opportunity, not patient-state information.
5. **Permutation control.** Compare the observed held-out increment to the within-hospital-by-landmark permutation distribution. If valid strata are too few, mark this test unavailable; do not interpret lack of permutations as support.
6. **Airway-bound sensitivities.** Recompute using latest invasive evidence within 24, 12, and 6 hours before L, preserving the exact parent cohort where the parent artifacts provide those variants. A change in denominator is not a post-hoc tuning choice and must be reported with all attrition.
7. **Observation weighting.** Estimate inverse probability of complete post-window observation from pre-D state/opportunity, hospital, and landmark only; truncate at fixed 1st/99th percentiles. Compare complete-window and weighted results. Extreme weights, failed positivity, or strong arm-specific competing exits make the result inconclusive.
8. **Disjoint label/time audit.** Verify no care-plan value, post-D respiratory value, post-D source count, treatment string, or endpoint-derived feature enters baseline. Verify that all windows use offsets and that equality at boundaries follows the exact open/closed definitions above.
9. **Negative control for copied persistence.** Require two separated observations as primary; repeat with a stricter 60-minute separation and with first/last rather than median only as sensitivity. A large change indicates chart persistence/artifact sensitivity.

## Prespecified result gates

These gates distinguish supportive information, adverse evidence, and lack of computable support. They are not p-value thresholds and do not establish clinical truth.

### Supportive only if all primary requirements hold

The result can be called computationally supportive of incremental label information only if, in the primary unbounded endpoint and at least two of the 24-, 12-, and 6-hour pre-L invasive-evidence sensitivities:

* at least five hospitals meet the 10-per-label complete-endpoint support rule and at least 50% of complete rows arise there, with no arm dominated by a single hospital beyond a prespecified 40% share;
* the evaluation-minus-no-trial persistent-improvement risk difference is positive, with at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions positive;
* state-plus-label improves both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss over state-plus-opportunity by at least 0.01, and the stay-cluster 95% interval for the increment excludes zero in the favorable direction;
* the label increment is not explained by the pre-D or lead-time placebo, does not fall within the valid permutation null, and survives the component/source ablations without leave-one-hospital-out reversal;
* missingness/competing-exit differences are reported and do not account for the value-based gain; and
* independent airway corroboration is present in at least 10% of rows in both arms. If this last requirement fails, the result is at most partially supportive/inconclusive, never fully supportive.

Even all gates passing supports only incremental predictive information in this eICU chart construct. It does not support actual SBT, extubation, readiness, appropriateness, treatment effect, quality, or patient benefit.

### Adverse computational result

The patient-state-information claim is adverse only when adequate endpoint support exists and at least one prespecified failure is reproduced in a bounded sensitivity: the label increment is no better than state-plus-opportunity; the risk difference or held-out increment reverses across hospitals; the observed increment is no larger than the pre-D/lead-time placebo or lies within a valid permutation null; the result disappears after a component/source ablation; or label-associated post-D documentation density is present without a value-based trajectory gain. An adverse result means the labels are not validated as incremental features for this endpoint in this snapshot. It does not prove absence of bedside evaluation or a documentation artifact.

### Inconclusive result

Call the experiment inconclusive when fewer than five hospitals meet the complete endpoint support rule; when no valid held-out arm contrast exists; endpoint/missingness/competing exits are too sparse or nonpositive; airway corroboration is unavailable; bounded definitions disagree; intervals span meaningful gain and no gain; or the execution cannot verify the parent artifacts, source hashes, label mapping, or window boundaries. Inconclusive is the correct result for an underpowered or unobservable question. No relaxed threshold, outcome-guided endpoint, pooled row-level bootstrap, or post-hoc label expansion is allowed.
