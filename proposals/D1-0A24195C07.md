# State-coupled union-frame bounds for accession-first documentary-M2 triage

## Episode-16 repair and clinical question

The clinical question remains deliberately bounded: 24 hours before an adult patient's first eligible liver resection for retrospectively pathology-confirmed hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), do semantics already stored in routine CT/MRI reports materially improve a laboratory-independent flag for multidisciplinary review of pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)? The flag is historical risk classification. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment, systemic or locoregional therapy, surveillance, or any other intervention.

This child preserves the parent's source-reconciled operative episodes, interval-valued case-record clocks, accession-first report units, all-row pathology composites, whole-row retention, 24-hour decision boundary with 12/48/72-hour stress tests, 2015–2018 development/2019 tuning/2020–2021 locked test, laboratory-independent B/S models, non-refitted same-fit S0, D=(S,S0), C=(S,B), flag-all and flag-none baselines, exact access frontiers, simultaneous clinical-margin inference, exact source bindings, and non-treatment claim limits.

It repairs the parent's most consequential residual defect. The parent calls its target a whole first-resection frame but removes a patient whenever admissible source/linkage/clock states alter index encounter, period, HCC linkage, or recorded-prior-treatment eligibility. The same unstable patients are removed from pattern-specific access denominators. A frequency cap or a fixed [-1,+1] contribution envelope does not recover the intended period-specific target: it neither couples membership to the episode, report bundle, and pathology outcome from the same state nor propagates those states through fitting and the access frontier. It can also misstate temporal transport by evaluating only the provenance-stable subset.

The repair is a fixed, source-enumerated patient superframe with state-specific eligibility and period membership. No patient with a possible test role is used for fitting or tuning. Every patient who can belong to the 2020–2021 target under at least one admissible state remains in the test superframe; sharp lower and upper ratio endpoints jointly vary target membership, episode, operation time, prior-treatment status, selected accessions, report visibility, pattern, and the pathology label. Thus the estimand is the partially identified mean net-benefit contrast in the actual but unresolved 2020–2021 first-eligible-resection population, not a mean conditional on documentary stability.

Two additional end-to-end repairs are inseparable from that change:

1. If index encounter changes, HCC eligibility and the all-row pathology composite also change. Outcome Y_i(s) is state-specific and may vary only with the same state that supplies membership, predictors, and timing; pathology from one possible encounter can never be paired with imaging from another.
2. Pattern-changing patients enter the CT-only, MRI-only, or dual-access denominator of the state in which that pattern occurs. They are not discarded from access counts or converted to an untracked fixed term. Bootstrap refitting and simultaneous inference nest the entire state optimization, and absence of support is separated from affirmative adverse evidence.

## What is known, what remains unresolved, and why it matters

Direct catalog, schema, header, and aggregate source inspection establishes patient/encounter keys; procedure labels, two procedure sources, and start/end strings; examination accession IDs, acquisition strings, machine values, and eventual report bodies; and untimed same-encounter pathology text. It does not establish that two procedure rows are one clinical operation, that a case-record midnight is an incision time, exact report authored/final/release/view times or version history, pathology specimen linkage or sampling adequacy, biological M2, recurrence, survival, clinician action, treatment effect, harms, costs, or patient benefit.

(2025; DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746, PMCID PMC12309289; frozen source [source checksum], [source checksum]) supports that MVI is pathology-determined, prognostically relevant, and unavailable directly for preoperative planning, while management implications remain controversial. Strongest evidence-supported claim now: the configured data make a historical documentary-M2 information-value experiment technically plausible and show enough procedure-source multiplicity that conditioning the test frame on provenance stability is not scientifically innocuous. Unresolved stored-content hypothesis P-Union: across every admissible realization of the actual 2020–2021 first-eligible-resection population, complete eventual stored CT/MRI semantics yield simultaneous one-sided lower confidence limits above 0.01 net-benefit units at threshold 0.30 for D, C, S versus flag-all, and S versus flag-none, and above zero for D and C at thresholds 0.25, 0.275, 0.30, 0.325, and 0.35.

P-Union concerns complete eventual stored content, not content available to a clinician 24 hours before surgery. T-Union defines a sufficient service-level region conditional on eventual content and adversarial missingness. Neither is a treatment-effect or patient-benefit hypothesis.

## Fixed superframes and leakage-safe temporal roles

Define source-enumerated universe U before model fitting as every patient with at least one admissible first-resection state in an analysis role and at least one state that could satisfy adult/HCC eligibility. Retain all states for each such patient, including ineligible and out-of-period states.

For patient i, let A_i={R_i(s): E_i(s)=1, s in Z_i}. Create disjoint fixed patient superframes:

- U_D: development is the only analysis role in A_i; at least one development-eligible state exists.
- U_T: tuning is the only analysis role in A_i; at least one tuning-eligible state exists.
- U_Q: test is in A_i, regardless of what other roles are also possible.

Any patient in U_Q is purged from U_D and U_T by patient key. A patient whose possible roles are development and tuning but never test is quarantined from both fitting and tuning and reported. Thus no test-possible patient contributes predictors, outcomes, preprocessing, hyperparameter choice, recalibration, permutation strata, or early stopping. This is a conservative model-construction choice, not exclusion from the test target. In the test estimand, every U_Q patient enters whenever the same selected state has E_i(s)=1 and R_i(s)=test and is absent from that realization's denominator otherwise.

Preprocessing and development-frequency categories are learned only from U_D, with one patient weight and all of that patient's admissible development-state values; they are independent of test rows and outcomes. Tuning/recalibration uses only U_T. Rebuild preprocessing in each bootstrap. Report |U_D|, |U_T|, |U_Q|, role-signature counts, minimum and maximum state-specific eligible denominators, and quarantine counts.

## Models and robust pre-test fitting

B uses age, sex, CT/MRI acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category, and—only for exposed accessions—frozen lesion diameter, lesion-count category, and explicit macrovascular thrombus with unknown indicators. S is exactly B plus present/absent/unknown capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity. S0 is never refit: apply S coefficients, preprocessing, penalty, and recalibration after replacing only the semantic block by its frozen state-zero vector. Assert bitwise S=S0 whenever semantic values are zero. W and A=W plus the identical semantic block remain secondary workflow diagnostics; unrestricted text is a ceiling diagnostic only.

For fixed model parameter theta, patient-state logistic loss is maximized over y in Y_i(s) and allowed report masks. Development risk for a global realization is

R_D(theta,z) = [sum_i 1(E_i(s_i)=1,R_i(s_i)=development) max_y,r loss(theta;i,s_i,y,r)] / N_D(z) + penalty(theta),

where the sum is over U_D, z=(s_i), and N_D(z) is the corresponding eligible denominator. Fit B and S by minimizing max_z R_D(theta,z). Hyperparameter selection and recalibration analogously minimize worst state-specific mean loss over U_T; all choices and deterministic optimizer tie-breaks are frozen before test access. Require strictly positive and clinically adequate minimum denominators over all feasible development and tuning realizations.

This finite minimax-of-convex-means problem is computable by an epigraph/cutting-plane solver. Its separation oracle uses binary linear-fractional optimization; for uncoupled patient states, verify it against Dinkelbach root finding and exhaustive enumeration. The model is one frozen robust fit, not a different fit selected after seeing a test state. Equal-state averaging, selecting the observed/eventual state, or treating optional eligibility as fixed is forbidden.

Use 1,000 development/tuning semantic-null repetitions. Permute the whole patient semantic state bundle—not individual fields or states—within frozen pre-test strata defined by stable analysis role, multiset of eventual patterns, state cardinality, and formatting family. Sparse strata follow a prespecified coarsening hierarchy or make the null gate inconclusive. Refit B/S, reconstruct S0, and recalibrate in every repetition. All-stored D at 0.30 must exceed the 97.5th percentile of the process-matched null distribution. Label permutation must be null; post-cutoff text injection, split-accession construction, and deliberate exact-midnight substitution must trigger controls.

## Partially identified test estimands

At threshold p, let w=p/(1-p) and I_Mi(s,r,p)=1[score_Mi(s,r)>=p]. For contrast j=(M1,M0),

d_i^j(y,s,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)] [I_M1i(s,r,p)-I_M0i(s,r,p)].

Primary contrasts are D=(S,S0) and C=(S,B); S versus flag-all and S versus flag-none are required. Compared models use the same patient, state, outcome, and mask. For global realization z, define q_i(s)=1[E_i(s)=1,R_i(s)=test], N_Q(z)=sum_i q_i(s_i), and

Delta_j(z,p)=sum_i q_i(s_i)d_i^j(y_i,s_i,r_i,p) / N_Q(z).

For each contrast, threshold, and access cell, sharp source-identified endpoints are L_j=min Delta_j and U_j=max Delta_j over all coherent patient states, labels, and masks, subject to access constraints and N_Q(z)>0. These are identification bounds, not confidence limits or biological truth. A patient enters numerator and denominator exactly when the same state places that patient in the target.

For all-stored P-Union, every selected nonempty eventual CT/MRI body is exposed. Variable-denominator bounds are solved exactly by fractional programming; independent patient states admit a Dinkelbach/separable oracle. The access problem uses a state-expanded binary linear-fractional MILP through Charnes-Cooper or Dinkelbach outer search with exact MILP separation. Compare production code with exhaustive joint-state enumeration for every generated fixture and random fixture through N<=12, tolerance 1e-10. episode16_partial_identification_reference.py/json demonstrates both a variable-membership ratio fixture and a pattern-changing access fixture; it is reference validation, not a clinical result.

## State-specific access frontier

Use h_g in {0,.05,...,1} for g in {C,M,CM}. In a selected state, C or M is fully accessible only if its sole nonempty selected accession is exposed; CM is fully accessible only if both selected accessions are exposed. CT-only, MRI-only, or neither remain feasible among CM patients not counted fully accessible.

For every global realization and each pattern impose

sum_i q_i(s_i)1[g_i(s_i)=g]full_i >= ceil(h_g N_g(z)),

where N_g(z)=sum_i q_i(s_i)1[g_i(s_i)=g]. If N_g(z)=0, the constraint is vacuous. A pattern-changing patient contributes to the pattern and access denominator selected by that same state. Pattern invariance is neither an exclusion nor a 95% gate. Pattern 0 remains in the whole test denominator.

Compute sharp L/U endpoints for every grid cell jointly over membership, state, pattern, visibility, and outcome. No sorted-cost or separable shortcut is accepted unless proved for variable membership and state-dependent pattern constraints. Apply coordinatewise monotone closure to one frozen simultaneous inferential family and report the raw support set, coverage-preserving upward-closed set, and Pareto-minimal antichain.

## Joint bootstrap and simultaneous inference

Use 2,000 outer patient bootstraps. Resample each patient once with all procedure rows, episode/linkage states, clock cells, accession components, pathology rows, eligibility/role states, and outcome intervals attached. Strata are frozen after state/HCC-eligibility construction but before model-result access: U_D, U_T, and each U_Q possible-role signature crossed with a prespecified coarse source-era signature. A duplicated patient receives bootstrap multiplicity but never inconsistent state draws. Dictionaries and adjudications stay frozen; state construction, preprocessing, robust fitting, tuning/recalibration, S0, all L/U endpoints, sensitivities, and the frontier are rebuilt inside every replicate.

Freeze one model-support family before test outcomes. It contains both identified endpoints for D, C, S-versus-all, and S-versus-none at p=0.30 for all-stored access and every frontier cell; D/C endpoints at all five thresholds for all-stored access and every candidate-support cell; and paired differences used by report-grain and procedure-clock sensitivity gates. Use a max-deviation bootstrap critical value over the entire family, including both directions, with familywise alpha_model=0.025 reserved for later composition with an audit. Report simultaneous lower confidence bounds for every L endpoint and simultaneous upper confidence bounds for every L and U endpoint. Pointwise intervals for a selected cell are not simultaneous.

The state adversary is nested within each replicate. Bootstrapping a favored state, averaging state-specific intervals, fixing the full-sample denominator, or adding uncertainty only after fitting is invalid. More than 2.5% failed refits/optimizations, any empty required denominator, fewer than 1,950 usable replicates, unstable tie-breaking, or failure of the frozen nonregular-endpoint diagnostic makes the family inconclusive. Reference execution verifies arithmetic and reproducibility, not asymptotic coverage.

All gates use the relevant source-identified range, not a favorable state:

- minimum test denominator across realizations at least 1,000;
- at least 75 adjudicated V=1 M2 in every test realization and minimum V=1 fraction at least 85%;

- every unresolved episode/linkage possibility represented as a state; uncertainty prevalence is reported but is not an exclusion gate;
- nonempty text in at least 80% of selected CT/MRI accessions and size/count extraction in at least 70% of nonempty accessions, using worst-state fractions;
- adequate minimum development/tuning denominators and event counts, frozen before test;
- each pattern needs minimum N_g>=100 and at least 20 V=1 M2 across every realization for a pattern-specific clinical statement;
- zero laboratory or forbidden post-decision lineage, exact deduplication, all join multiplicities reported, and all controls passing.

Retain raw-earliest-procedure-row and midnight-as-exact sensitivities. If either changes selected CT/MRI identity in at least 5% of possible test patients, changes any required all-stored L endpoint by at least 0.005, or changes a P/T result zone, label evidence clock-sensitive and prohibit a semantic-value or 24-hour boundary conclusion pending source-system adjudication. Sensitivities cannot rescue the primary.

## Supportive, adverse, mixed, and inconclusive zones

Supportive P-Union: all gates pass; at p=0.30 simultaneous lower confidence bounds for sharp lower endpoints L of D, C, S-versus-all, and S-versus-none exceed 0.01; simultaneous lower bounds for L_D and L_C exceed zero at all five thresholds; semantic-null and sensitivity gates pass. Conclude only that complete eventual stored report semantics made materially favorable actions within frozen S and complete S beat B/defaults in this historical source-uncertain documentary-M2 target under every represented state.

Supportive T-Union without audit: P-Union passes and at least one access cell passes identical simultaneous requirements after state-specific access constraints and monotone closure. Conclude only that the reported access vector would suffice under adversarial membership, episode/clock/linkage, pattern, visibility, and documentary outcome assignment.

Adverse to P-Union: with feasibility intact, the simultaneous upper confidence bound for a required sharp lower endpoint L is at or below its criterion, 0.01 at p=0.30 or zero for a plateau requirement. This falsifies the all-admissible-state guarantee. If the simultaneous upper bound for corresponding best-state endpoint U is also at or below the criterion, the result is uniformly adverse. A negative U upper bound indicates harmful action changes, not merely absence of material benefit.

Adverse to T-Union: for every grid cell, at least one required L endpoint has a simultaneous upper confidence bound at or below its criterion. This falsifies the prespecified claim that any grid vector guarantees margins. An empty support set based only on lower confidence limits is not adverse evidence.

Mixed: D supportive/C adverse means semantics alter actions favorably within fitted S but S does not beat optimized B. C supportive/D adverse does not attribute gain to runtime semantics. Signature-specific supportive and adverse results mean documentary uncertainty is consequential and no all-state claim is allowed. P supportive/T inconclusive means eventual stored-content value may exist while workflow transport remains unresolved.

Inconclusive: simultaneous intervals cross a boundary; the null gate is not exceeded without an adverse confidence bound; a gate fails; support is empty only because lower limits fail; or optimization/bootstrap/audit evidence is incomplete. Favorable AUC, unrestricted text, low ambiguity prevalence, a stable-frame-only result, or naive exact-midnight timing cannot rescue the claim.

## Exact-version external audit and joint error control

The configured snapshot cannot establish decision-time availability. A later audit must sample from fixed U_Q with known inclusion probabilities and retain the same patient/operation/accession IDs. It must obtain source-system operation IDs and validated operative times, exact report-version bodies with hashes, and authored/final/release and preferably clinician-view times relative to state-matched t_dec. Sampling strata must include all possible-role signatures; auditing only stable or ultimately eligible patients is invalid.

For frontier mapping, an accession is successfully accessible only when the source-system operation/clock is inside the predeclared state set, the same selected accession has a finalized/released version before t_dec, and its frozen semantic feature vector is identical to the eventual vector used by Harbor. A differing, partial, or missing pre-cutoff body counts as inaccessible for mapping. Estimate C, M, and CM full-access fractions with simultaneous one-sided design-based 97.5% lower bounds, treating failed linkage/nonresponse by prespecified worst cases. Because the frontier is upward-closed, its simultaneous lower-corner vector, rounded down to the grid, must lie in the internal 97.5% support set. Any clock/linkage state outside Z_i invalidates mapping and requires a full pipeline rebuild. Using differing earlier semantics is a new exact-version estimand requiring frozen-model rescoring and separately powered external evaluation, not a mechanical repair.

Allocate alpha_model=0.025 to the complete internal family and alpha_audit=0.025 to the complete audit-access/nonresponse family. The union bound gives composed familywise error at most 0.05 without independence. Pointwise access intervals, best-stratum selection, or clock checks outside the audit family are invalid. Release-time audit supports system availability only; actual use requires view logs. Even supportive joint audit does not establish treatment effect or patient benefit.
