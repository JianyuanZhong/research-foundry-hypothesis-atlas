# Episode 79 successor: capacity-robust numeric repeat-lactate information

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and advance

At the fixed 24-hour reassessment after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry, some eligible EICU patients have a repeat lactate available and others do not. The assigned parent asks whether the numeric repeat value changes a hospital-held-out, fixed 10% mortality-review queue after repeat availability, repeat age, and contemporaneous non-lactate surveillance are already represented. That isolates numeric information from the observation process, but a result at one arbitrary quota cannot show whether the numeric signal persists when fewer or more people can be selected.

This successor changes only the capacity estimand. It freezes the parent's all-baseline/L24 population, clocks, variables, four models, primary `R_full-R_process` comparison, D7 outcome, eventual-death guard, hospital-held-out fitting, and noncausal boundaries. It prespecifies the analytic capacity grid

`C = {0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.125, 0.15, 0.175, 0.20}`.

This spans one-half to twice the inherited 10% quota and resolves whether the result depends on that single cut point. These fractions are analytic operating points, not measured staffing limits, concurrent daily caseloads, or stakeholder-elicited utilities. EICU has no calendar dates or review-workflow data with which to call the grid operationally realistic.

**Primary falsifiable hypothesis H-capacity-value:** in the frozen all-baseline/L24 population, the D7-fitted hospital-held-out `R_full` queue captures at least 0.03 more source-defined D7 deaths per selected slot than the equal-capacity `R_process` queue at every capacity in `C`. Formally, the 95% lower confidence bound for the worst-capacity pooled contrast `theta7 = min(c in C) Delta7_value(c)` must be at least +0.03. Support also requires nonnegative worst-capacity lower bounds for the equal-hospital macro estimand, both frozen repeat-availability hospital strata, and eventual-discharge death in the same D7-fitted queues, plus prespecified deletion stability.

Three additional deaths per 100 selected slots is the inherited materiality anchor, not a demonstrated benefit threshold. A supportive computation would justify independent prospective silent validation of a numeric-value-using prioritization rule across measured capacities. It would not show that clinicians should order a repeat lactate, that any result was available or viewed at L24, that review changes treatment, that deaths are preventable, or that patients benefit.

## Strongest supported claim versus unresolved claim

Baysan et al. (2022; DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750`; PMCID `PMC9444407`) was inspected in frozen full-text XML, source ID `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]. In a 4,440-person MIMIC validation cohort of critically ill adults with sepsis, 2,347 died in hospital; adding 24-hour lactate to a recalibrated APACHE IV model increased the reported C-statistic from 0.62 (95% CI 0.60-0.63) to 0.64 (0.62-0.66). The strongest claim this supports is modest incremental prognostic information from 24-hour lactate in a lactate-measured cohort.

Sisk et al. (2021; DOI `10.1093/jamia/ocaa242`; PMCID `PMC7810439`) was inspected in frozen full-text XML, source ID `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]. Its review defines informative presence/observation and reports that 24 of 36 included studies used missing indicators or summary measures. This supports representing the measurement process in prediction; it does not validate the present surveillance features as attention, orders, or clinician concern.

The strongest source-specific evidence before execution is feasibility only: the inherited complete-source audit reports 2,302 eligible people in 30 hospitals, 1,163 repeat lactates, 607 D7 events, 1,695 D7 non-events, 779 eventual deaths, and 1,523 eventual survivors. Those counts must be reproduced; they are not evidence that `R_full` improves any queue.

The unresolved claim is whether the optional numeric repeat value adds a material, internally portable fixed-slot association after the repeat observation process is represented, uniformly over the prespecified capacity grid. No inspected paper establishes that claim. The three filesets in `references/research-ambition/README.md` are demonstrations of ambition only. Their explicit availability limits were inspected; no unavailable cancer main article or full STAR Methods is claimed read or used as evidence.

`R_attention = R_state` plus six summaries from valid resolved non-lactate lab rows in `[t0+720,L24]`, all revised by L24: `ln(1+routine marker-time pairs)` for the first 13 streams; `ln(1+blood-gas marker-time pairs)` for paO2/paCO2/pH; `ln(1+distinct labresultoffsets)` over all 16; observed-stream fraction/16; recency `(L24-latest qualifying offset)/720`; and no-qualifying-row indicator. ## Hospital-held-out fitting

For each outer hospital, exclude every one of its rows. In training hospitals only, winsorize continuous variables at the 1st/99th percentiles, standardize using training mean and population SD, then mean-impute missing continuous variables to zero after scaling. Do not scale binary indicators. Fit logistic regression with intercept, L2 penalty, `C=1`, `lbfgs`, `max_iter=10000`, `tol=1e-8`, no class weights, and seed `20260973`. Do not drop features.

Any nonfinite training value, zero post-winsorization variance, nonconvergence, or nonfinite held-out score makes that model noncomputable. Rank raw held-out linear predictors; probabilities are descriptive only. Save person ID, hospital, fold, feature order/origin, preprocessing constants, convergence, score, and outcomes. Hospital, discharge fields, Dinf, diagnoses, treatments, clearance, post-L24 data, and outcome-derived features are forbidden predictors.

## Capacity-grid estimands

Fit each held-out model once. For each `c in C` and frozen hospital h with complete-cohort size `n_h`, define `k_h(c)=ceil(c*n_h)`. For model m, `Q_m,h(c)` is the top `k_h(c)` raw held-out scores, with exact ties broken by ascending `patientunitstayid`. Quotas must be equal between models within each hospital/capacity. Set `K(c)=sum_h k_h(c)`.

For outcome `o in {7,inf}`, let `S_m,h^o(c)=sum_{i in Q_m,h(c)} D_i^o` and `P_m,h^o(c)=S_m,h^o(c)/k_h(c)`. Define

`Delta7_value(c) = [sum_h S_full,h^7(c) - sum_h S_process,h^7(c)] / K(c)`.

The single primary scalar is

`theta7 = min_{c in C} Delta7_value(c)`.

Therefore `theta7>=0.03` means at least three additional source-defined D7 events per 100 equal slots at every prespecified grid capacity, not merely on average. Report the full capacity-response curve and `c*=argmin_c Delta7_value(c)` descriptively; selecting a favorable capacity post hoc is forbidden.

The equal-hospital guard is
`theta7_macro=min_c mean_h[P_full,h^7(c)-P_process,h^7(c)]`.

For stratum `s in {low,high}`, define `K_s(c)=sum_{h in s}k_h(c)`,
`Delta7_s(c)=[sum_{h in s}(S_full,h^7(c)-S_process,h^7(c))]/K_s(c)`, and
`theta7_s=min_c Delta7_s(c)`.

For the eventual-death guard, use the same D7-fitted scores and queues:
`DeltaInf_value(c)=[sum_h S_full,h^inf(c)-sum_h S_process,h^inf(c)]/K(c)`,
`thetaInf=min_c DeltaInf_value(c)`.
No Dinf model or Dinf-optimized queue is allowed.

At each capacity define additions `A_h(c)=Q_full,h(c)\Q_process,h(c)`, removals `R_h(c)=Q_process,h(c)\Q_full,h(c)`, `s_h(c)=|A_h(c)|=|R_h(c)|`, `M(c)=sum_h s_h(c)`, and `rho(c)=M(c)/K(c)`. For each outcome define `G_o(c)=sum_h[sum_A D_i^o-sum_R D_i^o]` and, when `M(c)>0`, `Psi_o(c)=G_o(c)/M(c)`. Enforce `Delta_o(c)=G_o(c)/K(c)=rho(c)*Psi_o(c)` and `|Delta_o(c)|<=rho(c)`. If M=0, G and Delta are zero and Psi is undefined. Never report switch yield without M/K or use it to change the primary state.

For every capacity report K, both capture counts/rates, Delta, macro/stratum contrasts, M, rho, G, Psi, queue overlap, exact tie counts, sensitivity/unselected D7 events, substitution hospitals, `s_h/k_h`, and D7-event/alive-discharge/still-hospitalized-at-H7 composition in queues and switches. Repeat the grid for secondary baselines, clearly labeled nonprimary.

## Uncertainty, stability, and feasibility gates

Use 2,000 copied-hospital refit bootstrap replicates, seed `20260973`. Sample the 30 original hospitals with replacement. A hospital drawn multiple times contributes copied evaluation rows and corresponding training weight, but all copies of the same original hospital form one exclusion group: no prediction for that original may be trained on any copy of itself. Refit preprocessing and all four D7 models; regenerate held-out scores, all seven quotas, contrasts, secondary baselines, and queue decompositions.

For each replicate and capacity save the joint tuple `(c,K,M,rho,G7,Delta7_value,Psi7,Ginf,DeltaInf_value,PsiInf,Delta7_macro,Delta7_low,Delta7_high)`. Then compute within that replicate `theta7_b, theta7_macro_b, theta7_low_b, theta7_high_b, thetaInf_b` as the minima over the locked grid. This joint minimum preserves cross-capacity dependence and is the estimand; it is not a post-hoc multiplicity correction. Use two-sided percentile 95% intervals (2.5th/97.5th percentiles) for capacity-specific values and every minimum functional.

Require >=1,900 finite replicates for every required capacity contrast and all five minimum functionals. For each capacity, report a Psi interval only if >=1,900 replicates have M>0; otherwise label it denominator-fragile, report the zero-substitution fraction, and do not impute or continuity-correct. M=0 does not by itself make fixed-slot Delta noncomputable.

Perform fixed-design leave-one-original-hospital deletion on the already held-out predictions without refitting, recomputing the full grid and each minimum. Support requires `theta7>0` in >=80% of finite pooled deletions, `theta7_macro>0` in >=70%, and each of `theta7_low, theta7_high` >0 in >=70% of deletions that leave the stratum represented. Report deletion signs for thetaInf and whether one hospital dominates K, events, G, or M at any capacity.

Do not repair such failures by changing population, clocks, estimands, outcomes, model, capacity grid, or falsification criteria; those are substantive changes requiring a new Lead child.

## Ordered result states and falsification

Apply the first matching state. Point estimates, AUROC/calibration, secondary models, and switch yields cannot rescue the primary.

1. **Not computable:** any feasibility gate above fails.
2. **Adverse—opposite at some capacity:** the 95% upper bound for `theta7` is <0. Numeric F is associated with worse D7 capture somewhere in the locked band.
3. **Adverse—eventual-capture tradeoff:** the lower bound for `theta7` is >=0.03 but the upper bound for `thetaInf` is <0. Uniform near-term enrichment accompanies evidence of eventual-death capture loss somewhere in the same band.
4. **Adverse—below capacity-robust materiality:** the upper bound for `theta7` is <0.03. This falsifies at least three additional D7 events per 100 slots throughout the band, while allowing a smaller effect or a gain at selected capacities.
5. **Adverse—internally nonportable:** the lower bound for `theta7` is >=0.03, but the upper bound for `theta7_macro`, `theta7_low`, or `theta7_high` is <0. A pooled uniform gain is directionally contradicted by a prespecified site/process estimand.
6. **Supportive capacity-robust numeric increment:** all computation gates pass; the lower bound for `theta7` is >=0.03; lower bounds for `theta7_macro, theta7_low, theta7_high, thetaInf` are >=0; and deletion proportions meet 80% pooled and 70% macro/low/high thresholds. The strongest supported statement is only that, in this EICU snapshot, the locked full model captured at least three more source-defined D7 events per 100 equal slots than the process model throughout the 5%-20% grid, without detected eventual-capture loss or prespecified internal directional contradiction.
7. **Inconclusive:** every other computable pattern, including intervals crossing 0.03 or zero, favorable point estimates without required bounds, failed deletion proportions without a contradictory interval, or discordant guards. No capacity-specific favorable result can be promoted as primary.

An adverse result does not show lactate is biologically unimportant or never prognostic; it rejects the locked materiality/direction claim for this queue estimand. An inconclusive result does not establish equivalence. A supportive result remains noncausal and does not establish utility or benefit.
