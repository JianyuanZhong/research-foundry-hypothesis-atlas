# Whole-panel, leave-one-patient-out stability of clean report semantics for documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Targeted successor and clinical decision

This proposal is a targeted synthesis of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. The first parent is controlling wherever the parents differ: interpretation uncertainty is represented by six immutable corpus-wide reader books and exactly nine radiology×pathology book pairs, not by selecting a different reader form for each patient or item. The second parent contributes a distinct finite-corpus composition-stability question. This child does not reintroduce its item-local synthetic-reader envelope or its uncensored multi-patient deletion radius.

The consequential decision is whether a retrospective signal is sufficiently robust to justify the next expensive evidence step: acquiring report-version/release/view logs and conducting a prospective silent-mode study of a fixed multidisciplinary-review queue before HCC resection. The output is not a triage, resection, transplant, systemic-treatment, surveillance, or deployment rule.

The unresolved question is whether clean meanings in eventual stored pre-cutoff CT/MRI reports add a clinically material number of encounter-documentary high-grade microvascular-invasion (M2) captures at fixed review capacity after two distinct threats are respected simultaneously:

1. the same actual radiologist and pathologist interpretation patterns must be preserved across the corpus; and
2. a favorable universal margin must not depend on retaining one particular test patient.

A result robust to source ambiguity and all nine actual reader-book pairs can still be composition-fragile. Conversely, ordinary bootstrap confidence claims are not justified by this snapshot's unknown sampling frame and the nonsmooth minimax/top-K estimand. An exact leave-one-whole-patient-out endpoint is an identified finite-corpus stress test, not a substitute for population inference.

## 2. Supported evidence, unresolved hypothesis, and advance

### Strongest claim supported before the experiment

Direct catalog, table-schema, live-header, and row inspection supports only the following. The HCC snapshot contains patient/encounter-linkable encounters, procedure rows, CT/MRI examination rows with an accession-like identifier, acquisition-like start time and eventual report text, and same-encounter pathology text. The pathology source has no time, specimen/accession, slide, block, section, sampling-site, distance, or adequacy field. Radiology has no report authorship/finalization/release/view/version time. There are no raw images or slides, recurrence, survival, treatment response, clinician review actions, or queue-capacity measurements.

The episode-58 full pathology audit read all 46,395 rows without sampling: 32,386 patient-encounters, 8,932 with multiple rows, maximum 14 rows per encounter, and 16 exact duplicate rows after the first. All 32,386 patient-encounters had nonblank text in at least one of `病理/检查所见/检查诊断`. These are availability and multiplicity facts, not cohort counts or validated HCC/M2 labels.

The inspected full text of Yim et al. (PMID 27570686; PMCID PMC5001784; frozen Europe PMC XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports 101 annotated HCC radiology reports, partial-match interannotator F1 0.93 for entities and 0.90 for relations, and describes anaphora, split antecedents, prior measurements, and uncertainty. It motivates whole-report interpretation but does not validate this Chinese rubric or establish a correct reader.

The inspected full text of Cong et al. (DOI 10.3748/wjg.v22.i42.9279; PMCID PMC5107692; frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) describes a seven-point baseline gross-sampling protocol including tumor-liver junction and adjacent/distant peritumoral samples. The HCC source cannot establish that this protocol was followed or link the stored pathology words to a specimen. Thus the endpoint must remain encounter-documentary M2.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

> Among adults in the frozen first-eligible source-documented HCC-resection frame, one locked `G_clean` model using only source-byte-traceable, context-quarantined concepts from eventual stored whole-accession CT/MRI canonical text improves encounter-documentary M2 capture by strictly more than 5 per 100 fixed review positions over both independently fitted `R_clean` and same-fit `Gclean_mask`, in every admissible coupled source state, pathology-terminal completion, and predeclared whole radiology×pathology reader-book pair, separately in 2020 and 2021; and all four universal lower-margin claims remain strictly above 5 after deletion of any one whole frozen test patient.

The first conjunct is the primary parent's whole-panel finite-corpus claim. The second is the new composition-stability claim. It is falsified by one attained patient/book/source/pathology witness at or below 5, provided all post-deletion evidence-sufficiency gates pass.

The substantive advance over the parents and usual single-consensus model evaluations is a clinically interpretable separation of three uncertainty layers: actual supplied reader-pattern sensitivity, whole-patient finite-corpus influence, and still-unidentified target-population uncertainty. Support would justify only the next prospective evidence step. It would not establish correctness of the labels, robustness to unseen readers, biological M2, report availability at decision time, benefit, or transportability.

## 3. Population and temporal boundaries

For each cutoff offset `d`, retain at most one state-specific episode per patient:

- age at least 18;
- earliest eligible source-documented liver-resection episode under the frozen hepatobiliary procedure dictionary;
- same-encounter pathology composite supports documentary HCC-frame membership under the selected pathology book and allowed terminal;
- no recorded prior qualifying HCC resection, transplant, TACE/embolization, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[c_d-365d,c_d)`.

The primary analytical cutoff is `c_d=t_op-24h`; locked sensitivities use `d in {12,48,72}`. CT/MRI acquisitions must lie wholly within `[c_d-90d,c_d)`. Acquisition time is not report release or view time, and “no recorded prior treatment” is not biological treatment-naivety.

Preserve the primary parent's source-state reconstruction:

- exact deduplication of all six procedure fields while retaining raw source ordinal;
- same-patient/same-encounter/same-calendar-day provisional operation linkage;
- point clocks only for validated nonmidnight anesthesia-system starts;
- date-like or unvalidated-midnight case-record starts represented as calendar-day intervals `[date,date+24h)`;
- boundary partitioning for competing/missing clocks, episode, year, examination window and prior-treatment membership;
- patient-wide prior-treatment lookup before interval filtering; and
- every ambiguity ends in an emitted finite state, a machine-checkable impossibility, or an adjudication-only exclusion retained in the outer universe.

Nonmembership is never encoded as `Y=0`.

Chronology quarantine is possibility-based over all nine panel pairs and all source/terminal states. `U_T` contains every patient eligible in any 2020 or 2021 world and is excluded from fitting/tuning. Among the remainder, `U_V` contains anyone eligible in any 2019 world. Among the remainder, `U_D` contains anyone eligible in any 2015–2018 world. Development is 2015–2018; 2019 locks preprocessing and hyperparameters; 2020 and 2021 are untouched separate tests. Data from 2022 onward are audit-only.

## 4. Frozen source objects and whole-reader books

A radiology unit is `u=(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` and contains every exact-deduplicated component in raw order with immutable field and source-byte boundaries. Blank `检查号` cannot supply primary semantics. The inherited parser emits reversible canonical text nodes `V`, markup/attribute nodes `A`, and parse-error lineage `E`. Semantic spans may originate only from reversible `V` bytes. Parse-disagreeing, nonreversible, treatment/pathology/specimen/MVI-grade-contaminated, or unresolved-context units receive one whole-unit unavailable block; phrases are not selectively removed and patients are not dropped.

A pathology object is `v=(患者主索引,就诊号,composite_hash)`. Exact-deduplicate across all six source fields but retain all raw ordinals; serialize every `病理/检查所见/检查诊断` field in raw-row/field order with boundaries and byte offsets. Because the source has no item/accession/time field, every pathology row in the selected encounter forms one indivisible documentary composite. No row is assigned to a resection specimen.

Before semantics, outcome, model score, split role or top-K status is exposed, a steward freezes every reachable usable radiology unit and every reachable nonempty pathology composite without filtering on HCC or grade. The same two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists each complete every radiology item; a third submits a complete adjudication form for every item. The same two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists each complete every pathology item; a third submits a complete adjudication form for every item. Incomplete expected forms, corrupt provenance, or unresolved qualifications trigger global inconclusive status and cannot shrink the cohort.

Each radiology form is an indivisible concept-and-span vector covering the inherited features: modality-specific maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis and ascites, with explicit uncertain/not-mentioned/source-unavailable levels.

Each pathology form indivisibly carries HCC-frame judgment, conditional grade judgment, spans/context, and a deterministically generated terminal set using only `OUT, IN0-A, IN1-A, IN0-U, IN1-U`. `OUT` has no Y; `INy-A` is anchored; `INy-U` is an allowed unanchored completion. No HCC call is borrowed from one form and grade from another.

Define three complete radiology books `B^R_{R1},B^R_{R2},B^R_{RA}` and three complete pathology books `B^P_{P1},B^P_{P2},B^P_{PA}`. The primary panel set is exactly
`G={R1,R2,RA}×{P1,P2,PA}`.
In one world, one `g=(j_R,j_P)` applies across every item, patient, year, cutoff, fitting/tuning stage and model arm. The optimizer cannot switch readers by item, patient, year, model, concept, or deletion. `(RA,PA)` is a secondary adjudicated-only diagnostic and cannot rescue failure of the nine-pair envelope.

A world is `omega=(g,{s_i},{tau_i})`, where `s_i` is one coherent source state and `tau_i` is allowed by the selected pathology form. The same world supplies membership, year, predictors, outcome, denominators and ties to both arms of a contrast. Denote the finite universe `Omega_book+`.

## 5. Variables, baselines, models, capacity, and undeleted endpoints

Preserve four information sets:

- `B(Z)`: acquisition-only diagnostic baseline;
- `R_clean(Z,S,Q)`: independently optimized reduced comparator with the identical nonsemantic surface and availability information;
- `G_clean(Z,S,Q,X_clean)`: independently optimized full semantic model;
- `Gclean_mask`: the fitted `G_clean` applied with no refit, recalibration or threshold change after each complete semantic block is replaced by its training-defined unavailable block while `Z,S,Q` remain byte-identical.

`Z` includes age, sex, CT/MRI/unit availability, eligible-unit count, recency, component/accession ambiguity and development-grouped `机器型号`. `S` contains only raw/canonical/markup surface quantities. `Q` is reason-free whole-concept-block availability. `X_clean` is supplied by the selected complete radiology book. Pathology, Y, reader identity/agreement, hashes, form time, ambiguity width, identifiers, unrestricted tokens, labs, interactions, splines and test-derived features are forbidden predictors.

Use the inherited additive logistic model with unpenalized intercept, one patient-balanced outcome-blind preprocessing map over the outer development ledger, byte-identical shared columns between `R_clean` and `G_clean`, and one robustly certified zero-slope threshold `A_M` per model. For `alpha in {.25,.5,.75,1}`, use 100 log-spaced lambdas from `A_M/alpha` to `10^-4 A_M/alpha`; pure ridge uses 100 finite values from `A_M` to `10^-4 A_M` without claiming an all-zero endpoint. Fit one coefficient vector per information set by normalized minimax development loss over all nine book pairs/source/terminal states, with exact cutting-plane/Dinkelbach certificates. Tune independently on 2019 worst-state fixed-K performance. Test forms/outcomes remain sealed until preprocessing, hyperparameters, coefficients, `N_ref` and K are frozen.

Compute
`N_ref=min_{a=2015,...,2019; d in {12,24,48,72}; omega in Omega_book+}N_a(omega,d)`
and `K_rho=floor(rho*N_ref)` for `rho in {.05,.10,.20}`.
Confirmatory `K=K_.10`; require `N_ref>=500` and `K>=50`. Capacities 5% and 20% are sensitivity analyses only.

For each test year and every undeleted world require at least `K+1` eligible patients, not merely K. Also require a sharp minimum anchored-grade fraction at least 0.85 and at least 50 eligible `Y=1` events. These minima use the same nine books, source states and terminals; equality at 0.85 passes and fewer than 50 events fails.

For locked model M, year a and world omega:
`T_M(a,omega)=100/K * sum_{i in TopK_M(a,omega)}Y_i(omega)`,
selecting exactly K whole patients by locked score, frozen SHA-256 patient tie key and raw episode backpointer.

Co-primary contrasts are
`Delta_nested=T_Gclean-T_Rclean` and
`Delta_mask=T_Gclean-T_Gclean_mask`.
For each `e=(a,c)`, with `a in {2020,2021}`, compute attained
`I_e^0=[L_e^0,U_e^0]=[min_omega Delta_e(omega),max_omega Delta_e(omega)]`
using the identical world in both arms.

## 6. New exact leave-one-whole-patient-out estimand

Freeze `U_T` before any test result. For every `j in U_T`, deletion removes all of j's source rows, competing states, radiology/pathology objects, membership/outcome terminals and ranking possibilities from both arms and both test years. Forms remain in the immutable book and audit trail; no book is reconstructed and no item is reread. Because possibility-based quarantine already excluded `U_T` from fitting/tuning, deletion cannot alter preprocessing, hyperparameters or coefficients. `N_ref` and K also remain unchanged.

For each coordinate define
`L_{e,j}^{-1}=min_{omega in Omega_book+} Delta_e(omega\{j})`
and
`U_{e,j}^{-1}=max_{omega in Omega_book+} Delta_e(omega\{j})`,
then
`L_e^{-1}=min_{j in U_T}L_{e,j}^{-1}` and
`U_e^{-1}=max_{j in U_T}U_{e,j}^{-1}`.

The all-world `K+1` gate makes exact-K ranking defined after deletion of any one eligible patient. It must also be replayed directly as
`min_{omega,j}N_a(omega\{j})>=K`.
For the deletion claim, additionally require sharp post-deletion minima of anchored-grade fraction at least 0.85 and eligible Y=1 events at least 50. Failure of a post-deletion adequacy gate is inconclusive, not evidence of fragility or adverse semantic value.

The successor's stability conjunct for coordinate e is `L_e^{-1}>5`. Equality to 5 fails. Every minimizing deletion must emit a patient-protected hash, panel pair, source/terminal states, before/after arm-specific top-K hashes, whether the patient or a replacement occupied either top K, score/rank/outcome arithmetic, and all raw/form backpointers. Report per-patient lower endpoints and whether one patient breaks multiple coordinates, but never call their distribution a sampling distribution or influence probability.

A full multi-patient breakdown radius is intentionally not confirmatory: with fixed K it becomes right-censored by roster depletion, so “infinite” can mean no evaluable deletion rather than robustness. The exact singleton estimand directly answers the clinical escalation question without inventing a population-confidence interpretation.

## 7. Exact computation and feasibility

Enumerate all nine `g` exactly. Conditional on g, source-state and selected-form terminal choices remain patientwise finite. Robust fitting uses exact patient-domain separation inside normalized Dinkelbach/cutting-plane optimization, while taking the maximum over all nine global books. One coefficient vector per information set is reported; no book-specific primary model exists.

After scores are frozen, solve each undeleted and deletion endpoint as a state-expanded mixed-integer top-K problem. Variables select one coherent state per retained patient and exactly K eligible patients in each arm. Rank-inversion constraints enforce the exact total order `(linear predictor,SHA-256 tie key,raw episode backpointer)`; generate them lazily until no violation remains. For deletion, either enumerate every `j in U_T` and solve the inherited endpoint problem or use a one-deletion selector shared across both arms; both implementations must agree.

Certification requires zero unresolved integer gap, numerical feasibility gap at most `1e-8`, raw-state and quotient replay, order invariance, all tied maximizers, complete source/form/book/deletion backpointers, exhaustive equality on synthetic fixtures through 12 patients, and exhaustive/raw agreement on real-data shards through 20 patients. No state cap, sampling, beam search, favorable incumbent, approximate bound, or timeout result can support or falsify the hypothesis. Any required failure yields inconclusive status.

## 8. Falsification and one-hot interpretation

After all source, annotation, adequacy, fit and exact-solver gates pass, each undeleted coordinate is:

- universally supportive iff `L_e^0>5`;
- heterogeneous iff `L_e^0<=5<U_e^0`;
- all-world adverse iff `U_e^0<=5`.

The global result is exactly one of:

1. `inconclusive` with precedence if any mandatory gate fails;
2. `stable-whole-panel-universal-supportive` if all four `L_e^0>5` and all four `L_e^{-1}>5`;
3. `whole-panel-supportive-but-single-patient-fragile` if all four `L_e^0>5` but at least one `L_e^{-1}<=5`, with an attained deletion witness;
4. `universal-adverse-without-positive-coordinate` if all four `U_e^0<=5`;
5. `decisive-heterogeneity` otherwise, with radiology-, pathology-, joint-panel-, or deletion-sensitivity labels only when the corresponding paired witness is stored.

A stable result supports only: within this frozen eventual-report corpus, clean report concepts added more than five encounter-documentary M2 captures per 100 fixed positions under every declared source/pathology completion and every supplied whole-reader pair in both years, and no singleton whole-patient deletion reversed that margin.

A fragile result supports the undeleted parent claim but falsifies the successor stability claim; it does not imply the deleted patient is erroneous. Heterogeneity falsifies universal robustness and identifies an attained dependency, not the correct reader or source state. Universal adverse falsifies the strict >5 margin in every declared undeleted world but does not prove semantics useless or exclude smaller gains. Inconclusive is not adverse evidence.

No output is a p value, confidence interval, causal effect, future-population guarantee, unseen-reader guarantee, biological-M2 result, real-time availability result, treatment effect, or deployment authorization.

## 11. Evidence needed for stronger conclusions

Biological M2 requires pathology time plus specimen/accession/slide/block links and documented sampling-site/extent adjudication under a recognized protocol. Real-time use requires accession-linked report version/final/release/view logs and validated operation clocks. Unseen-reader robustness requires a new locked multi-reader study. Population inference requires a defined target population, consecutive ascertainment or known inclusion probabilities, site/calendar coverage and a prespecified inferential model appropriate to this nonsmooth robust top-K estimand; a locked external or later temporal cohort is preferable. Capacity acceptability requires a prospective candidate stream, staffing, review duration and queue measurements. Clinical benefit requires external validation followed by a controlled outcomes study.
