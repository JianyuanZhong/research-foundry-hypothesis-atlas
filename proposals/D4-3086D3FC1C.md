## Frozen estimand and computation

With non-GI as reference, `G=(G_CRC,G_otherGI)`, `C=(age at L, sex, repeat haemoglobin, repeat MCV, repeat platelet count, lag_late)`, and `S=(adverse Hb slope, adverse MCV slope, adverse platelet slope)`, the primary six-dimensional null was

`E[(G-pi(C)) tensor (S-g(C))] = 0`.

The score order was fixed as CRC-Hb, CRC-MCV, CRC-PLT, otherGI-Hb, otherGI-MCV, otherGI-PLT. Two separate binary ridge-logistic nuisances estimated the CRC and other-GI indicators; separate ridge-linear nuisances estimated each slope. Training-fold continuous predictors alone were standardized; sex and lag remained unstandardized. Penalties were tuned over `10^-4,...,10^4`, with the largest penalty selected under a `1e-12` loss-tie tolerance; logistic intercept and `lag_late` were unpenalized.

The execution used 20 repeated five-fold outer cross-fits under seeds `20260909` through `20260928`, jointly stratified by three-level group, lag, and sex. Each participant's six-score vector was averaged across repetitions before participant-level covariance and inference; the 100 held-out fold appearances were never treated as independent rows. All 23,000 nuisance fits converged, every held-out score was finite, and every outer training and test fold contained all groups with required stratum support. Binary held-out prediction ranges were 0.0246-0.1162 for CRC and 0.0380-0.0933 for other GI; none crossed the prespecified operational extreme-overlap threshold.

Primary inference used the robust covariance of the 998 averaged participant contributions and 20,000 independent standard-normal participant multipliers applied to centered fixed score contributions. It also produced a multiplier-calibrated six-dimensional ellipsoid and max-studentized simultaneous component intervals. The prespecified covariance failure threshold was `10^10`.

## Benchmark, falsifications, and exploratory analysis

The prespecified ordinary unpenalized three-category multinomial benchmark compared `C` with `C+S`, using non-GI as reference. Its added six-slope-block likelihood ratio was 2.162915475. All fits converged, no bootstrap fit failed, and the 5,000-sample parametric-bootstrap plus-one p-value was 0.9052189562 (chi-square reference p=0.9041219168). This was concordant with the primary global non-rejection.

Twenty joint-slope permutation diagnostics used seeds `20261909` through `20261928`. All three slope columns were permuted together within lag-by-sex strata, group and `C` were held fixed, group nuisance predictions were reused, and every slope nuisance was retuned within training folds. Each diagnostic used 5,000 participant multipliers. None had p<0.05; the minimum p was 0.05038992 and the maximum Wald statistic was 12.40734. These diagnostics did not show repeated extreme behavior but do not establish biological absence or exchangeability.

The exploratory two-dimensional RDW replacement used field `30070` and 20,000 multipliers. Its Wald statistic was 0.8514071 and multiplier p=0.6512174; covariance condition number was 1.4121. RDW was not permitted to open or rescue the primary gate.

No prespecified diagnostic failure occurred.

## Executable ambiguity disclosures

The following operational choices were required without changing the frozen scientific design:

1. The parent did not prescribe inner-fold stratification. Inner folds were stratified by three-level cancer group within outer training data. Joint group-by-lag-by-sex inner stratification was impossible because the smallest outer-training joint stratum contained four people for five folds.
2. Linear nuisance outcomes remained in their observed annualized units; only continuous predictors were standardized, consistent with the parent wording.
3. Closed-test objects were implemented as two three-moment contrast families: CRC-minus-otherGI score blocks and the CRC score block under the non-GI-reference parameterization. The maximum of their quadratic multiplier statistics supplied two-contrast FWER adjustment.
4. Severe overlap was operationalized prospectively as any held-out binary probability below `1e-6` or above `1-1e-6`.
5. RDW replacement was two-dimensional because one RDW trajectory was crossed with the two non-reference group indicators.
6. The parent did not prescribe a multiplier count for each permutation diagnostic; 5,000 were used per permutation, while primary and RDW inference each used 20,000.

## Conclusion, falsification meaning, and claim limits

The strongest conclusion supported by this execution is that this prespecified selected-recorded-case experiment did not provide evidence of adjusted six-dimensional adverse-CBC-trajectory heterogeneity among CRC, other-GI, and non-GI first-diagnosis groups. The result is computationally valid and benchmark-concordant, but scientifically inconclusive. It is not evidence of equivalence, biological identity, absence of clinically important heterogeneity, or failure of CBC trajectories in another target population. No clinically meaningful equivalence margin was prespecified.

A supportive result would have required global rejection, at least one closed-testing contrast rejection, coherent direction, stable diagnostics, and benchmark concordance; that pattern was not observed. A genuinely adverse result capable of weakening the mechanism hypothesis would require stable, sufficiently precise estimates evaluated against a prespecified clinically meaningful incompatibility or equivalence margin, which is unavailable. The observed non-rejection therefore remains inconclusive rather than adverse proof. Diagnostic instability, benchmark discordance, broad intervals, or dependence on RDW would also have forced an inconclusive result; none occurred, but their absence does not transform non-rejection into equivalence.

The population/prognosis gate remains `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED`. Selection on future recorded malignancy can induce collider bias, and diagnosis lag is post-landmark. Available data do not certify registry completeness and do not provide pathology/stage adjudication, symptoms, FIT, endoscopy, treatment, protocolized routine-care CBC timing, outcomes among people without recorded malignancy, harms, costs, or external validation. Computation can verify the reported cohort aggregates, folds, nuisance diagnostics, statistics, calibration counts, gate logic, and result-consistent interpretation. It cannot establish source-code clinical correctness, registry completeness, mechanism, causality, prospective utility, transportability, or patient benefit.

No incidence, prospective-risk, causal, screening, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, lead-time, referral-utility, completeness, transportability, or patient-benefit claim is supported. Stronger biological or clinical conclusions require clinical coding/pathology/registry adjudication and a separate population-based study with people not selected on future cancer, protocolized repeat CBC measurements, clinically specified margins, and external validation.
