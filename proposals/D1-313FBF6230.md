# Lactate–circulatory discordance at first ICU discharge

## Scientific deliverable

The future solver must newly construct and fit an auditable, leakage-safe MIMIC-IV
3.1 study of whether persistent measured lactate after recorded circulatory-support
withdrawal identifies a different early post-ICU failure pathway than lactate and
support that are concordant. Completion is a data-bound estimand, not a claim that
the phenotype is true tissue hypoperfusion or that ICU discharge policy should be
changed.

Required outputs are:

1. `run_manifest.json`, including catalog [source checksum], MIMIC
   snapshot `[source checksum]`,
   exact source hashes/paths, archive members, schema hashes, item-label and unit
   assertions, locked thresholds/windows/topology rules, split seeds, software and
   output hashes;
2. `cohort_audit.parquet`, with one earliest valid adult ICU stay per subject,
   joins, alive-at-outtime rule, exclusions, lactate/support eligibility, and
   observation-coverage flags;
3. `feature_ledger.parquet`, containing every eligible lactate and MAP observation,
   every candidate vasoactive interval, its validity/status checks, measurement
   masks, and 12 one-hour rows in `[outtime-12h,outtime)`;
4. `phenotype_audit.parquet`, with lactate trajectory class, recorded circulatory
   state, discordance class, unknown/contradictory cells, and sensitivity recodings;
5. `topology_outcome_audit.parquet`, with all post-index ICU stays, transfers,
   documented non-ICU bridge evidence, death/discharge timing, and the lower and
   upper return endpoints;
6. held-out baseline and alternative predictions, standardized cumulative
   incidences, contrasts, calibration, IPCW Brier/log loss where defined,
   bootstrap/split uncertainty, falsification results, and
   `interpretation.md` mapping each conclusion to computed output.

## Unresolved claim, evidence boundary and hypothesis

The expert seed `[starting question]` proposes that persistent hyperlactatemia
has different meanings when perfusion is improving versus worsening, but it is
explicitly untested and supplies no local cutoff or validated phenotype. The
available evidence supports only that MIMIC contains dated lactate, vital-sign,
vasoactive, ICU-discharge, transfer, death and ICU-return records. It does not
support a conclusion about microcirculation, clinician intent, fluid response, or
discharge readiness.

The primary unresolved claim is narrower and computable: among adults with a valid
first-ICU-discharge landmark and adequate repeated lactate observation, does
persistent measured lactate in a recorded-off circulatory-support state have a
higher 48-hour ICU-return-compatible cumulative incidence than lactate that is low
in the same state, and does this contrast differ from patients with active recorded
vasoactive support? The prespecified hypothesis is that lactate-high/recorded-off
(a discordant recorded state) has a larger early return-compatible contrast and a
different death-versus-return composition than lactate-low/recorded-off, while
lactate-high/active-support is the highest-burden concordant state. The alternative
direction—no contrast after measurement/process adjustment or a death-dominant
rather than return-dominant pattern—is equally falsifying.

This advances the parent’s respiratory-versus-vasoactive temporal-order question by
testing whether a metabolic signal changes the interpretation of circulatory
withdrawal. It may inform whether a lactate result should be treated as a distinct
discharge-risk phenotype, rather than folded into a single support-order label.
It is prognostic/observational, not a causal test of giving fluids, continuing
vasopressors, or delaying discharge.

## Population, index and exposure

Use the read-only MIMIC-IV 3.1 ZIP
`[internal dataset path]`,
[source checksum].
Select adults (`patients.anchor_age >= 18`) with a valid `icu/icustays` row,
`intime < outtime`, whose earliest ICU stay for the subject is discharged alive
from the ICU and remains within the corresponding `hosp/admissions` interval.
Join `icu/icustays` to `hosp/admissions` on `subject_id,hadm_id`, and to same-stay
events on `subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id`; join age/sex/year group from
`hosp/patients` on `subject_id`. Do not use `patients.dod` to define an outcome.

The primary window is the 12 hours before `outtime`, left-closed and right-open.
For the primary repeated-lactate subset, require two numerically valid blood-lactate
observations from `hosp/labevents` in that window, at least two hours apart, with a
final observation in `[outtime-6h,outtime)`. Use item IDs 50813 (Lactate, Blood)
and 52442 (Lactate, Blood); assert their labels, fluids and units against
`hosp/d_labitems` before harmonizing them. A sensitivity analysis uses only 50813,
only 52442, and all verified blood-lactate assays separately. Values, units and
duplicate timestamps are audited; no imputation may create a lactate observation.

Primary lactate classes are locked before test evaluation: low if both values are
<2.0 mmol/L; persistent-high if both are >=2.0; resolving-high if the prior is
>=2.0 and final is <2.0; and indeterminate otherwise. The threshold is a recorded
measurement convention, not a diagnosis. Sensitivities vary the threshold over
1.5/2.0/2.5 mmol/L and the repeat window over 6/12/24 hours, with all changes
locked from training/validation only.

Recorded circulatory support is derived only from valid `icu/inputevents` intervals
with `starttime`, `endtime`, `statusdescription`, and item-label checks in
`icu/d_items`. The primary vasoactive dictionary is norepinephrine 221906,
vasopressin 222315, epinephrine 221289, dopamine 221662 and phenylephrine 221749;
concentration/legacy variants are admitted only when a local `d_items` audit
verifies the same medication label and `linksto=inputevents`. An interval is active
if it overlaps `[outtime-6h,outtime)` and passes start/end/status validity. A
patient is “recorded-off” only when a prior valid vasoactive interval is observed,
its end precedes the final six-hour window, and no valid interval overlaps that
window; absence without prior positive support is `unknown`, never “off”.
Near-boundary intervals use locked delta tolerances of 0/15/30 minutes. A separate
audit records whether `continueinnextdept` or status fields make the interval
ambiguous. No cessation is inferred from missing rows.

The primary four-cell comparison is low/recorded-off, persistent-high/recorded-off,
low/active-support and persistent-high/active-support, with resolving-high and all
unknown/indeterminate cells retained as audit strata rather than silently excluded.
MAP from `icu/chartevents` item IDs 220052 (arterial mean) and 220181 (non-invasive
mean), plus HR 220045, RR 220210, SpO2 220277, O2 flow 223834, FiO2 223835 and
PEEP 220339, are pre-index covariates and observation masks. Their labels/units and
`linksto=chartevents` must be asserted from `icu/d_items`. The parent’s
respiratory support/order features are reproduced as a prespecified covariate and
interaction, not used to redefine the lactate phenotype.

## Outcomes and partial identification

Follow from `outtime` for 6, 24 and 48 hours, censoring at hospital end where
appropriate. Use `hosp/transfers` (`subject_id,hadm_id,transfer_id,eventtype,careunit,
intime,outtime`) and later `icu/icustays` rows to timestamp the next ICU stay. The
upper return endpoint U is any later ICU stay beginning within the horizon. The
lower return-compatible endpoint L requires a documented non-ICU transfer interval
after the index ICU outtime and before the later ICU intime; missing or contradictory
transfer topology remains unknown. Preserve the parent’s sensitivity to bridge
duration (0/15/30/60 minutes), transfer permutations, and a +6-hour transfer shift.

Death is the first in-hospital death event from `hosp/admissions.deathtime` or the
validated hospital-expire field, and alive hospital discharge is
`hosp/admissions.dischtime`, with deterministic pre-registered tie handling. L and U
are not both treated as observed truths: report lower-bound L CIFs, upper-bound U
CIFs, and the resulting contrast interval for each phenotype. A return-only signal
that exists only in U is not called confirmed ICU escalation.

The primary estimands are standardized 6/24/48-hour CIF contrasts against
low/recorded-off for L and U, separately for death, and the death-to-return CIF
composition. Use complete-case repeated-lactate estimates as the primary measured
subset, then report the full-cohort observation-process estimand with explicit
unknown/untested cells. Do not condition on post-outtime treatment, ward monitoring,
later notes, or discharge destination as a predictor.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Use fixed patient-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test splits with seeds 17, 29 and
43. Fit all thresholds, scaling, missingness rules and tuning on train/validation;
no subject may cross splits.

The transparent baseline is pooled one-hour penalized logistic cause-specific
competing-hazard models for L, U-only, death and alive discharge, plus a model for
aggregate ICU return. Nested versions contain (i) final/prior lactate only, (ii)
MAP/physiology and support only, (iii) observation intensity/masks only, (iv) the
parent’s order features, and (v) the full prespecified inputs. Standardize held-out
CIFs to the primary four-cell population. Report absolute risks and contrasts, not
only odds ratios.

The substantive alternative is an observation-aware two-domain latent semi-Markov
model on the identical feature ledger. Its latent metabolic states are low,
persistent-high, resolving and unknown; circulatory states are active, recorded-off
and unknown; transition durations span the same eight six-hour intervals, while a
separate observation model predicts when lactate/MAP/vasoactive evidence is recorded.
The fitting target is the same four post-outtime event hazards/CIFs and held-out
subject outcomes. This model can reveal whether ordered lactate persistence and
support duration explain risk that the endpoint phenotype loses, or whether the
apparent signal is primarily selective testing/recording. It cannot recover true
perfusion, unrecorded support or clinician intent. Compare held-out calibration,
IPCW Brier/log loss, uncertainty, latent entropy and stability—not a small score
gain. The baseline remains primary if the latent model is unstable, high-entropy,
poorly calibrated, or changes the estimand rather than clarifying it.

A causal target-trial emulation of fluid/pressor treatment is deferred: available
records support exposure reconstruction but not adequate indication, fluid
responsiveness, treatment-limitation or microcirculation labels. A GRU/TCN is also
deferred because it would add capacity without a clearer scientific answer unless
the ordered phenotype is populated and remains unexplained by the observation-aware
model. The prior parent’s order-only design is retained as a comparator, not treated
as a method ban.

## Falsification, uncertainty and interpretation

Use subject bootstrap 95% intervals, all three split seeds, event/cell counts,
positivity/effective sample size, missingness and contradiction rates, calibration
intercept/slope, IPCW diagnostics, topology-bound width, and latent posterior entropy.
Prespecified falsifications are:

- permute lactate order/values within patient and support-intensity strata while
  preserving assay counts and observation masks;
- permute respiratory-versus-vasoactive order from the parent while preserving all
  marginal support features;
- remove procedure markers and analyze chart-only respiratory evidence;
- fit observation-only, process-only, physiology-only and support-only models;
- replace charttime/starttime/endtime with storetime as a timestamp negative control;
- use pseudo-landmarks 6 and 12 hours before outtime with outcomes shifted;
- vary lactate threshold/window, delta tolerance, bridge rule, and tie tolerance;
- stratify by first/last ICU careunit, admission type and anchor-year group without
  cross-subject calendar comparisons.

Supportive evidence requires adequate repeated-lactate cell support and positivity,
a non-wide L contrast with uncertainty excluding the locked null, concordant direction
between baseline and latent alternative, held-out calibration, attenuation under
lactate/order permutation, and persistence after explicit observation-process
adjustment. A result can support the measured-lactate estimand for U while remaining
inconclusive for L.

Adverse evidence is a null/reversal, disappearance after process adjustment,
dependence on a single assay or missingness rule, persistence after permutation,
poor calibration, or a signal only in U. A death-only signal rejects the proposed
return-dominant interpretation but may support a different competing-risk description.
Inconclusive evidence includes sparse repeated-lactate cells, high indeterminate or
unknown prevalence, broad topology intervals, poor positivity, unstable thresholds,
split instability, or high latent entropy/model disagreement.

Computation can establish reproducible recorded-data feature construction, the
measured-lactate estimand, competing-risk CIFs, topology bounds, held-out prediction,
calibration and sensitivity consistency. It cannot establish tissue hypoperfusion,
microcirculatory dysfunction, fluid responsiveness, extubation success, clinician
intent, planned versus unplanned return, preventability, causal treatment effects,
readiness for discharge, clinical utility, or outcomes after leaving hospital. Those
claims require chart adjudication, treatment-limitation and ward-monitoring evidence,
external validation, and likely a prospective or causal study.
