# UKB metabolic change and persistent cardiometabolic domain transition: confirmation-aware successor

## Episode-26 deliverable and substantive repair

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`, the highest-mu assessed valid UKB parent. It preserves the parent’s clinically important question, exact five-source binding, participant-level split, competing death, measurement-selection diagnostics, source-concordance audit, and matched current-level/change comparison. The substantive repair is to make the confirmation endpoint a two-stage estimand rather than treating a later confirming code as if it were an ordinary first event.

## Unresolved clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

Evidence inspected supports the narrower claim that baseline or composite glycaemic/lipid measures are associated with later coded cardiometabolic disease and that multistate models can describe disease progression. The frozen Europe PMC searches `[source checksum]` and `[source checksum]` returned recent UKB studies of baseline AIP/TyG/pulse pressure and cardiometabolic progression; their abstracts do not establish that assessment-linked within-person HbA1c, triglyceride, or HDL change adds information beyond the later current level, or that such a signal survives a reproducibility-style confirmation rule. The imported expert seed `[starting question]` motivates this question but is explicitly untested.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among UK Biobank participants with one established coded cardiometabolic domain at the first panel who return for a valid second assessment and remain in that same domain, does assessment-linked change in HbA1c, triglycerides, and HDL predict a subsequent persistent transition into an additional cardiometabolic domain beyond second-panel current levels, domain, duration, and prespecified covariates?

The primary falsifiable hypothesis is that dated change adds prognostic information in the selected repeat-assessment cohort, and that at least part of any incremental signal persists when the target is a two-code confirmation separated by 30–365 days. The primary clinical relevance is surveillance: if the later current value is not sufficient and prior deterioration identifies higher risk of a persistent additional domain, a prospective monitoring study could test whether trend-informed review is useful. A null finding would support the narrower conclusion that, in this selected cohort and with these measurements, current level captures the available prognostic information. Neither result establishes that changing a biomarker causes disease, that repeat testing improves outcomes, or that a threshold should guide care.

The distinction between estimands is essential. E1 asks whether the first additional domain is recorded after d1. E2 asks whether that first-code state progresses to a reproducibly coded confirmation. A single-code association that disappears for E2 is evidence of coding/ascertainment sensitivity, not evidence of robust clinical progression.

The export contains field-instance values and assessment-linked dates, not verified specimen collection times, adjudicated onset, severity, symptoms, treatment/adherence, or reasons for repeat testing. Exposures are therefore called **assessment-linked changes** and outcomes **recorded or confirmed coded transitions**, never biological slopes or clinical onset.

## Population and temporal design

Restrict to age 40–69 inclusive at d0, valid sex, five-source `eid` overlap, valid d0 and d1 dates from assessment (`53-0.0`, `53-1.0`), d0 < d1, and gap 365–3,650 days. Require agreement of main and assessment dates after normalization. At both d0 and d1, exactly one target domain must be present, it must be the same domain, and its earliest valid paired code date must be no later than d0. Exclude death on or before d1.

Freeze the phenotype after direct field inspection:

- diabetes: ICD-10 E10–E14 or ICD-9 250;
- cardiovascular disease: ICD-10 I20–I25, I50, I60–I69 or ICD-9 410–414, 428, 430–438;
- chronic kidney disease: ICD-10 N18/N19 or ICD-9 585;
- negative-control appendicitis: ICD-10 K35 or ICD-9 540.

Normalize code case and whitespace. A diagnosis record is valid only when the code and date share the same suffix. Scan every suffix. Keep code-only, date-only, and invalid-date records in an audit, but they cannot create an event. Dates are recorded dates, not onset.

For marker j, require finite canonical biological_samples values at both instances 0 and 1. Reverse HDL so worsening is positive. Compute annualized assessment-linked change `(x1-x0)/((d1-d0)/365.25)`; do not call it a biological slope. Existing-domain duration is `(d1 - first-domain-date)/365.25`. Optional creatinine/cystatin-C are descriptive renal-discordance sensitivities only and cannot silently enter the primary exposure.

Primary time zero is d1. Follow to the earliest event, death, observed censor boundary, or five years after d1. Do not impose a future one-year washout in the primary analysis. A d1+365 delayed-entry analysis is a labeled reverse-causation sensitivity, not the primary estimand.

## Confirmation-aware outcome estimands

### E1: first recorded additional domain

For each target domain absent at d1, take the earliest valid paired post-d1 code date. A unique earliest new domain is `SECOND_DOMAIN_UNIQUE` with its route from the d1 domain. If multiple target domains share the earliest date, classify `SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI` and retain the member set; never choose a route arbitrarily. A same-day death takes precedence in the primary analysis, with reverse precedence as sensitivity. Death is absorbing and competing.

Define `C_obs` as the maximum finite date across the outcome dates used (`41280-*`, `41281-*`, `40000-*`). It is not automatically an administrative cutoff. If source-level closure cannot be documented, report the observed-boundary estimand and label any five-year summary conditional on that unverified assumption.

### E2: two-stage confirmed transition

E2 is a state-process estimand:

- State 0 at d1: exactly one stable target domain and no additional domain.
- State 1: the first post-d1 valid paired code for an additional domain, with unique/multi-domain identity retained.
- State 2: the first qualifying confirmation of the new domain, defined by two distinct code/date slots for the same domain with `t_first < t_confirm`, 30 <= `t_confirm-t_first` <= 365 days. A repeated slot on the same date does not confirm.
- Death is a competing absorbing state from state 0 or state 1.
- Censoring is at the earliest of `C_obs`, d1+5 years, or a boundary-limited confirmation opportunity; it is not coded as “no confirmation” when 365 days of observation are unavailable.

The event time for the 0→1 transition is `t_first`; the 1→2 transition occurs at `t_confirm`. Thus a participant is not labeled confirmed at `t_first` using future information, and the analysis can distinguish predictors of first recording from predictors of subsequent persistence. Participants in state 1 who die before confirmation contribute a competing 1→death transition. If a first code occurs too near `C_obs` to allow 365 days, the confirmation process is right-censored for that participant, not treated as a negative confirmation.

Fit a transition-specific multistate model from d1 with 0→1, 0→death, 1→2, and 1→death hazards where support permits. Report state occupancy and cumulative incidence by horizon. The primary robust-progression contrast is the predicted probability of state 2 by a boundary-supported horizon, with E1 and 1→2 hazards reported separately. A sensitivity uses a 90–730-day confirmation window only if support and clinical interpretability are adequate; it is not silently pooled with the primary 30–365-day definition. The E2 rule is reproducible persistence of coded records, not adjudication or proof of independent clinical encounters.

For both E1 and E2, emit participant-level event/state files and tie tables by d1 domain, transition, event category, death tie, endpoint version, split, and locked-test status. Preserve all code-only/date-only/invalid-date cells, first-code dates, confirmation pairs, multi-domain member sets, deaths, censor dates, and confirmation-opportunity flags.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

All primary models use identical participants, transition architecture, death handling, time zero, censoring, split, preprocessing, weighting, uncertainty procedure, and locked evaluation.

### B0: current-level baseline

For each supported transition, fit cause-specific Cox or transition-specific hazards using d1 HbA1c, triglycerides, HDL, d1 domain, existing-domain duration, age, sex, d1–d0 gap, BMI `21001-0.0`, SBP `4080-0.0`, DBP `4079-0.0`, smoking `20116-0.0`, and center `54-0.0`. Fit-frozen restricted cubic splines apply to continuous predictors; categorical missingness follows a fit-defined rule. No d0-to-d1 change enters B0. B0 answers what current state alone predicts.

### M_change: coordinated burden and discordance

Using fit-partition standardized annualized changes, let z_H be HbA1c worsening, z_T triglyceride worsening, and z_R reverse-HDL worsening. Define C=(z_H+z_T+z_R)/sqrt(3), D1=(z_H-z_T)/sqrt(2), and D2=(z_H+z_T-2z_R)/sqrt(6). If empirical covariance is nonzero, estimate an orthogonalizing transform in fit data and freeze it.

Fit M_common=B0+C and M_full=B0+C+D1+D2 with exactly the same transition hazards, death transitions, population, split, censoring and evaluation. Prespecified domain interactions are permitted only where route support passes a threshold. The joint M_full-vs-B0 contrast tests whether dated change adds information; M_common-vs-B0 tests whether coordinated worsening suffices; M_full-vs-M_common tests whether marker discordance adds beyond common deterioration. The mechanistic alternative can reveal a coherent burden signal that separate current values lose, or marker-specific disagreement hidden by a common score. It is not presented as a validated biological latent factor.

A two-panel attention/recurrent model is deferred for a scientific reason, not because neural methods or GPUs are prohibited: two observations cannot identify meaningful sequence structure, and extra capacity cannot resolve assessment-date timing or return selection. A four-instance mixed-effects slope is deferred because verified assay instances 2/3 are absent. Revisit either only with at least three verified dated panels or a bounded diagnostic showing stable added information.

## Split, uncertainty, evaluation and interpretation

Hash participants, never rows, with SHA-256(namespace `ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1`, dataset, eid) modulo 100: 0–59 fit, 60–69 model/threshold decisions, 70–79 locked test, 80–99 reserved and unread. Fit transformations, weights, splines, model forms and alert thresholds only in fit/tuning data.

Report transition coefficients and 95% intervals; boundary-supported state-occupancy/cumulative-incidence predictions; integrated/time-dependent Brier score; calibration intercept, slope and error; discrimination where a binary contrast is defined; restricted mean event-free time; and paired 200-resample participant-bootstrap intervals for B0, M_common, and M_full contrasts. Report absolute risk differences and alert summaries at predeclared 5%, 10%, and 20% review thresholds, but call them computational summaries rather than validated actions.

Supportive requires at least 20 E1 events and 20 E2 state-2 events overall, at least 5 E2 state-2 events in the locked test, adequate support for every claimed route/transition, stable source/tie construction, and adequate weighting positivity. The locked uncertainty must show a prespecified incremental Brier/calibration improvement or at least 10% relative net-benefit improvement at a threshold chosen before locked evaluation. The direction must not reverse under assay-completeness weighting, source-concordance restriction, or the one-year lag. Appendicitis and within-state change permutation must not show a comparable signal. Five-year language additionally requires documented administrative cutoff provenance. A coordinated-burden conclusion requires C to add beyond B0; a discordance conclusion requires D1/D2 to add beyond C. State-2 support is a robustness claim about coded persistence, never adjudicated disease.

Adverse is an informative null/reversal or worse locked performance, disappearance after duration or completeness adjustment, a comparable negative-control/permutation signal, or no incremental information from dated change. This falsifies the selected conditional coded-event prognostic claim, not all metabolic biology or causal effects.

Inconclusive is inadequate state-1/state-2 or route support, failed positivity/effective sample size, unstable source/tie construction, an unverified boundary for the claimed horizon, poor convergence/calibration, intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, or unresolved selection/timing/phenotype sensitivity. A threshold result without expert-approved action is inconclusive for policy value. If a confirmation opportunity is systematically truncated at the data boundary, E2 is inconclusive rather than a negative finding.

Computationally checkable claims include hashes and headers, five-way joins, field locations, paired code/date construction, flow, date gaps/duration, state/event/tie/censor files, fitted models, locked predictions, uncertainty, calibration, alert tables, permutation, and sensitivity outputs. Clinical onset, independent encounter validity, reasons for testing/return, treatment/adherence, causal effects, monitoring benefit, transportability, nonreturner generalization, and policy validity require clinical adjudication, expert review, unavailable evidence, or another study.

## Alternatives and compute plan

B0 is retained because it is the simplest clinically interpretable answer to “does the later current level suffice?” M_common/M_full are selected over an unrestricted marker-change versus one-axis comparison because their nested, dimension-matched contrasts distinguish coherent deterioration from discordance under one state architecture. The confirmation-aware multistate formulation is selected over a single retrospectively confirmed endpoint because it avoids labeling the first code using future information and explicitly handles death and boundary censoring.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted B0, M_common, and M_full transition hazards; newly estimated first-code and confirmation-state probabilities; uncertainty for the incremental contrasts; locked absolute-risk/alert comparisons; and an output-linked supportive/adverse/inconclusive conclusion. A readiness package alone does not establish completion.

Expected solver work is CPU-first tabular fitting. The solver planning envelope is 16 CPUs, up to 8 GPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds.
