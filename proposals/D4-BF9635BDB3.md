## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens repeatedly nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, FABP1, CHI3L1,
IL-6/CRP family) as disease-specific preclinical biomarkers. It is unresolved whether a
protein-diagnosis association marks a disease-specific process (claim A) or mainly a shared systemic
morbidity/inflammation/aging (allostatic-load) axis elevated across many diagnoses and tracking
cumulative disease burden (claim B) [K1]. This decides whether a circulating protein signature is a
credible disease-specific early-detection/early-intervention target or only a generic risk/severity
correlate -- a consequential decision for biomarker programs and for the interpretation of
"disease-specific" proteomic hits.

## Leading claim vs strongest rivals and the discriminating observation
- **Claim A (disease-specific):** a protein's association is enriched for its target diagnosis beyond
  an axis shared across unrelated diagnoses.
- **Claim B (shared systemic axis):** the association is largely a projection of one cross-disease
  axis (inflammation/aging/allostatic load) [K1].
- **Strongest rival (reverse causation / subclinical disease at draw):** proteomic differences may
  reflect a prodromal or undiagnosed disease state present at the single draw rather than an
  antecedent systemic exposure; proteomic dysregulation is detectable up to ~15 years before a
  clinical diagnosis [K2]. This rival predicts that "shared-axis" signal will be concentrated in
  participants with any active morbidity/health-contact code (Z-chapter context) and in the organ
  system closest to the target diagnosis.
- **Measured-proxy caveat:** measured plasma abundances are imperfect proxies of underlying biology
  and carry organ-specific and shared genetic architecture [K3].
- **Discriminating observation:** a held-out cross-disease **transfer gap** Delta_D = AUC(D) for a
  protein/axis signature on its own disease D minus the mean AUC on a set of pre-specified
  *unrelated* disease blocks, evaluated out-of-sample, with a within-panel permutation band and a
  disease-orthogonalized-axis sensitivity. Claim A predicts Delta_D clearly above the null band;
  claim B predicts Delta_D inside the null band; the reverse-causation rival predicts Delta_D that
  shrinks materially when restricted to participants without the nearest-organ-system code and/or
  when a comorbidity-count covariate is included.

## Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative), recorded before selection
- **Baseline:** age + sex only (and a flexible-age spline variant). Establishes how much of any
  transfer is demographic.
- **Substantive alternative 1 (mechanistic/latent):** a low-dimensional **shared systemic axis**
  (first principal component / latent factor of the residualized protein matrix), plus the
  per-(protein,disease) **attenuation fraction** AF = 1 - |beta_adj|/|beta_unadj| after residualizing
  on that axis. This reveals *how much of each named association is shared-axis vs residual*
  -- information the age+sex baseline cannot provide.
- **Substantive alternative 2 (learned):** an **elastic-net multi-protein** classifier per disease
  block with participant-level sample splitting (fit the axis/weights on a discovery half, estimate
  the transfer gap in the held-out half). Reveals whether a *learned* multi-protein combination
  transfers across unrelated diseases better than the single shared 1-D axis, i.e. whether
  shared-axis transfer is a property of that one component or of the whole panel.
- **What each reveals that the other loses:** the 1-D axis collapses all shared structure into one
  score (interpretable, low variance explained ~0.17 in the parent's probe); the elastic net can
  exploit residual shared structure but is not directly interpretable as a single "axis". The
  comparison tests a substantive uncertainty (is shared transfer a 1-component or a
  panel-wide property?) rather than chasing a small predictive gain.
- **Target/inputs/eval:** target = out-of-sample AUC per disease block and Delta_D; inputs = the
  2,923 protein columns and prevalent ICD-10-derived disease blocks; split = participant-level
  discovery/hold-out halves; uncertainty = within-panel permutation band (>=1,000 permutations) plus
  the negative-control V01-Y98 block. Approximate budget: CPU-feasible (tabular, n=53,014); a bounded
  GPU run is optional only if the elastic-net repetition count warrants it. Selection reason: keeps
  the baseline and both alternatives on the same question, per the shared method-exploration rule.

## Analysis and falsification
- Pre-specified: fit the shared axis and elastic-net weights on the discovery half; estimate
  per-disease-block AUC and Delta_D on the hold-out half; build the null band by permuting disease
  labels within panel; report the attenuation fraction AF and the disease-orthogonalized-axis
  sensitivity.
- **Supportive of claim B:** Delta_D inside the permutation null band AND the first axis variance
  fraction above the band's 95th percentile AND negative-control block AUC not distinguishable from
  the band.
- **Adverse to claim B (supports claim A):** Delta_D clearly above the null band for a specific
  disease block, with AF small (association survives axis residualization).
- **Inconclusive:** broad permutation overlap, wide hold-out AUC intervals, or blocks failing the case
  rule; also if the reverse-causation restriction (excluding nearest-organ-system codes) materially
  attenuates Delta_D, the shared-axis reading is not established and a temporal design is required.
- These criteria are computable by an automatic verifier from the solver's outputs; whether a
  surviving residual association is a *mechanism* is **not** verifiable here and requires clinical
  adjudication, a documented ICD-10 code list, and a temporal/incident design not available in the
  joinable namespace.

## Accepted limitations
Prevalent-state/cross-sectional only (no incidence/washout/causal claim); inpatient ICD-10
under-ascertainment; comorbidity is a coarse ICD-10-derived proxy; the reverse-causation restriction
is a proxy, not a true disease-free-at-draw definition; the chapter diagnostic does not guarantee
power for the final case-case estimand and is not an association result.
