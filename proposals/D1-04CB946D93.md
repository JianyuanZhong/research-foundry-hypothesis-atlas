# Episode 15 child — treatment-stream corroboration of a documented respiratoryCare start

The parent’s respiratoryCare/respiratoryCharting design is preserved; this child adds the eICU `treatment` stream and explicitly separates treatment-record documentation, availability, timing, and clinical semantics.

## Scientific opening, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

The selected parent asks whether a pre-landmark state/process vector predicts a later strict documented respiratoryCare interval start, and whether that start is corroborated by a separate respiratoryCharting stream. A remaining consequential boundary is that respiratoryCharting may still be another documentation interface rather than an operational treatment record. eICU’s `treatment` table supplies a third, separately stored stream with ICU-relative `treatmentoffset`, hierarchical `treatmentstring`, and `activeupondischarge`. It may test operational convergence, but it does not prove that a treatment was ordered, delivered, started, stopped, or clinically indicated.

The strongest supported claim from the inspected evidence is that eICU links timestamped clinical tables across hospitals while only some monitoring/documentation processes are archived [K1], that temporally structured EHR prediction can be evaluated across centers while semantic harmonization remains a limitation [K2], and that missingness, irregular timing, and preprocessing should be made explicit in structured EHR analyses [K3]. Those works do not validate the semantics or completeness of eICU respiratoryCare, respiratoryCharting, or treatment rows. The parent’s positive-looking design is not an observed result and is not inherited as evidence.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** Among adult first-ICU S6 stays with no valid documented active respiratoryCare vent interval at minute 360, the parent pre-landmark state/process vector has a coherent hospital-held-out increment for a later strict documented respiratoryCare interval start only when that start is corroborated within a prespecified time window by a source-labeled respiratory treatment record in the independent `treatment` table. The increment should remain coherent after chart-availability, treatment-availability, ICU-exit, end-of-life, conflict, and source-time controls. If it predicts treatment-row availability or generic documentation density but not treatment-concordant starts, or if concordance is concentrated near exit or disappears under source-time nulls, the treatment stream adds documentation complexity rather than genuine corroborating information.

The estimand remains recorded documentation:

- `R_start_doc`: the parent’s strict, unambiguous documented respiratoryCare interval-start boundary.
- `T_resp_record_doc`: a row in `treatment` whose raw string belongs to a frozen source-labeled respiratory-treatment vocabulary.
- `C_RT_doc`: an `R_start_doc) with at least one `T_resp_record_doc` in [t−30,t+30] minutes.
- `T_start_doc`: the first qualifying treatment record in (360,720] before ICU exit, reported separately from `R_start_doc`.
- `C_RCT_doc`: the parent’s `R_start_doc) that has both qualifying respiratoryCharting and treatment records in their respective ±30-minute windows; this is a secondary three-stream endpoint.

A treatment row is never called a delivered intervention, ventilation, treatment initiation, benefit, or indication. A positive `C_RT_doc) supports reproducible cross-table documentation concordance only.

Exclude suctioning, physiotherapy, weaning, and removal from this candidate initiation-oriented subset. The exclusions are operational class definitions, not claims about clinical meaning.

## Interpretation and decision rules

**Supportive** requires: strict respiratoryCare and treatment audits are reproducible; the treatment class is frozen outcome-blind; at least five viable hospitals have positive treatment opportunity and event support; C_RT_doc and independently defined T_start_doc are directionally coherent in held-out hospitals for both model families; the paired process increment exceeds the inherited negligible margin where estimable with uncertainty; and the result is not explained by availability weighting, EOL, discharge adjacency, treatment timing, class choice, conflict handling, chart-only effects, or one hospital. This supports a reproducible cross-table documented respiratory transition. It does not support delivered ventilation/oxygen, treatment efficacy, causality, mechanism, or clinical deployment.

**Adverse** includes R_start_doc without treatment concordance; a treatment increment confined to generic availability; disappearance in treatment-observable or weighted analyses; treatment rows clustering near exit/EOL; large sensitivity to the time window; treatment/chart concordance explained by the schedule null; one-hospital or one-vocabulary dependence; or a boosting-only gain. If C_RCT_doc remains positive but C_RT_doc does not, retain the parent’s chart-concordance interpretation and conclude that treatment does not add corroboration.

**Inconclusive/deferral** includes too few strict starts or viable hospitals, poor positivity/effective sample size, unstable raw vocabulary, contradictory offsets, unresolved treatment-class semantics, or intervals spanning meaningful and negligible margins. An imprecise null is not refutation. Continue to blinded critical-care adjudication only if the operational cross-table claim passes. Revise to a documentation/availability study if selection dominates. Defer invasive, physiologic, administered-treatment, causal, and actionability claims in all cases without order/administration/device provenance, reliable clinical event times, and expert review.

## Verifier and clinical limits

A verifier can check the treatment source path/hash/header, ordinary-file status, exact columns, patientunitstayid joins, cohort and temporal boundaries, raw-string preservation, frozen class rule, offset windows, treatment availability, no-post-window leakage, competing exits, folds, null assignments, saved predictions, uncertainty, and conclusion-to-output links. It cannot decide whether treatmentstring means an order, administration, delivered therapy, ventilator support, oxygen delivery, clinical initiation, or benefit; whether treatmentoffset is care time; whether separate tables are institutionally independent; or whether a clinical decision should change. Those claims require unavailable order/administration/device/interface evidence, blinded clinician adjudication, and an external or prospective study.

The treatment stream therefore provides genuine scientific information only if it produces stable, independent operational corroboration beyond chart availability and generic documentation density. If not, it adds complexity and should be reported as an informative negative/deferral rather than retained as a stronger clinical endpoint.
