# Executed UKB baseline-only decision-value experiment

## Status and question

This is the executed child of `[prior hypothesis]`, preserving its assessed-valid frozen design. The question was whether baseline-only cystatin-C discordance, `Z = log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)`, adds worthwhile decision value over baseline creatinine-eGFR, age, and sex for low repeat creatinine-eGFR status at the next observed repeat among selected first-repeat attenders.

The computable estimand is schedule-marginal status at the next observed repeat in the selected cohort. It is not four-year risk, cumulative incidence, onset time, measured GFR, adjudicated CKD, chronicity, causal benefit, or a policy that changes visit timing. The fixed-horizon stop remains mandatory: `STOP_UNIDENTIFIED_FIXED_HORIZON` applies to any such interpretation.

## Aggregate result

The managed executor completed successfully. All 100 model fits converged, all 16,372 participants received finite held-out predictions in every repeat, and all capacity counts were exact.

- Primary 2% net-benefit contrast: `Delta_NB_0.02 = 0.0000554705`, or `0.0555` net true-positive equivalents per 1,000. The 98% paired participant-bootstrap percentile interval was `[-0.0004681, 0.0005713]`. The prespecified worthwhile floor was 0.001 and the threshold gate did not pass.
- Primary fixed 10% capacity contrast: `Delta_E10 = +3.7` outcomes per 1,637 slots. The 98% interval was `[-4.901, 12.901]`. The prespecified worthwhile floor was +5 and the capacity gate did not pass.
- The threshold contrast was positive in 7/10 repeats; the capacity contrast was positive in 10/10 repeats. Repeated positivity alone does not satisfy the frozen interval and practical-floor gates.
- Calibration and support passed: Mc-B calibration intercept `-0.01280`, slope `0.99625`, observed-minus-predicted risk `-0.0000189`; pooled 2% actions/events were 3,100/213 for M0-B and 3,119/214 for Mc-B.
- All three timing bands met the minimum support requirements. The two-degree-of-freedom interaction p-value was 0.07684 and no supported timing-band simultaneous upper bound was below zero; the schedule-robustness audit therefore did not block the schedule-marginal analysis.
- The 10% capacity event decomposition was `-1.7` outcomes in baseline eGFRcr 60–<75 and `+5.4` in eGFRcr ≥75, summing to +3.7. Mean model overlap Jaccard was 0.7501.
- Baseline-only log-loss difference was `-0.0011512` overall, `-0.0132978` in the borderline stratum, and `-0.0003290` outside it. These are explanatory diagnostics, not decision-policy evidence.

## Frozen classification

`INCONCLUSIVE`.

This is not a supportive policy signal because neither co-primary practical/interval gate passed. It is not the adverse operational-stop classification because the 98% upper bounds did not rule out both worthwhile floors (`0.0005713` is below the 0.001 floor, but `12.901` is not below +5). It is also not threshold-policy-uncertified: calibration and pooled support passed. The result is therefore inconclusive rather than evidence of no biological information or evidence of clinical benefit.
