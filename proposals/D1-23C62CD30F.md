# A frozen operational-calibration and domain-held-out shadow replay for pulmonary discharge handoff

## Decision question and targeted repair

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. The clinical question, population, chronology, exact atom and route, controls, sampling, ledger, and conclusion ceiling are inherited and frozen. The unresolved bridge is operational rather than a new clinical endpoint: a stored-text placement result can be reproducible in a retrospectively assembled corpus yet fail when a prospective silent service receives a late, missing, duplicated, or differently serialized note, or when its workflow mix differs from the pooled retrospective mix.

The new hypothesis is therefore:

> **H1 (operational bridge hypothesis):** Conditional on an already eligible, route-frozen pulmonary opportunity, the inherited target-linked placement indicator `B` and route can be emitted from a strictly information-available discharge packet under a pre-specified data contract with stable behavior across held-out MIMIC calendar bands and terminal-service/workflow domains, without silently converting unavailable, late, duplicate, or conflicting evidence into a positive placement.

This is not a claim that the recommendation was delivered, viewed, clinically appropriate, safe, completed, or beneficial. MIMIC cannot establish those facts. A supportive result only nominates a separately governed prospective **silent** shadow run that measures them directly.

## What is supported versus what remains untested

The available evidence supports construction of admission-linked radiology and discharge artifacts, their storage timestamps, a unique canonical discharge summary under the inherited rules, and service/admission joins. It supports only a retrospective stored-documentation estimand. It does not establish actual notification, recipient responsibility, viewing, interface rendering, order/referral/scheduling, outside care, completion, patient preference, or clinical correctness.

The unresolved claim tested here is narrower and falsifiable: whether the frozen text-defined output remains measurable and fails closed when the same information is presented as an operationally realistic, time-ordered packet, and whether that measurement is robust to held-out service/calendar domains. The replay is a calibration and transportability screen, not prospective validation or causal inference.

## Frozen inheritance and prohibited changes

The following are immutable from the parents and must be copied verbatim into the executable protocol and ledger:

* first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity MIMIC pulmonary frame;
* reciprocal pre-discharge radiology report/addendum chronology with `D = admissions.dischtime`, and the inherited report/addendum eligibility and canonical-DS selection rules;
* one exact canonical discharge summary (DS), the inherited `G=(E, terminal curr_service, W)` atom, and the inherited `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER` route derivation;
* route-independent unit enumeration, target freeze, sealed route-concealed C/S/H/holistic panels, independent challenge and negative controls, and all-unit adverse precedence (one unresolved or unsafe unit controls its admission);
* the inherited H+ exact atom-preserving placement indicator `B`, pre-label target/control universe, two-phase positive-probability whole-admission sampling, additive distinct-admission ledger including fixed controls and one reserve, and maximum 900 distinct reviewed admissions;
* the inherited HT/Hájek finite-population estimands, nested design uncertainty, atom-level gates, default-DEFER/no pooled rescue/no neighboring-atom rescue, and no model/consensus as a substitute for adjudication.

No additional reader-selected admissions, targets, routes, labels, outcomes, or post-discharge evidence may tune the replay. No POE, orders, referrals, prescriptions, pharmacy, scheduling, or other intervention-like field may enter target selection, route, or B.

## Prospective-contract shadow replay

For each already-sampled admission, construct a packet at a sequence of fixed cutoffs relative to `D`: `D-24h`, `D-6h`, `D-1h`, `D`, `D+1h`, and `D+24h`. These cutoffs are simulation checkpoints, not new eligibility windows. A note is packet-visible iff its permitted `storetime` is non-null and `storetime <= cutoff`; `charttime` cannot make a late-stored note visible. The canonical DS remains the inherited artifact; if its storetime is after a cutoff, the packet has `DS_UNAVAILABLE`, and no `B=1` is allowed. At `D` the packet is the candidate silent discharge packet; later cutoffs are a delayed-arrival stress test only and never alter the frozen target or route.

The prospective data contract is frozen before target labels: one immutable event per `(hadm_id, atom_id, packet_cutoff, parser_version)` with source note IDs, source `storetime`s, cutoff, route, `B`, and one of `AVAILABLE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `DUPLICATE_CONFLICT`, `MISSING_TIME`, `PARSE_UNKNOWN`, or `DEFER`. The first observed output is append-only; later-arriving text may create a new version but may not overwrite an earlier packet. Exact duplicate `note_id`s are deduplicated by identity; conflicting text or field ordinals are `DUPLICATE_CONFLICT` and fail closed. Missing timestamps, malformed dates, unavailable DS, and any parser ambiguity are `DEFER`, not negative evidence and not successful placement.

For each admission, compute without new human labels:

* `availability(D)`: whether all source components required by the inherited route and `B` were visible by `D`;
* `latency`: `B`'s first eligible packet cutoff minus `D`, reported only where a finite storage clock exists;
* `serialization_integrity`: whether replaying rows in nondecreasing `storetime`, then stable `(note_id, field_ordinal)` order, reproduces the frozen output exactly;
* `late-arrival`, `duplicate/conflict`, `unknown`, and `DS-unavailable` indicators; and
* `domain` = `(anchor_year_group, terminal curr_service)` with explicit `UNKNOWN` levels, never dropped or pooled into a favorable domain.

The `D+` checkpoints are not outcomes and cannot be used to relabel a `D` failure. They quantify an operational failure mode and preserve the distinction between a missing MIMIC artifact and missing care.

## Domain-held-out design and controls

Keep the parent's positive-probability sample and admission ledger unchanged. Use the exact inherited design weights for every replay estimand. Freeze domain membership from admissions/patients/services before any H/C/B labels. Report the full eligible census for computational availability where feasible, and the sampled finite-population inference for all label-dependent endpoints.

The primary held-out stress test is leave-one-domain-family-out: separately evaluate each sufficiently supported `anchor_year_group` and terminal-service domain while the parser and all thresholds remain frozen. A domain with fewer than 30 sampled admissions or fewer than 10 finite, non-unknown target/control opportunities is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not merged. The pooled result cannot rescue a failed domain. Domain-level point estimates are descriptive; no raw calendar-year transport claim is made.

Use the inherited target/control and route-scrambled controls, with these additional outcome-blind operational controls:

* **Cutoff permutation control:** shuffle packet cutoffs within admission before running the parser; any apparent improvement over the correctly ordered replay indicates cutoff leakage.
* **Storetime isolation control:** replace all permitted times by a common sentinel while retaining text and IDs; positive availability/latency findings must disappear or become `DEFER`.
* **Field-order perturbation control:** reverse `field_ordinal` in a synthetic copy of detail rows; the parser must either reproduce only when order is irrelevant by the frozen dictionary or fail closed, never silently change a positive route.
* **Domain-label permutation control:** permute domain labels after outputs are sealed; domain contrasts must be null within finite-sample uncertainty, while the overall output is unchanged.
* **Late-arrival monotonicity control:** adding a row with `storetime > cutoff` cannot change the earlier packet output. Violations are a hard failure.

Synthetic control packets are code tests, not clinical observations and are excluded from the estimand and ledger.

## Estimands, preregistered gates, and inference

The primary operational estimand is the finite-population, design-weighted proportion of frozen target/control opportunities with `B=1` available at `D` and with exact output invariant under canonical serialization. Secondary estimands are (i) the proportion `B=1` only after `D`, (ii) delayed-arrival distribution, (iii) packet-level `DEFER`/unknown rate, and (iv) domain-specific differences in availability and output invariance. All are admission-level adverse: an admission is positive for an adverse endpoint if any decision-relevant unit is adverse or unresolved, using inherited worst-unit precedence.

Use HT totals and Hájek ratios with the parent's phase-1/phase-2 inclusion probabilities; calculate exact finite-population design variance where the realized sample permits and nested phase-1/phase-2 resampling for the inherited uncertainty. Report point estimates, two-sided 95% design intervals, effective sample size, sampled and census denominators, and missingness. Do not use model-based shrinkage, pooled random effects, or imputation to pass a domain.

Before labels, freeze operational governance margins:

* `B-at-D` availability lower confidence bound at least 0.90 in every supported domain;
* `D`-packet parser/serialization exact-match lower confidence bound at least 0.98 in every supported domain;
* adverse `DEFER + unknown + conflict` upper confidence bound at most 0.05 for the target and at most 0.05 for each fixed control family;
* no more than 0.05 absolute difference between target and route-scrambled control in the specificity contrast, with simultaneous 95% intervals;
* no supported-domain target availability or exact-match point estimate more than 0.10 below the pooled target estimate; and
* every cutoff and permutation control must pass its deterministic monotonicity/leakage assertions.

These are operational nomination margins, not clinical safety thresholds. Margins are not estimated or changed after inspecting labels. If any supported domain lacks positive-probability support, finite effective sample size, or the required control, its gate is `INCONCLUSIVE` and no pooled rescue is permitted. A global nomination requires every supported domain to pass; otherwise the result is adverse or inconclusive as specified above.

## Interpretation and falsification

**Supportive:** all supported held-out domains pass all gates; `D` availability and exact serialization pass; late/unknown/conflict rates are bounded; target/control and route-scrambled controls behave as preregistered; and all ledger, seal, monotonicity, and source-manifest checks pass. This supports only that a source-bound, fail-closed representation is ready to be tested in a separately governed prospective silent shadow run with actual packet receipt, view, responsibility, UI rendering, downstream orders/referrals, scheduling/completion, outside-care capture, patient preference, and independent clinical adjudication collected prospectively.

**Adverse:** a supported domain fails availability, serialization, adverse-unknown, specificity, or leakage controls; an earlier packet changes after a late row; or a parser emits a positive placement from unavailable/conflicting data. Then no prospective nomination is made for that domain or globally if the global gate requires all domains. This identifies an operational bridge failure, not clinical harm.

**Inconclusive:** sparse domain support, missing storage times, absent canonical DS, non-identifiable joins, excessive nonresponse/unknowns, or variance intervals too wide for a gate. Report the failure mode and do not convert it to success by dropping domains, using later text, or changing thresholds.

The experiment cannot determine delivery, viewing, responsibility, appropriateness, safety, follow-up, benefit, or causality. It also cannot prove transport to another hospital, exact future calendar years, or prospective workflow equivalence. Those stronger claims require an actual-calendar silent implementation with event logs and independent clinical review, followed—if warranted—by a governed comparative effectiveness or safety study. The replay's computationally checkable claims are source availability, chronology, serialization, parser determinism, design-weighted documentation rates, domain contrasts, and control behavior only.
