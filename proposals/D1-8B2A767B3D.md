# Airway-independent temporal falsification of the eICU ventilation-documentation state audit

## Why this child is a substantive repair

This child of `[prior hypothesis]` preserves the locked target-independent denominator variants, strict physiology and exclusions, variant-specific first-stay freezing, fixed landmarks, and post-freeze care-plan classification. It does not reconstruct or alter the cohort, population, or estimand. It completes the parent's missing work: independently classified present and future windows, explicit state transitions, component-level discordance, full parser accounting, stay-clustered confidence intervals, hospital-cluster and common-hospital robustness, landmark robustness, and empirical validation of interval-field encoding.

The repair changes the scientific interpretation. The parent's conservative airway-containing proxy suggested substantially less positive evidence in `unbounded_only`. However, overlap class is itself determined by airway recency. The repaired analysis therefore reports that protocol proxy for continuity but treats it as descriptive and partly tautological, and uses an airway-independent documentation proxy for the non-tautological falsification. Under that proxy, the large contrast disappears. This is an adverse/inconclusive result for the claim that the old-airway group has materially less contemporaneous ventilation-related documentation; it is a useful negative finding, not a validation of active ventilation.

## Value-aware components and ordered states

Mappings were restricted to inspected values. Invasive airway values are `oral ett`, `nasal ett`, `tracheostomy`, `double-lumen tube`, and `cricothyrotomy`; `no artificial airway` and `none` are negative. Strong chart positives are inspected active `RT Vent On/Off` values, invasive-compatible values of `Mechanical Ventilator Mode` or `Ventilator Support Mode`, and numeric `Ventilator Population=1`. Plausible setting families are bounded set rate, set tidal volume, machine minute volume, machine tidal volume, and machine pressure. A contemporaneous treatment string containing mechanical ventilation but excluding non-invasive ventilation is separate corroboration. Intubation and extubation note types are timestamped corroboration.

NIV, CPAP, BiPAP, spontaneous, and pressure-support signals are retained as alternative-modality components and are not automatically forced to be invasive negatives. Explicit negatives are RT Off/Suspended, trach-mask/T-piece, no artificial airway, and extubation. Positive and negative evidence within 30 minutes is `conflicting`; otherwise the latest decisive event wins; neither is `ambiguous`.

Two prespecified documentation proxies are reported:

1. **Protocol proxy (continuity/descriptive):** exact strong chart evidence, intubation note, window-local invasive airway plus at least one plausible setting family, or a plausible setting plus mechanical-ventilation treatment. It contains the component defining overlap class and is therefore not an independent test of group differences.
2. **Airway-independent proxy (non-tautological sensitivity):** exact strong chart evidence, intubation note, at least two plausible setting families, or setting plus mechanical-ventilation treatment. It omits airway entirely.

Both are structured documentation proxies. Neither adjudicates active invasive ventilation.

## Executed results

Full-source extraction generated exactly all 3,788 locked rows; transition matrices sum to every group denominator. The deterministic finalizer ran 5,000 percentile-bootstrap replicates with fixed seeds. Stay clustering is primary because stays can contribute repeated landmarks. Hospital clustering is a documentation-system sensitivity.

### Present states

Protocol positive rates were 126/1,148 (10.98%) in `unbounded_only`, 228/1,462 (15.60%) in `retained_24h_only`, and 1,138/1,178 (96.60%) in `retained_6h`. The huge unbounded-versus-6h risk difference was -85.63 percentage points (stay-cluster 95% CI -87.89 to -83.30; hospital-cluster -92.62 to -77.48). This cannot be treated as independent evidence: by construction, window-local airway occurred in 0/1,148, 0/1,462, and 1,178/1,178 rows, respectively. The protocol result mainly restates group formation.

Airway-independent positive rates were instead 1,079/1,148 (93.99%), 1,402/1,462 (95.90%), and 1,083/1,178 (91.94%). The risk difference for `unbounded_only` minus `retained_6h` was +2.05 points (stay-cluster 95% CI -0.41 to +4.49; hospital-cluster -2.67 to +9.55). Versus `retained_24h_only` it was -1.91 points (stay-cluster -3.65 to -0.12; hospital-cluster -4.74 to +0.94). Thus there is no hospital-robust material deficit in the old-airway group, and the direction versus 6h is opposite the original hypothesis.

Equal-weight common-hospital estimates reinforce that conclusion. Requiring at least 10 rows from each compared group, the airway-independent difference was -1.55 points across 16 hospitals for unbounded versus 24h (hospital range -20.77 to +8.33) and +0.52 points across 10 hospitals for unbounded versus 6h (range -13.16 to +19.00). Hospital heterogeneity is substantial.

### Component discordance and dominance

At least two plausible setting families occurred in 1,076/1,148 (93.73%) unbounded, 1,401/1,462 (95.83%) 24h-only, and 1,100/1,178 (93.38%) 6h rows. By contrast, strong chart positives occurred in 0, 1, and 37 rows; intubation notes in 0, 0, and 6; and contemporaneous mechanical-ventilation treatment in 128, 231, and 173. Therefore the airway-independent proxy is overwhelmingly a setting-documentation proxy and does not independently establish invasive support.

Raw coexistence of protocol-positive and negative components occurred in 32 retained-6h rows; 21 were temporally within 30 minutes and classified conflicting. Present protocol state counts in retained-6h were 1,138 positive, 21 conflicting, 15 ambiguous, and 4 negative. Raw protocol discordance was zero in the other two groups under the mapped components. Alternative-modality signals occurred in 3, 14, and 13 rows. These are component coexistence findings, not clinical adjudications of modality.

### Future timing window and transitions

Airway-independent future positive rates were 85.80% unbounded, 85.77% 24h-only, and 82.94% 6h, decreases from present of -8.19 points (stay-cluster 95% CI -10.22 to -6.12), -10.12 (-11.95 to -8.41), and -9.00 (-10.98 to -7.08), respectively. Hospital-cluster intervals also excluded zero. These declines show that future-window evidence is not identical to present evidence, but they do not prove cessation because future observation is not guaranteed.

Exact airway-independent transitions include positive-to-positive 968/1,079 unbounded, 1,237/1,402 24h-only, and 961/1,083 6h; positive-to-ambiguous 105, 132, and 93; and ambiguous-to-positive 17, 17, and 13, respectively. These future-only positives demonstrate why future evidence cannot be called a null placebo.

For the airway-containing protocol, future positive rates were 13.07%, 34.20%, and 52.21%. The retained-6h present-to-future decrease was -44.40 points (stay-cluster 95% CI -47.74 to -41.17; hospital-cluster -51.81 to -33.22), whereas unbounded changed +2.09 points with both cluster intervals crossing zero, and 24h-only increased +18.60 points. Protocol transitions are mechanically sensitive to which window contains airway documentation and are a timing diagnostic, not evidence of bedside state change.

Across landmarks, airway-independent present positivity remained high and setting-dominated: 92.0%-95.8% in unbounded, 93.4%-97.6% in 24h-only, and 89.8%-93.0% in 6h. No stable landmark pattern recovers the parent's large non-tautological deficit.

### Interval-field semantic falsification

On cohort stays, `ventendoffset` was exactly zero in all 130,612 respiratoryCare records. A literal active-interval rule therefore identified zero present intervals in every group. Treating zero as an open-ended current interval would identify 62 unbounded, 140 24h-only, and 889 6h rows, but this is an explicitly unvalidated encoding sensitivity and must not enter the primary state. `priorventstartoffset` and `priorventendoffset` describe prior intervals and remain excluded from current-state classification.

## Strongest supported claim, hypothesis decision, and falsification

The strongest supported claim is that structured ventilator-setting documentation is highly prevalent across all three locked airway-lookback classes, while strong value-specific chart, note, and treatment corroboration is sparse. Once airway is removed, the old-airway class does not show the large documentation deficit implied by the parent's airway-containing state result; hospital-cluster intervals cross zero for both comparisons, common-hospital effects are near zero on average, and setting families dominate the proxy. The stale-airway hypothesis is therefore not supported by this non-tautological structured-documentation experiment and remains clinically unresolved.

Support for the original hypothesis would have required a materially lower airway-independent positive rate in unbounded rows with stay- and hospital-cluster intervals excluding zero in the same direction, consistency across landmarks and common hospitals, and no single setting family dominating. Those falsification gates were not met. An adverse result is a similar or higher rate in unbounded rows; that occurred versus retained-6h. An inconclusive result is a small contrast with hospital intervals crossing zero and proxy dominance by nonspecific documentation; that describes the overall evidence.

This negative result does not show that old airway records are valid, that any row was actively invasively ventilated, or that broad settings are clinically equivalent across groups. Resolving active ventilation requires blinded episode-level expert adjudication of tube/device insertion and removal, ventilator mode and settings, NIV modality, temporal continuity, and discharge/transfer boundaries, ideally against a prespecified gold standard. A stratified random adjudication sample enriched for proxy discordance and present/future transitions would estimate positive predictive value, negative predictive value, and inter-rater reliability. Active invasive ventilation, SBT delivery, liberation quality, care quality, causality, and patient benefit cannot be established automatically from this audit and require clinical review or a separate outcome study.

## Computability and interpretation gates

Automatically checkable claims are: exact inherited row/group counts; source parser counts; window boundaries; absence of pre-L carry into future classification; mapped component counts; mutual exclusivity and transition sums; landmark/hospital tables; bootstrap seeds, replicates, estimates, and intervals; and observed interval-field distributions. Not automatically checkable are clinical truth of ventilation, continuity, modality, clinician intent, SBT delivery, quality, causal effects, benefit, or transportability.

The conclusion must follow the computed direction. Supportive output may justify only a documentation-state difference. Adverse output must be reported as evidence against the operational hypothesis, not recoded. Inconclusive output must retain uncertainty and component dependence. No result from this design permits a claim of clinically adjudicated active invasive ventilation.
