# Proposal: specificity-invariant post-TACE trajectories versus coding-process artifacts

## Episode 16 child and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of assessed valid parent [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the HCC snapshot, one encounter-constrained first TACE-like index per patient, day-45 risk set, days 46-365 follow-up, dynamic first post-day-45 diagnostic-contact landmark, measurement-selection estimands, capture-inclusive observation state, patient-level temporal split, competing pathways, uncertainty, falsification and strict clinical evidence boundary.

The single remaining threat it repairs is endpoint semantics. Encounter identity prevents nearby diagnostic and therapeutic encounters from being silently merged, but a therapeutic-looking procedure string can still be a local billing/coding or care-capture state rather than disease-specific reassessment or treatment. This is material in the actual source: procedures contains 338,040 rows and 7,781 labels; standalone hepatic angiography is the most frequent relevant label (33,332), while explicit catheter embolization, arterial chemoembolization, bundled embolization/angiography and literal TACE occur with different frequencies. A broad lexical outcome could therefore produce a trajectory association that is really a label-family or encounter-complexity association.

The repair is a preregistered endpoint-specificity ladder and a coding-process comparator on the same risk sets. The strict therapeutic endpoint is the primary target; the parent broad TACE-family endpoint is a replication sensitivity; diagnostic-code-only, non-liver and pre-index placebo targets are falsification controls. Every endpoint receives the same B0/Bcode/B2 and M3 analysis, split, horizon, standardization target and uncertainty. The future solver must newly construct endpoint and episode manifests, fit locked models, estimate endpoint-specific incremental trajectory information and produce a specificity/capture interpretation map. No computation here is presented as a solved result.

## Evidence boundary and unresolved claim

The strongest evidence supports only that the HCC snapshot contains linkable encounters, procedures, diagnoses, dated labs and dated examinations, and that the procedure vocabulary mixes diagnostic and therapeutic-looking strings. A read-only audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 15,874 without a start time and 7,781 distinct labels. Relevant examples include 33,332 hepatic arteriography rows, 27,411 transcatheter hepatic arterial embolization rows, 26,385 arterial chemoembolization rows, 10,775 rows combining embolization and angiography, 8,609 transarterial chemoembolization (TACE) rows and 1,538 literal TACE rows. The lab file has 28,159,928 rows: 272,615 AFP-related, 525,310 albumin-related, 308,946 total-bilirubin, 1,590,235 platelet-related and 297,735 INR-related rows. Numeric availability is not validated clinical measurement because there is no laboratory unit column. Diagnoses have no native timestamp. These are source facts, not evidence of indication, viable tumour, response, recurrence, benefit, mortality or outside care.

The unresolved question is:

> After a first recorded TACE-like episode for HCC, does an early AFP/hepatic trajectory contain reproducible information about entering a later recorded therapeutic pathway that survives progressively stricter procedure semantics and adjustment for measurement, encounter and coding intensity, or does it mainly predict who is measured and how local care is coded?

The primary specificity-gradient hypothesis is:

1. Among adults in the exact day-45 risk set with no encounter-constrained therapeutic liver-directed episode through day 45, B2 versus Bcode will reduce the locked-test alert fraction at fixed 90% sensitivity by at least 5 percentage points for a first strict therapeutic TACE-coded episode with a prior recorded reassessment opportunity, with a patient-bootstrap 95% interval excluding zero.
2. The direction will replicate for the parent broad TACE-family endpoint, and the strict-versus-broad gain difference will be no more than 5 points. A larger broad-endpoint gain is evidence against disease-specific interpretation.
3. The same contrast at the dynamic first post-day-45 diagnostic-only contact landmark will be present for strict therapeutic TACE, but not for diagnostic-code-only, non-liver capture or pre-index placebo outcomes under identical calibration and split rules.
4. M3 is a process-model test, not a complexity contest: it should show whether a latent biomarker state predicts strict therapeutic transitions beyond separate observation/coding states, but need not beat B2 to support the scientific question.

These are reproducible local recorded-care criteria, not treatment thresholds, causal effects, clinical utility or recommendations.

## Population, episode construction and time

Use only HCC and one index per patient. Aggregate every child table to (Patient Master Index, Encounter Number) before joining encounters; never make a many-to-many child join.

1. Parse procedures.Start Time and End Time. The primary episode groups timed procedure rows with the same Patient Master Index and Visit Number, ordered by start time, with rows within 24 hours grouped only inside that encounter. Retain raw labels, row count, family hits, composite status, source and missing-time flags. Untimed rows cannot define an event and are reported in the audit.
2. Define the first index as the earliest timed encounter-constrained episode containing literal TACE, a label containing transarterial chemoembolization, or both hepatic artery and embolization. Retain exact ties. Keep the parent 24-hour patient-time grouping, same-encounter same-start-date grouping and no-24-hour same-encounter grouping as locked sensitivities.
3. Require age >=18 and nonmissing Sex from the index encounter. Require a diagnosis name containing hepatocellular carcinoma on a two-key joined encounter within 180 days before through 7 days after index. Since diagnoses has no native time, inherit encounters.Encounter Time only for this cohort criterion and report the ordering limitation.
4. Restrict index time to 2025-01-01 00:00:00 or earlier for a nominal 365-day horizon. Report maximum valid follow-up for every index. Complete-horizon primary analysis requires day-365 local representation; right-censored analysis is secondary.
5. Exclude a patient from the later-action risk set if an encounter-constrained therapeutic liver-directed episode occurs after the first 24 hours through day 45; report that early pathway separately. Diagnostic-only contact is not automatically excluded.
6. Stop fixed-landmark predictors at day 45 and use days 46-365. Use 2010-2022 fit, 2023 tuning and 2024 through the cutoff locked test when each has support; otherwise deterministic participant hashing, labelled internal. No participant crosses splits.
7. The dynamic analysis starts at the first encounter-constrained diagnostic-only contact after day 45. Freeze predictors strictly before that contact and estimate 30-, 90- and 320-day competing transition risks. This is prognostic recorded-care analysis, not a causal intervention comparison.

## Endpoint-specificity ladder

Create the vocabulary audit before fitting. Normalize only Unicode, whitespace and case; do not use model-generated semantic expansion. Freeze positive token lists and counts in procedure_vocabulary_audit.csv.

- Y_strict_TACE: an episode has a row whose original label contains literal TACE, chemoembolization, transarterial chemoembolization, transcatheter hepatic arterial embolization, or hepatic arteriography and chemoembolization. Standalone hepatic arteriography, catheterization and insertion do not qualify. If a strict label co-occurs with an unresolved composite or alternate therapeutic family, classify it mixed_or_unclassified for the primary strict endpoint.
- Y_broad_TACE: the parent definition: literal TACE, a label containing transarterial chemoembolization, or both hepatic artery and embolization, with the same encounter-constrained identity. This is a replication endpoint, not confirmed therapy.
- Y_diagnostic_code_only: hepatic/arterial angiography, catheterization or insertion tokens, including hepatic artery angiography or hepatic artery catheterization, but no strict or broad therapeutic token and no alternate therapeutic family.
- Y_alternate_liver: an audited non-TACE liver-directed family, including resection, transplant, ablation, radiotherapy, local perfusion or other frozen family labels.
- Y_mixed_or_unclassified: competing family hits, ambiguous composites or labels not safely assignable. Never silently promote these to therapeutic events.
- Y_non_liver: a timed non-liver procedure episode, used as a capture-control outcome with its own first-event risk set.
- Y_end_local_observation: no later qualifying encounter, admission or discharge timestamp under the administrative rule. This is not death, outside-care cessation or deterioration.
- Y_observed_no_therapeutic_action: no classified therapeutic event through day 365 while locally observable.

The primary endpoint is first Y_strict_TACE from day 46 through day 365, descriptively stratified by whether a strict local reassessment opportunity occurred before that episode. A strict opportunity is a dated liver-relevant lab or liver/abdominal examination in the preceding 14 days, excluding the episode encounter; 30-day and encounter-only rules are sensitivities. This remains an opportunity construct, not adjudicated imaging or tumour reassessment. Broad, diagnostic-only, alternate, mixed, non-liver and administrative states compete in the transition system.

## Predictors and capture/coding separation

Trajectory availability requires at least one parseable numeric AFP in days -30 to -1 and days 7-45, plus at least one of albumin, total bilirubin, the observed coagulation-ratio assay or platelets in each window. Preserve partial patterns and missingness. Use each assay’s last numeric pre-index and first early-post value; retain log1p AFP, changes, frozen direction indicators, assay identity, timing and measurement counts. Do not pool AFP isoform with total AFP, infer units, construct ALBI/MELD or apply unit-dependent thresholds.

All models stop at day 45:

- B0 is a regularized discrete-time competing-transition baseline using pre-index history, demographics and ordinary observation features.
- Bcode is B0 plus measurement availability/missingness, encounter and examination timing, and explicit coding-process features: timed procedure-row count, distinct procedure-label count, composite-label fraction, same-encounter procedure density, procedure source distribution/missingness, diagnostic-code-only count, mixed/unclassified count, non-liver procedure count, dated examination opportunity and orders opportunity. It has no numeric AFP/hepatic values.
- B2 is Bcode plus AFP/hepatic values, changes, timing, assay identity and missingness. The primary scientific contrast is B2 minus Bcode; B2 versus B0 is a decomposition.

Fit observation/transport weights on fit data only for a full-index target; report overlap, positivity, truncation and effective sample size. Show both paired-observed and full-index standardization. Do not impute unobserved response. No fixed-landmark model may use future lab, diagnostic contact, procedure, reassessment, treatment or follow-up representation.

At the dynamic landmark rebuild B0/Bcode/B2 from history strictly before contact. Bcode may contain coding and observation opportunity up to the landmark, but not post-contact fields.

## Substantive learned/process alternative

M3 is a sparse irregular-time joint state/process model on the same endpoint ladder and split. It has separate latent states for biomarker/hepatic trajectory, observation/care-contact intensity and coding/episode-complexity intensity, assay-specific observation models and competing hazards for strict TACE, broad TACE, diagnostic-only code, alternate liver, non-liver and end-local-observation transitions. These latent states are statistical constructs, not validated physiological states.

M3 is useful because paired B2 summaries lose asynchronous timing, curvature, biomarker discordance and separation of measurement intensity from code complexity. It can reveal whether a latent biomarker state predicts the strict endpoint after process states are represented. It must use no future record at either landmark and share targets, calibration, uncertainty and bootstrap units with B2. A transformer is deferred because extra sequence capacity cannot repair missing intent, code semantics, endpoint adjudication or outside-care linkage. Causal treatment-effect, response, image/text and external-validation studies are deferred for the same evidence-bound reasons, not because neural methods or GPUs are categorically excluded.

The actual solver deliverable is newly fitted B0/Bcode/B2 and, if supported, M3 models; endpoint-specific locked predictions; cause-specific hazards and cumulative incidence; 90%-sensitivity alert curves; calibration, log-score and Brier outputs; patient-clustered bootstrap intervals; episode and split manifests; coding/observation diagnostics; specificity-gradient and endpoint-contrast reports; negative-control/permutation outputs; and an interpretation file mapping each conclusion to exact output fields, estimates and intervals.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive computation requires:

- strict-endpoint B2 versus Bcode reaches at least a 5-point alert-fraction reduction at locked 90% sensitivity with a bootstrap interval excluding zero, and broad TACE is directionally consistent;
- strict-versus-broad gain differs by no more than 5 points and the result is stable under episode identity sensitivities;
- no comparable gain occurs for diagnostic-code-only, non-liver or pre-index placebo outcomes, with stability under 14/30-day and encounter-only reassessment and continued-observation/last-contact sensitivities;
- calibration, log score/Brier, event support, missing-time rate, mixed/unclassified rate, positivity and threshold uncertainty are acceptable;
- if M3 is attempted, it converges and either improves the strict locked target by at least 5 points or mean log score by at least 0.02 with uncertainty; otherwise nonconvergence/deferral is reported rather than repaired after seeing the test.

Adverse evidence includes null/reversed strict gain; Bcode explaining nearly all B2 gain; greater gain for broad or diagnostic-code-only outcomes than strict TACE; strong signal for non-liver or disappearance; sensitivity to vocabulary or patient-time merging; gain confined to high-contact/coding-density strata; poor overlap/calibration; or M3 placing signal in observation/coding states rather than the biomarker state. This supports measurement, care-capture or coding explanations and does not establish that the trajectory lacks value outside this record.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few strict transitions, sparse dynamic landmarks, excessive missing procedure times, high mixed/unclassified proportion, failed calendar support, positivity failure, unstable intervals or failure to distinguish endpoint layers. Rules cannot be relaxed after the locked outcome.

Computation can establish only reproducible local recorded-care stratification and robustness to prespecified coding/observation controls. It cannot establish recurrence, viable tumour, radiologic response, treatment intent, appropriateness, treatment benefit/failure, survival, death, outside-care treatment, causal effect, clinical utility or external validity. Those require expert adjudication of procedure indications/bundles, radiology/pathology with valid timing, intent/eligibility, mortality/outside-care linkage and independent/prospective validation.
