## Feasibility gates and result meanings

Before a supportive or adverse mortality interpretation require exact source/schema/header/catalog identities, one-to-one eids, inherited O1/O1_hold/n1 and roster hashes, valid death parsing with no unresolved conflict, at least 40 deaths in the union of the two lists, at least 10 deaths in each list, at least 10 deaths in their symmetric difference, exact fixed denominators, valid chronology, successful paired bootstrap, and unchanged parent outputs. These are anti-vacuity gates, not power guarantees. An outcome-blind aggregate preflight may inspect only death-field availability/parseability and date range, not scores, ranks, rosters, selected yields, or post-visit kidney outcomes.

Supportive: all gates pass and the one-sided 95% upper limit is strictly < +10/1,000. This supports only no detected material excess of recorded nine-year all-cause death on AG1 versus G1.

Adverse: all gates pass and the one-sided 95% lower limit is strictly > +10/1,000. This is evidence against the prespecified no-material-excess qualification; it does not establish that cystatin testing causes harm or that G1 improves health.

Inconclusive: every other state, including sparse deaths, wide/boundary-touching intervals, zero variance, parsing conflict, or noncomputability. It is not equivalence, safety, or evidence of no difference.

Emit the parent labels unchanged and the independent mortality label. Emit `low_UACR_signal_without_recorded_mortality_excess` only if the parent low-UACR label and mortality label are both supportive; emit `low_UACR_signal_with_recorded_mortality_concern` if the parent supports and mortality is adverse; emit `low_UACR_signal_mortality_unresolved` if the parent supports and mortality is inconclusive.
