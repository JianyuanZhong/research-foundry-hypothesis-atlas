# Episode 23 targeted child: assay-result information beyond care/documentation workflow

## Opening and hypothesis

The strongest supported claims are bounded. Li et al. [K3] found clinically consequential post-TACE ALBI trajectories in a separately adjudicated HCC cohort. Shmatko et al. [K1] establish the importance of temporal masking in longitudinal record prediction. Urbut et al. [K2] motivate fixed-time prospective evaluation and explicit selection diagnostics. None establishes that an HCC routine-record assay change is biological, that a TACE row proves intent/completion, or that workflow intensity has been separated from result content in this snapshot.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among eligible HCC patients, does within-person assay-result change through day 42 add held-out information for a later operational repeat-TACE record beyond a stable pre-index assay profile and the care/documentation workflow and assay-observation opportunities that generated the recorded measurements?

The leading explanation is result content carrying information beyond workflow. The strongest rival is that dense encounters, orders, examinations, medications, procedures, and lab opportunities make both Delta42 and later TACE records more observable. Other rivals are stable severity/recording phenotype and timing/availability artifacts. This experiment is predictive/descriptive, not mechanistic, causal, or a utility evaluation. A loss of the Delta42 increment would redirect the next study toward capture/adjudication; survival after workflow adjustment would justify assay calibration and clinical-outcome validation.

## Estimands and nested information sets

Let t0 be the first eligible TACE start; Hpre=[t0-365,t0), H28=[t0,t0+28 days], H42=[t0,t0+42 days]. P_stable is an assay/sample-type-specific pre-index lab profile. Delta28 and Delta42 are fold-frozen within-person changes from the pre-index reference to valid H28/H42 lab events; missing baseline is not zero. Let L_w be pooled discrete-time hazard loss on [43,57), [57,71), ..., [169,181) days.

Fit the same endpoint, risk grid, masks, split and uncertainty for:

- C_clock: M0/B0, risk-bin/time-at-risk and observation-clock features, no result content.
- W_nonlab: C_clock plus values-free care workflow through day 42: encounter timing/coverage, department, admit/discharge and duration validity; order identity/status/frequency and all order times; examination identity/start time (not text or diagnosis); medication identity/dose/route/type/frequency/start/end; and non-TACE procedure label/start/end activity before the prediction boundary.
- W_opportunity: W_nonlab plus values-free lab metadata: row counts, valid-time indicators/timing, assay identity, specimen type, result-present flags, and assay-by-window availability. It excludes qualitative result and quantitative result values.
- W_opportunity+P_stable+Delta42; the stable-only W_opportunity+P_stable arm is required.

Primary new contrast:
G_assay,O4 = L_w(W_opportunity+P_stable) - L_w(W_opportunity+P_stable+Delta42).

Also report G_opportunity=L_w(C_clock)-L_w(W_opportunity), G_stable|W=L_w(W_opportunity)-L_w(W_opportunity+P_stable), and G_total|W=L_w(W_opportunity)-L_w(W_opportunity+P_stable+Delta42), for O0-O4 and O_planproxy_cap. Positive G_assay means result values add predictive information beyond workflow; it does not identify biology.

Repeat the nested C/W/stable/Delta28 comparison at H28/R28. Retain the parent's Q28-42 audit and day-28-frozen transport weights. Q is diagnostic only, never a predictor or endpoint relabeler.

## Methods and alternatives

Transparent baseline: pooled-logistic discrete-time hazard with intercept/risk-bin indicators, elastic-net alpha=0.5. Fit C_clock, W_nonlab, W_opportunity, stable and Delta variants for O0-O4/O_planproxy_cap and Q separately. Within five chronological grouped development folds learn assay/workflow dictionaries, missingness, reference construction, scaling, imputation, lambda, Q specification and weight support/truncation. Test remains untouched.

Matched learned alternative: two-stream GRU (process stream and result stream), one layer, hidden 32, dropout .10, 32/16 head, Adam 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, max 50 epochs, eight-epoch early stop, seeds 17/29/41. Fit clock-only, process-only, process+stable and process+stable+within on identical folds. It can reveal nonlinear assay combinations and irregular timing lost by block-additive elastic-net; no complexity or small gain earns preference.

Planning estimates (not measured): audits on 4 CPUs/16 GiB for 1–3h; elastic-net/Q/1,000 bootstrap/permutations for 1–3h; three-seed GRU for 2–5h on 4 CPUs/16 GiB. One allocated A100 is optional for GRU with explicit cuda:0. Future solver envelope: 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, up to 8 GPUs, 28800s. Deferred: transformer, narrative NLP, imaging, latent biological model, mechanistic response simulation and causal treatment analysis because required validated text/images/units/intent-response/outcomes are absent. Revisit only after decomposition survives and dependencies are supplied.

## Uncertainty, falsification and decision gates

Report patient-level paired log loss, Brier/integrated Brier, calibration, risk-bin counts, discrimination secondarily, paired availability, O0-O4/process/Q counts, unknowns, latency, support, weight tails and ESS. Use 1,000 patient-level paired bootstrap resamples of untouched test and one max-|t| simultaneous 95% family over endpoints, nested blocks, methods, ordinary/weighted arms and falsifiers.

Frozen falsifiers: (1) mask all Qualitative Result/Quantitative Result while retaining lab identity/specimen/time/missingness; (2) permute Delta42 within assay label/sample type within development folds, preserving timing/availability; (3) compare W_nonlab versus W_opportunity; (4) timing/source-only model with no values; (5) remove days 35–42 lab rows; (6) repeat at H28/R28; (7) Q strata R42/care-truncated/record-loss/unknown; (8) compare O4 with O0-O3/O_planproxy_cap; (9) process-feature permutation within baseline strata as a diagnostic only.

Supportive: both methods have positive simultaneous lower bounds for G_assay,O4 in ordinary and weighted arms; H28 direction is compatible; W_opportunity does not match full content; value masking and Delta permutation remove the gain; timing/process endpoints do not reproduce it; support/ESS pass. This supports only workflow-separated predictive information in recorded assay values. Adverse: W_opportunity explains the gain, value masking leaves it, Delta permutation survives, process-only or O_planproxy_cap is positive, the result is one-method/one-endpoint/one-latency, or weighting removes it; this favors workflow, stable severity, timing or representation without identifying which. Inconclusive: inadequate Delta/O4 support, unknowns, positivity/ESS failure, wide intervals, method disagreement or H28/R42 incompatibility; do not tune windows/gates to rescue.

Continue to assay calibration and adjudication only after the supportive gate. Redirect to capture/workflow work if W_opportunity explains the result. Defer/abandon the assay-content interpretation after result masking/permutation falsifies it. Report no directional decision when support/uncertainty is inconclusive.
