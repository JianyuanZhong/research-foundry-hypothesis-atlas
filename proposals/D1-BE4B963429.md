# Source-bound successor: documentary-unit versus workflow-burden envelope for HCC documentary-M2 allocation

Parent anchors: \`[prior hypothesis]\` (dual patient-slot/whole-accession capacity) and \`[prior hypothesis]\` (patient identity/content and lifecycle-unknown robustness).

## 1. Targeted repair and unresolved question

The mature design correctly prevents a patient-slot result from hiding unequal numbers of eligible whole accessions. Its remaining decision-validity weakness is different: one accession is still treated as one unit. The HCC source has multirow accessions with nonidentical components, and the source provides narrative payloads whose size varies. Thus a queue can pass the fixed \((K,C)\) accession-unit contract while imposing materially different documentary handling on the reviewer. Conversely, replacing an accession with its rows or text length without a predeclared rule would invite tuning and would falsely imply that source size is reading time.

The repair is a prelocked structural-workload envelope. The inherited unit-budget result remains primary for documentary-unit capacity. Three source-computable ledgers are then evaluated with the same patients, score bytes, tie keys, coupled source/onset/reader/pathology worlds, deletion rules, \(K\), and threshold:

1. accession units: one cost per eligible nonblank whole accession;
2. raw examination components: one cost per raw examination row in that accession, including duplicate and conflicting rows (no representative row is selected);
3. component-plus-text burden: the raw-row count plus a fixed text-size term based only on the examination fields \`Examination\`, \`Examination Findings\`, and \`Examination Diagnosis\`.

The third ledger is a retrospective structural shadow because report lifecycle is unavailable. It must never be described as time, work relative value, availability, or a deployable cost known at \(t_{\rm dec}\).

The falsifiable question is:

> At a fixed patient-slot budget and under every prelocked documentary-burden ledger, does the frozen report-semantic queue retain more documentary-M2 patients than acquisition-only, report-surface, masked-semantic, and locked random queues, while retaining the inherited patient-level identity/content and temporal guarantees?

This is a resource-validity hypothesis, not a claim that semantic prioritization improves treatment or that documentary-M2 is biological microvascular invasion. The clinical advance is to determine whether an apparent benefit survives plausible structural explanations for reviewer burden. If it survives only the one-accession ledger, the result cannot justify an archive-linked workflow validation on the basis of the unit count.

## 7. New structural-burden ledgers and fixed capacities

For each eligible whole accession \(u\), in the same source/operation/accession world:

- \(w^U_u=1\);
- \(w^R_u=n_u\), the number of raw examination rows with the same \((Patient Master Index,Encounter Number,Examination Number)\), including identical duplicates and conflicting components;
- \(w^T_u=n_u+\left\lceil b_u/512\right\rceil\), where \(b_u\) is the total UTF-8 byte length of the exact raw-field values in \`Examination\`, \`Examination Findings\`, and \`Examination Diagnosis\` across all rows of \(u\), and the text term is zero when all three fields are blank.

The divisor 512, the byte encoding, blank handling, inclusion of \`examination\` and both report fields, and duplicate policy are fixed in the signed 2019 artifact. No percentile, year, outcome, reader, threshold, or queue result can select or alter them. \(R\) is a raw-component ledger; \(T\) is a deliberately simple component-plus-text shadow. They are not claims about human reading time.

For \(\ell\in\{U,R,T\}\), let \(W^\ell_{\rm ref}\) be the minimum \(\sum_i\sum_{u\in i}w^\ell_u\) across all required 2015–2019 development/lock states, and freeze \(C_\ell=\lfloor0.10W^\ell_{\rm ref}\rfloor\) in 2019. Require \(C_R,C_T\ge50\), and require a nonempty primary event/eligible-patient support under every ledger. The same 5% and 20% fractions are descriptive sensitivity analyses only and do not replace the 10% confirmatory capacities.

A ledger is source-computable, not operationally observed. Costs are reserved for all eligible units whether \(M=1\) or \(M=0\), so missing content cannot create free capacity. For an identity-ambiguous accession, retain every admissible world and report the full cost witness; never use a favorable cost world in isolation. Zero-accession patients remain in the denominator with zero cost, and malformed/blank/unreproducible units are not silently converted into cheap units.

For every ranked method list \(\pi_M(\omega,q)\) and every \(\ell\), define the longest-prefix queue:
\[
S_{M,\ell}^{K,C_\ell}(\omega,q)=
\{\pi_M(1),\ldots,\pi_M(m)\},
\quad
m=\max\{j:j\le K,\sum_{r=1}^j\sum_{u\in i_r}w^\ell_u\le C_\ell\}.
\]
If the next ranked patient would exceed capacity, stop; do not skip, backfill, rerank, or spend unused capacity. Use the same \((K,C_\ell)\), tie key, and cost rule for \(B,R,G,G_{\rm mask}\), the all-content reference, and each of 256 hash-random permutations. The inherited \(K\)-only queue remains diagnostic only. Anchored patient deletion leaves EMPTY positions and freezes surviving ranks and costs.

## 8. Estimands and gates

For every coupled cell \(z=(\omega,O,y,d,r,\delta)\), let \(H_{M,\ell}(z)\) be documentary-M2 patients captured by \(S_{M,\ell}\), \(m_{M,\ell}(z)\) its offered patient count, and \(U_{M,\ell}(z)\) its used ledger cost. Preserve the inherited margin:
\[
D_{G,C_\ell}(z)=100[H_{G,\ell}(z)-H_{C,\ell}(z)]/K,
\quad C\in\{B,R,G_{\rm mask}\},
\]
tested by exact integer cross-products; equality with 5 fails. Report \(H,m,U,U/C_\ell\), EMPTY positions, and the patient/accession witnesses. \(H/U\) is descriptive only and is never substituted for the fixed-capacity estimand.

Preserve the inherited distinct-patient identity and content losses. For the union \(D_y\) of required label-stable reference patients, take the maximum loss for each patient across its eligible coupled states:
\[
\theta_{\rm ID,y}=|D_y|^{-1}\sum_i L_{\rm ID}(i,y,q),\quad
\theta_{\rm CONT,y}=|D_y|^{-1}\sum_i L_{\rm CONT}(i,y,q).
\]
The primary content certificate remains zero in both 2020 and untouched 2021. Report source-only loss separately; never use row/accession counts as the denominator.

Add the structural workload-envelope estimand:
\[
\Delta_{\rm env}(q)=
\min_{\ell\in\{U,R,T\}}\ 
\min_{z\in{\cal Z}_{20}\cup{\cal Z}_{21}}
\min_{C\in\{B,R,G_{\rm mask}\}}D_{G,C_\ell}(z).
\]
The confirmatory resource gate is \(\Delta_{\rm env}(q^*)>5\), together with every inherited comparator/random/deletion/source/content gate. This is a worst-ledger, worst-coupled-state finite-census estimand. It deliberately answers a stronger question than the accession-only margin. A unit-only pass with \(\Delta_{\rm env}\le5\) is not workflow-burden support.

\]

Freeze every rejected lower threshold and its first patient/accession/ledger/capacity witness before reading 2021.

Passing means:
\[

=\theta_{\rm ID,2021}=\theta_{\rm CONT,2021}=0,
\quad \Delta_{\rm env}(q^*)>5,
\]
with all inherited contracts and exact witnesses passing.

## 10. Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

Use this precedence:

1. \`computationally_inconclusive\`: hash/ordinal/parser replay, declared join, state coherence, exact arithmetic, queue, cost ledger, witness, deletion, threshold-freeze, or prose-linkage verification fails.
2. \`feasibility_inconclusive\`: required patient/event counts, \(K\), any \(C_\ell\), complete reader/pathology books, nonvacuity, or source identity reconstruction fails.
3. \`selection_adverse\`: no valid sub-complete \(q^*\) passes the frozen 2020 envelope.
4. \`unit_capacity_adverse\`: a valid unit ledger fails the margin/content/source contract.
5. \`structural_burden_adverse\`: the unit ledger passes but \(R\) or \(T\) fails; report the exact ledger, patient, accession, and comparator witness. This means accession-unit superiority is not robust to measurable documentary complexity and cannot support a workflow-allocation interpretation.
6. \`temporal_nonreplication_adverse\`: the locked 2020 result fails in 2021 under any required ledger or content certificate.
7. \`finite_corpus_structural_envelope_supportive\`: every ledger and inherited contract passes in both years, with exact patient-level and queue witnesses.
8. Append \`operational_visibility_unidentified\` because HCC has no report lifecycle fields, and append \`workflow_capacity_unidentified\` unless an external time-motion/RVU/image-burden bridge has independently calibrated a ledger.

Supportive is limited to a temporally replicated finite-corpus statement: the semantic queue retains the documentary endpoint under patient-slot, accession-unit, and prelocked structural-burden budgets. It does not say that reports were viewable, that reviews took a particular time, or that a service is adequately staffed. An adverse result is a failure of the specified property, not proof that semantic imaging is clinically useless. An inconclusive result is neither support nor failure.

## 11. External evidence bridge and clinical limits

To convert the structural envelope into a workflow claim, a separate archive/workflow study must collect immutable report-version IDs and hashes; authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrieved/readable/view timestamps; image-series and prior-comparison counts; protocol/modality and report complexity; blinded expert start/stop review time per whole accession; validated local RVU/work-relative-value mapping; staffing, queue delay, interruptions, and non-reporting duties; and specimen-linked pathology adjudication. A prospective silent-mode study must then assess whether the queue changes clinician review/action, with safety, fairness, transportability, and patient outcomes measured as appropriate.

Possible external bridge interpretations are predeclared: calibrated positive time/RVU association supports using the corresponding envelope as a local capacity approximation; weak or non-monotone calibration makes the HCC structural result non-operational; observed lifecycle/readability failure makes any availability bridge adverse; and missing or biased bridge sampling is inconclusive. None of these external claims can be inferred from HCC.

Automatic verification can check source lineage, raw bytes/ordinals, declared joins, locked cost formulae, ledger budgets, queue arithmetic, state coherence, frozen threshold replay, and output-to-conclusion linkage. It cannot check true identity, clinical semantic correctness, report lifecycle, actual burden, pathology biology, clinician action, benefit, safety, fairness, or transport.
