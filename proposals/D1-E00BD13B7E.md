## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE/resection/ablation) for HCC, surveillance couples serial serum AFP
with scheduled cross-sectional imaging. The parent branch asks whether the within-patient post-LDT AFP slope
predicts time to local progression (and whether that hazard differs by first modality, by landmark-imaging
status, and after detection/observation-bias correction). Every prior episode attacks **validity threats on the
pooled/conditional hazard**. None asks a question that directly governs *who* the biomarker can be used for:

> Is the AFP-slope hazard **transportable across AFP's measurement range**, or is it confined to patients whose
> baseline AFP already sits in an informative (elevated) range? If the slope only predicts progression when
> baseline AFP is high, then AFP-slope-guided surveillance intensification is not justified for AFP-low patients.

This is the biomarker-**applicability** decision, distinct from the parent's *imaging-concordance* stratum
(IMGneg/IMGpos) and from the modality effect-modification (which modality). AFP is known to have low
discriminative sensitivity at low levels [K2], so a slope hazard estimated on a pooled cohort can be driven
entirely by the high-AFP subgroup. Resolving this changes whether an AFP-slope trigger is recommended
cohort-wide or only within the informative AFP range.

## 2. Hypothesis (falsifiable)
- **H1 (leading claim):** the per-1-SD within-patient AFP-slope hazard for subsequent local progression is
  present **in both** baseline-AFP strata (AFP-low and AFP-high), i.e. the slope is a transportable early signal.
- **Strongest rival R_applicability:** the pooled slope hazard is driven by the AFP-high stratum; in the AFP-low
  stratum the slope HR attenuates toward 1.0. This makes the slope a *marker of an already-informative AFP
  range* rather than an independent trajectory signal, so an AFP-slope trigger would misfire in AFP-low patients.
- **R_timing (secondary):** an AFP-low "signal" is produced by draws scheduled *because* AFP moved (informative
  observation); the parent's surveillance-intensity machinery is reused, not replaced.

## 3. Inherited frozen design (inherited verbatim; not modified)
- **Population:** HCC diagnosis + ≥1 qualifying liver-directed procedure; index = earliest qualifying procedure.
- **Landmark:** t* = index + 180 d (90 d as a pre-specified sensitivity landmark).
- **Exposures:** per-patient **AFP slope** and **ALBI slope** fit ONLY on draws in (index, t*]; same-draw
  AFP + albumin + total-bilirubin pairing within ±2 d on the lab timestamp; ALBI = 0.66·log10(bilirubin) − 0.085·albumin.
- **Primary endpoint:** modality-restricted abdominal **local-progression** event {增大,进展,复发,新发,增多} on
  `examinations`, with 转移 (metastasis) as a competing event; censoring via `出院时间`; retreatment proxy secondary.
- **Four rivals, conjunctive falsification; no survival/mRECIST claim.**

## 4. This child's ONE added element: baseline-AFP effect modification (applicability transportability)
Define **baseline AFP** = the AFP draw nearest to the index procedure within [index − 30 d, index + 7 d]
(exclude if none). Split at the cohort median of baseline AFP (a pre-specified, unit-free rule, because lab
units are not labelled on this source). Strata: **AFP_low** (≤ median) and **AFP_high** (> median).

**Primary re-fit:** the frozen landmark Cox (per-1-SD AFP slope + ALBI slope + first modality + age/sex + draw
intensity) is re-estimated **with a pre-specified AFP_slope × AFP_stratum interaction** (1-df LRT), reporting
stratum-specific AFP-slope HRs and 95% CIs, and the hazard ratio of HR(AFP_high)/HR(AFP_low).
Reused from the parent: the (a) surveillance-intensity covariates over (t*, censor], (b) AFP-independent
ALBI-slope negative control, and (c) intensity-adjusted re-test.

**Branches (pre-declared):**
- **Supportive (H1):** AFP-slope HR > 1 with 95% CI excluding 1 in **both** strata, AND interaction p ≥ 0.10
  (or HR_ratio within [0.66, 1.50]) → transportable across the AFP range.
- **Adverse = R_applicability:** AFP_low HR CI includes 1 (≈1) while AFP_high HR > 1, OR interaction p < 0.05
  with HR(AFP_high) > HR(AFP_low) → signal confined to the informative AFP range; slope-triggered intensification
  not justified in AFP-low patients.
- **Inconclusive:** AFP_low slope-estimable < 150 OR AFP_low events < 50 → the interaction is underpowered; report
  descriptive stratum HRs with CIs and do NOT declare transportability; OR baseline AFP missing for > 30% of the
  cohort; OR undated lab draws.
- **Precise-null:** both stratum HRs within ±0.10 of 1 with tight CIs (upper bound < 1.5) and a flat ALBI negative
  control → no slope signal in either stratum.
- **Selection guard (mandatory):** report AFP_low vs AFP_high and vs the full indexed cohort on surveillance-
  intensity covariates and modality mix; if the strata are non-exchangeable on observation intensity, the
  intensity-adjusted re-test is the reported estimate (the parent's guard machinery).

## 5. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative)
- **M0 (baseline):** the frozen **pooled** single-Cox per-1-SD AFP-slope hazard (the parent's estimand). It
  estimates one average slope hazard and cannot reveal applicability heterogeneity across the AFP range.
- **M1 (this element, chosen):** the **stratified + interaction** Cox. Same data, same endpoint, same time origin;
  it can reveal that the pooled hazard is not transportable and can bound *for whom* the biomarker applies.
- **M2 (alternative, deferred):** a **shared-parameter joint model** of the AFP trajectory (latent true slope with
  within-patient measurement error) and the survival endpoint, which could separate true trajectory from draw
  noise. **Deferral reason:** it changes the estimand (functional form) and the discovery budget favours the
  pre-specified applicability test; **revisit condition:** if the pooled and stratified slopes disagree, or if a
  bounded reliability diagnostic shows the AFP slope is poorly reproducible (low λ), M2 becomes the primary.
  A bounded reliability diagnostic (median AFP draws per patient in (index,t*]; variance of the OLS slope) is a
  planned solver feasibility output, not required for M1.
- Simple methods remain valid here because M1 directly answers a decision-relevant transportability question that
  M0 cannot; M2 is a genuine scientific alternative (measurement-error separation), not a complexity bonus.

## 7. Falsification and what each outcome means
- **Supportive** (H1): an AFP-slope hazard present in both strata would show the biomarker is cohort-transportable,
  supporting cohort-wide AFP-slope-guided surveillance intensification — pending mRECIST adjudication.
- **Adverse** (R_applicability): a null AFP-low stratum would show the pooled hazard is driven by the
  already-informative AFP range, so an AFP-slope trigger is not justified in AFP-low patients — a negative result
  that changes the recommendation.
- **Inconclusive:** underpowered AFP-low stratum → do not claim transportability; report CIs.
- **Precise-null:** no slope signal in either stratum → the trajectory biomarker carries no progression hazard.
- **Falsifiers that would sink the whole branch:** `IMGmissing`/lab-missing dominates, or the lexical endpoint
  cannot be separated from imaging-descriptor prevalence (selection guard fails).

  does not replace adjudication.
- No mortality / mRECIST PFS on this island → **no survival or causal claim**.
- AFP-slope and ALBI-slope are estimated from sparse, unit-unlabelled draws; the baseline-AFP split uses the
  cohort median precisely because units are unlabelled (a limitation, not a threshold recommendation).
- Single institution; surveillance-selected; conditional independence and measurement-error assumptions unverified.
- **No HR, interaction or stratum count is computed in discovery.** The solver must fit M1 and report:
