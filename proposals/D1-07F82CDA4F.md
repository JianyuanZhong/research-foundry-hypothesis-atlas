# Episode-3 provisional hypothesis: persistent discordant AKI recovery beyond current severity

Status: provisional research task, not a completed result. No cohort count, effect estimate, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adults with an ICU stay and a prespecified creatinine-defined AKI episode who remain observable at a fixed 48-hour landmark, persistent creatinine improvement without concurrent urine-output improvement will identify higher subsequent risk of renal deterioration or in-hospital death than concordant recovery, after adjustment for the same landmark severity, treatment state, and observation intensity.

The clinically consequential unresolved claim is incremental prognostic information: whether the ordering and persistence of discordance adds information beyond current creatinine, current urine output, and current illness state. This is not a causal claim about fluid status, renal physiology, or treatment benefit.

## Why this matters and what is unresolved

The expert seed observes that creatinine and urine output can recover discordantly and that a creatinine-only recovery label may miss persistent renal vulnerability. Existing evidence supports the measurement concern, not the proposed association: Haines et al. found that eGFR creatinine systematically overestimated measured kidney function after prolonged critical illness, while eGFR cystatin C was closer to measured GFR in 38 ventilated patients [K1]. Li et al. identify the 2012 KDIGO framework as the highest-quality AKI guidance and emphasize standardization alongside implementation and population-applicability gaps [K2]. Tang et al. used a strict 24-hour MIMIC-IV/eICU landmark with creatinine, AKI stage, urine output, and severity to predict observed CRRT initiation, but explicitly treated CRRT as clinician-driven rather than objective necessity and reported limited external calibration and incremental value [K3]. None establishes that discordant recovery predicts subsequent outcomes. The leading explanation is that discordance reflects a real persistent vulnerability; the strongest rival is that it is a proxy for current severity, diuretic/vasopressor exposure, differential urine measurement intensity, or noise in a single creatinine or urine summary. A matched current-state baseline versus a persistent trajectory analysis can distinguish these explanations in a falsifiable way, although neither can establish mechanism.

## Baseline, alternative, and falsification

The simple baseline is a landmark logistic or Cox model using current creatinine, current urine-output summary, creatinine slope, AKI stage, age/sex, available severity, treatment exposure, and measurement intensity. The substantive alternative is a compact trajectory model using the same pre-landmark creatinine and urine-output sequences, including slopes, nadir, persistence, and co-movement. Both use identical patient splits, leakage-safe temporal windows, held-out evaluation, calibration, discrimination, uncertainty, and measurement-density sensitivity analyses. The alternative is retained because persistence and ordering can distinguish true discordance from a noisy cross-sectional mismatch; it is deferred if sequence support is inadequate.

Support requires a prespecified directionally consistent association and reproducible incremental calibration/discrimination in held-out data, with uncertainty and sensitivity analyses showing the signal is not explained solely by missing urine measurements, diuretic/RRT exposure, or current severity. Adverse results are no incremental signal, reversal, or instability after these adjustments. Inconclusive results include sparse discordant cells, inadequate follow-up, or wide uncertainty. None supports mechanism or treatment benefit.

## Missing evidence and limitations

The association is untested. The exact AKI threshold, urine normalization, minimum coverage, outcome adjudication, and cohort/event counts have not yet been computed. MIMIC lacks measured GFR, standardized cystatin C for all patients, reliable fluid-status adjudication, and complete urine measurement. Clinical adjudication of AKI/recovery and RRT indication is required before treatment claims. Cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid because timestamps are subject-specific shifts.

The natural-history transformer demonstration is not pursued because its UK Biobank training data and disease-history modality are unavailable, and a transformer would not resolve this AKI measurement uncertainty. The Bayesian longitudinal demonstration is not reproduced because its genetic inputs are unavailable; the compact EHR-only trajectory alternative is a bounded adaptation, not a reproduction. The cancer multimodal demonstration is not pursued because MIMIC-CXR images and the main paper/STAR Methods are unavailable; its lab-only idea is not needed for this narrower question.
