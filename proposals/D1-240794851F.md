# Hypotension Duration × Inflammatory Burden Interaction Predicts Vasopressor Resistance and Mortality

## Hypothesis to Test

In patients with septic shock requiring vasopressor therapy in the eICU, the **combined burden of hypotension duration and systemic inflammation** (measured as time-weighted hypotension × inflammatory marker levels) is independently associated with **vasopressor resistance** and **in-hospital mortality**, beyond the effects of baseline severity alone.

**Formal Statement:**
After adjusting for age, APACHE II score, comorbidities, and hospital characteristics, the interaction term between hypotension duration and inflammatory burden (CRP/hs-CRP) will be significantly positive (β_interaction > 0, p < 0.05), indicating that patients with both prolonged hypotension AND high inflammation have synergistically increased risk of vasopressor resistance and mortality.

## Clinical Importance

1. **Personalized Vasopressor Management:** Identify patients at high risk of vasopressor resistance who may benefit from earlier escalation or alternative strategies.

2. **Improved Risk Stratification:** Combined hypotension-inflammation metric improves risk prediction beyond traditional severity scores (APACHE II, SOFA).

3. **Understanding Treatment Heterogeneity:** Explains why similar BP targets yield different outcomes in patients with different inflammatory profiles.

4. **Guideline Development:** Informs more nuanced BP target recommendations based on inflammatory status.

## Substantive Advance Over Existing Knowledge

**Closest Relevant Work:**
- Existing "duration of hypotension" hypothesis ([prior hypothesis]) focuses only on hypotension duration and AKI
- Existing "hospital vasopressor strategy" hypothesis ([prior hypothesis]) focuses on practice variation, not patient biology

**Substantive Advances:**
1. **Multi-dimensional risk:** Combines temporal (duration) and intensity (inflammation) dimensions
2. **Vasopressor resistance outcome:** More directly actionable than AKI for clinicians
3. **Interaction effect:** Tests multiplicative risk rather than additive effects
4. **Mortality as primary:** More clinically relevant than organ injury alone

## Study Population

**Population:** Adults in eICU with septic shock requiring vasopressor therapy, ICU stay ≥ 48 hours.

**Inclusion:**
- Received vasopressor(s) for ≥ 6 hours
- Continuous BP monitoring (vitalPeriodic)
- At least one CRP or hs-CRP measurement
- Followed ≥ 24 hours after final vasopressor dose

**Exclusion:**
- Non-septic shock
- ICU stay < 48 hours
- Missing critical vital sign or lab data

### Computed Variables

1. **Hypotension Duration:** Sum of all episode durations where mean BP < 65 mmHg or systolic < 70 mmHg
2. **Inflammatory Burden:** Baseline CRP (within 12h of admission) and peak CRP during stay
3. **Vasopressor Resistance:** Dual agent use OR dose escalation ≥2x OR vasopressor-free interval > 4 hours
4. **Outcomes:** 30-day mortality, ICU death, length of stay

## Analysis Plan

### Baseline Model
Compare outcomes between:
- Low risk: hypotension duration < 12h AND baseline CRP < 10 mg/L
- High risk: hypotension duration ≥ 12h OR baseline CRP ≥ 10 mg/L

### Substantive Alternative (Interaction Model)
Cox proportional hazards with interaction:
```
hazard(t) = h0(t) × exp(β1×duration + β2×inflammation + β3×duration×inflammation + γ×covariates + u_j)
```
- Test β3 for significance (interaction effect)
- Include hospital random effects (u_j ~ N(0, σ²))
- Robustness checks with alternative thresholds and time-varying models

## Falsification Criteria

**Supported:** β3 > 0 and significant (p < 0.05), effect persists after hospital adjustment

**Falsified:** β3 ≈ 0, no interaction after adjustment, or inverse relationship

**Inconclusive:** Small effect size (<10% risk increase), conflicting sensitivity analyses

## Required Additional Evidence

**Clinical Adjudication:**
1. Definition of septic shock (clinical vs. EICU criteria)
2. Optimal CRP thresholds for risk stratification
3. Vasopressor resistance definition (clinical consensus needed)

**Missing Evidence:**
1. Procalcitonin, IL-6, or other inflammatory markers not in eICU
2. Interventional data on modifying hypotension duration or inflammation
3. Long-term outcomes (1-year survival)

## Method Comparison

**Baseline:** Simple risk stratification (4-group comparison)
**Alternative:** Interaction model with hospital random effects

**Information Gained:** Baseline identifies independent risks; interaction model captures synergistic risk and provides interpretable effect modification.
