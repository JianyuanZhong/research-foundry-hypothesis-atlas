# Does M2 triage value survive removal of documentation-workflow signals? A verification-bounded, threshold-robust temporal transport test

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

The intended decision remains narrow: before first curative-intent liver resection, should an adult with apparently resectable hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) be flagged for multidisciplinary review because the resection specimen may show pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)? A flag is not a recommendation for wider resection, neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment.

**Strongest evidence already supported.** The HCC source contains timestamped procedures, CT/MRI report text, and laboratory results that can be restricted to a preoperative window, plus untimed same-encounter pathology text. The parent aggregate audit directly scanned 338,040 procedure and 46,395 pathology rows. Its broad first-resection cohort showed that a naive anywhere-in-document M2 search is contaminated by boilerplate, that anchored selected M0/M1/M2 fields were common through 2021, and that selected-grade ascertainment collapsed after 2021. Preliminary anchored M2 counts were 75 in 2020 and 24 in 2021 before final exclusions and adjudication. These are feasibility findings, not model performance or true prevalence.

Inspected literature supports that severe/M2 MVI is associated with worse prognosis, that the Chinese M0/M1/M2 definition uses number and distance of invaded microvessels, and that inadequate non-tumoral sampling can underestimate grade (Li et al., DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746, frozen full-text XML `[source checksum]`; Chen et al., DOI 10.3350/cmh.2025.0167, frozen full-text XML `[source checksum]`). A 2026 multicenter study inspected in full-text XML used irregular margin and AFP to predict **binary MVI**, with external validation and decision-curve analysis (Huang et al., DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034, frozen source `[source checksum]`). It does not validate this dataset, the M2 endpoint, or a treatment decision.

**Unresolved claim.** The parent can show an incremental signal at one threshold while still learning report templates, machine/workflow changes, or selective test ordering rather than transportable clinical content. Also, net benefit at an unevaluated threshold is only a weighted classification result; it is not evidence that referral or treatment improves outcomes.

**Primary hypothesis.** In the locked 2020–2021 all-patient target frame, a template-stable semantic report-plus-laboratory model (S) will retain clinically material classification value over the unchanged structured baseline (B): the worst-case lower bound of paired `ΔNB(S-B)` at `p_t=0.30` will be at least 0.01 with a patient-bootstrap lower 95% confidence limit above zero; the simultaneous lower confidence band will remain above zero for every threshold from 0.25 through 0.35; S will exceed a workflow-only negative-control model (W) throughout that interval; and removing unrestricted n-grams will cost no more than 0.005 net-benefit units versus the parent's unrestricted extension (U) at 0.30. This is a falsifiable historical transportability and threshold-robustness claim, not a patient-outcome claim.

The advance over the parent is substantive: it separates clinical report content from documentation-process signals, requires value across a threshold plateau rather than at a knife-edge cutoff, retains every ungradable patient in sharp partial-identification bounds, and makes the strongest permissible conclusion “robust weighted M2 classification value in the 2020–2021 institutional frame.” Stakeholder acceptance of the threshold and benefit of model-guided care remain untested.

## Frozen population and information clocks

The unit is one patient (`患者主索引`) and one first eligible index resection.

1. From `procedures`, take the patient's first valid-`开始时间` partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resection using an exact-string operation dictionary reviewed before outcome access. Emit every included and excluded `手术` value and frequency. Exclude transplant, biopsy, puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and repeat/recurrence surgery.
2. Require age at least 18 and same-encounter pathology that explicitly identifies HCC and is adjudicated as belonging to the index resection. Do **not** require an MVI grade. Because pathology has no timestamp or specimen identifier, the composite-key link is necessary but not sufficient; ambiguous specimen linkage is `V=0`, not an exclusion.
3. Define `t0 = procedures.开始时间 - 24 hours`. Require at least one liver CT or MRI with `examinations.开始时间` in `[t0-90 days,t0)`. Eligible laboratory measurements have `labs.检验时间` in `[t0-30 days,t0)`. No value at or after `t0` is a predictor.
4. Primary untreated cohort: exclude prior HCC-directed resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy found in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during `[t0-365 days,t0)`. Previously treated patients form a separately reported sensitivity cohort with the same endpoint and clocks.
5. Development is 2015–2018; tuning and logistic recalibration use 2019 only; the locked test is 2020–2021. The 2013–2014 records remain excluded for sparse selected grades. Frozen models may score 2022 through snapshot end 2025-11-11 only for predictor/template drift. No current-era accuracy or net-benefit claim is permitted unless the parent's prospective adjudication gate recovers at least 85% of grades and at least 75 M2 outcomes.
6. All records for a patient remain in one period. Development-only dictionaries, feature rules, template fingerprints, assay mappings, imputation, scaling, winsorization, vocabulary, models, recalibration, thresholds, and adjudication rules freeze before test access.

`examinations.开始时间` is acquisition time, not report-finalization or actual decision-access time. A blinded chart audit is required to assert availability; code can establish only that acquisition preceded `t0`.

## Exact pathology-only endpoint and verification

The endpoint is unchanged: `Y=1` only for pathology-defined M2; `Y=0` for M0 or M1. Let `V=1` mean that index-specimen M0/M1/M2 is trustworthy and `V=0` otherwise.

A parser may assign a grade only from the immediate selected value following an anchored field such as `MVI提示风险分级[:：]`, stopping before explanatory bullets/definitions, or from a prespecified synonymous selected-value construction approved under blinded review. Unanchored M2 text, binary `微脉管侵犯：有/无`, positive-ungraded MVI, blank vessel-number/distance fields, and absence of MVI language cannot assign M0, M1, or M2. Discordant selected fields are conflicts. Pathology is never a predictor.

Retain the parent's adjudication: two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, random samples of at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 M1 stratified by year/template, every `V=0` test record, and at least 100 contemporary `V=0` records per year. A third resolves disagreement. Require class-specific PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95, and kappa at least 0.90. Reviewers may derive grade from explicit number/distance language using the frozen definition; inadequate text, specimen linkage, or sampling remains `V=0`. Raw slides and the sampling protocol are unavailable, so review cannot establish the biological truth missed by tissue sampling.

Two blinded radiologists review 200 imaging reports for size/count/macrovascular thrombus and, separately in 2020 and 2021, at least 150 reports enriched for each added semantic feature and for parser-negative cases. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in at least 90%, count-category agreement at least 90% with kappa at least 0.80, and per-year PPV and sensitivity at least 0.85 for every added binary feature. Audit 100 reports per period for whether report content was actually available before `t0`; failure to establish availability makes the run inconclusive.

## Models: clinical content versus workflow

Fit nested elastic-net logistic regressions with identical development/tuning and 2019 recalibration.

**B: unchanged structured baseline**

- age and sex;
- latest eligible CT/MRI maximum lesion diameter, count (1, 2, at least 3), modality (CT only, MRI only, both), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown indicators;
- last eligible exact-assay albumin, total bilirubin, INR, and platelet count, continuous plus missing indicators.

**S: primary template-stable extension**

All B variables plus only frozen, clinically semantic rule extractions from `检查所见` and `检查诊断`: complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular or non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity; and last eligible exact-assay AFP, ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, and creatinine, each as a continuous value and missing indicator. No character n-grams, report/examination code, year, machine, report length, accession number, template identifier, pathology/MVI term, or postoperative text may enter S.

**U: unrestricted-text comparator**

The parent's extension: S plus development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after identifier/date/accession/pathology/MVI removal. U is not primary. Audit its 100 largest coefficients before unblinding.

**W: workflow-only negative control**

B plus report count and length, exact `检查` category, `机器型号`, formatting-derived template family, and presence/absence (not value) of each extension assay. W contains no S semantic feature or extension assay value. W tests whether documentation and ordering process alone reproduce the apparent gain; it is not a deployable model.

Template family is derived without outcomes from punctuation, line-break/section order, repeated boilerplate spans, `检查`, and `机器型号`; clinical terms and numbers are masked. Clustering is fit in 2015–2018 and frozen. Two radiologists review the 20 largest families for coherent format, not outcome. Unseen test formats map to “unseen,” never to a nearest outcome-associated family.

For S, repeat the locked analysis after neutralizing all extension-lab missing indicators and imputing development medians within exact assay name. This sensitivity analysis asks whether S depends on ordering behavior. It does not replace the primary all-missingness model.

## Whole-frame decision analysis

Every model predicts every 2020–2021 target-frame patient, including `V=0`. Evaluate thresholds `p=0.20,0.21,...,0.40`; the fixed primary remains `p_t=0.30`. For model M, `NB_M(p)=TP/N-[p/(1-p)]FP/N`, where N is the whole target frame.

For any paired contrast M1-M0 and each unverified patient, use the exact outcome-free contribution:

- identical flags: `[0,0]`;
- M1=1, M0=0: `[-w,+1]`;
- M1=0, M0=1: `[-1,+w]`;

where `w=p/(1-p)`. Add observed contributions for `V=1`, the adverse endpoint for each `V=0` to obtain `L(p)`, and the favorable endpoint to obtain `U(p)`, then divide by N. Apply the same bounds to S versus B, U, W, flag-all, and flag-none.

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps of the entire target frame. Report pointwise percentile intervals and a simultaneous 95% band over the 21-threshold grid using the bootstrap maximum absolute deviation from each bound. Define the threshold-robustness set `R` as thresholds where the simultaneous lower band for `L_ΔNB(S-B)` exceeds zero and S's lower-bound net benefit exceeds both default strategies. Its width and contiguous components are outputs, not tuning targets.

At 0.30 report sharp bounds and uncertainty by test year, sex, age below/at least 65, modality, AFP missingness, and the four largest test template families (others pooled), without subgroup claims when fewer than 20 verified M2 or 100 patients occur. Among `V=1`, report paired AUPRC, AUROC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV, explicitly labeled selection-sensitive. Model `V` using only pre-`t0` B variables plus year/template and report standardized differences; do not claim inverse weighting identifies missing outcomes.

## Gates, falsification, and interpretation

The test is interpretable only if the parent's gates pass: at least 1,000 test patients, at least 75 adjudicated M2, `V=1` for at least 85%, all test `V=0` reviewed, pathology and radiology reliability gates met, all models score identical rows, report text nonempty in at least 80%, size/count jointly extractable in at least 70%, each B lab available in at least 70%, no required variable changes missingness by more than 15 percentage points, and S calibration slope 0.80–1.20, intercept -0.10–0.10, sensitivity at least 0.70 and PPV at least 0.50 among V=1. Additionally, every S feature must pass its reliability gate separately in 2020 and 2021; unseen template prevalence must be reported; and no forbidden feature may appear.

**Supportive:** all gates pass; `L_ΔNB(S-B,0.30)>=0.01` with lower 95% limit above zero; `[0.25,0.35]` is contained in R; S's simultaneous lower bound exceeds W across that interval; the one-sided lower 95% bound for `L_ΔNB(S-U,0.30)` is above -0.005; neutralizing extension missingness indicators leaves `L_ΔNB(S-B,0.30)>0`; and neither test year's point lower bound is negative. The strongest conclusion is that adjudicated semantic report/lab content adds historically transport- and threshold-robust weighted M2 triage value beyond B in this institutional 2020–2021 frame, even under adversarial assignment of unrecoverable grades.

**Adverse:** with all gates passed, the upper 95% bound for `U_ΔNB(S-B,0.30)` is below 0.01, or S cannot beat a default strategy, falsifying material incremental classification value. Positive value only at an isolated threshold, material inferiority to U, equality with W, or loss of value after missingness neutralization falsifies the stronger documentation-independent transportability claim even if a single historical threshold looks favorable. AUPRC cannot rescue an adverse decision result.

**Inconclusive:** event/reliability/availability gates fail; pathology linkage or grading cannot be recovered; simultaneous bands span benefit and no benefit; or sharp missing-outcome bounds are too wide. This is neither evidence of no association nor evidence of utility.

The 0.25–0.35 interval is a prespecified robustness stress test, not an elicited clinical preference. Claiming that any threshold is acceptable requires hepatobiliary surgeons, radiologists, pathologists, operational leaders, and patients to quantify referral burden and missed-M2 tradeoffs before deployment. The dataset lacks tumor-board actions, treatment assignment, recurrence, reliable complete follow-up, death registry linkage, quality of life, harms, capacity, and costs. Therefore even a supportive result cannot show that referral or changed treatment improves survival, recurrence, quality of life, or cost-effectiveness. Those claims require stakeholder work and a prospective impact study.

## Harbor compiler and verifier contract

The compiler must preserve the population, first-resection rule, `t0`, windows, untreated primary cohort, 2015–2018/2019/2020–2021 split, exact M2-versus-M0/M1 endpoint, V rules, all-target-frame denominator, B/S/U/W definitions, fixed 0.20–0.40 grid and 0.30 primary threshold, sharp bound arithmetic, simultaneous bootstrap band, R definition, gates, and falsification zones. Changing any is a scientific child.

Computationally checkable: source hashes/headers/columns; operation and assay dictionaries; composite-key joins; deduplication/multiplicity; one index per patient; attrition; period disjointness; anchored parsing including a unit test where selected M0 followed by boilerplate M2 remains M0; V counts; feature timestamps; identical score rows; exclusions of pathology/postoperative/workflow variables from S; masking and vocabulary freeze for U; W containing no clinical extension values; template clustering without outcomes; bound arithmetic; bootstrap bands; subgroup suppression; and links from every conclusion to machine-readable outputs.

The verifier must reject correct metrics followed by a treatment/survival claim; complete-case support when worst-case bounds fail; threshold cherry-picking; calling pointwise intervals simultaneous; treating W as a clinical model; an unanchored M2 label; calling current-era non-identifiability model failure; or calling imprecision adverse. It must accept justified supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations.

Not automatically checkable: Chinese clinical semantics, adequacy of tissue sampling, correct specimen linkage, true report availability at the decision, radiologist/pathologist qualifications, whether the threshold tradeoff is acceptable, and whether model-guided care helps patients. These need expert adjudication, unavailable evidence, stakeholder review, or another study.
