# Decision-time-robust semantic M2 triage: joint bounds for unavailable reports and unverified pathology

## Clinical question, evidence, and advance

The intended decision is narrow: 24 hours before a first eligible liver resection, should an adult in this institution's HCC resection frame be flagged for multidisciplinary review because the specimen may show pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)? A flag is not a recommendation for wider resection, neoadjuvant/adjuvant therapy, or any other treatment.

**Strongest claim already supported.** The read-only HCC snapshot has operation start times, CT/MRI acquisition times and eventual report text, assay-specific laboratory values and one laboratory time, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. Inherited complete scans counted 338,040 procedure and 46,395 pathology rows; a broad first-resection audit found 75 preliminary anchored M2 labels in 2020 and 24 in 2021 before final exclusions and adjudication. It also proved that an anywhere-in-document M2 search is invalid because selected M0 can be followed by boilerplate defining M2. These are feasibility and label-construct findings, not prevalence or performance.

Inspected public full text supports that MVI/M2 is pathology-defined and prognostically important, that Chinese M0/M1/M2 grading depends on the number and distance of invaded microvessels, and that inadequate sampling can underestimate grade (Li et al., DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746; Chen et al., DOI 10.3350/cmh.2025.0167). Huang et al. reported an externally validated irregular-margin/AFP model for binary MVI, not M2 (DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034). Fan et al. found only modest retrospective discrimination for LDH and MVI severity and cautioned against using LDH alone to change treatment (DOI 10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944; frozen full-text source [source checksum]). None establishes decision-time report availability, M2 triage value here, a 0.30 preference threshold, or patient benefit.

**Unresolved claim.** The parent tests semantic report/laboratory value conditional on documentation, ordering, template, machine, and modality process. Its main remaining weakness is temporal: examinations.Start time records acquisition, while no authored, finalized, signed, released, or viewed time exists. Eventual stored text may come from a valid pre-cutoff acquisition whose interpretation was not available at the decision. Calling it all preoperative can create an unmeasured future-information advantage.

**Primary hypothesis.** In the locked 2020–2021 all-patient target frame, semantic strategy S will add material M2 triage value over process/provenance-matched W at p=0.30 even under patient-wise adverse assignment of (a) whether the last timestamp-eligible CT and MRI reports were actually available at the decision and (b) M2 status for every unverified/ambiguous pathology endpoint. The sharp lower bound for ΔNB_dec(S−W,0.30) must be at least 0.01 with patient-bootstrap lower 95% limit above zero, and the simultaneous lower band must exceed zero throughout 0.25–0.35. Failure with passed gates falsifies robust semantic value; an interval spanning material benefit and no benefit is inconclusive.

The advance is a decision-time claim partially identified from exactly what the snapshot records. The conventional “all acquisition-eligible reports available” estimate remains computable but is secondary and cannot establish decision-time utility.

## Population, index, and clocks

The unit is one patient (patient master index) and one first eligible index resection.

1. In procedures, select the earliest valid start time whose normalized exact surgery belongs to a frozen, clinically reviewed partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resection dictionary. Exclude transplant, biopsy, puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and explicit repeat/recurrence operations. Break exact-time ties by visit number then normalized surgery. Emit all included/excluded raw values, frequencies, ties, and one-index-per-patient assertion before outcomes.
2. Require age at least 18 and same-encounter pathology text identifying HCC in the index surgical encounter. This is a resected, retrospectively pathology-confirmed HCC frame; it does not prove preoperative HCC certainty, resectability, or curative intent. Do not require an MVI grade or secure specimen linkage. Missing grade, positive-ungraded MVI, conflict, or uncertain linkage is V=0 and never an exclusion. Patients without any same-encounter HCC-identifying pathology cannot be proven members of this frame and are outside it; outcome bounds do not repair that selection limit.
3. Lock t_dec = procedures.Start Time minus 24 hours. The inherited 24-hour buffer is prespecified, not selected from outcomes. Records at or after t_dec are unavailable. Repeat only as labeled 12-, 48-, and 72-hour-buffer sensitivities; none replaces the primary.
4. A liver CT/MRI row is acquisition-eligible when examinations.Start time is valid and in [t_dec−90 days,t_dec). This does not prove Examination findings or Examination diagnosis was finalized or visible. Require at least one eligible CT/MRI. After exact-row deduplication, bundle by patient, encounter, nonmissing examination number, and Start time; a missing examination number forms a row-level bundle. Freeze modality dictionaries from exact Examination. Select at most the latest CT and latest MRI bundle by Start time, with deterministic examination number/normalized Examination tie breaks. Report multiplicity and discarded bundles.
5. A lab is timestamp-eligible when labs.Test Time is valid and in [t_dec−30 days,t_dec). Take the last exact Test/Specimen Type row with deterministic source-order ties. There is no separate specimen, result, or finalization time, so call values timestamp-eligible, not proven decision-visible. A prespecified sensitivity masks values with Test Time in [t_dec−48 hours,t_dec); disagreement limits interpretation but does not replace the primary.
6. Preserve the no-recorded-prior-treatment primary cohort: exclude recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during [t_dec−365 days,t_dec), using frozen reviewed dictionaries and valid source times. Report exclusions by table and invalid times. This does not prove biological treatment-naivety. Previously treated patients form a sensitivity cohort.
7. Development is 2015–2018, penalty selection and logistic recalibration use 2019, and 2020–2021 is untouched test. Assign by index operation date and keep each patient in one period. Exclude 2013–2014. Score 2022–snapshot end only for predictor/template drift and endpoint identifiability, with no contemporary performance claim.
8. Freeze operation, HCC, treatment, modality and assay dictionaries; bundle/tie rules; text masks; semantic rules; template construction; preprocessing; penalties; calibration; thresholds; availability states; pathology rules; and gates before reading test outcomes.

## Endpoint and verification state

Y=1 only for pathology-defined M2; Y=0 for M0 or M1. V=1 means a trustworthy index-specimen M0/M1/M2 assignment under the frozen position-aware parser and adjudication protocol; otherwise V=0.

Assign grade only from the immediate selected value after an anchored field such as MVI risk grade[:：], stopping before definitions, explanatory bullets, historical clauses, or a new specimen section, or from a frozen synonymous selected-value construction accepted in blinded development review. Handle negation, not seen, multiple specimens, and discordance. Unanchored M2, binary microvascular invasion: present/absent, positive-ungraded MVI, blank number/distance, absent MVI language, definitions, and conflicts cannot assign grade. Pathology never predicts.

Two independent parsers must agree on locked test. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 sampled by year/template, and every test V=0; a third resolves disagreement. Preserve PPV at least 0.98 by class, sensitivity at least 0.95, kappa at least 0.90, and parser disagreement no more than 5%. Review interprets only available text. Raw slides, block counts, vessel-distance measurements, sampling protocol, and specimen IDs are unavailable; biological grade and sampling adequacy cannot be established automatically, so residual uncertainty remains V=0.

## Decision-availability states

For patient i, q_i is the number (0, 1, or 2) of selected CT/MRI bundles with nonempty eventual report text. Let r range over Ω_i={0,1}^{q_i}; 1 means that report is assumed finalized and visible at t_dec, and 0 means unavailable. Empty stored text has state 0 only. The state is shared by compared models: the analysis cannot choose one state for S and another for W.

At state 0, mask every report-body-derived item from that bundle: size, lesion count, macrovascular thrombus, report length/sections/template, semantic findings, and unrestricted text. Acquisition facts—valid Start Time, modality from Examination, bundle count, distinct-Examination Number count without identifiers, and machine availability/development-frequency category from Machine Model—remain shared provenance variables. At state 1, stored report variables are exposed. No report-availability probability, monotonicity assumption, or imputation is allowed because there is no finalization clock.

To avoid unavailable-state extrapolation, development uses state augmentation: each patient contributes every Ω_i state with total patient weight one divided equally across states. Penalty folds, recalibration, resampling, and permutation remain patient-grouped. The frozen pipeline scores every patient under every feasible state. Augmentation is a robustness construction, not evidence that any state occurred.

## Models and baselines

Use elastic-net logistic regression on the same V=1 development patients, selected/recalibrated in 2019, and evaluated on identical test patients/states.

**B, structured baseline:** age and sex; timestamp-eligible exact-assay albumin, total bilirubin, INR, and platelet values with missing indicators; modality/acquisition provenance; and, only in report-available states, maximum lesion diameter, count (1, 2, ≥3), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus with unknown indicators.

**W, process/provenance-matched primary comparator:** B plus acquisition count, time since last acquisition, exact examination categories, machine model availability/development-frequency category, and extension-assay presence/count/time-since-last. In report-available states it also receives report character/line/section counts and an outcome-blind formatting family after masking clinical words, numbers, dates, identifiers, and values. W never receives added semantic imaging findings or extension-assay numeric values and is not a deployable clinical model.

**S, semantic strategy:** exactly W plus, when a report is available, frozen present/absent/unknown states for complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity from Findings and Examination Diagnosis; plus timestamp-eligible exact-assay AFP, ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, and creatinine values. Report semantics are missing in unavailable states. Lab ordering/missingness already resides in W. With no unit field, never merge assay names by apparent meaning or apply external cutoffs; use development-only within-exact-assay winsorization/standardization, with within-assay/year ranks as sensitivity.

**U, diagnostic text ceiling:** W plus report-available development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after removing identifiers, dates, accession strings, pathology/MVI terms, and post-cutoff text. U cannot rescue failed S−W.

Also compute flag-all and flag-none. No year, raw examination number, raw machine identifier, diagnosis, procedure name, pathology, clinical-document text, or post-t_dec content enters S. Blinded radiologists perform inherited extraction review by year and CT/MRI provenance; that validates stored-text interpretation, not decision availability.

## Primary joint partial-identification estimand

For p, set w=p/(1−p) and A_Mi(r,p)=1[score_Mi(r)≥p]. Paired contribution is:

c_i(y,r,p)=y(A_S−A_W)−w(1−y)(A_S−A_W).

For V=1, L_dec(p)=N⁻¹Σ_i min over r in Ω_i of c_i(Y_i,r,p), and U_dec uses the maximum. For V=0, additionally minimize/maximize over y in {0,1}. The denominator N is the entire test target frame. Thus no patient is dropped or imputed, acquisition never proves report availability, and the same availability state drives both models. At most four report states times two endpoint states makes the bound exact and computable. Apply identical shared-state arithmetic to S versus B/U/defaults and to each model's net benefit.

The fixed primary threshold is 0.30; evaluate 0.20,0.21,…,0.40 only as a robustness grid. Use 2,000 whole-frame patient bootstraps carrying all states/rows of sampled patients. Report percentile intervals for both endpoints and simultaneous 95% bands using bootstrap maximum absolute deviation. Define R_dec as thresholds where the simultaneous lower band for L_dec(S−W) exceeds zero and S's lower-bound NB exceeds flag-all and flag-none.

At 0.30 decompose width into: endpoint ambiguity alone with all reports available; report-availability ambiguity among V=1; and the joint primary interval. Report the fraction whose S or W flag changes across Ω_i. These diagnose uncertainty but do not identify latent states.

The secondary acquisition-complete estimate sets every nonempty selected report to state 1. The strict no-uncertified-report estimate sets every state to 0. Positive acquisition-complete results with joint bounds spanning harm and material benefit are inconclusive for decision-time value.

Report joint bounds by test year, sex, age below/at least 65, AFP availability, template where available, and CT-only/MRI-only/dual acquisition provenance. Infer only with at least 100 target-frame patients and 20 V=1 M2 outcomes. Among V=1, report AUPRC, AUROC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV for all-report and no-report scenarios as selection-sensitive diagnostics. Modeling V or report sensitivity cannot identify missing outcomes/availability.

## Falsification, gates, and interpretation

Run 1,000 process-matched semantic-null repetitions. Within period, permute the complete added semantic block across patients within frozen year, CT/MRI provenance, template-availability, and extension-assay-availability blocks; keep B, W, Y/V, patient grouping, and each patient's states fixed. Refit/recalibrate each repetition. Use the inherited small-block hierarchy. Observed L_dec(S−W,0.30) must exceed the 97.5th percentile. Label permutation and full patient semantic shuffle must be compatible with zero. A post-cutoff model is a leakage demonstration only.

Interpret only if: test N≥1,000 and adjudicated M2≥75; V=1≥85%; every test V=0 is reviewed; pathology/radiology reliability and both parsers pass; models score identical patients/states; every report-derived feature is masked at state 0; all clocks obey windows; all-report text is nonempty ≥80%; core size/count extractability ≥70%; each B lab availability ≥70%; required-variable missingness shifts ≤15 percentage points from 2019 to either test year; CT-only and MRI-only meet stratum minima; no forbidden feature enters S; and permutation is estimable. Failed gates are inconclusive, not model failure.

**Supportive:** all gates pass; L_dec(S−W,0.30)≥0.01 with bootstrap lower limit >0; 0.25–0.35 lies in R_dec; CT-only and MRI-only each have positive lower bounds with lower limits >0; neither test year's point lower bound is negative; and observed statistic exceeds the matched-null 97.5th percentile. Strongest allowed conclusion: the frozen semantic strategy added historically threshold-robust weighted M2 triage value over measured workflow/provenance in this institution's 2020–2021 resection frame despite adverse assignment of unverifiable report availability and pathology grade. It does not prove reports were available, imaging semantics alone caused gain, or triage benefits patients.

**Adverse:** with gates passed, the upper 95% limit for U_dec(S−W,0.30) is <0.01, falsifying clinically material robust gain; <0 indicates harm. Pooled support is adverse to modality robustness if an estimable CT-only/MRI-only upper limit is ≤0. Positive W−B with failed S−W, or failure to exceed the matched null, supports only a workflow/provenance explanation. Better AUPRC or positive all-report secondary analysis cannot rescue an adverse primary result.

**Inconclusive:** any validity/event/state/overlap/permutation gate fails; joint intervals include material benefit and no benefit; acquisition-complete support disappears under availability bounds; or pathology/report uncertainty makes bounds too wide. Attribute width to endpoint ambiguity, report availability, or sampling uncertainty. Inconclusive is neither no association nor utility.

Reject all-report support presented as decision-time support; complete-case support when joint bounds fail; different states for S and W; V=0 imputed as M0; S−B semantic attribution without S−W; pooled support with harmful/unestimable required modality; cherry-picked thresholds; pointwise intervals called simultaneous; or treatment, recurrence, survival, external-validity, or contemporary-performance claims. The snapshot lacks tumor-board actions, treatment response, reliable complete follow-up/death linkage, patient-reported outcomes, workload, and costs. Support justifies prospective validation with explicit report-finalization capture; adverse evidence argues against robust semantic value; inconclusive evidence identifies whether timestamps, pathology ascertainment, or events are missing.
