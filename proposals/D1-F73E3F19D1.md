## Unresolved question and clinical importance
In ICU patients, does the burden of low mean arterial pressure (systemic MAP<65 mmHg) during the first 24 h predict later acute kidney injury (KDIGO-AKI in (24,72]h) as a **physiologic dose** (H-dose), or is the association a **monitoring/charting artifact** (H-observation) or a marker of **treatment intensity** (H-treatment)? The answer matters for a perfusion-target decision: if dose is real, a MAP target reduces AKI risk; if the signal is documentation density, targeting the number would be ineffective and possibly harmful.

## Evidence status
Supported: MAP is associated with AKI in ICU cohorts; EHR/measurement frequency is itself driven by healthcare processes [K1]; E-values bound unmeasured confounding [K2]; MAP is among leading AKI predictors in MIMIC-IV/eICU [K3], closest prior work). Unresolved/untested: separation of H-dose from H-observation and H-treatment with a fitted estimator on eICU.

## Estimand, baselines, alternative
- Estimand: adjusted risk difference (RD) per 30-min MAP<65 burden, hospital-clustered SE; three conditioning sets — monitoring-only / treatment-only / both.
- Baseline **B1**: single uncentered logistic logR on burden (K1-style).
- Alternative **A1**: fitted within-hospital between-estimator logistic + AIPW-style RD + E-value bound [K2] + leave-one-hospital-out transport. A1 reveals what B1 loses: within-vs-between separation, hospital clustering, and sensitivity to unmeasured prognosis/anticipation.
- Deferred **A2**: spline/flexible dose-response (revisit if A1 shows non-linearity).
- Detection **negative control**: channel-disjoint Spearman(burden, creatinine-draw count) vs Spearman(burden, non-renal-draw count) in (24,72]h.

## Falsification criteria
- Supportive H-dose: adjusted RD>0, CI excludes 0, E-value>=1.5, negative control near-null.
- Adverse H-observation: detection negative control non-null and of similar magnitude to the exposure–outcome association.
- Adverse H-treatment: effect attenuates to null under treatment conditioning.
- Inconclusive: CI includes 0 with wide bounds, or inconsistent across conditioning sets.
