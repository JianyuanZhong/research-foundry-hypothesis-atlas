# Episode 33: does late lactate trajectory add risk information beyond the late value itself?

## Unresolved claim, clinical importance, and advance

The valid parent established that a fixed 12–24-hour lactate pair is available at useful scale after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed row: 1,468 measured adults in 96 hospitals, with 33 outcome-blind exposure-balanced hospitals and 1,198 people before modeling. It did **not** establish that less than 20% clearance is informative beyond the absolute follow-up lactate already available at the 24-hour reassessment. That distinction matters because nonclearance is mathematically determined by baseline and follow-up values, and the parent’s clearance and nonclearance groups can differ greatly in the late value. A clinician deciding whether to escalate reassessment can already see that late value.

**Primary falsifiable hypothesis:** in the parent’s exact 24-hour landmark population, after restricting inference to measured overlap in the absolute selected late lactate and pre-window recorded context, late nonclearance (<20%) is associated with at least a **+10 percentage-point** higher subsequent hospital-mortality risk than clearance (>=20%). The +10 margin is retained from the parent and was locked before outcome modeling; a smaller favorable direction does not support this claim.

This is a prognostic incremental-information hypothesis. It is not a treatment effect, not proof of occult hypoperfusion, and not evidence that ordering serial lactates or acting on clearance improves outcome. A supportive result would justify a prospectively synchronized decision-impact study. An adverse result would argue against promoting the binary trajectory as a large risk discriminator beyond the late value in this recorded population.

The strongest current evidence supports narrower claims. The parent supports source feasibility, not the incremental association. The inspected full-text 2021 Surviving Sepsis Campaign guideline (Evans et al., DOI 10.1007/s00134-021-06506-y; acquired source [source checksum], [source checksum]) gives only a weak, low-quality-evidence suggestion to guide resuscitation toward decreasing lactate and cautions that lactate must be interpreted in clinical context. The inspected full-text 2026 Li et al. trajectory study (DOI 10.2196/92838; PMCID PMC13478678; acquired source [source checksum], [source checksum]) reports trajectory associations in selected sepsis-associated AKI cohorts and modest external discrimination; it does not test this broad EICU pressor-recorded population, this two-point trajectory, or added value beyond the selected late lactate. The inspected full-text Yang et al. study (DOI 10.3389/fmed.2026.1880921; PMCID PMC13457294; source [source checksum], [source checksum]) found a high-risk early perfusion contour but explicitly limited EICU transportability by measurement-dependent complete-case selection. None establishes the unresolved claim.

## Primary estimand and analysis

The estimand is the overlap-population standardized risk difference

RD_current = P(D=1 | X=1) - P(D=1 | X=0),

where overlap is defined using the absolute late lactate F plus variables recorded before the late window. This asks whether trajectory separates prognosis among people comparable on what the current late lactate and prior recorded context show. It is not a causal intervention contrast.

Before reading outcomes, construct one C=10 L2 logistic exposure model for X with:

- log(F), log(F)^2, and F collection elapsed time;
- age, gender, t0, and hospital indicators;
- baseline periodic MAP and heart-rate medians in [t0-60,t0);
- in [t0,t0+720): median systemicmean, fraction of observed adjacent-time intervals below 65 mm Hg, median heartrate, and distinct observation count;
- latest valid creatinine, BUN, total bilirubin, AST and ALT in [t0-360,t0+720], using audited labname/unit/revision rules, with missing indicators.

Do not include B in this exposure model: B is the historical information that creates the trajectory and is deliberately the candidate signal being tested against F. This omission is not a confounding claim. Winsorize continuous context variables at pooled 1st/99th percentiles and scale them using rules frozen before D; keep log(F) un-winsorized because F is already range restricted. Use overlap weights 1-e for X=1 and e for X=0, normalized within X. Estimate each weighted death risk directly and subtract; no outcome regression may replace this primary estimator.

Use seed 20260933 and 5,000 copied-hospital, then copied-uniquepid-within-hospital/exposure bootstrap replicates; refit preprocessing and the exposure model in every replicate. Report crude risks only as descriptive QC. Prespecified non-rescuing sensitivities are: (i) exact 0.5-mmol/L F bins crossed with hospital, retaining cells containing both X groups and weighting cells equally; (ii) current-value-only outcome model versus the same model plus X under leave-one-hospital-out prediction, reporting Brier score, calibration slope/intercept and AUPRC without a separate support threshold; and (iii) F restricted to 2–4 mmol/L with no changed margin.

Required gates before interpretation: reproduced parent counts; >=12 eligible hospitals and >=500 people; >=50 deaths in each X group; all fitted e in [0.02,0.98]; group effective sample size >=40; no person contributes >5% and no hospital >35% of group weight; absolute weighted SMD <=0.10 for every exposure-model column including both F terms; >=95% of bootstraps finite; and >=80% finite leave-one-hospital-out primary refits. Hospital selection is exposure-count-only.

## Falsification and meaning

- **Supportive:** every gate passes, the 95% bootstrap interval lower bound is >=+10 points, and >=80% of finite leave-one-hospital-out RDs are positive. Meaning: the recorded trajectory contains a large, transportable prognostic association not explained by measured absolute F and prior context; prospective synchronized validation and a decision-impact study are warranted.
- **Adverse to the +10 claim:** every gate passes and the interval upper bound is <+10. If the interval remains above zero, direction may be positive but is below the locked materiality margin; if it crosses zero, direction is unresolved.
- **Opposite direction:** every gate passes and the interval upper bound is <0. This falsifies the hypothesized direction but does not show that nonclearance is protective; measurement selection or residual case mix remain plausible.
- **Inconclusive:** any population, overlap, event, balance, concentration, convergence or site-deletion gate fails, or the interval includes +10. No sensitivity can rescue the primary classification.

A supportive result does not establish that obtaining B, changing lactate, giving fluid/vasopressor, or initiating source control benefits patients. An adverse result does not show that trends are useless in individual care or other populations. An inconclusive result identifies the exact missing support or data requirement.

## Evidence limits and verifier contract

Computationally checkable: source hashes/headers; complete-file, no-sampling scan; t0/person/landmark rules; lactate units/revision/conflict/ties; F elapsed time; hospital gate; parent-count reproduction; feature cutoffs; exposure model; weights/balance/ESS; cluster bootstrap; site deletions; and deterministic result classification.

The verifier must pass correct computations paired with appropriate supportive, adverse, opposite and inconclusive interpretations. It must reject: a correct supportive RD paired with a causal or treatment-benefit conclusion; a correct adverse-to-margin result described as “no association”; an overlap-gate failure paired with an effect estimate; and correct feasibility counts presented as confirmation of the hypothesis. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility only.

Not computationally adjudicable in EICU: true norepinephrine administration/start/dose, sepsis or shock truth at t0, arterial versus venous specimen, assay indication, fluids, beta-agonists, hepatic clearance, source control, tissue perfusion, treatment limitations, exact death time/cause, and whether acting on trajectory helps. Those require clinical review and richer prospectively synchronized data; utility requires a decision-impact study, and treatment effects require randomized or credible target-trial evidence.
