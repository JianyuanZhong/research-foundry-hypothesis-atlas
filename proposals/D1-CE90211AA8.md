# Proposal: observation-aware early post-TACE trajectories and recorded retreatment

## Parent, scientific deliverable, and substantive repair

This is a targeted successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the HCC island, one index per patient, the day-45 landmark, the recorded-repeat-TACE estimand, and the transparent-versus-joint-model comparison. It makes two substantive repairs.

First, a managed read-only audit of the actual lab source found the parent’s proposed `国际标准化比值` label was not present among the observed names. The frozen coagulation feature is therefore the observed `凝血酶原时间比值` assay, described as a coagulation-ratio proxy rather than INR. No unit-dependent clinical score will be calculated. Second, the study adds a prespecified non-liver-procedure capture control and separates the complete-paired-biomarker estimand from the all-index cohort missingness estimand. A phenotype that predicts unrelated procedures as strongly as repeat TACE, or that only appears in the selected measured subset, is evidence for recording/eligibility selection rather than a disease-specific signal.

The future solver must newly construct the cohort and fit B0, B1, B2, M3, and the missingness-weighted sensitivity; produce locked-test patient predictions, phenotype associations, observation-process diagnostics, the non-liver capture-control result, and uncertainty intervals. Completion is established by those machine-readable outputs and an interpretation file that links each claim to computed estimates and prespecified rules. This discovery episode does not solve the clinical question.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the inspected local evidence is narrower than a clinical-response claim: structured HCC diagnoses, TACE-named procedures, dated laboratory measurements, and dated examination reports are present; AFP, albumin, bilirubin, a coagulation-ratio proxy, and platelet counts are observed with heterogeneous naming and sampling. Existing clinical reasoning and the public literature motivate AFP response and hepatic function as potentially informative after liver-directed treatment, but neither the local files nor the inspected references establish that a fall in AFP is true residual-tumor clearance, that a repeat TACE is recurrence, or that the association is independent of who is tested and retained in care.

The primary hypothesis, frozen before fitting, is:

> Among adults with a structured HCC diagnosis and a first recorded TACE-like procedure who have parseable paired AFP measurements, an early AFP improvement accompanied by non-deteriorating hepatic-marker proxies is associated with lower odds of a subsequently recorded TACE-like procedure during days 46–365. The association will remain directionally present after adjustment for measured laboratory/examination capture intensity and will not be equally strong for an unrelated non-liver procedure.

This is an adjusted prognostic association with a local recorded action. It is not a causal treatment effect, recurrence endpoint, survival endpoint, radiologic response endpoint, or clinical-utility estimate.

## Population and time zero

Use one index episode per patient. Identify the earliest procedure row whose `手术` matches case-insensitive `TACE`, `动脉化疗栓塞`, or both `肝动脉` and `栓塞`. Retain the raw procedure name and source. Collapse same-patient qualifying procedure rows within 24 hours into one episode; retain duplicate row and name counts. Use `开始时间` as the index time, with the joined encounter time only as a declared fallback for an otherwise eligible procedure with no procedure start.

Require:

- age >=18 and nonmissing `性别` at the index encounter;
- `诊断名称` containing the literal `肝细胞癌` on an encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after index;
- exact join on `患者主索引` and `就诊号`;
- an index date no later than 2025-01-01, so a nominal 365-day local follow-up can be checked against the snapshot;
- local ascertainment through day 365 for the binary primary outcome, with incomplete follow-up excluded and reported rather than coded as no event.

The landmark is day 45. No record with time after day 45 may enter prediction. The primary outcome is the first later TACE-like episode with 45 < days <=365. The secondary disease-action outcome is the first later procedure matching the TACE rule or containing `切除`, `消融`, `射频`, or `微波`. The capture-control outcome is the first later procedure not in that liver-directed rule, among procedures with a usable start time. It is a control for general procedure recording, not a negative biological outcome and not proof of absence of disease.

Use relative within-patient intervals; do not make claims about exact calendar timing beyond the recorded split year. Report both the broad TACE-like rule and a literal-name sensitivity restricted to `TACE` or `动脉化疗栓塞`.

## Features and estimands

The primary paired-biomarker population requires at least one parseable numeric `甲胎蛋白` in days -30 to -1 and at least one in days 7 to 45. Use the last pre-window and first early-post value. For each hepatic assay, retain last pre and first early-post values when available and a missingness indicator. Define the frozen early phenotype as at least a 50% fall in log1p AFP plus no deterioration in at least two of albumin, total bilirubin, the coagulation-ratio proxy, and platelet count; define each direction before fitting, based only on within-assay direction, and do not impose clinical units or normal ranges. Also retain continuous log1p AFP change, assay-specific changes, and censoring/inequality flags.

B0 is a regularized logistic model containing age, sex, index encounter department, index procedure family, pre-index HCC/cirrhosis/portal-vein/liver-metastasis/lung-metastasis/bone-metastasis/ascites diagnosis indicators and counts, prior liver-directed procedure counts, pre-window assay values and missingness, and measured follow-up capture before day 45 only.

B1 adds the frozen AFP/hepatic trajectory phenotype and continuous changes.

B2 adds only observed process variables known by day 45: distinct encounter dates, lab rows and distinct lab dates, assay-specific days-to-first measurement and availability, relevant-examination count/days-to-first/examination-family indicators, and optional explicitly labeled completed-record proxies from orders. Do not describe these as access, adherence, clinical appropriateness, or true surveillance; they are record-capture proxies. Do not include a paired-AFP indicator within the paired population because it is constant there.

M3 is the substantive alternative: an irregular-time joint state/observation model with two latent proxy states over days -30 to 45 (tumor-activity proxy informed by log1p AFP, and hepatic-function proxy informed jointly by the four observed hepatic assays), assay-specific random intercepts/slopes or a low-rank state-space representation, and an observation submodel for whether each assay/examination is recorded conditional on prior measurements, baseline covariates, and latent state. Link the day-46 onward discrete-time hazard of first recorded repeat TACE to posterior day-45 levels, slopes, state uncertainty, capture intensity, baseline history, and index procedure family. Fit only on fitting patients; do not use future observations to smooth the day-45 state. M3 tests whether asynchronous trajectory shape plus measurement opportunity contains information that last-value/snapshot features lose. It cannot recover true tumor burden, hepatic reserve, treatment response, or access.

The all-index cohort is a separate estimand. Fit a training-only model for probability of being paired-biomarker complete among all otherwise eligible index patients, inspect overlap, and use stabilized inverse-probability weights (1st/99th percentile truncation) to transport the complete-case phenotype association toward the observed all-index cohort. If overlap fails, report the sensitivity as inconclusive rather than extrapolate.

## Splits, evaluation, uncertainty, and falsification

Use patient-level calendar-year splits if the local index-year field is present and complete: 2010–2022 fitting, 2023 tuning, and 2024–2025-01-01 locked test. Otherwise use the catalog’s participant-hash discovery buckets with fixed fitting/tuning/test subranges. No patient may cross splits; publish split-membership hashes. A grouped random split is a stability sensitivity, not the primary result. All parser rules, standardization, imputation, penalties, latent settings, and weight models are fixed or learned without locked-test outcomes.

For B0, B1, B2, and M3 on the same paired population, report adjusted phenotype OR and 95% patient-bootstrap or declared robust intervals, AUROC, PR-AUC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, and mean log score on the locked test. The key comparison is B2 versus M3, with paired log-score and calibration differences; a 0.02 mean-log-score margin is an operational model-comparison margin, not a clinical utility threshold. Report all-index weighted OR and overlap diagnostics separately. Report the non-liver capture-control association with the same feature construction and uncertainty.

Supportive evidence for the primary claim requires: phenotype OR <=0.80 with a 95% interval entirely below 1.0; direction preserved under literal TACE naming and 90-/180-day window sensitivities; less than 30% attenuation after B2; no comparable phenotype association for the non-liver capture-control outcome; and M3 improving locked-test mean log score over B2 by at least 0.02 with an interval excluding zero without materially worse calibration. Examination flags, if used, must be reported as capture diagnostics; an adjudicated subset is needed before calling them response evidence.

Adverse evidence includes a null/reversed phenotype association, >=30% attenuation after B2 or weighting, a strong phenotype-by-testing interaction, equal prediction of unrelated procedures, instability under naming/window/assay rules, poor calibration, or an M3 gain that disappears with leakage and observation controls. A report lexical flag dominated by template/modality, with poor clinician agreement or no action association, is adverse to proxy use.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few paired patients/events in locked test, failed weight overlap, wide intervals spanning the margin, missing or non-dateable examinations, high censoring/unknown units, failed M3 convergence, or unavailable clinician review. Inconclusive means unresolved, not confirmation.

## Evidence limits and clinical advance

A supportive result would advance knowledge by showing that a precisely defined early biomarker trajectory has a reproducible association with local recorded retreatment beyond a measured observation process, while the capture control helps distinguish disease-specific signal from generic follow-up intensity. That could motivate a prospective validation study of early reassessment. It would not justify changing surveillance or retreatment in practice by itself.

The records cannot establish residual viable tumor, radiologic response, Barcelona stage, treatment intent, death, outside-care capture, true hepatic decompensation, or benefit from an escalation policy. These require time-aligned expert-adjudicated imaging/pathology, mortality/claims linkage, treatment intent, external validation, or prospective clinical follow-up. The verifier can check cohort construction, time leakage, model fitting, metrics, uncertainty linkage, and whether interpretations follow from outputs; it cannot establish those clinical claims.

## Reference dispositions and alternatives

The inspected natural-history demonstration supports a bounded adaptation of structured disease-sequence learning, but the local complete paired-AFP population is small, the endpoint is a clinician action, report text is unvalidated, and there is no external HCC cohort; a transformer is deferred, not banned. Revisit it only with a larger validated outcome set, reliable timestamps, or an external test cohort.

The inspected Bayesian demonstration provides a substantive rationale for M3’s latent longitudinal state and explicit observation likelihood. The proposed adaptation is EHR-only and does not reproduce its genetic analysis; no local genetic input or genetic claim is assumed.

The accessible cancer demonstration supplement documents lab-only and image-only ablations and missingness/cohort-invariance ideas, but the main article and full STAR Methods were unavailable and were not claimed to have been read. HCC has no image files, so the full multimodal experiment is not proposed. Its lab-only/missingness insight is represented by B2 and M3, not by a reproduction claim.

Baseline B0/B1/B2 and M3 answer the same recorded-action question on the same landmark, population, split, and uncertainty scale. The baseline preserves interpretability and makes incremental information auditable. M3 can reveal asynchronous latent trajectory and measurement-process structure lost by snapshots. The transformer alternative could reveal event-order motifs but is deferred for data/validation reasons above. GPU use is not scientifically required: tabular fits and a low-rank state model should be CPU-feasible; GPU use is permitted only if a measured allocated probe justifies it. The current inputs expose an 8-GPU A100 inventory, delivery limits, and a 7,200-second discovery science limit but no separate solver-planning-limit object; future solver time/memory estimates are therefore unverified and must be recorded by the executor rather than assumed.
