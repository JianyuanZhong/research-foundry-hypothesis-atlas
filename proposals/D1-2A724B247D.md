# ICU-recorded IV furosemide or bumetanide after post-shock stabilization: partition- and RRT-repaired proposal

## Unresolved question, evidence boundary, and clinical advance

Positive fluid balance after resuscitation is associated with poor outcomes, but association does not show that removing fluid improves outcomes. The indexed abstract of the 2017 systematic review by Silversides et al. (DOI `10.1007/s00134-016-4573-3`; frozen Europe PMC search `[source checksum]`; full paper not inspected) reports 11 heterogeneous randomized conservative/deresuscitative trials (2,051 patients), with more ventilator-free days but no precise mortality benefit. RADAR-2 was a 180-patient randomized feasibility trial of a bundled conservative-fluid/active-removal strategy, not isolated loop initiation; its indexed report (DOI `10.1007/s00134-021-06596-8`; frozen source `[source checksum]`; full text not inspected) reports lower day-3 balance but underpowered clinical outcomes. A 2026 full-text RADAR-2 secondary analysis (McMullan et al., DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404`; frozen Europe PMC XML source `[source checksum]`, source [source checksum]) found no statistically detectable biomarker kidney/perfusion differences but states that its size and imprecision cannot exclude clinically important effects. The 2026 Orieux et al. review (DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`; full Europe PMC XML inspected from frozen source `[source checksum]`, source [source checksum]) calls ICU renal congestion hypothesis-generating, without validated diagnostic criteria, therapeutic targets, or outcome-driven interventional evidence.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is therefore that heterogeneous conservative/deresuscitative bundles can reduce recorded balance and may improve ventilator-free days; available evidence does not establish the benefit or renal safety of initiating actual IV furosemide/bumetanide at this stable landmark. The unresolved claim is a treatment effect for a narrower bedside decision: initiate or re-initiate an ICU-recorded IV loop within 24 hours versus withhold all loops for 24 hours after post-shock stabilization. The advance is a reproducible target-trial emulation with an exact decision time, actual administration exposure, treatment-confounder feedback adjustment, patient-centered benefit, separately bounded mortality and RRT signals, and explicit observation limits. It is not a claim that an EHR phenotype proves renal congestion or that a null observed RRT contrast proves renal safety.

## Hypothesis and estimand

Among invasively ventilated adults at ICU hour 48 after vasopressor-treated shock who are currently stable off vasopressors for six hours, have at least 50 mL/kg documented positive ICU balance, adequate recent urine output, potassium at least 3.0 mmol/L, and no loop administration in the preceding 12 hours, initiating or re-initiating ICU-recorded IV furosemide or bumetanide within 24 hours increases 28-day days alive and free of invasive ventilation versus withholding every loop diuretic for 24 hours.

The clinical-relevance gate is a VFD28 A-minus-B point estimate of at least +1 day with a 95% interval excluding zero. The safety gates are an upper 95% bound below +3 percentage points for excess 28-day mortality and below +5 percentage points for excess observed new RRT by day 7. These are decision thresholds for this experiment, not equivalence margins establishing safety. The RRT endpoint is a combined ascertainment signal, not proof of renal safety, because ICU events are time-resolved but hospital procedure codes are date-only and both sources may miss clinical context.

A secondary, exploratory modifier is a larger VFD contrast when a pre-t0 result-available chest report explicitly describes pulmonary vascular/interstitial edema. This is not a validated renal-congestion phenotype.

## Documented balance and treatment strategies

“Documented positive balance” is not total-body water, fluid overload, or congestion. From `icu/outputevents`, use nonnegative numeric `value` at charttime in recognized mL/L units. Absence is missing, not zero. Freeze urine itemids 226557, 226558, 226559, 226560, 226561, 226563, 226564, 226565, 226566, 226567, 226584, 226627, 226631, 226713 and 227489; repeat a sensitivity excluding mixed urine/irrigant 226566/227489 and perioperative 226627/226631. Primary eligibility requires valid output in every pre-t0 12-hour block. Emit six-hour capture and repeat with all eight blocks complete, 30/100 mL/kg thresholds and continuous balance.

Strategy A is the first positive, non-rewritten ICU `inputevents` administration of furosemide itemids 221794 or 228340, or bumetanide 229639, starting in `(t0,t0+24h]`; no oral loop is allowed before that initiation. Strategy B has no furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide or ethacrynic acid administration by any route in `(t0,t0+24h]`. Care after hour 24 is unrestricted. The original 6- and 12-hour initiation windows remain sensitivities, not replacements for the 24-hour estimand.

Reject Not Given, Hold, Confirmed/order-only, Stopped and Not Started. Route is the aggregate of nonblank detail-row routes; blank remains unknown. Orders and prescriptions never define treatment. Deduplicate same-drug ICU/eMAR events within 15 minutes, report overlap and discordance, and require sensitivity to ICU events corroborated by accepted same-drug eMAR within 15 minutes and to eMAR events with explicit IV route. A material opposing or greater-than-one-day VFD sensitivity conflict with a paired bootstrap interval excluding the conflict threshold forces interpretation inconclusive; duplication alone is not a validity threshold.

Clone each eligible person to both strategies at t0. Censor A at the first nonprotocol oral loop, ICU discharge before qualifying initiation, or hour 24 if untreated; censor B at the first loop or alive discharge with unknown medication during grace. Death during grace is retained as VFD=0 and is never treated as nonadherence. Estimate inverse-probability adherence/censoring weights. The estimand is the per-protocol mean VFD28 difference A minus B in this eligible start/restart population, conditional on consistency, positivity, correct measurement and no unmeasured confounding.

## Outcomes and RRT observation repair

Primary VFD28 runs from t0 through day 28, but MIMIC does not observe ventilation outside linked ICU stays and an alive discharge must not be deterministically carried forward as alive and unventilated. Construct valid invasive-ventilation intervals from `icu/procedureevents.itemid=225792` across every observed ICU stay and readmission for the subject, union overlaps, and clip them to the linked `icustays.intime,outtime` intervals and the 28-day horizon. Death known by day 28 gives VFD=0 under every completion; death uses the earliest available `admissions.deathtime`, `patients.dod`, or `hospital_expire_flag`, with early/late scenarios for date-only DOD.

For each survivor, emit observed ICU time, observed ventilated time, observed alive/nonventilated time, and alive time outside linked ICU observation. Define `VFD_L` by assigning all unobserved alive time ventilated and `VFD_U` by assigning it nonventilated. Both lie in [0,28]. The per-protocol VFD effect is partially identified as `Delta_L = mean_w(A,VFD_L)-mean_w(B,VFD_U)` through `Delta_U = mean_w(A,VFD_U)-mean_w(B,VFD_L)`; compute both with the clone-censor weights and paired subject-level bootstrap intervals. This is an identification interval from missing observation, not a confidence interval. Report alive-discharge carry-forward, hospital-observed VFD, alive/extubated time while observed, 48-hour liberation by day 7 with early discharge unknown, and ICU/hospital-free days only as sensitivities. If a DOD boundary changes either VFD bound or its interpretation branch, the result is inconclusive.

### Combined observed new-RRT endpoint

The day-7 safety endpoint is the first delivered dialysis/RRT event after t0 and before t0+7 days, as the union of:

1. Exact-time ICU events in `icu/procedureevents` with itemids 225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809 or 225955, valid `starttime/endtime/storetime`, and an accepted delivered procedure status. Use the event interval and starttime for ordering; retain stay_id, hadm_id, itemid, statusdescription and all timestamps. An ICU event overlapping t0 is pre-existing for eligibility and is not a new event.

2. Dated hospital delivered-dialysis procedure rows in `hosp/procedures_icd`, joined on `icd_code,icd_version` to `hosp/d_icd_procedures`, limited to dictionary-confirmed delivered codes: ICD-9 3995 (hemodialysis), 5498 (peritoneal dialysis), and ICD-10-PCS 5A1D00Z, 5A1D60Z, 5A1D70Z, 5A1D80Z and 5A1D90Z. Do not count ICD-9 3895 venous dialysis catheterization or access/shunt procedures as delivered RRT. Restrict codes to the index `hadm_id`; join keys are `subject_id,hadm_id`, and retain `seq_num,chartdate,icd_code,icd_version,long_title`. These codes establish a delivered procedure category but not an exact clock time.

Because `procedures_icd.chartdate` is date-only, report a three-state date bracket rather than silently assigning midnight:

- Definite in-window hospital-code events have `chartdate` strictly after `date(t0)` and strictly before `date(t0+7 days)`.
- Ambiguous-boundary hospital-code events have `chartdate = date(t0)` or `chartdate = date(t0+7 days)`; they may be in or out of the exact window.
- Definite out-of-window hospital-code events have `chartdate < date(t0)` or `chartdate > date(t0+7 days)`.

The primary combined endpoint reports a lower ascertainment bound using only exact-time ICU events plus definite in-window hospital codes, and an upper bound adding both ambiguous boundary-date hospital codes. The primary safety contrast must be recomputed under both bounds. For pre-t0 eligibility, exclude exact-time ICU RRT overlapping t0 or beginning before t0, and exclude hospital delivered codes with `chartdate < date(t0)`; a conservative sensitivity excludes any index-admission hospital code on `date(t0)` as potentially pre-t0. A same-day hospital code is never given a fabricated time.

Report separately: ICU-only events; definite hospital-code additions; ambiguous-date additions; procedure-code coverage among ICU events; and the proportion of subjects whose RRT status changes between lower and upper bounds. If the RRT-harm gate changes classification across the bracket, or if missing/unknown procedure status, unmatched dictionary rows, admission-link failures, or incomplete ward capture are material, label renal-safety interpretation inconclusive. Death before an RRT event is a competing event; report cause-specific cumulative incidence and the bracketed risk difference, rather than coding death as no RRT.

This combined endpoint supports only the claim “no detected excess in the observed, bracketed RRT signal under the stated ascertainment.” It cannot support “renal safety,” absence of dialysis, prevention of AKI, or absence of kidney injury. Those stronger claims require time-complete ward records, validated clinical adjudication of dialysis indications and AKI, or another study. Creatinine, potassium, AKI stage, hypokalemia, new vasopressor use, MAP<55, balance and urine output remain secondary observed outcomes; post-discharge labs are unknown, not normal.

## Result logic

Supportive requires all of: the lower 95% bootstrap bound for `Delta_L` exceeds +1 VFD day, so benefit survives the worst admissible ventilation completion and sampling uncertainty; upper 95% bound for excess 28-day death <+3 points; upper 95% bound for excess observed day-7 RRT below +5 points under both lower and upper ascertainment bounds; expected balance/urine process direction; sufficient arm size/ESS; acceptable exposure, missingness and RRT ambiguity; and passed falsification. This supports external validation and a pragmatic randomized trial, not clinical benefit, renal safety, standard care or practice change.

Adverse is the upper 95% bootstrap bound for `Delta_U` <=0, meaning benefit is absent even under the optimistic ventilation completion, mortality-harm lower bound >=+3 points, or observed RRT-harm lower bound >=+5 points under the prespecified ascertainment interpretation. Favorable balance cannot rescue respiratory or safety harm. If the RRT result is adverse under the upper bound but not the lower bound, or vice versa, the renal-safety component is inconclusive rather than favorable or harmful.

Inconclusive/mixed includes `Delta_L` failing the supportive threshold while `Delta_U` remains compatible with benefit; wide confidence or identification intervals; benefit without both safety margins; conflicting VFD sensitivities; nonpositivity; material eMAR conflict; residual pre-t0 imbalance; missing follow-up; a lower/upper RRT bracket that changes the gate; missing ward capture; unknown procedure status; or an unvalidated text interaction. Nonsignificance is neither equivalence nor safety. A null or opposite validated imaging interaction falsifies only the modifier, not the primary hypothesis.
