# Can retrospective text reliably route a pulmonary-nodule handoff to clinician-confirmed propagation versus reconciliation?

## Decision question, strongest supported claim, and unresolved claim

The prospective choice is not between silent automation and human judgment. It is whether a live workflow should (1) prepare a structured target/action/due-date draft for clinician confirmation, (2) require side-by-side clinician reconciliation, or (3) suppress a target-specific handoff action because the contemporaneous record already contains a documented resolution. MIMIC can test whether retrospective, pre-departure text supports a reproducible **routing phenotype**. It cannot establish that an auto-draft is clinically safe, that a recommendation is guideline-appropriate, that a clinician saw it, or that any workflow improves follow-up.

The strongest evidence already supported is narrower: the inherited complete scan found 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, and 331,794 unique canonical `DS` admission keys; only 17 `DS` rows lacked `storetime`; ordinary `DS_store-D` was often positive; and a broad retrieval proxy found 3,764 pulmonary reports. These are feasibility and storage diagnostics, not adjudicated prevalence or benefit.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> Within the unchanged first-eligible-admission denominator and inherited ample storage-opportunity stratum, a recommendation-unit representation based only on evidence stored by departure can identify a robust subset whose entire admission is suitable for clinician-confirmed structured propagation, while routing conflicts, mixed plans, multiplicity, and reviewer uncertainty to reconciliation. This triage must reduce manual review without increasing text-supported unsafe routing relative to review-all, and its conclusion must survive reviewer-disagreement bounds. A nonthoracic comparator may test process generality but may not select the intervention.

This child repairs four decision-validity problems in the parent. First, “no visible contraindication” is no longer called clinical actionability. Second, supersession is derived from independently extracted contemporaneous attributes rather than a panel’s global judgment that also defines the outcome. Third, recommendation-unit multiplicity is nested under the frozen admission denominator so one admission cannot be counted repeatedly or called auto-routable when another recommendation requires review. Fourth, net benefit is not identified from MIMIC without utilities: policy choice is based first on a utility-free routing profile and partial order; any scalar net value is a sensitivity analysis over explicitly reported weights or an externally escrowed governance envelope.

## Recommendation units nested under the frozen admission unit

The admission remains the primary denominator and bootstrap cluster. To prevent multiplicity from being hidden, construct a radiology-only recommendation-unit manifest `Q` **before any discharge artifact is opened**.

A unit is one executable payload `{pulmonary target group, chest/thoracic CT action, one due interval/date}`. Multiple nodules governed by the same sentence/action/interval are one unit; distinct actions or due intervals are separate units. Two reviewers independently mark source character offsets and component IDs. Ambiguous splitting/merging is `Q_U`, never resolved using discharge text. Every unit receives `q_id=hash(subject_id,hadm_id,component_note_id,sorted_source_offsets)` and a component-specific `R_store_q`. Publish `m_i`, the number of units in admission `i`, including zero/unknown diagnostics, and the number of mixed-interval and mixed-component admissions.

The inherited admission-level index component and its `R_store/G/H/E_N` are unchanged. Unit-level clocks `G_q=DS_store-R_store_q` are descriptive and used only to prevent unsafe routing: an admission can enter the structured-draft lane only if the inherited index is in `E_N` and every unresolved unit proposed for drafting independently has `G_q>=24h`, known reciprocal chronology, and a schema-complete payload. This conservative extension cannot add an admission to `E_N`; it can only divert a mixed admission from draft to reconciliation/unknown.

Recommendation-level results use each `q` once, with patient/admission clustering, but decision-facing burdens are admissions per 100 `N`. No sum over `q` may be presented as patients or admissions. Also report recommendations per routed admission as workload, not prevalence.

## Outcome-independent evidence packets and atomic labels

All evidence is restricted to data stored by `D`; later text, POE, later imaging, death after departure, outside-care inference, and downstream outcomes are prohibited from all routing packets.

### Packet A: observable contextual veto, not “clinical actionability”

Two discharge-experienced physicians independently receive the operative radiology chain, structured admission/disposition variables known by `D`, and a canonical `DS` copy prepared by an independent redaction team. All target-specific pulmonary lesion, follow-up action, interval/date, named specialist, referral/order, refusal, and superseding-plan spans are removed with recorded character offsets. Timestamps, `G/H/V`, propagation labels, downstream traces, and study policy are absent.

Reviewers assign only:

* `C_VETO`: explicit contemporaneous non-target context states that no outpatient follow-up action can be pursued (for example, a global comfort-only disposition). Because such contexts are denominator exclusions, this triggers an eligibility audit.
* `C_NO`: no explicit contextual veto is visible in the allowed packet.
* `C_U`: redaction, missing context, or ambiguous status prevents classification.

`C_NO` means **no observable veto**, not that follow-up is appropriate, active, desired, or safe. The design never converts absence of evidence into clinical actionability.

Redaction is audited in `max(50,10%)` randomly selected packets, stratified by formatter/redactor and unit multiplicity, by a third redactor plus a clinician. Any residual target-plan leakage in more than 2% of audited packets, removal of material non-target context in more than 2%, or an absolute `C`-rate difference of at least 0.05 between randomized redactors blocks routing conclusions. Seeded out-of-dictionary target phrases test leakage before real labels are released.

### Packet B: independent attribute extraction and deterministic plan relation

Two different physicians receive the unredacted operative radiology chain and the unique canonical `DS` only when `DS_store<=D`. They receive no clock/gap bins, Packet-A labels, later events, comparator results, or policy hypothesis. For every `q`, each reviewer independently marks exact `DS` spans and separately records target linkage, action/modality, due interval/date, accountable next step if stated, active-versus-historical/conditional status, explicit rationale, scheduled-versus-proposed wording, and target-linked informed non-pursuit. Reviewers do not answer “is this superseding?” or “should this be automated?”

A locked deterministic crosswalk assigns:

* `L_F`: target, active chest/thoracic CT action, and compatible exact interval/date are present.
* `L_O`: no target-specific action span exists.
* `L_I`: target/action/timing is present but incomplete.
* `L_EQ`: an explicit active target-linked action is operationally no later/less protective than the radiology payload and has a concrete next step; this is **text-documented equivalence**, not verified completion or clinical adequacy.
* `L_DEC`: explicit target-linked informed non-pursuit or target-linked goals-of-care decision.
* `L_CON`: explicit target-linked action conflicts with modality/timing or is less protective, without an explicit contemporaneous rationale satisfying the locked replacement rule.
* `L_U`: linkage, timing, active status, responsibility, rationale, extraction, or unit alignment cannot be resolved.

“Follow with PCP” alone is `L_O`; specialist mention without target-linked action and timing is `L_U`; a stated scheduled compatible CT is `L_EQ` but remains unverified; incompatible timing without adequate explicit rationale is `L_CON`. A plan first appearing in `DS_store>D` cannot resolve departure. Stale artifacts remain outside `E_N`. This atomic extraction preserves inherited `Z/Y` but avoids asking one panel to define both the outcome and its clinical interpretation.

### Reviewer disagreement is data, not repaired ground truth

Keep both initial labels. Consensus/adjudicator labels are descriptive only. For each unit, define a **robust set** (both initial reviewers agree on all decision-relevant attributes) and a **possible set** (the union of all labels compatible with either review, missingness, and the deterministic crosswalk). Any disagreement that can change routing is primary `U`, even if a third reviewer breaks the tie.

Report pairwise confusion matrices, raw agreement, kappa, prevalence-adjusted agreement (AC1), reviewer-specific rates, disagreement reasons, and adjudicator movement. Directional routing requires: no more than 10% decision-changing disagreement in `E_N`; the same policy conclusion under every compatible reviewer completion; and no leave-one-reviewer or confidence-stratum reversal. Kappa is diagnostic, not a substitute for partial identification.

## Admission-level prospective routing lanes

Join `Q`, `C`, unit-level `L`, and inherited `V/H/Z/Y` only after every packet label is frozen. Unit-level draftability requires `C_NO`, robust `L_O`, schema-complete unconditional payload, known `G_q>=24h`, and no unresolved branch or prerequisite. Unit-level reconciliation need is robust `L_I` or `L_CON`; `L_U`, `C_U`, or reviewer disagreement is uncertain; `L_F/L_EQ/L_DEC` is documented resolution/fidelity.

Assign each admission in `E_N` exactly once:

1. `A_DRAFT`: at least one unit is robustly draftable and **every other unit** is either robustly faithful/resolved or robustly draftable; no unit is reconciliation-needed, uncertain, `Q_U`, short-lead, or context-vetoed. This supports only a clinician-confirmed prepared draft.
2. `A_RECON`: at least one unit is robustly incomplete/conflicting, or the admission contains mixed active payloads requiring a human choice, with no decision-changing uncertainty.
3. `A_RES`: every unit is robustly faithful, `L_EQ`, or `L_DEC`; no new action is routed.
4. `A_NONE`: all units have an explicit `C_VETO`, with no contradictory unit evidence; audit denominator eligibility.
5. `A_UN`: any `C_U`, `L_U`, `Q_U`, decision-changing reviewer disagreement, unresolved unit chronology, or mixture not safely mapped above.

The priority rule is `A_UN > A_RECON > A_DRAFT > A_RES/A_NONE`; thus multiplicity can never make a mixed admission look auto-routable. Outside `E_N`, retain labels descriptively but do not call them ample-opportunity propagation failures.

Primary burdens are `B_k=100*n(A_k)/N` for every lane, including zero/unknown cells, with the `n(E_N)/N` decomposition. Recommendation-level counts and `m_i` describe workload only. Report the complete inherited-`Z/Y` to admission-lane matrix.

## Utility-free policy comparison and nonidentified net benefit

Evaluate four locked, admission-level policies against the robust and possible label sets:

* `P_NONE`: no artifact-content action.
* `P_REVIEW_ALL`: manual review of every `E_N` admission.
* `P_DISC`: manual review of every inherited classifiable discordance.
* `P_TRIAGE`: clinician-confirmed structured draft for `A_DRAFT`, manual reconciliation for `A_RECON` and `A_UN`, and no new target action for `A_RES/A_NONE`.

For each policy report per 100 `N`: draft count; manual-review count; robust draftable admissions captured; robust reconciliation admissions captured; resolved admissions unnecessarily reviewed; **unsafe-draft envelope** (any drafted admission that either initial review can classify as conflict, resolution/non-pursuit, contextual veto, unknown, or mixed); missed-reconciliation envelope; and abstention/unknown burden. These quantities are computable without assuming that one false draft equals one missed reconciliation or one review.

A utility-free dominance claim is allowed only if one policy is no worse on unsafe drafts, missed reconciliation, and robust capture, and strictly reduces manual review or abstention, under simultaneous confidence bounds and all compatible reviewer assignments. In particular, retrospective evidence favors taking `P_TRIAGE` to a prospective pilot over `P_REVIEW_ALL` only if: the simultaneous upper bound for unsafe drafts is lower than 5 per 100 drafts; the lower bound for capture of robust `A_RECON` exceeds 90%; at least 20 manual reviews per 100 `E_N` are saved; and no compatible reviewer completion reverses any condition. These are prespecified routing-performance benchmarks, not estimates of patient utility or deployment safety.

MIMIC does not identify a universal threshold probability or scalar net benefit. Therefore the parent's `t=0.90` and `t=0.50` decision-curve claims no longer select an intervention. As a sensitivity, define normalized net value

`NV_lambda(P)=TP_D + TP_R - lambda_U*UnsafeDraft - lambda_M*MissedRecon - lambda_R*ManualReview - lambda_D*Drafts`,

where one correctly routed robust draft or reconciliation is normalized to 1. Report the exact policy frontier over `lambda_U in {1,5,10,20}`, `lambda_M in {1,2,5,10}`, `lambda_R in {0.01,0.05,0.10,0.25}`, and `lambda_D in {0,0.01,0.05}`; do not average over this grid. A scalar recommendation is permitted only if a clinical-governance group prospectively escrows a narrower utility set before labels are unblinded and the same policy has positive simultaneous lower incremental `NV` throughout that entire set. Otherwise utility-dependent policy choice is explicitly inconclusive even if classification accuracy is high.

## Census, feasibility, uncertainty, and stop gates

Review the full frozen pulmonary `E_N` census; do not stop when a favorable event count is reached and do not sample outcome rows for the primary result. Before clinical review, publish event-blind counts of `N`, `E_N`, `Q`, multiplicity, unresolved chains, and packet workload. Failure of review capacity is a feasibility failure, not permission to redefine `N`.

Source/key confirmation stops for any source hash/schema change, denominator-hash drift, duplicate canonical `DS`, nonreciprocal operative chain, impossible timestamp, or failure of `Y/J_N` marginals to sum to `N`. Retain inherited gates `N>=200`, `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, no duplicate `DS`, and acceptable clock/membership bounds, but strengthen decision-facing feasibility:

* at least 100 admissions in `E_N`;
* at least 60 admissions exposed to each evaluated nontrivial trigger;
* at least 40 robust-core `A_DRAFT` admissions for a draft-routing conclusion and at least 40 robust-core `A_RECON` admissions for a reconciliation conclusion;
* simultaneous 95% interval width no greater than 0.15 for each decision-facing trigger PPV/capture quantity;
* simultaneous interval width no greater than 2 admissions per 100 `N` for the corresponding absolute burden;
* decision-changing reviewer disagreement no greater than 10%, unknown admission lane no greater than 10%, and unchanged conclusions under full compatible label and membership bounds.

Count failure makes that lane and every utility calculation involving it inconclusive; it does not become evidence of no burden. Comparator failure affects specificity only.

Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates and a max-statistic simultaneous 95% family carrying all inherited cells, recommendation multiplicity, robust/possible labels, admission lanes, policy profiles, calibration contrasts, and comparator estimates. Because reviewer labels are crossed with cases, additionally use a two-way subject-and-reviewer bootstrap sensitivity. Primary partial-identification endpoints are the extrema over all logically compatible initial-reviewer, unknown, and membership completions; consensus cannot narrow them. Report pointwise intervals only descriptively.

## Falsification and verifier checks

1. Recomputed hashes, schemas, complete-source row counts, denominator hash, and unique `DS` key must match; otherwise stop.
2. The same `N` underlies every output; admission lanes and `Y` each sum to `N`, and recommendation counts can never be labeled admission counts.
3. `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` holds row-wise; no stale, delayed, or unresolved row enters inherited `E_N`.
4. Creating `Q` after opening discharge text, merging units because the `DS` merged them, or selecting a unit by its outcome fails verification.
5. A multi-unit admission with one pure omission and one conflict/unknown must route to `A_RECON/A_UN`, never `A_DRAFT`.
6. Packet A contains no target-plan spans or prohibited timestamps/downstream fields. `C_NO` must print as “no observable veto,” never “clinically actionable.”
7. Packet B reviewers extract attributes; code, not reviewers, applies the locked relation. PCP-only, vague specialist, scheduled compatible CT, target-linked refusal, and incompatible interval synthetic cases map respectively to `L_O,L_U,L_EQ,L_DEC,L_CON`.
8. Post-`D` plans, POE rows, later CT, later death, and eventual outcomes cannot change `C`, `L`, `Q`, `E_N`, or an admission lane.
9. Replacing all disagreements with adjudicator consensus must not be the primary analysis. A supportive conclusion that disappears under either initial review or compatible completion is inconclusive.
10. A high raw discordance rate with mostly `L_EQ/L_DEC`, contextual uncertainty, or mixed units cannot be called draftable. Pure robust schema-complete omissions with no other unit map to `A_DRAFT`.
11. The policy table must expose unsafe drafts, missed reconciliation, manual review, draft workload, capture, and abstention separately. Favorable cancellation in scalar `NV` cannot rescue failure of a safety/capture benchmark.
12. No fixed threshold or utility weight may be described as MIMIC-estimated. If no externally escrowed utility envelope exists, the scalar preferred policy is “utility-dependent/inconclusive.”
13. `X_SCHEMA` is assigned without discharge text/outcomes. Broad `X`, schema subset, organ-family, matched, and leave-one-out results are all shown; heterogeneity cannot be averaged away.
14. Institutional calibration, threshold, charttime placebo, reviewer leave-one-out, redactor, formatter, multiplicity, and standardization/matching reversal checks retain their inherited stop interpretations.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results

**Supportive for a prospective clinician-confirmed structured-propagation pilot** requires a nonzero robust-core `B_DRAFT`; all strengthened count/precision, redaction, disagreement and membership gates; simultaneous unsafe-draft upper bound below 5%; robust reconciliation-capture lower bound above 90%; at least 20 manual reviews saved per 100 `E_N` versus review-all; and no utility-free dominance reversal under compatible labels. This supports testing a prepared draft with mandatory clinician accept/modify/reject and reason capture. It does not support silent insertion or deployment.

**Supportive for a prospective clinician-reconciliation pilot** requires a nonzero, reproducible `B_RECON`, at least 40 robust events, acceptable precision/unknown bounds, and materially better capture per manual review than inherited discordance-review while making no unsafe draft claim. Mixed-unit or conflict enrichment supports side-by-side review rather than copying.

**Supportive for a bundled pilot** requires both sets of criteria and prospectively separable outcomes. **Supportive only for availability/timing work** occurs when delayed/stale/short-window burdens dominate and inherited calibrated `Delta_A` supports a storage-timing phenotype, without calling it visibility failure.

**Adverse to structured propagation** includes unsafe-draft bounds at or above 5%, little review saving, common mixed/conflicting units, or absence of a robust draft core despite adequate precision. **Adverse to targeted reconciliation** includes no reproducible enrichment or no workload advantage versus review-all despite adequate events. A comparator showing a generic process is adverse only to nodule specificity, not to the pulmonary routing result.

**Inconclusive** includes sparse `E_N` or lane events, wide simultaneous intervals, review-capacity failure, redaction leakage, decision-changing disagreement/unknowns above 10%, source/chain/key/clock failure, multiplicity ambiguity, threshold or reviewer reversal, utility-dependent policy rankings without an escrowed envelope, or sparse/heterogeneous comparator families. Sparse events are not a null finding.

## Claims MIMIC can and cannot support

MIMIC can support, conditional on its stored text and explicit reviewer uncertainty, the frozen admission and recommendation-unit counts; storage chronology; text-documented concordance, omission, conflict, resolution and uncertainty; reproducibility of a conservative routing phenotype; admission-level workload and routing-error envelopes; institutional storage calibration; and whether a schema-portable documentation pattern generalizes across adequately represented nonthoracic families.

MIMIC cannot establish recommendation appropriateness, active clinical necessity, complete competing-risk context, actual draft safety, contemporaneous visibility, author awareness, UI exposure, communication, patient preference outside documented text, accountability, a true linked outpatient order, scheduling, outside care, adherence, malignancy, harm, causal mechanism, clinical utility weights, or intervention benefit. A prospective pilot must collect report finalization/view events; artifact version/edit/sign/transmission times; clinician accept/modify/reject and reasons; target-linked order/referral/scheduling and responsible team; communication receipt; preference/competing-risk context; outside records; and follow-up outcomes. Clinical governance must define utilities and ratify safety before any randomized or deployed workflow study.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

This child leaves the parent's exact MIMIC provenance, first-eligible all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal chronology, unique canonical artifact, `F/C_O/C_I/R/A/U_D`, `G=DS_store-R_store`, `V/H/Z`, stale/delayed exclusions, institutional controls, and noncausal boundary unchanged. It replaces retrospective “actionability” with observable-veto language, decomposes supersession into independently extracted attributes, makes disagreement a partial-identification problem, prevents recommendation multiplicity from inflating or misrouting admissions, and removes unidentifiable threshold utilities from intervention selection. The nonthoracic comparator is radiology-defined, organ-stratified, and limited to process generality.

The remaining uncertainty is fundamental: even perfectly reproducible pre-departure text routing is not clinical ground truth. A supportive result identifies the most defensible **prospective workflow pilot** and the cases in which human reconciliation remains necessary; it does not establish that structured propagation is safe, beneficial, or ready for use.
