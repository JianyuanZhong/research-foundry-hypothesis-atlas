# Actionability-confirmed decision frontier for pulmonary-nodule handoff discordance

## Decision question, strongest supported evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

The decision is not whether ample-gap text discordance exceeds an unvalidated 10% prevalence cutoff. It is whether a future reconciliation policy would encounter enough **clinically actionable, unresolved propagation failures** to justify a prospectively instrumented pilot at a locally acceptable review burden, and whether the appropriate pilot is nodule-targeted or generic across semantically transportable recommendation types. MIMIC-IV can estimate storage-and-text phenotypes and support blinded clinical adjudication. It cannot estimate the effect, harm, benefit, uptake, or workload of an intervention.

The strongest supported evidence remains narrow and inherited: the snapshot contains a feasible retrieval pool, a unique canonical `DS` for almost every admission key, and substantial ordinary discharge storage latency. The assessed parent correctly freezes the first eligible opportunity per subject, uses `G=DS_store-R_store` rather than recommendation-to-discharge lead, keeps the complete `V x H x Z` and `F/C_O/C_I/R/A/U_D` states over all `N`, separates stale and delayed artifacts, and calibrates institutional latency. It has not shown recommendation appropriateness, actionability, propagation failure, preventability, or benefit.

The remaining flaw is decision validity. The parent calls discordance operationally material when a lower confidence bound exceeds 10%, although 10% is neither tied to review capacity nor to the consequences of false alerts. Moreover, its nonthoracic comparator pools actions whose clinical prerequisites and discharge affordances may differ, so a contrast cannot by itself choose a nodule-specific versus generic intervention.

This child tests the following conditional, falsifiable hypothesis:

> Among frozen pulmonary opportunities with a timely canonical artifact and at least 24 hours of positive recommendation-to-artifact storage gap, expert-confirmed discharge-actionable recommendations have a nontrivial joint burden of unresolved omission or interval/action discordance. A reconciliation pilot is decision-supported only if the simultaneous lower partial-identification frontier exceeds a locally predeclared maximum reviews-per-confirmed-actionable-failure (or equivalent review-burden ratio). A nodule-targeted rather than generic pilot is supported only on exact clinically transportable comparator strata; otherwise intervention class remains unresolved.

“Nontrivial” is deliberately not replaced by another universal percentage. Before discharge content is unblinded, an independent clinical-governance file must lock the maximum acceptable reviews per confirmed actionable failure, the relative burden of reviewing a nonfailure, capacity, and whether a missed confirmed actionable failure receives one standardized decision unit. Harbor computes the full frontier even if that file is absent, but without a locked local threshold it may describe the phenotype only and must label the intervention decision inconclusive. Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive labels below concern next-study choice, never clinical benefit.

## The repaired content-opportunity clock and all-N joint state

For each frozen opportunity define:

* `R_store`: `storetime` of the adjudicated recommendation-bearing report/addendum component;
* `DS_store`: `storetime` of the unique canonical `DS`;
* `G = DS_store - R_store`, in hours: the only primary observed content-opportunity clock;
* `V`, the canonical artifact storage state relative to departure: `V_D` if `DS_store <= D`; `V_24` if `D < DS_store <= D+24h`; `V_72` if `D+24h < DS_store <= D+72h`; `V_168` if `D+72h < DS_store <= D+168h`; `V_late` if `DS_store > D+168h`; and `V_U` if unresolved;
* `H`, the recommendation-to-artifact gap state when both storage times are known: `H_stale` if `G < 0`; `H_0_6` if `0 <= G < 6h`; `H_6_24` if `6 <= G < 24h`; `H_24_72` if `24 <= G < 72h`; `H_72plus` if `G >= 72h`; and `H_U` otherwise;
* `Z`, the eventual canonical artifact text state `F_text, C_O_text, C_I_text, R_text, U_text`, using the same blinded content definitions regardless of storage time. `Z` is descriptive for delayed artifacts and cannot overwrite `Y=A` at departure.

The primary data object is the complete joint all-`N` table `J_N = count(V,H,Z)/N`, accompanied by the inherited `Y` table. It must include zero cells and unknown cells. This table makes three chronology distinctions that the parent’s recommendation-to-discharge lead could not make:

1. `V_D,H_stale`: the artifact was observed stored by discharge but before the recommendation, so there was no positive observed incorporation window;
2. `V_D,H_0_6` through `H_72plus`: the artifact was stored by departure after the recommendation, with the actual positive storage-to-storage content opportunity measured by `G`;
3. `V_24/V_72/V_168/V_late`: the eventual artifact may have a positive and even long `G`, but it was delayed at the departure boundary and remains `A` in the primary state.

The primary ample, timely content-opportunity stratum is locked as

`E_N = {V = V_D and H in (H_24_72, H_72plus)}`.

The 24-hour threshold is an operational decision margin, not a proven human editing threshold. `H_72plus` is displayed separately and is a prespecified stricter sensitivity. Near-gap strata are reported but are not the reference required to establish persistence. `D - R_store` is retained only as a descriptive decomposition satisfying `D - R_store = (D - DS_store) + G`; it cannot define a content opportunity stratum or support a mechanism conclusion.

Within every `V_D,H` stratum, report all five `Z` categories, not only classifiable cases. Define conservative discordance as

`p_C,N(h) = [n(C_O_text)+n(C_I_text)] / n(V_D,H=h)`,

so `R_text` and `U_text` remain in the denominator rather than being deleted. Also report multinomial proportions for `F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text` and the all-opportunity joint burden `j_C,N(h)=n(V_D,H=h,C)/N`. For `E_N`, combine the two ample bins by the same rule to obtain `p_C,N(E)`. A complementary upper content bound assigns `U_text` to `C`; membership bounds additionally assign unresolved `DS_store`/`R_store`/chain states to or away from `E` in all logically compatible ways. A supportive content conclusion must survive the conservative lower-compatible assignment; unknowns can prevent support but cannot manufacture it.

`H_stale` is never included in a positive-gap content-failure estimand. It remains visible in `J_N`, and its `Z` distribution is reported as stale text concordance/discordance only. Delayed `V` strata retain eventual `Z` to describe transition patterns at +24h, +72h, and +168h, but no delayed artifact enters `E_N` and eventual fidelity cannot be called a departure-time handoff.

## Actionability, interval fidelity, and decision-facing estimands

### Blinded actionability adjudication independent of propagation

After `N`, report/addendum chains, and the operative recommendation are frozen—but before discharge text, `DS_store`, `G`, downstream traces, or comparator outcome is exposed—two independent panels review a fixed pre-`D` packet. The packet contains the full operative radiology chain and only those pre-discharge admission-note elements needed to identify explicit contraindication, superseding plan, goals-of-care limitation, specialist ownership, or already scheduled resolution. No later note, order, image, outcome, or discharge artifact is permitted.

A thoracic radiologist plus pulmonologist/hospitalist classify recommendation eligibility/appropriateness separately from discharge actionability:

* `Q_D` (definitely discharge-actionable): on available pre-`D` evidence, an explicit discharge disposition is warranted and one of the feasible actions can be named;
* `Q_P` (probably actionable): likely actionable, but an unavailable clinical fact could reverse the judgment;
* `Q_N` (not discharge-actionable): a documented pre-`D` contraindication, superseding decision, completed/scheduled resolution, or explicit governance rubric makes discharge reconciliation unnecessary;
* `Q_U`: insufficient or conflicting evidence.

They also adjudicate source interval fidelity as `L_exact`, `L_equivalent` (a locked equivalence map such as 12 months versus one year), `L_materially_different`, or `L_U`, and rate whether a discrepancy would change the action, timing category, or ownership (`K_major`, `K_minor`, `K_U`). These are clinical-semantic judgments, not claims of harm. Reasons and source spans are retained. Consensus never erases pre-consensus labels. A third reviewer resolves disagreement. Reviewers are blinded to canonical discharge content and all storage intervals.

A separate content panel, receiving only the frozen recommendation abstraction and canonical `DS` text, retains `F_text/C_O_text/C_I_text/R_text/U_text` and applies the locked interval-equivalence map. It does not judge whether the recommendation should have been made. Thus recommendation eligibility, clinical actionability, interval fidelity, and propagation are distinct dimensions. `R_text` remains an alternate resolution, not failure.

### Complete joint states and partial identification

Preserve the inherited all-`N` `J_N=count(V,H,Z)/N`, departure `Y`, and every stale/delayed/unknown safeguard. Add `J_Q=count(V,H,Z,Q,L,K,S)/N` with zero cells. No `Q` or `S` category changes membership in frozen `N`; no later artifact or event converts an opportunity. The primary ample, timely stratum remains exactly `E_N={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. Stale artifacts and every post-discharge artifact remain outside content-incorporation estimands.

Define a **definite actionable propagation failure** (`APF_D`) only when `E_N`, `Q_D`, and `Z in {C_O_text,C_I_text}` hold, no `R_text` alternate resolution is present, and interval/action review is classifiable. Define `APF_major` as the subset with `K_major`; it is a text/action discrepancy category, not an adverse event. Report:

* `alpha_D=n(E_N,Q_D)/n(E_N)`, definite actionability among ample timely opportunities;
* `phi_D=n(APF_D)/n(E_N,Q_D)`, propagation failure conditional on definite actionability;
* `psi_D=n(APF_D)/N`, confirmed actionable-failure yield per frozen trigger-eligible opportunity;
* `psi_major=n(APF_major)/N`;
* all corresponding joint numerators per `N`, not only conditional percentages.

The conservative lower bound counts only dual-review `Q_D` and classifiable `C_O/C_I`; disagreement, `Q_P/Q_U`, `U_text`, unresolved interval, `H_U`, and `V_U` do not create failures. The compatible upper bound allows `Q_P/Q_U`, content unknown, and logically eligible timing unknowns to be failures. Also report a reviewer-label partial-identification interval: lower = intersection of the two independent reviewers' definite labels; upper = union of definite/probable labels. Consensus is secondary. If lower and upper bounds imply different decisions, the result is inconclusive. `Q_N` is never silently removed: it remains in `N` and supplies an explicit estimate of recommendation-ineligible/nonactionable burden.

### Workload and value frontier replacing the universal 10% rule

For the deterministic future policy “surface every frozen-eligible recommendation for reconciliation,” the MIMIC-observable workload proxy is one review per `N`; MIMIC does not observe actual alert delivery or review time. Let `r=psi_D` be confirmed actionable failures per candidate review. Report `reviews_per_APF=1/r` (infinite if zero) with simultaneous bounds. For a standardized review-burden ratio `c>=0`, compute the **triage value index**

`TVI(c)=r-c*(1-r)`.

One unit denotes finding one confirmed actionable propagation failure and `c` denotes the burden assigned to reviewing a candidate without such a failure. `TVI` is a transparent decision index, not health net benefit. Report its lower/upper partial-identification curve on the locked grid `c={0,0.01,0.02,0.05,0.10,0.20,0.50,1}` and the break-even ratio `c*=r/(1-r)`. Also report capacity-normalized yields for 10, 25, 50, and 100 reviews; these are frontiers, not recommendations.

An independent governance JSON, hashed before discharge-content adjudication, may supply `max_reviews_per_APF`, `c_range`, annual review capacity, and the acceptable lower-bound rule, with a written rationale based on staffing and intended action. It may not use MIMIC outcomes. The compiler validates that values are finite/nonnegative and applies both mathematically equivalent criteria (`lower r >= 1/max_reviews_per_APF` and lower `TVI(c)>0` over the declared range). If the file is absent, altered, or declared after unblinding, Harbor reports the frontier but makes no intervention-warranted label. This is the targeted replacement for `tau_abs=0.10`; neither 10% nor any new universal prevalence threshold drives the conclusion.

For nodule-targeted versus generic selection, compute the same `psi_D`, review-yield, and `TVI` curves in exact action-schema comparator common support, standardized only on locked inherited covariates. Compare policies with `Delta_psi=psi_D,N-psi_D,Xexact^std` and `Delta_TVI(c)`. The broad heterogeneous `X` contrast is descriptive only. A generic policy is not declared superior merely because pooled nonthoracic discordance is high.
