# Fixed-denominator full verification of the EHR vasopressor-restart endpoint

## Exact repair, clinical question, and evidence boundary

This targeted child preserves the completed symmetric MIMIC-IV restart-instability audit and every inherited adverse or noncausal finding. It changes neither the frozen retrospective population nor any audit rule or result. It repairs the exact catalog bindings and replaces the parent's outcome-dependent target of 200 adjudicable EHR restarts per arm, optional upward revision, sampled EHR negatives, and unspecified sequential rule with one locked prospective design: **6,000 consecutive first patients (3,000 whose qualifying stop is NE and 3,000 whose qualifying stop is VP), full reference verification of every eligible patient, no sample-size adaptation, no favorable stopping, and prespecified exact-binomial accuracy, equivalence, futility, and information gates.**

The unresolved question is:

> Among consecutive eligible adults with a genuine first non-simultaneous cessation attempt of one of two concurrently delivered vasopressors, does a locked EHR six-hour restart flag identify pump-native resumed delivery, and separately intent-qualified hemodynamic rescue, with adequate arm-specific validity and symmetry?

This matters because restart supplies 59 of 73 failures in the inherited endpoint. A misclassified or differentially classified restart can dominate a later clinical endpoint even if every calculation is reproducible. The strongest evidence now supported is narrower: MIMIC's interval-derived restart flag is computable, restart-dominated, and materially unstable under plausible symmetric transaction reconstruction. The prospective hypothesis remains untested. Neither phase estimates a stopping-order effect, proves pump truth from MIMIC, establishes safety/equipoise, or recommends NE-first or VP-first cessation.

## Phase 2: locked prospective full-verification study

### Consecutive population, unit, recurrence, and temporal boundaries

Before first enrollment, escrow the participating sites, 48-month accrual window, phenotype, file contract, mappings, thresholds, analysis code, and fixed sample size. Sites must pass a 14-day run-in in which pump, monitor, EHR, and adjudication-clock offsets are bounded within 30 seconds for at least 99% of monitored time and pump export completeness is at least 95%; run-in patients are excluded.

Screen every ICU admission in forward time. Eligibility at a candidate cessation attempt requires: age >=18; synchronized pump-confirmed concurrent NE and VP delivery for >=6 hours; NE >0 to 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 to 0.04 U/min; neither delivered dose increased in the preceding 30 minutes; no pump-confirmed epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine delivery in the preceding two hours; alive and physically in ICU; at least three valid MAP observations in three of four preceding 30-minute bins including the final bin, with median >=65 and minimum >=55 mmHg; and a non-simultaneous cessation attempt of one agent while the comparator remains pump-delivered. Eligibility cannot use future restart, reference outcome, export completeness after entry, or adjudicability.

A **cessation attempt** is a pump-native transition from positive delivered flow to zero delivered flow for the candidate stopped agent while the comparator remains positive. Both a clinician order/documentation stop and a pump-only execution qualify; source is retained. Simultaneous means both agents reach zero within the escrowed +/-60-second mapped-clock tolerance and is ineligible. The primary unit is the **first qualifying cessation attempt per patient across the entire enrollment window**, linked by the site master-patient identifier across admissions; no patient can enter the primary cohort twice. Observed-stop arm `A` is the drug actually first stopped (NE or VP), not a randomized assignment and not a treatment comparison.

Retain every later qualifying attempt for that patient in a separate recurrent-episode registry under identical full verification. A recurrent attempt requires (i) at least 24 hours after the prior `t0`; (ii) a newly established six-hour concurrent-delivery baseline satisfying all eligibility criteria; and (iii) no overlap of six-hour outcome windows. Analyze recurrent episodes only as a marginal descriptive estimand with patient-cluster bootstrap intervals and report first versus recurrent heterogeneity; they never augment the primary fixed denominator or rescue a failed primary gate.

Let pump time be the master clock. Site-specific affine clock maps estimated from immutable synchronization markers convert monitor and EHR timestamps to pump time; maps and uncertainty are frozen before labels. `t0` is the first pump timestamp at which the stopped agent's delivered flow becomes zero. The observation window is `(t0, min(t0+6h, death, physical ICU departure)]`. The EHR flag `E=1` is at least one locked positive normalized `inputevents`-analog interval for that stopped drug whose mapped `starttime` lies in that window; otherwise `E=0`. An event whose clock-uncertainty interval crosses `t0`, six hours, death, or departure is reference/EHR **indeterminate**, not moved to the favorable side. Death or departure before six hours truncates observation and is reported separately; absence of later rescue after truncation is not a negative outcome.

Accrue exactly 3,000 primary first patients in each observed-stop arm, total 6,000, or stop at 48 months. The fixed arm quota prevents abundant NE-first cases from substituting for VP-first information. No blinded or unblinded sample-size revision, denominator-triggered extension, replenishment of indeterminates, or favorable early stop is allowed. If either quota is not met by 48 months, the symmetry claim is inconclusive; arm-specific estimates may still be reported.

### Two separate reference standards, full verification, and matching

Verify **all 6,000 primary first patients and every recurrent registry episode**, regardless of `E`, by two independent critical-care/pharmacy reviewers blinded to arm-level aggregates and to downstream comparative outcomes. They may view the time-local pump trace, EHR audit trail, workflow and physiology needed for classification. A third reviewer resolves disagreements. Report raw agreement, Cohen's kappa where defined, reconciliation rate, reasons, and indeterminate fractions. Adjudication does not delete a row.

Define two immutable labels:

* **Pump-native resumed delivery `R_P=1`:** a genuine >=5-minute pump-off interval beginning at `t0`, followed within the observation window by matched positive delivered flow meeting the locked duration/bolus criterion. It does not assert intent.
* **Intent-qualified rescue `R_I=1`:** `R_P=1` plus concordant time-stamped evidence, adjudicated independently, that resumption was intended for hemodynamic support rather than a documentation correction, line/bag/channel transition, technical pause, transport continuation, or other non-rescue process. `R_I=0` requires affirmative classification as non-rescue or no resumed delivery; insufficient intent evidence is indeterminate, never negative.

Match EHR and pump events deterministically within patient/drug/window after clock mapping: construct candidate pairs within +/-5 minutes, rank by smallest absolute mapped-time distance, then exact drug/channel association, then immutable source ID; solve one-to-one minimum-cost matching, so one pump event cannot validate multiple EHR rows and vice versa. Unmatched events remain unmatched. Any unresolved drug mapping, conflicting pump channels, selective export gap, clock interval crossing a boundary, or reviewer-unresolvable state is `U`, retained in a 3x3 `E in {0,1,U}` by `R in {0,1,U}` table.

Do not merge the standards. Passing `R_P` but failing `R_I` means the EHR flag detects resumed delivery but not intent-qualified rescue; it cannot support an intent-qualified clinical endpoint. Passing `R_I` does not make MIMIC transactions pump truth.

### Locked estimands and exact analysis

For each standard separately and for each observed-stop arm, compute on definite cells:

`PPV=P(R=1|E=1)`, `NPV=P(R=0|E=0)`, `sensitivity=P(E=1|R=1)`, and `specificity=P(E=0|R=0)`.

PPV is the primary accuracy quantity because the inherited endpoint is restart-dominated. Sensitivity/specificity/NPV are co-required diagnostics made unbiased with respect to verification by the full consecutive design. For PPV and sensitivity, require at least 400 definite denominator observations per arm and standard (`n(E=1,R definite)>=400` and `n(R=1,E definite)>=400`). Specificity and NPV each require at least 400 definite denominator observations per arm as well. This is an **information gate**, not an accrual target: failure after fixed N is inconclusive and cannot trigger extension.

For each proportion, report the estimate and one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson bounds. PPV is the clinical acceptance metric: it must have point estimate >=0.80 and lower bound strictly >0.70. For sensitivity, specificity, and NPV, support requires the lower bound strictly >0.70; these three diagnostics have no additional 0.80 point-estimate requirement. Any accuracy metric is adverse if its upper one-sided 95% bound is strictly <0.70; otherwise it is inconclusive. At denominator 400, the lower-bound criterion alone begins at 296/400, the PPV rule begins at 320/400, and adverse ends at 264/400. The executed exact calculation for the locked PPV rule gives support probabilities for a true PPV of 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, and 0.85 of 0.0000039, 0.0108, 0.5299, and 0.9972; adverse probabilities are 0.0466, 0.0000357, <4.2e-11, and <3.1e-21, respectively. Thus a true value exactly at the minimum acceptable point is deliberately not called supportive reliably; the design has high support probability only when PPV materially exceeds 0.80. The 0.70 floor, 0.80 PPV target, and 0.10 symmetry margin require prospective clinical ratification; they are operating thresholds, not facts learned from MIMIC.

For each metric, reference standard, and arm pair, compute NE-minus-VP difference and an unpooled Newcombe interval from Wilson score intervals. Symmetry support uses two one-sided alpha=0.05 tests, equivalently a 90% CI lying **strictly inside (-0.10,0.10)**. Symmetry is adverse if a two-sided 95% Newcombe CI lies wholly at or beyond either margin; otherwise inconclusive. No phrase such as “when the target permits it” is allowed. Seeded 300,000-replicate operating calculations at 400 definite denominators per arm estimate symmetry-support probabilities of 0.778 for true PPVs 0.80/0.80, 0.900 for 0.85/0.85, 0.326 for 0.85/0.80, and about 0.011 at the exact 0.10 boundary (0.90/0.80 or 0.75/0.85). These operating characteristics expose rather than conceal the low power near the equivalence boundary.

No indeterminate row is dropped from robustness analysis. For every metric and difference, enumerate all integer-compatible assignments of the aggregate 3x3 `U` cells to binary E/R states within each arm, subject only to immutable observed margins, and recompute estimates and intervals. The primary supportive conclusion requires the gate under the definite-cell analysis **and every joint completion**; no independent per-cell extrema that cannot coexist are combined. Report the attainable lower/upper envelope and the completion attaining each extreme. If `U/N >5%` in either arm for either standard, or reviewer reconciliation exceeds 10%, endpoint validation is inconclusive regardless of envelopes.

The study-wide supportive claim is an intersection-union result: for **both `R_P` and `R_I`**, in **both arms**, PPV, sensitivity, specificity, and NPV must pass accuracy and symmetry, their information gates, joint-completion robustness, and data-quality/transport gates. No multiplicity-adjusted efficacy claim is made: every required null must be rejected, and one failure prevents global support. Report site/unit/pump-system/configuration estimates; no configuration may have >25% of its eligible primary rows unrepresented by valid synchronized capture, >10% absolute indeterminacy, or a 95% CI for PPV or sensitivity wholly below 0.70. Also report leave-one-site-out estimates; these diagnose transport and cannot override the pooled gate.

### Locked monitoring, futility, and stopping

A blinded data-quality monitor sees only counts by arm/site/configuration: screened, eligible, enrolled first patients, recurrent episodes, export/clock missingness, unresolved mappings, adjudication completion, and indeterminate/reconciliation counts. It never sees E/R accuracy cells. Pause a site immediately for clock run-in failure, pump export completeness <90% over any rolling seven-day period, >5 percentage-point differential missingness by arm, evidence capture alters medication delivery, or governance/safety concern. Rows already eligible remain in the denominator and are classified or indeterminate.

An independent accuracy monitor alone sees pooled labels at fixed information looks after **100, 200, and 300 definite EHR-positive denominators in an arm/reference standard**. Use nonbinding, adverse-only exact futility: stop accrual and declare that standard/arm adverse only if a one-sided **99.5% Clopper-Pearson upper bound** for PPV is <0.70. This spends at most 0.5% per look and, by Bonferroni across three looks, at most 1.5% under any `PPV>=0.70`. There is no early symmetry declaration, no favorable stopping, no target revision, and no futility based on a raw point estimate. The final one-sided 95% rules remain unchanged; early stopping cannot create support.

Stop the entire study as operationally infeasible if the 48-month window ends before both 3,000-patient arm quotas, or if governance determines synchronized full verification cannot be maintained. This is inconclusive, not equivalence or adverse accuracy. Stop a standard as adverse at a planned futility look only under its locked boundary. Continue collecting the other standard only if ethically and operationally justified and prespecified; it cannot rescue the failed standard. The executed information calculation shows that 3,000 patients yields at least 400 events with probability <4e-9 at a 10% event rate, 0.014 at 12%, 0.996 at 15%, and essentially 1 at 20%; inherited EHR restart rates (46/84 NE and 13/53 VP) imply high EHR-positive information but are explicitly not pump-native or intent-qualified prevalence estimates.

### Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

* **Supportive for pump-native delivery:** all `R_P` gates pass. This supports use of the locked EHR flag to measure resumed pump delivery in the enrolled configuration mixture only.
* **Supportive for intent-qualified rescue:** all gates pass for both `R_P` and `R_I`. This supports carrying the exact locked restart component into a separately governed future study, conditional on equivalent capture and population. It does not validate the other inherited composite components.
* **Adverse:** any final arm/standard accuracy upper bound <0.70; a 95% arm-difference CI wholly outside +/-0.10; planned adverse futility; material configuration-specific failure; or pump-only success with intent-qualified failure. This falsifies the corresponding raw restart use and requires pump/intent-native ascertainment or endpoint redesign.
* **Inconclusive:** fewer than 400 definite observations for any required denominator; failure to accrue both fixed arm quotas; excessive indeterminacy/reconciliation; incomplete or differential export; unresolved clocks/mappings; accuracy or symmetry intervals crossing their decision boundaries; or incompatible site workflows. Inconclusive cannot be repaired by extending N, pooling arms, deleting first patients, substituting recurrent episodes, changing thresholds, or retrospective relabeling.

A finding may be adverse for one standard and supportive for the other; report both without collapsing them. Poor sensitivity is adverse even with high PPV because a future arm difference can be distorted by missed rescues. Low PPV is adverse even with high sensitivity because restart dominates the inherited failures.
