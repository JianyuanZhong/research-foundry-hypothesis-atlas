# A bounded preoperative review queue for M2 triage under missing or stale information

## Clinical decision, supported evidence, and unresolved claim

The decision remains whether an adult undergoing apparently resectable HCC surgery should be automatically flagged for multidisciplinary review because pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2) may be present. The flag or review referral is not a recommendation for wider resection, neoadjuvant therapy, adjuvant therapy, or any other treatment.

The selected parent already addresses whether report-derived features and routine laboratories add net benefit to a fixed M2 flag at `p_t=0.30`, with anchored pathology parsing, an all-target-frame denominator, and worst-case bounds for ungradable pathology. This proposal retains that exact endpoint and design and adds a distinct decision hypothesis: whether timestamp-eligible content before `t_0` can support abstention and route uncertain or incomplete cases to scarce expert review.

**Strongest supported claim.** The parent’s inspected evidence supports that M2 is a pathology-defined high-risk phenotype. A current full-text systematic review of 52 imaging-based MVI studies reported less consistent performance in independent external validation than internal validation (SROC 0.85 versus 0.90) and concluded that clinical applicability still requires rigorous prospective multicenter evaluation (Feng et al., 2026, DOI `10.2196/82000`, frozen full-text XML source `[source checksum]`). This motivates transport validation; it does not support this model or policy. The HCC source audit supports an observable acquisition/test chronology for examinations and laboratories, but not decision-time report availability. It also shows that pathology and clinical documents have no timestamp, while vitals, transfers, and front-page contain identifiers only.

**Untested claim.** In a locked 2020–2021 target frame, can a fixed selective policy that sends incomplete/stale records or borderline predictions to review cover at least 80% of pathology-defined M2 cases while keeping the review queue at or below 25% of patients, and reduce automatic false-positive exposure by at least 0.02 per target-frame patient relative to forced `p_t=0.30` prediction? This is a transportability and workflow claim, not a claim that a reviewer will improve treatment or safety.

**Primary hypothesis.** Compared with forced E-model flagging at `p_t=0.30`, S will meet all three targets: the bootstrap lower 95% limit for the lower bound of M2 triage coverage is at least 0.80; the bootstrap upper 95% limit for review fraction is at most 0.25; and the bootstrap lower 95% limit for the lower bound of reduced automatic non-M2 flags is at least 0.02 per target-frame patient. These are prespecified evaluative targets, not established stakeholder preferences.

The substantive advance is a decision rule that explicitly acknowledges an information boundary and finite review workload. It does not replace the parent’s primary net-benefit hypothesis, and it does not convert missing pathology into a negative label.

## Population, joins, and frozen clocks

Use exactly the parent population and periods:

1. One patient (`Patient master index`) and one first eligible index partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resection, identified from `procedures` using a clinically reviewed exact `Surgery` dictionary. Exclude transplant, biopsy, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and repeat/recurrence resection. Emit all included and excluded strings and frequencies.
2. Require same-encounter pathology that explicitly identifies HCC, but do not require MVI grade for eligibility. Join same-encounter tables on (`Patient Master Index`, `Encounter Number`) after exact-row deduplication and report multiplicity. Longitudinal history uses `Patient Master Index`.
3. Define `t_0 = procedures.start time - 24 hours`. Require an eligible CT or MRI examination in `[t_0-90 days, t_0)`, using `examinations.examination`, `findings`, `diagnosis`, and `start time`. Use laboratories in `[t_0-30 days, t_0)`, using `labs.test`, `quantitative result`, `qualitative result`, `specimen type`, and `test time`. No predictor after `t_0`.
4. The primary cohort excludes HCC-directed resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `procedures`, `medications`, or `orders` during `[t_0-365 days, t_0)`. Previously treated patients are a sensitivity cohort with history indicators.
5. Development is 2015–2018, tuning/recalibration is 2019, and the locked target frame is 2020–2021. Years 2022–2025 are drift/ascertainment audit only unless the parent’s pathology-recovery gate is met. Preprocessing, dictionaries, imputation, vocabulary, thresholds, and policy rules are frozen before target-frame access.
6. Every target-frame patient receives an action and a model prediction, including `V=0`. Pathology is never a predictor.

## Pathology-only endpoint and uncertainty

Use the parent’s endpoint without modification: `Y=1` is adjudicated pathology-defined M2; `Y=0` is M0 or M1. `V=1` means a trustworthy grade is recoverable and `V=0` means it is not.

The parser may assign a grade only from the immediate selected value after an anchored field such as `MVI risk grade:`, stopping before definitions or explanatory bullets, or from a synonymous selected-value construction approved during blinded adjudication. Unanchored boilerplate M2, binary positive/ungraded MVI, blank vessel-count/distance fields, absent MVI language, and discordant fields cannot assign Y. The two-reviewer adjudication sample, third-reviewer conflict resolution, PPV/sensitivity/kappa gates, and the parent’s rule that inadequate tissue information remains (V=0) are retained. Raw slides, sampling protocol, pathologist identity, and true pathology report time are unavailable.

Before fitting, two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and output, independently review every parser-assigned M2 and every conflict; random samples of at least 150 parser M0 and 150 parser M1 stratified by year/template; every `V=0` patient in the 2020–2021 target frame; and at least 100 `V=0` records per year in 2022–2025. A third reviewer resolves disagreement. Require class-specific PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95, and kappa at least 0.90. Two qualified imaging reviewers also retain the parent’s audit of 200 reports for size/count/macrovascular thrombus and 100 reports per period for timestamp-eligibility: size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in at least 90%, count-category agreement at least 90% with kappa at least 0.80, and PPV/sensitivity at least 0.85 for each added binary feature.

For every action metric, compute exact results among `V=1` and sharp lower/upper bounds over `V=0`. The denominator is the entire target frame. Use 2,000 patient-level bootstrap resamples of the entire target frame; do not use complete-case analysis as the primary result or inverse-probability weighting as if it recovered unknown outcomes.

## Observable information-availability proxy

Define `A=1` only when all of the following are true before `t_0`:

- at least one eligible CT/MRI row has nonempty `findings` or `diagnosis` and `start time` in `[t_0-90 days, t_0)`;
- at least three of the four baseline exact-assay laboratory domains—albumin (`Albumin`), total bilirubin (`Total bilirubin`), INR (`International normalized ratio`), and platelets (`Platelet count`)—have a result in `[t_0-30 days, t_0)`;
- no selected field is taken from pathology, `clinical_documents`, or any post-`t_0` row.

Record the number and age of eligible imaging/lab rows, report nonempty status, and missingness separately. Use freshness bins (0–7, 8–30, 31–90 days) and year/template strata. Laboratory units are absent from the source; never pool assay names or impose cross-assay cutoffs. `A` must be called “timestamp-eligible information present,” never “report available at the decision.”

## Models and selective policy

Fit the parent’s nested elastic-net models B and E with exactly the parent’s preprocessing and feature exclusions:

- B: age, sex, latest eligible CT/MRI size/count/modality/macrovascular thrombus with unknown indicators, and latest eligible albumin, total bilirubin, INR, and platelets with missing indicators.
- E: B plus rule-extracted capsule, irregular margin, satellite/peritumoral features, development-only character 3–5-gram TF-IDF from `examinations.Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis`, and AFP/ALT/AST/GGT/ALP/creatinine with missing indicators.

Strip identifiers, dates, accession numbers, pathology/specimen language, explicit MVI terms, and copied pathology text. Examination reports labelled pathology/specimen are ineligible. Audit the largest extension terms before outcome unblinding.

The forced benchmark F is E risk at least 0.30, exactly as in the parent. The selective policy S is frozen before the 2020–2021 test:

- route to review if `A=0`, or if `A=1` and E risk is in `[0.20, 0.40)`;
- automatically flag if `A=1` and E risk is at least 0.40;
- automatically do not flag if `A=1` and E risk is below 0.20.

This is a transparent abstention rule, not a new complex model. Report the actual review fraction; the primary feasibility gate is at most 0.25. The values 0.20, 0.40, 80% coverage, and 25% workload are evaluative workflow targets, not known stakeholder preferences; they require prospective stakeholder review before use. The locked test thresholds are not re-tuned to force a desired workload. Report sensitivity at review-fraction bands 0.10, 0.20, and 0.25 only as descriptive operating curves, not as post hoc primary thresholds.

## Estimands and analysis

Let `T_i=1` when S assigns either automatic flag or review, `Q_i=1` when S assigns an automatic flag, and `F_i=1` when forced E-model risk is at least 0.30. The primary estimand is retrospective M2 triage coverage, `C_S = P(T=1 | Y=1)`. “Review” is an assigned action, not evidence that review happened.

The sharp outcome-missingness bounds are explicit. Let `P_obs` be the number of observed M2 cases, `TP_obs` the observed M2 cases with `T=1`, `U_T` the ungradable cases with `T=1`, and `U_notT` the ungradable cases with `T=0`. Then:

- lower coverage = `TP_obs / (P_obs + U_notT)`;
- upper coverage = `(TP_obs + U_T) / (P_obs + U_T)`.

Undefined zero-denominator bootstrap replicates are retained and reported, not silently dropped. Compute the same bounds for F and for the prespecified baselines below.

The paired reduction in automatic non-M2 flags is
`D_FP = sum[I(Y=0) * (F_i - Q_i)] / N`,
where `N` is the entire target frame. Because S automatic flags are a subset of F flags, each contribution is nonnegative. For `V=0`, the lower bound assigns unknown cases `Y=1` and the upper bound assigns them `Y=0`. Report the review fraction `P(R=1)`, bounded M2 enrichment in review, and all metrics by year, sex, age group, modality, AFP missingness, A status, and imaging/lab freshness.

Required baselines are: B forced at 0.30; E forced at 0.30 (F); flag all; flag none; missingness-only routing (review when `A=0`, otherwise use F); and uncertainty-only routing (review for E risk in `[0.20,0.40)`, otherwise use F). These component baselines test whether S gains from combining information completeness with model uncertainty rather than merely enlarging a queue.

The secondary decision-utility analysis retains the parent’s `p_t=0.30`, `w=3/7`, and whole-frame net-benefit arithmetic. A review action has no observed clinical outcome. Therefore calculate only scenario analyses:
`NB_S = [auto TP - w*auto FP + s_R*review M2 - w*(1-sp_R)*review non-M2]/N - c_R*review/N`,
over reviewer sensitivity `s_R in {0,0.5,0.8,1}`, specificity `sp_R in {0.5,0.8,0.95,1}`, and review cost `c_R in {0,0.01,0.05}` net-benefit units, with exact V-missingness bounds at each grid point. Oracle review is an optimistic scenario, not an observed effect; a no-benefit review scenario is conservative. Retain the parent’s fixed-threshold E-versus-B delta-NB as a benchmark. Do not choose a favorable threshold or reviewer scenario after test access.

Use 2,000 patient-level bootstrap resamples of the entire target frame for coverage bounds, paired differences, and D_FP. Report point bounds and percentile 95% intervals for each bound separately. Complete-case estimates are secondary and selection-sensitive; inverse-probability weighting cannot recover unknown pathology truth.

## Feasibility and validity gates

The test is interpretable only if all inherited parent gates remain satisfied:

- final 2020–2021 target frame has at least 1,000 patients, at least 75 adjudicated M2 cases, and `V=1` for at least 85%; every test `V=0` record is reviewed;
- parser and imaging adjudication pass the thresholds above;
- B, E, F, and S produce aligned outputs for every target-frame patient, and no test-informed preprocessing, pathology feature, untimed document, or post-`t_0` row is present;
- eligible imaging report text is nonempty in at least 80%, structured size/count are jointly extractable in at least 70%, each B laboratory is present in at least 70%, and no required variable changes missingness by more than 15 percentage points from development to test;
- inherited vocabulary/support-overlap checks pass; and
- among `V=1`, E has calibration slope 0.80–1.20, intercept -0.10–0.10, sensitivity at least 0.70, and PPV at least 0.50 at 0.30, with uncertainty reported.

Failure of a validity gate makes the action-policy result inconclusive. Exceeding the prespecified 25% review workload is an adverse test of transportability only when all validity gates pass.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** all parent pathology, imaging, event, and recoverability gates pass; the bootstrap lower 95% limit for the lower bound of `C_S` is at least 0.80; the bootstrap upper 95% limit for review fraction is at most 0.25; and the bootstrap lower 95% limit for the lower bound of `D_FP` is at least 0.02. This supports a retrospective, institution-specific workload/coverage claim: the timestamp-eligible proxy can prioritize cases for review while covering most adjudicated M2 cases. It does not show that a reviewer received the report, changed care, or improved safety.

**Adverse:** all validity gates pass, but the bootstrap upper 95% limit for the upper bound of `C_S` is below 0.80, the bootstrap lower 95% limit for review fraction exceeds 0.25, or the bootstrap upper 95% limit for the upper bound of `D_FP` is below 0.02. Any one falsifies a component of the prespecified triage target in the tested era. It does not prove that another policy or a prospective workflow would fail.

**Inconclusive:** a validity gate fails; pathology recovery or event count is inadequate; `V=0` bounds or bootstrap uncertainty span a target; or results lie between the supportive and adverse zones. Drift that raises review workload is adverse only when its bootstrap lower limit exceeds 0.25 and all validity gates pass. Inconclusive is not evidence of no clinical value.

**Contemporary drift:** for 2022–2025, report A prevalence, freshness, report/template distribution, E flag/review rates, and parser recoverability. Do not claim current utility unless the parent’s three-tier outcome recovery gate is met. A missing report-finalization timestamp remains a transportability limitation even if retrospective coverage is favorable.
