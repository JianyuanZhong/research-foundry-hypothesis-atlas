# All-randomized validation of a vasopressor strategy-failure endpoint by exact risk-difference distortion

## Decision, strongest supported evidence, and unresolved claim

The consequential future decision is whether a locked EHR endpoint can represent the randomized risk difference in failure of a norepinephrine-first (NE-first) versus vasopressin-first (VP-first) discontinuation strategy without enough differential measurement error to reverse a 0.05 absolute noninferiority decision. The parent correctly anchored validation to randomized assignment, but then calculated accuracy only among patients who had a post-randomization qualifying cessation. That selected population is neither an intention-to-treat (ITT) population nor protected by randomization: assignment can itself affect whether cessation is implemented, crossed over, simultaneous, pre-empted by death, or followed by ICU exit. This child does not call an adherer subset ITT.

The validation target is therefore changed to a clinically explicit **all-randomized composite endpoint**, while preserving the parent's exact decision-distortion concept:

> Among every first eligible patient randomized to NE-first or VP-first, can a locked EHR reconstruction classify the same all-randomized strategy-failure endpoint as pump-native delivery, vital-status/ICU-boundary data, and blinded intent adjudication, with a simultaneous 95% upper bound on absolute EHR-versus-reference risk-difference distortion strictly below 0.025 over true arm risks 0.10–0.40 and true VP-first minus NE-first risk differences from -0.10 to +0.10?

The strongest evidence already supported remains much narrower. MIMIC shows that the retrospective endpoint is computable, sparse, restart-dominated, and transaction-rule-sensitive; it cannot show randomized effects, physical delivery, cessation or rescue intent, reliable absence of mechanical circulatory support (MCS), or prospective interface validity. The unresolved experiment tests measurement agreement for one prespecified all-randomized composite. A supportive validation would not establish efficacy, safety, equipoise, treatment benefit, noninferiority, or that the composite's treatment-policy handling is clinically acceptable. Before first randomization, an independent critical-care, trial-methods, pharmacy/device, and patient panel must ratify the composite dispositions, the risk region, and the 0.05 clinical margin. Replacing the composite with a while-on-treatment, hypothetical, principal-stratum, actual-stop, or qualifying-cessation-only estimand requires a new validation study.

## Frozen inherited findings and evidence limits

All inherited negative findings remain frozen rather than being reinterpreted as support. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible MIMIC landmarks, observational actions were only 211 NE-first (3.49%) and 167 VP-first (2.76%), both below the 5% positivity gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort contains 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate composite failures. These strata are neither randomized nor exchangeable and cannot support a treatment comparison. Of 73 failures among 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models produced negligible Brier improvement (approximately 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, both below 0.01) and adverse later transport; no score or subgroup advances.

The complete input-event audit used all 137 inherited episodes and every row retained after declared stay, item, and time filters. It reproduced all boundaries and 59 raw restarts. Five symmetric reconstruction rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first and 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries. These are label-instability findings, not pump truth. `statusdescription`, transaction chain, dose, caregiver, `storetime`, open-bag/transfer fields, and intermittent MAP cannot prove physical delivery, true cessation, rescue intent, false positives, or MCS absence. Fixed temporal separation, first-patient primary units, full positive and negative verification, recurrence separation, clock escrow, independent intent adjudication, configuration versioning, and the noncausal boundary are retained.

## Population, randomization, and temporal boundaries

At least six adult ICUs and two pump–EHR interface families participate. Each exact configuration is the tuple of site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, and clock-map method/version. Each configuration must first pass a new 50-episode technical run-in: at least 95% must have unique patient–pump–channel–drug linkage, escrowed clock maps, and complete pump export from two hours before the candidate assignment through the endpoint horizon or earlier closure; at least 90% must have contemporaneous evidence suitable for intent adjudication. After infrastructure repair, one repeat run-in of 50 wholly new episodes is allowed. A second failure makes that configuration feasibility-only. Run-in patients are excluded from calibration and confirmation.

Eligibility is frozen before randomization and cannot depend on subsequent capture, implementation, actual first-stopped drug, rescue, outcome, or adherence:

1. age at least 18 and alive in an adult ICU;
2. linked pump-delivered NE and VP concurrently for at least six continuous hours;
3. at the start of the final 30 pre-randomization minutes, NE greater than 0 and no more than 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP greater than 0 and no more than 0.04 U/min, with neither increased during those 30 minutes;
4. no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours;
5. at least three valid MAPs spanning at least three of four prior 30-minute bins, including the final bin, with median MAP at least 65 and minimum MAP at least 55 mmHg; and
6. the treating team documents before assignment that either protocolized first-stop strategy is clinically permissible.

The first eligible randomization per person is the sole primary unit. `tr` is the immutable randomization time. Randomization is 1:1 in configuration-stratified permuted blocks, and all primary tables retain `Z=0` for assigned NE-first and `Z=1` for assigned VP-first. `A`, the drug actually stopped first, is post-randomization and is diagnostic only. The assignment must be initiated within 120 minutes, but the primary denominator contains every randomized patient whether or not this happens.

For a protocol-concordant qualifying cessation, `t0` is the first pump-native delivered-rate timestamp after `tr` and no later than `tr+120 min` at which the **assigned drug** falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below threshold continuously for five minutes while the comparator remains above its threshold. Programmed rate without linked delivery is insufficient. A nonassigned-drug cessation first is crossover, and crossing of both drugs within five minutes is simultaneous; neither creates a qualifying `t0` for the primary composite. A later apparent assigned-drug cessation cannot erase those failures.

For a qualifying cessation, the rescue horizon is `(t0, min[t0+6h, death, ICU exit]]`. For a patient without qualifying cessation, implementation status is assessed through `min[tr+120 min, death, ICU exit]`. Every source clock has a prospectively escrowed affine map recording offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving handling, fitting rows, residuals, and immutable version. The boundary band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds; a configuration needing a wider band fails qualification.

## The all-randomized reference and EHR labels

Each randomized patient receives one mutually exclusive reference disposition and one EHR disposition. The resulting binary labels `Y_R,Y_E in {0,1,?}` refer to the same composite and the same assigned strategy. They do not switch to the actually stopped drug.

### Reference label `Y_R`

`Y_R=1` (strategy failure) for the first applicable determinate condition:

1. **death before completion:** death after `tr` and on or before the applicable 120-minute implementation boundary or six-hour post-`t0` horizon, whether or not a cessation or rescue was already recorded;
2. **crossover:** the nonassigned drug is the first drug to meet the five-minute pump-native off definition within 120 minutes while the assigned drug remains above threshold;
3. **simultaneous cessation:** both drugs meet their off definitions within five minutes of one another within 120 minutes;
4. **nonimplementation:** no protocol-concordant qualifying cessation begins by `tr+120 min`, including clinician override, no drug stopped, aborted attempt, or alive ICU exit before a qualifying cessation; or
5. **qualifying-cessation rescue:** after protocol-concordant `t0`, linked delivered pump rate for the assigned drug again reaches its index threshold continuously for at least five minutes within the horizon and two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators conclude from contemporaneous timestamped evidence that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion.

`Y_R=0` only if a protocol-concordant qualifying cessation occurs within 120 minutes, there is no intent-qualified rescue or death before closure, and either (a) six complete hours are observed or (b) the patient exits the ICU alive after `t0` with complete pump, EHR, and vital-status capture through exit and the assigned drug remains off at exit. Thus alive ICU exit is an explicitly defined competing success **for this endpoint**, not censoring and not an assertion that no later ward rescue occurred. If the later trial instead seeks rescue through six clock hours regardless of location, it must collect post-ICU pump/medication truth and revalidate that different endpoint.

`Y_R=?` if no determinate earlier failure is present but linkage, export, clock mapping, vital status, boundary capture, or rescue intent is unresolved enough to change the binary disposition. Technical/procedural pause, bag or syringe replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, mistaken-stop correction, and uninterrupted delivery fragmented only in documentation are not rescue; if adjudicators cannot distinguish them from intent-qualified rescue, the label is `?`, not 0. Death is never treated as missing, and loss of capture is never silently treated as no event.

### EHR label `Y_E`

The locked EHR algorithm reconstructs the same assigned-strategy composite using only mapped EHR infusion intervals plus the trial's EHR-visible randomization record and EHR death/ICU-boundary feeds; it cannot use pump-delivered rates, adjudicated intent, transaction-repair fields, or `Y_R`.

For NE, local rates mapped to MIMIC item 221906 are normalized by retaining mcg/kg/min or dividing mcg/min by a contemporaneous valid weight of 30–300 kg; rates above 5 mcg/kg/min are rejected. For VP, local rates mapped to item 222315 retain U/min or divide U/hour by 60; rates above 0.2 U/min are rejected. A positive EHR interval requires `endtime>starttime`. Chain, status, `storetime`, caregiver, open-bag, transfer, and order fields are recorded diagnostics but cannot repair the primary intervals.

Using the same thresholds and five-minute persistence rules on EHR intervals, `Y_E=1` if the EHR reconstruction shows any of: death by the applicable closure; nonassigned drug first; both drugs crossing within five minutes; no assigned-drug qualifying cessation by 120 minutes; or, following an EHR-qualified assigned-drug cessation, a new positive EHR interval for the assigned drug with mapped `starttime` in `(t0_E,t0_E+6h]` before earlier death/ICU exit. A row already spanning `t0_E` is not a new restart. `Y_E=0` requires EHR-qualified assigned cessation within 120 minutes, no reconstructed restart or death, and complete EHR capture to six hours or alive ICU exit with the assigned drug still off. `Y_E=?` if mapped timestamp uncertainty crosses an ordering, five-minute, 120-minute, restart, death, or exit boundary, or EHR completeness is insufficient to distinguish 0 from 1.

This EHR composite deliberately treats every determinate implementation deviation as endpoint-positive. It is not a pure rescue label. Component-level confusion tables are mandatory so apparent aggregate agreement cannot hide opposite errors in nonimplementation and rescue. The parent pump-only resumption and actual-stopped-drug restart labels remain secondary interface diagnostics, not substitutes for `Y_R`.

There is no post-randomization exclusion and no ITT claim for a selected qualifying-cessation subset. Component-specific analyses among patients with `t0`, by `A`, by adherence, or by `Z×A` are explicitly descriptive estimands.

## Decision-distortion estimand and exact simultaneous inference

For randomized arm `z`, define

`Se_z=P(Y_E=1 | Y_R=1,Z=z)` and `Sp_z=P(Y_E=0 | Y_R=0,Z=z)`.

For a hypothetical all-randomized true strategy-failure rate `p_z`, the EHR rate is

`q_z=Se_z p_z + (1-Sp_z)(1-p_z)`.

For `Delta_R=p_1-p_0`, `Delta_E=q_1-q_0`, and `theta=(Se_0,Sp_0,Se_1,Sp_1)`, measurement distortion is exactly

`b(theta,p)=Delta_E-Delta_R`.

The clinically ratified region remains

`Omega={(p_0,p_1): 0.10<=p_z<=0.40, |p_1-p_0|<=0.10}`,

with vertices `(0.10,0.10),(0.10,0.20),(0.20,0.10),(0.30,0.40),(0.40,0.30),(0.40,0.40)`. These are design assumptions, not MIMIC prevalence estimates.

For each arm, construct two-sided Clopper–Pearson intervals for sensitivity and specificity with `alpha=0.0125` for each of the four intervals. Their Cartesian product has Bonferroni simultaneous coverage at least 95%. Because distortion is affine in each parameter and in `(p_0,p_1)`,

`B_U=max_{theta in C,p in Omega}|b(theta,p)|`

is attained at one of 16 parameter corners and six region vertices. Enumerate all 96 combinations and retain the signed maximizing witness. The primary gate is strict: `B_U<0.025`; equality fails. PPV, NPV, EHR positivity, reference prevalence, component-specific accuracy, McNemar discordance, and timing are descriptive and cannot override this gate.

### Exact scalable aggregate-count partial identification

Every patient appears in a `Z x Y_E x Y_R` 3x3 table. No person-level `2^m` enumeration is allowed. For each arm, compress unknown labels into the nine aggregate counts `n00,n01,n10,n11,n?0,n?1,n0?,n1?,n??`. A completed 2x2 table is represented only by `(TN,FN,FP,TP)`.

The exact feasible support is the support of the following integer generating product:

`TN^n00 FN^n01 FP^n10 TP^n11`

multiplied by

* `sum_j TN^(n?0-j) FP^j` for `Y_E=?,Y_R=0`;
* `sum_j FN^(n?1-j) TP^j` for `Y_E=?,Y_R=1`;
* `sum_j TN^(n0?-j) FN^j` for `Y_E=0,Y_R=?`;
* `sum_j FP^(n1?-j) TP^j` for `Y_E=1,Y_R=?`; and
* `sum_{a+b+c+d=n??} TN^a FN^b FP^c TP^d` for `Y_E=?,Y_R=?`.

The compiler implements this as sparse bounded-knapsack convolution over **counts**, merges identical `(TN,FN,FP,TP)` states after every factor, and stores three coordinates because total `N_z` fixes the fourth. With the operational indeterminacy ceiling of 5%, complexity is polynomial in the number of unknown records and unique aggregate states rather than exponential in persons. If a sparse support exceeds the escrowed memory limit, an exact integer branch-and-bound uses the five aggregate allocation blocks as variables; node bounds use Clopper–Pearson monotonicity over attainable success/denominator ranges. A solver timeout is computationally inconclusive, never a pass.

For speed and an independently checkable formulation, fixed region vertex and sign `s in {-1,+1}` make `s*b` separable by arm. In each completed arm table, coefficient signs select the appropriate lower or upper Clopper–Pearson endpoint for sensitivity and specificity. Optimize the arm-specific term over its reachable aggregate support, combine the two exact arm optima plus the known constant, and maximize over 12 vertex/sign cases. This returns the identical joint worst-case `B_U` without taking a Cartesian product of patient completions or arm supports. The executable supporting script supplies the aggregate-count oracle, the generating allocations, and small-table tests through eight fully unknown records; the compiler must test its sparse-DP and branch-and-bound answers against that oracle for every synthetic table with 0–8 indeterminate records per arm and against direct person-level enumeration only in those tiny tests. Production analysis never enumerates persons.

Overall indeterminacy above 5%, above 7.5% in an assignment-by-configuration cell containing at least 20 patients, or an arm difference above 5 percentage points is operationally adverse and precludes support even if the numerical worst-case bound passes. The exact aggregate completion bound is primary. A Jeffreys/posterior missingness sensitivity analysis at missingness odds ratios 0.5, 1, 2, and 5 remains secondary and cannot rescue failure.

## Executable immutable sample-size rule

After run-in, 1,200 consecutive all-randomized first patients form a calibration/reliability phase and are excluded from confirmatory accuracy. Calibration can detect adjudication, clock, capture, component-prevalence, or accrual infeasibility, but it cannot change confirmatory `N`, the distortion region, the endpoint, thresholds, or the acceptance gate. A firewall statistician sees assignment, configuration, calendar accrual, disposition component, whether `Y_R` is determinate, and first/recurrent status, but no `Y_E`, `Y_E x Y_R` error table, accuracy, or narrative.

The confirmatory sample is immutably fixed at **12,532 randomized first patients: 6,266 per assignment arm**. The supporting executable uses no simulation and applies this exact conservative algorithm:

1. Fix the planning alternative at determinate `Se_0=Se_1=0.99`, `Sp_0=Sp_1=0.995`; this is a planning alternative, not an observed-data acceptance threshold.
2. For candidate per-arm size `n`, use exact binomial quantiles with tail `0.0125` to form a reference-positive count envelope uniform over `p_z in [0.10,0.40]`: the lower count has `P_{p=0.10}(R_+<L)<=0.0125`, and the upper count has `P_{p=0.40}(R_+>U)<=0.0125`. Binomial stochastic ordering makes this valid for every prevalence in the interval.
3. At every integer `r in [L,U]`, compute exact `0.0125` upper-tail thresholds for false negatives under `Binomial(r,0.01)` and false positives under `Binomial(n-r,0.005)`. Convert their worst cases to the same two-sided Clopper–Pearson limits used at analysis.
4. Take the rectangular lower envelope of sensitivity and specificity across every `r`, then evaluate all 16 parameter corners and six `Omega` vertices. The eight tail events are lower/upper reference-count events in two arms plus false-negative and false-positive events in two arms; Bonferroni therefore guarantees simultaneous probability at least `1-8(0.0125)=0.90` that the resulting confirmatory confidence rectangle is no wider than this planning envelope.
5. Select the smallest integer `n` whose envelope gives strict `B_U<0.025`, and explicitly evaluate `n-1` to prove minimality under this locked rule.

Execution gives `n=6,266` per arm, total `12,532`; the reference-positive envelope is 574–2,593 per arm, the worst sensitivity lower limit is 0.9590882625, the worst specificity lower limit is 0.9876640004, and the resulting upper distortion envelope is 0.0249998947. At `n=6,265`, it is 0.0250022411 and fails. Thus the integer rule, constants, quantile conventions, strict inequality, and adjacent-integer check are immutable. This is an exact binomial-tail **conservative assurance lower bound**, not a Monte Carlo power estimate and not a claim that observed prevalence, determinacy, operations, or accuracy will meet the alternative.

Confirmation enrolls exactly 12,532 first randomized patients with one common start and lock, or closes at 48 months. There is no denominator-triggered, arm-specific, component-specific, success, or unblinded-accuracy stopping and no post-lock extension. Total first patients can reach 13,732 including calibration, plus fully verified recurrent episodes. A calibration projection beyond 48 months, inability to sustain six sites/two families, or failure of clinical governance makes confirmation infeasible before launch. Mathematical impossibility, infrastructure failure, inadequate adjudication, excessive indeterminacy, or failure to reach the immutable lock is inconclusive rather than adverse endpoint evidence.

It records all constants, the chosen and previous integer, exact witnesses, and aggregate-completion unit tests. The parent's illustrative Monte Carlo is retained only as historical evidence that small generic-accuracy denominators were inadequate; it is not used for this design.

## Adjudication, crossover diagnostics, recurrence, and transport

Two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators review pump/monitor traces, workflow events, cointerventions, and contemporaneous intent evidence but not `Y_E`, transaction diagnostics, assignment-specific accuracy, or each other's labels. A third blinded adjudicator resolves disagreement. After the first 100 new calibration patients, raw agreement below 85% or both Gwet AC1 and kappa below 0.70 suspends accrual. Clarification cannot inspect `Y_E x Y_R`; reliability restarts on 100 wholly new patients, with earlier patients feasibility-only. A second failure is operationally inconclusive.

Report component incidence by `Z`: qualifying cessation, nonimplementation reason, crossover, simultaneous cessation, death, alive exit, intent-qualified rescue, and indeterminate status. Report `A`, adherence, time to attempt, crossover reason, and `Z×A` accuracy only as post-randomization diagnostics. A Holm-significant assignment-by-component accuracy interaction with absolute difference above 0.10, cancellation of component errors that lets the global composite pass while any common component (at least 100 events per arm) has sensitivity below 0.80, or an above-0.025 increase in distortion under adversarial adherence-indeterminate allocation is adverse. None changes the all-randomized primary denominator.

All later qualifying randomizations/episodes are collected under identical capture and disposition rules but cannot increase primary denominators. A recurrent opportunity needs a new six-hour dual-drug baseline and must begin at least 24 hours after the previous `t0` or randomization if no `t0`; closer transitions remain one episode. Retain original `Z` unless a separately governed re-randomization occurs before the new eligibility lock. Report episodes per patient, sequence, interval, arm switching, component rates, sensitivity, specificity, and distortion using patient-cluster bootstrap stratified by `Z` and configuration (at least 10,000 samples) and diagnostic marginal GEE. A recurrent bound above 0.05 or more than 0.025 worse than the first-patient bound is adverse for repeated use but cannot alter the primary result.

A transportable claim requires at least six sites and two interface families. No site, configuration, or family may contribute above 30% of either arm's reference-positive or reference-negative denominator. Each arm/reference-status denominator must be at least 100 in four configurations and 200 in three. Report exact configuration/family tables, hierarchical binomial estimates, prediction intervals, and leave-one-site/configuration/family-out exact aggregate-completion bounds. Every leave-out mixture must retain `B_U<0.025`. For a configuration with at least 100 reference-positive and 500 reference-negative patients per arm, its conservative bound must be below 0.05. Sparse configurations are inconclusive, not homogeneous. A prediction interval implying sensitivity below 0.90 or specificity below 0.98, adjusted configuration accuracy range above 0.10, or material assignment-by-configuration interaction is adverse. Support applies only to enumerated locked configurations.

A configuration stops after linkage, clock, or export failure in above 5% of its latest 50 episodes and undergoes a new run-in. It cannot re-enter the confirmatory mixture after repair; later rows are feasibility-only. Version changes create new configurations. Inability to maintain four active sites is operationally inconclusive.

## Verifier requirements and falsification

The verifier must reproduce eligibility before randomization; exactly one primary unit per person; block balance; retention of every randomized patient under `Z`; deterministic classification of nonimplementation, crossover, simultaneous cessation, death, alive exit, qualifying cessation, and rescue; independent `Y_E` and `Y_R` clocks; full positive/negative verification; the complete 3x3 tables; aggregate-count feasible supports; all Clopper–Pearson intervals; all vertex/sign or 96 corner/vertex evaluations; the maximizing completion and distortion witness; the adjacent-integer sample-size result; common lock; recurrence; matching; concentration; and leave-out gates.

Required adversarial synthetic cases include:

1. Excluding nonimplementers or requiring `t0` produces a passing adherer analysis while the all-randomized composite fails: reject the selected analysis.
2. Identical actual-stop accuracy but differential crossover/nonimplementation reconstruction by `Z` gives `B_U>=0.025`: fail.
3. Crossover followed later by assigned cessation and no rescue: reference remains failure; later `t0` cannot overwrite it.
4. Simultaneous cessation reconstructed as single-drug cessation: count the appropriate composite error.
5. Death before `t0`, death after `t0`, alive exit before `t0`, and alive exit after `t0` must follow the frozen dispositions above, not censoring conventions chosen after outcomes.
6. High PPV and sensitivity but low specificity at `p=0.10`: fail if distortion fails.
7. Separate metric-wise imputations pass while one joint aggregate completion fails: report the joint failure and witness.
8. Aggregate sparse-DP/branch-and-bound results differ from the supplied small-table oracle or from tiny person-level exhaustive tests: invalidate computation.
9. A compiler changes `6266`, a quantile tail, strict inequality, endpoint component, or region based on calibration: invalidate the lock.
10. Timestamp exactly at `tr+120 min`, `t0`, five minutes, six hours, death, or exit; spanning/duplicate intervals; clock uncertainty crossing ordering: exercise `?` rules.
11. Missing EHR-negative patients, denominator-triggered truncation, arm-specific closeout, post-lock extension, omitted crossover, or configuration-version pooling: invalidate computation.
12. Global pooled pass with sparse configuration or leave-one-out reversal: no transport support.
13. Recurrent episodes counted as independent primary patients or used to rescue a primary failure: reject.
14. Correct adverse or inconclusive outputs described as validation, treatment benefit, safety, or NI: reject the conclusion.

Computational verification can establish source-row accounting, labels from supplied streams under the frozen rules, exact count optimization, confidence bounds, sample-size arithmetic, gates, and whether prose follows results. It cannot establish that linkage proves physical delivery, infer intent without expert adjudication, establish MCS absence, ratify the composite/margin/risk region, approve randomization, determine safety/equipoise, or establish causal treatment benefit.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive:** exactly 12,532 confirmatory patients complete; the worst joint aggregate completion gives `B_U<0.025`; indeterminacy, component, clock, adjudication, assignment/adherence, recurrence, and transport safeguards pass; and every required leave-out analysis passes. This supports only that, for the enumerated configurations and ratified all-randomized composite within `Omega`, measurement distortion can be bounded below half the 0.05 NI margin. In a later trial using exactly this endpoint, if the EHR-based upper confidence limit for `Delta_E` is below `0.05-B_U`, then `Delta_R<0.05` follows within the validated measurement model and region; if its lower limit is at least `0.05+B_U`, inferiority follows. The boundary zone is measurement-inconclusive. Validation alone never authorizes NI.

**Adverse:** completed data give `B_U>=0.025`; assignment-specific implementation or rescue error is consequential; crossover, simultaneous cessation, death/exit, or restart components show clinically important cancellation or poor accuracy; recurrent performance is materially worse; or configuration evidence shows harmful heterogeneity. This is valuable evidence that the EHR composite can distort a later randomized RD/NI decision. High pooled accuracy, PPV, actual-drug symmetry, or a favorable treatment estimate cannot override it.

**Inconclusive:** randomization is not clinically permissible; the panel does not ratify the all-randomized composite, risk region, or margin; projected/actual completion exceeds 48 months; the immutable sample does not complete; capture, clock, linkage, adjudication, indeterminacy, configuration, or reference operations fail; aggregate exact optimization does not finish; or the later trial uses a different population, implementation policy, clock, endpoint, handling of death/exit/crossover, or estimand. Inconclusive does not support endpoint validity, noninferiority, safety, or benefit.

The substantive advance is therefore honest and bounded: randomization can coherently validate a future randomized decision only by validating an endpoint defined for **all randomized patients**. It cannot borrow the language of ITT protection while selecting those who achieve a post-randomization cessation. Whether this explicit composite is itself the clinically right future trial outcome remains an untested governance and trial-design question.
