# Randomization-anchored validation of the EHR vasopressor-restart endpoint by exact risk-difference distortion

## The clinical decision and the evidence boundary

The inherited experiment correctly asks whether an EHR reconstruction of restart agrees with intent-qualified pump rescue, but its PPV/sensitivity >0.80 and ±0.10 symmetry gates do not answer the decision question. PPV changes with rescue prevalence, and even apparently similar accuracy can shift a future randomized risk difference enough to reverse a 5-percentage-point noninferiority conclusion. The successor therefore tests a stronger and directly falsifiable claim:

> In a prospectively randomized endpoint-validation cohort, the locked EHR restart label has assignment-arm- and configuration-specific sensitivity and specificity precise enough that, over prespecified true rescue rates of 0.10–0.40 per arm and true NE-first versus VP-first risk differences between -0.10 and +0.10, the simultaneous 95% upper bound on absolute EHR-versus-reference risk-difference distortion is strictly less than 0.025.

The 0.025 gate is half of a prospectively fixed 0.05 absolute noninferiority margin for the later rescue endpoint. The event-rate/effect region is a design region, not a MIMIC estimate: 0.10 is the lowest rate at which the inherited fixed-sample validation remains plausibly feasible, 0.40 covers a common-to-high rescue risk, and ±0.10 covers both the NI boundary and clinically adverse differences. Before the first randomized validation patient, an independent critical-care, trial, and patient panel must ratify that region and the 0.05 margin. Changing either is a new scientific design, not a compiler repair.

A supportive result says only that measurement error, under the validated configurations and population, can be bounded tightly enough to support a safeguarded future risk-difference analysis. It does not establish either stopping order's benefit, safety, noninferiority, equipoise, causal effect, or clinical importance. Those require a separately governed randomized trial. If clinical governance will not permit randomized stopping-order assignment, an observational actual-stopped-drug validation may still characterize interfaces, but it cannot pass this decision-anchored gate.

## Why randomized assignment, not actual stopped drug, is the primary validation stratum

Let `Z=0` denote randomized NE-first and `Z=1` randomized VP-first. Let `A` be the drug actually stopped first. In a future trial, the estimand and decision are indexed by `Z`; `A` is post-randomization and can differ from `Z` through crossover, simultaneous stopping, implementation delay, clinician override, or pump workflow. Because the EHR algorithm is drug- and workflow-specific, `Z` can affect measurement through `A` and through arm-specific documentation. Stratifying only on `A` conditions on post-randomization behavior, can mix the randomized groups differently, and can show symmetric accuracy even when the intention-to-treat arms have differential error. Conversely, a difference between NE-stopped and VP-stopped strata is not automatically treatment-dependent bias in the randomized contrast.

Therefore the primary 2×2 reference tables, sensitivity, specificity, and distortion map are indexed by randomized assignment `Z`, preserving assignment regardless of crossover. Randomization is 1:1 in permuted blocks stratified by configuration and first versus permitted re-randomization status. `A`, adherence to the assigned first stop, time from assignment to cessation, crossover reason, and `Z×A` cells are prespecified diagnostic variables. Sensitivity and specificity are also reported by `A` and by `Z×A`, but these post-assignment estimates cannot replace the `Z`-specific primary estimates. A Holm-significant `Z×A` interaction with an absolute accuracy difference >0.10, or a >0.025 increase in the distortion bound after assigning all adherence-indeterminate patients adversarially, is adverse evidence about treatment-dependent measurement.

The endpoint-validation cohort must be embedded in a clinically governed randomized stopping-order pilot or in the opening, firewalled validation phase of the later trial. Eligibility is assessed before randomization. The endpoint analysis population is all randomized patients in whom the protocol-defined qualifying cessation is attempted. Failure to implement the assigned cessation within 120 minutes, simultaneous cessation, or stopping the nonassigned drug first is never silently excluded: it is an implementation deviation. Primary measurement accuracy is computed for the first actual qualifying cessation while retaining `Z`; the trial must separately prespecify whether implementation failure is a clinical endpoint component. This validation cannot decide that estimand choice. A later trial using an all-randomized composite or a different clock needs corresponding validation rather than transporting these results by assertion.

Consecutive pre-randomization eligibility is unchanged:

1. age at least 18, alive in an adult ICU;

The episode `t0` remains pump-native: the first delivered-rate timestamp after `tr` when either study drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below for five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Both crossing within five minutes is simultaneous. Programmed rate without linked delivery is insufficient. Report whether the first-stopped drug equals assignment, all deviations, and patients lacking a qualifying `t0`; never replace `tr` or `t0` after observing rescue.

The observable window is `(t0, min[t0+6h, death, ICU exit]]`. Death/exit closes a determinate window only with complete pump and EHR capture through closure. A gap creates an indeterminate reference, not a negative. Each source clock has an escrowed affine pump-time map with offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving handling, fitting rows, residuals, and immutable version. Its boundary band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds; needing a wider band fails qualification.

The frozen EHR label `E` uses local fields mapped to MIMIC item 221906 for NE and 222315 for VP. NE mcg/min is divided by contemporaneous valid weight 30–300 kg; mcg/kg/min is retained; normalized rates >5 are rejected. VP units/hour is divided by 60; units/min is retained; normalized rates >0.2 are rejected. `E=1` when a new positive EHR interval for the actually stopped drug has `endtime>starttime` and mapped `starttime` in `(t0,t0+6h]` before earlier closure. A row spanning `t0` is not new. Chain, status, `storetime`, caregiver, open-bag, transfer, and order fields cannot repair the primary label. `E=?` if the prespecified 95% mapped timestamp uncertainty set crosses `t0`, six hours, or earlier closure.

The reference `R=1` requires (i) linked delivered pump rate for the stopped drug again reaches its index threshold continuously for at least five minutes after the qualifying off interval within the window, and (ii) two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators conclude from contemporaneous timestamped evidence that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion after cessation. Bag/syringe replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, technical/procedural pause, mistaken-stop correction, and uninterrupted delivery fragmented only in documentation are reference-negative. Adjudicators see pump/monitor traces, workflow events, cointerventions, and contemporaneous intent evidence but not `E`, transaction diagnostics, assignment-specific accuracy, or each other's labels. A third blinded adjudicator resolves disagreement. `R=?` covers clock/export/linkage failure or intent that remains indeterminate.

After 100 new calibration episodes, raw agreement <85% or both Gwet AC1 and kappa <0.70 suspends accrual. Manual clarification cannot inspect `E×R`; reliability restarts on 100 new patients, with old patients feasibility-only. A second failure is operationally inconclusive. Pump-only resumption is a secondary interface-fidelity reference and cannot replace intent-qualified rescue.

## Decision mapping and exact partial identification

For assignment arm `z`, define

`Se_z=P(E=1|R=1,Z=z)` and `Sp_z=P(E=0|R=0,Z=z)`.

PPV, NPV, reference prevalence, EHR positivity, McNemar discordance, and timing are descriptive because PPV/NPV depend on prevalence. For a hypothetical true rescue rate `p_z`, the mapped EHR rate is

`q_z = Se_z*p_z + (1-Sp_z)*(1-p_z)`.

For `Delta_R=p_1-p_0` and `Delta_E=q_1-q_0`, measurement distortion is

`b(theta,p)=Delta_E-Delta_R`, where `theta=(Se_0,Sp_0,Se_1,Sp_1)`.

The fixed clinical region is `Omega={(p_0,p_1): 0.10≤p_z≤0.40, |p_1-p_0|≤0.10}`. Its six vertices are (0.10,0.10), (0.10,0.20), (0.20,0.10), (0.30,0.40), (0.40,0.30), and (0.40,0.40).

For each assignment arm, construct exact two-sided Clopper–Pearson intervals for sensitivity and specificity at alpha 0.0125 each, yielding a Bonferroni simultaneous coverage of at least 95% for the four parameters. The primary uncertainty set `C` is their Cartesian product. Because `b` is multilinear in `theta` and affine in `(p_0,p_1)`,

`B_U = max_{theta in C, p in Omega} |b(theta,p)|`

is exactly attained at one of 16 parameter corners and six `Omega` vertices. The verifier enumerates all 96 combinations and records the maximizing witness. The primary endpoint-acceptability gate requires `B_U < 0.025`; equality fails. This is exact conservative mapping, not a fitted correction and not an assumption of nondifferential error.

Every eligible episode has `E,R in {0,1,?}` and appears in a full 3×3 table. For primary inference, enumerate every joint binary completion of all `?` labels consistent with determinate source data. Recompute all four exact intervals and `B_U`; the reported bound is the maximum over joint completions. Exact integer/corner optimization is permitted only after equality to exhaustive person-level enumeration is unit-tested for every small table with 0–8 indeterminate people. Overall indeterminacy >5%, >7.5% in any assignment-by-configuration cell with at least 20 patients, or a between-arm difference >5 percentage points is operationally adverse and precludes support regardless of the numerical worst-case gate.

A secondary probabilistic analysis places independent Jeffreys Beta(0.5,0.5) posteriors on each determinate arm/reference-status error probability, samples joint indeterminate completions under explicitly varied missingness odds ratios 0.5, 1, 2, and 5, and reports the posterior distribution of `B`, probability `B<0.025`, and probability of reversal at the +0.05 NI boundary. It is a sensitivity analysis only; it cannot rescue failure of exact partial identification.

### Consequence for a later trial

With higher rescue risk defined as worse and NI margin +0.05 for `Delta_R=VP-first minus NE-first`, validation with `B_U<0.025` supports a guarded decision rule. If the future EHR-based upper confidence limit for `Delta_E` is below `0.05-B_U`, then `Delta_R<0.05` follows within the validated measurement model and region. If its lower confidence limit is at least `0.05+B_U`, inferiority follows. Results in the boundary zone are measurement-inconclusive and require pump-reference analysis or more information. An EHR point estimate or interval crossing zero is not evidence of benefit, and validation alone never authorizes NI. Trial sampling uncertainty, missing outcomes, nonadherence, estimand validity, safety, multiplicity, and causal interpretation remain separate.

## Calibration, sample size, and immutable confirmatory lock

The inherited temporal separation is retained. After run-in, randomize and fully verify 1,200 consecutive first patients in calibration; they are excluded from confirmatory accuracy. A firewall statistician sees `Z`, configuration, calendar accrual, whether `R` is determinate positive/negative/indeterminate, and first/recurrent status, but no `E`, `E×R` table, error count, accuracy, or narrative.

Using calibration, form simultaneous Jeffreys lower bounds for the four total-population cell probabilities `P(Z=z,R=1)` and `P(Z=z,R=0)`, assigning `R=?` adversarially. Find the smallest even `Nconfirm` that has at least 0.90 joint binomial probability of obtaining at least 600 determinate R-positive and 3,000 determinate R-negative first patients in each assignment arm. Then run a prespecified exact binomial operating-characteristic calculation at the demanding design point `Se_0=Se_1=0.99`, `Sp_0=Sp_1=0.995`, over calibration-compatible reference prevalences, and increase `Nconfirm` until the probability that the simultaneous exact gate has `B_U<0.025` is at least 0.90. This design point is a planning alternative, not an acceptance threshold; lower observed performance simply falsifies acceptability. Freeze the larger N before any confirmatory patient. If no N≤15,000, six sites/two families are unavailable, or projected completion exceeds 48 months, confirmation is infeasible. At most one wholly new 1,200-patient calibration is allowed after documented configuration expansion.

## Recurrence and configurations

All later qualifying episodes are collected under the same pump, EHR, clock, adjudication, and disposition rules but cannot increase primary denominators. A recurrent episode requires a new six-hour dual-drug baseline and `t0` at least 24 hours after the previous `t0`; closer transitions stay within the episode. A patient is not automatically re-randomized: retain original `Z` unless a separately governed, prespecified re-randomization occurs before the new episode. Report episodes/patient, sequence, interval, arm switching, and first-versus-recurrent `Se`, `Sp`, and distortion. Use patient-cluster bootstrap stratified by `Z` and configuration (≥10,000 samples) and marginal GEE diagnostically. A recurrent bound >0.05 or >0.025 worse than the first-patient bound is adverse for repeated use and cannot be hidden by the primary result.

Randomization is configuration-stratified. A transportable claim requires at least six sites/two interface families; no site, configuration, or family contributes >30% of any of the four reference-status denominators; each assignment/reference-status denominator is at least 100 in four configurations and 200 in three. Report each configuration/family, hierarchical binomial estimates, prediction intervals, and leave-one-site/configuration/family-out exact bounds. Every leave-one-out mixture must retain `B_U<0.025`. For a configuration with at least 100 R-positive and 500 R-negative patients per arm, its conservative bound must be <0.05. Sparse cells are inconclusive, not homogeneous. A prediction interval implying `Se<0.90` or `Sp<0.98`, adjusted configuration accuracy range >0.10, or material assignment-by-configuration interaction is adverse. Support applies only to enumerated locked configurations.

A configuration stops after linkage, clock, or export failure in >5% of its latest 50 episodes and undergoes a new run-in. It cannot re-enter the confirmatory mixture after repair; later rows are feasibility-only. Version changes create new configurations. Inability to maintain four active sites is operationally inconclusive.

## Verifier requirements and adversarial cases

The verifier must reproduce first-patient selection before outcomes; randomization balance and configuration blocks; `Z` retention despite `A`; full positive/negative verification; 24-hour/new-baseline recurrence; clock maps and boundary labels; the 3×3 tables; all exact Clopper–Pearson intervals; all 96 corner/vertex evaluations for every joint completion; `B_U`, witness, sample-size lock, NI correction zone, matching, site concentration, and leave-out gates. It must compare corner optimization with brute-force grid/linear programming and exhaustive completion for small tables.

Required synthetic cases include:

1. High PPV and sensitivity in both actual-stopped-drug strata but differential specificity by randomized assignment that gives `B_U≥0.025`: reject.
2. A crossover/Simpson construction where `A`-specific performance is identical but `Z`-specific performance differs: primary assignment gate must fail.
3. Nearly identical `Se/Sp` by `Z` with exact `B_U<0.025`, all operations/transport gates passing: support measurement only; reject prose claiming treatment benefit.
4. PPV >0.80 but low specificity at p=0.10: fail distortion despite favorable PPV.
5. Indeterminate labels whose separate metric-wise imputations pass but a joint completion fails: report the joint worst case.
6. Timestamp exactly at `t0`, six hours, or closure; spanning intervals; duplicate pump/EHR events; clock uncertainty crossing a boundary.
7. Missing EHR-negative episodes, denominator-triggered truncation, arm-specific closeout, post-lock patients, changed configuration version, or omitted crossover: invalidate computation.
8. Future NI example with EHR upper limit below `0.05-B_U`: allow only the bounded measurement implication; an upper limit inside `[0.05-B_U,0.05+B_U]` is inconclusive; no benefit statement is allowed.
9. Sparse configuration or leave-one-site-out reversal: global pooled pass cannot establish transport.
10. Recurrent episodes counted as independent primary patients or used to rescue a first-patient failure: reject.
11. A correct adverse or inconclusive result interpreted as endpoint validation: reject; a negative conclusion that the endpoint can distort the decision is valid.

Computational verification can establish table integrity, label derivation from supplied streams, exact bounds, and whether reported conclusions match gates. It cannot establish that linkage proves physical delivery, infer rescue intent, establish MCS absence, ratify the 0.05 clinical margin, approve randomization, determine safety/equipoise, or establish causal benefit. Those require device governance, expert adjudication, clinical review, and the later trial.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive means the fixed sample completes; all joint-completion exact bounds give `B_U<0.025`; indeterminate, recurrence, clock, adjudication, assignment/adherence, and transport safeguards pass; and leave-out analyses do not reverse the gate. This supports a bounded measurement-error correction for only the enumerated configurations and stated region.

Adverse means completed data give `B_U≥0.025`, assignment-specific error or crossover produces consequential differential measurement, specificity/sensitivity or configuration performance is inadequate, recurrent performance is materially worse, or paired/event evidence shows systematic missed or spurious rescue. It is valuable evidence that the EHR endpoint can distort a later RD/NI decision. High PPV, pooled accuracy, or actual-drug symmetry cannot override it.

Inconclusive means calibration requires >15,000 or >48 months; reference-status denominators are inadequate at the immutable lock; exact indeterminate bounds straddle the gate; randomization/adjudication/capture/clock/configuration operations fail; clinical governance does not ratify the region/margin; or the later trial uses a different population, clock, endpoint, or estimand. Inconclusive never supports endpoint validity, noninferiority, safety, or treatment benefit.
