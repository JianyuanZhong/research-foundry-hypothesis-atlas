## 1. Branch question (frozen, inherited verbatim)
"In the imaging-negative-at-landmark (IMGneg) stratum, is the within-patient post-LDT serum AFP slope a
tumour-burden signal or procedure-induced release, and does it predict progression?"

## 2. Unresolved question, clinical importance, and the advance over each parent
Clinicians repeat serum AFP after liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / ablation / resection) and must decide when
to re-image and whether a rising AFP means residual tumour. Two *complementary, never-compared* Episode-42
children sit on this branch:
- `candidate-7c65615d` — a shared-parameter joint longitudinal–survival model that estimates a per-patient
  **tumour-burden slope** `b_i` and a per-procedure **release amplitude** `A_i` with decay `τ` in **one**
  likelihood, and links both to the IMGneg progression hazard.
- `candidate-e6b6ee52` — a same-population **restricted all-draw slope** `M0_res` with a procedure-intensity
  adjustment, whose MEASURED probe proved the parent's decay-clean filter estimand is **not resolvable**: only
  **116/51,381 (0.23%)** of LDT patients and **58/15,807 (0.37%)** of IMGneg patients have ≥2 decay-clean
  (gap-30) draws, and **99.35%** of AFP draws fall within 30 d of a preceding procedure (median 1 day).

**Observed / already supported by evidence (not in question here):**
- Post-treatment HCC relapse is residual-disease-linked, so a rising tumour-burden marker is a plausible relapse
  signal [K1].
- A *transient* AFP rise ("flare") after liver-directed therapy is real and occurs **without** progression
  [K2]; hepatectomy/ablation also perturb circulating analytes via injury/regeneration.
- A dynamic biomarker should be linked to the hazard through a joint longitudinal–survival model rather than a
  plug-in concurrent value [K3].

**Unresolved claim the experiment tests:** whether, *within the same patients and using every draw*, the post-LDT
AFP trajectory contains a **tumour-burden slope** that predicts IMGneg progression **after** an explicit
procedure-timed release component is estimated.

**Why a filter cannot answer it (this is the crossover's core).** The parent's `M_decay` filter removes the
release window by **dropping draws and patients**: it simultaneously (a) removes periprocedural draws, (b)
shrinks/changes the eligible **patient set** to those whose procedure spacing keeps ≥2 decay-clean draws, and (c)
still cannot represent a release extending past 30 d. The measured 0.23%/0.37% eligibility shows arm (b) is not
merely a nuisance but the dominant effect — a `M0`-vs-`M_decay` gap is **not attributable** to tumour biology.
`M0_res` is therefore the honest **simple baseline**, and the **joint model is the only viable route** to
separate burden slope from release without discarding the 99.35% periprocedural draws.

**Strongest live rival — `R_pulse`:** the post-LDT AFP slope–outcome association is **procedure-timed release**;
once the pulse is estimated the residual tumour-burden slope does not predict progression. A second rival,
**`R_selection`**, is inherited from `candidate-e6b6ee52`: the tentatively "cleaner" association is attributable
to the treatment-schedule / patient-composition change induced by any draw-filtering.

## 3. Inherited frozen design (verbatim; unchanged in this child)
- **Population:** HCC diagnosis (diagnoses `诊断名称`) AND ≥1 liver-directed procedure (procedures `手术`).
- **Index** = earliest qualifying LDT `开始时间`; **t\*** = index + 180 d (90 d sensitivity).
- **Exposure (M0 / M0_res):** per-patient OLS slope of `log10(AFP)` over draws with `检验时间` in (index, t\*];
  AFP = labs `检验`='甲胎蛋白', numeric `定量结果`; **same-draw** albumin + total bilirubin within ±2 d of
  `检验时间`; ALBI = 0.66·log10(bilirubin) − 0.085·albumin; AFP/albumin/bilirubin **look-alike exclusions**
  (`甲胎蛋白异质体`/AFP-L3, `前白蛋白`, `糖化白蛋白`, `白蛋白/球蛋白/白球比`, `总胆红素` flags etc.).
- **Landmark imaging status** over `检查-开始时间` in [t\*−45 d, t\*+45 d]: **IMGneg** = a qualifying abdominal
  study whose `检查所见` AND `检查诊断` contain **none** of {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多, 转移}; IMGpos
  otherwise; IMGmissing if none.
- **Outcome:** modality-restricted abdominal **local progression** (lexical event = any of
  {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多}) with `转移` as a **competing event**; censor via encounters `出院时间`.
- **Controls:** age, sex, baseline tumour/procedure variables, surveillance intensity (draw count, imaging
  cadence); **ALBI-slope negative control**; conjunctive falsification; **NO survival / mRECIST claim**.
- **Join keys:** `患者主索引` + `就诊号`; time fields `开始时间` (procedure/check), `检验时间` (lab),
  `出院时间` (encounter).
- **Snapshot:** `[source checksum]`.

## 4. The crossover: two arms on the SAME patients and question
Both arms use the **identical IMGneg patient set**, the **identical outcome**, and the **identical (index, t\*]**
window. They differ only in how the AFP trajectory is represented.

**Arm B0 — baseline (simple): restricted all-draw slope `M0_res`.**
Fit the parent's Cox model on IMGneg with the per-patient OLS slope of `log10(AFP)` over **all** (index,t\*]
draws, plus the pre-declared **procedure-intensity adjustment** (count of LDT events in (index,t\*], and days
since nearest preceding procedure) and the standard covariates. Deliverable: HR per 1-SD of `M0_res`.

**Arm B1 — alternative (shared-parameter mechanistic): joint model with exponential release pulse.**
- Longitudinal submodel for `log10 AFP_ij` over (index,t\*]:
  `log10 AFP_ij = μ + u_i + b_i·(t_ij − t0) + Σ_j A_i·exp(−max(0, t_ij − s_ij)/τ) + ε_ij`,
  where `u_i` = random intercept, `b_i` = **tumour-burden slope** (random), `A_i` = **periprocedural release
  amplitude** (random, per procedure-event), `τ` = decay time constant, `s_ij` = `开始时间` of the most recent
  LDT at draw `t_ij`, `ε_ij` = measurement error.
- Survival submodel (progression hazard on [t\*, censor]):
  `λ_i(t) = λ0(t)·exp(β_b·b_i + β_A·A_i + γ'X_i)`, `λ0` flexible (B-spline / Weibull).
- **Association parameters of interest:** `β_b` (tumour-slope HR) and `β_A` (release-amplitude HR). Fit by
  maximising the joint observed-data log-likelihood (Laplace/AGQ over random effects).

**What B1 reveals that B0 loses:** B0's single number `M0_res` is a *forced blend* of burden slope and
procedure-timed release; because 99.35% of draws are within 30 d of a procedure, B0 cannot tell whether its slope
is tumour biology or release. B1 estimates the release pulse (`A_i`, `τ`) **inside the same likelihood**, so the
residual burden slope `b_i` is separated from release **within the same patients** (no population selection), and
`A_i` becomes a directly testable rival predictor. An OLS slope cannot be identified separately from a pulse it
is absorbing; the joint model can, or can fail identifiably (Section 6).

## 5. FIT SPLIT / DATA
- Population identical to parents (no draw filtering for B1; B0 also uses all draws then restricts to the same
  IMGneg set). **No train/test split** at cohort scale; landmark structure is respected (covariates fixed at t\*).
- Uncertainty: **patient-level bootstrap** (B0, ≥500 replicates) and **profile / bootstrap CI** (B1); the
  shared-parameter fit is CPU-scale for the ~10^4-patient IMGneg cohort with ~10^5 draws.

## 6. IDENTIFIABILITY INCONCLUSIVE GUARD (pre-declared)
Because draw timing is near-periprocedural (99.35% within 30 d; median 1 day), `A_i`, `τ` and `b_i` may be
**confounded**: if almost every draw lies inside the release window, the pulse can absorb the slope. Therefore the
child **declines the primary burden-slope claim** (forces **Inconclusive**) if EITHER:
- **(i) identifiability fails:** the joint fit does not converge to a stable optimum, OR the profile likelihood for
  `β_b` (and `τ`) is non-identified (flat/wide profile, Hessian ill-conditioned, or bootstrap gives a
  pathological/negative-variance or non-finite spread) under the observed draw-timing distribution; OR
- **(ii) power/event floor:** IMGneg **events < ~30** (lexical progression events in the IMGneg landmark cohort),
  so the hazard is not estimable.
This guard is pre-committed and cannot be reapplied post hoc to rescue a claim.

## 7. Pre-committed branches (numeric thresholds, mapped to distinct tests)
Primary contrast = `β_b` (burden-slope per-1-SD HR) in IMGneg from B1. `HR_A` = release-amplitude HR. `HR_res` =
baseline `M0_res` HR (B0). z from profile/bootstrap CI.

- **Supportive (tumour-burden signal survives pulse estimation):** B1 `β_b` HR 95% CI excludes 1 **and** the CI
  is contained in a *meaningful* band (lower bound ≥ 1.15 for a per-1-SD protective→risk direction, i.e. ≥15% HR
  per SD) **and** the identifiability guard passes **and** events ≥30. Interpretation: a residual burden slope
  predicts IMGneg progression **after** `A_i`/`τ` are estimated. Distinguishes from B0 by *surviving* the pulse.
- **Adverse — `R_pulse`:** `β_b` CI **includes 1** (or collapses to null) while `HR_A` CI **excludes 1** (release
  amplitude carries the signal). Test: `β_b` null AND `β_A` non-null ⇒ the slope–outcome association is
  procedure-release, not burden.
- **Adverse — `R_selection`:** the B1 `β_b` point estimate is materially attenuated (≥50% HR attenuation) versus
  the matched B0 `M0_res` on the **same patients**, consistent with the earlier association being a
  draw-timing/procedure-schedule artefact rather than biology. Test: B0-vs-B1 attenuation of the slope HR.
- **Inconclusive:** Section-6 guard triggers (non-identified joint model **or** IMGneg events <30), OR the `β_b`
  CI is so wide it cannot exclude an HR of 1.15 **and** of 0.87 (imprecise). Declare non-estimable, not a null.
- **Precise-null:** identifiability guard passes, events ≥30, and the B1 `β_b` CI **excludes** a meaningful
  burden effect (entire 95% CI within [0.87, 1.15]) with `HR_A` likewise null. Interpretation: no detectable
  burden-slope or release-amplitude progression signal in IMGneg.
- **Conjunctive falsification:** the primary claim additionally requires the **ALBI-slope negative control** to be
  null; a non-null ALBI slope weakens causal/burden interpretation and is reported.

## 11. Alternatives not chosen, and what would revisit them
- **Filter-based decay-clean slope `M_decay` (parent leader).** Not chosen as primary estimator: MEASURED
  non-estimable (0.23%/0.37%). Revisit only if a future snapshot yields ≥50% IMGneg eligibility (the parent's
  pre-declared threshold).
- **Pure `M0_res` without the joint model.** Retained as **baseline B0**, not the primary: cannot separate release
  from burden under 99.35% periprocedural clustering — that is the estimand gap B1 fills.
- **Per-patient (random) `τ_i`** instead of a population `τ`. Deferred: adds random-effect dimension and
  identifiability risk; revisit if a population `τ` fits poorly or the guard triggers on `τ` non-identification.
- **Full vs partial labs scan / alternative biomarker (PIVKA-II).** Not chosen: probe already decisive for
  eligibility; PIVKA-II is out of the frozen question.
- **GPU acceleration.** Not chosen: CPU-scale fit for ~10^4 patients; **CPU-only**, no GPU justified.

## 12. Limits, adjudication, and claims that need another study
- **Design only**; no HR/slope/fit/LRT/likelihood computed here.
- The lexical progression endpoint (`增大/进展/复发/新发/增多`, `转移` competing) needs **radiologist mRECIST
  adjudication** before any clinical-truth claim; **no survival / mRECIST claim** is made.
- Single institution; partial labs scan; no lab units; periprocedural-draw confounding is a genuine identifiability
  threat (Section 6). `A_i`/`τ`/`b_i` separation on this snapshot may be **non-identifiable** — that outcome is
  Inconclusive, not evidence for or against the burden signal.
- Claims that are **computationally checkable**: HRs, CIs, `τ̂`, convergence/identifiability diagnostics. Claims
  requiring **clinical adjudication or another study**: endpoint truth, external validity, mechanistic
  interpretation.

## Key references (exactly three, inspected abstract-only; see `key-references.json`)
- **[K1]** Mingazzini A, et al. *Current Perspectives on the Use of Adjuvant Pembrolizumab for HCC.* 2026.
  `doi:10.2147/jhc.s613651`. Residual/molecular disease is the recognized substrate for post-treatment relapse —
  bounds the premise that a rising burden marker can track residual disease. **Limit:** review; no AFP kinetics.
- **[K2]** Yoshida M, et al. *Transient increases in serum AFP … following proton therapy does not necessarily
  indicate progression of HCC.* Oncol Lett 2019;17(3):3026-3034. `doi:10.3892/ol.2019.9922`. Post-LDT AFP flares
  are frequent and **not** significantly associated with response; median time-to-peak 1 month — direct evidence
  for the procedure-release rival `R_pulse`. **Limit:** single-centre proton cohort (n=82); no joint model.
- **[K3]** Guo W, Wang T. *Semiparametric Joint Modeling for Survival Analysis with Longitudinal Covariates.*
  J Am Stat Assoc 2026 (ahead of print). `doi:10.1080/01621459.2026.2615849`. Jointly modelling the longitudinal
  trajectory and survival via a shared parameter is the principled link between a dynamic biomarker pattern and
  the hazard. **Limit:** methodology only; not HCC/AFP.
