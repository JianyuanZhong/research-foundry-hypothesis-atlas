## Assay and unit repair
The HCC metadata states that lab numeric results have no separate unit column. Therefore the primary analysis will not pool assays or treat raw values as interchangeable. It will use prespecified within-assay summaries (first value, last value, slope/linear trend, and missingness) for exact assay names such as `白蛋白, 总胆红素, 国际标准化比值, 甲胎蛋白, 肌酐, 丙氨酸氨基转移酶, 血小板计数, 凝血酶原时间, 尿素`, retaining `标本类型` and excluding conflicting or non-numeric measurements. A sensitivity analysis may standardize only when a defensible within-source assay mapping is established; absent that, no unit conversion is allowed.

## Baseline versus time-aware alternative
The simple baseline is penalized logistic regression using age, sex, department, first available assay values, treatment/procedure indicators available by time zero, and documentation intensity. The substantive alternative is a patient-level time-aware sequence/state-space model using ordered assay values, time since admission, specimen type, missingness, and treatment timing. The alternative can reveal persistence, ordering, and cross-marker change that a snapshot loses. Both are fit on the same patient-level split and evaluated with held-out discrimination, calibration, uncertainty, and negative-control timing checks. CPU is appropriate for the bounded tabular/state-space study; GPU is not required.

## Falsification and interpretation
Supportive evidence requires a prespecified held-out improvement with uncertainty excluding a trivial margin and consistency across department, documentation, missingness, and assay sensitivity analyses. Adverse evidence is no improvement, degradation, or negative-control failure. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse qualifying events, unstable assay semantics, imprecise intervals, or inability to adjudicate the proxy. Any result establishes predictive association only, not mechanism, causality, or clinical harm.
