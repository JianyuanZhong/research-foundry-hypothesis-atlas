## Clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

After a person has a current blood-biomarker panel, does retaining the earlier panel improve the allocation of one finite participant-level review workload across cardiovascular, renal and metabolic risk, compared with using the current panel and the interval since the prior assessment?

The decision is one shared workload: a review is performed once per selected participant and may address all three domains. Let N be the eligible paired-assessment population and K=floor(0.10N). Each model produces one score per participant and exactly the K highest-scoring participants form one list. There are no separate cardiovascular, renal and metabolic lists, so overlap cannot increase workload.

For participant i, let Y_id=1 when a qualifying recorded event in domain d occurs after T1, within H=T1+5 years, and before any earlier recorded death, where d is C, R or M. Let U_i=(Y_iC+Y_iR+Y_iM)/3, with equal fixed domain weights chosen before outcomes are inspected. For selected list A_m:
V_m(K)=K^-1 sum(i in A_m) U_i
and Delta V(K)=V_M1b(K)-V_M1a(K).

This is recorded CKM-domain events per reviewed participant under a fixed participant workload. Secondary outputs are the fraction of all recorded domain events captured by the list, each domain's yield/capture, and selected participants with zero target-domain events. A person with two domains contributes twice to the numerator but consumes one review slot; this is the intended utility.

The primary hypothesis is positive decision-relevant incremental value: H_A:Delta V(K)>=0.01 against H_0:Delta V(K)<=0. The 0.01 is a study-scale practical margin, not a validated clinical-benefit threshold. A null or negative held-out utility difference, instability, calibration harm, or reversal under adequately supported repeat-selection weighting falsifies the proposed benefit.

A positive result would show improved analytic allocation in this selected UKB cohort. It would not show that repeat testing should be ordered, that review changes treatment or outcomes, or that a biomarker is causal. The advance over ordinary added-predictor discrimination and the prior pathway-wise design is a single capacity-constrained multi-domain estimand: gain per participant reviewed, with domain-specific safety diagnostics retained.

## Models and one-list policy

Use one deterministic participant-level outer split:
fold = integer(SHA256(UTF-8(decimal(eid)))) mod 5.
Publish its manifest. Imputation, scaling, feature screening, penalties, calibration, tuning and repeat-selection weights are fit within the relevant training fold.

- M0 audit: current instance-1 panel, approved age, approved sex and T1 calendar year.
- M1a transparent baseline: M0 plus L. It is selection-aware because it includes repeat spacing but no prior values.
- M1b transparent history model: M1a plus all eight instance-0 fields. Fit three regularized logistic probability models, one per domain, with a common additive feature form and fold-local standardization/penalty selection. This is the primary history-versus-baseline comparison.
- M2 learned alternative: three gradient-boosted probability models using exactly M1b inputs, identical participants, labels and folds. Freeze a training-only grid for nonlinear thresholds, current-by-prior discordance and interactions with L. M2 cannot replace the primary nested comparison or win by worsening calibration.

For M1a, M1b and M2, create one score S_i=(p_iC+p_iR+p_iM)/3 and one global held-out list A_m of K=floor(0.10N) participants. Compute K once for the eligible evaluation cohort, not once per domain or fold. Resolve ties by ascending SHA-256 of decimal eid. Evaluate descriptive capacities floor(0.05N) and floor(0.20N), predeclared. Every selected participant receives one analytic review label; no clinical action occurs.

Report primary Delta V(K), V_m(K), paired intervals, list additions/removals, unique list size (must equal K), overlap, domain-count distribution, yield/capture/sensitivity/PPV/false-review proportion by domain, and zero-event reviews. Also report one-vs-domain and first-event Brier/log loss, calibration intercept/slope, reliability curves and decision-curve summaries. M2-minus-M1b utility and calibration are secondary; interaction/shape summaries require fold stability.

## Selection-aware and temporal transport

The paired cohort is selected by having a second assessment. Among all participants with a valid T0 panel, estimate repeat probability using only T0 biomarker values, age, sex and T0 calendar year. Fit within training folds and report repeat rate, covariate balance, overlap, stabilized inverse-probability weights and effective sample size. Do not use T1 or post-T1 outcomes. This is a transport sensitivity, not causal correction.

A weighted reversal of Delta V(K) is adverse for transport to T0-eligible participants only when positivity and ESS are adequate; poor support is inconclusive. For temporal transport, fit on eligible T1<=2012-12-31 participants and evaluate without refitting on eligible 2013 participants, with a K rule defined within that held-out era. Report only if both eras meet support gates. This is not external validation.

## Uncertainty and interpretation rules

Use 2,000 paired participant bootstrap replicates stratified by outer fold and joint domain-event count, preserving out-of-fold predictions and recomputing the one global K. State whether models are refit; prediction-level intervals condition on fitted outer models and omit training uncertainty. Report fold estimates, interval widths, calibration uncertainty and complete list ledgers.

Supportive evidence requires all semantic/timing/ascertainment gates, no integrity/leakage failure, at least 100 events in each domain and 200 eligible participants for each claimed domain, Delta V(K)>=0.01 with a two-sided 95% interval excluding zero, no domain capture decrease greater than 0.02 versus M1a, and M1b calibration intercept shift at most 0.05 and slope within 0.10 of M1a in supported domains. Conclusions must name computed utility/list/uncertainty files. The margin is not clinical validation.

Adverse evidence is supported Delta V(K)<=0, practically negative utility, failure to preserve a domain under the shared budget, calibration deterioration, or reversal under adequately supported weighting or era transport. Brier improvement alone is adverse to the policy claim if it does not improve shared utility or worsens list composition/calibration. Inconclusive means a failed semantic/timing/ascertainment gate, sparse events, ambiguous pairing, incomplete observation, poor overlap/ESS, unstable folds/interactions, wide intervals, or missing artifacts. It is not a null clinical conclusion.

## Falsification and integrity

Run and retain:
- domain-label permutation within training folds and capacity strata;
- prior-panel permutation within strata of current risk, L, missingness and repeat propensity;
- code/date position permutation and an intentionally shifted-position control, which the pairing audit must reject;
- frozen label/date permutation and a negative-control pre-T1 recorded endpoint;
- complete-case versus fold-local missingness handling;
- raw-prior versus raw-change sensitivity only if field semantics authorize arithmetic;
- leakage scans for dates, outcomes, preprocessing, calibration, weighting and list construction;
- fold stability, calibration, event support, domain overlap and list-membership stability.

A history gain that survives the appropriate prior permutation is not credible. Any falsification or integrity failure stops interpretation. These checks establish computation/leakage properties, not causality.

## Alternatives, deliverable and resources

M1b is selected as primary because its nested comparison isolates prior values from repeat spacing and its coefficients remain clinically legible. M2 is a substantive same-input alternative: nonlinear thresholds, current-prior discordance and interval-dependent history could reveal information lost by the additive model. They share population, target, split, inputs and evaluation.

A dated disease-history transformer is deferred because this export has field-position arrays but no certified dated disease sequence; it would confound position with chronology. A mechanistic organ-reserve model is deferred because analyte identities, units, assay quality, specimen dates and organ mappings are uncertified. Imaging/visceral-fat, proteomics, CHIP, genetics/PRS and cancer-stage branches are deferred because the full catalog lacks the required verified raw modalities or validated semantics. Revisit these branches only when those named dependencies are supplied. No branch is excluded merely for using neural networks or GPUs.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted fold-safe M0/M1a/M1b/M2 models; out-of-fold probabilities; one-list ledgers for every model/capacity; Delta V(K), domain capture/yield and calibration outputs with paired uncertainty; selection and temporal-transport diagnostics; and falsification/integrity records. Completion requires those artifacts and a conclusion linked to computed outputs. A complete fail-closed gate certificate is a valid computational deliverable when semantics or ascertainment fail, but is neither a positive nor negative clinical result.

## Evidence limits

Computationally checkable claims are source integrity, field presence, joins, cohort flow, code/date pairing conditional on the certified codebook, held-out predictions, exact K arithmetic, utility/capture calculations, uncertainty, calibration, weighting, temporal transport, leakage and permutation behavior.

Clinical experts or additional evidence must certify code meanings, specimen timing, biomarker units and assay validity, disease onset/severity, death ascertainment, complete observation, whether an event merits review, acceptable domain weights and action thresholds. Repeat-assessment selection limits generalizability. External validation in a differently ascertained cohort and a prospective policy or randomized review study are required before recommending repeat testing, referral, treatment or deployment. No observational result here establishes causal benefit.
