# Provisional hypothesis: discordant AKI recovery in MIMIC-IV

Status: provisional repair of [prior hypothesis]. No computed cohort, outcome, or effect results are claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adult MIMIC-IV ICU patients with an acute kidney injury episode and a creatinine measurement near a prespecified 48-hour landmark, patients whose creatinine has improved but whose urine output remains low, or whose cumulative fluid balance remains positive, will have higher subsequent in-hospital death or persistent/worsening kidney dysfunction than patients with concordant creatinine and urine-output recovery. The comparison will use only information available before the landmark.

This is a prognostic hypothesis, not a causal claim. Discordance may reflect delayed clearance, ongoing tubular injury, fluid overload, or differences in monitoring and treatment. The experiment tests whether the discordant phenotype adds information beyond a creatinine-only recovery phenotype.

## Falsifiable test

Use a first qualifying adult ICU stay per hospitalization with an AKI episode and a creatinine measurement near 48 hours after ICU admission. Define creatinine recovery, urine-output recovery, and cumulative fluid balance using only pre-landmark observations. Compare discordant versus concordant recovery for in-hospital death and persistent/worsening kidney dysfunction after the landmark, with a prespecified follow-up window and competing-risk handling.

A parsimonious baseline uses pre-landmark severity, creatinine trajectory, treatment, and demographics. A substantive time-aware alternative uses the ordered pre-landmark trajectories of creatinine, urine output, and fluid balance with the same patient split and outcome. The alternative can reveal whether temporal ordering adds information beyond a single landmark value; it does not establish mechanism.

Supportive evidence would be a prespecified incremental association for discordance with uncertainty, robust to monitoring-intensity adjustment and a sensitivity analysis. Adverse evidence would be no incremental association, disappearance after monitoring adjustment, or reversal in the prespecified sensitivity analysis. An imprecise null is inconclusive, not decisive refutation.
