# Self-contained reconstruction of eICU ventilation care-plan semantic coverage and outcome ascertainment

## Question and advance

The strongest claim supported by the two parents is narrow: in their frozen eICU landmark cohort, broadening two exact Ventilation care-plan labels to all literal `ventilated -` values added almost no documented receipt and did not improve hospital-held-out prediction of a later structured respiratory-setting reduction; accounting for incomplete outcome ascertainment did not produce a persuasive rescue. The unresolved claim tested here is whether that negative result survives when the denominator and outcome are rebuilt transparently from the frozen raw eICU snapshot rather than imported from ephemeral `parent_rows.csv` or `observation_competing_state_rows.csv` artifacts.

This is clinically useful because care-plan fields are tempting low-cost proxies for ventilator-weaning processes in multisite EHR studies. A reproducible negative result warns against silently treating sparse local documentation as bedside evaluation, while a failed reconstruction or favorable broad-label result would falsify that warning. The advance is an executable raw-source lineage with explicit filter flows, first-stay selection, source-wide vocabulary enumeration, outcome reconstruction, competing-state accounting, and positive controls.

## Hypothesis, estimands, and falsification

Hypothesis: after target-independent reconstruction, all literal `ventilated -` care-plan values beyond the two exact labels will still add less than 2 percentage points of coverage in every bounded-airway cohort and will not improve later structured respiratory-setting prediction over generic documentation-process controls across hospitals. Outcome ascertainment weighting and an all-row five-state sensitivity will not provide concordant hospital-held-out evidence of rescue.

Primary estimands are broad-minus-process and broad-minus-exact differences in row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss under leave-one-hospital-out prediction. Secondary metrics are Brier score, AUROC, calibration, hospital direction, broad-label coverage, and state frequencies. Repeated landmarks are clustered by `sid` in uncertainty calculations.

Supportive semantic-undercoverage evidence requires at least a 5-point coverage gain, alternatives in at least five unbounded hospitals and at least three hospitals in two bounded variants, and broad models improving both micro and macro loss over process and exact models in unbounded and at least two bounded variants with upper uncertainty bounds below zero. Adverse evidence requires less than 2-point gain in every bounded variant, no bounded predictive support, and adverse broad-minus-process macro direction in at least two bounded variants. A selection rescue additionally requires concordant favorable IPW and five-state results in unbounded and at least two bounded variants with adequate effective sample size. Otherwise ascertainment is inconclusive. Reconstruction is falsified by changed landmarks, multiple selected stays per person within a definition, source-join mismatches, receipt mismatches, outcome-row mismatches, or failure to reproduce the prespecified four primary row counts; discrepancies must be traced rather than forced away.

## Population and exact temporal design

At landmarks `L = 1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200` ICU minutes, candidates are adults (`age >=18`, `> 89` mapped to 90) still in ICU (`unitdischargeoffset > L`). Eligibility uses only pre-landmark data:

- latest valid FiO2 and PEEP/CPAP in `[L-360,L)`, with FiO2 `<=0.50` and PEEP `<=8`;
- at least two valid SpO2 values in `[L-120,L)`, median `>=88` and `<100`;
- latest valid respiratory rate in `[L-120,L)` from 8 through 35;
- median invasive plus non-invasive MAP in `[L-120,L)` at least 65;
- no positive end-of-life discussion offset before `L`;
- no exact `Spontaneous - adequate` care-plan value in `[L-1440,L)`.

Four prespecified ventilation bounds are retained: latest prior exact airway without a recency bound, and any exact airway in `[L-1440,L)`, `[L-720,L)`, or `[L-360,L)`. Exact airway values are `Oral ETT`, `Nasal ETT`, `Tracheostomy`, `Double-Lumen Tube`, and `Cricothyrotomy`. Within each definition, the first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid` is selected by `unitvisitnumber`, then `patientunitstayid`; all qualifying landmarks on that stay remain. Care-plan targets are not attached until these keys are frozen.

Classification is inclusive `[L,L+360]`. Exact receipt is either `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` or `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`; broader receipt is any normalized source-wide Ventilation value beginning `ventilated -`. The later outcome window is `(L+360,L+1080]`, only after classification. The computable binary outcome is any valid decrease from the latest pre-landmark value in FiO2 (0.05), PEEP (2), pressure support (2), or ventilator rate (2); peak-pressure labels are excluded. No paired component is missing, not a non-event. Complete outcome rows require `unitdischargeoffset > L+1080` and nonmissing outcome.

Before that restriction, every classification-complete row is assigned exactly one state: ICU death by `L+1080`, live ICU discharge by `L+1080`, complete computable outcome, still in ICU with at least two distinct respiratory-chart offsets but no paired outcome, or still in ICU with sparse respiratory documentation. Live discharge is not called success.

## Raw bindings and execution

Frozen eICU 2.0 snapshot: `[source checksum]`. Files are ordinary gzip CSVs with no enclosing archive member, under `[internal dataset path]`.

- `patient.csv.gz`: `patientunitstayid`, `uniquepid`, `hospitalid`, `age`, `unitvisitnumber`, `unitdischargeoffset`, `unitdischargestatus`.
- `respiratoryCare.csv.gz`: `patientunitstayid`, `respcarestatusoffset`, `airwaytype`, `ventstartoffset`, `ventendoffset`, `priorventstartoffset`, `priorventendoffset`.
- `respiratoryCharting.csv.gz`: `patientunitstayid`, `respchartoffset`, `respchartvaluelabel`, `respchartvalue` (entry/type fields were schema-checked but not needed for the estimand).
- `vitalPeriodic.csv.gz`: `patientunitstayid`, `observationoffset`, `sao2`, `respiration`, `systemicmean`.
- `vitalAperiodic.csv.gz`: `patientunitstayid`, `observationoffset`, `noninvasivemean`.
- `carePlanGeneral.csv.gz`: `patientunitstayid`, `cplitemoffset`, `cplgroup`, `cplitemvalue`.
- `carePlanEOL.csv.gz`: `patientunitstayid`, `cpleoldiscussionoffset`.

All joins use `patientunitstayid = sid`; repeated analysis rows are keyed by `definition, sid, landmark`. `reconstruct_parent.py` scans the seven raw tables and emits target-independent membership plus filter flows. `denominator_fitness.py` independently extracts pre/post respiratory features and outputs the downstream row table. `careplan_vocabulary_audit.py` scans all 3,115,018 care-plan rows before modeling. `observation_competing_state_audit.py` independently re-extracts care-plan, patient discharge, and respiratory support and checks inherited fields.

Models use median imputation with missing indicators, standardization, one-hot landmark/label encoding, and `C=1` logistic regression. Hospitals are held out in turn. The semantic analysis refits models during stay-cluster resampling; this bounded execution used only 5 replicates and is therefore directional, not a stable confidence interval. The ascertainment analysis used 20 fixed-prediction stay-cluster replicates. Observation probabilities are clipped to `[0.05,0.95]`, inverse weights capped at 10, and normalized within held-out hospital. These reduced replicate counts are an explicit computational limitation; parent 100-replicate results are not substituted for child estimates.

## Executed results

Raw reconstruction exactly reproduced the prespecified primary denominators: 3,722 unbounded, 2,612 at 24 hours, 1,774 at 12 hours, and 1,178 at 6 hours. Classification-complete counts were 3,671, 2,575, 1,748, and 1,159; complete computable counts were 3,296, 2,273, 1,555, and 1,018. No definition/person selected more than one stay.

The source-wide normalized Ventilation vocabulary was exhaustive at eight values, including four literal `ventilated -` values. Alternative-only coverage gains in the complete computable sets were only 0.091%, 0.132%, 0.129%, and 0.098%, and occurred in one eligible hospital. Broad-minus-process macro log-loss differences were adverse (positive) in all four variants: `+0.00089`, `+0.00192`, `+0.00277`, and `+0.00749`; micro differences were `+0.00099`, `+0.00825`, `+0.00936`, and `+0.01538`. Thus the semantic broadening explanation met the adverse gate and no variant met predictive support. Five bootstrap replicates are insufficient for precise probabilities, but the deterministic coverage failure and all point directions independently reproduce the parent conclusion.

Across the 9,153 classification-complete rows, state counts by unbounded/24h/12h/6h were:

- complete computable: 3,296 / 2,273 / 1,555 / 1,018;
- sparse respiratory documentation: 258 / 217 / 138 / 98;
- adequate charting but no paired outcome: 24 / 16 / 12 / 10;
- live ICU discharge: 59 / 42 / 22 / 17;
- ICU death: 34 / 27 / 21 / 16.

All positive controls passed: exact receipt, source hospital/discharge offset, post-window respiratory row counts, complete-case logic, expected vocabulary, and early discharge status. IPW effective-sample fractions were 0.907, 0.847, 0.861, and 0.846. Weighted broad-minus-process macro log-loss differences remained adverse: `+0.00090`, `+0.00529`, `+0.00642`, and `+0.01485`; 20-replicate intervals for 12h and 6h excluded zero (`[+0.00147,+0.01051]`, `[+0.00684,+0.02411]`). However, the all-row five-state model moved favorably, so the prespecified combined ascertainment gate was **inconclusive selection sensitivity**, not adverse reinforcement. Crucially, no IPW or multinomial variant satisfied the concordant rescue rule.

Therefore the strongest child-supported conclusion is: the negative semantic-undercoverage finding survives transparent raw reconstruction at the levels of cohort identity, negligible added coverage, adverse hospital-held-out binary-outcome point estimates, and absence of an ascertainment rescue. The competing-state analysis does not establish that selection is irrelevant; it remains inconclusive because the multinomial sensitivity and binary IPW answer different documentation-state questions and point in different directions.

## Interpretation limits and further evidence

Every exposure and respiratory outcome is a structured-documentation proxy. This study does not establish actual invasive ventilation, bedside evaluation, spontaneous breathing trial, extubation readiness, extubation, durable liberation, quality, benefit, harm, or causality. Missing exact labels are not evidence that evaluation did not occur. Automatic verification can check source hashes, row counts, windows, joins, vocabulary, outcome computation, model predictions, and stated gates; it cannot validate bedside semantics. Stronger clinical conclusions require device/event data, workflow metadata, note or chart adjudication by clinicians, external-site replication, and adequately replicated uncertainty runs.
