# Destination-aware day-28 endpoint for the hour-48 post-shock IV-loop target trial

**Parent:** `[prior hypothesis]`

## Decision, evidence boundary, and falsifiable claim

This targeted successor preserves the parent's MIMIC-IV population, ICU-hour-48 landmark, actually delivered 24-hour IV-loop strategies, two-clock contract, unit-safe fluid phenotype, measured-overlap estimand, clone-censor analysis, ALDL7 bounds, mortality/RRT gates, and exact joins. It changes the primary endpoint because “alive index-hospital discharge” mixes lower-acuity discharge, hospice, transfer to another acute hospital, transfer to long-term acute care, and administrative anomalies.

The strongest intervention claim supported by evidence already available is modest. In the inspected frozen full text of McMullan et al., *Critical Care Explorations* 2026;8:e1404, DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404`, source `[source checksum]`, a randomized secondary analysis found no statistically detectable differences in lactate, AKIRisk, or urinary cystatin-C under protocolized conservative fluid management with active deresuscitation; the authors emphasize modest sample size and imprecision. This supports feasibility of fluid removal and absence of a detected biomarker harm, not clinical benefit, renal safety, or the effect of initiating one loop dose after shock. The inspected frozen full text of Boyle et al., *Am J Respir Crit Care Med* 2026, DOI `10.1093/ajrccm/aamag238`, source `[source checksum]`, explains why death and discharge are competing events and why longitudinal state/component reporting can be more informative than an isolated organ endpoint. It does not validate any discharge category as recovery or patient benefit.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among invasively ventilated adults at ICU hour 48 who have recovered from vasopressor-treated shock, have unit-safe cumulative fluid balance at least +50 mL/kg, retained urine output and potassium at least 3.0 mmol/L, and lie in prespecified measured treatment overlap, a sustained strategy of initiating an actually delivered ICU IV loop within 24 hours increases **recorded-death-free lower-acuity discharge by day 28 (RDLAD28)** by more than 5 percentage points versus no loop by any route during that window, without a definitively adverse AIHD28 or ALDL7 signal or excess recorded day-28 death or observed new day-7 RRT.

This tests an association interpretable as a per-protocol causal contrast only under consistency, positivity, correct models, and no unmeasured confounding. MIMIC cannot establish the last condition. A supportive result prioritizes external validation and a randomized trial; it does not justify routine diuresis, prove recovery, or establish a causal effect.

## Endpoint hierarchy and exact construction

Set `t0=icustays.intime+48h`; day 28 is `t0+28d`. The primary RDLAD28 succeeds iff: (1) `hospital_expire_flag=0`; (2) `t0<dischtime<=t0+28d`; (3) normalized `discharge_location` is exactly one of `ASSISTED LIVING`, `HOME`, `HOME HEALTH CARE`, `PSYCH FACILITY`, `REHAB`, or `SKILLED NURSING FACILITY`; and (4) there is no possible recorded death by day 28.

`patients.dod` is date-only. Represent it as `[D,D+1d)`. Conservative primary coding treats nonmissing `D<=t0+28d` as possible recorded death and failure. The optimistic sensitivity treats death as failure only if `D+1d<=t0+28d`; report both and their bound. A `dod` before or overlapping t0 is a temporal contradiction and failure. Missing `dod` means no captured death in the documented window, not verified survival.

Every subject receives one mutually exclusive day-28 state, in priority order: (1) possible recorded death by day 28; (2) recorded-death-free lower-acuity discharge; (3) recorded-death-free discharge to ACUTE HOSPITAL or CHRONIC/LONG TERM ACUTE CARE; (4) recorded-death-free HOSPICE discharge; (5) recorded-death-free ambiguous/anomalous discharge (`AGAINST ADVICE`, `OTHER FACILITY`, `HEALTHCARE FACILITY`, `DIED`, missing, or unmapped); (6) still in the index hospital at day 28; (7) other/temporal contradiction. Emit index-hospital death separately within state 1. Hospitalization beyond day 28 is observed failure, not presumed death. Transfer and hospice are not called death; lower-acuity discharge is not called recovery, functional independence, or freedom from later institutional care.

The fixed hierarchy is: (1) RDLAD28 primary; (2) all state-specific standardized risks/RDs and original destination-agnostic AIHD28 as mandatory decomposition/discharge-practice sensitivities; (3) ALDL7 bounds as respiratory consistency endpoint; (4) recorded day-28 death and observed new day-7 RRT as co-safety gates; (5) alive ICU discharge with no observed same-admission return before the earlier of day 7 or hospital discharge as descriptive only.

Destination sensitivities are AIHD28; lower-acuity discharge without DOD; HOME/HOME HEALTH CARE only; adding OTHER FACILITY/HEALTHCARE FACILITY; treating LTACH as qualifying; day 14/day 21; and strata by `anchor_year_group`, first ICU, service, and admission type. Map-dependent sign reversal or important heterogeneity by practice strata is inconclusive. Use `anchor_year_group`, not shifted admission year, across subjects.

## Falsification and branches

Freeze attrition, strategies, deviations, units/status/routes, late rows, overlap, weights, ESS, endpoint maps, and anomaly rules before outcomes. Eventual A/B must not predict pre-t0 six-hour creatinine, urine, or MAP slopes after adjustment; absolute standardized difference >0.10 or multiplicity-controlled P<0.05 blocks causal interpretation. Weighted trajectory covariates must have absolute standardized differences <=0.10. Expected urine/balance direction verifies delivery only.

**Supportive:** primary RDLAD28 RD >+0.05 with 95% lower limit >0; optimistic DOD bound and destination maps do not reverse sign; AIHD28 and ALDL7 are not definitively adverse; upper 95% excess recorded death <+0.03 and RRT <+0.05 under both brackets; all process, overlap, ESS, balance, timing, missingness, residual-bias, and sensitivity gates pass. This supports trial prioritization only.

**Adverse:** 95% upper limit of RDLAD28 RD <=0; or upper limit AIHD28 RD <=0; or upper limit ALDL7 `RD_U` <=0; or lower 95% bound of mortality harm >=+0.03; or lower bound RRT harm >=+0.05. Fluid response cannot override these.

**Inconclusive:** primary fails support while permitting >+0.05; safety upper gates remain uncleared without definitive harm; DOD bounds, maps, practice strata, anomaly exclusions, or analyses conflict; overlap/adherence/ESS is sparse; modest residual confounding reverses the estimate; or falsification/missingness/timing fails. Nonsignificance is not equivalence/safety. A map-dependent positive result is inconclusive, not a recommendation.
