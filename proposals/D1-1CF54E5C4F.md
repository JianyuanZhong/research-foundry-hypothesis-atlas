# Episode-22: capacity-fixed, modality-pattern-valid MDT review allocation for documentary M2

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and advance

The decision is which adults approaching a first eligible HCC liver resection receive a scarce preoperative multidisciplinary-team (MDT) review because the eventual resection pathology may document high-grade microvascular invasion (M2). The tested action is **allocation of a fixed number of review slots**. It is not a recommendation about resection margin, transplant, neoadjuvant/adjuvant treatment, locoregional or systemic therapy, or surveillance.

**Strongest claim supported before the experiment.** Direct catalog, schema, header, and aggregate source inspection establishes that the HCC snapshot contains patient-linked encounters, two-source procedure rows, CT/MRI examination accession components with acquisition starts and eventual report bodies, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. It does not contain report author/final/release/view times, report version history, pathology time, specimen/slide/block identifiers, pathology sampling adequacy, raw images, an MDT capacity/workload audit, clinician actions, treatment effects, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, or patient benefit. The sole laboratory clock is semantically ambiguous and lacks collection/final/release/view semantics and units; all laboratory values and process derivatives therefore have zero primary lineage.

Current full text supports importance, not the proposed claim. Li et al. (2025; DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746; PMCID PMC12309289; frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) describes MVI as pathology-defined and prognostic, unavailable directly before surgery, with management evidence still controversial. He et al. (2025; DOI 10.3390/cancers17203314; PMCID PMC12564389; source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports direct M0/M1/M2 prediction in 437 complete-data resections using contrast MRI radiomics and laboratories, including 67 M2 cases and a 132-patient second-campus cohort. Huang et al. (2026; DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034; source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports a binary-MVI CT-margin/AFP model in 487 development and 256 external-validation patients (AUC 0.740/0.781), with blinded raw-image review and seven-point pathology sampling. Li et al. (2026; DOI 10.1186/s12957-026-04229-2; PMCID PMC13005532; source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports M2 associated with recurrence and mortality in 243 complete-follow-up R0 resections under seven-point sampling and two-pathologist grading. None validates routine stored-report semantics, this snapshot's documentary M2, decision-time report visibility, a capacity-fixed queue, or clinical benefit.

The expert demonstrations were inspected as methodological exemplars only. The full natural-history and Bayesian articles support attention to temporal validation, calibration, uncertainty, and selection bias. The cancer article's main text and STAR Methods remain unavailable; only its publisher supplement and bibliographic metadata were inspected, and no claim here relies on unavailable text.

**Primary stored-content hypothesis P.** Under the locked 2020–2021 union frame, absolute review capacities preregistered from an external service contract, and maximal exposure of every selected nonempty eventual CT/MRI body, strategy S will capture more adjudicated-or-worst-case documentary-M2 cases per 100 identical review slots than both (i) its same-fit semantic ablation S0 and (ii) independently optimized structured/basic-report baseline B. For each contrast, the simultaneous lower confidence limit must exceed the preregistered material margin `delta` additional M2 captures per slot.

**Pattern-access hypothesis A.** Conditional on P, the registered minimum access vector for CT-only, MRI-only, and both modality-specific components of dual-bundle patients lies in the exact robust support set H: under every coherent source/outcome state and every report mask meeting those minima, the same fixed-capacity gains remain above `delta`.

**Conditional workflow claim W.** W requires separate representative evidence that the external capacity really exists and that the exact report versions scored were final/released and preferably viewed by `t_dec`. Neither fact is identified in HCC. Even supportive P, A, and W establish historical information allocation/conditional workflow feasibility—not improved decisions, treatment benefit, or patient outcomes.

The substantive advance over the parents is a single decision-scale synthesis. It replaces a risk-threshold net-benefit estimand with an externally auditable fixed-slot queue while preserving exclusive CT/MRI bundle patterns. It fixes (1) percentage capacities that changed with state-specific denominators, (2) fractional final slots, (3) an accession split that manufactured visibility units, (4) a pooled or “any dual report” access coordinate that hid CT/MRI composition, (5) a sorted additive frontier invalid after top-K ranking, (6) non-monotone pointwise confidence bands, and (7) the impossible claim that H could be empty after the identical all-visible endpoint supported P.

## Externally preregistered capacity contract

Before pathology grades, semantic extracts, test-period counts, fitted scores, or outcomes are inspected, the service must register:

1. calendar/service cycles `b` (for example monthly), an **absolute integer** added-review budget `K_b` for each cycle, and `K_+=sum_b K_b`;
2. the existing MDT pathway, recipients, eligibility, slot fungibility, maximum acceptable queue, reserved urgent capacity, and whether an unfilled slot may move across cycles (default: no);
3. available reviewer-person minutes, the audited distribution of added minutes per reviewed case, and a conservative workload rule showing that every `K_b` is feasible;
4. the clinically material yield increment `delta` (additional documentary-M2 captures per review slot), false-review burden considered acceptable, and named stakeholders approving it;
5. audit dates, source-system provenance, missing-log handling, and a protocol hash proving that `K_b` and `delta` predate outcome access.

No fractional slot exists and no capacity is recomputed as a percentage of a terminal state's eligible denominator. Every model must fill exactly `K_b` slots among eligible patients in cycle b. If any admissible state has fewer than `K_b` eligible patients, that cycle and the primary contract are infeasible/inconclusive; neither `K_b` nor the population may be changed after seeing this.

The configured data contain no such audit. If it remains absent, locked computational sensitivities use `q in {0.05,0.10,0.20}`: `K_b(q)=floor(q*|U_b|+0.5)` with exact .5 rounded upward, where `U_b` is the outcome-blind union of patients with any admissible state in cycle b. These integers are frozen once from U and remain unchanged across terminal states, and their locked research margin is `delta=0.05` additional documentary-M2 captures per slot (five per 100 slots). They are research benchmarks, not observed service capacity; workload and primary clinical-contract conclusions remain inconclusive.

## Models, same-fit ablation, and queues

Fit interpretable elastic-net logistic models using a patient-wise worst-admissible-state convex loss in 2015–2018; apply the same state rule for 2019 tuning/recalibration, semantic-null refits, and every bootstrap.

- `Z`: age, sex, CT/MRI acquisition indicators, bundle age/count, empty-body indicator, and development-frequency machine category. No identifier, year, diagnosis/pathology/procedure text, laboratory feature, or post-cutoff content.
- `R_core`, present only when its whole bundle is exposed: frozen maximum lesion diameter, lesion-count category, and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown/not-mentioned coding.
- Independent `B = Z + R_core`: fit, tune, and recalibrate from scratch without the richer semantic block.
- `S = Z + R_core + C_R`, where `C_R` is a frozen present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned block for capsule complete/interrupted/absent, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity.
- Same-fit `S0`: never refit. Use S's exact coefficients, intercept, preprocessing, penalty, and recalibration; replace only `C_R` by its frozen masked-state vector. When no semantic bundle is exposed, S and S0 scores, ranks, and queues must be bitwise identical.

Two blinded radiologists validate extracted report features by year, modality, and stored pattern. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in >=90%, count agreement >=90% with kappa >=0.80, and PPV/sensitivity >=0.85 for each binary feature in every pattern used for a modality-specific claim.

Within every service cycle b and terminal state, each model ranks eligible patients by its state-specific score. Bitwise-equal scores use descending `SHA256(snapshot_id || protocol_hash || patient_key)`; the salt/protocol hash is frozen before outcomes, and no outcome enters ties. Take exactly the first `K_b`. The same absolute `K_b` applies to S, S0, B, and an outcome-blind hash-only no-feature queue. Random queues use 1,000 frozen seeds as descriptive references. Flag-all and flag-none are reported only as workload-incomparable reference actions; they cannot establish a fixed-capacity superiority claim.

For terminal state/mask r and outcome assignment y, let `Q_Mib(r)` identify membership in model M's top-`K_b` queue and
`Cap_M(r,y)=sum_b sum_i y_i Q_Mib(r)`.
The primary fixed-denominator contrasts are

`D_K=(Cap_S-Cap_S0)/K_+` and `C_K=(Cap_S-Cap_B)/K_+`.

Also report absolute captured-case differences, each model's `Cap_M`, queue precision `Cap_M/K_+`, false-review count `K_+-Cap_M`, and state-specific recall `Cap_M/P(s,y)`. Recall's positive denominator varies and is never substituted for the fixed primary denominator.

## Exact four-coordinate access frontier

Use `h=(h_C^CT,h_M^MR,h_CM^CT,h_CM^MR)` on the preregistered grid `{0,0.1,...,1}^4`. The first two coordinates concern the disjoint CT-only and MRI-only strata; the last two separately guarantee CT and MRI access among dual-bundle patients. No independence or overlap assumption is made.

For every joint terminal assignment z, define state-specific pattern counts `N_C(z),N_M(z),N_CM(z)` and exposed counts `E_C^CT,E_M^MR,E_CM^CT,E_CM^MR`. A mask satisfies h exactly when

- `E_C^CT >= h_C^CT N_C`,
- `E_M^MR >= h_M^MR N_M`,
- `E_CM^CT >= h_CM^CT N_CM`,
- `E_CM^MR >= h_CM^MR N_CM`.

Because counts are integer, these are exactly the corresponding ceiling-count requirements without changing queue capacity; they remain linear despite variable pattern denominators. O contributes its fixed masked scores and outcomes to every queue optimization and is never removed. If a pattern is absent in every admissible state, its coordinate is structurally not applicable; if present but sparse (`N_g<100` or <20 V=1 M2 in the reference state), the global queue result remains computable but modality-specific interpretation is inconclusive.

For each h and contrast j, solve the lower endpoint
`theta_j(h)=min_{coherent z,y,mask satisfying h} j_K`
and corresponding upper endpoint by maximization. This is not the parent's sorted per-patient cost algorithm: top-K ranks couple all patients, so additive sorting is invalid.

The exact state-expanded mixed-integer program chooses one reachable joint state per patient; enforces one eligible cycle, access ratios, unknown-label alternatives, and exactly `K_b` selected patients per model/cycle; and encodes top-K membership from ordinal ranks of every possible `(float64 score, frozen tie hash)` pair. A chosen patient is above a cycle/model threshold iff queued. Require zero integrality gap, replayed queues, and equality with exhaustive enumeration for every fixture with N<=12. The supplied six-patient oracle enumerated 768 joint assignments, 81 four-coordinate points and 324 endpoints with variable C denominators, exact K=2 queues, outcome-blind ties, and zero coordinate-monotonicity violations.

Because each coordinate is an **at least** constraint, increasing h shrinks the feasible mask set, so every robust lower endpoint is coordinatewise nondecreasing. Use a common unstudentized simultaneous max-deviation critical value across all grid points; subtracting one common value preserves this order. Any remaining numerical violation is an error, not something to repair by cherry-picking. Define
`H={h: L_D(h)>delta and L_C(h)>delta}`
after all gates. H is upward closed; report its complete Pareto-minimal antichain and all endpoints. The all-visible vector `(1,1,1,1)` is exactly P's access endpoint, so supportive P mathematically requires `(1,1,1,1) in H`; “P supportive but H empty” is a verifier failure, not an adverse scientific result.

## External report-version/access audit and joint uncertainty

A conditional W claim requires a representative source-system audit from the same era/workflow using the identical population rules, `t_dec`, three-key accession bundle, component-time interval, modality map, latest-bundle rule, and O/C/M/CM definitions. For every selected bundle, retrieve authored/final/release time, preferably clinician-view time, and an immutable version ID or content hash. The scored body must exactly match the version proven available by `t_dec`; if it differs, re-extract and rescore that audited version under the frozen model. Acquisition/order time, a later nonempty body, or accession match without version identity is insufficient.

Estimate the four access proportions and their joint lower/upper bounds with patient clustering, service-cycle/calendar stratification, missing logs, and repeat examinations. Preserve observed CT/MRI overlap in CM. Round lower bounds down and upper bounds up to the 0.1 frontier grid.

Use three prospectively separated error families: internal model/frontier inference at one-sided simultaneous 97.5% confidence (`alpha_M=.025`), access-audit bounds at simultaneous 98.75% (`alpha_A=.0125`), and any sampled capacity/workload quantities at simultaneous 98.75% (`alpha_K=.0125`). Bonferroni gives joint noncoverage <=5%; deterministic scheduled slots need no sampling interval but not representativeness. W is conditionally supported only when the capacity audit passes and the access lower vector lies in H. If the access upper vector lies outside upward-closed H, access is adversely incompatible. Lower outside but upper inside is inconclusive/mixed access evidence. Missing audit, nonrepresentativeness, missing version identity, or unit mismatch is inconclusive.

## Exact result zones and permitted conclusions

Only after every validity gate passes, classify each primary contrast j in {D,C}: **supportive** if `L_j>delta`; **adverse/non-material** if `U_j<=delta` (and harmful if `U_j<=0`); otherwise **unresolved**. A valid external capacity contract is an interpretation gate for primary P. Without it, apply these classes separately to each locked q benchmark and label them stored-content sensitivity results; primary P and W remain inconclusive.

- **Supportive stored-content P:** D and C are both supportive and the semantic-null test passes. Conclude only that, at identical fixed slots, eventual stored CT/MRI semantics selected materially more documentary-M2 cases than the same-fit semantic mask and independently fitted B in this historical frame.
- **Adverse P:** the null test fails, or at least one contrast is adverse and neither is supportive. State which contrast is non-material/harmful. All stored content could not establish the conjunction.
- **Mixed P:** with the null passed, one contrast is supportive and the other adverse. D supportive/C adverse means incremental semantic action changes do not beat B; C supportive/D adverse means independently refitted strategy differences, not runtime richer semantics, may explain value. Do not recommend S. A decisive sign reversal across locked capacity/buffer sensitivities is also reported as sensitivity-mixed without changing the primary label.
- **Inconclusive P:** any validity gate fails, the external capacity contract is absent, or—when the null passes—no contrast is adverse and at least one is unresolved (supportive+unresolved or unresolved+unresolved). An adverse+unresolved pair is adverse to the conjunction under the preceding rule. Positive AUC, point estimates, complete-case labels, an unrestricted text ceiling, or an isolated h point cannot rescue it.
- **Access/conditional W:** H and its antichain are computable stored-content requirements, not observed access. With valid capacity and version audits, lower access in H supports only conditional historical workflow feasibility; upper access outside H is adverse access evidence; overlapping bounds are inconclusive. Without either audit, W is inconclusive regardless of P. No result establishes reviewer acceptability, clinical action, treatment efficacy, survival, cost-effectiveness, or patient benefit.
