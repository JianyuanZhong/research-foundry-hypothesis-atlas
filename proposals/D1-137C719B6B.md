## 3. Exact estimands and hypothesis

For configuration z=(year, source world, reader pair, full-roster qualifying mask, anchored deletion), let N be the binary-outcome roster, E its documentary-M2 cases, K the frozen quota, h_M positives among policy M's exact top K, and f_M=K-h_M. For p in [0,1):

`NB_M(p)=[h_M-f_M p/(1-p)]/N=[h_M-Kp]/[N(1-p)]`.

Thus G beats no review iff `p<h_G/K`; equality fails strict support. G beats capacity-matched random K iff `N h_G>K E`, independent of p. Against equal-K C, the sign depends only on h_G-h_C; retain the parent's clinically material `100(h_G-h_C)/K>5` for C in {B,R,Gmask}.

For year y define sharp extrema over every z:

- `delta_C,y=min_z 100(h_G-h_C)/K`;
- `epsilon_rand,y=min_z (N h_G-K E)/(K N)`, excess top-K fraction over prevalence;
- `p_safe,y=min_z h_G/K`;
- `B_max,y=max_z (K-h_G)/h_G`, +infinity if h_G=0.

When p_safe>0, `B_max=(1-p_safe)/p_safe`. The exact universal preference set favoring G over no review is `[0,p_safe)`.

**Primary threshold-free hypothesis:** separately in 2020 and 2021, every delta_C>5 and epsilon_rand>0. One attained configuration satisfying `20(h_G-h_C)<=K` or `N h_G<=K E` falsifies it. Simultaneously estimate p_safe and B_max without calling them clinically acceptable.

For a later preregistered clinically reasonable `P_clin=[p_L,p_U]`, universal action compatibility requires the primary to pass and `p_U<min_y p_safe,y`. Equality fails. If p_L is at/above the minimum frontier, label range-adverse; if the interval straddles it, preference-heterogeneous. Without a valid interval, `clinical_action_status=bridge_pending`. The inherited 10/15/20/25/30% points are descriptive landmarks only.

## 8. Disjoint results and conclusions

Emit one HCC label:

1. `computationally_inconclusive` if not Q;
2. `patient_sufficiency_infeasible` if Q and not A;
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse` if Q/A and comparator/random fails under all-exposable content;
