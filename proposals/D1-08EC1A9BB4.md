# Episode 96 successor: stable documentary-M2 validation after findings-only semantic quarantine

Parent: [prior hypothesis].

## 1. Clinical question and substantive advance

The narrow decision is whether a scarce preoperative multidisciplinary review queue should prioritize adults approaching a first eligible HCC liver resection when the historical question is whether the eventual resection pathology documents high-grade microvascular invasion (documentary M2). The action under study is allocation of a fixed number of review positions. It is not a recommendation for resection, transplantation, embolization, ablation, systemic therapy, surveillance, or any patient treatment.

The parent established a source-attributed, no-refit replay in which semantic features sourced wholly from the examination findings field `Examination Findings` are retained and features sourced from the diagnosis/impression field `Examination Diagnosis`, mixed fields, or unresolved provenance are masked. Its unresolved limitation is endpoint interpretability: a queue can appear successful against a maximum-content documentary label because one pathology reader/book called a case M2, even when qualified books disagree. That would leave two different next studies: a report/feature-lifecycle audit versus pathology adjudication. The current HCC release cannot decide biological MVI, specimen adequacy, report release or clinician action.

This successor tests whether the findings-only allocation property survives a predeclared, stricter endpoint requiring pathology-book agreement. The advance is a decomposition of two threats that the parent leaves coupled:

1. dependence on conclusion/impression text; and
2. dependence on a reader-specific documentary pathology label.

A positive stable-endpoint result would make the finite-corpus queue claim more useful for selecting cases for prospective radiology reread and pathology adjudication. It would still not establish that the signal is image-derived, available before surgery, clinically correct, acted upon, or beneficial.

## 2. Evidence and unresolved hypothesis

The strongest HCC-supported claim before this experiment is only that the source contains separate examination findings and diagnosis fields, a time-like examination start, whole-accession identifiers, and multiple pathology narrative records joinable by patient and encounter. A complete source audit found 419,996 examination rows and 392,854 nonblank accession keys; among accessions, 20,611 had findings without diagnosis, 39,462 had diagnosis without findings, and 332,653 had both. This supports feasibility of a field quarantine, not report availability or diagnostic truth. Pathology has no usable time, specimen/block/slide identifier, or examination accession.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> At the parent’s frozen threshold (q^*), the findings-only score (G_F), with no refitting or recalibration, retains a clinically material finite-census advantage (strictly greater than 5 documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed review positions) over every inherited comparator under a stricter stable-documentary-M2 endpoint, in every coupled source/onset/reader/pathology world and in both untouched test years 2020 and 2021.

The design also predeclares the adverse alternative: the parent’s apparent gain is not robust to pathology-book agreement, even if it remains positive for the maximum-content endpoint. That result would direct the next evidence investment toward pathology adjudication rather than report-semantic deployment.

A pass supports only a finite-census stable-documentary-M2 allocation statement. Interpretation precedence:

1. computationally inconclusive for any source/schema/hash, ordinal/byte replay, join, manifest, score, fixed-capacity, coupled-world, book-registry, or output-linkage failure;
