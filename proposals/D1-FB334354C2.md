## 0. Hypothesis vs conclusion (what is already supported, what is untested)

**Already supported (NOT re-tested here):** SHR / relative glycemia is *associated* with
short-term mortality in selected ICU cohorts. HbA1c is *selectively* measured in EHR/ICU data,
and the **presence** of a laboratory order is prognostic independently of the value [K2].
HbA1c-as-chronic-glycemia degrades with renal impairment [K3]. These are settled descriptive
regularities/measurement facts.

**Untested claim this experiment tries to FAIL (H-relative):** at the same absolute admission
glucose `G_adm` and the same severity, the *relative* (acute-on-chronic) glycemia component
`RG = log(G_adm) − log(Gc)` carries incremental 28-day-mortality information that is
(i) not attributable to `G_adm`, and (ii) not produced by same-stay HbA1c measurement
selection or HbA1c measurement invalidity. Read **descriptively/predictively**: this tests
incremental prognostic information and separability, **not mechanism or causation**.

**Leading rivals** (must be discriminated, not assumed away): R-select (measurement selection
/ informative observation), R-validity (HbA1c invalid under renal/anemia/transfusion states),
R-absolute (everything already in `G_adm`).

## 1. Predeclared artifact signature (negative control FIRST) — unchanged

If the apparent independent `RG` effect is a **measurement-selection / informative-observation
artifact** rather than signal, the predeclared signature (BEFORE fitting) is:

- **S1 (order/label permutation).** Permute HbA1c-derived `Gc` across subjects (keeping the
  observed instrumentation and the set of measured labs fixed) and refit M1→M2. Under a
  genuine value signal the increment must COLLAPSE to the equivalence margin. Under R-select
  the increment is driven by *who got measured* [K2], so a **negative-control EXPOSURE =
  measured-HbA1c indicator / chronic-anchor-availability indicator alone** (no value) should
  reproduce a *materially similar* M1→M2 increment to the value-carrying `RG`. Predeclared
  sign: **positive** (measured/anchor-available ⇒ higher observed risk through observation
  intensity).
- **S2 (magnitude equivalence).** If the artifact explanation is correct, the M1→M2 McFadden
  ΔR² from the *measurement-only* negative control lies within the equivalence band of the
  ΔR² from the *value* `RG` (both CIs inside ±band). If the value `RG` exceeds the
  measurement-only control by more than the margin, the artifact explanation is disfavored.
- **S3 (negative-control OUTCOME).** A prespecified outcome not plausibly caused by glycemia
  (bounded composite; §4) must show no `RG` association; a non-null `RG`→NCO association is
  predeclared as evidence of residual confounding/selection.

## 3. Primary estimand (M0→M1 incremental information, with equivalence margin) — unchanged

- **M0** = `G_adm` + severity + age/sex (+ admission_type).
- **M1** = M0 + `RG` (separable log-ratio term).
- **Estimand**: ΔAUC(M0→M1) and ΔMcFadden-R²(M0→M1), each with bootstrap 95% CI, plus the
  adjusted `RG` coefficient. **Predeclared equivalence/non-inferiority margin: ΔAUC ≤ 0.005
  and the `RG` coefficient CI inside ±0.05 per unit** constitute a *declared null of no
  incremental information* — an INFORMATIVE NEGATIVE, not an inconclusive one.
- **M2** = M1 + HbA1c-measurement/care-intensity propensity (IPW or covariate).
  **M3** = negative-control exposure and negative-control outcome.
- **Decisive contrast**: the M1→M2 change is the R-select discriminator; the M1→M2 Δ vs the
  M0→M1 Δ is the object claimed as the "substantive unresolved".

## 6. Method exploration on the SAME question (B0 vs A1)

Both alternatives answer the **identical** estimand (§3) on the **identical** population and
split (§2). The scientific question they must separate is not "which predicts 0.002 AUC
better" but **whether the value-carrying `RG` increment is distinguishable from the
value-free measurement/observation increment** (R-select) — a discrimination B0 cannot make.
The now-observed N (8,892 same-stay exposed; 686 deaths) is large enough for both to be fit
within budget, so A1 is not deferred for sample size.

- **B0 — interpretable penalized logistic (baseline).**
  - *Inputs*: M0/M1 design matrices from §2 (G_adm, RG, age, sex, admission_type, severity,
    renal/anemia/transfusion/diabetes flags).
  - *Fitting target*: 28-day mortality; ridge/elastic-net logistic; adjusted ORs for `G_adm`
    and `RG`; restricted cubic spline for shape.
  - *Split*: locked earliest-70% / latest-30% by `intime`.
  - *Uncertainty / baseline comparison*: bootstrap 95% CI for ΔAUC and ΔMcFadden-R²
    (M0→M1), read against the predeclared equivalence margin; B0 is the reference against
    which A1's value-vs-selection separation is judged.
  - *Resource*: CPU, <1 h, <8 GB (estimate; unverified for this branch).
- **A1 — joint latent measurement-selection + outcome (Heckman/EM-style) model (alternative).**
  - *Inputs*: the same columns, **plus** a selection equation on the observed HbA1c/anchor-
    availability indicator (measurement propensity from care-intensity covariates:
    admission_type, severity, renal/anemia flags, prior-utilization proxies).
  - *Fitting target*: a two-equation latent-variable model — selection (measured/anchored)
    coupled to the mortality outcome — fit by EM or as inverse-Mills-ratio-augmented logistic;
    reports a selection-corrected `RG` coefficient and the explicit contribution of the
    probability-of-measurement term on the same scale.
  - *Split / uncertainty*: same locked split; bootstrap for the selection-corrected `RG`
    coefficient CI and for the partial increment.
  - *What A1 reveals that B0 loses*: B0 conditions on selection by covariate adjustment and
    assumes missingness is captured by observed covariates; A1 models the latent selection
    process explicitly, so it can separate "value `RG` predicts death" from
    "probability-of-measurement predicts death" — attacking the R-select rival directly and
    yielding a selection-corrected `RG` estimate B0 cannot identify.
  - *Resource*: CPU, ≤1 h, <8 GB (estimate). Fallback if EM fails to converge is the
    inverse-Mills-ratio-augmented logistic on the same locked split.
- **Selection / deferral reason.** B0 is the primary, auditable equivalence-margin test; A1 is
  retained because the decisive contrast (value vs measurement increment) is exactly the
  R-select discriminator, and A1 is the only one of the two that can identify it. A1's
  identifiability depends on the selection equation's covariate set, not on N; if EM does not
  converge, the inverse-Mills-ratio fallback is used and that choice is documented rather than
  silently replaced by a smaller model.

## 7. What each outcome means (supportive / adverse / inconclusive) — unchanged

- **Supportive (H-relative):** M0→M1 CIs exclude the equivalence band with the expected sign;
  negative-control EXPOSURE far below the value increment; NCO null; strata homogeneous.
  → relative glycemia adds separable prognostic information.
- **Adverse (R-select / R-validity / R-absolute):** M0→M1 within the equivalence band
  (informative negative) OR the measurement-only negative control reproduces the increment
  OR the NCO is non-null OR the effect drifts across anemia/transfusion/renal strata
  (R-validity). → the "independent relative-glycemia" claim is **disfavored**; SHR should not
  be deployed as a stratification/enrichment marker on this evidence.
- **Inconclusive:** power-gate INCONCLUSIVE band, or a `RG` interval crossing the margin.
  → no scientific claim; report design and state the required evidence.
