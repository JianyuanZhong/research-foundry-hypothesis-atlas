# Proposal: history-conditioned meaning of concordant post-TACE laboratory pathways

## Episode, parent, and actual scientific deliverable

This Episode-29 proposal is a substantive child of assessed-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]`, selected by the Lead as the highest-TrueSkill assessed HCC design. It preserves the parent’s encounter-constrained first timed TACE-like index, all-index early-transition and local-observation-loss accounting, L=14/L=45 information boundaries, component-resolved AFP/albumin/total-bilirubin profile, strict procedure-plus-order concordance endpoint, procedure-only and ambiguous competing states, and recorded-care-only interpretation.

The new unresolved issue is treatment-history effect modification. The earliest liver-directed episode recorded locally is not necessarily the patient’s first treatment ever. A patient with no earlier local liver-directed procedure may be entering an initial treatment course, while a patient with earlier local procedures may be receiving retreatment or a recurrent course. The same early laboratory trajectory may therefore have different meaning for subsequent recorded care. The parent makes the endpoint more auditable, but it does not test whether its trajectory contrast is transportable across this clinically important, partially observed history.

The future solver must newly produce:

1. An index/history manifest for every eligible patient, including locally recorded prior liver-directed procedure burden, time since last prior episode, history category, and an explicit “unknown outside history” interpretation.
2. L=14 and L=45 component trajectory manifests and all-index early-transition manifests, with no silent exclusion of early events or missing laboratory observations.
3. Strict concordant C2, procedure-only C1, broad/ambiguous C0, diagnostic/technical, non-liver and local-observation-loss outcomes with source-concordance audit.
4. A primary history-by-profile interaction estimate for the first post-boundary C2 recorded episode, plus pathway-specific cumulative incidence and calibration/log-score outputs.
5. Matched transparent additive and interaction competing-risk fits and a substantive hierarchical irregular-time latent multi-source alternative, all on the same cohort, target, split and uncertainty procedure.
6. Locked predictions, patient-level uncertainty intervals, overlap/positivity diagnostics, negative-control and permutation results, and `interpretation.json` mapping each conclusion to an output, endpoint tier, time boundary, split and criterion.

Completion is a newly fitted/estimated comparison and an evidence-bounded interpretation, not a presumed supportive result. A null interaction is a valuable result if the design has adequate support and uncertainty; it does not establish that treatment history is complete or that the laboratory profile measures biological response.

## Strongest supported evidence and unresolved claim

The strongest available evidence is operational rather than clinical. The frozen HCC snapshot is a set of ordinary read-only CSVs. The raw audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 106,963 rows matching a broad TACE-like lexical screen, 105,044 encounters, 1,810,646 diagnoses with 344,383 rows containing the HCC name, and 16,730,319 non-drug orders. Procedure records contain `procedure`, `start time`, `end time` and `procedure source`; encounters contain age, sex and three encounter timestamps; labs contain named assays and measurement timestamps; orders contain exact order/open/start/end timestamps. These facts establish that the proposed records and joins exist. They do not establish treatment completion, treatment intent, tumor response, recurrence, progression, survival, or causal treatment benefit.

The parent’s strongest supported design claim is that a stricter time-concordant procedure-plus-order evidence tier can be compared with procedure-only and ambiguous recorded-care tiers using available dated sources. The unresolved claim tested here is narrower and new:

> Does the association between a favorable early AFP/hepatic-function profile and a later concordant locally recorded therapeutic episode differ by prior locally recorded liver-directed treatment history, after standardizing pre-index disease/care and observation opportunity?

Primary directional hypothesis, stated before fitting:

> Among adults with an encounter-linked structured HCC diagnosis and a first eligible locally recorded TACE-like episode, a favorable early profile (AFP decrease on the log1p scale with non-worsening albumin and total bilirubin within assay-defined directions) will have a different 320-day predictive contrast for C2 in the locally history-naive group (no earlier eligible local liver-directed episode) versus the locally previously treated group (one or more earlier eligible episodes), with the interaction remaining after capture adjustment and being weaker or absent for unrelated non-liver recorded-care endpoints.

This is a prognostic/measurement hypothesis about a locally recorded pathway, not a causal treatment-effect hypothesis. The primary interaction is the difference-in-differences of standardized C2 risks between favorable and discordant profiles across the two history strata. The design margin is a 3 percentage-point difference in the history-specific profile contrast at 320 days, with a patient-level 95% interval excluding zero for the interaction; this is a prespecified decision threshold, not an expected or observed effect.

Clinical importance: a single laboratory-triggered reassessment rule can be misleading if an apparently favorable profile predicts different subsequent care in treatment-naive-proxy and previously-treated-proxy patients. A stable interaction would justify prospective adjudication of history-aware reassessment strategies; a null interaction with good overlap would support a simpler common rule for future validation. A strong association only in C1/C0, or only in observation-rich patients, would redirect attention to coding and capture rather than disease-specific monitoring.

## Population, history, index and temporal boundaries

Use one first eligible index episode per patient.

1. Read `procedures` and create primary 24-hour within-patient/within-encounter episodes from valid `Start Time`. The strict TACE-like lexical rule is literal `TACE`, `transarterial chemoembolization`, or both `hepatic artery` and `embolization`; hepatic angiography alone is not therapeutic. Preserve all tied rows, source labels and invalid/missing-time rows in an audit. Use the encounter-constrained earliest timed episode as t0.
2. Link the index to `encounters` on (`Patient master index`, `Encounter number`). Require age >=18 and nonmissing `Sex`. Require a `diagnoses` row with `Diagnosis name` containing `hepatocellular carcinoma`, linked to an encounter whose `Encounter time` lies in [t0-180 days, t0+7 days]. Because `diagnoses` has no diagnosis timestamp, this is an encounter-time HCC ascertainment proxy, not disease onset.
3. Require t0 <= 2025-01-01. Do not infer death, survival, outside care or discharge as an outcome.
4. Define prior local history using all valid timed procedure episodes strictly before t0 for the same patient, with exact treatment-ontology labels frozen on training data. Primary categories are: locally history-naive-proxy (zero earlier eligible liver-directed episode), locally previously treated-proxy (>=1 earlier eligible liver-directed episode), and indeterminate/unknown (invalid timing, unresolved mixed label, or history information insufficient for the primary ontology). Report counts and do not call the first category treatment-naive.
5. The primary ontology includes prior strict TACE-like episodes; a secondary overlay includes other clearly liver-directed procedure labels (resection, ablation, transplant, radiotherapy or other frozen liver-directed strings) as a sensitivity. Mixed/compound rows remain auditable and are not silently forced into a clinical treatment sequence.
6. Include every eligible index patient in the all-index population, including early post-index events, incomplete trajectories and no later local record. The parent day-45 continuity risk set (no primary-ontology liver-directed episode through day 45) remains a secondary comparison only; the primary full-index analysis retains early states as absorbing competing transitions.
7. Use information boundaries L=14 and L=45. For each L, use the last eligible numeric pre-index component value and the first eligible numeric post-index value in days 7-L. Freeze exact assay names `Alpha-Fetoprotein`, `Albumin`, and `Total Bilirubin` in training only. Apply assay-specific within-assay direction rules; do not pool undocumented units or name the profile ALBI/MELD. Preserve qualitative results, inequalities, specimen type, exact delay, counts and missingness. All predictors stop at L.
8. For post-boundary outcomes, begin follow-up after L and end at day 365 after t0; report 30, 90 and 320-day cumulative incidence. The all-index multi-state analysis models first transitions from t0 through day 45, including C2/C1/C0/diagnostic/non-liver/observation-loss states.

## Outcomes and observation safeguards

Construct mutually exclusive first post-boundary recorded states:

- C2 primary: a strict TACE-like procedure episode with valid `procedure start time` linked on the same patient/encounter keys to a non-drug order whose `non-drug order` passes the same training-frozen TACE-like order screen, with `order time` or `start time` within -24/+24 hours of the procedure episode time. This is corroborating recorded evidence, not proof of intent, completion or therapeutic success.
- C1: strict timed TACE-like procedure episode without a qualifying same-encounter order.
- C0: timed broad/ambiguous/mixed liver-directed episode not meeting strict criteria.
- Separate diagnostic/technical-only, non-liver and local-observation-loss states as competing states.
- Secondary workflow-supported tier: C2 plus a liver/upper-abdominal examination from `examinations` in the preceding 30 days, screened only by `examination` and `start time`. `examination findings` and `examination diagnosis` are narrative sensitivity evidence only, not adjudication.

Aggregate each child source before joins to prevent many-to-many expansion. Use same-encounter matching primarily; cross-encounter matching is a sensitivity. Repeat order windows +/-6, +/-24 and +/-72 hours, exact observed `order status` sensitivities, and a no-canceled/status-restricted sensitivity only when the observed values support a prespecified rule. Do not use the order contributing C2 evidence as a predictor for that C2 endpoint.

History is measured only in local procedure records. Outside treatment, complete lifetime history, treatment indication, technical success, radiologic response and clinician intent are unavailable. This is why the interaction is explicitly history-stratified local-record evidence, not a biological treatment-history effect.

## Estimand, model comparison and evaluation

For history stratum g in {locally history-naive-proxy, locally previously treated-proxy, indeterminate}, profile a in {favorable, discordant, unavailable}, boundary L in {14,45}, endpoint k and horizon h, estimate:

`Delta^L_{k,g}(h) = P(first recorded state k by h | profile=favorable, g, L, standardized pre-index history/capture) - P(first recorded state k by h | profile=discordant, g, L, standardized pre-index history/capture)`.

The primary interaction is `Delta^L_C2,previously-treated(320) - Delta^L_C2,history-naive(320)`, with the indeterminate stratum reported rather than discarded. Report absolute risk differences first and ratios only when denominators are stable. The all-index estimand is the vector of early transition probabilities including observation loss. No estimand is causal or a biological response.

Use a patient-level temporal split: 2010-2022 fit, 2023 tuning, 2024-2025-01-01 locked test when support allows; otherwise deterministic patient-hash split declared before fitting. No patient crosses splits. Fit the exact lexical vocabularies, assay mappings, history ontology, thresholds, interaction specification, standardization/weights, common-support rules and sparsity handling on training only. Use patient-clustered bootstrap or patient-level resampling for intervals, with stratified support counts and effective sample size.

Transparent baseline B_add is a regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazard/multi-state model with main effects for demographics, index encounter, diagnosis proxy, pre-index procedure burden/timing/history category, pre-index capture intensity, medication/order/examination availability, component profile, exact delays/counts/inequality flags/missingness, and early transitions. It has no profile-by-history interaction in the baseline; it estimates the additive common-rule contrast.

Transparent B_int adds only the prespecified profile-by-history interaction and reports standardized history-specific contrasts, calibration, Brier score and dynamic log score. Incremental value is judged by the interaction estimate and its uncertainty, not by a small generic prediction gain. Report event counts and common-support gates before interpreting a stratum.

M_latent is a hierarchical irregular-time multi-source model with shared history-stratum parameters and partial pooling of profile effects. It represents latent recorded therapeutic evidence as generating procedure, same-encounter order and optional workflow-examination observations with source-specific timing kernels, missingness/capture intensity and weakly informative priors or penalization. It couples the evidence process to competing-transition hazards and the component trajectory, and is fit only on the same patient split/target. It can reveal whether the history interaction is robust to graded source discordance and irregular measurement timing, information lost by hard C2/C1/C0 labels. It is scientifically useful only if it converges, improves held-out source calibration/log score or materially clarifies uncertainty, and yields stable interaction intervals under prior sensitivity. It cannot validate the latent state as clinical response.

Compute choice: CPU is the default because the cohort manifests and transparent fits are tabular; M_latent is a modest repeated likelihood fit. The future solver planning envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB memory and 28,800 seconds, inherited as a planning limit from the assessed parent, not a measured runtime. The raw audit was measured on ordinary CPU; no full model was fitted in discovery, so model runtime and latent convergence are unverified. GPU is optional, not a merit criterion: if a solver benchmarks a repeated latent fit and requests one allocated A100, it must use `cuda:0`; ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not evidence. If CPU/GPU infrastructure or convergence fails, report it and preserve the transparent analysis rather than changing the scientific question.

## Falsification, interpretation, and evidence limits

Required checks:

1. Reproduce parent C2/C1/C0 continuity, early transitions and source-concordance audit. A failure is a packaging/definition problem.
2. Test the pre-specified profile-by-history interaction on C2, then separately C1, C0, diagnostic/technical and non-liver states. A similar interaction for non-liver states is adverse to disease-specific interpretation.
3. Repeat history ontology with strict TACE-only, broad liver-directed, encounter-only and mixed/indeterminate exclusions. A reversal driven by one lexical rule is adverse or inconclusive.
4. Permute eligible prior-history episode times within patient while preserving labels, and recompute the history strata. A similar interaction after permutation is adverse to a temporal-history interpretation.
5. Permute assay timestamps/values within patient within eligible windows while preserving assay/specimen/count structure; run a pre-index placebo trajectory. Preserved interaction indicates timing/capture artifact.
6. Repeat C2 at +/-6/+/-24/+/-72 hours, same-encounter versus cross-encounter, exact status-restricted and workflow-supported tiers. Instability or no valid matches means ascertainment fragility/inconclusive evidence.
7. Compare all-index and day-45 continuity estimands; retain early actions and observation-loss states. A large discrepancy must be reported as landmark-selection sensitivity.
8. Report laboratory availability, testing delay, number of measurements, local encounter/order/examination capture and inverse-observation-weighted sensitivity. If positivity or effective sample size fails in either history stratum, do not claim an interaction.
9. Require M_latent convergence, posterior/predictive checks, held-out source log score/Brier/calibration and prior-sensitivity intervals. Nonconvergence or prior-dominated interaction is inconclusive and cannot justify the latent model.
10. Lock all output-to-conclusion links. The verifier must test that each conclusion cites computed estimates, intervals, endpoint tier, history category, boundary and split.

Supportive evidence requires adequate support/overlap in both history strata; consistent interaction direction at L=14 and L=45; meaningful C2 contrast beyond B_add and capture-only covariates; weaker/null non-liver and timestamp-placebo interactions; stable ontology/time/status sensitivities; calibrated locked-test predictions; and converged M_latent with prior-robust interaction. This supports only a robust history-conditioned association with locally recorded concordant care and motivates chart/radiology adjudication or prospective history-aware monitoring research.

Adverse evidence includes no C2 interaction while C1/C0 interaction persists; effect concentration in procedure-only, ambiguous, canceled/status-sensitive or observation-rich records; a similar non-liver interaction; timestamp permutation persistence; poor overlap; or reversal across history definitions. These favor coding, workflow, missing outside history or selection explanations.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse C2 cells, no valid concordant matches, insufficient history support, positivity/effective-sample-size failure, unstable lexical classification, missing timestamps, temporal split failure, latent nonidentification/prior domination or intervals crossing the design margin. It does not establish no biological response, no recurrence, no treatment benefit or no value of reassessment.

Computationally checkable claims include paths, schema IDs, raw headers, key aggregation, time windows, vocabulary freezing, history strata, leakage control, transitions/CIFs, predictions, calibration, bootstrap intervals, permutations and links from conclusions to outputs. Clinical adjudication is essential for intent, completion, technical success, imaging/pathology response, viable tumor, recurrence/progression, liver failure, death, survival, benefit, appropriateness, utility, completeness of treatment history and external validity. Those require expert chart/radiology/pathology review, linked mortality/outside-care data, and prospective or external validation.
