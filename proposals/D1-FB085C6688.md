## Strongest supported claim versus unresolved claim

The complete-source execution now supports the computational claim that, under the exact frozen construction and compact pipeline, adding P to B substantially lowered held-out error for both albumin and total bilirubin in the selected complete-triplet population. The 20-resample fixed-prediction bootstrap intervals were positive, both tested fold partitions were positive, and each observed estimate exceeded the 95th percentile of a 10-permutation P-linkage diagnostic.

These bounded diagnostics do not establish stable generalization, clinical utility, event specificity, biological change, toxicity, recovery, safety, benefit, response, or causality. The unresolved claim is whether the assay-specific gains remain reproducible under the full locked resampling, timing, calibration, influence, placebo-clock, observation-selection and weighting diagnostics. Serial persistence and the clinical measurement process are plausible explanations even if the prediction increment is robust.

## Interpretation and falsification branches

The bounded results are supportive of assay-specific incremental prediction under this one compact pipeline: contemporaneous P contains appreciable short-horizon information about the next observed same-assay Y beyond remote B and the compact core. Because the bootstrap resamples already-computed out-of-fold errors rather than refitting the full pipeline, because there were only 20 resamples, two partitions and ten permutations, and because timing/placebo/influence/selection/calibration analyses remain unresolved, the scientific result remains provisionally supportive rather than definitive.

- Supportive after full diagnostics: positive assay-specific held-out improvement with full-pipeline uncertainty, calibration improvement, non-dominant influence, stable locked timing selectors, adequate overlap/ESS, and no comparable pre-event placebo gain. Conclude only incremental prediction among selected, observed repeat-TACE episodes.
- Adverse: adding P worsens or fails to improve held-out prediction, the gain collapses with full resampling/selection adjustment, or is attributable to a few observations, one selector, baseline extremes, or comparable pre-event predictability. Conclude no robust incremental information under the frozen design or that serial persistence/measurement selection is a sufficient explanation.
- Inconclusive: broad or sign-crossing full-pipeline uncertainty, sparse fold support, unstable weights, influential cases, timing reversal, failed reconstruction, non-estimable placebo, or absent unit/adjudication evidence. Do not widen windows, pool assays, or substitute endpoints.

All held-out folds met the inherited minimum support gate: albumin had 8 systemic and 23–24 comparator rows per fold; bilirubin had 8–9 systemic and 23–24 comparator rows. This does not eliminate small-sample optimism.
