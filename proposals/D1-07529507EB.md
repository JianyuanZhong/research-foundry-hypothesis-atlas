# Episode-86 successor: certified patient-bundle capture under fixed review capacity

## 1. Parent synthesis and the one new decision question

This successor has assessed-valid parents [prior hypothesis] and [prior hypothesis]. It is HCC-only and uses snapshot [source checksum] and source-catalog SHA-256 [source checksum].

It preserves, without changing definitions:

- the adult first eligible hepatobiliary-resection population, operation/acquisition clocks, patient-disjoint 2015–2018 development, 2019 lock, separate 2020 and 2021 tests, and 2022+ audit;
- complete immutable radiology and pathology reader books, crossed book-specific states, frozen B/R/G/Gmask, fixed-K queues, comparator/random margins, anchored deletion, score/tie manifests, and documentary-M2;
- exact inherited q_req, q_LS, q_M2, and q_dec, including lower-threshold witnesses, strict equality-to-one failure, and nesting when defined;
- Episode-85 q_DOC patientwise source certification;
- Episode-85 q_LS_U05 conditional uncertainty reserve with exact one-sided family-wise Clopper–Pearson bounds; and
- operational_metadata_status=unavailable.

The one new decision question is:

> After the inherited full-documentary target is defined, can a prespecified patient-bundle exposure policy achieve that target with strictly less stored-content exposure than the inherited uniform threshold policy?

This is a resource-allocation estimand, not another retention conjunction. q_DOC asks whether protected patients and all K positions are certified under one common threshold. The new quantity asks whether the same target can be achieved with less exposure by capturing complete eligible accession bundles for selected patients. It informs whether a service should investigate prioritized bundle retrieval under fixed archive/review capacity.

The optimization is a finite-corpus evaluation oracle, not a deployable selector: it may use the already-frozen target sets to measure the best attainable resource burden. It must not be described as prospective clinical policy or evidence of benefit.

## 2. Clinical importance, evidence boundary, and falsifiable hypotheses

### Supported evidence

The catalog and read-only source audit establish structural feasibility only. HCC has 12 ordinary CSVs with encounter keys, procedure and examination times, whole-examination accession identifiers, examination text/metadata, same-encounter pathology text, and dated medication/order fields. Prior full scans recorded 105,044 encounter, 338,040 procedure, 419,996 examination, 46,395 pathology, 4,097,517 medication, and 16,730,319 order rows; they found 392,854 nonblank whole-accession keys, 16,534 multirow accessions, maximum 12 rows/accession, 72 blank accession IDs, and 8,932 encounters with multiple pathology rows, maximum 14. These are structural facts, not clinical-performance findings.

The acquired full-text systematic review PMC13360217 / DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028 reports 36 AI early-recurrence studies, 6/36 external validations, inconsistent calibration and decision-curve reporting, and no model generalized to clinical practice. Its endpoint is early recurrence, not HCC stored-content or documentary-M2. The local research-ambition demonstrations were inspected as rigor examples. The cancer demonstration's main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; only its supplement and metadata are available.

### Untested claims

The inherited unresolved claims are documentary: q_LS tests retention of every current-book, book-stable documentary-M2-positive patient; q_LS_U05 tests a conditional 5% patient-loss reserve; q_DOC tests patientwise accession completeness and K certified positions; q_dec tests equality to the maximum-content queue.

The new claim is:

> In the same required 2020/2021 states, if the inherited full-documentary target is feasible, there exists a patient-bundle exposure mask with exact exposed-accession burden strictly below the actual burden of the inherited uniform onset mask at q_dec, while preserving frozen G scores/ties, S_max, P_LS protection, patientwise certification, and K certified positions.

A null/adverse result is useful: the target requires uniform or more costly bundle exposure in this finite corpus. Neither result establishes report finalization, retrieval, readability, clinician viewing, MVI, recurrence, survival, safety, utility, causal effect, fairness, or patient benefit.

## 4. Frozen models and exact inherited frontiers

Use the frozen predictors exactly: B(Z) contains age, sex, acquisition count/recency, modality/ambiguity, and development-grouped machine; R adds report surface/availability fields; G adds frozen radiologist-book clinical content; Gmask is the identical fitted G with semantic blocks masked without refitting. Capacity-matched random capture is K*Y/N with exact integer cross-products.

Patient IDs, test labels, pathology, labs, untimed notes, future rows, reader identity, and test-derived dictionaries are forbidden predictors. Freeze patient-balanced fitting, preprocessing, feature order, coefficients, penalty, binary64 scores, tie keys, and K_rho=floor(rho*N_ref) in 2019. Require N_ref>=500, K_.05>=25, K_.10>=50, K_.20>=100; each test state N>=K_.10 and at least 50 documentary-positive events. rho=.10 is primary; .05/.20 are locked sensitivities; d=24h is primary.

Two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists plus adjudicator create three complete immutable radiology books; two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists plus adjudicator create three pathology books. The nine radiology-by-pathology pairs are fixed across all patients, years, cutoffs, source/onset worlds, models, thresholds, and deletions. Pathology terminals remain OUT, IN0-A, IN1-A, IN0-U, IN1-U; complete states map them to Y_i(a,b) in {0,1}; pathology is never a predictor and is documentary-M2, not biological MVI.

For each state use exact fixed-K queues. S_G(s,q) is the non-EMPTY incomplete-content G queue; S_max(s) is the same pipeline with all source-clean eligible accessions exposed. Anchored deletion removes all records of one patient without refit, rerank, backfill, or requotation; the offered slot remains EMPTY. Retain strict 100*(H_G-H_C)/K>5 for C in {B,R,Gmask} and the exact random-K gate in every required year/state/deletion.

For nonpathology state a and pathology books b', current book b:
- U_LS(a)={i:Y_i(a,b')=1 for every b'};
- P_common(a)=intersection over b' of {i:i in S_max(a,b') and Y_i(a,b')=1};
- P_LS(a,b)=S_max(a,b) intersection U_LS(a);
- P_M2(a,b)={i:i in S_max(a,b) and Y_i(a,b)=1}.

Thus P_common subseteq P_LS subseteq P_M2 subseteq S_max. Preserve exactly:
q_req=min{q in R:PASS(q)};
q_LS=min{q in R:PASS(q) and every required J_LS=1}, J_LS=1[P_LS subseteq S_G];

Protected sets must be nonempty; undefined/equality-to-one fails. When defined, q_req <= q_common <= q_LS <= q_M2 <= q_dec. Empty P_common makes only q_common diagnostic-undefined.

## 5. Preserved uncertainty and patientwise certification

At each q and required cell c=(a,b), set m_c=|P_LS(c)|, x_c=|P_LS(c) minus S_G(c,q)|. Patient episodes, not rows, are independent. Any m_c=0 makes the reserve undefined. After the complete state registry is frozen, M=|R|*max_q|C(q)| and alpha_cell=.05/M. Compute exact one-sided U_c=BetaInverse(1-alpha_cell;x_c+1,m_c-x_c), storing integer inputs, decimal output, library/version, and hash. For x=0 use 1-alpha_cell^(1/m); for x=m use 1. U_PASS requires inherited PASS, nonempty protected sets, and U_c<=.05 in every required cell. q_LS_U05=min{q in R:U_PASS(q)=1}; evaluate every threshold directly. Interpret only conditionally on exchangeability within each frozen state and fixed documentary books; under a fixed-census interpretation classify the operational uncertainty claim inconclusive.

For each patient define A_i(s,q)=1 iff every eligible nonblank accession has M=1. No-accession patients are not A_i=1; inherited n_A feasibility excludes vacuity. Blank, contradictory, or unavailable accession gives A_i=0. Define J_DOC=1[P_LS subseteq {i:A_i=1}] AND J_LS. Define S_cert=S_G intersection {i:A_i=1} and K_cert=|S_cert|; never backfill. q_DOC is the minimum q for which inherited PASS, nonempty protected sets, J_LS, J_DOC, and K_cert=K hold in every required 2020/2021 state with Q>=q. Preserve q_LS<=q_DOC when both are defined. q_DOC is stored-content certification only.

## 6. New patient-bundle resource frontier eta_B

For each required state c and binary exposure mask z, define T_c(z)=1 only if:

1. all source, schema, clock, frame, nonvacuity, book, score/tie, comparator, random, year, reader, source, and deletion gates pass;
2. frozen G under z gives exactly S_max(c), preserving q_dec;
3. P_LS(c) is retained;
4. every patient in P_LS(c) has every eligible accession exposed;
5. K_cert^z(c)=K_c with no EMPTY certified slot.

A mask is patient-bundle if all eligible source-clean accessions of each patient are exposed together. Invalid accessions are fixed unexposed. The mask may choose bundles but may not refit G, alter target sets/clocks, use future rows, or create labels. Solve exactly over binary patient variables, replaying the unchanged pipeline, lexicographically minimizing: (i) maximum state-wise exposed-accession fraction, (ii) total exposed-accession count, (iii) canonical patient-ID bit-vector. Define eta_B as the minimum maximum exposed eligible accession fraction across required cells among masks with T_c(z)=1 for all c. Report exact integer fractions, maximizing cell, selected-bundle hash, and independently checkable lower-bound certificate. If infeasible, eta_B is undefined.

Define the inherited uniform reference burden from the actual onset mask at the first threshold attaining the inherited full-documentary target:
eta_U=max_c[exposed eligible accessions under M(q_dec)/n_A(c)].
If q_dec is undefined/equal one, eta_U is undefined. The single new falsifiable comparison is bundle_gain=eta_U-eta_B>0. Support requires strict exact rational inequality. Equality or negative gain is adverse to resource efficiency, not to biology. This differs from q_DOC: q_DOC tests feasibility under a common threshold; eta_B tests the minimum burden of a distinct bundle policy for the stronger S_G=S_max target.

At every candidate and optimum emit exposure counts/fractions, patient counts, Q, S_G^z, S_max, ranks/ties, EMPTY positions, K, K_cert, P_LS, P_M2, P_common, all hashes, every inherited gate, state-wise objective, bundle membership, optimizer/library/version, and an independent certificate. Emit an immediate lower-cost infeasible witness. Do not present the oracle as deployable; a prospective rule requires pre-t_dec information, archive telemetry, and clinician outcomes.
