## Unresolved question, evidence and advance

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence is structural: this snapshot contains paired instance-0/instance-1 fields for a prespecified eight-field blood panel, assessment dates, demographic fields, position-matched inpatient diagnosis code/date arrays, and death-date fields. The source catalog and direct-header audit support their availability as columns. They do not by themselves establish analyte identities, units, specimen collection times, disease onset, code-domain meaning, individual observation end, or complete five-year ascertainment.

The parent asks whether prior values add proper-score information, especially in an ambiguous current-only risk band and under an early-to-late calendar shift. That remains an unresolved prognostic-information claim. A clinically important gap remains: a small average Brier improvement need not change a finite review or prevention-research queue. The new question is therefore whether retaining the prior panel changes a constrained decision in a useful direction.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among UKB participants with a valid paired panel and a verified current assessment landmark, within the prespecified central 20th–80th percentile band of current-only recorded-target risk, adding the prior panel to current values plus repeat-spacing metadata increases held-out capture and positive predictive yield of a fixed-capacity review queue for a later qualifying recorded cardiorenal/metabolic target, without material loss of calibration; the incremental queue value remains directionally consistent when the early-fitted policy is applied without refitting to the 2013 landmark era.

This is a predictive and policy-independent ranking hypothesis, not the causal effect of ordering a repeat test, monitoring, referral, or treatment. The primary operational contrast is between M1a (current panel plus spacing) and M1b (M1a plus prior panel). M0 (current panel without spacing) remains necessary to separate information in repeat spacing from information in prior values.

For each held-out partition, rank only the prespecified current-risk middle-band participants by predicted five-year target probability (with death handled as a competing recorded category) and select the top fixed workload. The primary workload is (K=\lfloor0.10 n_{early,middle}\rfloor), frozen from the early eligible middle-band population and used unchanged for the 2013 no-refit test when the late population can support K; 5% and 20% fractions and a fold-local proportional workload are sensitivities, not substitutions for the primary. The primary estimands are:

- incremental target capture: target events in the M1b top-K list minus target events in the M1a top-K list, divided by all eligible target events in the same held-out middle-band population;
- incremental target yield: target events per selected person, M1b minus M1a;
- paired list turnover: additions and removals caused by prior values;
- safety/competing-event summaries: death capture and target/death composition among selected people;
- proper-score and calibration contrasts, and the early-to-late no-refit difference in the capture/yield contrast.

A result supporting the hypothesis would show a prespecified positive capture and yield contrast, with uncertainty, no material calibration harm, and stable direction in the late no-refit test. This would justify external validation or prospective impact research on history-aware review. It would not justify a clinical threshold, automatic referral, more frequent testing, or treatment.

## Models, split and policy evaluation

Use one deterministic participant-level five-fold hash split, `SHA256(UTF8(decimal(eid))) mod 5), with no `eid` in multiple folds. All imputation, scaling, regularization, band thresholds, tuning, weighting and calibration are training-fold-only.

- M0: current position-1 panel, approved age, approved sex and T1 calendar year.
- M1a: M0 plus verified repeat spacing L.
- M1b: M1a plus all eight literal position-0 panel values; this is the primary history addition.
- A1: M1b plus prespecified current/prior contrasts and the middle-band interaction; interpretive sensitivity only.
- M2: a small gradient-boosted multinomial model with exactly M1b inputs and a fixed depth/regularization grid. M2a removes prior fields but retains every other input. M2 is not allowed to replace the nested M1a-versus-M1b estimand.

When timing and ascertainment gates pass, a regularized discrete-time competing-risk hazard implementation may be reported as a separately labelled sensitivity. Otherwise use the recorded target/death/none multinomial endpoint without censoring claims.

Within each held-out fold and era, apply the frozen M0 q20/q80 cut points and select exactly K people from the middle band. Report deterministic tie handling, selected-list size, target capture, target yield, death capture, target/death composition, list additions/removals, multiclass Brier/log loss, calibration intercept/slope, reliability/resolution, and observed-versus-predicted target/death counts. Use the same participants, endpoint, folds and queue capacity for M1a and M1b. The late test holds all early-fitted models, thresholds, weights and calibration fixed.

Use paired participant bootstrap intervals preserving era, folds, middle-band membership and queue capacity. A bootstrap may estimate test-sample uncertainty; training uncertainty is included only if the prespecified early refit sensitivity is run.

## Falsification and interpretation

Retain source/schema hashes, raw headers, row counts, key uniqueness, join coverage, field-gate results, cohort flow, endpoint pairing, interval/horizon checks, fold manifest, predictions, queue lists and bootstrap seeds. Run:

- endpoint-label permutation within fold and era;
- prior-panel permutation within era, current-risk band, interval, missingness and repeat-selection strata;
- deliberate one-position code/date shifts, which must be detected and rejected;
- early/late label permutation;
- capacity-label randomization and deterministic-tie audit;
- complete-case versus prespecified fold-local missingness handling;
- raw prior values versus change representation only when timing and units are certified;
- calibration, fold-instability, selection-overlap and late support checks.

Supportive evidence requires all semantic/timing gates, at least 100 target events overall, the middle-band cell support above, no integrity/leakage failure, a positive M1b-minus-M1a target-capture and yield contrast whose 95% interval excludes zero, no material calibration harm, and the same direction in the no-refit 2013 evaluation. The proper-score contrast should also favor M1b, but queue capture/yield is the primary decision test. A stronger result has stable selection-weighted contrasts and a reproducible M2 shape; a list change without concentration improvement is not support.

Adverse evidence is zero or negative incremental capture/yield, gain attributable only to spacing (M0 to M1a), disappearance after repeat-selection weighting or linkage-breaking permutation, calibration harm, late reversal, unstable fold/site/era behavior, or a Brier gain that fails to change queue concentration. This weakens the case for retaining prior values for this recorded-risk review task; it does not prove no value in another population or endpoint.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed field or outcome semantics, unverified ascertainment, sparse target events/cells, insufficient late K support, overlap/ESS failure, unstable weights, malformed pairs, fold instability, contradictory endpoint modes, or conflicting missingness/queue results. Inconclusive is not evidence against the hypothesis.

Computational verification can check source integrity, cohort flow, gate logic, folds, model fits, held-out predictions, queue arithmetic, calibration, uncertainty and falsification behavior. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for biomarker meaning, specimen timing, disease onset, complete ascertainment, action thresholds, patient benefit, cost-effectiveness, causal repeat-testing effects, treatment decisions, mechanism, external transport, and prospective policy impact. No automatic verifier can establish those claims.

## Method alternatives, compute and actual deliverable

The simple baseline is M1a: current values plus verified repeat spacing. It is clinically interpretable and directly tests whether prior values add information beyond the obvious timing/selection signal. M1b is the smallest substantive history alternative and answers the primary scientific question.

M2/M2a use exactly the same inputs, population, folds, endpoint, middle-band definition and queue capacity. M2 can reveal nonlinear threshold, current/prior discordance, and interval interactions that an additive model averages away.
