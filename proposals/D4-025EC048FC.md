# Certified-field protocol with an assumption-conditioned fallback for baseline-only kidney-status prioritization

## Successor and exact question

This is a substantive successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead's exact question, population boundary, schedule-marginal estimand, source snapshot, strict 9/10 model dimensions, and noncausal claim limits. The unresolved question is:

> Among participants with complete baseline measurements who attend the first available exported repeat that is 2--8 years after baseline and has complete repeat measurements, does baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance add useful prioritization beyond sex, age, and baseline creatinine-equation eGFR for the kidney-status label observed at that repeat?

"Useful" is tested only through the predeclared incremental observed-schedule metrics: the 2% action-threshold net-benefit contrast and the 10% fixed-capacity number of observed low-status labels captured. The target is not a fixed-horizon risk, CKD diagnosis, chronicity, measured GFR, kidney failure, treatment benefit, utility, or benefit from ordering a test. A positive result would justify only a follow-up study with adjudicated renal outcomes and observed decisions; a negative result would be evidence against adding discordance to this particular schedule-marginal prioritization rule; a technical or unstable result is inconclusive.

The substantive repair is a two-lane evidence gate. The certified lane can support the prespecified scientific test only after field-level assay/unit/date/missing-code evidence and equation provenance pass explicit machine-checkable requirements. The fallback lane is fully executable when those semantics are unavailable: it runs structural validation, raw-value and date diagnostics, and (only when an operator supplies an assumption manifest) the identical analysis under one or more assumption bundles, but labels every derived quantity `ASSUMPTION_CONDITIONED_UNVERIFIED` and forbids support, adverse, or biological conclusions. Thus missing metadata no longer causes an ambiguous half-run, while an apparently reproducible half-run cannot masquerade as a certified kidney result.

## Field certificate, equation certificate, and status machine

Two separate frozen JSON inputs are required: `field_certificate.json` and `equation_certificate.json`. A certificate is evidence, not a prose assertion. Every certificate records canonical JSON bytes, SHA-256, creation time, source URL or local artifact, source version/retrieval date, and the exact implementation commit/hash that consumed it.

`field_certificate.json` must have one entry for each of `31-0.0`, `21003-0.0`, `21003-1.0`, `53-0.0`, `53-1.0`, `30700-0.0`, `30700-1.0`, `30720-0.0`, `30720-1.0`, containing: field ID, table and column, instance/array, semantic label, specimen/matrix, assay/platform or measurement method, unit, date representation where applicable, valid domain, every certified missing token, and evidence URL/artifact hash. It must explicitly state whether instance 1 is a repeat collection and whether the date values are exact collection dates. It must establish compatible analyte, specimen, assay, and unit definitions across instances 0 and 1 or provide a frozen conversion with its own evidence. A field is `VERIFIED` only if those claims are supported by a frozen local metadata artifact or a frozen public field/coding response whose bytes and URL/version are retained. An operator declaration without evidence is `ASSUMED`, never `VERIFIED`.

`equation_certificate.json` must contain canonical formula text and constants, sex coding, unit requirements, DOI/PMID/PMCID, source artifact hash, and numerical test vectors (raw input, expected output, tolerance). Verify the 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation against Inker et al. (DOI `10.1056/nejmoa2102953`, PMID `34554658`, PMCID `PMC8822996`) and the 2012 cystatin-C-only equation against Inker et al. (DOI `10.1056/nejmoa1114248`, PMID `22762315`, PMCID `PMC4398023`). Formula provenance does not certify the UKB assay.

The status machine is fail-closed and machine-readable.

1. `STRUCTURAL_ONLY`: schemas/headers/keys are checked; no biomarker, date, eGFR, cohort, model, or biological output is produced.
2. `FALLBACK_ASSUMPTION_CONDITIONED`: a complete assumption manifest is supplied, but at least one field or equation-semantic claim is `ASSUMED`; the full computational pipeline may run, with every output and directory carrying this status and with no hypothesis verdict.
3. `CERTIFIED_EXECUTION`: all required field entries and equation test vectors are `VERIFIED`, source/hash/join checks pass, and all analysis gates pass. Only this status can produce the prespecified supportive/adverse/inconclusive scientific classification.
4. `STOP_FIELD_UNCERTIFIED`, `STOP_CERTIFICATE_CONFLICT`, `FAIL_SCHEMA_BINDING`, `FAIL_DATE_PARSE`, `FAIL_MODEL_SPEC`, or `FAIL_ANALYSIS`: no scientific verdict is emitted.

A certificate conflict (for example, two sources disagree on units or date semantics) is not resolved by choosing the more convenient source; it is a stop requiring expert review.

## Concrete fallback execution

The fallback is not a silent imputation of metadata. Its required `assumption_manifest.json` has a stable `bundle_id`, an entry for every field above, and explicit values for unit, missing tokens, date format, instance interpretation, assay compatibility, and equation version. Each entry is marked `ASSUMED`, cites the reason and source of the assumption, and records alternatives if more than one plausible interpretation exists. A run is rejected if any required item is absent. The manifest is hashed and carried into every output.

When no manifest is supplied, run only the structural branch and write aggregate header/row/key diagnostics plus a list of missing certificate items. When a manifest is supplied, execute every declared bundle independently; never select the bundle with the most favorable result. For each bundle:

* Preserve raw tokens and retain an audit table of token counts before conversion. Apply only the bundle's explicit missing-token set; do not globally recode negative values. Nonmissing unparsable numeric values and nonmissing unparsable dates are `FAIL_DATE_PARSE`/`FAIL_VALUE_PARSE`, not missing.
* Convert and calculate exactly as specified below, but label all biomarker/eGFR/cohort/model results assumption-conditioned. Emit a side-by-side sensitivity table of mask counts, outcome rate, fold metrics, and both contrasts across bundles. The fallback can identify which unverified semantic assumption changes the answer; it cannot call that sensitivity biological evidence.
* Run a mandatory formula-only unit-test suite and a raw-value distribution report before model fitting. If any implementation result differs from the declared equation test vectors beyond tolerance, stop the bundle.
* No fallback output may be used to update the certificate or to claim that the field is a creatinine, cystatin C, measured GFR, CKD, or a clinical outcome. The only permitted conclusion from the fallback is `UNVERIFIED_INCONCLUSIVE` or a technical failure.

This gives an executable route to diagnose and reconcile the local metadata gap without converting a guessed unit or date interpretation into a scientific result. After a later expert supplies evidence, the exact same manifest can be replaced by a field certificate and rerun; fallback results remain provenance/audit artifacts, not certified evidence.

## Certified population and schedule-marginal target

After, and only after, status is `CERTIFIED_EXECUTION`, apply masks in this order. Use certified missing tokens only, require finite positive ages and laboratories and valid sex, and report every field-specific loss.

* `J`: one-to-one joined rows.
* `B`: valid baseline sex, age, date, creatinine, and cystatin C.
* `R_date`: valid repeat date, strictly `date1 > date0`, and unrounded `(date1-date0)/365.25` in inclusive `[2,8]`.
* `R_complete`: valid repeat age, positive finite repeat creatinine, and positive finite repeat cystatin C.
* `S_A`: `R_complete` and baseline `eGFRcr_0 >= 60`.

Instance 1 is the first available qualifying exported repeat field, not the first chronological clinical test. Define attendance `A=1` among B before inspecting the repeat outcome when the certified date interval, repeat age, both repeat laboratories, and interval bound are valid; otherwise A=0. Descriptively compare baseline distributions and missingness in A groups. A five-fold cross-fitted attendance model may use only baseline sex, age, baseline eGFRcr/eGFRcys, and baseline missingness indicators; fold-training medians are allowed only for that attendance model. Report clipped/normalized inverse-attendance weights and effective sample size as a selection sensitivity, never as identification of unobserved outcomes. Repeat fields, elapsed time, and future measurements cannot enter the outcome score.

The primary outcome is `Ycr = I(eGFRcr_1 < 60)`; `Ycys = I(eGFRcys_1 < 60)` is secondary. The estimand is `P(Ycr=1 | X, A=1)` under the observed repeat schedule in complete first-repeat attenders. It is not a two-year or eight-year event probability, onset, cumulative incidence, persistent CKD, or a treatment/action effect.

## Equations and exact 9/10-dimensional models

After field verification, convert serum creatinine from certified micromoles/L as `Scr_mg_dL = raw_30700 / 88.4`; retain raw and converted values. Cystatin C must be certified in mg/L or have a certified conversion. With age in years and F=1 female, F=0 male:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^F`, where `(k,a)=(0.7,-0.241)` for female and `(0.9,-0.302)` for male.

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^Age * 0.932^F`.

`Z = log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)`. M0 uses `[F, age, eGFRcr]`; M1 adds Z. For each continuous variable use the explicitly defined four-column basis: clip to fixed bounds age `[40,80]` and eGFRcr `[60,120]`, set `k1=lower`, `k2=lower+(upper-lower)/3`, `k3=lower+2*(upper-lower)/3`, `k4=upper`, `d_j(u)=max(u-k_j,0)^3/(k4-k_j)` for j=1,2,3, and columns `[u,d1-d3,d2-d3,d3]`. Center and population-scale columns with training-fold moments; zero SD becomes 1. Standardize F and Z with training-fold moments, also zero-SD to 1. No imputation is used in primary S_A.

Hard assertions are M0 `1 + 4 + 4 = 9` standardized non-intercept columns and M1 `9 + 1 = 10`. Any other dimension is `FAIL_MODEL_SPEC`, not repaired silently. Fit float64 sklearn logistic regression with `penalty='l2', C=1.0, solver='lbfgs', fit_intercept=True, class_weight=None, max_iter=1000, tol=1e-10, random_state=0`; convergence warnings, nonfinite predictions, or `n_iter_ >= 1000` fail.

Use ten deterministic outcome-stratified five-fold repeats with seeds `104729,104759,104773,104779,104789,104801,104827,104831,104849,104851`. Stable-sort by `(label,eid)`, assign round-robin folds within label, and reuse the exact assignment after permutations. Produce paired OOF predictions for identical participants and assert one prediction per participant/model/repeat.

## Prespecified estimands, uncertainty, and falsification

For q=`0.01,...,0.20`, select top `ceil(q*n)` paired OOF risks by stable descending risk and ascending numeric eid; report capture, event fraction, PPV, and `DeltaC(q)`. At thresholds `{0.001,0.0025,0.005,0.0075,0.010,0.015,0.020,0.030,0.040,0.050,0.075,0.100,0.150,0.200}`, report NB `=TP/n-FP/n*p/(1-p)`, DeltaNB, treat-all and treat-none. The primary practical summaries remain DeltaNB at 2% and DeltaC at 10%; any practical-floor classification is prespecified, not selected after seeing results.

Use 4,000 paired participant bootstrap draws, seed `520241`, recomputing frozen OOF capacity/threshold/calibration grids. Report separate two-sided simultaneous 98% max-|T| bands for capacity, DeltaNB, calibration, and supported strata; zero/nonfinite studentizing SD is a declared failure. Use 200 outcome-label permutations, seed `520242`, within frozen baseline-eGFR strata `[60,75)` and `>=75`, reconstructing labels and folds exactly. For the predictor-null, permute Z within baseline eGFRcr deciles and sex. Run reversed-label and private elapsed-time leakage sentinels; neither can be a policy claim.

At 5--15% capacities, use the globally selected cohort and deterministic ties; do not rerank within timing bands. If reporting 10% timing decomposition, bands are `[2,4)`, `[4,6)`, `[6,8]`, require N>=1,000 and 20 events for directional descriptions, and contributions must sum exactly to pooled DeltaC. Timing is descriptive schedule heterogeneity, not horizon conditioning. Record fold quotas, selected N/events, overlap, Jaccard, entrants/leavers, all reclassification identities, and repeat-to-repeat signs.

Support requires `CERTIFIED_EXECUTION`, exact source/schema/hash/join integrity, valid 9/10 dimensions and predictions, complete uncertainty/null arithmetic, no material calibration deterioration, a contiguous 5--15% positive simultaneous lower DeltaC band, repeat/stratum stability, observed statistics more favorable than outcome nulls, and no comparable predictor-null, reversed-label, or leakage gain. Adverse evidence is a reproducibly negative incremental curve, material calibration harm, null-compatible gain, leakage gain, or failed/incomplete computation. Inconclusive evidence includes field stop, certificate conflict, bands crossing zero, isolated gains, unstable schedule strata, poor attendance positivity, or unexplained anchor mismatch. No fallback bundle can be supportive or adverse scientifically; it is always unverified/inconclusive or technical failure.

## Claim limits and verification

A verifier can check certificate structure and hashes, schema/headers/keys, raw token diagnostics, status transitions, exact masks, equations/test vectors, 9/10 dimensions, fold isolation, baseline-only feature provenance, predictions, bootstrap/permutation arithmetic, identities, and that the final label follows the declared gate. It cannot establish that a certificate is truthful beyond its evidence, that the exported analytes have clinically appropriate assay comparability, that an eGFR label is measured GFR or CKD, that repeat attendance is representative, or that ordering/testing changes outcomes. Chronicity, measured GFR accuracy, causal benefit, harms, cost-effectiveness, and transportability require expert assay review, adjudicated persistent kidney disease and measured GFR, observed decisions, utility data, and external/prospective validation. Keep eids, rows, predictions, fold assignments, actions, selections, weights, and resampling arrays private; publish only aggregate diagnostics and certificate/manifest hashes.
