# Baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance and repeat kidney status: assay-certified, selection-aware prioritization

## Lineage and question

This is a targeted successor to `[prior hypothesis]` and incorporates the fold-aware null repair in `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the baseline-only, schedule-marginal estimand and the inherited first-repeat-attender evidence boundary, while repairing three execution blockers: (i) field and assay certification, (ii) interpretation of the field-instance-1 repeat date, and (iii) complete-repeat selection and threshold limitations.

The falsifiable question is: **among adults with a baseline creatinine eGFR at least 60 and a qualifying first-repeat observation, does baseline cystatin-C/creatinine discordance improve baseline-only prioritization of future repeat kidney-function status beyond age, sex, and baseline creatinine eGFR?** The action represented is prioritizing a repeat kidney-function assessment; the experiment does not estimate whether ordering it improves outcomes.

Existing guidance and prior evidence support confirming an abnormal kidney marker and considering combined creatinine/cystatin-C estimation when creatinine may be inaccurate and GFR affects a decision. They do not establish that this UK Biobank discordance score improves testing decisions, a universal testing threshold, CKD chronicity, measured GFR, or patient-important outcomes. The advance is a deployable, explicitly schedule-marginal predictive experiment with an auditable assay gate and transparent missingness boundary, rather than a causal or clinical-utility claim.

## Certified variables and kidney-status outcomes

## Falsification and integrity tests

1. **Assay/field audit:** certification record, units, field-instance mapping, conversion code, and aggregate value counts must pass independent reproduction. Failure blocks all biological or predictive interpretation.
2. **Leakage sentinel:** code audit must prove absence of repeat date, repeat assays, repeat age, attendance, interval, or any future-derived field from M0/M1. A deliberately leaked model may be run only as a debugging sentinel.
3. **Fold-aware label-null:** for 200 within-baseline-stratum outcome permutations (seed 520242), reconstruct outcome-stratified folds under each permuted label, refit both models, and recompute all capacity/threshold statistics. Compare the observed statistic to the empirical statistic-specific null (not zero); report randomized-plus-one tail fractions. If the observed primary curve is not more favorable than the prespecified 98% favorable-tail null envelope, incremental predictive evidence is not certified.
4. **Predictor-null:** permute `Z` within baseline eGFRcr deciles and sex, refit M1 with identical fold/preprocessing rules, and require incremental curves to collapse toward their empirical null. Persistent large gains indicate leakage or implementation error.
5. **Reversed-label negative control:** rerun the frozen pipeline for `1-Ycr`; a discordance-specific conclusion cannot depend on an equally large gain for this artificial target.
6. **Timing sensitivity:** report `[2,3)`, `[3,5)`, `[5,8]` interval bands and the secondary outcomes, without changing the primary schedule-marginal estimand. A direction that exists only in an interval band or only for one unadjudicated equation is not a general kidney-status finding.
7. **Selection sensitivity:** compare complete-case and predeclared weighted descriptive results; large selection dependence must be reported as uncertainty, not corrected by causal language.

A failed integrity or null test is a blocking failure for the corresponding claim, not evidence of clinical harm or evidence that discordance is biologically irrelevant.

## Decision gates and limits

* **Supportive for predictive prioritization only:** assay and source audits pass; M1 is calibrated acceptably relative to M0; the simultaneous 98% lower band for `DeltaC(q)` is positive across the contiguous 5–15% capacity region, stable across repeats and the prespecified baseline strata, and survives the empirical label-null and predictor-null checks. Concordant direction for at least one prespecified repeat-status sensitivity strengthens, but is not required for, the creatinine-status claim. This supports only baseline conditional ranking in the selected complete-repeat cohort.
* **Adverse:** M1 reproducibly worsens calibration or capacity capture, or the apparent gain is null-compatible, leakage-related, assay-unresolved, or selection-fragile. A negative result argues against incremental prioritization value in this evidence boundary, not against cystatin C in all clinical uses.
* **Inconclusive:** bands cross zero across the capacity region, curves vary materially by repeat or stratum, assay/platform certification is incomplete, null refits do not complete, or only isolated threshold points improve. No threshold recommendation follows.

Even a supportive result cannot establish benefit from ordering a repeat test, acceptable false-positive burden, cost-effectiveness, downstream treatment benefit, causal utility, CKD chronicity, measured GFR accuracy, assay interchangeability, or transportability. Those require external utility/cost data, clinical adjudication, prospective observation of the testing decision, and preferably measured GFR or repeated clinical records.
