# MerTK inhibitor response: target-specific signal or assay-compatible alternatives?

**Status: proposed; no new Harbor analysis has been executed.** This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It narrows the question from a future therapeutic-window claim to the closest computable discriminator available: whether the historical inhibitor-associated expression response is coherent and context-modified in the exact paired assay contrasts, while explicitly recognizing that neither dataset has a target-specific negative control or target-engagement measurement.

## Question, clinical importance, and bounded claim

**Falsifiable Harbor hypothesis:** In E-MTAB-8316, paired UNC106238-versus-vehicle macrophage expression contrasts under common LPS stimulation will show a donor-consistent response enriched for prespecified MerTK-relevant signaling/efferocytosis programs, rather than an undifferentiated stress/inflammatory shift. In the distinct E-MTAB-8873 FLS coculture assay, inhibitor-labeled conditions will be descriptively checked for concordant context-specific FLS responses between MerTK/CD206-positive and negative macrophage cocultures where exact sample names allow comparison. A coherent, directionally consistent response is evidence for a context-associated drug-response signature—not proof that MERTK caused it.

**Why resolution matters:** MerTK prioritization trades suppression of inflammatory myeloid signaling against preserving homeostatic stromal and pro-resolving function. FLS subsets have essential homeostatic roles, so transcript shifts in coculture cannot be interpreted as therapeutic benefit without considering functional harm [K1]. A recent RA framework also predicts redundant stromal–myeloid resolution circuits; a lower inflammatory transcript score may coexist with unrepaired resolution failure [K2]. Matrix and fibroblast–myeloid work further shows that disease activity can reflect tissue organization and matrix context, not a single receptor program [K3]. The consequential next decision is whether to advance MERTK-specific perturbation, deprioritize the inhibitor evidence, or first resolve a basic assay attribution problem.

**Evidence-supported today:** The selected parent records that the closest study reports MerTK-positive remission-associated macrophage states, FLS repair observations, and inhibitor-associated transcript changes using these deposited assays. Its own evidence explicitly marks the relevant primary publication as abstract-only and notes the assay overlap and dose uncertainty. The source files inspected here independently establish paired LPS/vehicle/UNC106238 labels in E-MTAB-8316, and sorted MerTK/CD206 coculture and inhibitor labels in E-MTAB-8873. These are published/recorded design facts, not a newly computed result.

**Untested claim:** A MerTK-related signature in these data is specific to MERTK inhibition, mediates a protective or harmful function, or defines a safe cell-selective therapeutic window. The strongest rival is drug/off-target action, carryover into the coculture, activation-state differences, cell sorting/composition, donor effects, or measurement/annotation artifacts. E-MTAB-8316's nominal 250 dose is recorded as an SDRF field without a verified dose unit in the design JSON; E-MTAB-8873 does not state compound identity or dose in its SDRF/design rows. Neither source has target engagement, genetic perturbation, drug washout/carryover measurement, dose response, functional efferocytosis, or clinical outcome. The existing assays therefore cannot choose MERTK-specific action over these rivals, even with a positive expression result.

## Method alternatives and decision

**Simple baseline selected for Harbor:** library-normalized, paired log-expression differences by the six verified E-MTAB-8316 specimen contrasts; summarize pair effects, medians and uncertainty, with leave-one-monocyte-donor-cluster-out sensitivity. Exact inputs are the 12 count columns and the paired design fields above; fit target is each feature's treated-minus-control response and prespecified module summary; split is grouped leave-one-monocyte-donor-out (four clusters) as influence/stability analysis, not external validation. Evaluation is effect direction, magnitude, interval, pair consistency, and dependence on donor clusters; no predictive-accuracy claim. This is CPU work, likely seconds to a few minutes for 58,302 × 12 after input I/O; estimate not measured. It is selected as primary because it preserves the actual randomized/paired contrast structure and avoids pseudo-replication.

**Mechanistic alternative on the same contrast:** a fixed signed pathway/response-network analysis over the same six pairwise differential-expression vectors. Before inspecting effects, map measured features to a versioned, frozen human curated gene-set/network snapshot, and compare MerTK/TAM-receptor-linked signaling and efferocytosis modules against broad LPS/NF-κB, generic stress, and matrix/homeostasis rivals; use paired ranked enrichment or module scores, report gene coverage and leave-one-donor-cluster-out stability. No neural net or de novo pathway discovery is warranted with four distinct monocyte donors. Inputs remain the exact 12 E-MTAB-8316 columns plus a declared frozen gene-set file; fitting target is ranked paired response/module coherence, grouped split is the same four donor clusters, evaluation is module direction/coverage, rank stability and contrasts with rival modules, and uncertainty is cluster-level resampling. This could reveal whether effects are organized in a biologically coherent MERTK-proximal pattern rather than diffuse activation—information the gene-by-gene baseline discards. It still cannot establish target specificity, and any curated overlap is not causal proof. That annotation file/version is not in the configured assay sources and must be acquired/frozen before execution; if it cannot be secured, defer this alternative and report only the baseline rather than inventing gene sets. Planned compute is CPU, 2–4 cores, <4 GiB RAM and under 10 minutes after mapping (unmeasured); no GPU is justified. The two approaches answer the same uncertainty, not merely compete on a predictive score.

**Selection before Harbor results:** run the simple paired baseline as the exact computable deliverable; preserve the pathway alternative as a bounded, conditional analysis because it adds substantive mechanistic organization but has a missing annotation dependency. Do not shrink to an easier cell count or present a future functional study as part of Harbor. Stop/revisit the model choice if the input condition labels fail validation or if available gene coverage makes the pathway alternative uninterpretable. An uncomputed hypothesis is not a finding.

## Future cell-resolved perturbation needed for target attribution

If the historical response is coherent enough to justify follow-up, perform a prospective randomized donor-blocked coculture study in human RA synovial tissue with FLS and separately sorted MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage populations. Within each donor and macrophage context, compare non-targeting control, at least two independent MERTK CRISPRi/siRNA reagents, knockdown plus MERTK rescue, vehicle, dose-ranging UNC106238 and an inactive/orthogonal pharmacologic control. Match LPS exposure and coculture timing across arms; wash macrophages before coculture and directly quantify residual compound in supernatant/cells to test carryover. Verify MERTK protein and target engagement (including proximal phospho-signaling) before interpreting transcription. Analyze macrophage and FLS compartments separately at prespecified early signaling and 24–48 h expression endpoints. Functional outcomes must include macrophage efferocytosis of labeled apoptotic cells, FLS inflammatory cytokine/chemokine secretion, and an independently measured matrix-repair readout. Donor is the unit, cells are nested observations; preregister donor number after pilot variance and a donor-level interaction model.

The target-specific interpretation requires genetic perturbation to phenocopy drug effects, rescue to reverse them, target engagement to track dose, and the effect to survive washout/carryover controls. If drug shifts persist with MERTK rescue or fail to follow MERTK perturbation, favor off-target/carryover explanations. If transcription changes but functional endpoints do not, classify as an expression response only. If effects differ by macrophage context, that supports cell-context-dependent target biology, not clinical safety. Clinical benefit and systemic therapeutic window would still require clinical-grade pharmacology, human safety evidence, expert review, and a clinical study.
