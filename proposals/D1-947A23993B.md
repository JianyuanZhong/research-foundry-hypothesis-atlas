## Predeclared estimand-support tiers

**Tier 2, fold-local joint-overlap diagnostic.** A state/opportunity/landmark cell is reportable only when its support map was learned from training hospitals alone, every mapped predictor is finite, neither arm is outside the training support range, and the held-out test cell has at least 10 rows in each exact arm, at least 20 total rows, at least five distinct stays in each arm, and at least 20 distinct stays total. **Tier 3, stable transported residual.** A state/opportunity residual label signal can be interpreted as supported only if Tier 2 includes at least five cells spanning at least five held-out hospitals, both arms occur in every included cell, no cell contributes more than 25% of supported rows, at least 70% of included cell estimates are directionally concordant with the aggregate M1-minus-M0 increment, and no single hospital contributes more than 25% of supported rows. **Tier 4, operational review support.** A named K, `H_E`, or `H_T` review target is interpretable only if its applicable opportunity-supported denominator is available in held-out hospitals, its unresolved states remain explicit, and all fixed threshold/cost/capacity tables are complete.
