# Proposal: diagnostic-versus-therapeutic pathway validity after TACE

## Episode, parent, and substantive repair

This Episode-14 child evolves assessed valid parent `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 36.24). It preserves the parent’s one-index HCC cohort, first recorded TACE-like episode, day-45 landmark, days 46–365 follow-up, observation/measurement-selection estimands, reassessment-anchored versus unanchored pathway contrast, capture-inclusive administrative observation state, patient-level temporal split, and explicit limits against interpreting local codes as response, intent, benefit, mortality, or outside-care status.

The remaining endpoint-validity problem is material. In the inspected procedures source, `Hepatic Arteriography` is the most frequent single procedure label (33,332 rows), while many TACE records are bundled combinations of angiography, embolization and infusion. A label such as angiography can be a diagnostic/reassessment step, a component of a therapeutic episode, or a same-day bundle. If all liver-directed procedure rows are treated as competing therapeutic outcomes, a model may appear to identify “escalation” while actually identifying diagnostic contact. Conversely, censoring at a diagnostic procedure can erase the transition from reassessment to treatment. The repair is a prespecified diagnostic-only versus therapeutic episode ontology and a two-stage/multistate analysis in which diagnostic-only contact is an intermediate state rather than silently equated with treatment.

This is a data-bound adaptation, not a reproduction of any demonstration. The natural-history/Delphi example motivates dated-history representations, but its original cohort, implementation and external validation are not reproduced. The Bayesian longitudinal example motivates the irregular-time latent-state alternative, but its genetic analysis is not imported because this HCC island has no genetic inputs. The cancer/Oncoformer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable in the supplied bundle; only the accessible supplement limitations are respected, and HCC has no image files. A transformer, imaging model, causal treatment-effect model and report-text response model remain deferred for endpoint/context validity, absent images, absent validated treatment intent/eligibility/outcomes, and unreliable temporal text semantics.

## Evidence-supported claim and unresolved hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence is operational: the frozen HCC snapshot contains linkable dated encounters, procedures, labs and examinations. Direct read-only audit measured 105,044 encounter rows, 338,040 procedure rows, 1,810,646 diagnosis rows, 344,383 rows whose diagnosis name contains `hepatocellular carcinoma`, and 106,963 rows matching the parent's TACE lexical rule. Procedure start time was missing for 15,874 rows. These are availability and coding facts, not evidence of treatment indication, therapeutic intent, viable tumour, radiologic response, recurrence, mortality, or benefit. The procedure source also has large calendar-volume changes and records through 2025–2026; the primary index cutoff and horizon must therefore be applied by exact parsed timestamps, not by assuming year labels imply complete follow-up.

The unresolved clinical-research question is:

> Among adults with HCC and a first recorded TACE-like episode who are free of a liver-directed therapeutic episode through day 45, does an early AFP/hepatic trajectory add reproducible information about a later therapeutic TACE-containing recorded pathway with a prior local reassessment opportunity, beyond information about who is measured, who remains locally observable, and who first undergoes diagnostic-only liver contact?

The primary hypothesis is prespecified as a local recorded-care prediction claim:

> On a locked temporal test, adding the early trajectory and its missingness pattern to an observation-aware baseline will reduce the alert fraction at 90% sensitivity for a first therapeutic, reassessment-anchored TACE episode by at least 10 percentage points, with a patient-bootstrap 95% interval excluding zero, after standardizing the measured-trajectory estimand to the full day-45-eligible cohort. The anchored gain must exceed the corresponding gain for therapeutic TACE without a prior local reassessment opportunity by at least 5 percentage points, with an interval excluding zero, and must not be reproduced for a diagnostic-only angiography transition, a non-liver capture control, or a pre-index placebo. The observation-only comparator must not explain nearly all of the trajectory gain.

The margins are operating criteria for reproducible information in this local record. They are not treatment thresholds, clinical utility, causal effects, or evidence that a patient needs TACE.

## Population and temporal boundary

Use one index per patient.

1. Read the ordinary, read-only HCC CSV sources and aggregate every child table to (`Patient master index`, `Encounter number`) before joining to `encounters`; never form many-to-many joins.
2. Parse procedure `start time` and group rows for a patient within 24 hours into an episode. Retain raw names, source, start/end times, encounter key, row count, family hits, missing-time flags and composite status. Rows without a parseable start time cannot define an event; report them rather than backdating from an encounter.
3. Define the index as the earliest timed episode containing literal `TACE`, a name containing `transarterial chemoembolization`, or both `hepatic artery` and `embolization`. Preserve a frozen vocabulary audit and list exact-tie, mixed and unclassified episodes.
4. Require age >=18 and nonmissing `sex` on the index encounter.
5. Require a diagnosis name containing `hepatocellular carcinoma` on a joined encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after index. Because `diagnoses` has no diagnosis timestamp, inherit the joined encounter timestamp only for this cohort criterion and report a sensitivity excluding uncertain same-encounter temporal ordering.
6. Include only index timestamps <= 2025-01-01 00:00:00, so that the nominal 365-day administrative horizon is explicit. The solver must show the actual maximum available follow-up for every index and exclude an index from the primary complete-horizon analysis if day 365 is not represented in the local record. A sensitivity can use all index dates with right-censoring, but cannot call it a complete one-year outcome.
7. Exclude and separately report any post-index therapeutic liver-directed episode after the first 24 hours through day 45. The primary risk set starts at day 45 only for patients without such a therapeutic episode.
8. Stop all predictors at day 45. Follow-up is days 46–365.

The patient-level temporal split is 2010–2022 fit, 2023 tuning, and 2024 through the exact 2025-01-01 cutoff as locked test, provided each split has sufficient index and outcome support. If a calendar split fails a prespecified minimum event/support check, use deterministic participant hashing and label it internal; do not silently merge periods or tune on the locked test. No participant crosses splits.

## Endpoint ontology and estimands

Create an auditable episode ontology before modeling.

- `therapeutic_TACE`: the episode contains the frozen TACE family, including TACE/embolization/infusion combinations, after the parent’s lexical and family audit.
- `alternate_therapeutic_liver_directed`: a timed episode meeting a prespecified therapeutic family rule but not `therapeutic_TACE`, such as a liver-directed ablation or resection family if present in the audited vocabulary.
- `diagnostic_only_liver_contact`: a liver/abdominal angiography or examination-like procedure with no audited therapeutic family hit and no therapeutic component in the same 24-hour episode.
- `mixed_or_unclassified`: a bundle containing both diagnostic and unclassified/therapeutic-looking names that cannot be resolved by the frozen rule. It is never silently promoted to a therapeutic outcome.
- `non_liver_procedure`: a timed procedure outside the liver-directed families, used both as a competing local-care state and a capture control.
- `end_of_local_observation`: no later qualifying encounter, admission or discharge timestamp under the administrative local-contact rule; this is not death, outside-care cessation or clinical deterioration.
- `observed_no_therapeutic_action`: no classified therapeutic action through day 365 while the record remains locally observable.

The primary estimand is the transition from the day-45 risk state to the first `therapeutic_TACE` episode, separately for episodes preceded by a strict local reassessment opportunity and those without one. The strict reassessment flag uses only records strictly before the therapeutic episode and excluding its encounter: at least one dated liver-relevant lab or dated liver/abdominal examination in the preceding 30 days, using the parent’s frozen assay and examination-name screens. Encounter-only and 14-day/30-day context windows are prespecified sensitivities. This is a documentation-process construct, not adjudicated imaging or tumour reassessment.

Diagnostic-only contact is modeled as an intermediate transition D, not automatically as therapeutic treatment and not automatically as censoring. The solver must report:

- direct day-45 -> therapeutic TACE transition hazards and cumulative incidence;
- day-45 -> diagnostic-only contact -> therapeutic TACE transition probabilities;
- therapeutic TACE after diagnostic-only contact versus therapeutic TACE without diagnostic-only contact;
- first diagnostic-only contact as a separate process outcome;
- the parent’s simpler competing-event definition as a secondary sensitivity;
- pathway-specific calibration and event counts.

If diagnostic-only contact is too sparse, too ambiguous, or too entangled with same-day bundles to support the multistate transition, the primary interpretation is inconclusive, not a forced therapeutic result. A therapeutic episode that is bundled with angiography remains therapeutic under the frozen family rule; a diagnostic-only episode cannot be used as evidence of TACE intent.

## Predictors and information boundary

Trajectory availability A=1 is defined before modeling as at least one parseable numeric AFP in days -30 to -1 and days 7–45, plus at least one of albumin, total bilirubin, the observed coagulation-ratio assay, or platelets in each window. Preserve partial observation patterns; do not silently complete-case the full cohort.

For each assay, use the last parseable pre-index value and first parseable early-post value. Retain log1p AFP, continuous changes, frozen direction indicators, assay identity, timing, number of measurements, and missingness. No cross-assay pooling, unit inference, ALBI/MELD construction, or unit-dependent threshold is permitted: `labs` has no separate unit column.

The observation model predicts A using only index history and variables known by day 45: age, sex, index year, department, procedure/composite history, encounter/contact intensity, assay availability, examination opportunities, diagnostic-only contact, non-liver/unclassified procedure counts, and missingness. Fit it on the training split only. Estimate a full-index predictive-standardization target and a paired-observed A=1 target; report overlap, positivity and weight truncation. Do not call weighting a causal effect or assume testing is random.

Baseline history contains pre-index procedure and diagnosis-history counts, assay summaries/missingness and day-45-known capture features. `examinations` contributes dated opportunities only; report text is excluded from primary inference. `orders` is a sensitivity-only dated opportunity/capture feature and never a completed treatment. No future reassessment context, post-day-45 record, future test, or future procedure is a predictor.

Use the parent’s administrative end-of-local-observation definition from the maximum valid encounter, admission or discharge timestamp after day 45, with an encounter-time-only sensitivity. Report capture-inclusive and continued-local-observation analyses.

## Matched methods and actual deliverable

Fit all methods on the same cohort, endpoint ontology, split, and uncertainty procedure.

1. **B0 transparent baseline**: regularized discrete-time transition hazards using pre-index history, day-45 observation features, and diagnostic-only-contact status, without post-index laboratory values.
2. **Bobs selection/pathway comparator**: B0 plus trajectory availability, measurement pattern, diagnostic-only transition indicators and observation-process variables, but no laboratory values. This tests whether apparent gain is only who gets measured or who enters the diagnostic pathway.
3. **B2 trajectory model**: Bobs plus AFP/hepatic values and changes, all-index missingness features, and the same multistate transitions. Report both A=1 and training-only observation-weighted full-index estimates.
4. **M3 substantive learned/mechanistic alternative**: a low-rank irregular-time joint state-space model with separate latent tumour-marker activity and hepatic-function trajectories, assay-specific observation models, diagnostic-only contact intensity, and transition-specific hazards for diagnostic-only, therapeutic TACE and alternate therapeutic states. It must predict the same horizon, context strata and endpoint ontology, cannot smooth the day-45 state using future data, and must expose observation intensity and transition probabilities.

The scientific comparison is B2 versus Bobs (trajectory information beyond selection and diagnostic contact), full-index standardization versus A=1 (transport through measurement selection), anchored versus unanchored therapeutic TACE, and M3 versus B2 on the same target. M3 could reveal asynchronous biomarker shape, trajectory discordance, and whether diagnostic contact is an intermediate state that paired summaries lose. B2 is the interpretable operating model; M3 is not selected because it is complex, but because the multistate/irregular-time mechanism is the scientific uncertainty. If event support, positivity or convergence is inadequate, defer M3 and preserve that reason rather than replacing it with a generic high-capacity model.

The future solver must newly construct `analysis_manifest.json`, cohort/index/landmark/split manifests, procedure episode and vocabulary audits, diagnostic-versus-therapeutic event audit, observation state, fitted B0/Bobs/B2 parameters, and (if feasible) M3 checkpoints/parameters. It must output locked predictions, transition CIFs, pathway calibration, alert curves, B2–Bobs and B0–B2 gains, complete-pair versus full-index estimates, anchored/unanchored contrasts, diagnostic-contact transitions, overlap/weight diagnostics, patient-clustered bootstrap intervals, and an `interpretation.json` mapping every conclusion to an output field, estimate, interval and one of supportive/adverse/inconclusive. Completion is demonstrated by these fitted outputs and uncertainty, not by readiness and not by a presumed positive result.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires all of the following:

- the prespecified >=10-point B2-versus-Bobs alert reduction at 90% sensitivity for anchored therapeutic TACE, after valid full-index standardization, with an interval excluding zero;
- the anchored gain exceeds the unanchored gain by >=5 points with an interval excluding zero;
- the result is stable under the 14-day/30-day context, encounter-time-only contact and continued-observation sensitivities;
- the result is not explained by diagnostic-only contact, the non-liver capture control, or the pre-index trajectory placebo;
- procedure vocabulary, mixed/unclassified and missing-time rates are acceptable, overlap is adequate, calibration is not materially adverse, and there is sufficient event support;
- if M3 is attempted, it converges and improves the same locked target by a prespecified >=5-point alert reduction versus B2 or >=0.02 mean log-score improvement, with calibrated uncertainty.

Adverse evidence includes no or reversed anchored gain; equal or larger gains for unanchored TACE, diagnostic-only contact, non-liver control or placebo; Bobs explaining nearly all of B2’s apparent gain; gain confined to high-contact strata; diagnostic-only episodes dominating the label; mixed/unclassified same-time bundles changing the conclusion; poor overlap or calibration; material instability across context windows; or M3 nonconvergence. These results weaken the disease-specific recorded-care interpretation and support a capture/pathway explanation.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few anchored therapeutic events, sparse diagnostic-only transitions, high missing procedure or lab times, unresolved same-day bundles, inadequate one-year support, calendar-split failure, positivity failure, wide intervals spanning margins, or insufficient M3 support. Do not relax the endpoint ontology, horizon, thresholds or falsification rules after locked outcomes.

A supportive result would justify expert review of procedure intent and radiology, prospective surveillance evaluation and independent external validation. It would not justify changing a patient’s treatment. Clinical adjudication or additional data are essential for TACE indication, planned versus reactive intent, eligibility, radiologic response, viable tumour, treatment failure/benefit, mortality, outside care, appropriateness, clinical utility, and external validity. The computation can establish only reproducible local recorded-care stratification, the separation or failure of diagnostic-versus-therapeutic pathways, and the stability of the observation-aware predictive claim.

## Compute, alternatives, and selection rationale

The direct source/header and encounter/procedure/diagnosis audit was read-only and took approximately 7 seconds in the discovery shell; this is measured audit time, not fitting time. Discovery science time is limited to 7,200 seconds. The future solver envelope is up to 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB memory and 28,800 seconds; B0/Bobs/B2, episode construction, multistate summaries and bootstrap are expected to fit on CPU, but exact preprocessing and fit time are unverified. M3 should begin with sparse CPU fitting and checkpoints. If a bounded diagnostic shows repeated irregular-time optimization benefits materially from acceleration, request one allocated A100 through `ehr_job_submit`, use `cuda:0` inside that allocation, and report image/infrastructure dependence separately. No GPU and no proposer/solver weight training is required.

The transparent B0/Bobs/B2 family is selected because it directly isolates the scientific contribution of the trajectory from measurement and diagnostic-pathway selection and is auditable under sparse event support. M3 is retained because diagnostic contact and biomarker observation are time-varying processes that paired summaries lose; its role is to test a substantive mechanism, not to chase a small predictive gain. A generic transformer is deferred because the unresolved problem is pathway/endpoint validity and selection, not raw sequence capacity. The deferred branch should be revisited only with a validated procedure-intent/radiology label set, richer temporal semantics, independent mortality/outside-care linkage, adequate event support, and an external cohort.
