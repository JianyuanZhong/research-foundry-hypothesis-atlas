# Provisional hypothesis: macrophage–fibroblast context modifies the intervention-associated fibroblast transcript response

Status: provisional and evidence-bound; no inferential result has been computed.

## Proposed relationship

Among rheumatoid-arthritis fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS) exposed to the interventions represented in the available E-MTAB-8316 assay, the direction and magnitude of the intervention-associated transcript response will depend on macrophage/coculture context rather than being context-independent. Operationally, this is an intervention-by-macrophage-context interaction, estimated after accounting for donor pairing and repeated context measurements.

This is a hypothesis about an intervention-associated expression pattern, not a claim that any drug acts directly on a target or that the expression change is causal.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The available configured molecular dataset is reported to contain expression measurements and intervention, macrophage/condition, and donor-related metadata. That supports feasibility of testing a context interaction, but does not establish the interaction. The unresolved claim is whether the intervention contrast changes reproducibly with macrophage context after donor-aware adjustment, rather than reflecting donor composition, batch, or condition assignment. Existing evidence does not establish direct target action, mechanism, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.

## Planned test and interpretation

The simple baseline is a donor-aware additive model with separate intervention and macrophage-context terms. The substantive alternative adds their interaction. Fit both on the verified E-MTAB-8316 samples, using a prespecified donor-aware held-out split or cross-validation and uncertainty intervals. Support requires a reproducible interaction with uncertainty excluding the null and stability across donors. A robust null or reversal is adverse evidence against context dependence. Imprecision, too few independent donors, failed key/assay verification, or unstable estimates are inconclusive.

The interaction model can reveal whether the intervention response changes with microenvironment, information lost by an additive baseline. It cannot distinguish direct target action from indirect transcriptional response. Mechanistic perturbation, target-specific measurements, clinical outcomes, independent technical replicates, and verified assay contrasts are required for stronger claims.

## Exact data dependency and limitation

The planned computation requires the E-MTAB-8316 expression matrix and design/assay metadata, joined only through documented sample keys. Required fields are intervention/treatment labels, macrophage or coculture-context labels, donor/patient identifiers, technical batch or assay fields where present, and gene-expression values. The exact local source paths, archive members, table names, columns, and join keys remain to be re-verified against the dataset catalog and assay registry; this draft does not claim they were re-inspected at this checkpoint.

## Falsifiable next test

Fit the donor-aware additive and interaction models only where the verified design supports the interaction. Report interaction estimates, uncertainty, donor-level stability, and calibration. No screening result can establish direct target causality or clinical benefit.

## Missing evidence

No computed result, verified local binding, clinical adjudication, or complete literature dossier is available at this checkpoint. The exact source bindings and assay registry contrasts must be verified before execution. These are limitations, not inferred facts.
