# Lab-independent decision-time-bounded M2 triage: deployable report semantics without unverified laboratory visibility

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and hypotheses

The historical decision remains narrow: 24 hours before a first eligible liver resection, could a frozen, interpretable strategy flag an adult in this institution's retrospectively pathology-confirmed HCC resection frame for multidisciplinary review because the resection specimen may show pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)? The output is a weighted historical classification flag. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

**Strongest claim supported now.** Direct catalog and source-header inspection establishes that the HCC snapshot contains operation start times, CT/MRI acquisition times and eventual report text, assay-specific laboratory values with only one field called `Test time`, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. The laboratory table has exactly `Patient master index, encounter number, test, qualitative result, quantitative result, specimen type, test time`: it has no collection, result-entry, verification, finalization, release, clinician-view, panel/accession, or unit field. In a source-order audit of 250,000 laboratory rows, 363 exact assay strings and 18 specimen strings appeared; this demonstrates heterogeneity, not visibility or prevalence. Therefore `Test time<t_dec` supports only timestamp eligibility. It does not support the stronger claim that the row, result, or value was available to a clinician at `t_dec`.

Inherited full scans counted 338,040 procedure rows and 46,395 pathology rows. A broad first-resection audit found 75 preliminary anchored M2 labels in 2020 and 24 in 2021 before final exclusions and adjudication, and demonstrated that an anywhere-in-document M2 search is invalid because a selected M0 can be followed by boilerplate defining M2. These are feasibility and construct-validity findings, not final prevalence, performance, visibility, or utility.

Relevant public full text was inspected. The Chinese MVI-TTG definition classifies M2 as more than five proximal foci within 1 cm and/or any focus beyond 1 cm; MVI severity has prognostic relevance, but pathological sampling and grading remain limitations (Li et al., DOI `10.1111/jcmm.70746`, PMCID `PMC12309289`, frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). A 2026 retrospective multicenter complete-case study used CT margin irregularity and AFP positivity to predict binary MVI, reporting AUC 0.740 in 487 training patients and 0.781 in 256 external-validation patients, with exploratory decision-curve analysis (Huang et al., DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`, frozen full HTML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). That study does not validate M2, routine report semantics, historical result visibility, this dataset, or treatment benefit.

**Unresolved clinical-value hypothesis C.** In the locked 2020–2021 whole target frame, does the laboratory-independent deployable semantic strategy `S` add at least 0.01 net-benefit units over the laboratory-independent structured baseline `B` at threshold 0.30, despite worst-case patient-wise uncertainty in radiology-report availability and pathology grade? The margin is one net true-positive equivalent per 100 target-frame patients at the threshold's false-positive weight, not evidence of patient benefit. Support also requires positive increment across 0.25–0.35 and superiority to flag-all and flag-none.

**Unresolved attribution hypothesis A.** Does the identical radiology-semantic block add positive net benefit after measured radiology workflow/provenance variables are already present, comparing the nested `A=W+C_R` with `W`, under the same report and pathology uncertainty? This can support incremental modeled information beyond measured workflow features; it cannot establish semantic causality or absence of unmeasured process confounding.

These claims remain independent. `S-B` is the deployable clinical-value estimand; `A-W` is the nested workflow-attribution estimand; non-nested `S-W` is descriptive only. Attribution failure cannot erase a supported clinical-classification contrast, and attribution support without `S-B` support is not deployable clinical value.

### Why laboratory exclusion is the primary repair

A two-state “all laboratories available/all unavailable” bound is not exact: different results can finalize at different times. An exact latent-state construction would need one availability bit for every selected result row, use the union of those bits across compared models, and mask the whole row-derived vector—value, qualitative result, assay/specimen identity, presence, missingness, and timing—when unavailable. The source has neither a panel/accession key nor evidence supporting panel-wise correlation. Full independent state augmentation is finite, but its state count grows as `2^m` per patient in addition to radiology states; arbitrary grouping would sacrifice sharpness. Because every availability combination remains possible, the no-lab state can never be excluded, while model fitting and 2,000 full-pipeline bootstraps become disproportionately burdensome.

The primary policy therefore never queries the laboratory table. This is not an imputation and not an assumption that results were unavailable: it is a deployable information policy whose output is invariant to laboratory availability. Every laboratory-derived field, including row existence, assay ordering/presence, specimen, missingness, count, and time-since-test, is absent from `B,S,W,A,U`. Eventual-record laboratory analyses are diagnostics only. This repair converts the tested semantic block from mixed report-plus-laboratory information into radiology-report semantics alone and resolves the future-information gap without unsupported availability assumptions.

The substantive advance is an honest answer to whether routine report semantics alone can add decision-time-bounded M2 triage value over demographics, acquisition facts, and basic report extraction, and whether that same block adds information conditional on measured documentation workflow. Existing complete-case imaging-plus-serology models do not answer either question.

## Pathology-only endpoint and clinical adjudication

`Y=1` only for pathology-defined M2; `Y=0` for M0 or M1. `V=1` means a trustworthy index-specimen M0/M1/M2 assignment under the frozen position-aware parser and adjudication; otherwise `V=0`.

Assign grade only from the immediate selected value after an anchored field such as `MVI risk grade[:：]`, stopping before definitions, explanatory bullets, historical clauses, or a new specimen section, or from a frozen synonymous selected-value construction accepted in blinded development review. Handle negation, `not seen`, multiple specimens, and discordance. Unanchored M2, binary `microvascular invasion: present/absent`, positive-ungraded MVI, blank vessel-number/distance fields, absent MVI language, definitions, and conflicts cannot assign M0/M1/M2. Pathology never predicts.

Two independently implemented parsers must agree on locked test records. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 sampled by year/template, and every test `V=0`; a third resolves disagreement. Require class-specific PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95, Cohen kappa at least 0.90, and parser disagreement no more than 5%. Reviewers may derive grade only from explicit number/distance language under the frozen definition. Inadequate text, ambiguous specimen linkage, or inadequate sampling remains `V=0`.

Raw slides, specimen identifiers, block counts, vessel-distance measurements, and sampling protocol are unavailable. Text adjudication cannot establish biological M2 missed by tissue sampling.

## Shared radiology report-availability states

For patient `i`, let `q_i` be the number (0, 1, or 2) of selected CT/MRI bundles with nonempty eventual report text and let `Ω_i^R={0,1}^{q_i}`. One means the report is assumed finalized and visible at `t_dec`; zero means unavailable. Empty stored text has state zero only. No probability, independence, or monotonicity assumption is imposed.

At state zero, mask every report-body-derived item from that bundle: size, lesion count, macrovascular thrombus, report length/sections/template, semantic findings, and unrestricted text. Acquisition facts—valid acquisition time, modality from `Examination`, bundle count, distinct-`Examination number` count without the identifier, and development-frequency machine-availability category—remain. At state one, stored report variables are exposed. The same `r∈Ω_i^R` is used for every compared model for that patient. Choosing different states for S, B, W, or A is forbidden.

Development and 2019 recalibration use state augmentation: each patient contributes every feasible report state with total weight one, divided equally across states. Cross-validation, recalibration, bootstrapping, and permutation remain patient-grouped. Augmentation is a robustness construction, not evidence that a state occurred.

Laboratory variables do not receive a primary latent state because they are absent by policy. A compiler must not treat their absence as a missing-value feature or infer availability from `Test time`. Reliable result-release times recovered by an independent source-system audit would motivate a new scientific child, not a mechanical repair.

## Laboratory-independent models and baselines

All principal models are elastic-net logistic regressions fitted on the same `V=1` 2015–2018 patients and report-state augmentation. Fit a frozen alpha/penalty grid in 2015–2018; select the simplest setting within one bootstrap standard error of minimum patient-weighted 2019 log loss; then estimate one weighted logistic recalibration intercept and slope in 2019 without feature refitting. Development-only category handling, winsorization where applicable, and scaling apply. Every model scores every test patient under every report state.

**B — deployable structured/basic-report baseline**

- age and sex;
- modality and acquisition provenance available before report text: CT/MRI indicators and machine-availability category, but no raw identifier;
- only in report-available states, frozen extractions for maximum lesion diameter, lesion count (1, 2, at least 3), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown indicators.

B contains no laboratory column, laboratory-derived presence/missingness signal, report formatting, semantic C feature, year, diagnosis, procedure text, pathology, or post-cutoff content.

**S — deployable radiology-semantic strategy**

Exactly B plus radiology-semantic block `C_R`: only in report-available states, frozen present/absent/unknown extractions from `examination findings` and `examination diagnosis` for complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular or non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity.

S contains no report/examination code, raw machine identifier, report length, formatting/template family, accession number, year, laboratory field, procedure/diagnosis/pathology term, unrestricted n-gram, or post-cutoff content.

**W — radiology workflow/provenance negative control**

Exactly B plus acquisition count and time since last acquisition; reviewed non-semantic protocol category from exact `Check`; development-frequency machine category (never a raw identifier); and, in report-available states only, character/line/section counts and outcome-blind formatting family. Construct template family in 2015–2018 after masking clinical words, all numbers, dates, identifiers, and values, using punctuation, line breaks, section order, and repeated boilerplate. Unseen formats map to “unseen.” W receives no C_R feature and no laboratory-derived field. W is not proposed for deployment.

**A — process-adjusted radiology-semantic model**

Exactly `W+C_R`, where `C_R` is bit-for-bit identical to S's added block. Thus A-W is nested and isolates the incremental modeled information in report semantics conditional on measured radiology workflow/provenance. A is an attribution instrument, not the deployable candidate.

**U — diagnostic unrestricted-text ceiling**

A plus report-available development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after removing identifiers, dates, accession strings, pathology/MVI terms, and postoperative text. Audit the 100 largest absolute coefficients before unblinding. U contains no lab features, is secondary, and cannot rescue either failed claim.

Two blinded radiologists review at least 200 reports for B's size/count/thrombus extraction and, separately in each test year and CT/MRI provenance, at least 150 reports enriched for each C_R feature and parser-negative examples. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in at least 90%, count-category agreement at least 90% with kappa at least 0.80, and per-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity at least 0.85 for every C_R binary feature. This validates stored-text interpretation, not report visibility.

## Primary exact joint partial-identification estimands

For threshold `p`, let `w=p/(1-p)` and `A_Mi(r,p)=1[score_Mi(r)≥p]`. For contrast `k=(M_1,M_0)`, define

`d_i^k(y,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)][A_M1i(r,p)-A_M0i(r,p)]`.

For `V_i=1`,

`L_k(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i min_{r∈Ω_i^R} d_i^k(Y_i,r,p)`

and

`U_k(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i max_{r∈Ω_i^R} d_i^k(Y_i,r,p)`.

For `V_i=0`, each minimum and maximum additionally ranges over `y∈{0,1}`. `N` is the entire 2020–2021 target frame. With at most four report states and two endpoint states per patient, brute-force enumeration is exact. No patient is dropped, no V=0 is imputed, acquisition never proves report visibility, and no lab timestamp proves result visibility.

Compute joint report-availability/pathology-grade bounds for S-B (claim C), A-W (claim A), descriptive S-W, S-U, every model versus flag-none, and every model versus flag-all. For one model's net benefit use flag-none as comparator; flag-all has fixed action one and uncertain outcome is enumerated in the same state.

Intervals are coordinatewise sharp because patient states and missing outcomes are otherwise unrestricted. Endpoints for different contrasts can arise from different latent assignments and must not be called one jointly attained latent world. Requiring lower endpoints to pass is a conservative all-report-state guarantee. Sampling simultaneity does not change that interpretation.

The primary threshold is 0.30. Evaluate `p=0.20,0.21,…,0.40`; 0.25–0.35 is the prespecified robustness plateau, not an elicited clinical preference. Report flags and incremental flags for every model/state.

At 0.30 decompose interval width into pathology ambiguity with all reports available, report-availability ambiguity among V=1, and full joint ambiguity. Report the fraction whose action changes across `Ω_i^R`. Secondary all-report and no-report scenarios identify neither actual visibility nor the primary effect.

## Feasibility, validity, and reliability gates

Global interpretation requires:

- test N at least 1,000, adjudicated V=1 M2 at least 75, V=1 at least 85%, and review of every test V=0;
- both pathology parsers and pathology/radiology reliability thresholds above;
- identical patients and shared report states across models; exact masking of every report-derived variable at state zero; no forbidden feature or post-cutoff value;
- a machine-checkable zero-column intersection between every primary model matrix/serialized pipeline and all fields or derivatives originating from `labs`;

- no required primary variable's missingness shifting by more than 15 percentage points from 2019 to either 2020 or 2021; unseen template and machine-category drift reported;
- in both all-report and no-report V=1 diagnostics, S recalibration slope 0.80–1.20, intercept -0.10–0.10, sensitivity at least 0.70, and PPV at least 0.50 at 0.30;
- at least 100 patients and 20 V=1 M2 in each estimable CT-only and MRI-only analysis. Failure limits modality-specific generality but does not erase a valid pooled estimate; a harmful stratum forbids cross-modality robustness;
- complete bootstrap output and brute-force bound unit tests.

The inherited “each B laboratory available in at least 70%” gate is deleted because laboratories are not required primary variables. Claim C uses global, endpoint, extraction, calibration, and bootstrap gates. ## Prespecified result zones and bounded conclusions

**C supportive:** all C gates pass; the simultaneous lower confidence limit for `L_{S-B}(0.30)` is greater than 0.01; the simultaneous lower limit for `L_{S-B}(p)` is greater than zero at every threshold 0.25–0.35; S's lower-bound net benefit simultaneously exceeds flag-all and flag-none throughout the plateau; and neither test year's point lower bound is negative. Strongest conclusion: the laboratory-independent S policy has a material, threshold-robust historical weighted M2 classification increment over laboratory-independent B in this 2020–2021 institutional frame under the stated report/pathology uncertainty.

**C adverse:** with gates passed, the simultaneous upper confidence limit for `U_{S-B}(0.30)` is below 0.01, ruling out the prespecified material increment. An upper limit below zero supports classification harm. Failure of S to beat a default is adverse at that threshold. AUPRC gain, oracle-lab success, or all-reports-visible success cannot rescue an adverse primary decision result.

**C inconclusive:** gates fail or the simultaneous band spans the 0.01 margin. A positive point estimate with bounds spanning no material benefit is inconclusive, not supportive. Width must be attributed to report state, pathology, extraction, event count, or sampling uncertainty.

**A supportive:** all A gates pass; the simultaneous lower confidence limit for `L_{A-W}(p)` is above zero at every threshold 0.25–0.35; and observed 0.30 exceeds the matched-null 97.5th percentile. Strongest conclusion: the frozen radiology-semantic block contributes weighted M2 classification information beyond measured radiology workflow/provenance. This is neither causal attribution nor proof that all workflow confounding was measured.

**A adverse:** with gates passed, the simultaneous upper limit for `U_{A-W}` is at or below zero at any required plateau threshold, or the observed statistic does not exceed matched null. This falsifies robust attribution even if S-B is positive.

**A inconclusive:** reliability, overlap, permutation, or inference gates fail, or simultaneous bands span zero. This does not alter C.

Joint interpretation is mandatory:

- C and A supportive: deployable laboratory-independent historical increment is supported, and the same report-semantic block adds information beyond measured workflow.
- C supportive; A adverse/inconclusive: deployable historical increment is supported, but it must not be characterized as semantic rather than workflow-related.
- A supportive; C adverse/inconclusive: report semantics add information conditional on workflow, but no clinically material deployable increment is established.
- Both adverse: the corresponding prespecified hypotheses are falsified.
- Either inconclusive: name the failed gate or dominant uncertainty; do not convert imprecision into no association.

S-W remains descriptive and never gates either claim. Better U or eventual-record lab performance is diagnostic only.

Even dual support cannot show improved treatment selection, recurrence, survival, quality of life, harms, capacity, or cost-effectiveness. Those conclusions require prospective capture of information availability and clinician response, treatment and follow-up data, stakeholder threshold elicitation, and another study.
