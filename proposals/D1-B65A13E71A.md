# All-randomized composite validation of the vasopressor endpoint by exact risk-difference distortion

## Purpose, evidence boundary, and substantive repair

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It retains the parent's frozen MIMIC findings, prospective pump/intent requirements, clock escrow, adjudication safeguards, configuration transport rules, recurrence separation, and noncausal evidence boundary, but makes two changes required for a coherent randomized validation.

First, the primary validation endpoint is defined for **every eligible randomized patient**, rather than only for patients in whom a post-randomization qualifying cessation happened. A qualifying cessation and the actual first-stopped drug remain observed process variables and secondary diagnostic strata. They do not define the primary denominator. Thus the validation target is an all-randomized composite that could actually be used in an intention-to-treat (ITT) trial; no claim of ITT protection is made for an adherer subset.

Second, partial identification is specified as an exact aggregate-count optimization. The five indeterminate cells of each 3x3 E,R table are represented by integer allocations, with deterministic pruning/dominance or integer-program enumeration over aggregate counts. No person-level 2^m completion is used in the confirmatory analysis. Exhaustive person-level completion is retained only as a small-table oracle (m <= 8 unknown persons) to verify the aggregate implementation.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In a prospective, randomized, configuration-stratified validation cohort, an EHR-only all-randomized composite failure label has assignment-arm-specific sensitivity and specificity whose simultaneous confidence set implies a worst-case absolute distortion below 0.025 in a future randomized risk difference over the prespecified rescue/failure-rate region 0.10--0.40 and true-arm RD region [-0.10,+0.10].

The 0.025 threshold is half of a separately ratified 0.05 absolute noninferiority margin. The rate/effect region and margin must be approved by an independent critical-care, trial, and patient panel before enrollment; they are not learned from MIMIC. A supportive result validates only a measurement-error bound for the enumerated composite, population, configurations, clocks, and region. It does not establish which stopping order is beneficial, safe, noninferior, preferable, or causally effective.

Of 73 failures among 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models produced negligible Brier improvements (approximately 0.000174 and 0.000111) and adverse later transport. The complete input-event transaction audit covers all 137 episodes and found that five symmetric reconstruction rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices; the inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries.

These data support only that the retrospective endpoint is computable, restart-dominated, and transaction-sensitive. MIMIC lacks randomized assignment, pump-delivered streams, validated cessation/rescue intent, cross-clock maps, reliable MCS absence, and the prospective interface configurations. It cannot identify the proposed randomized measurement estimand.

## The primary all-randomized endpoint

Let `Z=0` be randomized NE-first and `Z=1` randomized VP-first. Eligibility is assessed and frozen before randomization. Let `tr` be randomization/strategy-assignment time. The endpoint is a binary **failure** endpoint, so larger risk is worse.

### Reference composite R

For every randomized patient, define the protocol's implementation interval as `[tr, tr+120 minutes]` and the outcome window as `(t0, min(t0+6 hours, death, ICU exit]]` when a qualifying cessation exists. Pump-native delivery and adjudicated intent are the reference sources.

1. `I=1` (successful assigned-stop implementation) only if, within 120 minutes after `tr`, the assigned drug is actually stopped first in the locked pump-native sense: its delivered rate crosses below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below for five continuous minutes while the other study drug remains above threshold. Both drugs crossing within five minutes is simultaneous and is not a successful assigned stop. Programmed rate without linked delivery is insufficient.
2. `I=0` if the assigned cessation is not achieved by 120 minutes, the nonassigned study drug is stopped first, stopping is simultaneous, or a captured clinician override/crossover prevents implementation. A death before an achieved stop is an implementation failure. A technically unobserved deadline is not silently made a failure; it is `R=?` unless complete pump/EHR/clinical capture establishes the protocol-defined failure.
3. For `I=1`, let `H=1` if, within six hours after the qualifying cessation and before earlier complete closure, the stopped drug again reaches its index threshold continuously for at least five minutes and two independent blinded critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators conclude that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion. Bag replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, technical pause, mistaken-stop correction, and uninterrupted delivery fragmented in documentation are `H=0`. `H=?` covers missing/discordant clock, export, linkage, or intent evidence after third-adjudicator review.
4. The primary reference endpoint is

`R = 1` if `I=0`, or if `I=1 and H=1`;
`R = 0` if `I=1 and H=0` with complete capture;
`R = ?` otherwise.

This makes nonimplementation part of the same clinically relevant failure composite instead of conditioning it away. The protocol must have clinical governance approval for treating failure to implement the assigned stop as an endpoint component. If governance rejects that component, the randomized validation must instead use a separately approved composite definition; it may not silently revert to a selected cessation subset.

The endpoint's ITT risk is `p_z=P(R=1|Z=z)` in the complete reference population under the locked closure rules. It is not a per-protocol effect and not the risk among those with `I=1`.

### EHR composite E

`E=1` is the EHR-only reconstruction of the same failure composite for every randomized patient:

* an EHR-native implementation audit, using the locked assignment and deadline rules, identifies failure to achieve the assigned stop by 120 minutes, nonassigned-first/simultaneous stop, or documented crossover/override; or
* when implementation succeeds, a new positive EHR interval for the actually assigned/stopped study drug starts strictly after the EHR-native stop boundary and before six-hour closure, with the same locked rate/unit rules.

`E=0` requires positive evidence of successful assigned implementation, complete observable follow-up to the relevant closure, and no qualifying EHR restart. An interval spanning the stop boundary is not new. `E=?` is assigned whenever mapped timestamp uncertainty crosses an implementation or outcome boundary, required linkage/capture is incomplete, or the available EHR evidence cannot distinguish a protocol failure from an unobserved event. Missingness is never a negative. The actual first-stopped drug `A` and an EHR label based on `A` are secondary diagnostics only; primary E is indexed to randomized assignment and the all-randomized composite.

This definition deliberately validates an endpoint that is present for every randomization. It does not claim that an EHR can observe pump delivery or intent without the prospective reference standard.

## Population, design, clocks, and adjudication

Enroll consecutive adults in an adult ICU who, before randomization, have linked pump-delivered NE and VP for at least six continuous hours; NE >0 and <=0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 and <=0.04 U/min with neither increased during the final 30 pre-randomization minutes; no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours; at least three valid MAP observations spanning at least three of four preceding 30-minute bins including the final bin, median MAP >=65 and minimum MAP >=55 mmHg; and treating-team judgment that either protocolized stopping order is permissible. The latter is clinical governance, not derivable from MIMIC.

Use at least six ICUs/sites and two pump-EHR interface families. A configuration is the complete tuple of site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication build, drug-library version, and clock-map method/version. Each configuration has a 50-episode technical run-in, requiring >=95% unique patient-pump-channel-drug linkage and complete export from two hours before `tr` through six hours after `t0` or complete closure, and >=90% contemporaneous intent evidence. Failed configurations repeat on 50 new episodes; a second failure is feasibility-only. Run-in patients are excluded from calibration and confirmation.

Randomize 1:1 in permuted blocks stratified by configuration and first/re-permitted re-randomization status. Eligibility, `Z`, and `tr` are locked before all post-randomization data. The first eligible randomized patient per person is the primary unit; recurrent episodes are collected separately and cannot increase primary denominators or rescue a failed primary result. A recurrent episode requires a new six-hour dual-drug baseline and `t0` at least 24 hours after the previous `t0`; it retains the original `Z` unless a separately governed re-randomization occurs before the new episode.

Actual stopping (`A`), adherence, crossover reason, time to implementation, simultaneous stopping, and `Z x A` cells are diagnostics. They are reported but never used to redefine the primary denominator. A large crossover or differential implementation rate can make the clinical trial estimand unattractive, but it cannot be hidden by selecting adherers.

Every source clock has an escrowed affine pump-time map with offset, drift, time zone, DST handling, fit rows, residuals, and immutable version. The boundary band is the run-in 99th-percentile absolute residual capped at 60 seconds; a required wider band fails qualification. Death or ICU exit closes a determinate window only with complete capture through closure. A gap creates `?`, not a negative. Two blinded adjudicators see pump/monitor traces, workflow events, cointerventions, and contemporaneous intent evidence, but not E, aggregate assignment results, or one another's labels; a third adjudicator resolves disagreement. After 100 calibration episodes, raw agreement <85% or both Gwet AC1 and kappa <0.70 suspends accrual. Clarification cannot inspect E x R.

## Exact estimand and simultaneous partial identification

For assignment arm `z`, among the all-randomized composite outcomes, define

`Se_z = P(E=1 | R=1,Z=z)` and `Sp_z = P(E=0 | R=0,Z=z)`.

PPV, NPV, marginal E positivity, and prevalence are descriptive. For a hypothetical true arm failure risk `p_z`, the EHR risk is

`q_z = Se_z p_z + (1-Sp_z)(1-p_z)`.

For `Delta_R=p_1-p_0` and `Delta_E=q_1-q_0`, distortion is

`b(theta,p) = Delta_E - Delta_R`, where `theta=(Se_0,Sp_0,Se_1,Sp_1)`.

The locked clinical region is `Omega={(p0,p1): 0.10<=pz<=0.40, |p1-p0|<=0.10}`. Its six vertices are `(0.10,0.10)`, `(0.10,0.20)`, `(0.20,0.10)`, `(0.30,0.40)`, `(0.40,0.30)`, `(0.40,0.40)`. Because b is affine in each parameter and p, the maximum over a rectangular confidence set and Omega is exactly attained at 16 confidence-set corners times six Omega vertices. The verifier must enumerate all 96 combinations and retain the signed maximizing witness.

For each arm, derive two-sided exact Clopper-Pearson intervals for sensitivity and specificity with per-parameter alpha 0.0125. Their Cartesian product has at least 95% simultaneous coverage by Bonferroni over four parameters. The primary bound is

`B_U=max_{theta in C, p in Omega}|b(theta,p)|`.

Acceptability requires strict `B_U < 0.025`; equality fails. The bound is decision-relevant, conservative, and does not assume nondifferential error.

### Aggregate exact handling of E,R in {0,1,?}

Within each assignment arm, retain the full nine aggregate counts `n_00,n_01,n_10,n_11,n_?0,n_?1,n_0?,n_1?,n_??`, where the first index is E and the second is R. Fixed cells contribute directly to `(TN,FN,FP,TP)=(00,01,10,11)`. Unknown cells are allocated by integer variables:

* `?0 -> (TN,FP)`;
* `?1 -> (FN,TP)`;
* `0? -> (TN,FN)`;
* `1? -> (FP,TP)`;
* `?? -> (TN,FN,FP,TP)` with total allocation equal to `n_??`.

For each assignment arm and each feasible aggregate allocation, compute `Se=TP/(TP+FN)` and `Sp=TN/(TN+FP)` confidence endpoints from the exact CP formulas. Joint completion means one allocation is used simultaneously for all four endpoints; metric-wise separate imputations are forbidden.

The confirmatory algorithm is exact and scalable in the following sense. It does not expand people or binary strings. For each arm it constructs a table of feasible sufficient-count states `(r=TP+FN, tp=TP, n0=TN+FP, tn=TN)` from the five aggregate unknown groups using integer convolution. At each convolution step, retain only states that are not dominated for any of the finite objective directions generated by the 96 corner/vertex evaluations; ties are retained with a witness allocation. Equivalently, the compiler may solve, for each of the 96 signed corner objectives, the finite integer optimization over the four allocation counts and use exact CP endpoint lookup. Counts are bounded by the arm total, so this is an aggregate-count dynamic program/integer program with polynomial state storage after dominance pruning, not person-level enumeration. Integer arithmetic is used for counts; CP endpoints use a pinned SciPy/R implementation and recorded version.

For every signed corner/vertex objective, optimize the two arms independently because the table completion constraints factor by randomized arm. Take the maximum over objectives and retain one aggregate allocation witness per arm. This proves that the joint worst case reduces to aggregate tables plus the 96 confidence/effect corners. The implementation must be cross-checked against exhaustive person-level binary completion for every small synthetic table with <=8 unknown persons per arm; equality of the maximum, not agreement of a preferred imputation, is required. The existing small-table oracle is retained at `[internal dataset path]` and must not be mistaken for the large-table production optimizer.

The indeterminacy safeguards remain: overall `?` >5%, >7.5% in any assignment-by-configuration cell with at least 20 patients, or an arm difference >5 percentage points is operationally adverse and precludes support, regardless of a numerical bound. Joint worst-case exact bounds are primary; Jeffreys missingness models (odds ratios 0.5, 1, 2, 5) are sensitivity analyses only.

## Deterministic sample-size and operating-characteristic lock

The confirmatory sample is fixed before the first confirmatory patient. There is no calibration-derived adaptive N, denominator-triggered accrual, arm-specific closeout, or success stopping.

The immutable planning constants are CP alpha 0.0125 for each of four two-sided intervals, assurance tail 0.0125 for each of eight exact binomial events, design sensitivity 0.99, design specificity 0.995, failure-risk range [0.10,0.40], true RD range [-0.10,+0.10], and distortion gate 0.025. The exact deterministic rule is:

1. For an even per-arm candidate `n`, take the exact binomial lower/upper envelope of reference-positive counts over p in [0.10,0.40] using the 0.0125 tails.
2. For every count in that envelope, take exact upper binomial-tail limits for false negatives with true rate 0.01 and false positives with true rate 0.005, again at tail 0.0125.
3. Convert those integer thresholds to CP lower bounds for sensitivity and specificity.
4. Use the worst lower envelopes as the lower sides of a rectangular confidence set and upper side 1. Enumerate 16 x 6 corners; find the smallest even per-arm n for which the worst distortion is strictly below 0.025 and the Bonferroni assurance is at least 0.90 uniformly over the locked prevalence interval.
5. Lock `N_confirm=2n` and never change it based on observed R prevalence.

The executable calculation at `[internal dataset path]`, run output `[internal dataset path]`, gives the adjacent-integer lock `n=6,266` per assignment arm, hence `N_confirm=12,532`; at n=6,265 the bound is 0.025002241068110165, while at n=6,266 it is 0.024999894705478087. The eight-tail Bonferroni assurance is exactly 0.90. Output hashes in this workspace are: script [source checksum]; JSON [source checksum].

A separate 1,200-patient calibration/run-in may assess capture, adjudication, prevalence, and operational feasibility, but it is excluded from confirmatory estimates and cannot alter N. If governance does not ratify the composite, if six sites/two interface families cannot remain active, if N_confirm cannot accrue within 48 months, or if reference/clock/adjudication quality fails, the result is inconclusive. At lock, fewer than 600 reference-positive or 3,000 reference-negative patients in either arm is inconclusive rather than a reason to extend or selectively enroll. These counts are feasibility gates, not a replacement for the fixed N.

The planning assumption is not an acceptance threshold: observed performance below the design point can fail the distortion gate. The deterministic calculation also does not guarantee that indeterminate data, transport, crossover, or clinical governance will pass.

## Future-trial interpretation

A future all-randomized trial must use the same composite, assignment-indexed EHR label, clock, implementation deadline, and closure rules, or perform a new validation. Let its EHR risk-difference upper confidence limit be `U_E`. If `U_E < 0.05-B_U`, the bounded measurement implication is `Delta_R < 0.05` under the validated model and region. If its lower limit is at least `0.05+B_U`, inferiority follows. An interval in `[0.05-B_U,0.05+B_U]` is measurement-inconclusive. This is not a benefit claim: an EHR estimate crossing zero does not establish benefit, and endpoint validation alone does not establish safety, equipoise, clinical appropriateness, or causal effect.

If a future trial instead defines failure only after an actual cessation, conditions on adherers, or treats nonimplementation as missing, the validated all-randomized composite does not transport. It needs a new validation and a separately stated per-protocol or treatment-policy estimand. Randomized validation is coherent only for a protocol that accepts the composite's implementation component; otherwise the appropriate conclusion is that this design cannot support the later decision.

## Configuration, recurrence, and transport gates

The primary population is first randomized patients, with recurrent episodes secondary and patient-clustered. No site, configuration, or interface family contributes >30% of any of the four reference-status denominators. Each of four configurations has at least 100 reference-positive and 500 reference-negative observations per arm, and three configurations have at least 200 per arm; sparse cells are inconclusive. Report assignment-arm and configuration-specific estimates, hierarchical summaries, prediction intervals, and exact leave-one-site/configuration/family-out bounds; every leave-out mixture must retain `B_U<0.025`. A configuration with at least 100 positives and 500 negatives per arm must itself have conservative bound <0.05. Prediction implying Se<0.90 or Sp<0.98, adjusted accuracy range >0.10, material Z-by-configuration interaction, or recurrent bound >0.05 or >0.025 worse than first-patient bound is adverse.

Configuration linkage, clock, or export failure >5% in the latest 50 episodes triggers a new run-in and excludes repaired rows from the confirmatory mixture. Version changes create new configurations. At least four active sites must remain; otherwise the study is operationally inconclusive.

## Falsification, interpretation, and verifier requirements

Support requires fixed-N completion, all-randomized disposition coverage, `B_U<0.025` under the joint aggregate worst case, acceptable indeterminacy, adjudication/clock/capture reliability, configuration and leave-out gates, and no material recurrent degradation. It supports only bounded measurement error for the all-randomized composite. Adverse evidence includes `B_U>=0.025`, assignment-dependent error, differential implementation/crossover that makes the planned clinical strategy incoherent, poor sensitivity/specificity, recurrence degradation, configuration heterogeneity, or systematic pump-positive/EHR-negative rescues. High PPV, actual-stop symmetry, pooled accuracy, or successful adherer-only analysis cannot override adverse primary evidence. Inconclusive results include inadequate reference counts, missing all-randomized dispositions, unratified composite or margin, infeasible fixed N/time/site requirements, failed randomization, unresolved clocks/linkage/intent, or a later trial using a different endpoint/estimand.

The verifier must check eligibility and randomization precede post-randomization fields; exactly one all-randomized disposition per eligible patient; no `I=1` conditioning in the primary denominator; correct composite truth table; correct boundary uncertainty and closure; complete nine-cell tables including EHR-negative and implementation-failure patients; CP intervals; the exact 96 corner/vertex evaluations; aggregate DP/optimization versus person-level oracle for <=8 unknowns; fixed N and adjacent-integer minimality; all transport and leave-out gates; and future-trial interpretation. Synthetic adversarial cases must include crossover/Simpson behavior, identical actual-stop accuracy but differing Z accuracy, EHR-positive PPV with low specificity at p=.10, all-randomized nonimplementation failures, joint-indeterminate completion defeating separate imputations, clock-boundary ties, missing negatives, arm-specific truncation, recurrent double counting, and unsupported benefit/NI prose.

Computation can establish table integrity, deterministic labels from supplied streams, exact bounds, and whether conclusions match gates. It cannot establish physical delivery from EHR linkage, rescue intent without adjudication, MCS absence, safety/equipoise, clinical ratification of the composite or margin, treatment benefit, or causal trial effects.
