# Respiratory-transition evidence and early failure after first ICU discharge

## Scientific deliverable and claim discipline

The future Harbor solver must newly construct one leakage-safe adult first-ICU-discharge cohort and fit two matched models on the same patient-level splits and record-derived inputs. Completion is demonstrated only by the following newly generated artifacts:

1. `run_manifest.json`: exact source paths, source/catalog/snapshot hashes, schema hashes, archive members, dictionary labels, software versions, split seeds, locked rules, resource use, and output hashes.
2. `cohort_audit.parquet`: every candidate first stay, eligibility flags, first-stay selection, temporal checks, 12-hour completeness, coverage, exclusion reason, split and analysis-population flags.
3. `hourly_features.parquet`: twelve one-hour rows in ([t-12mathrm h,t)), channel values, support evidence, interval clipping, observation masks and process features; no value with clinical time at or after (t=	exttt{outtime}).
4. `transition_evidence_audit.parquet`: every selected chart, input and procedure row, raw value/time, validity decision, evidence tier, half-window state, contradiction, missingness state, and final cross-domain exposure label.
5. `transfer_outcome_audit.parquet`: every subsequent ICU candidate and every candidate transfer interval, overlap calculations, ICU-unit vocabulary decision, topology label, event time and tie resolution.
6. `bound_cif.parquet`: 6-, 24- and 48-hour CIFs for the documented-bridge lower bound (L), any-return upper bound (U), in-hospital death and alive hospital discharge; bound widths and prespecified contrasts.
7. Locked patient-level test predictions from both the transparent baseline and the observation-aware factorized hidden semi-Markov alternative, with calibration, IPCW Brier score, overlap, state diagnostics and subject-bootstrap uncertainty.
8. `falsification_outputs.parquet` and `interpretation_map.parquet`: each diagnostic and each prose conclusion linked to computed rows, estimates, intervals, event counts, positivity and evidence limits.

No association, model performance, clinical benefit or causal effect is claimed in this proposal. Readiness and source probes are not clinical results.

## Question, evidence boundary and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports only that MIMIC contains heterogeneous recorded respiratory and vasoactive support signals near ICU outtime and that later ICU returns have incomplete transfer topology. It does not establish successful extubation, actual continuous ventilation, clinician intent, discharge appropriateness, deterioration, preventability or a treatment effect.

The unresolved claim is whether a conservative, record-compatible respiratory transition separates early post-discharge pathways from an oxygen-only transition, and whether apparent separation is still present when the recorded observation process and competing death/discharge events are handled explicitly.

Primary hypotheses, tested as associations rather than interventions:

- the invasive-liberation-compatible recorded transition has a larger 48-hour return-compatible contrast than the oxygen-only transition;
- recorded vasoactive cessation without a respiratory transition has a larger 48-hour death contrast than either respiratory transition;
- joint recorded respiratory-plus-vasoactive withdrawal has the greatest total early-failure burden, while its return and death components may differ.

A contrast is not a clinical rule. A positive result means that the specified record phenotype is associated with the specified observable outcome under the MIMIC measurement process.

Create six one-hour bins in each half: (H_0=[t-12h,t-6h)), (H_1=[t-6h,t)). A respiratory observation bin has at least one valid numeric row among O2 flow, FiO2, PEEP, respiratory rate or SpO2; a MAP observation bin has a valid row for 220052 or 220181. The primary covered population requires at least two respiratory and two MAP observation bins in each half. Raw respiratory half-states are:

- `R_off_recorded`: no positive O2/FiO2/PEEP and no procedure marker in either half, with respiratory coverage in both halves;
- `R_O2_transition`: positive O2 flow or FiO2 in (H_0), no positive O2/FiO2/PEEP in (H_1), no PEEP or intubation/NIV/extubation marker in the whole window, and channel-level observations sufficient to establish the final non-positive state;
- `R_inv_liberation`: a valid extubation marker in \\(H_1\\), positive PEEP or FiO2 in \\(H_0\\), and no positive PEEP/FiO2 in \\(H_1\\). Respiratory state assignment is independent of vasoactive state, so this state can form the prespecified joint group. It is only “compatible with liberation”; it is not continuous ventilation or successful extubation;
- `R_other_or_unknown`: active, contradictory, inadequate or otherwise unclassifiable respiratory evidence.

Raw vasoactive half-states are:

- `V_cessation_recorded`: a valid listed vasoactive interval overlaps (H_0) and no valid listed interval overlaps (H_1);
- `V_active_recorded`: a valid listed interval overlaps (H_1);
- `V_no_recorded_interval`: no valid listed interval overlaps either half, explicitly an absence-of-record state rather than proof of physiologic off;
- `V_unknown`: invalid or contradictory timing.

The final primary exposure is mutually exclusive and assigned by this ordered ledger, with `other_or_unknown` retained rather than merged:

1. `recorded_off_no_vasoactive_interval`: R_off_recorded + V_no_recorded_interval;
2. `oxygen_transition_no_vasoactive_interval`: R_O2_transition + V_no_recorded_interval;
3. `invasive_liberation_compatible_no_vasoactive_interval`: R_inv_liberation + V_no_recorded_interval;

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Both methods consume the same locked ledger, twelve hourly rows, q imputations, topology labels, covariates and split assignments. No method may use text, post-outtime data or a different cohort.

The transparent inferential baseline is a pooled one-hour discrete-time cause-specific hazard model. Fit separate outcome systems for L and U, each with death and alive discharge as competing causes; for L, an earlier U-only return is censoring for the directly documented lower-bound endpoint. Include exposure indicators, age, gender, anchor_year_group, ICU LOS, admission_type/location, prior transfer count and transfer density, final/preceding physiology summaries, support intensity/duration, channel masks, row counts and process features. Fit predeclared nested models (physiology/support only, observation/process only, full) with training-only standardization and validation-locked regularization. Convert predicted hazards to held-out CIFs by standardization to the covered test population. Also report unadjusted Aalen-Johansen CIFs. This is the primary estimand because every covariate and label is auditable.

The substantive alternative is an observation-aware factorized hidden semi-Markov model on the same 12xP tensor. Respiratory latent states are `unknown, off, oxygen, ventilatory, liberation-compatible`; vasoactive states are `unknown, no-recorded-interval, active, cessation`. Each factor has duration-dependent transitions, persistence constraints, and an explicit emission model for the same physiology/support values plus source-specific masks, row counts and procedure markers. A third post-landmark location process has states ICU, documented non-ICU bridge and unknown; its hazards feed L/U/death/discharge but never replace the observed bounds. Fit emissions, transition/duration parameters, observation-mask model and outcome-head parameters by training-set composite likelihood; choose duration cap, hidden dimension and regularization by validation likelihood plus calibration/Brier, then lock test predictions. State labels are latent representations, not clinical adjudication. Any model-derived latent transition CIF must lie within [L,U] under every declared bridge-capture/false-bridge grid or be reported as invalid.

The HSMM is scientifically substantive because it can test whether ordering, persistence, duration and discordance between respiratory and hemodynamic domains contain information lost by fixed H0/H1 summaries. The baseline tests the clinically interpretable observable association. Agreement is required for stronger claims; a predictive improvement alone is not a scientific success criterion.

A generic GRU/TCN is deferred: it would add sequence capacity without resolving the evidence-tier and topology uncertainty. Causal discharge-policy effects, readiness/intent NLP, ward monitoring adequacy, external validation, continuous ventilator waveforms, MIMIC-CXR images and outside-hospital readmissions are deferred because the required adjudication or modalities are unavailable.

## Uncertainty, falsification and interpretation gates

Use subject-level bootstrap (2000 replicates if within the approved solver envelope) for CIFs, compatible contrasts, calibration and Brier scores, preserving the L/U algebra within every replicate. Report 95% percentile intervals, event counts, subgroup overlap, effective sample size, calibration intercept/slope, IPCW Brier, model convergence, HSMM state entropy and split stability across seeds 17/29/43. If 2000 replicates do not fit the approved envelope, report the approved smaller number and the measured limit; do not silently omit uncertainty.

Falsification and robustness outputs are locked:

- all clinical-time leakage checks, including `storetime)-only negative control;
- within-window order shuffling and +6-hour shifted transfer topology;
- physiology-only, support-only, masks/process-only and combined ablations;
- removal of procedure markers, channel-specific analyses, broad respiratory recoding and q/window/coverage sensitivities;
- transfer-careunit permutation, ICU-vocabulary removal, bridge-duration/tolerance grid;
- pre-landmark pseudo-return negative control;
- discharge-as-censoring sensitivity;
- admission type, first/last ICU unit, anchor-year and transfer-density strata;
- latent-state stability and out-of-bound rejection.

Supportive evidence requires a prespecified contrast’s worst-case L/U interval to remain directionally stable at 6, 24 and 48 hours, its uncertainty to exclude zero before any directional claim, adequate event support/overlap, baseline and HSMM agreement on observable outcomes, topology permutation to remove the signal, and no dependence on storetime or one channel. This supports only a robust record-compatible association.

Adverse evidence is subtype reversal or collapse after observation/q adjustment, a signal only in U with null/adverse L, dependence on one procedure or topology rule, persistence after topology permutation, non-positivity, or an out-of-bound latent estimate. A broad respiratory signal with no subtype separation is adverse to the subtype hypothesis but may support only nonspecific recorded instability.

Inconclusive evidence is wide intervals, sparse strata, high unknown/discordant prevalence, unstable q/topology frontiers, high HSMM state entropy, poor overlap, failed convergence or disagreement between methods. No inconclusive result is converted into a negative clinical claim.

The interpretation map must label each conclusion as computationally checkable, dependent on unavailable clinical adjudication, or requiring another study. Automated verification can check bindings, transformations, timestamps, event labels, fits, uncertainty and prose-to-output consistency. It cannot establish actual ventilation, extubation success, intent, ward deterioration, preventability, discharge appropriateness, causal effects or benefit from a monitoring policy.
