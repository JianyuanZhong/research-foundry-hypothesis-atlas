# Episode 97 integrated source-symmetric H120 recorded-policy experiment

Parents: `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`.

## Unresolved question and substantive advance

Among adults recorded as invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48 after recent vasopressor-supported shock, with materially positive recorded fluid balance and adequate recorded perfusion, urine output and potassium, is starting or restarting a qualifying furosemide/bumetanide ICU input record during the following 12 hours associated with a greater probability of being alive and demonstrably outside a recorded ICU at the five-day landmark, without exceeding prespecified recorded renal, RRT, death or terminal-disposition harm margins?

This is clinically important because deresuscitation may facilitate recovery from critical illness, but premature diuresis may worsen renal perfusion. The decision is consequential and current evidence does not establish the optimal timing or identify who benefits. The strongest available evidence supports only equipoise: the inspected full text of the cited MIMIC-lineage literature in the parent candidates describes heterogeneous, low-certainty diuretic evidence and unresolved timing/selection questions. The research-ambition demonstrations motivate longitudinal temporal validation, calibration and bias audits; they do not provide evidence for this treatment question. The unavailable cancer main article and STAR Methods were not inspected and are not used as evidence.

The experiment tests a recorded ICU-input policy association, not a causal treatment effect. Its substantive advance is a single frozen contract that (i) preserves the demonstrated feasibility repair that V0 procedure `storetime` is a completion/finalization clock rather than a validated baseline row-entry or awareness clock, (ii) uses an outcome-independent H120 recorded-topology primary with explicit unknownness rather than rewarding missing ICU data, and (iii) measures eMAR concordance symmetrically in both arms without allowing eMAR to overwrite the primary exposure.

## Population, clocks and treatment

For each discovery subject, order ICU stays by `(intime,stay_id)), select the earliest broad qualifying opportunity and never reopen a later stay. Set `t0=intime+48 hours`; require adult age from `anchor_age + calendar_year(intime)-anchor_year`, valid admission linkage, `admittime<=t0<outtime), and the frozen recent-pressor, MAP, weight, urine, potassium, positive unit-safe balance, ECMO/RRT and loop-washout gates.

V0 is exactly one accepted item-225792 row on the selected stay with parseable `starttime<endtime`, `starttime<=t0<endtime`, and status in `FinishedRunning, Stopped`. Multiple accepted crossings, Paused-only rows, invalid times, unknown statuses and key mismatches are excluded. Define `C0=min(t0+12 hours,V0.endtime)`. V0 `endtime` is a retrospective process boundary, not future bedside knowledge.

V0 `storetime` cannot alter eligibility, C0, exposure, outcome, covariates, weights or branches. Report missingness and the distributions of `starttime->storetime` and `endtime->storetime`, including status strata. Independently rebuild the parent’s non-V0 as-of subset: E_M_minus_V0 excludes a subject only when late/missing non-V0 timestamped rows change a prespecified gate or ambiguity; E_G_minus_V0 is the strict all-relevant-rows-stored-by-t0 sensitivity. Neither is allowed to reinterpret MIMIC as a live decision system.

Create equal clones at t0. A12 is compatible with the first qualifying loop onset strictly in `(t0,C0)`; B12 is compatible with no qualifying onset through C0. Use half-open intervals, exact duplicate collapse, linkorderid course grouping, finite positive mg or mg/hour rules and frozen status/unit rules. Death, explicit ambiguity, ICU exit and loop/C0 precedence are shared by both clones. No post-baseline variable or extubation outcome can affect E_R, arm, clone entry or weights.

## Primary endpoint and secondary measurement

The primary is H120-RINO at `H120=t0+120 hours=intime+168 hours`, not “day 7.” Classify with this fixed precedence:

1. Definite failure `[0,0]` for death by H120 supported by admissions death fields and consistent hospital-expire timing.
2. Otherwise failure `[0,0]` if any valid same-subject ICU stay crosses H120.
3. Definite recorded non-ICU success `[1,1]` only if the admission is open at H120, no ICU stay crosses H120, and exactly one same-admission transfer interval crossing H120 names a non-ICU careunit.
4. Unknown `[0,1]` for live discharge before H120, missing/conflicting closure, no or multiple crossing transfer rows, ICU/transfer disagreement, careunit gap, DOD day ambiguity, or outside-system closure. Absence of an ICU row is never success.

Compute normalized Hájek lower and upper risks and the interval `[L_A-U_B,U_A-L_B]`; never impute unknowns as failures or successes. Report all state components and unknown reasons.

Airway-task/extubation and 48-hour airway-event-free recovery are secondary mechanism diagnostics using procedureevents item 227194 and the frozen recurrent-airway/chart item set, with a single predeclared 48-hour observation rule. They cannot replace H120-RINO or be called durable extubation. If the airway diagnostic reverses while H120 does not, report discordance rather than selecting the favorable endpoint.

In both arms, independently classify the symmetric eMAR matrix during `[t0,C0)`: administration-like events require loop medication name, accepted event text, no affirmative complete-dose-not-given detail, and a valid non-oral/IV route or positive dose evidence. Missing/conflicting route or dose is unknown. Require selected-stay ICU topology using transfers and icustays. Report I+M+, I+M-, I+M-unknown, I-M+, I-M-, I-M-unknown. eMAR never overwrites I. A secondary administration-like interpretation is available only if both arms have at least 70% classifiable, one-sided 95% lower P(M+|I+) >=0.80 and upper P(M+|I-) <=0.10; otherwise it is exposure-inconclusive.

## Analysis, outcomes and falsification

Preserve the parent’s outcome-independent five-fold cross-fitted arm-specific compatibility models, pre-hour history covariates, numerator-free clipped/truncated weights, normalized Hájek estimators, 1,999 successful subject bootstraps, centered one-sided tests and Holm FWER across the frozen H120, renal, RRT, terminal and mortality families. Rebuild population, folds, preprocessing, models, weights, outcomes and multiplicity in every bootstrap. Use the parent’s explicit sample/ESS/balance/positivity minima and material-reversal definition.

Secondary harms are recorded creatinine worsening (with fixed baseline/follow-up clocks), observed post-landmark RRT use with exact and date-bracketed bounds, in-hospital death and terminal-disposition harm. These are records/proxies, not KDIGO AKI, incident kidney failure, survival, safety or receipt.

Supportive requires valid computation, minimum counts, overlap/balance, a simultaneous H120 lower-bound contrast exceeding the frozen benefit margin, all harm margins satisfied, no adjusted adverse family, no material sensitivity reversal, and acceptable unknownness. It supports only an association compatible with an early ICU input-record policy.

Adverse requires a validity-passing, multiplicity-controlled unfavorable H120 or harm contrast; it argues against prioritizing the recorded policy in this population but does not prove loop toxicity or inefficacy. Margin-falsified means the valid H120 upper bound fails to exceed the prespecified benefit margin. Inconclusive includes bounds crossing margins, excessive H120 unknownness, failed eMAR concordance for an administration claim, failed non-V0 as-of concordance, failed overlap, negative-control signal, or era/endpoint reversal. Population, join, split, clock, bootstrap or schema failures are invalid computation, not clinical evidence.
