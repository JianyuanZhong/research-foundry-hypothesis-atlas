# Capacity-robust patient protection for a report-robust HCC documentary-M2 queue

## Targeted successor and unresolved question

This Episode-80 child evolves assessed-valid [prior hypothesis] and [prior hypothesis]. It preserves their source-defined adult first eligible liver-resection population, coherent operation-clock alternatives, preoperative acquisition windows, 2015–2018 development / 2019 lock / separate 2020 and 2021 tests, complete whole-accession report units, global radiology-by-pathology reader worlds, fixed patient-balanced B/R/G/Gmask scores, same-fit semantic mask, exact q_req and q_M2 patient-retention gates, age–recorded-sex subgroup gate q_safe, coupled uncertainty, anchored whole-patient deletion, and fail-closed claim limits.

The remaining decision-validity gap is capacity robustness. A fixed-K queue can pass at one quota while losing documentary-M2 patients, or changing substantially, when the available review service is smaller or larger. Daily staffing, referral volume, and acceptable queue size are not available in this snapshot. The computable question is therefore:

> Across a prespecified range of hypothetical fixed review capacities, does incomplete preoperative stored-report exposure preserve the documentary-M2 patients that the corresponding maximum-content queue would have offered, while maintaining the inherited comparator and subgroup safety gates?

The substantive advance is a capacity frontier, not a new predictor. It distinguishes a result safe only at one arbitrary quota from one stable across plausible fixed-capacity operating points. It does not assert that any quota is clinically acceptable or that reviewing a documentary-M2 patient improves care.

## Evidence boundary and hypothesis

The strongest available evidence is source feasibility, not deployment validity. The HCC catalog, full table metadata, and direct source audit establish patient/encounter-linked procedures, acquisition-like CT/MRI times, eventual report narratives, and same-encounter pathology narratives. The source has no radiology finalization/release/view time, pathology time or specimen/accession identifier, raw images or slides, pathology sampling adequacy, MDT workload/capacity, clinician response, treatment assignment, recurrence, survival, harms, cost, or patient utility. The configured snapshot is institutional and its release manifest is absent.

The inspected literature motivating the parent question supports that MVI/M2 is clinically prognostic but does not validate this report-content queue or show that triage changes outcomes. The cited 2026 retrospective MVI study and 2026 review of early-recurrence models are contextual evidence only; neither supplies report visibility, a prospective queue, or treatment benefit for this experiment.

The confirmatory hypothesis is:

- At report-bundle coverage rho=0.10, for every complete source/onset/reader/pathology/year/deletion state, and for each capacity fraction k in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} of the 2019-frozen state-specific reference population, the incomplete-content G queue passes all inherited strict G-versus-B, G-versus-R, G-versus-Gmask, capacity-matched-random, and q_safe age-by-recorded-sex documentary-M2 gates; and every documentary-M2 patient in the corresponding maximum-content G queue is retained.
- The capacity frontier q_cap, defined below, is <1 in both 2020 and 2021 jointly.

The primary claim is only retrospective robustness of a fixed scoring rule under hypothetical stored-content masks. A supportive result cannot establish actual report visibility, biological MVI, fairness, treatment benefit, safety, or deployment readiness.

The person-level unit is one first eligible episode. The queue unit is one patient and one slot. Sex is the administrative recorded 性别 value and age is the recorded 年龄; q_safe remains a documentary subgroup check, not a fairness or biological-sex claim.

## Capacity perturbation estimands

Let N_ref(z) be the 2019-frozen reference population size for the same information set and K_f(z)=floor(f*N_ref(z)), with f in {0.05,0.10,0.20}. Require in 2019 N_ref>=500, K_.05>=25, K_.10>=50, and K_.20>=100; each test state must have N>=K_.10 and at least 50 documentary-positive events. If a claimed capacity has fewer than 25 slots or a test state lacks the required events, that capacity is feasibility-inconclusive rather than silently dropped.

For each capacity and complete state z, construct frozen-score top-K queues for incomplete reports and maximum stored content. EMPTY is retained under anchored singleton deletion; deleting a patient never backfills or requotas. Let S_G,f(z) and S_max,f(z) be the non-EMPTY patient sets.

The inherited gates are evaluated separately at every f, year, and complete state:
- 100*(H_G,f-H_C,f)/K_f > 5 versus each C in {B,R,Gmask}.
- Exact random-K cross-product equivalent to 100*H_G,f/K_f - 100*Y/N > 5.
- q_safe: no loss of documentary-M2 captures versus B, R, Gmask, or random within each predeclared 性别 × age cell (female/male × 18–64/65+), with demographic-resolution and zero-event cells inconclusive.
- Inherited q_req/q_M2 gates and their exact boundary witnesses.

Define the capacity-specific retained-positive predicate J_M2,f(z)=1[P_max,f(z) subseteq S_G,f(z)], where P_max,f(z)={i in S_max,f(z):Y_i(z)=1}.

Define CAP_PASS(q) as true only when, for every q-attainable state with Q(z)>=q, every year in {2020,2021}, and every f in {0.05,0.10,0.20}, all inherited gates and J_M2,f=1 pass. Define q_cap=min{q in attainable report-coverage ratios: CAP_PASS(q)=1}. Emit q_req, q_M2, q_safe, and q_cap only where denominators are defined. The expected nesting is q_req <= q_M2 <= q_cap; if this fails, stop as computationally inconclusive and expose the exact state/capacity witness. This nesting is a verifier invariant, not a biological assertion.

At every q_cap boundary state and every capacity emit H counts, N, Y, K, n_A, e, Q, protected queue hashes, S_max minus S_G positive/negative counts, S_G minus S_max positive/negative counts, EMPTY slots, ordered-slot disagreements, and age-by-recorded-sex cell counts. Report full capacity curves; no favorable capacity or state may be selected post hoc.

## Inference and falsification

Use 2,000 patient-level bootstrap replicates within calendar period, rebuilding coupled states, robust fitting, all capacities, queues, deletion, and frontier endpoints. Use one simultaneous max-|t| band over all capacity × year × comparator × state endpoint coordinates and frontier summaries; report pointwise intervals only descriptively. More than 2.5% failed replicates, incomplete state replay, or missing bootstrap lineage is computationally inconclusive. Use 1,000 development-only semantic-null repetitions that permute the entire report semantic block within year, modality, provenance, report-state cardinality, and formatting-family availability; the observed all-report capacity curve must exceed the 97.5th null percentile for the confirmatory signal.

Primary labels are mutually exclusive and precedence ordered:
1. computationally_inconclusive: any raw-row/order, whole-accession, clock, state coupling, reader-book, fixed-score, queue, deletion, frontier, invariant, bootstrap, or null replay fails.
2. feasibility_inconclusive: population, denominator, slot, event, subgroup, or capacity requirements fail.
3. maximum_content_signal_adverse: maximum-content G fails any inherited comparator, random, year, state, deletion, or q_safe gate at any prespecified capacity.
4. capacity_retention_adverse: maximum-content gates pass but q_cap is undefined or q_cap=1; at some nontrivial coverage, at least one capacity loses a maximum-content documentary-M2 patient.
5. capacity-robust_supportive: every primary gate passes, q_cap<1, simultaneous uncertainty supports the prespecified >5 capture margins, and the semantic-null control is passed at all three capacities.
6. inconclusive: simultaneous limits cross the boundary, a capacity is unresolved, or null/inference requirements are incomplete.

A supportive result means the stored-content G rule is robust across these three hypothetical queue sizes in this finite historical corpus. It may justify acquiring report finalization/view logs and designing a silent-mode operational replay. It does not justify deploying the queue, selecting a clinically acceptable quota, claiming fairness or safety, inferring biological MVI, or changing surgery, surveillance, systemic/local therapy, transplantation, or any treatment.

An adverse result at one capacity cannot be rescued by another capacity. If 5% or 20% fails while 10% passes, report the capacity-specific adverse witness and do not claim general capacity robustness. If q_cap is below 1 but q_safe or inherited comparator gates fail, classify as mixed/non-supportive. If point estimates pass but simultaneous lower limits do not, classify as inconclusive. If the all-report semantic-null control fails, report association/sensitivity only and do not attribute the result to semantic content.

## Additional evidence requirements and verifier limits

A future source-system audit must establish which complete report version was finalized/released and visible by t_dec, with report and patient identifiers, timestamps, version hashes, and an operational replay of the same fixed-capacity queue. It must report overall and CT/MRI-specific coverage with a stated confidence procedure. Stored eventual text, an accession number, an order time, or a nonempty report is not evidence of visibility.

Clinical adjudication is required for the procedure dictionary, Chinese radiology/pathology semantics, specimen linkage and pathology sampling adequacy. Expert/stakeholder review is required for whether 5%, 10%, or 20% capacity is plausible, whether the 5-per-100 margin is meaningful, workload costs, harms, acceptability, and any action threshold. Prospective workflow and outcome data are required for clinician action, treatment effects, recurrence, survival, patient utility, and benefit.

The automatic verifier can check source hashes/headers, complete table/path bindings, raw-ordinal preservation, joins and multiplicity, clock intervals, temporal roles, forbidden-feature absence, report-state coupling, fixed-score equality, K arithmetic, queue/hash/deletion replay, q_req/q_M2/q_safe/q_cap definitions and nesting, subgroup/event requirements, bootstrap/null lineage, and conclusion-to-output linkage. It cannot establish clinical semantics, reader qualifications, actual visibility, specimen adequacy, fairness, capacity acceptability, patient safety, treatment effects, recurrence, survival, or benefit. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility only.
