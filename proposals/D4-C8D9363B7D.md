# Deployment-stability and kidney-information benchmark for the fixed-budget raw-grip cystatin-C policy

## Decision and substantive repair

The selected parent [prior hypothesis] already tests whether a one-column raw-grip policy G beats fitted B/F policies and the transparent lowest-eGFRcr threshold-nearest policy T, and restricts downstream support to incident inpatient N18 rather than an N17/N18 composite. Its remaining decision-validity weakness is transportability: its two held-out partitions are random participant-hash partitions from the same UKB recruitment frame. A policy can pass those splits while its yield changes by assessment center, enrollment era, or availability of the kidney-specific urine marker clinicians commonly use.

This child retains the parent's locked population O, time zero, 2021 equations, development/test/replication hash locks, exact 20% assay budgets, one-column raw-grip representation, development-only preprocessing, placebo tests, joint missing-label bounds, N18 endpoint, multiplicity, calibration, sex-stability gates, adverse/inconclusive branches, and noncausal limits. It adds:

1. Frozen-score deployment stability by exact assessment center and enrollment era, with an estimand and eligibility rule that separates heterogeneity from low subgroup power.
2. A nested ACR-complete kidney-information benchmark. This tests whether grip adds value beyond observed baseline urine albumin/creatinine information, but cannot become a population-wide claim because the source audit finds the two urine fields jointly observed in only 152,858/502,370 (30.43%) biological-sample rows before O filtering.

The center/era repair is the primary new repair. The ACR analysis is a conditional secondary estimand, not a reason to redefine O or claim routine ACR availability for everyone.

## Evidence boundary and hypothesis

The inherited evidence supports association and prediction of creatinine-cystatin eGFR differences in UKB, not fixed-budget assay-policy superiority, measured-GFR validity, or patient benefit. UKB fields can test biochemical reclassification and association with captured inpatient diagnosis codes. They cannot adjudicate persistent CKD, establish true GFR, prove a muscle mechanism, establish equitable access, measure costs or harms, or show that testing changes management.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> With all scores frozen in development, G has a positive and clinically material biochemical yield advantage over T and remains stable across eligible UKB assessment-center and enrollment-era strata in both locked partitions. In participants with valid baseline urine albumin and urine creatinine, adding grip to the inherited kidney-relevant baseline model provides additional H yield beyond the otherwise identical ACR model. If urine availability is too incomplete or N18 strata are sparse, that ACR or downstream stability claim is inconclusive rather than favorable.

The primary biochemical estimand remains Delta_H(G:T) per 1,000 cystatin assays, with the parent's Delta_H(G:B), Delta_H(G:F), ratio, D, placebo, calibration, missingness and sex gates unchanged. The new scientific claim is only that a grip allocation rule does or does not retain incremental biochemical value under observed center/era variation and, conditionally, beyond observed ACR.

## Frozen policies and conditional ACR benchmark

Retain the parent definitions exactly. Scr is 30700-0.0 divided by 88.4; use its sex-specific 2021 eGFRcr, combined eGFRcr-cys H, eGFRcys discordance D, B, G, W/Q/F, placebos and T. Development is h<60, test is 60<=h<80, and replication is h>=80 for SHA256(utf8("ukb-grip-cys-v3|"+canonical_decimal_eid)) mod 100. Each primary policy ranks all O in each partition and takes floor(0.20 times the partition size), with the parent's exact tie rule. No locked labels, center, era, ACR, N18 or future field can change primary scores or thresholds.

The urine fields are 30500-0.0, microalbumin/albumin in urine in mg/L, and 30510-0.0, enzymatic urine creatinine in micromol/L. Result flags 30505-0.0 and 30515-0.0 are retained for integrity reporting. Define U=1 when albumin is finite and nonnegative and creatinine is finite and positive. Compute uACR = 1000 times 30500 divided by 30510 in mg/mmol and log_uACR = log1p(uACR).

Because U is only about 30% in the full biological-sample table, do not replace parent G/B/F/T or make an all-O ACR claim. In the nested O_U equal to O intersect U:

- Fit B_U using parent B covariates plus one development-O_U winsorized and standardized log_uACR.
- Fit G_U as B_U plus exactly one development-O_U winsorized and standardized raw maximum-grip column. No grip interaction, spline, residualization, handedness or missing indicator.
- Fit no model or transform in test or replication.
- Define T_U as lowest frozen eGFRcr within O_U with the same hash tie rule.
- Select exactly floor(0.20 times the size of each O_U partition) for each nested policy.

This nested budget is a secondary conditional estimand and does not alter the parent's global-O budget or conclusions. The ACR incremental contrast is G_U versus B_U, with G_U versus T_U diagnostic. Claim it only if each lock has at least 5,000 O_U participants, at least 80% valid H labels, at least 100 selected assays per policy, and at least 100 observed H events across G_U union B_U union T_U. These are fixed feasibility gates, not tuning criteria. Otherwise report the calculation and call the ACR question inconclusive. ACR missingness cannot be interpreted as absent albuminuria and cannot be imputed from future values.

## Stability estimands

For every frozen primary policy S in B,G,F,T, lock p in test,replication, and stratum s, report:

- global selected size and Y_H(S) = 1000 times selected H count divided by selected count;
- within-stratum selected assay count and Y_H(S given s) using selected assays in s as denominator;
- selection fraction, selected assays divided by all O participants in s;
- H, D, H times K18, K17 and Kany diagnostics under inherited missing-label rules.

The exact budget is global within each lock. A stratum's per-assay denominator is the number of that policy's selected assays in that stratum, not the stratum population size. This keeps coverage differences separate from assay efficacy. Zero selected assays makes that stratum undefined and inconclusive.

The primary center stability set is exact 54-0.0 values with at least 5,000 O participants and at least 100 selected assays for both G and T in the relevant lock. The primary era stability set is E1–E4 strata with the same thresholds. Apply eligibility using O and policy membership only, never outcomes. Smaller strata remain descriptive; non-significance in a small stratum is not evidence of equality.

Within every eligible stratum, compute paired participant-level G:T contrasts for H and H times K18, 2,000 within-stratum paired bootstrap resamples, and the inherited common unknown-assignment joint-bound procedure. Compute pooled lock-stratified contrasts and interaction contrasts theta_s minus theta_pool. Use simultaneous 95% max-|t| intervals over the prespecified center and era interaction family plus inherited comparisons. Never refit, recalibrate or select a favorable subgroup.

Stable support requires the parent's pooled biochemical G:T gates in both locks and a simultaneous 95% upper bound below 2 H reclassifications per 1,000 for the largest eligible-stratum departure from the pooled contrast, with ratio lower bound at least 0.90. Make the analogous N18 statement only if parent N18 sufficiency and subgroup event requirements pass.

Demonstrated instability requires an eligible center or era with a simultaneous 95% upper bound below -2 per 1,000 for G:T H yield, or a ratio upper bound below 0.90, while data integrity passes. A negative point estimate with a wide interval is inconclusive, not adverse. Stability is inconclusive when eligibility fails, intervals cross margins, locks disagree, missing-label bounds cross margins, or chronology/integrity fails. An overall biochemical result can remain supported but limited to the pooled observed UKB frame.

For subgroup N18 stability require at least 30 observed H times K18 events in the compared selected-set union. Otherwise do not turn wide intervals into a claim of stability or inefficacy; report biochemical stability and mark downstream transportability inconclusive.

## Change and uncertainty

Compared with [prior hypothesis], this child adds an exact-center and consent-era transportability estimand, separates selection coverage from per-assay efficacy, uses simultaneous interaction uncertainty with adverse-versus-inconclusive rules, and adds a conditional ACR-complete comparator with exact fields and availability safeguards. The population, time zero, primary budgets, policies, N18 endpoint and causal boundary remain locked. The key uncertainty is whether enough eligible center/era selected counts and N18 events exist; the feasibility audit is preserved at analysis/episode6_stability_audit.json. Even if ACR is too incomplete for a population conclusion, a valid center/era result can show whether raw grip is stable beyond eGFRcr-based allocation; an adverse or inconclusive result prevents overclaiming deployment readiness.
