# A fixed-budget correlated-error and packet-feasibility stress test for conservative pulmonary discharge routing

## Executive decision question and targeted repair

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` (primary) and `[prior hypothesis]` (correlated-error anchor). It preserves their frozen clinical target and does not replace the scientific schema. The decision remains whether a pulmonary recommendation opportunity in a first-eligible adult admission can be assigned to a conservative `AUTO`, clinician `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` route well enough to justify only a prospective, silent, within-site validation of the exact observable signature. It is not a study of whether a recommendation is guideline-concordant, whether a discharge plan is clinically right, whether a route was delivered or viewed, or whether any intervention improves outcomes.

The unresolved threat is correlated adjudication error under a realistic review contract. Separate labels, high agreement, or a third adjudicator can still reproduce the same error if reviewers read the same artifacts, share the same codebook, leak route or stratum information, or are selected only after a favorable route is emitted. A second unresolved threat is feasibility: a protocol that requires several panels and counterfactual controls may exceed the available admission, packet, or reviewer budget, making its apparent safety non-actionable. This child therefore adds a pre-label fixed allocation and an additive, admission-conserving workload/packet ledger, while retaining an independent stress-test panel and adverse-compatible inference.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Under the frozen all-opportunity denominator, route-independent selection, reciprocal chronology, canonical discharge artifact, and conservative worst-unit routing, the proposed route remains admissible in a preallocated, roster-disjoint correlated-error challenge; route-invariant controls do not change it, route-decisive controls change it only as specified, and the complete protocol fits the fixed 900-distinct-admission and preregistered reviewer/packet budgets. Failure of any supported atom's adverse-compatible challenge bound, any leakage or chronology control, or the workload/packet contract blocks that atom or the whole nomination, even if ordinary component agreement is high.

A supportive result advances the evidence from reproducible stored-text routing to a bounded, independently stress-tested documentation process. It does not establish clinical truth or benefit. An adverse result is valuable: it identifies shared-text/codebook error, leakage, route instability, or impractical burden before a live workflow is exposed to it. An inconclusive result is not a favorable result and causes default `DEFER` or no selection.

## What is supported, what is tested, and what remains unavailable

The frozen MIMIC snapshot supports admission and discharge times, subject linkage, service chronology, radiology reports and addenda with chart and storage times, radiology structured-detail reconstruction, discharge summaries with chart and storage times, and discharge-detail reconstruction. The strongest supported claim is that a retrospective source-bound artifact and a finite-population audit can be constructed with an explicitly defined information boundary. Stored text does not establish physical visibility at departure, transmission, recipient, responsibility, action, order/referral, scheduling, outside care, patient preference, follow-up completion, safety, utility, or benefit.

The experiment tests a narrower documentation claim: whether an emitted admission-level route is compatible with independent labels for actionability and explicit discharge-plan supersession/conflict, and whether that compatibility survives a whole-admission challenge designed to reveal shared error. The actionability panel assesses whether required target/modality/action/interval content is explicit and available; the supersession panel assesses explicit target-linked discharge-plan continuation, replacement, cancellation, or conflict. Neither panel assesses appropriateness. The holistic challenge is an additional measurement system, not a gold standard. Without external truth, the protocol reports compatible intervals and adverse bounds rather than a point estimate of shared reviewer error.

Delivery, receipt, viewing, rendering, responsibility, appropriateness, safety, completion, causal benefit, outside-plan capture, and transport require prospective workflow logging, independent clinical adjudication with richer context, another site, or a governed comparative study.

## Pre-label population, packets, and route-concealed views

For each eligible admission, construct all recommendation units from the reciprocal radiology chain and freeze the exact target atom, unit identity, `E`, terminal service, workflow class, and clock before sampling. Construct the one canonical DS from `note/discharge`, then ordered `discharge_detail`; no later note can revise the primary result. A source component is available at cutoff `t` exactly when its nonmissing `storetime<=t`. Retain checkpoints `D-24h`, `D-6h`, `D-1h`, `D`, `D+1h`, and `D+24h`; only `D` enters the primary estimand. Later packets are delayed-arrival stress tests and cannot relabel the `D` state.

Every selected admission receives an immutable source-bound packet with pseudonymous `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, `D`, cutoff, note IDs, source storetimes, ordered detail rows, reciprocal-chain identity, exact atom(s), terminal service, `G`, explicit availability/error states, and cryptographic hash of the unredacted artifact. Review views are generated independently from this manifest. Actionability reviewers see only the reciprocal radiology chain/detail and permitted admission/death/disposition fields; supersession reviewers see the frozen atom and canonical DS/detail view; holistic challenge reviewers see the complete permitted packet. None sees route, sampling stratum, possible-calendar class, terminal-service domain, weights, other labels, challenge allocation, reviewer workload, or any post-`D` evidence. Route derivation is executed only after all labels and seals are append-only finalized.

The actionability and supersession rubrics remain exactly those in the primary parent. `A_ACTIONABLE` requires explicit target/lesion identity, modality/exam, unconditional action, and due interval/date, with no unresolved negation, hypothetical, history-only, optional language, or ambiguity. `A_NONACTIONABLE` is an explicit negative/conditional/historical/underspecified documentation state; `A_UNKNOWN` covers unavailable, contradictory, unreadable, unresolved, or unresolvable disagreement. The supersession states are `S_CONFIRM`, `S_NONE`, `S_SUPERSEDES`, `S_CONFLICT`, and `S_UNKNOWN`, with `S_SUPERSEDES` requiring explicit target linkage and an extractable changed action. Absence of a plan is never evidence of delivery or responsibility.

## Fixed allocation and additive admission/reviewer ledger

### Allocation is frozen before labels

Let `N_A` be the complete eligible-admission count and let `n_h` be the inherited fixed sample count in each of the five outcome-blind sampling strata, including the sentinel census and all formula/cap rules. The inherited sample is not changed. A run is `NO_SELECTION` if the inherited formula yields more than 900 distinct admissions, any required cell lacks positive inclusion probability, or any source/manifest check fails. Otherwise, select admissions exactly once using the inherited phase-1/phase-2 seed and store each admission's first and second-phase inclusion probabilities.

Add a correlated-error challenge from the already selected admissions, never from the unsampled frame. This consumes no additional distinct admissions and cannot trigger a top-up. Let `n=Σ_h n_h`. Before labels, compute a deterministic allocation with fixed challenge budget `C=120`:

* If `n<120`, set `c= n`; otherwise set `c=120`.
* Allocate `c_h` by largest-remainder allocation to the five inherited strata using quotas `c*n_h/n`, with ties broken by fixed stratum order `S_ZERO,S_LOW,S_MID,S_HIGH,S_UNKNOWN`.
* Within each stratum, select exactly `c_h` admissions by the pre-label protocol hash of `(subject_id,hadm_id,challenge_seed)`, ascending; no route, atom, label, time, text content, panel result, or workload enters the key.
* If a challenge atom/cell has fewer than two selected challenge admissions, it is not padded or borrowed. Its holistic gate is `UNSUPPORTED/INCONCLUSIVE`; this is a deliberate no-rescue rule. The global challenge remains computable, and an atom may not be nominated without its own support.

The challenge allocation is conditional on the inherited admission draw. Challenge summaries use the inherited admission weight multiplied by the inverse conditional challenge probability within its stratum. Primary route-admissibility remains estimated from the full selected sample and its inherited weights; challenge findings are a pre-specified secondary gate and are never substituted for the primary denominator. All units and all panel labels remain attached to the admission; there is no unit-level sampling or weighting.

### Ledger rows and exact charge rules

Create one ledger row per distinct selected admission, keyed by immutable pseudonymous admission ID. The row contains: `selected`, `challenge`, `stratum`, `possible_calendar`, `terminal_service`, `workflow_class`, `packet_version_hash`, `packet_bytes`, `packet_characters`, `component_review_slots`, `component_adjudication_slot`, `holistic_review_slots`, `holistic_adjudication_slot`, `control_review_slots`, `control_adjudication_slots`, `planned_minutes`, `actual_minutes`, `physician_minutes`, `nonresponse`, `packet_infeasible`, `seal_status`, and `reason_code`. A second audit table contains one row per reviewer task with admission key, panel, role, roster hash, start/stop timestamps, minutes, response, disagreement, and seal hash. Reviewer IDs are pseudonyms, and roster overlap is tested cryptographically.

The following are fixed charges for capacity planning, while actual minutes are also recorded and used for the observed workload estimate.

* Every selected admission: one packet-build/serialization task, one actionability pair (2 reviewer slots), one actionability disagreement-adjudication slot reserved but charged only if required, one supersession pair (2 slots), one supersession adjudication slot reserved but charged only if required, and one locked route/control-integrity check.
* Every challenge admission: two whole-admission holistic reviews using disjoint rosters and independently written codebooks, one H3 challenge review when H1/H2 disagree or when a locked shared-error fixture is present, plus the fixed control set below. H3 is not invoked by a favorable route and cannot be omitted because H1/H2 agree on a potentially wrong label.
* Every challenge admission with feasible spans: one route-irrelevant pair, one route-decisive pair, one target-link omission/addition pair, and one nuisance-format pair. Controls are generated by a locked transformation manifest before labels. Pair order is randomized independently in each panel. Control transformations are charged as packet-generation/serialization tasks and review tasks; if a transformation is not mechanically feasible from pre-label span rules, it is marked `CONTROL_INFEASIBLE`, not hand-edited and not silently dropped.

A task count is an integer, not an estimate. The planned person-minutes for admission `i` are

`L_i = 12 + 2*10 + 2*10 + 15*I(actionability disagreement reservation used) + 2*12*I(challenge_i) + 12*I(H3_i) + 4*8*I(route-irrelevant feasible) + 4*8*I(route-decisive feasible) + 4*8*I(target-link feasible) + 4*8*I(nuisance feasible)`.

The constants are capacity-planning slots, not clinical thresholds: 12 minutes for packet construction/QA; 10 minutes for each independent component review; 15 minutes for a required actionability adjudication; 12 minutes per holistic review; 12 minutes for H3; and 8 minutes per control review. Supersession adjudication, when required, is charged an additional fixed 15 minutes; the explicit ledger formula must include it as `+15*I(supersession disagreement)`. The implementation must calculate the complete expression from pre-label structural feasibility and from sealed disagreement flags, report both planned upper bound and observed minutes, and never replace a missing time by zero. To make the accounting additive and auditable, every term is separately materialized as a ledger column; no bundled “panel burden” is permitted.

The fixed global contract is: at most 900 distinct admissions; at most 120 challenge admissions; at most 1,400 physician-equivalent person-hours across component, holistic, control, adjudication, packet-QA, and reserve tasks; at most 7,200 total review/task slots; and at most 50,000 UTF-8 characters in any single reviewer packet view, excluding cryptographic hashes and machine-readable manifest metadata. These are operational nomination limits, not safety claims. The 1,400-hour and 7,200-slot ceilings are checked using the planned upper-bound ledger before labels and again using actual sealed task rows after review. If the planned upper bound exceeds a ceiling, the run is `NO_SELECTION` rather than adaptively shrinking, reallocating, or removing a panel. If a source packet exceeds 50,000 characters or cannot be losslessly serialized, it is `PACKET_INFEASIBLE`; it is retained as an adverse/unknown admission state, never truncated, summarized by a reviewer, or complete-case deleted.

A fixed 30-admission reserve is charged in the ledger before selection as one packet-QA slot per reserve admission and is not automatically activated. It is released only if a source/serialization defect is discovered before labels and only under a predeclared, label-independent replacement rule; otherwise its charged capacity remains unused. Reserve admissions never repair a sparse atom, challenge cell, nonresponse, or adverse result. The ledger reports (i) distinct admissions, (ii) admission-packet count, (iii) independent reviewer assignments, (iv) adjudications, (v) control packets, (vi) physician-equivalent hours, (vii) bytes/characters, (viii) failed/infeasible packets, and (ix) unused budget. Thus duplicated readings and controls increase reviewer workload but never inflate the clinical denominator.

The protocol includes a pre-label dry run using no panel labels: serialize a fixed hash-selected 30-admission feasibility subset, measure packet bytes/characters, parse all time fields, and execute all transformations in fixture mode. This dry run cannot tune parser thresholds or allocations. If its deterministic extrapolated upper bound violates the packet or workload ceiling, the run stops before any clinical review. Its measurements are source-bound engineering feasibility evidence, not a clinical result.

## Correlated-error challenge and leakage isolation

The challenge uses three roster-disjoint lanes. H1 and H2 receive the same frozen whole-admission packet but independently randomized order, independent pseudonyms, separate staff, and independently written/hashed codebooks. Codebooks must encode target, action, interval, polarity, recipient/responsibility, replacement, conflict, unit inventory, and departure-artifact status, but may not share examples, training cases, or label-derived revisions. H3 receives only after H1/H2 seals, sees no route or component labels, and marks each route-defining assertion `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, or `INDETERMINATE`. H3 is an additional measurement system and does not resolve truth by majority.

Each panel records source spans/field ordinals, atom components, availability, uncertainty reason, and its label. No panel emits a route. A locked Cartesian route function combines sealed actionability/supersession labels with the inherited route; route voting, consensus deletion, and favorable unknown assignment are prohibited. For every admission, compatible route sets retain all adverse-consistent interpretations. An unresolved or shared-wrong possibility cannot support `AUTO`; whole-admission worst-unit precedence maps it to `RECON` or `DEFER` according to the inherited rule.

Before labels, generate the following control families when mechanical span conditions permit:

1. **Route-invariant pair**: preserve target/action/timing/polarity and all route-defining spans while permuting unrelated section order or replacing a non-target sentence with provenance-matched text. Route and unit inventory must be unchanged.
2. **Route-decisive pair**: change exactly one locked atomic field, such as explicit replacement versus no replacement or sufficient versus insufficient `H`; route may change only according to the locked route table.
3. **Target-link pair**: mask or replace the discharge-plan target with an unrelated target. `S_SUPERSEDES` must become `S_NONE` or `S_UNKNOWN`, never remain linked.
4. **Chronology pair**: shift DS `storetime` after `D` without changing text. Primary `S_CONFIRM`/`S_SUPERSEDES` becomes `S_UNKNOWN`; a later packet cannot repair the `D` result.
5. **Contradiction pair**: inject a precise changed interval or incompatible modality in a synthetic fixture. It must yield `S_CONFLICT` or `S_SUPERSEDES`, exclude `AUTO`, and preserve all other fields.
6. **Shared-wrong fixture**: plant a deliberately wrong but internally coherent atomic plan in all component and holistic views. Agreement may be high, but H3/compatible-set analysis must prevent certification; no agreement statistic may override this fixture.
7. **Minority-unit fixture**: make one unit unknown or conflicting in an otherwise clean admission. Whole-admission `AUTO` must be excluded.
8. **Leakage/serialization controls**: route permutation, domain/stratum permutation, cutoff permutation, field-order permutation, note-ID pseudonym permutation, packet hash mismatch, append-order violation, and roster-overlap canaries. Any route signal that survives route-label permutation, or any panel change induced by a route-irrelevant field, is a failure.

The verifier checks that all transformations and fixtures were frozen before clinical labels, that the expected answer was not disclosed to reviewers, that source text is unchanged, and that no public service receives clinical text.

## Primary estimand, challenge estimands, and adverse-compatible shared-error bounds

For admission `i`, let `r_i` be the inherited emitted route and `K_i` the independent conservative admissible-route set from actionability and supersession states. The primary estimand is unchanged:

`Theta_R = Σ_i w_i I(r_i ∈ K_i) / Σ_i w_i`,

with route-specific admissibility, excess `AUTO`, missed reconciliation, and unresolved-route mass. `TIMING` is reported separately and collapsed into the `DEFER` family only for the primary route-family summary. Unknown and disagreement states are adverse-compatible; an unresolvable unit cannot support `AUTO`.

For challenge admissions, define `Q_i=1` if every available H1/H2/H3 interpretation is route-compatible, and `Q_i=0` if any compatible interpretation is adverse to the emitted route. Report design-weighted `P(Q=1)` and its exact finite-population interval, conditional on the fixed challenge allocation. Also report:

* H1--H2 agreement and H3 support intervals;
* parent-component versus holistic route-discordance intervals;
* upper adverse-compatible `AUTO` contamination and lower capture of `S_SUPERSEDES`, `S_CONFLICT`, and `A_UNKNOWN` by `RECON/DEFER`;
* route-invariant control-flip and route-decisive-control-violation rates;
* packet infeasibility, nonresponse, disagreement, adjudication, and reviewer-hours by stratum, temporal domain, service domain, and candidate atom;
* lower/upper bounds under the compatible-set analysis, with no latent-class point estimate unless an external identifiability constraint is independently documented before labels.

A three-rater latent-class model is diagnostic only. It cannot narrow the primary adverse bounds because conditional independence and clinical correctness are not identified from these panels. The primary uncertainty uses design-weighted HT/Hájek totals, phase-1/phase-2 variance, nested Rao--Wu resampling retaining all admission-level labels together, and exact hypergeometric inversion for rare adverse events. One simultaneous max-statistic 95% family covers pooled, temporal, service, route, panel, challenge, control, and ledger gates. A missing label or broken packet is adverse-compatible, not complete-case deletion.

## Baselines and threshold governance

The inherited selector is the primary comparator under the identical pre-label sample, packets, weights, and labels. Secondary diagnostics, fixed before labels, are: (a) a naive selector that maps a precise actionable atom with an available canonical DS to `AUTO` and late/missing DS to `DEFER`; (b) the parent-only component audit without the holistic stress gate; (c) favorable-consensus and marginal-majority summaries; and (d) a single-codebook duplicate diagnostic. These diagnostics cannot nominate a route or tune any threshold. No model is fitted for the primary decision.

Governance must approve the following operational margins before packet generation and record them in the manifest. They are nomination/workflow thresholds, not clinical safety thresholds:

* simultaneous lower 95% bound for `Theta_R >=0.90` overall and in every supported temporal/service domain;
* simultaneous upper bound for excess `AUTO <=0.05` overall and in every supported domain;
* simultaneous upper bound for adverse-compatible `AUTO` challenge contamination `<=0.05`, `RECON`/`DEFER` capture of superseding/conflicting/unknown cases `>=0.95`, and parent-versus-holistic discordance `<=0.05` for `AUTO`, `<=0.10` for `RECON`, `<=0.05` for `TIMING`, and `<=0.10` for `DEFER`;
* route-invariant control-flip upper bound `<=0.02` and decisive-control violation upper bound `<=0.05`;
* actionability and supersession unknown/disagreement mass `<=0.05` in each claimable domain; otherwise that route/domain is inconclusive;
* packet infeasibility and panel nonresponse upper bound `<=0.05`; control-infeasibility is separately adverse-compatible and blocks the affected atom if its bound is not below `0.05`;
* physician-equivalent hours `<=1,400`, total task slots `<=7,200`, distinct admissions `<=900`, challenge admissions `<=120`, and packet view length `<=50,000` characters;
* supported-domain lower `Theta_R` no more than `0.10` below pooled, absolute excess-`AUTO` drift no more than `0.02`, and workload ratio no more than the inherited `1.50` margin, all simultaneously.

These values are frozen governance nominations based on the source-bound feasibility contract and parent protocol, not selected after labels. If local governance refuses any margin or budget before labels, the corresponding nomination is `NOT_APPROVED`, not converted to a favorable result. If challenge coverage or a domain's event/ESS/positive-probability requirements is inadequate, the route/domain is `INCONCLUSIVE` and defaults to `DEFER`; it cannot be pooled away. A census does not eliminate uncertainty about clinical correctness and does not permit a broken packet or unavailable panel to be favorable.

## Falsification and verification criteria

The run is computationally invalid if any of the following occurs: source/schema hash mismatch; changed read-only source; duplicate or nonunique join; wrong adult or first-eligible rule; wrong reciprocal chronology; charttime substituted for storetime; DS detail treated as independently timed; post-`D` evidence enters the primary packet; route/stratum/domain/weight leakage; route derived before seals; roster overlap; reused codebook/examples; non-additive or double-counted ledger; sample or challenge top-up; untracked reserve activation; unit-level weighting; packet truncation; missing reviewer task; favorable conversion of unknown; or altered source text.

The scientific route rule is falsified, or its claimable domain is blocked, by any of: an adverse-compatible challenge interpretation making an `AUTO` admission require `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`; failure of a route-invariant or decisive control; a minority-unit failure; excess `AUTO` above its upper bound; insufficient capture of explicit supersession/conflict/unknown cases; domain-specific failure hidden by a pooled pass; or workload/packet ceilings exceeded. A high H1--H2 agreement statistic cannot rescue a shared-wrong fixture. Failure is reported at the smallest predeclared atom/domain when all global controls pass; a global seal or denominator failure invalidates the run rather than being localized.

The verifier recomputes source hashes, manifests, eligibility, domains, target freeze, reciprocal chains, packets at every cutoff, route outputs, panel seals, codebook hashes, challenge allocation, inclusion probabilities, every ledger term, task counts, packet sizes, HT/Hájek totals, exact/replicate bounds, and all simultaneous gates. It independently tests correct computation with unsupported conclusions: even if every computation passes, it must reject any claim of safety, appropriateness, delivery, benefit, causality, responsibility, or transport. It also tests supportive, adverse, and inconclusive fixtures, including shared-wrong agreement, missing labels, late DS, target swap, contradiction, route permutation, formatting perturbation, and unsupported domain.

## Interpretation and clinical advance

**Supportive:** The frozen selector has high independently adjudicated route admissibility, low adverse-compatible excess `AUTO`, high conservative capture of superseding/conflicting/unknown cases, stable route-invariant/decisive controls, bounded disagreement and missingness, and a complete additive ledger within all budgets in every claimed domain. This supports only a prospective silent validation of the exact site-local signatures, with all unsupported signatures forced to `DEFER`.

**Adverse:** Shared-wrong or holistic challenge bounds cross a gate, a route-invariant perturbation changes route, a decisive perturbation is mishandled, a chronology/leakage control fails, a minority unit is hidden, a domain fails, or the packet/reviewer contract exceeds budget. The affected atom/domain is not nominated; a global failure stops all nomination. This falsifies the proposed documentation-routing process, not clinical benefit or clinician quality.

**Inconclusive:** Sparse challenge support, census-only cells with wide exact bounds, unavailable or late canonical DS, unresolved panel disagreement, nonresponse, control infeasibility, packet corruption, or unapproved governance/budget leaves the corresponding result unresolved. Unknown is never recoded as `S_NONE`, `A_ACTIONABLE`, or successful delivery.

The substantive clinical advance is an executable answer to a consequential pre-live question: can conservative routing survive correlated, shared-text adjudication error and fit a finite review contract without silently converting reviewer agreement into clinical truth? The additive ledger makes every duplicate review, adjudication, control, packet, reserve, and distinct admission count visible and prevents workload from being hidden by unit-level summaries. The challenge makes agreement falsifiable rather than self-validating, while the source-bound chronology and route-concealed views preserve the inherited target.

MIMIC cannot establish whether a recommendation was actually received, seen, communicated, owned, ordered, scheduled, completed, appropriate, safe, beneficial, or causally effective. A passing retrospective result therefore requires a governed prospective silent bridge that logs finalization, transmission, recipient, receipt/view/rendering, responsibility, reconciliation, orders/referrals/scheduling, patient preference, outside-plan capture, and follow-up, with independent clinical adjudication. Live automation or causal claims require that further study.
