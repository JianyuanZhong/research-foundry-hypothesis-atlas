# A sealed split-view probability audit of pulmonary-nodule admission routing

## Decision bottleneck and targeted advance

The unresolved prerequisite remains exactly the parent's: over the frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC population, can the admission-level labels `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` be reproduced with sufficiently low unsafe cross-route contamination to nominate a prospective *silent* validation? The clinical danger is not merely a missed recommendation. An admission that appears copyable can contain a second, rare unit requiring reconciliation, have insufficient pre-departure time, or contain unresolved context; routing such an admission to `AUTO` could hide the unit on which safe handling depends.

The parent solved outcome-enriched case review and unit multiplicity with exhaustive frame construction, a safety-sentinel census, positive-probability admission sampling, all-unit review within selected admissions, and finite-population inference. Its remaining validity threat is **incorporation/circularity**: reviewers who help establish recommendation units, plan attributes, context, or a candidate route can influence the evidence later used to verify that same route. Route-triggered verification of sampled `AUTO` calls can also make verification depend on the index result. Agreement can therefore be inflated even when every sampled admission has a correct design weight.

This targeted child preserves the inherited population, chronology, states, estimands, margins, MIMIC bindings, admission sampling design, worst-unit routing, and noncausal scope. It changes only how component evidence and verification labels are produced:

1. eligibility is sealed before any unredacted pulmonary discharge-plan view or route sample is available;
2. two independent **index lanes** label recommendation, context, and departure-plan components from separated evidence views and never vote on routes;
3. locked code, not reviewers, links components and derives routes across all compatible label combinations;
4. every probability-sampled admission, not only index-positive admissions, receives an independent, untouched **audit-lane** review plus a de-novo minority-unit safety check;
5. all labels, packet hashes, code hashes, and role assignments are sealed before any route is computed or disclosed; and
6. design-based estimators and adverse partial-identification bounds retain nonresponse, boundary disagreement, redaction uncertainty, and unobserved rare cases rather than deleting or imputing them optimistically.

This is a test of leakage-resistant, text-defined route purity and workload. It is not a diagnostic-accuracy study with a clinical gold standard. The methodological rationale is consistent with the concerns about patient spectrum, verification, review, incorporation, withdrawals, and indeterminate results formalized in QUADAS (Whiting et al., 2003, DOI `10.1186/1471-2288-3-25`; full Europe PMC text inspected, source `PMC305345`). The expert-selected natural-history demonstration was also inspected for its explicit separation of training and external validation and its warning that learned data biases remain; it motivates rigor but is not evidence for this routing hypothesis (Shmatko et al., 2025, DOI `10.1038/s41586-025-09529-3`).

## Stage 1: inherited positive-probability admission sample, frozen without route labels

After `N_A`, canonical keys, reciprocal chains, clocks, and deterministic minimum/maximum span inventories are frozen—but before any human route component or audit label—assign each admission hierarchically to the inherited outcome-blind sampling strata:

1. `S_SENTINEL`, probability 1: reciprocal-chain defect; missing/contradictory time; missing/unreadable canonical artifact; parser failure; `U_BOUNDARY` signal; at least two possible units; different actions or due windows; negation/conditionality conflict; target-linked decision/non-pursuit/definitive-treatment language; or discordant pulmonary-plan spans in `DS`.
2. `S_TIMECLOCK`: no sentinel, but any deterministic unit has `G_u<24h`, unknown `G_u`, `DS_store>D`, or stale chronology.
3. `S_ZERO`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and no deterministic target-linked pulmonary plan span in `DS`.
4. `S_PARTIAL`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and target text is found but action, timing, responsibility, polarity, or linkage is absent/discordant.
5. `S_COMPLETE`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and deterministic target/action/timing/responsibility or replacement-plan elements are present.

These are sampling strata, never candidate routes. They are hidden from every reviewer. Because deterministic screens can be wrong, every non-sentinel admission retains positive inclusion probability.

Within each noncensus stratum use simple random sampling without replacement with a seed derived from the sealed protocol hash. For `S_ZERO`, take the larger of 300 or the finite-population sample size for worst-case proportion 0.5 and ±4 percentage-point 95% precision, capped at `N_h`. For each other noncensus stratum, take the larger of 120 or the corresponding ±7-point sample, capped at `N_h`:

`n_h = ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25 / {e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`.

Add the full sentinel census. The inherited 900-admission workload ceiling is fixed before labels. If the mandatory census plus formula samples exceed 900, obtain resources or declare feasibility failed. Never reduce `n_h`, omit sentinels, replace admissions, or select by apparent route, disagreement, expected safety, reviewer availability, response, or packet difficulty.

For admission `i` in stratum `h`, `pi_i=1` for the sentinel census and `pi_i=n_h/N_h>0` otherwise; `w_i=1/pi_i`. Within a sampled stratum, `pi_ij=n_h(n_h-1)/[N_h(N_h-1)]`; across strata `pi_ij=pi_i*pi_j`; certainty-stratum contributions have zero sampling variance. Freeze `N_h,n_h,pi_i,pi_ij`, seed, ordered sampled IDs, and an explicit no-replacement list.

## Stage 2: access-controlled split views and independently sealed labels

### Common controls

All packet content must be available by `D`; radiology and discharge content used for departure decisions must also meet the inherited `storetime` rules. Automated and manual scans reject post-`D` text, later encounters/outcomes, POE, eventual `Z`, proposed routes, sampling strata, inclusion weights, `G/H/V` category names, expected direction, and labels from another role.

Before labels, freeze and hash: packet-generation code; redaction rules; component dictionaries; JSON schemas; source character-offset convention; cross-view unit-matching code; route code; synthetic fixtures and expected outputs; reviewer-role roster; pseudonymization keys held by a data custodian; and the analysis plan. Reviewers use role-specific admission pseudonyms so they cannot link records across views. File permissions prevent cross-role access. Record packet hash, view type, reviewer ID, open/submit timestamps, schema validation, and seal hash. Messaging about cases across roles is prohibited and audited.

Each initial component is labeled twice independently. Reviewer 2 cannot see reviewer 1. Before real packets, the protocol randomly and immutably pairs `R-I1/K-I1/S-I1` as index lane 1 and `R-I2/K-I2/S-I2` as index lane 2; it analogously pairs `R-A1/K-A1/S-A1` and `R-A2/K-A2/S-A2` as audit lanes 1 and 2. These pairings define the two “initial calls” in the inherited estimands. All off-diagonal pairings and the full cross-product are retained as adverse compatibility analyses, so a favorable arbitrary pairing cannot determine a gate. A reviewer may occupy only one of recommendation, context, departure-plan, or audit-safety roles and only one lane. No person who performed eligibility, redaction, sampling, packet construction, or code development can label an index or reference component. Labels are append-only. A third opinion, if collected, is collected for **all** sampled admissions in a prespecified component, not triggered by a route or disagreement; it adds a compatible label and cannot replace initial labels. Primary inference uses the two prespecified lane calls and the full compatible set.

### Index recommendation view `R-I`

Two independent radiology physicians see only the reciprocal operative radiology chain. They do not see `DS`, admission context, clocks, sampling strata, or any prior abstraction. Absolute times are replaced by ordered component tokens; the trusted chronology service later supplies clocks to code. Each physician independently enumerates every potential unit and records source note/character offsets plus atomic target cluster, action/modality, exact due interval/date, polarity, operative component, and supersession. They also mark unresolved coreference/splitting. The union of minimum/maximum compatible inventories is retained; no consensus may delete a minority unit.

### Index context view `K-I`

Two independent discharge physicians see permitted admission/disposition variables and canonical departure-available `DS` after a separate redaction operator masks every pulmonary target/action/timing/recipient/order/preference span. They see no radiology text, clocks, plan labels, strata, or route. They label only `K_OPEN`, `K_CLOSED`, or `K_UNKNOWN`, quote non-target evidence or identify the missing fact, and seal independently. `K_OPEN` means no observable departure-frozen stop; it does not mean follow-up is clinically appropriate.

Every selected admission receives an independent redaction audit before route release, not merely `K_CLOSED` or a route-dependent subset. The auditor sees the source and redacted pair but no labels and records target-plan leakage and removal of decision-relevant non-target context. Any observed leakage/removal above 2%, a simultaneous upper bound above 2%, or any single leak plausibly revealing the expected route blocks all context-dependent route conclusions. A breached packet is retained adversely; it is not silently repaired.

### Index departure-plan view `S-I`

Two independent physicians see the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain and the unredacted canonical `DS`, with source times replaced by order tokens. They do **not** see `R-I` abstractions, context evidence/labels, trusted clocks, strata, deterministic screen outputs, other reviewers, or routes. They independently enumerate recommendation units de novo and, for each, submit quoted radiology and discharge offsets and atomic fields: target linkage/mention; compatible chest/thoracic action; exact timing/date; responsible recipient/team or scheduling; active versus historical/conditional polarity; explicit informed non-pursuit; explicit definitive replacement; and cross-plan conflict.

Locked matching code links `R-I` and `S-I` units only by compatible normalized target/action/timing/polarity tuples and, where both views independently quote the same raw source, admission-local source offsets. It never exposes an `R-I` inventory or selected span to an `S-I` reviewer. Exact compatible matches preserve both labels. One-to-many, many-to-one, missing, or conflicting matches create `U_BOUNDARY` and all compatible unit inventories; humans cannot choose the favorable match after seeing a route. Frozen code maps plan atoms to the inherited `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, `S_EQ`, `S_CON`, `S_PART`, `S_0`, or `S_UN` hierarchy. Vague “PCP follow-up,” generic specialty mention without action/timing, and unlinked orders do not resolve a unit.

### Independently sealed reference/audit views `R-A`, `K-A`, `S-A`

Every sampled admission, regardless of apparent index route, response difficulty, or sentinel status, is independently reviewed by a second roster that has never seen index labels, index component cards, deterministic strata, weights, or route outputs. It repeats the three views above with fresh pseudonyms and fresh packet renderings. `R-A`, `K-A`, and `S-A` reviewers submit the same atomic schema de novo. Audit labels are not adjudications of index labels; they are an independent measurement replicate.

In addition, one senior **minority-unit safety auditor** reviews the complete admissible pre-`D` radiology chain, canonical `DS`, and permitted context for every sampled admission. The auditor sees no index/audit labels, clocks as categories, routes, strata, or expected direction. The only task is to enumerate any additional recommendation unit, unresolved unit boundary, incomplete/conflicting unit, timing ambiguity, context uncertainty, or cross-unit interaction, with quoted offsets. The auditor never votes on a route. This all-sample check replaces route-triggered `AUTO` verification and prevents differential verification: an apparently `COMPLETE`, `ZERO`, or non-`AUTO` admission receives exactly the same safety check as an apparent `AUTO` admission.

Index and audit labels are released to the trusted analysis service only after all due labels or explicit nonresponse records are sealed. The service verifies packet/code hashes, joins role-specific pseudonyms through the custodian, and runs route code. Reviewers never see the resulting routes during collection.

## Locked route derivation and adverse compatibility

For each index lane and each audit lane, code forms all cross-products of independently sealed recommendation, context, departure-plan, redaction, trusted-clock, and unit-matching states. It does not select a favorable pairing. Unit status is inherited:

* `U_RESOLVED`: `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, or `S_EQ` with all required explicit evidence;
* `U_OPEN`: `K_OPEN`, `S in {S_0,S_PART,S_CON}`, and resolved `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING`: otherwise active/unresolved but `G_u<24h` or clock unresolved;
* `U_CLOSED`: `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER`: `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, boundary ambiguity, unreadable content, redaction breach, missing component, or compatible disagreement crossing unit statuses.

Aggregate whole admissions in the unchanged precedence:

1. any `U_DEFER` gives `A_DEFER`;
2. otherwise any `U_TIMING` gives `A_TIMING`;
3. if all units are resolved/closed, give `A_NOACTION`;
4. `A_AUTO` only if every open unit is wholly omitted (`S_0`), all other units are reproducibly resolved/closed, every unit clock is ample, and there is no boundary uncertainty, redaction breach, or cross-unit conflict;
5. any incomplete, conflicting, interaction-dependent, or mixed admission gives `A_RECON`; call it `A_MIXED` when at least one pure omission coexists with a reconciliation unit.

One unsafe minority unit controls the admission. Duplicating safe units cannot change its route or its admission sampling weight. Each prespecified index and audit lane first yields a single locked route call. Separately, for source `q in {I,A}`, the robust route set `Q_i^q` is the union over the two lane calls, all off-diagonal cross-view label combinations, minimum/maximum unit inventories, any prespecified third labels, safety-auditor findings, and all documented unknown assignments. A robust singleton exists only if every compatible assignment yields one route. Otherwise the decision-facing route is `A_DEFER`, while the full set is retained.

## Exact inherited finite-population estimands

The inference population and denominator remain all frozen `N_A`. The sampling and analysis unit is the admission, also the first and only admission per subject. Units are nested observations and never receive weights.

### Route burdens

For `r in {AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,MIXED}`, define the inherited finite-population total `T_r=sum_{i in N_A} I(R_i=r)` and burden `B_r=100*T_r/N_A`, with `MIXED` nested within `RECON`, not added twice. The primary index route is the robust singleton from `Q_i^I`, with non-singletons decision-faced as `DEFER`. Estimate

`T_r_hat = sum_{i in s} I(R_i^I=r)/pi_i`, and `B_r_hat=100*T_r_hat/N_A`.

Report lane-specific and favorable-consensus routes only as secondary sensitivity analyses. Also estimate all `Y`, `V/H/Z`, `J_A`, chronology, and non-`E_A` margins so exhaustive cells sum to `N_A` after weighting.

### Reproducibility

For each route retain weighted raw agreement, category-specific positive agreement, Gwet AC1, kappa, each directional confirmation probability, and stratum-specific values. Primary index reproducibility is the lower directional confirmation between the two independently prespecified index lanes. Independently report index-to-audit confirmation and the probability that compatible routing crosses `AUTO` versus `{RECON,TIMING,DEFER}` or `RECON` versus `{AUTO,NOACTION}`. Consensus cannot satisfy a gate.

### `AUTO` purity and rare unsafe minority risk

Preserve the parent's target:

`C_AUTO = P(unsafe event | at least one initial index AUTO call)` over the finite `N_A`.

Let `d_i=1` if either initial index lane calls `AUTO`. Let `x_i=1` when `d_i=1` and any of the following occurs: the other index lane calls `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`; any audit-compatible route is `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`; the all-sample safety auditor identifies an additional/unsafe unit, timing limitation, decision-relevant unknown, or boundary interaction compatible with a non-`AUTO` route; a required audit label is missing; or a documented unknown can be resolved adversely to non-`AUTO`. Then `C_AUTO=sum x_i/sum d_i`. Estimate its numerator and denominator by Horvitz–Thompson totals and the ratio by the Hájek ratio `sum_s w_i x_i / sum_s w_i d_i`; always report both estimated totals.

This is route purity against an independently labeled text-defined audit, not clinical safety or guideline truth. Zero sampled events is not zero finite-population risk.

Retain:

`D_MIX = P(A_RECON or A_DEFER | any unit appears purely omitted)`,

the weighted rate of additional/unsafe units newly found in non-sentinel admissions, and the rate at which deterministic one-unit admissions are found to contain at least two units. These quantities directly test whether rare unit multiplicity defeats apparent simplicity.

### RECON, TIMING, and DEFER capture

Using the union-compatible independent audit as the text-defined safety reference, retain:

* `Se_RECON`: probability index route is `RECON` or `DEFER` when any audit unit is incomplete, conflicting, mixed, or interaction-dependent;
* `PPV_RECON`: probability that audit evidence contains such an unsafe-content state among index `RECON` calls;
* `PPV_TIMING`: probability every unresolved active unit preventing content routing truly has `G_u<24h` or an unresolved clock among index `TIMING` calls;
* `Se_DEFER`: probability index route is `DEFER` whenever audit context, plan meaning, unit boundary, artifact, redaction, or routing is decision-relevantly uncertain;
* `leak_AUTO`: probability an audit-reference `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` admission is called `AUTO` by either index lane.

These are unchanged process properties, now measured without incorporating index labels into the audit reference.

## Survey estimation, nonresponse, and simultaneous uncertainty

### Complete-response design estimators

Under stratified SRS without replacement, Horvitz–Thompson totals are design-unbiased. For a scalar admission outcome `y_i`, use

`V_hat(T_y_hat)=sum_h N_h^2*(1-n_h/N_h)*s_yh^2/n_h`,

with zero contribution from census strata. For ratios, report the Hájek point estimate and Taylor linearization using `z_i=y_i-R_hat*d_i`:

`V_hat(R_hat)= {sum_h N_h^2*(1-n_h/N_h)*s_zh^2/n_h} / T_d_hat^2`,

where `T_d_hat=sum_s w_i*d_i`; report numerator and denominator totals as well. Never present a ratio if its estimated denominator is zero or its route-denominator effective sample size fails the inherited gate.

Construct at least 10,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates independently within each noncensus stratum; keep certainty admissions fixed and carry every unit, view, label, and response state in an admission together. The same replicate drives all route burdens, ratios, agreement quantities, workload, and unknown-compatible endpoints. A max-statistic yields one simultaneous 95% family across `B_AUTO,B_RECON,B_TIMING,B_DEFER,B_MIXED,C_AUTO,D_MIX,Se_RECON,PPV_RECON,PPV_TIMING,Se_DEFER,leak_AUTO` and route-specific agreement.

For rare and zero events, also invert the exact stratified finite-population distribution. For unconditional binary totals use stratum hypergeometric inversion. For `C_AUTO`, enumerate within each stratum the integer finite-population counts of `{unsafe AUTO, safe AUTO, non-AUTO}` compatible with observed sample counts and invert the product multivariate-hypergeometric tail. Use the widest valid simultaneous upper bound from exact inversion, bootstrap, and adverse partial identification. The output must remain positive when zero unsafe events are observed unless every relevant admission is censused.

### Nonresponse and incomplete labels

No selected admission may be replaced. “Nonresponse” includes an unreadable packet, reviewer withdrawal, late or schema-invalid label, access failure, missing component, or missing safety audit. Base inclusion weights never change. Response-propensity weighting, complete-case deletion, last observation carried forward, consensus imputation, and missing-at-random assumptions are prohibited from the primary analysis.

For every estimand, each incomplete selected admission contributes a set of all states compatible with known source facts. Compute sharp sample-level lower and upper endpoints by optimizing binary/category assignments subject to mutually exclusive exhaustive routes; for ratios use linear-fractional optimization over numerator and denominator assignments in every survey replicate. Specifically, an index `AUTO` with missing audit evidence is unsafe for the upper `C_AUTO` bound; a packet missing the index route is allowed to be an unsafe `AUTO` in the upper bound and any route in burden bounds. Unknown clocks cannot be assigned ample for a favorable endpoint. Redaction breach, unit-boundary ambiguity, and disagreement crossing routes are adverse. Publish complete-response estimates only as diagnostics beside, never instead of, these bounds.

Report response rates and weighted nonresponse burden by stratum and by pre-label deterministic attributes. Such comparisons may diagnose nonresponse concentration but cannot justify ignorability. If adverse bounds cross a route margin, that route is inconclusive or adverse regardless of complete-case performance.

## Prespecified baselines and unchanged route gates

Baselines retain the same population, weights, audit labels, and uncertainty rules:

1. marginal-majority route assignment;
2. deterministic sampling-stratum label alone (`S_ZERO` AUTO-like, `S_PARTIAL` RECON-like, `S_TIMECLOCK` TIMING-like, sentinel DEFER-like);
3. recommendation-unit majority routing, expected to fail mixed admissions;
4. favorable consensus that discards disagreement, shown only to quantify circular optimism;
5. complete-case unweighted estimates, shown only to expose selection/nonresponse bias; and
6. an explicit **incorporated-reference** sensitivity in which index component labels are allowed to adjudicate the audit reference. It is prohibited from gates; its optimism relative to the sealed audit quantifies the circularity repaired here.

Governance must approve margins before any real route labels are released. If approved unchanged, inherited defaults are `tau_AUTO=0.05`, `rho=0.90`, `AC1=0.70`, `sigma_RECON=0.95`, `pi_RECON=0.80`, `pi_TIMING=0.95`, and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`. They are feasibility margins for silent validation, not acceptable harm rates.

All routes first require: stable hashes/schemas and denominator; retrieval audit pass; no duplicate canonical `DS`; `U_R<=5%`; `U_D<=5%`; complete packet-role separation with no access breach; redaction pass; at least 200 `N_A`; at least 60 `E_A`; overall design effective sample size at least 150; `max(w_i)/sum(w_i)<=0.10`; and simultaneous all-`N_A` burden half-width no greater than 5 admissions per 100.

* **AUTO:** at least 30 sampled index candidate-AUTO admissions and route-denominator effective sample size at least 100; lower simultaneous confirmation/raw-agreement bound `>=rho`; AC1 lower bound `>=0.70`; upper `C_AUTO<=tau_AUTO`; upper `D_MIX<=0.10`; upper `leak_AUTO<=0.02`; no safety-auditor-confirmed unresolved minority unit left compatible with `AUTO`; lower `B_AUTO>0`.
* **RECON:** at least 30 sampled index `RECON` and 30 audit unsafe-content admissions, effective sample size at least 80 for each denominator; lower confirmation `>=rho`; lower `Se_RECON>=sigma_RECON`; lower `PPV_RECON>=pi_RECON`; upper `AUTO` leakage among these cases `<=0.02`; lower `B_RECON>0`.
* **TIMING:** at least 30 sampled index `TIMING`, effective sample size at least 80; lower confirmation `>=rho`; lower `PPV_TIMING>=pi_TIMING`; zero observed use of index-only `G` when another active unit has `G_u<24h`; lower `B_TIMING>0`.
* **DEFER:** at least 30 sampled independent-audit uncertain-reference admissions, effective sample size at least 80; lower confirmation `>=rho`; lower `Se_DEFER>=sigma_DEFER`; no adverse-compatible unknown routed `AUTO`.

All confidence limits used in gates are simultaneous and adverse-compatible. Routes pass independently; one cannot compensate for another. Two-lane silent validation requires both `AUTO` and `RECON`. Reconciliation-only silent validation may be nominated if `RECON` passes but `AUTO` fails on contamination/multiplicity, with all uncertainty disclosed. Missing governance approval, a broken seal, or a forbidden selection yields only `NO_SELECTION`.

## No-selection rules

The following are forbidden after the protocol hash and before final interpretation:

1. no sampling based on an index/audit route, reviewer disagreement, minority-unit finding, response, workload, or expected result;
2. no removal, replacement, downweighting, or relabeling of selected difficult/nonresponsive admissions;
3. no audit restricted to `AUTO`, apparent omissions, sentinels, or deterministic high-risk cases;
4. no unit sampling, unit weighting, or counting multiple units as independent admissions;
5. no use of index labels, index spans, index routes, consensus, or reviewer discussion in audit labels;
6. no use of audit labels or routes to repair index labels or revise matching/routing code;
7. no favorable matching of recommendation and plan inventories; all compatible cross-products remain;
8. no post-label change to eligibility, first-admission choice, `D`, reciprocal-chain rules, canonical `DS`, `G_u`, `E_A`, margins, sample sizes, estimands, or gates;
9. no stopping when purity first looks favorable, no sample-size adaptation based on route prevalence/events, and no reopening only discordant cases;
10. no complete-case, unweighted, model-imputed, or consensus-only result may trigger selection.

A violation invalidates the affected route; a role/access or label-to-audit leak invalidates the confirmatory run. The data may inform a newly proposed child, but cannot be silently reused as untouched confirmation.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. Recompute source/schema hashes and complete row counts. Hash/header/denominator drift, unexplained count mismatch, or duplicate canonical `DS` stops analysis.
2. Prove reciprocal links in both directions. A one-way, cross-subject, or cross-admission link cannot define an operative component.
3. Check row-wise `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`; only each `G_u` controls timing. Changing the index clock cannot rescue another under-24-hour unit.
4. Reproduce `N_A`, first-eligible selection, sampling stratum, `pi_i`, `pi_ij`, ordered sample, and zero replacements from sealed hashes and seed.
5. Scan each role packet for forbidden material. Seed post-`D`, route, stratum, clock-category, POE, target-plan, and other-role-label canaries; any undetected canary fails the relevant leakage control.
6. Attempt cross-role linkage using packet contents and metadata. Shared pseudonyms, filenames, sortable admission keys, or timestamps beyond the view's minimum fail masking.
7. Verify append-only seals: alter one atomic label after submission and require hash failure. Route code must not execute until all labels/nonresponse records are sealed.
8. Synthetic incorporation trap: allow index labels to resolve audit ambiguity and show the incorporated sensitivity improves; the primary sealed result must remain unchanged.
9. Differential-verification trap: fixtures called `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, and `NOACTION` must all receive the same audit components and safety review. Route-triggered audit code must fail.
10. Unit-matching trap: one-to-many and many-to-one offset/tuple mappings must produce `U_BOUNDARY`, never the mapping yielding the most favorable route.
11. Minority-unit trap: an admission with nine pure omissions and one conflict must be `A_MIXED` within `A_RECON`; duplicating the nine safe units must not alter route or weight.
12. Non-sentinel trap: place rare unsafe admissions only in `S_COMPLETE` or `S_ZERO`; design-weighted estimates must recover their finite-population burden with nominal simulation coverage. The sentinel census alone must not be claimed to prove safety.
13. Unequal-fraction trap: create known strata with different sampling fractions; unweighted purity must fail while HT/Hájek analysis and intervals retain design behavior.
14. Pairwise-inclusion trap: compare closed-form HT variance with exhaustive repeated sampling in small synthetic finite populations.
15. Zero-event trap: zero observed unsafe `AUTO` cases must return a positive exact upper bound unless the relevant finite population is a census.
16. Nonresponse trap: concentrate missing labels among unsafe, high-weight admissions. Complete-case estimates may look favorable, but adverse bounds must block the route.
17. Boundary/redaction/unknown perturbations: assign every ambiguous state favorably and adversely; only the adverse-compatible simultaneous limit can pass.
18. Threshold perturbation: display `G_u>=12h` and `>=72h` sensitivities without changing `N_A`; nomination requires no adverse route reversal at 72 hours. Stale/post-departure artifacts remain excluded.
19. POE nonleakage: adding/removing the optional POE appendix cannot alter any primary label, state, route, burden, or gate.
20. Baseline trap: unit-majority, favorable consensus, deterministic stratum, and incorporated-reference analyses cannot nominate a route when sealed worst-admission routing fails.
21. Claim checker: computed outputs may support finite-population text-defined burden, reproducibility, contamination, capture, and workload only. Any output narrative asserting guideline appropriateness, clinical truth, clinician awareness/failure, deployment safety, causal benefit, follow-up completion, or patient outcome is rejected.

The verifier can mechanically check hashes, packet fields, seals, role separation logs, sample reproduction, joins, clocks, route code, estimator formulas, replicate behavior, adverse assignments, gates, and whether conclusions match outputs. It cannot establish clinical appropriateness, the truth of missing context, actual report viewing, workflow safety, utility margins, or patient benefit; those require clinical adjudication and prospective evidence.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

**Supportive for prospective silent validation** requires exact source/denominator and retrieval audits, intact separation/seals, all-sample route-independent verification, adequate design events/effective sample size/precision, reproducible index and index-to-audit routes, and simultaneous adverse-compatible purity/capture bounds meeting every relevant gate. For `AUTO`, the finite-population bound must include non-sentinel unsafe admissions, new minority units, multiplicity, nonresponse, redaction, and boundary uncertainty. Support means only that the frozen text-defined gate merits observation in a live silent environment.

**Supportive for reconciliation-only validation** occurs if `RECON` reproducibility and audit-defined unsafe-content capture pass while `AUTO` fails because sealed auditing finds contamination, mixed admissions, or additional units. This is a useful negative finding against copy-oriented routing. A two-lane silent study requires both `AUTO` and `RECON` gates.

**Adverse** means adequately precise evidence that `AUTO` contamination exceeds its margin; independent audit repeatedly finds an unsafe minority unit hidden by simple-case screening; sealed views disagree across decision-relevant routes; timing is converted into a content failure; or `RECON`/`DEFER` misses unsafe/uncertain cases. It rejects the corresponding retrospective nomination, not every possible future system.

**Inconclusive** includes retrieval undercoverage; broken role separation or packet leakage; excessive `U_R/U_D`; redaction failure; selected nonresponse whose adverse bounds cross a margin; insufficient route events/effective sample size; wide simultaneous intervals; mandatory census above the workload ceiling; unstable unit matching; absent governance approval; or any post-label protocol change. Sparse `AUTO` is not evidence against its existence; report its upper burden bound. Passing another route cannot rescue an inconclusive one.

## Required prospective evidence and strict scope

A supportive result freezes the extractor, denominator rule, reciprocal-chain logic, unit schema, evidence views, redaction, matching and route code, audit manual, margins, and no-selection rules for a prospective silent study of consecutive adult discharges. New data must include immutable report finalization/addenda, report availability/viewing, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission times, physical departure, every unit, responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing-risk context, and final transmitted artifact. Independent experts remain blinded to silent routes and later outcomes. At least 100 route-positive admissions and 30 in every claimed route are required, or a prespecified accrual stop.

Only after successful silent validation could a governed route-stratified trial compare usual care with clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation in validated `AUTO` admissions and side-by-side reconciliation in validated `RECON` admissions. Correction/dismissal, inappropriate insertion, artifact fidelity, burden, responsibility, communication, scheduling, follow-up completion, and harms require new workflow/outcome data and expert safety review. MIMIC cannot establish them.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The clinical estimand is unchanged. The advance is that an admission can no longer validate a route through labels or adjudication that helped create that route, and verification no longer depends on being called `AUTO`. Split views prevent recommendation, context, timing, and plan judgments from being jointly steered toward a route; sealed labels prevent discussion and consensus from repairing disagreement; locked cross-product code prevents favorable component matching; an untouched probability sample gives every apparently simple admission a known positive chance of independent audit; all-unit review preserves the worst minority unit; and exact/adverse survey bounds expose nonresponse and rare unsafe cases rather than treating them as absent.

Uncertainty remains substantial. The operational retrieval dictionary can miss semantic opportunities; the negative audit bounds but cannot prove complete coverage. Eligibility is an expert-defined operational frame, not clinical truth. Independent experts can share interpretive conventions and make correlated errors. The audit is a second text reading, not a gold standard. `DS` is an incomplete stored artifact; `G_u` is storage-to-storage rather than actual viewing/editing opportunity; redaction may remove or reveal context; and unsampled admissions remain known only through randomization-based inference. Governance margins are operational prerequisites, not utilities. Therefore even an entirely favorable result supports only prospective silent validation, never automation, guideline correctness, clinician fault, causal benefit, or patient safety.
