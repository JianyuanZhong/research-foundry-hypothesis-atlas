# Provisional endpoint-freezing repair (HCC trajectory versus care process)

## Proposed relationship
Among adults with an exact inpatient HCC diagnosis, an ordered 7-day within-assay laboratory trajectory may add held-out information about a subsequent inpatient procedure-based escalation proxy beyond an admission snapshot and measured care-process variables. The unresolved claim is whether any apparent trajectory signal remains for a prespecified non-routine procedure after routine HCC treatment and documentation intensity are represented. This is a predictive proxy hypothesis, not a claim that a procedure is death, ICU admission, clinical harm, or a causal treatment effect.

## Evidence and current limits
The inherited source audit found 104,554 exact HCC patient/admission keys, 338,040 procedure rows, 321,207 timed HCC procedure rows, 15,774 untimed HCC procedure rows, and 20,052 HCC procedure rows after day 7 and before discharge (6,779 keys). These are ascertainment/support counts, not validated outcomes. The procedure source has only `患者主索引`, `就诊号`, `手术`, `开始时间`, `结束时间`, and `手术来源`; it has no validated procedure ontology, indication, severity, or clinical outcome field. A concrete label dictionary and event-support audit are therefore required before fitting.

## Falsifiable test and missing evidence
Freeze a deterministic routine-HCC procedure dictionary from the observed `手术` labels, then test whether the trajectory model improves held-out prediction of the first post-day-7, pre-discharge non-routine procedure over the snapshot baseline, with uncertainty and a timing-shifted negative control. Support would require a nontrivial, stable incremental association for the non-routine proxy and absence of the effect in the negative control; no improvement, confinement to routine procedures, disappearance after process adjustment, or a positive negative control would be adverse. Sparse events, unstable label coverage, missing timing, or inability to adjudicate meaning would be inconclusive. Clinical experts must adjudicate whether selected procedures represent deterioration; the available data cannot establish that.

The next step is to inspect the exact procedure labels and source schema, count support for each dictionary rule and endpoint stratum, and publish a child only after the dictionary and audit are frozen. No model has been fitted and no fitted result is claimed.
