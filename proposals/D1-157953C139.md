## Clinical question and evidence boundary

The unresolved, falsifiable question is whether two prespecified early post-event laboratory direction indicators—albumin strictly lower than its pre-panel value and total bilirubin strictly higher than its pre-panel value—add reproducible out-of-fold prediction of whether the event-linked encounter remains administratively open beyond 72 hours, beyond information available before the selected early panel.

The strongest claim supported before this execution was construction feasibility: the frozen source rules yield the specified episodes, clocks, panel, and endpoint. The experiment tests the incremental predictive claim; it does not establish that these laboratory directions represent morbidity, toxicity, safety, recovery, discharge readiness, medical necessity, treatment effect, causality, clinical utility, or policy relevance. `D72=1` means only that `encounters.出院时间 > event_time + 72 hours`; it is a recorded event-to-discharge timing endpoint.

## Population, clocks, and endpoint

The frozen pathway ontology and repeat-event selection are unchanged. The reconstruction produced 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator pathway patients, then 159 systemic-record and 526 comparator first repeat-event episodes.

For each assay, `B` is the latest valid value on TACE2 calendar days -30 through -1. `P` is the latest valid value in the selected repeat-event encounter during `[event_time-72 hours,event_time)`. `H` uses valid values at or after exact TACE2 and before `event_time-72 hours`, strictly before selected `P`, and outside the repeat-event encounter. No cross-assay substitution is used.

The fixed common early panel is one shared timestamp in the selected repeat-event encounter, within inclusive `[12 hours,36 hours]`, chosen by minimum absolute distance to +24 hours and earlier timestamp on a tie. The early indicators are:

- `E_alb_down = 1[early_albumin < P_albumin]`;
- `E_bili_up = 1[early_bilirubin > P_bilirubin]`.

Equality is zero. The endpoint is `D72 = 1[discharge_time > event_time + 72 hours]`, with equality zero. The fixed cohort contains 131 episodes, 34 systemic-record and 97 comparator; 48 are `D72=1` and 83 are `D72=0` (15/34 and 33/97 by arm). A +96-hour sensitivity endpoint uses the same construction and `D96 = 1[discharge_time > event_time + 96 hours]`.

The complete-source audit found 28,159,928 laboratory rows, 476,846 exact target-assay rows, 476,820 valid uncensored numeric-time rows, seven duplicate assay-timestamp groups, and zero discordant duplicate groups. All 131 event encounters linked to an encounter discharge timestamp; no panel was after discharge, admission was after the event, or discharge preceded the event.

## Prespecified prediction experiment

The primary population is the 131 episodes with the selected common panel observed. The reduced model is:

`Q_pre: D72 ~ BP_alb_down + BP_bili_up + arm + age + sex + event_year + TACE2_to_repeat_days + P_old_lead_hours + panel_hours + H_any + logN + logR`.

The full model is:

`Q_early: Q_pre + E_alb_down + E_bili_up`.

`BP_alb_down` and `BP_bili_up` compare `P` with `B`. `H_any`, `logN`, and `logR` are the frozen shared historical-process summaries, with assay bindings and clocks retained exactly. Continuous predictors are standardized using training data only. Folds are deterministic SHA-256 assignments within arm-by-endpoint cells, five folds, using the fixed disposition salt in the executable. Each fold fits a logistic model with an unpenalized intercept and fixed slope ridge penalty `lambda=1`, minimizing mean binomial loss plus `0.5*sum(beta^2)` by deterministic L-BFGS; there is no tuning, fallback learner, or outcome-dependent feature selection.

The estimand is the paired pooled out-of-fold Brier increment:

`Delta_D72 = Brier(Q_pre) - Brier(Q_early)`.

Positive values favor the early indicators. The supportive margin remains `Delta_D72 >= 0.02`; this is a prespecified practical margin, not a claim that smaller gains are clinically useful.

Diagnostics include 1,000 full-refit bootstrap replicates stratified by primary fold, arm, and endpoint class; 500 patient-specific early-panel linkage-destruction permutations within arm and primary fold; four alternate deterministic partitions; no-refit leave-one-out influence; albumin-only and bilirubin-only nested components; and the +96-hour clock sensitivity. The observation-selection experiment is a negative control, not an imputation model.

## Observation-selection experiment

The pre-outcome denominator is all 242 repeat-event episodes with both assays represented in `B` and `P`: 55 systemic-record and 187 comparator. The fixed panel was observed in 34/55 and 97/187, for 131/242 overall. Wilson 95% intervals are 0.4861–0.7348 for systemic-record, 0.4475–0.5892 for comparator, and 0.4784–0.6030 pooled.

Let `O=1` indicate that the fixed common panel exists. The process model predicts `O` from the pre-panel process and timing variables, including assay-specific historical observation summaries. The value model adds the two prior-history-to-`P` direction indicators. The same fixed-ridge, five-fold, training-only-standardized procedure is used. The selection gate passes only if `Delta_O < 0.01` and observed `Delta_O` does not exceed the 95th percentile of 500 assay-specific linkage permutations. This does not imply missing at random, and no weighting or imputation is used to recover the 111 unobserved panels or their outcomes.

## Executed aggregate results

The primary D72 reduced model had Brier score 0.22951975 and log loss 0.65122236. The early-indicator model had Brier score 0.22949521 and log loss 0.65117897. Thus:

- `Delta_D72 = 0.00002454`;
- 1,000/1,000 bootstrap replicates were estimable;
- bootstrap 95% interval: `[-0.00046688, 0.00075939]`;
- fraction of bootstrap increments `<=0`: 0.512;
- early-panel linkage-permutation 95th percentile: 0.00089545;
- observed gain did not exceed that percentile;
- alternate-partition gains: 0.00002454, -0.00014464, 0.00002112, -0.00015617, -0.00019287; median -0.00014464;
- leave-one-out delta range: `[-0.00001845, 0.00006060]`, with a sign flip across leave-one-out cases;
- albumin-only gain: -0.00009821;
- bilirubin-only gain: 0.00012347.

The +96-hour sensitivity reverses the direction slightly: reduced Brier 0.15607671, early-model Brier 0.15611727, `Delta_D96=-0.00004055`. This is not evidence of timing-specific incremental prediction.

The observation negative control had process-model Brier 0.13256691 and value-model Brier 0.13260024, giving `Delta_O=-0.00003333`; its 500-permutation 95th percentile was 0.00026761, and the observed value did not exceed it. Nonetheless, observation rates were strongly related to prior history: the aggregate standardized differences for `alb_hist_any` and `bili_hist_any` were approximately 2.49 and 2.43, and one assay-process mismatch occurred among 242 episodes (none among 131 observed-panel episodes). This is evidence of substantial selection structure, not evidence for MAR.

## Interpretation branches and falsification criteria

The observed primary gain is far below 0.02, its bootstrap interval includes zero, half the bootstrap replicates are nonpositive, alternate partitions frequently reverse sign, and the leave-one-out diagnostic changes sign. The prespecified supportive branch therefore fails. The appropriate conclusion is an inconclusive-to-precise-small/null result for this selected archive: there is no reproducible evidence that the two early direction indicators improve prediction of recorded +72-hour open status under this design, while the data and uncertainty do not justify ruling out very small or unstable effects. The linkage and +96-hour checks provide no corroboration for a specific early-panel signal. The component results do not support a stable assay-specific claim.

A materially negative early-model increment replicated across bootstrap and partitions would be adverse evidence against the incremental predictive claim; that did not occur consistently here. A large positive gain with a bootstrap interval away from zero, partition stability, no influential sign reversal, linkage-permutation separation, and a passed selection gate would have supported reproducible prognostic information only for this administrative endpoint and selected observed-panel denominator. A null or sign-unstable result, as observed, is scientifically informative: it falsifies the stronger claim of robust incremental prediction in this archive but not all possible relationships in other populations, clocks, assays, or outcomes.

The selection gate numerically passes (`Delta_O<0.01` and below its permutation threshold), but the large history-observation imbalance means it cannot license an ignorability assumption or generalization to the 111 unobserved panels. Clinical interpretation would additionally require unit and reference-range validation, analytic variation, treatment and supportive-care context, adjudicated outcomes, expert review, and external/prospective validation.
