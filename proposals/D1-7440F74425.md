# Paired-change and observation-process audit of hepatic-reserve trajectories after TACE2 by recorded immediate systemic pathway

## Clinical question and advance

The clinically consequential question is whether a systemic HCC-directed medication record appearing during days 1–14 after a patient's second qualifying TACE identifies a different subsequent hepatic-reserve course than a TACE2-only recorded pathway. The practical vulnerability in a fixed day-30–90 laboratory comparison is that a lower (or higher) follow-up albumin or bilirubin may chiefly reflect pre-existing hepatic reserve, and that follow-up values may be observed preferentially in one recorded pathway. This proposal retains the parent design's common time zero and exposure definition, but makes the primary evidence a within-person change and requires an empirical audit of repeated observations and the observation process before interpreting that change.

The strongest claim already supported by the complete-source audit is design feasibility, not a treatment effect: 6,586 adult/HCC adjacent TACE pairs were available after strict TACE episode construction and diagnosis/age restrictions; 4,671 had no ontology-matched systemic medication record on or before TACE2; 1,925 met the 2018–2024 and day-14 observation gate before final pathway ambiguity exclusions. The locked primary arms contained 317 immediate systemic-record patients and 1,492 TACE2-only patients, with paired exact albumin and bilirubin baseline plus day-30–90 values in 100 and 393, respectively. The unresolved claim is whether the pathway contrast persists after subtracting each person's pre-TACE2 level and after accounting for arm-specific laboratory observation and assay timing. No result can establish administration, adherence, causal benefit, treatment intent, radiographic response, toxicity, survival, or superiority.

This is a substantive advance over a fixed follow-up-level analysis: (1) baseline and follow-up are both explicitly measured in the same person, (2) the analysis reports the density and timing of all eligible measurements rather than silently selecting a single value, (3) it separates a clinically interpretable post-index change from an observation-process association, and (4) it uses pre-index pseudo-changes and non-hepatic negative-control analytes to test whether an apparent pathway contrast is a general surveillance/channeling artifact. Exposure remains a recorded pathway, never administration or causal treatment.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Among adults with HCC who have an earliest adjacent pair of strict qualifying TACE calendar-day episodes separated by 14–180 days, no matched systemic record on or before the TACE2 calendar day, and at least 14 days of institutional observation after TACE2, the primary exposure is mutually exclusive:

* **Immediate systemic-record pathway:** at least one qualifying systemic medication record on TACE2 calendar days 1–14, with the medication record treated only as a record/order.
* **TACE2-only recorded pathway:** no qualifying systemic medication record on or before TACE2 and no qualifying systemic medication record on days 1–14, and no generic systemic-treatment procedure-only record on days 1–14. Generic-procedure-only patients are ambiguity/unclassified, not controls.

The prespecified hypothesis is that the immediate systemic-record pathway has a smaller within-person worsening of exact-assay albumin and total bilirubin over the day-30–90 window than the TACE2-only pathway: albumin change (follow-up minus baseline) is less negative or positive, without a larger bilirubin increase. This is an association hypothesis about a recorded pathway.

### Primary estimand

For each assay separately, baseline is the closest valid numeric `定量结果` in days −30 through −1 from the TACE2 timestamp, and follow-up is the closest valid numeric value in days 30–90, using one locked selection rule: minimize absolute distance to TACE2 for baseline and to day 60 for follow-up; resolve ties by earliest `检验时间`, then stable source row order. Exact assay names are `白蛋白` and `总胆红素`; no unit conversion or composite score is allowed. The primary contrast is the adjusted difference in mean within-person change between pathways among persons with that assay's paired observations. Report assay-specific denominators, raw means, medians, and robust 95% confidence intervals. A joint “both assays” subset is descriptive and cannot replace the two assay-specific estimands.

The paired-change contrast is not claimed to remove confounding: baseline change can still be affected by indication, severity, post-TACE complications, and differential follow-up. Accordingly, baseline level and baseline missingness are covariates, not evidence of exchangeability.

### Repeated-measure trajectory estimand (prespecified feasibility/secondary)

For each exact assay, retain every numeric observation in TACE2 days 30–90 and assign it to four fixed bins: days 30–44, 45–59, 60–74, and 75–90. Within a bin, use the median per patient. A patient contributes a trajectory if at least two bins are observed; a slope is descriptive, and a mixed-effects/repeated-measures model is secondary only if both arms have adequate bin coverage. The preferred secondary model is assay value as a function of days since TACE2 (restricted cubic spline or linear day term fixed before inspection), pathway, and pathway-by-time interaction, with a patient random intercept and pre-index baseline adjustment. The interaction estimates differential post-index trajectory, not treatment effect.

Do not interpolate values across missing bins, carry forward, or treat an unmeasured value as stable. A day-60–120 paired window is a timing sensitivity, not a replacement primary endpoint. Because later TACE or systemic records are post-exposure events, they are not covariates and do not censor the primary laboratory analysis.

## Observation, assay timing, and baseline audit

Before modeling an arm contrast, produce an arm-by-year audit containing:

* cohort size, prior-systemic exclusions, generic-procedure-only exclusions, and final arm size;
* number with any numeric exact assay at baseline, any at day 30–90, and a paired value;
* number with at least one value in each of the four post-index bins, at least two bins, and at least three bins;
* distribution of baseline day, follow-up day, number of follow-up measurements, and calendar time between measurements;
* assay-specific parse failures, duplicate same-time values, specimen-type distribution, and implausible/non-numeric values requiring adjudication;
* last institutional observation and whether it reaches day 30, 60, 90, and 120, separately by pathway and year.

The primary complete-case numbers must not be represented as if missing laboratories equal normal laboratories. Fit an assay/window-specific observation model for having a baseline, follow-up, and paired outcome using only pre-TACE2 information: age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, TACE2 year/month, department, prior encounter and procedure counts in 90/180 days, prior treatment-label indicators, diagnosis proxies, baseline assay availability, baseline assay value where available, and last observation before TACE2. For day-30–90 repeated measures, additionally model the probability of each bin being observed using only variables measured before that bin and do not use the measured outcome itself. Report missingness odds by pathway and standardized differences in observation predictors.

Use inverse-probability-of-observation weighting as a sensitivity, with prespecified truncation at the 1st/99th percentiles and a record of any positivity failure. Because observation may depend on unrecorded deterioration or outside care, weighting is not a solution to informative missingness. If complete-case and weighted estimates differ materially in direction or if weights are unstable, label the clinical interpretation inconclusive.

## Confounding control and calendar/agent selection

Describe pathway counts and assay availability by TACE2 year (2018–2024), month, and encounter department. Describe each recorded agent class within the immediate systemic arm without using agent class to imply administration. Agent composition is a channeling diagnostic, not a causal subgroup claim. The primary propensity/overlap model uses only pre-TACE2 variables: age, sex, TACE interval, TACE2 calendar year/month with prespecified flexible terms, department, prior encounter/procedure counts, prior procedure-label history, diagnosis proxies, and pre-TACE2 exact laboratory values (albumin, bilirubin, INR, platelets, AFP, creatinine, sodium) with missingness indicators. Do not adjust for day-14 drug composition, post-index laboratory values, post-index imaging, later TACE3, or later systemic records.

Use propensity-score overlap weights and weighted linear regression for each paired change, with robust patient-level uncertainty. Report pre/post-weighting standardized mean differences, propensity distributions and effective sample size overall and by year. If a calendar stratum has no overlap, do not repair it by choosing a favorable era after seeing outcomes. The 2020–2024 restriction is a prespecified sensitivity only; retain 2018–2024 as primary if the gate is met. A sparse agent category is descriptive and should not be tested as a separate efficacy comparison.

## Analysis sequence and decision gates

1. Freeze cohort, ontologies, windows, assay selection, tie rules, covariates, weighting/truncation and interpretation thresholds before examining pathway outcome estimates.
2. Report an attrition table from source rows through strict episodes, adjacent pairs, HCC/adult restrictions, no-prior-systemic restriction, observation gate, era restriction, generic ambiguity exclusion and final arms.
3. Report assay-specific baseline/follow-up/paired and bin-level observation tables, with timing and missingness by arm and calendar year.
4. Report unadjusted within-person changes and baseline levels.
5. Fit the pre-TACE2 propensity overlap-weighted paired-change models for albumin and bilirubin separately; include baseline value to increase precision but describe the estimand as an association among paired observed records.
6. Repeat with observation weights, broad TACE sensitivity, 2020–2024 sensitivity, and day-60–120 window. Repeat with a stricter assay timing window (baseline −14 to −1 and follow-up 45–75) only as a prespecified timing sensitivity, not as a selected replacement.
7. Analyze repeated-bin trajectories if at least two bins are observed in both arms and the arm with fewer observations has at least 50 patients with trajectories; otherwise report descriptive bin counts only. Do not claim that a trajectory model is infeasible merely because a preferred random-effects model fails; downgrade to descriptive feasibility.
8. Apply the negative-control and pre-index pseudo-change checks before writing a clinical interpretation.

The primary validity/interpretation gates are: both primary arms preserve ambiguity exclusions; overlap is adequate overall and within retained years; each arm has effective sample size >=100; at least 100 exposed paired outcomes remain in the designated 2018–2024 primary window; assay timing is not materially different after prespecified adjustment; observation weights are stable; and complete-case and observation-weighted directions do not materially conflict. The exposed paired count is exactly at the inherited feasibility threshold, so any further restriction that drops below 100 makes the result precision-limited rather than permitting an outcome-driven window change.

## Negative controls and falsification

Use at least one non-hepatic analyte that is actually present with adequate paired availability after the exact cohort is built. Candidate negative controls, selected by a locked availability rule before outcome inspection, are exact assay names among `血红蛋白`, `白细胞计数`, `肌酐`, `钠`, and `血小板计数`; the final choice must be the first in this list with at least 50 paired exposed observations and at least 100 paired comparator observations. If no candidate meets this rule, state that the negative-control analysis is unavailable, not null. Analyze its within-person change with the same timing and weights but do not call a negative-control result proof of no bias. A material pathway contrast in a selected non-hepatic control suggests residual surveillance, calendar, or generalized severity channeling.

Use two earlier pre-TACE2 pseudo-windows, for example baseline window −90 to −31 versus −30 to −1 and −180 to −91 versus −90 to −31, requiring the same assay, numeric parsing and nearest-value rules and no post-TACE2 information. A material pathway difference in these pre-exposure changes falsifies the claim that the post-index contrast is specifically generated after TACE2, or indicates residual channeling/temporal leakage. A null pre-index contrast is only reassuring; it does not establish exchangeability.

Repeat strict versus broad TACE episode ontologies, placebo exclusion and bevacizumab-partner handling. A material reversal or arm-composition change indicates exposure/episode ontology instability. Compare year-specific results and the 2020–2024 sensitivity. Lack of positivity, unstable weights, or strong year interaction with sparse strata blocks a pooled clinical interpretation. A pseudo-index timing placebo is optional only if it can be generated without using future records; if not, explicitly mark it unavailable.

## Branches for interpretation

* **Supportive:** both exact assays directionally show less worsening in the immediate systemic-record arm, with confidence intervals reported; overlap and ESS gates pass; observation-weighted and complete-case estimates agree materially; trajectory slopes or bin contrasts are compatible; no material pre-index pseudo-change or negative-control signal; and strict/broad, calendar and timing sensitivities do not reverse the direction. This supports only a robust association between the recorded pathway and observed hepatic-reserve change. It does not support hepatic protection, treatment benefit, or causality.
* **Adverse:** the systemic-record pathway has greater albumin decline and/or bilirubin increase in paired and adequately weighted analyses, with sensitivity agreement. This argues against assuming a liver-preserving association, but cannot show medication harm because indication, severity, administration, dose, intent and post-TACE events are incompletely observed.
* **Precise null:** for each primary assay, the prespecified confidence interval is narrow enough to exclude a materially meaningful difference on that assay's observed numeric scale, while all overlap, measurement, observation, ontology and negative-control gates pass. Units and validated clinical thresholds are absent, so this is not equivalence in liver function or safety.
* **Falsified:** the temporal premise or recorded-pathway construction fails; strict/broad definitions materially reverse the result; a strong pre-index pseudo-change or negative-control contrast appears; calendar strata lack common support; or generic procedure-only records cannot be separated from the comparator. This falsifies the relevant design/robustness claim, not a causal treatment hypothesis.
* **Inconclusive:** fewer than 100 exposed paired primary outcomes, insufficient trajectory coverage, wide intervals, unstable observation weights, materially discordant complete-case and weighted results, assay timing imbalance that cannot be modeled, or missing clinical adjudication needed for the claimed endpoint. Inconclusive results must not be resolved by choosing another follow-up window or treating missingness as stability.

## Clinical adjudication and unavailable evidence

The experiment can computationally check cohort construction, timestamps, exact assay changes, repeated-measure coverage, missingness, overlap, weighting diagnostics, negative-control contrasts and recorded-event timing. It cannot establish that a medication was administered, taken, or intended for HCC; cannot validate units or laboratory reference ranges; and cannot infer combination delivery, response, progression, TACE refractoriness, treatment discontinuation, hepatic toxicity attribution, mortality, survival, or complete outside-care follow-up. Diagnosis timing is a coding proxy. Imaging CSV reports lack image files and are not automatically adjudicated. The HCC metadata identifies nominal vitals/transfers tables and no reliable Child–Pugh/performance status, complete outside-care capture, vital-status linkage or survival endpoint. Any claim about radiographic response, clinical decompensation, toxicity attribution or survival requires medication administration records, clinician review of imaging and intent, validated units, adjudicated outcomes and vital-status/out-of-system linkage, ideally in a prospective or multi-institutional study.

## Change from the assigned parent

This child preserves the parent's TACE2 eligibility, 14–180-day adjacent-pair rule, day-14 landmark, recorded medication pathway, ambiguity exclusions, 2018–2024 primary era and day-30–90 outcome window. It deepens the design by making the paired within-person change the explicit primary outcome rather than treating fixed follow-up levels as sufficient, and by requiring an empirical audit of all eligible laboratory records, assay timing, repeated-bin trajectory coverage, duplicate/parse behavior and arm-specific observation. It adds locked negative-control analyte selection, two pre-index pseudo-change checks, explicit calendar/month/department and agent-composition audits, and trajectory-specific observation models. The secondary transition analysis remains descriptive and does not alter the primary laboratory estimand. Remaining uncertainty is dominated by confounding by indication/severity, informative missingness and incomplete institutional observation, plus absent administration, intent, validated laboratory units, imaging adjudication, vital status and survival evidence.
