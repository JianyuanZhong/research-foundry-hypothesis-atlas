## Unresolved question, importance and substantive advance

At ICU hour 48, should an adult who is still recorded as invasively ventilated, has recently completed a vasopressor course, remains markedly fluid positive, and has adequate recorded MAP, urine output and potassium begin or restart an ICU-input-system furosemide or bumetanide delivery during the next 12 hours while the baseline recorded ventilation interval remains open, or avoid one until that interval closes?

This is consequential because fluid removal might facilitate airway liberation, but renal injury, dialysis, death or an unfavorable disposition could outweigh respiratory benefit. The full XML of Bircher et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13154636`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100075`, frozen source `[source checksum]`, file [source checksum]) was inspected and states that optimal timing, volume and duration of active fluid removal remain uncertain and current practice relies heavily on clinical judgment. The full XML of Orieux et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13218125`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`, source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) was inspected and explicitly calls renal congestion hypothesis-generating, without established diagnostic or therapeutic implications. The full XML of Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13476641`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`, source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) was inspected: 26 randomized trials with 1,652 participants compared heterogeneous diuretic strategies, largely in heart-failure or cardiovascular-surgery populations, with mostly low or very-low-certainty evidence. These sources support uncertainty and clinical importance; they do not support benefit from the proposed policy.

The strongest claim currently supported is narrower: MIMIC-IV 3.1 contains computable ICU procedure, medication-input, physiology, fluid-accounting and same-admission outcome records, and a retrospective hour-48 recorded-policy association can be defined. The available source does not establish true shock resolution, physiologic congestion, actual medication receipt, clinician awareness, extubation readiness, durable airway independence, renal safety, exchangeability or causality.

The latest parent attempted to test real-time validity by requiring the selected invasive-ventilation procedure row to have `storetime<=t0`. A new treatment- and outcome-blind discovery-partition audit directly shows why that rule is invalid as a decision-availability test. Among 8,709 stays with exactly one accepted item-225792 interval crossing their hour-48 landmark, only 23 (0.264%) had `storetime<=t0`. Median `starttime→storetime` was 112.23 hours, whereas median `endtime→storetime` was 0.05 hours. This source pattern is compatible with completion/finalization recording and does not validate `storetime` as row-entry or clinician-awareness time. Requiring it by t0 therefore makes the timestamp cohort structurally fail without testing clinical validity.

The substantive advance is to preserve the retrospective unique-V0 experiment while removing V0 `storetime` from inferential eligibility. The completion-clock pattern becomes a mandatory non-gating measurement-limit result. Strict as-of checks remain for every other timestamp-bearing pre-t0 gate. This makes the hypothesis falsifiable again without pretending MIMIC provides a missing ventilation-order-entry clock.

## Hypothesis and exact tested claim

In the prespecified retrospective clinical-time discovery population E_R, assignment-compatible follow-up under recorded-loop initiation A12 versus avoidance B12 is hypothesized to increase the lower partial-identification bound for five-day recorded 48-hour airway-event-free extubation by more than 0.05, while the upper association limits remain below +0.03 for recorded creatinine-rise/death and terminal harm and below +0.05 for recorded RRT/death.

Operationally, supportive-recorded requires the 95% bootstrap lower confidence limit for `DeltaL=pA_lower-pB_upper` to exceed +0.05, the frozen harm upper limits to remain below their margins, no Holm-rejected adverse family, and no material reversal in required feasible sensitivity analyses. This tests a recorded association only.

The hypothesis is falsified by any of: E_R infeasibility; failure of frozen sample/ESS/event minima; a 95% upper confidence limit for `DeltaU=pA_upper-pB_lower` at or below +0.05 after validity passes (margin-falsified); a Holm-rejected adverse family; failure of non-V0 temporal concordance; or a prespecified material reversal. A feasible, valid result meeting none of supportive, adverse or margin-falsified criteria is statistically inconclusive. No effect estimate was computed during proposal development.

## Falsification branches and clinical meaning

- Supportive-recorded: all E_R computation/minimum/validity gates pass; 95% lower limit for airway DeltaL >+0.05; chart and continuous-ICU lower limits >0; renal/RRT/terminal limits remain below frozen margins; no Holm adverse family; E_M_minus_V0 concordance passes and no required feasible sensitivity materially reverses. This supports replication or a prospective trial, not treatment use.
- Adverse-FWER: validity passes and at least one adverse family is Holm rejected. This is a record-defined observational concern that could de-prioritize or motivate review; it does not prove toxicity.
- Margin-falsified: validity passes, no adjusted adverse family, and the 95% upper limit for airway DeltaU <=+0.05. This rejects the prespecified five-point recorded-proxy margin only.
- Temporal-validity-inconclusive: non-V0 Jaccard/retention <0.90, feasible E_M_minus_V0 material reversal, or failure of non-V0 as-of computation.
- V0-timestamp-unavailable: always report whether the completion-clock audit confirms that no validated baseline row-entry timestamp exists. This is a measurement limitation, never treatment evidence and not by itself a primary failure.
- Other-validity-inconclusive: source, ambiguity, positivity, calibration, balance, endpoint, observation, renal-ascertainment, contamination, process or required sensitivity gate fails.
- Statistically inconclusive: feasible and validity-passing but no directional branch.
- Primary infeasible: E_R source/schema/state/population/strategy/model/endpoint/bootstrap/minimum failure.

Supportive, adverse and inconclusive results all remain observational. Stronger claims about actual receipt/nonreceipt, physiologic benefit/harm, durable extubation, KDIGO AKI, renal safety, clinician decision quality, subgroup actionability, transportability or a bedside recommendation require blinded critical-care/nephrology adjudication using fuller notes and records, validated airway/medication linkage, external replication and preferably randomization.
