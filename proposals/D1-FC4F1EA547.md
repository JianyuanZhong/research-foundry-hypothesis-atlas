# HCC successor: response information versus surveillance and label ascertainment

## Status and substantive change

This is a substantive successor to [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the parent’s question, HCC-only population, chronological evaluation, exact source bindings, and three inspected references, but replaces the parent’s generic “unrelated event” negative control with a prespecified two-part measurement-validity test. The new test asks whether an EHR response label has information that is specific to the subsequent liver-disease endpoint rather than being a marker of who is observed, hospitalized, or selected for treatment. No model or clinical outcome has been computed.

The strongest evidence currently supports that baseline tumor burden, liver reserve, AFP, treatment heterogeneity, and imaging features are prognostic in retrospective HCC cohorts [K1], that imaging features require complementary expert interpretation [K2], and that detection, immortal-time, and ascertainment bias are material threats in hospital follow-up [K3]. It does not establish that this local EHR response trajectory adds information after measured observation opportunity, nor that local examination text is a validated radiologic response or recurrence label.

The unresolved claim is: among patients with a first documented locoregional HCC treatment and sufficient observable follow-up, does an explicitly documented early response trajectory add held-out information about subsequently documented HCC recurrence/progression after conditioning on baseline disease/treatment and measured surveillance, while failing to predict a prespecified non-liver negative-control outcome at the same observation opportunity?

## Cohort, index, and temporal boundaries

T0 is the earliest qualifying first locoregional procedure in procedures (手术, 开始时间), prespecified as TACE/embolization, thermal ablation, hepatic resection, or other locoregional treatment; ambiguous terms remain “other.” Require an HCC diagnosis in diagnoses (诊断名称, 诊断类型) or an adjudicated imaging/diagnosis report. Exclude a prior qualifying treatment in the available record. Baseline is 90 days before through T0 and includes age, sex, treatment category, pre-T0 tumor-burden proxy, cirrhosis/liver-disease proxy, AFP, liver-function labs, and prior treatment count. No post-T0 field enters baseline.

The response window is T0+30 through T0+90 days. A liver imaging report in examinations (检查, 检查所见, 检查诊断, 开始时间) must explicitly support complete response, partial response, or indeterminate; missing is not complete response. Follow-up begins at the last response-window imaging date (or T0+90 if none), ends at first explicit post-response recurrence/progression, loss to follow-up, documented death, or administrative end 2026-01-01. Recurrence requires dated post-response imaging or a diagnosis linked to a valid post-response encounter; history, postoperative status, and orders alone are not outcomes. The primary analysis requires 180 days observable follow-up; all-follow-up inverse-probability-of-censoring is secondary.

## Observation-process and measurement variables

For every patient derive, without using post-response outcome text:

1. Response ascertainment: whether at least one liver imaging report in T0+30–T0+90 explicitly supports complete, partial, or indeterminate response; report date, modality, number of reports, and category.
2. Observation opportunity: all dated encounters and imaging reports in T0–T0+365, number and timing of liver imaging reports in T0–T0+90 and T0+90–T0+365, and interval from T0 to first post-response imaging.
3. Surveillance orders: dated orders in orders (医嘱(非药品), 开立时间, 开始时间, 结束时间, 医嘱状态, 频次) requesting liver/upper-abdominal imaging or related surveillance, used only as an observation-process descriptor and never as an outcome.
4. Treatment-selection proxies: treatment category/intensity at T0, subsequent qualifying procedures, and temporally valid orders/medication exposure when available. These are prognostic/selection descriptors, not causal treatment effects.

The observation audit reports, by response category and surveillance stratum, number with explicit response, number with post-response imaging, number with any encounter, follow-up duration, and number with documented recurrence. It compares recurrence ascertainment across response categories after accounting for measured observation opportunity. This can distinguish a signal concentrated in intensively observed patients from one that persists across measured strata, but cannot identify unmeasured outside-hospital surveillance or label error.

## Prespecified negative-control and measurement-validity test

The primary negative-control outcome is a first dated, non-liver, non-treatment-related event after the response window, defined before fitting as an examination or diagnosis whose text explicitly names a non-liver organ/system and does not mention HCC, liver, hepatic, tumor, recurrence, progression, or treatment. Examples are renal, pulmonary, cardiac, gastrointestinal, or orthopedic events; the exact lexical list and exclusions are frozen in a versioned code artifact before outcome labels are inspected. The event must occur after the last response-window imaging date (or T0+90) and before administrative end. It is not used as a predictor or as a reason to censor the primary outcome.

The negative control is evaluated with the same chronological patient split, covariates, observation variables, and model family as the primary recurrence analysis. The key comparison is the response coefficient/effect and the response-augmented held-out calibration/Brier improvement for the negative-control outcome, contrasted with the corresponding primary-outcome result. A response signal for the negative control, or a large difference in negative-control ascertainment across response groups after observation adjustment, is adverse evidence for a surveillance/ascertainment explanation. A null negative control is not proof of no bias.

A second, prespecified measurement-validity check is temporal anchor specificity. For each response category, compare the rate of recurrence labels in the 30 days immediately after the response-window imaging with the rate in the 30 days before that imaging, using only events whose dates are available. A response label should not be interpreted as a valid marker of subsequent disease if it predicts a pre-imaging “recurrence” as strongly as a post-imaging recurrence. This is a diagnostic of label timing and ascertainment, not a causal test. It is reported separately from the primary estimand and is not allowed to redefine the primary outcome after seeing results.

The primary estimand remains the patient-level, chronological held-out comparison of (i) baseline + measured observation process versus (ii) that model plus response trajectory for subsequent documented HCC recurrence/progression among patients with adequate observation opportunity. The negative-control and temporal-anchor results are validity gates, not replacements for the primary estimand.

## Baseline and longitudinal alternatives

Baseline: penalized Cox or pooled logistic model using only pre-T0 variables (age, sex, treatment, tumor-burden proxy, liver disease, AFP, liver function, time since diagnosis), with missingness indicators. Fit on earlier index years, tune on a middle validation year, and evaluate on held-out later years; never split by row. Report integrated Brier score, time-dependent AUC, calibration intercept/slope, risk differences, and bootstrap intervals.

Alternative: patient-level gradient-boosted survival model adding response category and longitudinal summaries (AFP slope/level, liver-function change, imaging count/timing, residual-disease indicators) from T0 through the response window, plus measured observation-process variables. This is scientifically useful because it tests whether temporal response information adds what baseline and measured observation intensity lose; it is not selected for complexity. A latent residual-versus-de-novo mechanistic model is deferred because local data cannot distinguish etiologies without validated imaging adjudication, pathology/genomics/ctDNA. CPU is appropriate; GPU is not needed. Planned solver envelope: 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, 2 GPUs, 28800 seconds; no model has been fitted.

## Uncertainty, falsification, and interpretation

Supportive: response remains associated after observation adjustment and the response-augmented model improves held-out calibration/Brier score with stable direction across treatment strata, calendar periods, and adequate-surveillance strata; recurrence ascertainment is not materially different across response groups after accounting for measured observation opportunity; the negative-control outcome does not show a comparable response signal; and the temporal-anchor check does not show a comparable pre-imaging signal.

Adverse: association disappears after observation adjustment, is confined to one modality or one observation stratum, reverses across periods, or the alternative is worse than baseline; this favors burden, selection, or ascertainment explanations. A response signal for the negative control or a comparable pre-imaging recurrence signal is also adverse.

Inconclusive: sparse adjudicated response/recurrence, wide uncertainty, poor calibration, or excessive missingness; this does not refute biology. A null in poorly observed patients is not evidence that response has no biological meaning.

Computation can check joins, dates, leakage, surveillance definitions, model fitting, and conclusion/output consistency. It cannot establish mechanism, treatment benefit, residual viable tumor, or an action threshold. Expert radiology/clinical adjudication of response, recurrence, tumor burden, and treatment intent is essential; prospective validation and ideally imaging/pathology or molecular residual-disease data are needed for stronger claims.
