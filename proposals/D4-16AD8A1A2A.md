# Prospective thigh muscle fat infiltration and falls-report risk in UK Biobank

## Question, evidence boundary, and clinical value

**Unresolved question.** In UK Biobank participants with visit-2 body MRI and later assessment, does posterior-thigh MRI muscle fat infiltration (MFI) predict a fall reported for the year before visit 3, after accounting for visceral adipose tissue (VAT), muscle volume, prior falls, measured function and activity? The hypothesis is a positive incremental conditional association of higher visit-2 bilateral posterior-thigh MFI with the visit-3 any-fall response among participants with at least a 366-day gap between visits 2 and 3.

The closest inspected work reports broad associations of UKB thigh MFI with mortality and incident disease [K1]; another reports UKB thigh MRI volume/distribution associations with grip weakness and falls [K2]. Neither inspected abstract establishes this lagged MFI-specific association independent of VAT and baseline function. A review identifies unresolved disease/disuse versus aging explanations and nonstandard imaging measures [K3]. These works support the question's relevance, not the hypothesis's truth or novelty beyond this specific contrast. K1 was inspected as an Europe PMC abstract only; K2 as publisher abstract/introduction passages; K3 as abstract/section 1. Limits and byte-hashed receipts are preserved in `supporting/key-references.json` and the three attached source excerpts.

Falls can lead to injury, fracture, disability and loss of independence. A robust MFI association distinct from VAT and baseline function would justify external validation of muscle-quality phenotyping for functional risk assessment; a precise near-null would argue against adding this MRI measure to falls phenotyping. The result is neither a validated screening threshold nor evidence that changing MFI prevents falls. This proposal tests an association/prediction claim, not mechanism or causality. It is not an exhaustive novelty search.

**Strongest supported claim now:** UKB fields and date-ordered return visits are available for a bounded prospective self-report association test; a prior aggregate-only scan found 4,962 participants with at least 366-day follow-up and nonempty outcome values, before clinical response-code adjudication. This is not a fitted result or confirmed event count. **Claim under test:** whether MFI retains a meaningful conditional association with subsequent any-fall self-report beyond VAT, muscle volume, adiposity and baseline function/history. **Strong rival:** the association reflects age, general adiposity, inactivity/disuse, pre-existing frailty or illness, recall/measurement, differential follow-up, or selection into MRI and return assessment. Adjustment and temporal ordering weaken some rival predictions but cannot eliminate confounding, reverse causation, or healthy-attender bias.

## Estimand, analysis, and method comparison

Primary estimand: adjusted odds ratio for visit-3 self-reported any fall per one-standard-deviation higher visit-2 posterior MFI in the defined return-visit MRI cohort, conditional on the listed baseline covariates. It is a conditional association, not a causal effect. Tabulate raw response codes before deriving outcome; fail closed if unexpected codes occur until mapped from an authoritative source. Check distributions, missingness, duplicate/unmatched `eid`, impossible dates, units, field quality, endpoint event count, and complete-case flow. Report missingness and perform a prespecified missing-data sensitivity only if enough participants and defensible assumptions permit it; do not silently impute unknown endpoint responses.

**Baseline:** logistic regression on the eligible participants with standardized continuous covariates entered linearly, categorical covariates using verified categories, and the prespecified MFI + VAT + muscle-volume + baseline-function/history terms. Compare the identical adjustment model without MFI to the model with MFI. Report adjusted OR, 95% CI, calibration/Brier score and discrimination with participant-level stratified five-fold out-of-fold evaluation; do not treat predictive performance as causal evidence.

**Substantive alternative:** generalized additive logistic model on the same subjects, outcome and inputs, with prespecified smooth terms for MFI and VAT plus their interaction; retain adjustment set and evaluate using the same folds and uncertainty scheme. This may reveal a nonlinear threshold or MFI association concentrated at high VAT that a linear coefficient masks, directly separating additive/general-adiposity from effect-modification patterns. It is not chosen for small predictive gain. Estimate uncertainty for the smooth contrast (bootstrap or appropriate model intervals) and report calibration/Brier, not AUC alone. If event support is inadequate for stable flexible fitting, defer the GAM and call the comparison inconclusive rather than reducing the cohort or changing the question. Use no GPU: this is a small-to-moderate tabular task. Solver envelope in `inputs.json` is 16 CPUs, 256 GiB, 8 GPUs and 28,800 seconds; an unmeasured planning estimate is 2–8 CPU-hours for raw extraction/quality checks, logistic/GAM fits, bootstrap and folds. No full study was run in discovery.

## Decision criteria and claim limits

Before fitting, a clinical reviewer must define a meaningful effect margin for conditional odds/risk differences; absent that margin, report effect estimates and uncertainty without calling a small association clinically important. **Supportive:** positive adjusted MFI association with CI excluding null and stable direction/magnitude in prespecified checks, beyond VAT/function; flexible fit shows a stable, uncertainty-supported MFI pattern or interaction. This supports an incremental prognostic association in selected UKB returnees and warrants external validation, not causality, mechanism, clinical utility or treatment thresholds. **Adverse:** adequate event support and precision with near-null/opposite MFI estimate excluding the expert-set meaningful margin, while baseline function/VAT explain observed association and no stable flexible pattern emerges. This weakens this specific added falls signal, not all myosteatosis biology. **Inconclusive:** sparse outcomes, wide interval spanning meaningful benefit and null, unexpected codes, unstable fits, missingness, or selection sensitivity; an imprecise null is not refutation.

Computationally checkable: population filters, field/time bindings, same-namespace joins, code classification, outcome support, fitting, point estimates, intervals, model comparison and whether written conclusions correctly track those outputs and their uncertainty. Not automatically checkable: clinical meaning of the margin, whether self-report matches adjudicated falls, MRI MFI validity/quality, residual confounding or whether MFI causes decline. Stronger claims need expert adjudication and preferably event dates/severity, activity and time-varying disease data, external cohort validation, and intervention/mechanistic evidence. A null can inform whether to stop, revise measurement, or seek those data; a supportive association proceeds to independent validation, not clinical deployment.

## Deferred alternatives and demonstrations

A learned disease-sequence model is not selected: this estimand has only sparse MRI/function visits and a single recall outcome, so a dense sequence representation is unlikely to resolve the measurement/selection rival better than the prespecified spline interaction; revisit if dated longitudinal trajectories become relevant. No neural/GPU method is banned generally. Delphi/generative disease sequences are not a fit here because the question is one imaging exposure and later survey response, not disease ordering. Oncoformer is not pursued because cancer outcomes and chest images are irrelevant and its main article/full STAR Methods were unavailable in the demonstration bundle (supplement only inspected for that summary). ALADYNOULLI targets longitudinal latent disease/genetic trajectories, not this contrast; it would be a changed EHR-only model, not a necessary adaptation. These are scientific deferrals, not claims that the methods are infeasible in general.
