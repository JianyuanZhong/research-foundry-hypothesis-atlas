## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens (Olink/SomaScan) nominate single circulating proteins (e.g. GDF15, CHI3L1, FABP1, IL-6/CRP family) as predictors of many diseases. Competing explanations for a robust cross-disease protein signal:
- **A (disease-specific):** the protein marks a process preferential to disease *D*.
- **B (shared systemic axis):** it marks a shared allostatic-load / inflammation / multimorbidity state ([K2], [K3]).
- **C (treatment/operative correlate):** it changes with surgery / operative management / care intensity rather than disease biology ([K1]).

The unresolved question: **after removing the shared healthy-vs-diseased and disease co-occurrence structure, does any individual protein retain a disease-preferential residual that is not explained by operative-treatment state?** This is consequential: it decides whether a nominated protein is a disease-biology candidate or only a marker of being co-diagnosed / under operative treatment — i.e. whether it is worth advancing to a disease-specific assay or intervention.

## Leading claim vs strongest rival, and where they differ
- Leading claim: at least some proteins retain a **disease-preferential** residual (A) beyond shared axes.
- Strongest rival: the residual is fully explained by **operative/treatment state (C)** and/or shared multimorbidity (B); i.e. among diseased-only participants with the SAME (unrelated) operative state, the disease-preferential transfer gap collapses.
- Observation on which they differ: the case-case transfer gap Δ_D^treat, computed on a held-out participant half within a fixed unrelated-procedure stratum, relative to a within-stratum permutation null. A survives if Δ_D^treat exceeds the null band; C is favored if the raw gap is positive but Δ_D^treat collapses.

## Design (what the solver must fit/estimate)
- **Population:** Olink `n_eid` participants carrying disease D (ICD-10 3-char block) or a pre-specified unrelated disease D' from a clinically adjudicated block list; restricted to a fixed, adjudicated **unrelated OPCS procedure-family** stratum.
- **Primary estimand:** Δ_D^treat = AUC of a disease-preferential score separating D from D' on a **participant-level held-out half**, computed within the unrelated-procedure stratum, minus a **within-stratum permutation null** (1,000 label permutations within age-decile × sex × comorbidity strata).
- **Baseline:** a 1-D shared-axis score (first principal component / aggregate of the 2,923 proteins).
- **Learned/mechanistic alternative:** elastic-net over the 2,923 proteins OR a jointly estimated latent-axis model; the alternative reveals **per-protein residuals orthogonal to the shared axis**, i.e. which specific proteins carry disease-preferential information that the 1-D baseline discards. Justification: the baseline can only answer "is there a shared axis"; the learned alternative can answer "which protein, if any, adds disease-preferential signal beyond it".
- **Inclusion rule:** disease block with ≥200 cases and ≥50 cases within the adjudicated unrelated-procedure stratum.
- **Negative controls:** no-ICD reference group; external-cause ICD-10 V01–Y98; OPCS-present-without-disease.
- **Falsification:** supportive = Δ_D^treat > upper permutation band; adverse = raw/case-case gap positive but Δ_D^treat collapses into the band (supports C); inconclusive otherwise (imprecise null does not refute A).
- **Fitting budget:** tabular, ≤16 CPU / CPU-first; ~2,923 predictors × ~tens of thousands of rows → minutes to low hours on CPU; a GPU is not required but may accelerate repeated permutations. Task-specific fitting is permitted within the solver envelope; this episode does not launch it.
