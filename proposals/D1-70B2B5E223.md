## Event, clocks, and endpoint

For each eligible adult HCC patient, retain the first repeat TACE event 15–90 calendar days after TACE2, one event per patient. For each assay and event, reconstruct:

- B: latest valid assay in TACE2 day −30 through −1;
- P: latest valid assay in the selected repeat encounter in `[event_time−72 hours, event_time)`;
- Y: valid assay in the selected repeat encounter in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, nearest +24 hours, with deterministic earlier-time/source-order tie breaks.

Require strict `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`, positive P lead, one patient-assay row, and laboratory-independent event selection. Age and sex are taken from the latest encounter at or before TACE2. The endpoint is the observed same-assay Y value; conditioning on repeat TACE and a complete observed triplet is acknowledged selection conditioning and does not support population-wide claims.

## Forward-calendar design

The primary calendar blocks are fixed before inspecting any future Y values and are non-overlapping 365-day intervals:

- training: event dates strictly before 2022-01-01; test: `[2022-01-01, 2023-01-01)`;
- training: event dates strictly before 2023-01-01; test: `[2023-01-01, 2024-01-01)`;
- training: event dates strictly before 2024-01-01; test: `[2024-01-01, 2024-12-31)`.

For every assay and block, construct an outcome-blind eligibility table from event, B, and P availability only. The support table is formed before Y is selected and uses event/B/P availability and arm. Complete-Y rows are then reported separately as an observation/selection diagnostic and are the rows available for fitting and evaluating Y. Thus complete-Y loss cannot silently redefine the support gate.

The interpretation gate requires, in both pre-origin training and future test support, at least 20 eligible B/P/event episodes total, at least 10 systemic-record episodes, and at least 10 comparator episodes. Ungated estimates are descriptive regardless of their point estimate or conditional interval.

## Nested estimand and analysis

For each assay separately, compare the same frozen ridge pipeline in two nested models:

```
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: Y ~ B + P + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
```

The primary estimand in each block is pooled test-set standardized RMSE gain:

`[RMSE(M0) - RMSE(M1)] / SD_test(Y)`.

Positive gain means that P improves future prediction conditional on the locked core; it is not a causal effect. Standardized MAE gain, prediction errors, and arm-specific errors are secondary diagnostics. Arm-specific quantities diagnose mixture and support; they are never interpreted as treatment contrasts.

All preprocessing is learned strictly from complete-Y training rows before the origin: age median imputation with missingness indicator, sex encoding, variance filtering, centering/scaling, ridge penalty selection, and fitting. The alpha grid is `[0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100]`, with inner leave-one-patient-out tuning and deterministic smaller-alpha tie breaking. No test outcome is used in feature processing, tuning, or fitting.

Use 500 paired patient-frequency bootstrap replicates within observed arm strata, holding the two pre-origin fitted prediction vectors paired. The interval is conditional on the fitted pipelines and test-sample resampling; it is not uncertainty over institutions, model development, all calendar eras, or unobserved outcomes.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

A gated block is supportive for that cutoff only if the reconstruction and clock assertions pass, the outcome-blind gate passes, pooled standardized RMSE gain is positive with its paired conditional interval above zero, and neither arm diagnostic reverses direction. This supports incremental forward prediction in that selected calendar mixture only.

A gated adverse result is an interval at or below zero, repeated degradation of M1, or a material directional reversal under adequately supported composition. It would falsify the claimed incremental prediction for the tested era/population, not prove that B alone is adequate and not establish a biological mechanism.

The result is inconclusive for reproducible calendar transport if only one block passes, later blocks are sparse or comparator-dominated, intervals are wide, observation fractions are low, outliers dominate, or any denominator/clock/source assertion fails. Reproducible transport requires at least two adequately supported, preferably non-overlapping later blocks with precise positive gains and no material adverse composition diagnostic. This dataset is not expected to meet that stronger criterion if later systemic support remains sparse.
