# Provisional hypothesis: discordant AKI recovery in MIMIC-IV

Status: provisional repair of [prior hypothesis]; no computed results are claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adult MIMIC-IV ICU patients with an acute kidney injury episode and a creatinine measurement near a prespecified 48-hour landmark, patients whose creatinine has improved but whose urine output remains low, or whose cumulative fluid balance remains positive, will have higher subsequent in-hospital death or persistent/worsening kidney dysfunction than patients with concordant creatinine and urine-output recovery. The comparison will adjust only for information available before the landmark.

The claim is prognostic, not causal. Discordance may reflect delayed clearance, ongoing tubular injury, fluid overload, or differences in monitoring and treatment. The experiment tests whether the discordant phenotype adds information beyond a creatinine-only recovery phenotype.

## Concrete design to verify

- Population: adult ICU stays in MIMIC-IV with an AKI episode and a creatinine measurement near the landmark; first qualifying ICU stay per hospitalization.
- Time origin: ICU admission; landmark is 48 hours after ICU admission when the required measurements exist.
- Exposure: creatinine recovery versus non-recovery, urine-output recovery versus non-recovery, and cumulative fluid balance, using only observations before the landmark. Thresholds and units must be fixed from the inspected MIMIC-IV item definitions before analysis.
- Outcomes: in-hospital death and persistent/worsening kidney dysfunction after the landmark, with a prespecified follow-up window and competing-risk handling.
- Baseline: a parsimonious logistic/Cox model using pre-landmark severity, creatinine trajectory, treatment, and demographics.
- Alternative: a time-aware model or landmarked sequential analysis using the ordered pre-landmark trajectories of creatinine, urine output, and fluid balance, with the same split and outcome. This can reveal whether the added information is a temporal pattern rather than a single landmark value; it does not establish mechanism.
- Falsification: no incremental prognostic information for discordance, disappearance after monitoring-intensity adjustment, or reversal in a prespecified sensitivity analysis would challenge the hypothesis. An imprecise null is inconclusive, not refutation.
