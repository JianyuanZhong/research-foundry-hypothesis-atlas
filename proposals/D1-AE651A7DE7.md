# Cross-domain physiologic discordance at ICU minute 360

Status: Episode-27 substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. This is an observational prognostic experiment about a recorded physiologic phenotype, not a causal test-ordering study, treatment recommendation, hospital ranking, or evidence that an alert or additional testing improves survival. No model has been fitted.

## Unresolved question and advance

The parents have separated aggregate laboratory process from temporal observation history, vital-monitoring intensity, carePlanEOL documentation, death versus alive-discharge specificity, and result time versus revision/information-availability time. The remaining patient-linked question is:

> At ICU-relative minute 360, does cross-domain discordance—biochemical/metabolic stress while recorded pressure and heart-rate observations appear reassuring—identify a clinically consequential state beyond the parent model's additive laboratory/vital state and laboratory-process features?

This could inform a future, prospectively validated decision about which apparently stable patients merit repeat physiologic assessment or closer review. It tests a joint clinical mechanism, not another observation count, timestamp, or surveillance-workflow signal.

The strongest available evidence is feasibility and schema evidence only. The inherited outcome-blind audits support a large multi-hospital eICU snapshot and revision-safe laboratory rows. A local source scan verified labels `lactate`, `anion gap`, `bicarbonate`, `HCO3`, `creatinine`, `BUN`, `pH`, `paO2`, `paCO2`, and `FiO2`, but also showed material interface/unit heterogeneity. No model performance, occult hypoperfusion, physiologic truth or clinical intent is established.

The unresolved claim is that a reproducible cross-domain phenotype—especially metabolic stress with non-hypotensive recorded vitals—adds transportable information about later recorded ICU death, rather than reflecting generic observation practice, revision/interface behavior, treatment selection, care-limitation documentation, or an already captured marginal value.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Among adults reaching minute 360 in their first valid ICU stay, a prespecified metabolic-laboratory/recorded-vital discordance phenotype and continuous physiologic-axis score improve held-out-hospital prediction and calibration of recorded ICU death through minute 1,800 beyond the inherited revision-safe laboratory-process, laboratory-state, vital-state, vital-intensity, admission-context and carePlanEOL features, with a smaller or null increment for recorded alive ICU discharge.

The primary phenotype is outcome-free. In the last 60 minutes, with laboratory rows restricted to `labresultoffset in [0,360]` and `labresultrevisedoffset <= 360 or null`, call a patient laboratory-positive when at least two of these three verified-unit components are abnormal: lactate >=2 mmol/L, anion gap >=16 mmol/L-equivalent, or bicarbonate/HCO3 <20 mmol/L-equivalent. Call recorded vitals reassuring only when the frozen pressure source (systemic mean pressure when valid, otherwise noninvasive mean pressure), heart rate and, when present, SpO2 satisfy MAP >=65 mmHg, heart rate <=100/min and SpO2 >=92%. Missing SpO2 is not reassuring; it is a separate missingness stratum. The primary discordance indicator is laboratory-positive plus reassuring recorded vitals. Insufficient measurements are unknown, not negative.

The paired continuous metabolic-stress score is `z(lactate) + z(anion gap) - z(bicarbonate)`, standardized using development hospitals only. pH is a sensitivity component, not a replacement. Renal (creatinine+BUN) and oxygenation (PaO2/(FiO2/100), requiring verified PaO2 and FiO2) axes are secondary. Thresholds are interpretable challenges, not treatment targets.

The hypothesis is falsified for the patient-linked mechanism if the discordance increment is absent/adverse after parent adjustment, is reproduced by lab/vital linkage permutation, is no larger than a matched vital-process interaction, is equally or more pronounced for alive discharge, occurs only in one interface or hospital regime, depends on post-360 revisions, or disappears when the near-landmark interval is masked. High-capacity improvement without linkage and specificity checks is not supportive.

Require the stay to reach minute 360.

All predictors are known by minute 360. The primary state window is (300,360]; parent process features use [0,360]. No post-360 predictor, Apache mortality/APACHE APS field, hospital-ID predictor, or outcome-dependent codebook choice is allowed.

Retain the parent outcome: event of interest is selected `unitdischargestatus == Expired` with `unitdischargeoffset in (360,1800]`; competing event is `unitdischargestatus == Alive` in that interval; neither by minute 1,800 is administrative censoring. Fit cause-specific discrete-time hazards in 24 one-hour bins and derive recorded-death CIF with alive discharge competing. Audit unexpected statuses, duplicate first-stay selections, invalid offsets and impossible combinations; do not silently recode them. Death after alive ICU discharge, hospital mortality, latent death and post-ICU outcomes are outside the estimand.

Primary estimands are macro-hospital held-out differences in death-specific integrated competing-risk Brier score and calibration at 600, 1,200 and 1,800 minutes. Patient-weighted results, death CIF discrimination, parallel discharge CIF and fixed-burden review diagnostics are secondary.

## Comparable baseline and alternative

The transparent baseline `B_add` is pooled elastic-net discrete-time cause-specific logistic regression with the inherited parent feature set: admission context; revision-safe laboratory state and aggregate/availability process; periodic/aperiodic vital state and process; carePlanEOL timing/presence; missingness; and 24 outcome bins. It has no cross-domain products or discordance indicators. All scaling, imputation, regularization and calibration are learned in development hospitals only.

The primary scientific alternative `M_disc` adds only the outcome-free metabolic discordance, continuous metabolic-stress score, renal and oxygenation axes, and prespecified lab-stress-by-vital-reassurance interactions. It can reveal whether a coherent physiologic regime matters when separate additive coefficients lose the cross-domain relationship. Its coefficient uncertainty and calibration are scientific outputs, not merely a predictive gain.

A secondary learned alternative `T_cross` is a small two-layer CPU MLP with separate death/discharge hazard heads over the same fit-standardized domain vectors, raw state summaries, missingness and parent adjustment features. It has no hospital ID, post-360 data or extra temporal bins. Use a parameter-matched non-interaction MLP, domain-label shuffle and response-surface check. Retain `T_cross` as evidence only if held-out calibration/Brier improvement over `B_add` is uncertainty-compatible and agrees with stable `M_disc`; otherwise report it as negative/inconclusive. A GRU/TCN is deferred because the parents already test temporal order/persistence and revision/result clocks. A mechanistic state-space model is deferred because waveform, specimen/order timestamps and adjudicated latent organ states are unavailable.

## Split, uncertainty and review diagnostic

Use five outer folds holding out approximately 20% of hospitals; keep all stays for a `uniquepid` together. Within development hospitals use a 75/25 hospital-stratified fit/validation split. Freeze codebook, thresholds, exclusions, imputation, standardization, hyperparameters, calibration and thresholds in fit only.

Use at least 1,000 hospital-cluster bootstrap resamples for 95% intervals, or a prespecified hierarchical hospital model if unstable. Report macro-hospital and patient-weighted results, with hospital/episode/death/discharge/censor counts and discordance-positive/unknown counts per fold. Do not use patient-level normal intervals as primary transport uncertainty.

Within the recorded-vital-reassuring subgroup compare models at fixed burdens of 0.5%, 1%, 2% and 5% per held-out hospital. Report reviews per 100 episodes, recorded-death capture, PPV, false-review burden and number needed to review. A cell needs at least 20 hospitals, 200 episodes, 20 recorded deaths, 20 alive discharges and 50 patients with sufficient primary measurements. This is a label-relative review diagnostic, not net benefit, clinical utility or an instruction to order a test.

Support gates: at least 100 eligible hospitals, 50 event-bearing hospitals, 1,000 recorded deaths, 1,000 alive discharges, about 20 hospitals per fold where feasible, at least 30% coverage for every retained primary analyte/vital field (or 20% with two observations), at least 80% of held-out hospitals with overlapping revision-safe support, and non-sparse positive/negative primary discordance strata. Failed gates make the mechanism claim inconclusive and do not authorize relaxing the cohort, landmark or outcome.

## Falsification and interpretation

Save: (1) within-hospital, baseline-risk-stratified permutation of laboratory histories against vital histories, preserving marginal distributions and recording density but breaking patient linkage; (2) same-lab/earlier-vital alignment and masking of (330,360] to test near-landmark artifacts; (3) matched vital-process interaction, lab-only, vital-only, additive-state-only and parent `B_avail` fits; (4) death-CIF versus alive-discharge-CIF contrast; (5) strict revision-safe, common-analyte, revision-known-only and interface-stratified fits; (6) treatment-context strata using the explicitly bound infusion/respiratory/intake-output tables; (7) site-regime strata using hospital descriptors but no site predictors; and (8) `T_cross` order/label shuffle, non-interaction control and response-surface checks.

Support requires all gates, death-specific `M_disc-B_add` improvement with uncertainty compatible with a meaningful improvement and no material calibration harm, smaller/null discharge increment, attenuation under linkage permutation and temporal masking, stability across hospitals/interfaces, and fixed-burden improvement in the reassuring-vital subgroup. Adverse evidence is null/adverse interaction, no linkage attenuation, matched vital-process effect, equal/larger discharge effect, post-360/interface/site dependence, or treatment/documentation-only concentration. Inconclusive evidence includes failed gates, intervals spanning zero and the prespecified meaningful effect, unstable folds, insufficient verified units, high unknownness, irreproducible codebook, or a learned gain without an interpretable falsification-consistent pattern.
