# Episode 66 compiler-contract child: lineage certificate before respiratory outcomes

## Scope, parentage, and unchanged frozen question

This is a substantive child of the assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves their frozen scientific question and estimand rather than reopening the settled transportability or endpoint vocabulary findings.

The frozen question is: among the locked eICU person-landmarks that received one of two exact care-plan labels, does receipt of `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` rather than `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` contain reproducible information about a later persistent respiratory patient-state trajectory, conditional on strictly pre-label respiratory state and documentation opportunity? The estimand is a descriptive conditional association and incremental hospital-held-out predictive-information contrast. It is not a treatment effect, SBT-performance estimate, extubation-readiness estimate, liberation estimate, quality measure, or clinical-benefit estimate.

The frozen denominator and time axis remain exactly 540 person-landmarks, 443 unique first qualifying ICU stays/persons, and 74 hospitals; first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid`; landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes; first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`; and `D` equal to that label row's `cplitemoffset`. Repeated landmarks in a retained stay remain rows and are clustered by stay. Exact target labels, inherited pre-landmark exclusions, baseline/outcome windows, endpoint thresholds, hospital-held-out comparisons, and proxy-only interpretation are not changed.

## Most important repaired weakness

The parents specify excellent endpoint and model definitions, but their execution contract still permits a fatal ambiguity: a compiler may either use a missing parent artifact or reconstruct an apparently matching 540-row cohort without proving that every inherited filter, tie rule, first-stay choice, and action-row identity is the same. A matching row count is not lineage identity. Reading outcome rows before resolving that ambiguity risks outcome-selected reconstruction and silently changes the estimand.

This child makes the experiment a two-phase, fail-closed compilation:

1. **Lineage phase, before any respiratory outcome read:** resolve or deterministically rebuild the frozen population and exact action rows from only the identity/exposure/inherited-eligibility sources; write a canonical lineage certificate, row digests, and source/filter audit. No `respiratoryCharting`, `respiratoryCare`, `vitalPeriodic`, or `treatment` payload rows may be opened in this phase, except schema/header inspection and source-hash verification.
2. **Outcome phase, only after certificate pass:** join the certificate one-to-one to the seven prespecified outcome/diagnostic source tables, freeze the outcome-blind parser and label dictionary before arm-specific summaries, and execute the persistent endpoint and held-out analysis.

The certificate must prove exact identity, not only cardinality. If the supplied parent artifacts cannot be found, or the deterministic rebuild cannot reproduce their row-level canonical digest and all inherited flags, execution stops as **lineage infeasible/inconclusive** before outcome rows are read. No approximate cohort and no association estimate is allowed. This repairs executability and auditability without changing the frozen question or estimand.

## Evidence boundary

The strongest evidence inherited from the parents is limited to the exported structured record: the two exact values are sparse and hospital-concentrated; earlier setting associations were peak-pressure dominated and non-peak/sedation mappings were site-dependent; and independent durable-liberation ascertainment did not meet its prespecified requirements. Those findings do not establish whether bedside daily extubation evaluation or an SBT occurred.

The untested claim remains only that the exact label adds reproducible information about a later persistent charted respiratory state beyond pre-D state and documentation opportunity. Any supportive result is a statement about this eICU chart construct. It cannot establish label semantics, delivered device settings, clinician intent, SBT performance, extubation, reintubation, readiness, treatment effect, benefit, or harm. eICU lacks raw waveforms, device logs, complete narrative notes, local interface dictionaries, SBT checklists, adjudicated extubation/reintubation outcomes, and post-ICU follow-up.

No computed cohort counts, endpoint counts, effect estimates, model metrics, or gate decisions are claimed by this proposal. The counts 540/443/74 are acceptance criteria inherited from the frozen lineage, not results of this invocation.

## Phase 1: deterministic lineage certificate

### Artifact acceptance

The compiler first searches only declared candidate-support/workspace locations for the two frozen artifacts. It accepts them only when content hashes, exact headers/types, and canonical row digest agree. It then verifies:

* exactly 540 rows, 443 distinct `patientunitstayid`/first qualifying stays, and 74 `hospitalid` values;
* one row per `(patientunitstayid, landmark)`, no duplicate, and only the five allowed landmarks;
* one first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid`, with no later unit visit silently substituted;
* exact arm strings, `L <= D <= L+360`, and `D == decision_offset`;
* identity equality for `sid`/`patientunitstayid`, `uniquepid`, `hospitalid`, and `landmark` between cohort and action rows;
* no same-time contradictory first target labels under the inherited rule; and
* all inherited exclusion flags and source-row identifiers agree with the parent certificate.

Canonical sorting is `(patientunitstayid, landmark, D, cplgeneralid)` with explicit numeric/string normalization recorded in the manifest. The compiler computes a row digest over all identity, label, timing, selected source IDs, and inherited-filter flags, then a file hash. Count equality alone is a fatal failure.

### Rebuild path, if and only if parent artifacts are unavailable

Rebuild is permitted only from the frozen parent recipe supplied as candidate support, not from prose paraphrase or outcome-dependent code. The recipe must enumerate every source table, column, predicate, missing-value rule, tie rule, offset boundary, first-stay ordering, and row digest input. The rebuild must scan full source files and write per-filter counts and selected source-row IDs. It must reproduce the expected 540/443/74 identity and the parent row digest. If any expected artifact hash differs, the compiler must label the lineage `reconstructed-but-not-byte-identical` and stop before outcome rows; a coincidental 540 rows is insufficient.

This is deliberately stricter than silently treating an approximate reconstruction as a replication. If the parent recipe is not available in a machine-readable form, the only valid result is feasibility failure/inconclusive. The proposal does not claim that either parent artifact or recipe is present in this invocation workspace.

### Exposure verification

For each certified row, scan `carePlanGeneral` only in the lineage phase and verify the exact first target label in `[L,L+360]`, with `D` equal to `cplitemoffset`. Preserve all eligible landmarks. Verify that no post-D care-plan row influenced eligibility. Record selected `cplgeneralid`, `cplgroup`, raw `cplitemvalue`, `activeupondischarge`, offset, and same-time competing rows. Exact strings are compared after no semantic expansion; whitespace/case normalization, if inherited by the parent, must be explicitly stated and applied identically. Write `lineage_certificate.json` with status `verified`, `failed`, or `reconstructed_nonidentical`; `lineage_filter_counts.json`; `lineage_rows.csv`; `lineage_row_digests.csv`; and `phase_gate.json`. A failed gate produces no endpoint/model output and no scientific positive or negative result.

## Phase 2: persistent respiratory-state endpoint

After lineage verification, freeze the parser and dictionary before reading arm-specific summaries. No care-plan value, care-plan count, note, treatment value, or post-D opportunity variable enters the primary endpoint or baseline.

For each certified `(stay, landmark)` row, define `B=(D-720,D]`, pre-D placebo `P=(D-1440,D-720]`, primary observation window `O=(D+360,D+1800]`, and horizon sensitivity `O12=(D+360,D+1080]`. Clinical offsets, not entry offsets, determine inclusion. A numeric observation is valid only when the full trimmed value matches a signed decimal/scientific token, is finite, and passes fixed bounds: FiO2 `[0,100]` percent representation; PEEP/PEEP-CPAP `[0,60]`; pressure support `[0,60]`; set rate `[0,100]`; `sao2` `[0,100]`; respiration `[0,200]`. Reject blanks, units, ranges, inequalities, sentinel text, and out-of-range values. Do not convert fractions, interpolate, carry forward, or infer units.

The outcome-blind exact component mapping must be frozen and source-wide enumerated before arm-specific counts:

* FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`;
* PEEP: `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`;
* pressure support: `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`;
* set rate: `Vent Rate`, `VS RESP RATE`.

Matching is case-sensitive after the declared outer-whitespace trim. Peak pressure, plateau pressure, mean airway pressure, pressure control, peak flow, total/spontaneous rate, and oxygen-flow labels are excluded from the primary. The executable must enumerate every encountered `respchartvaluelabel`, matched family, and unmatched label; substring matching is forbidden.

For each component/window require at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. Summarize all valid values by median, with counts, unique timestamps, first/last offset, largest gap, parse rejections, and missingness reason. The support domain passes if at least one non-peak component's post median is lower than its B median by at least 0.05 FiO2, 2 cm H2O PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, 2 cm H2O pressure support, or 2 breaths/min set rate. A one-component missingness cannot be imputed from another component.

The safety domain uses `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration` with the same two-observation/separation rule and medians. Safety passes when available signals do not fall by more than 2 percentage points or rise by more than 4 breaths/min. A signal missing in either required comparison makes the primary endpoint missing; a sensitivity requires both signals and another permits at least one, both frozen before outcomes.

The primary state is mutually exclusive:

1. `persistent_improvement`: support passes, safety passes, and at least one post-O `respiratoryCare` row has nonmissing `airwaytype` or a nonmissing ventilator interval field;
2. `observed_non_improvement`: complete endpoint criteria are available but support fails or safety fails;
3. `endpoint_missing`: no competing exit but the minimum support/safety observations fail;
4. `competing_exit`: discharge/death/truncation occurs on or before the end of O, before complete observation; classify using `patient.unitdischargeoffset`, `unitdischargestatus`, and `unitdischargelocation`.

An exit at or before `D+360` is additionally flagged as no post-washout opportunity. No exit is recoded as improvement or ordinary non-improvement. Support-only and no-airway-corroboration endpoints are secondary sensitivities and may not replace the primary.

## Pre-D opportunity, leakage, and held-out analysis

Construct pre-D state and opportunity only from values/timestamps at or before D. State features are recent and median pre-D non-peak settings, safety values, fixed pre-D trends from P versus B, source presence, counts, distinct clinical times, and median entry lag. Opportunity-only features are counts, distinct labels/times, respiratory-care rows/types, vital observations, treatment rows, and entry-lag summaries, with no respiratory values and no care-plan variables. The label model adds only the exact binary label to the identical state-plus-opportunity features. A label-only model is descriptive and not a sufficient baseline.

Before fitting, write a leakage audit containing maximum predictor clinical offset, maximum entry offset, all predictors with time `>D`, and source/column. Any post-D value, post-D count, post-D treatment, post-D respiratory-care row, care-plan count, or outcome-derived availability in a baseline column is a hard stop. The post-D opportunity profile is reported only as an ascertainment diagnostic.

The primary transport comparison is leave-one-hospital-out. All imputation, scaling, feature selection, regularization, observation weights, and calibration are fit on training hospitals only. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss/Brier/calibration, AUROC only where both classes occur, and evaluation-minus-no-trial predicted/observed risk differences. Report each hospital's label support, endpoint states, missingness, competing exits, and fold availability. Repeated landmarks are clustered by `patientunitstayid`; a `uniquepid` clustering sensitivity is descriptive.

The label-contrast transport gate requires at least five hospitals with at least ten complete endpoint-observed rows under each label and at least 50% of complete endpoint-observed rows in those hospitals. If the gate fails, report the four-state distribution and prediction metrics if defined, but call the label-contrast estimand inconclusive. No forced contrast is made in a fold containing one arm only.

Use a stay-cluster bootstrap with fixed seed `61061`, refitting the full pipeline and preserving hospital strata; target 1,000 replicates, or at least 500 if resource failure is documented before result inspection. A within-hospital-by-landmark label permutation preserves arm counts and stays; run 1,000 only if at least 20 valid two-arm strata exist, otherwise report unavailable/inconclusive.

## Falsification and prespecified gates

Run the same endpoint/parser on the pre-D placebo P versus B. Run the lead-time placebo with exposure time `D-720` while retaining the fixed D-relative outcome window. Remove each support and safety component one at a time, remove airway corroboration, and separately calculate peak pressure only as an ablation. Predict post-window documentation density as a negative control. Compare 6-hour and 12-hour horizons and 24/12/6-hour pre-L invasive-evidence sensitivities only when the frozen lineage provides those variants. Repeat the primary with 60-minute observation separation and first/last summaries as artifact-sensitivity diagnostics.

Call the result computationally supportive only if the verified lineage passes; endpoint support and hospital overlap pass; the positive risk difference has at least four of five estimable hospital directions; state-plus-label improves both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss by at least 0.01 over state-plus-opportunity with a favorable stay-cluster interval excluding zero; the increment exceeds pre-D/lead-time placebo and valid permutation null; no single component/source/site explains it; missingness/competing exits do not account for the value-based gain; and airway corroboration is present in at least 10% of rows in both arms. Even then, the claim is incremental information in this structured chart construct only.

Call the result adverse only when endpoint support is adequate and a bounded failure is reproduced: no label increment over the baseline, reversal across held-out hospitals, placebo/permutation agreement, dependence on a single component/source/site, or label-associated documentation density without value-based trajectory gain. Adverse means the labels are not validated as incremental features for this endpoint in this snapshot; it does not prove absent bedside evaluation or a documentation artifact.

Call it inconclusive when lineage cannot be verified, any source/schema/hash/window/parser audit fails, fewer than five hospitals meet the support gate, no held-out arm contrast exists, endpoint/missingness/competing exits are too sparse or nonpositive, airway corroboration is unavailable, bounded definitions disagree, or uncertainty spans meaningful gain and no gain. No relaxed threshold, outcome-guided label expansion, pooled row bootstrap, or post hoc endpoint is permitted.
