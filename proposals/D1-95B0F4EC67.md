# MIMIC AKI discordance: residual severity and observation-opportunity audit

Status: design-only repair child of [prior hypothesis]. No cohort scan, model fit, Harbor execution, or result was run.

## Question, evidence boundary and advance

Among adults with a first creatinine-defined AKI episode in the first eligible ICU stay, does a strictly lagged discordance between direct-urine recovery and positive recorded input/balance predict seven-day hospital death or ICU-recorded RRT, component by component, when the strongest rival—residual physiologic severity and differential observation opportunity—is explicitly audited?

The unresolved claim is a MIMIC-specific observed prognostic association, not a causal effect, true GFR, true volume status, complete RRT signal, or mechanism. The strongest supported premise is that kidney-protection practice combines hemodynamic/support and creatinine/urine monitoring and can be associated with renal outcomes in competing-risk analyses [K1], while recorded fluid accumulation has heterogeneous, low-certainty outcome evidence [K2]. This child adds a time-ordered severity/measurement audit to distinguish those explanations while preserving the parent’s lagged estimands. It is not a reproduction of any cited work.

The unchanged observed, noncausal estimands are:
RD_D(L,s)=F_D(7d | discordant,s)-F_D(7d | concordant,s)
and

where F is component-specific Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence in the same risk set and predeclared support/order stratum s.

## Matched temporal alternative

The fixed summary loses whether support preceded renal observation, whether discordance persisted across bins, and whether measurement density changed before the phenotype. The alternative is a small one-layer two-stream GRU, hidden width 32 or 64 with dropout fixed before fitting, separate renal and support/observation encoders, masks, shared context and separate cause-specific heads for the same hospital-death and ICU-recorded-RRT competing states after Ltime. It uses 3, 7 and 11 bins from t0 through Ltime-6h for L=24,48,72 and receives no washout, post-landmark or outcome data. Temporal convolution is a fallback only if a stable GRU is unavailable. It is a bounded adaptation, not a reproduction of Delphi [K3].

Use deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test, seed 20261002; all admissions for a subject stay in one partition. Fit scalers, cutpoints, vocabulary, class weights, stopping and penalties on training only; validation tunes and test is untouched. Preserve split manifest, counts, preprocessing statistics, parameter count, epoch and seed.

Interpret temporal outputs only if the full model has stable held-out component CIF calibration/Brier and, at L=48 with directional support at L=24 or 72, either: (a) renal-only/full retains the contrast in adequate non-support-first and low/medium-observation strata where support-only does not; (b) sequence order/persistence changes support-first versus renal-first calibration in the audited direction; or (c) sequence-versus-summary differences change a component-specific interpretation and are attributable to order/persistence. AUROC gain alone fails.

## Supportive, adverse and inconclusive criteria

Supportive requires a directionally consistent component contrast at L=48 with replication at another landmark, overlap across severity/observation strata, <50% attenuation after the audit in estimable strata, no dependence on the current-window sensitivity, and no support-first/high-surveillance/support-only dominance. Declare before fitting a clinically nontrivial threshold of a 2-percentage-point absolute seven-day CIF difference with uncertainty; this is a decision aid, not clinical benefit. Interpret separately for death and ICU-recorded RRT as observed prognostic associations.

Adverse is >=50% attenuation or reversal; severe imbalance/little overlap; a surveillance gradient as large as the renal contrast; a contrast confined to high observation or support-first strata; support-only matching full while renal-only adds no component-specific information; disappearance after contaminated urine exclusions; or dependence on current-window measurements. This favors severity/observation explanations but does not prove normal kidney function, benign fluid status or no treatment effect.

Inconclusive is failed dictionary/unit audit; burden unknown in most rows; sparse strata; fewer than 10 component events in key cells; intervals spanning clinically important effects; unstable held-out calibration; inconsistent death timing; or ICU-discharge selection that prevents meaningful RRT interpretation. An imprecise null is not refutation.
