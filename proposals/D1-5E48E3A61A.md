TITLE
A two-stage recorded-pathway study after second TACE: immediate systemic co-initiation and later third TACE as non-symmetric decisions

CLINICAL QUESTION, ADVANCE, AND CLAIM LADDER

After a second recorded chemoembolization for HCC, do patients with a systemic-agent record almost immediately after TACE2 have a different fixed-time hepatic laboratory trajectory from patients who remain untreated systemically through day 14 and later undergo a third TACE? This distinction matters because the former is plausibly a planned peri-TACE combination pathway, while the latter is a later repeat-procedure pathway after additional clinical evolution. Treating both as if they were alternative treatments chosen on the same day would be temporally false.

Strongest claim already supported by complete-source evidence: these are empirically different recorded pathways. In the new strict-ontology 2018–2024 audit conditional on institutional observability through day 14, immediate systemic initiation occurred at median day 2 (IQR 1–3), whereas later third TACE among day-14-at-risk patients occurred at median day 54.5 (IQR 43–68). The audit found 309 immediate-systemic/no-early-TACE3 pathways and 542 day-14-at-risk/later-TACE3-first pathways. This establishes temporal separation and computability only. It does not establish medication administration, intent, response, toxicity, survival, or benefit.

Unresolved hypothesis tested: among the same TACE2-eligible population and using outcomes anchored to the same TACE2 clock, the immediate-systemic recorded pathway is associated with less subsequent albumin decline without greater total-bilirubin increase than the later-third-TACE pathway. The primary contrast is prognostic/descriptive by observed 90-day pathway, not a causal contrast between symmetric decisions. A no-event benchmark is retained to expose selection. A nested stage-2 analysis asks a different question only among patients still free of either pathway at day 14: which baseline features and early laboratory course predict a recorded third TACE during days 15–90 rather than no recorded event through day 90? Late systemic initiation is too sparse for a credible active comparator and is reported, not pooled.

Substantive advance over both parents: this design preserves TACE2 as one time zero and the day-14 exposure window, but does not call all day-14 noninitiators “TACE-only.” It separates later third TACE, later systemic initiation, no recorded event, early third TACE, and ties. It also rejects the arm-specific-index comparison because systemic records cluster around day 2 while TACE3 occurs around day 55. The two stages answer non-symmetric questions and never interpret later TACE as a day-2 alternative.

PRIMARY COMMON-TIME-ZERO ESTIMAND

Population time zero is the TACE2 calendar day. At the day-14 observation landmark, classify the subsequently observed 90-day pathway:
A. immediate systemic co-initiation: first strict qualifying systemic-agent record on days 1–14 and no TACE3 before or on that record; exclude same-day TACE2 medication rows;
B. later third-TACE pathway: no systemic or TACE3 record through day 14, followed by strict TACE3 first on days 15–90 before any systemic record;
C. observed no-event benchmark: no qualifying systemic or TACE3 record through day 90 and institutional timestamp observability through day 90;
D. separately report late-systemic-first on days 15–90, early TACE3 on days 1–14, same-day ties, ambiguous systemic-procedure-only records, and patients not observable through the required horizon.

The primary estimand is the adjusted difference A minus B in mean standardized within-exact-assay change from the closest valid value on TACE2 days -30 through -1 to the closest valid value to day 60 within days 30–90, separately for exact assay names Albumin and Total Bilirubin. Standardize each change by the pooled baseline-assay standard deviation within retained calendar-year blocks; retain raw values and ranges. Group C is a benchmark, not a third treatment arm. Because B is defined by an event that occurs after day 14 and its outcome lab can occur before or after TACE3, this estimand describes the fixed-time trajectory associated with the eventual recorded pathway. It is not an effect of administering systemic therapy instead of TACE3.

Before fitting the primary model, report for every selected follow-up lab whether it preceded, occurred on, or followed the pathway event. If more than 30% of B measurements precede TACE3, retain the common-clock prognostic estimand but prohibit mechanistic wording about post-TACE3 injury. A secondary event-anchored within-B description uses the closest albumin and bilirubin values in TACE3 days -30 to -1 and +30 to +120; it has no systemic comparator and is not combined with the primary estimand.

STAGE-2 NESTED/SEQUENTIAL ANALYSIS

At day 14, form the risk set with neither systemic treatment nor TACE3 during days 1–14. Estimate recorded cumulative first-event proportions through day 90 for later TACE3, later systemic initiation, and no observed event; this is not a survival cumulative incidence because death and complete outside follow-up are unavailable. The prognostic model compares B with C using only information through day 14: age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, era/month, department, prior utilization/procedures, diagnosis proxies, pre-TACE2 labs, and acute TACE2-to-day14 laboratory changes. Report discrimination and calibration only as prognosis of recorded pathway. Do not compare B causally with A in this model. The 34 late-systemic-first patients (23 with institutional timestamps through day 90) are described as a sparse competing path and trigger a negative feasibility result for any later systemic-versus-TACE3 contrast.

OUTCOMES, OBSERVATION, AND ANALYSIS

Primary outcomes are separate continuous changes in exact standard assays Albumin and Total Bilirubin. Parse Quantitative Result numerically and use Test Time. Do not pool acute assay names, calculate ALBI, apply clinical cutoffs, or assert units: the laboratory table has no unit column. Report assay value ranges by year, arm, and specimen type; impossible discontinuities or year-specific scale shifts trigger laboratory adjudication and suspension of comparative interpretation.

Report unadjusted distributions, missingness, follow-up-lab timing relative to TACE2 and pathway event, and group-specific paired-outcome fractions. Fit a propensity model for A versus B only as a prognostic case-mix adjustment using variables available by day 14 for both groups; use overlap weights and doubly robust linear regression with robust intervals. Do not include post-day14 response, labs, imaging, or pathway events as confounders. Show effective sample size, propensity overlap, year-stratified estimates, complete-case estimates, and inverse-probability-of-laboratory-observation estimates. Because B is future-classified, weighting cannot create exchangeability or causal identification.

Covariates: age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, TACE2 year/month, department, encounter and procedure counts in prior 90/180 days, prior resection/ablation/transplant procedure labels, encounter-linked diagnosis proxies before TACE2 for cirrhosis, portal hypertension, ascites, encephalopathy, variceal bleeding, hepatic insufficiency, portal-vein tumor thrombus and extrahepatic metastasis, plus closest pre-TACE2 exact albumin, total bilirubin, INR, platelets, AFP, creatinine and sodium. Any tumor size/number, viable enhancement, portal-vein invasion, spread, response/progression, ascites, or intent claim from examinations requires locked clinician abstraction of CT/MRI reports; automated lexical flags are retrieval aids only.

Crossover is part of the pathway, not censoring. For each initial group report systemic and TACE events by days 30, 60, 90, 120, 180 and 365 and combinations thereof. Do not call crossover treatment failure, discontinuation, rescue, response, or progression.

EMPIRICAL COMPLETE-SOURCE AUDIT

No row sampling. Job [research job] scanned 105,044 encounters, all procedure records with 322,166 valid procedure timestamps, 344,383 HCC-containing diagnosis rows, 33,467 medication rows matching the broad drug lexicon (32,229 after placebo/bevacizumab partner handling), and all 28,159,928 laboratory rows. It reconstructed 38,742 strict patient-day TACE episodes, 6,589 earliest adjacent pairs, 6,586 HCC/adult pairs, 4,671 without a partner-qualified prior systemic record, and 1,925 with day-14 observability in 2018–2024.

Within those 1,925, the sequential audit found 309 immediate-systemic/no-early-TACE3, 542 day-14-at-risk/later-TACE3-first, 34 day-14-at-risk/later-systemic-first, 976 day-14-at-risk/no recorded event by day 90, and 64 early-event/tie/other paths. Timestamp observability through day 90 was 251/309, 408/542, 23/34, 897/976, and 50/64, respectively. Exact baseline plus days30–90 albumin and bilirubin pairs were available jointly in 97/309 immediate systemic, 283/542 later TACE3, 17/34 later systemic, and 115/976 no-event patients. Baseline plus days60–120 pairs were 80/309, 198/542, 11/34, and 160/976. Within the later-TACE3 pathway, 163/542 had baseline plus paired exact assays in TACE3+30 to +120; that count uses the TACE2 baseline in the feasibility audit and the executable event-anchored secondary must recompute a pre-TACE3 baseline.

Crossover was substantial. Among 310 immediate-systemic-first patients in the closely related first-event classification, later TACE was recorded in 14 by day30, 94 by day60, 152 by day90, 179 by day120, 220 by day180, and 247 by day365. Among 542 later-TACE3-first patients, systemic treatment was recorded in 0, 46, 72, 84, 109, and 166 by the same horizons. Among 1,605 people with no systemic record by day14, systemic/TACE3/both by day90 were 119/602/92 and by day365 were 396/1,183/346. These are recorded transitions, not clinical outcomes.

The immediate arm has 97 rather than 100 paired outcomes under the newly stricter sequential exclusions; therefore the primary is marginally feasible, with a prespecified minimum of 80 paired outcomes and overlap-weighted ESS >=80. Results must emphasize uncertainty. The discrepancy from parent arm counts (317 and 1,492) reflects stricter pathway partitioning and different prior/bevacizumab handling and must be reconciled in a flow table rather than hidden.

SUPPORTIVE, ADVERSE, PRECISE-NULL, FALSIFIED, AND INCONCLUSIVE BRANCHES

Supportive: after strict ontology, era, overlap, observation, and negative-control gates, A versus B shows at least 0.20 pooled baseline SD less albumin decline, its 95% CI excludes zero, and the upper 95% CI for excess bilirubin increase is <=0.10 SD; direction is compatible across complete-case, observation-weighted, year-blocked, broad-TACE, and bevacizumab sensitivities. This supports only that the immediate-systemic recorded pathway marks a more favorable fixed-time hepatic laboratory trajectory.

Adverse: A shows at least 0.20 SD greater albumin decline or bilirubin increase with a 95% CI excluding zero, consistently across gates. This is evidence against assuming a liver-preserving recorded-pathway association. It does not establish systemic-drug toxicity or harm because administration and confounding by disease state are unresolved.

Precise null: for both albumin and bilirubin, the complete 95% intervals lie within -0.10 to +0.10 baseline SD and observation/ontology sensitivities agree. Conclude that no prognostically meaningful difference at the prespecified scale was detected between these recorded paths in this source. Do not infer therapeutic equivalence.

Falsified: suspend the pathway contrast if the temporal reconstruction fails to retain clear separation; the 67-person eligibility discrepancy cannot be explained; strict versus broad TACE or drug ontology reverses direction; a pre-TACE2 negative-control laboratory trajectory differs by >=0.20 SD after weighting; impossible lab scales are detected; future information enters covariates; or selected follow-up lab timing is inadvertently arm-indexed rather than TACE2-indexed. These outcomes falsify the proposed operationalization, not the biological efficacy of either therapy.

Inconclusive: paired outcomes <80 in A or B; overlap-weighted ESS <80; no propensity overlap; extreme observation weights; complete-case and observation-weighted directions disagree; material year heterogeneity; more than 30% of B outcomes precede TACE3 when a post-TACE3 mechanism is asserted; event ascertainment depends materially on the day-90 observation gate; or clinician imaging/intent adjudication is needed but unavailable. Report feasibility/pathway counts and stop. The stage-2 late-systemic contrast is already negative-feasibility (34 total, 17 paired outcomes) and cannot be promoted.

EVIDENCE LIMITS

Computationally checkable: cohort reconstruction; dates; ontologies; path assignment; observation timestamps; assay pairing; lab changes; missingness; crossover; overlap; model estimates; uncertainty; negative controls; and whether conclusions obey the branches.

Not computationally established: medication administration/adherence; same-day or multi-agent regimen delivery; HCC treatment intent; radiographic response/progression; TACE refractoriness; tumor stage, performance status or Child-Pugh; true hepatic toxicity; symptomatic decompensation; mortality or survival; outside treatment; causal superiority; or a patient-level recommendation. Diagnosis rows lack native timestamps and may only provide inherited encounter coding proxies. Stronger claims require medication-administration data, unit validation, clinician review of imaging/intent/toxicity, vital-status and out-of-system linkage, and a prospective or multi-institutional study.
