# Provisional successor: donor-aware intervention-by-macrophage-context interaction in E-MTAB-8316

Seed: D2-T1. Status: provisional and evidence-bound; no inferential result has been computed.

## Proposed relationship

In the rheumatoid-arthritis FLS/monocyte coculture experiment represented by E-MTAB-8316, the intervention-associated FLS transcript response is hypothesized to depend on macrophage context (the monocyte/macrophage condition or phenotype), rather than being a context-independent FLS response. Operationally, the intervention-by-context contrast should be reproducible after accounting for donor pairing and repeated condition measurements.

This is an intervention-associated expression hypothesis. It is not a claim of direct target action, causal efficacy, or clinical benefit.

## Clinical importance and advance

A context-independent response would support a simpler target-prioritization rule. A reproducible interaction would instead indicate that the same intervention has different transcriptomic consequences in different inflammatory microenvironments, motivating microenvironment-specific evaluation and measurement of macrophage state before candidate testing. The substantive advance over a drug-response table is an explicit donor-aware test of whether the intervention contrast changes with macrophage context, rather than merely ranking mean expression changes.

## Evidence-supported versus untested claims

The catalog/assay registry supports that the configured E-MTAB-8316 assay contains 12 condition samples, 58,302 features, four verified FLS donor clusters (SA049, SA051, SA104, SA114), six nested monocyte donors, and repeated context measurements. It also warns that cell-type labels and the experimental readout must be reconciled with the protocol before cell-specific claims. The registry does not establish an interaction, direct drug-target action, clinical efficacy, or mechanism. The unresolved claim is whether intervention-by-macrophage-context interaction is reproducible after donor-aware adjustment.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Use all 12 condition samples from the verified E-MTAB-8316 assay, restricted to the four FLS donor clusters SA049, SA051, SA104 and SA114 and their documented nested monocyte donors. Average repeated measurements within each donor/condition before exploratory inference; do not treat the 12 samples as 12 independent donors. The temporal boundary is the experiment's recorded intervention/context exposure; if the design does not provide a time field, no time-course or causal temporal claim is permitted.

## Computable comparison

Baseline: a donor-aware additive model for each gene's response, with separate intervention and macrophage-context terms and no interaction. Include donor fixed effects or use donor-paired contrasts, and account for the nested monocyte-donor structure. The model is fitted to the verified expression values and design columns.

Alternative: the same donor-aware additive model plus the prespecified intervention-by-context interaction. The interaction is the scientific estimand: whether the intervention contrast differs by macrophage context. Compare models using a prespecified gene-level interaction estimate and uncertainty, with donor-aware resampling or a held-out donor split where the design permits. Report effect size, uncertainty, donor-level stability, and the number of independent donor clusters supporting each contrast. Do not use a small predictive gain as the primary criterion.

A simple descriptive paired contrast is an additional baseline: average each donor/condition, calculate intervention contrasts within context, and compare those contrasts across contexts. This preserves the donor-level information and makes the interaction estimand transparent. The interaction model is selected as the substantive alternative because it tests the scientific uncertainty directly; a more complex neural or high-dimensional model is deferred because it would add flexibility without resolving the small independent-donor problem.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive result: a prespecified interaction estimate is nonzero with uncertainty excluding the null, is stable across the independent donor clusters, and is not explained by a documented batch/technical field. This supports context-dependent intervention-associated expression only.

Adverse result: a precise, reproducible null or consistent reversal across donors rejects the proposed context dependence for the tested genes/contrast. It does not prove no biology or no clinical effect.

Inconclusive result: wide uncertainty, too few independent donor clusters, missing/ambiguous donor or context fields, failed matrix-design join, or protocol/readout ambiguity. An imprecise null is not decisive refutation.

## Missing evidence and stronger claims

The exact current design columns, join key, and assay-registry contrast labels still require execution-time inspection; no computed result is claimed here. The dataset lacks verified clinical outcomes, direct target measurements, independent technical replicates sufficient for causal inference, and a validated mechanism. Cell-specific interpretation requires protocol reconciliation. Direct target action, clinical efficacy, and mechanism require additional perturbational, target-specific, and clinical studies.

## Alternatives not chosen

A purely predictive high-dimensional model was not selected because the central uncertainty is an interaction estimand with only four FLS donor clusters, not prediction accuracy. A mechanistic model is deferred because the available data do not identify direct target action. Revisit either alternative only if independent donors, validated context labels, and a prespecified mechanistic perturbation become available.
