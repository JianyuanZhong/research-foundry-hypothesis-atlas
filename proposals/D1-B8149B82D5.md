## Why the mismatch is material

The two rules target different populations.

- The Episode-77 rule asks about people whose first recorded ICU stay is itself at least 48 hours long and has a unique accepted invasive-ventilation record crossing hour 48.
- The frozen rule asks about each person's first recorded ICU stay that creates that broad hour-48 ventilated opportunity. An earlier short, nonventilated, or non-unique-V0 stay is not an opportunity and does not block a later broad opportunity. Once the earliest broad opportunity is selected, however, failed admission, age, era, or clinical phenotype gates exclude the subject and never reopen a later opportunity.

A treatment- and outcome-blind audit against the frozen MIMIC-IV 3.1 archive applied the current unique-V0 grammar to both rules. The leader rule reproduced 5,752 records. The frozen rule identified 7,682 subjects with any broad opportunity and retained 7,612 after the admission/age checks; 5,752 overlapped, none were leader-only, and 1,860 were frozen-rule-only. Thus the intended broad population is 1,860 records larger, 32.34% of the leader denominator. There were 721 subjects with multiple broad opportunities, which makes explicit ordering and no-reopening rules necessary.

The population choice also changes real-era composition. Leader counts versus repaired broad counts were 1,624 versus 2,395 (2008-2010), 1,234 versus 1,615 (2011-2013), 1,302 versus 1,669 (2014-2016), 1,096 versus 1,373 (2017-2019), and 496 versus 560 (2020-2022). The earliest-era proportion changes from 28.23% to 31.46%, and the latest-era proportion from 8.62% to 7.36%. Therefore Episode 77's era mixture, standardization weights, era-specific support, early/late stability, models, bootstrap rebuilds, and conclusion gates must all be recomputed. These counts establish population impact and computability only. No phenotype eligibility, arm assignment, H120 state, harm, or effect was inspected.

The historical Episode-22 audit reported 7,733 pre-admission and 7,663 post-admission landmarks under its then-current crossing grammar. Those historical counts demonstrate the intended precedence but are not substituted for the new audit, which applies the current unique-V0 rule.

## Highest-precedence population contract

The following language replaces Episode 77's sentence “Select each subject's earliest ICU stay by `(intime,stay_id)` before phenotype gates” and controls every cohort audit, model, bootstrap, sensitivity, and verifier fixture:

> 1. **Participant split first.** From `hosp/patients.subject_id`, encode each nonmissing identifier as canonical unsigned base-10 ASCII and compute SHA-256(`ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1` + NUL + `mimic` + NUL + canonical `subject_id`) modulo 100. Retain buckets 0-79. Do not inspect any encounter, event, note, or outcome row for subjects in buckets 80-99. All encounters and modalities follow the participant.
>
> 2. **Enumerate broad opportunities before selecting a stay.** For every ICU stay of a retained subject, use `icu/icustays.subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id,intime,outtime`. Require nonmissing parseable three-key identity and `intime,outtime`; define `t0=intime+48h`; and require the half-open hour-48 condition `intime<=t0<outtime`.
>
> 3. **Unique V0 is part of “broad.”** Join `icu/procedureevents` on all of `subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id`. An accepted crossing row has `itemid=225792`, `statusdescription` exactly `FinishedRunning` or `Stopped`, parseable `starttime<endtime`, and `starttime<=t0<endtime`. Exactly one accepted source row must cross t0; zero or more than one is not a broad opportunity. Distinct rows are not semantically merged to manufacture uniqueness. `storetime` remains a completion-lag diagnostic only and cannot determine broad-opportunity status, eligibility, treatment, weights, or outcomes.
>
> 4. **Select the earliest broad opportunity.** Restrict to stays satisfying steps 2-3, order them by `(icustays.intime,icustays.stay_id)`, and select the first. An earlier ICU stay that is short, malformed, not invasively ventilated at hour 48, or has zero/multiple accepted V0 crossings is not a broad hour-48 opportunity and does not block this selection.
>
> 5. **Apply admission validity and age only to the selected broad opportunity.** Join `hosp/admissions` on `subject_id,hadm_id`; require one matched row with parseable `admittime,dischtime` and `admittime<=t0<dischtime`. Join `hosp/patients` on `subject_id`; require `anchor_age + year(icustays.intime) - anchor_year >=18`. Missing/malformed values or failure of either gate exclude the subject. They never reopen a later broad opportunity.
>
> 6. **Apply all remaining inherited gates to that one opportunity.** Require Episode 77's nonmissing accepted `anchor_year_group` before arm construction, then apply the inherited pressor, MAP, weight, urine, potassium, +50 mL/kg balance, RRT/ECMO, loop-washout, ambiguity, and measurement gates. Any failure excludes the subject without searching another stay.
>
> 7. **Audit the selection.** Emit every candidate broad opportunity and its rule-specific flags; the selected `subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id,t0,V0`; counts of zero, one, and multiple broad opportunities; reasons earlier ICU stays were not broad; postselection admission/age/era attrition; and a forbidden counterfactual count of subjects who would enter only if a later broad opportunity were allowed after the selected broad opportunity failed a downstream gate. The participant split and all attrition must be treatment- and outcome-blind.

A malformed ICU stay cannot itself be broad because valid keys and boundaries are constitutive of the broad definition. A malformed or short earlier ICU stay therefore does not veto a later first broad opportunity. In contrast, once a valid broad opportunity is selected, invalid admission linkage, age failure, missing/unseen era, or any clinical phenotype failure excludes without later reopening.
