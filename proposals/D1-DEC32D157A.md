# Episode 84 successor: measurement-status-conditional calibration transport for a fixed-capacity numeric repeat-lactate queue

## Clinical question, hypothesis, and substantive advance

At the fixed 24-hour landmark after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry, the Episode 82 parent asks whether a numeric repeat-lactate ranking rule trained only in low-repeat-availability hospitals transfers to high-availability hospitals, and vice versa, after the repeat-observation process is represented. That question is clinically consequential because a fixed number of patients, rather than everyone above an absolute risk threshold, may be eligible for review.

The unresolved interpretation problem is that fixed-capacity ranking and absolute-risk calibration are different properties. A monotone calibration error does not change a within-hospital top-k queue, while good average calibration does not imply that adding the numeric repeat value improves the queue. Episode 82 makes target calibration a gate and also calls calibration failure noncomputable. That can conceal a useful distinction: a rule may transport for fixed-slot prioritization but require local recalibration before probabilities are shown or probability thresholds are used.

This successor makes one substantive advance over Episode 83: it replaces the population-average calibration screen with a joint measurement-status-conditional screen. A positive calibration error among F_available=1 patients and a negative error among F_available=0 patients can cancel in the pooled mean, so the pooled q_pop is retained only as a descriptive diagnostic. The primary calibration estimand is the maximum absolute mean-risk error across both F_available strata, both directed transfers, and both frozen models. This preserves the Episode 82/83 population, sources, clocks, variables, directed source-to-target fits, capacity grid, queue estimand, uncertainty, deletion analysis, and noncausal limits. It never recalibrates on target outcomes and keeps ranking and calibration as separate conclusions.

Primary queue hypothesis H-queue: for both directed transfers a→b in {low→high, high→low} and every c in C={0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20}, adding the numeric repeat value to R_process increases source-defined seven-day death capture by at least 0.03 deaths per occupied slot. Let Delta7(a→b,c) be that equal-capacity contrast and tau7=min over directions and capacities Delta7(a→b,c). Support requires the two-sided 95% lower bound for tau7 to be at least 0.03, equivalent to at least 3 additional D7 deaths per 100 occupied slots at every capacity in both directions, with the inherited eventual-death and deletion guards.

Calibration hypothesis H-cal-cond: for every directed transfer d∈{low→high, high→low}, model m∈{R_process,R_full}, and measurement-status stratum z∈{0,1}, the frozen source-trained probability has target mean-risk calibration error no greater than δ=0.05:
e(d,m,z)=mean_{i∈T_{d,z}}(D7_i−p_{d,m,i}), where T_{d,z} is the target people with frozen F_available=z and p=expit(η) from the unmodified frozen source-trained raw linear predictor. The primary joint estimand is q_cond=max_{d,m,z}|e(d,m,z)| across all eight cells; no status-weighted average is used. The status contrast κ(d,m)=e(d,m,1)−e(d,m,0), the eight cell means, and the population-average e_pop(d,m) are descriptive only. Thus opposite errors in F_available=1 and F_available=0 cannot cancel into support for H-cal-cond. The 0.05 margin is an inherited operational screen, not a validated safety or utility threshold.

Support for H-cal-cond requires the upper endpoint of the two-sided joint 95% percentile interval for the replicate-level q_cond to be ≤0.05. Adverse evidence requires its lower endpoint to be >0.05; this means at least one measurement-status/direction/model cell fails the margin, without claiming that the point-estimate argmax alone identifies the failing cell. Every other result is calibration-inconclusive. Target outcomes are used only after scoring to evaluate these errors and diagnostics; they never change preprocessing, coefficients, scores, status labels, strata, capacities, or queues. Population-average calibration support cannot substitute for H-cal-cond, and conditional calibration support cannot rescue a failed ranking hypothesis.

The strongest combined result is support for H-queue plus H-cal-cond. Support for H-queue with adverse or inconclusive H-cal-cond means only that the ordering transported under fixed quotas; it explicitly requires local labeled calibration work before displaying absolute risks or using a probability threshold. H-cal-cond without H-queue cannot rescue a failed numeric-repeat queue claim.

## Fixed-capacity queue estimands and algebra

For direction d=a→b, let K_b(c)=sum over target hospitals k_h(c), and let S_m,h^o(c) be the number of outcome-o events in Q_m,h(c), for o in {7,inf}. Define

Delta7(d,c) = [sum_h S_full,h^7(c)-sum_h S_process,h^7(c)]/K_b(c)

and tau7(d)=min_c Delta7(d,c), tau7=min_d tau7(d). Define DeltaInf(d,c), tauInf(d), and tauInf identically using Dinf but the same D7-fitted scores and queues. No Dinf model or Dinf-optimized queue is permitted.

## Ordered result states and falsification

First classify H-cal-cond independently:

- Calibration supportive: the original-data cell gate passes; all eight conditional diagnostics are estimable and converged; at least 1,900 joint q_cond replicates are finite; and upper95(q_cond)≤0.05. This supports the operational mean-risk screen simultaneously in every direction, model, and F_available stratum.
- Calibration adverse: the original-data cell gate passes; all eight diagnostics are estimable and converged; at least 1,900 q_cond replicates are finite; and lower95(q_cond)>0.05. This falsifies the conditional screen because at least one of the eight cells is beyond the margin; report the cellwise estimates and intervals without treating a point-estimate argmax as a multiplicity-adjusted cell claim.
- Calibration inconclusive: every other pattern, including the q_cond interval crossing 0.05, a failed cell gate, a nonestimable/separated/nonconvergent cell regression, or fewer than 1,900 finite q_cond replicates. A supportive pooled q_pop with conditional inconclusiveness is not conditional calibration support.

Then apply the first matching overall state:

1. Not computable: source/hash/header/schema/key mismatch; exact inherited flow or outcome counts fail; strata, clocks, variables, revision/conflict/tie rules, folds, model, or capacity grid change; a required directed R_process or R_full fit is nonfinite/nonconvergent; post-L24 or target-outcome leakage occurs; a queue has wrong cardinality; copied-hospital grouping is wrong; a required queue functional has <1,900 finite bootstrap replicates; queue algebra fails; or a required queue artifact is missing. A conditional calibration gate failure alone is not state 1 when queue computation is valid.
2. Adverse opposite direction: any directed capacity has upper95[Delta7(d,c)]<0.
3. Adverse asymmetric transfer: one direction has upper95[tau7(d)]<0.03 while the reverse direction has lower95[tau7(d)]>=0.03. This falsifies symmetric queue portability without identifying why.
4. Adverse eventual-death tradeoff: lower95(tau7)>=0.03 but upper95(tauInf)<0.
5. Adverse below materiality: both directions have upper95[tau7(d)]<0.03.
6. Supportive queue and measurement-status-conditional calibration transport: lower95(tau7)>=0.03, lower95(tauInf)>=0, no directed capacity has upper95(Delta7)<0, deletion proportions pass in both directions, and H-cal-cond is supportive.
7. Supportive fixed-slot ranking transport but adverse conditional calibration: all H-queue support conditions in state 6 pass and H-cal-cond is adverse. The fixed-capacity ordering claim is supported, but source-trained absolute probabilities fail the joint 0.05 mean-risk screen in at least one measurement-status cell; local labeled recalibration/validation is required before probability display or thresholding.
8. Supportive fixed-slot ranking transport with unresolved conditional calibration: all H-queue support conditions pass and H-cal-cond is inconclusive. The queue result is still interpretable as an ordering result only; it does not support absolute-risk transport.
9. Inconclusive queue transport: every other computable pattern, including intervals crossing zero or 0.03, deletion instability without a contradictory interval, or favorable mixed-stratum sensitivity with failed directed transfer.

The queue hypothesis is falsified by states 2–5, supported by states 6–8, and unresolved by state 9. H-cal-cond is falsified only by its adverse calibration classification, supported only in state 6, and unresolved when inconclusive. State 7 or 8 can occur even when q_pop appears small; that is an intended result demonstrating why pooled calibration cannot answer the status-conditional question. Adverse queue evidence does not mean lactate has no prognostic value. Conditional calibration support does not imply useful ranking. Inconclusive is not equivalence. No state implies that measuring repeat lactate, displaying a probability, reviewing a patient, or treating a patient improves survival.
