## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Multimorbidity risk models and staging tools (e.g. CKM staging, comorbidity indices) represent disease burden as a **set** or **count**. Clinicians instead reason about **sequence** — diabetes-then-hypertension vs hypertension-then-diabetes. It is unresolved whether the **order** of first inpatient diagnosis of two co-present cardiometabolic diseases carries prognostic information about downstream ICD-10 accrual beyond (a) mere co-presence, (b) age at onset, (c) the inter-diagnosis interval, and (d) same-encounter ascertainment/care-coupling. Resolving this decides whether sequence belongs in multimorbidity stratification or whether the set/count view is adequate — a useful negative finding either way.

## Leading claim vs strongest rivals, and the discriminating observation
- **Leading claim (A):** among people with a fixed co-present cardiometabolic pair, the order of first appearance adds information about downstream accrual (Δ_order ≠ 0) beyond the set/count baseline.
- **Rival B1 — ascertainment / care-coupling (strongest):** apparent "order" reflects which condition was first *tested/coded* when both are jointly evaluated, not a biological sequence. Inpatient ICD-10 under-ascertainment and same-encounter co-coding can manufacture order. [K3]
- **Rival B2 — shared multiorgan continuum:** order is a projection of one graded cardiometabolic process, so the useful signal is stage/number of systems, not pairwise order. [K2]
- **Rival B3 — network/hub structure:** temporal co-occurrence is better explained by a few pivotal diagnoses with high centrality than by pairwise ordering per se. [K1]

**Discriminating observation (new in this child):** add a **screening-coupling control contrast**. Compare the order-feature gain Δ_order on the *biologically distinct* target pair E11 (T2D) / I10 (essential hypertension) against a *mechanically coupled* control pair E11 (T2D) / E78 (lipid disorders), whose codes are frequently raised in the same encounter/lipid panel. Prediction under A: the biological pair shows an order gain that is not smaller than the coupled control. Prediction under B1: the coupled control shows an equal-or-larger "order" signal (or a degenerate tie structure) driven by co-testing, exposing the ascertainment channel. A pre-registered tie-exclusion category (same-day, day-resolution dates) is retained from the parent.

## What answering it would change
- If Δ_order survives the coupling-control contrast and the permutation null, pairwise sequence is a candidate stratifier and justifies a prospective/incident-cohort replication where true onset timing is observable.
- If the coupling control reproduces or exceeds the effect, the set/count (or continuum-stage) representation is adequate and sequence should not be promoted — a decisive negative that redirects biomarker/prognosis programmes away from sequence features.

## Estimand, population, baseline and analysis (frozen design)
- **Population:** adults in the frozen clinical table with both members of a pre-registered pair present as first-occurrence ICD-10 blocks, with both diagnosis dates available and discordant (non-same-day) — else the tie-exclusion category.
- **Exposure:** order indicator of the pair (which condition first), plus inter-diagnosis interval.
- **Primary estimand:** Δ_order = out-of-sample log-loss (or AUC) gain of a model containing the order feature over an otherwise identical **set/count baseline**, for the target pair E11/I10, evaluated on a held-out split; compared against the coupling control pair E11/E78.
- **Baselines:** (i) set/count-only model with age+sex; (ii) same + inter-diagnosis interval and pre-index diagnosis count. **Substantive learned alternative:** gradient-boosted/elastic-net model over the ordered pair + covariates, with permutation-based feature importance — reveals whether order carries nonlinear/interacting information the count baseline loses.
- **Null:** within-stratum label permutation (age-decile × sex × pre-index count), 1,000 draws, preserving the co-present set.
- **Adjustment:** age, sex, inter-diagnosis interval, pre-index diagnosis count (the parent's B1-motivated adjustment).
- **Pre-specified outcome bands:** supportive = Δ_order target-pair CI excludes 0 AND is ≥ coupling-control Δ_order; adverse = coupling control Δ_order ≥ target Δ_order (ascertainment channel dominates); inconclusive = CIs overlap/too wide.

## Falsification criteria
Δ_order collapses to ~0 under adjustment and the permutation null, OR the coupling-control pair reproduces an equal-or-larger order signal → claim A is not supported on this table.

## What supportive / adverse / inconclusive mean, and required stronger evidence
- **Supportive:** sequence plausibly adds information among co-present pairs on this cross-sectional inpatient-ascertained data → justify a prospective/incident cohort with true onset timing.
- **Adverse:** sequence is largely an ascertainment/care-coupling artifact → do not promote order features; set/count or continuum-stage representation suffices.
- **Inconclusive:** underpowered after tie exclusion → report effective discordant n and defer.
Stronger conclusions (causal sequence, biological mechanism, sequence-as-stratifier in care) require: true incident-onset timing (not first in-patient code), death/censoring dates, a care-intensity proxy, and clinical adjudication of the 3-char code blocks — none available in the configured data. These are explicitly deferred, not claimed.
