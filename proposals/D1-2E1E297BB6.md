# Successor B: audited result for the locked 12–24-hour lactate landmark, with an explicit covariate-unit repair

## Decision and result

This successor preserves the parent's population, t0/person selection, 12–24-hour landmark, lactate exposure, hospital-mortality outcome, overlap-weighted risk-difference estimand, +10 percentage-point margin, outcome-blind site gate, uncertainty procedure, and falsification rules.

The complete EICU audit reproduced the parent's outcome-blind feasibility exactly: 1,468 measured people (957 X=0, 511 X=1), then 33 exposure-balanced hospitals and 1,198 people (764/434). After that gate, deaths were 244/764 (31.9%) in X=0 and 250/434 (57.6%) in X=1.

The pre-specified overlap-weighted association was risk 57.03% versus 29.54%, RD +27.49 percentage points. The 5,000 copied-hospital-then-copied-person bootstrap was 100% finite, with a 95% RD interval of +20.39 to +34.43 points; all 33 leave-one-hospital-out refits were finite and positive. Balance and concentration were strong (maximum model-column absolute SMD 0.0126; ESS 616.8/397.5; maximum person weight share 0.20%; maximum hospital share 9.46%).

The locked classification is INCONCLUSIVE, not supportive: the propensity-range gate failed narrowly because the minimum fitted propensity was 0.01980, below the required 0.02. The locked rules make any failed population, event, balance, convergence, or site-deletion gate inconclusive, even when the exploratory interval exceeds +10 points. The result therefore does not establish the clinical +10-point claim.

## What is known versus tested

The audit supports only a computable prognostic association in the measured, exposure-balanced EICU cohort. It does not resolve whether late nonclearance is a causal treatment target, proves tissue hypoperfusion, proves administered norepinephrine or dose, or shows that triggering a 24-hour review improves care.

The unresolved claim remains: among adults with an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed row, lactate at least 2 mmol/L, and survival to the 24-hour reassessment landmark, does less than 20% clearance during hours 12–24 identify at least a 10-point higher subsequent hospital-mortality risk than at least 20% clearance?

## Substantive repair returned for Lead approval

The parent says adjustment laboratories must be audited by name and unit but does not specify the accepted unit strings or whether unit conversion is permitted. That is a genuinely noncomputable reproducibility ambiguity, distinct from the already observed propensity gate failure. This successor does not silently treat the name-only implementation as definitive.

The child binds the adjustment analytes as exact case-insensitive name/unit pairs: creatinine/mg/dL, BUN/mg/dL, total bilirubin/mg/dL, AST (SGOT)/U/L, and ALT (SGPT)/U/L; no conversions, interface-unit substitutions, direct bilirubin, or urinary creatinine. It retains the parent's revision, collection, conflict, latest-value, missingness, and t0+720 deadline rules. The child must rerun the inferential model after this unit audit. The current result is therefore an aggregate audit result and an exploratory association, not a final claim under the repaired covariate definition.

A second operational detail is made explicit rather than hidden: for vitalPeriodic's five-minute summaries, the MAP-low fraction is the proportion of adjacent pairs of valid MAP observations for which both endpoints are below 65 mmHg. It is not a duration-weighted raw-waveform quantity, because the catalog says raw waveforms are unavailable. This definition must remain fixed before rerun.

## Interpretive gates

Supportive would require every locked gate to pass, a bootstrap lower bound >=+10 points, and at least 80% positive leave-site-out RDs. Adverse would require every gate to pass but an interval upper bound <+10; opposite would require every gate to pass and an interval upper bound <0. This audit is inconclusive because the propensity gate fails. A future repaired rerun that passes all gates and retains the interval would support only prognostic association; a repaired rerun with an upper bound below +10 would be adverse to the clinically relevant margin; an upper bound below zero would falsify the direction. A further failure remains inconclusive.

No EICU computation can adjudicate true norepinephrine administration or dose, specimen type/indication, fluids, beta-agonists, liver function, source control, tissue perfusion, treatment limitations, exact death time/cause, or decision utility. Causal treatment claims require randomized or credible target-trial data; actionability requires prospective decision-impact evaluation.
