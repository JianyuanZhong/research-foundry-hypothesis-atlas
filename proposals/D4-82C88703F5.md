# UKB metabolic progression: source-canonical change and confirmed second-domain transition

## Successor purpose and scientific deliverable

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s conditional two-panel question, exact UKB bindings, participant-level split, route/death comparison, simultaneous-event handling, tie safeguards, `C_obs` boundary audit, selection diagnostics, and evidence limits. It makes one substantive scientific repair: the parent’s principal outcome is a first administrative code, which cannot distinguish a robust new cardiometabolic condition from an isolated coding/ascertainment event. The child requires the solver to report both the earliest recorded transition and a prespecified confirmed-transition estimand.

The future solver must newly fit and freeze:

1. a five-source header, SHA-256, duplicate-`eid`, overlap, and overlapping-field-agreement audit;
2. a deterministic source-canonical two-panel exposure file, including the main/biological-samples assay concordance audit;
3. a participant flow separating assessment return, stable one-domain eligibility, assay completeness, and source-concordant completeness;
4. the exact paired ICD code/date phenotype, earliest-event/tie file, `C_obs` and administrative-closure audit;
5. matched current-level and change-augmented route-specific competing-risk fits for both recorded and confirmed endpoints;
6. locked predictions, route/death cumulative incidence, calibration, Brier, discrimination, restricted mean event-free survival, absolute-risk alert summaries, and 200-resample participant-bootstrap uncertainty;
7. return/completeness weighting, source-concordance, confirmation-window, lag, timing and negative-control outputs; and
8. a conclusion linked to those outputs and classified as supportive, adverse, or inconclusive.

Readiness, source agreement, model convergence, a nonzero coefficient, or improvement in one metric is not completion. No full-cohort fit or result is claimed in this proposal.

## Unresolved question, evidence, and hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports associations between baseline metabolic indices and later coded cardiometabolic multimorbidity, and supports Cox/multistate descriptions of progression. The current public UK Biobank evidence inspected here is Xue et al., *Cardiovascular Diabetology* (2026), DOI `10.1186/s12933-026-03255-4`, frozen source `[source checksum]`; its abstract reports baseline AIP-related associations and multistate progression, not the incremental value of assessment-linked within-person HbA1c/triglyceride/HDL change after a repeat assessment. The frozen Europe PMC search `[source checksum]` likewise supports that background only.

The unresolved clinical/estimand question is:

> Among UK Biobank participants with an established single coded diabetes, cardiovascular disease, or chronic kidney disease domain who return for a valid second assessment and remain in that domain, does assessment-linked change in HbA1c, triglycerides and HDL add route-specific prognostic information beyond panel-1 levels, domain identity, disease duration, and prespecified covariates—and does that information predict a robustly confirmed additional domain rather than only one administrative code?

This matters because a clinician deciding whether current metabolic values are sufficient for risk review needs to know whether the patient’s prior change contains reproducible information, not merely whether a flexible model can fit a broad diagnosis code. A positive result could motivate a prospective study of trend-informed surveillance; a null result would support the narrower conclusion that current levels add what is learnable from these two selected assessments. Neither result establishes that changing a biomarker causes disease, that repeat testing benefits patients, or that a policy threshold is clinically useful.

The primary prognostic claim is deliberately conditional: in the complete, observed repeat-panel cohort, change adds information for the first subsequent additional coded target domain beyond the stated baseline information, and the direction is not an artifact of a single code, source duplication, or obvious repeat-assessment selection. The key robustness claim is tested separately using a confirmed transition endpoint. A single-code signal that disappears under confirmation is not treated as evidence for clinical progression.

The local export contains field-instance values and assessment-linked date proxies, not verified specimen collection times, assay replication, adjudicated diagnosis onset, severity/stage, symptoms, treatment/adherence, or reasons for repeat testing. Therefore the exposure is called **assessment-linked change**, not a biological slope, and the endpoint is called **recorded/confirmed coded transition**, not disease onset.

### Resolution of the assay-source ambiguity

The export duplicates the three core assay fields in `main` and `biological_samples`; `assessment` has the dates/covariates but none of these assay columns. The design uses `biological_samples` as the sole canonical exposure source because its table binding is the biological-sample table and its schema explicitly contains the assay fields. The `main` assay columns are a provenance/integrity check only: they are never averaged, substituted per participant, entered as extra predictors, or treated as independent replicates.

Before cohort filtering, compare each overlapping `main`/biological_samples value after numeric normalization at each of the six assay fields (three markers, instances 0/1). Record both-missing, both-present-equal, one-missing, and present-but-unequal cells. A finite pair is equal if absolute difference is no more than `max(1e-8, 1e-6*|value|`). The primary exposure retains the canonical biological_samples value when it is finite. It flags any main disagreement and does not use the main value to repair it. A source-concordant sensitivity restricts to participants with both sources finite and equal for all six canonical assay fields. If any date in main and assessment disagrees after date normalization, exclude that participant from the primary exposure cohort and report the mismatch; a sensitivity uses assessment dates only. This is a source-integrity safeguard, not a measurement-error estimate.

A bounded direct audit read complete headers and the first 1,000 data rows in file order from main, assessment and biological_samples. All three had 1,000 unique `eid` values in that probe and a 1,000-row three-way overlap. For every non-missing assay value in that probe, main and biological_samples matched exactly: HbA1c 931/931 at panel 0 and 28/28 at panel 1, HDL 862/862 and 28/28, and triglycerides 951/951 and 31/31. The corresponding date values in main and assessment matched for 1,000/1,000 at panel 0 and 32/32 non-missing panel-1 pairs. These are availability and integrity checks, not full-cohort counts, prevalence estimates, or scientific results. The source file SHA-256 values are recorded in `datasets/ukb/README.md` and `datasets/ukb/metadata.json`; the solver must re-emit them before reading rows.

Horizontally join the five tables one-to-one on `eid`. Require no duplicate identifiers, report five-way row overlap, and require overlapping fields to agree under the rules above. Do not join to genomics, online follow-up, or additional_exposures for this experiment; those remain accessible but are outside this prespecified question.

## Population, time zero, and exposures

Use participants aged 40–69 inclusive at d0, with valid sex and five-source `eid` overlap. Define d0=`53-0.0` and d1=`53-1.0` from assessment, after requiring agreement with main; require d0<d1 and an interval of 365–3,650 days. The date is an assessment-linked proxy; do not infer specimen timing or elapsed time from instance labels.

Freeze the codebook after inspecting the field strings:

- diabetes: ICD-10 E10–E14 or ICD-9 250;
- cardiovascular disease: ICD-10 I20–I25, I50, I60–I69 or ICD-9 410–414, 428, 430–438;
- chronic kidney disease: ICD-10 N18/N19 or ICD-9 585;
- negative control: ICD-10 K35 or ICD-9 540.

Normalize code case and whitespace. A valid diagnosis requires a same-suffix code/date pair. Scan every listed suffix, retaining code-only, date-only, invalid-date and pre-d1 records in the audit but never allowing them to create an event. For each domain, the first valid paired date on or before the relevant landmark establishes recorded domain presence. At both d0 and d1 require exactly one target domain, the same domain at both panels, and that domain’s first paired date is no later than d0. Exclude death on or before d1.

Define existing-domain duration=(d1−first-domain-date)/365.25. Require finite canonical biological_samples values for HbA1c, HDL and triglycerides at both instances 0 and 1. Reverse HDL so worsening is positive. Compute annualized assessment-linked change `(x1−x0)/((d1−d0)/365.25)`; do not call it a biological slope. Standardize changes using fit-partition parameters only. Keep optional creatinine/cystatin-C out of the primary exposure; use them only in a predeclared renal-discordance descriptive sensitivity if the solver confirms endpoint support.

Primary time zero is d1. Follow each participant to the earliest event, death, observed censor boundary, or five years after d1. Do not impose a future one-year washout in the primary analysis. A d1+365 delayed-entry analysis is a labeled reverse-causation sensitivity only. The primary target is conditional on being observed, same-domain-stable and assay-complete at d1; it is not a claim about non-returners.

## Two outcome estimands: recorded and confirmed progression

### E1: earliest recorded second-domain transition

After d1, for each target domain not present at d1, take the earliest valid paired code/date. If exactly one new domain has the earliest date, classify its unique route from the d1 domain. If two or more new target domains share that earliest date, classify `SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI` and retain the member set; never assign it arbitrarily to a route. A same-day death takes precedence over a new-domain event; reverse precedence is a labeled sensitivity. Emit `SECOND_DOMAIN_UNIQUE`, `SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI`, `DEATH`, or `CENSOR`.

Let `C_obs` be the maximum finite date across all `41280-*`, `41281-*`, and `40000-*` values used in the analysis. It is not automatically administrative closure. A five-year E1 incidence or calibration statement requires documented source-level administrative completeness; otherwise report only the observed-boundary estimand and label five-year summaries conditional on the unverified boundary assumption.

### E2: confirmed second-domain transition

E2 is the endpoint-validity repair. For each domain, identify post-d1 valid paired code/date records. A domain is confirmed only if it has two distinct code/date slots with valid dates `t_first < t_confirm`, `t_confirm−t_first >= 30` days, and `t_confirm−t_first <= 365` days. The E2 event date is `t_confirm`, because this is the first time the data show the prespecified confirmation; the first code date is retained as an auxiliary field. A repeated code on the same date does not confirm a condition. The earliest confirmed new domain is selected, with the same unique-versus-multi-domain rule and same-day death precedence as E1. Death before confirmation remains a competing event. If the source boundary prevents a full 365-day confirmation opportunity, the participant is retained in the E1 analysis but E2 is flagged as boundary-limited and cannot support an unqualified five-year claim.

E2 is not adjudicated disease onset or proof that two code slots are independent clinical encounters. It is a reproducible robustness endpoint for distinguishing a persistent administrative record from a single code. If its support is inadequate, E2 is inconclusive rather than silently omitted.

For both estimands, report pre-d1 target codes, code-only/date-only cells, invalid dates, post-d1 first dates, confirmation pairs, simultaneous target-domain dates, death ties, censor dates and all member sets. Produce a participant-level event file and a tie table by d1 domain, event category, route, endpoint version, split and locked-test status.

## Participation and measurement-selection diagnostics

Report the flow in this order:

1. all source rows and five-way `eid` overlap;
2. age, sex and d0 date eligibility;
3. d0 exactly-one-domain eligibility;
4. valid d1 observation and allowed gap;
5. same-domain survival/retention at d1;
6. complete d0 canonical assays;
7. complete d1 canonical assays;
8. main/biological_samples source-concordant subset;
9. E1 and E2 unique/multi-domain, death and censor support.

Among d0-eligible participants, model valid d1 date observation using d0-available age, sex, d0 domain, duration, d0 markers, d0 BMI/BP/smoking/center, d0 calendar and prospective gap variables only. Separately describe who survives and remains in the same coded domain through d1. Do not put post-d0 survival, d1 stability, or d1 assays into a purported pre-d1 return model.

Among d1-stable participants with complete d0 assays and valid d1 date, model canonical d1 assay completeness using d0 assays, dates/gap, age, sex, d0 domain, duration, center and d1 date availability. Fit stabilized inverse-probability weights only in fit data, clip at fit 1st/99th percentiles, and report positivity and effective sample size. Label this an assay-completeness/observed-repeat sensitivity; it cannot identify unobserved changes in non-returners. A separately labeled return-weighted exploratory analysis may be attempted under explicit d0-MAR assumptions only with adequate positivity. Source-concordant restriction is reported separately from weighting. If direction or uncertainty changes materially across these analyses, the claim is selection/source-sensitive and inconclusive.

## Matched current-level baseline and mechanism-inspired alternative

Every model below uses the identical eligible cohort, source-canonical exposures, event architecture, death hazard, time zero, censoring rule, split, weighting, preprocessing, uncertainty procedure and locked evaluation. Each is fit separately for E1 and E2.

### B0: simple current-level baseline

For each supported existing-domain-to-unique-new-domain route, and for the multi-domain cause and death, fit cause-specific Cox hazards with panel-1 HbA1c, triglycerides and HDL, existing domain, existing-domain duration, age, sex, d1–d0 gap, BMI `21001-0.0`, SBP `4080-0.0`, DBP `4079-0.0`, smoking `20116-0.0`, and center `54-0.0`. Continuous terms use fit-frozen restricted cubic splines; categorical missingness is handled by a fit-defined rule and reported. No d0-to-d1 change enters B0. The baseline answers: what can panel-1 current state predict for the next recorded or confirmed transition?

### M_change: common deterioration plus discordance

This is a fixed, mechanism-inspired alternative, not a claim of a validated biological latent factor. It tests whether a shared metabolic deterioration signal and/or marker-specific discordance adds information beyond B0.

Let `z_H`, `z_T`, and `z_R` be fit-partition standardized annualized changes in HbA1c, triglycerides and reverse-HDL. Define:

- `C=(z_H+z_T+z_R)/sqrt(3)`, the coordinated-deterioration component;
- `D1=(z_H−z_T)/sqrt(2)`;
- `D2=(z_H+z_T−2z_R)/sqrt(6)`.

If empirical covariance is nonzero, estimate an orthogonalizing transform in fit data and freeze it. Fit `M_common=B0+C` and `M_change=B0+C+D1+D2` with the same route-specific hazards and only prespecified domain interactions when route support passes the threshold. The joint contrast M_change versus B0 tests incremental change information; M_change versus M_common tests whether discordance adds beyond common deterioration. The common component can reveal a coherent burden signal that separate current-level terms miss; D terms can reveal a marker-specific pattern hidden by a single common score. Conversely, no C/D increment means that, for the selected prognostic purpose, the current level is sufficient in this data.

To reduce regression-to-the-mean overinterpretation, repeat M_change after residualizing each change on its panel-1 value and gap using fit-only regressions, with the residualization rule frozen before locked evaluation. This is a sensitivity, not a new primary hypothesis. The two main/bio copies cannot estimate assay reliability because they are duplicated export fields; no reliability correction is claimed. A source-concordant restriction and the residualized-change sensitivity are the available checks against source/measurement artifacts.

A richer neural/recurrent sequence model is deferred for a scientific reason: only two verified assay panels are available, so it cannot learn meaningful within-person sequence structure or resolve date-proxy timing and return selection. Revisit only with at least three verified dated assay panels or evidence from a bounded diagnostic that an added representation reveals stable information beyond C/D under the same endpoint and selection checks. This is not a blanket CPU/GPU prohibition.

## Split, uncertainty, evaluation and falsification

Hash participants, never rows, using SHA-256(namespace `ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1`, dataset, `eid`) modulo 100:

- 0–59 fit;
- 60–69 model and threshold decisions;
- 70–79 locked test;
- 80–99 reserved and unread until the primary locked analysis is frozen.

Fit source-concordance rules, date/code normalization, spline knots, standardization, residualization, weighting, model forms and review thresholds only in fit/tuning data. The reserved split is not used to rescue a failing hypothesis.

For each endpoint and model, report route coefficients and 95% intervals, cause-specific hazards, competing-risk cumulative incidence at the observed boundary and only at five years if closure is documented, integrated/time-dependent Brier, calibration intercept/slope/error, discrimination, restricted mean event-free survival, and paired 200-resample participant-bootstrap intervals for M_common−B0, M_change−B0 and M_change−M_common. Report absolute-risk summaries at prespecified 5%, 10% and 20% review thresholds: alert rate, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, number needed to review and decision-curve net benefit. These are computational summaries, not approved clinical actions.

Falsification and artifact checks are mandatory:

- rerun with one-year delayed entry and lag 0/2/5 years; the primary d1 landmark remains unchanged;
- rerun with gap 182–5,475 days and alternative stable-domain definitions;
- within-state permutation of panel-1 change among participants in the same d1 domain and fit/tuning stratum;
- appendicitis negative control using paired K35/540 codes and dates;
- source-concordant assay subset and main-only assay sensitivity, reported as provenance sensitivities, never as independent validation;
- E1 versus E2 confirmation endpoint comparison;
- same-day death precedence reversed as a labeled tie sensitivity;
- explicit `C_obs`/administrative-cutoff audit and boundary-limited E2 counts.

Supportive evidence requires all of the following for the claimed endpoint: at least 20 total events and 5 locked-test events, adequate support for each claimed unique route, stable source/tie construction, adequate positivity/ESS, a prespecified worsening-direction interval excluding the null for C or discordance, and a locked-test improvement in paired Brier/calibration or at least 10% relative net benefit at a pre-locked threshold. The direction must not reverse under assay-completeness weighting, source-concordant restriction, or the one-year lag; appendicitis and within-state permutation must not show a comparable effect. A five-year statement additionally requires documented administrative closure.

For the substantive progression claim, E1 support alone is insufficient if E2 has adequate support and the change signal disappears or reverses on E2; that result is endpoint-fragile and classified adverse for robust progression or inconclusive if E2 lacks precision. A supportive common-burden conclusion requires C to add beyond B0 on both the relevant E1/E2 interpretation and without requiring D. A supportive discordance conclusion requires D1/D2 to add beyond C with route support. If E2 is underpowered, report E1 as a recorded-code result and mark robust clinical confirmation inconclusive.

Adverse means no incremental information from change on a supported endpoint, worse locked performance, an informative null/reversal after duration or source/selection adjustment, a comparable negative-control/permutation signal, or E1-only support contradicted by adequately supported E2. This falsifies the selected conditional prognostic claim, not all metabolic biology or a causal intervention hypothesis.

Inconclusive means inadequate endpoint/route events, failed positivity or ESS, unstable source/tie construction, unresolved date disagreement, boundary failure for the claimed horizon, poor convergence/calibration, intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, E2 confirmation scarcity, or unresolved selection/timing/phenotype sensitivity. A threshold result without expert-approved action and clinical utility data is inconclusive for policy value.

Computationally checkable claims include source hashes/headers, exact table and field bindings, five-way joins, canonical-source and duplicate-source agreement, cohort flow, code/date pairing, confirmation logic, ties, censor boundary, fitted models, locked predictions, uncertainty, falsification and sensitivity outputs. Clinical onset, code validity, reasons for return/testing, treatment/adherence, severity, causal effects, monitoring benefit, transportability, nonreturner generalization and policy value require adjudicated outcomes, unavailable evidence, expert review or another study.

## Compute plan and actual deliverable

The actual scientific deliverable is a newly constructed, frozen participant-level exposure/outcome dataset plus newly fitted B0, M_common and M_change models for E1 and E2, with uncertainty and output-linked classification. A readiness check or completed source audit is not a solved result.

Discovery measurements were read-only header and first-1,000-row audits using ordinary shell/Python and took approximately 23 seconds of shell time across the two checks. Full-cohort parsing, event construction, route support, weighting, fitting, bootstrap and E2 confirmation support are unmeasured. The separate solver-planning envelope in `inputs.json` is 16 CPUs, up to 8 GPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds; the proposed workload is CPU-first because it is tabular Cox/competing-risk fitting with a small number of fixed transformations and 200 participant bootstraps. The available A100 GPUs are not required by the scientific design. If a bounded implementation diagnostic shows that repeated bootstrap fits exceed the envelope, reduce bootstrap parallelism or use analytic sandwich intervals only as a prespecified computational fallback, and report the change; do not replace E2 or shrink the cohort to make it fit. Resource estimates beyond the measured audit are planning estimates, not runtime guarantees. No proposer/solver model training is authorized.

## Alternatives not chosen and revisit criteria

- B0 is retained as the interpretable current-level baseline because it answers the clinical comparison directly.
- M_change is retained over the parent’s unequal marker-change versus one-axis comparison because C/D gives a dimension-matched, mechanistically interpretable test of coherent deterioration versus discordance under identical route/death hazards.
- The new E2 confirmation endpoint is selected because it tests whether a prognostic signal survives a concrete administrative-robustness criterion; it is not assumed to be a clinically adjudicated endpoint.
- A recurrent/attention sequence model is deferred: two verified panels do not support meaningful sequence learning, and extra capacity cannot solve selection or date-proxy uncertainty. Revisit with at least three verified panels and demonstrated incremental information.
- A formal measurement-error latent-variable model is deferred: main and biological_samples are duplicated export fields, not independent replicates, so reliability is unidentified locally. Revisit only with assay replicates, laboratory QC/reliability data, or a validated external error model.
- Causal treatment or monitoring-policy analysis is deferred because treatment, adherence, clinical utility, expert thresholds and adjudicated onset are unavailable.
- Genomics, imaging, accelerometry, proteomics beyond the listed assays, raw narratives and external validation are not verified dependencies for this question and are not silently substituted.

## Demonstration, seed and evidence limits

The natural-history demonstration was inspected as a methods/compute example for dated structured histories; no reproduction or Danish external validation is claimed. The Bayesian longitudinal/genetic demonstration was inspected, but its genetic analysis is not reproduced because validated local genetic inputs are unavailable; the present two-panel EHR question does not require that modality. The cancer demonstration’s supplement was inspected for its accessible ablation context, but the main article and complete STAR Methods remain unavailable and raw images are absent; no multimodal claim is made.

UKB expert seed 09 motivated this line and has a valid descendant. Other UKB seeds remain deferred rather than disproven because required timing, modality, staging, rare-event support, validated PRS, or external-validation inputs are missing or unverified. Retrieved references are evidence for reasoning, not novelty certificates.

The final interpretation must remain internal, prognostic and evidence-bound: even supportive computational results do not establish biological progression, causality, treatment benefit, surveillance benefit, generalization to non-returners or policy utility. Those conclusions require clinical adjudication, missing data/evidence, expert review and/or another prospective study.
