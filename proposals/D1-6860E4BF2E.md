# Equal-completed-workload capacity/access frontier for prefix-only preoperative M2 review

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and advance

The decision is whether a scarce multidisciplinary review service should use routine preoperative CT/MRI report semantics to allocate review slots among adults approaching a first eligible HCC resection. The endpoint is high-grade microvascular invasion (documentary M2) in the eventual resection pathology. The action is review allocation only: this experiment does not recommend wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant/adjuvant treatment, locoregional/systemic treatment, surveillance, or any other care.

The strongest claim supported by the HCC snapshot is only that it contains patient/encounter-linked procedures and encounters, acquisition-like examination timestamps and report text, untimed pathology narratives, and dated medication/order records. It has no radiology authored/final/release/view time or immutable version, pathology time/specimen/slide/block identifier or sampling protocol, MDT roster/capacity ledger, clinician action, treatment, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or benefit. Thus the computable claim is historical stored-content allocation under reconstructed arrival times. It is not evidence that a report was visible at the decision time, that an MDT could deliver the workload, or that review changes outcomes.

The latest valid parent made prefix-only, calendar-paced allocation primary, correctly preventing future test patients from competing for earlier slots. This child repairs its remaining decision-utility ambiguity: the parent reports an all-stored-content result and a conditional access frontier, but does not make the clinically actionable question—how much exact report availability is required before a capacity-constrained policy remains useful—the primary estimand. This proposal adds a common completed-review estimand so an apparent gain cannot be explained by one policy simply using more slots. It estimates a joint capacity/access frontier and requires the same benefit margin at every tested offered workload, completed workload, and access guarantee inside the claimed frontier. A favorable result identifies a concrete target for an external workflow audit; it does not assert that the target is met.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Let s index the complete, source-consistent outer states. A state jointly fixes the operative episode and clock, selected encounter and year, prior recorded treatment, examination accession/component clock and modality pattern, report-body mask, pathology composite/outcome, and chronological arrival. No attributes may be mixed across states.

Use separate service cycles y=2020 and 2021. Set
N_min,y = min_s N_y(s), and freeze K_yq=floor(q N_min,y) for q in {0.05,0.10,0.20}. These are available-data workload benchmarks, not observed service capacity. An external audit may replace them with preregistered absolute slots; HCC alone cannot do so.

For model M in {S, S0, B}, replay a frozen prefix-only policy pi_M,q. At each arrival, the action can use only that patient’s admissible pre-decision record, the frozen fitted score, calendar day, cumulative slots used, and a development/tuning release curve. It cannot use future arrivals, final annual N, future test ranks, outcome labels, or another model’s actions. S is interpretable report semantics plus the structured baseline; S0 masks only semantic fields while retaining S’s exact fitted coefficients/preprocessing/calibration/policy; B is independently fitted structured, report-body-free baseline. Equal K and identical release schedules are mandatory. Unused slots remain unused.

Define R_offer(M,s,y,q)=100/K_yq times the number of assigned patients with Y=1, U=number assigned/K_yq, and F=number assigned with Y=0 per 100 offered slots. The primary service-level contrasts are D_offer=R_offer(S)-R_offer(S0) and C_offer=R_offer(S)-R_offer(B). Because utilization may range from 0.90 to 1.00, add a common completed-workload target J_yq=ceil(0.90 K_yq). During each chronological replay, define A_i^J(M)=A_i(M) times 1[r_M(i)<=J_yq], where r_M is that policy's acceptance ordinal; stop counting after J and never pad or substitute using outcomes. Define R_J(M)=100/J_yq times the number of Y=1 among A_i^J(M), with D_J=R_J(S)-R_J(S0) and C_J=R_J(S)-R_J(B). These are equal-completed-review priority contrasts; D_offer/C_offer remain service-level contrasts. The five-capture margin is required for both families and is a review-yield margin, not a treatment-effect margin. Failure of any policy to reach J in a coherent state is inconclusive.

Report access by four exact guarantees on a 0.05 grid: h_C for C-pattern CT, h_M for M-pattern MRI, h_A for CM patients with at least one modality report visible, and h_B<=h_A for CM patients with both visible. A visible report means the entire selected accession body is exposed; component rows cannot be split. For each (q,h_C,h_M,h_A,h_B), solve the exact state-coupled adversarial visibility/action problem, with unknown pathology outcomes and modality choices jointly retained. The primary frontier is the smallest coordinatewise access guarantee at which, for every q, year, and admissible state, simultaneous lower limits for both D_J/C_J and D_offer/C_offer exceed 5, every policy reaches J, and worst-state utilization is at least 0.90. If no cell satisfies this, the frontier is empty.

The primary hypothesis is:

H1-frontier: there is a nonempty access frontier, and at every claimed frontier cell the 97.5% simultaneous lower bounds for D_J, C_J, D_offer, and C_offer exceed 5 documentary-M2 captures per 100 completed/offered reviews respectively in both 2020 and 2021, every policy reaches J, utilization >=0.90, and all evidence gates pass.

A secondary attainability check compares online S capture with the state-matched retrospective top-K oracle. Its simultaneous lower ratio must exceed 0.80 at every year/q. Oracle-positive but prefix-negative results are adverse for operational attainability, not supportive.

Falsification is prespecified. A valid simultaneous upper bound <=5 for any required D_J, C_J, D_offer, or C_offer at any claimed year/q/access cell falsifies the joint claim at that cell; an empty frontier falsifies the access-capacity hypothesis. An attainability upper bound <0.80 falsifies transport from retrospective ranking to chronological policy. If offered contrasts support but an upper bound for D_J or C_J is <=5, the apparent service gain is not established as better prioritization at equal completed workload. Bands crossing the margin are inconclusive, not positive.

## Population and temporal design

The person unit is 患者主索引 and same-encounter joins use (患者主索引, 就诊号). Exact duplicate rows are retained in a raw-ordinal ledger. Two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician freeze the operative dictionary and reconcile 手术系统 and 病案手术 rows. A clinically linked valid anesthesia-system 开始时间 is exact; an unvalidated case-record midnight/date proxy is [d,d+24h). Missing or competing clocks remain explicit alternatives. Select the first eligible episode separately in every coherent state.

Eligibility is age >=18 at that encounter, all-row same-encounter pathology explicitly supporting HCC, and no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy during [t_dec-365d,t_dec), using state-matched dated procedures, medications, and orders. This is not biological treatment-naivety. Set t_dec=t_op-24h and test 12/48/72-hour sensitivities. Develop in 2015–2018, tune and freeze thresholds/release curves in 2019, and test 2020 and 2021 separately. Any patient with a possible later-period state is quarantined from earlier fitting. 2022 onward is drift-only because grade ascertainment must first be audited.

Arrivals are ordered by t_dec. Interval clock alternatives that cross another arrival or release boundary are enumerated; same-time ties use a frozen hash of 患者主索引. The annual release curve is learned only from 2015–2019 arrival timing, monotonized and frozen before test replay. There is no end-of-year filling, recall, replacement, or future-information use.

## Report unit, outcome, predictors, and baselines

Before modality filtering, group every nonblank examination accession on (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号), retaining all component rows. A whole accession is eligible only if its state-specific component clocks place the entire bundle in [t_dec-90d,t_dec); the primary conservative clock uses the maximum valid component start, with conflicting/missing clocks retained as alternatives. Select at most the latest eligible accession per modality. Blinded abdominal radiologists map each bundle to CT, MRI, non-target, or unresolved. Concatenate 检查所见 and 检查诊断 in frozen source order. Blank 检查号 rows are logged and excluded from semantic units.

Compose all deduplicated pathology rows on the selected encounter using 病理, 检查所见, and 检查诊断. The frozen parser assigns Y=1 only to an anchored selected-value M2 and Y=0 only to anchored M0/M1. Boilerplate, unanchored mentions, missing/uncertain grade, conflicts, uncertain specimen linkage, or inadequate sampling produce Y in {0,1}, never silent exclusion. Two Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors adjudicate every test unknown/conflict and all parsed M2, with a third resolving disagreement. The endpoint remains documentary M2, not biological M2.

B includes state-selected age, sex, CT/MRI/O acquisition indicators, eligible accession count, acquisition-to-decision recency, component count, and development-frequency machine category. It excludes report body, labs, diagnoses, pathology, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff facts. S adds frozen interpretable semantic fields for lesion diameter/count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites, each coded present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned. S0 uses S’s exact fitted pipeline after masking only these semantic fields; S and S0 must be bitwise identical when all semantic fields are masked. Fit S and B with the parent’s patient-wise worst-state elastic-net logistic objective. Report first-come, lottery, none, age/sex-only, and retrospective top-K policies as negative/control benchmarks.

## Inference, access frontier, and validity gates

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits, rebuilding state ledgers, 2019 tuning, policies, access actions, and endpoint bands; freeze original K values. Use one max-centered 97.5% simultaneous family over D_J/C_J, D_offer/C_offer, years, capacities, clock buffers, all frontier cells, attainability ratios, and null tests. Use 1,000 development/tuning semantic-null permutations stratified by year, modality/pattern, accession cardinality, and formatting family with full refits.

Require N_min,y>=500, >=50 adjudicated M2 events per claimed year envelope, anchored-grade rate >=85%, all test unknowns reviewed, parser kappa >=0.90, M2 PPV >=0.98 and sensitivity >=0.95, modality agreement >=0.95, unresolved modality <=1%, claimed pattern >=100 patients and >=20 M2, semantic feature PPV/sensitivity >=0.85, no required-feature/pattern shift >15 percentage points, all/no-report calibration slope 0.80–1.20 and intercept -0.10–0.10, worst-state utilization >=0.90, exact slot/release accounting, complete state/backpointer replay, optimizer/brute-force agreement on small fixtures, monotone access limits, and zero laboratory/forbidden lineage. More than 2.5% failed bootstrap replicates invalidates support.

Supportive results require all gates, a nonempty frontier, every policy reaching J, both equal-completed-workload contrasts D_J/C_J and both offered-capacity contrasts D_offer/C_offer above 5 in both years at every q and claimed frontier cell, and attainability ratio above 0.80. This separates priority quality from service yield. The conclusion is limited to robust historical stored-report semantic value under a specified prefix policy and the minimum exact access guarantee needed in this source-state envelope.

Adverse results include a valid upper bound <=5 for any required D_J/C_J or D_offer/C_offer cell, an empty frontier, or attainability <0.80. If only the equal-workload family is adverse, label the result utilization-explained rather than claiming semantic prioritization. Conclude that the proposed policy does not meet the preregistered review-yield target under that capacity/access condition, even if an all-store or retrospective oracle is favorable. Mixed year/q/modality results fail the joint claim and should be reported cell-by-cell. Inconclusive results include crossed uncertainty, failed adjudication/calibration/null/state/bootstrap/optimizer gates, or incomplete access evidence.

Automatic verification can establish headers and hashes, filters and attrition, patient-disjoint temporal splits, state completeness/backpointers, chronological replay, no future reads, frozen K/release/thresholds, S0 identity, exact access coupling, frontier monotonicity, optimizer agreement, bootstrap/null calculations, and conclusion-to-zone linkage. It cannot establish report visibility, clinical identity or semantic correctness, pathology sampling adequacy, acceptable MDT workload, clinician action, patient benefit, or fairness. Those require expert adjudication, an external report-version/view audit, a real capacity/time-and-motion audit, and then prospective silent-mode followed by randomized or pragmatic impact evaluation with treatment, harms, recurrence, survival, cost, and equity outcomes.

## Compiler/verifier contract

Compilation must preserve the union roster, coupled clock/report/pathology/access states, accession-first bundles, all-row documentary outcome, temporal quarantine, annual absolute K, frozen release curve, prefix-only replay, equal offered and equal completed workload contrasts, D_J/C_J and D_offer/C_offer, attainability, and the joint capacity/access frontier. The verifier must test both correct computation with unsupported conclusions and correct interpretations of supportive, adverse, mixed, and inconclusive results. It should reject claims of report access, clinical utility, treatment benefit, recurrence/survival, or fairness unless the stated external audits or another study provide those data.
