# When a ventilation care-plan label is a warning signal: a fail-closed test of near-term respiratory deterioration

## Status, unresolved claim, and clinical importance

This is a substantive evolution of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. The lineage has already tested the exact care-plan labels against transient and persistent respiratory-chart improvement proxies, transportability, vocabulary expansion, documentation controls, and durable liberation. Those analyses support a narrower conclusion: the labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated, their earlier chart associations were unstable or dominated by selected components, and available fields cannot adjudicate an actual spontaneous breathing trial, extubation readiness, liberation, or clinician intent.

The strongest remaining clinically consequential unresolved claim that the available structured eICU data can test is the safety direction of the label. **Among the frozen labelled person-landmarks, does the exact `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` label, compared with `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`, identify a higher risk of a subsequent concordant respiratory deterioration signal or ICU death, beyond strictly pre-label respiratory state and documentation opportunity?** The converse is also tested: a null or reversal is evidence against using either label as a multicenter early-warning signal. This is a descriptive conditional association and transportable prediction estimand, not an effect of withholding a trial, a causal effect, a quality measure, or a clinical recommendation.

The advance is not another re-labeling of improvement. It tests the clinically important adverse branch that prior liberation/improvement experiments did not resolve, and requires deterioration in both a support domain and an independent physiologic-safety domain. A single copied setting, a post-label treatment, or disappearance of charting cannot define an event. The design also separates the composite from its component signals and from death, so a positive result cannot be mistaken for proof of extubation failure or treatment harm.

## Outcome-blind parser freeze and temporal windows

Write and hash a parser manifest before any arm-specific endpoint counts or comparisons are inspected. Preserve the validated parent parser exactly. Trim labels but require exact case-sensitive equality to these respiratory-chart labels:

* FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`
* PEEP: `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`
* pressure support: `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`
* set rate: `Vent Rate`, `VS RESP RATE`

Parse only full tokens matching `^[+-]?(?:\d+(?:\.\d*)?|\.\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\d+)?$`; reject blanks, ranges, inequalities, embedded units, and all other strings, recording reason and label. Retain FiO2 only in `[21,100]` without multiplying fractions; values such as `0.4`, `2`, or `3` are invalid/unit-ambiguous. Retain PEEP and pressure support in `[0,60]`, set rate `[0,100]`, SpO2 `[0,100]`, and respiration `[0,200]`. Parse numeric source fields with the same full-token rule.

Use pre-state windows `(D-1440,D-720]` and `(D-720,D]`. Use a six-hour post-decision grace period to reduce immediate chart-copy contamination, then early `(D+360,D+1080]` and late `(D+1080,D+1800]` windows. For every component and safety signal, require at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. Collapse exact duplicate clinical-time/value rows after retaining source IDs and deterministic `(clinical_offset, entry_offset, source_id)` order. ## Primary adverse endpoint and secondary endpoints

Define a safety-concordant respiratory deterioration signal only from the blinded post-D `respiratoryCharting` and `vitalPeriodic` streams. First calculate pre, early, and late medians as above.

A **support-escalation component** is present when at least one component meets its fixed increase threshold in both early and late windows relative to the pre-D median: FiO2 increase `≥10` percentage points, PEEP increase `≥3` cmH2O, pressure-support increase `≥5` cmH2O, or set-rate increase `≥5` breaths/min. Component values must be available in pre, early, and late periods. A component is not credited if either post median is lower than the pre median. These thresholds and the requirement for persistence are locked before counts.

A **physiologic-safety deterioration component** is present when either (i) median SpO2 is at least 3 percentage points below pre-D in both early and late windows, or (ii) median respiration is at least 6 breaths/min above pre-D in both early and late windows. Require valid pre, early, and late observations for both safety signals; the safety component is missing rather than negative when a required signal is unavailable. The thresholds are proxies for deterioration, not validated severity criteria.

The primary binary endpoint `respiratory_deterioration_or_death_24h` is positive when either:

1. both a persistent support-escalation component and a persistent physiologic-safety deterioration component are present; or
2. `unitdischargeoffset < D+1800` and trimmed `unitdischargestatus` equals `Expired` case-insensitively.

Death is included because it is clinically consequential and observable in the structured discharge field, but is reported separately and never treated as proof of respiratory causation. A patient who dies before required post-window observations is an event, not endpoint missingness. `discharge_transfer` and other non-Expired early exits remain competing exits, not observed negatives. Death must be used only when discharge timing is before the horizon; discharge status/location cannot be inferred from care-plan or chart disappearance.

Report separately: persistent support escalation; persistent SpO2 decline; persistent respiration rise; the conjunction without death; death by 24 hours; and a nonpersistent diagnostic endpoint using one post window. The diagnostic endpoint is not allowed to replace the primary endpoint. Add fixed respiratoryCare sensitivities: (a) require any post-window nonempty `airwaytype`/`airwayposition` or genuinely nonmissing ventilator interval; (b) require a ventilator interval overlapping `(D+360,D+1800]`, with `ventstartoffset < D+1800` and `ventendoffset > D+360`; and (c) restrict to a latest pre-D exact airway type in `{Oral ETT,Nasal ETT,Tracheostomy,Double-Lumen Tube,Cricothyrotomy}` at recencies 360, 1,440, and 4,320 minutes. `Other` and `No Artificial Airway` do not qualify. These are ascertainment sensitivities, not validation of the primary endpoint. `vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean` is an optional secondary hemodynamic/documentation sensitivity only.

Assign mutually exclusive statuses for every frozen row: `observed_event`, `observed_nonevent`, `death_before_horizon`, `competing_discharge_transfer`, `truncation`, `no_pre_support`, `no_post_support`, `no_pre_safety`, `no_post_safety`, `time_tie_conflict`, `parser_rejection_only`, `no_chart_source`, or `source_error`. Death is reported as an event subtype as well as a status. Preserve all 540 rows in the status table and tabulate status by arm, landmark, hospital, and source availability. Do not call absent charting a clinical improvement or deterioration.

## Baselines, analysis, and uncertainty

Use three pre-D baseline models, all frozen before arm comparison: (A) hospital/landmark and demographic context only (`hospitalid`, `unittype`, `unitvisitnumber`, `age`, `gender`, `ethnicity`, `hospitaladmitsource`, landmark); (B) A plus respiratory state (pre-window FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, set rate, SpO2, respiration, valid-observation counts, recency, medians, and missingness/time-tie indicators); and (C) B plus pre-D documentation opportunity (counts and distinct clinical offsets in respiratoryCharting, vitalPeriodic, and respiratoryCare, with no post-D quantities). Include the frozen label in a fourth model only; do not include any other care-plan value, label count, post-D care, treatment, endpoint availability, discharge timing, or endpoint-derived variable. All predictors must have clinical time `< D`; record a machine-readable leakage audit.

The primary descriptive contrast is arm-specific risk difference for the primary endpoint among endpoint-observable rows, with cluster-robust 95% intervals from a fixed-seed bootstrap of `patientunitstayid` (and a `uniquepid` cluster sensitivity). Report full-denominator competing-exit and missingness tables. For prediction, use genuine leave-one-hospital-out evaluation. Every fold fits preprocessing, imputation of predictors only, calibration, regularization, and any observation model on training hospitals; no held-out outcome or endpoint availability is used in training. Compare baseline A, B, C, and C plus the frozen label using logistic regression with prespecified ridge penalty selected within training data, plus a simple calibrated baseline-risk model. Report micro and equal-hospital-macro log loss and Brier score for the composite, and separately for death when fold support permits; AUROC is secondary and omitted when undefined. Do not fit a model when outcome support is inadequate.

Require before an arm contrast is interpreted: at least five estimable hospitals, at least 10 observed primary endpoint rows in each arm, at least 50% of analyzed rows contributed by those hospitals, no hospital contributing over 40% of either arm, and at least two hospitals with observed events in each arm for direction checks. If a gate fails, return `inconclusive_endpoint_support` and report feasibility only. Use a fixed-seed within-hospital-by-landmark permutation test preserving the frozen row set and arm counts, with 1,000 permutations when at least two arms and outcome support exist in every permutation stratum; otherwise state permutation infeasibility rather than pooling incomparable hospitals.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

Predeclare and execute: (1) a same-parser pre-D placebo using an interval before D; (2) a D-720 lead-time placebo; (3) leave-one-support-component-out and leave-one-safety-signal-out composites; (4) the single-post-window diagnostic; (5) chart-only, respiratoryCare-corroborated, interval-overlap, and airway-recency sensitivities; (6) documentation-density negative controls using only pre-D observation counts; (7) the death-only endpoint; and (8) fixed within-hospital-by-landmark arm permutations. The pre-D placebos must not show an arm contrast of comparable magnitude; if they do, interpret the post-D association as likely baseline/documentation confounding rather than prospective warning information.

Call the result **supportive** only if all gates pass, the hypothesized no-trial arm has a prespecified positive excess risk (or better held-out risk prediction) over daily-evaluation in the primary composite, the stay-clustered 95% interval excludes zero, the label increment over model C is at least 0.01 in both micro and equal-hospital-macro held-out log loss (with interval excluding zero), at least 4 of 5 estimable hospital directions agree, and the signal persists after each single-component leave-out and in at least two of the three airway-recency variants. The pre-D and lead-time placebos must be null or materially smaller, and the observed contrast must exceed the feasible permutation null. This supports only reproducible incremental information in a structured respiratory deterioration construct.

Call the result **adverse** if the label adds no predictive information over state/opportunity, the daily-evaluation arm is not lower risk, the direction reverses across hospitals, the placebos are comparable, or the association is explained by documentation density or one component. This is valuable evidence against multicenter early-warning or quality-use interpretations; it is not evidence that daily evaluation causes harm or benefit.

Call the result **inconclusive** for cohort mismatch, insufficient events/overlap, excessive missingness or competing exits, non-estimable hospital-held-out folds, intervals spanning the meaningful threshold, or discordant component/source/airway results. A death-only signal without the blinded respiratory conjunction is reported as mortality association, not respiratory deterioration. A positive single-window endpoint with persistent composite failure is transient chart association, not support. A positive association confined to one hospital is local documentation evidence at most, not multicenter transportability.

Computationally checkable claims are exact lineage, parser behavior, temporal ordering, endpoint status, source availability, model leakage, held-out metrics, uncertainty, and falsification outputs. The computation cannot establish actual delivered ventilator settings, patient distress, SBT performance, extubation readiness, liberation, reintubation, clinician intent, treatment harm, quality, or causality. Those require expert adjudication against complete clinical records, device/waveform and workflow data, and an independent/prospective cohort.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to both parents, this child preserves the validated frozen cohort, exact exposure, decision time, exclusions, source snapshot, parser, separated windows, repeated-observation safeguards, endpoint-status accounting, held-out transport, cluster uncertainty, and proxy limitations. Its substantive change is to test the unresolved adverse/safety direction with a new concordance endpoint: persistent escalation in respiratory support plus persistent independent physiologic deterioration, or separately observable early ICU death. It precludes the previous improvement endpoint from being relabeled as harm, separates death and component outcomes, and makes placebos and documentation controls consequential to interpretation. The main remaining uncertainty is endpoint scarcity and semantic validity: even a reproducible result would indicate only that the label contains predictive information in exported structured chart streams, not that a no-trial decision caused deterioration or that the label is suitable for bedside triage.
