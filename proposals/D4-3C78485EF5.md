# UKB metabolic deterioration and first observed multimorbidity transition

## Scientific deliverable

The future solver must newly estimate whether change in three repeated metabolic biomarkers adds prognostic information beyond second-panel levels for a participant-level first observed transition from one cardiometabolic disease domain to a second. Completion requires:

1. a frozen, auditable five-source eid-joined cohort and source-concordance report;
2. a participant-level exposure/outcome file with paired code/date logic, death competition, split assignment and observation-opportunity states;
3. fit-frozen estimates from a current-level baseline (B0) and a coordinated-change/discordance model (M_change) on the same participants and outcomes;
4. locked-test predictions, calibration/discrimination and uncertainty comparisons;
5. a separate confirmation-qualified analysis, reported only where participant-level follow-up opportunity is directly supported; and
6. an explicit supportive, adverse or inconclusive decision that never converts missing observation opportunity into a negative outcome.

This is an internal UK Biobank prognostic adaptation, not a reproduction of a demonstration paper and not a causal treatment-effect study.

## Hypothesis and clinical importance

Among adults who already have exactly one of diabetes, cardiovascular disease or chronic kidney disease at a repeat metabolic assessment, does worsening across HbA1c, triglycerides and HDL predict the first recorded appearance of a second domain beyond current biomarker levels and routine covariates?

The falsifiable hypothesis is that, after adjustment for second-panel levels, established-domain duration, age, sex, BMI, blood pressure, smoking, assessment gap and center, coordinated worsening between the two verified panels is associated with a higher rate of a first observed second-domain transition. Increasing HbA1c and triglycerides and decreasing HDL should point in the worsening direction.

The clinical question is whether someone with established diabetes, cardiovascular disease or kidney disease should be treated as having a changing cross-domain risk state rather than a static current-level profile. A positive result could motivate clinical testing of a trajectory-based trigger for integrated prevention or closer review; it would not itself justify changing care.

The strongest claim supported before this study is only that the snapshot contains two assessment instances of selected metabolic measures and linked structured diagnosis/date and death fields, so a dated held-out prognostic comparison is computable in principle. The expert UKB-09 seed and inspected research demonstrations motivate this question but do not establish it here. The unresolved claim is incremental prognostic information under a valid d1-origin risk set, not an association created by selecting people who later return or receive another code.

Two panels cannot identify a biological turning point, causal deterioration, treatment response or precise onset date. The study therefore makes an intentionally bounded administrative/prognostic claim.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Use one row per eid after a five-source inner join. Require population, assessment, biological_samples, health_outcomes and main to contain the participant; age 40–69 inclusive from population field 21022-0.0; valid sex from population field 31-0.0; finite assessment dates d0 = assessment field 53-0.0 and d1 = assessment field 53-1.0; d0 < d1 with a 365–3,650 day gap; finite canonical HbA1c, HDL and triglyceride values at biological_samples instances 0 and 1; exactly one established domain at both d0 and d1, whose first valid paired diagnosis date is no later than d0; and no death on or before d1.

This stable-one-domain requirement prevents a new index disease between panels from being confused with progression. Excluded participants remain in the flow audit. No participant is selected because a later diagnosis or assessment exists.

For marker j, define annualized change as:

  (value at d1 - value at d0) / ((d1 - d0) / 365.25).

Reverse HDL before standardization so larger values always mean worsening. These are assessment-instance changes, not specimen-time slopes: the catalog says field instance/array is not elapsed time, and verified specimen collection dates are unavailable.

The primary time origin is d1. The primary E1 window is the first valid post-d1 event through d1 + 3,650 days, with a 30-day delayed-entry sensitivity for same-episode ascertainment. Codes in the first 30 days are retained and reported, not silently discarded.

## Outcomes and opportunity-aware censoring

Scan every paired code/date suffix; suffix order is not assumed chronological. A diagnosis is valid only when its code and date share the same suffix. Preserve code-only, date-only, invalid-date, duplicate-date and pre-landmark cells in the audit; none is an event by itself.

Domains are:

- diabetes: ICD-10 E10–E14 or ICD-9 250;
- cardiovascular disease: ICD-10 I20–I25, I50, I60–I69 or ICD-9 410–414, 428, 430–438;
- chronic kidney disease: ICD-10 N18/N19 or ICD-9 585;
- negative control: ICD-10 K35 or ICD-9 540.

Use health_outcomes fields 41270-0.0 through 41270-0.258 paired with 41280-0.0 through 41280-0.258; ICD-9 fields 41271-0.0 through 41271-0.46 paired with 41281-0.0 through 41281-0.46. Death is from paired fields 40000-0.0 and 40000-1.0. Retain the exact suffix and date used for every event.

E1 is the first observed transition from the one established domain to either absent domain. A same-date onset of both absent domains is SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI. Death on or before a tied transition date has primary precedence and competes with disease; reverse precedence is a sensitivity. E1 means first recorded occurrence, not adjudicated onset.

E2 is a confirmation-qualified secondary endpoint. For each absent domain, t1 is its first valid post-d1 paired code/date. A confirmation is the earliest later distinct date t2 for that domain with 30 <= t2-t1 <= 365 days. Multiple slots on one date count once. The participant-level event is the earliest confirmation across absent domains; ties are CONFIRMED_MULTI, with death precedence as above.

E2 is a d1-origin estimand, not confirmation among people selected for eventual t1. Pending participants remain in the d1 risk set. The solver must not condition entry on t1 or t2.

For every participant and absent domain, emit:

- C_source, the descriptive maximum finite date over relevant arrays;
- death_date, direct evidence of observation at death;
- opportunity_supported(k), only when a direct participant-level observation/closure field or verified source-release cutoff demonstrates observation through t1(k)+365; and
- B_UNKNOWN(k), an alive episode without such evidence.

A last diagnosis date is not a last-contact date. A cohort-wide maximum code/death date is not administrative closure. An alive participant without a qualifying confirmation is B_UNKNOWN/right-censored unless direct opportunity through t1+365 is documented. Do not call it U (supported opportunity without confirmation). If a closure variable not represented in the catalog is discovered, it may be used only after its exact field, provenance and validation audit are emitted.

For E2, a confirmation before the supported boundary is an event; death before confirmation is competing; supported opportunity with no confirmation is U; and unsupported alive follow-up is B_UNKNOWN. A participant may be U for one domain while remaining followed for the other. The primary report must separate observed transitions, deaths, supported no-event opportunities and opportunity-unknown episodes.

E1 event counts may be descriptive for all d1-origin participants. Population incidence, calibration and discrimination require an opportunity-supported analysis. If adequate participant-level opportunity does not exist, E2 and any population no-event claim are inconclusive; no global boundary substitution is allowed. Any cumulative-incidence horizon is called supported only when direct opportunity covers it.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Use identical participants, d1 origin, outcome states, death precedence, opportunity handling, split, transformations, weights, uncertainty and locked test set.

B0 is a route-specific cause-specific hazard or discrete-time complementary-log-log model with d1 HbA1c, triglycerides, HDL, established domain, domain duration, age, sex, BMI, SBP, DBP, smoking, center, assessment gap and fit-defined missingness indicators. Fit-frozen splines are allowed. It can model nonlinear current risk but has no d0-to-d1 change.

M_change adds a fit-standardized coordinated score C, the mean of standardized HbA1c change, triglyceride change and reverse-HDL change, plus two fit-orthogonal marker discordance contrasts. B0+C tests coherent movement; M_change-B0 tests whether change adds information; M_change-(B0+C) tests whether out-of-step movement matters. The alternative could reveal a coordinated deterioration phenotype or marker-specific discordance that current levels lose. It is a scientific comparison, not a search for a tiny score improvement.

A temporal transformer was considered and deferred. Using exactly the same d1 covariates, d0/d1 assay tokens and pre-d1 paired diagnosis/date tokens, it could model nonlinear ordering that B0/M_change compress. It would use the same d1-origin E1/E2 targets, participant split, calibration and Brier evaluation. Two verified assay panels, no reliable alive-person closure and administrative rather than adjudicated encounter sequences make it more likely to learn coding intensity/missingness than a distinct metabolic mechanism. Revisit after at least three verified dated panels or validated encounter/last-contact data and a bounded pilot showing information beyond M_change. This is a data-dependent deferral, not a neural-network or GPU prohibition.

A latent measurement-error model is also deferred: main and biological_samples are export copies, not independent replicates; assay QC or an external reliability model is required.

## Split, analysis and uncertainty

Hash the participant, never outcome slots, using SHA-256(namespace ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1, dataset, eid) modulo 100: 0–59 fit, 60–69 model/threshold decisions, 70–79 locked test and 80–99 reserved until the primary analysis is frozen. Fit all mappings, standardization, splines, missingness, weights and state simplifications without locked/reserved data. A participant has one split across both absent domains.

Report E1 route-specific estimates and death as a competing event with opportunity strata. Do not conventionally censor alive participants at C_source. If a validated opportunity variable is found, model censoring and check positivity in fit data before using IPCW. Otherwise report only opportunity-conditional performance and label full-cohort incidence unsupported.

For E2, report confirmation, death, U and B_UNKNOWN separately at d1 origin and only over supported horizons. Do not evaluate solely among t1/t2 participants.

Use locked predictions, time-dependent Brier score, calibration, supported-horizon discrimination and restricted mean event-free time only where censoring is supported. Use paired 200-resample participant bootstrap intervals for B0+C-B0 and M_change-B0, holding the test split fixed; disclose any computational fallback. Report worsening-direction and route-specific intervals. Threshold/net-benefit summaries are exploratory, not approved actions. ## Falsification and decision criteria

Mandatory falsification includes 30-day delayed entry; alternate assessment gaps; 0, 2 and 5-year lag choices; alternate code subsets; within-stratum change permutation; K35/540 negative control; source-concordant versus canonical-only assays; E1 versus E2; reversed death precedence; and checks that B_UNKNOWN is never U and pending death is not ordinary censoring.

Supportive evidence requires at least 20 observed events overall and 5 locked-test events, at least 3 locked-test events for any route claim, stable joins/ties, acceptable fit and calibration, adequate opportunity support for a confirmation/incidence claim, a worsening-direction interval excluding the null, and a paired locked-test improvement in Brier/calibration or at least 10% relative net benefit at a frozen operating point. It must survive source, duration, gap and lag sensitivities without a comparable permutation/negative-control signal. E1-only support is not robust confirmation support.

Adverse evidence is no incremental information or worse locked performance on a supported endpoint; disappearance after duration, observation or source adjustment; a positive permutation/negative-control result; or E1 improvement that disappears under adequately supported E2. This rejects the incremental-information claim in this snapshot but does not prove metabolic change biologically irrelevant.

Inconclusive means sparse events/routes, absent or materially incomplete opportunity support, B_UNKNOWN dominating, failed positivity/effective sample size, materially divergent timing/source sensitivities, uncertainty spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, or failed calibration/convergence. If no direct closure evidence exists for alive participants, the solver may report the checkable observed-event audit and fits but must label population no-event, confirmation-incidence and clinical-action claims inconclusive.

## Unavailable evidence and provenance

The health-outcome arrays are linked administrative/self-report-derived records; dates are recorded occurrences, not adjudicated biological onset or independent encounters. Missing are verified prescriptions, treatment/adherence, specimen dates, sufficient lifestyle trajectories for causal analysis, raw imaging, waveforms, validated narrative extraction, external validation, source-release closure and participant last-contact fields. No two-panel result establishes a turning point.

A verifier can check source hashes/headers, joins, filtering, pairing, event/tie logic, opportunity labels, fits, predictions, uncertainty and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish clinical onset, causal effects, clinical validity, monitoring benefit, transportability, fairness or policy utility; these require adjudication, missing evidence and another study.
