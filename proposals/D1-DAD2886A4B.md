# eICU semantic transportability audit of ventilator care-plan labels across hospitals and decision times

## Status, parents, and substantive advance

This proposal is a substantive child of the assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen 540-person-landmark cohort, the exact label time D, the completed failure of durable-liberation ascertainment, and the completed 12-hour chart-action analysis. It does not retry durable liberation, treat discharge bridging as success, or interpret a chart change as bedside care.

The first parent established that the durable endpoint cannot be validated in this eICU snapshot: independent liberation markers are rare and strict 48-hour follow-up is incomplete. The second parent showed that short-horizon charted setting and paired infusion-rate changes are computable, but the setting contrast is inverse and peak-pressure dependent, while sedation-rate contrasts are imprecise and hospital-dependent. Those results do not resolve the strongest remaining question: whether the two exact care-plan labels have a stable *operational meaning* across hospitals and landmark/decision times, or whether they are site- and workflow-specific documentation conventions.

The substantive advance here is to make semantic transportability, rather than a pooled label-to-outcome association, the primary estimand. A label is considered transportable only if its prevalence, timing, observable pre-D state, post-D chart trajectory, component composition, ascertainment, and independent-marker relationship are sufficiently invariant across hospitals and decision times. The audit can therefore produce a clinically useful adverse result even when no valid outcome or causal analysis is possible: it can show that an apparently common multicenter quality or CDS phenotype is not semantically comparable.

## Clinical question and hypothesis

Among the exact parent-eligible ventilated person-landmarks, do `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` encode two comparable latent care-plan states across eICU hospitals and fixed decision times, as judged by their observable documentation signatures?

The primary falsifiable hypothesis is **semantic invariance**: after conditioning descriptively on landmark and measured pre-D state, the label-specific distribution of chart-observable signatures is stable across hospitals, with no material hospital-by-label or hospital-by-label-by-landmark interaction. A secondary hypothesis is that, if labels are semantically comparable, their prevalence and decision-delay distributions should be sufficiently overlapping to permit within-hospital comparison and leave-one-hospital-out transport.

The hypothesis is not that either label causes ventilator reduction, identifies true readiness, represents an SBT, or improves an outcome. The labels are index-documentation states; the audit tests whether their observable meaning is shared.

### Evidence boundary before this experiment

The strongest evidence-supported claims from the parents are: (1) the strict cohort and exact labels can be reconstructed reproducibly; (2) independent liberation documentation is too sparse and post-event coverage too incomplete for a durable-liberation endpoint; and (3) within the only three hospitals with at least 10 rows under each label, the 12-hour setting composite was less frequent after the evaluation label, was dominated by peak inspiratory pressure, and attenuated without that component, while sedation-rate results were sparse and directionally heterogeneous. These are not evidence of a common semantic failure by themselves because they could reflect patient mix, component availability, or charting workflow.

The unresolved claim tested here is stronger and distinct: whether a common operational interpretation of the labels survives explicit hospital and decision-time measurement-invariance diagnostics. Supportive, adverse, or inconclusive results will be declared without asserting clinical truth.

## Fixed population, temporal design, and exposure

Use the exact frozen artifact `eicu_strict_cohort.csv`, [source checksum], containing 540 person-landmark rows from 443 unique ICU stays/persons: 309 evaluation and 231 no-trial. The first eligible ICU stay per `uniquepid` is retained, with all eligible landmarks in that stay. Landmarks are 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes after ICU admission.

At each landmark L, exposure is frozen as the first exact `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue` in `[L,L+360]` equal to either target label. Decision time D is that row's `cplitemoffset`; no post-D field may enter exposure or pre-D adjustment. Preserve all parent exclusions: adult status, pre-landmark invasive evidence, no pre-landmark end-of-life discussion, no prior 24-hour `Spontaneous - adequate` marker, strict pre-landmark physiology screen, no discharge at or before D, and exclusion of contradictory same-time first labels.

The primary transportability population is all 540 rows for prevalence and ascertainment descriptions, but comparative label-signature contrasts are reported in two prespecified tiers:

1. **Overlap tier:** hospitals with at least 10 observations of each exact label, expected from the completed parent result to be hospitals 248, 252, and 420 (303 rows: 130 evaluation and 173 no-trial). This tier is the only one used for hospital-by-label interaction summaries.
2. **Sparse-site tier:** every other hospital, reported individually or in shrinkage-free descriptive tables only. No sparse site is pooled as if it had within-hospital positivity.

Decision times are represented by both landmark stratum (24, 48, 72, 96, 120 hours) and continuous delay `D-L` in minutes. The latter is a documentation-latency diagnostic, not an adjustment for a post-exposure event.

## Observable semantic signature

For every person-landmark, construct a prespecified signature using only values whose time falls in the stated interval. Missing components remain unavailable and are not coded as no action.

### A. Label use and timing

For each hospital, label, and landmark report denominator, label prevalence among parent-eligible landmarks, number of unique stays/persons, D-L distribution (median and interquartile range), same-time contradictory-label count, and proportion of target-label rows with a subsequent or preceding related care-plan state. Report hospital metadata alongside, not merged into, these estimates.

### B. Pre-D state and trajectory

In `(D-1440,D)` and the two parent-defined pre-D windows `(D-1440,D-720]` and `(D-720,D]`, compute availability and median/quantiles for numeric ventilator chart components: FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, set ventilator rate, and peak inspiratory pressure, using the exact respiratory-chart labels and numeric parsing from the parent action artifact. Also compute available pre-D SpO2 (`vitalPeriodic.sao2`), respiration, systemic mean pressure, and noninvasive mean pressure. Summaries are signature descriptors, not clinical readiness measures.

### C. Post-D chart trajectory

In `(D,D+720]`, independently of the label, compute each component's paired availability and reduction indicator with the locked parent thresholds: FiO2 at least 5 percentage points, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP at least 1, pressure support at least 1, set rate at least 1, and peak inspiratory pressure at least 1. Report the broad composite, each component, and a support-core composite excluding peak pressure. For each observed change, retain the number of measurements, first qualifying offset, and whether the component is the sole contributor. This directly tests whether a site-specific component drives apparent semantic similarity.

For the same window, compute paired same-exact-drug-name rate availability and at least-10%-or-0.01-unit reduction for the locked sedative/analgesic set (propofol, fentanyl, midazolam, dexmedetomidine, lorazepam, ketamine, morphine). Rates remain drug-specific and are not treated as sedation interruption.

### D. Independent-marker relationship and ascertainment

Report care-plan spontaneous markers, RT `RT Vent On/Off` with value `Off`/`off`, and exact ETT-removal treatment strings in `(D,D+1440]`, separately and as the parent-defined union. Report marker overlap, timing separation, and whether an independent marker occurs before or after each setting/rate change. Report strict 48-hour coverage only as an ascertainment limitation using the parent's six-table coverage rule; do not recompute or reinterpret the durable endpoint.

For every signature component, report the label-specific availability rate. An absent row is not a negative observation. This separates semantic heterogeneity from differential documentation opportunity.

## Estimands and analysis

The primary estimand is the vector of hospital-specific label contrasts in observable signature components, standardized to the overlap-tier landmark distribution: for each hospital h and component k, `RD_hk = P(signature_k=1 | evaluation,h) - P(signature_k=1 | no-trial,h)`, with separate estimands for availability and documented change. The target is descriptive measurement invariance, not a causal effect.

The secondary estimand is the hospital transportability discrepancy for each component: the maximum absolute difference between `RD_hk` and the leave-one-hospital-out reference contrast, with percentile bootstrap intervals clustered by `uniquepid`. Use person-level resampling within hospital, retaining all landmarks for sampled persons, and state that only three overlap hospitals make these intervals exploratory.

Analysis proceeds in a locked sequence.

1. Reconstruct and hash-verify the 540-row cohort, exact label, hospital, landmark, and D; assert one row per source cohort key and no post-D contamination.
2. Produce label prevalence, overlap, timing, and signature availability tables for every hospital and landmark. No comparative effect is reported for sites lacking both labels.
3. In the overlap tier, calculate hospital-specific and row-count-standardized RDs for every signature component, availability, peak-excluded setting composite, broad composite, sedation-rate proxy, and independent-marker proxy.
4. Fit descriptive binomial generalized linear models or linear-probability models with hospital, landmark, and label main effects plus hospital-by-label and landmark-by-label interactions. Pre-D covariates are limited to standardized available medians of FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, set rate, peak pressure, SpO2, respiration, and MAP, and D-L; missingness indicators are included only to describe ascertainment and are not imputed as physiologic normality. Cluster uncertainty by `uniquepid`. With three hospitals, do not use conventional hospital-cluster asymptotics or claim population inference.
5. Quantify a signature-distance diagnostic using weighted absolute standardized mean differences for pre-D state, availability, and post-D components, and report component-level values rather than collapsing them into a post hoc score. A prespecified semantic-invariance flag is raised if any of the following occurs in the overlap tier: a hospital-by-label interaction 95% interval excludes zero for a primary component; all hospitals do not share the same sign for a primary component; the peak-excluded contrast differs from the broad composite by at least 5 percentage points; or label-specific availability differs by at least 10 percentage points in a component needed for the composite. These are audit thresholds, not clinical cutoffs.
6. Repeat all contrasts by landmark and by excluding each overlap hospital in turn. A leave-one-out result is a stability diagnostic only; it cannot repair sparse positivity.
7. Repeat with a falsification window `(D-1440,D]` using the same paired-change definitions where possible. The pre-D window is a trajectory diagnostic, not an untreated counterfactual. Compare post-minus-pre contrasts descriptively.

No adjusted strategy-outcome model, causal estimate, durable-liberation estimate, or bedside-action conclusion is authorized, regardless of whether semantic gates pass.

## Falsification and decision rules

### Supportive pattern

The labels may be considered *provisionally transportable as documentation phenotypes* only if, in the overlap tier, (i) both labels have adequate prevalence at each included hospital and landmark or sparse strata are explicitly excluded; (ii) availability differences for core components are below 10 percentage points; (iii) hospital-specific label contrasts have common direction and interaction uncertainty compatible with zero for all prespecified primary components; (iv) the peak-excluded and broad composites agree within 5 percentage points; (v) independent-marker prevalence and timing do not show a material label-by-hospital discrepancy; and (vi) leave-one-hospital-out contrasts remain within 10 percentage points of the pooled overlap contrast. This would justify only prospective semantic validation or use as a carefully named documentation phenotype, not a clinical recommendation.

### Adverse pattern

Semantic invariance is falsified if any one of the following is observed with adequate denominators: a component's availability or label contrast crosses the 10-point threshold in hospital-specific comparison; interaction evidence or direction reversal shows hospital-specific meaning; the composite is materially driven by one site-specific component, especially peak pressure; label prevalence or D-L is concentrated in different landmarks/sites; independent markers are absent or differentially available by site; or leave-one-out estimates materially change the sign or exceed the 10-point discrepancy threshold. The parent evidence already makes peak-pressure dependence and limited hospital overlap plausible adverse signals, but this child does not claim the new transportability result before execution.

### Inconclusive pattern

Results are inconclusive if fewer than three hospitals retain at least 10 rows per label after exact reconstruction, any primary component has fewer than 10 available rows per label in the comparison, source-time semantics cannot distinguish missingness from no documentation, or independent markers are too sparse to evaluate their relationship. Inconclusive means the common meaning is unresolved, not that labels are valid or invalid.

## Interpretation and clinical importance

A supportive result would establish only that these exact strings behave similarly as structured documentation across the observed overlap sites and decision times. It would not establish actual SBT delivery, extubation, readiness, appropriateness, causal benefit, or safe deployment in a new hospital.

An adverse result would be clinically consequential: multicenter quality dashboards or CDS systems should not count the labels as a common liberation phenotype without site-specific mapping and validation. The appropriate advance would be to require hospital-specific dictionaries, explicit device-state/SBT documentation, and measurement invariance checks before comparing rates or building decision support. An adverse finding could reflect workflow or coding conventions, patient selection, differential charting, or true management heterogeneity; this audit cannot distinguish those explanations.

An inconclusive result identifies the minimum missing evidence rather than supporting a conclusion. It would motivate prospective collection of standardized SBT start/completion, ventilator device settings and modes, extubation and reintubation timestamps, sedation hold/interruption actions, drug units, and post-ICU follow-up, with clinical adjudication across hospitals.

The completed parent results remain unchanged: durable liberation is not validated, and the prior 12-hour association is not re-estimated as an outcome claim. No result here can establish what clinicians actually did at the bedside. Clinical adjudication and another dataset are required for true extubation, SBT performance, readiness, appropriateness, post-ICU reintubation, and patient benefit.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Compared with the parents, the primary question changes from whether a label predicts a pooled 12-hour chart action to whether the label's *measurement signature* is invariant across hospitals and decision times. The frozen cohort, D, exact strings, negative durable-liberation finding, and prior action algorithms are preserved. New outputs are explicitly stratified by hospital and landmark, include label and component availability, documentation latency, independent-marker relationships, and leave-one-hospital-out stability, and use pre-D trajectories as diagnostics rather than controls.

The principal uncertainty is that only three hospitals have adequate within-hospital label overlap and independent markers are sparse. Therefore even a clean audit can support at most provisional transportability of a documentation phenotype. It cannot support a common latent clinical construct or bedside/causal claim.
