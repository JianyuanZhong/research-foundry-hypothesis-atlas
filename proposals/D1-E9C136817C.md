# Versioned cutoff-valid bridge for patient-level HCC review queues

## 1. Successor and unresolved clinical claim

This is a targeted child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent repaired an important problem: equal aggregate documentary-M2 capture can conceal substitution of the patients offered scarce review. It therefore introduced q_dec, the exact patient-level conditional coverage threshold at which a fixed-K semantic queue equals a same-world maximum-stored-content queue.

The remaining gap is the bridge from stored eventual examination text to a usable preoperative service. A patient-level lower bound on complete exposure does not prove that an archive supplied every component of the same examination accession, the same immutable report version, or that it was finalized and technically retrievable by the 24-hour decision time. HCC contains neither report-version identifiers nor finalization, release, retrieval, readability, amendment, retraction, or view timestamps.

The clinical question is:

> For adults approaching a first eligible HCC resection, can a fixed-capacity review queue based on report semantics preserve the same patients selected by the maximum stored-content queue under the worst compatible HCC source states, and what versioned archive evidence is required before that result can inform workflow?

The decision remains allocation of scarce review, not resection extent, transplantation, systemic/local treatment, surveillance, or any patient-level treatment recommendation.

The strongest currently supported claim is structural: the HCC snapshot contains encounter-linked procedures, acquisition-like CT/MRI examination times, multirow accession-linked narrative fields, and same-encounter pathology narratives. The parent’s internal stored-content claim is falsifiable. Neither the HCC source nor the public search establishes biological MVI, actual report availability, clinician viewing/action, benefit, or utility.

## 2. Hypothesis and estimands

The confirmatory internal hypothesis at rho=0.10 and d=24 hours is:

> Maximum stored report content passes every inherited comparator, random-review, and anchored-singleton-deletion gate in both 2020 and 2021; q_req<1; and q_dec<1, where q_dec is the smallest exact patient-complete conditional bundle-coverage threshold for which those gates and fixed-K patient-set equality hold in every compatible coupled state satisfying Q>=q.

q_req and q_dec are finite-corpus documentary quantities, not hospital coverage rates. q_dec compares the G queue with a same-world maximum-stored-content G oracle using the same source/operation world, accession set, reader/pathology books, fit, deletion state, score serialization, tie key and original K positions. Patient-set equality is primary; ordered-slot disagreement is diagnostic.

The child adds a separately reported external bridge. For each HCC-derived eligible accession u=(Patient master index,Encounter number,Examination number), an archive is cutoff-valid only if all raw examination components map one-to-one to immutable archive component/version IDs, a fixed canonical content hash matches, the complete version was finalized, technically retrievable and readable by t_op-24 hours, it was not retracted by then, timezone/clock provenance is recorded, and no unresolved amendment, competing version, missing component or post-cutoff substitution remains. The earliest time at which all components meet those conditions is the accession onset.

For patient i in year y and source world omega, external completeness is 1 only when every u in the frozen HCC eligible set A_i,omega is versioned, hash-matched and cutoff-valid. The bridge denominator remains the HCC-derived A, never an archive-defined denominator. Retain nonresponders and estimate year-specific design-based simultaneous lower bounds L_2020 and L_2021; define q_ext,L=min(L_2020,L_2021). q_ext,L>=q_dec is required for a patient-level archive bridge. q_ext,L>=q_req is sufficient only for the narrower aggregate-capture bridge.

If archive timestamps or versions admit multiple compatible states, every state must pass. A favorable archive completion cannot be selected.

## 6. Outcome partition and falsification

Emit one primary internal label:

- computationally_inconclusive: any lossless replay, source/schema/hash, whole-accession grouping, clock, coupled-state, score/tie, frontier, witness, deletion or verifier check fails.
- feasibility_inconclusive: N/K/event/n_A or structural denominator requirements fail.
- maximum_content_signal_adverse: maximum content fails an inherited comparator, random or deletion gate.
- conditional_coverage_adverse: q_req is undefined or equals 1.
- mixed_capture_supportive_queue_adverse: q_req<1 but q_dec is undefined or equals 1; aggregate capture survives while patient selection does not.
- documentary_decision_supportive_bridge_pending: q_dec<1, its immediately lower attainable threshold fails when one exists, all internal witnesses and uncertainty pass, but no valid external archive bridge is available. This is finite-corpus stored-content support only.
- documentary_decision_and_archive_bridge_supported: the preceding internal support holds and q_ext,L>=q_dec with complete year-specific simultaneous bounds and exact component/version/hash/cutoff mapping.
- archive_bridge_adverse: internal support holds but the external archive’s proven cutoff-valid patient coverage is below q_dec.
- archive_bridge_inconclusive: external identity, version, component completeness, timestamp or clock provenance is unresolved.

Supportive internal results do not establish report viewing, biological MVI, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, fairness, transportability or patient utility. Favorable sensitivity cutoffs cannot rescue or veto the 24-hour primary result. A point estimate, AUC, pooled-year coverage, eventual nonempty text or q_ext,L>=q_req cannot support the patient-level bridge.

## 7. External evidence and verifier contract

The external audit must use the frozen HCC-derived patient/accession frame and retain all nonresponders. It must provide patient/encounter/accession crosswalk, raw examination row ordinals and hashes, immutable report-version/amendment/retraction IDs, component-complete canonical content hashes, authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrievable/readable timestamps with timezone and clock provenance, operation-clock mapping, locked input hash and patient I=0/incomplete/complete disposition. It must not redefine the population, modality, operation, pathology or A denominator. If archive ambiguity remains, evaluate all compatible states.

Workspace outputs include source/header/hash/replay and raw-component ledgers, accession-grain and operation-clock audits, coupled-state/onset ledger, patient-complete strata, q_req and q_dec exact witnesses, fixed-K queue signatures/set differences, deletion logs, interpretation.json and external_bridge_audit.json. The external audit is separate from HCC; HCC itself will normally emit bridge-pending because the required archive columns do not exist.

The verifier must independently check all 12 HCC source hashes/schemas, BOM/raw ordinals, accession grouping, operation intervals, protected keys, model/hash/tie replay, same-world oracle comparison, no-backfill deletion, exact PASS and DEC_PASS frontiers, q_dec>=q_req, external component/version conditions and conclusion-to-output links. Fixtures must include a multirow accession with one component available, later amended text, a retracted version, distinct raw rows with duplicate text, timezone/boundary intervals, pooled coverage with one-year failure, equal aggregate capture with different patient queues, archive hash mismatch, missing nonresponders and q_req-passing but q_dec-failing coverage.

The verifier can establish computation, provenance, exact stored-content queue validity, and whether prose follows outputs. It cannot establish Chinese clinical semantics, reviewer qualifications, actual release/viewing, specimen linkage/sampling adequacy, biological M2, clinical action, treatment effect or patient benefit. Those require expert adjudication, source-system evidence, stakeholder review, prospective workflow/outcome study or another dataset.

## 8. Advance and remaining uncertainty

The substantive advance is a version-faithful bridge from the parent’s patient-level stored-content queue predicate to the evidence needed for a real preoperative service. It prevents a pooled or accession-count archive percentage from masquerading as component-complete, cutoff-valid patient coverage. It preserves the parent’s clinically meaningful patient-substitution falsification and makes the next data collection auditable.

The proposal remains deliberately conditional: absent an external report archive, HCC can falsify or support only the finite historical stored-content predicate. It cannot claim that reports were available to clinicians, that documentary M2 is biological MVI, or that queueing changes outcomes.
