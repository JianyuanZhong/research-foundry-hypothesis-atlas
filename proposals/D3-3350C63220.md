The unresolved claim is stronger than an ordinary group difference: after accounting for contributing donors and repeated context measurements, the positive-macrophage contrast should be specific to the macrophage-condition comparison and should not be reproduced by donor, macrophage composition/ratio, treatment, batch, or targeted-panel artifacts. This is a hypothesis, not an executed result. The inherited evidence supports the plausibility of reciprocal macrophage–FLS communication and MerTK-associated resolution in RA synovium, but does not establish this assay’s response, direct ligand-receptor action, causality, or clinical benefit.
