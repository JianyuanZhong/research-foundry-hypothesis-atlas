# Is the retrospective pulmonary-nodule handoff route transportable, or is it a subgroup/documentation artifact?

## Decision question and targeted repair

The parent defines a conservative, admission-level route from departure-frozen MIMIC evidence: clinician-confirmed structured propagation only for admissions whose observably open unresolved recommendation units are all reproducible pure omissions with adequate unit-specific storage opportunity; clinician reconciliation for incomplete, conflicting, mixed, or interaction-dependent plans; timing-limited, resolved/no-action, and defer states remain separate. That is a clinically defensible *nomination rule*, not an appropriateness judgment or an intervention.

A remaining threat is hidden concentration. A pooled `A_AUTO` or `A_RECON` burden could be generated almost entirely by one coarse era, terminal service, admission context, document format, reviewer pair, or missing-context pattern. Pooled agreement can coexist with unstable routes in the very contexts where a future silent-validation study would operate. MIMIC contains only one institution and no live workflow data, but it can falsify the weaker claim that the retrospective nomination is internally transportable across its observable environments.

This child therefore preserves the parent's frozen denominator, chronology, unique canonical discharge artifact, recommendation-unit clocks, evidence states, worst-case admission aggregation, uncertainty bounds, and noncausal limits. It adds a **crossed-reader, leave-one-observable-domain-out transport audit** of the already frozen deterministic route. No predictive model is trained and no subgroup-specific rule is optimized. The audit asks whether one common retrospective nomination survives genuine held-out coarse eras, services, admission contexts, documentation regimes, and missing-context stress tests after overlap restrictions and simultaneous multiplicity control.

The strongest evidence presently supported remains only source feasibility: a complete, zero-sampling metadata scan found 546,028 admissions, 364,627 patients, 593,071 service rows covering 546,012 admissions, 94,458 ICU stays in 85,242 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology notes (`RR` 2,295,635; `AR` 25,720), 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, and 331,794 unique `DS` admission keys, with 17 missing `DS storetime` values. The released `anchor_year_group` has five populated bands (2008–2010 through 2020–2022), admissions span nine `admission_type` values, and `curr_service` spans 21 codes. These are whole-table structural counts, not counts of adjudicated pulmonary opportunities or routes. The parent also inherited a 3,764-report pulmonary retrieval proxy; it is not an adjudicated denominator.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among the unchanged first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity admissions in the parent's ample, departure-available stratum, the frozen admission route and prospective-validation priority are stable under initial-reviewer-compatible and unknown-adverse bounds across evaluable observable era, terminal-service, admission-context, and documentation-regime domains. A route is transportable only if each held-out domain independently preserves the nomination, route-specific burdens differ by no more than prospectively ratified stability margins on common support, uncertainty does not concentrate in any domain, and no reviewer or missing-context pattern drives the result.

A supportive result would justify only a prospective silent-validation study that is explicitly stratified by these same environments. It would not establish clinical appropriateness, safety, benefit, external-site transportability, or deployment readiness.

## Frozen denominator, reciprocal chronology, canonical artifact, clocks, and routes

Before discharge text, `DS_store`, route outcomes, or transport domains derived from discharge formatting are opened, recreate and hash the parent's pulmonary eligibility manifest. The primary denominator `N_A` remains the first eligible admission per subject:

* adult; exact admission-linked radiology `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional or optional plans, explicit no-follow-up, in-hospital death, the locked hospice/comfort-only transition, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* never infer nodule size, smoking, malignancy risk, life expectancy, preference, recommendation appropriateness, or missing context.

Eligibility reviewers remain blinded to discharge data and every route/domain outcome. Reconstruct report/addendum chains only through reciprocal, subject-consistent, admission-consistent detail links. Every operative component must have `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`; only a later material pre-`D` component in the reciprocal chain supersedes an earlier component. Missing nodes, nonreciprocal/contradictory links, unresolved operative components, or missing/contradictory recommendation times remain `U_R`, retained in the cascade and bounds and never called omissions.

The canonical artifact remains the unique exact `note_type=="DS"` row per `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. Any duplicate in the confirmatory extraction stops the run. Missing artifact, text, linkage, or `storetime`, and unreadable/contradictory content remain unknown. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

For all `N_A`, preserve unchanged:

* departure state `Y={F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}` (faithful; omitted; incomplete/discordant; text-supported alternate resolution; known `DS_store>D`; unknown), with all cells summing to `N_A` and delayed fidelity unable to overwrite `A`;
* `R_store`, `DS_store`, `G=DS_store-R_store` hours;
* `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, eventual `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}`, and every zero/unknown cell of `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A`;
* primary ample departure-available stratum `E_A={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. No `G<0`, `DS_store>D`, or unknown-clock row enters it. Verify `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` row-wise, but only `G` defines opportunity.

Preserve the parent's radiology-only atomic recommendation inventory `U=(target cluster,action/modality,due interval/date,recommendation polarity)`, generated without discharge outcome. Shared target/action/interval is one unit; distinct actions or windows are separate; reciprocal restatements are deduplicated; a material addendum updates rather than duplicates a unit. `U_BOUNDARY` retains both minimum- and maximum-unit compatible inventories. Each unit retains `(subject_id,hadm_id,unit_id,note_id,operative component,R_store,target span,action,interval/date,boundary provenance)` and `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u`. `N_R` remains nested in `N_A`; the admission remains the decision, multiplicity, and resampling unit.

No post-`D` content, POE, downstream encounter, or outcome is visible. Keep every initial label; third-party adjudication never erases the compatible set.

## Observable domains frozen before route labels

A domain manifest is generated after the eligibility/unit manifest but before any clinical route label is released. Exact raw values and prespecified families are both published; no sparse level may be merged after its outcome is seen.

### 1. Coarse observable era (`E`)

Use exact released `patients.anchor_year_group` values: `2008 - 2010`, `2011 - 2013`, `2014 - 2016`, `2017 - 2019`, `2020 - 2022`, plus missing/other. This is a documentation-era proxy only. Do not use shifted `year(admittime)` to order different subjects, estimate a yearly slope, or claim secular causation.

### 2. Terminal service (`SVC`)

Primary groups are exact last-pre-`D` `curr_service` codes. A locked descriptive family sensitivity maps:

* medicine: `MED,CMED,OMED,NMED`;
* general/specialty surgery: `SURG,CSURG,NSURG,ORTHO,PSURG,TSURG,VSURG,TRAUM,GU,GYN,ENT,EYE,DENT`;
* observation: `OBS`;
* psychiatry: `PSYCH`;
* other/missing: every unlisted or absent value.

Results must show exact-code estimates before family estimates. A family cannot rescue an adverse adequately sized code-level holdout.

### 3. Admission context (`CTX`)

Use exact `admission_type` as the primary axis. Report locked families secondarily: planned/procedural (`ELECTIVE`,`SURGICAL SAME DAY ADMISSION`), emergency/urgent (`EW EMER.`,`DIRECT EMER.`,`URGENT`), and observation (`AMBULATORY OBSERVATION`,`DIRECT OBSERVATION`,`EU OBSERVATION`,`OBSERVATION ADMIT`). Independently audit ICU exposure (yes/no), admission location (`EMERGENCY ROOM`, transfer, referral/procedure, other/missing), and discharge-location family (home/home-health; facility/rehab/SNF/acute hospital; against advice; hospice/death eligibility audit; other/missing). Cross-products are exploratory unless they satisfy the intersection gates below.

### 4. Documentation regime (`DOC`)

Documentation regimes are outcome-blind, mechanically derived, and locked before adjudication:

* radiology chain: `RR_only` versus reciprocal `RR_AR` chain versus unresolved;
* radiology storage lag `R_store-charttime`: `<0`, `0–6h`, `6–24h`, `>24h`, missing;
* radiology surface format: presence/ordered sequence of exact line-initial headings from the locked list `EXAMINATION,INDICATION,TECHNIQUE,COMPARISON,FINDINGS,IMPRESSION`, reduced to `standard_complete` (both FINDINGS and IMPRESSION), `impression_only`, `other`, or parser-unknown;
* discharge surface format after target-plan spans are masked: line-initial heading skeleton from a locked list, reduced without body semantics to `structured>=4 headings`, `limited 1–3`, `none`, or parser-unknown; and target-masked character-count quartile, with cut points frozen on all unique pre-`D` canonical `DS` artifacts before labels.

The heading parser uses capitalization, delimiters, and line boundaries only; it cannot use route words, target-plan presence, reviewer labels, or eventual text. Two independent auditors review a stratified random 100-document parser sample while blinded to routes. More than 5% heading-regime error or any target-plan leakage blocks documentation-transport claims. `discharge_detail.field_name` is not used because inspection showed it contains only `author`.

### 5. Missing-context pattern (`MISS`)

Before Panel A/B outcomes, define a bit vector for missing/invalid released context: absent terminal service at/before `D`, blank `discharge_location`, absent admission location/type, missing ICU linkage status due to key failure (distinct from verified no ICU), missing/unparseable target-masked DS skeleton, unresolved reciprocal chain, `U_BOUNDARY`, and redaction/packet-integrity failure. Report each bit separately and a locked `complete` versus `any_missing` contrast. `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, and reviewer disagreement are outcomes of the audit, not ingredients used to define a favorable complete-case domain; their concentration by every predeclared domain is reported separately.

## Crossed-reader adjudication transport

A single agreeing reviewer pair can create false stability. For each clinical panel recruit at least four qualified readers. Randomize admissions, not recommendation units, to balanced reader pairs, stratified by the five era bands, exact/service family, exact admission type, ICU, unit multiplicity, and documentation regime. All units in an admission stay with the same pair. At least `max(60,20% of E_A)` bridge admissions, sampled without route information and balanced across domains, are read independently by every reader in that panel. Each reader must see at least 30 `E_A` admissions and at least 10 bridge admissions in every domain for which a reader-domain interaction is claimed; otherwise that interaction is non-evaluable.

Readers remain blinded exactly as in the parent. The route is generated by frozen code, not reader vote. Primary labels are the set of all initial-reader-compatible assignments. Consensus is secondary. Report reader-specific route burdens, pair-specific confusion matrices, category agreement, kappa and Gwet AC1, and variance components for admission, reader, and reader-by-domain as diagnostics. Leave-one-reader-out must preserve the nomination in every evaluable domain. No estimated reader effect is used to “correct” labels or train a classifier.

## Estimands: transport of a fixed nomination, not prediction

Let `r` be `AUTO`, `RECON` (with `MIXED`), `DEFER`, `TIMING`, or `NOACTION`; let `g` index a group on one domain axis. The raw domain burden is

`B_r(g)=100 * n(E_A and domain=g and A_r) / N_A(g)`,

where `N_A(g)` is all frozen index admissions in that domain, so non-`E_A` chronology remains visible. Also report the within-opportunity composition

`P_r(g)=n(E_A and domain=g and A_r)/n(E_A and domain=g)`.

For `AUTO`-relevant groups report `D_MIX(g)` exactly as in the parent. For every quantity compute:

1. consensus estimate (secondary);
2. extrema over all initial-reader-compatible assignments;
3. extrema additionally assigning route-relevant `K_UNKNOWN,S_UN,U_BOUNDARY`, unknown content, and missing packet facts adversely;
4. minimum/maximum recommendation-unit inventories.

The primary heterogeneity estimands for axis `a` are:

* `H_r(a)=max_{g,h}|P_r(g)-P_r(h)|` on evaluable groups;
* `H_MIX(a)=max_{g,h}|D_MIX(g)-D_MIX(h)|`;
* `U_a=max_g P(A_DEFER | E_A,g)` and the maximum domain excess over the pooled defer fraction;
* `C_a`, the fraction of evaluable leave-one-domain-out folds preserving the same priority under all compatible assignments.

Raw differences answer whether current MIMIC environments differ. To distinguish obvious case-mix concentration from lack of overlap, also estimate a **descriptive common-support standardized** version. For each axis separately, balance each group to the pooled `E_A` distribution on variables not defining that axis: age band (`18–64,65–79,>=80`), sex as recorded, 3/6/12-month interval, `H_24_72/H_72plus`, one versus multiple recommendation units, ICU, exact admission-type family, terminal-service family, discharge-location family, radiology addendum status, and target-masked DS length quartile. When service or admission context is the axis, omit its own variable from balancing. Use entropy balancing fixed before outcomes; this is not a causal adjustment. Publish unweighted results first, weights, standardized differences, maximum normalized weight, effective sample size, and discarded/nonoverlap cells.

An axis has adequate overlap only if each evaluated group has at least 30 `E_A` admissions, at least 10 `A_AUTO+A_RECON` compatible events, weighted effective sample size at least 25, maximum normalized weight no greater than 0.10, and every balanced absolute standardized difference no greater than 0.10. At least three groups must pass for era, service, admission type, and documentation axes; binary stress axes require both groups. Failure yields “not transport-evaluable,” never pooling by convenience.

### Leave-one-domain-out internal-external audit

This is an audit of a deterministic policy, not model cross-validation:

1. For each eligible group `g` on one axis, withhold all admissions in `g`.
2. Apply the unchanged parent priority rule and all reliability/purity/event gates to the remaining groups, carrying full reviewer/unknown bounds. No thresholds, route crosswalks, heading rules, weights, or mappings may be refit.
3. Unlock `g`; calculate its route burdens, uncertainty, `D_MIX`, and priority independently.
4. A fold passes only when the held-out group supports the same nomination (auto-first, reconciliation-first, two-lane, reconciliation-only, or no selection), satisfies the parent's route-specific event/precision and purity gates, and its common-support route burdens stay within the ratified stability margins.

The stability margins are not utilities: before labels are unlocked, governance must ratify `5 admissions per 100 E_A` for each of `P_AUTO` and `P_RECON`, `5 per 100 E_A` for defer excess, and `0.10` for `D_MIX`, or replace them with stricter prespecified values. If margins are not prospectively ratified, report heterogeneity and folds but make no “transportable” nomination. A favorable average cannot compensate for one adequately powered adverse held-out group.

## Multiplicity, partial identification, and gates

Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates; all admissions/units for a subject share a weight. Each replicate carries inherited `Y,J_A`, route burdens, unit inventories, every reader-compatible/unknown bound, balancing weights, held-out folds, and calibration controls. A two-way subject-and-reader bootstrap is a sensitivity for crossed-reader quantities.

One max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers the decision-facing endpoints across all predeclared axes: `P_AUTO,P_RECON,P_DEFER,D_MIX`, their maximum pairwise differences, and held-out fold pass/fail margins. Exact code levels that meet gates are included. ICU, admission-location, discharge-location, and the four documentation features are included as named secondary axes in the same family. Pairwise intersection tables use Benjamini-Hochberg FDR at 0.05 and can only find vulnerability, never rescue a primary transport claim.

Preserve every parent feasibility gate (`N_A>=200`, `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, no duplicate canonical `DS`, at least 60 `E_A`, route event counts, simultaneous precision, <=10% combined unknowns, redaction and reviewer reliability, threshold and POE non-leakage). A transport claim additionally requires:

* at least three overlap-passing groups per multicategory primary axis and both groups per binary axis;
* at least 30 `E_A` and 10 route events in each held-out group, plus the parent's 30-route-event gate for any route-specific priority claimed in that group;
* simultaneous interval half-width <=5 admissions per 100 `E_A` for held-out `P_AUTO/P_RECON/P_DEFER` and <=10 percentage points for `D_MIX`;
* all compatible and unknown-adverse bounds within ratified margins;
* no held-out fold priority reversal, no leave-one-reader-out reversal, and no parser/redactor/formatter effect that crosses a margin;
* no domain with `P_DEFER>0.10` or decision-changing disagreement >0.10 unless the simultaneous upper excess over pooled is <=0 and the global priority is unchanged under adverse assignment;
* the same conclusion in raw and common-support analyses. A sign reversal or margin crossing between them is adverse; overlap failure is inconclusive.

Counts below gates are not evidence of stability or absence. “No selection” in a held-out group is a failed transport fold if the training groups selected a route; universal no-selection can be a valid adverse result against retrospective nomination.

## Falsification and adversarial verifier rules

1. Recompute all source hashes/schemas and complete-source counts; stop on drift, duplicate canonical `DS`, denominator-hash change, impossible time, nonreciprocal operative chain, or marginal not summing to `N_A`.
2. Domain labels and documentation parser version/hash must be frozen before route labels. Any post-outcome regrouping, sparse-cell merging, margin tuning, or choice of “best” era/service invalidates transport claims.
3. Shifted `year(admittime)` cannot define cross-subject era. `anchor_year_group` may be called only a coarse observable era proxy.
4. The last service row must satisfy `transfertime<=D`; a synthetic post-`D` service transfer cannot alter `SVC`.
5. The DS heading parser may use line form only. Inserting/removing pulmonary plan words without changing headings must not alter `DOC`; changing target content must not reassign a documentation regime.
6. `discharge_detail.field_name` cannot define DS sections. The current source contains only `author` and verifier fixtures enforce this.
7. Withholding a domain must not refit eligibility, unit boundaries, route mappings, family mappings, parsers, margins, or purity rules. There is no outcome-prediction model or AUC.
8. An average pass with one adequately sized held-out reversal is adverse, not “mostly transportable.” A sparse heldout is inconclusive and cannot be omitted from the denominator of folds without being shown.
9. Duplicating recommendation units cannot change admission burdens, domain membership, or bootstrap weights. One omission plus one conflict remains `A_MIXED` within `A_RECON` in every domain.
10. Consensus cannot repair a reviewer-domain reversal. Primary extrema use all initial labels; leave-one-reader-out and all-reader bridge results must be shown.
11. Complete-case stability with an `any_missing` reversal or defer excess is adverse to transport. Missing cases cannot be silently excluded or assigned favorable routes.
12. Failure of entropy balance, excessive weights, or raw-versus-standardized reversal makes that axis inconclusive/adverse as prespecified; extrapolated estimates cannot support transport.
13. All multiplicity-adjusted intervals and zero/unknown cells are emitted. Selecting only `AUTO` or only a favorable family fails verification.
14. Parent chronology remains immutable: no `G<0`, stale, post-`D`, unknown-clock, later faithful text, POE, or downstream outcome can upgrade a route or held-out fold.
15. Route stability must never be rendered as appropriateness, expected benefit, safety, equal urgency, clinician failure, or external validity.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive for internal transportability and the next prospective silent-validation study** requires all parent gates plus all overlap, multiplicity, parser, crossed-reader, partial-identification, and leave-one-domain-out gates. Every evaluable held-out era, service, exact admission type, and documentation axis must preserve the same retrospective priority; raw and common-support estimates must stay inside ratified margins; missing-context and reviewer stress tests must not reverse it. This supports freezing one candidate gate for a prospective silent study, with mandatory stratified accrual and reporting in these domains.

**Supportive but restricted** occurs when the nomination passes only a clearly prespecified subset (for example medicine services with standard-complete documents) while adequately sized other domains fail. The conclusion is not “transportable”; it is that a future silent study, if pursued, must restrict initial evaluation to the passing context and separately investigate failures. It cannot deploy there.

**Adverse to pooled retrospective nomination** includes any adequately powered held-out priority reversal, route-burden difference beyond a ratified margin, `D_MIX` purity failure in one group, concentration of defer/disagreement/missingness above gates, a reader-domain or parser/redactor effect crossing a margin, or stability that exists only after nonoverlap weighting or favorable consensus. This means the pooled route is too context-dependent to nominate one general prospective gate; it does not prove that either workflow is clinically harmful.

**Inconclusive** includes sparse domains/events, wide simultaneous intervals, absent governance margins, overlap/ESS failure, excessive weights, parser audit failure, source/key/clock failure, >10% uncertainty, insufficient crossed-reader bridges, or incompatible raw and standardized results. Sparse services/eras remain visibly non-evaluable rather than being merged away. Comparator or institutional-calibration failure remains limited to those claims and cannot erase an otherwise valid pulmonary context finding.

## What is computable and what remains unavailable

MIMIC can compute frozen opportunity and chronology states; recommendation multiplicity and unit clocks; departure-frozen text-supported routes; domain-specific route burdens; reader-, missingness-, and documentation-regime uncertainty; common-support descriptive standardization; and whether a fixed retrospective nomination survives observable held-out domains within this snapshot.

MIMIC cannot establish recommendation guideline appropriateness, clinical necessity, true supersession, actual report finalization/viewing, author awareness, UI exposure, complete competing illness/preferences, outside plans, responsibility, order/referral/scheduling completion, communication, malignancy, harm, utilities, causal mechanisms, intervention effects, or patient benefit. It is one institution; even perfect internal transport does not prove external-site transport. `anchor_year_group` does not identify exact secular changes, and documentation regimes are observable proxies rather than EHR software/version metadata.

After a supportive result, the next study remains prospective and silent. It must enroll consecutive eligible discharges; capture immutable report finalization/addendum and viewing, artifact draft/version/edit/sign/transmission, trigger time, responsibility, target-linked order/referral/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing-risk context, and final departure artifact; and adjudicate routes blinded to the silent system. Accrual and calibration must be stratified by era/site, service, admission context, and documentation regime, with at least 100 route-positive admissions and at least 30 per claimed route/domain or a maximum accrual period. Only after silent validation could a governed route-stratified workflow trial be designed.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, this child does not change the clinical population, chronology, outcome states, recommendation units, admission routing, purity rule, or noncausal interpretation. It repairs the pooled-result vulnerability by requiring observable-domain internal-external validation, overlap diagnostics, simultaneous heterogeneity bounds, missing-context stress tests, and a crossed-reader design. It makes “transportable” a demanding falsifiable claim rather than an impression from pooled agreement, and it can reveal a restricted setting in which silent validation is defensible versus a context-dependent gate that should not advance.

The fundamental uncertainty remains: stable documentation-based routing is not clinical truth. Even a fully supportive result nominates only a prospective silent validation of a clinician-mediated gate; it does not infer appropriateness, safety, benefit, or deployment readiness.
