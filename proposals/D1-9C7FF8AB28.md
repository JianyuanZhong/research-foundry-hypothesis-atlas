## Byte-level unit identity and deterministic H1/H2/H3 matching

All parsing uses UTF-8, Unicode NFC, ASCII LF, no locale-dependent sorting, and source text retained byte-for-byte. Outer whitespace is trimmed only where the schema contract says so. Identifiers are strings and never passed through floating point. Timestamps are UTC-naive ISO-8601 with six fractional digits when present; null is JSON `null`, distinct from `""`, `UNKNOWN`, and `ABSENT`.

The canonical unit identity is

```text
U=(subject_id,hadm_id,source_note_id,reciprocal_chain_id,unit_ordinal)
```

where `source_note_id=(subject_id,note_id)`, `reciprocal_chain_id` is emitted by the inherited reciprocal parser, and `unit_ordinal` is zero-based in the locked extracted chain. A non-unique or un-emittable ordinal produces `U_BOUNDARY`; it is retained adverse-compatibly, not dropped. Chain order is `(storetime,charttime,note_seq,note_id)` with nulls after non-null values and literal note-key tie-break. No note text, row order, provider, author, route, or atom may define identity.

Each panel emits the exact assertion tuple

```text
(U,target_cluster,action_modality,due_interval,polarity,recipient,responsibility,replacement,conflict)
```

with each non-identity field equal to a locked coded value, `ABSENT`, or `UNKNOWN`. H1 and H2 independently emit ordered unit inventories and tuples. H3 emits an independent support/contradiction/indeterminate assessment for each already serialized unit and the eight-fact evidence vector; H3 does not create, delete, merge, or reorder units and does not enter the H1/H2 inventory reproducibility numerator.

### Matching function

Matching is a pure function with no fuzzy similarity, majority vote, semantic reviewer override, or route input. First require the same exact admission key. For reciprocal restatement matching, require identical complete `U`; identical `U` plus identical complete assertion tuple is `DUPLICATE_RESTATEMENT`, otherwise the records remain separate versions/states. Distinct note IDs, chain IDs, ordinals, or competing targets remain distinct units even when text is identical.

For target linkage across H1/H2, compare in this fixed order:
`(target_cluster,action_modality,due_interval,polarity,recipient,responsibility)`. For each field: identical coded values are `MATCH`; `ABSENT` versus any non-`ABSENT` value is `NONMATCH`; distinct known coded values are `NONMATCH`; either-side `UNKNOWN` retains both `MATCH` and `NONMATCH` branches with reason `UNKNOWN_WILDCARD`. `replacement` and `conflict` are independent: any difference retains separate states and is adverse-compatible. The pair result is `MATCH`, `NONMATCH`, `AMBIGUOUS_MATCH`, `DUPLICATE_RESTATEMENT`, or `DISTINCT_UNIT`, with a proof trace. One-to-many mappings are retained as all admissible branches, never forced to the most favorable mapping.

The deterministic H1/H2 inventory reproducibility indicator is defined before labels as

```text
D_REPRO(i)=1 iff
  sorted canonical H1 inventory and sorted canonical H2 inventory
  have identical admission key, unit identities, and complete assertion tuples
  after only DUPLICATE_RESTATEMENT collapse;
D_REPRO(i)=0 otherwise.
```

`UNKNOWN` causes `D_REPRO=0` unless the two complete serialized tuples are identical including `UNKNOWN`; it is never wildcard-matched into a reproducible positive. H3 is excluded from `D_REPRO` by design and is reported separately as support concordance and evidence-gap coding. H1/H2/H3 outputs remain separate measurement systems for compatible-set analysis; no panel is a gold standard.

## Label-independent actionability eligibility and complete NA/adverse table

Eligibility is computed after the frozen source frame and canonical DS selection but before H1/H2/H3 labels, routes, atoms, disagreements, bridge success, weights, or challenge allocation. `BRIDGE_ELIGIBLE(i,u)=1` iff: the admission key is unique and in the frame; `U` is unique, reciprocal-chain-resolved, pre-`D`, and has non-null `R_store<D`; the extractor emits nonempty source text and an unambiguous target/action/due structural envelope; the canonical `DS` exists uniquely with nonempty text, non-null note ID and `DS_store`; DS detail is either uniquely keyed or explicitly flagged ambiguous; and the join is exact on `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. Failed conditions are retained in a pre-label eligibility table.

The complete two-field coding contract is:

| eligibility code | actionability code | exact condition | estimand treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| `NA_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | `NA_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | structural predicate false: no unit, unresolved chain, missing required clock, absent/nonunique DS, malformed envelope, or bad key | excluded only from the predeclared eligible-pair target-preservation denominator; counted and weighted separately; never success/failure |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `NA_NO_TARGET` | eligible structural pair but locked parser explicitly codes target envelope `ABSENT` | excluded from target-bearing denominator; reported as an NA subtype; not route `NOACTION` |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `NA_NO_ACTION` | target envelope exists but codebook explicitly declares no action modality, outside this actionability construct | excluded from target-preservation denominator; never clinical inaction and never route `NOACTION` |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `ACTIVE_EXACT` | exact target, action, due interval and compatible polarity/recipient/responsibility are represented in DS with no conflict/replacement | favorable value only for exact-preservation functional |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `ACTIVE_PARTIAL` | target linked but required action or due field is absent/unknown | adverse-compatible; descriptive partial state retained |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `SUPERSEDED_EXPLICIT` | DS explicitly documents replacement/supersession or conflict | adverse; never successful propagation |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `TARGET_CONTRADICTED` | DS explicitly negates or contradicts target/action | adverse |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `TIMING_UNRESOLVED` | required chronology or due interval cannot be resolved after eligibility | adverse-compatible; no imputation |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `DS_UNREADABLE` | stored DS/detail exists but is malformed, inaccessible, or packet-integrity fails | adverse-compatible; not complete-case deletion |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `NONRESPONSE` | required reader output absent or malformed | adverse-compatible missingness |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `DETAIL_AMBIGUOUS` | duplicate/conflicting detail rows prevent unique interpretation | adverse-compatible; no favorable tie-break |
| `ELIGIBLE` | `BRIDGE_INCONCLUSIVE` | eligible evidence cannot distinguish the preceding states | inconclusive/adverse-compatible |

`NA_*` is permitted only for the listed structural or explicit absence states. It is forbidden for nonresponse, disagreement, unreadable text, timing conflict, detail ambiguity, contradiction, replacement, or unfavorable results. A zero-unit admission and a route `DEFER` admission do not receive an actionability NA merely because a route is absent or adverse: zero-unit is recorded as `NA_NOT_ELIGIBLE` for the unit-level bridge and route `NOACTION` remains descriptive; a structurally eligible `DEFER` unit is coded by evidence (`ACTIVE_PARTIAL`, `TIMING_UNRESOLVED`, `NONRESPONSE`, or other applicable adverse state), never by route. The all-opportunity eligibility count and each NA subtype are reported rather than silently removed.

## Falsification fixtures and gates

Before real labels, materialize and hash the truth table, unit/match fixtures, availability manifest, eligibility table, codebooks, allocation, serializer and ledger. The verifier must assert:

1. identical `U` and tuple collapses only as `DUPLICATE_RESTATEMENT`; same text with a different note ID/ordinal remains distinct;
2. every UNKNOWN target field yields both match branches, while ABSENT versus coded is NONMATCH;
3. H1/H2 inventory comparison is invariant to row reordering, locale, and worker order, and H3 cannot alter `D_REPRO`;
4. null, empty, UNKNOWN and ABSENT serialize distinctly;
5. malformed/nonunique ordinal becomes `U_BOUNDARY`, retained adverse-compatibly;
6. the source audit assigns all eight listed facts `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` in this MIMIC snapshot, while a synthetic available-but-null field maps to `UNKNOWN`;
7. absent DS is `(NA_NOT_ELIGIBLE,NA_NOT_ELIGIBLE)`, explicit no target/action map to their NA subtypes, and a missing reader output is `NONRESPONSE`;
8. exact, partial, replacement, contradiction, unresolved timing, unreadable and ambiguous detail map to their exact adverse codes;
9. route-invariant nuisance transformation preserves eligibility, inventory, match and bridge code; route-decisive one-field transformation follows the locked route table; infeasible control is `CONTROL_INFEASIBLE`;
10. unanimous H1/H2/H3 support still retains shared-error adverse-compatible states;
11. `OMEGA_EMPTY`, duplicate keys, broken seal, zero ratio denominator, and ledger overflow fail closed;
12. changing bridge labels cannot change pre-label eligibility, atom, route, stratum, sample, weight, or availability manifest.

Use inherited simultaneous 95% coverage, exact finite-population inversion and Rao-Wu max-statistic family and all inherited atom-specific workload/event/ESS/weight gates. A bridge claim additionally requires positive pre-label target-bearing support and its locked exact-preservation lower/adverse upper bounds; absence of an available workflow fact cannot be counted as either success or failure. A route-invariant flip, deterministic serialization/matching failure, adverse-compatible AUTO state, bound failure, nonresponse/control failure or ledger violation is adverse for the affected claim; sparse/undefined support, unresolved parser states, unavailable target, or wide compatible bounds is inconclusive.

## Evidence limits and conclusion ceiling

MIMIC radiology text is not imaging. Stored `storetime` is not visibility or finalization. `curr_service` and ICU exposure are proxies, and no configured source establishes the eight workflow facts listed above. The protocol therefore cannot claim awareness, communication, responsibility, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside-plan incorporation, follow-up, appropriateness, safety, harm, utility, benefit, causality, or transport. A supportive result identifies only a reproducible stored-text signature for prospective within-site silent validation that records actual-calendar viewing, edit/sign, transmission, responsibility, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside pathways and outcomes. Live automation or clinical action requires that additional study and expert governance.
