Adverse requires certified reproducible negative incremental evidence (upper interval below zero and at least 8/10 adverse repeats), material calibration harm, or a prespecified reproducible null/control replication that explains the apparent primary gain. A failure of practical floors with intervals crossing zero is scientific inconclusive. Poor positivity, incomplete controls, nonfinite computation, unresolved certification, technical failure, or anchor mismatch is operational/technical inconclusive. A secondary outcome that is null while Ycr is positive is not automatically adverse to the narrow Ycr prioritization estimand, but it prevents the stronger claim of marker-general or assay-robust signal.
