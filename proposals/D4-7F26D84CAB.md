# Certified-field and attendance-boundary repair for the baseline-only cystatin-C experiment

## Lineage and targeted change

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s selected complete first-repeat-attender cohort, UKB snapshot, baseline-only feature boundary, ten cross-fitting seeds, 2% net-benefit and 10% fixed-capacity event-yield estimands, practical floors, calibration/support checks, conditional participant bootstrap, aggregate-only release, and the explicit prohibition on fixed-horizon, causal-benefit, measured-GFR, or clinical-utility claims.

The remaining substantive flaw is not a tuning choice. The parent binds fields by number but does not make field certification a reproducible prerequisite, although the local UKB catalog explicitly says that field labels, units, and missing-value meanings are absent for most fields. More importantly, the protocol does not explicitly bind the age used in the repeat creatinine equation. A repeat eGFR outcome must use repeat assessment age (`21003-1.0`), not baseline age, while baseline eGFR uses `21003-0.0`. A protocol that silently uses one age for both can change the binary outcome and therefore both policy estimands. This child makes those two requirements hard execution gates and narrows the attendance interpretation so that the analysis cannot be mistaken for a deployable policy in all enrolled participants.

## Evidence boundary and hypothesis

The evidence-supported claim is only that the frozen files contain the named columns and that an earlier, pre-repair managed implementation reported the parent’s cohort anchors and provisional aggregate estimates. Those estimates are not results of this repaired protocol and are not re-certified here.

The untested claim is conditional and algorithmic: among people with complete baseline measurements who subsequently have a valid first repeat assessment with the required repeat laboratory fields within the observed 2–8-year interval, does baseline cystatin-C discordance add practically worthwhile discrimination for the observed next-repeat low-eGFR status, relative to the baseline creatinine/age/sex model, when compared at a fixed 2% action threshold and a fixed 10% selection capacity?

This is not a claim that a clinician should act at 2%, that testing improves outcomes, that cystatin C changes treatment benefit, or that the result estimates risk at a common horizon. The selected cohort is defined retrospectively by future attendance and assay completeness. It is a schedule-marginal, ascertainment-conditional comparison among observed first-repeat attenders, not a policy effect in all baseline-eligible participants.

## Baseline models and policy estimands

Retain M0-B with baseline sex, centered/scaled spline basis for baseline eGFRcr and baseline age, and Mc-B with the standardized linear `Z` column; all training-only spline knots, moments, deterministic source-order stratified folds, seeds, logistic solver settings, warning gates, and held-out prediction coverage from the parent remain frozen. Assert that neither repeat dates, repeat analytes, elapsed time, attendance status, outcome, or future-derived ordering enters the design matrix or preprocessing.

For each repeat and model, use raw held-out probabilities for threshold actions `I(p>=p_t)` at 1%, 2%, and 5%, and compute NB `TP/N - FP/N*p_t/(1-p_t)`. The 2% contrast is the arithmetic mean of repeat-specific Mc-B minus M0-B NB. For capacity, allocate exactly 10% (target 1,637) and 5% (target 819) in each repeat across held-out folds by floor plus largest remainder, with ascending fold-index remainder ties and stable descending probability/source-order ties. The 10% contrast is observed event yield under this fixed ranking capacity, not treatment benefit.

Define the timing-band decomposition `[2,4)`, `[4,5)`, `[5,8]` only by restricting the already globally selected 10% indicators; never rerank within a band. The participant timing interaction remains descriptive schedule heterogeneity among the selected attenders. It must be reported as post-selection conditioning and cannot certify schedule invariance, a fixed-horizon risk, or an action-modified attendance effect.

Retain calibration, support, arithmetic identities, repeat-specific signs, 4,000 frozen-action participant bootstrap, primary 98% intervals, exactly the parent’s eight-member simultaneous secondary family, zero/nonfinite-SD failure, band-size gates, and all supportive/adverse/inconclusive gates. Add one interpretation check: a report containing “clinical utility,” “benefit,” “treatment effect,” “improves outcomes,” “fixed-horizon risk,” “measured GFR,” or “recommended testing interval” must fail verification unless supported by an additional randomized/clinical adjudication study not present here. A positive result can support only reproducible incremental discrimination/selection value in this observed repeat-attender stratum; a negative result is evidence against the prespecified worthwhile incremental signal in that stratum; an interval crossing the practical floor is inconclusive.

## Falsification and stop rules

Falsify the protocol if any field certificate fails, source hash/header/schema binding changes, joins are not one-to-one, date parse failures are nonzero, impossible chronology remains, the parent mask anchors do not reproduce, repeat-age equation binding is violated, any forbidden future variable appears in a feature or tie-break path, convergence warnings or nonfinite predictions occur, quotas/identities fail, calibration/support gates fail, or bootstrap SD/critical-value mechanics are undefined. These are computational failures, not adverse scientific findings.

A supportive label requires all computation and calibration/support gates plus both practical-floor conditions: 98% lower bound above zero and point at least 0.001 for `Delta_NB_0.02`, and 98% lower bound above zero and point at least +5 for `Delta_E10`, with at least 8/10 positive repeats on each axis and Mc-B above treat-none and treat-all where specified. A two-axis operational stop requires both 98% upper bounds below their practical floors; directional adversity additionally requires an upper bound below zero and at least 8/10 adverse signs on that axis. Otherwise classify as inconclusive. None of these gates establishes benefit, safety, or clinical utility.

## What remains unresolved

The repaired experiment can establish exact computational behavior and an evidence-bounded conditional comparison. It cannot establish assay traceability, chronic kidney disease chronicity, measured GFR, representativeness, attendance transportability, a common follow-up horizon, treatment effects, harms, patient preferences, or the net benefit of actually ordering cystatin C. Those require field-level expert review, richer clinical data, and prospective or randomized evaluation. The substantive advance over the parent is that a repeat-age error and undocumented field semantics can no longer silently alter the outcome, while the observed-attender estimand is explicitly prevented from being presented as a deployable baseline policy.
