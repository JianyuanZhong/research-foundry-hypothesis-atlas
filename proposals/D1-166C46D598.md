# Episode 8 — resumable support audit for observed renal-recovery discordance

## Scientific deliverable

Newly derive and freeze a one-row-per-hospitalization MIMIC-IV 3.1 landmark cohort, estimate the prespecified post-landmark mortality contrast, and fit two models on the same support-defined population. Completion requires:

1. a read-only provenance manifest and cohort-flow table;
2. a resumable, member-by-member support audit with dictionary-label/unit checks, duplicate and storage-time accounting, all phenotype cells, selective Foley coverage, weight support, outcome observability, and event counts;
3. an unadjusted and pre-landmark-adjusted contrast between creatinine-recovered/late-Foley-nonrecovered and creatinine-recovered/late-Foley-recovered stays, with subject-clustered uncertainty and a cause-specific competing-event sensitivity;
4. a transparent ridge-logistic baseline and a nonlinear six-hour temporal tree model, both newly fitted on the identical subject-level split, target, population and test set, with urine/fluid ablation;
5. held-out discrimination, proper scoring, calibration and bootstrap uncertainty, plus grouped feature-family summaries;
6. prespecified source, threshold, coverage, weight and observation-process falsifications; and
7. a supportive, adverse or inconclusive conclusion generated from computed outputs.

No cohort count, event count, effect estimate or model result is asserted here. Prior row-level audits were canceled before producing output. This version repairs feasibility by decomposing the audit into checkpointed stages and making incomplete support an explicitly inconclusive result; it does not relax the phenotype or silently substitute a creatinine-only question.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The inspected local paper references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt reports, in a selected cohort of 38 mechanically ventilated patients, divergence between creatinine- and cystatin-C-based estimates and iohexol-measured GFR near ICU discharge, with creatinine-based estimates overestimating kidney function and creatinine/cystatin-C divergence tracking muscle loss. That is evidence that creatinine recovery can be an imperfect physiologic proxy in prolonged critical illness. It does not establish the prevalence, prognosis, mechanism or treatment relevance of discordance between creatinine and charted Foley output in MIMIC-IV.

The expert seed [starting question] similarly proposes discordant recovery but labels it untested and warns that measured kidney function is unavailable. The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults with an observed early creatinine rise, early observed Foley oliguria, apparent creatinine recovery by a fixed 72-hour ICU landmark, dense paired Foley observation and observable follow-up, is creatinine recovery without late charted Foley-output recovery associated with higher subsequent same-admission in-hospital mortality than concordant recovery?

The clinical importance is a common but consequential interpretive risk: a falling creatinine may be treated as reassuring even when observed urine output remains low. A reproducible association would motivate prospective validation using cystatin C, measured GFR, muscle mass, volume assessment and complete urine capture. It would not show that low Foley output measures true GFR, that creatinine recovery causes harm, or that diuretic/RRT management should change.

## Resumable audit and actual filtering

The primary analysis uses no row sampling. It uses the accessible catalog discovery partition and filters full source members to eligible stay_id/(subject_id,hadm_id) keys and verified item lists. The audit is staged so an interrupted large-member scan never invalidates already completed work:

1. Read the small dictionary, patients, admissions and ICU-stay members first. Validate headers and source manifest, construct first-stay keys, apply age and 72-hour eligibility, and write a compact eligible-key file plus a checkpoint.
2. Stream chartevents, outputevents, labevents, inputevents and procedureevents one archive member at a time using only declared columns and chunked decompression. Filter item IDs before retaining rows; retain only rows whose key is in the compact eligible-key set and whose clinical time can fall in index-stay windows. Write per-member append-only shards and a checkpoint containing member name, source hash, rows read, rows retained, invalid rows and last completed stage. Do not hold a large member in memory and do not rescan completed members.
3. Resolve exact duplicates before aggregation. For same clinical timestamp, retain source indicators, report disagreements, and use the prespecified median only for valid same-item creatinine records after exact duplicates are removed. For the primary observed analysis require storetime <= t0; report charttime-only support separately. Clinical time is charttime, or starttime for treatments/procedures, never storage time.
4. Emit the support manifest before fitting. It must contain flow at every gate, item labels/units, raw/valid/duplicate/late-storage rows, all four creatinine rise/recovery cells, Foley observation density, early-oliguria support, weight support, outcome observability, death-field discrepancies and event counts. If the stage deadline is reached, preserve the checkpoint and return explicit incomplete-audit/inconclusive status; never infer zero support or fit on a partial scan.

This is a bounded feasibility repair, not a scientific relaxation. A compiler may use a local temporary decompression cache and atomic checkpoint files, but source files remain read-only and derived artifacts remain in the workspace. The audit gets 2 CPUs, 8 GiB RAM and up to 90 minutes per managed stage within the 7,200-second science budget. If a full stage cannot complete within that bound, the promised scientific result is inconclusive/deferred rather than replaced.

## Population, windows and phenotype

The unit is one row per hospitalization's earliest valid ICU stay, sorting by intime, then stay_id. Require anchor_age >= 18, valid intime < outtime, t0 = intime + 72 hours < outtime, nonmissing dischtime > t0, and no deathtime <= t0. The estimand conditions on being alive and in the index ICU at exactly 72 hours, on observed paired support, and on observable same-admission follow-up. Multiple hospitalizations remain distinct; all rows for a subject stay in one split and uncertainty clusters by subject.

Use strict half-open windows relative to index intime:

- W0 = [0,24) hours;
- W1 = [24,48) hours;
- W2 = [48,72) hours.

For valid positive creatinine values in mg/dL, require at least one primary-observable value in each window. Let C0=min(W0), C1=max(W1), Cpeak=max(W0 union W1), and Clate=min(W2). The observed early rise is C1 >= C0+0.3 mg/dL OR C1/C0 >= 1.5. Apparent recovery is Clate <= Cpeak-0.3 mg/dL OR Clate/Cpeak <= 0.75. This is an observed AKI-like screen, not KDIGO AKI or true GFR.

For Foley item 226559, retain nonnegative mL values with audited mL units. Aggregate into relative hourly bins and sum within each hour. Require at least 18 distinct observed hours, first-to-last observed span at least 20 hours, and maximum gap no more than 4 hours in each of W0 and W2. Divide recorded volume by the full 24-hour window and positive W0 weight, never by observed hours. Require W0 recorded Foley output <0.5 mL/kg/hour (early observed oliguria). W2 recovery is >=0.5 and non-recovery <0.5 mL/kg/hour. A recorded zero is data; an absent hour is missing. The primary population requires W0 admission weight item 226512 or, under a predeclared item-priority rule, daily weight 224639 in W0 with primary storage availability.

The primary contrast is among stays with early rise plus apparent creatinine recovery and dense paired support:

- discordant: creatinine recovered, W2 Foley output did not recover;
- concordant: creatinine recovered, W2 Foley output recovered.

Report all four creatinine rise/recovery cells, lower-density cells, missing-weight cells, no-early-oliguria cells and charttime-only cells so selective measurement is visible.

## Outcomes and analysis

The primary outcome is all-cause in-hospital death with t0 < deathtime <= dischtime. Use hospital_expire_flag as a consistency check, report missing/impossible fields and flag/time discrepancies, and require reconcilable outcome observability.

Secondary outcomes are first new RRT after t0 and before dischtime using the verified procedure items; later same-admission ICU readmission is a later ICU intime > index outtime and before dischtime. Describe death versus new RRT and readmission as competing first events. Death-or-RRT and length of stay are descriptive.

Estimate unadjusted risks, risk difference and risk ratio with subject-clustered bootstrap 95% intervals. Then estimate a standardized adjusted risk contrast using regularized logistic regression with variables available by 48 hours: age, sex, care unit/admission context, C0/C1/rise severity, W0 output and coverage, pre-48 MAP/HR/RR/SpO2/lactate summaries, vasoactive/ventilation/pre-landmark RRT indicators, measurement missingness, and optional admission-coded CKD/ESRD proxy. Never adjust the primary contrast for W2 Foley, W2 density, Clate, post-t0 treatment or outcomes. A cause-specific time-to-event sensitivity treats death and RRT as distinct first events and discharge as censoring/competing observation termination.

This is a conditional, noncausal prognostic association in a support-defined survivor population. It is not an effect of urine output, a treatment recommendation or a clinical decision rule.

## Baseline versus substantive alternative

Both models use the same eligible support-defined population before assigning discordant/concordant labels, the same post-t0 in-hospital death target, and a deterministic subject-level split within accessible discovery buckets: 60% train, 20% validation, 20% locked test. All admissions for a subject remain together. Hyperparameters and feature choices are chosen only using training/validation; the test set is read once.

The simple baseline is ridge logistic regression using age, sex, care context, creatinine levels/slopes, pre-48 physiology/support indicators, measurement counts and missingness. It excludes Foley, weight-normalized output and fluid channels. It is transparent and tests whether the proposed output signal adds beyond a creatinine-centered snapshot/trajectory.

The substantive alternative is CPU HistGradientBoostingClassifier over six-hour bins in [0,72) for creatinine, Foley output, weight-normalized output, input-minus-output proxy, MAP, HR, RR, SpO2, lactate, vasoactive exposure, ventilation and RRT. Each channel contributes last/mean/min/max, within-bin slope or variation where defined, count and missingness. Fit the full model and a prespecified urine/fluid ablation on the same split. This alternative can reveal persistence, timing, nonlinear thresholds and interactions that terminal summaries lose. Its purpose is scientific information about trajectory structure, not a small predictive-score contest, and it cannot rescue a failed primary mortality contrast.

Report held-out AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, calibration curves, subject bootstrap intervals and grouped feature-family/urine-ablation comparisons. Require at least 20 events and 20 non-events in training and test and estimable calibration; otherwise model claims are inconclusive. Budget is 2 CPUs, 8 GiB RAM, no GPU, at most 90 minutes after the support audit. Neural sequence or latent-GP models are deferred because this episode's bottleneck is verified paired support, not representational capacity; their extra assumptions do not recover missing GFR or catheter completeness.

## Falsification and interpretation

Prespecified falsifications and sensitivities are:

- repeat with denser and less-dense coverage strata and report observation-gap distributions;
- storage-available versus charttime-only support;
- ICU-chart-only versus hospital-lab-only creatinine;
- absolute/relative thresholds, unweighted mL/hour, and W0-weight alternatives;
- exclude pre-t0 RRT;
- permute Foley timestamps within stay while preserving values and counts;
- permute labels within matched support/creatinine strata where feasible;
- fit an observation-density/process-only model.

Supportive evidence requires a higher discordant mortality risk with a 95% interval excluding the null, direction preserved under dense/storage/source/threshold checks, no dominant observation-process explanation, and acceptable calibration; it supports only a conditional observed prognostic signal.

Adverse evidence is a precise null or reversal under dense, storage-available support, or attenuation explained by measurement/process controls. A temporal-model improvement without the discordant-versus-concordant mortality signal is adverse for the renal-discordance hypothesis, not a rescue.

Inconclusive evidence includes incomplete member scans, inadequate paired support or events, differential observability that cannot be separated from phenotype, irreconcilable outcomes, leakage, failed falsification code, unstable uncertainty/calibration, or resource failure. No threshold is relaxed after results are seen.

The verifier can check provenance, schema/item bindings, joins, time/duplicate rules, cohort flow, support counts, model fits, uncertainty and whether conclusions follow computed outputs. It cannot establish true GFR, complete Foley capture, muscle loss, volume status, treatment indications, causality, bedside utility or external validity. Those require clinical adjudication, cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle and volume measurements, prospective monitoring and external validation.
