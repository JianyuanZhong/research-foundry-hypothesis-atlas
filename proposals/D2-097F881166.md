## Proposed relationship

In the configured E-MTAB-8316 rheumatoid-arthritis FLS/monocyte coculture experiment, the intervention-associated FLS transcript response is hypothesized to depend on macrophage/coculture context rather than being a context-independent response. The primary estimand is a prespecified intervention-by-context interaction, estimated after accounting for the recorded donor structure and repeated context measurements.

The intervention is the recorded UNC106238 condition. The context is the recorded macrophage/monocyte condition or phenotype. The hypothesis is about an intervention-associated expression pattern, not direct drug-target action, causal efficacy, mechanism, or clinical benefit.

## Grounding and clinical importance

The available E-MTAB-8316 material is a targeted transcriptomic experiment in which FLS and monocyte/macrophage context are represented. A reproducible interaction would show that the same intervention has different transcriptomic consequences in different inflammatory microenvironments, which could change how a therapeutic target is prioritized and whether macrophage state must be measured before testing. A context-independent response would support a simpler prioritization rule.

This is a substantive advance over a generic “target is associated with disease” statement: it tests whether the apparent intervention signal is stable across the microenvironment or is contingent on it. The strongest currently supported claim is only that the configured experiment contains the relevant assay/design fields and donor structure; it does not support the interaction claim. The unresolved claim is whether the interaction is reproducible after the prespecified donor-aware analysis.
