## Unresolved question and advance

The expert UKB-03 seed proposes that normal creatinine-based eGFR can miss people at risk of severe AKI when cystatin C and grip are considered. The local data cannot establish severe AKI, KDIGO stage, measured GFR, muscle mass, or a causal effect of testing. The strongest available evidence is structural: the four required UKB files contain creatinine, cystatin C, grip, assessment dates, and 259 paired inpatient diagnosis/date positions. The locally read Haines ICU study supports only physiological plausibility in 38 mechanically ventilated patients with iohexol and ultrasound measurements; it does not establish this UKB prognosis.

The primary falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among repeat-assessment participants with T1 creatinine-based eGFR >=90 and no previously recorded N17/N18 diagnosis, adding a contemporaneous cystatin-C-based eGFR to a routine creatinine, age, sex, calendar-year, repeat-spacing and grip baseline improves held-out five-year capture of a first recorded inpatient N17 event in a fixed-size review list, without unacceptable calibration harm; the increment is larger among participants with lower grip.

This is a confirmatory-assay value question: it asks whether cystatin C could change renal-risk review among people classified as preserved by routine creatinine. It is not a trial of ordering cystatin C, treatment, screening, or patient benefit. Relative to the parent, cystatin C is isolated as the newly added measurement, while grip is already in the baseline and is an effect modifier. The parent’s combined discordance-plus-low-grip phenotype remains a secondary summary, avoiding attribution of every apparent gain to an arbitrary joint cutoff.

## Models, split and estimand

Freeze participant split as integer(SHA256(UTF-8(decimal(eid)))) mod 5. Fit preprocessing and models within folds. M0 is transparent routine information: age, sex, T1 calendar year, L, eGFRcr and mean grip. M1 adds eGFRcys and eGFRcys-by-grip interaction, retaining the main eGFRcys effect; CRP is sensitivity-only. M2 is a matched shallow gradient-boosted competing-risk/multinomial model with exactly M1 inputs, participants, split, missingness policy and K policy. M2 is scientifically substantive because it can reveal nonlinear eGFRcys thresholds or interactions lost by M1; it cannot rescue failed M1 semantics or ascertainment. A sequence transformer, latent GP, full multimodal model, imaging body-composition model and PRS interaction are deferred because their required certified dated sequences, genetics, images, body-composition measures or validated PRS/outcome semantics are unavailable or do not answer this assay-value question.

Set K=floor(.10*n_early) before outcomes, require K>=40 and n_late>=K, and use the same absolute K for early T1<=2012-12-31 and late T1 in 2013. Select K unique people by held-out five-year N17 risk with deterministic eid-hash ties. Fit early and apply to late without refitting or changing cutpoints. Primary contrast is Delta_capture = capture(M1,K)-capture(M0,K), with list yield, cumulative incidence, overlap and additions/removals. Report Brier/log loss, calibration intercept/slope, time-dependent discrimination, competing-risk estimates, and grip-effect heterogeneity as secondary diagnostics. Use 2,000 participant bootstrap resamples stratified by era and eligibility, two-sided 95% intervals, and Holm correction over capture, yield and transport outputs.

## Falsification and result interpretation

Use outcome permutation within era/fold; cystatin-C block permutation within sex, current-risk, interval and missingness strata; independent cystatin-C permutation preserving M0 risk; random-K lists; early/late label permutation; complete-case versus fold-local missingness; a pre-T1 negative-control endpoint; and deliberately shifted code/date pairing that must fail integrity. If the M1 gain survives breaking cystatin-C linkage while preserving M0 risk, eligibility, missingness, spacing and workload, attribution to cystatin C is falsified. CRP attenuation is reported without mediation language.

Supportive requires all semantic/unit/timing/code-date/horizon/integrity/support gates; >=100 eligible N17 events overall, >=40 in each reported era, K>=40; late Delta_capture and its two-sided interval wholly above 0.05; no early/late sign reversal; and M1 calibration degradation no worse than 0.05 intercept or 0.10 slope relative to M0. These are research criteria, not validated clinical-benefit thresholds, and the gain must track the prespecified cystatin-C-by-grip pattern rather than calendar time, spacing or missingness.

Adverse means no gain, reversal, calibration harm, gain explained by selection/spacing/CRP/missingness, failed attribution permutation or no extra events in the fixed list. Inconclusive means failed semantics/timing, incomplete follow-up, malformed pairs, sparse events, poor positivity/ESS, wide intervals, or contradiction between recorded and certified prospective modes. Even supportive results require a prospective implementation/clinical-effect study and adjudicated AKI before claims about ordering tests, treatment or patient benefit.
