# Observed renal-recovery discordance at a fixed 72-hour ICU landmark — episode 6 repair

## Scientific deliverable

Newly derive and freeze one row per hospitalization's earliest valid adult ICU stay from the read-only MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot, then estimate a prespecified noncausal prognostic contrast. The deliverable is complete only after the row-level support audit has been reproduced and inspected and includes:

1. a provenance manifest, cohort-flow counts, duplicate/storage-time audit, all four phenotype-cell counts, coverage and missingness by cell, and post-landmark outcome counts;
2. the primary standardized post-72-hour in-hospital mortality contrast with subject-clustered 95% intervals, plus a cause-specific time-to-event sensitivity;
3. two newly fitted models on the identical eligible population and subject-held-out split: a transparent ridge-logistic baseline without urine/fluid channels and a nonlinear six-hour temporal model with urine/fluid ablation;
4. held-out AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and bootstrap uncertainty, with grouped feature-family summaries;
5. threshold, source-specific creatinine, chart-time versus storage-available, coverage-density, weight/unweighted and pre-landmark-RRT sensitivities, plus within-stay time-permutation and care-process falsification;
6. a final supportive, adverse or inconclusive classification tied to computed outputs.

No clinical effect or fitted result is asserted by this proposal. The audit is a prerequisite; unavailable support is not converted into a zero or a negative.

## Evidence-supported versus unresolved claim

The inspected kidney-function evidence supports a measurement concern: in a small selected critical-illness study, creatinine-based and cystatin-C-based estimates became discordant and creatinine could overestimate measured GFR near ICU discharge. It does not establish the prevalence, prognostic value or mechanism of an observed creatinine/Foley discordance phenotype in this MIMIC snapshot. Cystatin C, measured GFR, muscle measurements and clinician adjudication are unavailable here.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults alive at exactly 72 hours in their hospitalization's earliest valid ICU stay who have an observed early creatinine rise, apparent creatinine recovery, dense paired Foley observation, and observed early oliguria, creatinine recovery without late Foley-output recovery is associated with higher subsequent same-admission in-hospital mortality than concordant creatinine and Foley recovery.

This is an association between observed chart phenotypes. Support would motivate validation with cystatin C/measured GFR and prospective renal monitoring; it would not show that creatinine recovery is harmful, that oliguria is the mechanism, or that treatment should change.

Let `C_early=min(creatinine in early)`, `C_rise=max(creatinine in rise)`, `C_peak=max(creatinine in early+rise)`, and `C_late=min(creatinine in recovery)`. Require all three windows. Observed rise is `C_rise >= C_early+0.3 mg/dL OR C_rise/C_early >=1.5`. Apparent recovery is `C_late <= C_peak-0.3 mg/dL OR C_late/C_peak <=0.75`. Preserve continuous changes. These are observed proxies, not complete KDIGO AKI or baseline renal-function adjudication.

For each early and recovery 24-hour window, require at least 18 distinct observed chart hours, first-to-last span at least 20 hours, and maximum inter-observation gap no more than 4 hours. Compute volume as deduplicated sum divided by the full 24 hours and weight; never divide by observed-bin count. Weight is a scaling variable, not an outcome. Report a separate unweighted sensitivity and do not mix patients without primary weight into the primary contrast.

Early observed oliguria is early volume <0.5 mL/kg/hour. Late Foley recovery is recovery volume >=0.5 mL/kg/hour. Primary cells are only among observed creatinine rise + apparent recovery + dense early/recovery Foley + early oliguria:

- discordant: late Foley non-recovery;
- concordant reference: late Foley recovery.

The other creatinine/output combinations remain descriptive and are not silently substituted if the primary cells are empty.

## Matched baseline and substantive temporal alternative

The baseline is ridge logistic regression for post-t0 death using age, sex, first care unit/context, creatinine summaries and slopes, pre-48-hour physiology/treatment indicators, CKD/ESRD proxy, measurement counts and missingness, with no urine/output/fluid features. It tests whether the observed output channel adds prognostic information beyond a transparent creatinine-centered model.

The substantive alternative is CPU-fitted HistGradientBoostingClassifier or Harbor-equivalent nonlinear tree for the same population, outcome, t0, split and predictor boundary. Build six-hour bins over `[0,72)) for creatinine, Foley output, weight-standardized output, input-minus-output, MAP, heart rate, respiratory rate, SpO2, lactate, vasoactive exposure, ventilation and RRT; retain last/mean/min/max, identifiable slope or variation, count and missingness. Fit both on the same subject-level 60/20/20 train/validation/test split, tune only on grouped validation, and use the test set once. Fit a urine/fluid ablation. Compare proper scores and calibration with subject bootstrap intervals, not just AUROC. The alternative can reveal timing, persistence and interactions that terminal summaries lose; it is not a license to rescue a failed phenotype contrast with a small predictive gain.

After support passes, budget <=90 minutes, 2 CPUs, 8 GiB RAM, no GPU. The actual scientific deliverable is the fitted risk contrast plus both held-out model evaluations and urine/fluid incremental-information result. Completion outputs are the frozen cohort/audit, effect estimates with uncertainty, held-out predictions/metrics, sensitivity/falsification results, and evidence-bounded interpretation.

## Gates, falsification and interpretation

The support audit must be independently reproducible from `work/mimic_renal_support_audit_episode6.py` and output `work/mimic_renal_support_audit_episode6.json`. Hard no-go/inconclusive gates are: unavailable source/member or schema mismatch; failure to reproduce joins/timestamps; either primary cell <50 stays; fewer than 10 deaths in either primary cell; no storage-available weight for the primary cohort; or inability to distinguish missing output from zero. Model fitting is inconclusive if the training/test partitions lack at least 20 events and 20 non-events, calibration cannot be estimated, or uncertainty is unstable. These thresholds are feasibility rules, not evidence for or against the hypothesis.

Falsification includes phenotype-label permutation within eligible subjects, within-subject permutation of event times, chart-time versus storage-available comparison, source-specific creatinine, coverage-density strata, an output/chart-density-only model, unweighted and threshold analyses, and removal of pre-landmark RRT. Any strong phenotype association reproduced by density/source indicators alone is adverse for the renal interpretation.

Supportive evidence requires a higher discordant mortality risk with a 95% interval excluding the null, persistence across storage/source/coverage/threshold checks, no dominant chart-density explanation, and incremental urine/fluid information beyond the baseline with calibrated held-out performance. Adverse evidence is a near-null or reversed contrast, loss under dense/storage-available or source checks, or a process-only explanation; nonlinear performance alone does not rescue it. Inconclusive evidence is inadequate support/events, extreme differential observability, threshold instability, failed calibration, leakage, or irreproducible computation.

Computation can establish data availability, joins, timing, observed phenotype prevalence, fitted associations, predictive metrics, uncertainty and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish true GFR, complete urine capture, clinical mechanism, causality, treatment benefit, clinical utility or external validity. Those claims require cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle and volume-status assessment, treatment indication data, clinician adjudication, and external/prospective validation.
