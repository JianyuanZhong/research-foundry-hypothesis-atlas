## 1. Unresolved question, three rivals, and clinical importance

ICU teams titrate vasopressors/fluids to a MAP target, and a longer (0,24]h time below MAP 65 mmHg (systemicmean burden) is widely reported to track higher creatinine-rise (KDIGO) AKI. But the *cause* of that association is unresolved, and the three explanations carry different clinical consequences:

- **H-dose (leading, physiologic).** The (0,24]h MAP<65 burden is an independent ischemic dose that raises (24,72]h creatinine-rise AKI risk. Implication: "time below target" is a tractable treatment target.
- **H-observation (rival A, measurement).** Sicker or more-monitored patients get both more hypotension *charting* and more creatinine *draws*, so the outcome is *detected* more often without a proportionate injury. Implication: part of the apparent dose effect reflects testing intensity (K2 grounding: Agniel et al., healthcare processes distort lab predictive value).
- **H-treatment (rival B, confounding by indication).** Lower MAP is a marker of co-occurring vasoactive/fluid/inotrope intensity; the burden→AKI relation is confounded by the treatment that both follows hypotension and itself affects renal injury. Implication: modelling care intensity must come first.

**Observation on which they differ.** Adjusting for the *monitoring/draw-intensity* channel alone vs the *vasoactive-treatment* channel alone yields materially different AIPW risk differences (RD) for burden→AKI. H-dose predicts the RD is stable and non-null across *all* three conditioning sets (monitoring-only, treatment-only, both); H-observation predicts the RD attenuates toward ~0 under monitoring-only conditioning; H-treatment predicts attenuation toward ~0 under treatment-only conditioning. These are separable because the two channels are measured by different source columns (lab draw counts vs infusionDrug vasoactive rows), and their negative-control behaviour should differ (a pure detection channel should also inflate *non-renal* draw counts, whereas a treatment channel should not).

**Day-zero check already executed (parent probe, reused).** A raw detection negative control on frozen sources gives Spearman(burden, creatinine draws)=+0.1250 vs Spearman(burden, non-renal draws)=+0.1219 → contrast +0.0031, i.e. the *creatinine* channel rises only marginally more than a non-renal draw channel. This is near-null and sign-flips versus an earlier reported −0.063, so the raw H-observation lean is **criterion-fragile**; only the *fitted* AIPW can discriminate the rivals. That is the deliverable below.

## 2. Closest inspected work and the remaining question (three key references, [K1]–[K3])

Time-weighted MAP<65 burden predicts creatinine-KDIGO AKI (base logistic AUC 0.725); waveform morphology adds nothing, and the signal vanishes after adjustment for procedure type/comorbidity. Establishes the scalar burden→AKI signal under **no** monitoring/treatment channel. *Remaining:* whether that signal survives explicit double-robust modelling of the detection and treatment channels.
- **[K2]** Agniel, Kohane & Weber 2018 BMJ (doi:10.1136/bmj.k1479), abstract_only. Across 272 lab tests / 669,452 patients, healthcare-process intensity shapes the predictive value of EHR labs. *Remaining:* no eICU MAP-burden→creatinine detection-quantification; this proposal supplies one.
- **[K3]** VanderWeele & Ding 2017 Ann Intern Med (doi:10.7326/M16-2607), abstract_only. E-value = minimum confounder strength to explain away a risk-ratio association. *Remaining:* needs a concrete clinical estimand and data to be applied here.

**Evidence vs untested claim.** *Established (observed):* burden correlates with creatinine-AKI in an adjusted base model [K1]; testing intensity biases lab-based outcomes [K2]; the E-value formalises unmeasured-confounding sensitivity [K3]; on these frozen sources the raw non-renal negative control is near-null (+0.0031) and the vasoactive channel is densely populated. *Unresolved (what this experiment tests):* whether a **fitted doubly-robust RD** for a fixed burden increment (a) remains non-null under monitoring-only *and* treatment-only conditioning (→ H-dose) or (b) attenuates toward null specifically under one channel (→ H-observation or H-treatment).

## 4. Estimand and methods (frozen for compilation)

**Estimand.** AIPW (doubly-robust) **risk difference** for a 30-min increase in `(0,24]h` MAP<65 burden on `(24,72]h` KDIGO-AKI, hospital-clustered SE, computed under **three pre-specified conditioning sets**:
- **S1 monitoring-only:** S/X = age, gender, `unitadmitsource`, `apachescore`, `predictedicumortality`, non-renal draw count, MAP charting count.
- **S2 treatment-only:** S/X = age, gender, `apachescore`, vasoactive-active indicator/minutes, fluid intake.
- **S3 both:** S1 ∪ S2.

Treatment/regime encoding: for the dose estimand use a continuous-exposure AIPW (bang-bang / generalized propensity with a normalised dose) or a binary high-burden contrast (≥30 min vs <30 min, K1/K2-consistent). Nugget (propensity) models: logistic/GBM; outcome model: gradient boosting or regularised logistic; cross-fitting (5-fold) to remove overfitting bias; report the RD with 95% CI.

**Sensitivity.** E-value for each RD [K3]; leave-one-hospital-out transport (re-estimate dropping each of 208 hospitals, report the distribution of RDs) to test whether the contrast is driven by a few sites.

**Method comparison (must be reported side-by-side):**
- **B1 — base logistic (K1-style).** Logistic AKI ~ MAPburden + age + sex + baseline severity; no channel modelling. Reference baseline; shows the *unconditional* adjusted association.
- **A1 — fitted AIPW (discriminating).** The S1/S2/S3 RDs above; this is the *decision-relevant* computation that separates H-dose from H-observation/H-treatment.
- **A2 — flexible spline (deferred).** Dose-response spline on burden (natural cubic spline, 3–4 df) to check monotonicity/threshold. *Deferred:* A2 answers *shape*, not *channel attribution*; B1-vs-A1 already carries the falsification logic, and A2 adds no information that separates the three rivals. Revisit A2 only if A1 returns a non-null, channel-stable RD and a threshold question remains.

**What A1 reveals that B1 loses.** B1's coefficient mixes the detection and treatment pathways into one number; A1 re-weights the same cohort so that the RD is identified within each pre-specified channel while remaining doubly robust to misspecification of either the propensity or the outcome model. If the burden→AKI RD collapses to ~0 under S1 alone but survives S2/S3, the K1-style scalar signal is largely an ascertainment artifact; if it collapses only under S2, it is largely treatment confounding; if it survives all three, a physiologic-dose reading (H-dose) is the most parsimonious.

**Resources.** CPU-only, tabular (≈32k rows, ~208 clusters); 2–4 cpus, 8–16 GB, no GPU; expected runtime minutes, not hours. Estimated, not yet measured for the full AIPW (the parent probe ran 140 s CPU).

## 5. Falsification criteria (pre-specified)

Primary quantity: RD_30 (per 30-min burden) with 95% hospital-clustered CI under S1/S2/S3.

- **Supportive of H-dose:** |RD_30| CI excludes 0 under **all** S1, S2, S3, and |RD_30| attenuates by ≤ ~30% from S1→S3 (i.e. adding the treatment channel does not remove the effect); E-value ≥ 2 and leave-one-hospital-out RDs keep the same sign. → "time below MAP target" retains an independent dose signal worth targeting.
- **Adverse for H-dose / supports H-observation:** S1 RD_30 CI includes 0 (attenuates toward null with monitoring adjustment) while the *unadjusted*/B1 association is non-null; and the creatinine-draw channel rises no faster than the non-renal negative control (contrast ≈ 0, reproducing the probe's +0.0031). → the scalar signal is substantially ascertainment-driven.
- **Adverse for H-dose / supports H-treatment:** S2 (or S3) RD_30 CI includes 0 while S1 stays non-null; E-value small (< 1.5). → the signal is largely confounding by indication.
- **Inconclusive:** CIs overlap 0 across channels *and* all are wide (underpowered), or the E-value is small (≤1.5) with a non-null point estimate, or sign is unstable across leave-one-hospital-out. → no rival is favoured; the branch is not decidable on these data.

**Decision rule:** if any single rival is not separable (RD null under both S1 and S2 yet B1 non-null), report the estimand as an *associational* bound, do not claim a mechanism, and escalate as a proposed child (do not silently change the design).
