## Decision and substantive advance

The clinical decision remains whether, at ICU hour 48 after recent recorded pressor support and six recorded pressor-free hours, a markedly fluid-positive adult who is still invasively ventilated should be followed under a policy compatible with starting or restarting a recorded furosemide/bumetanide delivery during the next 12 hours before linked airway closure, versus a policy compatible with avoiding initiation.

The Episode-61 parents correctly repair the major recording-time defect: MIMIC-IV 3.1 `icu/outputevents` does have `storetime`, but clinical chart time and database recording time can disagree. They distinguish the retrospective clinical-time population (`P_RT`) from timestamped audit/inferential sets (`Q_TS`, `M_TS`, `A_ALL`) and retain clockless structural/ICD residuals. They also correctly avoid calling ICU input rows proof of bedside receipt, and they use equal clones rather than an outcome-selected baseline overlap target.

This child preserves that population, t0, A12/B12 strategies, equal-clone Hájek estimand, bounded outcomes, uncertainty, multiplicity, source bindings and noncausal boundary. It adds one remaining clinically material safeguard: a recorded-policy association should not be treated as decision-relevant unless the analysis demonstrates that the future A/B label does not predict a clearly pre-treatment outcome after its independently rebuilt compatibility weights, and it should report how often the ICU-input delivery record can be linked to a medication order and an eMAR administration record. The new panels are interpretation and validity tests, not treatment-effect estimates.

No treatment effect or outcome contrast was estimated while proposing this design.

Thus the unresolved clinical claim is: in the defined population, compatibility with A12 is associated with a clinically meaningful improvement in a bounded recorded airway endpoint without renal, RRT, mortality or terminal-disposition margins being crossed, compared with B12. The unresolved methodological claim is whether that association is robust to a pre-treatment placebo test and whether the ICU delivery record has enough cross-source coherence to support even a recorded-policy interpretation. A positive result supports only external validation or randomization planning; it does not establish receipt, causal benefit, safety or a recommendation.

## New pre-treatment placebo/indication panel

After independent fitting of each set’s five subject folds and numerator-free sequential compatibility weights, compute a negative-control outcome that is fully pre-treatment and cannot be caused by the post-t0 loop policy:

- endpoint `NC_MAP6`: among clone subjects with complete pre-t0 MAP observation, whether the median valid MAP in `(t0-6h,t0]) is <65 mmHg or the MAP gate is ambiguous;
- endpoint `NC_Urine6`: among subjects with complete selected-urine opportunity in `(t0-6h,t0]), whether urine is ≤0.1 mL/kg/h or the urine gate is ambiguous.

These are not clinical outcomes and do not claim that treatment cannot affect the patient’s pre-t0 state; they test whether future strategy compatibility remains predictably associated with already-recorded instability after adjustment. Calculate arm-normalized weighted risk differences A12−B12 with the same clone-hour weights, subject-cluster bootstrap and 95% intervals. Use complete pre-t0 opportunity only for the placebo panel’s denominator; never use this complete-case restriction for the primary outcomes. Require each placebo panel to have at least 100 observations per compatible arm. Rebuild it independently for `P_RT), and for feasible `M_TS)/`A_ALL) branches under their own clocks.

Prespecify before execution:

- a placebo pass if each primary placebo contrast’s two-sided 95% interval is contained in [−0.05,+0.05] and neither absolute point contrast exceeds 0.03;
- a placebo failure if either endpoint’s interval excludes 0 with absolute point contrast >0.05, or if either absolute point contrast is >0.10;
- otherwise placebo inconclusive.

A placebo failure makes the corresponding treatment result **confounding-validity-inconclusive**, regardless of the direction of the airway or harm estimates. A placebo pass does not establish exchangeability. A placebo-inconclusive result cannot support a causal or practice claim and is reported alongside the primary branch.

Add a quantitative, non-gating bias tipping panel. For sensitivity parameters `d∈{0.05,0.10,0.20,0.30}` (maximum prevalence difference in an unmeasured binary indication) and `g∈{0.10,0.20,0.30,0.50}` (maximum arm risk difference conditional on that indication), report `B=d*g), subtract the worst-direction bias from the airway lower bound and add it to every adverse upper bound, and identify the smallest grid pair that crosses the +0.05 airway or a harm margin. This is a transparent sensitivity calculation, not a proof of unmeasured confounding; the plausibility of d and g requires clinical expert review. No “robust to unmeasured confounding” phrase is permitted.
