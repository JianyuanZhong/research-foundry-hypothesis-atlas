# Availability-gated transfusion timing in acute recorded hemoglobin decline

## Status, question and scientific deliverable

This is a targeted, substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's clinically important, explicitly noncausal question: among adult ICU episodes with comparable low Hb, does the observed association of a recorded PRBC start with 24-hour deterioration differ for an acute recorded Hb fall versus stable low Hb and record-defined oxygen-supply/demand stress?

The repair addresses an estimand/measurement defect found by direct source audit. The parent's PRBC exposure was based on an input row's `starttime` and audited `storetime`, but did not require the exposure row to be available by the end of the exposure window. In the observed MIMIC sample, Hb `storetime` commonly lagged `charttime` by 0–6 hours (bounded scan: item 51222, 39,400 rows in the scanned prefix; 23,298 stored within 0–1 h, 14,621 within 1–6 h, 1,476 within 6–24 h, and 5 >24 h; this is not a cohort estimate). A PRBC row charted in [t0,tau) but stored after tau can therefore make treatment status depend on information recorded after the exposure decision and near the outcome boundary.

The future solver must newly produce either a gate-passing availability-controlled observed-risk experiment or a complete not-estimable audit. No prevalence, fitted coefficient, risk, or clinical conclusion is claimed here. Completion requires: an all-candidate flow; row-level Hb and PRBC availability manifests; the parent joint G/S/T support table; held-out competing-risk contrasts; transparent and latent-Hb fits; temporal transport; sensitivity to exposure availability and timing; falsifications; uncertainty; and a conclusion ledger.

The unresolved claim is:

> In adult ICU decision episodes with first available qualifying Hb 6.5–8.0 g/dL, no observed recent PRBC/massive-transfusion proxy, and adequate Hb-trajectory/stress coverage, does the adjusted observed-risk association of an availability-confirmed recorded PRBC start in the next six hours differ between an acute recorded Hb fall and stable low Hb, within high versus low record-defined stress?

This remains an observed-risk heterogeneity question. It is not a transfusion effect, a bleeding diagnosis, an ischemia diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation. The new primary comparison is availability-confirmed exposure versus availability-confirmed no recorded start; the parent’s start-time-only exposure is a secondary sensitivity. If the primary contrast differs materially from the parent sensitivity, documentation timing is itself part of the apparent association and the uncorrected estimate is not a valid decision-time estimand.

Set tau=t0+6 hours. For G, use item-51222 rows with valid g/dL units, finite value, `charttime <=t0`, and `storetime <=t0`. ## Outcomes and estimand

Require alive, in the indexed ICU, and exposure-ascertainable at tau. In the strictly non-overlapping (tau,tau+24h] window, define first mutually exclusive D/E/X/N/U exactly as follows: D is earliest in-hospital death using `hosp/admissions.deathtime` and `hospital_expire_flag`; E is first new invasive ventilation (224385 or 225792), new listed pressor when none active at tau, or new renal replacement (225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809, 225955); X is ICU exit before D/E; N is observed in the indexed ICU through tau+24h without D/E; U is missing or irreconcilable follow-up. Tie precedence is D, E, X, with raw ties retained. Exclude pre-t0 ventilation, active pressor at tau and pre-existing dialysis from “new” events. Do not treat discharge as recovery. Primary Y=D or E, with D, E, X, N and U separately reported.

For each stress/trajectory cell (s,g), estimate on held-out data the model-standardized observed-risk difference:
`Delta_(s,g)=P_hat(Y=1 | T=1,s,g)-P_hat(Y=1 | T=0,s,g)`,
standardized to the eligible empirical target distribution on prespecified common support. This is not a counterfactual. Primary contrasts are H_G,high, H_G,low, equal-stress H_G, and H_3way exactly as in the parent; report stress contrasts, crude cell rates, D/E components, calibration, overlap, ESS and observation-process diagnostics. The parent start-time-only exposure is a paired sensitivity evaluated on the same eligible rows.

## Baseline, substantive alternative and selection

All methods share rows, t0/tau, exposure gate, G/S rules, covariate whitelist, outcomes, split, support gates and bootstrap.

B0 is a transparent penalized four-bin discrete-time competing-risk hazards model for D, E and X. It includes availability-confirmed T, G, S and their interactions, current Hb, prior gap, pre-t0 physiology/support/severity/context, interpretable recent non-PRBC inputs, diagnosis/procedure indicators, measurement masks/counts, time since measurement and store lag. Training-only imputation/scaling/spline/penalty selection is required.

M1 is a local-linear irregular-sampling latent-Hb measurement model coupled to the same hazards. It uses all eligible pre-t0 item-51222 observations, times, units and observation indicators to estimate latent Hb level/slope and measurement noise in development only; hazards use latent level, slope, T-by-slope and T-by-S terms. It tests whether trajectory heterogeneity survives correction for noisy measurements and irregular sampling, information B0’s thresholded nearest-pair representation loses. Success is stable interaction estimates and calibrated uncertainty, not a small AUC/Brier improvement.

Use a deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 split in development eras 2008–2016, 2017–2019 locked calibration, and 2020–2022 untouched transport; no outcome retuning/refitting on transport. If an era or cell lacks support, report not estimable. A bounded CPU convergence/runtime check precedes repeated M1 fits; request one allocated A100 only if within the future solver envelope and explicitly use cuda:0. Discovery measured archive scan runtime (~9 seconds for a bounded prefix audit), not full cohort or M1 runtime. Future solver resources must be declared from its planning limits; GPU is a feasibility tradeoff, not scientific evidence.

The parent GRU remains deferred because the new uncertainty is Hb measurement/availability, not generic sequence order. A causal g-formula/TMLE/dynamic regime is deferred because intent, time-varying confounding, censoring and support are unavailable. Text-based bleeding/ischemia adjudication, images, waveforms, genetics and a large transformer are deferred for unavailable or unvalidated dependencies and do not become valid by added complexity.

## Gates, uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Primary interpretation requires every T-by-G-by-S cell >=50 episodes and >=20 known Y events; each G state >=10 D and >=10 E events for components; >=80% known ascertainment; treatment support [0.05,0.95] after prespecified trimming; ESS >=100 per trajectory state/stress stratum; weighted standardized differences <=0.25; zero post-t0 leakage/impossible time/unit failure; and unresolved PRBC rows <=5% of otherwise eligible exposure records. The stricter t0 and new U_T flow are reported, not hidden. If exposure availability gate failure exceeds 5%, the primary estimand is unavailable; report the parent sensitivity descriptively only. Do not merge G/S states or recode U_T/U/N/X to rescue support.

Use 1,000 subject-level bootstrap refits when feasible, report completed/failure counts and distinguish full-refit from conditional intervals. Report simultaneous 95% intervals for H_G, H_G,high, H_G,low, H_3way and D/E components, calibration/Brier, overlap/ESS, later T among T=0, store-lag distributions, U_T, G-indeterminate and follow-up missingness.

Lock these falsifications: (1) permute T within current-Hb band, G, S, care unit and era; (2) permute G within current-Hb band, T, S, care unit and era; (3) fit observation-only/mask models without Hb values; (4) predict pre-t0 deterioration from future T; (5) non-PRBC process negative control; (6) reverse Hb order in M1; (7) earlier placebo trajectory and shifted exposure labels; (8) compare availability-confirmed versus parent start-time-only exposure; (9) repeat 50811, continuous slope, thresholds, gaps and OR/PACU rules; (10) scan all model matrices for post-t0 chart/store/start/end times, terminal predictors and subject partition violations. A late-storetime sensitivity that reproduces the primary result is reassuring; a large divergence falsifies the uncorrected timing interpretation.

Supportive evidence requires all gates, concordant B0/M1 direction and clinically meaningful simultaneous primary interval outside [-0.05,0.05], acceptable calibration, permutation collapse, no comparable observation-only result, no dominant unsupported D/E component, and direction in both locked later eras. It supports replication and prospective adjudication of a record-defined stratifier. Adverse evidence includes null/reversal, B0/M1 disagreement, mask parity, permutation persistence, future-T association, strong primary-versus-sensitivity divergence, care-context concentration, miscalibration or temporal reversal; this challenges the stratifier or timing interpretation, not transfusion biology. Inconclusive evidence includes high U_T, sparse cells, poor availability, G-indeterminate flow, U/X uncertainty, failed overlap/ESS, unstable bootstrap or M1 convergence, wide intervals or non-estimable transport. The correct output is “not estimable with this snapshot.”

## Clinical evidence boundary

MIMIC lacks validated active/occult bleeding and blood-loss rate, phlebotomy/hemodilution, transfusion intent, blood availability/refusal, completed-unit receipt, compatibility, clinician appropriateness, ECG-confirmed ischemia, symptoms, coronary anatomy, echo/cardiac output, direct oxygen delivery/consumption, microcirculation, complete post-ICU rescue capture, randomization and external validation. Inputevents are record-defined exposure evidence, not guaranteed bedside receipt; EMAR does not solve this without a validated product map. Hb decline may reflect bleeding, dilution, sampling variation or testing process; stable low Hb does not establish chronic anemia. A supportive result establishes only reproducible availability-controlled observed-risk heterogeneity in this MIMIC snapshot. Causal benefit/harm, mechanism, policy, threshold, utility and generalizability require expert adjudication, richer indication/treatment data, external/temporal validation and preferably randomized or target-trial-quality evidence.
