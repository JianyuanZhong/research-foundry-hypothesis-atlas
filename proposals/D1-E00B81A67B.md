# HCC episode-14 crossover: endpoint coherence under day-42 observation selection

Status: design only. This is a crossover of the endpoint-triangulation parent
([prior hypothesis]) and the day-42 observation-selection parent
([prior hypothesis]). No cohort count, fitted model, endpoint
prevalence, effect estimate, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Scientific question and exact hypothesis

The unresolved question is whether a laboratory trajectory observed by day 42 contains
reproducible held-out information about a later coherent, operational retreatment
episode, after two distinct explanations have been addressed:

1. a literal local procedure code may be a documentation shortcut rather than a
   coherent hepatic-arterial treatment episode; and
2. reaching day 42 with an observed laboratory record may select patients, services,
   or documentation pathways that are already closer to later retreatment.

The strongest claim supported by the inspected prior evidence is narrower. [K1] shows
that treatment-adjacent imaging/radiomics can predict outcomes in a selected, externally
tested repeat-TACE cohort, but it does not validate this site's laboratory signal or
procedure coding. [K2] treats HCC treatment response as an imaging/necrosis-aware
construct, not a procedure or laboratory code. [K3] shows how documented imaging
outcomes, missingness, exposure classification, and treatment selection can prevent a
recorded comparison from identifying treatment effect. None establishes that a local
literal TACE row is intent-confirmed, that an operational composite is imaging response,
or that a day-42 laboratory trajectory is independent of observation selection.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the inherited first-index TACE population that is observable through day 42,
> the pre-index-to-day-42 trajectory of eight recorded laboratory labels adds
> held-out information beyond baseline state, care workflow, and the latest day-42
> snapshot for a later operational repeat-treatment episode; the increment remains
> directionally positive under fold-frozen inverse-probability weighting for reaching
> day 42 and is present for a triangulated endpoint (O1), not only for the literal
> code (O0).

O0 is the literal-code anchor. O1 is the primary endpoint-coherence boundary. O2 is a
secondary stricter corroboration only when exact available medication fields pass its
support gate. This is a predictive/descriptive claim about recorded information. It
does not claim treatment intent, imaging response, benefit, mechanism, survival,
causality, or external validity.

A supportive result would justify a prospective or linked-data study with scheduled
laboratory collection, treatment intent/scheduling, and expert imaging adjudication.
An adverse result would redirect attention to workflow or endpoint coding. An
inconclusive result would identify a data/support failure rather than refute the
biologic or prognostic value of the assays.

## Frozen endpoint hierarchy and endpoint rules

All endpoint dictionaries are frozen before held-out scoring and before comparing
endpoints. Normalize labels only for matching (NFKC, case-fold, trim, remove spaces
and full-width punctuation); preserve raw-value hashes and match flags in audit files.
Do not search examination findings or diagnosis narratives for endpoint evidence.

### O0: literal exact-code TACE

O0 is the first distinct-start later procedures row whose normalized Surgery is
exactly tace, at 43 <= t-t0 < 181 days. Its event time is the procedure
Start Time. A same-visit corroborator is not required.

### O1: semantic hepatic-arterial procedure plus same-visit corroboration

Define the frozen semantic procedure predicate P_sem on procedures.surgery as either:

- the exact normalized lexical representation tace, or
- a label containing hepatic artery and embolization, and at least one of chemotherapy or
  perfusion, while containing none of radiofrequency, microwave, ablation, biopsy,
  puncture, or drainage.

The exact lexical tace route is retained in P_sem so the known literal
representation is not artificially excluded; the semantic aliases are the actual
endpoint-boundary test. A P_sem row remains only a procedure record, not intent or
response.

A later O1 event is the first P_sem row at 43 <= t-t0 < 181 that has:

1. the same patient and Encounter Number as the procedure, plus a valid encounter link:
   encounter time within two elapsed days of t, or
   Admission Time <= t <= Discharge Time when both admission and discharge are valid; and
2. at least one same-visit corroborator:
   - an examination row whose Examination matches a frozen liver/hepatic-artery or
     upper-abdomen imaging-type dictionary and whose valid Start Time is in
     [t-42,t+7], or
   - an order row whose Non-drug Order matches exact TACE or hepatic-arterial
     embolization/chemotherapy/contrast order terms and whose valid
     Order time (availability) or valid start time is in [t-42,t+7].

The event time is always the qualifying procedure start time, never an earlier
order or examination. The primary corroboration window is intentionally broad enough
to audit a documented episode; a pre-procedure-only [-42,-1] window is a
prespecified falsification/sensitivity endpoint, with [-28,-1] and [-7,-1] as
additional sensitivities. The +7 component is never used as predictor information.

### O2: stricter medication corroboration, conditionally enabled

O2 is O1 plus a same-visit medication row with a valid medication
Start Time in [t-1,t+1]. It is enabled only if a pre-fit source audit confirms all
of the following:

- Medication is nonmissing often enough to support a frozen exact-value dictionary;
- valid start time and the patient/visit key are present for the mapped values; and
- the mapping is based on exact source values or an externally documented exact
  dictionary, not an outcome-derived fuzzy search.

Drug type, route of administration, dose, unit and end_time are audited for support and
conflicting records but do not create an indication. No medication is assumed to be
TACE-specific merely because it is clinically plausible. If this gate fails, O2 is
reported as unavailable/not estimable and is not recoded as a negative, not used to
rescue O1, and not used in multiplicity as if it were a null result.

If enabled, the O2 event requires O1 and a mapped medication row on the same
patient/visit. The medication row is endpoint evidence only and is excluded from all
day-42 predictors, selection models and censoring models.

### Positive, negative and censor rules

For each endpoint, collapse duplicate rows with the same patient and procedure start
while retaining counts and raw-label hashes; choose the earliest distinct qualifying
start. A patient is positive at the earliest qualifying event. A P_sem or literal row
without the required corroboration is a near miss, not a positive O1/O2 event. If the
patient has no qualifying event and obs_end >= t0+181, it is a complete
operational negative for that endpoint. If obs_end falls in [t0+43,t0+181), it is
right-censored, not a negative. R42 is required before endpoint modeling. Untimed
candidate events, missing encounter linkage, missing corroborator time, and same-visit
key mismatches are explicitly coded as unsupported/near-miss reasons, not imputed.

For O2 only after its exact-field gate passes, absence of a qualifying mapped
medication corroborator in an otherwise complete observed episode is a negative
recording outcome. If the medication source or time coverage is demonstrably missing,
retain the case as a source-coverage limitation and report an O2 support failure rather
than claiming medication absence.

## Laboratory predictors and observation-selection weighting

The inherited eight exact assay labels are alpha-fetoprotein, albumin, prealbumin,
Total bilirubin, direct bilirubin, alanine aminotransferase, platelets, and
platelet count. For each label, use valid test time, the latest nonduplicate
row in [t0-365,t0) as B0, and rows in (t0,t0+42] for S42/T42. Preserve
Qualitative result, quantitative result, specimen type, inequalities and censoring tokens; do
not turn qualitative or censored results into numeric values and do not pool assays
without units. T42 contains terminal baseline-to-S42 changes, transition categories
and ordered knots in (0,14], (14,28], (28,42], with availability/missingness held in
P_obs rather than interpreted as assay values.

Use these nested predictor blocks:

- M0: baseline demographics and index context available from the inherited design;
- B0: baseline assay states;
- P_obs: pre-index and day-42 opportunity/availability indicators, including assay
  counts, valid timing, missingness and source coverage;
- P_care: measured care workflow/process summaries, with no endpoint dictionary;
- A0 = M0+B0+P_obs+P_care;
- A_snap42 = A0+S42;
- A_change42 = A0+terminal B0-to-S42 changes;
- A_traj42 = A0+T42.

The primary contrast for endpoint j is the held-out loss increment
D_snap,j = L(A_snap42,j)-L(A_traj42,j), with D_base,j =
L(A0,j)-L(A_traj42,j) reported alongside it. Positive values mean the trajectory
improves prediction relative to the named comparator. The same contrasts are computed
for O0, O1 and enabled O2, ordinary and selection-weighted, plus the day-28
replication arm (S28/T28, outcome window still [43,181)).

### R42 selection model

Fit R42 on the complete eligible index set inside each development fold using only
information available no later than t0+28:

- numerator covariates: M0, B0, pre-index P_obs and pre-index P_care;
- denominator covariates: the numerator plus non-content process marks through day 28:
  encounter/source row availability, dated lab/order/examination/medication
  opportunities and valid-time indicators; no post-day-28 assay values;
- no O0/O1/O2 procedure, examination, order or medication evidence, no endpoint
  label, no future event, and no post-day-42 information.

The selection weight for an R42=1 patient is the fold-frozen stabilized inverse
probability weight
SW42 = Pr(R42=1 | numerator) / Pr(R42=1 | denominator). Fit both models
using development data only; apply the frozen denominator/numerator models to the
held-out test. Clip at development-fold 1st/99th percentile support limits, record
the unclipped and clipped tails, and do not reselect the clipping rule after viewing
test losses. A denominator with inadequate overlap, more than 5% clipping, or
weighted ESS <100 is a selection-support failure and makes the weighted conclusion
inconclusive. Report R28 as a prespecified day-28 observation-selection comparison,
not as evidence that day-28 assays are biologically equivalent to day-42 assays.

The selection model is a transport/observation analysis, not causal adjustment. It
does not prove that a trajectory would have been observed under a different care
pathway. Endpoint censoring weights, if inherited, are separately fit and never
combined with endpoint evidence in SW42.

## Analysis and uncertainty

Fit transparent elastic-net pooled-logistic discrete-time hazards for each endpoint
and each nested block. Person-period rows inherit the patient-level SW42 and any
fold-frozen censoring weight. Use development-only parsing, imputation, scaling,
dictionary freezing, observation/censoring models and hyperparameter choice. The
untouched test set is scored once.

Report paired held-out log loss, Brier/integrated Brier, calibration, discrimination,
event/risk-set counts, complete negatives/censoring, R42/R28 coverage, weighted ESS,
weight tails, and O0/O1/O2 concordance. Use patient-level paired bootstrap on the
held-out predictions and one max-|t| family across the predeclared primary endpoint
contrasts, O0/O1/O2, ordinary/weighted analyses and fixed falsifiers. The 0.005
log-loss resolution used for a workflow-dominance audit is operational only, not a
clinical MCID. Do not call a small predictive difference clinically important without
clinical utility evidence.

Fixed falsifiers and boundary tests are:

1. **Selection robustness:** compare ordinary and SW42 results; disappearance under
   stable overlap is adverse to a day-42 trajectory interpretation.
2. **Day-28 landmark:** compare the same trajectory-versus-snapshot contrast in R28
   using only data through day 28 and the same later outcome window.
3. **Late documentation:** remove lab/process rows in (t0+35,t0+42] while preserving
   the predeclared cutoff and refit the same frozen analysis.
4. **Pre-procedure corroboration:** replace the broad endpoint window with
   [-42,-1], then [-28,-1] and [-7,-1], keeping event time at the procedure.
   Erasure supports a lead-time/documentation explanation.
5. **Process-only rival:** compare the trajectory increment with the gain from M0 to
   A0. If a process-only block matches the trajectory increment within the frozen
   resolution/uncertainty rule, retain only workflow-associated prediction.
6. **Timing/content permutation:** within patient and assay label, permute result
   content across admissible times while preserving timing/availability; separately
   permute assay labels within development-frozen strata. A matched increment supports
   generic measurement-volume or timing artifact.
7. **Early-alias and horizon controls:** repeat after the early-alias exclusion and
   for [43,90]/[91,181) without changing the hypothesis after seeing results.

## Transparent baseline and substantive learned alternative

The selected confirmatory method is the transparent elastic-net pooled-logistic hazard
above. It directly estimates auditable nested information increments, handles
qualitative/censored assay content without invented units, and makes SW42, endpoint
coherence, support, and censoring inspectable. Its target is the same held-out
operational event risk for O0/O1/enabled O2; it uses the same split, blocks, weights,
metrics, bootstrap and falsifiers.

The substantive alternative is a compact time-aware GRU over the identical allowed
event stream from t0-365 through t0+42. Each token contains channel/table, assay label
when applicable, preserved numeric/qualitative/inequality content, relative time, and
time since prior record. It has separate O0/O1/O2 hazard heads and matched
A0/snapshot/change/trajectory ablations. Train three development seeds only; freeze
the untouched patient-disjoint test and apply the same SW42 at scoring. Evaluate
paired test log loss/Brier/calibration and seed range, with patient bootstrap. It can
reveal nonlinear ordered patterns, irregular timing, and recovery-then-worsening
combinations that terminal knots and elastic-net summaries lose. It cannot recover
missing units, imaging, intent, outside care, or medication indication. If GRU-only
improvement is not reproduced by the transparent contrasts, label it
representation-sensitive rather than trajectory-established.

Planning estimates are unverified: materialization, endpoint audits, selection models,
elastic-net fits, bootstrap and permutations are approximately 1-4 hours on 4 CPU
cores/16 GiB; the GRU sensitivity is approximately 2-6 hours on 4 CPU/16 GiB with one
allocated A100. The primary requires no GPU. Under the shared hardware contract a GPU
job must request one allocation and use cuda:0; an unallocated shell CUDA check is
not evidence of absence. Discovery time and the future solver envelope are separate.
Do not switch to the GRU for a small predictive gain.

Not chosen: a larger transformer, narrative NLP, latent causal/intent modeling, or a
mechanistic liver-response model. They require validated temporality, intent or
response labels, assay units/reference ranges, imaging/report data, or outside-care
capture absent from this snapshot. This is a dependency decision, not a blanket
rejection of complex or GPU methods.

## Interpretation gates

**Supportive** requires: O0 anchor and O1 endpoint-coherence analyses have adequate
event/support and overlap; the simultaneous lower bound for D_snap is positive in
ordinary and SW42 analyses; direction persists in the day-28/late-window controls;
the process-only and timing/content permutations do not match; and the transparent
analysis is directionally reproduced by the learned sensitivity when that sensitivity
is feasible. O2 may provide reinforcing evidence only when enabled and supported. The
result supports recorded laboratory trajectory information for an operational
repeat-treatment episode, not actual retreatment intent, response, benefit, mechanism,
or causality.

**Adverse** means the O1 trajectory increment is absent after selection weighting,
only O0 supports it, the result is erased by pre-procedure corroboration or removal of
late records, or a process/timing permutation matches it. This rejects or narrows the
reusable coherent-episode interpretation for the supported endpoint; it does not show
that the assays lack prognostic value.

**Inconclusive** means O1/O2 event support is inadequate, exact O2 fields fail their
gate, R42 positivity/overlap/ESS fails, censoring is unstable, uncertainty is wide,
or the source cannot distinguish a negative from missing observation. Do not rescue an
inconclusive result by changing windows, dictionaries, subgroups, or weights. Freeze
the result and obtain a better data source.

Clinical adjudication remains essential. The files lack treatment intent or scheduling,
imaging and radiology response, validated examination interpretation, medication
indication, assay units/reference ranges, reliable death, outside-care capture, and
external validation. Claims of imaging response, treatment benefit, causal effect,
survival, or transport beyond this HCC source require linked charts/imaging, expert
review, prospective scheduled measurement, or another independent study.
