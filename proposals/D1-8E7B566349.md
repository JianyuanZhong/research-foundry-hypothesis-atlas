## 1. Targeted audit and substantive child repair

The substantive advance is therefore from “the same number of documentary-M2 captures survives missing bundles” to “the same patients would be offered for a fixed-capacity review, subject to the same stored-content oracle.” This is closer to the validity of a preoperative triage decision, while remaining explicitly documentary rather than biological or clinical-outcome evidence.

## 2. Clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

The unresolved decision question is:

> At 24 hours before an adult patient’s first eligible HCC resection, does the certified patient-complete report-bundle exposure reserve preserve not only the semantic model’s aggregate documentary-M2 capture advantage, but also the identity of patients offered in its fixed-(K) review queue?

This matters because a review service acts on patients, not on an aggregate event count. A coverage contract that preserves (H_G) but changes queue membership can redirect scarce expert attention and may change a downstream clinical decision. HCC can test the former and the stored-content version of the latter; it cannot test actual clinician action or benefit.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is only that the HCC snapshot contains encounter-linked procedures, acquisition-like examination times, multirow whole-accession examination records with narrative fields, same-encounter pathology narratives, and temporally labelled medication and non-medication orders. The source does not contain report release/read/view timestamps, raw images, specimen-level pathology linkage, clinical actions, treatment benefit, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or patient utility.

The untested claim is whether report-bundle missingness can be tolerated without changing the fixed-(K) patient set selected by the locked G score. The confirmatory hypothesis at (
ho=.10,d=24) is:

> Maximum stored content passes every inherited comparator, random, and anchored singleton-deletion gate separately in 2020 and 2021; (q_{
m req}<1); and the new decision-validity frontier (q_{

## 8. Primary interpretations and falsification

Use mutually exclusive one-hot labels:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: source replay, report-grain grouping, operation interval audit, coupled-state generation, exact score/tie replay, patient-complete certificate, queue-oracle replay, threshold frontier, boundary witness, or verification fails.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: any locked (N), (K), event, (n_A>0), or structural-denominator gate fails.
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: maximum stored content fails an inherited comparator, random, or deletion gate in 2020 or 2021.
4. `conditional_coverage_adverse`: maximum content passes but (q_{
m req}) is undefined or (q_{
m req}=1); no positive conditional missing-bundle reserve is certified.
5. `mixed_capture_supportive_queue_adverse`: maximum content passes, (q_{

m dec}) is undefined or (q_{
m dec}=1). Aggregate documentary capture is robust under the parent contract, but patient-level queue identity is not. This falsifies the stronger decision-validity hypothesis while preserving the narrower capture result.
6. `documentary_decision_supportive_bridge_pending`: maximum content passes, (q_{
m dec}<1), (q_{
m dec})’s immediately lower threshold fails when one exists, all exact witnesses verify, and all (N,n_A,e) certificates are complete. This supports only finite-corpus stored-content queue validity and documentary robustness.
