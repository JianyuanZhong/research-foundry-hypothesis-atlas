## 2. Why this matters and what is already known

The strongest claim supported by the available HCC evidence is structural and documentary: examination rows contain patient/encounter identifiers, a nonblank whole-accession candidate, an acquisition-like time, modality/context and machine fields, and narrative fields; pathology rows contain same-encounter narrative content. The parent adds a precise finite-corpus statement about conditional patient-complete stored-content exposure and fixed-(K) documentary capture. Neither establishes that the semantic content is patient-linked rather than a proxy for acquisition/workflow, or that the resulting priority list is stable enough to be a meaningful review allocation.

A current public review is consistent with this unresolved gap: Lei et al. (DOI `10.1016/j.iliver.2026.100240`, acquired Europe PMC source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) describes promise for imaging-derived HCC AI but identifies standardization, interpretability, and multicenter validation as implementation limitations. That literature does not supply HCC report-release data, validate this documentary endpoint, or establish a clinical threshold. The local HCC experiment addresses the narrower, computable question of patient-linked semantic prioritization under source and reader uncertainty.

The substantive advance over the parent is therefore not a larger model. It tests whether a report-content advantage is a stable, patient-linked decision signal after coverage has been certified. A positive result supports only a finite-corpus documentary-prioritization property and identifies the evidence needed for a workflow study. A negative result is clinically useful: it says that an apparently favorable capture contract should not be treated as sufficient justification for prioritizing patients by semantic report content.

## 7. Stored content, visibility, truth, action and utility are separate

The experiment uses the following noninterchangeable meanings:

- **Stored-content allocation:** which examination rows/accession bundles are in the frozen HCC snapshot and which semantic values are fed to (G).
- **Report visibility:** whether an authored/finalized report was released, ingested, retrievable or readable at 24 hours. HCC does not observe these events; onset states are compatible hypothetical states only.
- **Biological truth:** actual specimen-level microvascular invasion or other tumor biology. HCC has no specimen/slide/accession linkage and the endpoint is documentary M2.
- **Clinical action:** a clinician viewing a result, changing a queue, choosing surgery or selecting treatment. No such field is available.
- **Treatment benefit and outcomes:** response, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, capacity and downstream events. These are unavailable.
- **Patient utility:** stakeholder-defined preferences and benefit/harm tradeoffs. Unavailable.

Thus even a supportive result is a documentary ranking result conditional on stored content and a certified finite-corpus bundle contract. It cannot be called clinical utility, treatment benefit, capacity, report visibility, biological MVI validity or patient benefit.

## 10. What can and cannot be concluded

Computationally checkable claims are: source lineage; lossless inclusion/filter logic; patient/accession bundle predicates; (N,n_A,e,Q,q_{
m req}); exact fixed-(K) rankings; documentary-M2 captures under the frozen books; inherited margins; pairwise queue concordance; and the 256 patient-content placebo results.

Expert adjudication is still required for the procedure and treatment dictionaries, modality/context interpretation, adult/episode decisions, Chinese-language pathology and radiology books, and any decision about whether documentary M2 is clinically meaningful. HCC cannot establish specimen-level MVI truth because pathology has no specimen, slide, accession or time fields. It cannot establish report release/viewing, operational readability, workflow capacity, actual clinical action, treatment response, recurrence, survival, safety, cost or patient utility.

If the hypothesis is supportive, the next study is a prospective or retrospective archive-linked silent-mode workflow evaluation with immutable report-version crosswalks, authored/finalized/released/ingested/retrievable/readable/viewed timestamps, real queue and staffing data, clinician actions, competing workload, harms/costs and stakeholder-defined utility. If treatment or biological claims are desired, a separately adjudicated specimen-linked cohort and outcome study are required. No conclusion here licenses treatment change or deployment.
