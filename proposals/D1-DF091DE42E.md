# Recommendation-store to discharge-store opportunity: absolute and analogous-action-calibrated persistence of pulmonary-nodule handoff discordance

## Decision question, unresolved hypothesis, and targeted advance

The clinical design decision is whether a future pulmonary-nodule handoff study should prioritize: (1) getting an actionable radiology recommendation into the observable record earlier relative to discharge, (2) reconciling that recommendation into the discharge artifact once the artifact has had a meaningful observable opportunity to incorporate it, (3) a hospital-wide reconciliation process for many incidental imaging actions rather than a nodule-specific workflow, or (4) downstream tracking, exchange, and communication that MIMIC cannot directly observe.

The assessed parent correctly freezes every eligible opportunity before discharge-artifact timing or content is examined, separates delayed storage from content discordance, and calibrates ordinary institutional storage latency. Its content clock is nevertheless mismatched to the decision. `D - R_store` measures how long the recommendation was observably stored before `D = dischtime`; it does not measure how long it preceded the actual stored discharge artifact. A recommendation can therefore be called “ample lead” even when the discharge artifact was stored just before it or immediately after it. Conversely, a recommendation stored near discharge can precede a discharge artifact stored days later by a long interval. The parent’s requirement that content discordance be at least five percentage points higher in ample than near discharge-lead strata is also directionally unsound: clinically important discordance could be uniformly high after both short and long report-to-artifact opportunities.

This child separates two clocks:

* `L_D = D - R_store` remains a boundary clock used only to describe whether delayed discharge storage (`A`) is concentrated when recommendation storage occurred near discharge.
* `W = DS_store - R_store` becomes the sole content-opportunity clock. Content persistence is evaluated after nonnegative `W`, regardless of whether the discharge artifact was stored before or after `D`.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is that pulmonary-nodule plan discordance remains absolutely common when the recommendation-bearing report/addendum was stored at least 24 hours before the canonical discharge artifact, and that its magnitude either exceeds similarly timed nonpulmonary incidental imaging actions (a nodule-specific phenotype) or is also high for those actions (a broader cross-action reconciliation phenotype). A positive ample-minus-near content contrast is neither required nor sufficient.

The strongest claim already supported is narrower. In this MIMIC snapshot, complete-source retrieval found a feasible pool of recommendation-like radiology reports, unique canonical discharge-summary rows for all observed discharge keys, and substantial ordinary discharge `storetime` latency. Those audit observations support computability and the need for institutional calibration; they are not adjudicated nodule prevalence or evidence of visibility, communication, adherence, causality, or benefit.

The strongest result this experiment can support is an observable storage-and-text phenotype: after blinded clinical adjudication, a prespecified absolute burden of discordant nodule plans persists across a nonstale report-to-discharge-artifact storage interval, and that burden is or is not greater than an analogous-action benchmark under adequate overlap. It cannot show that an author saw or ignored a report; that either timestamp is signature, finalization, display, transmission, or physical departure time; that a patient or clinician received a plan; that follow-up was clinically appropriate or completed outside MIMIC; or that an intervention would improve outcomes.

## Preserved discharge-boundary states

At `D`, every frozen nodule opportunity receives exactly one inherited state:

* `F`: DS observed stored by `D`, classifiable, and faithfully carries the patient-specific nodule, chest/thoracic CT action, and exactly compatible interval/date.
* `C_O`: DS observed stored by `D`, classifiable, but lacks the patient-specific actionable plan.
* `C_I`: DS observed stored by `D`, classifiable, but nodule/action/timing is incomplete or discordant without an adjudicated alternate resolution.
* `R`: DS observed stored by `D` with an adjudicated alternate resolution, such as refusal/preferences, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, or scheduled outside follow-up.
* `A`: canonical DS has known `storetime > D`; this denotes storage timing only.
* `U_D`: missing DS `storetime`, absent/unreadable/contradictory linkage, or unclassifiable text.

`A` remains `A` at the primary boundary even if its eventual text can be classified. Report all-`N` transition matrices at locked `D`, `D+24h`, `D+72h`, and `D+168h`. These boundaries are institutional-storage descriptors, not grace periods or proof of availability.

## Two clocks and the corrected all-opportunity content partition

For the adjudicated recommendation-bearing component define:

* `R_store = note/radiology.storetime`.
* `DS_store = note/discharge.storetime` of the canonical DS.
* `L_D = D - R_store` in hours, the observed recommendation-storage lead before the discharge boundary. Use it only for the inherited delayed-storage chronology analysis.
* `W = DS_store - R_store` in hours, the observed report-recommendation-store to discharge-store gap. This is the sole content-opportunity clock.
* `T_DS = DS_store - D`, used to identify timely and delayed artifacts.

None of these is a draft, finalization, signature, UI display, author-awareness, transmission, communication, or patient-receipt timestamp.

Freeze the `L_D` strata before DS outcome classification: `[0,6)`, `[6,24)`, `[24,72)`, and `>=72` hours, plus `U_L`. They remain relevant only to `P(A | L_D stratum)` and the ordered delayed-storage description. They cannot label content opportunity.

For every frozen `N`, create one exhaustive content-opportunity state `Z`, without restricting to `DS_store <= D`:

1. `S_STALE`: both storetimes known and `W < 0`. The DS artifact predates the recommendation component and has no nonnegative observed incorporation opportunity. Its text may be described, but it is structurally excluded from `C_O/C_I`; it cannot be called a failure to incorporate later-stored content.
2. `W_NEAR`: `0 <= W < 6h`.
3. `W_SHORT`: `6 <= W < 24h`.
4. `W_AMPLE`: `24 <= W < 72h`.
5. `W_LONG`: `W >= 72h`.
6. `U_W`: either storetime is missing/contradictory or component/artifact linkage is unresolved.

Within each nonnegative `W` state, blinded discharge reviewers classify eventual canonical DS text as `F_W`, `C_O_W`, `C_I_W`, or `R_W` with the same semantic rules used at `D`. Reviewers are shown the adjudicated source recommendation and DS text but are blinded to `W`, `L_D`, boundary state, downstream traces, and analog/nodule comparison results. `U_TEXT` remains explicit. The content-discordance indicator is `C_W = C_O_W or C_I_W`; `R_W` is never merged with discordance.

A delayed artifact is therefore handled without selection: an opportunity with `DS_store > D` remains `A` in boundary outcome `Y`, but, if `W >= 0`, its eventual static DS text is classified in the appropriate `W` stratum. An extremely delayed DS is not discarded; report `T_DS` bands `(0,24],(24,72],(72,168],>168h` jointly with `W` and content, and repeat content estimates after separating `T_DS >168h` as a locked sensitivity. This sensitivity cannot replace the primary all-`N` table. A stale artifact remains in `N` as `S_STALE`; it is not included in a discordance numerator or silently removed from reported denominators.

The primary output is the integer joint cube over all frozen `N`:

`count(Y in {F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}, Z in {S_STALE,W_NEAR,W_SHORT,W_AMPLE,W_LONG,U_W}, eventual_text in {F_W,C_O_W,C_I_W,R_W,U_TEXT})`.

Its cells must sum exactly to `N`. Publish all-`N` joint probabilities and marginals before any conditional percentage. Thus neither timely storage, nonstale ordering, eventual text availability, nor adequate opportunity can become an unreported post-index denominator.

## Estimands and corrected persistence decision rules

All estimands are descriptive. Let adequate observed content opportunity be `H = {W_AMPLE,W_LONG}`, i.e. `W >=24h`. `W_LONG (>=72h)` is a prespecified stronger persistence stratum, not a replacement selected from results.

### 1. Boundary timing phenotype

Retain the parent’s timing estimands using the correct boundary clock:

* `P(A | L_D in [0,6h))`, `P(A | L_D >=72h)`, and their risk difference.
* The full ordered `P(A | L_D stratum)` gradient.
* All-`N` joint probabilities for each `L_D`/`Y` cell and unknown bounds.

A positive near-minus-long contrast can support a race-compatible storage chronology only with ordinary-discharge and radiology-lag controls. It says nothing about content persistence.

### 2. Absolute adequate-opportunity content persistence

Report:

* `p_N^H = P(C_W | Z in H, eventual text classifiable)` with `F_W,R_W,U_TEXT` displayed and best/worst-case unknown bounds.
* `p_N^LONG = P(C_W | Z=W_LONG, eventual text classifiable)`.
* `j_N^H = P(Z in H and C_W)` over all frozen `N`.
* `j_N^NONSTALE = P(W>=0 and C_W)` over all frozen `N`.
* the complete all-`N` cube, so the sizes of stale, near/short, adequate, delayed, and unknown states cannot be hidden.

Absolute adequate-opportunity persistence is decision-positive only if all of the following locked operational conditions hold:

1. the simultaneous 95% lower confidence bound for `p_N^H` is greater than `0.10` and its point estimate is at least `0.20`;
2. at least 50 nodule opportunities are in `H`, with at least 20 `C_W` events;
3. the all-`N` burden `j_N^H` has point estimate at least `0.05` and simultaneous lower bound greater than zero;
4. `U_R <=5%`, `U_W+U_TEXT <=5%`, reviewer reliability passes, and worst-case unknown allocation does not move `p_N^H` below 0.10;
5. the conclusion is not confined to `T_DS>168h`, one organ/report type, stale records, or a source/timestamp anomaly.

The 10%, 20%, and 5% thresholds are prospective operational decision margins, not validated clinical harm thresholds. Report estimates even when they fail. Uniformly high discordance in near, short, ample, and long `W` strata can satisfy this absolute rule; no positive ample-minus-near difference is required.

### 3. Analogous-action-calibrated persistence

Compute:

* `p_X,std^H`, the standardized analog content-discordance prevalence in `H`.
* `Delta_C^H = p_N^H - p_X,std^H`.
* `Delta_C^LONG = p_N^LONG - p_X,std^LONG`.
* the same contrasts against exact-CT analogs (primary) and all-modality analogs (secondary).

A **nodule-specific persistent content phenotype** requires the absolute rule above and, additionally, point estimate `Delta_C^H >=0.05` with simultaneous 95% lower bound greater than zero, at least 80% nodule target mass represented in analog calibration, standardized analog effective sample size at least 50, at least 20 discordant events across each compared group, no empty retained cell, and no sign reversal for `Delta_C^LONG` or the deterministic matched sensitivity. This does not establish causality or an author mechanism.

A **cross-action persistent reconciliation phenotype** is supported when the nodule absolute rule passes, analog `p_X,std^H` independently meets the same absolute 20% point-estimate/10% lower-bound rule, and the simultaneous interval for `Delta_C^H` lies within the prespecified descriptive comparability band `[-0.05,0.05]`. This supports studying a general incidental-action reconciliation process rather than a nodule-only tool. It is not equivalence of clinical importance across organ systems.

If the nodule absolute rule passes but `Delta_C^H` is neither positive by the nodule-specific rule nor contained in the comparability band, the correct result is “persistent nodule discordance; specificity unresolved.” If analog overlap, reliability, or event gates fail, absolute nodule persistence may still be reported, but nodule-specific versus cross-action prioritization is inconclusive.

A low near-opportunity rate cannot rescue a failed absolute rule, and a positive `p_N^H - p_N^NEAR` cannot by itself support persistence. Conversely, a nonpositive ample-minus-near contrast cannot falsify persistence if the locked absolute rule passes.

## Falsification and robustness tests

1. **Clock-unit tests:** include synthetic records where `(R_store=D-100h, DS_store=D-99h)` gives `L_D=100h` but `W=1h`, and `(R_store=D-1h, DS_store=D+100h)` gives `L_D=1h` but `W=101h`. Any content classification by `L_D` fails verification.
2. **No timely-summary conditioning:** recompute from the all-`N` cube and assert that every `A` with known nonnegative `W` contributes an eventual content state. A content table restricted to `DS_store<=D` is invalid.
3. **Stale-artifact test:** every `W<0` record must be `S_STALE`, remain in `N`, and be absent from `C_W` numerators. Calling its omission content discordance is invalid.
4. **Delayed-tail test:** report `T_DS` bands and repeat after isolating `>168h`. If absolute persistence exists only in the extreme tail, the routine reconciliation interpretation is adverse/inconclusive.
5. **Absolute-versus-gradient adversarial test:** a synthetic 40% discordance rate in every nonnegative `W` stratum must pass the absolute rule if uncertainty/event gates pass, despite `p_H-p_NEAR=0`. A 2% versus 0% ample-minus-near difference must fail the absolute rule.
6. **Analog calibration test:** a 30% nodule and 30% standardized analog rate may support a cross-action phenotype if the simultaneous difference is contained within `[-0.05,0.05]`; it cannot be called nodule-specific. A 30% versus 10% result may support nodule specificity only if all absolute, overlap, uncertainty, and stability gates pass.
7. **Institutional-latency calibration:** compare `N` with full `O`, standardized `O`, and `M` at all four boundaries. Generic delay blocks a nodule-specific timing claim but does not erase a separately supported content phenotype.
8. **Radiology-lag negative control:** compare `storetime-charttime` and recommendation-before-`D` storage patterns across `N`, `X`, and `I`. A timing signal explained by generic report storage blocks nodule-specific timing interpretation.
9. **Charttime placebo:** repeat descriptors using `charttime`; charttime-only association cannot support storage opportunity, visibility, or content persistence.
10. **Analog semantic falsification:** publish organ-specific fidelity and agreement. Reversal across most organ families, concentration in one poorly adjudicated family, or failure of exact-CT controls blocks nodule-specific calibration claims.
11. **Threshold stability:** repeat adequate opportunity at `W>=12h` and `W>=72h`, retaining the locked `W>=24h` primary. A sign reversal in analog excess or loss of absolute burden confined to one arbitrary threshold blocks specificity prioritization; thresholds never create or remove `N`.
12. **Comparator reversal:** an absolute difference of at least 0.02 between full-standardized and deterministic-matched estimates for a decision contrast is a calibration warning and blocks prioritization.
13. **Permutation checks:** permute nodule/analog labels within exact retained calibration cells and permute `W` bins within admission-structure/time cells. Calibrated contrasts must collapse toward null apart from Monte Carlo error; all-N counts and boundary states must remain invariant.
14. **Duplicate, hash, and timestamp audit:** a duplicate canonical DS key, nonreciprocal addendum used as canonical, impossible parsed timestamp, changed source/schema hash, row-count drift without a declared snapshot change, or joint cube not summing to its frozen denominator stops execution.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

### Supportive

* **Race-compatible boundary-storage signal:** near-discharge `L_D` has materially greater `A` than long `L_D`, with a declining ordered pattern, adequate simultaneous precision, and no ordinary-DS or generic radiology-lag explanation. This supports a prospective timestamp-crosswalk study, not a visibility claim.
* **Absolute persistent nodule content discordance:** the locked `p_N^H` and `j_N^H` absolute rules pass after stale separation, delayed-tail analysis, unknown bounds, and adjudication gates. This supports studying structured reconciliation even if discordance is equally high in near-opportunity strata.
* **Nodule-specific persistent phenotype:** the absolute rule plus calibrated `Delta_C^H` rule and all overlap/stability gates pass. This prioritizes a nodule-focused prospective reconciliation component, without attributing human fault.
* **Cross-action persistent phenotype:** both nodule and analog absolute rules pass and the simultaneous calibrated difference lies within `[-0.05,0.05]`. This prioritizes a general incidental-imaging-action reconciliation study rather than a nodule-only tool.
* **Bundled phenotype:** a calibrated boundary timing signal and an absolute content phenotype both pass. The next intervention should measure and separately test both components.

### Adverse to a proposed mechanism

* Absolute nodule discordance remains below the operational thresholds with sufficiently narrow simultaneous bounds and robust unknown handling.
* Apparent content discordance is confined to stale artifacts, missing/unclassifiable records, or DS artifacts stored more than 168 hours after discharge.
* A purported nodule-specific excess disappears under exact-CT analog calibration, reverses across organ families or the `W_LONG` stratum, or is explained by poor overlap.
* The timing signal is comparable to ordinary DS latency or non-nodule radiology storage lag.
* Both nodule and analog discordance are low: this redirects attention toward unobserved communication/tracking or other processes; it does not prove handoff adequacy.

Adverse means the specified observable phenotype was not supported. It does not establish the opposite human mechanism or clinical benefit of another intervention.

### Inconclusive

Fewer than 200 adjudicated nodule opportunities; sparse `H`/`W_LONG` strata or events; analog target coverage below 80%; empty cells or ESS below 50; broad intervals crossing a decision margin; excess `U_R/U_D/U_W/U_TEXT`; poor reviewer reliability; semantic noncomparability of analogs; duplicate DS rows; contradictory chronology; unstable delayed-tail or threshold results; comparator reversal; failed permutation/unit tests; or source/schema mismatch are inconclusive. Analog failure cannot invalidate a well-estimated absolute nodule burden, but it blocks nodule-specific versus cross-action selection. Inconclusive is not evidence of no problem.

## Exact verifier scope and evidence limits

A computational verifier can check:

* snapshot/source/schema hashes, archive members, headers, complete-source row counts, and declared no-row-sampling scans;
* exact `(subject_id,hadm_id)` and `(subject_id,note_id)` joins, reciprocal addendum chains, first-eligible-subject rules, frozen `N` and `X`, population nonoverlap, and one canonical DS per admission;
* that `N` and `X` are fixed before DS text/storetime outcomes are read;
* arithmetic for `D`, `R_store`, `DS_store`, `L_D`, `W`, `T_DS`, all boundary indicators and exhaustive bins;
* that `L_D` is used only for boundary timing, `W` is the only content-opportunity clock, stale rows cannot be content failures, and delayed `A` rows with known nonnegative `W` remain in eventual content accounting;
* that every all-opportunity cube sums to its frozen denominator and every conditional result is accompanied by its all-denominator joint burden and selection fraction;
* identical content semantics for nodule and analog actions, fixed analog retrieval branches, weights/cells/ESS/target coverage, ordinary/matched controls, and no outcome-informed matching;
* simultaneous intervals, integer-compatible unknown bounds, reviewer-gate outputs, absolute and calibrated decision logic, sensitivity/falsification outputs, and linkage of every conclusion to computed results;
* adversarial examples in the falsification section, including uniformly high content discordance, tiny positive gradients, stale records, delayed artifacts, equal high analog rates, nodule-specific excess, generic storage delay, and unsupported causal/visibility language.

The verifier cannot adjudicate whether a finding is truly incidental, whether a recommendation or alternate resolution is clinically appropriate, whether organ-specific analogs are substantively comparable, or whether text faithfully conveys a nuanced plan without expert review. It cannot infer finalization, signature, UI visibility, author awareness, transmission, communication, patient receipt, outside-system care, adherence, malignancy, safety, causal effect, or benefit. Those stronger claims require expert adjudication, prospective timestamp/UI/communication capture, external linkage, and an appropriately designed intervention study.
