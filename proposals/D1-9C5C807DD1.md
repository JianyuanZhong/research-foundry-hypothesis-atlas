# Fail-closed, source-isolated compiler contract for the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Purpose and inherited frozen design

This is a substantive compiler-ready successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It repairs execution and interpretation weaknesses only; it does not change the Lead-frozen scientific question, cohort, exposure, landmarks, windows, parser, endpoint, estimands, clustering, transport boundary, or noncausal interpretation.

The frozen cohort is exactly 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons, and 74 hospitals. Use the first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid`; preserve all eligible repeated landmarks within that stay and cluster by `patientunitstayid`. Landmarks are `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission. At each landmark, select the first exact target care-plan row in `[L,L+360]`, with `D = cplitemoffset` and a verified `D == decision_offset`. Exposure is the literal current label, with exactly two arms:

* `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` (`evaluation`)
* `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` (`no_trial`)

The inherited clinical-time windows are `B=(D-720,D]`, `E=(D,D+720]`, and `T=(D+720,D+2160]`. The locked respiratory parser uses only FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate, with the inherited aliases, finite/bounded parsing, FiO2 fraction/percentage conversion, duplicate and same-time conflict rules, a minimum of two observations at distinct offsets separated by at least 30 minutes per component, and at least three of four components required for a state. Fixed absolute thresholds are FiO2 `0.10` fraction, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`.

The ordered five-state all-row outcome is fixed as
`K ∈ {competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.
Finite `unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160` has precedence. Death, discharge, transfer, and truncation are visible competing exits; invalid or unavailable exit time is separate and never silently interpreted as alive. Primary displays are fixed-arm-denominator all-row probabilities `p_k(a)` and `Δ_k=p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`. Conditional classifiable probabilities `q_c(a)` and `δ_c` are secondary. All quantities are descriptive associations with named structured-chart constructs, not treatment effects.

The strongest claim currently supported is only that the two strings are sparse, heterogeneous care-plan documentation. They may reflect state, opportunity, site workflow, entry timing, persistence, endpoint-source dependence, or exits. They do not establish an SBT, extubation evaluation, readiness, clinician intent, delivered ventilator settings, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The falsifiable claim tested is narrower: after pre-D state and value-blind opportunity adjustment, the exact current label has a temporally directional, source-reproducible incremental association with the locked post-D `K` trajectory, not merely a pre-D/persistent documentation signal, and it supports a bounded manual chart-review allocation task only if all prespecified held-out support, uncertainty, temporal placebo, source-isolation, and transport gates pass.

## Auditable temporal and source-isolation implementation

Emit `source_manifest.json`, `filter_counts.json`, and immutable row-level `frozen_population_verified.csv` and `exposure_verified.csv` before fitting. For every derived field record source table, source column(s), clinical-time inequality, window, transformation, missing/invalid status, and whether it is an input, outcome, placebo, or diagnostic. A field with ambiguous time, unvalidated semantic text, or nonfinite value is excluded or value-blind opportunity `O`, never state `S`.

Emit three disjoint manifests before outcomes are read: `pre_D_baseline.json`, `post_D_endpoint.json`, and `post_D_diagnostic.json`. Every predictor baseline record must satisfy source clinical time `< D`; `respchartentryoffset` can never replace `respchartoffset`. Only the exact current label is exposure verification and `M1` input. No care-plan value, care-plan count, historical label, post-D field, endpoint-derived field, or target status may enter a predictor, support map, fold assignment, cutpoint, calibration, null, or review calculation.

Use a machine-readable one-to-one feature dictionary with disjoint blocks:

* `S`: value-derived finite pre-D respiratory proxies from locked B-window component summaries/trends, valid pre-D airway facts from `respiratoryCare`, and finite pre-D `vitalPeriodic.sao2`/`respiration` summaries.
* `O`: value-blind row counts, distinct clinical timestamps, spacing/window availability, missingness, entry-lag summaries, respiratory-care counts/interval counts, vital counts/timestamps/spacing, and treatment row counts.
* `C`: only fixed design covariates, landmark, age category, and explicitly inherited pre-D covariates.

Persist a deterministic data-lineage check that every feature belongs to exactly one block and that removing a source removes both its value fields and any source-specific support proxies as declared. The label belongs to no block.

Create fixed leave-one-hospital-out folds. All landmarks of a stay remain in one fold; hospital ID is never a predictor. Fit imputation, scaling, regularization, calibration, S/O maps, support decisions, and all cutpoints in training hospitals only. Use the prespecified low-dimensional multinomial family `M_C`, `M_O`/`Mopp`, `M_S`, `M_0=C+S+O`, and `M_1=M_0+current_label`, with fixed grid, solver, tolerance, and deterministic tie-break recorded in `compute_manifest.json`. No outcome-dependent model choice is permitted.

Evaluate identical folds, windows, models, transforms, and denominators in three source views:

1. `ALL`: all permitted sources and fields.
2. `RC`: respiratoryCharting/respiratoryCare state and opportunity fields plus `C`, excluding vitalPeriodic values.
3. `VP`: vitalPeriodic state and opportunity fields plus `C`, excluding respiratoryCharting and respiratoryCare value fields; only explicitly permitted value-blind counts/timestamps remain.

The locked `K` remains respiratory-chart based in all views. VP is a provenance stress test, not a replacement endpoint. A gain disappearing when endpoint-adjacent opportunity is removed is source dependence, not state validation; a gain in an independent view is reproducible association with named chart constructs, not semantic confirmation.

## Temporal-direction and documentation placebo batteries

Predeclare and evaluate each placebo separately, with no target or post-D field in predictors:

* `K_pre`: locked parser trajectory from `P=(D-1440,D-720]` into B using pre-D respiratoryCharting clinical time and airway corroboration. Unsupported states are `pre_unobservable`, never negative.
* `O_pre_shift`: value-blind respiratoryCharting opportunity in P, requiring at least two distinct clinical timestamps spanning at least 30 minutes.
* `O_E_RC`, `O_T_RC`: value-blind respiratoryCharting opportunity in E and T, each requiring at least two distinct clinical timestamps.
* `O_E_VP`, `O_T_VP`: value-blind vitalPeriodic opportunity in E and T, requiring at least two finite rows at distinct offsets spanning at least five minutes.
* `K_future`: future anchor `D_pl=D+720`, historical current label retained as exposure, freshly parsed trajectory in `(D_pl,D_pl+720]` and `(D_pl+720,D_pl+2160]`, same parser, exit precedence, and all-row accounting. Original and future windows must be checked for overlap; overlap, exit, invalid anchor, and unavailable states are explicit.
* Documentation-only pseudo-targets: construct fixed targets from post-D source presence/opportunity only, never value, using the same support rules and fold-local transforms. Keep them in `post_D_diagnostic.json`; they cannot be called clinical outcomes.

A positive `K_pre`, `O_pre_shift`, or future-anchor increment is adverse to a temporally clean current-label interpretation; a null is only reassuring. A future-anchor increment is reported as persistence, evolving state, or site process evidence without choosing among these explanations. Placebos with insufficient support or overlap domination are inconclusive, not favorable. Cluster by stay and report first-landmark-only sensitivity.

Audit label persistence without changing exposure: exact-label recurrence, elapsed time since prior same label, number of prior landmark labels, and equality to immediately preceding valid label. Compare `M1` versus `M0` in first-landmark-only, no-overlap, and repeated-landmark strata. Historical labels are not predictors; if a diagnostic persistence feature is tested, it is a separate predeclared model and cannot support the current-label increment. A gain confined to recurrence or repeated landmarks is adverse to temporal specificity.
