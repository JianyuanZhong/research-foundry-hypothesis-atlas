## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
Discharging a patient alive from the ICU is a consequential, weakly standardized decision. The parent
predicts 48 h unplanned ICU readmission and 7 d post-discharge death from a fixed pre-discharge window,
and the lineage has repeatedly added *discrimination* negative controls (Delta_spec, Delta_obj). Two gaps
remain, both consequential. (i) No child has asked whether the footprint changes a **decision** (net
benefit at plausible discharge thresholds) rather than merely raising AUROC. (ii) The parental chain's own
declared residual rival - a **discharge-time capacity / care-process common cause** - has been named since
episode 39 but never bound to tables. [K1] shows ICU mortality models transport across hospitals while
**discharge timing does not**, i.e. discharge is largely organizational; [K3] warns discharge/post-ICU
care is capacity- and workforce-constrained. Both make the capacity rival a first-class explanation the
physiology reading must beat. [K2] shows prior MIMIC-IV readmission work claims AUC/NRI/IDI increments
without net benefit or capacity adjustment.

## 2. Hypothesis / strongest rival
- H1 (leading): after including a full documentation/escalation block, an objectively-measured pre-discharge
  instability footprint adds **decision-utility net benefit** at prespecified thresholds beyond static
  severity, and >=75% of that net benefit survives conditioning on discharge-time ICU/ward capacity.
- H0 (rival): any apparent benefit is a **discharge-time capacity/process artifact** (patients are
  discharged and re-admitted under bed pressure, not because of residual physiology) and collapses once
  capacity is included [K1,K3].
Predictions differ on the same held-out contrast: H1 keeps most of Delta_NB after the capacity block; H0 does not.

## 3. Design (fixed from parent unless noted)
- Population: adults (`hosp/patients.anchor_age >= 18`), discharged ALIVE from FIRST ICU stay, `icu/icustays.los >= 1` day. Key `(subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id)`.
- Landmark `t0 = icu/icustays.outtime`; predictor window `[t0-24h, t0)`; data at/before t0 only (no future leakage).
- Outcomes: (a) 48 h unplanned ICU readmission = next `icu/icustays.intime` after t0 within 48 h (same subject_id); (b) 7 d post-discharge death via `hosp/patients.dod` / `hosp/admissions.deathtime` in (t0, t0+7d].
- Split: locked 70/30 temporal split by t0 (inherited boundary 2169-10-11 22:55:40); held-out touched once.
- Ladder: M0 static severity (age, `admission_type`, first-day SOFA-surrogate labs, Charlson from `hosp/diagnoses_icd`) -> M1 (+level means) -> M2 (+decoupled dynamics D1/D2) with M-obs measurement-intensity block.
- **NEW DELTA 1 - primary estimand is DECISION UTILITY, not AUROC.** Decision-curve net benefit (Vickers-style)
  `NB(pt) = TP/N - FP/N * pt/(1-pt)` at pt in {2.5,5,10,20}%. `Delta_NB(pt) = NB(M2,pt) - NB(M0,pt)`,
  paired 1000-bootstrap 95% CI. Prespecified clinical margin: claim "decision-relevant" only if `Delta_NB>=0.01`
  with CI lower bound >0 at >=2 of 4 thresholds for the composite. AUROC/AUPRC reported only as secondary.
- **NEW DELTA 2 - discharge-time CAPACITY (bed-pressure) rival, first operationalization.** From `icu/icustays`
  count concurrent occupancy at t0 (intime<=t0<outtime, same subject excluded) = ICU_occ(t0); from
  `hosp/transfers` (subject_id, hadm_id, careunit, eventtype, intime, outtime) ward/ICU occupancy and
  boarding in `[t0-6h,t0)`. Prespecify `Delta_NB_cap` = Delta_NB after adding the capacity block. Retention
  = `Delta_NB_cap / Delta_NB`; require >=75%.
- Controls: permuted-label `Delta_NB <= 0.005`; M-obs measurement-intensity block; null-window
  `[t0-48h,t0-24h)` `Delta_NB_null <= 0.5 * Delta_NB`.

## 4. Falsifiability / decision rule (prespecified)
- Supportive of H1: `Delta_NB>=0.01` (CI lower>0) at >=2/4 thresholds AND retention >=75% after capacity AND permuted <=0.005 AND `Delta_NB_null<=0.5*Delta_NB`.
- Adverse (H0 favored / valuable negative): `Delta_NB<=0` OR CI includes 0 OR retention <50% -> the footprint is largely a capacity/process artifact; park the branch.
- Inconclusive: point estimate in (0,0.01) with wide CI, or controls contradictory, or a category has <30 held-out events.

## 5. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative)
Interpretable nested logistic ladder (M0->M1->M2) = primary baseline: estimable on CPU, stable, and directly
readable as net benefit. Substantive learned alternative = a small irregular-time (GRU-D-style) encoder over
the same `[t0-24h,t0)` chart series (icu/chartevents) with the same splits/estimand, using 1 A100 (declared
envelope; CPU fallback fine). It could reveal nonlinear/irregular-sampling dynamics the summary ladder loses
(esp. whether net benefit comes from trajectory shape vs level). Selection/deferral: run the ladder first; run
the encoder only if the ladder contrast is inconclusive-but-nonzero; progress worker may judge marginal value.
Rationale for not over-investing in the encoder: the scientific question is decision utility + capacity
confounding, not maximal discrimination.
