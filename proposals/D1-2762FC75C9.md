# From retrospective discordance to a prospectively testable pulmonary-nodule handoff gate: recommendation units, admission-level safe routing, and departure-frozen evidence

## Decision question and substantive repair

The clinical decision is not whether a discharge document is literally concordant with a radiology report. It is which *next prospective workflow experiment*, if any, is justified: clinician-confirmed structured propagation for low-discretion omissions, clinician reconciliation for conflicts or incomplete plans, a two-stage routing gate that separates those tasks, or no content intervention.

The parent made raw discordance more decision-relevant by adjudicating context and superseding plans, but its decision-curve analysis still turns unvalidated threshold probabilities into an apparent intervention choice. MIMIC provides neither utilities for an inappropriate copied surveillance plan versus a missed plan nor a validated prospective trigger. Moreover, one admission may contain multiple target/action/timing recommendations. Treating each recommendation as independent overweights complex admissions, while a single admission-level label can hide a dangerous mixed case.

This child therefore removes retrospective decision-curve net benefit from intervention selection. It preserves the parent's frozen first-eligible all-opportunity admission denominator, reciprocal report/addendum chronology, unique canonical discharge artifact, corrected `G = DS_store - R_store` clock, exhaustive departure/joint states, institutional timing controls, provenance, and noncausal limits. It adds an atomic recommendation-unit inventory and makes a conservative **two-stage admission-routing gate** primary:

1. determine whether any departure-frozen recommendation unit remains observably active and unresolved, while retaining unknowns rather than inferring clinical appropriateness; then
2. route the admission to clinician-confirmed structured propagation only if *every* active unresolved unit is a reproducible pure omission with no cross-unit conflict; route any incomplete, conflicting, mixed, or interaction-dependent admission to clinician reconciliation; defer any admission with decision-relevant uncertainty.

Recommendation-unit burdens are secondary workload estimands. The primary intervention unit is the index admission because the discharge artifact and prospective workflow are admission-level and because a single unsafe unit should prevent silent or bulk propagation of the admission's other units.

The strongest evidence-supported claim remains limited. The inherited complete-source, zero-sampling scan found 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 unique canonical `DS` admission keys, only 17 missing `DS storetime` values, substantial ordinary discharge storage delay, and 3,764 retrieval-proxy pulmonary reports. These proxy reports are not adjudicated opportunities; no prevalence, actionability, routing burden, or intervention effect has been computed.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Within the frozen first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity admissions whose canonical discharge artifact was stored by departure at least 24 hours after the denominator-defining operative recommendation component, departure-frozen evidence and unit-specific clocks permit reproducible separation of (i) admissions in which every observably active unresolved unit also had at least 24 hours of positive storage opportunity and is a pure omission suitable for clinician-confirmed structured propagation from (ii) admissions in which every observably active unresolved unit had that opportunity but at least one is incomplete, conflicting, mixed, or interaction-dependent and requires clinician reconciliation. Admissions containing an active unresolved unit with less opportunity are retained as timing-limited rather than miscalled content failures. Separation remains identifiable under individual-reviewer and unknown-compatible bounds and yields enough admission-level events and precision to justify a prospective silent validation of the two-stage gate.

This is falsified as a retrospective routing hypothesis if the departure-frozen record cannot reproducibly establish the required unit boundaries or superseding-plan facts, if disagreement/unknown bounds permit clinically different admission routes, if mixed admissions make recommendation-unit and admission-level conclusions diverge materially, or if route-specific event counts and interval precision fail. Even supportive retrospective results do not establish deployment value, comparative effectiveness, or that structured propagation is safer than reconciliation; they nominate a frozen policy for prospective validation.

## Frozen admission denominator and reciprocal chronology

Before reading any discharge text, discharge `storetime`, later trace, comparator outcome, or routing outcome, create and hash the radiology eligibility manifest. The primary denominator `N_A` remains exactly the parent's first eligible admission per subject:

* adult; exact admission-linked radiology report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional/optional plans, explicit no-follow-up, in-hospital death, hospice/comfort-only transition under the locked rubric, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* do not infer nodule measurements, smoking exposure, malignancy risk, life expectancy, recommendation appropriateness, or missing context.

Report/addendum chains are reconstructed only from reciprocal, subject-consistent, admission-consistent detail links. Every operative component must have `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. A later material pre-`D` component supersedes an earlier component only within the reciprocal chain. Missing nodes, nonreciprocal or contradictory links, unresolved operative components, or missing/contradictory recommendation times are `U_R`, retained in the eligibility cascade and bounds, and never called omissions. Eligibility reviewers see no discharge artifact, `DS_store`, later event, or proposed route.

The canonical artifact is the one `note/discharge` row with exact `note_type=="DS"` per `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. A duplicate canonical key stops the confirmatory run. Missing artifact/text/linkage/`storetime` and unreadable or contradictory content remain unknown. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

## Preserved departure and joint states

For every admission in `N_A`, preserve the complete departure state `Y`:

* `F`: `DS_store<=D`, classifiable, and faithful patient-specific pulmonary target, chest/thoracic CT action, and compatible exact interval/date;
* `C_O`: `DS_store<=D`, classifiable, but the patient-specific actionable plan is omitted;
* `C_I`: `DS_store<=D`, target/action/timing is incomplete or discordant without a departure-frozen valid alternate resolution;
* `R`: `DS_store<=D` and contains a text-supported alternate resolution under the hierarchy below;
* `A`: known `DS_store>D`, regardless of later content;
* `U_D`: artifact/store/linkage/text is missing, unreadable, contradictory, or unclassifiable.

They sum to `N_A`. Later fidelity cannot change `A` at departure.

Retain `R_store` for the operative recommendation component, `DS_store`, and `G=DS_store-R_store` in hours. Retain `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, and eventual `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}` exactly as in the parent. Publish every zero/unknown cell of `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A` and its deterministic crosswalk to `Y`. The primary ample, departure-available stratum remains frozen as `E_A={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. No `G<0` or `DS_store>D` row can enter the primary content-routing estimands. `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` is a checked identity, not an alternate opportunity definition.

## Atomic recommendation-unit inventory without changing the denominator

After `N_A` is frozen, radiology reviewers enumerate every pulmonary recommendation unit in each index admission that independently satisfies the same eligibility, chain, and pre-`D` storage rules. This creates `N_R`, nested within `N_A`; it does not add subjects or admissions. The unit derived from the denominator-defining operative component is the index unit. For every additional unit calculate `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u` and its unit opportunity class using the same `H` cut points; no admission can enter an intervention route unless every active unresolved unit has `G_u>=24h`.

A recommendation unit is the smallest operative tuple

`U=(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, recommendation polarity)`.

Co-mentioned nodules with one shared action and due interval are one unit. Different actions or due windows are separate units. Repeated wording and reciprocal addendum restatements are deduplicated; a material addendum change creates the final operative version of the same unit, not an additional event. If reviewers cannot determine whether phrases represent one or multiple units, mark `U_BOUNDARY` and retain both the minimum-unit and maximum-unit compatible inventories. No consensus is allowed to erase this boundary uncertainty.

Each unit carries `(subject_id,hadm_id,unit_id)`, source `note_id`, operative component, `R_store`, target span, action, interval/date, and boundary provenance. Unit IDs are derived and deterministic. The admission remains the resampling, multiplicity, and primary decision unit.

## Departure-frozen adjudication and superseding-plan hierarchy

All packets are generated from data known stored by `D`. No post-`D` note content, downstream event, POE row, later encounter, diagnosis, follow-up imaging, or outcome may be shown or used. File manifests record included source rows, `storetime`, redaction offsets, and prohibited-field scans.

### Panel A: observable disposition compatibility, not clinical appropriateness

At least two independent physicians experienced in hospital discharge review receive the operative radiology chain, admission/disposition variables, and the canonical `DS` after a separate span team redacts every pulmonary target, action, timing, recipient, specialist, order, and target-linked preference span. Packets exclude all gap/timing labels, eventual notes, downstream data, Panel B labels, and intervention language.

Panel A assigns:

* `K_OPEN`: no *observable* departure-frozen, non-target context closes the possibility of a pulmonary disposition action;
* `K_CLOSED`: an explicit non-target context makes any such action inapplicable, such as death before departure or a documented global decision against all follow-up; this should be rare after denominator exclusions and triggers eligibility audit;
* `K_UNKNOWN`: available context cannot establish openness because relevant competing illness, preferences, outside plans, prognosis, or other facts are absent or ambiguous.

`K_OPEN` is deliberately not called clinically appropriate or necessary. It means only that MIMIC contains no observable context-level stop after target-plan spans are hidden. Reviewers record the exact supporting sentence, confidence, and missing fact that could change the label.

The redaction dictionary is locked before labels. A third redactor audits a stratified 10% sample, including every `K_CLOSED` case. Any residual target-plan leakage or removal of non-target context in more than 2% of packets, with the upper one-sided 95% bound also above 2%, blocks routing conclusions.

### Panel B: unit-specific departure-plan state

A separate dual-physician panel receives only the operative radiology unit abstraction and unredacted canonical `DS` that was stored by `D`. It does not receive `G/H/V`, Panel A labels, downstream data, POE, comparator results, or the study's expected direction. For each recommendation unit it records exactly one state, with quoted evidence and provenance:

1. `S_DEC`: explicit target-linked informed non-pursuit or target-linked goals-of-care decision.
2. `S_DEF`: explicit target-linked definitive diagnostic or therapeutic management that is documented as replacing interval surveillance. A generic statement that disease was “addressed” is insufficient.
3. `S_EQ`: an executable same-or-more-protective future plan for the same target: compatible chest/thoracic action no later than the operative due window, plus explicit timing/date and accountable recipient/team or documented scheduling. A vague specialty referral, “PCP follow-up,” or an unlinked order is insufficient.
4. `S_CON`: the target is recognized but action/modality/timing conflicts or is less protective, or two target-linked plans conflict, without an explicit rationale that establishes replacement.
5. `S_PART`: target-linked text exists but action, timing, responsibility, active status, or relation to the operative recommendation is incomplete.
6. `S_0`: no target-specific plan or only generic follow-up language.
7. `S_UN`: target linkage, plan meaning, timing, or supersession cannot be determined from the departure-frozen text.

The hierarchy is evidentiary, not a claim of actual clinical correctness. `S_DEC`, then `S_DEF`, then `S_EQ` are text-supported resolutions only when all required elements are explicit. If a higher-level claim is incomplete, the unit falls to `S_PART` or `S_UN`, not to a lower certainty resolution. `S_CON` never resolves a recommendation. Plans documented only after `D` are excluded. Plans in stale artifacts remain descriptive and cannot demonstrate incorporation of the operative component.

### Panel C: content mapping without route labels

A content panel independently classifies each unit's discharge representation as faithful, wholly omitted, incomplete, conflicting, text-supported resolved, or unknown, using the same departure-frozen artifact. It is blind to Panel A, the intended route, and timing bins. The route is then generated deterministically from Panels A-C; reviewers do not directly vote for an intervention.

### Individual-reviewer partial identification

Retain every pre-consensus label. For each unit construct the compatible label set from the two primary reviewers; a third adjudicator adds a label but does not delete the disagreement set. Primary bounds range over all combinations consistent with individual labels and explicit logical constraints. Consensus estimates are secondary. Directional conclusions require the same admission route under (i) consensus, (ii) every individual-reviewer-compatible assignment, and (iii) unknown-compatible adverse assignments. This prevents kappa or forced consensus from masquerading as ground truth.

## Two-stage admission-routing gate

First derive a unit status, then conservatively aggregate to the admission.

### Stage 1: is an observable active unresolved unit present?

For each unit in `E_A`:

* `U_RESOLVED` if `S in {S_DEC,S_DEF,S_EQ}`;
* `U_OPEN` if `K_OPEN`, `S in {S_0,S_PART,S_CON}`, and `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING` if `K_OPEN`, `S in {S_0,S_PART,S_CON}`, but `G_u<24h` or the unit clock is unresolved;
* `U_CLOSED` if `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER` if `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, `U_BOUNDARY`, unreadable content, or reviewer-compatible assignments cross these states.

An admission with any `U_DEFER` is `A_DEFER` for primary routing. An admission with any `U_TIMING` and no `U_DEFER` is `A_TIMING`; it is a timing-limited case and cannot support a content-route conclusion. If all units are resolved/closed, it is `A_NOACTION`. Otherwise it enters Stage 2.

### Stage 2: propagation versus reconciliation

Among admissions with one or more `U_OPEN`:

* `A_AUTO`: every open unit is `K_OPEN + S_0 + wholly omitted`, all non-open units are reproducibly resolved/closed, no unit-boundary uncertainty exists, and there is no cross-unit target/action/timing conflict. This nominates clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation of the exact radiology payload; it never authorizes silent insertion.
* `A_RECON`: any open unit is incomplete or conflicting (`S_PART`, `S_CON`, incomplete/conflicting content), or the admission contains multiple plans whose interaction cannot be represented by independent copying. The whole admission is routed to side-by-side clinician reconciliation.
* `A_MIXED`: at least one pure-omission unit and at least one reconciliation unit. `A_MIXED` is a named subset of `A_RECON`, never an automation success; report it separately to quantify the harm of recommendation-unit-only routing.

This worst-case aggregation is conservative but matches the admission-level discharge artifact. A recommendation-unit analysis alone may suggest many copyable omissions while concealing that they occur in admissions that also contain conflicts. Conversely, if `A_MIXED` is rare and unit and admission conclusions agree, a future system may safely implement unit-specific routing after prospective validation.

## Primary estimands and intervention-priority rule without unverifiable utilities

All quantities use the all-opportunity admission denominator `N_A` unless stated otherwise. Report subject-clustered simultaneous 95% intervals, reviewer-compatible bounds, unknown-compatible bounds, and unit-boundary bounds.

### Admission-level burdens

* `B_AUTO = 100*n(E_A and A_AUTO)/N_A`.
* `B_RECON = 100*n(E_A and A_RECON)/N_A`, with `B_MIXED` separately.
* `B_DEFER = 100*n(E_A and A_DEFER)/N_A`.
* `B_TIMING = 100*n(E_A and A_TIMING)/N_A`.
* `B_NOACTION = 100*n(E_A and A_NOACTION)/N_A`.

Also report the opportunity fraction `100*n(E_A)/N_A`; every category, including zero and unknown, must sum to `N_A` after adding non-`E_A` chronology states.

### Recommendation-unit workload, secondary

Using `N_R`, report units per 100 index admissions and the fractions `U_RESOLVED`, pure omission, incomplete/conflicting, closed, and defer. Report the within-admission unit-count distribution and intraclass correlation. Do not use `N_R` as if units were independent. The key multiplicity diagnostic is

`D_MIX = P(admission is A_RECON or A_DEFER | admission contains at least one unit-level pure omission)`,

with simultaneous bounds. This estimates how often a recommendation-level auto rule would encounter a competing admission-level reason not to auto-route.

### Routing composition contrasts

* `RD_ROUTE = [n(A_AUTO)-n(A_RECON)]/N_A`.
* `RD_MIX = n(A_MIXED)/N_A`.
* Reclassification table from inherited `Y/Z` to `A_AUTO/A_RECON/A_DEFER/A_NOACTION`, retaining all unknowns.

These are workload composition contrasts, not treatment-effect or utility contrasts.

### Predeclared priority decision

The retrospective study may nominate a **prospective validation priority**, not deployment:

1. **Auto-first silent validation** only if reliability and feasibility gates pass; the simultaneous lower reviewer/unknown-compatible bound for `RD_ROUTE` is above zero; `B_AUTO` has a lower bound above zero; and the upper bound for `D_MIX` is no greater than 0.10. The 0.10 value is an operational purity margin for deciding whether an auto-route classifier is worth validating, not a patient-harm utility. It must be approved prospectively by clinical governance; if governance does not approve it before outcome analysis, no auto-first choice is made.
2. **Reconciliation-first silent validation** only if the simultaneous upper bound for `RD_ROUTE` is below zero and `B_RECON` has a lower bound above zero.
3. **Two-lane silent validation** if both burdens have lower bounds above zero but neither dominates, provided `A_AUTO` remains identifiable and `D_MIX` passes the approved purity margin. The gate keeps lane outcomes separate.
4. **Reconciliation-only validation** if pure unit omissions exist but `D_MIX` exceeds its margin or admission routes are unstable; this directly falsifies recommendation-unit-only auto-routing.
5. **No retrospective intervention selection** if governance has not fixed the purity margin, reliability/precision fails, or unknown-compatible bounds cross clinically different priorities.

No net-benefit, threshold-probability, or implied false-positive/false-negative exchange rate is computed. A site can later combine these transparent burdens with externally specified costs and harms, but MIMIC cannot identify them.

## Clustered inference, multiplicity, adjudication uncertainty, and feasibility

The subject/index admission is the resampling unit. Recommendation units never receive independent bootstrap weights. Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates. Each replicate carries all `Y`, `J_A`, unit inventories, route cells, admission burdens, unit workloads, reclassification, timing controls, comparator weights, and every uncertainty bound.

Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family for the decision-facing set: `B_AUTO`, `B_RECON`, `B_MIXED`, `B_DEFER`, `B_TIMING`, `RD_ROUTE`, `D_MIX`, the portable comparator contrast, and retained availability contrasts. Report pointwise intervals only as descriptive. Do not create dozens of interval/action/service subgroup claims; prespecified subgroup tables are exploratory and use the same family or false-discovery control, with no intervention-priority conclusion from a subgroup.

Report pre-consensus confusion matrices, raw agreement, category-specific agreement, kappa/Gwet sensitivity where meaningful, reviewer-specific route rates, confidence, and reasons for uncertainty. Reliability gates are at least 90% raw agreement and kappa or Gwet AC1 at least 0.70 for eligibility, unit boundary, `K`, `S`, content, and final derived route. If prevalence makes kappa unstable, both kappa and AC1 are shown; the raw-agreement and reviewer-compatible route-stability gates still apply. Consensus never repairs a failed gate.

### Census and event/precision gates

The confirmatory analysis reviews the full frozen `N_A`, not an outcome-enriched sample. A stratified pilot of 50 admissions may refine only the manual, redaction, and unit-boundary codebook before labels are wiped and the final protocol/hash is frozen; pilot outcomes cannot set thresholds or exclusions.

Directional routing conclusions require all of the following:

* `N_A>=200`, `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, no duplicate canonical `DS`, and at least 60 admissions in `E_A`;
* at least 30 admissions in each route for a route-specific priority claim; at least 40 total `A_AUTO+A_RECON` admissions for a two-lane claim;
* simultaneous interval half-width no greater than 5 admissions per 100 `N_A` for each claimed all-`N_A` burden, and no greater than 10 percentage points for `D_MIX`;
* `K_UNKNOWN + S_UN + U_BOUNDARY + unknown content <=10%` within `E_A`, and the same priority under all compatible bounds;
* redaction audit, prohibited-field scan, reviewer reliability, and leave-one-reviewer-out route stability all pass.

Counts below these gates are inconclusive, not evidence of absence. If all observed `A_AUTO+A_RECON` events are zero, report the simultaneous upper bound; do not call content intervention unnecessary unless a separately approved operational minimum lies above that bound.

For `X_PORT`, retain the parent's gates: at least 100 adjudicated comparator admissions, 30 in its ample stratum, 20 propagation/reconciliation admissions, no empty pulmonary-supported standardization cell, maximum normalized weight `<=0.10`, and effective sample size `>=50`. Failure makes specificity inconclusive only.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. **Source/key invariance:** recompute all five source hashes and schemas, stream complete sources, and stop on hash/schema drift, duplicate canonical `DS`, denominator-hash drift, impossible timestamps, or nonreciprocal operative chains.
2. **Denominator invariance:** `N_A` is identical for chronology, content, unit inventory, adjudication, calibration, and comparator exclusion. Every all-admission marginal sums to `N_A`.
3. **Clock identity:** row-wise verify `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; only `G` defines `H/E_A`.
4. **Departure freeze:** packets containing any `storetime>D`, downstream trace, later encounter, POE, or eventual outcome fail. Replacing a delayed artifact with faithful later text cannot change `Y=A`, `q_N(0)`, or `E_A`.
5. **Unit-boundary adversarial set:** shared action/interval for multiple nodules is one unit; different due intervals are separate units; repeated addendum language is deduplicated; unresolved coreference becomes `U_BOUNDARY`. Code must reproduce minimum/maximum inventories.
6. **Superseding hierarchy:** “PCP follow-up” is `S_0`; target-linked specialist mention without action/timing is `S_PART` or `S_UN`; scheduled compatible CT with timing and responsibility is `S_EQ`; explicit target-linked non-pursuit is `S_DEC`; explicit definitive replacement is `S_DEF`; unexplained incompatible interval is `S_CON`. No later evidence upgrades a state.
7. **No circular direct route voting:** clinical reviewers assign evidence states only. The route is generated by frozen code. Review packets omit route labels, gaps, downstream events, and expected direction.
8. **Redaction leakage:** seed target/action/timing/recipient phrases outside the dictionary. Failure above the 2% packet-error rule blocks `K` and routing conclusions.
9. **Mixed-admission test:** an admission with one pure omission and one conflict must be `A_MIXED` within `A_RECON`, never `A_AUTO`, even if the recommendation-unit majority is copyable.
10. **Unknown worst case:** any decision-relevant reviewer disagreement, `S_UN`, `K_UNKNOWN`, unknown content, or unit-boundary ambiguity routes the primary admission to `A_DEFER`; favorable consensus may appear only secondarily.
11. **Recommendation-unit weighting trap:** duplicating a unit within an admission cannot change admission burdens or priority. Bootstrap weights are constant within subject/admission.
12. **No-utility test:** removal of all decision-curve thresholds must not alter burdens or routes. No result may be described as expected clinical benefit or net benefit.
13. **Priority logic:** synthetic auto-dominant, recon-dominant, mixed, sparse, and bound-crossing datasets must produce respectively auto-first, recon-first, two-lane/reconciliation, inconclusive, and no-selection outputs under the frozen rules.
14. **Chronology sensitivity:** display `G>=12h` and `G>=72h` sensitivities without changing `N_A`; a priority requires directionally compatible 72-hour results. `H_stale` and post-departure `V` never become incorporation failures.
15. **Institutional calibration:** standardized versus matched availability contrasts differing by `>=0.02`, reversing sign, lacking overlap, or having low effective sample size block availability prioritization, not content routing.
16. **Comparator portability:** a broad-versus-`X_PORT` change `>=0.05` or sign reversal prohibits broad-comparator specificity claims. Sparse portable comparison is specificity-inconclusive.
17. **Reviewer perturbation:** leave out each reviewer, confidence tier, unit-count stratum, interval, and action subtype. Any priority driven by one reviewer or unresolved stratum is inconclusive.
18. **POE non-leakage:** adding/removing the optional POE appendix cannot alter `K/S/content/unit/route` labels; an unvalidated POE row never proves supersession.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results

**Supportive for the two-stage gate as the next prospective experiment** requires reproducible unit boundaries and departure-frozen evidence states, acceptable redaction and unknown bounds, at least one nonzero conservatively bounded route burden, adequate events/precision, and stable admission routes under every individual-reviewer-compatible assignment. This supports only a prospective silent validation of the frozen gate.

**Supportive for auto-first validation** requires the preapproved purity margin, all gates, a simultaneous lower bound `RD_ROUTE>0`, nonzero bounded `B_AUTO`, and upper-bound `D_MIX<=0.10`. It supports testing clinician-confirmed prepopulation for `A_AUTO`; it does not support silent automation.

**Supportive for reconciliation-first validation** requires all gates, a simultaneous upper bound `RD_ROUTE<0`, and nonzero bounded `B_RECON`. A high `A_MIXED` burden specifically supports admission-level reconciliation over recommendation-unit auto-routing.

**Supportive for a two-lane validation** occurs when both route burdens are nonzero and precise but neither dominates, while auto-route purity remains acceptable. The lanes must remain analytically and operationally separable.

**Adverse to recommendation-unit-only auto-routing** is an upper purity bound above the approved margin, material `A_MIXED`, unstable unit boundaries, or any compatible assignment that changes an apparently auto admission to reconciliation/defer. **Adverse to retrospective intervention selection** is failure to identify utilities or a governance margin; this does not erase descriptive burden estimates. **Adverse to a discharge-content strategy** can be claimed only if active unresolved route burden is absent with a sufficiently narrow upper bound relative to a prospectively approved operational minimum; otherwise it is merely sparse.

**Inconclusive** includes insufficient opportunities/events, wide simultaneous intervals, poor reviewer reliability, disagreement-compatible route changes, excessive unknowns, redaction leakage, source/chain/key failure, clock leakage, threshold sensitivity reversal, or absent governance approval for the purity margin. Comparator failure makes specificity inconclusive, not pulmonary routing null.

## Mandatory prospective validation after a supportive retrospective result

MIMIC cannot validate a live trigger or compare interventions. Freeze the retrospective extractor, unit schema, route code, and all margins before deployment into a new environment.

### Phase P1: prospective silent validation

Enroll consecutive adult discharges with a newly finalized eligible pulmonary recommendation. Capture immutable report finalization/addendum events, report availability/viewing, discharge draft creation/version/edit/sign/transmission, exact trigger time, every candidate recommendation unit, clinician/team responsibility, target-linked order/referral/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing-risk context, and final departure artifact. Run the gate silently; it must not modify care.

Independent experts, blinded to the system route and later outcomes, adjudicate the same departure-frozen reference. Primary P1 endpoints are admission-route confusion matrix, `A_AUTO` positive predictive value with all uncertainty adverse, mixed-admission miss rate, defer rate, and calibration by site/service. Require at least 100 route-positive admissions and at least 30 in every route for which validation is claimed, or a prespecified maximum accrual period; use site and patient clustering. Re-estimate rather than import MIMIC prevalence. Any temporal leakage, drift, or failure of the preapproved route-purity margin stops progression.

### Phase P2: governed workflow trial

Only after P1, randomize within frozen route strata to usual care versus: clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation for validated `A_AUTO`, and side-by-side reconciliation for validated `A_RECON`. Keep arms separable. Measure inappropriate-plan insertion, clinician correction/dismissal and reasons, accepted artifact fidelity at signature, time and alert burden, responsibility assignment, target-linked order/referral/scheduling, communication receipt, and later completion. Clinical outcomes and harm require longer follow-up and expert safety adjudication. Cluster randomization or stepped implementation may be needed to prevent clinician contamination; the design depends on site workflow and is not inferable from MIMIC.

## What the retrospective experiment can and cannot establish

MIMIC can quantify, conditional on explicit departure-frozen expert labels, storage opportunity; exhaustive discharge states; recommendation-unit multiplicity; text-supported resolutions; admission-level pure omission, reconciliation, mixed, defer, and no-action burdens; reviewer reproducibility and partial-identification bounds; and whether a frozen two-stage gate is sufficiently identifiable and feasible to enter prospective silent validation. It can also calibrate storage timing and portable process specificity.

MIMIC cannot establish recommendation guideline appropriateness, actual report finalization/viewing, author awareness, UI exposure, who was accountable, patient or clinician communication, outside-system plans, validated outpatient ordering/scheduling/completion, malignancy, harm, utilities, intervention effects, or clinical benefit. The canonical discharge note is an observed stored artifact, not a complete contemporaneous chart. Expert agreement is not ground truth.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

Relative to the parent, this child makes the intervention decision more defensible in four ways. First, it replaces unverifiable decision-curve utilities with transparent admission burdens, route composition, a governance-approved purity margin, and an explicit no-selection state. Second, it inventories atomic recommendation units but selects routing at the admission level, exposing mixed admissions that a unit-only estimand would misclassify. Third, it tightens the departure-frozen superseding hierarchy and relabels context review as observable compatibility rather than clinical actionability, reducing hindsight and overclaiming. Fourth, it carries individual-reviewer, unknown, and unit-boundary uncertainty through a conservative two-stage gate with clustered simultaneous inference and precision-based feasibility criteria.

Uncertainty remains substantial. Redacted context can omit facts; the same canonical artifact supplies both observable plan evidence and the content under study; unit boundaries and supersession are text judgments; `G` is storage-to-storage rather than true editing opportunity; and the operational purity margin is governance-dependent. Accordingly, a supportive result nominates a prospective silent validation policy, not a proven intervention or a claim that clinicians failed to act.
