### Analysis and result logic

Retain `e(X) in [0.10,0.90]`, common `e(X)(1-e(X))` clone tilt, stabilized hourly weights, doubly robust risks, and at least 500 subject bootstraps. Add baseline status/missingness. Re-emit attrition after COMFORT exclusion, status-specific overlap/ESS, standardized differences, and every inherited gate.

Emit three machine-checkable decisions:

1. **ANHATD magnitude:** supported only if the lower 95% limit of RD is >+0.05; margin-falsified if the upper limit is <=+0.05; otherwise unresolved.
2. **Respiratory compatibility:** nonharm supported only if the lower 95% confidence limit for `RD_ALDL,L` is >-0.05; adverse if the upper 95% confidence limit for `RD_ALDL,U` is <=-0.05; otherwise unresolved. Bootstrap both bound endpoints and preserve lower<=upper in every replicate.
3. **Clinical branch:** supportive only when ANHATD magnitude and respiratory nonharm are supported, mortality upper-limit harm is <+0.03, both RRT upper-limit harms are <+0.05, and all parent plus code-status gates pass. Adverse if ANHATD upper limit is <=0, respiratory status is adverse, mortality lower-limit harm is >=+0.03, or both RRT lower-limit harms are >=+0.05. Every other result is inconclusive.

Thus +2 ANHATD points can margin-falsify the >5-point claim without implying harm. +7 points with ALDL bounds spanning -20 to +5 is clinically inconclusive, not supportive. Better fluid balance cannot override unresolved or adverse clinical gates.
