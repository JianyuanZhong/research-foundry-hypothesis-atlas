# Semantic falsification of the airway-independent structured-setting signal

## Lead decision requested

This child preserves the population, landmarks, windows, first-stay freezing, physiology, exclusions, and target-independent construction of assessed-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not redefine the clinical target. It asks the narrower question the parent left unresolved: is the highly prevalent airway-independent `>=2` ventilator-setting-family signal specific to ventilation documentation, or is it largely a consequence of repeated setting bundles and general ICU/respiratory documentation density?

The executed result is mixed and fails the gate required to upgrade the setting signal into a clinically interpretable ventilation proxy. Immediate identical bundles and any single family do **not** explain the signal, and generic non-respiratory nursing/vital density alone transports poorly across hospitals. However, a common set-RR/set-VT pair covers 79.5% of positive rows, target record density is strongly correlated with contemporaneous non-target respiratory workflow, and within-hospital models using generic plus non-target respiratory activity discriminate target positivity well (AUC 0.871) but do not transport across hospitals (leave-hospital-out AUC 0.677). The safest conclusion is therefore adverse/mixed: the signal is not mere global chart density or simple copy-forward, but the available data do not establish ventilation-specific clinical state beyond site-specific respiratory charting workflow. Do not upgrade the proxy.

## Claims separated by evidentiary status

### Strongest claim supported before this child

In the exact 3,788 inherited person-landmark rows, two or more plausible structured setting families are recorded in the six hours before the landmark in approximately 92-96% of each airway-lookback group. The parent also established that stronger value-specific chart evidence, intubation notes, and mechanical-ventilation treatment entries were much less prevalent. Thus the parent supported high availability of structured setting documentation, not active invasive ventilation.

### Unresolved hypothesis tested here

Among the exact inherited rows, present-window `>=2` setting-family positivity represents ventilation-focused documentation rather than (a) immediately repeated identical setting bundles, (b) a single component or fixed component pair, or (c) routine ICU/respiratory chart intensity. Falsification requires independent controls, adjacent within-row windows, site and landmark robustness, and clustered uncertainty.

### Strongest claim supported after execution

The `>=2` setting-family signal is not predominantly an immediate identical-bundle artifact and is not dependent on one setting family. Yet its apparent specificity is entangled with non-target respiratory workflow and hospital documentation systems. This supports only a statement about structured respiratory-documentation organization. It does not support active ventilation, invasive modality, continuity, treatment delivery, extubation, durable liberation, clinician intent, quality, causality, or benefit.

## Semantic and component falsification

### Repeated-identical-bundle control

At each setting timestamp, form a sorted signature of distinct `family=value` entries. Compare it with the immediately preceding setting timestamp in that stay. An equal signature within 1,440 minutes is an **immediate repeated-identical bundle**, not proven software copy-forward. A qualifying positive window has at least one timestamp carrying enough target evidence; changed-bundle sensitivity requires at least one signature that differs from the preceding signature (or has no eligible predecessor).

This intentionally cannot distinguish deliberate re-entry of unchanged clinically appropriate settings from software copy-forward. Only chart audit, entry-interface metadata, or clinician adjudication can do that.

### Component dominance

Report each family’s prevalence among positives, all ten family-pair coverages, and reconstruct positivity after removing each family. A single component is decisively dominant only if its removal retains fewer than 50% of original positives. Fixed-pair concentration is reported even when it does not meet that single-component gate.

### Documentation-density controls

Compare the target with:

- Non-target respiratory records, timestamps, labels, and prespecified control-family breadth.
- Generic nursing records, timestamps, and label breadth.
- Generic aperiodic-vital records, timestamps, and populated-value counts.

Descriptive Spearman correlations assess co-documentation. Logistic models predict present-window target positivity using log-transformed density controls plus landmark. Five-fold group cross-validation is conducted both by hospital (transport to unseen sites, no site fixed effects) and by stay with hospital fixed effects (within-site discrimination without leakage of repeated rows from a stay). AUC is interpreted as falsification of independence, not as a clinical prediction endpoint; calibration losses are compared with fold prevalence baselines.

### Robustness and uncertainty

- Row rates and 3,000-replicate percentile intervals clustered separately by stay and hospital.
- Landmark-specific estimates at all five frozen landmarks.
- Equal-hospital summaries among hospitals with at least 10 rows.
- Nine common hospitals contributing at least 10 rows to every frozen group.
- Paired prior/present/future differences, clustered by stay and hospital.
- Future restriction to observation through `L+360`.

## Executed results

### Bundle semantics

Present target positivity was 3,577/3,788 = 94.43% (stay-cluster 95% interval 93.53-95.27%; hospital-cluster interval 90.67-96.44%). Of target-positive rows, 3,280/3,577 = 91.70% contained at least one changed/non-identical bundle. Across 9,002 qualifying bundle timestamps, 930 = 10.33% were immediately identical to the prior signature. Thus simple adjacent identical-bundle repetition does not explain most positive windows. The group-specific repeated-identical fractions were 9.72% (`retained_24h_only`), 4.91% (`retained_6h`), and 16.57% (`unbounded_only`). These are evidence against the prespecified copy-dominance gate, not evidence that software copy-forward is absent.

### Components

Among positive rows, set RR appeared in 86.66%, set VT in 83.20%, machine minute ventilation in 81.13%, machine pressure in 83.31%, and machine tidal volume in 62.04%. Removing any one family retained 89.21-99.27% of original positives, so no single family was indispensable. However, set RR plus set VT co-occurred in 2,845 rows and covered 79.54% of all positives; machine minute ventilation plus machine pressure covered 76.88%. The signal is therefore redundant across families but substantially concentrated in common setting pairs. This concentration is compatible with templated respiratory workflows and prevents interpreting family count as multiple independent clinical confirmations.

### Generic and respiratory chart density

Generic non-respiratory nursing/vital density alone had leave-hospital-out AUC 0.512 and within-hospital leave-stay-out AUC 0.782. It therefore did not satisfy the prespecified `>=0.80` adverse gate and had essentially no discrimination when transported to unseen hospitals. By contrast, adding contemporaneous non-target respiratory activity yielded within-hospital leave-stay-out AUC 0.871, while leave-hospital-out AUC was only 0.677. Target-record count had weak correlations with nursing record count (Spearman 0.083) and negative weak correlation with generic vital activity (about -0.076), but strong correlations with non-target respiratory record count (0.787) and control timestamps (0.782).

This pattern rejects the simple explanation that overall ICU chart volume alone produces the setting signal. It also shows that target positivity is embedded in site-specific respiratory charting intensity. Because the respiratory-control model crossed the adverse AUC gate and transport degraded markedly, documentation specificity beyond respiratory workflow is not established.

### Temporal, landmark, hospital, and observation robustness

Present target positivity exceeded the prior window by 2.56 percentage points (stay-cluster interval 1.55-3.52; hospital-cluster 0.88-5.32) and fell in the future by 8.98 points (stay-cluster -10.12 to -7.86; hospital-cluster -12.83 to -6.87). Changed-bundle positivity behaved similarly (+2.35 points present versus prior; -8.34 future versus present). Non-target respiratory controls also rose present versus prior (+2.03 points) and fell future versus present (-4.38 points), whereas generic nursing and vital presence was nearly flat. The target movement is stronger than the binary respiratory-control movement, but the shared direction and strong count correlations indicate common respiratory workflow rather than an independent state marker.

Present target rates were stable across landmarks: 95.41%, 94.56%, 94.11%, 93.98%, and 93.57% from L=1,440 through 7,200. They were 95.83%, 93.38%, and 93.73% in `retained_24h_only`, `retained_6h`, and `unbounded_only`, respectively. In nine common hospitals with at least 10 rows in every group, corresponding rates were 96.48%, 97.25%, and 94.85%. Yet among 46 hospitals with at least 10 rows, equal-hospital target positivity was 90.03%, with hospital rates ranging from 20% to 100%; equal-hospital changed-bundle positivity was 76.90%, ranging from 0% to 100%. Hospital heterogeneity therefore remains consequential even though aggregate direction is stable.

Observation through `L+360` was available for 3,739/3,788 = 98.71%. Future positivity was 85.45% in all rows and 85.93% in fully observed rows; changed-bundle positivity was 78.25% and 78.74%. Discharge truncation does not explain the future result.

## Falsification decision

Predeclared operational gates in the executed finalizer were:

1. Adverse copy dominance if >50% of qualifying timestamps are immediately identical or fewer than 50% of target-positive rows contain changed evidence: not triggered (10.33%; 91.70%).
2. Adverse single-component dominance if removing any family retains <50% of positives: not triggered (minimum retention 89.21%).
3. Adverse generic non-respiratory density if site-adjusted leave-stay-out AUC is >=0.80: not triggered (0.782; unseen-hospital AUC 0.512).
4. Adverse generic-plus-non-target-respiratory density if site-adjusted leave-stay-out AUC is >=0.80: triggered (0.871; unseen-hospital AUC 0.677).

Because one substantive falsification gate is triggered, pair concentration is high, and hospital heterogeneity is large, the result is **adverse/mixed**. The inherited structured-setting signal must not be upgraded to active invasive ventilation or used as an adjudicated state. A narrower statement survives: the signal is not explained primarily by immediately identical bundles or general non-respiratory chart volume and appears to be a recurring respiratory-documentation pattern.

## Meaning of possible result directions

- Supportive would have required low immediate repetition, robustness to component removal, weak discrimination from all controls (including non-target respiratory activity), stable hospital/landmark results, and target temporal changes not mirrored by controls. Even then, support would be limited to documentation specificity.
- Adverse is observed when controls explain the target, a component/pair dominates, or site adjustment/transport reveals workflow dependence. It blocks proxy upgrading; it does not prove the chart entries are clinically wrong.
- Inconclusive would apply if controls lacked coverage, clustered intervals were directionally unstable, or observation truncation dominated. Those conditions do not explain the principal result here, although the inability to distinguish deliberate re-entry from software copy-forward remains unresolved.

## Essential adjudication and unavailable evidence

Clinical validation requires episode-level review of ventilator flowsheets and device connectivity, respiratory therapist/nursing narrative, airway presence and type, ventilator mode, bedside timestamps, extubation/reintubation events, and ideally waveform/device logs. Interface audit trails are needed to identify true copy-forward versus deliberate unchanged-setting entry. These eICU sources cannot establish invasive status, continuous support, an SBT, durable liberation, quality, intent, causality, or benefit. A representative blinded chart-adjudication study across high- and low-prevalence hospitals is required before any clinical-state use.

## Substantive advance over the parent

The parent established component dominance by structured settings but could not determine what those settings represented. This child directly stress-tests their semantics without changing a single inherited row or target window. It separates three mechanisms: global chart intensity, respiratory-workflow intensity, and immediately repeated bundles; quantifies pair and leave-one-component dominance; and adds within-row temporal, hospital, landmark, common-hospital, clustered, and discharge-observation checks. The advance is a clinically consequential negative boundary: settings appear more specific than generic ICU activity, but not independent enough of site-specific respiratory documentation to justify treating them as a ventilation state. That boundary prevents false certainty while defining the exact adjudication needed for a future validation study.
