# Outcome-blind validity gate for the fixed hour-24 interhospital-transfer risk-marker question

## Decision and unresolved hypothesis

This child preserves the parent's adult first-ICU-stay population, concordant transfer/direct definitions, receiving-ICU hour-24 landmark, hours-24–72 endpoint window, supported 19-hospital restriction, and noncausal risk-marker question. It runs the unresolved prerequisite before any adjusted transfer–outcome analysis: exact new-support labels, clocks, overlap, disposition, and adjudication feasibility.

The unresolved hypothesis is that, among adult first ICU stays alive/in ICU at hour 24, concordant interhospital-transfer provenance provides at least a 2-percentage-point incremental held-out association with first new organ support or hospital death during hours 24–72 after arrival/trajectory, hospital, and observation-process adjustment, and that marker contribution is not attenuated below 2 points by an admissible ordered observation-aware model.

The completed outcome-blind audit is mixed but decision-changing. Measured overlap passes. Strict new-support labels are sparse and source-dependent, especially transferred IMV/RRT. The parent's balanced 240-positive-packet audit and >=100 transferred-event gate are not constructible. A 240-packet broad-trigger audit is constructible but can refine mappings, not establish the parent's component-specific PPV lower bounds. No adjusted transfer–outcome association was estimated.

## Evidence and clinical importance

A severe-AKI cohort found no higher adjusted mortality and identified healthy-enough-to-transfer selection, immortal time, unknown transfer rationale, and unavailable travel time [K1]. A matched ventilated-sepsis study found similar mortality but longer stay, while lacking timed physiology, transfer timing/reason, and sending/receiving-hospital detail [K2]. A transferred-only tertiary-MICU cohort found transfer-day organ dysfunction and arrival oxygen requirement predicted early death, showing why arrival state matters without identifying transfer harm [K3].

The strongest supported claim is limited: transfer provenance marks different selection, arrival severity, observation, and disposition processes, while published adjusted mortality evidence is context-dependent. This audit adds that measured eICU overlap is adequate but competing discharge differs strongly and conservative new-support labels are sparse.

Clinical value is receiving-ICU triage calibration. A validated residual marker could justify retaining provenance in deterioration assessment and collecting missing referral/transport variables. Attenuation would argue against an extra provenance penalty. Endpoint invalidity would show eICU cannot answer this version without adjudication.

This is a risk-marker boundary test, not a transfer-effect study. The model toggle is not a causal intervention because transfer has no coherent manipulable version here and eICU lacks sending-hospital course, referral decisions, transport history, and patients considered but not transferred.

## Overlap audit

An outcome-blind transfer-propensity diagnostic used hospital, demographics, diagnosis, unit type, hospitaladmitoffset, weight, DNR, and apacheApsVar organ variables. Values -1 became missing; no outcomes/support/disposition entered.

Within hospital, 1,430/1,606 transferred stays (89.0%) fell inside direct 1st–99th propensity percentiles, passing >=80%. ATT-weighted direct ESS was 6,597, and maximum absolute weighted SMD was 0.055, passing <0.10.

This demonstrates measured support, not exchangeability. It is a single diagnostic fit; the solver must cross-fit preprocessing/propensity in frozen person folds and repeat on final B0 features. Missing sending/transport variables remain unmeasured.

## Adjudication feasibility

The proposed 40 positive packets per transfer-group/component are not constructible: strict transferred cells have 0 IMV, 22 pressor, 4 RRT. Broad one-source triggers cannot be called truth merely to fill cells.

A demonstrated alternative has 20 broad candidates per group/component (120 packets) plus 60 trigger-negative sentinels per group (120), totaling 240 component packets from 232 unique stays. Review packets hide transfer, hospital, death, and post-event disposition; private design weights retain group/hospital. Source inventory is computationally feasible: treatment rows in 156 packets, respiratory care 20, respiratory chart 151, infusion 106, intake/output 223, note 93, care plan 239.

Two ICU clinicians independently determine whether support occurred, type, genuinely new status after hour 24 versus continuation/delayed documentation, earliest onset, acute/chronic RRT, and indeterminate status; third-clinician adjudication resolves disagreement. Patient-level clustering handles 8 repeated component packets.

This sample can detect gross differential ascertainment and refine mappings. Twenty positives per cell cannot establish lower-95%-bound >=0.70 PPV in every component/group. Activation requires expanded review for precision or a Lead-approved endpoint child.

Clock audit found entry before event in 4,706/1,330,099 respiratory-chart rows and 38,074/870,710 intake/output rows; none of 206,942 note rows. Median/p95 entry delay was 0/100, 0/169, and 18/736 minutes. Conservative labels exclude impossible rows; entry-delay/source-removal checks are mandatory.

## Matched B0 and M1

Both use identical stays, raw pre-landmark rows, outcomes, person folds, and hospital support. All one uniquepid rows remain in one of five hospital/transfer-stratified folds. Preprocessing, overlap, tuning, and calibration occur inside training folds. No discharge, APACHE outcome, post-landmark value, or review decision enters features.

Common six-bin inputs: values, masks, time since last, median/min/max/last, source counts, unique hours, longest gap, and entry delay for vitalPeriodic, vitalAperiodic, lab (revision available by landmark), respiratoryCharting/Care, positive-rate infusions, treatment, intakeOutput, and nurseCharting. Static inputs are demographics, diagnosis, unit/hospital, admission timing/weight, DNR, apacheApsVar (with -1 missing), and hospital metadata.

B0 is a discrete-time multinomial elastic-net over 12 four-hour post-landmark intervals for first deterioration, alive ICU discharge, or continued ICU stay; preserve cause-specific death/support outputs. Compress each pre-landmark family to first-six-hour and 24-hour worst/last, six-bin OLS slope, observed-bin count, longest gap, and entry-delay summaries. Include hospital fixed effects and only transfer interactions with landmark IMV and broad diagnosis. Fit paired transfer/no-transfer models. Report held-out log loss, Brier, calibration, cumulative incidence, and marker contrast with 2,000 person-and-hospital clustered bootstraps.

M1 is a six-step 16–32-state GRU/state-space model using identical raw bins. Values, masks, elapsed time, source counts, and entry delays update state. Auxiliary heads predict next-bin values/masks; a competing-risk head predicts the same 12-interval target. Hospital is regularized; transfer enters only final outcome head. Fit paired versions on identical folds/three seeds.

M1 preserves asynchronous organ ordering and informative observation timing lost by B0 extrema/slopes; it cannot recover sending history or mechanism. Prefer M1 only if calibration-slope deviation is no worse than B0 by >0.10, Brier no worse by >0.005, mask-calibration error <=0.05 overall/by group, three seeds agree, and source/downsampling checks are stable. Otherwise B0 governs.

Measured final label/overlap runtime was ~26 seconds on 8 CPUs plus ~14 seconds packet inventory. Solver estimates: extraction/B0 8–16 CPUs/64–96 GiB, 1–3 hours; bootstrap 16 CPUs, 2–4 hours; M1 one allocated A100-80GB plus 8 CPUs/64 GiB, 1–3 hours/fold. Solver estimates are unmeasured.

## Activation, falsification, interpretation

Do not fit/interpret B0/M1 until:

1. Semantics/support: >=90% concordance, >=15 hospitals, >=1,200 transferred (passed); >=100 transferred primary events (failed: 75).
2. Expanded blinded review: pooled and group/component weighted PPV >=0.80, lower 95% bound >=0.70, absolute group PPV difference <=0.10, sentinel false-negative yield <=0.05 (not yet testable).
3. Cross-fitted overlap: >=80% inside support, ESS >=50% transferred, |SMD|<0.10 (diagnostic passed).
4. Observation-mask calibration <=0.05 overall/by group; PPV/missing-window differences <=0.10; stable after opportunity downsampling/sparse-quartile exclusion.
5. Four-state competing analysis plus death/support decomposition, DNR and near-landmark-death exclusions, live ICU/hospital discharge.
6. No site >25% transferred information; leave-site direction reversals <=20%; cross-hospital repeat-person exclusion changes contrast <1 point.
7. Entry availability, duplicate collapse, source removal, and hour-18/30 checks change contrast <1 point and do not cross both 0 and 2.

Supportive after gates: both B0/admissible M1 marker contrasts >=2 points, M1 lower bound >0, attenuation <50%, and transfer improves held-out calibration/risk separation. This supports only a receiving-ICU risk marker.

Adverse: gates pass but B0 upper bound <2 points, or admissible M1 upper bound <2 with >=50% attenuation. This favors measured trajectory/hospital/observation explanations, not equivalence, benefit, or no causal harm.

Inconclusive: interval spans 0 and 2; models disagree without M1 admissibility; or label/event/overlap/site/disposition gates fail. The observed result is inconclusive for transfer but adverse for endpoint readiness. Continue only blinded adjudication. Death-only or longer-window redesign requires a scientific child, not compiler repair.
