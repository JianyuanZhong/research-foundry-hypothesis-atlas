# Dated metabolic change and the transition to cardiometabolic multimorbidity

## Deliverable and unresolved claim

The scientific deliverable is a newly fitted, participant-level locked comparison of a current-level prognostic model with a dated two-panel transition model. Completion requires: a frozen codebook and cohort flow; repeat-measurement availability and date-integrity diagnostics; fitted coefficients and uncertainty; transition-specific five-year risks; locked-test calibration, Brier score, discrimination and decision-curve outputs; 200-resample participant-level uncertainty intervals; and a negative-control/permutation audit. A readiness check or a significant coefficient is not a solved clinical result.

Available evidence supports the narrower claim that baseline glycaemic, lipid, renal and anthropometric measurements are associated with later coded cardiometabolic disease and that disease progression can be represented with multistate models. A recent Europe PMC search also returned UK Biobank studies of baseline atherogenic indices and cardiometabolic progression. Neither establishes that an observed within-person change, after conditioning on the latest level and repeat-measurement selection, adds reliable information about a clinically distinct transition.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In UK Biobank adults with two dated, complete biomarker panels and fewer than two cardiometabolic disease domains at the second panel, annualized worsening in HbA1c and triglycerides and/or falling HDL is jointly associated with a higher cause-specific hazard of entering a second cardiometabolic domain beyond the latest levels and baseline state. The added signal is larger for the 1-domain-to-2-domain transition than for 0-to-1 onset, and is not of comparable size for incident acute appendicitis.

This is a prognostic association and incremental-information estimand, not the causal effect of changing a biomarker, evidence of mediation, or evidence that a monitoring intervention improves outcomes.

## Clinical importance and substantive advance

A current value can look acceptable while a recent dated deterioration indicates a different near-term risk. If the change signal survives conditioning on the latest level, interval length and measurement availability, it could justify a prospective strategy of repeat testing or risk review in selected patients. If it adds no reliable information, trajectory alerts would add burden without a defensible signal. The substantive advance is a direct test of dated incremental information and illness deepening, rather than another cross-sectional biomarker association or an uncalibrated black-box score.

The result is deliberately limited to two observed panels. Field instance numbers are not treated as elapsed time, no biomarker instances 2–3 are invented, and two points are not described as a latent long-term trajectory.

## Population and time

Include participants aged 40–69 inclusive at recruitment, with valid sex, complete core HbA1c/triglyceride/HDL values at both main instances 0 and 1, valid dated assessment panels with date 1 later than date 0 and a primary interval of 1–10 years, no contradiction across nonmissing assessment dates, and fewer than two cardiometabolic domains by panel 1. Exclude those dead before panel 1. These criteria define the repeat-panel population and do not imply representativeness of UKB.

Define panel change for marker j as (value at date 1 minus value at date 0) divided by elapsed years. Reverse-code HDL so worsening is positive. Standardize changes using fitting buckets only. The primary landmark is date 1 plus a 365-day washout; events during washout are excluded. Follow to first dated second-domain event, death, administrative outcome end or five years after entry. Report the trusted administrative end and five-year opportunity. Secondary washouts are 0 and 2 years; secondary intervals are 0.5–15 years.

Participants with zero domains start in state 0; those with exactly one domain start in state 1 and contribute the first additional-domain outcome. Those with at least two at panel 1 are excluded. Split deterministically by `eid` using catalog buckets 0–59 fit, 60–69 validation, 70–79 locked test, and 80–99 reserved and never read for fitting, tuning, threshold choice or subgroup decisions. Record the catalog namespace and hash construction.

## Baseline, alternative and estimands

The simple baseline M0 is a cause-specific Cox model for time to the first second-domain event, using latest HbA1c, triglycerides and HDL; latest creatinine and cystatin C in a prespecified secondary model; baseline state; age, sex, latest BMI, valid blood pressure and smoking; interval length; and missingness indicators. Restricted-cubic-spline knots are fixed from fit data, with robust participant-level standard errors. All models use identical cohort, delayed entry, censoring, codebook and split.

The substantive alternative M1 is a transition-specific multistate illness-deepening model with states 0 (no domain), 1 (one domain), 2 (at least two domains) and D (death). Permit 0→1, 0→D, 1→2 and 1→D, with a direct 0→2 transition only if dated outcome evidence supports it; otherwise treat it as interval-censored in a predeclared sensitivity analysis. M1 uses the same covariates and latest levels as M0, then adds the three standardized dated changes and allows their coefficients to differ between 0→1 and 1→2. This tests whether change is specifically informative about accumulation of a second domain; it is not merely a contest for a small predictive score gain.

The primary estimand is the vector of conditional cause-specific log-hazard coefficients for the three changes in the 1→2 transition, with a joint null that all are zero. Report marker-wise 95% intervals, the joint test, transition-specific five-year cumulative incidence at fixed low/typical/high change profiles with latest values held equal, and the contrast between M0 and M1. Prognostic standardization is not a causal intervention contrast.

## Measurement process, uncertainty and locked evaluation

Among participants with a valid panel-0 core panel, fit a panel-1 availability model using only panel-0 values, age, sex, BMI, smoking and blood pressure. Report availability by age, sex and panel-0 risk strata. If positivity is adequate, repeat estimates with stabilized inverse-probability-of-repeat weights truncated at the 1st and 99th percentiles; otherwise label transport analysis inconclusive. Audit interval length, marker ranges, missingness, extreme changes and correlation of change with latest value. No imputation or interpolation of instances 2–3 is permitted.

Fit and select only in buckets 0–69. Evaluate once in buckets 70–79. Report five-year cause-specific and competing-death cumulative incidence, time-dependent and integrated Brier score, discrimination, calibration slope/intercept and error, fixed-threshold decision-curve net benefit at 5%, 10% and 20%, restricted mean event-free survival, and results by sex, age below/above 55 and baseline state. Use paired 200-resample participant-level bootstrap intervals for locked metric differences and save seed and indices. The clinical-information threshold is fixed before test evaluation: call incremental usefulness only if the joint change evidence is directionally worsening with a 95% interval excluding the joint null, the locked-test Brier or calibration/decision-curve result improves by at least 0.01 absolute without calibration harm in either sex or baseline-state subgroup, and the result survives the key sensitivity analyses.

Falsification includes shuffling change vectors within baseline state and sex in fit/validation, re-running the frozen pipeline, and requiring no comparable signal. Acute appendicitis is a prespecified disease-specificity negative control. A comparable appendicitis association, a permutation signal, or an apparent gain that disappears on locked calibration falsifies the incremental/disease-specific claim.

## Interpretation

Supportive evidence is a directionally consistent joint worsening signal, especially in 1→2, locked improvement meeting the fixed threshold without subgroup calibration harm, persistence across washout/interval/competing-death analyses, a materially weaker appendicitis association, and no permutation signal. It supports incremental prognostic information in the selected repeat-panel population.

Adverse evidence is a null or reverse signal, disappearance after latest levels/interval and measurement process are included, similar appendicitis performance, a permutation signal, or no locked calibrated improvement. This rejects the proposed incremental-information claim; it does not prove biomarker change is harmless or that monitoring is ineffective.

Inconclusive evidence is insufficient complete dated pairs or 1→2 events, code/date correspondence failure, inadequate repeat-measurement positivity, intervals too wide to exclude clinically important effects, or severe subgroup calibration failure. Inconclusive is not converted into support or refutation.

Computationally checkable claims are file/schema/hash bindings, joins, cohort counts, date ordering, value rules, code/date extraction, split integrity, model fits, estimates, intervals, cumulative incidence, calibration, Brier/decision curves, permutation behavior and sensitivity outputs. Clinical adjudication is unavailable: outcomes are coded rather than chart-reviewed; treatment changes, adherence, repeat-assessment indication, socioeconomic confounding and exact assay dictionaries are incomplete. Validated phenotype algorithms, chart review, treatment data, external validation and an intervention study would be required before a universal monitoring policy or causal clinical claim.

## Alternatives and demonstration dispositions

The expert UKB seed 09 is adapted concretely: its question about metabolic deterioration beyond current levels becomes the dated two-panel, transition-specific estimand above. The other imported UKB seeds remain deferred: organ-age/proteomics, infection/protein effect modification, CHIP sequence, cancer staging/treatment, sleep regularity/accelerometry, and PRS interactions lack verified local inputs or require a different question. The fat-distribution seed lacks raw imaging; the kidney-discordance seed lacks acute-care laboratory trajectories.

The three demonstrations were considered as method ideas, not topic requirements. Delphi's learned dated disease histories are a plausible future adaptation, but two verified biomarker panels do not justify a rich sequence model and the external Danish validation is unavailable. ALADYNOULLI's longitudinal latent/Gaussian-process approach would require assumptions and, for its genetic analysis, unavailable verified genetic inputs; an EHR-only latent model is deferred because it would not resolve the present two-panel uncertainty. Oncoformer's multimodal EHR-plus-image design cannot be reproduced because raw images are absent; a lab-only transformer could be revisited only if it tests transition heterogeneity beyond M1 with locked calibration and uncertainty, rather than complexity for its own sake. The accessible cancer main article and full STAR Methods were not inspected and are not claimed here; only the available supplement/summary limitation is relied on.

Before method selection, the baseline and M1 alternative were compared on the same cohort, outcomes and split. M0 loses transition-specific information by collapsing 0→1 and 1→2; M1 can reveal whether change predicts illness deepening specifically, while remaining interpretable and computationally feasible. A learned sequence alternative was deferred because only two dated biomarker observations are verified. CPU-first is appropriate: Cox and multistate fitting are expected to fit within the solver planning envelope of 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB RAM and 28800 seconds, but that full-fit estimate is unverified. Discovery source probing measured header/availability on all four relevant UKB CSVs and the catalog; no GPU probe was needed. Future solver compute must be measured from its declared implementation, not inferred from the A100 inventory.
