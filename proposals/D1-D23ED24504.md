# Nested documentation-surface HCC experiment: fixed-capacity incremental value of eventual report content

## 1. Assigned repair and scientific decision

This child repairs one defect in valid parent [prior hypothesis] while preserving its identifiable design. In the parent, the content model had information `B + content`, whereas the independently fitted surface model had `B + surface`. Those models were not nested, and a positive surface contrast automatically vetoed support. That rule could reject genuine incremental semantic signal merely because report length, field completion, or formatting also ranks documentary M2.

The repair uses one shared hierarchy of information:

- `Z`: nonsemantic acquisition and patient variables;
- `S`: documentation-surface variables;
- `X`: blinded, adjudicated meanings of eventual stored CT/MRI report text.

The independently optimized reduced comparator is `R(Z,S)`; the full model is `G(Z,S,X)`. Thus every nonsemantic acquisition and surface variable available to the full model is also available to its reduced comparator. A second contrast masks all `X` values in the already fitted `G` while leaving `Z,S` unchanged. The surface contrast `R(Z,S)-B(Z)` diagnoses surface signal but is not a veto. This is the substantive change. Population, source/clock/report/pathology coupling, temporal split, fixed-K ranking-composition estimand, whole-patient report-access bounds, and non-causal limits are retained.

The downstream clinical decision is deliberately preliminary: whether these data justify acquiring real report-version/access logs and conducting a prospective silent-mode study of scarce multidisciplinary review capacity. This experiment is not a triage, referral, resection, transplant, or treatment rule.

## 2. Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and advance

### Strongest claim supported before the experiment

The frozen HCC snapshot contains linkable patient/encounter rows, source-documented procedure names with start/end fields, examinations with acquisition-like start fields and eventual narrative report text, and same-encounter untimed pathology narratives. It can support a retrospective, future-defined source cohort and an encounter-documentary M2 endpoint.

It cannot establish that a listed operation was completed; that pathology came from the selected resection specimen; that M2 wording represents biological microvascular invasion under a specimen-linked standard; or that the eventual examination text was authored, final, released, unchanged, or viewed before any decision. It contains no report-version/access log, pathology timestamp, specimen/accession/slide/block/sampling identifier, source images, referral roster, reviewer action, recurrence, survival, treatment response, harm, cost, equity measure, or clinical benefit.

A 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis of 52 imaging deep-learning studies (19,531 patients) reported lower summary performance under independent external than internal validation and emphasized heterogeneity, scarce independent validation, and the need for prospective multicenter evaluation (Feng, Qu, and Han, J Med Internet Res 2026;28:e82000, DOI 10.2196/82000, PMCID PMC12954728). I inspected the frozen Europe PMC full-text XML, source [source checksum]. This establishes clinical motivation and methodological caution, not validity of report-derived concepts or this local documentary endpoint.

The research-ambition README was inspected. The natural-history and Bayesian demonstrations have frozen full articles and supplements; the Cell cancer article body and full STAR Methods remain unavailable, with only metadata and a supplement available. No unavailable paper text is claimed as inspected, and none determines this topic or method.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

> Among adults in the future-defined first eligible source-documented HCC resection frame, with same-encounter documentary HCC pathology and no recorded qualifying HCC treatment in the preceding year, does a locked full model using eventual stored CT/MRI report meanings capture more encounter-documentary M2 cases in exactly the same fixed top-K capacity than (i) an independently optimized nested baseline containing all of the same nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface information and (ii) the same fitted full model after complete semantic-content masking, by more than 5 captures per 100 ranking positions in both 2020 and 2021 across every admissible coupled source/outcome state?

The margin of 5 captures per 100 fixed positions is prespecified as a ranking-composition threshold. It is not a sensitivity, PPV, treatment effect, utility, service benefit, or patient-benefit threshold.

### Substantive advance

A supportive result would be stronger than “content beats acquisition variables” and more interpretable than “surface is null.” It would show that adjudicated stored-text meaning adds fixed-capacity retrospective capture beyond an independently optimized model with the identical acquisition/documentation surface, and that the fitted full score actually depends on those meanings when masked. A positive surface signal may coexist with that result and would flag documentation-process dependence and transportability risk. It would not erase incremental content evidence.

## 3. Coupled state, clocks, and temporal quarantine

A complete admissible state `theta` jointly records:

1. patient/encounter links and raw row backpointers;
2. the first eligible source-documented procedure episode;
3. procedure start/end interpretation and calendar assignment;
4. recorded prior-treatment status;
5. examination-unit partition, components, acquisition interval, modality, text presence, surface values, and content adjudication;
6. same-encounter pathology composition and set-valued documentary outcome;
7. for the secondary analysis, each whole-patient CT/MRI visibility assignment.

Only source-supported alternatives are admissible. Extrema operate over complete states; the computation may not splice a favorable operation alternative from one state to an examination, pathology, content annotation, or access assignment from another.

A validated non-midnight timestamp is a point. A date-only value or unvalidated midnight is `[date,date+24h)`. Missing, competing, adjacent-day, and discordant values remain alternatives. A procedure row is evidence only of a source-documented procedure entry, not anesthesia, completion, resection, or lack of cancellation.

For `d in {12,24,48,72}` hours define analytical cutoff `c_d=t_op-d`; `d=24` is confirmatory and the others are locked sensitivities. This cutoff is not a clinical decision, report-release, referral, or access time.

Development is 2015-2018. The 2019 cohort is used only for hyperparameter choice, preprocessing lock checks, and descriptive calibration. Separate locked tests are 2020 and 2021. A patient whose admissible state can enter a later period is quarantined from all earlier fitting and tuning. A patient ambiguous between test years remains one coupled alternative set and cannot appear twice. Data from 2022 onward are ascertainment/drift audits only and cannot alter a prior score, threshold, K, dictionary, annotation rubric, or conclusion.

## 4. Population and documentary outcome

For each `d`, include one row per patient satisfying all of:

1. age at least 18 in the selected linked encounter;
2. the patient's earliest eligible source-documented procedure episode under a versioned hepatobiliary-surgery dictionary;
3. at least one same-encounter pathology row whose composed text explicitly supports HCC after blinded pathology adjudication;
4. no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[c_d-365d,c_d)`, searched patient-wide in dated procedure, medication, and non-drug-order rows.

Before any outcome inspection, two hepatobiliary surgeons independently classify every distinct procedure/order/medication source label encountered by the rule as include, exclude, qualifying prior treatment, or unresolved; a third resolves disagreements. The signed dictionary stores the exact normalized and raw labels, source table/column, version, adjudicator decisions, and hash. Unresolved labels create state alternatives or exclusion, never silent negatives. This is “recorded-treatment-naive,” not biologically treatment-naive. Untimed diagnosis rows may corroborate wording but cannot date treatment or establish a negative history.

For the endpoint, exact-deduplicate and concatenate in raw order every pathology row with the selected `(患者主索引, 就诊号)`, using `病理`, `检查所见`, and `检查诊断`. Two Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to reports, scores, year, and access analysis, apply a frozen rubric; a third resolves disagreements:

- `Y=1`: M2 is explicitly anchored to microvascular invasion or an HCC MVI grade in the composed encounter text.
- `Y=0`: an anchored M0/M1 statement excludes M2 in that same relevant context.
- `Y={0,1}`: grade missing, unanchored, boilerplate, contradictory, attribution uncertain, or text inadequate.

Unknowns stay set-valued in every primary calculation; they are not coded negative or imputed. Parser-only, adjudicated, and all-row outcome envelopes are reported separately. Interpretation requires at least 85% anchored adjudicated grade and at least 50 lower-envelope M2 events in each claimed test year. These are feasibility gates, not proof of specimen linkage or biological truth.

## 5. Examination units and exact information sets

Construct units before temporal filtering as `(患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号)`, retaining raw ordinals and all component rows. Blank `检查号` is identity-unknown and cannot provide identity-safe semantics. Conflicting modality labels, component starts more than 24 hours apart, or evidence of merged identities generate coupled alternatives using deterministic normalized `(检查, calendar-date-of-start)` partitions; no row is silently discarded.

For each patient and cutoff, retain at most the latest eligible whole CT and latest eligible whole MRI with acquisition interval wholly compatible with `[c_d-90d,c_d)`, ordered by latest admissible component start, raw ordinal, frozen patient hash. Concatenate complete-unit `检查所见` and `检查诊断` fields in raw component order. No phrase-level selection is allowed.

Two abdominal radiologists independently classify each selected unit as CT, MRI, other, or unresolved and annotate: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites. Each concept is `present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned/source-text-unavailable`; a third reader resolves disagreement. Readers are blinded to pathology, score, year, and access results. This adjudicates what stored text says, not what images or biology show. Report agreement, unresolved fractions, and adjudication completeness; do not report feature sensitivity, specificity, or PPV against unavailable images.

Define the shared blocks exactly:

- `Z` (nonsemantic acquisition/patient): age, sex; CT/MRI/other unit indicators; eligible-unit count; acquisition-to-cutoff recency; component count; identity/accession ambiguity; source-text-unavailable indicator; and machine model `机器型号` grouped by development frequency with rare/unseen categories. No identifier value itself enters.
- `S` (documentation surface): for each retained whole modality unit, Unicode code-point count of the untrimmed concatenated text; separate nonblank indicators for `检查所见` and `检查诊断`; digit, Unicode punctuation, whitespace, Han-character, and other-character proportions with denominator fixed to total code points; component-row count; blank/conflicting `检查号`; and field-separator count. `S` contains no token, n-gram, concept flag, hand-selected phrase, pathology, diagnosis, laboratory, or clinical-document content. Acquisition recency and machine category remain in `Z` and are not duplicated in `S`.
- `X` (semantic content): the modality-specific adjudicated concept categories above, including explicit uncertain, not-mentioned, and source-text-unavailable levels. The CT and MRI blocks are separate.

For every numeric surface variable, source-unavailable or hypothetically hidden units are encoded as standardized zero plus a dedicated unavailable indicator; observed empty text remains distinguishable from an unavailable report. Categorical surface variables use a dedicated unavailable level. These encodings are created once from development data and are identical in `R` and `G`.

All normalization conventions, Unicode version, field separators, category levels, missing codes, and development means/scales are frozen before 2020 outcomes are inspected.

## 6. Statistical family and nested models

Use additive elastic-net logistic regression with an unpenalized intercept. One-hot categorical variables use frozen reference levels; continuous variables are centered and scaled with 2015-2018 statistics; zero-variance features are removed by a logged development-only rule. No interactions, splines, or free text are allowed. The mixing grid is `alpha in {0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1}`; for each alpha use 100 log-spaced lambdas from the development `lambda_max` to `10^-4 lambda_max`. Ties in tuning use, in order: larger penalty, fewer nonzero coefficients, smaller alpha, lexical parameter serialization.

Fit these models independently on 2015-2018 within each complete source/outcome state and choose each model's hyperparameters independently on 2019 by worst-state fixed-K capture, never by 2020/2021:

- `B(Z)`: acquisition-only diagnostic baseline.
- `R(Z,S)`: independently optimized reduced comparator.
- `G(Z,S,X)`: full model. Its `Z,S` columns, preprocessing, missing codes, and column order are byte-identical to those in `R); only `X` is appended.
- `Gmask`: no refit. Apply the fitted `G` after replacing every `X` category, including semantic not-mentioned/uncertain indicators, with the training-defined source-text-unavailable level. Keep every `Z,S` value unchanged.

The reduced model is nested in information, not forced to inherit full-model coefficients. This matters: `R` is allowed to optimize all available nonsemantic signal fairly, while `Gmask` asks whether the fitted full score itself relies on semantics. Platt calibration on 2019 is descriptive only, uses no test data, and cannot alter ordering or rescue a failed fixed-K result.

No pathology, diagnosis, lab, clinical-document text, identifier, calendar year, post-cutoff acquisition, test annotation frequency, or access allocation may influence predictors, tuning, K, or ties.

## 7. Fixed-capacity estimands

For state `theta`, offset `d`, year `a`, and `q in {0.05,0.10,0.20}`, let `N_a(theta,d)` be eligible index size before pathology grade, semantic annotation, or test outcome is consulted. Freeze

`N_ref = min_{theta,d,a in 2015..2019} N_a(theta,d)`, and `K_q=floor(q*N_ref)`.

The confirmatory capacity is `q=0.10`; require `N_ref>=500`, `K_.10>=50`, and at least `K_.10` eligible patients in every required test state. The 0.05 and 0.20 capacities are locked sensitivities and are reported only where `K_q>=50`; failure of a sensitivity capacity does not redefine or rescue the primary K.

Rank by locked score, then frozen SHA-256 patient tie key, then raw episode backpointer. Select exactly K whole patients. For `M in {B,R,G,Gmask}`:

`T_M(theta,d,a,q) = 100/K_q * sum_{i in TopK_M} Y_i(theta)`.

With set-valued outcomes, report exact lower and upper top-K sums. This estimand is documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed ranking positions in a retrospectively defined eventual corpus. It is not prevalence, recall, availability, utilization, referral opportunity, clinical utility, or benefit.

Confirmatory contrasts are:

- `Delta_nested = T_G - T_R`: content added to the independently optimized identical-surface baseline.
- `Delta_mask = T_G - T_Gmask`: effect of removing content from the same fitted full model while preserving surface.
- `Delta_surface = T_R - T_B`: diagnostic surface contribution; not part of the support conjunction and never a veto.

The universal primary hypothesis requires both `Delta_nested>5` and `Delta_mask>5` at `d=24,q=.10` in both 2020 and 2021 across every admissible coupled state. Exactly equal K makes model utilization identical by construction.

## 8. Inference and semantic falsification

Use 2,000 paired patient-cluster bootstrap replicates. Each replicate resamples whole patients, reconstructs linked coupled states, refits `B,R,G`, repeats 2019 tuning, applies `Gmask` without refitting, and recomputes rankings and outcome extrema. Model comparisons use the same resampled patients and state. More than 2.5% failed replicates, a state dropped from a replicate, or an undefined standard error makes the affected family inconclusive.

For the confirmatory family, form max-T simultaneous one-sided 97.5% bounds over both confirmatory contrasts, both test years, and every required source/outcome state at `d=24,q=.10`. With bootstrap standard error `s_j`, use the 97.5th percentile of the maximum centered studentized error across coordinates for lower bounds; analogously use the maximum negative centered error for upper bounds. A zero/undefined `s_j` is inconclusive unless an exact finite-state proof supplies the corresponding bound. Sensitivity `d,q` coordinates and access cells have prespecified separate simultaneous families and cannot rescue the primary family. Report `Delta_surface` with simultaneous two-sided 95% bounds as a diagnostic.

Perform 1,000 development/tuning-only whole-block permutations. Move the complete `X` block among patients within frozen strata of year, modality pattern, unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and deciles of the `S` score; keep `Z,S`, outcomes, and source rows fixed. Refit `G`, repeat 2019 tuning, and recompute the scalar conjunction statistic
`H=min_j[min(Delta_nested_j-5, Delta_mask_j-5)]`
over required 2020/2021 coordinates using the locked evaluation pipeline. The one-sided randomization p-value is `(1 + count(H_perm >= H_observed))/1001`; supportive interpretation requires `p<=0.025`. This falsifies semantic alignment while preserving surface structure. It does not validate concepts against images.

The result table must include model/version, contrast, year, `d,q,N_ref,K`, state and outcome-completion identifiers, point and simultaneous bounds, top-K patient-set hash, lower/upper event sums, event and eligibility gates, annotation quality, surface-signal category, permutation result, source/code/dictionary/rubric hashes, and raw-result locator.

## 9. Exact whole-patient report-access bounds

No observed report-access fraction exists. The secondary analysis therefore enumerates hypothetical whole-unit visibility, never imputes report availability.

For each patient let `A=(A_CT,A_MRI)` with absent modalities fixed to zero. Partition CT-only, MRI-only, and CT+MRI using the same examination state. For each stratum/modality, prespecify marginals in `{0,.25,.50,.75,1}`; convert to integer counts by frozen largest remainder. In the dual-modality stratum, overlap is unconstrained except by the marginals and its exact feasible range is reported. A patient assignment exposes or hides an entire retained unit.

Visibility changes information identically for comparator and full models:

- `Z` acquisition variables remain because an examination entry and acquisition-like start can exist without report access.
- If a unit is hidden, every report-derived `S` value for that unit is replaced by its locked surface-unavailable code in both `R` and `G`; every `X` value is replaced by semantic source-text-unavailable in `G`.
- `Gmask(A)` uses the same `Z,S(A)` as `G(A)` but masks `X` regardless of visibility. No model is refit.

For each access cell compute exact joint extrema over patient allocations, complete source states, and outcome completions for `Delta_nested(A)=T_G(A)-T_R(A)`, `Delta_mask(A)=T_G(A)-T_Gmask(A)`, and diagnostic `Delta_surface(A)=T_R(A)-T_B`. A certified mixed-integer program may use one four-state visibility variable per patient, precomputed model score for each visibility state, exact top-K membership with frozen ties, marginal-count constraints, and set-valued outcome variables. Require zero optimality gap. Timeout, infeasibility, state omission, or nonzero gap is inconclusive.

Mandatory endpoint identities are:

- all reports visible reproduces primary `R,G` bitwise;
- semantic-only masking with reports visible reproduces `Gmask` while preserving `S`;
- all reports hidden masks both `S` and `X`, and `G(A=0)=Gmask(A=0)` bitwise;
- for every `N<=12` fixture, exhaustive enumeration equals the optimizer bit-for-bit.

An access cell supports only hypothetical robustness if simultaneous lower bounds for both incremental contrasts exceed 5 across all its allocations/states. A positive surface contrast is reported but does not veto that conclusion. These are allocation bounds, not release probabilities, missing-at-random estimates, or evidence that any report was actually available.

## 10. Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

### Supportive

All source, lineage, annotation, event, temporal, bootstrap, permutation, and optimization gates pass; the confirmatory family is nonempty; and every simultaneous lower bound for both `Delta_nested` and `Delta_mask` is strictly greater than 5 in both years and every required state. This supports only robust retrospective incremental capture by adjudicated meaning in the eventual stored corpus.

Classify surface evidence separately:

- `surface-positive` if the simultaneous lower bound for `Delta_surface` exceeds 5;
- `surface-not-above-margin` if its simultaneous upper bound is at most 5;
- `surface-uncertain` otherwise.

A supportive semantic result remains supportive in all three classes. Surface-positive means that report form also carries ranking information and increases concern about workflow/site dependence; it is not proof of confounding and not a veto.

### Adverse

With valid computation, the universal content hypothesis is adversely resolved if any required coordinate has simultaneous upper bound at most 5 for either incremental contrast. An upper bound below zero establishes lower fixed-K documentary capture for the full model in that scope. If `Delta_surface` is positive while incremental content is adverse, the data support surface-associated ranking but not content added beyond that surface. They do not prove that semantic features are false or that biological MVI is absent.

### Inconclusive

Bounds crossing 5 without an adverse upper bound; discordant years; insufficient N/K or lower-envelope events; unresolved or incomplete adjudication; incompatible/unenumerated states; leakage; failed temporal quarantine; excessive bootstrap failures; permutation failure; optimizer uncertainty; or an unlinked conclusion is inconclusive. A gate failure is not converted to an adverse biological result. Sensitivity and access results cannot rescue an inconclusive/adverse primary test.

## 13. Clinical adjudication and claims requiring another study

Clinical experts must adjudicate the procedure/prior-treatment dictionary, stored report meanings, modality conflicts, and pathology wording. An automatic verifier can check that those signed artifacts were used as frozen, but cannot establish their clinical correctness.

Specimen-linked pathology with timestamps and sampling protocol is required to test biological M2. Raw images and an image-reference process are required for imaging-feature accuracy. Report author/final/release/version/view logs are required for actual pre-decision availability. A prospective silent-mode study with a real candidate stream, review capacity, actions, and monitored subgroup performance is required for operational validity and fairness. A controlled impact study with treatment, harms, recurrence, survival, resource use, and costs is required for clinical utility or benefit. External sites are required for transportability.

Accordingly, the strongest possible supportive conclusion is: “In this frozen retrospective HCC eventual-report corpus, adjudicated report meanings added more than the prespecified fixed-K documentary-M2 capture margin beyond the same acquisition/documentation surface, robust to declared source/outcome states.” Nothing causal or prospective follows automatically.
