## Interpretation limits and verifier contract

A supportive computation establishes only that the exact frozen AG1 list has higher absolute yield of participants with low observed one-snapshot UACR and a later inpatient N18 code. It does not establish true or persistent nonalbuminuric CKD, measured filtration, complete capture, specimen chronology, causal benefit from testing, mechanism, actionability, safety, workflow feasibility, cost-effectiveness, fairness, or transportability. Those require repeated clinically timed eGFR/UACR, measured GFR, outpatient/primary-care and kidney-replacement data, individual linkage censoring/emigration, medications and downstream actions, chart/nephrologist adjudication, costs/harms/equity review, external validation, and a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.
