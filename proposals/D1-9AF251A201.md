## Frozen finite ontology and panels

Before packet generation, independent governance freezes and hashes the ontology, packet schemas, synthetic examples, role qualifications, redaction rules, control transformations, missingness rules, workload ledger, margins, and output shell. No MIMIC case is used for training or ontology tuning. All reviewers are roster-disjoint from eligibility, R/K/S, H1/H2/H3, redaction, route, and packet roles; labels, quoted spans, missing-fact fields, response status, and review times are append-only. No consensus replaces individual labels.

Two actionability reviewers receive the reciprocal pre-D radiology chain, permitted admission/death/disposition fields, and independently target-redacted canonical DS context. They do not see route, atom, inherited labels, unredacted target realization, POE, post-D content, or outcomes. They choose exactly one: `C_PORTABLE_ACTIVE` (active target/action/window with no clinically material choice needed for a pending structured field), `C_CHOICE_ACTIVE` (active work but modality/window/priority/recipient or competing context requires clinician choice), `C_NO_ACTION` (explicitly inactive/completed/contraindicated/declined or incompatible goal), `C_UNDETERMINED`, or `C_PACKET_INVALID`. Every label cites spans and missing facts that could change it; none asserts guideline correctness.

Two separately staffed supersession reviewers see only the radiology recommendation and pre-D target-linked DS statements with limited local context and ordering. They enumerate linkage and status and assign `S_EXACT_SUPERSESSION`, `S_CONFLICT_OR_MODIFICATION`, `S_NONE`, or `S_UNKNOWN`. Exact supersession requires explicit target-linked replacement/completion/cancellation with no competing compatible interpretation. Omission, paraphrase, service code, author, order-like row, or intuition never establishes supersession.

Locked compatibility mapping yields `K_P` only when all compatible states are `C_PORTABLE_ACTIVE` and `S_NONE`; `K_R` when all remain active and at least one is `C_CHOICE_ACTIVE` or `S_CONFLICT_OR_MODIFICATION`; `K_N` when any includes `C_NO_ACTION` or `S_EXACT_SUPERSESSION`; and `K_U` for undetermined/invalid, active-versus-no-action conflict, or unresolved linkage/boundary. `K_N/K_U` dominate `K_P`; one such unit blocks `AUTO`; `K_P+K_R` gives `RECON`; non-singletons and unresolved admissions are `DEFER`. Clinical states never rewrite inherited routes.

## Measurement integrity, utilities, and shared error

The following are pre-label **measurement-integrity** gates, not clinical harm or utility thresholds: at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 non-sentinel challenge admissions per claimed route/atom when feasible; simultaneous lower separated-panel support agreement `>=0.90`; upper parent/holistic route discordance `<=0.05` for AUTO/TIMING and `<=0.10` for RECON/DEFER; upper route-invariant, nuisance, and route-swap actionability flips `<=0.02`; decisive-control violations `<=0.05`; packet invalidity/nonresponse/control infeasibility `<0.05` unless censused; atom ESS `>=50`, normalized maximum weight `<=0.10`, positive denominator, all inherited gates; and design-weighted physician-hours plus elapsed packet time within a separately frozen absolute workload ledger. If any packet/panel burden exceeds the ledger, the affected atom fails and the review cannot be selectively reduced.

These thresholds do not mean that 5% is safe or that `K_P` is beneficial. No point loss weights, net-benefit threshold, acceptable missed-opportunity rate, harm valuation, decision-curve preference, or stakeholder utility may be inferred from them. Utilities can be elicited only after measurement outputs, from an independently approved and timestamped stakeholder process, and can govern a new prospective protocol; without that process, results are integrity findings only and the mapper remains `DEFER`.

Agreement cannot identify shared wrongness. Primary inference uses sharp adverse-compatible finite-population bounds over all observed labels, nonresponse, packet defects, redaction/boundary uncertainty, and unit states, with inherited HT/Hájek estimators, exact inversion where applicable, at least 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates, and one simultaneous max-statistic 95% family. Report sensitivity curves for `kappa` including unrestricted `[0,1]`, but do not choose a nonzero `kappa_max` from agreement, panel count, kappa statistics, controls, or opinion. A nonzero bound may govern only if an independent, ascertainment-described external validation source, with frozen linkage, transport assumptions, and uncertainty, documents an upper bound before labels. If absent, set `kappa_max=1` for adverse analysis and force every prospective mapper to `DEFER`; no latent-class model or consensus narrows the primary bounds.

## Interpretation and evidence ceiling

Supportive requires inherited gates, at least one support-qualified atom, all integrity and workload gates, and an independent pre-label basis for the declared shared-error envelope. It supports only a prospective within-site silent measurement/workflow validation of exact observable signatures, with all others forced to DEFER. Adverse means an adequately precise supported atom fails an integrity/control gate or has adverse-compatible active-choice, no-action, supersession, or unresolved states beyond its gate; that atom is excluded despite agreement. Inconclusive includes no feasible atlas or <=900 workload, sparse events, low ESS, broad bounds, absent external error basis, nonresponse, packet/control infeasibility, broken seals, or unavailable departure evidence. Under unrestricted shared error the honest operational result is DEFER.

MIMIC lacks actual viewing/delivery/acknowledgement, finalization/signature, physical departure visibility, named responsibility, patient preference, outside plans, target-linked orders/referrals/scheduling, completed follow-up, harms, workload displacement, utility, or causal outcomes. A separately approved prospective actual-calendar silent bridge must capture those events and independently adjudicate actionability before exposure; a later governed comparative study is required for safety, utility, or benefit. No forward-calendar or external-site claim is permitted.
