# Fixed-capacity documentary-M2 HCC experiment: eventual report content with exact patient-level access bounds

## 1. Proposed child and remaining flaw repaired

This child synthesizes the two assigned valid parents:

- [prior hypothesis] supplies the identifiable primary estimand: eventual stored report content in a retrospective, future-defined source cohort, with report access treated as unobserved and bounded only by exact patient-level allocations.
- [prior hypothesis] supplies the clinically useful content-versus-documentation-surface negative control and the blinded dual-reader stored-text annotation requirement.

The remaining synthesis repair is to make these one experiment rather than two partially overlapping experiments. In particular:

1. all source, clock, examination-unit, text-interpretation, pathology-outcome, and hypothetical-allocation choices belong to one coupled admissible state; extrema may not combine favorable choices from different states;
2. the primary result is only the retrospective eventual-corpus result, not a report-access result;
3. the access analysis is a secondary exact partial-identification calculation over whole-patient allocations, not a fraction-indexed availability claim;
4. the surface model is fitted independently and is a negative control, but its failure or success cannot prove that semantic features are clinically true;
5. radiology readers adjudicate what the stored text says. They do not create an image gold standard, and no feature PPV, sensitivity, biological MVI, or imaging-truth claim is made;
6. joint inference is defined over all declared years, buffers, capacities, models, source states, outcome completions, and access-bound summaries. A favorable point estimate in one state is never sufficient.

This is a meaningful child of both assigned parents because it retains the exact report-allocation bounds from the first and the content-versus-surface falsification design from the second, while removing their incompatible primary interpretations.

## 2. Clinical question, evidence boundary, and advance

The consequential downstream decision is whether a scarce future multidisciplinary review capacity should be investigated as a possible way to prioritize documentary review of patients whose eventual resection-associated pathology record contains an M2-like finding. The proposed analysis is not a triage rule, referral rule, treatment rule, or service evaluation.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is only documentary: the HCC snapshot links patient/encounter rows, procedure rows with start/end fields, examination rows with acquisition-like start times and eventual narrative text, and same-encounter pathology narratives. It can therefore support a retrospective source cohort and a documentary endpoint. It does not show that an operation was completed, that a report was authored or released before a decision, that a clinician viewed the report, that pathology was from the resection specimen, or that the patient had biological MVI/M2.

The source also lacks report-authored/final/released/viewed/version timestamps, a report-access log, a referral roster, reviewer capacity or action, pathology time, specimen/slide/block/sampling identifiers, raw images, recurrence, survival, treatment, harm, cost, or benefit. The examination text is an eventual stored corpus. It is not evidence of contemporaneous availability.

A public systematic review read from its frozen Europe PMC XML source (Neves and Soares, Cancers 2026;18:2028; DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028; PMCID PMC13360217; acquired source [source checksum]) reports that 35/36 reviewed AI early-recurrence HCC studies were retrospective and 6/36 reported external validation, with inconsistent calibration and clinical-utility reporting. That motivates temporal locking and claim discipline, but it does not answer whether eventual CT/MRI report content adds value for this HCC source, nor does it validate this endpoint. The research-ambition README was inspected; the Cell demonstration's main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not treated as read evidence.

The falsifiable unresolved hypothesis is:

> In adults in a future-defined, source-documented first eligible HCC resection episode with same-encounter documentary HCC pathology and no recorded qualifying HCC treatment in the preceding year, does a locked model using eventual stored CT/MRI report content capture more encounter-documentary M2 cases in a fixed top-K list than both (a) its complete-content-masked version and (b) an independently fitted nonsemantic baseline, while a documentation-surface-only negative control does not exceed the same margin, across the declared temporal and source-uncertainty states?

The prespecified practical margin is 5 additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed ranking positions. It is a ranking-composition margin, not a treatment-effect, sensitivity, PPV, utility, or patient-benefit threshold.

The substantive advance is a source-identifiable test of whether interpretable stored report content contributes beyond nonsemantic acquisition information and beyond report form. A positive result would justify obtaining real report-version/access logs and designing a prospective silent-mode study. It would not establish that report content was available, actionable, biologically valid, safe, equitable, or beneficial.

## 3. One coupled admissible state

Let a complete state \\(theta\\) jointly contain:

- the selected patient and encounter links;
- the first eligible source-documented procedure episode;
- the admissible procedure start/end interval and calendar year;
- recorded prior-treatment status;
- the examination-unit partition, component membership, admissible starts, modality label, and text interpretation;
- the locked content-feature annotations;
- the same-encounter pathology composition and set-valued documentary outcome;
- the whole-unit patient-level report-allocation assignment for a secondary access analysis.

A state may select only source-supported alternatives. If a boundary is ambiguous, every admissible alternative remains in the state ledger. An extremum is taken over complete states, not by independently choosing the most favorable operation date, report date, modality, annotation, pathology label, and allocation.

A validated non-midnight timestamp is a point. A date-only or unvalidated midnight timestamp is the half-open interval [date, date+24 hours). Missing, competing, adjacent-day, and source-discordant rows remain alternatives. No procedure source is treated as proof of anesthesia, scheduling, cancellation, completion, or resection.

For d in {12, 24, 48, 72} hours, define c_d = t_op - d. This is an analytical cutoff only. It is not a clinical decision time, report-release time, referral time, or availability time. The 24-hour state is primary; the other offsets are locked sensitivities.

Development is 2015-2018, score/model locking and calibration are 2019, and 2020 and 2021 are separate locked tests. A patient's admissible state that reaches a later period is quarantined from every earlier fit. A patient ambiguous between test years remains one coupled set of alternatives, not two independent patients. 2022 onward is an ascertainment/drift audit only.

## 4. Population and endpoint

The population is intentionally conditional and selected:

1. age >=18 on the linked encounter;
2. the patient's earliest eligible procedure episode under the frozen hepatobiliary-surgery dictionary;
3. at least one same-encounter pathology row whose composed text explicitly supports HCC after pathology text adjudication; and
4. no recorded earlier HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in [c_d-365 days, c_d), searched patient-wide using dated procedure, medication, and non-drug-order fields.

This is recorded-treatment-naive, not biologically treatment-naive. Patients without the source-documented procedure/pathology frame, patients with indistinguishable planned or cancelled procedures, and patients outside the extract are not controls or outcome negatives. Untimed diagnosis rows can corroborate wording but cannot date treatment, define a negative treatment history, or enter predictors.

The endpoint is encounter-documentary M2. Compose every exact-deduplicated pathology row sharing the selected patient/encounter key from pathology fields 病理, 检查所见, and 检查诊断. Two blinded Chinese-reading pathologists independently apply a frozen context rubric; a third resolves disagreements. The rubric assigns:

- Y=1 only when an M2-like statement is textually anchored to microvascular invasion or HCC grade;
- Y=0 only when an anchored M0/M1 statement excludes M2 in the relevant context;
- Y={0,1} for missing grade, unanchored mention, boilerplate, conflict, uncertain attribution, or inadequate text.

Untimed pathology cannot be made postoperative by adjudication. Missing specimen, accession, sampling, or pathology time cannot be reconstructed. All unresolved values remain set-valued in every primary result. Report parser-only, pathologist-adjudicated, and all-row envelopes separately. A gate requiring at least 85% anchored adjudicated grade and at least 50 lower-envelope M2 events in each claimed test year is a feasibility/interpretation gate, not evidence of biological truth.

## 5. Examination unit, stored-text annotation, and models

Construct examination units before filtering as (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号), retaining every component row in raw order. A blank 检查号 is identity-unknown and cannot supply identity-safe semantic content. Conflicting modality labels, component starts more than 24 hours apart, or merged-identity evidence generate coupled unit alternatives with deterministic normalized (检查, calendar-date-of-start) partitions and raw backpointers.

Two blinded abdominal radiologists independently label each selected whole unit as CT, MRI, other, or unresolved, and independently annotate the following prespecified stored-text concepts:

maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites.

Each concept is present, absent, uncertain, or not-mentioned when the stored text supports that distinction; source-text-unavailable is retained when text is blank. A third radiologist resolves disagreements. Readers are blinded to pathology outcome, model score, year, and any claimed access result. This is dual-reader adjudication of the meaning of stored text. Agreement, adjudication completeness, unresolved proportions, and test-retest or inter-reader summaries may be reported. No PPV, sensitivity, specificity, imaging truth, biological feature truth, or clinical diagnosis claim is derived from this process. If a feature's text meaning cannot be determined, it remains unknown rather than being forced to absent.

For each patient retain at most the latest eligible whole CT and latest eligible whole MRI in [c_d-90 days, c_d), by maximum admissible component start, raw ordinal, then frozen patient hash. Concatenate all selected components' 检查所见 and 检查诊断 in raw order. The model receives a complete stored unit or an explicit source-text-unavailable mask, never a hand-selected phrase.

Define:

- B: independently fitted nonsemantic baseline with age, sex, CT/MRI/other indicators, eligible-unit count, acquisition-to-cutoff recency, component count, identity ambiguity, source-text-missing, and development-frequency 机器型号 category. It excludes report semantics, pathology, diagnosis, laboratory, clinical-document text, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff information.
- C(E): independently locked content model using B plus the adjudicated CT/MRI content flags. The two modality blocks and missingness codes are fixed before test results are viewed.
- C0: the same fitted C model with every content feature and its semantic missingness indication replaced by the training-defined source-text-unavailable code. C0 is not refit; this is a same-fit masking contrast.
- F: independently fitted B plus a prespecified documentation-surface block: whole-unit character count, field-presence indicators for 检查所见 and 检查诊断, digit/punctuation/whitespace/Chinese-character proportions, report component-row count, source/accession ambiguity, acquisition recency, and 机器型号 category. F contains no lexical tokens, clinical flags, pathology, diagnosis, or selected phrases. It is a negative control for documentation form, not a clinical model.

The surface variables are deliberately not interpreted as harmless. A nonzero F contrast means documentation form can rank differently; it does not show that semantics are unhelpful. The primary content hypothesis requires both interpretable-content contrasts to pass while the surface contrast does not.

Fit B, C, and F independently in 2015-2018 using patient-clustered worst-state elastic-net loss over a frozen penalty/mixing grid. Use 2019 only for frozen tuning, calibration, and score-locking. C0 inherits all C preprocessing and coefficients. No test outcome, test pathology text, test report annotation, test access allocation, or post-cutoff row may influence feature selection, tuning, K, or conclusion rules.

## 6. Fixed-capacity estimand

For each complete state theta, offset d, calendar year a, and q in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}, let N_a(theta,d) be the eligible index size before pathology grade, report semantics, or test outcomes. Freeze

N_ref = min over all admissible theta, d, and a in {2015,2016,2017,2018,2019} of N_a(theta,d)

and K_q=floor(q N_ref). The confirmatory capacity is q=0.10; q=0.05 and q=0.20 are locked sensitivity capacities. Require N_ref>=500, K_q>=50 for every claimed q, and at least K_q members in every claimed test state.

Rank each patient by locked score, then frozen patient hash, then raw episode backpointer. Exactly K_q patients are selected. For model M in {C(E), C0, B, F}, define

T_M(theta,d,a,q) = 100/K_q × sum of Y_i(theta) over the top-K_q patients.

This is documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed ranking positions in a future-defined source cohort. It is not prevalence, recall, triage yield, utilization, capacity achieved, opportunity-to-review, or clinical utility.

The primary contrasts are:

- D_C = T_C(E) - T_C0: same-fit incremental contribution of eventual stored content;
- A_C = T_C(E) - T_B: content versus an independently optimized nonsemantic baseline;
- A_F = T_F - T_B: documentation-surface negative-control contrast.

The confirmatory conjunction is D_C>5 and A_C>5 in 2020 and 2021, with simultaneous upper A_F<=5, under every required complete source/outcome state. All models select exactly K_q, so service utilization is identically one and must not be interpreted. An outcome-aware top-K oracle can be reported as a retrospective ceiling only.

## 7. Exact patient-level report-allocation bounds

The source contains no report-authored, finalized, released, viewed, versioned, or access-log field. Therefore no primary result is indexed by an observed availability fraction.

For a secondary stress analysis, define a whole-unit visibility mask for every patient: (A_CT,A_MRI) in {0,1}^2, with absent modalities fixed at zero. Partition patients into CT-only, MRI-only, and CT+MRI strata using the same examination-unit state. For each stratum and modality, choose a prespecified marginal fraction from {0, .25, .50, .75, 1.00}, convert it to an integer count by frozen largest remainder, and define the admissible allocation set as every patient-level assignment satisfying those counts. In the dual-modality stratum, CT and MRI overlap is unconstrained except by the two marginal counts; report the possible overlap range rather than assuming independence. Every allocation exposes or masks whole units, never phrases or rows.

For a fixed allocation, C's modality-specific feature block is visible only when its A flag is one; masked blocks receive the fixed source-text-unavailable code. The model coefficients remain the already locked coefficients. This is a hypothetical application of the locked score, not a fitted availability model.

For each access cell alpha, compute exact extrema of D_C, A_C, and A_F over the allocations, source states, and outcome completions jointly. A certified mixed-integer formulation may use one four-state access variable per patient, precomputed score for each allowed visibility state, exact top-K membership with the frozen tie key, modality-count constraints, and the set-valued outcome bounds. For N<=12 fixtures, exhaustive enumeration must agree bit-for-bit. Any nonzero optimality gap, timeout, infeasible state, or fixture disagreement makes that cell inconclusive.

The endpoints are mandatory checks:

- all modalities visible reproduces C(E) bitwise;
- all modalities masked reproduces C0 bitwise;
- intermediate cells report lower and upper bounds, never a point access estimate.

An access cell is robust for the content claim only if its simultaneous lower bounds for both D_C and A_C exceed 5 and its simultaneous upper bound for A_F is at most 5 across every required state. This is a hypothetical stored-content frontier. It is not a report-release probability, missing-at-random assumption, deployment feasibility result, or claim that any patient could have been reviewed.

## 8. Joint uncertainty, inference, and falsification

The uncertainty object is the single coupled state theta. Sampling uncertainty is added through a patient-cluster bootstrap, not substituted for state uncertainty. Use 2,000 prespecified patient-cluster bootstrap replicates. Each replicate resamples patients, rebuilds admissible procedure/examination/pathology states, refits B/C/F, repeats 2019 tuning and calibration, reconstructs fixed-K rankings, and recomputes source/outcome/access extrema. No bootstrap replicate may independently pair a procedure state from one alternative with a pathology or access state from another.

Construct one max-T simultaneous one-sided 97.5% family across the confirmatory and declared sensitivity coordinates: D_C, A_C, and A_F; 2020 and 2021; q=.05/.10/.20; d=12/24/48/72; the finite admissible source and outcome states; and each declared access cell. The result table must report, for every coordinate, the point estimate, simultaneous lower and upper bounds, K, N_ref, state/access identifiers, top-K outcome envelope, event count, gate vector, source hash, code hash, and raw-result locator. A universal conclusion is made only from the coordinatewise worst bound after this joint family is formed. If a family is computationally partitioned, the partition and multiplicity correction are frozen before results and the combined family is still reported.

For the semantic falsification test, perform 1,000 development-only permutations that move the entire adjudicated semantic block among patients within frozen strata of calendar year, modality pattern, unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and documentation-surface bins, while leaving the surface block and outcome rows in their original source positions. Refit C/C0 and recompute the complete locked pipeline. The permutation null must preserve surface structure and fixed-capacity bookkeeping while breaking the observed semantic-content/outcome alignment. A content contrast that survives only as a surface contrast is not supportive.

Required computation/interpretation gates are:

- source snapshot and catalog hashes match;
- every used header matches the declared schema, including UTF-8 BOM handling;
- exact patient/encounter/unit joins and retained raw ordinals/backpointers;
- one index row per patient and no duplicate patient contribution;
- complete coupled-state ledger and test-year quarantine;
- N_ref/K feasibility;
- no forbidden predictor lineage from pathology, diagnosis, labs, clinical documents, identifiers, year, or post-cutoff data;
- whole-unit construction and deterministic tie keys;
- independent B, C, and F fits, with C0 bitwise inherited from C;
- dual-reader modality/content annotation, third-reader disagreement resolution, and explicit unresolved-text accounting;
- pathology parser/adjudication completeness and set-valued unknown handling;
- max-T bootstrap/permutation reproducibility;
- exact access extrema and brute-force equality on all small fixtures;
- <=2.5% failed bootstrap replicates;
- no required content-pattern shift greater than 15 percentage points from the 2019 lock set.

Reader agreement, content adjudication completeness, and unresolved proportions are quality descriptors/gates for interpreting a stored-text representation. They are not feature accuracy against an unavailable image standard. No calibration threshold may rescue a failed ranking estimand; calibration is descriptive and cannot alter the fixed-K list.

Supportive result: all gates pass, the stated family is nonempty, every lower simultaneous bound for D_C and A_C is strictly greater than 5 across both years and every required q/d/source/outcome state, and every corresponding upper bound for A_F is at most 5. For any secondary access claim, the same rule must hold for every allocation in the declared cell or for the exact cell extrema. This supports only robust retrospective eventual-corpus ranking potential.

Adverse result: the computation is valid and a required universal contrast has simultaneous upper bound at most 5; an upper bound below zero supports lower documentary ranking potential for content in that scope. If A_F exceeds 5 while D_C or A_C does not, documentation form—not interpretable content—has the observed ranking signal in that scope. A valid adverse result does not show that biological M2 is absent.

Inconclusive result: bounds cross 5; required state families are incompatible; K or event feasibility fails; annotation, pathology, temporal, join, permutation, bootstrap, or optimizer gates fail; a claimed cell has no certified solution; or a conclusion cannot be linked to an output row. State or gate failure is not a biological negative.

## 10. What computation can and cannot establish

Automatic verification can establish source hashes and headers; exact joins and filters; raw-row/backpointer retention; patient uniqueness; interval/state coupling; examination-unit construction; model lineage; training/tuning/test separation; fixed K and ties; C(E)/C0 bitwise identity; independent baseline/surface fits; bootstrap/permutation arithmetic; certified access extrema; and conclusion-to-output linkage.

Verifier fixtures must include:

- K=100 with 40 versus 27 documentary events gives 13 per 100;
- identical top-K sets give zero, and swapping model order negates a contrast;
- fewer than K or unresolved outcomes are never padded or silently coded negative;
- complete masking makes C0 identical to the C pipeline with source-text-unavailable semantics;
- later source rows cannot change an earlier locked result;
- incompatible operation, examination, pathology, or access alternatives cannot be mixed;
- all-visible and all-masked access endpoints reproduce C(E) and C0 bitwise;
- a small N<=12 access instance agrees exactly between brute force and the optimizer;
- tie scores follow the frozen patient hash and raw backpointer;
- a surface-only feature cannot enter C, and a semantic feature cannot enter F;
- correctly computed adverse, supportive, and inconclusive outputs receive their respective labels;
- numerically correct results labeled as referral performance, real-time report availability, preoperative utility, treatment effect, biological M2, recurrence benefit, workflow efficiency, fairness, or patient benefit are rejected.

A verifier cannot establish that the operation was completed, that the report was available or viewed before a decision, that documentary M2 is biological MVI/M2, that a clinician would act, that capacity is acceptable, or that the ranking improves treatment, recurrence, survival, safety, cost, equity, or transportability. Those claims require expert clinical review, timestamped/versioned operational data, specimen-linked pathology or image review, and a prospective silent-mode or impact study. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility and interpretation discipline, not clinical truth.
