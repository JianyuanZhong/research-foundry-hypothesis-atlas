# Proposal: a correctly identified, capture-calibrated reassessment boundary after TACE

## Episode, parent, and scientific deliverable

This episode-10 successor evolves assessed valid parent [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the HCC island, the first-index and day-45 landmark, paired AFP/hepatic trajectory, competing recorded-care outcomes, observation/censoring audit, and evidence limits. It repairs a material decision-boundary error: if alert burden is to be minimized subject to a sensitivity requirement, the tuning threshold must be the largest predicted-risk threshold that still attains the required sensitivity. The parent’s stated “smallest threshold” would produce the broadest alert set and is inconsistent with its own clinical-boundary estimand.

The actual deliverable is newly constructed cohort and split manifests, newly fitted predictions, and a locked-test comparison of:

1. B0: regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazard models without the early post-index trajectory, but with pre-index clinical history and day-45 observation opportunity;
2. B1: B0 plus the frozen early AFP/hepatic trajectory, without post-day-45 information;
3. B2: B1 plus day-45-known observation-process features; and
4. M3: a substantive irregular-time latent trajectory/observation model on the same population, outcomes, censoring and splits.

For each model, the solver must estimate a 320-day cumulative risk for first combined recorded liver-directed action and separate risks for repeat-TACE-like and alternate liver-directed actions. It must select 80%, 90% and 95% sensitivity operating boundaries on the 2023 tuning period using the corrected largest-threshold rule, then evaluate alert fraction, sensitivity, PPV, false-alert fraction, alerts per recorded liver-directed event, calibration, Brier/log score and patient-bootstrap uncertainty on the locked 2024–2025 test period. No locked-test outcome may select a threshold.

The primary new scientific output is not a generic score: it is whether adding the trajectory yields a lower-alert, action-specific reassessment boundary at a fixed sensitivity, and whether that boundary survives capture controls and temporal sensitivity. The 90% boundary is a research operating point, not a clinical treatment cutoff.

## Unresolved clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

After TACE, a clinician may need to decide whether routine surveillance is adequate or whether a patient should be reassessed for another liver-directed intervention. An early AFP/hepatic trajectory might contain information about residual disease or recovery, but a recorded repeat procedure is also shaped by testing, access, local practice, eligibility and return to this institution. The unresolved question is therefore whether the trajectory supports a specific, reproducible recorded-care boundary beyond baseline severity and care capture.

The strongest claim supported by the inspected local evidence is narrower: the snapshot contains linkable encounter, diagnosis, procedure, laboratory and examination records with within-person time fields. A measured source audit found 105,044 encounter rows, 338,040 procedure rows, 28,159,928 laboratory rows, 1,810,646 diagnosis rows, 190,022 AFP rows, and 43,815 encounter-linked patients. It also found 344,383 diagnosis rows containing the literal 肝细胞癌 and 206,401 containing 肝硬化. These counts establish feasibility and ascertainment constraints only. They do not establish radiologic response, viable tumor, recurrence, treatment failure, benefit, survival or a useful trigger.

Primary hypothesis H1:

> Among adults with a structured HCC diagnosis and a first recorded TACE-like procedure who have no liver-directed procedure after index +24 hours through day 45, the B2 model with the prespecified early AFP/hepatic trajectory will yield a more specific recorded-care reassessment boundary than B0 without that trajectory: at the corrected largest threshold attaining at least 90% IPCW sensitivity for first combined recorded liver-directed action during days 46–365, B2 will reduce the locked-test alert fraction by at least 10 absolute percentage points versus B0, with a 95% patient-bootstrap interval excluding zero, while no comparable trajectory-specific reduction appears for the non-liver capture control or pre-index placebo trajectory.

This is a prognostic hypothesis about recorded local care. It is not a hypothesis that the trajectory measures viable tumor, that a recorded action means progression or treatment failure, or that a future alert or treatment improves patient outcomes.

Secondary model-information hypothesis:

> M3 will reduce the corrected 90%-sensitivity alert fraction by at least 5 absolute percentage points versus B2, with calibrated locked-test predictions, stable convergence and no loss of non-liver/placebo specificity.

A smaller M3 difference is an exploratory representation result, not a clinically meaningful boundary advance.

## Population, index, landmark and temporal boundaries

Use one earliest eligible index per patient. A TACE-like procedure is a procedures row whose 手术, case-insensitively, matches TACE, contains 动脉化疗栓塞, or contains both 肝动脉 and 栓塞. Collapse qualifying rows within 24 hours and anchor the earliest 开始时间. Require age >=18 and nonmissing 性别 in a joined encounter. Require a diagnosis name containing the literal 肝细胞癌 in a joined encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after the index; diagnosis time is inherited only from the joined encounter because diagnoses has no time field. Use indices through 2025-01-01 only when a complete 365-day horizon is possible.

The day-45 landmark risk set excludes any repeat-TACE-like or alternate liver-directed procedure after index +24 hours through day 45. These early actions are reported as an early pathway and are not coded as non-events. Alternate liver-directed procedure names use a frozen audit of liver-specific resection/ablation terms containing 切除, 消融, 射频 or 微波, with a liver/hepatic context requirement; ambiguous generic names are reported separately and excluded from the strict primary outcome rather than silently treated as liver-directed.

From day 46 through day 365, define the first recorded competing action:

- repeat TACE-like procedure;
- strict alternate liver-directed procedure;
- first non-liver procedure as a capture-control outcome.

Report combined liver-directed cumulative incidence and separate cause-specific/cumulative-incidence outputs. Non-liver procedure is a capture control, not a biological competing event. No recorded action is not called successful treatment, disease-free status or absence of progression.

If liver-directed and non-liver procedures share the same first timestamp, label a same-time tie and exclude it from the primary ordered contrast; report sensitivity assignments to each cause. Preserve raw procedure names, strict/broad vocabulary counts and exclusions in the audit artifact.

Define local observation time as the latest dated joined encounter among 就诊时间, 入院时间 and 出院时间. Censor at the first day after last observed local contact or day 365, whichever comes first, unless an outcome occurs first. Fit inverse-probability-of-censoring models on fit data only, report overlap/truncation/calibration, and repeat in a stable-ascertainment subset with an encounter on or after day 365. No death or outside-care action is imputed. The full-index analysis reports trajectory availability/missingness and observation opportunity; it does not impute an unobserved biological response or call an availability association a transported response effect.

## Frozen trajectory and available variables

For AFP, use the last parseable numeric value in days -30 through -1 and first numeric value in days 7 through 45. Let z=log1p(AFP); define AFP improvement as z_post <= 0.5*z_pre. For albumin, total bilirubin, 凝血酶原时间比值 and platelets, use last pre-index and first early-post numeric values. Non-deterioration means early >= pre for albumin/platelets and early <= pre for bilirubin/coagulation-ratio proxy. The local assay is not called INR; no ALBI/MELD or cross-assay pooling is permitted because labs has no separate unit column.

The primary phenotype is AFP improvement plus non-deterioration in at least two hepatic proxies. Retain continuous changes, assay/qualitative flags, missingness, and a complete paired-trajectory indicator. Day-45-known capture features include distinct encounter days, lab row/day counts, assay-specific availability and first-observation timing, examination count/timing and family flags, and optionally dated order counts from 开立时间, 开始时间 and 结束时间. An order is not completion. Examination narrative is excluded from the primary model; any lexical sensitivity is unvalidated and cannot be called radiologic adjudication.

B0 uses age, sex, encounter department, pre-index diagnosis/procedure-family counts, prior liver-directed actions, pre-index assay summaries/missingness and day-45 observation opportunity. B1 adds the frozen trajectory. B2 adds only day-45-known capture features. All models use the same risk set, action definitions, censoring, split and locked-test cases.

M3 is an irregular-time low-rank state-space model with AFP-informed activity and hepatic-function latent states, patient random intercepts/slopes, assay-specific informative-observation submodels and competing action hazards. It estimates day-45 state/slope using only measurements available by day 45 and returns posterior uncertainty. Its scientific purpose is to test whether asynchronous shape, discordance and measurement intensity explain action-specific risk beyond the hand-defined phenotype; it is not included merely to chase a small predictive gain.

## Splits, corrected boundary rule, uncertainty and falsification

Primary index-date split: 2010–2022 fit, 2023 tuning, 2024–2025-01-01 locked test, with no patient crossing splits. If dates fail, use deterministic participant-hash buckets and explicitly label them internal, not external validation. Freeze parsing, vocabulary, imputation, model settings, censoring models and feature construction before locked-test evaluation.

For each model and target sensitivity s in {0.80,0.90,0.95}, evaluate candidate thresholds on the tuning set. Choose the largest threshold t for which training-only-IPCW sensitivity for combined liver-directed action is at least s; if no candidate reaches s, record boundary failure rather than lowering the rule silently. Ties at the same predicted risk use one threshold. The largest-threshold rule minimizes the flagged fraction among thresholds meeting the sensitivity constraint. Threshold uncertainty is assessed by patient bootstrap with threshold re-selection inside each tuning resample, plus a fixed-threshold sensitivity analysis; no test outcomes select t.

On locked test report cause-specific and cumulative-incidence-sensitive performance, alert fraction, PPV, false-alert fraction, alerts per liver-directed event, calibration-in-the-large, calibration slope, Brier score, mean log score, and patient-bootstrap intervals. Report B2-B0 and M3-B2 absolute alert-fraction contrasts at the same boundary target, plus event support and weight diagnostics.

Support for H1 requires: (a) B2 reaches the 90% target within the declared uncertainty convention; (b) B2 lowers alert fraction versus B0 by >=10 percentage points with a 95% interval excluding zero; (c) the result is directionally stable in complete-follow-up, strict-vocabulary and 80/95% sensitivity analyses; (d) trajectory-specific gain is not comparable for the non-liver control or pre-index placebo; and (e) no material calibration failure or weight-positivity failure. Calibration/log-score improvement alone is not H1 support.

Support for M3 requires at least 5 percentage points lower alert fraction than B2 at the same 90% sensitivity, calibrated locked-test risk, stable convergence and no worsening of specificity controls. Adverse evidence includes null/reversed alert-burden gain, attenuation after B2 capture adjustment, comparable control/placebo gain, unstable thresholds, failed overlap/weights, complete-follow-up reversal, strict-vocabulary reversal, M3 nonconvergence or calibration failure. If availability predicts action but the paired trajectory does not, the interpretation is measurement/care capture, not disease-specific response. Inconclusive evidence includes too few eligible paired patients/events, high unknown follow-up, sparse/incomparable assays, high tie prevalence, unresolved vocabulary ambiguity, unstable bootstrap intervals or failed latent convergence.

## Interpretation boundary and required evidence

A supportive result establishes only that a local EHR-derived rule can stratify later recorded liver-directed action with a quantified alert burden in this source and time split. It could motivate a prospective silent reassessment study and expert adjudication. It does not establish recurrence, radiologic response, viable tumor, treatment failure, survival, treatment benefit, clinical utility, cost-effectiveness, or a recommendation to repeat TACE.

The unavailable or inadequate evidence includes image files, validated radiology-response labels, explicit treatment intent, reliable tumor burden/number/size, mortality linkage for this analysis, outside-care capture, access/eligibility reasons, validated laboratory units, an external institution and prospective impact outcomes. Clinical documents/pathology are not time-aligned for this prediction, and examination narrative is not a validated imaging ontology. Stronger conclusions require time-aligned expert imaging/pathology review, treatment-intent and eligibility data, mortality/outside-care linkage, external validation, and prospective impact evaluation with expert-specified harms and benefits. The verifier can check data binding, computation, leakage controls, uncertainty, operating-rule implementation and whether conclusions follow the outputs; it cannot establish clinical truth.
