## Clinical opening, supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

At postoperative hour 48 after a first recorded HCC liver resection, clinicians must decide whether the early course warrants intensified respiratory/fluid review and imaging. Intervention-requiring pleural or abdominal collections are consequential after hepatectomy: Wang et al. included thoracentesis and abdominal drainage in a clinician-adjudicated 30-day complication composite [K1], Sakamoto et al. defined clinically relevant post-hepatectomy pleural effusion by thoracentesis or thoracic drainage [K2], and Clavien–Dindo grading treats invasive intervention as grade III [K3]. These works do not validate this extract's codes as unplanned complications and do not establish that laboratory ordering predicts intervention.

Strongest claim already supported: the inherited full-census audit found 300 of 326 candidate procedures with independent postoperative examination, procedure-specific order, or treatment-course context, after excluding seven unrelated/ambiguous transhepatic-biliary records, three pre-existing-fluid-only records, and sixteen procedure-only unresolved records. This supports a reproducible phenotype of context-supported recorded postoperative invasive pleural/abdominal fluid intervention. It does not establish unplanned intent, causation by hepatectomy, chronicity, or Clavien–Dindo status.

New feasibility evidence: the outcome-blind diagnostic reconstructed 8,883 eligible 2018–2022 landmark encounters, scanned 28,159,928 lab rows, and found 122,445 selected numeric 0–48-hour rows. Pooled, 3,075/8,883 (34.6%) had at least two broad domains represented in at least two 12-hour bins, but only 217/8,883 (2.44%) had all three domains so represented and only 4/8,883 (0.045%) had all three in at least three bins. The assignment's 29.3% figure is the exact 2020 stratum (508/1,734); 2022 was 30.4%, rather than 29.3% being a pooled estimate. Renal/fluid assays were especially non-longitudinal: creatinine and urea each had two-bin coverage in 203/8,883 (2.29%), and sodium in 193/8,883 (2.17%). Thus three-domain coupling and a dynamic-factor primary are not identifiable in these data.

Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis: among adults still hospitalized and endpoint-free at 48 hours after their first recorded HCC liver resection, the signed 0–48-hour change direction of four adequately repeated exact assays—国际标准化比值 (INR), 纤维蛋白原 (fibrinogen), 丙氨酸氨基转移酶 (ALT), and 降钙素原 (procalcitonin)—improves prediction of the first context-supported recorded invasive pleural/abdominal fluid intervention in (48,336] hours beyond (i) an identical latest-state/observation/treatment baseline and (ii) an unordered rival given the same values, masks, time span, and absolute change magnitude.

This is a prognostic temporal-information hypothesis. It does not claim that any assay causes fluid accumulation, that a coefficient identifies physiology, or that treatment based on the model improves outcomes. A supportive result would justify external/prospective confirmation of a compact POD2 ordered-history phenotype. An adverse result would favor latest state and abandon directional history for this endpoint. An inconclusive result would identify sparse event-linked repetition, drift, observation practice, calibration, or endpoint interpretation as the bottleneck.

## Frozen preprocessing and leakage controls

Only records timestamped in [T0,T0+48h] form predictors. Parse numeric values only from 定量结果. Median-collapse duplicate same-label/same-time values after logging duplicate counts. Do not convert units or pool labels. Winsorize each label at development-era 1st/99th percentiles and standardize by development-era median/IQR; zero-IQR channels are dropped with a logged reason. Reuse development transforms in all later eras.

For each exact label, define latest as the last value in [0,48h], with latest hour and hours-to-landmark. Missing standardized values become zero only after adding a missing indicator; missing times become zero only after their indicator. This latest definition is identical in B0, U1, and T1.

For each of the four ordered assays, bin at [0,12), [12,24), [24,36), [36,48], using the median if multiple values occur in a bin. Let first and last be the earliest and latest evaluable bin values. Set A=1 only when at least two distinct bins span at least 12 hours. If A=1, define elapsed hours h between bin midpoints, absolute rate M=|last−first|/h, and signed rate D=(last−first)/h. Otherwise set h, M, and D to zero. Standardize h, M, and D using development-era parameters among available records. This is a deliberately minimal first-to-last history test; it does not estimate nonlinear recovery.

Common static inputs: centered age, sex, log1p valid operation duration plus missing flag, centered operation year, laparoscopic lexical flag, and coarse major-resection flag (hemihepatectomy, extended/major, or at least three named segments).

Common observation inputs: for every frozen label, latest missingness and hours-to-landmark; for the four ordered labels, numeric row count, distinct-bin count, and A; total unique 0–48-hour lab timestamps. Common treatment inputs: presence and first-start hour for albumin (白蛋白), loop diuretic (呋塞米|托拉塞米|布美他尼), vasopressor (去甲肾上腺素|肾上腺素|多巴胺|多巴酚丁胺|间羟胺|血管加压素), antibiotic (头孢|哌拉西林|美罗培南|亚胺培南|万古霉素|利奈唑胺|左氧氟沙星|莫西沙星|替加环素|甲硝唑|阿米卡星), and hemostatic (氨甲环酸|酚磺乙胺|蛇毒血凝酶|维生素K|凝血酶|卡络磺钠). Names, routes, starts/stops, and counts are descriptive context; dose effects and treatment effects are forbidden interpretations.

## Exact same-split fits

### B0: latest-state baseline

Fit unweighted ridge logistic regression with unpenalized intercept to static, observation, treatment, and latest standardized values for all fourteen exact labels. No interaction or feature selection is allowed. Select λ from {0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100} by leave-one-calendar-year-out folds in 2018–2021, minimizing mean validation Brier score and choosing the largest λ within one standard error of the minimum. Refit frozen B0 on 2018–2022.

### U1: same-information unordered rival

U1 nests B0 and adds, for each ordered assay, elapsed span h and absolute rate M. Availability A is already in the common block. U1 receives the same raw first/last values, masks, bin counts, timestamps, and latest state as T1, but M is invariant to exchanging first and last. It tests whether any gain comes from having an additional historical value, time span, or change magnitude rather than direction. Select a trajectory-block λ from the same grid using the same development folds while holding the B0-block penalty at its selected value; refit on 2018–2022.

### T1: smallest identifiable ordered-history model

T1 nests U1 and adds exactly four signed rates D, one for each ordered assay. Hold the B0 and U1 penalties fixed and select the four-feature signed-block λ from the same grid by the same development-fold Brier rule. Refit on 2018–2022. Coefficient signs are learned; no adverse orientation is imposed, particularly for fibrinogen. T1 can show prognostic directional information discarded by U1. It cannot identify a physiologic pathway.

### G0-latest: learned nonlinear latest-state control

Fit histogram gradient boosting on B0 features only, with maximum leaves {3,7}, minimum leaf {50,100}, learning rate 0.05, and 200 iterations, selected by development leave-one-year-out Brier. This asks whether nonlinear current severity explains an apparent T1 gain. It does not test history.

### Deferred alternatives

The former three-domain coupled summaries and masked dynamic-factor model are prespecified deferred, not conditional primary fits. The observed 2.44% all-domain two-bin coverage, 0.045% all-domain three-bin coverage, and approximately 2.2–2.3% renal repetition make cross-domain state dynamics structurally underobserved. A dynamic model could reveal latent cross-assay coordination only in data with denser aligned trajectories; here it would mainly learn masks and common ordering practice. Revisit only with a new outcome-blind diagnostic showing at least 40% of patients and 50 development events with all three domains in at least three bins spanning 24 hours, plus stable masked reconstruction across years/seeds. A supervised GRU/transformer is likewise deferred because 106 events through tuning do not justify learning a temporal representation and it would not solve absent renal trajectories.

## Falsification, metrics, and uncertainty

Primary metrics are T1−B0 and T1−U1 paired Brier differences. Also report log loss, AUROC, AUPRC, calibration intercept/slope with plots, and decision-curve net benefit at 1%, 2%, 3%, and 5%.

Use 2,000 paired patient bootstraps stratified by locked-test calendar year for frozen-model 95% intervals. Use 200 end-to-end patient bootstrap refits, resampling within calendar year separately in development, tuning, calibration, and test. Report event/effective sample sizes, convergence failures, and full distributions.

Required falsifications:

- Direction ablation: in each of 100 fixed-seed replicates, independently multiply each available signed-rate feature by +1/−1 within test year and assay, leaving latest values, absolute rates, elapsed times, masks, counts, medications, and all other inputs fixed; apply frozen T1 without refitting. This isolates direction from magnitude/observation information.
- Timing-only control: static variables, masks, hours-to-landmark, row/bin counts, total timestamps, medication indicators/times, and h, but no lab values, M, or D.
- Values-only model: latest values, M, and D without observation/treatment features; and a subset analysis among patients with at least one A=1 channel. Gain confined to masks/timing cannot be called laboratory recovery.
- Treatment-indication control: refit B0/U1/T1 without medications. Medication coefficients are never treatment effects.
- Latest-state nonlinear control: G0-latest must not match a claimed ordered gain.
- Endpoint sensitivities: inherited S1 all 326 candidates; S2 primary plus 16 procedure-only unresolved; pleural and abdominal components; examination-free support; order-free support; union versus cross-source corroborated events. None replaces the primary.
- Era/source controls: annual risk/event/coverage and procedure-source tables; 2020–2023 development/calibration sensitivity evaluated in 2024–2025. Cross-source corroboration is not an independent cohort.
- Negative controls: outcome permutation within year × first-event procedure source and +28-day within-encounter timestamp shift where possible. Above-chance negative-control performance triggers leakage investigation and an inconclusive conclusion.

Supportive requires all of:

1. T1−B0 Brier ≤−0.0010 with paired 95% CI below zero;
2. absolute AUPRC gain over B0 ≥0.02 with paired 95% CI above zero;
3. locked-test T1 calibration slope 0.8–1.2 and intercept −0.20–0.20 after frozen 2023 mapping;
4. T1−U1 Brier CI below zero, establishing direction beyond identical latest state, elapsed time, and absolute change magnitude;
5. direction ablation removes at least 50% of the T1-versus-B0 Brier gain, and timing-only/G0-latest do not match T1;
6. direction agrees for S1/S2 and examination-free/order-free definitions without catastrophic annual/source reversal.

This supports prognostic value of directional assay history for a recorded context-supported intervention—not mechanism, unplanned complication, treatment utility, or transportability.

Adverse requires adequate G1 event-linked support and stable endpoint/source coverage, plus either a T1−B0 interval whose lower limit is greater than −0.0010 (excluding meaningful benefit), or no T1 advantage over U1 with preservation of at least 75% of apparent gain after direction ablation. Prefer B0 and abandon the ordered-direction claim for this endpoint. A nonlinear latest-state win also argues against temporal ordering, not for a mechanism.

Inconclusive includes inadequate G1 event-linked support; intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit; failed calibration; strong era/source reversal; gain confined to timing/missingness; endpoint-definition reversal; excessive bootstrap failures; or positive controls. An imprecise null does not refute directional history. Obtain denser repeated data, endpoint review, or external confirmation rather than reintroducing three-domain coupling.

Labels are unplanned post-hepatectomy complication treatment; planned/prophylactic drain care; chronic pre-existing fluid management; duplicate-only record; unrelated procedure; or insufficient evidence. Reviewers remain blinded to 0–48-hour predictors and model scores.

Only if strict-positive observed PPV is at least 0.80, Wilson lower bound at least 0.70, binary-label kappa at least 0.70, and no excluded stratum has at least 0.50 unplanned-complication yield may results additionally be described as applying to a phenotype predominantly adjudicated as unplanned complication treatment in the reviewed sample. Otherwise retain only the recorded-intervention claim. Enriched negatives cannot estimate population sensitivity. Passing does not establish causation, grade for every event, prospective utility, or transportability.
