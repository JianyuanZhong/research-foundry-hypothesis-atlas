# Fixed-lock, first-patient multicenter validation of EHR vasopressor restart against intent-qualified pump rescue

## Clinical decision, evidence boundary, and unresolved hypothesis

The prerequisite decision is whether a locked binary EHR reconstruction of “restart of the first-stopped vasopressor within six hours” is accurate, complete, arm-symmetric, and transportable enough to serve as an endpoint component in a later discontinuation-order trial. This experiment does **not** compare discontinuation strategies.

Every inherited MIMIC result is frozen. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, assigned actions were rare: 211 norepinephrine-first (NE-first; 3.49%) and 167 vasopressin-first (VP-first; 2.76%), both below the 5% gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort has 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate composite failures. The arms are neither randomized nor exchangeable and are too small for a causal comparison. Of 73 failures among 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvement (about 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, below the 0.01 gate) and adverse later transport; no score or subgroup advances.

The complete transaction audit used all 137 inherited episodes and all rows remaining after declared stay, item, and time filters. It recovered every boundary and all 59 raw restarts. Five symmetric reconstruction rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries. These are findings of EHR-label instability, not pump truth. Status, chain, dose, caregiver, `storetime`, open-bag/transfer fields, and intermittent MAP cannot establish delivered drug, genuine cessation, rescue intent, false-positive rate, or MCS absence.

The unresolved prospective hypothesis is:

> In first qualifying patients in both observed first-stop strata, the locked episode-level EHR restart has PPV and sensitivity greater than 0.80 for intent-qualified pump-native rescue, and each NE-first minus VP-first metric difference is strictly within ±0.10, under full consecutive verification, joint worst-case indeterminate bounds, a fixed common confirmatory lock, and configuration-level transport gates.

A supportive result validates only this restart component in enumerated pump–EHR configurations. It cannot show that rescue causes harm, stopping order causes rescue, either order is preferable, the endpoint is clinically important, or a trial would be safe or beneficial.

## Population and estimand hierarchy

### Consecutive eligibility

Recruit at least six adult ICUs with synchronized smart-pump, monitor, and EHR exports. Each configuration completes a fresh 50-episode technical run-in after definitions are locked. At least 95% must have unique patient–pump–channel–drug linkage, measured clock maps, and complete pump export from two hours before index through the observable six-hour window; at least 90% must have contemporaneous intent evidence. Infrastructure may be repaired, but thresholds, windows, labels, estimands, margins, and adjudication categories may not change. A failed configuration repeats with 50 new episodes; a second failure makes it feasibility-only. Run-in episodes are excluded.

After run-in, capture and fully verify **every consecutive qualifying cessation**, regardless of subsequent EHR or reference status. Qualification uses only information available at cessation:

1. age at least 18 years, alive in an adult ICU;
2. linkable pump-delivered concurrent NE and VP for at least six continuous hours immediately before cessation;
3. at the start of the final 30 pre-index minutes, NE >0 and ≤0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 and ≤0.04 U/min, with neither increased during that interval;
4. no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours;
5. at least three valid MAP observations spanning at least three of four prior 30-minute bins, including the final bin, with median MAP ≥65 and minimum MAP ≥55 mmHg; and
6. a non-simultaneous first cessation of one study drug while the comparator continues pump-native delivery.

Both drugs crossing below threshold within five minutes is a simultaneous stop and is excluded and counted. A procedure/transport pause known by `t0` is excluded and counted. Actual MCS type, active/standby state, and support setting are captured; absence is never inferred from silence.

### Primary first-patient estimand

A person contributes only their first qualifying cessation across all participating ICUs during the confirmatory phase. This patient-level unit matches a possible future patient-randomized endpoint and removes within-person recurrence from ordinary primary score inference. No later event, outcome, capture state, or arm is used to select the first episode.

For arm `a`, with `E` the locked EHR label and `R` the intent-qualified reference:

* `PPV_a = P(R=1 | E=1, arm=a)`;
* `Sensitivity_a = P(E=1 | R=1, arm=a)`;
* `Delta_PPV = PPV_NE − PPV_VP`;
* `Delta_Sens = Sensitivity_NE − Sensitivity_VP`.

The global confirmatory claim is the conjunction of all four arm-specific accuracy claims and both arm-symmetry claims.

### Fully verified recurrent-episode estimand

Later qualifying episodes are not discarded from data collection. Every later episode receives the same pump capture, EHR test, reference adjudication, and disposition. A recurrent episode requires both drugs again to satisfy the full six-hour baseline and `t0` at least 24 hours after the prior `t0`; candidate transitions within 24 hours or without a new baseline remain transitions within the prior episode and are counted, not re-indexed.

The recurrent-episode target is a separate secondary marginal estimand among all qualifying episodes. Report episodes per patient, episode sequence, interepisode time, arm switching, first-versus-recurrent prevalence and accuracy, and effective patient-cluster count. Use patient-cluster bootstrap stratified by arm and configuration (at least 10,000 resamples) and marginal GEE with patient clusters and configuration fixed effects as sensitivity analyses. Recurrent results cannot rescue a failed first-patient claim. A clinically material first-versus-recurrent difference (>0.10 for PPV or sensitivity) makes extension of the endpoint to repeated episodes adverse even if the first-patient claim passes.

## Locked clocks, index test, and reference

### Pump-native index and observable window

`t0` is the first pump timestamp at which the first-stopped drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below for at least five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above its threshold. Drug-library identity, concentration, patient, channel, weight, and delivered-rate stream must be linkable; a programmed rate alone is insufficient.

The target window is `(t0, min[t0+6 hours, death, ICU exit]]`. Death or ICU exit is a determinate closure only when pump and EHR capture are complete through closure. A technical/export/linkage gap before closure makes `R` indeterminate, never negative. Eligibility does not depend on future capture, rescue, EHR restart, physiology, death, or exit.

Before validation, each source clock has an escrowed affine mapping to pump time, including offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving handling, fitting rows, residuals, and immutable map version. The configuration-specific boundary band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds. A required wider band fails qualification.

### Frozen EHR test

The EHR algorithm is escrowed before reference adjudication and uses local fields mapped to the MIMIC-compatible specification:

* NE item 221906: retain mcg/kg/min; convert mcg/min using contemporaneous valid weight 30–300 kg; reject normalized rate >5 mcg/kg/min.
* VP item 222315: divide units/hour by 60; retain units/min; reject normalized rate >0.2 U/min.

`E=1` when a new positive stopped-drug EHR interval has `endtime > starttime` and deterministically mapped `starttime` in `(t0,t0+6 hours]`, before death/exit if earlier. A row already spanning `t0` is not a new interval; pump rescue concealed inside it remains `E=0` and can be a false negative. Chain, status, `storetime`, caregiver, open-bag, transfer, and order fields cannot repair the primary label.

An otherwise qualifying start is `E=?` when its prespecified mapped 95% timestamp uncertainty set crosses `t0`, six hours, or an earlier death/exit boundary. Deterministic mapped-time classification and exclusion of boundary cases are secondary sensitivities; analysts never move an event after seeing `R`.

### Intent-qualified pump-native rescue

`R=1` requires both:

1. after the qualifying off interval, linked delivered pump rate for the stopped drug again reaches its index threshold continuously for at least five minutes within the observable target window; and
2. two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators conclude from timestamped contemporaneous evidence that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion after cessation.

Bag/syringe replacement, pump/channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, technical/procedural pause, mistaken-stop correction, and uninterrupted delivery segmented only in documentation are reference-negative for intent-qualified rescue. The pump algorithm runs before adjudication. Adjudicators see pump/monitor traces, workflow events, cointerventions, and contemporaneous intent evidence, but not `E`, transaction-chain/status diagnostics, aggregate accuracy, or each other's label. They assign rescue-positive, pump resumption without rescue intent, technical/workflow continuation, no resumption, clock/export/linkage failure, or intent-indeterminate. A third blinded adjudicator resolves disagreement.

After the first 100 adjudicated calibration episodes, raw agreement <85% or both Gwet AC1 and kappa <0.70 suspends accrual. The manual may be clarified without viewing `E×R` accuracy, then reliability restarts on 100 new calibration patients; prior patients remain feasibility data. A second failure makes the study operationally inconclusive. Report all agreement statistics. A secondary pump-only reference removes intent and measures interface fidelity, but cannot replace the clinical target.

## Complete dispositions, deterministic event matching, and indeterminate bounds

Every screened cessation receives one disposition: eligible first patient, eligible recurrent episode, simultaneous stop, known procedure/transport pause, other ineligibility reason, capture/linkage failure, or duplicate transition within an episode. Every eligible episode has `E∈{0,1,?}` and `R∈{0,1,?}`; there is no complete-case deletion. Report the full 3×3 table by arm, phase, site, configuration, interface family, person, and first/recurrent status.

Episode labels are primary. Secondary event analysis enumerates all pump resumptions after qualifying five-minute off spells and all new positive EHR intervals. Map all event times with the escrowed map, then perform one-to-one maximum-cardinality, minimum-total-absolute-lag bipartite matching within patient, episode, drug, and window under a locked ±10-minute caliper. Ties resolve by earlier pump time, earlier mapped EHR time, then immutable source-row ID. Matches cannot cross `t0`, death/exit, six hours, person, episode, or drug. Report unmatched pump/EHR events, fragments, duplicates, signed lag, and median/95th-percentile absolute lag. Matching cannot alter `E` or `R`.

For confirmation, enumerate every joint binary completion of every `?` label subject only to determinate data. For each completion compute arm PPV, sensitivity, and both arm differences. The conservative arm-specific lower confidence bound is the minimum lower bound over completions. Each difference uses the union envelope of its score intervals over **joint** completions: minimum lower endpoint to maximum upper endpoint. Thus incompatible metric-specific imputations cannot manufacture accuracy or equivalence. The compiler may replace literal person-level enumeration with an exact integer/corner optimization over the 3×3 cells, but must unit-test equality against exhaustive enumeration for every small table with 0–8 indeterminate patients. Determinate-only estimates and identification intervals are secondary.

Indeterminacy >5% overall, >7.5% in any contributing arm-by-configuration cell with at least 20 first patients, or an arm difference >5 percentage points is adverse for operations and prevents support even if worst-case statistical gates happen to pass.

## Independent blinded calibration and fixed confirmatory lock

A fixed lock is necessary because stopping when `E`-positive or adjudicated `R`-positive denominators reach 220 is outcome-dependent. The prior repeated internal-pilot rule is therefore replaced by two temporally separate phases.

### Calibration phase

After run-in, fully verify 1,200 consecutive **first qualifying patients** across all passing configurations. These patients remain in all feasibility, pump-only, recurrence, and transport descriptions but are excluded from confirmatory accuracy estimates. This separation is deliberate: it permits ordinary fixed-sample confirmatory intervals without relying on an unproven blinded sample-size-re-estimation argument.

A firewall statistician may see only arm, `E` denominator membership, `R` denominator membership, indeterminate flags, configuration, first/recurrent flag, and calendar accrual. The statistician may not see any `E×R` cross-tab, true-positive/false-positive/false-negative count, accuracy estimate, metric difference, or adjudication narrative. For each of the four total-population denominator probabilities—NE/E-positive, VP/E-positive, NE/R-positive, VP/R-positive—calculate the Jeffreys Beta(0.5,0.5) posterior 1.25th percentile from calibration counts. For each lower prevalence, find the smallest confirmatory `N` giving at least 0.975 binomial probability of 220 denominator observations. Lock `Nconfirm` to the maximum of the four values. This Bonferroni construction gives a planning lower bound of 0.90 for attaining all four targets; it is an operating target, not an inferential alpha claim.

Before the lock, protocol-identical sites/configurations may be added only after their run-in, to improve accrual and configuration representation. If any lower prevalence implies `Nconfirm >6,000`, fewer than six qualified sites/two interface families are available, or observed accrual cannot complete that `Nconfirm` within 48 months after confirmation begins, declare confirmation infeasible. Do not change thresholds, enrich an arm, subsample negatives, substitute pump positivity for intent positivity, or relax margins. Calibration may be repeated only after a documented configuration expansion, using 1,200 entirely new first patients; the old calibration remains feasibility data. At most one repeat is allowed.

### Confirmatory phase

Escrow the participating configurations, `Nconfirm`, common start date, and common lock before the first confirmatory patient. Enroll every consecutive qualifying first patient and fully verify all eligible recurrent episodes. Lock after exactly `Nconfirm` first patients or at 48 months, whichever comes first. The hard cap is 6,000 confirmatory first patients (7,200 validation first patients including calibration, plus fully verified recurrent episodes). All sites continue to the common lock; there is no arm-specific closeout, denominator stopping, early success, accuracy futility, unblinded look, alpha spending, or post-lock extension. Mathematical impossibility, infrastructure failure, or adjudication failure may halt operations but yields an inconclusive result. If any metric denominator is <220 at lock, confirmation is inconclusive and attained precision is reported.

The new bounded stress test uses the frozen MIMIC arm shares (84/137 NE, 53/137 VP) and E-positive fractions (46/84 NE, 13/53 VP), while varying unknown arm-specific reference prevalence. It is planning evidence only. At a common 15% reference prevalence, the oracle fixed confirmatory requirement is 4,293 patients; after a separate 1,200-patient conservative calibration, only 69.2% of 3,000 simulations declared a ≤6,000 lock feasible and the median feasible lock was 5,424. At 20%, 99.7% were feasible and the median lock was 4,101. At 10%, the VP reference denominator has only 0.800 probability of reaching 220 by 6,000, and the calibration almost always rejects feasibility. At 5%, the cap is impossible. These are not prevalence estimates.

The identity `P(E=1|arm)×PPV = P(R=1|arm)×Sensitivity` is also enforced in interpretation. If frozen E positivity transported, both metrics >0.80 would require conditional reference prevalence of 0.438–0.685 in NE-first and 0.196–0.307 in VP-first. Hence very low prospective reference prevalence combined with similar E positivity would be adverse evidence about joint PPV/sensitivity, not merely a reason to accrue longer.

At denominator 220, 186 correct classifications are required for a one-sided 95% Wilson lower bound strictly above 0.80. In inherited calculations, component power is 0.619 at truth 0.85, 0.949 at 0.88, and 0.996 at 0.90; exact score-equivalence power at 220 per arm is 0.916 for 0.90 versus 0.90, 0.141 for 0.90 versus 0.82, and 0.011 for 0.92 versus 0.80. Under equal 0.90 accuracy and 50% metric prevalence, simulated six-gate success was 0.868. These properties prevent interpreting 220 as guaranteed global power.

## Confirmatory inference

For each arm-specific PPV and sensitivity, use a one-sided 95% Wilson lower bound and require it to be **strictly greater than 0.80**. For each NE-minus-VP difference, use the 90% Miettinen–Newcombe score interval with continuity correction and require the full joint-completion envelope to be **strictly inside (-0.10,0.10)**. Equality at any boundary fails that gate.

The six-component conclusion is an intersection-union test: all four accuracy and both symmetry gates must pass. No multiplicity adjustment is needed for the global conjunctive claim, because support requires rejection of every component null; this does not provide simultaneous 95% coverage for individual components. If the global claim fails, individual components are descriptive and cannot be called confirmatorily validated.

The first-patient fixed-lock estimate is primary. A configuration-stratified patient bootstrap (at least 10,000 samples) is a dependence/mixture sensitivity; reversal of any pass makes the claim inconclusive. Report specificity, NPV, prevalence, index positivity, paired E-minus-R prevalence discrepancy, McNemar discordance, pump-only performance, timing, and documentation strata secondarily. Prespecified clock-band and off-duration sensitivities cannot redefine the test.

## Configuration-level multisite transport gate

A configuration is fixed by site, ICU, pump vendor/model and software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, and clock-map method/version. Any change creates a new configuration requiring a fresh run-in; pre-change and post-change data are never silently pooled.

A transportable claim requires at confirmatory lock:

* at least six sites and two pump–EHR vendor/configuration families;
* both arms represented at at least four sites and two families;
* no site, configuration, or family contributes >30% of any of the four metric denominators;
* for every arm-metric, denominator ≥40 in at least three configurations and ≥20 in at least four configurations.

Report configuration and family estimates, leave-one-site/configuration/family-out analyses, hierarchical binomial estimates, heterogeneity variance, and 95% prediction intervals. For every supported configuration (metric denominator ≥40), each conservative arm-metric lower bound must exceed 0.70. A prediction interval extending below 0.70, adjusted configuration range >0.15, or Holm-significant interaction accompanied by absolute difference >0.10 is adverse for a transportable endpoint. Leave-one-out analyses must retain all global gates. Sparse support is inconclusive, not evidence of homogeneity. Support can apply only to explicitly enumerated locked configurations.

Stop a configuration for linkage, clock, or pump-export failure in >5% of its latest 50 episodes and require a new run-in. A configuration repaired after confirmation starts cannot re-enter the confirmatory mixture; its later data are feasibility-only. Global operations are inconclusive after two reliability failures or inability to maintain four active validation sites.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** all four conservative arm-specific Wilson lower bounds exceed 0.80; both joint-completion 90% difference envelopes are strictly within ±0.10; all fixed-lock denominator, full-verification, indeterminate, recurrence, clock, adjudication, and configuration transport gates pass; and no prespecified robustness check reverses a decision. This supports only the restart component for the listed configurations and first-patient population.

**Adverse:** a completed arm-specific accuracy gate fails; either symmetry envelope is not equivalent; low reference prevalence is incoherent with observed E positivity and claimed joint accuracy; indeterminacy is excessive/differential; recurrent performance is materially worse; paired errors or event matching show systematic missing/spurious events; or adequately supported configuration heterogeneity violates transport gates. High PPV cannot compensate for low sensitivity, and pooled accuracy cannot conceal an arm/configuration failure.

**Inconclusive:** calibration projects >6,000 confirmatory first patients or >48 months; a denominator is below 220 at the fixed lock; an interval touches/crosses a threshold; joint indeterminate bounds prevent classification; capture, linkage, clocks, adjudication, site concentration, or configuration support fail; or operations stop. Inconclusive does not support accuracy, equivalence, homogeneity, treatment benefit, or extending accrual after labels are known.
