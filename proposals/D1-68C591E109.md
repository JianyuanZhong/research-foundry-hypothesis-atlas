# Decision-time-bounded M2 triage: separate tests of deployable incremental value and semantic information beyond workflow

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and prespecified claims

The historical decision is narrow: 24 hours before a first eligible liver resection, could a frozen, interpretable strategy flag an adult in this institution's retrospectively pathology-confirmed HCC resection frame for multidisciplinary review because the resection specimen may show pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)? The flag is a weighted classification output. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

**Strongest claim currently supported.** Direct source and catalog inspection establishes that the HCC snapshot contains operation start times, CT/MRI acquisition times and eventual report text, assay-specific laboratory values with one laboratory time, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. Inherited full scans counted 338,040 procedure rows and 46,395 pathology rows. A broad first-resection audit found 75 preliminary anchored M2 labels in 2020 and 24 in 2021 before final exclusions and adjudication, and showed that an anywhere-in-document M2 search is invalid because a selected M0 may be followed by boilerplate defining M2. These are feasibility and construct-validity findings, not final prevalence, performance, report availability, or utility.

Relevant public full text was inspected. The Chinese MVI-TTG definition classifies M2 as more than five proximal foci within 1 cm and/or any focus beyond 1 cm; severe/M2 MVI is associated with poorer prognosis, while inadequate non-tumoral sampling can undergrade MVI (Li et al., DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746, frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, source [source checksum]; Chen et al., DOI 10.3350/cmh.2025.0167, frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, source [source checksum]). A 2026 retrospective multicenter study used irregular CT margin and AFP to predict **binary MVI**, reporting AUC 0.740 in 487 training patients and 0.781 in 256 external-validation patients and only exploratory decision-curve utility (Huang et al., DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034, inspected frozen full HTML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). That complete-case image study does not validate M2, routine report semantics, decision-time availability, this dataset, or treatment benefit.

**Unresolved clinical-value claim (C).** In the locked 2020–2021 whole target frame, does the deployable semantic strategy `S` add at least 0.01 net-benefit units over the structured baseline `B` at threshold 0.30, despite worst-case patient-wise uncertainty in report availability and pathology grade? The materiality margin 0.01 means one net true-positive equivalent per 100 target-frame patients at the threshold's false-positive weight; it is not a patient-benefit claim. Support also requires positive incremental value across 0.25–0.35 and superiority to flag-all and flag-none.

**Unresolved attribution claim (A).** Does the same frozen semantic block add positive net benefit after measured workflow/provenance variables are already present, comparing the nested model `A=W+semantics` with `W`, under the same uncertainty? This can support "incremental information beyond measured workflow features"; it cannot prove that semantics caused the gain or that all workflow confounding was measured.

These are independent prespecified claims. Requiring simultaneous support for non-nested `S-B` and `S-W` is logically unsound: `W` is a deliberately non-deployable negative control, `S-W` changes semantic and workflow blocks in opposite directions, and a workflow model may be highly predictive without negating deployable `S-B` value. Accordingly, `S-B` is the clinical-value estimand, `A-W` is the nested attribution estimand, and `S-W` is descriptive only. Failure of attribution never retroactively falsifies a supported clinical-classification contrast, and attribution without `S-B` support is not clinical value.

The substantive advance over both parents is therefore a two-axis answer: whether an implementable semantic strategy improves weighted historical M2 classification over routine structured data, and separately whether the semantic block contributes beyond measured documentation process. Both use exact shared states for unknown report visibility and unverified pathology and preserve every target-frame patient.

## Frozen population, index, and clocks

The unit is one patient (`Patient Master Index`) and one first eligible index resection.

1. In `procedures`, select the earliest valid `Start Time` whose normalized exact `Surgery` is in a frozen, clinically reviewed dictionary of partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resections. Exclude transplant, biopsy, puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and explicit repeat/recurrence operations. Break exact-time ties by `Visit Number` and then normalized `Surgery`. Before outcome access, emit every included/excluded raw operation value and frequency, invalid times, ties, and the one-index-per-patient assertion.
2. Require age at least 18 and same-encounter pathology text explicitly identifying HCC in the index surgical encounter. This defines a retrospectively resected, pathology-confirmed institutional frame; it does not prove preoperative HCC certainty, resectability, or curative intent. Do not require an MVI grade or secure specimen link. Missing grade, positive-ungraded MVI, conflict, or uncertain specimen linkage becomes `V=0`, never an exclusion. Patients without any same-encounter HCC-identifying pathology are outside the observable frame; outcome bounds cannot repair that selection.
3. Define `t_dec = procedures.Start Time - 24 hours`. The 24-hour buffer is inherited and fixed, not outcome-selected. Records at or after `t_dec` cannot predict. Report labeled 12-, 48-, and 72-hour-buffer sensitivities, none replacing the primary.
4. A liver CT/MRI row is acquisition-eligible only when `examinations.Start Time` is valid and in `[t_dec-90 days,t_dec)`. This is acquisition time, not authored/finalized/released/viewed time. Require at least one acquisition-eligible CT/MRI. After exact-row deduplication, bundle by `(Patient Master Index,Visit Number,Examination Number,Start Time)`; a missing `Examination Number` creates a row-level bundle. Freeze CT/MRI dictionaries from exact `Examination`. Select at most the latest CT and latest MRI bundle by `Start Time`, then `Examination Number` and normalized `Examination` for ties. Emit multiplicity and discarded bundles.
5. A lab row is timestamp-eligible only when `labs.Test Time` is valid and in `[t_dec-30 days,t_dec)`. Select the last exact `(Test,Specimen Type)` row, using source order only for exact-time ties. Because no unit or separate collection/result/finalization field exists, never combine assay names by apparent meaning or apply external cutoffs. Call values timestamp-eligible, not proven visible. A required sensitivity masks every lab in `[t_dec-48 hours,t_dec)`.
6. Primary no-recorded-prior-treatment cohort: exclude recorded HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during `[t_dec-365 days,t_dec)`, using frozen reviewed dictionaries and valid source times. Report exclusions by source and invalid time. This does not establish biological treatment-naivety. Previously treated patients form a separately labeled sensitivity cohort.
7. Development fitting is 2015–2018; penalty selection and logistic recalibration use 2019 only; 2020–2021 is untouched test. Assign by index operation date, keep all records from a patient in one period, and exclude 2013–2014. Frozen pipelines may score 2022 through snapshot end 2025-11-11 only for predictor/template drift and endpoint identifiability. No contemporary performance claim is allowed.
8. Freeze dictionaries, bundle/tie rules, text masks, semantic rules, pathology rules, state construction, preprocessing, imputation, winsorization, scaling, model penalties, calibration, thresholds, bootstrap family, permutation strata, and gates before test outcomes are read.

Harbor must record actual counts for every filter, invalid/missing clocks, exact-row duplicates, join multiplicity, dictionary values, exclusions, selected bundles, availability states, and outcome states. Sources remain read-only; row-level derivatives remain private workspace files.

## Pathology-only endpoint and verification

`Y=1` only for pathology-defined M2; `Y=0` for M0 or M1. `V=1` means a trustworthy index-specimen M0/M1/M2 assignment under the frozen position-aware parser and adjudication; otherwise `V=0`.

Assign grade only from the immediate selected value after an anchored field such as `MVI risk grade[:：]`, stopping before definitions, explanatory bullets, historical clauses, or a new specimen section, or from a frozen synonymous selected-value construction accepted in blinded development review. Handle negation, `not seen`, multiple specimens, and discordance. Unanchored M2, binary `microvascular invasion: present/absent`, positive-ungraded MVI, blank vessel-number/distance fields, absent MVI language, definitions, and conflicts cannot assign M0/M1/M2. Pathology never predicts.

Two independently implemented parsers must agree on locked test records. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 sampled by year/template, and every test `V=0`; a third resolves disagreement. Require class-specific PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95, Cohen kappa at least 0.90, and parser disagreement no more than 5%. Reviewers may derive grade only from explicit number/distance language under the frozen definition. Inadequate text, ambiguous specimen linkage, or inadequate sampling remains `V=0`.

Raw slides, specimen identifiers, block counts, vessel-distance measurements, and the sampling protocol are unavailable. Text adjudication cannot establish biological M2 missed by tissue sampling.

## Shared decision-availability states

For patient `i`, let `q_i` be the number (0, 1, or 2) of selected CT/MRI bundles with nonempty eventual report text and let `Ω_i={0,1}^{q_i}`. A 1 means the report is assumed finalized and visible at `t_dec`; 0 means unavailable. Empty stored text has state 0 only. No probability, independence, or monotonicity assumption is imposed.

At state 0, mask every report-body-derived item from that bundle: size, lesion count, macrovascular thrombus, report length/sections/template, semantic findings, and unrestricted text. Acquisition facts—valid acquisition time, modality from `Examination`, bundle count, distinct-`Examination Number` count without the identifier, and a development-frequency machine-availability category—remain available. At state 1, stored report variables are exposed. The same `r∈Ω_i` is used for every compared model for that patient; choosing one state for S and another for B, W, or A is forbidden.

To avoid unsupported extrapolation at unavailable states, development and 2019 recalibration use state augmentation: each patient contributes every feasible state with total weight one, divided equally across states. Cross-validation, recalibration, bootstrapping, and permutation are patient-grouped. Augmentation is a robustness construction, not evidence that any state occurred.

If an independent source-system audit later recovers reliable finalization/release times, states may be narrowed only in a new scientific child. Manual belief about likely availability cannot narrow `Ω_i` in this experiment.

## Models and baselines

All four principal models are elastic-net logistic regressions fitted on the same `V=1` 2015–2018 patients and augmented states. Fit a frozen alpha/penalty grid in 2015–2018; select the simplest setting within one bootstrap standard error of the minimum patient-weighted 2019 log loss, then estimate one weighted logistic recalibration intercept and slope for that selected model in 2019 without feature refitting. Development-only exact-assay medians, winsorization limits, and scaling apply. Every model scores every test patient under every state.

**B — deployable structured baseline**

- age and sex;
- timestamp-eligible albumin, total bilirubin, INR, and platelet values, exact-assay/specimen indicators, and missing indicators;
- modality/acquisition provenance;
- only in report-available states, frozen extractions for maximum lesion diameter, lesion count (1, 2, at least 3), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown indicators.

**S — deployable semantic strategy**

Exactly B plus the semantic block C:

- only in report-available states, frozen present/absent/unknown extractions from `Examination findings` and `Examination diagnosis` for complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular or non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity;
- timestamp-eligible exact-assay numeric AFP, ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, and creatinine, with exact-assay/specimen and missing indicators.

S contains no report/examination code, raw or categorized machine identifier beyond B's shared acquisition indicator, report length, formatting/template family, accession number, year, procedure/diagnosis/pathology term, unrestricted n-gram, or post-cutoff content.

**W — workflow/provenance negative control**

Exactly B plus acquisition count and time since last acquisition; reviewed non-semantic protocol category from exact `examination`; machine availability and development-frequency category (never raw identifier); extension-assay presence/count/time-since-last; and, in report-available states only, character/line/section counts and outcome-blind formatting family. Construct template family in 2015–2018 after masking clinical words, all numbers, dates, identifiers, and values, using punctuation, line breaks, section order, and repeated boilerplate. Unseen formats map to "unseen." W receives no added semantic finding and no extension-assay numeric value. W is not proposed for clinical deployment.

To make ordering behavior comparable, every extension-assay missing/presence indicator used by S is also in W. The semantic difference is the numeric result, not whether clinicians ordered the assay.

**A — process-adjusted semantic model**

Exactly W plus the identical semantic block C used in S. Thus A versus W is nested and isolates the incremental modeled information in C conditional on measured workflow/provenance. A is an attribution instrument, not the deployable candidate, because it includes W's process variables.

**U — diagnostic unrestricted-text ceiling**

A plus report-available development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after removing identifiers, dates, accession strings, pathology/MVI terms, and postoperative text. Audit the 100 largest absolute coefficients before unblinding. U is secondary and cannot rescue either failed claim.

Two blinded radiologists review at least 200 reports for B's size/count/thrombus extraction and, separately in each test year and CT/MRI provenance, at least 150 reports enriched for each C feature and parser-negative examples. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in at least 90%, count-category agreement at least 90% with kappa at least 0.80, and per-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity at least 0.85 for every C binary feature. This validates stored-text interpretation, not report availability.

## Exact joint partial-identification estimands

For threshold `p`, set `w=p/(1-p)` and `A_Mi(r,p)=1[score_Mi(r)≥p]`. For contrast `k=(M_1,M_0)`, define the paired patient contribution

`d_i^k(y,r,p) = [y-w(1-y)] [A_M1i(r,p)-A_M0i(r,p)]`.

For `V_i=1`,
`L_k(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i min_{r∈Ω_i} d_i^k(Y_i,r,p)` and
`U_k(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i max_{r∈Ω_i} d_i^k(Y_i,r,p)`.

For `V_i=0`, the minimum and maximum additionally range over `y∈{0,1}`. The denominator `N` is the entire 2020–2021 target frame. With at most four report states and two endpoint states per patient, brute-force enumeration is exact. No patient is dropped, no `V=0` is imputed, and acquisition never proves report visibility.

Compute this joint report-availability/pathology-grade bound for `S-B` (claim C), `A-W` (claim A), descriptive `S-W`, `S-U`, each model versus flag-none, and each model versus flag-all. For a single model's net benefit, use flag-none as the comparator; flag-all has fixed action 1 and its uncertain outcome is enumerated in the same state.

These intervals are coordinatewise sharp because patient states and missing outcomes are otherwise unrestricted. Endpoints for different contrasts can arise from different latent assignments and must not be presented as jointly attained. Requiring the lower endpoint of each primary contrast to pass is a conservative all-state guarantee. Sampling simultaneity below does not turn coordinatewise endpoints into one latent world.

The primary threshold is 0.30. Evaluate `p=0.20,0.21,…,0.40`; 0.25–0.35 is the prespecified robustness plateau, not an elicited clinical preference. Report flag counts and incremental flags for every model/state so net benefit is interpretable as workload-weighted classification.

At 0.30 decompose interval width into pathology ambiguity with all reports available, report-availability ambiguity among `V=1`, and the full joint interval. Report the fraction whose S, B, W, or A action changes across `Ω_i`. Secondary scenarios set all nonempty reports to available or all reports to unavailable. Neither identifies the actual state.

## Simultaneous inference, falsification, and secondary analyses

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps, resampling patients within each calendar period and carrying all of each sampled patient's rows and states. Refit the full development/tuning/recalibration pipeline in each replicate. The inferential family contains both `L` and `U` endpoints for `S-B`, `A-W`, `S-W`, and S versus both default strategies over all 21 thresholds. Use the 95th percentile of the bootstrap maximum absolute centered deviation on the net-benefit scale to form one two-sided 95% simultaneous band; also report pointwise percentile intervals. Zero-variance coordinates remain exact and are not divided by zero. Report convergence and failed-replicate counts; more than 2.5% failed replicates is an inference-gate failure.

Run 1,000 process-matched semantic-null repetitions for claim A. Within development/tuning period, permute the complete C block between patients within frozen year, CT/MRI acquisition provenance, report-state cardinality, formatting-family availability, and extension-assay presence pattern, using a prespecified coarsening hierarchy when a cell has fewer than 20 patients. Keep B/W variables, Y/V, patient grouping, and each patient's state masks fixed; refit and recalibrate A. The observed `L_{A-W}(0.30)` must exceed the 97.5th percentile. A full patient semantic shuffle and a label permutation must yield intervals compatible with zero. A post-cutoff feature model is a leakage demonstration only.

Required sensitivities are: mask labs in the final 48 hours; replace extension-lab numeric values with exact-assay development medians while retaining ordering indicators; within-exact-assay/year ranks instead of numeric scaling; 12/48/72-hour decision buffers; and previously treated patients separately. They cannot replace the primary.

At 0.30 report bounds by test year, sex, age below/at least 65, CT-only/MRI-only/dual acquisition provenance, AFP availability, and the four largest available test template families (others pooled). Suppress inferential claims with fewer than 100 patients or 20 `V=1` M2 events. Among `V=1`, report paired AUPRC, AUROC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV under all-report and no-report scenarios, explicitly labeled selection-sensitive. Model V using only pre-`t_dec` B, year, and template availability and report standardized differences; do not claim inverse weighting identifies missing grades or report states.

## Feasibility, validity, and reliability gates

Global computation and endpoint interpretation require:

- test N at least 1,000, adjudicated `V=1` M2 at least 75, `V=1` at least 85%, and review of every test `V=0`;
- both pathology parsers and pathology/radiology reliability thresholds above;
- identical patients and shared states across models; exact masking of every report-derived variable at state 0; no forbidden feature or post-cutoff value;
- all-report text nonempty in at least 80% of the frame, B size/count jointly extractable in at least 70%, and each B laboratory available in at least 70%;
- no required variable's missingness shifts by more than 15 percentage points from 2019 to either 2020 or 2021; unseen template prevalence and machine-category drift reported;
- in both all-report and no-report V=1 diagnostics, S recalibration slope 0.80–1.20, intercept -0.10–0.10, sensitivity at least 0.70, and PPV at least 0.50 at 0.30;
- at least 100 patients and 20 V=1 M2 in each estimable CT-only and MRI-only analysis. Failure limits modality-specific generality but does not erase an otherwise valid pooled estimate; a demonstrably harmful stratum forbids a claim of cross-modality robustness;
- complete bootstrap output and brute-force bound unit tests.

Claim C uses the global, endpoint, extraction, calibration, and bootstrap gates. Claim A additionally requires valid workflow masks, at least 20 patients in each terminal permutation block after prespecified coarsening, and estimable matched-null repetitions. Failure of an attribution-only gate makes claim A inconclusive, not claim C.

## Prespecified result zones

**Clinical-value support (C supported):** C gates pass; the simultaneous lower confidence limit for the sharp lower bound `L_{S-B}(0.30)` is **greater than 0.01**; the simultaneous lower limit for `L_{S-B}(p)` is greater than zero at every p from 0.25 through 0.35; S's lower-bound net benefit simultaneously exceeds flag-all and flag-none throughout that plateau; the final-48-hour lab mask leaves the 0.30 lower limit above zero; and neither test year's point lower bound is negative. Requiring the confidence limit—not merely the point estimate—to exceed 0.01 repairs the parent's mismatch between a materiality hypothesis and an interval that only excluded zero. Strongest conclusion: S has material, threshold-robust historical weighted M2 classification increment over B in this 2020–2021 institutional frame under the stated all-state uncertainty.

**Clinical-value adverse:** with C gates passed, the simultaneous upper confidence limit for `U_{S-B}(0.30)` is below 0.01, ruling out the prespecified material increment. An upper limit below zero indicates classification harm. S failing to beat a default strategy is adverse at that threshold. Isolated AUPRC improvement or all-reports-available success cannot rescue an adverse decision result.

**Clinical-value inconclusive:** C gates fail, or the simultaneous band spans the 0.01 materiality margin. A positive point estimate with bounds spanning no material benefit is inconclusive, not supportive; imprecision is not evidence of no association.

**Semantic-attribution support (A supported):** A gates pass; the simultaneous lower confidence limit for `L_{A-W}(p)` is greater than zero at every p from 0.25 through 0.35; the observed 0.30 statistic exceeds the matched-null 97.5th percentile; and removing extension-assay ordering indicators from both A and W while median-neutralizing unavailable numeric results does not reverse the 0.30 point lower bound. Strongest conclusion: the frozen semantic block contributes weighted M2 classification information beyond the measured workflow/provenance block. This is not causal attribution and does not establish absence of unmeasured workflow confounding.

**Semantic-attribution adverse:** with A gates passed, an interval's simultaneous upper limit is at or below zero at any required plateau threshold, or the observed statistic does not exceed the matched null. That falsifies the prespecified robust attribution claim even if S-B is positive.

**Semantic-attribution inconclusive:** A-specific reliability, overlap, permutation, or inference gates fail, or simultaneous bands span zero. This does not alter claim C's independently determined status.

The joint interpretation is explicit:

- C and A supported: deployable historical increment is supported, and the same semantic block adds information beyond measured workflow.
- C supported; A adverse or inconclusive: deployable historical increment is supported, but it must not be described as semantic rather than workflow-related.
- A supported; C adverse or inconclusive: semantics add information conditional on workflow, but no clinically material deployable increment is established.
- Both adverse: the corresponding prespecified hypotheses are falsified. Either inconclusive: identify whether report-state, pathology, extraction, event, overlap, or sampling uncertainty caused the width.

The descriptive non-nested `S-W` result is reported in every case but never gates C or A. Better U performance is diagnostic evidence of omitted text information, not success.
