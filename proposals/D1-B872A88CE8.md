# Can preoperative HCC report semantics add deployable M2-triage value beyond routine structure—and beyond workflow artifacts?

## Clinical decision, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The historical decision is whether, 24 hours before a patient's first liver resection, routinely available information could flag a patient for multidisciplinary review because the resection specimen may show pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2). The flag is a risk-triage output. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy, transplantation, surveillance intensity, or any other treatment.

**Strongest claim already supported by evidence.** The inspected HCC catalog and direct CSV headers establish that the snapshot contains patient-linked operation times, CT/MRI acquisition times and eventual report text, laboratory times and values, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. They do not establish when an imaging report was finalized or visible, whether `Test time` precedes result availability, whether pathology belongs unambiguously to the index specimen, or whether tissue sampling was adequate. Published parent audits report that anchored M0/M1/M2 fields occur through 2021 and that naive anywhere-in-document M2 searches are contaminated by explanatory boilerplate; those are feasibility findings, not validated prevalence or model performance.

The inspected literature supports clinical relevance without resolving the proposed question. Yao et al. define M2 by the number and distance of invaded microvessels and report worse recurrence and survival with increasing MVI grade in 227 patients with solitary resected HCC (DOI `10.21037/hbsn-22-411`, inspected full HTML source `[source checksum]`). Chen et al. describe the Chinese seven-point sampling protocol and warn that inadequate non-tumoral parenchyma can underestimate grade (DOI `10.3350/cmh.2025.0167`, inspected full-text XML `[source checksum]`). Huang et al. externally validate irregular margin plus AFP for **binary MVI**, not M2, and call decision-curve utility exploratory (DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`, inspected full-text XML `[source checksum]`). None establishes M2 triage value in this dataset, report availability at the decision, semantic attribution, the clinical acceptability of a 0.30 threshold, or patient benefit.

**Unresolved question.** Does a simple, operational semantic strategy improve weighted M2 classification over a deployable structured baseline, and is any gain more consistent with clinical semantic content than with documentation and test-ordering workflow? An apparently positive model can fail either requirement: S may not improve on routine structured information, or it may merely exploit report templates, machine provenance, and selective assay ordering. Stored report text may also have become visible only after the historical decision, and pathology grade may be unavailable or unreliable.

**Substantive advance.** This two-parent successor makes two distinct prespecified claims, requires both for full support, and evaluates both with the same patient-level latent report-availability state and pathology-grade uncertainty. It therefore separates clinical incremental value, `S−B`, from semantic attribution beyond measured workflow, `S−W`, without letting eventual report storage stand in for decision-time access or dropping patients with unknown grade. The strongest possible result is historical, institution-specific, uncertainty-bounded M2 classification value that merits prospective validation; it is not treatment-effect evidence.

## Co-primary hypotheses and estimands

At the fixed risk threshold `p=0.30` in the locked 2020–2021 target frame:

1. **Clinical incremental-value claim (C):** the operational semantic strategy S has a sharp worst-case paired net-benefit gain over the deployable structured baseline B of at least 0.01 per target-frame patient, with sampling uncertainty excluding zero, and retains positive gain across `p=0.25–0.35`.
2. **Semantic-attribution claim (A):** S has the same material, uncertainty-bounded gain over workflow/process model W, retains it across `p=0.25–0.35`, and exceeds a process-matched semantic-null distribution.

These are co-primary components of one conjunctive hypothesis. **Full support requires both C and A.** C without A can support only a historical S-versus-B classification increment whose source is unresolved; A without C can support only detectable semantic signal that lacks material incremental clinical value over B. Neither alone supports deployment.

Net benefit is `NB_M(p)=TP/N−[p/(1−p)]FP/N`, with `N` equal to the entire locked target frame. The 0.01 margin means one additional true-positive-equivalent per 100 target patients at the stated false-positive weight. It is prespecified as a classification materiality margin, not an elicited patient or clinician utility.

## Frozen population, index, and clocks

The unit is one patient (`Patient Master Index`) and one first eligible index resection.

1. Exact-row deduplicate each source and report duplicate and join multiplicity. Break exact-time ties by `Encounter Number`, then normalized `Procedure`, then source row order. Emit every raw included/excluded operation value and frequency, all ties, and a one-index-per-patient assertion before outcome access. Do not skip an earlier qualifying resection to select a later better-documented one.
2. Require age at least 18 and pathology text in the index surgical encounter that identifies HCC. This creates a retrospectively pathology-confirmed resected-HCC frame; it does not prove preoperative diagnostic certainty, resectability, R0 status, or curative intent. Do not require an MVI grade or secure specimen linkage. Missing or ambiguous grade/linkage is `V=0`, not exclusion. Patients without any same-encounter HCC-identifying pathology are outside the computable frame; outcome bounds cannot repair that selection.
3. Set `t_dec = procedures.Start Time − 24 hours`. The buffer is frozen and not chosen from outcomes. Records at or after `t_dec` are forbidden predictors. Labeled 12-, 48-, and 72-hour buffer analyses are sensitivity analyses only.
4. A liver CT/MRI row is acquisition-eligible when `examinations.start time` is valid and lies in `[t_dec−90 days,t_dec)`. At least one such row is required. After exact-row deduplication, bundle rows by `(patient master index, visit number, nonmissing examination number, start time)`; a missing `examination number` creates a row-level bundle. Freeze CT/MRI dictionaries from exact `examination` values. Select at most the latest CT and latest MRI bundle by `start time`, breaking ties by `examination number`, normalized `examination`, and source row order. Report discarded bundles and multiplicity.
5. Concatenate deduplicated bundle text in source order. When more than one selected report is available, aggregate core values deterministically: maximum reported lesion diameter; highest count category (1, 2, or at least 3); any explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus; and for each semantic feature, present if any report is positive, absent only if every available report explicitly supports absence, otherwise unknown. Contradictory statements are unknown and enter radiology review.
6. A laboratory row is timestamp-eligible when `labs.test time` is valid and lies in `[t_dec−30 days,t_dec)`. For every frozen exact `test`/`specimen type` pair, take the last row, breaking equal-time ties by source order and reporting them. There is no unit or separate result-finalization field: never merge assay names by apparent meaning, apply external cutoffs, or call values proven decision-visible. A prespecified sensitivity masks values with `test time` in `[t_dec−48 hours,t_dec)`.
7. The primary cohort has no **recorded** prior HCC-directed resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during `[t_dec−365 days,t_dec)`, using frozen reviewed dictionaries and valid times. Report exclusions by source and invalid time. This does not establish biological treatment-naivety. Previously treated patients form a separate sensitivity cohort.
8. Development is 2015–2018. Hyperparameter selection and logistic recalibration use 2019 only. The untouched test is 2020–2021, assigned by index-operation date with every patient's records kept in one period. Exclude 2013–2014. Frozen scoring of 2022 through snapshot end 2025-11-11 is permitted only for predictor/template drift and endpoint-identifiability summaries; no contemporary performance claim is allowed.
9. Before test outcomes are read, freeze operation, HCC, treatment, modality and assay dictionaries; bundle and tie rules; report aggregation; parser rules; text masks; template construction; preprocessing; elastic-net settings; calibration; thresholds; availability states; pathology rules; and all gates.

Harbor must emit actual counts at every filter, invalid and missing clocks, raw dictionary frequencies, exclusions, duplicate counts, many-to-one join multiplicity, selected/discarded bundles, and period disjointness. Private row-level derivatives remain in the workspace; source files remain read-only.

## Pathology-only endpoint and adjudication

`Y=1` only for pathology-defined M2. `Y=0` only for M0 or M1. Under the Chinese definition, M0 has no MVI; M1 has 1–5 MVI sites within 1 cm of the tumor; M2 has more than 5 sites within 1 cm or any site beyond 1 cm. `V=1` means that the frozen parser plus adjudication supports a trustworthy index-specimen M0/M1/M2 assignment. Every other state is `V=0`.

Assign grade only from the immediate selected value after a position-anchored field such as `MVI risk grade[:：]`, stopping before definitions, explanatory bullets, historical clauses, or a new specimen section, or from a synonymous selected-value construction frozen after blinded development review. Handle negation, `not seen`, multiple specimens, and discordance. Unanchored M2 text, binary `microvascular invasion: present/absent`, positive-ungraded MVI, blank number/distance fields, absent MVI language, explanatory definitions, and conflicting fields cannot assign grade. Pathology is never a predictor.

Two independently implemented parsers must agree on the locked test, with disagreement no more than 5%. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 sampled by year and template, and every test `V=0`; a third resolves disagreement. Require class-specific PPV at least 0.98, sensitivity at least 0.95, and kappa at least 0.90. Reviewers may derive grade only from explicit available number/distance language under the frozen definition. Ambiguous specimen linkage, inadequate text, or unknown sampling remains `V=0`.

This adjudication interprets stored text; it cannot recover raw slides, block counts, exact vessel distance, sampling adequacy, or a missing specimen identifier. The exact joint bounds below therefore retain every residual `V=0`.

## Shared decision-availability states

For patient `i`, let `q_i` be the number, 0–2, of selected CT/MRI bundles with nonempty eventual report text. Define `Ω_i={0,1}^{q_i}`; `r_j=1` means report `j` is assumed finalized and visible at `t_dec`, while `r_j=0` means unavailable. Empty stored text has state 0 only. The **same complete state vector** is used simultaneously for B, S, W, U, and default comparisons; a contrast may never assign favorable availability to one model and adverse availability to another.

At state 0, mask every report-body-derived item from that bundle: lesion size/count, macrovascular thrombus, semantic findings, report length/sections/template, and unrestricted text. Acquisition facts remain: valid `Start Time`, modality from `Examination`, bundle count, distinct-`Examination Number` count without raw identifiers, and machine-availability/development-frequency category from `Machine Model`. At state 1, stored report variables are exposed. No availability probability, monotonicity assumption, nearest-template imputation, or claim that acquisition proves visibility is allowed.

To prevent unavailable-state extrapolation, development and 2019 recalibration use state augmentation: each patient contributes all states in `Ω_i` with total patient weight one, divided equally across states. Splits, penalty selection, calibration, bootstrap, and permutation stay patient-grouped. This is a robustness construction, not evidence that any state occurred. The acquisition-complete scenario (all nonempty reports available) and strict no-uncertified-report scenario (all states 0) are secondary diagnostics only.

## Models and baselines

Fit separate elastic-net logistic regressions using the same `V=1` development patients and state weights. Choose penalty settings without test access and fit a 2019 logistic recalibration intercept and slope for each frozen model.

**B—deployable structured baseline.** Age and sex; modality (CT only, MRI only, both) and acquisition provenance; last timestamp-eligible exact-assay albumin, total bilirubin, INR, and platelet values with missing indicators; and, only for report-available bundles, maximum lesion diameter, count (1, 2, at least 3), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus with unknown indicators. B contains no extension semantic feature, extension assay, report-format feature, raw identifier, machine identity, or template family.

**S—operational semantic strategy.** Exactly B plus frozen present/absent/unknown extractions for complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular or non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity from `examination findings` and `examination diagnosis` when reports are available; and last eligible exact-assay AFP, ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, and creatinine numeric values with missing indicators. Exact assay/`specimen type` pairs receive separate development-only winsorization and standardization; within-exact-assay/year ranks are a sensitivity. S contains no W-only process feature, n-gram, year, exact examination code, raw `examination number`, raw machine identifier, procedure name, diagnosis, pathology, clinical-document text, or post-`t_dec` content.

**W—workflow/process negative control.** Exactly B plus acquisition count and time since last acquisition; exact `Examination` categories; machine availability and development-frequency category, never raw identity; extension-assay presence/count/time-since-last but never extension-assay values; and, in report-available states, character/line/section counts and an outcome-blind formatting family built after masking clinical words, numbers, dates, identifiers, and values. Clustering is fit in 2015–2018 and frozen; unseen formats map to “unseen.” W never receives added semantic findings or extension assay values. W is deliberately nondeployable and is not a clinical alternative. `S−W` tests whether S beats measured workflow/provenance prediction; it does not prove a causal semantic mechanism or absence of unmeasured workflow confounding.

**U—unrestricted-text ceiling, secondary.** S plus report-available development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after removing identifiers, dates, accession strings, pathology/MVI terms, and post-cutoff text. Audit its 100 largest absolute coefficients before unblinding. U cannot rescue either failed primary claim. Report S−U at 0.30; a one-sided lower limit above −0.005 supports that interpretable S captures nearly all available text gain but is not a third primary hypothesis.

Also evaluate flag-all and flag-none. For S, repeat the primary analyses after neutralizing extension-lab missing indicators and imputing development medians within exact assay; this asks whether S relies on ordering behavior. It does not replace the primary model.

Two blinded radiologists review at least 200 reports for core size/count/macrovascular thrombus and, separately in each test year, at least 150 reports enriched for every semantic feature and parser-negative cases, with CT- and MRI-specific representation. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in at least 90%, count-category agreement at least 90% with kappa at least 0.80, and per-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity at least 0.85 for every added binary feature. Review validates interpretation of stored text, not historical availability.

## Exact joint outcome-and-availability bounds

For threshold `p`, let `w=p/(1−p)` and `A_Mi(r,p)=1[score_Mi(r)≥p]`. For contrast `X=M_1−M_0`, the patient contribution is

`c_i^X(y,r,p)=y[A_M1i(r,p)−A_M0i(r,p)]−w(1−y)[A_M1i(r,p)−A_M0i(r,p)]`.

For `V_i=1`, set `l_i^X=min_{r∈Ω_i} c_i^X(Y_i,r,p)` and `u_i^X=max_{r∈Ω_i} c_i^X(Y_i,r,p)`. For `V_i=0`, minimize or maximize jointly over `r∈Ω_i` and `y∈{0,1}`. Then

`L_X(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i l_i^X`, and `U_X(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i u_i^X`.

For one model, use `b_i^M(y,r,p)=A_Mi(r,p)[y−w(1−y)]` and the same extrema. Compute these exact patient-wise extrema for S−B, S−W, S−U, W−B, each model versus flag-all/flag-none, and each model's NB. With at most four report states and two outcome states per patient, enumeration is exact and sharp under the stated unrestricted patient-specific uncertainty set. Do not substitute complete cases, midpoint assignment, inverse weighting, or separate model-specific states.

At `p=0.30`, decompose interval width into pathology ambiguity with all reports available, report-availability ambiguity among `V=1`, and the full joint interval. Report the fraction of patients whose flag changes over `Ω_i` for each model. These identify the source of imprecision, not the latent truth.

Use 2,000 whole-target-frame patient bootstraps of frozen test predictions and all patient states. Report percentile intervals for both sharp endpoints. For co-primary inference, create a joint simultaneous 95% band from the bootstrap maximum absolute deviation over both contrasts, S−B and S−W, and all 21 thresholds `0.20,0.21,…,0.40`. This controls threshold and co-primary multiplicity. Define `R_C` and `R_A` as thresholds where the relevant simultaneous lower band for `L_X` exceeds zero and S's lower-bound NB exceeds both default strategies. The fixed materiality test remains 0.30; `0.25–0.35` is a robustness plateau, not a threshold search.

The global confirmatory decision is an intersection-union test: both co-primary components must pass, so no post hoc substitution or alpha recycling is allowed. U comparisons, neutralized-missingness analyses, buffer analyses, subgroups, discrimination metrics, and drift summaries are secondary; report their uncertainty and use Holm correction within any explicitly inferential secondary family. Otherwise label them descriptive.

At 0.30 report joint bounds by test year, sex, age below/at least 65, AFP availability, CT-only/MRI-only/dual acquisition provenance, and the four largest test template families when available, pooling others. Suppress inference if a subgroup has fewer than 100 target-frame patients or 20 `V=1` M2 events. Among `V=1`, report paired AUPRC, AUROC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV for acquisition-complete and no-report scenarios, labeled selection-sensitive. Modeling `V` or report availability is descriptive and cannot identify missing outcomes or visibility.

## Workflow falsification and validity gates

Run 1,000 process-matched semantic-null repetitions. Within each period, permute the complete added S block at patient level within frozen year, CT/MRI provenance, template-availability, and extension-assay-availability strata; preserve each donor block internally, reapply the recipient's report-state masks, and keep B, W, Y/V, patient grouping, and states fixed. Refit and recalibrate every repetition using the inherited deterministic small-block pooling hierarchy. The observed `L_{S−W}(0.30)` must exceed the 97.5th percentile. Label permutation and unrestricted patient-level semantic shuffle must be compatible with zero. Any post-cutoff model is a leakage demonstration only.

No primary claim is interpretable unless all applicable gates pass:

- locked test `N≥1,000`, at least 75 adjudicated M2 outcomes, `V=1≥85%`, and every test `V=0` reviewed;
- both pathology parsers and pathologist reliability gates pass;
- radiology core and every S semantic feature pass year- and modality-specific reliability gates;
- identical patients, outcomes, and shared states are used by all models; every report-derived feature is masked in unavailable states;
- all clocks obey the frozen windows, test outcomes were untouched, and no forbidden feature enters B or S;
- acquisition-complete report text is nonempty for at least 80%, core size/count is jointly extractable for at least 70%, and every B laboratory concept is timestamp-eligible in at least 70%;
- no required-variable missingness changes by more than 15 percentage points from 2019 to either test year; unseen-template prevalence is reported;
- CT-only and MRI-only each meet subgroup minima;
- state-weighted S calibration among `V=1` has slope 0.80–1.20, intercept −0.10–0.10, sensitivity at least 0.70, and PPV at least 0.50 at 0.30; these are selection-sensitive adequacy gates, not population estimates;
- the process-matched permutation is estimable and all compiler unit tests pass.

A failed gate makes the affected claim inconclusive, not adverse. Better AUPRC, AUROC, or an acquisition-complete estimate cannot override a failed gate or adverse decision result.

## Prespecified result zones and permissible conclusions

Evaluate C and A separately before assigning the global label.

**C is supportive** only if all gates pass; `L_{S−B}(0.30)≥0.01`; the bootstrap lower 95% limit for that sharp lower endpoint is above zero; `[0.25,0.35]⊆R_C`; S's lower-bound NB exceeds flag-all and flag-none throughout that interval; neutralizing extension-lab missingness leaves `L_{S−B}(0.30)>0`; and neither test year's point lower bound is negative.

**A is supportive** only if all gates pass; `L_{S−W}(0.30)≥0.01`; its bootstrap lower 95% limit is above zero; `[0.25,0.35]⊆R_A`; CT-only and MRI-only each have positive lower endpoints with lower limits above zero; neither test year's point lower bound is negative; and the observed statistic exceeds the matched-null 97.5th percentile.

**Fully supportive** requires both C and A. The strongest permissible conclusion is: “In this institution's 2020–2021 first-resection frame, the frozen semantic strategy had material, threshold-robust weighted M2 classification value over the routine structured baseline and over measured workflow/provenance, even under adverse assignment of unknown selected-report visibility and unresolved pathology grade.” It remains a historical classification statement. It does not prove reports were actually visible, semantics caused the gain, a threshold is acceptable, clinicians would act, or patients would benefit.

**Adverse to C:** with gates passed, the upper 95% limit for `U_{S−B}(0.30)` is below 0.01, or S's upper-bound NB does not beat a default strategy. An upper limit below zero indicates net harm. An estimable required threshold/year stratum with an upper endpoint at or below zero falsifies the threshold/temporal robustness component.

**Adverse to A:** with gates passed, the upper 95% limit for `U_{S−W}(0.30)` is below 0.01; an upper limit below zero indicates harm relative to W. Positive W−B with failed S−W, failure to exceed the matched semantic-null distribution, loss of S−B after missingness neutralization, or an estimable CT-only/MRI-only upper endpoint at or below zero falsifies the full documentation-independent attribution claim.

**Inconclusive for a claim:** a gate fails; the joint interval or its sampling interval includes both material benefit and no material benefit; the required simultaneous band spans zero; report/pathology uncertainty dominates; or a required subgroup/permutation is not estimable. Inconclusive is neither evidence of no association nor evidence of utility.

The global interpretation is explicit:

- C supportive and A supportive: fully supportive.
- C adverse: globally adverse to deployable incremental value, regardless of A.
- C supportive but A adverse: S may outperform B historically, but semantic attribution is falsified; the dual hypothesis is not supported and workflow/provenance remains a plausible explanation.
- A supportive but C adverse: semantic information may be detectable, but it lacks the prespecified clinical increment; the dual hypothesis is adverse.
- One component supportive and the other inconclusive, or both inconclusive: globally inconclusive.
- Acquisition-complete support with failed joint bounds is inconclusive for decision-time value.

Negative or inconclusive findings remain useful: they identify whether the limitation is lack of clinical increment, workflow attribution, report timestamps, pathology ascertainment, extraction reliability, or event count.

## Harbor compiler and verifier contract
