# Executed audit of leakage-safe latest intermediate albumin or bilirubin before repeat TACE

## Frozen question and claim boundary

For adults already selected for a recorded first repeat TACE, the prespecified question remains whether one numeric, leakage-safe, latest same-assay intermediate value `L` improves prediction of the next observed albumin (`白蛋白`) or total bilirubin (`总胆红素`) beyond current `P`, remote baseline `B`, compact covariates, and identical controls for assay-history presence, count, and recency.

The strongest previously supported claim was feasibility. The unresolved claim was an out-of-sample standardized RMSE gain of at least 0.05. This execution produces informative provisional model results, but the frozen reconstruction gate did not pass: the independently reconstructed eligible base was 2,991 rather than the reference 2,992. Under the prespecified branch order, the experiment is therefore **reconstruction-inconclusive**, and no inferential/supportive/null branch may be asserted. The discrepancy is localized upstream of outcomes and did not alter the downstream frozen arm, event, triplet, or history counts.

This remains a noncausal prognostic-information experiment. The endpoint is a clinician-timed next observed assay, not hepatic failure, toxicity, treatment benefit, clinical utility, or a recommendation about testing or retreatment.

## Canonical assay table and clock checks

The implementation constructs event records first, selects `B`, `P`, `Y`, and `L` independently per exact assay, asserts each patient appears once per assay, and uses explicit namespaces: `B_value/B_time`, `P_value/P_time`, `Y_value/Y_time`, `L_value/L_time`, `H_any`, `N_H`, `logN`, and `R_hours`. The resulting private table has exactly one row per `(patient, assay)` and was not published because it contains patient keys and exact times.

The locked clocks were retained:

- `B`: latest valid uncensored value on TACE2 days -30 through -1;
- `P`: latest value in `event_encounter` during `[event_time-72h,event_time)`;
- `Y`: same-encounter post-event value in `(event_time,event_time+72h]` nearest +24h;
- `H`: same assay, from `tace2_time`, before `event_time-72h`, before `P`, and outside `event_encounter`;
- `L`: latest qualifying `H`, with `N_H` based on distinct timestamps.

Assertions prohibited event-encounter history, history at/after `P`, history in the current-state buffer, and post-event history. Canonical uniqueness passed. Assay gates exactly reproduced:

- albumin triplets: 40 systemic, 118 comparator; `H_any`: 37/108; `N_H>=2`: 18/42;
- bilirubin triplets: 41 systemic, 119 comparator; `H_any`: 38/108; `N_H>=2`: 18/42.

The lab scan reproduced 476,846 exact target rows and 476,820 valid uncensored numeric-time rows, with 4 invalid numeric strings and 22 invalid clocks. The execution's duplicate audit was limited to target rows attached to selected repeat-event patients and therefore does not independently reproduce the inherited global seven duplicate groups; it found none in the selected population. The inherited complete-source audit remains required for the global duplicate gate.

## Estimator and uncertainty audit

The deterministic paired LOOCV OLS estimand is coherent for this narrow prediction contrast. Every held-out patient was excluded from training; imputation, zero-variance dropping, centering/scaling, and rank checks were training-only; the models used identical retained shared columns and differed only by `L`; and the intercept was unpenalized. All primary folds passed finite/full-rank gates.

The 2,000 arm-stratified bootstraps resampled the stored paired LOOCV tuples with PCG64 seed `20260910`; all were estimable. This uncertainty is coherent only **conditional on the realized cross-fitted predictions**. It does not propagate refitting/training-set or cohort-sampling uncertainty and cannot support population-wide, utility, or transportability claims. A refit-within-bootstrap design would answer a stronger uncertainty question but would constitute a substantive design change and was not substituted here.

## Provisional computed results, subordinate to the failed reconstruction gate

### Albumin

Among 158 triplets, `M_obs` RMSE was 3.1731 and `M_value` RMSE was 3.0169; standardized `Delta_value=0.0319`, below the design-sized 0.05 margin. MAE was 2.3639 versus 2.1276 (`Delta_MAE=0.0483`). Cross-fitted R-squared was 0.5769 versus 0.6175. Conditional tuple-bootstrap 95% interval was [-0.0080, 0.0726], one-sided p=0.0590 and Holm-adjusted p=0.1179.

Numeric-linkage destruction gave a 95th-percentile null delta of 0.0042; the observed 0.0319 exceeded it. Results stayed positive among `H_any=1` (`delta=0.0279`), under 48-hour `P` (`0.0311`), under first-post-event `Y` (`0.0297`), and under every single-patient deletion (range 0.0236 to 0.0389; maximum change 0.0083). Thus the provisional favorable direction is not explained by the prescribed linkage, history-missingness, timing, or influence checks, but it neither reaches 0.05 nor excludes zero and cannot be interpreted past the reconstruction gate.

### Total bilirubin

Among 160 triplets, `M_obs` RMSE was 9.6254 and `M_value` RMSE was 9.6872; `Delta_value=-0.00453`. MAE was 6.4744 versus 6.4908 (`Delta_MAE=-0.00120`). Cross-fitted R-squared was 0.4984 versus 0.4919. Conditional tuple-bootstrap 95% interval was [-0.0110, 0.00527], one-sided p=0.8211 (Holm-adjusted 0.8211).

The observed delta did not exceed the numeric-linkage null 95th percentile (0.00312). The direction remained approximately null/adverse among `H_any=1` (-0.00067), with 48-hour `P` (-0.00485), first-post-event `Y` (-0.00470), and all single-patient deletions (-0.00542 to -0.00097). If reconstruction had passed, the interval upper bound below +0.05 would meet the proposal's conditional **precise-null-for-the-design-sized-increment** criterion, not equivalence or proof that history has no biological value. Because reconstruction did not pass, this remains only a provisional diagnostic.

## Ordered interpretation and falsification

The formal result is **reconstruction-inconclusive**. The one-person eligible-base/prior discrepancy and incomplete independent global duplicate reproduction occur before the inferential branches and require resolution without outcome-guided tuning. Consequently:

- supportive evidence is not claimed for albumin despite its positive, linkage-specific provisional signal;
- adverse degradation is not claimed for bilirubin because its interval spans zero;
- precise null is not formally claimed, although bilirubin's conditional interval is provisionally incompatible with a +0.05 gain;
- no two-assay supportive statement is available.

Once reconstruction is reconciled, the predeclared branches remain: supportive only with delta >=0.05, interval excluding zero, Holm-adjusted one-sided p<0.05, nonnegative MAE gain, linkage superiority, and all fragility gates passed; adverse only if the interval lies wholly below zero; precise null only if the upper bound is below +0.05; otherwise no demonstrated increment or inconclusive as specified. The 0.05 threshold is a design convention, not a validated clinically important difference.
