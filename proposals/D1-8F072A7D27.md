# Temporal-direction and source-attribution successor for the frozen eICU respiratory-label design

## Inheritance and substantive advance

This is a substantive successor of the assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead-frozen scientific design exactly. It does not change the population, exposure, landmarks, decision time, exclusions, parser, endpoint states, estimand, clustering, transport target, or noncausal boundary.

The immutable population is 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons, and 74 hospitals. Retain the first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid`; retain repeated landmarks within that stay as rows and cluster by `patientunitstayid`. Use `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission. For each landmark select the first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`; set `D = cplitemoffset` and verify `D == decision_offset`. The only arms are the exact strings `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` (`evaluation`) and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` (`no_trial`).

Retain the inherited windows `B=(D-720,D]`, `E=(D,D+720]`, and `T=(D+720,D+2160]`, the locked parser for FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate, its exact aliases, finite/bounded parsing, FiO2 fraction/percentage conversion, duplicate and same-time conflict rules, minimum two observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes per component, and at least three of four components per state. Retain thresholds FiO2 `0.10` fraction, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`.

The ordered all-row outcome remains

`K ∈ {competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.

Finite `unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160` has precedence; death, discharge, transfer, inability to complete T, and invalid or unavailable exit time remain explicit administrative/competing statuses. Never recode an unobservable row as a respiratory negative or silently drop it. The primary displays remain fixed-arm-denominator `p_k(a)` and `Δ_k = p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`; conditional classifiable `q_c(a)` and `δ_c` remain secondary. These are descriptive associations with named structured-chart constructs, not treatment effects or clinical truth.

The strongest evidence supported before execution is only that the two literal strings are sparse, heterogeneous structured-chart documentation and may encode a mixture of pre-D state, source opportunity, site workflow, selection, exits, and persistence. No available evidence establishes an SBT, extubation evaluation, readiness, clinician intent, delivered setting, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The falsifiable question remains: **after pre-D state and value-blind opportunity adjustment, does the exact current label add reproducible, temporally directional information about the locked post-D K trajectory in held-out hospitals, beyond label persistence and endpoint-source opportunity?** A separate bounded question is whether any such information can prioritize a fixed-capacity manual chart-review queue. It is not a bedside or patient-utility decision.

The substantive advance over both parents is a single executable temporal-and-attribution contract. It (i) proves that each primary predictor is available before D under clinical time, (ii) separates current-label information from residual state, opportunity, and source provenance, (iii) requires source-isolated and historical-label placebo checks, (iv) attributes any out-of-fold increment to predeclared blocks without treating attribution as causality, and (v) fails closed when support, overlap, transport, attribution stability, or uncertainty is inadequate.

## Temporal-direction contract

Before any model or arm-stratified output, emit `pre_D_baseline.json`, `post_D_endpoint.json`, and `post_D_diagnostic.json`. Each feature record must name table, column, clinical-time field, inequality, window, transformation, block, and permitted use. The compiler must mechanically assert for every primary predictor that its clinical offset is `< D`; entry-clock time never substitutes for clinical time. Record separately rows with clinical time `< D` but entry time `>= D`; these are retained only under the declared clinical-time rule and reported as an entry-lag audit, never silently treated as contemporaneously available. Any predictor derived from post-D values, K/H statuses, exit information, placebo targets, current care-plan fields beyond exposure verification, or future labels invalidates the primary analysis.

Use mutually exclusive blocks with a machine-readable feature dictionary:

- `S`: finite value-derived pre-D respiratory state proxies, including locked B-window component summaries/trends, valid pre-D airway facts, and finite pre-D `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `respiration` summaries;
- `O`: value-blind source opportunity, including row counts, distinct clinical timestamps, spacing/window availability, missingness flags, entry-lag summaries, respiratory-care row/interval counts, vital row/timestamp counts and spacing, and treatment row counts;
- `C`: fixed design covariates only, including landmark, age category, and explicitly inherited pre-D covariates.

No feature may occur in two blocks. Ambiguous, nonfinite, source-presence, or unvalidated text fields are O or excluded, never S. The exact current label belongs to no block and enters only M1. Fit deterministic leave-one-hospital-out folds, assigning all landmarks of a stay to one fold and never using hospital ID as a predictor. Training-only imputation, scaling, calibration, regularization, feature maps, support cells, and cutpoints are frozen before held-out target values are read.

Use the fixed nested multinomial family `M_C`, `M_O=C+O`, `M_S=C+S`, `M_0=C+S+O`, and `M_1=M_0+exact current label`, with fixed low-dimensional specification, solver, tolerance, grid, and tie-break declared in `compute_manifest.json`. Store one out-of-fold prediction, target status, fold, hospital, stay, landmark, and denominator flag per row.

## Source isolation and temporal placebos

Evaluate the same folds, nested specifications, clinical windows, and training-only transformations in predeclared views:

- `ALL`: all permitted sources and fields;
- `RC`: respiratoryCharting/respiratoryCare state and opportunity fields plus C, excluding vitalPeriodic values;
- `VP`: vitalPeriodic state and opportunity fields plus C, excluding respiratoryCharting/respiratoryCare value fields; only explicitly permitted value-blind counts/timestamps may remain.

A view that removes an endpoint-adjacent source tests provenance, not semantic validity. Any gain that disappears in a source-isolated view is source-dependent; a gain that persists in another view is only reproducible association with named chart constructs.

Freeze these diagnostics without altering primary K:

1. `K_pre`: locked parser from `P=(D-1440,D-720]` to B, using only pre-D respiratory clinical time and airway corroboration; unsupported states are `pre_unobservable`, never a negative.
2. `O_pre_shift`: value-blind respiratoryCharting opportunity in P, requiring at least two distinct clinical timestamps spanning at least 30 minutes; unavailable is explicit.
3. `O_E_RC`, `O_T_RC`, `O_E_VP`, and `O_T_VP`: value-blind post-D opportunity targets with the declared minimum distinct timestamps and five-minute span for VP.
4. `K_future`: future anchor `D_pl=D+720`, historical current label retained, and newly parsed `(D_pl,D_pl+720]` and `(D_pl+720,D_pl+2160]` trajectory with the same parser and exit precedence. Original-label and future-anchor windows must be checked for overlap; overlap, competing exit, invalid anchor, and unavailable windows are explicit statuses.

A positive pre-D parser or opportunity increment is adverse to a clean temporally directional residual-state interpretation but does not identify the cause. A positive future-anchor increment blocks a contemporaneous interpretation and is reported only as compatible with persistence, evolving state, or site process. Nulls are reassuring at most; unavailable or overlap-dominated diagnostics are inconclusive, never favorable.

Audit within-stay persistence: exact-label recurrence, prior same-label time, prior-label count, and equality with the immediately preceding valid label. Compare M1 versus M0 in first-landmark-only, no-overlap, and repeated-landmark strata. Historical labels cannot enter M1; any separate persistence model is diagnostic only. A gain confined to recurrent or repeated rows is adverse to temporal specificity.

## Attribution and falsification contract

Attribution is descriptive model accounting, not causal attribution. For each held-out fold and source view, emit the complete nested out-of-fold loss/Brier decomposition `M_C -> M_O -> M_S -> M_0 -> M_1`, with row/stay/hospital denominators, K-state status counts, and the exact training-only feature dictionary. The label increment is the held-out difference `M_1-M_0`; do not infer it from in-sample coefficients, post-hoc model selection, or a favorable subset.

For each supported hospital, landmark, S×O cell, and source view, emit:

- out-of-fold M1-minus-M0 multiclass log-loss and Brier increments;
- M_O-minus-M_C and M_0-minus-M_S increments, so opportunity and state contributions cannot be mislabeled as current-label information;
- permutation or drop-column diagnostics for each predeclared block and feature, performed inside training folds and evaluated only on held-out rows;
- feature/block attribution totals, signs, uncertainty, and the fraction of folds/hospitals with the same direction;
- exact source provenance, clinical-time eligibility, out-of-range/missing status, and whether the attribution is supported.

Do not use attributions to define the cohort, choose the parser, change cells, select a target, or claim bedside mechanisms. A label gain with unstable signs, a gain attributable only to O, a gain confined to one source/component/site, or a gain that fails when current-label persistence is removed is not residual patient-state evidence. `interpretability_attribution.json` must link every reported increment to out-of-fold prediction rows, target definition, support set, source view, and uncertainty artifact; missing linkage makes the run computationally invalid.

Preserve the broad hospital-by-landmark opportunity-preserving label permutation and the complete-stay S/O-conditional label-vector permutation. Use seed `61061`; attempt at least 1,000 valid arrangements when feasible or enumerate all unique arrangements when fewer. Assert unchanged stay IDs/signatures, vector lengths, exact arm counts, hospital membership, landmark/cell counts, and no stay splitting. Fewer than 20 valid global arrangements, fewer than five hospitals, a lost supported cell, or any failed invariant is inconclusive. An observed label increment typical of either conditional null is adverse to incremental information beyond the conditioned structure. Do not call a permutation envelope a confidence interval.

## Support, transport, and bounded review gates

Within each training fold, derive scalar S and O scores and deterministic tertile bins, using median split and documented feature-order sparse collapse only if necessary. Cross with landmark; apply frozen cutpoints without clipping to held-out rows. A supported cell requires at least 10 rows in each exact arm, at least 20 total, finite training maps, and no unresolved block assignment. Record held-out in-range, out-of-range, missing, and invalid counts by feature, hospital, and cell.

Define supported hospitals before outcome comparison as hospitals with at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows in each arm and at least one supported S×O×landmark cell. Report both `theta_micro(H*)` (row-weighted held-out quantities) and `theta_macro(H*)` (equal-hospital mean), with exact denominators. Also report equal-cell and row-weighted supported-cell means, label prevalence, site reversals, leave-one-supported-hospital-out influence, and out-of-range contribution. Transport interpretation is unavailable unless at least five hospitals satisfy the support rule, represent at least 50% of supported rows, no site contributes over 25%, and no more than 25% of supported hospitals reverse the prespecified direction. Residual S/O interpretation additionally requires at least five supported joint cells spanning at least five hospitals, both arms, and no cell above 25% of supported rows. Unsupported cells are unavailable, not negative evidence.

Keep `vitalPeriodic` separate. Define `H_E,H_T ∈ {alert,no_alert,physiologic_unobservable}`; opportunity requires two finite observations at distinct offsets spanning at least five minutes; alert requires at least two valid observations with `sao2 < 88` or `respiration > 30`. These are summary-vital alerts, not hypoxemia, tachypnea, respiratory failure, harm, or adjudicated outcomes.

For the only operational exercise, compare held-out M0 and M1 for `Y_K=1{K=classifiable-escalation}`, `Y_HE`, and `Y_HT` on applicable supported denominators. Use thresholds 0.10 through 0.90, costs 0.05/0.10/0.20/0.30/0.50, unresolved penalties 0.25/0.50/1.00, unresolved-review costs 0.00/0.05/0.10, and capacities 10%/20%/30%. Emit review/no-review/abstain tables versus M0, treat-none, treat-all-observed-target, and deterministic fixed-capacity policies. Exits and unobservable states remain unresolved.

Call a target operationally decision-relevant only if valid held-out M1 improves both micro and macro loss, has positive incremental net benefit over M0 at two adjacent fixed costs and one capacity, and has a stay-cluster bootstrap interval excluding zero. Stronger structured-chart triangulation additionally requires concordance for K and the separate H target in at least two supported overlap hospitals. This is a review-allocation result only, not clinical guidance or patient utility.

## Uncertainty and fail-closed interpretation

Use fixed-seed `61061` stay-cluster bootstrap, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates. Resample complete first qualifying stays within hospital, carrying all landmarks, labels, predictors, K/H/placebo statuses, and attribution rows together; refit fold-local pipelines and recompute support, transport, views, null-compatible summaries, and review metrics. Never row-bootstrap or nest permutations inside bootstrap. Fewer than 500 valid replicates makes uncertainty-dependent claims inconclusive.

Computational invalidity includes source/schema/catalog/hash/member mismatch; absent or divergent frozen artifacts; duplicate/non-one-to-one joins; wrong labels, landmarks, D, parser, windows, units, inequalities, or K/exit precedence; post-D or entry-time leakage; care-plan contamination; duplicated block membership; fold/stay leakage; placebo overlap leakage; invalid source attribution; failed null invariants; unsupported transport/action claims; or missing artifact-to-conclusion linkage. Set `verification.status=invalid` and do not interpret estimates.

Use three explicit conclusion gates:

- **Supportive** only when the exact computed artifact, denominator, held-out support, uncertainty interval, source view, temporal placebos, persistence audit, and conditional nulls satisfy their prespecified gates. The strongest permitted statement is incremental prediction of the named structured K/vital constructs, or bounded review prioritization.
- **Adverse** when a valid supported analysis shows no robust label increment, a temporal placebo/persistence increment, conditional-null compatibility, source/opportunity confinement, or transport reversal. State the compatible explanations without selecting among them.
- **Inconclusive** for failed feasibility, unavailable artifacts, insufficient support/overlap, out-of-range dominance, unavailable placebos, invalid timing, failed exchangeability, calibration failure, fewer than 500 bootstrap replicates, or missing verification linkage. Inconclusive is not a favorable or adverse result.

No result can establish bedside evaluation, extubation intent, readiness, delivered ventilator settings, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality. The release lacks raw waveforms, synchronized device/workflow logs, local interface dictionaries, validated bedside labels, adjudicated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, post-ICU follow-up, and external prospective replication. Expert chart adjudication, workflow/device data, validated outcomes, and another external or prospective study are required for stronger clinical conclusions. The demonstrations in `references/research-ambition/README.md` were inspected only within their stated availability limits; no unavailable cancer article or methods are claimed as read.
