# Proposal: component-resolved AFP/hepatic trajectories and unconditioned recorded pathways after an encounter-constrained TACE-like index

## Successor, scientific deliverable, and substantive repair

This is a targeted child of assessed valid parent \`[prior hypothesis]\`. It preserves the verified HCC index ontology, 24-hour encounter-constrained episode construction, day-45 landmark/risk set, unconditioned first recorded-pathway outcome, competing recorded states, patient-level temporal evaluation, capture and placebo falsification, and clinical evidence boundary.

The remaining ambiguity is consequential: the parent’s “hepatic-reserve direction” can be read as a clinically validated reserve score even though the HCC snapshot has no laboratory-unit column, no validated thresholds, and heterogeneous assay names. Collapsing albumin and total bilirubin into one worsening/non-worsening label also hides whether the signal is driven by one component, by asynchronous sampling, or by local measurement intensity. This child therefore makes the primary exposure component-resolved and assay-standardized. A two-component summary is a prespecified contrast only; it is never called ALBI, MELD, liver failure, or a clinical reserve measurement.

The actual future deliverable is a newly constructed, leakage-safe index/episode cohort and landmark feature set; a locked patient-level temporal comparison of transparent CPU models and an irregular-time joint state-space model; patient-clustered uncertainty for pathway-specific absolute contrasts; and an interpretation manifest linking every conclusion to computed estimates, intervals, calibration/overlap diagnostics, and a frozen supportive/adverse/inconclusive rule. Discovery has not fitted these models and claims no result.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence is only that dated local laboratory measurements, encounters, procedures, and examination/order opportunities can be linked within patient and encounter. It does not establish tumor burden, viable tumor, response, treatment intent or completion, or hepatic reserve in clinical units.

The unresolved question is whether early post-index AFP change contains pathway-relevant information beyond the two separate hepatic-marker trajectories and the probability of being observed. The clinically meaningful decision analogue is whether the pattern could distinguish patients more likely to enter a subsequent recorded liver-directed pathway from patients whose next record is diagnostic-only, non-liver care, or local observation loss. This is a prognostic recorded-care question, not a treatment recommendation or causal question.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among adults with an eligible first recorded TACE-like episode and no primary-ontology liver-directed therapeutic episode through day 45, the joint, component-resolved pattern of AFP direction, albumin direction, and total-bilirubin direction is associated with a different cause-specific distribution and cumulative incidence of first recorded pathways from days 46–365. In particular, an AFP decrease accompanied by non-worsening albumin and non-worsening total bilirubin will have a higher cumulative incidence of a subsequent specific/bundled liver-directed therapeutic record than an AFP decrease accompanied by worsening of either hepatic marker, after accounting for pre-index history and observation/capture intensity. The same contrast should not be reproduced by a non-liver capture outcome or a pre-index placebo trajectory.

The hypothesis is falsified if the component-specific interaction is null or reverses, is explained by measurement/capture variables, appears similarly for non-liver procedures or the placebo, is confined to one assay/source or to ambiguous diagnostic bundles, or is unstable under the encounter-only episode sensitivity. A null or adverse result would be informative about this recorded-pathway hypothesis; it would not prove absence of biological response.

## Component-resolved exposure and estimands

The required laboratory source is \`[internal dataset path]`, table \`labs\`, schema \`datasets/hcc/table-38aad8c54471332f.json\`, joined by \`患者主索引, 就诊号\`. Required fields are \`检验\`, \`定性结果\`, \`定量结果\`, \`标本类型\`, and \`检验时间\`. There is no unit field.

Freeze a training-only assay vocabulary and publish included/excluded names and reasons. Include AFP names such as \`甲胎蛋白\` only when the training audit confirms the exact name is the intended AFP assay; include \`白蛋白\` but exclude \`前白蛋白\`, urine albumin, glycated albumin, and other unrelated names; include \`总胆红素\` and explicitly exclude direct, conjugated, and generic bilirubin substitutes. Preserve exact assay name and specimen type. If multiple exact assay names are retained, standardize each assay separately and never pool raw values.

For every patient-index, use measurements strictly before the index and within days 7–45 after the index, using \`检验时间\`. Select the last eligible pre-index and first eligible post-index value for each exact assay, retaining measurement times, delays, counts, qualitative/inequality flags, and missingness. Parse only numeric \`定量结果\`; preserve qualitative and inequality results as nonnumeric observations, not silently imputed numbers.

For each exact assay, estimate a training-only robust within-assay scale from eligible repeated measurements (with a predeclared minimum-support rule). Convert the pre-to-post difference to an assay-specific standardized change; do not interpret its magnitude in clinical units. Use a fixed training-only tolerance to define decrease/non-decrease/uncertain for AFP, albumin, and total bilirubin. If a scale or paired measurement is unsupported, the component is unavailable/uncertain, not assigned a clinical category.

The primary component state is the cross-classification of:

- AFP: decrease versus non-decrease/uncertain/unavailable.
- Albumin: non-worsening versus worsening/uncertain/unavailable, where worsening means a sufficiently negative standardized change.
- Total bilirubin: non-worsening versus worsening/uncertain/unavailable, where worsening means a sufficiently positive standardized change.

The main clinical contrast is AFP decrease plus both hepatic components non-worsening versus AFP decrease plus worsening of either component. Component-specific contrasts are mandatory so that any apparent “reserve” effect can be attributed to albumin, bilirubin, both, or neither. A complete-case paired estimand is reported separately from a full-index estimand that retains all availability indicators. No clinical cutoff, unit conversion, ALBI/MELD score, or biological reserve claim is permitted.

The primary estimand is the standardized absolute contrast in cause-specific discrete-time hazards and cumulative incidence at 30, 90, and 320 days after day 45 for the first primary-broad liver-directed recorded transition and its competing recorded states. Report:

1. the AFP-by-albumin-by-bilirubin interaction and the prespecified component contrasts;
2. absolute CIF contrasts for \`S_explicit\`, \`S_bundle\`, \`A_ambiguous\`, \`U_mixed\`, and \`D_event\` overlays;
3. the full-index and paired-observed versions;
4. a capture decomposition against non-liver procedure and administrative observation states; and
5. encounter-only versus 24-hour episode sensitivity.

Observation weighting, if used, is predictive standardization only: estimate testing/contact probabilities on training data, check positivity and effective sample size, truncate by a frozen rule, and do not describe it as causal adjustment.

## Matched CPU baseline and substantive irregular-time alternative

All models use the same cohort, day-45 information boundary, outcomes, splits, uncertainty procedure, and locked test.

- **B0, history-only:** regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazards using age, sex, index year/department, pre-index diagnosis/procedure counts, prior local contact, and follow-up availability.
- **Bobs:** B0 plus pre-index and day-45 measurement/contact features: encounter intensity, lab availability by component, examination opportunity, order opportunity, procedure-source mix, index-to-follow-up interval, and local-observation measures. It tests whether capture alone explains the signal.
- **Bmain:** Bobs plus separate AFP, albumin, and total-bilirubin standardized changes, timing, component missingness, and main effects.
- **Bint (primary transparent test):** Bmain plus the prespecified three-way component interaction and its predeclared specificity/capture interactions. Use regularization fixed on tuning data, report coefficients/contrasts, calibration, overlap, and patient-clustered intervals. The estimand—not a small score gain—is the scientific comparison.
- **Mstate:** an irregular-time multi-output state-space model with separate latent AFP, albumin, and total-bilirubin states; assay-specific observation models; explicit observation/contact intensity; and competing transition hazards. Fit only on information available through day 45. It can reveal asynchronous sampling, nonlinear change, uncertainty in each component, and whether a composite is being driven by a single assay—information lost by Bint’s endpoint categories. Evaluate on exactly the same locked pathways and report posterior component contrasts, calibration, log score, and convergence/sensitivity to observation modeling.

The alternative is retained because it tests a substantive uncertainty about latent asynchronous component trajectories and informative sampling. It is not selected because it is more complex or because it might improve discrimination. A generic transformer is deferred: the limiting missing evidence is indication, intent, completion, response, diagnosis time, outside-care linkage, and units—not sequence-model capacity. Reconsider only if adjudicated clinical outcomes, validated modality labels, or an external linkage become available.

CPU tabular fitting is expected to fit within the solver planning envelope, but end-to-end runtime is unmeasured. Mstate convergence and any acceleration benefit are unverified. The planning envelope is up to 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, 28,800 seconds; a future solver may request one allocated A100 only after a bounded diagnostic shows material benefit, use the allocated device as \`cuda:0\`, and preserve checkpoints. Discovery used no model fitting or GPU job.

## Falsification, uncertainty, and interpretation

Before locked evaluation, freeze assay rules, tolerance, outcome ontology, margins, split, bootstrap count, and sparse-cell rules. Use patient-level bootstrap or an equivalent patient-clustered procedure for intervals; never bootstrap rows independently.

Supportive computation requires all of the following:

- Bint’s prespecified component interaction produces a directionally and materially nonzero full-index pathway contrast with a patient-level interval excluding the null/margin, acceptable calibration, and adequate overlap.
- The direction and substantive contrast are present in explicit and bundle outcomes, not only ambiguous/mixed or diagnostic-only records, with an event-support threshold declared before testing; sparse overlays are labeled inconclusive.
- Component-specific outputs show whether both markers contribute; a result driven only by one unverified assay is not called a hepatic-reserve interaction.
- The effect is not erased by Bobs, training-only observation weighting, high-contact restriction, source/assay sensitivity, or 24-hour versus encounter-only episode identity.
- The pattern is not similar for non-liver capture outcomes or the pre-index placebo trajectory.
- Mstate converges and its posterior component-specific pathway contrasts are directionally compatible with Bint; any improvement in same-target log score/calibration is reported with uncertainty and is not treated as clinical proof.

Adverse computation includes a null/reversal after observation adjustment; a single-assay or single-source effect; a large non-liver/placebo effect; concentration in ambiguous diagnostic contact; poor overlap or calibration; instability under episode grouping; Mstate nonconvergence or observation-model sensitivity; or an effect that disappears when albumin and bilirubin are kept separate. These support measurement, coding, episode identity, or local-retention explanations.

Inconclusive computation includes inadequate paired numeric support, unstable training vocabulary, qualitative/inequality dominance, sparse component cells/events, positivity failure, insufficient follow-up, temporal-split failure, intervals crossing the prespecified margin, or no reliable separation of observation from pathway outcomes. Null and adverse results do not prove no biological response.

The verifier can check source paths, joins, temporal leakage, episode construction, model fitting, locked predictions, competing-risk calculations, uncertainty, calibration, and whether each conclusion is linked to output fields. It cannot adjudicate TACE indication, clinical intent, technical completion, radiologic response, viable tumor, liver failure, death, survival, outside care, appropriateness, treatment benefit, or utility. Those claims require expert clinical adjudication and a separately linked/ prospective or external study with outcome ascertainment.
