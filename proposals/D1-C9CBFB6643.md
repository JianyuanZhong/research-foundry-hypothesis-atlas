# Opportunity-aware observation and a predeclared transport envelope for the frozen repeat-TACE HCC P-beyond-B experiment

## Targeted successor and unresolved claim

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not alter the frozen HCC repeat-TACE prognostic schema, the selected event, the assay-specific B/P/event/Y clocks, the complete-triplet rows, the nested M0/M1 models, the non-overlapping annual forward blocks, or the P-beyond-B estimand. It repairs the remaining weakness that an outcome-blind B/P risk set can still contain episodes with no practical opportunity for a post-event assay, while later calendar blocks are too sparse and too observation-selected to support transport.

The unresolved, falsifiable question remains narrowly prognostic:

> In a selected repeat-TACE episode with remote baseline B and contemporaneous pre-repeat P available, does adding P improve forward prediction of the next observed same-assay value Y beyond B and the locked decision-time core, and is that incremental prediction supported in a calendar block whose pre-Y covariate support and outcome-observation opportunity are adequate?

The strongest existing evidence is narrower: the predecessor's full-source audit supports favorable P-beyond-B point estimates in the 2022 block, whose outcome-blind B/P support and observed-Y fraction were adequate by the prespecified gates. It does not establish transport to 2023 or 2024. In 2023, only 9 systemic BP-eligible episodes existed and Y was observed in 29/43; in 2024, Y was observed in only 14/70 (1/9 systemic). The new experiment distinguishes absence of assay from absence of clinical/contact opportunity and makes the calendar target explicit rather than treating all later observed triplets as transport evidence.

This is a predictive association experiment, not a causal treatment comparison. The systemic-record label is a recorded-pathway covariate and composition diagnostic only. No claim about TACE response, toxicity, decompensation, survival, treatment effect, benefit, safety, utility, or actionability is permitted.

## Frozen clocks and estimand (unchanged)

For each selected repeat event and each assay:

- `event_time` is the selected repeat procedure `start time`.
- `B` is the latest valid uncensored assay on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1.
- `P` is the latest valid assay in the selected repeat encounter in `[event_time−72 hours,event_time)`.
- `Y` is the valid assay in that encounter in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, selected nearest +24 hours with inherited deterministic tie handling.
- Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`, positive P lead, and one patient-assay row. Event selection is never informed by laboratory values.

For fixed origins `2021-01-01`, `2022-01-01`, `2023-01-01`, and `2024-01-01`, training events satisfy `event_date < origin`; test events are `[origin, origin+365 days)`, right-open and non-overlapping. Keep the locked models and all training-only preprocessing:

```
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
```

Use training-fold age median and missingness indicator, sex coding, variance filtering, centering/scaling, inner leave-one-patient-out alpha selection over `[0.01,0.1,1,10,100]`, and ridge fitting exactly as inherited. The primary estimand remains assay-specific standardized held-out RMSE gain `[RMSE(M0)-RMSE(M1)]/SD_test(Y)` on complete triplets, with paired arm-stratified patient-frequency bootstrap intervals conditional on each fixed pre-origin fit. MAE, bias, arm errors, drift, and influence remain secondary. The observation/opportunity work below never reweights, imputes, augments, or removes rows from this estimator.

## New outcome-blind opportunity decomposition

For every reconstructed selected repeat event, construct the following risk-set fields before reading or using the assay-specific Y value. These fields use encounter dates, procedure time, and laboratory/contact presence only; they do not use Y's numeric value or select the analysis event.

1. `BP_eligible`: inherited valid B and P with strict `B_time < P_time < event_time` and positive P lead.
2. `encounter_coverage`: the selected event encounter has a parseable `Discharge time` at or after `event_time + 72h`. If discharge is missing or earlier, set false rather than imputing.
3. `post_event_contact`: at least one encounter for the same patient has a parseable start (`admission time`, fallback `visit time`) in `(event_time,event_time+72h]`, or the selected encounter itself covers the window. This is a contact indicator, not an outcome.
4. `any_lab_contact`: at least one valid-timestamp laboratory row of any assay in `(event_time,event_time+72h]`, linked to the selected event encounter or a same-patient encounter whose start is in that window. Do not require albumin or bilirubin and do not use its numeric result. This measures assay-system contact and remains a selection diagnostic.
5. `source_window_covered`: event time plus 72 hours is earlier than the maximum valid encounter/lab timestamp in the frozen snapshot. It is a dataset-boundary diagnostic, never a reason to rewrite the Y clock.
6. `Y_observed`: the inherited exact assay-specific Y exists under the frozen Y selector. `complete = BP_eligible & Y_observed` remains exactly the frozen analysis row definition.

Report an outcome-blind funnel by origin, assay, and arm: all selected events -> B -> P -> BP eligible -> encounter coverage -> post-event contact -> any-lab contact -> exact Y observed. Also report `Y_observed` within each opportunity stratum, with Wilson 95% intervals. The primary opportunity stratum is `BP_eligible & encounter_coverage`; `post_event_contact` and `any_lab_contact` are nested process descriptions, not substitutes for Y and not new estimands.

The opportunity diagnostic must preserve the order of operations: contact variables are frozen before assay-specific Y is inspected; no post-event assay value, diagnosis text, medication, or outcome enters event selection, risk-set membership, model fitting, or transport weights. If an event encounter lacks a parseable discharge, do not classify it as covered; report missingness separately.

## Calendar transport envelope and sparse-era rule

Retain the predecessor's pre-Y calendar-support diagnostics among BP-eligible rows: logistic calendar-membership model using only `B`, `P`, age, sex, P-lead hours, TACE2-to-repeat days, and prespecified arm; future propensity range versus training range; fraction outside range; normalized training transport ESS and arm-specific ESS; same-arm nearest-neighbor distance using the training leave-one-out 95th percentile. These are diagnostics only and never generate primary-analysis weights.

Add opportunity strata to the calendar ledger. For each block and arm report BP-eligible N, encounter-covered N, post-event-contact N, any-lab-contact N, exact-Y N, and the ratios covered/BP, contact/BP, any-lab/BP, and Y/covered. A block can support a transport interpretation only if, for **both assays**, all of the following were prespecified before inspecting RMSE results:

- outcome-blind BP support: at least 20 BP-eligible episodes total and at least 10 in each descriptive arm in both training and test;
- frozen complete-triplet support: at least 20 complete triplets total and at least 10 in each arm in both training and test;
- encounter coverage in the future test is at least 80% overall and at least 70% in each arm, with at least 10 covered episodes in each arm;
- exact Y observation among covered future BP-eligible episodes is at least 80% overall, and its Wilson interval is not clearly below the pre-origin covered rate;
- calendar transport ESS is at least 10 in each arm, no future propensity is outside the training range, and no more than 5% of same-arm future BP-eligible episodes exceed the training nearest-neighbor 95th percentile.

These are interpretation gates, not post hoc exclusions: all blocks and point estimates are still reported. If a block fails any gate, its RMSE is descriptive and cannot be pooled as evidence for calendar transport. The primary temporal claim is restricted to the set of blocks passing all gates; if only one block passes, report a cutoff-specific supportive result and explicitly declare multi-era transport untested/inconclusive. Never collapse 2023/2024 into a pooled later era to manufacture arm support.

For observed-Y selection, retain the predecessor training-only logistic observation model from pre-Y covariates, predicted observation range, Brier score, observed-case inverse-probability ESS, and observed-versus-missing pre-Y SMDs. The model is a warning diagnostic only: no MAR assumption, inverse-probability correction, imputation, or reweighted RMSE is allowed. Add the opportunity-conditioned selection contrast:

```
Y_rate_BP       = P(Y_observed=1 | BP_eligible)
Y_rate_covered  = P(Y_observed=1 | BP_eligible & encounter_coverage)
Y_rate_contact  = P(Y_observed=1 | BP_eligible & any_lab_contact)
```

A large increase from `Y_rate_BP` to `Y_rate_covered` localizes administrative/encounter follow-up as a major cause of missing Y; a persistently low `Y_rate_contact` indicates assay-specific missingness despite documented contact. Neither pattern establishes missing at random or recovers unobserved Y.

## Prespecified falsification and interpretation branches

**Supportive.** Reconstruction, duplicate and clock assertions pass; a future block passes every outcome-blind support, coverage, observation, and calendar gate for both assays; M1 improves standardized held-out RMSE over M0 with a paired interval above zero for at least one assay and no contradictory assay-specific result; and opportunity-conditioned selection does not show a material unexplained collapse. The conclusion is restricted to incremental short-horizon prediction in that supported selected calendar mixture. It is not a clinical decision rule or evidence of benefit.

**Adverse.** In a fully supported block, M1's interval is at or below zero for both assays, or repeated degradation occurs, or opportunity-conditioned Y observation is high but the P increment disappears. This challenges the incremental prognostic claim in the selected observed population; it does not prove B alone is sufficient, establish mechanism, or imply treatment ineffectiveness.

**Observation-selection adverse.** Coverage/contact is adequate but Y observation is low, materially lower than training, has low observed-case ESS, or observed-versus-missing pre-Y SMDs are large; or 2023/2024 remain low despite adequate contact. Then favorable complete-triplet RMSE is selection-entangled and cannot support transport. In particular, the inherited audit already indicates 2023 Y=29/43 (0.674; systemic 5/9) and 2024 Y=14/70 (0.200; systemic 1/9), so the expected result is a transport boundary rather than a positive later-era claim.

**Inconclusive/negative-feasibility boundary.** Any denominator, clock, source hash, or full-scan assertion fails; a block has fewer than the predeclared arm/coverage thresholds; discharge/contact fields are unparseable at a rate that prevents opportunity classification; units/reference ranges are absent; or later blocks fail gates. The appropriate result is that the dataset supports at most the specific supported cutoff and cannot test multi-era transport. Do not fit a constant-outcome model or infer clinical non-events from absent claims.

## What computation can and cannot establish

The computation can verify source hashes and paths; complete row scanning; literal HCC/TACE filters; composite keys and duplicates; the inherited 319/1,491, 159/526, and 40/118/41/119 denominators; exact B/P/event/Y clocks; non-overlapping boundaries; outcome-blind opportunity funnel; calendar overlap/ESS; training-only fitting; predictions; RMSE/MAE/bias; paired bootstrap; drift/influence; and selection diagnostics. It cannot verify laboratory units/reference ranges, severity, tumor burden, imaging response, repeat-TACE rationale, medication administration, transfusion/fluid exposure, external-care capture, mortality, clinical decompensation, causal effects, utility, actionability, or a safe decision threshold. These require adjudicated clinical data, validated assay metadata, reliable exposure and external-care capture, and independent temporal/external/prospective validation.

The parent audit is the relevant feasibility evidence: it used no sampling, passed inherited denominators/clocks, found 242 BP-eligible episodes per assay, and showed 2022 support but severe later Y-observation loss. This successor's contribution is not a new schema or a post hoc rescue; it is an executable distinction between opportunity failure and assay observation failure plus a frozen rule that converts sparse later eras into an explicit negative-feasibility/transport boundary.
