# Endpoint–direction discordance before ICU-to-ward transfer

Status: proposed substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. No outcome model has been fit, no discordance result is claimed, and no late-era outcome has been opened. This child preserves the frozen 49,157-transfer population, landmark, strict final-24-hour information set, raw 48-hour endpoint, structured first-transition adjudication, pooled and temporal transport analyses, source bindings, and test-opening order. It asks the narrower consequential question left unresolved by the parent: does trajectory history change risk specifically when the current endpoint state and recent direction tell different stories?

## Scientific opening, evidence boundary, and advance

The strongest inspected evidence supports three bounded premises. A longitudinal model using MIMIC-IV and an external hospital predicted seven-day ICU readmission/death better than SWIFT, but its daily averaging could miss short-term variability and the authors explicitly identified operational factors, treatment limitation, and distinct composite components as limitations [K1]. A systematic review found that longitudinal, routinely collected predictors often characterized higher-performing ICU-readmission models, while populations, windows, endpoints, and risk of bias varied [K2]. A large two-hospital EHR study showed that whether and when measurements occur can carry more prognostic information than their values because the record reflects care processes as well as patient state [K3].

Those findings do not establish the claim tested here. Neither the literature nor the parent's feasibility audits show whether a patient who looks reassuring now but has been worsening should be ranked above an equally reassuring patient who has been stable, or whether a patient who remains concerning but has been improving should be ranked below an equally concerning patient who has not improved. Apparent discordance could instead be current severity, intensified observation, delayed charting, destination/service selection, treatment response, measurement error, or the transfer decision itself.

The strongest claim currently supported is feasibility: the frozen cohort and strict approximate-era roles contain 2,607 raw adverse composites overall and 936, 436, 341, and 288 events in development, validation, locked 2017–2019 transport, and locked 2020–2022 stress roles. Decision-time leakage and administrative endpoint discordance are real and measurable. No predictor result, trajectory effect, mechanism, or monitoring benefit is supported yet.

**Frozen parent hypothesis.** Prespecified final-24-hour trajectory features improve raw 48-hour three-state prediction over the endpoint-state S0 model, with endpoint-adjudicated and transport corroboration.

**New falsifiable discordance hypothesis.** Conditional on the same S0 current endpoint state, support, transfer context, and observation-process features, recent measured-value direction changes calibrated R+D risk primarily when direction and current state disagree: “reassuring-now/worsening” should correct risk upward and “concerning-now/improving” should correct risk downward. A prespecified discordance model must improve prediction more in discordant than concordant patients in the pooled test and both locked late-era tests; the pattern must not be reproduced by observation-process inputs alone or disappear under the adjudicated R+D first-transition endpoint.

**Leading claim versus rivals.** The leading claim is a bounded prognostic one: final-24-hour direction contains patient-specific information not summarized by the current endpoint. Endpoint sufficiency predicts no reliable discordance-specific correction. Measurement-process artifact predicts equal or better correction from masks, counts, recency, caregiver count, and store lag without values. Transfer-selection artifact predicts confinement to destination/service/process strata or to valid-live-discharge prediction rather than R+D. Treatment response and measurement error can also create value direction and remain indistinguishable from physiology with these data.

**Clinical advance.** At a fixed ward-review capacity, this experiment tests whether history changes which apparently reassuring or concerning transfers should receive review—not merely whether a more complex model has a better average score. Support would justify external and prospective evaluation of a discordance-aware review rule. Adverse evidence would favor current endpoint state plus process context and discourage adding trajectory complexity for this decision. Neither result establishes that delaying transfer, adding monitoring, or retaining an ICU bed improves outcomes.

## Prespecified current state, recent direction, and discordance

Use the parent's twelve left-closed two-hour bins and fixed plausibility rules. Derive direction from values only after the same decision-time availability filter. Device mappings and preprocessing are frozen in development before any test outcome is opened.

For each of six domains define a continuous clinically anchored concern score `c_j`, with 1.0 corresponding approximately to the frozen abnormal boundary:

- HR: `abs(HR-80)/30`;
- BP: `max((110-SBP)/20, (75-MAP)/10, 0)`, using the parent's invasive-over-noninvasive bin rule;
- RR: `abs(RR-17)/7`;
- SpO2: `max((96-SpO2)/4,0)`;
- temperature after Fahrenheit conversion: `abs(tempC-37.15)/1.15`;
- oxygen support: maximum of `max((FiO2-0.21)/0.09,0)`, `flow_L_min/4`, and the development-frozen exact device-support score, where high-flow/reservoir-face-mask/noninvasive-positive-pressure/invasive-airway categories score at least 1 and missing documentation remains unknown.

These scores are analysis coordinates, not validated severity scales. Report their distributions and exact device map.

**Current endpoint state.** For each domain, use the last valid decision-time-available observation no more than four hours before `t0`. `current_concerning=1` when its concern score is at least 1; `current_reassuring=1` when below 1; otherwise current state is unknown. For BP and oxygen, combine the available component scores by their maximum. This current-state definition is never backfilled from after `t0`.

**Recent direction.** In `[t0-12h,t0)`, fit a Theil–Sen slope to bin-median concern scores only when at least four bins are observed and their centers span at least six hours. Let `d_j=6*slope_j`, the predicted six-hour change in concern units. Direction is worsening when `d_j>=0.25`, improving when `d_j<=-0.25`, and flat otherwise; it is unknown if support fails. The frozen robustness threshold is 0.50, and a sign-only analysis is descriptive. Neither threshold may be chosen from validation/test performance.

Per domain:

- reassuring-now/worsening (RW): current score <1 and worsening;
- concerning-now/improving (CI): current score >=1 and improving;
- concordant/flat: concerning+worsening, reassuring+improving, or flat;
- unknown: endpoint or direction unavailable.

For each transfer record `n_RW`, `n_CI`, `n_concordant`, and `n_unknown`, the signed net discordance `n_RW-n_CI`, and four mutually exclusive reporting strata: RW-only, CI-only, mixed RW+CI, and no discordant domain. “Direction-classifiable” requires at least three domains with known endpoint and direction. Less-supported transfers remain in every fitted model and overall estimand but are labeled indeterminate for subgroup interpretation. Formal subgroup claims require at least 500 transfers and 50 R+D events pooled and at least 300 transfers and 30 R+D events in each locked late era; otherwise the subgroup result is automatically inconclusive.

This definition is fixed before outcomes and uses no fitted risk, event, test residual, or post-transfer information. Discordance is an effect-modification target, not a cohort selected after observing model gains.

## Baseline and learned alternative at matched specificity

### D0: endpoint-state comparator

D0 is the parent's temporal S0-E (and pooled S0), refit unchanged. It contains age, sex, admission/ICU/service/destination context, ICU history/LOS, transfer time, last value and freshness, GCS, recent ventilation/vasopressor state, code status, and observation-process variables (masks, counts, recency, store lag, caregiver count). It is deliberately strong: the new comparison is beyond current endpoint severity and recorded care process.

### D1: simple prespecified discordance model

Fit one ridge multinomial model to all development patients on the frozen raw three-state target. D1 adds to D0 only:

- the six continuous `d_j` values with missing indicators;
- per-domain current-state × direction categorical interactions;
- `n_RW,n_CI,n_concordant,n_unknown`, signed net discordance, and RW×CI mixed flag.

No univariate screening, subgroup-specific fitting, or data-driven knot/threshold selection is permitted. Continuous D0 and direction terms use development-fitted scaling; ridge strength is selected by validation multinomial log loss. D1 therefore tests a small, inspectable set of endpoint-conditioned direction terms. It loses nonlinear cross-channel order, transient relapse, and interactions not represented by these summaries.

Fit matched D0-T/D1-T pooled multinomial six-hour first-transition hazards on the same static inputs and frozen R/D/L/W endpoint rules. D1-T asks whether any discordance gain concerns R+D rather than live-discharge selection; it does not replace the raw-target primary comparison.

### L2-D: endpoint-anchored learned value/process sequence alternative

Fit a compact two-stream causal TCN on the same 12 bins and raw three-state target. Its prediction is an additive residual to D0 logits, so the learned model must explain information beyond the current endpoint comparator:

- value stream: development-standardized observed and forward-filled values for the frozen HR, BP, RR, SpO2, temperature, FiO2, oxygen-flow, and device-support channels; no mask, gap, count, caregiver, or store-lag input;
- process stream: masks, time since last measurement, within-bin counts, nonnegative store-lag summaries, negative-lag flags, and caregiver counts; no measured value;
- endpoint/static anchor: frozen D0 inputs and D0 logits;
- encoders: separate two-layer causal convolutions, kernel 3, dilations 1 and 2, channels 32 or 64, dropout 0 or 0.2; concatenate encoded streams with the endpoint/static anchor before a three-class residual head;
- optimization: Adam learning rate 1e-3 or 3e-4, early stopping on validation log loss, five fixed seeds, equal-weight seed ensemble, and validation-only multinomial temperature scaling.

To avoid stacking leakage, use five-fold subject-grouped out-of-fold D0 logits for development examples; use the full-development D0 for validation and locked tests. All folds fit preprocessing independently inside the development role. Hyperparameters are chosen once in validation. Learned models are never selected by a pooled or late test.

Mandatory matched ablations:

1. **process+endpoint:** remove values; asks whether recorded observation process reproduces discordance correction.
2. **value+endpoint:** remove process stream; asks whether values add without timing/count information.
3. **endpoint-clamped detrended value:** within each channel estimate the decision-time Theil–Sen slope from its observed development-format bins and subtract the fitted linear trend while retaining the last observed value; refit the same value+endpoint model. This removes first-order direction while preserving endpoint, masks, and much of the value level/distribution.
4. **within-person order permutation:** at evaluation, keep the final bin fixed and apply 20 frozen shared permutations to the preceding 11 value bins across channels, leaving process and endpoint streams unchanged.
5. **patient-level value-sequence permutation:** permute value sequences within frozen destination family, out-of-fold D0 risk decile, and development-frozen density quintile; process and endpoint streams stay with the patient.
6. **full L2-D versus D1:** asks whether nonlinear order/cross-channel information changes the decision beyond the simple discordance contract.

The detrending and order ablations are perturbation evidence, not causal interventions; treatment responses and measurement error remain embedded in values. L2-D is retained only if calibrated, stable across seeds, and substantively improves discordant-patient prediction beyond D1 in both locked eras. Otherwise D1 is preferred. A latent physiological/observation-state model remains deferred because one site without continuous sensors, staffing/policy measures, or transfer-intent adjudication cannot identify the latent separation. Revisit it with an external environment or independent measurement process.

## Prespecified support, adverse evidence, and falsification

**Discordance-specific trajectory information supported:** in both locked late eras, D1 has acceptable overall calibration; `Delta_disc>0` with its 95% interval above zero; point `Delta_disc>=0.002`; `J>0` in both eras with at least the combined-late interval above zero; RW-only observed residual and D1 correction are positive while CI-only residual and correction are negative; and D1-T improves R+D IBS without the gain being confined to L. The pooled result must not be adverse. This supports endpoint-conditioned prognostic information, not mechanism.

**Measured-direction pattern supported, bounded:** discordance-specific support above; L2-D full exceeds process+endpoint in both late eras; full-versus-detrended and full-versus-order-permuted gains are positive in both with the combined-late interval above zero; and patient-level value permutation removes at least half of the full-versus-D0 point gain in each late era. This supports information in recorded value direction/order beyond recorded observation process. It does not establish physiology, untreated natural history, or benefit.

**Simple model adequate:** D1 satisfies discordance-specific support and L2-D's 95% upper bound excludes an additional 0.002 discordant Brier gain in both late eras, with no extra capture above 2 R+D events per 1,000 at every quota. Prefer D1.

**Endpoint sufficiency favored/adverse to the new hypothesis:** with required support and acceptable calibration, the 95% upper bound for `Delta_disc` is below 0.002 in both late eras, `J` is nonpositive, and RW/CI residuals do not have the prespecified opposite signs. This rules out the material discordance correction, not every trajectory association.

**Measurement-process rival favored:** process+endpoint matches or exceeds full L2-D while value+endpoint and D1 do not improve discordant prediction, or value detrending/permutation leaves the gain intact. This does not prove values contain no physiology.

**Transfer/endpoint-selection rival favored:** an apparent raw-target gain disappears or reverses for adjudicated R+D, is confined to L, or materially reverses across supported destination×density cells or locked eras. This blocks an unqualified review-allocation claim but does not identify the responsible operational mechanism.

**Adverse:** D1 or L2-D worsens discordant Brier or R+D IBS with an interval excluding zero, worsens event capture at fixed capacity, or endpoint sensitivities reverse the conclusion. Do not call improving patients safe or worsening patients unstable on this basis.

**Inconclusive:** discordance support gates fail; intervals span zero or materiality bounds; calibration fails; seed results are unstable; pooled and temporal results conflict; endpoint sensitivities disagree; or only one directional discordance group is supported. An imprecise null is not falsification. Mixed RW+CI patients remain descriptive unless separately supported.

No favorable subgroup, threshold, horizon, seed, ablation, or late-era result may rescue an adverse frozen primary result. All exact estimates and conclusion statuses must link to computed output paths.

## Leakage and post-selection traps

The verifier must fail the experiment for any of the following:

- using `charttime<t0` without `storetime<t0`, post-`t0` notes/measurements/treatments/services, or a future transfer to define a predictor;
- calculating direction from post-`t0` values, from a window wider than the frozen final 24 hours, or by backfilling an unknown endpoint;
- choosing concern-score anchors, direction thresholds, minimum domain support, discordance strata, or device mappings using pooled/test outcomes;
- defining discordance from D0/D1 residuals, predicted-risk disagreement, or outcome status rather than the frozen value rules;
- fitting only discordant patients, excluding indeterminate patients from the overall model, or conditioning the frozen cohort on trajectory availability;
- preprocessing across splits, patient leakage across folds/eras, in-sample D0 logits for learned development stacking, or calibrating on either late test;
- using outcome-informed risk deciles for permutation, selecting a favorable neural seed, opening 2020–2022 before freezing 2017–2019 outputs, or repeatedly peeking;
- interpreting adjustment, ablation, or transport as proof of physiology, treatment response, preventability, or monitoring benefit.

## Alternatives and revisit conditions

Selected: D1 plus endpoint-anchored two-stream L2-D because they answer the same endpoint–direction question at matched target, inputs, splits, calibration, and evaluation specificity; L2-D can reveal nonlinear relapse/cross-channel/order information D1 discards.

Deferred: an endpoint-only score with a post hoc discordance subgroup (selection bias); separate subgroup models (unstable and changes the estimand); a latent physiological-state model (unidentifiable here); dynamic post-transfer landmarks (use future information and define another decision); causal transfer-delay effects (no intervention/positivity/operational data).
