## Why this unresolved question matters

The clinical question remains whether, 24 hours before an adult's first eligible liver resection for retrospectively pathology-confirmed HCC, routinely stored CT/MRI report semantics add clinically material information to a laboratory-independent flag for multidisciplinary review of pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (M2). Such a flag is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant/adjuvant therapy, systemic or locoregional treatment, or surveillance.

The strongest claim supported now is limited. Direct schema, header, and aggregate source inspection establishes operation labels, start/end strings, a two-valued procedure-source field, examination accession IDs and acquisition strings, eventual report bodies, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. It does not establish that two procedure rows are the same clinical operation, that a case-record midnight value is an actual incision time, report final/release/view time, report version history, pathology specimen linkage or sampling adequacy, recurrence, survival, clinician action, treatment benefit, harms, or costs.

A full exact-row-deduplicated procedure scan contained 336,907 rows. A deliberately broad, non-final feasibility proxy (label contains 肝 and 切除 with stated organ/transplant exclusions) found 60,125 rows, 26,544 patients, 29,995 encounters, and 1,749 raw labels. Of 27,805 proxy rows from `病案手术`, 23,537 had midnight starts; only 8 of 32,320 `手麻系统` proxy rows did. There were 27,203 proxy encounters represented in both sources, 26,991 same-encounter calendar days represented in both, and 13,208 same-exact-label/day cross-source groups. In 26,995 encounters the nearest cross-source starts were within 24 hours. These aggregates establish material source-clock multiplicity; they do not prove row identity or clock validity. The proxy never defines the analytic cohort.

The inspected full XML of Li et al. (2025; DOI `10.1111/jcmm.70746`, PMCID `PMC12309289`; frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) supports that MVI is pathology-determined, prognostically relevant, and unavailable directly for preoperative planning, while management implications remain controversial. The inspected full HTML of Huang et al. It does not validate M2, routine report text, this procedure-clock reconstruction, report availability, this institution, or treatment benefit.

## Accession-first reports and clock-dependent states

## Models, estimands, and robust fitting

B uses age, sex, acquisition indicators, development-frequency machine category, and—only for exposed accessions—frozen lesion diameter, lesion-count category, and explicit macrovascular thrombus with unknown indicators. S is exactly B plus present/absent/unknown capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity. S0 is never refit: use S coefficients, preprocessing, penalty and recalibration while replacing only the semantic block by its frozen state-zero vector. Assert bitwise `S=S0` whenever semantics are zero. W and A=W plus the identical semantic block remain secondary workflow diagnostics; unrestricted text is a ceiling diagnostic.

Development, tuning, recalibration, bootstrap, and null refits minimize patient-wise worst-case label/clock/visibility logistic loss:
`sum_i max_{y in Y_i,(k,r) in Gamma_i} [log(1+exp(z_theta(x_i(k,r))))-y*z_theta(x_i(k,r))] + elastic-net penalty`.
Rebuild preprocessing and optimization in every resample.

At threshold p, `w=p/(1-p)`, `I_Mi(k,r,p)=1[score_Mi(k,r)>=p]`, and for j=(M1,M0):
`d_i^j(y,k,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)]*[I_M1i(k,r,p)-I_M0i(k,r,p)]`.
Primary contrasts are D=(S,S0) and C=(S,B); S versus flag-all and flag-none are required. Every sum divides by whole identifiable-frame N.

## Supportive, adverse, mixed, and inconclusive results

- **Supportive P-Clock:** all gates pass; all-stored simultaneous lower limits for D/C and both default contrasts exceed .01; threshold-plateau and semantic-null gates pass; and neither report-grain nor procedure-clock sensitivity is material. Conclude only that complete eventual report semantics made materially favorable actions within frozen S and complete S beat B in this historical identifiable documentary-M2 frame under all feasible operation clocks.
- **Supportive T-Clock without audit:** P-Clock passes and the exact support set is nonempty with invariant, adequately sized patterns. Conclude only that reported access vectors would suffice under adversarial operative-clock, visibility, and documentary-outcome assignment. Actual decision-time availability remains unresolved.
- **Adverse:** with feasibility gates passed, a simultaneous upper limit for a required D/C margin is <=0.01; the support set is empty; or a naive midnight/raw-row analysis is favorable while the source-reconciled clock-robust analysis has an upper limit <=0.01. The last result specifically falsifies value robust to operative-clock provenance. A negative upper limit <=0 indicates evidence of harmful action changes, not merely absence of material benefit.
- **Mixed:** D supportive/C adverse means semantics alter actions favorably within fitted S but S does not beat optimized B. C supportive/D adverse does not attribute value to runtime semantics. P supportive/T inconclusive means stored-content value may exist but workflow transport is unresolved.
- **Inconclusive:** intervals cross a decision boundary; null/default tests fail without an adverse confidence bound; any event, frame, clock, linkage, pattern, pathology, solver, resampling, extraction, or sensitivity gate fails; or exact-version audit is absent for a decision-time claim. Favorable AUC, a point estimate, unrestricted text, or a naive exact-midnight analysis cannot rescue the claim.
