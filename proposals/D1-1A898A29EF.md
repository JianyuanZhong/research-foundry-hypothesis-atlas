# Episode 12: fully observed alive-discharge endpoint for the trajectory-calibrated post-shock IV-loop target trial

**Parent:** `[prior hypothesis]`

## Substantive repair and endpoint choice

This successor preserves the parent's namespaced subject partition, population, ICU-hour-48 landmark, two-clock event/measurement contract, unit-safe fluid phenotype and weight hierarchy, actually delivered 24-hour loop strategies, clone-censor analysis, measured-overlap and trajectory diagnostics, recorded mortality and observed-RRT safety gates, exact MIMIC bindings, and interpretation limits. It changes the primary endpoint because ALDL7 is often only interval identified after ICU or hospital departure.

The primary endpoint is **alive index-hospital discharge by day 28 after t0 (AIHD28)**. It is preferable to "alive ICU discharge without readmission by day 7": ICU discharge is strongly affected by local bed and transfer practice, `icustays.outtime` is missing in 14 of 94,458 source ICU rows, and MIMIC cannot verify no ICU readmission after index-hospital discharge or at another hospital. A hierarchy is retained, but not as interchangeable co-primary outcomes:

1. AIHD28 is the fully classified primary patient-centered index-hospital outcome.
2. Inherited ALDL7 lower/upper bounds are a key respiratory consistency endpoint.
3. Recorded day-28 death and observed new day-7 RRT remain non-negotiable co-safety gates.
4. Alive ICU discharge with no *observed same-admission* ICU return before the earlier of day 7 or index-hospital discharge is a descriptive process outcome only. It is never called "no readmission by day 7."

AIHD28 removes observation-driven unknown classes, but it is not 28-day survival, functional recovery, home discharge, freedom from rehospitalization, or proof of respiratory recovery. Hospitalization beyond day 28 is an observed failure to achieve this endpoint, not presumed death. An alive index discharge remains a success even if a later recorded death exists; the latter is reported separately as safety evidence. No absence of a post-discharge death or readmission record is interpreted as survival or freedom from readmission.

## Evidence boundary, unresolved claim, and advance

The strongest currently available intervention evidence supports only that protocolized conservative fluid management with active deresuscitation can remove fluid without a statistically detectable worsening of lactate or the measured kidney-injury markers in a modest randomized secondary analysis. McMullan et al., *Critical Care Explorations* 2026;8:e1404, DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404`, inspected frozen full-text source `[source checksum]`, reports no detectable between-group differences in lactate, AKIRisk, or urinary cystatin-C and explicitly notes modest sample size and imprecision. This does not establish clinical benefit, renal safety, or the effect of initiating one loop diuretic after shock.

A 2026 review of early ARDS trial endpoints (Boyle et al., *Am J Respir Crit Care Med*, DOI `10.1093/ajrccm/aamag238`, inspected frozen full text `[source checksum]`) supports the general methodological point that ICU discharge and death are competing events that can make organ endpoints nonrandomly missing, and that discharged-alive status is a distinct clinical state. It does not validate AIHD28 as a surrogate for survival, function, or patient preference.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among invasively ventilated adults at ICU hour 48 who have recovered from vasopressor-treated shock, have unit-safe cumulative balance at least +50 mL/kg, retained urine output and potassium at least 3.0 mmol/L, and fall within prespecified measured treatment overlap, a sustained strategy of initiating an actually delivered ICU IV loop within 24 hours increases the probability of alive index-hospital discharge by day 28 by more than 5 percentage points versus no loop by any route during that window, without a definitively adverse ALDL7 signal or excess recorded day-28 death or observed new day-7 RRT.

The advance is resolution of a consequential initiation decision using an endpoint fully classifiable within the index admission while preserving respiratory partial-identification bounds rather than silently treating discharge as durable liberation. A supportive result would prioritize external validation and a randomized trial; it would not establish exchangeability, causal truth, or a treatment recommendation.

The endpoints were not interchangeable: 198 of 674 definite ALDL7 successes lacked AIHD28, while 1,926 of 3,872 definite non-liberation failures nevertheless achieved AIHD28. ### Frozen partition, unit, and time

`SHA256(ASCII("ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1") + NUL + ASCII("mimic") + NUL + ASCII(canonical base-10 subject_id)) mod 100`.

Set `t0 = icu/icustays.intime + 48 hours`. The treatment window is `(t0,t0+24h]`; AIHD28 ends at `t0+28d`; ALDL7 and RRT end at `t0+7d`.

### Population

Require a linked ICU interval containing t0, age >=18, and positive-duration invasive ventilation item 225792 with `starttime <= t0 < endtime`. Primary accepted statuses are `FinishedRunning` and `Stopped`; `Paused` is a prespecified uncertainty sensitivity.

Require at least one positive delivered vasopressor segment before `t0-6h` and no segment overlapping `(t0-6h,t0]`; median MAP >=65 mmHg in `(t0-3h,t0]`; unit-safe cumulative balance >=+50 mL/kg from ICU admission through t0; valid weight 30–300 kg; urine output >0.1 mL/kg/h in `(t0-6h,t0]`; and latest decision-available potassium item 50971 or 52610 in `(t0-12h,t0]`, numeric mmol/L >=3.0. Exclude active ECMO or delivered RRT at t0 and delivered loop by any route in `(t0-12h,t0]`. Earlier loops remain allowed; never-loop-since-ICU is sensitivity.

Vasopressor itemids are epinephrine 221289/229617, dopamine 221662, phenylephrine 221749/229630/229631/229632, norepinephrine 221906, and vasopressin 222315. MAP itemids are 220052/220181/225312. Match ICU joins on `subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id` and hospital joins on `subject_id,hadm_id`.

### Two-clock contract

Partition every source row before constructing risk sets. Retrospectively ascertained events/states are delivered `icu/inputevents` drug/pressor segments and `icu/procedureevents` ventilation, airway, ECMO, and RRT segments. Clinical `starttime,endtime` establish what occurred; `storetime` is provenance, not an eligibility exclusion. At boundary tau, use only clinical-time content through tau. Future endtime, final status, total amount, or post-tau segment content cannot become a baseline/hourly covariate. For crossing segments, use positive rate and elapsed duration only; never prorate a future final total. Boluses occur at starttime.

Decision-available measurements in `chartevents,outputevents,labevents,emar`, and radiology require clinical/chart time <=tau and nonmissing `storetime <=tau`. Radiology detail inherits both clocks from its parent. Date-only ICD `chartdate` cannot enter real-time eligibility or weights. Emit clinical-only rows, decision-available rows, late-entry exclusions, and retrospective event classifications by source and arm. Material arm-differential missingness or reversal under charttime-only sensitivity blocks causal interpretation.

### Unit-safe balance and weight

For input volume, use delivered `icu/inputevents` rows with valid keys/times/status and positive `amount` only when normalized `amountuom` is mL, or L converted x1000. For infusions crossing t0, use positive mL/hour rate times elapsed hours through t0. For boluses ending by t0, use positive mL amount at starttime. For output, use positive numeric `icu/outputevents.value` in mL (or L x1000), `charttime in [intime,t0]`, and `storetime <=t0`. Add 227488 to input only when positive mL and decision-available. Do not import dialysis outputs from chartevents. Require at least one input and output row and no six-hour recording gap before t0. Complete-hour coverage and +30/+100 mL/kg thresholds are sensitivities.

### Delivered strategies and estimand

Strategy A is first qualifying actually delivered ICU IV furosemide 221794/228340 or bumetanide 229639 in `(t0,t0+24h]`. Strategy B is no actual furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide, or ethacrynic acid by any route in that interval. ICU delivery requires valid start/end/store times, accepted delivered status, and positive dose convertible to mg or positive mg/hour over positive duration. Exclude canceled, rewritten, not-started, order-only, nonpositive, and unknown-unit rows. Deduplicate only overlapping same `orderid/linkorderid` segments.

Join `hosp/emar_detail` to `hosp/emar` on `subject_id,emar_id,emar_seq`; inherit `hadm_id,charttime,storetime`; accept actual-administration text plus positive dose/product amount. Blank route is unknown, not IV. Same-drug ICU/eMAR records within 15 minutes are concordant, not duplicate doses. Report concordant-ICU and explicit-IV-eMAR sensitivities.

Clone each eligible subject to both strategies at t0 and censor each clone at first deviation through hour 24. Death in-window remains failure. ICU discharge before A initiation censors A; alive hospital discharge ending medication observation censors B according to the inherited contract. For the new terminal endpoint, if AIHD28 and censoring share an exact timestamp, endpoint occurs first only when the source explicitly records alive hospital discharge; otherwise censor first. Report this rare ordering explicitly. Post-hour-24 treatment is unrestricted. Estimate per-protocol effects with inverse-probability adherence/censor weights; crude initiator comparisons are descriptive.

The primary estimand is
`ψ = Pr(AIHD28^A=1 | measured overlap) - Pr(AIHD28^B=1 | measured overlap)`,
the 24-hour sustained-strategy per-protocol risk difference among subjects whose baseline probability of A conditional on pre-t0 covariates/trajectories is in [0.10,0.90]. This is a counterfactual estimand only under consistency, positivity, correctly modeled censoring, and no unmeasured confounding; MIMIC cannot verify the last condition. The all-eligible intention-to-treat-style association is secondary.

### Trajectory, overlap, and analysis

The t0 propensity model uses only decision-available pre-t0 demographics; admission type/year/unit/service; prior completed-admission comorbidity and loop exposure; weight; cumulative balance and six-hour slope; urine, creatinine, lactate, potassium and slopes; MAP, pressor presence/dose and time since cessation; FiO2/PEEP/support; sedation; crystalloid/albumin; prior oxygenation; and validated timely radiology flags. Summarize at t0 and 3/6/12-hour lookbacks with missingness indicators. Never use post-treatment response, index-discharge diagnoses, future notes, or endpoint components.

Report calibration, overlap histograms, positivity by trajectory stratum, standardized differences, weight tails, adherence, and ESS. Use overlap weights for baseline treatment and stabilized hourly inverse-probability adherence/censor weights truncated at 1st/99th percentiles. Primary analysis is a doubly robust weighted estimator of arm-specific AIHD28 risks and RD with at least 500 subject bootstraps. A weighted Aalen–Johansen display treats alive discharge as the event and in-hospital death before discharge as competing; its day-28 cumulative incidence must reconcile with the corresponding unaugmented weighted binary risk (the augmented primary estimate need not be numerically identical), with subjects still hospitalized counted as no success. Unweighted and covariate-adjusted models are checks.

Hourly censor models update only lagged decision-available MAP/pressor, urine/balance, creatinine/lactate/potassium, FiO2/PEEP/support, sedation, crystalloid/albumin, and imaging. Do not adjust a treatment-affected intermediate as a baseline confounder.

Repeat trajectory-restricted estimates across prespecified respiratory, renal, and hemodynamic strata only where overlap/ESS pass. Report a quantitative residual-bias grid: minimum prevalence-difference/odds-ratio strength of one unmeasured binary readiness factor related to both initiation and AIHD28 needed to move the RD to 0 and +0.05. Prespecified pre-t0 slopes are negative-control outcomes.

Treatment probability <0.01 or >0.99 in >10% of person-hours, fewer than 100 adherent subjects in an arm, ESS <75 in either arm, or a large fraction outside overlap makes the per-protocol decision claim infeasible/inconclusive.

### Benefit-harm display

### Falsification and interpretation branches

Before outcomes, freeze and emit attrition, strategies, grace/deviation events, units/status/route exclusions, late entries, overlap, weights, ESS, and negative controls. Eventual A/B must not predict pre-t0 six-hour creatinine, urine, or MAP slopes after trajectory adjustment; standardized difference >0.10 or multiplicity-controlled P<0.05 blocks causal interpretation. Prespecified trajectory covariates must have weighted standardized differences <=0.10. Expected 24-hour balance/urine direction confirms delivery only, not benefit. Material reversal across temporal, trajectory, fluid, endpoint, destination, exposure, or overlap sensitivities is inconclusive.

**Supportive:** primary overlap/adherent AIHD28 RD point estimate >+0.05 with 95% lower limit >0; ALDL7 is not definitively adverse (95% upper limit of `RD_U` >0); upper 95% excess recorded day-28 death <+0.03; upper 95% excess observed RRT <+0.05 under both date brackets; process, positivity, ESS, balance, temporal, missingness, residual-bias, and sensitivity gates pass. This supports external validation and a randomized trial only.

**Adverse:** the 95% upper limit of AIHD28 RD is <=0, or the 95% upper limit of ALDL7 `RD_U` is <=0, or the lower bound of recorded mortality harm is >=+0.03, or the lower bound of observed RRT harm is >=+0.05. Better fluid balance cannot override these outcomes.

**Inconclusive:** AIHD28 fails support but still permits >+0.05 benefit; safety upper gates are not cleared without definitive harm; ALDL7 conflicts without meeting its adverse rule; overlap/adherence/ESS is sparse; modest residual confounding can reverse the estimate; falsification fails; temporal contradictions or missingness are differential; date brackets disagree; or prespecified analyses conflict. Nonsignificance is not equivalence or safety. AIHD28 support with a definitively adverse ALDL7 result is adverse, not a discharge-policy recommendation.
