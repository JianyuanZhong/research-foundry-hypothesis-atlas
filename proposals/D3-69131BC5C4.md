## Falsifiable test and interpretation

Restrict primary contrasts to `treatment=none`: positive-macrophage versus FLS-only and positive-macrophage versus macrophage-negative, within matched FLS donor contexts where available. Estimate feature-level log-expression contrasts with a small count offset, using donor-aware paired/stratified contrasts or a contributing-donor random-effects model and donor/condition-preserving permutation uncertainty. Mixed conditions are a secondary composition check; LPS and inhibitor conditions are mechanistic context, not independent causal replicates.

Supportive evidence would be a reproducible condition-specific signature with donor-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable negative-control signal. Adverse evidence would be no separation, reversal, or a signal confined to one donor/feature after adjustment. Unresolved donor mapping, insufficient independent donor contexts, low detectability, or uncertainty too large to distinguish communication from composition or technical artifacts would be inconclusive.

The baseline is a transparent donor-aware contrast with donor-level uncertainty. A substantive alternative is a hierarchical/regularized model across the 445 measured features that could reveal coordinated pathway structure lost by single-feature contrasts; it is deferred unless metadata integrity and signal support it. Both use identical inputs, contrasts, donor-stratified split/permutation, outcome, and uncertainty evaluation. Neither establishes direct mechanism or clinical effect.
