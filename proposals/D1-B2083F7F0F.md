# Value-aware independent corroboration audit of RT Vent transitions

## Substantive child and unresolved claim

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the inherited all-stay event cohort, 30-minute same-direction deduplication, 6-hour half-open pre/post windows, >=2 setting-family signal, complete-window and conflict-clean sensitivity, shifted/Continued controls, hospital heterogeneity, and the strict semantic boundary that structured concordance cannot prove active invasive ventilation, intubation, extubation, liberation, continuity, causality, or benefit.

The parent established a large bidirectional discontinuity: the setting-family signal rose after `RT Vent On/Off=Start` and fell after `Off`/`Suspended`, including strict-window and hospital-clustered sensitivities. Its main unresolved limitation was provenance: the RT transition and settings might be generated by the same respiratory documentation workflow. The new falsifiable question is narrower:

> Among the inherited all-stay Start and Off/Suspended episodes, do contemporaneous value-bearing respiratoryCare airway/interval observations occur in the expected temporal direction and receive independent corroboration from treatment pathways or note text, while the inherited >=2 setting-family rise/fall persists in corroborated, unsupported, and ambiguous strata?

Support would require temporally ordered airway/device evidence plus a nonzero, reproducible second-source corroboration rate, expected setting direction within corroborated strata, and no dependence on one hospital or one semantic subtype. An adverse result is absence of independent second-source corroboration, substantial component discordance, or reversal/attenuation of the setting contrast in the value-aware strata. Inconclusive results apply if interval sentinels, sparse notes/treatments, or unresolved airway semantics prevent a meaningful gold-standard comparison.

## Population and temporal rules

The primary population is the parent's independently defined 4,103 deduplicated episodes: 2,720 Start and 1,383 Off/Suspended, from 4,120 raw qualifying transition rows in 12 hospitals. Same-direction records within 30 minutes are represented by the earliest record. No care-plan label or frozen 3,788-row landmark is used to define this cohort. For every event time `T`, source rows are assigned to `[T-360,T)` or `[T,T+360)`; no carry-forward is permitted.

For each event, compute the inherited setting positivity (`>=2` distinct families) and each corroborator separately. Define a value-aware event state rather than a single ventilation label:

* **Airway/device-supported:** contemporaneous `oral ett` or `tracheostomy`, or a nonzero interval whose `ventstartoffset`/`ventendoffset` is coherent at the event time; explicit `no artificial airway` is negative evidence.
* **Treatment-supported:** directional treatment pathway in the corresponding window.
* **Note-supported:** directional note marker in the corresponding window.
* **Independent corroborated:** at least two of the three source domains in the expected direction, with domain component flags retained. One domain alone is not called independent corroboration.
* **Unsupported:** no expected-direction domain in the post-window.
* **Ambiguous:** exactly one expected-direction domain without a qualifying conflict.
* **Conflicting:** an opposite-direction domain in the post-window; report rather than silently resolve.

Report Start and literal Off separately from Suspended, and report Off/Suspended jointly only as inherited. For each stratum, estimate setting pre/post rates and paired changes, component discordance, treatment/note/airway availability, and hospital-specific rates. Preserve the inherited complete-window/conflict-clean, fixed-bin, Continued, shifted-sham, opposite-transition, and clustered uncertainty analyses. The key added estimand is whether the inherited setting change remains when restricted to independent corroboration, and whether unsupported/ambiguous episodes show the same change.

## Executed full-source result

The executable script is `[internal dataset path]`; output is `[internal dataset path]`. It scanned all 20,168,176 respiratoryCharting rows, 865,381 respiratoryCare rows, all treatment rows, and all 2,254,179 note rows. The derived result was executed after correcting a syntax error before the successful run; the source files remained read-only.

The full-source scan reproduced 4,120 raw and 4,103 deduplicated transition episodes. In the executed value-aware audit, post-window respiratoryCare evidence was present for 89.52% of Start episodes and 66.52% of Off/Suspended episodes, but it was not independently corroborated by a directional treatment or note marker in the same ±6-hour event neighborhood: the computed independent-post rate was 0% for both transition directions. Thus the added joint corroboration gate fails, not because the parent temporal signal disappears, but because the available second-source evidence is temporally sparse or non-overlapping. Most episodes were therefore ambiguous under the strict two-domain rule (89.52% of Start and 66.52% of Off/Suspended); unsupported post evidence was 10.48% and 33.48%, respectively. In the complete-window/conflict-clean sensitivity, independent corroboration remained 0% in 2,503 Start and 1,189 Off/Suspended episodes.

The inherited setting signal was still directional in this implementation: 6.03% pre versus 65.40% post for Start, and 37.53% pre versus 1.52% post for Off/Suspended. The strict complete/conflict-clean values were 5.87% to 63.88% for Start and 37.93% to 1.43% for Off/Suspended. These are source-level temporal associations, not clinical-state estimates. Hospital heterogeneity remained material: 11 hospitals contributed at least 10 strict Start episodes and 8 at least 10 strict Off episodes; Start post-setting positivity ranged 47.2%–76.1% and Off post-setting positivity ranged 0%–14.3% among these hospitals. No hospital supplied a nonzero independent-post rate under the two-domain definition.

The source profile also exposed a semantic limitation requiring retention: respiratoryCare has 61,942 `oral ett` and 8,468 `tracheostomy` values, but 24,085 `no artificial airway` values; interval fields are overwhelmingly sentinel-like (`ventendoffset=0` in 865,224/865,381 rows, `ventstartoffset=0` in 685,205/865,381 rows). Therefore interval evidence cannot be treated as a validated continuous device interval. Treatment has recognizable pathways (including 117,481 mechanical-ventilation rows, 11,411 endotracheal-tube insertion rows, 8,760 removal rows, and 19,578 ventilator-weaning rows), and notes contain explicit marker text, but neither domain co-occurred directionally within the event neighborhoods in this execution.

## Interpretation and falsification

The strongest supported child claim is adverse/inconclusive to the proposed independent-corroboration upgrade: the inherited setting rise/fall persists as a structured RT/setting association, but the available respiratoryCare airway/interval evidence is not independently joined by treatment or note evidence at the same transition times. The evidence is consistent with a common RT documentation workflow, sparse asynchronous treatment recording, stale or sentinel interval fields, or all three. The result does not refute the parent temporal concordance; it prevents that concordance from being called value-aware independent triangulation.

Supportive criteria would require nonzero two-domain corroboration, expected Start/Off ordering in that subset, persistence across strict and hospital-restricted analyses, and lower discordance than unsupported episodes. Adverse criteria are met by the zero independent gate and the substantial hospital range. Inconclusive criteria remain met for clinical semantics because no available structured field establishes bedside device connectivity or actual intubation/extubation. A future adjudication sample should oversample the discordant and apparently unsupported episodes, link all source rows with exact offsets, and estimate PPV/NPV, sensitivity/specificity, and inter-rater agreement against ventilator/device logs and expert chart review.

## Explicit limits

Computationally checkable claims are source counts/hashes, field availability, event deduplication, window assignment, value mappings, setting-family rates, component discordance, hospital coverage, and the zero independent-corroboration result. The dataset cannot automatically establish that `Start` means active invasive ventilation, that `Off` or `Suspended` means extubation or cessation, that an airway lexical value is current rather than copied, that a nonzero interval is device-connected, or that a setting change improves care. Clinical adjudication, device logs, waveform/audit trails, and exact tube insertion/removal timing are required for those claims.
