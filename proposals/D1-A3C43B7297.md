### Panel matching and actionability truth table

Each panel record has `packet_id`, exact `unit_ref` or `NO_UNIT_CLAIM`, nine-field assertion `(target_cluster,action_modality,due_interval,polarity,recipient,responsibility,replacement,conflict)` plus `reader_state`, eight workflow codes, and packet hash. A different admission is `CROSS_ADMISSION_NONMATCH`; malformed, duplicate, out-of-domain, or missing required fields is `MALFORMED_MATCH`/`NONRESPONSE`; no panel may create a source unit.

For the six target-defining fields, identical coded values are `MATCH`; `ABSENT` versus non-`ABSENT` is `NONMATCH`; distinct known codes are `NONMATCH`; `UNKNOWN` against any admissible value retains both `MATCH` and `NONMATCH` branches tagged `UNKNOWN_WILDCARD`. Replacement and conflict are never erased. `DUPLICATE_RESTATEMENT` requires identical `U` and complete assertion tuple; equal text at a different note, chain, or ordinal is `DISTINCT_UNIT`. The matcher returns an ordered set of states, never a vote.

### Route and compatible-set truth tables

The parent route is recomputed from sealed atomic fields, not accepted from a panel. `m=0` yields descriptive `NOACTION`. Any unknown unit, boundary, nonresponse, unresolved timing, or incompatible mapping yields `DEFER` or the inherited adverse compatible route set. Any replacement/conflict or `C_I_text` yields `RECON`; late active units yield `TIMING`; only all known, nonconflicting, active units timely at the inherited clock can yield `AUTO`. A minority adverse unit controls the admission.

For each admission, `Omega_i` is the Cartesian product of parent lanes, H1/H2/H3, bridge states, and permitted shared-error flags. Every observed nonmissing field is retained exactly; an error flag may be shared by any subset, including all panels; no value is deleted or imputed. `UNKNOWN_WILDCARD`, `AMBIGUOUS_MATCH`, `NONRESPONSE`, workflow unavailability, H3 reproducibility failure, and `U_BOUNDARY` remain in `Omega_i`. Empty `Omega_i` emits `OMEGA_EMPTY`, fails closed, and is never replaced by consensus. Joint numerator/denominator contributions are optimized jointly with exact rational arithmetic; zero feasible denominator is `UNDEFINED`, not zero.

## Canonical ledger: one source of truth for burden and eligibility

### Ledger identity and row schema

The ledger is a JSONL table with one canonical row per event. It is immutable after seal except for appending actual execution fields; no row is deleted or rewritten to improve a result. The unique ledger key is

```text
ledger_id = SHA256(protocol_hash || admission_key || unit_ref || activity_id || reader_id || event_ordinal)
```

where absent components are explicit serialized `null`, not empty strings. `admission_key=(subject_id,hadm_id)`, `unit_ref=U` or `null`, and `reader_id` is `SOURCE`, `H1`, `H2`, `H3`, `BRIDGE`, `QA`, or `SYSTEM`.

Every row contains: `ledger_id`, `protocol_hash`, `source_snapshot`, `admission_key`, `unit_ref`, `activity_id`, `reader_id`, `event_ordinal`, `required_state`, `planned_minutes`, `planned_physician_hours`, `actual_start`, `actual_end`, `actual_minutes`, `actual_physician_hours`, `row_status`, `reason_code`, `input_manifest_hash`, `output_manifest_hash`, `admission_charge_flag`, `challenge_charge_flag`, and `sha256`. `row_status` is one of `PLANNED`, `COMPLETE`, `NOT_REQUIRED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED_BEFORE_START`; no unlisted status is accepted.

A charge is counted exactly once if `admission_charge_flag=1` or `challenge_charge_flag=1` for its declared activity. Repeated units and repeated readers do not increment the distinct-admission count. A task serving both parent and bridge is one processing event with one charge; separate personnel executions must have separate activity IDs and each must lie within its corresponding predeclared suballocation. No hidden retry, top-up, or post-label activity is allowed.

The following is the only binding workload contract. `A` is the union of distinct sampled admissions; `C` is the pre-label challenge subset, `C<=300`, `C<=floor(A/3)`, and `A<=900`. The bridge is carved inside these rows, never added.

|---|---|---:|---:|
| `SETUP_SEAL_CLOSE` | once per run | 120 total | 3,600 total |
| `ELIGIBILITY_RETRIEVAL` | every sampled admission | 0.30 | 10 |
| `INDEX_CONTEXT_PACKET` | every sampled admission | 0.45 | 20 |
| `INDEPENDENT_AUDIT` | every sampled admission | 0.50 | 10 |
| `REDACTION_MINOR_UNIT_QA` | every sampled admission | 0.45 | 5 |
| `BASE_RECONCILIATION` | every sampled admission | 0.30 | 5 |

Thus base charge is `2.00` hours and `50` packet minutes per sampled admission; challenge increment is `1.50` hours and `50` packet minutes per challenged admission. The exact planned ceilings are

```text
A = |distinct admission_key with required base activity|
C = |distinct admission_key with required challenge activity|
physician_hours_plan = 120 + 2.00*A + 1.50*C
packet_minutes_plan   = 3600 + 50*A + 50*C
physician_hours_plan <= 5070 when A<=900,C<=300
packet_minutes_plan   <= 63600 when A<=900,C<=300
```

The arithmetic `120+2*900+1.5*300=5070` and `3600+50*900+50*300=63600` is a mandatory fixture. A conflicting legacy display such as `2,370` or `84,000` is not a second budget and must be rejected as a ledger/schema inconsistency. Actual charges must satisfy, row by row and in aggregate,

```text
actual_physician_hours <= physician_hours_plan
actual_packet_minutes   <= packet_minutes_plan
actual_minutes <= planned_minutes for each activity row
actual_minutes is null only for PLANNED or NOT_REQUIRED
```

A row exceeding its own maximum, an uncharged required event, duplicate charge key, activity outside this table, challenge work without `challenge_charge_flag=1`, `A>900`, `C>300`, or arithmetic disagreement yields `LEDGER_FAIL` and `NO_SELECTION` for the run. If a required task cannot be completed within its row maximum, it is charged for work actually done and coded `INCONCLUSIVE` or `FAILED`; it is not retried or silently omitted.

The bridge suballocation is a ceiling within the parent rows: structural bridge audit is at most `0.05 h/2 min` inside `ELIGIBILITY_RETRIEVAL`; target envelope and packet assembly at most `0.15 h/5 min` inside `INDEX_CONTEXT_PACKET`; bridge state coding at most `0.10 h/3 min` inside `INDEPENDENT_AUDIT`; bridge key/match QA at most `0.10 h/2 min` inside `REDACTION_MINOR_UNIT_QA`; bridge hash reconciliation at most `0.05 h/1 min` inside `BASE_RECONCILIATION`. Challenge bridge coding is at most `0.10 h/3 min`, `0.10 h/3 min`, `0.05 h/2 min`, and `0.05 h/2 min` inside the four challenge rows respectively. These are not additions and cannot authorize extra work.

## Explicit prospective-silent nomination gates

### Gate states and precedence

Every gate has `gate_id`, `scope` (`GLOBAL`, `ATOM`, or `BRIDGE`), `input_manifest_hash`, exact numerator/denominator or Boolean inputs, threshold, comparison operator, state, and reason. The only gate states are `PASS`, `FAIL`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, and `NOT_REGISTERED`. The decision precedence is

```text
NOT_REGISTERED or FAIL       -> NO_SELECTION
INCONCLUSIVE                 -> NO_SELECTION (report inconclusive, never adverse clinical claim)
required NOT_APPLICABLE      -> NO_SELECTION
all required gates PASS      -> CLAIMABLE_FOR_PROSPECTIVE_SILENT_ONLY
```

`NOT_APPLICABLE` is permitted only for a route/atom whose pre-label manifest proves that the corresponding estimand has an empty declared domain and the protocol explicitly says the gate is not needed; it cannot be used for a missing denominator, missing data, sparse support, or zero events. A zero/undefined denominator is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not pass.

The parent’s operational constants are sealed before labels. For executable verification they are registered as: simultaneous family coverage `0.95`; `tau_AUTO=0.05`; leakage upper bound `0.02`; mixed-admission upper bound `0.10`; lower confirmation `0.90`; lower agreement coefficient `0.70`; lower `Se_RECON=0.95`; lower `PPV_RECON=0.80`; lower `Se_DEFER=0.95`; lower `PPV_TIMING=0.95`; route-invariant flip upper bound `0.02`; decisive-control violation upper bound `0.05`; missing/unknown/control-infeasible upper bound `0.05`; minimum sampled route-positive count `30`; minimum non-sentinel challenge count `10`; minimum route-denominator ESS `50`; minimum occupied atom population `max(30,ceil(0.02*N_A))`; normalized maximum design weight `0.10`; overall design ESS `150`; minimum `N_A=200`; and minimum eligible-opportunity count `E_A=60`. These are operational nomination gates, not clinical harm thresholds.

For the secondary bridge, the following thresholds are frozen operational criteria, not claims of clinical correctness: lower simultaneous exact-preservation bound `L_exact >= 0.90`, upper simultaneous adverse-state bound `U_adverse <= 0.05`, and route-invariant bridge-control flip upper bound `0.02`. If a parent codebook or governance manifest supplies a different registered constant, the sealed protocol hash must contain it before labels; an absent or conflicting value is `NOT_REGISTERED`, never analyst-selected.

## Interpretation and no-overclaiming conclusion

Supportive execution would mean that the exact stored-text protocol state, compatible-set analysis, canonical ledger, controls, and evidence map passed for a named atom. It would justify only a prospective, within-site, silent validation of that exact signature, with all other signatures forced to `DEFER`. It would not establish actionability, communication, responsibility, appropriateness, actual care, safety, utility, benefit, causality, or transport.

Adverse execution includes source or serialization failure, H3 non-reproducibility, incompatible route states, a primary or bridge bound crossing its registered gate, control failure, ledger overflow, NA misuse, or an overclaiming evidence map; the affected atom is excluded, and a global contract failure yields `NO_SELECTION`. Inconclusive execution includes no eligible opportunity, zero/undefined denominator, sparse atom, wide shared-error interval, nonresponse, unresolved chain, infeasible control, missing workflow evidence needed for a stronger claim, or missing registered threshold. Inconclusive is not evidence of clinical failure or safety.

The strongest permissible conclusion is that a predeclared relationship between stored pre-departure radiology text and a stored canonical DS was or was not observed under the declared compatible states. Any stronger claim requires expert review with the actual image and intended clinical context, and any workflow claim requires a prospective silent study with direct workflow instrumentation and later clinical outcomes.
