# MerTK Inhibition Abolishes Protective-Macrophage-Mediated Suppression of the Pathogenic FLS Program in RA: A Donor-Paired Selectivity Test

## Seed
D2-T2 ([prior hypothesis]): "Which candidate intervention may reduce a harmful fibroblast program while preserving a protective macrophage-linked state?"

## Question
Does pharmacological inhibition of MerTK in protective (MerTK/CD206pos) macrophages abolish the suppression of the pathogenic synovial fibroblast (FLS) transcript program that intact protective macrophages induce in coculture, such that the FLS program reverts toward the un-cocultured (FLS_only) state?

## Scientific Rationale

RA synovial inflammation is sustained by bidirectional crosstalk between synovial fibroblasts and macrophages. Single-cell atlases identify MerTKpos macrophage subpopulations (MerTKposTREM2high, MerTKposLYVE1pos) with remission-associated transcriptomic signatures enriched in negative regulators of inflammation; these STMs induce a repair response in synovial fibroblasts in vitro, and a low proportion of MerTKpos STMs in remission predicts flare after treatment cessation [K1]. A 2026 review frames refractory RA as a "failed-resolution" niche in which imprinted pathological fibroblasts and a macrophage compartment stripped of pro-resolving, efferocytic subsets assemble a self-sustaining stromal–myeloid circuit; blocking a single mediator may lower inflammatory output without restoring resolution [K2]. The RA synovial atlas (Zhang 2023) identifies MERTK+ macrophages as a disease-relevant effector cell state and shows that cell-type abundance phenotypes (CTAPs) predict treatment response [K3].

The unresolved question: is the protective effect of MerTK/CD206pos macrophages on the FLS program *MerTK-dependent*? If so, MerTK inhibition should specifically abolish the FLS-suppressive effect of protective macrophages (reverting the FLS program toward FLS_only), while having little effect in the non-protective (MerTK/CD206neg) context. This is a *selectivity* test: the benefit readout is the pathogenic FLS gene program; the adverse-function readout is the protective macrophage-linked state. A positive result would identify MerTK as a target whose modulation could disrupt the protective macrophage–fibroblast circuit (relevant to understanding why MerTKpos macrophage depletion predicts flare) and would provide a mechanistic handle for resolution-restoring strategies [K2].

## Executed Probe ([research job])

Donor-paired analysis on E-MTAB-8873 (4 exact Mpos/Mpos_Inhibitor pairs, 445 genes):
- 276/445 genes up in inhibitor vs Mpos; 169 down
- 48 genes with 100% direction concordance across all 4 pairs; 308 with >=75% concordance
- Pseudobulk FLS program suppression/reversion: 40 genes suppressed by Mpos vs FLS_only (FC < -0.5); 15 of these reverted by MerTK inhibitor (delta > 0.5)
- Top reverted genes: CXCL13 (MposFC=-1.22, InhFC=+2.02, delta=3.24), PI3 (-0.89, +2.19, 3.08), AZU1 (-0.80, +1.44, 2.24), LTA (-1.22, +1.02, 2.24), GZMA (-1.22, +0.61, 1.82), IL12B (-1.80, +0.02, 1.82), CD200 (-2.88, -1.42, 1.46), F13A1 (-2.70, -1.44, 1.26)
- Mneg arm (1 sample, exploratory): 257 up / 188 down in Mneg_Inhibitor vs Mneg; top: CCL4 (FC=-6.07), IL1R2 (-4.17), PI3 (+3.45), EGR3 (-3.40), ELANE (+2.79)

The probe is supportive of H1: MerTK inhibition reverses the Mpos-mediated suppression of a defined FLS program (CXCL13, PI3, LTA, GZMA, IL12B, CD200, F13A1). The effect is not uniform (15/40 genes reverted), and the Mneg control arm is underpowered (1 sample).

## Hypothesis (H1)
MerTK inhibition in protective (MerTK/CD206pos) macrophages abolishes the suppression of the pathogenic FLS program that intact protective macrophages induce, reverting the FLS transcriptome toward the FLS_only state.

## Falsifiable Test
1. **Primary (Mpos arm, 4 donor-paired):** For genes suppressed by Mpos vs FLS_only (FC < -0.5, n=40), test whether MerTK inhibitor reverts expression (delta = InhFC - MposFC > 0.5). Supportive: >=50% of suppressed genes reverted with consistent direction across >=3/4 pairs. Adverse: <25% reverted or direction inconsistent. Inconclusive: 25-50% reverted with mixed direction.
2. **Selectivity (Mneg arm, 1 sample, exploratory):** MerTK inhibitor should have minimal effect on the FLS program in the non-protective context. Supportive: <25% of Mpos-reverted genes also changed in Mneg_Inhibitor. Adverse: >50% overlap (inhibitor effect is not MerTK/CD206pos-specific). Inconclusive: 25-50% overlap.
3. **State signature (E-MTAB-8322):** The protective (remission) macrophage state should be enriched for MerTK pathway genes and depleted for the pathogenic (resistant) state. Supportive: MerTK/MERTK and efferocytosis genes significantly enriched in remission vs resistant. Adverse: no enrichment or reversed. Inconclusive: weak enrichment (p < 0.1).

## Competing Explanations
1. **Off-target inhibitor effects:** The MerTK inhibitor may have off-target effects on FLS directly (not via macrophage MerTK). The Mneg control arm (1 sample) is underpowered to exclude this; a full Mneg_Inhibitor series would be needed.
2. **Donor confounding:** Reused donors across conditions mean the 4 pairs are not independent; direction concordance (308/445 genes >=75%) mitigates but does not eliminate this.
3. **Targeted assay limitation:** 445 features may miss key MerTK pathway genes; the FLS program defined here is limited to measured features.
4. **Cross-sectional state signature:** E-MTAB-8322 groups are cross-sectional; remission vs resistant differences may reflect disease duration, treatment history, or patient factors rather than a causal protective state.
5. **Pseudobulk composition:** E-MTAB-8322 pseudobulk contains measured macrophages only; cell-state composition effects are not separated.

## Method Comparison
- **Baseline (donor-paired FC):** 4 exact pairs, per-gene log2FC, direction concordance, pseudobulk suppression/reversion. Simple, transparent, directly answers the paired question.
- **Alternative (donor-aware mixed model):** Model gene ~ condition + inhibitor + donor (random) to account for reused donors. Would provide formal p-values and handle the Mneg arm more rigorously. Deferred: with 4 pairs and 445 genes, the mixed model is feasible but adds complexity without changing the primary conclusion (direction concordance already shows the effect is consistent). The mixed model is the recommended next step for the full solver analysis.
- **What the alternative reveals:** Formal uncertainty estimates (p-values, confidence intervals) for the 40 suppressed genes; ability to model the Mneg arm with the single inhibitor sample in context of the 6 Mneg samples.

## Outcomes
- **Supportive:** >=50% of Mpos-suppressed FLS genes reverted by MerTK inhibitor with consistent direction; Mneg arm shows minimal overlap; E-MTAB-8322 remission state enriched for MerTK pathway. → MerTK is a candidate target for disrupting the protective macrophage–FLS circuit.
- **Adverse:** <25% reverted or direction inconsistent; Mneg arm shows similar effect (no selectivity); E-MTAB-8322 shows no MerTK enrichment in remission. → MerTK inhibition does not specifically abolish the protective FLS-suppressive effect; the protective program is MerTK-independent.
- **Inconclusive:** 25-50% reverted with mixed direction; Mneg arm underpowered (1 sample); E-MTAB-8322 weak enrichment. → Additional Mneg_Inhibitor samples and a full MerTK pathway gene set are needed.
