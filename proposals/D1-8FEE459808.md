# A fixed nested probability audit of pulmonary-nodule admission routing: one complete challenge lane, probability validation, and two-phase correction

## Decision bottleneck and targeted advance

The unresolved clinical prerequisite remains whether the frozen admission routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` are reproducible and sufficiently resistant to unsafe minority-unit errors to justify a prospective **silent** validation. The selected parent makes this question leakage-resistant, but its confirmatory sample sends every selected admission to two radiology reviewers, two discharge-plan reviewers, two masked-context reviewers, two holistic challengers, and a redaction audit. At the 900-admission ceiling, this can exceed 7,200 initial physician reviews before redaction audit or any invalid-packet handling. That burden can itself make an otherwise valid experiment infeasible.

This child changes only the confirmatory review allocation, reference-error stress test, and corresponding design-based estimators. It preserves without relaxation:

* the first-eligible, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity denominator `N_A`;
* reciprocal report/addendum reconstruction using only operative components available by departure;
* the sole exact canonical discharge summary (`DS`), the exhaustive `Y`, `V/H/Z`, `J_A`, and unit-specific `G_u` chronology;
* deterministic safety-sentinel census, positive inclusion probability for every nonsentinel admission, no unit sampling, and review of every possible unit in each selected admission;
* mutually firewalled radiology, discharge, masked-context, and independent holistic evidence views, sealed atomic labels, no route voting, and locked exhaustive compatibility enumeration;
* admission-level inference, conservative worst-unit precedence, adverse disagreement/nonresponse/unknown handling, simultaneous finite-population uncertainty, and all sparse-event, leakage, masking, precision, and claim gates;
* the rule that retrospective support can nominate only prospective silent validation, never clinical deployment.

The operational repair is a **fixed, accuracy-blind nested two-phase design**. Every admission in the parent's master probability sample receives one complete firewalled component lane plus one independent holistic challenge (`R1/D1/K1/H1`). Before any clinical packet is opened or any label or route exists, a known-probability validation subsample is drawn within every hidden sampling stratum. Only that preselected subsample receives independently staffed duplicate panels (`R2/D2/K2/H2`) and human audit of two independently rendered masks. Full-population duplicate-panel quantities are recovered with exact two-phase Horvitz–Thompson difference estimation, not complete-case or unit-weighted analysis. No route-triggered verification, post-label top-up, favorable consensus, replacement, adaptive stopping, or sample reallocation is allowed.

Under the prespecified allocation below, the planned physician-review count is `4n + 5m`, where `n` is the master sample and `m` the validation subsample, rather than at least `8n` plus redaction audit in the parent. With large strata, `m` is about 30% of `n`, giving about `5.5n` reviews rather than `8n`; the exact burden is computed from frozen `N_h,n_h,m_h` before labels. Small strata may be censused, so savings are not assumed. If the fixed design cannot meet the inherited precision gates or approved resource budget, the result is an accuracy-blind feasibility failure, not permission to weaken inference.

## Two-phase finite-population estimands and inference

The finite population, denominator, sampling unit, analysis unit, and subject remain aligned at all `N_A`. Units never receive weights. For master admissions use `w1_i=1/pi_h`; for validation admissions use `w2_i=1/(pi_h*q_h)=N_h/m_h`.

For any binary all-population property `y_i` observed under the full duplicate workflow (for example `I(A*=AUTO)`), define its lane-1 proxy `x_i` observed for every master admission (for example `I(A1=AUTO)`). The primary exact two-phase difference estimator is

`T2_hat(y)=sum_{i in s1} x_i/pi_i + sum_{i in s2} (y_i-x_i)/(pi_i*q_i)`.

Because both samples are fixed SRSWOR within stratum and the correction coefficient is locked at 1, this estimates the finite-population duplicate-workflow total without treating validation cases as a complete-case cohort. Also report the direct validation HT estimate `sum_{s2}y_i/(pi_i*q_i)`; a material discrepancy is adverse model-free evidence of an unstable phase-1 proxy. No fitted prediction, post-label calibration, imputation, or estimated regression coefficient can replace either design estimator.

Use master HT/Hájek estimates for lane-1 route burdens and `H1` challenge properties. Use two-phase difference estimates for duplicate-workflow route burdens, H-union unsafe contamination, mask defects, and robust corrections. Use direct validation HT/Hájek estimates for lane agreement and confirmation. Required quantities retain the parent definitions, separately for lane 1 and corrected duplicate workflow:

Sparse validation events, even with many master events, cannot be repaired after labels. AUTO and RECON pass independently; a reconciliation-only silent study remains allowable when RECON passes and AUTO fails. Any missing governance decision or global gate yields `NO_SELECTION`.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

**Supportive** requires exact integrity, retrieval, separation, masking, design, workload, event, ESS, precision, reliability, purity, and capture gates to pass for both the complete master challenge and the two-phase corrected duplicate workflow. The lower-burden lane must remain within the locked correction margin under the adverse H union, both mask renderings, all compatible unit boundaries, and every reviewer block. This would support only a prospective silent validation of the frozen extraction, packet, single-lane routing, and probability-QA process.

**Supportive for RECON only** occurs when RECON reproducibility and unsafe-content capture pass in both phases but AUTO fails because contamination, minority units, masking, correlated-reviewer sensitivity, route correction, or sparse AUTO evidence crosses a gate. That is a useful negative finding for copy-oriented routing and may nominate only a reconciliation-focused silent test.

**Adverse** includes unsafe/uncertain AUTO contamination above margin, a duplicate-lane correction that reverses AUTO, repeated H2 findings missed by H1, renderer-specific route changes, a reviewer block with adverse reversal, generic negative-pair matching, recurrent nonsentinel extra units, timing based on the index unit alone, or failure of RECON/DEFER to capture unsafe/uncertain cases. It rejects the affected retrospective route; it does not prove that no future governed workflow can work.

**Inconclusive** includes retrieval undercoverage; failed hashes, access, canaries, or masking; master or validation design infeasibility; resource count above the preapproved budget; sentinel master sample above 900; sparse route/reference events; inadequate ESS; wide simultaneous intervals; unestimable reviewer blocks; excessive phase correction, H disagreement, renderer disagreement, unknowns, or nonresponse; compatibility explosion; or any request to tune or enlarge samples after labels. Accuracy-blind infeasibility should be reported before unnecessary review when possible. Sparse AUTO is not evidence that automation is unnecessary.

## Computational claims, clinical truth, and required next evidence

A verifier can check exact source and schema hashes; population and temporal rules; reciprocal links; unique DS; subject disjointness; both probability samples and inclusion probabilities; packet allowed fields; mask/canary logs; sealed label order; compatibility enumeration; worst-unit routing; two-phase estimates and nested replicate uncertainty; workload; gates; and whether conclusions match supportive, adverse, or inconclusive outputs.

A verifier cannot establish that either holistic physician is clinically correct, that reviewer errors are independent, that masking removes every cognitive cue, that a recommendation is appropriate, that omitted context or outside plans do not exist, or that a stored note was viewed, edited before physical departure, transmitted, communicated, ordered, scheduled, followed, or beneficial. H1/H2 agreement and crossed-roster diagnostics measure reproducibility of independent stored-text review, not clinical truth. `G_u` is storage-to-storage opportunity, not human opportunity.

A passing retrospective result therefore only nominates a consecutive prospective silent study. That study must capture immutable report finalization/addenda and viewing, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission times, physical departure, all recommendation units, route-independent expert adjudication, responsibility, target-linked orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan and preference attestation, final transmitted artifacts, correction/dismissal, drift, and workload. Require at least 100 route-positive admissions and 30 in every claimed route or a prespecified accrual stop. Only after silent validation could a governed trial test clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation for validated AUTO cases and side-by-side reconciliation for validated RECON cases. Clinical appropriateness, safety, communication, completed follow-up, harms, and benefit require that additional study and expert governance.

## Substantive change and remaining uncertainty

The parent’s scientific question, denominator, route semantics, and evidence ceiling are unchanged. The substantive advance is that duplicate expert review is treated as a validation measurement, not an obligatory census layer: every master admission still receives all-unit firewalled component review and an independent all-route holistic challenge, while duplicate component/challenge/mask review is allocated by a pre-label probability design and propagated to `N_A` with an exact two-phase correction. Fixed sequencing, no rescue review, and pre-label viability checks reduce resource use without outcome-adaptive sampling. Two independent mask renderings, an adverse H union, balanced disjoint rosters, and reviewer-block sensitivities make correlated error and imperfect masking more visible rather than assuming independence.

Residual uncertainty is irreducible in MIMIC. A smaller validation phase can be too sparse, and the correct result is inconclusive. Both H reviewers may share a mistaken clinical model; no statistical correction turns stored-text adjudication into clinical truth. Noneligible development text may fail to expose eligible-language edge cases. Probability sampling bounds finite-population error but cannot certify that no rare unsafe admission exists. Governance thresholds remain operational prerequisites, not learned harm utilities. Thus a passing result establishes only a feasible, leakage-resistant, design-validated retrospective text-routing process worthy of prospective silent validation.
