# Patient-reach and completeness, version-faithful visibility frontier for documentary-M2 review

## Targeted successor and falsifiable hypothesis

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that valid parent's first-eligible HCC-resection population, coherent operative-clock envelope, 24-hour decision cutoff and locked sensitivities, 90-day imaging and 365-day treatment lookbacks, temporal quarantine, encounter-documentary M2 outcome, whole-accession report radiology units, indivisible pathology composites, six corpus-wide reader books and nine global radiology-by-pathology book pairs, patient-balanced locked B/R/G/Gmask models, fixed absolute review capacity K, three comparator-closed contrasts, strict margin of more than five documentary-M2 captures per 100 review positions, exact source-world optimization, version-faithful effective-visibility bits, and restriction of one full-roster mask after singleton deletion.

It repairs two linked interpretation defects left by the parent. First, `C_PB` is a patient-weighted completeness fraction, not literally the proportion of patients who can receive any report: a patient with one visible report and nine hidden reports contributes 0.10, while a patient with one visible report contributes 1.00. Second, the parent’s named outcome classes could overlap—for example, an accession-only pass can occur simultaneously with a patient-balanced brittle or infeasible result. The child retains `C_PB` because it protects against prolific-imaging patients dominating the estimand, but adds an explicit patient reach gate and makes the primary labels a disjoint decision tree.

Define patient reach as the fraction of the complete eligible patient roster with at least one version-faithful visible accession. The primary 0.80 availability requirement is deliberately conjunctive: at least 0.80 patient reach and at least 0.80 patient-balanced completeness. This means “80% available” has a precise clinical reading—at least one usable report for 80% of eligible patients—while the completeness conjunct prevents the result from hiding widespread missing components. The two thresholds are prespecified service-design criteria, not evidence-supported clinical standards; the HCC source cannot establish that either target is safe, feasible, or beneficial.

The new unresolved hypothesis is:

> In each of 2020 and 2021, every coherent source world and every version-faithful full-roster mask meeting both 0.80 patient reach and 0.80 patient-balanced completeness preserves all six strict >5 documentary-M2 capture margins—G over independently optimized acquisition-only B, G over independently optimized report-surface R, and G over its same-fitted semantic mask Gmask—before deletion and after removal of any one whole test patient, with every survivor retaining identical accession bits and features.

A margin equal to five fails. A world in which no mask meets the dual availability requirement falsifies this prespecified stored-content service criterion rather than passing vacuously. The old accession-weighted frontier and the single-metric `C_PB` frontier remain diagnostics; neither can authorize the primary conclusion.

This is a retrospective finite-corpus documentary-review experiment. It does not test biological M2, treatment selection, report timeliness in the source hospital, actual capacity, clinician action, recurrence, survival, safety, or patient benefit.

## Version-faithful patient-balanced visibility

For every temporally eligible nonblank accession u, construct the parent's immutable eventual pipeline-input hash:

`H_HCC(u)=SHA256(schema_version || parser_version || length-delimited ordered tuples(source_row_ordinal, field_name, exact raw bytes, V/A/E lineage))`

for every exact-deduplicated `exam findings` and `exam diagnosis` component. It commits to component count/order, field boundaries, empty fields, and parser lineage. Similar wording, impressions alone, edit distance, equal model score, or reader judgment cannot substitute for exact identity.

The hypothetical bit r_u exposes the whole eventual eligible report block when 1 and replaces every body-derived S/Q/X block by its development-defined operational-unavailable block when 0; acquisition-only Z remains. The same S/Q mask applies to Gmask and B is unchanged. Operationally, r_u may equal 1 only if an external archive proves before c_d that the exact whole version was finalized, retrievable by the intended scoring service, uniquely crosswalked to `Examination number`, and byte-identical at the complete pipeline input to H_HCC(u). Preliminary, amended, retracted, unmatched, inaccessible, or nonidentical versions cannot count. Missing archive evidence is unknown, not proof of unavailability.

For full test-year world omega, let I_a(omega) be the complete eligible patient roster, N=|I|, and let E_i(omega) be all temporally eligible nonblank CT/MRI accession units for patient i before semantic-source availability filtering. Let n_i=|E_i|. Source-unavailable units remain in E_i but have r_u forced to zero. A patient with n_i=0 contributes zero and is never dropped. This is an all-roster denominator: absence of an eligible accession is an observed zero for the availability estimand, not a reason to condition the denominator on report-bearing patients.

Define the two primary availability coordinates:

`A_reach(r,omega) = (1/N) * sum_i 1(sum_{u in E_i} r_u > 0)`.

`C_PB(r,omega) = (1/N) * sum_i [ 1(n_i>0) * (1/n_i) * sum_{u in E_i} r_u ]`.

`A_reach` is the patient-level reach measure: each patient contributes one unit if at least one whole eligible accession is visible and zero otherwise. `C_PB` is the patient-balanced within-roster completeness measure: each patient contributes at most one unit regardless of scan count, and a patient with ten eligible accessions contributes one tenth per visible accession. Patients with n_i=0 contribute zero to both measures. Source-unavailable accessions remain denominator units with forced-zero bits. The maxima `A_reach^max(omega)` and `C_PB^max(omega)` are computed with every version-releasable unit visible and every forced-unavailable unit zero; neither maximum conditions on successful parsing or external access.

For c in the frozen grid `{0,.01,...,1.00}`, define the primary full-roster family

`R_PR,c(omega)={r: A_reach(r,omega)≥c and C_PB(r,omega)≥c}`.

The confirmatory family is `R_PR,.80`; empty families never pass. The old families `{r:C_PB≥c}` and the accession-weighted family are retained only as diagnostics. At every c, compute the exact worst-case endpoints over every coherent world and every mask in the corresponding family, using one common mask across comparator arms; do not replace a universal minimum with a favorable maximizing mask. For each whole test patient j, remove j and all of j's states, outcomes, and units, and restrict that same mask to survivors. Do not recompute N, n_i, weights, c, or a quota; do not expose a replacement accession. Models, books, roles, preprocessing, K, ties, states, and every survivor's bit and feature hash stay byte-identical. Require N^{-j}≥K.

The primary 0.80 claim requires, in every world, `A_reach^max≥.80`, `C_PB^max≥.80`, and a nonempty `R_PR,.80`; all six undeleted plus all six anchored-singleton lower endpoints over the union of every `R_PR,.80(omega)` must be strictly >5. For deletion, the mask must first belong to the undeleted full-roster family; its bits and features are then restricted to survivors, so deletion cannot manufacture availability.

Report the smallest grid value `c_PR*` at which the dual availability family is nonempty in every world and the universal conjunction holds through all higher attainable grid values; if no such value exists, it is undefined. Separately report `b_reach` and `b_complete`, the largest attainable reach and completeness among masks witnessing each failed availability gate, and `b_margin`, the largest dual-eligible coverage pair attached to a failed margin coordinate. A supportive result requires no dual-eligible failing witness, not merely a favorable optimizer. The extrema and direct witness enumeration must agree.

Recompute the parent's accession-weighted frontier on the identical worlds and masks as a secondary multiplicity diagnostic. It cannot support the patient-balanced claim. Report overall, CT-only, MRI-only, and dual-modality patient-balanced coverage descriptively; no modality claim is made without its event/roster gates.

## Exact computation and falsification

Compile finite patient domains carrying membership/year/Y, Z/S/Q/X, accession sets and rational patient weights, locked scores, source/form/terminal hashes, ties, and raw backpointers. Exact MIP or a certified equivalent chooses one coherent state per patient and one full-roster whole-accession mask, then ranks exactly K patients per arm. Encode both `A_reach` and `C_PB` with exact rationals or a proved lossless integer scaling, including the binary any-visible-accession indicator. For each year, enumerate or certify the minimum of every contrast over all coherent worlds and every mask in `R_PR,.80(omega)`, separately for undeleted and anchored-singleton deletion. Each contrast uses the same world and mask across arms. A solver that returns only a best mask, or optimizes the six contrasts in a way that omits a different failing coordinate, is not a valid universal test.

Require zero integer gap, numerical feasibility and residual ≤1e-8, complete rank-inversion constraints, all tied maximizers, raw/quotient replay, and exhaustive agreement with brute force on fixtures through 12 patients and real shards through 20. A timeout, state cap, sampled masks, favorable incumbent, unexplained branch, missing witness, rational-scaling loss, changed survivor bit/feature, or failed replay is inconclusive.

The primary estimand is the exact frozen finite corpus. Bootstrap diagnostics cannot create a population or confidence claim and cannot change the exact primary label.

One-hot primary result (exactly one label; diagnostics below are not labels):

1. `inconclusive`: a required source, dictionary, roster, annotation, chronology, event, N/K, leakage, fit, exact-weight, replay, solver, or mask-invariance gate fails, or the required extrema cannot be certified. No scientific direction is assigned.
2. `dual-availability-infeasible`: all computation gates pass, but at least one coherent world has no mask with both `A_reach≥.80` and `C_PB≥.80` (equivalently, its certified maximum for at least one coordinate is <.80). This falsifies the prespecified dual availability criterion in this corpus; it does not prove reports were late.
3. `dual-availability-robust-supportive`: all gates pass, every world has a nonempty dual-availability family, and every undeleted and anchored-singleton contrast endpoint over every admissible world/mask is strictly >5. This is the only supportive label.
4. `dual-availability-robust-adverse`: all gates pass and every world has a nonempty family, but at least one certified admissible world/mask/deletion witness has a required contrast ≤5. This falsifies the universal robustness hypothesis. It does not prove radiology is useless, nor that the contrast is biologically or operationally beneficial.

The labels are disjoint by construction: apply the four tests in numerical order, and stop at the first true condition. The verifier must assert exactly one label and reject any output that reports a primary label together with another primary label. The former “accession-only artifact,” “visibility-brittle,” “semantic-evidence-only,” “quota-policy-only,” and localized-coordinate descriptions become orthogonal diagnostic flags, which may legitimately co-occur:

- `accession_only_artifact` = the old accession-weighted criterion passes while the dual patient criterion fails;
- `reach_failure` and `completeness_failure` identify which availability coordinate blocks feasibility or robustness;
- `visibility_brittle` = the dual family exists but a required margin has a ≤5 witness;
- `semantic_evidence_only` = all Delta_R and Delta_mask requirements pass but at least one Delta_B requirement fails;
- `quota_policy_only` = all Delta_B requirements pass but at least one semantic requirement fails;
- `strong_adverse_coordinate` = the maximum over all admissible witnesses for a coordinate is ≤5;
- `mixed_or_localized` = a required coordinate has witnesses on both sides of five.

Each flag is computed from stored extrema and witnesses, never from prose. Thus a dual-availability-adverse result may also be accession-only artifact or semantic-evidence-only; that overlap is intentional and is not a violation of the primary one-hot partition.

A supportive result means that, within this frozen eventual-report corpus, every admissible mask gives at least one exact whole report to at least 80% of patients and at least 80% patient-balanced report completeness, and the fixed-capacity documentary-M2 margins survive all source worlds and anchored one-patient deletions. It is a robustness result about stored documentary inputs, not proof that clinicians saw them. An adverse result falsifies that universal stress claim; it does not establish no clinical value. An infeasible result says the prespecified availability target cannot be met in at least one source world. Inconclusive means the evidence or exact computation cannot resolve even those finite-corpus claims. Favorable AUC, an accession-weighted pass, an isolated reader/world, or a post-hoc relaxed denominator cannot rescue a failed primary result.
