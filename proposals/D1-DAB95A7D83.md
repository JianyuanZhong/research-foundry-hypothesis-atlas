## 0. The scientific opening (the parent's own conceded defect)

The parent already repairs the parent's death-conflation defect and won two blinded comparisons.
Its own text concedes the fatal residual rival it cannot separate:

- **P (modifiable process):** deep sedation delays liberation mainly by *suppressing the daily
  awakening / sedation-interruption process* — a protocol lever (screened by the SAT process).
- **Q (physiology / severity):** the depth effect is direct/non-process, or is largely a proxy for
  the underlying illness (confounding by indication). Under Q depth-only protocols mislead.

The two readings are currently observationally identical because the parent measures only the
**total** deep-sedation-fraction → liberation effect. They differ on a *measurable quantity the
parent never computes*: the **fraction of the total effect that runs through the wake-up process**.

**Leading claim (P):** a substantial, positive share of the deep-sedation-fraction effect on
liberation is mediated by deferral of the daily wake-up (SAT) process.
**Strongest rival (Q):** the indirect fraction ≈ 0 (effect is direct/severity), or the mediating
item is a charting artifact.
**Ancillary rival (R-conf):** unmeasured confounding by indication fully explains the M2 effect
(now bounded quantitatively via E-value).

Separating observation: the prespecified **natural indirect effect (NIE)** and **mediated fraction**
through the wake-up process, holding the competing risk of death, with the mediator measured
strictly after t*. Supportive of P ⇒ the clinical lever is the *process* (protocolised SAT);
adverse ⇒ the lever is depth/physiology or the signal is confounding.

## 1. Clinical importance

Time to liberation drives ICU LOS, VAP and delirium. K2 [Girard 2008, PMID 18191684] shows a
randomised paired SAT/SBT protocol increased ventilator-free days (14.7 vs 11.6 d) and lowered
1-year mortality — so the process axis is causally real and clinically consequential. The open
question is whether the *observational depth association* in MIMIC-IV lives on that process. If
yes, the actionable target is a wake-up/SAT protocol; if no, depth is a severity marker and
depth-only sedation targets mislead. This changes the decision either way.

## 3. Design and estimands

Population, t*, window, split, ladder and controls are inherited unchanged. Define:
- `D` = deep-sedation fraction of RASS observations in `[t*, t*+12 h]` with value in [-5,-3]
  (continuous; binary ≥50 % retained as the B0 baseline).
- `M` = process-mediator indicator/level from itemid **223780** recorded **strictly after t*** (no
  future information): e.g. `M=1` if ≥1 wake-up deferral is documented in `(t*, t*+24 h]` (value
  "No/Not Sedated" is the pure deferral event; other reasons are pre-specified as a sensitivity
  recoding). Fallbacks (prespecified, only if the primary mediator is too sparse): 223849
  ventilator-mode / spontaneous-breathing transitions, then 223911; else the active-comparator
  drug-class contrast. If a fallback is used, the **primary mediated estimand is declared
  INCONCLUSIVE** (no silent substitution).
- Outcome: time t* → 7 d with mutually exclusive first events **liberation** (227194, no
  re-intubation 225792 within 48 h), **death** (competing, `admissions.deathtime`), or censored.

**PRIMARY DELTA — mediation decomposition of the total effect of `D` on liberation.**
Estimator (prespecified): a **landmark + interventional/natural-effect decomposition** on the
cause-specific hazard scale for liberation, with `M` measured after t* and a
**mediational g-formula** cross-check. Report the **NIE**, the **natural direct effect (NDE)**,
and the **mediated fraction** = NIE / (NIE + NDE) = NIE / total, each with **bootstrap 95 % CIs**
(≥1000 resamples) under the competing-risk (cause-specific) framework; report the death CIF
alongside so the decomposition is never read as pure liberation delay.

*Identifying assumptions (stated explicitly):*
1. **No unmeasured exposure–mediator confounding** (and no unmeasured mediator–outcome
   confounding conditional on M0–M2) — the strongest and least testable assumption; E-value
   bounds it for the total effect only.
2. **No unmeasured exposure–outcome confounding** conditional on the ladder (weaker version for
   the natural effects).
3. **Positivity / overlap** of mediator levels across exposure values; and **no mediator–outcome
   confounder affected by exposure** (cross-world consistency). Deferral reasons that are also
   treatment decisions (e.g. "Unstable Vent Status") are treated as potential
   exposure-induced confounding and analysed in sensitivity.
4. **Temporal ordering:** `M` strictly after t* and before the outcome event.
5. **Consistency / no interference** (single ICU episode per stay).

**SECONDARY DELTA — E-value for the M2 cause-specific HR.**
Report the **E-value** (VanderWeele & Ding) for the M2 adjusted cause-specific HR for liberation,
for the point estimate and the CI limit nearest the null, quantifying how strong an unmeasured
confounder (e.g. latent severity) would need to be to explain away the association. This bounds
the R-conf rival; it does **not** prove absence of confounding.

## 4. Baselines and alternatives (method exploration, same question)

- **B0 (simple baseline):** binary sustained-deep flag; Cox / fixed-horizon logistic on M0→M2;
  death ignored or censored. Loses the dose gradient, the death/liberation distinction, and any
  process mechanism.
- **A1 (parent):** competing-risks Fine-Gray CIF + cause-specific Cox + dose spline. Reveals
  whether the depth association is death-conflation; reports only the TOTAL effect.
- **A2 (this child):** mediation decomposition of the total effect into a **process (wake-up/SAT)
  indirect path** and a direct path, plus E-value. Reveals *where* the effect lives — a modifiable
  protocol process vs direct physiology/severity — which changes the clinical lever. **This is the
  substantive alternative to A1: A1 gives the total effect but is blind to its composition.**
- **Deferred:** time-varying IPTW / MSM target-trial emulation and a latent-score joint model —
  deferred because they need stronger sequential-exchangeability assumptions on RASS dosing than
  the mediation decomposition; revisit only if the mediator is too sparse for A2.
- **Fitting target / split / evaluation:** landmark t*; locked earliest-70 %/latest-30 % `intime`
  split; **NIE, mediated fraction, death CIF, cause-specific HR, E-value, all with CIs**; negative
  control (analgesia exposure) and permuted-label control. **Resources:** CPU-only tabular MIMIC-IV
  (`lifelines`/`statsmodels`/NumPy + bootstrap); **no GPU needed or claimed**; budget < a few hours.

## 5. Falsification criteria (prespecified)

- **Supportive (P):** M2 total cause-specific HR < 1 (CI excludes 1) AND a substantial **positive
  mediated fraction through 223780 with a bootstrap CI excluding 0**; negative-control analgesia
  exposure null; permuted-label control null; the indirect path is not explained solely by
  exposure-induced mediator confounding in sensitivity.
- **Adverse (Q):** total effect survives M2 but **mediated fraction ≈ 0 (CI includes 0)** — the
  effect is direct/severity; OR the effect vanishes under M1/M2 with a **rising death hazard**
  (mortality conflation); OR the analgesia negative control shows a parallel spurious effect
  (R2 measurement/indication).
- **Adverse (R-conf):** M2 HR is not robust — its **E-value is small** relative to plausible
  confounding by indication (i.e. a mild/moderate unmeasured severity confounder could explain it).
- **Inconclusive:** **223780 too sparse across stays/after t* to identify an indirect effect**
  (the prespecified mediator-fallback then applies and the primary mediated estimand is declared
  inconclusive); wide NIE/mediated-fraction CIs; mediator prevalence so low the positivity
  assumption fails; or RASS documentation completeness too low to estimate the fraction.
- **Not established by this design (requires other work):** any causal claim (needs an RCT or an
  explicit instrument); RASS charting completeness; whether residual latent severity is fully
  absorbed by M2; the cross-world "no unmeasured exposure–mediator confounding" assumption.

## 6. Interpretation of outcomes and limits

Supportive ⇒ the clinically actionable lever is the wake-up/SAT *process*, not depth per se —
prioritise protocolised awakening. Adverse ⇒ depth is largely physiological/severity or
confounded — deprioritise depth-only protocols. Inconclusive ⇒ establish wake-up documentation
before deciding. **Limits:** single-centre retrospective; RASS charting completeness unknown ([K3]
validates the scale, not EHR documentation); mediation assumptions are strong and partly untestable;
association ≠ causation; cohort prevalence and power **not** established in discovery.
**Computationally checkable:** the competing-risks/NIE/mediated-fraction/E-value computations,
event counts, CIs. **Requires clinical adjudication or another study:** whether the cross-world
assumptions hold, whether a residual severity axis absorbs the effect, and any causal claim.
