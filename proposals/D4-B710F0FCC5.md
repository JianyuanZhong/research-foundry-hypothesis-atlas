# Honest two-resource comparison of UACR-first versus immediate cystatin-C allocation

## Clinical decision, supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

A service with capacity to order cystatin C for exactly 20% of adults with baseline race-free creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m² must decide whether to allocate immediately from information that does not require urine, or first attempt UACR for everyone, wait for a usable result, and allocate from the UACR-usable group. The assigned parent correctly separated protocolized UACR-first performance, immediate UACR-free performance, and unidentifiable observed timing. Its strongest remaining weakness is resource and comparator symmetry: the branches use the same cystatin count, but no direct screen-first-versus-immediate contrast is required and the extra urine-screen burden is not a mandatory computed resource output.

The strongest evidence supported before opening any locked outcome remains narrow. The inherited read-only audit reconstructed O=134,118 and usable instance-0 UACR for 130,257 (97.12%); the biological schema and physical header contain the needed urine and kidney biomarkers but no collection, result-release, order, or allocation timestamp. Thus UACR is calculable for V, but its actual pre-order availability is unknown. The inherited inspected full text of Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041; frozen source [source checksum]) supports association of participant characteristics with creatinine–cystatin eGFR differences and an internally validated enriched model with AUC about 0.75. It does not establish measured GFR, fixed-capacity policy value, UACR chronology, persistent CKD, workflow feasibility, cost, or benefit.

The new unresolved, falsifiable decision hypothesis is:

> In both locked partitions, a protocolized screen-first strategy that attempts UACR for all O, waits for a usable result, and selects the frozen AG top n from V will detect at least 2 additional combined-equation eGFR<60 reclassifications per 1,000 cystatin assays versus immediate frozen G selection from all O, with point yield ratio at least 1.10, simultaneous interval above no difference, positive marker-discordance yield, and positive directional-swap yield. If inherited N18 event sufficiency passes, it will add at least 1 reclassification followed by first 10-year inpatient N18 per 1,000 assays. These assay-yield gains must replicate while using the identical absolute n=floor(0.20×|O|) in each partition and while reporting the non-common resource of |O| UACR attempts for screen-first versus zero for immediate allocation.

Even supportive results would establish only greater target yield per equal cystatin assay under an imposed UACR-first protocol. They would not establish total-resource superiority because UACR collection, processing, delay, failure, patient burden, money, downstream actions, and utility are unavailable.

## Analysis, uncertainty, and falsification

Freeze the direct contrast specification in development before locked labels are opened. Add `SF_AG:IM_G` H, D and H×K18 contrasts to the inherited simultaneous max-|t| family; use the same 2,000 participant-paired bootstrap over O_p with no refitting. In a bootstrap draw, reconstruct both top-n selected sets using frozen scores and deterministic tie handling. Apply inherited participant-level joint missing-H/D bounds, >1% selected cystatin-missingness gate, feasible ratio bounds, censoring variants, calibration, sex-stability and N18 event-sufficiency rules.

The assay-yield hypothesis is supportive only if:

- |V_p|≥n_p and both selected counts equal n_p in development, test and replication;
- in test and replication, the adverse joint-bound `Delta_H(SF_AG:IM_G)≥2/1,000`, its simultaneous interval is above 0, point H ratio is at least 1.10 with feasible simultaneous interval above 1, and the SF_AG-minus-IM_G directional-swap H yield exceeds the reverse;
- Delta_D is positive in both locks with pooled partition-stratified simultaneous interval above 0;
- every frozen UACR censoring/missingness variant preserves positive Delta_H and Delta_D in both locks;
- if inherited N18 sufficiency passes for both directional swap sets, adverse-bound Delta_H18 is at least 1/1,000 with pooled simultaneous interval above 0;
- all inherited AG_V:A_V, G_V:A_V, all-O G:B/F/T, calibration, placebo, label, sex-stability, missingness, replication and N18 requirements also pass.

The direct result is adverse with adequate precision if either lock excludes the +2/1,000 H materiality target, excludes the 1.10 ratio, shows a nonpositive H or D contrast, has reverse directional-swap yield, or, when event-sufficient, excludes the +1/1,000 H×K18 target. This means the imposed screen-first policy did not provide the prespecified per-cystatin gain versus immediate G; it does not show UACR is clinically useless.

The direct result is inconclusive if intervals cross margins, locks disagree, |V_p|<n_p, exact capacities differ, labels or swaps are sparse, a feasible denominator can be zero, N18 is insufficient for the consequential endpoint, UACR variants reverse sign, or an inherited integrity/calibration/sex-stability gate fails. Sparse N18 allows only a biochemical assay-yield label; it cannot support the clinical-consequence hypothesis.

Resource interpretation is a separate axis:

- `assay_yield_supportive_total_resource_unresolved`: all supportive direct and inherited gates pass, cystatin capacity is equal, and the output explicitly records |O_p| versus zero UACR attempts.
- `assay_yield_adverse`: a direct adverse criterion is met with adequate precision.
- `assay_yield_inconclusive`: statistical or computational gates do not resolve the contrast.
- `observed_preassay_not_identifiable`: required timestamps remain absent regardless of performance.
- `overall_workflow_winner_not_identifiable`: always true in this dataset because no common valuation of urine attempts, delay, burden, money, actions, harms, or benefits exists.

A favorable AG_V:A_V result alone cannot support screen-first over immediate G. A favorable G_V:A_V result cannot be relabeled immediate because V membership depends on urine usability. A favorable SF_AG:IM_G result cannot be called equal-total-resource superiority. Conversely, the unavoidable extra UACR ledger does not turn an adequately estimated adverse yield result into mere resource uncertainty.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The timing parent prevented calculable UACR from masquerading as pre-order UACR. This child repairs the next decision gap: it directly compares the best inherited protocolized screen-first allocation with the inherited immediate grip policy on the same O-defined absolute cystatin capacity, while exposing that screen-first consumes |O| additional urine attempts and excludes UACR-unusable participants. The experiment can now falsify a clinically relevant per-cystatin advantage without pretending the workflows consume the same total resources.

The strongest possible supportive conclusion is that, under an imposed UACR-first protocol, SF_AG finds more prespecified targets per equal cystatin assay than immediate G and merits a prospective workflow comparison. Choosing or recommending the workflow still requires UACR turnaround, burden, monetary cost, actions, utility, adjudicated kidney status, and external/prospective evidence that UKB does not contain.
