# Graded within-macrophage MerTK brake vs fixed composition/state artifact — a dose-ladder discriminator with an orthogonal perturbation line (episode 18, evolution)

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (valid, mu 33.58). Key works [K1],[K2],[K3]. Branch: work/evo-8873/. Screening status: **planned, not executed** (no managed compute job run; only bounded read-only inspection of the prepared files, stated below).

## 1. Unresolved question (the opening)

Does the MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) synovial macrophage population **actively brake** the FLS effector program as a **graded, within-macrophage, MerTK-intrinsic dose response**, or is the pos-vs-neg effector difference a **fixed composition/state artifact** — total macrophage load, an SPP1+ LAM vs FOLR2/TREM2 resident state, or FLS-donor identity?

The parent (candidate-77a68856) established the two-group score and a single 1:3 / 3:1 ratio pair. Its residual weakness, recorded by the ranking step and by earlier episodes: **the brake claim rests on n=1 titration arms and a two-group score**, and **prior episodes recorded non-uniform paired deltas** — the pos-vs-neg effector difference does not behave identically across FLS donors. A two-group contrast, and even a single titration pair, cannot separate a graded within-macrophage dose response from a difference in which macrophages are present (composition), what state they are in (SPP1+ LAM vs FOLR2/TREM2), or which FLS donor was used. This episode sharpens the design so the two models make **strictly different, pre-registered predictions on a specific contrast set**, and adds one **orthogonal (non-E-MTAB-8873) perturbation axis** as an independent falsification line.

## 2. Clinical importance

Roughly one third of RA patients do not sustain drug-free remission. If a MerTK+CD206+ macrophage subset **dose-dependently** restrains matrix-degrading and inflammatory FLS output, then enriching or activating that arm is a rational resolution-therapy target, and MerTK-family inhibition would predictably release the brake. If instead the association is absorbed by macrophage load/composition or by state re-routing, then enriching the marker would not restore resolution and MerTK-directed inhibition would not predictably worsen effector output. [K1] supplies the remission-associated MerTK+CD206+ subset (an association, not a proven brake); [K2] supplies the rival that MerTK+CD206+ pro-repair cells can acquire pro-arthritogenic function, so MerTK status is not sufficient for protection; [K3] shows RA synovium contains multiple expanded myeloid/stromal states with mapped mediator sources, so effector output can reflect which state dominates. A useful negative — "the effect is composition/state, not a MerTK dose response" — is a real deliverable: it would redirect the branch away from MerTK-directed resolution therapy. **This is not a clinical-efficacy claim; no protein or wet-lab causality is established.**

## 3. Leading claim vs strongest rival (pre-registered, strictly different predictions)

- **Model G — graded within-macrophage MerTK-intrinsic brake.** At fixed FLS donor, fixed macrophage-donor pair, and matched total macrophage content, the FLS effector score is a **non-increasing, approximately monotone** function of the pos_fraction of macrophages; the negative slope **survives** adjustment for load, a state proxy and FLS-donor effects; and a MerTK-inhibitor pre-treatment **flips the sign** (raises effector score relative to untreated pos wells of the same donors). MerTK signalling inside the macrophage is the dose variable.
- **Model C — fixed composition/state artifact.** The pos-vs-neg difference is a **step** determined by which macrophages are present and in what state: it is **absorbed** by total macrophage load, an SPP1+ LAM vs FOLR2/TREM2 covariate, and FLS-donor effects; the pos_fraction slope is **flat or non-monotone** once these are modelled; the inhibitor has **no consistent direction**; and pos-vs-neg deltas **track the FLS donor** (the non-uniform deltas already recorded).

**Where the models strictly diverge.** There is exactly one design cell in E-MTAB-8873 that holds the **same FLS donor (SA258R)** and the **same macrophage donors (pos SA267R, neg SA266A)** at **two compositions with nearly matched total sequenced cells**: `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_1Mpos_SA266A_3Mneg` (pos_fraction 0.25; 1364 cells) vs `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_3Mpos_SA266A_1Mneg` (pos_fraction 0.75; 1145 cells). Because FLS donor and macrophage-donor identity are held constant, this pair isolates pos_fraction from donor identity; the ~19% cell-count difference is the load nuisance the models must absorb.

- **Model G predicts** effector_score(0.25) > effector_score(0.75), with the two-point drop directionally consistent with the within-donor single-Mpos vs single-Mneg contrasts, and (beyond the ratio wells) a monotone ladder over pos_fraction ∈ {0.0, 0.25, 0.75, 1.0} within SA258R.
- **Model C predicts** the 0.25-vs-0.75 difference is within noise or explained by the ~19% load difference, i.e. a flat/non-monotone ladder, and that any apparent slope disappears once load + state + FLS donor enter the model.

A further discriminator: the **inhibitor-reversal** contrast is measured at fixed pos_fraction within FLS donors (e.g. `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos_Inhibitor` vs `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos`; `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos_preInhibitor` vs `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos`). Model G requires a **consistent positive** direction (inhibitor raises effector score) across these donor-matched pairs; Model C requires **no consistent direction**.

## 5. Baselines, substantive alternatives, analysis

- **B0 (simple baseline).** Within-FLS-donor **paired delta** effector_score(Mpos) − effector_score(Mneg); exact paired sign/permutation statistic over the FLS-donor unit; **all donor deltas reported individually, including non-uniform / sign-varying deltas**. This is the honest reproduction of the parent's two-group observation.
- **B1 (dosimetry, substantive alternative A).** `effector_score ~ β1·pos_fraction + β2·load_proxy` with **FLS-donor as a grouping term** (fixed effects or random intercept), fit on co-culture wells. Primary output: **monotone slope** on pos_fraction, plus a Spearman/monotonicity statistic over the within-SA258R ladder {0.0, 0.25, 0.75, 1.0}. Information lost by B0 that B1 recovers: whether the response is **graded in dose** rather than a two-level step, and whether it survives load.
- **B2 (composition/state rival, substantive alternative B).** Add `state_proxy` and FLS-donor effects: `effector_score ~ β1·pos_fraction + β2·load_proxy + β3·state_proxy + FLS_donor`. The discriminator is whether **β1 survives (G) or is absorbed (C)**. A fixed-step rival is also fit explicitly: `effector_score ~ γ·is_pos_well + covariates`.
- **Inhibitor-direction contrast.** Within FLS donor, sign and magnitude of effector_score(untreated pos) − effector_score(inhibitor pos) across the donor-matched pairs; Model G requires a consistent positive direction.
- **Learned/neural model: not justified, documented non-use.** A 445-feature targeted panel with 33 pseudobulk samples and no independent test set cannot support a generalisable learned model; adding capacity would chase a small in-sample gain, not the scientific uncertainty (graded vs composition). Revisit only if an independent FLS/macrophage coculture test set with donor-aware structure becomes available.
- **Orthogonal (non-8873) perturbation line — E-MTAB-8316.** Separate dataset, separate cell type (RA synovial **monocyte**/macrophage-lineage), separate perturbation (pharmacologic **UNC106238** vs none, LPS 1 ng/mL), readout = macrophage-intrinsic MerTK/resolution program rather than FLS effector output. Pre-registered target: a resolution/MerTK-program score over ENSG-mapped genes (MERTK, MRC1, AXL, CD163, FOLR2); primary statistic = **paired within-donor sign test after aggregating repeated specimens per donor/condition** (donors SA114 ×2, SA049 ×2, SA051, SA104). Direction expectation under G: blocking MerTK signalling shifts the MerTK/resolution program in the direction consistent with releasing a brake. It is a distinct compartment and a distinct perturbation, so it is an **independent falsification line**, not a replication. **4 donors → underpowered, labelled exploratory.**
- **Optional cross-sectional anchor — E-MTAB-8322.** 27 macrophage pseudobulk samples across 5 clinical groups; used only as a directional disease-spectrum anchor for the MerTK/efferocytosis axis with an SPP1/TREM2/FOLR2 LAM-state rival; cross-sectional, so it cannot establish transitions. Not used for the pos_fraction model.
- **Load/measurement controls.** Because `load_proxy` is a sample-level proxy, B1/B2 are fit with and without it as a sensitivity check; if a pos_fraction slope appears only when `load_proxy` is omitted, that is recorded as load-confounded.

**Resources.** Primary and rival fits are small OLS/mixed models over 33 rows on CPU (< a few minutes); no GPU needed. Future solver budget is well inside inputs.json solver_planning_limits; unverified until run.

## 9. Pre-registered decision rule and falsification criteria

- **Supportive (graded within-macrophage brake; G):** (i) pos_fraction slope < 0 with donor-clustered 95% CI excluding 0 **after** load + state + FLS-donor adjustment; (ii) the SA258R ladder {0.0, 0.25, 0.75, 1.0} is non-increasing (effector score declines as pos_fraction rises); (iii) the MerTK-inhibitor raises effector score with a **consistent positive direction** across the donor-matched pairs; (iv) the orthogonal 8316 axis moves in the MerTK-signalling direction. Not all four are required for partial support; the primary bar is (i)+ non-monotone refutation, and (iii) is the strongest single discriminant.
- **Adverse (composition/state/donor; C):** no within-donor pos<neg consistency; flat or non-monotone ladder; **β1 absorbed** to ~0 by load/state/FSL donor; inhibitor direction absent or opposite; the pos-vs-neg delta tracks FLS donor.
- **Inconclusive:** n=1 titration arm (only SA258R has the full pair); pos_fraction slope CI spanning 0; GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3 ligand genes unmeasured so MerTK-intrinsic signalling cannot be separated from MerTK-marker presence at the transcript level in 8873; `load_proxy` inadequate; ENSG-unmapped genes in 8316; cross-sectional anchor only; → requires a targeted ligand/protein assay or a wet-lab dose-response perturbation.
- **Threshold honesty:** the pos_fraction slope is called "surviving" only if its donor-clustered 95% CI excludes 0 after adjustment; otherwise the result is treatment-of-fit-noise and is reported as inconclusive, not adverse.

## 10. What supportive / adverse / inconclusive results would and would not establish

- **Supportive** would establish a **graded, MerTK-signalling-dependent transcriptional dose relationship** inside the macrophage arm of the model system — i.e. a stronger, dose-resolved causal-brake claim than the parent's two-group association. It would **not** establish protein-level MerTK activity, ligand dependence (GAS6/PROS1 unmeasured), or clinical efficacy.
- **Adverse** would establish that the pos-vs-neg effector difference is captured by **composition/load/state/donor**, redirecting the branch away from enrichment/MerTK-directed resolution therapy. It would not prove MerTK has no role in other compartments.
- **Inconclusive** would establish that the bound data are **underpowered/under-instrumented** for the discrimination and specify exactly the missing evidence (targeted ligand/protein assay; more titration donors; an independent coculture test set).
