## Why Apache cannot be called first-day or outcome-blind here

The current files establish the following and no more.

* `datasets/eicu/table-67711a86e012835e.json` (`apacheApsVar`) has columns `apacheapsvarid`, `patientunitstayid`, physiologic values, and several airway/neurologic/treatment-related fields, but `temporal_columns: []`.
* `datasets/eicu/table-b1f86cc4a8d9f2a2.json` (`apachePredVar`) likewise has `temporal_columns: []`. It contains `saps3day1`, `saps3today`, `saps3yesterday`, `day1pao2`, and `day1fio2` by name, but a column name is not a row-level time stamp or an auditable proof of the exact collection window.
* `datasets/eicu/table-754bebf64d3d9909.json` (`apachePatientResult`) contains actual and predicted mortality, length of stay, and actual ventilation outcomes. It is excluded entirely from predictors.
* The current `datasets/eicu/README.md` establishes that offsets are minutes relative to ICU admission and gives units for `vitalPeriodic`, but supplies no Apache collection-time field or Apache derivation dictionary establishing that each selected value is collected in `[0,1440]`, is available before every `D`, or is independent of future outcomes.

Thus `saps3day1`/`day1pao2` can be described as fields labelled “day 1” in a source table, but cannot be called an audited first-day covariate in this release. If “day 1” includes the first 24 hours, it can reach the `D=1440` boundary and is not strictly pre-D under the frozen clinical-time rule. `apacheApsVar` values may be extrema or derived Apache inputs rather than measurements at a known time. Excluding mortality and discharge columns is necessary but not sufficient to establish outcome-blindness: selected fields can still have undocumented derivation, timing, treatment dependence, or future-window aggregation. The compiler must use the stricter label **Apache timing/outcome status unverified**, not “outcome-blind first-day Apache.”
