This is a hypothesis, not an executed result. ## Falsifiable test

Use all 33 verified condition records with a defensible contributing-donor representation. Primary contrasts are restricted to treatment=none: MerTK/CD206-positive macrophage co-culture versus FLS-only and versus macrophage-negative co-culture, within the same FLS donor context where available. Mixed macrophage conditions are secondary composition checks; LPS and MerTK-inhibitor conditions are mechanistic context, not independent causal replicates.

For each feature, estimate the condition contrast in log-expression space after a small count offset. Use a donor-aware paired/stratified contrast or contributing-donor random-effects model, with donor/condition-preserving permutation uncertainty. Pre-specify an effect-size threshold (for example log2 fold change 0.25) and donor-level false-discovery/permutation threshold 0.05, contingent on the design supporting the estimand. Report effects, uncertainty intervals, permutation results, donor support, and negative-control behavior.

Supportive evidence would be a reproducible condition-specific signature with donor-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable negative-control signal. Adverse evidence would be no separation, reversal, or failure after donor/condition adjustment; a signal confined to one donor or feature is artifact-prone. Inconclusive evidence would include unresolved donor mapping, insufficient independent donor contexts, low detectability, or uncertainty too large to distinguish communication from composition or technical artifacts.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The transparent baseline is a donor-aware paired/stratified contrast per feature with donor-level uncertainty and condition-preserving permutation. It directly tests the prespecified condition effect and exposes which donors contribute.

A scientifically substantive alternative is a hierarchical/regularized model across the 445 measured features, retaining donor and condition effects while borrowing information across features. It could reveal coordinated pathway-level structure that single-feature contrasts lose, but cannot establish direct ligand-receptor action or causality. It is deferred unless metadata integrity and adequate feature-level signal are established. Both methods use the same inputs, contrasts, donor-stratified split/permutation, outcome, and uncertainty evaluation. No neural model is justified at this stage because the scientific uncertainty is condition specificity, not predictive performance.

The actual scientific deliverable is a fitted donor-aware contrast table and prespecified condition-specific signature test, including effects, uncertainty, permutation results, negative-control behavior, and an explicit supportive/adverse/inconclusive interpretation. No full study result is claimed.

## Missing evidence and limits

The strongest rivals are FLS donor identity or age/sex differences; macrophage composition or ratio; batch/technical effects; LPS or MerTK-inhibitor treatment rather than macrophage exposure; and targeted-panel measurement or cell-count differences. Paired donor contexts, FLS-only and macrophage-negative controls, condition-preserving permutations, and explicit treatment labels address but do not eliminate these rivals.

Expression association cannot establish direct target action, ligand-receptor causality, clinical transition, or treatment benefit. Clinical phenotype, treatment history, age completeness, and independent external validation are not assumed available.
