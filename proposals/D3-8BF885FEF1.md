## 1. What changed from the parent, and why (the two gaps this closes)
The parent established the design: decompose the RA remission-vs-treatment-resistant synovial-macrophage
difference into a **within-cell program** (H1) vs a **resolution-like abundance/composition** effect (H2),
with a depth/curated-scope artifact rival (H3), and used E-MTAB-8316 only as a **cross-sectional direction
anchor**. Two consequential gaps remained:

- **Gap 1 — circularity.** The parent's within-cell score AND its abundance fraction both derive from the
  *same* curated resolution-like module (MERTK/MRC1/CD163/FOLR2/LYVE1/TREM2/TIMP1 axis, 18 genes). A variance
  decomposition in which the "within-cell" and "composition" terms are built from the same genes is circular:
  it can "survive adjustment" trivially because the two terms are part of one construct. This episode fixes it
  by defining the **composition axis data-driven and disjoint** from the curated module.
- **Gap 2 — MERTK-dependence is untested.** The parent's MERTK link is only a cross-sectional *direction
  anchor*; a cross-sectional association cannot separate an active MERTK-dependent program from a
  bystander/composition marker. This episode upgrades it to a bounded **within-specimen perturbation test**
  (UNC106238 vs none, paired by `other_donor_id`/`donor_id`).

**Claim stages are kept separate:** the E-MTAB-8322 decomposition is **observational** (cross-sectional groups);
the E-MTAB-8316 test is **perturbational** (compound), and only *in-vitro*.

## 2. Unresolved question, importance and advance over the closest inspected works
Question: In RA, is the drug-free-remission vs treatment-resistant synovial-macrophage difference carried by a
**within-cell regulatory program** that is **MERTK-dependent** (perturbationally reducible), or is it a
**composition/abundance or donor-state** effect with no within-cell MERTK response?

- Leading claim (H1): a within-cell macrophage program separates remission from resistant AND is reducible by
  MERTK inhibition in the same specimen.
- Strongest rival (H2, bystander/composition): the cross-sectional difference reflects the **abundance** of a
  resolution-like population (donor state/selection); the within-cell residual after a *disjoint* composition
  adjustment is ~0 and MERTK inhibition does not move it.
- Second rival (H3, artifact): the signal tracks cell-number/sequencing depth or the curated module's scope.

Clinical importance: RA drug-free remission vs treatment resistance is a consequential clinical fork
(continue/withdraw therapy). If a **MERTK-dependent within-cell program** carries the axis, MERTK is a directly
testable target for sustaining remission. If instead the difference is **composition/donor-state**, then MERTK
is a marker, not a lever — a distinction that changes the next target/decision.

Advance over the closest inspected works:
- [K1] Alivernini 2020 associates MerTK+CD206+ STM subsets with remission **cross-sectionally**; it cannot
  separate composition from within-cell state or show the program is perturbationally reducible.
- [K3] Zhang 2023 (CTAPs) shows synovial **cell-abundance** classes predict response — the strongest rival that
  must be excluded before a within-cell claim is entertained, but it does not provide a within-specimen
  perturbation.
- [K2] Ye 2025 supplies the mechanism context that a *discordant or composition-dominant* result is
  informative, not merely negative.
This proposal adds the two things none of them provide: (i) a **non-circular** composition axis (data-driven,
disjoint from the curated module) and (ii) a **within-specimen paired MERTK perturbation** of the same program.

## 4. Method exploration: simple baseline vs substantive alternative (required)
**Baseline (parent).** Pseudobulk group-mean difference + curated 18-gene module score, per-donor.
- Exact inputs: the 13 primary donors' matrices → donor pseudobulk → module mean.
- Loses: all cell-level information; cannot express "hold composition fixed"; the abundance fraction is built
  from the same module → the H1-vs-H2 contrast is circular; sensitive to depth/cell-number (H3).

**Substantive alternative (proposed solver deliverable).**
(a) *Data-driven composition axis (non-circular).* On the shared gene space (intersection of the 27 donors'
`genes.tsv`; use the single donor-invariant list), within **CD68+ (and/or CD14+) macrophage cells**, select
highly variable genes with the **curated 18-gene module removed** (disjoint by construction). Run PCA →
k-nearest-neighbour graph → Leiden clustering at ≥2 resolutions; per-donor **cluster-fraction vector** is the
composition metric. Reduce it to 1–3 **composition PCs** (PCA over the fraction vector) so the composition
axis has degrees of freedom comparable to the within-cell term. Report the **gene-set overlap** between the
clustering features and the module (>0 genes ⇒ flag circularity) as a computed diagnostic.
*Why not circular:* the composition axis is defined on genes disjoint from the within-cell module score; the
within-cell term is a *separate* curated score. The decomposition therefore asks whether the module score
adds explanatory signal **beyond** an independent composition axis — it cannot survive trivially.
(b) *Within-cell program term.* Per-cell curated module score (MERTK/MRC1/CD163/FOLR2/LYVE1/TREM2/TIMP1...18).
Fit a donor-aware model: `score ~ condition + compositionPCs + log(n_umi) + (1|donor)` (Gaussian on log-CPM
module score, or a per-donor mixed/GLM). Report the variance decomposition: fraction of the
remission-vs-resistant difference attributable to (composition PCs) vs (within-cell term after composition).
- Split: **leave-one-donor-out (LOO)** over the 13 remission+resistant donors; naive/HC held out as external
  reference. Permutation null (shuffle `condition` within the donor set) for the within-cell term.
- Uncertainty: bootstrap CI (donor-level resampling) on the within-cell term and the composition adjustment.
- Fitting/estimand target: the **between-group (remission−resistant) within-cell coefficient after composition
  adjustment**, and the composition-vs-within-cell variance split.
(c) *Within-specimen MERTK perturbation (E-MTAB-8316).* Compute the same module score per sample
(`none` vs `UNC106238`). **Average repeated context measurements within `donor_id`/`condition`** (SA049 and
SA114 each contribute 2 specimens) so unit of inference = **specimen pair**, then paired difference
(UNC−none) across the 6 `other_donor_id` pairs; report Wilcoxon signed-rank and donor-level bootstrap CI
(n=4 distinct donors). Paired, within specimen, so donor/state composition is held fixed by design —
this is what the cross-sectional parent could not do.
*What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses:* whether the remission axis is a within-cell program
that survives an **independent** composition adjustment, and whether that program is **perturbationally
reducible** by MERTK inhibition — neither is expressible in pseudobulk, and the composition axis is now
causally independent of the within-cell score.
- Resource/time budget: **CPU-only**, matrices ≈1.2 GB total, minutes to a few tens of minutes
  (clustering + a handful of fits + LOO); no GPU. Planned, not measured.

## 5. Outcomes and falsification criteria (SUPPORTIVE / ADVERSE / INCONCLUSIVE)
**SUPPORTIVE (H1, MERTK-dependent within-cell program):**
- E-MTAB-8316: paired (UNC−none) module score is **reduced** with a **consistent sign in ≥5/6 specimen
  pairs** and donor-level bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0.
- AND E-MTAB-8322: the within-cell term **survives** the disjoint composition adjustment (LOO effect CI
  excluding 0, sign agreement ≥0.7 across LOO donors), i.e. the composition axis does not absorb it.

**ADVERSE (H2, bystander/composition or donor-state):**
- Within-cell term shrinks toward ~0 after adjusting for the **disjoint** composition PCs (CI includes 0,
  LOO sign inconsistent), i.e. the difference is carried by the data-driven composition axis; **and/or**
  the E-MTAB-8316 paired (UNC−none) effect CI includes 0 with no consistent sign ⇒ the program is not
  perturbationally reducible.

**INCONCLUSIVE / defer:**
- depth/`n_umi`/cell-number covariate dominates, or the within-cell term flips sign when the CD68+ gate or
  clustering resolution changes; or fewer than 3 of 6 pairs usable; or the module is widened beyond 18 genes
  and the effect disappears. An underpowered null is **not** read as refutation of H1.

Falsification statement: H1 is falsified if (i) the within-cell term is null/opposite-sign across LOO donors
**while** the independent composition axis carries the group difference, **or** (ii) the within-specimen
MERTK perturbation shows no consistent reduction.

## 6. Essential adjudication / unavailable evidence, and honest limits
- **E-MTAB-8316 is in-vitro** (LPS-stimulated monocyte/FLS-coculture context with `cell_type:"monocyte"`), an
  *in-vitro proxy* for MERTK-dependence — NOT the synovial-macrophage tissue state. The perturbational result
  constrains whether the MERTK program is reducible, not the tissue mechanism.
- **UNC106238 target engagement/selectivity is assumed, not proven**; and the drug-associated expression
  response is not proof of direct target action (may be off-target/autocrine). Requires expert review/wet-lab.
- The resolution-like "cluster" definition and any cell-type annotation are **automated design choices**, not
  clinically adjudicated identity; metadata `cell_type` labels are not verified cell identity.
- **No twelve-independent-donor claim**: 6 specimen pairs but only 4 distinct monocyte donors; inference is
  exploratory paired, averaged within donor/condition.
- Cross-sectional groups only in E-MTAB-8322 (no transition/causal claim); GSE95588 donor identity unresolved
  (direction anchor only). No cross-dataset donor joins.
- Key references are **abstract-level**; no primary-data claims are made from them.
- Claims that **cannot be checked computationally** and need expert review / wet-lab / another study:
  MERTK dependence in *synovial tissue* macrophages; UNC106238 selectivity and on-target engagement; clinical
  interpretation of the resolution-like cluster; whether the program causally sustains drug-free remission.
