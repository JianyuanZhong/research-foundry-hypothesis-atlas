# Provisional repair: E-MTAB-8873 macrophage-context hypothesis

## Proposed relationship

Among adult female RA type-B synovial-cell pseudobulks in the frozen E-MTAB-8873 targeted assay, the none-treatment contrast between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage co-culture has a reproducible direction for at least one measured feature instance, after accounting for the recorded cell count and the complete set of contributing donors. This is an association/context hypothesis, not a causal macrophage-mechanism claim.

The clinically relevant unresolved question is whether a macrophage-context signal is reproducible across donor sets or is instead explained by donor composition, cell-count differences, or a malformed donor assignment. A stable signal would justify an independent, donor-aware validation or perturbation study; it would not establish a pathway, treatment effect, or clinical benefit.

## Evidence-supported versus untested claims

The inspected frozen design and matrix support only the following: E-MTAB-8873 contains 33 records and 445 feature-instance rows; the design includes none-treatment positive and negative macrophage co-culture samples; donors are reused and sometimes contribute multiple samples; and one record has conflicting donor metadata. The assay registry states that this is a targeted 445-feature assay and that multiple contributing or reused donors require explicit dependence modeling. No expression contrast, model fit, uncertainty estimate, or biological result has been executed.

The untested claim is that a context-associated feature-instance contrast survives donor-set dependence, cell-count adjustment, and exclusion of the malformed record. The strongest possible conclusion from this experiment is a reproducible targeted-assay association. It cannot establish macrophage causality, direct target action, whole-tissue abundance, effects on unmeasured genes, or clinical utility.

## Population and temporal boundary

The primary population is exactly the none-treatment samples with condition equal to:
- `co-cultured with MerTK/CD206pos macrophages`, or
- `co-cultured with MerTK/CD206neg macrophages`.

The malformed `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA260R_Mpos` record is excluded from the primary contrast because its `donor_id` is `SA256A | SA256A, SA260R` and its source metadata contains two conflicting individual assignments. It is retained in a data-quality report. Mixed positive/negative co-culture samples, LPS samples, inhibitor samples, and not-co-cultured controls are not substitutes for this primary contrast.

The prespecified candidate pairs are:
1. `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mneg` versus `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos` (491 versus 368 cells).
2. `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mneg` versus `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mpos` (396 versus 383 cells).
3. `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA266A_Mneg` versus `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_Mpos` (1469 versus 1207 cells; macrophage donor composition differs).
4. `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` versus `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mneg` (540 versus 440 cells; macrophage donor composition differs).
5. `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` versus `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos` (1144 versus 1060 cells).

Pair 3 and pair 4 are deliberately retained to test whether a context signal survives changes in macrophage donor composition. They are not treated as independent biological replicates.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Baseline: for each feature instance, calculate Mpos-minus-Mneg on log(count + 1), with the recorded `cells` value as a prespecified offset (or an equivalent count model using `cells`). Fit a regularized linear model with condition, cell-count offset, and complete contributing-donor-set indicators. Report the pair-specific contrasts, donor-set indicators, and uncertainty.

Scientifically substantive alternative: fit a structured hierarchical model with a fixed context effect and random effects for the complete contributing-donor set (and, if estimable, FLS donor), preserving feature-instance identity and feature-level uncertainty. The alternative tests whether the direction is reproducible across donor sets and how much variation is attributable to donor-set/sampling dependence; the fixed-indicator baseline loses that decomposition.

The actual scientific deliverable is a newly fitted contrast/decomposition with uncertainty, a donor-set sensitivity report, and a feature-instance-level result table. No result is claimed here.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive: a stable context direction with uncertainty that survives donor-aware fitting, cell-count adjustment, and the stricter sensitivity subset. The result must be linked to computed outputs and uncertainty.

Adverse: the signal disappears after donor-set matching or cell-count adjustment, is driven by one donor set, reverses in the matched subset, or is not reproducible across the prespecified pairs.

Inconclusive: too few independent donor sets, unstable estimates, failed feature reconciliation, inability to separate context from treatment/batch, or insufficient information to fit the hierarchical alternative.

Neither supportive nor adverse results establish causality, mechanism, whole-tissue composition, effects on unmeasured genes, or clinical benefit. Those stronger claims require independent donor replication, complete batch/processing-order covariates, clinical adjudication of macrophage phenotype, and perturbation or another study.

## Missing evidence and limitations

The three-work inspected literature receipts and key-reference file remain incomplete at this checkpoint. The available evidence does not establish prior analysis of this exact contrast or novelty. The targeted assay does not measure absent genes. The design lacks complete batch/processing-order covariates and independent biological replication beyond the recorded donor sets. The assay registry explicitly warns against ordinary donor t-tests because donors are reused and multiple donors contribute to samples.

## Method selection and resource estimate

The fixed-indicator baseline is selected as the primary computable check because it is transparent, uses the exact available inputs, and directly tests the context contrast. The hierarchical alternative is retained because it answers the scientifically substantive question of donor-set reproducibility and dependence. A neural or more complex predictive model is deferred: with 33 pseudobulk samples, reused donors, and no independent validation, added model capacity would not resolve the primary donor-dependence uncertainty. Revisit it only if an independent cohort or perturbation experiment becomes available.

Estimated future solver workload: CPU is sufficient for the 445-by-33 matrix and small linear/hierarchical fits; no GPU is scientifically required. This estimate is a planning estimate, not an executed benchmark.
