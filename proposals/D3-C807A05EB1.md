## 1. The scientific opening

Synovial tissue macrophages (STMs) in rheumatoid arthritis separate into a MerTK+CD206+ compartment
enriched in sustained remission and a MerTK- compartment enriched in active disease. The abundance of the
first compartment — MerTK+ proportion below 47.5%, or MerTK+:MerTK- ratio below 2.5 — is proposed as a
flare-risk readout used to frame **therapy-withdrawal decisions** in patients in clinical remission [K1].
That proposal contains an unexamined mixture assumption: it treats the compartment as a bag of two kinds
of cell, so that two patients with the same MerTK+ fraction have, for biomarker purposes, the same
macrophage compartment.

Episode 1 of this campaign measured, in the donor-matched in-vitro co-culture deposit E-MTAB-8873, that
the MerTK/CD206 **sort label** is a weak descriptor of the one function the biomarker's story rests on:
the published MerTK+-versus-MerTK- direction replicated in 9/9 within-run contrasts, but with 64–88%
per-cell overlap, donor-matched effect sizes of d = 0.028 / 0.217 / 0.042 (one of four near 0.5), against a
between-macrophage-donor sigma of **0.2226** on the same axis — as large as the entire pure-arm span
(`[prior hypothesis]`, jobs [research job] / [research job]).

**That is an observation without an explanation, and the two available explanations have opposite clinical
consequences.**

- **E1 — clinically patterned state.** Within-label macrophage state is set (at least partly) by the
  patient's disease and treatment state. The in-vitro donor variance is then a shadow of clinical state,
  the "same" MerTK+ cell is not the same cell in two patients, and a proportion-only biomarker is an
  incomplete surrogate: it will misclassify a patient whose MerTK+ cells are present but transcriptionally
  active-like.
- **E2 — constitutional donor idiosyncrasy.** Within-label state varies between individuals for reasons
  orthogonal to clinical state (genetics, residency history, biopsy site). Then the proportion biomarker is
  appropriately robust in ignoring state, and the real methodological lesson is that every experiment of
  this kind needs a within-donor design, because between-donor contrasts of a handful of donors are
  dominated by a nuisance component.

Neither episode 1 nor episode 2 could separate these: both work in vitro, in a 445-feature targeted panel
or in 12 bulk libraries, and in both the donor is confounded with the donor's clinical state by design.

## 2. What the evidence already supports, distinguished from what we test

1. **Supported** [K1]: MerTK+CD206+ STM abundance is associated with sustained remission and with flare
   risk after therapy cessation, and sorted MerTK+ versus MerTK- STMs induce divergent fibroblast programs.
   Limits of that evidence: observational; the ROC cut-offs were fitted in the derivation sample.
2. **Supported** (episode-1 measurement, above): the sort label explains much less of the instructing
   signal than the published framing implies, and macrophage-donor identity explains a comparable amount.
3. **Supported** (independent synovial single-cell cohorts, see [K2]): myeloid cell *states* in synovium
   differ between high- and low-inflammation tissue.
4. **Already claimed by the source authors, therefore NOT our novelty claim [K1, abstract + Data
   Availability, inspected verbatim].** [K1]'s abstract claims "unique remission transcriptomic signatures"
   for two MerTK+ subpopulations, and its Data Availability statement states that the Supplementary Dataset
   contains "condition-specific markers of MerTKpos STM clusters identified by comparison of two clinical
   conditions, multiple conditions"; an Extended Data caption describes "scaled pseudobulk expression of
   significantly enriched pathways by each patient group within each of identified STM clusters". So the
   *existence* of within-subset, clinically contrasted expression differences in these exact cells is
   already asserted. **Discovering that such differences exist would not be a discovery.** Those tables are
   paywalled and were not inspected (PMC ID converter: "Identifier not found in PMC" for PMID 32601335;
   Europe PMC `isOpenAccess N / inEPMC N / hasSuppl N`), so we also cannot reuse them.
5. **NOT established, and the subject of this proposal — a sufficiency question, not an existence
   question:** whether the two-bin MerTK proportion is a **sufficient statistic** for the clinically
   patterned donor-level variation in the synovial macrophage compartment; how the clinical signal
   *partitions* between that proportion and within-subset state, with donor-level uncertainty; and whether
   any within-subset component survives the treatment, severity, age, ambient and depth confounds that
   [K1] gives no inspectable evidence of having addressed and that [K2] explicitly adjusts for ("age, sex
   and cell count per sample"). To the limit of a bounded, non-exhaustive scan (`work/refs/novelty.md`), no
   inspected work reports that partition, its uncertainty, or which component alone would suffice.

## 3. The unresolved claim, its rivals, and what separates them

> **H-state (E1).** In synovial tissue, the within-subset transcriptional state of macrophages carries
> remission-versus-active-RA information that is **not** recoverable from the MerTK+/MerTK- proportion
> alone, and the within-subset shift is **concordant in direction across subsets** — i.e. a program, not a
> relabelling of composition.

> **H-composition (E2).** All the donor-level clinical information at the biomarker's resolution is in the
> proportion. Conditional on composition, within-subset state is donor-idiosyncratic noise, uncorrelated
> with clinical stratum and discordant across subsets.

> **H-treatment.** Any apparent within-subset clinical patterning is drug imprinting: remission and
> treatment-resistant donors are treated, treatment-naive donors are not. Predicted observable: the state
> axis aligns with glucocorticoid/DMARD-responsive genes and separates **treated from untreated** donors
> better than it separates remission from active disease. This is the strongest rival and is given a
> dedicated internal control (§6).

> **H-resolution.** The composition/state distinction is partly definitional: at fine enough clustering
> resolution any state shift can be re-described as composition. Predicted observable: the state share of
> the signal collapses monotonically to zero as resolution increases, with no stable plateau. This is why
> the primary claim is anchored at **k = 2**, the biomarker's own resolution, where "composition" is
> exactly the published quantity and nothing else.

> **H-artefact.** Ambient RNA, dissociation stress, sequencing depth or cell-number imbalance (591 to
> 10775 cells per donor [OBSERVED, design.json]) generate an apparent state axis. Probes in §6.

### Clinical consequence of each answer
**H-state** ⇒ the withdrawal-decision readout should not be a cell-counting assay alone; the next
measurement to validate prospectively is a within-compartment state score, and a patient with an adequate
MerTK+ fraction but an active-like MerTK+ state is a candidate for *not* withdrawing therapy.
**H-composition** ⇒ the simple proportion biomarker is the right object, its failures are not explained by
hidden state, and effort should go to standardising the counting assay; additionally, the episode-1
in-vitro donor variance becomes a nuisance that mandates within-donor experimental designs.
**H-treatment confirmed** ⇒ the compartment readout in treated patients is partly a drug readout, which
would be a consequential measurement warning for any biomarker derived from treated cohorts.
None of these is a clinical conclusion from this study; all are statements about what should be measured
next. This is a 25-donor cross-section, not a withdrawal trial (§9).

**Provenance is clean.** 86 files hashed; every per-donor `matrix.mtx`/`genes.tsv`/`barcodes.tsv` matches
`assay-registry.json` `input_sha256` and the 81 README entries with **zero mismatches**; all 27

the per-donor per-gene **column sums of the per-cell matrices reproduce the prepared pseudobulk exactly**
(max absolute and relative deviation 0.0 in all 27 samples; job-…`j2_matrix`). The prepared pseudobulk is
therefore a lossless summary of the admitted per-cell data, and the solver may move between the two levels
without a reconciliation step.

**Depth and filtering.** The deposit is already cell-filtered (minimum 780 UMI, median 3,959; genes/cell
median 1,344), so the usual empty-droplet filter is a no-op and the authors' filtering decision is
inherited and not reconstructible. MT fraction is centrally low (median 0.037) with a 1–6% damaged tail
(max 0.95), so an MT cut-off is a real pre-specification, not an inheritance.

**The central measurement constraint, measured rather than assumed.** All 43 requested panel genes are
present and uniquely mapped, but *the two genes defining the published positive gate are the two sparsest*:
MERTK is detected in **8.1%** of cells (6,414 counts in 314.8 M) and MRC1 in 18.8%; CD48 14.6%, S100A12
3.9%. A per-cell binary gate on the published marker pair is **not operable**: 63.6% of cells are
positive for neither side and 4.1% for both, i.e. only 32.3% are assignable. Extending the marker sets
moves the problem (31.0% unassignable, but 20.0% double-positive). A module-score gate with an explicit
ambiguous band is operable: at band t = 0.25, 20.7% are called +, 14.1% −, 65.3% ambiguous, and the
`t = 0` convention is a trap because `delta_core` carries a point mass at −0.0787 for every cell with zero
counts in all four genes.
**Consequence adopted into the design (§6):** the primary composition readout is *not* a hard per-cell
MerTK gate; it is a **soft, score-based compartment assignment with a declared ambiguous band**, and the
claim is explicitly about a *transcriptomic surrogate* of a flow-cytometric protein gate. The published
biomarker is a protein proportion; transcript dropout is not evidence that the protein dichotomy is a
continuum, and this proposal does not make that inference.

**The dichotomy nevertheless exists as emergent, label-blind structure.** With no labels of any kind used
in fitting (2,000 pooled-variance HVGs → 50 PCs), k-means k = 2 is essentially perfectly stable
(bootstrap ARI 0.9999) and k = 9 recovers a clean MerTK+-like cluster (15,134 cells; pos_core +0.490,
neg_core −0.190, COL1A1+ 2.0%) and a clean MerTK−-like cluster (10,901 cells; neg_core +0.743, pos_ext
−0.161). But k = 9 itself is only moderately stable (ARI 0.746 ± 0.087) and **no Leiden resolution on the
scanned grid returns 9 communities** (7 → 11 → 13; the 7-community partition is stable at ARI 0.935).
The number 9 from the source publication is therefore **not a privileged label-blind resolution here**,
which is a direct, measured argument for anchoring the primary claim at k = 2 and reporting the rest as
a resolution curve (§6).

**Two measured threats to interpretation, both carried into the rivals and the sensitivity plan.**
(i) *Stromal/ambient content*: PRG4 is detected in 39.6% of cells (1.16 M counts, the second-largest total
in the panel) and COL1A1 in 22.8%; 34.2% of macrophage-marker-positive cells also carry COL1A1/PRG4
counts. Three of the nine label-blind clusters are COL1A1-dominated (0.67/0.72/0.90 positive fraction;
16,399 cells = **25.4%** of the deposit), and a 45-fold cluster-to-cluster ratio cannot be produced by
uniform ambient soup, so the deposit contains **both** diffuse ambient and real co-encapsulated stromal
content. Depth correlation is weak (Spearman 0.095), so it is not a library-size artefact.
(ii) *Technical structure masquerading as state*: two of the nine clusters are a low-depth group (median
UMI 1,574) and a high-MT group (median MT 0.085). Additionally, marker-score **differences** are nearly
depth-free while marker-score **levels, detection rates and gate proportions** are strongly
depth-dependent, and depth varies by donor — so the composition readout, not the state readout, is the
depth-fragile one.
**Not controllable with the admitted data [OBSERVED]:** no raw unfiltered droplet matrix, so SoupX /
CellBender ambient fractions are **not estimable** (only ambient markers as covariates); no hashing or
genotype demultiplexing, so doublets cannot be separated from ambient definitively; no matched
same-sample stromal reference (macrophage-only deposit); no per-library dissociation time, operator,
batch date or chemistry lot, so **batch-versus-clinical-group confounding cannot be excluded by data** and
is declared a structural limitation.

**Confound probes are measurable.** TSC22D3/GILZ is detected in 46.2% of cells (the best-powered single
glucocorticoid-response probe), IFI6 42.2% and ISG15 32.6% for interferon; FKBP5, KLF9 and PER1 are each
~13–14% and are usable only as a module or at pseudobulk level. The actual group-wise comparison is
[DEFERRED TO SOLVER] by the pre-registration rule (§7).

**Non-degeneracy of the composition readout [OBSERVED, declared partial unblinding, §7].** Across the 27
samples with donor identity withheld and order shuffled, the per-donor MerTK+ score fraction (core score,
band 0.25) has median 0.305, IQR 0.29, range 0.029–0.590, SD 0.166, with **no degenerate sample** at 0 or
1. The published 47.5% cut-off lies inside that range. The composition axis is therefore real and variable
across donors; nothing in this statistic identifies which donors are in remission.

## 6. Estimand, pre-registered decision rule, internal controls

### 6.0 Reframing forced by the rivals branch (adopted)
The question is **not** the metaphysical "composition or state?" — at fine resolution any state shift is
re-expressible as finer composition, and [K2] does exactly that, defining "expanded/depleted" as
differences in relative abundance *within a cell type*. The well-posed, decision-relevant version is
**sufficiency**: *given only the two-bin MerTK proportion — the quantity a withdrawal decision would
actually use — how much clinically patterned macrophage variation remains unexplained?* That is
resolution-independent on the predictor side, and it is what §3's H-state/H-composition become below.

### 6.1 Analysis population [OBSERVED bindings]
19 donors: remission 7 vs active 12 (naive 6 + resistant 6). Arthritis donors only, hence **female only**
(all 23 arthritis donors are female; the 4 HC are 3M/1F, ages 22–48) — the HC stratum is a **descriptive
anchor only, never a statistical control** (§10 R2). `SA149P`/`UPASA149` excluded from every inferential
arm identically; UPA is a secondary stratum. Independently measured eligibility:
`n_donors_both_bins_ge_150 = 19` of the 21 non-HC verified samples have ≥150 cells in both MerTK bins
(`work/ac9614b9fe2543169/method/out/job1_summary.json`), and 52,453 / 52,786 non-HC cells pass QC.

### 6.2 Construction of the two axes [OBSERVED feasibility, §4.3]
- **Composition axis** `p_i` = per-donor MerTK+ fraction from the **label-blind k = 2 partition** of the
  2,000-HVG PCA space (bootstrap ARI **0.9999**), which is the biomarker's own resolution. Secondary
  definition: extended MerTK/MerTK− module score with a **declared ambiguous band** (45.05% ambiguous at
  t = 0.25). The published per-cell two-marker gate is **not usable** (63.64% of cells positive for
  neither side; a sign-only rule has a point mass at −0.0787 shared by all all-zero cells and would
  manufacture a ~79% MerTK− compartment out of dropout). **Gate coverage must be reported beside gate
  proportion** at every step, because coverage is depth- hence donor-dependent while score *differences*
  are near depth-free (Spearman 0.018).
- **State axis** `s_i^{(b)}` = donor loading on the leading within-bin pseudobulk component, bin
  b ∈ {+,−}, estimated with the evaluated donor **held out** (no donor contributes to the axis used to
  score it). Donor-level pseudobulk only, per [K3]; any cell-level test is exploratory and must be shown
  against a between-donor-variance null.

### 6.3 Two pre-registered estimands
**(A) Variance-partition estimand** `π_state` = share of within-bin donor-level pseudobulk variance
attributable to clinical stratum **after conditioning on `p_i`**, versus the residual donor component.
Null distribution: **exact permutation of the clinical label across donors** (not across cells — cell-level
permutation is anticonservative per [K3]). C(19,7) = **50,388** assignments, so the exact two-sided
minimum attainable p is **3.97 × 10⁻⁵**; the full null is enumerable, not sampled.

**(B) Sufficiency estimand** `ΔAUC` = leave-one-donor-out out-of-donor discrimination of
remission-versus-active from `p_i` alone, versus from `p_i` + `s_i^{(+)}`, `s_i^{(−)}`. This is the direct
operationalisation of "is the two-bin gate a sufficient statistic?".

### 6.4 Frozen decision rule
**Supportive of H-state** requires **all four**:
1. `π_state` exact permutation p ≤ 0.05 at the primary k = 2 resolution;
2. `ΔAUC ≥ 0.10` under LODO with the lower 80% bootstrap bound > 0;
3. the stratum contrast vector survives projection of **all four nuisance scores** (§6.6) — i.e. after
   removing the GC, acute-inflammation, stress and contamination axes, cosine similarity with the
   unprojected contrast ≥ 0.5 and criterion 1 still met;
4. **cross-bin concordance measured *after* step 3.** Note the trap the rivals branch exposed: raw
   concordance of the MerTK+ and MerTK− contrasts is predicted *both* by a coordinated program (H-state)
   *and* by a sample-level technical artefact (H-artefact). Concordance is therefore admissible as program
   evidence **only** on the nuisance-projected residual, and raw concordance accompanied by nuisance-score
   correlation is scored as artefact, not program.

**Supportive of H-composition (E2)** — the useful negative: `π_state` p > 0.2 **and** `ΔAUC ≤ 0` with
upper 80% bound < 0.10. Reported as a positive result for the published biomarker, plus the methodological
consequence that this experimental family requires within-donor designs.

**Treatment-dominant (H-treatment)** — pre-registered as a *separate verdict, not a footnote*: the same
statistics computed for **naive (6) vs resistant (6)**, two equally *active* strata differing only in
treatment exposure (C(13,6) = 1,716 assignments, min p = 1.17 × 10⁻³), and for **remission (7) vs
resistant (6)**, two *treated* strata differing in disease state — the only informative treated-vs-treated
comparison available. If the naive-vs-resistant separation is ≥ the remission-vs-active separation, or if
the leading axis correlates with the GC score at |ρ| ≥ 0.6, the verdict is treatment-dominant regardless
of criterion 1.

**H-resolution** — `π_state` is reported as a **resolution curve** at k = 2 (primary), 3, 4, 6, 9 and
Leiden res 0.5/1.0/1.5. Measured context: k = 9 is **not** a privileged resolution in this deposit
(bootstrap ARI 0.746 ± 0.087; no Leiden resolution on the scanned grid returns 9 communities — 7 → 11 → 13)
and NMI(cluster, donor) rises 0.121 (k = 2) → 0.253 (k = 9) → 0.545 (Leiden 1.0), so fine clusters are
increasingly donor-structured. H-resolution is supported if `π_state` declines monotonically into the null
by k ≈ 9 with no plateau; a result that vanishes once sub-clusters are allowed is a **re-description**, and
the proposal commits to saying so.

**Inconclusive** — anything else. Pre-committed as the single most likely outcome, and it must be reported
as a bound plus the required-donor number, never as weak support.

### 6.5 Power [measured inputs, analytic calculation — Lead-computed, not a managed job]
Using the only across-donor dispersion statistic this episode was authorised to unblind (per-donor MerTK+
fraction SD **0.166**, median 0.305, IQR 0.29, range 0.029–0.590, no degenerate sample), a two-sample
donor-level comparison at 7 vs 12 with α = 0.05, power 0.80 detects a difference of **0.221 in absolute
MerTK+ fraction (≈ 1.33 SD)**. That is larger than the published 47.5% cut-off's distance from the observed
median, so **this deposit is honestly underpowered for a moderate composition effect**, let alone a state
increment. Equivalent numbers: 10 v 10 → 0.208; 15 v 25 → 0.152; 20 v 34 → 0.131. Required n per group is
≈ 63 for a 0.5-SD effect, ≈ 16 for 1.0 SD, ≈ 7 for 1.5 SD. The total SD is an upper bound on the
within-group SD, so these MDEs are conservative; and because `p_i` enters estimand (B) as a covariate, the
state increment is tested against a *residual* with a different (unmeasured) scale. **The deliverable
therefore includes the required-donor number as a first-class output**, not only a verdict.
The n-dependent measurement floor is itself measured and will be used as the weighting prior rather than
assumed away: pooled technical variance per gene fits `tech_var = 24.638 / n_cells − 0.0094` (r² = 0.931),
with per-donor cell counts spanning **591–10,775**.

### 8.1 Baseline (primary for the frozen thresholds) — CPU
Exact inputs: the 19 eligible per-donor 10x triplets (`<SAMPLE>.matrix.mtx/.genes.tsv/.barcodes.tsv`,
keyed on ENSG — 0 duplicate ENSG but 34 duplicate symbols over 68 rows [OBSERVED]). Pipeline: QC →

with age as a covariate → leading within-bin component with the scored donor held out → `π_state` and
`ΔAUC` → exact 50,388-permutation null → resolution curve → the five nuisance arms of §6.6.
Fitting target: the donor-level contrast vector and the two scalar estimands. Split: leave-one-donor-out,
whole donors only (never cells — the one discipline carried from the natural-history demonstration).
Evaluation: out-of-donor AUC with bootstrap interval, exact permutation p, resolution curve, nuisance
deltas. Budget, anchored on measurement: the binding branch's five jobs cost **457.76 s** total with 0 GPU
seconds, including the 52,453-cell × 2,000-HVG PCA/clustering/bootstrap job; the full baseline
(≈ 19 folds × 9 resolutions × 6 nuisance arms, with a cheap permutation statistic) is estimated at
**1–2 CPU-hours on 16 cores**, inside the 28,800 s solver envelope. This estimate is extrapolated from
measured single-job costs, not itself measured end-to-end.

### 8.2 What the baseline cannot see
It fixes composition first and state second, so it cannot report the joint uncertainty of the two; it
compresses each donor-bin to one point whose noise is depth-dependent (the measured floor
`24.638/n_cells − 0.0094` is applied as a weight, not modelled); and above all **a pseudobulk mean cannot
distinguish a shift of the whole within-bin distribution from a shift confined to a minority of cells.**
Those two have different clinical readings: the first says a MerTK+ compartment is globally re-programmed,
the second says it contains a distinct minority population the two-bin gate cannot count. The measured
regime makes this acute: PC1 alone holds **67.3–69.8%** of variance and ICC_bio runs **0.957–0.999**, so the
donor component dominates and a sequential point estimate is fragile exactly where the answer lives.

### 8.3 Substantive alternative (confirmatory arm) — donor-aware latent-variable model, GPU
A cell-level generative latent-variable model (scVI/MrVI-style) with **donor as an explicit nuisance
covariate** and a composition-free per-sample representation, fitted on the same 19 donors' cells, then
compared across the clinical strata in latent space with posterior uncertainty. Fitting target: the
per-donor cell-state distribution (not its mean). It reveals three things the baseline loses: (i) whole-
distribution versus minority-subpopulation shift; (ii) calibrated donor-level uncertainty under the
measured n-dependent noise; (iii) a **composition-invariant sample embedding**, which is the direct
technical answer to H-resolution rather than a sensitivity curve. Identifiability at 19–25 donors is **a
deliverable, not an assumption**: the arm must report whether the clinical latent is separable from the
donor latent under donor-label permutation, and a failure to separate is a reportable result.
**Device decision and its evidence:** 52,453 cells × 2,000 genes is small, so a *single* fit does not need
an A100; the GPU case is the **repetition** — 19 LODO folds × resolution and nuisance arms × permutation
refits. Requested: 1 GPU, `ehr-discovery-gpu:[internal campaign]`. Two honest caveats: (1) ordinary shells here
receive `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""`, so device feasibility can only be established by an allocated
`job_submit(gpus=1)` probe, which **was not run this episode — the GPU estimate is unverified**;
(2) the image dependency is real and measured: `scanpy`, `igraph`, `leidenalg` are **absent** from the
cached image, `pip install` succeeds but the packages are not importable in the installing process, and a
second job with `PYTHONPATH=<branch>/.local/lib/python3.12/site-packages` was required [OBSERVED, binding
branch]. `scvi-tools` presence is likewise unverified. This is an infrastructure dependency for the
compiler, reported separately from scientific feasibility.

### 8.4 Selection
**Baseline is primary** for the frozen thresholds, because at 19 donors its null is *exact* and its estimand
is interpretable without a model-selection argument; the latent-variable arm is **required as a
confirmatory arm**, and **disagreement between the two arms is itself a reportable outcome** (it would
localise the signal to a cell minority invisible to pseudobulk). Neither is selected for being simple or
for being learned.

### 8.5 Alternative considered and not selected, with its revisit trigger
**Neighbourhood differential-abundance testing (Milo / scCODA / CNA as in [K2]).** Scientifically apt —
it is the state of the art for "which sub-population changed" — but it answers the abundance question at a
resolution this deposit was **measured** not to support: k = 9 bootstrap ARI 0.746 ± 0.087, no Leiden
resolution returning 9 communities, one k = 9 cluster drawing 56.6% of its cells from a single donor, and
NMI(cluster, donor) = 0.545 at Leiden 1.0. At 19 donors its neighbourhoods would be donor-defined, so its
output would be unfalsifiable here. **Revisit trigger:** a synovial deposit with ≥ 50 donors, or raw
droplet matrices permitting ambient correction, or per-library batch metadata — any one of which would make
the fine-resolution arm interpretable. Also not selected: cross-deposit projection onto E-MTAB-8873's
445-feature axis, kept as an optional descriptive arm only (different platform, different donors, targeted
panel — a null projection says nothing about absent genes).

## 9. What each outcome does and does not establish
- **Supportive (H-state).** Establishes that in synovial tissue the macrophage compartment carries
  remission-associated information beyond the MerTK+ proportion, and names the program. It does **not**
  establish that the state is causal for flare, that it is modifiable, or that a state readout would
  outperform the proportion prospectively; that requires a longitudinal withdrawal cohort with biopsies,
  which exists in no admitted source.
- **Adverse (H-composition).** A useful negative: it would support the published biomarker's mixture
  assumption at its own resolution and would redirect the episode-1 donor-variance observation to a
  constitutional/technical explanation, with the concrete methodological consequence that within-donor
  designs are mandatory in this experimental family. Bounded by power (§6) — an imprecise null is a bound,
  not a refutation.
- **Inconclusive.** Pre-committed as a likely single outcome at this donor count; yields a bound plus the
  required-donor calculation, and must not be reported as support for either side.
- **Treatment-dominant.** Also useful: it would mean compartment readouts derived from treated cohorts are
  partly drug readouts, and that the biomarker should be validated in treatment-naive remission.

**Not computationally checkable by any verifier**, requiring clinical adjudication or new data: whether any
state difference precedes or follows flare; whether the 47.5%/2.5 cut-offs transport outside their
derivation sample; whether a state score would change a withdrawal decision; the true drug exposure of each
donor (the design file records `treatment: null` for every sample [OBSERVED] — the stratum label is the only
treatment information we have, which is itself a limitation of the treatment-confound analysis).

## 10. Rival explanations carried
Full text in `work/refs/rivals.md`; each rival states its predicted observable, whether it is adjudicable
with the admitted data, and its probe. Ranked by threat:

**R1 Treatment / glucocorticoid imprinting — STRONGEST, and not adjustable away.** Treatment is
near-collinear with the primary contrast *by construction*: remission and resistant donors are on therapy,
naive donors are not. Worse, it attacks the biomarker's own measurement basis — an inspected 2024 Nat
Commun source states verbatim that "CD206, CD163, MerTK — known to be highly expressed in M2 — are
reported GR transcriptional targets", and that GILZ/TSC22D3 "enhances efferocytosis by macrophages"
(species limit: mouse BMDM / DSS colitis, `work/refs` sha256 6964aa57…); a 2026 Sci Rep source reports
that glucocorticoid exposure of healthy-donor monocytes polarised them toward a "C1Q+ CD163+" subtype
(context limit: adrenocortical carcinoma, in-vitro monocyte-derived, sha256 5d2016a6…). So "high MerTK+
fraction in remission" and "recently steroid-exposed" are **not separated by these data**. Carried as the
dedicated §6.4 treatment-dominant verdict and the §6.6(a) GC arm, with the naive-vs-resistant and
remission-vs-resistant internal contrasts. Not refuted by adjustment — a steroid-induced reparative pool
could itself be the mechanism of durable remission, and that ambiguity is reported, not resolved.

**R5 Depth and cell-number imbalance — fully testable and must be.** 591–10,775 cells per donor (18-fold).
Measured noise floor `tech_var = 24.638/n_cells − 0.0094` (r² = 0.931). Carried as §6.6(e) cell-count-matched
subsampling, donor-level pseudobulk per [K3], and mandatory reporting of gate **coverage** beside gate
proportion (coverage is depth-dependent; score differences are not, Spearman 0.018).

**R4 Ambient RNA and dissociation stress — measured to be large.** 34.16% of macrophage-marker-positive
cells also carry COL1A1/PRG4; 25.4% of cells sit in three COL1A1-dominated label-blind clusters (45-fold
enrichment over clean clusters, so not uniform ambient). Carried as §6.6(c)/(d), run with and without those
clusters. **Structurally uncorrectable:** no raw droplet matrix exists in any admitted source, so no
SoupX/CellBender correction is possible; no hashing or genotyping, so doublets are unresolvable.

**R2 Age and sex — makes the HC arm unusable as a control.** All 23 arthritis donors female 42–77; HC 3/4
male, 22–48. Sex is structurally inseparable across the healthy boundary; age varies within arthritis
(42–77) and enters as a donor-level covariate. Primary analysis restricted to the female arthritis donors;
HC descriptive only; XIST/Y-gene scoring to confirm the confound empirically.

**R6 The composition/state distinction is partly definitional — not falsifiable as originally posed.**
[K2] performs its within-cell-type analysis as *relative abundance* of neighbourhoods "within a cell type",
which is inspected evidence that the distinction is resolution-dependent. Answered by the §6.0 reframing to
sufficiency plus the §6.4 resolution curve, with the explicit commitment that a result vanishing once
sub-clusters are allowed is a re-description.

**R7 Severity / reverse causation — caps the claim.** The axis may simply track current inflammatory load
(IL1B, TNF, CXCL9/10/11, S100A8/A9, SPP1, IFN). With one cross-sectional biopsy there is **no temporal
ordering**, so "state maintains remission" and "remission produces state" are observationally
indistinguishable here. Carried as §6.6(b) and as a hard scope limit: **no flare outcome exists in
E-MTAB-8322** — [K1]'s cut-offs came from a separate n = 22 flow cohort (11 flared / 11 did not), so every
withdrawal-relevant statement is a cross-cohort extrapolation and is labelled as one. The published
47.5% / ratio-2.5 values are **second-hand** from an earlier episode's excerpt; the refs branch could not
re-recover them and they are not presented as directly inspected here.

**R8 Donor identity and relatedness — this *is* the E2 null, plus a data hazard.** If donor explains far
more within-bin variation than stratum and donor loadings do not cluster by stratum beyond chance, E2 holds
and the published proportion biomarker is appropriately robust — a useful positive, not a failure.
Measured context: ICC_bio 0.957–0.999, PC1 67.3–69.8%. Hazard: `SA149P`/`UPASA149`
(`possible_related_or_repeated_donor_exclude_inference`) excluded identically from every inferential arm,
included only as a labelled sensitivity arm, never silently pooled.

**R3 Disease duration and biopsy joint site — NOT ADJUDICABLE [OBSERVED, Lead-verified].** I enumerated
every field of all 27 `E-MTAB-8322.design.json` records: `sample_id, donor_id, donor_status,
other_donor_id, age, sex, tissue, cell_type, condition, treatment, source_metadata, source_name, cells`,
and every `source_metadata` key (organism/organism part/age/sex/developmental stage/cell type/individual/
disease/disease staging/response to treatment). **There is no disease duration, no joint site, no biopsy
method, no drug name, dose or timing, and no library/batch field.** `treatment` is `null` in all 27 records;
`Characteristics[response to treatment]` merely restates the stratum ("naïve to treatment" 6 / "remission" 7
/ "resistant to treatment" 6 / "undifferentiated arthritis" 4 / "not applicable" 4). Consequence: duration,
anatomy and processing batch **cannot be excluded by these data at all**. This is declared an unresolved
limitation, not a deferred step. The only available partial proxy is a lining/sublining signature
(CD55/PRG4/HAS1, LYVE1, TIMD4, FOLR2) scored per donor, reported as a proxy and not as site metadata.

## 12. Honest limits
1. **Power.** 7 vs 12 donors. Measured-input MDE = 0.221 absolute MerTK+ fraction (≈1.33 SD) at 80% power
   (§6.5). The honest modal outcome is *inconclusive*, delivered as a bound plus a required-donor number
   (≈16 per group for a 1.0-SD effect, ≈63 for 0.5 SD). An imprecise null is not a refutation.
2. **Treatment is recorded only as a stratum label** — `treatment: null` in all 27 records; no drug, dose,
   duration or time-since-dose. The GC confound can be *characterised* but never adjusted out (R1).
3. **No duration, joint site, biopsy method or batch field exists** (R3, Lead-verified field enumeration).
   Processing-batch/calendar-period confounding with stratum cannot be excluded by these data.
4. **HC stratum is age- and sex-confounded** (3/4 male, 22–48, vs all-female arthritis 42–77): descriptive
   anchor only.
5. **Cross-sectional, one biopsy per donor, no time axis** — no transition, trajectory or treatment-response
   claim is admissible, and severity-versus-mechanism is not separable (R7).
6. **No flare outcome in this deposit.** Any withdrawal-relevant reading is a cross-cohort extrapolation to
   [K1]'s separate n = 22 flow cohort, and the 47.5%/2.5 cut-offs are cited second-hand.
7. **Macrophage-only deposit** — the prepared pseudobulk and the per-cell matrices contain measured
   macrophages only, so no fibroblast, T/B-cell or whole-tissue component is available; yet 25.4% of cells
   are in COL1A1-dominated clusters, so the label itself is imperfect (R4).
8. **No raw droplet matrix, no hashing, no genotyping** ⇒ ambient fraction not estimable, doublets not
   resolvable, and the SA149P/UPASA149 relatedness **cannot be resolved by data** — only handled by
   consistent exclusion.
9. **The published per-cell two-marker gate is not reproducible at transcript level** (63.64% unassignable).
   Everything rests on a label-blind k = 2 surrogate that is highly stable (ARI 0.9999) but is *our*
   construct, not the clinical flow gate; the correspondence between them is **unverified and unverifiable
   in these data**, and that is a first-order limitation on transporting any result to the biomarker.
10. **Composition versus state is resolution-dependent by construction** (R6); only the sufficiency
    formulation of §6.0 is well-posed.
11. **One remaining provenance item is unclosed:** an independent re-hash job for `catalog.json` was still
    queued at branch close under compute concurrency 1. The harness's own input record matches the
    README-declared value `7cf76c53…baa0`, but that is harness-recorded, not an independently completed
    job result. All 81 triplet files *were* independently re-hashed with 0 mismatches.
12. **Infrastructure, not science:** `scanpy`/`igraph`/`leidenalg` absent from the cached image and
    importable only via an explicit `PYTHONPATH`; `scvi-tools` presence and the single-GPU timing of §8.3
