# Episode 10: estimand-correct overlap test of the pH-conditioned SpO2 blind spot

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and advance

When released monitor SpO2 is 96–97%, is acidemia associated with at least a 10-percentage-point higher probability of an observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg among observations sharing the same hospital and similar measured oxygenation context, or is the larger full-cohort association not reproduced in the region of measured overlap?

This matters because a large, reproducible blind spot would justify a prospective synchronized study of pH-aware oxygen assessment. A smaller or imprecise difference would argue against escalating a concurrent association into extra blood-gas testing or oxygen-titration decision support. This experiment does not test whether changing pH, SpO2, FiO2, or oxygen therapy changes outcomes.

The inspected full-text XML of Delgado et al. (Antioxidants 2026; DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712) describes a retrospective single-tertiary-center study of 21,406 adults and 717,064 paired measurements. It reports higher PaO2 in acidotic states at comparable SpO2, modification by FiO2, and generally physiologic PaO2 at SpO2 96–97%. That evidence supports physiologic plausibility and a single-center association. It does not establish the EICU +10-point contrast, robustness to measured case mix, multi-hospital transportability, a pre-draw alert, causality, toxicity, or treatment benefit.

The strongest claim supported before the proposed inferential execution is narrower: the exact EICU cohort and overlap estimand are computable. Complete-source audit reproduces 705 eligible observations and 237 overlap observations. Correct symmetric weights give a descriptive acidemic risk 6.779%, normal risk 3.824%, and RD +2.955 points, with ESS 87.7 and 105.7. These are reference-execution/QC outputs, not confirmatory clinical evidence. The unresolved hypothesis is:

**Primary hypothesis:** in the prespecified measured-overlap population, the symmetric overlap-weighted acidemic-minus-normal RD for observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg is at least +10 percentage points.

A stronger conjunctive interpretation additionally requires the inherited all-705-observation standardized RD to clear +10 points and the inherited multi-hospital transportability gates. The substantive episode-10 advance is to replace the parent's mistakenly computed acidemic-population ATT diagnostic (normal ESS 38.5) with its actually specified symmetric estimand, freeze the resampling target, and make sparse ten-event behavior non-negotiably inconclusive unless the interval truly clears the clinical threshold.

## Outcome, overlap population, estimands, and baselines

The primary outcome is observed PaO2 >120 mm Hg. Secondary outcomes are PaO2 >150, continuous PaO2, and PaO2 <60; none can rescue a failed primary.

- `floor(FiO2_percent/10)`;
- `floor(PaCO2/20)`, with missing PaCO2 a separate level; and
- `floor(draw/1440)` for ICU day 0, 1, or 2.

Do not concatenate unescaped values to define identity. Retain cells with at least one observation from each pH group. Reconcile 237 rows, 197 people, 198 stays, 57 cells, and 9 hospitals: 110 acidemic and 127 normal observations with ten QC events (6 and 4). The nine sites are 122, 183, 184, 318, 394, 420, 443, 458, and 459.

For cell s with counts n1s and n0s, assign each acidemic row weight n0s/(n1s+n0s) and each normal row n1s/(n1s+n0s). Normalize within exposure group. The primary estimand is the weighted observed acidemic risk minus weighted observed normal risk in the population whose measured site/oxygenation context overlaps. It is not the 705-row estimand and is not causal.

Report group weight sums, risks, RD, cell counts, effective sample sizes `(sum w)^2/sum(w^2)`, maximum normalized row weights, each site's fraction of total weight, original-group exclusion fractions, and weighted summaries of exact FiO2, PaCO2/missingness, draw, age, sex, unit type, and admission source. Do not fit a high-dimensional outcome model to ten events.

Baselines and fixed comparators are:

- unweighted risks/RD in the 237 rows;
- the inherited acidemic-population ATT weighting (acid=1; normal=n1s/n0s), explicitly secondary;

The B2 RD is a descriptive concurrent standardization, not an intervention.

## Uncertainty and transportability

For the primary overlap interval use 5,000 deterministic hierarchical bootstrap replicates, seed 20260909, conditional on the frozen 237-row overlap source:

2. within each sampled hospital copy, sample its `uniquepid` clusters with replacement, with draw count equal to that hospital's observed person count, carrying all rows;
3. assign every hospital and person copy a new ID;
4. rebuild typed cells using the new hospital-copy ID, discard replicate cells lacking one group, recompute symmetric weights and RD.

More than 10% undefined replicates, either observed group ESS <50, one original site >50% of total observed overlap weight, or sign reversal after deleting any site with at least ten overlap rows forces an inconclusive primary interpretation regardless of the nominal interval.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive for the primary measured-overlap magnitude** requires the overlap RD lower 95% bound >=+10 points and every overlap diagnostic to pass. The strongest allowed primary conclusion is: “Within this retrospective construction, a >=10-point concurrent pH-conditioned difference persisted in the prespecified measured site/oxygenation overlap population.” Do not call it physiologic proof.

**Full support** additionally requires the B2 standardized RD lower bound >=+10 and every multi-hospital gate. Only then may the result be described as sufficiently reproducible to justify prospective synchronized validation.

**Adverse to +10** means the overlap upper bound <+10. An upper bound <0 supports an opposite association in this estimand. A precise +6-point RD is adverse to the material threshold but supports a smaller association. If B2 supports while overlap is adverse, conclude that the full-cohort association did not survive this measured-overlap challenge; do not attribute the discrepancy uniquely to confounding because target populations differ.

**Inconclusive** includes an interval containing +10; failed reconciliation; >10% bootstrap failure; ESS/site-concentration failure; sign reversal under required person, timing, band, or site-deletion sensitivities; or full-cohort support with overlap imprecision. A point estimate above +10 with interval -2 to +22 is inconclusive. Pooled support with failed site gates is not transportability support.

The corrected 5,000-replicate reference execution produced overlap RD +2.955 points with 95% percentile interval -7.875 to +15.535, 5,000/5,000 defined replicates, ESS 87.7/105.7, maximum site weight 45.3%, and no leave-site-out sign reversal. Under the frozen rule, that computed result is **inconclusive** about +10—not supportive and not adverse—because the interval contains +10. It is still retrospective snapshot evidence, not clinical truth. The inherited prior-pH alert remains computationally infeasible (24 availability-safe 5–60-minute rows and no balanced hospital), and self-control remains unresolved (56 exposure-switching people, eight events, six people discordant on exposure and outcome, zero outcome-discordant tight pairs). Concurrent results cannot rescue either branch.
