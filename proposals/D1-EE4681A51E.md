## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, surveillance couples
serial serum AFP with scheduled cross-sectional imaging. The parent branch asks whether the
within-patient post-LDT AFP slope predicts time to local progression, and whether that hazard
differs by first modality; the parent's child adds the imaging-negative conditioning. Both are
fit with a **cause-specific Cox** model in which metastasis / re-treatment is a competing event.

The unresolved question this child targets is an **estimand question**: when the post-LDT AFP
slope is elevated, *which* downstream event does it flag? Serum AFP reflects tumour biology
broadly, and after LDT a rising slope may anticipate **local, ablatable/retreatable progression**
(a surveillance/local-escalation decision) *or* **systemic progression / new metastasis / early
re-treatment** (a different decision: staging and systemic therapy). A cause-specific Cox hazard
for local progression answers only "does the slope change the local-progression *rate among those
still at risk*", and it deliberately ignores that a large competing hazard of metastasis/re-treatment
removes patients from the local-progression risk set. The clinical decision (intensify local
surveillance and retreat vs. restage for systemic therapy) depends on the **absolute** risk of
each event type, i.e. the cause-specific **cumulative incidence function (CIF)**, which the parent
estimand does not report. This is a genuine opening: the direction of the slope's absolute-risk
attribution has, to our knowledge, not been separated in this design, and the two competing
explanations make opposite predictions for the CIF while being nearly indistinguishable in a
cause-specific hazard.

Clinical importance: if the slope's absolute-risk signal is carried mainly by the competing
metastasis/re-treatment event, then acting on a rising AFP as a *local-progression* trigger would
be misdirected — the actionable output would be earlier restaging, not intensified local imaging.

## 2. Closest inspected prior work and the gap

Three works were inspected (abstract-level; frozen provider responses attached, see
`key-references.json` and `sources/`):

- **[K1]** Gu et al. 2026 (ctDNA/MRD in HCC recurrence surveillance) — states that "radiologic
  relapse may lag behind biological recurrence", i.e. blood-based serial markers can carry
  information before imaging. This **supports** testing serial AFP-slope information but does
  **not** establish which event type (local vs systemic) a rising marker precedes.
- **[K2]** Yuza & Pawlik 2026 (ICI conversion framework) — positions "tumour-marker kinetics,
  imaging response, liver function … as inputs into candidacy rather than as response measures
  alone", i.e. a serial marker is used to drive a **decision**, not merely to score response.
  This motivates making the slope's decision target explicit, and shows the field currently
  treats marker kinetics as a single undifferentiated decision input.
- **[K3]** Liu et al. 2026 (pretransplant AGR, two-centre cohort) — analyses time to recurrence
  "with death before recurrence as a competing event" using **Fine–Gray**; a concrete demonstration
  that HCC recurrence endpoints are analysed in a competing-risks framework when absolute risk
  matters. It is **not** about AFP kinetics and does not resolve the local-vs-competing attribution.

Gap: no inspected work separates whether a serial post-LDT marker's absolute-risk signal is
attributable to the local-progression event or to the competing systemic/re-treatment event. The
substantive advance over [K1]/[K2]/[K3] is the pre-specified **CIF decomposition** of the parent's
own slope, not a new marker.

## 3. Inherited design (verbatim from parent `[prior hypothesis]`)

- **Population**: patients with an HCC diagnosis AND >=1 liver-directed procedure
  (TACE / resection / ablation). Index = earliest qualifying procedure.
- **Landmark**: t* = index + 180 d. AFP and ALBI slopes are fit ONLY on `(index, t*]`.
- **Same-draw triples**: AFP + albumin + total bilirubin within +/-2 d on `检验时间`;
  `ALBI = 0.66*log10(bilirubin) - 0.085*albumin`.

  as the competing event; censor via `出院时间` (last discharge).
- **Primary model**: landmark cause-specific Cox for the per-1-SD AFP slope, adjusted for the
  parent's surveillance-intensity covariates; ALBI-slope used as a negative control; four rivals
  each mapped to a distinct test; conjunctive falsification. No survival claim.

  (IMGneg stratum, defined from `检查` over [t*-45 d, t*+45 d]).

## 4. The ONE added element (this child): competing-risks estimand reframing

Keep the parent fit `M0` (cause-specific Cox for local progression). Add `M6`:

1. Estimate the **cause-specific** hazard ratio (HR_cs) for the per-1-SD AFP slope for the local
   event (baseline) AND separately for the competing event (metastasis/re-treatment), from the
   same adjusted landmark model.
2. Estimate the **subdistribution hazard ratio (HR_sd, Fine–Gray)** for the local event, and the
   **CIF difference** at a pre-specified horizon (12 months post-landmark) between the top vs
   bottom quartiles of the AFP slope.
3. **Attribution test**: a pre-specified contrast of the slope's coefficient for the local event
   vs the competing event (seemingly-unrelated/stacked cause-specific fit; 1-df Wald/LRT).

Method comparison (recorded before selection):
- **M0** (baseline): parent cause-specific Cox + modality interaction. Recovers: the relative
  local-progression hazard; **loses** the absolute risk and any attribution across event types.
- **M6** (substantive alternative): Fine–Gray subdistribution + CIF decomposition + attribution
  contrast. Reveals whether the slope's absolute-risk signal sits on local progression
  (→ local surveillance/retreatment decision) or on the competing systemic event
  (→ restaging/systemic-therapy decision) — information M0's estimand structurally cannot expose.
- Deferred: joint shared-parameter AFP/ALBI model (kept deferred from the parent branch with the
  same revisit condition: only if the two-stage slope's measurement-error correction would change
  the sign of the attribution contrast). Reason for not selecting it here: it addresses estimation
  error, not the estimand gap this child is built to resolve.
- Resource: pure tabular fitting (`lifelines`/manual Fine–Gray or `cmprsk`-equivalent), CPU,
  minutes-scale; GPU not required.

## 6. Branches and falsification (pre-specified)

- **Supportive**: HR_cs(local) > 1 with 95% CI excluding 1, AND HR_sd(local) > 1, AND the
  slope's local-vs-competing attribution contrast is nonsignificant (p >= 0.05) i.e. the signal is
  not preferentially systemic, AND >=75 local events, AND the 12-month CIF difference is directionally
  consistent with HR_sd. → interpretable as a slope that flags local, actionable progression.
- **Adverse (R_competing)**: HR_cs(local) ~ 1 while HR_cs(competing) > 1, OR the attribution
  contrast significant (p < 0.05) with the competing-event coefficient larger → the slope's
  absolute-risk signal is carried by the systemic/re-treatment event; a rising AFP is **not** a
  local-progression trigger.
- **Inconclusive**: <75 local events, or the competing-event count is too small to identify the
  attribution contrast, or censoring/last-contact so incomplete that the CIF is unidentifiable.
  Pre-declared: no heterogeneity/attribution claim is made below these thresholds.
- **Precise-null**: HR_cs(local) and HR_sd(local) both within +/-0.10 of 1 with a tight CI and a
  flat ALBI negative control.

Conjunctive falsification: the branch is decided by the joint pattern (HR_cs, HR_sd, attribution
contrast, CIF difference), not by any single p-value. Falsification of the parent claim would
require the attribution contrast to be significant *against* the local-progression reading
(R_competing) — i.e. this child can **overturn**, not merely confirm, the parent's interpretation.

## 7. Honest limits

- The local-progression endpoint is a **lexical** rule over `检查所见`/`检查诊断`; it requires
  radiologist mRECIST adjudication and is not a validated outcome. This limit is inherited, not
  resolved here.
- Competing-event definition (metastasis / re-treatment) is proxy-based; re-treatment may be
  driven by the slope itself (informative), inflating the competing hazard — a pre-declared
  sensitivity removes re-treatment from the competing definition.
- Single institution; `出院时间` is last-discharge, so CIF beyond the observed window is
  extrapolation and is not reported as a survival claim.
- No outcome, HR, CIF or attribution estimate is computed in discovery; those are the solver's
  first outputs. The three key works are abstract-only.
