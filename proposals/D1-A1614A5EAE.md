# Admission-anchored documentation transportability of early physiology in HCC-coded care

## Decision and substantive successor hypothesis

This is a substantive scientific child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent’s informative laboratory contrast is still vulnerable because its main B1-minus-B0 estimand is learned only after an admission reaches R12 (no early procedure, discharge or unresolved procedure timing). The same documentary label can influence that selection, and A/P/T/U are lexical/documentation labels without clinical validation.

This child changes the primary estimand to an admission-anchored, all-eligible-admission dynamic competing-state forecast. It retains the parent’s 12-hour physiology window and 24–72-hour clinical horizon as a secondary landmark transport estimand, but no longer interprets that conditional contrast as the population-level result.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitting the following on a locked patient split:

1. a frozen HCC eligibility rule and four non-clinical documentation encodings, with overlap, sparse-stratum and Stage-0 audits;
2. an admission-anchored discrete-time model for every eligible admission, using only information available before each 2-hour prediction interval;
3. a matched transparent baseline, early-laboratory model and irregular latent hepatic/renal trajectory alternative on the identical split and terminal target;
4. a neutral heterogeneity functional and a selection-transport sensitivity analysis, with label-robustness bounds;
5. locked predictions, calibration, cumulative-incidence contrasts and patient-clustered uncertainty; and
6. timestamp, observation-process, terminal-label, lexical-encoding and Stage-0-selection falsifications plus a claim-to-evidence table.

The primary falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission, the incremental predictive value of assay-specific early laboratory history for the 72-hour first recorded-care terminal state is heterogeneous across documentary strata after admission-level standardization, and that heterogeneity is stable across prespecified label encodings. The directional A-versus-U/T expectation is secondary: if it exists, the encounter-context coding should show a larger early-lab increment than residual/ambiguous or treatment/status coding, but this direction is not asserted when the lexical encoding changes it.

The null is no robust heterogeneity: the B1-minus-B0 contrast is equal within uncertainty across label encodings and strata after accounting for the all-admission outcome process. A result that is heterogeneous only after R12 conditioning is evidence of selection, not transportable phenotype heterogeneity.

This is a documentation-transportability question, not a claim that A is active HCC, P is pathology-confirmed, T is inactive disease, or U is a biological subgroup.

## What is known and what is unresolved

The strongest available evidence is documentary and temporal. The HCC diagnosis source has 1,810,646 rows, 105,044 unique encounter keys and 105,044 keys matching the parent’s frozen HCC-core lexical rule. The parent’s full-source audit found 105,044 encounters, 101,457 valid admissions, 101,440 nonnegative stays, 338,040 procedure rows (322,166 parseable starts; 15,874 missing/invalid starts), 28,159,928 nonmissing numeric lab results, and 3,804,333 lab rows joined in hours 0–12. Its provisional documentary counts were A=73,415, P=696, T=2,134 and U=28,799 among HCC-core keys. These are feasibility facts, not locked child results.

What remains unknown is whether early assay-specific laboratory history carries a reproducible, clinically relevant recorded-care signal across documentation strata once every eligible admission—including early discharge, early procedure and unresolved timing—is represented. No available table establishes active HCC, recurrence, stage, tumor burden, treatment indication, receipt, response or benefit. Diagnosis rows have no native time. Narrative labels in diagnosis, documents, pathology and examinations are not validated context-aware adjudication.

## Population, time and terminal estimand

Use HCC snapshot `[source checksum]`, catalog `[internal dataset path]` (catalog [source checksum]). All sources are ordinary read-only CSV files; there are no archive members.

Eligibility is deterministic and ordered:

- normalize whitespace/encoding in `患者主索引` and `就诊号`;
- join `encounters` to `diagnoses` on those two columns;
- require age >=18, parseable native `入院时间` and `出院时间`, nonnegative stay, admission in [2011-01-01, 2026-01-01), and discharge before 2026-01-01;
- apply the pre-locked HCC-core lexical rule (the child must print the exact regular expression and every matched-string audit);
- retain the earliest eligible encounter per `患者主索引`, ordered by native `入院时间` then `就诊号`; do not substitute a later admission for an invalid earlier one;
- exclude names, identity numbers, phone, insurance/card number and hospitalization number from all features.

Let t0 be native `入院时间`. For each admission, construct the first terminal state through t0+72 hours:

- P: first procedure row with parseable native `开始时间`, `开始时间 >= t0`, before native `出院时间`, and no earlier terminal state;
- D: native discharge before a valid P and within 72 hours;
- U: a linked procedure row has missing/invalid `开始时间) that could alter the ordering of P or D; this is an unresolved documentation state, not a timed clinical event;
- N: no P, D or U by 72 hours.

For equal timestamps, retain all rows in the audit and use deterministic row order only for reproducibility; report ties. A missing/invalid procedure time never becomes N. The solver must emit conservative and optimistic U bounds: U is allowed to precede the first valid event in the conservative construction and is allowed after the observed valid event/deadline in the optimistic construction. The primary report carries both bounds and does not claim U has a clinical time.

The primary model is admission-anchored. Divide [0,72) into 2-hour half-open intervals. At each interval, fit/score the hazard of the first P, D or U terminal state using only data available before the interval start. For U admissions, all lab-derived predictors after t0 are suppressed because the unknown procedure could precede them. No event, discharge, procedure, or lab row after an interval start may be used to predict that interval. The primary all-admission estimand is the standardized B1-minus-B0 difference in 72-hour CIF for P, with the full P/D/U/N terminal vector and proper multiclass scores also reported. This is an observed-recorded-care forecast estimand, not a treatment effect.

For the parent-comparable question, estimate a secondary 12-hour landmark estimand only among R12 (no P/D/U before 12 hours and still inpatient), with labs strictly in [0,12) and P/D/U outcomes in [24,72). Transport this conditional contrast to the all-eligible-admission covariate distribution using a stabilized inverse-probability-of-R12 model based only on pre-admission covariates and documentation encoding. Report overlap, weight truncation and a Rosenbaum-style selection-sensitivity envelope (Gamma 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0). If positivity or effective sample size fails, the transported estimand is inconclusive rather than a claim about all admissions. The admission-anchored dynamic result remains primary.

## Documentation channels and robust heterogeneity estimand

Do not call A/P/T/U phenotypes. Derive the parent’s frozen precedence labels from HCC-core `诊断名称` and `诊断类型` only, with exact matched strings printed:

- A: type in `入院诊断`, `主治医生诊断` or `出院诊断), with no treatment/post-treatment modifier and no `未知`, `疑似`, `待查` or `考虑` marker;
- P: type `病理诊断`, unless assigned by the explicit conflict rule;
- T: treatment/status modifier in the diagnosis name, unless assigned P;
- U: every remaining HCC-core encounter, including conflicts, blanks, uncertainty and other types.

To make the estimand robust to lexical validity, the solver must fit/report the same analysis under four frozen encodings: (L1) the full precedence A/P/T/U rule; (L2) context-type versus non-context; (L3) modifier/status versus non-modifier; and (L4) diagnosis-type-only versus diagnosis-name-modifier-only. L2–L4 are sensitivity encodings, not better labels. The exact four-way overlaps and every sparse cell are outputs.

For encoding r and label cell s, let Δ(r,s) be the B1-minus-B0 72-hour P CIF contrast after standardizing to the same all-eligible-admission distribution of age, sex, department, era and admission time-of-day. The primary heterogeneity functional is the label-encoding-stability range H = max_{r,s} Δ(r,s) - min_{r,s} Δ(r,s), accompanied by an omnibus interaction test and the A-versus-U/T directional contrast only under L1. Use a hierarchical varying-slope model with partial pooling across s as the inferential shrinkage model, but publish cell-specific estimates and effective support. Do not collapse P or T into U. If a cell has fewer than 200 eligible admissions or effective sample size below 100 after the R12 transport weights, it is descriptive and its directional contrast is inconclusive; the all-admission pooled result still reports it in the terminal audit. These thresholds are feasibility gates, not evidence for a clinical subgroup.

## Matched baseline and substantive irregular alternative

Use one deterministic patient-level split: hash buckets 0–59 for fitting, 60–69 for selection, 70–79 for locked evaluation; reserve other buckets only if explicitly declared. All encodings, dictionaries, assay selection, regularization, latent dimension and U rule are frozen before locked evaluation.

- B0 (simple baseline): discrete-time cause-specific multinomial/logistic hazards for P/D/U/N with age, sex, department, calendar era, admission time-of-day, prior encounter count, assay-specific pre-admission summaries and one selected documentation encoding. It uses the same 2-hour interval target and same patient split.
- B1 (matched early-summary model): B0 plus only assay-specific lab history observed strictly before the current interval and no later than 12 hours after t0: first/last value, change, elapsed time, count, missingness/density and a supported within-assay slope. For an admission with U, suppress post-t0 lab predictors. B1 is the transparent estimate of early-lab information gain.
- B2 (substantive irregular alternative): a low-dimensional state-space/GP-style latent hepatic-reserve and renal-stress model with assay-specific offsets/noise, irregular `检验时间`, shrinkage, posterior state uncertainty and coupled competing-state heads; update only with rows strictly before the current interval and stop at 12 hours. It includes documentation-encoding interactions as random slopes. B2 can reveal nonlinear coupled trajectories, timing and uncertainty that B1 summaries lose; it is not a validated physiologic score.

B0/B1/B2 must predict the identical terminal state and be scored on identical held-out patient intervals. Primary scientific comparison is B1 versus B0 and its robust heterogeneity; B2 versus B1 is a secondary model adequacy comparison, never a complexity reward. An optional GPU is scientifically unnecessary for sparse B0/B1 and low-dimensional B2; use CPU first. If repeated state-space fitting materially benefits, request one allocated A100 with `gpus:1` and use `cuda:0`; ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not evidence against feasibility.

## Uncertainty, falsification and interpretation gates

Report locked per-admission and per-interval probabilities, multiclass log loss, multiclass Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, P/D/U/N CIFs, B1-minus-B0 contrasts, Δ(r,s), H, Stage-0/R12 flow, transport weights/overlap, sensitivity envelope and B2-minus-B1. Use at least 500 patient-clustered paired bootstrap replicates over locked predictions; state whether refitting occurs and never use bootstrap test outcomes to select the model. Hierarchical intervals must show both partial-pooled and cell-specific uncertainty.

Required falsifications:

- permute lab times within patient and assay, then values within assay; a physiology-specific increment should attenuate;
- permute terminal states within era/service and separately permute Stage-0 states; gains should disappear;
- compare laboratory values with count/density/missingness-only features; a density-only gain is observation-process evidence;
- add the forbidden [12,24) lab window only as a leakage sentinel;
- repeat exact-boundary, midnight and conservative/optimistic U constructions;
- permute documentation labels within era/service preserving margins and rerun all four encodings;
- compare the all-admission dynamic estimate with the R12-only and transported estimates; a large divergence is selection evidence;
- repeat L1 using type-only and modifier-only labels; a claim supported by one lexical view only is not robust;
- use procedure text families or timed orders only as a gated secondary analysis after a fit/selection-only dictionary audit.

Supportive evidence requires coherent source joins, adequate event support, calibrated B1, an uncertainty-supported nonzero all-admission increment, stable heterogeneity across at least L1–L4, attenuation under timestamp/terminal-label permutation, no dominance by density, and B2 gains that are calibrated and stable rather than merely larger. The directional A>U/T expectation is supportive only if it survives all label encodings and selection sensitivity.

Adverse evidence is a null or opposite robust interaction, persistence after label/timestamp permutation, density-only gain, major sensitivity to U/midnight rules, or a large conditional-versus-admission divergence. This refutes the stated transportability hypothesis or indicates documentation/selection artifact; it does not show that early physiology has no clinical value.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse P/T cells, low R12 overlap, extreme transport weights, high U mass, unstable lexical encodings, wide patient-clustered intervals, nonconvergence, assay-unit ambiguity, or no way to separate documentation opportunity from clinical state. Do not collapse these into a positive claim.

Computationally checkable claims are exact source hashes and bindings, cohort/terminal flow, feature time provenance, model fit, held-out scores, calibration, contrasts, uncertainty and falsification outputs. Active-HCC status, diagnosis validity, pathology confirmation, stage, tumor burden, indication/intent/receipt/completion, response, toxicity, outside-hospital care, mortality, causal benefit and deployment utility require clinical adjudication, missing modalities or another prospective study. A positive local recorded-care result would justify prospective validation of an adjudicated, time-stamped cohort; it would not justify changing treatment or monitoring.

## Alternatives deferred and revisit evidence

Raw clinical-document or diagnosis-text sequence learning is deferred from the primary analysis because those fields have no native time and no validated scope/temporality; revisit only with a time-stamped adjudicated chart sample and a validated ontology. Imaging/pathology confirmation is deferred because pathology has no time and examinations lack validated ground truth; revisit with time-linked adjudicated imaging/pathology. A causal treatment-effect analysis is deferred because indication, receipt, completion, counterfactual eligibility and clinical outcomes are absent. A high-capacity transformer is deferred because the question is documentation-transportability and the low-dimensional irregular model directly tests timing/coupling; revisit only if B2 shows reproducible residual nonlinear structure and a larger adjudicated cohort is available.
