# State-conditioned target-label receipt and transportability audit

## Episode-19 advance

This candidate synthesizes the two selected assessed-valid parents without changing their scientific object. The target-independent denominator lineage, strict pre-landmark physiology and exclusions, variant-specific first-stay freezing, fixed landmarks, and post-freeze four-state care-plan classification remain locked. The new question is whether the denominator/receipt conclusion is stable after conditioning on contemporaneous ventilation-documentation states, rather than treating all denominator rows as exchangeable.

The clinically important unresolved claim is that the exact Ventilation care-plan labels provide a usable multicentre target for otherwise eligible ventilated care. Existing executed evidence supports only two narrower statements: (1) target receipt is sparse and concentrated in a target-independent denominator, and (2) apparent differences in an airway-containing ventilation proxy are largely altered or reversed when airway evidence is removed. The untested claim is whether target receipt and site concentration persist within comparable documentation states, ambiguity/conflict strata, and clinically bounded airway definitions. If receipt is sparse even among state-positive rows, a label-defined comparison is not merely selected on ventilation state; it is additionally selected on site-specific documentation. If receipt differs mainly because the state proxy itself is airway-defined, that is measurement dependence, not clinical transportability.

## State definitions and variants

The primary state audit reports four mutually exclusive states: positive, negative, ambiguous, and conflicting. Components remain available separately rather than being collapsed.

* `respiratoryCare.csv.gz` contributes last pre-L `airwaytype`, recent airway counts, and interval fields `ventstartoffset`/`ventendoffset`. Invasive lexical values are `oral ett`, `nasal ett`, `tracheostomy`, `double-lumen tube`, and `cricothyrotomy`; `no artificial airway` and `none` are negative; blank/other values are ambiguous. `priorventstartoffset` and `priorventendoffset` are not substituted for current status.
* `respiratoryCharting.csv.gz` contributes `RT Vent On/Off` values (`continued`/`start` positive, `off` negative, others ambiguous) and exact case-insensitive equality to the 18 locked value-bearing device labels. A value must be nonblank and not `none`, `null`, `na`, `n/a`, or `nan`; label presence alone is not positive.
* `treatment.csv.gz` contributes invasive corroboration only for `treatmentstring` containing mechanical ventilation while excluding non-invasive/noninvasive ventilation. NIV remains an alternative-modality signal.
* `note.csv.gz` contributes timestamped `Intubation` and `Extubation` note-type components only; note text is never used or exported.

The **airway-containing protocol proxy** includes exact strong chart evidence, intubation note, window-local invasive airway plus a plausible setting family, or plausible setting plus invasive treatment. It is descriptive and partly tautological because airway recency defines the overlap groups. The **airway-independent falsification proxy** omits airway entirely and requires exact strong chart evidence, intubation note, at least two plausible setting families, or setting plus invasive treatment. Both are documentation proxies, not adjudicated ventilation.

Bounded variants use the latest explicit invasive airway within 48, 24, and 6 hours before L, while preserving all other denominator rules. Interval variants report closed/current interval interpretation separately from the zero-as-open-ended sensitivity; because `ventendoffset` is zero-dominated, open-zero interpretation is not primary evidence.

## Target receipt and estimands

Only after denominator selection, inspect `carePlanGeneral.csv.gz` rows with `cplgroup='Ventilation'` and `cplitemoffset` in `[L,L+360]`. Exact target values are `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`. The earliest target is classified as evaluation or no-trial; same-time contradictory targets are conflict; non-target Ventilation rows are `other_ventilation_value`; absence of any Ventilation row is `no_target_label`. `carePlanEOL.csv.gz` (`cpleoldiscussionoffset`) is used only in the inherited exclusion lineage.

For each lineage, variant, state, group, landmark, and hospital, report:

1. row count, distinct stays, distinct patients, distinct hospitals, selected-stay fraction, and complete `[L,L+360]` observation fraction;
2. target receipt (any exact target), evaluation, no-trial, conflict, other value, and no-target-label proportions;
3. target concentration by hospital (number and fraction of hospitals with any receipt, HHI, effective hospital count, largest-site share), with Jeffreys 95% intervals for proportions;
4. common support: hospitals with at least 10 rows in each compared state/group and, separately, at least 10 rows in each of all five landmark cells; equal-landmark hospital-standardized receipt and site range;
5. row-, stay-, and hospital-cluster bootstrap intervals for state contrasts in positivity, ambiguity, conflict, and target receipt; and leave-one-hospital-out ranges;
6. selection into the target-conditioned 540-row positive-control cohort, including overlap and attrition from each denominator lineage.

The principal state-conditioned estimand is the risk difference in target receipt between state-positive and state-ambiguous rows within each airway/lookback variant, not a causal effect. The transportability gate requires directionally compatible estimates across the airway-containing and airway-independent proxies, bounded variants, landmarks, common-support hospitals, and both stay- and hospital-cluster intervals. A state contrast cannot be interpreted clinically if the state proxy is setting-dominated or its event semantics are unadjudicated.

## Executed evidence carried forward and episode result

The selected parents executed full-source audits. Their parser counts for target-stay records were 130,612 `respiratoryCare`, 1,749,763 `respiratoryCharting`, 268,734 `treatment`, and 149,481 `note` records, with zero reported field-width mismatches, decode replacements, bad IDs, or bad target-stay clinical offsets. The 3,788-row value-aware state audit used exact membership in the 18 locked labels and achieved zero reconciliation mismatches against its parent state fields.

The state-conditioned measurement result is:

* airway-containing protocol positivity was 10.98% (126/1,148), 15.60% (228/1,462), and 96.60% (1,138/1,178) in unbounded-only, 24-hour-only, and 6-hour-retained rows; the 6-hour contrast is structurally non-independent because window-local airway occurred in 0/1,148, 0/1,462, and 1,178/1,178 rows;
* airway-independent positivity was 93.99% (1,079/1,148), 95.90% (1,402/1,462), and 91.94% (1,083/1,178). The unbounded-minus-6-hour difference was +2.05 percentage points, with stay-cluster 95% interval -0.41 to +4.49 and hospital-cluster interval -2.67 to +9.55; versus 24-hour-only it was -1.91 points, with stay interval -3.65 to -0.12 and hospital interval -4.74 to +0.94;
* setting families dominated the airway-independent proxy (at least two plausible families in 93.73%, 95.83%, and 93.38%); strong chart positives were 0, 1, and 37 rows, intubation notes 0, 0, and 6, and invasive treatment 128, 231, and 173;
* target-independent receipt in the broader 4,685-row lineage was 124/4,685 (2.65%), comprising 89 evaluation (1.90%), 35 no-trial (0.75%), 281 other Ventilation values (6.00%), and 4,280 no-target-label (91.36%). The complete six-hour window sensitivity was 123/4,625 (2.66%); only 25/89 hospitals supplied any exact target, 64 supplied none, and hospital 420 supplied 73/124 (58.9%) target rows. Target-receipt HHI was 0.3595 (effective hospital count 2.78). Among 12 hospitals meeting the stringent all-five-landmark denominator support rule, equal-landmark standardized receipt ranged 0–7.36% (median 0.85%).

The key synthesis is adverse to a broad, transportable target-label phenotype but inconclusive about clinical ventilation. The apparent large protocol-state contrast does not survive removal of airway evidence, while target receipt remains sparse and highly site-concentrated in the independent denominator. Thus the data support a documentation-selection problem, not a claim that state-positive rows are truly ventilated or that target labels represent SBT delivery or bedside decisions.

## Uncertainty, selection, and gates

Jeffreys intervals should accompany all sparse receipt cells; clustered bootstrap is primary for repeated landmarks. Existing parent uncertainty already shows that the 6-hour state contrast is large under the airway-containing rule but is not independent of group construction, whereas airway-independent hospital intervals cross zero. The receipt estimate's ordinary binomial standard error is approximately 0.24 percentage points, but this is not a substitute for hospital-cluster uncertainty; hospital concentration is the dominant uncertainty/transportability issue.

Supportive evidence for a robust state-conditioned transportability claim would require: exact positive-control identity; target receipt materially above 5% or broadly distributed across denominator hospitals within state-positive rows; compatible state contrasts under the airway-independent proxy and all bounded variants; common-support intervals excluding a clinically material site-driven difference; and no domination by one setting family. These gates are not met by the carried-forward results.

Adverse evidence is sparse target receipt, many hospitals with zero targets, large largest-site share/HHI, failure of common support, and disappearance of the protocol contrast under the airway-independent proxy. Those findings are observed. An inconclusive result is required where hospital intervals cross zero, common-support cells are sparse, bounded variants select different stays, or setting traces dominate; these conditions apply to the non-tautological state comparison. The correct episode conclusion is therefore adverse for transportability of the exact target labels and inconclusive for the clinical meaning of the state proxy.

## Clinical limits

Automatically checkable claims are row/stay/hospital counts, exact joins, offsets and windows, parser accounting, positive-control identity, state transition sums, target class counts, HHI, common-support definitions, bootstrap seeds/replicates, and artifact hashes. The data cannot establish actual continuous invasive ventilation, invasive versus NIV modality in every row, copied-forward status, clinician intent, SBT delivery, extubation evaluation, quality, causal effects, benefit, or external transportability. Those require blinded clinical adjudication against tube/device insertion/removal, ventilator mode/settings, respiratory flowsheets, temporal continuity, and discharge/transfer boundaries. A subsequent adjudication sample should be stratified by proxy (airway-containing vs airway-independent), state (positive/ambiguous/conflicting), hospital concentration, and present-to-future transition, estimating PPV/NPV and inter-rater reliability before any bedside or causal use.

This proposal does not revive the previously failed durable-liberation endpoint or inherited 12-hour action association. It preserves their negative/adverse findings and treats all target receipt as documentation receipt, not clinical action.
