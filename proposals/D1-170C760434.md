# Hypotension depth versus duration and observed renal deterioration: eICU

## Scientific question and falsifiable hypothesis

Among adults in a first ICU stay with a stable, comparable pre-landmark creatinine trajectory, does the depth-duration burden of MAP below 65 mmHg explain subsequent 24-hour observed creatinine deterioration beyond minimum MAP and low-MAP duration, and does it transport to hospitals not used for fitting?

The strongest evidence-supported claim before this experiment is narrower: ICU-relative MAP and serial creatinine-labelled laboratory rows are available in eICU, and prior work/seed proposals motivate time-weighted hypotension metrics. The data do not establish that hypotension causes AKI, that MAP 65 is a treatment threshold, or that an EHR creatinine signal is adjudicated AKI.

The unresolved claim is whether two patients with the same nadir and comparable pre-window state but different accumulated depth below 65 have different future renal-signal risk, after accounting for the parent duration measure. The primary hypothesis is that greater area below MAP 65 (mmHg-min), residualized/standardized after minimum MAP and low-MAP minutes, is associated with a positive 24-hour cumulative-incidence risk difference for a first observed creatinine deterioration signal. A secondary hypothesis is that a time-ordered model will distinguish prolonged shallow from brief deep hypotension beyond summary exposures, and that a pattern must reproduce in held-out hospitals to count as evidence.

This is an observational risk-marker study, not a causal treatment comparison. Its clinical advance would be to determine whether monitoring summaries should preserve cumulative depth as well as time, helping prioritize what a future prospective blood-pressure intervention should measure. A null result would be useful: it would support the simpler minimum/duration representation and discourage overinterpreting depth-duration scores.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Use adults (`age >=18`, with eICU age >89 mapped to 90), first ICU stay (`unitvisitnumber=1`), nonmissing hospital, and `unitdischargeoffset >=2160` minutes. Resolve duplicate/conflicting first-visit rows by earliest valid admission and report the conflict. Candidate landmarks are t = 12, 18, ..., 72 hours after ICU offset 0. Retain t only with at least six hours before t, at least 24 hours before `unitdischargeoffset`, no EOL offset at/before t, and the stable creatinine criteria below.

For the primary cohort, retain at least two numeric creatinine results in [t-48h,t], both with `labresultoffset <= t` and `labresultrevisedoffset <= t`, from a label/system/interface mapping that passes the scale audit. Baseline is the median of the two most recent comparable results; record both offsets and the pre-t slope (difference divided by elapsed hours). Do not use future or Apache creatinine for baseline. Keep the one-result last-value cohort as a named sensitivity only.

For valid periodic systemic mean MAP values in [-50,300], sort by `observationoffset`. Each value contributes for min(5, next offset-current offset) minutes, bounded by [t-360,t), with no carry-forward over gaps. Require at least 80% observed coverage. Compute covered minutes, low-MAP minutes, low-MAP fraction, minimum, mean, SD, last value, and depth-duration area:
`AUC65 = sum(max(0,65-MAP_i)*covered_minutes_i)`.
Do not count unobserved intervals as normotension. Repeat coverage 70%/90%, thresholds 60/70, and the aperiodic MAP source sensitivity.

## Outcome, competing events and estimand

The primary outcome is the first retained comparable numeric creatinine in (t,t+24h] satisfying >= baseline+0.3 or >=1.5*baseline, only after the label/interface/scale audit; this is called an observed creatinine deterioration signal, not KDIGO AKI. A future value is not imputed when absent.

Classify mutually exclusive processes: renal signal before death/discharge within 24 hours; ICU death before signal; alive ICU discharge/transfer before signal; no signal by 24 hours with an observed future comparable creatinine; or no future observation, which is observation censoring rather than a negative. Death and alive discharge are competing events.

The primary estimand is the standardized 24-hour cumulative-incidence risk difference per one training-set SD increase in AUC65 residualized on minimum MAP and low-MAP minutes, within prespecified minimum-MAP strata and the eligible-landmark distribution. Fit an observation model from pre-t covariates and early observation history; truncate inverse-probability observation weights at the 1st/99th percentiles. Report the joint model coefficient and standardized risk difference, plus duration-only and depth-only contrasts, cause-specific and subdistribution summaries. Report stable-baseline inclusion, future-observation and weight diagnostics.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternatives

Fit a regularized multinomial competing-risk pooled-logistic baseline M_min using minimum-MAP splines, pre-window MAP/heart-rate/respiration/SpO2 summaries and slopes, coverage, stable creatinine median/slope/interval, age/sex/ethnicity, time since admission, admission Apache covariates/scores, documented ventilation and pressor-record indicators, and missingness indicators.

Fit M_duration by adding low-MAP minutes/fraction and minimum interaction. Fit M_depth by adding AUC65 and its prespecified interaction. Fit M_joint with duration and AUC65 together. Compare standardized CIFs, calibration, Brier score, frozen-threshold decision curves and bootstrap intervals on the same landmarks; the scientific comparison is whether depth adds information beyond duration, not a small AUROC contest.

The substantive learned alternative is a six-bin temporal convolution or small GRU T_shape using the same cohort and only data timestamped <=t: hourly MAP values/masks, heart rate, respiration, SpO2, creatinine value/observation history and slope, documented pressor/ventilation histories, admission covariates and explicit masks. Fit T_min (summary/minimum inputs) and T_shape (ordered trajectory). Its additional information is ordering, recovery shape, shallow-versus-deep episodes and irregular observation patterns discarded by scalar duration/AUC summaries. Use three seeds, patient-within-hospital fitting/early stopping, and untouched hospital evaluation; never use hospital ID as a predictor. A temporal model is scientifically useful only if its calibration or a prespecified depth/recovery pattern reproduces in held-out hospitals.

Expected resources under `inputs.json`: 2 CPUs, <=8192 MiB, no GPU required; approximately 20–40 minutes extraction, <15 minutes per regularized model and 45–90 minutes for both temporal variants. The actual deliverable is newly fitted M_min, M_duration, M_depth, M_joint, T_min and T_shape, not an audit-only report.

## Falsification and interpretation

Gate interpretation on scale/label support, paired pre/future creatinine support, MAP coverage, within-stratum duration/AUC contrast, stable weights and supported held-out hospitals. Compare observation-weighted, complete-case and unweighted analyses; periodic/aperiodic MAP; 60/65/70 thresholds; 70/80/90% coverage; stable-baseline versus one-result sensitivity; EOL and near-discharge exclusions; and abrupt MAP-coverage-loss exclusions.

Preserve MAP values/counts/coverage but permute their temporal order. A shape-specific claim should attenuate; a pure AUC claim need not. Use the pre-landmark placebo creatinine-rise rule in [t-24h,t), and compare future-lab observation as a process outcome. Check whether AUC is driven by a few extreme values or by missingness.

Supportive results require a positive AUC residual contrast with a 95% interval excluding zero, persistence across primary label/coverage/observation sensitivities, weak placebo association, non-dominance by death/discharge, and either reproducibility in held-out hospitals or a reproducible temporal depth/recovery pattern. This supports only an observational marker for an observed creatinine deterioration signal and motivates prospective study.

Adverse results include a precise null/negative AUC contrast, attenuation after duration adjustment, disappearance after observation adjustment, strong placebo or temporal-permutation association, extreme-value/missingness dominance, competing-event dominance, or held-out failure. These support the simpler minimum/duration representation or documentation selection as the stable signal; they do not prove no renal harm.

Inconclusive results include non-comparable creatinine scales, insufficient paired baselines/future observations, no exposure contrast, unstable observation weights, too few eligible hospitals, or wide uncertainty. No treatment recommendation follows.

Computationally checkable: source hashes and schemas; filters/joins; offset safety; MAP integration and AUC; stable baseline and label audit; event/competing-event classification; fitted model artifacts; CIF/risk differences; uncertainty; calibration/Brier/decision curves; patient/hospital split integrity; transport and falsification outputs. Not established: adjudicated KDIGO AKI, urine output, outpatient baseline, perfusion, true pressor dose/continuity, fluids/nutrition, clinician intent, reasons for absent observations, or clinical benefit from acting on a warning. Those require clinical adjudication, richer records or prospective study.

## Demonstrations, seeds and alternatives

The Delphi demonstration is a method-boundary adaptation only: its structured temporal learning motivates T_shape, but its UK Biobank cohort and original training are not reproduced in eICU. The ALADYNOULLI demonstration cannot be reproduced because verified eICU genetics are unavailable; a small EHR-only temporal model is explicitly a different model and is included only as a matched alternative. The Oncoformer main paper/complete STAR Methods remain unavailable in the bundle and eICU has no images; no multimodal reproduction claim is made.

The eICU-03 duration seed is the parent lineage; this child tests the unresolved depth-versus-duration refinement. eICU-02/eICU-08 testing-process seeds are retained as prior comparator branches. Hyperoxia is deferred because PaO2/FiO2 and pulmonary-status semantics are not yet verified; ventilatory burden is deferred because plateau pressure and delivered tidal volume are absent from the inspected schema; nighttime discharge/transfer/recovery remain observationally plausible but lack organizational or post-ICU evidence; insulin-hypoglycemia is deferred because the bounded audit found explicit insulin orders/infusions but mixed routes/units, no guaranteed administration continuity, and intake/output labels dominated by flush/residual documentation rather than a reliable nutrition-interruption state. These branches should be revisited only if the missing dependency is resolved.

Completion requires a machine-readable cohort/label/observation manifest, newly fitted M_min/M_duration/M_depth/M_joint/T_min/T_shape, competing-event and weight-support tables, standardized estimates and uncertainty, held-out transport, parent one-result comparison, and all falsification outputs. No computed clinical effect is asserted in this proposal.
