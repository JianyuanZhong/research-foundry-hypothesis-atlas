The unresolved decision question is:

> Can the frozen grip rule AG1 be considered safe to take forward against the frozen FFM rule AF1 if its overall fixed-capacity H1/D1 contrast is favorable but its allocation contribution is materially worse in either recorded-sex stratum?

The new falsifiable hypothesis is a qualification, not a duplicate overall contrast:

> At the same immutable 466-assay capacity, AG1 is non-inferior to AF1 for both equation-defined H1 and D1 allocation yield within each UK Biobank recorded-sex stratum (field 31-0.0 = 0 or 1), when each stratum's contribution is expressed per total assay slot.

The clinical importance is that a favorable aggregate list can mask subgroup harm when the two lists have different sex composition. A service deciding whether to prospectively evaluate grip rather than FFM needs to know whether a positive total result is compatible with a material loss of equation-defined target yield among one recorded-sex group. This is clinically consequential allocation-safety information, while remaining narrower than fairness, biological mechanism, or patient benefit.

This child adds one four-component recorded-sex safety family to the already frozen AG1-versus-AF1 experiment. It does not fit sex-specific models, alter either list, add a new biomarker, create an AG1/AF1 replacement contrast, or repeat the parent low-UACR analysis.

Support would mean only that the frozen AG1 list does not show a prespecified material loss relative to AF1 in either of the two available recorded-sex strata for both one-record equation-defined endpoints. It would justify carrying this safety qualification into a prospective head-to-head testing-strategy study. It would not establish equal access, equal burden, equal cost, absence of harm, or benefit.

## New estimands

Let s be recorded sex, with F=s=0 and M=s=1 only for compact labels; report the source coding as 0/1 in all machine-readable output. Let S_AG and S_AF be the frozen AG1 and AF1 rosters and let E_i be the inherited H1 or D1 label for eid i.

For E in {H,D} and s in {0,1}, define the fixed-total-capacity contribution contrast

`Q_E,s = 1000/466 * [sum(i in S_AG, sex_i=s) E_i - sum(i in S_AF, sex_i=s) E_i]`.

This uses the total 466 assay slots as denominator, so it measures the stratum’s contribution to the service-wide allocation yield. It is not a within-sex prevalence comparison and must never be divided by the number of selected people in that stratum. The identity `Q_E,0 + Q_E,1 = Q_E` must hold for the parent’s overall AG1-minus-AF1 contrast.

Report, separately for each s and policy, selected count, observed H/D count, missing H/D count, H/D numerator, and yield per 466; report AG-only and AF-only membership and endpoint counts, overlap/Jaccard, and directional swaps by sex. Verify each stratum and total identity from the same eid-level contribution vector. A stratum with identical AG1 and AF1 membership has an exactly zero contrast and is not made inconclusive merely because it has no swaps.

H1 and D1 are inherited, not redefined:

- `H1=1` iff race-free 2021 eGFRcr-cys1 <60.
- `D1=1` iff race-free 2021 eGFRcys1/eGFRcr1 <=0.70.
- Scr is `30700-1.0/88.4` in mg/dL and Scys is `30720-1.0`; the inherited sex-specific 2021 equations, age, and constants are used exactly.
- Invalid visit-1 cystatin makes both labels missing but never changes membership or the denominator. These are one-record equation-defined phenotypes, not measured GFR, persistent CKD, or sarcopenia.

For missing labels, use one shared latent binary value per eid across both policies and both strata. With `c_i=I(i in S_AG)-I(i in S_AF)`, calculate exact sharp lower and upper bounds on every `Q_E,s` by adding `min(0,c_i)` and `max(0,c_i)) to the stratum-specific numerator. A shared member cannot receive policy-specific labels. The parent’s missingness gate remains in force; additionally, a nonempty stratum must have computable list counts and endpoint labels. If a stratum has directional swaps, require at least 3 AG-only and 3 AF-only eids and at least 5 observed labels for each endpoint in each directional side; otherwise that stratum’s safety result is inconclusive. These are anti-vacuity gates, not power claims.

## Analysis and uncertainty

The new family is `F65_SEX={Q_H,0,Q_D,0,Q_H,1,Q_D,1}`, in that order. It is evaluated only after all inherited source, fit, roster, and no-leakage checks, but its result never rescues an adverse or inconclusive parent result.

Use exactly the parent’s 2,000 paired participant-level bootstrap draws over canonical-eid-sorted O1_hold:

`Generator(PCG64DXSM(full_unsigned_big_endian_SHA256(UTF8("ukb-grip-visit1-bootstrap-v1|"+b))))`

for zero-based draw b; draw 2,334 row indices with replacement, convert to participant multiplicities, keep both fixed 466-person rosters and all denominators, and never refit, rerank, refill, or bootstrap policies independently. Recompute the four sex-stratum sharp bounds in every draw using shared eid labels.

When all four series have finite positive bootstrap SE and at least 95% finite draws, use the parent’s one-sided 95% max-t procedure and NumPy-linear quantile convention jointly over exactly the four components. Otherwise use the parent’s componentwise Bonferroni fallback with m=4 and one-sided tails .05/4, recording the fallback. Any component with fewer than 95% finite draws or zero variance is inconclusive. Report family order/hash, all draw counts, bounds, SEs, limits, and fallback reason.

The prespecified safety margin is -2 per 1,000 total assay slots for each endpoint and stratum. Define:

- `recorded_sex_safety_supportive`: all source/roster/label/uncertainty/anti-vacuity gates pass, and the simultaneous lower limit for every Q_H,s and Q_D,s is strictly greater than -2/1,000.
- `recorded_sex_safety_adverse`: all gates pass and at least one simultaneous upper limit for Q_H,s or Q_D,s is <= -2/1,000. This falsifies non-inferiority in at least one recorded-sex stratum; it does not prove AF1 superiority or patient harm.
- `recorded_sex_safety_inconclusive`: every other result, including a limit touching/crossing -2 without satisfying the adverse rule, a sparse stratum, a gate failure, missingness ambiguity, nonpositive variance, or noncomputability. Inconclusive is not equivalence.

The support rule is intentionally conjunctive: one apparently safe stratum cannot offset a materially adverse other stratum. The adverse rule is intentionally disjunctive: one reliably adverse stratum falsifies the safety qualification. This does not change the parent’s AG1:AF1 H1/D1 estimand or conclusion.
