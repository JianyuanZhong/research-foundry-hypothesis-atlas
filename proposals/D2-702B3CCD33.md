## Proposed relationship

The apparent MerTK-inhibitor-associated transcriptional interaction between macrophage context (Mpos versus Mneg) and treatment in E-MTAB-8873 is specific to the stated context, rather than being explained by pseudobulk composition, reused or mixed donors, or an unbalanced generic treatment effect. ## Evidence and unresolved claim

The parent candidate already supplies three inspected, claim-linked works supporting biological plausibility: MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets are associated with remission and fibroblast repair responses [K1]; macrophage reprogramming can alter inflammatory mediator production [K2]; and macrophage–fibroblast repair circuits are context-, timing-, and niche-dependent [K3]. These sources motivate the question but do not establish an E-MTAB-8873 effect.

The strongest available evidence supports only that the targeted assay can measure intervention-associated expression under defined macrophage-coculture conditions. The unresolved claim is whether the context-by-inhibitor contrast survives controls for pseudobulk composition, donor reuse/mixing, and treatment/control balance. No E-MTAB-8873 effect estimate has been computed in this episode.

## Falsifiable negative-control/stratified test

1. Audit the exact counts/design and retain only contrasts with verified inhibitor, control, macrophage-context, and temporal labels. Inhibitor samples are only in Mpos/Mneg contexts and no verified temporal field exists; therefore do not claim a time-resolved or causal effect.
2. Define macrophage-linked and fibroblast-linked programs only from the 445 measured features, retaining source identifiers. Do not use unmeasured genes.
3. Fit a donor-aware additive baseline for condition, treatment, and measured program expression. Compare it with the prespecified context-by-inhibitor interaction model. Use donor-clustered or permutation uncertainty that respects repeated/mixed donors.
4. Add a composition negative control: estimate the interaction after standardizing or stratifying by cell_type and contributing-donor composition where the design permits, and compare the result with the unadjusted interaction. A composition-sensitive interaction that disappears under this control is evidence against specificity, not evidence for it.
5. Add a treatment negative control: compare inhibitor-associated changes with the verified untreated/control contrast within the same context and donor strata. A similar generic treatment effect across contexts weakens the specificity claim.
6. Report sample-level and program-level estimates, the number of independent donor units, missing/mixed-donor flags, and uncertainty. Do not select a favorable subgroup after seeing results.

Supportive evidence requires a prespecified interaction that remains directionally consistent after composition and treatment negative controls, with uncertainty that excludes the prespecified null or is otherwise explicitly judged adequate. Adverse evidence is a null/reversed interaction, disappearance after composition adjustment, or a generic treatment effect indistinguishable across contexts. Inconclusive evidence includes unstable contrasts, missing controls, insufficient independent donor information, ambiguous feature definitions, or imprecise estimates.

## Outcomes and limits

Supportive results would justify a follow-up mechanistic study of the identified context-specific program. Adverse results would favor a generic intervention, donor, or composition explanation. Inconclusive results would identify missing controls, inadequate independent units, or unstable feature definitions. Expression response is not direct target action, causality, protection, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.
