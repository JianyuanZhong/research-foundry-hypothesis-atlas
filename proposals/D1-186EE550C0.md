# Does a leakage-safe early hepatic laboratory panel add information for a fixed +72-hour administrative disposition endpoint after repeat TACE?

## Targeted repair and substantive advance

This is an alternative substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`, published in response to its failed compilation request for scientific revision. The parent’s clinical question is defensible as an information question, but its primary endpoint is intrinsically weak for a compiler-executable clinical experiment: `Y` is the next *observed* assay value, and its clock is clinician- and workflow-dependent; exact next-observation selection differs across patients, is conditioned on laboratory availability, and cannot be interpreted as hepatic deterioration, treatment toxicity, or a clinically actionable state. The parent’s LOOCV OLS is also fragile at approximately 40/118 and 41/119 assay-specific triplets, with rank checked only at execution. A favorable result could therefore be an artifact of observation timing rather than a clinically coherent post-event landmark.

I therefore concretely cross over to the already audited, fixed +72-hour endpoint, while labeling it administrative throughout. The endpoint is not discharge readiness, morbidity, hepatic decompensation, toxicity, benefit, or a treatment decision. It is only whether the linked repeat-TACE encounter remains administratively open after 72 hours:

`D72 = 1[valid linked encounters.discharge time > event_time + 72 hours]`; equality is zero.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> Among the frozen repeat-TACE episodes with an observed common early albumin/bilirubin panel, adding the two prespecified early laboratory direction indicators to a leakage-safe pre-panel model improves out-of-sample prediction of `D72` by at least 0.02 Brier units.

This is a prognostic information test at a fixed landmark, not a causal analysis. The advance over the parent is a common, event-anchored outcome clock and a single simple nested model contrast that is executable without choosing a patient-specific “next” laboratory observation. The 0.02 margin is a design convention, not a validated clinical utility threshold.

## Evidence already established versus claim being tested

The inherited complete-source, outcome-blind reconstruction supports feasibility only: 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator pathway patients, 159 and 526 selected repeat-TACE events, and assay/history availability sufficient for a common early panel in 34 systemic and 97 comparator episodes (131 total) after the frozen B/P denominator of 55 systemic and 187 comparator episodes. The audited +72-hour execution support reports 48 `D72=1` and 83 `D72=0` episodes, all with valid linked encounter chronology and no selected panel after discharge. It found a primary Brier improvement of `0.000024385`, with conditional bootstrap 95% interval `[-0.000485606, 0.000828495]`; this is an executed reference result, not a premise for the child’s conclusion. The observation-process audit found strong coupling between prior panel history and panel observation (32/37 versus 2/18 in the systemic arm; 93/108 versus 4/79 in the comparator arm), so the child retains a descriptive selection diagnostic and makes no MAR, ignorability, or missing-outcome-recovery claim.

The untested claim remains the prespecified incremental-value hypothesis above. A positive association or prediction result cannot establish that prolonged encounter status reflects liver injury or that monitoring or treatment should change.

Retain censoring markers and specimen type, but primary models use uncensored numeric rows. For each repeat event and assay:

- `B`: latest valid uncensored value on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1.
- `P`: latest valid uncensored value in `event_encounter` in `[event_time−72h,event_time)`, excluding exact event time.
- `H_rows`: same assay, time `>= tace2_time`, `< event_time−72h`, `< P_time`, normalized encounter different from `event_encounter`, valid and uncensored. Preserve every timestamp.

## Simple nested analysis and estimand

Fit one prespecified pooled logistic regression with an unpenalized intercept and fixed slope ridge penalty `lambda=1`; no tuning, interactions, nonlinear terms, feature selection, fallback, or arm-specific effect. Use five deterministic SHA-256 arm-and-D72-balanced folds. In every held-out fold, fit all imputers, standardizers, and coefficients on training rows only. Median-impute age; encode sex with literal male reference and fixed female/unknown-other-missing indicators, dropping only training-zero-variance indicators. Standardize continuous predictors using training data only.

The pre-panel model is:

`Q_pre = logit^-1(intercept + BP_alb_down + BP_bili_up + arm + age + sex + event_year + TACE2_to_repeat_days + P_old_lead_hours + panel_hours + H_any + logN + H_any*log(1+R_hours/24))`.

The early model is identical plus `E_alb_down + E_bili_up`; no other feature changes. `P_old_lead_hours` is the time from the older of the two P timestamps to event time, and `panel_hours` is the common panel time from event time. If the exact feature definition cannot be reconstructed without ambiguity, omit that feature identically from both models and record the deviation before fitting; never replace it with outcome-informed alternatives.

Primary estimand:

`Delta_D72 = Brier(D72,Q_pre) - Brier(D72,Q_early)`.

Positive values favor the early panel. Report both Brier scores, training-prevalence null Brier, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, prediction range/variance, arm-stratified descriptive errors, and paired per-episode squared-error differences. Coefficients are descriptive and cannot substitute for out-of-fold performance.

Uncertainty uses 1,000 fixed-seed patient bootstrap resamples within arm of the frozen 131 episodes, refitting the complete fixed pipeline within each resample; require at least 95% estimable replicates and report percentile 95% interval and `(1+#Delta<=0)/(1+#estimable)`. This is conditional model uncertainty, not transportability or clinical utility uncertainty. Use Holm correction only if making a two-component family claim; otherwise the early block is one primary contrast.

## Falsification and sensitivity checks

1. **Linkage destruction:** permute the pair `(E_alb_down,E_bili_up)` jointly within arm, preserving every process, timing, B/P, covariate, and outcome variable, for 500 fixed-seed permutations. Support requires observed `Delta_D72` above the 95th permutation percentile.
2. **Partition stability:** repeat the exact analysis under four additional predeclared fold salts without changing features or endpoint. A favorable result is fragile if fewer than 4/5 gains are positive or the median gain is below zero.
3. **Influence:** delete each episode, refit using the fixed folds and training-only transformations, and report the gain range and sign changes. A favorable result is influence-sensitive if any deletion reverses the sign or changes the gain by at least 0.01.
4. **Timing robustness:** one locked secondary analysis replaces the common panel window with the first eligible same-encounter panel in `(event_time,event_time+96h]`; it is not used to select a favorable result. A sign reversal in a retained, empirically distinct cohort downgrades support.
5. **Observation-process diagnostic:** in all 242 both-B/both-P episodes, model early-panel observation `O` with a process-only model using arm, age, sex, event year, TACE2-to-repeat days, H_any, logN, and history recency, then a nested model adding the two intermediate numeric values only. Report cross-fitted Brier difference and 500 within-arm linkage permutations. This is descriptive; it cannot establish MAR, ignorability, exchangeability, or recover the 111 unobserved panels. Do not weight the primary outcome model.
6. **Administrative endpoint audit:** report missing/invalid `Discharge Time`, discharge before panel, exact equality at +72h, and whether the linked encounter is the selected repeat encounter. No broadening to other encounters or discharge proxies is allowed.
7. **Negative feasibility:** retain the parent’s pseudo-current finding as non-estimable and do not widen its clock after seeing outcomes.

## Ordered interpretation branches

- **Reconstruction-inconclusive:** any source/hash/schema mismatch, failure to reproduce the locked cohort counts, parser facts, panel counts, D72 counts, join cardinality, or clock assertions.
- **Computationally inconclusive:** fewer than five valid balanced folds, rank/finite-fit failure, nonfinite predictions, pooled outcome variance zero, or fewer than 95% estimable bootstrap replicates.
- **Adverse:** all computational gates pass and bootstrap 95% interval lies wholly below zero. Conclude only that early directions worsen prediction of the administrative endpoint.
- **Supportive but administrative:** all gates pass; `Delta_D72>=0.02`; bootstrap lower bound is above zero; gain exceeds the linkage-permutation 95th percentile; at least 4/5 partition gains are positive with positive median; no influence or timing reversal. Conclude only that the recorded early panel adds reproducible out-of-sample information for whether this linked encounter remains administratively open beyond 72 hours in selected observed repeat-TACE episodes. Do not translate this into discharge readiness, hepatic failure, toxicity, benefit, causality, or monitoring policy.
- **Precise null:** all computational gates pass, interval is not wholly below zero, and its upper bound is below `+0.02`. Conclude that this design rules out a design-sized 0.02 Brier increment for this administrative forecast under the conditional uncertainty convention; it does not rule out biology or clinical utility.
- **No demonstrated increment:** point estimate below 0.02 without adverse or precise-null interval; report the estimate and uncertainty without claiming equivalence.
- **Fragile/selection-sensitive:** a favorable point estimate fails the linkage, partition, influence, timing, or observation-process diagnostic gate. Report the conditional result and downgrade to inconclusive.
- **Inconclusive:** any interval spanning both meaningful adverse degradation and +0.02, unresolved execution ambiguity, or missing required diagnostic output.

## Clinical limits and verifier boundary

The snapshot has no laboratory units/reference ranges or analyzer harmonization, no Child–Pugh/MELD, tumor burden, radiographic response, technical TACE intent/success, verified medication administration/adherence, transfusion/albumin infusion/fluid balance, infection/bleeding adjudication, outside care, reliable mortality, reasons for prolonged stay, bed/scheduling context, or validated discharge readiness. Diagnoses inherit encounter time and are lexical proxies. Conditioning on repeat TACE, B/P availability, and early-panel observation can induce selection/collider bias. `D72` is administrative and must never be called a clinical outcome.

A verifier can check source hashes and headers, full scans, joins, cohort/event reconstruction, parser and duplicate handling, exact B/P/H/panel/D72 clocks, fold assignment, training-only preprocessing, fitted predictions, Brier/bootstrap/permutation arithmetic, and branch precedence. It cannot establish hepatic injury, treatment effect, discharge readiness, clinical utility, mechanism, or policy. Those require adjudicated outcomes, verified exposures and care context, unit-harmonized assays, external validation, and expert review or prospective evaluation.
