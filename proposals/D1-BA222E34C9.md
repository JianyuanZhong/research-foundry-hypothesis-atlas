# Episode 48 result: late-value-balanced binary lactate nonclearance is not identifiable in this EICU risk set

## Resolution, clinical importance, and substantive advance

At a 24-hour reassessment, does binary lactate nonclearance (<20%) mark at least a 10-percentage-point higher subsequent hospital-mortality risk than clearance after comparing patients on the absolute late lactate already visible to the clinician and on recorded pre-late-window context?

This is a prognostic incremental-information hypothesis, not a treatment-effect or biomarker-mechanism hypothesis. It matters because the clinician already sees the selected 12–24-hour lactate. A crude nonclearance contrast can be large simply because nonclearance is a deterministic function of baseline and follow-up values. Retaining and acting on the earlier value requires evidence that trajectory adds information among patients with comparable current values.

The strongest prior EICU evidence supports two narrower claims. First, [prior hypothesis] estimated a +27.15-point overlap-weighted mortality association (95% interval +20.13 to +33.91) using pre-12-hour context but not the absolute late lactate; its primary result was formally inconclusive because one propensity was 0.019198, below its locked 0.02 bound. Second, [prior hypothesis] found only a +0.00479 leave-hospital-out AUROC gain (95% interval −0.00849 to +0.02286) from adding continuous trajectory to a current-value model; that +0.02 material-gain hypothesis was also inconclusive. Neither establishes the present >=10-point late-value-balanced binary association.

The complete-source Episode-48 diagnostic resolves an essential prerequisite adversely: with the late value in the frozen exposure model, fitted propensities span 0.000410 to 0.992557, far outside the required [0.02,0.98]. Thus the exact available EICU data do not provide measured overlap for this binary comparison. The deterministic primary label is **inconclusive**. The computed overlap-weighted point RD is −0.99 percentage points (43.77% versus 44.76%), but it is explicitly noninferential because the positivity gate failed and must not be called adverse, null, opposite, or protective. No bootstrap interval can rescue a failed gate.

This is a substantive advance over the parents: it shows why the large pre-current-value association cannot answer the incremental question. Conditioning on the current late lactate exposes structural separation created by defining X from B and F. Further threshold relaxation, trimming, rounding, or alternative modeling on the same rows would be a new Lead-approved estimand, not a repair.

Current literature supports importance, not the tested claim. The inspected full-text Li et al. 2026 study (DOI 10.2196/92838; PMCID PMC13478678; frozen source [source checksum]; [source checksum]) used >=3 lactate/albumin measurements in selected SA-AKI cohorts, found trajectory-outcome associations, attenuation under weighting, and modest external discrimination. It does not test this broad pressor-recorded EICU population, this two-point binary exposure, or a late-value-balanced absolute risk difference.

## Estimand, model, and gate-stop execution

The intended estimand remains the normalized overlap-weighted risk difference

`RD_current = weighted P(D=1|X=1) − weighted P(D=1|X=0)`

in the population defined by a fixed C=10 L2 logistic model for `P(X=1|Z)`. Z contains `log(F)`, `log(F)^2`, F collection elapsed time, age, gender indicators, t0, hospital indicators, latest periodic MAP and heart rate in [t0−60,t0), periodic MAP median, duration-weighted adjacent-interval fraction below 65, heart-rate median and distinct observation count in [t0,t0+720), and latest valid creatinine, BUN, total bilirubin, AST and ALT in [t0−360,t0+720] released by t0+720, with missing indicators. B is deliberately omitted because it is the historical information under test.

Exact chemistry system-unit pairs are creatinine/mg/dL, BUN/mg/dL, total bilirubin/mg/dL, AST (SGOT)/Units/L and ALT (SGPT)/Units/L. At duplicate periodic offsets use medians. Continuous variables are pooled 1st/99th-percentile winsorized, median-imputed, and standardized using outcome-blind rules. Overlap weights are 1−e for X=1 and e for X=0, normalized separately by X. The outcome is not used to fit the exposure model.

## Falsification and result meanings

The frozen >=10-point hypothesis is not supported, but it is also not statistically falsified because the required overlap population is unsupported.

- Supportive would require every gate, a 95% interval lower bound >=+10 points, and >=80% positive finite site deletions. It would justify prospective synchronized validation, not treatment.
- Adverse to +10 would require every gate and interval upper bound <+10. That condition cannot be invoked here.
- Opposite would require every gate and interval upper bound <0. It would still not prove protection.
- Inconclusive is mandatory when any population, positivity, event, balance, concentration, convergence, bootstrap, or site gate fails. This is the observed result because positivity fails.

The exact additional data required are prospectively synchronized B and F measurements in enough patients spanning the current-F range with both clearance groups at each clinically relevant F stratum, plus prespecified clinical context and treatment/measurement indications. Merely adding more similarly selected EICU rows or relaxing the gate does not establish overlap.
