# Episode 96 successor: a label-free L24 goals-of-care review boundary

## Clinical decision and hypothesis

At 24 hours after the first qualifying vasopressor exposure, a receiving ICU may have exactly 10% of each hospital's eligible slots for senior goals-of-care/escalation-plan review. The clinically consequential decision is which patients should receive those scarce reviews, not merely which patients are high risk.

The parent’s audited cohort and transport design are retained. The substantive change is the decision target: the queue is intended to surface patients who are both still open for a documented goals-of-care discussion at L24 and at risk of dying within the next six days. The primary endpoint is a data-defined workflow opportunity:

`G7_i = 1` iff the patient is Expired by `H7=t0+11520` and has no finite documented EOL discussion offset in `[0,L24]`, where `L24=t0+1440`.

This is not labeled “missed care” or preventable death. It means only “D7 death without a recorded discussion in this EICU table.” The action-policy gate is:

`open_i = 1` iff no finite `carePlanEOL.cpleoldiscussionoffset` is in `[0,L24]`.

For every hospital, rank open patients first by the portable score and then closed patients by the same score; take exactly `k_h=ceil(0.10 n_h)` rows. Thus all capacity is used, but a documented discussion is never displaced by an undiscussed patient merely because of score noise. The same open-first policy is applied to process, random, and held-out-local references. The score itself does not use EOL fields.

The strongest available evidence supports only a modest prognostic contribution from 24-hour lactate in selected sepsis cohorts (Baysan et al., 2022, PMCID PMC9444407: reported recalibrated C-statistic 0.62, increasing to 0.64 with 24-hour lactate) and the importance of representing informative measurement processes (Sisk et al., 2021, PMCID PMC7810439). It does not establish goals-of-care documentation, fixed-capacity review yield, cross-practice transport, or patient benefit. The unresolved claim is:

> In the audited EICU cohort, can a source-practice, source-label-only score with optional repeat lactate identify a fixed 10%-per-hospital queue whose open-first yield of `G7` events is materially above the otherwise identical process-only queue and not materially below a held-out local-label benchmark, in both repeat-practice directions?

A supportive result would justify only a prospective workflow/impact study of this review policy. It would not justify displaying a probability, changing treatment, or claiming that any death was avoidable.

## Primary estimand

Keep the parent’s frozen Low and High strata and directions `d in {Low-to-High, High-to-Low}`: 15 target hospitals and the opposite 15 source hospitals in each direction. Let `Q^P_{d,h}`, `Q^0_{d,h}`, and `Q^L_{d,h}` be, respectively, the portable `R_full`, portable `R_process`, and held-out-local `R_full` queues after the identical open-first rule. Let `K_d=sum_h k_h`.

`Y^x_{G7,d} = sum_{h in T_d} sum_{i in Q^x_{d,h}} G7_i / K_d`, for `x in {P,0,L}`.

The single primary estimand is:

`M_G7 = min_d [Y^P_{G7,d} - max(Y^0_{G7,d}+0.03, Y^L_{G7,d}-0.02)]`.

The primary hypothesis is `M_G7 >= 0`; deployment-boundary failure is `M_G7 < 0`. The +0.03 and -0.02 are predeclared experimental margins (three additional data-defined workflow-opportunity events per 100 occupied slots, and at most two per 100 below the local reference), not clinical-utility or preventable-death thresholds. Capacity remains fixed at 10%; other capacities are diagnostics only.

The parent’s D7 queue estimand remains a secondary continuity analysis. It must be emitted, but cannot rescue or overturn the G7 primary state. Dinf remains a separate horizon warning only.

The hypothesis is falsified by a valid failure of either margin in either direction, especially failure to exceed `R_process` or failure against the local-label boundary. It is also falsified as a deployable *open-review* policy if open-first queues do not preserve exact capacity, repeatedly select closed patients before open patients, or fail the prespecified support/provenance checks. A crossed interval, deletion instability, sparse EOL support, or adverse computation is inconclusive rather than evidence for or against clinical benefit. The old D7 claim can remain favorable while G7 is adverse; that means mortality ranking did not establish a goals-of-care workflow boundary.

A supportive result means only that this retrospective, structured-documentation endpoint was enriched in a fixed-capacity, cross-practice simulation. It does not establish that a discussion was clinically indicated, that preferences were unknown, that a death was preventable, that review changes treatment or comfort, or that documentation is complete. EOL documentation is a workflow-sensitive proxy and can reflect preference, timing, staffing, transfer, or missingness. The experiment cannot establish actual vasopressor indication, specimen/repeat-lactate availability or viewing, review capacity, patient/family preference, communication quality, treatment effects, utility, safety, fairness, external/temporal transport, or impact. Clinical adjudication, synchronized workflow and capacity data, preference/context review, prospective temporal/external validation, and an impact or randomized study are required for those stronger conclusions. Reference execution would establish computational feasibility only.
