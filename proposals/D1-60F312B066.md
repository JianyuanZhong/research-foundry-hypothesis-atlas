# A minimax support-atlas audit with a blinded longitudinal target-anchor challenge for domain-safe pulmonary-nodule admission routing

## Decision bottleneck and targeted advance

The clinical estimand is unchanged from `[prior hypothesis]`: in the frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC population, can the admission-level `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes be reproduced with sufficiently low text-defined unsafe cross-route contamination to nominate only a prospective **silent** validation? Every recommendation unit in a sampled admission is reviewed, one unsafe minority unit controls the admission, and no conclusion concerns guideline correctness, clinician fault, actual safety, follow-up completion, causal benefit, or patient outcomes.

The parent prevents a route from validating itself through split evidence views, sealed atomic labels, independent all-route auditing, locked cross-product route derivation, and adverse finite-population inference. Its remaining consequential threats are **domain masking, unsupported transport, and shared adjudication/source ambiguity**. A pooled `AUTO` contamination bound can pass while a later possible-calendar band or a particular service/workflow signature is unsafe. The same pre-departure text can also make every reviewer agree on a wrong target identity, while the parent has no independent, target-linked longitudinal challenge to expose that failure. Conversely, requiring nominally adequate performance in every small subgroup can create an impossible 900-admission design and invite post hoc pooling, hierarchical borrowing, or favorable regrouping.

This targeted child preserves the parent's population, chronology, canonical discharge artifact, exhaustive states, unit clocks, component firewalls, all-unit worst-admission routing, route-independent audit, inherited route estimands and margins, fixed sample-size formulas, finite-population inference, and strict noncausal ceiling. It adds a route-blinded, post-seal **longitudinal target-anchor challenge** using later subject-linked radiology as an independent source artifact; this is a falsification/validity check, not a follow-up outcome or rescue label. It replaces pooled or loosely subgrouped release logic with a **pre-label support atlas and minimax certificate**:

1. construct conservative possible-calendar, exact terminal-service, and workflow-complexity signatures before any route label;
2. determine, solely from frozen frame counts and the inherited sample budget, which signatures are statistically auditable; rare, unknown, unseen, or unsupported signatures are never pooled into a release claim and prospectively default to `DEFER`;
3. allocate the unchanged within-stratum sample totals across auditable signatures and a residual envelope by deterministic maximin rules, retaining known positive inclusion and pairwise probabilities for every admission;
4. estimate each supported signature's contamination, minority-unit failure, capture, reproducibility, and workload with simultaneous adverse finite-population bounds;
5. let the worst supported signature, not the pooled average or a shrinkage estimate, govern nomination; and
6. challenge the frozen units with later same-subject radiology artifacts, scored by a disjoint anchor panel after route labels and primary estimates are sealed; explicit target contradictions can block an atom, while no later report is never treated as nonadherence or failure; and
7. bind any retrospective nomination to an explicit prospective bridge in which actual future dates are used, novel signatures are quarantined, and no route is exposed to clinicians.

This is deliberately narrower but more honest than unrestricted “transport.” A favorable result may nominate silent validation only for an explicit allow-list of supported within-MIMIC signatures. It cannot establish forward temporal or external-site transport.

## Blinded longitudinal target-anchor challenge (the targeted repair)

The parent’s reviewers can share an error because they all read the same pre-departure radiology chain. To make that failure clinically falsifiable without pretending that MIMIC contains follow-up completion, add a separately sealed **target-anchor challenge**. It uses a later radiology artifact as a distinct temporal source and asks only whether a target parsed at discharge can be recognized as the same target later. It does not ask whether the recommendation was guideline-appropriate, whether a patient complied, whether a lesion was malignant, or whether an outcome improved.

### Exact extraction and temporal boundary

For every sampled `N_A` admission, after `D` and all primary route/index/audit labels are frozen, query the complete ordinary-file `[internal dataset path]` (`note/radiology`; columns `note_id,subject_id,hadm_id,note_type,note_seq,charttime,storetime,text`) and its companion `[internal dataset path]` (`note/radiology_detail`; columns `note_id,subject_id,field_name,field_value,field_ordinal`). Join on `(subject_id,note_id)`, retain every detail ordinal and both `parent_note_id` and `addendum_note_id`, and reuse the frozen reciprocal-chain parser. Do not use `note/discharge`, POE, diagnoses, or procedures for this challenge.

A candidate anchor report has the same `subject_id`, a nonmissing `charttime` strictly after `D` and no later than the within-subject interval `D+180*24 hours`, and a nonmissing `storetime` satisfying `storetime>=charttime`; an `AR` report is eligible only through a valid reciprocal `RR` chain. A report with missing/contradictory times, unresolved parent/addendum links, unreadable text, or uncertain finality is `ANCHOR_UNKNOWN`, not discarded. The 180-day interval is a relative within-subject interval; no raw shifted calendar year is compared across subjects. Reports with the same `(subject_id,note_id)` are one artifact, and an addendum revises rather than duplicates its parent.

First freeze an automated high-recall candidate set using only the already sealed pulmonary/nodule/opacity and thoracic-CT lexicons plus target-independent report metadata; the later-report candidate rule cannot inspect route, unit state, sample cell, or outcomes. The exact candidate rule, source row numbers, and hashes are sealed before anchor reviewers see any labels. If no eligible later artifact exists, the admission has `ANCHOR_NO_OPPORTUNITY`; this is a missing-observation state, not a negative anchor.

### Independent anchor labels

Two reviewers from a roster disjoint from eligibility, recommendation, context, departure-plan, safety, and data-quality roles receive a packet containing the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, the deterministic operative-unit inventory rendered as opaque unit IDs with target-span text removed, and one or more candidate later radiology artifacts. They see no route, route components, clock category, service/era atom, discharge plan, POE, post-label outcome, sample weight, or other reviewer labels. They independently label each candidate later artifact relative to each opaque unit: `ANCHOR_MATCH` (the later artifact explicitly identifies the same target by unambiguous continuity such as same lesion/location and/or an explicit reference to the prior finding), `ANCHOR_CONFLICT` (it explicitly identifies a different target or directly contradicts the unit’s target identity), `ANCHOR_NO_MATCH_MENTION` (the artifact contains a relevant pulmonary finding but does not establish continuity), or `ANCHOR_UNKNOWN` (ambiguous, technically inadequate, unresolved linkage, or unreadable). They may not infer a match from mere presence of a chest CT or from an identical recommendation interval.

A report can support at most one match per unit, and multiple later artifacts do not create multiple observations: for each unit use the ordered first explicit match, first explicit conflict, otherwise no-match/unknown rule frozen before labels. Reviewer disagreement is retained as `ANCHOR_U`; it is not resolved by consensus. A senior adjudicator may independently read disagreement packets only to create a separate sensitivity assignment; the primary analysis treats every disagreement as compatible with the adverse state. Anchor reviewers cannot change eligibility, unit boundaries, routes, clocks, or any primary label.

### Anchor estimand and clinically anchored gate

Define an admission-level anchor challenge outcome only among admissions with at least one eligible later artifact and at least one operative unit that the artifact could plausibly address. `a_i=1` if any active unit has an explicit `ANCHOR_CONFLICT` under either independent lane or an `ANCHOR_U` compatible with conflict; `o_i=1` if the admission has an anchor opportunity; and `m_i=1` if the admission has an explicit match under both lanes for every active unit that is explicitly addressed. `m_i` is a reproducibility diagnostic, not a claim that all units were followed. Also retain unit-level counts of explicit matches, conflicts, no-match mentions, unknowns, and candidate reports.

For each final atlas atom, estimate the design-weighted opportunity total, the adverse anchor-conflict ratio `A_CONFLICT,g = sum w_i a_i / sum w_i o_i`, and the lower bound for dual-lane explicit concordance among explicitly addressed units. Use the same HT/Hájek estimators, nonresponse/unknown adverse assignment, exact inversion, and max-statistic simultaneous family as the primary gates. The anchor gate is **not** applied when the atom has fewer than 30 sampled opportunity admissions or when the finite-population lower opportunity bound is zero; those atoms are unsupported/inconclusive and cannot be placed on an `AUTO` allow-list. For an atom with an adequate opportunity denominator, require a simultaneous upper bound `U95(A_CONFLICT,g)<=0.05` for `AUTO` and require no conflict-compatible unresolved target among an `AUTO` admission. The threshold is a prespecified operational source-validity margin, not a clinical harm rate. `RECON` may report the anchor conflict rate and must not use a favorable anchor to override its own route gates.

This challenge is deliberately asymmetric. An explicit later contradiction is a clinically meaningful falsification of target continuity and blocks the affected `AUTO` atom; a later report that does not mention the target, or no later report, cannot prove an unsafe recommendation because outpatient imaging and external care are incompletely observed. Anchor evidence is a secondary source-validity gate and cannot repair a missing discharge artifact, route disagreement, under-24-hour clock, or unsafe minority unit.

## Fixed maximin probability sample within 900 reviewed admissions

* for every other noncensus stratum, take the larger of 120 or the corresponding ±7-point sample, capped at `N_h`; and
* `n_h=ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25/{e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`.

The sentinel census plus all formula samples must be at most 900 admissions. If it exceeds 900, obtain resources under a new approved protocol or declare feasibility failure. Never reduce a required sample, omit sentinels, sample units, replace admissions, or adapt to route prevalence, unsafe events, disagreement, response, or workload.

4. break exact ties lexicographically by `(h,E,service,W)`.

## Preserved estimands and domain-conditional estimands

The inferential and sampling unit remains the first eligible admission; recommendation units are nested observations. Preserve all inherited pooled totals, burdens, chronology states, route agreement measures, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, `leak_AUTO`, additional-unit rate, deterministic-one-to-multiple error, and workload.

Preserve the parent's adverse event definition: `d_i=1` if either initial index lane calls `AUTO`; `x_i=1` when `d_i=1` and the other index lane, any audit-compatible route, minority-unit safety audit, missing audit, redaction/boundary failure, timing limitation, or adverse unknown is compatible with `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`. `C_AUTO=sum_i x_i/sum_i d_i`,

estimated by the Hájek ratio of HT totals with numerator and denominator always reported. This is text-defined audit contamination, not clinical harm.

`T_yg_hat=sum_{i in s} w_i I(G_i=g)y_i`

and Hájek ratios of domain HT totals. Also estimate the atom's design-weighted physician-hours per 100 atom admissions, packet-time quantiles, nonresponse, number of units, certainty-stratum share, maximum weight, normalized maximum weight, and Kish effective sample size for every denominator.

Define atom-level certification vector

`Theta_g={C_AUTO,g,D_MIX,g,leak_AUTO,g,confirmation_g,AC1_g,Se_RECON,g,PPV_RECON,g,PPV_TIMING,g,Se_DEFER,g,workload_g}`.

For a proposed route allow-list `L`, define its minimax safety summaries as the **worst simultaneous confidence endpoint**, not the worst point estimate:

* `M_AUTO(L)=max_{g in L} U95(C_AUTO,g)`;
* `M_LEAK(L)=max_{g in L} U95(leak_AUTO,g)`;
* `m_CONFIRM(L)=min_{g in L} L95(confirmation_g)`;
* analogous minima for capture and maxima for workload.

No population weighting, standardization, hierarchical shrinkage, empirical-Bayes estimate, regression interaction, pooled denominator, or favorable consensus may satisfy a primary atom gate. Pooled estimates and early/late contrasts are required diagnostics, but direct atom-specific absolute margins govern release. A stable difference around an unsafe level does not pass; a noisy difference between two individually safe supported atoms does not by itself fail.

## Finite-population inference, multiplicity, and unknowns

Treat each `(h,g)` and residual cell as a survey stratum. For scalar total `T_y`, use

`V_hat(T_y_hat)=sum_hg N_hg^2*(1-n_hg/N_hg)*s_y,hg^2/n_hg`,

with zero census contribution. For a ratio `R`, use Taylor linearization `z_i=y_i-R_hat*d_i` and divide the corresponding total variance by `T_d_hat^2`. Report numerator and denominator totals; do not report a ratio with zero estimated denominator.

Generate at least 20,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates independently within every noncensus design cell, retaining all units, views, labels, response states, and workload for an admission together; certainty admissions remain fixed. The same replicate drives pooled, atom, residual-envelope, minimax, diagnostic-domain, and workload quantities. Use a max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over all inherited pooled gates, every final atlas atom's primary route gates, and the minimax endpoints. The late possible-calendar challenge is included in that family. Secondary exact-code/path/admission/disposition diagnostics form a separately adjusted family and cannot rescue primary failure.

For rare or zero events, also invert the exact stratified finite-population distribution. Use hypergeometric inversion for unconditional binary totals and multivariate-hypergeometric enumeration of `{unsafe AUTO,safe AUTO,non-AUTO}` for `C_AUTO`, respecting cell sample counts. Use the widest valid simultaneous bound from exact inversion, bootstrap, and adverse partial identification. Zero sampled unsafe events yields a positive upper bound unless the entire relevant finite population is censused.

No selected admission is replaced. Missing, late, schema-invalid, unreadable, withdrawn, or access-failed reviews; missing safety audit; redaction breach; unknown clock; domain parse failure; service tie; and unit-boundary disagreement remain explicit. For every estimand, optimize all compatible exhaustive route/event assignments for sharp adverse lower and upper endpoints; use linear-fractional optimization for ratios inside each replicate. An index `AUTO` with missing audit evidence is unsafe for the upper bound. Unknown domain membership contributes to every compatible diagnostic domain and to residual, never to a favorable release atom. Complete-case, unweighted, model-assisted, and consensus estimates are diagnostics only.

The first-eligible rule requires unique `subject_id` in `N_A`; a duplicate stops the run rather than inviting a cluster correction. Balance reviewer assignments across era, service, and workflow before labels. Report reviewer-specific response, disagreement, and time, with a secondary reviewer-resampling sensitivity; primary bounds remain design-based and adverse.

## Baselines

Apply the identical frozen sample, labels, weights, and uncertainty to:

1. the parent's pooled gate;
2. a macro-group service/era analysis that would pool rare exact signatures;
3. marginal-majority route assignment;
4. deterministic sampling-stratum routing;
5. recommendation-unit majority routing;
6. terminal-service-majority and possible-era-majority routing;
7. a sentinel-only safety analysis;
8. favorable consensus, incorporated-reference, complete-case, and unweighted analyses; and
9. a hierarchical shrinkage sensitivity shown only to quantify how borrowing can make an unsupported atom appear certifiable.

None can nominate a route. The targeted repair is falsified computationally if a planted failing atom is allowed through because pooled or shrunken estimates pass.

## Prespecified certification and nomination gates

Governance must approve margins and the absolute workload budget before any real labels are released; otherwise `NO_SELECTION`. Retain inherited defaults: `tau_AUTO=0.05`, confirmation `rho=0.90`, AC1 `0.70`, `sigma_RECON=0.95`, `pi_RECON=0.80`, `pi_TIMING=0.95`, and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`. These are operational silent-validation prerequisites, not acceptable harm rates.

Every route first must pass all inherited pooled source, retrieval, denominator, chronology, redaction, seal/access, event, effective-sample-size, precision, and adverse-bound gates. In particular retain at least 200 `N_A`, at least 60 `E_A`, overall design ESS at least 150, normalized maximum weight at most 0.10, and simultaneous all-`N_A` burden half-width no greater than 5 per 100. Broken firewalls, absent governance, canonical duplication, or mandatory review above 900 blocks all selection.

An atom `g` is route-claimable only if it is in the final pre-label atlas, all domain fields are known, at least 30 sampled route/reference-positive admissions exist as applicable, at least 10 arise outside the sentinel census, route-denominator ESS is at least 50, normalized maximum weight within the atom is at most 0.10, and the route burden has a positive simultaneous lower bound. Lack of these conditions is unsupported, never a pass.

For each claimable atom:

* **AUTO:** at least 30 sampled candidate-`AUTO` admissions; lower confirmation/raw-agreement bound `>=0.90`; lower AC1 `>=0.70`; upper `C_AUTO<=0.05`; upper `D_MIX<=0.10`; upper `leak_AUTO<=0.02`; no safety-auditor-confirmed unresolved minority unit remains compatible with `AUTO`; among atoms with an adequate anchor-opportunity denominator, simultaneous upper `A_CONFLICT<=0.05` and no anchor conflict-compatible unresolved target in an `AUTO` admission; and upper workload below the approved absolute budget.
* **RECON:** at least 30 index `RECON` and 30 audit unsafe-content admissions; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `Se_RECON>=0.95`; lower `PPV_RECON>=0.80`; upper `AUTO` leakage among these cases `<=0.02`; and workload pass.
* **TIMING:** at least 30 index `TIMING`; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `PPV_TIMING>=0.95`; no use of an index-only clock when another active unit is under 24 hours; and workload pass.
* **DEFER:** at least 30 independent-audit uncertain-reference admissions; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `Se_DEFER>=0.95`; and no adverse-compatible uncertain admission routed `AUTO`.

The proposed allow-list for a route is exactly the set of pre-label atlas atoms that independently pass. The minimax endpoints over that set must also pass. There is no unrestricted nomination unless every occupied known atom passes and the simultaneous upper population burden of residual/unknown atoms is below 1%; otherwise silent validation must enforce the explicit allow-list and map all other signatures to `DEFER`. At least one atom and a positive lower bound on its route burden are required; an empty allow-list is `NO_SELECTION`.

Two-lane silent validation requires atom-level passes for both `AUTO` and `RECON`. A `RECON`-only allow-list may be nominated if its atoms pass while `AUTO` fails. A pooled pass cannot rescue a supported failing atom; a passing neighboring atom cannot rescue an unsupported one.

## No-selection rules

After protocol hash and before interpretation, prohibit:

1. changing population, first-eligible choice, `D`, reciprocal chains, canonical `DS`, unit schema, clocks, states, views, matching, route precedence, estimands, margins, or gates;
2. regrouping exact service codes, moving a rare/unknown atom into the atlas, changing calendar cutoffs, or redefining workflow after labels;
3. route-, event-, disagreement-, response-, workload-, or result-based top-ups, replacements, stopping, atom merging, or sample reallocation;
4. restricting audit to `AUTO`, sentinels, omissions, or apparent difficult cases;
5. sampling or weighting recommendation units;
6. using index evidence to repair audit labels or audit evidence to repair index labels;
7. favorable unit matching, consensus deletion, complete-case deletion, response weighting, or model imputation in a primary gate;
8. certifying a rare, residual, unknown, absent, or newly observed signature through pooling, adjacency, standardization, shrinkage, or clinical intuition; and
9. claiming forward or external transport from shifted dates or one-site retrospective text.

Any affected route/atom is invalidated. Data may motivate a new child, but cannot be silently reused as untouched confirmation.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. Recompute source/schema hashes, row counts, `N_A`, unique subjects, reciprocal chains, clocks, exhaustive states, domains, atlas, sample allocation, `pi_i`, `pi_ij`, and ordered draw from sealed inputs. Unexplained drift stops analysis.
2. Shifted-date trap: two subjects with the same raw shifted year but different `anchor_year_group` must follow their widened possible intervals. Boundary-crossing intervals must be `E_BRIDGE`; malformed values must be unknown.
3. Service trap: post-`D` rows cannot alter workflow; tied distinct terminal codes, missing times/codes, and absent rows become unknown. Source order cannot resolve a tie.
4. Atlas trap: create many small atoms. The deterministic support-pruning algorithm must move atoms to residual before labels and never merge them after seeing outcomes.
5. Allocation trap: rerun the integer optimizer and SRS from hashes/seeds. Processing order or labels cannot change cells, sample sizes, or IDs.
6. Positivity trap: every admission, including residual/unknown, must have `pi_i>0`; pairwise probabilities and no-replacement behavior must match the declared design.
7. Domain-masking trap: place unsafe `AUTO` events only in one supported late/service/workflow atom at a rate that leaves pooled `C_AUTO<0.05` but atom `C_AUTO>0.05`. Pooled analysis may pass; that atom and unrestricted nomination must fail.
8. Rare-domain trap: put all failures in a rare residual atom. The experiment may estimate a residual burden, but must never certify that atom or let a macro-group pass expose it to `AUTO`.
9. Shrinkage trap: make an unsupported atom's model estimate favorable through borrowing. The model result cannot alter certification.
10. Workload trap: keep pooled hours acceptable while doubling one atom's workload. That atom must fail without contaminating evidence for independently passing atoms.
11. Unknown trap: concentrate missing service/date/clock/audit/boundary evidence in high-weight unsafe admissions. Adverse bounds must block favorable certification.
12. Zero-event trap: zero unsafe events in a noncensus atom must yield a positive finite-population upper bound.
13. Multiplicity trap: nominal atom intervals may pass while simultaneous intervals fail; only simultaneous adverse limits govern.
14. Minority-unit trap: nine pure omissions plus one conflict must be `A_MIXED` within `RECON`; duplicating safe units changes neither route nor weight.
15. Differential-verification trap: every apparent route receives identical audit components and safety review.
16. Leakage/incorporation traps: forbidden packet canaries, shared pseudonyms, altered append-only labels, route-before-seal execution, or index-informed audit adjudication must fail controls and leave primary results unchanged or invalidate the run.
17. Nonresponse trap: concentrate missing labels among unsafe high-weight cases; favorable complete-case estimates cannot pass adverse bounds.
18. Finite-population trap: small synthetic populations with unequal cell fractions must recover known HT totals and nominal exact/simulation coverage; unweighted estimates should fail.
19. Prospective-signature trap: a novel service code or workflow combination must map to `DEFER`, never to its nearest retrospective atom.
20. Anchor trap: later reports outside `(D,D+180 days]`, reports from another subject, post-`storetime`/`charttime` contradictions, one-way addenda, and duplicate parent/addendum artifacts must not create opportunities; an explicit later target contradiction must block the affected AUTO atom, while no later report must remain `ANCHOR_NO_OPPORTUNITY` rather than a failure.
21. Shared-error trap: alter only the anchor-lane labels or create reviewer disagreement; primary routes must remain unchanged, while adverse anchor-compatible assignments widen the source-validity bound.
22. Claim checker rejects exact-calendar trend, external-site generalizability, guideline appropriateness, clinician awareness/failure, live safety, causal benefit, follow-up completion, or patient outcome language.

The verifier can mechanically check sources, joins, times, domain/atlas code, fixed allocation, probabilities, packet/access separation, seals, route code, finite-population estimators, exact/bootstrap bounds, adverse assignments, minimax gates, allow-list mapping, and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish clinical truth, service ownership, appropriateness, utility margins, actual viewing/editing/communication, external transport, or benefit.

## Interpretation

**Supportive** means the inherited pooled audit passes and at least one support-qualified atom independently satisfies every relevant simultaneous adverse route, workload, and (when opportunity is adequate) longitudinal anchor gate; all atoms in the stated allow-list pass, and minimax endpoints pass. This supports only a prospective silent test of the frozen route process in those exact observable signatures, with all other signatures forced to `DEFER`.

**Adverse** means a supported atom has adequately precise contamination, leakage, missed unsafe/uncertain states, poor reproducibility, minority-unit failure, or excessive workload beyond a gate. That atom is excluded even if pooled performance is favorable. A domain-specific negative result is consequential because it prevents a pooled average from licensing an unsafe workflow. An explicit later target conflict is an adverse source-validity result for the affected `AUTO` atom; absent or sparse later artifacts are inconclusive and do not imply failed follow-up.

**Inconclusive** includes no feasible atlas within 900 admissions, sparse route events, poor atom ESS, wide simultaneous bounds, excessive unknown/residual burden, retrieval undercoverage, nonresponse, broken seals, unapproved workload budget, or unavailable exact calendar mapping. Unsupported is not equivalent to adverse, but it still cannot be released. Failure to reject heterogeneity is not evidence of transport.

## Explicit prospective bridge and evidence ceiling

A retrospective allow-list is only a nomination. Before any live route is shown or used, freeze the extractor, eligibility rules, reciprocal-chain logic, unit schema, component views, matching/route code, margins, allow-list signatures, and default-`DEFER` mapper. Conduct a separately approved prospective silent study of consecutive adult discharges using real calendar dates and immutable report finalization/addenda, report availability/viewing, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing-risk context, and final transmitted artifact.

Prospective records matching a certified retrospective service/workflow signature enter a silent bridge stratum; any novel code, unknown field, changed service semantics, new workflow combination, or out-of-support signature is quarantined as `DEFER` and reported, never mapped by similarity. Independent experts remain blinded to silent routes and later outcomes. Prespecify at least 100 silent route-positive admissions overall and at least 30 per claimed atom, or an accrual stop; apply the same all-route minority-unit audit and simultaneous adverse contamination/capture gates. Actual-calendar early-versus-current transport is tested only there. This prospective study requires a new approved workload and is not smuggled into the present fixed <=900-admission retrospective burden.

Only after a successful silent bridge could a governed route-stratified trial compare usual care with clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation in validated `AUTO` signatures and side-by-side reconciliation in validated `RECON` signatures. Clinical appropriateness, correction/dismissal, inappropriate insertion, responsibility, communication, scheduling, follow-up completion, harms, utility, and benefit require new workflow/outcome data and expert governance.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The parent ensures that a route cannot validate itself. This child ensures that a large pooled domain cannot validate a route on behalf of a smaller unsafe domain and that statistical non-support cannot be disguised as transport. Its principal advance is a decision-ready, falsifiable support boundary: common signatures can be audited and certified only on their own adverse evidence; rare, unknown, and novel signatures remain visible in finite-population summaries but are not exposed to `AUTO`. Direct absolute minimax gates avoid both pooled rescue and the weaker logic that a nonsignificant interaction proves equivalence. The fixed atlas and maximin allocation use the inherited <=900-admission burden without outcome-driven top-ups.

Uncertainty remains material. `anchor_year_group` supports only widened possible-calendar bands, not exact retrospective dates. `curr_service`, ICU exposure, and service transitions are workflow proxies, not validated ownership. Strict atomization may yield few or no certifiable atoms; that is an honest design result, not a reason to pool. Independent experts can share correlated error; the disjoint later-artifact anchor challenge exposes some target-continuity errors but cannot establish ground truth. Later radiology is a source-validity probe, not evidence of follow-up completion, and may be absent despite care elsewhere. The canonical `DS` and storage clocks remain incomplete proxies for the actual transmitted artifact and opportunity to act. Retrieval can miss semantic opportunities. Therefore even a complete pass supports only a restricted within-site prospective silent bridge, never retrospective automation, forward or external transport, clinical safety, or benefit.
