# Corrected P-linkage diagnostic for the frozen repeat-TACE monitoring experiment

## Proposal status and relation to the parent

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s population, event definition, assay clocks, nested models, denominators, prognostic estimand, and noncausal claim boundary. The child repairs and evaluates the inherited linkage diagnostic: the inherited runner permuted B while leaving correctly linked P in M1, so it could not test whether the apparent incremental value of P depended on patient linkage.

The child’s hypothesis is:

> Among selected repeat-TACE episodes with an observed complete B/P/Y triplet, contemporaneous pre-repeat laboratory value P has reproducible out-of-fold prognostic information for the subsequent short-horizon laboratory outcome Y beyond remote baseline B and the locked compact decision-time core. Breaking only the patient linkage of numeric P should remove that incremental predictive signal, subject to the stated restricted exchangeability limitation.

This is a prognostic/predictive hypothesis, not a causal or clinical-outcome hypothesis. It does not assert toxicity, recovery, hepatic injury, treatment response, safety, clinical benefit, or a treatment effect.

## Frozen population and event construction

1. Identify HCC by literal `hepatocellular carcinoma` in `Diagnosis Name`, joined to the corresponding encounter time because diagnosis rows have no timestamp.
2. Identify TACE strictly by case-insensitive literal `TACE` or literal `chemoembolization` in `Surgery`.
3. Collapse procedure rows to one TACE episode per patient per calendar day, retaining deterministic earliest start-time/source-order resolution.
4. For each patient, select the earliest adjacent TACE pair whose calendar-day gap is 14–180 days. TACE2 is the second episode.
5. Require adult HCC documented by or before TACE2 and the parent’s observation-era rule: TACE2 calendar year 2018–2024 and recorded observation through at least TACE2 + 14 days.
6. Construct the recorded systemic-pathway arm from medication/procedure evidence on TACE2 days 1–14, excluding prior systemic exposure, placebo records, bevacizumab-only ambiguity, and generic target/immunotherapy procedure ambiguity exactly as in the parent. The comparison arm is the remaining eligible non-excluded pathway cohort.
7. Define the first subsequent TACE episode on TACE2 calendar days 15–90 as the repeat event, independently of laboratory availability or values.

## Frozen assay clocks and outcome

Analyze the two exact assay names `Albumin` and `Total Bilirubin` separately. Parse only signed decimal/scientific numeric values, recognizing inequality prefixes; the primary analysis retains uncensored values with no inequality prefix. For duplicate groups defined by patient, encounter, assay, and laboratory timestamp, exclude discordant values and resolve concordant duplicates deterministically by source order.

For every selected event and assay:

- B is the latest valid assay value on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1.
- P is the latest valid value from the repeat-event encounter in `[event time − 72 hours, event time)`.
- Y is the valid value from the same repeat-event encounter in `(event time, event time + 72 hours]` nearest +24 hours; exact-distance ties choose the earlier timestamp and then deterministic source order.
- Retain only rows satisfying `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`.
- Define `P_lead_hours = event_time − P_time` and `days = event calendar day − TACE2 calendar day`.

The estimand is the paired out-of-fold difference in predictive error between the two nested prognostic models, with RMSE gain standardized by the assay-specific sample SD of Y (`ddof=1`):

- M0: `Y ~ B + systemic_arm + age + sex + days + P_lead_hours`.
- M1: M0 plus contemporaneous P.

Age median imputation, age-missing indicator, sex indicators, zero-variance filtering, standardization, ridge selection over `{0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100}`, and all preprocessing are learned within each training fold. No outcome is used to form the population, clocks, or linkage strata.

## Corrected linkage diagnostic

The inherited diagnostic is invalid for the intended question because it permuted B, not P. M1 therefore retained the correctly linked patient P and could continue to show P’s predictive value regardless of B reassignment.

The corrected primary diagnostic uses the inherited deterministic arm-balanced five-fold outer partitions and keeps the following exactly fixed:

- B;
- the compact core (`systemic_arm`, age, sex, days, and P lead hours);
- Y;
- patient rows and row membership;
- inherited outer-fold membership;
- arm;
- broad event-era structure.

For each assay, reassign only the numeric P values within strata defined by arm × inherited outer fold × broad event era (2018–2021 versus 2022–2025), then refit M1 under the same cross-fitting and preprocessing rules. M0 predictions remain fixed; M1 is refit after each reassignment. The linkage placebo therefore breaks the patient linkage of P while preserving held-out support, arm composition, and a coarse temporal assay mix. It does not alter P timing, P lead hours, or the event definition.

This is explicitly a restricted linkage-placebo diagnostic. It is not an exact conditional-randomization test of `Y independent of P given B and core`, because raw P reassignment also changes P’s empirical dependence on B and the core. Exact conditional inference would require defensible exchangeability within the strata or a validated cross-fitted conditional generator for `P | B, core, arm, fold, era`. The observed-versus-placebo contrast must therefore be reported with this limitation rather than called an exact null test.

## Interpretation and falsification criteria

Supportive evidence is a positive M1-versus-M0 OOF gain for both assays, stable in sign across outcome-blind partitions, with uncertainty intervals away from zero and a restricted P-linkage-placebo distribution near zero that does not reproduce the observed gain. The executed results meet this operational pattern. The strongest supported conclusion is limited to reproducible short-horizon prognostic information from correctly linked contemporaneous P beyond B and the compact core in the selected complete-triplet episodes.

Adverse evidence would include a nonpositive primary gain, intervals materially spanning or concentrating below zero, frequent partition sign reversals, a linkage-placebo distribution comparable to the observed gain, or concentration of the result in a small number of influential rows. Such findings would weaken or falsify the claim of robust incremental predictive information, though they would not establish absence in unobserved or differently selected episodes.

Selection-sensitive evidence would include a materially different result under a prespecified, separately documented weighted estimand, low effective sample size, or strong dependence on complete-triplet availability. Weighting cannot establish missing-at-random laboratory observation and cannot remove unmeasured clinical selection.

Inconclusive evidence includes failure of denominator or temporal gates, failed computation, inability to estimate a bootstrap replicate, inadequate exchangeability justification for the restricted placebo, or evidence that a clinically meaningful interpretation cannot be adjudicated from the available data. A null restricted placebo is not by itself proof of conditional independence; a positive placebo contrast is not by itself proof of causality.

The dataset lacks laboratory units and validated reference ranges, adjudicated illness or hepatic injury, albumin administration, transfusion and fluid records, imaging/tumor burden, procedural details, reliable mortality, outside care, verified medication administration, and treatment rationale. Claims about toxicity, recovery, response, safety, benefit, treatment effect, mechanism, or clinical utility require these data, expert adjudication, or another study.

## Future conditional-null extension

If an exact conditional diagnostic is required, fit a cross-fitted model for `P | B, systemic_arm, age, sex, days, P_lead_hours` using training data only, diagnose calibration and residual dependence, and generate conditional P values for held-out rows while preserving the observed B/core/Y and outer folds. The resulting inference would still depend on the generator and its exchangeability assumptions. It must not be described as exact unless those assumptions and validation are defensible. This extension is not needed to label the present analysis as a restricted linkage placebo.
