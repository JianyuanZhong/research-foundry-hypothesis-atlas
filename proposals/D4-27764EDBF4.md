# Baseline-only decision-value experiment for continuous cystatin-C discordance

## Lineage, unresolved question, and clinical advance

This is a substantive prospective child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the exact UK Biobank snapshot, one-to-one source joins, selected first-repeat-attender cohort, inclusive 2–8-year visit boundary, equations, repeat creatinine-eGFR status outcome, ten repeated five-fold split seeds, and aggregate-only privacy boundary. It changes the scientific design where the executed parent identified a deployment blocker: observed elapsed time is removed from every deployable score, and the primary estimands become threshold- and capacity-specific decision quantities rather than another localization test.

The strongest claim already supported is narrow. In 16,372 selected complete first-repeat attenders, adding baseline `Z = log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)` to a flexible model containing future-known elapsed repeat time reduced held-out log loss overall and especially among the 1,038 participants with baseline eGFRcr 60 to less than 75. That same executed experiment did not establish useful event concentration: at 10% capacity it gained only 1 outcome overall, its borderline contribution was -2.4 outcomes, and its uncertainty included clinically unhelpful values. This is evidence of protocol-bounded probabilistic information, not CKD, measured GFR, utility, or deployability.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

**Among the same selected first-repeat attenders, a score computable entirely at baseline that adds continuous cystatin-C discordance to age, sex, and baseline creatinine-eGFR has worthwhile incremental decision value over the baseline creatinine-only score for identifying low repeat creatinine-eGFR status at the next protocol assessment: at a prespecified 2% action threshold it adds at least 1 net true-positive equivalent per 1,000 participants, and at a fixed 10% monitoring capacity it adds at least 5 observed low-status outcomes per 1,637 slots.**

This directly tests whether the parent's sharp log-loss localization failed fixed capacity because it merely sharpened probabilities among largely the same people, because its signal depended on future-known elapsed time, or because it changes decisions only near an absolute threshold rather than the top 10% rank tail. A supportive result would justify external prospective validation of a baseline cystatin-informed monitoring rule. A result that rules out both practical floors would provide a consequential stop signal against promoting the same-snapshot prediction finding to triage.

## The temporal identification boundary and mandatory stop gate

The available data do **not** identify a conventional fixed-horizon binary event policy. Each person has one low/not-low status measured at a variable first-repeat time. A positive status says only that eGFRcr was below 60 at that visit; it does not date onset, and status need not be absorbing. Consequently, treating positives as interval-censored onset events in `(baseline, repeat]` would invent monotonicity and cannot be used. There is no common four-year measurement, no serial measurements bracketing four years, no validated onset date, and no outcome ascertainment independent of future attendance.

Accordingly, the computable primary estimand is explicitly schedule-marginal:

> decision performance for low creatinine-eGFR status at the next observed protocol repeat among selected people whose repeat occurs 2–8 years after baseline, averaged over this cohort's observed repeat-time distribution.

Actual elapsed time may be used only to enforce the inherited cohort boundary and to audit temporal robustness; it is forbidden from all deployable design matrices, scaling, ranking, thresholding, recalibration, and tie breaking.

If compilation, verification, or interpretation requires “four-year risk,” cumulative incidence, time-to-event benefit, benefit of earlier testing, or a policy whose action changes visit timing, return `STOP_UNIDENTIFIED_FIXED_HORIZON` without fitting. The exact additional data needed are a prespecified common-horizon renal measurement or validated longitudinal event/onset ascertainment for baseline-eligible participants, follow-up/censoring coverage for non-events, and outcomes collected regardless of the modeled action. No current-status or interval-censored survival workaround may waive this gate.

The schedule-marginal experiment below remains computable and clinically informative as an internal triage falsification. It is not a claim of fixed-horizon clinical utility.

## Primary decision estimands and clinically interpretable policies

For threshold `p_t`, define model action `A_m(p_t)=I(p_m>=p_t)` from each repeat's raw held-out probability and

`NB_m(p_t) = TP_m/N - FP_m/N * p_t/(1-p_t)`.

The primary contrast is `Delta_NB_0.02 = NB_Mc-B(0.02)-NB_M0-B(0.02)`, averaged over the ten repeats and reported per 1,000 participants. A 2% threshold explicitly values one true positive as equivalent to 49 false-positive monitoring actions; it is an operational exchange rate for a low-burden confirmatory-monitoring pathway, not a clinically validated utility. The worthwhile floor is `Delta_NB=0.001`, one net true-positive equivalent per 1,000. Report model, treat-none (`NB=0`), and treat-all decision curves.

Prespecified secondary thresholds are 1% and 5%, corresponding respectively to 99 and 19 false-positive actions per true positive. They test whether any apparent value is confined to an unusually permissive or restrictive exchange rate. They cannot replace a failed 2% primary result.

The co-primary capacity estimand preserves the parent's operational policy: within each held-out fold and repeat, stably rank raw probabilities and select exactly the floor-plus-largest-remainder allocation summing to 1,637 people, 10% of `S_A`. Define `Delta_E10` as the ten-repeat mean difference in observed outcomes selected by `Mc-B` versus `M0-B`. Its worthwhile floor remains +5 outcomes per 1,637 slots. Prespecified secondary 5% capacity selects exactly 819 participants per repeat by floor plus largest remainder and reports `Delta_E5`; it is descriptive unless the 10% gate passes. Report selected risk, event capture, model overlap, and selection counts exactly.

## Falsification and outcome meanings

All classifications require passed computation, calibration and pooled support gates.

- **Supportive joint policy signal:** the 98% lower bound for `Delta_NB_0.02` is above zero, its point estimate is at least 0.001, it is positive in at least 8 of 10 repeats, `Mc-B` has positive net benefit versus both treat-none and treat-all at 2%, and the 98% lower bound for `Delta_E10` is above zero with point estimate at least +5 and at least 8 positive repeats. This supports only schedule-marginal baseline-input decision information in selected repeat attenders and motivates external prospective validation.
- **Threshold-specific but not capacity-compatible:** the 2% net-benefit gate passes but the 10% capacity gate does not. This would resolve the parent's pattern as probability movement useful at that explicit exchange rate but not as a fixed 10% rank policy; it is not evidence for a capacity-limited program.
- **Capacity-specific but threshold-uncertified:** the capacity gate passes while calibration or 2% net benefit fails. This supports rank concentration only and forbids probability-threshold deployment.
- **Adverse to worthwhile incremental policy / operational stop signal:** the 98% upper bound for `Delta_NB_0.02` is below the 0.001 worthwhile floor and the 98% upper bound for `Delta_E10` is below +5. If either upper bound is below zero with at least 8 adverse repeat signs, additionally label that axis directionally adverse. Ruling out both floors is evidence that the parent's log-loss localization does not translate into the prespecified baseline policy, not evidence against cystatin C biologically.
- **Inconclusive:** practical floors are neither passed nor ruled out, the two primary axes disagree without meeting a named mixed pattern, or temporal robustness is underpowered. Non-significance is never equivalence. `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED`, `THRESHOLD_POLICY_UNCERTIFIED`, and `STOP_UNIDENTIFIED_FIXED_HORIZON` are stopping outcomes, not negative biological results.

The hypothesis is falsified as a worthwhile joint policy if both practical upper-bound criteria are met. It is not falsified merely because the inherited +5 capacity floor fails at a point estimate, and it is not supported by log loss alone.
