# UKB metabolic progression: support-aware confirmed transition without immortal time

## Episode-26 child and actual scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s exact five-source UK Biobank binding, assessment-linked two-panel exposure, current-level versus dated-change estimands, source-agreement audit, participant split, competing death, tie handling, selection diagnostics, `C_obs` boundary caution, uncertainty, falsification and clinical evidence limits.

The targeted repair is the confirmation endpoint. Requiring a later same-domain code is scientifically useful, but treating only participants who eventually confirm as a baseline cohort, or coding “not confirmed” when a full observation window is unavailable, changes the estimand and can introduce immortal-time, ascertainment and boundary bias. This child therefore makes the confirmation question a prespecified multi-state estimand:

`S0` = eligible at panel 1 with one existing domain and no post-panel second-domain record;
`S1` = first additional target domain recorded after panel 1;
`S2` = that new domain confirmed by a distinct later same-domain code/date pair 30–365 days after its first record;
`U365` = no confirmation by 365 days after the first record when the entire calendar window is observed;
and `DEATH` = absorbing competing death from `S0` or `S1`.

The primary robust-progression quantity is the cumulative probability of reaching `S2` before death or `U365` by the study horizon, computed from the same panel-1 cohort. The first-record transition `S0→S1` remains a separate recorded-code estimand. The confirmation transition `S1→S2` is estimated only among people who enter `S1`, with its risk time beginning at the first record; no future confirmation is used to construct the panel-1 cohort or exposure. Participants whose full 365-day calendar window is not observable are boundary-censored for confirmation and are not silently treated as unconfirmed.

## Unresolved question, evidence and clinical importance

The strongest available evidence supports the narrower claim that baseline metabolic indices are associated with later coded cardiometabolic disease and that competing-risk or multistate models can describe coded progression. The inspected local export and public evidence do not establish that assessment-linked within-person HbA1c/triglyceride/HDL change adds information beyond panel-1 values for a subsequent additional domain, nor that a signal survives a reproducible persistence check.

The unresolved question is:

> Among UK Biobank participants aged 40–69 years at a valid first assessment-linked panel who have exactly one coded diabetes, cardiovascular disease or chronic kidney disease domain at both panels, return for a valid second panel, remain in the same domain and have complete paired core assays, does dated metabolic change add prognostic information beyond panel-1 levels, domain identity, disease duration and prespecified covariates for (i) the first additional coded domain and (ii) reaching a support-aware confirmed state rather than only one administrative code?

This matters because a clinician deciding whether current metabolic values are sufficient for risk review needs to know whether recent change carries reproducible information and whether that signal is robust to a single coded encounter. The substantive advance is not a larger model: it is a fair separation of three claims that are often conflated:

- first coded transition: when an additional domain first appears in the available record;
- confirmation after first record: whether the same domain is recorded again after a prespecified interval;
- clinical persistence or onset: a stronger claim that these data cannot adjudicate.

A supportive result could justify a prospective study of trend-informed surveillance. An adverse result could show that current panel-1 values are sufficient for the tested internal prognostic purpose, or that apparent change information is coding/selection fragile. Neither result establishes that changing a biomarker causes disease, that repeat testing benefits patients, or that any alert threshold is clinically useful.

The inspected natural-history demonstration supports the value of structured dated histories but is not reproduced; its external cohort and any richer training setup are not claimed here. The Bayesian longitudinal/genetic demonstration is not reproduced because validated local genetic inputs are unavailable. The cancer demonstration’s accessible supplement was inspected only for its available context; the main article and complete STAR Methods remain unavailable and no multimodal result is claimed. These demonstrations motivate careful temporal/state design, not a required method.

## The repaired outcome estimands

### E1: first recorded additional domain

For each target domain absent at d1, find the earliest post-d1 valid paired code/date. If exactly one domain has the earliest date, enter `S1_UNIQUE(domain)`. If multiple new domains share that date, enter `S1_MULTI(member_set)` and retain the member set. Do not assign a multi-domain event to an arbitrary route.

A same-day death takes precedence over a new-domain record; reverse precedence is a labeled sensitivity. The E1 event file contains d1, first-record date, state, route/member set, death-tie flag, censor boundary and split. E1 is the direct time-from-d1 estimand for a first recorded transition.

### E2: support-aware confirmation as a multi-state process

A domain is confirmed only when there are two distinct same-domain code/date slots with distinct valid dates `t_first < t_confirm`, with 30 <= `t_confirm - t_first` <= 365 days. Repeated fields with the same date do not create confirmation. A later code within 30 days does not confirm by itself, but a subsequent qualifying date within 365 days of `t_first` can. The event date is `t_confirm`; retain `t_first` and every candidate pair for audit.

The solver must fit the following process with all panel-1 covariates fixed at d1:

- `S0 -> S1_UNIQUE(domain)` or `S1_MULTI(member_set)` at the first additional-domain date;
- `S0 -> DEATH` for death before a first-domain event;
- from `S1`, `S1 -> S2_CONFIRMED` at the first qualifying same-domain confirmation;
- from `S1`, `S1 -> DEATH` if death occurs before confirmation;
- from `S1`, `S1 -> U365` exactly at `t_first+365` when no confirmation has occurred and the entire calendar window is observed.

For a unique first domain, `S2_CONFIRMED(domain)` means that same domain is confirmed. For a simultaneous first-domain state, use `S2_CONFIRMED_MULTI` if at least one member meets the confirmation rule and retain the confirmed member set; route-specific claims are restricted to unique states.

The state-transition formulation fixes the immortal-time defect: a participant is at risk for confirmation only after entering `S1`, and time from `t_first` to confirmation, death or `U365` is included. No participant is selected at d1 because they later confirmed. The direct robust-progression estimand is the state occupancy/cumulative incidence of `S2` from d1, obtained by composing the estimated transitions; the conditional confirmation estimand is the `S1 -> S2` transition among those who actually enter `S1`. These are different claims and must be reported separately.

“Full calendar window” is not “clinical confirmation opportunity.” The data do not reveal encounters, testing intensity, care outside the record, or reasons for a second code. Define the following explicitly:

- `CONFIRMED`: qualifying pair observed;
- `U365`: no qualifying pair by `t_first+365` and `t_first+365` is within the study horizon and observed source boundary;
- `BOUNDARY_LIMITED`: `t_first+365` exceeds the participant’s usable study horizon, `C_obs`, or the five-year d1 horizon before a full window; do not count this as U365;
- `DEATH_BEFORE_CONFIRMATION`: death before confirmation or U365, including a same-day death under the precedence rule;
- `NO_CLINICAL_OPPORTUNITY_IDENTIFIED`: not an outcome category. It is unavailable evidence, not a substitute for U365.

`C_obs` is the maximum finite date across the paired outcome date fields and death dates used in the analysis. It is not automatically an administrative cutoff. If source-level administrative completeness is not documented, report observed-boundary state occupancy and boundary-limited counts; do not make an unqualified five-year claim. If `t_first+365` is after `C_obs`, censor the confirmation process at the boundary and retain the case in E1. If a death or confirmation occurs before that boundary, use the observed event. The solver must also report whether a full confirmation window is available under an explicitly documented administrative cutoff if one can be established.

A secondary landmark check may begin at `t_first+30` among E1 cases who are alive and still observable, and estimate confirmation by `t_first+365` with the same fixed d1 predictors. This is not the primary d1 prognostic estimand and must not be compared with B0 after silently conditioning away first events. It is a consistency diagnostic for the `S1 -> S2` transition and is labeled post-event/selection-conditional.

## Matched simple baseline and substantive alternative

All models use the same d1 cohort, state definitions, death handling, censoring, participant split, preprocessing, weighting, uncertainty and locked evaluation. Neither model uses confirmation status, a future code, or a post-first-event measurement as a predictor.

### B0: current-level multi-state baseline

B0 fits transition-specific cause-specific hazards for `S0 -> S1_UNIQUE`, `S0 -> S1_MULTI`, `S0 -> DEATH`, `S1 -> S2_CONFIRMED`, `S1 -> U365`, and `S1 -> DEATH` where support permits. Predictors are panel-1 HbA1c, triglycerides, HDL, existing domain, existing-domain duration, age, sex, d1–d0 gap, BMI `21001-0.0`, SBP `4080-0.0`, DBP `4079-0.0`, smoking `20116-0.0`, and center `54-0.0`. Continuous terms use fit-frozen restricted cubic splines; missingness handling is fit-defined and reported. A pooled transition is allowed only as a prespecified fallback when route support fails, with route interpretation marked inconclusive.

B0 answers what panel-1 current state predicts for first recorded and support-aware confirmed transition.

### M_change: coordinated change plus marker discordance

M_change fits the identical transition architecture and adds fixed, fit-derived worsening-oriented change contrasts:

- `z_H`: standardized annualized HbA1c change;
- `z_T`: standardized annualized triglyceride change;
- `z_R`: standardized annualized reverse-HDL change;
- `C=(z_H+z_T+z_R)/sqrt(3)`: coordinated deterioration;
- `D1=(z_H-z_T)/sqrt(2)` and `D2=(z_H+z_T-2z_R)/sqrt(6)`: discordance contrasts.

If empirical covariance requires orthogonalization, estimate it in fit data and freeze it before locked evaluation. Fit `M_common=B0+C` and `M_change=B0+C+D1+D2`; prespecified domain interactions are added only when route support passes the threshold. The joint M_change-versus-B0 contrast tests whether dated change adds information. M_change-versus-M_common tests whether discordance adds beyond coordinated worsening. Both are evaluated for E1 and the multi-state E2 quantity using the same transition risk sets.

C can reveal a coherent burden signal that separate current-level terms or marker-specific changes obscure. D1/D2 can reveal a marker disagreement hidden by a single common score. This is a scientifically substantive alternative, not a claim that C is a validated biological latent factor. Residualizing each change on panel-1 value and gap, using fit-only regressions, is a sensitivity for regression-to-the-mean; it is not the primary estimand.

A recurrent/attention sequence model is deferred because only two verified assay panels are available: it cannot learn meaningful within-person sequence structure or solve date-proxy timing and repeat-selection uncertainty. Revisit with at least three verified dated panels and evidence that a richer representation adds stable information beyond C/D and the multi-state endpoint. This is a scientific deferral, not a prohibition on neural or GPU methods.

Report absolute-risk summaries at prespecified 5%, 10% and 20% review thresholds only as computational operating points: alert rate, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, number needed to review and decision-curve net benefit. They are not approved clinical actions.

Falsification and artifact checks are mandatory:

- compare E1 with the support-aware E2 state process;
- repeat with one-year delayed entry and 0/2/5-year lags, keeping d1 as the primary landmark;
- repeat with gaps 182–5,475 days and alternative stable-domain definitions;
- permute panel-1 change within d1-domain and fit/tuning strata;
- use paired K35/540 appendicitis codes/dates as a negative control;
- repeat under source-concordant, main-only and assay-completeness-weighted sensitivities, treating duplicate fields as provenance checks rather than validation;
- reverse same-day death precedence as a labeled tie sensitivity;
- report code-only/date-only, invalid-date, pre-d1, early-repeat, simultaneous-domain, confirmation-pair, death-tie, `U365`, boundary-limited and censor counts;
- compare the multi-state E2 composition with the labeled post-event landmark check.

Supportive evidence requires a prespecified endpoint with at least 20 total and 5 locked-test relevant events, adequate route/state support, stable five-source/tie construction, adequate positivity/ESS, and a worsening-direction interval excluding the null for C or discordance. The locked test must show a prespecified incremental Brier/calibration improvement or at least 10% relative net-benefit improvement at a threshold chosen before locked evaluation. The direction must not reverse under assay-completeness weighting, source-concordant restriction, boundary handling and one-year lag; appendicitis and within-state permutation must not show a comparable signal.

A supportive robust-progression conclusion requires E2 to have adequate confirmation support and the change signal to persist in the state-occupancy or confirmation-transition analysis. E1-only support is a recorded-code result, not confirmation. A supportive common-burden conclusion requires C to add beyond B0 without needing D; a supportive discordance conclusion requires D1/D2 to add beyond C with adequate transition support.

Adverse means no incremental information from dated change on a supported endpoint, worse locked performance, disappearance after duration/source/selection adjustment, a comparable negative-control or permutation signal, or an E1 signal contradicted by adequately supported E2. This falsifies the selected conditional internal prognostic claim, not all metabolic biology, clinical onset or causal intervention effects.

Inconclusive means inadequate E2 confirmation or transition support, failure to distinguish `U365` from boundary-limited cases, failed positivity/ESS, unstable source/tie construction, unresolved date disagreement, unverified administrative boundary for the claimed horizon, poor convergence/calibration, intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, or irreconcilable selection/timing/phenotype sensitivity. If E2 is sparse, report E1 as a coded result and classify the robust-progression claim inconclusive. Any alert result without expert-approved action thresholds and clinical utility data is inconclusive for policy value.

Computationally checkable claims include source hashes and headers, exact paths/tables/fields, five-way joins, canonical assay selection, paired code/date logic, state transitions, confirmation-window categories, split, fitted models, locked predictions, uncertainty and falsification outputs. Clinical onset, independent clinical encounters, code validity, reasons for return/testing, treatment/adherence, severity, causal effects, monitoring benefit, nonreturner generalization, transportability and policy utility require adjudication, unavailable evidence, expert review or another study.

## Alternatives not chosen and revisit criteria

- B0 is retained because it is the simplest clinically interpretable “current levels alone” comparator.
- M_common/M_change are retained over unequal marker-change versus one-axis models because C/D is dimension-matched and separates coordinated deterioration from discordance under identical state risk sets.
- The multi-state confirmation formulation is selected over a binary “ever confirmed” endpoint because it includes time at risk, death, deterministic U365 and boundary-limited censoring. A direct confirmation landmark is retained only as a labeled consistency check.
- A sequence neural model is deferred for the two-panel scientific limitation above, not because neural models or GPUs are disallowed.
- A formal assay-reliability/latent-variable model is deferred because main and biological_samples are duplicated export fields, not independent replicates; local reliability is unidentified. Revisit with assay replicates, laboratory QC or a validated external error model.
- A causal monitoring/intervention policy is deferred because treatment, adherence, clinical utility, approved thresholds and adjudicated onset are unavailable.
- Genomics, imaging, accelerometry, proteomics beyond the listed assays, raw narratives and external validation are not verified dependencies for this question and are not substituted silently.
