## Hypothesis to test

The effects on infection and progression to hematologic malignancy differ by CHIP gene and clone size.

## Study population and main data

Participants with sequencing data; clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential (CHIP), infection, and hematologic malignancy.

## Suggested approach

Distinguish the pathway to infection without prior hematologic malignancy from the pathway in which hematologic malignancy precedes infection.

## Scientific significance

Extend known CHIP associations by identifying gene-specific differences.

## Main limitations

Rare mutations may yield too few events, and reliable CHIP classification is required.
