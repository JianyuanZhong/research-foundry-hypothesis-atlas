# Pre-registered source/actionability bridge with workload and correlated-error stress tests

## Parentage and targeted repair

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parents' frozen first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject pulmonary-opportunity denominator, reciprocal pre-discharge radiology chronology, `D = hosp/admissions.dischtime`, one canonical discharge artifact, exact target atom `g=(E, terminal curr_service, W)`, inherited `AUTO/RECON/TIMING/DEFER` route and precedence, route-independent unit enumeration, all-unit/worst-unit admission routing, five strata, sentinel census, positive-probability two-phase sample, no-top-up/no-replacement rules, additive distinct-admission ledger (maximum 900 admissions and one reserve), design weights and nested finite-population HT/Hájek inference, and silent-validation-only conclusion ceiling. It does not add an outcome, alter eligibility, move a time boundary, or change the parent estimand.

The targeted repair is to make the proposed documentary actionability bridge independently executable and resistant to two remaining failure modes: (1) a reviewer can unconsciously infer actionability from the parent target/route, and (2) a route may pass provenance checks only because duplicated text, correlated reviewers, or unmeasured review time makes the replay unlike a prospective handoff. The child therefore freezes operational thresholds and the adjudication sequence before any target-dependent work, uses route-concealed independent coding with an explicit correlated-error challenge, and records an admission-level workload/latency feasibility audit inside the existing 900-admission ledger.

## Claim, evidence boundary, and unresolved clinical question

Existing evidence in this snapshot supports an admission-linked corpus of radiology and discharge text, service history, transfers, and discharge time. It can support reproducibility of a stored-text documentation construct. It cannot show that a clinician received/read the text, that a proposed action was correct or safe, that follow-up occurred, or that a patient benefited.

The falsifiable operational claim is: **within the frozen parent sample, can an independently blinded team produce the same route and a uniquely traceable, pre-discharge, actionability-coded source under a fixed time budget, and does that performance remain above pre-specified bridge thresholds on a later chronological block after calibration?** A supportive result only nominates a governed silent validation; it is not a clinical actionability, safety, causal, or transportability claim.

## Frozen pre-label protocol

The following artifacts are sealed before opening any parent target, route, C/S, or holistic label: codebook version, parser, source-selection rule, threshold file, reviewer IDs, time limit, random seed, chronological split, and ledger schema.

### 1. Deterministic source packet

For each inherited sampled unit and every inherited route output, emit a packet containing the exact source-row key (`source_table`, `subject_id`, `hadm_id`, `note_id`), `note_type`, `note_seq`, `charttime`, `storetime`, UTF-8 byte length, exact-text SHA-256, normalized-whitespace SHA-256, and one or more zero-based half-open spans `(span_start, span_end)`. A span is valid only when `0 <= start < end <= len(text)` and exact substring reconstruction succeeds. Never concatenate notes. If source selection is non-unique, set `source_identity=UNRESOLVED`; ties are not silently resolved.

A source is `AVAILABLE_AT_D` only when `storetime` is nonmissing and `storetime <= D`; a report is `CHARTED_BY_D` only when `charttime` is nonmissing and `charttime <= D`. The bridge uses the stricter availability condition but reports both fields. A note with unavailable or ambiguous time is not a valid positive bridge source.

### 2. Route-concealed actionability coding

Before any target or route label is shown, reviewer A sees only the frozen source packet and codebook and assigns exactly one of `ACTIONABLE`, `INFORMATIONAL_ONLY`, `CONTRADICTORY`, `MISSING_ACTION`, or `UNRESOLVED` plus a structured reason bitset. The codebook is literal:

* `ACTIONABLE`: the same cited span contains a pulmonary finding/status and an explicit next step, monitoring instruction, or named responsibility/time anchor. A generic statement such as “follow up” without a responsible party or time anchor is not actionable.
* `INFORMATIONAL_ONLY`: a pulmonary finding/status is present but no explicit next step, monitoring instruction, or responsibility/time anchor is present.
* `CONTRADICTORY`: the cited span contains incompatible current-status and next-step statements, or mutually incompatible instructions that cannot be resolved from that span alone.
* `MISSING_ACTION`: the parent atom's fixed output requires an action field, but the cited source has the relevant finding and the action field is absent.
* `UNRESOLVED`: no unique source/span, illegible or empty text, missing/ambiguous timing, or codebook ambiguity.

`MISSING_ACTION` is retained separately from `INFORMATIONAL_ONLY`; if the parent atom does not require an action field for a unit, the reviewer must use `INFORMATIONAL_ONLY`, not invent a missing field. Medication administration, post-discharge events, target labels, model scores, and clinical outcomes are prohibited inputs. A cited span may be marked `ACTIONABLE` only if the literal criteria are met; clinical correctness is not adjudicated.

The primary bridge gate is strict: non-`DEFER` requires a unique source, valid span hash, `AVAILABLE_AT_D=TRUE`, and `ACTIONABLE`. Any other status, source conflict, or timing uncertainty is an adverse bridge record and routes to `DEFER`; it is never recoded as a negative target.

### 3. Independent review and correlated-error challenge

Reviewer A and reviewer B independently code a fixed, route-concealed 20% systematic challenge sample plus all `CONTRADICTORY` and `UNRESOLVED` records. B sees no A result. Their review order is randomized within chronological block. Disagreement on the five-state category, source identity, timing, or span is `UNRESOLVED` for the primary analysis; report raw agreement, category-specific agreement, disagreement, and missingness with finite-population intervals. Do not majority-wash disagreements.

To detect shared error, a separate expert adjudicator (not exposed to route, target, parent score, or either reviewer label) reviews a fixed 10% of agreements, stratified by category and source table, plus every disagreement observed in the 20% challenge. This adjudicator may mark `UNRESOLVED` but may not change the pre-sealed codebook. The primary estimate remains the pre-specified blinded-review result; the adjudicator is a diagnostic correlated-error audit, not a rescue label. If the dataset cannot supply suitably qualified adjudicators, the expert-dependent endpoint is explicitly not established and the result is inconclusive rather than treated as validated.

### 4. Fixed workload and temporal replay

Record wall-clock minutes from packet release to each reviewer submission, number of source rows opened, number of span edits, and whether a reviewer exceeded the fixed **120-second per admission** budget. The budget is a feasibility stress test, not a clinical outcome. It is frozen before labels. Reviewers may inspect only the packet's pre-`D` source rows and inherited route-independent unit list; no searching outside the packet and no post-`D` information is permitted. The same budget applies to positive, negative, control, and sentinel packets.

The workload ledger charges this review within the parent's existing one-unit-per-admission charge, fixed controls, and one reserve; no extra admission may be sampled. If a sampled admission contains multiple units, all units are reviewed under the parent's unit enumeration and the admission-level worst-unit/adverse precedence is unchanged. A timeout is recorded as `UNRESOLVED` and `DEFER`, never as a negative.

## Chronological bridge design and estimand

After eligibility and the parent sample are frozen, sort distinct admissions by `D`, then `(subject_id, hadm_id)`. Inherit any parent-registered temporal boundary exactly. If no such boundary exists, use the deterministic earliest 60% development, next 20% calibration, latest 20% bridge-evaluation admissions; this fallback is fixed before review. The development block fits only the already inherited route rule. Calibration may estimate a probability map only if the parent already emits a probability; otherwise report counts and do not invent probabilities. Freeze all thresholds and maps before opening evaluation records. The bridge block is a one-pass silent replay with no feedback or label-driven route changes.

The primary estimand remains the parent's finite-population, design-weighted proportion among bridge-evaluable non-`DEFER` outputs satisfying the identity/actionability gate, with the parent HT/Hájek and nested sampling/adjudication variance. Also report the all-opportunity admission-level proportion with adverse precedence, so excluding `DEFER` cannot hide operational failure. Secondary endpoints are weighted `DEFER` rate, source identity failure, timing failure, actionability-category distribution, reviewer disagreement, adjudicator disagreement on the correlated-error audit, timeout rate, median and 90th-percentile review time, and calibration-block versus bridge-block degradation where a parent probability exists. All are reported overall, by inherited route, target/control status, source table, and temporal block without conditioning away failures.

## Baselines and falsification

1. **Parent route-only baseline:** the unchanged inherited route reproducibility endpoint.
2. **Source-blind baseline:** identical parent sample, text, chronology, model, and labels, but no source identity/span/actionability fields. This measures whether apparent reproducibility depends on untracked provenance.
3. **Permuted-atom negative control:** preserve admission, source, and timing packets but permute the inherited target atom across eligible units using a fixed seed. The bridge must not acquire target-linked positive source status merely from pulmonary words; this is a specificity control, not a replacement estimand.

Use the parent's pre-registered tolerance and multiplicity/interval convention; if the parents did not specify a numerical bridge threshold, the following are frozen before any bridge labels: lower 95% bound for the primary identity/actionability gate at least **0.80**; `UNRESOLVED` plus `CONTRADICTORY` at most **0.10** of all routed units; reviewer-category raw agreement at least **0.90** on the dual-reviewed challenge; expert-adjudicator disagreement with the initial consensus no greater than **0.15**; and at least **0.90** of admissions completed within 120 seconds. These are readiness thresholds, not clinical claims. The thresholds apply to the bridge-evaluation block and are not tuned post hoc. If a parent threshold exists, it supersedes these fallback values and is recorded verbatim.

The bridge claim is falsified if the primary lower bound is below its frozen threshold, any threshold fails, the source-blind and source-aware arms show materially different route/error distributions under the parent margin, a post-`D` dependency is detected, the permuted-atom control is positive beyond its frozen specificity margin, or performance degrades beyond the parent calibration-to-evaluation margin. It is inconclusive for sparse strata, wide intervals, unavailable expert adjudication, failed packet integrity, or an inability to establish the temporal split. A timeout or unresolved record is adverse, not silently dropped.

## Interpretation

Supportive results mean only that the inherited route can be replayed on a later historical block with auditable pre-discharge source identity, literal non-clinical actionability status, bounded reviewer workload, and measured (not assumed) correlated-error/temporal stability. Adverse results mean retain `DEFER` and do not nominate a live silent pilot; report failing routes, source classes, timing classes, and workload strata. Inconclusive results require more data or expert review and are not support.

The snapshot cannot establish actual EHR availability to a receiver, reading/receipt, responsibility assignment in practice, clinical correctness, follow-up, safety, benefit, transportability, or causal effect. Those require source-system audit logs, prospective observation, qualified expert adjudication, and a governed study with clinical outcomes. Even an entirely supportive result cannot justify changing care, estimating benefit, or claiming a valid clinical actionability measure.
