# Is the low-baseline-HBsAg "functional cure" signal of checkpoint blockade in HBV-related HCC specific to checkpoint blockade, or a generic consequence of starting tumour-directed therapy?

**HCC island, episode 2. Operation: restart** (`choose_work` seq 2; the two assessed board candidates
are transition context, not scientific ancestry).

**One-sentence hypothesis.** The enrichment of HBsAg decline at low baseline HBsAg in
checkpoint-inhibitor-treated HBV-HCC — reported as a 6.3-fold hazard and a 38.4% 24-month cumulative
incidence versus **zero** events in 85 non-ICI HBV-HCC controls [K1] — is **real and genuinely
post-index, but not specific to checkpoint blockade**: initiating *any* tumour-directed therapy in
HBV-HCC is followed by the same low-baseline-concentrated ≥1 log10 HBsAg decline, so the functional-cure
reading attributes to PD-1 blockade an effect that trans-arterial chemoembolisation and oral TKIs
produce at an indistinguishable rate.

**Revision notice (important for reading this file).** This hypothesis is *narrower and differently
located* than the one this episode started with. The original version claimed the signal was the
continuation of a **pre-existing decline trajectory**, measured through a self-controlled
slope-break estimand. Two pre-committed artefact audits executed before publication
(§1.2h, §1.2i) killed the estimand and partly killed that mechanism: the slope-break gradient is
reproduced **in full with no index event at all**, so it cannot carry a biological claim, whereas
the threshold endpoint is measured to be post-index-specific — patients in the low stratum had
*not* already fallen ≥1 log10 in the preceding two years. Both corrections run against the earlier
draft's interest and are reported as results, not smoothed away.

**Status discipline used throughout.** Claims are labelled MEASURED (computed this episode, frozen
output attached), PUBLISHED (from an inspected key reference), or PLANNED (for the solver). No
planned quantity is written as a result.

## 1. The opening

### 1.1 The specific scientific opening

PD-1 blockade is being actively discussed as a route to HBV functional cure, and the strongest
clinical evidence in HBV-HCC is [K1] (Mon et al., *J Hepatol* 2025): 118 + 44 HBsAg-positive
ICI-treated patients versus 85 HBV-HCC non-ICI controls, 6.8% and 9.1% HBsAg seroclearance over a
median 17.5 months versus **no case** in controls within 24 months, with the effect concentrated at
baseline HBsAg < 100 IU/mL (HR 6.274; 24-month cumulative incidence 38.4% in that stratum)
(PUBLISHED, abstract inspected). The premise study in chronic hepatitis B without cancer [K2] is
n = 24 at sub-oncologic dosing, with a mean 12-week HBsAg change of −0.30 log10 IU/mL and its single
seroclearance accompanied by a grade 3 ALT flare (PUBLISHED, abstract inspected).

The opening is not "nobody has looked". It is that **the published stratified effect has rival
explanations that the published design cannot exclude, and those rivals are directly measurable.**
In [K1] the comparator is a single 85-patient non-ICI HBV-HCC cohort with **zero** HBsAg-loss events,
so a comparator gradient of the same shape would be invisible by construction; the comparator's
own therapy (which in an HBV-HCC cohort is essentially never "none") is not reported in the abstract;
and baseline HBsAg level in a long-treated chronic-HBV population is not a randomly assigned
covariate — it marks both trajectory position and antigen dynamic range, with most patients on
nucleos(t)ide analogues (16/17 responders in [K1] were on NA, 94.1%, inspected in the frozen
abstract). Two rivals follow, and this episode separated them by measurement rather than argument:
**R-generic** (any tumour-directed therapy produces the same decline) and **R-artefact** (the
stratified estimator produces the pattern without any treatment effect).

### 1.2 What this episode MEASURED, and why it reverses the interpretation

All numbers below are frozen in the attached outputs. Units are **uncalibrated HBsAg index log10**,
never IU/mL (§2, unit discipline). Arms: ICI = first dated anti-PD-(L)1; LOCO = first TACE/HAIC in
patients never exposed to ICI or bevacizumab; TKI = first oral TKI, same exclusion. Index ≥ 2020-01-01.

**(a) The published stratified pattern reproduces here — in magnitude.** ≥1 log10 HBsAg decline
within 730 d, by quintile-boundary strata of baseline index (cuts 1048 / 1784 / 2365):
ICI lowest stratum **13.78%** (95% CI 10.56–17.56, n = 399) versus 1.67%, 1.90%, 4.63% in the three
higher strata. The within-arm low-minus-high gradient is **+9.15 pp (95% CI +5.28 to +13.02)**.
Taken alone this looks like a clean replication of [K1].

**(b) The GRADIENT is not ICI-specific; the LOW-STRATUM LEVEL may be.** The same gradient appears in
both never-ICI active comparators: LOCO lowest stratum 9.51% (7.12–12.38) with gradient +6.02 pp
(+3.12 to +9.07); TKI 8.33% (4.74–13.37) with gradient +7.24 pp (+3.34 to +11.68). Sub-cutoff HBsAg
shows the same shape (ICI 5.76%, LOCO 4.66%, TKI 5.56% in the lowest stratum). **A correction to the
earlier draft, against this proposal's interest:** concordant *gradients* do not mean concordant
*rates*. In the low stratum the ICI rate is 13.78% against 9.51% (LOCO), a crude **+4.3 pp**
difference whose confidence intervals overlap but which is the same order as the effect the
functional-cure hypothesis predicts. The earlier draft's flat assertion "it is not ICI-specific" was
therefore too strong for the endpoint data and is replaced by H1 as a genuine two-sided test (§1.4).

**(c) A pre-index slope gradient exists, but it CANNOT be interpreted — see (h).** Signed
within-patient pre-index HBsAg slope (log10 per 100 d, ≥2 values in days −1095 to −1, span ≥30 d)
is monotone in baseline stratum in **all three arms**, steeply negative at low baseline and
*positive* at high baseline: ICI −0.0457 → −0.0136 → −0.0056 → **+0.0087**; LOCO −0.1207 → −0.0258
→ −0.0013 → **+0.0239**; TKI −0.0513 → −0.0173 → −0.0044 → **+0.0048**. The earlier draft read this
as proof that baseline level is a proxy for pre-existing trajectory direction. That reading is
**withdrawn**: the stratifier is the right-hand endpoint of the very fit whose slope is being
reported, and (h) shows the same monotone pattern arises around an arbitrary sham date. The numbers
stand; the interpretation does not.

**(d) The self-controlled slope break is measured to be an ESTIMATOR ARTEFACT, not a finding.**
Paired post-minus-pre slope, median with 4000× bootstrap CI: ICI lowest stratum +0.0197 (+0.0036,
+0.0473), highest stratum −0.0416 (−0.0577, −0.0249), i.e. a low-minus-high break gradient of
+0.0613; LOCO low +0.0366 (+0.0172, +0.0832), TKI low +0.0201 (−0.0043, +0.0753). The earlier draft
treated this reversal as its headline mechanism ("the decline attenuates after start"). **(h) shows
it is reproduced in full with no index event**, so it is hereby demoted from a finding to a
quantified bias in the estimator, and the primary estimand has been moved off it (§3.2).

**(e) The overall ICI-versus-comparator contrast is null at every horizon.** ≥1 log decline:
365 d ICI 4.1% vs LOCO 4.3% vs TKI 2.5%; 730 d ICI 4.6% (3.76–5.62, n = 2055) vs LOCO 4.7%
(3.91–5.60) vs TKI 3.1%. Sub-cutoff HBsAg: 730 d ICI 1.9% vs LOCO 2.8%. Extending the window from
365 to 730 d moves the ICI rate by 0.5 pp, so the earlier short-horizon null is **not** merely a
follow-up artefact. The landmark DiD was pre-committed-AMBIGUOUS: D_ICI −0.047 (−0.085, −0.011),
contrast vs LOCO −0.036 (−0.111, +0.042), vs TKI −0.045 (−0.100, +0.012); the larger unpaired
contrast vs LOCO is −0.003 (−0.035, +0.030).

**(f) Two necessary guards, both MEASURED.** HBsAg platform break ≈ 2019-04 (within-patient
cross-era change −0.435 log10 vs −0.07/−0.08 within era) forces index ≥ 2020-01-01. Tumour-derived
serum HBsAg is negligible: instrumented within-patient elasticity of HBsAg on AFP −0.031
(−0.047, −0.015), pooled R² 0.004, so HBsAg is a hepatocyte-compartment readout and no
tumour-burden decomposition is needed (this is the one result I did not independently re-derive;
see PROVENANCE.md and deliverable 3).

**(g) Honest negative feasibility result.** A treatment-free negative control arm **does not exist
here**: 34,848 of the HCC patients have a recorded antitumour modality, and exactly **1** untreated
patient met the HBsAg baseline+follow-up requirement. There is therefore no untreated reference
group, and the generic-versus-specific question must be settled by cross-arm comparison (b) plus
*within-record* negative controls (h, i), not by an untreated arm. This is a real limitation of
design, stated rather than papered over — and it is also why (h) and (i) had to be run.

**(h) ARTEFACT AUDIT 1 — the slope-break gradient is reproduced with NO event.**
(`work/lead/sham_break.py` → `sham_break_results.txt`; interpretation rules R1–R5 written into the
script docstring before the output existed.) The break estimator shares one observation — the
"junction" value in days −60…−1 — between the stratifier and the right-hand end of the pre-slope
fit, so noise in that value must bias the break upward at low baseline and downward at high. Two
sham designs with no index event:
*A2, record-anchored* (sham index = each patient's middle HBsAg observation, every era-eligible
HBsAg-positive patient, n = 809 paired, no reference to treatment at all): sham break
q1_low **+0.0185 (−0.0175, +0.0504)**, q2 +0.0255 (+0.0092, +0.0442), q3 −0.0121 (−0.0262, −0.0001),
q4_high **−0.0552 (−0.0715, −0.0343)**, sham low-minus-high gradient **+0.0737 (+0.0350, +0.1093)**
— i.e. **120% of the real junction-anchored ICI gradient of +0.0613**, generated by the estimator
alone. The sham pre-slope gradient also reproduces (c): −0.0614 → +0.0180 across strata.
*A1, arm-anchored* (sham index = real index − 365 d, sham window entirely before any arm therapy,
n = 106): pooled q1_low +0.0271 (−0.0304, +0.1063), q4_high −0.0205 (−0.0450, +0.0094) — same sign
pattern, underpowered.
*Verdict against the pre-committed rules, stated exactly.* R1 as written required the **low-stratum**
sham break to be ≥ +0.010 with a CI excluding 0; the point estimate is +0.0185 but its CI straddles
0, so **R1 is not literally met**. The quantity §3.2 declares to be the scientific object — the
low-minus-high interaction — *is* reproduced, at +0.0737 with a CI excluding 0. I therefore record
the honest verdict: **the stratified slope-break interaction cannot be interpreted as biology in
this design**, while the low-stratum break alone is inconclusive rather than proven artefactual.
*Test B (junction-free respecification)* was executed and is **not decisive in this snapshot**:
requiring ≥3 pre-index values leaves ICI 170, TKI 52, **LOCO 6** patients. ICI q1_low break becomes
+0.0132 (−0.0282, +0.0348) and the gradient +0.0345 (−0.0402, +0.0645); but the same-patient
junction-anchored reference fit gives +0.0353 (−0.0206, +0.0917), so the apparent shrinkage from
+0.0613 is a **sample** change, not a specification change. The junction-free check is therefore
feasible in principle and underpowered in practice — which is precisely why the measurement-aware
hierarchical model (§3.5) is now the selected method rather than an aspiration.

**(i) ARTEFACT AUDIT 2 — the threshold endpoint SURVIVES the audit and is post-index specific.**
(`work/lead/endpoint_artefact.py` → `endpoint_artefact_results.txt`; rules E-R1–E-R4 pre-committed
in the docstring.) Since (h) disqualified the slope evidence, the hypothesis now rests on the
threshold endpoints, which are stratified on the same baseline value and so needed their own audit.
*Test D, backward/forward symmetry at the real index*: the ≥1 log10 **forward** decline gradient is
ICI **+9.15 pp (+5.24, +13.00)**, LOCO **+6.02 (+3.10, +9.06)**, TKI **+7.24 (+3.34, +11.68)**,
while the **backward** gradient — a value in days −730…−61 at least 1 log10 *above* the same
baseline, i.e. the patient had already fallen that far before the index — is **+1.41 (+0.00, +3.52)**,
**+1.22 (+0.00, +3.66)** and **+1.41 (+0.00, +4.23)**, with backward rates of **0.00%** in three of
four strata in every arm. Restricted to the same patients who have both windows (ICI 647, LOCO 308,
TKI 300) the forward gradient persists (+8.90, +4.56, +8.30 pp) and the backward gradient does not.
This satisfies **E-R3**: the gradient is specifically **post-index** in all three arms.
*Test C, sham-index endpoint gradient*: the monotone low-stratum enrichment is **not** reproduced at
a sham index — the sham pattern is U-shaped (21.17% / 7.77% / 9.63% / 18.77%) with a low-minus-high
gradient of only **+2.40 pp (−3.75, +8.56)** and sub-cutoff +0.87 pp (−4.98, +6.71), so **E-R1 is
not met**. (The sham set requires ≥4 HBsAg values and is a heavily sampled subgroup; the high sham
rates at *both* extremes are reported as an unexplained observation, not used as support.)
**Consequence, and it changes the hypothesis:** low-baseline patients did *not* already lose 1 log10
before the index, so "continuation of a pre-existing decline" is **rejected as the explanation of the
threshold endpoint**. Something that follows the initiation of tumour-directed therapy produces it —
equally after TACE/HAIC and after a TKI alone. The question is no longer *whether* the signal is
post-index but *what* produces it, and whether PD-1 blockade contributes anything on top.

**(j) The clash with [K1] that this makes explicit.** [K1] reports **no** HBsAg loss in 85 non-ICI
HBV-HCC controls within 24 months. Here the never-ICI comparator arms reach **9.51%** (LOCO) and
**8.33%** (TKI) for ≥1 log10 decline and 4.66% / 5.56% for sub-cutoff HBsAg in the low-baseline
stratum by 730 d. An 85-patient control arm would expect ~2–4 such events; observing zero is
*marginally* compatible by chance and fully compatible if that cohort's baseline distribution,
HBsAg sampling frequency or therapy mix differed. Which of those it is **cannot be determined from
the abstract**, which is all that was accessible (§6), so this is recorded as a quantified
discrepancy requiring the published methods or the original data — not as a refutation.

### 1.3 The strongest claim the available evidence supports — explicitly NOT the contribution

That checkpoint-inhibitor-treated HBsAg-positive patients sometimes show large HBsAg decline and
occasional seroclearance, that such events are concentrated among patients with low baseline
HBsAg [K1], and — added by this episode's audits — that the concentration is genuinely post-index
rather than a continuation of an already-completed decline (§1.2i). This proposal does not claim to
discover any of that. It does not claim [K1] is wrong about its observations.

### 1.4 The unresolved claim the solver will test

> **H1 (primary, revised by §1.2i).** The post-index, low-baseline-concentrated ≥1 log10 HBsAg
> decline is **not specific to checkpoint blockade**: the cause-specific cumulative incidence of
> (≥1 log10 HBsAg decline OR sub-cutoff HBsAg) at 18 months is the same, within a pre-specified
> margin, in the ICI arm and in never-ICI TACE/HAIC and TKI arms, and the **arm × baseline-stratum
> interaction** is null. H1 is falsified by an ICI-specific excess concentrated at low baseline that
> survives overlap weighting, the flare decomposition and the temporal holdout. MEASURED starting
> point, unadjusted and unweighted: low-stratum 730-d rates 13.78% (ICI) vs 9.51% (LOCO) vs 8.33%
> (TKI); overall 4.6% vs 4.7% vs 3.1%. The ICI excess at low baseline is +4.3 pp against LOCO
> before any adjustment, which is *not* nothing — H1 is a real test, not a foregone null.

> **H1b (mechanism split).** Any residual ICI-attributable decline is **cytolytic**, i.e. it is
> concentrated in patients with an on-treatment ALT flare, rather than reflecting non-cytolytic
> immune control. MEASURED context: flare lowers HBsAg in every arm and most strongly in the
> non-ICI LOCO arm (−0.145 vs −0.029), and flare prevalence is flat across baseline strata
> (ICI 32–37%, LOCO 42–48%), so flare does not itself generate the gradient.

> **H1c (responder fraction).** The arm-level mean is the wrong summary if response is confined to a
> subgroup. The solver must estimate a mixture responder fraction π per arm with uncertainty, and
> test whether π_ICI > π_comparator even where the means coincide. A credible interval on π that
> includes the comparator value is an informative bounded null, not a failure.

> **H2 (scale validity, gating).** Whether the ordinal index-scale strata can carry a dose–response
> claim at all. MEASURED warning: pre-index within-patient dynamic range is ~3× larger in the lowest
> stratum (median swing 0.257 log10, p90 0.779) than in the higher strata (0.062–0.088), while ≥1 log
> *rises* are rare everywhere (ICI 2.0% low stratum, 0.0% elsewhere). The asymmetry argues against
> pure symmetric noise, but the level-dependent dispersion must be quantified before any stratified
> effect size is interpreted as biology.

> **H3 (the new question §1.2i opens — what produces the generic decline?).** If the post-index
> low-baseline decline occurs equally after embolisation and after a TKI, the leading explanation is
> **loss of HBsAg-producing hepatocyte mass or function** caused by the index therapy (ischaemic
> necrosis, drug injury, immune flare), not restored antiviral immunity. Prediction: the decline
> scales with on-treatment ALT peak and with a rise in bilirubin / fall in albumin, is largest in
> the embolisation arm, and is attenuated in flare-free patients. **Strongest rival H3′:**
> antiviral pressure from nucleos(t)ide analogues started around the index. MEASURED evidence
> *against* H3′ already: the post-index fall is largest in patients with **no** recorded NA
> (−0.1383, frac large 0.176), intermediate in peri-index starters (−0.0862) and smallest in
> established NA users (−0.0507) — the opposite of what NA-driven suppression predicts, though the
> NA-none group is severity- and documentation-selected and this ordering is confounded by that.
> A third rival, **H3″ measurement**, is H2. H3 is secondary to H1 in this study and is powered as
> a mechanism-generating analysis, not a confirmatory one; it is stated so that a null H1 is not the
> end of the inquiry.

### 1.5 Why the answer matters either way

If H1 holds, a specific and currently attractive clinical inference — that low-HBsAg HBV-HCC
patients should be considered for ICI partly as an HBV functional-cure strategy, or that on-ICI HBsAg
decline is evidence of restored immune control — loses its observational support, because the same
decline follows embolisation and a TKI, neither of which restores HBV-specific T-cell immunity.
That matters beyond HCC: the inferential error it would identify (reading post-index HBsAg decline
in a treated liver-cancer cohort as evidence of immune control) is the inference the PD-1
functional-cure programme is being designed around, and the corrective comparator — *another active
liver-directed therapy*, not an untreated control — is cheap and currently unused. If H1 is
falsified, the effect survives its most serious confound in a cohort ~20× larger in ICI exposure
than [K1] with two active comparators and with the estimator artefact quantified and removed, which
is a materially stronger basis for a prospective functional-cure trial than any existing series.
Separately and whatever H1 shows, §1.2h names and quantifies a bias that applies to **any**
observational study that stratifies on a baseline antigen value and reads subsequent change from
it — including NA-cessation and therapeutic-vaccine analyses — and gives the two cheap negative
controls (sham index; backward window) that detect it. Both outcomes change what the next study is.

not be smuggled in. The cirrhosis text detector is an unvalidated local lexical tool per
`metadata.json/text_extraction` and is used only as a labelled sensitivity covariate.

## 3. PLANNED design

### 3.1 Population, arms, temporal boundaries

Index = first dated administration/procedure of the arm-defining therapy, on or after **2020-01-01**
(assay-era guard, §1.2f) through snapshot end. Arms as in §1.2. Co-administered TKI/anti-VEGF is
permitted in Arm I and recorded (it is standard of care); an ICI-monotherapy stratum is pre-specified.

Inclusion: HBsAg-positive baseline (last value days −60…−1, index ≥10, clear of the cutoff region);
≥1 post-index HBsAg within 730 d. Exclusion: liver transplant before or within the window (HBIG and
graft change the generative model); interferon exposure; baseline HBsAg-negative.

**Primary horizon 18 months** (feasible: 446 ICI patients observed beyond 420 d), with 24 months as a
pre-specified secondary (294 ICI beyond 540 d) and censoring handled in the time-to-event model rather
than by requiring long follow-up for inclusion. This horizon choice is forced by [K1]'s median
16.5 months to HBsAg loss or decline: a 6-month analysis cannot speak to that endpoint.

**Baseline strata** are the pre-registered quintile-boundary cuts MEASURED in the treated arms
(1048 / 1784 / 2365 index units), frozen here so the solver cannot re-tune them, plus a finer
decile analysis in the lowest stratum where the action is.

**The arms are not exchangeable and the proposal does not pretend they are.** Arm I patients have more
advanced disease. Defences, all pre-specified: (i) each patient is their own control, so time-invariant
between-patient confounding cancels; (ii) **pre-index trajectory is a conditioning variable, not a
gate to be passed**, and — revised by §1.2h — it must be entered through the hierarchical model's
per-patient latent slope with a level-dependent error term, **never** as a two-point pre-slope sharing
the junction observation with the stratifier, which is now measured to generate the entire stratified
break pattern on its own; (iii) overlap
weighting on baseline ALBI components, platelets, INR, AFP, PIVKA-II, baseline HBsAg stratum, NA state,
age, sex and calendar half-year, with weight diagnostics reported; (iv) negative-control outcome
(creatinine) must show no arm effect; (v) NA state recorded — MEASURED as nearly constant across strata
(ICI 76.2/75.8/76.4/77.2%; LOCO 66.0/64.6/66.2/69.1%), so NA cannot generate the gradient, though NA
*duration* is not recoverable and remains a limitation.

**Temporal split:** derivation = index before 2023-01-01; held-out temporal validation = 2023-01-01
onward. Regimens, NA prescribing and imaging practice all drift; a random split would hide that. No
post-index information enters any covariate.

### 3.2 Estimands

**The primary estimand was changed by §1.2h/§1.2i and the reason is recorded here.** The earlier
draft's primary was the slope-break contrast τ_s. The sham audit showed that estimator reproduces its
own stratified pattern with no index event, so it cannot be primary. The threshold endpoint passed the
same class of audit (backward-null, sham-non-reproducing) and is additionally [K1]'s own endpoint, so
it becomes primary. This is a demotion of the proposal's more distinctive estimand in favour of its
more auditable one.

1. **Primary (H1):** cause-specific cumulative incidence of (≥1 log10 HBsAg decline from baseline OR
   sub-cutoff HBsAg) by **18 months**, by arm × baseline stratum, with death/last-contact censoring
   and overlap weights; reported as arm contrasts *within* stratum and as the **arm × stratum
   interaction**. MEASURED crude anchors to beat: low-stratum 730-d 13.78% / 9.51% / 8.33%
   (ICI/LOCO/TKI), overall 4.6% / 4.7% / 3.1%. This is a direct, like-for-like comparison with [K1]'s
   endpoint, up to the ordinal-versus-IU/mL limit (§4).
2. **Secondary (continuous, junction-free and mandatory as specified):** τ_s = [slope change]_ICI,s −
   [slope change]_comparator,s from the hierarchical model's per-patient latent slopes, where the
   stratifier must **not** be an observation used in the pre-index slope. The two-point junction-anchored
   version may be reported only alongside its sham-index calibration from §1.2h. The scientific object
   remains the interaction τ_low − τ_high.
3. **H1c:** per-arm mixture responder fraction π with a credible interval, and π_ICI − π_comparator.
4. **H1b / H3:** the endpoint hazard, the slope change and π re-estimated with on-treatment ALT flare
   (≥3× 40 U/L), peak ALT, and change in bilirubin and albumin as effect modifiers; the flare-free
   subgroup carries any non-cytolytic claim, and the association of HBsAg fall with hepatocyte-injury
   markers *within* each arm is the H3 test. Pre-specified H3 reading: if the fall tracks ALT/bilirubin
   equally in LOCO and ICI, the generic-hepatocyte-loss explanation is supported over immune control.
5. **H2:** level-dependent measurement model — within-patient short-interval dispersion as a function
   of HBsAg level, fitted on pre-index data only, used to (a) report the ICC and (b) deflate the
   stratified effect sizes. If dispersion alone reproduces the observed gradient magnitude, the
   stratified claim is declared measurement-driven.
6. **Sensitivity (inert by MEASUREMENT, retained for completeness):** the AFP/PIVKA-II-conditioned
   intercept estimand from the earlier design; with elasticity −0.031 and R² 0.004 it is expected to
   be uninformative, and a solver finding otherwise would overturn §1.2f.

### 3.3 Narrative-radiology outcome: a measurement problem with its own validation requirement

Radiologic response is available only as free text (`检查所见`, `检查诊断`). Any use requires a
**mandatory labelled validation step**: ≥300 reports hand-labelled by the solver against a written
rubric, with reported sensitivity/specificity/kappa against the rule or classifier used; if agreement
is below a pre-specified floor the radiology covariate is dropped rather than used unvalidated.
Tumour response is a **covariate** here, not an endpoint, because HBsAg is not tumour-driven (§1.2f).

### 3.4 Falsification criteria, fixed in advance

Thresholds are on the **primary** estimand (18-month cumulative incidence, low-baseline stratum)
unless stated. They are stated in percentage points so the solver cannot re-scale them.

- **H1 supported (generic-therapy reading):** the weighted ICI-minus-comparator difference in
  18-month cumulative incidence in the low stratum has a 95% interval that **excludes a +5 pp ICI
  advantage** against *both* comparators, and the arm × stratum interaction interval covers 0;
  π_ICI ≈ π_comparator. ⇒ The low-baseline HBsAg decline follows the initiation of tumour-directed
  therapy generally and is not attributable to checkpoint blockade in this cohort. Report as a
  bounded negative with the comparator rates (which [K1]'s zero-event control arm did not provide)
  as the substantive contribution.
- **H1 falsified (ICI-specific effect survives):** the low-stratum ICI-minus-comparator difference is
  **≥ +5 pp with a 95% interval excluding 0 against both comparators**, *and* the arm × stratum
  interaction interval excludes 0, *and* it persists in the flare-free subgroup *and* in the 2023+
  temporal holdout. ⇒ A genuinely ICI-specific, non-cytolytic, low-baseline-concentrated effect in a
  cohort ~20× larger than [K1] with two active comparators; this would materially strengthen the
  case for a prospective functional-cure trial. The crude unweighted starting value is +4.3 pp
  against LOCO, so this outcome is live.
- **Cytolytic / generic-hepatocyte-loss reading (H3):** effect present but concentrated in flare
  patients, or absent in the flare-free subgroup, or tracking ALT/bilirubin equally across arms.
  ⇒ Hepatocyte loss, not restored non-cytolytic immune control; this is the reading [K2]'s single
  flare-accompanied seroclearance and [K3]'s 38.3% ALT>100 U/L rate make the default expectation,
  and it is the explanation the LOCO arm's −0.145 flare-associated fall already favours.
- **Measurement-driven reading (H2):** the level-dependent dispersion model reproduces the stratified
  gradient magnitude, or the sham-index calibration required in §3.6(1) reproduces the primary
  endpoint gradient (it did **not** in discovery: +2.40 pp, CI −3.75 to +8.56). ⇒ Report the
  stratified literature as scale-dependent; IU/mL recalibration becomes the required next evidence.
- **Secondary-estimand-only outcomes must not be promoted.** Because §1.2h showed the slope-break
  estimator is self-generating, any finding that appears **only** in τ_s and not in the primary
  endpoint must be reported as hypothesis-generating with its sham calibration attached.
- **Inconclusive:** the low-stratum arm difference has a 95% interval wider than ±5 pp (the relevant
  denominators are 399 / 515 / 180 patients in the low stratum, so this is a realistic outcome), or
  derivation and 2023+ holdout estimates disagree in sign. ⇒ Report as underpowered; do **not**
  convert a wide interval into a negative claim. An imprecise null does not refute [K1].

### 3.5 Methods: a simple baseline and a substantive alternative on the same question

**Baseline (B1, primary-estimand baseline): overlap-weighted stratified cause-specific
cumulative-incidence regression (Fine–Gray or Aalen–Johansen with weights) on the threshold
endpoint.** Inputs: baseline HBsAg stratum, arm, overlap weights from §3.1(iii), censoring at last
contact. Target: the 18-month arm × stratum contrasts of §3.2(1). This baseline is **simple,
computable on the full analysis set** (ICI 2,055 / LOCO 2,533 / TKI 878 with a forward value) and is
the estimand that passed the artefact audit, so it is not a straw man — it can answer H1 on its own.
What it cannot do: use the information in the *shape* of each patient's antigen trajectory, separate
a small effect in many patients from a large effect in few (H1c), or separate level-dependent
measurement noise from level-dependent biology (H2) — and §1.2h proved that the naive way of using
trajectory shape is actively misleading.

**Baseline (B2, the discredited one, retained as a calibration target only): stratified
self-controlled two-point DiD with bootstrap CIs.** Inputs: baseline HBsAg, one pre-index slope, one
post-index slope, arm, stratum. Target: τ_s. Already executed in reduced form this episode. **Its
measured defects are now decisive and of two kinds:** (i) it requires ≥2 pre and ≥2 post values,
retaining only 504/245/189 patients — 16% of the ICI analysis set and 4% of LOCO — while discarding
the 3,107/5,575/1,710 with a baseline and some follow-up; (ii) more seriously, §1.2h measured it to
reproduce its own stratified pattern on sham data (+0.0737 versus the real +0.0613), because it
treats each patient's slope as noiseless and shares the junction observation with the stratifier.
B2 is therefore kept **only** as the quantity the hierarchical model and its sham calibration must
explain, never as evidence.

**Selected alternative (A): hierarchical Bayesian longitudinal trajectory model with a two-component
mixture and an observation-intensity submodel.** Per-patient random pre-index level and slope, a
slope change at index, arm × stratum fixed effects on the slope change, a two-component mixture
(responder fraction π per arm) on the post-index slope, a **level-dependent observation-error
variance** (the H2 model, so measurement noise is not confused with biology), and a submodel for
testing intensity (sicker patients are measured more often; MEASURED post-ICI sampling density is
non-random). Inputs: all HBsAg values in days −1095 to +730 for every eligible patient, with the
covariates in §3.1(iii). Target: joint posterior over slope change, τ_s, π and the noise model.
Split: derivation pre-2023, temporal holdout 2023+. Evaluation: posterior predictive checks on held-out
trajectories, **simulation-based calibration**, coverage of τ, and comparison against B on the subset
where B is computable. Budget: CPU-adequate (MEASURED — the probes above ran in <2 min on one core);
estimated 2–6 h on 8–16 CPUs for the full MCMC with SBC replicates, well inside the 16-CPU /
28,800 s solver envelope. GPU is **not** required and is not requested; if SBC replicates dominate
runtime, a GPU-backed vectorised sampler is an optional accelerant, not a scientific requirement.

**Why A is now evidence-selected rather than preferred on sophistication.** §1.2h measured the exact
failure mode A is built to avoid: a two-point slope difference anchored on a noisy junction value
manufactures the stratified pattern. A's level-dependent observation-error term and per-patient latent
slope are the principled fix — the junction value becomes one noisy observation of a latent level
rather than the endpoint of a fit — and §1.2h's Test B showed the cheap junction-free fix is
**underpowered** in this snapshot (ICI 170, TKI 52, LOCO 6 patients with ≥3 pre values), so there is
no cheap substitute. A must additionally be validated against the sham-index data: **if A also
reproduces a stratified slope-change gradient on sham-indexed data, A is as biased as B2 and its
continuous estimand is dropped** (§3.6(1)). That is a pre-specified way for the expensive model to
fail, not a promise that it works.

**What A reveals that B loses:** (i) the 2,600 ICI patients B2 discards, which is the difference between
an inconclusive and a decisive answer on the continuous estimand; (ii) separation of level-dependent
measurement noise from level-dependent biology — the crux of H2, and impossible in B1/B2, neither of
which has a noise model;
(iii) the responder fraction π, so a subgroup effect hidden inside a null mean is detectable;
(iv) honest uncertainty under informative observation times.

**Rejected alternative, with its revival condition.** A two-compartment (hepatocyte + tumour) HBsAg
generative model was developed and rejected on MEASURED grounds: at the measured tumour share it is
**not recoverable** (simulation correlation 0.042–0.335), whereas the reduced decline-rate model is
(correlation 0.86, bias 0.002). It is also unnecessary, since the tumour share is ≈0 (§1.2f). Revive
only if IU/mL-calibrated HBsAg, HBV RNA / HBcrAg, or tumour HBV-integration status becomes available —
none is present in this snapshot.

## 4. What this study cannot settle

- **It cannot establish HBsAg seroclearance to clinical standards.** A single sub-cutoff index value is
  not confirmed seroclearance (which needs a confirmatory repeat and anti-HBs); the rates reported here
  are **upper bounds**, so a small number is the stronger statement. Clinical adjudication is required.
- **It cannot match [K1]'s IU/mL strata.** The index scale is uncalibrated and has no unit column; the
  comparison with "<100 IU/mL" is ordinal only. A refutation of [K1] *as stated in IU/mL* is therefore
  not available from this cohort — only a refutation of the stratified claim on an ordinal low-antigen
  stratum. This is the single most important limit on the contribution.
- **It cannot prove absence of a non-cytolytic effect.** Without HBV RNA, HBcrAg, anti-HBs titres or
  intrahepatic sampling, "no measurable HBsAg trajectory change" is not "no immunological effect".
- **It cannot settle NA contribution.** NA *state* is recorded but duration/adherence are not, and
  [K1]'s responders were almost all on NA.
- **It cannot speak to survival or tumour outcomes.** No mortality linkage in this snapshot. This also
  means the arms differ in unobserved competing risk: ICI patients are more advanced, so informative
  censoring by death is possible in the *direction that lowers* observed ICI event rates, and the
  cumulative-incidence analysis must treat loss of follow-up as a competing event and report the
  sensitivity of H1 to it.
- **It cannot identify the mechanism of the generic post-index decline (H3).** ALT, bilirubin and
  albumin are injury *proxies*, [K3] shows tumour progression drives most ALT elevation in exactly
  this population, and no histology, HBV RNA or intrahepatic antigen staining exists here. H3 can
  rank explanations by consistency, not establish one.
- **The audits in §1.2h/§1.2i bound the estimator, not the biology.** A sham-index analysis shows an
  estimator can generate a pattern; it does not show that any particular observed pattern *is*
  spurious. That is why the audit's consequence is a change of estimand plus a mandatory calibration
  deliverable, not a claim that [K1]'s result is an artefact.
- **It is single-centre and Chinese-genotype-dominant** (B/C), so generalisation to other genotypes is
  unsupported; external replication is required.
- **Follow-up is shorter than the published event horizon.** Median follow-up is ~4.6 months and only
  294 ICI patients are observed beyond 540 d, against [K1]'s median 16.5 months to the endpoint. The
  730-d endpoint rates MEASURED here barely exceed the 365-d rates, which argues the null is not purely
  a follow-up artefact, but the 24-month contrast remains underpowered and must be reported as such.

## 5. Disposition of the three expert research demonstrations

- **Delphi (Nature 2025, learned disease sequences).** Not adopted. A bounded adaptation —
  sequence-learned disease-history ordering as a covariate — fails on a concrete missing dependency:
  the HCC `diagnoses` table has **no date column at all** (`诊断名称`, `诊断类型` only), removing the
  dated-trajectory structure the method learns from. Not a dismissal of sequence models; the
  longitudinal part of *this* proposal is treated as the latent-trajectory problem it is (§3.5).
- **ALADYNOULLI (Nature 2026, Bayesian longitudinal latent structure).** The closest of the three,
  and its influence is explicit: §3.5's hierarchical latent-trajectory model is the same family of
  idea. Its genetic arm is not reproducible here (no genetics in this snapshot). Adaptation, not
  reproduction.
- **Oncoformer (Cell 2026, multimodal EHR + imaging).** Not adopted. Its main text and STAR Methods
  are **unavailable** in the local bundle (publisher 403/401; only supplement and bibliographic
  metadata present) and have not been read. Independently, this snapshot has **no image files** —
  `examinations` holds narrative reports only — so the multimodal experiment is impossible here.

## 7. UNRESOLVED objections

1. **Ordinal-versus-IU/mL mismatch (the most serious).** If the index scale's lowest stratum does not
   correspond to [K1]'s <100 IU/mL, this study tests a related but not identical claim. Unresolvable
   without assay recalibration; stated as a limit, not minimised.
2. **Conditioning on pre-index slope changes the estimand.** It is a defensible self-controlled
   contrast, but it is not the same quantity [K1] estimated, and the comparison must be made carefully
   rather than asserted. An adjusted association is still not a mechanism.
3. **Paired pre+post n is small** (504/245/189). Model A is motivated precisely by this, but partial
   pooling borrows strength under assumptions that SBC can check only partially.
4. **Informative observation times.** The intensity submodel is a model, not a fix; patients measured
   because they are deteriorating may differ in unmodelled ways.
5. **Index-event confounding.** ICI start is not random in time relative to disease course; the
   pre-index slope conditioning helps but cannot remove it.
6. **Flare proxy.** ALT ≥3× 40 U/L is a crude cytolysis proxy; [K3] shows tumour progression, not
   immune hepatitis, drives most ALT elevation here, so the flare split is confounded by progression.
7. **No untreated reference group exists** (§1.2g), so treatment-independence is inferred from
   cross-arm concordance and pre-index trends rather than demonstrated against an untreated arm.
8. **RESOLVED AGAINST THE PROPOSAL, and recorded as such.** The objection "the positive trajectory
   break could itself be artefactual" was executed as the pre-specified sham-break analysis (§1.2h).
   It came back **confirming the objection** on the interaction the proposal called its scientific
   object. Consequence: the primary estimand moved to the threshold endpoint, finding (d) was demoted
   to a bias measurement, the interpretation of (c) was withdrawn, and a sham calibration of the
   replacement model became a mandatory solver deliverable that can kill the continuous estimand.
9. **The low-stratum ICI excess of +4.3 pp is unexplained and may be real.** The crude low-stratum
   rates are 13.78% (ICI) vs 9.51% (LOCO), overlapping CIs. If weighting and censoring leave that
   difference intact, H1 fails and the functional-cure reading gains support in a much larger cohort
   than [K1]. The proposal is designed so this outcome is reportable, but it is currently the single
   most likely way the headline hypothesis is wrong.
10. **Test C's U-shaped sham pattern is unexplained** (21.17% low, 18.77% high, ~8–10% in the middle,
    with 16.92% sub-cutoff at high sham baseline). It does not reproduce the real monotone gradient,
    so it does not disqualify the primary endpoint, but a large decline rate at *high* sham baseline
    in a heavily sampled subgroup is an observation this proposal cannot currently explain, and it may
    indicate an assay-dilution or censoring behaviour at the top of the index range. Flagged for the
    solver's H2 analysis rather than hidden.
11. **The sham tests share the cohort with the real analysis.** They are internal negative controls,
    not independent replication; a design bias that is uniform across index definitions would evade
    them. External replication in an independent HBV-HCC cohort remains required.
12. **Arm definition by first dated administration ignores treatment switching.** LOCO and TKI patients
    may receive ICI later and are censored only by the never-ICI exclusion at cohort entry; the
    proposal's never-ICI arms exclude *any* recorded ICI, which conversely conditions on the future.
    Both choices are defensible and neither is neutral; the solver must report the primary contrast
    under both the as-entered and the never-exposed definitions.
