This child changes **exactly ONE** element of the parent: it **replaces the parent's M6 "corroborated progression" criterion** with a **slope-independent, duplication-guarded temporal corroboration endpoint (M7)**. Every other parent element (population, index, landmark t*, AFP/ALBI slope exposures, IMGneg/IMGpos/IMGmissing strata, the four rivals, the ALBI negative control, the surveillance-intensity covariates, the intensity-adjusted re-test, the conjunctive-falsification structure, the measured attainability figures and their thresholds) is inherited **verbatim**. No new modality-based element is introduced.

## 0. The ONE change in this child — and the real flaw it fixes

**The parent's M6 flaw (stated explicitly).** The parent's M6 declared a progression "corroborated" when **≥2 of three** indicators held: (1) lexical imaging progression; (2) **sustained AFP rise**; (3) **repeat liver-directed procedure**. Two of those three — the sustained-AFP-rise rule and the repeat-procedure rule — are **slope-driven / decision-driven**:

- The sustained-AFP-rise indicator is a **monotone function of the same AFP trajectory** whose slope is the exposure. Pairing an AFP-slope exposure with an outcome that fires when AFP rises is **circular**: it guarantees a positive exposure–outcome association under the null of a shared-lexical-pipeline artifact, because the exposure and one leg of the outcome are computed from the same lab series. M6 therefore **cannot** test the detection-bias rival R_measurement in the direction that matters.
- The repeat-procedure indicator is **decision-driven**: a second liver-directed procedure is itself ordered *because* AFP rose and/or a scan looked suspicious — i.e. the treatment decision is a downstream function of the exposure and of the same clinical reading. Using it as one of the two required corroborating legs re-imports the very channel the child claims to break, and adds guarantee-time structure.

Because M6 mixes one genuinely independent channel (free-text imaging) with two channels that are functions of the exposure/decision, an M6 attenuation result would be **uninterpretable**: it could arise from a real loss of signal *or* simply from M6 requiring two correlated-with-exposure legs. This child removes that defect.

**The M7 replacement (the child's whole contribution).** M7 is a **purely temporal, slope-independent** corroboration built ONLY on the imaging free-text channel and the clock:

> **M7 (corroborated event)** = the **first post-landmark qualifying progression report** — an `examinations` row with `开始时间 > t*` whose `检查所见` **OR** `检查诊断` contains any of {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多} — that is **CONFIRMED by a SECOND, DISTINCT qualifying report at `开始时间 ≥ first_report.开始时间 + 30 days`**. Distinctness requires **distinct `检查号`** AND **distinct `开始时间`**. A report is **EXCLUDED as a corroborating witness if its `检查号` equals the `检查号` of ANY pre-landmark or landmark-window study** (the **duplication guard**).

M7 uses **no AFP value, no AFP slope, and no procedure record** in the event definition. It therefore cannot be circular with respect to the AFP-slope exposure. It keeps the same lexical word set as the parent's imaging indicator, but it never *reads the exposure*, so a positive/negative M7 result is now informative for R_measurement. Note honestly what M7 does **not** by itself do: it tests **temporal corroboration of the recorded lexical event** (is the progression report reproduced on a later, independent study?), not radiologist-adjudicated truth. It removes the slope/decision circularity and the duplicated-row artifact; it does not establish mRECIST ground truth (see §8).

**Why the duplication guard is required (verified data fact).** In the frozen source the **same report text is duplicated across multiple `检查` rows that share one `检查号`** — e.g. 肾上腺 / 胰 / 胆 rows each carrying byte-identical liver-ultrasound text. Without the guard, a single physical report could be counted as both the first and the second "distinct" corroborating report, faking corroboration for a patient whose AFP happened to rise. Excluding any witness whose `检查号` matches a pre-landmark/landmark-window `检查号`, and requiring distinct `检查号` + distinct `开始时间`, kills that artifact. (For the same reason no modality-based element is built: `机器型号` is a machine-name field — e.g. `ALOKA4000`, `PHILIPS HD11`, `GE E9-III` — not a clean CT/MRI modality.)

**Primary test.** Within the **IMGneg** stratum, fit the inherited per-1-SD AFP-slope landmark Cox **twice** — once with the parent's M0 endpoint (single-report lexical event) and once with **M7** (corroborated event) — and report the AFP-slope **HR and 95% CI for each**, plus a **1-df likelihood-ratio test of the event-definition × AFP-slope interaction**. The differential-observability logic: if an imaging-independent AFP signal is real, it should survive requiring *temporal corroboration* and the M0→M7 direction should be consistent; if the M0 signal is a shared-lexical-pipeline / duplicated-row artifact, it should **attenuate to the null under M7** while remaining positive under M0, or the event-definition interaction should favour M0.

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance (inherited verbatim)
After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, surveillance couples serial serum AFP with scheduled cross-sectional imaging. The parent asks whether the within-patient post-LDT AFP slope predicts time to local progression, and whether it does so specifically when the landmark imaging is **clean** (no progression descriptor). That is the *surveillance-adjunct* decision: whether serial AFP should intensify imaging when the current scan is uninformative.

- **H1 (leading claim):** among patients with a clean landmark imaging (IMGneg), a higher within-patient AFP slope over (index, t*] still predicts a higher hazard of subsequent progression — an **imaging-independent early AFP signal**.
- **Strongest rival R_concordance (imaging-concordance artifact):** AFP rises in patients whose landmark/interval imaging already carries sub-threshold suspicion, so within the stringently IMGneg stratum the slope HR attenuates to ~1 while it remains elevated in IMGpos.
- **The decisive rival R_measurement (this lineage's target):** IMGneg is defined by the **absence** of lexical progression words in examination text, while the (M0) outcome is the **presence** of the **same words**. A single shared lexical pipeline that under-records subtle findings can both assign true progressors to IMGneg and then score them as events via the identical words — **manufacturing** the association. The parent's M6 leg tried and failed to separate this (see §0); M7 does so without importing the exposure.
- **Secondary rival R_timing:** an apparent IMGneg signal is produced by intervening scans in (t*, event) scheduled *because* AFP rose (informative observation; the parent's surveillance-intensity machinery is reused, not replaced).

The M7 element makes H1 and R_measurement **differentially observable** without the circularity that crippled M6: if the association is real it survives requiring independent temporal corroboration; if it is a shared-text / duplicated-row artifact it collapses when the endpoint is forced to be corroborated on a distinct later study.

## 4. THE ONE added element (b) — slope-independent, duplication-guarded temporal corroboration (M7)
**M7 (corroborated event):** the first post-landmark qualifying progression report (`examinations` row with `开始时间 > t*` and (`检查所见` OR `检查诊断`) containing any of {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多}) **confirmed by a second, distinct qualifying report at `开始时间 ≥ first_report.开始时间 + 30 days`**; the two witnesses must have **distinct `检查号` AND distinct `开始时间`**; a witness is **EXCLUDED if its `检查号` equals the `检查号` of ANY pre-landmark or landmark-window study** (duplication guard against byte-identical text rows sharing one `检查号`, e.g. liver-US text replicated across 肾上腺/胰/胆). Event time = `first_report.开始时间`. Competitors/censoring unchanged from the parent (转移 competing event; censoring via `出院时间`).

**Independence property.** M7's definition reads **only** the imaging free-text fields and the clock — **no AFP value, no AFP slope, no procedure record**. It therefore cannot be a monotone function of the AFP-slope exposure; unlike M6's sustained-AFP-rise and repeat-procedure legs, it does not re-import the exposure or the treatment decision. This is what makes M0-vs-M7 differentially observable for R_measurement.

**Primary estimand.** Within IMGneg, the per-1-SD AFP-slope HR (95% CI) under **M0** and under **M7**, plus the **1-df LRT of event-definition × AFP-slope**. Report also: number of M7 events, fraction of IMGneg patients with any post-landmark qualifying report, M0↔M7 event concordance, the IMGpos replication (same M0/M7 contrast), the ALBI negative control, and the intensity-adjusted ΔHR.

## 5. Method alternatives (baseline vs substantive alternative) — inherited framing
- **M0 (baseline)** = parent's lexical-only landmark Cox within IMGneg + imaging-status interaction. Its event and its IMGneg stratum share the same words.
- **M7 (alternative)** = same Cox, but the event requires **temporal corroboration on a distinct, duplication-guarded later study**; exposure and covariates identical, so the M0↔M7 difference isolates the corroboration requirement.
- **What M7 reveals that M0 loses:** M0 cannot tell a genuine imaging-independent AFP signal from a shared-text/duplicated-row artifact because its event and stratum are built from identical words. M7 forces corroboration through the clock and distinct-study structure while **staying independent of the exposure** (unlike M6), so it can attribute an M0→M7 attenuation to the measurement artifact rather than to circularity.
- **Why not keep M6:** M6's sustained-AFP-rise and repeat-procedure legs are functions of the exposure/treatment decision (§0), so its "corroboration" is not independent — it cannot falsify R_measurement and can create a spurious positive. M7 is the smallest slope-independent replacement.
- **Deferred alternative (recorded):** a joint latent-class / shared-parameter observation-event model estimating sensitivity/specificity of the lexical endpoint and latent true progression. **Revisit condition:** revisit once M0↔M7 event concordance is estimable and strictly between 0 and 1 (a saturating concordance of ~1 makes latent-class Se/Sp non-identifiable).
- **Budget:** CPU-only, minutes to low tens of minutes; GPU not required.

## 7. Falsification branches (pre-declared; supportive / adverse / inconclusive)
- **Supportive:** M7 IMGneg HR > 1, CI excludes 1, **≥20 M7 corroborated IMGneg events**, and the per-1-SD HR direction is **consistent** between M0 and M7 (both > 1) → H1 is supported after removing the slope/decision circularity and the duplicated-row artifact; AFP may add imaging-independent early signal when the scan is clean.
- **Adverse R_measurement:** M7 IMGneg HR attenuates to a CI including 1 while M0 HR > 1, **or** the event-definition × AFP-slope **LRT p < 0.05 favours M0** → the M0 signal is a shared-lexical-pipeline / duplicated-row artifact; H1 is refuted in its current form.
- **Adverse R_concordance:** M7 IMGneg HR ≈1 (CI includes 1) while IMGpos HR > 1, or imaging-status × AFP-slope interaction p < 0.05 with IMGpos > IMGneg → the signal is confined to imaging-suspicious patients.
- **Inconclusive:** **M7 IMGneg corroborated events < 20**, or **< 50% of IMGneg patients have any post-landmark qualifying report**, or IMGmissing > 50%, or undated landmark images → descriptive HR only; no heterogeneity claim.
- **Precise-null:** both M0 and M7 IMGneg HR within ±0.10 of 1 with a tight CI and a flat ALBI negative control.

Interpretation guard: an imprecise null is Inconclusive, not refutation.

## 8. Honest limits (inherited + M7-specific)
Lexical endpoints require radiologist/clinical **mRECIST adjudication** before any clinical-truth claim; **M7 tests temporal corroboration of the recorded lexical event, not adjudicated truth**, so M7-positive evidence is necessary-but-not-sufficient for a real progression signal. **No mortality / mRECIST PFS** is ascertainable here → no survival claim. Single institution. Lab results carry no units (within-assay use only). **IMGmissing ≈96%** (33,858/35,339) confines the conditional arm to IMGneg n≈602 — the M7-corroborated event count may fall below the pre-declared 20-event floor, in which case the Inconclusive guard fires; the interaction/LRT is underpowered and this guard is pre-declared. The duplication guard depends on `检查号` being a faithful physical-report key; if one physical report were split across distinct `检查号`s, the guard would over-count corroboration, which is why distinct `开始时间` is also required. **NO outcomes, HRs or concordance values were computed in this discovery episode**; the attainability counts are the reused, measured sibling probe ([prior hypothesis]), reported as a measured probe, not a result. Any causal claim needs expert review or another study.
