# Observation-process discrimination for the post-treatment AFP trajectory vs same-draw ALBI trajectory prognostic question in HCC: a pre-specified negative-control analyte slope plus observation-intensity adjustment

Island: hcc (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records). Episode 9. Evolution (child) of
`[prior hypothesis]` (episode-7 leader, valid, mu ~31.6, reference_delivery complete).
Status: design + probe-verified bindings; **RESULTS ARE NOT COMPUTED HERE**.

## 1. Unresolved question, the opening, and the single remaining objection attacked

The parent tests whether the *within-patient AFP trajectory* adds prognostic information for dated
radiologic progression beyond baseline AFP AND beyond same-draw liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics, under a
landmark design with a detection-timing stratum (E_early first post-landmark study vs E_late >=90 d)
and a mandatory time-dependent Cox.

**The objection this child attacks (the one substantive addition).** Even with a dated radiologic
endpoint and an E_early/E_late stratum, imaging is not scheduled at random. If a rising AFP is used to
bring a scan forward, a high AFP slope will *stochastically precede* a progression report because it
triggered the earlier scan (informative-observation / "AFP-triggers-imaging"), with no faster tumour
growth. A timing stratum *describes* this pattern but cannot *exclude* it, because the same
observation process also generates the strata. The decisive, falsifiable discriminator is a
**negative-control analyte**: an analyte drawn on the SAME lab grid that has no plausible causal link
to HCC progression. If the observation process manufactures AFP-slope signal, then the trajectory
slope of that HCC-unrelated analyte should *also* predict progression. If the negative-control slope is
null while AFP survives observation-intensity adjustment, the observation-process rival is weakened.

This is exactly the falsifiable contrast the parent lacks; it is a within-cohort, pre-specified
addition inside the SAME frozen design, so the question is evolved, not restarted.

**Clinical importance.** The actionable difference is unchanged from the parent but now adjudicable:
if AFP slope predicts progression only through the scan-triggering process (negative control also
predicts), the correct clinical change is surveillance INTERVAL policy and *not* AFP-triggered
escalation; if AFP survives both the observation-intensity adjustment and the negative-control null,
the reading supports AFP-informed escalation. A negative control also protects against the
embarrassing failure mode of publishing a "biomarker" that is actually a practice-pattern artefact.

## 3. The ONE added element (pre-specified)

**(a) Negative-control analyte slope.** Analyte = exact label **肌酐** (serum creatinine) in the SAME
`labs` table and SAME `(index, t*]` window, fit with the SAME mixed-model slope machinery as AFP.
Creatinine is routinely drawn, is HCC-unrelated, and reflects renal clearance rather than tumour
activity, so under the null it should show NO true association with progression. A nonzero
negative-control slope HR is direct evidence of observation/practice confounding. Look-alikes MUST be
excluded (exact label `肌酐` only): `肌酐（急）`, `肌酐尿素比`, `尿肌酐测定`, `体液肌酐`, `24小时尿肌酐`,
`尿白蛋白/尿肌酐`, `尿钙/尿肌酐` (all seen in the probe, all excluded).

**(b) Observation-intensity covariate.** `N_imaging` = number of dated `examinations` reports in
`(index, t*]`; `N_labs` = number of distinct `检验时间` draws in `(index, t*]`. Entered as continuous
covariates and as a sensitivity adjustment.

**(c) Sensitivity model.** Primary Cox re-fit adding `N_imaging` and `N_labs` (and, as a second
sensitivity, the index->first-post-landmark-scan interval). Report the AFP-slope HR before vs after
this adjustment and its attenuation.

## 5. Estimand, analysis, and explicit result mapping

**Estimand (unchanged).** HR per 1-unit increase in the within-patient log-AFP slope on the primary
radiologic-progression endpoint, adjusted for baseline AFP, baseline ALBI, same-draw ALBI slope,
age/sex, treatment type, and (in the sensitivity model) `N_imaging`+`N_labs`.

**Primary model.** Landmark Cox at t* for time to first radiologic progression; primary exposure =
mixed-model log-AFP slope; mandatory competitor = same-draw ALBI slope. Mirror with a time-dependent
Cox on the same exposure. Negative-control model: identical pipeline with the `肌酐` slope replacing
the AFP slope, on the same cohort and window.

**Mapping (pre-specified, conjunctive):**
- **SUPPORTIVE:** AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 (HR>1); AND E_late AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1; AND the
  effect holds in the time-dependent Cox; AND it is **robust to observation-intensity adjustment**
  (still CI excludes 1 after adding `N_imaging`+`N_labs`); AND **negative-control (肌酐) slope HR CI
  includes 1** (null). Reading: trajectory information not explained by scan-triggering; supports
  AFP-informed escalation.
- **ADVERSE:** negative-control slope also predicts progression with a similar nonzero HR
  (CI excludes 1, HR>1), OR the AFP effect **collapses** after observation-intensity adjustment (CI
  now includes 1), OR AFP HR>1 holds in E_early but not E_late / vanishes in the time-dependent Cox,
  OR only the ALBI slope is significant. Reading: the signal is observation/practice pattern, not
  tumour activity; change surveillance interval, do not escalate on AFP.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** wide CIs in either the AFP or negative-control model, or event count below the
  pre-set floor (too few events); OR the imaging-flag audit fails the pre-registered agreement floor
  -> fall back to the retreatment proxy and report as inconclusive for radiologic progression.

**Falsification criteria.** The conjunction in SUPPORTIVE must hold; a single nonzero negative-control
slope or AFP attenuation under observation-intensity adjustment falsifies the "biologically-grounded
AFP-slope" reading. The negative-control slope is a *necessary-not-sufficient* check: a null negative
control plus a surviving AFP slope is consistent with, but does not prove, a tumour-activity mechanism.

## 6. Rivals, limits, unascertainable evidence

Rivals mapped to distinct tests: baseline reserve (adjust baseline AFP/ALBI); surveillance intensity
(adjust `N_imaging`+`N_labs`, NEW); regression-to-the-mean (two-draw vs three-draw slope);
measurement/detection timing (E_early/E_late; time-dependent Cox); **observation-process /
AFP-triggers-scan (NEW: negative-control `肌酐` slope)**.

Honest limits: the progression flag remains a LEXICAL extraction from report text and is UNVALIDATED
(pre-registered manual audit, agreement floor, explicit fallback); creatinine co-occurs with the
prognosis-relevant workup, so a *null* negative control is informative but not proof of mechanism;
mortality / mRECIST PFS unascertainable -> NO survival claim; single institution; lab units absent
(`定量结果` used as reported); **NO outcomes are computed in discovery** — the event rate, the
imaging-flag audit, and the estimability of the `肌酐` slope on `(index,t*]` are the solver's first
feasibility outputs. Clinical adjudication of progression reports, and any causal claim, require
expert review or another study; the automatic pipeline can only establish computation and internal
consistency.
