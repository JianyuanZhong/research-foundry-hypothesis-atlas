# Observation-process-standardized and competing-exit-aware test of the frozen eICU respiratory-label proxy

## Status, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

This is an independent substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen scientific schema exactly: 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays, 74 hospitals, the first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`, landmarks `{1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after unit admission, the two exact care-plan labels, decision time `D`, inherited exclusions, raw-scale thresholds, and the fixed clinical windows. It remains a descriptive, noncausal chart-proxy estimand; the label is not treatment, clinician intent, quality, or a probability of benefit.

Prior lineage evidence supports that the literal labels are sparse and heterogeneous across hospitals, that respiratory-chart mappings are incomplete and dominated by documentation opportunity in some branches, and that this snapshot lacks validated bedside SBT/extubation, delivered-setting, reintubation, or blinded clinical adjudication. The parents correctly made endpoint parsing and fail-closed lineage executable, but their principal complete-observed risk difference still leaves a clinically important ambiguity: informative post-D observation can determine who enters the complete endpoint set, while discharge, death, and truncation can remove the opportunity to observe the outcome. A raw complete-case contrast therefore conflates respiratory trajectory with ascertainment and selective survival.

The substantive advance is a pre-specified **observation-process-standardized competing-exit estimand**. Every frozen row remains in the denominator. Competing exits are retained as events rather than silently censored, and endpoint missingness is treated as an explicit observation-censoring process with pre-D/landmark-history inverse-probability weights. The primary contrast is consequently a weighted all-row improvement incidence with a clearly stated missing-at-random-for-the-chart-process assumption, accompanied by an unweighted four-state accounting and fixed sensitivity bounds. No computed finding is claimed here.

## Hypothesis and exact estimand

The falsifiable hypothesis is: conditional on the locked labelled denominator and pre-`D` respiratory state/opportunity, the exact label `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` has a positive, reproducible incremental association with a later **persistent, safety-preserving, structured-chart respiratory trajectory**, after standardizing the endpoint-observation process and retaining death, discharge/transfer, and truncation as competing exits; the label increment transports to hospitals not used for fitting.

For row `i`, let `tau_i = D_i + 1800`. The primary outcome state at `tau_i` is one of `persistent_improvement`, `observed_non_improvement`, `death`, `discharge_transfer`, or `truncation`; a row without a competing exit but without complete endpoint ascertainment is `endpoint_missing`. Exit time is `unitdischargeoffset`; exit cause is fixed from `unitdischargestatus` and `unitdischargelocation`. Persistent improvement is evaluated only from the frozen endpoint windows below and is assigned at `tau_i`, not at the first favorable chart entry. Thus exits are competing terminal states, not ordinary endpoint missingness.

The primary estimand is the arm contrast in a target-population, observation-standardized probability of persistent improvement by `tau` before a competing exit:

`RD_obsstd = E_target[ I(A=1) * Y^I(1) /? ]` is not used as a causal potential-outcome quantity. Instead, use the explicitly associational functional

`p_a^* = (1/N) sum_i I(A_i=a) * [w_i I(S_i=persistent_improvement)] / [w_i I(A_i=a)]`,

where weights are defined below only for rows that are not competing exits and are endpoint-observed, while rows with competing exits contribute their observed competing state with weight 1 in a companion competing-risk decomposition. Operationally, report the arm-specific weighted multinomial probabilities `(p_a^I, p_a^N, p_a^death, p_a^discharge, p_a^truncation)` over all 540 rows, with `p_a^I` the weighted improvement probability and `RD_obsstd = p_1^I-p_0^I`. The denominator is the fixed arm row count, not the complete-case count. This is a descriptive standardization to the pooled pre-specified observation regime, not a causal intervention or treatment effect.

A second, mathematically separate target is the **cause-specific cumulative incidence under observed follow-up** for each exit cause, estimated from the exact exit offsets. It is reported at `tau` to show how much arm difference is attributable to death versus non-death exit. Because persistent improvement is a tau-assigned trajectory classification rather than an event-time process, it is not assigned an artificial event time and is not put into an Aalen-Johansen risk set as though its onset were observed. The decomposition must reconcile to the all-row state table up to the documented observation-censoring standardization.

The observation-standardized functional assumes, within hospital/landmark/arm strata, conditional independent endpoint ascertainment given the locked pre-D features and the pre-specified observation history, and positivity of being observed among non-exiting rows. These are assumptions about this chart process, not clinical truths. A failure of positivity or sensitivity to the fixed selection-model bounds is a falsification/inconclusive result, not permission to redefine the cohort.

## Observation process and competing exits

First classify each frozen row before any weighting:

1. If `unitdischargeoffset` is nonmissing and `<=D+1800`, assign exactly one competing exit. Trim and case-fold `unitdischargestatus`; `Expired` is `death`. Otherwise a classifiable status/location is `discharge_transfer`; unclassifiable status/location is `truncation`. Also flag whether exit is `<=D+360`.
2. Among rows without a competing exit, assign `endpoint_missing` if any required repeated component, safety signal, or airway corroboration is absent, with a mutually exclusive first reason and all-reason flags (`no_pre_support`, `no_post_support`, `no_safety`, `no_airway_corroboration`, `parser_rejection`, `time_tie_conflict`, `source_absence`, `source_error`).
3. Otherwise assign `persistent_improvement` or `observed_non_improvement` according to the frozen endpoint.

Do not call discharge/death/truncation censoring, do not impute them as non-improvement, and do not let post-D observation support change eligibility, arm, `D`, or the endpoint dictionary.

Define an outcome-blind observation history `H_i` from source/opportunity information available by each relevant ascertainment boundary: pre-D respiratory/vital source presence, counts, distinct clinical times, maximum gaps and entry lags; and, only for a row still alive/in-unit, post-D chart-opportunity indicators through `D+360` (source presence/counts/times and respiratory-care presence), not parsed post-D values, endpoint medians, safety pass/fail, or endpoint state. The boundary is fixed before looking at the O-window values. Fit a pooled discrete observation model for `R_i=1` (complete primary endpoint ascertainment) among non-competing rows, conditional on `A`, landmark, hospital training stratum, age, inherited pre-D physiologic-screen covariates, and `H_i`. Hospital is a stratum, not a held-out outcome rate. The model is a regularized logistic model with all transformations, missing indicators, and coefficients fit within the training hospitals of each leave-one-hospital-out fold. It must report positivity and calibration; no endpoint-derived quantity may enter the observation model.

Use stabilized inverse-observation weights `w_i = Pr(R=1 | A,L)` / `Pr(R=1 | A,L,H_i)` for non-competing rows with `R=1`; competing exits retain weight 1 and are not in the observation denominator. The primary estimate uses uncensored observed rows with these weights and fixed arm denominators, with a prespecified numerical cap of 20 only as a computational safeguard; report the uncapped distribution and fraction capped. If the cap binds in more than 5% of weighted rows, or estimated observation probability is below 0.05 in any arm-by-landmark stratum with support, the weighted estimand is flagged unstable and cannot be called supportive. A fixed sensitivity uses caps 10 and 5; these are diagnostics, not post hoc choices. A complete-case unweighted RD is reported only as a comparator and never as the primary answer.

Because missingness can remain informative after `H_i`, run a fixed selection-model sensitivity: multiply odds of observation for missing rows by `Gamma` in `{0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2}` (equivalently tilt the fitted observation odds before renormalization), refit no outcome model, and recompute `p_a^I`. The primary claim must remain directionally positive and above the meaningful increment across the predeclared grid, or be labelled selection-sensitive/inconclusive. This is a sensitivity bound on the chart process, not evidence that missing values are clinically normal.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

Run the unchanged P-versus-B pre-D placebo, D-720 lead-time placebo, stricter 60-minute spacing, leave-one-component-out and source-family ablations, documentation-density negative control, fixed airway-recency and airway-interval sensitivities, and the unweighted complete-case comparator. The observation model and weighting recipe are frozen before arm-specific endpoint results; all attrition and reasons remain in `trajectory_rows`.

A computationally **supportive** result requires every gate below: exact parent lineage and parser/source verification; at least five hospitals each with at least 10 complete primary endpoint rows per arm and at least 50% of complete rows in those hospitals, with no hospital above 40% of either arm; observation positivity and cap/selection diagnostics pass; the observation-standardized improvement RD is positive with at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions positive; label-added held-out prediction improves both micro and equal-hospital-macro log loss by at least 0.01 over the identical state-plus-opportunity model with a stay-cluster 95% interval excluding zero; the gain survives the fixed Gamma grid (or its failure is explicitly selection-sensitive), component/source ablations, both placebos, and the valid permutation null; no gain is explained by weighted missingness or competing exits; and airway corroboration is present in at least 10% of each arm. This supports only reproducible incremental information in this structured chart construct.

An **adverse** result requires adequate support and a pre-specified null or contradiction: weighted RD/held-out label increment is absent, negative, permutation-compatible, placebo-sized, reversed across hospitals, dependent on one component/source/site, or disappears after observation standardization while documentation opportunity remains predictive. This is adverse to the transportable chart-proxy claim, not proof that bedside evaluation was absent or that missingness was purely administrative.

The result is **inconclusive** if lineage/parser gates fail; fewer than five supported hospitals or no held-out arm contrast; competing exits dominate so no arm-specific improvement-versus-exit comparison is estimable; observation positivity fails; weights are unstable or heavily capped; the Gamma sensitivity crosses zero or the meaningful increment; endpoint/airway support is too sparse; state and ascertainment cannot be separated; or fixed variants disagree. No relaxed threshold, post hoc label expansion, endpoint-derived weight, complete-case-only conclusion, or redefined denominator is allowed.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to both parents, this child does not alter the frozen cohort, label, `D`, exclusions, raw-scale endpoint, windows, or proxy schema. It changes the primary statistical target from an implicitly selected complete-observed contrast to an explicit all-row observation-standardized multinomial contrast: death, discharge/transfer, and truncation remain competing terminal states, while non-exit endpoint missingness is modelled as a prespecified observation process and stress-tested under fixed selection tilts. It also requires fold-specific refitting of observation weights and uncertainty propagation, so transport is tested without importing held-out hospital ascertainment rates. Remaining uncertainty is substantial: conditional observation exchangeability and positivity cannot be proved from these data, endpoint observations may encode unmeasured severity, and the structured fields cannot validate bedside liberation. A positive result therefore remains a falsifiable chart-process association, never a clinical treatment conclusion.
