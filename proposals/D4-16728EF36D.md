It is a new same-data follow-up motivated by an estimand defect, not an independent replication, and the prior global p-value does not open, close, or donate alpha to this experiment.

## Mathematically identified direct pairwise estimands

For pair `q` in `{CO,CN}`, all expectations below are over its fixed pair population `P_q`. Let `A_q=1` denote CRC and `A_q=0` its named comparator. Define

- `e_q(C)=Pr_q(A_q=1|C)`,
- `g_q(C)=E_q[S|C]`, and
- `mu_qa(C)=E_q[S|A_q=a,C]` for interpretation only.

The primary three-vector is

`beta_q = E_q[(A_q-e_q(C))(S-g_q(C))] / E_q[(A_q-e_q(C))^2]`.

Because `A_q` is binary and both nuisances are the true pair-specific conditional means,

`beta_q = E_q[e_q(C)(1-e_q(C)){mu_q1(C)-mu_q0(C)}] / E_q[e_q(C)(1-e_q(C))]`.

Thus each component is a directly interpretable overlap-weighted average conditional mean difference in the observed annualized adverse-oriented marker units, CRC minus the named comparator, within that pair only. Positive values mean more adverse CRC trajectories; negative values mean less adverse CRC trajectories. This identity is the repair: no third-group mean enters either target. It is associational and relies on positivity and nuisance adequacy; it is not causal standardization.

The nulls are `H_CO: beta_CO=(0,0,0)` and `H_CN: beta_CN=(0,0,0)`. No equivalence margin is available, so failure to reject either null is not evidence of equality.

## Frozen nuisance models, cross-fitting, and estimator

Fit each pair separately. Use 20 repeated five-fold outer cross-fits under seeds `20260909,...,20260928`. Within each pair, outer folds are stratified jointly by `A_q`, `lag_late`, and sex. Every outer test fold must contain each observed joint stratum, every training fold must contain at least four people in each stratum, and train/test overlap must be zero. Pair assignments are frozen before fitting. The same CRC participant may have different fold indices across the two pair populations; shared multiplier inference below, not forced fold identity, preserves cross-pair dependence.

Within every outer training fold:

- Fit `e_q` by binary ridge logistic regression of `A_q` on exactly `C`. The intercept and `lag_late` coefficient are unpenalized.
- Fit one pooled ridge-linear model for each component of `g_q`, regressing that observed adverse-oriented slope on exactly `C`; the intercept is unpenalized.
- Standardize continuous predictors age, repeat Hb, repeat MCV, and repeat platelet using outer-training means and population SDs. Do not standardize sex, lag, or slope outcomes.
- Select ridge penalties separately from `10^-4,...,10^4` by five-fold inner CV confined to the outer training fold, using group-stratified inner folds, log loss for `e_q`, MSE for `g_q`, a `1e-12` tie tolerance, and the largest tied penalty. Held-out people influence none of scaling, tuning, or fitting.

For repetition `r` and held-out person `i`, calculate `R_ir=A_i-e_hat_ir(C_i)`, `V_ir=S_i-g_hat_ir(C_i)`, numerator contribution `N_ir=R_ir V_ir`, and denominator contribution `D_ir=R_ir^2`. Average contributions within participant over the 20 repetitions: `N_i=mean_r N_ir` and `D_i=mean_r D_ir`. Estimate

`beta_hat_q = mean_i(N_i)/Dbar_q`, where `Dbar_q=mean_i(D_i)`.

The centered three-vector influence contribution is

`phi_iq=(N_i-beta_hat_q D_i)/Dbar_q`.

Use one participant-level covariance `V_q=cov_q(phi_iq)` and robust SE `sqrt(diag(V_q)/n_q)`. Repeated appearances are never treated as independent. This score is Neyman-orthogonal to first-order perturbations of both pair-specific conditional-mean nuisances under the stated moment model.

## Multiplicity hierarchy and shared calibration

Historical global non-rejection is reported but is not a gate. The new primary family contains exactly the two three-dimensional pairwise nulls and no others.

Embed each `phi_iq` into the original 998-person universe with zeros for people outside `P_q`. Draw exactly 20,000 independent standard-normal participant multipliers using seed `20280909`, sharing each person's draw across both pair calculations. In each draw compute pair-specific mean perturbations using denominator `n_q`, and quadratic statistics `T_q*=n_q b_q*' V_q^-1 b_q*`. Observed statistics are `T_q=n_q beta_hat_q' V_q^-1 beta_hat_q`.

Use the draw-wise maximum `M*=max(T_CO*,T_CN*)` for single-step strong familywise control. Report:

- family omnibus `p_family=(1+sum[M*>=max(T_CO,T_CN)])/(20001)`;
- pair-adjusted `p_adj,q=(1+sum[M*>=T_q])/(20001)`.

A named pair is rejected only if its `p_adj,q<0.05`; nominal local p-values are audit-only. If at least one pair rejects, component localization uses the 95th percentile of the maximum absolute studentized multiplier perturbation across all six components. Report all six family-simultaneous intervals, but interpret a component only inside a pair whose adjusted quadratic test rejected. No sites, interactions, alternative groupings, or RDW results may enter this primary family.

## Calibration/support gates, benchmark, and falsifications

The primary computation is invalid if a hash changes; the 998/75/69/854 or 144/929 counts change; an `eid` is duplicated/lost; source/event/date reconciliation fails; any third-group person enters a pair; any fold lacks the frozen joint-stratum support; held-out leakage occurs; a prediction/score is nonfinite; fewer than 20,000 shared finite multipliers are produced; `Dbar_q<0.01`; any held-out propensity lies outside `[0.01,0.99]`; covariance is non-positive-definite or has condition number above `10^10`; or statistic definitions differ between observed and multiplier calculations. Such a result is `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED_COMPUTATION` and has no scientific interpretation.

Report for every pair and repetition: propensity range, log loss, intercept-only log loss, Brier score, selected penalties, calibration intercept/slope, slope-residual MSE and intercept-only MSE, convergence, fold support, `Dbar`, covariance eigenvalues/condition, and multiplier tails. A technically complete result is nevertheless calibration-inconclusive if any calibration fit is nonfinite, median propensity log-loss regret versus intercept-only exceeds `0.01`, any marker's median slope-MSE ratio versus intercept-only exceeds `1.10`, or five or more of 20 propensity calibration slopes are nonpositive. These gates were frozen before pairwise outcomes.

Benchmark each pair with ordinary unpenalized binary logistic regression of `A_q` on `C` versus `C+S`, using outer-unrelated full-pair predictor standardization and 5,000 fitted-null parametric bootstrap replicates under fixed observed predictors. Use seeds `20290909` for CO and `20290910` for CN. Report three-df LR statistics and plus-one p-values; apply Holm adjustment across the two benchmark p-values. This benchmark is not the estimand. A primary pair rejection without Holm-adjusted benchmark rejection, or a Holm-adjusted benchmark rejection where the corresponding primary pair does not reject, is method-discordant and therefore scientifically inconclusive.

Run two frozen falsifications:

1. Under seeds `20261909,...,20261928`, jointly permute all three slope columns within lag-by-sex strata separately inside each pair, hold `A_q` and `C` fixed, reuse the unchanged propensity predictions/folds, retune all linear nuisances training-only, and use 5,000 multipliers per diagnostic. This is not an exact biological null. Any nonfinite run or four or more nominal diagnostic p-values below 0.05 in either pair is a repeated-extremes calibration failure and makes a supportive primary pattern inconclusive.
2. Replace the three-marker block by adverse RDW trajectory from field `30070`, using the same pairwise direct estimand and shared 20,000-multiplier two-pair FWER calibration. RDW is exploratory and cannot open or rescue a primary gate. If RDW rejects while a primary pair rejects, specificity is unresolved and the supportive label is downgraded to inconclusive; RDW non-rejection does not validate mechanism.

## Prospective supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

A `SUPPORTIVE_SELECTED_CASE_PAIRWISE_LOCALIZATION` result requires all computation and calibration gates, at least one pair-adjusted primary rejection, a family-simultaneous component interval wholly above zero in that supported pair, no family-simultaneous component interval wholly below zero in that pair, concordant Holm-adjusted benchmark rejection, no repeated-extremes falsification failure, and no RDW pair rejection. It supports only that, among these selected recorded cases and in that named pair-specific covariate-overlap population, CRC had a more adverse adjusted trajectory in at least one frozen CBC marker. It does not establish occult bleeding, inflammation, pathology, stage, or clinical usefulness.

An `ADVERSE_DIRECTION_SELECTED_CASE_ASSOCIATION` requires the same technical and benchmark support but a supported pair with at least one family-simultaneous interval wholly below zero and none wholly above zero. It weakens the directional hypothesis that recorded CRC has more adverse trajectories than that comparator, while still showing association rather than equivalence. Mixed precisely positive and negative components are `ASSOCIATION_DETECTED_BUT_MECHANISTICALLY_INCONCLUSIVE`.

A non-rejecting family or pair, broad intervals, calibration/support failure, benchmark discordance, repeated-extremes failure, RDW co-rejection, or rejection without component localization is `INCONCLUSIVE_PAIRWISE_LOCALIZATION`. In particular, stable non-rejection is not adverse/equivalence evidence because no clinically justified near-zero margin was prespecified. The prior global non-rejection and a new pairwise non-rejection may be described as concordant same-data non-rejections, never as independent replication or proof of no pairwise difference.
