## 1. Scientific Opening

The D3-M2 seed asks: "Which competing macrophage–fibroblast or endothelial–fibroblast mechanism is distinguishable by perturbation?"

**Established evidence (not re-tested here):**
- Alivernini et al. [K1] showed MerTKposCD206pos synovial tissue macrophages (STMs) induce a "repair response" in synovial fibroblasts (FLS) in a micrococulture system, and that a low MerTKpos:MerTKneg ratio predicts disease flare.
- Kuo et al. [K3] showed HBEGF+ inflammatory macrophages drive FLS invasiveness via EGFR; MERTK marks a distinct phagocytic macrophage cluster.
- Wei et al. [K2] showed endothelial-derived NOTCH3 signaling drives perivascular/sublining FLS identity in RA.

**Unresolved question:** The molecular mechanism by which MerTKpos vs MerTKneg macrophages differentially program FLS is uncharacterized. Is MerTK signaling the specific conduit of the MerTKpos→FLS response, and is that response mechanistically distinct from the MerTKneg→FLS inflammatory response?

**Discovery-phase probe (executed this episode, bounded, not the full study):** A within-donor pathway-score screen on E-MTAB-8873 (5 FLS donors with full Mpos/Mneg/FLS_only triplets) showed that the inflammatory (chemokine/cytokine) FLS score rises in BOTH Mpos and Mneg coculture vs FLS_only (mean Δ log1p +0.47 Mpos, +1.51 Mneg), while the TGFβ/ECM "repair" score does not rise in Mpos (mean Δ −0.31). This is **adverse to the naive framing** that Mpos induces a clean repair signature in this system, and motivates the refined, more discriminating hypothesis below: the testable question is whether the two macrophage states program **differentially distinct** FLS transcriptional states, and whether MerTK inhibition specifically ablates the Mpos-specific component. The probe also confirmed (a) the contrasts are identifiable (5 paired donors), (b) macrophage marker scores are elevated in all coculture samples (residual-macrophage-RNA confound must be quantified), and (c) only 3 Mpos_Inhibitor and 1 Mneg_Inhibitor within-donor pairs exist (H2 is underpowered and exploratory).

**Clinical importance:** If MerTK-dependent signaling specifically drives a distinct FLS program, MerTK-targeted therapy would have a defined mechanistic effect on the synovial fibroblast compartment (beyond macrophage depletion), informing why MerTK blockade shows variable efficacy in RA. If the Mpos-specific FLS response is MerTK-independent, the clinical relevance of MerTKpos macrophage frequency is likely indirect (e.g., via phagocytosis of apoptotic cells rather than direct FLS signaling).

## 2. Hypotheses

**H1 (Macrophage-state-specific FLS programming):** In E-MTAB-8873, Mpos and Mneg coculture induce **differentially distinct** FLS transcriptional states: the Mpos vs FLS_only and Mneg vs FLS_only within-donor contrasts yield non-overlapping gene-level signatures (Mpos-specific up-regulated genes vs Mneg-specific up-regulated genes), with the Mpos signature enriched for TGFβ/ECM/anti-inflammatory genes (TGFB1, COL1A1, COL1A2, COL3A1, FN1, TIMP1, THBS1, TGFBR1, TGFBR2) and/or anti-inflammatory regulators (IL1RN), and the Mneg signature enriched for inflammatory chemokines (CXCL10, CCL2, CCL5, CXCL8, IL6, IL1B, TNF, ICAM1). [K1][K3]

**H2 (MerTK dependence of the Mpos-specific signal, exploratory):** MerTK inhibitor pre-treatment of Mpos macrophages specifically ablates the Mpos-specific FLS signature (reversal toward FLS_only for Mpos-specific genes), while MerTK inhibitor pre-treatment of Mneg macrophages does not further alter the Mneg inflammatory signature. **Power caveat:** only 3 within-donor Mpos_Inhibitor pairs and 1 Mneg_Inhibitor pair exist; this contrast is underpowered and reported as exploratory with per-donor effect sizes, not as a significance test. [K1]

**H3 (Cross-dataset pathway coherence, exploratory):** In GSE95588_d1d2d3d4, within-patient drug-vs-baseline comparisons (MT and MTF contexts) show that drugs expected to suppress EGFR/HBEGF (A77, AG1478) or TLR4 (GW9662) shift the perivascular (SPARC, COL3A1, ACTA2, MYH11) vs repair/ECM (COL1A1, COL1A2, FN1, TIMP1, THBS1) fibroblast balance in the direction predicted by the cross-tissue stromal atlas (Notch/EGF ligand-driven perivascular phenotype) [K2], providing pathway-level coherence independent of the MerTK perturbation. **Constraints:** donor mapping unresolved → exploration only, no inferential claims; coculture r1/r2 reuse of GSE57723 data is excluded from this matrix.

**What this is NOT:** This does not re-claim the NOTCH3→FLS identity axis [K2], the HBEGF→EGFR→invasiveness pathway [K3], or the MerTKpos→repair phenotype [K1]. The novel contribution is the **perturbation-based dissection** of whether MerTK signaling is the specific molecular conduit of the MerTKpos→FLS response and whether the two macrophage states program mechanistically distinct FLS states.

## 4. Analysis Plan (solver experiment)

### 4.1 Baseline: within-donor paired differential expression (E-MTAB-8873)
1. For each of the 5 FLS donors with full triplets, compute log1p(MolsPerCell) per gene.
2. Contrasts: Mpos vs FLS_only; Mneg vs FLS_only; Mpos vs Mneg; Mpos_Inhibitor vs Mpos (3 pairs); Mneg_Inhibitor vs Mneg (1 pair).
3. Per-gene paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests (n=5 for main contrasts; n=3/1 for inhibitor contrasts reported descriptively), Benjamini-Hochberg FDR across the 445 panel genes; median log2 fold change as effect size.
4. **Contamination control (required):** compute macrophage marker score (CD14, CD163, MERTK, TREM2, SPP1) per sample; report per-contrast contamination deltas. If Mpos and Mneg contamination differ substantially, re-run the main contrast after regressing out the macrophage marker score and report both results.

### 4.2 Alternative (mechanistic): pathway-coherence scoring
- **Repair/ECM score:** mean log1p of TGFB1, COL1A1, COL1A2, COL3A1, FN1, TIMP1, THBS1, TGFBR1, TGFBR2, TGFBR3, TGFBI.
- **Inflammatory score:** mean log1p of IL6, IL1B, TNF, CXCL8, CXCL10, CCL2, CCL5, CCL20, ICAM1, IL1RN.
- **MMP/TIMP net-remodeling ratio:** mean log1p(MMP1, MMP3, MMP9, MMP13) − log1p(TIMP1).
- **What this reveals that gene-level DE loses:** (a) pathway coherence (do all 11 repair genes shift together, indicating a program, vs scattered single-gene changes); (b) net matrix-remodeling direction (MMP/TIMP balance) invisible in single-gene DE; (c) ligand–receptor pairing (TGFB1 up only with TGFBR1/TGFBR2 up indicates signaling capacity). The discovery probe already computed these scores (adverse-to-naive-H1 result, Section 1); the full analysis re-computes them per donor with per-gene decomposition.

### 4.3 GSE95588 pathway validation (exploratory)
- Within-patient, within-context (MT vs MTF) drug-vs-baseline deltas for NOTCH score (NOTCH3, JAG1, JAG2, DLL4), EGFR/HBEGF score (HBEGF, EGFR, EREG, EGF, ADAM17), perivascular score (SPARC, COL3A1, ACTA2, MYH11), repair/ECM score (COL1A1, COL1A2, FN1, TIMP1, THBS1).
- The discovery probe (probe3) already computed 46 drug-vs-baseline deltas; observed pattern: EGFR inhibitors (A77, AG) reduce the EGFR/HBEGF score in MTF context (P2: −0.98, −1.43; P3: −0.11, −0.54) with mixed perivascular effects; TLR4 inhibitor (GW) reduces perivascular score in 3 of 4 MTF/MT contexts. This is **exploratory coherence, not validation** (donor mapping unresolved, n=1–2 per drug per patient).

## 5. Falsification Criteria

**Supportive:**
- H1: Mpos-specific and Mneg-specific up-regulated gene sets (FDR<0.1) have low overlap (<20% shared genes), Mpos-specific set enriched for repair/ECM or anti-inflammatory genes, Mneg-specific set enriched for inflammatory chemokines; pathway scores show divergent Mpos vs Mneg trajectories per donor.
- H2: Mpos_Inhibitor vs Mpos reverses ≥50% of the Mpos-specific signature in ≥2/3 donors; Mneg_Inhibitor vs Mneg shows minimal change.
- H3: EGFR/TLR4 inhibitors shift perivascular→repair balance in the predicted direction in ≥2 patients.

**Adverse:**
- H1: Mpos and Mneg induce indistinguishable FLS states (high gene-set overlap, no pathway-score divergence) → the MerTKpos/Mneg distinction does not translate to differential FLS programming in this system.
- H2: MerTK inhibitor does not specifically ablate the Mpos-specific signal (Mpos_Inhibitor ≈ Mpos, or Mneg_Inhibitor shows comparable changes) → the Mpos-specific response is MerTK-independent.
- H3: Drug effects on NOTCH/EGFR scores are uncorrelated with fibroblast phenotype scores → cross-dataset pathway coherence is weak.

**Inconclusive:**
- H1: Partial separation (some Mpos-specific and Mneg-specific genes, but substantial overlap or inconsistent direction across donors).
- H2: Partial reversal in 1/3 Mpos_Inhibitor donors, or n=1 Mneg_Inhibitor pair inconsistent.
- H3: Weak/inconsistent drug–phenotype associations across patients.

**Not established by this analysis:** direct causal mechanism (computational analysis of existing data), in vivo relevance (in vitro coculture), inhibitor specificity (single compound, no genetic knockout), and clinical translation (requires expert adjudication and additional studies).
