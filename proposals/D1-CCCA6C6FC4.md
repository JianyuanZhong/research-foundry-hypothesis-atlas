## Hypothesis to test

Patients whose creatinine falls without improvement in urine output or fluid status remain at high risk of subsequent adverse outcomes.

## Study population and main data

Patients with acute kidney injury; creatinine, urine output, and fluid balance.

## Suggested approach

Assess recovery at a fixed time and analyze subsequent death and kidney events.

## Scientific significance

Improve on assessments of recovery based only on creatinine.

## Main limitations

Without measured kidney function, the analysis can identify only suspected discordant recovery.
