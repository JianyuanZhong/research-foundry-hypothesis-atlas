# Ascertainment-triangulated transport audit for frozen HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B prediction

## Targeted question and advance

This proposal is a compatible successor to `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE cohort, event reconstruction, B/P/event/Y clocks, non-overlapping annual forward blocks, locked ridge models, and noncausal standardized held-out RMSE estimand. It does not add a treatment estimand, alter eligibility, pool assays, or turn a recorded order into an administered treatment.

The predecessor's strongest supported claim is narrow: in the selected complete-triplet population, the 2022 forward block is adequately supported and contemporaneous P may improve short-horizon prediction of albumin and bilirubin beyond B and the locked core. The executed audit also shows 2023 and 2024 outcome observation collapse (29/43 and 14/70 BP-eligible episodes with Y, respectively), with very sparse systemic arms. Consequently, later favorable complete-triplet estimates cannot establish calendar transport.

The unresolved, falsifiable question is whether later missing Y reflects a measurable failure of post-event assessment opportunity/capture, rather than merely random laboratory nonrecording, and whether the frozen P-beyond-B contrast survives a transparent bounded completion of missing Y. The substantive advance is an ascertainment triangulation that separates three observable stages without claiming any of them is clinical follow-up: (i) an encounter/examination opportunity signal, (ii) a linked clinical-document presence signal, and (iii) exact assay observation under the frozen Y clock. It then reports a training-range partial-identification bound for the same predictive contrast. This localizes calendar weakness and tests selection sensitivity while avoiding MAR, causal, clinical-utility, or patient-benefit assumptions.

The design is inspired only at the level of research ambition by the inspected demonstrations: temporal external validation and explicit handling of selection are important, but the HCC sources lack the multimodal, validated outcomes, and external cohorts used in those papers. No claim is made that the HCC data reproduce their models or endpoints.

## Frozen cohort, clocks, model, and estimand

For every selected event, preserve exactly:

- `B`: latest valid uncensored assay on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1.
- `P`: latest valid assay in the selected repeat encounter in `[event_time−72 hours,event_time)`.
- `Y`: valid assay in that encounter in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, nearest +24 hours with the inherited deterministic tie rule.
- Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time`, positive P lead, one patient-assay row, and no lab-informed event selection.

Use origins `2021-01-01`, `2022-01-01`, `2023-01-01`, and `2024-01-01`; training events satisfy `event_date < origin`, and each future block is `[origin, origin+365 days)`. Preserve all parent preprocessing and fitting: age median and missingness indicator learned in training, sex indicators, variance filtering, training centering/scaling, inner leave-one-patient-out alpha selection over `[0.01,0.1,1,10,100]`, and ridge fitting.

For each assay retain:

```
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
```

The primary estimand remains observed complete-triplet standardized held-out RMSE gain `[RMSE(M0)-RMSE(M1)]/SD_test(Y)`, with paired arm-stratified patient-frequency bootstrap conditional on fixed pre-origin fits. No ascertainment variable, bound, weight, or missing-Y completion may change the primary estimator.

## Ascertainment-triangulation layer

Construct the outcome-blind risk set before reading Y: selected event -> B available -> P available -> `BP_eligible`, using only event timing and pre-Y data. For every BP-eligible episode and assay, create aggregate-only indicators:

- `exam_opportunity`: at least one `examinations` row for the same patient and selected repeat encounter with valid `开始时间` in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`.
- `encounter_followup`: at least one additional `encounters` row for the patient with valid `就诊时间` in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`; do not use it to redefine the selected encounter or Y.
- `document_presence`: nonempty linked `clinical_documents` row for the selected repeat encounter. Because documents have no timestamp, label this an encounter-level proxy.
- `Y_observed`: a valid assay-specific Y under the frozen lab clock.

Report the funnel and Y-observation fractions overall and by systemic/comparator arm, calendar block, and each mutually exclusive ascertainment pattern. The mutually exclusive pattern order is: exam plus follow-up encounter, exam without follow-up encounter, follow-up encounter without exam, document-only proxy, and none; document presence can also be reported as a separate cross-tab because it is not time-resolved. Suppress or mark non-estimable any cell with fewer than 5 episodes; never infer a mechanism from a small cell.

Fit, separately within each training origin, a prespecified logistic model for `Y_observed` among BP-eligible episodes using only B, P, age, sex, P-lead, TACE2-to-repeat days, arm, calendar block, and the three ascertainment indicators. The ascertainment indicators are measured before or independently of reading Y; the document indicator is explicitly a proxy. Apply the frozen model to future BP-eligible episodes. Report calibration-free descriptive quantities only: observed fraction with Wilson interval, predicted probability range, Brier score, and inverse-observation-probability ESS among observed cases. Do not use these probabilities for primary reweighting, imputation, or exclusion, and do not call the model MAR evidence.

The calendar transport diagnostic remains outcome-blind: among BP-eligible episodes, fit training-only calendar-membership propensity using B, P, age, sex, P-lead, timing, and arm, but not Y or ascertainment indicators. Report future propensity outside the training range, normalized transport ESS overall and by arm, maximum normalized weight, and same-arm nearest-neighbor extrapolation. These weights are diagnostics only.

## Bounded missing-Y stress test

For each assay and origin, fit M0/M1 only on pre-origin observed complete triplets, freeze predictions, and retain all future BP-eligible rows. Define `L_train=min(Y_train)` and `U_train=max(Y_train)` from training observed Y only. If the training range is degenerate, or any later observed Y falls outside `[L_train,U_train]`, label the bound unsupported and do not silently widen it. A full-source assay range may be shown only as a non-temporal descriptive check.

For observed future Y, use `e_j=(Y-p_j)^2`. For missing Y in `[L_train,U_train]`, bound each model's squared error by the convex endpoint formula: minimum at `clip(p_j,[L_train,U_train])`, maximum at one of L/U. Bound the direct M1-minus-M0 contrast because its per-row difference is affine in unknown Y. Report overall and arm-specific intervals for all BP-eligible episodes, the unchanged observed complete-triplet RMSE gain, missing counts/fractions, and three locked endpoint patterns: all missing at L, all at U, and the per-row least-favorable endpoint for M1 versus M0.

Compute a deterministic tipping point for a common displacement of all missing Y values from the observed-case empirical mean, clipped to `[L_train,U_train]`; if no root exists, report sign-robust under this mathematical scenario; if the interval contains zero, report selection-sensitive/unidentified. Do not call any endpoint a plausible imputation, and do not standardize a missing-Y primary gain by test SD. Use training SD only for an optional secondary display.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires reconstruction/clock assertions, both outcome-blind and complete-triplet support gates, an adequately supported block, the parent RMSE criterion, no material arm-specific ascertainment imbalance, and a bounded all-BP-eligible contrast that remains favorable throughout the pre-origin range. This supports only a selected short-horizon prognostic association in that calendar mixture under the explicit bound, not transport or benefit.

Adverse evidence is a nonpositive locked contrast in an adequately supported block, repeated M1 degradation, a bounded sign reversal, or a strong ascertainment gradient in which Y observation is concentrated in one opportunity pattern. This challenges the incremental prediction interpretation or demonstrates selection sensitivity; it does not prove B is sufficient or identify a mechanism.

Selection-sensitive evidence is a favorable observed complete-triplet result with a bounded interval crossing zero, a tipping point near the support limits, or large Y-observation differences across measured opportunity patterns. Inconclusive evidence includes failed arm gates, unsupported ranges, low observation ESS, outside-calendar support, influential episodes, or non-estimable small cells. Preserve the parent's interpretation: 2022 may remain supportive locally; 2023 and 2024 remain inconclusive for transport, especially the 2024 systemic arm, unless an analysis genuinely passes all prespecified gates without changing the estimand.

The computation can verify source hashes, full scans, keys, duplicate handling, event reconstruction, exact clocks, annual boundaries, outcome-blind eligibility, opportunity indicators, bounded arithmetic, and aggregate row accounting. It cannot establish that an examination was clinically indicated or completed, assay units/reference ranges, disease severity, imaging response, medication administration, external-care capture, mortality, hepatic failure, treatment effect, safety, utility, actionability, or patient benefit. Those conclusions require adjudicated clinical outcomes, validated assay metadata, reliable exposure and external-care capture, expert review of records, and an independent temporal or prospective cohort.

## Compiler and verifier requirements

The compiler must preserve the parent pipeline byte-for-byte in scientific behavior and add only the aggregate ascertainment indicators, outcome-blind calendar diagnostics, and training-range missing-Y bound layer. Required inputs are the seven frozen HCC CSV sources above; outputs are aggregate JSON/CSV with source paths/hashes, denominator assertions, support/selection flags, opportunity-pattern counts, bounds, tipping-point status, and branch labels. Do not emit identifiers, raw lab values, clinical text, or notes.

The verifier must deliberately test that: a favorable observed-case estimate with a bound crossing zero is selection-sensitive; a favorable later estimate with failed arm support is descriptive only; an opportunity indicator is not treated as clinical follow-up; document presence is not assigned a false timestamp; endpoint bounds do not alter primary complete-triplet RMSE; and a bounded robust sign is not upgraded to multi-era transport, causality, clinical utility, or benefit. The verifier may establish computation and interpretation consistency, but not the unavailable clinical claims.
