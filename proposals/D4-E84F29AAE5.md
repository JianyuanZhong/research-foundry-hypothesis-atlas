## Clinical question and substantive advance

The strongest available evidence supports that a UKB paired-panel experiment is feasible in principle: the frozen catalog contains two assessment dates, two instances of eight numeric panel fields, age/sex fields, and dated outcome-code arrays that can be joined horizontally by eid. The local evidence does not establish what the eight fields measure, their units, specimen timing, assay quality, disease onset, or complete outcome ascertainment.

The unresolved claim is decision-facing and falsifiable:

> In participants whose current-plus-spacing model places them in a prespecified intermediate current-risk band at the repeat assessment, does adding the prior panel produce a calibrated, practically meaningful target-risk revision, and is that revision consistent across landmark-age groups and T1 calendar eras (2012 versus 2013)?

The substantive advance over the parent is a different estimand. The parent estimates an age contrast in held-out Brier improvement. This proposal estimates the magnitude and stability of the risk change communicated to an intermediate-risk person when prior history is added, including whether the change is transportable across observed repeat-assessment eras. This can reveal clinically meaningful heterogeneity hidden by an overall average error reduction, while making no claim that the illustrative bands are treatment thresholds.

The current-risk band is defined by the held-out, fold-local calibrated target probability from the current-plus-spacing model:
0.025 <= p0* < 0.10.
The primary extreme cells are landmark age <55 versus >=65 crossed with T1 calendar year 2012 versus 2013; age 55-64 and any other supported T1 year are descriptive sensitivities. The direction of risk revision is not assumed.

## Evidence boundary

Approved local metadata supports 21022-0.0 as age at recruitment in years and 31-0.0 as sex coding (0 female, 1 male). The field-instance convention explicitly says instance index is not elapsed time; local per-field labels, units and missing-value meanings are generally absent. The assessment-date relationship and biomarker specimen ordering must be verified before deriving time.

The strongest evidence therefore supports exact field availability and a bounded paired-observation design, not biomarker meaning or clinical utility. The experiment must fail closed if it cannot verify:

1. age unit/coding, overlapping-field agreement, and valid numeric ranges;
2. that 53-0.0 and 53-1.0 are comparable assessment dates;
3. labels, units, valid ranges and missing-value codes for all sixteen panel cells;
4. outcome code system, target-domain mapping, same-position code/date pairing, and 40000 death-date semantics;
5. observation end/complete ascertainment through five years after T1.

A bounded first-100,000-row source audit found all required headers and 1,351 rows meeting date/age/sex, 0.25 < L <= 5 years and all-sixteen-cell completeness checks, including 627 T1=2012 and 724 T1=2013 observations. These are feasibility counts only, not the final cohort or event count.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Define T0=53-0.0 and T1=53-1.0 only after the comparable-date gate. Let L=(T1-T0)/365.2425. Include unique participants with valid age/sex, 0.25 < L <= 5 years, numeric dictionary-valid values in all sixteen panel cells, valid T1 current panel and valid A_T1 = 21022-0.0 + L. This is an age-at-landmark proxy, not date of birth.

Exclude only a verified qualifying target record on or before T1; never call participants disease-free. For the primary recorded five-year horizon require T1 <= 2013-12-31. Define the endpoint as the earliest qualifying target, death, or no qualifying recorded category strictly after T1 through T1+5 years. Pair each outcome code with the date at the same position; do not sort code and date arrays independently, and apply predeclared death precedence for same-date events. The target requires a predeclared mapping and at least two distinct verified clinical domains. If complete ascertainment is unavailable, a recorded-category analysis may proceed only without incidence/censoring or disease-free language. If biomarker, assessment or outcome semantics fail, do not fit the hypothesis.

## Models, decomposition and estimands

Use a deterministic participant-level five-fold hash split on eid. All preprocessing, model selection, calibration and weights are fold-local.

- M0: current position-1 panel, recruitment age, sex and T0 calendar year.
- M1a: M0 plus verified spacing L (the current-versus-spacing baseline).
- M1b: M1a plus all eight position-0 panel values, with prespecified prior-minus-current contrasts.
- M2: a bounded gradient-boosted multinomial probability model using the M1b input set.
- M2-no-prior: matched M2 using M1a inputs.

Fit fold-local calibration for M1a and M1b and call their target probabilities p0* and p1*. Define the primary current-risk band by held-out p0*: 2.5% <= p0* < 10%. The primary estimand in cell (g,e) is:
R_g,e = E_w[p1* - p0* | current-risk band, landmark-age g, T1-era e].

The four extreme cells are g in {A_T1<55, A_T1>=65} and e in {T1=2012, T1=2013}; 55-64 and other supported years are descriptive sensitivities. The primary hypothesis is a conjunctive transport/value claim: each extreme-cell R_g,e has absolute magnitude at least 0.010, all four have the same sign, and their maximum pairwise absolute difference is less than 0.010. The direction is estimated rather than assumed. Report raw and calibrated revisions, M0-to-M1a and M1a-to-M1b changes, 0.05-0.10 and 0.01-0.15 band sensitivities, continuous age/era interaction surfaces, calibration intercept/slope, reliability/resolution, log loss, and illustrative 5%/10% boundary crossing. These thresholds are descriptive and not validated clinical utilities.

## Selection, era overlap and uncertainty

Fit repeat-observation propensity among T0-complete participants using only T0 panel, recruitment age, sex and T0 year. Use stabilized inverse-probability weights, truncating at the fold-local 1st/99th percentiles. Fit T1-era overlap weights inside each outer fold from current panel, L, age, sex, T0 year, repeat propensity and training-fold baseline risk; normalize within era. Report unweighted, selection-weighted and era-overlap-weighted cell estimates, balance, maximum weights and ESS. Do not use held-out outcomes, p1* or observed threshold movement in weight fitting. Fail the transport claim as inconclusive if positivity, band support or ESS is poor; do not extrapolate.

Use paired participant bootstrap over the entire cross-fitted prediction/weight pipeline, simultaneous intervals for the four-cell range and calibration metrics, and fold-stability summaries. Require at least 200 eligible participants and 30 target events per extreme cell for a claim of stability. The future solver must publish gate counts, band flow, predictions, weights, calibration arithmetic and bootstrap manifests.

## Falsification and interpretation

Permute endpoint labels within fold and repeat-selection strata; calibrated prior revisions and age/era contrasts should collapse. Permute prior panels within age, era, current-risk, interval, missingness and selection strata while preserving current panel and spacing; M1b’s increment should collapse toward M1a. Recompute with deliberately broken code/date pairing to verify that the integrity audit detects it, and compare complete-case with prespecified fold-local missingness handling only if dictionary-valid missingness is available. Compare additive M1b and M2, unweighted and weighted analyses, landmark and recruitment age, and T1-era definitions.

Supportive evidence requires all semantic/timing/ascertainment gates, at least 100 overall target events, minimum cell support, adequate selection and era overlap, no integrity failure, same-sign non-negligible calibrated revisions in all four extreme cells, and a four-cell range below 0.010 with uncertainty that is compatible with the equivalence margin. Stronger support requires calibration and M2 sensitivity agreement. Adverse evidence is near-zero revision, opposite signs, a range at or above 0.010, disappearance after calibration/weighting, or an effect explained by spacing. Inconclusive evidence includes failed semantic or ascertainment gates, sparse events/cells, poor overlap/ESS, unstable fold or bootstrap estimates, broken pairing, or unresolved T1 date/age semantics. Inconclusive is not evidence against the hypothesis.

## Clinical limits and additional evidence required

The data cannot by themselves establish the analyte identities, assay units, specimen collection order relative to assessment, assay quality, disease onset, complete capture of events, clinically selected action thresholds, treatment benefit, causal effect of repeat testing, optimal retesting schedule, mechanism, or transport outside this selected paired-observation support. Clinical adjudication and field dictionaries are required for biomarker/domain mapping; outcome adjudication and an independently verified observation window are required for incidence-like interpretation; external validation is required before deployment. A supportive result remains a conditional recorded-outcome prognostic-information result in a selected UKB repeat-observation cohort.
