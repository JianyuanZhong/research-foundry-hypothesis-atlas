# Cross-table temporal concordance of frozen eICU care-plan landmarks with literal RT Vent On/Off sequences

## Substantive child, unresolved question, and importance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the exact frozen 540 person-landmarks, 443 unique first qualifying stays/persons, 74 hospitals, five landmarks (1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200 minutes), literal care-plan labels, first-label rule, decision time D, exclusions, and completed parent findings. It also preserves the RT parent's full-source literal extraction, exact-record deduplication followed by deterministic earliest-anchored 30-minute clustering, separate Off and Suspended states, fixed ICU observation boundary, and prohibition against bedside ventilation or extubation interpretations.

The strongest inherited claim is that the two care-plan labels are hospital-dependent documentation phenotypes and that the literal RT values Off and Suspended encode different respiratory-charting workflows in their own event-indexed population. Neither result establishes actual spontaneous-breathing-trial delivery, extubation evaluation, ventilator discontinuation, extubation, liberation, quality, benefit, or causality. The unresolved hypothesis tested here was narrower and cross-table: after D, do `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` have enough literal Start, Off, and Suspended coverage to exhibit different first events or clean sequences within 6, 24, or 48 hours, and is any difference reproducible under observable follow-up, prior-history stratification, hospital common support, leave-one-site-out analysis, and repeated-landmark clustering?

This question is consequential because a structured care-plan label could appear operationally meaningful without concordance to even the same database's literal RT transition stream. The executed result is a falsification/measurement result: literal transition coverage is far too sparse for the proposed cross-table comparison. The setting-family trajectory remains computable but is adverse to a stable common-site distinction.

## Preserved RT extraction and temporal experiment

The full 20,168,176-row respiratory scan reproduced 4,120 raw decisive records, 4,120 exact-unique decisive records, and 4,094 deterministic anchored clusters: 2,711 Start, 1,007 Off, 366 Suspended, 9 mixed Start/off-direction conflicts, and 1 mixed Off/Suspended ambiguous cluster. Exact tuples are deduplicated before clustering. Each cluster begins at its earliest record and includes records through anchor plus 30 minutes. Conflicts and the ambiguous Off/Suspended cluster are excluded from clean outcomes; Off and Suspended are never pooled.

For each frozen landmark, prior history is any clean cluster with anchor `<D`. A cluster at exactly D is reported separately and belongs to neither prior nor post-D; none occurred. The first post-D event is the earliest clean anchor satisfying `D < event <= unitdischargeoffset`. Fixed horizons are D+360, D+1440, and D+2880 minutes. Complete structural follow-up requires `D+h <= unitdischargeoffset`; ICU discharge never counts as success. Generic respiratory-chart support in every consecutive six-hour bin is a separate sensitivity and does not redefine structural completeness.

The literal endpoints are first/any Off, Suspended, or Start and adjacent Start-to-Off, Off-to-Start, Off-to-Suspended, and Suspended-to-Off sequences within each fixed horizon. The fixed setting partitions are `[D,D+360)`, `[D+360,D+1440)`, and `[D+1440,D+2880)`. Repeated landmarks are correlated; 76 stays contribute more than one landmark (173 rows), so uncertainty is stay-clustered, with hospital-clustered uncertainty separately. Hospital common support requires at least five complete landmarks per label; site-specific contrasts, equal-hospital summaries, and leave-one-hospital-out estimates are reported.

The prespecified literal-event sufficiency gate requires at least 20 complete-case events overall, at least 5 per label, and at least 3 hospitals with at least 5 complete rows per label and at least one event in each label. Failure stops adjusted, bootstrap, or semantic interpretation for that endpoint.

## Executed event coverage, history, and truncation

Only 18/540 landmarks had any clean literal RT history before D: 12/309 evaluation (3.88%) and 6/231 no-trial (2.60%). Latest prior states were Start for 17 and Suspended for 1; no latest prior Off was present. This severe lack of prior literal-state coverage precludes meaningful conditional sequence modeling.

Structural complete follow-up was high but generic chart support differed by label:

- 6 hours: 309/309 evaluation and 230/231 no-trial structurally complete; generic every-six-hour support 275/309 (89.0%) and 227/230 (98.7%). No complete landmark had any clean Start, Off, or Suspended event.
- 24 hours: 293/309 evaluation and 219/231 no-trial complete; generic support 231/293 (78.8%) and 206/219 (94.1%). Only three complete evaluation landmarks had a first event (2 Off, 1 Suspended); no complete no-trial landmark did.
- 48 hours: 252/309 evaluation and 194/231 no-trial complete; generic support 174/252 (69.0%) and 173/194 (89.2%). The same three complete evaluation events and no no-trial event were present.

Across all landmarks observed until the earlier of each horizon or ICU discharge, there were no clean events by 6 hours; by 24 hours there were 3 evaluation events versus 1 no-trial Off in a landmark truncated before 24 hours; by 48 hours there were 4 evaluation events (3 Off, 1 Suspended) versus 1 no-trial Off, with one evaluation Off and the no-trial Off outside complete 48-hour rows. Across the entire remaining ICU stay, only 8/540 landmarks ever had a clean post-D event. No landmark had post-D Start within 48 hours and no requested clean sequence occurred. Thus every Off, Suspended, Start, and sequence endpoint failed the sufficiency gate. Zero observed events do not imply absence of a bedside action; they document lack of this literal field.

## Fixed-bin setting trajectory and hospital support

The setting-family outcome was common but did not yield a stable positive daily-evaluation distinction. Among structurally complete rows:

- 0-6 hours: evaluation 249/309 = 80.6%, no-trial 214/230 = 93.0%, unstandardized evaluation-minus-no-trial RD -12.46 points. Stay-bootstrap 95% -19.03 to -7.05 and hospital-bootstrap -23.49 to -4.49 (300 bounded replicates).
- 6-24 hours: 234/293 = 79.9% versus 199/219 = 90.9%, RD -11.00 points. Stay-bootstrap -16.95 to -4.77 and hospital-bootstrap -21.60 to -3.30.
- 24-48 hours: 188/252 = 74.6% versus 166/194 = 85.6%, RD -10.96 points. Stay-bootstrap -18.41 to -3.69 and hospital-bootstrap -19.39 to -1.38.

These pooled differences are strongly exposed to inherited hospital-label imbalance. Only hospitals 248, 252, 417, and 420 had at least five complete rows per label at all horizons. Equal-hospital RDs were -2.72 points at 0-6 hours, +1.21 at 6-24 hours, and -4.36 at 24-48 hours. Site direction was mixed at every horizon. At 0-6 hours, leaving out hospital 417 made the equal-site estimate negative, while omitting hospitals 248, 252, or 420 left a value near zero or slightly positive. At 6-24 hours, site RDs ranged from -8.82 to +12.86 points and leave-one-site-out estimates ranged from -2.67 to +4.56. At 24-48 hours, site RDs ranged from -13.62 to +4.73 points and all leave-one-site-out estimates were negative (-7.39 to -1.28 points), but magnitudes remained small and only four sites supported comparison.

Prior-history conditioning does not repair the finding. In the dominant no-prior-history stratum, evaluation-minus-no-trial setting RDs were -12.39, -10.58, and -10.82 points across the three bins. The prior-Start stratum comprised only 17 landmarks and is too sparse for stable inference.

Hospital concentration remained asymmetric: all evaluation rows spanned 67 hospitals with HHI 0.078 and top-site share 19.1%; no-trial rows spanned 25 hospitals with HHI 0.245 and top-site share 41.6%. At 48 hours, the no-trial top-three share was 77.8%. This is adverse to broad hospital transportability.

## Falsification verdict and strongest supported claim

1. **Literal cross-table event concordance: inconclusive because of extreme sparsity.** No literal event endpoint passed the prespecified gate. There is no estimable comparative first-Off, Suspended, Start, or Start/Off sequence effect. The isolated 3 versus 0 complete-case 24/48-hour events cannot support a label contrast.
2. **Structured setting trajectory: adverse to a reproducible common-site care-plan distinction.** Pooled rates were lower after the evaluation label, but equal-hospital estimates were near zero or modestly negative, site directions were mixed, and only four common-support hospitals were available. This does not reproduce a stable positive operational trajectory and is compatible with hospital workflow, chart-density, and population differences.
3. **Prior-history adjustment: infeasible.** Only 18 landmarks had prior literal history and no latest prior Off existed.
4. **Observation: structurally adequate for enumeration, but documentation support is differential and declines with horizon.** ICU-boundary completeness was 99.8%, 94.8%, and 82.6% overall at 6, 24, and 48 hours; generic support was consistently lower for evaluation landmarks. Absence of literal events is therefore a documentation-coverage finding, not a clinical-state finding.
5. **Hospital transportability: adverse.** Label overlap and no-trial concentration restrict inference to four adequately supported hospitals; no unobserved-hospital generalization is justified.
6. **Clinical semantics: unresolved and not computationally testable here.** Independent timestamped device connection/disconnection, invasive versus noninvasive mode, airway insertion/removal, actual SBT and extubation attempts, extubation/reintubation, oxygen support, narrative context, and blinded adjudication are unavailable.

The strongest supported claim is that the exact frozen care-plan labels have almost no temporal coverage in the literal `RT Vent On/Off` Start/Off/Suspended stream: only 18 landmarks have prior history, no clean event occurs within 6 hours, only three complete evaluation landmarks and no complete no-trial landmark have any event within 24 or 48 hours, and no post-D Start or clean sequence is observed. This falsifies use of that literal stream as a comparative cross-table validation endpoint in the frozen cohort. It does not falsify actual bedside evaluation, trial, discontinuation, or extubation.

Supportive evidence would have required passing event gates with a consistent label contrast across common-support hospitals and stable leave-one-site-out estimates. Adverse evidence would be a well-supported reversal or reproducible lack of concordance despite adequate literal-event capture. The observed result is principally inconclusive from event sparsity, with adverse hospital and setting-trajectory transportability. Stronger clinical conclusions require a separate validation study with independent timestamped device and airway evidence, local workflow metadata, narrative review, and expert adjudication sampled across hospitals, labels, prior states, and discordant cases.
