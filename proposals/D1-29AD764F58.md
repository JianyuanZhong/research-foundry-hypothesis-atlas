# Sharp patient-balanced, version-faithful, mask-anchored visibility frontier for documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Targeted successor

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid [prior hypothesis]. It changes one scientific object: the full-roster visibility constraint. Every accession still remains an indivisible, exactly identified masking unit, but each report-bearing patient contributes total denominator mass one regardless of how many eligible CT/MRI accessions that patient has.

Everything else is frozen: the adult first-eligible source-documented HCC-resection population; coherent episode, operation-clock and prior-treatment states; primary 24-hour preoperative cutoff and 12/48/72-hour diagnostics; 90-day CT/MRI acquisition window; possibility quarantine; 2015-2018 development, 2019 locking and separate 2020/2021 tests; encounter-documentary M2 outcome; whole-accession V/A/E objects; six immutable corpus-wide reader books and nine global book pairs; B, R_clean, G_clean and same-fit Gclean_mask; patient-balanced outcome-blind fitting; fixed historical K; three comparator-closed contrasts; strict greater-than-five capture margin; exact outer extrema; exact whole-input version identity; and one full-roster mask restricted without replacement after singleton deletion.

The parent counts every releasable accession equally. A patient with six accessions therefore supplies six times the coverage mass of a patient with one although review, K-slot allocation, outcome and deletion are patient-level. The repair preserves partial visibility but first computes a fraction inside each patient's packet and then averages those fractions equally across patients.

The unresolved primary hypothesis is:

> Conditional on every inherited all-content gate passing, every full-cohort version-faithful mask with patient-balanced effective visibility at least 0.80 preserves all six 2020/2021 comparator margins strictly above five, before deletion and after removal of any one whole test patient, in every coherent source/pathology/reader world, while every surviving accession bit remains unchanged.

The confirmatory test is directly at 0.80. A mask at exactly 0.80 whose margin is at most five falsifies the claim. The sharp adverse frontier b_PB is the greatest patient-balanced coverage attained by any failing undeleted or anchored-singleton witness. Support is equivalent to b_PB<0.80, or to no failing mask at any coverage. This avoids incorrectly calling an attained failing boundary a “smallest sufficient” continuous threshold.

## 5. Primary patient-balanced effective visibility

For test year a and full coherent world omega, let E_a(omega) be all eligible patients. Let A_pa(omega) be patient p's source-clean, releasable, decision-eligible CT/MRI whole accessions under the parent's rules, n_p=|A_p|, and P+_a(omega)={p in E:n_p>0}. Patients with n_p=0 remain in ranking, outcome and deletion calculations but have no counterfactual release bit.

For each u in A_p, r_u is the parent's indivisible effective-visibility bit. Define:

q_p(r) = (sum over u in A_p of r_u) / n_p.

C_PB(r;omega) = (sum over p in P+ of q_p(r)) / |P+|
              = (sum over p in P+ and u in A_p of r_u/n_p) / |P+|.

Each report-bearing patient has total mass one. One of one visible contributes 1; one of two contributes 1/2; one of six contributes 1/6. No component is partially visible.

P+ rather than all E is primary because this frontier isolates visibility among potentially releasable report evidence, preserving the parent's source-clean denominator. Mandatory companion C_roster=(|P+|/|E|)C_PB assigns n_p=0 patients zero and prevents a conditional 80% result from being misdescribed as 80% of all review patients. Report |E|, |P+|, |E-P+| and their world/year variation. If P+=0 in any required world, the primary is inconclusive.

For c in [0,1], the full-roster family is:

R_PB,c(omega) = {whole-accession masks r: C_PB(r;omega)>=c}.

World, A_p, n_p and weights are fixed before ranks, outcomes and mask optimization. They cannot depend on model arm, Y, score or top-K status.

Mandatory paired diagnostics are legacy accession coverage C_A=(sum r_u)/(sum n_p), patient-any C_any=mean over P+ of 1[q_p>0], patient-complete C_all=mean over P+ of 1[q_p=1], C_roster and the full n_p distribution. None substitutes for C_PB.

Singleton invariance is exact: if n_p=1 for every p in P+, then C_PB=C_A=C_any=C_all for every mask, and this child must reproduce the parent's mask family and margins bit-for-bit.

## 7. Sharp frontier, exact optimization and monotonicity

Let D_e(a,omega,r,j) be a coordinate's exact capture margin, where j is none or one eligible deleted patient and deletion uses the anchored mask. A failure satisfies D_e<=5.

Define b_PB as the maximum exact C_PB(r;omega) over every coherent world, global book pair, one of six coordinates, full-roster mask and undeleted/deleted j that yields failure. Record NONE if there is no failing witness at any coverage. All-content failure gives b_PB=1 and cannot be rescued by partial visibility.

At c, the lower envelope L_e(c) minimizes D_e over worlds, masks with C_PB>=c and anchored deletions. Since R_PB,c2 is a subset of R_PB,c1 for c2>c1, L_e(c) must be nondecreasing. A particular margin may worsen when one more report is exposed; monotonicity is required only of the universal lower envelope created by nested feasible sets.

The 0.80 hypothesis passes iff all inherited all-content gates pass and no failing mask has C_PB>=0.80, equivalently b_PB<0.80 or b_PB=NONE. Equality fails. Report the maximum failing mask and nearest higher passing attainable coverage, but do not call the latter a unique continuous minimum.

Use exact rational coefficients 1/n_p. For every fixed world/book/coordinate/deletion, a certified MIP or equivalent enforces exact K rankings in both arms and maximizes rational C_PB subject to the integer failure inequality 100(captures_G-captures_comparator)<=5K. Enumerate all finite worlds, books, coordinates and deletions and take the exact maximum. Require zero integer gap, exact rational replay, residual <=1e-8, complete rank-inversion separation, all tied maximizers, raw/quotient replay and brute-force agreement on fixtures through 12 patients and real shards through 20. Timeout, state cap, sampled mask, favorable incumbent, omitted world/book/deletion or failed replay is inconclusive.

Fixtures must include: all n_p=1; one six-accession patient versus six singleton patients; equal C_A but unequal C_PB; equal C_PB but unequal C_any/C_all; an individual-mask nonmonotonicity example with monotone L_e; and deletion of a visible singleton where survivor coverage falls but survivor bits do not change.

## 8. Denominator and modality stress tests

These are prespecified diagnostics and cannot rescue the primary:

1. Recompute b_A under legacy C_A. At 0.80 report exact paired masks and whether C_A and C_PB classifications agree. Disagreement is denominator-sensitive, never averaged away.
2. Compute q_min=min_p q_p, a stringent every-patient packet frontier. It answers a different operational question and cannot replace the equal-patient mean.
3. Compute patient-any and patient-complete frontiers to show whether partial credit drives the result.
4. Report C_roster and P0 burden. A conditional C_PB pass cannot be called population coverage unless the all-roster quantity independently supports that wording.
5. Freeze each whole accession as CT, MRI or unresolved using all components and the inherited modality dictionary before outcomes. A conflicting bundle remains one accession and a coherent modality/source state; never duplicate it across CT and MRI.
6. For CT-only, MRI-only and mixed patients report n_p, n_p,CT, n_p,MRI, q_p, q_p,CT and q_p,MRI distributions and group C_PB.
7. Recompute a modality-balanced sensitivity C_MB=mean_p [mean over modalities present for p of q_pm], and exact 0.80 diagnostics with simultaneous CT- and MRI-specific patient-balanced constraints where strata exist. Equal modality weighting is sensitivity only because HCC provides no clinically validated CT-versus-MRI information weights.

Any C_PB/C_MB or modality-pattern reversal is modality-sensitive and blocks a generic CT/MRI operational statement pending clinical review. Sparse or absent strata are reported, not imputed.

## 9. Falsification and interpretation

Use one primary label:

1. inconclusive: any source/header/hash, population, chronology, form/book, N/K/event, leakage, denominator, version/mask, rational replay or exact-solve gate fails;
2. version-faithful patient-balanced mask-anchored supportive: all all-content gates pass and b_PB<0.80 or b_PB=NONE;
3. all-content supportive but patient-balanced visibility-brittle: all content passes but b_PB>=0.80;
4. semantic-evidence-only: both years' Delta_R and Delta_mask pass all-content and anchored-singleton gates but a Delta_B coordinate fails;
5. quota-policy-only: both Delta_B years pass but not all semantic coordinates pass;
6. falsified-localized: gates pass and none above applies; report year, comparator, world, book, C_PB, n_p pattern, mask and deletion witness. A coordinate is all-world adverse only if its upper endpoint is <=5.

Attach non-one-hot denominator-sensitive, modality-sensitive and high-P0 descriptors; they cannot convert adverse or inconclusive to supportive.

Support means only that no allowed full-roster placement with at least 80% equal-patient fractional visibility erased the frozen documentary-M2 capture margins before or after anchored singleton deletion. Brittle/adverse means such a witness exists, not that semantics are useless or reports late. Inconclusive means the evidence or computation cannot resolve the question. Negative results prevent an accession average from licensing an unsuitable audit.
