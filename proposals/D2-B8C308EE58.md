# Context-specific intervention response: working proposal

## Question
Does the intervention-associated transcript response in fibroblast–macrophage systems depend on the cellular context, and can that dependence identify a target whose modulation is more likely to alter a pathogenic fibroblast program than a protective macrophage-linked state?

## Current evidence status
This is a hypothesis-stage proposal, not an executed result. The seed D2-T1 names GSE95588 and E-MTAB-8316 as inputs and requires donor-aware matched contrasts and controls. The domain guide confirms that GSE95588 has prepared count matrices and design metadata, and E-MTAB-8316 has four FLS donor clusters, six nested monocyte donors, and twelve condition samples. Repeated context measurements must be averaged within donor/condition before paired exploratory inference. No twelve-independent-donor claim is permitted.

## Proposed estimand
A donor-aware contrast comparing intervention-associated transcript changes in macrophage–fibroblast coculture versus fibroblast-only or vehicle/unstimulated controls, where available. The primary estimand is the difference in mean log2 fold-change (or a comparable effect size) for candidate target-associated transcripts between contexts, averaged within donor and condition.

## Competing explanations
1. The observed context dependence reflects donor composition or batch effects rather than a biological interaction.
2. The intervention response is driven by off-target effects or medication-specific artifacts.
3. The transcript response does not reflect direct target action or functional change.
4. The coculture assay captures a mixed-cell signal that cannot be attributed to a single cell type without cell-specific labeling.

## Falsifiable test
If the intervention-associated transcript response is context-dependent, the effect size for candidate target-associated transcripts should differ significantly between macrophage–fibroblast coculture and fibroblast-only/vehicle controls after donor-aware adjustment. A null or reversed contrast would falsify the specific context-dependence claim.

## Method comparison (pending)
A simple donor-aware linear model baseline and a mechanistic or learned alternative (e.g., a small neural model or pathway-level simulation) should be compared on the same question. The baseline estimates context-specific effect sizes with donor fixed effects. The alternative could reveal nonlinear context interactions or pathway-level dynamics that the linear model loses. Resource estimates and selection criteria are pending.
