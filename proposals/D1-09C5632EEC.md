# Provisional HCC hypothesis (episode 2 repair)

## Proposed relationship
Among adults with a first documented HCC diagnosis in the configured HCC hospital dataset, receipt of a recorded liver-directed procedure within 30 days of index is associated with lower 1-year all-cause mortality than no recorded procedure, after adjustment for baseline age, sex, comorbidity/liver-disease burden, documented stage when available, and documentation intensity.

This is an observational treatment-association hypothesis, not a causal treatment-benefit claim. The clinically important unresolved claim is whether the apparent association survives adjustment for disease severity and selection/documentation processes. The strongest rival explanations are stage/tumor burden, liver reserve and performance status, access/clinician selection, documentation intensity, and informative loss to follow-up. A result that disappears after stage/documentation adjustment, or is concentrated among densely documented patients, would support a rival explanation rather than treatment benefit.

## Grounding and evidence limits
The HCC dataset card and prior inspected candidate record identify read-only HCC tables for diagnoses, encounters, procedures, medications, examinations, labs, clinical documents, pathology, orders, vitals and transfers. The relevant joins are within HCC using the documented key pair (患者主索引, 就诊号), with patient-level aggregation on 患者主索引. The diagnosis table can define the first HCC index; procedure labels can define early liver-directed treatment; encounters provide index timing and baseline demographics; examinations, labs, documents and pathology can provide stage, severity and documentation covariates. The exact source paths, table names, columns and read-only status are recorded in datasets/hcc/README.md and the linked table JSON files.

The prior candidate reports that headers and representative rows were inspected and that HCC diagnosis/procedure vocabularies contain liver-cancer and TACE/resection/ablation labels. Those are prior observations, not newly verified counts in this episode. No effect estimate is claimed here.

Critical missing evidence is whether any reliable death/mortality field exists in the HCC source rows. The prior design explicitly says that, absent a validated death field, 1-year mortality is unavailable and the fallback last-contact endpoint is informative and not equivalent to mortality. Stage, performance status, treatment intent, and external mortality capture also require validation or expert adjudication. Vitals/transfers are identifier-only in the catalog and cannot supply vital values or transfer timing.

## Falsifiable test
Construct the first qualifying HCC diagnosis cohort, define early treatment from a prespecified procedure label set during index through 30 days, and compare the operational 1-year endpoint with an adjusted baseline model. Use the same patient-level split, endpoint, uncertainty intervals and calibration/overlap diagnostics for a selection/observation-aware alternative. Pre-specify negative timing controls, stage/documentation stratification, procedure-only versus medication-inclusive sensitivity analyses, missingness and censoring sensitivity analyses. Support requires reproducible adjusted association with adequate overlap and robustness; adverse means null/reversal/disappearance under severity or documentation adjustment; inconclusive means sparse treatment, poor stage coverage, inadequate follow-up or wide intervals.

The actual deliverable is a newly fitted, uncertainty-quantified treatment-association analysis with exact cohort construction and endpoint limitations. It cannot establish causal benefit without validated mortality, treatment intent, clinical stage/performance adjudication, and preferably a prospective or quasi-experimental design.
