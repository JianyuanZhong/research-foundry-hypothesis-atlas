## Prediction protocol

Before outcomes are read, freeze feature construction. Fit paired ten-repeat, five-fold cross-fitted ridge logistic models with identical folds for both models and stratification within the four `E×Y` cells. Seeds are outer `[260911,...,260920]`; inner seed is `outer_seed+1000+outer_fold`. M0 contains sex, a four-column Harrell restricted-cubic-spline basis for `eGFRcr_0`, a three-column restricted-cubic-spline basis for age, all 12 age-by-eGFR products, and four sex-by-eGFR products. Training quantile knots are eGFR `(0.05,0.275,0.50,0.725,0.95)` and age `(0.05,0.35,0.65,0.95)`; boundary knots are the first/last training knots. Center and scale only nonbinary columns using training-fold moments; zero variance or coincident knots invalidates that fit. M1 adds only `E` and `E*(eGFRcr_0-60)/10`. No repeat value, date, interval, attendance, repeat completeness, or outcome-derived quantity is allowed as a predictor.

Tune ridge lambda within each outer training partition by unweighted nested five-fold Bernoulli log loss over `10^(-4),10^(-3.5),...,10^4`, selecting the largest one-standard-error lambda. Average the ten out-of-fold probabilities per participant. Clip only probabilities for log loss to `[1e-6,1-1e-6]`; do not clip Brier or ranking values, and count clipping.

Primary contrast is `Delta_Brier=1000*(mean((Y-p0)^2)-mean((Y-p1)^2))`. Secondary fixed-capacity contrast uses `K=ceil(0.10*N)` and `Delta_rank10=1000*(sum(topK(M1),Y)-sum(topK(M0),Y))/K`. Ties use ascending SHA-256 of UTF-8 `episode86-renal-repeat-status-v1|` concatenated with the private `eid`; no identifiers are published. Use 2,000 successful paired participant-bootstrap replicates (maximum 2,500 attempts, seed 260921), percentile 95% intervals, and report calibration-in-the-large, slope, Brier, and repeat/interval-stratum calibration. The estimand remains predictive ranking among observed eligible repeaters, not clinical utility.
