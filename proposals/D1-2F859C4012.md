## 0. What changed vs the parent (the defect being repaired)

The parent tested whether the stress-hyperglycemia-ratio
`SHR = admission glucose (mg/dL) / (28.7 x HbA1c(%) - 46.7)` predicts mortality **independently of
absolute admission glucose**. Its key models entered *absolute glucose and SHR together* as separate
regressors. That is an **identifiability defect**, not a polish issue:

- `SHR` contains the numerator glucose, so `glucose` and `SHR` are strongly co-linear (SHR is
  approximately `glucose x (1/Gc)`); the regression is ill-conditioned and the "adjusted SHR
  coefficient" is not cleanly interpretable as a relative-glycemia effect — it is a poorly-scaled
  contrast that depends on how glucose enters.
- The parent had **no pre-registered negative-control exposure** to separate a genuine acute-on-chronic
  signal from baseline-metabolic (chronic-glycemia) confounding.
- The parent's cohort size and event count were **unverified**.

This child fixes (1) the estimand/decomposition, (2) adds a pre-registered negative-control exposure,
(3) adds an executable power gate, and (4) specifies a substantive alternative model (A1) that targets
the latent-selection rival B0 cannot identify. It keeps the parent's measurement-propensity IPW,
validity strata, negative-control outcome, locked temporal split and falsification ladder.

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance

The unresolved question is **not** whether an SHR–mortality association exists (multiple MIMIC-IV
cohorts report one; K1, K2). It is **whether the *relative* acute-on-chronic elevation carries
prognostic information that is separable from (i) absolute admission glucose and (ii) selective /
invalid HbA1c measurement — or whether the apparent "independent" SHR effect is an artifact.**

Clinical importance: SHR is promoted as a cost-free risk-stratification / trial-enrichment marker.
If the *relative* signal is separable, "stress hyperglycemia" is better read as a relative/metabolic
derangement and SHR-based stratification is defensible. If it is **not** separable once absolute
glucose, severity and HbA1c-measurement propensity are accounted for, SHR should not enrich trials or
triage — an actionable negative that prevents deploying a redundant biomarker. Both outcomes change a
decision.

## 2. Estimands: separating relative from absolute (the collinearity fix)

Let `G = admission glucose` (mg/dL), `Gc = 28.7 x HbA1c(%) - 46.7` (HbA1c-implied chronic/average
glucose, mg/dL), and `R = log(G) - log(Gc)` (the log-ratio; `R = log SHR`). Use standardized (per-SD)
inputs. The prespecified, full-rank parameterizations are:

- **Decomposition model (primary):** `outcome ~ G_z + Gc_z + G_z:Gc_z + covariates`.
  Because `G` and `Gc` are distinct quantities, entering them **separately** (instead of `G` and
  `SHR`) removes the built-in collinearity; the interaction tests whether the acute component's
  effect depends on the chronic level.
- **Log-ratio (relative) parameterization:** `outcome ~ log(G) + R + covariates`, where
  `R = log(G) - log(Gc)`. This is an exact, full-rank re-parameterization of `(log G, log Gc)`.
  The coefficient on `R` is the effect of **relative elevation at fixed absolute level** and is
  **exactly separable from absolute glucose**; the coefficient on `log(G)` is the absolute/level
  effect at fixed ratio.

**Which estimand answers which hypothesis:**

- **H-relative** is answered by `β_R` (the contrast on the pure relative term) being positive and the
  relative contrast in the decomposition model surviving adjustment: *at a fixed absolute glucose,
  higher relative elevation predicts higher risk*.
- **R-absolute** is answered by whether the ratio term adds anything once the level is included:
  `β_R ≈ 0` (and `Gc_z ≈ 0`) with good precision ⇒ only absolute glucose/severity matter.
- A fitted restricted cubic spline in `R` (and in `SHR`) is reported for shape, but the **primary
  inferential estimands are `β_R` and the `G_z`, `Gc_z` coefficients**, not the raw SHR odds ratio.

## 3. Pre-registered negative-control EXPOSURE (chronic glycemia Gc alone)

Fit `outcome ~ Gc_z + covariates` (no absolute glucose, no ratio). This treats HbA1c-implied
**chronic glycemia alone** as a pre-registered **negative-control exposure**.

- **Directional prediction that discriminates H-relative from R-select / baseline-metabolic:**
  - If the effect is genuinely **acute-on-chronic (H-relative)**, chronic glycemia alone should be
    **weak** (per-SD `Gc_z` coefficient near null, or clearly smaller than `β_R`), while `β_R` carries
    the signal. (K3: personalizing care to the HbA1c-implied chronic level gave ~no mortality
    difference, RD 0.10% [-0.26%, 0.93%] — bounding chronic glycemia's independent prognostic weight.)
  - If the apparent SHR effect is **baseline-metabolic / R-select** (sicker or more-dysglycemic
    patients are both more likely to have HbA1c measured AND more likely to die), chronic glycemia
    alone (`Gc_z`) should be **positive and non-trivial**, with `β_R` adding little. The discriminating
    statistic is the ratio `β_R / β_Gc_z` (and its bootstrap CI): **>>1 favors H-relative; ≈1 or <1
    with a non-null `Gc_z` favors R-select / baseline-metabolic.**
- Pre-registration rule: report this contrast regardless of the primary result; do not select it after
  seeing outcomes.

## 4. Measurement-selection and validity arms (retained, for R-select / R-validity)

- **Measurement-propensity (IPW).** Among **all** first-ICU stays with a measurable admission glucose
  (whether or not HbA1c was measured), fit `Pr(HbA1c measured | severity, age/sex, admission type,
  diabetes flag)`, then refit the decomposition/log-ratio outcome models **weighted by
  `1/ê`** (truncated at the 1st/99th percentile). Attenuation of `β_R` under IPW measures selection
  bias. (R-select prediction: `β_R` attenuates >50% and the NCO below lights up.)
- **Validity strata (R-validity).** Refit within strata defined by anemia (lowest hemoglobin tertile
  from `labevents` Hb itemids 51222/50811/51640), red-cell transfusion (from `chartevents`/`emar`,
  feasibility checked at compile time), and renal function (baseline creatinine `50912` tertile).
  Test whether `β_R` is stable vs flips/null across strata. (R-validity prediction: instability.)
- **Negative-control OUTCOME (NCO).** Prespecified NCO = **hospital-acquired venous thromboembolism**
  (`diagnoses_icd` ICD-10-CM codes I26.x / I80.x / I82.x via `d_icd_diagnoses`) — plausibly linked to
  severity/immobility/measurement-selection but with no established acute-glycemia mechanism. Compare
  the standardized per-SD SHR effect on the primary outcome (28-day mortality) vs the NCO:
  **R-select predicts the two effects are similar (ratio ≈ 1); H-relative predicts the NCO effect is
  much weaker (ratio >> 1).** The NCO's no-glycemia-mechanism assumption is a clinical-adjudication
  claim (see §9), not something these data prove.

## 5. Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcomes (frozen design)

- **Population:** adults (>=18; `patients.anchor_age`; MIMIC-IV ages are de-identified by
  `anchor_year` shift) with a **first ICU stay** (`icustays`, earliest `intime` per `subject_id`) and a
  measurable **admission glucose**. Index time `t0 = icustays.intime`.
  - Admission glucose = first `labevents` glucose (`50931` Chemistry primary; sensitivity `50809`/
    `52027` blood-gas, `52569`) with `charttime` in `[t0-6h, t0+24h]`.
  - HbA1c = `labevents` itemid `50852` ("% Hemoglobin A1c"; sensitivity `50854` "Absolute A1c") with
    `charttime` in `[t0-24h, t0+24h]`; valid-range filter applied (`valueuom` and numeric range checked).
  - `Gc = 28.7 x HbA1c(%) - 46.7`; `SHR = G / Gc`; `R = log(G) - log(Gc)`.
- **Keys/joins (MIMIC-IV):** `subject_id` (patient), `hadm_id` (admission), `stay_id` (ICU stay).
  `labevents` -> `subject_id` (+`hadm_id`); `icustays` links `hadm_id`<->`stay_id` with
  `intime`/`outtime`; `admissions` gives `admittime`/`dischtime`/`deathtime`/`hospital_expire_flag`;
  `patients` gives `gender`/`anchor_age`/`anchor_year`/`dod`; `diagnoses_icd` + `d_icd_diagnoses` give
  diabetes / anemia / VTE flags; `chartevents` supplies severity/observation-intensity features.
- **Outcome (primary):** 28-day all-cause mortality = event if `admissions.deathtime - intime <= 28d`;
  otherwise 0 if `dischtime - intime <= 28d` (administratively censored). **Linkage to the national
  Death Index is NOT in the configured tables -> mortality is hospital-recorded only; out-of-hospital
  death after discharge is unobservable** (see §9).
- **Covariates:** age, sex, admission type, an ICU severity score from `chartevents`/`labevents`
  (SOFA-style components), sepsis flag, known-diabetes flag, baseline creatinine, anemia flag.
- **Adjustment ladder:** M0 = severity + age/sex; M1a = M0 + `G_z`; M1b = M0 + `G_z` + `Gc_z`
  (+ interaction `G_z:Gc_z`); M1c = M1b + `R` (= the log-ratio contrast); M2 = M1c + measurement-
  intensity/observation features; M2-IPW = M1c reweighted by `1/ê`; M3 = permuted-label negative
  control.
- **Split:** one locked earliest-70% / latest-30% by `icustays.intime` (MIMIC-IV per-subject date
  shifting makes this a leakage-safe ORDERING proxy, not a calendar-era split). Model selection is on
  the earliest 70%; the latest 30% is held out for confirmation.

## 7. Method comparison on the SAME question (B0 vs A1)

- **Baseline B0 (interpretable, CPU):** L2-penalized logistic regression on the decomposition
  (`G_z`,`Gc_z`, interaction) and log-ratio (`log G`,`R`) parameterizations, reporting `β_R`,
  `β_Gc_z` and `β_G` as per-SD odds ratios with bootstrap CIs; spline for shape. B0 is the
  clear, low-variance point estimate and the primary inferential vehicle.
- **Alternative A1 (substantive, CPU/EM, < 1 h):** a **joint measurement-selection + outcome model**
  (Heckman-style / pattern-mixture with a shared latent factor): a probit selection equation for
  `HbA1c measured` and a mortality (logistic or probit) outcome equation, coupled by a shared latent
  factor `u` (correlated errors, estimated by EM / limited-information ML). Fitted on **all** first-ICU
  stays with admission glucose (selection defines who contributes the outcome), with the relative term
  `R` in the outcome equation.
  - **What A1 reveals that B0 cannot:** the R-select rival is *exactly* a latent-selection problem.
    B0 conditions on the measured cohort and can only attenuate by observed-propensity reweighting;
    A1 uses the **unmeasured** component of selection (the correlated error), so it can identify how
    much of `β_R` is selection on the unmeasured severity/dysglycemia axis vs a genuine relative-
    glycemia effect. A1 and B0 agreeing on a positive `β_R` strengthens H-relative; A1 shrinking
    `β_R` toward null while B0 keeps it flags R-select.
- **Exact inputs:** archive members `mimic-iv-3.1/hosp/labevents.csv.gz` (glucose `50931`/`50809`/
  `52027`/`52569`, HbA1c `50852`/`50854`, creatinine `50912`, hemoglobin `51222`/`50811`/`51640`),
  `icu/icustays.csv.gz`, `hosp/admissions.csv.gz`, `hosp/patients.csv.gz`, `hosp/diagnoses_icd.csv.gz`,
  `hosp/d_labitems.csv.gz`, `icu/chartevents.csv.gz`.
- **Fit target:** `β_R` (and the `Gc_z` negative-control coefficient) in M1b/M1c, plus the A1
  bias-corrected `β_R`. **Split:** earliest-70% fit / latest-30% confirmation. **Evaluation:**
  out-of-sample AUROC and calibration of M0 vs M1a vs M1b vs M1c; bootstrap CI for the `β_R` vs
  `β_Gc_z` contrast and for the primary vs NCO effect ratio. **Baseline comparison:** A1 vs B0 on
  identical folds/margins. **Approximate budget:** CPU-only, ~4 cores / 16 GB / **< 1 h** (tabular,
  modest N; EM converges in tens of iterations) — no GPU justified or requested; consistent with the
  solver envelope (`solver_planning_limits`: 16 CPU / 8 GPU / 256 GB / 8 h planning-only).
- **Selection/deferral reason:** A1 adds the minimal latent machinery that targets the *one* rival B0
  structurally cannot identify (unmeasured measurement selection); it is deferred only if the power
  gate is not met or selection/EM fails to converge, in which case B0 + IPW is reported as the honest
  (reduced) result. Chosen over a neural model because tabular N is modest and the scientific target
  is a single latent-selection contrast, not a flexible predictor.

## 8. Falsification criteria (prespecified)

- **Supportive (H-relative):** `β_R` per-SD > 1 with 95% CI excluding 1 in M1c; attenuation under IPW
  < 25%; `β_R / β_Gc_z >> 1` (chronic glycemia alone weak); NCO effect ratio >> 1 (NCO null); stable
  across validity strata; permutation control null.
- **Adverse (informative negative):** `β_R` CI includes 1 once `G_z` is in M1a (R-absolute); **OR**
  attenuation under IPW > 50% with `Gc_z` positive and `β_R/β_Gc_z <= ~1` (R-select / baseline-
  metabolic); **OR** NCO reproduces the "SHR" signal (primary/NCO ratio ≈ 1, R-select); **OR** `β_R`
  flips/null across anemia/transfusion/renal strata (R-validity). Any of these is a publishable
  negative.
- **Inconclusive:** power gate not met (fewer than 200 HbA1c-measured stays / 40 events / 10 in the
  smallest stratum); wide CIs; too few patients for strata; M2 features or RBC-transfusion exposure
  unavailable. Report INCONCLUSIVE; do not substitute an easier question or an uncomputed envelope.

## 9. Honest limits, missing evidence, required clinical adjudication

- Single-centre retrospective MIMIC-IV; **association only, no causal claim.**
- **Mortality is hospital-recorded** (`admissions.deathtime`); out-of-hospital death is unobservable,
  so 28-day mortality is partly administratively censored (no Death-Index linkage in configured tables).
- **HbA1c measurement is selective** (the very rival R-select); prevalence/power are **not observed**
  until the §6 gate runs.
- HbA1c reliability in acute illness (anemia/transfusion/renal function) is a **clinical/laboratory
  adjudication** issue (supports R-validity); it can be **bounded, not resolved**, from these data —
  requires expert review / a marker-independent standard (e.g., continuous glucose monitoring), which
  is not in the configured tables.
- The **NCO** rests on the clinical judgement that hospital-acquired VTE has no acute-glycemia
  mechanism; that assumption needs clinical review and cannot be verified computationally.
- The negative-control exposure `Gc` assumes HbA1c validly indexes chronic glycemia in the acute
  setting; if invalid (R-validity), `Gc` is a noisy negative control — a limitation, stated.
- Microcirculatory vs stress mechanisms are not diagnosable here; those require another study.
