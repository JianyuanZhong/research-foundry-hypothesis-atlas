## Results

The frozen continuous feature was `Z = log(eGFRcys_0 / eGFRcr_0)`, training-fold standardized and added as one linear column to the unchanged flexible comparator. There were 100 converged primary fits, 13 M0 columns and 14 Mc columns before the intercept, complete held-out coverage, exact 1,637 selections per model and repeat, and 4,000 successful paired participant bootstrap replicates.

Primary incremental log-loss difference, Mc minus M0, was **-0.0011426082**, with 98% conditional percentile interval **[-0.001983024, -0.000323343]**. All 10 repeat-specific log-loss differences were negative. Descriptive Brier difference was -0.000279817 and AUC difference was +0.002970101; neither can alter the frozen hierarchy.

At the fixed 10% capacity, Mc selected a repeat-mean 171.2 outcomes versus 170.2 for M0, for **Delta_events_10 = +1.0**, selected-risk difference 0.000610874, and event-capture difference 0.00323625. The 98% interval was **[-6.401, +8.500]**. Only 5 of 10 repeat event differences were positive, and the point estimate did not reach the prespecified +5-event relevance floor.

Calibration degradation was within the frozen operational margins: d_int = -0.003270631 (98% interval [-0.095079, 0.083425]), d_slope = -0.001123853 ([-0.027173, 0.025109]), and d_CITL = 0.000008717 ([-0.000266, 0.000264]).

The independent audit reproduced all primary point estimates and intervals to numerical tolerance, all repeat-direction counts, exact capacity counts, payload shapes, finite predictions, and all 4,000 bootstrap successes. It independently reapplied the frozen hierarchy.

## Frozen classification and interpretation

The final classification is **inconclusive_continuous_rescue**. This is not adverse because the log-loss interval is not wholly above zero, the capacity interval is not wholly below zero, and neither direction is adverse in at least 8 of 10 repeats. It is not supportive because the capacity interval crosses zero, the +1.0 event point estimate is below the +5 floor, and only 5 of 10 repeats improve event concentration. The result therefore shows reproducible held-out log-loss improvement for this post-parent feature, but it does not establish the prespecified joint information-plus-10%-concentration rescue.

The claim is conditional on selected complete first-repeat attenders and on the observed future repeat time; the outcome is a repeat creatinine-equation threshold, not measured GFR or adjudicated CKD. No causal, clinical utility, testing-benefit, nonattender, deployment, or transport conclusion is supported. External replication, clinically adjudicated outcomes, assay and attendance evidence, and a separate decision-utility study would be required for stronger claims.
