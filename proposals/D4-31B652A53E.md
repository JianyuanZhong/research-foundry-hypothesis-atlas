## Question, evidence and advance

The strongest available evidence is structural: paired columns for an eight-field panel, two assessment dates, age/sex, 259-position inpatient diagnosis code/date arrays, and death dates are present in the snapshot. Headers/catalog do not establish analyte identities or units, specimen timing, disease onset, diagnosis validity, individual observation end, or complete five-year ascertainment.

The unresolved clinical decision is whether retaining the immediately preceding panel changes a constrained risk-review queue in a useful direction, beyond the current panel and the information that a person returned for repeat assessment and the spacing of that return. This is a substantive advance from average score improvement to a prespecified finite-capacity decision estimand. It is not a causal test of repeat testing, monitoring, referral, treatment, or benefit.

Primary hypothesis: among people with valid paired panels and a verified current assessment landmark who fall in a prespecified current-only middle-risk band, adding the prior panel to current values plus repeat-spacing information increases held-out capture and positive yield for a later qualifying recorded cardiorenal/metabolic target at 10% review capacity, without material calibration harm; the direction persists when models fitted through 2012 are applied without refitting to 2013.

Positive results motivate external validation and prospective impact research; negative results support current values plus spacing for this recorded-risk task. Neither establishes disease incidence, optimal capacity, action thresholds, causality, patient benefit, or transport.

## Models and primary capacity estimand

Use deterministic participant-level five-fold split SHA256(UTF8(decimal(eid))) mod 5. No eid spans folds. Imputation, scaling, tuning, weighting, calibration and thresholds are training-fold-only.

M0 is current position-1 panel plus age, sex and T1 calendar year. M1a adds L and is the transparent current-plus-spacing baseline. M1b adds all eight position-0 values and is the primary history addition. A1 adds prespecified current-minus-prior contrasts as an interpretive sensitivity. M2 is a small shallow gradient-boosted multinomial model with exactly M1b inputs; M2a is the same learner with prior fields removed. M1a/M1b answer the nested scientific question; M2/M2a are matched alternatives, not replacements.

Generate one prediction per eligible participant: cross-fitted early predictions, and predictions from models fit through 2012 without refitting for 2013. Pool predictions across folds within each era. Apply the frozen band. The primary cohort-level workload is K_e=floor(0.10*n_e,A), where n_e,A is the entire era’s middle-band population. Select exactly K_e from the pooled era by target probability, with deterministic eid ties—not K_e separately per fold. This is a comparable 10% capacity in early and late eras. Use unchanged early K in late, and 5%/20%, only as sensitivities.

Report paired M1b-minus-M1a target capture (target events in top-K divided by all target events in the same era band), target yield, additions/removals, death capture and target/death composition; Brier/log loss; calibration intercept/slope; and observed-versus-predicted counts. Report late-minus-early change in the paired capture/yield contrast.

## Uncertainty, falsification and meaning

Use participant bootstrap preserving era, fold, band and queue capacity; plan 1,000 replicates and report the actual number. Include fold instability, selection overlap/ESS, calibration, missingness, and late support. Falsifications are endpoint-label permutation within era/fold; prior-panel permutation within era/band/interval/missingness/repeat-selection strata; one-position code/date shifts (must be detected); early/late label permutation; capacity-label randomization; deterministic-tie and leakage audits.

Support requires all gates, >=100 target events overall, cell support, no integrity/leakage failure, positive M1b-minus-M1a capture and yield with 95% interval excluding zero in early and same direction in 2013, no material calibration harm, and persistence after selection weighting. Adverse evidence is zero/negative capture/yield, gain attributable only to M0-to-M1a spacing, disappearance under weighting/permutation, calibration harm, late reversal, or Brier gain without queue concentration. Inconclusive evidence includes failed semantics/timing/ascertainment, sparse events, capacity/support/ESS failure, malformed pairing or unstable folds. Inconclusive is not evidence against the hypothesis.
