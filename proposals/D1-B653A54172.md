# Biological reserve decline versus ascertainment intensity after first TACE in recorded-compensated HCC

## Scientific deliverable and clinical decision

This is a substantive successor to assessed-valid parent [prior hypothesis]. It preserves, exactly, the parent’s first high-specificity TACE index, the recorded-compensated HCC cohort, the baseline [index−14 days, index] window, the early exposure [index+3 days, index+14 days] window, and the outcome boundary [index+15 days, index+90 days]. It does not reinterpret TACE as an intervention assignment.

The unresolved clinical claim is whether early post-TACE deterioration in measured liver-reserve biology is associated with a subsequently recorded hepatic-decompensation event beyond the intensity with which the patient was observed and documented. This matters when deciding whether an apparently compensated patient’s early laboratory change should prompt prospective assessment and caution about another locoregional treatment. A signal that persists for a stricter corroborated record and after a prespecified observation-process stress test would justify expert adjudication and prospective validation. A signal that is concentrated in diagnosis-only records, or is reproduced by process variables alone, would caution that surveillance and coding—not biological reserve—may explain the apparent risk.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted and estimated:

1. a fixed interaction estimand for early worsening × baseline reserve for the parent diagnosis endpoint D;
2. the same paired estimand for a stricter corroborated endpoint C when event support permits;
3. a prespecified decomposition into biology-only, ascertainment-process-only, and joint models, with paired uncertainty;
4. a locked comparison of a static regularized baseline and a temporal learned model on the same endpoint and held-out patients; and
5. outcome-pathway, process-intensity, negative-control and corroboration sensitivity results.

Completion requires a cohort-flow and data-opportunity table; one row per patient-index; exact procedure, HCC, compensation, assay and outcome dictionaries; counts of D, C and diagnosis-without-C; process-feature distributions; fitted coefficients/risk contrasts; locked predictions; calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, AUROC, AUPRC and decision-curve tables; patient-bootstrap intervals; and all falsification outputs. A schema audit or a fitted model without the biological/process decomposition is not completion.

## Evidence-supported versus untested claims

Before this experiment, the strongest supported claim is only that the local HCC snapshot contains encounter-linked procedures and diagnoses, timestamped examinations, and repeated timestamped laboratory records sufficient for a prognostic observed-care analysis. The source audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 1,810,646 diagnosis rows and 28,159,928 laboratory rows; it also found relevant assay and decompensation labels. This establishes data availability, not TACE completeness, clinical adjudication, valid text interpretation, complete follow-up, or biological truth.

Evidence already supported by the parent is that the exact first high-specificity TACE/recorded-compensated design is feasible enough to yield a bounded audit, and that a paired diagnosis-versus-examination record is available as a proposed proxy. The fresh vocabulary audit found 51,539 broad-rule matched procedure rows, including labels such as ‘hepatic arterial chemoembolization’, ‘transcatheter hepatic arterial embolization + arterial chemoembolization + hepatic arteriography’ and ‘TACE’; raw diagnosis counts included 1,980 rows containing ascites, 414 hepatic encephalopathy, 161 liver failure and 786 upper gastrointestinal bleeding. These are raw availability counts, not final cohort or event counts.

The untested claim is:

> In the exact parent cohort, early worsening of the six liver-reserve assays predicts D and the stricter C through the biological trajectory itself: the early-worsening × baseline-reserve interaction remains positive after controlling for observation opportunity measured before the outcome, and C is not explained mainly by the diagnosis-recording pathway. If the joint association is no stronger than process-only prediction, or if D is positive but C is null and D-without-C is concentrated among highly observed patients, the biological interpretation is not supported by this snapshot.

This is a prognostic association, not a causal TACE effect, a biological-failure estimate, a treatment recommendation, an estimate of monitoring benefit, or a deployable score.

## Population and temporal design

Use HCC snapshot `[source checksum]`. Every listed HCC source is an ordinary read-only CSV; no archive member is required.

1. Join `procedures` to `encounters` on exact (`patient master index`, `visit number`). Parse `procedures.start time`; discard unparseable times and unresolved encounter keys. The primary high-specificity TACE vocabulary is `procedure` equal to TACE case-insensitively, or containing hepatic-artery language (`hepatic artery` or `transcatheter hepatic artery`) together with `embolization` and `chemotherapy`. Retain raw label, normalized label, source row order and linked encounter time. Do not infer TACE from medications, orders, diagnoses, examinations or documents. The broader hepatic-artery embolization vocabulary is a sensitivity only.

2. Index is the earliest qualifying primary TACE start per `patient master index`; exclude any patient with an earlier qualifying primary TACE in `procedures`. Require a non-uncertain HCC diagnosis on or before index: normalized `diagnoses.diagnosis name` containing `hepatocellular carcinoma` or the prespecified `liver cancer` term, linked to `encounters.visit time`; use `admission time` only as the parent’s fallback when `visit time` is unparseable. Exclude uncertainty, historical, hypothetical and mixed-malignancy labels under the frozen lexical rules; report an ambiguity audit.

3. Define recorded compensation exactly as the parent: no qualifying pre-index or index-time diagnosis of `ascites`, `hepatic encephalopathy`, `liver failure`, `acute liver failure`, `chronic liver failure`, `upper gastrointestinal bleeding` or `gastrointestinal bleeding`, after normalized matching and conservative exclusion of uncertain labels. Diagnoses have no native time field, so event time is derived only through the (`Patient Master Index`, `Encounter Number`) encounter link. This is a recorded-compensation proxy, not adjudication. Retain a broader non-compensated cohort as a sensitivity/transportability table, not as a replacement population.

4. Preserve the exposure windows exactly: baseline [index−14, index] and early [index+3, index+14]. Follow-up begins strictly after index+14 and ends at index+90. For each of albumin, total bilirubin, INR/PT, platelets, creatinine and sodium, choose the nearest valid observation to the landmark with deterministic timestamp and source-row-order tie breaking. Labs may be linked across later encounters using the exact patient/encounter key; no patient-only crosswalk is permitted. Keep assay identity, days-from-index, delay, availability and counts.

5. Apply the parent’s minimum-component rule for complete baseline/early six-assay exposure. For the primary binary analysis, require an encounter at or beyond index+90 for an ascertainment-complete negative. A qualifying decompensation label at or before index+14 is excluded from the new-event outcome. Patients without a complete-window negative are retained for a prespecified censored time-to-recorded-event sensitivity, not silently coded as negative.

## Outcomes and the new ascertainment decomposition

### D: parent diagnosis outcome

D is the first new qualifying diagnosis during [index+15, index+90] for ascites, hepatic encephalopathy, hepatic failure or upper gastrointestinal bleeding. Normalize `Diagnosis Name`; exclude uncertainty, negation, historical, hypothetical and clearly non-current labels under the frozen context rules. Link each diagnosis to its encounter-derived time. Report component and first-time counts. An inpatient encounter, a diagnosis of HCC, or a death-related row is not itself a D event.

### C: strict corroborated proxy

C=1 only if D=1 and, for the first qualifying D encounter/domain, the same (`Patient Master Index`, `Encounter Number`) has an examination row with parseable `Start Time` in [index+15, index+90] whose `Findings` or `Examination Diagnosis` contains the same-domain positive concept:

- ascites: `ascites` or `peritoneal effusion`;
- encephalopathy: `hepatic encephalopathy`;
- bleeding: `upper gastrointestinal bleeding`, `gastrointestinal bleeding`, `hematemesis` or `melena`;
- hepatic failure: `hepatic failure` or a frozen exact mapped hepatic-failure term.

Use the local conservative context parser to reject explicit negation, uncertainty, family-history, hypothetical and historical markers where its supported axes apply. Ambiguous text yields C=0 with an ambiguity flag. An exam elsewhere in the patient record does not corroborate D. Examination text is an unvalidated lexical proxy, not independent clinical adjudication.

For every patient-index report mutually exclusive categories: no D, D without C, and C. Also report a secondary D-plus-new-treatment flag from `medications` within [D-time−1 day, D-time+7 days] using a frozen drug dictionary (for example lactulose/rifaximin for encephalopathy and diuretic/albumin patterns for ascites), but never promote treatment to an independent endpoint: indication and response confounding are unavoidable.

### Process variables and estimands

The process block is frozen before any outcome model and is measured no later than index+14:

- pre-index intensity in [index−14, index): number of distinct encounters, laboratory rows, distinct assay-days, examination rows and orders;
- early intensity in [index, index+14]: the same counts, plus medication rows, distinct encounter-days, assay-specific counts/delays and whether discharge is recorded;
- index opportunity: `Encounter department`, index encounter duration from `Admission time`/`Discharge time` where parseable, and missingness indicators.

Counts are log1p-transformed; impossible dates and unresolved keys are audited, not imputed. Early intensity may be a response to early clinical change, so this is an ascertainment stress test and prognostic decomposition—not causal adjustment for a confounder. Outcome-window encounters, examinations and labs are never covariates in the primary model because the event can generate them; they are reported after outcome assignment to describe differential ascertainment.

Fit four low-dimensional prespecified models for D and, if supported, C:

- M0 static baseline: age, sex, index department, prior HCC history, baseline six assay values, baseline reserve score, baseline assay availability/recency; no early values or early process counts.
- M_Bio: M0 plus the six early-minus-baseline assay changes and the prespecified continuous early-worsening × baseline-reserve interaction; no early process counts.
- M_Process: M0 plus pre-index and early process variables; no early assay changes.
- M_Joint: M0 plus early assay changes and process variables, including the interaction.

Use ridge logistic regression with training-split standardization and a fixed penalty grid chosen only on validation. The estimand is the paired change in predicted absolute risk for a +1 SD early worsening at prespecified training-derived baseline-reserve quartiles, evaluated under median and upper-quartile process profiles; also report the interaction coefficient and its paired bootstrap interval. Report M_Bio−M_Joint attenuation and M_Process performance as descriptive decomposition quantities, not proof of biological isolation. If C has fewer than 20 complete-case events or fewer than 10 events in either C/non-C stratum, do not fit an unstable C regression: report C incidence, C/D concordance, exact-binomial intervals, and paired descriptive contrasts.

The principal falsification is pathway/process discordance: support requires that early biology retain a positive C contrast in M_Joint while M_Process is materially weaker and D-without-C is not the dominant process-linked pathway. A result is not called supportive because M_Bio predicts D.

## Baseline versus substantive temporal alternative

The simple baseline is M0/M_Joint ridge logistic regression. It is transparent, low-dimensional and necessary for estimating the prespecified interaction and checking whether any learned gain is clinically substantive rather than a small predictive improvement.

The substantive learned alternative is a patient-level temporal gradient-boosted tree model over the same six assays and same endpoint. Inputs from [index−14, index+14] are assay identity, numeric value, time-from-index, baseline/early-window flag, assay-specific missingness and counts, plus age, sex, prior HCC history and index context. Fit two locked feature versions: biology block (values and timing) and joint block (biology plus process counts). The process-only ablation is also fit when support allows. The target is D, and C only if the event gate passes; no feature after index+14 is allowed.

Use a patient-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test split, stratified by outcome category, with a later-index holdout if enough events remain. Fit/tune only on train/validation, calibrate on validation, and lock test predictions. Cap at 500 trees and 10 configurations, 4 CPUs and 16 GB RAM; use streamed extraction of the 2.2 GB labs file and preserve checkpoints/model artifacts. The bounded science budget is 7,200 seconds; expected extraction and fitting is approximately 20–60 minutes after schema audit. Evaluate both alternatives on identical held-out patients and each common endpoint with Brier, calibration intercept/slope, AUROC, AUPRC, decision-curve net benefit and 1,000 successful patient-bootstrap replicates where feasible. Compare biology versus joint/process-only feature blocks. Feature importance is exploratory; an apparent gain driven by counts, missingness or timing of observation is process dependence, not biological evidence. No threshold is selected from the test set.

A latent GP/mixed-effects trajectory model is not chosen: it could estimate patient-specific slopes, but the current uncertainty is whether the observed endpoint is ascertainment-driven, and the six-assay observation schedule plus sparse C events do not justify its additional missing-data and local-optimum assumptions. A transformer/generative sequence model is likewise deferred because capacity would not resolve unvalidated endpoint text and could amplify surveillance shortcuts. These are question-specific deferrals, not blanket exclusions of neural networks or GPU use. Revisit them only after adequate corroborated events and a demonstrated need for latent trajectory inference.

## Uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Use a fixed-seed patient bootstrap that resamples patient-index rows and refits both paired endpoint/process models within each replicate. Report at least 1,000 successful replicates where support permits; use exact binomial intervals for sparse C incidence. Preserve source and schema hashes, row counts, raw-to-normalized vocabularies, assay exclusions, cohort-flow exclusions, split seed, model artifacts, bootstrap outputs and software versions.

Supportive result: the early-worsening × baseline-reserve risk contrast is positive for D and C; C is not trivial or wholly D-without-C; the C contrast remains materially present in M_Joint relative to M_Bio; M_Process is weaker; and the temporal model’s improvement, if any, is not primarily from process variables. This supports incremental prediction of a stricter observed-care corroboration proxy and a reason to seek blinded clinical adjudication. It does not establish true decompensation, causality, or monitoring utility.

Adverse result: C is null/reverse while D is positive; the D/C association attenuates materially after process variables; M_Process matches M_Joint; D-without-C dominates among highly observed patients; or a frozen non-hepatic negative-control diagnosis shows a similar early-worsening/process interaction. This supports ascertainment or generic surveillance as a plausible explanation and refutes the stronger biological interpretation in this snapshot. It does not show that reserve decline is clinically irrelevant.

Inconclusive result: fewer than the event gate, wide paired intervals, unreliable time parsing, no ascertainment-complete negatives, high assay-unit ambiguity, severe missingness/process imbalance, unstable lexical mapping, or failed later-index holdout. Report the data limitation rather than collapsing C into D or declaring a negative biological finding.

Automatic verification can check read-only source hashes, ordinary-file/member status, table and column existence, exact joins, first TACE and HCC/compensation filters, exact windows, pre-day-15 exclusions, complete-window negatives, same-encounter C linkage, category exclusivity, process-feature cutoff, patient-level split, fitted artifacts, bootstrap intervals and that conclusions match computed outputs. It cannot adjudicate Chinese clinical text, distinguish true decompensation from a coded proxy, recover uncaptured outpatient events/deaths/transplants, determine treatment indication, establish causality, or prove that prospective monitoring improves care. Those require blinded expert chart review, better longitudinal capture, external validation and an intervention or prospective study.

## Provenance, alternatives and evidence limits

The source guide is `datasets/README.md`; the HCC guide is `datasets/hcc/README.md`; full HCC metadata is `datasets/hcc/metadata.json`. The three research demonstrations were inspected under `references/research-ambition/README.md` and `methods-and-compute.md`: Delphi supports a bounded structured temporal adaptation but its UKB/Danish reproduction is unavailable; Oncoformer’s full main text/STAR Methods and image modality are unavailable, so only a structured/lab adaptation is relevant; ALADYNOULLI’s genetic inputs and latent assumptions are unavailable and unnecessary here. The expert-seed README confirms there are no HCC seeds; imported UKB, MIMIC-IV and eICU candidates are not HCC parents and no cross-dataset join is justified.

The selected alternatives are retained because they answer different scientific uncertainty: M0/M_Joint gives an interpretable, uncertainty-quantified process decomposition, while temporal boosting can reveal nonlinear timing/asynchronous assay patterns that a composite score loses. Neither is rewarded for complexity or small predictive gains. The deferred models may be revisited only if event density, clinical adjudication and a specific latent-trajectory question justify them.

The snapshot lacks validated decompensation adjudication or severity, reliable hospital identity, complete outpatient history, complete death/transplant capture, tumor stage, imaging response, recurrence, TACE dose/intent, treatment assignment and deployment outcomes. Pathology and clinical documents cannot provide a timestamped primary outcome under this design. No claim of mortality benefit, TACE effect, true incidence, clinical utility or deployment is permitted.
