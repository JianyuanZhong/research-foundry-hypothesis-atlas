# Capacity-frontier robustness of all-offered UACR-first versus immediate cystatin-C allocation

## Clinical decision, supported evidence, unresolved claim, and advance

A health service must choose how to allocate a constrained number of cystatin-C assays among adults aged 40–69 whose baseline 2021 race-free creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) is 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m². One strategy attempts UACR in every eligible adult, waits for a usable result or a protocol-defined failure, uses a frozen fallback after failure, and allocates cystatin C using the all-offered AG score. The alternative allocates immediately over the same eligible adults using the UACR-free G score. Episode 13 compared these strategies only when cystatin capacity was exactly 20% of each lock. Services can face tighter or looser constraints, and two ranked lists can cross as capacity expands. A recommendation resting on one arbitrary operating point is therefore unresolved.

The strongest public evidence already supported is narrower. Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041; Europe PMC full-text XML source [source checksum], inspected) studied 468,969 UK Biobank participants, developed models in 375,175, internally validated them in 93,794, and reported validation AUC 0.75 and calibration 1.00 for an enriched model. Their abstract reported that 25% had eGFRcys at least 15 mL/min/1.73 m² lower than eGFRcr. This supports that creatinine–cystatin discordance is common and internally predictable from available characteristics. It does not establish a constrained allocation strategy, incremental grip or UACR value, a capacity frontier, measured-GFR accuracy, CKD chronicity, cost-effectiveness, implementation feasibility, treatment action, benefit, or external transportability.

The unresolved hypothesis is that the direct all-offered advantage of intention-to-obtain-UACR-first AG over immediate G is robust across a prespecified range of constrained cystatin capacities, rather than occurring only at 20%. The substantive Episode-14 advance is a decision frontier at C={0.05,0.10,0.15,0.20,0.25,0.30,0.40}, spanning one-quarter to twice the parent capacity. This is a scenario range, not evidence that every point is feasible in a particular service; local capacity, turnaround and cost data are unavailable. Population, recruitment time zero, outcomes, development/test/replication split, score definitions, fitting, fallback, missing-label bounds, internal baselines, margins and evidence limits remain frozen. Scores are fitted once and never reoptimized by capacity.

This remains a retrospective deterministic allocation analog. “Intention-to-obtain” is an imposed protocol, not an observed UKB action or chronology. It does not estimate causal benefit or total resource use.

## Primary falsifiable capacity-frontier hypothesis

For lock p in {test, replication} and capacity c in C, define n_pc=floor(c×|O_p|). AG_pc is the top n_pc members of O_p ranked by the frozen all-O AG score, using observed snapshot UACR when usable and the frozen development median fallback otherwise. G_pc is the top n_pc members of O_p under the frozen UACR-free G score. Both policies use exactly n_pc cystatin assays and the same candidate pool at each c.

Broad biochemical frontier support requires all of the following under the adverse joint missing-label assignment:

- At every c in C and in both locks, Δ_H(c)=Y_H(AG_pc)−Y_H(G_pc) is at least 2 additional H targets per 1,000 assays, the H-yield ratio is at least 1.10, the simultaneous lower limits are above 0 and 1 respectively, and the equal-size AG-only minus G-only directional swap yield is positive.
- At every c in C, Δ_D(c) is positive in each lock and its pooled simultaneous lower limit is above 0.
- The unchanged 20% W0 immediate and W1_ITO UACR-first internal gates support; all integrity, missingness, calibration, placebo, sex, UACR-variant and replication gates pass.

Thus a favorable 20% result cannot rescue failure elsewhere, and a favorable development or pooled point cannot establish robustness. The experiment also emits a label at each c and the maximal consecutive grid interval containing 20% whose direct biochemical gates support. This interval is descriptive of tested grid points only; no interpolation or untested continuous crossing threshold is allowed.

If the frozen N18 information thresholds pass at every c, broad prognostic support additionally requires adverse-bound pooled Δ_H18(AG:G,c) at least 1 per 1,000 with simultaneous lower limit above 0 at every c, plus the unchanged W0 and W1_ITO 20% N18 gates. Sparse N18 information makes chronic consequence unresolved, not adverse.

## Meaning of results and evidence still required

A supportive result means only that, within both internal UKB locks, the frozen snapshot/fallback AG ranking identifies materially more equation-defined H and D targets than G at every tested equal-cystatin capacity from 5% through 40%, under prespecified uncertainty and multiplicity control. N18-coded enrichment may be added only if its gates pass. This supports prospective comparison across locally relevant capacities; it does not establish a preferred workflow, lower cost, feasibility, CKD diagnosis, better action or benefit.

An adverse result means adequately precise evidence against the broad frontier claim at one or more capacities or against an essential internal policy claim. A crossing can show that the 20% ranking does not generalize. It does not show causal superiority or patient benefit of the alternative.

An inconclusive result means the available data cannot establish broad robustness or adequately refute it because of uncertainty, missing labels, sparse swaps/events, instability, lock conflict, integrity or computation. It is not equivalence. A capacity-limited supportive interval is informative for future design but cannot be called a universal service recommendation.

Actual local capacity plausibility and workflow choice require assay throughput, turnaround, staffing, workload, monetary cost, UACR burden, failure/retry and downstream action data. Actual chronology and success require participant-level UACR offer/order, collection, release, invalid/failure reason, retry and deadline timestamps plus cystatin list/order timestamps. Persistent kidney disease requires repeated albuminuria and filtration measurements; kidney truth requires measured GFR and clinical adjudication; complete prognosis requires outpatient events, emigration/censoring and chart review; transportability requires external validation; benefit and harms require prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy evaluation with patient-centered outcomes and expert review.
