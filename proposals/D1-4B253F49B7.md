# HCC early inpatient escalation hypothesis (episode 5 working draft)

## Provisional hypothesis
Among adults with an exact documented hepatocellular carcinoma diagnosis and an inpatient encounter, a prespecified 7-day within-assay laboratory trajectory may add held-out information about a subsequent inpatient procedure-based escalation-of-care proxy beyond an admission-baseline snapshot, age/sex, department, treatment/procedure indicators, and documentation intensity. The direction and magnitude are untested. This is not a claim about death, ICU admission, or clinical harm.

## Why it matters
A temporal signal could distinguish evolving decompensation from a stable high-risk baseline and identify when an early monitoring review might be warranted. The substantive advance over static prognostic work is a falsifiable test of whether ordering, persistence, and cross-marker change add information in this HCC population. The strongest claim currently supported is only that the HCC snapshot contains joinable encounter, diagnosis, laboratory, and procedure records; no prediction or causal effect has been observed.

## Test and interpretation
Compare a penalized logistic snapshot baseline with a time-aware sequence/state-space alternative using the same patient-level split and covariates. The alternative can reveal persistence, ordering, and cross-marker interactions lost by a snapshot. Report held-out discrimination, calibration, uncertainty, negative-control timing checks, and sensitivity to department, documentation, missingness, and assay definitions. Supportive results require a nontrivial held-out improvement with uncertainty and stable sensitivity analyses; adverse results include no improvement, degradation, or failed negative controls; sparse events or unstable units/procedure mapping are inconclusive.

## Missing evidence and limits
The exact post-window cohort/event counts, procedure-category dictionary, assay units/specimen harmonization, and clinical adjudication of harm are unresolved. No model has been fitted. Treatment indication, selection, documentation intensity, and delayed follow-up are plausible rivals; adjustment cannot establish mechanism or causality.

## Method alternatives
The simple baseline is CPU tabular logistic regression. The substantive alternative is a bounded CPU sequence/state-space model; a GPU is not required. The alternative is retained because it tests temporal structure, not merely a small predictive gain. A larger neural sequence model is deferred unless the bounded diagnostic shows adequate event support and runtime.
