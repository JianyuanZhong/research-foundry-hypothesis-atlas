It repairs six defects:

1. Alive index-hospital discharge was fully computable but combined hospice, acute-hospital transfer, home, rehabilitation, skilled nursing, and long-term acute care as if they had one clinical meaning.
2. Death and remaining hospitalized at day 28 were both coded 0 without requiring a state decomposition; hospice and acute transfer were only sensitivities.
3. The binary endpoint could be called a success despite recorded post-discharge death before day 28.
4. The parent censored the A clone at ICU exit before initiation and the B clone when hospital discharge ended medication observation. Because discharge is itself the outcome, this could remove favorable terminal events asymmetrically during the grace period.

6. The hypothesis asked for benefit greater than 5 percentage points, but the branch rules did not separately decide whether that margin was supported, falsified, or unresolved.

The repaired primary outcome is **alive index-hospital discharge by day 28 to a recorded destination other than hospice or acute hospital (ANHATD28)**. It remains an administrative transition endpoint, not recovery. It counts rehabilitation, skilled nursing, long-term acute care, home health, and other non-hospice/non-acute destinations as events; their exact frequencies and separate risks must therefore be displayed. A strict community-discharge sensitivity is required. Hospice, acute-hospital transfer, index-hospital death, prolonged hospitalization, and invalid destination records remain distinct observed states and may not be collapsed in reporting.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and clinical advance

The strongest intervention claim supported by inspected evidence remains limited. McMullan et al., *Critical Care Explorations* 2026;8:e1404, DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404`, inspected frozen full text `[source checksum]`, reports no statistically detectable between-arm differences in lactate, AKIRisk, or urinary cystatin-C in a modest randomized secondary analysis of protocolized conservative fluid management and active deresuscitation; the authors explicitly state that clinically important effects remain possible. It does not establish patient benefit, renal safety, or the effect of one post-shock IV-loop initiation.

Boyle et al., *Am J Respir Crit Care Med* 2026, DOI `10.1093/ajrccm/aamag238`, inspected frozen full text `[source checksum]`, explains why death and discharge are competing events for organ-recovery endpoints and why state-based reporting can prevent unlike failure modes from disappearing inside one summary. It does not validate discharge destination as function, survival, or patient preference.

Wang et al., *NPJ Digital Medicine* 2026, DOI `10.1038/s41746-026-02563-z`, inspected full-text XML `[source checksum]`, emphasizes that EHR target trials identify data-constrained estimands when treatment, follow-up, or outcomes cannot realize the conceptual trial. This supports the explicit observability limits below; it does not make exchangeability true.

Official MIMIC documentation was acquired and inspected, not merely searched. The admissions page, frozen source `[source checksum]`, defines `discharge_location` as administrative disposition, maps `HOSPICE` to UB-04 50/51 and `ACUTE HOSPITAL` to 02/81/86, and warns that internal codes may be consolidated. The patients page, frozen source `[source checksum]`, says `dod` combines hospital and Massachusetts vital-record sources, provides out-of-hospital mortality up to one year after discharge, and censors later deaths. It does not guarantee perfect linkage or supply exact time of post-discharge death.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the measured-overlap population of invasively ventilated adults at ICU hour 48 who have recovered from vasopressor-treated shock, have cumulative balance at least +50 mL/kg, retained urine output, and potassium at least 3.0 mmol/L, assignment to initiate an actually delivered ICU IV loop within the next 24 hours increases ANHATD28 by more than 5 percentage points versus no loop by any route during that window, without a definitively adverse ALDL7 signal or excess recorded day-28 mortality or observed new day-7 RRT.

Resolving this claim would distinguish a potentially consequential initiation policy from a short-term fluid-removal association while refusing to equate leaving one hospital with recovery. A supportive result can prioritize external validation and randomization. It cannot justify loop administration, a discharge policy, or a causal bedside claim.

### Primary endpoint and competing states

For each clone define `H=t0+28d`. Use this deterministic precedence:

1. If `hospital_expire_flag=1`, use `deathtime` only when `t0<deathtime<=dischtime`; otherwise use `dischtime` as the flag-confirmed death time when it lies after t0. If that chosen time is in `(t0,H]`, classify index-hospital death by day 28. A `deathtime` on a flag-0 row or after `dischtime` is an anomaly, not primary death evidence.
2. Otherwise, if `hospital_expire_flag=0` and `t0<dischtime<=H`:
   - `discharge_location=HOSPICE`: hospice discharge;
   - `discharge_location=ACUTE HOSPITAL`: acute-hospital transfer;
   - `discharge_location=DIED` or missing: invalid/discordant destination failure;
   - every other exact category: ANHATD28 event.
3. If no event occurred and `dischtime>H`, classify alive/undischarged from the index hospital at day 28.
4. All remaining contradictions are invalid temporal state failures.

Thus `Y_ANHATD28=1` only for state 2's last subcategory. Every clone is binary classified, but binary reporting must be accompanied by weighted probabilities for index death, hospice, acute transfer, invalid destination, and prolonged hospitalization. It must reconcile with the unaugmented weighted binary risk. Do not censor competing states.

### Estimand and analysis

The primary data-defined estimand is

`psi = Pr(ANHATD28 under assignment to A | measured overlap) - Pr(ANHATD28 under assignment to B | measured overlap)`,

where A and B are the repaired initiation strategies above and measured overlap means baseline probability of observed A conditional on prespecified pre-t0 covariates in `[0.10,0.90]`. Under consistency, positivity, correct adherence/censor and outcome models, and conditional exchangeability, this can be interpreted as a per-protocol risk difference. Because MIMIC cannot establish bedside readiness, goals of care, treatment intent, or no unmeasured confounding, the guaranteed claim is an adjusted data-defined association.

Hourly censor models update only lagged decision-available physiology and care.

Restrict to `e(X) in [0.10,0.90]`. Apply the common overlap-target tilt `e(X)*(1-e(X))` identically to both clones; do not apply arm-specific baseline treatment weights after cloning. ## Falsification and result branches

Report two separate machine-checkable decisions.

**Magnitude hypothesis status**

- Supported only if the lower 95% confidence limit for ANHATD28 RD is greater than +0.05.
- Falsified at the prespecified clinically important margin if the upper 95% limit is at most +0.05.
- Unresolved if the interval includes effects both above and at/below +0.05.

**Clinical evidence branch**

- **Supportive:** magnitude status is supported; the upper 95% limit of recorded-mortality `RD_M28,U` is below +0.03; the upper 95% limit of observed-RRT `RD_RRT,U` is below +0.05 under both date brackets; ALDL7 is not definitively adverse (upper 95% limit of `RD_ALDL,U` is above 0); the any-discharge, primary, community-discharge, and stricter non-high-acuity point estimates do not reverse sign under the event/ESS rule; and all overlap, balance, temporal, missingness, falsification, and sensitivity gates pass. This supports only external validation and a randomized trial.
- **Adverse:** the upper 95% limit of ANHATD28 RD is at most 0; or the upper 95% limit of `RD_ALDL,U` is at most 0; or the lower 95% limit of recorded-mortality `RD_M28,L` is at least +0.03; or the lower 95% limit of observed-RRT `RD_RRT,L` is at least +0.05 under both date brackets. Better balance cannot override this branch.
- **Inconclusive:** every other result, including a precisely positive but no-more-than-5-point effect, unresolved destination sensitivity, plausible mortality/RRT harm not definitively present, sparse overlap/adherence, modest residual confounding sufficient to reverse the estimate, failed negative controls, or conflicting prespecified analyses.

Thus a precise +2-point effect can falsify the >5-point hypothesis while remaining clinically inconclusive rather than being mislabeled harmful. Nonsignificance is not equivalence; lack of recorded death or RRT is not safety.
