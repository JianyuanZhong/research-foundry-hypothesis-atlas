# UKB 31: Do menopause-related lipoprotein changes exceed those due to weight change?

Admission: feasibility_required. Verify participant overlap, measurement dates, missingness and event support before committing this design.

## Hypotheses to Be Tested

Women entering menopause may still experience changes in ApoB and lipoprotein particles when weight changes are small, and these changes are accompanied by increased visceral fat.

## Study population and key data

Women whose two assessments spanned natural menopause; repeated NMR, biochemical, and anthropometric measurements, with MRI for subset validation.

## Basic Approach

Compare within-individual changes with those in individuals of similar age who have not undergone menopause, using the actual interval and HRT information; MRI changes are limited to repeat imaging.

## Significance of the Research

Separate the distinct effects of age, the menopausal transition, and fat distribution on metabolism.

## Main Challenges

Menopause timing is primarily self-reported; HRT and surgical menopause need to be handled separately; two measurements cannot depict a complete trajectory.

## Life Sciences

Sex, hormones, and metabolic redistribution

## Competing Explanations

Aging and reduced activity jointly cause hormonal and lipid changes.

## Minimal validation and falsification criteria

First verify the number of people with repeated blood draws spanning menopause; if there is no additional change after accounting for age and HRT, this does not support an independent effect of menopause.
