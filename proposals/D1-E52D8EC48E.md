# Leakage-safe preoperative HCC MVI value-structure experiment

Status: targeted child of [prior hypothesis]. A new full-source timing/leakage audit and repaired-cohort audit were executed; no outcome model or hypothesis test was fitted. The clinical question, explicit pathology endpoint, strict serial cohort concept, and temporal holdout are preserved. The estimand and primary comparator are tightened so a positive result can be attributed neither to ordinary trends/extrema nor to index/report timing artifacts.

## Scientific opening, evidence boundary, and repair

The strongest evidence-supported claim is that a static preoperative score using AFP, tumor size, and tumor margin achieved C-statistics near 0.81 in two Chinese validation cohorts, while MVI itself is confirmed histopathologically [K1]. Three-tier pathology grading defines M0, M1, and M2, and a selected resection series associated M2 with recurrence and mortality [K2]. Routine observation timing and intensity can encode care behavior rather than biology [K3]. These are prior findings, not results in this HCC snapshot.

The unresolved question is whether pre-resection laboratory value structure contains reproducible information about pathology-confirmed MVI beyond both (a) a current static laboratory state and (b) simple interpretable longitudinal summaries. The exact repaired hypothesis is:

> Among adults undergoing their first recorded HCC resection, with one qualifying resection calendar day in the pathology-linked encounter, no recorded HCC-directed procedure or high-specificity antitumor medication in days −180 through −1, and at least two strict numeric laboratory days in days −90 through −1, a value-sequence model will improve locked 2020–2021 prediction of M1/M2 versus M0 over a matched transparent trajectory model containing latest/earliest values, trends, extrema, variability, span, and prespecified cross-marker interactions; the gain will attenuate when within-patient value order is destroyed and will not depend on observation-process inputs.

This remains clinically consequential as immediate pre-resection risk stratification and postoperative risk counseling. It does **not** establish that the prediction was available when anatomic extent, margins, or neoadjuvant treatment were chosen: multidisciplinary decision, consent, and scheduling timestamps are unavailable. A plan-time decision-support claim therefore requires another study.

The leading explanation is that coordinated temporal value structure across tumor markers, liver injury, synthetic function, coagulation, and inflammation carries information not represented by transparent univariate trends and extrema. The strongest rivals are ordinary monotone trends/peaks, static severity, tumor burden, prior treatment, selective repeat testing, administrative index timing, report hindsight, assay/era drift, and pathology template/sampling drift. Association after adjustment cannot identify invasive biology.

## Observed audit and smallest repair

The attached aggregate audit read pathology, procedures, encounters, labs, examinations, and medications from snapshot `[source checksum]`.

Observed findings:

- Of 10,353 adults linked to a first recorded qualifying resection, 9,401 (90.8%) procedure starts were exactly 00:00. The reliable index is therefore the **resection calendar day**, not an intra-day operative timestamp.
- Pathology has no timestamp or specimen/procedure identifier. Eighty linked encounters had qualifying resections on more than one calendar day; same-encounter linkage alone cannot uniquely assign pathology in these cases. They are excluded pending adjudication. Multiple component procedure names/times on one calendar day are collapsed as one operative episode rather than excluding 9,002 likely duplicate/component records.
- Among serial patients, nominally preoperative examination rows included postoperative language for 1,668 patients, pathology language for 114, and explicit MVI language for 4. The examination field `开始时间` is not a verified report-finalization clock. All report text is therefore removed from primary predictors; a report-derived benchmark cannot rescue or support the primary claim.
- The procedure screen found 182 patients with recorded directed treatment in days −180:−1. A high-specificity medication-name screen found 54, including 22 not found by procedures. The repaired primary exclusion uses their union. Medication indication remains unavailable.
- Repaired eligibility—single resection calendar day, first recorded resection, no directed procedure or high-specificity antitumor medication in −180:−1, and two numeric lab days—retains 5,153 adults and 1,927 M1/M2 outcomes. Support remains train 2014–2018: 2,184/924 positive; validation 2019: 985/459; locked test 2020–2021: 1,352/503. Thus the repair does not create a sparse endpoint.
- In the broad serial audit, 2,135/5,372 patients had a lab on day −1. The cutoff below uses the start of the resection calendar day, so every eligible lab date is strictly earlier despite unreliable operation time.

These aggregates establish feasibility and expose timing risks. They do not validate labels, prove prior-treatment absence, or support the hypothesis.

## Population, clocks, and outcome

Index episode is the earliest recorded qualifying liver-resection **calendar day** per `患者主索引`, in the same `(患者主索引, 就诊号)` encounter as explicit HCC pathology and exactly one MVI grade parsed as the first `M0|M1|M2` after `MVI提示风险分级:`. A qualifying procedure name contains liver and resection and excludes transplant/donor, biopsy/puncture, drainage, abscess/cyst, and isolated cholecystectomy terms. Exact duplicate rows and component rows on the same calendar day are collapsed. Exclude an encounter with qualifying resections on more than one calendar day.

Include age ≥18 and index years 2014–2021. Exclude missing procedure date, encounter-level conflicting MVI grades, no explicit grade, any earlier recorded qualifying resection, and any recorded directed treatment in calendar days −180 through −1. Directed treatment is the union of procedure names matching embolization/intervention, ablation/radiofrequency/microwave, infusion/chemotherapy/radiotherapy, or targeted therapy and high-specificity medication names/classes for systemic antitumor therapy. Freeze the dictionaries before fitting and publish all matched names for clinical review. “No recorded prior treatment” is not lifetime treatment-naive and does not cover outside care.

Define `Tcut` as 00:00 at the start of the index resection calendar day. Eligible events satisfy `Tcut−90 days <= 检验时间 < Tcut`, i.e. calendar days −90 through −1. Same-calendar-day surgery labs and all postoperative data are prohibited. Require strict numeric results on at least two distinct dates. The source does not distinguish collection, result, and release time; using only prior calendar dates prevents post-resection leakage but cannot prove real-time availability at an earlier planning decision.

Primary outcome is pathology M1/M2 versus M0. Secondary is ordered M0/M1/M2; M2 versus M0/M1 is exploratory. MVI is not recurrence, survival, mechanism, or treatment benefit.

Freeze temporal sets: 2014–2018 training; 2019 validation/model selection; 2020–2021 locked primary test. Report but do not fit 2022–2023 (near-empty). Use 2024–2025 only as reporting-drift stress evidence; it cannot rescue primary failure. No patient crosses sets.

## Matched methods

All primary models use identical patients, cutoff, labels, demographic variables, exact lab labels, and training-frozen preprocessing. Strict numeric parsing accepts only one unqualified number; inequalities remain missing/ambiguous. Collapse exact duplicates; same-label/same-timestamp numeric duplicates use the median. Never pool different labels by value. Require each modeled label in ≥100 training patients. Use within-label/year training-era robust ranks plus frozen scaling; annual raw distributions and abrupt discontinuities are mandatory drift outputs.

### B0: current-state baseline

Elastic-net logistic regression with age, sex, service, calendar year, latest value per supported label, age of latest value, missing indicators, number of measurement dates, total rows, and span. Prespecified clinical variables (AFP, PIVKA-II, platelets, albumin, bilirubin, ALT, AST, INR/PT, GGT, ALP, WBC, hemoglobin, creatinine) receive restricted cubic splines. B0 answers whether any history is needed beyond current state and observation amount.

### Btraj: matched transparent trajectory comparator

Elastic-net logistic regression/GAM on all B0 inputs plus, for every supported exact label: earliest, minimum, maximum, median, mean, standard deviation, range, robust Theil–Sen slope versus days-to-index, time of minimum/maximum, trapezoidal time-weighted mean, largest absolute adjacent change, and label-specific observation count/span. Prespecified cross-marker features are latest-value and slope/extrema interactions for AFP–PIVKA-II, ALT–AST, albumin–bilirubin, INR–platelets, and GGT–ALP. All transforms, knots, interactions, winsorization, and penalties are training-frozen.

Btraj captures ordinary direction, peaks, troughs, variability, timing of extrema, and clinically interpretable co-trends. It is the primary comparator for a **learned value-structure** claim. If Btraj improves over B0 but the learned model does not improve over Btraj, the useful result is transparent trends—not learned ordering.

### T-value and T-full

Fit the parent’s modest masked transformer (≤4 layers, width ≤128, ≤8 heads) to exact lab-label ID, robustly scaled value, relative calendar day, and panel/day ID, with static age/sex/service/year added after pooling. Cap at 512 tokens with deterministic day-stratified retention preserving the last value and earlier dates. Five fixed seeds; class-weighted binary cross-entropy; validation-only early stopping.

- `T-value`: values, labels, relative day/order; observation gaps, event counts, and panel intensity masked to constants.
- `T-full`: adds gaps, panel co-measurement, and observation intensity.

The primary contrast is `T-value − Btraj`, not `T-value − B0`. `T-full − T-value` estimates care/measurement-process information and never supports tumor-biology language.

A multivariate latent trajectory model remains deferred: without units/platforms and with sparse irregular labels, its factors would be less reliably identified. Revisit only if T-value beats Btraj and factor recovery is demonstrated in simulation. CPU is appropriate for B0/Btraj; one allocated A100 is reasonable for five-seed T models, but GPU use is not merit.

## Evaluation and falsification

On the locked 2020–2021 test, report paired AUROC, AUPRC, log loss, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, calibration-in-the-large, and decision-curve net benefit at risks 0.20/0.30/0.40/0.50. Use 2,000 patient bootstrap replicates for paired 95% intervals. Report validation-selected 80%-sensitivity threshold performance and subgroup calibration by sex, age <65/≥65, service, and year when ≥20 events. Tumor-size subgroups are unavailable leakage-safely and must not be fabricated.

Support for learned temporal value structure requires all of:

1. `T-value − Btraj` test AUROC ≥0.02 with paired 95% interval excluding 0 and Brier improvement ≥0.01 with interval excluding 0;
2. calibration slope 0.8–1.2 after validation-frozen recalibration and positive net benefit versus Btraj at ≥2 adjacent thresholds without material harm elsewhere;
3. no direction reversal in validation versus test or 2020 versus 2021;
4. within-patient value-order permutation, preserving each label’s values, dates/counts, latest value, extrema, and Btraj summaries, removes at least half the Brier gain;
5. T-full-only gain is not used as support.

Mandatory checks:

- Compare B0, Btraj, T-value, and T-full on identical patients. Publish paired differences for every step.
- Run order destruction engineered to preserve Btraj summaries; a simple random permutation that changes slopes/extrema is insufficient.
- Shift `Tcut` to seven calendar days before resection. Because this is only a proxy for planning time, persistence corroborates earlier availability; failure confines utility to immediate pre-resection care.
- Fit demographics/observation-only and labs-only controls. Report text is never added to primary models.
- Repeat after excluding any recorded directed treatment at any prior institutional time, exact-label-only restriction, same-day panel collapse, and exclusion of all encounters with multiple procedure times (overstrict linkage sensitivity).
- Audit outcome-label permutation, feature timestamps, split isolation, annual prevalence/calibration/raw value distributions, and 2024–2025 template drift.
- Before fitting, a hepatopathologist blinded to predictors reviews 200 stratified encounters (M0/M1/M2, multiple-procedure components, conflicts, and era-shift cases). Require ≥95% overall grade accuracy, ≥90% per era/grade stratum, and explicit judgment that pathology plausibly belongs to the one-day resection episode. Failure is inconclusive, not a model result.
- A clinician reviews the complete frozen treatment dictionaries and a stratified sample of medication/procedure matches and nonmatches. This bounds false exclusions but cannot recover outside care.

Adverse evidence is a precise T-value result excluding material gain over Btraj, T-value matching Btraj while Btraj beats B0, gain surviving summary-preserving order destruction, T-full-only gain, or temporal miscalibration/reversal. These favor transparent trends, static state, observation practice, or drift.

Inconclusive evidence includes broad intervals, adjudication failure, abrupt assay shifts, unstable seeds, failed overlap/calibration, result confined to day −1 through −6, or material dependence on ambiguous linkage/treatment definitions. An imprecise null does not refute biology.

A supportive result establishes internal, temporally held-out predictive value of learned pre-resection laboratory ordering beyond specified transparent summaries. It does not establish independence from tumor burden, because leakage-safe report timing is unavailable; it does not identify a mechanism, justify altered surgery, prove treatment benefit, or establish external validity. Those require timestamped finalized imaging, decision clocks, standardized assays, specimen linkage/sampling review, external prospective validation, and a decision-impact study.

## Solver deliverable, verifier boundary, and resources

The solver must newly produce the immutable cohort/exclusion flow; deduplicated operative-episode table; treatment dictionary and match audit; pathology adjudication manifest; frozen preprocessing; B0/Btraj/T-value/T-full fits with five seeds; validation records; locked predictions; paired uncertainty/calibration/net-benefit outputs; summary-preserving permutation and all sensitivities; drift tables; and `claims.json` mapping conclusions to supportive/adverse/inconclusive rules.

The verifier can check source hashes, columns, joins, calendar clocks, treatment union, one-resection-day rule, no exam/pathology/post-cutoff features, split isolation, training-only preprocessing, engineered-summary parity, permutation invariants, metric arithmetic, bootstrap pairing, and whether conclusions follow computed rules. It must reject a correct computation paired with claims of causality, tumor-burden independence, plan-time utility, treatment benefit, or external validity. It cannot establish pathology adequacy, outside treatment, medication indication, assay equivalence, clinical decision timing, or specimen linkage.

Measured audits completed in approximately 43–68 seconds on 4 CPUs/32 GiB. Planned full run: preprocessing/B0/Btraj on 8–16 CPUs and ≤64 GiB for 1–2 hours; five-seed T-value/T-full on one allocated A100 80 GiB plus 8 CPUs/64 GiB for 3–6 hours; bootstrap on 16 CPUs for 1–2 hours. Neural estimates are unverified but fit the solver envelope. Use `cuda:0` only inside an allocated job.

## Alternatives and revisit triggers

- B0 is retained to quantify the total value of history.
- Btraj is mandatory and selected as the attribution baseline; it exposes interpretable trends/extrema/interactions that B0 loses.
- T-value is selected only to test residual ordering/nonlinear co-evolution beyond Btraj.
- T-full is a care-process diagnostic, never biological evidence.
- AFP/size/margin benchmark is exploratory only: without report-finalization time it cannot enter the primary leakage-safe comparison. Revisit with timestamped finalized CT/MRI.
- Plan-time surgical utility is deferred until MDT/consent/scheduling clocks exist.
- Latent GP/state-space factors are deferred until stable sequence gain and assay harmonization exist.
- 2024–2025 fitting remains prohibited for primary confirmation.
