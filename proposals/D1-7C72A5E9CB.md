# Does the post-treatment AFP trajectory predict tumour progression, or merely the decision to re-image? A landmark, detection-controlled test in the HCC island

Island: hcc (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records; snapshot `[source checksum]`).
Episode 6. Child of `[prior hypothesis]` (top-mu valid candidate; mu 30.997).
Status: design + probe-verified bindings. RESULTS ARE NOT COMPUTED HERE (discovery only).
The event rate, imaging-flag validation and every HR/C-index are the SOLVER's outputs.

## 1. Unresolved question, opening and clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy for HCC (TACE / resection / ablation), clinics repeat serum panels at
follow-up, and the interval to the next re-imaging is one of the few levers they control. The parent
line asked whether the *within-patient trajectory* of AFP adds prognostic information beyond the
*pre-treatment baseline value*, and whether it adds anything beyond liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics
measured in the same draws. Its central unresolved objection, stated in its own critique, is the
**outcome and its circularity**: the endpoint was either a practice-pattern "time to retreatment"
proxy or an unvalidated lexical radiologic-progression flag, and AFP/ALBI are themselves inputs to the
retreat/re-image decision. A trajectory that "predicts progression" may therefore be predicting the
*decision to look* (detection/surveillance intensity) rather than *tumour biology* — exactly the
time-dependent/attenuation warning in [K2].

**Opening:** the `examinations` table has ~1.7M dated reports carrying progression/recurrence
descriptors (probe: within the first 400k rows, 转移 25,148, 增大 35,752, 复发 5,031, 进展 3,646,
新发 867; 399,719/400,000 rows dated). Because the table also contains non-imaging exams
(常规心电图, 住院肺功能, 手术标本检查与诊断...) a progression flag MUST be restricted to abdominal
liver imaging modalities — a concrete, checkable specification the parent left loose.

**Clinical importance:** if the AFP slope carries information about radiologic progression that is
independent of (a) baseline AFP, (b) contemporaneous ALBI kinetics and (c) the re-imaging decision
itself, tailored AFP-triggered re-imaging is justified. If the association collapses under a
detection control, AFP-only monitoring should be de-emphasised and intervals tied to liver function.
Either outcome changes a surveillance-interval decision; a clean null is useful.

## 2. Substantive advance over the parent and over [K1]-[K3]

- Over the **parent** (this is the required advance): add a pre-specified **detection-process
  negative control**. The same landmark exposures are fit against a *control outcome* = time from t*
  to the next liver-imaging exam REGARDLESS of its result, and the progression-vs-control contrast is
  the discriminator (§5). The parent had no such control, so it could not separate biology from the
  decision to look.
- Over [K1] (trajectory-based HCC biomarker model): K1 integrates trajectories into a prognostic
  model but does not adjudicate a detection/surveillance rival; we test whether the trajectory
  signal survives one.
- Over [K2] (landmark/time-dependent methodology): we apply the caution to a concrete HCC
  surveillance decision rather than assert it.
- Over [K3] (AFP+ALBI combined scoring): K3 shows AFP and ALBI jointly track outcome but does not
  separate a tumour-burden marker from a liver-reserve marker measured in the same draws, nor
  address who gets re-imaged; our same-draw head-to-head with a detection control does.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, index, landmark (inherited, not re-litigated)

- Unit: patient `患者主索引`; encounter key `就诊号`.
- Population: documented HCC in `diagnoses` (`诊断名称` matching 肝细胞癌 / 原发性肝癌 terms) AND >=1
  liver-directed procedure in `procedures` (`手术` in {TACE, 肝切除/肝癌切除, 消融/射频, 微波,...},
  parseable `开始时间`). Index = earliest such procedure `开始时间`; restrict to the observed data
  range (approx. 2010-07-29 .. 2026-01-01).
- Landmark t* = index + 180 days (90-day landmark is a sensitivity variant). Exposure slopes fit
  ONLY on same-draw pairs in (index, t*]; follow-up starts at t*; censoring at last `出院时间`.
  This removes immortal-time/lead-time bias and future-draw leakage.
- Trajectory cohort: >=1 same-draw AFP+albumin+total-bilirubin pair (matched within +/-2 days on
  `检验时间`) in (index, t*]. Endpoint cohort: uncensored at t* with >=1 dated post-landmark
  liver-imaging exam.

## 5. Estimand, comparator, detection control, falsification

- **M0 (simple baseline):** Cox on baseline AFP + ALBI + age/sex/treatment type + imaging intensity.
- **M4 (substantive alternative):** joint linear-mixed model for AFP and ALBI (random slopes) over
  (index, t*]; the fitted per-patient AFP slope and ALBI slope enter a landmark Cox. The alternative
  reveals change-vs-level information and separates tumour burden (AFP) from liver reserve (ALBI)
  that M0 loses; it is a small tabular fit (CPU-feasible).
- **Primary outcome P:** time from t* to first post-landmark liver-imaging exam whose text carries a
  progression descriptor (the lexical flag is UNVALIDATED — §6).
- **Control outcome C:** time from t* to the next liver-imaging exam regardless of result.
- **Key contrast (the discriminator):** estimate the AFP-slope (and ALBI-slope) HR for P and for C.
  *Detection/surveillance rival predicts* AFP slope → C strongly, and the P association attenuating
  toward 1 after adjusting for imaging intensity. *Tumour-biology claim predicts* AFP slope → P, with
  the P association surviving imaging-intensity adjustment and the scheduled-vs-triggered contrast.
- **Scheduled-vs-triggered proxy:** classify each post-landmark imaging exam as "scheduled" (interval
  close to that patient's running median inter-exam interval) vs "triggered" (short gap after an
  abnormal/sampled AFP draw); test whether the AFP-slope signal is confined to triggered imaging.
- **Falsification (conjunctive).** Declare NO independent trajectory signal if ALL hold: (i) both
  M4 slope HR CIs for P include 1; (ii) bootstrap 95% CI of held-out delta-C-index (M4 over M0 for P)
  lies within ±0.01; (iii) the AFP-slope→C HR is materially larger than the AFP-slope→P HR OR the P
  association vanishes after imaging-intensity adjustment. Supportive requires (i)/(ii) to fail in the
  direction of a positive C-index gain AND the P association to survive the detection control.

## 6. Essential adjudication / unavailable evidence (kept explicit)

- The imaging progression flag is a **lexical extraction, not validated**: metadata.json validates
  only a cirrhosis lexical-context detector. Pre-registered sub-step: manual audit of a deterministic
  sample of flagged/unflagged liver-imaging reports for precision/recall, kappa vs the retreatment
  proxy and vs `diagnoses` recurrence text, with a pre-set agreement floor; if the floor fails, fall
  back EXPLICITLY to the retreatment proxy rather than silently substituting.
- Mortality and mRECIST PFS are **unascertainable** in this source (no registry, out-of-hospital
  deaths); no survival claim is made. Radiologist adjudication of progression, external validity and
  causal interpretation require expert review / another study — an automatic verifier cannot establish them.
- Single institution; no lab units; no outcomes computed in discovery.

## 7. Method-exploration record (baseline vs alternative)

Simple baseline M0 (baseline-value-only Cox) and substantive alternative M4 (joint mixed-effects
AFP+ALBI slopes → landmark Cox) are both specified before selection (§5) on the same question,
population and splits; the alternative is justified by the change-vs-level and AFP-vs-liver-reserve
information it recovers. Selected design = M0 vs M4 with the detection control layered on both. No GPU
work is implied (small tabular fits); a bounded GPU probe is unnecessary and would not change the question.
