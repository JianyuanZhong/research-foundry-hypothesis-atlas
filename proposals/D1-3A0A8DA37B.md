# A discharge-boundary decomposition of pulmonary-nodule handoff failure, with observation, death, and outside-care unknownness kept separate

## Decision question and substantive advance

A radiologist's incidental pulmonary-nodule recommendation can fail through different mechanisms that imply different interventions. The recommendation may not be finalized/available by departure, a canonical discharge artifact may be stored only later, an artifact stored by the boundary may omit or distort the interval-specific plan, or the plan may be faithful while subsequent care is simply unobservable in MIMIC. These states must not be collapsed into “follow-up failure.” The clinically consequential decision is whether a prospective workflow should first improve pre-discharge finalization/availability, discharge-artifact content reconciliation, or downstream tracking/cross-system capture.

Published evidence supports a communication and tracking problem, but not that discharge text causes surveillance or improves cancer outcomes. The unresolved, falsifiable claim here is narrower: among all first eligible pre-discharge recommendation opportunities, a prespecified all-opportunity state decomposition can distinguish recommendation finalization/availability, artifact storage timing, faithful content, observation, death, and outside-care unknownness; and one of the process gaps may dominate by a prespecified margin. `storetime` is an observed storage timestamp only: it is not signature, bedside visibility, transmission, patient receipt, or departure-time availability.

The substantive repair over the parent is an executable hierarchy. The denominator `N` is frozen after eligibility and pre-discharge recommendation-state adjudication but before reading discharge text, discharge storage timing, or later trace. Primary states are assigned over all `N`, not in a post-storage subset. Downstream follow-up is a separate all-`N` competing-observation outcome, not evidence that the discharge state caused care.

## Population and pre-discharge recommendation state

Unit is the first eligible recommendation admission per subject. Include adults age >=18, using `anchor_age + year(admittime)-anchor_year` and treating represented age 91 as 90+, with a CT/CTA chest (or lung-base coverage when explicitly supported) linked by `(subject_id,hadm_id)` and `admittime-12h <= charttime <= dischtime`. Repeat with zero-hour and ED-boundary sensitivities. Require a patient-specific, unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months after an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity. Exclude screening, nodule surveillance already underway, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, explicit no-follow-up, conditional/optional recommendations, hospice/comfort-only transition, and in-hospital death. Do not require an unavailable guideline truth such as pack-years or radiographic appropriateness; record those as unknown.

Reconstruct report/addendum chains only from reciprocal, subject- and admission-consistent detail links. At `D=dischtime`, a component qualifies as pre-discharge knowledge only when `charttime <= D` and `storetime <= D`, or when a blinded reviewer explicitly marks event-time uncertain. A recommendation first introduced or materially changed after `D` is not an eligible opportunity; it remains in the screening cascade. Missing nodes, contradictory links, missing times, and charttime/storetime conflicts form an unresolved recommendation-state category `U_R`, never an omission or availability failure. Two independent radiology-trained reviewers adjudicate eligibility, CT coverage, incidental status, unconditionality, interval, addendum meaning, and timestamp uncertainty using only information available through `D`, blinded to discharge content and later imaging. Select the first eligible admission only after adjudication, using base charttime, storetime, note_id as deterministic ties.

Freeze `N` before any discharge text/timing, later trace, CT outcome, or death information is inspected. Report the complete cascade: all lexical candidates, metadata-linked candidates, exclusions, late-addendum candidates, `U_R`, and final `N`.

## Primary discharge-boundary decomposition

Use the sole DS row for each `(subject_id,hadm_id)` as the canonical artifact; if a future source refresh has multiple rows, stop and lock a new version rule. The primary state is assigned over all `N`:

* `F`: canonical DS stored by `D` (`storetime <= D`), text classifiable, and faithful: patient-specific nodule plus chest/thoracic CT action plus exact interval/date compatible with the radiology recommendation.
* `C`: canonical DS stored by `D`, classifiable, but content not faithful. Subclass `C_O` (no patient-specific actionable plan) and `C_I` (nodule/action/timing incomplete or discordant without an explicit alternate resolution). Copied/negated/historical/generic text is not faithful.
* `R`: canonical DS stored by `D` and an explicit documented alternate resolution (for example refusal/preference, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, or scheduled outside follow-up). It is neither faithful success nor content failure; documentation is assessed, not appropriateness.
* `A`: canonical DS exists but is not observed stored by `D` (`storetime > D`), with known timing. This is a storage-delay signal, not proof of failed finalization, invisibility, or communication failure. At +24h and +7d, report transition to later `F/C/R` as a delayed-artifact descriptive matrix without retroactively changing `A`.
* `U_D`: no DS row, missing DS storetime, unreadable/contradictory linkage, or unclassifiable text. It remains unknown, not `A`, omission, or discordance.

`F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D` are mutually exclusive and exhaustive over `N`. Report the full multinomial vector and `p_A - p_C`, with joint confidence intervals and partial-identification bounds when `U_R` or `U_D` is unresolved. The conditional content distribution among stored-by-discharge artifacts is secondary and must not replace the all-opportunity denominator. Record chronology markers for report chart/store time, recommendation-introducing component chart/store time, DS chart/store time, and stale-artifact status. A DS stored before the recommendation became available is coded stale rather than blamed for content omission; a DS stored after `D` remains `A` regardless of later faithfulness. `charttime` never substitutes for storage/visibility.

The decision-facing process test is operational, not a clinical-quality threshold: predeclare a margin `m=0.05` and test whether the lower 95% bound for `p_A-p_C` exceeds `m` (availability/storage-first) or the upper bound is below `-m` (content-first). If neither holds, do not select a mechanism. Require >=200 adjudicated `N`, >=20 `A` and `C` events where applicable, `U_D<=5%`, reviewer agreement/raw and AC1 gates, and simultaneous interval width <0.10 before mechanism prioritization. Otherwise report descriptive feasibility only. The margin is a workflow-prioritization rule and must not be presented as a validated quality benchmark.

## Secondary all-opportunity observation outcome

For every `N`, compute due date as index report `charttime +` 3/6/12 calendar months. Start the all-`N` observation clock at `D`; report an artifact-timed sensitivity using `max(D, DS.storetime)` without changing `N`. Define mutually exclusive states by due date plus a fixed 42-day window, preserving timestamp ambiguity:

1. same-system timely chest CT/CTA before an interval-certain death;
2. death before the window ends with no prior timely CT;
3. no timely CT/death but a later MIMIC radiology or admission trace after the window (continued observation without timely outcome);
4. no later trace, hence outside-care/loss-of-capture unknown;
5. CT/death ordering indeterminate when date-only/coarsened times overlap.

`dod` is interval-censored to `[dod 00:00,dod+1 day)` unless a more precise death time exists; do not claim death preceded a CT when intervals overlap. In-hospital death is a competing event, not failed surveillance. State 4 is not nonadherence and state 3 is not proof of adherence. Report the joint state vector by each primary discharge state, but do not condition the primary process estimand or follow-up estimand on later trace. Any inverse-probability-of-observation analysis is sensitivity-only under explicit, unverifiable missing-at-random assumptions. Outside care, orders, referral, patient receipt, and appointments are unavailable.

## Falsification and analysis

Dual adjudication is required for eligibility, faithful content, alternate resolution, nodule-focused CT, and death/CT time ordering. Report raw agreement, kappa/AC1 and uncertainty. Validate lexical-screen sensitivity on adjudicated cases; if screening misses candidates, no adjusted result is allowed. Primary analysis is descriptive multinomial risks with exact/Wilson or simultaneous bootstrap intervals. Adjusted faithful-content versus omission associations with same-system timely CT are exploratory only, using fixed pre-outcome covariates from patients, admissions, diagnoses/dictionary, services, transfers, and ICU tables, and only after overlap/event gates. It is an association, not an intervention effect.

Required negative controls are pre-index chest CT, non-chest imaging in the due window, continued-trace state, acute-admission versus nodule-focused CT, and exact versus 0/12-hour linkage. A control association as large as the purported handoff association, a signal confined to acute/non-nodule CT, strong differential observation, or reversal under timestamp bounds makes the downstream result inconclusive. Supportive results require a stable process mechanism across exact/conservative time rules, adequate adjudication, and no control explanation; they justify a prospective timestamp-crosswalk and workflow study only. Adverse results mean storage delay/content failure is low or alternate resolutions dominate, redirecting work to tracking, scheduling, patient communication, or cross-system exchange. Inconclusive results include sparse adjudicated `N`, unresolved chains, agreement failure, timing reversal, poor overlap/events, or unknownness bounds that cross the margin.
