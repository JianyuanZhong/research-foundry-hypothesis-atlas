# Feasibility-governed actionability and supersession audit for the frozen pulmonary discharge handoff

## Assignment, parents, and scope of the repair

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]` (primary) and `[prior hypothesis]` (correlated-error and independent-challenge anchor). It does not replace their scientific schema. The following are frozen and may not be changed by the compiler, panels, or observed results: the first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity denominator; reciprocal pre-discharge radiology chronology; one canonical discharge artifact; exact target atom and conservative admission-level routing; route-concealed split panels and the independent holistic challenge; positive-probability whole-admission sampling; all-unit adverse precedence; finite-population adverse inference; additive workload accounting; and the prospective-silent-only conclusion ceiling.

The repair is limited to three executable gaps. First, actionability and supersession labels are made operationally exact, including precedence when text is incomplete or contradictory. Second, governance thresholds are selected and frozen by a source-bound, label-blind feasibility procedure rather than chosen after seeing panel labels. Third, the packet and reviewer ledger is additive and auditable, with a deterministic allocation that can never exceed 900 distinct admissions or the approved reviewer-hour envelope. A failure of feasibility is an honest `INCONCLUSIVE` result; it cannot be repaired by dropping difficult admissions, reducing controls, or pooling domains.

## Clinical decision question and falsifiable hypothesis

A pulmonary imaging recommendation can be reproducible in stored text yet still be unsafe to place on an automatic handoff path if it lacks a target-linked action or interval, or if a later discharge plan changes or conflicts with it. The available records cannot show that a recommendation was viewed, transmitted, assigned to a responsible clinician, followed, or beneficial. They can test a narrower prerequisite for a prospective silent workflow:

> Among all frozen first-eligible pulmonary recommendation opportunities, does the inherited admission-level route select only routes independently compatible with (i) documentation sufficient to act on and (ii) an explicit, target-linked discharge plan that confirms, supersedes, or conflicts with the recommendation, while preserving adverse detection of unknown and minority-unit failures?

The prespecified hypothesis is:

> **H1.** Relative to the inherited selector, the feasibility-governed selector that allows `AUTO` only for fully actionable, available, non-superseded, non-conflicted units, uses `RECON` for actionable competing or superseded plans, and uses `DEFER` for unknown/unavailable/unsafe units has a lower adverse-compatible excess-`AUTO` rate and at least the preregistered route-admissibility/capture margins in every claimable temporal and terminal-service domain.

This is falsifiable by route-concealed independent labels and held-out controls. It is not a claim of guideline concordance, clinical appropriateness, delivery, safety, benefit, or causality.

The strongest claim supported by the local snapshot is only that admission-linked stored documents, their storage times, service transitions, and structured detail rows can be assembled into a reproducible retrospective information-boundary experiment. The unresolved claim is whether an emitted route is documentation-operationally admissible under independently defined actionability and supersession states. Resolving delivery, appropriateness, responsibility, patient communication, safety, completion, benefit, or a causal effect requires evidence absent from MIMIC and a prospective study.

## Canonical packet and information boundary

For every selected admission, create immutable append-only packets at `D-24h`, `D-6h`, `D-1h`, `D`, `D+1h`, and `D+24h`. A row is visible at cutoff `c` exactly when its required nonmissing `storetime<=c`; the `D` packet alone defines the primary estimand. Post-`D` packets are delayed-arrival stress tests and never relabel a primary state. Include the pseudonymous admission key, `D`, cutoff, source note IDs, source storetimes, ordered detail rows, reciprocal chain, exact atom spans/components, terminal service, `G`, state flags, and a cryptographic hash of the unredacted canonical artifact.

Packet feasibility is a declared state, not an undocumented preprocessing decision. A selected admission is `PACKET_FEASIBLE` only if all required source rows are read, identity-joinable, time-parsable, deterministically serialized, and renderable into each assigned role-specific view without truncation. A missing/late/duplicate-conflict/malformed/unreadable component is retained with its exact state and is `PACKET_UNKNOWN` or `DEFER` for the affected endpoint. It is never complete-case deleted. Text is never silently shortened; if a configured renderer cannot represent the complete artifact, the packet is infeasible and the endpoint is unknown. Automated packet construction time is recorded separately from human review time.

The actionability panel sees the reciprocal radiology chain and its detail reconstruction plus only the inherited permitted admission/disposition fields. It does not see DS text, emitted route, sampling stratum, domain, another panel, workload, or post-discharge evidence. The supersession panel sees the same frozen radiology atom view and the canonical DS/ordered DS-detail view; it does not see actionability labels, route, domain, sampling, workload, or outcomes. The independent holistic challenge and all inherited split-view panels remain route-concealed, roster-disjoint, and sealed before joins. Reviewer identity, packet hashes, view assignment, labels, review times, and adjudicator decisions are append-only and released only after all target and sample freezes.

## Exact actionability rubric and precedence

The rubric is fixed before panel assignment. Every inherited recommendation unit receives one state from each independent reviewer and one adjudicated state if needed. Reviewers may record source note ID, field name, exact character/span offsets, atom components, and reason codes only. They may not infer lesion size, malignancy risk, smoking, life expectancy, guideline appropriateness, patient preference, responsibility, or what should have happened.

`A_ACTIONABLE` requires all of the following in one frozen, available reciprocal evidence set: (1) an explicit target/lesion identity linked to the inherited `E`; (2) an explicit modality or exam; (3) an unconditional action (for example obtain, repeat, or follow the specified exam); (4) an explicit due interval or date that parses to the inherited `W`; (5) no unresolved negation, hypothetical/history-only language, optional/conditional language, or unit boundary ambiguity; and (6) no contradiction among reciprocal report components or their structured detail. The exact interval/date need not be clinically appropriate; it must be explicit and internally consistent with the inherited atom. If any required component is absent, late, conflicting, unparseable, or unavailable, the reviewer must not convert it to `A_NONACTIONABLE`; the result is `A_UNKNOWN`.

`A_NONACTIONABLE` is reserved for an explicit, source-available text state that is definitively not an actionable recommendation under the frozen dictionary: explicit negation of follow-up; an explicit statement that no follow-up is needed; purely historical or hypothetical language; an explicitly optional/conditional recommendation where the condition is not met or cannot be established from the permitted view; or a unit failing a preregistered atom component with no unresolved source/interpretation issue. `A_UNKNOWN` covers missing/late/conflicting source, unresolved coreference or negation, unreadable text, uncertain unit boundary, or an unresolvable reviewer disagreement. A disagreement remains `U_A` until adjudication; after adjudication an unresolvable case is adverse-compatible `A_UNKNOWN` and cannot support `AUTO`.

A reviewer must use a fixed precedence when multiple spans occur within one unit: explicit negation of the same target/action dominates a positive span only when the text establishes that the negation applies to the same target and episode; otherwise the unit is `A_UNKNOWN`; a later explicit correction in the same reciprocal chain dominates an earlier draft only when identity and ordering are established; otherwise contradiction is `A_UNKNOWN`. No reviewer may use a later packet to repair the `D` state.

## Exact supersession rubric and precedence

A separate, independently staffed pair reviews the frozen radiology atom and canonical DS. Supersession is a documentation relation, not a clinical-quality judgment. Every unit receives exactly one final state.

* `S_CONFIRM`: the DS explicitly repeats the same target, modality, and due interval/date, or explicitly links its unchanged plan to the radiology recommendation. Equivalent date/interval representations are normalized by a frozen calendar/interval function; month-end behavior and date arithmetic are fixed in code before labels.
* `S_NONE`: a primary DS is available and the panel finds no explicit competing, cancelling, replacing, or incompatible target-linked action in the DS. Generic language such as “follow up with your doctor,” a nonspecific problem-list mention, an absent paragraph, or absence of a DS cannot be upgraded to `S_CONFIRM` and cannot be treated as evidence of delivery. It is `S_NONE` only when the available DS was reviewed and the rubric found no explicit competing plan; unavailable evidence is `S_UNKNOWN`.
* `S_SUPERSEDES`: the DS explicitly cancels or replaces the radiology action, or explicitly changes its modality or due interval/date, **and** the changed action is linked to the same target by an extractable target span or unambiguous frozen atom identity. A mere mention of another disease or an unlinked plan is not supersession.
* `S_CONFLICT`: the DS and radiology contain contemporaneous incompatible actionable plans for the same target and no deterministic precedence can be established, or a purported change has target linkage but an unresolvable action/date contradiction. Conflict takes precedence over `S_NONE`.
* `S_UNKNOWN`: missing/late/duplicate/conflicting/unreadable DS or radiology, missing target linkage, unresolved coreference, unavailable source, or an unresolvable reviewer disagreement.

When several DS passages exist, the canonical DS text and ordered detail are one artifact. A clear explicit cancellation/replacement with target linkage is `S_SUPERSEDES`; two incompatible active instructions without a clear precedence are `S_CONFLICT`; a clear unchanged repetition is `S_CONFIRM`; otherwise the relevant unknown rule applies. A DS stored after `D` cannot produce a primary `S_CONFIRM`, `S_SUPERSEDES`, or `S_CONFLICT`, even if its charttime precedes `D`. Unknown and unresolved states are retained in compatible sets, with the adverse interpretation excluding `AUTO`.

## Locked route-admissibility estimand

Let `r_i` be the inherited route after all units and `G` are evaluated, and let `K_i` be the conservative set of routes independently compatible with all final panel states. The route selector itself is not altered by panel labels. Define `K_i` by the following deterministic rules.

* `AUTO` is in `K_i` only when every decision-relevant unit is `A_ACTIONABLE`, every required radiology source is available by `D`, the plan state is `S_CONFIRM` or `S_NONE`, no unit is `S_SUPERSEDES` or `S_CONFLICT`, the inherited timing clock is finite and `H>=24h`, and no target, chronology, contradiction, or packet state is unresolved.
* `RECON` is in `K_i` when at least one unit is actionable but an explicit superseding/competing plan, a resolvable mixed plan, or a panel discrepancy requires human reconciliation. `RECON` is not admissible for a fundamentally uninterpretable or unavailable atom unless the inherited route explicitly classifies that exact state as reconciliation rather than defer.
* `DEFER` is in `K_i` when any source availability, target/unit identity, timing, actionability, supersession, contradiction, or panel state is unknown/unresolved, or when an active unit is not safe to auto-forward. `TIMING` is reported as a `DEFER` subtype when insufficient `H` is the only established barrier.
* Whole-admission precedence is adverse: any unit that excludes `AUTO` excludes admission-level `AUTO`; any actionable competing/superseded unit requires `RECON` or `DEFER` according to the above rules; no clean unit rescues a problematic unit.

The primary estimand is the finite-population design-weighted proportion

`Theta_R = sum_i w_i I(r_i in K_i) / sum_i w_i`,

with weights fixed by the two-phase, positive-probability whole-admission design and with unknown panel states represented by adverse-compatible lower/upper bounds. Report `Theta_AUTO`, `Theta_RECON`, and `Theta_DEFER` components, excess automatic routing `P_w(r_i=AUTO and AUTO not in K_i)`, missed reconciliation `P_w(r_i in {DEFER,TIMING} and RECON in K_i)`, unknown/disagreement mass, panel nonresponse, packet infeasibility, and all-unit minority failures. Compare only under identical sample, weights, packets, and labels with the inherited selector and a non-tuning naive comparator that maps an exact actionable atom with an available canonical DS to `AUTO`, otherwise `DEFER`. No model, consensus rescue, neighboring-atom rescue, or pooled-domain rescue is permitted.

## Label-blind feasibility and threshold governance

All governance quantities below are operational nomination margins, not clinical safety thresholds. They are frozen before opening any panel label. To prevent a convenient threshold from being selected after seeing results, register the following finite tier table and deterministic choice rule before sampling:

| tier | lower bound for `Theta_R` | upper bound for excess `AUTO` | lower bound for adverse-case capture by `RECON/DEFER` | maximum panel unknown/disagreement | maximum workload multiplier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| strict | 0.95 | 0.02 | 0.98 | 0.03 | 1.25 |
| standard | 0.90 | 0.05 | 0.95 | 0.05 | 1.50 |
| exploratory | 0.85 | 0.10 | 0.90 | 0.10 | 2.00 |

The tier-selection procedure uses only the source-bound manifest and a governance worksheet, never a panel label, emitted route outcome, or post-`D` evidence. It is run once after the eligible opportunity census and before target/control/sample seals. For each tier, calculate whether the planned positive-probability allocation can provide its required denominator and event support in every predeclared temporal/service claim cell; whether the worst-case count of packets, duplicate reviews, adjudication reserve, and fixed controls fits the 900-admission ceiling; whether the complete source-bound views can be rendered without truncation; and whether source availability/unknown maxima make the tier logically impossible even if every reviewed label is adverse. Select the strictest tier whose label-blind feasibility predicates pass. If none passes, terminate with `INCONCLUSIVE` and do not inspect labels. The selected tier is written into the immutable manifest and cannot be relaxed after sampling or label release. The exploratory tier is not a clinical recommendation; it merely defines a possible silent-bridge audit if stricter finite-sample precision is impossible.

Within the selected tier, preregister the following additional gates, with the standard values shown above if standard is feasible: two-sided simultaneous 95% lower bound for `Theta_R` at least the tier value in the overall population and every supported temporal/service domain; simultaneous upper bound for excess `AUTO` no greater than the tier value; lower bound for capture of `S_SUPERSEDES`, `S_CONFLICT`, and `A_UNKNOWN` by `RECON/DEFER` at least the tier capture value where support permits; actionability and supersession unknown/disagreement mass no greater than the tier unknown value; no domain estimate more than 0.10 below pooled and no absolute excess-auto domain drift above 0.02; and all inherited route-purity, minority-unit, timing, support, serialization, domain-held-out, leakage, and control gates. These drift margins are fixed governance margins, not data-derived safety limits.

The panel rubric itself is not softened by tier. `A_ACTIONABLE` and `S_SUPERSEDES` always require the exact definitions above. Tier selection changes only the evidence required to nominate a route for a prospective silent validation. A route/domain with no positive support, census-only representation, sparse events, failed packet feasibility, or a wide adverse-compatible interval is `INCONCLUSIVE`, never rescued by a neighboring route or pooled estimate. `RECON`/`DEFER`-only nomination remains possible when `AUTO` fails, but no retrospective result authorizes automation.

## Exact additive sampling and reviewer/workload ledger

The unit of sampling is an admission, never a recommendation unit or reviewer task. Freeze the inherited five outcome-blind sampling strata, sentinel census, phase-1 and phase-2 inclusion probabilities, positive inclusion in every occupied `(sampling stratum, possible-calendar, terminal-service)` cell, fixed controls, and one charged reserve. Let `S` be the inherited set of selected distinct admissions after deterministic de-duplication; let `N=|S|`. The allocation algorithm is:

1. Freeze the source census and all occupied cells before labels.
2. Apply the inherited sentinel census and fixed phase-1/phase-2 probabilities.
3. Add the inherited fixed route-irrelevant, route-decisive, chronology, redaction, target-link, contradiction, minority-unit, and domain controls according to their already frozen positive-probability rules. Controls are admissions already in `S` whenever possible; a control never creates a second copy of an admission.
4. Add exactly one reserve admission per inherited ledger rule, assigned its prespecified probability and charge. Reserve is held sealed and is not substituted adaptively after a label is seen.
5. Sort by the frozen pseudorandom key and retain the first admissions under the hard admission cap. If the inherited allocation would exceed 900, the prelabel run is infeasible and ends `INCONCLUSIVE`; it may not lower a cell, delete controls, or sample units instead.

The ledger is an additive table with one row per distinct selected admission and columns `admission_id`, inherited sampling components, `sentinel`, `phase1`, `phase2`, `fixed_control_flags`, `reserve`, `p_i`, `w_i=1/p_i`, packet view count, actionability reviewer slots, supersession reviewer slots, inherited challenge slots, adjudication reserve slots, maximum minutes for each slot, actual minutes, nonresponse, and packet-feasibility state. Every selected admission is charged once for packet construction and once per assigned human slot, even when it is simultaneously a sentinel, control, or reserve. A duplicate admission is never charged as a second sampled unit.

To make the added burden auditable without assuming labels, set fixed time caps before sampling: actionability reviewer 1 and 2, 8 minutes each; actionability adjudicator reserve, 12 minutes; supersession reviewer 1 and 2, 10 minutes each; supersession adjudicator reserve, 15 minutes. Adjudicator reserve is charged for every selected admission as a worst-case budget, not only observed disagreements. Thus the added maximum is `N*(2*8+12+2*10+15)=63N` reviewer-minutes, or at most 945 physician-hours at `N=900`; if the approved inherited contract has a smaller remaining envelope, the label-blind feasibility worksheet computes `H_added_max=63N/60` and rejects any allocation whose `H_inherited_max + H_added_max` exceeds that envelope. The compiler must display both the inherited ledger charge and this additive charge; it may not hide added review in an existing slot. A governance-approved lower cap can be registered before sampling, but no cap may be changed after labels.

If the actual panel uses non-physician readers, their role and rate are declared before assignment and their hours are still recorded separately; no role substitution is inferred from the data. A timeout, refusal, or incomplete view is `NONRESPONSE` and adverse-compatible unknown, with its reserved minutes still charged. No selected admission is removed to improve workload. If the resulting total exceeds the approved contract, the result is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not a post hoc reduction of panels. Automated hash/serialization work is reported separately and does not masquerade as reviewer time.

The packet-level feasibility bound is likewise exact. Let `F_i=1` only if the complete `D` packet and all role-specific redacted views for admission `i` pass identity, time, serialization, hash, and render checks. The prelabel worksheet reports `sum_{i in S} 1`, `sum F_i`, and the maximum possible reviewer slots for all `N` selected admissions. Primary estimates retain `F_i=0` admissions with `A_UNKNOWN/S_UNKNOWN` states and corresponding weights. No packet is silently discarded. Post-`D` packet failure cannot alter the primary result.

## Independent panels, correlated-error protection, and controls

Actionability and supersession panels have separate reviewer rosters, separate adjudicators, separately randomized packet orders, and independently worded but presealed codebooks. The holistic challenge from the correlated-error parent remains roster-disjoint and whole-admission, with duplicate codebooks and a third layer where already specified. No reviewer sees another panel's labels, route, sampling probability, domain, workload, or outcomes. Routes are computed by locked code only after all panel seals; no reviewer votes a route.

Retain all inherited falsification controls: panel-label permutation; removal of target/action/interval spans; target-link swaps; DS-storetime shifts after `D`; injected changed modality/interval contradictions; precise radiology atom with no DS; one unknown/conflicting minority unit in a clean admission; late-arrival monotonicity; storetime isolation; field-order and cutoff permutation; domain-label permutation; roster collision; broken seal order; duplicate identity; and source-hash controls. The target-link swap must convert `S_SUPERSEDES` to `S_NONE` or `S_UNKNOWN`; the chronology shift must convert the primary supersession state to `S_UNKNOWN`; the minority-unit fixture must exclude whole-admission `AUTO`.

Add two prespecified control families. First, create synthetic/held-out fixtures by perturbing only one permitted span at a time: remove action, interval, modality, or target; replace a target with an unrelated target; change an interval while retaining all other words; and insert explicit negation. Expected states are registered before execution and must be exact. Second, estimate reviewer dependence without treating agreement as truth: duplicate cases are assigned to distinct rosters, report individual disagreement and compatible route sets, and compute adverse-compatible bounds under the inherited partial-identification/shared-error sensitivity grid. A latent-class or consensus model may be diagnostic only. It cannot narrow the primary interval or turn agreement into a gold standard without an independently justified prelabel identifiability constraint.

## Inference, baselines, and falsification criteria

Use the inherited Horvitz–Thompson totals and Hájek ratios with the known two-phase inclusion probabilities, admission-level clustering, nested Rao–Wu or equivalent design resampling, and simultaneous multiplicity control across pooled, temporal, service, route, actionability, supersession, and control endpoints. Preserve finite-population exact hypergeometric bounds for rare adverse events; zero observed events is not zero population risk. Unknown panel states are evaluated adversely for automatic routing and reported separately. All recommendation units in a sampled admission travel together and receive one admission weight.

The primary baseline is the inherited route under exactly the same frozen packets and target universe. Secondary baselines are the naive actionable-plus-DS-to-`AUTO` rule and the inherited pooled/domain gate. No baseline is tuned on panel labels. Report absolute addressable masses per 100 frozen opportunities, not only conditional percentages, so that a small but consequential supersession subgroup cannot disappear into a pooled denominator.

A supportive result requires the selected tier's simultaneous lower/upper bounds, positive support, packet feasibility, all inherited controls, domain gates, and additive ledger to pass; low adverse-compatible excess `AUTO`; and high capture of actionable competing/superseding/unknown cases by `RECON/DEFER`. An adverse result is excess `AUTO`, missed supersession or unknown units, low route admissibility, a domain/minority failure, leakage/chronology/control failure, correlated-error sensitivity that invalidates the bound, or an over-cap ledger. This falsifies the proposed route-selection rule or its claimable domain, not patient benefit or clinician performance. An inconclusive result is sparse support, census-only representation, wide exact bounds, packet/render failures, unresolved panel nonresponse/disagreement, source join failure, or inability to satisfy the label-blind feasibility contract. It is not converted to a favorable result by complete-case analysis or pooled averaging.

The verifier can recompute source hashes, archive extraction, joins, eligibility, reciprocal chronology, canonical DS, atom inventory, service/calendar domains, sample probabilities, packet checkpoints, seals, route output, `K_i`, weights, finite-population bounds, ledger, and all gates. It can detect leakage, wrong clocks, wrong threshold selection, wrong precedence, unsupported claims, and conclusions inconsistent with outputs. It cannot determine whether a reviewer correctly judged clinical appropriateness, whether a discharge plan was actually delivered or viewed, whether a clinician was responsible, whether follow-up occurred outside MIMIC, or whether any route caused benefit or harm.

## Interpretation and evidence required next

If supportive, the maximum conclusion is a site-local prospective silent validation nomination of the frozen documentation route, with actual report finalization, DS drafting/signing/transmission, recipient/responsibility, packet receipt/view/rendering, reconciliation, patient preference, downstream order/referral/scheduling, outside-plan capture, and follow-up completion logged prospectively. The retrospective result never permits a live alert, copied recommendation, order, referral, patient-facing instruction, or automated care.

Even a supportive `S_SUPERSEDES` label does not establish that the replacement plan was clinically better or appropriate. Independent clinical adjudication with richer context is needed for appropriateness; prospective workflow data are needed for delivery, viewing, responsibility, and completion; a governed comparative study is needed for safety, utility, benefit, or causality; and another site is needed for transport. The experiment's substantive advance is a source-bound, route-concealed, feasibility-governed distinction between actionable documentation, explicit supersession/conflict, and unresolved evidence, while preserving a conservative admission-level safety sentinel and refusing unsupported clinical claims.
