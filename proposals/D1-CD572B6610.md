# Age–recorded-sex documentary safety frontier for the report-robust fixed-capacity HCC queue

## 1. Targeted successor and single consequential defect

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that assessed-valid parent's adult first-eligible HCC resection population, source and operation alternatives, whole-accession CT/MRI units, 90-day acquisition window, 365-day recorded-prior-treatment exclusion, 24-hour confirmatory cutoff, 2015–2018/2019/2020/2021 temporal quarantine, same-encounter documentary-M2 endpoint, nine global reader-book pairs, locked B/R/G/Gmask pipelines, fixed K, exact patient-complete `q_req`, patient-set `q_dec`, comparator/random/anchored-deletion gates, and fail-closed evidence limits.

The remaining consequential defect is group-level decision safety. The parent can certify all of the following simultaneously:

- G captures more documentary-M2 cases overall than B, R, Gmask, and random review;
- the offered G patient set is unchanged from its same-world maximum-stored-content version; and
- `q_dec < 1`.

Those facts do not prevent G from capturing fewer documentary-M2 cases than a simpler comparator within women, older adults, or an age-by-recorded-sex intersection. Overall gain can conceal a group-specific loss. Queue identity protects patients against report-bundle missingness relative to the G oracle; it does not test whether the oracle or its preserved queue disadvantages a clinically recognizable group.

This child adds one outcome-only, no-refit safety frontier, `q_safe`. It asks whether the locked G queue retains the parent's overall and identity guarantees while losing no documentary-M2 captures to any inherited comparator in four predeclared age-by-recorded-sex cells. This is a finite-corpus documentary non-harm test, not proof of fairness, biological equivalence, treatment benefit, or equitable care.

- `M18_64`: `性别 = 男` and `18 ≤ 年龄 < 65`;
- `M65P`: `性别 = 男` and `年龄 ≥ 65`.

The boundary is age 65 at the index encounter. Do not infer age from dates or sex from names, diagnoses, notes, or treatment. Do not collapse, impute, or exclude blank, nonnumeric, nonbinary, or duplicate-discordant values. If source-consistent encounter alternatives assign different cells, cell membership travels with the full coherent source world. If any included patient cannot be assigned in a complete world, emit `demographic_resolution_inconclusive`; do not claim four-cell safety. This does not alter adult population membership or any model input.

The four cells are fixed before test-outcome inspection. No subgroup discovery, favorable regrouping, threshold movement, sex-only rescue, age-only rescue, or pooling across 2020 and 2021 is permitted. Sex-only and age-only aggregates may be reported as diagnostics because they are deterministic sums of the four cells, but they cannot rescue a failed intersection.

For each year, state `z`, model `m ∈ {G,B,R,Gmask}`, and cell `g`, define:

- `Y_g(z) = sum_i 1{group_i(z)=g} Y_i(z)`;
- `H_m,g(z) = sum_i 1{group_i(z)=g, i is in the non-EMPTY fixed-K queue of m} Y_i(z)`.

For finite-corpus sensitivity among documentary positives, `TPR_m,g = H_m,g / Y_g` when `Y_g>0`. The primary comparisons use integer counts; for the same cell and state they are exactly equivalent to comparing these sensitivities.

A cell with `Y_g=0` is `subgroup_feasibility_inconclusive`, not safe and not adverse. The result must report `N_g`, `Y_g`, queue offers by model, `H_m,g`, and all deletions. No minimum cell size is invented for a superpopulation claim because none is made; singleton sensitivity is addressed by the inherited anchored deletion. A future clinical fairness claim requires independently justified cell definitions, sample-size planning, uncertainty, and stakeholder review.

## 7. New subgroup non-harm gates and `q_safe`

For every complete state and each evaluable cell, G passes comparator non-harm only if:

`H_G,g(z) - H_C,g(z) ≥ 0` for every `C ∈ {B,R,Gmask}`.

G passes the cell-specific random non-harm gate only if the exact integer cross-product is nonnegative:

`N(z) * H_G,g(z) - K * Y_g(z) ≥ 0`.

This random gate compares G's captured documentary-M2 count in cell g with the expected cell-g capture under K uniform reviews from the same state population. Use the parent's deletion-specific N/Y conventions and frozen K exactly; do not estimate random queues by Monte Carlo.

Define `GROUP_SAFE(z)=1` only if all four cells are resolved, all have `Y_g>0`, and all 16 non-harm inequalities (three queue comparators plus random across four cells) pass. Equality is permitted because this is a non-harm gate; the inherited overall claim still requires the strict greater-than-five capture margin.

For each exact rational `q` in the parent's threshold set `R`, define `SAFE_PASS(q)=1` only if:

1. `DEC_PASS(q)=1`; and
2. `GROUP_SAFE(z)=1` in every same complete coupled state with `Q_omega,2020 ≥ q` and `Q_omega,2021 ≥ q`.

If maximum stored content is subgroup-feasible and passes the inherited overall gates, define:

`q_safe = min { q in R : SAFE_PASS(q)=1 }`.

Report `q_safe`, `q_dec`, `q_req`, both differences `q_safe-q_dec` and `q_dec-q_req`, and verify exactly:

`q_safe ≥ q_dec ≥ q_req`.

For the immediately lower threshold when one exists, emit every failing world, year, cell, comparator, `N_g`, `Y_g`, G/comparator queue hashes, `H_G,g`, `H_C,g`, random cross-product, and responsible missing whole-accession bundles. Do not stitch the worst female state, worst older state, or worst comparator from different worlds into one artificial state.

This design distinguishes three questions:

1. `q_req`: are overall capture gates robust?
2. `q_dec`: are the same patients offered as under maximum stored content?
3. `q_safe`: are both properties retained without a documentary-M2 capture loss in any predeclared age-by-recorded-sex cell?

## 8. Analysis, feasibility, and falsification

Enumerate or exactly optimize over the same complete compatible state space as the parent. Reuse frozen scores and queues; no model is retrained for a cell. Compute cell counts by joining the fixed patient ledger to the index `encounters` row through `(患者主索引, 就诊号)` inside each coherent world. Hash all patient identities before derived output.

The confirmatory interpretation is one-hot and hierarchical:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: any source replay, schema/hash check, accession grouping, operation interval, temporal quarantine, world coupling, reader book, score/tie replay, no-backfill deletion, threshold search, cell join, or boundary witness cannot be verified.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: any inherited N/K/event/`nA>0` gate fails.
3. `demographic_or_subgroup_feasibility_inconclusive`: an included patient cannot be assigned to exactly one cell in a complete state, or any cell has `Y_g=0`.
4. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: maximum stored content fails an inherited overall comparator, random, or deletion gate.
5. `maximum_content_group_adverse`: maximum stored content passes inherited overall gates but fails any cell-specific comparator or random non-harm gate.
6. `conditional_coverage_adverse`: maximum content passes both overall and group gates, but `q_req` is undefined or equals 1.
7. `queue_identity_adverse`: `q_req<1`, but `q_dec` is undefined or equals 1; aggregate capture is robust but patient identity is not.
8. `group_safety_adverse`: `q_dec<1`, but `q_safe` is undefined or equals 1; aggregate capture and queue identity are robust, but at least one predeclared cell loses documentary-M2 captures to a comparator under some admissible missing-bundle state.
9. `documentary_group_safe_supportive_bridge_pending`: maximum content passes, `q_safe<1`, the immediately lower threshold fails when it exists, all exact witnesses verify, and all structural and cell certificates are complete.
10. `external_clinical_bridge_pending`: the HCC computation is valid but report-version/access, workflow, biological, utility, or deployment evidence is absent. This status accompanies, and never upgrades, the finite-corpus label.

Label 10 is a mandatory evidence-status field rather than an alternative scientific result; exactly one of labels 1–9 is primary. This removes the parent's ambiguity between a computational support label and a missing-external-bridge label.

Supportive means only that, in this frozen HCC corpus and uncertainty envelope, a positive patient-complete missing-bundle reserve preserves overall capture, maximum-content queue membership, and no-loss documentary-M2 capture in the four recorded demographic cells. It does not prove fairness or benefit.

Adverse means the stated stronger hypothesis is falsified at the relevant layer. It does not erase a narrower supported parent claim. Inconclusive means the required question cannot be evaluated; it is not adverse evidence about G. Favorable 12/48/72-hour results cannot rescue the 24-hour label, and adverse sensitivities cannot veto it.

## 11. Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

Changed from the selected parent: one exact nested frontier, `q_safe`, is added; it uses four fixed age-by-recorded-sex cells and the already locked queues to detect group-specific documentary-M2 capture loss that overall margins and maximum-content patient-set equality can miss. It adds a demographic resolution state, exact cell/comparator/random gates, boundary witnesses, and verifier tests for unsupported fairness claims. It also makes the finite-corpus result and external-bridge status separate fields.

Unchanged: population, operation and acquisition time boundaries, endpoint, whole-accession grain, source paths, source uncertainty, onset coupling, reader/pathology books, models, features, K, overall margins, random baseline, anchored deletion, `q_req`, `q_dec`, 24-hour primacy, and every nonclinical evidence limit.

The experiment can falsify the stronger claim that a report-robust fixed-capacity queue avoids documentary-M2 capture loss in all four recorded demographic cells. It cannot determine whether those cells exhaust clinically important inequity, whether the endpoint is biological truth, whether review changes care, or whether any patient benefits.
