# Episode 95: observation-aware ICU-day-7 topology after a recorded loop decision

Parents: [prior hypothesis] and [prior hypothesis].

## Unresolved clinical question and advance

After post-shock stabilization, an invasively ventilated ICU patient can remain substantially fluid-positive. Clinicians must decide whether to initiate or restart IV furosemide/bumetanide, balancing possible earlier ICU transition against renal injury, dialysis, and death. The unresolved question is:

> At the first broad adult ICU hour-48 opportunity, among patients still recorded as invasively ventilated, recently off vasopressors, fluid-positive, and with recorded MAP, urine, and potassium safeguards, is a qualifying recorded IV loop-delivery initiation/restart during the following 12 hours associated with at least a 5-percentage-point increase in the probability of being alive and not in a recorded ICU exactly at ICU day 7, without prespecified recorded renal, dialysis, mortality, or terminal-disposition harm?

This is a falsifiable recorded-policy association, not a causal treatment effect. Its advance over the repairable parent is substantive: ICU day 7 is exactly H120=t0+120 hours (intime+168 hours), rather than t0+7 days (intime+216 hours, ICU day 9); death takes precedence; and nonfatal disappearance from the MIMIC observation system is unknown, not recovery. The endpoint is therefore destination-neutral without silently equating home, rehabilitation, hospice, LTAC, or an unseen outside ICU.

The strongest evidence available supports only feasibility and plausibility. The MIMIC-IV 3.1 tables contain ICU stays, ventilation/procedure records, recorded inputs, transfers, admissions, labs, and death fields with the stated keys and clocks. Current public evidence is hypothesis-generating: the Europe PMC search result for Orieux et al. 2026 (PMCID PMC13218125; frozen search source [source checksum]) describes renal congestion as plausible but explicitly says interventional evidence and clinical utility remain unestablished. It does not validate this phenotype or exposure. No treatment effect is already supported by the source audit.

## Exposure policies and estimand

Continuous rows additionally require positive finite mg/hour rate; drug pushes require the frozen Drug Push/05-Med Bolus grammar and missing rate. For statuses FinishedRunning, ChangeDose/Rate, Stopped, and Paused, exposure contributes only on the half-open interval [starttime,endtime). Paused rows end exposure at endtime; gaps are not bridged by linkorderid. Remove exact duplicates after full-key ordering. Define A12 as the first repaired loop onset strictly in (t0,C0), and B12 as no repaired onset through C0. Onset exactly at C0 is ambiguous and excluded from the primary contrast. Treatment and closure are independent of extubation task X and endpoint fields.

Create equal A12 and B12 clones at t0. Follow each clone for 12 hours; censor only at the same prespecified shared closure rules (death, index-hospital discharge/terminal closure, or end of observed baseline ICU interval as defined by the frozen protocol), never asymmetrically. The primary estimand is the E_R equal-clone A12-minus-B12 recorded association in the lower and upper partial-identification bounds for H120-RINO. Report the lower contrast DeltaL=p_A,L-p_B,U and upper contrast DeltaU=p_A,U-p_B,L; no numerator substitution or complete-case outcome claim is allowed.

The main analysis is five subject-fold cross-fitted arm-specific L2 logistic compatibility models at clone-hours, frozen C=1 with C=0.1 fallback, using only pre-t0 demographics, unit/era, prior admission history, repaired loop/pressor history, MAP/urine/balance, creatinine/lactate/potassium, FiO2/PEEP/SpO2/mode/sedation, crystalloid/albumin, and missingness. Use numerator-free inverse compatibility weights clipped to [0.01,0.99], pooled first/99th percentile truncation, weight 1 if a frozen model fallback is invoked, and Hájek bounded probabilities.

## Primary endpoint and observation-aware state machine

Set H120=t0+120 hours, exactly ICU day 7. The primary H120-RINO state is a pair [lower,upper], with definite success [1,1], definite failure [0,0], or unknown [0,1].

First apply death precedence. Exact admissions.deathtime <= H120, or consistent hospital_expire_flag=1/discharge_location=DIED for an admission closed by H120, is death failure. patients.dod is date-only: interpret as [local midnight,next midnight); wholly before H120 is death failure, a date interval straddling H120 is unknown, and beginning after H120 is not death by H120. Conflicting indicators are unknown unless exact deathtime resolves them.

If not death, scan all same-subject admissions through H120. Join each to transfers on subject_id,hadm_id and require valid intime <= H120 < outtime; corroborate active ICU status by icustays on subject_id,hadm_id,stay_id with the same half-open clock. Then:

- [1,1] requires exactly one active admission with exactly one valid active non-ICU transfer in the frozen non-ICU list and zero corroborating active ICU stays.
- [0,0] requires one active admission with one valid active ICU transfer and one corroborating active ICU stay.
- A closed index admission with no active admission is [0,1], not success: MIMIC cannot establish outside-system ICU nonoccupancy.
- Multiple active admissions/transfers, missing or overlapping topology, malformed times, transfer/ICU disagreement, unknown/PACU/Nursery careunit, or unresolved death/disposition conflict are [0,1].

Report mutually exclusive state counts: definite death, in-system ICU, in-system non-ICU, out-of-system nonfatal closure, and unknown reason. Destination overlays from admissions.discharge_location (community, facility/rehabilitation, hospice/LTAC/acute hospital, unresolved) are descriptive and never alter RINO. Retain continuous non-ICU coverage and no ICU interval during (H120-48h,H120] as a topology sensitivity, and report AIFD7 or airway-status diagnostics only as subordinate components. The inherited day-9 H168=t0+168h endpoint is a labelled sensitivity, never the primary.

## Harms, baselines, falsification, and result branches

Primary and secondary recorded outcomes are H120-RINO; 48-hour and day-7 recorded airway-path diagnostics; creatinine rise under a frozen baseline and time window; new RRT from exact ICU procedure events plus dated hospital dialysis procedure codes with date-only bounds; mortality; and terminal-disposition states. No missing observation is carried forward as alive or safe. Use the same death-first rules for all harm windows.

Mandatory baselines are index-admission-only topology, sustained-topology RINO, H168 day-9 sensitivity, legacy Paused-ambiguous exposure, frozen balance/pressor timing variants, and symmetric eMAR/pharmacy concordance. A material endpoint conflict is a bound shift >=0.05, midpoint sign reversal with both absolute midpoints >=0.02, a favorable component contrast <=-0.03 while primary is supportive, or adverse-transfer contrast above the frozen +0.03 margin. A material exposure conflict is the analogous category change, bound shift >=0.05, or sign reversal. Sparse variants are measurement-limit/inconclusive, not adverse.

Run 1,999 successful PCG64 seed-480048 subject bootstraps, at most 2,499 attempts and <=5% failures, rebuilding cohort, exposure, models, weights, bounds, and all outcomes. Apply centered one-sided p-values and Holm FWER 0.05 across the prespecified outcome families. Require independently in E_R and E_M_minus_V0: N>=200, A12>=100, B12/shared closure>=100, ESS>=75 per arm, >=100 definite H120 successes and failures, weighted unknown <=0.10 per arm, and absolute arm unknown difference <=0.05.

Branch priority:

1. Primary infeasible: no directional claim.
2. Temporal/measurement or exposure-semantics inconclusive: no directional clinical claim.
3. Endpoint-composition inconclusive if a required topology sensitivity materially conflicts.
4. Adverse-FWER if a Holm-rejected recorded harm family occurs: recorded concern, not toxicity.
5. Margin-falsified if valid and the upper 95% limit for DeltaU_RINO <= +0.05: rejects the prespecified recorded-topology improvement only, not equivalence or safety.
6. Supportive recorded-topology if all gates pass, lower 95% limit for DeltaL_RINO > +0.05, no adjusted harm, no material conflicts, and harm upper limits remain below margins.
7. Statistical-inconclusive otherwise.

Supportive results justify replication, adjudication, and prospective randomization; adverse results motivate caution and a safety-focused study; inconclusive results identify the missingness, topology, receipt, or overlap limitation. None justifies a bedside recommendation from MIMIC alone.
