# Persistent charted respiratory-setting reduction does not add transportable label information in the frozen eICU cohort

## Parents and clinical question

This child combines `[prior hypothesis]` (the frozen multicenter label-transportability audit) with `[prior hypothesis]` (a target-independent persistent numeric respiratory-setting endpoint). The parent transportability result is preserved: in 540 selected person-landmark rows from 443 stays and 74 hospitals, the two literal care-plan labels had concentrated, hospital-dependent use and unstable proxy mappings. The earlier independent liberation ascertainment also remains failed. This child does not reinterpret those findings as proof that the labels are local documentation conventions.

The unresolved, narrower question is whether either exact label adds transportable predictive information for a subsequent persistent reduction in charted numeric respiratory settings beyond generic documentation opportunity and pre-landmark observation availability. This is a predictive transportability question, not a question about whether a bedside intervention occurred or benefited the patient.

The falsifiable hypothesis is that the broader/exact label feature improves hospital-held-out prediction of the endpoint with stable micro and equal-hospital macro log-loss, survives component and placebo checks, and is not explained by a single site or chart component. Failure, instability, or endpoint sparsity is adverse to using the label as a transportable feature, while not proving a specific documentation mechanism.

## Numeric endpoint and observation handling

Only the explicitly frozen canonical labels are used:

- FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, and `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`; percentages are converted to fractions and accepted only in `[0,1]`.
- PEEP: `PEEP`, accepted in `[0,50]` cmH2O.
- Pressure support: `Pressure Support`, accepted in `[0,60]` cmH2O.
- Set rate: `Vent Rate`, accepted in `[0,80]` breaths/minute.

`PEEP/CPAP`, peak inspiratory/peak pressure, SBT, RSBI, and other semantically adjacent labels are not silently merged into the primary endpoint. Peak pressure is excluded from the primary composite because the inherited audit showed peak-pressure dominance and did not establish that it is a delivered controlled setting.

For each row and each component, the baseline is the last valid observation in `[L-360,L]`. The outcome-blind post window is `(L+360,L+1080]`. A component is observed only when both pre and post observations exist. A persistent reduction requires at least two post observations at least 120 minutes apart, each at least the component threshold below baseline, and a post-window median at least the threshold below baseline:

- FiO2: 0.05 fraction (5 percentage points).
- PEEP: 2 cmH2O.
- Pressure support: 2 cmH2O.
- Set rate: 2 breaths/minute.

The row-level endpoint is one if any observed component meets this repeated criterion. Missing or unpaired components are unavailable and are never encoded as negative endpoint events. Endpoint availability is retained as a separate feature and denominator diagnostic. Duplicate timestamp values are retained rather than silently discarded; they cannot satisfy the 120-minute separation criterion unless distinct offsets provide the required separation.

## Prespecified analysis

The primary estimand is hospital-held-out predictive performance. For every hospital meeting the minimum held-out size of 20 rows and containing both endpoint classes, a model is fitted only in the other hospitals and evaluated in that hospital. Imputation, scaling, and logistic-regression fitting occur inside each training fold. Results include row-weighted micro log-loss, Brier score and AUROC, and equal-hospital macro log-loss.

Three feature sets are compared:

1. Process/documentation opportunity: landmark, decision offset, care-plan-row opportunity counts, distinct groups/values, endpoint availability, and pre-placebo availability.
2. Process plus exact label.
3. Process plus broader label.

Because the frozen cohort consists only of the two exact target labels, exact and broader labels are identical in this particular frozen analysis. This is a design fact, not evidence that broader labels are equivalent in other populations; an unrestricted broader-label comparison requires a separately defined cohort.

Uncertainty is estimated by 100 stay-cluster bootstrap replicates, sampling all landmark rows from a stay together and refitting preprocessing and each held-out-hospital model within each replicate. The bootstrap contrasts are equal-hospital macro-log-loss differences; negative values favor the augmented model. Hospital-direction counts and component-specific analyses are required for a positive gate.

Controls are diagnostic rather than causal:

- A pre-D/shifted-window trajectory placebo uses the same persistent rule in a prior time region.
- An unrelated `Tidal Volume (Set)` repeated-decrease endpoint is analyzed only where paired observations are available.
- Numeric component definitions and peak-pressure exclusion are sensitivity boundaries, not semantic adjudication.

## Executed result

The executable is `work/persistent_numeric_endpoint_verification.py`; its result files are `work/persistent_numeric_endpoint_results.json` and `work/persistent_numeric_endpoint_rows.csv`. The run produced 540 analysis rows and 75 persistent any-component events. There were 502 rows with at least one paired primary component and 38 rows without a paired primary component. Events by landmark were 27/129 at 1440, 24/147 at 2880, 14/111 at 4320, 4/77 at 5760, and 6/76 at 7200 minutes.

The held-out analysis had 326 evaluable rows across hospitals 248, 252, 253 and 420. Process-only, exact-label and broader-label models were numerically identical:

- micro log-loss 0.38882;
- micro Brier score 0.11411;
- micro AUROC 0.64015;
- equal-hospital macro log-loss 0.38838.

All 100 bootstrap replicates completed, and the exact-minus-process, broader-minus-process, and broader-minus-exact macro-log-loss contrasts were identically zero because the label variables are identical in the frozen target-labelled cohort. The pre-D placebo had micro log-loss 0.50858 and macro log-loss 0.48949 across the same four hospitals. The unrelated tidal-volume control was available in 197 rows across hospitals 248 and 252, with micro log-loss 0.18578, Brier score 0.03985, AUROC 0.47685, and macro log-loss 0.19085. These controls do not validate the primary endpoint and are not causal controls.

## Gate and interpretation

The result is adverse to the claim that the exact label provides additional transportable predictive information in this frozen cohort: the augmented models add no information to the process model, and exact versus broader cannot be separated here. It is also adverse to treating the endpoint as an established clinical outcome because it is a sparse, documentation-dependent chart proxy and the independent parent liberation validation failed.

This adverse result does not prove that the labels are hospital-specific documentation conventions, does not estimate a treatment effect, and does not establish delivered therapy. It does not establish bedside ventilation, invasive ventilation, SBT delivery, extubation, liberation, quality, benefit, harm, or causality. A charted numeric reduction may reflect recording practice, device state, clinician intent, measurement context, or actual setting change.

A supportive result would require a prespecified augmented-label advantage in both micro and macro estimands, stable across at least two independently bounded component definitions, nested bootstrap intervals excluding zero, adequate hospital coverage, and no component or hospital concentration. An adverse result is the observed absence of any label increment, especially with the prior hospital dependence and peak-dominance findings. An inconclusive result would apply if sparse availability, mixed component bounds, or broad intervals prevented separating these possibilities. Here the predictive increment is directly computable and null, while clinical meaning requires site dictionaries, device-linked semantics, clinician adjudication, and external replication.

## Reproducibility and limitations

Raw eICU files were read-only and were not modified. Derived artifacts are confined to the workspace. The script records source and artifact hashes, raw rows scanned, cohort/action join checks, endpoint definitions, availability, held-out hospitals, bootstrap count, and semantic limits.

The current implementation independently verifies frozen-row identity and adult membership but deliberately does not rebuild the parent’s complete first-qualifying-stay/strict-screen algorithm. It also evaluates the endpoint at the inherited landmark-relative windows rather than claiming a D-relative causal post-treatment outcome. These are explicit limitations. The 326-row held-out subset and 197-row unrelated-control subset are selected by prespecified model/evaluable-outcome rules, not population denominators. Stronger claims require a fully independently rebuilt frozen cohort, site-level adjudication of numeric-field semantics, device-linked data, and replication in another multicenter dataset.
