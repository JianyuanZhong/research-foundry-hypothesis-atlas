# Duration of Hypotension and Acute Kidney Injury in ICU Patients

## A Falsifiable Hypothesis for eICU-CRD

### 1. Hypothesis

At the same minimum (nadir) mean arterial pressure, patients with longer-lasting hypotension (greater fraction of ICU time with MAP < 65 mmHg) experience more subsequent acute kidney injury (AKI), independent of illness severity and vasopressor exposure. Time-weighted hypotension burden carries prognostic information beyond the nadir MAP alone.

### 2. Scientific Opening

**Established**: Hypotension (low MAP) is associated with AKI in shock. The conventional resuscitation target is MAP ≥ 65 mmHg (Surviving Sepsis Campaign). [K2]

**Unresolved**: Whether the DURATION of hypotension (time below threshold) carries independent prognostic information beyond the minimum MAP achieved. The expert seed [eicu-03] posits that at the same minimum BP, longer hypotension → more AKI.

**Leading claim**: Time-weighted hypotension burden (fraction of ICU time with MAP < 65 mmHg in the first 24h) is an independent predictor of AKI after adjusting for nadir MAP, age, gender, vasopressor use, and ICU length of stay.

**Strongest rival**: Duration is a proxy for illness severity / vasopressor exposure / longer ICU stay; once those are adjusted, duration adds no independent information (only the nadir matters).

**Discriminating observation**: A multivariable model with BOTH nadir MAP and hypotension-duration term. If the duration term remains significant (p < 0.05) and improves discrimination (AUROC) over the nadir-only model, the leading claim is supported. If the duration term is null after adjustment, the rival is supported.

**Evidence for the opening**:
- [K2] Dubech et al. 2026: Early norepinephrine initiation shortens hypotension duration and reduces fluid requirements; sustained adrenergic exposure has dose-dependent consequences. Perfusion-guided strategies (individualized BP targets) are central. This review supports the clinical relevance of hypotension duration but does not provide an effect estimate for duration → AKI.
- [K3] AKI-EPI 2 protocol: Defines the 2012 KDIGO AKI criteria (creatinine-based: ≥0.3 mg/dL increase within 48h, or ≥1.5× baseline within 7 days) and the epidemiological context for AKI in ICU patients.
- [K1] eICU-CRD v2.0: Multi-center database with 200,859 ICU admissions, including vital signs (systemicmean), laboratory measurements (creatinine), and vasopressor infusion records.

### 3. Clinical Importance

- AKI occurs in 25-50% of ICU patients and is associated with increased mortality, longer stays, and chronic kidney disease.
- If hypotension DURATION (not just nadir) is an independent AKI risk factor, this would:
  - Support more aggressive BP targets for patients with sustained (not just transient) hypotension
  - Inform monitoring intensity: patients with prolonged MAP < 65 may need closer renal monitoring
  - Provide a more stable risk measure than minimum BP alone (the expert seed's stated value)
- The finding would be actionable: hospitals could implement protocolized renal monitoring for patients with sustained hypotension.

### 4. Substantive Advance over Existing Knowledge

- [K2] is a review of catecholamine-sparing strategies; it does not quantify the independent effect of hypotension duration on AKI.
- [K3] defines the AKI endpoint and epidemiological context but does not examine hypotension duration as a predictor.
- **No prior study** has examined the independent contribution of time-weighted hypotension burden (fraction of time MAP < 65) to AKI risk, adjusted for nadir MAP, in a large multi-center ICU dataset. This is the specific gap.

### 5. Population

- **Source**: eICU-CRD v2.0 [K1]
- **Inclusion**: unitdischargestatus = 'Alive', unitstaytype = 'admit', unitdischargeoffset ≥ 1440 min (N = 103,251)
- **Additional requirements**:
  - ≥10 valid MAP measurements (systemicmean, plausibility filter 20-200 mmHg) over ≥12h span
  - ≥2 creatinine measurements with time stamps (for AKI definition)
- **Expected N after all filters**: ~15,000-20,000 (to be verified; 25,835 have sufficient MAP density)
- **Exclusions**: MAP measurements outside 20-200 mmHg (artifacts); patients with <2 creatinine measurements

### 6. Exposure: Time-Weighted Hypotension Burden

**Primary exposure**: Fraction of ICU time (first 24h) with MAP < 65 mmHg
- Computed from vitalPeriodic.systemicmean, observationoffset
- For each patient: (minutes with MAP < 65 in first 24h) / (total minutes with MAP data in first 24h)
- Requires ≥10 MAP measurements in first 24h for reliable estimation

**Secondary exposures**:
- Area under the MAP curve below 65 (AUC_below): ∫(65 - MAP(t))dt for MAP < 65, first 24h
- Nadir MAP: minimum systemicmean in first 24h
- Time to first MAP < 65: observationoffset of first MAP < 65

**Vasopressor exposure** (confounder):
- From infusionDrug: total vasopressor dose (norepinephrine-equivalent) in first 24h
- Vasopressor use: binary (any vasopressor in first 24h)

### 7. Outcome: Acute Kidney Injury (KDIGO)

**Primary**: AKI within 7 days of ICU admission [K3]
- Baseline creatinine: earliest creatinine in stay (or pre-ICU if available)
- AKI Stage 1: creatinine ≥0.3 mg/dL increase from baseline within 48h, OR ≥1.5-1.9× baseline within 7 days
- AKI Stage 2: creatinine ≥2.0-2.9× baseline within 7 days
- AKI Stage 3: creatinine ≥3.0× baseline, OR ≥4.0 mg/dL, OR RRT
- Composite: any AKI (Stage ≥1)

**Secondary**:
- AKI Stage ≥2 (moderate-severe)
- Need for renal replacement therapy (inferable from treatment.csv.gz — to verify)

### 8. Analysis Plan

**Primary analysis**: Multivariable logistic regression (hospital random intercept)
```
AKI ~ hypotension_fraction + nadir_MAP + age + gender + vasopressor_use + ICU_los + (1 | hospitalid)
```
- Key test: coefficient for hypotension_fraction (Wald test, p < 0.05)
- Report: adjusted odds ratio per 10% increase in hypotension fraction, with 95% CI

**Model comparison** (baseline vs alternative):
- **Baseline**: AKI ~ nadir_MAP + age + gender + vasopressor_use + ICU_los + (1 | hospitalid)
- **Alternative**: AKI ~ hypotension_fraction + nadir_MAP + age + gender + vasopressor_use + ICU_los + (1 | hospitalid)
- Compare: AUROC, calibration slope, NRI (net reclassification improvement), IDI (integrated discrimination improvement)
- **Justification**: The baseline answers "does nadir MAP predict AKI?"; the alternative answers "does DURATION add information beyond nadir?"

**Sensitivity analyses**:
- MAP threshold: 60 mmHg, 70 mmHg (instead of 65)
- Restrict to first 12h instead of 24h
- Exclude patients with vasopressor use (to test if duration effect is independent of treatment)
- Stratify by baseline creatinine (normal vs elevated)
- Use AUC_below instead of fraction (continuous exposure)

**Method comparison**:
- **Baseline**: Logistic regression with nadir MAP only
- **Learned alternative**: XGBoost with features: hypotension_fraction, nadir_MAP, AUC_below, time_to_first_hypotension, age, gender, vasopressor_use, ICU_los. Compare AUROC with vs without hypotension features to assess incremental predictive information.
- **Justification**: The regression answers the mechanistic question (is duration independently associated?); the ML model answers the predictive question (can we identify high-risk patients using the full MAP trajectory?). They are complementary.

### 9. Falsification Criteria

- **Supportive**: Hypotension-duration term independently associated with AKI (OR > 1.1 per 10% increase, p < 0.05) AND improves AUROC over nadir-only model (ΔAUROC > 0.01); consistent across hospitals (hierarchical model, positive between-hospital variance).
- **Adverse**: Duration term null after adjusting for nadir MAP + severity (p > 0.10) — only nadir matters; OR duration inversely associated with AKI (OR < 0.9).
- **Inconclusive**: <15,000 patients meet all inclusion criteria (insufficient power); OR >50% of patients have <10 MAP measurements in first 24h (insufficient recording density to define duration).

### 12. Limitations

1. **Recording density**: Only 25% of patients have ≥10 MAP measurements over ≥12h. The analysis is restricted to this subset, which may be enriched for sicker patients (more monitoring). Sensitivity analysis: compare characteristics of included vs excluded patients.
2. **No continuous arterial line**: eICU-CRD records periodic (not continuous) MAP. Time-below-threshold is estimated from discrete measurements, not true continuous monitoring. This is a measurement limitation, not a design flaw.
3. **Vasopressor confounding**: Vasopressor use is a confounder (treatment for hypotension). Adjusted for in the primary model, but residual confounding is possible. Sensitivity analysis: exclude vasopressor users.
4. **AKI definition**: Creatinine-based KDIGO criteria may miss early AKI (creatinine lags behind true GFR decline). Urine output data is not available in eICU-CRD for all patients.
5. **Single time period**: eICU-CRD data collection was 2014-2015. Generalizability to current practice is uncertain.

### 13. Feasibility

- **Data availability**: VERIFIED. 25,835 patients (25.0% of 103,251) have ≥10 MAP over ≥12h span. 190,464 patients have ≥1 creatinine. Overlap expected to be ~15,000-20,000.
- **Compute**: CPU-only. Logistic regression + XGBoost on ~20,000 patients with ~10 features. Estimated runtime: <1 hour on 8 CPUs.
- **Time**: Data extraction (MAP trajectory, creatinine series, vasopressor doses) is the main cost. Estimated 2-4 hours of compute for full cohort.

### 14. What Would Change the Next Decision

- **Supportive result**: Continue to external validation (MIMIC-IV), examine dose-response (fraction vs AKI stage), test if early (first 6h) vs late (6-24h) hypotension differ.
- **Adverse result**: Duration is a proxy for severity; focus on nadir MAP as the key predictor. Consider whether a different renal endpoint (e.g., RRT) would show a duration effect.
- **Inconclusive result**: Insufficient recording density. Consider restricting to patients with arterial lines (if identifiable) or using a different BP measure (SBP).
