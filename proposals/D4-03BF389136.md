## Decision-relevant question and evidence boundary

The seed asks whether people who appear to have preserved kidney function by creatinine but lower function by cystatin C, particularly those with weak grip, are at elevated risk of severe AKI. This matters because creatinine can be influenced by muscle mass, and missing a vulnerable person could affect medication review, kidney follow-up, or selection for a prospective cystatin-C study.

The cited Haines et al. critical-illness study was read from the locally available article extraction. It studied 38 mechanically ventilated ICU patients, serial creatinine/cystatin C, measured GFR in a subgroup, and rectus-femoris ultrasound. Its strongest support is that, in that ICU sample, creatinine-based estimates could substantially overestimate measured kidney function during prolonged critical illness and that cystatin-C/creatinine discordance may be related to muscle loss. It did not validate this UKB population, did not establish a population-level AKI prediction rule, and explicitly could not conclude that creatinine inaccuracies affect clinical outcomes.

The local UKB data support a narrower unresolved claim: at a dated baseline assessment, does a prespecified creatinine–cystatin-C discordance phenotype, and its combination with low grip, identify a higher rate of a later recorded inpatient acute-kidney-disease diagnosis than creatinine-based assessment alone? The original “severe AKI” claim is not supportable from this export and is retired. No KDIGO stage, urine-output criterion, ICU admission, creatinine trajectory, measured GFR, muscle mass, inflammation adjustment, disease onset, or treatment effect will be claimed.

## Hypothesis and estimand

Among UKB participants with a valid baseline assessment date and valid baseline creatinine, cystatin C, age and sex, without a prior recorded N17/N18 inpatient diagnosis, the primary hypothesis is:

> Participants with eGFRcreat >=60 and eGFRcys <60 mL/min/1.73 m2 (“creatinine-preserved/cystatin-lower” discordance) have a higher five-year risk of a first later inpatient N17 diagnosis, especially when baseline grip is in the sex-specific lowest quintile, than participants with eGFRcreat >=60, eGFRcys >=60 and grip at or above that quintile.

The primary contrast is the absolute five-year risk difference and risk ratio between the discordant/low-grip group and the concordant-preserved/high-grip reference. The prediction estimand is the cross-validated change in Brier score, calibration and decision-relevant risk movement when cystatin C and grip are added to a creatinine-only model. This is prognostic/record-triage evidence, not a causal effect of ordering cystatin C, improving grip, changing treatment, or preventing AKI.

A five-year horizon is defined from the date in 53-0.0 through 5*365.25 days. Participants are administratively censored at the available outcome observation boundary only if a valid end-of-observation field is identified; otherwise the primary analysis is a recorded-event classification with the negative label explicitly meaning “no qualifying record in the available source,” not disease-free. If this observation limitation cannot be resolved, incidence and survival claims fail closed.

## Exposure construction

After passing semantic gates, convert creatinine to mg/dL as required by the prespecified race-free CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation and compute eGFRcreat; compute eGFRcys with the prespecified race-free CKD-EPI 2012/2021 cystatin-C equation selected before inspecting outcomes, recording the exact equation/version. Do not use race. If the selected equation requires an input unavailable locally, fail closed rather than substitute.

Define:
- preserved creatinine: eGFRcreat >=60;
- lower cystatin estimate: eGFRcys <60;
- discordance D: preserved creatinine plus lower cystatin estimate;
- concordant preserved reference: both estimates >=60;
- low grip: the lower of available left/right grip values is not used; instead use the prespecified maximum of 46-0.0 and 47-0.0 as the participant’s best measured baseline strength, require both hands unless a missingness sensitivity is declared, and define sex-specific low grip as the within-sex baseline 20th percentile learned in training data only;
- primary four-level phenotype: concordant preserved/high grip (reference), concordant preserved/low grip, discordant/high grip, discordant/low grip.

The primary hypothesis is the discordant/low-grip versus reference contrast. Thresholds are fixed before outcome scoring. Continuous eGFR values and a continuous creatinine-to-cystatin-C ratio are secondary representations; the ratio is not called a muscle-mass measure.

## Outcomes and evidence limits

Primary outcome: first position-matched inpatient ICD-10 code with prefix N17 in 41270 and a nonmissing paired date in 41280, strictly after baseline and within five years. N17 is reported as “recorded inpatient acute kidney disease/acute kidney failure code,” not severe AKI and not adjudicated onset.

Secondary outcomes: first N18-prefixed inpatient code within five years (recorded chronic kidney disease), the composite N17-or-N18 first recorded kidney diagnosis, and all-cause death using the available death-date field if its semantics pass review. Report code-family counts and exact code-date pairing. Never sort the code and date arrays independently.

Prior N17/N18 codes are exclusion criteria only when their paired dates are valid and on/before baseline. Invalid or discordant code/date positions trigger an integrity failure; no repair by sorting is permitted. The design cannot distinguish community, outpatient, or undocumented kidney events, cannot establish complete ascertainment, and cannot adjudicate severity, reversibility, KDIGO stage, measured GFR, or clinical onset.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The transparent baseline is B0: age, sex, baseline assessment year, creatinine-derived eGFR (or raw creatinine only if the eGFR semantic gate fails and the estimand is relabelled), and prespecified grip. B1 adds cystatin-C-derived eGFR and the four-level discordance/grip phenotype. The primary incremental question is B1 versus B0 on the same participants, folds, outcome and horizon.

The substantive learned alternative is L1: gradient-boosted classification (or a regularized nonlinear model if the solver environment lacks the selected library) using exactly the B1 inputs: age, sex, year, creatinine, cystatin C, both grip values and derived discordance indicators. Fit within training folds with fold-local imputation, scaling if required, feature construction, and hyperparameter selection. Compare against B1 on identical participant-level five-fold splits. L1 can reveal threshold and interaction structure—particularly whether cystatin C matters only at low grip or across the creatinine-preserved range—that an additive model averages away. L1 is not allowed to redefine the phenotype, outcome, cut points, or primary estimand, and a small predictive gain without stable interaction shape is not evidence of a mechanistic kidney-reserve effect.

A mechanistic eGFR/low-muscle model is deferred: local field metadata do not verify assay timing, inflammation, body composition, or measured muscle mass; assigning a creatinine–cystatin ratio to muscle mass would overclaim. Revisit only when field dictionaries, specimen dates, inflammatory markers and an adjudicated reserve endpoint are available. The mature repeat-panel branch [prior hypothesis] tests a different question—whether prior panel values add information beyond a current panel and repeat spacing, with an early-through-2012 fit and 2013 no-refit transport test. UKB-03 is not a replacement for that branch: it tests biological proxy discordance and grip at one baseline and does not require repeat-panel chronology. The repeat-panel branch remains preferable for history-retention/transport; this branch is worthwhile only if its semantic and N17 support gates pass.

## Analysis, uncertainty and falsification

Use deterministic participant-level five-fold splits by eid. All preprocessing, eGFR construction, grip percentile estimation, missingness handling, model selection and calibration are training-fold-only. Generate held-out probabilities for B0, B1 and L1.

Report overall and phenotype-stratified five-year Brier score, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, observed-versus-predicted risk, absolute risk difference, risk ratio with confidence intervals, and cross-validated incremental Brier score for B1-B0. Use paired bootstrap resampling within fixed phenotype strata, preserving predictions and fold assignments; report percentile or BCa intervals and the number of failed support replicates. For the primary phenotype contrast require, before interpretation, at least 100 total N17 events, at least 30 N17 events in each primary comparison group, and at least 200 participants per group. If these gates fail, the N17 claim is inconclusive; CKD/composite outcomes may be reported only with their own support gates.

Falsification and integrity controls:
- permute N17 outcome labels within folds and baseline-year strata; the incremental and interaction findings should disappear;
- permute cystatin C within sex, baseline-year and creatinine quantile strata while retaining grip and creatinine;
- compare the prespecified phenotype to a negative-control outcome unrelated to kidney disease, selected before outcome inspection;
- repeat with N18 and composite outcomes without changing the primary N17 decision rule;
- test complete-case versus fold-local imputation;
- verify that deliberately mispairing 41270/41280 is detected and never accepted;
- audit pre-baseline exclusions, date ordering, missingness, duplicate eids, join coverage, numeric ranges, field-instance interpretation, and outcome observation limits;
- assess whether the finding is driven by calendar year, assessment centre, or repeat participation.

Supportive evidence requires passed semantic/timing/integrity/observation gates, adequate N17 support, a higher discordant/low-grip risk with an uncertainty interval excluding the null, calibrated incremental performance of B1 over B0 on held-out data, persistence under the missingness and selection sensitivities, and loss of the pattern under permutation. Stable L1 interactions can strengthen the scientific interpretation but cannot rescue a failed transparent contrast.

Adverse evidence is no difference or lower risk for discordant/low grip, a gain explained by age/year or selection, disappearance under missingness or permutation controls, poor calibration, or a direction reversal for N18/composite outcomes. This weakens the proposed recorded-risk phenotype but does not prove cystatin C has no value in ICU or other populations.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed field semantics, absent specimen timing when required for the chosen estimand, invalid code-date pairing, incomplete observation that precludes the declared horizon, sparse groups/events, wide intervals, fold instability, or disagreement between primary and essential sensitivity analyses. It is not evidence against the biological hypothesis.

Computationally checkable claims are file integrity, schemas, joins, counts, phenotype construction, dates, model fitting, held-out scores, uncertainty arithmetic and permutation behavior. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for true GFR, AKI severity/onset/reversibility, complete ascertainment, inflammation and body-composition confounding, causal testing/treatment effects, action thresholds, external validation, and whether cystatin C changes care.

## Disposition

This is a substantive UKB-specific repair of the imported seed, not a claim that the seed is already supported. It retires “severe AKI,” adds a defensible recorded N17 endpoint, fixes baseline timing to 53-0.0, binds exact source paths/tables/columns/keys, and makes the learned alternative comparable to the transparent baseline. The mechanistic reserve interpretation is explicitly deferred. If the semantic or support gates fail, retain the candidate as a transparent infeasible branch and continue with the mature repeat-panel branch.
