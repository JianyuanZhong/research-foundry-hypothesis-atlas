# Working Proposal: Cell-Type Specificity of Glycolytic/HIF-1α Metabolic Reprogramming in Treatment-Resistant RA

**Child of [prior hypothesis]** (5-condition gradient metabolic reprogramming in treatment-resistant RA macrophages).
**Status: PLANNED analysis. A bounded feasibility diagnostic (gene mapping) was executed in gen-ep9 ([research job], [research job], [research job]); the full inferential analysis has NOT been executed. Ensembl ID corrections were applied in gen-ep10.**

## Scientific Question

Is the glycolytic/HIF-1α metabolic program in treatment-resistant RA synovial macrophages a **macrophage-specific** state, or does it extend to synovial fibroblast-like cells (FLS) and monocytes?

## Why This Matters

The parent candidate (9e0d7d66) proposes a 5-condition gradient analysis in E-MTAB-8322 to identify metabolic genes specifically upregulated in treatment-resistant RA macrophages after adjusting for inflammatory state [K1]. This analysis is PLANNED, not executed. The present proposal tests a scientifically consequential extension: **cell-type specificity**.

- If the metabolic program is **shared** across macrophages, FLS, and monocytes, it suggests a synovial microenvironment-wide metabolic shift in treatment-resistant RA [K2], supporting broad metabolic interventions (e.g., metformin, mTOR inhibitors).
- If the program is **macrophage-specific**, it points to cell-autonomous metabolic reprogramming in macrophages [K3], supporting targeted metabolic interventions.
- This distinction has direct clinical implications for treatment strategy in difficult-to-treat RA.

## Evidence Base

### [K1] Jia et al. 2025 (Arthritis Res Ther 27:180, PMC12465665)
"The Glycolysis-HIF-1α axis induces IL-1β of macrophages in rheumatoid arthritis." Full-text inspected. This paper establishes the glycolysis-HIF-1α axis as a mechanistic driver of IL-1β production in RA macrophages, providing the molecular basis for the metabolic signature tested in the parent candidate. It does not test cell-type specificity.

### [K2] Liu et al. 2026 (Front Immunol 17:1691946, PMC13002828)
"Immunometabolism in rheumatoid arthritis: mechanisms, biomarkers, and the path to precision medicine." Full-text inspected. This review explicitly addresses **cell-type-specific immunometabolic dysregulation** in RA, with attention to T cells, macrophages, B cells, and fibroblast-like synoviocytes. It provides the framework for asking whether metabolic reprogramming is cell-type-specific or shared. It does not provide quantitative cross-cell-type validation from the same patient cohort.

### [K3] Wei & Xu 2026 (Front Immunol 17:1935792, PMC13591627)
"Failed resolution as a mechanistic basis of difficult-to-treat rheumatoid arthritis: stromal–myeloid crosstalk in the synovium and emerging resolution-restoring therapies." Full-text inspected. This paper proposes a "failed-resolution switch" involving reciprocal signaling between pathological fibroblasts and macrophages in difficult-to-treat RA, with metabolic determinants reinforcing the circuit. It directly motivates the question of whether metabolic reprogramming is a shared microenvironment feature or a cell-autonomous state. It does not provide quantitative transcriptomic validation across cell types.

## Analysis Plan (PLANNED)

### Step 1: Define metabolic signature in E-MTAB-8322
1. **Data preparation**: Load per-sample matrix files, sum to pseudobulk per donor (25 independent donors). Compute log2(count + 1) for the 10-gene panel.
2. **Inflammatory score**: Per-donor, compute mean expression of a predefined inflammatory gene set (e.g., TNF, IL1B, IL6, CXCL8, PTGS2, NOS2, CD80, CD86, HLA-DRA, HLA-DRB1) in each of the 5 conditions.
3. **Metabolic score**: Per-donor, compute mean expression of the 10 HIF/glycolysis genes (corrected Ensembl IDs).
4. **Residual analysis**: For each donor, regress metabolic score on inflammatory score. The residual represents metabolic expression not explained by inflammatory state.
5. **Gradient test**:
   - Kruskal-Wallis test on residuals across 5 conditions
   - Jonckheere-Terpstra test for monotonic gradient (HC < Naive < Remission < Resistant)
   - Pairwise Wilcoxon tests with BH correction
   - Condition-specificity F-test (ANOVA on residuals)
6. **Gene-level analysis**: For each of the 10 HIF/glycolysis genes, compute per-condition mean expression and test for condition-specific upregulation in Resistant RA after adjusting for inflammatory score.

### Step 2: Cross-assay validation in E-MTAB-8873 (FLS)
1. **Map metabolic signature**: The 10 HIF/glycolysis genes are all present in the ~445-gene panel (gene symbol format).
2. **FLS contrast**:
   - Primary: "FLS only" (baseline FLS) vs "FLS + MerTK+ macrophages" (FLS in inflammatory context)
   - Secondary: "FLS + MerTK+" vs "FLS + MerTK+ + LPS" (inflammatory stimulation)
   - Tertiary: "FLS + MerTK+ + inhibitor" (MerTK inhibition effect)
3. **Test**: For each of the 10 HIF/glycolysis genes, test whether expression in FLS co-cultured with macrophages is elevated compared to baseline FLS. Compute direction concordance with the macrophage signature.
4. **Limitation**: This tests whether the metabolic program is present in FLS in the context of macrophage crosstalk, not whether it tracks disease state (active vs remission).

### Step 3: Cross-cell-type validation in E-MTAB-8316 (monocytes)
1. **Map metabolic signature**: The 10 HIF/glycolysis genes are present in the full transcriptome (Ensembl format).
2. **Monocyte contrast**:
   - Primary: "none" (baseline monocytes) vs "UNC106238" (MerTK inhibitor-treated monocytes)
   - Paired design: 4 donors × 2 conditions × 3 replicates
3. **Test**: For each of the 10 HIF/glycolysis genes, test whether expression changes with MerTK inhibitor treatment. Compute direction concordance with the macrophage signature.
4. **Limitation**: This tests whether the metabolic program is present in monocytes and whether MerTK inhibition modulates it, not whether it tracks disease state.

### Step 4: Competing explanation tests
1. **Inflammatory confounding**: The residual analysis in Step 1 adjusts for inflammatory state. In Steps 2-3, compute inflammatory scores in FLS and monocytes and test whether the metabolic signal persists after adjustment.
2. **Assay differences**: The targeted panel (E-MTAB-8873) vs full transcriptome (E-MTAB-8322, 8316) may have different dynamic ranges and normalization. Use rank-based statistics (Spearman correlation) to minimize assay-specific effects.
3. **Cross-sectional design**: All contrasts are cross-sectional. The "active vs remission" contrast in the parent candidate is cross-sectional. This limits causal interpretation.
4. **Donor overlap**: E-MTAB-8873 donors (SA256A, etc.) are different from E-MTAB-8322 donors (SA131, etc.) and E-MTAB-8316 donors (SA049, etc.). No donor overlap. This is a strength (independent validation) but also a limitation (different patient populations).

## Falsification Criteria
- **Supportive**: The metabolic signature (top genes from E-MTAB-8322) shows a similar direction of effect in FLS (E-MTAB-8873) and/or monocytes (E-MTAB-8316), after adjusting for inflammatory state. Specifically: **≥7/10 genes show concordant direction in at least one validation dataset**.
- **Adverse**: The metabolic signature does NOT replicate in FLS or monocytes (**≤3/10 genes concordant in both validation datasets**), suggesting a macrophage-specific metabolic program.
- **Inconclusive**: The metabolic signature partially replicates (**4-6/10 genes concordant**), or the adjustment for inflammatory state is ambiguous, or the assay differences are too large to permit cross-assay comparison.

## What Would Change the Next Decision
- **If supportive**: The metabolic program is a synovial microenvironment-wide feature of treatment-resistant RA. Next step: test whether the metabolic program is associated with treatment response in a larger cohort, or investigate the mechanism of cross-cell-type metabolic coordination.
- **If adverse**: The metabolic program is macrophage-specific. Next step: investigate the cell-autonomous mechanism (e.g., HIF-1α stabilization, mitochondrial dysfunction) in macrophages.
- **If inconclusive**: The metabolic program is partially shared. Next step: identify which genes are shared and which are cell-type-specific, and investigate the mechanism of partial sharing.

## Method Comparison (Baseline vs Alternative)
- **Baseline**: Pathway-level metabolic score (mean of 10 HIF/glycolysis genes) from E-MTAB-8322.
- **Alternative**: Gene-level residual analysis (per-gene metabolic expression ~ inflammatory expression) from E-MTAB-8322.
- **What the alternative reveals**: The gene-level residual analysis identifies which specific metabolic genes are upregulated in resistant RA after adjusting for inflammation, rather than relying on a pre-defined gene set. This is more mechanistically informative and can identify novel metabolic genes not in standard pathway definitions.
- **Selection reason**: The gene-level analysis is the primary method; the pathway-level score is a sensitivity analysis.

## Data Limitations
1. **E-MTAB-8873 lacks disease-state contrast**: The FLS data does not include "active vs remission RA" conditions. The validation is limited to testing whether the metabolic program is present in FLS in the context of macrophage crosstalk.
2. **E-MTAB-8316 lacks disease-state contrast**: The monocyte data does not include "active vs remission RA" conditions. The validation is limited to testing whether the metabolic program is present in monocytes and whether MerTK inhibition modulates it.
3. **Cross-sectional design**: All contrasts are cross-sectional. No longitudinal transitions.
4. **No donor overlap**: The three datasets use different patient populations. This is a strength (independent validation) but also a limitation (different disease contexts).
5. **ALAS2 excluded**: Very low/zero expression in all three datasets (2/27, 0/33, 1/12 nonzero). The effective panel is 10 genes, not 11.
6. **Ensembl ID corrections (gen-ep10)**: Four gene IDs from gen-ep9 were incorrect (HIF1A, ENO1, GAPDH, PGAM1 mapped to wrong genes). LDHA and HK1 IDs were unresolved in gen-ep9. All six are now corrected and verified against the actual data files.
