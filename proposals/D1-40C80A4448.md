# HCC early inpatient escalation hypothesis (episode 2)

## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Among adults admitted with a documented HCC diagnosis, does a 7-day trajectory of liver, coagulation, renal, and tumor-marker laboratory measurements identify a subsequent inpatient escalation of care beyond the admission baseline? This matters because escalation can signal decompensation or treatment-related deterioration, but the dataset does not provide a validated death/ICU outcome. The substantive advance is to test whether temporal persistence and cross-marker change add clinically interpretable information beyond a static baseline, while explicitly distinguishing a computable proxy from clinical harm.

## Hypothesis
A prespecified, time-aware representation of laboratory measurements from admission through day 7 will improve held-out prediction of a subsequent inpatient escalation proxy over a baseline snapshot model, after accounting for admission department, age/sex, treatment/procedure indicators, and documentation intensity. The direction and magnitude are not assumed.

## Exact computable experiment
1. Cohort: encounters with nonempty `入院时间`, joined to `diagnoses` on `患者主索引 + 就诊号`, requiring exact `诊断名称=肝细胞癌`. Primary restriction is to the four liver-tumor departments observed in encounters: `肝肿瘤内科, 肝胆肿瘤与肝移植外科, 肝胆肿瘤内科, 肝肿瘤外科`; report the unrestricted exact-diagnosis cohort as sensitivity analysis. Do not infer pathology or stage from text.
2. Time zero is `入院时间`. Include labs with `检验时间 >= 入院时间` and `检验时间 <= 入院时间 + 7 days`, and exclude measurements after `出院时间`. Use only the prespecified exact assays listed above, retaining specimen type and missingness; do not pool assays with unknown or conflicting units.
3. Outcome proxy: a subsequent inpatient escalation event is the first qualifying `procedures.开始时间` after the 7-day feature window and before discharge, using a prespecified list of central-venous catheterization, liver resection, TACE/arterial embolization, ablation, or other major procedure categories. This is an escalation-of-care proxy, not death, ICU admission, or clinical harm. A secondary exploratory proxy may use a documented severe deterioration phrase in `clinical_documents`, but only after a blinded, prespecified text dictionary and expert review; it is not primary evidence of harm.
4. Baseline: penalized logistic regression using age, sex, admission department, first available values of each prespecified assay before/at admission, treatment/procedure indicators available by time zero, and documentation counts. Fit only on training patients.
5. Alternative: a patient-level sequence/state-space model using ordered assay values, time since admission, specimen type, missingness indicators, and treatment timing. It is scientifically substantive because it can reveal persistence, ordering, and cross-marker interactions lost by a snapshot. Compare on the same outcome and covariates; do not select the alternative by in-sample fit.
6. Split by `患者主索引`, never by row, with nested time-aware validation. Evaluate held-out discrimination, calibration, and uncertainty intervals; use a prespecified trivial-margin threshold and bootstrap or repeated splits. Negative-control timing checks use post-outcome measurements and permuted within-patient order.
7. Supportive result: trajectory model improves held-out discrimination/calibration beyond baseline with uncertainty excluding the trivial margin and remains directionally consistent across department, documentation, missingness, and assay sensitivity analyses. Adverse result: no improvement, degradation, or failure of negative controls. Inconclusive result: sparse qualifying events, unstable assay units/specimens, imprecise intervals, or inability to adjudicate the proxy.

## Prior-work context
[K1] Yang et al. (2026) reported that routine admission bilirubin, direct bilirubin, AST, and INR predicted day-7 total bilirubin in a small HBV cirrhosis cohort. This supports the clinical relevance of short-term laboratory response and motivates a snapshot comparator, but does not establish HCC prognosis or causal mechanisms.
[K2] Bui et al. (2026) reported that tumor stage, microvascular invasion, resection margin, and Child-Pugh status predicted postoperative disease-free survival in a retrospective HCC resection cohort, with modest discrimination. This bounds the claim that static tumor/liver factors are already important, but does not test early inpatient laboratory trajectories.
[K3] Millarch et al. (2026) reported that a temporal EHR model using ordered vitals, laboratory results, and medications predicted mortality across a nonlinear trauma trajectory and outperformed static severity scores. This motivates the sequence alternative, but is not HCC evidence and does not transfer automatically.

## Rival explanations and limits
Current severity, treatment indication, admission selection, documentation intensity, delayed follow-up, and assay/unit heterogeneity may create apparent trajectory value. Adjustment does not identify a mechanism or causal effect. The source metadata states that lab numeric results have no separate unit column, so cross-assay pooling is unsupported; local dates permit only within-participant intervals. No validated death, ICU, or clinical-harm field was found. Clinical adjudication is required to interpret escalation as harm and to validate any text-derived severe-event label. No model has been fitted and no effect has been estimated.

## Method comparison and compute
The snapshot regression is the simple, auditable baseline. The sequence/state-space alternative tests a substantive temporal question, not a small predictive gain. CPU is appropriate for the tabular baseline and a bounded state-space fit; GPU is not required unless a larger sequence architecture is later justified. The future solver must newly fit both models, estimate held-out performance and uncertainty, and produce the prespecified comparison; readiness checks are not scientific results.

## Missing evidence and next work
Verify the procedure-category outcome dictionary and event counts, harmonize assay units or restrict to within-assay standardized changes, audit documentation-derived proxies, and obtain expert adjudication for clinical harm. Literature evidence must be inspected and claim-mapped before final selection.
