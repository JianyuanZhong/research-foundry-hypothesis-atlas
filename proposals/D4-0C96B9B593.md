## Scientific question and hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports an association between frailty, grip or related reserve measures and mortality or perioperative vulnerability in colorectal-cancer populations [K1, K2], and supports separating CRC and non-CRC mortality with competing-risk methods [K3]. It does not establish that an objectively measured, prediagnostic reserve profile distinguishes durable host vulnerability from occult cancer decline.

The falsifiable hypothesis is: among incident C18-C20 cases, lower prediagnostic measured reserve at least two years before diagnosis is associated with a greater five-year cumulative incidence of non-cancer death than CRC-specific death, after adjustment for age, sex, BMI, smoking, calendar time, comorbidity and CRC subsite; adding reserve improves held-out non-cancer risk prediction over the same routine-data baseline. This is a prognostic/descriptive association claim, not a causal treatment-tolerance, stage, recurrence or mechanism claim.

The leading rival is timing or selection: a measurement close to diagnosis may capture occult cancer/cachexia, while missing stage, treatment and UKB volunteer selection may create the signal. The experiment distinguishes these explanations with lag strata, cause-specific comparisons, missingness/selection analyses and an external-cause negative-control endpoint. These checks can bound the rival, not establish mechanism.

## Falsification and interpretation

The timing falsification repeats the prespecified analysis in 0-<2, 2-<5 and >=5 years from assessment, retaining >=2 years as primary. A signal confined to 0-<2 years or disappearing at >=5 years is adverse to durable prediagnostic reserve and supports occult disease/timing, not confirmation.

Compare CRC-specific, other-cancer, non-cancer and unknown-cause patterns. A non-cancer increment that persists beyond two years, is not reproduced for CRC death, and is stable under cause sensitivities is supportive of prognostic specificity. A similar or larger CRC-death signal is adverse to the proposed separation and is compatible with stage/tumour burden; it does not prove a mechanism.

Compare all incident cases with reserve-complete cases on age, sex, lag, BMI, smoking, centre/available demographic fields, comorbidity and diagnosis year; model reserve availability and use inverse-probability weighting as a sensitivity. Report missingness by lag and outcome. Material disagreement between complete-case, imputed and weighted analyses makes the result selection-sensitive. Fit the external-cause V01-Y89 negative-control endpoint with the same exposure and timing rules. A strong matching external-cause association suggests general frailty, volunteer selection or coding artefact and is adverse to cancer-specific interpretation.

Report paired-record discordance, duplicate eids, impossible orderings, tied dates, unknown causes and counts by source. Repeat with 41270/41280 inpatient diagnosis records where feasible. Compare the competing-risk analysis with naive censoring of competing deaths. Use fixed exposure permutation and outcome permutation negative probes only after the primary analysis is locked; retained signal indicates leakage.

Supportive results require a non-cancer reserve association and calibrated held-out improvement over B0 in the >=2-year primary cohort, compatible uncertainty, persistence in >=5 years, no material selection instability, and no matching external-cause or CRC-specific signal. This supports a prognostic regularity and motivates external validation or prospective reserve measurement; it does not establish treatment tolerance, causality or mechanism.

Adverse results are no incremental information, reversal of the differential contrast, CRC-dominant association, timing-only association, strong external-cause association, or coding/order instability. Report the relevant rival explanation and do not claim durable host reserve.

Inconclusive results are sparse events, an undocumented tau_source, wide intervals crossing clinically relevant effects, severe missingness, cause instability, failed calibration or unavailable B0+D. Do not call an imprecise null a refutation; defer to longer verified follow-up, registry linkage, expert cause adjudication or an independent cohort.

## Clinical advance and limits

If supportive, the study would define a clinically useful prognostic boundary: whether low-cost prediagnostic reserve adds information about competing non-cancer mortality after CRC diagnosis beyond routine variables and timing. This could motivate prospective geriatric/nutritional assessment and a treatment-planning study. It cannot justify withholding therapy, infer toxicity or dose tolerance, establish a biological pathway, or generalize beyond UKB. TNM stage, grade, treatment, toxicity, performance status, validated frailty, adjudicated causes and external validation require linkage, expert review or another study.
