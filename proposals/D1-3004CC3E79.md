Candidate: evolution child of `[prior hypothesis]` (board top, mu 33.0, valid, refs complete).
Origin: expert seed `[starting question]` ("Asynchronous organ recovery").
Status: design frozen. Itemids re-verified THIS episode by reading `icu/d_items.csv.gz` and

## 1. Unresolved question
After circulatory stabilisation in sepsis/septic shock, some patients recover kidney and lung function
together, others recover one organ bed while the other stays impaired ("cross-organ recovery asynchrony").
The parent (ep-10) replaced the 7-day COMPOSITE deterioration outcome with an organ-MATCHED vs
CROSS-MATCHED contrast: does the slower-recovering limb predict failure of ITS OWN organ (asymmetry > 0),
or does it just reflect one shared severity/observation trajectory (asymmetry ~ 0)?
That reframing is the right discriminating step, but it retains a **measurement circularity for the renal
limb**: the renal predictor (creatinine/urine recovery) and the renal outcome (RRT escalation) are the
same variable family, so a positive renal asymmetry can be produced by the measurement itself (K3). This
episode keeps the parent's science and tightens only the renal-outcome orthogonality.

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival
- Leading claim (H1): recovery asynchrony carries **organ-specific information** - the matched-limb
  predicts its own organ's failure more strongly than the cross-limb (matched-minus-cross asymmetry > 0).
- Strongest rival (H0): asynchrony is downstream of a **single shared severity/observation trajectory**
  sampled unevenly across beds (K1 shared trajectory; observation-intensity M3); the two organ beds fail
  about equally, so asymmetry ~ 0, and any residual renal signal is creatinine/RRT measurement coupling.
- The two explanations differ on the sign/magnitude of the matched-minus-cross asymmetry after a shared
  inflammatory axis and observation intensity are held; this is a falsifying prediction, not merely a
  performance comparison.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries (no future information)
- Population: MIMIC-IV ICU stays (`icu/icustays`, key `stay_id`/`hadm_id`/`subject_id`) with sepsis /
  septic shock (sepsis3-style: suspected infection from `microbiologyevents` + antibiotics in
  `prescriptions`/`emar`, plus SOFA-relevant organ support; OR `hosp/diagnoses_icd` sepsis codes as a
  fallback sensitivity definition).
- t* = first timestamp of a **sustained MAP >= 65 mmHg for >= 2 consecutive hours without vasopressor
  escalation** (mapping `icu/chartevents` itemids 220052/220181; vasopressor inputevents 221906/221289/
  222315/221749/221662/221653), computed strictly BEFORE outcome measurement.
- Predictor window: [t*-24h, t*]. Outcomes: (t*, t*+7d]. No predictor time after t*; censor at ICU
  discharge/death.
- Split: one locked temporal split, earliest 70% of `icustays.intime` = development, latest 30% = holdout.

## 4. Variables and bindings (verified present in frozen archive)
- Renal limb (recovery), from `hosp/labevents` itemid **50912** creatinine + `icu/outputevents` urine
  226559/226560/226566/226627/226631/226713: slope of creatinine and urine output over [t*-24h, t*].
- Respiratory limb (recovery), `icu/chartevents` FiO2 **223835** + `hosp/labevents` pO2 **50821**:
  P/F and its trend; ventilation status `icu/procedureevents` 225792/225794, extubation 227194.
- M0 (same-time snapshot severity): SOFA-relevant components at t* (MAP, creatinine, lactate 50813,
  bilirubin 50885, platelets 51265, WBC 51301, GCS/RASS 228096) + vasopressor dose + vent status.
- M2* (independent shared inflammatory recovery axis, K1): WBC 51301 decay, platelet 51265 recovery,
  absolute lymphocytes 51133 over [t*-24h, t*]. Deliberately NOT built from the two organ limbs.
- M3 (observation intensity/ascertainment): count of labs/charts/vitals per organ in [t*-24h, t*].
- Renal outcome (orthogonalised): **new** RRT escalation after t* (`icu/procedureevents` 225441/225802/
  225803/225809/225955) in patients RRT-free at t*; sensitivity outcome = KDIGO creatinine stage
  increase with a >= 24h predictor-outcome separation to break same-measurement coupling.
- Respiratory outcome: reintubation or invasive-vent escalation after t* (227194, 225792/225794).

## 5. Analysis and estimand
- Primary estimand: matched-minus-cross difference in holdout AUROC (and in standardized logistic
  coefficient) for own-organ 7-day failure, adjusting M0 + M2* + M3, with 1000-sample bootstrap 95% CI.
- Method ladder (baseline vs alternative on the SAME question):
  - B0 baseline: interpretable logistic regression, prespecified covariates.
  - A1 alternative: joint latent-variable / dynamic-factor model that explicitly separates an
    organ-specific residual factor from a shared severity factor; A1 is the only arm that can attribute
    the asymmetry to organ-specific vs shared variance. B0 shows whether a simple additive model loses
    the shared/separable decomposition.
- Negative control: permuted-limb labels (predictor and outcome limbs scrambled) must yield asymmetry CI
  covering 0.
- Resource: CPU-only, tabular extraction + logistic/EM factor fit, budget < 1 h; no GPU needed.

## 6. Falsification criteria (prespecified)
- Supportive (H1): matched-minus-cross asymmetry >= 0.02 AUROC with 95% bootstrap CI excluding 0 on the
  locked holdout, for >= 1 organ bed, reproduced under the orthogonalised renal outcome.
- Adverse (H0): asymmetry <= 0, or its CI lies within +/-0.01 equivalence margin of 0 for both beds.
- Inconclusive: CI wide and straddling 0.02, or driven entirely by M3/observation intensity, or the
  permutation control is non-null (then the design fails internally and is not interpreted).

## 7. What each outcome would/would not establish
- Supportive -> evidence for organ-specific divergence beyond a shared inflammatory axis and observation
  intensity; still association, not mechanism or causality.
- Adverse -> asynchrony reduces to a shared severity/observation trajectory; then organ-asynchrony
  monitoring adds no organ-specific information and the line should be parked.
- Inconclusive -> retain; requires larger cohort/power or a renal marker independent of creatinine.

## Limits (honest)
- Single-centre retrospective; association only, no causal/mechanistic claim.
- Renal limb (creatinine/urine) biased by muscle mass and RRT (K3); cystatin C unavailable -> renal
  asymmetry attenuated/uncertain. Partly mitigated by orthogonalising the renal outcome (new RRT /
  delayed KDIGO), but not removed.
- Observation intensity adjusted (M3), not eliminated.
- Cohort prevalence/power not established in discovery -> inconclusive contrast is possible.
