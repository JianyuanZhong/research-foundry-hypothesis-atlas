# Is creatinine-cystatin-C discordance an acute-injury phenotype or only a nonspecific renal-risk marker?

## Successor question

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent tests whether positive creatinine-based minus cystatin-C-based eGFR discordance (D), especially with low grip, adds calibrated five-year prognostic information for a first hospital-coded N17 event. This leaves an important clinical ambiguity: does the signal identify acute-injury susceptibility, or is it merely a nonspecific marker of future renal coding and unrecognized chronic disease? The distinction changes whether a positive result would motivate follow-up around acute stressors or chronic-kidney-disease evaluation. This study estimates prognosis only; it cannot test whether either action improves outcomes.

The strongest directly relevant evidence is the locally inspected Haines et al. critical-illness study at references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt. In 38 mechanically ventilated adults it compared serial creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance and rectus-femoris ultrasound, supporting biological plausibility of creatinine-based overestimation during muscle loss. It does not establish low-grip meaning in community adults, future N17 versus N18 coding, or surveillance benefit.

The falsifiable claim is: among UK Biobank adults without recorded N17, N18 or N19 renal diagnoses at biomarker assessment, positive D, particularly with lower grip, is more strongly associated with a first subsequent hospital-coded N17 event than a first N18 event, after age, sex, body size, grip and creatinine-based eGFR; among those with a first N17 event, it is also associated with subsequent N18 coding more than the reverse sequence.

## Analysis and alternatives

The simple baseline is competing-risk cause-specific Cox with Aalen-Johansen:

M0: age, sex, BMI, weight, grip and eGFRcr.
M1: M0 plus eGFRcys (or D, never eGFRcr plus eGFRcys plus D together).
M2: M1 plus prespecified continuous D-by-grip interaction and restricted cubic splines for age and eGFRcr.

Fit separate hazards for N17-only, N18-only and mixed/other; death is censored for cause-specific hazards and retained in Aalen-Johansen. Fine-Gray is sensitivity. Transition models use transition-specific risk sets.

The substantive learned alternative is a CPU gradient-boosted multi-task discrete-time competing-risk model with exactly the M0-M2 inputs, participant split and five-year target. It has cause-specific outputs for N17-only, N18-only and mixed/other, and transition outputs only if support passes. Shallow trees and tuning/early stopping on the tuning partition constrain resources. It can reveal nonlinear D/grip thresholds and endpoint-specific heterogeneity missed by one interaction; a small discrimination gain is not a scientific success. Compare held-out cause-specific Brier, calibration intercept/slope, absolute event-type contrasts and accumulated-local-effect curves with bootstrap intervals.

Use deterministic SHA-256(eid) buckets 0-79, 80-99 for 60/20/20 training/tuning/test partitions; the final partition is not external validation. ## Falsification and limits

Supportive evidence requires passed unit/date/join gates, adequate N17-only and N18-only events, a directionally larger prespecified D-by-low-grip association for N17-only than N18-only reaching the materiality threshold, persistence under primary-inpatient and broad-history sensitivities, and concordant calibrated learned-model risks. If supported, N17-to-N18 asymmetry is secondary trajectory evidence.

Adverse evidence includes a larger N18 association, a null or reversed differential, loss under ascertainment/history sensitivities, or learned-model calibration that does not reproduce the competing-risk pattern. This argues against calling discordance acute-injury-specific; it does not prove cystatin C has no value.

Inconclusive evidence includes unverifiable units, invalid/unpaired dates, impossible administrative boundary, sparse events, missingness, duplicate joins, leakage, unstable bootstrap or nonconvergence. Do not replace failed N17/N18 analysis with an easier all-renal endpoint.

QA can check joins, formulas, event classification, risk sets, splits, calibration and whether conclusions follow outputs. It cannot establish AKI severity, CKD stage, measured GFR, causality, treatment or surveillance benefit, transportability, or clinical appropriateness. Those require adjudicated laboratory trajectories/urine output, measured-GFR and body-composition anchors, treatments, expert review and independent/prospective validation.
