# Absolute-slot, modality-audited review allocation for documentary M2

## Unresolved question, evidence boundary, and advance

The consequential but bounded decision is which adults should receive a scarce multidisciplinary review before a first eligible liver resection for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), because the eventual resection pathology may document high-grade microvascular invasion (M2). The action is allocation of a fixed number of reviews. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment, systemic or locoregional therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

**Strongest claim supported before this experiment.** Direct catalog, schema, header, and full aggregate source inspection establishes that the HCC snapshot contains patient-linked encounters, two-source procedure rows, CT/MRI examination accessions with acquisition-like starts and eventual narrative bodies, untimed same-encounter pathology narratives, and longitudinal medication/order records. It does not contain report authored/final/release/view times or immutable report versions, pathology time or specimen identifiers, sampling adequacy, raw images/slides, observed MDT capacity or reviewer hours, clinician action, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, treatment effect, or patient benefit. The laboratory table has one field named `检验时间` but no collection, result-entry, verification, release/view, accession/panel, or unit field, so laboratory values and laboratory missingness have zero primary lineage.

The aggregate audit in `episode22_source_audit.json` read the configured files without emitting identifiers or narrative text and confirmed 105,044 encounter rows, 338,040 procedure rows, 419,996 examination rows, 46,395 pathology rows, and 28,159,928 laboratory rows. Procedures comprise 107,124 `手麻系统` and 230,916 `病案手术` rows, with 15,874 missing and 183,354 midnight starts. A second full-source grain audit found 595 nonblank examination accessions with multiple start values and 8,932 pathology encounters with multiple rows. These facts support source-state, accession-first, and all-pathology-row reconstruction; they do not establish a cohort, report availability, M2 truth, or model performance.

Current full-text public evidence establishes importance, not the tested result. Li et al. (2026, DOI `10.1186/s12957-026-04229-2`, PMCID `PMC13005532`; frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports worse recurrence and mortality for M2 than M0/M1 in a 243-patient retrospective resection cohort. Neves and Soares (2026, DOI `10.3390/cancers18132028`, PMCID `PMC13360217`; frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) found that 35/36 HCC early-recurrence AI studies were retrospective, only 6/36 had external validation, and calibration and clinical-utility assessment were inconsistent. Huang et al. (2026, DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`; frozen full HTML `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) externally validated a binary-MVI CT-margin/AFP model in 487 development and 256 validation patients using raw-image review, complete required data, and defined pathology sampling; it does not validate M2 extraction, routine report semantics, report visibility, or review benefit here. The research-ambition papers are demonstrations of rigor, not evidence for this HCC claim; the Cell main article remains unavailable and is not claimed read.

**Substantive advance.** The modality-pattern parent has a rigorous CT/MRI access frontier but a risk threshold whose workload is not grounded. The queue parent makes workload explicit but calls state-specific percentages “fixed capacity”; when admissible source states have different eligible denominators, those percentages produce different numbers of reviews. This child instead holds an absolute integer number of review slots fixed across every source state, model, and bootstrap replicate, while preserving state-complete population uncertainty and adding an auditable CT-only/MRI-only/dual access frontier.

## Falsifiable hypotheses

Let \mathcal S be the complete, pre-outcome certified outer set of coherent source states. Each state jointly fixes operation episode and clock, selected encounter, eligibility and period, prior recorded treatment, accession-first report units and patterns, report masks, pathology composite, documentary outcome, and eligible denominator. No component may be mixed across states.

Before pathology grades or semantic features are read, define `N_min=min_s N_T(s)`. The available-data primary uses three fixed sensitivity slot counts `K_q=floor(q*N_min)` for `q in {0.05,0.10,0.20}`; freeze these integers once from the original ambiguity ledger, and never recompute them by state or bootstrap. Require `N_T(s)>=K_q` in every state. A separate external service contract may instead preregister one absolute integer `K_ext` for the same calendar/service unit, reviewer-hour ceiling, and materiality margin before outcome review. The HCC data do not contain that contract.

For model `M`, state `s`, and fixed slots `K`, let `Q_K(M,s)` be exactly the K highest-ranked eligible patients and define capture per 100 reviews

`R_K(M,s)=100*K^{-1} sum_i 1[i in Q_K(M,s)]Y_i(s)`.

The models are semantic strategy S, the same fitted S pipeline with its semantic block masked after fitting S0, and an independently fitted structured baseline B. Co-primary contrasts are `D_K=R_K(S)-R_K(S0)` for within-pipeline semantic attribution and `C_K=R_K(S)-R_K(B)` for complete-strategy performance.

**P-store.** With every selected nonempty eventual CT/MRI report body exposed, simultaneous one-sided 97.5% lower confidence endpoints for both D and C exceed five additional documentary-M2 cases per 100 reviews at every frozen K, over every certified outer state, while all extraction, calibration, null, event, state-completeness, and queue-integrity gates pass.

**P-access.** If P-store passes, there is a nonempty, prespecified CT/MRI access-guarantee set under which the same lower endpoints remain above five per 100 reviews when visibility masks are adversarial. This is a deterministic requirement frontier, not an estimate that reports were available.

**P-decision-time is not identified by HCC alone.** It is conditionally supportable only if separate, representative source-system audits show (i) an absolute capacity contract matching a tested K and (ii) exact-version report final/release/view availability whose simultaneous lower bounds enter the supportive access set. Even then the conclusion is workflow feasibility in this historical setting, not benefit from review.

## Population, temporal boundaries, and state certificate

The unit is a person identified by `患者主索引`; same-encounter joins use `(患者主索引,就诊号)`. Before outcome parsing, create a lossless raw-row ambiguity ledger and two nested state sets: a high-sensitivity outer source-consistent envelope and an adjudicated envelope. Every branch must terminate in a state or a recorded exclusion reason; no top-N truncation, sampled state approximation, or silent impossible-state deletion is permitted. Exact quotienting is allowed only with raw backpointers and independent replay. Primary support requires the outer envelope; adjudicated-only support is sensitive/inconclusive.

Two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician freeze the resection dictionary and cross-source episode rules. Enumerate defensible episodes across `手麻系统` and `病案手术`, including same-day component groupings and adjacent-date alternatives. Use a clinically linked valid `手麻系统.开始时间` as an exact start; treat a case-record midnight/date proxy without validated clock as interval `[d,d+24h)`. The first eligible episode is selected separately in each coherent state, never by raw-row order.

A state member must be age >=18 on the selected encounter, have all-row pathology text on that encounter explicitly supporting HCC, and have no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[t_dec-365 days,t_dec)`, using state-matched procedures, medications, and orders with valid source clocks. Recorded absence is not biological treatment-naivety.

Set `t_dec=t_op-24 hours`; repeat locked 12-, 48-, and 72-hour buffers. Develop in 2015–2018, tune penalty and nonnegative-slope recalibration only in 2019, and test once in 2020–2021. Every patient with any admissible test state is quarantined from development/tuning, even if another state assigns a different period or nonmembership. Use 2022 onward only for documentation-drift audit; make no current-era performance claim. Nonmembers in a state contribute neither outcome nor queue entry and are never coded as negatives.

## Report and pathology units

After exact-row deduplication with raw ordinals retained, group every nonblank examination accession on `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` before time filtering, modality assignment, text extraction, or visibility masking. One accession is indivisible. Use all component rows. A primary unit is time-eligible only if its component-clock state places the whole accession in `[t_dec-90 days,t_dec)`; the conservative deterministic case uses the maximum valid component start, while missing or materially conflicting components remain explicit alternatives. Blank `检查号` rows are logged but cannot become singleton semantic reports.

Two blinded abdominal radiologists freeze a development-only accession-level map assigning exactly one of CT, MRI, non-target, or unresolved/conflicted from all `检查` and `机器型号` components. An accession is never copied into both modalities. Select at most the latest eligible accession per modality in each state; ties use valid component clocks, normalized accession, then frozen source ordinal. Concatenate all `检查所见` and `检查诊断` components in frozen order. Patient stored patterns are O (no nonempty selected body), C, M, or CM. O remains in every denominator and queue.

For pathology, compose every deduplicated row on the state-selected encounter using `病理`, `检查所见`, and `检查诊断` before assigning one endpoint. A frozen position-aware parser may assign `Y=1` only from an anchored selected-value M2 statement and `Y=0` from an anchored M0/M1 statement. Boilerplate, unanchored mention, missing grade, binary-positive ungraded MVI, cross-row conflict, uncertain specimen linkage, or inadequate documented sampling yields unknown `Y in {0,1}`, never exclusion or favorable row selection. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors review all parsed M2, every test unknown/conflict, and stratified M0/M1 samples; a third resolves disagreements. The endpoint is documentary M2, not biological M2.

## Predictors, models, and queues

B is a compact laboratory-independent structured/process baseline: state-selected age and sex; CT/MRI/O acquisition indicators; number of eligible accession units; acquisition-to-decision recency; component count; and development-frequency machine category. It contains no report-body token, lesion measurement, semantic feature, diagnosis text, laboratory field, identifier, calendar year, pathology, or post-cutoff fact.

S uses the identical B block plus a frozen interpretable report block from selected `检查所见/检查诊断`: maximum stated lesion diameter; lesion-count category; capsule complete/interrupted/absent; irregular margin; arterial hyperenhancement; washout; peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity; satellite lesion; portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus; cirrhosis; and ascites, each with present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned coding. Radiologists and a bilingual NLP reviewer freeze synonyms, negation, uncertainty, and unit rules before test outcomes. S0 is never refit: apply S's exact coefficients, intercept, preprocessing, penalty, and recalibration after replacing only the semantic block with its masked-state vector. Full masking must make S and S0 scores, ranks, ties, queues, and D bitwise identical/zero.

Fit interpretable elastic-net logistic B and S with the inherited patient-wise worst-admissible-state convex loss in development and use the same rule for 2019 tuning/recalibration, every bootstrap, and semantic-null refit. Never average states equally or infer a visibility probability. Test queues contain exactly fixed K eligible patient slots in every state; ties use a frozen hash of `患者主索引`, never pathology. Weighted bootstrap duplicates retain cluster multiplicity, while K remains frozen.

Report capture, precision, recall, false-review count, queue overlap, AUPRC, AUROC, Brier score, and calibration, but only D and C at fixed K are primary. Prespecified references are S0, B, a seeded random eligible queue, and an age/sex-only queue at identical K. Discrimination cannot rescue adverse capture.

## Exact modality access frontier and external audits

A visibility bit exposes an entire selected accession body; zero masks every body-derived semantic and formatting field while preserving acquisition/process facts. For each coherent source state, define four guarantees on a frozen 0.05 grid:

- `h_C`: fraction of C-pattern patients whose CT accession is exposed;
- `h_M`: fraction of M-pattern patients whose MRI accession is exposed;
- `h_A`: fraction of CM-pattern patients with at least one accession exposed;
- `h_B`: fraction of CM-pattern patients with both accessions exposed, with `h_B<=h_A`.

Constraints use at least `ceil(h_g N_g(s))` state-specific eligible patients. O has no coordinate and remains fixed. Among singly exposed CM patients the adversary may choose CT or MRI. Pattern and membership may change with operation/clock state and therefore must be reconstructed inside, not before, each state.

For every K and D/C contrast, exact state-expanded mixed-integer optimization chooses coherent patient states, unknown outcomes, visibility masks, ranks, ties, and top-K queue membership to obtain lower and upper capture endpoints. Report-body access constraints, source states, and Y choices remain coupled. Validate all instances with union N<=12 against exhaustive enumeration; test all-unavailable, all-exposed, CT-only, MRI-only, both, O, ties, variable membership, and K infeasibility. Because “at least h” shrinks the feasible set as h rises, lower endpoints must be coordinatewise nondecreasing; failure is fatal. Define H as grid points whose simultaneous D/C lower limits exceed five for every K and whose gates pass; report the Pareto-minimal antichain. Sparse or absent patterns make that coordinate claim unavailable, not permission to delete patients.

The report audit must use the identical population, source-state clock, accession unit, modality map, latest-selection rule, and body hash. It must obtain authored/final/release time, preferably clinician-view time, and immutable version/hash proving that the exact body scored existed at `t_dec`. It reports simultaneous one-sided 97.5% lower confidence bounds for C, M, CM-any, and CM-both access, with patient clustering and calendar/workflow strata, rounded down to the grid. Acquisition/order time or eventual nonempty text is insufficient. Conditional decision-time transport requires the conservative audit vector to lie in H.

The capacity audit is separate and must preregister an absolute integer K for the same calendar/service unit, maximum queue and reviewer hours, reviewer role, slot fungibility, and minimum material capture gain. It may map to a tested K only without outcome-driven interpolation. A 97.5% simultaneous model/frontier family and a 97.5% joint capacity/report-audit family give joint noncoverage <=5% by Bonferroni; a preregistered joint resampling scheme may replace this. Missing or nonrepresentative audits leave deployment inconclusive.

## Analysis, gates, and interpretation

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates, rebuilding ledger states, accessions, pathology composites, preprocessing, fits, calibration, queues, and endpoints. K remains frozen. A max-centered-deviation procedure supplies one 97.5% simultaneous model family over D/C, three K values, four buffers, all-store endpoints, and every frontier point. More than 2.5% failed replicates, optimizer disagreement, or state-certificate failure invalidates support.

Use 1,000 development/tuning-only semantic-null repetitions permuting the complete semantic block within frozen year, modality/pattern, accession cardinality, and formatting family, followed by full robust refit and S0 reconstruction. Observed all-store D must exceed the simultaneous 97.5th null envelope. Label permutation must be null; post-cutoff injection must trigger leakage rejection.

Global support gates include `N_min>=1,000`; `N_T(s)>=K_.20` in every state; at least 75 adjudicated documentary M2 events in every claimed primary test envelope; trustworthy anchored grade in >=85%; every test unknown reviewed; parser kappa >=0.90, M2 PPV >=0.98 and sensitivity >=0.95; accession modality agreement >=0.95 and unresolved <=1%; each claimed pattern >=100 patients and >=20 M2; semantic feature PPV/sensitivity >=0.85 by claimed modality; no required-feature/pattern shift from 2019 to either test year >15 percentage points; all/no-report calibration slope 0.80–1.20 and intercept -0.10–0.10; exact K queues; complete inference; shared states; and zero forbidden/lab lineage.

- **Supportive stored-content:** every gate passes and simultaneous lower endpoints for both D and C exceed five additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 reviews for every K under all-store exposure and every outer state. Conclude only that eventual stored report semantics improved historical allocation within the fixed S pipeline and the complete S strategy beat B at equal absolute review workload in this documentary-M2 reconstruction.
- **Supportive access requirement:** stored-content support passes and H is nonempty. Conclude only that the reported CT/MRI guarantees would suffice under the prespecified adversarial masks; do not say they occurred.
- **Conditional workflow support:** both external audits pass under joint error control, K matches, and the report-access lower vector lies in H. Conclude conditional historical workflow feasibility, not deployment benefit.
- **Uniform adverse:** with valid computation/gates, the simultaneous upper endpoint for D or C is <=5 at a primary K across the complete outer envelope; <=0 denotes harmful or no incremental capture. This rejects material value at that K. All-store failure cannot be rescued by greater report availability.
- **Mixed/fragile:** some K values, modalities, buffers, or source states support and others do not; D supports but C does not; or C supports but D does not. The joint hypothesis fails, but absence of value is not established. D-only means semantics changed S's allocation without beating optimized B; C-only can reflect refitting/shared-feature differences rather than runtime semantics.
- **Inconclusive:** intervals cross five, source-state bounds straddle the margin, any gate fails, states are incomplete, K cannot be filled, evidence is sparse/ungradable, or an audit is absent/mismatched. Favorable AUC, complete cases, point estimates, unrestricted text, or post-hoc K cannot rescue it.

Operation identity, Chinese semantics, specimen linkage and sampling, biological M2, capacity/margin acceptability, actual report access, clinician action, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, and patient benefit require qualified review, source-system evidence, stakeholder agreement, prospective silent-mode validation, or another study.
