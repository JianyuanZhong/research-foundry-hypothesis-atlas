## Required outputs, uncertainty, and verifier boundary

The executable Harbor job must declare the read-only archive/catalog inputs and derived output paths. It must emit a machine-readable cohort row for every eligible subject and clone, all attrition and exclusion reasons, partition checksum, row-level temporal provenance, balance components and units, weight source/conversion, treatment/deviation evidence, overlap/propensity diagnostics, trajectory strata, adherence/censor weights, ESS, ALDL7 interval classes, RRT date brackets, mortality, secondary outcomes, bootstrap estimates, residual-bias grid and the final branch label. No final number may be reported without a linked computed output.

The verifier can check archive and catalog hashes, headers, table/member names, joins, partition, two-clock classification, no post-boundary contributions, fluid units/exclusions, weight hierarchy, strategies, eMAR concordance, cloning/censoring, overlap rules, trajectory covariates, weights/ESS, falsification tests, bootstrap uncertainty, partial-identification arithmetic, safety gates, utility-grid arithmetic and whether the supportive/adverse/inconclusive label follows the outputs. It must reject correct computation paired with unsupported conclusions, including calling fluid/urine process changes clinical benefit, calling a null RRT signal renal safety, treating unknown ALDL7 as success, treating all-eligible association as an equipoise effect, or making a treatment recommendation.

The verifier cannot establish actual bedside awareness of finalized rows, unmeasured indication or goals of care, exchangeability, adherence meaning beyond recorded delivery, completeness of fluid/urine/ventilation records, clinical extubation truth, ward/post-discharge ventilation or dialysis, note phenotype validity, renal safety, transportability, patient preferences, or net clinical utility. Those claims require clinical adjudication, unavailable evidence, external validation and preferably randomization.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Changed: the parent’s broad causal contrast is now explicitly secondary; the primary decision estimand is restricted to measured overlap and uses trajectory-calibrated baseline adjustment plus time-varying adherence/censor weights. Positivity, ESS, trajectory-stratum stability and a quantitative residual-indication grid are hard interpretation gates. A transparent, externally chosen harm-weight grid shows what benefit would be required to offset observed harm without pretending that MIMIC supplies preferences.

Unchanged: the discovery split, t0 and 24-hour window, actual-delivery definition, two clocks, unit-safe balance, ALDL7 partial identification, RRT/mortality gates, exact MIMIC provenance and verifier limits. The principal uncertainty remains unmeasured readiness/intent and incomplete observation after ICU transfer; if those threats are material, the correct result is inconclusive and the next step is adjudication or a randomized trial.
