# Episode 74: no-change recommendation after UKB successor audit

## Decision

Retain the latest valid record-density-standardized kidney-specificity experiment in
`[prior hypothesis]`, with its nested AFG1-versus-AF1 scientific
anchors `[prior hypothesis]` and
`[prior hypothesis]`. This is a deliberate no-change scientific
recommendation, not a request to refit or a compiler repair.

The current question is already the strongest clinically consequential question that
the configured UKB data can identify without unverified clinical assumptions:

> In the frozen O1_hold=2,334 and equal 466-slot AFG1-versus-AF1 comparison, does the
> AFG1-minus-AF1 contrast for first recorded inpatient N18 before death exceed the
> corresponding broad nonrenal diagnosis-record contrast after both are standardized
> to the same prespecified distribution of five-year pre-index nonrenal record
> density?

This asks whether grip adds kidney-specific allocation information after routine
variables, observed/fallback UACR, and bioimpedance-derived whole-body FFM, while
testing whether any apparent advantage is explained by differential prior diagnosis
recording. A favorable result prioritizes a prospective study; it does not justify
routine selective cystatin-C testing or clinical benefit.

## Why no new endpoint or subgroup is justified

The latest parent already contains the clinically useful alternatives considered here.

* Negative-control: the broad nonrenal code-record outcome G is computed from the same
  dated inpatient arrays and is standardized using RD0/RD1/RD2+ strata. Adding another
  generic control would be redundant and would spend the same 62-person swap
  information repeatedly.
* Competing risk: death is already separately reported as C5 and never counts as
  kidney disease or rescues a failed N18 contrast. Cause of death, emigration and
  censoring are unavailable.
* Subgroup: observed-low-UACR is already the inherited qualification. The parent
  audit reports that 58 of 62 directional AFG1/AF1 swaps are observed-low-UACR, so a
  new subgroup would be nearly the same contrast with less information. The
  prespecified RD standardization is a more informative qualification.
* Durability: a second N18 code or persistence definition would be a new estimand
  requiring reliable encounter/admission structure, recurrence rules, and a
  clinically adjudicated persistent CKD concept. The snapshot has position-paired
  diagnosis/date arrays but no encounter identifier, outpatient capture guarantee,
  emigration/censoring, or adjudication. It would also be sparser than the
  parent’s all-holdout total of 40 five-year N18-before-death events. It is not a
  defensible replacement for the first-event analysis.
* Alternative downstream endpoint: all-cause death is not kidney-specific and is
  already a competing-outcome diagnostic. Replacing N18 with death or a composite
  would allow nonrenal mortality to create apparent support, undoing the clinical
  question being tested.

The decision is therefore to preserve the frozen population, policies, endpoint,
standardization, margin, gates, bootstrap and interpretation. No design-defining
element is changed.

## Outcomes, record density and estimands

Compute inherited equation-defined H1/D1 and observed-low-UACR hierarchy first, with
shared missing-label bounds and no downstream rescue. These are not measured GFR,
persistent CKD or mechanistic outcomes.

For each O1_hold participant, define t5 as t1 plus five calendar years, mapping
February 29 to February 28. Pair every trimmed uppercase `41270-0.j` code only with
its same-position `41280-0.j` date, j=0,...,258. Let T18 be the earliest valid
same-position date whose code begins exactly N18. Let Tdeath be the earliest valid
date in `40000-0.0` or `40000-1.0`.

E18=1 iff `t1 < T18 <= t5` and (death absent or `T18 < Tdeath`). N17 never
satisfies E18; death on or before T18, including same-day death, prevents E18=1.
C5 is separately 1 for death in (t1,t5] with no prior qualifying N18. Death never
counts as E18.

The broad nonrenal control G is the first valid post-t1 code in the frozen initial
set `A B C D E F G H I J K L M S T Z`, no later than t5 and strictly before death.
N, R, O, P, Q and U are excluded. G is a dated diagnosis-record proxy, not an
admission count or clinical event.

Before policy-specific outcomes, calculate pre-index record density:
`R_i` is the count of positions containing a frozen-set nonrenal code with a
valid same-position date in [t1 minus five calendar years, t1). Repeated positions
count because no encounter identifier is available. Freeze RD0 (R=0), RD1 (R=1)
and RD2+ (R>=2), with `q_s=N_s/2334), before opening policy outcomes.

The mandatory inherited fixed-capacity baselines are:
`Y18(p)=1000*sum(E18_i for i in S_p)/466`,
`YG(p)=1000*sum(G_i for i in S_p)/466`,
`Delta18=Y18(AFG1)-Y18(AF1)`,
`DeltaG=YG(AFG1)-YG(AF1)`, and `Psi=Delta18-DeltaG`.
The parent’s one-event planning margin is
`m=1000/466=2.145922746781116`; it is not a validated utility or
cost-effectiveness threshold.

For each RD stratum s and policy p, use only the fixed policy-stratum denominator:
`r18_s(p)=sum(E18_i)/(count(S_p in s))` and
`rG_s(p)=sum(G_i)/(count(S_p in s))`.
Then:
`Y18_RD(p)=1000*sum_s q_s*r18_s(p)`,
`YG_RD(p)=1000*sum_s q_s*rG_s(p)`,
`Delta18_RD=Y18_RD(AFG1)-Y18_RD(AF1)`,
`DeltaG_RD=YG_RD(AFG1)-YG_RD(AF1)`,
`Psi_RD=Delta18_RD-DeltaG_RD`.
Report every stratum count, q_s, roster denominator, event numerator, rate and
weighted contribution, plus exact reconstruction identities. No survivor, complete-case,
event, swap or post-outcome denominator is allowed.

Use the inherited 2,000 participant-level paired bootstrap over canonical-eid-sorted
O1_hold, with frozen rosters and q_s in every draw and the inherited
PCG64DXSM seed construction. Report point estimates, SE, two-sided 95% intervals,
one-sided limits, finite-draw count, variance and the marked percentile fallback only
under its frozen condition. Do not refit or change the hierarchy.

## Gates, falsification and exhaustive meanings

Require all inherited provenance, chronology, duplicate-source, join, population,
roster, leakage, denominator and hierarchy checks. For N18 and RD interpretation also
require all three strata with at least 50 O1_hold participants, at least 10 members of
each policy per stratum, at least 10 E18 events in the AFG1 union, at least 3 in each
policy list, at least 4 across the 62-person symmetric difference, at least 4
symmetric-difference members per stratum unless the contribution is exactly equal,
at least 95% finite bootstrap draws and positive finite variance. Failed or
boundary-equal gates are inconclusive, never adverse.

Emit the inherited upstream, N18, death and parent-specificity labels first. Then emit
exactly one child label:

* `kidney_specificity_RD_supportive`: inherited hierarchy and N18 are supportive,
  all RD gates pass, the generic DeltaG one-sided upper limit is below m, and the
  one-sided lower limit for Psi_RD is above zero.
* `kidney_specificity_RD_adverse_generic`: all gates pass, the one-sided upper
  limit for Psi_RD is below zero, and the generic contrast is materially enriched.
  This falsifies the narrow standardized kidney-specificity claim, not clinical
  equivalence or AF1 superiority.
* `kidney_specificity_RD_mixed`: all gates pass and Psi_RD lower limit is above
  zero, but the generic contrast is materially enriched or standardized and
  unstandardized specificity disagree.
* `kidney_specificity_RD_inconclusive`: every other state, including sparse strata,
  failed integrity, nonpositive variance, wide limits, equality at a boundary or an
  inherited upstream/N18 failure.

A favorable child cannot rescue an inherited adverse or unresolved state. Support
means only a kidney-coded allocation contrast exceeding the generic record-density
contrast in this internal frozen comparison. Adverse results argue that generic
ascertainment is at least as plausible. Mixed results retain the generic-ascertainment
qualification. Inconclusive means unresolved, not no difference.

## Verification and claim boundary

Required outputs must include package/source/descriptor/schema hashes, row and join
counts, exact population and policy membership, t1/t5 and lookback chronology, malformed
and duplicate counts, RD strata and weights, inherited hierarchy, policy-specific
numerators/rates, all bootstrap summaries and gates, and conclusion sentences linked
to output keys.

Verifier fixtures must include supportive, adverse, mixed and inconclusive results,
correct arithmetic paired with unsupported conclusions, sparse or zero-information
strata, boundary equality, malformed/mispaired dates, N/R leakage into G, N17
substitution, same-day death, changed rosters/weights/denominators, leakage and
omitted output links.

Automatic checks can establish package identity, source identity, field presence,
one-to-one joins, encoded chronology, frozen membership, standardization, bootstrap
arithmetic, gates and conclusion consistency. They cannot establish complete clinical
capture, admissions, incident/persistent/adjudicated CKD, measured GFR, outpatient
disease, censoring/emigration, renal attribution of death, mechanism, actionability,
workflow, cost, fairness, transportability, safety or patient benefit. Those require
clinical adjudication, missing linkage, expert review, external validation and a
prospective study.
