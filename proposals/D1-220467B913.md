# Discordant recovery after acute kidney injury: a data-bound, falsifiable MIMIC-IV experiment

Parent: `[prior hypothesis]` (expert seed `[starting question]`, assessed **repairable**).
This is a **repair**: the seed's coherent clinical hypothesis is converted into a concrete,
falsifiable MIMIC-IV experiment with exact table/column bindings, a recovery landmark, a
baseline-vs-extended analysis plan, a bounded feasibility probe, and three inspected key
references. No full analysis is run here; only the bounded probe.

---

## 1. Hypothesis

**Supported claim (already established by prior work, not re-tested here):**
- Serum creatinine is an imperfect marker of kidney function in critical illness: creatinine-based
  eGFR systematically **overestimates** true (iohexol-measured) GFR after prolonged ICU stay,
  driven by muscle loss [K1].
- Urine output (UO) carries prognostic information **beyond** serum creatinine for AKI risk
  stratification [K2] and for subsequent RRT/CRRT initiation [K3].

**Unresolved claim (what this experiment tests):**
> In adult ICU patients who meet KDIGO creatinine-based AKI criteria and then show
> **creatinine-based recovery** (creatinine returns toward baseline), the subset whose recovery is
> **discordant** — i.e., creatinine has recovered but urine output remains low and/or net fluid
> balance remains persistently negative — has a **higher risk of in-hospital death and new renal
> replacement therapy (RRT)** than the concordant-recovery subset, **independent of** a
> creatinine-only baseline (creatinine recovery status + age + sex + illness severity).

The scientific opening: creatinine recovery is the de-facto "recovered AKI" signal used at the
bedside, but [K1] shows creatinine can fall while true GFR is still impaired (muscle loss lowers
creatinine generation). If so, a patient whose creatinine has "recovered" but whose UO/fluid status
has not may be misclassified as recovered and remain at high risk. This experiment asks whether that
discordance is a real, independently prognostic phenotype in a large EHR cohort, or an artifact of
measurement/selection.

**Leading explanation vs. strongest rival:**
- *Leading (mechanistic):* persistent low UO / negative fluid balance after creatinine recovery
  reflects residual renal hypoperfusion or tubular dysfunction not captured by creatinine → true
  higher risk.
- *Strongest rival (artifact):* the discordant phenotype is a proxy for **severity and selection** —
  sicker, more catheterized, more fluid-resuscitated patients both have low UO and die more, so the
  association is confounded by severity/selection rather than a distinct recovery biology.
- *Feasible comparison that separates them:* adjust for illness severity (SOFA/APACHE if computable),
  vasopressor exposure, and catheterization, and test the **incremental** prognostic value of the
  discordant indicator beyond the creatinine-only baseline (C-index, NRI/IDI). A severity-adjusted
  association with incremental discrimination supports the leading explanation; a null after
  adjustment supports the rival. (They remain not fully separable without measured GFR — see
  limitations.)

## 2. Study population

- **Source:** MIMIC-IV 3.1 (frozen archive `mimic-iv-3.1.zip`, snapshot
  `[source checksum]`).
- **Unit of analysis:** one ICU stay (`icu/icustays.stay_id`).
- **Inclusion:**
  - Adult: `hosp/patients.anchor_age >= 18`.
  - ICU stay length `icu/icustays.los >= 2.0` days (≥48 h) — ensures enough time for an AKI
    trajectory and a post-recovery observation window.
  - ≥1 serum creatinine measurement (`hosp/labevents`).
- **AKI definition (KDIGO creatinine criteria, simplified to what is computable in the probe;
  full 48 h / 0.3 mg/dL logic in the analysis):**
  - Baseline creatinine = earliest creatinine in the first 48 h of the ICU stay
    (`labevents.charttime` within `[icustays.intime, intime+48h]`); if none, earliest in the
    preceding 7 days of the same `hadm_id`.
  - AKI if, within the stay, peak creatinine ≥ 1.5× baseline (KDIGO stage ≥1 creatinine criterion)
    or absolute rise ≥ 0.3 mg/dL from baseline within 48 h.
- **Exclusion:** ICU stays with RRT **before** the recovery landmark (to keep "new RRT" a true
  post-landmark outcome); stays with no creatinine after the AKI peak (cannot define recovery).

## 4. Recovery landmark

- **AKI peak** = time of the maximum creatinine (`labevents.charttime` of the peak `valuenum`).
- **T_rec (recovery landmark)** = first time **after** the AKI peak at which creatinine returns to
  within 0.3 mg/dL of baseline (or < 1.5× baseline), i.e., the creatinine recovery criterion is met.
- **Eligibility at T_rec:**
  - ≥ 48 h of observation **after** T_rec (so UO/fluid status and outcomes can be measured post-
    landmark), and T_rec before ICU discharge.
  - **No RRT before T_rec** (excluded, so "new RRT" is a post-landmark event).
  - ≥ 1 urine-output record in the 24 h window around T_rec (for the UO axis); stays without any UO
    record are handled in the sensitivity/missing-data analysis, not silently dropped.

---

## 5. Discordant-recovery phenotype

At the **T_rec + 24 h** window, define the phenotype (creatinine criterion met by construction):
- **UO axis:** mean UO rate in the 24 h window **< 0.5 mL/kg/h** (using weight from §3). If the
  patient's pre-AKI UO is available (first 48 h), also test **< 50% of pre-AKI UO** as a sensitivity.
- **Fluid axis:** net fluid balance over the 24 h window **< −500 mL/day** (persistent negative
  balance) — sensitivity thresholds −250 and −1000 mL/day.
- **Discordant recovery** = creatinine recovered **AND** (UO axis met **OR** fluid axis met).
- **Concordant recovery** = creatinine recovered **AND** neither axis met.

This is a descriptive/phenotype definition; the analysis tests its prognostic independence.

---

## 6. Outcomes

- **Primary:** (a) **in-hospital death** (`admissions.deathtime` / `hospital_expire_flag`);
  (b) **new RRT after T_rec** (first RRT procedureevent `starttime` > T_rec, itemids in §3).
- **Secondary:** ICU mortality (death before `icustays.outtime`), 28-day mortality (death within
  28 days of T_rec), time-to-event for RRT.

---

## 7. Models and analysis plan

- **Baseline model (creatinine-only):** creatinine recovery status (recovered vs not, or time-to-
  recovery) + age + sex + illness severity (SOFA/APACHE if computable, else AKI peak stage +
  vasopressor exposure).
- **Extended model:** baseline + **discordant-recovery indicator** + UO trajectory features (24 h UO
  at T_rec, UO slope over the prior 48 h) + net fluid balance at T_rec.
- **Analyses:**
  1. **Phenotype prevalence** and **unadjusted** outcome rates (death, new RRT) by discordant vs
     concordant recovery.
  2. **Adjusted association:** logistic regression (death, new RRT) and Cox proportional-hazards
     (time-to-new-RRT) → adjusted OR / HR with 95% CI for the discordant indicator, adjusting for
     the baseline covariates.
  3. **Model comparison:** C-index (Harrell), calibration (calibration slope / plot), and
     **NRI / IDI** for the extended vs baseline model (if event counts allow).
  4. **Sensitivity analyses:** different UO thresholds (0.3/0.5/0.7 mL/kg/h), fluid-balance
     definitions (−250/−500/−1000 mL/day), extended-UO item set vs Foley-only, exclusion of
     RRT-before-landmark (already primary), restriction to sepsis-AKI (Sepsis-3 from
     `hosp/diagnoses_icd`), and a missing-UO indicator (to test catheterization selection).

---

## 8. Falsification criteria

- **Supportive:** adjusted HR/OR for the discordant phenotype **≥ 1.5 with 95% CI excluding 1**,
  **and** incremental C-index improvement (or significant NRI/IDI) of the extended over the
  baseline model.
- **Adverse (hypothesis refuted):** no association — adjusted HR/OR **< 1.2 with CI including 1**,
  and no incremental discrimination.
- **Inconclusive (underpowered / data-limited):** **< 100 discordant patients**, **< 50 outcome
  events** in the discordant group, or **UO data missing in > 50%** of AKI stays (catheterization
  selection makes the phenotype uninterpretable). In that case the result is reported as
  inconclusive, not as a null.

## 9. Bounded feasibility probe (executed; NOT the full analysis)

A bounded probe (duckdb over the frozen zip; full `labevents` never loaded into memory) was
**executed** (job `[research job]`, ~70 s) to establish cohort size, UO data
availability, and RRT/death event counts. See `feasibility-probe.md` for the exact counts and
`probe_results.json` for the machine-readable output. The probe uses the simplified AKI rule
(peak ≥ 1.5× baseline within the stay) and counts: adult ICU stays (≥48 h) with ≥1 creatinine;
of those, AKI; of AKI, with ≥1 UO record (Foley and extended set); with ≥1 RRT event; and
in-hospital deaths (for event-count context).

**Probe results (executed):** 46,337 adult ICU stays (≥48 h); 46,231 with ≥1 creatinine;
**29,956** meeting simplified AKI; of those, **29,038 (96.9%)** with ≥1 UO record (extended set)
and **25,275 (84.4%)** with a Foley record; **3,369 (11.2%)** with ≥1 RRT event; **5,326 (17.8%)**
in-hospital deaths.

**Interpretation:** UO availability (96.9%) is **well above** the 30% inconclusive-risk threshold,
so the proposal does **not** carry an inconclusive-risk flag for UO availability. Cohort size and
event counts (RRT 3,369; death 5,326) are ample for the planned landmark + adjusted-regression +
incremental-discrimination analysis. (Caveat: high availability does not rule out catheterization
*selection* — a missing-UO indicator and Foley-vs-extended sensitivity are still planned.) The
<100-discordant-patients / <50-events inconclusive gate still applies to the final phenotype result.

## 10. Alternatives, limitations, and what would require more data

- **UO measurement is selective:** UO is recorded mainly in catheterized patients; a low-UO signal
  may reflect catheterization/monitoring intensity (selection) rather than true oliguria. Mitigated
  by the missing-UO indicator and Foley-vs-extended sensitivity; not fully resolved.
- **Creatinine confounded by muscle mass** [K1]: a falling creatinine may reflect muscle loss, not
  recovery — the core reason the discordant phenotype may be real, but also why we cannot claim the
  phenotype measures "true GFR recovery."
- **Fluid balance confounded by diuretics and resuscitation:** net balance is a crude proxy;
  diuretic use (from `hosp/pharmacy`/`prescriptions`) is a sensitivity covariate, not fully modeled.
- **No measured GFR:** MIMIC has no iohexol/inulin clearance; the phenotype identifies *suspected*
  discordant recovery, not confirmed incomplete GFR recovery. Confirming the mechanism would require
  a study with measured GFR or cystatin C (not in MIMIC).
- **Single database:** MIMIC-IV is multi-hospital but a single EHR; external validation (e.g., eICU)
  is a future step, not part of this experiment.
- **Observational:** the adjusted association is prognostic, not causal; residual confounding by
  unmeasured severity remains. A supportive result motivates (but does not prove) a mechanism or a
  trial; it would not by itself justify changing RRT timing.

**Alternatives considered and deferred:**
- *Cystatin C–based recovery:* not available in MIMIC (no cystatin C itemid in `d_labitems`); would
  be the ideal mechanistic confirmation but is out of scope for this dataset.
- *Full trajectory/distributional model (à la [K2]):* a heavier ML approach that predicts sCr/UO
  distributions; deferred because the scientific question here is a **recovery-phenotype prognosis**
  comparison (baseline vs extended), for which a landmark + adjusted regression + incremental
  discrimination is the smallest adequate check. A trajectory model would add complexity without
  changing the estimand and is revisitable if the landmark approach is inconclusive.
- *GPU / neural sequence model:* not warranted for a tabular landmark regression; CPU is sufficient
  (probe and planned fits are small). No GPU quota is requested.
