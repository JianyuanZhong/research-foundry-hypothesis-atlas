# Does hypotension DURATION add AKI risk beyond DEPTH? An executed coverage + within-depth-stratum contrast in eICU 2.0

## 1. Unresolved question, clinical importance, advance
ICU teams titrate fluids/vasopressors to a MAP target. Time-weighted MAP<65 burden predicts
creatinine-KDIGO AKI ([K1] AUC 0.725), and cumulative MAP<65 duration tracks downstream organ outcomes
([K2] aOR 1.78 for >=30 min). Both model burden/duration additively as a scalar exposure. The
decision-facing question remains unresolved: **once a patient's deepest MAP is known, does cumulative
time-below-65 add AKI risk (conditional effect), or does depth alone suffice?** If conditional duration
adds risk, "time below target" is a distinct monitoring target; if not, duration alarms add no
information beyond a single worst value. Advance over [K1]/[K2]: a *depth-conditioned* contrast with a
recurrence/pattern rival [K2(Ren)] and sampling-intensity control, plus an executed measurement-coverage
bound that [K1]/[K2] do not report. [K3] bounds transportability.

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival
- H1 (leading): within a fixed depth stratum (quintile of min MAP in (0,24]h), longer cumulative time
  with MAP<65 in (0,24]h raises (24,72]h KDIGO creatinine-AKI odds — positive duration x depth interaction.
- Rival: any apparent duration effect reflects (a) depth/severity confounding, (b) temporal
  PATTERN/recurrence [K2], or (c) sampling-intensity/measurement artefact (more monitoring => more
  detection). Design separates (a) within-stratum ORs + interaction, (b) recurrence term, (c) lab-draw
  control + channel-disjoint urine-output confirm.

## 3. Design (frozen from parent)
Population: adult eICU 2.0, age >=16, alive at 24h landmark (`unitdischargeoffset`>=1440), baseline and
follow-up creatinine. Exposure: (0,24]h MAP burden (`vitalPeriodic.systemicmean`; min MAP=depth;
minutes/area<65=duration), MINUTES offsets. Landmark 24h. Outcome: (24,72]h KDIGO creatinine-AKI
(`lab`, `labname`='creatinine', follow-up/baseline >=1.5). Adjustment: severity/APACHE, age, gender,
vasopressor exposure (`infusionDrug`), hospital fixed effects; LOHO transport; calibration per [K3].
Key test: within-min-MAP-stratum ORs and duration x min-MAP (and duration x recurrence) interaction.

## 6. Falsification / outcomes
- Supportive H1: ADJUSTED within-stratum duration OR >1, interaction CI excluding 1, monotone trend,
  robust to lab-draw control and recurrence term.
- Adverse H1: adjusted within-stratum duration effect ~null / interaction CI spans 1 => depth alone
  suffices; the crude +3.24 pp is then attributed to confounding/pattern/sampling.
- Inconclusive: the ~25.5% MAP coverage limits power; if <10% coverage in a stratum => cannot separate H1
  from (a)/(c). (Coverage observed here is adequate but not generous, and is a stated limitation.)

## 7. Honest limits
Executed coverage + crude within-stratum contrast only; ADJUSTED interaction beta, E-value, LOHO,
recurrence term and urine-output mapping unexecuted. Crude contrast is NOT the H1 test and is confounded.
MDE is a standardized-predictor approximation. MAP is a periodic median; vasopressors not dose-normalized.
Association not causation; eICU-only internal LOHO transport, not external validation.
