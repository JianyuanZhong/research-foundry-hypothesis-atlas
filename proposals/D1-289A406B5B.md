# Acute recorded hemoglobin decline beyond stress: a noncausal MIMIC-IV experiment

## Scientific deliverable and evidence boundary

This is a complete crossover of the assessed-valid transfusion/stress design `[prior hypothesis]` and the assessed-valid hemoglobin-trajectory design `[prior hypothesis]`. The Lead owns the scientific choice. The core clock is fixed: a recorded PRBC start during the six hours after a qualifying low hemoglobin landmark, followed by a strictly non-overlapping 24-hour deterioration window.

The future solver must newly construct and audit the cohort, fit the transparent model and the prespecified latent-trajectory alternative, estimate the joint observed-risk contrasts, and produce held-out uncertainty, calibration, overlap, component outcomes, temporal transport and falsification results. Completion is either a valid gated estimate or a complete not-estimable report. No prevalence, event rate, fitted coefficient, risk, or clinical conclusion is claimed here.

The strongest available evidence for this proposal is limited to availability: the read-only MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains serial hemoglobin, ICU physiology, PRBC input, procedure, admission, patient and death fields with the schemas and dictionary labels audited below. The available research-ambition files were read as method/context demonstrations, not as evidence for this MIMIC hypothesis. They support considering a bounded chronological or latent longitudinal adaptation, but do not establish a transfusion result. The main article for the cancer demonstration remains unavailable; no main-paper or full STAR Methods claim is made.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among adult ICU decision episodes with a first qualifying hemoglobin of 6.5–8.0 g/dL, no observed recent PRBC or massive-transfusion proxy, and adequate trajectory/stress coverage, does the adjusted observed association between a recorded PRBC start in [t0,tau) and first deterioration in (tau,tau+24h] differ between an acute recorded hemoglobin fall and stable low hemoglobin, after accounting for current hemoglobin, the parent record-defined oxygen supply/demand-stress phenotype, pre-index physiology/severity, bleeding proxies, and observation process?

The primary incremental test is the joint A-by-trajectory-by-stress contrast. It asks whether trajectory heterogeneity remains after stress heterogeneity is represented, not whether a fall proves bleeding or ischemia. A positive result could prioritize prospective adjudication of acute-anemia context in transfusion trials or review; a null would argue against this EHR trajectory as a useful stratifier; a reversal or process-only replication would challenge the interpretation. All are observed-association findings, never causal transfusion effects.

## Population, landmark, trajectory and exposure

Join `icu/icustays` to `hosp/admissions` on `subject_id,hadm_id`, and to `hosp/patients` on `subject_id`. Keep adults with `anchor_age >=18`, preserving MIMIC's `anchor_age=91` representation for age >89. Order ICU stays by `intime,stay_id`; retain the earliest qualifying ICU episode per subject across all admissions. A sensitivity permits one episode per admission, but every subject remains in one partition and admissions are never mixed in a subject-level split.

Define t0 as the first eligible `hosp/labevents` item `itemid=51222` (Hematology Hemoglobin) with finite `valuenum`, accepted g/dL `valueuom`, valid `charttime) within the selected ICU interval, at least 12 hours after `intime`, and `storetime <= t0`. Deduplicate same-specimen rows under a rule frozen before outcome access: at most one selected row per `specimen_id`, sorted deterministically by `charttime,storetime,labevent_id`; report discarded/ambiguous duplicates. The index value must satisfy 6.5 <= Hb(t0) <= 8.0 g/dL. Item 50811 is a locked sensitivity source, not pooled into the primary landmark.

Let `tau=t0+6 hours`. T=1 is at least one valid PRBC administration-compatible input row starting in [t0,tau), with itemid 220996, 225168, 226368 or 227070, positive recorded `amount` or `totalamount` in mL, finite `starttime`, treatment-compatible `statusdescription), and no evidence that the row is only an order. T=0 is no qualifying recorded PRBC start during [t0,tau) with complete exposure ascertainment. Audit `endtime,storetime,amount,amountuom,rate,rateuom,totalamount,totalamountuom,isopenbag,continueinnextdept,statusdescription,orderid,linkorderid` and order-category/component fields for cancelled, rewritten, duplicated, overlapping, open-bag and ambiguous rows. Primary exposure is any start, not dose; first start, recorded >=1,000 mL, and exclusion of OR/PACU IDs are sensitivities. FFP, platelets and other products are not PRBC exposure.

Exclude a qualifying PRBC start in [t0-12h,t0), an administration interval active at t0, or procedureevents item 229620 in [t0-12h,t0). Where valid comparable mL records permit it, also flag cumulative PRBC >=2,000 mL in the preceding six hours as a major-bleeding/transfusion proxy. A frozen, label-reviewed ICD procedure/diagnosis list may add operative/trauma proxies only if frozen before outcome access; otherwise omit that code rule and report the limitation. Call the resulting restriction “no observed recent PRBC/massive-transfusion proxy,” never nonbleeding.

Episodes with death, ICU exit, missing/reversed times, or unresolved exposure ascertainment before tau remain in the flow but are not assigned T=0 and are not in the primary estimand. The primary exposure cohort is alive, in the indexed ICU at tau, and has an auditable six-hour exposure window. Store a reason for every exclusion.

For trajectory G, use only item-51222 rows with valid g/dL units, finite value, `charttime <= t0` and `storetime <= t0`. Select the nearest prior distinct specimen in [t0-24h,t0) whose time gap is 6–24 hours; tie-break by latest charttime, latest storetime and smallest labevent_id. Set `delta_hb=Hb(t0)-Hb(prior)):

- G=acute-fall if delta_hb <= -1.0 g/dL;
- G=stable-low if abs(delta_hb) < 0.5 g/dL;
- G=indeterminate otherwise, including no prior, invalid unit, gap outside 6–24 hours, unresolved specimen duplication, intermediate change or rise.

The threshold and selection rule are frozen before exposure/outcome inspection. Report the full indeterminate flow, gaps, units, repeated testing and event states; never call G bleeding, blood loss, chronic anemia or ischemia. Sensitivities use continuous slope delta_hb/gap, fall thresholds 0.5 and 1.5 g/dL, 12–24-hour prior gaps, and item 50811.

## Record-defined oxygen supply/demand stress

Define S in the 12-hour pre-t0 interval. Every primary feature row requires clinical time and recording `storetime <= t0`; interval rows require finite `starttime < endtime` and storetime <=t0, with clinical overlap. A chart-before/store-after row is excluded primarily and included only in a charttime-only sensitivity. Missingness is not zero stress.

Supply-side components are: lactate >=2 mmol/L from `hosp/labevents` items 50813/52442/53154 after local unit validation; MAP <65 mmHg from `icu/chartevents` items 220052/220181/225312 with at least two distinct valid times when used as a repeated signal; and positive-duration pressor intervals from `icu/inputevents` items 221289/229617 (epinephrine), 221662 (dopamine), 221749/229630/229631/229632 (phenylephrine), 221906 (norepinephrine), and 222315 (vasopressin). Items 221653 (dobutamine) and 221986 (milrinone) are sensitivity components only after label/unit validation.

Demand/support components are HR >=110 from item 220045, RR >=24 from item 220210, FiO2 >=0.40 from item 223835, PEEP >=5 from item 220339, and active invasive/non-invasive ventilation intervals from procedureevents items 225792/225794, with unit and interval validation. Troponin-T item 227429 and hospital troponin items 51002/51003/52642 are sensitivity-only because comparable positivity units are not guaranteed.

S=high requires at least one supply and one demand/support component, or at least two independent supply components. S=low requires no high rule, at least one valid supply observation and one valid demand/support observation, and at least two valid physiologic observations overall. All other episodes are S=indeterminate and are reported but excluded from the primary joint contrast. Report each component and coverage count separately.

## Outcome and observed-risk estimand

The outcome window is strictly (tau,tau+24h], so no outcome event can occur in the six-hour exposure window. Construct the first mutually exclusive state from `hosp/admissions.deathtime`, `hospital_expire_flag`, `icu/procedureevents.starttime/endtime/itemid`, and `icu/icustays.outtime`:

- D: in-hospital death is the earliest qualifying transition in the window;
- E: the earliest nonfatal escalation is new invasive ventilation (224385 or 225792), new listed pressor start when no target pressor is active at tau, or new renal replacement (225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809 or 225955);
- X: indexed ICU exit before D/E;
- N: remains under indexed ICU observation through tau+24h with no D/E;
- U: missing or irreconcilable times/follow-up.

When times tie, use a frozen precedence D, E, X and retain the raw tie. Require no qualifying ventilation in the pre-t0 six-hour baseline window, no active target pressor at tau, and no pre-existing qualifying dialysis before its event can count as new. Do not use discharge as recovery; ICU exit is an ascertainment/competing state. Primary Y=1 is D or E, with D and E separately reported and X/N/U shown in the flow and first-transition table. Report later PRBC starts among T=0 during the outcome window as contamination/process descriptors, plus a sensitivity censoring at first later start; neither is causal.

For each joint cell (s,g), define Delta_(s,g) as the held-out model-standardized difference in observed 24-hour Y risk between recorded labels T=1 and T=0, keeping each episode's pre-t0 covariates fixed. Standardization uses the empirical eligible target distribution restricted to prespecified common support; it is a prediction-standardization contrast, not a counterfactual treatment effect. The primary trajectory contrasts are:

- H_G,high = Delta_(high,acute-fall) - Delta_(high,stable-low);
- H_G,low = Delta_(low,acute-fall) - Delta_(low,stable-low);
- H_G = 0.5(H_G,high + H_G,low), an equal-stress-weight incremental trajectory contrast;
- H_3way = H_G,high - H_G,low, the prespecified PRBC-by-trajectory-by-stress interaction.

The parent stress contrasts Delta_(high,g)-Delta_(low,g) are also required. Report crude T-by-S-by-G rates before adjustment, adjusted Delta values, D/E decompositions, calibration, overlap and observation-process diagnostics. The claim under test is a reproducible H_G and/or H_3way of clinically meaningful magnitude, not a treatment effect.

## Matched baseline, substantive alternative and method choice

All methods use exactly the same rows, t0/tau, T/G/S rules, covariate whitelist, outcomes, exclusions, split, test set, support gates and bootstrap plan.

B0 is a transparent penalized discrete-time competing-risk hazards model in four six-hour bins over (tau,tau+24h], with separate D, E and X hazards. It includes T, G, S, T-by-G, T-by-S and T-by-G-by-S, current Hb, prior-to-index time gap, prespecified pre-t0 physiology/support/severity/context, recent non-PRBC inputs only when units are interpretable, diagnosis/procedure indicators, and measurement masks, counts, time-since-last-measurement and store lag. It uses training-only imputation, scaling, splines and penalty selection. Standardized risks are obtained from the fitted hazards; calibration intercept/slope and Brier score are reported overall and by joint cell. Hemoglobin-only and stress-only models/selectors are simple secondary comparators.

M1 is a substantive irregularly sampled local-linear latent hemoglobin measurement model coupled to the same competing-risk hazards. It uses every eligible pre-t0 item-51222 observation (time, value, unit audit and observation indicator) plus the same non-Hb pre-index covariates. Development-only parameters estimate a latent Hb level and slope at t0 with measurement noise and irregular timing; the outcome hazards include current latent level, latent slope, T-by-slope and the same T-by-S terms. It outputs the same standardized risks, H_G/H_3way, D/E components, calibration and uncertainty. M1 can reveal whether the acute-fall signal persists after separating noisy measurement and irregular sampling from a thresholded nearest-pair change—information B0 deliberately loses. A small AUC/Brier improvement without stable interaction estimates is not a scientific success.

Use a deterministic subject-level 70/15/15 split within development-era subjects, stratified only using pre-index T, G, S, Y and anchor_year_group; all episodes of a subject stay together. Fit on anchor-year groups 2008–2010, 2011–2013 and 2014–2016; use 2017–2019 as locked calibration and 2020–2022 as untouched temporal transport. Preprocessing, trajectory thresholds, model choices and latent parameters are fit without later-era outcomes; do not refit or retune on transport. If a listed era has inadequate support, report that transport estimand as not estimable rather than pooling eras.

M1 is selected over the parent's GRU as the substantive alternative because the new uncertainty is specifically measurement error and irregular Hb sampling, not generic multivariate order. The parent's masked chronological GRU is retained as a deferred option if a future question concerns multivariate temporal asynchrony or M1 fails its prespecified convergence checks; adding a second sequence learner now would not isolate the new uncertainty. Causal g-formula/TMLE or dynamic regimes are deferred because intent, time-varying confounding, censoring and support needed for a causal estimand are not observed. Note-based bleeding/ischemia classification is deferred because local text extraction is unvalidated for comprehensive clinical adjudication. Genetic latent models, images and waveforms are deferred because verified germline, MIMIC-CXR images and raw waveform inputs are unavailable. A large transformer is deferred because complexity cannot recover absent indication/adjudication evidence and would not answer a distinct question.

## Support, uncertainty and falsification

Before interpreting H_G or H_3way, require every A-by-G-by-S cell to have >=50 episodes and >=20 known Y events; each trajectory state >=10 D and >=10 E events for component claims; >=80% known outcome ascertainment; observed-treatment common support in [0.05,0.95] after only prespecified trimming; weighted ESS >=100 per trajectory state and stress stratum; weighted standardized covariate differences <=0.25; and zero primary post-t0 leakage or impossible time/unit failure. If unresolved PRBC status/unit rows exceed 5% of otherwise eligible exposure records, report the primary exposure estimand as unavailable pending review. These are estimability gates, not clinical validation. Do not merge G states, collapse S, change thresholds, or recode U/N to rescue support.

Use 1,000 subject-level bootstrap replicates, resampling all eligible rows for each subject together and refitting B0 and M1 when feasible. Report completed/failure counts and distinguish full-refit intervals from conditional-prediction intervals. Report 95% intervals, simultaneous for H_G, H_G,high, H_G,low, H_3way and D/E components, plus calibration, ESS, later-T-in-T=0, indeterminate trajectory and missingness measures.

Falsifications and sensitivities are locked before test outcomes:

1. Permute T within current-Hb band, G, S, care unit and anchor-year group; the joint interaction should collapse.
2. Permute G within current-Hb band, T, S, care unit and era; persistence indicates testing/process or model artifact.
3. Fit B-mask and observation-only models with masks, counts, time gaps, store lag, care context and severity but no Hb values or trajectory. Comparable H_G indicates documentation/attention rather than Hb information.
4. Predict a pre-t0 deterioration composite from future T; a strong association quantifies residual indication/severity and forbids treatment-effect language.
5. Use non-PRBC blood-product/process rows as an exposure-process negative control, explicitly not a biologic negative control.
6. Shuffle early/late Hb order in M1 while preserving values, masks and marginal measurement intensity.
7. Use a non-overlapping earlier placebo Hb trajectory, where available, and 24-hour earlier exposure-label shifts.
8. Repeat with item 50811, continuous slope, 0.5/1.5-g/dL thresholds, 12–24-hour prior gaps, item 225168 alone, OR/PACU exclusions, charttime-only availability, and stress components/thresholds separately.
9. Scan all model matrices for chart/store/start/end times after t0, terminal/death/dod fields used as predictors, post-t0 physiology, and duplicate subject partitions.

Supportive evidence requires all gates, acceptable calibration, a simultaneous 95% interval for the prespecified primary contrast outside [-0.05,0.05], concordant magnitude/direction in B0 and M1, permutation collapse, no comparable B-mask result, no dominant unsupported D/E component, and direction retained in both locked later eras without loss of overlap/ascertainment. Fixed-capacity secondary evaluation at q=5%, 10% (primary) and 20% compares trajectory-based signed selection with B0/stress-only/hemoglobin-only selectors; improvement must be material and calibrated, not merely a small predictive gain. Support justifies prospective adjudication or replication of a record-defined hypothesis.

Adverse evidence includes reproducible null/reversal, B0/M1 disagreement, B-mask parity, permutation persistence, strong future-T/pre-t0 association, concentration in one care context, miscalibration, or temporal reversal. It argues against this trajectory as a reliable stratifier, not against biological transfusion heterogeneity. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse cells, high G-indeterminate flow, U/exit uncertainty, failed overlap/ESS, unresolved units/status, unstable bootstrap or latent convergence, wide intervals, or non-estimable transport cells. The correct result is “not estimable with this snapshot,” with no clinical action.

## Required clinical adjudication and unavailable evidence

MIMIC does not provide validated adjudication of active or occult bleeding, blood-loss rate, phlebotomy/hemodilution, transfusion intent/rationale, blood availability/refusal, completed-unit receipt, compatibility, clinician appropriateness, ECG-confirmed ischemia, symptoms, coronary anatomy, echo/cardiac output, direct oxygen delivery/consumption, microcirculation, complete rescue capture after ICU exit, randomized assignment, or external validation. The acute recorded fall can be caused by true blood loss, dilution, sampling variation, or testing process; stable low Hb does not establish chronic anemia. PRBC input rows can represent documentation/order or setting-specific capture. Diagnosis/procedure codes and available notes are not a validated substitute for expert review; the local text extraction is explicitly unvalidated for broad diagnosis extraction.

A supportive result therefore supports only replication and prospective clinical adjudication with explicit bleeding, transfusion-intent, oxygen-supply/demand and ischemia definitions. Stronger causal, mechanistic, bedside-policy, utility and transport conclusions require expert-reviewed phenotypes, richer treatment/indication and follow-up data, temporal/external validation, and preferably randomized or target-trial-quality evidence.
