# Denominator-aware eICU audit with an explicit identity and feasibility stop

## Substantive evolution and clinical importance

This proposal is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the inherited scientific object rather than silently changing it: the exact 540 target-labelled person-landmarks, 443 unique stays/persons, 74 hospitals, five landmarks (1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, and 7200 minutes), decision time `D`, the two literal labels, the failed durable-liberation ascertainment, and the adverse/unstable 12-hour chart-proxy findings.

The unresolved clinically consequential question remains whether the two care-plan labels can support a denominator-aware, multicenter description of ventilated care or a transportable decision-support phenotype. The parent result is conditional on receipt of either target label and therefore cannot estimate target-label rates among all otherwise eligible ventilated stays. A valid denominator extension would determine whether the adverse conditional transportability signal persists when eligible stays without either target label are retained, and whether apparent hospital dependence is explained by pre-landmark recording coverage.

This child adds an executed feasibility audit and a hard computational stop. A full-source scan of the seven most directly relevant eICU files was submitted, but the managed job reached its 600-second deadline before producing a result. Independently, the exact inherited strict-cohort artifact and builder were not found in this workspace. Consequently, no denominator, prevalence, coverage-standardized estimate, or changed transportability conclusion is claimed. The failure is itself the result about current reproducibility: the requested identity-checked denominator cannot be established from the available execution and lineage evidence.

## Evidence boundary

The inherited evidence supports only that, within the frozen target-labelled cohort, label use is concentrated by hospital and the computable mappings are not robust enough for unqualified multicenter pooling. It does not establish label prevalence among all ventilated stays, local documentation conventions, clinician intent, actual SBT delivery, extubation, bedside action, quality, causality, or patient benefit.

The unresolved claim tested by the denominator experiment is narrower: after reconstructing exactly the same eligibility risk set while removing only the requirement to receive a target label, do target-label assignment rates and target-labelled chart-proxy mappings remain hospital- and coverage-dependent? This is descriptive transportability research. Hospital and documentation coverage are not randomized exposures, and source-record presence cannot be treated as absence of clinical state.

## Frozen inherited cohort and identity gate

The positive control is the inherited 540-row artifact identified by [source checksum], with required identity fields `sid`, `uniquepid`, `hospitalid`, `landmark`, `strategy`, and `D`. The reconstruction must reproduce all 540 rows, the 443 unique stays, all 74 hospitals, the five landmarks, the 309/231 label counts, exact literal labels, and every `D` value. It must also reproduce the inherited first qualifying stay/person rule, adult and missing-age handling, pre-landmark invasive-ventilation evidence, strict pre-landmark physiology screen, end-of-life and prior spontaneous-adequate exclusions, contradiction handling, and missingness conventions.

The denominator would contain one row per otherwise-eligible stay-landmark, including rows with no target label. At `[L,L+360]`, care-plan observations would be classified as exact evaluation, exact no-trial, other/ambiguous care-plan, or no target-label observation. No target observation would never be recoded as no trial. All event summaries would be left-joined so zero-record rows are retained.

Until the identity gate passes, denominator rates and all-eligible comparisons are prohibited. A broad patient-plus-respiratory-care cohort is not an acceptable substitute because it could change the inherited eligibility population.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive results would require an identity-passing, complete denominator with broad common support, materially reduced hospital assignment dispersion after prespecified coverage standardization, and stable parent proxy mappings across coverage strata. They would justify only semantic/workflow adjudication and prospective replication.

Adverse results would be concentrated all-eligible assignment, persistent hospital or coverage dependence, or proxy reversals after standardization. That would strengthen the warning against unqualified pooling but would not prove documentation conventions or clinical irrelevance.

Inconclusive results include the present identity/feasibility failure, unavailable thresholds or missingness rules, incomplete source coverage, or sparse common-support cells. The correct interpretation is that the denominator-aware question is not identifiable from the executed evidence, not that hospitals are equivalent or different.

An automatic verifier can check job state, declared inputs, source hashes when a completed scan exists, identity fields, row counts, exact labels and D, joins, denominators, coverage cells, and whether conclusions follow from computed outputs. It cannot establish intended label semantics, actual bedside action, SBT delivery, extubation, appropriateness, quality, causal benefit, or external transportability. Those require clinical adjudication, workflow metadata, and another study.
