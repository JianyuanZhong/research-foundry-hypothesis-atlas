## Evidence and unresolved claim

The inspected Haines et al. critical-illness study (CJASN 2023, DOI 10.2215/CJN.0000000000000203) enrolled 38 selected mechanically ventilated patients and reported divergence between creatinine- and cystatin-C-based kidney estimates, with creatinine overestimating measured GFR near ICU discharge and muscle loss correlating with creatinine/cystatin-C divergence. That supports a measurement concern. It does not establish the prevalence, prognosis, or mechanism of Foley-output/creatinine discordance in MIMIC-IV. The expert seed likewise explicitly labels its discordant-recovery statement untested and warns that measured kidney function is unavailable.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults in an explicitly observed paired-support population who have an observed early creatinine rise, early observed Foley oliguria, and apparent creatinine recovery by the 72-hour landmark, creatinine recovery without late charted Foley-output recovery is associated with higher subsequent same-admission in-hospital mortality than concordant recovery of creatinine and charted Foley output.

The clinical importance is that improving creatinine may be treated as reassuring even when observed output remains low. A reproducible association would justify prospective validation with cystatin C, measured GFR, muscle/volume assessment and complete urine capture. It would not show that creatinine recovery causes harm, that Foley output measures true GFR, or that treatment should change.

## Cohort, landmark and observability

The unit is one row per hospitalization's earliest valid ICU stay: sort valid stays within each `hadm_id` by `intime`, then `stay_id`; retain the first. Require `anchor_age >= 18`, nonmissing valid `intime < outtime`, `t0 = intime + 72 hours < outtime`, a nonmissing `dischtime > t0`, and no `deathtime <= t0`. The primary estimand conditions on being alive and still in the index ICU at exactly 72 hours and on the paired measurement support below. Multiple admissions are distinct rows, but all rows for a `subject_id` must remain in one data split and uncertainty must cluster by subject. MIMIC's subject-specific time shifting permits within-subject intervals, not cross-subject calendar comparisons.

Use half-open windows relative to index `intime):

- W0 = [0,24) hours, early;
- W1 = [24,48) hours, rise;
- W2 = [48,72) hours, recovery.

Clinical event inclusion uses `charttime` (or `starttime` for procedures/inputs), never `storetime`. For the primary as-observed analysis, a measurement additionally requires nonmissing `storetime <= t0`; missing or late storage time is not primary support. Report charttime-only support (including storage-missing/late rows) separately as an observability sensitivity, never by treating it as equivalent evidence.

## Phenotype definition

Creatinine is an observed screen, not KDIGO AKI. Retain positive numeric values whose audited unit is mg/dL. Require at least one valid primary-observable creatinine in each W0, W1 and W2. Let:

- `C0 = min(C in W0)`;
- `C1 = max(C in W1)`;
- `Cpeak = max(C in W0 union W1)`;
- `Clate = min(C in W2)`.

Call the early rise present if `C1 >= C0 + 0.3 mg/dL OR C1/C0 >= 1.5`. Call apparent creatinine recovery present if `Clate <= Cpeak - 0.3 mg/dL OR Clate/Cpeak <= 0.75`. Retain the continuous values and each component of the rule. The primary phenotype analysis is restricted to rise-plus-recovery; all four rise/recovery cells are reported in the audit.

For Foley, retain nonnegative numeric mL values for item 226559 with audited mL units. Remove exact duplicates, aggregate valid observations into relative hourly bins, and sum recorded volume within each hour. Require in both W0 and W2: at least 18 distinct observed chart hours, first-to-last observed-hour span at least 20 hours, and maximum gap between observed hours no more than 4 hours. This is a dense-observation gate, not proof of complete catheter capture. Divide total recorded volume by the full 24-hour window and body weight, never by observed hours; preserve an unweighted mL/hour sensitivity.

For the primary weight, use a positive item 226512 in W0; if absent, use a positive item 224639 in W0. Require charttime in W0 and `storetime <= t0`; if both exist, use the latest eligible value from the prespecified item-priority rule (226512 before 224639 if values are tied in time). Report missing/late weight separately. A latest-positive-by-t0 sensitivity is allowed only as a sensitivity, because it can incorporate post-W0 clinical evolution.

Require W0 recorded Foley output <0.5 mL/kg/hour (early observed oliguria). Define W2 Foley recovery as >=0.5 mL/kg/hour and non-recovery as <0.5. The primary cells, conditional on rise, recovery, dense W0/W2 output, early oliguria and weight support, are:

- discordant: creatinine recovery + W2 Foley non-recovery;
- concordant: creatinine recovery + W2 Foley recovery.

Do not replace these cells with creatinine-only recovery if paired support is absent. The full audit must report lower-density, missing-weight, no-early-oliguria, no-creatinine-support and all four creatinine/output cells so selective coverage is visible.

## Baseline and substantive learned alternative

The prediction population is the same primary support-defined population before assigning the discordant/concordant label: adults with the fixed 72-hour landmark, observed rise, early observed oliguria, dense W0/W2 paired Foley support, primary weight and outcome observability. The target is post-t0 in-hospital death. The subject-level partition is deterministic 60% training, 20% validation and 20% locked test, assigning every admission of a subject to one partition. Hyperparameters and feature choices are fixed or selected only within training/validation; the test set is read once.

Simple baseline: ridge logistic regression with age, sex, first care unit/admission context, creatinine C0/C1/Cpeak/Clate and slopes, pre-48 physiology/support indicators, CKD/ESRD sensitivity proxy, measurement counts and missingness. It excludes Foley output, weight-normalized output, fluid balance and all urine/fluid trajectory channels. It is transparent and directly tests whether observed output adds beyond a creatinine-centered risk estimate.

Substantive alternative: a CPU HistGradientBoostingClassifier (or equivalent bounded tree booster) on six-hour bins across [0,72) for creatinine, Foley output and observation density, weight-normalized output, input-minus-output proxy, MAP, HR, RR, SpO2, lactate, vasoactive exposure, ventilation and RRT. Each channel contributes last/mean/min/max, slope or within-bin variation where defined, count and missingness. Fit the full model and a urine/fluid ablation on the identical split. The alternative can reveal persistence, timing, nonlinear thresholds and interactions—especially whether output non-recovery after the creatinine peak carries information that terminal summaries lose. It is not selected for a small score gain and cannot rescue a failed primary phenotype test.

Report held-out AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and bootstrap intervals, plus grouped feature-family and urine/fluid-ablation comparisons. Require at least 20 events and 20 non-events in training and test and estimable calibration; otherwise model results are inconclusive. Budget after the support audit: 2 CPUs, 8 GiB RAM, no GPU, at most 90 minutes within the 7,200-second science limit. If full six-hour features exceed the budget, report the alternative as deferred/inconclusive rather than changing the question.

## Support gates, falsification and interpretation

Before fitting, the audit must emit a manifest with archive/member checks, row counts, flow at every gate, duplicate/storage-time counts, support and missingness by cell, and post-t0 event counts. Hard no-go/inconclusive gates are: missing archive member/schema/item/unit; unreproducible joins or time boundaries; either primary cell fewer than 50 stays; fewer than 10 post-t0 deaths in either cell; no primary weight support; inability to distinguish absent output from recorded zero; irreconcilable death fields; or inability to preserve the declared split and uncertainty. These are feasibility gates, not evidence for or against the hypothesis. No creatinine-only fallback or alternate question is authorized by this child.

Falsification and process checks, all prespecified, are:

- 18-hour versus denser coverage, observation-gap strata and an observation-density-only model;
- storage-available versus charttime-only support;
- hospital-lab-only versus ICU-chart-only creatinine;
- absolute/relative creatinine and Foley thresholds, unweighted output, W0-weight versus latest-by-t0 weight;
- exclusion of pre-t0 RRT;
- within-stay permutation of output timestamps while preserving values/counts, and label permutation within matched support/creatinine strata where feasible;
- a model using charting/process features without renal values.

Supportive evidence requires a higher discordant mortality risk, a 95% interval excluding the null, direction preserved under the dense/storage/source/threshold checks, no dominant density/process explanation, and a reproducible urine/fluid incremental-information result with acceptable held-out calibration. It supports only a conditional observed prognostic signal in the measured population.

Adverse evidence is a precise null or reversal, especially under dense storage-available support, or attenuation that is explained by source/coverage/process controls. A temporal model improvement without the prespecified discordant-versus-concordant mortality signal is adverse for the renal-discordance claim, not a rescue.

Inconclusive evidence is inadequate paired support/events, differential observability that cannot be separated from phenotype, unstable uncertainty/calibration/coverage weighting, unreconciled outcomes, leakage, failed falsification implementation, or failed resource limits. No threshold or gate may be relaxed after seeing results.

The verifier can check archive provenance, schemas, joins, timestamp/duplicate rules, cohort flow, item support, phenotype construction, model fits, uncertainty and whether each stated conclusion follows from computed outputs. It cannot establish true GFR, complete Foley capture, muscle loss, volume status, clinician-intended catheterization, treatment indications, causality, bedside utility or external validity. Those require cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle and volume assessment, clinical adjudication, expert review, prospective monitoring and external validation.

## Alternative decisions, provenance and change rationale

The chosen alternative is the six-hour nonlinear tree because it tests a substantive information question—whether timing, persistence and interactions in observed renal/output trajectories add beyond transparent creatinine summaries—within a bounded CPU budget. A sequence transformer or latent GP model was deferred: the local question has no validated need for an opaque representation, and their extra assumptions would not resolve missing true-GFR or selective Foley evidence. A mechanistic fluid-balance model was also deferred because complete intake/output capture, volume status and treatment indications are unavailable. The residual-instability ICU-discharge branch remains a separate assessed direction and is not substituted if this support audit fails. Diuretic, sedation and transfusion causal branches remain deferred for indication/time-varying-confounding limitations.

This is an adaptation, not reproduction, of the demonstrations. Delphi motivates dated trajectory versus snapshot comparisons, but UK Biobank/Danish validation data are unavailable. ALADYNOULLI's genetics-dependent analysis is not reproducible with the configured data; a latent EHR adaptation is not needed for this question. Oncoformer's full image/EHR study is unavailable because local images and the main methods are absent; its lab-only precedent does not supersede this renal design.

The substantive change from `[prior hypothesis]` is to freeze one primary support definition: W0-weight and storage-available paired observations, strict disjoint windows and half-open boundaries, explicit duplicate/storage-missing handling, outcome observability checks, a common prediction population before phenotype labeling, and no creatinine-only or alternate-question fallback. The prior audit's cancellation is preserved as a feasibility fact, not converted into a result.
