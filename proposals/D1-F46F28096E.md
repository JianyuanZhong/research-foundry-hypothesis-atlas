# Is the ICU MAP<65 → AKI association a physiologic dose, or an observation-process (ascertainment) artifact?

Lead working proposal — eICU 2.0 island, **episode 40**. Operation: **evolve** of the valid,
delivery-complete board leader `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 32.13, sigma 6.71,
rubric `life-science-stage-aware-v1`). Status: **design frozen by the Lead for compilation**.
Discovery executed only bounded read-only schema probes and literature receipts; the fitted
estimator is **NOT executed here** — this is design + verified bindings, not a solved result.

## 1. Unresolved question and why it matters
The island's line (gen 7–9) established an association between (0,24]h MAP<65 burden and
subsequent creatinine-KDIGO AKI, and probed dose-vs-timing (EARLY/LATE) and dose-vs-structure
(contiguity/recurrence). Every variant **assumes** the association reflects a physiologic dose
and treats charting/lab density as a nuisance covariate. The strongest unmodelled rival is
**informative observation**: in an open EHR, both the exposure (MAP<65 minutes are only observed
when BP is charted) and the outcome (AKI is only detected when creatinine is drawn) are generated
by the *same* clinical concern. If sicker/hypotensive patients are simply measured more, the
burden→AKI association can be substantially an ascertainment artifact. This is decision-relevant:
MAP≥65 targets and "time-in-target" monitoring guidance would be mis-specified if the association
is driven by observation intensity rather than modifiable hemodynamics.

## 2. Competing explanations and the discriminating test
- **H-dose (leading claim):** cumulative MAP<65 burden independently raises AKI risk; the
  association is robust to observation intensity.
- **H-observation (strongest rival):** the association is partly produced by differential
  observation — hypotension prompts more BP charting and creatinine sampling, so AKI is
  detected more often (and the *chance* of capturing a KDIGO rise increases).
- **Discriminating predictions:**
  1. Under H-observation the burden coefficient attenuates materially (>~50% on the log-odds)
     after adding observation-intensity covariates and collapses within monitoring-balanced strata;
     under H-dose it is stable (<~20% attenuation).
  2. Under H-observation burden strongly predicts a **negative-control detection outcome**
     (number of creatinine draws in (24,72]h, and whether ≥2 draws occurred); under H-dose this
     detection-channel association is weak.
  3. Under H-observation the hospital-level mean charting intensity correlates with the
     hospital's burden→AKI coefficient (LOHO transport of the effect).

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcomes
- **Population:** adult eICU stays, `patient.age`≥16, first ICU stay per `uniquepid`,
  `unitdischargeoffset`≥72h (to observe the outcome window), ≥1 creatinine in (0,24]h to anchor
  a baseline. Join key **`patientunitstayid`**. Offsets are **MINUTES**.
- **Landmark / windows:** hour-24 landmark; exposure window **(0,24]h**; outcome window
  **(24,72]h**; the **(24,48]** observability rule is applied so an outcome is counted only if a
  creatinine measurement exists in the window (ascertainment made explicit).
- **Exposure:** total minutes with `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean` <65 in (0,24] (primary), plus
  nadir and mean MAP; depth and burden from `systemicmean`.
- **Primary outcome:** KDIGO-creatinine AKI in (24,72]h = rise ≥0.3 mg/dL in 48h or ≥1.5×
  baseline, from `lab.labname='creatinine'`, `labresult`, `labresultoffset`.
- **Observation-intensity covariates (the rival made measurable):** creatinine draws per 24h
  (`lab` counts), BP charting frequency (`vitalPeriodic` rows/h in (0,24]).
- **Negative-control detection outcomes:** number of creatinine draws in (24,72]h; indicator of
  ≥2 draws.
- **Static severity / structure:** `apachePatientResult.apachescore`,
  `predictedicumortality`; hospital fixed/random effect via `patient.hospitalid`.
- **Treatment channel:** vasopressors/fluids from `infusionDrug`/`intakeOutput`/`admissionDrug`
  (drugname strings to be bound at compile; noted as an honest gap).

## 4. Analysis
Mixed-effects logistic regression of AKI on burden (+ static severity, hospital random effect),
**nested**: M0 base → M1 + observation-intensity covariates → M2 within monitoring-density-balanced
strata (matching or restriction to a fixed creatinine-draw band). Report the burden coefficient and
its % attenuation M0→M1→M2, CIs, and the detection-channel association. Leave-one-hospital-out
(LOHO) transport: per-hospital burden coefficient vs hospital mean charting intensity
(calibration, O:E, net benefit). **Baseline vs substantive alternative:** the logistic dose model
(baseline) vs a monitoring-density-aware model / causal-style estimator (mechanistic alternative)
that could reveal a dose effect *present only at equal observation* — information the plain
dose model loses.

## 5. Falsification and interpretation
- **Supportive of H-observation:** >~50% attenuation M0→M1 AND significant burden→detection-outcome
  association AND near-null burden effect in M2.
- **Adverse (H-dose supported):** <~20% attenuation, weak detection-channel association, burden
  effect preserved in balanced strata.
- **Inconclusive:** collinear observation covariates / insufficient monitoring-density variation /
  CIs crossing the pre-specified equivalence margin (pre-declared useful-negative branch).
Association/transport only — **no causal MAP-target claim**; mechanism not identifiable in eICU
(no order-set/staffing/bed data).
