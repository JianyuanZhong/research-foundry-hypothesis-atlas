# Boundary-safe all-opportunity experiment for incidental pulmonary-nodule handoff

## Purpose and substantive repair

This proposal synthesizes `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`, preserving the clinically important question while repairing four residual defects: (1) knowledge-at-discharge was not fully separated from information learned by reconstructing the complete addendum graph; (2) mutually exclusive content-state precedence was underspecified when faithful text, stale text, and alternate resolutions coexist; (3) exposure-specific and observation-conditioned surveillance clocks could be mistaken for causal follow-up; and (4) the verifier was not required to attack the most likely leakage and unsupported-claim paths.

The experiment asks a process question, not whether patients adhered or benefited:

> For every first eligible inpatient opportunity in which a pulmonary-nodule CT recommendation is already available by actual discharge, what discharge-boundary state is observable: storage delayed beyond discharge, faithful interval-specific propagation, actionable-content omission/discordance, documented alternate resolution, or unresolved data?

The primary decision is whether the *observable storage-timestamp signal* (`A`) is larger than the *observable content-failure signal* (`C_O + C_I`) over one frozen all-opportunity denominator. This does not establish that `storetime` represents visibility, signing, transmission, departure, communication, or clinical appropriateness. Same-system CT is a secondary descriptive observation outcome and cannot select the primary intervention target.

## Evidence boundary and falsifiable claim

The cited Kwan cohort (PMCID `PMC6625441`) supports an association between discharge-summary mention and timely imaging in a small inpatient/ED cohort, not a causal effect. The FIND report (PMCID `PMC10287591`) documents outside-system care and incomplete ascertainment, so absence from an integrated record is not nonadherence. The cited review (PMCID `PMC10944736`) supports heterogeneity in reporting and communication. The available evidence therefore supports the existence of a measurable transition problem, while the following remains untested in MIMIC:

`Delta = P(A) - P(C_O or C_I)` among all eligible opportunities `N`.

The operational availability-first hypothesis is `Delta > 0.05`; the 0.05 margin is explicitly an intervention-prioritization rule, not a clinical-quality or benefit threshold. Report sensitivity at margins 0 and 0.10. A support decision requires the simultaneous 95% lower bound for `Delta` to exceed 0.05, unresolved-state bounds not to reverse that result, and all annotation/precision gates. Otherwise the result is inconclusive for choosing a single mechanism. A content-first decision is the symmetric upper-bound result below -0.05. No result supports causality, cancer prevention, malignancy detection, surveillance adherence, or patient benefit.

## Report/addendum graph without future leakage

Construct a static integrity graph from all relation rows, but distinguish graph integrity from boundary knowledge.

1. A directed edge is admissible only when the relation field explicitly identifies a parent/addendum and endpoint note IDs resolve to rows. Validate subject and `hadm_id` consistency, note types (`RR`/`AR` when present), and both directions where both are supplied. A globally absent endpoint, contradictory subject/admission relation, non-deterministic cycle, or missing required boundary timestamp is `U_R` and is reported in the screening cascade.
2. A linked addendum whose row exists globally but has `storetime > t_D` is **late**, not missing and not an unresolved pre-discharge chain. It is excluded from the report state at discharge but retained as a post-boundary transition. This rule prevents learning of a later addendum from changing the earlier opportunity into a failure or from excluding it.
3. At knowledge boundary `t_D = admissions.dischtime`, the report state is the base report plus only linked addenda with nonmissing `storetime <= t_D`; no text, interval, negation, or addendum event stored later may define eligibility or exposure. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.
4. The recommendation-bearing state must be resolvable and available by `t_D`; define `t_R` as the largest `storetime` among the included base/addendum components. A recommendation first introduced only by a late addendum is not eligible. A recommendation present in the base by discharge remains eligible even if a later addendum changes it; the later change is a sensitivity/descriptive transition.
5. Primary graph strictness requires a reciprocal, subject/admission-consistent relation. A prespecified one-sided-edge sensitivity may quantify how many opportunities would change, but it cannot silently enter the primary state.

The index examination time is the base report `charttime`, because the recommendation refers to that examination. Require nonmissing base `charttime`; never replace it with `storetime`. Due dates use exact calendar-month addition (`relativedelta`) for 3, 6, or 12 months, not 30.4375-day arithmetic.

## Eligibility and frozen denominator

Scan every radiology row; lexical rules are high-sensitivity retrieval only. Two blinded radiology-trained reviewers adjudicate every screened candidate using the report, detail fields, and radiology history available through the report boundary, while blinded to discharge text and later outcomes.

Include adults aged at least 18 using the standard within-subject MIMIC age construction from `patients.anchor_age`, `anchor_year`, and admission date, with 90+ treated as a category. The base report must link on `(subject_id, hadm_id)` and satisfy `admittime - 12 hours <= charttime <= dischtime`; repeat the full cascade with a zero-hour boundary and with the available `edregtime/edouttime` sensitivity. Require CT/CTA chest or an adjudicated chest-containing examination using all `radiology_detail` exam fields and text. Require an incidental pulmonary/lung nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity and an affirmed patient-specific, unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months.

Exclude explicit screening, nodule surveillance, staging/oncologic surveillance, actively managed primary pulmonary malignancy, nonpulmonary nodules, conditional/optional recommendations dependent on unavailable risk, explicit no-follow-up recommendations, hospice/comfort-only transition, in-hospital death, and an explicitly superseding pre-discharge CT. Do not require undocumented Fleischner eligibility, pack-years, or guideline concordance. A prior local report establishing guideline-complete stability excludes the opportunity only when that conclusion is available by the index report boundary; absence of two years of local history is a missing-history indicator and a sensitivity stratum, not an exclusion.

Select the first eligible opportunity per subject by base `charttime`, then base `storetime`, then `note_id`, using no discharge text, later observation, or later outcome. Define `N` before inspecting DS content or DS timing. `N` includes no DS row, unknown DS `storetime`, delayed DS, or artifact-before-recommendation chronology. Missing admission links and globally unresolved report chains remain outside `N` but must be counted in an unconditional cascade with lower/upper eligibility bounds; they are never recoded as handoff failure.

## Canonical discharge artifact and mutually exclusive state hierarchy

The inherited full-source audit found one `DS` row per admission key. Revalidate this before compilation. In this frozen source, the sole `DS` row is canonical, regardless of its `charttime` or `storetime`. If duplicate `DS` rows or version ties are found, stop and freeze a new version-selection rule; do not select “latest” post hoc. `storetime` is the only observed-storage field.

For every `i in N`, assign exactly one strict state using this precedence:

1. `U_D`: no linked DS row, missing DS `storetime`, contradictory linkage, unreadable text, or inability to determine whether the artifact is classifiable. Unknown is not delayed and not omission.
2. `A`: a linked canonical DS exists with known `storetime > dischtime`. This is only “not observed stored by discharge.” It remains `A` even if its later text is faithful, omitted, discordant, or an alternate resolution.
3. `R`: DS stored by discharge and explicitly documents a resolution that applies to the index recommendation and supersedes or replaces CT follow-up, such as a documented goals-of-care decision, refusal/preference, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, already-scheduled outside follow-up, or a clinical decision that CT is unnecessary. A generic “follow up with PCP” is not an alternate resolution. If an explicit resolution coexists with a separate active CT plan, code both secondary flags and apply the codebook rule: `R` takes precedence only when it clearly supersedes the index CT; otherwise classify the active plan below and retain `resolution_present` as a flag.
4. `F`: DS stored by discharge and contains an active, patient-specific pulmonary-nodule mention, chest/thoracic CT action, and exactly the same 3/6/12-month calendar interval or an unambiguous equivalent due date. Copied report prose without an active plan, negation, history, family history, hypothetical language, and generic guideline boilerplate do not count.
5. `C_I`: DS stored by discharge and contains a patient-specific nodule or follow-up signal but has no action, no usable interval, a discordant interval/modality, or ambiguous interval language such as “several months” or “three to six months.” An explicit alternate resolution is `R`, not `C_I`.
6. `C_O`: DS stored by discharge and has no active patient-specific actionable nodule follow-up content.

The hierarchy is applied only after two transition reviewers independently abstract DS text while blinded to the radiology text, exact recommendation, chronology, and later care. Then a deterministic matching step compares abstracted fields with the locked report recommendation. Report raw agreement, Gwet AC1 and kappa for eligibility, exact interval, resolution, and state. Require consensus, raw agreement >=0.80, and AC1 >=0.70; if a domain fails, re-adjudicate once and then report reviewer-specific bounds only. Automated retrieval must have sensitivity >=0.95 with lower Wilson bound >0.90 against consensus; automated state replacement additionally requires PPV >=0.90 with lower bound >0.80.

The primary all-opportunity quantities are `p_A = A/N`, `p_C = (C_O+C_I)/N`, `p_F = F/N`, and the full multinomial state vector over `(U_D,A,R,F,C_I,C_O)`. The conditional content denominator is strictly `N_obs = F+C_I+C_O+R`, not `N` and not a ratio selected after seeing outcome. Report `N_obs/N` first and `(F,C_I,C_O,R)/N_obs` second. `A` is never in `N_obs`; `U_D` is never silently assigned to omission. An artifact stored before the recommendation is tagged `stale-before-recommendation`; it remains in `N_obs` and is classified by text, with omission/discordance reported as a stale-update subtype. The strict state remains unchanged in all later transition matrices.

For `U_D`, partial-identification bounds for `Delta` assign all unresolved units to `A` and then all unresolved units to `C`; units that would be `F`, `R`, or neither contribute zero to `A-C`, so these are valid extrema for this contrast. Do not describe the bounds as identifying visibility or content.

## Secondary same-system observation outcome

Do not use later trace to define `N`, DS state, or intervention choice. For every `N` opportunity, calculate due date from base `charttime` plus exact calendar months. The primary secondary event is a MIMIC radiology report with `charttime` strictly after `dischtime` and in `[due-42 days, due+42 days]`, adjudicated as nodule-focused; report any chest CT separately and distinguish acute-admission imaging. Use `dischtime` as the common time zero for the all-`N` descriptive outcome; do not use `max(dischtime, DS.storetime)` for one arm and `dischtime` for another in the primary contrast, because that makes the observation window exposure-dependent. A separate delayed-artifact descriptive table may report the `max` clock, clearly labeled non-comparable.

Assign a joint capture state for descriptive reporting: (1) timed same-system CT before recorded death; (2) recorded death before the due-window end with no earlier timed CT; (3) no timed CT/death but later MIMIC radiology or admission after `due+42`; (4) no subsequent local trace. The state is a capture/observation outcome, not adherence. `dod`, `deathtime`, and `hospital_expire_flag` are incomplete mortality evidence and are labeled accordingly. Do not call state 4 a failed follow-up or outside-care absence. An IPW analysis may be an explicitly assumption-labeled sensitivity only; it is not a repair for missing outside care.

The surveillance appendix is descriptive or stratified only. It must not claim that `F` causes imaging: `F` is a post-report documentation state confounded by discharge stability, service, utilization, and capture. If an adjusted association is explored, pre-specify it as a noncausal association, use only covariates measured no later than `t_D`, report positivity and standardized differences, and do not interpret the coefficient as an intervention effect. The primary intervention decision is based solely on the all-opportunity discharge state decomposition.

## Statistical gates and falsification

Report the entire cascade, all state counts, missingness, graph integrity, timing distributions, and `N_obs/N` before any model. Use exact multinomial score intervals and a fixed-seed 9,999 subject-level bootstrap for simultaneous intervals for `(p_A,p_C,p_F,Delta)` and state proportions; first-opportunity-per-subject makes subject the resampling unit. If studentization fails, use the prespecified non-studentized max-deviation interval and report exact intervals as a check.

A dominance conclusion requires at least 200 `N`, at least 20 `A` and 20 `C` events, `U_D <= 5%` of `N`, all annotation gates, and simultaneous `Delta` interval width <=0.10. If the gates fail, report a bounded descriptive state distribution only. A near-null result with adequate precision is adverse to a single availability/content prioritization; scarcity, unresolved bounds crossing the margin, poor agreement, or timing-sensitivity reversal is inconclusive. Report exact 3/6/12-month and CT-type strata descriptively; do not use exploratory strata to select a mechanism.

Mandatory falsification and data-quality tests are:

* zero-hour versus 12-hour admission linkage and `edregtime/edouttime` sensitivity;
* base-only versus reciprocal-graph report-state reconstruction, with late addenda held out from eligibility;
* an artificial future-addendum test in which a post-discharge negating or interval-changing addendum must not alter `N` or strict state;
* exact calendar arithmetic versus an intentionally wrong 30.4375-day implementation, which the verifier must detect;
* duplicate-DS/version-tie adversarial fixtures, missing `storetime`, missing DS, and artifact-before-recommendation fixtures;
* temporal placebo CT windows before the index and generic non-chest imaging in the due window;
* nodule-focused versus acute/symptom-driven CT separation and capture-state cross-tabs;
* a deliberately post-index-trace-conditioned analysis, which the verifier must reject as the primary estimand;
* negative-control documentation findings and generic utilization controls, interpreted as confounding/extraction warnings rather than adjusted away.

A same-direction temporal placebo, generic-utilization association as large as the nodule signal, a result driven by acute CT, or large `F`/`C` observation imbalance makes the surveillance appendix inconclusive. A positive primary `Delta` without a local timestamp crosswalk remains a storage-timestamp signal, not evidence of an availability workflow failure.

## Verifier scope and missing evidence

The automatic verifier can check source hashes, exact archive members, headers and schema fields; complete-row scan declarations; joins; first-subject selection; static graph integrity; cutoff-limited report states; strict `storetime <= dischtime`; sole-DS rule; mutually exclusive state precedence; exact month arithmetic; no post-index denominator restriction; partial-identification extrema; bootstrap/interval calculations; gate application; placebo and generic controls; and whether the written conclusion matches computed outputs.

It cannot establish true report visibility, signing/finalization, transmission, physical discharge time, patient notification, orders/referrals/appointments, outside imaging, undocumented alternate plans, nodule morphology from pixels, guideline appropriateness, smoking exposure, malignancy, cancer outcomes, adherence, or patient benefit. A prospective local audit linking signing, UI visibility, release/transmission, and physical departure is required before labeling `A` a workflow availability defect. A prospective stepped-wedge, factorial, or randomized transition intervention with outside-system capture is required for causal benefit claims.
