## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After liver-directed therapy (TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats serum AFP, albumin and
bilirubin. Open question: does the *within-patient AFP trajectory* add prognostic information beyond
**baseline AFP** and beyond **liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics measured in the SAME draws**, for time to
radiologic local progression - free of immortal-time/lead-time bias and of outcome misclassification?
Clinically this bears on whether serial AFP should be used for risk-adapted imaging intensity after LDT.

## 2. Hypothesis (falsifiable)
H1 (leading claim): a rising within-patient AFP slope measured on (index, t*] is associated with shorter time to
radiologic local progression independently of baseline AFP and same-draw ALBI kinetics.
Rival R0 (strongest): the association is an artifact of the *unvalidated* lexical progression endpoint - i.e. it
does not survive outcome-misclassification correction, or it is confined to reports failing adjudication.

## 4. THIS EPISODE'S ONE ADDED ELEMENT: outcome-ascertainment validation + probabilistic bias analysis
The primary endpoint is an UNVALIDATED lexical extraction. Pre-specify:
(a) Draw-level audit: a pre-declared random sample of 150 abdominal-liver progression-flag reports (stratified by
    descriptor) is manually adjudicated against report text as reference; pre-registered agreement floor = 0.80.
(b) If floor met: a probabilistic bias analysis propagating sensitivity Se in [0.70,0.95] and specificity Sp in
    [0.80,0.98] (Monte-Carlo, 20k draws) through the primary AFP-slope per-1-SD Cox hazard ratio; report the
    bias-corrected HR distribution and whether its 95% interval excludes 1 and keeps the sign.
(c) Descriptor-vs-adjudicated discordance is tabulated by stratum (triggered vs interval-scheduled).

### Falsification branches
- Supportive: audit agreement >=0.80 AND bias-corrected per-1-SD AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 with the pre-specified sign
  AND the monotone quartile trend persists (inherited p_trend<0.05, Q2-vs-Q1 CI excludes 1, >=30 events).
- Adverse: agreement >=0.80 but bias-corrected HR CI includes 1 (association attributable to misclassification), OR the
  effect is confined to reports that FAIL adjudication (descriptor false-positive).
- Inconclusive: agreement <0.80 (endpoint not validly ascertainable -> escalate to expert radiologist mRECIST adjudication
  or another study); <30 interval-scheduled events; or <10/quartile (tertile fallback pre-declared).
- Precise-null: conjunctive equivalence vs baseline-only model M0 within +/-0.01 C-index.

## 5. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative) - recorded before selection
- M0 (baseline): baseline-value landmark Cox (baseline AFP + baseline ALBI + age/sex/procedure type).
- M4 (alternative): joint mixed-effects models for AFP and ALBI slopes, individual BLUPs entered into the Cox.
  What M4 recovers that M0 loses: change-vs-level information and shrinkage across sparse/irregular draws; it estimates
  the rate of change rather than a single anchor value.
- Deferred with revisit conditions: shared-parameter observation-process joint model (addresses informative observation)
  and Fine-Gray competing-risks model - both computable from the same tables, revisited if the audit floor passes and a
  residual informative-observation objection remains. CPU-only, minutes-scale.
