# Availability-aware test of UACR-conditioned cystatin-C allocation

## Decision gap and hypothesis

The parent’s A and AG policies correctly ask whether raw grip adds information
after UACR and whether grip-only G can substitute for UACR. One clinically
consequential gap remains: A and AG rank every member of O by imputing an
unavailable UACR. That is a valid fixed-sample statistical comparison, but a
clinician cannot use a UACR-conditioned score when the baseline urine result is
not observed. The parent’s high UACR coverage does not establish that its
incremental claim survives this deployment restriction. The UKB export also has
no urine collection date or assay-cost field.

The strongest available evidence is feasibility, not benefit. The inherited
read-only audit found 130,257 usable UACRs among 134,118 O participants
(97.12% overall; 97.18% and 97.14% in the two locked partitions), while the
source-level audit below confirms the low-result flag and numeric fields. No
result has established that grip improves fixed-budget allocation when UACR is
actually observed, or that either policy improves care.

The successor hypothesis is:

> In each locked partition, among O participants with a valid observed baseline
> UACR (V), an observed-UACR-restricted AG policy will identify at least 2 more
> combined-equation eGFR<60 reclassifications per 1,000 cystatin assays than
> the corresponding restricted A policy, with yield ratio at least 1.10 and
> positive D contrast. Restricted grip-only G will be noninferior to restricted
> A at -1 reclassification per 1,000. If N18 event sufficiency passes, AG will
> provide at least 1 additional baseline-H reclassification followed by a
> 10-year inpatient N18 event per 1,000 assays and G will be noninferior to A.
> This must hold in both locks and cannot be earned by the parent’s imputed-
> all-O analysis.

This is one decision claim: whether grip adds value after a urine result is
actually observed, with every policy ranking the same observed-UACR pool. It
does not claim UACR is always available, costless, persistent albuminuria, true
GFR, CKD, mechanism, or patient benefit.

## Population, availability rule and policy construction

O is exactly the parent O: age 40–69 at recruitment; sex 0/1; valid baseline
assessment date; positive baseline creatinine and BMI; at least one finite
positive baseline left/right grip value; 2021 race-free eGFRcr in [60,90); and
no position-matched inpatient N17* or N18* diagnosis on or before baseline.
Any N17/N18 code with missing or invalid paired date remains an exclusion and is
reported.

Define V after O but before opening any cystatin label or health outcome:

- positive numeric 30500-0.0 and positive 30510-0.0; or
- blank 30500-0.0, 30505-0.0 trimmed exactly to <6.7, and positive
  30510-0.0.

Use the parent’s primary UACR = 1000 * albumin / urine creatinine in mg/mmol,
with a flagged albumin represented as 3.35 mg/L. A numeric-plus-flag
contradiction, missing/nonpositive urine creatinine, missing albumin without
the exact flag, any other flag, or a nonfinite ratio is not V and is an
integrity count. Field 30515-0.0 is the urine-creatinine result flag and is
never parsed as albumin. V is an observed-label stratum, not a new O:
population, time zero and all inherited denominators stay fixed.

For each partition p, set n_p=floor(0.20*|O_p|), exactly as in the parent.
Preflight must verify |V_p| >= n_p; if not, the added arm is inconclusive and
the inherited all-O arm is unaffected. For each of A, AG and G, rank only V_p
using its already frozen all-O score and the inherited tie hash, then select
exactly n_p. Call these A_V, AG_V and G_V. Thus each added policy uses exactly
the parent’s number of cystatin assays and 20% of O, not 20% of V. B_V, the
frozen B score restricted to V_p, is a descriptive model-capacity baseline and
cannot rescue the primary claim.

Fit, tune, winsorize, standardize and impute only in development O exactly as
the parent. Do not refit in V or either lock. Apply all six parent-frozen UACR
variants to the same V membership; the 0/3.35/6.7 censored-value and
development 1st/99th-percentile missing-UACR variants cannot change V or rescue
the primary result. Report V coverage by lock, integrity counts, and V-versus-O
characteristics before opening outcomes.

## Outcomes, estimands and uncertainty

Retain the parent’s outcomes. For S in V_p,

- Y_H^V(S) = 1000 * sum(H_i for i in S) / n_p;
- Y_D^V(S) = 1000 * sum(D_i for i in S) / n_p;
- Y_H18^V(S) = 1000 * sum(H_i*K18_i for i in S) / n_p.

The primary new contrast is Delta_H^V(AG_V:A_V), with exact feasible yield
ratio, overlap/Jaccard, and AG_V\A_V versus A_V\AG_V swap-set yields.
Secondary decision contrasts are Delta_H^V(G_V:A_V) and
Delta_D^V(AG_V:A_V), with the analogous G contrast. Report AG_V:B_V
descriptively. Keep parent all-O AG:A and G:A outputs adjacent so an
imputation-dependent claim is detectable.

In each lock, require for AG_V:A_V: adverse joint-bound Delta_H at least 2 per
1,000; ratio at least 1.10; simultaneous 95% intervals above 0 and 1; and
positive AG_V\A_V versus A_V\AG_V H yield. Require G_V:A_V to have a
one-sided simultaneous 95% lower bound above -1 per 1,000; call superiority
only if its two-sided interval is above zero. Require positive
Delta_D^V(AG_V:A_V) in both locks and a pooled partition-stratified paired 95%
interval above zero. All inherited parent G/B/F, placebo, calibration,
sex-stability, UACR-variant and missing-label gates remain mandatory.

For H*K18, use the parent’s participant-level joint missing-H bounds and exact
feasible ratio extrema. Require at least 20 observed-label H*K18 events in
each pooled AG_V\A_V and A_V\AG_V swap set, and in each G_V\A_V and
A_V\G_V swap set. If sufficient, require Delta_H18^V(AG_V:A_V) at least
1 per 1,000 with a pooled two-sided 95% interval above zero and a one-sided
lower bound for G_V:A_V above -1 per 1,000. Positive N17 or Kany cannot
rescue N18. Preserve the parent’s >1% missing-cystatin inconclusive rule and
death handling.

Use the inherited 2,000 participant-paired, no-refit bootstrap within each
lock, with max-|t| adjustment over the added V H, ratio, D and H18 family and
partition-stratified pooling. Do not subtract marginal missing-label bounds:
shared participants have one common unknown H assignment. Report standard
errors, interval endpoints, exact n, label counts, overlaps and swap-set
counts.

K18 remains the earliest valid paired inpatient N18* code/date in
41270-0.i/41280-0.i, i=0,...,258, with date in (time zero,time zero+10 years].
Death is the earliest valid 40000-0.0 or 40000-1.0, competing and preceding
same-day K18. N17 does not censor N18.

## Falsification and result meaning

Falsify the new actionable-increment claim if either lock has a nonpositive
AG_V:A_V H contrast, fails the 2/1,000 adverse bound or 1.10 ratio, has a
crossed simultaneous interval, lacks positive swap-set yield, or fails D.
Falsify substitution if G_V:A_V is inferior beyond -1/1,000 with its one-sided
bound. A sign reversal across any frozen UACR-value variant is inconclusive;
no sensitivity rescues a primary failure.

Fully supportive requires every inherited all-O gate plus both-lock V biochemical
gates, G_V:A_V noninferiority, UACR robustness, and—when event-sufficient—the
V N18 gates. It supports a prospective comparison of availability-aware
allocation strategies only.

If parent AG:A passes but AG_V:A_V fails, the apparent grip increment is
imputation-dependent and the observed-UACR decision is adverse. If AG_V beats
A_V but G_V is inferior, grip is a complement only where UACR is observed, not
a substitute. If G_V is noninferior but AG_V is not superior, grip-only
allocation may be a lower-information candidate among those with observed
UACR, but nothing follows for people without UACR. If A_V matches or beats
AG_V with adequate precision, raw grip adds no decision-relevant information
beyond observed UACR under this design.

Inconclusive means V_p<n_p; UACR integrity failure; parent or V missingness
bounds; sparse N18 swap events; partition disagreement; crossed intervals;
zero feasible ratio denominator; leakage; chronology/join failure; or
calibration/sex instability. Report biochemical results without upgrading an
inconclusive N18 result.

## Falsification fixtures and verifier boundary

Fixtures must include: treating 30515-0.0 as albumin; using 6.7 as measured;
allowing an imputed UACR participant into V; selecting 20% of V rather than
n_p; refitting A/AG/G in V; allowing cystatin, H, K18, death or future
assessment to define V or a score; changing V across UACR variants; unequal
A_V/AG_V/G_V candidate pools; marginal rather than joint missing-H bounds;
rounded eGFR or altered tie hashes; N17 censoring N18; same-day death order;
and reporting a parent all-O pass as an observed-UACR pass.

The verifier can check source hashes/headers, one-to-one eid joins, O identity,
time-zero and date windows, V rule, score reuse, exact n, lock separation,
leakage, outcomes, bounds, bootstrap intervals, gates and output-linked
supportive/adverse/inconclusive narratives. It cannot establish that V means
UACR was clinically available before a decision, that UACR is persistent
albuminuria, that combined eGFR is measured GFR, that N18 is adjudicated CKD,
that assay cost or harms are acceptable, that testing changes management, or
that results transport beyond UKB. Those require specimen workflow/timestamps,
repeat urine testing, measured GFR, outpatient/chart and prescription/action
data, costs, equity/external validation, and prospective implementation or
randomized testing-strategy evidence.

## Substantive advance

The parent answers whether grip adds to an imputation-completed UACR model.
This successor asks the decision a service can actually face after a baseline
urine result exists: does grip still improve who receives one of the fixed
cystatin assays when every compared policy ranks the same observed-UACR pool?
A failure prevents an apparent all-O increment from being treated as deployable
evidence. The design does not claim to solve assay cost, urine timing,
persistence, causal benefit or transportability.
