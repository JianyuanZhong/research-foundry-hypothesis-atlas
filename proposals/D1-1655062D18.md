# Blinded prevalence-adaptive, fixed-lock validation of EHR vasopressor restart against intent-qualified pump rescue

## 1. Decision question, evidence boundary, and unresolved hypothesis

The prerequisite clinical decision is whether a binary EHR-derived “restart of the first-stopped vasopressor within six hours” is accurate, complete, arm-symmetric, and interface-transportable enough to be an endpoint component in a later discontinuation-order trial. This experiment does **not** ask which vasopressor should be stopped first.

Every inherited adverse MIMIC finding is frozen rather than re-estimated or relaxed:

* Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, assigned actions were rare: 211 norepinephrine-first (NE-first; 3.49%) and 167 vasopressin-first (VP-first; 2.76%), both below the 5% positivity gate. The 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls.
* The observed-stop cohort contains 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate composite failures. The arms are neither randomized nor exchangeable and are too small for a causal stopping-order comparison.
* Among 73 raw failures in 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. Mechanical circulatory support (MCS) status is unknown in all 137 inherited episodes.
* Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvement (about 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, both below the frozen 0.01 gate) and adverse later transport. No prognostic score or subgroup advances.
* The no-sampling transaction audit processed all 137 inherited episodes after the declared stay/item/time filters and recovered all 137 stop boundaries and all 59 raw restarts. Five symmetric continuity rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is only that the EHR endpoint is computable, restart-dominated, and sensitive to transaction reconstruction. MIMIC transaction status, chain, rate, caregiver, `storetime`, intermittent MAP, and positive-only device records do not establish delivered drug, genuine cessation, clinician intent, rescue, false-positive or false-negative rates, or true MCS absence.

The unresolved prospective hypothesis is:

> Among the first qualifying episode per consecutively enrolled patient, in both observed first-stop strata, a locked EHR restart test has PPV and sensitivity greater than 0.80 for intent-qualified pump-native rescue, and the NE-first minus VP-first difference for each metric lies strictly within ±0.10, under full EHR-positive and EHR-negative verification, deterministic mapped-clock rules, joint conservative indeterminate bounds, and prespecified interface-transport gates.

A supportive result validates only this restart component for enumerated configurations. It cannot establish that rescue causes harm, that stopping order causes rescue, that either stopping order is preferable, that the endpoint is clinically useful, or that a later randomized strategy is safe or beneficial.

## 2. Population, temporal boundary, and primary unit

### 2.1 Infrastructure qualification

Recruit at least six adult ICUs and at least two pump-EHR vendor/configuration families with synchronized smart-pump, monitor, and EHR exports. Before validation, each site processes 50 new consecutive run-in episodes using all already locked clinical definitions. At least 95% must have unique patient-channel-drug linkage, measured source-clock mappings, and complete pump export from two hours before `t0` through the observable six-hour window; at least 90% must have a contemporaneous intent source. Infrastructure may be repaired, but thresholds, windows, labels, reference categories, estimands, and margins may not change. A failed site may repeat with 50 new episodes once; a second failure makes it feasibility-only. Run-in episodes are excluded.

The roster of primary and reserve sites/configurations is frozen before the first validation patient. Reserve-site activation during the first 600 primary patients may respond only to calendar accrual, site dropout, and configuration-representation fields, never E, R, an E-by-R table, or any accuracy result. No new configuration may enter after the one-time sample-size lock except as a separately reported feasibility stratum.

### 2.2 Consecutive qualifying cessations

Screen and reference-verify **every consecutive qualifying cessation**, whether EHR-positive, EHR-negative, or indeterminate. Eligibility uses only information available at cessation:

1. age at least 18 years, alive in an adult ICU;
2. pump-supported concurrent norepinephrine (NE) and vasopressin (VP) delivery for at least six continuous hours immediately before cessation;
3. at the beginning of the final 30 pre-index minutes, NE >0 and ≤0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 and ≤0.04 U/min, with neither increased during those 30 minutes;
4. no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours;
5. at least three valid MAP observations spanning at least three of the four prior 30-minute bins, including the final bin, with median MAP ≥65 and minimum MAP ≥55 mmHg; and
6. a non-simultaneous first cessation of one study drug while the comparator continues pump-native delivery.

Both drugs crossing below their threshold within five minutes is a simultaneous stop and is excluded and counted. A procedure or transport pause known at `t0` is excluded and counted. Actual MCS device type, active/standby state, and support setting are captured as prespecified strata; absence is never inferred from missing documentation.

### 2.3 Primary patient unit and recurrent-episode sensitivity

The **primary unit is the patient**, represented by that person's first qualifying episode across all participating ICUs during validation. Person identity is resolved across sites before inclusion; an unresolved cross-site identity is linkage-indeterminate and cannot be treated as a new independent patient. The first qualifying episode is selected solely by mapped `t0`, before E or R is known. Primary accrual, the 600-patient pilot, fixed `Nmax`, and all six confirmatory estimands count patients, not recurrent episodes.

All later qualifying cessations through the common database lock are nevertheless screened and fully verified as a prespecified recurrent-episode cohort. A new recurrent episode requires both drugs to satisfy a fresh six-hour concurrent-delivery baseline and `t0` at least 24 hours after the preceding `t0`. Candidate cessations within 24 hours or lacking a fresh six-hour baseline remain transitions within the existing episode and are counted but cannot become new indices. Recurrent-episode estimates use patient-cluster resampling stratified by arm, site, and configuration; report episodes per patient, time between episodes, arm switching, and effective cluster count. They are secondary and cannot rescue a failed primary result. This retains the future episode-level use case without treating correlated episodes as independent or allowing recurrent outcomes to drive accrual.

## 3. Locked clocks, EHR index test, and intent-qualified reference

### 3.1 Pump-native cessation and observable window

`t0` is the first pump timestamp at which the first-stopped drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below for at least five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Drug-library identity, concentration, patient, channel, weight, and delivered-rate stream must be linked; a programmed rate alone is insufficient.

The target window is `(t0, t0+6 hours]`. Death or ICU exit before six hours is a determinate closure only when pump and EHR capture are complete through that closure. A technical, export, clock, or linkage gap before the minimum of six hours, death, or ICU exit makes the affected label indeterminate, never negative. Eligibility never depends on future restart, rescue, physiology, death, ICU exit, or capture.

Before validation, each source clock is mapped to the pump clock with a frozen affine map, time zone and daylight-saving rule. Let `b` be the configuration's run-in 99th percentile absolute mapping residual, capped at 60 seconds. A configuration requiring `b>60` seconds fails qualification. Every mapped event has the fixed uncertainty interval `[mapped_time-b, mapped_time+b]`; analysts never move an event after labels are seen.

### 3.2 Escrowed EHR index test

The EHR algorithm and executable are versioned and escrowed before reference adjudication:

* NE item 221906: retain mcg/kg/min; divide mcg/min by contemporaneous valid weight 30–300 kg; reject normalized rate >5 mcg/kg/min.
* VP item 222315: divide units/hour by 60; retain units/min; reject normalized rate >0.2 U/min.

`E=1` when a new positive stopped-drug EHR interval has mapped `starttime` in `(t0,t0+6 hours]` and `endtime>starttime`. A row already spanning `t0` is not new. Thus a genuine pump rescue hidden within one spanning EHR interval remains `E=0` and can be a false negative. `orderid`, `linkorderid`, `statusdescription`, `storetime`, caregiver, `isopenbag`, and `continueinnextdept` are discrepancy descriptors and cannot repair the primary test.

If an otherwise qualifying EHR interval's fixed mapped-time uncertainty interval crosses `t0`, `t0+6 hours`, death, or ICU exit, set `E=?` for the primary bounds. Deterministic mapped-point classification and exclusion of boundary-near events are nonconfirmatory sensitivities.

### 3.3 Intent-qualified pump-native rescue reference

`R=1` requires both:

1. after the qualifying off interval, linkable delivered pump rate for the stopped drug again reaches at least 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP continuously for at least five minutes in the target window; and
2. two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators determine from timestamped contemporaneous evidence that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion after cessation.

Bag or syringe replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, procedural pause, mistaken-stop correction, and uninterrupted delivery segmented only in documentation are reference-negative for intent-qualified rescue. The pump algorithm runs before adjudication. Adjudicators see pump and monitor traces, workflow events, cointerventions, contemporaneous intent evidence, and unavoidable drug/arm identity, but not E, EHR chain/status diagnostics, aggregate denominators, accuracy, or each other's label. Categories are rescue-positive; pump resumption without rescue intent; workflow/technical transition; no pump resumption; clock/export/linkage failure; and intent-indeterminate. A third independently blinded adjudicator resolves disagreement.

Report raw agreement, Gwet AC1, and kappa. After the first 100 validation patients, raw agreement <85% or both corrected measures <0.70 suspends adjudication for manual clarification without viewing E or accuracy. Validation restarts with 100 new primary patients; prior patients remain feasibility-only. A second failure makes the study inconclusive. A secondary pump-only reference diagnoses interface fidelity but cannot replace the clinical target.

## 4. Deterministic event matching and complete verification

Episode labels are primary. For secondary event analysis, enumerate every pump resumption crossing the locked threshold after a qualifying five-minute off spell and every new positive EHR interval in the observable window. Collapse within-source fragments separated by at most 30 seconds only when drug, patient, channel, concentration, and continuity are established. Within patient, episode, and drug, perform one-to-one maximum-cardinality, minimum-total-absolute-lag bipartite matching under a locked ±10-minute caliper. Resolve ties by earlier pump time, earlier EHR time, earlier immutable source row identifier. An event cannot match twice or cross patient, episode, drug, `t0`, death/exit, or the six-hour boundary. Boundary-indeterminate events remain indeterminate.

Report matched and unmatched pump events, matched and unmatched EHR events, duplicate fragments, signed lag, median and 95th-percentile absolute lag. Matching cannot create, remove, or repair episode-level E or R. All EHR-negative episodes receive the same pump algorithm and intent review as EHR-positive episodes; no verification fraction, outcome-triggered chart review, or positive enrichment is allowed.

## 5. Estimands, joint indeterminate bounds, and inference

For primary arm `a` (NE-first or VP-first):

* `PPV_a=P(R=1 | E=1, arm=a)`;
* `Sensitivity_a=P(E=1 | R=1, arm=a)`;
* `Delta_PPV=PPV_NE-PPV_VP`;
* `Delta_Sens=Sensitivity_NE-Sensitivity_VP`.

Produce the complete `E∈{0,1,?}` by `R∈{0,1,?}` table by arm, site, configuration, MCS stratum, and primary/recurrent status. No enrolled primary patient is removed after E or R is observed.

The confirmatory bounds use **joint admissible completions**, not incompatible metric-specific imputations. For every arm, assign binary E and R values to all cells containing `?`, subject only to determinate labels; for every joint completion compute both arm metrics, their Wilson bounds, and both arm-difference score intervals. The conservative lower confidence bound for each PPV/sensitivity is the minimum lower limit over all completions. For each difference, the conservative envelope is the minimum lower and maximum upper endpoint over the same joint NE/VP completions. Aggregate-cell integer optimization is allowed instead of individual enumeration, but the compiler must unit-test equality to exhaustive enumeration on small tables. Undefined denominators cannot pass and are inconclusive. Determinate-only estimates and raw identification intervals are secondary.

Each arm-specific PPV and sensitivity passes only when its one-sided 95% Wilson score lower bound is **strictly greater than 0.80**. Each difference passes only when its 90% Miettinen-Newcombe/Newcombe hybrid-score interval is **strictly inside `(-0.10,0.10)`**. Touching a boundary fails that component. The global claim is a six-component intersection-union test: all four conservative accuracy and both conservative symmetry components must pass. No multiplicity correction is needed for this conjunctive global claim, but this does not create simultaneous 95% coverage or permit an individual metric to be called confirmatorily validated after global failure.

The primary analysis is first-patient-only. Report fixed-mixture Wilson/Newcombe results plus a site-stratified patient bootstrap with at least 10,000 resamples. If a bootstrap interval reverses a confirmatory pass, the global conclusion is inconclusive. Recurrent-episode patient-cluster bootstrap and GEE estimates are secondary. Specificity, NPV, R prevalence, E positivity, paired E-minus-R prevalence discrepancy, McNemar discordance, pump-only accuracy, timing, MCS, and transaction strata are descriptive. Four prespecified arm-by-site/configuration interaction families use Holm-adjusted two-sided 0.05 tests and simultaneous 95% intervals; no subgroup can redefine the test.

## 6. One-time blinded prevalence adaptation followed by a common fixed lock

### 6.1 Frozen precision target and inherited operating characteristics

The design target remains 220 determinate E-positive primary patients per arm for PPV and 220 determinate R-positive primary patients per arm for sensitivity. At denominator 220, at least 186 correct classifications are required for a one-sided 95% Wilson lower bound >0.80. In the inherited exact audit, component power is 0.619 at true accuracy 0.85, 0.949 at 0.88, and 0.996 at 0.90. Exact score-equivalence power at 220 per arm is 0.916 for 0.90 versus 0.90, 0.869 for 0.88 versus 0.88, 0.141 for 0.90 versus 0.82, and 0.011 for 0.92 versus 0.80. Under equal 0.90 metrics and moderate 50% prevalences, full six-gate success was 0.868, not 0.996. At the 0.80 boundary it was 0/10,000; with PPV 0.92 versus 0.80 it was 0.0078. These are planning properties, not evidence of prospective accuracy.

Frozen MIMIC proportions also show why the old 2,000-episode cap was incoherent. VP-first comprised 53/137 episodes and only 13/53 were E-positive. At those rates, 2,000 total episodes yield about 190 VP-first E-positive episodes and only 0.0129 probability of reaching 220; about 2,511 episodes are required for 90% probability. MIMIC provides no intent-qualified R prevalence.

### 6.2 Internal pilot and immutable lock tier

The first 600 consecutive **primary patients** across all qualified sites form a retained internal pilot. A firewalled statistician may receive only:

* arm and site/configuration;
* determinate E-positive membership;
* determinate R-positive membership and indeterminate status;
* primary-patient identity, calendar accrual month, and active-site status.

The statistician may not receive or derive the E-by-R cross-table, true/false-positive or true/false-negative counts, PPV, sensitivity, arm differences, matching, or any accuracy result. Quarterly operational reports before lock disclose only accrual, capture, and configuration representation; they never disclose the four denominator counts except to the firewalled statistician at the single 600-patient adaptation.

At exactly 600 primary patients, use Beta(1,1)-binomial posterior predictive simulation for arm shares and the four **marginal determinate-positive prevalences**, stratified by site/configuration and weighted by the frozen planned recruitment mixture. Evaluate three prespecified total-primary-patient lock tiers: 4,000, 5,000, and 6,000. Choose once the smallest tier whose posterior predictive probability of all four denominators reaching 220 is at least 0.90. If none qualifies, lock 6,000 and label denominator feasibility “at risk”; do not relax thresholds, enrich positives, change the estimand, or add a post hoc extension. The selected tier and the corresponding calendar projection are signed before any E-by-R table is opened.

Thereafter, all active sites accrue every eligible new primary patient until the common administrative lock: the earliest of (a) the selected Nth primary patient's `t0` followed by a fixed 30-day data/adjudication closeout, or (b) 48 months after the first validation patient followed by the same closeout. All sites stop together. **Actual attainment of 220 in any or all denominators never stops accrual; failure to attain 220 never extends it.** Faster-arm excess is retained. The only earlier global stop is an outcome-blind infrastructure impossibility: fewer than four active sites, a second adjudication-reliability failure, or documented inability to maintain required exports; it is operationally inconclusive.

The new bounded audit (`[research job]`; seed 20260909; 100,000 fixed-N replicates, 4,000 pilot replicates) stress-tested this rule under frozen MIMIC arm and E-positive rates and unknown R-prevalence scenarios. With 3% indeterminacy and 10% R prevalence in both arms, the probabilities of all four denominators at N=4,000/5,000/6,000 were 0, 0.0101, and 0.6439; the pilot labeled N=6,000 “at risk” in 90.35% of replicates, and actual target attainment after the selected lock was 0.639. With 15% R prevalence, those probabilities were 0.6471, 0.99995, and 1.0; tier selection frequencies were 11.0%/43.8%/31.3%, with 14.0% at-risk N=6,000, and actual attainment was 0.964. With 20% R prevalence, target attainment was 1.0 at every tier and 73.3% selected N=4,000. Thus 6,000 is a realistic ceiling rather than an assurance: low VP-first R prevalence can still yield an honest inconclusive study. Scenario prevalences are not prospective estimates.

Final inference uses the realized fixed-lock data and ordinary prespecified score intervals. Sample-size adaptation uses masked nuisance prevalences only; there is no accuracy look, alpha spending, early success, accuracy futility, denominator-triggered stopping, or recurrence-driven accrual.

## 7. Configuration-level multisite transport gate

A configuration is defined before analysis by site, ICU, pump vendor/model and software major version, integration engine, EHR medication-administration module/build, drug-library version, and clock-mapping method. A software or interface change creates a new stratum, requires a new 50-episode run-in, and cannot be silently pooled with its predecessor.

A transportable claim requires all of the following at common lock:

* at least six sites and two pump-EHR configuration families;
* both stop arms represented at at least four sites and two families;
* no site or family contributes >30% of any one of the four primary metric denominators;
* each arm-metric denominator is ≥40 at three sites and ≥20 at four sites.

Report configuration and site estimates, leave-one-site-out and leave-one-family-out analyses, hierarchical binomial estimates, and 95% prediction intervals. For every supported stratum with denominator ≥40, the conservative one-sided lower bound must exceed 0.70 for each observed arm-metric. Leave-one-site/family-out analyses must preserve all six global gates. A prediction interval extending below 0.70, adjusted site/configuration accuracy range >0.15, or Holm-significant interaction with absolute difference >0.10 is adverse for transportability even if pooled gates pass. Sparse support makes transport inconclusive, not homogeneous. Support may name only the exact validated configurations.

Stop a site's accrual for unresolved linkage, clock, or pump-export failure in >5% of its latest 50 episodes; it may resume only after a new passing run-in. Such operational decisions cannot change the common lock tier or create replacement primary patients after lock.

## 9. Falsification, interpretation, and verification boundary

**Supportive:** all four joint-conservative arm-specific lower bounds exceed 0.80; both joint 90% difference envelopes lie strictly within ±0.10; all four denominators reach 220 at the immutable common lock; capture, clock, adjudication, first-patient, and transport requirements pass; recurrent-episode cluster analysis and locked clock/off-duration sensitivities do not reverse a pass. This supports only the locked restart component at explicitly enumerated configurations.

**Adverse:** after adequate fixed-lock denominators, any conservative PPV or sensitivity gate fails; either arm difference is not equivalent; event matching shows systematic missed or spurious events; paired discordance is materially directional; or an adequately supported configuration violates the transport gates. High PPV cannot compensate for low sensitivity, pooled performance cannot compensate for arm/configuration asymmetry, and no favorable retrospective continuity rule may be selected post hoc.

**Inconclusive:** any denominator is <220 at the fixed lock; a confidence bound touches or crosses its threshold; joint indeterminate envelopes prevent a decision; primary and site-stratified results disagree at a gate; clock/linkage/export/intent/adjudication reliability fails; cross-site identity cannot preserve first-patient sampling; configuration concentration or prediction support is insufficient; or the 48-month/selected-N lock is reached without feasibility. Inconclusive is not adverse accuracy evidence and cannot authorize enrichment, extension, margin relaxation, or positive-only verification.

A verifier can check retrospective filters and inherited aggregates, prospective dispositions, first-patient selection, recurrence spacing, E/R tables, joint completions, score intervals, deterministic matching, masked tier-selection inputs, common-lock compliance, transport gates, and whether each reported conclusion follows from computed outputs. It cannot establish delivered drug or rescue intent without prospective device evidence and clinical adjudication; nor can it establish true MCS absence, endpoint clinical importance, safety, equipoise, causal stopping-order benefit, or treatment preference. Those require additional prospective data, expert review, and a separate interventional study.
