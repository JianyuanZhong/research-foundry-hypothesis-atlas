## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens nominate single circulating proteins (e.g. GDF15, CHI3L1, FABP1, IL-6/CRP family) as predictors / early-detection biomarkers of many diseases. The branch asks whether a robust cross-disease protein signal marks a **disease-specific process (A)** or a **shared systemic correlate (B)**. Episodes 15 and 17 eliminated chronological age/sex and disease co-occurrence/multimorbidity via case-case specificity. The strongest **uncovered** rival is **treatment/intervention state**: many circulating proteins change with surgery/operative management, drugs and care intensity, so an apparently disease-preferential protein signal could be a *treatment/procedure correlate* rather than disease biology. This distinction is consequential: it decides whether a candidate protein is a disease-biology target or only a marker of being under active operative treatment.

**Substantive advance over [K1-K3]:** rather than asserting either shared-module (B) or disease-specific (A) biology, this study makes the distinction falsifiable per protein with a held-out cross-disease **transfer-specificity gap** that must survive co-occurrence and operative-treatment contrasts, plus a permutation null. Contrast with the closest inspected work: **[K2]/[K3]** establish a shared axis but never separate per-protein disease-preferential residuals in a diseased-only design; **[K1]** warns that protein markers mix severity/treatment roles but gives no quantitative separation.

## Design (frozen estimand)
- **Population:** the 53,014 participants with Olink NPX AND dta rows (single `n_eid` namespace). Sex `n_31_0_0`, age `n_21003_0_0`.
- **Outcomes:** prevalent ICD-10 **3-char blocks** from `s_41270_0_*`. Exclude symptom/history/administrative blocks (Z*, R*); keep external causes (V01-Y98) only as a negative control. Exact list in `code_list.json`.
- **Treatment rival axis:** OPCS codes from `s_41200_0_*` (+ `s_41210_0_*`) summarised as (i) 3-char OPCS block flags and (ii) a procedure-count/intensity scalar. Pre-specify an OPCS-block x disease-block exclusion table marking procedures plausibly *caused by* each target disease D.
- **Primary estimand:** disease-preferential transfer gap `Delta_D = AUC(D vs unrelated disease set D')` on a **participant-level held-out half**, minus a co-occurrence-matched permutation null (permute D/D' labels within joint age-decile x sex x comorbidity-count strata). Report three variants:
  - `Delta_D^raw` — no adjustment;
  - `Delta_D^casecase` — diseased-only, age+sex+comorbidity adjusted;
  - `Delta_D^treat` (primary) — **stratification by UNRELATED procedures only** (OPCS blocks not plausibly caused by D, per the pre-specified exclusion table). A plain OPCS-**adjusted** estimate is secondary and explicitly **non-interpretable as a null** (collider risk: procedures are often consequences of the disease).
- **Baseline vs learned alternative (same estimand):** baseline = a single 1-D shared-axis score (leave-one-protein-out first PC of the shared protein covariance, fit on the discovery half); alternative = **elastic-net multi-protein** classifier fit on the discovery half. The learned alternative tests whether a sparse, cross-validated protein combination carries disease-preferential information the single 1-D shared axis loses (e.g. opposite-direction proteins that cancel), which is exactly the per-protein residual **[K2]/[K3] do not estimate.
- **Negative controls:** (i) participants with no prevalent ICD-10; (ii) external-cause block V01-Y98 as an unrelated reference block; (iii) OPCS-present-without-target-disease, to confirm the procedure axis is not simply re-encoding disease D.
- **Split/provenance:** fixed seed; `split_manifest.json` records half-assignment; the shared axis is fit **only** on the discovery half (leakage control).

## Falsification criteria (numerically explicit)
Permutation null band = 2.5th–97.5th percentile of `Delta_D` over **1,000** label permutations within strata. Minimum support per contrast: **>=200 cases for D and >=50 unrelated-procedure cases**; otherwise report underpowered.
- **Supportive (A):** for >=1 pre-specified disease D, `Delta_D^treat` remains **above the permutation band** (lower band bound > 0) after age+sex+comorbidity stratification on unrelated procedures.
- **Adverse (B):** `Delta_D^raw > 0` and `Delta_D^casecase > 0` but `Delta_D^treat` collapses into the null band → the apparent specificity was operative-treatment state, not disease biology.
- **Inconclusive:** sparse co-diagnosis/OPCS strata, <200 cases, unstable strata (fewer than ~30 unrelated-procedure cases in any matched stratum), or the read/probe limitation above → report as underpowered, not as support.

## What each outcome would / would not establish
- Supportive would establish that a disease-preferential protein signal can persist after removing shared systemic (case-case), demographic (age/sex) and operative-treatment contrasts *within this cross-sectional prevalent design* — evidence **for** a disease-preferential component. It would **not** establish causality, temporality or a mechanism, because there is no draw/diagnosis date anchor and OPCS under-ascertains outpatient treatment.
- Adverse would establish that at least one nominated "disease-specific" signal is better explained by operative-treatment/care state — a useful negative finding that reclassifies a candidate marker.
- Inconclusive/underpowered would leave the shared-axis-vs-specific question **unresolved**, not refuted. Because inpatient OPCS under-ascertainment biases `Delta_D^treat` toward the null, a null is inconclusive for outpatient-treated disease.
- Claims requiring **clinical adjudication** (ICD-10 block and OPCS exclusion lists) or **another study** (incidence/temporal ordering, genotype/pQTL Mendelian randomization, external cohort validation) are explicitly out of scope here.
