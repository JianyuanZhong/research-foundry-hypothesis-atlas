# Patientwise certified bridge for the HCC book-specific label-stable retention frontier

## Change note and remaining weakness

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the parent's documentary-only, fixed-capacity q_req/q_LS/q_M2/q_dec experiment, its exact HCC bindings, its 2015–2018 development / 2019 lock / separate 2020 and 2021 tests, its complete whole-accession corpus, its crossed pathology-book definition of q_LS, its coupled source/onset/reader/pathology states, fixed-K queues, anchored deletion, exact integer gates, and nonclinical interpretation.

The strongest remaining weakness is the bridge from an aggregate coverage threshold to a safe operational decision. The inherited Q is the fraction of a fixed acquisition frame whose eligible accessions are exposed under a hypothetical stored-content state. q_LS asks whether every protected patient remains in the incomplete-content queue at a given aggregate Q. That is a valid documentary retention estimand, but Q >= q does not imply that each protected patient's own eligible accessions are complete. A high aggregate Q can coexist with one protected patient having an unexposed accession. Further, HCC has no authored/final/release/retrieval/readability/view timestamps or report-version lineage. Calling q_LS an operational availability threshold would therefore be an unsupported claim.

The repair adds two explicitly subordinate outputs:

1. q_DOC: the minimum aggregate threshold at which every protected patient is individually complete for all eligible accessions in the same hypothetical stored-content state, is retained by S_G, and the certified queue still has all K positions. This is a computable patientwise necessary condition for a fixed-capacity queue policy; it is not a report-availability estimate.

2. operational_metadata_status: a fail-closed gate that is unavailable for this HCC snapshot unless external archive data provide immutable report/version lineage and as-of-t_dec lifecycle evidence. No q_DOC, even if favorable, can clear that gate.

This makes the clinical decision bridge concrete: a future fixed-capacity review service may only claim that it has a documentary prerequisite if q_DOC < 1, the protected sets are nonempty, and the certified patientwise queue has at least K positions. The present HCC result can never claim that reports were available to a clinician, that a review occurred, or that a patient benefited.

### Untested claims

The parent tests whether maximum stored content can outperform capacity-matched alternatives and whether, below full stored-content coverage, queue retention can be attributed to documentary labels stable across pathology books. This child tests the sharper operational prerequisite:

> Among patients who are documentary-M2-positive in every complete pathology book and selected by the current book's maximum-content fitted model, can the fixed-capacity incomplete-content queue retain every protected patient at a sub-full aggregate coverage threshold while also having complete stored content for each protected patient's own eligible accessions?

A supportive q_DOC < 1 is only a patientwise stored-content result. It does not establish that a report was finalized, retrievable, readable, or viewed by t_dec. An adverse q_DOC = 1 or undefined result identifies a concrete patientwise coverage failure or nonestimability; it does not establish biological MVI or patient harm.

## Preserved label-stable frontier

For fixed nonpathology state a, define the all-book label-stable universe:

U_LS(a)={i:Y_i(a,b')=1 for every pathology book b'}.

Retain the parent diagnostic:

P_common(a)=intersection over b' of {i:i in S_max(a,b') and Y_i(a,b')=1}.

For current pathology book b, retain:

P_LS(a,b)=S_max(a,b) intersection U_LS(a),

P_M2(a,b)={i:i in S_max(a,b) and Y_i(a,b)=1}.

Thus, for every a,b:

P_common(a) subseteq P_LS(a,b) subseteq P_M2(a,b) subseteq S_max(a,b).

Define J_LS(s,q)=1[P_LS(a,b) subseteq S_G(s,q)], and retain inherited J_M2 and J_dec. LS_PASS(q) is true only if inherited PASS(q) is true and J_LS(s,q)=1 for every eligible 2020/2021 state with Q(s,q)>=q. If maximum-content and feasibility gates pass and every required P_LS is nonempty:

q_LS=min{q in R:LS_PASS(q)=1}.

Retain q_req <= q_common <= q_LS <= q_M2 <= q_dec when the corresponding nonvacuity conditions hold. If P_common is empty, only q_common becomes undefined; it cannot veto nonempty P_LS.

No definition of q_req, q_LS, q_M2, or q_dec is changed by this child.

## New patientwise documentary certification frontier

The aggregate Q is not allowed to stand in for patient-level completeness. For every state s, threshold q, and patient i, define:

A_i(s,q)=1{every eligible nonblank accession of i has M=1 at q}.

The value is computed from the frozen whole-accession registry and the coupled onset state. A patient with no eligible accession is not assigned A_i=1; such a state is excluded by the inherited n_A feasibility rule. A patient with any blank-ID, contradictory, or incomplete accession is A_i=0 in that state. This is a source-content completeness indicator, not actual report availability.

Define the patientwise certification gate:

J_DOC(s,q)=1[P_LS(a,b) subseteq {i:A_i(s,q)=1}] AND J_LS(s,q).

For each nonvacuous eligible state, define DOC_PASS(q)=1 only when inherited PASS(q), every P_LS(a,b) is nonempty, J_LS(s,q)=1, J_DOC(s,q)=1, and K_cert(s,q)=K for every required 2020/2021 state at Q(s,q)>=q. Then:

q_DOC=min{q in R:DOC_PASS(q)=1}.

Because J_DOC includes J_LS, the verifier must establish q_LS <= q_DOC when both are defined. There is no asserted upper bound of q_DOC by q_M2 or q_dec: a patientwise content-completeness requirement can be stricter than queue retention.

At every q and immediately lower boundary, emit:

- Q,n_A,N,e,K, the number and proportion of exposed eligible accessions;
- |P_LS|, |P_LS intersection {A_i=1}|, and |P_LS minus {A_i=1}|;
- hashes of P_LS, the patientwise certified set {i:A_i=1}, and the protected uncovered set;
- each protected patient's accession count, exposed-accession count, and A_i bit;
- S_G rank/slot, EMPTY status, S_max membership, and cross-book Y/membership bit-vectors;
- q_LS, q_DOC, attained witnesses, score/model/queue hashes, and anchored-deletion results.

The primary q-frontiers remain exact finite-corpus quantities. q_DOC is a subordinate necessary-condition frontier and must never be presented as the proportion of reports available to clinicians.

### Fixed-capacity bridge output

At each threshold define the certified queue candidate:

S_cert(s,q)=S_G(s,q) intersection {i:A_i(s,q)=1}.

Do not silently backfill or enlarge capacity. Emit K_cert(s,q)=|S_cert(s,q)|, the certified reserve gap K-K_cert(s,q), and the ordered certified slots. If K_cert<K, label the policy state capacity_not_certifiable; an operational service may not interpret the unused positions as reviewed or as safe substitutions. If K_cert>=K, report only that a full-K source-content-certified queue can be formed in the synthetic state; the result remains subject to the operational metadata gate below.

For a future archive-linked replication, require an immutable report/version key linked to each whole accession (患者主索引,就诊号,检查号), plus predecessor/amendment/retraction lineage, authored/final/release/ingestion/retrieval/parse-readable/view timestamps with timezone provenance, and exact as-of-t_dec payload hashes. The archive must also provide complete nonresponder accounting and patient/year lower bounds proving that the unchanged N,n_A frame was not selectively lost. Those fields are not imported into the current HCC experiment and cannot redefine N,n_A,Q, eligible accessions, q_LS, or any label.

## Operational metadata gate

The catalog proves that HCC cannot clear an operational availability claim:

- examinations has temporal column only 开始时间; it has 检查, 检查所见, 检查诊断, 机器型号, and 检查号, but no report-version or lifecycle timestamps.
- clinical_documents has no temporal columns and is keyed only by (患者主索引,就诊号); it has narrative fields but no authored/final/release/view metadata.
- pathology has no temporal columns, no specimen ID, and no pathology accession key.
- encounters supplies 就诊时间, 入院时间, and 出院时间, but these are encounter clocks, not report lifecycle evidence.
- front_page, vitals, and transfers are identifier-only tables and cannot supply availability.
- No HCC table supplies timezone provenance, version lineage, parse/readability status, clinician viewing, action, or report retrieval telemetry.

Therefore set operational_metadata_status=unavailable for every current HCC state. This is a known data limitation, not a computational failure. A favorable q_DOC yields documentary_bridge_bounded_operationally_unverified; it cannot yield operationally_available.

## Falsification and one-hot interpretation

Use the following precedence-ordered primary interpretation:

1. computationally_inconclusive: source replay, header/schema/hash, raw ordinal, accession grouping, clock, coupled state, book completeness/global pairing, score/tie replay, fixed K, deletion, threshold, set/hash, boundary witness, or invariant check fails.
2. feasibility_inconclusive: N/K/event/n_A or fixed-frame requirements fail.
3. maximum_content_signal_adverse: maximum content fails any inherited comparator, random, year, reader, source, or deletion gate.
4. aggregate_capture_reserve_adverse: maximum content passes but q_req is undefined or equals 1.
5. label_stability_attribution_inconclusive: a required P_LS is empty; no vacuous inclusion is accepted.
6. book_specific_label_stable_retention_adverse: q_req<1 but q_LS is undefined or equals 1. Missing content can drop a label-stable positive selected by the current book's maximum-content model.
7. label_stable_retention_supportive_any_book_adverse: q_LS<1 but q_M2 is undefined or equals 1. Retention for label-stable positives is not enough to attribute every failure to stable labels.
8. dual_M2_retention_supportive_queue_fragile: q_M2<1 but q_dec is undefined or equals 1.
9. patientwise_documentary_certificate_adverse: after all computation and nonvacuity checks pass, q_DOC is undefined or equals 1. A q_DOC<1 result is required to provide both patientwise completeness and all K certified positions; aggregate retention alone does not provide that prerequisite.
10. patientwise_documentary_certificate_inconclusive: q_DOC cannot be defined because of a computation/nonvacuity issue specific to A_i, while the inherited frontiers remain valid. This is not biological adverse evidence.
11. full_documentary_queue_supportive_bridge_pending: q_dec<1, all inherited minimality/boundary witnesses pass, q_DOC<1, and K_cert=K at q_DOC in every required state. This supports only the frozen finite-corpus stored-content experiment.
12. Append operational_metadata_unavailable to every current HCC result. No current result may be labeled operationally available, clinician-action validated, biologically MVI-valid, beneficial, safe, fair, causal, or deployable.

A q_DOC=1 result is adverse to the patientwise documentary prerequisite but does not negate a favorable q_LS. Conversely, q_DOC<1 does not rescue any failed q_LS, q_M2, or q_dec. Exact equality to 1 fails. The 12/48/72-hour, rho=.05/.20, pooled-year, average-book, favorable-state, and favorable-deletion analyses cannot rescue a failed confirmatory result.

Supportive means only that the specified finite-corpus queue and patientwise stored-content conditions passed. Adverse means a prespecified documentary robustness condition was falsified. Inconclusive means provenance, feasibility, or nonvacuity prevented the test. Operational metadata unavailable means the necessary evidence for a real deadline was not collected; it is not evidence that reports were unavailable or that a clinician failed to act.
