## 1. The rival, restated so it can be wrong

- **H0 (this episode's target, generic care-escalation/monitoring artifact):** the decoupled dynamic footprint's
  held-out increment is a **non-specific** practice artifact - it reflects how intensely a patient was watched,
  ordered for and treated (a common cause shared with *any* downstream deterioration), so its increment is
  **~equal across readmission categories**, and adding explicit escalation-event features (block E) removes or
  shrinks it.
- **H1 (genuine organ-specific reserve loss):** genuine cardiorespiratory reserve loss should produce a
  **cardiorespiratory-specific** increment: the decoupled footprint's held-out 48 h increment for
  **cardiorespiratory** readmission exceeds that for **non-cardiorespiratory** readmission by a prespecified
  margin, and this specificity **survives** the escalation-event control block E.

These two make **opposite, computable predictions** on the same locked held-out split, using tables already
bound by the parent - so the contrast changes the conclusion either way.

## 2. THE ONE substantive addition: outcome-specificity negative control + escalation-event control block E

### 2.1 Outcome-specificity (negative-control) contrast

Primary estimands (held-out, first 48 h readmission `(t0, t0+48h]` per patient; same locked split):

- `Delta_C` = AUROC(M2) - AUROC(M1) for outcome `Y_cardio` (the 48 h readmission is cardiorespiratory, else 0).
- `Delta_N` = AUROC(M2) - AUROC(M1) for outcome `Y_noncardio` (the 48 h readmission is non-cardiorespiratory, else 0).
- **Specificity difference `S = Delta_C - Delta_N`** (primary specificity estimand; `S > 0` = cardio-specific).
- Escalation-controlled: `S_E = Delta_C|E - Delta_N|E`, i.e. recomputed with the M1/M2 ladder augmented by block E
  (`M1+E` vs `M2+E`), mirroring the parent's `Delta2` construction.

Primary evaluation is on the **shared held-out cohort** with two binary labelings (`Y_cardio`, `Y_noncardio`) so
the control group is identical across the two contrasts (a clean paired comparison). **Sensitivity:** restricted
sets - for each category, controls = held-out patients with no 48 h readmission plus that category's readmissions.
Paired inference: `S` is the difference of two paired AUROC increments; use a **1000-sample patient-level
bootstrap** for its 95 % percentile CI, and report `Delta_C`, `Delta_N`, `Delta_C|E`, `Delta_N|E`, AUPRC versions,
and DeLong tests for the correlated AUROCs.

### 2.2 Category assignment rule (prespecified; from readmission `hadm_id`)

For each 48 h readmission event, take the readmission stay's **`hadm_id`** (the `hadm_id` of the first ICU
`stay_id` with `icustays.intime` in `(t0, t0+48h]` for the same `subject_id`), then read
`hosp/diagnoses_icd` (`table-b12f3369d4b2601b`; columns `subject_id, hadm_id, seq_num, icd_code, icd_version`)
for that `hadm_id`. Join keys: `subject_id`,`hadm_id` (to `hosp/admissions` for the readmission `admittime`).

**CARDIO-RESPIRATORY block (any code matches):**
- ICD-10: circulatory `I00-I99`; respiratory `J00-J99`; sepsis `A40-A41`.
- ICD-9: circulatory `390-459`; respiratory `460-519`; septicemia `038.x` (septic shock `785.52`).
Dots are stripped; 3-character prefix matching for ICD-10 ranges and numeric-range matching for ICD-9.

**Rule (primary): ANY-code definition** - a readmission is CARDIO-RESP if at least one of its `diagnoses_icd`
codes falls in the blocks above; otherwise NON-CARDIO-RESP (the negative-control set).
**Tie-break (prespecified, for ambiguous/multi-category):** a readmission carrying BOTH a cardiorespiratory and a
non-cardiorespiratory code is assigned **CARDIO-RESP** (organ-specific failure present), never dropped.
**Sensitivity:** the same classification using `seq_num = 1` (principal diagnosis) only.
Both `S` (any-code, primary) and `S_seq1` (sensitivity) are reported; disagreement is reported explicitly.

## 3. Escalation-event control block E (explicit order/administration block)

Block E is built over `[t0-24h, t0)` (and per stay) from three tables, joined on `subject_id`,`hadm_id`,`stay_id`
where present. Columns verified this episode against the frozen catalog JSONs (Sec. 6):

  new-order rate by `order_type` (observed values include `Medications`,`General Care`,`Lab`,`IV therapy`,

  {`Vitals/Monitoring`,`Telemetry`,`Consults`,`Diet Order`,`IV access`,`Tubes/Drains`}, distinct

  enter_provider_id,charttime,medication,event_txt,scheduletime,storetime`): administration-event rate with
  `event_txt` in {`Administered`,`Started`,`Stopped`} (observed set also has `Confirmed`,`Flushed`,`Not Given`);
  per-hour counts of vasoactive/inotrope `medication` administrations; distinct `enter_provider_id`;

Block E is used **two ways**: (i) as a *control block* added to M1 and M2 to produce `S_E`; (ii) as a

(adverse to H1). No change is made to the parent's D1-D4 constructs or the M0/M1/M2 ladder.

## 5. Prespecified numeric bands for the specificity contrast (held-out)

**Supportive (H1 - cardiorespiratory-specific reserve loss):** ALL of
1. `S >= 0.015` with bootstrap 95 % CI **lower bound > 0.005** (cardio increment exceeds non-cardio by a
   margin beyond noise), AND
2. `Delta_C >= 0.02` with CI lower bound > 0.005 (the cardio arm is itself a real increment, reusing the parent's
   supportive level), AND
3. `S_E >= 0.010` with CI lower bound > 0 (specificity **survives** the escalation-event block E), AND
4. permuted-label `|S_perm| <= 0.005` with CI including 0.

**Adverse (H0 - generic monitoring/care-escalation artifact):** ANY of
1. `|S| <= 0.010` (increments ~equal across categories -> non-specific), OR the 95 % CI for `S` **includes 0**, OR
2. `S <= 0` with non-cardio exceeding cardio by `>= 0.015` (reverse specificity), OR
3. `S_E < 0.005` (block E absorbs the specificity -> escalation common-cause), OR
4. permuted-label `|S_perm| > 0.010` (specificity is an artifact of the pipeline).

**Inconclusive:** `0.010 < S < 0.015` with the CI spanning a boundary; OR bootstrap `S` CI width > 0.03; OR the
held-out **non-cardiorespiratory** readmission count < 200 (under-powered negative-control arm); OR the
any-code / `seq_num=1` classifications disagree enough to flip the band (`> 30 %` of readmissions
category-discordant) - then clinical adjudication is required. A code-based null does not by itself refute H1.

**Reading:** supportive requires the specificity to be present *and* robust to E *and* absent under permuted
labels. A non-specific (`|S|` small) or E-collapsing result is **ADVERSE** to H1 and is valuable evidence that the
footprint is (at least partly) a practice/monitoring artifact - exactly the parent's declared unresolved defect.

## 8. Falsifiers and honest limits (what an automatic check cannot settle)

- **Code-based outcome specificity is imperfect:** the readmission category comes from hospital discharge
  *codes* on the readmission `hadm_id`, not adjudicated ICU readmission cause; coding != physiology. Reported as
  a named limitation, mitigated (not removed) by the any-code + `seq_num=1` dual rule and the discordance check.
- **A non-specific increment is ADVERSE to H1 and is reported as such** - we do not label nulls "inconclusive"
  to rescue the claim; a code-based null is not a decisive refutation (Sec. 5).
- **Block E captures ordered/administrated care but not the reason order*** - residual confounding by indication
  and unmodeled escalation remain; `S_E` is a control, not identification.
- `"Unplanned"` readmission and post-discharge death provenance, and any causal/mechanistic reading, require
  **clinical adjudication / notes** - outside this design (association / incremental prognostic information only).
- All four key works are **abstract_only**; full texts were not read. Cohort counts are reused from the parent
  gate; category counts and per-stay coverage are **unverified** here and are first-class readiness checks.
- **Single most consequential remaining objection after this child:** the specificity contrast is a
  *negative-control* design - a cardiorespiratory-specific increment is consistent with organ-specific reserve
  loss *or* with organ-specific measurement/ordering habits (e.g. telemetry and ABGs are ordered preferentially in
  cardiac patients). Block E reduces, but does not eliminate, that channel; definitive separation needs chart
  review or a design that instrumentally varies monitoring cadence.
