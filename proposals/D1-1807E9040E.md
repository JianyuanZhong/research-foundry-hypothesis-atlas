# HCC day-42 trajectory: corroboration-timing falsifier

Status: concise design-only child of `[prior hypothesis]`; no fitted
result, event count, prevalence, coefficient, loss, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

## One change and the unresolved boundary

The parent already tests whether day-42 laboratory path information predicts a later
operational TACE episode after SW42 transport, and whether that gain is reproduced by
post-landmark documentation (`F_obs`) or pre-landmark planned care (`F_plan`). Those
controls are necessary but not sufficient: they do not test whether O1's same-visit
corroborator was available before the procedure or was entered after it. Thus an O1 gain
could still be a coherent procedure plus post hoc local documentation.

Make exactly one falsifier-level change: prespecify a corroboration-timing boundary for
the unchanged O1 event.

- `O1_pre`: the unchanged O1 procedure event with at least one qualifying same-visit
  examination/order corroborator in `[t-42,t-1]).
- `O1_postonly`: the unchanged O1 procedure event with at least one qualifying
  same-visit corroborator in `[t,t+7]), and no qualifying pre-procedure corroborator
  observed in `[t-42,t-1]). “Postonly” means no qualifying row in the observed source;
  it is not proof that care or intent was absent outside this EHR.
- Retain O1 as the primary endpoint, including events satisfying either timing class;
  retain O0, conditional O2, F_obs, F_plan, all parent windows, and all parent
  negative-control/permutation arms unchanged.

The new falsifier is the output-linked comparison of the O1 trajectory increment for
`O1_pre` versus `O1_postonly`, with the same endpoint event time (t), predictors,
weights, split and uncertainty as O1. The clinically useful decision is whether to pursue
a linked intent/imaging adjudication study or redirect toward documentation/scheduling
capture; this is not a test of treatment benefit.

## Evidence and bounded claim

[K1] supports the importance of repeat-TACE decisions and shows treatment-adjacent
prediction can be evaluated across internal and external cohorts, but does not validate
this site's codes or laboratory trajectory. [K2] defines HCC response as
imaging/necrosis-aware mRECIST/RECICL evidence, not a procedure or assay surrogate.
[K3] demonstrates that recorded imaging outcomes can remain ascertainment-dependent under
missing outcomes, exposure misclassification and confounding. None establishes local O1
validity.

The strongest supported claim is therefore only that a local EHR can contain a recorded
operational repeat-procedure signal worth testing. The unresolved claim is that the
day-42 path adds information about a coherent episode rather than about documentation
generated after that episode. A positive pre-procedure timing result weakens the
post-hoc explanation; it does not establish intent, response, mechanism, benefit,
survival or causality.

## Predictors, estimand, split and uncertainty

Retain exactly these eight assay labels in `labs.检验`: 甲胎蛋白, 白蛋白, 前白蛋白,
总胆红素, 直接胆红素, 丙氨酸氨基转移酶, 血小板, 血小板计数. Use latest valid
nonduplicate `检验时间` in [t0-365,t0) for B0 and rows in (t0,t0+42] for S42;
preserve `定性结果, 定量结果, 标本类型`, inequalities/censoring and missingness,
without inventing values or pooling units.

Retain the strict parent nesting:
`A0 -> A_snap -> A_term -> A_path`, where A0 is M0+B0+P_obs+P_care,
A_snap adds S42, A_term adds terminal B0-to-S42 changes, and A_path adds ordered
transitions over (0,14], (14,28], (28,42]. Define
`D_path,j = L(A_term,j)-L(A_path,j)`; positive means lower held-out loss after
adding path information. The transported estimand remains
`Psi^TR_{j,q}=E_I[L_j(A_previous,q)-L_j(A_q)]), with fold-frozen SW42 and
follow-up observation/censoring weights applied to [43,181) risk sets. Report companion
ordinary complete-R42 `Psi^LM`; do not average disagreement.

Use earliest 80% of index dates for development and latest 20% untouched test, all rows
of a patient together, deterministic ties, and five patient-grouped development folds.
Freeze parsing, dictionaries, imputation, scaling, SW42, censoring weights and
hyperparameters within development; score test once. Fit the same elastic-net
pooled-logistic discrete-time hazards to O0, O1, enabled O2, F_obs, F_plan, O1_pre and
O1_postonly under every nested block. Use patient-level paired bootstrap, calibration,
Brier/integrated Brier, log loss, discrimination, risk-set/event counts, R42/R28
coverage, clipping and ESS. Use one max-|t| simultaneous family expanded only to
include the new two timing falsifiers and their paired O1 contrasts. The inherited
support gate applies to every claimed endpoint: at least 50 overall events, at least
15 development and 15 test events, weighted ESS >=100, adequate overlap and no
frozen weight-tail failure; otherwise label that timing result unavailable/inconclusive.

## Falsification and next decision

- Supportive timing evidence: O1 has a positive simultaneous lower bound for D_path
  under both Psi^LM and Psi^TR; O1_pre also has a positive lower bound; O1_postonly
  is not O1-sized positive; F_obs/F_plan remain zero-compatible and below O1; and
  parent R28, late-record deletion, early-alias, pre-procedure and permutation controls
  do not erase the pattern. This supports only a pre-procedure-correlated recorded
  trajectory for an operational episode. It justifies a prospective/linked study with
  scheduled labs, procedure intent/scheduling and expert mRECIST imaging adjudication.
- Adverse timing evidence: O1 is positive but O1_pre is null/erased while O1_postonly
  is positive and O1-sized, or F_obs/F_plan is O1-sized positive. This supports
  post-hoc documentation/workflow as the leading explanation and redirects the next
  study to scheduling, provenance and outside-care capture; it does not show the assays
  lack biologic prognostic value.
- Inconclusive: either timing stratum fails event/overlap/ESS or censoring support,
  intervals are wide, or only one stratum is estimable. Do not recode missing
  corroboration as a negative, alter windows, or rescue the claim with O0/O2/GRU.

Even supportive timing cannot identify treatment intent or response because the source
lacks validated scheduling/intent fields, radiology findings/adjudication, assay
units/reference ranges, medication indications, reliable death and outside-care capture.
A clinical conclusion requires chart/image review and another independent or prospective
study.

## Alternative, deliverable and deferrals

The selected confirmatory method remains the patient-held-out elastic-net hazard because
its nested increments, SW42 weighting and timing falsifier are auditable. The matched
scientific alternative remains a compact time-aware GRU over exactly the same allowed
event stream (t0-365 through t0+42), eight assay labels/content, relative time and time
since prior record, with O0/O1/O2/F_obs/F_plan/O1_pre/O1_postonly heads and matched
A_snap/A_term/A_path ablations. Three development seeds, untouched patient test,
identical SW42/metrics/bootstrap and no endpoint rows or narrative findings are required.
It could reveal nonlinear ordered timing/recovery patterns lost by elastic-net summaries;
a GRU-only gain cannot override an adverse timing falsifier. Primary elastic-net work is
approximately 1-4 hours on 4 CPUs/16 GiB (planning estimate). GRU sensitivity is
approximately 2-6 hours on 4 CPUs/16 GiB and one allocated A100, using `cuda:0`;
unverified and deferred if imports/support fail. Ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not a
hardware conclusion.

New completion outputs are the parent outputs plus
`o1_corroboration_timing_audit.csv`, `o1_timing_heldout_contrasts.csv` and
`trajectory_decision_gate.json`, linking each timing conclusion to predictions,
losses, bootstrap intervals, support/ESS and the max-|t| family. No fitted output is
present here.

Deferred larger transformers, narrative NLP, latent causal/intent models and mechanistic
liver-response models remain deferred because the required validated intent/scheduling,
imaging, assay-unit, outside-care and adjudication evidence is absent. Revisit only when
those dependencies or an independent adjudicated response set is linked.
