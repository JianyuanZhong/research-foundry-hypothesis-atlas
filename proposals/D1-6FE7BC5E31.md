# Can hepatic paired-change outcomes precede major post-TACE2 care events?

## Substantive advance over the assigned parents

This child preserves the assessed parents' frozen common-TACE2 cohort, the days 1–14 recorded-pathway definition, deterministic ambiguity exclusions, day-14 institutional-observation landmark, 2018–2024 era, exact albumin and total-bilirubin selectors, pre-index adjustment set, overlap-weighted fixed-baseline estimand, observation-process analysis, and noncausal interpretation limits. It adds a value-blinded, complete-source event-order feasibility audit and uses its result to put an explicit viability bound on the intended day-30–90 paired-change endpoint.

The audit shows that a joint day-60-nearest pair can rarely be treated as a pre-major-event biochemical outcome in the systemic-record arm: only 31/99 pairs precede every later day-15–90 systemic or strict-TACE event, and only 22/99 are more than seven calendar days from every strict TACE. The intersection is 9/99. In the TACE2-only arm the corresponding counts are 220/387, 125/387, and 111/387. Every selected joint follow-up is admission-linked under the declared timestamp proxy. Thus the paired-change analysis remains computable as a fixed-baseline association with biochemical state observed under evolving recorded care, but a stable, event-independent hepatic-reserve interpretation is not feasible from this snapshot. The new proposal makes that conclusion an explicit design gate rather than an after-the-fact caveat.

## Clinical question and importance

Among adults with recorded hepatocellular carcinoma who reach the same strictly defined second qualifying TACE, is a systemic HCC-medication record during TACE2 calendar days 1–14 associated with a different later albumin and total-bilirubin change than ambiguity-excluded TACE2-only recorded care, and does any association survive explicit accounting for the events and care settings in which the laboratory values were observed?

The question is clinically consequential because albumin and bilirubin are often used in decisions about whether further locoregional treatment is tolerable and whether systemic treatment should be continued or considered. A laboratory value obtained during a subsequent admission, near repeat TACE, or after a later systemic record may reflect acute care, treatment selection, surveillance intensity, or evolving disease management rather than a stable hepatic state. Establishing that the intended outcome is or is not temporally prior to those events determines whether this EHR can support a useful biochemical comparison and prevents an apparently favorable association from being mislabeled as preservation attributable to the initial pathway.

## Evidence-supported claim versus untested hypothesis

### Strongest claim supported before outcome modeling

A complete-source scan of the frozen HCC snapshot establishes feasibility and outcome-context anchoring, not benefit, harm, administration, intent, causation, imaging response, progression, survival, or toxicity attribution.

The reconstruction contains 2,992 patients before medication classification, 319 systemic-record patients, and 1,491 ambiguity-excluded TACE2-only patients. Deterministic exact-assay selection yields joint baseline/follow-up pairs for 99/319 systemic-record patients and 387/1,491 comparator patients. Among these paired patients:

- Systemic-record arm: 31/99 follow-up pairs precede every later day-15–90 non-bevacizumab systemic record and strict TACE; 68/99 have a later qualifying event by at least one selected assay follow-up day; 22/99 are more than seven calendar days from every strict TACE; 9/99 satisfy both no-event-before-selection and procedure-distance criteria.
- TACE2-only arm: 220/387 precede every such later event; 167/387 have an event by selection; 125/387 are more than seven days from every strict TACE; 111/387 satisfy both criteria.
- All 99/99 and 387/387 joint pairs are admission-linked under the prespecified matching and admission-through-discharge-plus-one-day timing proxy. This is not adjudication of acute illness or the reason for testing.
- At least one selected follow-up assay is within plus or minus seven calendar days of a strict TACE for 77/99 systemic and 262/387 comparator paired patients.
- Selected event days in the full day-15–90 audit have median relative day 56 in both arms (388 event records in systemic and 718 in comparator; records are descriptive and may arise from more than one patient/event).

These findings support only that the endpoint is predominantly observed in an admission/procedure context and often after additional recorded care. They do not show that the initial recorded pathway caused a biochemical difference.

### Falsifiable hypothesis to test

Conditional on the day-14 observation population, measured pre-TACE2 covariates, adequate overlap, and observed exact assays, the systemic-record pathway will be associated with less albumin decline or a greater albumin increase from baseline to the deterministic target-nearest day-60 follow-up, without a larger total-bilirubin increase, than the TACE2-only recorded pathway.

This hypothesis is about the fixed days 1–14 recorded pathway and the later biochemical state observed under all subsequent recorded care. It is not a hypothesis about medication administration, adherence, delivered dose, treatment intent, an isolated early-treatment effect, a sustained strategy, toxicity, imaging response, progression, survival, or causal comparative effectiveness. A favorable result is clinically interpretable only as a context-bounded recorded-pathway association and only if the prespecified context, observation, overlap, negative-control, timing, and ontology checks do not undermine that interpretation.

## Frozen cohort and recorded pathways

### Common TACE2 time zero

Parse `procedures.开始时间`. A strict TACE row contains case-insensitive literal `TACE` or literal `化疗栓塞` and has a valid start time. Normalize to each patient's calendar day, collapse duplicate strict rows on a patient-day, sort episodes, and select the earliest adjacent pair whose gap is 14–180 calendar days. The second episode is TACE2; no later pair may replace it.

The complete-source audit reproduced 338,040 procedure rows, 72,273 valid-time strict TACE rows, 38,742 strict patient-day episodes, and 6,589 earliest adjacent qualifying pairs. After encounter-timed HCC qualification and adult eligibility, 6,586 pairs remained. The distinction between textual matches with invalid times and valid-time rows must be retained; only valid-time rows can define the cohort.

### HCC, adult, observation, and era restrictions

Require a diagnosis name containing literal `肝细胞癌` at an encounter-derived time on or before TACE2. Use the latest encounter at or before TACE2 for age, sex, and department; require numeric age at least 18. Define last institutional observation as the patient-level maximum of nonmissing encounter visit, admission, and discharge timestamps. Require that this maximum is at least TACE2 day 14 and restrict TACE2 year to 2018–2024. This yields 2,992 patients before medication classification. The day-14 requirement is institutional observability, not survival.

### Frozen days 1–14 pathway

Exclude any valid-time, ontology-matched nonplacebo medication record on or before the TACE2 calendar day, including bevacizumab. In days 1–14, classify a patient as systemic-record if there is at least one non-bevacizumab, nonplacebo ontology record. Bevacizumab qualifies only when a non-bevacizumab ontology record occurs for the same patient, same `就诊号`, and same calendar day. Bevacizumab-only grace-period records are ambiguous. For an otherwise unexposed patient, valid-time procedure text containing `分子靶向治疗`, `抗肿瘤免疫治疗`, `免疫治疗`, or `靶向治疗` is generic ambiguity. ## Exact assay outcome and timing rules

Use only exact `检验` labels `白蛋白` and `总胆红素`. Parse `定量结果` only when the entire trimmed string, after removing commas, is an optional leading `<`, `>`, `≤`, or `≥` followed by a signed decimal or scientific-notation number. Reject any other prefix or suffix; never extract a numeric substring from mixed text. Parse `检验时间` and calculate relative calendar days from TACE2.

For each assay separately:

- Baseline: latest exact assay on days −30 through −1; ties favor the latest timestamp on the latest calendar day.
- Follow-up: observation on days 30–90 minimizing `(absolute(day−60), day, negative timestamp)`, so equal-distance ties favor the earlier day and then the latest timestamp on that day.
- Change: selected follow-up minus selected baseline.

A joint pair requires valid baseline and follow-up for both assays. Albumin and bilirubin remain separate outcomes; no composite, ALBI score, external cut point, or unit conversion is permitted. The audit reproduced 99/319 systemic and 387/1,491 TACE2-only joint pairs.

## Primary analysis and observation process

For assay `a`, estimate the fixed-baseline association

`E_w[Δa | systemic days 1–14, paired observed] − E_w[Δa | TACE2-only days 1–14, paired observed]`.

Use overlap weights from a prespecified propensity model containing only pre-TACE2 variables: age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, year and month, department, prior 30/90/180-day encounter counts, prior 90/180-day procedure counts, prior resection/ablation/transplant text flags, encounter-timed diagnosis proxies for cirrhosis, portal hypertension, ascites, encephalopathy, variceal bleeding, portal-vein invasion and metastasis, and closest pre-TACE2 exact albumin, bilirubin, INR, platelet count, creatinine, and sodium with missingness indicators. AFP may enter only after exact-label enumeration is frozen. No days 1–14 detail, later event, selected assay context, follow-up value, or post-baseline variable may enter the primary propensity model.

Fit separate weighted change regressions for albumin and bilirubin, adjusting for arm, baseline assay, and prespecified core covariates whose post-weighting absolute standardized mean difference remains above 0.10. Report raw and weighted denominators, patient-level robust 95% intervals, overlap support, all covariate balance, weight distributions, and arm-specific effective sample size. Preserve raw-scale bilirubin and locked quantile/winsorized sensitivities; do not use external units or clinical thresholds.

Separately model baseline observed, follow-up observed, and paired observed status in all 1,810 frozen-arm patients using arm and pre-TACE2 predictors. Report observed fractions, adjusted arm contrasts, five-fold cross-validated AUC, calibration, predicted probability distributions, and missingness patterns. Multiply overlap weights by inverse predicted paired-observation probability for an observation-weighted sensitivity, reporting untruncated and 1st/99th-percentile-truncated versions, combined weights, and ESS. This addresses measured observation predictors only; it cannot remove post-baseline selection caused by the clinical state prompting testing.

## Falsification and interpretation criteria

A computationally supportive result requires successful reconstruction, overlap and balance, stable observation modeling, coherent albumin and bilirubin directions, and compatible adequately supported context sensitivities. It supports only a favorable association between the initial recorded pathway and later observed biochemical state under evolving institutional care. Because the feasibility audit already activates the outcome-context anchoring gate, it cannot support stable hepatic-reserve preservation independent of procedures or admissions.

An adverse result is a larger albumin decline and/or bilirubin increase in the systemic-record arm after the same checks. It argues against assuming a favorable association but does not establish medication-induced hepatotoxicity.

A precise null is a clinically reviewed narrow interval around no detectable contrast on the observed scale, with coherent assays and passed computational gates. It is not equivalence, equal safety, or absence of individual harm.

A context-falsified result is an apparent primary contrast that disappears or reverses in adequately supported no-event or procedure-distant subsets, is concentrated near repeat TACE, or tracks differential later pathway evolution. Report context dependence, not a causal attribution to the conditioned event.

A methodologically falsified result is a material pre-exposure negative-control contrast capable of explaining the post-index result, dependence on the day-0 boundary, reversal under locked timing/ontology rules, lack of overlap, or unresolved cohort mismatch. State the failed premise rather than selecting a favorable branch.

An inconclusive result includes low ESS, wide intervals, unstable observation weights, calendar nonpositivity, discordant assays, sparse selected subsets, universal admission anchoring, or inability to distinguish procedure-associated testing from a stable state. The systemic event-free intersection of 9 patients is expected to be descriptive only absent an explicitly met support threshold.

The pre-exposure negative control compares the same assay from the value closest to day −45 in days −60 to −31 with the frozen baseline in days −30 to −1 under the same overlap model. A material contrast that can explain the post-index estimate falsifies a newly emergent pathway interpretation. A null negative control does not establish exchangeability.

## What remains unavailable and what requires another study

Medication source rows do not establish administration, adherence, dose delivered, line of therapy, duration, indication, or intent. Later systemic records may be continuation, switching, or crossover. Later TACE may be planned, response-guided, progression-guided, toxicity-related, or driven by clinician preference. Encounter linkage and procedure proximity are timing proxies, not clinical adjudication. Diagnoses use encounter-derived coding dates. The snapshot lacks laboratory units, validated assay harmonization, complete Child–Pugh components, performance status, treatment rationale, imaging response/progression, complete outside-care capture, reliable vital status, and adjudicated toxicity.

Therefore do not calculate ALBI, apply external cut points, pool differently named assays, infer stable outpatient reserve, attribute toxicity, claim imaging response, progression, survival, treatment delivery, or causal benefit. A causal longitudinal comparison would require observed administration, dynamic severity and decision covariates, explicit treatment strategies, informative-censoring assumptions and methods, and preferably external/prospective validation. Stable hepatic reserve would require appropriately timed outpatient measurements or adjudicated clinical context. Repeat-TACE response or progression would require temporally adjudicated imaging. Survival would require complete vital-status linkage. The automatic verifier can check joins, source counts, time windows, ontology, arm labels, assay selection, context flags, weights, balance, ESS, intervals, and branch consistency; it cannot determine test indication, acute illness, administration, causal effect, clinical importance on unknown scales, imaging response, progression, or survival.

## What changed and remaining uncertainty

Relative to the paired-change parent, this child adds a prespecified event-order audit that directly tests whether the follow-up can be selected before major later recorded care. Relative to the context-decomposition parent, it quantifies the stricter intersection of event-free timing and procedure distance and uses the resulting sparse support to constrain interpretation. No cohort rule, time zero, days 1–14 pathway, outcome selector, or primary estimand was changed after seeing effect estimates. The primary remains the full fixed-baseline observed-pair association; restrictions are explicitly secondary selected-pathway sensitivities.

The substantive clinical advance is an honest determination of what the available EHR can and cannot answer: it can test a carefully weighted association with later recorded biochemical state, while it cannot identify a procedure-independent stable-reserve effect or causal treatment benefit. The central remaining uncertainty is whether the recorded laboratory context represents acute illness, routine inpatient surveillance, procedure response, or another clinical rationale; that requires adjudicated chart review, administration data, imaging, units, outside-care linkage, and vital status beyond this snapshot.
