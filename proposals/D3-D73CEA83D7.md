# Repaired provisional hypothesis: donor-aware macrophage-to-FLS communication in E-MTAB-8873

## Proposed relationship

In the frozen E-MTAB-8873 targeted expression experiment, co-culture of RA type B synovial cells (FLS) with MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages may induce a reproducible, condition-specific transcriptional response in FLS that is distinguishable from both FLS-only and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage co-culture after accounting for contributing donors and repeated context measurements.

This is a hypothesis, not a computed result. The unresolved claim is whether the paired condition design contains a stromal–myeloid communication signal. The available evidence motivates the question but does not establish an expression response, direct ligand-receptor action, causality, or clinical benefit.

Primary contrasts, restricted to treatment=none:
1. MerTK/CD206-positive macrophage co-culture versus not co-cultured;
2. MerTK/CD206-positive macrophage co-culture versus MerTK/CD206-negative co-culture.

Use a donor-aware paired/stratified contrast or contributing-donor random-effects model in log-expression space, with the contributing donor/context as the uncertainty unit. Use condition/donor-preserving permutation inference. Report feature effects, uncertainty, permutation results, donor counts, and negative-control behavior. Pre-LPS and inhibitor records are mechanistic context only, not independent causal replicates. Prespecify direction, effect-size, and donor-level error thresholds before inspecting results.

Supportive results require a reproducible condition-specific signature with donor-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable signal in the negative-control contrast. Adverse results include no separation, reversal, or failure under donor/condition-preserving permutations. Inconclusive results include unresolved donor mapping, insufficient independent donor contexts, low detectability, or uncertainty too large to distinguish communication from composition or technical artifacts.

Substantive alternative: a hierarchical/regularized model across the 445 measured features, borrowing information across features to reveal coordinated pathway-level structure that single-feature contrasts lose. Expression association cannot establish direct target action, ligand-receptor causality, clinical transition, or treatment benefit; those require perturbation, independent experimental evidence, or another study.
