# Temporal Feedback Structure of Macrophage-FLS Inflammatory Signaling in RA Synovial Tissue

## Scientific Opening

In rheumatoid arthritis (RA), synovial tissue exhibits a self-reinforcing inflammatory loop between macrophages and fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS) [K1]. The crosstalk is well documented: FLS secrete IL-34, CCL2, and CXCL12 that recruit and polarize macrophages toward the M1 phenotype, while M1 macrophage-derived TNF-α, IL-6, and IL-1β further activate FLS [K1]. However, the **temporal ordering** of this feedback is unresolved. Three non-equivalent explanations are compatible with the current evidence:

1. **Unidirectional macrophage-driven model**: Macrophage activation precedes and drives the FLS inflammatory response. FLS are passive recipients of macrophage-derived signals.
2. **Bidirectional feedback loop**: Macrophage and FLS activation are mutually reinforcing with no clear temporal precedence. Both cell types can initiate the loop.
3. **Composition-driven model**: The apparent "feedback" is an artifact of changes in cell composition (e.g., increased macrophage infiltration) rather than a genuine dynamic interaction between cell types.

This distinction is clinically consequential: if the loop is unidirectional, targeting macrophage activation may break the cycle; if bidirectional, both cell types must be targeted simultaneously; if composition-driven, the apparent feedback may not be a genuine mechanism.

## Hypothesis

**Hypothesis**: In RA synovial tissue, macrophage activation precedes and drives the FLS inflammatory response (unidirectional macrophage-driven model), rather than a bidirectional feedback loop or composition-driven artifact.

## Evidence Opening

The available datasets provide single-cell resolution of FLS and macrophages in RA synovial tissue with condition-specific contrasts:

- **E-MTAB-8873**: Single-cell RNA-seq of RA synovial tissue with Mpos (macrophage-positive) and Mneg (macrophage-negative) contrasts, providing direct evidence of macrophage-FLS crosstalk.
- **GSE95588**: Coculture data with macrophage and FLS, providing paired conditions that can test the temporal ordering hypothesis.
- **E-MTAB-8316**: Single-cell RNA-seq of OA-like tissue with inhibitor conditions, providing a negative control for the inflammatory crosstalk.
- **E-MTAB-8322**: Single-cell RNA-seq of RA synovial tissue, providing an independent cohort for validation.

## Competing Explanations

1. **Unidirectional macrophage-driven model**: Macrophage activation precedes and drives the FLS inflammatory response. Observable prediction: a significant positive interaction coefficient (β_3) for macrophage state × FLS inflammatory gene expression in the donor-aware mixed model.
2. **Bidirectional feedback loop**: Macrophage and FLS activation are mutually reinforcing with no clear temporal precedence. Observable prediction: a non-significant or negative interaction coefficient, with both macrophage and FLS inflammatory gene expression showing similar temporal patterns.
3. **Composition-driven model**: The apparent feedback is an artifact of changes in cell composition. Observable prediction: the interaction coefficient is driven by changes in macrophage proportion rather than within-cell expression changes.
4. **Donor-specific model**: The observed patterns are driven by donor-specific baseline states. Observable prediction: the interaction coefficient is not consistent across donors.

## Falsifiable Test

A donor-aware mixed model on Mpos vs Mneg contrasts in E-MTAB-8873 and GSE95588, with macrophage and FLS inflammatory gene expression as outcomes. The model includes:
- Fixed effects: macrophage state (Mpos vs Mneg), FLS inflammatory gene expression, and their interaction.
- Random effects: donor (to account for donor-specific baseline states).

**Estimand**: The interaction coefficient (β_3) for macrophage state × FLS inflammatory gene expression.

**Supportive result**: A significant positive β_3 (p < 0.05, 95% CI excluding zero) would support the unidirectional macrophage-driven model.
**Adverse result**: A non-significant or negative β_3 would support the bidirectional or composition-driven models.
**Inconclusive result**: A significant β_3 with a wide confidence interval or inconsistent direction across donors would be inconclusive.

## Method Alternatives

1. **Donor-aware mixed model** (primary): Accounts for donor-specific baseline states and provides a clear estimand (β_3 interaction coefficient).
2. **XGBoost**: A machine learning alternative that can capture non-linear interactions but lacks a clear estimand and is less interpretable.
3. **ODE model**: A mechanistic alternative that can model the temporal dynamics of macrophage-FLS crosstalk but requires additional assumptions about the functional form of the interaction.
