## 0. What changed vs the parent (the scientific defect repaired)

The parent's own admitted defect: RASS depth is a plausible **marker of the sedation
INDICATION** (agitation/delirium, ARDS, neuro/ICP) and of underlying severity, so its
competing-risks dose-response HR for "delayed liberation" can be **confounding by indication /
reverse causation** rather than a treatment effect. The parent already has: landmark `t*`,
[t*, t*+12 h] predictor window, continuous deep-sedation fraction (RASS in [-5,-3]) dose
gradient, cause-specific HR for liberation + Fine-Gray 7-day CIF difference with death as
competing event, a locked earliest-70 %/latest-30 % split by `icustays.intime`, and the
M0->M1->M2 ladder. **This child adds only the smallest tests that could flip the causal reading,
and keeps the estimand, gradient and ladder unchanged:**

1. **(i) Negative-control outcome (NCO):** a post-`t*` outcome plausibly unaffected by sedation
   depth but affected by severity — **new renal-replacement therapy (RRT) initiation after the
   landmark**, plus (secondary) **new/escalated vasopressor** requirement. A dose-response
   signal on the NCO indicates residual confounding by severity.
2. **(ii) Indication-stratified / conditioned comparison:** derive sedation-indication strata from
   `hosp/diagnoses_icd` (+`hosp/d_icd_diagnoses`), `hosp/prescriptions`, and `icu/chartevents`
   (delirium/agitation vs ARDS/hypoxemia vs neuro/ICP vs none) and test whether the dose-response
   persists WITHIN strata.
3. **(iii) Partial-identification bound:** a prespecified sensitivity bound (E-value style) for
   unmeasured-indication bias at a stated bias magnitude.

Every other element (landmark, window, split, ladders, estimand) is inherited unchanged.

## 1. Unresolved question and rivals

After an invasively ventilated adult first meets stabilization (`t*`), does **continued deep
sedation causally delay successful liberation**, or is the observed dose-response explained by the
**sedation indication / underlying severity**?

- **Leading claim (C):** sustained deep sedation in [t*, t*+12 h] causally prolongs time to
  successful extubation; effect persists within indication strata and is null on the NCO.
- **Strongest rival (R-indication):** deep sedation co-encodes the indication (agitation/delirium,
  ARDS, neuro/ICP) and a latent severity/arousal axis; the "dose" partly IS the state it is
  credited with causing. Predicts: effect attenuates or disappears within strata, and the NCO
  shows a parallel spurious severity signal.
- **Second rival (R-death):** a severity-driven mortality/exposure difference is misread as
  delayed liberation; separated by the competing-risks decomposition (inherited).
- **Measurement rival (R2):** apparent depth reflects RASS charting practice, not delivered
  sedation; bounded by the documentation-sparsity inconclusive rule.

**Separating observation.** Under C the cause-specific HR for liberation is <1 and the 7-day
liberation CIF difference negative, survives M0–M2, is monotone in the deep-sedation fraction,
**survives within each indication stratum**, and the NCO (new RRT) is **null**. Under
R-indication the within-stratum effect collapses toward the null and/or the NCO reproduces a
"dose-response", and the bias bound is small enough to explain the remaining signal.

## 2. Clinical importance

Time to liberation drives ICU LOS, VAP and delirium. If depth is causal, a light-sedation target
is a real lever; if depth is chiefly a severity/indication marker, depth-only protocols mislead and
effort should target the indication. The NCO + stratification + bound turn an unfalsifiable
"association" into a test whose result changes the decision.

## 4. Design (inherited estimand; new tests)

- **Population:** adults (>=18) with an ICU stay containing invasive ventilation (**225792**)
  crossing a stabilization window; not restricted to sepsis. Exclusions inherited (early
  liberation/death before `t*`, paediatric, non-ICU).
- **Predictor window:** [t*, t*+12 h]; primary exposure = continuous **fraction of RASS in [-5,-3]**;
  binary sustained-deep (>=50 %) retained for the B0 baseline.
- **Follow-up:** t* to 7 d; first event = liberation / death (competing) / censored.
- **Ladder:** M0 = age, sex, admission type, pre-t* LOS, MAP min, lactate, vasopressor use, PEEP,
  PaO2/FiO2; M1 = M0 + GCS at t*; M2 = M1 + sedative drug class.
- **Split:** earliest 70 % / latest 30 % by `icustays.intime`, locked before fitting.
- **NEW (i) NCO:** Cox/Fine-Gray for **new RRT after t*** among stays RRT-free at t*, using the
  same exposure and ladders. Same for vasopressor-escalation NCO. An exposure "effect" on the NCO
  is a residual-confounding flag (the NCO should be null under C).
- **NEW (ii) Indication stratification:** assign each stay one primary indication stratum from the
  ICD/prescription/chart proxies above; fit the cause-specific HR / CIF within strata and add an
  indication-stratum-by-exposure interaction. Report whether the dose-response survives.
- **NEW (iii) Partial-identification bound:** prespecified E-value-style bound on the HR for a
  stated unmeasured-indication bias magnitude (e.g. RR_UY = RR_AU = 1.3/1.5/2.0), reported as the
  bias magnitude that would move the HR to the null.

## 5. Estimand, baseline vs alternatives

- **Primary estimand (inherited CR):** cause-specific HR for liberation (deep vs light, M0–M2) +
  Fine-Gray 7-day cumulative-incidence difference (liberation), death competing; death CIF reported
  separately, never collapsed.
- **Baseline B0 (interpretable):** Cox/logistic on the binary sustained-deep exposure with M0–M2.
- **Substantive alternative A1 (dose + CR, inherited):** Fine-Gray/Cox on the continuous
  deep-sedation fraction (spline/decile gradient), keeping death competing.
- **NEW tests that could flip the reading:** NCO (i), within-stratum A1 (ii), bias bound (iii).
- **What the alternative reveals that B0 loses:** A1's gradient distinguishes a monotone
  dose-response from a threshold/selection artifact; the NCO and strata separate a within-indication
  causal gradient from an indication/severity marker (what B0 and K1-style single-model analyses
  cannot).
- **Approx budget:** CPU only, <1 h; `lifelines`/`statsmodels` (+ small NumPy Fine-Gray
  subdistribution weights); **no GPU needed or claimed.**

## 6. Falsification criteria (prespecified)

- **Supportive (C):** adjusted cause-specific HR <1 (95 % CI excludes 1) and negative 7-day
  liberation CIF difference; survives M0–M2; gradient monotone; **persists within every indication
  stratum with no stratum-by-exposure crossover**; NCOs (new RRT, vasopressor escalation) **null**;
  permutation control null; bounded bias magnitude needed to null the HR is larger than plausible.
- **Adverse (R-indication / severity-marker):** the deep-vs-light association attenuates to
  equivalence (HR within 0.9–1.1) once M1 is included, **or** the gradient collapses/nulls within
  strata, **or** the NCO reproduces a "protective/dose" signal, **or** a small unmeasured-indication
  bias (E-value magnitude) is enough to explain the signal.
- **Adverse (R-death):** liberation effect largely disappears under the competing-risks model while
  the death hazard/CIF is elevated in the deep group.
- **Inconclusive:** wide CI; minimum-events gate failed; RASS too sparse; strata too small to
  estimate; NCO itself under-powered; documentation completeness too low for the [t*, t*+12 h]
  fraction.

## 7. Interpretation and limits

Supportive -> supports targeting sedation depth as a causal lever (still needs RCT confirmation).
Adverse -> depth largely a severity/indication marker and/or a death conflation; deprioritise
depth-only protocols. Inconclusive -> need better sedation documentation/indication coding before a
decision. **Limits:** single-centre retrospective; RASS charting completeness unknown (K2 validates
the scale, not EHR documentation); confounding by indication only partly addressed (unmeasured
residual severity remains); association is not causation; **cohort prevalence, event counts and
power are NOT established in discovery** (no outcome rows read). **Computationally checkable:** the
CR/gradient computations, event counts, HRs, CIF_d, NCO estimates, stratum-specific HRs and the
numeric bias bound. **Requires clinical adjudication / another study:** whether a residual latent
severity axis fully absorbs the effect (unidentifiable here), whether indication strata are coded
completely, and any causal claim.
