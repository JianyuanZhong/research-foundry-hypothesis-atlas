## Hypothesis to test

Among people with a similar average biological age, greater differences between organ ages are associated with a higher risk of cardiorenal multimorbidity.

## Study population and main data

Participants with proteomics data; existing organ-age measures.

## Suggested approach

Study differences between organ ages in relation to the order of onset of diabetes, chronic kidney disease, and heart failure.

## Scientific significance

Investigate differences between organs rather than build another aging clock.

## Main limitations

Account for proteins shared across organ-age measures and for the influence of underlying disease.
