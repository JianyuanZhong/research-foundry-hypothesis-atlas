## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE/resection/ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats AFP, albumin and
bilirubin. The parent asks whether the *within-patient AFP trajectory* adds prognostic information
beyond baseline AFP AND beyond liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics measured in the SAME draws.

**Opening / evidence.** The parent's probe established that the `examinations` table contains
`>1.7M` dated reports carrying progression/recurrence descriptors, so the exposure scenario is real
and the progression event is dated, i.e. the *timing* of detection is recoverable. A bounded
re-probe here (first 200k examination rows) confirms the descriptor pool is large: 增多 30595,
增大 16700, 复发 2954, 进展 2055, 新发 432, and that abdomen-restricted study names and device
tokens are present (Section 4). No inspected work tests AFP-vs-ALBI kinetic added-value with a
lead-time / informative-observation discriminator in HCC [K1][K2].

**The still-unresolved objection (the target of this evolution).** Even with a dated radiologic
endpoint, imaging is not scheduled at random. If AFP is used clinically to bring a scan forward,
a rising AFP slope will *stochastically precede* a progression report merely because it triggered
the earlier scan ("lead-time / informative-observation" bias), without the tumour growing faster.
A landmark design alone cannot separate these. The board's own decisive reason for selecting the
parent (comparison-90b4e988a78bdcd3b6d6b87e, revision 120) and its sharpest noted limitation
(comparison-d545464c30a01699402a59ca, revision 94: *"both strata are defined by potentially
informed imaging"*) identify this as the one remaining unresolved objection.

**Clinical importance.** If AFP kinetics predict progression mainly by *triggering* scans, the
actionable reading is "AFP prompts imaging", and the correct change is surveillance INTERVAL
policy. If AFP kinetics predict progression *conditional on being scanned*, the reading is
biologically-grounded tumour-activity information that supports AFP-triggered treatment escalation.
These imply different clinical decisions, and a clean null (liver reserve alone) is also
decision-relevant because it argues for de-emphasising AFP-only monitoring.

## 3. THE ONE ADDITION — a scan-triggering negative-control test

The parent admits that `E_early`/`E_late` are **both** defined by imaging that may be informed by
AFP. This child adds a pre-specified **negative control on imaging-study timing** that attacks that
weakness directly by measuring whether AFP slope predicts *the arrival of any abdominal scan*,
independent of any progression descriptor.

**(a) Imaging-intensity model (negative control).** Fit a Cox / Andersen–Gill recurrent-event
hazard of **TIME TO NEXT INCIDENT ABDOMINAL IMAGING STUDY** — any study in `检查` under the SAME
abdominal cross-sectional modality restriction, **regardless of whether a progression descriptor is
present** — as a function of the SAME AFP slope + ALBI slope (and the same baseline covariates).
Each patient contributes an ordered sequence of incident abdominal scans after `t*`; the event
process is the scan process, not the report process.

**(b) Comparison against the progression-report hazard.** Fit the parent's primary progression
hazard (`HR_prog`, first local progression report after `t*`) on the identical cohort, exposure
window and slope scale. Compare the two AFP-slope hazard ratios.

**Pre-registered discriminating predictions.** Define, on the SAME per-1-SD-of-AFP-slope scale,
`R = HR_prog / HR_scan` (ratio of progression hazard-ratio to scan-timing hazard-ratio).

- Under **pure informative observation** (AFP merely triggers scans) the AFP slope predicts the
  scan-timing hazard strongly while the progression report carries little *additional* slope
  signal conditional on being scanned, so `HR_scan` is materially elevated and `R` is small.
- Under **tumour activity** the AFP slope predicts the progression report conditional on being
  scanned, so `HR_prog` is materially elevated and `R >= 1`.

**(c) Pre-registered decision rule (band = 0.6).**

| AFP-slope result | Pre-registered branch |
|---|---|
| `HR_prog > 1` (95% CI excludes 1) **and** `R >= 1.0` with 95% CI lower bound `> 0.6` | **Supportive** of the tumour-activity reading: the progression association is not merely a scan-trigger artefact. |
| `R <= 0.6` with 95% CI upper bound `< 1.0` (scan hazard dominates the progression hazard) | **Adverse**: interpret as "AFP prompts the scan"; the primary AFP→progression effect is substantially an ascertainment artefact, and the readout shifts to surveillance-interval policy. |
| Anything else (CI spanning the band; either HR imprecise; scan-intensity model not identifiable/fitting) | **Inconclusive**: no claim about instrument vs biology; report the interval and the required data. |

The band 0.6 is pre-specified (not tuned to the observed data) so that the adverse branch is
reachable and falsifiable. Both models use identical landmark population, `t*`, exposure window
`(index, t*]`, slope scale and covariate set, so `R` is a like-for-like contrast.

## 5. Method comparison — M0 (baseline-only) vs M4 (joint mixed-effects AFP+ALBI)

Both are fitted on the identical landmark cohort; the difference is what information they carry.

- **M0 — baseline-only Cox (baseline).** Cox proportional-hazards with baseline-only covariates
  (baseline AFP, baseline ALBI, age, sex, index procedure class). By construction it contains no
  trajectory information, so it bounds how much of any signal is already present at baseline. It is
  the honest floor and is required before any trajectory claim.
- **M4 — joint mixed-effects AFP + ALBI model (alternative).** A shared-parameter joint model: a
  linear mixed-effects submodel for `log(AFP)` and one for ALBI over `(index, t*]` (patient random
  intercepts and slopes), coupled to a survival (or recurrent-event, for the negative control)
  submodel whose hazard is associated with the subject-specific random slopes.

**Why M4, and what it reveals that M0 loses.** A two-stage/landmark Cox that plugs in a per-patient
OLS slope (the parent's implicit step) attenuates the slope by within-patient measurement error and
by unbalanced draw timing, and it ignores that unstable slopes come from few, irregularly spaced
draws. M4 uses every qualifying triple, propagates slope uncertainty into the hazard, and separates
the AFP trajectory from the co-measured ALBI trajectory within the same draws. That is exactly the
information M0 cannot contain and the two-stage approximation distorts. The comparison tests a
substantive uncertainty (does a within-patient trajectory carry hazard information at all, and is
it separable from same-draw liver reserve?), not a small predictive gain.

**Resource/time budget (approximate, UNVERIFIED).** Tabular, CPU-feasible. Cohort likely O(10^3)
patients with O(1–10) draws each in the 180-day window; a joint model (`JM`/`joineR`/`Stan` EM)
fits on CPU in minutes; no GPU required. Exact runtime is a solver feasibility output, not claimed
here.

## 6. Falsification criteria (conjunctive, inherited + the added term)

A supportive claim — "the within-patient AFP trajectory adds prognostic information beyond ALBI
kinetics" — holds ONLY IF ALL of the following hold:

1. Primary landmark Cox: AFP-slope `HR_prog > 1` with 95% CI excluding 1, **adjusted for the
   same-draw ALBI slope**.
2. Time-dependent Cox on the same exposure agrees in sign and non-trivial magnitude.
3. **Added term (negative control): `R = HR_prog / HR_scan >= 1.0` with 95% CI lower bound
   `> 0.6`** — the progression association is not substantially weaker than the pure scan-timing
   association.
4. The `E_late` (>90 d) stratum is directionally consistent.

Failing term 3 leads to the pre-registered **adverse** reading (scan-trigger / informative
observation), even if terms 1–2 hold. Failure of term 4 alone yields a lead-time-limited
conclusion. Any imprecise term yields **inconclusive**, and an imprecise null does not refute the
claim. The endpoint is an UNVALIDATED lexical extraction with a pre-registered audit, agreement
floor and fallback to the retreatment proxy; event rate and flag validation are the solver's FIRST
feasibility outputs and are themselves falsification-relevant checks.
