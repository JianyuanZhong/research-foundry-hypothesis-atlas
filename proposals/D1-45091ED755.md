# Patient-level documentary-target retention frontier for the certified HCC report-coverage contract

## 1. Targeted audit and substantive child repair

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent’s patient-complete conditional coverage contract, fixed-capacity queue, and exact same-patient queue equality are retained.

The audit finds that exact queue equality is appropriate but incomplete:

- If the operational safety claim is “no patient may be substituted in a fixed-capacity review queue,” exact set equality is the right conservative predicate and remains a mandatory co-primary certificate.
- It is too blunt as the only clinically focused diagnostic. A queue can fail exact equality because of a substitution among documentary-M2-negative patients, while the operationally more consequential event for the locked target is loss of a patient that the maximum-content model would have queued and whose same-encounter adjudicated documentary-M2 endpoint is positive.
- Conversely, aggregate capture equality is insufficient: one maximum-content documentary-M2-positive patient can be lost while another is gained, leaving the count unchanged.

The child adds a second, target-focused frontier using only the parent’s frozen G scores, whole-accession content masks, and parent’s adjudicated documentary-M2 label. It adds no feature, refit, external patient data, clinical utility weight, action label, or outcome. Exact queue equality remains reported and falsifiable; the new frontier does not declare any negative-patient substitution clinically harmless.

The substantive advance is a decomposition of decision robustness into:

1. zero-substitution queue safety (q_dec, inherited from the parent); and
2. no-loss retention of maximum-content documentary-M2-positive queued patients (q_M2dec).

This identifies whether an exact-queue failure is a target-positive loss or only a documentary-target substitution, while preserving the fail-closed interpretation that neither is validated clinical benefit or harm.

## 2. Clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

The unresolved decision question is:

> At 24 hours before an adult’s first eligible HCC resection, does a certified patient-complete report-bundle exposure reserve preserve the maximum-stored-content queue’s documentary-M2-positive patients when expert review capacity is fixed at K, even if exact identity of all K queued patients is not preserved?

This matters because review capacity is allocated to patients, and a compensated aggregate count can conceal loss of individual target-positive patients. The relevant available endpoint is documentary M2, not biological MVI, clinician action, or patient outcome.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is limited to the frozen HCC corpus: it has encounter-linked procedures and operative timing fields; accession-linked, multirow examination narratives with acquisition-like times; same-encounter pathology narratives; and enough locked parent inputs to replay fixed-capacity G queues under coupled stored-content worlds. The source does not contain report release/read/view timestamps, report-version provenance, raw images, specimen/block/slide linkage, clinical actions, treatment, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or utility.

The untested claim is that content loss does not remove any patient from the maximum-content queue who is positive on the parent’s same-encounter documentary-M2 endpoint. The confirmatory 24-hour hypothesis is:

> Under every complete coupled source/operation/onset/reader-pathology/deletion world that meets the inherited conditional-coverage threshold, the fixed-K G queue retains every maximum-stored-content queued patient with a positive parent-defined documentary-M2 label; therefore the exact threshold q_M2dec is below 1, while the exact queue threshold q_dec may be stricter.

The hypothesis is falsified if any admissible complete world at the reported threshold loses at least one maximum-content documentary-M2-positive queued patient. A positive result supports only finite-corpus documentary-target retention against the maximum-stored-content oracle.

## 4. New estimand

For a complete state z (source/operation world, onset state, cutoff, reader/pathology pair, deletion), let:

- S_G(z) be the set of nonempty protected patient hashes in the fixed-K queue from the observed visibility mask;
- S_max(z) be the set of nonempty protected patient hashes in the same-world fixed-K queue when every source-clean eligible accession is exposed;
- Y_i(z) be the parent’s fixed documentary-M2 label for patient i under the same book pair and world. Use exactly the parent’s label mapping; do not reinterpret it as biological MVI. Any parent-uncertain state is not silently converted to positive or negative;
- T_max(z) = {i in S_max(z): Y_i(z)=1}.

The inherited exact decision predicate is retained:

J_set(z) = 1{S_G(z) = S_max(z)}, including empty positions and no deletion backfill.

The new target-retention predicate is:

J_M2(z) = 1{T_max(z) is a subset of S_G(z)},

evaluated only when |T_max(z)| > 0. For every state also emit:

- n_M2max(z) = |T_max(z)|;
- loss_M2(z) = |T_max(z) - S_G(z)|;
- retention_M2(z) = 1 - loss_M2(z)/n_M2max(z);
- the protected hashes of lost maximum-content documentary-M2-positive patients;
- the symmetric queue difference and ordered-slot difference already required by the parent.

A state with n_M2max=0 is reported as target-denominator-zero, never as perfect target retention. If no complete state has a positive target denominator, q_M2dec is feasibility-inconclusive for the target-focused question.

For each exact q in the inherited R, define M2DEC_PASS(q) only if:

1. the inherited PASS(q) is true in every complete state with Q_(omega,2020) >= q and Q_(omega,2021) >= q;
2. the inherited J_set predicate is evaluated and reported in every such state; and
3. J_M2 is true in every such state with n_M2max > 0.

Define q_M2dec = min{q in R: M2DEC_PASS(q)=1}.

Report q_req, q_dec, q_M2dec, q_M2dec-q_req, and all exact boundary witnesses. The compiler must verify q_M2dec >= q_req; it must also report whether q_M2dec < q_dec, q_M2dec = q_dec, or either is undefined. A target-retention pass cannot override an exact queue failure for a claim of zero patient substitution.

## 5. Interpretation and falsification

Use mutually exclusive primary labels:

1. computationally_inconclusive: any source replay, accession grouping, operation-clock audit, coupled-world identity, locked-score/tie replay, deletion, patient-complete certificate, exact frontier, target-retention frontier, or independent verification fails.
2. feasibility_inconclusive: any inherited structural denominator, K, event, n_A, or positive target-denominator requirement fails.
3. maximum_content_signal_adverse: maximum stored content fails an inherited comparator, random, or deletion gate.
4. conditional_coverage_adverse: maximum content passes but q_req is undefined or equals 1.
5. documentary_target_loss_adverse: maximum content and inherited coverage pass, but q_M2dec is undefined or equals 1; at least one admissible target-positive queue loss remains at every positive reserve.
6. documentary_target_retention_exact_queue_adverse: q_M2dec < 1 but q_dec is undefined or equals 1. The target-positive patients are retained, but exact patient queue identity is not robust. This is not evidence that substitutions are clinically harmless.
7. documentary_decision_supportive: both q_M2dec < 1 and q_dec < 1, the immediately lower thresholds fail where one exists, all witnesses verify, and all structural certificates are complete. This supports only finite-corpus stored-content documentary queue robustness.
8. structural_entry_or_archive_bridge_pending: HCC computation is valid but the external report-identity, release/retrieval/readability, or operational workflow bridge is absent.

A favorable 12/48/72-hour sensitivity cannot rescue or veto the 24-hour label. No result supports biological MVI accuracy, treatment effect, safety, recurrence, survival, transportability, cost-effectiveness, or patient utility.

The distinction is explicit:

stored-content allocation -> report visibility -> documentary-M2 label -> biological truth -> clinical action -> treatment benefit -> patient utility.

Only the first and the parent-defined documentary label are available for this experiment.

Biological validity requires specimen/block/slide linkage, sampling adequacy, and blinded pathology adjudication. None is available in HCC; clinical_action_status=not_identified remains mandatory.

## 9. What changed and what remains uncertain

Changed: the child adds a predeclared, same-world q_M2dec frontier and state-level target-positive loss decomposition. It makes exact queue equality a clearly interpreted zero-substitution co-primary certificate rather than the sole proxy for clinical consequence. It preserves the parent’s adult HCC population, 24-hour confirmatory frame, temporal exclusions, exact paths/columns/keys, report-grain contract, coupled uncertainty, locked models, fixed K, thresholds, deletion rules, evidence boundaries, and no clinical-action claims.

Unchanged uncertainty: HCC has no report-release/view clock, report-version chain, raw images, specimen-level pathology linkage, validated biological endpoint, clinical action, outcome, harm, cost, or utility. A favorable q_M2dec result can only support a finite-corpus documentary-target retention statement; a favorable exact q_dec result can only support the stronger stored-content zero-substitution statement. Both require an external archive/workflow/specimen bridge before any clinical deployment claim.
