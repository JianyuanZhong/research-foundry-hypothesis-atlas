# Episode 18 inferential successor: the +10-point late acidemic SpO2 harm hypothesis is adverse

## Result and clinical meaning

The locked noncausal hypothesis was that, among the Episode-18 post-day-1 overlap population, recorded SpO2 94–95% rather than 96–97% marks at least a **+10 percentage-point** greater standardized hospital-mortality risk. The complete source-audited experiment falsifies that material prognostic claim.

The unchanged primary overlap estimate was 29.96% mortality in the 94–95% band versus 46.72% in the 96–97% band: lower-minus-higher risk difference (RD) **−16.75 points** and risk ratio 0.641. In all 5,000 prespecified hierarchical bootstrap replicates (seed 20260918), the percentile 95% interval for the RD was **−52.01 to +2.21 points**; no replicate failed. Because the upper bound is below +10 points, the locked material-harm hypothesis is **adverse**. Because the interval includes zero, direction remains unresolved: this does not establish a protective association, benefit of lower oxygenation, or an oxygen-target effect.

This is clinically useful negative evidence. The earlier concurrent SpO2/PaO2 mapping should not be promoted into a claim that this late lower-band phenotype marks a clinically material mortality excess in this EICU target. It does not settle oxygen-target safety, device validity, or causal treatment choice.

## Supported claim, tested claim, and advance

Before this run, source reconstruction and outcome-blind feasibility were supported: 556 eligible measurements, 440 earliest-stay indices, 156 parent exact-cell overlap rows, and a new 126-person post-24-hour primary cohort with adequate measured overlap. The −16.75-point pilot was explicitly noninferential.

This experiment tested the still-unresolved claim
[
Delta_{APACHE}=P_w(D=1mid L=1)-P_w(D=1mid L=0)ge 0.10,
]
where (L=1) is SpO2 94–95%, (L=0) is 96–97%, (D) is future hospital death, and (w) defines the measured-overlap population after standardizing first-day APACHE-IVa predicted hospital mortality, hospital, FiO2, PaCO2, pH, and index time.

The substantive advance is inferential resolution rather than another threshold search: the locked 5,000-replicate interval excludes the prespecified +10-point margin. Every computable robustness point estimate also remained below +10 points, including pre-index diagnosis and recorded treatment-limitation checks. The strongest supported conclusion is therefore adverse only to the material noncausal prognostic hypothesis. No causal conclusion follows.

## Measurable diagnosis and treatment-limitation confounding

Diagnosis augmentation used only `admissionDx` rows with `split_part(admitdxpath,'|',2)='All Diagnosis'`, one of ten fixed organ-system categories in path component five, and `admitdxenteredoffset<=1440` and <= index. Three people had no such row. APACHE predicted risk already incorporates diagnostic information, so this is a deliberately redundant sensitivity. Its negative RD was stable, but three fitted propensities were outside 0.05–0.95 because several categories had one or zero patients. It cannot prove diagnosis control is complete.

Recorded treatment limitation used the latest `carePlanGeneral` row at or before index with exact `cplgroup='Care Limitation'`; any exact value other than `Full therapy` was flagged. Twelve people were flagged. The analysis did not use `activeupondischarge`, because that field can encode future state. A separate marker required both `carePlanEOL.cpleoldiscussionoffset<=draw` and `cpleolsaveoffset<=draw`; one person met it. Excluding these markers preserved the adverse point direction, but reduced overlap/ESS. Absence means “not recorded in these tables by index,” not adjudicated unrestricted treatment intent. Notes, surrogate decisions, withdrawal timing, and unrecorded goals remain unresolved.

## Falsification, stopping, and continuation

The prespecified supportive rule was a lower 95% bound at least +0.10 with every gate passing. It was not met. The adverse rule was an upper bound below +0.10. It was met.

For this snapshot, stop further threshold/model/subgroup mining aimed at rescuing the **+10-point harm** claim: the primary upper bound is +2.21 points, all 5,000 replicates were defined, and every feasible robustness point estimate was below +10. Preserve the negative result. Do not reinterpret the negative point estimate as protection.

Continuation is justified only as a new study with genuine information gain: an independently collected, prospectively synchronized cohort should record waveform/device quality, verified arterial specimen and co-oximetry, delivered FiO2 and target intent, ABG indication, duration in each band, explicit treatment-limitation state before index, cause/time of death, and post-discharge outcomes. Precompute its sample size against the +10-point margin. If a computational defect in this locked run is documented, repair it as a child; do not change population, margin, estimand, or outcome as a “repair.” A causal oxygen-target or safety claim requires an assigned-target trial and clinical review.

Had the interval included +10, the result would have been inconclusive and same-snapshot subgrouping would still stop; continuation would require the external information-gain study. A failed required computation would justify only a mechanical rerun. Neither situation occurred.

## Verifier boundary

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes/headers, no source writes, filters/ties/joins, temporal inequalities, 556/440/156 and 164/158/126 reconciliation, forbidden-column non-use, diagnosis/care-plan cutoff rules, model matrix, overlap weights, bootstrap copy IDs/seed/count, intervals, diagnostics, sensitivities, and result-to-classification logic.

Automatic verification cannot establish arterial specimen identity, ventilation truth, device or waveform quality, delivered FiO2, target intent, complete goals of care, ABG indication, APACHE calibration/clinical availability, causal exchangeability, transportability, safety, or clinical utility. Those require clinical adjudication, missing measurements, external validation, and for treatment claims another design.

A correct output with this interval must pass “adverse to +10 with unresolved direction.” It must fail if described as no association, protection, lower-target benefit, oxygen/FiO2 effect, safety, device validation, routine-ABG guidance, or cross-hospital utility. Supportive and inconclusive synthetic fixtures from the parent remain unchanged.
