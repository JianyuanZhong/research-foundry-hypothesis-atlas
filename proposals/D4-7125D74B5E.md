# Raw maximum-grip cystatin C allocation: equal-capacity, frailty-controlled locked replication in UK Biobank

## Clinical decision, evidence boundary, and falsifiable hypothesis

A service able to order cystatin C for only 20% of adults with race-free creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m2 needs to know whom to assay. The clinically consequential question is not whether grip marginally improves discrimination, but whether one routinely collected grip value changes an equal-budget allocation enough to find otherwise undocumented low-filtration reclassification and subsequent kidney-event enrichment, beyond the same clinical baseline and available generic-function proxies.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is limited. Creatinine and cystatin C have different non-GFR determinants; muscle mass can contribute to discordance; and participant characteristics, including grip in an enriched model, can internally predict creatinine–cystatin eGFR differences in UK Biobank. The inspected full text of Yim et al. (PMCID PMC9747759; DOI 10.3389/fmed.2022.1021936) reports 138 Korean participants and uses bioimpedance skeletal muscle index and calf circumference, not handgrip or measured GFR. The inspected full text of Chen et al. (PMCID PMC10986041; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796) reports an internally split UKB discordance analysis; grip was one of many enriched predictors, but that study did not test exact assay budgets, locked replication, measured GFR, downstream benefit, or whether grip beats equal-capacity generic-function policies.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> In the inherited pre-assay UKB population and both locked partitions, a 20% policy whose model is the shared clinical baseline B plus exactly one directly standardized raw maximum-grip scalar will identify at least 2 more combined-equation low-filtration reclassifications per 1,000 assays than B and the development-frozen best same-one-scalar generic-function policy F. The gain will replicate, exceed frailty-stratified permutations that preserve the fitted grip model and exact assay budget, and enrich a prespecified creatinine–cystatin discordance label. Separately, if event sufficiency passes, G will add at least 1 reclassified person with a subsequent 10-year inpatient N17/N18 event per 1,000 assays versus B and will not be clinically inferior to F.

This tests participant-specific allocation value of raw maximum grip beyond B, equal added dimension, and two available generic-function proxies. It does not test that grip is muscle mass, that low grip causes marker discordance, that cystatin eGFR is true GFR, or that ordering cystatin prevents an event.

### Shared clinical baseline B

B contains the same intercept and basis columns for age, sex, BMI, and eGFRcr in every B/G/W/Q model. No cystatin value, H, D, N17/N18 outcome, death, or post-baseline value is a predictor.

### Primary raw-grip policy G: exactly one added basis column

Define raw maximum grip R_i as max(46-0.0,47-0.0) across finite positive available values; O guarantees at least one, so G has no grip imputation and no grip-missing indicator. Using development O only, compute the empirical 0.5th and 99.5th percentiles with the frozen linear interpolation rule (NumPy method="linear", equivalent to R type 7). Winsorize R at those two global—not sex-specific—limits. Let mu_R and sigma_R be the mean and population standard deviation (ddof=0) of the winsorized values in development O. If sigma_R is zero, G is noncomputable and the experiment is inconclusive. Set Z_R=(winsorized R-mu_R)/sigma_R and apply those frozen limits, mu_R, and sigma_R unchanged to development validation folds and both locked partitions.

Primary G is B plus Z_R once and only once. Z_R enters linearly with one coefficient. There is no residualization, spline, polynomial, threshold, interaction, sex-specific centering/scaling/winsorization, handedness term, side indicator, or extra missing indicator. Preprocessing is univariate and outcome-blind; it cannot import age-, sex-, BMI-, or eGFR-dependent functions. B is bit-for-bit the same design matrix in B and G, so G adds exactly one representational dimension.

A low or high fitted coefficient is learned from development H; “raw grip” is not “grip deficit,” “relative grip,” a sarcopenia diagnosis, or a direct muscle-mass measurement.

### Same-capacity generic-function policies W, Q, and frozen F

W is B plus one standardized walking-pace burden scalar from 924-0.0: 2 for slow (code 1), 1 for steady (2), 0 for brisk (3); blank, -7, -3, and any other value are replaced with the development-O mean, with no missing indicator. Q is B plus one standardized overall-health burden scalar from 2178-0.0: valid codes 1–4 retain their order; blank, -1, -3, and other values are replaced with the development-O mean, with no indicator. Standardize each scalar globally from development O with frozen mean and population SD. If an SD is zero, that policy is noncomputable. Report missing/special-code rates and complete-valid sensitivity.

Choose F as W or Q using only mean five-fold out-of-fold development H yield at exact 20% budget; a tie to 12 decimal places selects W. Freeze F before locked labels are read. B, G, W, and Q use identical B columns, fitting procedure, folds, and penalty grid; each active nonbaseline policy adds exactly one scalar basis and coefficient. Report both W and Q although only F enters primary comparisons.

### Frailty-conditioned same-model placebo policies

After G is fitted, hold its raw-grip preprocessing, coefficient, every B component, and penalty fixed. Within each locked partition, permute Z_R 500 times within 32 baseline strata: sex (2) × walking pace (slow/steady/brisk/other-or-missing) × health (excellent/good/fair-or-poor/other-or-missing). A cell with fewer than 20 participants merges first across health within the same sex/pace and then, if still below 20, into the same-sex stratum. Freeze and report the resulting cell map before outcomes are opened.

Seeds are SHA256("ukb-grip-placebo-v1|partition|draw"); record exact UTF-8 conversion from the full digest to the pseudorandom-generator seed and the permutation algorithm/version. Every draw permutes the single frozen Z_R vector, reranks all O in that partition, and selects the same n. These policies preserve fitted capacity and the within-stratum marginal raw-grip distribution while breaking participant-specific grip assignment conditional on coarse sex/function strata. They do not prove conditional independence, isolate muscle biology, or remove unmeasured frailty/body composition.

All policies break score ties by ascending SHA256(utf8("ukb-grip-cys-tie-v3|"+canonical_decimal_eid)). Also report random-20% expectation and deterministic random draws, oldest-first, lowest-eGFRcr-first, and universal testing as references, never substitutes for B or F.

## Estimands and analysis

For policy S in a partition:

Y_H(S)=1000×sum(S×H)/n; Y_D(S)=1000×sum(S×D)/n; Y_HK(S)=1000×sum(S×H×K)/n; Y_DK(S)=1000×sum(S×D×K)/n.

The inherited allocation estimand is Delta_H(G:B)=Y_H(G)-Y_H(B), with ratio Y_H(G)/Y_H(B). G must yield at least 2 additional H per 1,000 assays, ratio at least 1.25, and simultaneous two-sided 95% paired intervals excluding 0 and 1 respectively in each locked partition.

The fair-comparator estimand is Delta_H(G:F). Require at least 2 additional H per 1,000 and a simultaneous 95% paired interval above 0 in each locked partition. Decompose both contrasts exactly into equal-size G-only gains minus comparator-only losses; report overlap and Jaccard index. Positive aggregate yield with nonpositive G-only-versus-F-only yield is adverse.

Attribution has two gates. First, actual Delta_H(G:B) in each locked partition must exceed the 97.5th percentile of its 500 frailty-conditioned raw-grip placebo differences; report empirical p=(1+# placebo differences >= actual)/501. Second, Delta_D(G:F) must be positive in both locked partitions and its prespecified pooled, partition-stratified paired 95% interval must exceed 0. Passing shows replicated participant-specific allocation and biochemical-discordance information from the one raw-grip scalar beyond the tested equal-dimensional generic-function comparator. It does not establish a muscle or creatinine mechanism.

The decision-relevance estimand remains Delta_HK(G:B). If event sufficiency passes, require at least 1 additional H×K case per 1,000 assays and a pooled paired 95% interval above 0. Against F, require noninferiority: the one-sided 95% lower bound for Delta_HK(G:F) exceeds -1 per 1,000. Claim superiority to F only when its two-sided interval exceeds 0. Kidney-event yield is policy-specific enrichment, not benefit caused by testing and not a mechanism test.

Use 2,000 paired participant bootstrap resamples within partition; pooled intervals resample within locked partition, preserving partition strata. Models are not refit. For the two primary H contrasts and their ratios use max-|t| simultaneous bootstrap intervals, with a prespecified percentile fallback if studentization fails. Report H and D prevalence, sensitivity, PPV, selected-count calibration, calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, and decision curves per 1,000 assays. A G calibration slope outside 0.8–1.2 in either locked partition blocks supportive classification.

Report pace, health, age, sex, BMI, eGFRcr, raw maximum grip, and Z_R in G-only, B-only, and F-only sets. Report selection fraction, H/D/H×K yields, calibration, and swap-set summaries separately by sex, while retaining the overall exact 20% policy estimand. As a generic-vulnerability diagnostic, report 10-year death without prior K among H-positive selections and G:B/G:F contrasts. It has no automatic mechanistic threshold; excess non-kidney death warns that generic frailty remains plausible.

For K, estimate 10-year Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence with death competing in selected, overlap, and unique sets. Cause-specific Cox and Fine–Gray analyses are explanatory only and cannot replace assay-yield estimands or imply intervention effects.

## Raw-grip sex interpretation and sensitivity boundary

Raw maximum grip differs substantially by sex and body size. Although B includes sex, age, BMI, and eGFRcr, primary G imposes one global linear grip coefficient and global preprocessing. This is deliberate for an exact one-added-basis comparison, but it means G may partly exploit between-sex or body-size-associated strength distributions and may perform differently within sex. Overall superiority therefore does not establish sex-fair allocation, sex-invariant transportability, or a participant’s weakness relative to demographic expectations.

Before unlocking, freeze descriptive checks: development and locked distributions of raw/winsorized grip by sex; policy selection fractions and score distributions by sex; sex-specific swap yields and calibration; and a development-only grip×sex interaction test reported as heterogeneity, not used to alter primary G. Prespecified sensitivities are: (1) sex-specific standardization of raw grip; (2) B+Z_R+Z_R×sex; (3) the parent’s outcome-blind residual grip predicted from sex and spline age/BMI/eGFRcr with sex×linear-age and sex×linear-BMI; and (4) the parent’s richer raw-grip spline/interaction model. All are explicitly higher/different-capacity representations and cannot rescue failed primary G. Define a sex-instability flag, before outcomes are opened, as either primary H contrast (G:B or G:F) having a sex-specific point estimate <=0 for the same sex in both locked partitions while its overall contrast otherwise passes. That flag makes the clinical interpretation inconclusive; it does not invalidate computation or alter the overall estimand. Selection-rate imbalance or a development interaction alone is descriptive, has no automatic threshold, and instead requires equity/clinical review before prospective use.

## Event sufficiency, falsification, and result meanings

Before interpreting H×K, require at least 100 H×K events in G union B union F across both locked partitions; at least 20 H×K events separately in each pooled comparator-specific swap set G\B, B\G, G\F, and F\G; and at least 30 in a partition for any positive partition-specific event statement. If any condition fails, report point estimates and bounds but classify clinical relevance inconclusive; do not change endpoints or use development outcomes.

Mandatory computational falsification checks are: exact 20% counts; no partition overlap; one-to-one joins; exact code/date index pairing; no locked-label or K access before freeze; equation fixtures; no outcome predictor; identical B matrices; G design-column set exactly columns(B) union {Z_R}; one global development-only pair of winsorization limits and one global mu_R/sigma_R; no residualized/interacted/splined/sex-specific grip or grip-missing column in primary G; one extra scalar for W/Q; and deterministic tie/permutation reproduction with all 500 policies retaining n. Actual performance inside the placebo distribution is adverse to participant-specific raw-grip value.

Repeat G using maximum, mean, and minimum available positive hand measurements; no-winsorization; complete-valid pace/health; the four higher/different-capacity grip sensitivities above; absolute discordance below -15; one- and two-year outcome lags; and 10%/30% budgets. A sign reversal across hand definitions, benefit confined to poor-health/slow-walking strata or one sex, no higher H or D in G-only than F-only, or a result driven by one locked partition is adverse/unstable. Sensitivities cannot rescue failed primary 20% results.

Classify results as follows:

- Fully supportive: all G:B and G:F H thresholds pass in both locked partitions under adverse joint missing-label bounds and simultaneous intervals; G exceeds raw-grip placebo distributions in both; pooled D attribution passes with concordant partition signs; calibration and falsification checks pass with no sex-instability flag; and, after event sufficiency, H×K beats B and is noninferior to F. This supports prospective comparison of selective-testing policies, not routine adoption.
- Biochemical-only supportive: every H and attribution gate passes with acceptable calibration/sex stability, but H×K is sparse or its interval is inconclusive. Raw grip then improves biochemical allocation beyond tested capacity/function controls; clinical consequence remains unresolved.
- Adverse: G has Delta_H<=0 or ratio<=1, misses either inherited materiality threshold, fails F or placebo, has nonpositive pooled D attribution, is inferior on sufficiently powered H×K, fails swap decomposition, or has reproducible leakage/design/calibration failure. Strong W/Q performance is informative evidence for a cheaper generic-function policy.
- Inconclusive: intervals cross decision boundaries; locked partitions disagree; more than 1% of O lacks cystatin; adverse sharp bounds fail; event rules fail; a denominator can be zero; code/date integrity is unresolved; F was not frozen; or the prespecified sex-instability flag is present or hand-definition/frailty-stratum dependence prevents stable interpretation.

Aggregate feasibility, association, AUC improvement, pooled positive point estimates, or sensitivity-only success cannot trigger support.
