# Observable physiology-versus-care decomposition of fixed pre-exit lactate-by-perfusion prioritization

## Status, unresolved question and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s fixed 12-hour pre-exit ICU landmark, exact lactate-by-perfusion phenotype, dual clinical/recording-time coverage rule, fixed-capacity held-out estimand, context-shift evaluation, and record-defined competing R/D/S/N/U outcomes. No cohort scan, fitted model, prevalence, effect estimate, or clinical result is claimed.

The strongest available evidence is source/design-level only. The frozen MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains repeated lactate and MAP observations, vasoactive intervals, ICU support/procedure records, admission outcomes, transfer-unit intervals and demographic anchor-year groups. Read-only dictionary inspection confirms lactate 50813 (Lactate, Blood), MAP 220052 (Arterial Blood Pressure mean), 220181 (Non Invasive Blood Pressure mean), 225312 (ART BP Mean), Foley 226559, the 11 target vasoactive item IDs and the nine support-procedure IDs below. This establishes availability and labels, not event support, physiological validity, predictive performance, clinical appropriateness, or transportability.

The unresolved clinical question is:

> At a fixed 12-hour pre-exit ICU landmark, does the prespecified lactate-by-measured-perfusion signal add held-out fixed-capacity prioritization information for a record-defined ICU transition or timed death after accounting for the observable care/documentation process, and is any incremental signal present for death (D) as well as record-defined escalation (R)?

The parent’s pooled and context-shift result, if later computed, would still be vulnerable to a clinically important interpretation error. High lactate/MAP ascertainment, pressor exposure, Foley charting, support documentation and care-unit practice can all be associated with both the recorded phenotype and later R. Bdisc-minus-B1 is the locked phenotype-specific contrast, but it does not by itself show that the remaining signal is more physiologic than process-associated. Nor does the composite A=R-or-D reveal whether the signal is concentrated in a care-generated R or in the less intervention-defined death component D.

The substantive advance is an observable decomposition, not a claim to recover an unmeasured physiological state:

1. Separate measured value information (P), observation/documentation intensity (O), and care-exposure/support process (T) using prespecified, non-overlapping feature blocks.
2. Estimate conditional fixed-queue increments `P|C` and `C|P`, where C=O+T, without calling either a causal or variance decomposition.
3. Retain the parent’s exact B1 versus Bdisc phenotype contrast as the primary fixed lactate-by-perfusion test.
4. Decompose fixed-queue capture into R and D. R is a record-defined transition and remains process-sensitive; D is timed hospital death. A positive A result confined to R, or erased by process adjustment, is process-consistent and cannot support a physiology-oriented interpretation.

A supportive result would establish only that observable measured values and/or the fixed phenotype provide reproducible retrospective ranking information beyond specified process proxies in this selected, ascertainable MIMIC cohort. It would not establish latent physiology, causality, treatment benefit, monitoring benefit, appropriateness or preventability of R, clinician intent, complete ward escalation, or external transportability.

## Population, landmark, phenotype and outcomes

Use the earliest valid ICU stay per `hadm_id` with non-null `subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, intime, outtime` and `intime < outtime`, joined to `hosp/admissions` on `subject_id,hadm_id`. Define `t_F=intime+12 hours`, requiring `t_F<outtime` and `dischtime>t_F`. Live at `t_F` means `deathtime>t_F`, or null `deathtime` with `hospital_expire_flag=0`. Death by `t_F` is ineligible. Null or contradictory terminal status is retained as U in the all-candidate flow and never treated as survival; the strict estimable set E* excludes unresolved U. Keep exclusion reason codes.

The feature window remains `[intime,t_F]), split into `[intime,intime+6h]` and `(intime+6h,t_F]`. A point measurement requires finite clinical time and finite `storetime<=t_F`; an interval requires finite `starttime<endtime`, uses clinical time for overlap and direction, and requires `storetime<=t_F). Clip intervals only to the feature window. No post-`t_F` clinical event or terminal field may enter a feature, even if it was stored early.

Retain the exact parent phenotype. Lactate is item 50813, with early and late medians each at least 2.0 mmol/L for persistent lactate. Measured perfusion is improving when late-minus-early median MAP is at least 5 mmHg and late target-pressor minutes are no greater than early minutes; it is worsening when MAP change is at most -5 mmHg and target-pressor minutes are no less than early minutes. MAP uses item IDs 220052, 220181 and 225312 in canonical mmHg. Missing pressor observation is missing ascertainment, not zero. Discordant or insufficient cases are indeterminate and retained descriptively. The 4.0 mmol/L, MAP-only, all-MAP and pressor-only definitions remain sensitivity analyses.

Follow `(t_F,t_F+24h]` and order the first state:

- R is the earliest new record-defined ICU-level transition: a target-pressor interval beginning after `t_F); a qualifying `icu/procedureevents` start for item 224385, 225792, 225794, 225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809 or 225955; or a later ICU stay for the same subject/admission with a different `stay_id` and `intime>t_F`. Intervals active across `t_F` are not new starts.
- D is `hosp/admissions.deathtime` in the window and no later than tied R, respecting discharge.
- S is an alive `dischtime` boundary before R/D.
- N is ascertainable with no R/D/S by 24 hours.
- U is unresolved death, ordering or ascertainment.

If R and D tie, D precedes R. The primary adverse target remains A=R-or-D, while R, D, S, N and U are always reported separately. R is explicitly not rescue, necessary escalation or treatment failure.

## Observable decomposition and exact estimands

Freeze one subject-level 70/15/15 split by `subject_id` before outcome inspection. Use the same strict E* subjects, labels, split, source whitelist, preprocessing and test queue for every decomposition model. Let `k_w=max(1, round-half-up(w|E*_test|))` for w in 5%, 10% and 20%, with the same k for every model and deterministic tie-breaking frozen before test scoring.

Define three non-overlapping information blocks, all restricted to the pre-`t_F` window:

- **P, measured physiology:** finite value summaries and early-to-late changes for lactate 50813, the three MAP items, and the prespecified organ laboratories (ALT 50861, AST 50878, bilirubin 50885, creatinine 50912, bicarbonate 50882, hemoglobin 51222, platelets 51265 and WBC 51300). P contains values, not measurement counts, store-time lags or careunit. It is measured physiology, not an adjudicated latent physiological state.
- **O, observation/documentation process:** per-domain number of records, number of distinct clinical times, valid early/late-bin indicators, missingness masks, and chart-to-store lag summaries for those labs/MAP observations; Foley 226559 presence/count and timing; and the analogous process counts for target-pressor and pre-landmark support records. Every O field requires clinical and store time no later than `t_F`. O contains no clinical values.
- **T, care-exposure/support process:** target-pressor interval presence and minutes, pre-`t_F` active/past support-procedure indicators, and support status available by `t_F`; no dose or rate pooling. The exact pressor minutes used in the phenotype are explicitly in T. Thus the phenotype is not called pure physiology.

All models include a common X0: age, gender, admission type/location, first-careunit, landmark transfer-careunit, elapsed ICU time and prespecified non-terminal admission context. Terminal fields, post-cutoff features, `last_careunit`, notes, `dod` and `anchor_year` are excluded.

Fit transparent penalized discrete-time cause-specific R/D/S models with a three-cause hazard head and derive 24-hour CIFs:

- **B0/X0:** the parent’s transparent latest-state/context baseline.
- **B_C/X0+C:** X0 plus O and T, without any P values.
- **B_P/X0+P:** X0 plus P values, without O or T.
- **B_CP/X0+C+P:** union of the same prespecified blocks. Include the exact parent B1 history terms in this union, partitioned by their block membership; do not add a new variable in one block only.
- **B_CPF:** B_CP plus the parent’s exact lactate-by-perfusion augmentation F: persistent lactate, improving/indeterminate/worsening perfusion, early/late lactate/MAP/pressor terms and their fixed interaction. This is the decomposition companion to the unchanged parent Bdisc.

The parent B1 and Bdisc remain the locked primary pair: B1 is the matched order-blind history model and Bdisc is B1 plus only the prespecified F terms. If the implementation’s B1 terms cannot be partitioned without changing their definitions, the decomposition is reported as a failed pre-fit binding gate and B1/Bdisc remain the only estimable primary pair; no post-test redefinition is allowed.

For outcome component q in {A,R,D}, define
`Cap_q(M,w)=TP_q(M,k_w)/N_{q,test}`,
where A=R-or-D and `N_{q,test}` is the strict E* test count for q. PPV uses `TP_A/k_w`; R and D are separately reported at the same queue. The primary fixed-capacity parent estimand remains
`G^F_A(10)=Cap_A(Bdisc,10)-Cap_A(B1,10)`.
Secondary parent contrasts are the same at 5% and 20%, and versus B0/Bmask.

The new decomposition estimands are conditional predictive increments, not causal effects or additive attribution:

- `G^P_{q|C}(w)=Cap_q(B_CP,w)-Cap_q(B_C,w)`: measured-value information beyond observable care/documentation process.
- `G^C_{q|P}(w)=Cap_q(B_CP,w)-Cap_q(B_P,w)`: observable care/documentation process information beyond measured values.
- `G^F_{q|CP}(w)=Cap_q(B_CPF,w)-Cap_q(B_CP,w)`: exact fixed phenotype augmentation after both blocks.
- The order-dependent differences are intentional. Also report `G^P_{q|X0}=Cap_q(B_P)-Cap_q(B0)` and `G^C_{q|X0}=Cap_q(B_C)-Cap_q(B0)`; do not call them unique physiological or process variance. Report the non-additivity residual `Cap_q(B_CPF)-Cap_q(B_C)-Cap_q(B_P)+Cap_q(B0)` only as a descriptive interaction of predictive blocks, not an explanation of mechanism.

The primary scientific interpretation is based on the matched parent contrast and the decomposition, not on model complexity. A physiologic-association interpretation requires that the exact parent contrast meet its existing fixed-queue criterion, that `G^P_{A|C}` not be erased by C, and that the D component not be adverse. If D has the prespecified minimum event support and its subject-bootstrap 95% interval for `G^F_D(10)` excludes zero in the same direction, this is stronger evidence that the gain is not solely tied to recorded escalation. If D support is inadequate, the physiology-versus-process interpretation is inconclusive even if A or R is positive. An A/R-only gain is reported as record-prioritization association and is process-consistent, not physiological proof.

## Evaluation, gates and falsification

Primary scoring is held-out fixed-k capture at 10%, with 5% and 20% secondary workloads. Report for every model: A/R/D capture, PPV, false-alert rate, Brier scores for R/D/S, calibration intercept/slope/O:E, reliability by alert status, and subject-bootstrap intervals. Report random-allocation capture and PPV benchmarks using the same k. The context-shift analysis remains locked leave-one-transfer-careunit-by-anchor_year_group-out fitting/scoring with pre-fit support, overlap and ESS gates, no context-specific tuning, and macro/event-weighted results for A, R and D.

Pre-fit gates:

- source/member/schema/dictionary/link/unit audit passes;
- index, join, time, duplicate, reversed-interval and no-future-time audits pass;
- B1 terms have a deterministic P/O/T partition, or the new decomposition is labeled not estimable without altering B1;
- strict coverage, ascertainable E* and component event support meet frozen minimums; otherwise report the relevant estimand as inconclusive;
- each supported context has pre-fit train-event, overlap and ESS support;
- no post-`t_F` R/procedure row, terminal field, discharge field, or `last_careunit` enters predictors.

Falsifications include:

- chart-time-only versus chart-plus-storetime availability;
- early/late permutation within subject while preserving masks/counts;
- phenotype permutation within coverage and process strata;
- complete label permutation within split;
- chronology shuffle of the learned model;
- remove T from the exact phenotype companion only as a diagnostic, while retaining the original phenotype result unchanged;
- compare high versus low O strata, with strata defined using training-only observation-intensity cut points;
- compare R and D capture, and report U adverse/non-adverse bounds;
- 2.0 versus 4.0 mmol/L, MAP-only/all-MAP/pressor-only, 6-hour landmark, bin-width and terminal-bin omission sensitivities;
- verify that no process-only gain is being relabeled physiological and that no apparent gain is confined to S or administrative follow-up.

Supportive evidence for the parent’s bounded prioritization claim requires the parent gates, `G^F_A(10)>=5` percentage points with a subject-bootstrap interval excluding zero, Bdisc above random allocation and Bmask, and no erasure under timing, permutation, U-bound, R/D or context checks. Support for the added physiology-versus-process interpretation additionally requires a non-erased `G^P_{A|C}`, a directionally concordant D result with adequate D support, and no material calibration failure. This is a stronger interpretation gate, not a replacement for the parent estimand.

Adverse evidence is a null/reversed parent contrast; a process-only model matching the full model; the exact phenotype gain disappearing after the prespecified process adjustment; a gain confined to R with adverse or unsupported D; persistence under phenotype/timing/label falsification; or context reversal. Such evidence challenges the record-defined prioritization hypothesis but does not prove treatment harm or absence of physiological association.

Inconclusive evidence is failed coverage, event, U, partition, overlap/ESS or context gates; sparse D; intervals spanning both zero and the 5-point decision criterion; unstable calibration; or irreconcilable timing sensitivities. No workload, cutoff, block, endpoint or gate may be changed after test inspection.

Computationally checkable outputs are source provenance, schemas and labels, flow, joins, times, block manifests, phenotype coding, split integrity, predictions, CIFs, R/D/S capture, calibration, uncertainty, context support and falsification results. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for latent physiological mechanism, appropriateness/necessity/preventability of R, clinician intent, complete escalation ascertainment, treatment response/effect, monitoring benefit/harm, net utility, patient preferences and external transportability.

## Baseline, learned alternative, resource plan and selection

The simple primary instrument is the transparent B0/B_C/B_P/B_CP/B_CPF sequence. It permits an auditable decomposition and makes the exact F terms visible. The matched parent B1/Bdisc pair remains the decisive phenotype-specific comparison; Bmask is the process-only falsification.

The substantive learned alternative is a small channel-separated masked chronological GRU, J-decomp, using the same E* subjects, split, labels, six two-hour bins and fixed source whitelist. One channel contains P values and a separate channel contains O/T masks, counts, store-time summaries, pressor/support indicators and X0; a three-cause hazard head produces R/D/S CIFs. Hidden size 32 or 64, dropout, five fixed seeds and early stopping are selected only on train/validation. Evaluate J-decomp, P-channel ablation and O/T-channel ablation using the same held-out A/R/D capture, calibration and Brier outputs. It can reveal time-local physiology-process coupling and recovery/reversal patterns lost by block summaries, but a J-decomp gain cannot rescue a failed transparent F claim. Chronology shuffling must attenuate any ordered-information claim.

Both alternatives answer the same question with the same target and evaluation. Approximate future-solver plan: CPU-first 16 CPUs and 262,144 MiB, within the 28,800-second solver envelope; B-model fitting and bootstrap are expected CPU-first but runtime, convergence, event counts and bootstrap duration are unmeasured. J-decomp is expected to fit on CPU at this small architecture; an allocated GPU may be requested only if measured repeated fits require it, using the cached GPU image, declared resources and `cuda:0` inside the allocation. Discovery had 7,200 seconds and did not fit models. The hardware inventory records eight A100-SXM4-80GB devices, but availability is not a runtime guarantee. No proposer/solver weight training is authorized.

A latent mixture or mechanistic latent-state model is deferred because this snapshot has no adjudicated stabilization or validated physiological state labels; it could encode selective recording rather than physiology. A large transformer is deferred because scale does not resolve the observable-process ambiguity. Treatment-effect modeling is deferred because intent, counterfactual response and adequate time-varying confounding support are unavailable. These are evidence-bound deferrals, not blanket exclusions of neural networks, GPUs or structured data. Revisit them if an adjudicated physiology label, treatment-response data, external site or waveform/image modality becomes available.

The actual deliverable is newly fitted or estimated held-out decomposition outputs: B_C, B_P, B_CP and B_CPF predictions in addition to locked parent predictions; conditional A/R/D fixed-queue contrasts; R/D calibration and uncertainty; process-intensity and context-shift stability; and a conclusion ledger that classifies the result as supportive, adverse or inconclusive under the frozen gates. Readiness only checks packaging and limited probes; it cannot establish the scientific conclusion.

The child is worth selecting over the mature parent only if it passes validity review and its added decomposition remains computable under the partition/event gates. It has higher scientific value because it can distinguish three materially different outcomes—measured-value signal beyond observable process, process-dominated association, and an R-only versus D-concordant pattern—while retaining the parent’s established fixed-capacity question. If the P/O/T partition or D support fails, the mature parent should remain selected; this child must not silently weaken the parent’s estimand or convert an R-only result into a physiology claim.
