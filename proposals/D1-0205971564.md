# Audit and substantive alternative: hypotension burden with observation- and treatment-aware estimands

## Status and scientific opening

This is a working design note for parent [prior hypothesis]. It is not a completed analysis and claims no results. The parent asks whether time-weighted MAP<65 burden adds prognostic information beyond nadir, baseline kidney function, and MAP observation density. The remaining ambiguity is substantive: post-index vasopressor treatment is described but not incorporated into the primary estimand; the creatinine endpoint is not robust to creatinine measurement intensity; and the learned model must test a scientific uncertainty rather than a generic prediction gain.

The strongest evidence supports only that hypotension duration/severity and dynamic MAP features are clinically relevant and associated with kidney outcomes in prior cohorts [K1] [K2], while an ICU prevention bundle was associated with some kidney outcomes but did not isolate duration or establish causality [K3]. The unresolved claim is whether burden contains reproducible incremental prognostic information after separating measurement opportunity, post-index treatment response, and creatinine ascertainment.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Include adults with parsed age >=18 and <=89, first ICU stay per patienthealthsystemstayid, and at least one valid MAP in vitalPeriodic.systemicmean or vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean during the first 24 ICU hours. Require at least one MAP<65 and at least six valid MAP observations. Exclude unavailable baseline creatinine, documented pre-existing dialysis/renal replacement therapy, and fewer than 24 hours of follow-up. Define the index episode as the first contiguous MAP<65 interval after the first qualifying MAP, with a 30-minute grace period for missing observations; repeat with no grace, 60-minute grace, and thresholds 60/70.

Create a 5-minute grid from the first qualifying MAP through 24 hours. Use last observed MAP without extrapolation beyond 30 minutes. Primary exposure is fraction of covered time below 65; secondary exposure is area below 65. Retain nadir, median, observation count, covered fraction, median gap, and periodic/aperiodic source mix.

## Alternative estimand

Use a nested decomposition of incremental information:
1. Opportunity model: predict AKI from pre-index severity and measurement opportunity only (age, sex, ethnicity, hospital, unit type, admission source, baseline creatinine, pre-index MAP/heart-rate summaries, and MAP observation count/coverage/gap/source mix).
2. Burden model: add time-weighted burden and nadir to the opportunity model. This is the parent comparison, but burden is evaluated after observation opportunity is fixed.
3. Treatment-response model: add prespecified post-index treatment descriptors—time to first vasopressor, class, dose/rate, duration, and treatment timing—only as a secondary decomposition, not causal adjustment. It asks whether any burden association is concentrated in treated patients or attenuated after accounting for treatment response. It must never be interpreted as mediation or the effect of withholding treatment.
4. Ascertainment-robust outcome model: repeat the comparison using the creatinine ascertainment design below.

The estimand is a conditional prognostic contrast in this selected ICU cohort, not a causal effect of MAP, vasopressors, or observation intensity. Treatment descriptors are post-index and may be colliders; they are descriptive decomposition variables, not variables whose adjustment identifies a mechanism.

## Creatinine ascertainment

Primary outcome remains incident creatinine-based AKI through 72 hours after index: increase >=0.3 mg/dL from the minimum creatinine in the prior 24 hours or >=1.5 times that baseline, using numeric labname=creatinine, labresult, labresultoffset, and labmeasurenamesystem=mg/dL. Stage 2/3 is secondary. This is not full KDIGO AKI because reliable urine-output adjudication is unavailable.

Construct three prespecified outcome views:
- strict view: require at least two post-index creatinine measurements and at least one in each of 0–24 and 24–72 hours, with no missingness gap >12 hours within those intervals;
- observed-trajectory view: retain the parent outcome but require the minimum baseline and qualifying post-index measurement to be observed, and report number and timing of creatinine measurements;
- measurement-intensity stratification: stratify by creatinine observation count and median inter-measurement gap, and fit the same burden models within strata.

The strict view is a sensitivity outcome, not a replacement for the primary endpoint. A burden signal that appears only in the observed-trajectory view and disappears in the strict view is evidence of ascertainment dependence, not a biological duration effect. If strict-view events are too sparse, report inconclusive rather than relaxing the criterion after seeing results.

## Baseline versus substantive alternative

Simple baseline: multivariable logistic regression for primary creatinine AKI with nadir, baseline creatinine, age, sex, unit type, hospital, and MAP observation density. Compare burden-only, nadir-only, and burden+nadir using prespecified likelihood-ratio tests, adjusted odds ratios, calibration, and hospital-clustered bootstrap intervals. Repeat in strict and observed-trajectory views and treatment-response decomposition.

Substantive learned alternative: a regularized gradient-boosted tree model on the same 5-minute summaries, with nested hospital-level train/test split and time-ordered split within hospitals. Target and feature blocks are the same. Compare opportunity-only, opportunity+nadir, and opportunity+nadir+burden models on held-out discrimination, calibration, and decision-curve net benefit. It can reveal nonlinear burden–nadir interactions and heterogeneity by baseline kidney function or treatment timing that the linear baseline loses; it cannot identify a mechanism.

A mechanistic discrete-time survival alternative with cumulative burden and time-varying treatment is deferred unless event timing and missingness are adequate. It is not selected because current creatinine ascertainment and treatment timing do not support a defensible causal or mechanistic time-to-event estimand. Revisit it if strict outcome timing and missingness are characterizable.

## Split, uncertainty, and evaluation

Use hospital-held-out evaluation as primary transportability: all stays from a hospital are held out together. Within each training hospital, use time-ordered split by ICU admission offset or earliest qualifying MAP offset, with no future rows in training. Fit preprocessing, regularization, and model selection inside training folds only. Use hospital-clustered bootstrap intervals for primary adjusted OR and likelihood-ratio contrast. Report calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, AUROC/AUPRC only as secondary discrimination summaries, and decision-curve net benefit at prespecified risk thresholds. Do not select a model or threshold on held-out data.

Key outputs: burden+nadir versus nadir-only incremental association and uncertainty; the same contrast in strict versus observed creatinine views; whether it changes by creatinine measurement intensity; whether adding post-index treatment descriptors changes the descriptive association; and held-out calibration/decision value of the learned alternative versus simple baseline. A small AUROC improvement alone is not scientific success.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive: burden adds prespecified incremental association beyond nadir and observation opportunity, with upper 95% CI for adjusted OR excluding 1 and likelihood-ratio test favoring burden+nadir; direction is stable across hospitals, thresholds, grace periods, and observation-density strata; signal persists in strict creatinine view or is explicitly shown not to depend on creatinine measurement intensity; and the learned model improves calibration/decision value without relying on post-index treatment. This supports a reproducible prognostic duration signal, not a treatment target.

Adverse: burden adds no information beyond nadir/density, reverses after density adjustment, is confined to one hospital/source, or disappears in strict creatinine view. This favors nadir, severity, observation practice, or ascertainment explanations and stops the duration claim.

Inconclusive: wide intervals, sparse strict-view events, or materially different results under creatinine ascertainment and grace/threshold choices. Report uncertainty; do not call an imprecise null a refutation.

Unavailable evidence includes validated full KDIGO urine-output adjudication, reliable pre-ICU baseline creatinine for every stay, clinician-confirmed AKI, complete nephrotoxin exposure, and causal effect of raising MAP. Clinical adjudication or prospective/controlled study is required for stronger claims.

No discovery result is claimed.

It must report uncertainty, calibration, decision value, sensitivity analyses, and a conclusion tied to computed outputs. It must not report a causal treatment effect or full KDIGO AKI claim.

## Alternatives not chosen

The parent’s simple logistic model is retained as the interpretable primary test. The learned alternative is selected because it tests nonlinear burden–nadir interactions and transportability, not because complexity is intrinsically valuable. A discrete-time mechanistic model is deferred because available creatinine ascertainment and post-index treatment timing do not support a defensible causal or mechanistic estimand. A neural sequence model is not selected: the scientific uncertainty is decomposition of observation, treatment, and outcome ascertainment, and a tabular learned model is adequate. Revisit a sequence model only if the 5-minute exposure representation is shown to lose information needed to distinguish rivals.

## Evidence limitations

The three parent key works are reused as frozen evidence: K1 Flick et al. (postoperative hourly MAP hypotension duration/severity and exploratory AKI association), K2 Ao et al. (dynamic/individualized MAP features and kidney outcomes with confounding and external-validation limitations), and K3 Muralidharan et al. (ICU AKI prevention bundle association without isolating hypotension duration or causality). Their frozen source bytes and receipts are attached to the parent and should be preserved for any child; no new paper claim is made by this note.
