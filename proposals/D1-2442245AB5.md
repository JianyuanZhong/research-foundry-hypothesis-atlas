## Exact independent trajectory and executable status accounting

Use clinical offsets and fixed half-open/closed windows exactly as follows, relative to `D`:

* baseline `B = (D-720,D]`;
* early `E = (D,D+720]` (0--12 hours);
* late `T = (D+720,D+2160]` (12--36 hours).

For every one of the four prespecified components—FiO2 (including the frozen source-label aliases), PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate—parse only finite numeric values under a parser manifest frozen before any label-stratified result. FiO2 percentage/fraction handling, admissible bounds, whitespace/case normalization, malformed-value handling, duplicate rows, and same-time conflicting values must be deterministic and printed. `respchartoffset` is the measurement time; `respchartentryoffset` is never substituted and is used only for a documentation-lag diagnostic.

Within each window/component, retain all valid observations, their count, distinct offsets, first/last offsets, and median. A component is observed only with at least two distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. A state is observed only when at least three of four components are observed. Missing components are missing, never zero or unchanged. The source-wide enumeration of every `respchartvaluelabel` and `respcharttypecat`, including matched/unmatched counts, is frozen before reading arm-specific endpoint summaries.

Component change is assessed from the B median to the T median using the fixed thresholds: FiO2 absolute fraction 0.10, PEEP 2 cmH2O, pressure support 2 cmH2O, and set rate 2 breaths/min. An increase/decrease is recorded only when its corresponding threshold is met; otherwise it is neutral. The primary trajectory class is mutually exclusive:

1. **Sustained de-escalation**: B and T are state-observed, at least two components decrease, no component increases, and E is not an escalation.
2. **Escalation**: B and T are state-observed and at least two components increase, or a newly documented invasive airway occurs in E/T after no invasive evidence in B under the frozen airway rule.
3. **Stable/mixed**: every other classifiable B/T row, including one-component change, opposing changes, or neither threshold.

The primary endpoint does not use care-plan columns, treatment strings, or post-D features. `respiratoryCare` provides the prespecified independent airway corroboration/airway sensitivity and is not silently used to repair missing numeric respiratory settings. The exact airway lexical rule (endotracheal/tracheostomy/invasive-airway term after source-wide value enumeration) and the interval rule (`ventstartoffset <= time` and missing or `ventendoffset >= time`) are frozen before label comparison. A sensitivity uses only nonmissing active ventilator intervals; all airway values/counts are printed.

For each frozen row, create exactly one mutually exclusive `endpoint_status` with precedence evaluated before any class assignment:

* `competing_exit`: death, discharge, or transfer at/before the end of `T` (`unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160`), including no opportunity to complete `T`; report `unitdischargestatus` and `unitdischargelocation` without inferring clinical cause.
* `classifiable`: B and T state-observed, with a three-class trajectory assigned; E missingness is recorded and can only prevent de-escalation if the specified escalation check cannot be determined under the locked rule.
* `non_exit_unobservable`: alive/in-ICU through `D+2160` but the minimum observations needed to assign the primary trajectory are absent.

A row with `unitdischargeoffset <= D` or an unavailable/invalid exit time is never silently treated as a respiratory outcome. For a competing exit, record separate `exit_before_washout` when `unitdischargeoffset <= D+720`; for an exit in `(D+720,D+2160]`, record the exact exit interval. If an ICU exit and a measurement share a boundary, the defined `<=` exit precedence wins. Report exit type, timing, and endpoint status for all 540 rows. Do not condition the primary denominator on a post-D care-plan record or on endpoint availability without displaying the all-row accounting table.

The primary descriptive contrast is evaluation minus no-trial trajectory-class risk among classifiable, non-competing rows, with row counts and uncertainty clustered by stay. Alongside it, report the complete all-row vector by label over `{competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}` and label contrasts in competing-exit and observability probabilities. No complete-case contrast may appear without those all-row denominators. The predictive analysis models the mutually exclusive all-row status/trajectory outcome so the label cannot obtain credit merely by predicting whether a row was charted; it separately reports state-class metrics restricted to classifiable rows and missingness/exit metrics.

Prespecified sensitivities are the exact 24-hour late-horizon version `(D+720,D+1440]`, the non-peak support/safety endpoint, the airway-corroborated endpoint, and the non-corroborated setting endpoint. They are named before execution, retain all attrition, and cannot replace the frozen primary.

## Hospital-held-out analysis and clustering

The primary transport analysis is leave-one-hospital-out. Eligible folds and arm contrasts are determined before outcome-stratified summaries: a fold may contribute a label contrast only if both labels and both relevant trajectory support are present in its test data; no artificial relabeling, pooled random-row split, or held-out target-rate calibration is permitted. For each held-out hospital, report test rows/stays, five status counts, arm counts, class support, endpoint observability, competing exits, and whether a contrast is estimable.

Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro multiclass log loss and Brier score for the all-row status/trajectory outcome, plus calibration where defined; separately report class-specific observed-versus-predicted rates and the classifiable-row trajectory metrics. Compare label-added against the identical pre-D state/opportunity model. Repeated landmarks remain together within `patientunitstayid` in uncertainty procedures. Use stay-cluster bootstrap with fixed seed `61061`, refitting the complete pipeline per replicate and per hospital fold (minimum 500, target 1,000; report failures and exact valid count before any gate). A stay-cluster label permutation within hospital-by-landmark strata is run only for strata with both labels, preserving arm counts; if fewer than 20 valid strata exist, the permutation gate is unavailable/inconclusive rather than replaced by an invalid null.

Do not claim transportability if fewer than five hospitals have at least ten classifiable, non-competing rows under each label and at least 50% of all classifiable, non-competing rows lie in those hospitals. This is an estimability gate, not a clinical negative result. All 74 hospitals remain in descriptive status/label/missingness tables.

## Falsification and decision gates

Run all gates from machine-readable outputs, with no result-dependent changes.

1. **Independent endpoint integrity.** Pass only if primary trajectory code is isolated from care-plan columns after D, treatment strings, endpoint-derived feature selection, and outcome-guided parser changes. Any violation invalidates the endpoint analysis.
2. **Observability and competing exits.** Supportive feasibility requires at least 40% of the 540 rows classifiable and at least 10 hospitals with both labels among classifiable rows; adverse feasibility is below 20% classifiable or below five such hospitals; intermediate values are inconclusive. Report competing exits and non-exit unobservability separately; neither may be called stable/mixed.
3. **Incremental information.** Supportive requires label-added models to improve both micro and equal-hospital macro log loss over the identical baseline-plus-opportunity model for the all-row status outcome and the classifiable trajectory outcome in the unbounded primary plus at least two frozen airway/observation sensitivities, with stay-cluster uncertainty excluding zero in the favorable direction and no reversal in more than 25% of estimable held-out hospitals. If label gains only observability/exit prediction, the patient-state claim is not supportive.
4. **Trajectory coherence.** Supportive requires the evaluation/no-trial direction for de-escalation and the stringent safety-concordant sensitivity to agree in at least two prespecified overlap hospitals when each has at least ten rows per label, with no early-versus-late reversal. A settings-only gain that disappears under safety or independent airway sensitivity is adverse to the stronger patient-state interpretation.
5. **Pre-D and lead-time placebos.** Recompute the same component-change rules from a pre-D placebo window `(D-1440,D-720]` versus `B`, and shift the exposure time to `D-720` while retaining the outcome window. A comparable or larger placebo increment, or hospital-direction reversal, is adverse to a newly informative post-label interpretation.
6. **Documentation negative control.** Predict post-window source density, distinct times, and entry lag from the label and pre-D features. An opportunity gain without a value-based trajectory gain is adverse to the patient-state claim.
7. **Component/source ablation and permutation.** Remove each setting/vital component and independently remove airway corroboration. A result surviving only peak pressure, one source, one hospital, or a nonvalid permutation stratum is not supportive. Compare the observed increment with the valid within-hospital-by-landmark permutation distribution; unavailable permutations remain inconclusive.

Supportive results would establish only incremental prediction of this eICU structured-chart construct. Adverse results would show failure of added information for this endpoint in this snapshot, not absence of bedside evaluation. Inconclusive results would identify inadequate observation, competing exits, hospital overlap, parser/source instability, or failed parent reconstruction as the next data requirement. No threshold relaxation, endpoint relabeling, post-hoc vocabulary expansion, or row-level bootstrap is allowed.
