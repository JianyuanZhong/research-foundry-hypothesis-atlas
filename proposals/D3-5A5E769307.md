# Working proposal (episode 15): Is the RA Mpos-macrophage -> FLS program a required MerTK-signalling step, or FLS-donor/composition abundance?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis]
(valid, gen 4, board leader mu 31.9; itself the repair of [prior hypothesis], seed D3-M2).
Seeds engaged: D3-M2 (directional macrophage->FLS crosstalk), benchmarked by D3-M1 (composition vs
within-cell state) and D3-M3 (persistence vs remission).
Status: hypothesis-stage, data-bound. NO executed result is claimed for this evolution; the parent's
exploratory E-MTAB-8316 screening is cited only as prior evidence, not re-run.

## 1. Unresolved question
Alivernini 2020 [K1] associates a MerTK+CD206+ (Mpos) synovial macrophage state with drug-free RA remission,
but this is cross-sectional. The parent branch tested whether the MerTK+ macrophage state is a functional
driver whose effects on synovial fibroblasts (FLS) require MerTK signalling. Its EXECUTED E-MTAB-8316
screen (6 paired none-vs-UNC106238 experiments, genome-wide) was exploratory and weak (mean effect
concordance r=+0.284, rho=+0.158, sign-flip p=0.029; one of six pairs negative). It left two blockers:
(a) only 6 pairs, no independent within-set discrimination between a genuine signalling requirement and
donor/composition structure, and (b) the ligand/receptor side (GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3) is not measured by the
targeted panels, so the mechanism cannot be resolved by co-expression.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim this episode will test:
H1 (signalling-required): the FLS inflammatory program induced by co-culture with Mpos macrophages is
   MerTK-signalling-dependent, i.e. blocking macrophage MERTK (UNC106238) shifts the FLS program.
H0 (composition/abundance rival): the FLS program tracks simply how many Mpos macrophages are present
   (composition dose), and MERTK blockade adds nothing beyond that composition dose.

## 2. Clinical importance
If H1 holds, MERTK in the resolution-state macrophage is a druggable effector node shaping the fibroblast
compartment, strengthening MerTK-agonist/resolution-directed strategies and giving a mechanism for why
remission-associated Mpos cells predict drug-free remission. If H0 holds, the remission association is a
composition marker without a required signalling step, which changes the therapeutic hypothesis (drive
macrophage composition rather than engage MERTK) and argues against MerTK-directed monotherapy. Either
resolution informs a real decision, and an inconclusive result bounds how much the 445-feature panel can
ever say.

## 4. Design (baseline vs substantive alternative, same question)
Composition predictor: Mpos fraction f in each co-culture (0 Mneg, 0.25 1Mpos/3Mneg, 0.75 3Mpos/1Mneg,
1 Mpos), parsed from sample_id and cross-checked against design.condition.
Signalling predictor: matched-composition Mpos +/- MerTK inhibitor contrast, paired by FLS donor and
macrophage donor (verified matched pairs exist, e.g. SA256A, SA257A, SA266A Mpos +/- inhibitor).

Baseline (simple): per-gene linear model log2(CPM+1) ~ f (pooled dose-response), plus a paired sign test of
the inhibitor effect within matched pairs; null = sign-flip permutation within matched sets.
Substantive alternative: donor-aware repeated-measures/mixed model with FLS donor and macrophage donor as
(grouping) factors, fixed effects f (composition), inhibitor, and LPS axis; plus a low-rank factor model
that captures donor/composition latent structure so a "program" is only credited if it survives removal of
the dominant donor/composition factors.

Discriminating predictions:
- H0: FLS inflammatory score rises monotonically with f; the inhibitor effect at matched composition is
  within the sign-flip null band.
- H1: the inhibitor shifts a coherent FLS program beyond what f predicts, consistent across matched pairs
  and not explained by the low-rank donor/composition factors.

Analysis target population: E-MTAB-8873 co-culture samples with parseable composition (all 33 fall in
Mpos / Mneg / mixed / not-co-cultured); restrict primary inference to co-cultured samples with f defined;
treat not-co-cultured as the f=0 reference. Genome-wide triangulation in E-MTAB-8316 restricted to genes
shared with the 445-panel where relevant, or analyzed genome-wide as an independent set.

## 5. Falsification / outcomes
- Supportive of H1: inhibitor effect at matched composition exceeds the sign-flip null (e.g. p<0.05,
  pre-registered) AND the effect vector is coherent (a known inflammatory/resolution axis) AND composition
  dose-response does not fully absorb it. Does NOT establish direct MERTK engagement; requires wet-lab.
- Adverse to H1 (supports H0): composition dose-response is significant and the matched-composition
  inhibitor effect is within the null band; then the remission signal is better explained as composition.
- Inconclusive: effects in the predicted direction but underpowered (n<=3 matched pairs for some contrasts),
  discordant across pairs, or absorbed by the low-rank donor/composition factors. An imprecise null does
  not refute H1.

## 6. Does-not-establish
No direct proof of MERTK engagement; no ligand-side (GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3) mechanism (absent from panel);
no in-vivo mechanism; no clinical treatment claim; E-MTAB-8322 is cross-sectional macrophage data only
(not whole tissue); small matched-pair n; UNC106238 selectivity/dose translation require expert review.

## 7. Method alternatives record
Baseline = pooled donor-unaware dose-response + sign test. Substantive alternative = donor-aware
mixed/repeated-measures model + low-rank factor model. The alternative can reveal whether an apparent
MerTK-signalling "program" is actually the dominant donor/composition factor; the baseline alone cannot
separate these. Selected: run BOTH and require the inhibitor effect to survive the donor-aware model.
Deferred: single-cell subclustering of original E-MTAB-8873 cells (cells column available) if pseudobulk
results are inconclusive.
