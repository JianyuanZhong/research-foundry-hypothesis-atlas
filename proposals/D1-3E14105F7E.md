# Compiler-ready eICU test of care-plan labels against a persistent respiratory-state trajectory

## Assigned evolution and substantive advance

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen 540 person-landmark / 443-stay / 74-hospital lineage, the five landmarks, first qualifying stay rule, exact care-plan labels, decision time `D`, inherited exclusions, and the bounded interpretation of all structured fields as chart proxies. It does not reopen the already completed vocabulary or transportability audits.

The unresolved clinically consequential question is narrower: **conditional on the locked labelled cohort, pre-D respiratory state, and pre-D documentation opportunity, does receipt of `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` rather than `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` contain reproducible information about a subsequent persistent numeric respiratory support trajectory?** The estimand is descriptive conditional association and held-out predictive information, not a treatment effect.

The substantive compiler repair is an explicit, deterministic execution contract. It separates (i) source-wide, outcome-blind parser freezing, (ii) parent-lineage reconstruction and byte/identity checks, (iii) endpoint construction with an unambiguous state-assignment order, and (iv) model fitting with training-only preprocessing. No endpoint row, label-arm count, or model is read or computed until the parser and frozen population checks pass. The primary endpoint is deliberately observable from repeated numeric non-peak support observations; safety preservation and respiratory-care corroboration are retained as independent secondary endpoints rather than allowing sparse airway ascertainment to make the entire question unobservable.

## Evidence boundary and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest evidence already supported by the parents is limited to exported structured documentation: exact labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated; prior label-to-setting associations are peak-pressure dominated and not transportably stable after component scrutiny; independent liberation markers are too sparse for validated durable-liberation ascertainment; and broader care-plan vocabulary does not rescue the earlier transportability result. Those findings do not establish that clinicians did or did not perform a daily evaluation or spontaneous breathing trial.

The untested claim is that, after adjustment using only information available by `D`, the evaluation label has a positive and reproducible association with a later persistent reduction in at least one non-peak numeric support component, and adds held-out information beyond the same pre-D state/opportunity model without the label. Falsifiers include no incremental gain, a comparable pre-D placebo, documentation-opportunity-only prediction, dependence on one respiratory field or hospital, label-permutation equivalence, or inadequate overlap/endpoint observability.

A supportive computation would support only incremental information in this eICU structured-chart construct. It would not establish bedside delivery of a setting, an SBT, extubation readiness, extubation, liberation, quality, benefit, harm, or causality. An adverse computation would reject this label-as-incremental-feature claim for this endpoint and snapshot, not prove absence of bedside care or prove that the labels are documentation conventions. Sparse support is inconclusive, not negative clinical evidence.

## Outcome-blind parser freeze

Before reading arm counts or computing any label-stratified endpoint, scan all `respiratoryCharting` rows and enumerate every raw `respchartvaluelabel`. Normalize only by trimming, collapsing internal whitespace, and case-folding. Freeze a parser manifest containing every raw and normalized label, assigned family, exclusion reason, numeric conversion rule, physiologic bounds, parser version and SHA-256. The manifest must be generated without joining to care-plan labels or inspecting outcomes. Unmatched labels remain unmatched; no site-specific synonym, threshold, unit conversion, or label family may be added after arm counts.

The only primary support families are FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set respiratory rate. Peak inspiratory pressure is parsed only for a predeclared ablation and is excluded from the primary. Numeric parsing accepts finite values after the fixed trim rule. Empty, nonnumeric, nonfinite, ambiguous, or out-of-bound values are missing. Bounds are field-specific, fixed before analysis, and recorded in the parser artifact; no conversion between fractions and percentages or between units is inferred unless explicitly present in the frozen manifest and supported by the eICU convention. If a purported family has no exact reproducible source mapping, that family is unavailable and cannot silently define an event.

For every component/window retain valid count, distinct clinical-time count, first and last offset, median, maximum gap, excluded-value count, and missing reason. A component is observed only if it has at least two valid observations at distinct `respchartoffset` values separated by at least 30 minutes. Duplicate rows at an identical clinical offset are retained for audit but do not satisfy separation; for a median, all valid observations are used after a deterministic `(offset, respchartid)` sort.

## Primary endpoint and explicit state machine

For each person-landmark define, using offsets relative to `D`:

* baseline state window `B = (D-720, D]`;
* pre-D placebo window `P = (D-1440, D-720]`;
* primary post-label window `O = (D+360, D+1800]`;
* fixed 12-hour sensitivity `O12 = (D+360, D+1080]`.

The six-hour washout `(D,D+360]` is excluded from the primary endpoint. Equality follows exactly from the open/closed notation and is tested in a boundary audit.

For a non-peak support component observed in both `B` and `O`, calculate `delta = median(B) - median(O)`. A repeated support-reduction event is the union of the following prespecified thresholds:

* FiO2: `delta >= 0.05`;
* PEEP or PEEP-CPAP: `delta >= 2` cm H2O;
* pressure support: `delta >= 2` cm H2O;
* set respiratory rate: `delta >= 2` breaths/min.

The event is not generated by a single value, a first/last pair alone, peak pressure, a care-plan value, or a treatment string. Component events, medians, counts, and deltas are always reported separately so a union cannot conceal component dependence.

Assign the mutually exclusive primary observation state in this order:

1. **competing exit** if `patient.unitdischargeoffset <= D+1800`; report discharge status and location. Also flag `no_post_washout_opportunity` when `unitdischargeoffset <= D+360`.
2. **repeated support reduction** if no competing exit and at least one component is observed in both `B` and `O` and meets its threshold.
3. **observed no reduction** if no competing exit and every support component that is observed in both windows fails its threshold, with at least one complete support component pair.
4. **endpoint missing** if no competing exit but no component has a complete, separated pair in both windows.

This ordering ensures discharge/death/truncation is not coded as either improvement or ordinary non-improvement. The primary risk difference is evaluation minus no-trial event risk among non-competing rows with a complete support endpoint. All 540 rows must still have the four-state distribution and attrition reasons. A binary predictive sensitivity models the event with missingness and competing exit explicitly represented; any inverse-observation weighting is estimated only from pre-D variables, hospital and landmark, with 1st/99th percentile truncation fixed before outcome review.

### Independent secondary endpoints

1. **Safety-preserving support reduction.** Require the primary repeated support event plus `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration` each observed in both `B` and `O` under the same two-observation/30-minute rule. Require post-minus-pre median `sao2 >= -2` percentage points and respiration change `<= +4` breaths/min. Missing safety signals remain missing, not safe. Report both-signals-required and at-least-one-signal-required sensitivities.
2. **Airway-corroborated support reduction.** Require a `respiratoryCare` record in `O` with nonmissing `airwaytype` and/or at least one nonmissing interval among `ventstartoffset`, `ventendoffset`, `priorventstartoffset`, `priorventendoffset`. This is availability/corroboration, not proof of device delivery.
3. **Airway transition validation sensitivity.** Use only a parser frozen before outcome inspection: a time-ordered exact `airwaytype == No Artificial Airway`, or an exact normalized treatment-string ETT-removal candidate followed by no invasive-airway respiratoryCare record for 48 hours. If fewer than 10% of rows in either arm are observed under this sensitivity, label it unavailable/inconclusive and estimate no association. Do not call it extubation or liberation.
4. **Observation and competing-exit outcomes.** Report post-window respiratory rows, unique clinical times, source presence, entry-lag distribution, missingness, and exit states by arm. These diagnose ascertainment rather than serve as patient-state outcomes.

## Pre-D predictors, opportunity separation, and no-leakage audit

The state-plus-label model may use only pre-D information: B and P numeric medians/trends; pre-D component availability and observation counts; unique clinical times and inter-record gaps; source presence; respiratory-chart entry lag; pre-D vital summaries; fixed `landmark`; and, in a prespecified sensitivity, pre-D `noninvasivemean`. The opportunity-only model uses pre-D counts, times, source presence and lag but no respiratory values, label strings, care-plan variables, or post-D rows. The state-only model is identical to state-plus-label without the label. A label-only model is descriptive and cannot be the comparator.

Do not condition the primary estimand on post-D observation. Complete-window results are accompanied by the four-state distribution and a pre-D-only inverse-observation-weighted sensitivity. Post-D opportunity counts are reported as negative-control outcomes only. A machine audit must prove that no `carePlanGeneral` feature beyond the exposure, no post-D respiratory value/count/lag, no treatment string, and no endpoint-derived field enters baseline. Joins must be one-to-many aggregated to one `sid,landmark` row before merging models, with duplicate-key and row-multiplication checks.

## Hospital-held-out analysis and clustered uncertainty

The primary predictive estimand is leave-one-hospital-out evaluation. For every held-out hospital, fit parser-dependent summaries, imputation, missing indicators, scaling, regularization, observation weights and calibration using training hospitals only. Do not estimate held-out label rates, endpoint rates or calibration intercepts. Include hospital and landmark only as prespecified training covariates/strata; never encode held-out outcome rates.

Compare state-plus-label with the identical state-plus-opportunity baseline. Report per-fold training/test rows and `sid` counts, endpoint-state support, label-arm support, row-weighted micro log loss/Brier score, equal-hospital macro log loss/Brier score, calibration slope/intercept, AUROC only when both test classes occur, and observed/predicted evaluation-minus-no-trial risk differences. Separate discrimination/calibration for the four-state observation process so a model is not credited for predicting missingness alone.

Uncertainty is a stay-cluster bootstrap, not a row bootstrap. Within hospital strata, resample `sid` clusters and move all retained landmarks of a sampled stay together. Refit the complete preprocessing/model pipeline in each replicate, fixed seed `61061`, target 1,000 replicates and minimum 500 if declared before outcome comparison. If fewer than 500 complete refits are possible, publish the failure count and classify the inferential gate inconclusive. Permute labels only within hospital-by-landmark strata containing both arms, preserving `sid` clustering and arm counts; use 1,000 valid permutations when at least 20 valid strata exist, otherwise report the exact valid-stratum count and do not interpret the permutation gate as supportive.

## Support, adverse, and inconclusive gates

These are validity/falsification gates, not p-value thresholds.

### Computationally supportive only if all hold

* lineage is `byte_identical` or `deterministic_lineage_match` with no identity, count, predicate, label or `D` divergence; source/schema hashes and parser freeze verify;
* in the unbounded endpoint and at least two of pre-L invasive-evidence bounds (latest invasive evidence within 24, 12, and 6 hours), at least five hospitals each have at least 10 complete non-competing endpoint rows per label, at least 50% of complete rows arise there, and neither arm has more than 40% from one hospital;
* both labels occur in the held-out folds used for the arm contrast, the evaluation-minus-no-trial event risk difference is positive, and at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions are positive;
* state-plus-label improves both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss over state-plus-opportunity by at least 0.01, with a stay-cluster 95% interval excluding zero in the favorable direction;
* the increment is not comparable to the pre-D or lead-time placebo, does not fall within the valid within-stratum permutation distribution, and persists after each component/source ablation without a leave-one-hospital-out reversal;
* missingness and competing exits are reported and do not explain the value-based gain. The safety/airway secondary endpoint receives its own availability status and cannot upgrade an otherwise unsupported primary result.

Even if all conditions pass, the conclusion is limited to incremental predictive information in this eICU chart construct.

### Adverse only with adequate support

Call the primary patient-state-information claim adverse when support is adequate and a prespecified failure reproduces in at least one bounded definition: no label increment beyond state-plus-opportunity; hospital or leave-one-out direction reversal; observed gain no larger than the pre-D/lead-time placebo or permutation null; dependence on one component/source; or label prediction of post-D documentation density without a corresponding value-based trajectory gain. Report adverse results with the four-state and opportunity denominators.

### Inconclusive

Call the experiment inconclusive for lineage divergence, missing source/schema/parser verification, fewer than five supported hospitals, no valid held-out arm contrast, sparse endpoint/airway/safety overlap, severe or unresolvable arm-specific missingness/competing exits, failed weight positivity, fewer than 500 declared bootstrap refits, fewer than 20 valid permutation strata when permutation is required, or bounded definitions that disagree while intervals span a meaningful gain and no gain. No relaxed threshold, pooled row bootstrap, outcome-guided parser, post-hoc vocabulary expansion, or redefinition of the 540-row denominator is allowed.
