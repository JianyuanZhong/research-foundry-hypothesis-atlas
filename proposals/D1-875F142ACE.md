# Provisional child: HCC trajectory value versus care-process artifact

## Proposed relationship
Among adults admitted with a documented HCC diagnosis, the incremental value of an ordered 7-day liver/coagulation/renal/tumor-marker laboratory trajectory over an admission snapshot for predicting a subsequent inpatient procedure should be smaller when the endpoint is restricted to procedures that are not part of the routine HCC treatment pathway and when treatment indication and documentation intensity are explicitly represented. The primary estimand is a held-out comparison of two predictive models, not a causal effect.

The clinically important unresolved claim is whether temporal laboratory information contains information about a later escalation proxy beyond what is already encoded by treatment indication, care intensity, and documentation practices. A procedure endpoint is a computable proxy for escalation of care; it is not a validated measure of death, ICU admission, clinical deterioration, or harm.

## Source-bound evidence and uncertainty
The parent source audit is available at work/lead/endpoint-audit.json. It reports 105,044 encounter rows, 101,457 valid admission times, 104,554 patient/admission keys with exact diagnosis 肝细胞癌, 338,040 procedure rows, 7,781 procedure labels, 321,207 timed HCC procedure rows, and 15,774 untimed HCC procedure rows. It also reports 20,052 HCC procedure rows after the 7-day feature window and before discharge, spanning 6,779 keys, but does not provide a validated clinical endpoint dictionary. The audit's frequent labels include both routine HCC treatment procedures (for example, TACE/arterial embolization and liver resection) and potentially non-routine/supportive procedures; this ambiguity is the main reason the endpoint needs refinement.

## Concrete refinement
Use a two-endpoint, source-resolvable design. The primary endpoint is a non-routine post-window procedure: the first qualifying procedure after admission + 7 days and before discharge whose label is not in a prespecified routine HCC treatment dictionary. The routine dictionary is built only from the exact procedure labels and their source audit, and is frozen before model fitting; it includes labels explicitly containing TACE/arterial embolization/arterial chemoembolization, hepatic resection, liver ablation, liver transplantation, and their documented compound variants. A secondary endpoint retains the parent's broader procedure proxy for continuity.

To make the care-process rival operational, add two prespecified controls. First, a treatment-indication control uses only information available by time zero: admission department, age/sex, baseline labs, and indicators for pre-window routine HCC procedures and medications from the exact procedure and medication tables. Second, a documentation-intensity control uses counts of lab rows, distinct assays, procedure rows, medication rows, and nonempty clinical-document text by time zero, with the same counts repeated as a post-window exposure only in a descriptive sensitivity analysis. The primary model may adjust for these process variables but must not interpret adjustment as mechanism identification.

The negative control is a timing-shifted endpoint: predict a procedure in the same post-discharge or post-window interval using only a prespecified non-treatment label set, while excluding any procedure that occurs before the feature window. If the trajectory model's incremental value is concentrated in the routine-treatment endpoint but disappears for the non-routine endpoint or timing-shifted control, the result favors a care-process/indication explanation. If incremental value persists for the non-routine endpoint after process adjustment and remains absent in the negative control, that is supportive evidence for temporal information beyond the measured care process. Neither result establishes clinical harm or causality.

## Computable experiment
1. Cohort: exact 诊断名称=肝细胞癌, nonempty 入院时间, and the four liver-tumor departments 肝肿瘤内科, 肝胆肿瘤与肝移植外科, 肝胆肿瘤内科, 肝肿瘤外科; report the unrestricted exact-diagnosis cohort as sensitivity.
2. Feature window: labs from 入院时间 through 入院时间 + 7 days and no later than 出院时间, restricted to exact assays 白蛋白, 总胆红素, 国际标准化比值, 甲胎蛋白, 肌酐, 丙氨酸氨基转移酶, 血小板计数, 凝血酶原时间, 尿素; retain specimen type, missingness, and do not pool unsupported units.
3. Primary outcome: first non-routine qualifying procedure after the 7-day window and before discharge. Secondary outcome: first broader qualifying procedure after the window. Exclude untimed procedures and report their count as an ascertainment limitation.
4. Baseline: penalized logistic regression with age, sex, department, baseline assay values, time-zero routine-treatment indicators, medication indicators, and process/documentation counts.
5. Alternative: a patient-level ordered sequence/state-space model with assay value, time since admission, specimen type, missingness, and time-zero treatment/process indicators. It can reveal persistence, ordering, and cross-marker change that the snapshot loses. Compare on the same patient-level held-out split, outcome, and uncertainty procedure.
6. Evaluate held-out discrimination, calibration, and uncertainty intervals; use patient-level splits and repeated bootstrap or nested validation. Negative-control timing checks use post-outcome measurements and within-patient order permutation.
7. Supportive: non-routine endpoint shows a prespecified incremental trajectory advantage with uncertainty excluding the trivial margin, while the negative control does not. Adverse: advantage is confined to routine treatment, disappears under process adjustment, or appears in the negative control. Inconclusive: too few non-routine events, unstable label dictionary, missing timing, imprecise intervals, or inability to adjudicate clinical meaning.

## Missing evidence and limits
No model has been fitted and no effect or clinical harm has been estimated. The source has no separate laboratory unit column, no validated death/ICU field, and no independent expert adjudication of whether a procedure represents deterioration. The exact non-routine dictionary and event counts must be frozen from the procedure source before fitting. Clinical experts are required to interpret the endpoint and any text-derived deterioration proxy. The available evidence supports a bounded prediction study, not a causal or mechanistic claim.
