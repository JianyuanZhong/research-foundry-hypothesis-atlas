## 1. The opening

### 1.1 The premise that is in question

The ALBI score — `ALBI = 0.66·log10(total bilirubin) − 0.085·albumin`, with grade 1 ≤ −2.60,
grade 2 in (−2.60, −1.39], grade 3 > −1.39 — was introduced to replace Child-Pugh with an
objective, purely laboratory measure of hepatic reserve, and that objectivity is the explicit
reason it is adopted as a *serial* monitoring endpoint. The randomized-trial secondary
analysis that is the closest prior work to the parent branch states the premise verbatim: the
ALBI score "is a continuous value calculated from the total albumin and bilirubin values, **is
not influenced by other factors**, and has few missing values, making the ALBI score suitable
for monitoring the clinical course of liver function" [K1].

ALBI is not a research endpoint in HCC. It is an allocation instrument. It gates repeat
trans-arterial chemoembolisation (TACE) in intermediate-stage disease, it supplies the albumin
point of Child-Pugh, and the Asia-Pacific consensus position is that ALBI grade 1 is the state
in which subsequent systemic therapy delivers most benefit, so that preserving measured
reserve is itself a treatment goal.

### 1.2 The specific anomaly

In routine Chinese tertiary hepatology practice the premise is false in a mechanically
obvious but, as far as the inspected literature goes, unquantified way: **intravenous human
albumin (人血白蛋白) is infused**, and infused albumin is measured by the same assay as
endogenous albumin. A patient given 20 g of exogenous albumin has a better measured ALBI
without one additional gram of hepatic albumin synthesis.

That exogenous albumin raises measured serum albumin is not the contribution and is not in
doubt; it is a pharmacological fact and it is the stated therapeutic target of albumin
administration in cirrhosis [K2]. What is unresolved, and what makes this a clinical rather
than a bookkeeping question, is everything downstream of it:

1. **Magnitude and duration.** How large is the offset in ALBI units per gram, and for how
   many days does it persist? Albumin's plasma half-life in cirrhosis is of the order of
   weeks, so the "shadow" may be far longer than the few days clinicians intuitively allow.
   Nobody can censor a measurement for a contamination window whose length is unknown.
2. **Exposure at the decision point.** Does the shadow actually overlap the moments when ALBI
   is used — the pre-course assessment before repeat TACE, the assessment before systemic
   therapy, discharge?
3. **Decision consequence.** Does ALBI measured inside the shadow still discriminate who will
   decompensate? A uniform offset that moves everyone equally would shift grade boundaries but
   need not destroy discrimination; a selective offset, concentrated in the sickest patients
   who receive the most albumin, would.
4. **Which component carries the reserve signal.** If the albumin term is partly
   pharmacological and partly inflammatory/tumour-biological [K3], the bilirubin term and a
   non-infusible synthetic marker may carry more hepatic information than the ALBI scalar.

### 1.3 Why this cohort can answer it, and what makes it a test rather than a tautology

The reason this is a falsifiable scientific question and not an arithmetic identity is that
the obvious rival explanation is strong. Albumin is given to **sicker** patients, typically
around paracentesis, ascites, sepsis and post-operative hypoalbuminaemia. Any rise in measured
albumin after an infusion is therefore confounded three ways: by the pharmacological bolus
(the claim), by regression to the mean and genuine recovery from the acute illness that
prompted the infusion (rival R1/R2), and by fluid shifts and haemoconcentration (rival R3).
A simple before/after comparison cannot separate them, which is presumably why the magnitude
has not been pinned down in the serial-ALBI monitoring literature.

This cohort supplies a **negative-control outcome** that does separate them. **Prealbumin
(transthyretin, 前白蛋白) is measured 184,917 times** in this lab table (OBSERVED). It is
synthesised by the hepatocyte, it has a ~2-day half-life that makes it *more* responsive than
albumin to genuine changes in synthetic function, and — decisively — **it is not a component
of human albumin infusion products**. The three explanations therefore make different,
checkable predictions on the same patients, in the same windows, from the same blood draws:

| | measured albumin after infusion | prealbumin after infusion | gram dose-response in albumin | gram dose-response in prealbumin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pharmacological artefact (the claim)** | rises | flat | yes | no |
| **R1/R2 indication + genuine recovery** | rises | rises concordantly | no (dose is not the cause) | no |
| **R3 haemoconcentration / fluid shift** | rises | rises (all plasma proteins concentrate) | no | no |

and a fourth prediction distinguishes the claim from all of them: the albumin elevation should
**decay on albumin's own pharmacokinetic timescale**, not on the timescale of clinical
recovery, and the two are separable because prealbumin tracks the latter.

The design is two-sided and the null is publishable. If the standardised prealbumin jump
matches the standardised albumin jump, the artefact reading fails, [K1]'s premise survives
contact with routine care, and the correct conclusion is that post-infusion ALBI improvement
is recovery — a useful negative result that would also retire the parent branch's Claim M.

### 1.4 Distinguishing what is already established from what is being tested

- **Established, and explicitly NOT claimed as a contribution:** exogenous albumin raises
  measured serum albumin; the albumin term of ALBI has non-hepatic determinants including
  inflammation and tumour biology [K3]; albumin infusion is common in decompensated cirrhosis
  and is dosed to a measured serum-albumin target [K2].
- **Measurement claim under test (M):** in routine care the per-infusion offset in measured
  albumin is of a size and duration that materially contaminates ALBI, and it is
  *marker-specific* (absent in prealbumin) and *dose-graded*, which no recovery or
  haemoconcentration account predicts.
- **Decision claim under test (D):** ALBI values recorded inside that shadow are a degraded
  instrument at the points where they allocate treatment — lower discrimination for 180-day
  hepatic decompensation, and a quantifiable rate of grade reclassification.
- **NOT claimed, and not testable here:** that correcting ALBI improves patient outcomes; that
  albumin infusion harms or helps; any causal statement about TACE and reserve.

## 4. The Harbor experiment (PLANNED)

### 4.1 Population and temporal boundaries

**Population.** All patients in the snapshot with at least one routine same-day albumin +
total-bilirubin pair (OBSERVED 237,309 pairs in 42,489 patients). Two nested analysis sets:
(i) the *offset set* — infusion events with an infusion-free pre-measurement of both albumin
and prealbumin and a post-measurement of both within 1–3 days of the infusion, plus matched
infusion-free control windows (size to be reported by the solver; 55.8 % same-day prealbumin
coverage and 91,061 exposure patient-days make it well populated, but the exact event count is
NOT yet measured and must be reported before any estimate); (ii) the *decision set* — the
120,512 decision-point ALBI values at the four index types of §2c.

**Temporal boundaries.** Index dates restricted so a 180-day forward window lies inside the
snapshot. Derivation on index dates before 2023-01-01, held-out confirmation on 2023-01-01
onwards, patients assigned wholly to one side.


Derivation on index dates before 2023-01-01; held-out confirmation on 2023-01-01 onwards,
patients assigned wholly to one side so patient-level and temporal splits are simultaneously
respected. Index dates are restricted so that a 180-day forward window lies inside the
snapshot for the prognostic analyses.

### 4.2 Estimands

- **E1 (offset).** The within-patient change in measured albumin attributable to an infusion
  event, in g/L and in ALBI units, as a function of grams infused and of days since infusion —
  jointly with the same quantity estimated for prealbumin, standardised by each marker's
  between-patient SD so the two are on one scale.
- **E2 (shadow duration).** The number of days after an infusion at which the albumin
  elevation is no longer distinguishable from zero. This is the actionable deliverable: a
  washout period after which a serial ALBI may be read as hepatic.
- **E3 (exposure).** The fraction of decision-point ALBI values that fall inside that shadow.
- **E4 (misclassification).** The fraction of decision-point ALBI values that cross the grade
  1/2 or 2/3 boundary when the measured offset of E1 is removed.
- **E5 (decision consequence).** The difference in discrimination (AUROC, with calibration)
  of measured ALBI for 180-day hepatic decompensation, inside versus outside the shadow, and
  the discrimination of the corrected ALBI and of prealbumin at the same decision points.

### 4.3 Outcomes

Primary clinical outcome for E5: composite hepatic decompensation within 180 days of the
decision point — diagnosis text
(`腹腔积液|腹水|肝性脑病|肝昏迷|肝衰竭|肝功能衰竭|肝肾综合征|食管.*出血|胃底.*出血|上消化道出血`),
or a paracentesis/drain order (`腹腔穿刺|放腹水|腹腔引流管`), or a later lab showing ALBI grade
3 or total bilirubin ≥ 51. Secondary: non-delivery of the next planned TACE course within 180
days. These phenotypes are regex rules over Chinese clinical text and require sampled clinical
adjudication before any clinical claim (§6).

### 4.4 Pre-specified falsification criteria

**Claim M (the artefact).**
- *Supported* if the standardised post-infusion albumin jump exceeds the standardised
  prealbumin jump by ≥ 0.5 SD with a patient-clustered bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0, **and**
  the gram dose-response coefficient for albumin is positive with a CI excluding 0 while the
  prealbumin dose-response CI includes 0.
- *Refuted* if the two standardised jumps differ by < 0.15 SD with a CI excluding 0.5, or if
  prealbumin shows a gram dose-response of the same standardised magnitude as albumin. Either
  would mean the post-infusion rise is recovery or haemoconcentration, [K1]'s premise stands,
  and the parent branch's Claim M should be retired.
- *Inconclusive* if the difference CI straddles 0.15–0.5 SD; the pre-specified response is to
  report the interval and the shadow-duration estimate and to make no reclassification claim.

**Claim D (the decision consequence).**
- *Supported* if AUROC for 180-day decompensation is lower inside the shadow than outside by
  ≥ 0.03 with a bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0, **and** this survives stratification on
  pre-infusion ALBI and severity matching.
- *Refuted* if that difference CI lies within ±0.02, which is a clinically useful negative:
  the offset shifts grades but does not destroy the instrument, so the correct response is to
  re-cut grade thresholds inside the shadow rather than to discard the measurement.
- *Inconclusive* if the CI straddles.

**Mandatory validity gate on the negative control.** If, in infusion-free recovery windows,
prealbumin does **not** track albumin (pre-specified: correlation of their standardised changes
< 0.2), then prealbumin is an insensitive marker rather than a clean negative control, and the
whole marker-specificity argument is declared not interpretable; the deliverable then becomes
the measured insensitivity itself plus the dose-response and decay evidence alone.

**Mandatory observation-process gate.** If follow-up to 180 days differs between infused and
non-infused decision points by more than 10 percentage points, the E5 comparison must be
reported both unweighted and inverse-probability-of-observation weighted, and any difference
between them larger than the effect being claimed voids the E5 claim.

### 4.5 Baseline and substantive alternative on the same question

Both are fitted on the same events, the same temporal split and the same outcomes. The
deliverable is the pair, not one of them.

**B — Within-patient difference-in-differences with a negative-control outcome (primary
pre-specified inference).**
*Unit*: an albumin-infusion event with an infusion-free pre measurement and a post
measurement of both markers. *Model*: patient fixed effects, outcome = standardised change in
each marker, exposure = grams (explicit-gram subset) or vial count (ordinal subset) and days
since infusion, covariates age, sex, baseline marker level (to absorb regression to the mean),
hsCRP, total protein and haemoglobin (R3), concurrent paracentesis, diuretics and
renal-replacement, era. Inference by patient-clustered bootstrap. The E5 prognostic comparison
is a landmark logistic model with AUROC/calibration inside versus outside the shadow,
stratified on pre-infusion ALBI, plus the same for prealbumin. *Cost*: CPU only, single node,
well under an hour including 1,000 bootstrap resamples (estimated; the discovery diagnostics
of comparable size ran in minutes). B alone can answer Claim M and most of Claim D; it is not
a straw man.
*What B loses*: it uses only paired, fixed-window observations, so it discards every albumin
value whose patient has no pre/post prealbumin pair; it cannot produce a corrected ALBI for a
patient measured once, which is the case at most real decision points; it cannot estimate a
decay constant, so the shadow length must be imposed as a window rather than estimated — and
the shadow length is the actionable deliverable; and it cannot separate "this patient's
synthetic function was already recovering" from "this bolus raised the measurement" except by
the aggregate contrast.

**A — PK-informed latent endogenous-albumin state-space model (the substantive alternative).**
For patient *i*, measured albumin is modelled as an endogenous synthetic trajectory plus a sum
of decaying exogenous boluses:

`alb_i(t) = L_i(t)·λ_alb + Σ_j κ · D_ij · exp(−(t − t_ij)/τ) · 1{t ≥ t_ij} + ε_alb`
`pre_i(t) = L_i(t)·λ_pre + γ·CRP_i(t) + ε_pre`

with `L_i(t)` a smooth patient-level latent hepatic synthetic function (random walk or GP),
marker-specific loadings, `D_ij` the dose (grams where explicit, vial-count with an estimated
gram-per-vial scale otherwise), `κ` the g/L per gram, and `τ` the elimination time constant.
Total bilirubin can be added as a third loaded marker. Output: a per-timepoint **corrected
ALBI** = `0.66·log10(bilirubin) − 0.085·(alb − exogenous component)`, with uncertainty,
evaluated prognostically on the held-out temporal split against measured ALBI.
*What A reveals that B cannot*: `τ` estimated rather than assumed, i.e. the washout duration
with an interval — the quantity a clinician would actually need; a correction available at
single measurements, including the 48% of infusion events that give only a vial count and the
measurements with no paired prealbumin, because prealbumin pins `L_i(t)` wherever it exists
and the latent process carries that information to timepoints where it does not; and a
decomposition of recovery into synthetic improvement versus bolus on one scale.
*Pre-specified ways for A to lose, decided before it is fitted*:
(i) **Simulation-based identifiability gate** — simulate trajectories with known `κ`, `τ`,
`λ_pre` under this cohort's actual measurement times, and require recovery of `τ` within a
2-fold interval; if not, report non-identifiability and deliver B only.
(ii) **Sham-infusion negative control** — refit A with infusion times replaced by
record-anchored sham times in non-infused patients matched on encounter structure; if A
recovers a comparable `κ`, A is fitting encounter-related dynamics rather than pharmacology
and is discarded. This mirrors the sham tests that overturned an earlier hypothesis on this
island and is aimed at this proposal's own favoured reading.
(iii) **Marginal-value gate** — if A's corrected ALBI does not improve held-out discrimination
over the B-corrected ALBI by more than its own interval width, B is the deliverable and A is
reported as a null methodological finding.
*Cost*: estimated 4–10 h on 16 CPUs for a stratified-subsample HMC fit, or a bounded GPU
variant (NumPyro, `gpus=1`, per `skills/phai-hardware/SKILL.md`) for a full-cohort fit;
**both figures are unverified**, and a bounded mixing probe on ~500 patients is the first
solver step and its own go/no-go. **No GPU is required for the primary claim**, which B
answers on CPU.

**Alternatives considered and not chosen.** (a) A deep sequence model over the full lab
stream: deferred — it could add discrimination but cannot report `κ`, `τ` or a corrected
value, and the question is explanatory, not predictive. Revisit only if A's predictive
performance falls clearly below such a benchmark, which would itself indicate unmodelled
structure. (b) An instrumental-variable design using ward-level or clinician-level albumin
prescribing propensity: attractive for the outcome claim, but the dataset's `就诊科室` is a
coarse department label and no prescriber identifier exists, so the instrument cannot be
constructed here; recorded as the evidence that would justify revisiting the causal version.
(c) Cholinesterase as the negative control instead of prealbumin: rejected on measured
sparsity (8,159 results), retained as a sensitivity check.

## 5. What supportive, adverse and inconclusive results would mean

**Supportive on M and D.** A marker-specific, dose-graded, PK-shaped offset with a measurable
shadow duration would mean that ALBI — read at the pre-TACE, pre-systemic-therapy and discharge
decision points where 25.9 % of values sit within a week of an infusion — is partly a record of
what the pharmacy gave. The immediate, checkable consequences are a washout rule ("do not read
ALBI as hepatic within N days of X grams"), an infusion-censoring requirement for every serial
ALBI monitoring study including randomized secondary analyses like [K1], and a correction term
for the albumin point of composite scores. It would NOT establish that correcting ALBI improves
outcomes; that needs a prospective comparison.

**Supportive on M, null on D.** Entirely possible and clinically useful: the offset is real but
discrimination inside the shadow is unchanged, i.e. the contamination shifts everyone similarly.
The right response is then to re-cut grade thresholds inside the shadow, not to discard the
measurement — a cheaper fix than censoring.

**Adverse (M refuted).** If standardised prealbumin moves with albumin and shows the same gram
gradient, the post-infusion rise is recovery or haemoconcentration, [K1]'s premise survives
contact with routine care, and the parent branch's Claim M is retired. This is a publishable
negative that closes a live concern about a widely used score, and the design is powered to
deliver it because the exposure is large.

**Inconclusive.** With the negative-control gate failing (prealbumin insensitive even in
infusion-free recovery), or a difference interval straddling 0.15–0.50 SD, the honest output is
the measured interval plus the decay profile, and no reclassification claim. An imprecise null
would not refute the artefact.

**What no automatic verifier can settle.** Whether the composite decompensation phenotype is
clinically correct; whether a given sub-threshold ALBI is a true functional state; and any
causal claim about albumin administration. Those require sampled clinical adjudication,
mortality linkage, or another study.

## 6. What this design cannot resolve (UNRESOLVED)

1. **Causality about outcomes.** Nothing here tests whether albumin infusion helps or harms,
   or whether correcting ALBI improves decisions. Those need a trial.
2. **Confounding by indication** is controlled for the *measurement* claim by the negative
   control and the dose-response, but not for any outcome contrast between infused and
   non-infused patients. Outcome contrasts are therefore reported as associational.
3. **Prealbumin is itself confounded** by inflammation, malnutrition, renal function and
   steroids. A flat prealbumin is not automatically evidence of no synthetic recovery. The
   positive-control check and CRP adjustment bound this, but do not eliminate it.
4. **Vial gram content is not recorded** (`N支`/`N瓶`), so dose is ordinal for that subset;
   `单次用药计量单位` is empty for albumin products.
5. **No mortality linkage.** Disappearance from the system is not death. In-system morbidity
   is the only outcome available.
6. **Phenotypes need adjudication.** Decompensation, ascites and encephalopathy flags are
   regex rules over Chinese diagnosis and order text; hepatic encephalopathy is expected to be
   under-coded. A sampled dual-reviewed adjudication (proposed: 200 decision-point windows,
   reporting sensitivity and PPV per component) is a prerequisite for any clinical claim and
   is clinical work no automatic verifier can supply.
7. **Single centre, one laboratory, HBV-dominant, 85% male.** External validity unestablished.
   Whether the albumin assay is BCG or BCP is not recorded, and the two differ in bias.
8. **No tumour burden, no BCLC stage, no adjudicated radiological response**, so tumour
   progression as a shared driver of both low albumin and decompensation is only partly
   addressable (AFP, PVTT flags, treatment sequence).

## 7. Deliverable

Newly fitted/estimated, and the outputs that establish completion:
1. The executed R1–R5 diagnostic of `work/lead/pk_diagnostic.py` with its pre-committed rules
   unchanged: the standardised albumin-versus-prealbumin contrast with patient-clustered
   bootstrap CIs, in infused and matched control windows; the gram dose-response slopes for
   both markers; the haemoglobin and total-protein rival checks; the positive-control gate; and
   the decay table.
2. The measured offset in g/L and in ALBI units, and the shadow duration E2 with an interval.
3. The §2d reclassification table recomputed with the *measured* offset replacing the assumed
   2/4/6 g/L.
4. The E5 prognostic comparison (AUROC, calibration, inside vs outside the shadow, stratified
   and severity-matched, with prealbumin as an alternative predictor) on the held-out
   2023-onwards split.
5. Model A fitted, with its three pre-specified ways to lose reported whether or not it wins,
   including the sham-infusion negative control and the simulation identifiability gate.
6. The adjudication sampling frame (200 decision-point windows) specified for expert review.
A favourable prediction metric alone does not establish completion; items 1–4 must be reported
with their intervals even when they contradict the hypothesis.
