# A prospective safety case for pulmonary-nodule admission routing: frozen retrospective gate, route-specific silent validation, and separable randomized trials

## Decision question and substantive repair

The consequential decision is whether a departure-frozen pulmonary-nodule handoff gate is ready to move from retrospective identification into a live but silent workflow, and, if so, whether either of its two routes is ready for a randomized workflow trial. The routes are deliberately different:

1. `A_AUTO`: clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation only when every observably active unresolved recommendation unit is a reproducible pure omission and no competing unit makes copying unsafe; and
2. `A_RECON`: side-by-side clinician reconciliation when any active unit is incomplete, conflicting, mixed, or interaction-dependent.

The parent correctly removed utility-derived decision curves and made the admission, not the recommendation unit, the primary decision unit. Its remaining bridge was nevertheless not decision-valid: auto-lane progression depended on an externally approved `D_MIX<=0.10` “purity” margin, while silent validation did not fix clinically meaningful route errors, their denominators, the assurance supplied by a given sample, or the exact failures that would stop or defer a trial. MIMIC cannot identify a tolerable exchange rate between an inappropriate propagated plan, an omitted active recommendation, clinician burden, and delay.

This child therefore makes no retrospective purity threshold and makes no claim that retrospective route prevalence identifies value. It preserves the complete retrospective design, including the frozen first-eligible all-opportunity admission denominator, reciprocal report/addendum chronology, unique canonical discharge artifact, corrected `G=DS_store-R_store` clock, exhaustive departure and joint uncertainty states, atomic recommendation units, conservative admission aggregation, timing controls, exact read-only source bindings, and strict noncausal limits. The retrospective experiment can nominate the **whole frozen gate** for prospective study only on identifiability, reproducibility, event count, and precision. It cannot choose a clinical error tolerance.

The prospective bridge instead uses fixed clinical error categories and route-specific denominators. Silent validation is a safety-assurance study, not a simulated utility analysis. Progression of a route requires enough prospectively adjudicated cases to show, with an exact one-sided 95% bound, that no clinically disqualifying route error was observed: zero errors among at least 120 denominator-eligible cases gives an upper bound below 2.5%. This is not a claim that 2.5% harm is acceptable. It is the assurance delivered by a zero-event sample; any observed disqualifying error stops that route and triggers redesign. Noncritical over-routing, defer burden, alert volume, delay, and inequity are estimated transparently rather than collapsed into a utility score.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claims, and hypotheses

The strongest available evidence-supported claim remains descriptive. The inherited complete-source, zero-sampling scan found 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 unique canonical `DS` admission keys, only 17 missing canonical `DS storetime` values, substantial ordinary discharge storage delay, and 3,764 retrieval-proxy pulmonary reports. These retrieval proxies have not been clinically adjudicated and are not prevalence, route, actionability, or harm estimates. The present child inspected the MIMIC source guide and full schemas but does not claim to have recomputed those inherited counts.

The unresolved retrospective hypothesis is unchanged in substance:

> Within the frozen first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity admissions whose canonical discharge artifact was stored by departure at least 24 hours after the denominator-defining operative recommendation component, departure-frozen evidence and unit-specific clocks permit reproducible separation of admissions in which every observably active unresolved unit is a pure omission from admissions containing an incomplete, conflicting, mixed, or interaction-dependent unit, while timing-limited and uncertain admissions remain separate. The separation remains stable under individual-reviewer, unknown-compatible, and unit-boundary bounds and is precise enough to freeze a gate for prospective silent validation.

This is falsified retrospectively if unit boundaries or superseding-plan facts are not reproducible; if compatible reviewer assignments cross clinically different admission routes; if unknown, timing-limited, or mixed cases prevent stable routing; or if route-specific event counts and simultaneous precision fail. Retrospective support establishes only that a versioned gate can be taken forward silently.

The unresolved prospective silent-validation hypothesis is:

> In consecutive live eligible admissions, a version-frozen gate can emit timely `A_AUTO`, `A_RECON`, `A_NOACTION`, `A_TIMING`, or `A_DEFER` routes from information actually available at each route time, preserve every new addendum or plan change, and produce no adjudicated disqualifying auto-route error among at least 120 `A_AUTO` nominations and no adjudicated disqualifying reconciliation miss among at least 120 reference-standard must-reconcile admissions.

A disqualifying auto-route error is not “lack of purity” in the abstract. It is a nomination to prepopulate when any unit is inactive, superseded, explicitly declined, contradicted, wrong in target/action/timing/responsibility, already resolved, dependent on missing context, or otherwise requires reconciliation. A disqualifying reconciliation miss is a reference-standard must-reconcile admission that the gate sends to `A_AUTO` or `A_NOACTION`; `A_DEFER` is conservative and is not counted as an unsafe miss, although its burden is reported.

The unresolved randomized-trial hypotheses, tested separately by route, are:

* among prospectively validated `A_AUTO` admissions, clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation reduces departure disposition failure versus usual care; and
* among prospectively validated `A_RECON` admissions, side-by-side reconciliation reduces departure reconciliation failure versus usual care.

Both are superiority hypotheses. No MIMIC-derived noninferiority margin, utility, or harm exchange rate is used. Serious intervention-attributable content errors and delays are monitored as distinct safety events and can stop a lane regardless of efficacy.

## Frozen retrospective admission denominator and chronology

Before reading any discharge text, discharge `storetime`, later trace, comparator outcome, or route outcome, create and hash the radiology eligibility manifest. The primary denominator `N_A` remains the first eligible admission per subject under the parent's rules:

* adult; an exact admission-linked radiology report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`;
* an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* a patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT recommendation at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging or oncologic surveillance, conditional or optional plans, explicit no-follow-up, in-hospital death, hospice/comfort-only transition under the locked rubric, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* do not infer nodule measurements, smoking exposure, malignancy risk, life expectancy, recommendation appropriateness, or missing context.

Report/addendum chains are reconstructed only from reciprocal, subject-consistent, admission-consistent detail links. Every operative component must have `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. A later material pre-`D` component supersedes an earlier component only within its reciprocal chain. Missing nodes, nonreciprocal or contradictory links, unresolved operative components, or missing/contradictory recommendation times are `U_R`, retained in the eligibility cascade and bounds, and never called omissions. Eligibility reviewers see no discharge artifact, `DS_store`, later event, comparator result, or proposed route.

The canonical artifact is the one `note/discharge` row with exact `note_type=="DS"` per `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. A duplicate canonical key stops the confirmatory run. Missing artifact, linkage, `storetime`, text, unreadable content, and contradictory content remain unknown. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

## Preserved departure, chronology, and joint states

For every admission in `N_A`, preserve the exhaustive departure state `Y`:

* `F`: `DS_store<=D`, classifiable, and faithful patient-specific pulmonary target, chest/thoracic CT action, and compatible exact interval/date;
* `C_O`: `DS_store<=D`, classifiable, but the patient-specific actionable plan is omitted;
* `C_I`: `DS_store<=D`, but target/action/timing is incomplete or discordant without a departure-frozen valid alternate resolution;
* `R`: `DS_store<=D` and contains a text-supported alternate resolution under the frozen hierarchy;
* `A`: known `DS_store>D`, regardless of later content;
* `U_D`: artifact, storage time, linkage, text, or meaning is missing, unreadable, contradictory, or unclassifiable.

These states sum to `N_A`; later fidelity never changes `A` at departure. Retain `R_store` for the operative recommendation component, `DS_store`, and `G=DS_store-R_store` in hours. Preserve `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, and eventual `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}`. Publish every zero and unknown cell of `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A` and its deterministic crosswalk to `Y`.

The primary ample, departure-available stratum remains `E_A={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. No `G<0` or `DS_store>D` row enters primary content-routing estimands. Verify the row-wise identity `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; it is a check, not an alternate opportunity clock.

## Recommendation units and departure-frozen evidence

After `N_A` is frozen, enumerate every pulmonary recommendation unit in each index admission that independently satisfies the same eligibility, chain, and pre-`D` storage rules. This creates nested `N_R` without adding subjects or admissions. The denominator-defining unit is the index unit. For every additional unit calculate `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u`; no admission enters an intervention route unless every active unresolved unit has `G_u>=24h`.

A unit is the smallest operative tuple `U=(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, recommendation polarity)`. Co-mentioned nodules with one shared action and due interval are one unit; different actions or due windows are separate units; repeated wording and reciprocal addendum restatements are deduplicated; and a material addendum change creates the final operative version of the same unit. Unresolvable one-versus-many boundaries are `U_BOUNDARY`, retaining minimum- and maximum-unit compatible inventories. Each unit carries `(subject_id,hadm_id,unit_id)`, source `note_id`, operative component, `R_store`, target span, action, due interval/date, polarity, and boundary provenance. Admission remains the resampling, multiplicity, and decision unit.

All retrospective packets contain only data stored by `D`. No post-`D` note, downstream event, POE row, later encounter, diagnosis, follow-up imaging, or outcome may be shown. Manifests record source rows, `storetime`, redaction offsets, and prohibited-field scans.

### Context panel

At least two independent physicians experienced in discharge review receive the operative radiology chain, admission/disposition variables, and canonical `DS` after a separate span team redacts every pulmonary target, action, timing, recipient, specialist, order, and target-linked preference span. They assign:

* `K_OPEN`: no observable departure-frozen, non-target context closes the possibility of pulmonary disposition action;
* `K_CLOSED`: explicit non-target context makes any such action inapplicable, such as death before departure or a documented global decision against all follow-up; or
* `K_UNKNOWN`: available context cannot establish openness because competing illness, preferences, outside plans, prognosis, or other facts are absent or ambiguous.

`K_OPEN` is observable compatibility, not clinical appropriateness. Reviewers quote support and name missing facts. A third redactor audits a stratified 10%, including every `K_CLOSED` case. Residual target-plan leakage or removal of non-target context in more than 2% of packets, with the upper one-sided 95% bound also above 2%, blocks retrospective routing conclusions.

### Unit-plan panel

A separate dual-physician panel receives the operative radiology unit abstraction and unredacted canonical `DS` stored by `D`, but not `G/H/V`, the context label, downstream data, POE, comparator results, intended route, or expected direction. It records quoted evidence and one state:

1. `S_DEC`: explicit target-linked informed non-pursuit or target-linked goals-of-care decision.
2. `S_DEF`: explicit target-linked definitive diagnostic or therapeutic management documented as replacing interval surveillance.
3. `S_EQ`: executable same-or-more-protective future plan for the same target, with compatible chest/thoracic action no later than the due window, explicit timing/date, and accountable recipient/team or documented scheduling.
4. `S_CON`: recognized target but conflicting or less-protective action/modality/timing, or mutually conflicting target-linked plans, without explicit rationale establishing replacement.
5. `S_PART`: target-linked text exists but action, timing, responsibility, active status, or relation to the operative recommendation is incomplete.
6. `S_0`: no target-specific plan or only generic follow-up language.
7. `S_UN`: target linkage, plan meaning, timing, or supersession cannot be determined.

The hierarchy is evidentiary, not clinical ground truth. Incomplete higher-level evidence falls to `S_PART` or `S_UN`; `S_CON` never resolves a recommendation; plans first documented after `D` are excluded; stale artifacts cannot demonstrate incorporation.

### Independent content panel and uncertainty

A content panel independently labels each unit faithful, wholly omitted, incomplete, conflicting, text-supported resolved, or unknown, without route labels or timing bins. Frozen code derives routes; reviewers never vote directly for an intervention.

Retain every pre-consensus label. A third adjudicator adds a label but does not delete the two-reviewer disagreement set. Primary partial-identification bounds range over all individual-reviewer-compatible assignments, explicit logical constraints, unknown-adverse assignments, and unit-boundary inventories. Consensus estimates are secondary. A retrospective route conclusion requires the same admission route under consensus and every compatible assignment.

## Frozen retrospective admission gate

For each unit in `E_A` derive:

* `U_RESOLVED` if `S in {S_DEC,S_DEF,S_EQ}`;
* `U_OPEN` if `K_OPEN`, `S in {S_0,S_PART,S_CON}`, and `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING` if `K_OPEN`, `S in {S_0,S_PART,S_CON}`, but `G_u<24h` or its clock is unresolved;
* `U_CLOSED` if `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER` if `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, `U_BOUNDARY`, unreadable content, or compatible assignments cross states.

Aggregate conservatively:

* any `U_DEFER` makes the admission `A_DEFER`;
* otherwise any `U_TIMING` makes it `A_TIMING`;
* if all units are resolved/closed, it is `A_NOACTION`;
* `A_AUTO` requires every open unit to be `K_OPEN + S_0 + wholly omitted`, every other unit reproducibly resolved/closed, no boundary uncertainty, and no cross-unit target/action/timing conflict;
* `A_RECON` includes any open incomplete/conflicting unit or any interaction that cannot be represented safely by independent copying;
* `A_MIXED` is a named subset of `A_RECON` with at least one pure-omission unit and at least one reconciliation unit. It is never an auto success.

`A_AUTO` nominates clinician-confirmed prepopulation only; it never licenses silent insertion. The admission-level worst-case rule prevents a copyable majority of units from hiding one dangerous minority unit.

* `B_AUTO=100*n(E_A and A_AUTO)/N_A`;

* `B_DEFER=100*n(E_A and A_DEFER)/N_A`;
* `B_TIMING=100*n(E_A and A_TIMING)/N_A`;
* `B_NOACTION=100*n(E_A and A_NOACTION)/N_A`;

* `RD_ROUTE=[n(A_AUTO)-n(A_RECON)]/N_A` and `RD_MIX=n(A_MIXED)/N_A`; and
* the full inherited `Y/Z` to route reclassification table, retaining zeros and unknowns.

Recommendation-unit workloads are secondary: units per 100 index admissions; unit-state fractions; unit-count distribution; within-admission correlation; and `D_MIX=P(admission is A_RECON or A_DEFER | admission contains at least one unit-level pure omission)`. `D_MIX` remains a clinically interpretable warning about unit-only routing, but **no value of `D_MIX` is an acceptance margin or intervention-selection rule**.

The retrospective study nominates the whole frozen gate for P1 only if source, chronology, redaction, reliability, event, precision, and compatible-bound gates pass, at least one actionable route has a simultaneous lower bound above zero, and the route code can be frozen without a post-outcome change. It does not choose auto-first, recon-first, or a utility-optimal lane. P1 accrues all routes; each route earns progression separately from prospective safety evidence. If `A_AUTO` is absent, sparse, or unstable, P1 may validate `A_RECON` only. If both are sparse or unstable, no content workflow proceeds.

The full `N_A` is reviewed; a pre-confirmatory pilot of 50 admissions may refine only packet mechanics, redaction, and the unit-boundary manual, after which labels are wiped and protocol and hashes frozen. These inherited feasibility thresholds do not represent patient-harm utilities.

Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates. Each replicate carries all `Y`, `J_A`, unit inventories, routes, burdens, reclassification, timing controls, comparator weights, and uncertainty bounds. Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family for `B_AUTO,B_RECON,B_MIXED,B_DEFER,B_TIMING,RD_ROUTE,D_MIX`, the portable comparator contrast, and retained availability contrasts. Recommendation units never receive independent weights.

Report pre-consensus confusion matrices, raw and category-specific agreement, kappa and Gwet AC1 sensitivity, reviewer-specific routes, confidence, and uncertainty reasons. Retain the reliability gates of at least 90% raw agreement and kappa or AC1 at least 0.70 for eligibility, unit boundary, `K`, `S`, content, and final derived route; consensus does not repair failure. ## Phase P1: prospective silent validation

### Population, clock, and immutable versions

At each participating site, prospectively enroll every consecutive adult inpatient admission in which a newly finalized or materially amended admission-linked radiology report triggers the frozen pulmonary eligibility extractor before discharge transmission. The enrollment denominator `N_LIVE` includes all triggers, duplicates, later-ineligible adjudications, routes, technical failures, and missing-field cases; nothing is dropped because the system deferred or failed. Link repeat emissions within an admission and count the admission once for primary route denominators, while retaining every emission for stale-route and alert analyses.

The system runs silently: no recommendation content, route, or alert is displayed to clinicians and no clinical artifact is modified. Freeze extractor version, unit parser, eligibility rules, route code, vocabulary, user-interface mock payload, source-event mappings, and hashes before accrual. The route is recomputed after every material addendum, discharge-plan change, explicit preference/goals entry, linked order/referral/scheduling event, or responsibility change. Every old route is invalidated; failure to retract or update a route is adjudicated as a stale-route error.

Use real prospective timestamps, not MIMIC proxies:

* `T_report_final`: source-system final signature time;
* `T_report_available`: time the final report or addendum becomes available in the EHR used by the discharge team;
* `T_trigger`: extractor receipt and route-computation time;
* `T_route`: immutable route emission time;
* `T_draft_k`: creation and every saved discharge-draft version;
* `T_sign`: responsible clinician signature/lock time;
* `T_transmit`: patient/recipient transmission time; and
* `T_depart`: physical departure time.

A route can be trial-eligible only if emitted from the current report/plan state before `T_sign`; late routes are `P_TIMING`, never content failures or trial candidates.

### Prospective reference standard

Two independent physicians with pulmonary/radiology follow-up and discharge-workflow expertise adjudicate all route-positive admissions, all system defers, all apparent no-action cases, and a fixed random 20% sample of trigger-negative/ineligible admissions. They are blinded to the silent route and to post-departure outcomes. They receive all information verifiably available by each route time, including report versions, plan and order/referral/scheduling states, preferences, goals, outside-plan attestation, responsibility, and discharge drafts. They first adjudicate each route-time snapshot, then the final pre-transmission snapshot. A third physician adjudicates disagreement without erasing the original compatible set.

At each snapshot the reference admission state is:

* `P_PROP_COMPAT`: every active unresolved unit is a pure omission; the exact proposed target/action/timing/responsibility payload is current and correct; no unit is suppressed, superseded, declined, conflicting, already resolved, or dependent on unavailable context;
* `P_MUST_RECON`: at least one active unit is partial, conflicting, mixed, newly changed, suppressed/overridden without accepted resolution, or interaction-dependent, such that independent prepopulation is unsafe;
* `P_RESOLVED`: all units have explicit, current, target-linked accepted resolution, definitive replacement, informed non-pursuit, or completed accountable plan;
* `P_TIMING`: the current report/plan state was not available early enough for a route before signature; or
* `P_INDET`: required context, unit boundary, active status, responsibility, or evidence remains indeterminate.

Clinical guideline appropriateness beyond the chart is not silently inferred. If deciding whether a recommendation should remain active requires images, outside records, longitudinal history, or specialist judgment not captured prospectively, the state is `P_INDET`; it does not become an auto-compatible success.

A separate endpoint panel, blinded to route and later trial arm, compares the signed/transmitted artifact and accountable actions with the reference units. A safety panel may be unblinded only when attribution to the route or intervention is required. Adjudicators quote evidence, source event IDs, event times, and the missing fact that would change an indeterminate label.

### Clinically fixed error categories

The following categories are mutually auditable and are never combined into a utility score:

1. **Disqualifying auto-route error (`E_AUTO`)**: the gate emits `A_AUTO` when the route-time reference is not `P_PROP_COMPAT`. Subtypes are inactive/already resolved; explicit informed decline or global non-pursuit; definitive or equivalent superseding plan; active conflicting/partial/mixed plan; wrong target; wrong modality/action; wrong timing/date; wrong or missing responsibility; unresolved unit boundary; missing decision-relevant context; stale report/addendum; or stale plan state.
2. **Disqualifying reconciliation miss (`E_RECON_MISS`)**: the reference is `P_MUST_RECON`, but the gate emits `A_AUTO` or `A_NOACTION`. A conservative `A_DEFER` is not an unsafe miss; it is counted in defer burden.
3. **Inappropriate propagated plan (`AE_PROP`)**: in P2, intervention-supplied content remains in the signed or transmitted artifact although the contemporaneous reference says it is inactive, superseded, declined, conflicting, wrong in target/action/timing/responsibility, duplicated, or indeterminate. This includes stale content not withdrawn after a material addendum or plan change.
4. **Suppressed or overridden active recommendation (`AE_SUPPRESS`)**: the system hides, downgrades, deletes, or fails to redisplay an adjudicated active unit after an addendum or plan change; or an intervention-induced clinician action removes/overrides it without an explicit target-linked clinically accepted rationale. Clinician disagreement with radiology is not itself an error if explicitly reconciled with rationale and responsibility.
5. **Failure to reconcile (`AE_UNRECON`)**: by `T_transmit`, at least one active unit lacks an explicit disposition of accept, modify/replace, or decline, together with target linkage, action, timing when follow-up remains active, accountable recipient, and rationale for modification/decline.
6. **Alert burden (`AE_ALERT`)**: route notifications, reminders, duplicates, post-acknowledgement alerts, dismissals, active-screen time, and clinician-reported interruption. The intervention is technically limited to one initial route notification per admission and one reminder only if unacknowledged; any duplicate, wrong-recipient, or post-acknowledgement alert is a protocol deviation.
7. **Delay (`AE_DELAY`)**: report-availability-to-route, route-to-view, route-to-disposition, discharge-ready-to-sign, sign-to-transmit, and order/referral/scheduling times, plus any independently adjudicated intervention-attributable missed transport, medication, appointment, discharge day, or clinically necessary action. Continuous times and prespecified 50th/90th percentiles are reported; MIMIC supplies no “acceptable” delay cutoff.
8. **Failure of communication or execution (`AE_EXEC`)**: no accountable recipient, no documented communication receipt, no target-linked order/referral/scheduling when the accepted disposition requires it, cancellation without reconciliation, or noncompletion by the due window. These are prospective process outcomes, not inferable from MIMIC.
9. **Inequity (`AE_EQUITY`)**: differences in trigger eligibility, route, defer, `E_AUTO`, `E_RECON_MISS`, `AE_PROP`, `AE_UNRECON`, alert burden, or delay across lawfully measured groups. These contrasts are descriptive with simultaneous intervals unless a separately powered protocol is mounted; absent or structurally missing attributes prohibit an equity claim.

### Route-specific denominators and confirmatory P1 estimands

Publish a complete flow from `N_LIVE`. The following denominators are fixed and cannot be changed after labels:

* `N_AUTO`: every admission with at least one silent `A_AUTO` emission from the current state before signature. If any emission is disqualifying, the admission is an error even if a later rerun corrects it.
* `N_REF_RECON`: every adjudicated admission-time snapshot whose compatible reference set contains `P_MUST_RECON`; snapshots are aggregated to admission level by worst observed unsafe miss. Admissions routed `A_DEFER` remain in this denominator but are not unsafe misses.
* `N_GATE_RECON`: every admission with a timely `A_RECON` emission.
* `N_ALL_ELIG`: all prospectively adjudicated eligible admissions, including timing, defer, technical failure, missing fields, and no action.

The two confirmatory P1 estimands are:

* `p_AUTO=E_AUTO/N_AUTO`; and
* `p_RECON_MISS=E_RECON_MISS/N_REF_RECON`.

Each uses an exact one-sided 95% binomial upper confidence bound with admission as the unit and a site-cluster sensitivity by cluster bootstrap. A route passes silent safety assurance only with **zero observed errors and at least 120 denominator admissions**, for which the exact one-sided upper bound is below 2.5%. One or more errors fails the route; there is no acceptable-error margin to negotiate after seeing results. Correlated repeat emissions cannot inflate the denominator.

For an auto-lane trial, both `N_AUTO>=120` with zero `E_AUTO` and `N_REF_RECON>=120` with zero `E_RECON_MISS` are required because auto safety depends on both positive-route compatibility and not diverting must-reconcile cases. For a reconciliation-lane trial, require `N_GATE_RECON>=120`, no gate-induced suppression of an active unit, and at least 120 adjudicated reference must-reconcile admissions to characterize capture; over-routing of auto-compatible cases to reconciliation is not called a safety success and is reported as workload.

Descriptive P1 outputs, always with numerator and route-specific denominator, are the full route/reference confusion matrix; defer and timing rates over `N_ALL_ELIG`; false ineligible/trigger-negative rate from the 20% sample; stale-route and rerun rates; route timeliness; wrong-recipient and duplicate-alert simulations; route burden by site/service; inter-reviewer compatible bounds; and all nine adverse-category precursors. No descriptive value can rescue a failed confirmatory safety category.

### P1 accrual, precision, and progression rules

Accrue until each proposed trial lane reaches its stated denominators or 18 months, whichever occurs first. At least two sites and at least two service families must contribute to any multisite progression claim; report site-specific denominators rather than pooling away a site failure. A lane is deferred, not declared safe, if it fails to accrue 120 denominator cases, if adjudication-compatible assignments change a disqualifying classification, if safety-critical fields are missing in a would-be route-positive case, if event ordering cannot be reconstructed, or if the frozen code changes.

Required P1 progression conditions are:

1. source-to-route audit completeness and replayability for every route-positive case;
2. no wrong-patient/cross-encounter payload, nonreciprocal addendum, prohibited post-route hindsight, or unversioned input;
3. the zero-event/120-case confirmatory rules for the relevant lane;
4. the inherited 90% raw agreement plus kappa or AC1 at least 0.70 for eligibility, unit boundary, reference state, endpoint content, and derived route, with compatible-set stability;
5. every trial-eligible route emitted before signature from the latest state and invalidated after each material change;
6. no unresolved serious workflow or privacy event; and
7. a frozen trial protocol, UI, alert cap, responsibility mapping, data dictionary, adjudication manual, and statistical code before randomization.

Any `E_AUTO` stops auto progression. Any `E_RECON_MISS` caused by auto/no-action routing stops auto progression and requires whole-gate review. A wrong-patient payload, ignored explicit non-pursuit, ignored active override, stale payload after a material addendum, or loss of audit provenance immediately pauses all silent runs. A changed extractor, route rule, context field, payload schema, or UI is a new gate version and must restart P1; it cannot be called a mechanical repair.

Equity results do not receive an underpowered pass/fail label. Every critical error is shown by measured group. A critical error concentrated in any group stops the affected lane just as it would in the full population. Sparse groups remain explicitly uncertain and require enhanced P2 monitoring; absence of measurable attributes blocks an equity conclusion.

## Phase P2: two separable route-stratified randomized workflow trials

### Trial population and randomization

Only a P1-passed route may enter P2. Continue consecutive live screening and report the full `N_LIVE` flow. Before clinicians see any route, randomize eligible admissions 1:1 within the frozen route stratum, site, service family, and report-to-predicted-discharge band. Use patient/admission-level allocation with concealed assignment and retain clinician clustering in analysis. If a site cannot prevent contamination under individual randomization, it must prespecify cluster-period randomization and inflate sample size using the P1 upper confidence limit for the intracluster correlation; it may not switch designs after outcome inspection.

`A_AUTO` intervention is exact structured prepopulation beside the source recommendation. Nothing enters the signed/transmitted artifact until the responsible clinician affirmatively accepts or edits every unit; reject and defer are always available; every material source or plan change invalidates prior acceptance. `A_RECON` intervention is a side-by-side display of source recommendation, current discharge plan, orders/referrals/scheduling, and explicit per-unit choices to accept, modify/replace, decline, or defer, with rationale and accountable recipient. Control is usual discharge workflow without gate display or alerts. The two intervention arms and analyses remain separate.

### Confirmatory trial endpoints

For every randomized admission, independent blinded endpoint adjudicators use the final contemporaneous reference and signed/transmitted artifact.

* **Auto-lane primary endpoint: departure disposition failure.** Numerator: any active unit is omitted, incomplete, conflicting, inaccurately propagated, unresolved, or lacks accountable responsibility at `T_transmit`. Denominator: all randomized P1-valid `A_AUTO` admissions, analyzed by intention to treat.
* **Reconciliation-lane primary endpoint: departure reconciliation failure.** Numerator: any active unit lacks explicit accept, modify/replace, or decline disposition with required target/action/timing/responsibility and rationale where applicable at `T_transmit`. Denominator: all randomized P1-valid `A_RECON` admissions, analyzed by intention to treat.

Each lane tests superiority of intervention versus usual care using an adjusted risk difference with site and randomization strata fixed effects and clinician-cluster-robust uncertainty. The two lane hypotheses form one Holm-controlled two-sided familywise alpha 0.05. Report unadjusted risks, adjusted risk difference, risk ratio, 95% simultaneous intervals, missing-outcome adverse bounds, and per-protocol use descriptively. A supportive lane requires its Holm-adjusted interval for the risk difference to exclude zero in the favorable direction and no safety stop. Failure to reject is inconclusive, not equivalence or proof of no value.

The initial planning target is at least 350 admissions per arm per lane. This supplies approximately 90% power at a two-sided 0.025 test to detect a reduction from 20% to 10% under independent binary outcomes; these are prospective planning assumptions, not MIMIC estimates or an acceptance margin. Before randomization, inflate for the upper P1 estimate of clinician/site clustering and fixed expected loss of endpoint ascertainment; never reduce below 350 per arm. If the control rate is lower or clustering larger, the result may be inconclusive. No unblinded sample-size adaptation or post hoc endpoint redefinition is allowed.

### Trial safety, burden, delay, execution, and equity outcomes

`AE_PROP` and `AE_SUPPRESS` are individually listed sentinel safety outcomes in both lanes, with exact confidence bounds and independent attribution. `AE_UNRECON` is the route-specific primary failure where applicable and is also decomposed by target, action, timing, responsibility, and rationale. Report `AE_ALERT` per 100 randomized admissions and per exposed clinician, number of admissions with more than the allowed one alert plus one reminder, dismissals and reasons, active-screen time, and clinician-reported interruption. Report all `AE_DELAY` clocks as distributions and paired workflow components; separately list every adjudicated intervention-attributable missed transport, missed medication, missed appointment, extra discharge day, or delayed clinically necessary action. Report `AE_EXEC` at departure, 30 days, and the recommendation due window, with loss to follow-up separate.

Report outcomes and route allocation by the prespecified measurable equity groups, with missingness, group-specific numerator/denominator, simultaneous intervals, and treatment-by-group interaction estimates. These are descriptive unless separately powered. Do not infer race, ethnicity, gender, language, disability, deprivation, or rurality from names or free text. A trial can establish average workflow effects while remaining unable to establish equity in sparse or unmeasured groups.

### Safety pauses, stopping, and defer rules

An independent safety board reviews masked aggregate efficacy but unmasked sentinel harms after 25, 50, 100, and each additional 100 intervention-arm admissions per lane. There is no early efficacy stopping. Immediately pause the affected lane after any independently confirmed intervention-attributable:

* wrong-patient or wrong-target payload;
* plan that contradicts an explicit informed decline, goals-of-care decision, definitive replacement, or current active clinician recommendation and persists to signature/transmission;
* wrong modality/action or due time that persists to signature/transmission;
* system suppression of an active recommendation or failure to retract stale content after an addendum/plan change;
* unauthorized silent insertion, cross-encounter disclosure, or loss of the audit trail; or
* delay causing a missed clinically necessary action, missed transport with material harm, extra hospitalization, or other serious adverse event.

The board adjudicates whether the event is intervention-attributable and whether a route may resume unchanged. Any content, route, alert, responsibility, or invalidation change requires a new version and repeat P1 before resumption. Two or more reproducible duplicate/wrong-recipient/post-acknowledgement alert protocol deviations at a site pause that site's intervention pending technical review; such review cannot erase accrued events.

Defer or stop a lane as inconclusive for failure to accrue the fixed sample in the prespecified period, inability to ascertain the primary endpoint, loss of source-event completeness, route drift, contamination that invalidates allocation, adjudication instability, or safety-board determination that continued exposure is not justified. Do not declare futility or equivalence solely from a nonsignificant interim estimate. The other route may continue only if the failure mechanism cannot affect it and the safety board documents that separation.

## Adversarial verification

The executable study must test at least the following cases:

1. **Source and schema invariance:** recompute all five retrospective source hashes and schemas; stop on drift, duplicate canonical `DS`, denominator-hash drift, or impossible timestamps.
2. **Denominator invariance:** the same `N_A` is used for chronology, content, unit inventory, calibration, and comparator exclusion; every all-admission marginal sums to `N_A`.
3. **Clock identity:** verify `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; only `G` defines `H/E_A`.
4. **Departure freeze:** no post-`D` content or downstream event can change retrospective `Y`, `Z`, or route.
5. **Unit boundaries:** shared action/interval is one unit; different due intervals are separate; addendum restatement is deduplicated; unresolved coreference is `U_BOUNDARY`; minimum/maximum inventories reproduce.
6. **Supersession:** generic “PCP follow-up” is not an equivalent plan; incomplete specialist mention is partial/unknown; compatible scheduled CT with timing and responsibility is equivalent; explicit informed non-pursuit and definitive replacement remain distinct; unexplained incompatibility is conflict.
7. **Mixed admission:** one pure omission plus one conflict is `A_MIXED` within `A_RECON`, never `A_AUTO`.
8. **Unknown adverse assignment:** any decision-relevant uncertainty makes primary route `A_DEFER`; favorable consensus cannot erase it.
9. **No unit inflation:** duplicating a unit cannot change admission routes, burdens, P1 denominators, or bootstrap weights.
10. **No utility leakage:** changing or deleting any decision-curve threshold or retrospective `D_MIX` value cannot change route nomination or P1 progression.
11. **Live-time replay:** P1 route computation uses only events recorded and available by `T_route`; backfilled fields cannot improve a prior route.
12. **Addendum invalidation:** a material addendum or new explicit plan invalidates every old route and payload. Failure to rerun is a stale-route error.
13. **Route-specific denominators:** an `A_AUTO` error remains in `N_AUTO` even if later corrected; a must-reconcile admission routed `A_DEFER` remains in `N_REF_RECON` but is not an unsafe miss; no admission can disappear for missing fields.
14. **Zero-event assurance:** 0/120 passes the mathematical exact-upper-bound condition; 1/120 fails by the categorical stop rule even if an estimated rate appears small; 0/119 defers for insufficient assurance.
15. **Inappropriate propagation:** exact copied text that was superseded, declined, wrong in timing, or attached to the wrong target is `AE_PROP` even if a clinician clicked accept.
16. **Override distinction:** explicit clinician modification with rationale and responsibility is reconciliation, not suppression; silent system deletion or induced unsupported override is `AE_SUPPRESS`.
17. **Failure to reconcile:** mentioning the nodule without action/timing/responsibility does not pass; each active unit requires explicit disposition.
18. **Alert accounting:** duplicate, wrong-recipient, and post-acknowledgement alerts remain counted despite dismissal; hidden background computation is not an alert.
19. **Delay accounting:** a faster note signature cannot conceal delayed transport, order, referral, or scheduling; distributions and adjudicated consequential events are both reported.
20. **Equity:** missing protected attributes remain a published category; no free-text inference; subgroup denominator changes and critical events are visible.
21. **Trial ITT:** intervention assignment remains in the denominator after nonuse, clinician rejection, route invalidation, or crossover; per-protocol results cannot replace ITT.
22. **Conclusion discipline:** synthetic supportive, adverse, sparse, and missing-data results must respectively yield route-specific support, safety stop, inconclusive accrual, and defer—not claims of clinical benefit unsupported by the computed endpoints.

## Interpretation of supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results

**Retrospectively supportive** means the frozen admission gate is reproducible, bounded, non-sparse, and precise enough to study live. It does not establish that any recommendation was guideline-appropriate, seen, or beneficial, and it cannot choose a tolerable error rate.

**P1 supportive for auto** requires 0/at least 120 disqualifying auto errors and 0/at least 120 disqualifying reconciliation misses, complete replayable provenance, timely current-state routes, stable expert-compatible labels, and no sentinel workflow event. It supports only randomizing clinician-confirmed prepopulation; it does not support silent propagation or deployment.

**P1 supportive for reconciliation** requires its fixed accrual, replay, capture, stability, and no-suppression conditions. It supports only a reconciliation trial. High defer or over-route burden is adverse to efficiency and may make a trial infeasible, but is not mislabeled as patient harm.

**P1 adverse** includes any disqualifying route error, stale or wrong-patient payload, ignored explicit decline/override, failure to capture a must-reconcile state, or inability to reconstruct current-state chronology. The affected route stops and requires a new version. Sparse accrual, excessive indeterminacy, missing safety fields, or wide site-specific uncertainty is **inconclusive**, not safe.

**P2 supportive** for a lane requires a Holm-adjusted superiority result on its fixed route-specific failure endpoint, conclusions stable under missing-outcome adverse bounds, and no safety stop. This supports the tested clinician-mediated workflow at the studied sites. It does not establish fewer cancers, better survival, or universal cost-effectiveness.

**P2 adverse** includes any sentinel intervention-attributable inappropriate propagated plan, suppressed active recommendation, serious delay, privacy event, or other listed stop. Increased alert burden, ordinary delay, or inequity without a sentinel event can make implementation undesirable, but those judgments remain visible rather than converted to an invented utility. A harmful or burdensome auto lane does not invalidate a separately safe reconciliation lane.

**P2 inconclusive** includes failure to accrue, non-significant superiority, unexpectedly low control-event rate, larger clustering, missing primary endpoints, contamination, route drift, adjudication instability, sparse equity strata, or loss of follow-up. It cannot be reported as equivalence, noninferiority, safety, or lack of clinical need.

## What is computable and what requires additional evidence

Retrospective MIMIC can compute source-complete storage opportunity, exhaustive departure states, recommendation-unit multiplicity, text-supported resolutions, conservative admission routes, mixed/defer/timing burdens, reviewer reproducibility, compatible bounds, and institutional storage controls. An automatic verifier can check hashes, joins, chronology, denominator conservation, route logic, bounds, confidence intervals, sample-size rules, prospective event accounting, randomization, endpoint computation, and whether reported conclusions follow from those outputs.

MIMIC cannot establish actual finalization/viewing, clinician awareness, UI exposure, accountable responsibility, outside-system plans, current patient preference, complete competing-risk context, recommendation guideline appropriateness, validated ordering/scheduling/completion, malignancy, intervention-caused delay, patient harm, utilities, or clinical benefit. P1 requires new workflow logs and departure-frozen physician adjudication. P2 requires prospective randomization, signed/transmitted artifacts, intervention telemetry, safety attribution, and follow-up. Cancer diagnosis, radiation burden, downstream invasive procedures, quality of life, cost-effectiveness, and survival require longer follow-up, additional data, clinical expert review, and potentially another trial.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

This child replaces a governance-dependent retrospective purity margin with a falsifiable prospective safety case. It fixes the live population and clocks; preserves every trigger and defer; defines current-state reference classes; names disqualifying auto and reconciliation errors; fixes their route-specific denominators; gives an interpretable zero-event sample and exact precision rule; separates confirmatory safety assurance from descriptive burden, delay, execution, and inequity; and specifies which failures stop one lane, the whole gate, or progression. It then turns the two routes into separable randomized superiority trials with explicit clinician confirmation, distinct primary endpoints, sentinel safety monitoring, and intention-to-treat denominators.

Important uncertainty remains. Even complete prospective chart capture may omit outside facts; expert reference states are fallible; zero events in 120 supplies limited assurance rather than proof of safety; trial planning assumptions may be wrong; clinician behavior and alert effects are site-specific; and rare harms or inequity may remain unmeasured. These limitations are reasons for staged evidence and explicit defer states, not reasons to infer utilities from MIMIC.
