# HCC differential-Y observation strata and fail-closed falsification envelope

## Targeted successor

This targeted successor preserves the frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B prognostic design of `[prior hypothesis]`: the selected cohort, repeat-event construction, exact B/P/event/Y clocks, annual forward blocks, locked nested ridge models, outcome-blind support funnel, and noncausal evidence boundary are unchanged. The substantive repair is to make the unresolved **differential Y observation** question executable rather than treating an overall missing-Y bound as sufficient.

The falsifiable question remains: among selected HCC repeat-TACE episodes with remote baseline assay B and pre-repeat assay P, does P improve prediction of the subsequent assay Y beyond B and the locked pre-event core? The new question is whether that apparent improvement is robust to the observed, assay-specific observation process. Albumin and total bilirubin are never pooled: each has its own Y, observation indicator, training range, model predictions, ascertainment strata, and bound.

This is prognostic association only. It is not treatment response, causal effect, assay utility, a TACE action rule, safety, survival, or clinical benefit.

## What is known and what is not

The inherited full-source audit reconstructed 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator patients, 159 and 526 selected repeat events, 242 BP-eligible episodes per assay, and 158 complete albumin versus 160 complete bilirubin triplets overall. The 2022 future block had 29 BP-eligible episodes per assay and 28 observed outcomes; later blocks have materially poorer outcome ascertainment (including 14/70 observed in 2024 and 1/9 systemic). Thus an observed complete-triplet gain can be selected, and the fact that an examination, encounter, or document exists does not establish that the assay was completed or that the result is clinically interpretable.

The available evidence supports only feasibility of a local, selected short-horizon prognostic comparison and an outcome-blind observation audit. It does not support an empirical claim that P is robustly incremental, that missing Y is missing at random, or that either assay reflects liver function consistently: lab units, reference ranges, disease severity, tumor burden, imaging response, transfusion/fluid exposure, external-care capture, mortality, and hepatic failure are unavailable or unverified.

Let `N_s` be all BP-eligible rows in stratum s and `n_mis,s` its missing count. For observed rows, `d_i=(Y_i-p0_i)^2-(Y_i-p1_i)^2`. For missing rows, evaluate the affine contrast

```
d_i(y) = 2*y*(p1_i-p0_i) + p0_i^2-p1_i^2
```

at L and U. The fixed-prediction stratum bounds are

```
Delta_s,lo = (sum_observed d_i + sum_missing min(d_i(L),d_i(U))) / N_s
Delta_s,hi = (sum_observed d_i + sum_missing max(d_i(L),d_i(U))) / N_s.
```

## Falsification and interpretation

Support for robust incremental prognostic information requires successful source/hash/reconstruction/clock assertions, both support gates, an estimable observed-case result, no material differential-observation warning, and a global Delta interval strictly above zero under the declared training-range scenario. Even then, the claim is limited to selected short-horizon prognostic association in the tested calendar mixture; category-specific positivity is not sufficient for robust support.

Adverse evidence is a nonpositive primary result in an adequately supported block, repeated M1 degradation, or a global bound wholly nonpositive. This challenges incremental prediction but does not prove B sufficiency or mechanism.

Selection-sensitive evidence is favorable complete-case performance with a global bound crossing zero, a nearest common-displacement tipping root near zero, materially different albumin versus bilirubin observation fractions, category intervals with opposite signs, or large observed/missing covariate gradients. This is the principal expected outcome under severe selective observation and must not be reported as support.

Unsupported/inconclusive evidence includes failed support gates, small strata, empty/degenerate training range, future out-of-range Y, nonfinite predictions, nonestimable SD/bootstrap, low transport or observation ESS, influential patient clusters, or dependence on full-source ranges. A 2022 local result cannot be generalized to 2023/2024 absent adequate support and observation robustness.

The verifier must test both positive and negative synthetic branches: a favorable observed-case gain with zero-crossing all-BP bounds must be labeled selection-sensitive; a nonpositive observed gain must remain adverse even if an ascertainment stratum is favorable; missing Y must never be recoded; albumin and bilirubin must never be pooled; category presence must never be treated as completed assay follow-up; documents must never supply time; all-BP denominators must be preserved; deterministic bounds must never be labeled CIs; and out-of-range future Y must invalidate, not widen, the temporal bound.

Computational checks can establish hashes, headers, duplicate-safe joins, clocks, funnels, differential observation tables, prediction finiteness, endpoint arithmetic, roots, and bootstrap bookkeeping. They cannot establish assay units, clinical severity, tumor burden, imaging response, TACE intent/administration, completed care, external-care capture, mortality, hepatic failure, causality, utility, actionability, or benefit. Those require clinical adjudication and independent temporal, external, or prospective evidence.
