# Terminal calibration audit for the ventilation care-plan receipt lineage

## Hypothesis and clinical importance

The unresolved claim was that sparse receipt of the exact eICU ventilation care-plan labels might still support a reproducible, clinically useful *documentation-availability* measurement object after explicit hospital, workflow, and documentation-density calibration. This matters because a reproducible receipt object could support data-quality triage and identify where structured care-plan measurement is feasible; conversely, treating a sparse local documentation pattern as a bedside ventilation or extubation measure could mislead quality measurement and clinical research.

The strongest inherited evidence supports only that exact structured receipt is sparse, highly concentrated in one hospital, and not transportable under the parent audit. It does not support actual ventilation, daily extubation evaluation, spontaneous breathing-trial delivery, extubation, quality, benefit, or causality. The proposed child test added pre-landmark workflow/documentation calibration but retained the parent population and label semantics unchanged.

## Prespecified calibration experiment

Within each hospital, a five-fold `StratifiedGroupKFold` grouped by `uniquepid` compares a landmark-only receipt baseline with a regularized logistic model including ward/unit workflow and the prespecified documentation-density variables. A site is eligible for this model only if it has at least 20 target receipts, at least 100 non-receipts, and at least 100 unique persons. A cross-fitted enhanced-model top quintile is the proposed fixed-capacity documentation-availability object. Its receipt rate receives a 2,000-replicate person-cluster bootstrap interval and must contain at least 10 examples of each exact label. Metrics are log loss, Brier score, AUROC where defined, mean prediction, and five-bin calibration error. Leave-one-hospital-out models omit hospital and ward identity and test generic transport.

Before any result-dependent interpretation, the rescue gate required at least two independently eligible hospitals for each qualifying variant; enhanced versus baseline log loss improvement of at least 0.005, lower Brier score, maximum five-bin calibration error at most 0.05, top-quintile receipt lower bootstrap bound at least 0.20, and at least 10 of each exact choice in each site object. The unbounded definition and at least two of the three bounded definitions additionally had to pass the generic held-out-hospital log-loss majority-improvement criterion.

## Execution and deterministic feasibility result

The complete resource-managed execution was submitted as job `[research job]` with the script, parent artifact, and read-only `patient.csv.gz`, `carePlanGeneral.csv.gz`, and `carePlanCareProvider.csv.gz` declared as inputs. It failed at the 600-second deadline before writing its JSON/CSV outputs (`TimeoutError: Job deadline reached`). Therefore no model metric, bootstrap interval, or leave-one-hospital-out metric is claimed from that job.

The terminality decision does not depend on those unfinished model metrics. The already-frozen parent site-count artifact `[internal dataset path]` shows that, for the unbounded definition, hospital 420 contains 980 rows and 73 target rows, while every other hospital has at most 7 target rows. The bounded airway variants are sparser outside hospital 420. Adding workflow/documentation predictors cannot create target receipts, non-receipts, or independent hospitals, and hence cannot make a second hospital meet the predeclared minimum of 20 target receipts. Consequently every tested variant fails the necessary `>=2` site-object condition before model fitting. This is an exact support failure, not an inference from a failed or underpowered model.

## Interpretation and falsification

This child is terminal-adverse for the stated use: explicit hospital/workflow/documentation calibration cannot rescue a reproducible multicenter or replicated site-specific receipt object in this eICU snapshot under the frozen support requirements. A supportive result would have required two qualifying hospitals and robustness across the unbounded plus two bounded definitions, which is mathematically precluded by the observed target-count support. An adverse result is the observed failure of that necessary replication condition. The timed-out model execution is an implementation/performance limitation and leaves numerical calibration estimates unavailable, but it does not make the deterministic support conclusion inconclusive. A future study could be inconclusive or supportive only with additional data containing materially more independent target receipts across hospitals, or with a newly approved design goal and population—not by silently relaxing the present gate.

The conclusion is limited to fitness for a structured documentation-receipt object in this snapshot. It does not establish that the labels are false, merely local conventions, or unrelated to bedside care. Clinical meaning would require device/airway evidence, contemporaneous workflow metadata, and blinded expert adjudication. Claims about actual ventilation, spontaneous breathing trials, extubation, quality, benefit, or causal effects require those additional data and study designs.
