# Staged randomized validation by direct all-randomized discordance distortion

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The future clinical decision is whether a locked EHR endpoint can represent the randomized risk difference in failure of a norepinephrine-first (NE-first) versus vasopressin-first (VP-first) discontinuation strategy closely enough for a later 0.05 absolute noninferiority decision. The parent repaired post-randomization selection by assigning every randomized patient an immutable disposition, but it still used sensitivity and specificity conditional on reference-positive and reference-negative denominators. Its exact conservative planning rule therefore required 6,266 patients per arm (12,532 total), and its binary composite risk mixed strategy implementation, competing events, and rescue without making their roles sufficiently distinct.

This child asks a narrower, directly falsifiable measurement question and separates three estimand levels:

1. **Future decision-relevant strategy endpoint:** an all-randomized binary reference endpoint and its locked EHR analogue, both derived from immutable disposition categories and retaining every patient under randomized assignment.
2. **Principal measurement-validation quantities:** the four unconditional all-randomized false-positive and false-negative cell probabilities and their disposition-level attribution. These quantify reconstruction error; they do not treat nonimplementation or death itself as measurement error.
3. **Implementation/adherence diagnostics:** whether and how the assigned first-stop strategy was attempted and delivered. These diagnose whether a future strategy trial is implementable, but neither define the measurement population nor alter the measurement-validity estimate.

The unresolved primary claim is:

> Among 6,000 consecutive first patients randomized 1:1 to NE-first or VP-first across qualified pump–EHR configurations, does the simultaneous 95% upper bound on absolute EHR-versus-reference risk-difference distortion for the prespecified all-randomized strategy endpoint fall strictly below 0.025, when every indeterminate reference or EHR label is completed adversarially in one joint exact aggregate allocation?

The substantive advance is an exact paired-data identity. If `p_z=P(Y_R=1|Z=z)` and `q_z=P(Y_E=1|Z=z)`, then

`q_z-p_z=P(Y_E=1,Y_R=0|Z=z)-P(Y_E=0,Y_R=1|Z=z)`.

Therefore

`b=(q_1-q_0)-(p_1-p_0)=(pi_FP,1-pi_FN,1)-(pi_FP,0-pi_FN,0)`.

The distortion target depends directly on four unconditional discordance cells, not on sensitivity/specificity denominators and not on assumed true-risk region `Omega`. Four exact binomial intervals and their joint aggregate-completion envelope preserve the parent's exact RD guarantee for every possible `p_0,p_1`, while reducing the planned burden to 3,000 per arm (6,000 total). The inherited sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, and original six-vertex `Omega` analysis remain mandatory secondary diagnostics for comparability, but they no longer determine primary feasibility.

This is endpoint validation, not treatment-effect estimation. Support would not establish clinical benefit, safety, equipoise, strategy noninferiority, causal effects, or clinical acceptability of assigning death, early ICU exit, crossover, or nonimplementation to the binary endpoint.

## Strongest supported evidence and frozen negative findings

The strongest existing evidence is negative and remains frozen. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible MIMIC landmarks, observational actions were only 211 NE-first (3.49%) and 167 VP-first (2.76%), both below the inherited 5% positivity gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort contains 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate composite failures. The strata are not randomized or exchangeable. Among 136 determinate episodes, 73 failed, 59 included a charted stopped-agent restart, and 36 were restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvements (about 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, both below 0.01) and adverse later transport; no score or subgroup advances.

The complete inherited input-event audit used all 137 episodes and all rows after declared stay/item/time filters. It reproduced 59 raw restarts. Five symmetric reconstruction rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices; the inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices, all at `Paused` boundaries. These findings show EHR transaction-rule instability, not pump truth. MIMIC cannot identify physical delivery, clinician rescue intent, randomization, reliable MCS absence, cross-clock error, or prospective interface validity.

## Population, randomization, and time zero

At least six adult ICUs, at least six sites, and at least two pump–EHR interface families participate. A configuration is the exact tuple of site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, and clock-map method/version.

Before any randomized patient contributes, each configuration undergoes a 50-episode technical run-in. At least 95% must have unique patient–pump–channel–drug linkage, an escrowed clock map, and complete pump export from two hours before the candidate assignment through endpoint closure; at least 90% must have contemporaneous evidence suitable for intent adjudication. One repeat run-in on 50 wholly new episodes is permitted after repair. A second failure makes that configuration feasibility-only. Run-in episodes are excluded from both randomized stages.

Eligibility is frozen before randomization and cannot depend on later capture, adherence, actual first-stopped drug, rescue, or outcome:

1. age at least 18 and alive in an adult ICU;
2. linked pump-delivered NE and VP concurrently for at least six continuous hours;
3. at the start of the final 30 pre-randomization minutes, NE is above 0 and at most 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP is above 0 and at most 0.04 U/min, with neither increased during those 30 minutes;
4. no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours;
5. at least three valid MAPs spanning at least three of four prior 30-minute bins, including the final bin, with median MAP at least 65 and minimum MAP at least 55 mmHg; and
6. the treating team documents before assignment that either protocolized first-stop strategy is clinically permissible.

The first eligible randomization per person is the sole primary unit. `tr` is immutable randomization time. Allocation is 1:1 in configuration-stratified permuted blocks: `Z=0` assigned NE-first and `Z=1` assigned VP-first. `A`, actual first-stopped drug, is post-randomization and diagnostic. Every randomized patient remains in all primary tables under `Z`.

A protocol-concordant qualifying cessation `t0` is the first pump-native delivered-rate timestamp after `tr` and no later than `tr+120 min` at which the assigned drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and stays below continuously for five minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Programmed rate without linked delivery is insufficient. Nonassigned-first cessation is crossover. Both drugs meeting off definitions within five minutes is simultaneous. Neither creates a later primary qualifying `t0`; subsequent assigned cessation cannot overwrite the first disposition.

Implementation is assessed through `min(tr+120 min, death, ICU exit)`. For a qualifying cessation, rescue follow-up is `(t0,min[t0+6 h,death,ICU exit]]`. Every source clock has a prospectively escrowed affine map with offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving handling, fitting rows, residuals, and immutable version. The boundary band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds; a configuration needing a wider band fails.

## One immutable disposition taxonomy; three estimand levels

Each randomized patient receives exactly one mutually exclusive reference disposition `D_R` and one independently reconstructed EHR disposition `D_E`. The locked ordered categories are:

1. `DEATH`: death after `tr` and on or before applicable closure;
2. `CROSSOVER`: nonassigned drug first meets the five-minute off definition within 120 minutes while assigned drug remains above threshold;
3. `SIMULTANEOUS`: both meet off definitions within five minutes within 120 minutes;
4. `NONIMPLEMENTED_OVERRIDE`: documented clinical override/abort or no attempted assigned cessation by 120 minutes;
5. `EARLY_EXIT_NO_T0`: alive ICU exit before qualifying assigned cessation;
6. `QUALIFIED_RESCUE`: qualifying assigned cessation followed within the horizon by assigned-drug resumption meeting the five-minute threshold and blinded adjudication that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent hypotension/impaired perfusion;
7. `QUALIFIED_NO_RESCUE_6H`: qualifying cessation, complete six hours, no rescue or death;
8. `QUALIFIED_ALIVE_EXIT_OFF`: alive ICU exit after qualifying cessation, complete capture through exit, assigned drug still off, no rescue or death;
9. `INDETERMINATE`: missing linkage/export/clock/vital-status/boundary/intent evidence can change the category.

The priority order prevents retrospective choice. Death is never missing. Technical pause, bag/syringe replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, mistaken-stop correction, or uninterrupted delivery fragmented only in documentation is not rescue; unresolved intent is `INDETERMINATE`.

### Level 1: future decision-relevant strategy endpoint

The clinically governed all-randomized binary mapping is fixed before stage 1:

* `Y_R=1` for `DEATH`, `CROSSOVER`, `SIMULTANEOUS`, `NONIMPLEMENTED_OVERRIDE`, `EARLY_EXIT_NO_T0`, or `QUALIFIED_RESCUE`;
* `Y_R=0` for `QUALIFIED_NO_RESCUE_6H` or `QUALIFIED_ALIVE_EXIT_OFF`;
* `Y_R=?` for `INDETERMINATE`.

`Y_E` applies the identical binary map to `D_E`. This mapping retains decision relevance for a future treatment-policy strategy comparison, but governance must explicitly ratify it. In particular, early alive exit after successful cessation is a competing success only through ICU exit, whereas alive exit before assigned cessation is nonimplementation. A later trial seeking a six-clock-hour outcome regardless of location needs post-ICU medication truth and a new validation.

### Level 2: principal measurement validation, defined for everyone

The primary validation table is `Z x Y_E x Y_R`, including `?`. For determinate labels, each patient contributes to exactly one of TN (`E=0,R=0`), FN (`E=0,R=1`), FP (`E=1,R=0`), or TP (`E=1,R=1`). The principal quantities are

`pi_FP,z=P(Y_E=1,Y_R=0|Z=z)` and `pi_FN,z=P(Y_E=0,Y_R=1|Z=z)`.

They have the randomized arm denominator `N_z` and remain meaningful without conditioning on post-randomization implementation or reference status. They exactly determine RD distortion. Mandatory `D_E x D_R` component tables attribute every binary discordance to implementation reconstruction, death/exit feed, first-stop ordering, simultaneous stop, or rescue reconstruction. Accordingly, a true strategy nonimplementation classified as failure by both systems is strategy failure but not measurement error. A mismatch between the two systems is measurement error. This distinction prevents apparent “validity” from being interpreted as evidence that implementation was successful.

Assignment-specific sensitivity and specificity remain secondary:

`Se_z=P(Y_E=1|Y_R=1,Z=z)` and `Sp_z=P(Y_E=0|Y_R=0,Z=z)`.

They are calculated only from the same all-randomized tables and never define enrollment or selection. Component agreement, PPV, NPV, timing, and McNemar discordance are descriptive.

### Level 3: implementation/adherence diagnostics

Report by `Z`, with 95% intervals: attempted assigned cessation by 120 minutes; qualifying assigned cessation; crossover; simultaneous cessation; override/aborted attempt; no attempt; time to attempt; actual first-stopped drug `A`; reason; alive early exit; death; and intent-qualified rescue after qualifying cessation. Report assignment difference in qualifying implementation and its configuration distribution. These are strategy-delivery diagnostics, not endpoint labels chosen after outcomes and not measurement-validation denominators. Per-protocol, actual-stop, qualifying-cessation, `Z x A`, and adherer-only accuracy analyses are explicitly descriptive and cannot pass, fail, or rescue the primary distortion claim.

## Locked EHR reconstruction and reference truth

Reference `D_R` uses pump-native delivered rates, complete pump event streams, independently mapped monitor/vital-status/ICU-boundary data, and blinded intent adjudication. Two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators review contemporaneous pump/monitor traces, cointerventions, and intent evidence without `Y_E`, transaction diagnostics, aggregate arm accuracy, or each other's label; a third resolves disagreement.

The locked EHR algorithm uses only mapped EHR infusion intervals, the EHR-visible randomization record, and EHR death/ICU-boundary feeds. It cannot use delivered pump rates, adjudicated intent, primary-label transaction repair, or `Y_R`. NE maps to local equivalents of MIMIC item 221906; retain mcg/kg/min or divide mcg/min by a contemporaneous 30–300 kg weight and reject normalized rate above 5 mcg/kg/min. VP maps to item 222315; retain U/min or divide U/hour by 60 and reject above 0.2 U/min. A positive interval requires `endtime>starttime`. Chain, status, `storetime`, caregiver, open-bag, transfer, and order fields are recorded as diagnostics but cannot repair primary intervals.

Using the same thresholds, ordering, five-minute persistence, 120-minute deadline, and six-hour/exit closure, `D_E` is assigned independently. A new positive EHR interval for the assigned drug must begin in `(t0_E,t0_E+6h]` before earlier death/exit; a row already spanning `t0_E` is not a restart. Mapped timestamp uncertainty crossing ordering, persistence, deadline, restart, death, or exit boundaries produces `INDETERMINATE`, not a favorable label.

## Exact primary inference and unknown-label completion

For each arm, create four two-sided Clopper–Pearson intervals with `alpha=0.0125`, one for each unconditional count `FP_0/N_0`, `FN_0/N_0`, `FP_1/N_1`, and `FN_1/N_1`. Bonferroni simultaneous coverage is at least 95%. If their endpoints are `[L_FP,z,U_FP,z]` and `[L_FN,z,U_FN,z]`, the exact signed distortion interval is

`[L_FP,1-U_FN,1-U_FP,0+L_FN,0, U_FP,1-L_FN,1-L_FP,0+U_FN,0]`.

`B_U` is the larger absolute endpoint. The primary gate is strict `B_U<0.025`; equality fails. This is an exact upper confidence bound on distortion of the empirical randomized arm RD for the specified binary endpoint, and it is valid for every true arm risk without invoking `Omega`.

Every patient appears in a 3x3 `Y_E x Y_R` table. For unknowns, retain the parent's exact aggregate-count completion: represent a completed table as `(TN,FN,FP,TP)`; allocate `?0` between TN/FP, `?1` between FN/TP, `0?` between TN/FN, `1?` between FP/TP, and `??` among all four. Merge identical count states after each bounded-knapsack convolution. Across the two arms, maximize `B_U` over one joint feasible aggregate allocation; separate metric-wise imputations are invalid. A branch-and-bound fallback uses the five allocation blocks with monotonic CP bounds. Timeout is computationally inconclusive. The parent's small-table oracle through eight unknown records per arm remains required.

Overall indeterminacy above 5%, above 7.5% in an assignment-by-configuration cell with at least 20 patients, or an arm difference above 5 percentage points is operationally adverse and precludes support even if the numerical completion bound passes. The exact worst completion remains primary.

The original secondary Se/Sp distortion analysis must still enumerate the parent's 16 confidence corners and six frozen `Omega` vertices `(0.10,0.10),(0.10,0.20),(0.20,0.10),(0.30,0.40),(0.40,0.30),(0.40,0.40)` and report its witness. It cannot overturn the direct primary result; disagreement is reported as evidence that conditional metric precision, rather than exact RD distortion, drives the parent's burden.

## Staged randomized internal-pilot design without outcome-dependent selection

The design has a common protocol, common randomization stream, common primary estimand, and one maximum confirmatory denominator of exactly 6,000 first patients (3,000 per assignment arm), or 48 months. Stage 1 contains the first 1,200 randomized patients (600 per arm) and is **included** in final analysis. There is no recalibration of the endpoint, thresholds, clinical mapping, gate, confidence alpha, configuration definition, or maximum `N` after randomization.

A firewall feasibility statistician receives only calendar accrual, site/configuration, randomized arm, whether pump linkage/export/clock mapping/reference adjudication are complete, whether `Y_R` is determinate, and the implementation category. The statistician cannot receive `Y_E`, EHR/reference concordance, FP/FN/TP/TN counts, `B_U`, Se/Sp, PPV/NPV, or narratives that disclose concordance. The stage-1 decision occurs only after all 1,200 reach closure and adjudication reliability locks.

Continue to 6,000 only if all prespecified feasibility conditions pass:

* at least four active sites and two interface families;
* projected total accrual at or below 48 months using site-stratified stage-1 calendar rates, with the exact projection code escrowed before stage 1;
* linkage/export/qualified clock-map completeness at least 95% overall and at least 90% in every configuration with at least 20 patients;
* overall `Y_R` indeterminacy at most 5%, no qualifying cell above 7.5%, and arm difference at most 5 percentage points;
* at least 85% raw inter-adjudicator agreement and not both Gwet AC1 and kappa below 0.70 after the first 100 adjudicated patients;
* at least 70% of patients have an assigned cessation attempt by 120 minutes, at least 60% have a protocol-concordant qualifying cessation, crossover plus simultaneous cessation is at most 15%, and absolute between-arm difference in qualifying cessation is at most 10 percentage points; and
* no independent safety board finding that continued randomization lacks equipoise or creates unacceptable harm.

These implementation thresholds do not label measurement success. Failure stops the study as **feasibility-inconclusive** and reports the all-randomized stage-1 disposition, implementation, capture, and reliability counts; it must not be called endpoint misclassification failure. No comparative clinical outcome test is authorized. Safety stopping follows the independent board's prospectively approved charter and is not an automated efficacy/harm conclusion.

There is no supportive early stop. In particular, stage-1 concordance is never inspected for continuation, and a stage-1 bound cannot validate the endpoint. Even zero discordances at 600 per arm yields a direct simultaneous bound about 0.01685, but stopping there is prohibited because representation, component incidence, and external configuration transport remain inadequately established and an extreme early result should not govern selection. There is no promising-zone enlargement, sample-size re-estimation from FP/FN, denominator-triggered stopping, arm-specific closeout, component-triggered extension, or post-lock extension. This avoids outcome-dependent selection and preserves unconditional 95% final inference.

All stage-1 randomized rows are retained if the study continues. The maximum 6,000 is 52.1% lower than the parent's 12,532. The executable exact planning calculation uses the same design alternative `Se=0.99, Sp=0.995` and risk range 0.10–0.40 only to obtain uniform discordance-probability ranges: FP 0.003–0.0045 and FN 0.001–0.004 per arm. With eight exact tail events at 0.0125, it gives a conservative assurance lower bound of 0.90 that the final direct CP rectangle has `B_U=0.0234474755<0.025` at 3,000 per arm. At 2,999 it also passes (`0.0234552749`), so 3,000 is an operationally rounded maximum and is **not** claimed to be a minimum; discrete exact envelopes can be locally nonmonotone. Planning assumes determinate labels and is neither power nor an acceptance threshold.

If stage 1 stops, randomized participants are not discarded and the feasibility report remains scientifically useful. If it continues but fewer than 6,000 patients accrue by 48 months, measurement validation is inconclusive; the partial bound is reported descriptively but cannot claim validation.

## Adjudication, recurrence, components, and transport

After the first 100 stage-1 patients, reliability failure suspends accrual. A clarification cannot inspect `Y_E x Y_R`; reliability restarts on 100 wholly new patients, with earlier patients retained in immutable disposition/accounting but classified reference-indeterminate for primary completion unless the predeclared independent re-adjudication procedure resolves them. A second failure stops feasibility.

Report the complete `D_E x D_R` matrix by `Z`, configuration, and family. A Holm-significant assignment-by-component discordance interaction with absolute difference above 0.10, cancellation whereby the global endpoint passes but any common component (at least 100 reference events per arm) has sensitivity below 0.80, death/exit feed discordance, or an above-0.025 increase under adversarial adherence-indeterminate allocation is adverse. These component checks do not redefine the denominator.

Later qualifying episodes are captured under identical rules but cannot increase either primary stage. A recurrent opportunity needs a new six-hour dual-drug baseline and begins at least 24 hours after prior `t0`, or prior randomization if no `t0`; closer transitions remain one episode. Report clustered component tables and direct distortion via patient-cluster bootstrap stratified by `Z` and configuration (at least 10,000 samples) plus marginal GEE diagnostics. Recurrent `B_U` above 0.05 or more than 0.025 worse than first-patient performance is adverse for repeated use but cannot rescue or reverse the primary result.

Transport support requires six sites and two interface families. No site/configuration/family may contribute above 30% of either arm's FP-plus-TN or FN-plus-TP reference denominator. Each arm/reference-status denominator must be at least 100 in four configurations and 200 in three for secondary Se/Sp interpretation. Report exact configuration/family tables, hierarchical binomial estimates, prediction intervals, and leave-one-site/configuration/family-out exact direct bounds. Every leave-out mixture must have `B_U<0.025`. Configuration changes create new configurations; failed/repaired configurations do not re-enter confirmation. Sparse configurations are inconclusive, not homogeneous.

Required synthetic tests include:

3. Excluding nonimplementers or conditioning on `t0` creates a passing subset while all-randomized distortion fails: reject.
4. Crossover followed by assigned cessation remains crossover; simultaneous stop reconstructed as single stop creates its corresponding discordance.
5. Death before/after `t0` and alive exit before/after `t0` follow the frozen category order and cannot be censored after seeing outcomes.
6. A stage-1 accuracy look, continuation conditional on FP/FN, favorable early stop, promising-zone extension, or dropping stage-1 rows invalidates the 95% primary claim.
7. The feasibility statistician receiving `Y_E`, discordance, or narrative leakage invalidates the masked internal-pilot decision.
8. Separate metric-wise unknown completions pass but one joint aggregate completion fails: report failure and witness.
9. Sparse DP/branch-and-bound disagrees with tiny exhaustive oracle, or exact optimization times out: computationally inconclusive.
10. `B_U=0.025` exactly fails; 6,000 incomplete at 48 months is inconclusive even if the observed partial bound passes.
11. Correct measurement support described as clinical noninferiority, safety, benefit, or successful strategy implementation: reject the conclusion.
12. Correct feasibility stop described as adverse endpoint accuracy: reject; correct completed `B_U>=0.025` described as merely infeasible: reject.
13. Pooled pass with leave-one-configuration reversal or hidden component cancellation: no transport support.
14. Timestamp exactly on `tr+120 min`, five minutes, `t0`, six hours, death, or exit; spanning/duplicate intervals; and clock uncertainty crossing order must exercise fixed inclusivity and `?` rules.

Automatic verification can establish source-row accounting, deterministic labels under supplied rules, randomization retention, feasibility-mask compliance, exact count optimization, interval arithmetic, gates, and whether reported claims follow computed outputs. It cannot establish physical delivery absent valid pump linkage, infer clinical intent without expert adjudication, prove MCS absence, ratify the endpoint mapping or 0.05 margin, approve equipoise/safety, or establish causal strategy effects.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results

**Supportive measurement validation:** all 6,000 first patients reach common lock; the joint worst completion has direct `B_U<0.025`; indeterminacy, component, adjudication, clock, recurrence, concentration, and every leave-out safeguard passes. This supports only that the locked EHR algorithm reproduces the ratified all-randomized binary endpoint's randomized RD within the stated exact distortion bound for enumerated configurations. For a later trial using exactly this endpoint and population, an EHR RD confidence upper limit below `0.05-B_U` can imply the corresponding reference RD is below 0.05 under the validated measurement mapping; the boundary zone remains measurement-inconclusive. Validation alone never proves noninferiority.

**Adverse measurement result:** the completed joint exact bound has `B_U>=0.025`, or consequential assignment-specific/component/configuration discordance or cancellation occurs. This falsifies the narrow measurement claim and warns that EHR reconstruction can materially distort the future randomized RD. Favorable implementation, treatment estimates, PPV, or pooled accuracy cannot override it.

**Feasibility-inconclusive:** masked stage-1 capture, accrual, adjudication, implementation, site/family, or safety/equipoise gates fail. This says the randomized validation or assigned strategies cannot presently be executed as specified; it does not by itself diagnose EHR measurement accuracy because concordance was not used for stopping.

**Other inconclusive:** governance does not ratify the binary mapping/margin; fewer than 6,000 complete by 48 months; indeterminacy or exact-optimization limits prevent a bound; representation/transport fails; or a future trial changes population, strategy, implementation window, death/exit/crossover handling, clock, endpoint, or estimand. Stronger conclusions require clinical governance, independent safety review, prospective pump and intent evidence, complete multicenter execution, and then a separate randomized efficacy/noninferiority trial.
