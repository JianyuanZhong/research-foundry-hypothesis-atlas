# Current-landmark, opportunity-preserving transport audit of the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

This proposal is a compiler-ready successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead-frozen scientific schema exactly: 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons, and 74 hospitals; first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`; landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission; the first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`; decision time `D = cplitemoffset`; inherited pre-landmark exclusions; exact literal labels; fixed B/E/T windows and raw thresholds; an independent three-class trajectory; the five-state all-row accounting; repeated-landmark clustering by `patientunitstayid`; and descriptive, noncausal interpretation.

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence is only that the two literal care-plan values are structured chart labels with sparse and heterogeneous hospital distribution, and that respiratory-chart proxies can be affected by observation opportunity. The data do not establish that either label denotes a bedside spontaneous breathing trial, extubation evaluation, clinician intent, readiness, delivered ventilator settings, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or a causal intervention. The unresolved and falsifiable claim is narrower: does the exact label at the current landmark add reproducible information about an independently charted later respiratory-support trajectory beyond information available before the current decision time, after preserving documentation opportunity, competing ICU exits, hospital composition, and repeated-landmark dependence? Does that increment survive when the null preserves both opportunity and the tendency for a stay to carry a persistent label across landmarks?

The substantive advance over both parents is a **current-landmark transport and persistence audit**. The parent five-state endpoint, site-balanced transport envelope, value-blind ascertainment masks, and opportunity-preserving permutation remain primary/frozen. New diagnostics distinguish a genuinely current label increment from (a) a stay-level label/documentation propensity carried across repeated landmarks, (b) pre-D opportunity imbalance, and (c) a few-site composition artifact. They use no new clinical endpoint, no relabelling, no post-D feature, and no result-dependent site selection. A positive result must survive a stricter no-cross-landmark training/prediction audit and a stay-level, opportunity-preserving null; failure is adverse or inconclusive under explicit gates, never evidence of absent bedside meaning.

## Fixed independent endpoint and total-state algebra

Use clinical offsets, never entry offsets, for the locked disjoint windows relative to each row's current `D`:

* `B = (D-720,D]`;
* `E = (D,D+720]`;
* `T = (D+720,D+2160]`.

Before label-stratified output, enumerate source-wide `respchartvaluelabel` and `respcharttypecat` values with matched/unmatched counts, all `airwaytype` values and interval-presence patterns, and all discharge status/location values. Freeze a parser manifest before using arm, site, or landmark results. Parse only finite numeric values for the four frozen components: FiO2 (with the inherited aliases and fixed fraction/percentage conversion and bounds), PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate. Specify normalization, malformed/out-of-bound handling, duplicate collapse, same-clinical-time conflict handling, and parsing reasons. No result-dependent mapping or unit conversion is permitted.

A component is observed only with at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes; a state is observed only with at least three of four observed components. Missing is not zero, unchanged, or carried forward. Compare B and T medians using the frozen absolute thresholds: FiO2 fraction `0.10`, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`.

For a row not competing with an exit, assign exactly one trajectory. Sustained de-escalation requires observed B/T states, at least two component decreases, no increase, and no E escalation. Escalation requires observed B/T states plus at least two increases, or a newly documented invasive airway in E/T after no invasive evidence in B. Stable/mixed is the remaining classifiable state. A required indeterminate E airway check cannot create de-escalation; the row is `non_exit_unobservable` unless an independent increase or airway rule assigns escalation. `respiratoryCare` is airway corroboration/sensitivity only and never fills numeric settings. Its active interval remains `ventstartoffset <= time` and missing or `ventendoffset >= time`.

Every row receives one ordered primary state

`K ∈ {competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.

`competing_exit` has precedence whenever finite `unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160`, including death, discharge, transfer, or loss of opportunity to complete T. Record exact exit offset, `unitdischargestatus`, `unitdischargelocation`, and `exit_before_washout = 1{unitdischargeoffset <= D+720}`. Invalid or unavailable exit timing is recorded as an administrative `invalid_exit_time` reason under the conservative inherited rule, not silently treated as survival. No exit row can be assigned a respiratory trajectory.

The unchanged primary descriptive estimand is the five-state all-row vector

`p_k(a) = N_a^{-1} Σ_i 1{A_i=a,K_i=k}`, `Δ_k = p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`,

with fixed arm denominators and stay-clustered uncertainty. The conditional classifiable display is

`q_c(a) = count(A=a,K=classifiable-c) / count(A=a,K is classifiable)`, `δ_c=q_c(evaluation)-q_c(no_trial)`,

only after its support gate, always alongside all-row denominators, exits, and unobservability. Neither quantity is causal or a bedside effect.

## Value-blind ascertainment and leakage barriers

Emit separate machine-readable manifests for `pre_D_baseline`, `post_D_endpoint`, and `post_D_diagnostic`. The pre-D feature vector `X` may contain only B summaries/missingness, baseline airway indicator, available `sao2` and `respiration`, age category and inherited pre-D covariates, current landmark, and pre-D opportunity. Pre-D opportunity is restricted to clinical time `<=D` and records source-row counts, distinct clinical timestamps, distinct component labels, minimum-spacing indicators, and `respchartentryoffset-respchartoffset` lag summaries for `respiratoryCharting`; respiratoryCare airway-row counts; vitalPeriodic counts; and treatment-row counts. No label string, care-plan count, treatment string, post-D value/count, endpoint-derived flag, future exit, or post-D source density enters `X`.

For every row, independently derive value-blind opportunity indicators from the frozen parser and source records:

* `O_B`: B has the minimum three-component/two-separated-observation opportunity;
* `O_E`: the E airway check is determinable, with separate `O_E_no_rows`;
* `O_T`: T has the minimum three-component/two-separated-observation opportunity;
* `V_T`: T has at least one valid parsed value for each frozen component.

Retain malformed, ambiguous, no-record, and insufficient-spacing reasons. Report the complete `(C,O_B,O_E,O_T,V_T)` table by arm, hospital, and landmark before any endpoint interpretation. These masks cannot classify `K`, erase an exit, or impute values.

Fit the parent observation-only target and value-masked endpoint diagnostic using only pre-D features, current exact label, landmark, fold/stratum, and permitted prior-history fields. Post-D values, trajectory class, care-plan fields beyond frozen exposure, and future exits are prohibited. A mask-only gain comparable to the full K gain is documentation/ascertainment information, not respiratory-state information.

Add a strict as-entered audit sensitivity: use `respchartentryoffset` only to retain observations whose finite entry offset is no later than the end of that observation's assigned clinical window (`<=D` for B, `<=D+720` for E, `<=D+2160` for T). Clinical offset still defines window membership. Rows losing observations solely to late entry retain missingness/administrative reasons and cannot become stable or worsening. If entry-time availability or semantics fail the source audit, this sensitivity is unavailable/inconclusive.

## Current-landmark persistence audit

This section is a diagnostic layer and does not alter the frozen endpoint or estimand.

1. **Landmark history fields.** For each current row, derive only from rows at earlier frozen landmarks in the same stay: number of prior labelled landmarks, whether any prior label exists, prior label sequence, whether the current label equals the immediately preceding label, whether a label switch occurred, and prior-landmark count. These fields are used only in a predeclared persistence diagnostic and are never included in primary `M0` or `M1`, because they are not available in the frozen current pre-D clinical snapshot. A current-row analysis that uses them must be clearly labelled history-augmented and secondary.
2. **No-cross-landmark transport audit.** In each leave-one-hospital-out fold, fit the primary `M0` (pre-D state plus opportunity, no label) and `M1` (same X plus current exact label) on training rows, but enforce a second grouping constraint: all rows from a single `patientunitstayid` remain together in training or evaluation, and no earlier landmark from an evaluation stay is available in training. This is already required for every bootstrap and permutation; it must be explicitly asserted in fold manifests. Report metrics separately for first labelled landmark rows and repeated labelled landmark rows, while retaining the unstratified all-row primary metric. A label gain only among repeated rows, or a gain that vanishes on first labelled rows, is adverse to a current-label interpretation and may indicate persistence/documentation history.
3. **Stay-level persistence null.** In addition to the parent within-hospital-by-landmark permutation and the opportunity-stratified within-hospital-by-landmark permutation, run a predeclared null that permutes entire stay-level label sequences within hospital-by-landmark-compatible opportunity strata. When exact landmark-wise arm counts cannot be preserved by a whole-sequence permutation, do not approximate: report the null unavailable/inconclusive. The valid null must preserve stay cluster, number of labelled landmarks, current-landmark counts, hospital, landmark, and a pre-D opportunity stratum. Record attempted and valid strata, sequence lengths, number of valid permutations, and the deterministic seed. If the observed label increment is typical under this persistence-preserving null, it is adverse to the interpretation that the current label adds respiratory-state information.
4. **Label persistence decomposition.** Report current label prevalence, first/repeated landmark status, switch versus persist status, and site distributions descriptively for all 540 rows. Do not treat a switch as a clinical event or a persistence pattern as bedside intent. Compare M1-M0 in first rows, repeated rows, and switch/persist strata only where support is finite; no subgroup is selected based on result direction.

The sequence null is a stress test, not a replacement for the primary arm contrast. If it is infeasible because only one arm sequence can satisfy a stratum, preserve the primary results but mark the stronger current-landmark conclusion inconclusive.

## Hospital transport envelope

The primary transport procedure remains leave-one-hospital-out with no hospital predictor, held-out target-rate calibration, or hospital-specific intercept. Fit all preprocessing, imputation, scaling, regularization, probabilities, and observation weighting on training hospitals only. Generate held-out probabilities for all five K states. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss and Brier score, with calibration only under finite support, and separately report all-row K, classifiable trajectories, competing exits, and non-exit unobservability.

For a fixed site-balanced envelope, retain all 74 hospitals descriptively and define eligible hospitals before reading effect direction: at least 10 classifiable, non-competing rows per exact arm, and at least 10 rows per arm for any all-row target being contrasted. If fewer than five hospitals meet classifiable support, the site-balanced trajectory envelope is unavailable/inconclusive. Otherwise report each eligible site's paired held-out M1-M0 increment and descriptive K contrast, the equal-site mean, median, IQR, 10%-trimmed mean only when at least 10 sites exist, sign concordance, leave-one-site-out maximum change, and representation fraction. No site may be removed because its direction is unfavorable. No hospital may contribute more than 25% of supported classifiable/non-competing rows for a cross-hospital patient-state interpretation.

Repeat the site envelope for first-landmark rows and repeated-landmark rows where support permits. This is a prespecified descriptive diagnostic, not a new estimand. A positive overall site envelope that is driven by repeated rows while first-row results are null is adverse to current-label transport. A positive result requires concordance of row-weighted and equal-site held-out loss increments, at least five supported hospitals representing at least 50% of supported classifiable/non-competing rows, no more than 25% eligible-site directional reversals, and no single-site dominance. Otherwise report heterogeneity or inconclusive transport.

## Falsification, baselines, and uncertainty

The baselines are: (M0) pre-D respiratory state plus pre-D opportunity, without the current label; (M1) identical X plus the exact current two-level label; and an opportunity-only negative control with no respiratory values and no label strings. A history-augmented diagnostic adds only earlier-landmark persistence fields and must never replace M0/M1. All model choices and preprocessing are fixed and trained within the training hospitals of each fold.

Run, before interpreting any label increment:

* broad within-hospital-by-landmark stay-preserving label permutation;
* pre-D opportunity-stratified permutation with deterministic training-partition bins, sparse-bin collapsing, and at least 20 valid nonempty strata required;
* the stay-sequence persistence-preserving null above;
* pre-D placebo `(D-1440,D-720]` versus B and the exposure-time shift to `D-720` with the locked outcome window;
* component ablations for FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, set rate, vital safety, and airway corroboration;
* airway-corroborated and non-corroborated sensitivities;
* fixed 24-hour late-horizon and other inherited endpoint/observation sensitivities, with attrition retained;
* exit decomposition by `<=D+720` versus `(D+720,D+2160]`, status/location, invalid exit time, non-exit unobservable, and the three classifiable states;
* documentation outcomes for post-window source density, distinct clinical timestamps, component-label counts, and entry lag.

Use stay-cluster bootstrap, fixed seed `61061`, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates, refitting the complete held-out pipeline. Report attempted, valid, failed, effective clusters, and support loss for site-balanced and first/repeated diagnostics. Never use row-level bootstrap or replace unsupported site replicates with row resampling.

## Fail-closed interpretation gates

* **Invalid computation:** parent artifact/hash mismatch; wrong source or schema hash; duplicate or non-one-to-one joins; wrong exact labels or landmarks; boundary inequality error; parser integrity failure; post-D leakage; use of care-plan fields outside exposure verification; failure to assign exactly one K state to every row; or exit precedence violation. Do not interpret estimates.
* **Feasibility:** supportive only if at least 40% of all 540 rows are classifiable and at least 10 hospitals have both exact labels among classifiable rows. Below 20% classifiable or below five such hospitals is adverse feasibility; intermediate is inconclusive. This does not turn unobservable rows into negative clinical outcomes.
* **Transport:** no transported patient-state description unless at least five hospitals have at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows per arm and represent at least 50% of supported rows, the row-weighted and equal-site held-out increments agree, no site dominates more than 25%, and no more than 25% of estimable hospitals reverse the prespecified direction. First/repeated landmark disagreement is heterogeneity, not a reason to select one result.
* **Opportunity:** if the observation-only or value-mask increment is comparable to K, if arm/site differences in O-patterns are not reported, or if the full gain is confined to `competing_exit`, `non_exit_unobservable`, entry lag, source density, or masks, the result is adverse to a respiratory-state interpretation and may support only documentation/exit information.
* **Currentness:** computational support for a current-landmark information claim requires M1 to improve both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss for all-row K and classifiable trajectories in the unbounded primary plus at least two fixed endpoint/observation sensitivities; the stay-cluster favorable interval excludes zero; the opportunity-preserving and persistence-preserving nulls do not make the observed increment typical; and first-landmark and repeated-landmark diagnostics do not show a prespecified persistence-only gain. Failure of the sequence null support gate makes the currentness claim inconclusive, not negative.
* **Falsification:** a comparable/larger placebo increment, early/late reversal, one-component or one-source survival, or site-direction reversal is adverse to a newly informative current-label interpretation. A settings-only signal disappearing under airway or opportunity sensitivity is adverse to the stronger patient-state interpretation.

Supportive output establishes only incremental prediction of this structured eICU chart construct, potentially robust within the observed supported hospitals and landmark design. It does not establish bedside semantics, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, causality, or external validity. Adverse output means the label increment is not robust for this endpoint/snapshot or is explainable by observation, exit, persistence, or site composition; it does not prove that bedside evaluation was absent or clinically unimportant. Inconclusive output identifies failed artifact recovery, sparse classifiability or site overlap, unsupported sequence nulls, unavailable entry clocks, parser instability, or failed positivity. Stronger conclusions require blinded clinical adjudication of SBT/extubation/reintubation, synchronized device logs, local interface dictionaries, complete workflow data, post-ICU follow-up, expert review, and external/prospective replication.
