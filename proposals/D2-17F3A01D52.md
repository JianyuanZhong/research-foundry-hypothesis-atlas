# Is the MerTK/CD206pos macrophage brake on the RA FLS effector program effector-SPECIFIC and graded, or a global composition/technical artifact? A per-FLS-cell specificity-controlled test (E-MTAB-8873)

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (island leader, valid, mu 34.18). Key works [K1],[K2],[K3]. Branch: work/lead/. Screening status: **planned, not executed** (only read-only inspection of prepared files/archive members; see `sources/DATA_PROBE_8873_celllevel.txt`).

## 1. Unresolved question (the opening)

The island's leading candidate showed that the per-FLS-cell effector readout is a better basis than pseudobulk means, but its primary statistic (effector-high FRACTION at a fixed cutoff) still cannot, on its own, separate a **graded, effector-specific per-cell brake** (H_G) from two rivals it explicitly names: (a) a **global composition/technical artifact** (library size, recovered cell number, ambient macrophage RNA carried into the FLS capture), and (b) a **fixed two-state step** (H_C). The parent's discriminating observation therefore remains incomplete: a fall in the effector-high fraction is consistent with all three. The smallest check whose result would change the next decision is to add a **co-measured specificity/negative control** and an explicit **distribution-shape** test on the same per-FLS-cell matrices.

## 2. Clinical importance

If the MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) subset **dose-dependently and specifically** lowers the FLS matrix-degrading/effector program, restoring that subset or its signalling is a target-prioritisation decision distinct from generic immunosuppression [K3]. If the FLS effect is a composition/technical artifact or a state marker, MerTK positivity is a poor standalone target and resolution-restoring therapy should not be prioritised on this evidence. The decision hinges on specificity, not on the raw direction of the association.

## 3. Hypotheses and the observation that separates them

- **H_G (effector-specific graded brake).** Raising the MerTK/CD206pos macrophage fraction within the same FLS donor lowers the FLS effector program **per cell**, and this is **specific** to the effector program (larger than the matched non-effector control gradient) and **survives** adjustment for a macrophage-restricted ambient-RNA control. The shift is distribution-wide (graded).
- **H_C (artifact or fixed step).** The effector-high-fraction change is (i) comparable in a matched non-effector control score (global technical/library-size artifact), (ii) explained by per-cell macrophage-restricted reads captured in the FLS wells (ambient-RNA artifact), or (iii) a two-state step with no dose-graded distribution-wide shift.

**Pre-registered predictions** on the composition-clean within-donor pair (verified member names): `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_1Mpos_SA266A_3Mneg` (pos_fraction 0.25) vs `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_3Mpos_SA266A_1Mneg` (0.75), plus the SA258R ladder {0.0 `..._FLS_only`, 0.25, 0.75, 1.0 `..._FLS_SA267R_Mpos`}.
- H_G predicts: effector-high fraction at 0.25 > 0.75; ladder monotone non-increasing; **effector gradient > control gradient** (F1); effector difference **survives** adjustment for per-cell macrophage-restricted read fraction (F2); distribution-wide (not bimodal step) shift (F3); donor-matched MerTK-inhibitor reversal de-represses the effector fraction (F4).
- H_C predicts: flat/inverted gradient, control gradient >= effector gradient, effector signal abolished by ambient adjustment, and/or a two-state step without dose grading.

## 4. Estimand and statistics

- **Primary estimand.** Difference in per-FLS-cell effector-high fraction between 0.25 and 0.75 within FLS donor SA258R, where "effector-high" uses a cutoff fixed on the FLS-only baseline, with an FLS-donor-clustered 95% CI and adjustment for recovered-cell load and macrophage-state covariates.
- **Effector score genes (verified present, `Reference_end` rows):** MMP1|NM_002421.3, MMP3|NM_002422.4, MMP9|NM_004994.2, MMP13|NM_002427.3, MMP14|NM_004995.3, IL6|NM_000600.4, CXCL8|NM_000584.3, CXCL10|NM_001565.3, CCL2|NM_002982.3, CCL5|NM_002985.2.
- **F1 specificity control (non-effector).** Reference/structural score over verified non-effector rows: GAPDH|NM_002046.5, PGK1|NM_000291.3, FTH1, COL1A1, COL1A2, COL3A1. Falsifier: control gradient >= effector gradient (global technical artifact).
- **F2 ambient-RNA control.** Per-FLS-cell fraction of counts from macrophage-restricted rows MERTK|NM_006343.2, MRC1|NM_002438.3, CD163|NM_004244.5. Falsifier: primary effector difference loses CI-separation after adjustment for this score (the drop merely tracks ambient macrophage RNA).
- **F3 shift-vs-mixture.** Compare distribution-wide shift (per-cell mean/median effect size, KS / Q-Q) against a change confined to one component (change in effector-high fraction with a fitted two-component mixture). H_G: broad proportional shift; H_C: bimodal step or no shift.
- **F4 reversal.** Donor-matched MerTK-inhibitor arms (e.g. `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos_Inhibitor` vs `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos`) — direction check only (n=1).

## 6. Method comparison (required reasoning)

- **Baseline (retained):** pseudobulk sample-mean effector score with well-name composition proxies (the parent's grandparent). Cheap; already carries the composition test; loses the per-cell distribution.
- **Substantive alternative (chosen):** the per-cell, specificity-controlled analysis above. What it reveals that the baseline cannot: whether the effector change is **specific** (vs a global technical/library-size artifact) and **distribution-wide** (graded) vs a **subpopulation step** — the exact distinction between H_G and H_C. A heavier neural/compositional model adds no information for this estimand (the uncertainty is the shape and specificity of a per-cell distribution, not a predictive gain).
- **Resource estimate (solver envelope, unverified on allocation):** 4 SA258R members x ~1400 cells x 445 features ≈ 2.5M values; fits in CPU memory (<2 GB) after row selection; a CPU job suffices (minutes). No GPU required. This is a planning estimate, not measured on our allocation.

## 7. Falsification criteria

Reject H_G if any of: primary effector-high-fraction CI includes 0; sign inverted; ladder non-monotone; **control gradient >= effector gradient (F1)**; effector difference abolished by ambient-RNA adjustment (F2); change is a two-state step with no dose grading (F3); inhibitor reversal inconsistent (F4). Each maps to an explicit adverse/inconclusive outcome.

## 8. Supportive / adverse / inconclusive outcomes

- **Supportive:** CI excludes 0 in the predicted direction, monotone ladder, effector > control gradient, survives F2 adjustment, distribution-wide shift, consistent F4 → graded effector-specific brake signature.
- **Adverse:** flat/inverted or control-explained or step-only → MerTK positivity is a state/technical marker; deprioritise MerTK-directed resolution therapy on this evidence.
- **Inconclusive:** n=1 ladder arms, 445-feature targeted panel, FLS-only members and absent macrophage readout prevent separation → report unresolved, not negative.

## 9. Limits

FLS-only cell-level members (MERTK+ 1–3%; per-macrophage MerTK dosage not measurable); 445-feature targeted panel (GAS6/PROS1/TYRO3 absent); n=1 ratio arms and n=1 inhibitor arms; no protein/wet-lab causality; cross-sectional E-MTAB-8322 anchor only; **not a clinical-efficacy claim**. MerTK-intrinsic signalling causality, protein-level confirmation and independent-donor replication require another study and expert review.
