# Joint model of the AFP/ALBI trajectory AND the observation (draw/scan) process for progression after liver-directed therapy for HCC

Island: `hcc` (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records). Episode 8. Child (evolution) of
`[prior hypothesis]` (episode-7 leader, assessed valid). Status: **design only;
NO outcomes are computed here and no compute job is launched.** This is a targeted repair of the
parent's own remaining objection: its landmark / time-dependent Cox strata cannot rule out that the
detection-time stratum is itself confounded by *who is drawn and scanned when*.

## 1. Unresolved question, opening and clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE/resection/ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats AFP, albumin and
bilirubin, and imaging is not scheduled at random. The parent showed a dated radiologic progression
endpoint exists (`examinations`, ~1.7M dated reports with progression/recurrence descriptors) and
that a detection-timing stratum (E_early/E_late) plus a time-dependent Cox can separate some
time-origin artefacts. **The still-unresolved objection (this repair):** if a rising AFP slope is
what triggers an extra draw or an earlier scan, then a landmark or time-dependent Cox — which
conditions *within* the observed sampling and scan sequence — will attribute to "tumour activity"
an association that is partly or wholly generated by the observation process itself
(informative-observation / lead-time bias). Neither comparator models the process that generates
the extra observations, so the E_early/E_late stratum can still be confounded.

**Evidence for the opening:** the parent's probe P1 established the examinations table contains
dated progression/recurrence descriptors (`迁移` 25107, `增大` 13115, `复发` 4973, `进展` 3645 in the
first 420k rows), so scan times are recoverable and the informative-scan mechanism is plausible and
testable. [K2] shows a longitudinal marker and a *potentially informative visiting process* can be
jointly modelled with a shared-parameter gap-time mechanism — exactly the missing component — but
it was developed on HIV visit times and reports no HCC, AFP, ALBI or radiologic endpoint. [K1]
shows a joint longitudinal+survival model is estimable in on-demand TACE HCC with one marker and no
observation-process submodel (abstract_only, n=30, 11 events). [K3] shows a landmark and a
time-dependent Cox on the same real-world exposure can *disagree*, so neither comparator is
authoritative on its own. No inspected work estimates an AFP-vs-same-draw-ALBI kinetic contrast in
HCC with the observation process modelled.

**Clinical importance — two different decisions.** If the AFP-slope → progression association
survives only after modelling who gets sampled/scanned, the actionable reading is a
**surveillance-interval / imaging-trigger** policy (AFP prompts imaging). If it survives *and* the
observation-process association is weak, the reading is tumour-activity information supporting
**AFP-informed treatment escalation**. A clean null in the joint model (liver reserve alone) argues
for de-emphasising routine AFP-only trajectory monitoring. These are different clinical decisions.

## 4. Methods: baseline, substantive alternative, and the new PRIMARY estimator

- **M0 baseline (simple):** landmark Cox at t* on baseline AFP + baseline ALBI grade + age/sex/
  treatment type + draw/diagnostic intensity. Recovers level-based prognosis only; cannot separate a
  rising trajectory from a high-but-flat one and cannot separate AFP from liver reserve.
- **M4 substantive (two-stage):** linear mixed-effects submodels for log AFP and for ALBI fit on
  (index, t*], empirical-Bayes slope estimates entered into the landmark and time-dependent Cox.
  Recovers *change vs level* and *AFP vs same-draw liver reserve*. What it LOSES: it assumes the
  draw/scan times are non-informative (missing-at-random), so a slope that itself triggers more
  sampling/imaging can bias both the fitted slope and its hazard.
- **M5 PRIMARY (new, pre-specified):** a **shared-parameter joint model of the marker trajectories
  AND the observation process**:
  - Longitudinal submodel: two linear mixed-effects submodels (log AFP; ALBI) on (index, t*],
    random intercept + slope, submodels linked by correlated random effects.
  - Observation-process submodel: a gap-time (or intensity) mechanism for the times of *draws* (from
    `labs` `检验时间`) and, separately, of *imaging studies* (from `examinations` `开始时间`),
    linked to the marker submodels by correlated random effects and/or a log-hazard term in the
    current latent marker value (i.e. does a higher current true AFP raise the rate of the next draw
    / the next scan?).
  - Event submodel: Cox for time to first post-t* radiologic progression whose hazard depends on the
    shared latent AFP and ALBI trajectories; the primary estimand is the marker→event association
    (current-value and/or random-slope link), alpha_AFP and alpha_ALBI, estimated jointly with the
    observation process (method template: [K2], developed for a marker + informative visiting
    process).
  - **Observability assumptions (explicit):** (i) visit/scan intensities are correctly specified and
    their dependence on the marker is fully captured by the shared random effects + current value;
    (ii) independent censoring conditional on the shared effects; (iii) no unmeasured common cause of
    sampling and progression; (iv) the lexical progression flag is accurate (see limits). Each is a
    falsifiable or auditable assumption, not a certainty.

**Comparison of what each recovers / loses (recorded before selection):**

| model | recovers | loses / assumes |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | baseline level prognosis | change vs level; AFP vs liver reserve |
| M4 | changed-vs-level; AFP vs same-draw ALBI | draw/scan times non-informative (MAR) |
| M5 (PRIMARY) | marker→event association net of the sampling process; quantifies how informative sampling is | correct visit/scan intensity + joint identifiability; cannot exclude unmeasured common causes |

## 5. The discriminating contrast and falsification

The pre-specified quantity that separates "AFP slope predicts progression" from "who gets sampled/
scanned when" is the **change in the marker→event association between M4 (landmark/time-dependent
Cox) and M5 (joint model)**, together with the **observation-process association parameter** (does
current true AFP predict the next draw / next scan?).

- **S1 SUPPORTIVE:** the M5 joint-model AFP association CI excludes 1 with the expected direction
  (HR>1 / positive slope link), AND is consistent with the E_late stratum (E_late AFP effect CI
  excludes 1), AND the observation-process association is estimated with acceptable precision so the
  contrast is not simply unidentified. Reading: AFP kinetics carry progression information not
  explained by the observation process; supports AFP-informed treatment escalation.
- **S2 ADVERSE:** the AFP association CI excludes 1 in M4 (landmark and/or time-dependent Cox) but
  **includes 1 in M5**, and/or the observation-process association shows current AFP strongly
  predicts the next draw/scan (CI excluding 1); and/or only the ALBI link survives. Reading: the
  landmark signal is informative observation / practice pattern; argues for AFP as an imaging trigger,
  not an escalation criterion.
- **S3 FALSIFICATION / NULL:** both slope associations include 1 in M4 AND M5 and the M5-vs-M0
  delta-C-index bootstrap CI lies within a pre-set equivalence margin (initially +/-0.01).
  Reading: trajectories add nothing over baseline levels; de-emphasise routine trajectory monitoring.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** wide CIs or M5 non-convergence. **Pre-declared fallback:** (i) reduce the joint
  model to a single shared random effect per marker (intercept-only linkage); if it still does not
  converge, (ii) report an observation-intensity-weighted landmark Cox (IPTW on draw/scan intensity)
  as an explicitly approximate comparator, and (iii) label the result inconclusive for the full joint
  estimand rather than reporting a comparator as if it were the primary result. A wide-CI null is
  INCONCLUSIVE, not adverse; event rate is the solver's first feasibility output.

**Conjunctive falsification (preserved):** S1 additionally requires the retreatment concordance
kappa >= a pre-set floor; if that floor fails, the radiologic-progression result is reported as
inconclusive and the retreatment proxy is used as a secondary, weaker endpoint. Because the primary
estimator is now the joint model, S1/S2 also require the joint model to converge and to be reported
with the observation-process parameter so the two readings are actually separated.

## 6. Rivals mapped to distinct tests

Baseline reserve (adjust baseline AFP/ALBI); surveillance intensity (the observation-process
submodel itself + draw/scan intensity adjustment in M0/M4); regression-to-the-mean (two-draw vs
three-draw slope comparison); measurement/detection timing (E_early vs E_late; landmark vs
time-dependent vs joint). Distinguish only descriptive/predictive claims from mechanism: the joint
model can at most show the association is not explained by the modelled observation process; it
cannot establish causality.

## 7. Honest limits and unascertainable evidence

- The progression flag is a LEXICAL extraction from report text and is **UNVALIDATED** (frozen
  metadata validated only a cirrhosis lexical detector); a pre-registered manual audit
  (precision/recall), an agreement floor and explicit fallback are required.
- Mortality and mRECIST PFS are unascertainable -> **NO survival claim**.
- Single institution; lab units absent (AFP used as reported, log-transformed); no external
  validation; **no outcomes computed in this discovery episode**.
- The joint model requires correct specification of the draw/scan intensity; if sampling is driven by
  unmeasured factors, M5 can still be biased. Clinical adjudication of progressions and any causal
  interpretation require expert review or another study; an automatic pipeline can establish
  computation and internal consistency only.

## 8. Deferred alternatives (not chosen now, with reason)

- Full Bayesian shared-parameter joint model with alternative association structures and a
  competing-risks / multi-state progression+retreatment endpoint: deferred until the frequentist M5
  is shown to converge.
- Neural / Gaussian-process latent-trajectory model with a visit-intensity process (cf. the
  ALADYNOULLI-style learned-trajectory example): deferred because the scientific question is the
  observation-process contrast, which a transparent shared-parameter model answers; a learned latent
  model would be revisited only if the parametric trajectory is shown to misfit.
- External validation or prospective AFP-triggered imaging study: requires data beyond this snapshot.
