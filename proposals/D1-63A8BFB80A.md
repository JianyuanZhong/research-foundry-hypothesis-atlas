# Source-bound successor: semantic-attribution quarantine for heterogeneous documentary-M2 allocation

Parent anchors: \`[prior hypothesis]\` (fixed patient/whole-accession documentary-capacity envelope) and \`[prior hypothesis]\` (patient-level identity/content/lifecycle-unknown certificate).

## 1. Targeted repair and unresolved clinical question

The two parent designs establish complementary protections. The first asks whether a report-semantic queue remains superior when a fixed patient-slot budget is coupled to accession-, raw-component-, and component-plus-text ledgers. The second asks whether the same result survives conservative patient identity and stored-content integrity checks. A remaining clinically material validity defect is semantic attribution.

The inherited \(G\) arm is described as using frozen radiologist-book clinical-content variables, but a stored narrative field is not automatically a uniquely attributable, as-of-decision clinical observation. A patient can have several eligible accessions, duplicated or conflicting components, ambiguous modality/context, or content whose lifecycle is unknown. If semantic features are assembled across those rows, the queue may be credited with a “clinical” signal that cannot be tied to one clean accession or established as present at \(t_{\rm dec}\). The current source has no authored, finalized, released, retrieved, viewed, or read timestamp. This is an operational-identifiability problem, not evidence that the semantic model is clinically useless.

The repair is a prelocked semantic-attribution shadow arm and certificate:

- retain the inherited \(B\), \(R\), \(G\), \(G_{\rm mask}\), all patient and documentary-capacity estimands, all comparator/random/deletion gates, and the original 2020-only threshold rule \(q^*\);
- add a byte-identical-coefficient \(G_{\rm SA}\) replay in which the inherited semantic feature block is retained only for a patient-state whose every semantic contribution is traceable to a unique, source-clean, content-stable, modality-eligible examination accession and to the exact raw field/row ordinals used by the frozen feature manifest;
- replace the complete semantic block by the frozen training-defined unavailable block, without refitting, whenever attribution is incomplete, an accession is content-ambiguous, the semantic provenance manifest is missing, or the content is only a lifecycle-unknown stored narrative;
- evaluate \(G_{\rm SA}\) at the already frozen \(q^*\) and under the same fixed \(K\), \(C_\ell\), tie keys, coupled worlds, and whole-accession queues.

The falsifiable question is:

> Does the frozen report-semantic allocation result retain its documentary-M2 advantage and patient-level source certificate when semantic content is allowed to influence ranking only through explicitly accession- and field-attributed inputs, while lifecycle availability remains unknown?

This tests whether the result is robust to a clinically consequential attribution interpretation. It does not test treatment benefit, biological MVI, report viewing, or actual workflow time.

A supportive result would show that the inherited queue passes and the no-refit semantic-attribution replay passes the predeclared diagnostic robustness gate in both the selected 2020 cohort and untouched 2021. An adverse result would show that the semantic advantage or patient-level certificate depends on content that cannot be attributed under the declared source evidence. An inconclusive result would mean that provenance, source replay, or the required frozen semantic manifest cannot be established. None of these outcomes proves or disproves clinical usefulness.

## 3. Population, chronology, and fixed estimands

Use the inherited population without modification:

- adults aged \(\ge18\);
- first eligible source-documented hepatobiliary resection episode per patient;
- a procedure matching the surgeon-reviewed hepatobiliary-resection dictionary;
- exclusion of an earlier qualifying resection and recorded transplant, TACE/embolization, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in \([t_{\rm op}-365\mathrm d,t_{\rm op})\);
- patient-level episode key \((患者主索引,就诊号)\), retaining encounter keys for patient-wide medication/order joins.

Set \(t_{\rm op}\) to the qualifying procedure/anesthesia \`开始时间\` from \`procedures\). Treat date-like values as half-open day intervals \([date,date+24\mathrm h)\), not exact clock observations. Set \(t_{\rm dec}=t_{\rm op}-24\mathrm h\). An examination is acquisition-eligible only when its \`开始时间\` interval is wholly within \([t_{\rm op}-90\mathrm d,t_{\rm dec})\). Retain 12-, 48-, and 72-hour onset states as descriptive monotone sensitivities; 24 hours is confirmatory.

Keep patient-disjoint chronological roles exactly:

- 2015–2018: development;
- 2019: all dictionaries, preprocessing, feature/provenance manifests, models, books, tie keys, capacities, seeds, and rational threshold grid locked;
- 2020: selection of \(q^*\) only under the inherited gate;
- 2021: one untouched temporal confirmation at frozen \(q^*\);
- 2022 onward: audit only.

No 2020/2021 outcome, capacity, attribution failure, semantic witness, or identity result can modify a 2019 artifact or \(q^*\). The \(G_{\rm SA}\) replay is a diagnostic robustness analysis and cannot select, lower, or replace \(q^*\).

Retain the fixed patient-slot estimand \(K=\lfloor0.10N_{\rm ref}\rfloor\), with \(N_{\rm ref}\) and all nonvacuity conditions frozen in 2019. Retain the whole-accession and structural ledgers:

\[
w^U_u=1,\qquad
w^R_u=n_u,\qquad
w^T_u=n_u+\left\lceil b_u/512\right\rceil,
\]

where \(u=(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)\), \(n_u\) is every raw examination-row count including identical and conflicting duplicates, and \(b_u\) is the UTF-8 byte length of exact raw values in \`检查\`, \`检查所见\`, and \`检查诊断\), with zero text term when all three are blank. Freeze \(C_\ell=\lfloor0.10W^\ell_{\rm ref}\rfloor\) for \(\ell\in\{U,R,T\}\) exactly as inherited. These are source-computable ledgers, never minutes, RVUs, review time, availability, or workflow capacity.

For method \(M\), ledger \(\ell\), coupled world \(\omega\), and threshold \(q\), retain the longest-prefix queue:
\[
S_{M,\ell}^{K,C_\ell}(\omega,q)
=\{\pi_M(1),\ldots,\pi_M(m)\},
\]
where \(m\) is the largest \(j\le K\) whose cumulative whole-patient accession cost is at most \(C_\ell\). Do not skip, backfill, rerank, or spend unused capacity when the next patient exceeds capacity. Every queue has exactly the same patient set, \(K\), \(C_\ell\), tie key, EMPTY-position rule, source/reader/pathology world, and anchored-deletion rule. The primary documentary-M2 estimand remains the patient count captured under these fixed budgets, not \(H/U\) or a cost-normalized surrogate.

## 5. Source states and inherited identity/content safeguards

For each coupled world construct the episode, operation clock, CT/MRI eligibility, whole-accession identity, treatment exclusion, source-clean status, ledger values, and onset state together. A whole accession is never reduced to a selected representative row.

Use inherited onset categories \(O=0\) (readable by 72h), \(O=1\) (first readable in (72,48]h), \(O=2\) (first readable in (48,24]h), \(O=3\) (first readable in (24,12]h), and \(O=4\) (not readable by 12h, later/never, or unresolved), with visibility vector \((1\{O=0\},1\{O\le1\},1\{O\le2\},1\{O\le3\})\). These are outer possibilities for stored content, not observed report availability.

Retain the parent identity/content audit. Normalize identity signatures only by documented whitespace trimming and full-width/half-width normalization; use exact hashing, with no fuzzy matching, transliteration, probabilistic linkage, or inferred values. Flag, without resolving, a patient when an identity signature maps to multiple patient keys, a patient has conflicting nonblank identity signatures, or \((就诊号,检查号)\) occurs under multiple patient keys.

For each examination row preserve exact raw values, CSV field boundaries, source ordinal, source hash, and payload digest over \`检查\`, \`检查所见\`, \`检查诊断\`, \`开始时间\`, and \`机器型号\`. An accession is content-ambiguous for the certificate if it has conflicting payload digests, blank/nonblank mixture, cross-patient collision, irreconcilable modality/time parse, or failed byte/ordinal replay. Identical repeated rows remain one accession-level contribution with multiplicity retained.

The inherited patient certificate \(C^{ICA}_{iy}(\omega)\) is one only if every eligible accession for patient \(i\) is uniquely attributed and content-stable under those rules and no identity ambiguity applies. It is a stored-source consistency certificate only. It does not certify report lifecycle or clinical semantics.

## 6. New repair: semantic attribution certificate and no-refit shadow replay

### 6.1 Required feature provenance

For every semantic feature \(f\) in the frozen 2019 \(G\) block, the compiler must emit a provenance record containing:

1. model block and feature position/hash;
2. exact source table and field, which for HCC semantic examination features must be \`examinations\` and one or more of \`检查\`, \`检查所见\`, or \`检查诊断\` unless the inherited 2019 manifest explicitly documents another permitted HCC examination field;
3. source row ordinals and whole-accession keys used;
4. the exact raw field byte digest before normalization;
5. the deterministic normalization/tokenization/parser version and output digest;
6. the aggregation rule over rows/accessions;
7. the missing/unavailable value and whether the feature is semantic or report-surface.

A feature may not be called semantic merely because it was produced from a report-like string. Its source field and aggregation must be recoverable from the 2019 manifest and exact raw row registry. If a feature has no such mapping, its semantic block is uncertifiable.

### 6.2 World-specific semantic eligibility

For patient \(i\), year \(y\), coupled world \(\omega\), let \(F_i(\omega)\) be the set of all semantic features used in the frozen score. Define the semantic attribution certificate \(C^{SA}_{iy}(\omega)=1\) only if all of the following hold:

- every raw row contributing to every \(f\in F_i(\omega)\) has a nonblank, nonambiguous whole-accession key \((患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)\);
- each contributing accession is inside the inherited CT/MRI acquisition window and passes the inherited modality/context parser;
- every contributing accession has unique patient attribution, stable payload digest, and reproducible row/field provenance under the parent \(C^{ICA}\) rules;
- the semantic feature's source field, raw digest, ordinal set, normalization digest, and aggregation output exactly match the frozen 2019 feature manifest;
- the feature value is derived only from content available in that coupled source world; no reader, pathology, clinical-document, future, or cross-world value is borrowed;
- if the feature block is defined at patient level, the entire declared aggregation set is present and attributable. No favorable subset of accessions may be selected after seeing the endpoint, score, queue, year, ledger, reader, pathology, or onset state;
- no semantic value depends on report finalization, release, retrieval, viewing, or reading status, because those fields do not exist.

If any condition fails, set \(C^{SA}_{iy}(\omega)=0\) and replace the entire semantic block with the frozen unavailable block. Do not partially retain favorable semantic features. The source/identity flags themselves never enter the score.

This is deliberately conservative. A lifecycle-unknown stored narrative can be used in the inherited retrospective \(G\) and in \(G_{\rm SA}\) only as a stored-content feature with explicit lifecycle-unknown status; it cannot be described as a report that was available, read, viewed, released, or finalized at \(t_{\rm dec}\).

### 6.3 Shadow score, ranking, and estimands

For every patient and world, calculate the \(G_{\rm SA}\) score from the byte-identical \(G\) coefficients and either the full frozen semantic block (if \(C^{SA}=1\)) or the frozen unavailable block (if \(C^{SA}=0\)). Recompute exact score bytes and apply the inherited protected tie key. Do not tune a new threshold.

The primary \(G\) documentary-M2 result and all inherited \(G\) versus \(B,R,G_{\rm mask}\) comparator margins remain unchanged. Define the semantic-attribution diagnostic margin for \(C\in\{B,R,G_{\rm mask}\}\):
\[
D^{SA}_{G,C,\ell}(z)
=100[H_{G_{\rm SA},\ell}(z)-H_{C,\ell}(z)]/K,
\]
and the paired queue displacement:
\[
J^{SA}_{\ell}(z)
=|S_{G,\ell}^{K,C_\ell}(z)\triangle
  S_{G_{\rm SA},\ell}^{K,C_\ell}(z)|.
\]
Report \(H,m,U,U/C_\ell\) descriptively, EMPTY positions, changed ranks, and patient/accession provenance witnesses. \(H/U\) is never an estimand.

The primary new patient-level shadow loss is:
\[
d^{SA}_{iy}(q,\omega)=
1\{i\in P_{LS}(y,\omega)\}
1\{i\notin S_{G_{\rm SA}}(y,\omega,q)
\ \mathrm{OR}\ A_i(y,\omega,q)=0
\ \mathrm{OR}\ C^{ICA}_{iy}(\omega)=0
\ \mathrm{OR}\ C^{SA}_{iy}(\omega)=0\}.
\]
Use the patientwise worst coherent state, not an average over states:
\[
\theta^{SA}_y(q)=
\frac{1}{|D_y|}
\sum_{i\in D_y}
\max_{\omega\in W_y(i)}d^{SA}_{iy}(q,\omega),
\]
where \(D_y\) is the unique inherited label-stable patient union and an empty \(D_y\) is nonvacuous-inconclusive. Emit one contribution per patient, the worst-state witness, queue rank/EMPTY status, \(A_i\), \(C^{ICA}\), \(C^{SA}\), feature provenance, and stable output hashes.

The inherited \(\theta_y\) and \(\theta^{ICA}_y\) remain separately reported diagnostics. No lower aggregate loss, favorable state, favorable reader, favorable pathology book, or favorable deletion can rescue a patient-level failure. The finite corpus provides no superpopulation probability or confidence interval.

## 7. Baselines, selection, and gates

The original selection is unchanged:
\[
q^*=\min\{q\in{\cal R}:q<1,\ G_{20}(q)=1\},
\]
where \(G_{20}\) is the complete inherited 2020 gate, including comparator, random-\(K\), deletion, source/content, temporal, nonvacuity, and exact-score checks. If no \(q<1\) qualifies or \(q^*=1\), report the inherited adverse selection status; do not use \(G_{\rm SA}\) to rescue it.

At frozen \(q^*\), evaluate \(G_{\rm SA}\) in 2020 and once in untouched 2021. The diagnostic semantic-attribution gate is strict and predeclared:

- every required source/schema/hash/ordinal/feature-provenance replay passes;
- all inherited primary \(G\) gates pass;
- \(G_{\rm SA}\) is computed with byte-identical coefficients, frozen missing block, same patient set, same \((K,C_\ell)\), same whole-accession longest-prefix rule, same coupled states, same tie key, and no refit;
- \(G_{\rm SA}\) exceeds every \(B,R,G_{\rm mask}\) comparator by strict \(>5\) points at every required \((\omega,O,y,d,r,\delta,\ell)\) primary cell in both 2020 and 2021, with exact integer cross-products;
- the patientwise semantic-attribution loss is zero in both 2020 and 2021: \(\theta^{SA}_{2020}(q^*)=\theta^{SA}_{2021}(q^*)=0\);
- inherited \(C^{ICA}\) loss remains zero in both years;
- all structural ledgers \(U,R,T\) pass their inherited worst-ledger gate for the primary \(G\) result. \(G_{\rm SA}\) is reported under all three ledgers, but cannot replace the inherited primary estimand.

The \(>5\) shadow comparator condition is a robustness gate, not a claim that the no-refit shadow model is a deployable model. If the parent protocol defines a different exact comparator/random-\(K\) gate in its frozen artifact, that exact stronger rule controls; the compiler must not weaken it.

## 8. Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive precedence

Return one primary status in this order:

1. **computationally_inconclusive** if any source/catalog/schema hash, raw ordinal/byte replay, declared join, temporal quarantine, feature-to-source mapping, frozen model/hash, exact-score/tie, fixed-\(K\), EMPTY, patientwise-max, no-refit, output-linkage, or \(q^*\)-freeze check fails;
2. **feasibility_inconclusive** if required \(N\), \(K\), event, book, label-stability, or nonvacuity conditions fail;
3. **selection_adverse_no_subcomplete_threshold** if the inherited computation has no \(q<1\) passing \(G_{20}\), or \(q^*=1\);
4. **inherited_gate_adverse** if any inherited primary comparator, random-\(K\), deletion, identity/content, structural-ledger, or temporal gate fails;
5. **semantic_attribution_adverse** if inherited selection is valid but any required \(G_{\rm SA}\) comparator margin is not strict \(>5\), any patientwise \(\theta^{SA}\) is nonzero, any semantic feature is masked in a required cell, or the frozen semantic result changes under the attribution certificate;
6. **semantic_attribution_supportive** only if all inherited gates and all predeclared \(G_{\rm SA}\) shadow gates pass in 2020 and untouched 2021 with exact witnesses;
7. append **lifecycle_availability_evidence_inconclusive** to every status because the source lacks finalization/release/retrieval/view timestamps.

“Supportive” means only that the finite HCC snapshot's frozen semantic queue property is robust to the declared source-attribution quarantine under fixed documentary budgets. “Adverse” means the property depends on semantically unattributed or non-reproducible content, or the shadow queue fails the strict margin; it does not prove semantic imaging is useless. “Inconclusive” means computation, feasibility, or the unavailable lifecycle evidence prevents the stronger interpretation.

Automatic verification can establish source lineage, exact feature provenance, deterministic masking, queue arithmetic, temporal freeze/replay, and whether conclusions follow from computed witnesses. It cannot establish true patient identity, semantic clinical correctness, report lifecycle, specimen-linked biology, clinician action, safety, fairness, transportability, cost-effectiveness, or benefit. Those require expert review, source-system lifecycle/archive evidence, specimen-linked pathology, linked outcomes, and/or a separate prospective silent-mode or decision-appropriate interventional study.

Automatic verification can establish deterministic provenance, masking, source lineage, finite-census queue arithmetic, chronology, and whether conclusions follow from computed outputs. It cannot decide whether the conservative certificate corresponds to true human identity, whether the stored text is clinically correct, whether a report was available in care, whether pathology is specimen-linked, or whether the queue would improve a consequential decision. Those require expert adjudication, lifecycle/archive data, specimen linkage, linked outcomes, and another study.
