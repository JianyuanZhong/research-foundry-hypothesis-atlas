## Unresolved question, evidence, and hypothesis

The clinical decision remains: after vasopressor-treated shock has resolved, should a markedly fluid-positive adult who remains invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48, with adequate recorded MAP, urine output and potassium, start/restart an ICU-input-system recorded furosemide or bumetanide delivery during the next 12 hours while the uniquely identified baseline recorded ventilation interval remains open, or avoid such onset until that interval closes or 12 hours elapse?

The strongest supported claim is limited. MIMIC-IV 3.1 contains time-stamped but optional procedure, chart, medication-input, laboratory, output, transfer and disposition records from which this recorded policy and bounded outcomes can be computed. The inspected full XML of Kuriyama et al. 2026 (DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`; PMCID `PMC13476641`; frozen source `[source checksum]`) reports heterogeneous, mostly low- or very-low-certainty trial evidence and does not establish the benefit, safety or timing of this policy. Napoli et al. 2026 (DOI `10.62675/2965-2774.20260480`; PMCID `PMC13399242`; frozen full XML `[source checksum]`) uses 48-hour reintubation as a primary outcome and reintubation or death within seven days as a secondary outcome, while judging the four-trial evidence low-certainty and underpowered. Neither paper validates MIMIC airway records or this observational contrast.

The remaining weakness is that inherited RHA-E48-D5 becomes positive after X+48 hours even when exact death or a recurrent airway record occurs later before `D7=t0+7d`. The ordered audit in `episode67_source_audit.md` first established source semantics without treatment or outcomes, then demonstrated that 102/1,826 (5.6%) broad unique-V0 records meeting a procedure/death-only X+48 candidate rule had an exact later contradiction by D7: 61 later airway procedures and 45 later exact deaths, with overlap. This does not estimate an effect or final-cohort rate. It falsifies the assumption that X+48 always captures the clinically relevant day-7 path.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> In the prespecified measured-overlap population, the A12-minus-B12 association lower bound for alive, recorded, day-7 durable airway liberation (ARDL7) exceeds +0.05, while one-sided upper limits remain below +0.03 for recorded creatinine-rise/death and terminal harm and below +0.05 for recorded RRT/death, with no Holm-rejected adverse family, no material 48-hour/day-7 reversal, and all identification and observation gates passing.

This is a hypothesis about a recorded-policy association. It is not a claim that loop diuretics were received, caused extubation, improved survival, or are safe.
