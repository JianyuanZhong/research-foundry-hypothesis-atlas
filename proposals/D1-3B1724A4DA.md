# Provisional hypothesis: discordant AKI recovery and subsequent ICU deterioration

Status: provisional, episode 1. This is a research question, not a completed result.

## Proposed relationship
Among adults with an ICU stay and a prespecified acute kidney injury (AKI) onset, creatinine improvement without concurrent improvement in urine output will identify a distinct subgroup with higher risk of subsequent ICU deterioration than creatinine improvement with improving urine output. The unresolved claim is that the discordance adds prognostic information beyond the patient’s current severity and AKI stage, rather than merely reflecting persistent illness or measurement practice.

## Grounding from available data and expert context
The supplied MIMIC expert seed `[starting question]` (candidate `[prior hypothesis]`) proposes exactly this opening: patients whose creatinine falls without improvement in urine output or fluid status may remain at high risk. It is explicitly untested; I am not treating it as evidence of an effect. The MIMIC source catalog exposes `hosp/labevents` (creatinine), `icu/outputevents` (urine output), `icu/icustays` (ICU time and outcomes), `icu/inputevents`/`icu/ingredientevents` (fluid and treatment context), and `hosp/admissions` (disposition and hospital outcome). These are the concrete data needed to test the proposed relationship. The exact archive is `mimic-iv-3.1.zip`, with the documented table members listed in `datasets/mimic/README.md`; the source snapshot is read-only.

No effect estimate, prevalence, or clinical conclusion has been computed yet. The seed’s cited cystatin-C paper is not yet inspected here and therefore is not used as evidence.

## Falsifiable test
Define AKI onset from serial creatinine using a prespecified KDIGO-compatible rule, then define a landmark 24 hours after onset with adequate observations. Classify recovery as creatinine improvement with urine-output improvement, creatinine improvement with urine-output non-improvement, or neither. Compare subsequent 48-hour ICU deterioration (new/worsening renal replacement therapy or escalation of organ support, with death as a competing outcome) using a time-varying model that conditions only on information available at the landmark. Pre-specify sensitivity analyses for measurement frequency, fluid balance, vasopressor exposure, and exclusion of patients already receiving renal replacement therapy.

The hypothesis is supported if discordant recovery has a clinically meaningful, uncertainty-quantified excess risk after adjustment and remains directionally consistent in sensitivity analyses. It is adverse if the association disappears after accounting for current severity/measurement intensity or if the effect is confined to patients with missing urine-output observations. It is inconclusive if event counts, landmark eligibility, or outcome ascertainment are too sparse or too dependent on unavailable adjudication.

## Uncertainties and missing evidence
MIMIC does not provide measured GFR or cystatin C, so this can test only suspected discordant recovery, not true kidney function. Urine output and treatment records are selectively measured; fluid balance is an imperfect proxy for volume status. The available data cannot establish a causal mechanism or prove that changing fluids/monitoring would improve outcomes. Clinical adjudication of AKI stage, renal replacement therapy indication, and deterioration is required before treatment claims. Exact column names, units, joins, and row-level feasibility still require schema inspection and a bounded diagnostic.

## Method comparison to develop
A simple baseline will be a prespecified landmark logistic/Cox model using AKI stage, current creatinine/urine-output categories, and key severity covariates. A substantive alternative will model the joint trajectory of creatinine and urine output over the landmark window (for example, a two-state latent recovery model or a regularized survival model with trajectory features) to distinguish persistent oliguria from transient measurement gaps. The alternative could reveal whether timing and persistence matter, information lost by a single landmark category; it must be compared on held-out temporal splits with calibration and uncertainty, not selected solely for a small predictive gain. CPU is adequate for the baseline; the alternative’s resource requirement is currently unverified.
