# Episode 91: competing-death and temporal durability qualification of the frozen AG1-versus-AF1 experiment

## Decision question and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`, with `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]` retained as direct scientific/allocation and endpoint parents. The immediate parent already standardizes to prior nonrenal diagnosis-record density and contrasts a broad nonrenal recorded-diagnosis endpoint. This child does not repeat that analysis.

The unresolved threat is competing death combined with temporal fragility. A five-year first inpatient N18 contrast can be difficult to interpret if one roster has a different death-first pattern: death removes the person’s opportunity for a later N18 code, while a late-window contrast can also reflect changing observation and coding processes. The parent reports death as a diagnostic, but does not require the N18 contrast to be present early or require death-first risks to be compatible between policies.

The new falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the exact frozen O1_hold=2,334 and equal-capacity n1=466 comparison, the AG1-versus-AF1 five-year first recorded inpatient N18-before-death enrichment is temporally durable: it is already positive by a prespecified two-year landmark, and neither policy has a distinguishable excess of death before N18 through two or five years that could make the five-year contrast a survivor/opportunity artifact.

This advances the inherited question from “is there a five-year recorded N18 contrast?” to “is that contrast an early, reproducible signal under comparable competing-death experience?” A supportive result would justify an externally adjudicated durability study of the same targeting idea; it would not establish CKD, measured GFR, causal benefit, or deployment. An adverse durability result would be clinically useful: a five-year positive contrast that appears only after two years would not support treating it as a robust near-term kidney-targeting signal.

The strongest available evidence supports only feasibility and plausibility. The inherited audits support exact cohort/roster reconstruction, availability of paired inpatient diagnosis/date arrays and two death fields, and 40 all-holdout five-year N18-before-death events before policy outcomes are opened. A current Europe PMC search also returns standard competing-risk analyses that treat death as a competing event, including kidney-outcome work, but that search evidence does not establish this AG1/AF1 contrast. No available evidence supports AG1 superiority, measured-GFR accuracy, incident or persistent CKD, causal benefit, or transportability.

## Gates, falsification and result taxonomy

Before any supportive or adverse label, require exact catalog/snapshot/source/descriptor/schema/header/key identities; one-to-one eid joins; exact O/O1/O1_hold and n1 reconstruction; exact parent model and roster artifacts; no outcome leakage; row-level agreement of every one of the 520 endpoint fields between main and health_outcomes for O1_hold; no pre-index inpatient N17/N18; strict code/date position pairing; valid t1/t2/t5 chronology; no death on or before t1; correct same-day death ordering; exact E2/E5/EL/C2/C5 identities; at least 10 E5 events in AG1 union AF1, at least 3 E5 events in each policy list, at least 4 E5 events in the symmetric difference, and at least 5 E2 events in AG1 union AF1 with at least 2 in each policy list. Require at least 95% finite paired bootstrap draws and positive variance for every tested component. These are information gates, not power claims.

Any changed denominator, roster, capacity, t1, t2 or t5 rule; fit/rank/refill using an outcome; missing-as-none for malformed fields; N17 substitution; code/date mispairing; boundary inclusion error; same-day-death error; duplicate disagreement; non-one-to-one join; invalid date silently discarded; bootstrap mismatch; or conclusion not keyed to computed outputs is a falsification/noncomputation failure. Gate failure is inconclusive, never adverse.

The successor emits exactly one downstream qualification after the inherited parent labels:

- **temporal_competing_supportive** requires the inherited five-year E18 parent result to be supportive; all successor gates; the simultaneous F91 lower limit for Delta5 at least m; the simultaneous lower limit for Delta2 strictly above 0; and both two-sided 95% F91 intervals for DeltaC2 and DeltaC5 to contain 0. The last condition means no statistically distinguishable death-first contrast at the prespecified horizons; it is not equivalence and does not prove absence of mortality imbalance. Support means the observed five-year recorded N18 enrichment is compatible with an early signal and with unresolved, rather than demonstrably differential, death-first capture.

- **temporal_competing_adverse** requires all gates, the inherited five-year parent result supportive, and the simultaneous upper limit for Delta2 strictly below 0. This falsifies the prespecified durable-early-signal qualification: any five-year support is not reproduced by two years. It does not falsify five-year N18 enrichment itself, prove AF1 superiority, prove a coding artifact, or establish harm.

- **temporal_competing_inconclusive** is every other result: inherited parent adverse/inconclusive; early contrast touching or crossing zero; death contrast distinguishable in either direction; sparse E2 or unstable late/death states; missing finite draws; boundary equality; identity failure; or any provenance/chronology/noncomputability failure. A death imbalance is deliberately inconclusive rather than adverse because these data cannot identify whether it reflects biology, capture, censoring, or selection.

Report the late increment even though it is not a support gate. A positive five-year result with Delta2 nonpositive and DeltaL positive is specifically labeled a late-only pattern and is adverse for durability when the strict Delta2 upper gate is met. A five-year result that is adverse cannot be rescued by early or death diagnostics. The H1/D1 hierarchy, AF1 comparison and parent labels cannot be rescued by this child.
