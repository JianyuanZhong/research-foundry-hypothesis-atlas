## Scientific change and unresolved decision

The parent asks whether prior values should be retained when a single fixed number of review slots is available for people in a frozen central current-risk band. This child changes the scientific estimand from the value of prior levels to the value of within-person change: when current values and the interval between assessments are already known, does a verified deterioration signal from the prior-to-current pair alter who should occupy the same limited review list?

Primary question:

Among participants in the parent’s frozen A band, does adding the eight-field within-person change vector to current values, age, sex, and verified repeat spacing increase held-out capture and yield of subsequent recorded target events when exactly K review slots are available, and does that increment remain stable when an early-fitted model is applied without refitting to the 2013 assessment cohort?

K is defined before outcome inspection as floor(10% of the eligible early A population), and the same K is used in late A. This is a capacity-normalized longitudinal information estimand, not a claim that a 10% list is a validated clinical workflow.

The expert UKB-09 seed motivates the question but is not evidence. Its proposed “cardiovascular and kidney multimorbidity” is narrowed here because the locally verified outcome binding supports the parent’s recorded circulatory T/D/N endpoint, not a certified kidney-disease or multimorbidity phenotype. This child tests whether change adds information for this recorded circulatory review task. A positive result would justify a prospective study of change-aware review prioritization; a negative result would argue against retaining a second measurement merely to calculate change for this task. Neither result establishes disease onset, treatment benefit, or clinical utility.

## Models and matched alternatives

All models use the same eligible people, endpoint, split, frozen A band, K, held-out evaluation and uncertainty procedure.

- M0 current-only risk: current eight-field panel + age + sex + T1 calendar year.
- M1a transparent baseline: M0 + L, the verified interval between T0 and T1. This is the primary counterfactual because it includes current levels and repeat spacing but no prior level or change.
- M1d transparent change model: M1a + eight signed, training-fold-standardized changes. For field j, d_ij=s_j*(x_current-x_prior)/a_j, where s_j is the dictionary-certified worsening direction and a_j is a positive training-fold scale fixed before validation. It is a predictive change estimand, not a claim that two points identify a turning point.
- M2a matched learned baseline: shallow gradient-boosted multinomial T/D/N model with exactly M1a inputs.
- M2d matched learned change model: the same booster and prespecified small depth, learning-rate, regularization and tree-limit grid with exactly M1d inputs. Training-only early stopping/inner validation is permitted.

M1d is the primary transparent test. The learned M2d alternative is scientifically substantive because it can detect nonlinear change thresholds, current-change discordance and interactions with L that an additive model can average away. Its value is evaluated by fixed-K event capture/yield, calibration, fold stability and temporal stability, not by a small score gain. It cannot rescue a failed semantic, timing, ascertainment or M1d primary gate.

The literal change vector is unavailable unless the field dictionary certifies all s_j, units and cross-instance comparability. If the dictionary only supports numeric comparability but not a clinical worsening direction, report a separately labelled representation audit if useful, but do not call it deterioration or use it for the primary result.

## Primary and continuity estimands

For model M and era e, let R_M,i be the held-out predicted probability of T and B_M,i(K) indicate membership among the K largest R_M,i in A. For the primary unweighted analysis, and separately for frozen selection weights w_i:

C_A(M,e) = sum_i w_i * 1(Y_i=T) * B_M,i(K) / sum_i w_i * 1(Y_i=T)

P_A(M,e) = sum_i w_i * 1(Y_i=T) * B_M,i(K) / sum_i w_i * B_M,i(K).

The primary deterioration increment is:

Delta_C_change(e) = C_A(M1d,e) - C_A(M1a,e)
Delta_P_change(e) = P_A(M1d,e) - P_A(M1a,e)

The primary hypothesis is Delta_C_change(late)>0.05, with a complete two-sided 95% interval above the prespecified five-percentage-point operational margin, no early-to-late direction reversal, and tau_C_change = Delta_C_change(late)-Delta_C_change(early) within [-0.05,0.05]. The margin is a research decision margin, not a validated clinical-benefit threshold. Report actual K, selected counts, capture/yield numerators and denominators, missed-target rate, D capture/yield, and reviewed versus non-reviewed T/D/N frequencies.

Report the matched learned contrast Delta_C_learned=C_A(M2d)-C_A(M2a), its yield analogue, calibration and stable component/interaction summaries as a secondary scientific alternative. Also report M0 versus M1a to show spacing contribution, M1d versus M0 to show total augmentation, and M1b versus M1a as the parent’s prior-level continuity contrast. A favorable prior-level result does not support the change hypothesis; a favorable learned score does not support deterioration unless the same change-specific permutation and semantic gates pass.

Retain the parent continuity estimands:
d(g,e)=BS(M1b,g,e)-BS(M1a,g,e)
D(e)=d(A,e)-[d(L,e)+d(U,e)]/2
tau_A=D(late)-D(early)

Report multiclass Brier/log loss and calibration for all models in early and late eras. These are continuity results, not replacements for the new fixed-K change estimand.

## Interpretation

Supportive evidence requires all structural, code-domain, semantic, direction, timing, ascertainment and join gates; feasible identical K; adequate support and overlap; calibrated held-out predictions; positive transparent Delta_C_change(late) beyond 0.05 with a complete interval above the margin; no material early-to-late reversal; consistent selection-weighted direction; stable fold-level behavior; and no permutation or random-capacity failure. This supports only a conditional, capacity-normalized association between a verified two-assessment change vector and capture of the specified recorded inpatient T endpoint in the supported UKB A-band population. The learned model can strengthen the scientific account if its matched change increment is stable and reveals prespecified nonlinear or discordant patterns, but a learned improvement alone is not proof of a deterioration mechanism.

Adverse evidence includes no change increment, worsening or reversed capture, a gain explained by spacing or prior levels rather than change, disappearance under selection weighting or change permutation, poor calibration, fold instability, early-to-late reversal, or invalid null controls. This weakens the case for using a repeat-derived change signal for this recorded-risk review task; it does not prove no value for another population, outcome or workflow.

Inconclusive evidence includes missing semantic/direction/timing/ascertainment certification, malformed arrays, death disagreement, sparse events, poor support, broad intervals, conflicting missingness sensitivities, nonidentifiable bands, or an unstable learned model. It is also inconclusive if a change signal is measurable but cannot be distinguished from repeat-selection bias, assay drift, treatment, regression to the mean or calendar-practice change with the available data.

Computationally checkable claims are source/header/schema integrity, joins, flow counts, exact endpoint construction, event ordering, change construction, model fits, held-out predictions, K selection, capture/yield arithmetic, calibration, bootstrap intervals, overlap/ESS and permutation behavior. Clinical review or another study is required for analyte meaning beyond the approved dictionary, specimen validity, disease onset, outpatient disease, severity, kidney disease or multimorbidity, complete ascertainment beyond supplied certification, treatment effects, action utilities, clinical thresholds, external validation and prospective clinical policy evaluation. The two measurements cannot identify a turning point or establish a biological mechanism. No automatic verifier can establish those clinical claims.

The late cohort is an internal temporal application within the same UKB discovery partition, not external validation. Selection weights address only the declared repeat-panel/eligibility sensitivity model. They do not repair participation bias, missing external populations, changing ascertainment or clinical practice.

## Alternatives considered and revisit conditions

The chosen transparent baseline is M1a because it is the clinically interpretable counterfactual: current values and time between measurements are available at the decision point, while change is withheld. M1d is selected because its exact signed, scaled inputs test the seed’s claim directly and allow an additive estimate of information beyond current levels. M2a/M2d are matched learned alternatives with the same target, split, inputs, endpoint and uncertainty; the booster can reveal thresholds and current-change discordance that a linear additive model averages away. They are not selected for complexity or a small predictive gain.

The parent’s M1b prior-level comparison, fixed-K allocation, 10%-within-Q policy and equal-quota scenario are retained as continuity/sensitivity outputs, not silently relabeled as evidence for deterioration. Raw unsigned changes are deferred unless a direction dictionary is unavailable; using them as primary would conflate deterioration with arbitrary numeric movement. A latent factor or mechanistic aging model is deferred because the catalog does not provide verified analyte semantics, specimen timing or a validated mechanistic target. Disease-sequence, genetic, imaging and multimorbidity extensions are deferred because the configured files do not provide the required verified modalities or endpoint definitions. Revisit them only with a certified field dictionary, event chronology, kidney/multimorbidity adjudication and a distinct prespecified estimand.
