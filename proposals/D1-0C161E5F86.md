# Two-axis loss--instability audit for HCC documentary-M2 retention

## 1. Targeted successor and unresolved question

This Episode-84 child of `[prior hypothesis]` makes one focused repair. It preserves the established adult first-resection population, operation-clock alternatives, 24-hour decision deadline, 90-day CT/MRI and 365-day prior-treatment windows, 2015--2018 development/2019 lock/2020 and 2021 tests, complete reader books, locked B/R/G/Gmask scores, fixed-K queues, strict comparator and random-capture gates, coupled source/onset/reader/pathology states, anchored deletion, q_req, q_stable, q_M2 and q_dec, and all existing nonclinical limits.

The remaining threat is attribution. In the parent, q_stable < q_M2 is evidence that some maximum-content documentary-M2 positives are book-dependent, but the comparison can still combine two mechanisms:

1. incomplete stored report bundles can remove an oracle-selected patient from the G queue; and
2. the patient's documentary M2 label can change between qualified pathology books.

A q frontier that includes the inherited aggregate PASS requirement can also be dominated by q_req, hiding which positive stratum actually drives the frontier. Neither mechanism is biological MVI, and neither is actual report unavailability.

The unresolved question is therefore: among patients selected by the same maximum-stored-content G queue, is the incomplete-bundle loss concentrated in documentary-positive patients whose label is stable across pathology books, concentrated in patients whose documentary label is unstable, or present in both strata in the same state? This is clinically useful because it identifies the next evidence investment: an operational report-bundle/availability audit, pathology adjudication and specimen linkage, or both. It is a retrospective documentary attribution, not a causal claim about either source of error.

The substantive advance is a pre-PASS decomposition. The child adds two retention-only frontiers, `r_stable` and `r_unstable`, and an exact statewise 2x2 ledger. It retains q_stable and q_M2 as the decision-facing endpoints but verifies:

`q_stable = max(q_req, r_stable)`

`q_M2 = max(q_req, r_stable, r_unstable)`

on the same finite threshold lattice, with explicit non-vacuity and boundary witnesses. This shows whether a q_stable/q_M2 gap is truly positive-stratum-specific rather than merely inherited from the aggregate gate.

## 7. New class-specific estimands and interaction ledger

For each nonpathology state a and complete pathology book b, let `S_max(a)` be the common maximum-content queue. Define:

- `D(a) = {i in S_max(a): Y_i(b)=1 for every complete pathology book b}`, the stable-positive set;
- `U(a) = {i in S_max(a): Y_i(b)=1 for at least one book and Y_i(b)=0 for at least one book}`, the unstable-positive set;
- `A(a)=D(a) union U(a)`, the any-book-positive set.

These are documentary classes only. Do not call D biological MVI, U true/false disagreement, or either book a reference standard.

For every q in R and every allowed a, compute the exact 2x2 ledger:

- `D_retained=|D intersect S_G(a,q)|`, `D_lost=|D minus S_G(a,q)|`;
- `U_retained=|U intersect S_G(a,q)|`, `U_lost=|U minus S_G(a,q)|`.

Also emit denominators, exact fractions `D_lost/|D|` and `U_lost/|U|` when defined, and the rational finite-corpus contrast

`Delta_int(a,q)=U_lost/|U| - D_lost/|D|`.

Delta is descriptive evidence of differential concentration of incomplete stored-content loss; no p-value, causal interaction, or transportable effect is claimed. Record undefined cells rather than imputing zero. At each q include hashes of D, U, A, lost sets, retained sets, EMPTY positions and ordered-slot disagreements. Report the full statewise ledger, not just a pooled average; a pooled average cannot override a failing allowed state.

Define retention-only class frontiers, without the inherited aggregate PASS condition:

`r_stable = min {q in R: D(a) subseteq S_G(a,q) for every allowed a with Q(a)>=q}`.

`r_unstable = min {q in R: U(a) subseteq S_G(a,q) for every allowed a with Q(a)>=q}`.

Require D(a) nonempty in every required state for r_stable, as the parent requires for q_stable. Require U(a) nonempty in every required state for r_unstable. If U is empty in any required state, set r_unstable and the pathology-specific attribution to undefined with an explicit zero/empty reason; do not accept vacuous universal support. q_stable and q_M2 remain exactly the parent's PASS-constrained definitions. In the non-vacuous same-state implementation, verify the exact decomposition:

`q_stable=max(q_req,r_stable)`;

`q_M2=max(q_req,r_stable,r_unstable)`;

and therefore `q_req<=q_stable<=q_M2<=q_dec`. Any failure of this identity, state alignment or set partition is computationally inconclusive.

For every class frontier, emit the immediately lower exact threshold witness and its statewise `D_lost,U_lost,Delta_int`. Also emit the high-coverage witness at `q_hi=max{Q(a):Q(a)<1}` when such a state exists. These witnesses make the next decision depend on observed losses at the frontier, not on a favorable mean or an arbitrary cutoff.

## 8. Pre-specified evidence decision

Keep the inherited primary one-hot outcome unchanged and emit a separate one-hot `attribution_result`:

1. `attribution_inconclusive`: source/book/queue/partition/invariant failure, a required class is empty, no valid lower witness exists, or signs are mixed without a prespecified dominance pattern. This does not falsify retention.
2. `pathology_adjudication_priority`: U is nonempty in every required state, `r_unstable>r_stable), and every minimal r_unstable/q_M2 failure witness has U_lost>0 and D_lost=0, with positive Delta wherever both denominators exist. This supports investing first in pathology specimen/accession/time linkage and expert adjudication for the documentary instability.
3. `report_bundle_queue_audit_priority`: `r_stable>r_unstable), and every minimal r_stable/q_M2 failure witness has D_lost>0 and U_lost=0, with negative Delta wherever both denominators exist. This supports investing first in an operational report-bundle/queue audit, while still not establishing actual availability.
4. `joint_report_pathology_signal`: a valid minimal boundary witness has D_lost>0 and U_lost>0 in the same state, or both class-specific loss cells are required across the q_M2 witnesses. This is the computable interaction result and requires both evidence streams; it is adverse to either single-source attribution.
5. `no_observed_class_loss`: both retention-only frontiers are <1, all required high-coverage/boundary ledgers have D_lost=U_lost=0, and q_M2<1. This is supportive of a finite documentary retention reserve, but supplies no preference for an unavailable external evidence bridge.

Precedence is computational/feasibility inconclusive, then the inherited maximum-content/aggregate/patient-retention outcomes, then this attribution result. Equality is not strict dominance: r_unstable=r_stable or r_stable=r_unstable without a clean witness is attribution-inconclusive unless the same-state two-cell condition qualifies as joint_report_pathology_signal. A stable loss is never recoded as pathology-only, and an unstable label is never recoded as biological truth.

Supportive primary outcomes mean only that the frozen finite-corpus stored-content queue property passed. Adverse outcomes falsify the relevant documentary retention claim under at least one allowed state. Inconclusive outcomes mean the data or computation cannot identify the claim. The verifier must reject q=1 described as supportive, a failed computation described as adverse, or any conclusion claiming biological MVI, actual report availability, clinical action, benefit, safety or fair substitution.

## 9. Exact data required beyond HCC

HCC has no report authored/final/release/ingestion/retrieval/view timestamps, immutable report/version IDs or amendment lineage. An operational bridge requires those event logs, payload hashes at the deadline, parsing/readability records, all nonresponders and a simultaneous patient-level lower bound on the unchanged N/n_A frame. HCC cannot distinguish missing source storage from missing report release or clinician access.

HCC also lacks pathology specimen/accession/time linkage, sampling protocol, blocks/slides, raw images and a validated MVI adjudication standard. Expert pathology review can adjudicate documentary labels and, with tissue linkage, biological MVI; agreement among books alone cannot establish biological MVI. Queue rank-to-action mapping, review capacity, treatment, recurrence, survival, harms, quality of life, utility, fairness and benefit require workflow observation, linked outcomes, prospective silent-mode validation and ultimately a decision-appropriate interventional or implementation study.
