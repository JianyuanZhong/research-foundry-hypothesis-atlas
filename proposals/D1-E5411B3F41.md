# Reader-envelope robustness of canonical report semantics for fixed-capacity documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Targeted successor and frozen scientific target

This proposal is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that parent’s source-coupled first eligible resection population, canonical visible-text surrogate, whole-unit template/treatment/pathology quarantine, chronology-safe finite ambiguity ledger, documentary M2 endpoint, fixed `K_.10`, additive elastic-net logistic capacity, 2015–2018 development/2019 tuning/separate 2020 and 2021 tests, exact same-world outer extrema, strict margin greater than 5 captures per 100 positions, repaired pure-ridge grid, and nondeployment/nonbiological claim limits.

The sole scientific repair is to stop replacing blinded-reader disagreement about radiology concepts with one third-resolved label. Three independent complete reader annotations are retained as observed alternative encodings. Reader assignment is added to the parent’s finite state envelope, and the one locked full model is fitted, tuned, and evaluated robustly across those encodings. No reader consensus, majority label, estimated latent truth, reliability weight, new predictor family, outcome, temporal boundary, population rule, capacity, or margin is introduced.

Sections 1–10 of the parent and its normative Section 11 remain controlling except where this proposal explicitly replaces concept adjudication, `X_clean`, the full-model robust objective, tuning/evaluation worlds, result labels, required outputs, and verifier fixtures. A compiler change to population, clocks, estimand, outcomes, model class, capacity, margin, or falsification rules is a scientific change requiring a proposed child, not a mechanical repair.

## 2. Supported claim, unresolved claim, and clinical advance

### Strongest claim supported before this experiment

The exact HCC snapshot contains encounter-linkable procedures, examinations, pathology, medications, non-drug orders, and encounter demographics. It supports construction of the parent’s source-defined first-resection frame and finite source-state alternatives. Examination rows contain an accession-like `examination number`, acquisition-like `start time`, `examination` modality text, `machine model`, and eventual `examination findings`/`examination diagnosis` narratives. Those narratives can be transformed into the parent’s source-byte-traceable canonical text-node stream `V`, markup stream `A`, and parse-error ledger `E`; clean whole CT/MRI units can then be independently annotated if the expert-review gates pass.

The source contains no radiologist concept annotations, reader roster, reader identity, reader qualifications, consensus history, raw CT/MRI images, report author/final/release/version/view time, pathology time, pathology specimen/accession/slide/block linkage, sampling record, recurrence, survival, treatment response, workflow action, harm, cost, or benefit. Therefore neither a single concept label nor a reader-uncertainty set is already supported by source data. Both require new blinded clinical review. Documentary M2 remains an untimed same-encounter text abstraction, not established biological high-grade microvascular invasion.

A current full public article was inspected: Huang et al., 2026, DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`, frozen full-HTML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]. In that image-review study, two blinded radiologists independently assessed qualitative HCC CT features; reported Cohen kappa was 0.82 for tumor margin, 0.79 for capsular interruption, and 0.85 for intratumoral hyperplastic vessels, with discrepancies resolved by consensus. It supports only the premise that clinically used imaging-feature interpretation can vary between readers despite predefined criteria. It does not quantify Chinese report-text annotation, validate this rubric, make any reader biologically correct, or validate documentary M2.

The research-ambition README was inspected. It reports full article availability for the natural-history and Bayesian demonstrations but not for the Cell cancer main article/full STAR Methods. No unavailable Cell article text is claimed as read or used.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

Among adults in the parent’s frozen first eligible source-documented HCC resection frame, with same-encounter documentary HCC pathology and no recorded qualifying HCC treatment in the preceding 365 days:

> Does one locked model using only radiologist-annotated concepts anchored to clean canonical text from whole pre-cutoff CT/MRI accessions improve encounter-documentary M2 capture by strictly more than 5 per 100 positions at exactly the same fixed `K_.10`, over both (i) an independently optimized identical-surface/availability comparator and (ii) the same fitted model under complete concept masking, for every admissible coupled source/outcome state and every admissible assignment of one of the three observed blinded-reader annotation bundles to each patient, separately in 2020 and 2021?

The advance over the parent is not a more accurate “gold standard.” It is a stronger, computable robustness claim: support cannot depend on the third resolver choosing one clinically contestable meaning. Conversely, failure identifies reader-sensitive or uniformly insufficient ranking value under the declared observed-reader envelope; it does not identify which reader is correct and does not negate the underlying biology.

Clinical importance and solver difficulty are separate. If report-semantic ranking exceeds the margin only under one reader’s interpretation, it is not mature enough to justify a fixed-capacity clinical review pathway. Universal support would justify source-rendering/version-log acquisition and prospective silent-mode evaluation, not triage, treatment, or benefit claims.

## 5. Finite reader-uncertainty representation

### 5.1 Three independent complete bundles

After a rubric/manual and frozen examples are finalized using no pathology grade, model score, test outcome, top-K result, or year-role information, three qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists independently annotate every distinct possible selected CT/MRI whole unit appearing in any `S+` state. Presentation order is independently randomized. They cannot see each other’s labels or spans.

For each unit `u`, modality `m`, and reader `j in J={1,2,3}`, serialize one complete bundle `B_umj` containing:

- the inherited full concept vector for maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites;
- every inherited categorical level, including `present`, `absent`, `uncertain`, `not-mentioned`, and `source-text-unavailable`, plus any parent-rubric normalized value carried by diameter/count;
- for each `present` or `uncertain` item, all signed `V` character spans and reversible row/field/source-byte intervals;
- for each `absent` or `not-mentioned` item, signed confirmation that the complete `V` unit was reviewed and no positive span was used;
- reader pseudonym, rubric version/hash, blinded-session identifier, completion timestamp, and bundle SHA-256.

If `Q_m=0`, every reader bundle for that modality is deterministically the one `quarantined-unavailable` block and no semantic value is retained. If `Q_m=1`, missing task completion is not converted to unavailable: it fails the annotation-completeness gate.

No consensus concept, third-resolved concept, majority vote, Dawid–Skene/latent-class estimate, confidence-weighted average, probability target, or adjudicator-preferred label enters a primary feature. Mechanical data-entry correction is allowed only against the reader’s signed source form, before outcomes are opened, with old/new bytes and reason retained.

### 5.2 Whole-bundle and whole-patient coupling

A plausible alternative is an observed reader’s complete interpretation, not a componentwise invention. For source state `s in S_i+`, reader `j` supplies the complete patient concept vector

`X_ij(s) = concat(B_u,m,j)`

for every selected CT/MRI unit in that state. The same reader index applies to all modalities and selected units for that patient. It is forbidden to combine reader 1’s capsule label with reader 2’s thrombus label, choose labels concept-by-concept, or choose spans from a different reader.

Define the finite augmented patient-state set

`S_i^R = unique_bytes{(s,j,X_ij(s)): s in S_i+, j in {1,2,3}}`.

Exact byte-identical duplicates may be quotient-collapsed with multiplicity and backpointers; semantically similar but nonidentical bundles may not. Nonmember states do not multiply by meaningless reader choices. A complete analysis world selects exactly one augmented state per patient subject to all inherited relational/source constraints. This represents a roster in which each patient could have been interpreted by any one of the three observed blinded readers. It is finite and directly computable.

The envelope is deliberately universal over observed per-patient reader assignments. Its extrema are not probabilities and may be conservative because they allow the assignment that is worst for the contrast. A diagnostic restricted to three corpus-wide worlds (all patients reader 1, all reader 2, all reader 3) must also be reported, but it cannot rescue the primary. Unobserved fourth readers, different institutions, rubric drift, or componentwise hypothetical labels are outside the primary envelope and require another study.

### 5.3 Reliability summaries are descriptive, not truth

Before pathology outcomes are opened, publish by concept and modality on `Q=1` units:

- the exact three-way label table and unique complete-vector count;
- all three pairwise confusion matrices and raw exact agreement;
- three-way exact agreement;
- pairwise Cohen kappa with every inherited label as a nominal category, together with category prevalences and denominators;
- counts of label disagreements, span disagreements conditional on the same positive/uncertain label, and reader-specific `uncertain`/`not-mentioned` rates.

Undefined kappa due to a zero denominator or degenerate marginal is reported as undefined, not zero or one. No reliability cutoff is a gate, weight, exclusion rule, or proof of accuracy. Low agreement with universally supportive ranking is still supportive for the ranking hypothesis; high agreement with submargin capture is adverse. Agreement assesses consistency of these annotations, not biological truth or image accuracy.

The categorical design dictionary is fixed by the rubric. The one development preprocessing map is outcome-blind and patient-balanced over the augmented development alternatives: each development patient receives total weight one, split deterministically equally across its unique `S_i^R` byte states. Duplicate reader bundles do not gain extra weight. Scaling/category order and the shared `Z,S,Q` columns are then frozen for validation and test. This weighting is preprocessing only; it does not average labels in model fitting or evaluation.

`unsafe_raw` remains diagnostic under the parent’s restrictions. A single resolved-label or majority-vote model may be reported only as `resolved_label_diagnostic`; it cannot support, veto, or rescue the primary reader-robust claim.

## 7. Robust fitting, tuning, masking, and exact evaluation

For model `M`, hyperparameter `h=(alpha,lambda)`, and augmented development world `omega`, retain normalized logistic loss and define

`beta_hat_M(h) = argmin_beta [max_{omega in W_D^R:n_D(omega)>0} L_M(beta,omega) + lambda P_alpha(beta)]`.

For `G_R`, `omega` includes the coupled source/outcome state and one observed-reader bundle choice per included patient. For `R_clean` and `B`, reader-duplicate worlds are quotient-collapsed because their information sets do not depend on `j`. Only one `G_R` is fitted for each h; three reader-specific models are forbidden.

The parent’s exact Dinkelbach separation and convex epigraph cutting-plane master remain controlling. The separator operates over `S_i^R`, preserving each complete reader bundle as an atomic patient-state payload. It must achieve omitted-world violation at most `1e-8`, primal-dual gap at most `1e-8`, and two canonical separations adding no world. Store reader assignments in active/witness worlds as well as all inherited cuts, residuals, KKT/primal-dual certificates, software versions, and seeds. Any approximate, componentwise, truncated, sampled, beam, or time-limit incumbent is nonconfirmatory.

The parent’s repaired regularization construction remains unchanged, except that `A_G`, its zero-slope KKT certificate, and all `G_R` fits use the augmented minimax loss. Pure ridge retains no finite all-zero requirement. The full serialized grid is frozen before 2019 evaluation.

Hyperparameters are selected without 2020/2021 outcomes:

- tune `G_R` on the complete 2019 augmented superframe by maximum sharp worst-world fixed-K documentary-M2 capture over `W_V^R`, using the inherited deterministic tie order;
- tune `R_clean` and `B` independently on their source worlds exactly as in the parent;
- do not choose a separate h per reader and do not use agreement metrics in selection.

For year `a in {2020,2021}`, world `omega in W_a^R`, and model `M`:

`T_M(a,omega)=100/K * sum_{i in TopK_M(a,omega)}Y_i(s_i)`.

The source/outcome state `s_i` and reader choice `j_i` are shared across all arms in a contrast. The co-primary contrasts are

- `Delta_nested_R = T_GR - T_Rclean`;
- `Delta_mask_R = T_GR - T_GRmask`.

Compute certified attained extrema

`[delta_e^-,delta_e^+] = [min_{omega in W_a^R}Delta_e, max_{omega in W_a^R}Delta_e]`

for each year-by-contrast coordinate `e`. Store each extremizing source state, patient-to-reader assignment, three score vectors, both top-K sets, Y values, arithmetic trace, and witness hash. The integer optimization gap must be zero with reported numerical gap at most `1e-8`. Raw and lossless quotient ledgers must replay identically. Extend the inherited exhaustive synthetic and real-shard tests to augmented states.

Also report, without changing labels, the extrema restricted to each corpus-wide reader, pairwise top-K Jaccard overlap, score-rank correlation, and the number of selected patients changed solely by reader bundle while holding a source witness fixed.

## 8. Exact result zones and falsification

Mandatory gates are evaluated before any result label. In addition to every inherited gate, require:

- all three independent complete bundles for every possible selected `Q=1` unit in `S+`;
- valid bundle hashes, reader/session provenance, blindness attestation, and no post-outcome edits;
- 100% source-byte-valid spans under the parent’s `V/A/E` rules;
- exact atomic whole-bundle/whole-patient coupling and no componentwise label mixing;
- reader choice changes only `X`, never `Z,S,Q`, population, time, source state, Y, denominator, or K;
- no reader identity/agreement/disagreement feature;
- one robust `G_R` fit per h and no reader-specific/test refit;
- byte-identical `Z,S,Q` and exact score/action equality of `G_R` and `G_Rmask` whenever all concept blocks are unavailable;
- certified complete augmented-state separation, replay, fitting, tuning, and endpoint extrema.

Once all gates pass, each coordinate is exactly:

- **supportive** if `delta_e^- > 5`: every declared source/outcome state and every observed-reader assignment exceeds the margin;
- **heterogeneous** if `delta_e^- <= 5 < delta_e^+`: at least one declared world exceeds and at least one fails the margin;
- **all-world-adverse** if `delta_e^+ <= 5`: no declared world exceeds the strict margin.

Equality to 5 is adverse. The global labels are mutually exclusive:

1. **Universal-reader-and-source-supportive** only if all four coordinates are supportive.
2. **Decisive-reader/source-threshold-heterogeneity** if at least one coordinate/world is above 5 and at least one coordinate/world is at or below 5.
3. **Universal-adverse-without-positive-coordinate** if every coordinate has `delta_e^+<=5`.
4. **Inconclusive** if any mandatory gate fails, regardless of favorable numerical fragments.

For heterogeneous results, attach machine-computable noncausal descriptors: `reader-sensitive` if holding at least one source state fixed, two valid reader assignments fall on opposite sides of 5; `source-sensitive` if holding at least one reader assignment fixed, two source states fall on opposite sides; `mixed` if both. These descriptors locate threshold sensitivity but do not identify the correct world.

Any `delta_e^-<=5` falsifies the universal conjunction. Adverse means the prespecified robust semantic strategy failed its strict margin; it does not prove no smaller effect, no value in report meaning, or no biological predictability. Heterogeneous means the threshold conclusion depends on declared source/reader interpretation. Inconclusive means the exact experiment was not validly instantiated or solved and is not a negative scientific result.

A supportive conclusion is limited to: “Within this frozen retrospective eventual-report corpus, one locked clean canonical-report concept model added more than 5 encounter-documentary M2 captures per 100 fixed positions over both semantic controls in every declared source/outcome state and every assignment of the three observed blinded-reader bundles, separately in 2020 and 2021.”

It may not be shortened to claims of annotation accuracy, radiologist truth, raw-image findings, timely displayed reports, biological M2 prediction, statistical significance, future generalization, operational workload acceptability, treatment selection, or patient benefit.

- a structured claim-to-output map listing each reported sentence, supporting artifact/field, and whether it is computationally checkable or requires clinical evidence.

Mandatory verifier fixtures must include:

1. three identical complete bundles and an exactly replayed supportive result;
2. one valid reader bundle that changes top-K enough to put an endpoint at exactly 5 while another exceeds 5, producing heterogeneous—not supportive—classification;
3. all reader/source worlds at or below 5, accepted as adverse;
4. missing third-reader annotation, post-outcome edit, invalid span, or unqualified/incomplete attestation, each forcing inconclusive;
5. an illegal Frankenstein bundle combining different readers’ concepts or CT/MRI blocks, rejected even if favorable;
6. majority vote, third-resolved labels, latent-truth estimation, reliability weighting, single-reader fitting, reader-specific models, reader ID as a feature, or test refitting, each rejected;
7. a reader choice that changes `Q`, source state, membership, Y, denominator, or K, rejected;
8. exact agreement near one with adverse endpoints and low agreement with supportive endpoints, both accepted with appropriate interpretation, proving reliability is not the target or truth;
9. global-reader diagnostics favorable while the full per-patient reader envelope fails, with no rescue of the primary;
10. `G_Rmask` refit or changed `Z,S,Q`, rejected;
11. correct supportive, adverse, heterogeneous, and failed-gate outputs paired with their appropriate conclusions, all accepted independently of hypothesis confirmation;
12. numerically correct outputs paired with “reader-correct,” “image-confirmed,” “displayed preoperatively,” biological-M2, significance/generalization, treatment, workload, or benefit claims, rejected.

The verifier can establish source/header/hash consistency, reader-file completeness, atomic bundle coupling, span lineage, forbidden-feature absence, preprocessing/model identity, robust optimization certificates, fixed K, exact extrema, threshold labels, and linkage of permitted conclusions to computed outputs. It cannot establish reader qualifications or independence beyond documents, correctness of Chinese interpretation, which label is clinically or biologically true, renderer fidelity, raw-image findings, operation completion, report finalization/release/view, pathology specimen linkage/sampling, biological M2, future transportability, workflow response, capacity acceptability, treatment effect, harms, cost, fairness, or benefit.

Those stronger claims require radiology/informatics review with actual source rendering and image access; accession-linked report version/finalization/release/view logs; validated operation clocks and completed-procedure records; specimen-linked pathology and sampling review; more independent readers/sites; and prospective silent-mode then appropriately controlled clinical evaluation. Negative or reader-heterogeneous findings remain scientifically valuable because they identify that the canonical-report semantic strategy is not robust enough for the stated fixed-capacity decision claim.
