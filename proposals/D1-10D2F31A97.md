## Substantive repair and decision value

This child preserves Episode 18's clinically consequential hour-48 post-shock question, exact discovery partition, eligibility phenotype, fluid and weight rules, delivered-loop/eMAR definitions, overlap target, ANHATD28 primary outcome, RTHD28 and observed-RRT guards, true-mortality and ALDL7 evidence-limit outputs, margins, noncausal interpretation, and external-data requirements. It repairs one estimand-level defect: the parent says that a terminal event has priority during the 24-hour grace period, but does not define the intervention exception created by that event, does not make all hospital discharge states assignment-closing events, and does not distinguish exact assignment-closing time from the possibly invalid day-28 state. A compiler could therefore (incorrectly) censor the unsatisfied A clone after early death/discharge, assign early terminal cases to B alone, allow treatment after discharge to determine adherence, or call a live row with destination `DIED` a successful discharge. Any of those choices creates immortal-time or incompatible-strategy bias.

The repaired estimand is the risk difference under assignment at `t0` to either of two pragmatic 24-hour strategies while the patient remains alive and in the index hospital: (A) permit clinician-chosen timing but require a qualifying ICU-recorded delivered furosemide/bumetanide initiation by hour 24, or (B) require no actual loop administration by any route through hour 24. An exact index-hospital death or any exact index-hospital discharge before the requirement is resolved closes the treatment opportunity without artificial censoring in every still-compatible clone. The terminal category is then an outcome. This is an explicit competing-event exception in both strategies, not postbaseline eligibility, per-protocol exclusion, treatment assignment to B, or evidence that treatment was withheld.

A treatment-blind audit of the unchanged broad discovery frame read all rows required from `patients`, `admissions`, `icustays`, and `procedureevents` without examining loop treatment. ## Existing evidence, supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

Relevant passages were inspected in the frozen full XML of McMullan et al.'s 2026 RADAR-2 secondary analysis (DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404`; source `[source checksum]`). That study compared a conservative-fluid plus active-deresuscitation bundle with usual care and reported no statistically detected between-group difference in lactate, AKIRisk, urinary cystatin-C, or vascular-injury biomarkers; its modest size and imprecision leave clinically important effects possible. It does not isolate this loop-initiation decision or establish patient-centered, renal, respiratory, or mortality safety.

Relevant full-text XML passages were also inspected for Garcia-Albeniz et al. (2026, DOI `10.1002/cam4.71950`; source `[source checksum]`). Their method example directly shows that excluding people who die during a treatment-initiation grace period can create severe immortal-time bias and that cloning/censoring/weighting retains such early outcomes. Wang et al.'s 2026 operational EHR target-trial review (DOI `10.1038/s41746-026-02563-z`; source `[source checksum]`) supports explicit trial specification while emphasizing that EHR data generation can make a conceptual estimand unidentifiable.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is therefore limited: protocolized deresuscitation can change fluid management without a detected biomarker safety signal in a modest randomized secondary analysis, and grace-period analyses must retain early outcomes to avoid immortal-time bias. Neither claim supports a causal benefit, safety claim, or bedside recommendation for isolated loop initiation here.

**Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis:** in the prespecified measured-overlap population, assignment at `t0` to strategy A rather than B increases day-28 recorded alive non-hospice/non-acute-hospital index discharge (ANHATD28) by more than 5 percentage points, while ruling out more than 3 points higher recorded terminal/hospice disposition (RTHD28) and more than 5 points higher observed post-landmark RRT use through day 7.

This advances bundle-level evidence to a specific, delivery-coherent decision and an internally consistent grace-period estimand. Even a supportive result is Harbor-supportive evidence for external validation and a pragmatic trial, not proof of benefit or safety.

## Treatment strategies and exact event clocks

Let `G=t0+24h`.

**A — initiate:** first qualifying ICU-recorded delivered furosemide/bumetanide with clinical start in `(t0,G]`: `icu/inputevents.itemid` 221794, 228340, or 229639; valid subject/hadm/stay keys; nonmissing start/end/store times; positive duration; status `FinishedRunning`, `ChangeDose/Rate`, or `Stopped`; and positive amount convertible to mg or positive mg/hour rate over positive duration. `Paused` is sensitivity. Unknown units, nonpositive, cancelled, not-started, and order-only rows are not delivery.

**B — avoid:** no actual furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide, or ethacrynic-acid administration by any route in `(t0,G]` while still alive and in the index hospital. Require nonmissing eMAR `charttime` and `storetime`. Join `hosp/emar` to every `hosp/emar_detail` sibling by `subject_id,emar_id,emar_seq`, retain `parent_field_ordinal`, then aggregate once per eMAR event. Use a frozen case-folded, punctuation-normalized whole-drug dictionary. Require positive `dose_given` or `product_amount_given`, no true `complete_dose_not_given`, and event evidence `Administered`, `Delayed Administered`, `Administered in Other Location`, `Partial Administered`, `Started`, `Started in Other Location`, or `Restarted`. Report every accepted label. Missing route still deviates B. Orders never define treatment.

A qualifying ICU segment is also an actual loop: it satisfies A and censors B once, never censors A as “nonqualifying.” Any other actual loop before A satisfaction—including route-known or route-missing eMAR without a qualifying ICU segment—censors both still-unsatisfied A and B. Aggregate concordant ICU/eMAR evidence before event ordering to prevent double transitions. The primary exposure remains ICU-recorded delivered loop, not verified IV loop and not a dose or sustained-treatment strategy. Route-explicit IV eMAR, ICU/eMAR ±15-minute concordance, and contemporaneous-storetime classifications are sensitivities.

Define exact index-hospital assignment-closing time `T` from `hosp/admissions`, never from date-only `patients.dod`:

1. If `hospital_expire_flag=1`, use a valid `deathtime>t0` no later than `dischtime`; otherwise use valid `dischtime>t0` as the recorded-death fallback.
2. If `hospital_expire_flag=0`, use valid `dischtime>t0` for every destination: exact `HOSPICE`, exact `ACUTE HOSPITAL`, other valid live destination, missing/`DIED`, or discordant live-death state.
3. If timestamps disagree, use the earliest defensible post-`t0` exact hospital endpoint to close assignment, label the day-28 state invalid, emit all raw fields, and apply the anomaly gate. Date-only DOD can bound day-28 mortality but cannot close an hourly treatment opportunity.

`icustays.outtime` is not a primary terminal event: it may be ICU-to-ward transfer while the 24-hour hospital strategy remains active. Include current location in `H_j`; report ICU exit before G and the structural inability to observe a new qualifying ICU segment afterward. A prespecified secondary ICU-availability strategy closes both treatment requirements at the first ICU exit, retains both compatible clones, and permits treatment thereafter; it is a distinct estimand and cannot rescue or overturn the primary branch.

## Clone/censor state machine

Clone every eligible subject to A and B at `t0`. Use exact timestamps for transitions; hourly intervals `j=0,...,23` are model rows, not event-time coarsening. At every timestamp, priority is `T` first, qualifying A delivery second, other actual loop third, and G last. Reverse terminal/treatment ties is a sensitivity. At exact G, terminal first; otherwise qualifying treatment can satisfy A and censor B before the unsatisfied-A check.

For an A clone initially `compatible_unsatisfied`:

- qualifying A delivery before `T` satisfies A; freeze its treatment-adherence weight and continue outcome follow-up;
- a nonqualifying actual loop before satisfaction artificially censors A at its clinical time;
- if `T<=G` occurs first, change to `closed_by_terminal_while_compatible`; do not censor, do not require later initiation, ignore post-terminal medication rows, and retain the terminal outcome;
- if event-free and unsatisfied at G, censor A at G.

For a B clone initially `compatible_loop_free`:

- any actual loop before `T` and through G artificially censors B at its clinical time;
- if `T<=G` occurs first, change to `closed_by_terminal_while_compatible`; do not censor and retain the terminal outcome;
- if loop-free and event-free through G, change to `adherent_complete`.

After `T`, no exposure event can satisfy or violate either strategy. “Closed by terminal” is not “A initiated,” “B assigned,” censoring, survival, or missingness. Early death, hospice, acute transfer, valid other live discharge, and invalid live discharge are copied to both clones that were compatible immediately before T. If one clone had already deviated, only the still-compatible clone retains the event. This rule prevents the immortal-time defect of excluding early terminal events from A.

Emit one row per subject/clone/event and one row per clone/hour with prior state, next state, clinical event time, storetime, source/table/row provenance, location, terminal category, compatibility, censor reason, and probability. Assert no post-terminal transitions and exactly one assignment-closing state per retained clone.

## Estimand and analysis

The primary data-defined estimand is
`RD=P(ANHATD28^A=1)-P(ANHATD28^B=1)`
among baseline subjects whose cross-fitted baseline probability
`e0(X0)=P(Q<min(N,T,G) | X0)`
lies in `[0.10,0.90]`, where Q is qualifying initiation and N is a different actual loop. Terminal events and nonqualifying loops are competing events in estimating e0. Never classify an early terminal subject as observed B only, condition on survival/hospitalization at G, or use realized future treatment/outcome for the overlap set. Apply common tilt `e0(1-e0)` identically to both clones.

Fit cross-fitted pooled-logistic artificial-censoring models separately by arm. The denominator is the probability of the observed compatible transition given frozen baseline and prior decision-available `H_j`; the numerator uses frozen baseline only. Terminal closure is a competing outcome state, not censoring and not included in a “survived grace” denominator. Stop multiplying at A satisfaction, terminal closure, strategy deviation, or G completion as applicable. Truncate cumulative stabilized weights at 1st/99th percentiles and report untruncated results. Include an exact-event multinomial-hazard implementation as a numerical check on the pooled censor models.

Prespecified baseline/history variables remain demographics, era/type, service/careunit, prior completed-admission diagnoses and loop exposure, weight, balance and urine trajectories, creatinine/potassium/lactate, MAP/pressor dose and cessation time, FiO2/PEEP/oxygenation/ventilation, sedation, crystalloid/albumin, procedures, transfer location, and missingness. Later diagnoses, notes, response, terminal destination, and outcomes are forbidden predictors.

Estimate crude, common-overlap, and overlap-plus-clone-censor arm risks and RDs. Day-28 multistate risks use weights frozen at adherence resolution/terminal closure and must reconcile exactly with binary indicators. Use subject-level cross-fitting and at least 500 subject bootstraps rerunning overlap selection and all models. Report calibration, standardized differences, event/adherence counts, early-terminal counts in each clone, probability/weight tails, ESS, and arm-specific weighted multistate cumulative incidence. Observed-odds multipliers 0.5/0.75/1/1.33/2 are stochastic-policy analyses, not unmeasured-confounding tests. Preserve the binary readiness/goals-of-care tipping grid and pre-`t0` slope placebo outcomes.

Mandatory bias demonstrations are not alternative primary estimates: (i) drop/censor A clones terminal before initiation, (ii) assign all early terminal untreated patients to B only, and (iii) landmark at G among survivors still hospitalized. Report their RDs beside the repaired estimate. A difference demonstrates design sensitivity; it does not validate either estimate.

## Day-28 and safety-guard outcomes

At `H28=t0+28d`, classify the index admission into exactly one state: recorded index-hospital death; exact HOSPICE discharge; exact ACUTE HOSPITAL discharge; recorded alive non-hospice/non-acute destination; prolonged index hospitalization; or invalid/discordant. A terminal state occurring during grace remains that same day-28 state; it is not reclassified by later data. For flag-confirmed death use valid `deathtime`, otherwise valid post-`t0` `dischtime`. A flag-live discharge with missing or `DIED` destination or discordant deathtime is invalid, never success.

ANHATD28 is recorded alive non-hospice/non-acute index discharge by H28. Report any live discharge, exact HOME/HOME HEALTH CARE, CHRONIC/LONG TERM ACUTE CARE-as-failure, every destination, day 14/21, era/service/unit, anomaly-excluded, same-system readmission, and recorded-DOD sensitivities. Discharge is not recovery, external survival, or freedom from readmission.

RTHD28 is harmful. Its lower indicator is recorded index-hospital death or exact HOSPICE discharge by H28. Its upper indicator additionally includes missing/`DIED` live destinations and irreconcilable death/discharge states by H28. Compute arm and sharp effect bounds `Delta_L=pA_L-pB_U`, `Delta_U=pA_U-pB_L` with one-sided bootstrap limits. Acute-hospital transfer is ANHATD failure but not RTHD unless independent death/hospice evidence exists. RTHD is an administrative terminal-disposition signal, not true mortality or hospice appropriateness.

True day-28 mortality remains mandatory: exact in-hospital death plus date-only `hosp/patients.dod` interval `[00:00,+1d)`; missing post-discharge vital status remains `[0,1]`. Report arm risks, sharp effect bounds, interval widths, and unknown fractions. The prior corrected broad audit found 3,047/7,733 (39.4%) unknown and 1,744 DOD dates strictly after H28 known no-death-by-H28. This cannot support mortality safety without external linkage.

ALDL7 remains the parent's bounded secondary outcome using extubation items 227194/225468/225477, invasive ventilation 225792, intubation 224385, linked ICU intervals, and death precedence. Its prior broad-frame unknown fraction was 3,261/7,733 (42.2%). A sharp upper effect bound below -0.05 can trigger an adverse signal, but absence cannot establish respiratory safety.

Observed post-landmark RRT through day 7 is harmful but is not new RRT, AKI, or renal safety. ## Falsification, gates, and branches

The >5-point primary hypothesis is supported only if the lower one-sided 95% limit of ANHATD28 RD is >+0.05. It is margin-falsified if the upper limit is <=+0.05; otherwise it is unresolved.

**Harbor-supportive evidence** requires primary magnitude support; RTHD28 conservative `Delta_U` upper limit <+0.03; observed-RRT conservative upper-effect upper limit <+0.05; no ALDL7 definitive adverse trigger; no qualified destination sign reversal; and every temporal, state, unit, overlap, calibration, negative-control, weight, bound, and grace-consistency diagnostic passing. This supports trial prioritization only.

**Adverse evidence** occurs if the ANHATD28 upper limit <=0; RTHD28 `Delta_L` lower limit >=+0.03; observed-RRT lower-effect lower limit >=+0.05 under both date brackets; or ALDL7 upper-effect upper limit <-0.05. These are recorded adverse signals, not biological proof.

**Inconclusive** covers all other patterns: primary margin falsification without harm, wide RTHD/RRT bounds, residual-bias tipping, destination conflict, failed diagnostics, or inadequate overlap. A precise +2-point estimate falsifies the >5-point claim but does not prove no benefit.

Before effects, declare infeasible/inconclusive if either strategy has <100 adherent/compatible-complete subjects, ESS <75, >10% person-hours have denominator probability <.01 or >.99, >1% temporal/state contradictions, >10-point anomaly imbalance, material differential documentation lag, failed negative controls, or nonreconciling state/bound arithmetic. Also fail if any pre-terminal compatible A clone is removed solely for not yet initiating; any pre-terminal loop-free patient appears only in B; any terminal timestamp is derived solely from date-only DOD; any post-terminal medication changes adherence; any valid early terminal state differs between compatible clones; or binary outcomes do not reconcile with the weighted multistate table. Report final clinical attrition and exact overlap; broad audit counts do not pass these gates.

## Verification contract and limits

Required outputs are partition proof and attrition; broad-audit reproduction; one row per subject/clone; raw two-clock treatment evidence; fluid/weight components; aggregated ICU/eMAR events; every clone-hour and exact-event transition; early-terminal counts by category and clone; overlap, weights, ESS, and calibration; complete day-28 destination states; ANHATD28, RTHD28, true-mortality, ALDL7, and RRT individual/arm/effect bounds; bootstrap endpoints; biased-design demonstrations; sensitivities; gate booleans; magnitude status; and final branch. Every numerical conclusion must name its output field.

Synthetic fixtures must verify: death before loop retains both clones with ANHATD=0/RTHD-lower=1; HOME discharge retains both with ANHATD=1; HOSPICE retains both with ANHATD=0/RTHD-lower=1; ACUTE HOSPITAL retains both with ANHATD=0/RTHD-lower=0; missing/`DIED` live destination yields ANHATD=0 and RTHD interval [0,1]; qualifying loop then death satisfies A, censors B, and retains A death; exact death/loop tie applies terminal-first; qualifying loop exactly at G satisfies A and censors B; no loop through G censors A and completes B; date-only DOD cannot close grace; and post-terminal medication is ignored. Test supportive, adverse, margin-falsified, and sparse/inconclusive numerical outputs. Also test correct computations paired with unsupported causal, IV, safe, recovery, survival, renal-safety, or practice claims; those conclusions must fail.

The verifier can establish hashes, headers, joins, partition, item/status/unit rules, event ordering, clone transitions, bounds, margins, arithmetic reconciliation, and whether reported claims follow from computed outputs. It cannot establish clinician intent/readiness, congestion, goals of care, hospice appropriateness, actual route where missing, functional recovery, complete death/readmission/dialysis, exchangeability, transportability, or causal benefit.

True mortality safety requires external vital-status linkage with exact death dates/times through H28. Renal and readmission safety require post-discharge dialysis, creatinine, encounters, and cross-system linkage. Treatment fidelity requires route/dose verification, administrations after index discharge/transfer, and blinded chart review of contraindications, congestion, and goals of care. Functional benefit requires patient-reported or functional outcomes. A consequential treatment recommendation requires expert review, external validation, and preferably a randomized pragmatic trial of the fully specified initiation policy.
