# A two-phase, route-blind verification audit of pulmonary-nodule admission routing

## Decision question and substantive advance

The unresolved decision is whether the frozen admission routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` are sufficiently reproducible and textually conservative to justify prospective silent validation. The leading parent makes labeling leakage-resistant, but routinely requires two R, two D, two K, and two H reviews per sampled admission plus redaction audit. That burden can exhaust a 900-admission budget before sparse route domains support useful bounds. Nor does agreement between similarly trained physicians eliminate correlated error.

This child retains the parent's exact first-eligible all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum chains, unique canonical `DS`, unit-specific clocks, exhaustive states, subject-disjoint development, sealed evidence views, conservative worst-unit routing, sentinel census, positive sampling probability in every nonsentinel stratum, all-unit review within each selected admission, adverse unknown handling, design-based finite-population inference, and noncausal ceiling. It changes confirmatory verification only:

1. Every selected admission receives one firewalled primary R/D/K-A lane, where K-A uses the parent's phrase-level length-preserving mask.
2. Before any packet opens, a route-independent second-phase probability audit is drawn within every sampling stratum. Every audit admission receives a separately staffed duplicate R/D/K-B lane and two method-diverse holistic challenges. K-B instead deletes each target-bearing sentence, one sentence on either side, and target-naming headings without revealing original span length. Audit membership is selected without routes or review outcomes and is never enriched or topped up after route frequencies are known.
3. A smaller route-independent masking-stress subsample is drawn simultaneously within phase 2. It receives an unmasked context-only K+ review and source-versus-mask audit; K+ may expose cue dependence adversely but can never favorably rescue a route.
4. The phase-2 allocation is fixed by an accuracy-blind internal precision simulation using only stratum sizes, deterministic packet-complexity variables, planned costs, and governance margins. Its objective is to minimize the maximum anticipated design effect/half-width across prespecified binary error estimands under a least-favorable range, not to favor an expected result.
5. All selected admissions still receive all-unit review; expensive duplicate and holistic review is concentrated through known second- and third-phase probabilities rather than outcome-triggering. Sparse route domains are handled with joint finite-population bounds and explicit precision failure, never outcome-based enrichment.

This tests whether a feasible two-phase process preserves conservative routing and whether independent, method-diverse text review detects errors missed by the primary lane. It does not test clinical truth.

## Supported claim, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

**Supported now:** frozen MIMIC contains the admission, patient, report, reciprocal-link, canonical discharge artifact, text, and storage-time fields needed for a subject-keyed, departure-frozen audit. The parent reports 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 unique canonical `DS` rows, 17 missing discharge `storetime` values, and reciprocal-link fields. These are feasibility facts, not route results.

**Unresolved claim:** a fixed, route-blind two-phase probability design can achieve adequate effective denominators and simultaneous precision for all-population route burdens and independently detected primary-lane errors within a feasible physician-hour budget, while preserving conservative admission routing.

**Hypothesis:** in frozen `N_A`, the two-phase audit will show (i) primary-lane route burdens with simultaneous half-width at most 5 admissions per 100; (ii) independently audited unsafe-primary-`AUTO` contamination upper bound below 5%; (iii) lower capture by primary `{RECON,DEFER}` of audit-defined unsafe content at least 95%; (iv) lower capture by primary `DEFER` of audit-defined decision-relevant uncertainty at least 95%; (v) lower confirmation at least 90% for each nominated route; and (vi) feasible workload, all after unknowns, nonresponse, panel discordance, masking failure, and all compatibility assignments are carried adversely.

A sparse or zero route domain cannot be called perfect. Failure to attain its prespecified denominator or precision is inconclusive for that route and cannot be repaired by reviewing route-positive cases.

## Phase 1: positive-probability master sample

Freeze the parent's hierarchical, outcome-blind strata before labels:

1. `S_SENTINEL`: reciprocal defect; missing/contradictory time/artifact; parser failure; `U_BOUNDARY`; at least two possible units; differing actions/windows; negation/conditionality conflict; target-linked decision/non-pursuit/replacement; or discordant DS spans. Census, `pi1=1`.
2. `S_TIMECLOCK`: no sentinel but any `G_u<24h`/unknown, `DS_store>D`, or stale chronology.
3. `S_ZERO`: no sentinel, ample clocks, no deterministic target-linked DS plan span.
4. `S_PARTIAL`: no sentinel, ample clocks, target text but apparently missing/discordant component.
5. `S_COMPLETE`: no sentinel, ample clocks, apparently complete/replacement pattern.

For each nonsentinel stratum use SRS without replacement and positive `pi1_h=n1_h/N_h`. `S_ZERO` takes the larger of 300 or the finite-population worst-case 0.5 sample for ±4 percentage points at 95%; other nonsentinel strata take the larger of 120 or ±7 points, capped at `N_h`, using `ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25/{e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`. The master ceiling remains 900; if the sentinel census plus fixed samples exceeds it, obtain resources or declare infeasible. Never reduce fractions after labels.

Every master admission receives one R, one D, and one K-A reviewer from disjoint pools. R sees only the full reciprocal radiology chain and relative order and seals all quoted units. D sees only canonical predeparture DS text and seals all plausibly target-linked plan propositions without a radiology abstraction. K-A sees the parent's length-preserving target-plan-masked DS plus allowed admission/disposition/service/ICU variables and seals `K_OPEN/K_CLOSED/K_UNKNOWN`. Mask A replaces target/action/timing/recipient/pursuit spans with length-preserving mask tokens while retaining section boundaries. Reviewers see no route, stratum, clock state, other labels, downstream evidence, or POE. Invalid spans, nonresponse, and packet failure remain adverse.

Redaction audit remains independent: every primary `K_CLOSED`, every phase-2 admission, and a fixed 20% SRS of other master admissions. Leakage or decision-relevant over-redaction adds `K_UNKNOWN`; direct route/stratum/other-panel leakage stops the study. Population upper bounds for both leakage and over-redaction must be at most 2%.

## Phase 2: fixed route-independent audit

Before any phase-1 packet opens, draw phase 2 independently within each master-sample stratum by SRSWOR with fixed conditional probability `pi2_h=m_h/n1_h>0`. Audit selection may use only sealed stratum, deterministic unit-count/ambiguity indicators, packet length bins, and planned cost. It cannot use component labels, H labels, routes, expected direction, review times, reviewer identity, nonresponse, or any audit outcome.

Choose integer `m_h` once through a sealed accuracy-blind minimax program. Candidate allocations must include at least 30 admissions and 15% of phase 1 in every nonempty stratum, census strata with fewer than 30, and at least 40% of `S_SENTINEL`. Under binary finite populations with stratum prevalences on the fixed grid `{0.005,0.01,0.02,0.05,0.10,0.25,0.50}` and all allocations of errors across deterministic complexity bins, simulate at least 20,000 repeated two-phase samples. Select the lowest-cost allocation whose worst-case anticipated effective size is at least 100 and one-sided 95% half-width at most 5 points for an error domain of population burden at least 5%; otherwise choose the allocation minimizing worst-case half-width under the sealed physician-hour cap. Ties use lower maximum weight, then a hashed seed. This is design calibration, not evidence about actual accuracy. Hash all candidate allocations, losses, chosen `m_h`, `pi2_h`, and seed before review.

Each audit admission receives:

* a duplicate R/D/K-B lane staffed by physicians with no development, primary-lane, or cross-panel role and no subject overlap in their assignments with a role they performed elsewhere; K-B sees the same allowed context variables but a second deterministic mask that deletes each target-bearing sentence, one neighboring sentence on each side, and any target-naming heading, without showing mask type or original length;
* H1, a thoracic-radiology plus transitions-of-care physician pair working independently from paired source evidence and then unioning sealed atomic challenge labels without consensus erasure;
* H2, a generalist hospitalist using a different prespecified checklist and independently de-novo enumerating units and plan conflicts from randomized source order.

K-A/K-B context support is decision-facing only when its quoted non-target evidence survives both transformations byte-for-byte. Any decision-relevant difference, uncertain unit boundary, leakage, or over-redaction adds `K_UNKNOWN`; neither mask can favorably override an adverse component. H1/H2 see paired operative radiology and canonical DS, but no component labels, routes, strata, absolute clocks, expected direction, downstream evidence, or POE. They label `Q_UNSAFE`, `Q_UNCERTAIN`, `Q_CLEAR_OMISSION`, or `Q_NOACTION` with quotes. Either challenge's unsafe finding is unsafe; either uncertainty is uncertain. Agreement is reported, but shared agreement is not clinical truth. An H finding can adversely prevent `AUTO`, add `RECON/TIMING/DEFER`, or expose missed units; it can never favorably rescue a primary route. Unknown timing remains timing/deferral compatible.

At the same pre-review draw, select within each nonempty phase-2 stratum an SRSWOR masking-stress subsample of `c_h=min[m_h,max(30,ceil(0.20*m_h))]` (census when smaller), conditional probability `pi3_h=c_h/m_h`. A separately staffed K+ reviewer sees the unmasked canonical DS plus exactly the K allowlist, but no R abstraction, labels, route, stratum, clock, H output, downstream evidence, or POE. An independent redaction auditor compares source, Mask A, and Mask B. K+ disagreement or newly visible target-plan cues is adverse mask dependence and adds `K_UNKNOWN`; K+ agreement cannot rescue a disagreement or support clinical truth. Seal `c_h`, membership, and seed with phase 2. Third-phase nonresponse is adverse.

No route-triggered top-up, replacement, sequential stopping, sample-size re-estimation from observed labels, or targeted audit of primary `AUTO` is permitted. If a route is sparse, report its burden bound and precision failure. All phase-2 admissions have joint inclusion `pi_i=pi1_h*pi2_h`; conditional pairwise probabilities are known from the two nested SRSWOR stages. Phase-2 nonresponse remains included and adverse.

## Finite-population estimands and inference

The finite population is all frozen `N_A`; one admission per subject makes admission, subject, sampling, and analysis units identical. Units remain clustered within admission. Let `I1_i,I2_i,I3_i` denote phase inclusion and `w1_i=1/pi1_h`, `w2_i=1/(pi1_h*pi2_h)`, and `w3_i=1/(pi1_h*pi2_h*pi3_h)`.

Phase-1 estimands use `w1`: HT route totals `T_hat_r=sum I1_i*w1_i*I(primary robust route=r)` and burdens `B_hat_r=100*T_hat_r/N_A` for `AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,MIXED`; `MIXED` is nested. Exhaustive `Y,V,H,Z,J_A,E_A` margins must sum to `N_A`.

There is no gold-standard label. For each admission define an adverse union `E_i=1` if the duplicate robust route differs adversely from the primary route; either H is `Q_UNSAFE/Q_UNCERTAIN` contrary to primary `AUTO`; K-A, K-B, or sampled K+ differs decision-relevantly; redaction fails; an additional unit is found; or any required review/span is missing or invalid. Each decision-facing error estimand is reported as a design-weighted disagreement envelope: its lower endpoint counts explicit observed adverse findings; its upper endpoint additionally assigns all relevant panel disagreement, unknowns, invalid/missing reviews, masking uncertainty, and nonresponse adversely, then adds simultaneous sampling uncertainty. This envelope bounds method-detectable discordance, not clinical error. Also report `delta*`, the minimum hypothetical shared-blind-spot prevalence among currently concordant nonadverse cases that would make the AUTO upper margin exceed 5%; `delta*` is a tipping point, not an estimated error rate.

Phase-2 estimands use `w2` and Hájek domain ratios with explicit numerator and denominator totals:

* `U_AUTO=P(E_i=1 | primary lane otherwise calls AUTO)`;
* `leak_AUTO=P(primary calls AUTO | audit union Q_UNSAFE/Q_UNCERTAIN or duplicate route adverse)`;
* `Capture_RECON=P(duplicate robust route in {RECON,DEFER} | either challenge or duplicate components identify unsafe/mixed content)`;
* `Purity_RECON=P(explicit unsafe/incomplete/conflicting/mixed finding | primary RECON)`;
* `Purity_TIMING=P(every blocking unresolved unit has G_u<24h or unresolved clock | primary TIMING)`;
* `Capture_DEFER=P(duplicate robust route=DEFER | any audit method identifies decision-relevant uncertainty)`;
* `D_MIX=P(duplicate robust route in {RECON,DEFER} | any audit method finds pure omission plus an unsafe minority unit)`;
* additional-unit discovery outside sentinel; R/D/K primary-versus-duplicate directional confirmation; H1/H2 disagreement; and correlated-output signal `P(H2 adverse | primary and H1 agree nonadverse)-P(H2 adverse | otherwise)`.

Phase-3 estimands use `w3`: Mask-A-versus-B, each mask-versus-K+, and source-audit leakage/over-redaction rates, overall and for primary `AUTO`. The correlated-output signal and mask comparisons can detect disagreement patterns but cannot distinguish correlated error from shared correct interpretation or shared blind spots.

No latent-class prevalence, sensitivity, specificity, posterior truth class, or reviewer-accuracy estimate may enter a primary endpoint or gate. An explicitly non-decision-facing sensitivity analysis may be displayed only if its prespecified local-dependence likelihood is identified, no reviewer accuracy is fixed, label switching is resolved without candidate routes, and conclusions are invariant across fitted dependence structures; otherwise suppress it. Even a fitted model does not establish clinical truth.

Workload estimands include design-weighted physician-hours per 100 `N_A`, packets and units, immutable active minutes excluding gaps over 30 minutes, duplicate/challenge/stress counts, sentinel share, maximum normalized weight, and Kish effective size overall and per domain.

Use exact two- and three-phase stratified HT variance from the nested SRSWOR pairwise inclusion probabilities, plus at least 10,000 nested Rao-Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates carrying each admission and all units/labels together. For rare/zero events invert the corresponding nested stratified multivariate-hypergeometric distribution; use the wider valid bound. Within every replicate, enumerate unknown-compatible lower/upper states before confidence limits. A single max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers route burdens, disagreement-envelope endpoints, all safety/capture quantities, confirmation, missed-unit, mask leakage/over-redaction, and workload. No model-based interval may replace design-based inference.

Baselines with identical weights are marginal-majority route; deterministic stratum mapping; recommendation-unit majority; favorable consensus; deliberately leaky D receiving R; complete-case unweighted estimates; and equal phase-2 allocation. Baselines cannot nominate routes. Apparent improvement from leakage or consensus is adverse.

## Interpretation and evidence boundary

**Supportive:** all integrity, workload, precision, route-specific, leakage, masking, deletion-stability, and adverse-uncertainty gates pass. This supports only prospective silent validation of frozen code and shows that a feasible probability audit estimates reproducibility and method-detectable disagreement in stored text; it does not validate clinical correctness.

**Supportive for RECON only:** method-detectable unsafe/mixed content is reproducibly captured by RECON/DEFER, but AUTO is too sparse, contaminated, masking-dependent, uncertain, or imprecise. This is useful adverse evidence against copy-oriented routing and may nominate a reconciliation-only silent study.

**Adverse:** duplicate or challenge review places primary AUTO above its disagreement margin; primary RECON/DEFER misses method-detectable adverse cases; method-diverse H2 detects adverse content despite primary/H1 agreement; K-A/K-B/K+ diverge decision-relevantly; reviewer or training-site deletion reverses a gate; generic matching increases AUTO under negative pairing; index-only clocks alter routing; or leakage/consensus materially improves apparent accuracy. This rejects the affected retrospective route, not all future workflows.

**Inconclusive:** retrieval/hash/access failure, excessive unknowns or masking defects, infeasible sentinel/master burden, phase-2/3 budget or completion failure, sparse route/audit events, inadequate effective size, wide disagreement envelope, unidentified or unstable dependence model, compatibility explosion, or need to tune on audit outcomes. Fixed or accuracy-blind redesign can be proposed only as a new untouched protocol; the current result cannot be rescued.

Independent stored-text adjudication is not clinical truth. MIMIC lacks physical departure, report viewing and draft/version/sign/transmission histories, outside records, actual responsibility, preferences, recommendation appropriateness, orders/referrals/scheduling completion, and patient outcomes. `G_u` measures storage-to-storage opportunity only. Correlated-error diagnostics expose disagreement patterns but cannot establish truth without an external reference.

A passing result may nominate only a consecutive prospective silent study with immutable report finalization/addenda and viewing, discharge drafts/edits/sign/transmission and physical departure, all recommendation units, responsibilities, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing risks, orders/referrals/scheduling, final transmitted artifact, route-blinded expert safety review, drift, and workload. Clinical deployment, safety, utility, and benefit require subsequent governed evaluation; no retrospective MIMIC result establishes them.

## Advance and residual uncertainty

Relative to the parent, this design makes panel burden an estimand and a gate, uses one primary split-view lane for all master admissions, and reserves independent duplicate, dual-mask, unmasked stress, and method-diverse challenge panels for route-independent probability subsamples selected before outcomes. It replaces a purported independent text-review reference with a design-weighted disagreement envelope, an explicit shared-blind-spot tipping point, reviewer/training-site deletion sensitivity, and a prohibition on decision-facing latent-class truth estimates. It therefore increases the feasible master denominator without zeroing any nonsentinel probability, preserves all-unit and worst-unit safeguards, and converts sparse routes into honest finite-population precision failures rather than biased enrichment. The audit can reveal correlated output, mask dependence, and method-detectable adverse disagreement but cannot measure clinical error or truth. The principal unresolved risks are rare domains, shared source limitations and shared blind spots, masking cues, governance-dependent margins/cost ceilings, and absence of prospective workflow evidence.
