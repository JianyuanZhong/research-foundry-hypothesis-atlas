# All-offered intention-to-obtain-UACR versus immediate cystatin-C allocation

## Episode-13 decision and scientific advance

A health service with cystatin-C capacity for 20% of adults whose creatinine eGFR is 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m² must choose between two implementable list-construction strategies: allocate immediately from UACR-free information, or first request UACR for everyone, wait for a usable result or a declared failure, and then allocate. The highest-ranked valid parent makes both branches executable but deliberately stops short of comparing them. The second valid parent adds a comparison between AG restricted to snapshot-usable UACR rows and all-O G; that mixes a per-protocol selected-result population with an all-offered population.

This child makes the clinically direct comparison intention-to-test in all O. It changes no O/L/V definition, time zero, score, fit, lock, cystatin capacity, biochemical label, N18 boundary, inference engine, margin, or inherited gate. It adds one all-O contrast using already-frozen AG and G scores. Participants in O\V remain eligible under AG through the parent's development-frozen median fallback; they are never dropped, backfilled, or treated as successful future collections.

The strongest evidence already supported is narrower. Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041; full-text JATS XML inspected as frozen source [source checksum]) studied 468,969 UK Biobank participants, internally validated prediction of creatinine–cystatin eGFR differences, and reported validation AUC about 0.75 for an enriched model. Let τ_alloc be list commitment time, t_U_collect urine collection time and t_U_result usable result-release time. A row is prior only if collection≤result≤τ_alloc, post only if result>τ_alloc, and unknown if any required time is absent. Never infer timing from instance 0, 53-0.0, same-row storage, nonmissing urine or cystatin.

The mandatory pre-outcome audit reports timing_fields_present=false, assignable_prior_count=0, assignable_post_count=0, timing_unknown_count=|V_p|, timing_unknown_fraction=1, and observed_preassay_estimand_evaluable=false. Zero assignable rows means unclassifiable, not post-decision collection.

- W1_ITT_protocolized_uacr_first_all_O: the service attempts UACR for every O participant, waits for completion/failure, uses observed UACR when usable and the frozen median fallback after failure, then applies A/AG/G to all O. AG:A tests grip increment after an intention-to-obtain-UACR protocol; G:A tests substitution. This is the service-wide UACR-first estimand and prevents failures being silently excluded. It does not show that median fallback is clinically optimal.
- W1_PP_protocolized_uacr_first_usable_V: conditional usable-result performance among V, comparing AG_V:A_V and G_V:A_V at unchanged O-based capacity. It is per-protocol/usable-result evidence and may not be labeled all-offered, real-time or observed pre-assay.
- W0_immediate_no_uacr_all_O: immediate allocation uses only G versus B,F,T in O. A, AG and every V ranking are inadmissible because UACR or UACR usability is not yet known.
- Wobs_observed_preassay: actual UKB availability before allocation is nonevaluable. It must contain evaluable=false, no numeric yield/contrast/interval and reason required_timestamps_absent.

## Estimands, missing labels, bootstrap, and simultaneous families

For fixed selected set S and its branch’s n_p:

Y_H(S)=1000Σ_i I(i∈S)H_i/n_p;
Y_D(S)=1000Σ_i I(i∈S)D_i/n_p;
Y_H18(S)=1000Σ_i I(i∈S)H_iK18_i/n_p.

Report exact selected count, observed-label count, each yield, contrasts, ratios, overlap, Jaccard and challenger-only minus comparator-only decomposition. The denominator is always n_p, never observed labels.

For a selected missing H or D, the single-policy lower/upper contribution is 0/1. For contrast S:C let c_i=I(i∈S)−I(i∈C). Its joint lower numerator is the observed numerator plus Σ_missing min(0,c_i), and its upper is the observed numerator plus Σ_missing max(0,c_i); use c_iK18_i for H×K18. Thus a missing H can enter the H×K18 upper bound only when K18=1. One common unknown value is used for a participant shared across policies. Obtain exact ratio extrema over these same binary assignments; never divide marginal bounds. Any feasible zero comparator numerator makes the ratio undefined and the branch inconclusive. More than 1% of O missing valid cystatin makes every allocation-performance branch inconclusive.

Use B=2,000 paired bootstrap draws, with fixed transforms, models, policy membership, n_p and V. For partition name test or replication and zero-based decimal draw b, seed NumPy Generator(PCG64DXSM(seed)) with the unsigned big-endian integer represented by the full digest SHA256(UTF8("ukb-grip-bootstrap-v3|"+partition+"|"+b)). Sort O_p by canonical decimal eid, then call Generator.integers(0,|O_p|,size=|O_p|,endpoint=false,dtype=int64); use resulting multiplicities as weights and retain n_p as denominator. Record NumPy and bit-generator versions. All comparisons in a partition share the draw. Pooled draw b independently resamples each lock with that lock's seed and sums weighted numerators and fixed denominators; never pool participants before resampling. Models and selections are never refit. Missing labels contribute zero to bootstrap point estimates and are handled separately by sharp bounds.

Define three prespecified simultaneous families:

- F_H contains, in both locks, Δ_H for G:B, G:F, G:T, AG:A, G:A, AG_V:A_V and G_V:A_V, plus log H-yield ratios for G:B, G:T, AG:A and AG_V:A_V: 22 statistics.
- F_D contains pooled partition-stratified Δ_D for G:F, G:T, AG:A, G:A and AG_V:A_V: 5 statistics. The first, second, third and fifth also require positive point signs in each lock; G:A is noninferiority at −1 per 1,000.
- F_18 contains pooled Δ_H18 for G:B, G:F, G:T, AG:A, G:A, AG_V:A_V and G_V:A_V: 7 statistics.

For family component j, estimate bootstrap SE s_j. If every s_j is finite and positive and at least 95% of ratio replicates are finite, calculate t_bj=(θ_bj−θ_j)/s_j and q_0.95 as the empirical 95th percentile (NumPy linear quantile) of max_j|t_bj|. The two-sided simultaneous interval is θ_j±q_0.95s_j; exponentiate log-ratio endpoints. For simultaneous one-sided lower bounds use q_L, the 95th percentile of max_j[(θ_j−θ_bj)/s_j], and lower=θ_j−q_Ls_j. If studentization fails, use componentwise Bonferroni percentile intervals with two-sided tail 0.025/m or one-sided tail 0.05/m, report fallback=true, and classify any component with fewer than 95% finite replicates inconclusive. A feasible zero primary ratio denominator is inconclusive regardless of bootstrap behavior.

## Calibration, placebo, sex, robustness, and event gates

In each lock, among observed L, report H prevalence, sensitivity, PPV, selected-count calibration, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and fixed-capacity decision curves for all fitted primary policies. Estimate intercept and slope by unpenalized logistic regression of H on the frozen score logit. Also estimate A/AG within V∩L. Finite slopes for G in O, AG in O and AG in V must each lie in [0.8,1.2] in both locks for full support. A finite out-of-range slope is an adverse stability finding; separation, nonconvergence or an undefined slope is inconclusive. Intercepts are descriptive.

For the inherited participant-specific placebo, freeze G. In each lock create strata sex(2)×pace(slow/steady/brisk/other)×health(excellent/good/fair-or-poor/other). Cells below 20 merge first across health within sex/pace and then into the same-sex stratum; freeze and report the map before H is read. For draw d=0,…,499, seed NumPy Generator(PCG64DXSM(seed)) from the full unsigned big-endian SHA256 digest of UTF8("ukb-grip-placebo-v1|"+partition+"|"+d). Visit final strata in lexicographic order of their sex/pace/health labels, visit members in ascending canonical eid, and call Generator.permutation once per stratum to permute frozen Z_R. Record NumPy and bit-generator versions. Hold B, the G grip coefficient, transforms and penalty fixed, rerank O and select n_p. Actual Δ_H(G:B) must be strictly above the NumPy-linear 97.5th percentile of the 500 placebo contrasts in each lock; report p=(1+#placebo≥actual)/501. Failure is adverse to participant-specific raw-grip value. Placebos do not prove muscle mechanism or eliminate residual frailty.

Before outcomes, freeze sex-specific reporting for all primary policies. The sex-instability flag is true if any superiority contrast G:B, G:F, G:T, AG:A or AG_V:A_V has a sex-specific H point contrast ≤0 for the same sex in both locks while its overall contrast otherwise meets its biochemical support gates. A flag makes clinical interpretation inconclusive. Selection imbalance or a development grip×sex interaction is descriptive and requires equity review, but has no automatic threshold.

For UACR, unavailability over 5% in either lock, a sign reversal of Δ_H or Δ_D for AG:A or AG_V:A_V in any of the six frozen variants, or changing V across variants is inconclusive. Variants, C/CG, HbA1c, hand maximum/mean/minimum, no winsorization, complete-valid pace/health, sex-specific grip scaling, grip×sex, residual/richer grip, absolute D, 1/2-year lags and 10%/30% capacities are sensitivity analyses only and cannot rescue primary failure. A sign reversal across hand definitions, benefit confined to one sex or coarse frailty stratum, or one-lock-only benefit is unstable/adverse as reported.

N18 support is evaluated only if at least 100 observed-label H×K18 events occur in the union of all policies relevant to the branch across both locks, at least 20 occur in each pooled directional swap set for every branch comparison, and at least 30 occur within a lock for a positive lock-specific N18 statement. Missing H cannot satisfy counts. For W0 the comparison sets are G/B/F/T; for W1_ITT they add A/AG and both directions of AG:A and G:A; for W1_PP they require A_V/AG_V/G_V and both directions. Failure makes chronic-kidney consequence unresolved, not adverse. Report Aalen–Johansen N18 cumulative incidence with competing death; Cox/Fine–Gray, K17, Kany and death-without-prior-K18 are descriptive only.

## Exact classification function and result meanings

First run immutable-source, join, equation, lock, leakage, transform, model-column, convergence, tie, budget, date-pairing, V and timing checks. Any failure makes affected performance branches computationally inconclusive; Wobs remains observed_preassay_not_identifiable independently.

A biochemical superiority gate with materiality m is supportive only when its adverse joint difference bound is ≥m, its simultaneous lower confidence bound is >0, and any required ratio has point estimate and adverse feasible lower bound at least its target with simultaneous lower ratio bound >1. It is adverse with adequate precision when its simultaneous upper difference bound is below m, its feasible ratio upper bound or simultaneous ratio upper bound is below the target, or its required directional swap yield is ≤0. Otherwise it is inconclusive. A noninferiority gate is supportive when its adverse joint difference bound is above −1 and its simultaneous one-sided lower bound is >−1; adverse when its favorable joint upper bound or simultaneous upper bound is ≤−1; otherwise inconclusive. D gates follow the same zero or −1 margins. Placebo failure and finite out-of-range calibration are adverse; undefined calibration, variant sign reversal, sex instability, excessive missingness or sparse/invalid computation are inconclusive.

W0 biochemical supportive requires every G:B/G:F/G:T H difference gate, the G:B and G:T ratio gates, positive directional swaps, both-lock placebo, positive both-lock and pooled D versus F/T, calibration and no sex flag. W1_ITT biochemical supportive requires AG:A H difference/ratio/D/swap gates in both locks, G:A H and D noninferiority, all six UACR variants concordant, calibration and no sex flag. W1_PP biochemical supportive requires its preflight plus AG_V:A_V H difference/ratio/D/swap gates in both locks, G_V:A_V H noninferiority, all variants concordant, calibration and no sex flag. The compound biochemical claim requires all three branches supportive; one branch cannot rescue another.

If biochemical gates pass and N18 is sufficient, full N18 support requires adverse-bound Δ_H18(G:B)≥1 and simultaneous pooled interval >0; G:F and G:T lower bounds >−1; adverse-bound Δ_H18(AG:A) and Δ_H18(AG_V:A_V) each ≥1 with pooled intervals >0; and G:A and G_V:A_V lower bounds >−1. A sufficiently powered superiority result is adverse if its upper interval is below +1 or wholly ≤0; noninferiority is adverse if its upper interval is ≤−1. Boundary-crossing intervals are inconclusive.

Emit exactly one global performance label:

- full_supportive: all three biochemical branches support, every branch-specific N18 sufficiency threshold passes, and every N18 materiality/noninferiority gate supports.
- biochemical_allocation_supportive_chronic_consequence_unresolved: all three biochemical branches support but any N18 sufficiency condition fails or a non-adverse N18 interval crosses its boundary.
- adverse: at least one adequately precise biochemical or sufficiently powered N18 gate is adverse, with branch-specific reasons; negative results remain clinically useful.
- inconclusive: no adverse gate is established, but at least one required branch fails support because of uncertainty, missingness, instability, integrity or sparsity.

Also emit exactly one label for each branch: immediate_no_uacr_supportive, immediate_no_uacr_adverse or immediate_no_uacr_inconclusive; uacr_first_itt_supportive, uacr_first_itt_adverse or uacr_first_itt_inconclusive; uacr_first_usable_supportive, uacr_first_usable_adverse or uacr_first_usable_inconclusive; and always observed_preassay_not_identifiable for Wobs. Interpret combinations explicitly: PP support with ITT adverse/inconclusive does not establish service-wide UACR-first value; ITT support with PP adverse indicates failure/imputation or candidate-pool dependence; W1 support with W0 adverse suggests the planned UACR step may be necessary; W0 support with W1 adverse means adding/conditioning on UACR does not validate the UACR-first strategy. If both W0 and W1 support, UKB still cannot choose between them because turnaround, burden, costs, actions, harms and utility are absent.

No development result, pooled point alone, C/CG, sensitivity, N17, Kany or longer follow-up can rescue a failed primary gate.

## Verification contract and limits

The result file must contain source/hash/header audits; O/L/V counts and exclusions; timing audit; preprocessing/fold/grid/fit hashes; coefficients and F choice; selected eid hashes and counts; all yields, bounds, swaps, bootstrap seeds/fallbacks/families; calibration, placebo, sex and N18 sufficiency outputs; four workflow blocks; and machine-generated reasons for the global label.

Mandatory adversarial fixtures reject: wrong creatinine/UACR units; confusing 30505 with 30515; treating <6.7 as measured 6.7; changing O, V or n; using 20% of V; admitting imputed UACR into V; refitting in V or locks; any B-column mismatch; G other than B+Z_R; AG other than A+Z_R; locked preprocessing; altered folds/grid/ties/seeds; rounded-eGFR T ordering; outcome leakage; marginal rather than joint bounds; independently bootstrapped policies; mispaired diagnosis/date arrays; N17 censoring N18; same-day death after N18; V evidence labeled immediate/all-offered/observed-preassay; all-O median-fallback evidence labeled usable-result; numeric Wobs output; assignable_prior_count=0 interpreted as post-decision urine; and correct supportive, adverse or inconclusive arithmetic attached to the wrong workflow or an unsupported clinical conclusion.

The automatic verifier can establish source identity and field absence; joins; O/L/V membership; equations; partitions; deterministic fits and rankings; exact capacity; labels and chronology; bounds; resampling; multiplicity; gates; and whether conclusions follow computed outputs. It cannot establish true pre-order UACR availability, assay turnaround, persistent albuminuria or reduced eGFR, measured GFR, adjudicated CKD, complete event capture, muscle mechanism, fairness, clinical action, benefit, harm, cost-effectiveness or external transport. Those require participant-level urine collection/result and allocation/order timestamps, repeat urine and filtration measurements, measured-GFR/body-composition data, outpatient/chart/prescription/action data, nephrology and equity review, an external cohort, and ultimately a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.

## Advance over the selected parent

The selected parent’s timing distinction is retained. The substantive advance is to make the protocolized UACR-first decision complete: all offered participants now contribute to W1_ITT, including failed UACR attempts through the already-frozen median policy, while W1_PP separately answers performance after a usable result. Immediate UACR-free allocation and empirically observed chronology remain separate. The proposal is now executable without undocumented inherited artifacts: every population, score, fold, tuning rule, tie, seed, estimand, uncertainty family, gate, workflow label and verifier boundary is specified in one scientific node.
