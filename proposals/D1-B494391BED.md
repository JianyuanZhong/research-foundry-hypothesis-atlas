# From pulmonary-nodule handoff phenotypes to a falsifiable intervention-choice matrix

## Consequential decision, strongest current evidence, and unresolved claim

The decision is not merely whether discharge-summary discordance is common. It is which next prospective intervention, if any, is justified by the observable MIMIC phenotype: (1) departure-time artifact availability/signature/transmission work; (2) propagation of a still-eligible recommendation into an available discharge artifact; (3) structured reconciliation that permits a clinically coherent superseding or alternate-resolution plan rather than forced copying; (4) a generic cross-action workflow rather than a pulmonary-nodule module; or (5) downstream tracking, exchange, and communication measurement. These targets are mutually distinguishable only after recommendation eligibility, artifact chronology, content state, alternate resolution, and capture limitations are kept separate.

The strongest evidence already supported is narrow and is preserved. In snapshot `[source checksum]`, a complete unsampled workflow audit streamed 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, and all 331,794 `DS` rows. It found 331,794 unique `DS` admission keys, 17 missing discharge `storetime` values, and ordinary discharge storage latency with median 3.55 hours, p90 101.38 hours, and p95 202.02 hours after discharge. A broad retrieval screen found 3,764 possible pulmonary recommendation reports across 3,641 admissions and 3,528 subjects; these are retrieval candidates, not adjudicated opportunities or prevalence. The parent also correctly established the recommendation-to-artifact clock `G=DS_store-R_store`, the frozen all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal report/addendum reconstruction, exhaustive departure and joint states, institutional calibration, and strict storage-versus-visibility/noncausal limits.

What remains untested is the intervention-decision claim. Text discordance among timely artifacts can reflect at least four different things: a still-eligible recommendation that was not propagated; a recommendation whose clinical actionability cannot be established from available information; a documented superseding/alternate-resolution plan; or a generic actionable-recommendation process weakness. A pooled nonthoracic comparator cannot resolve these alternatives if it combines actions and targets with different semantics. Nor can a prevalence margin alone say whether availability, content reconciliation, exception handling, a generic engine, or downstream tracking deserves priority.

This child tests the following falsifiable hypothesis:

> After freezing every radiology-defined pulmonary-nodule opportunity and restricting content-incorporation claims to canonical discharge artifacts stored by departure and after the operative recommendation, a clinically reviewable subset will remain in which blinded experts judge the recommendation to be a high-confidence MIMIC-observable handoff action and the discharge artifact neither faithfully propagates it nor documents a coherent superseding/alternate-resolution plan. The absolute burden, review yield, and mutually exclusive burden contrast with departure-time unavailability will identify whether availability or eligible propagation is the larger observable upstream target. Family-specific, rather than heterogeneously pooled, analogous-action estimates will determine whether a generic cross-action pilot is supported, nodule specificity is supported, or intervention scope remains unresolved.

The experiment can falsify each part independently. It can find that most apparent discordance is actionability-uncertain or coherently superseded; that delayed artifact availability is the larger upstream state; that eligible propagation gaps are precise and larger; that the phenotype replicates across multiple separately adjudicated action families; or that counts, reliability, overlap, and uncertainty do not permit a decision. None of these results estimates intervention effect or clinical benefit.

## Nested recommendation actionability without changing `N`

The key repair is to distinguish radiology eligibility from high-confidence clinical handoff actionability under the evidence MIMIC actually contains. After `N` is frozen, a separate dual-review panel receives only the complete pre-`D` radiology chain abstraction, admissible admission/death/hospice facts used by the eligibility rubric, and the locked actionability questions. It is blinded to canonical discharge text and timing, later events, comparator labels/outcomes, and downstream traces. It assigns exactly one `K` state:

* `K_E` — high-confidence **MIMIC-observable handoff-eligible**: the operative recommendation is explicit, unconditional, patient-specific, temporally operative at `D`, and sufficiently specified for a discharge handoff under the available evidence;
* `K_N` — not handoff-eligible under observable evidence because a pre-`D` radiology-chain component unequivocally retracts/resolves the target or makes the recommendation nonoperative; this should be rare because manifest eligibility is frozen and triggers an eligibility-disagreement audit rather than silent denominator deletion;
* `K_U` — actionability cannot be established because material context is unavailable or ambiguous, including inability to assess prior stability, risk, competing care, or whether the recommendation remains appropriate from the available sources.

`K` is nested inside `N`; no `K` state adds or removes a pulmonary opportunity. `K_E` does not mean guideline-concordant or clinically beneficial. It means only that the recommendation is sufficiently action-ready to test observable handoff propagation. `K_U` is not omission and cannot be reassigned by discharge content. Publish `count(K)/N` and least-favorable bounds. A directional eligible-propagation claim requires `K_U<=10%` and pre-consensus raw agreement at least 90% with weighted kappa at least 0.70; otherwise actionability and intervention choice are inconclusive.

The sources do not include complete progress notes, longitudinal outside imaging, a validated guideline-risk model, or complete order/communication data. Therefore experts must use `K_U` whenever those missing facts are material. This explicit uncertainty is preferable to pretending that an unconditional radiology sentence proves clinical appropriateness.

## Correct clock, departure state, and complete joint state

For every frozen opportunity define:

* `R_store`: `storetime` of the adjudicated operative recommendation component;
* `DS_store`: `storetime` of the unique canonical `DS`;
* `G=DS_store-R_store`, in hours, the only primary observed recommendation-to-artifact content-opportunity clock;
* `V_D` if `DS_store<=D`; `V_24` if `D<DS_store<=D+24h`; `V_72` if `D+24h<DS_store<=D+72h`; `V_168` if `D+72h<DS_store<=D+168h`; `V_late` if `DS_store>D+168h`; `V_U` if unresolved;
* `H_stale` if `G<0`; `H_0_6` if `0<=G<6h`; `H_6_24` if `6<=G<24h`; `H_24_72` if `24<=G<72h`; `H_72plus` if `G>=72h`; `H_U` otherwise.

Retain the exhaustive inherited departure state `Y` over all `N`:

* `F`: canonical `DS` stored by `D`, classifiable, with patient-specific nodule, chest/thoracic CT action, and exactly compatible interval/date;
* `C_O`: stored by `D`, classifiable, but lacks the patient-specific actionable plan;
* `C_I`: stored by `D` with incomplete or discordant target/action/timing and no documented alternate resolution;
* `R`: stored by `D` and documents an alternate resolution, including refusal/preferences, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, or scheduled outside follow-up;
* `A`: known `DS_store>D`, regardless of eventual content;
* `U_D`: canonical artifact/store time/linkage/text missing, unreadable, contradictory, or unclassifiable.

`F+C_O+C_I+R+A+U_D=N`. Eventual faithful content never converts `A` to `F`. Content in a `DS` stored before `R_store` is stale/coincidental chronology, not evidence of incorporation.

Add a decision-facing content-resolution state `W`, adjudicated by separate content reviewers who receive the frozen recommendation abstraction and canonical `DS` text but are blinded to `R_store`, `DS_store`, `D`, gap band, downstream traces, and pulmonary-versus-comparator hypothesis labels as far as target wording permits:

* `W_P` — faithful propagation of target, action, and compatible interval/date;
* `W_O` — eligible plan omitted;
* `W_I` — target/action/interval present but materially incomplete or discordant;
* `W_S` — a coherent, concrete superseding or alternate-resolution plan is documented, with the responsible pathway and replacement/disposition sufficiently specified; refusal/preferences and goals-of-care resolution may qualify;
* `W_Q` — alternate/superseding language exists but is ambiguous, nonconcrete, or its clinical coherence cannot be judged from available evidence;
* `W_U` — text/linkage is unreadable, contradictory, or otherwise unclassifiable.

`W_S` is not a propagation failure and must never be forced into `F`, `C_O`, or `C_I`; `W_Q` remains unknown rather than a success. The inherited `R` maps to `W_S` only when the stricter coherence rubric is met. Report the deterministic crosswalk and every discrepancy. A naive intervention that simply copies the original recommendation would be contraindicated as an interpretation whenever `W_S` or `W_Q` is present; those states motivate exception-aware reconciliation, not automatic propagation.

The complete primary object is `J_N=count(K,V,H,W,Y)/N`, including zero and unknown cells. Its hash and row count must be invariant across analyses. The primary ample, timely incorporation opportunity remains `E={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. The 24-hour boundary is retained as an operational sensitivity-resistant window, not asserted to be a human editing threshold; publish all positive-gap bands, and repeat at 12 and 72 hours. `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` is a descriptive identity only and cannot define an incorporation opportunity.

Post-discharge artifacts (`V_24/V_72/V_168/V_late`) and stale artifacts (`H_stale`) are never eligible content-incorporation opportunities. Their eventual `W` states remain transition descriptions only. This prevents a late faithful summary from becoming a success and prevents matching stale text from being called propagation.

## Absolute estimands with clinical units

Prevalence alone is not the decision metric. Publish the following mutually interpretable quantities, all with subject-clustered simultaneous uncertainty and compatible-unknown bounds.

1. **Departure-time unavailable burden**
   * `B_A=100*n(A)/N`: canonical artifacts unavailable in storage at departure per 100 frozen pulmonary opportunities.
   * `B_A^excess(b)=100*[q_N(b)-q_O^std(b)]` at `b=0,24,72,168` hours: pulmonary-opportunity excess over standardized ordinary institutional storage latency per 100 opportunities. Raw `B_A` measures the observable target volume; calibrated excess distinguishes nodule-associated from institution-wide latency but is not causal.

2. **High-confidence eligible propagation burden**
   * Primary `B_P=100*n(K_E,V_D,H in {H_24_72,H_72plus},W in {W_O,W_I})/N`: ample-clock eligible propagation failures per 100 **all frozen opportunities**.
   * `p_P=n(W_O+W_I)/n(K_E,E)`: failures per 100 high-confidence, timely, ample-clock recommendations presented for content review.
   * `NNR_P=1/p_P` (with `p_P` expressed as a proportion): discharge artifacts requiring review to encounter one observed eligible propagation failure. Report interval-valued `NNR_P`, not infinity-truncated point estimates.
   * Repeat `B_P`, `p_P`, and `NNR_P` for every positive `H` band. `H_stale`, delayed `V`, `K_U`, `W_S`, `W_Q`, and `W_U` never enter the numerator.

3. **Resolution and uncertainty burdens**
   * `B_S=100*n(K_E,E,W_S)/N`: coherent superseding/alternate-resolution plans per 100 all opportunities.
   * `B_Q=100*n(K_E,E,W_Q)/N`: ambiguous exception plans per 100 all opportunities.
   * `B_KU=100*n(K_U)/N` and `B_WU=100*n(K_E,E,W_U)/N`.
   * `G_exception=(n(W_S)+n(W_Q))/n(W_O+W_I+W_S+W_Q)` within `K_E,E`: the fraction of apparent nonfaithful-copy cases requiring an exception pathway rather than blind propagation. This is a guardrail yield, not a failure rate.

4. **Observable upstream allocation contrast**
   * `D_PA=B_P-B_A`: eligible propagation failures minus unavailable-at-departure artifacts per 100 frozen opportunities. These numerators occupy mutually exclusive timely-versus-delayed states, so the contrast compares observable target volumes without claiming equal remediability, harm, or intervention efficacy.
   * Also report `D_PA^excess=B_P-max(0,B_A^excess(0))`, explicitly labeled a specificity/calibration contrast. Do not replace raw `D_PA` with it: institution-wide unavailability remains clinically relevant even if pulmonary excess is zero.

5. **Intervention workload frontier rather than an invented safety cutoff**
   * For every integer review capacity `c` from 1 to 100 canonical artifacts per 100 `N`, publish whether capacity covers the entire `K_E,E` review pool and the observed number of `W_O+W_I`, `W_S`, `W_Q`, and `W_U` states encountered under exhaustive review. Report `NNR_P` and the fraction of reviews that would require exception handling.
   * For availability, publish delayed artifacts per 100 `N` at departure and residual delays at +24/+72/+168 hours.
   * A local governance team can overlay its prospectively declared maximum acceptable reviews per detected gap, staffing capacity, and relative cost of unavailable versus discordant artifacts. MIMIC supplies the empirical frontier; it cannot invent the utility weights. No 10% prevalence threshold is used to declare an intervention clinically worthwhile.

A descriptive phenotype is **statistically resolved** only when all adjudication gates pass, there are at least 20 numerator events for the relevant burden, and the simultaneous 95% confidence interval width is at most 5 events per 100 `N` for `B_A`/`B_P` or 10 percentage points for conditional `p_P`/`G_exception`. These are precision requirements, not claims that smaller burdens are unimportant. If not met, the corresponding intervention choice is inconclusive.

Each family gets its own manifest `N_Xf`, eligibility cascade, unknown bounds, event/precision gates, and complete joint table. Report all cell counts, fallbacks, weights, maximum normalized weight, effective sample size, and match shortfalls.

For each family compute:

* `p_P,Xf^std`: standardized eligible propagation-failure probability within `K_E,E`;
* `B_P,Xf`: family failures per 100 frozen family opportunities;
* `Delta_f=p_P,N-p_P,Xf^std` and ratio `RR_f=p_P,N/p_P,Xf^std`, with undefined/zero cases handled explicitly;
* family-specific `W_S`, `W_Q`, and unknown rates.

A family is decision-adequate only with at least 100 adjudicated opportunities, at least 30 `K_E,E` rows, at least 20 propagation failures across the pulmonary/family comparison, no empty pulmonary-supported standardization cell after locked fallback, maximum normalized weight `<=0.10`, weighted effective sample size `>=50`, and reliability/unknown gates. Sparse or nonoverlapping families remain visible but cannot drive scope.

The scope rules are deliberately conservative:

* **Evidence supporting a nodule-targeted pilot** requires a resolved absolute pulmonary `B_P/p_P` phenotype and simultaneous lower bounds `Delta_f>0` for every decision-adequate comparator family. This is comparative evidence of higher pulmonary review yield, not proof of a nodule-specific mechanism.
* **Evidence supporting a generic cross-action pilot** requires a resolved absolute eligible-propagation phenotype in pulmonary opportunities and independently in at least two decision-adequate action families. It does not require rates to be equal and does not pool unlike actions. The claim is replication of an observable process gap across actions, not semantic equivalence.
* If only one comparator family is adequate, family results conflict, estimates reverse under matching, or pulmonary excess is unresolved, intervention scope is **inconclusive**. A pulmonary absolute finding may still support a pulmonary pilot, but MIMIC cannot call the mechanism nodule-specific or generic.
* Comparable/high rates in a pooled heterogeneous `X` can never by itself trigger a generic intervention label. Conversely, sparse `X` cannot erase an independently resolved pulmonary burden.

## Computable intervention-decision matrix

Populate every cell below from the complete joint states and simultaneous intervals. A row may be resolved supportive, adverse, or inconclusive independently. No row authorizes deployment; it identifies the next prospective study and its required guardrails.

### Row 1: Departure-time availability/signature/transmission study

Observable trigger: raw `B_A` is precisely estimated and exceeds `B_P` (`D_PA` simultaneous upper bound `<0`), or `B_A` remains substantial on a locally declared workload frontier even when `D_PA` is unresolved. Calibration determines scope: positive stable `B_A^excess(0)` suggests a pulmonary-opportunity-associated storage phenotype; high `B_A` with negligible excess suggests institution-wide latency.

Adverse: `B_A` is precise and smaller than `B_P` (`D_PA` lower bound `>0`) and residual delay at +24 hours is low. Inconclusive: missing times, calibration reversal, standardization failure, or the `D_PA` interval crosses zero.

Decision meaning: MIMIC can prioritize measuring true draft/final/signature/visibility/transmission timestamps. It cannot tell whether the artifact was visible, signed, sent, received, or causally delayed, so it cannot choose a UI alert or claim that faster storage improves care.

### Row 2: Eligible recommendation-propagation reconciliation study

Observable trigger: actionability/reliability gates pass; `B_P` and `p_P` are precisely resolved with at least 20 `W_O+W_I` events; the review-yield frontier is compatible with prospectively supplied local capacity; and either `D_PA` has lower bound `>0` or availability and propagation are both retained as co-primary targets. The intervention must fire only for `K_E`, timely post-recommendation artifacts and must distinguish `W_O/W_I` from `W_S/W_Q`.

Adverse: the simultaneous upper bound for `B_P` is below the local predeclared minimum addressable volume or the lower confidence bound of `NNR_P` exceeds the local maximum review burden; most apparent failures resolve to `K_U`, `W_S`, or `W_Q`; or propagation reverses under locked clock/actionability sensitivity. Because local utility thresholds are unavailable in MIMIC, absent an external declaration this row may be statistically resolved but operationally undecided.

Decision meaning: MIMIC can quantify eligible gaps and review yield. It cannot establish that an author saw the report, that the plan was clinically appropriate, or that reconciliation would improve completion or outcomes.

### Row 3: Exception-aware supersession/alternate-resolution workflow

Observable trigger: `B_S` or `B_Q` is nonzero and precisely estimated, and `G_exception` shows that a consequential share of nonfaithful-copy cases requires preserving, clarifying, or adjudicating an alternate plan. This does not compete with propagation as a prevalence test: it is a safety/design guardrail. Every prospective content intervention must offer `accept original`, `modify`, `supersede with reason/responsibility`, and `uncertain/escalate` states rather than automatic copying.

Adverse to forced-copy design: any clinically verified `W_S` exists, substantial `W_Q/K_U` persists, or reviewer disagreement concentrates in alternate-resolution language. Inconclusive: poor agreement or missing context prevents coherent-versus-ambiguous classification.

Decision meaning: MIMIC can show that exception handling is observably needed. It cannot determine the correctness of a superseding plan without complete chart review and clinical governance, and it cannot infer whether the plan was communicated or ordered.

### Row 4: Nodule-targeted versus generic cross-action scope

Nodule-targeted evidence follows the familywise positive `Delta_f` rule. Generic-process evidence requires independently resolved absolute propagation gaps in pulmonary plus at least two adequate action families. Mixed, sparse, or reversal-prone family results make scope inconclusive. A single semantically heterogeneous pooled comparator is never sufficient.

Decision meaning: MIMIC can determine whether the same storage/text phenotype replicates in separately defined action families. It cannot prove a common human mechanism or justify one universal UI. A generic intervention still needs action-specific wording, exception logic, and prospective usability/safety testing; a nodule-targeted result supports a focused pilot, not exclusivity.

### Row 5: Bundled/factorial upstream study

Observable trigger: both `B_A` and `B_P` are precisely resolved and neither dominates (`D_PA` simultaneous interval contains zero), or each remains material under locally declared capacity/utility thresholds. The next study should separate availability/signature/transmission and content-reconciliation components, with component-specific timestamps and a factorial or sequential design. Eventual fidelity must never be credited to the departure-time availability component.

Adverse: one burden is precisely negligible under the local decision frontier and the other dominates. Inconclusive: either axis fails precision or calibration.

Decision meaning: MIMIC can identify coexistence; it cannot estimate interaction or decide whether bundling is beneficial.

### Row 6: Downstream tracking/exchange/communication study

Observable trigger for **measurement priority only**: upstream states show low/precisely bounded `B_A` and `B_P`, high `W_P`, and no broad actionability/exception uncertainty, yet the inherited same-system appendix shows many no-timely-CT or no-later-trace states. Because outside care is unobserved, this does not establish a tracking failure.

Adverse to an upstream text-only priority: upstream availability and eligible propagation burdens are precisely low. Inconclusive for downstream intervention: always, unless prospective or linked data establish order placement, scheduling, transmission, patient receipt, outside-system imaging, completion, and clinical outcome.

Decision meaning: MIMIC may redirect the next data collection downstream; it cannot decide that a navigator, registry, patient reminder, order intervention, or communication intervention is warranted.

## Analysis, uncertainty, and falsification

Use dual independent review with third-review adjudication for pulmonary eligibility, `K`, and `W`, reporting pre-consensus confusion matrices and raw agreement/kappa separately for incidental status, operative component, interval, `K`, faithful propagation, omission/incompleteness, and `W_S/W_Q`. Eligibility reviewers remain blinded to discharge outcomes. Actionability reviewers remain blinded to discharge content/timing. Content reviewers remain blinded to clocks and downstream outcomes. Consensus never repairs a failed reliability gate.

Retain inherited feasibility gates: at least 200 pulmonary `N`; `U_R<=5%`; `U_D<=5%`; no duplicate canonical `DS`; at least 30 pulmonary `K_E,E` rows; at least 20 propagation failures for a directional content conclusion. Require `K_U<=10%` and `W_U+W_Q<=10%` for a propagation-versus-resolution decision; otherwise publish bounds and call the affected row inconclusive. Each independently interpreted gap/action family must meet its stated event/overlap gates.

Use a subject-cluster bootstrap with fixed reproducible seed and at least 10,000 replicates. Resample subjects with all rows; recompute standardization weights and deterministic matches within each replicate. One replicate carries every `K,V,H,W,Y` cell, unknown-compatible bound, `B_A`, `B_A^excess(b)`, `B_P`, `p_P`, `NNR_P`, `B_S`, `B_Q`, `G_exception`, `D_PA`, `D_PA^excess`, every family-specific absolute and comparative estimand, and downstream appendix state. Construct one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over all decision-facing burdens and contrasts. Pointwise intervals may be displayed but cannot drive labels.

Unknown handling has three layers: observed unknown as its own category; content bounds assigning `W_U/W_Q` in all logically compatible ways; and full membership bounds allowing unresolved `K`, `H`, `V`, or chain states into/out of `E` and failure under least-favorable assignments. Unknowns can prevent a decision but cannot manufacture support. Never report a complete-case rate without its all-`N` numerator and denominator.

The following falsification tests are mandatory:

1. **Source/key stop rule:** recompute all source hashes, schemas, complete row/key diagnostics, and canonical `DS` uniqueness. Changed source/schema, duplicate canonical key, impossible timestamp parse, or nonreciprocal chain used as canonical stops confirmatory work.
2. **Frozen denominator:** hash and row count of `N` must be identical in eligibility, `K`, availability, content, comparator, transition, and downstream outputs. Every `K,V,H,W,Y` combination must sum to `N`; family tables must sum to their frozen `N_Xf`.
3. **Clock identity:** verify `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` for every known row. Only `G` defines content opportunity.
4. **Stale leakage:** every `G<0` row remains outside propagation estimands, whether its text matches or not.
5. **Delayed leakage:** every `DS_store>D` row remains `Y=A` and outside propagation estimands; eventual faithful text cannot alter `B_A`, `B_P`, or departure state.
6. **Eligibility leakage:** changing discharge text/timing in synthetic cases cannot change `K`; changing `K` cannot change membership in frozen `N`.
7. **Supersession adversary:** a timely ample-gap artifact with a concrete coherent alternate plan must be `W_S`, not `W_O/W_I`; an ambiguous statement must be `W_Q`, not success. Forced-copy recommendations must be rejected by verifier interpretation.
8. **Uniform-high adversary:** equal 30% eligible propagation failure in every positive gap bin remains a positive absolute phenotype even with no gap gradient.
9. **Availability-versus-propagation adversary:** synthetic mutually exclusive burdens must select availability when the simultaneous interval for `D_PA` is below zero, propagation when above zero, and bundled/undecided when crossing zero. Code must not compare incompatible conditional denominators.
10. **Comparator heterogeneity adversary:** one high and one low adequate action family must yield scope-inconclusive; pooling them into an apparently pulmonary-like rate must not yield a generic label. Two independently resolved family gaps may support a generic pilot even if their rates differ.
11. **Calibration robustness:** compare full standardized `O` with `M`; a sign reversal or absolute difference at least 0.02 in availability contrast blocks scope/prioritization. Within each `X_f`, standardization-versus-matching sign reversal or absolute `Delta_f` difference at least 0.05 blocks that family.
12. **Radiology-lag control:** compare `R_store-charttime` with each adequate `X_f` and `I`, and stratify by `<6`, `6-24`, `>=24h`. Material reversal blocks specificity claims, not the all-pulmonary descriptive burden.
13. **Charttime placebo:** replace `R_store` with `charttime` only in labeled placebo output. Disagreement is a timestamp-semantics warning requiring prospective crosswalk; placebo cannot support a decision.
14. **Threshold stability:** repeat positive-gap cutoffs at 12 and 72 hours and report all locked bins. No sensitivity may add/remove `N`; a directional reversal makes the content-priority row inconclusive.
15. **Reviewer falsification:** leave one reviewer, interval, action family, and target family out. A conclusion driven by one reviewer or one semantically ambiguous family is inconclusive.
16. **Permutation software checks:** permute pulmonary/family labels within locked standardization cells and gap labels within pulmonary timely rows. Null-centered distributions test leakage/software only, not scientific null hypotheses.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive for a measurable eligible-propagation target** means `K`/`W` reliability and unknown gates pass, at least 20 ample-clock `W_O+W_I` events exist, `B_P` and `p_P` satisfy simultaneous precision requirements under least-favorable compatible bounds, and clock/reviewer sensitivities do not reverse direction. It supports quantifying a prospective reconciliation pilot’s workload; operational adoption remains conditional on an externally declared capacity and utility frontier.

**Supportive for availability priority over propagation** means the corresponding gates pass and the simultaneous upper bound of `D_PA` is below zero. **Supportive for propagation priority over availability** means its simultaneous lower bound is above zero. If the interval crosses zero while both burdens are resolved, the MIMIC-supported next design is bundled/factorial or utility-dependent, not a forced winner.

**Supportive for exception-aware reconciliation** means coherent or ambiguous alternate-resolution states are reproducibly present and precisely quantified. This is adverse to automatic copying, not adverse to a reconciliation workflow. A large `W_S` fraction weakens the claim that raw text discordance equals propagation failure.

**Supportive for nodule-targeted scope** and **supportive for generic cross-action scope** follow the familywise rules above. They are intentionally not complements. Both may fail because action families are sparse or mixed; both cannot be inferred from a pooled heterogeneous comparator.

**Adverse to an eligible-propagation explanation** includes a precise low `B_P` under an externally declared minimum addressable volume, a review burden beyond an externally declared maximum, a phenotype dominated by `K_U/W_S/W_Q`, or reversal after proper clock/actionability controls. **Adverse to automatic propagation** includes any verified coherent supersession and substantial actionability/exception uncertainty. **Adverse to nodule specificity** is familywise nonpositive pulmonary excess with adequate precision; it does not erase an absolute pulmonary or generic phenotype. **Adverse to upstream text-only priority** is precise low availability and propagation burden.

**Inconclusive** includes insufficient `N`, `K_E,E`, events, action families, overlap, or effective sample size; excessive `U_R/U_D/K_U/W_U/W_Q`; poor reviewer agreement; duplicate canonical `DS`; failed chain/timestamp/hash checks; extreme weights; calibration or comparator reversal; intervals too wide for the stated precision rules; unresolved external workload/utility thresholds; or failed adversarial tests. A statistically resolved burden with no local utility declaration is operationally undecided, not clinically negligible.

Negative findings remain consequential. If most apparent discordance is coherent supersession, the result falsifies a simple propagation intervention. If availability dominates, it redirects measurement toward artifact lifecycle. If pulmonary and family-specific propagation burdens are low, it argues against spending the next study on upstream text while still not proving downstream failure. If every axis is imprecise, the correct conclusion is that this snapshot cannot choose an intervention.

## Retained downstream observation appendix

For each frozen pulmonary opportunity, retain the parent’s same-system observation vector without using it to define `N`, `K`, or any upstream process denominator. Compute the exact calendar-month due date from the adjudicated 3/6/12-month recommendation plus a fixed 42-day window beginning at `D`, then classify: same-system timely chest CT/CTA; death before the window with no prior timely CT; later MIMIC admission/radiology trace without timely CT/death; no later trace; or indeterminate CT/death ordering. Use `max(D,DS_store)` only as a labeled artifact-timed sensitivity. Treat `patients.dod` as `[dod 00:00,dod+1 day)` unless `admissions.deathtime` is more precise.

Do not call no-trace or no-timely-same-system-CT nonadherence, failed tracking, or missed care. Outside imaging and communication are unobserved, and note release coverage does not establish continuous capture. The appendix can motivate prospective downstream measurement only; it cannot select a tracking intervention.

## Exactly what MIMIC can and cannot decide

MIMIC can computationally decide, subject to expert labels and uncertainty gates:

* how many frozen pulmonary opportunities occupy each `K,V,H,W,Y` state;
* whether an artifact was stored by `D`, whether storage occurred before/after recommendation storage, and how this compares with ordinary institutional latency;
* the all-opportunity burden and review yield of high-confidence observable eligible propagation gaps;
* how often available text documents coherent or ambiguous alternate resolution;
* whether unavailable and eligible-propagation states differ in absolute observable volume;
* whether the storage/text phenotype independently replicates across separately defined CT, MRI, and ultrasound action families;
* whether computed labels follow the decision matrix, bounds, precision, and falsification rules.

MIMIC cannot decide whether a report was visible, final, signed, acknowledged, or transmitted; whether a summary draft existed earlier; who authored/read it; whether the recommendation was guideline-appropriate given unavailable history/risk; whether an alternate plan was truly correct; whether an order was placed, scheduled, exchanged, or completed outside the system; whether a clinician or patient received or understood it; whether follow-up prevented harm; or whether an intervention works. Choosing/deploying a UI requires prospective event logs for report availability/finality/acknowledgment, artifact draft/version/signature/transmission, user interactions and overrides. Choosing a communication intervention requires sender/recipient/channel/receipt data. Choosing an order or registry intervention requires order, referral, scheduling, cancellation, completion, and outside-exchange linkage. Clinical correctness requires complete chart review and governance. Relative benefit, safety, alert burden, and patient outcomes require a prospective separable pilot or randomized/factorial trial.

## Verifier boundary and substantive advance

An automatic verifier can check source hashes and members, schemas, complete scans, exact joins/filters, first-subject selection, frozen-manifest order, reciprocal chains, canonical uniqueness, all timestamp arithmetic, mutually exclusive states, all-`N` numerators, unknown bounds, control construction, weights/effective sample size, bootstrap/simultaneous intervals, workload frontier, familywise scope rules, and whether conclusions follow the matrix. It must reject conclusions based on `D-R_store`, stale or post-discharge artifacts, complete-case denominators, `W_S` counted as failure, `K` used to shrink `N`, arbitrary pooled comparator rates, or an unrecorded utility threshold.

The verifier cannot establish clinical actionability, semantic fidelity, or coherent supersession without expert adjudication, and neither verifier nor adjudicator can establish the unavailable UI, communication, order, outside-care, causal, or benefit claims.

This successor preserves the parent’s exact source provenance, unsampled audit, frozen first-eligible all-opportunity denominator, recommendation-to-artifact clock, complete joint state, canonical unique `DS`, reciprocal-chain rules, stale/delayed exclusions, institutional controls, +24/+72/+168 calibration, downstream appendix, unknown bounds, and strict noncausal boundary. Its substantive advance is decision validity: it nests blinded MIMIC-observable actionability without changing `N`; separates eligible propagation failure from coherent and ambiguous supersession; expresses absolute burden as addressable events per 100 all opportunities and review yield/NNR; compares mutually exclusive availability and propagation target volumes; replaces a heterogeneous pooled comparator decision with family-specific replication/contrast rules; and makes downstream tracking a prospective measurement decision rather than a claim inferred from missing traces. The remaining unresolved quantities—local utility thresholds, true visibility/communication/order states, clinical correctness, and intervention effect—are explicitly assigned to prospective governance and trial data rather than hidden inside prevalence cutoffs.
