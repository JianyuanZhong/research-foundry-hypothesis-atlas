## Hypothesis to test

Proteins measured before illness that influence infection onset differ from those that influence organ damage after infection.

## Study population and main data

Participants without infection at baseline; proteomics and hospital diagnoses.

## Suggested approach

Analyze hospitalization for infection and infection accompanied by organ damage separately, and compare the effects of the same protein across these outcomes.

## Scientific significance

Distinguish potential targets for preventing infection from targets for preventing severe disease.

## Main limitations

Diagnostic codes may not support precise disease staging; disease tolerance cannot be measured directly.
