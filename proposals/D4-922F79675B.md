## Unresolved clinical question and advance

Cancer programs have a finite survivorship-review capacity, but post-cancer heart-failure risk differs by cancer site and calendar era. A pooled reserve score could appear useful simply because it selects sites with more heart failure, or because the distribution of survivors and treatment eras changes. The unresolved question is:

> Among people with an incident cancer and no recorded heart failure at the cancer landmark, does a pre-cancer physiological-reserve panel add transportable value for a fixed-capacity review queue within cancer-site strata, and does that value persist when an entire supported site is held out of model fitting?

The primary falsifiable claim is that adding pre-cancer grip, creatinine-derived kidney function, pulse and systolic pressure to age/sex/cancer-site/cancer-year/landmark-timing controls improves held-out 10%-capacity capture of first recorded I50 events in a site-balanced evaluation, with death competing, and retains a directionally consistent increment in leave-one-supported-site-out testing. A pooled improvement without within-site or held-out-site improvement is adverse to the claimed transportable reserve hypothesis.

This is a substantive advance over candidate-790: it retains its actionable queue estimand but tests whether the queue is portable across cancer programs rather than only useful in the pooled UKB case mix. It does not claim that reserve causes heart failure, that referral improves outcomes, or that the score is ready for clinical deployment.

## Models, split and site-transport estimand

Use participant folds SHA256(UTF8(decimal(eid))) mod 5. The primary temporal evaluation fits cancer landmarks 2010-2015 and applies with no refit to 2016-2018. All imputation, standardization, spline knots, thresholds, weights and site eligibility decisions are training-fold operations.

Mbase is a transparent discrete-time complementary-log-log competing-hazard model with separate annual I50 and death hazards using age at T0, sex, complete prespecified cancer-site group, cancer year and TC-T0. Mlinear adds the four reserve inputs linearly. Mnonlinear uses the identical cohort, split, controls and inputs, replacing the four reserve terms by degree-3 restricted cubic spline bases plus prespecified grip×eGFRcr and pulse×systolic interactions. Mlinear is the transparent scientific test; Mnonlinear is a substantive matched learned alternative that can reveal threshold or cross-system coupling lost by linear terms.

Primary evaluation: within each supported cancer-site stratum in the late held-out test, rank people by competing-risk five-year I50 CIF from Mbase and Mlinear, select the top 10% of that stratum, and combine strata into one site-balanced queue with equal prespecified weight per supported stratum. Report both site-balanced capture/CIF contrasts and the operational pooled top-10% queue from candidate-790. The primary contrast is Mlinear minus Mbase in site-balanced 10% event capture and selected-versus-unselected CIF; the pooled queue is a secondary operational estimand, not allowed to substitute for site-balanced evidence.

Transport test: for each supported site group with adequate training events, fit on all other supported groups in the early period and apply without refit to that held-out group in the late period. Do not fit a separate model within the held-out site. Require overlap diagnostics for predictors and predicted risk; if a site has inadequate events or non-overlap, its transport result is inconclusive. A prespecified pooled model with site indicators is retained as a sensitivity, not evidence of within-site transport by itself.

Report integrated Brier score, five-year calibration intercept/slope, CIF, top-10% capture, selected-versus-unselected CIF, site-specific contrasts, leave-one-site-out contrasts and paired list changes. Use 1,000 participant-level bootstrap replicates for the primary contrasts and 95% intervals; resample participants, never person-years. Also report fold-to-fold, site-to-site and early/late variability.

## Falsification and interpretation

Require source/header/key, field-semantics, unit, date/code-pairing, event-support and observation gates before clinical interpretation. Support requires at least 100 people in the overall primary selected list, at least 50 qualifying I50 events overall, at least 10 events in selected and unselected groups, a 95% interval excluding zero for Mlinear versus Mbase in the site-balanced primary contrast, the same direction in the no-refit late test or prespecified cross-fold check, and no single supported site contributing all of the contrast. Transport support additionally requires at least two supported site groups with stable leave-one-site-out direction, overlap/ESS pass, and no material calibration harm. Mnonlinear support requires reproducible late-test improvement over Mlinear plus a stable prespecified threshold or interaction surface; a list change without better held-out concentration is not a scientific gain.

Adverse evidence is null/reversed reserve concentration, worse calibration, loss of effect under site-balanced evaluation, failure in every held-out site, loss under no-refit transport, or survival after breaking reserve-outcome linkage. A pooled improvement that disappears within sites is adverse to the transportable-reserve claim. Inconclusive evidence is inadequate events, unsupported cancer strata, failed observation completeness, non-overlap, unstable intervals, malformed pairs, unresolved units/labels, contradictory recorded-event and prospective modes, or only one usable site. Inconclusive neither supports nor refutes reserve.

Falsification audits: permute I50 dates within site/year/fold strata; permute each reserve input within age-band/sex/site/year strata; randomize capacity labels; shift one position within cancer and diagnosis code/date pairs and require an integrity failure; omit site controls; complete-case versus fold-local imputation; permute landmark year and early/late labels; exclude first 90 days after TC; and repeat with a negative-control non-cardiac diagnosis endpoint chosen before fitting. A reserve finding that survives linkage-breaking permutations is falsified as attribution.

Supportive evidence establishes only conditional association with recorded inpatient I50 events in this UKB export and, if gates pass, site-transportable ranking under this split. It does not establish biological reserve, heart-failure adjudication, causality, referral benefit, treatment selection, cost-effectiveness or external validity. Those require phenotype/stage/treatment/outpatient data, expert adjudication and prospective impact research. Automatic verification can check computation, bindings, splits, reported outputs and whether conclusions follow their intervals; it cannot establish those clinical claims.

## Baseline, alternative and deferred methods

The baseline is Mbase because it measures the information available from age, sex, cancer site, cancer year and landmark timing and prevents site mix from being mistaken for reserve value. Mlinear is the smallest interpretable reserve addition. Mnonlinear uses the same inputs, people, folds, hazards and capacity; it can reveal nonlinear thresholds and physiological coupling that Mlinear averages away, while preserving clinical comparability. A boosted tree is deferred because flexibility without an interpretable site-transport gain would not answer a more consequential question. A disease-sequence transformer is deferred because the local outcome arrays are sparse code/date slots, not a certified dated sequence; the demonstrated method is used only as methodological motivation. ALADYNOULLI-style latent/GP modeling is deferred because validated organ-age labels, specimen timing and genetics are unavailable. Oncoformer full multimodal reproduction is impossible because raw chest X-rays and the main article/complete STAR Methods are unavailable; no such claim is made. UKB-10 high-genetic-risk protection is deferred because genomics is derived metadata without a validated disease-specific PRS and protective factors/outcomes need a separate prespecified audit. UKB-06 infection/CV is deferred because protein identities/units/timing and infection setting/severity are uncertified.
