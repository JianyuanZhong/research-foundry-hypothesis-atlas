# Finite-ledger robust nested-content experiment for fixed-capacity documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Clinical decision, evidence boundary, and assigned synthesis

This proposal asks a narrow retrospective question: among adults in a source-defined first eligible hepatocellular-carcinoma (HCC) resection frame, can meanings adjudicated from eventual stored preoperative CT/MRI report text improve the capture of encounter-documentary M2 cases within exactly the same fixed number of ranking positions, beyond all of the same nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface information?

The downstream decision is whether a result is strong enough to justify obtaining real report-version/access logs and conducting a prospective silent-mode study of scarce multidisciplinary-review capacity. This experiment is not a triage, referral, resection, transplant, surveillance, or treatment rule.

This child combines two assessed-valid parents under a strict boundary. Candidate `e196aaf577a41ca99fe2ab0d` supplies the clinical question, population, clocks, nested information sets `R(Z,S)` and `G(Z,S,X)`, same-fit `Gmask`, fixed-K documentary-M2 capture, separate 2020 and 2021 tests, exact HCC bindings, and mutually exclusive conclusions. Candidate `3b864dc1ac8b6155af6e08dc` supplies only finite patient-state construction, chronology-safe superframes, normalized worst-world fitting, Dinkelbach/cutting-plane separation, sharp state-coupled test optimization, and solver certification.

The synthesis makes four consequential repairs:

1. It replaces undefined fitting “within every complete global state” with one robustly fitted model per information set over a frozen finite patient-state ledger.
2. It replaces an unenumerated family of state coordinates with four identified targets—two co-primary contrasts in each of two test years—each represented by a sharp lower and upper state-coupled extremum.
3. It bootstraps those extrema themselves, not a latent collection of global-world coordinates.
4. Any required state-generation, fitting, extremum, bootstrap, permutation, or certification failure forces the affected confirmatory result to `inconclusive`.

No claim is made about real-time report availability, biological M2, treatment benefit, or transportability.

## 2. What is known, what is untested, and why it matters

### Strongest claim supported before this experiment

The configured HCC snapshot contains patient- and encounter-linkable rows; procedure labels with source and start/end fields; examination accession identifiers, acquisition-like starts, machine labels, and eventual narrative findings/diagnosis fields; timed medication and non-drug-order rows; and untimed same-encounter pathology narratives. These fields can support a future-defined source cohort, a finite set of defensible source/clock/report alternatives, and an encounter-documentary M2 endpoint.

They do not establish that a procedure entry was completed; that two source rows represent the same operation; that pathology came from the selected resection specimen; that M2 wording is biological high-grade microvascular invasion under a specimen-linked sampling standard; or that an eventual examination narrative was authored, final, released, unchanged, or viewed before a clinical decision. The snapshot has no report-version/access log, pathology time, specimen/accession/slide/block identifier, pathology sampling record, source images, multidisciplinary-review roster or action, recurrence, survival, treatment response, harm, cost, equity outcome, or clinical benefit.

Feng, Qu, and Han’s 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis included 52 imaging deep-learning studies and 19,531 patients, reported lower summary performance under independent external than internal validation (SROC 0.85 versus 0.90), and emphasized heterogeneity, scarce independent validation, and the need for prospective multicenter evaluation (J Med Internet Res 2026;28:e82000; DOI `10.2196/82000`; PMCID `PMC12954728`). For this synthesis, the frozen Europe PMC full-text XML was inspected (source ID `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). It motivates independent temporal testing and restrained interpretation; it does not validate report-derived concepts, M2 grading, this cohort, or a treatment effect.

The research-ambition README was inspected. It records complete frozen article/supplement availability for the natural-history and Bayesian demonstrations, but only metadata and a supplement—not the main article or full STAR Methods—for the Cell cancer demonstration. No unavailable text is claimed as read, and none of those demonstrations dictates this topic or method.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

> Among adults in the finite-ledger first eligible source-documented HCC resection target, with same-encounter documentary HCC pathology and no recorded qualifying HCC treatment in the preceding year, does one locked full model using eventual stored CT/MRI report meanings capture more encounter-documentary M2 cases in exactly the same fixed top-K capacity than both (i) an independently optimized nested comparator containing identical nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface information and (ii) the same fitted full model after complete semantic masking, by more than 5 captures per 100 positions in every admissible state world, separately in 2020 and separately in 2021?

The margin of 5 captures per 100 fixed positions is a prespecified ranking-composition threshold. It is not sensitivity, PPV, treatment effect, utility, workload acceptability, or patient benefit.

### Substantive advance

A supportive result would isolate stored-text meaning more sharply than “content beats acquisition variables.” `R(Z,S)` allows all nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface signal to compete fairly; `G-Gmask` asks whether the already fitted full score actually relies on meaning without the confounding effect of refitting. Robust fitting and sharp test extrema prevent a favorable source reconstruction from being selected after the fact. Separate 2020 and 2021 targets prevent year pooling or compensation. The advance is therefore evidence about robust retrospective fixed-capacity capture by adjudicated eventual report meaning, not deployment readiness.

## 3. Population, analytical clocks, and finite state ledger

### 3.1 Population rule

For cutoff offset `d`, include one row per patient only in a state satisfying all of:

- age at least 18 in the selected linked encounter;
- the patient’s earliest eligible source-documented procedure episode under a frozen hepatobiliary-surgery dictionary;
- at least one same-encounter pathology composite explicitly supporting HCC after blinded pathology adjudication;
- no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[c_d-365d,c_d)`, searched patient-wide in dated procedure, medication, and non-drug-order rows.

The primary offset is `d=24` hours, with locked `d in {12,48,72}` hour sensitivities. For an operative clock `t_op`, `c_d=t_op-d`. This is an analytical cutoff, not a report-release, clinician-view, referral, or clinical-decision time.

Before outcomes are inspected, two hepatobiliary surgeons independently classify every distinct procedure/order/medication label touched by the rule as include, exclude, qualifying prior treatment, or unresolved; a third resolves disagreement. The signed dictionary retains normalized and raw labels, source table/column, counts, decisions, versions, adjudicators, and hash. Unresolved labels create explicit alternatives or exclusion; they never become silent negatives. “No recorded prior treatment” is not biological treatment-naivety.

### 3.2 Episode and clock reconstruction

Exact-deduplicate procedure rows across all six fields while retaining source path and raw ordinal. Construct same-patient, same-encounter, same-calendar-day provisional episodes. A blinded linkage review enumerates defensible alternatives for adjacent-day rows, conflicting source/label patterns, invalid durations, and multiple qualifying dates. Rows may be collapsed only by a clinically justified rule or source-system operation identifier; absent evidence preserves alternatives.

A validated non-midnight anesthesia-system start may be a point clock. A case-record date, date-only string, or unvalidated midnight is the interval `[date,date+24h)`. Missing, discordant, and competing values remain alternatives. For each candidate interval, partition at every point where an examination accession enters or leaves `[c_d-90d,c_d)`, a prior-treatment row enters or leaves `[c_d-365d,c_d)`, or calendar-role/year membership changes. One representative plus required open/closed boundary limits is evaluated per cell. Because all eligibility and feature predicates are constant within a cell, this produces a finite exact clock representation rather than arbitrary time sampling.

Each operative reconstruction orders all admissible episodes across all available years before selecting the earliest eligible episode. A listed procedure is evidence of a source entry only—not anesthesia, completion, resection, or absence of cancellation.

### 3.3 Frozen patient-state ledger

For patient `i`, emit a finite set `S_i`. Each ledger row is

`s=(e,k, role/year memberships, prior, J_CT, J_MRI, Z, S, X, Y, provenance)`,

where `e` is one admissible episode reconstruction; `k` is one breakpoint-defined clock cell; role/year memberships include development, 2019, 2020, 2021, outside, or nonmember; `prior` is recorded prior-treatment status; `J_CT,J_MRI` are selected whole examination units; `Z,S,X` are the resulting feature blocks; `Y` is the set-valued documentary endpoint completion; and provenance contains every originating row backpointer, rule, breakpoint, adjudication artifact, and reason code.

State generation is relational, not Cartesian. A state’s encounter determines its age, index pathology, procedure, clock, eligible examinations, prior-treatment search, features, outcome, and year together. No operation from one reconstruction may be combined with an examination, pathology outcome, annotation, or treatment status from another. Clinician-adjudicated impossible combinations are removed with signed reasons; unresolved combinations remain. Nonmembership is an allowed state, not `Y=0`.

The ledger, generator version, all row hashes, state counts, exclusions, and a canonical serialization hash are frozen before model fitting. No maximum-state truncation, random state sampling, marginal maximization, or dropping “hard” patients is allowed. If a patient’s admissible states cannot be exhaustively emitted or exactly represented by verified constraints, the confirmatory computation is inconclusive.

## 4. Chronology-safe superframes and temporal lock

Define the possible-test superframe

`U_T={i: exists s in S_i with eligible_2020(i,s)=1 or eligible_2021(i,s)=1}`.

Every patient in `U_T` is excluded from all fitting and tuning, even if another state places that patient in 2015–2019 or outside analysis. Among remaining patients, define `U_V` by the existence of an eligible 2019 state. Among those remaining, define `U_D` by the existence of an eligible 2015–2018 state. Earlier, later, and never-eligible states are extraction audits. This priority assignment is a chronology safeguard, not a claim that the latest possible state is true.

Development is 2015–2018. The 2019 superframe is used only for hyperparameter selection, preprocessing lock checks, and descriptive calibration. The two locked test targets are separate:

- `P_2020(q)={i in U_T: eligible_2020(i,s_i)=1}`;
- `P_2021(q)={i in U_T: eligible_2021(i,s_i)=1}`.

A test world `q=(s_i:i in U_T)` chooses one state per patient. A patient ambiguous between 2020 and 2021 remains in one ledger and can be a member of only the year implied by the chosen state. The annual estimands are never pooled, averaged, or allowed to compensate for each other. Rows from 2022 onward are ascertainment/drift audits only and cannot alter preprocessing, models, K, annotations, thresholds, or conclusions.

Report `|U_D|,|U_V|,|U_T|`; certainly/possibly eligible counts by year; sharp minimum/maximum annual membership; and counts whose index encounter, year, prior-treatment status, report unit, or outcome varies.

## 6. Models: one robust fit per information set

Use additive elastic-net logistic regression with an unpenalized intercept, frozen one-hot reference levels, development centering/scaling, and logged removal of zero-variance development features. No interactions, splines, unrestricted text, or test-derived features are permitted. The grid is `alpha in {0,.25,.5,.75,1}` and 100 log-spaced `lambda` values from development `lambda_max` to `10^-4 lambda_max`. Ties prefer larger penalty, fewer nonzero coefficients, smaller alpha, then lexical parameter serialization.

Fit exactly one locked model for each information set:

- `B(Z)`: acquisition-only diagnostic baseline;
- `R(Z,S)`: independently optimized reduced comparator;
- `G(Z,S,X)`: full model, with byte-identical `Z,S` preprocessing and order to `R`;
- `Gmask`: no fit and no recalibration. Apply the fitted `G` after replacing every `X` level—including not-mentioned and uncertain—with the training-defined source-text-unavailable level, leaving every `Z,S` value unchanged.

For model `M`, parameter vector `beta`, hyperparameter `h`, development world `q=(s_i:i in U_D)`, membership `a_i^D(s_i)`, and logistic loss `ell`, define

`L_M(beta,q)=sum_i a_i^D(s_i) ell(Y_i(s_i), beta; x_i^M(s_i)) / sum_i a_i^D(s_i)`.

Set-valued outcomes are represented as explicit ledger completions coupled to their source state. For each `h`, fit

`beta_hat_M(h)=argmin_beta [ max_{q: n_D(q)>0} L_M(beta,q) + lambda P_alpha(beta) ]`.

Thus there is one minimax fit for `B`, one for `R`, and one for `G` per candidate hyperparameter—not one model per global state. `R` and `G` choose hyperparameters independently. Select each model’s hyperparameters on 2019 by maximizing its sharp worst-state fixed-K documentary capture, using no 2020/2021 outcomes, with the prespecified tie order. Descriptive 2019 Platt calibration may be reported but cannot alter rankings or rescue a fixed-K result.

### Exact Dinkelbach separation and cutting-plane fitting

For fixed `beta`, state-specific losses are constants. To separate the worst normalized world, solve the finite linear-fractional problem over one-hot state variables `x_is`:

`max_x [sum_is x_is a_is ell_is(beta)]/[sum_is x_is a_is]`,
subject to `sum_s x_is=1` for every patient and `sum_is x_is a_is>=1`.

Dinkelbach iteration at value `c` solves the exact binary subproblem

`max_x sum_is x_is a_is[ell_is(beta)-c]`

with the same one-state-per-patient and positive-denominator constraints. The subproblem is solved to proven integer optimality; update `c` to the attained ratio until the residual is at most `1e-10`.

The outer convex epigraph master minimizes `t+lambda P_alpha(beta)` subject to `L_M(beta,q_r)<=t` for every separated world `q_r). Alternate globally solved convex masters and exact separation until the largest omitted-world violation is at most `1e-8`, the master primal-dual gap is at most `1e-8`, and two consecutive canonical separations add no world. Store every cut, objective, residual, KKT/primal-dual certificate, state hash, and solver/version/seed. Warm starts may accelerate computation but cannot remove certification. Approximate state sampling or a local/noncertified separation cannot support a primary result.

## 7. Fixed-K annual estimands and sharp state-coupled extrema

Before test outcome or semantic annotation is consulted, compute the sharp minimum eligible count `N_a(q,d)` for every development/tuning calendar year `a=2015,...,2019` and offset `d in {12,24,48,72}` from the structural ledger, then freeze

`N_ref=min_{a,d,q} N_a(q,d)`, and `K_q=floor(q N_ref)` for `q in {.05,.10,.20}`.

The confirmatory capacity is `q=.10`. Require `N_ref>=500`, `K_.10>=50`, and at least `K_.10` eligible members in every admissible 2020 and 2021 world. The .05 and .20 capacities are sensitivities only where `K_q>=50`; they cannot redefine or rescue the primary K.

Within a chosen state world, rank each model by locked score, frozen SHA-256 patient tie key, and raw episode backpointer. Select exactly K whole patients. For model `M`, year `a`, and world `q`,

`T_M(a,q)=100/K * sum_{i in TopK_M(a,q)} Y_i(s_i)`.

The co-primary contrasts are

- `Delta_nested(a,q)=T_G(a,q)-T_R(a,q)`;
- `Delta_mask(a,q)=T_G(a,q)-T_Gmask(a,q)`.

The diagnostic surface contrast `Delta_surface=T_R-T_B` is reported separately and is never a semantic-support veto.

For each `a in {2020,2021}` and `c in {nested,mask}`, compute the four sharp identified intervals

`I_ca=[delta^-_ca,delta^+_ca]=[min_q Delta_c(a,q), max_q Delta_c(a,q)]`.

The same chosen patient state supplies membership, year, report units, `Z,S,X`, and `Y` to both models in a contrast. The models may rank different top-K sets, but no component may be borrowed across worlds.

Each extremum is solved as a state-expanded mixed-integer program. One-hot variables choose one state per patient; separate top-K variables choose exactly K eligible patients for each model; precomputed pairwise score/tie precedence indicators enforce that no lower-ranked selected patient can coexist with a higher-ranked unselected eligible patient; and state-by-selection products carry documentary outcomes. The state choices are shared across both arms of a contrast. Require proven integer optimality, zero unresolved integer gap (and reported numerical gap at most `1e-8`), deterministic equality after state-order permutation, and equality with exhaustive enumeration for every synthetic fixture through 12 patients and every real-data shard through 20 patients. Frozen score serialization fixes all comparisons. Infeasibility, timeout, omitted state, inconsistent top-K, or failed certificate makes that confirmatory family inconclusive.

This estimand is documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed ranking positions in an eventual-report corpus. It is not prevalence, recall, report availability, referral opportunity, clinical utility, or benefit.

## 8. Inference on four robust extrema

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates stratified by frozen superframe. A sampled patient copy carries its entire state ledger and provenance. Every replicate repeats robust `B,R,G` fitting, independent 2019 hyperparameter selection, no-refit `Gmask`, and all eight extremum solves: lower and upper endpoints for the four contrast-by-year targets. K and all dictionaries, rubrics, preprocessing definitions, tie rules, and original ledger-generation rules remain locked.

Inference is only on the four pairs `(delta^-_ca,delta^+_ca)`; no confidence family is constructed over unenumerated global worlds. From the centered bootstrap distribution, construct one max-absolute-studentized 95% simultaneous family over the eight endpoint parameters. Store `[L^-_ca,U^-_ca]` for each lower extremum and `[L^+_ca,U^+_ca]` for each upper extremum. This family permits separate, simultaneous statements that every world exceeds the margin (`L^->5`), at least one world exceeds it (`L^+>5`), at least one world is at or below it (`U^-<=5`), or even the best world is at or below it (`U^+<=5`). Studentization uses the bootstrap standard deviation for each endpoint; zero or undefined scale requires an exact finite-state bound or causes inconclusive status.

More than 2.5% failed replicates, any replicate that drops a ledger state or cannot select K, any nonfinite endpoint, `delta^- > delta^+`, any reversed endpoint interval, `L^- > U^+`, or any failed extremum certificate makes the primary result inconclusive. Sensitivity offsets/capacities use separate prespecified families and cannot rescue the primary.

Run 1,000 development/2019 whole-block semantic-null repetitions. Permute each patient’s complete `X` state collection—not rows or individual concepts—within frozen strata of chronology role, possible-period signature, modality-pattern support, state cardinality, identity ambiguity, and documentation-surface-score decile. Keep `Z,S`, outcomes, and source rows fixed; refit the entire robust `G` pipeline and recompute the four lower extrema. With
`H=min_ca(delta^-_ca-5)`,
the one-sided p-value is `(1+#(H_perm>=H_obs))/1001`. Universal support additionally requires `p<=.025`. This tests semantic alignment conditional on the design; it does not validate concepts against images.

## 9. Mutually exclusive annual and global conclusions

All required source, lineage, adjudication, sample/event, fitting, extremum, bootstrap, permutation-execution, and solver gates are evaluated first. Failure of any mandatory primary gate forces the global primary label to `inconclusive`, regardless of favorable partial values. Numerical fragments may be reported as failed-run diagnostics only.

For each robust coordinate `e=(year,contrast)`, derive two evidence flags from the same simultaneous endpoint family:

- `Pos_e` if `L^+_e>5`, certifying that at least one admissible world is above the margin;
- `Neg_e` if `U^-_e<=5`, certifying that at least one admissible world is at or below the margin.

Then assign exactly one coordinate label, in order:

1. `coordinate-supportive` if `L^-_e>5` (every admissible world is above the margin; this implies `Pos_e`);
2. `coordinate-heterogeneous` if `Pos_e` and `Neg_e` (certified worlds occur on both sides of the margin);
3. `coordinate-adverse-without-certified-positive-world` if `Neg_e` and not `Pos_e`;
4. `coordinate-inconclusive` otherwise.

Exact equality belongs to `Neg`, matching the strict hypothesis. The stronger condition `U^+_e<=5` is reported as `even-best-world-adverse`, but is not required to falsify the universal claim. Valid ordering of the two extrema and their simultaneous intervals makes the coordinate labels disjoint.

For each year, apply the same evidence partition across its two contrasts:

1. `year-supportive`: both coordinates are `coordinate-supportive`;
2. `year-heterogeneous`: at least one `Pos` and at least one `Neg` occur within that year, including within one coordinate;
3. `year-adverse`: at least one `Neg` and no `Pos` occur within that year;
4. `year-inconclusive`: otherwise.

After valid execution, assign exactly one global label:

1. **Universal-supportive:** all four coordinates are `coordinate-supportive` and the valid semantic-null test has `p<=.025`.
2. **Decisive-threshold-heterogeneity:** at least one `Pos` and at least one `Neg` occur anywhere across the four coordinates, including within a single coordinate.
3. **Universal-adverse-without-certified-positive-coordinate:** at least one `Neg` occurs and no `Pos` occurs.
4. **Inconclusive:** no `Neg` but not all coordinates are supportive; all four are supportive with valid `p>.025`; or any mandatory gate/computational failure.

A valid `p>.025` blocks universal semantic support but does not erase valid `Pos/Neg` heterogeneity or a `Neg`-only adverse result. A failed permutation execution is not a large p-value; it is computational failure and therefore inconclusive. The result artifact contains a one-hot four-element global label vector. Pooling years, averaging contrasts, choosing a favorable world, or allowing one contrast/year to compensate for another is forbidden.

A supportive `Delta_nested` coordinate means robust fixed-K incremental capture beyond the independently optimized identical-surface comparator. A supportive `Delta_mask` coordinate means the same fitted full score loses more than the margin when semantics are removed. Either alone is insufficient. A `Neg` flag falsifies the corresponding universal margin claim by certifying at least one admissible at-or-below-margin world; `U^+<=5` additionally shows that even the best world is adverse. Neither finding shows that biological MVI or the report concepts are false. Heterogeneity is with respect to the prespecified threshold, not proof of a biological interaction or calendar mechanism.

Supportive results justify the next audit/prospective study. Adverse results falsify the universal margin claim while preserving the possibility of smaller or scope-specific signal. Inconclusive results preserve uncertainty and cannot be called negative biological findings.

## 12. Required outputs, falsification, and verifier contract

The execution must publish:

- canonical patient-state ledger and provenance tables;
- dictionary/rubric/adjudication hashes and disagreement/completeness reports;
- superframe membership and sharp annual membership ranges;
- `N_ref`, K, event/annotation gates, and actual filter counts at every stage;

- permutation statistic/p-value; diagnostic/sensitivity/access results in separate families;
- annual labels and exactly one global label, each linked to exact computed outputs.

The hypothesis is falsified at the prespecified margin if any `Neg` flag is certified. Coexistence of any `Pos` and any `Neg` is decisive threshold heterogeneity and also falsifies the universal conjunction. A favorable point estimate with `L^-` at or below 5 is not support. A favorable identifiable subset cannot rescue adverse or inconclusive all-ledger extrema.

Automatic verification can check source hashes/headers; exact deduplication and joins; row-backpointer retention; breakpoint completeness; state coherence and one-state-per-patient constraints; superframe quarantine; feature lineage and byte-identical shared `Z,S`; independent `R/G` optimization; no-refit `Gmask`; Dinkelbach residuals and cutting-plane gaps; fixed K and deterministic ties; exact top-K/extremum arithmetic; brute-force fixtures; bootstrap/permutation reconstruction; simultaneous bounds; one-hot labels; and whether every conclusion follows from computed gates and endpoints.

The verifier must accept valid adverse and inconclusive interpretations rather than reward hypothesis confirmation.

The verifier cannot establish clinical procedure identity, completeness or plausibility of expert-generated states, Chinese semantic correctness, reviewer qualifications, pathology specimen linkage or sampling, actual report finalization/view, threshold/workload acceptability, clinician response, treatment effect, benefit, fairness, or transportability. Reference execution proves computational feasibility and conclusion linkage only, not scientific truth.

## 13. What supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results permit

The strongest permitted supportive statement is:

> In this frozen retrospective HCC eventual-report corpus, adjudicated stored-report meanings added more than the prespecified fixed-K documentary-M2 capture margin beyond the same acquisition/documentation surface, and the same fitted full score lost more than that margin under semantic masking, robust to all declared patient states separately in 2020 and 2021.

It does not say the report was available in real time, the procedure occurred as recorded, M2 was biological truth, clinicians would act, treatment would help, or the result transports.

An adverse result says the universal >5 fixed-capacity margin failed, potentially even in the most favorable admissible world for a named year/contrast. It does not prove semantic concepts are useless, pathology M2 is absent, or smaller effects do not exist.

An inconclusive result means identification, sampling uncertainty, event/annotation sufficiency, or computation did not separate the extrema from the margin. It is not a negative finding.

Testing biological M2 requires specimen-linked pathology, timestamps, slides/blocks, and sampling adjudication. Testing real-time availability requires report author/final/release/version/view logs joined to the selected accession and validated operation clocks. Operational validity requires prospective silent-mode review with a real candidate stream, capacity, clinician actions, and subgroup monitoring. Treatment, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, and patient benefit require a separately designed controlled outcomes study. Transportability requires external sites and new locked evaluation.
