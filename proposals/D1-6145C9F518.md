# Which pulmonary-nodule handoff intervention is actually indicated? Recommendation eligibility, clinical actionability, superseding plans, and propagation failure

## Decision question and falsifiable advance

A high rate of discharge-text discordance does not by itself identify an intervention. An eligible radiology recommendation can be clinically inactive at departure, explicitly replaced by a better plan, incompletely represented in a way that needs clinician reconciliation, or simply omitted despite remaining active and being suitable for structured propagation. These states imply different prospective actions. A semantically broad nonthoracic-imaging comparator can calibrate a process phenotype, but cannot decide whether pulmonary recommendations should be auto-propagated, reconciled by a clinician, or left alone.

This successor preserves the assessed parent's frozen pulmonary opportunity denominator, corrected `G = DS_store - R_store` content clock, complete departure and joint states, exact MIMIC bindings, unsampled audit, calibration controls, and strict noncausal limits. It adds a blinded, two-panel clinical adjudication and an explicit superseding-plan hierarchy before assigning an intervention-relevant phenotype.

The strongest claim already supported is limited: MIMIC contains a feasible, completely auditable storage-and-text experiment; the inherited full-source scan found 331,794 unique canonical `DS` admission keys, only 17 missing discharge `storetime` values, substantial ordinary discharge storage latency, and 3,764 retrieval-proxy pulmonary reports. Those proxy reports are not adjudicated opportunities, and no content prevalence or intervention effect has been computed.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among the frozen pulmonary-nodule opportunities with a canonical discharge artifact stored by departure at least 24 hours after the operative recommendation component was stored, a clinically important subset remains recommendation-level action-eligible at departure, has no valid superseding plan, and nevertheless lacks the active pulmonary action in the canonical artifact. Within that subset, pure omission is sufficiently enriched for a low-discretion structured propagation intervention, whereas interval/action conflict or uncertain supersession is enriched for clinician reconciliation rather than automation.

This is tested as an intervention-mapping hypothesis, not merely a prevalence threshold. It is refuted for structured propagation if blinded experts cannot reliably separate active recommendations from inactive/unknown recommendations and valid superseding plans, if the eligible trigger has inadequate positive predictive value under conservative uncertainty bounds, or if propagation addresses no meaningful absolute burden compared with a review-all strategy. It is refuted for a reconciliation-specific phenotype if conflict/uncertain-resolution cases are not measurably enriched among cases sent to review. Negative or inconclusive results remain useful: they can redirect the next study to artifact availability, better clinical context capture, downstream tracking, or communication.

## New blinded adjudication: eligibility is not actionability

### Stage 1: recommendation eligibility

The frozen `N` establishes that an unconditional recommendation was textually eligible in radiology. It does not establish that the recommendation was clinically appropriate or still active at departure. Preserve that distinction in every output.

### Stage 2A: context-level actionability, blinded to propagation outcome

An independent panel of at least two board-certified physicians with hospital-discharge experience receives: the operative radiology chain and abstraction; admission/disposition variables; and a derived copy of the canonical `DS` in which all pulmonary-nodule, follow-up-imaging, interval/date, named-specialist, and target-specific plan spans have been redacted by a separate span team. Review packets exclude `R_store`, `DS_store`, `G/H/V`, downstream traces, comparator labels, and the unredacted propagation outcome. The span team follows a locked dictionary plus manual review, records every removed character offset, and does not assign actionability. A 10% random packet audit by a third redactor must find neither residual target-plan leakage nor removal of non-target goals-of-care/context language above a 2% packet-error margin; otherwise actionability conclusions stop.

The clinical panel answers only whether, absent a target-specific resolution, an explicit pulmonary follow-up disposition action would ordinarily remain warranted in the canonical handoff given the context actually observable in this packet:

* `K_A`: action-eligible; no visible context makes an explicit disposition action inapplicable;
* `K_N`: action-ineligible for an explicit discharge follow-up action because a clear non-target-specific context applies (for example, death before departure, a documented global comfort-only decision, or a clearly documented inability to pursue any follow-up). Because in-hospital death and locked hospice/comfort transitions are already denominator exclusions, `K_N` should be rare and triggers an eligibility-rule audit;
* `K_U`: actionability cannot be judged from available MIMIC context, including missing facts needed to assess competing illness, outside plans, informed preferences, or recommendation appropriateness.

`K_A` means recommendation-level handoff actionability under available evidence, not guideline appropriateness or clinical necessity. Reviewers record `definite/probable/uncertain` confidence and which missing fact could change the answer. Consensus cannot coerce `K_U` into `K_A` or `K_N`.

### Stage 2B: explicit superseding-plan adjudication

A separate physician panel receives the unredacted operative recommendation abstraction and canonical `DS` text, but no timestamps/gap bins, downstream events, Stage 2A labels, broad study hypothesis, or comparator results. It assigns exactly one superseding state:

* `S_0`, no target-specific alternate resolution: no pulmonary plan, or only generic language such as “follow with PCP” without the nodule, action, and operative timing;
* `S_EQ`, valid equivalent or more definitive operational plan: the same pulmonary target is addressed by an action that is no later/less protective than the operative recommendation and includes an executable next step. Examples are already scheduled compatible chest CT, a concrete pulmonary/thoracic clinic takeover with timing and responsibility, or tissue/diagnostic management that explicitly replaces surveillance. Mere specialty mention or referral without an action/timing is not sufficient;
* `S_DEC`, explicit informed non-pursuit: target-linked patient refusal/preference or target-linked goals-of-care decision documents that surveillance will not be pursued;
* `S_CON`, conflicting or less protective alternative: the target is recognized but the action, modality, or timing conflicts with the operative recommendation without an explicit rationale adequate to establish replacement;
* `S_UN`, apparent alternate plan but its target linkage, timing, responsibility, active status, or adequacy cannot be resolved from the text.

`S_EQ` and `S_DEC` crosswalk to `R_text`/`Y=R`; `S_0` does not. `S_CON` is a reconciliation state, not a valid resolution. `S_UN` remains unknown and is bounded. A plan documented only in an artifact stored after `D` cannot supersede the departure state. A target-specific plan in a stale artifact can describe prior intent but cannot demonstrate incorporation of the operative component; stale rows stay outside `E_N`.

### Stage 2C: propagation phenotype and intervention lane

Only after both panels freeze labels, join `K`, `S`, `V/H/Z`, and `Y` into the complete all-`N` table `J_N^*=count(V,H,Z,K,S)/N`, including impossible-cell diagnostics, zero cells, reviewer confidence, and unknowns.

Within `E_N`, define mutually exclusive prospective lanes:

1. `P_AUTO` — structured-propagation candidate: `K_A`, `S_0`, and `C_O_text`. The operative target/action/interval is active under available evidence and wholly absent. A prospective tool could present or prepopulate the exact radiology payload, but final acceptance still requires a clinician.
2. `P_RECON` — clinician-reconciliation candidate: `K_A` and either `C_I_text` or `S_CON`; or any apparent target plan whose consistency cannot safely be resolved automatically. This lane requires side-by-side review, not silent auto-insertion.
3. `P_RES` — already resolved: `S_EQ` or `S_DEC`, regardless of literal verbatim concordance. These are not propagation failures.
4. `P_UN` — intervention eligibility unknown: `K_U`, `S_UN`, unresolved content, or disagreement/reliability failure. These cannot be counted as propagation failures or safe automation candidates.
5. `P_NONE` — action-ineligible under available context (`K_N`) with no contradictory evidence; report separately and audit denominator eligibility.

For rows outside `E_N`, preserve the same clinical labels descriptively but do not call them content-propagation opportunities. `V_D,H_stale` is stale chronology; `V_D,H_0_6/H_6_24` is limited observed storage opportunity; `V_24/V_72/V_168/V_late` is departure-artifact unavailability. Their appropriate prospective tests are timing/availability or real-time reconciliation designs, not evidence that an ample-opportunity author failed to propagate content.

## Primary clinically interpretable estimands

All estimands are reported with subject-clustered simultaneous 95% intervals and conservative unknown-compatible bounds. The principal scale is cases per 100 frozen opportunities, not only conditional prevalence.

1. **Absolute auto-propagation-addressable burden**

   `B_AUTO = 100 * n(E_N and P_AUTO) / N`.

   This is the number per 100 frozen pulmonary opportunities that a structured propagation candidate rule could address in the observed ample, timely storage setting. Also report its decomposition into the eligible opportunity fraction `n(E_N)/N` and lane yield `PPV_AUTO = n(P_AUTO)/n(E_N and triggerable C_O/C_I/S_CON/S_UN)` under the locked trigger implementation. The primary trigger is radiology-eligible recommendation plus timely positive `G>=24h` plus absence/incompleteness detected in the stored-by-departure artifact; because such a trigger is retrospective in MIMIC, its prospective implementation would require live report and artifact events.

2. **Absolute clinician-reconciliation burden**

   `B_RECON = 100 * n(E_N and P_RECON) / N`.

   Report the risk difference `RD_lane = [n(P_AUTO)-n(P_RECON)]/N` only as a workload composition contrast; it is not comparative treatment effectiveness.

3. **Resolution and uncertainty burdens**

   `B_RES = 100*n(E_N and P_RES)/N`, `B_UN = 100*n(E_N and P_UN)/N`, and `B_NONE = 100*n(E_N and P_NONE)/N`. These prevent a high raw `C_O+C_I` rate from being mistaken for an addressable propagation failure. Report the reclassification matrix from inherited `Z/Y` to lanes, especially the fractions of raw discordance reclassified as valid resolution, reconciliation, or unknown.

4. **Actionability-adjusted propagation risk**

   `p_PROP = n(P_AUTO or P_RECON)/n(E_N and K_A and S not in {S_EQ,S_DEC})`, retaining unknowns in lower/upper bounds. This measures observable propagation/reconciliation need among action-eligible unresolved recommendations; it is secondary to all-`N` burdens.

5. **Prospective trigger decision curve, not an arbitrary prevalence cutoff**

   Compare three rules for identifying `P_AUTO`: trigger none; trigger every classifiable `E_N` discordance; and the locked conservative `P_AUTO` rule. For threshold probabilities `t={0.80,0.90,0.95}`, report net benefit per 100 opportunities

   `NB_t = 100/N * [TP - FP * t/(1-t)]`,

   where a true positive is expert-adjudicated `P_AUTO` and a false positive is a triggered `P_RES`, `P_NONE`, `P_RECON`, or definitively non-auto case; `P_UN` is assigned as false positive in the conservative bound and true positive only in the upper bound. These high thresholds are prespecified because silently inserting an inactive, superseded, or conflicting surveillance plan is not a benign false alert. The proposal does not claim that any one threshold is a universal clinical utility ratio. A targeted propagation study is supported only if the conservative simultaneous lower bound for `NB_t` exceeds both trigger-none (zero) and trigger-all at at least `t=0.90`, and the `B_AUTO` lower bound exceeds 0. This expresses positive expected decision value and nonzero addressable burden without asserting a validated harm threshold. Report all three thresholds so governance can substitute its own utility ratio.

6. **Reconciliation enrichment**

   Define the locked review trigger as any `C_I_text`, `S_CON`, or `S_UN` among classifiable `E_N`. Estimate sensitivity, positive predictive value, and absolute review yield for `P_RECON`, with `P_UN` conservatively adverse. Reconciliation targeting is supported only if its conservative net benefit is positive over review-none and review-all at `t=0.50` and its simultaneous lower bound for enrichment over the auto lane,

   `Delta_RECON = P(P_RECON | review trigger) - P(P_RECON | auto trigger)`,

   is above zero. The 0.50 threshold states the clinically interpretable requirement that a majority of reviewed cases truly require interpretation rather than pure copying; it is a prospective workload choice, not a safety threshold.

No minimum count alone establishes clinical importance. Feasibility gates below govern whether these quantities can be interpreted; decision support comes from absolute burden plus trigger accuracy/net benefit and uncertainty.

The calibrated difference in actionability-adjusted propagation burden is

`Delta_PORT = P(P_AUTO or P_RECON | E_N, pulmonary) - P_std(P_AUTO or P_RECON | E_X, X_PORT)`.

This contrast can support a nodule-specific versus portable generic payload phenotype if overlap and precision pass, but it no longer chooses the intervention. Neither result proves equal clinical urgency or benefit.

## Falsification and adversarial checks

1. **Source/key stop rule:** recompute all five source hashes and schemas; scan complete sources; stop for changed hash/schema, duplicate canonical `DS`, nonreciprocal operative chain, impossible timestamp, or denominator-hash drift.
2. **Denominator invariance:** the same hashed `N` must underlie eligibility, chronology, content, adjudication, calibration, transition, and appendix outputs. Every `Y`, `J_N`, and `J_N^*` marginal sums to `N`.
3. **Clock identity:** check row-wise `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; only `G` defines `H/E_N`.
4. **Stale and delayed leakage:** no `G<0` or `DS_store>D` row can enter `P_AUTO/P_RECON` primary estimands. Replacing a delayed artifact with perfectly faithful text must not change `Y=A`, `q_N(0)`, or `E_N` membership.
5. **Supersession adversarial set:** synthetic examples must classify (a) “PCP follow-up” alone as `S_0`; (b) named pulmonary takeover without timing/action as `S_UN`, not resolution; (c) explicitly scheduled compatible CT as `S_EQ`; (d) target-linked informed refusal as `S_DEC`; and (e) unexplained later/incompatible interval as `S_CON`. Any silent conversion of `S_UN/S_CON` to resolved fails verification.
6. **Actionability blinding:** deliberately seed target/action phrases outside the redaction dictionary and measure leakage. Above the locked 2% packet-error margin, stop intervention mapping. `K` rates must not differ materially by randomized packet formatter/redactor; an absolute difference `>=0.05` or sign reversal in lane contrasts is a redaction warning.
7. **Outcome-blind label test:** timestamps/gap bins and downstream traces must be absent from all clinical review packets. File-level checksums and automated prohibited-field scans are required.
8. **Uniform-discordance test:** synthetic 30% discordance in all positive gap bins must remain an absolute phenotype; no near-versus-ample gradient is required.
9. **Resolution-mixture test:** if raw discordance is 30% but most cases are reproducibly `S_EQ/S_DEC` or `K_U`, code must not label 30% propagation failure. Conversely, pure `K_A,S_0,C_O` must map to `P_AUTO`.
10. **Lane-separation test:** a synthetic sample with pure omissions and no conflict must favor the auto lane and not manufacture reconciliation enrichment; conflict/uncertain-plan enrichment must favor review rather than automation.
11. **Decision-curve falsification:** targeted propagation is adverse if conservative net benefit is no better than trigger-none or trigger-all at `t=0.90`, even when raw discordance exceeds 10%. Reconciliation targeting is adverse if its conservative net benefit is nonpositive at `t=0.50` or no enrichment over the auto trigger is demonstrated.
12. **Comparator portability:** broad `X` and `X_PORT` results are both shown. A material change (`>=0.05`) or sign reversal demonstrates semantic heterogeneity and prohibits the broad comparator from supporting specificity. Sparse `X_PORT` yields specificity-inconclusive, not pulmonary-null.
13. **Standardization/matching reversal:** a sign reversal or absolute difference `>=0.05` between standardized and matched `Delta_PORT`, extreme weight, or inadequate effective sample size blocks specificity.
14. **Institutional calibration:** compare full-standardized and matched availability contrasts. Difference `>=0.02`, sign reversal, empty support, or low effective sample size blocks availability prioritization.
15. **Radiology lag and charttime placebo:** repeat locked lag strata and labeled `charttime` placebo. Material disagreement warns that MIMIC timestamps cannot select a workflow mechanism; placebo cannot support one.
16. **Threshold stability:** display `G>=12h` and `G>=72h` sensitivities without changing frozen `N`. A lane conclusion requires directionally compatible 72-hour results; instability is inconclusive, not a post hoc threshold change.
17. **Reviewer leave-one-out:** remove each reviewer, confidence stratum, interval, and action subtype. A conclusion driven by one reviewer or unresolved category is inconclusive.
18. **POE non-leakage:** adding or removing the optional `poe/poe_detail` appendix cannot alter primary `K/S/Z/lane` labels. A POE row without validated target/due-date linkage never counts as a superseding outpatient order.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive for a structured-propagation study** requires reproducible `K/S/content` adjudication, acceptable unknown bounds, nonzero conservatively bounded `B_AUTO`, and conservative targeted-rule net benefit above both trigger-none and trigger-all at `t=0.90`. This supports prospectively testing a clinician-confirmed structured import of target/action/due interval. It does not support silent automation or claim that anyone ignored a report.

**Supportive for a clinician-reconciliation study** requires reproducible `P_RECON`, positive conservative review-trigger net benefit at `t=0.50`, and a simultaneous lower bound for `Delta_RECON>0`. This supports a prospective side-by-side discrepancy/alternative-plan review, not automatic replacement of clinician judgment.

**Supportive for a bundled content intervention** occurs when both lanes satisfy their criteria. Their outcomes must remain separable prospectively. **Supportive for availability/timing work** retains the inherited calibrated `Delta_A` rule and applies when delayed/stale/short-window states dominate rather than ample timely propagation lanes.

**Adverse to structured propagation** includes nonpositive decision value, predominantly valid superseding plans, inadequate trigger PPV, or negligible bounded addressable burden. **Adverse to reconciliation targeting** includes no enrichment over broad review or nonpositive decision value. These findings may favor downstream tracking/communication or no discharge-content intervention; they do not prove follow-up was completed.

**Inconclusive** includes inadequate events, wide simultaneous intervals, too many `K_U/S_UN/U_text`, poor reviewer reliability, redaction leakage, failed source/chain/key audit, clock leakage, threshold reversal, unstable lanes, or inability to construct a portable comparator. Pulmonary intervention mapping and nodule specificity are reported separately: comparator failure cannot erase a valid pulmonary result, and pulmonary mapping failure cannot be rescued by comparator prevalence.

## What MIMIC can decide, and what requires another study

MIMIC can decide, conditional on blinded expert labels, how many frozen opportunities have timely positive storage-to-storage opportunity; how their discharge text maps to faithful, omitted, conflicting, valid superseding, and unknown states; whether experts can reproducibly identify a recommendation-level action-eligible, non-superseded propagation phenotype from available evidence; the absolute workload per 100 opportunities; whether locked retrospective trigger rules have useful adjudication-based accuracy/net benefit; whether the phenotype resembles a portable analogous-action process; and whether artifact storage delay is institution-wide or relatively enriched.

MIMIC cannot establish recommendation guideline appropriateness, actual report visibility, draft/final/signature status, author awareness, UI exposure, alert receipt, who was accountable, patient/clinician communication, outside-system plans, outpatient order placement, scheduling, receipt, adherence, malignancy, harm, causal mechanism, intervention effect, or clinical benefit. Even expert adjudication cannot recover missing clinical context. A prospective study must collect versioned report finalization and viewing events; artifact creation/edit/sign/transmission timestamps; UI trigger and clinician accept/modify/dismiss actions with reasons; target-linked outpatient order/referral/scheduling data; responsible clinician/team; communication receipt; patient preference and competing-risk context; outside records; and follow-up outcomes. A separable pilot or randomized workflow study with clinical governance is required before deployment or benefit claims.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

This successor preserves the frozen first-opportunity denominator, reciprocal chain rules, unique canonical artifact, exact `F/C_O/C_I/R/A/U_D` departure state, corrected `G=DS_store-R_store` clock, complete `V x H x Z` state, stale/delayed exclusions, source hashes and joins, complete unsampled audit, ordinary/radiology/matched calibration, uncertainty bounds, and strict noncausal limits.

The substantive repair is decision validity. Raw discordance is no longer equated with propagation failure. Blinded context-actionability and separate superseding-plan panels classify active, resolved, conflicting, and unknowable recommendations; explicit rules prevent vague referral language from masquerading as resolution. The all-`N` absolute burdens and trigger decision curves distinguish low-discretion propagation from clinician reconciliation. The heterogeneous analogous comparator is narrowed through an outcome-blind payload-portability gate and is used only for specificity, never to select the intervention.

Uncertainty remains: redacted discharge context is an imperfect substitute for contemporaneous clinical knowledge; the same `DS` necessarily supplies the observable superseding plan; `G` is storage-to-storage rather than editing time; expert agreement is not ground truth; decision thresholds encode prospective workflow utilities that governance may value differently; and portable nonthoracic actions may still differ clinically. Therefore a supportive result identifies the most defensible next prospective intervention to test, not an intervention that is already warranted for deployment or known to improve care.
