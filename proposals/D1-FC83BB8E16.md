# Pre-repeat deterioration and immediate post-repeat biochemical state: a pooled, exact-clock trajectory association

## Decision and substantive advance

This child combines the exact pre-repeat state design in `[prior hypothesis]` with the locked three-panel inferential repair in `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves without revision the frozen common-TACE2 construction, 319 systemic-record and 1,491 TACE2-only pathways, first strict repeat-TACE events in 159 and 526 patients, assay-specific complete triplets of 40/118 for albumin and 41/119 for total bilirubin, and the strict common-timestamp triplets of 40/118. No outcome value or direction was used to choose these selectors.

The clinically clearer unresolved question is: **among patients selected for a first recorded repeat TACE and for whom all three required assay states are observed, does within-person deterioration from the exact pre-TACE2 baseline to the state strictly before repeat TACE predict the immediate strictly post-repeat biochemical state?** The primary association is pooled across pathways and adjusted for pathway. Pathway modification is secondary and gated by overlap, estimand-linked precision, multiplicity, and influence. “Vulnerability” is not the estimand: at most, a stable adverse association would identify a candidate *marker of biochemical vulnerability* for prospective study. It would not establish biological vulnerability, TACE toxicity, a systemic-treatment effect, treatment benefit, or clinical eligibility.

This is substantively stronger than the parents because it (1) centers the clinically consequential three-state question rather than a between-pathway pre-event contrast; (2) makes the better-supported arm-pooled trajectory coefficient primary rather than the sparse arm interaction; (3) adds an explicit baseline-only predictive benchmark; and (4) replaces most arbitrary pass/fail conventions with confidence-interval categories tied to a prespecified standardized magnitude, plus transparent overlap, effective-sample-size, influence, timing, and selection analyses. It does not claim that these diagnostics repair unmeasured post-baseline selection.

### Untested hypothesis

For each assay, among event- and assay-selected patients, a one-standard-deviation more adverse change from pre-TACE2 baseline to the strictly pre-repeat state is associated with a more adverse immediate post-repeat assay level after adjustment for the pre-TACE2 level, pathway, and locked timing/baseline covariates.

Albumin and bilirubin are analyzed separately, with adverse orientation defined before fitting: albumin deterioration is `B_albumin - P_albumin`, while bilirubin deterioration is `P_bilirubin - B_bilirubin`. A positive standardized trajectory coefficient therefore means that greater pre-repeat deterioration predicts a worse post-repeat state. The hypothesis concerns association and prediction in the selected observed cohort. It does not assert that the procedure caused the post value or that the early pathway caused either trajectory.

## Primary estimand, model, and baseline comparison

For each assay separately, standardize within its locked complete-triplet cohort:

- `X_a = D_a / SD(D_a)`;
- `O_albumin = −(Y_albumin−mean(Y_albumin))/SD(Y_albumin)`;
- `O_bilirubin = +(Y_bilirubin−mean(Y_bilirubin))/SD(Y_bilirubin)`;
- orient and standardize `B_a` in the same adverse direction as `O_a`.

The primary estimand is `betaD_a`, the population least-squares coefficient for `X_a` in the observed event/triplet cohort:

`O_a = beta0 + betaD_a X_a + betaB_a Bstd_a + betaA_a A + gamma'Z + error`,

where `A=1` for the systemic-record pathway. `Z` is limited, before outcome inspection, to age, sex, TACE1–TACE2 interval, TACE2 year, repeat-event day, baseline-to-pre-panel elapsed days, hours from pre panel to event, and hours from event to post panel. Continuous terms are centered and represented linearly in the primary fit; nonlinearity is checked with restricted cubic splines only as a sensitivity. Use one row per patient and HC3 robust 95% confidence intervals. Also report the equivalent raw-scale coefficient and arm-adjusted predicted post levels over the common observed range of `X_a`.

This parameterization is nonredundant: `B_a` and `D_a` determine the pre-repeat state, but `P_a` is not added separately. `betaD_a` describes persistence/prediction of the preceding within-person trajectory conditional on baseline; it is not an independent causal effect of “change.” The coupling-sensitive regression `(Y−P) ~ (P−B)` is secondary only and cannot override the post-level primary result.

The prespecified baseline comparator omits `X_a` but retains `Bstd_a`, arm, and `Z`. Compare it with the trajectory model using repeated five-fold cross-validation with folds fixed by a hash of `Patient Master Index`: report out-of-fold RMSE, mean absolute error, and cross-validated R-squared, with patient bootstrap 95% intervals for the incremental difference. This measures incremental prediction in the same selected cohort, not transportable clinical utility. Calibration plots are descriptive; no model or threshold is tuned.

Raw distributions and raw within-person changes at all three states remain required. Standardization permits assay-comparable magnitude statements but does not supply clinical units or a validated clinical threshold.

## Minimum interpretable magnitude and confidence-interval interpretation

Before fitting, define `delta=0.20` post-state SD per one trajectory SD as the minimum *study-scale* magnitude worth clinical adjudication. This is a conventional standardized benchmark, not a validated minimum clinically important difference. Unit validation and hepatology/interventional-radiology review are required before calling any estimate clinically important.

Report ordinary assay-specific 95% intervals and multiplicity-protected Bonferroni 97.5% intervals for the two co-primary assays (95% family-wise confidence). The following primary categories use the Bonferroni intervals; ordinary 95% intervals remain visible for estimation.

- A coefficient interval entirely above 0 supports an adverse-direction pooled association; an interval entirely above `+0.20` supports an association at least as large as the study-scale prioritization magnitude.
- An interval entirely below 0 is adverse to the directional hypothesis; an interval entirely below `−0.20` supports a meaningfully opposite association on the study scale.
- An interval wholly inside `[−0.20,+0.20]` is a precise study-scale null for that assay.
- An interval spanning both `−0.20` and `+0.20` is magnitude-inconclusive.
- Intervals excluding zero but not excluding `±0.20` support a directional association whose substantive magnitude remains uncertain.

Do not require arbitrary p-value/ESS/count conjunctions beyond estimability. Report both assays; a global “biochemical” statement requires directionally coherent Holm intervals in the strict common-timestamp cohort. Discordant assays are mixed, not averaged into an unvalidated composite.

## Secondary, gated pathway modification

Only after reporting the pooled model, add `X_a × A`. The interaction is the systemic-minus-comparator difference in standardized trajectory slope. Always report its estimate and interval as exploratory, but permit a pathway-modification statement only under all of these estimand-linked conditions:

1. arm-specific trajectory distributions and their 5th–95th percentiles are shown, predictions are restricted to their intersection, and the model matrix is full rank; lack of usable common support makes modification non-estimable rather than negative;
2. arm-specific and total effective sample sizes under each reported weighting scheme, leverage, DFBETAs, Cook distances, and leave-one-out estimates are shown; every leave-one-out refit must retain the same interval category relative to zero and `delta` for a stable claim;
3. the multiplicity-protected Bonferroni 97.5% interaction interval lies wholly above `+0.20` or wholly below `−0.20`; an interval wholly within `[−0.20,+0.20]` supports no study-scale meaningful modification, while all other intervals are inconclusive;
4. the conclusion category is unchanged in the strict common-timestamp cohort and under the locked timing selectors that remain estimable. Any opposite-sign alternative interval excluding zero falsifies timing-stable modification; wide alternatives indicate imprecision rather than contradiction.

No global pathway-modification claim is allowed unless albumin and bilirubin are directionally coherent in the adverse orientation. These gates use the estimand and its uncertainty rather than asserting that a particular raw count or ESS guarantees power.

## Locked result branches and falsification

- **Supportive and clinically prioritizable for adjudication:** reconstruction and clocks are exact; at least one multiplicity-protected primary interval is wholly above `+0.20`, the other assay is not precisely opposite, incremental out-of-fold prediction improves, and timing/influence/selection analyses retain the same category. Conclude only that pre-repeat deterioration marks a worse immediate post-repeat biochemical state in selected observed patients and merits prospective validation.
- **Supportive direction, uncertain magnitude:** a multiplicity-protected interval excludes zero above it but not `+0.20`. Report association without claiming a clinically important magnitude.
- **Adverse to the hypothesis:** a stable interval lies below zero; a value below `−0.20` is a study-scale meaningfully opposite association. Do not reinterpret it as protection or benefit.
- **Precise study-scale null:** both multiplicity-protected intervals lie within `[−0.20,+0.20]`, with adequate observed overlap and stable selectors/influence. Conclude that effects of that standardized size are not supported in this selected cohort, not biological equivalence, safety, or absence of vulnerability.
- **Mixed:** albumin and bilirubin point in clinically discordant adverse orientations. Report assays separately; do not pool them.
- **Selection-, timing-, or influence-sensitive:** interval categories differ across weighting, locked selectors, or deletion diagnostics. State exactly which selection or measurement choice governs the inference.
- **General serial trajectory:** the negative-control association is comparable. Retain only the bounded prediction statement and reject event-specific or procedural-response language.
- **Inconclusive:** intervals span both meaningful directions, overlap is weak, effective information is sparse, weights are concentrated, controls are unavailable, or assay/timing results are discordant.
- **Methodologically falsified:** frozen counts cannot be reproduced; pre/post clocks cross the event boundary; cross-timestamp pseudo-panels enter a common-timestamp analysis; event or panel selection uses values; or the model is rank-deficient. Stop inferential interpretation.

Pathway modification remains secondary. It may be reported as supported, precisely absent at `delta`, or inconclusive only according to its separate gated rules; it cannot replace an adverse or null pooled result.
