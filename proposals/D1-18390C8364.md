# RT Vent Off versus Suspended durability audit: recurrence and observation falsify a shared liberation-like interpretation

## Substantive child, clinical importance, and exact claim

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the inherited literal RT transition extraction, exact-record and 30-minute cluster deduplication, Start direction control, fixed-window setting-family definition, prior self-controlled charting result, shifted-sham/Continued controls, adverse airway/treatment/note corroboration, frozen-landmark non-overlap, and strict semantic limits. The substantive change is to stop pooling `Off` with `Suspended` and test whether either chart value marks a durable documentation state by measuring later literal `Start` recurrence over fixed 6-, 24-, and 48-hour horizons while separating death, live discharge, complete ICU observation, and truncation.

This matters because an explicit `Off` field could be tempting as an extubation or ventilator-liberation endpoint. Rapid reversal, persistent setting activity, discharge-dependent loss to observation, or materially different behavior for `Suspended` would make that use unsafe. The strongest inherited evidence is only that the >=2-setting-family marker changes in the expected direction around structured Start versus pooled Off/Suspended records: +81.4 percentage points after Start and -35.4 points after pooled Off/Suspended, much larger than shifted-sham behavior, while independent airwaytype, local treatment, and note evidence failed. The unresolved hypothesis tested here was narrower and falsifiable: literal `Off`, but not necessarily `Suspended`, should be followed by little charted Start recurrence through 48 hours, sustained low setting-family activity among fully observed episodes, and interpretable observation support. No event is called extubation, successful liberation, reintubation, or a bedside state.

## Population, temporal boundaries, and actual filtering

The source population is all patient-linked decisive RT records. The inherited deduplication is held fixed: exact transition tuples are collapsed, then sorted records in a stay are clustered beginning at the earliest record and including records through +30 minutes. A mixed Start/Off-direction cluster is a conflict. A cluster containing only literal Off or only literal Suspended defines its corresponding index group; the one mixed Off+Suspended cluster is excluded because its semantic label is ambiguous.

Actual unsampled filtering was:

- 4,120 raw and 4,120 exact-unique Start/Off/Suspended records;
- 4,094 30-minute clusters: 2,711 Start, 1,007 literal Off, 366 literal Suspended, 9 mixed-direction conflicts, and 1 mixed Off+Suspended cluster;
- require a patient row, nonmissing `unitdischargeoffset`, index time `T >= 360` to provide the complete inherited six-hour pre-window, and `T <= unitdischargeoffset`;
- retain 1,063 index episodes in 749 stays at 12 hospitals: 713 Off episodes in 662 stays at 12 hospitals and 350 Suspended episodes in 130 stays at 8 hospitals.

Crucially, a later Start is **not** excluded, because doing so would condition on the recurrence outcome. This differs appropriately from the parent's conflict-clean +/-6-hour self-controlled calibration, which remains preserved as a separate result. Sixty-four Off and 84 Suspended episodes had another decisive transition within +/-6 hours.

## Computable outcomes and estimands

For each literal index label and each horizon `h` in 360, 1,440, and 2,880 minutes, recurrence is the first later deduplicated literal Start cluster with `T < Start <= T+h` and before/on `unitdischargeoffset`. A charted Start is not called reintubation. Status precedence is:

1. `recurrent_start` if recurrence occurs before the horizon, including before an early ICU boundary;
2. `complete_no_recurrence` if no recurrence and `unitdischargeoffset >= T+h`;
3. `icu_death_before_horizon` if no recurrence, early ICU boundary, and unit status Expired;
4. `live_icu_discharge_before_horizon` if no recurrence, early ICU boundary, and unit status Alive;
5. unknown truncation otherwise.

The primary descriptive risk is recurrence among episodes with complete ICU observation through each fixed horizon. Wilson intervals are reported separately by literal label. A secondary robustness denominator requires at least one respiratoryCharting timestamp in every consecutive six-hour post-index bin; this is documentation support, not proof of continuous clinical observation. Stay- and hospital-clustered 3,000-replicate bootstrap intervals estimate the absolute Suspended-minus-Off recurrence difference. Hospital rates are descriptive; only hospitals with at least five complete episodes per label are compared.

Setting/activity trajectories are rebuilt without carry-forward in `[T-360,T)`, `[T,T+360)`, `[T+360,T+1440)`, and `[T+1440,T+2880)`. Generic respiratory-chart presence, setting-family positivity, family counts, and chart times distinguish an absent setting marker from wholesale respiratory documentation loss. The categorical oxygen/device classes use their own `respchartoffset` and would always be shown separately, but availability was zero.

## Executed recurrence, competing status, and observation results

### Literal Off

Among 713 Off episodes, complete-observation recurrence was:

- 6 hours: 12/647 = 1.85% (Wilson 95% interval 1.06%-3.21%);
- 24 hours: 23/583 = 3.95% (2.64%-5.85%);
- 48 hours: 32/403 = 7.94% (5.68%-10.99%).

At 6 hours there were 635 complete/no-recurrence episodes, 12 recurrent Starts, 41 live ICU discharges, and 25 ICU deaths before the horizon. At 24 hours these counts were 560, 23, 102, and 28. At 48 hours status counts were 371 complete/no-recurrence, 33 recurrent Starts overall, 280 live discharges, and 29 ICU deaths; 32 recurrent events belonged to the strict complete-through-48-hour denominator because one occurred before an early boundary. This distinction is retained rather than silently treating discharge as success. Hospital-death-by-horizon counts were 43, 53, and 57, but hospital outcome does not provide post-ICU RT recurrence ascertainment.

Generic respiratoryCharting was present in 99.7% of complete pre-6-hour Off windows, 100% of complete post-6-hour windows, 88.7% of structurally eligible 6-to-24-hour windows, and 85.1% of eligible 24-to-48-hour windows. Requiring generic support in every six-hour bin retained 647, 403, and 238 complete Off episodes; recurrence was 1.85%, 5.71%, and 13.03%, respectively. The higher 48-hour supported rate demonstrates that an unqualified complete-stay denominator can understate observed recurrence when documentation thins.

The inherited setting-family marker fell sharply after literal Off: 67.3% in the complete pre-6-hour window versus 7.7% in complete post-6-hour windows, then 11.0% at 6-24 hours and 15.1% at 24-48 hours among structurally eligible episodes. This is sustained documentation-state de-escalation, but not proof of extubation or clinical liberation.

### Literal Suspended

Among 350 Suspended episodes, complete-observation recurrence was:

- 6 hours: 9/344 = 2.62% (Wilson 95% interval 1.38%-4.90%);
- 24 hours: 25/330 = 7.58% (5.18%-10.94%);
- 48 hours: 34/298 = 11.41% (8.28%-15.52%).

Status counts at 6 hours were 335 complete/no-recurrence, 9 recurrent Starts, 5 live ICU discharges, and 1 ICU death; at 24 hours, 305, 25, 19, and 1; at 48 hours, 264, 40 recurrent Starts overall, 45 live discharges, and 1 ICU death, with 34 recurrent events in the strict complete-through-48-hour denominator. Hospital-death-by-horizon counts were 2, 3, and 6.

Generic respiratory chart support remained very high: 99.1% pre, 100% post-6 hours, 99.1% at 6-24 hours, and 99.0% at 24-48 hours. Every-six-hour support retained 344, 317, and 270 complete episodes, with recurrence 2.62%, 7.89%, and 11.11%. Thus Suspended's persistent activity cannot be dismissed as simple chart disappearance.

The setting-family marker differed sharply from Off: 39.4% pre, 29.9% post-6 hours, 51.8% at 6-24 hours, and 51.7% at 24-48 hours. Rather than a sustained off-like documentation regime, Suspended was followed by persistent/rebounding ventilator-setting charting.

### Label contrast, site support, and semantic falsification

Suspended-minus-Off complete recurrence differences were +0.76 points at 6 hours, +3.63 points at 24 hours, and +3.47 points at 48 hours. Both stay- and hospital-clustered intervals crossed zero: stay intervals were -1.39 to +3.06, -0.39 to +8.11, and -2.38 to +9.76 points; hospital intervals were especially broad (-0.89 to +11.20, -1.00 to +15.68, and -4.20 to +24.34 points). Only five hospitals had at least five complete episodes of each label at 6/24 hours and four at 48 hours. Hospital 71 showed much higher Suspended recurrence (20.0%, 27.8%, 42.9%) than Off (3.2%, 3.5%, 5.3%), whereas other hospitals were closer or sparse. Therefore recurrence alone does not establish a transportable label difference.

A required semantic falsification exposed a design trap: if the inherited self-controlled criterion of no other decisive transition within +/-6 hours were reused for the recurrence cohort, six-hour Start recurrence becomes exactly 0/649 Off and 0/266 Suspended **by construction**. That restriction is valid for isolating a charting discontinuity but invalid for estimating early recurrence. This child preserves the parent's calibration result while explicitly removes outcome-conditioned conflict cleaning from the durability estimand.

## Prespecified interpretation gates and result

A **supportive durability-like documentation result for Off** required all of: <=5% complete-observation recurrence by 24 hours and <=10% by 48 hours with Wilson upper bounds <=10% and <=15%; sustained post-index setting positivity <=20% in both 6-24- and 24-48-hour bins; at least 70% generic documentation support in each eligible bin; and no contradiction from categorical device evidence. Off met these narrow documentation gates (3.95% and 7.94%; upper bounds 5.85% and 10.99%; setting positivity 11.0% and 15.1%; generic support 88.7% and 85.1%), but device evidence was unavailable rather than affirming. The strongest support is therefore only that literal Off often marks a relatively durable **structured respiratory-documentation regime** during the remaining observed ICU stay.

An **adverse shared-semantics result** was prespecified if Suspended showed >=30 percentage points higher setting positivity than Off in either later bin, recurrence exceeded Off without stable site support, or Off/Suspended pooling masked qualitatively different trajectories. Suspended exceeded Off by approximately 40.8 and 36.5 points at 6-24 and 24-48 hours and had higher recurrence point estimates, with limited site overlap and clustered uncertainty. The shared Off/Suspended liberation-like interpretation is therefore falsified. Suspended should not be pooled with Off for a durable off-state phenotype.

An **inconclusive clinical-state result** applies when independent airway/device evidence is absent, early live discharge prevents post-ICU recurrence observation, or site representation is sparse. All apply. Even for Off, the clinical claim remains inconclusive: 280/713 left the ICU alive before 48 hours, categorical oxygen/device evidence was absent, prior independent airway/treatment/note corroboration failed, and no post-ICU RT record exists. Live discharge is truncation, not success.

## Strongest supported claim, unresolved claims, and clinical advance

The strongest supported claim is that eICU literal `RT Vent Off` and `Suspended` are not interchangeable structured states. In the exact available data, Off is followed by sustained low setting-family charting and 7.9% charted Start recurrence among episodes observable through 48 hours, whereas Suspended is followed by setting activity around 52% in both later bins and 11.4% recurrence. The recurrence difference is not stable enough across hospitals to claim a transportable quantitative distinction, but the trajectory divergence is large and makes pooling semantically unsafe. This is a clinically consequential measurement result because it blocks an apparently natural but invalid endpoint construction and identifies Off as the only label meriting future adjudication.

The unresolved claim is whether Off identifies actual ventilator discontinuation, extubation, durable invasive-ventilation liberation, or safe clinical success. Exact additional data required are independently timestamped ventilator connection logs, invasive versus noninvasive mode, tube insertion/removal and extubation/reintubation times, oxygen device after the event, RT/nursing narrative, post-ICU respiratory support, and blinded clinician adjudication sampled across hospitals and discordant Off/Suspended episodes. A subsequent validation study should estimate sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, inter-rater agreement, and site transportability against that bedside reference.

Automatically checkable claims are source hashes and row counts; literal value profile; patient joins; event offsets; exact and 30-minute deduplication; conflict and mixed-label exclusions; actual attrition; first subsequent Start; fixed-horizon status; discharge/death rules; generic six-hour support; setting-family trajectories; device-field availability; Wilson intervals; clustered bootstrap implementation; and the semantic zero-recurrence trap. An automatic verifier cannot establish extubation, invasive modality, liberation, intent, benefit, quality, causality, or post-ICU durability.
