## 1. Unresolved question (unchanged from parent)

AKI "recovery" is almost always declared by serum creatinine returning toward baseline [K2]. Yet creatinine is a poor measure of true kidney function during critical illness: eGFR-creatinine overestimated measured (iohexol) GFR roughly two-fold after prolonged ICU stay, diverging progressively with muscle loss [K1]. This creates a specific, testable mechanism for a clinical blind spot: creatinine may fall (signalling "recovery") while true kidney function and urine output remain impaired.

**Unresolved claim to test (H1):** Among ICU patients who meet creatinine-defined AKI, those whose creatinine has returned to near-baseline at a fixed 72h landmark but who remain oliguric ("discordant recovery") have higher risk of subsequent in-hospital death, post-landmark RRT initiation, and hospital length of stay than patients with concordant recovery (creatinine-recovered AND non-oliguric), after adjustment for severity, fluid balance and diuretic exposure.

**Leading rival explanations:** (a) residual confounding by illness severity; (b) diuretic use masking oliguria in the concordant group; (c) fluid overload lowering creatinine by dilution; (d) urine-output charting gaps misread as oliguria; (e) reverse causation (pre-terminal oliguria). The design addresses each as far as the data allow and labels what it cannot.

**What each outcome would mean.** Supportive (adjusted OR for death, discordant vs concordant, lower 95% CI above threshold with adequate discordant-group size): creatinine-only recovery assessment is unsafe as a stand-alone reassurance signal; recovery phenotyping should incorporate urine output [K2]. Adverse (CI excludes meaningful harm on primary and kidney composite): persistent oliguria after creatinine recovery behaves like a benign tolerance state — also useful, since it justifies not escalating monitoring. Inconclusive (small discordant group or CIs spanning the no-decision band): no practice signal; methodological note only.

**Clinical importance:** post-AKI decisions (monitoring intensity, fluid/liberalization, discharge readiness, nephrology follow-up) key off creatinine. A universally charted marker (urine output) that flags a high-risk "recovered" subgroup would directly inform monitoring decisions. MIMIC-IV has no measured GFR or cystatin C, so this study identifies *suspected* discordant recovery only [K1]; causal claims about intervening on oliguria require prospective/experimental data. Current large-cohort MIMIC-IV AKI work concentrates on RRT-initiation prediction with urine output as one predictor among many [K3], not on the recovery stage.

## 4. Design (unchanged from parent except threshold calibration from observed sizes)

**Population:** adult (>=18) ICU stays with KDIGO creatinine-defined AKI within the first 7 ICU days (rise >=0.3 mg/dL/48h or >=1.5x baseline; baseline = lowest creatinine from 7 days before admission through AKI onset). One ICU stay per hospitalization (earliest intime). Exclusions: RRT before landmark, <48h ICU stay, no urine-output charting in 24h before landmark, missing charted weight for the kg-normalized oliguria criterion.

**Landmark t0 = 72h after first AKI criterion.** Exposure (only data <= t0): creatinine-recovered = creatinine at t0 <= baseline+0.3 mg/dL AND <=1.25x baseline (sensitivity <=1.5x); persistent oliguria = mean urine output <0.3 mL/kg/h over 12h before t0 (charted weight). Groups: A concordant (recovered, non-oliguric), B discordant (recovered, oliguric), C non-recovered (reference).

**Outcomes (after t0 only):** primary in-hospital mortality (hospital_expire_flag); secondary 28-day mortality (dod), post-landmark RRT initiation, ICU/hospital LOS, kidney composite (RRT or recurrent creatinine AKI stage >=1 after t0).

**Analysis:** multivariable logistic regression for in-hospital death (Cox for time-to-event) comparing B vs A adjusted for age, sex, baseline creatinine, peak AKI stage (creatinine and UO criteria separately), SOFA-proxy components at t0 (vasopressors from inputevents, ventilation from procedureevents/chartevents, bilirubin/platelets/paO2), sepsis (diagnoses_icd ICD-9/10 at run time), cumulative fluid balance and loop diuretic dose 0-72h, and urine charting density. Report adjusted OR/HR with 95% CI.

**Falsification criteria (retained unchanged; calibrated by observed sizes):** supportive if the adjusted OR for in-hospital death (B vs A) has lower 95% CI > 1.15 with >=250 discordant-recovery patients in the final adjusted cohort; adverse if the CI excludes meaningful harm (upper 95% CI < 1.15) on both the primary outcome and the kidney composite; inconclusive if final B < 250 or CIs span 0.8-1.5, or if the dilution control reverses the direction of the estimate. Probe observation: B=263 (>=250) with 82 events at crude mortality 31.2% — adequate for the primary contrast, though near the boundary; the final B will shift with the added absolute AKI criterion, RRT exclusion and charted-weight requirement. If the final adjusted cohort yields B < 250, the prespecified verdict is inconclusive; a widened oliguria window (24h) may be reported as secondary but cannot rescue the primary.

**Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative; unchanged from parent):** Baseline = single-window phenotype + logistic/Cox (interpretable, prespecified). Alternative 1 = group-based trajectory modeling of hourly urine output from AKI onset to t0 (does risk track cumulative/trajectory oliguria burden rather than current status — is discordance a state or a process?). Alternative 2 = IPTW for loop diuretic exposure. Trajectory modeling and IPTW are secondary exploratory analyses; the baseline phenotype remains primary. CPU-only (sklearn/lifelines/statsmodels; extraction measured at minutes on 2 CPUs).

**Leakage control:** all exposure variables computed strictly <= t0; outcomes strictly > t0; baseline creatinine window ends before AKI onset; one stay per hospitalization (sensitivity: all stays with robust SE).

**New sensitivity analyses motivated by the probe's coverage finding:** (i) compare baseline characteristics and outcomes of creatinine-recovered patients with vs without landmark-window urine charting (is the A/B split selected on health status?); (ii) repeat the primary contrast with the charting rule widened from 12h to 24h before t0, reporting both.

## 5. Claim scope and limits

- Single-center (Beth Israel); no transport guarantee. Observational association; cannot establish that treating oliguria in "recovered" patients improves outcomes.
- No measured GFR/cystatin C -> suspected discordant recovery only [K1]; the muscle-loss mechanism is plausible but unverifiable here.
- Urine charting gaps/catheter timing: now MEASURED (42.8% of recovered patients lack landmark-window urine charting); mitigated by the charting-density covariate, the exclusion rule and the two new sensitivity analyses, not eliminated. Differential charting by health status remains a live selection threat to the A/B contrast.
- Probe numbers are feasibility/calibration evidence, not hypothesis confirmation; crude group mortality in the probe is confounded by severity — the full study's adjusted landmark analysis is the test.
- Novelty bounded by non-exhaustive search; [K2] (recovery definition unsettled) and [K3] (MIMIC-IV AKI work concentrated on RRT prediction) frame the gap.
