# Provisional hypothesis: visceral fat versus muscle fat infiltration as divergent risk phenotypes

Status: provisional and unverified. This is a hypothesis for a future Harbor experiment, not an observed result.

## Proposed relationship

Among UK Biobank participants with body-composition MRI and repeated functional measurements, at similar total body fat, higher visceral fat may be more specifically associated with subsequent metabolic disease, whereas higher posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) may be more specifically associated with subsequent functional decline or falls.

The clinical question is whether fat distribution identifies distinct downstream trajectories rather than merely predicting one generic health-risk score. A positive result could support targeted body-composition assessment; a null or adverse result would argue against separating these phenotypes for risk stratification.

## Falsifiable test

Use a temporally ordered cohort with baseline body-composition measurements and prespecified subsequent outcomes. Compare a simple total-adiposity baseline with a model adding visceral fat and posterior-thigh MFI, using the same participants, split, and outcome definitions. Estimate whether the two composition measures show distinct, reproducible associations with metabolic versus functional/fall outcomes, with uncertainty and calibration/discrimination or trajectory summaries. Falsification includes no incremental specificity after total adiposity and confounding checks for age, sex, baseline health, measurement timing, and selection.

Supportive results would show distinct, held-out associations with uncertainty consistent with the prespecified direction. Adverse results would show no divergence, reversed associations, or instability after adjustment. Inconclusive results would arise from inadequate overlap, sparse events, unresolved timing, or wide uncertainty; they would not refute the hypothesis.

## Method comparison

The simple baseline is a prespecified total-adiposity model. The substantive alternative adds visceral fat and posterior-thigh MFI to test composition-specific information. A learned or mechanistic alternative may be deferred until feasibility and event support are verified; if used, it must answer the same divergence question and be compared on held-out data, not merely optimized for prediction.
