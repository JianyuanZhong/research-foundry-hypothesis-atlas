# Provisional hypothesis: MerTK-inhibitor response is context-specific, not a generic donor or treatment artifact

## Status

Provisional design-stage hypothesis for episode 11. This is not an executed result and does not establish direct MerTK action, causality, protection, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.

## Proposed relationship

In the available E-MTAB-8873 targeted macrophage/fibroblast coculture assay, the MerTK-inhibitor-associated transcriptional response differs between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage contexts. The primary estimand is a donor-aware, condition-verified context-by-inhibitor interaction. A secondary descriptive question is whether this interaction separates a macrophage-linked resolving/repair-associated program from a fibroblast-linked inflammatory or harmful program.

## Grounding and unresolved claim

The strongest available evidence supports biological plausibility, not this effect: MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets have been associated with remission and repair-related fibroblast responses; macrophage functional reprogramming can alter inflammatory mediator production; and macrophage–fibroblast repair circuits can be context-, timing-, and niche-dependent. The unresolved claim is whether an intervention-associated context interaction is specific to the stated macrophage context after accounting for pseudobulk composition, reused/mixed donors, and generic treatment or assay effects. The drug-associated expression response cannot by itself prove direct target action.

## Missing evidence and limits

Drug treatment does not identify a direct target, and no patient outcome is available. A future wet-lab perturbation or clinically adjudicated cohort would be required for mechanistic or therapeutic claims.

## Alternatives not chosen

A generic predictive model is deferred because it would not distinguish context-specific biology from donor or treatment composition. A full neural sequence model is deferred because the available assay has no verified longitudinal patient trajectory or temporal outcome; this is a dependency decision, not a blanket rejection of learned methods.
