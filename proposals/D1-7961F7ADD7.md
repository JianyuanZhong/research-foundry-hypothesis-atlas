# Can invasive-MAP burden outperform nadir after separating perfusion history from line and creatinine observation?

## Status and substantive advance

This is a planned measurement-first child of `[prior hypothesis]`, with one completed outcome-blind/label-support audit. It preserves the clinical question, the fixed hour-24 landmark, MAP exposure in ICU minutes [0,1440), and the strictly subsequent creatinine-defined AKI target in (1440,10080]. It does not inspect or report post-landmark AKI.

The parent treats `24 × observed low-MAP bins / observed bins` as first-day burden. That quantity is a monitored-time summary, not an identified all-clock-time burden when arterial-line placement/removal is informative. The new audit shows this distinction is consequential even after the parent's ≥202/288-bin rule. The child therefore adds a sealed measurement experiment, explicitly models both pressure and observation, and makes outcome interpretation contingent on measurement and label-support gates. It also corrects the parent's unimplementable phrase “first chronological stay per person”: eICU has no cross-hospital admission date. The computable population is the first ICU unit visit in each health-system hospitalization (`unitvisitnumber=1`, `unitstaytype='admit'`); repeated `uniquepid` values are grouped in resampling, and a one-record-per-`uniquepid` analysis is prespecified rather than falsely called chronological.

The future solver must newly fit: (i) transparent nadir-only and nadir-plus-observed-burden models; (ii) an outcome-blind gap-reconstruction baseline and joint pressure/observation state model; (iii) a matched observation-aware temporal AKI model; and (iv) the creatinine-observation model and selection-sensitivity analyses. Completion is the full set of measurement, label-support, transport, prediction, uncertainty, and interpretation outputs below; confirmation is not required.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and clinical importance

The strongest claim supported by inspected work is limited. Hypotension definitions are highly heterogeneous, most ICU studies report more AKI with hypotension, but a recent meta-analysis did not find an aggregate AKI association [K1]. FINNAKI found lower time-adjusted MAP independently associated with five-day AKI progression in severe sepsis under dense, quality-controlled multicenter monitoring [K2]. KDIGO supports timed creatinine criteria but explicitly warns that creatinine-only use, elevated admission creatinine, missing urine output, and uncertain baseline miss or misclassify AKI and do not establish cause [K3].

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among adult first ICU unit visits that remain in ICU at hour 24, have adequate invasive-MAP support, an admission and pre-landmark creatinine, no creatinine-defined progression through hour 24, and subsequent creatinine opportunity, cumulative low-MAP burden contains hospital-transportable prognostic information for strictly subsequent creatinine-defined AKI beyond a flexible MAP nadir term; that information is not explained entirely by arterial-line observation, vasopressor response, evolving pre-landmark renal dysfunction, or hospital testing conventions.

A stable positive result would justify summarizing cumulative monitored hypotension alongside nadir in monitoring and prospective study design. An adverse precise result would argue against elevating a noisier cumulative metric over nadir. Neither sets a MAP target or shows that raising pressure prevents AKI.

The leading explanation is a sustained perfusion insult. The strongest rival is a coupled care process: sicker patients retain arterial lines, receive vasopressors, and get more creatinine tests, so “duration” records surveillance and treatment rather than stable pressure exposure. They differ predictively. A perfusion-history signal should survive transparent missingness bounds, pseudo-gap recovery, pre-hour-24 renal trajectory adjustment, observation weighting, whole-hospital holdout, and order ablation. A care-process signal should track line boundaries, pressor response, testing intensity, or site conventions and attenuate or reverse when those are tested.

## Completed bounded outcome-blind and label-support audit

The reproducible scan `diagnostic.py` read the full patient, vitalPeriodic, lab, and infusionDrug files in 40.6 seconds on 8 CPUs/32 GiB. It did not construct post-hour-24 AKI. Its broad support cohort retained `unitvisitnumber=1` admission stays >24 hours, ≥202 valid invasive-MAP bins, and admission plus pre-landmark creatinine; it intentionally precedes day-1 AKI exclusion.

Observed counts were 107,153 adult first-unit stays, 28,496 with any valid first-day invasive MAP, and 14,397 in the broad support cohort. There were 93,295 distinct people among the 107,153 stays and 2,032 people represented across more than one hospital, proving that patient grouping must accompany hospital splitting.

The parent’s 70% threshold does not make the all-day burden point identified:

- 6,746/14,397 (46.9%) began after minute 60 or ended before minute 1,380; only 6,315 had observations within 30 minutes of both boundaries.
- 7,480 (52.0%) had at least one internal >5-minute gap and 1,599 (11.1%) had a gap >60 minutes.
- The logical full-day burden width obtained by assigning every missing five-minute bin to non-hypotension versus hypotension was median 1.50 hours, p75 3.67, p90 5.50, maximum 7.17. In the 70–79% coverage band its median was 5.92 hours, versus 0.50 in the ≥90% band.
- Across 67 hospitals with ≥50 support stays, the p10/p50/p90 proportions with sparse boundaries were 0.330/0.455/0.646 and mean burdens were 2.76/3.70/5.74 hours. These are monitor-convention signals as well as possible case-mix signals.

Label opportunity is substantial but informative, so lack of a test cannot be a negative outcome. Of 14,397 support stays, 13,347 had any post-landmark creatinine and 13,270 had one by minute 4,320. There were 1,061 live/dead early-discharge stays without a 48-hour post-landmark test and 218 deaths by day 7 without a post-landmark test. Across the 67 hospitals, post-landmark creatinine availability ranged from 84.2% at p10 to 98.1% at p90. Availability was 89.9%, 90.4%, and 94.2% across 70–79%, 80–89%, and ≥90% MAP-coverage bands.

Vasopressor recording rose from 16.9% in the lowest burden quintile to 49.1% in the highest. Mean admission-to-last-pre-landmark creatinine change ranged from -0.054 to -0.008 mg/dL across burden quintiles; this aggregate pattern does not eliminate individual evolving dysfunction. Post-creatinine availability varied nonmonotonically from 90.7% to 94.4% to 91.8% across burden quintiles. These are outcome-blind observations, not AKI associations.

## Population, clocks, and outcome

Primary unit: an adult first ICU unit visit within a health-system hospitalization (`unitvisitnumber=1`, `unitstaytype='admit'`), alive and in ICU at minute 1,440. Require ≥202 of 288 valid invasive-MAP bins in [0,1440), admission creatinine in [-360,360], at least one creatinine in (-360,1440], and no exposure-window creatinine progression: no rise ≥0.3 mg/dL from admission and no value ≥1.5× admission through minute 1,440. Day-1 `dialysis=1` is an exclusion sensitivity because its exact time is unavailable. The executable reports repeated-person counts; all records of a `uniquepid` stay in one partition and bootstrap cluster. A sensitivity retains one deterministic smallest-`patientunitstayid` record per person, explicitly not called chronologically first.

Exposure remains [0,1440); landmark is minute 1,440. Outcome is first strictly subsequent creatinine-defined AKI before minute 10,080 and before ICU discharge: ≥0.3 mg/dL rise from the last pre-landmark value within 48 hours, or ≥1.5× admission by day 7. The general all-pairs 48-hour rule is a sensitivity. Live discharge and death before adequate measurement are competing/truncating events, never non-events. Urine-output AKI, RRT initiation, etiology, and community-acquired AKI are not claimed.

## Measurement stage: transparent baseline and observation-aware alternative

Deduplicate same-stay/same-offset MAP by median; reject <20 or >180 mmHg; retain abrupt changes as flags. Construct 288 five-minute bins and preserve their missingness.

M0 is transparent:

- nadir = minimum observed valid `systemicmean`;
- monitored-time burden = `24 × n(MAP<65)/n(valid)`;
- full-clock logical burden interval = `[24×nlow/288, 24×(nlow+nmissing)/288]`;
- coverage, first/last observed offset, internal missing minutes, maximum gap, gap after low MAP, longest low run, episode count, and four six-hour burdens.

M1 is a small joint hidden-state/observation model on the same raw inputs: 288-bin invasive MAP values and masks, time since last value, clock hour, and vasopressor-entry masks, plus the same static covariates used by the outcome baseline. A two-state latent pressure process has smooth autoregressive transitions; a separate observation hazard depends on prior observed MAP, latent state, pressor entry, clock time, and a training-hospital random intercept. It yields posterior full-clock burden, uncertainty, ordering, fragmentation, and low-MAP→pressor→recovery versus persistent-low trajectories. It may reveal information M0 destroys, but its latent values are not ground truth or proof that an arterial line was physically present.

Fit M0/M1 without AKI labels on training hospitals. In ≥95%-coverage, boundary-supported stays (first ≤30, last ≥1410, no gap >15), hide contiguous blocks sampled from empirical gap lengths and starts within training hospitals, stratified by prior MAP <65, pressor mask, and clock hour. Test unchanged models in held-out hospitals. M1 passes only if it reduces burden MAE by ≥20% versus observed-bin rescaling and capped 60-minute last-observation carry-forward, has absolute MAE ≤0.5 hour, and its nominal 90% burden intervals cover 85–95% with median width ≤2 hours overall and no hospital stratum with MAE >1 hour. This pseudo-missing test is necessary, not sufficient: observed re-entry cannot validate true MAP while an undocumented line was absent.

If M1 fails, no latent full-day burden claim is allowed. The study may still complete as an adverse measurement result, with outcome modeling limited to monitored-time burden and the prespecified ≥90%-coverage/boundary-supported sensitivity. If that sensitivity has <1,500 AKI events or <20 hospitals after final exclusions, defer the biological comparison rather than relax the landmark.

## Matched AKI models and estimand

Freeze connected patient/hospital groups into approximately 70/15/15 train/validation/test partitions; no `uniquepid` or hospital crosses. If cross-hospital links create an unusable giant component, exclude cross-hospital-repeat people from the primary transport split and report them separately. Require ≥20 hospitals and ≥100 outcome events in each split before opening test outcomes; rotate one documented salt at most.

B0 is penalized logistic/discrete-time competing-risk regression with a training-only restricted cubic spline of nadir; age, sex, ethnicity, admission source, unit type, weight, admission creatinine, last pre-landmark creatinine, admission-to-last change/slope, pre-landmark creatinine count/timing, MAP coverage/boundary/internal-gap features, and non-BP/non-creatinine apacheApsVar severity with missing indicators.

B1 adds a spline of monitored-time burden and its logical-width interaction. The primary conditional estimand remains the test-population standardized risk difference and ratio at training-set burden p75 versus p25 within 5-mmHg nadir strata and common support. Report B1 versus B0 held-out Δlog loss, Brier score, AUROC/AUPRC, calibration intercept/slope, net benefit, pooled and equal-hospital estimates, and 1,000 patient-within-hospital cluster bootstraps.

T1 uses the M1 posterior hourly low-MAP probabilities/uncertainty, observed hourly nadirs/masks, time-since-observation, and hourly pressor entries with B0 static covariates. A six-basis smooth functional coefficient plus low-MAP→pressor and pressor→recovery transition terms preserves order and treatment response. It has the same population, outcome, split, raw inputs, and evaluation as B1. Compare T1 with B1; ablate order, observation hazard, and pressor channels. T1 can show that late/refractory or fragmented exposure contains transportable information lost by total burden, but cannot identify renal autoregulation or vasopressor effects.

## Creatinine observation, discharge, and hospital-convention tests

Before fitting AKI, fit out-of-fold models for (a) any post-landmark creatinine, (b) creatinine by minute 4,320, and (c) live discharge/death before a qualifying test, using only landmark-available variables. Report calibration and propensity overlap by hospital, coverage, burden quintile, and discharge timing.

Primary outcome estimation among observed labels uses stabilized inverse observation weights truncated at 1st/99th percentiles and a discrete-time competing-event formulation. Report the unweighted observed-label estimate, ≥2-test subset, fixed (1440,4320] test window, one-per-person, ≥90%-coverage/boundary-supported, and pattern-mixture tipping analyses in which unobserved AKI odds range from 0.5× to 2× matched observed odds. Post-landmark test count never enters the clinical predictor.

Hospital artifacts are falsified with whole-hospital holdout, equal-hospital weighting, within-hospital estimates, hospital-level forest plots, and a negative diagnostic predicting hospital from coverage/boundary/gap features. A signal that disappears after target-free measurement calibration, within-hospital comparison, or omission of high-leverage monitor-convention hospitals is inconclusive, not mechanistic evidence.

B2 adds day-1 pressor entry classes/count/first/last/hour masks to B1. Report attenuation but do not call it mediation because pressors may be response, confounder, mediator, or collider. Excluding day-1 creatinine progression and adjusting the last value/change define a conditional post-landmark question; they do not prove that renal injury was absent at hour 24.

## Falsification and interpretation

Support for cumulative burden requires all of:

1. M1 passes its sealed pseudo-gap gates, or the B1 result is stable in the ≥90%-coverage/boundary-supported sensitivity without latent extrapolation.
2. B1's held-out standardized p75-versus-p25 burden risk difference is >0 with a hospital/patient-cluster 95% interval excluding 0 and Δlog loss versus B0 is >0 with its interval excluding 0, without calibration deterioration.
3. Direction persists under observation weighting, fixed-window testing, pattern-mixture sensitivity through at least 0.75–1.5× unobserved odds, within-hospital analysis, and removal of high-leverage hospitals.

T1 is separately supported only if it improves held-out log loss over B1 with interval excluding 0 and stable nonconstant temporal/response structure survives order, observation, and pressor ablations. T1 failure does not negate an aggregate burden result.

Adverse evidence is an upper 95% bound ≤1 absolute percentage point for the standardized B1 burden contrast plus no held-out log-loss gain, or a stable reverse association. Measurement-stage failure is adverse to an all-clock-day burden claim. Near-zero hospital-transportable gain despite within-hospital association is adverse to deployment portability.

Inconclusive evidence includes intervals spanning 0 and +1 point, M1 miscalibration, sign change across logical/selection bounds, inadequate overlap/events, strong hospital heterogeneity, or dependence on one coverage, threshold, testing, or pressor specification. Do not tune thresholds after test opening.

A supportive result establishes a transportable conditional prognostic association, not causal hypoperfusion, a treatment effect, an optimal MAP threshold, or a vasopressor recommendation. An adverse result does not prove hypotension harmless or nadir causal. True arterial-line presence/removal intent, delivered pressor dose, fluid/cardiac output/nephrotoxins, pre-illness renal baseline, urine-output/RRT AKI, and etiology require device metadata, medication/nursing adjudication, longitudinal pre-ICU data, clinician review, or another prospective study.

## Compute, deliverables, and verifier boundary

The completed diagnostic used 8 CPUs/32 GiB for 40.6 seconds. Proposed full work fits the solver envelope (16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB, 28,800 seconds): 16 CPUs, 64 GiB, ≤6 hours for ingestion, M0/M1 estimation, B0/B1/B2/T1 fitting, and 1,000 clustered bootstraps. CPU is justified because the selected state-space and spline models have few states/parameters over roughly five million bins. No GPU is required. A temporal convolutional network was considered but deferred: it could learn episode morphology, yet does not better isolate the observation-process rival and adds site-shortcut risk. Revisit only if T1 passes, residual within-hour morphology remains, and ≥1,500 events remain; then budget one A100, 4 CPUs, 32 GiB, ≤2 hours, five seeds.

Required outputs: cohort flow; repeated-person/cross-hospital audit; source/unit/artifact table; coverage and logical-width distributions; pseudo-gap masks and M0/M1 calibration/MAE; label-opportunity and competing-event tables; frozen split manifest; fitted B0/B1/B2/T1 objects; held-out predictions; calibration/discrimination/net-benefit tables; standardized contrasts and clustered intervals; hospital forest/influence plots; temporal weights and ablations; pattern-mixture grid; and machine-readable claim links.

An automatic verifier can check source hashes, clocks, exclusions, no post-landmark leakage, grouping, split isolation, missingness construction, AKI code, model fits, predictions, metrics, intervals, gates, and whether reported conclusions match supportive/adverse/inconclusive outputs. It cannot establish true line presence, physiologic perfusion, clinician intent, AKI etiology, causal treatment effects, or actionability.

## Alternatives and revisit record

A ≥90%-coverage/boundary-only study is simpler but discards the informative sparse tail and changes the target population; it remains a sensitivity. Pure worst-case burden bounds are assumption-light but too wide in the audited cohort to answer burden versus nadir alone. The selected joint observation/state model is the smallest substantive alternative that directly tests why duration may look informative. A TCN is deferred for the stated residual-morphology trigger. Full causal g-methods are deferred because delivered doses, fluids, nephrotoxins, urine output, and treatment intent are not adequately identified.
