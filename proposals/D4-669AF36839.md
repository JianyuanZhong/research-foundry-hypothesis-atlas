# Prespecified localization of continuous cystatin-C discordance information

## Lineage and substantive advance

This is a substantive successor to assessed-valid, executed and independently audited `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not reopen the adverse binary result or change the parent's selected population, temporal boundary, target, comparator, feature, prediction protocol, capacity definition, uncertainty boundary, or evidence limits. The parent found that the continuous feature

`Z = log(eGFRcys_0 / eGFRcr_0)`

improved average held-out log loss reproducibly (`Delta_LL = -0.00114261`, conditional 98% interval `[-0.00198302,-0.00032334]`, favorable in 10/10 repeats) but produced only `+1.0` outcome at global fixed 10% capacity (conditional 98% interval `[-6.401,8.5]`, positive in 5/10), below the frozen `+5` floor. The joint result was correctly inconclusive: average probabilistic information was supported under that protocol, while meaningful capacity concentration was not.

The unresolved, clinically consequential mechanism question is whether that probabilistic information is concentrated close to the creatinine-eGFR threshold at which clinical staging decisions are commonly made, or is diffuse across the eligible eGFR range. This distinction determines whether a future validation should target a narrow borderline subgroup or a broad population, without implying that the protocol-derived threshold is CKD diagnosis or that testing changes outcomes. The prespecified hypothesis is:

> Among the same selected complete first-repeat attenders with baseline eGFRcr >=60, the incremental held-out log-loss benefit of continuous cystatin-C discordance beyond the flexible creatinine comparator is larger in the baseline borderline stratum eGFRcr in [60,75) than in the farther-from-threshold stratum eGFRcr >=75.

A secondary, jointly reported localization question asks whether any gain in fixed-capacity outcome concentration is similarly concentrated in the borderline stratum. The experiment is explicitly capable of supporting a diffuse pattern, a localized pattern, an adverse interaction, or no resolvable heterogeneity; it is not designed to select a favorable subgroup after viewing results.

## Evidence boundary and claims

Prior evidence supports only the following narrower claims: in this same UKB snapshot and selected repeat-attender cohort, the one-degree-of-freedom continuous log-ratio added repeat-stable held-out probabilistic information under the inherited model and conditional bootstrap protocol, while the fixed 10% concentration claim remained inconclusive. The earlier binary discordance feature was adverse under its own multiplicity-controlled protocol. Those are prior same-snapshot findings, not independent replication of this localization question and not evidence that either stratum is clinically useful.

This proposal tests effect heterogeneity of an observable prediction increment, not measured GFR, CKD chronicity/progression, biological mechanism, causal effect of cystatin-C ordering, treatment benefit, triage utility, or transportability. All inference is conditional on complete observed first-repeat attendance and on the observed elapsed interval, which is future-known for a baseline deployment setting. A positive result would justify external, baseline-deployable validation focused on the prespecified stratum; it would not justify clinical implementation.

## Strata frozen before any result access

The strata are defined solely from baseline `eGFRcr_0`, after `S_A` is fixed and before fitting or inspecting any outcome, prediction, loss, capacity, or event count:

- `G_border = I(60 <= eGFRcr_0 < 75)`;
- `G_far = I(eGFRcr_0 >= 75)`.

The 60–<75 interval is a clinically prespecified proximity band around the inherited 60 threshold; 75 is fixed a priori as a simple 15-unit separation boundary. There is no third stratum, data-dependent quantile, minimum-effect subgroup search, sex-specific cut, elapsed-time cut, or post-hoc rebinning. Participants exactly at 75 belong to `G_far`; participants exactly at 60 already enter `S_A`. Report stratum sizes and event counts only in aggregate, and stop localization inference if either stratum violates the inherited support/event gates. The all-cohort analysis remains the primary inherited information and capacity analysis; stratum analyses are a predeclared heterogeneity extension and do not replace it.

## Frozen estimands and models

Use the identical comparator and extension as the parent:

- `M0`: fitted intercept, sex, and separate natural-cubic spline bases for baseline eGFRcr, baseline age, and observed elapsed repeat time. Each basis has four columns, training-fold 25th/50th/75th percentile knots, training min/max boundaries, centered constraint and training-only column scaling.
- `Mc`: `M0` plus exactly one training-standardized linear `Z` column. It must be 13 versus 14 non-intercept columns, with no tuning and no additional biomarker, stratum indicator, or interaction in either model.

Fit both models in each of the ten inherited outcome-stratified round-robin five-fold repeats, preserving the parent's deterministic fold assignments and `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(solver='lbfgs', penalty='l2', C=1, fit_intercept=True, tol=1e-10, max_iter=2000, class_weight=None)`. All preprocessing, knots and scaling are training-fold only. Every selected participant must have one finite held-out prediction per repeat for each model. The stratum label is not supplied to the model; it is used only to evaluate prespecified effect heterogeneity, avoiding a model-capacity change.

### Primary localization estimand

For each stratum `g`, calculate paired held-out log loss over that stratum and repeat, and define

`Delta_LL_g = mean(logloss(Mc,g) - logloss(M0,g))`,

where negative favors Mc. The primary interaction contrast is

`H = Delta_LL_border - Delta_LL_far`.

A negative `H` indicates a larger continuous-discordance log-loss gain near the threshold; an `H` near zero indicates diffuse gain; a positive `H` indicates relatively greater gain farther from the threshold. Estimate this using the same participant-level paired repeated out-of-fold predictions and the inherited conditional 4,000-replicate bootstrap (`seed=520241`), carrying both predictions, outcomes and stratum labels together. The bootstrap is conditional on frozen splits and fitted predictions; it does not refit models or regenerate folds.

The all-cohort `Delta_LL` must be reproduced as an integrity anchor, not treated as independent replication. For localization, use a two-sided conditional 98% percentile interval for `H` and a fixed interaction margin of `0.0010` log-loss units, chosen before results as a small but operationally nontrivial contrast relative to the parent's `0.00114261` all-cohort gain. The primary localized-support condition is the entire interval for `H` below `-0.0010`; the primary diffuse-support condition is the entire interval within `[-0.0010,+0.0010]` together with both stratum point estimates favoring Mc and each stratum's interval excluding a materially adverse loss increase of `+0.0010`. Otherwise heterogeneity is inconclusive. The margin is a protocol decision threshold, not a clinical equivalence margin.

Because the two stratum estimates are related, report them and the interaction jointly. Do not claim that a non-significant interaction proves equality. Use no post-hoc subgroup claim based on whichever stratum has the smaller p-value.

For localization, calculate two prespecified secondary quantities per repeat and stratum:

1. **Global-capacity contribution**: the difference in selected outcomes in `G_border` and `G_far` between Mc and M0 under the global 10% ranking, with the two stratum differences summing exactly to the global event difference.
2. **Within-stratum capacity**: rank within each stratum using the same stable-tie rule and select exactly 10% of each stratum (largest-remainder integer quota fixed from that stratum's size), then compare selected outcomes under Mc versus M0. This is a stratum-conditional concentration estimand and not a claim about one shared service capacity.

These capacity quantities are secondary and multiplicity-controlled with the primary interaction: use one two-sided 98% simultaneous max-|T| bootstrap family for `H`, `Delta_events_border_global`, `Delta_events_far_global`, and the two within-stratum event differences, rather than treating any one favorable component as confirmatory. The global capacity supportive gate remains inherited and is not upgraded by subgroup findings. A clinically localized capacity pattern requires a positive borderline contribution with its simultaneous lower bound above zero and a predeclared practical floor of +2 outcomes per 10% stratum allocation, but can only be described as exploratory evidence if the parent global +5 gate remains unmet. Any stratum with fewer than 20 events or fewer than 1,000 participants fails the event/support gate for capacity localization; the all-cohort result is still reported.

If bootstrap resamples produce undefined losses because a resample lacks an outcome class, use the inherited deterministic finite-loss convention only if already implemented by the parent; otherwise mark the localization family uncertified rather than silently changing the estimator.

## Falsification and negative controls

The predeclared falsification is an outcome-label permutation placebo, not an orientation reversal. For each of 200 fixed-seed permutations (`seed=520242`, permutation index `b=1,...,200`), permute `Y` across participants within the frozen `G_border`/`G_far` strata, leaving every participant's predictors, stratum, repeat, fold assignment and capacity quota unchanged. Refit `M0` and `Mc` on each permuted training set using the identical training-only preprocessing and logistic-regression settings, then obtain paired held-out predictions and recompute the all-cohort `Delta_LL`, stratum `Delta_LL_g`, interaction `H`, and global and stratum capacity differences. The placebo is a null check for outcome-linked incremental information: because the permuted labels are independent of baseline `Z` conditional on stratum, the 200 placebo estimates should be centered near zero, with no reproducible favorable `Mc` advantage, no systematic negative `H`, and no systematic positive capacity contribution. Some individual permutations may be favorable by chance.

The placebo decision rule is fixed before results. Define `q_low` and `q_high` as the empirical 1st and 99th percentiles across the 200 placebo values for each statistic, and report the fraction of placebo replicates that are at least as favorable as the observed statistic (negative for log-loss/H, positive for capacity). The falsification gate passes only if the placebo distributions are approximately centered at their null expectation of zero (their two-sided 98% intervals contain zero), the favorable-tail fraction is no larger than 0.02 for the observed primary interaction and all-cohort `Delta_LL`, and no capacity placebo shows a systematic positive shift (its two-sided 98% interval contains zero). If a placebo distribution is materially favorable, excludes zero in the favorable direction, or the observed statistic is not more extreme than its corresponding placebo null, the result is falsification-failed/uncertified and cannot be called localized or diffuse support. This is a diagnostic, not an opportunity to rescue the primary result. The 200-permutation placebo null is separate from, and does not replace, the inherited conditional 4,000-draw bootstrap intervals; if refitting all permutations is infeasible under the declared resources, mark the falsification gate uncertified rather than silently substituting a cheaper procedure.

Do not add new negative-control biomarkers or outcomes after seeing results. The prior binary feature's adverse result and the parent's continuous result are retained as evidence context only. An adverse localization result means the prespecified interaction is in the opposite direction with its simultaneous interval beyond the adverse margin, or a falsification/semantic/integrity gate fails; it does not mean cystatin C has no biological relevance. If both strata show similar favorable log-loss increments but capacity remains null, that is evidence for diffuse probabilistic signal without demonstrated fixed-capacity concentration. If only the borderline stratum shows favorable loss and capacity contribution, that supports a localized hypothesis under this protocol. If the interaction is wide or contradictory across estimands, classify inconclusive.

## Decision rules

- **Localized supportive evidence**: all integrity, support, computation and falsification gates pass; the 98% interval for `H` is wholly below `-0.0010`; both the observed stratum direction and the joint family are consistent; and no adverse calibration or orientation criterion passes. This supports only a prespecified localization of observable held-out information, not clinical utility.
- **Diffuse supportive evidence**: all gates pass; `H`'s 98% interval lies within the predeclared equivalence-like band `[-0.0010,+0.0010]`; both stratum increments favor Mc and neither has a materially adverse interval; and the all-cohort information anchor remains favorable. This is protocol-bounded evidence of diffuse information, not proof of equal biological effects.
- **Adverse**: the interaction interval is wholly above `+0.0010` (far stratum materially better), or both primary and capacity-localization directions materially contradict the prespecified borderline hypothesis with simultaneous support, or a required source/fit/prediction/falsification gate fails. A support failure without a directional contradiction is not adverse.
- **Inconclusive**: intervals cross the localization margins, stratum support is sparse, the capacity quantities disagree with loss localization, or the parent-style joint information/capacity gates are not jointly satisfied. In particular, a favorable localized log-loss interaction cannot upgrade the parent's global capacity result, and a +1 global outcome difference cannot be promoted to useful concentration.

The proposal must report supportive, adverse and inconclusive outcomes even if the parent result makes one more plausible. A verifier can check source paths/hashes, joins, masks, equations, frozen strata, model columns, preprocessing, splits, prediction coverage, capacity arithmetic, bootstrap clustering, multiplicity and conclusion-to-gate consistency. It cannot establish assay validity, biological mechanism, CKD chronicity, clinical consequences, value of the margins, reasons for attendance/missingness, causal effects, clinical utility, or transportability; those require adjudication, external data, or a separate baseline-deployable decision study.
