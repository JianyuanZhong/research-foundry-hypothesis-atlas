# Provisional hypothesis: macrophage context modifies the intervention-associated FLS transcript response

Seed: D2-T1  
Status: provisional, evidence-bound draft; no inferential result has been computed.

## Proposed relationship

Among rheumatoid-arthritis fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS) exposed to the interventions represented in the configured E-MTAB-8316 assay, the direction and magnitude of the intervention-associated transcript response will depend on macrophage/coculture context rather than being context-independent. Operationally, the hypothesis is a reproducible intervention-by-macrophage-context interaction after accounting for donor pairing and repeated context measurements.

This is a hypothesis about an intervention-associated expression pattern. It is not a claim that a drug acts directly on a target, that the expression change is causal, or that a clinical benefit has been observed.

## Why it matters

Target prioritization is consequential when an intervention appears effective in one inflammatory microenvironment but ineffective or potentially harmful in another. A reproducible context interaction would motivate microenvironment-specific evaluation of candidate targets and would warn against treating a drug-associated expression response as universally applicable. The substantive advance is a prespecified test of whether the intervention contrast changes with macrophage context, rather than a descriptive drug-response table.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The persistent board and seed record support that the configured therapeutic-targets collection includes an E-MTAB-8316 assay with FLS, macrophage/coculture-related conditions, intervention-associated expression measurements, and donor-related metadata. That supports feasibility of testing a context interaction, but does not establish the interaction. The unresolved claim is whether the intervention-by-context interaction is reproducible after donor-aware adjustment, rather than reflecting donor composition, batch, condition assignment, or indirect transcriptional response.

## Falsifiable test

Use only samples whose verified design contains the intervention/treatment label, macrophage or coculture-context label, donor/contributor identifier where available, and expression values. Join matrix and design only through the documented sample key. Do not join incompatible identifier namespaces.

Fit a donor-aware additive baseline with separate intervention and macrophage-context terms, plus donor/technical factors where verified. Fit a substantive alternative adding the intervention-by-context interaction. Aggregate repeated measurements to the donor/context unit before inference, or use a donor-aware model with uncertainty that reflects reuse. If a paired pre/post intervention contrast is verified, use it; otherwise do not treat unpaired samples as paired.

Supportive evidence requires a prespecified interaction estimate with uncertainty excluding the null, stable direction across independent donor contributions, and no indication that it is driven by one reused donor or batch. Adverse evidence is a robust null or reversal, or evidence that the apparent interaction is explained by donor, batch, or condition assignment. Inconclusive evidence includes failed key/assay verification, too few independent donors, high imprecision, or unstable estimates. An imprecise null is not decisive refutation.

## Baseline and alternative

The additive model asks whether intervention and macrophage context have average associations without context-dependent intervention effects. The interaction alternative asks whether the intervention response changes with macrophage context, information the additive model loses. A more flexible hierarchical model is deferred unless the verified design has enough independent donors to estimate donor/context heterogeneity; it is not selected for predictive gain alone. A mechanistic perturbation alternative is deferred because direct target action and causal mediation are not established by this expression dataset.
