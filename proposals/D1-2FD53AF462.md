# Target-independent reconstruction of eICU ventilation-label receipt

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

Among adult eICU stays that independently satisfy a structured invasive-ventilation proxy and the inherited strict pre-landmark readiness screen, how often do the two structured Ventilation care-plan labels appear within six hours, and is their availability sufficiently broad and transportable to support the inherited labelled comparison?

The strongest existing evidence is not that a daily extubation evaluation or trial occurred. It is that, in a target-conditioned cohort of 540 labelled landmarks, label assignment and downstream setting/action proxies were strongly hospital- and landmark-dependent; durable liberation could not be validly ascertained. The unresolved claim tested here is whether those 540 labels represent substantial coverage of a target-independent eligible denominator. If receipt is sparse and site-concentrated after denominator-aware standardization, the label-defined comparison is falsified as a broadly representative multicentre clinical-practice contrast.

This matters because treating structured labels as interventions can produce apparently precise but clinically misleading estimates. A target-independent denominator distinguishes label availability and selection from patient eligibility.

## Exact computable experiment

### Sources, tables, columns, and joins

Use the eICU 2.0 snapshot [source checksum] and these read-only files:

- `patient.csv.gz` (`patient`): `patientunitstayid`, `uniquepid`, `hospitalid`, `age`, `unitvisitnumber`, `unitdischargeoffset`.
- `respiratoryCharting.csv.gz` (`respiratoryCharting`): `patientunitstayid`, `respchartoffset`, `respchartvaluelabel`, `respchartvalue`.
- `respiratoryCare.csv.gz` (`respiratoryCare`): `patientunitstayid`, `respcarestatusoffset`, `airwaytype`.
- `vitalPeriodic.csv.gz` (`vitalPeriodic`): `patientunitstayid`, `observationoffset`, `sao2`, `respiration`, `systemicmean`.
- `vitalAperiodic.csv.gz` (`vitalAperiodic`): `patientunitstayid`, `observationoffset`, `noninvasivemean`.
- `carePlanEOL.csv.gz` (`carePlanEOL`): `patientunitstayid`, `cpleoldiscussionoffset`.
- `carePlanGeneral.csv.gz` (`carePlanGeneral`): `patientunitstayid`, `cplitemoffset`, `cplgroup`, `cplitemvalue`.

Join all event tables to `patient.patientunitstayid`. Select first qualifying stays by `patient.uniquepid`, ordered by `unitvisitnumber`, then `patientunitstayid`.

### Population and temporal boundaries

Generate landmarks L = 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes before reading target labels. Require age >=18 and `unitdischargeoffset > L`.

Strict physiology uses only observations before L:

- latest normalized FiO2 in `[L-360,L)`, valid 0.21-1.00 (or 21-100 divided by 100), <=0.50;
- latest PEEP/PEEP-CPAP in `[L-360,L)`, valid 0-30, <=8;
- at least two valid SpO2 values (40-100) in `[L-120,L)`, median >=88 and <100;
- latest valid respiratory rate (0-100) in `[L-120,L)`, 8-35;
- median of valid invasive/noninvasive MAP values (20-200) in `[L-120,L)`, >=65.

Independent invasive-ventilation evidence requires contemporaneous FiO2 and PEEP plus either an `RT Vent On/Off` value of on/continued/start/started in `[L-360,L)`, or the latest `respiratoryCare.airwaytype` before L containing ETT, endotracheal, or trach. This rule does not use any care-plan ventilation state.

Exclude positive EOL discussion before L and `Spontaneous - adequate` in `[L-1440,L)`. Among stays with at least one qualifying landmark, choose the first stay per person without target-label conditioning and retain all qualifying landmarks in it.

Only after selection, inspect `carePlanGeneral` where `cplgroup='Ventilation'` in `[L,L+360]`. Classify the earliest target as evaluation (`Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`) or no-trial (`Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`); contradictory same-time targets are conflicts. If there is no target but another Ventilation value, classify other-ventilation-value; if no Ventilation row, classify no-target-label. Separately flag whether `unitdischargeoffset > L+360`.

### Positive control and analyses

Reconstruct the inherited target-conditioned cohort with its original care-plan-or-independent invasive evidence, restrictions, and first-stay rule. Require exact identity against the frozen 540 rows on stay, person, hospital, landmark, strategy, and decision offset.

Report attrition, receipt classes overall and by landmark, complete-window sensitivity, hospital coverage, hospital eta-squared, target HHI, selected-stay overlap, and equal-landmark hospital receipt rates. The prespecified stringent common-support diagnostic requires at least 10 denominator rows in each of all five landmark cells per hospital.

## Executed results

All seven sources were fully scanned, with catalog-consistent row counts: 200,859 patient; 20,168,176 respiratoryCharting; 865,381 respiratoryCare; 146,671,642 vitalPeriodic; 25,075,074 vitalAperiodic; 3,115,018 carePlanGeneral; and 1,433 carePlanEOL rows.

The positive control passed exactly: 540/540 rows, 443 stays, 74 hospitals, 309 evaluation and 231 no-trial labels, landmark counts 129/147/111/77/76, with zero missing or extra six-field identities.

Of 318,461 adult in-unit candidate landmarks, 24,109 passed strict physiology, 17,038 had independent invasive evidence, 5,490 had both, and 5,136 remained after exclusions. Target-independent first-stay selection yielded 4,685 landmarks in 2,576 stays/persons and 89 hospitals.

Receipt was sparse: 89 evaluation (1.90%), 35 no-trial (0.75%), 281 other ventilation values (6.00%), and 4,280 no target label (91.36%); no target conflicts occurred. Combined target receipt was 124/4,685 (2.65%). Restricting to 4,625 fully observed six-hour windows changed it minimally to 123/4,625 (2.66%). Receipt rates at 24/48/72/96/120 hours were 2.53%, 3.67%, 2.50%, 1.72%, and 2.47%.

Only 25/89 denominator hospitals supplied any target label; 64 supplied none. Only one hospital had >=10 target rows and only one had >=10 of each label. Hospital eta-squared was 0.0519 for target receipt and 0.1277 for evaluation versus no-trial conditional on receipt. Target-receipt HHI was 0.3595 (effective hospital count 2.78); hospital 420 contributed 73/124 targets (58.9%). Under the stringent common-support restriction, 12 hospitals had >=10 denominator rows at every landmark; their equal-landmark standardized receipt rates still ranged from 0 to 7.36% (median 0.85%). Thus hospital dependence persists after denominator-aware landmark standardization, while landmark receipt itself is low and nonmonotonic rather than showing the inherited 120-hour reversal as an estimable outcome effect.

Selection changed materially: only 116/443 inherited stays remained selected; 2,460 target-independent stays were new and 327 inherited stays were not selected. Exactly 124 frozen stay-landmark keys appeared in the new population.

## Falsification and interpretation

The broad-coverage hypothesis is adverse/falsified: structured target receipt covers only 2.65% of target-independent eligible landmarks and is concentrated in very few hospitals. This supports interpreting the inherited 540-row cohort as a highly selected documentation subset, not a broadly representative ventilated denominator.

A supportive result would have required substantial target receipt across most denominator hospitals, stable rates across landmarks, and little residual dispersion under common-support standardization. An inconclusive result would arise from positive-control failure, unstable independent-ventilation ascertainment, or insufficient denominator support; the identity control passed, but the structured ventilation proxy still requires clinical validation.

No result establishes actual SBT receipt, extubation evaluation, bedside action, quality, causality, or benefit. No-target-label is not no trial. Airway/settings evidence is not adjudicated continuous invasive ventilation. Durable liberation remains unascertainable; inherited 12-hour setting and sedation proxies remain adverse/unstable, peak-pressure dominated, and hospital/landmark dependent and were not revived. Stronger clinical conclusions require note/chart adjudication, validated ventilation episodes and treatment timing, and a separate causal study.

## Verification boundaries

Computationally checkable claims include source row counts and hashes, cohort attrition, exact positive-control identity, class counts, temporal-window rules, first-stay selection, overlap, hospital coverage, eta-squared, HHI, and common-support standardized rates. Whether the source fields denote true continuous invasive ventilation, actual trials/evaluations, clinician decisions, or care quality requires clinical adjudication and cannot be established automatically.
