# Transport-envelope and observation-opportunity stress test for the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

This is a substantive successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead-frozen schema exactly: 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons, 74 hospitals; the first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`; landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission; the first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`; `D = cplitemoffset`; inherited pre-landmark exclusions; repeated landmarks clustered by `patientunitstayid`; exact labels; fixed B/E/T windows, parser, thresholds, and descriptive noncausal estimand.

The strongest available evidence supports only that the two literal care-plan strings are sparse, hospital-concentrated structured documentation and that earlier chart-proxy associations are unstable. It does not establish that either string denotes an SBT, an extubation evaluation, clinician intent, delivered ventilator settings, readiness, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or a causal intervention. The unresolved claim is narrower and falsifiable: does adding the exact label to pre-`D` respiratory state and documentation opportunity improve prediction of an independently charted, persistent three-class respiratory-support trajectory, and does that increment survive transport across hospitals after separating documentation opportunity and competing ICU exits from respiratory-state information?

The substantive advance is a prespecified **transport envelope** and a sharper **opportunity-conditioned falsification**. The parent primary all-row five-state estimand and classifiable trajectory display remain unchanged. In addition to the parent leave-one-hospital-out metrics, the compiler will (i) quantify how much any label increment is driven by a few hospitals or by label/observation composition using predeclared site-balanced summaries and influence diagnostics, and (ii) run a second, more stringent within-hospital-by-landmark permutation that also preserves pre-`D` opportunity strata. This tests whether a label signal survives when the null retains the documentation process that can otherwise manufacture apparent transport. No result-dependent site selection, endpoint redefinition, or bedside interpretation is permitted.

## Hospital transport envelope (new secondary robustness layer)

The primary transport analysis remains leave-one-hospital-out, with no hospital predictor, held-out target-rate calibration, or held-out hospital-specific intercept. For each hospital fold, fit M0 and M1 on all other hospitals and produce held-out probabilities for all five K states. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss and Brier score, calibration where finite, and separate metrics for all-row K, classifiable trajectories, competing exits, and non-exit unobservability. Repeated landmarks stay together in every uncertainty resample.

In addition, calculate a prespecified site-balanced transport envelope from held-out fold outputs, without changing the primary micro estimand:

1. **Hospital support table.** For all 74 hospitals, report rows, distinct stays, both arm counts, each K-state count, classifiable non-competing counts, pre-D opportunity distributions, post-D opportunity distributions, exit timing bins (`<=D+720`, `(D+720,D+2160]`), and valid endpoint/entry-clock counts. Keep hospitals with zero or one arm in descriptive tables; do not create a contrast for them.
2. **Eligible-site contrast set.** Define eligible sites before inspecting effect direction as hospitals with at least 10 rows per exact arm among classifiable non-competing rows and at least 10 rows per arm for the all-row model where that target is evaluated. Report the number and fraction of all rows represented. If fewer than five sites meet the classifiable criterion, the site-balanced trajectory envelope is unavailable/inconclusive, while the all-row accounting still proceeds.
3. **Equal-site and leave-one-site-out summaries.** For each eligible site, calculate the paired held-out M1-M0 loss increment and the descriptive arm contrast in K states. Summarize the unweighted median, equal-site mean, 10%-trimmed equal-site mean (only if at least 10 eligible sites), interquartile range, sign concordance, and the maximum absolute leave-one-site-out change. These are robustness summaries, not a new causal or population estimand. A site may not be removed because its direction is unfavorable.
4. **Predeclared transport gate.** Do not describe the label increment as transport-robust unless at least five eligible sites each contain at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows per arm, they represent at least 50% of all classifiable non-competing rows, both the micro and equal-site held-out loss increments favor M1, and no more than 25% of eligible sites reverse the prespecified favorable direction. Otherwise label the finding adverse or inconclusive according to support and uncertainty, never as evidence of no bedside meaning.
5. **Influence and composition diagnostic.** Recompute the aggregate with each site omitted and compare label prevalence, K-state proportions, opportunity distributions, and exit proportions. A large change caused by one site or by arm/site opportunity imbalance is a transport warning. It is not corrected by post-hoc reweighting; a fixed site-balanced summary is reported instead.

The primary parent gate remains in force: no general transportability claim if fewer than five hospitals each have at least 10 classifiable, non-competing rows per arm and those hospitals contain at least 50% of all such rows. The new envelope makes the consequences of barely passing that gate visible rather than allowing an aggregate metric to conceal site concentration.

## Opportunity-preserving falsification and competing-exit robustness

Run all tests from machine-readable artifacts before interpreting Δ or M1-M0.

* **Opportunity-stratified label permutation (new).** Within hospital-by-landmark strata, permute labels only among rows sharing a pre-D opportunity stratum. Opportunity strata are formed once in each training partition from prespecified quantile bins of a scalar pre-D opportunity score (row count, distinct clinical times, and missingness indicators; no outcome, post-D value, or label), with deterministic collapsing of sparse bins. Preserve arm counts, landmark structure, and stay clusters. Run this in addition to the parent hospital-by-landmark permutation. Require at least 20 valid nonempty strata and record attempted/valid strata and permutations; fewer is unavailable/inconclusive, not a null. Compare observed increments with both null distributions. If the apparent increment is typical under the opportunity-preserving null, it is adverse to a state-information interpretation even if the broad null is unfavorable.
* **Competing-exit decomposition.** Report all-row K probabilities and label contrasts separately for exits by timing (`<=D+720` versus `(D+720,D+2160]`), exit status/location, invalid exit time, non-exit unobservable, and the three classifiable states. Never censor, impute, or reassign an exit to a respiratory class. An M1 gain confined to exit or non-exit-unobservable states is documentation/exit information only. If an exit timing or subtype is unavailable, report that missingness rather than infer death, transfer, or respiratory failure.
* **Placebos and temporal falsification.** Repeat the exact component rules for placebo `(D-1440,D-720]` versus B and shift the exposure time to `D-720` while retaining the locked outcome window, as parent sensitivities. A comparable/larger placebo increment or site-direction reversal is adverse to a newly informative post-label interpretation.
* **Source and component ablation.** Remove each of FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, set rate, vital safety, and airway corroboration in turn. The result is not supportive if it survives only one source, one component, one site, or an invalid permutation stratum. The 24-hour late-horizon, non-peak safety, airway-corroborated, non-corroborated, and fixed pre-D observation-weighted sensitivities remain named before execution and retain attrition.

## Uncertainty and interpretation gates

Use stay-cluster bootstrap, fixed seed `61061`, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates, refitting the complete held-out pipeline; report attempted, valid, failed, and effective-cluster counts. Use paired metrics and paired row/site summaries. No row-level bootstrap. For the site-balanced envelope, bootstrap eligible stay clusters within sites and report how many replicates lose site support; do not silently replace an unsupported replicate with a row bootstrap. Clustered intervals are descriptive uncertainty, not proof of clinical effect.

Supportive feasibility requires at least 40% of all 540 rows classifiable and at least 10 hospitals with both exact labels among classifiable rows. Below 20% classifiable or below five such hospitals is adverse feasibility; intermediate is inconclusive. Total-state accounting failure, parent hash mismatch, post-D leakage, duplicate joins, or endpoint/parser integrity failure is invalid. A computationally supportive label-information result requires M1 to improve both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss for all-row K and the classifiable trajectory in the unbounded primary plus at least two frozen endpoint/observation sensitivities; the stay-cluster favorable interval must exclude zero, no more than 25% of estimable held-out hospitals may reverse direction, and the opportunity-preserving permutation must not make the observed increment typical. Agreement of evaluation-minus-no-trial de-escalation direction with the stringent safety/airway sensitivity in at least two prespecified overlap hospitals is required. A gain limited to exit, non-exit unobservability, source density, entry lag, or the as-entered clock is adverse to a respiratory-state interpretation. Failed support, failed positivity, unavailable permutation, or missing as-entered clock is inconclusive rather than negative evidence.

Supportive output establishes only incremental prediction of this structured eICU chart construct. Adverse output indicates that added label information is not robust for this endpoint/snapshot or is explainable by observation/exit composition; it does not establish absence of bedside evaluation, harm, or lack of treatment. Inconclusive output identifies insufficient classifiability, sparse site overlap, parent artifact failure, parser instability, unavailable clocks, or inadequate opportunity support. Bedside semantics and stronger clinical claims require adjudicated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, synchronized device logs, local interface dictionaries, complete workflow data, post-ICU follow-up, expert review, and external/prospective replication.
