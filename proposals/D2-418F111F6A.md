# Context-specific intervention response: working proposal

## Question
Does the intervention-associated transcript response in fibroblast–macrophage systems depend on the cellular context, and can that dependence identify a target whose modulation is more likely to alter a pathogenic fibroblast program than a protective macrophage-linked state?

## Current evidence status
This is a hypothesis-stage proposal, not an executed result. The seed D2-T1 names GSE95588 and E-MTAB-8316 as inputs and requires donor-aware matched contrasts and controls. The domain guide confirms that GSE95588 has prepared count matrices and design metadata, and E-MTAB-8316 has four FLS donor clusters, six nested monocyte donors, and twelve condition samples. ## Estimand
The primary estimand is the donor-aware matched contrast in the intervention-associated transcript response between the intervention and vehicle/unstimulated conditions, stratified by cellular context (fibroblast vs. macrophage). The secondary estimand is the context-specific effect size for each candidate target, with uncertainty estimated from the donor-level variation.

## Competing explanations
1. The observed context dependence could reflect a general transcriptional response to the intervention rather than a context-specific therapeutic target.
2. The observed context dependence could be a technical artifact of the assay, such as differences in library preparation or sequencing depth.
3. The observed context dependence could reflect a difference in donor composition rather than a context-specific therapeutic target.

## Method comparison
- Baseline: Donor-aware matched contrast with paired exploratory inference, averaging repeated context measurements within each donor/condition.
- Alternative: A mechanistic model that incorporates the known fibroblast–macrophage interaction network to predict the context-specific response, compared against the baseline to assess whether the network structure improves the identification of context-specific targets.

## Falsification criteria
- A null or uniform response across contexts would falsify the context-dependence claim.
- A response that is not specific to the pathogenic fibroblast program but is present in both contexts would falsify the target-specificity claim.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes
- Supportive: A significant context-specific response in the pathogenic fibroblast program that is not present in the protective macrophage-linked state.
- Adverse: A uniform response across contexts, or a response that is not specific to the pathogenic fibroblast program.
- Inconclusive: A response that is present in both contexts but with different magnitudes, requiring further investigation to determine the biological significance.
