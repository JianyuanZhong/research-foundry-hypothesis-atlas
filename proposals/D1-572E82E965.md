# HCC nested trajectory increment beyond the day-42 snapshot

Status: substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. Design only. No cohort size, prevalence, fitted coefficient, loss, uncertainty interval, endpoint adjudication result, or clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Scientific opening and exact advance

The inherited proposal asks whether pre-index-to-day-42 assay trajectories add information beyond baseline, observation opportunity, and workflow. Its remaining boundary is narrower and decision-relevant:

> Among eligible HCC patients with a common index TACE and complete observation through day 42, does the *ordered path* of eight routine assay channels from the pre-index anchor through day 42 improve held-out prediction of a later recorded repeat-TACE event beyond baseline/history, measured opportunity/workflow, and the latest available day-42 snapshot?

The strongest evidence-supported claim is only that treatment-adjacent recorded information can be predictive and that recorded HCC outcomes require ascertainment and response-label caution [K1][K2][K3]. It is not established that a change is biologic, that it adds beyond the latest value, or that an apparent increment is independent of late documentation or clinician selection.

This child makes the test strictly nested. The inherited notation allowed the trajectory block to replace the latest snapshot, so its direct contrast could mix snapshot information with trajectory information. Here every trajectory model retains the same `A_snap` block. A second repair is algebraic: for a numeric assay, raw Δ = S42 - B0 is already determined by the baseline and latest snapshot. Therefore raw terminal change cannot by itself establish new temporal information in a linear model. The scientific increment is separated into:

1. a prespecified endpoint-change representation (nonlinear change basis and supported qualitative/inequality transition; a raw-Δ audit is also run but cannot be credited as independent information); and
2. intermediate ordered-path features, such as recovery-then-worsening, reversal count, early-versus-late direction, and time of an extremum, which are not determined by the two endpoint values.

The result can support only reproducible predictive/descriptive information about a recorded operational event. It cannot establish TACE intent, tumor response, treatment efficacy, toxicity, survival, mechanism, or a treatment policy.

## Population, time origin, and outcomes

Let t0 be the first eligible index procedure start for a patient. Normalize Unicode, trim and case-fold `surgery`; require it to equal `TACE`, and require nonmissing `patient master index`, `visit number`, and `start time`. Collapse exact duplicate patient/start index/event rows for counting, retaining duplicate counts and raw-label hashes. The patient is eligible only when the inherited all-source observation construction supports follow-up through t0+42 days. The reserved catalog partition is not an external validation cohort.

The predictor construction window is [t0-365 days, t0+42 days]. The baseline assay anchor uses [t0-365,t0); the post-index assay window is (t0,t0+42]. Latest values in the latter are S42, not necessarily measured exactly on day 42. Ordered knots are (t0,t0+14], (t0+14,t0+28], and (t0+28,t0+42]. Records after t0+42 are never predictors.

Exclude a literal later TACE with 0 < elapsed days < 43 from the later-event risk set; flag it as early-alias history rather than silently treating it as a negative. Follow-up begins at t0+43 and ends at the first event, inherited all-source `obs_end`, or t0+181, with fixed [43,90] and [91,181) sensitivities. Untimed candidate events are flagged and never imputed as timed events.

The event hierarchy is fixed:

- **O0 primary:** first later unique patient/start literal-code TACE with 43 <= elapsed days < 181.
- **O1 endpoint-validity boundary:** first later procedure satisfying the frozen semantic hepatic-arterial embolization predicate, with valid procedure start, same-patient/same-visit encounter linkage, and at least one timed same-visit examination or order corroborator in [t-42,t+7]. The event time is the procedure start, not the corroborator.
- **O2 secondary specificity check:** O1 plus the frozen same-visit medication predicate in [t-1,t+1].

A qualifying semantic procedure without corroboration is an O1/O2 near-miss and is negative only when its window is observed. O2 cannot rescue an unsupported O1. None of O0/O1/O2 is called response or intent. The solver must report duplicates, untimed rows, early aliases, near misses, risk-set denominators, and corroborator routes.

## Feature blocks and nested estimand

The blocks are constructed without complete-case exclusion.

- M0: pre-index age, sex, department; dated encounter/admission history; dated pre-index procedure counts; matched-encounter diagnosis counts where the encounter is dated before t0; and assay-agnostic pre-index utilization/history. No narrative text is silently converted to severity.
- B0: latest valid pre-index anchor per assay label, with numeric, qualitative, inequality/censoring and specimen-content fields kept distinct.
- Pobs: assay opportunity, per-label counts, gaps, missingness, timestamp coverage and post-index availability. This is never included in the path-content block.
- Pcare: dated non-TACE procedure, non-drug order, medication and encounter-process marks from t0 through t0+42, excluding candidate outcome rows and excluding assay values. It represents measured workflow, not intent.
- S42: the latest post-index assay record per exact label through t0+42, with numeric and nonnumeric content separated.
- Cterm: development-fitted, label-specific nonlinear basis for supported numeric S42-B0 change plus supported qualitative/inequality transitions. Raw Δ is retained as an audit feature. No assay units, reference ranges, or clinical thresholds are invented.
- Porder: intermediate knots and path shape only: early/middle/late direction, monotonicity, one-reversal and two-reversal flags, recovery-then-worsening/worsening-then-recovery flags, first-extremum knot, absolute-deviation knot, and terminal-direction discordance. These are recording patterns, not biologic states. Missing knots are explicit availability indicators already in Pobs; neutral imputation is fold-fitted.

Define:

- `A0 = M0 + B0 + Pobs + Pcare`
- `Asnap = A0 + S42`
- `Aterm = Asnap + Cterm`
- `Apath = Asnap + Cterm + Porder`

The primary nested question is `Asnap -> Apath`. `Asnap -> Aterm` separates endpoint-change/nonlinearity from intermediate ordering. The raw-Δ-only version is run as an algebraic audit: with B0 and S42 present, its scientific increment should be zero for an unrestricted linear representation; a nonzero implementation result is a leakage or feature-construction defect, not evidence for biology. This explicit audit prevents a representation artifact from being called trajectory information.

For endpoint j in {O0,O1,O2}, fit pooled discrete-time hazards over the observed follow-up days and compute patient-level held-out cumulative-risk predictions. Let L_j(A) be ordinary and inherited observation/censoring-weighted log loss, with weights fitted inside each development fold. The contrasts are:

- `D_term,j = L_j(Asnap) - L_j(Aterm)`
- `D_order,j = L_j(Aterm) - L_j(Apath)`
- `D_path,j = L_j(Asnap) - L_j(Apath)`

Positive values mean added held-out predictive information, not a causal effect. Brier/integrated Brier, calibration slope/intercept, calibration plots, discrimination, risk-set counts, effective sample size and weight tails are secondary diagnostics. No small loss improvement is automatically a clinical MCID.

## Baseline, split, fitting and uncertainty

Use the inherited deterministic earliest-80%/latest-20% eligible-index-date patient-disjoint split and five grouped development folds. Freeze within each development fold the label dictionary, duplicate and numeric parser, censoring/inequality encoding, transition rules, scaling, imputation, hazard discretization, observation/censoring models, model-selection rule, and falsification permutations. Touch the held-out test set once for final paired outputs.

Primary model: pooled-logistic discrete-time hazards with elastic-net regularization. Select penalty and elastic-net mixing only within development folds; use identical folds and outcome rows for all nested blocks. The transparent model is primary because coefficient/ablation paths expose whether the same measured severity, workflow, endpoint content, endpoint-change, and order blocks drive the contrast.

Matched learned alternative: a compact time-aware GRU over the same allowed event stream from t0-365 through t0+42. Each event has assay label or process-channel identity, numeric/qualitative/inequality content, relative time, time since prior event, and a tag identifying B0, S42, Pobs, Pcare, Cterm, or Porder. Use separate O0/O1/O2 hazard heads and the same Asnap/Aterm/Apath ablations, patient split, weights, held-out outcomes, losses, bootstrap and falsification controls. Use three fixed development seeds. The GRU can reveal nonlinear cross-assay and irregular ordered-path information lost by prespecified summaries; it cannot supply intent, imaging, units, outside-care events or causal separation.

Selection: elastic-net is confirmatory; GRU is a bounded representation sensitivity. If GRU supports an increment but elastic-net does not, call the result representation-sensitive and do not claim a robust trajectory state. Agreement strengthens reproducibility only. A small predictive gain is not a reason to replace the transparent analysis.

Uncertainty is a paired patient bootstrap over held-out predictions, with one simultaneous max-|t| family for the overall O0/O1/O2 path contrasts, term/order decompositions, and fixed falsifiers. Report ordinary and weighted estimates, risk-set and event counts, availability, ESS, censoring and weight tails. Unsupported O1/O2 or unstable pairings are INCONCLUSIVE, not dropped.

## Concrete falsification of late documentation

Run each control once on the frozen held-out procedure and outcome definitions:

1. **Day-28 lead-time erasure:** reconstruct S28 and the ordered path using only records through t0+28; separately remove all labs/process records in (t0+35,t0+42]. Use the identical folds, models and outcome horizon. A path increment that disappears only after this erasure is adverse to a durable pre-index-to-day-42 interpretation and compatible with late review/documentation.
2. **Timestamp/value permutation:** within patient and assay label, preserve the observed timestamps, number of records, missingness and qualitative/inequality pattern but permute result content among admissible time points in development-frozen strata. A matched increment after value permutation indicates timing/recording structure rather than assay content. A separate label permutation tests generic assay volume.
3. **Process-only accounting:** compare Pobs+Pcare alone, Asnap, Aterm and Apath. If process-only information matches the path increment within the simultaneous uncertainty/resolution rule, the workflow rival is favored.
4. **Pre-procedure/horizon controls:** repeat with same-visit corroborators restricted to [t-42,t-1], [t-28,t-1] and [t-7,t-1], and with [43,90] and [91,181) horizons. These are timing controls, not causal tests.
5. **Documentation limitation:** HCC labs have `test time` but no validated collection-versus-entry timestamp. Therefore survival of these controls cannot fully falsify delayed entry or a clinician’s unrecorded review. The strongest remaining documentation claim requires audit-log or independently timestamped collection data.

## Concrete falsification of measured severity selection

The severity rival says a path merely marks patients selected for repeat care by severity incompletely captured at baseline. Use three prespecified checks:

1. Build a development-only baseline acuity score from M0, B0, pre-index utilization, prior TACE/procedure counts, index department, encounter/admission duration and pre-index diagnosis coverage; calibrate inverse-probability weights for having an observed S42/path using baseline variables only. Refit the paired held-out contrasts under these weights.
2. Report Dterm, Dorder and Dpath within development-frozen tertiles of baseline acuity and within strata of prior TACE count and index inpatient/department route, requiring minimum support before a stratum is interpreted. No favorable stratum replaces the overall estimand.
3. Compare the all-patient missing-indicator analysis with complete-case analysis. A large complete-case-only increment that attenuates under baseline availability weighting is adverse to a trajectory-specific interpretation and supports selection/measurement opportunity as the explanation.

Persistence after measured baseline weighting and across supported acuity strata falsifies only the *measured* severity-selection explanation. It cannot rule out unmeasured tumor burden, clinician preference, scheduling, outside care or treatment intent. A conclusion beyond that requires expert adjudication and richer prospective data.

## Supportive, adverse and inconclusive gates

Before held-out scoring, freeze one resolution rule: a contrast is positive only if its simultaneous bootstrap lower bound is above zero and its support/weight audits pass. Require at least 50 overall O1/O2 events when interpreted, at least 15 development and 15 test events in any reported stratum, ESS >=100, nondegenerate observation/censoring support, no weight-tail failure, and adequate paired Asnap/Apath overlap.

- **SUPPORTIVE computational trajectory increment:** O0 and O1 pass support gates; Dpath is positive for both; Dpath for O1 remains positive versus Asnap; Dorder is positive or the learned/transparent representations agree on the same ordered-path direction; the day-28/late-record control does not erase the increment; value permutation is null; and process-only or baseline-severity-weighted controls do not explain it. This supports reproducible recorded temporal information beyond the latest snapshot. It does not establish clinical utility, biology, intent, response, or benefit.
- **ADVERSE to trajectory interpretation:** Dpath is absent after Asnap, Dpath is absent while Asnap is supported, the increment survives value permutation, is erased by late-record removal, is matched by process-only features, or attenuates to null after measured severity/availability weighting. Retain a narrower snapshot or workflow result when supported; do not say assays have no prognostic value.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** O1/O2 support fails, paired overlap/ESS is inadequate, uncertainty is wide, censoring/weights fail, or model representations disagree without a decisive falsifier. Freeze the direction and obtain better timing, adjudicated intent/response, or independent validation; do not select a favorable assay, subgroup or model.

A positive computational gate is not a claim that the information is clinically meaningful. Clinical meaningfulness requires an expert-specified decision threshold, prospective utility/harms assessment, or external validation. Those are not available here; the actual deliverable must label this residual limitation explicitly.

## Alternative-method record and resource plan

A transparent elastic-net hazard and a matched compact GRU address the same nested estimand, population, split, outcomes and uncertainty. The baseline loses irregular path order and nonlinear interactions; the GRU may reveal those, but its result is less auditable and can amplify documentation patterns. A larger transformer, NLP of examination/pathology/document text, latent treatment-intent model, and mechanistic liver-response model are deferred: timed intent, images, validated units/reference ranges and expert labels are unavailable. Revisit them only after those dependencies are supplied.

Planning estimates, not measured execution: the elastic-net materialization, fold fits, weighting, bootstrap and falsifiers are approximately 1–4 hours on 4 CPU and 16 GiB; the compact GRU is approximately 2–6 hours on 4 CPU and 16 GiB with one allocated A100, using `cuda:0` inside the allocation. The primary does not require a GPU. The GRU estimate remains unverified and depends on the configured CUDA/PyTorch image; a non-allocated shell CUDA result would not establish hardware absence.
