# Self-contained executable closure of the timing-aware cystatin-C allocation experiment

## Clinical decision, evidence boundary, and unresolved hypothesis

A health service with cystatin-C capacity for only 20% of adults whose creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) is 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m2 must decide whom to test. Three distinct information states matter: immediate allocation without UACR; a deliberately protocolized workflow that obtains and waits for a usable UACR before allocation; and the historical question whether UK Biobank UACR was actually available before an ordering decision.

The strongest evidence already supported is limited. Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041; frozen full-text XML source `[source checksum]` inspected) studied 468,969 UK Biobank participants and found that demographic, clinical, laboratory and lifestyle characteristics predict creatinine–cystatin eGFR differences; an enriched model had validation AUC 0.75. That supports association and internal prediction, not fixed-capacity policy value, measured GFR, UACR chronology, persistent CKD, clinical action or benefit. This episode’s physical-header audit confirms all required fields and confirms no collection/result/allocation timestamps. Inherited full-row feasibility work found O=134,118 and usable censor-aware instance-0 UACR in 130,257 (97.12%); no locked policy outcome was opened.

The unresolved hypothesis remains:

> At an exact cystatin-C capacity of floor(0.20×|O|), the development-frozen one-column raw-grip policy G will replicate superior biochemical target yield over B, the equal-dimensional generic-function comparator F, and lowest-eGFRcr-first T in both locked partitions, with N18-specific target-positive enrichment when event-sufficient. Conditional on a future service explicitly waiting for a usable UACR, AG_V will replicate incremental yield over A_V and G_V will be noninferior to A_V, while every all-O gate remains mandatory.

The quantitative gates below make this falsifiable. The actual historical pre-order UACR hypothesis is not testable: the required urine collection time, UACR result-release time and allocation/order time are absent.

## Exact common model implementation

For each continuous B variable age, BMI and eGFRcr, compute four development-training quantiles q=.05,.35,.65,.95 by NumPy linear interpolation (R type 7). If knots are not strictly increasing, that model is noncomputable. With knots k1<k2<k3<k4, use columns x and, for j=1,2,

`r_j(x)=[(x-k_j)_+^3-(x-k_3)_+^3(k_4-k_j)/(k_4-k_3)+(x-k_4)_+^3(k_3-k_j)/(k_4-k_3)]/(k_4-k_1)^2`.

B is intercept, sex, and x,r1,r2 for age, BMI and eGFRcr. For each fit, center and divide every non-intercept design column by its training-sample mean and population SD; a zero SD makes the model noncomputable. Apply frozen scaling to validation/lock rows. This transformation includes binary sex and is identical across policies containing B.

Fit ridge logistic regression by minimizing mean negative log likelihood plus `lambda×sum(beta_j^2)/2`, excluding the intercept from penalty. Candidate lambda values are exactly `10^k, k=-6,-5,…,6`. Assign the common five development folds by `int(SHA256(utf8("ukb-grip-cys-cv-v1|"+canonical_eid)),16)%5`. Within each CV training fold recompute its knots/scaling and fit each candidate lambda; score held-out mean log loss. Choose the largest lambda whose mean loss is within one standard error across the five held-out fold losses of the minimum. Ties at machine precision choose larger lambda. Refit on all development L with all-development transforms. Use float64 and require converged penalized-log-likelihood gradient infinity norm <=1e-7; otherwise the policy is noncomputable. These rules replace every earlier unspecified grid, spline parameterization, fold assignment or one-SE implementation.

All learned policy scores are fitted probabilities. Rank descending score and break exact score ties by ascending integer SHA256 of `"ukb-grip-cys-tie-v3|"+canonical_eid`.
