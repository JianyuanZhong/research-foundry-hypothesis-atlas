## Evidence boundary, hypothesis, and clinical advance

The strongest currently supported claim is that heterogeneous conservative-fluid/active-deresuscitation bundles can lower accumulated balance and may improve respiratory outcomes. They do not establish that starting a delivered IV loop at this precise post-shock decision causes durable liberation or is renally safe. The inspected full text of the 2026 RADAR-2 secondary analysis (McMullan et al., DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404`, frozen source `[source checksum]`) reports no statistically detectable kidney/perfusion-marker differences but emphasizes modest sample size and imprecision. The Silversides 2017 review and primary RADAR-2 report remain indexed evidence in the parent; their full texts were not inspected here. No claim is made from the unavailable main cancer demonstration paper.

The unresolved falsifiable hypothesis is: **among invasively ventilated adults at ICU hour 48 who have recovered from vasopressor-treated shock, have a unit-safe recorded cumulative balance at least +50 mL/kg, retain urine output, and have potassium at least 3.0 mmol/L, initiating/re-initiating actually delivered ICU IV furosemide or bumetanide within 24 hours increases the lower-bound probability of being alive and durably liberated from invasive ventilation at day 7 by more than 5 percentage points versus withholding every loop for 24 hours, without excess recorded death or observed new RRT.**

Resolution could determine whether this precise strategy/population merits a pragmatic randomized trial. This MIMIC experiment cannot establish efficacy, renal safety, standard care, or a treatment recommendation because indication, goals of care, residual confounding, ward events, and post-discharge events are incompletely observed.

## Prespecified result meanings

**Supportive:** `RD_L` point >+0.05 and its 95% lower limit >0; upper 95% excess recorded 28-day death <+0.03; upper 95% excess observed day-7 RRT <+0.05 under both date brackets; expected urine/balance process direction; all feasibility/positivity/temporal tests pass; and no material temporal, fluid-definition, exposure, or endpoint sensitivity conflict. This supports external validation and trial prioritization only.

**Adverse:** 95% upper limit of `RD_U <=0`, mortality-harm lower bound >=+0.03, or observed RRT-harm lower bound >=+0.05 under the specified brackets. Better fluid removal cannot override durable-liberation failure, death, or RRT harm.

**Inconclusive:** `RD_L` fails support while `RD_U` permits benefit; sampling or identification interval is wide; either safety gate fails; final successes/arm overlap/ESS are sparse; date brackets disagree; the two-clock, fluid, exposure, or endpoint sensitivities materially conflict; temporal falsification fails; or observation/adjudication is incomplete. Nonsignificance is not equivalence or safety.
