# Early post-TACE biomarker kinetics and need for subsequent liver-directed treatment in HCC

## Question and hypothesis

In patients with hepatocellular carcinoma receiving a first observed transarterial chemoembolization (TACE)-type procedure, does the trajectory of AFP together with hepatic reserve during the first 30 days identify patients who will undergo another liver-directed intervention during days 31–180, beyond information available before the index procedure?

Hypothesis: a larger early AFP decline and stable/improving albumin, bilirubin and coagulation measures will be associated with a lower hazard of subsequent liver-directed intervention. A longitudinal model using these trajectories will improve discrimination, calibration and decision-curve net benefit over a pre-TACE baseline model containing the last AFP and clinical covariates. The claim is prognostic association, not causation, tumor response, survival benefit or treatment recommendation.

## Evidence boundary and clinical importance

The local snapshot has 105,044 encounters for 43,815 patients from 2010-07-27 through 2026-01-01; 336,570 diagnosis rows labelled 肝细胞癌; 27,411 经导管肝动脉栓塞术 rows, 26,385 动脉化疗栓 rows and 8,609 explicit TACE rows. The lab table contains 28,159,928 rows, including 190,022 AFP, 238,293 albumin, 238,553 total bilirubin, 297,730 INR-labelled coagulation, 253,588 platelet and 228,128 creatinine rows. These are availability facts, not evidence that kinetics predict outcome.

The unresolved clinical question is whether early, routinely observed laboratory dynamics add actionable information after TACE. If so, they could support a prospective strategy for prioritizing early review or imaging; if not, they would argue against using AFP kinetics alone to change surveillance. Repeat intervention is an observable management endpoint, not validated radiographic progression or survival.

## Population and time

Use the catalog's participant-level SHA-256 split: discovery buckets 0–79; reserved 80–99 are inaccessible and are not external validation.

Index is the earliest qualifying TACE-type procedure per patient, with procedure start from 2011-01-01 through 2025-06-30 to permit 180 days of follow-up. Qualifying procedures are procedures.手术 containing one of: 经导管肝动脉栓塞术, 动脉化疗栓, 肝动脉造影化疗栓塞术（TACE）, or a clearly equivalent combined TACE phrase. Save the exact dictionary and counts. Do not use angiography-only rows. The row must have procedures.开始时间 and a matching encounters row.

Require HCC by a diagnosis label containing 肝细胞癌 or 肝癌 at or before index, using the linked encounter time because diagnoses has no native time. A document-based definition is a sensitivity analysis only because the catalog's lexical detector is not a validated diagnosis extractor. Exclude anyone with an earlier qualifying TACE in observed history; this is first observed, not first-ever.

Primary landmark cohort requires a numeric AFP in labs.定量结果 during index minus 30 days through before index and at least one AFP in days 0–30. Use the nearest pre-index measurement and nearest day-30 measurement, with an all-measurements mixed-model sensitivity analysis. Restrict the primary endpoint to days 31–180. Patients with no valid landmark or impossible dates are reported, not silently dropped.

## Outcomes and analysis

Primary outcome is the first repeat liver-directed intervention in days 31–180 from procedures.手术 using a frozen dictionary for repeat TACE plus liver ablation, resection or transplant. Report repeat TACE separately. Secondary outcomes are repeat TACE alone, liver-directed imaging in examinations after day 30, an explicitly unadjudicated possible-progression flag from new metastasis/vascular-invasion labels or narrative/examination terms, and subsequent encounters. Do not call any of these recurrence, radiographic progression, survival or treatment response.

Fit a baseline Cox or equivalent discrete-time hazard model and a Bayesian joint longitudinal-survival model. The latter fits irregular AFP and reserve trajectories with patient random intercept/slope or a low-dimensional latent hepatic state linked to days 31–180 event hazard. Include calendar era and measurement intensity; use shrinkage and patient-level uncertainty. The scientific estimand is the conditional association/prediction of observed repeat treatment.

Retain a meaningful learned alternative: a small regularized GRU/transformer or gradient-boosted event-history model over ordered labs, diagnoses and procedures from the pre-index window through day 30, using the same inputs and split. It is useful only if it reveals nonlinear temporal interactions, not for a tiny AUC gain. The joint model is primary because it respects irregular sampling and gives interpretable trajectory uncertainty; the learned model is a planned alternative or fallback if the joint model is unstable.

Use patient-level train/validation/test partitions from the catalog, selecting hyperparameters only on train/validation. Report 90- and 180-day Brier score, time-dependent AUROC/PR-AUC, calibration slope/intercept, integrated calibration error, decision-curve net benefit and paired patient-level bootstrap intervals. Report cohort/event counts, missingness, effective sample size and performance by calendar era and measurement density. The solver must produce frozen cohort/event dictionaries, model artifacts, held-out predictions, uncertainty summaries, calibration/discrimination/decision tables and interpretation of supportive, adverse and inconclusive results.

Supportive evidence requires a prespecified meaningful trajectory association with interval excluding the null, good calibration, and robust held-out improvement over baseline. This supports a candidate risk-stratification signal only. Adverse evidence is null/opposite association, no incremental value, poor calibration, or instability after adjustment for baseline burden and measurement intensity. Inconclusive evidence includes too few landmark patients/events, assay mismatch, wide intervals spanning relevant benefit and harm, nonconvergence or coding/measurement domination.

## Demonstration dispositions and alternatives

Delphi supports using dated structured event ordering, but UKB/Danish cohorts and external validation are unavailable; a bounded HCC sequence comparator is appropriate, not reproduction. ALADYNOULLI supports a longitudinal latent-state idea, but verified genetics are unavailable; this proposal adapts the EHR-only trajectory component. Oncoformer cannot be reproduced because the HCC source has no images and the main paper/STAR Methods are unavailable; its lab-only concept is retained as a secondary comparator, not a multimodal claim.

Deferred alternatives are a causal TACE-versus-resection comparison, because treatment intent, stage, tumor burden and time-varying confounding are missing; a pure descriptive AFP responder study, because it loses the baseline incremental test; a full multimodal transformer, because images are unavailable; and a genetic latent model, because genetics are unavailable. The measured discovery scans used 4 CPUs/16 GB and completed in 31 seconds for six payload tables and 210 seconds for labs, medications and orders. The future solver planning envelope is up to 16 CPUs, 256 GB RAM and optional GPUs. Neural sequence cost is unmeasured; use a bounded CPU pilot or one allocated A100 job if needed. No GPU is required for the primary joint model.

## Stronger claims requiring new evidence

The local data lack images, raw waveforms, validated tumor stage/burden, treatment intent/dose, death linkage, transplant registry, external follow-up and expert radiology/pathology adjudication. Diagnoses and documents may be copied forward; documents/pathology lack native time; lab units are absent; vitals/transfers are identifier-only. Thus the experiment can establish only an evidence-bounded association/predictive comparison for observed repeat treatment in this snapshot. Claims about radiographic response, recurrence, mortality, causal TACE benefit, or changing care require expert review, death/registry linkage and external validation.
