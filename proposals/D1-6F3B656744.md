## Evidence boundary and clinical importance

The lineage already supports only bounded facts: the exact exported labels are sparse and hospital-concentrated; prior setting associations are heterogeneous and vulnerable to peak-pressure and documentation effects; and durable liberation, extubation readiness, actual SBT performance, device truth, and clinical intent are not validly available from these structured fields alone. Those facts motivate, but do not prove, the present hypothesis.

The clinically important unresolved question is whether a care-plan distinction has reproducible information about a later *persistent* trajectory rather than a transient charted setting. If supported, the result would justify only further validation of a structured-chart signal for research; it would not justify using the label for bedside decisions. If adverse, it would warn against treating the label as a transportable respiratory phenotype. If endpoint support or lineage fails, the scientifically useful result is an explicit feasibility/inconclusive finding, not a smaller convenience cohort.

## Fixed temporal endpoint and state machine

The inherited comparison windows are half-open:

* prior state `P = (D-1440, D-720]`;
* baseline `B = (D-720, D]`;
* primary post window `O = (D+360, D+1800]`.

The primary persistent endpoint additionally splits `O` into an early window `E = (D+360, D+1080]` and late window `T = (D+1080, D+1800]`. The six-hour washout after D is preserved. A component reduction is eligible only when its B, E, and T summaries all exist and both E and T are no greater than B, with the fixed raw-scale reductions FiO2 at least `0.05`, PEEP at least `2`, pressure support at least `2`, or set rate at least `2`. The primary support domain is at least one non-peak component. Thus an early transient cannot qualify as persistent.

Safety uses only `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration`. Each signal requires repeated, separated B, E, and T observations under the same parser contract. In both post windows, median SpO2 may not be more than 2 percentage points below the B median and median respiration may not be more than 4 breaths/min above the B median. Missing either signal in any required period is missing, never safe. The primary endpoint is `persistent_improvement` when at least one eligible non-peak reduction and both-window safety pass; a secondary diagnostic, declared in advance, reports the parent-compatible single-window endpoint in O and cannot replace the primary. `respiratoryCare` is not required for primary ascertainment because it is a sparse documentation/context table rather than device truth; its fixed sensitivities are reported separately.

For every frozen row, assign exactly one top-level state in this precedence order before any complete-window contrast:

1. `competing_exit`: nonmissing `patient.unitdischargeoffset <= D+1800`. Trimmed, case-insensitive `unitdischargestatus == Expired` is `death`; otherwise a classifiable status/location is `discharge_transfer`; an unclassifiable status/location is `truncation`. Record a separate `exit_before_or_at_D_plus_360` flag because there is no post-washout opportunity.
2. `endpoint_missing`: no competing exit, but a required repeated support, safety, or parser/source condition is absent. Record one mutually exclusive first reason and all reason flags: no B component, no E component, no T component, no B/E/T safety, time-tie conflict, parser rejection, source absence, or source error.
3. `persistent_improvement`: complete primary ascertainment, eligible persistent support reduction, and safety pass.
4. `observed_non_improvement`: all primary requirements complete but the improvement condition is false, including safety failure.

All 540 rows remain in the state table. Report state, every reason, support component availability, and exit status by arm, hospital, and landmark. The primary complete-observed risk difference is accompanied by the all-row state distribution and a sensitivity treating death and non-death exits as separate competing event classes. No arm-specific complete-case deletion is permitted. A binary sensitivity may use only pre-D inverse-observation weights whose specification is frozen before endpoint read and fitted within training folds; it may not use outcome-derived weights.

## Falsification, interpretation, and gates

Without changing parser, cohort, thresholds, or outcome state machine, run:

* the P-versus-B pre-D placebo;
* the same endpoint after shifting the decision reference to `D-720` as a lead-time placebo;
* leave-one-component-out and source-family ablations, including a fixed peak-pressure ablation;
* stricter 60-minute observation separation;
* the declared single-window secondary endpoint;
* documentation-density negative controls with no respiratory value content; and
* all declared respiratory-care recency/interval sensitivities.

A **supportive** result requires all of the following: parent identity and parser gates pass; endpoint-support and arm-overlap gates pass; the primary risk difference is positive with at least 4 of 5 estimable hospital directions positive; label-added prediction improves over identical state-plus-opportunity prediction by at least 0.01 in both micro and equal-hospital-macro held-out log loss, with a stay-clustered 95% interval excluding zero; the increment survives component/source ablations; both placebos are absent or smaller; the observed increment exceeds a valid permutation null; missingness and competing exits do not explain the value-based gain; and every interpreted airway sensitivity has at least 10% observation in each arm. This supports only incremental information in this structured-chart construct.

An **adverse** result requires adequate support and a prespecified failure such as no label increment over opportunity/state, placebo- or permutation-sized increment, reversal across hospitals, dependence on one source/component/hospital, or documentation-density gain without value-based trajectory gain. It is a negative finding against transportable label information, not proof that no clinician evaluated extubation, no trial occurred, or that the label is mere artifact.

The result is **inconclusive** if either inherited artifact cannot be matched exactly; any source/schema/hash or parser gate fails; fewer than five hospitals meet support; no held-out arm contrast is estimable; endpoint support, airway sensitivity support, or competing-exit separation is too sparse; missingness differs enough that ascertainment cannot be separated from state; bounded sensitivities disagree materially; the permutation strata are insufficient; or uncertainty spans both zero and the predeclared meaningful increment. A single-window positive with persistent failure is classified as transient/non-persistent association, not supportive of the persistent hypothesis. No relaxed threshold, post-hoc label expansion, pooled row bootstrap, post-outcome parser change, or convenience denominator is allowed.
