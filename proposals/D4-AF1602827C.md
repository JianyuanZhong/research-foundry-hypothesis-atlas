## Scientific question and substantive advance

The parent’s strongest supported design is a nested test of whether a prior panel adds conditional information for a later recorded two-domain transition, with a calendar-era no-refit stress test. Its unresolved interpretability is fundamental: the catalog does not verify that biomarker instance 1 was collected at the assessment date, and it does not provide individual observation end or complete five-year ascertainment. Therefore a negative future record cannot safely be called disease-free, and a positive association cannot safely be interpreted as prospective risk from a specimen measured before onset.

This successor changes the estimand rather than hiding that limitation:

> Among repeat-assessed participants without a recorded two-domain transition by T0, does retaining the earlier eight-field panel improve classification at T1 of a two-domain transition already recorded between T0 and T1, beyond the current panel and repeat spacing, while distinguishing death recorded by T1?

This informs a consequential but narrower decision: whether a repeat-assessment workflow should flag a record for immediate multimorbidity reconciliation or clinician/coding review. It does not estimate disease onset, future incidence, the effect of ordering a repeat test, or treatment benefit. Its advance over the parent is that the primary outcome is bounded by the observed T0/T1 assessment interval and requires no post-T1 ascertainment window. The parent’s five-year prospective estimand remains an explicitly gated secondary, not an assumed interpretation of this primary result.

## Evidence boundary and falsifiable hypothesis

Local evidence supports the presence of paired field-instance columns, assessment-date fields, position-matched inpatient diagnosis/date arrays, and death-date fields. Approved local metadata verifies age and sex only; it does not verify the eight biomarker identities, units, missing-value meanings, specimen collection times, biological onset, outpatient completeness, or individual observation end. Public field metadata for 41270/41280 supports their structural array interpretation, but is not clinical adjudication.

The strongest claim supported before fitting is only that a computationally reproducible recorded-transition label can be constructed if the exact code/date pairing and codebook gates pass. The unresolved claim is incremental, held-out record-triage information from the prior panel.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis:

> In the predeclared central-60% ambiguity band of current-only recorded-transition risk, M1b (current panel + age + sex + T1 year + repeat spacing + prior panel) has a lower held-out multiclass Brier score than M1a (the same inputs without the prior panel), and its transition-class top-k review recall at a fixed review capacity is higher, with the ambiguity-band increment more favorable than the equal-weight average of the lower and upper risk tails. The result must be directionally consistent in early T1 assessments through 31 December 2012 and in a no-refit 2013 assessment set.

This is falsifiable: the primary claim fails if the prior increment is nonnegative, the ambiguity-versus-tail contrast is not negative, top-k recall does not improve, or the effect reverses in 2013. A positive result supports retention of prior values for this recorded-review task only.

## Estimand and labels

Let T0=53-0.0 and T1=53-1.0; these are assessment landmarks, not specimen dates. Let L=(T1-T0)/365.2425. For each domain d in C, R, M, let F_d be the earliest valid paired 41280-j date among all j=0,...,258 whose same-position 41270-j code is in that domain. The recorded transition date is:

F* = min{max(F_C,F_R), max(F_C,F_M), max(F_R,F_M)}

over finite domain pairs. Codes retain subcodes after only the approved representation normalization.

For an eligible participant, define the assessment-bounded three-class label Z:

- R (recorded transition): F* is strictly after T0 and on or before T1;
- D (death by landmark): the earliest nonmissing 40000-0.0/40000-1.0 death date is on or before T1 and is earlier than, or tied with, F*; death wins exact ties;
- N: no qualifying F* and no death record on or before T1.

A verified F* on or before T0 excludes the participant. A nonmissing candidate-domain code with a missing, malformed, or out-of-range same-position date is unknown-time and is excluded from the primary label, counted in the flow, and never assigned N. The date representation, code normalization, duplicate handling, and tie rule are frozen before outcome inspection. If codebook review fails, only a labelled computational audit may be produced.

Z is a recorded category, not a clinical diagnosis. N means no qualifying record in these arrays by T1, never disease-free. Participants with death after T1 are not censored for this primary label; they are N if no transition/death is recorded by T1, with this limitation reported. A separate sensitivity will report them if death is later available, but cannot repair missing ascertainment.

## Models, frozen ambiguity band, and evaluation

Use a prevalence-only P0 sanity baseline and regularized multinomial models:

- M0: current instance-1 panel + age + sex + T1 calendar year;
- M1a: M0 + L, isolating repeat-spacing/return information;
- M1b: M1a + all eight literal instance-0 panel cells, the primary history increment;
- M1c: M1a + eight raw changes, representation sensitivity only;
- M2a: gradient-boosted multinomial with exactly M1a inputs;
- M2: matched gradient-boosted multinomial with exactly M1b inputs.

Use one deterministic five-fold hash split by eid. All imputation, scaling, regularization, tuning, and calibration are training-fold-only. For each early fold, generate held-out probabilities for all eligible early participants. Let r be the out-of-fold M0 probability of R. Compute q20 and q80 from early out-of-fold r only; define Lband: r<q20, Aband: q20<=r<q80, Uband: r>=q80. Freeze those cut points and apply them to predictions from the early-fitted models in late 2013. No late labels, M1b/M2 predictions, or permuted values define a band.

For era e and band g, score multiclass Brier and log loss. Define d_g,e = BS(M1b,g,e)-BS(M1a,g,e), so d<0 favors retaining prior values conditional on current values and spacing. Define D_e=d_A,e-(d_L,e+d_U,e)/2. The primary estimate is D_early and its no-refit stability is tau=D_late,no-refit-D_early. Report d_A,e, D_e, tau, and paired 95% bootstrap intervals. Also report transition-class top-5%, top-10%, and top-20% review recall/precision for M1a/M1b, with capacity chosen before evaluation. These operational metrics are supportive context and do not provide an action utility without clinical review.

Hard support rules for the primary contrast are at least 200 participants and 30 R labels in each of six era-by-band cells and at least 100 R labels overall. Require q20<q80, finite predictions, stable fold support, no leakage, and no materially unstable missingness pattern. If any rule fails, report the audit and mark the corresponding claim inconclusive.

## Selection and sensitivity analyses

Because complete repeat panels are selected, define S=1 among T0-complete participants as valid T1 plus complete panel. Fit a regularized early-only propensity from T0 panel, age, sex, and T0 year; apply it to late T0-complete participants. Use stabilized weights P(S=1)/p(S=1|X), truncated at early 1st/99th percentiles. Report overlap, standardized differences, maximum weight and ESS. This is a selection-transport sensitivity under a missing-at-random assumption, not causal correction.

Prespecified sensitivities include complete-case versus fold-local missingness handling; all-positions 0-258 versus the retired 0-29 truncation as an integrity audit only; code/date pairing break test; candidate-domain unknown-time exclusion versus a labelled unknown-time table; single-domain versus two-domain recorded labels; death excluded versus three-class Z; M1a versus M1b versus M1c; early/late label permutation; and prior-panel permutation with bands held fixed. The outcome permutation must preserve era and fold structure and destroy predictor association. Any broken-pairing control must fail or materially change counts; otherwise the parser is not trusted.

## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive evidence for the primary hypothesis requires valid biomarker and assessment semantics, exact full-array pairing, codebook/coding review, all support gates, no leakage or integrity failure, d_A,early<0 and d_A,late<0 with 95% upper bounds below zero, D_early<0 and D_late<0 with upper bounds below zero, higher prespecified top-k review recall for M1b without unacceptable precision loss, and tau inside a prespecified stability margin of [-0.002,0.002] Brier units. The margin is transport stability, not clinical benefit. A stable M2 shape across folds and late no-refit data strengthens, but cannot replace, the transparent nested result.

Adverse evidence is a non-improving/worsening d_A, a gain explained by spacing M1a rather than prior values M1b, nonnegative or reversed D, no top-k improvement, permutation survival, broken-pairing survival, fold instability, weight sensitivity reversal, or late reversal. This argues against retaining prior values for this recorded-review task, not against all clinical value elsewhere.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed semantic or codebook review, sparse cells/events, q20/q80 nonidentifiability, unknown-time leakage, invalid pairing, unstable missingness/weights, wide intervals, or disagreement between primary and prespecified label sensitivities. It is not evidence against the hypothesis. If specimen timing or ascertainment gates fail, the prospective secondary is inconclusive or not estimable; the assessment-bounded recorded label may still be reported with its narrower wording.

## Alternatives not chosen and revisit rules

The simple nested multinomial comparison is primary because it answers the incremental history question transparently. The matched boosted M2 alternative is retained because it can expose nonlinear current/prior discordance and threshold structure. A learned disease-history transformer is deferred because the outcome arrays lack approved temporal semantics; revisit only after validated chronology. A mechanistic organ-score model is deferred because identities, units, quality and specimen timing are absent; revisit after field-level documentation. No alternative is excluded due to neural networks or GPU availability. A different outcome, landmark, band, estimand, or support threshold would be a new scientific child, not a compiler repair.
