## Clinical importance

Sparse, protocolized CBC trajectories could be inexpensive prioritisation signals if they add CRC-specific information beyond the current blood count. A valid population result could justify external validation of longitudinal CBC signals before costly diagnostic work-up studies. A valid recorded-case increment would be narrower: it could indicate that trajectories differ between recorded CRC and other recorded malignancies, motivating adjudicated disease-localization work. Neither result alone can justify screening, referral, colonoscopy, or treatment decisions. The prior selected-case non-rejections make an evidence-aware confirmatory analysis scientifically useful even if negative, because they can distinguish lack of robust within-snapshot support from the still-unanswered coverage-valid population question.

## Branch 1: certified population prognosis experiment

### Estimands and outcomes

Among the certified primary risk set entering at `E`, estimate:

- the adjusted cause-specific association of the three slopes with first CRC during `[E,H]`;
- CRC cumulative incidence with death and first non-CRC malignancy handled as competing events as appropriate;
- the paired incremental out-of-fold prognostic performance of trajectory versus comparator models for first CRC;
- CRC specificity `D = Delta AUC_CRC - Delta AUC_nonCRC`, using the identical eligible population, folds, and pipeline for first non-CRC malignancy.

The prediction increment is defined by paired changes in time-dependent AUC and Uno C-index, IPCW integrated Brier score, and horizon calibration when support permits. Capacity summaries at fixed 1%, 2%, and 5% are descriptive only and require valid certified censoring/competing-event information.

### Analysis

Fit locked low-dimensional ridge-penalized cause-specific Cox models with delayed entry at `E`. Estimate cumulative incidence by Aalen-Johansen; death is a competing event. Fine-Gray is sensitivity only. Use identical participant-level repeated outer splits for comparator and trajectory models. All scaling, allowed imputation, penalty tuning, censoring estimation, weight truncation, and calibration fitting are training-fold-local. Obtain paired participant-bootstrap intervals, preserving fold pairing.

Support gates are unchanged:

- at least 60 coverage-valid CRC events: full locked association/prediction and descriptive capacity analyses;
- 30-59: parsimonious association and descriptive censoring-aware analysis only, with no flexible prediction or strong calibration claim;
- fewer than 30: flow, audits, cumulative incidence, and at most the simplest prespecified penalized association; incremental prediction is inconclusive.

### Meaning of results

Support requires adequate coverage and event support, an adverse-direction joint trajectory association with uncertainty excluding the null, positive paired out-of-fold increment without Brier/calibration deterioration, and clean falsifications. CRC specificity additionally requires `D>0` with an interval excluding zero and compatible Brier results. A precise null or opposite direction would be adverse for this exact repeat-attender design, not proof that all CBC use is futile. Broad intervals, unstable weights, metric discordance, or gate failure are inconclusive.

## Branch 2: recorded-case confirmatory re-analysis/triangulation

### Population, outcome, and estimand

Use the same base/history construction, but include only participants with a valid suffix-paired first recorded malignant diagnosis in `[E,H]`. The target is the paired out-of-fold change from comparator to trajectory model in:

- primary proper-scoring metric: case-case log loss, with improvement defined as `DeltaLogLoss = LogLoss_comparator - LogLoss_trajectory`;
- secondary discrimination metric: `DeltaAUC = AUC_trajectory - AUC_comparator`.

This estimates incremental discrimination conditional on a recorded malignancy in the window, eligible history, measured fields, and the snapshot's ascertainment process. It is not `P(CRC)` in the source population, prognosis, survival, incidence, or a causal effect.

### Analysis

Use identical participant-level repeated outer splits, stratified by label and fixed age/sex strata where feasible, with all preprocessing and penalty choice inside training folds. Aggregate each participant's held-out prediction across repetitions before one paired participant-level bootstrap. Report paired log-loss and AUC estimates, intervals, exact class counts, fold support, convergence, and within-recorded-case calibration only when class support is adequate. Calibration must not be translated into population risk or PPV.
