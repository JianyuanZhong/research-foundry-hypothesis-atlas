# Nested evidence-investment gates for robust HCC report semantics

## 1. Successor question and consequential decision

This child of `[prior hypothesis]` preserves its first-resection population, preoperative clocks and windows, documentary-M2 endpoint, six immutable corpus-wide reader books and nine globally coupled radiology×pathology pairs, frozen 2015–2018/2019/2020/2021 temporal split, fixed-capacity models and comparators, exact source-state coupling, leave-one-whole-patient-out computation, and strict fail-closed interpretation. It does not weaken, average, or replace the parent's universal >5 criterion.

The remaining limitation is decisional. The parent maps any failure among four year×comparator coordinates to one broad non-supportive state. A failure isolated to one year or one comparator is scientifically different from failure across both years and both comparators, yet the parent does not predeclare whether either pattern warrants the cheaper missing-evidence step—acquisition of accession-linked report versions, release times and view logs—while withholding the more expensive prospective silent-mode multidisciplinary-team (MDT) study.

This successor tests a nested, cost-sensitive proposition:

> In the frozen first-resection HCC corpus, after every declared source/pathology completion, every one of nine corpus-wide reader-book pairs, and every singleton whole-patient deletion, (i) clean report semantics remain directionally beneficial over both an independently fitted reduced comparator and the same-fit semantic mask in both 2020 and 2021, and (ii) both contrasts exceed 5 additional encounter-documentary M2 captures per 100 fixed review positions in at least one common test year.

That is the new **timing-acquisition hypothesis**. It is weaker than, and nested beneath, the preserved **silent-study hypothesis**, which requires both contrasts to exceed 5 in both years under the same undeleted and deletion-stable universal envelope.

The two gates answer different investment questions without relabeling evidence:

- the new gate supports funding the bounded accession-linked report-timing acquisition and locked real-time replay only;
- the preserved all-coordinate >5 gate supports funding that acquisition plus preparation of a prospective silent-mode MDT study, with study launch still contingent on the acquired timing data satisfying a separately locked real-time replay protocol;
- neither result authorizes live decision support, treatment selection, or patient-facing deployment.

Thresholds are fixed before test unsealing. The directional floor is strictly >0; the material floor is strictly >5. Equality fails. A stakeholder change to these thresholds, population, estimand, outcomes, capacity, model, temporal split, or logic is a new scientific child, not a compiler repair.

### Unresolved claims

No result currently establishes that eventual report semantics add documentary-M2 captures, that they were available before resection, that clinicians saw them, that review capacity is feasible, that a population effect exists, or that action improves outcomes.

The new hypothesis asks whether a predeclared pattern of finite-corpus robustness is strong enough to make report-timing acquisition a rational next evidence purchase despite one localized material-threshold failure. The preserved parent hypothesis asks whether the stronger pattern is present for every coordinate. Both are falsifiable by exact attained endpoints; neither is a probability statement.

### Substantive advance

The advance is a nested evidence-to-investment map, not another model. It prevents two errors at once: treating a localized finite-corpus failure as equivalent to broad failure, and using a promising localized result to rescue the stricter silent-study claim. It also emits exact year-, comparator-, and patient-deletion witnesses so the next study addresses the observed weakness without deleting inconvenient cases or changing thresholds.

## 4. Source objects, documentary outcome, and reader books

A radiology unit is `u=(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` and includes every exact-deduplicated component in raw order with immutable field/source-byte boundaries. Blank `检查号` cannot supply primary semantics. The frozen reversible parser emits canonical visible-text nodes `V`, markup/attribute nodes `A`, and parse-error lineage `E`. Concepts may originate only from reversible `V` bytes. A parse-disagreeing, nonreversible, treatment/pathology/specimen/MVI-grade-contaminated, or unresolved-context unit receives one whole-unit unavailable block; phrases are never selectively removed and patients are never dropped.

A pathology object is `v=(患者主索引,就诊号,composite_hash)`. Exact-deduplicate across all six fields while retaining raw ordinals, then serialize every `病理/检查所见/检查诊断` field in raw-row/field order with byte offsets. Every pathology row in the encounter is one indivisible documentary composite because no source field links a row to a specimen or resection.

Before outcomes, scores, split roles or top-K status are exposed, a steward freezes all reachable usable radiology units and nonempty pathology composites without HCC/grade filtering. The same two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists annotate every radiology item and a third completes every adjudication form. The same two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists annotate every pathology item and a third completes every adjudication form. Incomplete expected forms, corrupt provenance, or unresolved qualifications make the experiment inconclusive; they cannot shrink the cohort.

Radiology forms remain indivisible vectors for modality-specific lesion size, count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis and ascites, with uncertain/not-mentioned/source-unavailable levels and byte spans. Pathology forms indivisibly combine HCC-frame judgment, conditional grade judgment, spans/context and deterministic terminals from `OUT, IN0-A, IN1-A, IN0-U, IN1-U`. `OUT` has no Y; no HCC call and grade call may be borrowed across forms.

The complete books are `B^R_R1,B^R_R2,B^R_RA` and `B^P_P1,B^P_P2,B^P_PA`; the primary set is exactly `G={R1,R2,RA}×{P1,P2,PA}`. One selected pair applies corpus-wide across every item, patient, year, cutoff, stage, arm and deletion. Item/patient/concept/year/model/deletion switching is forbidden. `(RA,PA)` is adjudicated-only diagnostic and cannot rescue failure of the nine-pair envelope.

A world is `omega=(g,{s_i},{tau_i})`, with one global book pair, one coherent source state per patient, and one terminal allowed by that selected pathology form. The same world supplies membership, year, predictors, outcome, denominator and ties to both arms of a contrast. The finite universe remains `Omega_book+`.

## 5. Variables, fixed-capacity models, and inherited endpoints

Preserve four information sets:

- `B(Z)`: acquisition-only diagnostic baseline;
- `R_clean(Z,S,Q)`: independently optimized reduced comparator with the same nonsemantic surface/availability information;
- `G_clean(Z,S,Q,X_clean)`: independently optimized semantic model;
- `Gclean_mask`: frozen `G_clean` applied after every complete semantic block is replaced by its training-defined unavailable block, with no refit, recalibration, threshold change, or change to `Z,S,Q`.

`Z` is age, sex, CT/MRI/unit availability, eligible-unit count, recency, component/accession ambiguity, and development-grouped `机器型号`. `S` contains raw/canonical/markup surface quantities only. `Q` is reason-free whole-concept-block availability. `X_clean` comes only from the selected radiology book. Pathology, Y, reader identity/agreement, hashes, form time, ambiguity width, identifiers, unrestricted tokens, labs, interactions, splines and test-derived features are forbidden predictors.

Use the parent's additive logistic model with unpenalized intercept, one patient-balanced outcome-blind preprocessing map over the outer development ledger, byte-identical shared columns, and robustly certified zero-slope threshold `A_M` per model. For `alpha in {.25,.5,.75,1}`, use 100 log-spaced lambdas from `A_M/alpha` to `10^-4 A_M/alpha`; pure ridge uses 100 finite values from `A_M` to `10^-4 A_M` without claiming an all-zero endpoint. Fit one coefficient vector per information set by normalized minimax development loss over all nine book pairs/source/terminal states using exact cutting-plane/Dinkelbach certificates. Tune independently on 2019 worst-state fixed-K performance. Seal test forms/outcomes until preprocessing, hyperparameters, coefficients, `N_ref` and K are frozen.

`N_ref=min_{a=2015,...,2019; d in {12,24,48,72}; omega}N_a(omega,d)`.
For `rho in {.05,.10,.20}`, `K_rho=floor(rho*N_ref)`; confirmatory `K=K_.10`, with `N_ref>=500` and `K>=50`. The 5% and 20% capacities and alternate cutoffs are sensitivity-only and cannot rescue a primary failure.

Every test-year world must have at least `K+1` eligible patients, anchored-grade fraction at least 0.85, and at least 50 eligible `Y=1` events. Replay the corresponding post-deletion minima directly. Any failure is inconclusive.

For locked model M:
`T_M(a,omega)=100/K * sum_{i in TopK_M(a,omega)}Y_i(omega)`,
with exactly K whole patients ranked by locked score, frozen SHA-256 patient tie key, and raw episode backpointer.

The two contrasts remain:
`Delta_nested=T_Gclean-T_Rclean`;
`Delta_mask=T_Gclean-T_Gclean_mask`.

For coordinate `e=(a,c)`, `a in {2020,2021}`, `c in {nested,mask}`, compute attained undeleted extrema
`[L_e^0,U_e^0]=[min_omega Delta_e(omega),max_omega Delta_e(omega)]`.

For every frozen `j in U_T`, delete all of j's rows, states, objects, terminals and ranking possibilities from both arms and both years while freezing books, forms, preprocessing, hyperparameters, coefficients, masks, `N_ref` and K. Compute

`U_{e,j}^{-1}=max_omega Delta_e(omega\{j})`,
`L_e^{-1}=min_j L_{e,j}^{-1}`, and
`U_e^{-1}=max_j U_{e,j}^{-1}`.

## 6. New nested gates and localization estimands

Define the deletion-inclusive lower and upper endpoints
`m_e=min(L_e^0,L_e^{-1})` and
`q_e=max(U_e^0,U_e^{-1})`.
These are finite-corpus extrema over the union of the undeleted corpus and every singleton-deleted corpus; they are not confidence bounds.

For each year define the noncompensatory annual bundle
`b_a=min(m_{a,nested},m_{a,mask})`.
No averaging or trade between comparators or years is allowed.

The preserved silent-study hypothesis is:
`H_silent: min_e m_e > 5`.

The new timing-acquisition hypothesis is:
`H_time: (min_e m_e > 0) AND (max_{a in {2020,2021}} b_a > 5)`.

Thus `H_silent => H_time`. The common-year requirement means 2020 nested and 2021 mask cannot be combined to manufacture one “material year.” The universal directional floor means a materially supportive year cannot offset a possible nonpositive contrast in the other year. Equality at 0 or 5 fails. Test-result-dependent selection of a year, comparator, cutoff, capacity, book, deletion, or threshold is forbidden.

For exact localization, emit:

- `F_5={e:m_e<=5}` and `F_0={e:m_e<=0}`;
- `C_e={j:L_{e,j}^{-1}<=5}` and `C_e^0={j:L_{e,j}^{-1}<=0}`;
- each patient's signature `S_j={e:L_{e,j}^{-1}<=5}`;
- whether `F_5` is year-localized (one test year), comparator-localized (one contrast), both, or cross-axis;
- whether one patient is a witness for multiple coordinates, with no patient excluded or reread.

These diagnostics are frozen descriptive functions of exact endpoints. Their counts/distributions are not sampling frequencies, influence probabilities, p values, or permission to amend the gate. A mask-localized failure means semantic reliance within the fitted model is not universally material; a nested-localized failure means the independently optimized reduced workflow can be competitive; a year-localized failure marks temporal inconsistency in this corpus. None identifies the correct reader/world or causal reason.

Enforce total order `(linear predictor,SHA-256 tie key,raw episode backpointer)` with lazy rank-inversion constraints until no violation remains. ## 8. One-hot decision and falsification logic

Mandatory source, annotation, adequacy, fitting, or exact-solver failure has precedence:

1. **inconclusive**: authorize neither investment from this experiment. Missingness or timeout is not adverse evidence.
2. **staged-full-supportive**: `H_silent` is true. This supports funding accession-linked timing acquisition and preparing the silent-mode MDT protocol. It does not establish real-time availability; launch remains contingent on locked replay of the latest actually released pre-cutoff version.
3. **timing-acquisition-only-supportive**: `H_time` is true and `H_silent` is false. This falsifies the full silent-study hypothesis while supporting only the cheaper timing acquisition plus prespecified root-cause audit. It does not support launching the silent study.
4. **acquisition-rule-adverse**: either some `q_e<=0`, or for each test year at least one of its two contrasts has `q_e<=5`. The first condition means at least one required coordinate is nonpositive in every declared undeleted/deleted scenario; the second means no complete test year can be materially positive on both contrasts in any declared scenario. Authorize neither investment from this evidence. This does not prove report semantics useless.
5. **mixed-localized**: all other complete results. The new universal acquisition hypothesis and the full hypothesis are both falsified, but at least one required claim remains world-, year-, comparator-, or deletion-sensitive rather than all-world adverse. Authorize neither investment automatically; use emitted witnesses to design an audit or new study without excluding cases or changing thresholds.

For every coordinate and threshold `theta in {0,5}`, report “universally supportive” only if `m_e>theta`, “all-world adverse” only if `q_e<=theta`, and “heterogeneous” otherwise. Numerically correct output with an unsupported conclusion fails verification.

A supportive result establishes only a finite-corpus statement about eventual stored reports and encounter-documentary M2 under the supplied books/states. It does not establish biological M2, report display before resection, clinician use, target-population performance, causal benefit, safety, fairness, cost-effectiveness, treatment choice, or deployment readiness. An adverse result rules out only the specified strict gate in the declared corpus; an inconclusive result says nothing directional.
