# Independent child: a crossed-panel partial-identification audit for correlated codebook error

## Decision question and scope

This child inherits `[prior hypothesis]` and its fixed decision question: in the frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC population, can text-defined `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes be nominated only for a prospective silent test? The child does **not** change the population, chronology, canonical discharge artifact, units, route precedence, domain allow-list, sampling design, or the noncausal ceiling. It repairs one remaining evidence-validity weakness: separated sealed panels can still share a codebook and jointly make the same error.

The unresolved question is narrower than clinical truth: after an atom-specific route passes the parent's split-view audit, is its apparent safety reproducible under a genuinely different adjudication ontology, and how much could an unobserved common-mode error change the route gate? The available retrospective text cannot identify clinical appropriateness, patient benefit, or the true intended follow-up. It can test stored-text reproducibility and can report an explicit identified set for latent text propositions under prespecified common-mode-error sensitivity. It cannot turn that set into a clinical truth claim.

## Crossed panel design, fixed before any real label

The parent already gives every selected admission all route-independent audit components. Add a **calibration layer**, not a new admission sample. It uses the same selected admissions and therefore cannot increase the fixed expert-review frame beyond 900 distinct admissions. Extra readings are physician-hours only.

1. **Codebook panel C.** Two roster-disjoint physicians independently receive the parent's sealed audit packet and locked atomic vocabulary. They label target, action/modality, interval, recipient/responsibility, polarity, replacement/conflict, clock sufficiency, and whether each unit can be `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`. They never see the other C label, route, atom, weights, or H labels.
2. **Holistic panel H.** Two different physicians independently receive the same permitted reciprocal pre-departure radiology and canonical DS text, but no atomic codebook, no route vocabulary, no extracted target/action spans, no domain, no sampling stratum, no C labels, and no parent route. They answer a frozen open-schema question: (a) quote the text spans that support each distinct pulmonary follow-up opportunity, (b) state whether the text unambiguously contains a patient-specific unconditional 3/6/12-month chest/thoracic CT plan, (c) state whether the stored pre-departure text supports a safe structured transfer with no unresolved target/action/timing/responsibility/polarity conflict, and (d) mark `UNKNOWN` whenever the source does not support a decision. H reviewers may use only the supplied text and permitted metadata; they may not infer guideline appropriateness, malignancy, patient preference, clinician intent beyond text, or completion.
3. **Crossed assignment.** Use a balanced incomplete block: every atom and inherited outcome stratum has C-C duplicate and H-H duplicate readings, and a prespecified fraction (at least 25% of each claimable atom's selected admissions, capped by the fixed reading budget) is read by one C and one H reviewer in a crossed pair. The fraction, reviewer matching, and all packet hashes are fixed before labels. If a cell cannot meet the crossed minimum, it is not claimable; it is not pooled with a neighbor.
4. **Independent holistic senior panel.** For every disagreement between C and H, every H-H disagreement, and a fixed outcome-blind 10% of concordant records in each final atom, a third fresh physician from neither roster writes an unconstrained adjudication memo and binary `TEXT_UNRESOLVED` flag. The senior sees only the source packet and the disagreement-free raw submissions with panel identities removed; it sees no route, domain, weight, or outcome. This memo is a measurement of a third text interpretation, not a gold standard. No consensus deletion is allowed.

The H schema is deliberately not a disguised translation of C. Its required free-text quotation, unconstrained opportunity inventory, and `TEXT_UNRESOLVED` option are scored only after it is sealed. H does not receive the parent unit list and may split or combine opportunities independently. A deterministic post-label crosswalk records one-to-many, many-to-one, omission, and addition; no favorable matching is permitted. An H open-schema disagreement remains adverse-compatible rather than being forced into C's units.

## Controls that detect process dependence without claiming a clinical gold standard

Before real labels, seal a control bank containing (i) synthetic text fixtures with known inserted/removable target/action/timing/polarity and explicit ambiguity, (ii) de-identified text-preserving perturbations that differ only in a known sentence or negation, and (iii) source-format and redaction canaries. Fixture truth is the generated fixture specification, not clinical truth. Randomly interleave controls, blind to panel and atom, with a fixed proportion and preserve control IDs separately from real MIMIC IDs. C and H must not share fixture labels or discuss them.

Controls measure codebook parse sensitivity/specificity, holistic extraction sensitivity, negation/addendum handling, packet truncation/redaction detection, and whether a supposedly independent H panel merely reproduces C's vocabulary. A panel failing a locked control gate invalidates that panel's affected batch and forces `NO_SELECTION` for affected atoms; controls never repair real labels. The control error bounds are one-sided Clopper-Pearson 95% bounds with finite fixture population correction and are carried as nuisance intervals, not plugged-in point estimates.

A second control is an **ontology-blind challenge set**: before labels, a separate physician group creates text questions and ambiguity cases from a held-out local corpus, without seeing this protocol or the C ontology. These questions test whether H reviewers independently recover the intended text proposition. Because the held-out corpus is not externally adjudicated, it is a reproducibility/control set only; it does not establish the clinical right answer.

## Correlated-error estimands and partial identification

For each admission and atom, retain the full joint submission, nonresponse, packet integrity, C-C agreement, H-H agreement, C-H agreement, crosswalk ambiguity, and senior `TEXT_UNRESOLVED`. Define `Q_i=1` when the C route is `AUTO`-compatible and `J_i=1` when the H panel independently supports an unambiguous safe structured transfer under its open schema. These are stored-text propositions, not clinical safety.

Report, with the inherited design weights and the same finite-population bootstrap/exact machinery:

* `R_CC,g=P(C1=C2 | g)`, `R_HH,g=P(H1=H2 | g)`, and `R_CH,g=P(C route is compatible with H route | g)`;
* the atom-specific C-to-H false-safe mass `F_CH,g=P(Q=1,J=0 | g)`, with all H disagreement and H unknowns assigned adverse-compatible values;
* a dependence contrast `K_g = R_CC,g + R_HH,g - 2R_CH,g`, reported descriptively only; high within-panel agreement with lower cross-panel agreement is evidence of codebook/common-mode concern, not proof of error;
* the senior-disagreement rate and the control-calibrated lower bound on the probability that an observed H/C disagreement is detectable by the protocol; and
* the parent's contamination, leakage, confirmation, capture, AC1, workload, and minority-unit quantities, both as observed-text bounds and after the correction below.

The latent proposition `T_i`—the proposition an ideal text adjudicator would make from the stored artifact—is not observed. Clinical truth is even further unavailable. Do not fit an independent-errors latent-class model, call H gold standard, or convert panel agreement into validity. Instead publish a **partial-identification envelope**. Let `eta_g` be the unobserved common-mode probability that both panel systems label a C-`AUTO` case safe while the ideal text adjudicator would label it unsafe. The design identifies no data-based nonzero upper bound for `eta_g` on arbitrary real text. Therefore predeclare a sensitivity set `eta_g ∈ [0, eta_max,g]`, where `eta_max,g` is the smaller of 1 and the conservative one-sided 95% upper bound from the ontology-blind controls multiplied by a governance-approved inflation factor (at least 2), and also report the assumption-free envelope `eta_g∈[0,1]` as a non-release diagnostic.

For any route ratio with denominator `D_g`, optimize numerator and denominator jointly over every compatible C/H/senior label and `eta_g` in that interval. A conservative corrected upper contamination endpoint is the sharp supremum of

`(X_CH,g + eta_g D_g + U_unknown,g + U_nonresponse,g) / D_g`,

where `X_CH,g` is the HT count of observed C-`AUTO` cases not supported safe by H, and `U_unknown` and `U_nonresponse` are the inherited adverse finite-population assignments. Use linear-fractional optimization inside each Rao-Wu replicate, retain exact hypergeometric inversion for sparse events, and take the widest simultaneous 95% endpoint across bootstrap, exact inversion, and the partial-identification optimization. The numerator and denominator HT totals are always reported. The same correction is applied to atom-level `D_MIX`, `leak_AUTO`, and any claim that depends on a safe C/H crosswalk; it is not applied to clinical harm because no clinical outcome is observed.

A route/atom can be nominated only if it passes the inherited gates and the corrected endpoint for **every** eta in the predeclared control-calibrated interval. If it passes only at `eta=0`, report apparent reproducibility but `NO_SELECTION`. If it passes under `eta_max` but fails the assumption-free envelope, say only that the result is conditional on the explicit sensitivity assumption. If no defensible control-calibrated `eta_max` exists, the correlated-error layer is inconclusive and no AUTO atom is released. H-H disagreement, senior uncertainty, or a one-to-many crosswalk cannot be silently treated as safe.

## Baselines and falsification

Run the identical sealed inputs against: the parent's codebook-only result; raw C-H disagreement without correction; favorable H consensus; a falsely assumed conditional-independent latent-class model; a pooled or macro-service correction; and the proposed adverse partial-identification envelope. None except the prespecified envelope can nominate.

Synthetic verification must plant: (1) a high-agreement shared codebook miss in one supported atom while pooled contamination passes; (2) independent C/H disagreement without common-mode error; (3) correlated C and H misses that controls do not fully reveal; (4) missing senior reviews concentrated among C-`AUTO`; (5) H one-to-many and many-to-one unit splits; (6) a novel service/workflow signature; and (7) zero observed unsafe cases. The implementation must allow the raw agreement and independent-errors model to look favorable while the adverse eta envelope blocks or conditions the claim. It must not reject solely because panels disagree when the corrected route remains within all prespecified bounds.

Additional run-stopping checks include roster overlap, packet-hash equality across supposedly different ontologies, C vocabulary leakage into H prompts, route/domain exposure, control leakage, post-label eta selection, use of clinical outcome language, and any increase above 900 distinct admissions. A failed seal or unapproved sensitivity range invalidates affected atoms rather than being repaired after seeing results.

## Interpretation and evidence ceiling

**Supportive** means that an atom passes the inherited all-route, worst-unit, domain allow-list and workload gates, C-H reproducibility is adequate, senior unknowns are adverse-accounted, and every corrected endpoint passes throughout the prespecified `eta` interval. This supports at most a prospective silent bridge for that exact observable atom, with all other and novel signatures forced to `DEFER`.

**Adverse** means that crossed-panel disagreement, senior text uncertainty, control-calibrated common-mode allowance, minority-unit failure, or workload pushes an atom beyond a gate. A pooled pass cannot rescue it. This is evidence that the retrospective text-routing process is not sufficiently reproducible for nomination, not evidence of patient harm or clinical inappropriateness.

**Inconclusive** means insufficient crossed coverage, no defensible control calibration, a broad eta interval, sparse atom support, wide simultaneous bounds, failed retrieval/seals, or inability to maintain the fixed 900-admission ceiling. The assumption-free eta envelope will generally be too broad; that is an explicit statement of non-identification, not a failed clinical hypothesis.

Even a supportive result cannot establish exact calendar transport, clinical appropriateness, clinician awareness, true responsibility, actual artifact viewing/editing/transmission, order completion, follow-up, patient outcome, safety, benefit, or external generalizability. Those require prospective actual-calendar silent records with independent expert review and, later, governed clinical outcome data. The prospective bridge remains separately approved and outside this retrospective <=900-admission workload.

## Substantive advance

The parent prevents route self-validation and pooled domain masking. This child adds an independent ontology and crossed-panel design, but more importantly refuses to treat independence as a fact: it quantifies observable cross-ontology reproducibility, calibrates only the measurement process with known controls, and carries unidentifiable shared-codebook error through a sharp adverse sensitivity envelope. Thus a high-agreement atom can fail or remain conditional when both panels may share an error, while a disagreement alone cannot manufacture a clinical-negative claim. The result is a falsifiable evidence-validity audit rather than a claim of clinical truth.
