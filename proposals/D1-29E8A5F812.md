# Is hour-24 transfer provenance a residual receiving-ICU risk marker, or an artifact of support labels, overlap, surveillance, and disposition?

## Status, evidence, and fixed hypothesis

This substantive child preserves the adult first-stay population, hour-24 landmark, hours 24–72 horizon, within-receiving-hospital question, two-percentage-point margin, and noncausal interpretation. It repairs four pre-compilation threats: incident-support labels, positivity, competing disposition, and interpretation of the transfer-indicator toggle. No adjusted transfer result has been computed.

Kudlow et al. used a pathway-specific transfer definition and found no established excess 30-day mortality after adjustment, while retaining residual-confounding limits [K1]. Rush et al. found similar mortality but longer stay among matched ventilated sepsis patients, making disposition opportunity a substantive rival [K2]. Patel et al. found transfer-day SOFA, high arrival FiO2, and pre-transfer arrest predicted early mortality within a transferred cohort, showing why arrival state matters without identifying an incremental transfer marker [K3].

Strongest supported claim: transfer cohorts can differ in arrival severity and length of stay; adjusted mortality evidence is context-specific and does not establish benefit or harm.

Unresolved hypothesis:

> Among adult first ICU stays alive and still in the receiving ICU at hour 24, and only within receiving hospitals/covariate regions with empirical transfer/direct support, concordant interhospital-transfer provenance contributes at least a two-point conditional 72-hour cumulative-incidence difference for clinically adjudicated new invasive ventilation, new vasoactive infusion, new RRT, or death after measured 0–24-hour state and trajectory are held fixed; this persists under an ordered observation-aware model and is not explained by differential detection or slower ICU disposition.

This is a residual risk-marker hypothesis, not a causal transfer effect. It does not infer sending-hospital course, transfer indication, travel time, eligibility, or an intervention that could switch history.

The leading explanation is unmeasured pre-transfer complexity. The strongest rival is joint artifact: transferred patients occupy different support, remain in ICU longer, are observed more, and hence have more opportunity for standing treatment rows to look incident. A marker must persist with adjudicated labels, overlap restriction, observation standardization, competing disposition, and compatible death/support contrasts. Positivity failure makes the toggle extrapolative, not supportive or adverse.

## Incident outcome specification

Endpoint is first high-specificity new invasive ventilation, continuous vasoactive infusion, acute RRT, or hospital death. “New” requires no evidence of that component at any offset <=1440, including negative offsets and untimed APACHE day-one support. First post-landmark documentation is not automatically onset.

Death is patient.hospitaldischargeoffset when hospitaldischargestatus="Expired". Alive index-ICU exit is unitdischargeoffset when unitdischargestatus="Alive" and location is not another ICU. Other-ICU transfer/ambiguous exit is administrative censoring/indeterminate, not benign discharge. Same-minute ties are indeterminate; death-priority is a sensitivity.

Vasoactive: normalize norepinephrine/Levophed, epinephrine/adrenaline, phenylephrine/Neo-Synephrine, vasopressin, dopamine in infusionDrug.drugname. Primary positive requires numeric drugrate>0; zero/missing is inactive. Onset is first positive infusionoffset in-window after no mapped/positive pre-landmark row. Strict sensitivity requires two positive rows 15 minutes–6 hours apart, or one plus shock/vasopressor treatment within +/-120 minutes. With no infusion entry clock, call this first documented active infusion.

Ventilation: pre-landmark exclusion uses apacheApsVar intubated/vent, any mechanical-ventilation/reintubation treatment, nonzero current/prior respiratoryCare start, explicit intubation note, invasive airway, or invasive mode <=1440. Incident onset requires (a) note.notetype="Intubation", deduplicated by stay/offset with noteenteredoffset>=noteoffset, or (b) nonzero respiratoryCare ventstartoffset/priorventstartoffset in-window, plus invasive treatment, airway/mode chart, or second hard source within +/-360 minutes. Broad treatment alone is insufficient; zero start offsets, NIV, CPAP, and oxygen therapy are excluded.

RRT: pre-landmark exclusion uses apacheApsVar.dialysis, prior dialysis treatment/catheter note, nonzero dialysistotal, or dialysis-specific IO label/path. Incident onset requires acute-specific treatment (acute hemodialysis or CVVH/CVVHD/CRRT; chronic renal failure excluded) plus a second row within 12 hours, catheter procedure packet, or adjudicated dialysis-specific intakeOutput evidence within +/-12 hours. Catheter or dialysistotal alone is insufficient.

Outcome-blind audit results explain the hierarchy. In 19 supported hospitals before final comfort exclusion: 219 first-positive vasoactive candidates; 295 broad treatment ventilation candidates but only 120 overlapped a hard note/respiratory-care signal; 134 acute-specific RRT candidates, with 36 carrying APACHE day-one dialysis. Of 110,108 mapped vasoactive rows, 10,325 had zero/missing numeric rate. These are diagnostics, not adjudicated event counts.

Freeze mappings, then create 360 deidentified packets: 240 algorithm positives balanced by component, hidden transfer group, hospital, and confidence; 60 near-miss negatives; 60 random negatives. Two ICU clinicians independently classify true new, continuation/prevalent, artifact, uncertain, and onset interval using complete pre-landmark support and only 12 hours after candidate time. Hide transfer, hospital, death/discharge, later outcomes; third clinician adjudicates. Retain sampling probabilities. Gate: pooled and component PPV >=0.80 (lower 95% >=0.70), weighted sensitivity >=0.80 (lower >=0.70), absolute transfer/direct PPV and sensitivity differences <=0.10, uncertain <=0.10. If review is unavailable/fails, no composite conclusion; death/disposition may proceed as a separately narrowed child.

## Identical inputs and two methods

All rows for one uniquepid remain in one of five hospital/exposure-stratified folds. B0 and M1 use identical stays, six four-hour bins, raw rows, masks, elapsed times, process counts, static fields, labels, common-support set, and folds. Training-only preprocessing/tuning.

Per-bin inputs: periodic/aperiodic HR, RR, SpO2, invasive/noninvasive BP; lab creatinine, BUN, sodium, potassium, bicarbonate, lactate, glucose, bilirubin, albumin, WBC, Hgb/Hct, platelets, pH, PaO2, PaCO2, FiO2, PEEP; adjudicated urine; GCS; pre-landmark ventilation/RRT/infusion/sedation/EOL evidence. Each family yields count, mask, elapsed time, median/min/max/last and entry delay where available. Static fields: age, sex, ethnicity, apacheadmissiondx, unit type, admission hour, hospitaladmitoffset, weight, hospital, APACHE day-one physiology/support/comorbidities. No predicted/actual APACHE outcome, discharge, or post-landmark predictor.

B0: discrete-time cause-specific pooled logistic models in 6-hour intervals for deterioration, alive non-ICU discharge, and other-ICU exit, elastic-net regularized. Each raw family is transparently summarized as first-6-hour and 24-hour worst/last, OLS slope, observed-bin count, longest gap, entry-delay summary. Include hospital fixed effects, transfer, and prespecified transfer interactions with admission category and landmark ventilation. Fit paired without/with transfer. This makes risk sets/disposition explicit and shows which domains attenuate the marker.

M1: six-step hierarchical GRU/state-space model, 16–32-dimensional latent state, with values, masks, elapsed time, process counts; separate next-value, next-mask, and three cause-specific hazard heads; regularized hospital random intercepts. Transfer enters final hazard heads only, not transition. Three seeds. M1 retains worsening/recovery order, asynchronous organs, and observation timing that extrema/slopes lose. It is not a transport mechanism and cannot recover sending history.

M1 admissibility: held-out deterioration Brier no worse than B0 by >0.005; calibration-slope absolute error no worse by >0.10; next-mask calibration error <=0.05 overall and each transfer group; transfer RD varies <1 point across seeds.

## Positivity and smallest rival-separating check

Estimate exposure propensity out of fold from common static/0–24-hour fields including hospital. Within hospital define direct support using direct 1st–99th score range plus frozen robust Mahalanobis/calipers for age, diagnosis, landmark support, APACHE physiology, and observation counts. Freeze intersection before outcomes and report excluded profiles.

Gates: >=15 hospitals, >=1,200 transferred, >=100 adjudicated transferred events; >=80% of transferred in support; capped ATT direct-weight ESS >=50% transferred N; weighted absolute standardized difference <0.10 for every prespecified static, organ, landmark-support, and observation field; no hospital >25% transferred information.

Completed outcome-blind feasibility: 19 hospitals, 1,611 transfer and 15,002 direct before final comfort exclusion; 2,048/2,216 (92.4%) hospital-source Other Hospital rows were unit-source concordant. A static/day-one model put 89.9% of transferred inside hospital-specific direct 1st–99th propensity ranges; capped ATT direct ESS ~7,092. Promising, but not final trajectory overlap/balance.

Smallest rival check is a frozen 2x2 on the same overlap cohort: broad versus adjudicated labels, crossed with ordinary versus observation-opportunity-standardized analysis. Model each post-landmark 6-hour interval’s observability from prior data only; censor/reweight to shared exposure-group opportunity, cap weights at frozen 1st/99th percentiles; repeat by bidirectional observation-row downsampling. Report death-only, support-only, and alive-discharge cumulative incidence. Do not adjust post-landmark observation as an ordinary baseline confounder.

Continue if strict labels/overlap pass and neither restriction changes either model RD >1 point or across 0/2. Revise to death/disposition if support labels fail but those clocks are valid. Stop composite direction if overlap fails or signal is confined to broad/high-observation data.

## Result gates

Supportive: every validity gate passes; B0 and admissible M1 each RD>=2 points; M1 lower 95%>0 and attenuation from B0<50%; empirical anchor concordant; strict-label, observation-standardized, death/support and disposition checks move <1 point and not across both 0/2; transfer improves held-out integrated Brier >=0.001 or decision net benefit over 5–20% without calibration harm. Supports only residual provenance marker.

Adverse: gates pass and both upper 95% bounds<2, or admissible M1 attenuation>=50% with upper<2. Favors measured trajectory/observation/disposition as sufficient for the margin; not equivalence unless precision excludes it.

Inconclusive: interval spans 0 and 2; any label/overlap/event/calibration/weight gate fails; admissible models disagree; strict-label or observation/disposition shift >1 point or crosses both boundaries; death/support materially oppose; leave-one-hospital-out reverses in >20% supported sites. Never select favorable component post hoc.

Mandatory sensitivities: seven-day death; death-only/components; exclude pre-landmark DNR/EOL and death within six hours; landmarks 18/30; conservative entry/revision clocks; other-ICU exit competing versus censoring; exclude cross-hospital repeated people; leave-one-hospital-out; source removal; duplicate collapse.
