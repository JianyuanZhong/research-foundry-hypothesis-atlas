## Exact executable biomarker definitions

The formulas below are the mandated race-free 2021 CKD-EPI equations. They are executed only after the assay certificate confirms the required units. Let `A` be age in years at the corresponding assessment, `female=1` when the certified coding of `31-0.0` is Female and `female=0` when Male; baseline sex is held fixed at repeat. Let `Scr` be serum creatinine in mg/dL and `Scys` cystatin C in mg/L. Let `k=0.7` and `alpha=-0.302` for female, and `k=0.9` and `alpha=-0.241` for male.

Creatinine-only:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k, 1)^alpha * max(Scr/k, 1)^(-1.200) * (0.9938^A) * (1.012 if female else 1.000)`.

Cystatin-C-only:

`eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-1.328) * (0.996^A) * (0.932 if female else 1.000)`.

Combined:

`eGFRcr_cys = 135 * min(Scr/k, 1)^alpha * max(Scr/k, 1)^(-0.544) * min(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-0.323) * max(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-0.778) * (0.9961^A) * (1.012 if female else 1.000)`.

The implementation must use floating-point exponentiation, no race coefficient, no rounding before thresholding, and exactly the sex-specific constants above. A numerical unit test is mandatory before row execution. For `female=0`, `A=50`, `Scr=1.0`, `Scys=0.8`, expected values are approximately `eGFRcr=89.419`, `eGFRcys=91.231`, and `eGFRcr_cys=91.191` mL/min/1.73m² (the verifier recomputes to tolerance 1e-6 using the displayed formulas). For `female=1`, the implementation must also test the female multipliers and `k=0.7`, `alpha=-0.302`; the code records the exact computed vector rather than relying on rounded prose values.

Define baseline and repeat variables with the same equations and corresponding assessment ages:

- `eGFRcr_0`, `eGFRcys_0`, `eGFRcr_cys_0` use baseline laboratory fields and `21003-0.0`.
- `eGFRcr_1`, `eGFRcys_1`, `eGFRcr_cys_1` use instance-1 laboratory fields and `21003-1.0`, with baseline sex held fixed.

The primary baseline discordance is `Z=log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)`. It is calculated only in a certified positive-finite cohort.

The primary outcome is `Ycr=I(eGFRcr_1 < 60)`, a repeat creatinine-equation threshold state over the variable 2–8 year schedule. Secondary outcomes are `Ycys=I(eGFRcys_1 < 60)`, `Ycomb=I(eGFRcr_cys_1 < 60)`, the four-level pair `(I(eGFRcr_1<60), I(eGFRcys_1<60))`, and continuous repeat eGFR summaries. Compare observed statistics with statistic-specific empirical nulls using randomized-plus-one tail fractions. 5. **Predictor null.** Permute `Z` within baseline eGFRcr deciles and sex, refit M1 with identical fold and preprocessing rules, and require incremental curves to collapse toward their empirical null. Persistent gains indicate leakage or implementation error.
6. **Reversed-label negative control.** Rerun the frozen pipeline for `1-Ycr`. A discordance-specific conclusion cannot depend on an equally large gain for this artificial target.
7. **Timing sensitivity.** Report `[2,3)`, `[3,5)`, and `[5,8]` bands and secondary equation-defined outcomes without changing the primary schedule-marginal estimand. A direction confined to one band or one unadjudicated equation is not a general kidney-status finding.

## Decision gates and interpretation

**Supportive for conditional predictive prioritization only.** A valid frozen certificate and all source/assay audits pass; M1 has acceptable calibration relative to M0; the simultaneous 98% lower band for `DeltaC(q)` is positive throughout 5–15%; the direction is stable across repeats and supported strata; and the result survives the label-null and predictor-null checks. Concordance in at least one prespecified equation-defined sensitivity strengthens, but is not required for, the primary creatinine-status claim. The result supports only baseline conditional ranking in the selected observed complete-repeat cohort.

**Adverse.** With certification passed, M1 reproducibly worsens calibration or capacity capture, or its apparent gain is null-compatible, leakage-related, or selection-fragile. This argues against incremental prioritization value within this evidence boundary, not against cystatin C in all clinical uses.

**Inconclusive.** Bands cross zero over the capacity region; curves vary materially by repeat or stratum; null refits do not complete; only isolated threshold points improve; or certification is incomplete. Under the current frozen snapshot the designated outcome is the more specific neutral QA state `STOP_ASSAY_METADATA_UNRESOLVED`, not an inconclusive scientific result. No threshold recommendation follows.

Even a supportive result cannot establish benefit from ordering a repeat test, acceptable false-positive burden, cost-effectiveness, downstream treatment benefit, causal utility, CKD chronicity, measured-GFR accuracy, assay interchangeability, or transportability. Those claims require external utility and cost data, prospective observation of testing decisions, clinical adjudication, repeated clinical records, and preferably measured GFR.
