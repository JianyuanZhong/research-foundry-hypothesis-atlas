# Evidence-trace reference-validity calibration for a clinically live departure obligation

## Decision question and substantive repair

The frozen decision remains whether any exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated for a prospective **within-site silent validation** of the inherited admission routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER`. Candidate `a823951...` makes component labels answerable to independent holistic review and deliberately does not turn agreement into truth. Candidate `883a38...` adds route-concealed actionability and target-linked supersession states. Their shared residual validity gap is that mutually reproducible reviewers can still call a radiology sentence copied into a discharge summary an active obligation even when it is boilerplate, conditional, already satisfied, cancelled, replaced, or merely historical.

This child adds an executable **artifact-supported departure-obligation reference** (`ADOR`) and calibration layer. It does not purport to recover a latent clinical gold standard. `ADOR` is a deliberately narrower, auditable proposition: the supplied MIMIC artifacts literally support a target-specific action that remains textually open at departure and is adopted as a discharge-facing plan. The added layer decomposes that proposition into span-cited proof obligations, computes copying/provenance flags without interpreting them as truth, uses the inherited independent holistic panel as a route-concealed reference measurement, and calibrates only the logical response to locked transformations. Thus agreement can no longer nominate an atom unless reviewers independently demonstrate why the action is live in the allowed artifacts.

The strongest claim currently supported by the available data is only that admission-linked radiology and canonical discharge text, their deidentified within-subject timestamps, radiology addendum links, and service/ICU proxies can be assembled reproducibly. The unresolved, falsifiable claim is: **at least one support-qualified inherited `AUTO` atom has no adverse-compatible sampled unit lacking the full `ADOR` proof obligations, passes the inherited simultaneous reference/control bounds, and therefore merits only prospective silent validation of that exact stored-text signature.** Failure of any proof obligation, reference/calibration gate, or inherited safeguard falsifies nomination for the affected atom; it does not show that care was inappropriate or harmful.

## Pre-label provenance map and route-concealed reference packet

Before any label, create a deterministic sentence/span provenance map from only each selected admission's eligible radiology chain and canonical DS. Preserve raw offsets. For each target/action-bearing span compute: source note key and `charttime/storetime`; exact NFKC/lowercase/whitespace-normalized hash; a punctuation-stripped token hash with dates and numbers retained; longest-common-token-subsequence fraction; token 5-shingle Jaccard similarity; within-admission radiology-to-DS exact match; near-copy flag only when both longest-common-token-subsequence fraction and 5-shingle Jaccard are `>=0.90`; and corpus frequency of the exact normalized span among all canonical DS artifacts, calculated before sampling labels. These frozen, algorithmic flags describe textual provenance. They never by themselves establish boilerplate, adoption, authorship, or invalidity.

R3 receives a separately pseudonymized, independently ordered whole-admission packet containing the allowed raw texts, raw offsets, reciprocal-chain diagram, `charttime`, `storetime`, `D`, explicit missingness, and provenance flags. It never receives route, atom, stratum, weights, expected control answer, parent/A1/A2/S1/H1/H2 output, post-`D` text, POE, or outcome data. Codebook `CB-ADOR-R3-v1`, roster, packet generator, normalization code, thresholds, forms, seal order, and hashes are frozen before review.

For every inherited recommendation unit R3 must quote raw character offsets and record `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, or `INDETERMINATE` for each of six proof obligations:

1. **Target continuity (`T`)**: the action is linked to the same pulmonary target through explicit laterality/lobe/size/morphology language, or the text explicitly states that it is the same target. A generic lung reference or competing lesions without resolvable linkage is indeterminate.
2. **Directive force (`F`)**: the canonical DS expresses an affirmative discharge-facing recommendation or commitment. A differential, educational statement, historical recommendation, bare radiology quotation, `may/could consider` without adoption, or condition whose antecedent is unresolved is not sufficient.
3. **Remaining action (`O`)**: the stated action remains textually open after the last eligible pre-`D` target-linked artifact. A later eligible span documenting that the same target/action was completed, cancelled, no longer indicated, or resolved contradicts `O`; uncertainty is indeterminate. This is documentary completion only, not proof of actual execution.
4. **Discharge adoption (`A`)**: the DS locally adopts the action as its plan through an explicit target-action statement plus at least one of timing, recipient, responsibility language, or an unambiguous discharge/follow-up-plan context. Exact or near-copied radiology wording without such a DS-local adoption bridge is `UNADOPTED_ECHO`; a common corpus-frequency flag is only a challenge cue and cannot decide `A`.
5. **No target-linked superseder (`S`)**: no later eligible pre-`D` radiology/addendum or canonical-DS span explicitly replaces, cancels, changes the interval/action, or records a contradictory plan for that same target. A competing non-target plan cannot supersede it; unresolved target identity makes `S` indeterminate.
6. **Portable action specification (`P`)**: target, requested action, and timing/trigger are explicit, and recipient/responsibility is explicit enough to reproduce the inherited route. Missing responsibility/recipient can support `RECON` but never `AUTO`.

The exhaustive `ADOR` state is mechanically derived after sealing: `LIVE_PORTABLE` only if all `T,F,O,A,S,P` are supported; `LIVE_RECONCILE` if `T,F,O,A,S` are supported but `P` is incomplete; otherwise the first applicable adverse-compatible set includes `UNADOPTED_ECHO`, `TENTATIVE_OR_CONDITIONAL`, `TEXTUALLY_COMPLETED_OR_RESOLVED`, `SUPERSEDED_OR_CONFLICTING`, `NON_TARGET_OR_INFORMATIONAL`, or `UNKNOWN`. Multiple compatible states remain a set. No referee chooses a route. The locked inherited Cartesian compatibility table derives routes only after every output is sealed. `LIVE_PORTABLE` is an observable-documentary state, not a clinical obligation.

## Independent adjudication, calibration, and controls without a gold standard

R3 is independent of A1/A2/S1 and H1/H2 by roster, codebook, pseudonym, ordering, and seal. After all seals, software compares unit inventory, cited spans, proof-obligation vectors, `ADOR` state, and inherited routes across systems. Consensus cannot delete a minority unit or narrow an adverse-compatible set; R3 is a reference measurement, not an oracle. A1/A2/S1 remain independent measurements of active action and supersession. The primary reference envelope credits a favorable unit only when every applicable sealed measurement supports the required proposition; all disagreement, absent span, packet corruption, nonresponse, or indeterminacy is adverse.

Use the **same inherited pre-label controls**, adding frozen expected `ADOR` transitions rather than creating extra cases or readings: (i) formatting/section-order/nuisance-token pairs must preserve all six proof obligations and unit inventory; (ii) a one-field timing, replacement, force, completion, target-link, or adoption-bridge edit may change only its prelisted obligation and downstream compatible state; (iii) masked/inserted recommendation or plan pairs have a frozen transition; and (iv) target-linked versus wording-matched non-target plan pairs must differ only in `T/S` as specified. Transformations require unambiguous raw offsets and are otherwise `CONTROL_INFEASIBLE`, never hand-edited. Reviewers are blind to pairing and expectation. These controls can falsify logical invariance/sensitivity and implementation correctness, but they cannot validate the clinical truth of an unedited record. A planted shared-wrong fixture must demonstrate that even unanimous labels do not escape the adverse reference envelope.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meaning; evidence ceiling

A supportive result is at least one exact support-qualified atom passing every inherited and `ADOR` gate. It supports only taking that stored-text signature into a separately approved prospective within-site **silent** bridge. It does not authorize an alert or establish that the recommendation was clinically correct.

An adverse result is an atom blocked by an unadopted echo, conditionality, textual completion, target mismatch, supersession, reference discordance, control violation, or bound crossing. It shows that the proposed stored-text route is insufficiently reference-valid for nomination under this protocol, not that clinicians failed or patients were harmed. If apparently favorable actionability is concentrated in copied but unadopted spans, that is a clinically important negative finding.

An inconclusive result is sparse atom/route coverage, an infeasible nested allocation or control, absent/corrupt packet, wide bounds, missing high-weight labels, inability to resolve target identity, workload failure, broken seal, or no passing atom. Inconclusive never means safe, transportable, or clinically actionable.

MIMIC cannot establish actual viewing, physical departure visibility, span authorship, signature/finalization, who was responsible, communication, patient preference, outside plans, order/referral placement, scheduling, execution, appropriateness, malignancy, follow-up, harm, utility, benefit, causality, or forward/external transport. Even `LIVE_PORTABLE` means only that the allowed stored artifacts support the six documentary propositions. Actual clinical-live obligation and any safety/benefit claim require a prospective actual-calendar silent study with observed viewing/edit/sign/transmission, responsible team, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside-plan reconciliation, and outcomes, plus clinical governance and expert review.
