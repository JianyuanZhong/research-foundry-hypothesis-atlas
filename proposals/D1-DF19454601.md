# Episode 74: is an optional-lactate queue efficient enough to justify prospective silent testing?

## Decision, unresolved question, and falsifiable claim

At the fixed 24-hour landmark after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry, suppose an informatics service must select exactly the same hospital-specific 10% of eligible records for review under either of two modeled policies. The reference policy uses clinical state plus late non-lactate surveillance (`R_attention`). The candidate policy additionally uses whether an eligible repeat lactate is recorded by the landmark, its age, and its value when present (`R_full`). For people without a repeat lactate, the candidate policy uses explicit absence and never imputes a future result.

The operational change being evaluated is therefore **queue substitution at fixed capacity**, not ordering lactate, withholding lactate, changing treatment, or adding review slots. For hospital h, the candidate queue can remove some people selected by `R_attention` and add the same number selected by `R_full`. Eventual hospital death is a prognostic prioritization target; it is not labeled preventable or responsive to review.

Episode 73 established a strong computable comparison against non-lactate surveillance, but its primary death-capture contrast did not specify whether favorable re-ranking was concentrated enough among the actual substitutions to be operationally meaningful. Episode 74 adds a paired substitution estimand and a locked efficiency gate while preserving the parent population, clocks, variables, models, hospital-held-out estimation, +3-percentage-point materiality margin, uncertainty, and noncausal limits.

The strongest existing evidence supports only this: late lactate can add modest prognostic information in a measured sepsis cohort, and routine observation presence/timing can itself be predictive. The Episode 73 evidence memo records direct full-text XML inspection of Baysan et al. (2022; DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750`; PMCID `PMC9444407`; frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) and Sisk et al. (2021; DOI `10.1093/jamia/ocaa242`; PMCID `PMC7810439`; frozen source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). Baysan et al. reported a recalibrated C-statistic of 0.62 for APACHE IV and 0.64 after adding 24-hour lactate in 4,440 MIMIC patients with sepsis. Sisk et al. defined informative presence/observation and found that 24 of 36 reviewed studies used missing indicators or summary measures. These findings do not establish optional-result queue value, specificity beyond surveillance, operational utility, treatment benefit, or transportability.

The unresolved primary claim is:

> Among adults who reach L24 after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry and have a valid baseline lactate at least 2 mmol/L, replacing an `R_attention` top-10% within-hospital queue with an equal-capacity `R_full` queue will (a) capture at least 3 additional percentage points of all eventual hospital deaths and (b) yield at least 0.10 net additional eventual decedents per substituted review slot, with nonnegative death-capture increments in both low- and high-repeat-availability hospital strata.

The 3-point margin is the inherited primary clinical-materiality anchor. The 0.10 substitution-yield margin means at least 10 net additional eventual decedents among the candidate-policy additions versus reference-policy removals per 100 substituted slots. It is an operational concentration anchor, not a stakeholder-elicited utility threshold. Both must pass; neither means that any death was preventable.

## Two modeled review policies and estimands

For every retained hospital h with n_h eligible people, set `k_h=ceil(0.10*n_h)`. Rank outer-hospital-held-out raw linear predictors within h, breaking exact ties by ascending `patientunitstayid`.

- Reference policy `P_attention` sends the k_h highest `R_attention` scores to queue `Q_attention,h`.
- Candidate policy `P_lactate` sends the k_h highest `R_full` scores to queue `Q_full,h`.

These are modeled silent-review policies, not observed usual care. Their equal quotas ensure that any candidate addition displaces exactly one reference selection. Define additions `A_h=Q_full,h \ Q_attention,h`, removals `R_h=Q_attention,h \ Q_full,h`, and substitution count `s_h=|A_h|=|R_h|`. Save person-level queue membership privately and report only allowed aggregates.

For any frozen hospital set S:

`C_m(S) = sum_h sum_{i in Q_m,h} D_i / sum_h sum_i D_i`.

The inherited primary death-capture estimand is:

`Delta10_attention = C_Rfull(all) - C_Rattention(all)`.

The new co-primary actionability estimand is net decedent yield per substituted slot:

`Psi10_swap = [sum_h(sum_{i in A_h}D_i - sum_{i in R_h}D_i)] / sum_h s_h`.

Its numerator is necessarily the candidate-minus-reference true-positive difference, but its denominator is the number of records whose review disposition changes. Report `100*Psi10_swap` as net additional eventual decedents per 100 substituted slots, total substitutions, substitutions/total queue slots, death proportions in additions and removals, and the equivalent number of substitutions per net additional decedent only when the net gain is positive. `Psi10_swap` is undefined if there are no substitutions.

The primary hypothesis requires the lower 95% bound of `Delta10_attention` to be >=+0.03 and the lower 95% bound of `Psi10_swap` to be >=+0.10. Low- and high-availability `Delta10_attention` lower bounds must each be >=0; calculate stratum-specific `Psi10_swap` descriptively with uncertainty but do not add a post hoc stratum threshold. A 20% queue repeats both estimands with `ceil(0.20*n_h)` and cannot rescue the primary.

Required decompositions remain:

- `Delta10_naive = C_Rfull - C_Rstate`;
- `Delta10_process = C_Rprocess - C_Rattention`;
- `Delta10_numeric_measured`: refit `R_full` and `R_process` only in the F-measured population and compare their within-hospital 10% queues there; this conditional analysis cannot replace the all-population primary;
- attenuation `Delta10_naive - Delta10_attention`, descriptive and noncausal;
- for every model and hospital: overlap, additions, removals, true positives, false positives, precision, sensitivity, unflagged deaths, and queue size.

Also compute swap yield for `R_process` versus `R_attention`. This indicates whether queue changes are largely driven by lactate availability/timing. It cannot identify a causal measurement-process mechanism.

## Hospital-held-out estimation

Use leave-one-hospital-out prediction on the fixed original hospital set. For each outer hospital and model:

1. Exclude that hospital entirely.
2. In the remaining hospitals only, winsorize continuous predictors at the 1st/99th percentiles, standardize with training means and population standard deviations, and mean-impute missing continuous values to zero after scaling. Use stated count transformations. Do not scale binary indicators.
3. Fit unweighted logistic regression with intercept, L2 penalty, `C=1`, solver `lbfgs`, `max_iter=10000`, `tol=1e-8`, no class weights, and seed 20260973.
4. Score the outer hospital only with that fold's constants. No feature may be dropped. No finite training values, zero post-winsor variance, nonconvergence, or nonfinite prediction makes the required model not computable.
5. Use raw linear predictors for within-hospital ranking. Save raw logistic probabilities only for descriptive AUROC, Brier score, log loss, and calibration plots. Do not call them clinically calibrated or use threshold decision curves.

A monotone recalibration cannot change within-hospital queue membership; calibration is not a primary gate. Any future probability display or absolute-risk threshold requires independent calibration work.

## Ordered result states and falsification

Apply exactly in this order, stopping at the first matching state.

1. **Not computable.** Any source/header/hash mismatch; undocumented failure to reproduce the frozen feasibility flow; missing key or clock; wrong one-person, revision, conflict, unit, range, or window logic; failure of any population gate; leakage across outer hospitals or copied groups; changed features; incomplete scores/quotas; nonfinite or nonconverged required fits; zero pooled substitutions; or fewer than 1,900 finite bootstrap replicates for any primary contrast. Do not substitute person-random splits, measured-only primary analysis, altered windows, post hoc features, or post-L24 values.
2. **Supportive and decision-concentrated.** All gates pass; pooled `Delta10_attention` lower bound >=+0.03; pooled `Psi10_swap` lower bound >=+0.10; both availability-stratum `Delta10_attention` lower bounds >=0; the deletion criteria pass; and the pooled 20% `Delta_attention` upper bound is >=0. This supports a material, efficiently concentrated prognostic queue substitution beyond the specified non-lactate surveillance comparator in this EICU snapshot. It justifies only independent prospective silent validation.
3. **Material capture but substitution-inefficient adverse result.** The pooled `Delta10_attention` lower bound is >=+0.03, but pooled `Psi10_swap` upper bound is <+0.10. The lactate queue captures materially more decedents under the inherited denominator, but the gain is too diffuse across changed review assignments to meet the locked actionability claim.
4. **Pooled but site-process-dependent adverse result.** Both pooled primary lower bounds meet their margins, but either availability stratum has `Delta10_attention` upper bound <0 or a required deletion criterion fails. This opposes availability robustness and bars transportability or deployment claims.
5. **Surveillance-explained adverse result.** `Delta10_naive` meets the +0.03 lower-bound criterion, while `Delta10_attention` has upper bound <+0.03. The lactate-specific materiality claim does not survive the locked surveillance comparator. This does not show that clinician attention causes the naive signal.
6. **Opposite.** Pooled `Delta10_attention` upper bound <0, pooled `Psi10_swap` upper bound <0, or both availability-stratum `Delta10_attention` upper bounds <0. Because both pooled estimands share the same true-positive-difference numerator, a sign discrepancy is a computation error. This falsifies benefit in the proposed direction for this population, comparator, landmark, and quota.
7. **Adverse to materiality/actionability.** No earlier state applies, and either pooled `Delta10_attention` interval is wholly nonnegative with upper bound <+0.03 or pooled `Psi10_swap` interval is wholly nonnegative with upper bound <+0.10. This rejects at least one locked positive margin while allowing a smaller nonnegative association.
8. **Inconclusive.** Every other computable result: an interval spans zero or a required margin, favorable point estimates lack required lower bounds, strata are discordant without meeting a prior state, or deletions are unstable without meeting the site-process rule. Inconclusive does not support deployment and cannot be rescued by secondary analyses.

Attach, without changing the ordered state: **numeric component supported** only if measured-population `Delta10_numeric_measured` lower bound >0; **process-dependent** if primary support occurs but the measured-population `Delta10_numeric_measured` upper bound <=0 or the process-increment point estimate is at least the full-primary point estimate; otherwise **numeric component unresolved**.

## Evidence limits, required next data, and verifier contract

Available structured evidence can compute the cohort, source-relative clocks, four score models, equal-capacity queues, substitutions, final hospital mortality, and uncertainty. It cannot determine that a lactate was ordered for a particular reason, collected or resulted when implied clinically, displayed, viewed, reviewed, or acted upon. Missing essential evidence includes adjudicated infusion administration/indication; specimen type and assay quality; order, specimen, result-release, display, and clinician-view timestamps; clinician concern; actual review actions and baseline workflow; hospital calendar period; treatment decisions; workload and stakeholder utility weights; death time/cause/preventability; counterfactual outcomes; and patient-centered harms.

Therefore, even a supportive result cannot establish that ordering repeat lactate, exposing its value, reviewing the queue, or treating selected patients improves care or survival. It cannot establish safety, fairness, cost-effectiveness, near-term rescue opportunity, or external transportability. Clinical informatics review must adjudicate timestamp and workflow semantics. A prospective independent cohort with order/result/display/review timestamps must test whether the policy is implementable, calibrated, stable, and additive to actual usual care. A silent prospective evaluation should precede any clinician-facing use; causal patient benefit requires a subsequent impact study, ideally randomized if the policy changes care.

Harbor must retain private person-level derivatives in the workspace and produce aggregate manifests: source hashes/headers, exact flow, revision/conflict audits, feature order, fold constants, one held-out score and queue indicator per person/model, paired additions/removals, all queue cells, bootstrap draws, intervals, deletions, convergence, deterministic state, and structured conclusions. Sources remain read-only; no private row or note is published.

The verifier can recompute hashes, headers, population logic, time and tie rules, features, held-out fits, scores, quotas, substitutions, estimands, intervals, deletion proportions, and the ordered result label. It must verify the algebraic identity between the swap numerator and queue true-positive difference and reject sign inconsistencies. Fixtures must include supportive, substitution-inefficient, site-process-dependent, surveillance-explained, opposite, adverse-to-margin, inconclusive, and noncomputable outputs. They must reject correct computations paired with unsupported claims such as “order lactate,” “visible at bedside,” “finds preventable deaths,” “improves survival,” “measures occult hypoperfusion,” “is fair,” or “transports externally.” An automatic verifier cannot adjudicate clinical timestamp meaning, preventability, actual workflow, utility weights, causal benefit, safety, or transportability.
