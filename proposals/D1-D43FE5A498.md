## Unresolved question, importance, and evidence boundary

Among adults recorded as invasively ventilated at ICU hour 48 after recent vasopressor support, with at least +50 mL/kg unit-safe recorded fluid balance, adequate recorded MAP, urine output and potassium, and no active RRT/ECMO or recent loop course, is starting or restarting a qualifying ICU `inputevents` furosemide/bumetanide record during the next 12 hours associated with at least a five-percentage-point increase in the lower partial-identification probability of being alive and directly observed outside a recorded MIMIC ICU at ICU day 7, without crossing prespecified recorded renal, RRT, death or terminal-disposition harm margins?

This is consequential because active de-resuscitation may hasten movement out of intensive care, but may also accompany renal injury, dialysis or death, and treatment selection may merely mark patients already improving. The substantive advance over the parents is coherence between the named clinical milestone and its clock while retaining a destination-neutral, death-penalized recorded topology endpoint. It also prevents an EHR validation timestamp from being translated into clinician awareness, intent, bedside availability or actual receipt.

The strongest currently supported claim is only that the frozen MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot has the required admission, ICU-stay, transfer, procedure, input, laboratory, output and death fields, that exact headers are present, and that a prior treatment/effect-blind corrected audit found H120 topology more observable than H168: among 5,752 broad earliest-stay unique-V0 records, H120 classified 765 death, 3,163 observed ICU, 1,367 observed non-ICU and 457 unknown (7.945%), while H168 classified 962, 2,406, 1,520 and 864 (15.021%). The prior full-inputevents blind audit parsed 10,953,713 rows and found variable start-to-store lags. These are source feasibility and documentation facts, not final-cohort arm support, effects, medication receipt, clinical recovery or safety.

Current public evidence supports equipoise, not benefit.
