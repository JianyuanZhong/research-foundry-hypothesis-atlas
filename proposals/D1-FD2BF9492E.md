# Residual surveillance selection after HCC resection: separating targeted ascertainment from ordinary care contact

Status: substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The medication-aware cohort and extraction support were audited; no hypothesis model was fitted. The prior biological recurrence route remains frozen behind its existing report-content gate.

## Scientific opening, claim boundary, and advance

The strongest evidence-supported clinical claim is that incomplete AFP response measured at the 4–6-week postoperative visit was strongly prognostic for recurrence and survival in a selected cohort of 549 patients with preoperative AFP >400 ng/mL [K1]. That finding does not establish that persistent AFP is residual tumor or that availability of the follow-up measurement is ignorable.

Routine EHR observation is generated by care. In a two-hospital study, whether and when common laboratory tests were ordered often predicted survival independently of their values [K2]. A methodological review distinguishes patient presence from longitudinal observation and warns that methods suitable for prediction do not automatically support explanation [K3]. These works establish that observation patterns can be informative; they do not establish why a particular HCC patient was measured or whether a latent factor is tumor biology.

The unresolved question is narrower and clinically actionable: after first observed HCC resection, does prior marker history predict *targeted* postoperative surveillance beyond the opportunity to observe the patient and measured illness burden, or is the apparent informativeness of measurement fully explained by ordinary contact and severity? The leading explanation is residual marker-directed surveillance selection. The strongest rival is that patients who are sicker or simply have more local care contacts receive more of every test, so AFP/PIVKA-II remeasurement and hepatic imaging contain no HCC-specific observation signal.

Resolving this matters because retrospective biomarker studies can otherwise select a highly monitored subgroup and misstate transportability or prognostic strength. The substantive advance over the parent is an estimand that separates generic local presence from the conditional choice of an HCC-targeted mark. It also constrains the learned model so that a generic-contact factor is anchored by negative controls before any residual surveillance factor is interpreted. This is an ascertainment study, not a recurrence or treatment-effect study.

## Hypothesis and exact estimand

Primary hypothesis: among adults at a day-60 landmark after a first observed HCC resection and without recorded HCC-directed procedure or systemic therapy through day 60, pre-landmark marker availability and trajectory are associated with the probability of an HCC-targeted observation during days 61–365 *conditional on local care opportunity and observed severity*, and this association is stronger than the corresponding association with generic-care negative-control marks.

The primary unit is the patient-week. For week t, define:

- G(t), generic local care opportunity: a dated encounter or a non-target clinical activity day derived from encounter records, non-marker laboratory testing, nonhepatic examinations, or non-antitumor medication. Target AFP/PIVKA-II tests, hepatic CT/MR, and HCC-directed treatment do not create G by themselves.
- L-M(t): AFP, PIVKA-II, or AFP-L3 measurement.
- L-I(t): injury-panel measurement.
- S(t): hepatic/upper-abdominal CT or MRI report-detection.
- T(t): recorded HCC-directed procedure or systemic medication; treatment intent is a secondary adjudicated mark, never a recurrence label.
- N-L(t): exact-label sodium, creatinine, or hemoglobin measurement.
- N-I(t): nonhepatic CT/MR.

The primary estimands are standardized day-61:365 risk differences and risk ratios for L-M and S comparing high versus low/clearing pre-landmark marker trajectory among marker-observed patients, conditional on G history and measured severity. The specificity contrast is the difference between that standardized association for targeted marks (L-M, S) and for negative controls (N-L, N-I). A second estimand compares marker-observed with marker-absent patients in the full cohort; it concerns ascertainment state, not an imputed biomarker value. T is secondary because medication indication and treatment intent are not natively encoded.

“No local event” through day 365 is observed local-system absence, not nonrecurrence and not ordinary right-censoring at the last contact. All index surgeries end by 2023; inspected source maxima extend to 2025-12-31 for laboratories and 2026-01-02 for examinations, permitting a 365-day local capture window. Outside care remains unavailable.

## Matched methods on identical inputs and split

Assign patients by `int(SHA256(患者主索引)[:8],16) mod 100`: 0–59 train, 60–69 validation, 70–79 locked test, 80–99 untouched reserve. Freeze cohort, labels, medication dictionary, transformations, and thresholds before test access. Both methods receive identical patients, dated events, covariates, and split; they differ only in representation.

### Baseline: transparent two-stage weekly model

Create 44 weekly records per patient for days 61–365. Fit:

1. a negative-binomial recurrent-contact model and pooled-logistic model for G(t);
2. separate elastic-net pooled-logistic/GEE models for L-M, L-I, S, T, N-L, and N-I, first unconditionally and then conditional on G(t) and prior G history;
3. among marker-observed patients, add train-scaled preoperative marker rank, latest day-15:60/preoperative log ratio, slope and measurement count; for everyone add age, sex, year, service, pathology proxies, prior-treatment state, index length of stay, days of generic labs/imaging/medication, injury peak/latest/slope, exact label/specimen, and PIVKA medication flags.

Report standardized 365-day risks, target-versus-control specificity contrasts, calibration, log loss, and patient-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals. This baseline is scientifically legible: it directly asks whether marker history changes the choice of targeted tests given observed care opportunity. It loses irregular within-week ordering, coupling between evolving values and observation, and patient-level residual heterogeneity.

### Alternative: anchored joint state/marked-process model

Fit a continuous-time constrained model with:

- an observed-value state for injury recovery, with positive loadings for ALT/AST/bilirubin/INR and negative loadings for albumin/platelets;
- a marker trajectory state for AFP and optional PIVKA-II/AFP-L3, estimated only where values support it and never called tumor;
- a generic-contact frailty U anchored with loading +1 on G and allowed to affect all event types;
- a target-selection frailty V anchored with loading +1 on L-M, constrained to zero on G and the generic controls N-L/N-I, with loadings estimated for S and T;
- exact label/specimen and calendar-era offsets, medication flags, observed pathology/severity, and history through t− only.

Compare nested models with U only versus U+V. V is evidence of residual covariance specific to HCC-directed observation after measured contact/severity, not proof of an unmeasured disease state. Evaluate held-out marked-process log likelihood, weekly calibration, Brier scores for S/T, posterior predictive event counts, loading/seed stability, and paired patient bootstrap intervals. Require three random seeds and simulation-based recovery of U and V (correlation ≥0.8, no factor swap, nominal interval coverage 90–98% in prespecified scenarios).

The alternative is scientifically useful only if it improves held-out targeted-mark fit without improving generic-control marks and yields stable target-specific loadings. Otherwise the transparent baseline is preferred. A generic transformer is deferred because better prediction would not resolve contact versus target selection. Inverse-intensity weighting is a sensitivity only; it cannot recover unobserved external care or absent outcomes.

## Negative controls, gates, and falsification

Negative controls are N-L and N-I, plus train-only permutation of marker values within calendar era and marker-observation stratum. A valid model should retain generic-contact structure but lose marker-value-specific target association after permutation. A “negative-control failure” is a marker association of similar size with N-L/N-I or a V loading materially different from zero on a generic mark.

Proceed with the primary baseline because N=15,575, S-or-T=1,616, and train/validation/test S-or-T counts are 970/151/152. Before fitting final models, require at least 100 medication-positive encounter snippets and 100 dictionary-negative oncology-like snippets reviewed by a pharmacist/hepatobiliary clinician with PPV and NPV ≥0.85; otherwise T is reported as unclassified treatment occurrence only. Require ≥50 marker-absent patients in validation and test (observed 56 and 73), ≥150 events for every primary fitted mark overall and ≥20 in validation and test, and no standardized-weight effective sample size below 100. Sparse “neither” and nonexistent marker-only states are descriptive, not modeled as four categories.

The joint alternative additionally requires ≥500 participants with two marker and two injury dates (observed 11,767), convergence in all three seeds, simulation recovery, and nondegenerate U/V loadings. Failure leaves the baseline ascertainment audit as the completed deliverable and makes latent-process conclusions inconclusive.

Supportive result: marker history has a stable contact-conditioned association with L-M and/or S, materially exceeds its association with both negative controls, and the U+V model improves held-out targeted-mark likelihood/calibration while V remains null for controls. Any naive complete-case S/T association must also change beyond its bootstrap uncertainty after observation modeling. This supports residual target-specific surveillance selection in this institution.

Adverse result: marker associations vanish after G and measured severity are included, are no larger than control associations, and U-only performs as well as U+V. This supports the ordinary severity/contact rival and argues against calling the observation pattern HCC-specific.

Inconclusive result: poor medication-review accuracy; sparse split-specific marks; positivity or effective-sample-size failure; unstable/factor-swapped latent components; incompatible assay eras; target-only encounters that prevent contact conditioning; or intervals spanning both clinically meaningful attenuation and amplification. An imprecise null is not adverse evidence.

## Claim limits and deliverable

The future solver must produce a frozen cohort flow and dictionary review; event support by split and measurement stratum; fitted baseline and U-only/U+V alternatives; standardized target and control contrasts with uncertainty; held-out metrics; simulation and seed diagnostics; and a machine-checkable supportive/adverse/inconclusive conclusion linked to those outputs.

The verifier can check source hashes, joins, temporal leakage, cohort and event definitions, split isolation, review-sample bookkeeping, fits, diagnostics, intervals, and whether the conclusion follows the thresholds. It cannot certify true recurrence, unrecorded outside contact, HCC treatment indication, clinician motivation, causal effects of testing, assay equivalence, or a biological meaning for V. Those require clinical adjudication, linked external data, or prospective scheduled surveillance.

## Alternatives retained and revisit triggers

- Four-state measurement multinomial: rejected after observed positivity failure; revisit only with ≥100 patients in every modeled cell in validation and test.
- Unanchored joint latent marked point process: rejected because shared random effects alone cannot distinguish severity/contact from target-specific selection.
- Anchored U+V model: selected as the substantive alternative because generic controls make its incremental claim falsifiable; still association-based.
- Baseline-only report: selected if U/V recovery fails; it remains clinically useful by quantifying contact-conditioned surveillance selection.
- Biological recurrence route: remains frozen. Reopen only under the parent’s adjudicated untreated-report gate or prospective scheduled imaging.
- Generic transformer: revisit only if the clinical target changes to prediction and sufficient adjudicated outcomes exist.
