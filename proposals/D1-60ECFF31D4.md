# Recorded downstream-care linkage audit for assay observation in frozen HCC repeat-TACE validation

## Targeted successor and narrow substantive repair

This is a targeted two-parent successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE population, event reconstruction, B/P/event/Y clocks, annual forward blocks, locked nested ridge estimand, assay-separated missing-Y bounds, outcome-blind ascertainment strata, and all noncausal evidence limits. It does not change the primary estimand or add a feature to either prognostic model.

The unresolved operational question is:

> Among outcome-blind `BP_eligible` repeat-TACE episodes, is recording of the short-horizon post-event assay `Y` more frequent when the source record contains independently recorded downstream-care processes in the same prespecified window, and is that linkage consistent across albumin and total bilirubin, arms, and calendar blocks?

The repair makes “observation” operationally relevant without calling an encounter, order, examination, medication, procedure, or document proof that an assay was completed, reviewed, clinically interpretable, administered, or beneficial. The estimand remains prognostic association with a recorded laboratory value, not treatment response, a causal effect, safety, survival, hepatic failure, utility, actionability, or patient benefit.

The strongest available support remains the parents’ narrow executed audit: selected short-horizon complete-triplet prediction is feasible locally, while later outcome ascertainment and transport are weak. The new downstream-care linkage results are not executed findings and must not be described as evidence until the compiler runs the full-source pass.

## Recorded downstream-care process construction

For every BP-eligible episode, construct process flags using only non-lab tables and only timestamps strictly after `event_time`. Define two non-overlapping windows:

* `early_process`: `(event_time, event_time + 24 hours]`;
* `late_process`: `(event_time + 24 hours, event_time + 72 hours]`.

The early window is the primary linkage window. The late window is a delayed documentation/care-capture diagnostic and must never be interpreted as a cause of the assay observed in the initial Y window. A row contributes to a process family only when its required timestamp is finite and falls in the stated window.

Define the following source-family flags separately, retaining the first valid timestamp and count of distinct composite keys/rows for audit:

1. `followup_encounter`: at least one additional encounter key, distinct from the selected repeat encounter, with finite `encounters.就诊时间` in the window. The selected encounter itself does not count as follow-up.
2. `examination_record`: at least one linked `examinations` row with finite `开始时间` in the window. This is an examination record/opportunity, not proof the examination was completed or read.
3. `non_drug_order`: at least one linked `orders` row with finite `开立时间` in the window; if `开立时间` is invalid, use finite `开始时间` only, and if both are invalid classify the row as untimed/unknown rather than none. `结束时间` alone cannot establish initiation. Retain `医嘱(非药品)` only for source audit and category counts; do not text-interpret it as a clinical service.
4. `medication_record`: at least one linked `medications` row with finite `开始时间` in the window. `结束时间` alone is not initiation. This indicates a recorded medication order only, not administration, delivery, adherence, or response.
5. `procedure_record`: at least one linked `procedures` row with finite `开始时间` in the window. `结束时间` and `手术来源` are retained for audit but do not convert a row into verified procedure completion; do not use the procedure name to assert a treatment was delivered.
6. `document_presence`: a nonempty linked `clinical_documents` row for the selected repeat encounter, reported separately as an untimed recording marker. It is not assigned to early or late and cannot prove downstream timing.

For each episode and window report all six family flags plus `timed_family_count` across the first five families. Pre-specify mutually exclusive primary linkage strata based on the early window: `none` (zero timed families), `single_family` (exactly one), and `multi_family` (at least two). Report source-specific flags and document presence alongside these strata; do not use the strata to redefine the population. If an episode has invalid timestamps in a source, emit source-specific `unknown_time` diagnostics and do not silently treat it as absent. For the late window report `late_none`, `late_single_family`, and `late_multi_family` descriptively only.

The primary linkage table, separately for albumin and bilirubin, origin/block, and arm, is among all BP-eligible episodes and contains: N, Y-observed count/fraction with Wilson 95% interval, missing-Y count/fraction, early stratum counts/fractions, and Y-observed fraction within each early stratum. Also report risk differences and exact or Wilson intervals only as descriptive association diagnostics, suppressing/marking cells with fewer than five BP-eligible episodes. Repeat the table by individual source family where support permits. A cross-assay table is restricted to episodes BP-eligible for both assays, keeps assay-specific Y denominators, and reports the paired observation pattern; no pooling or discarding assay-specific rows is allowed.

For observed Y only, report whether the earliest valid Y timestamp precedes or follows the first timed downstream-care timestamp. This is a temporal-order audit, not a new endpoint: `care_before_Y`, `Y_before_care`, and `no_timed_care`. Do not use this post-Y classification for any model or bound. It can reveal whether a nominally downstream process is plausibly upstream of the recorded assay or merely a consequence of recording it. If Y is missing, temporal order is unknowable and remains missing.

## Falsification and negative controls

The following checks are prespecified and outcome-blind wherever possible.

1. **Delayed-window negative control.** Compare Y-observation fractions by `late_process` stratum. A late-only process occurs after the end of the Y window and cannot establish completion of that Y. Any association is labeled care-capture/selection diagnostic; it cannot rescue a weak early linkage, alter bounds, or support clinical benefit. If late-only association is materially stronger than early association, flag possible documentation/capture bias.
2. **Pre-event negative-control process.** Construct the same five timed family flags in `(-72 hours, event_time]`, using only valid timestamps and never using them as model features. Report their association with Y observation. A strong pre-event gradient may indicate baseline severity or encounter intensity rather than downstream completion; it falsifies a simple interpretation that early post-event care recording alone explains observation. No null assumption is imposed, and no favorable result is required.
3. **Source-family specificity control.** Repeat the linkage table after leaving out each family in turn. A claimed operational linkage is not accepted if it exists only because one source family has a timestamp artifact or duplicate-key inflation. Any source-family duplicate anomaly or nonfinite-time rate that prevents a stable leave-one-family-out result makes the composite linkage inconclusive.
4. **Temporal-shift placebo.** Shift each episode's event clock by +7 days solely for a placebo window, while retaining the original event for all cohort/model definitions, and test whether the same records generate implausibly similar early-process/Y-observation linkage. This is a diagnostic for broad patient-level recording intensity, not an alternative clinical estimand. Do not run it if shifted windows overlap a subsequent selected event for the same patient; label that placebo unavailable rather than dropping the original episode.
5. **Negative interpretation branch.** Favorable observed complete-triplet prediction with an early-stratum global identification envelope crossing zero remains selection-sensitive. A positive early linkage does not validate MAR, and a negative or absent linkage does not prove assay noncompletion because external care and unrecorded completion are unavailable. A nonpositive primary prognostic result remains adverse even if a care stratum has high observation.

## Fail-closed rules and interpretation precedence

Stop or mark the linkage result `unsupported` if source hashes/headers, key uniqueness audit, inherited denominators, event clocks, or the outcome-blind BP risk set fail. If a required timestamp is invalid, use `unknown_time`; never impute time or classify it as `none`. If a source file cannot be fully scanned, the composite process result is unavailable and no linkage conclusion is made. Suppress/mark cells with fewer than five rows; do not collapse them post hoc. If a block has fewer than 20 BP-eligible episodes overall or fewer than 10 per arm, all linkage results are descriptive and interpretation is inconclusive.

The original missing-Y analysis remains unchanged: fit only on pre-origin observed complete triplets; use fixed predictions over all BP rows; retain training-range bounds; future out-of-range Y, degenerate range, nonfinite prediction, or failed denominator makes the bound unsupported. No process marker may be used to impute Y, reweight Y, alter the primary SD denominator, or change the all-BP denominator.

Interpretation precedence is: (1) computationally invalid/unsupported, (2) inconclusive for failed support, sparse cells, source-timing failure, or capture-confounded negative controls, (3) selection-sensitive when observation gradients or global bounds undermine robustness, (4) adverse when the primary prognostic contrast is nonpositive despite adequate support, and only then (5) limited supportive evidence if the inherited prognostic criteria and a clearly documented, non-differential early observation pattern both hold. Even the last label supports only selected short-horizon prognostic association with a recorded lab value. It cannot establish that downstream care was completed, that an assay was clinically interpreted, that TACE was administered with a known intent, that the assay changed care, or that any patient-important outcome improved.

Computationally checkable outputs are source/hash/header checks, duplicate-safe joins, exact clocks, process flags and counts, timestamp-order classifications, Wilson arithmetic, leave-one-family-out and placebo bookkeeping, prediction/bound arithmetic, and fail-closed labels. The configured data lack assay units and reference ranges, validated clinical completion/adherence, TACE intent/administration, tumor burden or imaging response, external-care capture, mortality, hepatic failure, toxicity, patient-reported outcomes, and utility. Those claims require chart adjudication and an independent prospective or externally linked study.

No HCC rows or new empirical result is claimed by this proposal. Full-source execution, including the care-process audit and all negative controls, is required before any result is reported.
