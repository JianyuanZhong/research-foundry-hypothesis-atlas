# Episode 17 provisional hypothesis: persistent discordant AKI recovery beyond current severity

Status: provisional and evidence-bounded. This is a research task, not a completed analysis; no cohort counts, effect estimates, or clinical conclusions are claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adults with an ICU stay and a prespecified creatinine-defined AKI episode who survive to a fixed 48-hour landmark, persistent creatinine improvement without concurrent urine-output improvement will identify higher subsequent renal deterioration or in-hospital death than concordant recovery, after adjustment for the same landmark severity, treatment state, and observation intensity.

This is an incremental prognostic hypothesis, not a mechanism, causal treatment effect, or proof of true renal recovery. The leading rival is that apparent discordance is generated by current illness severity, selective measurement, diuretic/RRT exposure, or clinician-driven treatment rather than persistent renal vulnerability.

## Evidence boundary

The inherited MIMIC-IV branch and its attached inspected works support a measurement concern: creatinine and urine output can change discordantly, and creatinine-based kidney-function assessment may be inaccurate in critical illness. [K1] supports the measurement concern, [K2] supports a prespecified creatinine/urine-output operational definition while highlighting implementation gaps, and [K3] bounds interpretation of subsequent CRRT initiation as clinician-driven rather than an objective indication. None establishes the proposed association, prevalence, measured GFR/cystatin C, fluid-status adjudication, or treatment benefit.

The unresolved claim is whether persistent discordance adds reproducible information beyond current severity and measurement intensity.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The simple baseline is a landmark logistic or Cox model with current creatinine, creatinine slope, urine-output summary, AKI stage, age/sex, severity, treatment, and measurement intensity. The substantive alternative is a compact trajectory model using pre-landmark creatinine and urine-output sequences (slopes, nadir, persistence, and co-movement), with identical patient splits, held-out temporal evaluation, calibration, discrimination, uncertainty, and measurement-intensity sensitivity analyses.

The alternative can reveal whether persistence and ordering contain information lost by a cross-sectional mismatch label. It is deferred if sequence support is inadequate. CPU is adequate for both; GPU use is not required unless the trajectory implementation becomes matrix-intensive.

## Falsification and interpretation

Support requires a prespecified directionally consistent association and reproducible incremental calibration/discrimination in held-out data, with uncertainty and sensitivity analyses showing it is not explained solely by missing urine measurements, diuretic/RRT exposure, or current severity. Adverse results are no incremental signal, reversal, or instability after these adjustments. Inconclusive results include sparse discordant cells, inadequate follow-up, or wide uncertainty. None supports mechanism or treatment benefit.

## Missing evidence and next checks

The association, eligible cohort size, and measurement density remain untested. Measured GFR/cystatin C, reliable fluid-status adjudication, complete urine measurement, and clinical adjudication of AKI/recovery and RRT indication are unavailable or incomplete. Cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid because timestamps are subject-specific shifts. No numerical result is claimed.
