# Proposal: report-concordant post-TACE trajectories without mistaking local text for response

## Episode 18 child and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of assessed valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It freezes that candidate's eligible HCC population, first encounter-constrained TACE-like index, day-45 risk set, unconditioned days 46-365 primary recorded-care transition, procedure-specificity tiers, diagnosis ascertainment overlays, observation process, temporal splits and evidence limits.

The new scientific repair is a time-aligned report-corroboration layer. The dataset has dated examination reports, but they are not validated radiologic labels. The future solver must therefore newly construct a frozen report vocabulary audit and test whether the biomarker trajectory signal is more specific to a pre-outcome documented report-concern pathway than to a report-reassuring, indeterminate, no-report or generic-care pathway. This tests a clinically important interpretation question without replacing the primary target or claiming response.

Completion requires:
1. an auditable cohort, index, day-45 landmark, episode, diagnosis and patient-level split manifest;
2. exact-assay AFP/hepatic trajectory features and observation states;
3. a frozen examination/report audit and report-link manifest;
4. newly fitted B0, Bobs, B2 and, if convergent, M3 models;
5. locked predictions, cause-specific hazards, 30/90/320-day cumulative incidences, calibration, log scores, alert curves and patient-clustered uncertainty;
6. primary, specificity-tier, report-concordance and negative-control contrasts; and
7. an interpretation file mapping every conclusion to an output, estimate, interval and prespecified rule.

Discovery has fitted no model and asserts no result.

## Strongest supported evidence and unresolved claim

The strongest available claim is only that this HCC snapshot contains linkable dated encounters, procedures, laboratory tests and examinations, plus diagnoses joined to encounters. Direct audits found 338,040 procedure rows and 1,810,646 diagnosis rows; the broad procedure audit and HCC diagnosis counts are recorded in `work/episode18-support.md`. These records can support local recorded-pathway prognosis and report-language ascertainment sensitivities. They do not establish procedure completion, intent, active tumor, radiologic response, recurrence, survival or benefit.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among adults with a first recorded TACE-like episode and no therapeutic liver-directed episode through day 45, does an early AFP/hepatic trajectory add reproducible information about a later local recorded therapeutic pathway beyond contact and measurement opportunity, and is that information preferentially aligned with a preceding dated report-concern proxy rather than a report-reassuring or generic-care pathway?

Primary hypothesis: on the full eligible day-45 risk set, B2 versus Bobs retains the parent’s prespecified incremental criterion—at least a 10 percentage-point reduction in the locked-test alert fraction at 90% sensitivity for the first broad recorded therapeutic transition, with a patient-clustered interval excluding zero and acceptable calibration/overlap. The report-concordance hypothesis is that, among adequately supported therapeutic episodes with a qualifying prior examination, the trajectory-associated contrast is larger for `R_concern` and for `S_bundle ∩ R_concern` than for `R_reassuring` or `R_indeterminate`, and is not reproduced for non-liver capture or pre-index placebo controls. This is a hypothesis about recorded report/pathway concordance, not response.

A primary result can remain informative even if the report overlay is sparse: it then supports only recorded-care prognostic stratification. If the report overlay reverses or is indistinguishable, the interpretation shifts toward coding, selection or capture rather than a disease-linked signal.

## Population, joins and time

Use the parent rules without silent changes:
- Aggregate every child table by `(患者主索引, 就诊号)` before joining to `encounters`; never create a many-to-many join.
- Build same-encounter procedure episodes from timed `procedures.开始时间`, retaining raw name, start/end, source, row count, missing-time and component flags. The locked primary index uses the earliest timed episode containing literal `TACE`, a name containing `动脉化疗栓塞`, or both `肝动脉` and `栓塞`; 24-hour within-encounter grouping and lexical/source sensitivities are retained.
- Require age >=18 and nonmissing `encounters.性别`; require a `diagnoses.诊断名称` containing `肝细胞癌` within 180 days before through 7 days after the index encounter, using `encounters.就诊时间` only as a proxy because diagnoses has no timestamp; report diagnosis type.
- Require index `就诊时间 <= 2025-01-01 00:00:00`; define nominal follow-up through 365 days and report local observed follow-up.
- Exclude primary-ontology therapeutic episodes through day 45 from the later-action risk set, report those early paths separately, and follow days 46-365. The primary outcome remains the first broad therapeutic transition, with alternate liver-directed, mixed/unclassified, non-liver and end-of-local-observation states competing.
- Patient-level temporal split: 2010-2022 fit, 2023 tuning, and 2024 through cutoff locked test when supported; otherwise deterministic patient hash. No patient crosses splits. Every vocabulary, threshold, imputation, weighting and M3 choice is fit-only.

## Report layer and estimands

The primary `P_broad`, `S_explicit`, `S_bundle`, `A_ambiguous`, `U_mixed`, `D_event`, `D_stable_baseline` and observation estimands remain exactly as in the parent.

Define a report as a row in `examinations` with nonmissing `开始时间` and a predeclared imaging/procedure-name screen from `检查` (for example CT/MR/MRI/磁共振/增强/超声/彩超/造影). The exact matched strings, counts, patients, encounters, dates and report completeness are frozen in `report_vocabulary_audit.csv` using fit data only. The report payload is only `检查所见` and `检查诊断`; no raw text is sent outside the private workspace.

For a dated report outside the outcome encounter and strictly before the subsequent therapeutic episode:
- `R_concern`: a frozen, conservative screen in either report field for concern terms such as residual/viable lesion, progression, new/enlarging lesion or suspicious enhancement;
- `R_reassuring`: a frozen screen for no-active-disease/no-enhancing-lesion/shrinkage-or-stability language;
- `R_indeterminate`: a qualifying report with neither category or with conflicting categories;
- `R_none`: no qualifying report before the event or within the report-observation window.

Negation, history and uncertainty rules use only the documented local unvalidated detector and are audited; no implicit clinical interpretation is added. A report is linked to an outcome only if its time is 7-90 days before the first outcome episode, outside that episode's encounter. Nearest-prior and any-prior windows are both prespecified sensitivities. Reports in the same encounter as the procedure are not predictors of that procedure. A report category is never used to label an unobserved biological state.

Two report analyses are required:
1. **Concordance overlay:** retain the unconditioned primary transition, then stratify its first event by `R_concern`, `R_reassuring`, `R_indeterminate`, `R_none`, and their intersections with `S_explicit`, `S_bundle`, `D_event`. This is a pathway characterization; the report is observed before the event and must not define eligibility.
2. **Proximal report sensitivity:** from the same day-45 risk set, model the first qualifying report at days 46-120 as concern, reassuring or indeterminate with report opportunity/no-report censoring explicit. Bobs includes report opportunity but not report payload; B2 adds the biomarker trajectory. This sensitivity tests whether the trajectory predicts documented concern before later therapy, but cannot repair missing outside imaging.

Never substitute a future report for the parent’s primary outcome, condition the primary population on a future report, or call any category recurrence, viable tumor, radiologic response or treatment intent.

## Models and the scientifically substantive alternative

Fit all methods to the same patients, outcomes, report windows, splits and uncertainty:
- **B0:** regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazards using age, sex, year, department, pre-index procedure/diagnosis history, day-45 contact and observation intensity, procedure source mix, missingness and local-observation variables.
- **Bobs:** B0 plus assay/examination opportunity, report opportunity, diagnosis-contact and local-retention features, but no numeric AFP/hepatic values or report payload.
- **B2:** Bobs plus exact frozen pre-index and days 7-45 AFP/hepatic numeric values, changes, timing, assay identity and counts. No units are inferred or pooled.
- **M3:** a sparse low-rank irregular-time state-space model with separate latent AFP and hepatic-function states, assay-specific observation models, report-observation intensity, and competing recorded transitions. It can expose asynchronous sampling and biomarker discordance that B2 summaries lose. Evaluate M3 on precisely the same locked primary and report-sensitive targets; require convergence and calibration before interpreting it.

A generic transformer is deferred because images, adjudicated report labels, procedure intent, diagnosis time, outside-care completeness and clinical response are unavailable. That is a dependency decision, not a ban on neural models or GPU use. B0/Bobs/B2 are expected CPU workloads; M3 is planned within 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds, with one allocated A100 (`cuda:0`) optional only if measured profiling shows a material benefit. These are planning estimates, not measured full-study runtime.

## Falsification and interpretation

Freeze all margins and bootstrap units before locked testing.

Supportive computation requires:
- B2 versus Bobs meets the parent’s >=10-point 90%-sensitivity alert-fraction margin with uncertainty excluding zero, good calibration and acceptable observation-weight diagnostics on `P_broad`;
- report-concern and `S_bundle ∩ R_concern` contrasts are directionally stronger than reassuring/indeterminate strata, with intervals and at least 100 locked events where a margin is claimed; otherwise report sparse/inconclusive;
- the result is not reproduced for non-liver capture or pre-index placebo, is not confined to high-contact patients, and remains directionally stable under nearest-prior/any-prior and observation-weighted sensitivities;
- the primary direction persists for `S_explicit` and `S_bundle` without requiring a future report, and M3, if run, converges and improves the prespecified same-target metric by at least 5 alert-fraction points or 0.02 mean log score with calibrated uncertainty.

Adverse evidence includes Bobs explaining the primary gain; report payload adding no information beyond report opportunity; equal concern and reassuring associations; disappearance in `S_explicit`/`S_bundle`/`D_event`; effects confined to one source/contact stratum; similar non-liver/placebo gains; reversal under time-link sensitivity; lexical instability or poor calibration/overlap. These findings support capture, coding, episode or text-ascertainment explanations—not a biological trajectory claim.

Inconclusive evidence includes low report coverage, too few report-concern or specificity events, high mixed/untimed procedure fractions, unresolved assay identity, insufficient follow-up, positivity/ESS failure, temporal-split failure, intervals spanning margins, M3 nonconvergence, or report-language conflicts. A supportive computation establishes only reproducible local recorded-report/pathway stratification. Radiologic response, viable tumor, recurrence, treatment need/intent/completion, causal benefit, survival, mortality, utility, recommendation, outside-care completeness and external validity require image-linked clinical adjudication, additional outcomes, prospective or independently linked data, or another study.

The verifier can check source bindings, leakage-safe joins, report term freezing, split integrity, predictions, metrics, uncertainty and interpretation-to-output linkage. It cannot establish whether a lexical report screen is clinically correct or whether a recorded pathway reflects active HCC or benefit.
