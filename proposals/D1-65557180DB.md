# Self-contained raw-source replication of semantic undercoverage in eICU care-plan ventilation labels

## Question and advance

The assessed-valid parents found that the two exact `carePlanGeneral` Ventilation labels were sparse and did not improve hospital-held-out prediction of a later structured respiratory-setting reduction. The unresolved, falsifiable alternative was semantic undercoverage: perhaps hospitals used omitted or local invasive-airway encodings, so the exact-label failure was an artifact of an incomplete vocabulary or denominator.

This child tests that alternative without relying on an ephemeral prior-workspace row artifact. It reconstructs the target-independent first-stay landmark denominator directly from the immutable eICU raw sources, audits the complete care-plan vocabulary outcome-blind, and compares exact, broader, and additional ascertainment definitions. The substantive advance is an independently executable replication of cohort membership and receipt concentration, with explicit filter flows and positive-control identity checks. It does not claim that any exported care-plan label establishes a bedside evaluation, spontaneous breathing trial, extubation, readiness, quality, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Frozen design

The reconstruction preserves the assessed lineage's adult target-independent first qualifying ICU stay per `uniquepid`, ordered by `unitvisitnumber` and `patientunitstayid`; landmarks 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes; strict pre-landmark physiology and recent FiO2/PEEP requirements; pre-landmark end-of-life and recent `Spontaneous - adequate` exclusions; and receipt in the inclusive `[L,L+360]` window. Four principal airway definitions remain `exact_unbounded`, `exact_airway_24h`, `exact_airway_12h`, and `exact_airway_6h`. The raw reconstruction also retains the parent broad RT-state and interval diagnostics, but these are sensitivity diagnostics and do not replace the frozen exact definitions.

Receipt is target-independent and classified from the first exact target timestamp in the classification window. The exact targets are normalized versions of `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`. Other Ventilation values are retained as `other_ventilation_value`, not silently treated as exact receipt. The later chart-proxy outcome remains documentation of a non-peak reduction in `(L+360,L+1080]`, with ICU discharge beyond the outcome window and no imputation of missing paired components. The raw reconstruction focuses on the denominator and receipt audit; the inherited hospital-held-out outcome model and ascertainment sensitivity remain bounded evidence rather than bedside validation.

## Executed raw results

The complete-source scan found 3,115,018 `carePlanGeneral` rows, 200,859 patient rows, 20,168,176 respiratory-charting rows, 865,381 respiratory-care rows, 146,671,642 periodic-vital rows, 25,075,074 aperiodic-vital rows, and 1,433 carePlanEOL rows. It found exactly eight normalized values in the Ventilation group: missing (7,581), `Non-invasive ventilation` (26,836), `Spontaneous - adequate` (190,809), `Spontaneous - tenuous` (32,587), `Ventilated - chronic dependency` (3,105), `Ventilated - rapid wean/extubation` (5,705), `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` (51,862), and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` (14,907). Thus no additional exported site-local lexical string was available for a vocabulary rescue.

The raw filter-flow reconstruction produced 3722 selected rows for `exact_unbounded`, 2612 for the 24-hour airway bound, 1774 for 12 hours, and 1178 for 6 hours before the later complete-outcome restriction. The broad parent RT-state diagnostic produced 4688 rows. The exact-receipt positive control was 540 reconstructed target-receipt rows, matching the frozen reference count of 540, across 443 stays and 74 hospitals, with zero missing or extra `(sid, landmark)` keys in the inherited check.

In the raw reconstructed exact-unbounded set, 108 of 3722 rows had an exact target receipt (2.902%), 18 hospitals contributed target receipt, 59 of 77 denominator hospitals were target-free, and target receipt HHI was 0.4674. The corresponding bounded sets were also sparse and concentrated. The prespecified raw gate was adverse across the primary definitions: each had at least 1000 rows, target receipt below 5%, at least half of denominator hospitals target-free, and target HHI above 0.20; the positive-control identity check passed. This supports the parents' conclusion that missing exported synonyms are not a plausible generic multicenter rescue in this snapshot.

The separate outcome-linked parent replication found, after complete outcome ascertainment, only 1–3 additional `Ventilated -` alternative rows per airway definition, all in one hospital, with 0.098–0.132 percentage-point broader coverage gains. Hospital-held-out broader-minus-process loss was positive in all bounded definitions, and 100 nested stay-cluster refits gave probabilities of worse equal-hospital macro loss of 0.79, 0.85, and 0.95 for 24-, 12-, and 6-hour bounds. The ascertainment parent retained all 9,153 classification-complete rows before outcome restriction and found no measured IPW rescue; 12- and 6-hour intervals remained adverse while the overall ascertainment classification was conservatively inconclusive because the strictest weighted shift crossed its sensitivity threshold.

## Falsification and interpretation

Support for semantic undercoverage would require a reproducible multisite expansion of exact receipt and a prespecified predictive rescue after generic documentation opportunity controls. The raw reconstruction falsifies that possibility at the vocabulary/coverage level: exhaustive enumeration yields no omitted string, and the exact-receipt positive control and filter flows are reproduced from raw sources. The outcome-linked analyses provide no bounded predictive rescue.

An adverse result means that further outcome-guided synonym expansion of this exported field is not supported as a generic multicenter structured predictor for the frozen chart proxy. An inconclusive result would mean that the raw binding, positive control, outcome ascertainment, or hospital support failed; that did not occur for the raw cohort/receipt audit, although sparse alternatives and fixed observed hospitals limit transport. A supportive result would justify prospective semantic/workflow adjudication, not bedside interpretation.

The result is limited to exported structured documentation. It cannot establish actual invasive ventilation, evaluation, SBT, extubation, readiness, care quality, benefit, harm, causal effects, or transport to unseen hospitals. Those claims require local interface metadata, direct device/SBT/extubation events, blinded clinical adjudication, and external or prospective validation.
