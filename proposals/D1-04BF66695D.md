TITLE
Validation-first durable documented liberation audit with explicit ascertainment gates for eICU decision-time association

SUBSTANTIVE CHILD CHANGE
This child retains the parent's validation-first estimand and strict landmark cohort, but makes the previously deferred 48-hour outcome executable rather than treating absence of a later marker as liberation. It adds (i) a fixed, fully observable event window, (ii) a tiered outcome definition that separates a weak care-plan signal from independent markers, (iii) an explicit documentation-density gate for every claimed durable interval, (iv) competing-risk and truncation rules, and (v) a locked sequence in which concordance and overlap are evaluated before any adjusted association. No conclusion is causal and no care-plan state is treated as proof of SBT, extubation, or bedside readiness.

CLINICAL QUESTION AND HYPOTHESES
Among adults with documented invasive-ventilation evidence and a strict pre-landmark physiologic screen, do the exact eICU care-plan states “Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation” and “Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial” identify distinct, reproducible decision-time trajectories toward a durable documented liberation proxy?

The validation hypothesis is that an exposure label temporally precedes a liberation signal and that the signal has adequate independent-marker concordance, recurrent-invasive-marker ascertainment, and comparable documentation across strategies and hospitals. Only if this validation hypothesis passes will the secondary hypothesis be examined: a documented daily-evaluation decision is associated with a higher 72-hour risk of the durable documented proxy than a documented no-trial decision. This is a noncausal association between recorded decisions and recorded outcomes, not an effect of an SBT or a recommendation for practice.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
The completed feasibility scan supports availability and a strict-cohort size, not clinical validity: 200,859 patient rows and 323,079 deduplicated nonnegative Ventilation care-plan rows were scanned; 3,743 adult candidate person-landmarks were formed; 540 remained after strict physiology, invasive evidence, EOL/prior-spontaneous exclusions, and first qualifying ICU stay per uniquepid, with 309 evaluation and 231 no-trial labels. There are 3 hospitals with at least 10 observations per strategy (248, 252, 420), but independent RT Vent Off is sparse (3/309 versus 2/231) and two-type first-marker concordance is 18/309 versus 4/231. These are reasons to audit, not evidence of benefit or harm.

STRICT PRE-LANDMARK SCREEN
For each candidate (sid,L), use only observations with event offset < L. Require FiO2 present in [L-360,L) with most recent normalized value <=0.50 (21–100% mapped to 0.21–1.00), PEEP present with most recent value <=8, at least two valid SpO2 values in [L-120,L) with median 88 to <100, latest valid RR 8–35/min in that window, and at least one valid invasive or noninvasive MAP with median >=65. Require pre-L invasive evidence from a preceding qualifying Ventilation care-plan state, or RT-on plus contemporaneous FiO2/PEEP, or ETT/trach airway plus settings. This screen is an operational enrichment criterion, not bedside readiness.

DURABLE OUTCOME: LOCKED ALGORITHMS
Let D be the first decision offset in [L,L+360]. All post-D outcome times are measured from D, but an outcome must be observable through its entire durability interval.

1. Candidate liberation signals, each with its own timestamp E, are:
   a. `CP`: first `Spontaneous - adequate` at offset D < E <= D+1440;
   b. `RT`: first exact `RT Vent On/Off` value Off/off at D < E <= D+1440;
   c. `ETT`: first exact endotracheal-tube-removal treatment at D < E <= D+1440.
No candidate after D+1440 is eligible for the primary 48-hour endpoint because its 48-hour follow-up would exceed the 72-hour horizon.

2. Define high-specificity recurrent invasive marker R after E as the earliest offset > E of: RT Vent On/Off value Continued/Start/Suspended when it denotes renewed invasive ventilation; an invasive Ventilation care-plan state (daily evaluation, no daily trial, chronic dependency, or rapid wean/extubation); exact ETT insertion/repositioning/reintubation/tracheostomy-support treatment; or a respiratoryCare status after E showing ETT/trach together with ventilator settings. A respiratoryCare `ventendoffset` field alone never creates either E or R.

3. Define observation adequacy for the putative no-recurrence interval (E,E+2880] as all of: (a) `unitdischargeoffset >= E+2880`; (b) no ICU death/discharge before E+2880; and (c) documentation in each successive 12-hour bin intersecting (E,E+2880] of at least one post-E source row from carePlanGeneral, respiratoryCharting, respiratoryCare, treatment, or vitalPeriodic/vitalAperiodic. A bin with only rows whose offsets are before E is not covered. This is a documentation gate, not an assumption that no chart row means no ventilation.

4. `durable_CP` is 1 only when E is the CP signal, observation adequacy holds, and no R occurs in (E,E+2880]. `durable_independent` is 1 only when E is RT or ETT, the same adequacy rule holds, and no R occurs. `durable_union` uses the earliest of CP/RT/ETT, but is reported only as a separate sensitivity and never substituted for independent validation. If a marker occurs but the 48-hour interval is not adequately observed, label it `truncated/unknown`, not failure. If death occurs before E, classify death before liberation as a competing outcome; death after E but before E+2880 is a competing failure and cannot be called durable. Live ICU discharge before E+2880 is unknown unless a qualifying signal occurred and the discharge location is not another ICU; it is not silently coded as durable.

5. Every outcome record must include `first_CP`, `first_RT`, `first_ETT`, `first_R`, event-to-recurrence time, follow-up end, death/discharge/truncation flags, 12-hour-bin coverage, and the algorithm label. Report 1-hour, 12-hour, and 24-hour recurrence-gap sensitivities, but keep 48 hours primary. A signal with no adequate follow-up is not a negative outcome.

VALIDATION GATES BEFORE ASSOCIATION
The primary report is the validation audit, stratified by strategy, landmark, hospital, and algorithm. For each strategy report denominators, CP/RT/ETT availability, process-field availability, first-event timing, pairwise time concordance, recurrence, adequate-follow-up proportion, unknown/truncation, death, and covered-bin density. The comparative gate passes only if all are true:

- at least three hospitals have >=10 observations per strategy, with both strategies estimable in each;
- in the restricted hospitals, each endpoint's necessary marker is available in >=10% of each strategy and neither strategy has >20 percentage-point absolute difference in adequate-follow-up or covered-bin availability unless an adjustment is prespecified before analysis;
- CP-only, independent-only, and union algorithms are estimable in both strategies, and independent event counts permit an interval estimate (otherwise independent validation is inconclusive);
- at least 80% of putative 48-hour intervals meet documentation adequacy in each strategy, and the between-strategy difference is <=20 points; this is a minimum ascertainment criterion, not a claim that the EHR is complete;
- the 24-hour pre-L temporal placebo (same marker algorithms in [L-1440,L)) has no prespecified material same-direction strategy contrast: absolute risk-difference point estimate >0.10 with its 95% interval excluding 0 is a fail, while a sparse/unestimable placebo is inconclusive;
- no outcome-definition reversal: if CP and independent estimates have opposite signs with both intervals excluding 0, validation fails; if one is sparse or intervals overlap, classify inconclusive rather than selecting the favorable algorithm.

A failed gate stops the adjusted strategy association. It does not stop reporting descriptive validation, negative findings, or feasibility. Thresholds are locked before looking at strategy-specific adjusted results.

GATED SECONDARY ASSOCIATION
Only if the above gate passes, use D as time zero and the observed first label in [L,L+360] as the exposure. Retain one strategy per landmark; exclude ambiguous same-offset labels. Use pre-L covariates only: age, admission diagnosis/type, ICU type/source, APACHE variables, pre-L ventilation duration/state, FiO2, PEEP, SpO2, RR, MAP, laboratory summaries, prior care-plan states, measurement counts, and time since last measure. Add decision delay D-L and landmark. Do not use any post-D marker, treatment, entry time, or future discharge variable as a covariate. InfusionDrug is point-charted without validated active intervals; vasopressor non-escalation is a sensitivity descriptor, never a normality exclusion.

Estimate a standardized 72-hour risk difference for `durable_independent` and separately for `durable_CP` in the restricted overlap population using overlap weighting or doubly robust standardization with hospital fixed effects. Check propensity overlap, effective sample size, maximum/stabilized-weight distribution, pre/post-weight standardized mean differences, and event/unknown counts. Cluster repeated landmarks by `uniquepid` and use hospital-level bootstrap only when at least three hospitals remain. If overlap, effective sample size, or events fail, report no model. First landmark per stay, >=20-per-strategy hospitals, leave-one-hospital-out, fixed versus random hospital effects, EOL exclusion, and chart-entry-time sensitivity are prespecified.

FALSIFICATION AND INTERPRETATION
- Temporal placebo: post-label association should not be mimicked by liberation signals in [L-1440,L). A positive placebo signals residual trajectory or documentation timing bias.
- Documentation placebo: compare covered bins, row counts, entry delays, and process-field density; an apparent contrast confined to CP and absent from independent markers is not supportive.
- Exposure-process check: SBT Method/RSBI/weaning/sedation fields may support coherent decision documentation but cannot validate that an SBT occurred.
- Recurrence audit: report exact recurrence component and time; materially different recurrence ascertainment across strategies fails durability interpretation.
- Algorithm consistency: agreement strengthens a documented-trajectory interpretation; discordance, especially CP-positive/independent-negative, is adverse or inconclusive rather than evidence of harm.
- Hospital and landmark sensitivity: report all restricted sets and leave-one-hospital-out estimates; a result driven by one hospital is not a multicenter claim.

Supportive result: gates pass, timing is coherent, independent markers and 48-hour coverage are adequate, placebo is null, and estimates are directionally consistent with uncertainty reported. This supports only a robust association suitable for prospective validation. Adverse result: strategy-specific missingness, poor coverage, discordance, or placebo signal means these labels cannot distinguish readiness decisions from documentation practice. Inconclusive result: sparse independent events, failed overlap, competing/truncated outcomes, or intervals spanning clinically important benefit and harm. A precise adverse association argues against assuming benefit but cannot establish causal harm.

UNAVAILABLE EVIDENCE AND CLAIM LIMITS
These tables cannot establish actual SBT performance, clinician rationale for no trial, bedside readiness, airway protection, secretion burden, sedation contraindications, planned procedures, true extubation/reintubation outside observed ICU documentation, post-ICU reintubation, or clinical benefit. `Spontaneous - adequate`, RT Off, and ETT-removal treatment are proxies with different ascertainment mechanisms. The durable endpoint means durable documentation under an explicit coverage rule, not true ventilator liberation. Expert chart adjudication, richer ventilator waveforms, complete medication intervals, or prospective follow-up would be required for those stronger claims. An automatic verifier can check source paths, exact labels, offsets, joins, filter counts, bin coverage, recurrence logic, overlap, model outputs, placebo tests, and whether conclusions match outputs; it cannot adjudicate clinical readiness or causality.

CHANGE SUMMARY FOR LEAD
The parent already repaired feasibility and correctly stopped causal inference when marker concordance was poor. This child turns the deferred durability audit into a deterministic endpoint: candidate events must occur by D+24h, require complete 48-hour follow-up, require 12-hour documentation coverage, distinguish unknown/truncation from failure, explicitly handle death and ICU discharge, and use independent-only results as a gate rather than allowing a care-plan-only union to manufacture reassurance. Remaining uncertainty is clinical validity of every proxy and the likely possibility that the gate fails because RT Off is rare; that failure is scientifically informative and must not be repaired by relaxing the outcome or imputing undocumented liberation.
