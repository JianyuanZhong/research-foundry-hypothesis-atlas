# Episode 77 repaired successor: seven-day death on the complete fixed-capacity queue population

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

At the fixed 24-hour reassessment after an early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed entry, does an optional repeat-lactate result and its observation process materially improve a silent, fixed-capacity queue for hospital deaths occurring during the next seven days, beyond an otherwise identical model that represents late non-lactate surveillance?

This is consequential because the parent's eventual-discharge endpoint mixes deaths soon after review with deaths after prolonged hospitalization. Episode 76 attempted a nearer endpoint but excluded everyone discharged after seven days, unnecessarily selecting the outcome frame. In the locked parent cohort, exact discharge status and offset classify seven-day hospital death for everyone: D7=1 only for an Expired discharge by the horizon; an Alive discharge at any time and an Expired discharge after the horizon are known seven-day nondeaths. This child preserves all 2,302 eligible people and fixed hospital quotas, changing only the modeled outcome.

The strongest available evidence is narrower. Inspected full text for Baysan et al. (2022; DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750; PMCID PMC9444407; frozen [source checksum]) reports modest incremental prognostic discrimination from 24-hour lactate in a measured sepsis cohort. Inspected full text for Sisk et al. (2021; DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242; PMCID PMC7810439; frozen [source checksum]) supports representing whether and when routine information is observed. Neither establishes optional-result queue value, seven-day value, ordering benefit, preventability, or patient benefit. The research-ambition papers are demonstrations, not evidence for this hypothesis; the cancer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not claimed as read.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis:

> In the complete locked EICU baseline/L24 population, replacing the hospital-held-out R_attention top-10%-within-hospital queue with equal-capacity R_full captures at least three additional seven-day hospital deaths per 100 review slots: pooled Delta7_slot has a 95% lower bound at least +0.03, and both repeat-availability strata and the equal-hospital macro estimand have nonnegative lower bounds.

The +0.03 margin is operational, not stakeholder-validated utility. This is a prognostic allocation hypothesis, not a test of ordering, result visibility, treatment, preventability, or outcome benefit.

## Locked population, clocks, and audited feasibility

Use all rows from read-only sources; sampling is forbidden and chunking is memory management only. Record chunks, filters, invalidations, conflicts, duplicates, and flow counts. Keep row-level derivatives private.

3. Per uniquepid keep the lexicographically smallest (t0, patientunitstayid); set L24=t0+1440.
4. Require hospitaldischargestatus exactly Alive or Expired and hospitaldischargeoffset>=L24.
5. Baseline B is a lab row with trimmed/case-folded labname=lactate, unit mmol/L, finite labresult in [0.2,30], collection in [t0-360,t0], and revision by t0+60. At each offset keep the greatest eligible revision; invalidate a greatest-revision tie with differing values and collapse identical ties to smallest labid. Select latest valid collection, tie by smallest labid, and require B>=2.
6. Optional F uses the same analyte/unit/range/revision/conflict rules, collection in [t0+720,L24], revision by L24. Select nearest t0+1080, then earlier offset, then smallest labid. Retain absence as F_available=0; never use a post-L24 revision.
7. Keep hospitals with >=30 people and freeze them before modeling. Require >=30 hospitals, >=1,000 people, >=200 D7 events, >=200 D7 nonevents, maximum hospital share <=15%, and both D7 classes in >=80% of hospitals.
8. Before outcome modeling, split hospitals at the median complete-population F availability (at/above high; below low), freeze labels, and let copies inherit them. This is an outcome-blind process stratum, not causal moderation or external transportability.

Set H7=L24+10080=t0+11520 minutes, seven days after the decision landmark. Define D7=1 iff status=Expired and hospitaldischargeoffset<=H7; otherwise D7=0. Every retained person has a definitive post-L24 hospital discharge status/offset, so nobody is censored for this cumulative-incidence indicator. An Alive discharge after H7 and an Expired discharge after H7 were both alive in hospital at H7. Do not call D7=0 eventual survival. Eventual hospital-discharge death D=1[Expired] is a secondary outcome on the same people and never shares a numerator with D7.

The no-sampling complete-source audit in episode77_horizon_audit.json, produced by audit_horizons.py, reproduced 13,023 adult one-person early-pressor stays reaching L24, 3,117 valid B>=2 before site gating, and 2,302 people in 30 hospitals (779 eventual deaths, 1,523 eventual survivors). It found 607 D7 events and 1,695 nonevents; all 30 hospitals had both classes, minimum 7 and median 18.5 events/hospital, maximum hospital share 7.34%. At H7, 611 had discharged alive and 1,084 remained hospitalized event-free; 172 of the latter later died. These establish feasibility and horizon logic, not predictive benefit. Execution must reproduce them or document a mechanical source-version mismatch and reapply every gate; it may not change the population/horizon to recover feasibility.

For vitalPeriodic accept heart rate 20-250/min, respiration 2-80/min, sao2 40-100%. Counts are timestamps, not export rows. These are five-minute summaries, not waveforms or display/view times.

R_attention adds six summaries from non-lactate rows collected in [t0+720,L24] and revised by L24: ln(1+routine marker-time pairs) for the first 13 streams; ln(1+blood-gas marker-time pairs) for paO2/paCO2/pH; ln(1+distinct collection offsets) across all 16; observed-stream fraction; recency (L24-latest offset)/720; and no-row indicator, with recency training-imputed if absent. R_process adds F_available, F age (L24-F_offset)/720, and missing-age indicator. R_full adds ln(F) and square; missing continuous F terms are training-fold imputed while availability stays explicit.

## Estimands, fitting, uncertainty, and falsification

For hospital h set k_h=ceil(0.10*n_h). Rank outer-hospital-held-out raw linear predictors within hospital, exact ties by ascending patientunitstayid. Let Q_m,h be top k_h, S7_m,h its D7 sum, and K=sum k_h. Primary Delta7_slot=(sum S7_full-sum S7_attention)/K; report 100*Delta7_slot. Delta7_macro is the hospital mean of the two queue precisions' difference. Compute pooled contrasts separately in frozen low/high availability strata.

Paired decomposition: A_h=Q_full\Q_attention, R_h=Q_attention\Q_full, s_h=|A_h|=|R_h|, M=sum s_h, G7=sum_h(sum_A D7-sum_R D7), rho=M/K, Psi7=G7/M if M>0. Require Delta7_slot=G7/K=rho*Psi7 and |Delta7_slot|<=rho. If M=0, require G7=Delta7_slot=0 and leave Psi7 undefined. Always report M, K, rho, G7, addition/removal event fractions, hospitals with swaps, and s_h/k_h. Yield is denominator-qualified explanation only and cannot rescue or veto the primary.

Use leave-one-hospital-out prediction. Exclude each hospital completely; in training only winsorize continuous predictors at 1st/99th percentiles, standardize by training mean/population SD, then mean-impute to zero; indicators unscaled. Fit logistic regression with intercept, L2, C=1, lbfgs, max_iter=10000, tol=1e-8, no class weighting, seed 20260977. No feature may be dropped. No finite values, zero post-winsor SD, nonconvergence, or nonfinite prediction makes the required model noncomputable. Use linear predictors for ranking. Probabilities/AUROC/Brier/log loss/calibration are descriptive; no clinical calibration or threshold-utility claim.

Use 2,000 copied-hospital refit bootstrap replicates, seed 20260977. Sample hospitals with replacement; copies contribute evaluation rows and training weight, but all copies of an original stay one exclusion group. Repeat preprocessing, all four fits, predictions, quotas, primary/macro/strata, 20% capacity, and paired algebra. Require >=1,900 finite replicates for pooled, macro, and both strata. Save each joint (G7,K,M,rho,Delta7_slot,Psi7), validate identities, report percentile intervals and zero-swap fraction, never impute yield. Fewer than 1,900 M>0 replicates creates a yield-denominator-fragile flag but not primary failure.

Fixed-design leave-one-original-hospital deletions recompute estimands without refitting. Support requires positive pooled Delta7 in >80%, each stratum in >=70%, and macro in >=70% of finite deletions. Report any hospital domination of slots, D7, G7, or M.

Secondaries: 20% queues; R_full-R_state and R_process-R_attention; conditional F-measured R_full-R_process after refitting both and new 10% quotas; overlap, additions/removals, sensitivity, precision, false positives, unflagged D7 events, ties and switches by hospital; and separate eventual-death refits/queues on the same people. No secondary rescues D7.

Apply the first matching state:

1. Not computable: integrity/flow/horizon mismatch; wrong age/person/t0/L24/B/F/revision/tie logic; failed gates; post-L24 predictor or hospital leakage; changed features; incomplete predictions/quotas; failed required fit; or <1,900 required replicates. Never substitute Episode 76's discharge-before-H7 subset, another horizon, random splits, F-complete cases, or eventual death.
2. Supportive near-term fixed-slot value with internal portability stress passed: pooled lower bound >=+0.03; both stratum and macro lower bounds >=0; deletion rules pass; 20% pooled upper bound >=0.
3. Adverse—opposite: pooled upper bound <0 or both stratum upper bounds <0.
4. Adverse—surveillance-explained: R_full-R_state lower bound >=+0.03 but primary R_full-R_attention upper bound <+0.03; this does not prove surveillance caused it.
5. Adverse—below materiality: primary upper bound <+0.03.
6. Adverse—internally nonportable: pooled lower bound >=+0.03 but either stratum/macro upper bound <0 or 20% upper bound <0.
7. Inconclusive: all other computable patterns, including crossing +0.03 or unstable deletion direction.

Support means modeled prognostic re-ranking only and merits prospective silent validation. Adverse falsifies the locked horizon/margin/comparator claim, not every lactate role. Inconclusive cannot prompt post hoc horizon/quota/feature searches. Discordant D7 and eventual-death secondaries indicate horizon specificity, not causality.

## Evidence limits and verifier contract

EICU computes recorded D7, features, rankings, queues, swaps, and internal uncertainty. It lacks true administration/indication, order/specimen/assay validity, release/display/view time, actual review, death cause/preventability, interventions, counterfactuals, workload, cost, equity, utility, and patient-centered outcomes. Narrative sections are removed and no note text is used. Clinical informatics must adjudicate clock/visibility semantics; stakeholders must establish capacity/utility; external synchronized data are needed for transport; causal benefit requires a prospective impact study, ideally randomized if care changes.

Write source/header/hash and flow manifests, horizon/revision/conflict audits, feature/fold/convergence records, private held-out scores/queues, aggregate queue/swap cells, bootstrap tuples/intervals, deletions, secondary horizon outputs, deterministic state, and structured claims.

The verifier must recompute the 2,302/30/607/1,695 frame; explicitly test that Alive-after-H7 and Expired-after-H7 map to D7=0 and remain included; reject Episode 76's discharge-before-H7 selection and post-L24 leakage; reproduce features, held-out fits, quotas, algebra, uncertainty, deletions, and labels. Fixtures cover supportive, opposite, surveillance-explained, below-margin, internally nonportable, inconclusive, zero-swap, denominator-fragile yield, and noncomputable cases. Reject correct numbers paired with claims that lactate should be ordered, was seen, prevents deaths, improves care/survival, or externally transports.

Computationally checkable claims are integrity, cohort/horizon counts, features, predictions, queues, contrasts, algebra, uncertainty, and labels. Visibility, preventability, clinical usefulness, fairness, causal benefit, and transportability require adjudication, missing evidence, or another study; an automatic verifier cannot establish them.
