TITLE
Cross-marker CKD-EPI threshold reclassification at the first repeat UK Biobank assessment

SUBSTANTIVE CHILD ADVANCE
This child preserves the parent’s export, baseline and repeat cohorts, inclusive 2–8-year interval, field-53 date binding, measured-baseline attendance sensitivity, folds, transformations, nuisance-model family, matched-control diagnostics, and strict two-wave/noncausal evidence boundary. It adds a prespecified threshold estimand rather than replacing the parent’s continuous P1/P2 estimands.

UNRESOLVED QUESTION AND HYPOTHESIS
The parent supports positive observable reciprocal persistence after adjustment and measured-baseline attendance weighting, but does not show whether baseline creatinine–cystatin-C discordance predicts crossing a clinically consequential estimated-filtration threshold at the first repeat. The primary, cross-marker hypothesis is:

H1: among participants with baseline eGFRcr >=60 mL/min/1.73 m2, greater baseline cystatin C than expected from baseline creatinine, age, sex, and age-by-sex predicts crossing to repeat eGFRcr <60 at the first repeat visit 2–8 years later.

A prespecified ordered secondary threshold is baseline eGFRcr >=45 to repeat eGFRcr <45. These are protocol-biomarker reclassification events, not CKD diagnoses, latent GFR decline, treatment recommendations, or drug-dosing claims. The exposure is a cross-fitted residual of baseline log cystatin C on baseline log creatinine and covariates; the outcome uses later creatinine-derived eGFR. Therefore the exposure marker is not the marker used to define the threshold outcome. No reverse direction is promoted to primary status; the parent’s P2 and its adverse matched-control result remain unchanged.

CLINICAL IMPORTANCE AND EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
The strongest evidence already available is that the two biomarkers show positive reciprocal observable persistence, with P1 discriminating from the selected direct-LDL and vitamin-D controls but P2 not doing so. The unresolved claim is narrower: whether cross-marker discordance can identify future crossing of a reporting threshold, conditional on being above that threshold at baseline. The experiment cannot establish true GFR, GFR slope, CKD chronicity, CKD onset/progression, causality, clinical utility, or benefit from acting on a classification.

EQUATION AND UNIT GATE
Before any threshold analysis, the executable must verify that the UKB field metadata or an authoritative field-specific record identifies 30700 as serum creatinine in micromoles/L and 30720 as cystatin C in mg/L, and must retain the frozen evidence and hashes. If either unit or assay calibration cannot be authoritatively established, the threshold child is not executable and the parent should be retained unchanged; do not infer units from distributions.

If the gate passes, convert Scr from micromoles/L to mg/dL by Scr_mgdl = Scr_umolL / 88.4, retain Scys in mg/L, and compute the 2021 race-free CKD-EPI equations with age in years and sex coded female=0/male=1. For eGFRcr, kappa is 0.7 female and 0.9 male, alpha is -0.241 female and -0.302 male, and the sex multiplier is 1.012 for female and 1 for male:
 eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/kappa,1)^alpha * max(Scr/kappa,1)^-1.200 * 0.9938^Age * sex_multiplier.
For eGFRcr-cys, kappa is 0.7 female and 0.9 male, alpha is -0.219 female and -0.144 male, and the cystatin exponents are -0.323 below/equal to 0.8 mg/L and -0.778 above 0.8 mg/L:
 eGFRcr-cys = 135 * min(Scr/kappa,1)^alpha * max(Scr/kappa,1)^-0.544 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^-0.323 * max(Scys/0.8,1)^-0.778 * 0.9961^Age * sex_multiplier,
where sex_multiplier is 0.963 for female and 1 for male. The primary threshold is based on eGFRcr only, not eGFRcr-cys, to preserve cross-marker separation. Equation provenance is Inker et al., NEJM 2021, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2102953; the available Europe PMC record identifies the paper and PMCID PMC8822996. The formula must be independently unit-tested against authoritative reference values before analysis.

ESTIMANDS AND ANALYSIS
The primary estimand is the attendance-weighted risk difference in the repeat eGFRcr<60 event per 1-SD increase in the baseline cross-fitted cystatin-C residual, among baseline eGFRcr>=60. The secondary ordered estimand is analogous for baseline eGFRcr>=45 and repeat eGFRcr<45. Use the parent’s ten-fold measured-baseline attendance models, stabilized continuous weights, replicate-specific 1st/99th-percentile truncation, and full baseline-pool participant bootstrap; do not round weights or treat them as counts. Fit weighted logistic regression with the residual exposure, restricted cubic spline of baseline eGFRcr (or baseline log creatinine with a prespecified mapping), age, sex, age-by-sex, actual elapsed years spline, and fold indicators. Report risk differences by standardizing model predictions to the weighted threshold-eligible population, with bootstrap percentile and simultaneous intervals for the primary and ordered secondary estimands. The unweighted repeat-attender analysis and the parent’s continuous P1/P2 models are comparator baselines. Do not adjust for repeat values or variables measured after baseline.

Because the threshold is clinically meaningful but the dataset has no adjudicated GFR, define a practical magnitude gate before execution: a supportive primary result requires a two-sided 95% simultaneous interval excluding zero and an estimated absolute risk difference of at least 2 percentage points per SD. This is a prespecified interpretability gate, not a claim that 2 percentage points is a validated patient-important difference. If the gate is not met, report association without threshold relevance. Control familywise error across primary and secondary threshold tests using a prespecified Holm procedure; controls and parent P1/P2 remain separate families.

FALSIFICATION AND INTERPRETATION
Falsification: the primary hypothesis fails if the simultaneous interval includes zero, the point estimate is nonpositive, or the association reverses materially under the parent’s measured-attendance sensitivity. A threshold-specific negative-control outcome is repeat direct LDL threshold crossing defined using baseline and repeat LDL only, analyzed with the same residual exposure and covariates; this is a specificity diagnostic, not a causal negative control. A null or positive LDL result weakens renal interpretation. Also report the reverse exposure direction as exploratory only, without re-labeling it as confirmatory.

Supportive results mean only that baseline cross-marker discordance is associated with later protocol eGFRcr threshold crossing under the measured attendance model and that the association meets the predeclared magnitude gate; they do not show true filtration loss or utility. Adverse results include failure of the unit/equation gate, a null or reversed primary association, material attenuation under weighting, failure of the magnitude gate, or a comparable LDL threshold association. Inconclusive results include a failed positivity diagnostic, sparse threshold cells, wide intervals, or dependence on repeat attendance that cannot be addressed with observed variables.

ESSENTIAL UNAVAILABLE EVIDENCE
No urine albumin, measured GFR, adjudicated CKD status/chronicity, medication exposure, indication, laboratory assay calibration/lot, hydration status, muscle mass, or clinical drug-dosing outcome is guaranteed by the listed fields. Two visits cannot distinguish biological trajectory from stable person-level levels, assay variation, treatment, or regression to the mean. Clinical adjudication is required before using these results for CKD diagnosis, staging, prescribing, or patient communication. External cohorts are required for transportability and prospective impact studies for utility.
