# Independent actionability and superseding-plan reference layer for pulmonary-nodule routes

## Decision question and targeted advance

The inherited decision is whether text-reproducible admission routes can be nominated for a prospective **silent** validation in the frozen MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity population. The parent establishes a stringent reproducibility and leakage audit, but panel agreement still does not answer the clinically consequential question: do the routes correspond to different discharge action needs?

This child tests that unresolved claim without changing the population, route, or estimand. It adds a blinded, route-independent reference layer that classifies each eligible recommendation unit as one of four mutually exclusive discharge needs:

1. **SP (safe structured propagation)** — the recommendation remains active at departure, is sufficiently determinate for a structured handoff, and no supported superseding/inactive plan is present;
2. **CR (clinician reconciliation)** — the recommendation remains potentially active, but target, timing, polarity, responsibility, or competing plan is materially ambiguous or discordant and requires human reconciliation;
3. **SI (superseded/inactive)** — an explicit, temporally applicable statement documents replacement, completion, cancellation, resolution, or no-further-action status;
4. **UK (unknowable)** — the available pre-departure artifacts cannot distinguish the above states.

The states describe the evidence-supported **need for a discharge workflow**, not guideline appropriateness, a clinician's fault, or patient benefit. Absence of a mention is never evidence of SI. Any unresolved uncertainty is retained as a compatible set and propagated adversely.

The substantive advance is a test of action-need separation rather than another panel-agreement test. Route labels are generated independently from sealed component labels exactly as in the parent; actionability and superseding adjudication cannot see a route, atom, sampling stratum, route-triggering status, parent labels, weights, or route results. Actionability labels are sealed before route/action cross-tabs are opened. No actionability label is used to define an inherited route. Thus a route can fail by being reproducible yet aligned with the wrong discharge need, while a disagreement that does not change the action need is not silently counted as clinical failure.

## Evidence already supported, unresolved claim, and falsifiable hypothesis

The available records support construction of an admission-linked radiology opportunity, reciprocal report/addendum chronology, a canonical discharge summary, service/ICU workflow proxies, and text-defined reproducibility measures. The records do **not** establish actual visibility at physical departure, clinician awareness, responsibility, communication, patient receipt, order/referral execution, follow-up, appropriateness, malignancy, or outcome.

The unresolved claim is narrower and testable: among support-qualified atoms, the inherited text routes have distinguishable and defensible discharge action needs, rather than merely reproducible labels. The primary hypothesis is:

> For every atom placed on an AUTO or RECON allow-list, the adverse-compatible action-need distribution is sufficiently concentrated in its prespecified intended need, and explicit superseding/inactive evidence is not misrouted as active propagation; the same claim must hold in a roster-disjoint, route-blinded holistic actionability challenge.

The null/falsification case is clinically important: if AUTO contains a nontrivial compatible CR, SI, or UK burden, or RECON contains a nontrivial compatible SI/UK burden, reproducibility does not justify the corresponding prospective action. A route may pass the parent's text-purity gates and still be withheld. A useful separation result supports only silent validation of the exact observable signature; it cannot establish safety or benefit.

## Frozen population, chronology, route, and workload

Do not redefine any inherited element. Construct the complete first-eligible, all-opportunity adult admission population `N_A`, including every eligible recommendation unit and every admission-level state. Preserve the parent's reciprocal pre-departure radiology/addendum chronology, canonical exact-`DS` discharge artifact, `G_u = DS_store - R_store,u` unit clock, exhaustive `Y`, `V`, `H`, and `Z` states, stale/post-discharge exclusions, all-unit inventory, and worst-unit admission routing with the exact inherited `AUTO/RECON/TIMING/DEFER` precedence. Preserve split-view seals, the pre-label support atlas, exact terminal-service/workflow atoms, positive-probability sampling, sentinel census, finite-population inference, route-independent all-route audit, independent holistic challenge, domain-specific no-rescue rules, default `DEFER`, and strict noncausal interpretation.

The workload is unchanged and must be at most 900 admissions: use the parent's sentinel census and fixed finite-population formulas (`S_ZERO` at least 300 and each other inherited stratum at least 120, capped by stratum population, subject to the parent's total ceiling). The actionability layer is nested in the same prelabel draw; it may not add admissions, top up sparse atoms, replace cases, enrich on a provisional route, or change inclusion probabilities. Every selected admission is action-adjudicated for every enumerated unit, including minority units that would control worst-unit routing. If the parent challenge allocation cannot provide the prespecified minimum support for an atom, that atom is UK/inconclusive for nomination, not pooled with another atom.

The actionability sample is selected and packetized before any action labels, route labels, disagreement review, or outcome summaries. All route/action estimates use the inherited admission-level design weights and Rao-Wu replicate scheme. Challenge summaries are conditional on the fixed challenge eligibility event and are never unweighted complete-case estimates.

## Noncircular actionability and superseding-plan adjudication

### Pre-label reference ontology

Before packet construction, governance freezes a codebook that contains no strings `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, no route table, no atom identifiers, no parent labels, and no performance thresholds. It defines observable fields separately:

* target identity and whether it is a pulmonary-nodule/follow-up target;
* action requested and polarity (recommend, discourage, completed, declined, conditional, unknown);
* timing/window and whether it is determinate enough for structured entry;
* proposed recipient/responsibility, recording this as text evidence rather than team truth;
* evidence status at `D` (active, explicitly completed/resolved, explicitly replaced/cancelled, conflicting, absent, unknown);
* competing plan relation (none documented, explicit alternative, explicit replacement, conflict, unknown);
* evidence span, source note ID, `charttime`, `storetime`, and a reason code for every field.

The four action-need labels are then a deterministic function of these atomic fields, with an explicit hierarchy for superseding evidence. Strongest evidence is a directly target-linked, temporally applicable explicit statement of replacement/cancellation/completion/no further action; next is an explicit alternative plan that is target-linked and applicable at departure; conflict between active recommendation and alternative plan is CR; mere omission, generic discharge language, or a missing field cannot create SI and defaults to UK when SP versus CR cannot be resolved.

The hierarchy is frozen before any route output is computed. It requires a positive textual assertion for SI, does not infer inactive status from no mention, does not infer clinician responsibility from author or service, and does not turn documentation quality into clinical appropriateness. A unit with any adverse-compatible CR, SI, or UK state cannot be called SP. A unit with adverse-compatible SI or UK cannot be called a clean CR intervention target. Mixed admissions retain every unit and route by the parent's worst-unit precedence.

### Firewalled panels

For each selected admission, create two independent unit/action packets from the sealed raw extract. Both contain the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain needed to identify the recommendation, the canonical DS and permitted DS-detail artifact, permitted admission/death/disposition context, exact missingness and timestamp metadata, and source IDs. Neither contains route, atom, stratum, design weight, parent component labels, post-`D` text, POE, or outcomes. The packets do not expose any precomputed recommendation flags or route-defining spans; reviewers locate and cite evidence themselves.

Panel A1 is a clinically trained actionability panel using `CB-A1`; panel A2 is roster-disjoint and uses a separately written `CB-A2` with independently worded definitions, independently ordered fields, distinct examples, and no shared training cases. A1 and A2 each independently extract all atomic fields and the SP/CR/SI/UK need label, with confidence and explicit unknown reasons. Neither panel sees the other's labels. Their packet ordering, pseudonyms, and work queues are independently randomized. A third roster-disjoint holistic panel S3 receives the same complete packet only after A1/A2 append-only seals. S3 is blind to all route/component/action labels and adjudicates the superseding/inactive hierarchy and whole-admission action-need set using an open-schema challenge; it may mark unresolved evidence rather than forcing consensus.

The panels are independent measurement systems, not a clinical gold standard. There is no consensus replacement, majority rescue, adjudicator selected because of disagreement, or route-triggered verification. If a separately governed external expert/reference subset is available, it is analyzed as an ascertainment-limited fourth measurement and cannot be called a gold standard or narrow the primary adverse bounds without an explicit prelabel validation assumption.

### Blinding and order safeguards

Route construction, atom assignment, and all parent component labels are sealed before action packets are released, while action labels remain sealed until the route/action analysis file is locked. Action panels cannot tune definitions after seeing route distributions. The analysis programmer receives only pseudonymous sealed outputs until the code, joins, and action-to-need mapping pass a hash check. Any roster overlap, codebook reuse, packet leakage, route-before-seal execution, post-label codebook edit, or transformation created after seeing labels invalidates the affected run.

## Estimands and adverse uncertainty

The primary estimand is the admission-level, design-weighted joint distribution
`P(N_r = r, K_u = k)` over inherited route `r` and independent action need `k` for every recommendation unit `u`, with the inherited worst-unit admission route retained. Report, by support-qualified atom and route:

* the vector of action-need proportions `(SP, CR, SI, UK)` and its finite-population 95% confidence interval;
* incompatible-need burden for each proposed action: AUTO incompatibility `{CR,SI,UK}`, RECON incompatibility `{SI,UK}`, and any inherited TIMING/DEFER action mapping stated before labels;
* the fraction of units with an explicit superseding/inactive assertion and the fraction for which a missing/ambiguous artifact forces UK;
* actionability discordance between the route and each independent panel, without treating panel agreement as correctness;
* admission-level worst-unit disagreement and minority-unit capture; and
* workload in physician-hours, packet-time, and unresolved/returned-packet rate.

All quantities are set-valued where labels disagree. Compute sharp compatible-set bounds: the favorable endpoint uses only labels jointly supported by all independent panels; the adverse endpoint assigns every unresolved or disagreement pattern to the most route-incompatible need allowed by the atomic evidence. Unknown, missing, corrupted, or nonresponsive packets are adverse-compatible and never deleted. Exact finite-population inversion and 20,000 Rao-Wu admission-cluster replicates produce one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over inherited gates, atom gates, action distributions, superseding detection, route/action discordance, and workload. A latent-class model may be shown diagnostically, but conditional independence and a clinical gold standard are not available; it may not narrow intervals or certify a route.

## Baselines and explicit gates

Run the locked route against: (i) the independent actionability layer; (ii) route-blind parent component labels; (iii) a naive text-majority/consensus diagnostic; (iv) a no-supersession model that treats every eligible recommendation as active; and (v) a documentation-only model that calls missing action fields CR. These baselines explain whether a finding depends on superseding hierarchy or consensus, but none can rescue a failing atom.

In addition to every inherited parent gate, an atom may be nominated only if all rules below pass simultaneously, with thresholds approved before labels:

* at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 non-sentinel challenge admissions for the route where feasible; otherwise the atom is unreleasable;
* simultaneous lower bound for A1/A2/S3 need-label support agreement is at least 0.90, and simultaneous upper route-versus-need incompatibility is at most 0.05 for AUTO and at most 0.10 for RECON and TIMING; DEFER makes no safety/actionability claim;
* for AUTO, the adverse-compatible upper probability of CR+SI+UK is at most 0.05, and the adverse-compatible lower probability of SP is at least 0.90; for RECON, the adverse-compatible upper probability of SI+UK is at most 0.10 and the adverse-compatible lower probability of CR is at least 0.80; explicit SI is separately captured with an adverse-compatible missed-supersession upper bound at most 0.05;
* no admission assigned AUTO may have an adverse-compatible minority unit requiring CR, SI, or UK; any such case is routed under inherited precedence to RECON/TIMING/DEFER and blocks AUTO nomination;
* route-invariant control flips remain at most 0.02 and route-decisive control violations at most 0.05; these controls test invariance/sensitivity, not clinical correctness;
* missingness, nonresponse, corruption, or control-infeasibility upper bounds are below 0.05 for a claimed atom unless the atom is censused; and
* the adverse upper workload remains below the same governance-approved absolute budget, separately by atom and panel.

These operational margins are nomination gates, not patient-harm thresholds. No pooled estimate, macro-service grouping, neighboring atom, favorable consensus, model imputation, shrinkage, latent-class point estimate, early/late contrast, sentinel-only result, or perturbation-control result may rescue a failing atom. Rare, unknown, unsupported, and novel atoms default to DEFER. Failure blocks the affected atom; failure of a global inherited gate blocks the global nomination. If action labels cannot distinguish SP from CR or SI from UK within the adverse envelope, the result is inconclusive and no route is promoted.

## Controls and falsification criteria

Construct all controls before action labels and store transformed artifacts separately. Do not alter primary MIMIC labels or estimands. For every feasible challenge admission include, where spans are unambiguous:

1. a route-irrelevant perturbation preserving target, action, timing, polarity, and superseding evidence while changing unrelated formatting/section order; action need and route must not change;
2. a route-decisive one-field perturbation changing only a locked timing or replacement field; the expected route transition is defined by the parent route table, while the action transition is defined by the prelabel ontology;
3. an omission/addition pair masking or inserting one recommendation/explicit plan statement at a prespecified location, with expected compatible-set transitions frozen before review; and
4. an author/signature-like nuisance-token perturbation preserving semantics, which must not change action need or route.

H1/H2 action panels review randomized pair orders without knowing the transformation or expected result. Shared-wrong planted plans, pooled-safe/domain-unsafe fixtures, missing high-weight labels, unsupported service/workflow signatures, latent-class nonidentifiability, duplicate-unit/restatement fixtures, and explicit replacement versus omission fixtures are mandatory. A route-invariant flip, an out-of-table decisive change, or any unsupported assignment of SI from omission falsifies the relevant implementation. Shared agreement on a planted wrong plan is reported as agreement but must leave the adverse compatible bounds wide; it cannot certify clinical actionability.

## Interpretation, supportive/adverse/inconclusive outcomes, and evidence ceiling

**Supportive:** all inherited and new atom-specific gates pass, with at least one support-qualified atom whose adverse action-need bounds meet its intended action. This supports only a prospective, within-site, silent validation of those exact MIMIC-observable signatures, with all others forced to DEFER. It does not support automatic live propagation, clinical safety, appropriateness, follow-up, or benefit.

**Adverse:** reproducibility passes but action-need incompatibility, missed explicit supersession, route-invariant flipping, decisive-control failure, or adverse minority-unit uncertainty crosses a gate. The affected route/atom is withheld; a finding of SI or UK is not evidence that care was inappropriate, only that the proposed action cannot be justified from the available artifact.

**Inconclusive:** sparse action events, infeasible challenge allocation, nonresponse, broken seals, missing chronology, wide compatible bounds, or absent prelabel identifiability constraints. Inconclusive is neither safety nor failure of the underlying clinical process.

MIMIC lacks imaging pixels (MIMIC-CXR is not in the configured snapshot), actual viewing/signature/communication logs, responsible-team identity, patient preferences, outside plans, target-linked orders/referrals, scheduling, completion, pathology/malignancy, and longitudinal outcomes. Deidentified dates preserve within-subject intervals but cannot support cross-subject calendar transport. A retrospective actionability reference layer remains expert interpretation of incomplete text. Prospective silent validation must add actual-calendar workflow observation, viewing/edit/sign/transmission and responsibility evidence, explicit target-linked orders/referrals and scheduling, patient/outside-care information, and clinical outcomes. Only an expert-governed prospective or interventional study could assess live safety, utility, causal benefit, or patient-centered effect.
