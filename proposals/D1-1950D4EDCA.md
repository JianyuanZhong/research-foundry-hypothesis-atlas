# Candidate Proposal — Pre-operative AFP and Liver-Function Reserve Predict Early Treatment Re-escalation After Curative Hepatectomy for HCC

**Island:** HCC (post-hepatectomy natural history) · **Episode:** 1 (Generation) · **Status:** deliverable, data-bound, discovery-phase (not yet fitted)

---

## 1. Unresolved claim and clinical consequence

**Unresolved claim.** After curative-intent hepatectomy for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), the **pre-operative laboratory profile** — specifically the **pre-operative AFP level** and **liver-function reserve** (albumin, total bilirubin, prothrombin time, platelet count, A/G ratio, hyaluronic acid) — **independently predicts the time to early treatment re-escalation** (first subsequent TACE or systemic therapy, a *surrogate* for relapse/progression) **beyond a baseline model using only static clinicopathologic features** (age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior locoregional therapy).

**Data-driven refinement of the Lead's framing (important).** The Lead's brief framed the exposure as "AFP level **+ slope/trajectory**." A bounded feasibility probe ([research job], succeeded) shows that a **pre-operative AFP slope is computable in only ~4.5% of the cohort** (1,160 / 25,933 hepatectomy patients have ≥2 pre-op AFP points within 0–90 d), whereas a **single pre-op AFP level is available in 91.5%** and a **pre-op liver panel in 95.6%**. Therefore the *primary* estimand is built on **pre-op AFP level + liver reserve** (feasible cohort-wide); the **AFP trajectory/slope** is retained as a **pre-specified sensitivity analysis in the 1,160-patient dense pre-op subcohort**, not the primary exposure. This is an honest, data-bound revision, not a weakening: it keeps the trajectory question alive where it is measurable and avoids an infeasible primary.

**Clinical consequence.** After curative resection, ~20–70% of HCC patients recur within 2 years and early recurrence drives mortality. There is **no standardized adjuvant-therapy guideline**; the timing of adjuvant/systemic therapy and the intensity of surveillance are currently decided on static features alone. A model that, from **routinely drawn pre-operative labs** (no extra imaging, no extra cost), identifies patients who will need re-escalation early would directly inform (a) surveillance intensity and (b) the decision/timing of adjuvant or early systemic therapy — a consequential, actionable natural-history decision.

**What this adds over the closest inspected work.**
- [K1] Hsu et al. 2026 built ML/Cox early-recurrence (≤24 mo) models on **static clinicopathologic + imaging features and excluded AFP from the final nomogram** (attributed to right-skew, despite high in-model weight) — i.e., the very biomarker this proposal centers was *dropped*.
- [K2] Bui et al. 2026 (n=295, C-index 0.64) identified **Child-Pugh** as a prognostic determinant but **excluded it from the final nomogram** — liver-function reserve under-used.
- [K3] Neves & Soares 2026 (systematic review, 36 studies / 14,716 patients) shows the field is **imaging-centric** (MRI 41.7%, CT 30.6%; **only 3 non-imaging models**), **75% single-center**, **97.2% retrospective**, and **externally validated in only 16.7%** of studies. A **lab-based model on a large real-world cohort (n≈26,778)** addresses a genuine, named gap.

## 2. Leading claim vs. strongest rival explanations

- **Leading claim (H1):** pre-op AFP level and liver-function reserve carry **independent** prognostic information for early re-escalation beyond static features.
- **Rival 1 — stage/burden confounding:** AFP level simply marks tumor burden/stage, not independent biology.
  *Differentiating test:* add CNLC stage + tumor size + prior-therapy count to the model; check whether the **AFP effect (HR) still excludes 1** after adjustment, and whether AFP contributes to discrimination **beyond** stage. If AFP vanishes once stage is in, Rival 1 is supported.
- **Rival 2 — clinician-behavior / surveillance selection:** "re-escalation" reflects how aggressively a clinician surveils and treats, not underlying biology.
  *Mitigation (not full refutation):* a fixed, pre-specified re-escalation definition (first TACE or systemic therapy after index), a fixed follow-up window, and sensitivity to the window (12/24/36 mo). Residual selection bias cannot be fully excluded in a single-center retrospective design — stated as a limitation.
- **Rival 3 — liver reserve proxies decompensation/survival, not recurrence:** the liver-reserve effect may capture hepatic decompensation (a survival/death signal) rather than recurrence biology.
  *Differentiating test:* model AFP and liver-reserve terms **separately** and in combination; report the liver-reserve effect on the **re-escalation** outcome (not death), and compare with a death-outcome sensitivity (death is sparse, n≈288, so this is a secondary check only).

These rivals are **not observationally fully separable** in this dataset (no imaging-confirmed recurrence, no randomized surveillance); the design is built to *bound* the leading claim and to report which rival best fits, not to prove mechanism.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries, index event, censoring, windows

- **Source:** `[internal dataset path]` (read-only). Snapshot `[source checksum]`. 12 source CSVs; all are ordinary files (no archive members to unpack).
- **Index event:** the patient's **first hepatectomy** = earliest row in `data_手术_8024330590283626027.csv` (procedures) where `手术` contains `切除`, with a valid `开始时间`. Baseline time = that `开始时间`.
- **Cohort (primary):** patients with **≥1 pre-operative (0–90 d before index hepatectomy) AFP measurement** (`检验`==`甲胎蛋白` in `data_检验_609065997844652188.csv`, `检验时间` within [index−90 d, index]). Feasibility probe: 23,730 of 25,933 valid-time hepatectomy patients (91.5%).
- **Exclusions (pre-specified):** (a) index hepatectomy with unparseable `开始时间`; (b) any **systemic therapy before index** (procedures containing `免疫`/`靶向` with `开始时间` < index `开始时间`) — these are not "re-escalation after curative intent"; (c) index hepatectomy within the **minimum follow-up window** of study end (define: index < 12 months before the latest encounter date in the dataset) so the primary 24-month window is observable.
- **Follow-up / censoring:** from index hepatectomy to **first re-escalation** (first TACE or systemic therapy *after* index) OR **administrative/loss censoring** at the patient's last encounter date (max `就诊时间`/`出院时间` in `data_基本信息_2500296761891079109.csv`) OR study end, whichever is first. Right-censoring bias for recent patients is a stated limitation.
- **Windows:** **primary at 24 months** (matches the "early recurrence ≤24 mo" convention used in [K1] and [K3]); **sensitivity at 12 and 36 months.**
- **Join keys:** `(患者主索引, 就诊号)` across all tables. **Time fields:** procedures `开始时间`; labs `检验时间`; encounters `就诊时间`/`入院时间`/`出院时间`.

## 4. Estimand and outcome

- **Primary outcome:** time from index hepatectomy to **first treatment re-escalation** = first subsequent procedure with `手术` containing `TACE`/`栓塞`/`动脉化疗`/`肝动脉造影` (TACE) **or** `免疫` (immunotherapy) / `靶向` (targeted) (systemic).
- **CRITICAL LIMITATION (stated):** re-escalation is a **relapse/progression SURROGATE**, not imaging- or pathology-confirmed recurrence. Confirmed recurrence diagnosis strings are sparse (~1.5k rows) and death is very sparse (288 rows), so a confirmed-recurrence primary is infeasible at this cohort size. Conclusions are about *time to re-escalation*, and any inference to true recurrence requires clinical adjudication / imaging confirmation not present in the data.
- **Estimand:** the **causal-agnostic prognostic association** — the difference in the distribution of time-to-re-escalation between patients with vs. without elevated pre-op AFP / impaired liver reserve, **adjusted for static features**, estimated within the 24-month window with right-censoring. This is a **predictive/associative** estimand, **not** a causal or mechanistic claim.
- **Secondary/competing outcomes:** death (`死亡` in diagnoses, sparse) and any recurrence-diagnosis string, reported descriptively and as a sensitivity only.

## 5. Feature definitions

- **Static baseline (B0):** age, sex (encounters); **CNLC stage** parsed from `诊断名称` (e.g. `肝细胞癌(CNLC Ia)`) in `data_诊断_7504718184492840569.csv`; **tumor size / satellite / vascular invasion** if parseable from `检查所见`/`检查诊断` in `data_检查_4203595081195465282.csv` (free text — partial, flagged); **count of prior TACE/ablation before index** (procedures).
- **Lab model (B1) = B0 +**
  - **Pre-op AFP level:** the **latest** pre-op `甲胎蛋白` `定量结果` within 0–90 d (primary); also median and max as sensitivity. AFP is right-skewed → model **log(AFP)** and/or a **threshold/binned** term (this directly addresses the right-skew that made [K1] drop AFP).
  - **Liver-function reserve:** albumin (`白蛋白`), total bilirubin (`总胆红素`), direct bilirubin (`直接胆红素`), PT (`凝血酶原时间`), platelet count (`血小板计数`), A/G ratio (`白球比值`), hyaluronic acid (`透明质酸`), ALT/AST (`丙氨酸氨基转移酶`/`门冬氨酸氨基转移酶`) — latest pre-op value, 0–90 d window.
  - **AFP trajectory (sensitivity, dense subcohort only):** linear slope of log(AFP) over pre-op points (requires ≥2 points, n≈1,160), plus AFP change first→last. **Not** in the primary model.
- **No unit column exists** on labs; values are **not pooled across assays** and are treated as assay-native (stated limitation). `定量结果` may be non-numeric/blank → parsed defensively.

## 6. Baseline vs. learned comparison (same question)

- **Baseline B0:** Cox proportional-hazards model on static features only (age, sex, CNLC stage, tumor size, prior-therapy count).
- **Learned/mechanistic B1:** (a) **Cox PH** with AFP level + liver-reserve terms (interpretable HRs — the mechanistic read on AFP level vs. stage), **and** (b) a **gradient-boosting time-to-event** model (XGBoost/LightGBM survival) on the same features.
- **What B1 reveals that B0 loses:** (i) whether AFP and liver reserve add **independent discrimination** beyond static features; (ii) the **shape of the AFP effect** (non-linear / threshold) that a static single-value baseline cannot see; (iii) in the dense subcohort, whether **AFP slope** (not just level) adds signal — the trajectory question.
- **Evaluation:** **C-index (concordance)**, **time-dependent AUC at 12/24/36 mo**, **calibration** (observed vs. predicted), **decision-curve analysis**; **bootstrap CI** (≥1000 resamples); **patient-level train/test split** (split by `患者主索引`, never by row, to prevent leakage). Report **B1 vs. B0 ΔC-index / ΔAUC with CI.**
- **Why both, not just the neural/GBM model:** the Cox coefficients give the *interpretable* independent-effect read required to adjudicate Rival 1 (AFP independent of stage); the GBM model tests whether non-linear/interaction structure adds discrimination. This is a substantive method comparison (interpretability + non-linearity), not a predictive-gain chase.

## 7. Falsification criteria (pre-specified)

- **Supportive:** B1 C-index (or 24-mo time-dependent AUC) exceeds B0 by a **pre-specified margin (≥ +0.02)** with bootstrap CI excluding 0, **AND** pre-op AFP level and/or liver reserve is an **independent predictor (HR 95% CI excluding 1)** in the Cox model after adjusting for static features. → supports H1; AFP/liver-reserve carry independent prognostic signal.
- **Adverse:** B1 does **not** improve over B0 (Δ CI includes 0) **AND** AFP trajectory/level and liver reserve are **not** independent predictors (HR CIs include 1) after stage adjustment. → supports **Rival 1** (AFP marks stage/burden); the lab profile adds nothing beyond static features.
- **Inconclusive:** re-escalation too sparse, censoring bias dominates (e.g. <30% of cohort observed to 24 mo), or the dense subcohort (n≈1,160) is too small for the trajectory sensitivity to be stable. → report honestly; the trajectory claim is deferred; the level+reserve primary may still be interpretable.

**What each outcome would and would not establish:** supportive → an *independent prognostic association* (predictive), **not** causality or mechanism, and **not** confirmed recurrence. Adverse → AFP/liver reserve are *redundant with stage* in this cohort. Inconclusive → no claim; a larger/longer cohort or confirmed-recurrence adjudication is required.

## 8. Honest limitations

1. **Re-escalation is a relapse surrogate**, not imaging/pathology-confirmed recurrence.
2. **Single-center, retrospective**; no external validation possible in this dataset (the field's main weakness per [K3]).
3. **No unit column** on labs; cross-assay pooling unsupported.
4. **Right-censoring** for recent patients (2024–2025 index) compresses the 24-mo window.
5. **CNLC stage / tumor size** are partially missing (parsed from free text).
6. **Sparse death (n≈288)** and **sparse confirmed-recurrence (~1.5k)** → death/recurrence are secondary only.
7. **Clinician-behavior selection** (Rival 2) cannot be fully excluded.
8. **Trajectory (slope) limited to ~4.5%** of the cohort → sensitivity, not primary.
