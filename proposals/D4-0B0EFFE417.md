## New decision-analytic layer

This layer is locked before looking at outcomes or model results and is run only after the primary analysis has finite, valid predictions. For each model `m∈{0,1}`, capacity `q` in the fixed grid `Q={0.05,0.10,0.15,0.20}`, and model-specific top-`K_q=ceil(qN)` set `A_m(q)`, calculate only from frozen out-of-fold probabilities and observed `Y`:

- `TP_m(q)=N^{-1} sum A_m(q)Y`;
- `FP_m(q)=N^{-1} sum A_m(q)(1-Y)`;
- `FN_m(q)=N^{-1} sum (1-A_m(q))Y`;
- `PPV_m(q)=sum A_m(q)Y / sum A_m(q)`;
- `Capture_m(q)=sum A_m(q)Y / sum Y` when `sum Y>0`.

For an explicit, dimensionless surrogate loss grid `G={(c_FP,c_FN): c_FP∈{0.25,0.5,1,2,4}, c_FN∈{0.25,0.5,1,2,4}}`, define

`L_m(q,c_FP,c_FN)=c_FP*FP_m(q)+c_FN*FN_m(q)`

and `DeltaLoss(q,c_FP,c_FN)=1000*[L_0-L_1]`. Positive values favor M1 under that declared surrogate loss. The grid is a sensitivity surface, not a fitted utility function: no pair is selected after seeing results, no clinical cost is assigned, and no loss ratio is presented as a recommendation. The report must include the full grid, not only the most favorable cell. At fixed `q`, the decomposition into FP and FN makes the result auditable and prevents a purported gain from hiding a larger miss burden.

Also report `DeltaPPV(q)`, `DeltaCapture(q)`, and selected-event counts, with simultaneous 98% bootstrap intervals over the complete predeclared `Q×G` family. This component is identifiable only as a conditional observed-repeat surrogate because every participant has observed repeat status within `S_A`; it does not identify the effect of offering or ordering confirmatory assessment, attendance, downstream treatment, cost, preference, harm, or clinical utility. It must not be called net benefit or decision-curve analysis.

A decision-layer result is supportive only if the parent’s full primary Brier support gate passes, M0 comparator and calibration gates pass, at least 8/10 repeat-specific Brier gains are positive, the bootstrap completes, and the predeclared surface shows a positive `DeltaLoss` interval for at least one capacity in `Q` across a contiguous, declared subset of the loss grid that includes `(1,1)`, with no sign reversal hidden by selecting a single favorable capacity. ## Interpretation and limitations

Supportive results mean only that certified baseline cystatin-C threshold status adds reproducible conditional information and may improve a predeclared capacity/loss surrogate for the protocol-defined repeat equation state among selected complete repeat attenders. Adverse results mean that cystatin augmentation worsens this frozen predictive/prioritization surrogate in this schedule boundary, not that cystatin C lacks value in other uses. Inconclusive results mean uncertainty, instability, calibration failure, sparse cells, contradictory components, or technical inability to complete the declared analysis; they do not support a threshold recommendation. Certificate, assay/equation, temporal, join, or execution failures are neutral technical stops, not biological findings.
