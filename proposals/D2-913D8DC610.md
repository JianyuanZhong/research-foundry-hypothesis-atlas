# Episode 9 repaired hypothesis: donor-aware intervention-by-macrophage-context interaction in E-MTAB-8316

Seed: D2-T1. Status: provisional and evidence-bound; no inferential result has been computed.

## Proposed relationship

In the configured E-MTAB-8316 rheumatoid-arthritis FLS/monocyte coculture experiment, the intervention-associated FLS transcript response depends on macrophage/coculture context rather than being a context-independent response. The intervention is the recorded UNC106238 condition; the primary estimand is the prespecified intervention-by-context interaction, tested after accounting for the four FLS donor clusters and nested monocyte-donor structure.

This is an intervention-associated expression hypothesis. It is not a claim of direct drug-target action, causal efficacy, mechanism, or clinical benefit.

## Clinical importance and advance

A context-independent response would support a simpler target-prioritization rule. A reproducible interaction would indicate that the same intervention has different transcriptomic consequences in different inflammatory microenvironments, motivating microenvironment-specific evaluation and measurement of macrophage state before candidate testing. The advance over a drug-response table is an explicit donor-aware test of whether the intervention contrast changes with macrophage context, rather than ranking mean expression changes.

## Population, temporal boundary, and computation

Use all 12 verified condition samples, restricted to SA049, SA051, SA104 and SA114 and their documented nested monocyte donors. Average repeated measurements within each FLS donor/condition before exploratory inference; do not treat samples as 12 independent donors. The temporal boundary is the recorded intervention/context exposure. There is no validated time-course field, so no temporal causal claim is permitted.

Baseline: donor-aware additive model for each gene's response, with separate intervention and macrophage-context terms and no interaction; use donor fixed effects or donor-paired contrasts and account for nested monocyte donors. Alternative: the same model plus the prespecified intervention-by-context interaction. Report interaction effect size, uncertainty, donor-level stability, and batch/technical sensitivity where fields exist. A simple descriptive paired contrast is an additional baseline: average within donor/context, calculate intervention contrasts, then compare contrasts across contexts.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive: a prespecified interaction is nonzero with uncertainty excluding the null, stable across independent FLS donor clusters, and not explained by a documented technical field. This supports context-dependent intervention-associated expression only.

Adverse: a precise reproducible null or consistent reversal across donors rejects context dependence for the tested genes/contrast. It does not prove no biology or no clinical effect.

Inconclusive: wide uncertainty, too few independent donor clusters, missing/ambiguous donor or context fields, failed matrix-design join, or protocol/readout ambiguity. An imprecise null is not decisive.

## Missing evidence and stronger claims

Clinical outcomes, direct target measurements, validated mechanism, independent technical replicates sufficient for causal inference, and a validated clinical endpoint are unavailable. Cell-specific interpretation requires protocol reconciliation. Direct target action, clinical efficacy, and mechanism require additional perturbational, target-specific, and clinical studies.

## Method comparison and deferred alternatives

The simple baseline is the donor-paired descriptive contrast. The substantive alternative is the donor-aware additive-versus-interaction model, which tests the scientific uncertainty directly and estimates uncertainty while preserving donor dependence. A high-dimensional predictive or neural model is deferred because only four independent FLS donor clusters are available and prediction accuracy is not the scientific question. A mechanistic model is deferred because the data do not identify direct target action. Revisit either alternative only with more independent donors, validated context labels, or a prespecified mechanistic perturbation.
