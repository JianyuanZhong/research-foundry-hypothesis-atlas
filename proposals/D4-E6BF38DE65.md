## Equations and outcome

After certificate validation, require serum creatinine in micromoles/L and convert `Scr=raw_30700/88.4` mg/dL exactly. Require cystatin C in mg/L or a certified conversion. With age in years and female indicator F=1 for female, use race-free 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine:

`eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^a * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) * 0.9938^Age * 1.012^F`, where `(k,a)=(0.7,-0.241)` female and `(0.9,-0.302)` male.

Use cystatin C equation `eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) * 0.996^Age * 0.932^F`. Define baseline discordance `Z=log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)` and repeat outcome `Y=I(eGFRcr_1<60)`. These are equation-derived status labels, not measured GFR, CKD, chronicity, kidney failure, or patient-important outcomes. Equation provenance is Inker et al. 2021, DOI `10.1056/nejmoa2102953`, PMCID `PMC8822996`.
