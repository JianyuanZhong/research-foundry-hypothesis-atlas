# A pre-label actionability and superseding-plan gate for pulmonary discharge handoff

## Decision question and substantive advance

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. The two parents establish complementary prerequisites: a source-bound, append-only, storetime-correct packet replay with held-out workflow/calendar-proxy stress tests, and domain-balanced finite-population audit that prevents a pooled result from hiding a failing service domain. The remaining clinically consequential bottleneck is not whether a string can be reconstructed. It is what a failed or successful reconstruction should cause a future workflow to do.

A radiology recommendation can be present and correctly copied yet no longer be actionable at departure because the patient or team explicitly superseded it, because the plan is conditional or incomplete, or because the available text does not establish who or what should act. Conversely, an apparently absent discharge placement may be an active unresolved recommendation that deserves clinician reconciliation rather than automatic propagation. Documentation discordance alone cannot distinguish these states. This proposal adds a pre-label, route-blinded actionability and superseding-plan decision-analysis layer while preserving the parents' frozen routing experiment.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> **H1 (decision-layer hypothesis):** Among the frozen first-eligible pulmonary opportunities, a route-independent, source-bound adjudication of (i) whether the exact recommendation remains active and actionable at departure and (ii) whether an explicit superseding plan displaces it can partition opportunities into a reproducible, conservative AUTO-eligible lane, a clinician-reconciliation lane, and a DEFER lane. The partition will remain stable in the storetime-available D packet and in every adequately supported held-out temporal-proxy and terminal-service domain, with no positive AUTO assignment when actionability, supersession, provenance, or chronology is unresolved.

The experiment tests a documentation-and-workflow construct, not appropriateness, safety, delivery, viewing, completion, or benefit. A supportive result only selects a narrowly defined prospective **silent** action-allocation study. It does not authorize an alert, order, referral, copied recommendation, or patient-facing intervention.

## What is already supported and what remains untested

The strongest available evidence is source feasibility: MIMIC contains admission-linked radiology and discharge notes with `charttime` and `storetime`, radiology detail rows that can reconstruct the inherited report/addendum chain, discharge-detail rows linked to the canonical discharge note, admission departure and death fields, and pre-departure service rows. The parents' frozen protocol can therefore define one first eligible admission per subject, one canonical discharge artifact, exact recommendation atoms and routes, positive-probability sampling, and design-weighted adverse uncertainty.

Those sources do not reveal whether a recipient saw a recommendation, whether a clinician was responsible, whether an interface displayed it, whether an order or referral was made, whether a patient agreed, whether outside care occurred, whether follow-up was completed, or whether the recommendation was medically appropriate. A discharge text is not a delivered handoff. MIMIC also lacks a defensible clinical gold standard for actionability or supersession. Independent experts can adjudicate a prespecified text construct, but agreement is not clinical truth.

The unresolved claim is narrower and operationally decisive: given a frozen recommendation atom and a storetime-valid discharge packet, can independent, route-blinded reviewers distinguish `ACTIVE_ACTIONABLE`, `ACTIVE_RECONCILE`, `SUPERSEDED`, `INACTIVE_NO_ACTION`, and `UNKNOWN` using explicit evidence rules, and can the resulting conservative lane determine whether the only permissible prospective silent action is AUTO, clinician reconciliation, or DEFER? The advance over either parent is an explicit decision layer with an auditable supersession hierarchy and prespecified utility/workload thresholds, rather than treating every discordance as an undifferentiated error or treating text reproducibility as permission to automate.

## Frozen packet and target/control universe

The child adds no new eligible admission and no reader-selected target. For each already frozen and sampled admission, construct the inherited packets at all six cutoffs. At cutoff `c`, a source note is visible exactly when nonmissing `storetime <= c`; `charttime` never substitutes. The canonical DS is `DS_UNAVAILABLE` before its storetime and remains unavailable if storetime is missing. A later packet can document delayed arrival but cannot rewrite the D-time result. Every packet has an append-only key `(hadm_id, atom_id, packet_cutoff, parser_version)` and records source note IDs, source storetimes, cutoff, frozen route, inherited `B`, and packet status.

The actionability layer is evaluated only on the D packet for the primary decision estimand. `D-24h`, `D-6h`, and `D-1h` quantify whether an action-allocation decision was possible before departure; `D+1h` and `D+24h` quantify delayed documentation only. D+ labels can never turn a D-time `AUTO` failure into a success. If an atom's required radiology chain, canonical DS, or relevant detail rows are unavailable, contradictory, late, or malformed at D, its D-time actionability and supersession status is `UNKNOWN_PACKET`, and its only admissible lane is `DEFER`.

The actionability target universe is the inherited exact atom universe, not all sentences. A target atom is included only if the inherited eligibility and target-freeze code identifies it before any new adjudication. The fixed controls are unchanged: the parent's negative and route-scrambled controls, plus synthetic packet controls that are excluded from inference and workload. No control can become a new target because a reviewer finds it interesting.

## Two independent, route-blinded decision panels

The protocol has two freshly rostered panels, with no reviewer overlap with the eligibility, target-unit, route, or primary audit panels. Reviewers see no route, domain, sampling stratum, packet cutoff labels, or outcomes. Panel packets are generated from the same sealed source manifest but have separately redacted and independently rendered views. Each panel records its own label, evidence spans, source note IDs, confidence, and an `UNKNOWN` reason. Individual labels remain available for adverse-compatible inference; majority consensus is descriptive only.

### Panel A: activity and actionability

Panel A sees the reciprocal radiology chain, the D-valid canonical DS and permitted admission/departure context, with all route strings, target-linked recommendation spans, modality/interval values, service-domain labels, and packet-status fields masked. It receives a canonical target placeholder and only the minimum atom identity needed to locate its corresponding text without disclosing the inherited route. It must answer whether the recommendation represented by that frozen atom is textually active at D and whether a future workflow would have a defined action object, without judging whether that action is medically appropriate.

The mutually exclusive activity/actionability label is:

* `AA_ACTIVE_EXPLICIT`: an unambiguous current plan at D identifies the exact target/action and due interval, with no conditional-only language, and identifies an action object (for example a follow-up test, referral, or clinician/patient task) in the permitted text; this means actionable as documentation, not clinically appropriate.
* `AA_ACTIVE_INCOMPLETE`: the recommendation remains active, but action, modality, interval, polarity, or responsible action object is incomplete or internally inconsistent; it requires clinician reconciliation.
* `AA_CONDITIONAL`: the recommendation is contingent on a result, symptom, preference, or event not resolved in the permitted packet; it is not AUTO-eligible.
* `AA_EXPLICIT_NO_ACTION`: the packet explicitly states that no follow-up/action is intended for this target; this is not a missed handoff.
* `AA_NOT_FOUND`: the frozen target is absent from the D discharge artifact while the radiology opportunity remains available; this is a documentation omission, not proof of clinical inaction.
* `AA_UNKNOWN`: unreadable, contradictory, missing, or insufficient source evidence, including a packet unavailable at D.

A label is `AA_ACTIVE_EXPLICIT` only when every atom component is present and the action scope is unambiguous. A generic mention of a nodule, a problem-list entry, a copied historical statement, or a recommendation without a due interval cannot qualify. The panel cannot infer patient preference, appropriateness, prognosis, responsibility, or completion.

### Panel S: superseding-plan adjudication

Panel S sees the same permitted D packet and radiology chain but does not see Panel A labels, routes, domains, or actionability labels. It codes whether an explicit plan at or before D displaces the exact recommendation. The supersession hierarchy is deterministic and must be applied in order:

1. `SS_DIRECT_REPLACEMENT`: an explicit current plan names the same target and replaces the exact modality/interval or states an alternative follow-up plan.
2. `SS_EXPLICIT_CANCEL`: an explicit current statement says the prior recommendation is cancelled, no longer needed, or intentionally not pursued for the target.
3. `SS_GOAL_OR_CONTEXT_CHANGE`: an explicit plan makes the target recommendation inapplicable because of a documented transition in goals or care setting; this is a text construct, not a judgment that the transition was appropriate.
4. `SS_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`: competing current plans exist and cannot be ordered or reconciled from permitted timestamps/text.
5. `SS_NONE_OBSERVED`: no explicit superseding statement is present.
6. `SS_UNKNOWN`: missing/unavailable/contradictory packet or insufficient evidence.

Only an explicit target-linked replacement or cancellation can be `SS_DIRECT_REPLACEMENT` or `SS_EXPLICIT_CANCEL`; absence of a recommendation is never itself supersession. A generic hospice, discharge, or outpatient statement is not a superseding plan unless it explicitly addresses the target. `SS_GOAL_OR_CONTEXT_CHANGE` does not establish a clinical decision or patient preference. Any two qualifying superseding categories, unresolved temporal ordering, or disagreement between reviewers is adverse-compatible `SS_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`, not a favorable replacement.

Panel S also records `superseder_note_id`, exact evidence span offsets, and the relevant `charttime/storetime`; a superseding statement whose storetime is after D cannot establish D-time supersession. Detail rows are linked to the parent DS note and cannot provide an independent timestamp. This explicit hierarchy addresses the key decision bottleneck without pretending that a text adjudicator knows what the clinical team should have done.

### Cross-panel decision state

After both panels are sealed, the compiler forms a deterministic cross-product, never a reviewer route vote. For each unit and packet:

* `ACTIVE_CLEAR` = `AA_ACTIVE_EXPLICIT` and `SS_NONE_OBSERVED`, with no unresolved disagreement, valid D availability, exact atom match, and inherited placement `B=1`.
* `ACTIVE_RECONCILE` = `AA_ACTIVE_INCOMPLETE` or `AA_CONDITIONAL`, or `SS_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`, or a target-linked active plan conflicts with the exact atom while no unambiguous replacement is established.
* `SUPERSEDED_CLEAR` = `AA_EXPLICIT_NO_ACTION` or `AA_ACTIVE_EXPLICIT` with `SS_DIRECT_REPLACEMENT`, `SS_EXPLICIT_CANCEL`, or `SS_GOAL_OR_CONTEXT_CHANGE`, provided the superseder is D-available and explicit.
* `NOT_DOCUMENTED` = `AA_NOT_FOUND` with `SS_NONE_OBSERVED` and valid radiology opportunity; this is an omission state.
* `DECISION_UNKNOWN` = any `AA_UNKNOWN`, `SS_UNKNOWN`, packet-unavailable/conflicting state, redaction breach, source identity failure, or unresolved panel disagreement.

For admission-level safety, any unit in `DECISION_UNKNOWN`, `ACTIVE_RECONCILE`, or unresolved supersession prevents `AUTO`; any `ACTIVE_CLEAR` unit paired with another adverse unit yields `RECON` or `DEFER` according to the rules below. `SUPERSEDED_CLEAR` is a resolved no-action unit and does not count as an omission. `NOT_DOCUMENTED` is not AUTO-eligible and enters the reconciliation lane unless the inherited parent route independently forces `DEFER`.

## Decision rule: AUTO, clinician reconciliation, or DEFER

The child does not replace the inherited route. It adds a conservative action-allocation gate to the route output. The final D-time lane is calculated after sealed labels using this exact precedence:

1. **DEFER** if any unit has unavailable/malformed/conflicting source data, `DECISION_UNKNOWN`, unresolved boundary, unresolved supersession, missing required D storetime, an unresolved safety/minority-unit state, or a parent `DEFER`/`TIMING` state that cannot be separated from an information-availability failure.
2. **CLINICIAN_RECONCILIATION** if no DEFER condition exists and at least one unit is `ACTIVE_RECONCILE`, `NOT_DOCUMENTED`, or a clear active unit fails exact target-atom placement (`B=0`) while the recommendation remains active; the reconciliation task must expose the source spans, competing plan, modality, interval, and unknown reason without silently copying text.
3. **AUTO_CANDIDATE** only if every decision-relevant unit is `ACTIVE_CLEAR`, the inherited route is `AUTO`, `B=1` for every unit, the canonical D packet is available and serialization-invariant, no control or route-scrambled signal indicates leakage, and all actionability/supersession gates below pass.
4. **RESOLVED_NO_ACTION** if all units are `SUPERSEDED_CLEAR` or explicit no-action and no active unit remains. This is reported separately and is not a prospective AUTO placement opportunity.

Thus `AUTO_CANDIDATE` means only “eligible for a future silent automation test under this documentation contract.” It does not mean auto-order, auto-referral, or that a clinician should have acted. In prospective implementation, all lanes other than a separately approved `AUTO_CANDIDATE` atom must be `RECON` or `DEFER`; no pooled rescue or neighboring-atom rescue is permitted.

## Decision-analysis estimands and fixed thresholds

All primary estimates are finite-population, design-weighted admission-level estimates using the inherited phase-1/phase-2 inclusion probabilities and worst-unit precedence. Units remain nested and are not independent observations. Report HT totals, Hájek ratios, exact design variance where supported, at least 20,000 Rao–Wu resamples, simultaneous 95% intervals, effective sample sizes, sampled/census denominators, and adverse-compatible lower/upper bounds when labels are missing or disputed.

Primary estimands, all defined before labels, are:

* `P_ACTIVE`: proportion of frozen opportunities with a D-valid active recommendation (`AA_ACTIVE_EXPLICIT` or `AA_ACTIVE_INCOMPLETE/CONDITIONAL`) and no clear supersession.
* `P_SUPERSEDED`: proportion with `SUPERSEDED_CLEAR`.
* `P_RECON`: proportion whose conservative action lane is `CLINICIAN_RECONCILIATION`.
* `P_DEFER`: proportion whose conservative lane is `DEFER`.
* `P_AUTO`: proportion meeting the complete `AUTO_CANDIDATE` rule.
* `P_NOT_DOCUMENTED`: active opportunities absent from the canonical D discharge artifact.
* `P_FALSE_AUTO`: admission-level adverse event in which any initial AUTO candidate is compatible with actionability uncertainty, unresolved supersession, nonexact atom placement, or a safety-auditor adverse unit.
* `R_AA` and `R_SS`: route-blinded panel reproducibility, reported separately by label and by atom; disagreement is not resolved in favor of consensus.
* workload: minutes per Panel A/S review, total physician-hours, design-weighted hours per 100 frozen opportunities, and the incremental hours required for each `AUTO`, `RECON`, and `DEFER` lane.

The following margins are governance nomination criteria, not clinical safety thresholds. They must be signed and hash-frozen before any real label is opened:

* For each claimable temporal-proxy and terminal-service domain, lower simultaneous 95% bound for reproducible `ACTIVE_CLEAR` among candidate-AUTO units is at least `0.90`, and lower bound for `P_AUTO` among all frozen opportunities is nonzero.
* Upper simultaneous 95% bound for `P_FALSE_AUTO` is at most `0.02`; upper bound for any unknown/conflict state that could be mistaken for AUTO is at most `0.05`.
* Lower simultaneous 95% bound for Panel A and Panel S exact-category reproducibility is at least `0.90`; Cohen kappa is diagnostic only and cannot pass a gate by itself. If either panel has systematic category disagreement, the affected lane is `DEFER`.
* The excess absolute burden of `CLINICIAN_RECONCILIATION` over the parent's documentation-discordance estimate must not be used as a success criterion; it is reported as a workload/decision consequence. The pre-approved maximum incremental actionability-layer burden is 240 physician-hours, and the compiler must show an additive ledger proving parent worst-case charge plus this child, fixed controls, reserve, duplicate panels, and all domain audits remain at or below the inherited 1,400-hour contract. If the ledger cannot fit without reducing a required parent review, the result is `NO_SELECTION`, not a smaller sample.
* For each supported domain, the upper 95% bound for design-weighted clinician-hours per 100 opportunities must be below the governance-approved absolute budget, and the ratio of reconciliation hours to the corresponding pooled value must be at most 1.50. This is workload feasibility, not benefit.
* A decision-analysis sensitivity table must be prepared before labels for cost ratios `c_AUTO:c_RECON:c_DEFER` in the fixed grid `(1:2:4, 1:4:8, 1:8:16)`. The selected lane is robust only if the same lane remains optimal under all three ratios after replacing uncertain labels with adverse-compatible bounds. If the optimal lane changes across the grid, the domain is `DEFER/INCONCLUSIVE` rather than optimized after seeing results.
* No supported domain may have an upper `P_FALSE_AUTO` or unknown/conflict burden more than 0.05 above the pooled estimate. A failing domain cannot be rescued by pooled or neighboring-domain estimates.

The cost grid is a transparent workload-and-uncertainty sensitivity analysis, not a claim of clinical utility or a surrogate for patient outcomes. There is no observed benefit outcome and therefore no causal decision curve. Any net-benefit terminology is prohibited unless prospectively supplied with clinically adjudicated harms and benefits.

## Domain, packet, and control structure

Domain membership is frozen before all actionability labels. Retain the parents' conservative possible-calendar bands derived from `anchor_year_group`, widened as `C_i=[a_i+q_i-1,b_i+q_i+1]`, with `T_EARLY`, `T_LATE`, `T_BRIDGE`, and `T_UNKNOWN`; never use raw shifted years. Retain exact terminal-service groups `S_MED_CODES`, `S_PROC_CODES`, `S_OBS`, `S_PSYCH`, `S_OMED`, and `S_OTHER_UNKNOWN`, plus explicit service-path and ICU diagnostics from the parent. Unknowns are never dropped. Each occupied `(sampling_stratum,T,S)` cell receives positive probability and follows the inherited fixed allocation and 900-admission ceiling.

Controls are label-independent and sealed before panel review:

1. **Route-scrambled actionability control:** permute inherited route labels across matched packets while preserving note text and atom identity. Actionability labels must not track the scrambled route; any excess AUTO assignment is a leakage failure.
2. **Target-absent control:** construct a matched within-admission negative unit using an exact lexical/dictionary rule frozen before target freeze, requiring a non-pulmonary, non-recommendation span with the same packet structure and a prespecified modality/interval-null state. It is a parser specificity control only, not a clinical comparison. If the frozen universe contains no positive-probability controls, report the control as unsupported and do not claim specificity.
3. **Superseder decoy control:** insert a synthetic explicit replacement statement into a copied packet with a storetime at D-minus-1-hour; Panel S must classify it as superseding, while an identical statement with storetime D-plus-1-hour must not change the D label. Synthetic records are excluded from estimands and ledger.
4. **Late-arrival monotonicity:** adding any note with storetime greater than cutoff cannot change the earlier actionability, supersession, route, or `B`; violation is a hard failure.
5. **Storetime isolation:** replace permitted times with a common sentinel. The system must return unavailable/DEFER, not infer availability from text order.
6. **Cutoff permutation:** shuffle cutoffs within admission. Improvement over correctly ordered packets indicates leakage.
7. **Detail-order perturbation:** reverse `field_ordinal` in synthetic detail rows. Output may remain unchanged only where the frozen dictionary is order-invariant; otherwise it must fail closed.
8. **Domain-label permutation:** permute domain labels after all outputs are sealed. Overall output must remain unchanged and domain contrasts must be null within simultaneous uncertainty.
9. **Nuisance-token and route-blinding challenge:** replace service names, packet state names, route tokens, and target identifiers with random pseudonyms in panel packets. A positive AUTO change is leakage.
10. **Adverse planted supersession:** plant an explicit but conflicting target-linked replacement in a synthetic packet at D and verify that the lane becomes RECON or SUPERSEDED, never AUTO.

Controls are code and measurement checks, not evidence of clinical correctness. Synthetic packets are never counted in any denominator.

## Analysis, uncertainty, and interpretation

The primary analysis is frozen before labels. Calculate each endpoint by sampling stratum and domain with inherited weights. For a binary endpoint `y`, use the parent HT total and finite-population variance; use Taylor linearization for ratios. Use one common set of at least 20,000 resamples for pooled estimates, each supported domain, all lanes, panel reproducibility, workload, and drift. Exact hypergeometric inversion is required for zero/rare events. For every label with missing review, redaction breach, disputed actionability, or disputed supersession, optimize over all compatible assignments. An initial AUTO with missing audit evidence is adverse in the upper bound. A disagreement cannot be allocated to the favorable state.

The primary lane is admission-level. An admission is AUTO-eligible only if every atom is `ACTIVE_CLEAR` and all parent operational gates pass; one unclear minority unit blocks automation. Secondary atom-level tables must expose which modality/interval/action family caused reconciliation or deferral, but cannot relax the admission rule. Report positive-probability support, maximum weight, normalized maximum weight share, Kish ESS, certainty fraction, nonresponse, reviewer workload and reviewer balance for every domain. Fewer than 30 sampled event/reference admissions, fewer than 10 non-sentinel events, or ESS below 50 makes the relevant domain `INCONCLUSIVE`; no event is not zero risk.

The result is **supportive** only if all inherited parent gates pass, all supported temporal/service domains pass the actionability, supersession, false-AUTO, reproducibility, workload, support, and control gates, the three cost-ratio analyses select the same lane for each claimed domain, and all seals, hashes, chronology, monotonicity, and additive workload checks pass. Support means only that the frozen text-defined process supports a prospectively governed silent validation in the explicitly supported domains. It does not support automatic care.

The result is **adverse** if any supported domain has an excessive false-AUTO bound, unresolved supersession assigned AUTO, poor panel reproducibility, a late-arrival or storetime leakage violation, a target-absent specificity failure, a workload budget failure, or a cost-grid instability that could change the lane. A finding of high `P_RECON` is not itself a protocol failure: it is evidence that a clinician-reconciliation lane is more defensible than automation. A high `P_SUPERSEDED` indicates that omission is not necessarily a handoff failure. A high `P_DEFER` indicates missing evidence or construct ambiguity and blocks automation.

The result is **inconclusive** for sparse domains, unsupported controls, too-wide bounds, missing packet clocks, nonresponse, failed reviewer separation, a budget that cannot accommodate the required panels, or disagreement that cannot be bounded. Inconclusive is not a favorable result and cannot be repaired by dropping domains, using D-plus text, taking consensus, imputing labels, or changing thresholds.

A correct computational result can establish source availability, exact chronology, packet serialization, deterministic lane derivation, design-weighted documentation/actionability rates, panel disagreement, workload, domain contrasts, and control behavior. It cannot establish that the adjudicated active plan was clinically appropriate, that a responsible clinician should act, that a recommendation was received or viewed, that an order/referral was completed, that follow-up occurred, or that any lane improves safety, benefit, equity, or outcomes. Those claims require prospective actual-calendar event logs, responsibility and viewing data, transmitted-artifact capture, patient preferences, independent clinical adjudication, and eventually a governed comparative study.

## Falsification and verification gates

The verifier must execute the following without opening labels until upstream checks pass.

1. Recompute source and schema hashes, row counts, duplicate keys, all joins, first-eligible selection, reciprocal links, canonical DS, source-note identities, packet cutoffs, domains, allocation, inclusion probabilities, and the additive ledger.
2. Assert that no prohibited intervention-like field is read by target, actionability, supersession, route, domain, allocation, or analysis code.
3. Assert that Panel A never receives route, target recommendation spans, supersession labels, or domain; Panel S never receives Panel A labels or routes; eligibility/unit reviewers never overlap rosters.
4. Verify every positive actionability/supersession label has source note ID, exact span, valid storetime, and a D-valid timestamp. A detail row cannot manufacture a timestamp.
5. Inject D-plus superseder text and confirm it cannot alter the D lane; inject D-minus explicit replacement and confirm it does.
6. Inject unresolved competing plans and missing times; output must be `DEFER`, never `AUTO` or favorable `SUPERSEDED_CLEAR`.
7. Reverse packet order, permute cutoffs, isolate storetime, perturb detail order, and permute domain labels; apply the predetermined pass/fail assertions.
8. Plant unsafe active minority units in a non-sentinel cell and in one terminal-service or late possible-calendar domain. Whole-admission and domain adverse bounds must block pooled AUTO nomination.
9. Plant a high pooled reconciliation burden in one service domain while pooled burden remains within budget. The domain workload gate must fail.
10. Re-run the actionability cost grid with fixed labels and adverse-compatible endpoints. Lane selection must be unchanged under a claimed robust nomination; otherwise it is inconclusive.
11. Verify that any missing audit, nonresponse, redaction breach, or reviewer disagreement enters the compatible adverse set and that zero events receive a positive exact upper bound.
12. Verify that a duplicated `subject_id` in the frozen population stops the run, and that duplicated safe units within an admission do not alter route or weight.
13. Verify append-only seals, no-change declarations for held-out domains, and claim-checker rejection of any narrative asserting clinical appropriateness, delivery, viewing, safety, benefit, causal effect, external transport, or exact calendar trends.

The verifier can check computation and whether written conclusions follow from output files. It cannot determine the clinical truth of an adjudicated active plan, the meaning of a terminal service as responsibility, or the utility of AUTO versus reconciliation without prospective clinical and workflow evidence.

## Expected decision consequences and remaining uncertainty

If the actionability layer passes, the appropriate next step is a silent, actual-workflow study in only the domains that independently pass. The study must log final report/addendum availability, DS draft/version/edit/sign/transmission, physical departure, recipient/responsibility, receipt/view/UI rendering, reconciliation actions, target-linked orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestations, patient preference, and independent appropriateness/safety outcomes. It must retain a default DEFER lane and must not silently convert missing events into successful handoff.

If the layer shows many clear active plans but poor exact placement, clinician reconciliation is the defensible next experiment; this is a clinically meaningful decision even though MIMIC cannot prove improved outcomes. If it shows many explicit superseding plans, the protocol should not call omissions failures and should measure the supersession workflow prospectively. If most cases are unknown or conditional, DEFER is the only defensible allocation and the data-contract or adjudication needs prospective repair. If workload is concentrated in a service or temporal-proxy domain, any silent study should be restricted or resourced there rather than licensed by pooled averages.

The substantive advance is therefore a decision-analysis layer that converts a text-routing audit into a conservative choice among three workflow lanes without pretending that retrospective documentation can establish clinical benefit. The remaining uncertainties are fundamental: MIMIC's storage clocks are proxies for availability, service is a proxy for workflow, actionability and supersession are expert-defined text constructs, exact real calendar dates are unavailable, and no actual delivery, viewing, responsibility, appropriateness, completion, safety, or benefit is observed. These limitations are preserved as explicit reasons that the experiment can nominate only a prospective silent validation, never retrospective automation or a causal clinical conclusion.
