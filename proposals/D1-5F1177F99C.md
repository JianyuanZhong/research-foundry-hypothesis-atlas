# Episode 72: a transportable, mutually exclusive respiratory trajectory with explicit observability and exit states

## Status, question, and substantive repair

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen parent cohort, first qualifying stay rule, five landmarks, exact exposure labels and decision time, inherited exclusions, the primary endpoint windows and thresholds, and the noncausal interpretation. It does not claim that a care-plan value documents an SBT, extubation evaluation, readiness, or benefit.

The lineage supports only bounded documentation findings: the two literal care-plan values are sparse and hospital-concentrated; earlier short-horizon associations were dominated by peak-pressure charting and were unstable across hospitals; and independent liberation validation is not available. The unresolved question remains: **among the frozen labelled person-landmarks, is either exact label associated with a subsequent persistent, independently charted respiratory-support trajectory after accounting for pre-label respiratory state and documentation opportunity, and does that association transport to hospitals not used for fitting?** This is a predictive/associational information question, not a causal treatment-effect question.

The substantive repair is to make endpoint assignment and transport executable rather than merely descriptive. Every landmark receives exactly one outcome state under an explicit precedence order; discharge, death, ICU transfer-related truncation, and incomplete source observation cannot be silently converted into improvement or ordinary non-improvement. The primary held-out analysis excludes hospital identity as a usable patient feature and reports both a joint four-state transport estimand and a complete-observation conditional risk contrast. Pre-D state and observation opportunity are separated from post-D outcome construction, including a strict audit of clinical time versus delayed entry time. Hospital folds, arm support, repeated landmarks, and missingness/exit mechanisms have finite-support rules that force an inconclusive result when transport cannot be estimated.

## Time windows, parsing, and independent trajectory

For each landmark, use the exact parent decision time `D` and disjoint windows:

* pre-D trajectory `P=(D-1440,D-720]`;
* baseline `B=(D-720,D]`;
* post-label outcome `O=(D+360,D+1800]`;
* horizon sensitivity `O12=(D+360,D+1080]`.

The six-hour washout ensures records at label time do not define response. Windows use clinical offsets (`respchartoffset`, `respcarestatusoffset`, `observationoffset`), not `respchartentryoffset`. Equality follows the displayed open/closed boundaries; all boundary audits are machine checked.

Parse only finite numeric values after trimming the source representation. Apply fixed field-specific physiologic bounds declared in the compiler before reading label-stratified outcomes; out-of-bound, empty, nonnumeric, and ambiguous values are missing, with no cross-component imputation or unannounced unit conversion. For each mapped setting family (FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, set respiratory rate), retain valid values, count, unique clinical timestamps, first/last offset, median, maximum gap, and parse reason. A window component is observed only with at least two valid observations at distinct offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. Medians, rather than last values, define the window summaries; first/last and 60-minute separation are sensitivities.

The primary support domain excludes peak inspiratory pressure, because the parent audit found it dominated the earlier composite and its semantic status is uncertain. A support reduction is present if at least one family decreases from the B median to the O median by the fixed parent thresholds: FiO2 at least 0.05 absolute fraction, PEEP at least 2 cmH2O, pressure support at least 2 cmH2O, or set rate at least 2 breaths/min. The same fixed thresholds are applied in O12. The mapping is case-insensitive and whitespace-normalized only where that exact normalization is frozen in the source audit; unmatched labels remain missing.

Safety requires, among signals available in both B and O, median SaO2 not to fall by more than 2 percentage points and median respiratory rate not to rise by more than 4 breaths/min. A signal is available only under the same two-observation/separation rule. If either signal observed in both windows violates its threshold, safety fails. If a signal is absent in one window, it is not silently safe. The primary safety rule requires both signals available in both windows; a prespecified at-least-one-signal sensitivity is secondary. Respiratory values and airway fields are outcome inputs only after D and never enter baseline features when their timestamps are post-D.

Airway corroboration requires at least one O-window `respiratoryCare` row with nonmissing `airwaytype` or a nonmissing interval endpoint among `ventstartoffset`, `ventendoffset`, `priorventstartoffset`, and `priorventendoffset`. This is corroboration of structured chart availability, not proof of device delivery. The invasive-airway lexical rule and an interval-only sensitivity are frozen source-wide before arm counts. `No Artificial Airway` and treatment-string ETT-removal checks are secondary validation only and cannot override a missing primary source.

### Exactly one outcome state

Outcome assignment is a pure function of the frozen row, D, source rows in the windows, and the patient exit boundary, with this precedence:

1. **Competing exit** if ICU follow-up is truncated before `D+1800` for O (or before `D+1080` for O12), where `unitdischargeoffset` is nonmissing and `unitdischargeoffset < end(O)`; include death status and discharge/transfer status or location in subtypes. If the source ends before the required horizon without a valid discharge boundary, classify as administrative truncation, not ordinary non-improvement. A discharge at or before `D+360` is additionally flagged as no post-washout opportunity. Boundary equality at the horizon is retained as complete follow-up under the exact `< end` rule.
2. **Observed endpoint** if follow-up reaches the O end and the primary minimum observations and safety/airway availability requirements are met. Within this state, assign exactly one of `persistent_improvement` (support reduction and safety pass) or `observed_non_improvement` (no support reduction or safety failure). These categories are mutually exclusive.
3. **Endpoint missing** if follow-up reaches the O end but minimum observation, safety availability, or required airway corroboration fails. Missingness reasons are component/window-specific and include no valid numeric values, fewer than two separated records, absent vital, absent airway corroboration, parse failure, and invalid interval.

The same precedence is applied to O12, with a separate state column; no row may have more than one primary state. Death is never recoded as improvement or non-improvement. A transport or ICU discharge before the endpoint is an exit, even if a final setting suggests de-escalation. Discharge after the endpoint horizon is not an exit for that horizon. Report unitdischargestatus and unitdischargelocation subtypes, but do not infer post-ICU respiratory outcome.

The primary descriptive quantities are the joint four-state distribution over all 540 rows, arm-specific state probabilities, and the observation/exit contrasts. The complete-observation conditional risk difference is evaluation minus no-trial persistent-improvement risk among observed, non-competing endpoints; it is never presented without its all-row denominator. The principal predictive estimand models the four mutually exclusive states jointly using all rows. A binary complete-endpoint sensitivity uses inverse probability of observation weights estimated from pre-D variables only, with fixed truncation and positivity diagnostics; it cannot replace the joint estimand.

The label-only model is descriptive. For the primary hospital-transport analysis, hospital ID is a fold/stratum and **not a patient predictor**: preprocessing, imputation, scaling, regularization, and observation weights are fitted on training hospitals only. A hospital-specific intercept or target rate is never imported into a held-out hospital. Landmark may be a prespecified training covariate; if its encoding has no support in a fold, that fold's affected metric is unavailable rather than repaired by target encoding.

Use leave-one-hospital-out folds. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro multiclass log loss/Brier score for the four-state outcome, plus fold-specific calibration and state probabilities. Separately report metrics for the observed-endpoint subdistribution and the observation/competing-exit process, so a label gain in predicting missingness is not mislabeled as a patient-state gain. Within-hospital evaluation-versus-no-trial risk differences are reported only where both arms and at least 10 complete endpoint-observed rows per arm exist. The transport label-contrast estimand requires at least five hospitals meeting that support rule and at least 50% of complete observed rows from those hospitals; otherwise it is inconclusive. A held-out fold with one label arm contributes only to predeclared broad four-state prediction metrics, never to an arm contrast.

## Falsification and result gates

Run all tests before interpreting the label contrast.

* **Leakage/integrity stop:** verify primary endpoint code imports no care-plan columns after D, no treatment strings, no post-D opportunity into baseline, and no outcome-guided label mapping or thresholds. Any failure invalidates the computation.
* **Pre-D placebo:** classify P versus B with the identical thresholds, observation, safety, airway, and exit rules. A pre-D contrast comparable to post-D is adverse to a newly emerging label interpretation.
* **Lead-time placebo:** shift the model decision time to `D-720` while retaining the prespecified post-D window; a comparable label gain indicates pre-existing trajectory or timing leakage.
* **Copied-chart falsification:** repeat with 60-minute separation and first/last summaries. A material reversal indicates persistence/artifact sensitivity.
* **Component/source ablations:** remove each setting family, each vital, and airway corroboration one at a time; peak pressure may appear only as a labelled secondary ablation. A result surviving only one field, peak pressure, one source family, or one site is not supportive.
* **Opportunity negative control:** predict post-window source density and entry lag from pre-D variables and label. A label effect on documentation without a value-based four-state gain supports workflow opportunity rather than patient-state information.
* **Airway and hard-event sensitivity:** use the frozen interval-only airway rule and secondary exact treatment-string ETT-removal/reintubation candidates. Fewer than 10% observed in either arm, absent standardized terminology, or no independent adjudication makes this validation unavailable; it cannot turn a missing primary endpoint into non-improvement.
* **Exit/missingness sensitivity:** compare complete-case, joint four-state, and pre-D-only inverse-observation-weighted analyses. Extreme weights, nonpositivity, or arm-specific exit imbalance that changes the conclusion makes the result inconclusive, not clinically adverse by itself.

Call the result **computationally supportive** only if artifact integrity passes; at least five hospitals meet support; at least four of five estimable hospital directions agree; state-plus-label improves both micro and equal-hospital macro log loss by at least 0.01 over state-plus-opportunity in the primary and at least two frozen airway/horizon sensitivities; the stay-cluster interval excludes zero; the gain exceeds pre-D/lead-time placebo and valid permutation null; and it persists across source ablations without being explained by missingness, exits, one hospital, or documentation density. Even then, the claim is only incremental prediction of this eICU chart-derived trajectory.

Call the result **computationally adverse** only when endpoint support is adequate and a bounded falsification is reproduced: no label increment beyond pre-D state/opportunity, hospital-direction reversal, placebo/permutation explanation, one-source dependence, or documentation-only gain. This does not prove that an evaluation did not occur or that patients were harmed.

Call the result **inconclusive** for failed parent artifacts/hashes, fewer than five supported hospitals, no valid held-out arm contrast, sparse or nonpositive state/exit observations, unavailable airway corroboration, incompatible bounded definitions, or intervals spanning meaningful gain and no gain. Never relax the endpoint, collapse exits into missingness, impute absent charting as unchanged, pool row-bootstrap uncertainty, or expand label vocabulary post hoc.
