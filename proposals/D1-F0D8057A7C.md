# Support-ascertainment repair: observed-opportunity discordant AKI

Status: proposed substantive child of [prior hypothesis]; design only. No cohort scan, model fit, or scientific result was run.

## Change from the selected parent

The parent’s question is retained: after a six-hour lag, does creatinine recovery discordance with direct urine output and/or recorded input-minus-output predict short-term hospital death and ICU-recorded RRT? The audit changes the support interpretation and estimand in three consequential ways.

1. The local dictionary invalidates the inherited expected extubation ID 225795: it is “Dextran 40” in `icu/inputevents`. Extubation is `icu/procedureevents.itemid=227194`; `225792` is “Invasive Ventilation” and `225794` is “Non-invasive Ventilation.” The corrected definition is binding-critical.
2. A same-window support or “support-first” contrast is not a treatment comparison. Support can be triggered by the renal process, severity, or documentation intensity. It is therefore a prespecified temporal compatibility diagnostic, never a primary adjustment set, principal-stratum causal effect, or mechanism test.
3. The primary estimand is explicitly an observed-ICU prognostic estimand, with separate endpoint-specific risk sets and observed antecedent-support and measurement-opportunity strata. “No observed support” means no qualifying record, not no treatment. ICU-recorded RRT means only RRT recorded while in the retained ICU; it is not all-hospital RRT.

These are substantive repairs to estimand and decision criteria, not compiler fixes.

## Scientific opening and falsifiable claim

Creatinine recovery can be discordant with urine output and recorded balance. A leading explanation is that the discordance is a clinically useful prognostic marker of renal recovery not captured by creatinine alone. The strongest rival is a support/ascertainment process: observed vasopressors, ventilation, administered inputs, diuresis, renal testing, and ICU retention reflect severity and observation practice, while support can change urine and recorded balance. Missing ward RRT, treatment intent, insensible losses, muscle mass, and measured GFR prevent causal or mechanistic identification.

The evidence inspected supports only bounded premises. [K1] reports that creatinine-based eGFR can overestimate measured kidney function during prolonged critical illness as muscle is lost. [K2] shows that urine-output windows and persistence choices affect AKI classification and mortality prediction. [K3] reports AKI/mortality associations alongside vasoactive drugs, ventilation, and positive recorded balance, and states that KRT was discretionary and fluid balance omitted insensible losses. None establishes this MIMIC phenotype or a causal support effect.

Hypothesis:

> Among adults in a first eligible ICU stay who are alive, remain in that ICU, have no pre-landmark ICU-recorded RRT, and have a classifiable renal state at each landmark, the six-hour-lagged discordant recovery state has a reproducible seven-day prognostic contrast for hospital death and for ICU-recorded RRT versus concordant recovery; the contrast is not wholly reproduced by observed antecedent support history or by renal/input/output measurement opportunity.

The claim is noncausal. Supportive results establish only a reproducible association in the observed population and strata. They do not establish that renal dysfunction caused death, that support caused the phenotype, or that ICU-recorded RRT represents all RRT.

For recorded balance, use `icu/inputevents` intervals overlapping A_L. This is recorded balance, not total-body balance. Require valid input and urine opportunity for a balance state.

Define observed measurement opportunity before inspecting outcomes:

- `O_L=complete): renal criteria met (>=2 urine bins and valid creatinine contribution) and balance input/urine are unit-valid in the required interval;
- `O_L=partial/unknown): a renal or input/urine requirement is not met or has invalid/missing timing;

## Support history and endpoint-specific estimands

Define antecedent history `H_L=[t0,max(t0,Ltime-24h))), which is empty at L=24, and support interval `S_L=A_L). Use only valid intervals with `starttime<endtime), clipped to the retained ICU.

For H_L, define mutually exclusive observed support-history strata: no qualifying support record, one qualifying non-RRT class, multiple classes, and unknown/invalid timing. Qualifying classes are vasopressor, ventilation, administered-volume and diuresis. These are records of support/process, not treatment assignment. For L=24 the support-history comparison is structurally unavailable and must be labelled so.

For S_L, report support-first when the first qualifying support start precedes the first renal-defining observation, renal-first for the reverse, simultaneous/indeterminate for ties/missing times, and neither if no qualifying support record. This is a temporal compatibility diagnostic only. Positive balance ordering is indeterminate unless its contributing input interval starts before the first renal observation. Do not adjust for S_L, W_L, support recorded after Ltime, or any outcome-derived support state.

Primary estimand for each L and outcome is:

> The seven-day observed-ICU prognostic cumulative incidence difference between discordant and concordant renal states, standardized over the observed eligible risk set, separately for hospital death and ICU-recorded RRT, with descriptive estimates within predeclared H_L and O_L strata when those strata meet the sparse-cell rule.

For hospital death, causes are death and alive hospital discharge; alive hospitalized at seven days is administrative censoring. For the RRT endpoint, the risk set requires retained-ICU presence at Ltime; causes are first ICU-recorded RRT, alive ICU exit, and hospital death before RRT, with the earliest clock event winning. ICU-recorded RRT is not post-ICU or ward RRT. Report RRT only as an ascertainment-limited endpoint.

Estimate Aalen–Johansen seven-day cumulative incidences and risk differences, with six-hour cause-specific discrete hazards as an implementation check. Provide unadjusted and standardized estimates. Antecedent H_L may be included as a descriptive prognostic standardization block only for L>24; it is not a causal adjustment for support. No causal effect, mediation, principal-stratum effect or mechanism is estimated.

## Sparse-cell and interpretation rule

Freeze this rule before fitting:

- An overall discordant-versus-concordant estimate is eligible only if each state has at least 50 subjects and the endpoint has at least 10 total events across the comparison risk set.
- A component or H_L/O_L stratum estimate is eligible only if each compared state has at least 50 subjects and at least 5 endpoint events in that stratum. Otherwise output counts and exact event proportions only and mark the contrast “not estimable—sparse,” without pooling strata or changing landmarks.
- Any landmark with >40% unknown/partial opportunity, or a corrected-label/interval failure affecting >5% of support rows, is a feasibility warning; it does not justify post hoc outcome selection. If the primary state is too sparse at all landmarks, defer the renal-discordance claim and retain only the ascertainment audit.
- If uncertainty spans a clinically important range, call the result inconclusive, not adverse.

Supportive: an eligible contrast persists in at least two landmarks, in the complete-opportunity sensitivity, and is not materially reproduced by H_L support-only gradients or S_L ordering. This supports a reproducible prognostic association only.

Adverse: the contrast attenuates/disappears in complete-opportunity strata, is confined to support-first/documentation-dense records, appears only in the current-window sensitivity, reverses across landmarks, or depends on the former erroneous 225795 label. This favors support/measurement rivalry and weakens the renal interpretation. It does not prove normal renal function.

Inconclusive: sparse cells, large unknown opportunity, wide intervals, inconsistent event clocks, or endpoint-specific discordance without stable interpretation. A temporal model gain without a stable component-specific contrast is predictive information, not evidence of renal mechanism.

Computationally checkable: dictionary labels, joins, state construction, interval clipping, landmark/washout, risk sets, AJ counts, sparse rule, split integrity, calibration and held-out metrics. Clinical adjudication/another study is required for treatment intent, complete ward RRT, measured GFR, muscle mass, true fluid balance, goals of care, causal effects and transportability.

## Baseline and temporal alternative

Primary transparent baseline: fixed summaries from exactly A_L and H_L, with creatinine level/peak/recovery/slope, AKI stage, three-bin urine and masks, recorded input/output composition and balance, weight/CVP, support class/presence/duration, O_L and documentation counts. Fit separate six-hour cause-specific discrete-time competing-risk heads for hospital death/alive discharge and ICU-recorded RRT/ICU exit/death. No W_L or post-L input is visible.

Substantive same-question alternative: a temporal model on identical six-hour bins from t0 through Ltime-6h, with separate channels and missingness masks for creatinine, urine, input/output, weight/CVP, vasopressors, ventilation using corrected `227194) termination, administered volume, diuresis, RRT and observation counts. Use a causal-time mask to exclude W_L and post-L values. Separate endpoint heads predict the same causes. Auxiliary outputs may summarize support-first/renal-first/simultaneous/unknown and persistent/resolving discordance, but these are descriptive process labels, not mechanisms.

Fit both using a subject-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test split, all admissions from a subject in one split. Evaluate held-out endpoint-specific calibration, cumulative-incidence calibration, time-dependent Brier, cause-specific discrimination, support/opportunity-stratum calibration, and bootstrap intervals for risk contrasts. Selection is based on whether timing/persistence changes the renal-versus-ascertainment interpretation, not a small AUROC gain. The baseline is scientifically valuable because it exposes the contrast and strata; the temporal alternative can reveal whether persistence/order/transitions explain residual association that fixed summaries lose.
