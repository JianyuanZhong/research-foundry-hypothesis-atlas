# Lab-visibility-safe M2 triage: a lab-blind primary test of deployable radiology semantics

## Decision, evidence boundary, and falsifiable advance

The historical decision remains exactly the Episode-7 decision: 24 hours before a patient's first eligible liver resection, could a frozen interpretable strategy flag an adult in this institution's retrospectively pathology-confirmed HCC resection frame for multidisciplinary review because the resection specimen may show pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2)? A flag is a weighted classification output, not a treatment recommendation.

**Strongest claim supported before this experiment.** Direct catalog, schema, and CSV-header inspection establishes that the HCC snapshot contains operation start times, liver CT/MRI acquisition times and eventual report text, exact-assay laboratory values with a single field called `检验时间`, and untimed same-encounter pathology text. The laboratory header is exactly `患者主索引,就诊号,检验,定性结果,定量结果,标本类型,检验时间`. It contains no order/accession identifier, collection time, laboratory receipt time, result-entry time, verification/finalization time, release time, correction status, or clinician-view audit. The non-drug order table has order/start/end times but no key linking an order to a lab result. Untimed clinical documents cannot repair that link. Therefore `检验时间<t_dec` supports only "timestamp-eligible stored result," not "numeric value available to the decision maker."

A sequential, non-random audit of the first 200,000 laboratory rows found all sampled time strings had second-level length 19, 353 exact assay labels and 18 specimen labels; many patient-encounter-time groups contained multiple rows. This describes the sample actually read and motivates retaining exact labels, but it cannot establish collection semantics, co-release of rows, finalization, or visibility. Inherited full scans counted 338,040 procedure rows and 46,395 pathology rows and found preliminary, pre-adjudication anchored M2 counts of 75 in 2020 and 24 in 2021. Those are feasibility findings, not final prevalence or performance.

Current public work shows why this omission matters clinically. Huang et al. combined AFP positivity with CT margin irregularity to predict binary MVI in complete-case resection cohorts (487 training, 256 external validation; AUC 0.740 and 0.781) and described decision-curve utility as exploratory (DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034; inspected frozen full HTML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). It does not validate M2, routine report-text semantics, this dataset, or whether a stored AFP value was visible at a historical cutoff. Albu et al. emphasize that EHR extraction and feature engineering determine the reliability and real-world applicability of dynamic prediction models (DOI 10.2196/73987; inspected frozen full XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]). Neither paper supplies the missing source-system timestamps here.

**Primary unresolved clinical-value hypothesis (C0).** In the locked 2020–2021 whole target frame, a laboratory-blind deployable semantic model `S0` has at least 0.01 more net benefit than the laboratory-blind structured model `B0` at threshold 0.30, under worst-case patient-wise report-visibility and pathology-grade uncertainty. It must also have positive incremental value throughout 0.25–0.35 and beat flag-all and flag-none there.

**Separate unresolved attribution hypothesis (A0).** The identical radiology-semantic block adds positive net benefit beyond measured nonlaboratory workflow/provenance, comparing the nested `A0=W0+C_R` with `W0`, under the same uncertainty.

The substantive advance is not a stronger model. It is a decision-time-valid estimand: no primary score, missingness indicator, tuning stratum, calibration variable, or action depends on a laboratory row. Thus support is interpretable without pretending `检验时间` proves result visibility. This narrows the claim from "radiology plus stored laboratory semantics" to the clinically cleaner question of whether routine radiology report semantics add material historical M2-triage value. The original lab-containing models are retained only as non-identifying diagnostic scenarios and cannot rescue a failed primary claim.

## Frozen population, index, clocks, and endpoint

The unit is one patient (`患者主索引`) and one first eligible index resection. The following Episode-7 elements are frozen.

1. From `procedures`, select the earliest valid `开始时间` whose normalized exact `手术` is in the prespecified, clinically reviewed dictionary of partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resections. Exclude transplant, biopsy, puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and explicit repeat/recurrence operations. Break exact-time ties by `就诊号`, then normalized `手术`. Emit all raw included/excluded values and counts, invalid times, ties, and one-index-per-patient assertions before outcome access.
2. Require age at least 18 and same-encounter pathology text explicitly identifying HCC in the index encounter. This is a retrospective pathology-confirmed resection frame, not proof of preoperative HCC certainty, resectability, or curative intent. Missing grade, positive-ungraded MVI, conflict, or uncertain specimen linkage is `V=0`, never an exclusion.
3. Set `t_dec=procedures.开始时间-24 hours`. Records at or after `t_dec` cannot predict. Repeat labeled 12-, 48-, and 72-hour-buffer sensitivities, none replacing the primary.
4. Require at least one liver CT/MRI acquired in `[t_dec-90 days,t_dec)`, based only on valid `examinations.开始时间`. After exact-row deduplication, bundle by `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号,开始时间)`; a missing `检查号` creates a row-level bundle. Select at most the latest CT and latest MRI bundle by `开始时间`, then `检查号` and normalized `检查`. Acquisition does not prove report availability.
5. Preserve the no-recorded-prior-treatment primary cohort: exclude recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in procedures, medications, or non-drug orders during `[t_dec-365 days,t_dec)`, using frozen reviewed dictionaries and valid source times. Previously treated patients remain a labeled sensitivity. This is not biological treatment-naivety.
6. Fit in 2015–2018, select penalty and recalibrate in 2019, and test once in untouched 2020–2021, assigned by index operation date with patients confined to one period. Exclude 2013–2014. Scores after 2021 may describe drift and endpoint identifiability only, not contemporary accuracy.
7. Freeze dictionaries, parsing, state construction, feature maps, preprocessing, penalties, calibration, thresholds, bootstrap family, permutation rules, and gates before reading test outcomes.

The pathology-only endpoint is unchanged: `Y=1` only for trustworthy index-specimen M2; `Y=0` for M0/M1. `V=1` requires agreement of two independent position-aware parsers and the frozen adjudication process. Grade only the immediate selected value after an anchored MVI-grade field (or a frozen synonymous selected-value construction), stopping before definitions, explanatory text, historical clauses, and new specimen sections. An unanchored "M2," binary MVI, positive-ungraded MVI, absent language, boilerplate, or conflict cannot assign grade.

Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 sampled by year/template, and every test `V=0`; a third resolves disagreement. Preserve class-specific PPV ≥0.98, sensitivity ≥0.95, kappa ≥0.90, and parser disagreement ≤5%. Raw slides, specimen identifiers, block counts, vessel-distance measurements, and sampling protocol remain unavailable; text review cannot establish biological M2 missed by sampling.

## Shared report states and laboratory exclusion

For patient `i`, let `q_i` be the number (0–2) of selected CT/MRI bundles with nonempty eventual report text and `R_i={0,1}^{q_i}`. State 1 assumes that bundle's report was finalized and visible at `t_dec`; state 0 masks it. Empty text has state 0 only. No probability, independence, or monotonicity assumption is made.

At state 0, mask every report-derived item from that bundle: size, lesion count, macrovascular thrombus, report length/sections/template, semantic findings, and unrestricted text. Acquisition facts—valid acquisition time, modality from `检查`, acquisition/bundle counts, distinct-`检查号` count without exposing the identifier, and development-frequency machine-availability category—remain. Every compared model receives the same patient state. Development and 2019 recalibration use all feasible report states with each patient's total weight one, grouped by patient.

**Primary laboratory rule:** do not read `定性结果` or `定量结果` into any primary feature matrix. Also exclude lab-derived exact-assay/specimen indicators, missing/presence indicators, counts, recency, and any permutation stratum. The lab table is not used to define the primary population, endpoint, or report state. A timestamped lab row is not converted to an order-time feature. This is stronger than a 48-hour lag and remains valid even if every sampled `检验时间` is a collection or assay time rather than a release time.

An independent source-system audit may later permit a scientific child to add values only if each result row can be linked to an accession/order and a reliable final/release timestamp before `t_dec`; actual clinician exposure additionally requires access-log evidence. Manual belief, same-encounter presence, time distance, common turnaround, or a nearby order cannot narrow uncertainty here.

## Primary models and baselines

Fit all principal models as elastic-net logistic regressions on the same `V=1` 2015–2018 patients and report-state augmentation. Use a frozen alpha/penalty grid; choose the simplest setting within one patient-bootstrap standard error of minimum patient-weighted 2019 log loss, then estimate one weighted logistic recalibration intercept/slope in 2019 without refitting features.

- **B0, deployable structured baseline:** age, sex; CT/MRI modality and acquisition provenance; and, only in report-visible states, reviewed extraction of maximum lesion diameter, lesion count (1, 2, ≥3), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown indicators.
- **S0, deployable semantic strategy:** exactly B0 plus radiology-semantic block `C_R`: report-visible present/absent/unknown extraction from `检查所见` and `检查诊断` for complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity.
- **W0, workflow/provenance control:** exactly B0 plus nonlaboratory acquisition count and time since last acquisition, reviewed non-semantic protocol category from exact `检查`, machine availability and development-frequency category (never raw identifier), and, in report-visible states only, character/line/section counts and outcome-blind formatting family. Template families are built in 2015–2018 after masking clinical words, numbers, dates, identifiers, and values. W0 is not deployable.
- **A0, process-adjusted semantic model:** exactly W0 plus the identical `C_R` used in S0. Thus `A0-W0` is nested. It tests incremental modeled information conditional on measured workflow, not causality.
- **U0, diagnostic ceiling:** A0 plus report-visible character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after removing identifiers, dates, accession strings, pathology/MVI terms, and postoperative content. U0 cannot rescue either primary claim.

Enforce `B0⊂S0`, `B0⊂W0`, `W0⊂A0`, and byte-identical `C_R` construction in S0 and A0. Non-nested `S0-W0` is descriptive only.

Two blinded radiologists retain Episode-7 extraction review: at least 200 reports for size/count/thrombus and, separately in each test year and CT/MRI provenance, at least 150 reports enriched for each `C_R` feature and parser negatives. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in ≥90%; count-category agreement ≥90% and kappa ≥0.80; and per-year/per-modality PPV and sensitivity ≥0.85 for every binary semantic feature. This validates stored-text interpretation, not report finalization.

## Exact primary estimands

For threshold `p`, let `w=p/(1-p)`, `a_{Mi}(r,p)=1[score_{Mi}(r)≥p]`, and for contrast `k=(M_1,M_0)` define

`d_i^k(y,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)][a_{M1i}(r,p)-a_{M0i}(r,p)]`.

For `V_i=1`:
`L_k(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i min_{r∈R_i} d_i^k(Y_i,r,p)`,
`U_k(p)=N^{-1}Σ_i max_{r∈R_i} d_i^k(Y_i,r,p)`.

For `V_i=0`, extrema also range over `y∈{0,1}`. `N` is every 2020–2021 target-frame patient; no patient is dropped or imputed. With at most four report states and two endpoint states, direct enumeration is exact.

Compute bounds for primary `S0-B0` (C0), primary `A0-W0` (A0), descriptive `S0-W0`, `S0-U0`, and every principal model versus flag-none and flag-all. Use the same `r` within each patient/model contrast. Endpoints are coordinatewise sharp under unrestricted patient states; endpoints from different contrasts need not describe one joint latent world.

Primary threshold is 0.30. Evaluate 0.20–0.40 by 0.01; 0.25–0.35 is the frozen robustness plateau, not an elicited clinical preference. Report state-specific flags and incremental flags. At 0.30 decompose width into pathology ambiguity with all reports visible, report uncertainty among `V=1`, and the joint interval; report action-instability fractions.

Use 2,000 patient bootstraps stratified by calendar period, carrying all patient rows/states and refitting tuning and recalibration. The simultaneous family includes both L and U for `S0-B0`, `A0-W0`, `S0-W0`, and S0 versus both defaults at all 21 thresholds. Form one two-sided 95% maximum-absolute-centered-deviation band; also report pointwise intervals. More than 2.5% failed replicates fails inference.

For attribution, run 1,000 process-matched null repetitions, permuting the complete `C_R` block among development/tuning patients within frozen year, CT/MRI acquisition provenance, report-state cardinality, and formatting-family availability, with a frozen coarsening hierarchy for cells <20. Keep B0/W0, Y/V, patient groups, and state masks fixed; refit/recalibrate A0. A full patient semantic shuffle and label permutation must be compatible with zero. No lab-derived stratum is allowed.

Required sensitivities retain 12/48/72-hour decision buffers and the previously treated cohort. The inherited final-48-hour lab mask is retired because primary models contain no labs. Report 0.30 bounds by test year, sex, age <65/≥65, and CT-only/MRI-only/dual provenance; suppress inference for N<100 or <20 `V=1` M2 events.

## Non-primary stored-lab diagnostics

To quantify how the Episode-7 conclusion could have depended on unverified lab values, replay its original B/S/W/A definitions. For diagnostics only, a row is timestamp-eligible when valid `检验时间` is in `[t_dec-30 days,t_dec)`; select the last exact `(检验,标本类型)` row, using source order only for exact-time ties. Evaluate two explicitly non-identifying scenarios: (1) all selected timestamp-eligible stored results exposed and (2) every laboratory value and every lab-derived indicator masked. Also report the inherited final-48-hour masking scenario. These are scenario analyses, not bounds on partial visibility and not evidence that either state occurred. They never enter the simultaneous primary family, never gate C0/A0, and cannot be called deployable performance.

For any displayed stored-lab model, preserve exact `检验` and `标本类型`; never pool apparent synonyms, infer units, apply external cutoffs, or link to non-drug orders. Emit the exact assay dictionary, invalid times, ties, duplicates, and selected-row provenance. A larger all-exposed score than S0 is only evidence that eventual stored values carry retrospective information.

## Gates and result interpretations

Global interpretation requires test N≥1,000, adjudicated `V=1` M2≥75, `V=1`≥85%, review of every test `V=0`, pathology/radiology reliability gates, identical patients and report states across models, no lab-derived primary feature, no post-cutoff feature, report text nonempty in ≥80%, B0 size/count jointly extractable in ≥70%, no required primary variable shifting >15 percentage points from 2019 to either test year, acceptable drift reporting, S0 recalibration slope 0.80–1.20/intercept -0.10–0.10 and sensitivity ≥0.70/PPV ≥0.50 at 0.30 in both all-report and no-report `V=1` diagnostics, complete bootstrap output, and exact-bound unit tests. Claim A0 additionally requires valid workflow masks, adequate terminal permutation blocks, and estimable null repetitions.

**C0 supportive:** gates pass; the simultaneous lower confidence limit for `L_{S0-B0}(0.30)` is >0.01; it is >0 throughout 0.25–0.35; S0's lower-bound net benefit exceeds flag-all and flag-none throughout that plateau; and neither test year's point lower bound is negative. Strongest conclusion: laboratory-blind routine radiology semantics have material, threshold-robust historical weighted M2-classification increment over B0 in this institutional frame under report/outcome uncertainty.

**C0 adverse:** with gates passed, the simultaneous upper confidence limit for `U_{S0-B0}(0.30)` is <0.01, ruling out the prespecified material increment; <0 indicates harm. Failure to beat a default at a threshold is adverse there. All-stored-lab or all-report success cannot rescue this.

**C0 inconclusive:** gates fail or the simultaneous band spans 0.01. A positive point estimate with a confidence limit below the margin is inconclusive, not support.

**A0 supportive:** A0 gates pass; the simultaneous lower confidence limit for `L_{A0-W0}(p)` is >0 for every p=0.25–0.35 and the observed 0.30 lower bound exceeds the matched-null 97.5th percentile. Strongest conclusion: `C_R` adds weighted M2-classification information beyond measured nonlaboratory workflow/provenance. This is not causal.

**A0 adverse:** gates pass but a simultaneous upper limit is ≤0 at any required plateau threshold or the matched-null criterion fails. **A0 inconclusive:** attribution reliability, overlap, permutation, or inference gates fail, or bands span zero.

C0 and A0 are interpreted independently. C0 support with A0 failure supports deployable historical increment but not semantic-versus-workflow attribution. A0 support with C0 failure supports conditional information but not material deployable value. Negative findings are valid and clinically useful because they prevent deployment claims based on retrospective lab leakage.

## Compiler and verifier contract

The compiler must preserve the population, first-resection rule, no-recorded-treatment cohort, `t_dec`, 90-day imaging window, temporal split, pathology M2 endpoint/V rules, all-target denominator, shared report states, state augmentation, B0/S0/W0/A0 nesting, C0/A0 separation, descriptive S0-W0, thresholds, per-patient extrema, inference family, permutation plan, gates, and result zones. Adding any lab-derived primary feature or changing population, clocks, estimand, endpoint, analysis, margin, or falsification criteria requires a scientific child.

Required artifacts include source hashes and literal headers; dictionaries/frequencies; attrition/join multiplicity; index and `t_dec`; selected/discarded image bundles; each report state and masked vector; Y/V provenance; B0/S0/W0/A0/U0 scores/actions; an automated assertion that no primary design-matrix column has lab lineage; per-patient contributions; width decomposition; bootstrap bands; null outputs; scenario outputs; and machine-readable conclusion links.

Computationally checkable claims include source/header agreement, deduplication/joins, temporal splits/windows, one index per patient, report masking and shared states, zero lab lineage in primary matrices/strata, no leakage, model nesting and identical C_R, V=0 enumeration, whole-frame N, exact bound arithmetic, simultaneous critical value, permutation blocking, suppression, and whether reported conclusions match zones. The verifier must accept correct supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations.

Reject: treating `检验时间<t_dec` or a 48-hour lag as proof of a visible numeric result; feeding lab values/presence into primary scores; calling all-stored-results diagnostics deployable or bounded; all-report support presented as decision-time support; complete-case replacement; different states by model; coordinatewise endpoints called one latent world; point estimates called support when simultaneous limits miss margins; pointwise intervals called simultaneous; S0-W0 used as a gate; A0-W0 called causal; W0/A0 called deployable; V=0 coded M0; threshold cherry-picking; discrimination rescuing adverse net benefit; or treatment/patient-benefit claims.

Not automatically checkable are Chinese clinical semantics, reviewer qualifications, actual report or lab finalization/visibility, specimen linkage, tissue adequacy, the clinical acceptability of threshold 0.30, clinician response, or patient benefit. A lab-augmented deployment claim requires row-linked collection/receipt/final/release/correction timestamps and preferably clinician access logs, plus a new external/prospective validation. The present exclusion does not establish that historical values were unavailable; it establishes only that this snapshot cannot identify their availability. Even dual support cannot establish treatment selection, recurrence, survival, quality of life, harms, workload, or cost-effectiveness.
