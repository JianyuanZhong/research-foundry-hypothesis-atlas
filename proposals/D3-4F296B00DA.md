## Falsifiable test

Compare treatment=none FLS-only, MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage co-culture, and MerTK/CD206-positive macrophage co-culture using donor-aware contrasts on the exact 33 samples and 445 features. Use a transparent predeclared baseline (for example, donor/context-adjusted condition contrasts with permutation uncertainty) and a substantive hierarchical alternative that borrows strength across features while retaining donor and context uncertainty. Predeclare a positive result as a reproducible condition-specific response with uncertainty compatible with the prespecified effect and a negative control pattern that rejects donor/treatment/assay explanations. An adverse result is a null or opposite response, or evidence that the apparent response is explained by the donor conflict, composition, treatment, or assay structure. An inconclusive result is imprecision, insufficient independent donor replication, or failure of the negative controls to distinguish the explanations.

Supportive results would justify a follow-up perturbational or spatial experiment; adverse results would weaken the proposed communication hypothesis; inconclusive results would identify the missing replication or measurement needed. None would establish clinical benefit.
