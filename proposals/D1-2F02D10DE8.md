# Episode 93 successor: can a source-only prognostic review score cross practice strata without local outcome labels?

## Unresolved question and clinical decision

An ICU receiving a prognostic review score from another practice environment must decide whether it can use that score to fill a constrained mortality-review queue and display its absolute risks, or whether it must first obtain local outcome labels and rebuild or recalibrate the model. The two selected parents leave complementary gaps. The calibration parent compares a source-only intercept with a local-label intercept but does not test whether patient ordering remains adequate for a fixed review capacity. The transfer parent tests ranking against a process-only comparator but does not make a same-score, local-label deployment reference the primary decision.

This successor makes the deployment boundary explicit:

> In each direction between the two outcome-blind EICU repeat-lactate practice strata, can the source-only full review score (i) retain target-hospital event yield within 0.02 D7 deaths per review slot of a target-stratum, hospital-held-out fit of the same full score, and (ii) satisfy a prespecified target absolute-calibration screen, with no Dinf tradeoff?

This is a conjunctive deployment estimand, not an additive score. Calibration answers whether the displayed risk can be interpreted as an absolute risk; fixed-capacity yield answers whether the score can prioritize scarce review slots. Both are necessary for the proposed use, and neither is allowed to compensate for the other. The local-label model is a historical benchmark, not a prospective intervention and not evidence that local labels are practically available.

A supportive result would justify only a narrow computational portability statement: in this EICU cohort, a score trained in the opposite outcome-blind measurement-practice stratum was not materially worse than a hospital-held-out local fit for the specified review task while meeting the specified calibration screen. It would not show patient benefit or that an ICU should order repeat lactate. An adverse result would identify a need for local validation/recalibration before this type of cross-practice deployment; it would not show that the score has no prognostic association. Intervals crossing a decision margin are inconclusive, not equivalence.

## Primary hypothesis, estimands, and decision margins

Let A and B be the low- and high-repeat-availability hospital strata, assigned before looking at outcomes. Analyze both directed transfers, A→B and B→A. Let d index a direction and let C={0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.125, 0.15, 0.175, 0.20}.

For each target hospital h with n_h eligible people, set k_h(c)=ceil(c n_h), and K_d(c)=sum_h k_h(c) over target hospitals in direction d. Rank within each target hospital by the raw linear predictor, descending, breaking ties by ascending patientunitstayid. Let Q^tr_h(c) be the portable source-only full-score queue and Q^loc_h(c) the queue from the target-stratum local-reference full score, both with exactly k_h(c) people.

The primary fixed-capacity estimand is the paired target event-yield contrast

Delta^7_{d,c} =
  [sum_h sum_{i in Q^tr_h(c)} D7_i -
   sum_h sum_{i in Q^loc_h(c)} D7_i] / K_d(c),

and tau^7=min_{d,c} Delta^7_{d,c}. The source-only score is considered non-inferior for review prioritization only if the two-sided copied-hospital 95% interval's lower endpoint for tau^7 is at least -0.02. Thus the portable queue may be at most two D7 deaths per 100 occupied review slots worse than a locally trained, held-out reference in the worst direction and capacity. The -0.02 margin is an operational, prespecified non-inferiority margin, not a validated utility threshold.

Repeat the same estimand with Dinf, yielding tau^inf. This is a guard against a D7 result being obtained by losing eventual in-hospital deaths. A supportive deployment state requires the lower endpoint for tau^inf to be at least -0.02 as well. D7 remains the primary outcome horizon: D7 is source-defined in-hospital death by H7, not post-discharge mortality.

For portable calibration, let p^tr_i=expit(eta^tr_i+delta_A) where eta^tr is the source-stratum R_full linear predictor and delta_A is estimated only from source-stratum hospital-held-out D7 labels. In each direction and for each F_available status s∈{0,1} and z^tr_i=1[p^tr_i>=0.10], define

E^tr_d(s,z)=mean(D7-p^tr | target direction d, F_available=s, z^tr=z)
and q^tr_d=max_{s,z}|E^tr_d(s,z)|,
q^tr_*=max_d q^tr_d.

The calibration screen is upper endpoint(q^tr_*) <= 0.05, with at least 30 original target people in each of the eight direction-specific portable threshold cells. The 0.05 bound is an analytic safety screen, not a clinical guideline or a claim that 10% is an action threshold. Report calibration-in-the-large, Brier score, and slope descriptively; none replaces q^tr_*.

Primary hypothesis H93 is the conjunction:

1. lower 95% endpoint(tau^7) >= -0.02;
2. lower 95% endpoint(tau^inf) >= -0.02;
3. upper 95% endpoint(q^tr_*) <= 0.05;
4. all cohort, support, leakage, convergence, deletion, and finite-replicate gates pass in both transfer directions.

This is a single deployment decision with two co-primary dimensions. There is no weighted combination of deaths-per-slot and absolute-risk error.

## Directed portable and local-reference fits

The primary portability claim is intentionally stronger than pooled calibration: queue performance is evaluated by paired, same-patient target-hospital queues against the same-score held-out local reference, while calibration is evaluated in all eight target cells. The two dimensions are conjunctive and are never averaged into a composite.

For direction A→B, fit R_full and R_process on all source-stratum A hospitals only, and score only target-stratum B hospitals; reverse for B→A. Source preprocessing and all source model parameters are frozen before target scoring.

The portable calibration offset delta_A is estimated from source hospitals only. For each source hospital g, fit the complete source pipeline on all other source hospitals, score g, pool all source out-of-fold eta values, and solve by deterministic bisection on [-20,20], tolerance 1e-12, for

sum_i[D7_i-expit(eta_i+delta_A)] - delta_A = 0.

Refit R_full on all source hospitals, score the target, and apply delta_A. No target outcome enters eta^tr, delta_A, or any portable preprocessing. The portable queue uses eta^tr; the intercept cannot change its order.

For the local reference, in each target hospital h fit the identical R_full pipeline using only target-stratum hospitals other than h, including target-stratum preprocessing estimated without h. Score h to obtain eta^loc_h and p^loc_h=expit(eta^loc_h). Thus every local-reference target patient is held out from the labels and preprocessing that create the comparator score. The local reference may use target outcomes from other hospitals; it is deliberately an oracle-like historical benchmark and is never allowed to enter the portable fit. Empty local training sets, wrong hospital exclusion, or target-label leakage into the portable path are not-computable.

For descriptive diagnosis only, retain the inherited source-only R_process queues and report portable R_full minus portable R_process at every c and with Dinf. This can show whether failure is specific to the optional repeat feature, but it cannot rescue H93 or replace the local-reference estimand.

## Falsification and deterministic result states

First classify **not computable** for any source/snapshot/hash/header/schema/member mismatch; failure of the exact cohort or stratum audit; altered clocks, revision/conflict/tie rules, features, folds, capacities, or outcome definitions; post-L24 leakage; target outcomes entering the portable path; held-out target labels entering a local target score; nonfinite fits/roots/scores; wrong queue cardinality or tie handling; invalid copied-hospital exclusion; missing outputs; fewer than 1,900 finite primary replicates; or failed required calibration-cell support.

Among computable results:

1. **Adverse calibration non-portability:** the lower 95% endpoint of q^tr_* is >0.05.
2. **Adverse ranking non-portability:** the upper 95% endpoint of tau^7 is <-0.02.
3. **Adverse eventual-horizon tradeoff:** D7 ranking and calibration pass, but the upper 95% endpoint of tau^inf is <-0.02.
4. **Supportive cross-practice deployment screen:** both directed transfers pass the D7 and Dinf non-inferiority bounds, q^tr_* passes the calibration bound, deletion stability passes, all cells/support gates pass, and no adverse state applies.
5. **Inconclusive:** every other computable pattern, including an interval crossing any margin, one direction passing while the other is unresolved, deletion instability, or a favorable R_process comparison with failed local-reference portability.

An adverse result falsifies only the specified portable-deployment claim. It does not establish that lactate is non-prognostic, that repeat measurement is useless, or that local recalibration will work prospectively. An inconclusive result is not equivalence. Support is conditional on the fixed cohort, horizon, capacities, margins, and score definition.
