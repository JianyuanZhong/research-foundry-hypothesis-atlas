# Fail-closed certified baseline-only UKB renal-marker discordance experiment

## Lineage, unresolved question, and substantive advance

This is a targeted, substantive successor of `[prior hypothesis]`, which is the assigned repairable parent. It preserves the parent’s clinically important baseline-only, observed-schedule question, population logic, 2–8-year interval, deterministic cross-fitting, corrected model dimensions, and narrow noncausal evidence boundary. It does not treat any inherited aggregate count as a result of this version.

The falsifiable question is:

> **Only after an explicit, frozen field-and-equation certificate authorizes scientific interpretation**, among UKB participants with complete certified baseline markers, baseline equation-derived creatinine eGFR at least 60, and complete certified exported instance-1 observations dated 2–8 years after baseline, does adding baseline continuous cystatin-C/creatinine eGFR discordance improve out-of-fold prioritization of the repeat equation-derived creatinine-eGFR state below 60 beyond sex, age, and baseline creatinine eGFR?

The clinical importance is bounded: a positive result would show incremental prioritization information for a constrained observed follow-up schedule, not that testing or treatment improves outcomes. The substantive advance over the parent is executable fail-closed semantics. A missing, incomplete, contradictory, inferred-only, or otherwise unaccepted field/equation certificate now causes a **hard neutral stop** before any named biological quantity, outcome, cohort, model prediction, capacity result, or null result is produced. No assumption-conditioned biological or model outputs are permitted. The capacity estimand is also made dimensionally explicit: `C_m(q)` is a selected-event **count**, while event fraction and PPV are separate dimensionless summaries; every count gate is in events, not fractions.

The strongest claim presently supported by the available evidence is only that the split-table schemas contain the required field identifiers and that a baseline-only comparison can be specified. The local metadata do not itself establish renal-field units, assay/matrix, missing-code meanings, date representation, or instance chronology. Published equation evidence does not establish those UKB export semantics. The proposed experiment tests incremental out-of-fold prioritization only after those facts are explicitly certified. It cannot establish CKD, persistent disease, measured GFR, treatment benefit, testing benefit, causal utility, cost-effectiveness, or transportability.

`C_m(q) = sum_{i in selected_m(q)} Ycr_i`,

which has units **selected events (a count)**. Define `DeltaC(q)=C_1(q)-C_0(q)` in selected events, not an event fraction. The primary capacity region is contiguous 5–15%, with q=10% highlighted.
