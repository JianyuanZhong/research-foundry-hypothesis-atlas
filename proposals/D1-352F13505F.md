# Prospective zero-shot separation of patient-responsive laboratory observation signal from hospital templates

## Status and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis] and a fully specified experiment, not an executed result. It preserves the parent's audited eICU cohort, 24-hour landmark, seven-day in-hospital mortality outcome, 14-analyte panel, six read-only bindings, ten whole-hospital folds, target-standardized estimand, corrected handling of unknown discharge status, and artifact, end-of-life (EOL), severity, and disposition checks.

The parent established an attribution design but estimated an unseen hospital embedding from that hospital's unlabeled first-day masks. That transductive diagnostic cannot answer the prospective decision here: can an observation-aware predictor be deployed at a new hospital while retaining patient-responsive signal and suppressing hospital-template shortcuts, without target outcomes, target-hospital fitting, or normalization over the target cohort?

The future solver must newly fit, inside each frozen outer fold: (1) the parent's value-only B-V and raw observation-aware B-VO elastic-net mortality models; (2) a simple source-fitted zero-shot residualization model ZR; and (3) an outcome-blind hierarchical recurrent invariant representation HINV followed by the same elastic-net mortality head. All target predictions must be computable one patient at a time from source-frozen parameters. Completion requires frozen cohort and split manifests, cross-fitted representations, four fitted mortality models, target predictions, representation and leakage diagnostics, source-derived stress tests, target-standardized transport estimates, uncertainty, gate results, and machine-readable conclusions. Confirmation is not required.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and clinical importance

The strongest evidence supports only that laboratory presence, frequency, and timing can contain outcome-associated healthcare-process information beyond values [K1]; eICU mortality-model discrimination and calibration can vary across hospitals [K2]; and recurrent models can exploit masks and elapsed times in irregular clinical sequences [K3]. The parent's audit additionally supports feasibility: 69 hospitals, 81,176 eligible stays, 80,581 labeled stays, 4,897 seven-day deaths, and 1,572,519 available numeric panel rows. None of this shows that a patient-responsive observation component can be separated prospectively from unseen-hospital conventions.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among adults still in the ICU and hospital at 24 hours, a source-only representation can preserve mortality-relevant laboratory observation history that responds to recorded patient state while reducing the excess log-loss transport penalty carried by source-hospital template directions, with no target-hospital outcome, aggregate normalization, embedding fit, recalibration, or threshold selection.

The leading rival is that the useful mask/count/gap signal and site template are not separable from available eICU data: removing source-hospital information may also remove clinician-concern/severity signal, while an unseen target template may lie outside the source template span. Artifact, disposition, EOL selection, or unmeasured severity could also create apparent success. Resolution informs a consequential deployment choice: proceed to silent prospective validation of an observation-aware zero-shot model, retain the safer value-only model, or require site-specific outcome-based recalibration in a separate study. This remains predictive model-safety evidence, not a causal claim about testing policy.

## Common outcome models

Fit on identical supervised rows and splits:

- B-V: the parent's elastic-net logistic model on common covariates and value summaries/trajectories, with no explicit process indicator.
- B-VO: the identical model plus raw process features.
- ZR: the identical B-V block plus the simple zero-shot residualized process representation below.
- HINV: the identical B-V block plus the learned shared invariant representation below.

For all four, search elastic-net mixing in {0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1} and C in {0.01,0.1,1,10,100}; ties choose stronger penalty. Inverse-frequency weights may be used only during optimization; evaluate unweighted probabilities. Fit source-calibration logistic intercept/slope recalibration only. ZR and HINV use the same outcome head so their difference tests representation, not mortality-model capacity.

## Simple zero-shot residualization baseline

Let O_i be the source-standardized explicit process vector and S_i the common covariates plus value summaries/trajectories; neither contains outcome, hospital descriptor, interface, revision delay, or discharge information. Inside each outer fold, cross-fit a multi-output ridge decomposition on source development rows:

O_i = B S_i + c_{h(i)} + epsilon_i,

where c_h is a source-hospital fixed-effect vector. Search ridge penalties {0.1,1,10,100} by source-calibration process reconstruction error. Stack c_h as rows of C, center them by source hospital size, take the weighted SVD of C, and let V_r contain its first r feature-space right-singular vectors. For a new patient, compute without hospital identity or cohort statistics:

R_ZR(i) = B S_i + (I-V_r V_r^T)(O_i-B S_i).

Thus the source-learned patient-state expectation is retained, while residual variation aligned with source-hospital templates is projected out. The target hospital is never centered or normalized. Training-row representations are cross-fitted so a stay does not construct its own nuisance prediction.

Choose r from {1,2,4,8,16} without mortality outcomes: use a source-development multinomial hospital classifier conditioned on S and select the smallest r for which, on source calibration, adding R_ZR to S improves hospital cross-entropy by <=0.01 nat/stay and macro one-vs-rest AUROC by <=0.02 over S alone. Also require R_ZR to improve held-out reconstruction of process features over analyte/bin marginals. If no rank passes both suppression and retention gates, ZR is inadmissible; do not choose r from target performance.

This transparent baseline can remove only site directions spanned by source hospitals and linear in the engineered process vector. It may discard genuine severity/concern signal or miss a novel target convention.

## Learned hierarchical invariant alternative

Fit an outcome-blind discrete-time model for each analyte a and bin t on source development masks:

logit P(M_iat=1 | past_i,t,h) = alpha_at + f_theta(V_i,<t,X_i)_a + g_phi(O_i,<t)_a + lambda_a^T z_h + kappa_t^T z_h.

V contains only canonical values/abnormality distances available before t plus common covariates X; O contains prior masks, cumulative counts, and elapsed times. f and g are separate one-layer 32-unit GRUs. The four-dimensional shrinkage-penalized z_h and analyte/time loadings are a source-hospital template branch. Mortality labels never enter this likelihood.

The shared patient representation R_HINV is the sequence of alpha, f, and g outputs and final shared hidden states; z_h and its loadings are never passed to the mortality head. Add a conditional adversary q_psi(h|R_HINV,S) trained to classify source hospital. Alternate adversary cross-entropy minimization with encoder minimization of mask negative log likelihood minus lambda_adv times adversary cross-entropy. This asks the shared branch to retain patient-responsive next-mask information while making source hospital no more predictable beyond S; the hierarchical site branch can absorb template signal.

Use Adam, batch size 256, learning rate {0.0003,0.001}, site L2 {0.001,0.01,0.1}, lambda_adv {0,0.01,0.1,1}, at most 100 epochs, patience 10, and five fixed seeds. Tune only on source calibration. An admissible setting must: (1) beat the analyte-by-bin marginal mask model in held-out source-hospital log loss with no material calibration worsening; (2) have mask log loss within one standard error of the best non-adversarial hierarchical fit; and (3) satisfy the same conditional site-leakage thresholds as ZR. Among admissible settings choose the smallest mask log loss; ties choose lower site leakage.

At a target hospital, freeze every parameter and set z=0. Compute R_HINV sequentially for one patient from that patient's own prior values/masks. Do not estimate a target embedding, update batch normalization, pool target patients, or use target outcomes. Report target mask log loss/calibration only as post-freeze evaluation. Optimization failure is inconclusive; reproducible inability across five seeds to satisfy mask-retention and site-suppression gates is evidence adverse to separability.

HINV can preserve nonlinear value-triggered remeasurement and long-range observation memory that ZR's linear projection loses. Its adversarial/hierarchical separation is assumption-dependent, so ZR agreement strengthens the result and an admissible discordance is inconclusive.

## Estimands, stress tests, and uncertainty

Primary metric is log loss. For model m in {B-VO,ZR,HINV} and target hospital h define:

Delta_m^E(h)=LL_m^E(h)-LL_BV^E(h),
Delta_m^I(h)=LL_m^(I standardized to h)-LL_BV^(I standardized to h),
T_m(h)=Delta_m^E(h)-Delta_m^I(h).

Negative Delta means observation information improves on B-V. Positive T means its incremental value degrades at the hospital boundary. Define prospective shortcut attenuation S_m(h)=T_BVO(h)-T_m(h); positive S means mitigation reduces raw B-VO excess transport loss. The primary model is HINV; ZR is the transparent comparator. Report hospital medians and size-weighted summaries, but the prespecified primary summary is the median across adequate-overlap hospitals.

Two source-derived, outcome-blind stress tests use only templates/bases fitted in source development and fixed common random numbers; they never estimate a target template:

1. Template-direction stress: add or substitute each source-hospital C/template direction in a target patient's process vector while holding values and covariates fixed. Report absolute logit change and log-loss change for B-VO, ZR, and HINV. This is a reliance diagnostic, not a realistic intervention.
2. Patient-response ablation: replace B S in ZR or the shared f/g history in HINV by its source-stratum mean while leaving the orthogonal residual and values fixed. Worsened external loss indicates use of the designated patient-responsive component; it does not identify clinician concern.

Also report raw and residual conditional hospital leakage, source and target next-mask log loss/Brier/calibration, AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, mortality calibration intercept/slope and O/E, net benefit at 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%, alerts per 100, and deaths captured.

Use 2,000 paired hierarchical bootstrap replicates, resampling hospitals then uniquepid within hospital and retaining all stays. Keep model, imputation, and stress-test comparisons paired. Report five-seed variability and stress-test Monte Carlo error separately. Control false discovery rate only for hospital-specific intervals; do not multiplicity-adjust the prespecified pooled estimands by selecting a favorable method.

## Fixed artifact, EOL, severity, and disposition gates

Artifact gate: rebuild process inputs under (a) primary conservative revised-offset availability, (b) exact stay/name/offset/value duplicate collapse, (c) one event per analyte per 15-minute batch, and (d) restriction to clinically harmonized system-unit/analyte cells represented in >=90% of source hospitals. Result-offset-only is a timing sensitivity, not the robust primary clock. Support requires the primary direction for HINV retention and S_HINV in all three robust variants, at least two variant intervals excluding the adverse direction, and no precise reversal. If effects disappear under robust encodings or track revision/interface signatures more strongly than source-template stress, a hospital-practice interpretation is adverse and documentation artifact is favored.

EOL/severity gate: repeat after excluding the 461 audited stays with a cpleoldiscussionoffset or cpleolsaveoffset in 0-1440; then in the 72,552 IVa-linked and 79,013 APS-variable-linked subsets add the fixed APACHE scores and nonlaboratory support proxies. Point-estimate directions must persist and no sensitivity may precisely reverse them. These checks bound recorded severity/EOL only; absent documentation and untimed APACHE fields cannot adjudicate intent.

Disposition gate: fit the same information contrasts to the four-state day-7 outcome with multinomial log loss and state-specific calibration. A separation claim requires the upper 95% bound for HINV minus B-V death-state log loss to be <=0 and cannot rest solely on predicting alive ICU/hospital discharge. A precise death-state harm, benefit confined to discharge states, or marked EOL attenuation is adverse to mortality-specific retention. Never condition on future discharge.

Also require value-only mask reconstruction, unit-map approval, hospital overlap, no single-hospital sign determination, and no interface/revision extreme-site dominance. Failure of a clinical unit map is a readiness block, not evidence for or against the hypothesis.

## Decision rules and falsification

Support for prospective separation requires all of:

1. Raw B-VO has source-internal utility (95% interval for median Delta_BVO^I<0) and excess transport fragility (95% interval for median T_BVO>0).
2. HINV is admissible and retains patient-responsive utility: 95% intervals for median Delta_HINV^I and median Delta_HINV^E are both <0, and patient-response ablation worsens external loss.
3. HINV attenuates the shortcut: the 95% interval for median S_HINV is >0, median T_HINV is not positive with its upper 95% bound <=0, and source-template stress changes HINV logits less than B-VO logits.
4. ZR is admissible and directionally agrees. If HINV supports but admissible ZR precisely fails, conclude that separation is method-dependent/inconclusive, not that complexity rescued the claim. If ZR is inadmissible because the linear representation underfits while HINV passes all gates, support is limited to the learned representation.
5. Artifact, EOL/severity, disposition, overlap, leakage, mask-retention, calibration, and no-single-hospital gates pass. Evidence must include calibration or net-benefit consequences, not only AUROC.

This supports only that a source-fitted representation retained predictive observation information and reduced measured hospital-boundary fragility in this historical eICU experiment.

Evidence adverse to separation includes precise loss of all ZR and HINV incremental utility after site suppression; persistent T_m>0 or S_m<=0 despite admissible representations; template-stress sensitivity equal to B-VO; inability of both methods to satisfy retention and leakage gates reproducibly; precise HINV harm relative to B-V; or artifact/disposition/EOL results explaining the apparent gain. A precise T_BVO<=0 is adverse to the premise that mitigation is needed, although it is reassuring for raw transport.

Results are inconclusive when intervals span supportive and adverse regions, target standardization has poor overlap, target events are sparse, five seeds disagree, optimization fails, only one method is admissible with contradictory evidence, target templates lie outside source support, or robust sensitivities are imprecise. An imprecise null does not establish invariance.

Abandon a zero-shot observation channel for this cohort if both admissible methods precisely lose utility or fail to reduce transport fragility across robust encodings. Retain B-V and investigate richer bedside/order-intent data. Abandon the shortcut-removal premise if B-VO already transports without excess loss. Continue to contemporary silent prospective validation only after supportive results; deployment thresholds, fairness, and safety require clinical review and another study.

## Method exploration, resources, and deferred alternatives

The actual new deliverable is the fitted ZR and HINV representations and their source-frozen target predictions, not a small AUROC gain. ZR is selected as the simple baseline because its projection and failure mode are auditable. HINV is selected because next-mask supervision can preserve nonlinear, time-ordered patient response while the hierarchy/adversary assigns source-site variation to a branch that is unavailable at target deployment. Both use the same inputs, splits, mortality target, and outcome head.

Deferred alternatives:

- Raw B-VO remains the positive reliance comparator, not a mitigation.
- The parent's transductive target embedding/normalization remains an attribution sensitivity only and is forbidden from prospective predictions.
- Distributionally robust mortality training without observation reconstruction could suppress hospitals by discarding all observation information; revisit if HINV optimization fails but a prespecified representation target is added.
- A domain-generalized GRU-D mortality model confounds outcome capacity with separation; revisit only after the matched outcome-head experiment shows underfit.
- A joint latent physiology/order-intent point process is deferred because order time/reason, staffing, local protocols, and treatments needed for identification are unavailable.
- Outcome-based target recalibration is a different, later study.

## Noncausal and availability limits

Even a supportive result does not establish that testing policy causes mortality, that tests are unnecessary, or that changing ordering improves care. “Patient-responsive” means predictable from recorded past values/covariates/history; it may contain unmeasured severity, treatment, concern, protocol, staffing, or error. “Hospital template” means source-hospital-predictive process direction, not identified policy or intent. eICU reflects 2014-2015 participating sites in one tele-ICU ecosystem; lab result time is not order time; revised time is a conservative proxy; post-discharge death, complete goals of care, staffing, order reason, and local protocol are unavailable.
