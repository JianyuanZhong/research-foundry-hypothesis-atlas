# Hypotension Duration and AKI: Working Draft

## Question
At the same minimum MAP, does longer duration of hypotension (MAP < 65 mmHg) predict subsequent AKI in eICU patients?

## Clinical Importance
Hypotension is a common ICU finding, and the duration of hypotension may be a more informative risk factor than minimum MAP alone. If confirmed, this could inform monitoring and resuscitation strategies.

## Data Bindings (eICU)
- Vitals: eicu_crd_vital (patient_id, timestamp, mean_arterial_pressure)
- Labs: eicu_crd_lab (patient_id, timestamp, item_id, value)
- Medications: eicu_crd_medication (patient_id, timestamp, medication_name)
- Demographics: eicu_crd_patient (patient_id, age, gender)
- Outcomes: eicu_crd_disposition (patient_id, discharge_status)

## Hypothesis
At the same minimum MAP, patients with longer duration of hypotension (MAP < 65 mmHg) have higher risk of subsequent AKI (KDIGO stage >= 2).

## Method Comparison
- Simple baseline: Time-weighted hypotension burden (hours MAP < 65) with logistic regression
- Learned alternative: Gradient boosting with time-series features (MAP trajectory, vasopressor exposure)
