# Executed CRC/other-GI/non-GI residual-moment experiment: stable global non-rejection remains inconclusive

## Parent, scientific question, and substantive advance

This is an executed and independently aggregate-audited child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen selected-recorded-case population, three exhaustive outcome groups, day-366 delayed entry, horizon and washout rules, corrected CBC semantics, comparator and lag definitions, six-dimensional orthogonal residual-moment target, global gate, closed testing, multinomial benchmark, falsifications, and population-level `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED` scope.

The strongest prior evidence-supported claim remains narrow: in the same 998 people selected because a first qualifying malignancy was recorded during `[E,H]`, the earlier broad CRC-versus-all-other-cancer analysis did not reject its adjusted CBC residual-moment null, but did not establish equivalence. The unresolved hypothesis tested here was whether that broad grouping masked a selected-case association difference among CRC, other gastrointestinal malignancy, and non-GI malignancy. The execution found stable global non-rejection by both the six-dimensional orthogonal score and multinomial benchmark. Therefore it does not support the masking/heterogeneity hypothesis, but broad simultaneous intervals, sparse groups, imperfect nuisance calibration, lack of an equivalence margin, and selected-case ascertainment prevent an absence or equivalence conclusion.

## Executed primary estimator

The comparator vector was exactly age at `L`, sex, repeat-visit haemoglobin, MCV, platelet count, and `lag_late`. Two binary ridge-logistic nuisances estimated CRC and other-GI group probabilities, with non-GI as reference. Three ridge-linear nuisances estimated the adverse haemoglobin, MCV, and platelet trajectories. The intercept and `lag_late` were unpenalized in each binary logistic nuisance. Continuous comparator scaling, all tuning, and nuisance fitting were outer-training-only. Five-fold inner tuning selected from `10^-4,...,10^4`, choosing the largest tied penalty. Because the smallest outer-training group-by-lag-by-sex cell had four people, five-way joint inner stratification was impossible; inner folds were training-only and stratified by three-level group. This operational resolution does not use held-out outcomes but remains an implementation limitation.

Twenty five-fold cross-fit repetitions used seeds `20260909` through `20260928`; every outer test fold retained all three groups and every joint group/lag/sex stratum, with zero train-test overlap. Each participant's six held-out score contributions were averaged across repetitions before one participant-level covariance was calculated. Score order was CRC-Hb, CRC-MCV, CRC-platelet, otherGI-Hb, otherGI-MCV, otherGI-platelet. Calibration used 20,000 independent standard-normal participant multipliers on centered fixed averaged scores with a plus-one p-value.

The six estimates were `(-0.0002909713, -0.0056062751, -0.0278568699, 0.0001557477, 0.0024524192, -0.0914875493)`, with robust SEs `(0.0015831237, 0.0056838648, 0.0743012538, 0.0016636868, 0.0059654562, 0.0963095129)`. The global statistic was `T=2.7128299270` on 6 dimensions; chi-square-reference `p=0.8439295` and multiplier plus-one `p=0.8459577`. The multiplier 95th percentile was `12.55932`, well above the observed statistic. Covariance eigenvalues were `(0.00206632, 0.00243329, 0.02967907, 0.03308927, 5.45391734, 9.31844169)` and condition number `4509.68`, below the frozen failure threshold of `10^10`. All simultaneous max-statistic 95% intervals crossed zero:

- CRC-Hb `[-0.00443115, 0.00384921]`
- CRC-MCV `[-0.02047069, 0.00925814]`
- CRC-platelet `[-0.22216913, 0.16645539]`
- otherGI-Hb `[-0.00419512, 0.00450661]`
- otherGI-MCV `[-0.01314841, 0.01805325]`
- otherGI-platelet `[-0.34335570, 0.16038060]`

The global gate was closed. Contrasts were computed only for audit and cannot be interpreted: CRC-versus-other-GI local multiplier `p=0.67077`, max-statistic familywise `p=0.83306`; CRC-versus-non-GI reference-parameterization local `p=0.78996`, familywise `p=0.91870`. Neither met closed testing.

## Diagnostics, benchmark, and falsifications

All 23,000 primary nuisance fits converged and every score was finite. Across 100 outer folds, the logistic penalty reached `10000` in 88 CRC fits and 95 other-GI fits. CRC cross-fitted log loss improved on intercept-only in only 11/20 repetitions and its calibration slope ranged from `-1.486` to `0.614`, including four negative slopes. Other-GI log loss improved in 19/20 and its calibration slopes ranged `0.314-0.922`. Held-out prediction ranges were `0.0246-0.1162` for CRC and `0.0380-0.0933` for other-GI, so the operational extreme-overlap threshold was not crossed. These diagnostics make the global non-rejection usable as a completed falsification attempt but not as evidence of equivalence.

The ordinary unpenalized three-category multinomial regression compared `C` against `C+S`. Its likelihood-ratio statistic was `2.1629155` on 6 df; chi-square-reference `p=0.904122` and 5,000-sample null parametric-bootstrap plus-one `p=0.905219`, with zero nonconvergent bootstrap fits. This agrees with the primary test in global non-rejection. Five of six benchmark coefficient signs agree with the residual moments; CRC haemoglobin differs (benchmark positive, residual moment negative), so the benchmark is not directional confirmation.

Twenty prespecified joint slope-block permutations within lag-by-sex strata used seeds `20261909` through `20261928`, held group and comparators fixed, reused the unchanged group nuisances, and retuned slope nuisances training-only. Each used 5,000 participant multipliers. All fits and statistics were finite; no permutation had `p<0.05`, the minimum was `0.05039`, and the p-value range extended to `0.88942`. This does not prove finite-sample validity but shows no repeated extreme implementation artifact. Exploratory RDW replacement was correctly two-dimensional (two group indicators by one RDW trajectory), non-rejecting (`T=0.85141`, multiplier `p=0.65122`), and did not open any gate.

An independent aggregate-only audit recomputed the primary Wald statistic from reported theta and covariance as `2.712829927044018`, absolute difference `4.44e-16`, reproduced covariance eigenvalues, and verified hashes, population, event reconciliation, all 100 fold-support records, score order, fit convergence, multiplier/bootstrap counts, gate logic, falsifications, privacy, and decision consistency. It found no failed checks. The audit did not read participant-level data and cannot adjudicate clinical coding or registry completeness.

## Result and evidence limits

The exact defensible decision is `INCONCLUSIVE_WITHOUT_EVIDENCE_OF_GROUP_HETEROGENEITY; NONREJECTION_IS_NOT_EQUIVALENCE`. Supportive evidence was not observed: the six-dimensional global gate did not reject, so no component or pairwise contrast may be interpreted. Adverse or equivalence evidence was also not established: there was no clinically justified residual-moment equivalence margin, simultaneous intervals are broad, CRC and other-GI groups are small, and CRC nuisance calibration is weak. The strongest computational claim is only that this frozen implementation found no statistically detectable adjusted six-dimensional residual-moment heterogeneity and that the ordinary multinomial benchmark also did not reject.

This remains a case-only selected-recorded-malignancy association. Conditioning on future recorded cancer can induce collider bias, and `lag_late` is post-landmark. The population-level prognosis/ascertainment gate remains `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED`. The data cannot automatically establish registry completeness, correct diagnosis, pathology, stage, occult bleeding or inflammation, mechanism, causality, incidence, prospective risk, screening performance, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, lead time, referral utility, transportability, patient benefit, harms, or costs. Stronger biological or clinical conclusions require clinical/registry/pathology adjudication and an independent population-based cohort with protocolized repeat CBC measurements.
