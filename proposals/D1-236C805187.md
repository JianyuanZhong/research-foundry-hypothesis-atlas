## Frozen endpoint and the execution stop

If the parent artifact is subsequently recovered or its full recipe is supplied, the next run must first verify the 540 rows, 443 distinct stays, 74 hospitals, five landmarks `{1440,2880,4320,5760,7200}`, first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`, exact arm strings, `L <= D <= L+360`, inherited exclusions, source-row digests, and canonical sorted row hash. Only after that pass may it freeze the outcome-blind parser:

* exact labels only: FiO2, FIO2 (%), Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2); PEEP, PEEP/CPAP; Pressure Support, PS above PEEP; Vent Rate, VS RESP RATE;
* full-token numeric parsing with no unit conversion, fraction conversion, interpolation, carry-forward, ranges, inequalities, or embedded units; bounds FiO2 `[0,100]`, PEEP/PS `[0,60]`, set rate `[0,100]`, SpO2 `[0,100]`, respiration `[0,200]`;
* at least two valid observations at distinct `respchartoffset` values separated by at least 30 minutes per component and interval, median aggregation, pre-D windows `(D-1440,D-720]` and `(D-720,D]`, and primary post window `(D+360,D+1800]`;
* persistent reduction thresholds of 0.05 FiO2 percentage points, 2 PEEP, 2 pressure support, or 2 set rate units, with no component substitution;
* primary composite requiring at least one non-peak reduction and no adverse change in both available `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration`; missing safety observations are endpoint-missing, not non-events;
* respiratoryCare corroboration as a separately reported chart flag, not proof of delivered settings, and recency sensitivities at the prespecified unbounded/24-hour/12-hour/6-hour definitions.

Early `unitdischargeoffset <= D+1800` must remain a competing exit, classified as death only for trimmed case-insensitive `unitdischargestatus=Expired`, otherwise discharge/transfer, or truncation when status/location cannot classify it. No early exit plus absent required observations is ascertainment missingness. The eventual analysis must report event, observed non-event, death, discharge/transfer, truncation, and missingness by arm, hospital, and landmark, then use leave-one-hospital-out baseline-versus-label models with pre-D predictors only, row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro metrics, stay-clustered bootstrap, and fixed-stratum permutations. ## Evidence and limits

The source audit establishes raw availability, exact headers, source identity, target prevalence, and broad respiratory endpoint ingredients. It does not establish clinical intent, actual ventilator delivery, extubation, SBT performance, reintubation, respiratory severity, quality, benefit, harm, or causality. The eICU metadata also records that narrative sections are removed and `vitalPeriodic` contains five-minute summary observations rather than raw waveforms. Clinical adjudication, local workflow/interface metadata, device-linked delivered settings, and an external or prospective validation cohort would be required for stronger bedside or clinical claims.
