# Working proposal (episode 4): Is the Mpos-macrophage -> FLS program a distinct state-shaping signal or generic inflammation, and does it track clinical remission?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (episode 3 selection, seed D3-M2).
Operation: evolve/repair. This child fixes the two limitations the parent explicitly left: the "445-feature panel must be fixed from source" and the MerTK-dependence estimand.

## Unresolved question and why it matters
RA synovial MerTK+CD206+ (Mpos) macrophages mark remission, but it is unresolved whether Mpos macrophages
*shape* fibroblast-like synoviocyte (FLS) state, or merely co-occur with a resolving joint. The parent claimed an
FLS "resolution-like response" that is "MerTK-signaling-dependent". A transcript-level claim of receptor/ligand
dependence is only testable if the axis members are measured in the assay used.

## Verified panel binding (executed this episode)
Matrix: E-MTAB-8873.counts.tsv.gz. Layout verified: header row = 34 columns = `feature` + 33 sample_ids
(sample_ids == design.json sample_id). 445 feature rows; feature string form `SYMBOL|REFSEQ|Reference_end|PolyA_n`.
Measured (verified by exact symbol match in feature column):
- TAM axis: MERTK (row 308, NM_006343.2), AXL (row 17). NOT measured: GAS6, PROS1, TYRO3.
- Macrophage markers/effectors: MRC1 (CD206), CD163, ITGAM, C1QA/C1QB, TGFB1/TGFB3/TGFBR1-3, MMP1/2/3/9/12/13/14.
Condition counts: {not co-cultured 6, Mneg 11, Mpos 13, Mneg+Mpos 3}; treatment {none 23, MerTK inhibitor 5, LPS 5}.
Therefore the panel CAN read the MERTK receptor transcript and downstream effector genes but CANNOT read the
GAS6/PROS1 ligand side. Estimand must be receptor-transcript + inhibitor-sensitivity, not "MerTK-signaling dependence".

## Design (frozen)
Population: 33 FLS (type B synovial cell) pseudobulk samples E-MTAB-8873; contributing/reused donors modelled explicitly.
Temporal: cross-sectional coculture; no longitudinal-transition claim.
- C1 state vs composition: Mpos (13) vs Mneg (11) FLS co-culture, donor-blocked (R1/R2/R5), 445 features.
- C2 specificity: correlate C1 effect vector with the LPS-vs-none effect vector (5 LPS samples). High correlation
  => generic TLR/inflammatory program; low => distinct Mpos-associated program.
- C3 receptor/inhibitor: Mpos+MerTK-inhibitor vs Mpos (5 inhibitor samples).
- C4 clinical anchor: project the C1 signature genes onto E-MTAB-8322 macrophage pseudobulk remission (7) vs
  resistant (6); Ensembl IDs (MERTK ENSG00000136574, AXL ENSG00000167601 present; GAS6 absent).
Orthogonal: E-MTAB-8316 LPS monocytes +/- compound UNC106238 (6+6; full transcriptome; MERTK/GAS6/PROS1 present),
donor-clustered (4 FLS clusters, 6 nested monocyte donors; repeated contexts averaged).

## Alternatives (baseline vs substantive alternative)
Baseline: per-gene donor-blocked paired t / moderated linear model on log-CPM, BH-FDR, on the 445 features.
Alternative: donor-aware low-rank (factor/PCA) decomposition separating a shared "inflammation" axis from a
Mpos-specific residual axis, tested against the baseline by whether the Mpos residual survives after regressing out
the LPS/inflammation factor. The alternative is informative because the baseline cannot distinguish "Mpos adds a
distinct program" from "Mpos adds more of the same inflammatory amplitude".

## Outcomes
Supportive: C1 signature reproducible across FLS donors, weakly correlated with the LPS axis, partially reversed by
inhibitor, and concordant in direction in E-MTAB-8322 remission>resistant.
Adverse: C1 collapses onto the LPS axis, or is fully explained by Mpos abundance, or is not reversed by inhibitor.
Inconclusive: donor effects dominate, n per arm too small after donor-blocking, or effect is measurement/allele driven.

## Falsification
Falsified if the donor-blocked C1 signature is (a) not separable from the LPS/inflammation axis (|r| above a
pre-registered threshold) AND (b) unchanged by the MerTK-inhibitor arm. MerTK-specific mechanism is NOT resolvable
by these transcript data (no ligand transcripts, no phospho-readout) and would require protein-level perturbation data.

## Key references and claim discussion
Leading claim: Mpos macrophages induce a distinct, donor-robust FLS state-shaping program tracking remission, separable
from generic inflammation and composition. The human association this rests on is that MerTK+CD206+ synovial macrophages
mark RA remission and a resolving state [K1]; that work supports the association but does not establish directionality of
an FLS effect or a receptor-ligand dependency test [K1]. Strongest rival: Mpos signatures may reflect TAM-receptor biology
in which MERTK and AXL are non-redundant with distinct ligand usage, so an unmeasured GAS6/PROS1 ligand side (verified
absent from the 445-feature panel) cannot license a "MerTK-signaling dependence" claim, bounding the estimand to
MERTK-transcript plus inhibitor-sensitivity [K2]. Second rival (composition/ontogeny): resident synovial macrophage
identity is ontogeny- and barrier-dependent [K3], so an apparent Mpos effect could be a composition/origin artifact;
the donor-blocked Mpos-vs-Mneg contrast, LPS-axis specificity test and composition-adjusted alternative separate this
rival from the leading claim [K3].
