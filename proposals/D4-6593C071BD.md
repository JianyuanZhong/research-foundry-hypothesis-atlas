# UKB 09: Leukocyte composition and infection outcomes at the same level of inflammation

Admission: feasibility_required. Verify participant overlap, measurement dates, missingness and event support before committing this design.

## Hypotheses to Be Tested

When CRP and total white blood cell counts are similar, those with relatively low lymphocyte and high neutrophil counts have a higher short-term risk of death after infection.

## Study population and key data

Adults without hematologic malignancies; pre-illness classification of blood cell counts, infections, and mortality.

## Basic Approach

Model categorical counts as compositional data, analyze disease onset and post-infection death separately, and test time since blood sampling and repeated measurements.

## Significance of the Research

Determine whether immune-cell composition provides information beyond total inflammation.

## Main Challenges

Cell counts cannot be used to infer cell function; immunosuppressive treatment and case selection need to be considered.

## Life Sciences

Immunity, infection, and injury tolerance

## Competing Explanations

Medication, occult disease, or nutritional status causes the count and mortality to change together.

## Minimal validation and falsification criteria

First examine results after auditing duplicate counts and excluding early events; if the finding is seen only in blood samples collected close to infection, it does not support a stable host state.
