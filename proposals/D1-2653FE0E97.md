# Mask-anchored visibility frontier for singleton-stable documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Targeted successor and repaired scientific object

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's first-eligible HCC-resection frame, coherent operative clocks and lookbacks, eventual-report/documentary-M2 evidence boundary, whole-accession report units, six immutable corpus-wide reader books and nine radiology-by-pathology book pairs, patient-balanced locked models, fixed absolute K, three comparator-closed contrasts, strict margin of more than five documentary-M2 captures per 100 positions, exact finite-corpus extrema, and report-release frontier.

The parent contains one consequential mismatch. It says one report-release mask is shared across paired undeleted and singleton-deleted calculations, but then defines the deletion frontier using a new post-deletion quota `ceil(c|A(omega minus j)|)`. If the optimizer is allowed to satisfy that reduced quota with a newly chosen mask, surviving accessions can change from hidden to visible or visible to hidden. If masks failing the new quota are discarded, deletion of a report-rich patient selectively removes otherwise admissible baseline patterns. Either reading changes report availability while supposedly changing only the patient. The resulting endpoint is not leave-one-patient-out stability of a fixed availability process.

This child makes the pair explicit and falsifiable. For every full test-year world, choose one baseline whole-accession release mask before any deletion. Every singleton calculation restricts that exact mask to surviving accessions; no surviving bit may change and no post-deletion coverage quota is imposed. K, models, books, ties, roles and all surviving patient states remain frozen. The deletion is an influence stress, not a new cohort promised to have coverage c.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Conditional on all-content comparator closure, there is a smallest mask-anchored release fraction `c_anchor* <= 0.80` such that, for every full-cohort whole-accession release mask exposing at least that fraction of releasable accessions, the locked full model retains all six strict >5 comparator margins in 2020 and 2021 both before deletion and after removal of any one whole test patient while retaining the same release bits for every survivor.

Equality of a margin to five fails. `c_anchor*=0.80` passes only when every required margin is strictly above five. This is a finite-corpus stored-content stress criterion, not an estimate of actual report timeliness, workflow capacity, clinical benefit, or a population parameter.

## 4. Whole source objects, outcome, and clinical adjudication

A radiology unit is `u=(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` and contains every exact-deduplicated component row in original order with immutable byte/field backpointers. Blank `检查号` cannot supply primary semantics. A reversible parser separates visible text, markup/attributes and parse errors. Semantic spans arise only from reversible visible bytes. Nonreversibility, contamination by treatment/pathology/specimen/MVI grade, or unresolved context makes the entire accession source-unavailable; no phrase is selectively removed and no patient is dropped.

A pathology object is `v=(患者主索引,就诊号,composite_hash)`, serializing every exact-deduplicated `病理/检查所见/检查诊断` field in row/field order. All pathology rows in the encounter remain one indivisible documentary composite and are never assigned to a specimen.

Before outcomes or scores are exposed, two qualified Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists plus a complete adjudicator read the complete radiology roster; two qualified Chinese-reading hepatobiliary pathologists plus a complete adjudicator read the complete pathology roster. Freeze radiology books `R1,R2,RA` and pathology books `P1,P2,PA`; exactly nine global book pairs apply corpus-wide. No patient-, item-, year-, mask-, model- or deletion-specific reader switching is allowed.

Radiology forms encode predeclared lesion burden, capsule, margin, enhancement/washout, peritumoral features, satellites, venous tumor thrombus, cirrhosis and ascites with uncertain/not-mentioned/source-unavailable terminals. Pathology terminals are `OUT, IN0-A, IN1-A, IN0-U, IN1-U`, where IN1 denotes encounter-documentary M2 and OUT has no outcome. This is not biological M2. Clinical correctness of dictionaries, readers, Chinese semantics, operative identity, and pathology sampling requires expert adjudication and is not established by computation.

## 6. Mask-anchored release and deletion estimand

For every reachable clean accession u in a full year/world, define a hypothetical whole-accession release bit r_u. If r_u=1, expose the full eligible report block. If r_u=0, preserve acquisition-only Z and replace every body-derived S/Q/X block with its development-defined operational-unavailable block in R_clean and G_clean; apply the identical S/Q masking to Gclean_mask. B is unchanged. Source-unavailable units stay unavailable and are not counted as releasable. One r is used across all arms and comparators.

For full world omega and year a let A_a(omega) be its releasable units. For each exact breakpoint c in the union of `m/|A_a(omega)|` plus 0 and 1, define the full-roster admissible family

`R_c(omega)={r: sum_{u in A_a(omega)} r_u >= ceil(c|A_a(omega)|)}`.

For each r in this family, compute all undeleted contrasts. Then, for every whole test patient j, form the deleted score tables by removing j, all j's states, outcomes and units and restricting the same mask:

`r^{-j}=r restricted to A_a(omega) minus A_j`.

No surviving bit may change. There is no new `ceil(c|A minus A_j|)` constraint and no visibility replacement. Models, preprocessing, books, roles, K, ties and all surviving states stay byte-identical. Require N^{-j}>=K. The fact that survivor coverage can be below c is intentional: deletion measures influence under the baseline process and is not interpreted as a new c-covered cohort.

For coordinate e define the attained undeleted lower endpoint as the minimum over omega and r in R_c(omega), and the mask-anchored singleton lower endpoint as the minimum over omega, r in R_c(omega), and eligible deletion j of the corresponding deleted contrast. Optimize each contrast with a coherent world and common mask across its arms. Define `c_anchor*` as the smallest breakpoint for which all six undeleted and all six anchored-singleton lower endpoints are strictly >5. If all-content c=1 fails, no partial-release pattern may rescue it.

The parent's deletion-renormalized quantity may be computed only as a clearly labeled diagnostic. It cannot support or falsify the anchored hypothesis because it changes two factors at once.

## 7. Exact computation, uncertainty, and falsification

Compile finite patient domains carrying membership/year/Y, Z/S/Q/X blocks, locked scores, unit hashes, source/form/terminal hashes, ties and raw backpointers. For each book pair and coordinate, exact MIP or certified equivalent chooses one coherent state per patient and one full-roster whole-accession mask, then ranks exactly K patients per arm. Deletion is implemented only by set subtraction and restriction of that chosen mask. Require zero integer gap, numerical feasibility and residual <=1e-8, complete rank-inversion constraints, all tied maximizers, raw/quotient replay, and exhaustive agreement with brute force on fixtures through 12 patients and real shards through 20. A timeout, state cap, mask sample, favorable incumbent, unexplained branch, missing witness, changed survivor bit or failed replay forces inconclusive.

The primary estimand is the exact frozen finite corpus. No confidence interval creates a population claim. If bootstrap diagnostics are retained from the parent, masks and deletions are inherited within each patient bootstrap and cannot change the exact primary label.

One-hot result:

1. `inconclusive`: any source, roster, annotation, chronology, event, N/K, leakage, fit, replay, exact-solve or mask-invariance gate fails.
2. `mask-anchored visibility-robust supportive`: all all-content gates pass, c_anchor* exists and c_anchor*<=.80.
3. `all-content supportive but anchored-visibility-brittle`: all-content margins pass but c_anchor*>.80.
4. `semantic-evidence-only`: both years' Delta_R and Delta_mask pass all-content/singleton gates but a Delta_B coordinate fails.
5. `quota-policy-only`: both Delta_B years pass but not all semantic coordinates pass.
6. `falsified-localized`: gates pass and none above applies; report the year, comparator, world, mask, breakpoint and deletion witness. A coordinate is all-world adverse only if its attained upper endpoint is <=5.

A supportive result means only that, in this frozen eventual-report corpus, any baseline release placement meeting c_anchor* preserves the strict documentary-M2 capture margins and those margins are not created by one patient's inclusion when availability is held fixed. An adverse/brittle result falsifies that universal stress claim; it does not prove report semantics useless or reports late. Inconclusive means the evidence or computation cannot resolve the claim.
