# Working proposal: ascertainment-conditional hypotension burden

## Question and substantive advance

The unresolved question is whether time-weighted MAP<65 burden adds a reproducible prognostic signal for incident creatinine-based AKI beyond MAP nadir, baseline kidney function, and the opportunity to observe MAP and creatinine. The parent supports only an association between hypotension duration/severity and kidney outcomes [K1] [K2], and an ICU prevention-bundle association that does not isolate hypotension duration or establish causality [K3]. The new claim is narrower and falsifiable: among ICU stays with prespecified adequate post-index creatinine ascertainment, does burden add prognostic information beyond nadir and MAP observation opportunity?

The advance is to make the leading ascertainment rival primary rather than secondary. A burden association that survives only when unobserved creatinine is treated as no event is not a duration signal. The primary estimand is therefore conditional on an explicit, pre-outcome-defined creatinine observation opportunity, with the observed-trajectory and strict views retained as prespecified sensitivity analyses. This does not claim that adequate observation is random, that missing creatinine means no AKI, or that the result is causal.

## Population, index, and exposure

Include adults with parsed age 18–89, the first ICU stay per patienthealthsystemstayid, at least one valid MAP in vitalPeriodic.systemicmean or vitalAperiodic.noninvasivemean during the first 24 ICU hours, at least one MAP<65, and at least six valid MAP observations. Exclude unavailable baseline creatinine, documented pre-existing dialysis/renal replacement therapy, and fewer than 24 hours of follow-up. Define the index as the first contiguous MAP<65 interval after the first qualifying MAP, with a 30-minute grace period for missing observations. Sensitivities are no grace, 60-minute grace, and thresholds 60 and 70.

Construct a five-minute grid from the first qualifying MAP through 24 hours. Use the last observed MAP without extrapolation beyond 30 minutes. Primary exposure is the fraction of covered time below 65; secondary exposure is area below 65. Retain nadir, median, MAP observation count, covered fraction, median gap, and periodic/aperiodic source mix. The baseline covariate block is fixed before the index: age, sex, ethnicity, hospital, unit type, admission source, baseline creatinine, pre-index MAP and heart-rate summaries, and pre-index observation opportunity. Do not use post-index treatment in the primary estimand.

## Prespecified creatinine ascertainment

Use numeric labresult for exact labname=creatinine, labmeasurenamesystem=mg/dL, and labresultoffset. Retain labresultrevisedoffset for revision auditing. Define the baseline as the minimum creatinine in the prior 24 hours. The outcome is incident creatinine-based AKI through 72 hours after index: at least 0.3 mg/dL above baseline or at least 1.5 times baseline. This is not full KDIGO AKI because reliable urine-output adjudication is unavailable.

Define ascertainment opportunity before looking at the outcome:
- Adequate ascertainment (primary): at least two post-index creatinine measurements, at least one in 0–24 hours and one in 24–72 hours, and no gap greater than 12 hours between the first and last qualifying measurement. This is a selection criterion, not a claim of random testing.
- Strict ascertainment (sensitivity): the same two-window requirement plus at least three post-index measurements and no gap greater than 12 hours anywhere in 0–72 hours.
- Observed-trajectory view (sensitivity): retain the parent outcome but require the baseline and qualifying post-index measurement to be observed; report measurement count and timing. Missing creatinine is never coded as no AKI.

The primary estimand is the adjusted prognostic contrast for burden+nadir versus nadir-only among the adequate-ascertainment cohort, with the outcome defined from observed creatinine only. It is conditional on adequate ascertainment and transportability is limited to patients who meet that observation pattern. Report the fraction excluded by each criterion, creatinine count and timing distributions by burden strata, and an explicit selection/transportability warning.

## Analysis and ascertainment sensitivity

Fit nested logistic models in the same adequate-ascertainment cohort and outcome view:
1. Opportunity-only: baseline severity and MAP observation opportunity, including MAP count, coverage, median gap, source mix, and pre-index clinical variables.
2. Opportunity+nadir: add MAP nadir.
3. Opportunity+nadir+burden: add time-weighted burden.

The primary comparison is model 3 versus model 2. Report adjusted OR, likelihood-ratio contrast, calibration, and hospital-clustered bootstrap uncertainty. Also compare opportunity-only with burden+nadir to show whether burden adds information after representing the observation process. A prespecified negative-control comparison uses observation-density-only versus a deliberately nonclinical observation feature (source mix or gap pattern) without burden, checking whether the contrast is merely documentation intensity; it is not causal identification.

Repeat the exact nested models in the strict view and observed-trajectory view. Stratify by creatinine observation count and median inter-measurement gap, and report hospital-specific estimates. Do not relax ascertainment criteria after seeing results. If strict-view events are sparse, label the result inconclusive.

Post-index treatment is secondary and descriptive. Extract prespecified vasopressor/inotrope names from infusionDrug using infusionoffset, drugrate, infusionrate, drugamount, volumeoffluid, and patientweight; retain medication order/start/stop fields and drugordercancelled, and treatment treatmentoffset/treatmentstring separately. Summarize time to first record, class, duration, rate/dose where numeric, and timing relative to index. Do not treat an order as administration or infer dose from missing fields. Repeat burden contrasts in treated versus untreated groups and add treatment descriptors only secondarily. Treatment is post-index and may be a collider; no treatment effect, mediation, or mechanism is estimated.

## Baseline versus learned alternative

The simple baseline is multivariable logistic regression with the fixed opportunity block, nadir, and burden. It is the primary test because it yields an interpretable adjusted OR, likelihood-ratio contrast, calibration, and uncertainty.

The substantive learned alternative is a regularized gradient-boosted tree model using the same five-minute summaries and feature blocks, with nested hospital-held-out evaluation and time-ordered splits within training hospitals. Compare opportunity-only, opportunity+nadir, and opportunity+nadir+burden. It can reveal nonlinear burden–nadir interactions and heterogeneity by baseline kidney function or treatment timing that the linear baseline loses. A small AUROC gain alone is not scientific success; the learned model must be interpreted alongside the primary contrast and ascertainment views.

A mechanistic discrete-time survival model with cumulative burden and time-varying treatment is deferred because creatinine ascertainment and treatment timing do not support a defensible causal or mechanistic time-to-event estimand. A neural sequence model is not selected because the central uncertainty is observation and outcome ascertainment; the tabular learned alternative is adequate. Revisit either only if the five-minute representation is shown to lose information needed to distinguish the rivals.

## Split, uncertainty, and deliverable

Use hospital-held-out evaluation as the primary transportability test: all stays from a hospital are held out together. Within each training hospital, use a time-ordered split by ICU admission offset or earliest qualifying MAP offset, with no future rows in training. Fit preprocessing, regularization, and model selection inside training folds only. Use hospital-clustered bootstrap intervals for the primary adjusted OR and likelihood-ratio contrast. Report calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, AUROC/AUPRC as secondary summaries, and decision-curve net benefit at prespecified risk thresholds. Do not select a model or threshold on held-out data.

The solver must newly construct the cohort and five-minute exposure grid; estimate the primary ascertainment-conditional burden contrast; produce strict and observed creatinine views; produce the treatment-response decomposition; and evaluate the learned alternative with hospital-held-out and time-ordered splits. It must report uncertainty, calibration, decision value, sensitivity analyses, and a conclusion tied to computed outputs. It must not report a causal treatment effect or full KDIGO AKI claim.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive results require burden to add prespecified incremental association beyond nadir and observation opportunity, with a 95% CI for the adjusted OR excluding 1 and a likelihood-ratio test favoring burden+nadir; direction should be stable across hospitals, thresholds, grace periods, observation-density strata, and the strict creatinine view. This supports a reproducible prognostic duration signal conditional on ascertainment, not a treatment target.

Adverse results include no incremental burden information beyond nadir/density, reversal after density adjustment, confinement to one hospital or source, or disappearance in the strict view. These favor nadir, severity, observation practice, or ascertainment explanations and stop the duration claim.

Inconclusive results include wide intervals, sparse strict-view events, or materially different results under creatinine ascertainment and grace/threshold choices. An imprecise null is not decisive refutation.

Unavailable evidence includes validated full KDIGO urine-output adjudication, reliable pre-ICU baseline creatinine for every stay, clinician-confirmed AKI, complete nephrotoxin exposure, and the causal effect of raising MAP. Clinical adjudication or a prospective/controlled study is required for stronger claims.

## Evidence and limitations

The three key works are reused as frozen evidence: K1 Flick et al. (postoperative hourly MAP hypotension duration/severity and exploratory AKI association), K2 Ao et al. (dynamic/individualized MAP features and kidney outcomes with confounding and external-validation limitations), and K3 Muralidharan et al. (ICU AKI prevention bundle association without isolating hypotension duration or causality). Their frozen source bytes and receipts are attached to the parent and must be preserved for this child. No new paper claim is made by this audit. The source audit is a feasibility check, not a study result.
