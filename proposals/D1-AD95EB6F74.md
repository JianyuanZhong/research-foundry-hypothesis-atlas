# All-admission documented-pathway mismatch and early physiology in HCC

## Scientific deliverable and substantive advance

The future solver must newly freeze an adult first-observed HCC-coded admission cohort, exact procedure and order dictionaries with an uncertainty audit, an exhaustive admission-anchored terminal-state label, and patient-level fitting/selection/test partitions. It must fit a matched transparent baseline B0, a same-input early-laboratory model B1, and an interpretable irregular longitudinal alternative B2; produce one locked prediction for every eligible admission; estimate the all-admission early-physiology increment and the prespecified documentation-mismatch contrast; and emit uncertainty, missing-time bounds, falsifications, and a claim-to-output table.

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent made plan-discordance clinically interesting, but its primary Q-versus-C contrast was restricted to a clear single-family documented-plan stratum. That makes the scientific conclusion vulnerable to sparse support, vocabulary choices, and selective plan documentation. This successor keeps the clinically consequential physiology-versus-recorded-pathway question but changes the primary risk set and outcome so that every eligible admission contributes. Missing, multiple, unassignable, and invalid-time records are observed uncertainty states, not silent exclusions or negative plans. The documented-plan mismatch contrast is retained across all documentation tiers as a prespecified secondary mechanism estimand, not as the sole evidence of clinical value.

## Supported evidence and unresolved hypothesis

The available local evidence supports dated encounters, HCC-like diagnosis strings, numeric laboratory records, non-drug orders, and procedure strings. The source audit found unique encounter identity keys, many-to-one diagnosis/procedure/order relationships with substantial encounter-level duplication, common routine order strings such as hepatic/renal/blood tests and nursing levels, and no field that establishes clinical intent or completion. The dataset does not establish pathology-confirmed HCC, stage, tumor burden, resectability, performance status, treatment intent, recommendation, scheduling, appropriateness, benefit, harm, or complete outside-hospital capture.

The unresolved question is:

> Among adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission, does numeric hepatic/renal information observed in the first 24 hours improve calibrated prediction of a later locally recorded liver-directed procedure during hours 24–72, and is any incremental signal larger for a procedure whose family is not corroborated by the pre-24-hour documented-order evidence than for one that is corroborated, when every admission remains in the analysis?

The primary hypothesis has two linked, falsifiable components:

1. Early physiology has a nonzero, clinically relevant held-out incremental association with any valid recorded liver-directed procedure in [24,72), beyond admission/pre-admission information and dictionary-independent documentation-capture variables.

2. The early-laboratory increment is larger for a documentation-mismatch later procedure than for a documentation-matched later procedure:
   H_doc = Delta_Qall - Delta_K > 0,
   where Delta_Qall and Delta_K are standardized B1-minus-B0 absolute probabilities for terminal mismatch and matched procedure states, respectively.

The primary all-admission estimand is Delta_T, the standardized B1-minus-B0 absolute probability of any valid 24–72-hour procedure, reported with the full terminal-state vector. H_doc is the key secondary mechanism estimand and is defined without restricting the cohort to clear plans. It is a recorded-documentation relationship, not true clinical discordance, unplanned care, a change in intent, a treatment effect, or a judgment of appropriateness.

Use a study relevance threshold of 0.01 absolute probability points for Delta_T and H_doc, with two-sided patient-clustered bootstrap intervals. This is a protocol relevance criterion, not a clinical utility threshold. If Qall or K support is inadequate, H_doc is inconclusive while Delta_T can still be interpreted.

## Exhaustive 72-hour terminal outcome

Every eligible admission receives exactly one primary terminal state at hour 72, with an attached family/timing audit. Precedence is:

- E0/A0/S0/X0: the first valid qualifying procedure starts in [0,24) and is before discharge, with its family retained.
- Utime: no earlier valid event is established, but at least one potentially qualifying procedure row has missing/invalid `开始时间) or cannot be ordered relative to admission/discharge in a way that could affect [0,72). This state prevents missing time from being treated as no procedure.
- H1-K: the first valid qualifying procedure starts in [24,72) before discharge and its family matches a single-family pre-24 order proxy.
- H1-Q: the first valid qualifying procedure starts in [24,72) before discharge and its family is not matched by the pre-24 documented-order evidence. H1-Q is subdivided as Qclear (a clear pre-24 family exists but differs), Qmulti (multiple/unassignable pre-24 evidence), Qnone (no qualifying pre-24 family), and Qunknown (pre-24 order opening time is missing/invalid). Qclear, Qmulti, Qnone and Qunknown are never collapsed in the audit; Qall is their prespecified pooled secondary contrast.
- D0: discharge before 72 hours, before any valid qualifying procedure and in the absence of Utime. Discharge is an observed care transition, not treatment failure.
- N1: no valid qualifying procedure before 72 hours, no Utime record, and no earlier D0. This means no locally recorded event in the time-bounded source, not stable disease, no intent, or no clinical change.

If an unresolved-time row could precede or follow a discharge or valid event, Utime takes precedence unless a valid event is already the demonstrably first event. If a discharge occurs before a valid procedure start, D0 wins. Procedure rows with valid starts after discharge or after hour 72 are not primary events and are retained for audit. Report E/A/S/X substates, event times, D0 timing, Utime causes, and all state counts. Sensitivity bounds reassign Utime to the earliest clinically plausible event classes; these are missing-time bounds, not adjudicated outcomes.

The primary target is the full vector {E0,A0,S0,X0,Utime,H1-K,H1-Q,D0,N1}. Delta_T is the B1-minus-B0 standardized probability for H1-K or H1-Q combined, averaged over every locked-test admission. H_doc is Delta_Qall minus Delta_K, where Qall is H1-Q and K is H1-K. The full vector and the dictionary-independent Delta_T are primary evidence; H_doc is uninterpretable if exact family semantics, Q/K support, or missing-time bounds fail.

These categories describe local documentation only. They are not intent, recommendation, scheduling, completion, or ground truth. H1-K requires P0=the later procedure family; every other later valid procedure is H1-Q with its tier retained.

The dictionary is not required to estimate Delta_T. The solver must report exact terms, excluded terms/reasons, order status `医嘱状态`, duration `医嘱期限`, frequency `频次`, `开始时间`, `结束时间`, duplicate rates, missing opening-time rates, and the distribution of P0=K/M/N/U. For the secondary pathway-change description, construct P1 from orders opened in [24,72) only for admissions not assigned E0/A0/S0/X0, Utime, or D0. Report P0-to-P1 transitions and any later procedure-family alignment descriptively. P1 is not a predictor and is never used to rescue a sparse H_doc result.

## Matched baseline and interpretable irregular alternative

B0 is a transparent multinomial terminal-state model for the full Y vector. It uses age, sex, admitting `就诊科室`, admission era/time descriptors, diagnosis-type descriptors, pre-admission encounter/procedure summaries, pre-admission exact-assay nearest value/recency/count/missingness, and dictionary-independent pre-24 documentation-capture variables (total order rows, valid-opening-time fraction, non-pathway order count, and missing-order-time indicator). It does not use a family-specific plan label in the primary analysis and contains no discharge field, post-24 lab, P1, later order density, later procedure, or outcome-derived feature.

B1 has exactly the same eligible admissions, terminal target, preprocessing, split, regularization and missingness handling as B0, and adds only the prespecified [0,24) lab summaries. The primary increment is B1 versus B0. A prespecified sensitivity adds P0 as a coarse K/M/N/U feature to both models; a large change in Delta_T after this addition is evidence that the result is documentation-capture dependent, not proof of a plan effect.

B2 is an interpretable irregular continuous-time coupled-state model using the same exact records and patient split. It has two low-dimensional latent coordinates labelled hepatic-reserve and renal-stress only as modelling constructs, assay-specific offsets/noise rather than pooled units, patient shrinkage, irregular elapsed-time updates, and an observation-intensity component restricted to [0,24). It receives every B0 input plus the exact assay observations/timestamps and has the same full terminal-state head. B2 can reveal nonlinear timing, coupled movement, and uncertainty from irregular sampling that B1's first/last summaries lose. It must report latent uncertainty and per-state predictions, not call latent coordinates diagnoses or validated scores.

The comparison is substantive: B0 establishes what admission/pre-admission information and documentation opportunity predict; B1 tests incremental early physiology; B2 tests whether timing/coupling and irregular sampling change the physiology signal or its documentation-tier stability. Compare B1 with B0 and B2 with B1 on locked multiclass log loss, Brier score, calibration, per-state probability error, Delta_T, H_doc, and bootstrap uncertainty. A small predictive gain without a stable all-admission contrast or calibrated state distribution is not scientific support.

A GRU/temporal-attention model is deferred unless locked B2 residuals show structured timing error; its added representational complexity would not repair unvalidated order semantics. Text/image/multimodal models are deferred because images are unavailable, clinical documents/pathology have no native acquisition time, and clinical text extraction is unvalidated for intent or pathway change. A validated liver score is deferred because lab units/reference ranges and adjudicated disease labels are absent. These are dependency-based deferrals, not categorical method bans.

## Supportive, adverse and inconclusive gates

Supportive evidence for the primary all-admission hypothesis requires: adequate support for the full state audit; no unresolved leakage; calibrated B1; Delta_T >=0.01 with a two-sided interval excluding zero; and attenuation under lab timestamp/value permutation. A stronger result requires B2 to improve held-out calibration or per-state error beyond B1 and preserve the direction/magnitude of Delta_T without being explained by pre-24 observation intensity.

Supportive evidence for the plan-mismatch mechanism additionally requires adequate K and Qall support; a positive H_doc >=0.01 with an interval excluding zero; stable Qclear/Qmulti/Qnone/Qunknown reporting or a prespecified pooled Qall result; persistence under exact-dictionary perturbation and order-status sensitivity; and attenuation under P0 label permutation within service/era. This supports only that early recorded laboratory information is differentially predictive of locally recorded procedures with versus without matching documented-order evidence.

Adverse evidence includes a null/opposite Delta_T, poor calibration, persistence after lab timestamp/value or terminal-state permutation, disappearance after adding observation-intensity variables, dependence on one assay/procedure/order string/service/era, or a large shift after P0 is added. For H_doc, adverse evidence includes null/opposite H_doc, survival after P0 permutation, domination by Qunknown/Utime, or instability under dictionary perturbation. These results do not prove physiology is clinically irrelevant; they refute or weaken this recorded-data hypothesis.

Inconclusive evidence includes dominant N1/Utime, sparse E/A/S/X or K/Qall states, incomplete discharge capture, high missing/invalid procedure-time burden, dictionary semantics that cannot be frozen, nonconvergence, wide intervals, state-label instability, or inability to distinguish the relevance threshold from zero. If H_doc is inconclusive but Delta_T passes its gate, report a supported dictionary-independent physiology increment with no plan-mismatch conclusion. If both fail, retain the negative or inconclusive result without selecting a post hoc endpoint.

## Falsification and sensitivity plan

The solver must emit:

1. A timestamp proof that every primary lab feature is from [0,24), every primary procedure state uses native `开始时间`, and no post-24 input, discharge predictor, P1, later order density, or outcome field enters B0/B1/B2.
2. Within-patient assay-time permutation preserving assay/value pairs, counts and missingness; separately permute values within assay. Physiology-specific timing/value signal should attenuate.
3. Primary terminal-state-label permutation within service/era strata, preserving state frequencies and pre-24 covariates.
4. Removal/addition of pre-24 observation count, missingness, gap, density and valid-opening-time variables to determine whether the signal is measurement opportunity.
5. Exact dictionary perturbation, leave-one-order-family/string-out, leave-one-assay-out, leave-one-procedure-family-out, duplicate/same-time handling, order status/cancellation sensitivity, and midnight-start sensitivity.
6. P0 category permutation within admitting service and era while preserving tier frequencies; survival of H_doc weakens plan-specific interpretation.
7. Missing-procedure-time bounds, Utime reassignment bounds, and sensitivity excluding Utime; no bound may be presented as observed clinical truth.
8. A routine/central-line/venipuncture workflow negative control from the same orders source, labelled a workflow falsification rather than a clinically null endpoint.
9. Complete-case clear-P0 Q/C analysis only as a secondary comparison, explicitly showing selection from the all-admission population.
10. Secondary [72,144) procedure/discharge reporting among primary members without a qualifying procedure before 72 hours, with native times and competing discharge, never used to redefine the primary target.
