# Provisional HCC hypothesis: temporal laboratory information versus care-process artifact

## Proposed relationship

Among adults with a documented HCC diagnosis during an inpatient admission, an ordered 7-day laboratory trajectory (liver, coagulation, renal, and tumor-marker measurements) will provide incremental held-out information for a subsequent inpatient procedure-based escalation proxy beyond an admission snapshot and measured care-process variables. The primary contrast is predictive, not causal.

The clinically important unresolved claim is whether temporal laboratory persistence/change reflects evolving patient state or mainly treatment indication, documentation intensity, and routine HCC care. A procedure endpoint is a computable proxy for escalation of care; it is not a validated measure of death, ICU admission, clinical deterioration, or harm.

## Evidence already available

The HCC source catalog and prior source audit identify encounter, diagnosis, laboratory, and procedure tables joined within the HCC dataset using the documented patient/encounter keys. The audit found approximately 104,554 HCC encounter keys and approximately 20,052 post-window procedure rows in the prior bounded scan. These are feasibility observations, not study results. The exact frozen endpoint dictionary, event counts under the final cohort rule, assay units, missingness, and clinical interpretation remain to be verified by the solver.

## Falsifiable test

Compare a prespecified snapshot baseline with a time-aware trajectory model on a patient-level held-out split. The primary estimand is the incremental held-out performance for a non-routine post-window procedure endpoint, with uncertainty intervals. Repeat the comparison for routine-treatment procedures and a timing-shifted negative-control endpoint. Support requires a reproducible incremental signal for the non-routine endpoint, a smaller signal for routine procedures, and no signal in the timing-shifted control. Adverse evidence is no incremental signal, a signal confined to routine/documentation-sensitive endpoints, or a negative-control signal. An imprecise interval spanning both clinically relevant benefit and harm is inconclusive.

## Missing evidence and limits

The dataset does not provide validated adjudicated outcomes, complete treatment indications, clinician rationale, or reliable causal identification. Procedure labels require expert review; laboratory units and endpoint support require direct solver verification. No model has been fitted and no effect is claimed here.

## Method comparison

The baseline is a regularized logistic model using admission demographics, department, admission laboratory values, treatment/procedure indicators, and documentation intensity. The substantive alternative is a time-aware representation using ordered within-assay values, time since admission, missingness patterns, and change/persistence features, fitted with the same held-out split and uncertainty evaluation. The alternative can reveal whether temporal ordering and cross-marker evolution add information lost by a snapshot; it does not by itself establish mechanism. CPU tabular fitting is expected to be feasible; any GPU use would be a future solver choice, not a discovery result.
