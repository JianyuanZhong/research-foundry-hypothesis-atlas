# Episode 9 provisional repair: HCC trajectory versus care-process artifact

## Proposed relationship
Among adults admitted with an exact inpatient HCC diagnosis and a valid admission time, an ordered 7-day laboratory trajectory should add held-out information about a subsequent, prespecified non-routine procedure beyond an admission snapshot and explicit care-process indicators. The primary endpoint is the first procedure after admission+7 days and before discharge whose label is not in a frozen routine-HCC-treatment dictionary. This is a predictive proxy hypothesis, not a claim that the procedure represents death, ICU admission, clinical deterioration, or harm.

## What is supported now
The inherited source audit supports feasibility, not an effect: the HCC snapshot contains joinable encounter, diagnosis, laboratory, procedure, medication, and clinical-document tables; exact `诊断名称=肝细胞癌` identifies 104,554 patient/admission keys; 20,052 timed HCC procedure rows occur after day 7 and before discharge (6,779 keys). These counts establish that a temporal comparison can be computed, not that the endpoint is clinically valid.

## Falsifiable test and methods
Compare a snapshot penalized logistic model (age, sex, department, first assay values, time-zero treatment/medication indicators, documentation counts) with a patient-level ordered sequence/state-space model using the same covariates plus within-assay temporal values, time since admission, specimen type, and missingness. Split by `患者主索引`, use nested time-aware validation and repeated bootstrap uncertainty. Evaluate held-out discrimination, calibration, and the incremental difference. Use post-outcome measurements and within-patient order permutation as negative controls. The sequence model can reveal persistence, ordering, and cross-marker interactions that the snapshot loses.

Supportive results require a nontrivial held-out trajectory advantage for the non-routine endpoint, stable across department/documentation/missingness/assay sensitivities, with uncertainty excluding the margin and no timing-shifted-control signal. Adverse results include no improvement, degradation, advantage confined to routine treatment, disappearance after process adjustment, or a positive negative control. Sparse events, unstable dictionary, missing timing, imprecise intervals, or inability to adjudicate clinical meaning are inconclusive.

## Unresolved evidence and limits
The routine-procedure dictionary and resulting non-routine event counts are not frozen in this provisional draft; assay units are absent; untimed procedures are excluded; and no model has been fitted. Clinical experts must adjudicate whether the procedure proxy represents meaningful deterioration. The inherited candidate supplies three inspected abstract-only works, but this draft does not claim their evidence establishes HCC trajectory value or causality. The future solver must freeze the dictionary, fit both models, estimate uncertainty, and link conclusions to computed outputs.
