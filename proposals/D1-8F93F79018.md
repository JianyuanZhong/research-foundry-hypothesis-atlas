# Proposal: procedure-bundle heterogeneity in early post-TACE recorded-care pathways

## Episode, parent, and actual scientific deliverable

This Episode-28 candidate evolves assessed-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]`, which tests whether an early laboratory profile predicts a later *concordant recorded therapeutic episode* (a strict TACE-like procedure plus a same-encounter TACE-like non-drug order), while retaining all eligible index patients, early transitions, observation loss, and procedure-only/ambiguous endpoint tiers.

The remaining clinically consequential uncertainty is not whether a procedure row can be corroborated, but whether the meaning of the early trajectory varies by the recorded index-procedure bundle. The raw HCC procedure table contains plain `TACE`, arterial chemoembolization, catheter/angiography/embolization combinations, and other mixed labels. A single reassessment rule may therefore combine materially different documented workflows. The available data cannot establish drug dose, technical success, lesion burden, treatment intent, or radiologic response; the proposal tests whether record-defined bundle heterogeneity changes the *predictive meaning of subsequent recorded care*, not whether one treatment is clinically superior.

The future solver must newly:

1. Build an auditable index-bundle manifest for every eligible patient, including the literal labels, source, timing, duration-validity flags, and bundle class.
2. Reproduce the parent all-index early-transition and L=14/L=45 endpoint-tier manifests before adding the interaction.
3. Fit the prespecified bundle-by-profile competing-transition models and estimate standardized profile contrasts within bundle class, with patient-level uncertainty, overlap and sparse-cell diagnostics.
4. Fit a matched irregular-time multi-source latent evidence model and compare held-out source prediction/calibration and bundle-specific contrasts under prior sensitivity.
5. Produce locked-test predictions, CIF/risk-difference tables, calibration/log-score summaries, falsification outputs, and an interpretation file linking each conclusion to exact outputs and evidence limits.

Completion is the new fitted/estimated deliverable and its uncertainty/interpretation artifacts; a supportive result is not required.

## Evidence, unresolved question, and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest available evidence is operational, not clinical. The frozen HCC snapshot contains 338,040 procedure rows, of which 322,166 have parseable start times in the inspected raw file, and 105,044 unique encounter rows for 43,815 patients. The procedure labels include 11,612 literal-TACE rows, 58,046 arterial-chemoembolization rows, 49,884 angiography-containing rows, 61,532 catheter-containing rows, and 33,619 labels containing an explicit plus/composite marker under the bounded audit. These counts establish coding and timing heterogeneity, not treatment completion or biological response. The encounter table begins in 2010-07-27 in the inspected snapshot, whereas procedure records extend earlier and into 2026; date eligibility and t0 linkage are therefore explicit.

The unresolved question is:

> Among adults with a structured HCC diagnosis and a first recorded TACE-like index, does the association between an early component laboratory profile and a later concordant recorded therapeutic episode differ by the index record's explicit procedure-bundle class, after standardizing pre-index history and observation opportunity? Or is apparent heterogeneity explained by coding source, measurement opportunity, early competing transitions, or local-care capture?

Primary noncausal hypothesis:

> A favorable early profile (AFP decrease with non-worsening albumin and total bilirubin) will be associated with a lower 320-day probability of a first concordant later recorded therapeutic episode, but the absolute profile contrast will differ between TACE-core, explicitly combined/angiography-documented, and other-strict index bundles, with the largest magnitude in the TACE-core stratum.

The direction and a 3 percentage-point minimum clinically-relevant design margin are prespecified decision criteria, not observed results. The interaction is the substantive target: if bundle-specific contrasts are indistinguishable, a common recorded-care monitoring association is more defensible; if they differ, a single profile rule should not be transported across documented workflows without adjudication. This is a predictive pathway estimand, not a causal effect, response measure, treatment comparison, or utility analysis.

Clinical importance follows from a consequential reassessment decision. A laboratory rule used after a liver-directed procedure should not silently conflate a plain therapeutic record with a composite workflow or with a coding-source artifact. A robust bundle-stratified association could motivate a prospective study that adjudicates procedure completion and imaging response before changing surveillance. A null interaction is also useful: it supports a simpler common recorded-pathway model, subject to the evidence boundary below.

## Population, index, bundle exposure, and temporal boundaries

Use one first eligible timed index episode per patient.

1. Read `procedures` and create primary 24-hour within-patient/within-encounter episodes from `开始时间`. Retain tied rows, all literal labels, `手术来源`, parseability and `结束时间` validity. The candidate index screen is literal `TACE`, `动脉化疗栓塞`, or both `肝动脉` and `栓塞`; hepatic angiography without therapeutic embolization is not an index. The earliest eligible timed episode is t0. Encounter-only episode grouping is a mandatory sensitivity.
2. Link to `encounters` by (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). Require age >=18 and nonmissing `性别`. Require a `diagnoses` row whose `诊断名称` contains `肝细胞癌`, linked to an encounter with `就诊时间` in [t0-180 days, t0+7 days]. Because diagnoses have no native time, this is a diagnosis-ascertainment proxy, not disease onset.
3. Require t0 <= 2025-01-01 for the full 365-day outcome opportunity. Include all eligible patients, including early events, missing trajectories and no later local record. Never condition the primary population on reaching L=14 or L=45.
4. Freeze bundle vocabulary and assignment from training data only. For each index episode, use the full retained label set, not a post-index label. Define:
   - **TACE-core:** at least one literal `TACE` or `动脉化疗栓塞`, no explicit `造影`, `插管`, `导管`, composite plus marker, or alternate liver-directed procedure term in the retained episode labels.
   - **Combined/angiography-documented:** a strict qualifying therapeutic label plus an explicit `造影`, `插管`, `导管`, or plus/composite marker.
   - **Other-strict:** qualifies by the arterial-embolization screen but does not satisfy either preceding class.
   - **Unresolved/mixed:** conflicting labels, invalid time, or labels not safely classifiable; never force into a class.
   These are record morphology strata, not validated treatment types. Report `手术来源` (病案手术 versus 手麻系统 where present), row count, duration-validity and encounter overlap separately; do not treat source or duration as dose, intent or technical success.
5. Use L=14 and L=45 information boundaries. At each L, use the last eligible numeric pre-index value and first numeric value in days 7-L for AFP, albumin and total bilirubin. Freeze exact assay names from training; preserve assay/specimen, qualitative/inequality flags, measurement count, delay and missingness. Define favorable, discordant, other and missing profiles without imputing an unobserved response. There is no lab-unit column, so do not pool undocumented units or call the profile ALBI/MELD.
6. For the parent-compatible endpoint, define mutually exclusive first post-boundary states through day 365: C2 concordant strict procedure plus same-encounter TACE-like order; C1 strict procedure-only; C0 broad/ambiguous/mixed liver-directed record; diagnostic/technical; non-liver; and administrative local-observation loss. The C2 order must share (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`) and have `开立时间` or `开始时间` within -24/+24 hours of the procedure episode. The order is corroborating documentation only. Report +/-6 and +/-72-hour matching and exact observed `医嘱状态` sensitivities.
7. Model all-index transitions from t0 through day 45, including early C2/C1/C0, diagnostic/technical, non-liver and observation-loss states. Separately report the day-45 continuity risk set (no primary-ontology liver-directed episode in (t0,t0+45 days]) as a sensitivity, not as the primary target. The post-boundary outcome window begins after L and ends at 365 days after t0; report CIFs at 30, 90 and 320 days and conditional post-boundary estimates.

## Estimand and analysis

For bundle class b, profile a and endpoint state k, estimate the predictive standardized contrast:

`Delta^L_{k,b}(h) = P(first recorded state k by h | favorable profile, bundle=b, L, standardized pre-index history/capture) - P(first recorded state k by h | discordant profile, bundle=b, L, standardized pre-index history/capture)`.

The primary target is the bundle interaction in `Delta^L_C2(320)), with a predeclared 3-point absolute interaction margin and patient-level 95% interval. Report C1-C2-C0 contrasts, absolute risks, risk ratios only when stable, and the proportion in unresolved/mixed class. Estimate both bundle-specific contrasts and a pooled common-effect model; test whether the interaction is stable at L=14 and L=45. Full-index transition probabilities are a separate vector and must retain early events rather than silently conditioning them away.

Use 2010-2022 fit, 2023 tuning and 2024 through 2025-01-01 locked test when support permits; otherwise deterministic participant-hash splits. No patient crosses splits. Fit bundle vocabulary, assay rules, profile thresholds, standardization, overlap/sparsity rules and any observation weights on training only. Use patient-clustered bootstrap or participant-level resampling for uncertainty. Require bundle-cell counts, event counts, effective sample size, overlap and missingness reports before interpreting an interaction.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Both methods use the same patients, bundle ontology, boundaries, endpoint tiers, temporal split and patient-level uncertainty.

**B_evidence: transparent competing-risk interaction baseline.** Fit regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazards/multi-state models for C2, C1, C0, diagnostic/technical, non-liver and observation-loss states. Predictors include age, sex, index encounter date/department, diagnosis-ascertainment features, pre-index procedure burden and timing, pre-index encounter/lab/order/examination capture, index source and timing, bundle class, the L-specific component values/changes/delays/counts/qualitative flags/missingness, and bundle-by-profile interactions. Exclude the post-index order used to define C2 from predictors for C2. Produce standardized CIF contrasts, coefficients, calibration, Brier score, dynamic log score and clustered intervals.

**M_bundle-latent: substantive irregular-time alternative.** Fit an irregular-time multi-view latent evidence model in which an episode-level latent recorded-therapeutic-care state generates procedure labels, same-encounter orders and optional examination workflow through source-specific timing kernels, missingness/capture intensity and false-positive/sensitivity parameters. Couple this to competing transition hazards and separate latent AFP, albumin and bilirubin trajectories, while allowing partial pooling of bundle-by-profile effects. Fit to the same training target with weakly informative priors or penalization and repeat prior-sensitive fits because no adjudicated gold standard identifies source sensitivities. Evaluate held-out source prediction, calibration, dynamic log score, Brier score, bundle-specific CIF contrasts and interaction intervals—not merely AUROC.

B_evidence is interpretable and directly tests whether explicit bundle stratification changes the recorded pathway association. M_bundle-latent can reveal graded source discordance, asynchronous laboratory information and uncertainty in the unresolved/mixed class that hard bundle labels lose. It is scientifically useful only if it converges, improves held-out source prediction/calibration or materially clarifies interaction uncertainty, and gives stable conclusions under prior sensitivity. It cannot convert the latent state into a validated treatment or response endpoint.

CPU is the default: the baseline is tabular and the latent alternative is a modest repeated likelihood fit. Future solver envelope: at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB memory and 28,800 seconds. These are planning limits, not measured runtimes; no full model was fitted in discovery. If repeated latent optimization materially benefits from an allocated A100, a solver may request one GPU and use `cuda:0`, but ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not evidence and infrastructure/convergence failure must be reported. Discovery and future solver budgets are separate.

## Falsification and interpretation

1. Reproduce the parent all-index early-transition and C2/C1/C0 tier manifests before adding bundle interaction. Mismatch is a packaging failure, not evidence.
2. Use a pre-index placebo profile built from two pre-index laboratory windows. A comparable bundle interaction before t0 supports measurement/coding selection rather than a post-index pathway signal.
3. Use non-liver and diagnostic/technical states as capture controls. Similar bundle-by-profile interactions there weaken disease/pathway specificity.
4. Repeat strict versus broad/mixed tiers, 24-hour versus encounter-only episodes, procedure-source strata, and order windows of +/-6, +/-24 and +/-72 hours. A result confined to one source, tier or window is adverse or requires coding review.
5. Permute assay value-to-time within patient preserving assay/specimen, and separately shift the outcome clock. Persistence after these permutations indicates timing or capture artifact.
6. Compare pooled versus bundle-stratified models and report unresolved/mixed cells. Large attenuation after source/capture covariates, unstable weights, poor overlap or sparse strata is adverse/inconclusive.
7. Repeat complete numeric pairs and qualitative/inequality-retained analyses. No analysis may call the profile response, reserve, treatment-naive status or liver failure.
8. Do not use C2-contributing orders, post-boundary examinations or post-index procedures as predictors for that endpoint. Document order-status and examination-workflow results as sensitivity/context only.
9. Require locked predictions, convergence, calibration, uncertainty and conclusion-to-output links. A complex model cannot rescue a weak endpoint.

Supportive evidence requires: reproducible parent manifests; adequate overlap and bundle-cell support; a prespecified, clinically meaningful bundle interaction at both L boundaries with interval excluding the null interaction and at least the design margin; incremental profile information beyond B_evidence without capture-only explanation; weaker placebo/control/permutation effects; stable endpoint/source sensitivities; and compatible M_bundle-latent estimates.

Adverse evidence includes null or reversed interaction, no incremental profile information, attenuation after capture adjustment, comparable placebo/control effects, source/tier instability, or latent-model disagreement. These findings favor coding/workflow or observation-selection explanations and argue against one recorded-care rule being interpreted as a biologically meaningful post-procedure signal.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse bundle cells, many unresolved/mixed or invalid times, poor positivity/overlap, insufficient follow-up, unstable lexical screens, temporal split failure, nonconvergence or prior-sensitive latent results. It does not establish absence of biological response or treatment benefit.

Computationally checkable claims include source/schema/path access, header and key integrity, leakage-safe manifests, bundle assignment, episode/time rules, CIF calculations, model fitting, calibration, uncertainty, overlap, falsification outputs and links from conclusions to generated tables. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for actual procedure completion, dose, intent, technical success, stage, radiologic/pathologic response, viable tumor, recurrence/progression, liver failure, death, outside care, appropriateness, benefit, utility and external validity.

## Demonstration and seed dispositions; alternatives not selected

The natural-history/Delphi demonstration supports a bounded dated structured-sequence comparison, but its Danish cohort/external validation is unavailable; this proposal uses only local HCC coded records and does not claim reproduction. The Bayesian longitudinal demonstration motivates the irregular-time latent trajectory/evidence alternative, but its genetic analysis is not reproduced because local genetic inputs are unavailable. The accessible Oncoformer supplement supports considering multimodal longitudinal modeling, but the main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and HCC image files are absent; no multimodal reproduction claim is made.

A generic text transformer is deferred because HCC clinical documents have no usable row-level time and no validated response labels. ALBI/MELD and other reserve scores are deferred because laboratory units, INR and validated thresholds are unavailable. Causal treatment-policy analysis, mortality/survival analysis and external validation are deferred because intent, assignment, completion, death and outside-care ascertainment are unavailable. A generic procedure-duration effect is deferred because `结束时间` is frequently missing/zero-duration and is not a validated dose or complexity measure. These are revisit conditions, not blanket method bans.
