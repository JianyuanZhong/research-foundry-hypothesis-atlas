For each direction, the fixed cross-fitted exposure was permuted 2,000 times within joint fold/sex/stable-rank age-decile/stable-rank baseline-outcome-marker-decile strata, retaining frozen outcome covariates. SUPPORTIVE, ADVERSE, AND INCONCLUSIVE LOGIC
Supported now: positive reciprocal observable persistence within the eligible repeat attenders; exact matched P1 renal-minus-control specificity against both selected controls; no adverse sign reversal in stratified permutations or attendance-weighted point estimates.

Not fully supportive: the all-four specificity gate fails because both P2 contrasts include zero after simultaneous/Holm inference; P1 exposure has a small elapsed-time association; attendance has a low-probability tail and lacks joint-bootstrap inference; and field-53 semantics are still uncertified.

Adverse evidence would be a reproducible sign reversal, primary contradiction under a valid diagnostic, simultaneous renal-minus-control contrast below zero where the frozen rule declares adverse, or severe positivity/ESS failure. None was observed here. Similar positive P2 control coefficients weaken reciprocal renal specificity but do not prove artifact.

Inconclusive evidence includes P2 specificity, attendance-weighted inference, MNAR selection, transportability, baseline-center sensitivity absent field-54 certification, and the temporal meaning of fields 53. The full experiment remains formally conditional/inconclusive until authoritative field-53 mapping is frozen. Public searches and official Showcase endpoint attempts did not yield acceptable certification; plausible date formatting/ranges are explicitly insufficient.
