## Result meanings

Supportive requires all parent endpoint/support/calibration/transport rules, supportive raw D1, concordant D1-perp interaction with intervals excluding zero in pooled test and both late eras, positive observed-minus-median-null interaction and Brier intervals, >=2 additional R+D events/1,000 reviewed at a supported quota without overall capture loss, attenuating/opposing reversal, and either G-pre corroboration or persistence in both no-support-change and observation-stable rows. L2-D evidence must not be reproduced by process-only/permuted histories. This supports ordered measured-value information and re-ranking, not mechanism or intervention benefit.

Coupling-adverse requires an informative null that reproduces favorable raw D1, no positive observed-minus-null interaction, Brier upper bound <0.002, no attenuating reversal, quota upper bounds <2/1,000 at every quota, and materially null D1-perp. This rejects the specified order attribution in favor of unordered repeated values plus endpoint coupling/mean reversion under this design.

Process-adverse requires absent/reversed value signal in both no-support-change and observation-stable rows while process-only or active-treatment patterns reproduce calibration, interaction and worklist shifts. This supports a treatment/documentation-associated predictor only.

Inconclusive includes structural or inferential null failure, failed G-perp balance, G-pre coverage <70%, intervals spanning material benefit and harm, unexplained raw/residual conflict, temporal calibration/support failure, endpoint disagreement, or unstable L2-D seeds. An imprecise null and G-pre null alone are not refutation.
