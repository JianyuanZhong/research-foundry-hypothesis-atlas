# Held-out, competing-exit and observation-process test of the frozen eICU respiratory-label estimand

## Status, parents, and substantive advance

This proposal is a substantive, compiler-ready successor of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen scientific schema exactly: the 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct ICU stays, 74 hospitals, first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`, landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission, the two literal care-plan labels, the decision time `D`, inherited exclusions, raw-scale thresholds, fixed windows, and the descriptive noncausal proxy estimand. Repeated landmarks in a stay remain rows. No eligibility, label, `D`, exclusion, or denominator is redefined based on endpoint support.

The strongest claim supported by the existing lineage is limited: the two exact care-plan values are sparse and hospital-concentrated; earlier structured-chart respiratory associations are heterogeneous and vulnerable to peak-pressure and documentation-process effects; and durable bedside liberation cannot be validated from this snapshot. The unresolved claim is clinically consequential but narrower and falsifiable: **after treating death, discharge/transfer, truncation, and respiratory-observation failure as explicit competing/observation states rather than silently censoring them, does the exact evaluation label versus exact no-trial label retain reproducible information about a persistent, safety-preserving respiratory-chart trajectory, and does that information transport to hospitals held out from all fitting and calibration?**

The substantive repair is to make the primary analysis a fixed-horizon, all-row competing-state transport test with a separately identified observation process. A label-associated trajectory is not counted as informative merely because one arm has more measurements or fewer exits. At the frozen horizon, every row is assigned exactly one clinically ordered state: death, non-death discharge/transfer, unclassifiable truncation, persistent observed respiratory improvement, observed non-improvement, or endpoint unobserved without an exit. The label increment is evaluated using a pre-D state-plus-opportunity baseline and a label-added model in leave-one-hospital-out folds. Pre-D opportunity predicts whether the endpoint can be observed; it is used only through training-fitted, bounded inverse-observation weights or an explicitly reported unweighted sensitivity, never through post-D endpoint availability or outcome-derived weighting. All preprocessing, observation models, calibration, thresholds, and weights are fit within the training hospitals of each fold. The primary estimand therefore tests information beyond ascertainment propensity and evaluates transport in genuinely unseen hospitals.

No endpoint, effect, model metric, or computed finding is claimed in this proposal.

## Frozen parser and respiratory endpoint

Before any arm-specific endpoint table, outcome count, model, or gate is read, write and hash `parser_freeze.json`. It must contain the exact label dictionary, numeric-token parser, bounds, windows, tie/duplicate rules, spacing rule, exit precedence, observation-process definitions, and code hash. A full-source inventory may enumerate unmatched labels and source coverage but cannot modify the parser, thresholds, or component set.

The fixed case-sensitive `respiratoryCharting.respchartvaluelabel` map is:

- FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`;
- PEEP: `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`;
- pressure support: `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`;
- set rate: `Vent Rate`, `VS RESP RATE`.

Trim outer whitespace only; do not use substring matching, synonym expansion, case folding, or post-hoc component selection. Peak/plateau/mean airway pressure, pressure-control, peak-flow, spontaneous/total-rate, and oxygen-flow labels are excluded from the primary map. Peak pressure may be retained only as a declared ablation.

For every numeric field, trim then require a complete signed decimal/scientific token matching `^[+-]?(?:\\d+(?:\\.\\d*)?|\\.\\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\\d+)?$`. Reject blanks, units, ranges, inequalities, nonnumeric values, and non-finite values while retaining raw value, parsed value, label, rejection reason, and parser version. Use fixed raw-scale bounds: FiO2 `[21,100]`, PEEP `[0,60]`, pressure support `[0,60]`, set rate `[0,100]`, SpO2 `[0,100]`, respiration `[0,200]`. Values such as `0.4`, `2`, or `3` for FiO2 are `unit_ambiguous_or_invalid`; never silently rescale, interpolate, carry forward, or substitute. The raw-scale decrease threshold is FiO2 at least `0.05`, PEEP at least `2`, pressure support at least `2`, or set rate at least `2`.

Compare the pre-D process window `P=(D-1440,D-720]` with baseline `B=(D-720,D]`. The primary post period is split into early `E=(D+360,D+1080]` and late `T=(D+1080,D+1800]`; the declared single-window secondary is `O=(D+360,D+1800]`. All boundaries are half-open. Every component and safety signal in every required period requires at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. Collapse exact duplicate clinical-time/value rows only after retaining IDs and a deterministic `(clinical_offset, entry_offset, source_id)` ordering. Distinct valid values at one clinical offset create `time_tie_conflict`, not an arbitrary choice. Summaries are medians, with counts, distinct times, first/last offsets, maximum gap, rejected counts, and reasons retained.

A component is persistently reduced only when its B, E, and T medians exist and each post median is no greater than B by the fixed component-specific threshold. Primary safety uses only numeric `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `vitalPeriodic.respiration`: in both E and T, SpO2 is no more than 2 percentage points below B and respiration is no more than 4 breaths/min above B. Missing either safety signal in any required period is missing, not safe. Primary airway corroboration requires at least one O-window `respiratoryCare` row with nonempty `airwaytype` or a genuinely nonmissing ventilator interval field. This is a chart corroboration flag, not proof of a device setting, airway status, or clinician intent. Persistent respiratory improvement requires persistent non-peak reduction, both-window safety pass, and corroboration.

## Explicit competing exits and observation-process states

For every frozen row, classify the outcome at `H=D+1800` in the following precedence order, retaining all secondary reason flags and never dropping rows for differential observability:

1. **Death exit** if `patient.unitdischargeoffset` is nonmissing and `<=H` and trimmed, case-insensitive `unitdischargestatus` equals `Expired`.
2. **Non-death discharge/transfer exit** if `unitdischargeoffset <=H`, status/location is classifiable, and the death rule does not apply.
3. **Truncation exit** if `unitdischargeoffset <=H` but status/location is unclassifiable.
4. **Persistent observed improvement** if no exit occurs by H, the endpoint requirements are complete, and the persistent condition passes.
5. **Observed non-improvement** if no exit occurs by H, all primary endpoint requirements are complete, and the improvement condition fails, including a safety failure.
6. **Endpoint unobserved** if no exit occurs by H but any required repeated component, safety, or corroboration observation is absent or invalid.

Flag exits by `D+360` separately because they precede the post-washout opportunity. A row with an exit by H is never endpoint-unobserved merely because respiratory rows are absent. For endpoint-unobserved rows retain a mutually exclusive first reason and all-reason flags: no pre-D component, no early component, no late component, no safety, no airway corroboration, parser rejection, source absence, time-tie conflict, and source error. Report state and reason by hospital, landmark, and arm only after lineage and parser gates pass.

The six-state vector is not interpreted as six equally desirable clinical outcomes. Death is an adverse competing exit in a descriptive safety interpretation; discharge/transfer is an ascertainment exit whose clinical meaning in this ICU snapshot is unavailable; truncation is data loss/administrative censoring; persistent improvement and observed non-improvement are structured-chart constructs; endpoint unobserved is a missing observation state. The primary model must preserve these distinctions. A sensitivity collapses the three exits only for a formally labeled competing-exit robustness check; it cannot replace the primary six-state analysis.

The primary complete-observed persistent-improvement risk difference is evaluation minus no-trial among rows with no exit and complete endpoint ascertainment, with the denominator, number of exits, and number unobserved shown alongside it. This is a descriptive conditional association and not a causal effect. A cause-specific or cumulative-incidence summary may be reported only as a descriptive secondary with death, non-death exit, truncation, improvement, non-improvement, and unobserved states retained; no claim of survival benefit or liberation is permitted.

## Observation process and held-out transport estimand

The observation process is defined before label comparison. For each frozen row, construct only pre-D opportunity features from the allowed sources: source presence; counts of rows; counts of distinct labels and clinical times; inter-record gaps; maximum gap; pre-D valid/rejected counts by component; and `respchartentryoffset-respchartoffset` lag summaries when both timestamps are pre-D. Do not use post-D rows, endpoint support, exit status, endpoint state, arm-specific rates, or any outcome-derived quantity in this opportunity model. A source-presence feature means documentation opportunity, not clinical normality.

The state-plus-opportunity baseline adds pre-D respiratory medians, slopes or differences, valid/rejected counts, safety state, airway recency, and the frozen opportunity features. The opportunity-only model contains no respiratory values, care-plan strings, label semantics, or post-D fields. The label-added model adds only the frozen binary exact arm to the identical state-plus-opportunity feature set. `hospitalid` and landmark are strata/covariates only; held-out label or outcome rates cannot enter training features.

For each leave-one-hospital-out fold, fit imputation, scaling, selection, regularization, calibration, and the observation model on training hospitals only. The observation model predicts `A=1` for an otherwise non-exiting row whose primary endpoint is ascertainable under the fixed parser, using only pre-D opportunity/state features. It is not allowed to use post-D counts, persistent-improvement indicators, exit information, or arm-specific endpoint rates. Estimate stabilized inverse-observation weights from training data within hospital-by-landmark strata using a prespecified logistic model or equivalent fixed regularized model; stabilize by the training-stratum marginal observation probability; clip probabilities to `[0.05,0.95]` before inversion. Record every clipped prediction and the effective sample size. Apply the frozen training-fitted model and coefficients to the held-out hospital without refitting or using held-out outcome/observation rates. If training positivity fails, if a held-out fold has no supported two-arm contrast, or if weights have effective sample size below 30 rows or below 10% of that fold, mark the weighted estimand unsupported rather than changing the clip or feature set. Report the unweighted all-row six-state model and the weighted ascertainment sensitivity separately; the weighted analysis cannot erase exits or convert missingness into improvement.

Fit a multinomial six-state outcome model (or a prespecified equivalent proper-scoring probabilistic model) at H. Evaluate the label-added and identical state-plus-opportunity baseline on held-out rows using multiclass log loss and multiclass Brier score, both unweighted and with the pre-D observation weights as the declared observation-process sensitivity. The primary transport contrast is baseline minus label-added loss, so positive improvement means lower loss after adding the exact label. Report row-weighted micro metrics and equal-hospital macro metrics, fold sizes in rows/stays/hospitals, state support, arm support, calibration when defined, and predicted-versus-observed state distributions. A fold with one label cannot provide an arm contrast and must not be repaired by relabeling. Repeated landmarks stay together under stay-level resampling; all held-out predictions are generated without training on the held-out hospital.

The observation-process test is informative only if a label increment remains after the state-plus-opportunity baseline and does not appear as an opportunity-only gain. Compare: (i) opportunity-only to state-plus-opportunity, (ii) state-plus-opportunity to label-added, (iii) unweighted to weighted six-state metrics, and (iv) complete-observed persistent-improvement association with the all-row state distribution. A gain confined to endpoint availability, observation density, one exit category, or one source is not supportive of a respiratory trajectory signal.

## Uncertainty, clustering, and falsification

Use a stay-cluster bootstrap with seed `61061`, 1,000 replicates, resampling complete `patientunitstayid` clusters within hospital strata and retaining all landmarks for selected stays while refitting the full held-out pipeline and observation model. A minimum of 500 replicates is required if execution shortfalls occur, and the shortfall must be recorded before results are inspected. A `uniquepid` cluster sensitivity is required. Do not use a pooled row bootstrap as the primary uncertainty estimate.

Permute the exact labels only within hospital-by-landmark strata containing both arms, preserving all rows and arm counts; use 1,000 valid permutations if at least 20 valid strata exist, otherwise report the exact valid-stratum count and mark the permutation gate inconclusive. Permutations must repeat the same held-out scoring pipeline and cannot shuffle exits, endpoint observations, or hospital IDs.

Run with parser, thresholds, and state precedence unchanged:

- pre-D `P` versus `B` placebo;
- lead-time placebo shifting exposure time to `D-720`;
- documentation-density negative control and opportunity-only comparison;
- 60-minute minimum-spacing sensitivity;
- leave-one-component-out and source-family ablations;
- chart-only versus airway-corroborated endpoint variants;
- fixed airway recencies of 360, 1,440, and 4,320 minutes before D;
- single-window `O` secondary versus the persistent E/T primary;
- unweighted versus training-only weighted six-state scoring;
- a sensitivity that separates early exits by `D+360` from exits in `(D+360,H]`.

No sensitivity may alter the frozen cohort, labels, D, primary windows, numeric bounds, or outcome precedence after inspecting results.

## Mechanical supportive, adverse, and inconclusive gates

A computationally **supportive** result requires all of the following:

1. Frozen cohort/action identity, source/schema hashes, parser, and leakage gates pass before arm-aware endpoint summaries.
2. At least five hospitals each have at least 10 non-exit, complete endpoint-observed rows under each label; those hospitals contain at least 50% of complete rows, and no one hospital contributes more than 40% of either arm. The six-state support and exit distributions are reported separately; this gate does not discard unsupported hospitals.
3. The label-added model improves over the identical state-plus-opportunity model in both unweighted and observation-weighted held-out micro log loss and equal-hospital-macro log loss by at least `0.01` in the prespecified direction, with a stay-cluster 95% interval excluding zero. The observation-weighted gain must not be explained by clipping or a fold with effective sample size below the declared threshold.
4. The label increment has the same direction in at least four of five estimable hospital-level contrasts, survives the valid within-hospital-by-landmark permutation null, and is not larger only in the opportunity-only comparison.
5. The complete-observed persistent-improvement association is positive with at least four of five estimable hospital directions positive, while all-row state accounting shows that the result is not produced solely by differential death, discharge/transfer, truncation, or endpoint-unobserved fractions.
6. The label increment persists under component/source ablations and both placebos, and airway corroboration is observed in at least 10% of each arm. This supports only reproducible incremental information in the specified structured-chart construct, not bedside intent, treatment effect, liberation, or benefit.

An **adverse** result requires adequate support but shows no state-plus-opportunity improvement, a label increment compatible with placebo or permutation, hospital-direction reversal, dependence on one component/source/hospital, a gain confined to the opportunity-only or unweighted missingness pattern, or a complete-observed contrast that disappears after explicit exit/state accounting. This is adverse to the frozen predictive claim; it does not prove that clinicians did not evaluate extubation, that labels are artifacts, or that no bedside effect exists.

**Inconclusive** is mandatory for any parent artifact/hash/schema/row-digest mismatch; parser or source failure; fewer than five supported hospitals; no held-out two-arm contrast; insufficient six-state or persistent endpoint support; observation-model positivity or effective-sample-size failure; competing exits too concentrated to distinguish states; severe arm-differential missingness that cannot be separated from state; disagreement across fixed endpoint or observation variants; bootstrap/permutation infeasibility; or uncertainty spanning both zero and the prespecified meaningful increment. A positive single-window result with failed persistent support is transient/non-persistent and not supportive of the primary claim. No relaxed thresholds, post-hoc label expansion, outcome-derived weights, pooled row bootstrap, complete-case-only conclusion, or held-out recalibration is permitted.

## Why this is a meaningful successor

Relative to the assigned parents, the cohort, labels, D, exclusions, windows, thresholds, and descriptive proxy estimand remain frozen. The repair is substantive rather than cosmetic: it prevents favorable-looking trajectories from being created by censoring death, discharge, transfer, or truncation; distinguishes unobserved respiratory documentation from competing exits; and makes observation-process adjustment a pre-D, training-only, held-out operation. The primary decision is now whether the label improves a fixed-horizon joint outcome distribution in unseen hospitals beyond measured respiratory state and opportunity, not whether a complete-case endpoint differs after silently conditioning on its availability. A supportive result would show transportable structured-chart information under explicit competing-state accounting; an adverse result would show that the apparent signal is not robust to observation and exits; and an inconclusive result would preserve the unresolved feasibility rather than force a clinical interpretation. No stronger bedside or causal conclusion is available from these data alone.
