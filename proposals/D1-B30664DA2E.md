# Context-robust fixed-capacity prioritization of lactate-by-perfusion at an ICU landmark

## Status, unresolved question, and scientific advance

This is a substantive successor to assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. No cohort scan, fitted model, prevalence, or clinical result is claimed in discovery.

The strongest available claim is source/design-level: the read-only MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot contains repeated lactate and MAP observations, vasoactive intervals, admission death/discharge fields, ICU support/procedure records, and transfer-unit timestamps from which a prespecified fixed-landmark risk study can be constructed. The audited dictionary identifies lactate 50813 (Lactate/Blood), MAP 220052 (Arterial Blood Pressure mean), 220181 (Non Invasive Blood Pressure mean), 225312 (ART BP Mean), Foley 226559, 11 target vasoactive item IDs, and nine support-procedure item IDs. This does not establish event support, physiological validity, treatment effect, or utility.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among adults alive and observed 12 hours after ICU admission with complete, pre-specified lactate/perfusion coverage, does adding the persistent-lactate-by-measured-perfusion phenotype improve held-out prioritization of record-defined ICU-level transition or timed death within 24 hours at a fixed 5%, 10%, or 20% review capacity—and does that gain persist when prediction is evaluated in an ICU-care-unit/year context not represented in model fitting?

The substantive advance is a context-robust decision estimand. The parent measured pooled fixed-queue capture; this child requires a locked context-shift evaluation using care-unit intervals from `hosp/transfers` and `anchor_year_group` from `hosp/patients`. It therefore tests whether the phenotype is reproducible beyond one pooled documentation mix, while remaining honest that a single MIMIC hospital is not external validation. The primary phenotype-specific contrast remains Bdisc minus the matched order-blind history model B1; Bdisc minus B0 is secondary.

A positive result is still retrospective risk prioritization, not monitoring benefit, treatment benefit, appropriate escalation, preventability, clinician intent, causal lactate/pressor effects, or transportability. Those require adjudicated/prospective or external evidence.

## Population, index, and endpoint

Use the earliest valid ICU stay per `hadm_id` with non-null `subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, intime, outtime` and `intime < outtime), joined to `hosp/admissions` on `subject_id,hadm_id`. The fixed landmark is `t_F=intime+12 hours`, requiring `t_F<outtime` and `dischtime>t_F). Require live status at t_F: `deathtime>t_F), or null `deathtime` with `hospital_expire_flag=0`. Missing flag with no death time is unresolved U, never survival; death by t_F is ineligible. Retain an all-candidate flow and exclusion reason codes.

Features use only `[intime,t_F]), split into `[intime,intime+6h]` and `(intime+6h,t_F]`. Every point measurement has finite clinical time and storetime at or before t_F; interval features have valid clinical start/end, use clinical time for overlap, and require storetime <= t_F. No post-landmark clinical event or terminal field is allowed.

Persistent lactate requires early and late medians of item 50813 >=2.0 mmol/L. Improving perfusion requires late-minus-early MAP >=5 mmHg and late target-pressor minutes no greater than early; worsening requires MAP <=-5 mmHg and pressor minutes no less than early. Missing pressor observation is missing ascertainment, not zero. Discordant/insufficient cases are indeterminate and retained descriptively, not merged. The 4.0 mmol/L threshold, MAP-only, all-MAP and pressor-only rules are sensitivity analyses.

Follow `(t_F,t_F+24h]) and order the first state: R is a new post-landmark target-pressor interval, a qualifying `icu/procedureevents` start (intubation 224385, invasive ventilation 225792, non-invasive ventilation 225794, hemodialysis 225441, CRRT 225802/225803/225805/225809/225955), or a later ICU stay for the same subject/admission with a different stay_id and intime>t_F. Active intervals crossing t_F are not new starts. D is `hosp/admissions.deathtime` in the window and no later than tied R, respecting discharge. S is an alive discharge boundary before R/D; N is ascertainable no R/D/S by 24 hours; U is unresolved death/ordering/ascertainment. If R and D tie, D precedes R. A=R-or-D is the adverse target; R, D, S, N and U are always reported separately. R is explicitly record-defined, not clinical rescue.

## Models, context-shift estimand, and evaluation

Freeze one subject-level 70/15/15 split by subject_id before any outcome inspection. The strict-gate E* excludes U for primary estimable scoring; all-E audit retains U and reports adverse-U/non-adverse-U bounds. Fit:

- B0: transparent penalized discrete-time cause-specific R/D/S model using admission context, demographics, first-careunit, transfer-careunit at t_F, elapsed ICU time, terminal valid organ/lactate/MAP state, support status, masks and counts.
- B1: matched order-blind history baseline using twelve one-hour bins of the same physiologic, organ, support and process domains (medians/ranges/variability, valid-bin/distinct-time counts, testing/Foley/pressor process), without early/late order or phenotype terms.
- Bdisc: B1 plus only persistent-lactate, improving/indeterminate/worsening perfusion, early/late lactate/MAP/pressor terms and their interaction.
- J1: small masked chronological GRU with six two-hour bins, the same input domains, three-cause hazard head, hidden size 32/64, dropout, early stopping and five seeds selected on train/validation only. It tests whether ordered persistence/reversal and nonlinear coupling discarded by summaries contain information; it cannot rescue a failed Bdisc claim.

The primary pooled estimand is fixed-k A capture at 10%: TP_A(Bdisc,k_10)/#A_test minus TP_A(B1,k_10)/#A_test, with k_w=max(1, round-half-up(w|E*_test|)) at w=5%,10%,20%; identical k per model and tie-breaking frozen before test scoring. Report random-allocation capture/PPV benchmark, PPV, false-alert rate, calibration intercept/slope/O:E, Brier, R/D capture, S/N/U and subject bootstrap intervals. The prespecified pooled criterion is >=5 percentage points with an interval excluding zero, Bdisc beating the random benchmark and Bmask, and no failure in timing/permutation/U-bound/R-D checks.

The new context-shift estimand is a locked leave-one-context-out evaluation. Define context c by the transfer-careunit covering t_F crossed with anchor_year_group, after grouping cells below a pre-fit minimum support into an explicitly named pooled context. For each eligible context c with adequate train events, fit on E*_train subjects outside c (validation remains outside c), then score only subjects in c with the same frozen feature whitelist, k rule, 10% capacity and no context-specific tuning. Report macro- and event-weighted Bdisc-minus-B1 capture across supported contexts, plus R/D capture and calibration. Context support, overlap and an effective sample-size gate are computed before outcomes are used to select cells; unsupported contexts are reported not silently pooled. This is internal context robustness, not external validation. A supportive context claim requires the pooled criterion and a predeclared majority of supported contexts with non-reversed direction and no material calibration failure; otherwise the pooled result is reported without robustness generalization.

## Method selection and compute

The transparent B0/B1/Bdisc comparison is the primary scientific instrument because its terms expose the phenotype and allow recalibration. J1 is a substantive same-input alternative: chronological bins can reveal persistence, reversal and time-local interaction that order-blind summaries lose. It is deferred as a sensitivity if strict support fails, not because neural methods or GPUs are disallowed. A latent mixture, large transformer and treatment-effect model are deferred: absent adjudicated stabilization, validated physiological labels, image/waveform inputs, treatment intent and counterfactual support, they would not answer this question.

Measured facts are the catalog/schema/archive/member/dictionary audits. Strict-gate prevalence, event counts, context support, overlap, calibration, convergence, bootstrap time and model runtime are unmeasured.
