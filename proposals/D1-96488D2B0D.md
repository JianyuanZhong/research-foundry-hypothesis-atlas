# HCC Episode-15 child: documentation-balanced endpoint coherence at day 42

Status: design only; no cohort count, fitted coefficient, event rate, loss, or clinical result is claimed. Parent: [prior hypothesis].

## Scientific opening and advance

A literal repeat TACE code may be a coding shortcut. Adding a semantic hepatic-arterial procedure label and a same-visit examination/order record could make a repeat-treatment episode more operationally coherent, but the same corroborators can instead measure which services document intensively. This is the remaining boundary in the inherited day-42 trajectory question. The clinical value is decision-relevant: if a laboratory trajectory survives a documentation-balanced, corroborated endpoint, it is worth investing in intent/scheduling and imaging linkage; if it disappears, a model of local documentation or observation is the more defensible next target.

The strongest available evidence is bounded. [K1] reports treatment-adjacent prediction in a selected repeat-TACE HCC cohort with external testing, but does not validate this EHR code or assay trajectory. [K2] establishes that clinical treatment response is an imaging/necrosis-aware construct, not a procedure or laboratory code. [K3] illustrates that recorded imaging outcomes and treatment comparisons are sensitive to ascertainment, undocumented outcomes, exposure classification, and treatment selection. None establishes that O1/O2 is treatment intent, response, benefit, or a mechanism.

The unresolved claim is narrow and falsifiable:

> Among the inherited first-index TACE population with valid day-42 observation, the pre-index-to-day-42 laboratory trajectory improves held-out prediction of a later O1 operational repeat-treatment episode beyond baseline, pre-landmark observation/care-process intensity, and the day-42 snapshot; the increment is preserved after fold-frozen R42 observation weighting and after standardizing to pre-event documentation opportunity. A stricter O2 medication corroboration is supportive only if its exact-field availability gate passes and its direction is concordant.

This is a predictive/descriptive claim about recorded operational episodes. It does not identify treatment intent, imaging response, causal effect, survival, benefit, or external validity.

The substantive Episode-15 advance is the documentation-balanced boundary test. O1 is still defined using only structured procedure/examination/order fields, visit keys, and timestamps; no examination findings, diagnosis narratives, clinical documents, pathology text, or outcome-derived narrative search is permitted. Before any later event is scored, freeze a pre-landmark documentation vector D42 from source availability/count/timing/process marks through day 42, excluding endpoint dictionaries and all post-day-42 evidence. Report the O1/O2 trajectory increment overall and within/follow-up-standardized D42 strata. If the increment is present only in high-D42 strata or is matched by a predeclared documentation-only process block, that is adverse to a coherent assay-trajectory interpretation and compatible with documentation intensity. This is an observational transport/measurement audit, not causal adjustment.

## Population, estimand, and time boundaries

Use the inherited eligible index set. For each patient, t0 is the earliest valid procedures row with NFKC-normalized, trimmed, case-folded procedure == tace, nonmissing patient master index, encounter number, and valid start time. Collapse exact patient/start duplicates for event counting while retaining all duplicates and raw-label hashes in an audit. No future procedure or endpoint evidence enters predictors.

Construct obs_end as the latest valid timestamp from the frozen source time fields listed below. It marks local observation coverage, not survival; the snapshot has no reliable death field and cannot separate outside care from absence of a local row. R42=1 if obs_end >= t0+42 days. Fit R42 selection models over the complete eligible index set within each development fold, then analyze R42=1 patients. The target is the inherited eligible index population under a fold-frozen observation transport analysis, not an assertion that inverse weighting recreates unobserved care.

Primary follow-up is day 43 through but excluding day 181. Stop at the first qualifying event, obs_end, or t0+181. Report fixed [43,90] and [91,181) sensitivities. A timed semantic or literal procedure in (t0,t0+43) is an early-alias audit and not a later-event negative. Untimed rows are unsupported, never imputed.

Use the frozen patient-disjoint chronological split: earliest 80% of eligible index dates for development and latest 20% for untouched test; date ties resolve by patient index order. Use five grouped development folds. The untouched test is scored once.

The primary nested estimand for endpoint j is D_snap,j = L(A_snap42,j) - L(A_traj42,j), with D_base,j = L(A0,j) - L(A_traj42,j) reported. Positive values mean the trajectory lowers held-out loss relative to the named comparator. The documentation-balanced version reports the same paired contrast after standardizing test predictions to prespecified D42 strata; it is not a new causal estimand.

## Frozen endpoint hierarchy

Normalize only labels used in the dictionaries by NFKC, case-fold, trim, remove spaces and full-width punctuation. Preserve raw-value hashes and match flags. Never inspect outcome-derived narrative text.

O0 is the anchor: the first distinct-start later procedures row whose normalized surgery is exactly tace, with valid start time in [t0+43,t0+181).

Define P_sem on procedures. Surgery as exact normalized tace, or a label containing hepatic artery and embolization, at least one of chemotherapy or infusion, and none of radiofrequency, microwave, ablation, biopsy, puncture, drainage. A P_sem row is still only a procedure record.

O1 is the first P_sem row at 43 <= t-t0 < 181 with (i) same Patient Master Index and Encounter Number, plus a valid encounter link, and (ii) at least one structured same-visit corroborator:

- examinations. examination matches the frozen liver/hepatic-artery or upper-abdomen imaging-type dictionary and valid start time is in [t-42,t+7]; or
- orders.medical orders (non-medication) matches exact TACE or hepatic-arterial embolization/chemotherapy/contrast order terms and valid order issue time (availability) or valid start time is in [t-42,t+7].

A valid encounter link is encounters.encounter time within two elapsed days of t, or valid admission time <= t <= discharge time; missing discharge makes the admission route false. Event time is always procedure start time, never an earlier order/examination.

O2 is O1 plus a same-patient/visit medications.medication-use row mapped by a frozen exact-value dictionary, with valid start time in [t-1,t+1]. O2 is enabled only if a pre-fit audit confirms nonmissing exact medication values, valid time and visit keys, and a non-fuzzy, non-outcome-derived mapping. Drug type, medication route, dose, unit and end time are audited but do not establish indication. If this gate fails, O2 is unavailable/not estimable, not a negative and not a multiplicity null.

A P_sem or literal row lacking O1 corroboration is a near miss. With complete follow-up it is O1-negative; with incomplete observation it is censored/unsupported according to the frozen rules. A literal O0-positive/O1-negative case is retained, not silently dropped. Same patient/start duplicates collapse after retaining row counts. Corroborator-only visits never create an event. Primary corroboration uses [t-42,t+7]; preprocedure-only [-42,-1], [-28,-1], and [-7,-1] are fixed lead-time/documentation sensitivities. The +7 component is never predictor information.

Before fitting, write near_miss_early_alias_audit.csv, stratified by split and endpoint, with literal rows failing O1, P_sem rows failing each linkage/window reason, corroborator-only visits, O1 rows lacking O2 support, unsupported medication mappings, duplicates/conflicting labels, untimed rows, aliases, early procedures, and event counts for every corroboration window. Do not use raw clinical text in the exported audit.

## Documentation-balance test

Define D42 before scoring outcomes and before endpoint dictionaries are applied. It contains only pre-index through day-42 source opportunity and care-process marks: per-source row-presence/counts, valid-time indicators, encounter/order/examination/medication opportunity counts, lab count/missingness/timing indicators, and P_care workflow summaries. It excludes procedure labels used by O0/O1/O2, corroborator match flags, all records after t0+42, narrative findings/diagnoses/documents/pathology, future events, and outcome-derived text. Freeze D42 construction and bins in development only.

Primary analysis fits the same nested elastic-net hazards for O0, O1, and enabled O2, with A0=M0+B0+P_obs+P_care, A_snap42=A0+S42, and A_traj42=A0+T42. The documentation-balanced report has three predeclared checks:

1. Report D_snap,j overall and within development-frozen D42 quintiles; standardize test predictions to the development D42 distribution. A positive simultaneous lower bound in all supported strata is the coherent-information pattern. A gain confined to a high-documentation stratum is a documentation-dependence warning, not a positive replication.
2. Compare the trajectory increment with the process-only gain from M0 to A0 and with a D42-only block added to M0. If the process/D42 block matches the trajectory increment within the frozen 0.005 log-loss audit resolution and uncertainty, retain only workflow-associated prediction.
3. Replace broad O1 corroboration by preprocedure-only windows and separately remove late predictor records in (t0+35,t0+42]. If O1 is erased when post-procedure corroboration is removed, or when late documentation is removed, the result is compatible with lead-time/documentation intensity.

These checks assess endpoint coherence and measurement opportunity, not treatment intent. A matched timestamp/content permutation within development-frozen patient/assay strata is an additional falsifier: a matched increment supports generic measurement volume/timing rather than assay trajectory.

## Observation selection

Fit R42 within each development fold over all eligible indices. Numerator uses M0, baseline assays, pre-index P_obs and pre-index P_care. Denominator adds only non-content process marks through day 28: source/encounter row availability, dated lab/order/examination/medication opportunities, and valid-time indicators. Exclude all O0/O1/O2 evidence, endpoint labels, future events, post-day-28 assay values, and post-day-42 fields. Apply stabilized SW42 = Pr(R42=1|numerator)/Pr(R42=1|denominator) to held-out R42=1 patients. Clip at development-fold 1st/99th percentile limits frozen before test scoring; report tails. More than 5% clipping, inadequate overlap, or weighted ESS <100 is a selection-support failure and makes weighted inference inconclusive. R28 is a fixed day-28 comparison, not evidence of biological equivalence.

## Outcomes, uncertainty, and falsification

For each endpoint and block report paired held-out log loss, Brier/integrated Brier, calibration, discrimination, event/risk-set and censor counts, R42/R28 coverage, O0/O1/O2 concordance, D42-stratum support, weight tails, and ESS. Use patient-level paired bootstrap on held-out predictions and one max-|t| family across primary endpoint contrasts, O0/O1/O2, ordinary/SW42, D42-standardized reports, and fixed falsifiers. Preserve unsupported strata as unsupported, not nulls.

Supportive requires O1 (and O0 anchor) to have adequate events/support and overlap; the simultaneous lower bound for D_snap,O1 is positive in ordinary and SW42 analyses; the direction survives D42 standardization across supported strata, preprocedure corroboration, day-28 and late-record controls; process/D42 and permutation controls do not match; and the transparent result is directionally reproduced by the matched learned sensitivity if feasible. O2 may reinforce but cannot rescue O1.

Adverse means O1 loses its increment after D42 standardization/selection weighting, only O0 supports the claim, O1 is erased by preprocedure restriction or late-record removal, or documentation/process/permutation controls match it. This narrows the recorded trajectory interpretation; it does not show assays lack prognostic value. Inconclusive means O1/O2 support, events, overlap, ESS, censoring, or strata precision fail, or a negative cannot be distinguished from missing observation. Do not change dictionaries, strata, windows or weights after results.

Claims about intent, imaging response, treatment benefit, mechanism, survival, outside care, or transport require treatment scheduling/intent fields, linked imaging and expert adjudication, validated medication indication, assay units/reference ranges, reliable death/outside-care capture, or an independent study.
