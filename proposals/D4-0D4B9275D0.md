# Grip-triggered cystatin C testing: a policy-specific clinical-yield experiment

## Unresolved question, clinical decision, and hypothesis

Creatinine-based eGFR can look reassuring when creatinine generation is low. Cystatin C can expose this discordance, but universal testing is not the decision addressed here. The decision is whom to test when a clinic can order cystatin C for only 20% of adults whose baseline race-free creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) is 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m².

The strongest evidence currently supports that downward reclassification by cystatin C is prognostic and that creatinine–cystatin differences track muscle mass and frailty; it does not establish measured GFR, a benefit from testing, or the best selective-testing rule. The inherited parent inspected full text for Shlipak et al. (NEJM 2013, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1214234; frozen source c4353d6f…), Potok et al. (AJKD 2020, DOI 10.1053/j.ajkd.2020.05.018; frozen source 02931ae9…), and Yim et al. (Front Med 2022, PMCID PMC9747759; reacquired full-text XML source e7f3681c…). The last was a 138-person hospitalized cohort proposing muscle-mass indications and lacked measured GFR, clinical-outcome validation, and external validation. These sources support the premise, not the policy claim.

**Primary joint hypothesis.** In an untouched UK Biobank test set and at an identical 20% assay budget, a targeting model that adds measured hand-grip strength to age, sex, BMI, and eGFRcr will, relative to the same model without grip:

1. detect at least 25% more participants per assay whose 2021 CKD-EPI combined creatinine–cystatin eGFR (eGFRcr-cys) is below 60 despite eGFRcr 60–89; and
2. detect at least 25% more participants per assay who both meet that laboratory reclassification and subsequently have a first recorded AKI hospitalization within 10 years.

For each ratio, the paired-bootstrap 95% confidence interval must lie wholly above 1.00. Both conditions must pass. The second endpoint is a policy-specific clinical-yield estimand: unlike the parent's pooled hazard ratio in the union selected by either model, it asks whether the actual additional allocation produces more reclassified people who later manifest kidney vulnerability. It remains prognostic and does not imply that testing or treatment prevents AKI.

## Variables and equations

Convert creatinine from µmol/L to mg/dL by dividing by 88.4. Compute race-free 2021 CKD-EPI eGFRcr as

`142 × min(Scr/k,1)^alpha × max(Scr/k,1)^−1.200 × 0.9938^age × 1.012(if female)`,

where `k=0.7, alpha=−0.241` for females and `k=0.9, alpha=−0.302` for males.

Compute 2021 CKD-EPI eGFRcr-cys as

`135 × min(Scr/k,1)^alpha × max(Scr/k,1)^−0.544 × min(Scys/0.8,1)^−0.323 × max(Scys/0.8,1)^−0.778 × 0.9961^age × 0.963(if female)`,

where `alpha=−0.219` for females and `−0.144` for males. The target is eGFRcr-cys <60. Secondary discordance is 2012 eGFRcys/eGFRcr ≤0.70, using the published 2012 cystatin equation.

Grip is maximum valid left/right baseline grip in kg; use the sole hand if only one is valid. Mean-hand and minimum-hand definitions are sensitivity analyses. BMI is kg/m². Public field metadata/units used at compilation must be frozen; local metadata supplies approved labels only for sex and age and forbids treating instance number as elapsed time.

## Targeting rules and locked test

Fit only to the development set, with no AKI outcome in training:

- **B, clinical baseline:** age, sex, BMI, eGFRcr.
- **G, grip augmented:** B plus maximum grip and sex×grip.

Continuous variables use restricted cubic splines with knots at development-set 5th, 35th, 65th, and 95th percentiles. Ridge penalty is chosen by five-fold participant-level cross-validation inside development. Freeze all preprocessing, knots, coefficients, penalty, and complete-case rules before opening the confirmatory test set.

In the test set, each model selects exactly `floor(0.20×N)` participants with highest predicted target probability. Break score ties by a third namespace-separated SHA-256 hash of `eid`. Report overlap and the equal-sized swap sets G-only and B-only. Also benchmark random 20% (analytic expected prevalence plus repeated deterministic draws), oldest-first 20%, and lowest-eGFRcr-first 20%. Universal testing is a cost reference.

For policy S, define:

- laboratory yield `Y_T(S)=1000 × sum[I(selected by S)×T]/n_assayed`;
- clinically consequential yield `Y_A(S)=1000 × sum[I(selected by S)×T×A]/n_assayed`, where A is first N17* before death within 10 years.

Primary estimands are `R_T=Y_T(G)/Y_T(B)` and `R_A=Y_A(G)/Y_A(B)`, plus paired absolute differences per 1,000 assays. Obtain percentile and BCa paired participant-bootstrap intervals from 2,000 resamples, refitting neither frozen model. If a denominator is zero, the ratio is not reported as infinity; use the paired difference and classify the ratio gate inconclusive.

Decompose both differences into G-only gains minus B-only losses. Report target sensitivity, PPV, assay counts, and target-model calibration slope/intercept. For AKI, report Aalen–Johansen 10-year cumulative incidence with death competing in target-positive and target-negative participants for G-only, B-only, overlap, and full eligible test cohort. Cause-specific Cox and Fine–Gray models adjusted for age, sex, BMI, and eGFRcr are explanatory secondary analyses; no adjusted HR may replace either policy gate.

Stop and classify the second gate inconclusive if there are fewer than 100 target-positive 10-year AKI events in the union selected by G or B or fewer than 20 in either unique swap set. Do not change the assay budget, population, target, or outcome after seeing this count.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** in the locked test set both R_T and R_A are at least 1.25, both paired 95% CIs are wholly above 1.00, their paired absolute differences are positive, event-count rules pass, and target calibration slope is 0.8–1.2. This supports prospective evaluation of grip-informed cystatin allocation.

**Adverse:** R_T≤1 means grip fails to improve laboratory targeting. R_T may pass while R_A≤1 or its CI excludes a clinically meaningful gain; that means grip finds more biochemical reclassification without demonstrating greater clinically consequential yield, falsifying the joint hypothesis. Lower/equal AKI incidence among G-only target positives, or disappearance after two-year lag, argues against kidney-vulnerability interpretation. Marked nonpersistence is adverse secondary evidence.

**Inconclusive:** point estimates favor grip but either CI crosses 1, either gain is <25%, event thresholds fail, calibration is poor, a denominator is zero, linkage uncertainty dominates, or results depend on one grip/target definition. “Not significant” with a wide interval is not equivalence.

A verifier can check source hashes and columns, joins, hash partitions, exact equations, chronology, code/date pairing, equal budgets and ties, frozen model isolation, yields, bootstrap intervals, event thresholds, and whether the narrative follows the gate. Fixtures must include: correct R_T support with adverse R_A; both supportive computations followed by an unsupported claim that testing prevents AKI; and wide intervals requiring “inconclusive.”

A verifier cannot establish that eGFRcr-cys is measured GFR, that N17* is adjudicated AKI, complete person-level hospital capture, assay economics, medication-dose safety, causality, transportability, or treatment benefit. Those require measured-GFR data, chart adjudication, country/person linkage review, economic inputs, nephrology review, and ultimately a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.
