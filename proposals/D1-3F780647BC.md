# Proposal: observation-selection repair of plan- and reassessment-context post-TACE pathways

## Episode, parent, lineage, and scientific deliverable

This Episode-25 child evolves the assigned assessed-valid parent \`[prior hypothesis]\` (Episode 24). It preserves that parent’s exact HCC population, encounter-constrained timed TACE-like index, day-45 landmark and risk set, strict-versus-broad pathway ladder, recorded-plan proxy \(P\), reassessment proxy \(R\), temporal split, clustered uncertainty, and noncausal evidence boundary. The earlier component-resolved AFP/albumin/total-bilirubin design remains the scientific target.

The substantive repair is measurement-selection transport. The parent adjusts for laboratory timing, counts, contact intensity and capture, but its \(A_F\) versus \(A_D\) contrast is still primarily identified among patients with a usable pre-index and day-7–45 panel. A clinician may order that panel because a patient is easier to observe, more symptomatic, or already returning for planned care. Thus a favorable recorded component pattern could be associated with the later recorded pathway because of who was measured, not because the recorded trajectory is transportable to the full eligible post-TACE risk set. This is a clinically consequential ambiguity for deciding whether a record-derived pattern is suitable for follow-up prioritization.

The parent’s plan/reassessment question is retained as a secondary context explanation: among the same full eligible cohort, does any trajectory association differ by a recorded future-plan proxy, and is the later strict TACE record preceded by a locally recorded reassessment workflow? \(P=0\) still means only no qualifying local order was found, never “unplanned.” \(R\) remains a descriptor of the observed later event, not a causal mediator or radiologic truth.

The actual solver deliverable is newly fitted selection-aware, context-stratified estimates on the parent’s locked cohort:

1. reconstruct the parent cohort and exact \(P\), \(R\), procedure episodes, assay states and competing recorded-care outcomes;
2. define the paired-panel observation indicator \(O\), audit its missingness and fit a training-only observation/transport model;
3. estimate the unweighted observed-panel contrast and the primary observation-transported full-eligible-cohort contrast for strict later TACE, overall and by \(P\);
4. compare the transparent weighted model with an observation-only/capture model and a substantive irregular-time latent alternative targeting the same estimand;
5. run selection, timing, plan-context, placebo, control-pathway and permutation falsifications;
6. return patient-clustered uncertainty, positivity/effective-sample-size diagnostics, calibration and a conclusion-to-output interpretation manifest.

No model has been fitted in discovery, and no clinical result is claimed.

## Strongest supported claim and unresolved question

The strongest available evidence supports data structure and local observability only: the frozen HCC snapshot has 12 ordinary CSV sources joined through \((patient master index, encounter number)\); procedures and labs have usable timestamps; the order source has dated non-drug order text and status fields; and the examination source has dated examination names plus narrative fields. The live headers and linked schemas confirm the fields used below. The parent’s bounded order scan verified term and timestamp availability, not population prevalence or clinical meaning.

The unresolved claim is not that a recorded \(A_F\) pattern is a biological response. The testable claim is narrower: after standardizing to the full eligible cohort’s pre-index and day-45 recorded observation/capture profile, the favorable-versus-discordant component contrast in the later strict recorded TACE pathway persists, and its magnitude/context is not explained by the paired-lab observation mechanism. This is a predictive, noncausal transport claim about local records.

The advance over the parent is an explicit estimand for the population to which a clinical surveillance rule would be applied. It separates (i) an observed-panel association, (ii) an observation-transported association for all eligible patients under a stated conditional observation assumption, and (iii) a latent irregular-time sensitivity analysis that uses incomplete measurement histories. The parent’s plan-positive/plan-negative and reassessment-proxied explanations remain intact rather than being conflated with missing laboratory observation.

## New estimand: observation-selection transport

Retain the parent’s exact assay rules. Let \(E\) be every adult, nonmissing-sex patient with a linked, eligible first index and no primary-ontology liver-directed therapeutic episode through day 45, with the parent’s \(t_0\le\) 2025-01-01 rule. Let \(O=1\) if the parent’s required exact-name AFP, albumin and total-bilirubin values are all usable: last numeric pre-index value and first numeric value in days 7–45, with the parent’s assay/specimen, qualitative/inequality flags and missingness rules. \(O=0\) remains in \(E\); it is not imputed to a clinical state.

Let \(X\) contain only information available by day 45 and already permitted by the parent: age, sex, index calendar time, department/admission/discharge fields, diagnosis-proxy history, pre-index assay availability and timing, non-lab contact/order/examination opportunity, procedure-source mix, coding density, ambiguity indicators, and \(P\). For primary overall estimates, standardize over all \(E\); for context estimates, standardize within \(P=0\) and \(P=1\). Do not use any post-day-45 field. Fit \(\pi(X)=Pr(O=1\mid X)\) in the training years only, assess calibration and overlap, and use prespecified stabilized inverse-observation weights with \(\pi\) clipped to [0.05, 0.95] only as a sensitivity rule. The untrimmed estimate and the common-support target \(E^\*\) must both be reported; trimming does not justify a claim about excluded patients.

Let \(Y_k(t)\) be the cumulative incidence of first mutually exclusive recorded state \(k\) from day 46 through day \(t\), with strict later TACE as the primary \(k\), broad/diagnostic/mixed/ambiguous/non-liver states and administrative local-observation loss as competing states. For \(p\in\{0,1\}\), define the parent observed-panel estimand
\[
\Delta^{obs}_p(t)=F_{strict,p}(t\mid A_F,O=1)-F_{strict,p}(t\mid A_D,O=1)
\]
and the new primary transport estimand
\[
\Delta^{E}_p(t)=\sum_x Pr(X=x\mid E,P=p)\{F_{strict,p}(t\mid A_F,X,O=1)-F_{strict,p}(t\mid A_D,X,O=1)\},
\]
estimated with the observation weights (or equivalent standardization) learned only in training. In continuous/high-dimensional \(X\), report the fitted standardization integral and effective sample size rather than pretending it is a randomized effect. The primary report is \(\Delta^E_p(320)\) and its interaction \(\Delta^E_0(320)-\Delta^E_1(320)\); \(\Delta^{obs}\), the original unstratified parent contrast and the common-support version are continuity/sensitivity outputs.

This is an association transported under a conditional observation/transport assumption, not a causal estimand. It does not identify an unrecorded laboratory value, TACE intent, technical completion, radiologic response, progression, death, or benefit. If positivity or the observation assumption is not credible from available fields, the valid result is an identified observed-panel association or an inconclusive transport analysis, not a biological conclusion.

## Falsifiable hypothesis and estimands

Retain the parent assay-specific, training-only component states. Let \(A_F\) be AFP decrease with non-worsening albumin and non-worsening total bilirubin, and \(A_D\) be AFP decrease with worsening albumin or total bilirubin. These are recorded assay-standardized categories, not response, reserve, or clinical-unit labels.

Define a recorded future-plan proxy \(P=1\) when any qualifying non-drug order is created after \(t_0\) and through \(t_0+45\) days, using the order’s \`order creation time\`, for the same patient and any linked encounter. The primary plan-specific lexical screen is frozen from fitting data: literal \`TACE\`, \`transarterial chemoembolization\`, or a combination of \`hepatic artery\` with \`embolization\`/therapeutic \`chemotherapy\`; imaging-only \`CTA\`/\`CTV\`, angiography-only, and \`intervention\` alone are not plan-specific and are retained as broad-order controls. Raw order status is retained but does not define completion. A canceled order can still be evidence that a plan was recorded; a non-canceled-status sensitivity is mandatory.

Define a dated reassessment-workflow proxy \(R=1\) for a strict post-day-45 TACE episode at time \(t_e\) if, in the open interval \((t_e-30\text{ days},t_e)\), there is a qualifying liver/upper-abdominal examination or non-drug assessment order. The primary proxy uses only the examination/order name and service/order time, not report interpretation. The qualifying name screen requires a liver/upper-abdominal/portal term and a CT, MRI/magnetic-resonance, ultrasound, enhancement, or contrast term; ambiguous names are retained separately. Narrative `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis` are sensitivity text fields only and are not radiologic adjudication.

The primary hypothesis is a selection-aware interaction:

> After observation transport to the full eligible cohort, \(\Delta^E_0(320)\) remains directionally lower and materially larger in magnitude than \(\Delta^E_1(320)\), with the prespecified target \(\Delta^E_0(320)\le -0.03\) and \(\Delta^E_0(320)-\Delta^E_1(320)\le -0.02\), subject to interval, positivity and common-support criteria. The transported contrast should remain compatible with the parent’s plan-negative/reassessment-proxied interpretation and should not be materially different from \(\Delta^{obs}\) after accounting for observation.

Here \(\Delta_p(t)=F_{\mathrm{TACE\_strict},P=p}(t\mid A_F)-F_{\mathrm{TACE\_strict},P=p}(t\mid A_D)\), estimated as a predictive, noncausal contrast on the same common-support distribution of baseline/capture covariates and schedule \(S\). Estimate the original unstratified parent contrast as the primary continuity output. Report \(\Delta\) for mutually exclusive first states: \`TACE_strict_plan_positive\`, \`TACE_strict_plan_negative\`, and, where event support permits, \`TACE_strict_reassessment_proxied\` and \`TACE_strict_unanchored\`. The reassessment split is a context descriptor of the already observed event, not a causal mediator analysis.

The plan-positive result is not itself “bad”: a trajectory contrast concentrated there would support a serial-plan explanation and weaken the claim that the trajectory identifies a newly emerging pathway. A plan-negative result is not an unplanned-treatment or progression result because absent orders may reflect missing capture.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

All models use the same patients, \(t_0\), day-45 information boundary, outcomes, temporal split, common-support rules, and locked test.

- \(B_0\): the parent history-only regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazard model.
- \(B_{\mathrm{time}}\): the parent schedule/capture baseline with measurement timing, assay availability/counts, contact intensity, examination/order opportunity, procedure-source mix, coding density, and ambiguous-label indicators, but no laboratory values.
- \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\): \(B_{\mathrm{time}}\) plus \(P\), raw order-status categories, order-to-index timing, and broad-order opportunity; no laboratory values. This tests whether plan/capture context alone explains the parent contrast.
- \(B_{\mathrm{traj}}\): \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\) plus separate assay-standardized component states, delays, missingness and uncertainty.
- \(B_{\mathrm{context}}\) (primary transparent test): \(B_{\mathrm{traj}}\) plus frozen component-profile-by-\(P\) interaction and prespecified plan-positive/plan-negative CIF contrasts; \(R\) is used only to classify the observed TACE context and is not used as a pre-event predictor of the same endpoint.
- \(B_{\mathrm{obs}}\) (observed-panel baseline): \(B_{\mathrm{context}}\) fitted only among \(O=1\), without observation weights. It estimates \(\Delta^{obs}\) and makes the parent’s selection target explicit.
- \(B_{\mathrm{IPO}}\) (primary transparent baseline): the same regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazard/CIF model as \(B_{\mathrm{context}}\), including the exact component states and \(P\)-interactions, fitted among \(O=1\) with training-frozen stabilized inverse-observation weights and standardized to \(E\). It tests transport, not a treatment effect.
- \(B_{\mathrm{no-lab}}\): the parent schedule/plan model fitted on all \(E\), with no lab values. If it reproduces the \(B_{\mathrm{obs}}\) or \(B_{\mathrm{IPO}}\) contrast, capture/selection rather than trajectory information is a sufficient explanation.
- \(M_{\mathrm{joint-context}}\): a substantive irregular-time multi-output state-space/marked-event model. It models separate latent AFP, albumin and bilirubin states with assay/specimen observation models, exact measurement times, missing/qualitative indicators, local contact intensity, and the order-plan process through day 45; its competing transition hazards output plan-stratified strict/broad/diagnostic/non-liver CIFs. Fit only through day 45 for predictors, and standardize its output to the same \(E\) and \(P\)-specific targets. It can reveal asynchronous/nonlinear latent component evolution, uncertainty from irregular measurement, and whether the plan interaction survives an explicit informative-observation model—information that categorical \(B_{\mathrm{IPO}}\) loses. It is scientifically substantive because it addresses latent trajectory and observation-process uncertainty, not because it is complex or GPU-based.

The learned alternative is CPU-first and may be deferred with an explicit checkpoint if convergence or positivity fails. A generic transformer is not selected: the unresolved limitation is plan/endpoint semantics and missing clinical adjudication, not representation capacity. ALBI/MELD or a mechanistic reserve model remains deferred because HCC has no lab units/validated thresholds and no INR/reserve adjudication. Full multimodal reproduction is impossible because HCC has no image files; the research-ambition README states that the cancer demonstration’s main article/full STAR Methods were unavailable, so only its accessible supplement was inspected. A causal treatment-policy analysis, survival/death model, and external transport study require unavailable intent, assignment, mortality, outside-care, and validation data.

## Split, uncertainty, falsification, and interpretation gates

Use 2010–2022 for fitting, 2023 for tuning, and 2024 through the cutoff as locked test when event support permits; otherwise use a deterministic patient-hash split declared before fitting. No patient crosses splits. Freeze procedure/order/reassessment vocabularies, endpoint and episode rules, assay tolerances, plan time windows, sparse-cell and overlap rules, model tuning, bootstrap count, and margin before locked evaluation. Do not use post-day-45 data in predictors.
For the new estimand, \(O\), \(\pi(X)\), clipping, weight stabilization, model tuning, calibration and common-support rules are frozen from training before evaluation. The observation model must be evaluated on the tuning year without using test outcomes; the locked test receives only the frozen observation model and feature transformations. Report both \(E\) and \(E^\*\) targets, weight sums and effective sample size by \(P\), and compare observed-panel versus transported estimates. No post-day-45 variable may enter either the observation model or outcome model.

 Use patient-clustered bootstrap or equivalent intervals, never row bootstrap. Report cause-specific hazards, CIF at 30/90/320 days after the landmark, \(\Delta_{P=0}\), \(\Delta_{P=1}\), their interaction, calibration, log score, overlap, effective sample size if weights are used, and plan/reassessment cell counts.

Mandatory falsifications:

1. **Parent continuity and schedule-only:** reproduce the parent unstratified strict contrast; compare \(B_{\mathrm{time}}\), \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\), and \(B_{\mathrm{context}}\). If \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\) reproduces the profile contrast, trajectory-specific interpretation is adverse.
2. **Plan-context interaction:** compare plan-positive and plan-negative contrasts with the prespecified sign. A similar or stronger plan-positive-only contrast favors serial recorded planning over a newly emerging pathway.
3. **Order temporal leakage/control:** repeat after excluding orders from the index encounter and orders whose \`order time\` is within the first 24 hours after \(t_0\); repeat with non-canceled status only. A large change indicates index-bundle or status sensitivity.
4. **Workflow specificity:** apply the same plan screen to non-liver/diagnostic order controls and apply reassessment labeling with imaging-only/angiography-only exclusions. Comparable profile-by-plan effects for non-liver workflows are adverse.
5. **Timestamp permutation:** retain the parent’s within-patient, exact-assay value-to-time permutation; additionally permute plan-order times within patient among eligible day-45 order rows while preserving order text/status and outcome. A preserved context interaction supports timing/capture artifact.
6. **Placebo and endpoint robustness:** retain the parent pre-index placebo, strict-versus-broad ladder, diagnostic/mixed/non-liver controls, and encounter-only versus 24-hour episode sensitivity. A comparable placebo/control interaction or ambiguous-bundle concentration is adverse.
7. **Latent-model check:** require convergence and observation-model sensitivity; incompatible \(M_{\mathrm{joint-context}}\) estimates are inconclusive/adverse, not grounds to select the favored model.


8. **Observation-selection falsification:** compare \(O=1\) versus \(O=0\) using \(B_{\mathrm{no-lab}}\) and report the association of observation status with every competing recorded state. A large unexplained \(O\)-outcome gradient, especially if it mirrors the \(A_F-A_D\) contrast, is adverse to transport.
9. **Weight and standardization falsification:** repeat with untrimmed, clipped, stabilized and common-support weights, alternative training-only observation models (regularized logistic versus shallow tree), and observed-panel standardization. A sign or margin reversal, weight-driven estimate, poor calibration, or effective sample size collapse is adverse/inconclusive according to whether the failure is systematic or unsupported.
10. **Label-permutation falsification:** within \(P\), calendar and observation-opportunity strata, permute \(A_F/A_D\) labels among \(O=1\) patients using a training-frozen seed. The transported contrast should collapse toward zero; persistence indicates leakage, residual coding structure, or invalid uncertainty.
11. **Missingness-pattern negative control:** replace \(A_F/A_D\) with pre-index measurement-count/availability strata and test the same weighted CIF contrast. A comparable result from this observation-only label is adverse to a trajectory interpretation.
12. **Joint-model target agreement:** require \(M_{\mathrm{joint-context}}\) to report the same full-\(E\) target and observation-model diagnostics. A materially incompatible result is inconclusive/adverse, not grounds to select the favored model.

Supportive computation requires the parent unstratified contrast to remain directionally compatible; \(\Delta^E_0(320)\) to meet the prespecified direction/margin with a patient-level interval excluding zero or the margin; a materially different, directionally weaker \(\Delta^E_1\); a small, explained difference between \(\Delta^{obs}\) and \(\Delta^E\); nontrivial incremental contribution beyond \(B_{\mathrm{no-lab}}\); adequate observation and plan-cell overlap, calibration and effective sample size; weaker observation-only, non-liver, placebo and permuted signals; stable strict-versus-broad and episode results; and a compatible converged joint model. This supports only a robust, observation-transported association with a recorded local pathway, more compatible with the parent’s plan-negative/reassessment context than with serial recorded planning or laboratory-selection alone.

Adverse computation is a null/reversal of the parent or transported contrast, a large observed-versus-full-cohort discrepancy, \(B_{\mathrm{no-lab}}\) explaining the signal, a plan-positive-only concentration, observation-only negative-control replication, permutation persistence, weight sensitivity, poor overlap, or joint-model disagreement. These favor scheduling, capture, coding, or measurement-selection explanations.

Inconclusive computation includes sparse \(O=0\) or plan cells, missing timestamps, unstable vocabulary, positivity failure, low effective sample size, poor observation-model calibration, intervals crossing the margin, inadequate follow-up, temporal-split failure, inability to separate strict therapy from ambiguous records, or joint-model nonconvergence. No null/adverse/inconclusive result establishes absence of biological response.

The verifier can check paths, schema IDs, headers, two-key aggregation, frozen screens, time boundaries, no leakage, episode/endpoint construction, context labels, competing-risk calculations, predictions, uncertainty, calibration, overlap, permutations, and conclusion-to-output links. It cannot adjudicate treatment indication or intent, order completion, technical success, radiologic/pathologic response, viable tumor, recurrence/progression, liver failure, death, mortality, survival, outside care, appropriateness, benefit, utility, or external validity. Those require clinical adjudication, linked outcomes, and another prospective/external study.
