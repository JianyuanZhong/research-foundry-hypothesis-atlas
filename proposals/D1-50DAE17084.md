# Lead-time-discriminating test of the AFP trajectory vs the same-draw liver-reserve (ALBI) trajectory for radiologic progression after liver-directed therapy for HCC

Island: hcc (single-institution Chinese hospital HCC records). Episode 7. Child (evolution) of
`[prior hypothesis]` (episode-4 leader, mu ~31, assessed valid, reference_delivery
incomplete). Status: design + inherited probe-verified bindings; RESULTS ARE NOT COMPUTED HERE.

## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE/resection/ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats AFP, albumin and
bilirubin. The parent asks whether the *within-patient AFP trajectory* adds prognostic information
beyond baseline AFP AND beyond liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics measured in the SAME draws. Its hardest
objection was outcome circularity: predicting the *decision to retreat* from the same labs that drive
that decision. The parent's own repair moved the endpoint to a dated radiologic progression event in
the `examinations` table.

**The still-unresolved objection this child attacks (the advance):** even with a radiologic endpoint,
imaging is not scheduled at random. If AFP is used clinically to bring a scan forward, then a rising
AFP slope will *stochastically precede* a progression report merely because it triggered the earlier
scan ("lead-time / informative-observation" bias), without the tumour growing faster. A landmark
design alone cannot separate these, because it does not look at *when* after the landmark the event is
detected. This is the same class of time-origin artefact demonstrated by [K3].

**Opening / evidence for the concern:** the parent's probe P1 established that the `examinations`
table contains ~1.7M dated reports with progression/recurrence descriptors (迁移 25107, 增大 13115,
复发 4973, 进展 3645 in the first 420k rows), i.e. the exposure-scenario is real and the endpoint is
dated, so the *timing* of detection is recoverable. No inspected work tests AFP-vs-ALBI kinetic
added-value with a lead-time discriminator in HCC [K1][K2].

**Clinical importance:** if AFP kinetics predict progression only among scans very early after the
landmark, the actionable reading is "AFP prompts imaging", and the correct change is surveillance
INTERVAL policy. If AFP kinetics predict progression across later scans too, the reading is
biologically-grounded tumour-activity information that supports AFP-triggered treatment escalation.
These imply different clinical decisions, and a clean null (liver reserve alone) is also decision-
relevant because it argues for de-emphasising AFP-only monitoring.

**Added by this child (pre-specified, one addition):** a *detection-timing stratum* on the primary
endpoint and a *dual-model robustness requirement*.
- Primary event = first dated radiologic progression descriptor after t*. Split into E_early = event
  at the first imaging study after t*, and E_late = event only at a study performed >= 90 days after

- Any supportive claim must hold in BOTH (a) the landmark Cox and (b) a time-dependent Cox on the
  same exposure, mirroring the disagreement [K3] shows is possible.

## 4. Method comparison (recorded before selection)

- M0 baseline (simple): landmark Cox on baseline AFP + baseline ALBI grade + age/sex/treatment type +
  surveillance (draw/diagnostic) intensity. Yields level-based prognosis only; cannot separate a
  rising trajectory from a high-but-flat one, and cannot separate AFP from liver reserve.
- M4 substantive (learned/mechanistic alternative): joint linear-mixed-effects submodels for log AFP
  and for ALBI fit on (index, t*], with the empirical-Bayes slope estimates entered into the
  landmark/time-dependent Cox. Reveals *change vs level* and *AFP vs same-draw liver reserve*; the
  slope separation is exactly what M0 loses. Chosen as primary because the question is about
  trajectory information; M0 is the mandatory benchmark and the increment (delta C-index, bootstrap
  CI) is reported against it.
- Deferred alternative (not chosen now): full shared-parameter joint longitudinal-survival model with
  link (as in [K2]); deferred to keep the two-stage estimator's failure modes transparent.

## 5. Analysis, falsification, and what each result would mean

Primary model: landmark Cox (t*) for time to first radiologic progression, exposure = mixed-model log
AFP slope, adjusted for the same-draw ALBI slope, baseline AFP, baseline ALBI, age/sex, treatment
type, and draw/diagnostic intensity. Secondary: retreatment-time Cox for the concordance check.

**S1 (supportive):** AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 with HR>1 AND E_late AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 AND
the retreatment concordance kappa >= pre-set floor AND the effect holds in the time-dependent Cox.
Interpretation: AFP kinetics carry progression information not explained by liver reserve or by
scan-triggering; supports AFP-informed surveillance/treatment timing.
**S2 (adverse):** AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 with HR>1 in E_early but NOT in E_late, OR the association
vanishes in the time-dependent Cox, OR only the ALBI slope is significant. Interpretation: the signal
is detection timing / practice pattern, not tumour activity; argues for reserving AFP for triggering,
not for escalation.
**S3 (falsification / null):** both slope HR CIs include 1 AND delta-C-index bootstrap CI within
+/-0.01 of M0. Interpretation: trajectories add nothing over baseline levels; de-emphasise routine
trajectory monitoring. (Note: S3 with wide CIs is INCONCLUSIVE, not adverse — event rate is the
solver's first feasibility output.)
**Inconclusive:** if the imaging-flag validation (agreement floor) fails, fall back to the retreatment
proxy and report the analysis as inconclusive for radiologic progression.

## 6. Rivals, limits, and unascertainable evidence

Rivals each mapped to a distinct test: baseline reserve (adjust for baseline AFP/ALBI), surveillance
intensity (adjust for draw/diagnostic counts), regression-to-the-mean (two-draw vs three-draw slope
comparison), measurement/detection timing (E_early vs E_late; time-dependent Cox).

Honest limits: the progression flag is a LEXICAL extraction from report text and is UNVALIDATED (the
frozen metadata validates only a cirrhosis lexical detector), so a pre-registered manual-audit
precision/recall, an agreement floor, and an explicit fallback are specified; mortality/mRECIST PFS
are unascertainable -> NO survival claim; single institution; lab units absent; NO outcomes are
computed in discovery. Clinical adjudication of progression reports, and any causal claim, require
expert review or another study; the automatic pipeline can only establish computation and internal
consistency.
