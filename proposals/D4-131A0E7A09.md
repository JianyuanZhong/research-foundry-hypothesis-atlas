# Provisional hypothesis: fat distribution separates metabolic from functional/fall risk trajectories

Status: provisional and uncomputed. This is a research design, not an observed result.

## Proposed relationship

Among UK Biobank participants with baseline body-composition MRI, at similar total adiposity, higher visceral fat will be more specifically associated with subsequent metabolic disease, whereas higher posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) will be more specifically associated with subsequent functional decline or falls. The unresolved advance is whether these two fat-distribution phenotypes identify distinct downstream trajectories rather than merely serving as interchangeable predictors of generic health risk.
