# Hypothesis: Cumulative Hypotension Burden Predicts Mortality in Mechanical Ventilation

## Scientific Question

**Primary Hypothesis**: In mechanically ventilated ICU patients, the cumulative burden of sustained hypotension (time-weighted exposure below systolic BP < 100 mmHg) independently predicts 28-day mortality, beyond the effects of vasopressor intensity, duration of mechanical ventilation, and minimum blood pressure.

**Rival Hypothesis**: Minimum blood pressure alone (or vasopressor intensity/duration) is the primary predictor of mortality, and cumulative hypotension burden adds no significant predictive value.

## Clinical Importance

1. **Guideline Implications**: Current Surviving Sepsis Campaign and other ICU guidelines focus on minimum blood pressure targets. This study could inform whether time-weighted exposure metrics would improve risk stratification.

2. **Vasopressor Management**: Understanding the relationship between hypotension burden and outcomes could help optimize vasopressor titration strategies, potentially reducing unnecessary vasopressor use while maintaining patient safety.

3. **Risk Stratification**: Identifying patients at high risk due to cumulative hypotension burden could enable earlier intervention and more personalized care.

## Study Population

- **Population**: Adult patients (≥18 years) in the eICU-CRD database who were mechanically ventilated
- **Inclusion Criteria**:
  - Age ≥ 18 years at admission
  - Mechanical ventilation for ≥ 24 hours
  - At least 48 hours of continuous vital sign monitoring (vitalPeriodic)
  - Available hospital-level data for hospital-level covariate adjustment
- **Exclusion Criteria**:
  - Missing vital sign data for ≥ 50% of ICU stay
  - Missing lab data for kidney function assessment
  - Patients with documented pre-existing end-stage organ failure (as proxy for extreme frailty)

## Main Data and Variables

### Primary Exposure: Cumulative Hypotension Burden
- **Definition**: Time-weighted sum of (1 - systolic BP / 100) for all observations with systolic BP < 100 mmHg
- **Table**: `vitalPeriodic`
- **Columns**: `patientunitstayid`, `observationoffset`, `systemicsystolic`
- **Temporal Boundary**: ICU admission to discharge or death

### Primary Outcome: 28-Day Mortality
- **Definition**: Death within 28 days of ICU admission
- **Table**: `patient` (via `uniquepid` and `hospitaldischargestatus`)
- **Note**: Mortality determined at discharge or within 28 days if discharge status unknown

### Key Covariates

#### Patient-level (from `patient` table)
- `gender`: Sex at admission
- `age`: Age at admission
- `ethnicity`: Race/ethnicity
- `admissionheight`, `admissionweight`: Anthropometrics

#### Hospital-level (from `hospital` table)
- `hospitalid`: Hospital identifier
- `teachingstatus`: Teaching hospital vs. non-teaching
- `region`: Geographic region

#### Treatment-related
- **Vasopressor Intensity**: Mean infusion rate from `infusionDrug` for vasopressors (norepinephrine, epinephrine, vasopressin, phenylephrine, dopamine)
- **Vasopressor Duration**: Time spent on vasopressors
- **Respiratory Support**: `respiratoryCare` table for ventilation settings

#### Confounding Variables
- **Lab Values**: `lab` table for:
  - Serum creatinine (baseline and serial)
  - Lactate (peak)
  - White blood cell count
- **Diagnosis**: `diagnosis` table for primary diagnosis codes
- **Comorbidities**: `pastHistory` table for chronic conditions

## Study Design

### Temporal Boundaries
- **Start**: ICU admission (`hospitaladmittime24`)
- **End**: Discharge, death, or 28 days post-admission (whichever occurs first)

### Analysis Approach

#### Baseline Model (Univariate)
- Logistic regression: Mortality ~ Cumulative Hypotension Burden
- Report odds ratio (OR) and 95% CI

#### Primary Analysis (Multivariable)
- Logistic regression: Mortality ~ Cumulative Hypotension Burden + Age + Sex + Ethnicity + Hospital Teaching Status + Region + Baseline Creatinine + Peak Lactate + Primary Diagnosis
- Report adjusted OR and 95% CI for cumulative hypotension burden

#### Comparison Models
1. **Minimum BP Model**: Mortality ~ Minimum Systolic BP + covariates
2. **Vasopressor Model**: Mortality ~ Mean Vasopressor Rate + Duration + covariates
3. **Full Model**: Include all of the above + cumulative hypotension burden

#### Effect Modification
- Test interaction between cumulative hypotension burden and:
  - Vasopressor intensity
  - Duration of mechanical ventilation
  - Teaching hospital status

## Falsification Criteria

The hypothesis would be **falsified** if:
1. Cumulative hypotension burden is not significantly associated with mortality after adjustment for all covariates (p > 0.05)
2. The association is entirely explained by minimum blood pressure (no independent effect after adjusting for minimum BP)
3. The association is entirely explained by vasopressor intensity/duration (no independent effect after adjusting for these)

The hypothesis would be **supported** if:
1. Cumulative hypotension burden is independently associated with mortality (p < 0.05)
2. The association persists after adjusting for minimum BP, vasopressor use, and other confounders
3. The effect size is clinically meaningful (e.g., OR > 1.2 for high vs. low burden)

The hypothesis would be **inconclusive** if:
1. The association is weak and borderline (0.05 < p < 0.1)
2. Effect modification suggests the relationship is only present in specific subgroups
3. Confounding by indication is suspected (e.g., patients with more severe disease receive both more vasopressors and have more hypotension)

## Missing Evidence and Limitations

### Missing Evidence
1. **Ground Truth for Vasopressor Response**: No data on individual patient hemodynamic response to vasopressors
2. **Precise Definition of "Sustained" Hypotension**: Need clinical expert input on what constitutes sustained vs. transient hypotension
3. **Baseline Kidney Function**: Need explicit definition of baseline creatinine (first 24h vs. admission)
4. **Pre-admission Data**: Limited information on pre-ICU health status

### Limitations
1. **Observational Design**: Cannot establish causality; confounding by indication possible
2. **Measurement Error**: Vital signs are 5-minute medians, not continuous
3. **Missing Data**: Potential for missing vital signs in some patients
4. **Generalizability**: eICU-CRD may not represent all ICU populations
5. **Threshold Selection**: Choice of systolic BP < 100 mmHg is arbitrary; need sensitivity analysis
