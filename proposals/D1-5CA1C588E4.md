# Label-to-action fidelity after daily extubation-evaluation labels

## Parent, status, and substantive evolution

This proposal is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent completed an endpoint-falsification audit: independent liberation markers were rare, complete 48-hour post-event documentation was approximately half of putative union events, and discharge bridging could not establish durable true extubation. This child does not retry that blocked durable-liberation association. It asks a narrower implementation question that is measurable without treating a care-plan proxy as extubation:

> Among otherwise comparable ventilated person-landmarks, does the label `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`, versus `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`, predict independently charted near-term ventilator-setting de-escalation or sedation-infusion de-escalation within 12 hours, and is that action ascertainment sufficiently complete and hospital-generalizable to support a descriptive implementation association?

The clinical advance is to test whether the label has observable operational meaning even when it fails as a durable-liberation endpoint. A positive result would show concordance between a documented plan and subsequent recorded management actions, not that an SBT occurred, that extubation was appropriate, or that the label caused the action. A negative result would show that the label is not a reliable implementation signal in this snapshot, even if the label itself is common. The parent’s negative finding remains intact: none of these actions validates true extubation or durable liberation.

## Fixed temporal design

For every retained person-landmark:

1. Exposure is fixed at D, the exact `cplitemoffset` of the first target label in the parent’s `[L,L+360]` interval. Same-time contradictions and all parent exclusions remain excluded.
2. Pre-D covariates are restricted to information available before D. The parent’s strict-screen fields (`fio2`, `peep`, `spo2_n`, `spo2_median`, `rr`, `map_n`, `map_median`), airway/prestate/EOL/prior-spontaneous indicators, landmark and decision delay, hospital, and any respiratory/infusion baseline value strictly before D are eligible. The primary feasibility run does not estimate an adjusted effect; any later association model must honor this lock and use no post-D covariate.
3. Primary action window is `(D,D+720]` (12 hours). It is chosen as a short implementation horizon, not a liberation or durability horizon. A 1-hour and 24-hour sensitivity should be reported in a full analysis.
4. A setting change is ascertainable only when the same conceptual variable has a numeric pre-D value strictly before D and a numeric post-D value in `(D,D+720]`. Missing post-D documentation is missing action ascertainment, not “no action.”
5. A setting de-escalation is prespecified as any of: FiO2 decrease by at least 5 percentage points; PEEP/PEEP-CPAP decrease by at least 1 cmH2O; pressure-support decrease by at least 1; set ventilator rate decrease by at least 1; or peak inspiratory pressure decrease by at least 1. The direction and component-specific deltas are retained. These are changes in charted settings, not proof of bedside implementation or clinical appropriateness.
6. A spontaneous-mode outcome requires a post-D `Mechanical Ventilator Mode` value whose exact charted text contains `spont`, `psv`, `cpap`, or `simv`. The available exact snapshot inventory found four mode rows in the 12-hour source window, all `AC/CMV`; the executed strict ascertainment therefore found zero positive spontaneous-mode rows. This is a computable negative finding about this field, not evidence that spontaneous breathing never occurred.
7. `SBT Method` documentation and `RT Vent On/Off=Off` are secondary process/proxy outcomes. They are never called SBT completion, SBT performance, extubation, or liberation.
8. Sedation-infusion de-escalation is a paired same-drug outcome: a documented numeric infusion rate strictly before D in `(D-1440,D)` followed by a numeric same-drug rate in `(D,D+720]` that falls by at least 10% of the baseline rate or at least 0.01 rate units. This is an administered-infusion record proxy; it is not an interruption unless an explicit stop/hold record is available. A direct interruption/de-escalation chart outcome requires an exact action value from respiratory or nursing charting and is reported separately.
9. A temporal placebo/trajectory diagnostic uses the analogous setting comparison in `(D-720,D]`, with baseline in `(D-1440,D-720]`. It is not a no-treatment control and cannot establish causality; it detects whether setting changes were already underway before the label.

## Predefined gates and analysis decision

Before any descriptive strategy contrast, require:

- at least three hospitals with at least 10 observations of each strategy;
- at least 20% pre/post ascertainment for the primary setting composite in each strategy;
- at least 10 primary setting-action events and 10 paired sedation-rate-decrease events in each strategy;
- report component-specific ascertainment, hospital-stratified rates, and a missingness table; never code an unobserved post-D setting as no action;
- retain the pre-D trajectory placebo and treat it as a diagnostic. A large pre-D differential, or a failure to establish comparable charting opportunity, blocks any language suggesting implementation fidelity or label-associated action.

The first three gates pass in the executed snapshot. The direct interruption and spontaneous-mode outcomes fail event-sufficiency gates (zero positives), so they are descriptive feasibility results only. The setting placebo is common (evaluation 119/308 available; no-trial 91/230), with similar available-case rates (38.6% versus 39.6%); it is not evidence for or against causality and is explicitly inconclusive as a no-treatment control. Accordingly, this child authorizes a noncausal, implementation/measurement association study for pre/post setting changes and paired infusion-rate changes, but not a causal treatment-effect model and not a liberation endpoint model.

A full descriptive analysis should use person-landmark rows as the unit, cluster uncertainty by `hospitalid` and `uniquepid` (multiple landmarks may occur in one ICU stay), report risk differences and confidence intervals only after the gates, and stratify by landmark. A covariate-adjusted model is optional and secondary, with covariates locked to pre-D only; it must not be interpreted causally because the plan label is observational and may be a response to unmeasured bedside readiness. The implementation estimand is the difference in the probability of a documented action within 12 hours, among rows with a defined label, not the effect of ordering a daily evaluation.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence for label-to-action fidelity would be a prespecified, reproducible excess of setting or infusion-rate de-escalation in the evaluation group, stable across the three overlap hospitals and landmarks, with high and strategy-balanced ascertainment, and no materially different pre-D setting trajectory. It would support the narrow claim that labels predict subsequently documented management changes.

Adverse evidence would be no between-label difference, reversal across hospitals, a large pre-D placebo difference, or action rates explained by one charting system or one component. It would imply that the label is not a generalizable implementation marker. The observed zero spontaneous-mode and SBT-method positives are adverse to using those fields as general implementation outcomes in this snapshot.

Inconclusive evidence includes missing post-D setting values, sparse direct action text, differential charting opportunity, or action changes that cannot be separated from routine repeated measurements. It cannot be repaired by treating missingness as no action or by using `ventendoffset` alone.

Computationally checkable claims are exact cohort reconstruction, pre/post field availability, numerical deltas under the stated thresholds, exact-label counts, hospital-stratified counts, and whether a positive spontaneous-mode/SBT/proxy string occurs. Clinical adjudication or another study is required to determine whether a recorded setting change was actually delivered at the bedside, whether an infusion-rate change constituted a sedation interruption, whether a patient underwent an SBT, whether the patient was ready for extubation, and whether the label caused the action. True extubation, durable liberation, post-ICU reintubation, and patient benefit remain outside this dataset design.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Changed from the parent: the endpoint is no longer durable liberation; exposure remains exactly fixed at D; the outcome is a short-horizon, independently recorded management action with explicit pre/post ascertainment and missingness handling. The study adds exact respiratory setting fields, infusion-rate trajectories, RASS/sedation chart availability, hospital overlap, and a pre-D placebo diagnostic. It preserves the parent’s failed liberation validation and does not reinterpret care-plan or RT-off proxies as extubation.

Uncertain: bedside action fidelity, unrecorded actions, clinical intent, residual confounding, repeated-landmark dependence, and whether the small field-specific reductions reflect clinically meaningful weaning. The zero spontaneous-mode/SBT results may reflect field sparsity or local documentation conventions rather than absence of those actions. These limits are reasons to keep the proposal noncausal and process-focused, not reasons to silently broaden the endpoint.
