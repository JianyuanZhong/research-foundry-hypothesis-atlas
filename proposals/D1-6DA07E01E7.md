# Proposal: history-conditioned meaning of early post-TACE trajectories

## Episode, parent, and scientific deliverable

This Episode-26 candidate evolves assessed-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's all-index population, L=14 and L=45 information boundaries, early-transition and local-observation-loss states, component-resolved AFP/albumin/total-bilirubin profile, competing recorded pathways, observation-process baselines, temporal locking and strict noncausal evidence boundary.

The new clinically substantive uncertainty is treatment-history effect modification. The earliest TACE-like episode in this local snapshot is not necessarily a patient's first treatment ever. A patient with no earlier locally recorded liver-directed procedure may be receiving an initial course, whereas a patient with earlier local procedures may have recurrent or retreatment care. A favorable early laboratory pattern could therefore have different implications for subsequent recorded care in the two groups. The parent adjusts for prior burden but does not make this interaction an estimand. This matters before using one post-TACE reassessment rule across patients.

The actual deliverable is newly constructed history/profile manifests and newly fitted locked-test estimates of history-by-profile interaction, not a presumed result. Required outputs are: `index_episode_manifest.csv`, `prior_liver_directed_history.csv`, `trajectory_manifest_L14_L45.csv`, `early_transition_manifest.csv`, `transition_cif.json`, `history_profile_contrasts.json`, `predictions.parquet`, `metrics.json`, `calibration.json`, `bootstrap_intervals.json`, `negative_controls.json`, `model_parameters.json`, `interpretation.json`, and `limitations.csv`. The interpretation file must link each claim to an estimate, interval, boundary, outcome, split and frozen rule.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports only an operational claim: the HCC snapshot has dated procedure, encounter and laboratory records that can define a reproducible first *locally recorded* TACE-like episode, a pre-index procedure-history proxy, and later recorded care. The source does not establish treatment indication, completion, response, recurrence, stage, or a complete treatment history. No model has been fitted in discovery.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults meeting the HCC diagnosis ascertainment proxy and having a first encounter-constrained timed TACE-like recorded episode, the association between an early favorable component profile (AFP decreases while albumin and total bilirubin are non-worsening) and later recorded liver-directed pathways is modified by pre-index locally recorded liver-directed history. In particular, the favorable-versus-discordant contrast in first strict repeat-TACE or alternate liver-directed CIF will differ between the recorded-history-naïve proxy group and the previously recorded-treatment group; a pooled contrast that ignores history will be misleading.

The primary directional expectation is a smaller or less favorable repeat-treatment contrast among patients with prior local liver-directed procedures, because prior treatment/recurrent-care contexts can produce different laboratory and pathway relationships. This direction is a prespecified hypothesis, not an expected result to be confirmed. A null interaction with stable stratum-specific estimates would support transportability of the profile association across the observed history strata; reversal is adverse to the proposed direction.

This can inform a consequential but limited decision: whether a future prospective study should validate one common post-TACE monitoring rule or stratify reassessment by treatment history. A supportive result motivates clinical adjudication and external validation; it does not justify changing treatment.

## Population, history, boundaries and outcomes

Construct one index per patient from `procedures`. Within each `(Patient master index, Visit number)`, group rows into 24-hour episodes using `Start time`; retain original `Surgery`, `Surgery source`, start/end timestamps and invalid-time audit. The earliest timed episode with literal `TACE`, `Arterial chemoembolization`, or both `Hepatic artery` and `Embolization` is the candidate index. Hepatic angiography alone and bare `Intervention` are not therapeutic index evidence. Run an encounter-only episode sensitivity. Require linked `encounters` with age >=18 and nonmissing `Sex`; require a `Diagnosis name` containing `Hepatocellular carcinoma` on a linked encounter whose `Visit time` is from 180 days before through 7 days after `t_0`. Because diagnoses have no time, this is an encounter-time ascertainment proxy, not disease onset. Restrict `t_0 <= 2025-01-01`.

Define history using only procedure rows with valid start times strictly before `t_0), preserving the two-key linkage:

- H0, recorded-history-naïve proxy: no qualifying primary or alternate liver-directed therapeutic procedure in `[t_0-365 days,t_0)`.
- H1, prior-recorded-treatment proxy: at least one qualifying liver-directed therapeutic procedure in that interval.
- HU, history indeterminate/insufficiently observed: invalid or missing pre-index procedure time, or a source/episode ambiguity that prevents classification. Do not silently remove HU; report it and use complete-case H0/H1 only as a sensitivity.
- Also report continuous/count history and time since most recent qualifying procedure where estimable. No H0 label may be described as treatment-naïve clinically, since care before the local snapshot and outside care are unavailable.

The qualifying history vocabulary is frozen from training data and is a tiered ontology: strict TACE terms; alternate liver-directed terms such as ablation, resection, transplant or radiotherapy only when their literal local procedure labels meet a prespecified screen; mixed/unclassified rows remain an auditable sensitivity tier. Do not use the index row or post-index rows to define H. The parent’s full-index transition model remains primary for early exits.

At L=14, use the last valid pre-index and first numeric lab value in days 7-14. At L=45, use the same pair with the post-index window days 7-45. For AFP, albumin and total bilirubin, freeze exact assay names from training; preserve specimen type, qualitative/inequality status, missingness, number of measurements and delay. Use within-assay changes or training-only standardized direction, never cross-assay raw pooling; there is no local lab unit column. Define A_F as AFP decrease plus non-worsening albumin and bilirubin, A_D as AFP decrease plus worsening of either, and retain other/missing profiles as separate categories rather than forcing classification. These are recorded laboratory profiles, not response or liver reserve.

Outcomes are first mutually exclusive recorded states after the applicable boundary through 365 days after `t_0): strict repeat TACE, alternate/mixed/unclassified liver-directed procedure, diagnostic/technical procedure, non-liver procedure, and administrative loss of local observation. For the all-index multi-state analysis model transitions from `t_0` through day 45, including early liver-directed action and observation loss. Report CIFs at 30, 90 and 320 days from `t_0`, and conditional post-boundary CIFs to day 365. A later strict-TACE reassessment context may use dated examination workflow in the prior 30 days, but `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis` are sensitivity-only text, never radiologic adjudication.

The primary estimand is the history-specific predictive contrast:
`Delta_{k,h}^{L}(t) = P(first recorded state k by t | A_F,H=h, standardized history/capture) - P(first recorded state k by t | A_D,H=h, standardized history/capture)`,
with the interaction `I_k^L(t)=Delta_{k,H1}^L(t)-Delta_{k,H0}^L(t)`. Standardize only to common pre-index covariate and observation-opportunity support; report overlap and effective sample size. This is not a causal treatment or response effect. Estimate both L=14 and L=45 and the full-index transition quantities; never condition away early events when describing the target population.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Use the same all-index patients, ontology, history strata, boundary, split and locked test.

B0 is a transparent competing-risk discrete-time hazard model with pre-index age, sex, index encounter timing/department, HCC ascertainment features, prior procedure counts/history stratum, pre-index component availability/values and local capture features. It includes H0/H1 interactions but no post-index trajectory.

B1 adds the categorical A_F/A_D/other component profile separately at L=14 or L=45, exact measurement delay/count and missingness, early-state indicators where permitted. Report history-by-profile CIF contrasts, not just AUROC.

The substantive alternative M1 is a hierarchical irregular-time multi-output state-space plus marked competing-transition model: separate latent AFP, albumin and bilirubin trajectories; assay/specimen-specific observation models; qualitative/inequality flags; pre-index procedure-history state; local observation intensity; early transitions; and partial pooling of history-by-profile effects. It can represent asynchronous nonlinear component changes and uncertainty in a history stratum that categorical B1 loses. Fit only on training data and compare on the same locked target using dynamic log score, Brier score, calibration, history-specific CIF contrasts, interaction intervals and patient-clustered bootstrap. The learned model is scientifically useful only if it changes interpretation of history/profile heterogeneity, not merely if it gives a small predictive gain.

The default is CPU: B0/B1 are tabular, and M1 is a modest latent model. The future solver envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB memory and 28,800 seconds; discovery used no model fit and no GPU. CPU convergence and memory for M1 are unverified. If repeated latent fits materially benefit, request one allocated A100 with `gpus:1` and use `cuda:0`; ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not evidence. Save a checkpoint and document deferral if convergence or positivity fails. The discovery budget is separate from the solver envelope.

## Splits, uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Freeze lexical procedure/history and assay screens, episode grouping, diagnosis proxy, windows, profile rules, HU handling, common-support rules and sparse-cell thresholds before fitting. Use 2010-2022 training, 2023 tuning and 2024 through 2025-01-01 locked testing where support permits; otherwise use deterministic participant-hash splitting. No patient crosses splits. Fit standardization, observation weights, vocabulary and any learned representation on training only. Use patient-clustered bootstrap or an equivalent participant-level interval.

Falsification and stress tests are:

1. Reproduce the parent’s all-index early-transition and L=14/L=45 estimates before adding the history interaction. Definition mismatch is a packaging failure, not evidence.
2. Compare H0/H1 interaction with a pre-index placebo profile built from two pre-index lab windows. A comparable placebo interaction suggests history-dependent measurement or coding rather than post-index information.
3. Use non-liver and diagnostic/technical first outcomes as capture controls. A similar interaction there weakens disease/pathway specificity.
4. Repeat strict versus broad/mixed procedure tiers, 24-hour versus encounter-only episodes, and procedure-source strata. A result confined to one coding tier/source is adverse or requires coding review.
5. Permute assay value-to-time within patient preserving assay/specimen, and separately shift the outcome clock; persistence indicates timing/capture artifact.
6. Compare B0 to B1, and B1 to M1, with observation-only models and missingness strata. Large attenuation after capture variables, unstable weights, poor overlap, or latent nonconvergence is adverse/inconclusive.
7. Repeat with complete numeric pairs, qualitative/inequality retained as missing indicators, and explicit HU rather than dropping it. No analysis may call HU treatment-naïve.
8. Report plan-order and dated examination context separately. A signal restricted to plan-status/index-bundle or similar to order-time permutations is not evidence of a biological profile effect.
9. Require held-out calibration, convergence, locked predictions, patient-level uncertainty and conclusion-to-output links. Do not select M1 for complexity.

Supportive results require reproducible parent estimates; history-specific profile contrasts in the prespecified direction at both boundaries or a stable non-null interaction with intervals excluding zero; adequate overlap and calibration; incremental B1 information beyond B0; weaker placebo/non-liver/permuted effects; stable strict/broad and episode sensitivities; and compatible M1 estimates. This supports only history-dependent association with locally recorded pathways and motivates adjudicated prospective validation.

Adverse results include null/reversed interaction, no incremental profile information, attenuation after capture adjustment, comparable placebo/control effects, coding/source instability, poor overlap, or learned-model disagreement. These favor the explanation that history labels or measurement opportunity encode local care rather than a robust disease-linked trajectory.

Inconclusive results include few H0/H1 events, many HU/invalid timestamps, sparse assay/history cells, unstable lexical ontology, insufficient follow-up, positivity failure, temporal-split failure, or M1 nonconvergence. They do not establish absence of biological response or treatment benefit.

The verifier can check paths, schema IDs, headers, two-key joins, frozen screens, history leakage, transition/CIF calculations, model outputs, uncertainty, calibration, overlap, falsifications and conclusion links. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for true treatment-naïve status, stage, intent, completion, technical success, radiologic/pathologic response, viable tumor, recurrence/progression, liver failure, death, outside care, appropriateness, benefit, utility and external validity.

## Demonstration dispositions and deferred alternatives

The natural-history/Delphi demonstration supports a bounded dated structured-sequence alternative, but its Danish cohort/external validation is unavailable; this proposal uses only local HCC records. The Bayesian longitudinal demonstration motivates the irregular-time latent-state alternative, but its genetic analysis is not reproduced because local genetic inputs are unavailable. The cancer/Oncoformer supplement supports considering a lab-only adaptation, but the main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and HCC images are absent; no multimodal reproduction claim is made.

A generic transformer is deferred because this question requires auditable history/profile effects and the available coded/narrative labels lack adjudicated clinical semantics; revisit if validated longitudinal text becomes available. ALBI/MELD and mechanistic reserve scores are deferred because lab units, INR and validated thresholds are absent. Causal treatment-policy and mortality analyses are deferred because intent, assignment, completion, death and outside-care ascertainment are unavailable. These are explicit revisit conditions, not blanket method bans.
