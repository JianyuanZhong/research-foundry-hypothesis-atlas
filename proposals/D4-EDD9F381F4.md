# Plasma protein–diagnosis associations: how much of each signal is a shared systemic axis vs disease-specific, tested out-of-sample?

Episode 11. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (episode 9, valid, mu 32.00).
Operation: evolve. Status: design + verified bindings; **no association result is claimed here** —
the solver fits the estimator and reports the pre-specified outputs.

## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens repeatedly nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, FABP1, CHI3L1,
IL-6/CRP family) as disease-specific preclinical biomarkers [K2]. It is unresolved whether a
protein–diagnosis association marks a disease-specific process (claim A) or mainly a shared
systemic morbidity/inflammation/aging (allostatic-load) axis elevated across many diagnoses and
tracking cumulative disease burden (claim B) [K1, K3]. This decides whether a protein is a credible
disease-specific early-detection target or only a generic risk/severity correlate.

## Substantive advance over the parent and inspected work
[K1] shows that a proteomic "disease-specific" signature is more parsimoniously explained by a shared
exposure (smoking) and that its associations extend across unrelated conditions — prediction is not
disease-specific biology. [K3] builds a shared proteomic allostatic-load score (ProAL50) associated
with many chronic diseases. Both leave open the *quantitative* attribution question in a single
cohort: for a given protein–diagnosis pair, what fraction of the association is the shared axis, and
does a **disease-specific residual survive out-of-sample**? The parent (episode 9) estimated the
shared axis and the association on the **same participants** (leave-one-protein-out only), which
admits optimism/leakage. This child's scientific delta:
1. **Participant-level sample splitting.** Estimate the shared systemic axis on a discovery half of
   `n_eid`; estimate the per-(protein, disease) attenuation fraction in the held-out half.
2. **Specificity contrast.** For each protein, contrast its held-out residual association with the
   target ICD-10 block against a negative-control disease block (external causes V01–Y98) and a
   permutation-null, to test whether any residual is *disease-preferential* rather than generic.
Claim A is supported only if a non-trivial protein subset keeps a large, target-preferential
held-out residual. Claim B is supported if attenuation is high and target-preferential residual is
absent. A large in-sample-vs-held-out drop in attenuation is itself a finding (overfit axis).

## Population, temporal boundaries, variables, outcome
- Population: UKB participants with an `n_eid` present in the Olink table
  (`[UKB data file]`, 53,014 rows, `n_eid` + 2,923 gene-symbol protein columns) and in
  the clinical table (`[UKB data file]`, 502,250 rows). Join key `n_eid`, same
  identifier namespace (both are UKB participant `eid`). No cross-dataset join.
- Temporal boundary: cross-sectional only. `n_53` (attendance/draw date), `n_40000` (death date),
  `n_40005` (cancer date) are confirmed ABSENT from the `n_eid` table, so no incidence, washout or
  reverse-causation window can be defined. Associations are prevalent-state.
- Exposure: the 2,923 protein columns (per-protein, standardized; log/rank handling in the solver).
- Outcome: prevalent ICD-10 from `s_41270_0_{0..258}` (259 columns); first-occurrence dates
  `s_41280_0_{0..258}` available. Disease units = ICD-10 3-character block; inclusion rule ≥200 cases
  among the 53,014 (else that block is dropped).
- Shared axis: first principal component of the standardized protein matrix, fit on the discovery
  half only; leave-one-protein-out axis for the protein being tested.
- Covariates: `n_31_0_0` (sex), `n_21003_0_0` (age at attendance).
- Estimand: attenuation fraction AF = 1 − |β_adj| / |β_unadj| for each (protein, disease) pair
  (covariate-adjusted association with vs without the shared-axis term, age+sex always included).
- Baselines: (i) age+sex only; (ii) flexible age spline; (iii) learned multi-protein elastic-net
  alternative with cross-validation (what the axis representation loses).
- Controls: positive-control (known inflammatory protein/disease pair), negative-control protein,
  negative-control outcome block (V01–Y98), permutation-null, positivity precondition.

## Supportive / adverse / inconclusive
- Supportive of claim B: median held-out AF ≥ 0.5 and no protein with a large target-preferential
  held-out residual; held-out AF ≈ in-sample AF (axis transfers).
- Supportive of claim A: a non-empty protein set with held-out AF < 0.3 AND target-preferential
  residual (target vs negative-control block separation beyond permutation band).
- Adverse to the design's own optimism check: held-out AF far below in-sample AF (axis overfit).
- Inconclusive: <200 cases for the tested block, non-identifiable axis, or permutation band
  overlapping all residuals.

## Required solver outputs
Held-out and in-sample AF per (protein, disease) pair; per-protein target-preferential residual with
permutation band; in-sample vs held-out AF comparison; baseline and elastic-net comparisons;
positive/negative-control pass/fail; reproducibility seed and split manifest.
