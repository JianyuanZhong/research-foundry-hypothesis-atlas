## Decision question, evidence boundary, and falsifiable hypothesis

The decision question is whether the inherited emitted route for an admission belongs to a conservative set of routes that can be defended from the stored, at-departure documentation: an `AUTO` route only when every active recommendation unit is sufficiently specified and not explicitly displaced; `RECON` when the text is actionable but competing, changed, or requires human reconciliation; and `DEFER` (with inherited `TIMING` subtype retained) whenever availability, target linkage, timing, contradiction, or interpretation is unresolved.

The strongest claim supported by the local sources is that admission-linked radiology and discharge artifacts, their storage times, structured detail rows, services, ICU workflow proxies, and admission/departure fields can be joined into a reproducible retrospective information-boundary experiment. The sources do not establish physical departure visibility, viewing, transmission, recipient, responsibility, order or referral execution, patient preference, appropriateness, safety, benefit, follow-up, outside care, or causality. The de-identification convention preserves within-subject intervals but invalidates cross-subject calendar alignment.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among the frozen first-eligible opportunities and the unchanged parent probability sample, the inherited selector's `AUTO` nominations will be admissible only when independent actionability and supersession panels classify every decision-relevant unit as action-ready, target-linked, non-conflicting, and available by `D`; explicit changed or competing plans and unresolved evidence will be captured by `RECON`/`DEFER` rather than incorrectly retained as `AUTO`, under the preregistered adverse bounds and workload contract.

A failure of this hypothesis rejects the affected atom or route nomination; it does not prove that any clinical plan was inappropriate or that a patient was harmed.

## Literal actionability rulebook

The actionability unit is the inherited recommendation unit, not a sentence and not an admission. A unit has the immutable fields `(target, action/modality, due timing, polarity)` after the parent's reciprocal deduplication and boundary rules. Reviewers may quote source spans and field names but may not infer missing clinical facts.

Normalize only with the frozen rulebook hash, which contains a finite dictionary of modality/exam synonyms, interval units, negation markers, conditional markers, historical markers, hypothetical markers, and target-link markers. Normalization is string/grammar normalization only; it does not use a learned model or post-label tuning. Every field receives one of `PRESENT_EXPLICIT`, `ABSENT_EXPLICIT`, `CONFLICTING`, or `UNKNOWN` plus source span(s). A reviewer cannot create a field from implied medical knowledge.

For each unit, assign exactly one of the following after independent review and locked adjudication.

* `A_ACTIONABLE`: all four required fields are explicit and internally consistent; target identity is linked to the frozen radiology unit; an unconditional action/modality is stated; a due interval or unambiguous relative timing is explicit; the source component containing the action is available (`storetime` nonmissing and `<=D`); and there is no unresolved negation, conditional/optional language, history-only/hypothetical language, contradictory restatement, or unit-boundary ambiguity. A date is usable only if it is explicitly parseable under the frozen within-subject/relative-date rule; a calendar date that cannot be reconciled to the record's permitted relative time is `UNKNOWN`, not actionable.
* `A_NONACTIONABLE`: the source is available and the text explicitly negates follow-up, makes the action conditional/optional, marks it historical or hypothetical, or explicitly lacks at least one required atom field. This is a documentation category, not a conclusion that clinical follow-up was unnecessary.
* `A_UNKNOWN`: source missing, late, unreadable, conflicting, malformed, reciprocal chain unresolved, target/coreference/negation unresolved, required timing unparseable, or disagreement remains after the locked adjudicator. `A_UNKNOWN` is never recoded as nonactionable merely because no positive span was found.

Two reviewers independently record the four field states, source spans, availability state, and reason code before seeing each other's labels. A third adjudicator sees only the packet and the two append-only records. If the adjudicator cannot establish every required field from the permitted view, the released state is `A_UNKNOWN`. Reviewer disagreement is retained as `U_A` in the raw ledger; the released conservative set includes `A_UNKNOWN` whenever either admissible reading is unknown.

Actionability is not guideline appropriateness, malignancy risk, urgency correctness, patient preference, feasibility, ownership, receipt, or benefit. A precise but clinically wrong recommendation can still be `A_ACTIONABLE` in this documentation sense.

## Literal supersession rulebook

The supersession panel uses the same frozen unit and a canonical DS restricted to `note_type==DS`. The panel records DS source spans, note IDs/storetimes, target-link evidence, old/new action and modality, old/new timing, polarity, and an explicit precedence reason. Generic statements such as “follow up with your doctor,” a nonspecific problem-list mention, or absence of a plan cannot establish supersession.

Assign one mutually exclusive state per inherited unit, using this fixed precedence when more than one DS statement is present.

1. `S_UNKNOWN` if the canonical DS is missing, nonunique, late, unreadable, contradictory at serialization, target linkage is unresolved, or independent labels cannot be resolved from the supplied view.
2. `S_CONFLICT` if two contemporaneous same-target plans are explicitly incompatible and the artifact provides no determinate precedence. This includes incompatible modality, interval/date, polarity, or cancellation versus continuation with no explicit later/authoritative relation under the frozen text rule. Do not infer precedence from author, service code, note position, or presumed clinical authority.
3. `S_SUPERSEDES` if the DS explicitly links to the same frozen target and explicitly cancels, replaces, changes, or declines the radiology action, with the changed action/modality/timing or explicit no-follow-up/alternative action extractable. A changed plan is not required to be clinically better; the label records only the textual relation.
4. `S_CONFIRM` if the DS explicitly links the same target and repeats the same action/modality and due interval/date, or explicitly says to continue the radiology plan without changing it.
5. `S_NONE` if the DS is available and no explicit same-target competing, cancelling, changing, or confirming plan is present. `S_NONE` means “not observed in this artifact,” never delivery, absence of an outside plan, or clinical appropriateness.

If the DS contains an explicit alternative but target linkage is not exact, release `S_UNKNOWN`, not `S_SUPERSEDES`. If it contains an explicit same-target change plus an unresolved second incompatible plan, release `S_CONFLICT`. `S_SUPERSEDES` and `S_CONFLICT` exclude `AUTO`; unresolved `S_UNKNOWN` also excludes `AUTO`.

## Deterministic route-admissibility set and unknown handling

Let `r_i` remain the inherited admission route after all inherited units, clocks, and route inputs are evaluated. `TIMING` remains a separate emitted subtype but is included in the reported `DEFER` family only for the added route-admissibility summaries. Let `K_i` be the set of routes compatible with every unit under the following adverse rule; there is no consensus vote.

* `AUTO` is in `K_i` only if every decision-relevant unit is `A_ACTIONABLE`, has `S_CONFIRM` or `S_NONE`, has no `S_SUPERSEDES` or `S_CONFLICT`, all required source components are available by `D`, and the inherited finite unit clock satisfies `H>=24` hours. Any `A_UNKNOWN`, `U_A`, `S_UNKNOWN`, `S_CONFLICT`, unavailable source, or under-24-hour active unit removes `AUTO`.
* `RECON` is in `K_i` when at least one unit is documentation-actionable but has an explicit superseding/competing plan or an actionable mixed state requiring human reconciliation, including a resolvable actionability/supersession disagreement. It is not admissible for fundamentally unavailable or uninterpretable evidence unless the inherited route explicitly places that state in reconciliation rather than defer.
* `DEFER` is in `K_i` when any source availability, target/unit identity, contradiction, timing, actionability, or supersession state remains unknown/unresolved, or when an active unit is not safe to auto-forward in the documentation rule. An inherited `TIMING` route is reported as `DEFER` subtype only when the established barrier is insufficient `H`.

All units are evaluated. One adverse minority unit controls the admission; no favorable unit, consensus, domain, pooled estimate, shrinkage, later note, or panel majority rescues it. If a panel label is nonresponsive or the packet is corrupted, the adverse-compatible set includes every route that could be blocked by that missing evidence and therefore excludes `AUTO`; the missingness indicator remains separate from the substantive label.

The primary added estimand is the unchanged parent-weighted admission-level proportion

`Theta_R = sum_i w_i I(r_i in K_i) / sum_i w_i`,

with inherited design weights and nested uncertainty. Report `Theta_AUTO`, `Theta_RECON`, and `Theta_DEFER` components, excess `AUTO` (`r_i=AUTO` and `AUTO not in K_i`), missed reconciliation (`r_i in {DEFER,TIMING}` and `RECON in K_i`), unresolved mass, and unit-level actionability/supersession distributions. These are finite-population stored-documentation/operational estimands, not clinical outcomes or treatment effects.

## Threshold governance and source-feasibility registration

All numerical margins below are frozen in a governance manifest before any real label, route result, or panel disagreement is opened. The manifest contains the protocol hash, rulebook hash, source/schema hashes, fixed sample counts, atom counts, expected reviewer roster, capacity, endpoint definitions, and sign-off timestamp. No threshold is learned, optimized, or justified by labels. Numerical margins are operational prerequisites for nominating a prospective silent validation, not acceptable clinical harm rates.

Retain all inherited margins exactly, including the inherited `tau_AUTO=0.05`, confirmation `rho=0.90`, `AC1=0.70`, route capture/precision margins, simultaneous family, and atom event/ESS/weight requirements. Add these fixed actionability/supersession gates:

* In every claimable atom, the lower simultaneous 95% bound for independent released actionability support among candidate `AUTO` units is at least `0.90`; the upper simultaneous adverse-compatible fraction of `A_UNKNOWN` or unresolved actionability among candidate `AUTO` units is at most `0.05`.
* In every claimable atom, the lower simultaneous 95% bound for explicit same-target supersession/conflict capture by `RECON` or `DEFER` is at least `0.95` among units independently identified as requiring that route; the upper adverse-compatible fraction of `AUTO` among such units is at most `0.05`.
* The upper simultaneous adverse-compatible fraction of `AUTO` units with `A_ACTIONABLE` plus `S_NONE/S_CONFIRM` that nonetheless remain route-incompatible under another admissible reading is at most `0.05`.
* Independent panel disagreement/nonresponse/packet missingness is treated adverse-compatibly and must have upper bound at most `0.05` in each claimable atom; otherwise that atom is `INCONCLUSIVE`.
* The inherited challenge, leakage, chronology, route-invariance, decisive-control, domain, support, ESS, workload, and all-unit gates must pass simultaneously. No new gate can relax an inherited gate.

The source-feasibility check is performed before labels using only the frozen population and allocation: a claimable cell must have at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 non-sentinel admissions where the inherited protocol requires them, positive inclusion probability, route-denominator ESS at least 50, and at least two challenge admissions per occupied final atlas atom when feasible under the inherited sample. If any requirement cannot be met from pre-label counts, the atom is marked unsupported or `INCONCLUSIVE`; it is not merged, topped up, or declared safe. A census cell does not convert panel agreement into clinical truth.

For rare events, use inherited exact finite-population inversion and the widest valid simultaneous bound from exact inversion, nested Rao-Wu replicates, and adverse-compatible partial identification. Unknown states are not complete-case deleted. A latent-class model is diagnostic only unless an independent pre-label identifiability constraint exists; it cannot narrow the primary bounds.

## Exact additive workload and admission ledger

The inherited ledger is copied verbatim and remains the controlling contract: every selected admission receives all inherited component, audit, redaction, minority-unit, holistic, challenge, and fixed-control work; the sentinel census and exact formula samples are unchanged; no additional admission may be recruited; reserve status is fixed before labels; duplicate readings do not increase the distinct-admission denominator; and total distinct admissions must be `<=900`.

The child adds an explicit, auditable row to that ledger for each selected admission and for each pre-label challenge control packet assigned to it. Define `n_sel` as the already-frozen number of selected admissions, `n_res` as the already-frozen reserve count, and `q_ctrl` as the already-frozen number of additional transformed control packets in the inherited challenge universe. These are counts, not outcomes. The maximum added physician-minute charge is

`M_add = 46*n_sel + 46*q_ctrl`,

where the 46-minute unit is fixed as follows: actionability reviewer A1 6 minutes, actionability reviewer A2 6, actionability adjudicator 8, supersession reviewer S1 8, supersession reviewer S2 8, and supersession adjudicator 10. Every selected admission receives the base actionability and supersession packet. Every control packet receives the same relevant review pair and adjudication rule; if a control does not contain the source spans needed for a panel's label, it is a software-level control-infeasible result and is not silently hand-edited. `q_ctrl` and its packet identities are frozen before labels.

For transparency, the ledger records separate columns, not only the sum:

`L = (n_sel, n_res, q_ctrl, M_parent_actual, M_parent_cap, M_add_base, M_add_ctrl, M_total_cap, h_total, h_per_atom, max_packet_minutes, nonresponse_count, adjudication_count)`.

`M_add_base=46*n_sel`; `M_add_ctrl=46*q_ctrl`; `M_total_cap=M_parent_cap+M_add_base+M_add_ctrl`. The parent absolute workload budget and its inherited 1.50 workload-ratio margin are unchanged. Governance must verify before labels that `M_total_cap` is within that same approved absolute budget and that every claimable atom's atom-specific worst-case charge (including its allocated controls and reserve charge if the inherited ledger charges reserve) is within the same budget. If this pre-label inequality fails, the protocol returns `NO_SELECTION`; it is never fixed by lowering minutes, dropping adjudicators, dropping controls, or reducing the sample.

Actual minutes are recorded by role and packet, including start/end timestamps, nonresponse, interruption, and adjudication. A role may finish early, but the predeclared cap remains the upper-bound charge used for feasibility. A case exceeding its role cap remains labeled under the same rulebook and is charged its actual time; any atom or global budget failure is adverse. Reviewers cannot stop after an apparently favorable label. Reserve admissions are not used to repair a failed sample; if the inherited ledger requires their fixed charge, they are charged before labels exactly as specified, and if not selected they remain unreviewed.

The admission ceiling is checked twice: once after the inherited draw and again after packet materialization. The second check must still equal `n_sel<=900`; adding a transformed packet, a duplicate panel reading, or an adjudicator never adds a subject-admission row. A mismatch, hidden duplicate, or route-triggered reviewer allocation invalidates the run.

## Controls and falsification criteria

Freeze all controls and expected transformations before labels. The controls are diagnostics, never evidence of clinical correctness and never replacements for real units.

1. Remove target/action/interval spans from the actionability view. The released state must be `A_UNKNOWN` or `A_NONACTIONABLE`, never `A_ACTIONABLE`.
2. Replace a DS target span with a provenance-matched unrelated target in a synthetic control. `S_SUPERSEDES` must become `S_NONE` or `S_UNKNOWN`, never remain linked.
3. Shift DS `storetime` to after `D` without changing text. The primary state must become `S_UNKNOWN`; a later packet cannot repair it.
4. Inject one explicit incompatible modality or interval. The output must be `S_CONFLICT` or `S_SUPERSEDES`, and `AUTO` must be excluded.
5. Provide a precise radiology atom but no available canonical DS. `AUTO` cannot be gained from radiology precision alone.
6. Set one unit in a clean admission to `A_UNKNOWN` or `S_CONFLICT`. Whole-admission `AUTO` must be excluded.
7. Permute irrelevant formatting/author-like tokens or unrelated section order. The actionability/supersession state and route must remain invariant; author-like text cannot establish responsibility or precedence.
8. Reorder field rows, addenda, or packet serialization without changing the locked source identity. Outputs must remain invariant; duplicate detail reconstruction must not create an extra unit.
9. Permute panel labels relative to routes. Any apparent route-admissibility signal that survives the predeclared null permutation indicates linkage or leakage.
10. Plant supersession only in one held-out service/possible-calendar atom while pooled results pass. The atom-specific gate must reject that atom; pooled performance cannot rescue it.

A run is invalid if any panel sees route, atom/domain/stratum, weights, outcomes, another panel's output, or post-label transformed text; if a control is made after labels; if labels are joined before seals; if unknowns are favorable; if a roster overlaps; or if a route is selected before all required seals.

## Analysis, interpretation, and evidence ceiling

Recompute source and schema hashes, joins, reciprocal links, canonical DS, availability clocks, inherited units/states/routes, exact sample and weights, packets, panel seals, actionability/supersession fields, `K_i`, ledger, HT/Hájek estimates, exact bounds, Rao-Wu replicates, and simultaneous gates. Use the same all-admission unit and nested design uncertainty as the parents. Report feasibility counts separately from label estimates and preserve raw disagreement and unknown reasons.

Supportive results require all inherited gates, at least one support-qualified atom, the new actionability/supersession gates, control invariance, domain-specific workload pass, and a positive lower bound for the proposed route burden. They support only a site-local prospective silent validation of the exact stored-documentation route with all unqualified signatures forced to `DEFER`.

Adverse results include an excess-`AUTO` bound crossing its gate, missed explicit supersession/conflict, actionability unknown mass crossing its gate, a route-invariant control flip, a chronology or leakage failure, an atom-specific workload failure, or a minority-unit failure. These falsify the proposed documentation route or its domain claim, not clinical benefit, safety, or clinician performance.

Inconclusive results include infeasible pre-label source support, too few required atom/route cases, census-only or zero-positive cells where the parent requires noncensus support, broad partial-identification bounds, nonresponse, packet corruption, broken seals, missing/late canonical DS, control infeasibility, or lack of approved workload capacity. Inconclusive is not evidence of safety and never permits `AUTO`.

The verifier can establish only computational claims: source identity, chronology, target linkage under the locked rubric, route derivation, unknown handling, finite-population estimates/bounds, sample probabilities, workload arithmetic, leakage, and control behavior. It cannot establish whether a recommendation was medically appropriate, whether a DS plan was clinically superior, whether an artifact was seen or transmitted, who was responsible, whether a patient acted, whether follow-up occurred, or whether a route improved outcomes. Those claims require expert clinical adjudication with richer context and a prospective silent study logging finalization, viewing, transmission, recipient/responsibility, orders/referrals, scheduling, patient preference, outside care, and outcomes. Causal benefit or safety requires a separately governed comparative study.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to the primary parent, this child makes the actionability and supersession constructs executable at field, span, linkage, precedence, and timestamp level; defines an explicit three-way unknown-safe route set; preregisters governance and source-feasibility conditions without label-driven threshold tuning; and supplies an additive minute-by-minute ledger whose pre-label worst case can be checked against the unchanged parent budget and 900-admission ceiling. Relative to the correlated-error parent, it retains disjoint holistic panels, independent codebooks, adversarial pairs, and adverse-compatible partial identification while applying the same safeguards to these new panels.

The remaining uncertainty is substantive rather than hidden in prose: independent reviewers are not a clinical gold standard and may share error; the canonical DS may not have been visible at physical departure; service and author fields do not prove ownership; MIMIC cannot show delivery, appropriateness, safety, benefit, or causality; and sparse atoms may honestly yield no selection. Those limitations are preserved as explicit no-rescue and prospective-evidence requirements.
