# Proposal: history- and capture-conditioned interpretation of concordant post-TACE pathways

## Episode and scientific deliverable

This Episode-30 successor evolves assessed-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent’s foundational repair is retained: a downstream event is graded as a strict timed liver-directed procedure, a same-encounter order-concordant procedure (C2), procedure-only (C1), or broad/ambiguous (C0), with early transitions and local observation loss retained rather than silently conditioned away.

The remaining clinically consequential uncertainty is whether even C2 is transportable across patients. A patient without an earlier locally recorded liver-directed procedure may have an initial course; a patient with earlier local procedures may be undergoing retreatment. The snapshot cannot establish treatment history outside this hospital. The same AFP/hepatic trajectory can therefore have different recorded-pathway meaning, and a pooled estimate could be misleading for reassessment decisions.

The actual future deliverable is newly constructed and fitted, not a presumed positive result:

1. Reproducible index, early-transition, laboratory-profile, local-history, capture, endpoint-tier and patient-temporal-split manifests for every eligible index patient.
2. A prespecified three-level local-history modifier: no earlier locally recorded liver-directed episode, earlier episode, and indeterminate/insufficient pre-index observation, with counts and overlap diagnostics.
3. Matched transparent competing-transition models and a hierarchical irregular-time multi-source model estimating profile contrasts within history strata and their interaction, for C2 and C1/C0 overlays.
4. Locked-test standardized cumulative-incidence contrasts, calibration/log scores, patient-clustered intervals, missing-history sensitivity bounds, and negative-control/placebo results.
5. An interpretation map linking every conclusion to the exact output, endpoint tier, history stratum, boundary, split and uncertainty criterion.

Completion is the new estimation of the history-by-profile interaction and its sensitivity across endpoint/capture definitions. A null interaction is a valid result; no fitted result is claimed in discovery.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

Available evidence supports only that this HCC snapshot has dated procedures, orders, examinations, encounters and labs that can be joined by patient/encounter keys. The audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 28,159,928 lab rows, 16,730,319 non-drug-order rows and 105,044 encounters; it found 190,022 AFP, 238,293 albumin and 238,553 total-bilirubin rows. These are availability facts, not clinical associations. Procedure labels are heterogeneous and include diagnostic hepatic angiography and mixed bundles; orders frequently contain repeated composite plans.

The unresolved claim is:

> Does the association between an early post-index AFP/hepatic component profile and a later concordant recorded therapeutic episode differ by prior locally recorded liver-directed history, after standardizing pre-index disease-contact and measurement opportunity? Or is the apparent pathway signal invariant or explained by capture/workflow intensity?

Primary hypothesis (noncausal):

> Among adults with a structured HCC diagnosis and a first eligible recorded TACE-like episode, the favorable-versus-discordant early component-profile contrast for first C2 concordant recorded therapeutic episode through day 320 is different in the prior-local-history strata; specifically, a favorable profile is hypothesized to show a smaller absolute C2 risk contrast after an earlier local liver-directed episode than in the no-earlier-local-episode stratum.

The direction is clinically motivated but not assumed to be true. The primary estimand is the interaction in standardized absolute cumulative incidence at day 320, with a prespecified 3 percentage-point practical margin and patient-level 95% interval. C1 and C0 contrasts, procedure-only and workflow-supported tiers, and early transition probabilities are secondary ascertainment analyses. No contrast is causal or evidence of response, progression, treatment completion or benefit.

This matters because a single laboratory-based reassessment rule could be inappropriate if recorded prior treatment changes the meaning of the same trajectory. A stable interaction across C2 and C1, with weak effects on non-liver and diagnostic controls, would motivate prospective chart/radiology adjudication and history-aware reassessment research. A null interaction would support a pooled recorded-care association, still requiring validation. A reversal or history-only signal would caution against one-size-fits-all interpretation.

## Population and time

Use one first eligible timed index per patient from `procedures`, forming 24-hour episodes within (`Patient master index`, `Encounter number`) using `Start time`; retain tied rows and source labels. The frozen strict screen includes literal `TACE`, `Transarterial chemoembolization`, or both `Hepatic artery` and `embolization`; hepatic angiography alone is not therapeutic. Require linked `encounters` age >=18, nonmissing `Sex`, and a `diagnoses` row with `Diagnosis name` containing `Hepatocellular carcinoma` linked to an encounter `Encounter time` in [t0-180,t0+7] days. Require t0 <= 2025-01-01. Diagnosis is an encounter-time ascertainment proxy, not onset.

Retain all eligible patients, including early therapeutic events, missing trajectories and disappearance from local records. Model full-index transitions t0 through day 45. Use L=14 and L=45 information boundaries: last eligible pre-index values and first eligible numeric values in days 7-14 or 7-45. Predictors stop at L; downstream windows end at day 365 after t0, with primary post-boundary C2 CIF at day 320. A day-45 no-early-therapeutic risk set is a parent-comparison sensitivity only.

## History modifier and outcomes

Aggregate child tables before joining to encounters using the two-key join (`Patient Master Index`,`Encounter Number`). Define prior local history using only timed procedures with valid `Start Time) strictly before t0 and outside the index 24-hour episode:

- H0: no earlier strict or broad liver-directed episode observed in the local procedure file;
- H1: at least one earlier episode with a liver-directed label under the frozen broad screen, reporting strict TACE-like and other liver-directed subcounts;
- HU: insufficient valid pre-index local observation (for example no encounter history before t0 in the available local record or invalid/missing prior procedure times), retained rather than classified as H0.

H0 is “no prior local record,” not treatment naive. The broad screen and a strict-only H1 sensitivity must be frozen from training data. Report history burden and time since last local episode without treating them as complete exposure history.

Primary C2 is a strict timed procedure episode matched to a same-key `orders` row whose `Orders (non-medication)` passes the same training-frozen TACE-like order screen and whose `Order time` or `Start time` is within +/-24 hours of the procedure episode. C1 is strict procedure-only not C2; C0 contains broad/ambiguous/mixed liver-directed records. Diagnostic/technical, non-liver, and administrative local-observation-loss states are competing. Examination workflow using `Examination` and `Start time` is a secondary tier; `Examination findings` and `Examination diagnosis` are sensitivity-only narrative evidence. Orders do not prove intent or completion.

Define the AFP/hepatic profile component-wise: exact training-frozen assay names `alpha-fetoprotein`, `albumin`, `total bilirubin`; retain numeric, qualitative/inequality, specimen, timestamp, delays, counts and missingness. Do not pool undocumented units or name ALBI/MELD. A favorable profile is a prespecified AFP decline plus non-worsening albumin/bilirubin; the solver must also report continuous components and avoid replacing missing response with imputation.

## Baseline, alternative and analysis

B_history is a transparent regularized discrete-time cause-specific competing-transition model, matched across L=14/L=45, H0/H1/HU, C2/C1/C0, splits and uncertainty. Predictors are age, sex, index timing/department, HCC ascertainment, prior procedure burden/timing/history, encounter/order/examination/medication capture, pre-index component levels and the early profile, exact delays and missingness. Standardize profile-group contrasts to the same history/capture distribution; report within-history contrasts and the profile-by-history interaction. Exclude endpoint-defining order evidence from predictors.

M_history is a substantive hierarchical irregular-time multi-view latent model. A latent history-conditioned recorded-care state generates source-specific procedure/order/examination evidence with timing kernels and missingness/capture processes, while a smooth latent AFP/hepatic trajectory feeds competing hazards. Hierarchical stratum effects share information but allow H0/H1/HU differences; weakly informative prior sensitivity and held-out source prediction are required. This can reveal whether the interaction is a graded source-discordance/measurement phenomenon rather than a hard history label, information lost by the baseline. It is useful only if it converges, improves held-out source log score/Brier/calibration or clarifies uncertainty, and gives stable interaction intervals across priors. It cannot validate the latent state clinically.

Use patient-level temporal split: 2010-2022 fit, 2023 tuning, 2024-2025-01-01 locked test when support permits; otherwise deterministic participant-hash split. Fit lexical screens, assay rules, history thresholds, models, standardization and weights on training only. Use patient-clustered bootstrap or patient-level resampling. Require overlap/positivity diagnostics, calibration, Brier/dynamic log score, CIF and absolute risk differences with intervals. Planned solver envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds; these are unverified planning limits, with CPU default. No GPU is required; an allocated A100 would be an optional implementation check, not scientific merit.

## Falsification and result interpretation

Required checks include:

1. Reproduce parent C2/C1 continuity and full-index early transition accounting; failures indicate packaging/definition errors.
2. Test the history interaction across C2, C1, C0, strict-only history, +/-6/24/72-hour order windows, order status sensitivity and same- versus cross-encounter matching.
3. Test non-liver and diagnostic/technical endpoints; similar profile-by-history interactions weaken disease-specific interpretation.
4. Permute eligible post-index order times within patient and eligible assay times while preserving names/specimens/counts. Persistence after permutation is adverse to timing/pathway interpretation.
5. Compare H0/H1/HU capture distributions, history positivity/weight stability, complete-pair versus qualitative-as-missing analyses, observation-weighted versus unweighted estimates, and L=14 versus L=45.
6. Run pre-index placebo trajectories and department/capture controls. A strong placebo or capture-only signal is adverse.
7. Require latent convergence, posterior/predictive checks, held-out source performance and prior-stability; nonconvergence or prior-dominated interaction is inconclusive.

Supportive evidence requires adequate H0/H1/HU overlap; a practically meaningful, directionally prespecified C2 interaction on both L boundaries; compatible C1/C0 specificity pattern; weak unrelated-endpoint/placebo effects; stable timing/status sensitivities; calibrated predictions; and a converged alternative that does not materially change the interaction under prior sensitivity. It supports only history-dependent association with locally recorded evidence and motivates adjudicated validation.

Adverse evidence includes a null/reversed C2 interaction with a procedure-only interaction, capture/non-liver/placebo effects, instability from history definition/order window, poor overlap, or latent prior domination. These favor coding, measurement, local-history misclassification or workflow explanations.

Inconclusive evidence includes sparse C2 cells in H1/HU, no valid matches, positivity failure, insufficient pre-index observation, unstable lexical/assay definitions, temporal-split failure, or missing evidence. It does not establish absence of response, progression, benefit or treatment effect.

The verifier can check source paths, schemas, headers, key aggregation, timestamps, split isolation, endpoint/history construction, fits, predictions, intervals, calibrations, permutations and output-to-conclusion links. It cannot establish outside treatment history, intent, completion, technical success, radiologic/pathologic response, recurrence/progression, mortality, survival, benefit, utility, appropriateness or external validity. Those require clinical chart/radiology/pathology adjudication, linked mortality/outside-care data and prospective/external validation.
