Title: Positive-control reproducibility and documentation-measurement falsification for the eICU ventilated landmark cohort

Scientific question
Can the inherited eICU person-landmark cohort be reconstructed from the frozen raw snapshot using an independently implemented, fully specified eligibility algorithm, with exact preservation of the 540 target-labelled rows and their decision times? This is a prerequisite measurement question, not a denominator estimate and not a clinical-effect question.

Clinical importance
The proposed denominator audit would quantify how often either target care-plan label is documented among otherwise-eligible ventilated landmarks and how that documentation varies by hospital and landmark. Those quantities are uninterpretable if the cohort restriction itself is not reproducible. A failure to reproduce the positive control is clinically consequential because it identifies a measurement/transportability threat: apparent site or time variation could reflect undocumented cohort-construction conventions rather than care-plan documentation. The result therefore falsifies the assumption that the inherited target-labelled cohort is a sufficiently specified, portable measurement construct.

Evidence already supported
The inherited assessed-valid work reports a frozen target-labelled cohort of 540 person-landmarks, 443 unique stays/persons, 74 hospitals, five landmarks (1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200 minutes), and labels 309 daily-extubation-evaluation and 231 no-daily-extubation-trial. The inherited attrition targets are 3 ambiguous landmarks; 3,743 adult landmarks observed through decision time; 2,232 with invasive-ventilation evidence; 667 with the strict physiologic screen; 573 with both; 548 after end-of-life/prior-spontaneous exclusions; and 540 after first qualifying stay selection. These are inherited aggregate targets, not evidence that the present independent implementation reproduces them.

Untested/falsifiable claim
An independent implementation of the inherited rules, applied to the complete frozen eICU snapshot, will reproduce the inherited positive-control cohort. The claim is falsified if any locked aggregate, landmark-by-label count, decision-time convention, or row-identity digest differs. Aggregate agreement alone is insufficient for exact row identity.

6. Stop the denominator extension under every failed or unverifiable identity result. In particular, do not remove target-label receipt, do not retain neither-label rows for rate estimation, and do not report target-label coverage or hospital/landmark transportability rates.

Primary outcomes
The primary outcome is exact positive-control reproducibility: row-key set equality and exact D, followed by equality of all locked attrition and stratified counts. Secondary outcomes are the location and magnitude of discrepancies by rule component, including strict-screen, invasive-evidence, exclusion, stay-selection, hospital, landmark, and label strata.

Falsification criteria
The inherited reproducibility claim is falsified by any nonzero row-key symmetric difference, any differing decision offset, or any mismatch in the locked aggregate/landmark counts after the exact inherited algorithm is executed. A rule-grid match that lacks row-level digest comparison is inconclusive, not positive. A mismatch that disappears only under an unprespecified variant demonstrates specification sensitivity and remains a measurement limitation; it does not authorize a denominator.

Interpretation gates
Supportive: exact row-key and D equality, all locked counts equal, and the matched builder and digest are independently archived. Only then may the Lead extend the same eligibility predicate by removing target-label receipt and perform the denominator-aware audit.
Adverse: the current observed mismatch, or a mismatch persisting across the preregistered grid. Conclude that the cohort restriction is not independently reproducible from available specification; report the denominator estimand as not identified by this workspace. This is a useful falsification of measurement portability, not evidence about ventilation management or patient benefit.
Inconclusive: aggregate agreement without row-level access, or several equally plausible rule variants matching different subsets. Conclude that identity is unresolved and request the missing artifact, exact code, or adjudicated rule specification.

Evidence limitations
Care-plan labels are chart documentation and are not proof of an SBT, extubation evaluation at bedside, extubation, durable liberation, quality, causal effect, or clinical benefit. The prior independent durable-liberation ascertainment failed and the adverse/unstable 12-hour chart proxies remain frozen; they are not outcomes of this reproducibility experiment. Missing charting is not absence of ventilation, action, or care. The inaccessible inherited artifact prevents exact row-level comparison here. Any final denominator result would additionally require clinical adjudication of label semantics and documentation completeness, and a separate study for causal or benefit claims.
