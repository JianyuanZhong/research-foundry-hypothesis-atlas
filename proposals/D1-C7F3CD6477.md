# Episode 96 successor: a fixed-capacity, label-free D7 review boundary with an absolute random-review anchor

## Decision and unresolved question

At the 24-hour landmark, a receiving ICU may have exactly 10% of eligible patients available for mortality-review or reassessment, but no local outcome labels with which to fit a score. The decision is whether a score trained in the opposite EICU repeat-lactate practice stratum should determine that queue.

The unresolved question is:

> In each cross-practice direction, does a source-practice, source-label-only score containing the optional repeat lactate enrich a fixed 10%-per-hospital queue for D7 death by at least 3 percentage points over the otherwise identical random-review queue, while also clearing the parent’s process-only increment and remaining no more than 2 points below the held-out local-label benchmark?

This is a retrospective deployment-boundary hypothesis. The 3-point random-review margin is an operational materiality margin for deciding whether a prospective impact study is justified; it is not a preventable-death rate, a staffing threshold, or a validated clinical utility threshold.

The substantive advance over Episode 95 is an absolute clinical baseline: what happens when the same slots are assigned at random. The process-only and held-out-local safeguards remain, so a favorable absolute yield cannot be mistaken for transportability. The experiment remains a ranking/enrichment experiment, not evidence that review improves outcomes.

## Evidence versus hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports a bounded rationale only. The parent’s supplied EICU audit reconstructs a feasible 2,302-person, 30-hospital cohort with baseline lactate, optional repeat lactate, structured monitoring, and D7/Dinf discharge outcomes. Baysan et al. (DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750; PMCID PMC9444407), as recorded in the parent, reported only modest discrimination change when 24-hour lactate was added to an APACHE-IV model; it did not test a fixed-capacity queue or source-practice transport. Sisk et al. (DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242; PMCID PMC7810439), also recorded by the parent, motivates accounting for observation processes but does not establish transport of a repeat-lactate signal between EICU hospitals. Diab et al. (DOI 10.3389/fmed.2025.1679297; frozen source ID [source checksum]) studied 574 adults at one ED and reported a borderline adjusted association between lactate clearance and in-hospital mortality (OR 0.66, 95% CI 0.42–1.04); it did not study this EICU landmark, fixed capacity, or cross-hospital label-free transport. These sources support why the question is plausible, not that it is true. The research-ambition demonstrations were inspected as demonstrations of rigor, not as evidence for this hypothesis; the cancer demonstration’s main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable in the supplied references.

The untested claim is the joint, worst-direction queue margin below. Prognostic ranking, workflow benefit, causal effect, preventability, fairness, and clinical adjudication are separate claims and are not inferred from a supportive result.

## Primary estimand and hypothesis

Use the frozen Low and High practice strata from the parent. For direction d in {Low-to-High, High-to-Low}, let T_d be the 15 target hospitals and S_d the opposite 15 source hospitals. For target hospital h, let n_h be its eligible cohort size and k_h = ceil(0.10 n_h). Let K_d = sum_h k_h.

For each target hospital, construct four queues of exactly k_h distinct eligible patients, ranked by the raw linear predictor with ascending patientunitstayid as the only tie-breaker:

- P: portable R_full, fitted only on S_d;
- 0: source-only R_process, fitted only on S_d;
- L: held-out local-label R_full, fitted on T_d excluding h and scored only on h;
- R: random review. Its expected D7 yield is computed without random Monte Carlo as hospital D7 prevalence times k_h: R_d = [sum_h (k_h/n_h) sum_i D7_i] / K_d. Also emit 2,000 fixed-seed without-replacement random queue draws as a diagnostic; they cannot replace the exact expectation.

For x in {P,0,L}, Y^x_d = [sum_h sum_{i in Q^x_dh} D7_i] / K_d.

The single primary estimand is:

M_queue = min_d { Y^P_d - max(Y^R_d + 0.03, Y^0_d + 0.03, Y^L_d - 0.02) }.

The primary hypothesis is M_queue >= 0; deployment failure is M_queue < 0. Thus the portable queue must beat random review and the process-only baseline by at least 0.03 D7 deaths per occupied slot, and be no more than 0.02 below the held-out local-label benchmark, in the weaker direction. Component yields and contrasts are reported, but only M_queue determines the primary state.

This is a risk-enrichment and transport estimand. It does not estimate a treatment effect of review, a causal effect of lactate testing, the number of preventable deaths, or benefit from assigning staff to the queue. A random-review yield is an absolute prognostic baseline, not an impact-control arm.

## Deterministic result states

Supportive requires all provenance, cohort, support, computation, and stability gates below; at least 1,900 of 2,000 joint copied-hospital replicates finite; deletion stability; and the two-sided 2.5th-percentile endpoint of M_queue at least 0.

Adverse requires valid computation and the 97.5th-percentile endpoint of M_queue below 0. This rejects the exact joint label-free deployment boundary. It does not show that repeat lactate has no prognostic information.

Inconclusive applies when the interval crosses zero, fewer than 1,900 joint replicates are finite, deletion instability occurs, support or audit fails, or any required computation is invalid. A favorable point estimate, one favorable direction, a stress capacity, a calibration result, or Dinf cannot create a supportive result.

The six stress capacities {0.05, 0.075, 0.125, 0.15, 0.175, 0.20} are diagnostics only. Capacity is fixed at 0.10 before analysis and cannot be selected from results.

Dinf is a secondary horizon warning only. Emit the same queue contrasts using Dinf. If its interval is adverse, report “D7 [state]; eventual in-hospital horizon warning.” Do not call the score safe, validated, or beneficial. Dinf compatibility does not establish eventual-mortality transport.

## Interpretation and clinical limits

Supportive means only that, in this fixed retrospective EICU construction, a source-practice R_full ranking enriched a 10%-per-hospital queue for D7 discharge-record death above random review and the two transport safeguards in both directions. It would justify a prospective, independently labeled impact study of a predefined reassessment workflow. It would not show that review changes care, that any death was preventable, that repeat lactate causes risk, that a patient needs fluid or vasopressor escalation, or that staffing the queue improves survival.

Adverse means the exact no-local-label deployment boundary failed under the prespecified capacity and margins. It does not mean lactate lacks prognostic information, that random review is clinically equivalent in every setting, or that a different capacity or population should be selected post hoc.

Inconclusive means the available EICU data cannot distinguish the boundary from its null after uncertainty and stability controls. It requires more labeled hospitals, better temporal/external validation, or a revised design approved as a new Lead proposal; it must not trigger threshold, capacity, subgroup, or time-window mining.

EICU cannot establish true norepinephrine administration or indication; transfer on ongoing drug; dose/rate comparability; specimen type, collection rationale, assay validity or result availability; staffing or actual review capacity; review action; preventability; treatment limitations; exact death time/cause; tissue perfusion; or fairness and clinical utility. vitalPeriodic is a five-minute summary rather than a raw waveform, and narrative notes are not used. Clinical medication/specimen adjudication, synchronized workflow and capacity data, expert fairness/utility review, independent temporal/external validation, and a prospective impact or randomized study are required for those stronger claims. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility only.
