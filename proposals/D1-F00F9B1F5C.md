# Leakage-safe two-point laboratory history beyond the current pre-repeat assay

## Targeted repair and evidence boundary

At a recorded first repeat TACE, does a small summary of the patient's prior albumin or total-bilirubin record improve prediction of the next observed same-assay value beyond the contemporaneous pre-repeat value and a compact decision-time core?

The strongest supported claim is feasibility and missingness, not association. A complete-source audit scanned every required row in encounters, diagnoses, procedures, medications, and labs, sampled nothing, and inspected only cohort membership, clocks, assay availability, repeated-history counts, and missingness. It did not calculate assay directions, differences, slopes, predictor-outcome associations, arm-outcome associations, or models. It reproduced 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator patients, 159/526 selected repeat events, albumin triplets of 40/118, and bilirubin triplets of 41/119. In `[TACE2_time,event_time-72h)`, excluding every row from the selected repeat-event encounter, at least one same-assay timestamp exists for albumin in 37/40 systemic and 108/118 comparator triplets and for bilirubin in 38/41 and 108/119. Only 18 systemic and 42 comparator patients per assay have at least two timestamps; only 8/13 have three or more. A slope or multi-parameter trajectory is therefore not supported, but a two-point representation using the already-required remote anchor and the latest interval value is feasible.

The unresolved hypothesis is that this locked two-point history improves paired out-of-fold prediction of `Y` beyond `P`. Support would establish only reproducible incremental prediction of the next observed laboratory measurement in selected, observed repeat-TACE episodes. It would not establish causation, toxicity, hepatic decompensation, treatment benefit, clinical utility, or a reason to change treatment.

## Locked parsimonious history

For the same assay, form the leakage-safe interval `[exact TACE2_time,event_time-72h)`. Exclude every row whose `就诊号` equals `event_encounter`, regardless of timestamp. Let `L` be the latest valid uncensored result in this interval, with later source order resolving a same-time concordant duplicate. Define:

- `D = 1` if `L` exists, otherwise 0;
- `H = L-B` when `D=1`; impute `H=0` when `D=0` and include `D`.

`B` and `P` enter both models; hence `H` tests whether the later pre-repeat state relative to the remote anchor adds information, not whether any remote value beats the current assay. This two-degree-of-freedom addition is the most complex history supported by the audit. Do not add slope, count, recency, interactions, other assays, or require observed `L`. Report history availability by arm, assay, and fold before outcomes. The 13/158 and 14/160 patients lacking `L` remain in the estimand.

## Nested prediction experiment

Fit each assay separately on its complete-triplet population, using identical patients and folds:

`M0: Y ~ P + B + arm + age + sex + TACE2_to_repeat_days + P_lead_hours`

`M1: Y ~ P + B + arm + age + sex + TACE2_to_repeat_days + P_lead_hours + H + D`

Use [prior hypothesis]'s outcome-blind, arm-balanced five-fold rule and primary salt `hcc-repeat-tace-b-beyond-p-v1-r00`; preserve r01-r19 partition sensitivities. In each outer training fold only: median-impute age, include age-missing indicator, encode fixed sex indicators, remove variance-zero columns symmetrically, standardize continuous predictors, and choose ridge penalty `{0.01,0.1,1,10,100}` separately for each model by inner leave-one-patient-out MSE, smaller penalty on a tie. Intercept is unpenalized. Do not replace the model after seeing outcomes.

Primary estimand for assay `a`:

`Delta_H,a = [RMSE(M0)-RMSE(M1)]/SD(Y)`

using paired pooled out-of-fold predictions and one pooled sample SD. Higher favors history. Report native RMSE/MAE, standardized MAE increment, calibration intercept/slope, prediction variance, fold errors, penalties, and arm-stratified descriptive errors. There is no arm interaction or treatment-effect estimand.

Use the parent's 2,000-replicate fixed-seed stratified paired-tuple bootstrap, 200-replicate full-pipeline bootstrap, two-assay Holm correction, partition criteria, patient-linkage permutation (permute `(H,D)` jointly within arm/fold), timing variants, influence checks, and repeat-event/triplet observation-weight sensitivity. The standardized design margin remains 0.05 and is not a clinical importance threshold. A panel-level statement requires both assays to pass the parent's concordance logic; otherwise report separately.

## Falsification, feasibility, and branch precedence

1. **Negative feasibility:** if fewer than 80% of complete triplets in either arm have `L`, any primary held-out fold has fewer than five `D=1` patients per arm, `D` is variance-zero in a training fold, or M1 is non-executable, do not fit/promote the history test. Preserve the audit as evidence that richer history is unavailable and retain the B-beyond-P parent as the feasible assay. The audit passes this pre-outcome gate: primary folds contain 6-8 systemic and 21-22 comparator `L`-observed patients for albumin, and 7-8 and 20-23 for bilirubin.
2. **Reconstruction/computation failure:** snapshot/count/parser/join/clock mismatch, leakage, inadequate held-out arm size, zero pooled outcome variance, or bootstrap estimability failure is inconclusive and stops favorable interpretation.
3. **Selection-, timing-, partition-, or influence-sensitive:** apply the parent's gates; any failure takes precedence over support. A sign reversal in an adequately retained timing variant, weighted reversal/difference >0.05, poor overlap/ESS, >4/20 partition sign failures, or influential-patient reversal is inconclusive.
4. **Adverse degradation:** with all gates passed, an interval wholly below zero or materially worse calibration means the locked history worsened prediction under this model, not that historical monitoring is harmful.
5. **Supportive:** all gates pass; `Delta_H >= 0.05`; unadjusted interval excludes zero; Holm-adjusted one-sided bootstrap tail probability <0.05; MAE increment nonnegative; observed gain exceeds the 95th percentile of linkage permutations; at least 16/20 partitions are positive with median >=0.05; and full-pipeline, timing, calibration, influence, and selection checks are nonadverse. Conclude only incremental prediction of the next observed same-assay value.
6. **Precise null:** all gates pass and the upper interval bound is below 0.05. Conclude no detectable gain of the design-sized magnitude in this selected cohort/model, not equivalence or absence of biology/utility.
7. **No demonstrated increment:** point estimate <0.05 or linkage destruction reproduces it, without precise-null or adverse interval.
8. **Inconclusive:** uncertainty spans meaningful degradation and +0.05 or a remaining prespecified ambiguity exists.

The pseudo-current event-specific gate is a locked negative-feasibility branch: although 18/42 have two historical timestamps, only 2 systemic and 5 comparator patients per assay have a pseudo-current pair plus a history observation separated by 72 hours. Do not fit it, loosen the separation, or claim event specificity/mechanism.

## Clinical limits and verifier boundary

HCC lacks laboratory units/reference ranges, adjudicated decompensation/complications, Child-Pugh/MELD context, imaging/tumor response, performance status, repeat-TACE rationale/technical details, transfusion/albumin/fluid balance, verified administration/adherence, outside care, and reliable mortality. The outcome is the next observed measurement; conditioning on repeat TACE and complete observation can induce collider bias.

A verifier can check hashes, full scans, paths/columns/keys, population/event/triplet counts, history missingness and exclusions, folds, nested training-only computation, uncertainty, falsification gates, and conclusion-branch consistency. It cannot establish clinical importance, causality, treatment safety/benefit, or decision utility. Those require unit-validated assays, expert-adjudicated outcomes and exposures, complete follow-up, external validation, and prospective net-benefit study.
