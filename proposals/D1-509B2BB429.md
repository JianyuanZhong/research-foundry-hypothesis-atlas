## Scientific question and fixed estimand

The parent’s exact unresolved claim remains:

> In the inherited first dated exact-code TACE population, among patients observable at the day-42 landmark, does the pre-index-to-day-42 trajectory across the eight recorded assay labels add held-out information beyond the latest day-42 snapshot, after baseline severity, measured observation/care process, and fold-frozen R42 observation-selection weighting, for a later operational repeat-TACE record?

The primary endpoint is O0: the first later unique literal-code TACE in `[t0+43,t0+181)`. O1 and O2 remain the inherited endpoint-validity boundary and secondary stricter endpoint: O1 is the frozen semantic hepatic-arterial procedure plus valid same-patient/same-visit timed examination or order corroboration; O2 additionally requires the frozen same-visit medication corroboration. These are operational records, not adjudicated imaging response or intent.

The primary estimand is the held-out, patient-level difference in weighted expected discrete-time log loss:

- `A0 = M0 + B0 + P_obs + P_care`;
- `A_snap42 = A0 + S42`;
- `A_traj42 = A0 + T42`;
- `D42-vs-snap_j = L_w(A_snap42,j) - L_w(A_traj42,j)`, for endpoint `j` in O0 (primary), O1 (boundary), and O2 (secondary if its support gate passes).

Here `B0` is the latest valid pre-index assay record in `[t0-365,t0)`, `S42` is the latest valid record in `(t0,t0+42]`, and `T42` contains the terminal baseline-to-S42 transition plus ordered knots in `(0,14]`, `(14,28]`, and `(28,42]`. Availability/missingness stays in `P_obs`, as in the parent. The same models also report `L_w(A0)-L_w(A_traj42)`; this is a secondary increment, not a replacement estimand.

R42 is defined exactly as inherited: all-source `obs_end >= t0+42 days`; R28 analogously uses 28 days. The stabilized R42 weight is fitted only from information available by day 28, frozen inside each development fold, and excludes any post-day-28 assay content or O0/O1/O2 evidence. It is a transport/observation adjustment, not a causal correction. The pre-day-42 R28 arm and the parent’s late-window, process-only, timestamp/value, horizon, corroborator, support, ESS, and weight-tail controls remain fixed.

## Matched method comparison

Both methods receive the same patient rows, endpoint labels, 14-day discrete-time risk grid `[43,57), [57,71), ..., [155,169), [169,181)`, right-censoring/event handling, R42 weights, R28 arm, split, ablations, and uncertainty. The grid is a modeling representation; the clinical time window and first-event estimand do not change.

### Confirmatory baseline: elastic-net pooled logistic hazard

For each endpoint, fit one pooled logistic hazard over person-risk-bin rows, with event rows ending at the first O0/O1/O2 event and non-event rows ending at censoring or day 181. Use an intercept, risk-bin indicators, and the specified feature block; elastic-net penalty with alpha=0.5 and lambda selected only by inner grouped development-fold log loss. Fit the parser, within-assay robust scaler, imputation/token dictionary, and any weight model on development data only. Do not standardize across assays. Use the same procedure for A0, A_snap42, A_change42, A_traj42, and the R28 S28/T28 arm.

The workflow-only comparator is an assay-content-free block containing M0 plus predeclared visit/order/examination/medication timing and counts through the relevant cutoff, plus assay opportunity/missingness indicators but no assay result values or assay-specific result tokens. It tests whether a trajectory result is adding content beyond opportunity and workflow. `A0` still contains B0 exactly as inherited; the primary nested comparison remains A_snap42 versus A_traj42. The primary output is paired patient-level held-out log-loss and Brier/integrated-Brier contrast, not a threshold-selected classifier gain.

### Learned alternative: compact time-aware GRU

Use the identical event stream from `t0-365` through `t0+42`: one event per valid lab row for the eight labels, sorted by relative time with deterministic tie ordering. Each event contains label identity, numeric content after an assay-specific development-fold robust transform when present, exact qualitative/inequality token when present, sample type, relative days from t0, time since the patient’s previous lab event, and an assay-opportunity/channel tag. No untimed narrative, examination finding, diagnosis, medication indication, image, or post-day-42 field enters the sequence. M0 and workflow covariates are concatenated at the hazard head exactly as in A0. A time-only ablation retains timestamps, label identity, sample type, and opportunity/missingness while masking all result content; the content-enabled model restores the result tokens/numeric values. This is the direct test of content beyond timing/opportunity.

Architecture: one GRU layer, hidden size 32, dropout 0.10, a two-layer head of widths 32 and 16, and one hazard logit per risk bin; approximately 32 hidden units is fixed before scoring. Train with weighted discrete-time Bernoulli log loss, right-censoring masks, and the same R42 weights as the baseline. Use Adam (learning rate 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4), at most 50 epochs, early stopping after 8 epochs without improvement on a grouped development-fold validation split, and three fixed seeds (17, 29, 41). The final test set is never used for architecture, token, scaler, weight, epoch, or seed selection. Report the mean and seed range; do not select a favorable seed. The GRU must reproduce the same A0, A_snap42, A_change42, A_traj42, S28/T28, workflow-only, and time-only contrasts. If a sequence-length cap is technically required, freeze it at 512 events per patient before fitting and report the number and identity of truncated patients; otherwise use packed variable-length sequences.

The GRU can expose nonlinear ordered patterns, irregular spacing, and recovery-then-worsening combinations that the baseline’s deterministic summaries lose. It cannot supply assay units, an adjudicated response, clinical intent, outside care, or a mechanism. A GRU improvement alone is representation-sensitive evidence, not proof of biology or a reason to call a small metric change clinically important.

## Split, uncertainty, support, and fixed falsifiers

Sort eligible index patients by t0 and assign the earliest 80% of patients to development and the latest 20% to an untouched test set; no patient occurs in both. Within development, use five grouped folds for all fitting, tuning, parser/scaler/token/weight fitting, and GRU early stopping. This is the same deterministic patient-disjoint temporal split for both methods.

Use one predeclared simultaneous uncertainty family: 1,000 patient-level paired bootstrap resamples of the untouched test set, with the max-|t| adjustment across O0/O1/O2, A_snap42-versus-A_traj42 and A0-versus-A_traj42 contrasts, R42-weighted and ordinary versions, and the fixed falsifiers. Report 95% simultaneous intervals, event/risk-set counts, calibration, Brier/integrated Brier, discrimination as secondary, R28/R42 support, weight tails, ESS, paired availability, missing times, qualitative/inequality counts, and truncation if any. A minimum event/ESS rule inherited from the parent is a hard gate; do not rescue failure by selecting subgroups or methods.

Run once, frozen before held-out scoring:

1. Primary ordinary versus stabilized R42-weighted comparison; non-overlap or unstable tails makes the transport estimand inconclusive.
2. R28 pre-day-42 arm and removal of lab/process rows in `(t0+35,t0+42]`; loss of the increment is adverse to a durable trajectory interpretation.
3. Workflow-only and GRU time-only comparisons; if they match content-enabled performance within the predeclared uncertainty/resolution rule, the result is workflow/timing-associated rather than assay-content evidence.
4. Within-patient assay-label/result-content permutation preserving admissible timestamps and availability; a surviving increment indicates timing/measurement-volume artifact.
5. Fixed [43,90]/[91,181) horizon and inherited [t-42,t-1]/[t-28,t-1]/[t-7,t-1] pre-procedure corroborator controls.
6. O0/O1/O2 concordance and early semantic-procedure-alias/near-miss audit.

Supportive requires positive simultaneous lower bounds for the O0 and O1 trajectory-versus-snapshot contrasts, retention after R42 weighting and the late/R28 controls, no matching workflow/time/permutation control, and stable direction across elastic-net and GRU. Adverse means the increment is absent after A0, snapshot-only, erased by weighting or late truncation, or matched by workflow/time/permutation; retain only the narrower supported operational prediction. Inconclusive means inadequate events/ESS/overlap, wide uncertainty, endpoint collapse, or representation instability; freeze the direction and acquire better data rather than changing windows or subgroups.

Elastic-net is selected as confirmatory because the scientific deliverable is a nested, auditable information increment under severe endpoint and measurement limits. It makes coefficient blocks, weighting, leakage boundaries, falsifiers, and uncertainty inspectable. The GRU is retained as a matched sensitivity because it can test whether irregular ordering and nonlinear combinations are systematically lost by the baseline; its use is justified by this scientific uncertainty, not by model novelty or a small expected metric gain. Defer a larger transformer, untimed-document NLP, latent causal/intent model, and mechanistic liver-response model: the available data lack timed intent/response labels, validated narrative temporality, assay units/reference ranges, images/reports, and outside-care capture. Revisit the GRU only if baseline support passes and the scientific question remains representation-sensitive.
