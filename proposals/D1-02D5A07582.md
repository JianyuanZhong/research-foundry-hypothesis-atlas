# From adjudicated route prevalence to an end-to-end selective gate: held-out nomination error, context-envelope stability, and abstention

## Decision question and substantive repair

The inherited experiment can estimate how many frozen first-eligible pulmonary-nodule admissions experts place in clinician-confirmed propagation (`A_AUTO`) or clinician reconciliation (`A_RECON`). That is necessary but not sufficient to decide whether either route merits prospective silent validation. A prevalent expert-defined route can still be operationally uninformative if a computable gate cannot reproduce it out of sample, if apparent pure omissions disappear when all available departure-frozen context is reviewed, or if a route generates many false nominations and deferrals in unfamiliar clinical contexts.

This third substantive successor therefore retains the parent's denominator, reciprocal report/addendum chronology, unique canonical discharge artifact, recommendation-unit clocks, complete `Y/V/H/Z` states, departure-frozen superseding hierarchy, conservative admission-level routing, reviewer-compatible uncertainty, institutional timing controls, comparator, provenance, and noncausal limits. It adds a **locked, end-to-end, selective nomination experiment**. The experiment separates:

1. the expert reference burden of each inherited route;
2. the held-out ability of a locally computed gate to recover that route without directly predicting an intervention label;
3. the burden of route nominations that are incompatible with at least one defensible expert/context assignment;
4. stability when the gate and reviewers receive a core versus maximally available departure-frozen MIMIC context envelope; and
5. explicit abstention and no-selection when a route is prevalent but not reproducibly nominable.

No model output is treated as clinical appropriateness. No arbitrary utility is attached to a false propagation versus false reconciliation nomination. Route-specific error tolerances and minimum prospective workload must be approved by clinical governance before confirmatory labels are opened; absent that approval, the experiment can report operating curves but must return **no route selected**.

## Evidence already supported and unresolved hypothesis

A complete, zero-sampling source scan in this workspace verified 546,028 `hosp/admissions` rows, 364,627 `hosp/patients` rows, 2,321,355 radiology notes (2,295,635 `RR`, 25,720 `AR`; 1,176,597 missing `hadm_id`), 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, and 331,794 discharge rows, all exact `note_type="DS"`, with 17 missing discharge `storetime` values. Radiology detail contains 2,913,024 each of `exam_name` and `exam_code`, 168,603 `cpt_code`, and 25,735 each of `parent_note_id` and `addendum_note_id`; discharge detail contains only 331,794 `author` rows. The inherited retrieval scan found 3,764 pulmonary retrieval proxies, but those are not adjudicated opportunities. Thus the strongest current claim is source and chronology feasibility, not route prevalence, route accuracy, or intervention value.

The unresolved falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the frozen first-eligible all-opportunity admission population, a gate learned and calibrated only on development subjects can, on untouched test subjects, selectively nominate at least one inherited admission route (`A_AUTO` or `A_RECON`) with nonzero precisely estimated workload, lower false-nomination burden than a prevalence-only constant-route baseline, stability under individual-reviewer, unit-boundary, unknown, and expanded-context assignments, and acceptable preapproved route-specific error and context-transport tolerances. Admissions with timing limits, mixed plans, unresolved boundaries, inadequate context, or nonsingleton route sets are retained as timing/defer rather than forced into a route.

The hypothesis is adverse for a route if it is common under consensus but cannot beat its constant-route baseline out of sample, its worst-compatible false-nomination bound exceeds the approved tolerance, context expansion or leave-context-out evaluation changes the route materially, or required coverage is achieved only by suppressing uncertainty. Failure of both routes means no route merits prospective silent validation from MIMIC, even if adjudicated discordance is prevalent.

## Departure-frozen reference with a context-envelope challenge

No packet may contain data stored after `D`, later encounters, downstream outcomes, follow-up imaging, diagnoses without a pre-`D` timestamp, or proposed route language. Packet manifests list every source row, time, text offset, transformation, and prohibited-field scan.

### Envelope 1: core evidence

`W_CORE` contains the operative reciprocal radiology chain, the canonical `DS` stored by `D`, and admission/disposition facts required by the inherited panels. Separate blinded physician panels retain the parent's assignments:

* context compatibility `K_OPEN`, `K_CLOSED`, or `K_UNKNOWN` after pulmonary target/action/timing/recipient/preference spans are redacted;
* unit plan state `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, `S_EQ`, `S_CON`, `S_PART`, `S_0`, or `S_UN`, with quoted evidence;
* content representation: faithful, wholly omitted, incomplete, conflicting, text-supported resolved, or unknown.

`K_OPEN` means only no observable stop in the supplied text, not that follow-up is appropriate. `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, and `S_EQ` remain evidentiary resolutions under the inherited strict hierarchy. Generic “PCP follow-up,” unlinked orders, vague referrals, or post-`D` text cannot resolve a unit. Reviewers never vote directly for a route.

### Envelope 2: maximally available departure-frozen MIMIC context

A different independently randomized panel adjudicates `W_FULL`, which adds to `W_CORE`: every exact `(subject_id,hadm_id)` radiology report with both `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`, its reciprocal details, pre-`D` service history, exact admission-linked transfers beginning by `D`, ICU exposure, and the non-target portions of the canonical `DS`. This is the maximum relevant context available in the configured snapshot, not a complete chart. Target-plan spans remain masked for context panel A and unmasked only for plan/content panels. Untimestamped discharge diagnoses, post-`D` material, and POE are excluded from primary labels.

For each admission define `CSET_CORE` and `CSET_FULL`, the sets of admission routes compatible with every individual reviewer, unknown assignment, and minimum/maximum unit inventory. The primary reference set is their conservative intersection if nonempty; if they are disjoint or either crosses clinically different routes, the admission is `A_DEFER_CTX`. Context expansion can demote a route to defer/reconciliation but cannot silently make an uncertain admission auto-eligible.

Primary context-instability estimands are:

* `CTX_FLIP_r = P(r not in CSET_FULL | CSET_CORE={r})` for `r in {A_AUTO,A_RECON}`;
* `CTX_DEFER = P(A_DEFER_CTX | E_A)`;
* `CTX_NET_r = [n(CSET_CORE={r})-n(primary reference is singleton {r})]/N_A`.

These quantify dependence on observable context completeness, not real-world appropriateness. A third redactor audits a stratified 10% of both envelopes, including all `K_CLOSED` and a random sample from every terminal-service family. Residual target-plan leakage or loss of material non-target context over 2%, with one-sided 95% upper bound also above 2%, blocks route conclusions.

Retain all pre-consensus labels. A third adjudicator adds evidence but never deletes the two-reviewer compatible set. Report confusion matrices, raw/category agreement, kappa and Gwet AC1, confidence, quoted evidence, and reasons for uncertainty. Consensus is secondary and cannot repair failed reviewer-compatible or context-envelope stability.

## Inherited conservative admission route hierarchy

For each unit in `E_A`, retain:

* `U_RESOLVED` for `S_DEC/S_DEF/S_EQ`;
* `U_OPEN` for `K_OPEN + S_0/S_PART/S_CON + G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING` for otherwise open unresolved units with `G_u<24h` or unresolved clock;
* `U_CLOSED` for reproducible `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER` for `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, `U_BOUNDARY`, unknown content, or reviewer-compatible assignments crossing states.

Aggregate worst-case at admission level. Any `U_DEFER` or envelope conflict is `A_DEFER`; otherwise any `U_TIMING` is `A_TIMING`; all resolved/closed is `A_NOACTION`. Among admissions with `U_OPEN`, `A_AUTO` requires every open unit to be a reproducible wholly omitted `K_OPEN+S_0` unit, all other units reproducibly resolved/closed, no boundary uncertainty, and no cross-unit conflict. Any incomplete/conflicting/interaction-dependent unit produces `A_RECON`; a pure omission plus reconciliation unit is `A_MIXED`, a named subset of `A_RECON`, never auto. This hierarchy is immutable for development, calibration, test, comparator, and prospective nomination.

## End-to-end computable gate without direct route prediction

### Frozen census and partition

The route-reference study remains a census of all `N_A`; no outcome-enriched sampling is allowed. Before any discharge labels are generated, assign subjects by `SHA256(snapshot_id || subject_id || fixed_salt)` to development (40%), calibration (20%), or untouched test (40%). Because `N_A` contains at most one admission per subject, there is no subject leakage. Publish partition hashes and pre-label balance for interval, admission type, ICU, length-of-stay bin, terminal service, discharge location, report exam family, and unit-count proxy. Labels from calibration/test cannot change vocabulary, features, hyperparameters, context families, thresholds, or exclusions.

Cross-subject calendar time is not valid in MIMIC, so this is explicitly not temporal validation. Heterogeneity is challenged by held-out subjects and leave-one-context-family-out evaluation, not fabricated calendar eras.

### Inputs and models

The gate sees only fields available by `D` in the corresponding envelope and uses local computation. It must reproduce the sequence rather than classify the final intervention route directly:

1. radiology candidate retrieval and reciprocal-chain reconstruction;
2. recommendation-unit span/boundary extraction;
3. candidate sets for `K`, `S`, and content state;
4. inherited deterministic unit and admission aggregation.

A transparent baseline uses locked lexical/span rules derived only in development. The candidate model uses word and character n-grams from permitted note spans plus structured `exam_name/exam_code/cpt_code`, admission type, ICU, terminal service family, discharge-location family, locked length-of-stay bin, recommendation interval, unit count, and storage-clock bins; fit L2-regularized logistic/multinomial models for each intermediate predicate. Choose `C` only from `{0.01,0.1,1,10}` by subject-level five-fold development cross-validation. No author identity, subject/hospital identifiers, shifted calendar year, post-`D` fields, POE, downstream outcomes, or direct route labels are features. A more complex model may be descriptive but cannot select a route.

On calibration data, create split-conformal compatible label sets for eligibility, unit boundary, `K`, `S`, and content. Propagate their Cartesian product through the immutable hierarchy. The gate emits `NOM_AUTO` or `NOM_RECON` only when every compatible intermediate assignment yields the same route and the corresponding full-context route set is a singleton; otherwise it emits `NOM_TIMING`, `NOM_NOACTION`, or `NOM_DEFER`. Calibration may tighten abstention thresholds to satisfy preapproved route-specific false-nomination tolerances, but may not alter reference definitions or inspect test labels.

The test partition is evaluated once. After unblinding, refitting on test or reporting a more favorable post-hoc threshold is prohibited. A final frozen model may be refit on development plus calibration only after the confirmatory result, solely as the artifact proposed for prospective silent validation.

### Retrieval-negative audit

An end-to-end gate also requires evidence that retrieval is not merely a high-purity sliver. Construct the full adult exact-admission-linked report universe with `charttime in [admittime-12h,D]`; keep all gate-positive reports. From gate-negative reports, draw a fixed-seed probability sample before discharge labels: 300 reports from each of four mutually exclusive strata—pulmonary/nodule lexical near-miss, chest-exam without nodule terms, non-chest exam with recommendation language, and all remaining reports—sampling without replacement or taking the census if a stratum has fewer than 300. Save stratum sizes, inclusion probabilities, selected `(subject_id,note_id)` hashes, and Horvitz-Thompson weights. Two blinded reviewers adjudicate inherited report eligibility without discharge data. Estimate missed eligible reports/admissions with exact or survey-bootstrap intervals; unknown sampled negatives are adverse in sensitivity bounds. This 1,200-report audit is the only probability sample; route reference and every gate-positive candidate remain census reviewed.

A retrieval sensitivity estimate cannot add sampled missed cases to `N_A` after the denominator is frozen. Instead, any confirmed missed eligible admission proves denominator undercoverage and triggers one prespecified retrieval-rule revision using development data, after which the denominator and all partitions are rebuilt and rehashed before confirmatory labels. A missed eligible case discovered after the single revision makes end-to-end sensitivity inconclusive; it is not silently patched.

## Primary estimands and baselines

All route burden quantities retain `N_A` as denominator and subject/admission as analysis unit. Report inherited `B_AUTO`, `B_RECON`, `B_MIXED`, `B_DEFER`, `B_TIMING`, `B_NOACTION`, `RD_ROUTE`, `RD_MIX`, `D_MIX`, recommendation-unit workloads, `Y/J_A`, availability controls, and comparator results with reviewer-, unknown-, boundary-, and context-compatible bounds.

On untouched test admissions, for each `r in {AUTO,RECON}` define:

* nomination workload `C_r=100*n(NOM_r)/N_A,test` and, separately, per 100 test `E_A` admissions;
* worst-compatible false-nomination burden `FNB_r=100*n(NOM_r and r is excluded by any defensible reference assignment)/N_A,test`;
* conservative nomination purity `PUR_r=1-P(r excluded by any defensible assignment | NOM_r)`; every unknown/context disagreement is adverse;
* missed-reference burden `MISS_r=100*n(primary reference singleton r and NOM_r absent)/N_A,test`;
* `ABSTAIN=100*n(NOM_DEFER)/N_A,test` and reason-specific decomposition;
* `CALERR_r`, absolute difference between calibration-predicted and observed worst-compatible nomination error, with reliability diagrams; this is calibration, not clinical-risk probability.

Baselines are fixed before test labels:

1. **No-selection:** nominate neither route; zero false nominations and zero route coverage.
2. **Prevalence-only:** on calibration data choose the more prevalent singleton route and nominate it for every otherwise route-eligible `E_A` admission, preserving timing/defer exclusions. This tests whether aggregate prevalence alone carries discriminative information.
3. **Locked lexical gate:** the transparent development-only rule system with the same hierarchy and abstention.
4. **Oracle reference burden:** expert singleton route prevalence, explicitly an unattainable descriptive ceiling rather than a model comparator.

A useful gate must improve on prevalence-only discrimination: the subject-clustered simultaneous lower bound for `PUR_r - PUR_r,prevalence` must exceed zero while `C_r` has a lower bound above zero. Compare workload and false-nomination burdens transparently; do not combine them into a utility score.

## Clinical-context robustness and scoped transport

Predeclare context families without test outcomes: terminal service (medicine, surgery, neurologic, cardiac, other), ICU versus non-ICU, admission type, discharge-location family, 3/6/12-month interval, chest versus extra-thoracic index exam family, length-of-stay bin, one versus multiple recommendation units, and `H_24_72` versus `H_72plus`. Missing structured context is its own level.

For each family report nomination, false-nomination, purity, miss, and defer estimates. Run leave-one-terminal-service-family-out and leave-one-exam-family-out fits using development plus calibration only, then evaluate the omitted family in test. A route may be nominated for prospective silent validation only in a **supported scope**: strata with at least 20 test nominations, acceptable simultaneous precision, and upper worst-compatible false-nomination bound below the preapproved tolerance. Sparse strata are outside scope, not merged post hoc. If a pooled route passes but any sufficiently sized predeclared stratum or leave-family-out result exceeds tolerance or reverses superiority over prevalence-only, the pooled route is not selected; a narrower scope is allowed only if fixed from calibration before test unblinding.

This is an internal context-transport challenge, not external validation. It cannot establish performance at another hospital, in a current EHR, or under a live trigger.

## Inference, gates, and route selection

Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates. Carry complete admission/unit inventories, partitions, survey-negative weights, all reviewer/context-compatible assignments, comparator weights, and route predictions together. Use a max-statistic simultaneous 95% family for `B_AUTO`, `B_RECON`, `B_MIXED`, `B_DEFER`, `B_TIMING`, `RD_ROUTE`, `D_MIX`, `CTX_FLIP_AUTO`, `CTX_FLIP_RECON`, `CTX_DEFER`, `C_r`, `FNB_r`, `PUR_r`, `MISS_r`, `ABSTAIN`, calibration error, retrieval undercoverage, portable-comparator contrast, and availability contrasts. Test-stratum analyses use the same family or are explicitly exploratory.

Retain inherited feasibility gates: `N_A>=200`, `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, no duplicate canonical `DS`, at least 60 `E_A`; at least 30 reference admissions per claimed route and 40 total `A_AUTO+A_RECON` for two lanes; claimed all-`N_A` burden half-width no greater than 5 per 100 and `D_MIX` half-width no greater than 10 points; total `K_UNKNOWN+S_UN+U_BOUNDARY+unknown content<=10%` within `E_A`; redaction, prohibited-field, source, chronology, reliability, and leave-reviewer-out gates pass. Retain at least 90% raw agreement and kappa or Gwet AC1 at least 0.70 for eligibility, boundary, `K`, `S`, content, and derived route. Consensus never repairs failure.

Add nomination gates:

* at least 30 untouched-test nominations for a route and at least 20 in every stratum included in its proposed scope;
* simultaneous test upper bound for worst-compatible false-nomination proportion among nominations is at or below the route-specific tolerance fixed by governance before labels; the inherited auto-route safeguard also remains: upper-bound `D_MIX<=0.10` under the prospectively approved operational purity margin;
* simultaneous lower bound `PUR_r-PUR_r,prevalence>0`, nonzero lower `C_r`, and no adverse calibration/context-envelope or leave-family-out result;
* test `ABSTAIN` and `MISS_r` are reported and below governance-approved operational maxima; they cannot be optimized away by narrowing the denominator;
* retrieval-negative audit and one allowed development-only rule revision produce no confirmed eligible miss in the locked audit, with the weighted upper undercoverage bound below a preapproved retrieval tolerance;
* the same route/scope under consensus, every individual-reviewer-compatible assignment, minimum/maximum unit inventories, unknown-adverse assignment, `W_CORE/W_FULL`, and 72-hour opportunity sensitivity.

Decision outputs are:

1. **Auto-route silent-validation candidate** only if all inherited `A_AUTO` purity rules and new held-out auto nomination gates pass. This proposes silent testing of clinician-confirmed prepopulation, never silent insertion.
2. **Reconciliation-route silent-validation candidate** only if held-out reconciliation nomination gates pass. It proposes silent side-by-side flagging, not a claim that reconciliation benefits patients.
3. **Two-lane candidate** only if each lane independently passes in a separable supported scope; one lane cannot borrow events or purity from the other.
4. **Scoped candidate** only for calibration-predeclared context strata that pass; no extrapolation to excluded strata.
5. **No selection** if tolerances were not approved before labels, neither route beats prevalence-only, any required bound crosses a different decision, retrieval/context robustness fails, or events/precision are insufficient.

The prevalence difference `RD_ROUTE` may describe workload but no longer selects a route by itself. Thus high prevalence with poor nominability correctly produces no selection.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. Recompute all file/schema hashes and full row counts; stop on drift, denominator hash change, duplicate canonical `DS`, impossible time, or broken reciprocal chain.
2. Verify every all-admission marginal and partition sums to the frozen `N_A`; recommendation units never add admissions.
3. Verify `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; only unit-specific `G_u` defines content opportunity. Stale/post-departure rows never become failures.
4. Inject later faithful text: `Y=A`, `E_A`, packets, and routes must remain unchanged.
5. Shared-action nodules form one unit; differing windows form separate units; duplicate addendum wording does not create a unit; unresolved coreference creates min/max `U_BOUNDARY` inventories.
6. Verify hierarchy examples: generic PCP language=`S_0`; target-linked but incomplete referral=`S_PART/S_UN`; scheduled compatible CT with timing/responsibility=`S_EQ`; explicit non-pursuit=`S_DEC`; definitive replacement=`S_DEF`; unexplained incompatible plan=`S_CON`.
7. A pure omission plus conflict is `A_MIXED` within `A_RECON`, never `A_AUTO`; duplicating units cannot alter the admission route or bootstrap weight.
8. Seed target-plan leakage and removed non-target context in both envelopes; failure of the 2% rule blocks conclusions.
9. Add a relevant pre-`D` radiology context sentence that changes openness/supersession: `W_FULL` must expose a route-set change or defer; `W_CORE` may not be presented as complete context.
10. Force reviewer disagreement, unknown plan, unknown boundary, or disjoint envelope sets: the primary route must defer, even if consensus is favorable.

12. Move one subject across development/calibration/test or allow test vocabulary/threshold fitting: partition/leakage checks must fail.

23. Any conclusion containing “appropriate,” “benefit,” “harm reduction,” “clinician failure,” “deploy,” or causal language must fail verification unless explicitly framed as unavailable evidence or a future-study question.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive for a route** means its inherited expert reference is identifiable and nonzero, the locked gate nominates it on untouched subjects with positive precise workload, lower false nomination than prevalence-only, acceptable preapproved worst-compatible error, stable core/full-context and leave-family-out performance, and all chronology/reliability/uncertainty gates pass. This supports only taking the frozen route and supported scope into prospective silent validation.

**Supportive for two lanes** requires each lane to pass independently. A high expert `A_AUTO` burden cannot compensate for an unreliable auto gate, and a strong reconciliation gate cannot validate auto. A narrow supported scope warrants a scoped prospective study, not broad deployment.

**Adverse to auto nomination** includes high `D_MIX`, any compatible reconciliation/defer assignment among auto nominations above tolerance, context expansion revealing competing plans, or purity no better than prevalence-only. **Adverse to reconciliation nomination** includes predominantly resolved/no-action reference among its nominations, high context/reviewer instability, or no improvement over prevalence-only. **Adverse to both** is prevalent discordance without out-of-sample discrimination: this shows prevalence-only route nomination is uninformative and favors no prospective gate from these data.

**Inconclusive** includes sparse test nominations, wide simultaneous bounds, failed reliability/redaction/source/clock checks, excessive defer/unknown burden, inability to approve tolerances before labels, retrieval-negative undercoverage, calibration drift, unsupported context strata, or conflicting 24/72-hour conclusions. Failure of `X_PORT` affects specificity only. Zero events require reporting an upper bound; they are not evidence that intervention is unnecessary unless compared with a prospectively approved minimum.

## Required prospective evidence and noncausal limits

MIMIC can establish source-linked retrospective opportunity, departure-frozen text states, recommendation-unit multiplicity, expert/context reproducibility, end-to-end held-out nomination error, abstention, and internal context robustness. It cannot establish actual report finalization/viewing, draft availability, author awareness, responsibility, outside plans, patient preference, complete competing-risk context, live trigger fidelity, target-linked ordering/scheduling/completion, clinical appropriateness, utility, intervention effect, malignancy, or benefit. The canonical `DS` and all available radiology/structured rows remain an incomplete chart; expert agreement and conformal coverage are not truth.

After a supportive result, freeze the source-to-unit extractor, intermediate label sets, hierarchy, abstention thresholds, supported scope, and tolerances. In a new site enroll consecutive adult discharges, capture immutable report/addendum finalization and viewing, draft/version/sign/transmission events, actual context and outside-plan attestations, responsibility, linked orders/referrals/scheduling, and final departure artifacts. Run silently with no care modification. Independently adjudicate route confusion, worst-case route purity, mixed-plan misses, defer/miss rates, drift, and site/service calibration. Require at least 100 route-positive admissions and at least 30 in every claimed route or a fixed maximum accrual period. Any temporal leakage, context drift, or tolerance failure stops progression. Only a later governed route-stratified trial can test clinician-confirmed propagation or reconciliation effects, workload, inappropriate insertion, completion, harm, or benefit.

## Substantive advance

The parent asks whether expert-adjudicated route burdens are large and precise enough to nominate a prospective study. This successor asks the harder and more decision-relevant available-data question: whether a frozen end-to-end gate can **find** those routes on untouched admissions without hiding false nominations, reviewer ambiguity, missing context, or unfamiliar-service failure. It replaces prevalence-only priority with held-out selective prediction, a probability audit of retrieval negatives, maximal available context envelopes, route-specific error/coverage estimands, explicit baselines, scoped transport, and no-selection. The result remains retrospective and noncausal, but it directly decides whether any computable route is sufficiently reproducible to merit the next prospective silent-validation study.
