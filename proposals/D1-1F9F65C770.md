# A probability-verified admission-routing gate: exhaustive opportunity construction, safety-sentinel census, and design-based purity bounds

## Decision bottleneck and substantive alternative

The unresolved prerequisite is not whether some pulmonary-nodule recommendations are absent from discharge summaries. It is whether the conservative admission-level labels `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` can be reproduced with sufficiently small unsafe cross-route contamination over the frozen first-eligible all-opportunity population to justify a prospective *silent* validation. A favorable retrospective result must not be manufactured by reviewing only obvious omissions, by counting recommendation units as independent, by resolving disagreement through forced consensus, or by assuming that no observed unsafe case means none exists.

The parent correctly freezes one first eligible admission per subject, reconstructs reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum chains, uses the unique canonical `DS`, defines unit-specific `G = DS_store - R_store`, and routes a whole admission by its worst unit. Its confirmatory plan nevertheless requires full multi-panel route adjudication of every admission in `N_A`. That census is expensive, may encourage a simplified single-review workflow, and does not state how a finite review program can estimate all-population route reproducibility and purity without outcome-enriched selection.

This child makes a substantively different test. It keeps **exhaustive deterministic source processing and exhaustive eligibility adjudication**, but uses a locked, stratified probability sample for expensive route adjudication. Every admission has a known positive inclusion probability. Admissions carrying any deterministic safety-sentinel signal are reviewed with probability one; the remainder are sampled without replacement using strata frozen before clinical route labels exist. Every recommendation unit in every selected admission is reviewed—units are never sampled. Horvitz–Thompson/Hájek finite-population estimates, randomization-based simultaneous intervals, independent double review, a third AUTO verification, and reviewer/unknown-compatible bounds target the complete `N_A`, not the reviewed subset. The design therefore reduces adjudication while preserving an unbiased all-opportunity estimand and making the residual chance of a rare unsafe AUTO admission explicit rather than invisible.

The strongest evidence presently supports only source feasibility. A complete local scan confirmed 2,321,355 radiology rows (`RR=2,295,635`, `AR=25,720`), 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 discharge rows all typed `DS`, 331,794 unique `(subject_id,hadm_id)` canonical keys, zero duplicate keys, and 17 missing discharge `storetime` values. Radiology detail contains 25,735 `parent_note_id` and 25,735 `addendum_note_id` rows, supporting reciprocal reconstruction. These are source facts, not adjudicated opportunity, route, purity, or clinical-benefit results.

## Evidence-supported claim, test claim, and hypothesis

**Supported now:** the frozen MIMIC files contain the report, addendum-link, discharge-artifact, admission, and storage-time fields needed to construct a departure-frozen review frame; the local profile above does not establish semantic eligibility or any route prevalence.

**Unresolved claim tested here:** a blinded, atomic-label process plus deterministic worst-case aggregation yields reproducible route labels and a sufficiently pure `AUTO` subset at the all-`N_A` level, after accounting for probability sampling, disagreement, unknowns, retrieval error, recommendation multiplicity, and finite event counts.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the frozen first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity population, exhaustive chronology and unit extraction followed by prespecified stratified probability verification will show that admission-level `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` labels are reproducible under independent review, that the upper simultaneous design-based bound on unsafe contamination of `AUTO` is below a governance-fixed margin, and that reconciliation/deferral capture of every observed unsafe or ambiguous minority unit remains above governance-fixed sensitivity margins. These properties remain true when all reviewer disagreements and unknowns are assigned adversely, and each nominated route has adequate events, effective sample size, and precision.

Failure of any route-specific gate produces `NO_SELECTION` for that route. Even complete support nominates only prospective silent validation of the frozen gate. It does not establish guideline appropriateness, safe automation, workflow benefit, clinician error, or patient benefit.

## Stage 0: exhaustive, frozen all-opportunity denominator

Before any discharge text, discharge `storetime`, route, or later trace is inspected, stream all 2,321,355 radiology rows and all detail rows. Hash the complete extraction code, lexical/exam candidate rules, every included and excluded `note_id`, and the random seed for a retrieval-negative audit.

The operational eligibility frame is deliberately broad: every exact admission-linked report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]` that has any pulmonary/nodule/opacity term, any follow-up/recommendation term, any 3/6/12-month or equivalent calendar expression, any chest/thoracic CT expression, or an exam-code/name combination designated by the locked high-recall dictionary. All deterministic positives and all parser/chain ambiguities receive independent dual eligibility review. Retrieval rules may increase workload but cannot use discharge content or be tuned to route outcomes.

The primary `N_A` is exactly one first eligible admission per subject after this exhaustive candidate review:

* adult and exact admission-linked report in the stated interval;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* patient-specific, unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional or optional plans, explicit no follow-up, in-hospital death, locked hospice/comfort-only transition, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* do not infer nodule size, smoking, malignancy risk, life expectancy, recommendation appropriateness, or missing clinical context.

Sort eligible admissions within subject by `admittime`, then `hadm_id`; retain the first. This first-eligible rule is applied once, before outcome inspection, and its manifest hash is reused everywhere. Eligibility disagreement and reciprocal-chain uncertainty are retained as `U_R`; they are not silently excluded or called omissions.

Report/addendum chains require subject- and admission-consistent reciprocal links in detail: an `AR` parent relation and the corresponding `RR` addendum relation must agree. Every operative component must have `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. A later material pre-`D` component supersedes only within that reciprocal chain. Missing nodes, one-way links, contradictory links, unresolved operative text, or missing/contradictory recommendation times remain `U_R`.

A fixed probability sample of deterministic retrieval-negative exact admission-linked radiology reports is reviewed for missed denominator opportunities, stratified by chest CT exam name/code and pulmonary lexical fragments. Use at least the finite-population sample needed so that zero misses gives a simultaneous one-sided 95% upper bound of 0.5 missed eligible opportunities per 100 negatives in each audit stratum, or census a stratum if smaller. Any confirmed missed eligible report, or an upper bound implying more than 5% denominator undercoverage relative to estimated `N_A`, invalidates the retrieval dictionary and blocks route nomination; revise and repeat Stage 0 without inspecting route outcomes. This audit assesses frame coverage. It never changes inclusion probabilities in the route sample after that sample is drawn.

## Canonical artifact, chronology, states, and unit clocks

For each `N_A` admission, require exactly one `note/discharge` row with `note_type=="DS"`. A duplicate `(subject_id,hadm_id)` canonical key stops the run. Missing artifact, text, linkage, or `storetime`, unreadable text, or contradiction is retained as unknown. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

Preserve the exhaustive parent departure state `Y` for every `N_A`: `F` faithful; `C_O` omitted; `C_I` incomplete/discordant; `R` text-supported alternate resolution; `A` known `DS_store>D`; and `U_D` unknown artifact/content. Preserve `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, eventual `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}`, and every zero/unknown cell in `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A`. Later faithful text cannot change departure state `A`.

For each operative recommendation unit `u`, retain `R_store,u`, `DS_store`, and

`G_u = DS_store - R_store,u` in hours.

The index-unit `G` remains the parent clock. The checked identity is `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`; only `G_u` defines unit opportunity. The primary ample set `E_A` remains index `V_D` with index `H in {H_24_72,H_72plus}`, but no admission can be content-routed unless every active unresolved unit has resolved `G_u>=24h`. `G_u<24h`, unknown clocks, stale artifacts, and post-departure artifacts cannot become content failures.

Exhaustive deterministic unit extraction runs for all `N_A` before sampling. It retains every potential pulmonary recommendation span and produces a minimum and maximum inventory under ambiguity. The semantic unit remains the smallest operative tuple `(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity)`: shared action/window for co-mentioned nodules is one unit; different actions/windows are separate; reciprocal restatements are deduplicated; a material addendum revises the unit rather than creating a new event. Unresolved coreference or splitting is `U_BOUNDARY`. The final human inventory is established only for sampled admissions, but deterministic multiplicity and ambiguity signals are available for sampling. No recommendation unit is ever independently sampled or weighted.

## Stage 1: prespecified admission probability sample

### Outcome-blind safety strata

After `N_A`, canonical keys, chain status, clocks, and deterministic span inventory are frozen—but before any physician route labels—assign every admission to exactly one hierarchical stratum:

1. `S_SENTINEL`, sampled with probability 1: any reciprocal-chain defect; missing/contradictory time; missing/unreadable canonical artifact; parser failure; `U_BOUNDARY` signal; two or more possible recommendation units; two different actions or due windows; negation/conditionality conflict; target-linked decision/non-pursuit/definitive-treatment language; or two discordant pulmonary-plan spans in the `DS`.
2. `S_TIMECLOCK`: no sentinel, but any deterministic unit has `G_u<24h`, `G_u` unknown, `DS_store>D`, or stale chronology.
3. `S_ZERO`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and no target-linked pulmonary plan span is deterministically detected in the `DS`.
4. `S_PARTIAL`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and target text is detected but one or more action, timing, responsibility, polarity, or linkage elements are absent or discordant.
5. `S_COMPLETE`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and a deterministic target/action/timing/responsibility or replacement-plan pattern is present.

These are sampling strata, not clinical routes. Their labels and rules are never shown to reviewers. A deterministic screen may be wrong; all non-sentinel strata therefore retain positive random sampling probabilities.

Within `S_TIMECLOCK`, `S_ZERO`, `S_PARTIAL`, and `S_COMPLETE`, sample admissions by simple random sampling without replacement with seed derived from the hashed protocol. For `S_ZERO`, choose the larger of 300 admissions or the finite-population sample size for worst-case proportion 0.5 and ±4 percentage-point 95% precision, capped at `N_h`. For each other noncensus stratum, choose the larger of 120 or the finite-population sample size for worst-case proportion 0.5 and ±7 percentage-point precision, capped at `N_h`. The finite-population formula is

`n_h = ceil[N_h*z^2*0.25 / {e_h^2*(N_h-1)+z^2*0.25}]`, with `z=1.96`.

All `S_SENTINEL` admissions are added. A workload ceiling of 900 sampled admissions is fixed before route outcomes. If mandatory census plus formula-based samples exceed 900, the study may obtain more resources or declare adjudication feasibility failed; it may not lower inclusion probabilities, drop sentinel admissions, or cherry-pick strata after viewing labels. Selected packet nonresponse remains in the analysis with every decision-relevant state assigned adversely. Record `N_h,n_h,pi_h=n_h/N_h`, seed, ordered sampled IDs, replacements (none), review completion, and reasons.

This scheme does not claim deterministic sentinels capture all rare unsafe units. It makes all detected high-risk admissions a census and uses a probability sample plus a simultaneous finite-population upper bound to quantify missed unsafe admissions among apparently simple cases. A silent-validation nomination is prohibited if that residual bound exceeds the preapproved margin.

## Stage 2: blinded atomic review without direct route voting

All packet material must have `storetime<=D`. Automated prohibited-field scans reject post-`D` text, eventual outcomes, later encounters, POE, proposed routes, sample stratum, `G/H/V`, and expected direction.

### Radiology unit abstraction

Two independent physicians see only the reciprocal operative radiology chain and timestamps suppressed to the minimum needed to establish order. Each independently enumerates units and records quoted spans for target cluster, action/modality, exact due interval/date, polarity, operative component, and supersession. They do not see the discharge artifact. Boundary disagreement creates the union-compatible minimum/maximum unit inventories; a third adjudicator may add an opinion but cannot erase the initial compatible set.

### Departure context

Two independent physicians experienced in discharge review receive admission/disposition variables and the canonical departure-available `DS` after a separate redaction team removes pulmonary target/action/timing/recipient/order/preference spans. They label only `K_OPEN`, `K_CLOSED`, or `K_UNKNOWN` and quote the non-target evidence or missing fact. `K_OPEN` means no observable departure-frozen stop; it is not clinical appropriateness. Every `K_CLOSED` packet and a probability sample of at least 10% of other packets receives independent redaction audit. Any target-plan leakage or removal of decision-relevant non-target context above 2%, or a simultaneous upper bound above 2%, blocks route conclusions.

### Discharge-plan attributes

Two independent physicians receive the frozen radiology-unit abstraction and the unredacted canonical `DS`; they do not receive clocks, sample stratum, context labels, downstream evidence, or intervention language. For every possible unit they independently record atomic binary/nominal fields with quoted spans: target linkage; target mention; compatible chest/thoracic action; exact timing/date; responsible recipient/team or scheduling; active versus historical/conditional status; explicit informed non-pursuit; explicit definitive replacement; and cross-plan conflict. Frozen code maps these attributes to `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, `S_EQ`, `S_CON`, `S_PART`, `S_0`, or `S_UN` using the parent's strict hierarchy. Vague “PCP follow-up” is not an executable plan; generic specialty mention lacks action/timing; an unlinked order does not resolve a unit. Plans stored after `D` are invisible.

Reviewers never vote `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`. For each initial reviewer combination, frozen code derives a route. Retain all initial labels. Third adjudication supplies an additional label only; consensus is secondary and cannot remove disagreement or repair failed reliability.

### Independent AUTO verification

Every sampled admission that either initial-reviewer route calls `AUTO` receives an additional independent physician verification packet with source spans in randomized order and no initial labels. The verifier must affirm that all units were captured and that none is incomplete, conflicting, timing-limited, unresolved, or interaction-dependent. Any verifier-identified minority unit is an unsafe AUTO contamination event and routes the robust admission to `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` as appropriate. This verification is complete within sampled AUTO candidates, not outcome-subsampled.

## Deterministic worst-case admission routing

For every compatible reviewer assignment, derive unit status:

* `U_RESOLVED`: `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, or `S_EQ` with all required explicit evidence;
* `U_OPEN`: `K_OPEN`, `S in {S_0,S_PART,S_CON}`, and resolved `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING`: otherwise active/unresolved but `G_u<24h` or clock unresolved;
* `U_CLOSED`: `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER`: `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, boundary ambiguity, unreadable content, or a compatible disagreement crossing statuses.

Aggregate at admission level in this fixed precedence:

1. any `U_DEFER` gives `A_DEFER`;
2. otherwise any `U_TIMING` gives `A_TIMING`;
3. if all units are resolved/closed, give `A_NOACTION`;
4. `A_AUTO` only if every open unit is wholly omitted (`S_0`), all other units are reproducibly resolved/closed, every unit clock is ample, there is no boundary uncertainty and no cross-unit conflict;
5. any incomplete, conflicting, interaction-dependent, or mixed admission gives `A_RECON`; name `A_MIXED` when at least one pure omission coexists with a reconciliation unit.

Thus one unsafe minority unit controls the admission regardless of how many copyable units exist. Duplicating a unit cannot alter an admission route or sampling weight. The **robust route set** is the union across both initial reviewers, third opinions, minimum/maximum unit inventories, and allowed unknown assignments. A robust single label exists only when this set is a singleton. Otherwise the decision-facing route is `A_DEFER`, while compatible alternatives remain reported.

## Estimands over the full frozen population

The inference population and denominator are always `N_A`; the sampling unit is the admission, which is also the first and only index admission per subject. Let `w_i=1/pi_h`. Use design weights, not unit counts.

### All-population route burdens

For `r in {AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,MIXED}`, estimate the finite-population total

`T_r_hat = sum_{i in sample} w_i I(R_i=r)`

and burden per 100 opportunities

`B_r_hat = 100*T_r_hat/N_A`.

Use robust singleton routes primarily; consensus and each reviewer separately are secondary. Also estimate the full `Y`, `V/H/Z`, chronology, and non-`E_A` margins so all cells sum to `N_A`. `A_MIXED` is nested in `A_RECON` and is not added twice.

### Pairwise route reproducibility

For each route report weighted raw agreement, category-specific positive agreement, Gwet AC1 and kappa, with stratum-specific values. For a route called by reviewer 1, estimate reviewer-2 confirmation and repeat with reviewers exchanged. The primary reproducibility quantity is the lower of the two directional confirmation probabilities. Also estimate the probability that reviewer-compatible routing crosses `AUTO` versus `{RECON,TIMING,DEFER}` and `RECON` versus `{AUTO,NOACTION}`. Agreement from consensus is not a gate.

### AUTO purity and unsafe-minority risk

Define an unsafe AUTO event if an initial `AUTO` admission is assigned `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` by the other initial reviewer, the independent verifier, any compatible unit-boundary inventory, or adverse resolution of a documented unknown. Estimate

`C_AUTO = P(unsafe event | at least one initial AUTO call)`

as a design-weighted ratio over all sampled AUTO candidates. Report the simultaneous one-sided upper randomization bound. Selected-case nonresponse and unresolved verifier findings are unsafe. The residual probability-sampling uncertainty among unsampled `S_ZERO` admissions is included in this bound; zero observed events is never reported as zero population risk.

Report separately:

`D_MIX = P(A_RECON or A_DEFER | any unit appears purely omitted)`

and the weighted rate of newly discovered additional/unsafe units in sampled non-sentinel admissions. These directly test whether a rare unit or multiplicity was missed by deterministic screening.

### RECON, TIMING, and DEFER safety capture

Using the union-compatible expert reference as the safety reference, estimate:

* `Se_RECON`: probability a candidate label is `RECON` or `DEFER` when any unit is incomplete, conflicting, mixed, or interaction-dependent;
* `PPV_RECON`: probability such an unsafe-content reference exists among candidate `RECON` calls;
* `PPV_TIMING`: probability every unresolved active unit preventing content routing truly has `G_u<24h` or an unresolved clock among candidate `TIMING` calls;
* `Se_DEFER`: probability the candidate route is `DEFER` whenever context, plan meaning, unit boundary, artifact, or reviewer-compatible routing is decision-relevantly uncertain;
* `leak_AUTO`: probability a safety-reference `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` admission is called `AUTO` by either initial reviewer.

These are text-defined process properties, not clinical correctness.

### Workload

Report actual numbers of eligibility packets, sampled admissions, units, initial physician reviews, third adjudications, redaction audits, and AUTO verification packets; minutes per packet and total physician-hours; `N_h/n_h/pi_h`; maximum weight; Kish effective sample size overall and for each route denominator; units per admission; and the proportion of burden consumed by the sentinel census. Extrapolate review workload per 100 `N_A` admissions with design-based intervals. Do not imply that lower review time is clinical benefit.

## Inference, baselines, and simultaneous route gates

Use exact stratified without-replacement point estimators and finite-population variance. Construct at least 10,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates that respect strata and carry all units within an admission together. For zero rare events, additionally invert the stratified hypergeometric sampling distribution; use the wider valid bound. A single max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers `B_AUTO,B_RECON,B_TIMING,B_DEFER,B_MIXED,C_AUTO,D_MIX,Se_RECON,PPV_RECON,PPV_TIMING,Se_DEFER,leak_AUTO` and route-specific agreement. Unknown-compatible lower/upper bounds are carried within every replicate before taking confidence limits.

Baselines are prespecified:

1. marginal-majority route assignment, evaluated with the same design weights;
2. deterministic sampling-stratum label alone (`S_ZERO` as AUTO-like, `S_PARTIAL` as RECON-like, `S_TIMECLOCK` as TIMING-like, sentinel as DEFER-like), demonstrating whether expert atomic review adds discrimination;
3. recommendation-unit majority routing, expected to fail mixed-admission safety;
4. favorable consensus that discards disagreement, shown only to quantify optimism;
5. complete-case unweighted sample estimates, shown only to demonstrate selection bias relative to the design-weighted analysis.

Governance must approve the operational margins before any route labels are released. The default confirmatory feasibility margins, if approved unchanged, are: `tau_AUTO=0.05` maximum unsafe AUTO contamination; `rho=0.90` minimum route confirmation/raw agreement; `AC1=0.70`; `sigma_RECON=0.95` minimum unsafe-content capture; `pi_RECON=0.80` minimum RECON positive predictive value; `pi_TIMING=0.95`; and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`. These margins nominate a silent-validation target; they are not utilities or acceptable patient-harm rates.

All routes first require: stable hashes/schemas and denominator; retrieval audit pass; no duplicate canonical `DS`; `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`; redaction pass; at least 200 `N_A`, 60 `E_A`, overall design effective sample size at least 150, maximum normalized analysis-weight share `max(w_i)/sum(w_i)<=0.10`, and simultaneous all-`N_A` route-burden half-width no greater than 5 admissions per 100. Route-specific requirements are:

* **AUTO:** at least 30 sampled candidate AUTO admissions and route-denominator effective sample size at least 100; lower simultaneous confirmation/agreement bound `>=rho`; AC1 lower bound `>=0.70`; upper `C_AUTO<=tau_AUTO`; upper `D_MIX<=0.10`; upper `leak_AUTO<=0.02`; no verifier-confirmed unresolved unsafe minority left compatible with AUTO; and lower `B_AUTO>0`.
* **RECON:** at least 30 sampled RECON and 30 unsafe-content reference admissions, effective sample size at least 80 in each denominator; lower confirmation `>=rho`, lower `Se_RECON>=sigma_RECON`, lower `PPV_RECON>=pi_RECON`, upper AUTO leakage among these cases `<=0.02`, and lower `B_RECON>0`.
* **TIMING:** at least 30 sampled TIMING admissions and effective sample size at least 80; lower confirmation `>=rho`, lower `PPV_TIMING>=pi_TIMING`, zero observed use of index-only `G` when another active unit is under 24 hours, and lower `B_TIMING>0`.
* **DEFER:** at least 30 sampled uncertain-reference admissions and effective sample size at least 80; lower confirmation `>=rho`, lower `Se_DEFER>=sigma_DEFER`, and no adverse-compatible unknown routed AUTO. A high defer burden is permitted but must be reported as limited automation feasibility.

AUTO, RECON, TIMING, and DEFER gates are simultaneous and independent: passing one cannot compensate for failing another. A two-lane silent validation requires both AUTO and RECON gates. Reconciliation-only silent validation may be nominated if RECON passes but AUTO fails specifically on contamination/multiplicity, provided no uncertainty is concealed. If any required governance margin is absent, the only selection is `NO_SELECTION`.

## Falsification and adversarial checks

1. Recompute all source and schema hashes; stream complete files. Hash drift, header drift, denominator-manifest drift, or duplicate canonical `DS` stops analysis.
2. Verify complete source counts against the observed profile: 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 detail rows, 331,794 `DS` rows/unique keys, and 17 missing discharge `storetime`; a mismatch requires explanation and a new frozen protocol.
3. Prove reciprocal report/addendum links in both directions. A one-way or cross-subject/admission link cannot define an operative component.
4. Verify row-wise `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u`; only unit `G_u` determines timing. Changing the index-unit clock cannot rescue another unit with `G_u<24h`.
5. Reject any packet containing post-`D` content, later outcomes, POE, route/stratum labels, or expected direction. Replacing `DS` with later faithful text must not alter departure labels.
6. Re-run the exact seed and reproduce every stratum, inclusion probability, and sampled admission. Dropping low-yield, difficult, nonresponsive, or high-weight packets fails no-selection.
7. Synthetic design test: create known finite populations with rare unsafe cases concentrated in sentinel, non-sentinel, and one-unit-majority patterns; verify unbiased weighted burdens and nominal simultaneous coverage. Unweighted estimates must fail when sampling fractions differ.
8. Any sampled admission is reviewed exhaustively for units. A fixture with nine pure omissions and one conflict must route `A_MIXED` within `A_RECON`, never AUTO. Duplicating the nine safe units cannot change its route or weight.
9. A missed extra unit in a non-sentinel admission counts against `C_AUTO` and the non-sentinel missed-unit rate. If the upper bound exceeds its margin, AUTO cannot pass even when all detected units agree.
10. Reviewer perturbation: compute both reviewer directions, leave-one-reviewer-out, third-verifier, minimum/maximum inventories, and adverse unknown assignments. A route that changes across a clinically different category is DEFER for the primary analysis.
11. Redaction perturbation seeds target/action/timing/recipient phrases both inside and outside the dictionary. Leakage/removal above the locked bound blocks context-based routes.
12. Baseline trap: recommendation-unit majority and favorable consensus must not be allowed to pass AUTO when worst-case admission routing fails.
13. Sampling trap: an AUTO-pure sampled subset with known unequal sampling fractions must not be summarized as the all-population purity without weights and finite-population bounds.
14. Zero-event trap: zero observed unsafe AUTO cases must still emit a positive one-sided upper bound; a point estimate of zero alone cannot trigger nomination.
15. Nonresponse trap: selected but unreadable/unreviewed admissions are assigned adverse route-compatible states and remain weighted; complete-case deletion fails.
16. Threshold perturbation: display `G_u>=12h` and `>=72h` sensitivities without altering `N_A`; AUTO or RECON nomination requires no adverse route reversal at 72 hours. Stale/post-departure artifacts remain excluded.
17. POE nonleakage: adding or removing the optional appendix cannot alter unit, state, route, burden, or gate output.
18. Claim verification: computed outputs may support reproducibility, design-weighted burdens, and text-defined contamination only. Any conclusion asserting clinical appropriateness, safe deployment, causal benefit, awareness, communication, or patient outcome must be rejected.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

**Supportive for prospective silent validation** means the exact source/denominator and retrieval audits pass; probability-weighted all-population routes have adequate events and precision; the relevant route's independent agreement and AC1 gates pass; unknown-compatible assignments do not cross routes; and its safety/purity bounds pass simultaneously. AUTO support specifically requires that rare unsafe minority units, multiplicity, and verifier disagreement remain below the preapproved upper bounds. This supports testing the frozen extraction-and-routing process silently in a live environment, not populating a discharge summary.

**Supportive for reconciliation-only validation** occurs when RECON reproducibility and unsafe-content capture pass but AUTO contamination, mixed-admission burden, or additional-unit detection fails the AUTO gate. That is a clinically useful negative result against copy-oriented routing. **Supportive for a two-lane silent study** requires both AUTO and RECON gates; route outcomes and workloads remain separate.

**Adverse** includes reproducible evidence that AUTO contamination exceeds its margin, an unsafe minority unit is repeatedly hidden by the deterministic simple-case screen, mixed admissions are common, timing is misclassified as content failure, or RECON/DEFER fails to capture unsafe/uncertain cases. Such findings reject the corresponding retrospective route nomination; they do not prove that no future system could work.

**Inconclusive** includes undercoverage in retrieval, excessive unknowns, reviewer disagreement, failed redaction, insufficient route events/effective sample size, wide simultaneous intervals, mandatory sentinel census exceeding the fixed workload ceiling, nonresponse, unknown-compatible route crossing, or absent governance approval. Sparse AUTO is not evidence that automation is unnecessary; report its upper burden bound. Passing RECON cannot rescue failed AUTO, and comparator or timing failures cannot be relabeled content failures.

## Required prospective evidence after support

A supportive MIMIC result freezes the extractor, reciprocal-chain logic, unit schema, sampling-independent route code, reviewer manual, and margins for a live silent study. Consecutive adult discharges must capture immutable report finalization/addenda, report availability and viewing, discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission times, physical departure, every candidate unit, clinician/team responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences and competing-risk context, and final artifact. The silent gate changes no care. Independent experts blinded to route and later outcomes assess route confusion, AUTO positive predictive value with all uncertainty adverse, mixed-admission miss rate, timing accuracy, deferral capture, drift, and workload. At least 100 route-positive admissions and 30 in every route claimed are required, or a prespecified accrual stop.

Only after silent validation could a governed route-stratified trial compare usual care with clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation in validated AUTO admissions and side-by-side reconciliation in validated RECON admissions. Correction/dismissal, inappropriate insertion, accepted artifact fidelity, time/alert burden, responsibility assignment, communication, orders/scheduling, follow-up completion, and harms require new workflow data and expert safety adjudication. MIMIC cannot answer those questions.

## Advance over the parent and remaining uncertainty

This child preserves the parent's denominator, reciprocal chronology, canonical artifact, unit clocks, exhaustive states, worst-case mixed-admission routing, and noncausal limits, but replaces a full route-label census with an auditable two-phase verification design. The meaningful advance is not simply fewer reviews: it makes the reviewed admissions a known probability sample of the complete frozen population, gives every admission positive inclusion probability, censes deterministic safety sentinels, verifies every unit within selected admissions, and converts unobserved rare unsafe cases into an explicit finite-population upper bound. Independent atomic double review and complete third verification of sampled AUTO candidates reduce direct route-voting circularity. Route-specific purity, capture, reliability, event, effective-sample-size, and precision gates prevent a strong common route or numerous safe units from hiding a dangerous minority route.

Important uncertainty remains. A deterministic retrieval dictionary can miss semantic opportunities; the negative audit bounds but cannot prove zero misses. A probability sample gives calibrated uncertainty, not certainty about every unsampled admission. The canonical `DS` is an incomplete stored artifact; `G_u` is storage-to-storage rather than actual viewing/editing opportunity; redacted context may omit decisive facts; and expert text agreement is not clinical truth. Governance margins are operational prerequisites, not learned utilities. Accordingly, even a fully supportive result establishes only that these retrospective, text-defined routes are reproducible and pure enough to merit prospective silent validation.
