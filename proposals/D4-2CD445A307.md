# UKB muscle quantity versus quality/function: critical alternative

Status: planned Harbor experiment; no fitted result or clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Question, evidence, and hypothesis

The strongest inspected evidence supports narrower claims. EWGSOP2 makes low strength central, uses low quantity/quality to confirm sarcopenia, and treats poor physical performance as severe disease [K1]. In UKB MRI, adverse thigh composition and especially high muscle-fat infiltration (MFI) were associated with later mortality after adjustment including low grip [K2]. A recent UKB MRI study reports cross-sectional associations of a posterior/anterior thigh-muscle ratio with weak grip and falls beyond total muscle mass [K3]. None establishes that the available MFI and grip fields predict a strictly later mobility change in the same MRI-anchored people, or that such a signal is biological rather than pain/arthritis/neurologic weakness, body-size/MRI measurement confounding, or selective repeat assessment.

Hypothesis:

> Among UKB adults aged at least 50 with complete bilateral thigh MRI quantity, posterior-thigh MFI, and contemporaneous grip at assessment instance 2, after matching on the same quantity and observed body-size/current-function covariates, higher MFI and lower grip increase the calibrated absolute risk of a later worsening in usual walking pace and of a later falls outcome. A muscle-quality/function interpretation is supported only if the increment persists in diagnosis-restricted and repeat-attendance sensitivity analyses, with the same direction at a 730-day lag.

The leading explanation is that quantity and quality/function encode different lower-limb reserve. The strongest rival is an indication/measurement explanation: painful hand or joint disease and neurological disease lower grip, MRI values are confounded by body size and unavailable sex, and return for a later assessment is selected. Persistence is compatible with prognostic information, not proof of mechanism, causality, treatment effect, or clinical utility.

This advances [K2] from mortality to later mobility, and [K3] from cross-sectional distribution associations to a date-ordered test using different available quality/function fields. It is an adaptation, not a reproduction.

## Exposures and estimands

Fit transformations in development only.

- Q is the sum of the four bilateral anterior/posterior thigh fat-free volumes; preserve all four components.
- MFI is the bilateral mean of `23355-2.0` and `23356-2.0`.
- Grip is the maximum valid left/right grip; bilateral-complete is primary, side-specific/discordant analyses are sensitivity.
- Baseline pace/falls are baseline state, not dated incident events.

After coding resolution, primary pace outcome is later category lower than baseline, reporting worsening/unchanged/improvement. Primary falls outcome is later value above the verified no-fall category, with full ordinal counts secondary. Falls asks about the last year and is not a dated fall event.

The main estimand is population-standardized absolute risk difference under quantity matching: MFI +1 development SD and grip -1 SD versus both at development means, holding Q and observed baseline covariates at each person's value. Also report nested Q-only -> Q+MFI, Q-only -> Q+grip, and Q-only -> Q+MFI+grip risk contrasts, component risks, and 95% intervals. A 1 percentage-point lower-bound marker is a scale descriptor, not a treatment threshold. This is prognostic/descriptive, not causal.

Matched learned alternative: CPU gradient-boosted trees for each endpoint using exactly the same participants, inputs, missingness policy, T0/T1 rule, split and outcomes. It can reveal Q-by-MFI, Q-by-grip, thresholds, and side/compartment interactions that additive regression loses. It is substantive only if a held-out quantity-matched risk contrast or rival interpretation changes; a small rank-metric gain is not enough. The transparent model is primary because it exposes the estimand and is auditable with roughly 4,500 endpoint pairs. The learned model is retained to test nonlinear scientific structure. ## Rival falsification

Pain/arthritis/neurologic rival: repeat the primary analysis in participants without prespecified pre-T0 inpatient code families, report each diagnosis stratum and same-index code-adjusted model. A grip signal that disappears while MFI remains stable supports the rival; a persistent result is only compatible with a quality signal because outpatient pain and examination remain missing.

MRI/body-size/sex rival: adjust using the available age/BMI/weight/height fields; compare bilateral and side/compartment estimates, and report body-size sensitivity. Sex cannot be adjusted or stratified from this verified table; a result is explicitly limited by that missing evidence. Strong attenuation or side-specific instability is adverse for the biological interpretation.

Repeat-selection/reverse-timing rival: compare unweighted results with baseline-only inverse-probability weighting for having a valid later assessment, report weights/positivity, and repeat at >=730 days and by early/late T1. Do not condition on post-T0 variables. A large weighting shift or short-lag-only signal is adverse; an imprecise weighted result is inconclusive.

Supportive evidence requires at least one endpoint passing support/positivity/calibration gates, a positive held-out Q+MFI or Q+grip increment beyond Q-only with 95% interval above zero and lower bound at least 1 point for a prespecified endpoint, compatible direction across pace/falls or lag sensitivity, no diagnosis-clean collapse, and no large return-selection shift. This supports validation/adjudication research, not deployment, causality or mechanism.

Adverse evidence is a held-out null/reversal, Q-only equivalence, baseline-only association, body-size attenuation, diagnosis-restricted collapse, large weighting shift, short-lag-only pattern, or a learned rank gain without the prespecified risk contrast. It weakens this data-bound claim, not all muscle-quality hypotheses.

Inconclusive evidence is unverified outcome coding, failed code/date pairing, fewer than 100 holdout events, missing class support, unstable calibration/positivity, or intervals spanning zero and the scale marker. An imprecise null is not refutation.
