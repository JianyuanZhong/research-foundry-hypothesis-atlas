# Per-cell FLS discrimination of a graded MerTK/CD206pos brake vs a fixed composition/state artifact (E-MTAB-8873), with an explicit unmeasured-macrophage limit

Domain: therapeutic_targets (D2). Seed: D2-T2. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis] (valid, mu 32.86, gen 4). Key works [K1],[K2],[K3]. Branch: work/lead/. Screening status: **planned, not executed** (no managed compute job was run; only bounded read-only inspection of the prepared files and archive members, receipt in `sources/DATA_PROBE_8873_celllevel.txt`).

## 1. Unresolved question (the opening)

In RA synovium, does the MerTK/CD206pos (MRC1+) macrophage population **actively exert a graded, dose-like brake** on the FLS matrix-degrading/effector program, or is the pos-vs-neg FLS-effector difference a **fixed composition/state artifact** (well-level macrophage load, an SPP1+ LAM vs FOLR2/TREM2 resident state, or FLS-donor identity)?

The parent established a two-group score over mostly n=1 ratio arms and read the FLS effector program from **pseudobulk sample means** with **well-name composition proxies**. Two recorded weaknesses remain: (a) sample means cannot separate a graded per-cell response (a subpopulation shifts or every cell shifts slightly) from a fixed composition shift; (b) `pos_fraction`/`load_proxy` are read from well names, not measured. This child changes the **discriminating statistic** (per-cell distribution and response fraction) and the **measurement basis** (cell-level archive members), and it states one thing the data cannot do (see §4).

## 2. Clinical importance

Roughly one-third of RA patients do not sustain drug-free remission, and the "difficult-to-treat" ceiling is now framed as a **failed inflammation-resolution program** in which a macrophage compartment stripped of pro-resolving, efferocytic (MerTK+) subsets co-assembles a self-sustaining stromal–myeloid circuit [K3]. If the MerTK+ subset **dose-dependently** lowers the FLS effector program, then restoring that subset/its signalling is a target-prioritisation decision distinct from simply suppressing inflammation; if instead the FLS effect is fully explained by composition/state, MerTK positivity is a **state marker** and a poor standalone target. Resolving which is true changes whether MerTK-directed resolution therapy is worth prioritising.

## 3. Hypothesis and the rival that makes it falsifiable

- **Hypothesis H_G (graded brake).** Increasing the MerTK/CD206pos macrophage fraction within the same FLS donor lowers the FLS effector program *per cell* — the whole effector-score distribution shifts left and the **fraction of effector-high FLS cells** falls monotonically with pos_fraction, beyond any well-level load/state adjustment.
- **Rival H_C (fixed composition/state).** The pos-vs-neg FLS difference is a step change from macrophage presence/composition or FLS-donor identity; the per-cell FLS effector distribution is **flat** (or explained by load/state) across pos_fraction once composition and FLS-donor are accounted for.

Pre-registered discriminating predictions on the one cell in the design that holds FLS donor and macrophage donors fixed while varying composition (verified names): `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_1Mpos_SA266A_3Mneg` (pos_fraction 0.25) vs `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_3Mpos_SA266A_1Mneg` (0.75), plus the SA258R ladder {0.0 (FLS_only, 1332 cells), 0.25 (1364), 0.75 (1145), 1.0 (Mpos, 1207)}.
- H_G predicts: effector-high FLS **fraction** at 0.25 > 0.75, monotone non-increasing across the ladder; and a donor-matched **MerTK-inhibitor reversal** de-represses the effector fraction (inhibitor arms present in the design).
- H_C predicts: within-noise, load-explained, or fully absorbed by FLS-donor + macrophage-state covariates; no monotone response-fraction gradient and no inhibitor-reversal.

**Estimand.** Per-FLS-cell FLS effector score (pre-registered 9-gene set MMP1/MMP3/MMP9/MMP13, IL6, CXCL8, CXCL10, CCL2, CCL5; all verified present as `Reference_end` rows in the panel). Primary statistic: difference in **effector-high fraction** (top-tercile cutoff fixed on FLS-only baseline) between 0.25 and 0.75 with FLS-donor-clustered 95% CI; secondary: distribution shift (per-cell mean and median) and monotonicity over the ladder.

**Method comparison (required reasoning).**
- **Baseline (retained):** pseudobulk sample-mean effector score with well-name `pos_fraction` and `load_proxy` covariates, as in the parent. Cheap, and it already carries the composition test.
- **Substantive alternative:** per-cell analysis of the 445-feature FLS matrices, quantifying the *distribution* (fraction of effector-high cells, quantile shift) rather than the mean. What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses: baseline means are identical whether a subset of FLS cells strongly responds or all cells respond weakly — a mechanistic distinction the brake hypothesis makes. Chosen over a neural/compositional model because the uncertainty is about the *shape* of a per-cell distribution, not a predictive gain; a heavier model would not add scientific information for this estimand.
- Bounded resource estimate: 33 samples × ≤~1400 cells × 445 features = ~20M values; fits comfortably in CPU memory (<2 GB) after row selection. No GPU needed; a CPU job suffices (~minutes). Discovery-time verification was read-only and unallocated, so this remains a **solver-envelope estimate, not measured on our allocation**.

## 4. Data bindings (re-verified this episode) and the essential limit

- `prepared/E-MTAB-8873.counts.tsv.gz`: 34 columns (`feature` + 33 samples), 445 feature rows; join key `sample_id`; contains MMP1|NM_002421.3, MMP3|NM_002422.4, MMP9|NM_004994.2, MMP13|NM_002427.3, IL6|NM_000600.4, CXCL8|NM_000584.3, CXCL10|NM_001565.3, CCL2|NM_002982.3, CCL5|NM_002985.2, MERTK|NM_006343.2, MRC1|NM_002438.3, TREM2, FOLR2, SPP1. **GAS6, PROS1, TYRO3 are absent** from the panel.
- `prepared/E-MTAB-8873.design.json`: list of 33 records; fields `sample_id`, `donor_id`, `donor_status` (=`requires_contributing_donor_model`), `other_donor_id`, `age`, `sex`, `tissue`, `cell_type`, `condition` (e.g. `co-cultured with MerTK/CD206pos macrophages`), `treatment` (e.g. `macrophages pre-treated with MerTK inhibitor`), `cells`, `source_metadata`. Multiple/reused donors ⇒ ordinary donor t-tests are **disabled**; donor-clustered/resampled inference required.
- Cell-level archive members `E-MTAB-8873/*_RSEC_MolsPerCell.txt.gz` (e.g. `R2_SA258R_FLS_only_...`, `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_1Mpos_SA266A_3Mneg_...`): 446 columns (`Cell_Index` + 445 features) × 1332–1364 cells; gene symbols carry the same `SYMBOL|NM_x|Reference_end` form, so features align between prepared and per-cell files by symbol.
- **ESSENTIAL LIMIT (measured, do not overstate):** in the per-cell members the population is FLS (`COL1A1+` 100%), and macrophage identity is near-absent (MERTK+ 1–3%, PTPRC+ ≤23 cells; THY1/PDPN/LYZ undetected in the panel). Therefore **per-macrophage MerTK dosage and MerTK-intrinsic signalling are NOT measurable** here. This design tests the FLS-side per-cell response to well composition; it cannot separate MerTK *signalling* from MerTK *marker presence*, and it cannot observe the macrophage responder cell. A macrophage-intrinsic test requires unavailable protein/functional data.

## 5. Analysis plan and falsification

1. Per sample: map features→symbols; restrict to the 9 effector genes and FLS identity genes; compute a per-cell effector score (log1p-normalised, panel-restricted).
2. Fix the "effector-high" cutoff on the FLS-only baseline (SA258R_FLS_only) at the 67th percentile; hold it fixed for all wells.
3. Primary contrast: effector-high **fraction** difference between 1Mpos_3Mneg (0.25) and 3Mpos_1Mneg (0.75) within donor SA258R, with FLS-donor-clustered bootstrap 95% CI (well-level resampling to respect well dependence).
4. Adjust for well-level `load_proxy` (measured: total counts/cells per well) and macrophage-state proxy (SPP1 vs FOLR2/TREM2), plus FLS-donor fixed effect.
5. Ladder monotonicity (SA258R {0.0,0.25,0.75,1.0}); donor-matched MerTK-**inhibitor** reversal arms as a direction check.
6. **Falsifiers.** H_G is rejected if: the donor-clustered 95% CI for the 0.25→0.75 fraction difference includes 0; or the sign is inverted; or the ladder is non-monotone after load/state adjustment; or the inhibitor arms move the effector fraction in the wrong direction.
   H_C is rejected (and H_G strengthened, not proven) if the fraction gradient survives load+state+donor adjustment with the predicted sign and the inhibitor reversal is consistent.
7. **Outcome meanings.**
   - *Supportive* (H_G survives): a per-cell, composition-adjusted brake signature — justifies a MerTK-resolution-target follow-up and a donor-matched functional assay.
   - *Adverse* (flat/inverted): the pos-vs-neg FLS difference is composition/state; MerTK positivity is a **state marker**, and MerTK-directed therapy is deprioritised.
   - *Inconclusive*: n=1 ladder arms, targeted 445-feature panel, and absent macrophage readout prevent separation — report as unresolved, not negative.
8. Retained: exploratory orthogonal line = E-MTAB-8316 UNC106238 vs none (4 donors, underpowered, ENSG→symbol mapping, selectivity literature-bounded); optional cross-sectional anchor E-MTAB-8322 (27 samples/27 donors; SA149/SA149P relatedness unresolved; cross-sectional only).

## 6. What this does not establish

No protein/wet-lab causality; not a clinical-efficacy claim; no MerTK-intrinsic mechanism claim; no macrophage-level dosage claim; the composition axis is limited to n=1 ladder arms and a 445-feature targeted panel.
