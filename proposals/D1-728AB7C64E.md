# Proposal: plan- and reassessment-context repair of schedule-robust post-TACE pathways

## Episode, parent, lineage, and scientific deliverable

This Episode-24 child evolves assessed-valid parent \`[prior hypothesis]\` (Episode 23), whose parent is \`[prior hypothesis]\` (Episode 22), with the earlier component-resolved lineage recorded in the parent record. The parent’s schedule-robust, component-resolved AFP/albumin/total-bilirubin question is preserved: first encounter-constrained timed TACE-like index, day-45 landmark and risk set, strict-versus-broad pathway ladder, observation/capture controls, patient-temporal split, clustered uncertainty, and noncausal evidence boundary all remain primary design elements.

The remaining clinically consequential ambiguity is treatment-context/endpoint validity. A later strict TACE-containing procedure record can be a serial procedure that was already being operationally planned, a procedure preceded by local reassessment workflow, or a newly recorded action. The snapshot cannot establish indication, intent, technical completion, radiologic response, progression, death, or outside care. However, it does contain dated non-drug orders and dated examination records. This child uses them to test whether the parent trajectory contrast is specific to a *plan-negative and reassessment-proxied recorded pathway*, rather than merely reflecting serial scheduling or generic local capture.

The actual solver deliverable is newly fitted context-stratified, schedule-standardized estimates on the parent’s locked cohort:

1. construct and audit a training-frozen recorded-plan proxy from \`orders\`;
2. construct dated reassessment-workflow proxies from \`examinations\` and orders;
3. estimate the parent favorable-versus-discordant component contrast separately for strict TACE records with and without those proxies, plus the original unstratified strict endpoint;
4. compare matched schedule-only, schedule-plus-plan, trajectory, and profile-by-context models;
5. fit or explicitly defer a joint irregular-time component/observation/plan-process model;
6. return patient-clustered uncertainty, overlap, calibration, timestamp and workflow falsifications, and a conclusion-to-output interpretation manifest.

Discovery has not fitted these models and claims no clinical result.

## Strongest supported claim and unresolved question

The strongest available evidence supports only data structure and local observability: the HCC snapshot has 12 ordinary CSV sources, linked by \`(患者主索引, 就诊号)\`; procedures and labs have usable timestamps; the procedure source contains heterogeneous liver-related labels; the order source contains dated non-drug order text and status fields; and the examination source contains dated examination names plus narrative findings/diagnoses. The existing parent audit reported 338,040 procedure rows/7,781 labels and selected-name lab counts, but these are availability audits, not response or outcome findings.

The unresolved question is whether the parent’s favorable recorded component pattern is associated with a later strict recorded TACE pathway specifically in contexts that are not already marked by a locally recorded future-plan proxy, and whether that contrast is stronger when the later record is preceded by a dated reassessment workflow. This would make the recorded-pathway interpretation more clinically informative than an undifferentiated repeat-procedure endpoint. It remains a prognostic association with local records.

The advance over the parent is substantive: it separates two clinically different explanations for a later TACE record—serial planned care versus a pathway emerging after the day-45 landmark—using fields actually present in the HCC snapshot. It does not convert these proxies into intent or claim that plan-negative means unplanned.

## Falsifiable hypothesis and estimands

Retain the parent assay-specific, training-only component states. Let \(A_F\) be AFP decrease with non-worsening albumin and non-worsening total bilirubin, and \(A_D\) be AFP decrease with worsening albumin or total bilirubin. These are recorded assay-standardized categories, not response, reserve, or clinical-unit labels.

Define a recorded future-plan proxy \(P=1\) when any qualifying non-drug order is created after \(t_0\) and through \(t_0+45\) days, using the order’s \`开立时间\`, for the same patient and any linked encounter. The primary plan-specific lexical screen is frozen from fitting data: literal \`TACE\`, \`动脉化疗栓塞\`, or a combination of \`肝动脉\` with \`栓塞\`/therapeutic \`化疗\`; imaging-only \`CTA\`/\`CTV\`, angiography-only, and \`介入\` alone are not plan-specific and are retained as broad-order controls. Raw order status is retained but does not define completion. A canceled order can still be evidence that a plan was recorded; a non-canceled-status sensitivity is mandatory.

Define a dated reassessment-workflow proxy \(R=1\) for a strict post-day-45 TACE episode at time \(t_e\) if, in the open interval \((t_e-30\text{ days},t_e)\), there is a qualifying liver/upper-abdominal examination or non-drug assessment order. The primary proxy uses only the examination/order name and service/order time, not report interpretation. The qualifying name screen requires a liver/upper-abdominal/portal term and a CT, MRI/magnetic-resonance, ultrasound, enhancement, or contrast term; ambiguous names are retained separately. Narrative \`检查所见\` and \`检查诊断\` are sensitivity text fields only and are not radiologic adjudication.

The primary hypothesis is an interaction:

> After the parent’s schedule/capture standardization, the \(A_F-A_D\) CIF contrast for a first later strict TACE record with \(P=0\) is directionally lower and materially larger in magnitude than the contrast for \(P=1\), with a prespecified target of \(\Delta_{P=0}(320)\le -0.03\) and interaction \(\Delta_{P=0}(320)-\Delta_{P=1}(320)\le -0.02\), subject to interval and overlap criteria. The contrast is expected to be more compatible with the plan-negative/reassessment-proxied endpoint than with the plan-positive endpoint.

Here \(\Delta_p(t)=F_{\mathrm{TACE\_strict},P=p}(t\mid A_F)-F_{\mathrm{TACE\_strict},P=p}(t\mid A_D)\), estimated as a predictive, noncausal contrast on the same common-support distribution of baseline/capture covariates and schedule \(S\). Estimate the original unstratified parent contrast as the primary continuity output. Report \(\Delta\) for mutually exclusive first states: \`TACE_strict_plan_positive\`, \`TACE_strict_plan_negative\`, and, where event support permits, \`TACE_strict_reassessment_proxied\` and \`TACE_strict_unanchored\`. The reassessment split is a context descriptor of the already observed event, not a causal mediator analysis.

The plan-positive result is not itself “bad”: a trajectory contrast concentrated there would support a serial-plan explanation and weaken the claim that the trajectory identifies a newly emerging pathway. A plan-negative result is not an unplanned-treatment or progression result because absent orders may reflect missing capture.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

All models use the same patients, \(t_0\), day-45 information boundary, outcomes, temporal split, common-support rules, and locked test.

- \(B_0\): the parent history-only regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazard model.
- \(B_{\mathrm{time}}\): the parent schedule/capture baseline with measurement timing, assay availability/counts, contact intensity, examination/order opportunity, procedure-source mix, coding density, and ambiguous-label indicators, but no laboratory values.
- \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\): \(B_{\mathrm{time}}\) plus \(P\), raw order-status categories, order-to-index timing, and broad-order opportunity; no laboratory values. This tests whether plan/capture context alone explains the parent contrast.
- \(B_{\mathrm{traj}}\): \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\) plus separate assay-standardized component states, delays, missingness and uncertainty.
- \(B_{\mathrm{context}}\) (primary transparent test): \(B_{\mathrm{traj}}\) plus frozen component-profile-by-\(P\) interaction and prespecified plan-positive/plan-negative CIF contrasts; \(R\) is used only to classify the observed TACE context and is not used as a pre-event predictor of the same endpoint.
- \(M_{\mathrm{joint-context}}\): a substantive irregular-time multi-output state-space/marked-event model. It models separate latent AFP, albumin and bilirubin states with assay/specimen observation models, exact measurement times, missing/qualitative indicators, local contact intensity, and the order-plan process through day 45; its competing transition hazards output plan-stratified strict/broad/diagnostic/non-liver CIFs. Fit only through day 45 for predictors. It can reveal asynchronous/nonlinear latent change and whether the plan interaction survives measurement error and informative observation, which categorical \(B_{\mathrm{context}}\) loses. This is a scientific alternative because the uncertainty concerns latent component evolution jointly with the recorded plan process, not because it is complex or GPU-based.

The learned alternative is CPU-first and may be deferred with an explicit checkpoint if convergence or positivity fails. A generic transformer is not selected: the unresolved limitation is plan/endpoint semantics and missing clinical adjudication, not representation capacity. ALBI/MELD or a mechanistic reserve model remains deferred because HCC has no lab units/validated thresholds and no INR/reserve adjudication. Full multimodal reproduction is impossible because HCC has no image files; the research-ambition README states that the cancer demonstration’s main article/full STAR Methods were unavailable, so only its accessible supplement was inspected. A causal treatment-policy analysis, survival/death model, and external transport study require unavailable intent, assignment, mortality, outside-care, and validation data.

## Split, uncertainty, falsification, and interpretation gates

Use 2010–2022 for fitting, 2023 for tuning, and 2024 through the cutoff as locked test when event support permits; otherwise use a deterministic patient-hash split declared before fitting. No patient crosses splits. Freeze procedure/order/reassessment vocabularies, endpoint and episode rules, assay tolerances, plan time windows, sparse-cell and overlap rules, model tuning, bootstrap count, and margin before locked evaluation. Do not use post-day-45 data in predictors. Use patient-clustered bootstrap or equivalent intervals, never row bootstrap. Report cause-specific hazards, CIF at 30/90/320 days after the landmark, \(\Delta_{P=0}\), \(\Delta_{P=1}\), their interaction, calibration, log score, overlap, effective sample size if weights are used, and plan/reassessment cell counts.

Mandatory falsifications:

1. **Parent continuity and schedule-only:** reproduce the parent unstratified strict contrast; compare \(B_{\mathrm{time}}\), \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\), and \(B_{\mathrm{context}}\). If \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\) reproduces the profile contrast, trajectory-specific interpretation is adverse.
2. **Plan-context interaction:** compare plan-positive and plan-negative contrasts with the prespecified sign. A similar or stronger plan-positive-only contrast favors serial recorded planning over a newly emerging pathway.
3. **Order temporal leakage/control:** repeat after excluding orders from the index encounter and orders whose \`开立时间\` is within the first 24 hours after \(t_0\); repeat with non-canceled status only. A large change indicates index-bundle or status sensitivity.
4. **Workflow specificity:** apply the same plan screen to non-liver/diagnostic order controls and apply reassessment labeling with imaging-only/angiography-only exclusions. Comparable profile-by-plan effects for non-liver workflows are adverse.
5. **Timestamp permutation:** retain the parent’s within-patient, exact-assay value-to-time permutation; additionally permute plan-order times within patient among eligible day-45 order rows while preserving order text/status and outcome. A preserved context interaction supports timing/capture artifact.
6. **Placebo and endpoint robustness:** retain the parent pre-index placebo, strict-versus-broad ladder, diagnostic/mixed/non-liver controls, and encounter-only versus 24-hour episode sensitivity. A comparable placebo/control interaction or ambiguous-bundle concentration is adverse.
7. **Latent-model check:** require convergence and observation-model sensitivity; incompatible \(M_{\mathrm{joint-context}}\) estimates are inconclusive/adverse, not grounds to select the favored model.

Supportive computation requires the parent unstratified contrast to remain directionally compatible; \(\Delta_{P=0}(320)\) to meet the prespecified direction/margin with a patient-level interval excluding zero or the margin; a materially different, directionally weaker \(\Delta_{P=1}\); nontrivial incremental contribution beyond \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\); adequate plan-cell overlap and calibration; weaker non-liver/placebo/permuted signals; stable strict-versus-broad and episode results; and a compatible converged joint model. This supports only a robust association with a recorded local pathway whose context is more compatible with “not locally pre-planned” than with serial-plan explanation; it does not establish unplanned care, response, progression, benefit, or intent.

Adverse computation is a null/reversal of the parent contrast, \(B_{\mathrm{plan}}\) explaining it, a plan-positive-only or status/index-bundle concentration, similar control/placebo/permuted effects, poor overlap, endpoint instability, or joint-model sensitivity. These favor scheduling, capture, coding, or measurement explanations.

Inconclusive computation includes sparse plan-negative or reassessment cells, missing order timestamps, unstable lexical vocabulary, positivity failure, inadequate follow-up, temporal-split failure, intervals crossing the margin, inability to separate strict therapy from ambiguous records, or joint-model nonconvergence. No null/adverse/inconclusive result establishes absence of biological response.

The verifier can check paths, schema IDs, headers, two-key aggregation, frozen screens, time boundaries, no leakage, episode/endpoint construction, context labels, competing-risk calculations, predictions, uncertainty, calibration, overlap, permutations, and conclusion-to-output links. It cannot adjudicate treatment indication or intent, order completion, technical success, radiologic/pathologic response, viable tumor, recurrence/progression, liver failure, death, mortality, survival, outside care, appropriateness, benefit, utility, or external validity. Those require clinical adjudication, linked outcomes, and another prospective/external study.
