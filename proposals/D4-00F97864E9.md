## Unresolved clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

Among adults with a valid paired UK Biobank assessment and the same eight-field biochemical panel at both assessments, does retaining the earlier panel improve prediction of a subsequent recorded major circulatory inpatient event beyond the current panel and the time between assessments, particularly when current-only risk is ambiguous? Does that conditional information remain stable when models fitted through 31 December 2012 are applied without refitting to participants whose later assessment occurred in calendar year 2013?

This matters because a historical result might affect whether a clinician or research triage system treats a current borderline risk estimate as needing review. The clinically consequential decision is whether historical measurements contain reproducible conditional prognostic information worth retaining; it is not whether repeat testing, treatment, referral, or monitoring causes benefit.

Primary hypothesis:

> In the prespecified central 60% of current-only recorded-event risk, adding the prior biochemical panel to current values and verified assessment spacing improves held-out prediction of the later three-category recorded endpoint more than the equal-weight average of the lower and upper current-risk tails, and the corresponding contrast is retained within a prespecified practical transport margin when early-through-2012 fits are applied without refitting to the 2013 cohort.

The strongest available evidence supports the existence of the literal field-instance cells, assessment date fields, position-matched code/date arrays, death-date fields, and one-to-one eid joins. Public field lookup provides contextual descriptions of these fields, but the local catalog does not contain a complete field dictionary, clinical adjudication, or individual observation-end certificate. The unresolved claim is therefore conditional prediction of a fully specified recorded endpoint, with any incident-like or clinical interpretation gated on ascertainment evidence.

Let T0 = 53-0.0 and T1 = 53-1.0. The solver must certify that field 53 is the assessment-centre date and that these instances are comparable assessment landmarks. T0 and T1 are not specimen dates; blood collection timing is a separate evidence gate. Let H = T1 + five calendar years, with leap-day handling recorded in the manifest.

Exclude anyone with a target code/date pair on or before T1, because the primary question is conditional on no previously recorded target in these fields. Exclude anyone whose death date is on or before T1. For the remaining participant:

- T means the earliest qualifying paired target date strictly after T1 and no later than H;
- D means death strictly after T1 and no later than H, occurring before the target or on the same date as a target;
- N means neither a qualifying target nor death is recorded through H.

If a target occurs before death, T takes precedence. If target and death share a date, D takes precedence. N is never called disease-free or unaffected. A five-year N category is accepted only after a verified certificate of complete outcome ascertainment through H for the relevant participants and calendar range. The recorded endpoint is based on inpatient diagnosis recording and death registry dates. It does not establish disease onset, outpatient disease, severity, reversibility, adjudicated cardiovascular disease, or absence of disease.

## Selection, split and frozen ambiguity band

Create a flow from the population anchor through valid age/sex, T0/T1, interval, complete paired panel, prior-event exclusion, early/late era, outcome ascertainment, frozen risk band, and T/D/N endpoint. Report every count, including malformed outcome positions, target events, deaths, exclusions, and six era-by-band cells.

Among T0-complete participants, define S=1 as valid paired T1 plus complete panel and accepted endpoint eligibility. Fit a regularized selection model in early data using only pre-T1 T0 panel values, age, sex and T0 calendar year. Apply it unchanged to late T0-complete participants. The stabilized sensitivity weight is w_i = P(S=1)/p-hat(S=1|X_i), with numerator from the early training prevalence and truncation at early 1st and 99th percentiles. Report overlap, maximum weight, standardized differences and effective sample size by era and band. This is a measured-selection sensitivity under a missing-at-random assumption, not a causal correction.

Use one deterministic participant-level five-fold hash split on eid within early data. All imputation, scaling, regularization, hyperparameter selection, calibration and selection modeling are training-fold-only. In each fold, fit M0 on the other four folds and produce held-out three-class probabilities for every eligible early participant. Define q20 and q80 from all eligible early out-of-fold M0 probabilities for class T using one recorded quantile algorithm. Assign:

- L: r < q20;
- A: q20 <= r < q80;
- U: r >= q80.

The same q20/q80 and half-open labels apply to late predictions from early-fitted models. No late outcome, late quantile, M1b output, nonlinear output, or permuted prior field may define a band. If q20 or q80 is nonfinite, q20 >= q80, or the band is nonidentifiable, fail closed.

## Transparent baseline and substantive learned alternative

All models use the same participant population, endpoint, split, frozen bands, held-out predictions and uncertainty.

- M0: current eight-field panel, age at recruitment, sex and T1 calendar year.
- M1a: M0 plus verified repeat spacing L.
- M1b: M1a plus all eight literal prior-panel values. The primary prior-information increment is M1b versus M1a.
- M1c: M1a plus eight raw changes current minus prior, only as a representation sensitivity after timing and units pass. It cannot replace M1b versus M1a.
- M2: shallow gradient-boosted multinomial model with exactly M1b inputs.
- M2a: matched shallow gradient-boosted multinomial model with exactly M1a inputs.

M0/M1a/M1b/M1c are regularized multinomial probability models with fold-local preprocessing. M2/M2a use a small prespecified depth/learning-rate/regularization grid, fixed tree limit and training-fold-only early stopping or inner validation. No model may use the outcome, event dates, late data, or future information in feature construction.

M2 can reveal stable nonlinear current/prior discordance and spacing interactions that additive M1b averages away. It is scientifically substantive only if its shape summaries are stable across early folds, its calibration is acceptable, and the same early-fitted models behave coherently in the late no-refit evaluation. A small predictive improvement without a stable transparent nested contrast is inconclusive and cannot establish a clinical utility or mechanism.

A waist/grip/body-composition alternative is explicitly deferred. Assessment and main visibly contain physical/anthropometric IDs including 48, 49, 50, 21001, 21002 and many 23xxx fields, and source values are present. Genomics contains derived metadata fields such as 22000-series and 262xx fields. However, the local catalog does not certify field labels, units, missing-value meanings, visit comparability, raw sequence/variant semantics, or clinical utility. A new body-composition or genetic estimand would require a separate scientifically approved child with field-level dictionaries, timing and endpoint justification. ## Outcomes, uncertainty and analysis

For each era, band, model and weighting choice, report multiclass Brier score, multiclass log loss, one-vs-rest calibration intercept and slope for T/D/N, reliability and resolution, observed-versus-predicted target/death frequencies, and fixed descriptive risk movement at 1%, 2.5%, 5%, 10% and 15%. These thresholds are descriptive and are not validated clinical utilities.

For e in {early, late}, define BS(M,g,e) as the weighted multiclass Brier score within band g. Let d_g,e = BS(M1b,g,e) - BS(M1a,g,e), so negative values favor adding prior values. Define the primary interaction contrast:

D_e = d_A,e - (d_L,e + d_U,e)/2.

The no-refit transport estimand is tau_A = D_late,no-refit - D_early. The practical stability margin is [-0.002, 0.002] Brier units. This is a transport criterion, not a clinical-benefit threshold. Also report the direct d_A,e increment; D is not the overall population improvement.

Use paired participant bootstrap intervals by resampling with replacement within each fixed era-by-band cell, retaining fold, band, endpoint, predictions and weight. Compute d_g,e, D_e and tau_A from paired predictions. A permitted sensitivity refits the complete early pipeline in prespecified bootstrap samples and recomputes early q20/q80 before applying those cut points to late predictions. If model-training uncertainty is not included, state that explicitly. Report failed-support bootstrap replicates rather than changing bands or endpoints.

Hard support gates for interpreting the primary interaction and transport claim are at least 100 T events overall, at least 200 eligible participants and 30 T events in each early-L, early-A, early-U, late-L, late-A and late-U cell, and late weighted ESS at least 200 and at least half the corresponding unweighted cell in every band. Require finite weights and adequate early-to-late covariate overlap. If any gate fails, the claim is inconclusive; no band redefinition is permitted.

## Interpretation rules

Supportive evidence requires:

- all semantic, code-domain, timing, ascertainment, join and integrity gates pass;
- at least 100 target events and all six era-by-band support cells pass;
- direct d_A,early and d_A,late are negative with 95% upper confidence bounds below zero;
- D_early and D_late are negative with 95% upper confidence bounds below zero;
- the selection-weighted sensitivity does not reverse the direct A-band or D direction;
- M1b versus M1a calibration shifts satisfy absolute intercept shift <=0.05 and absolute slope shift <=0.10 for every endpoint class and era;
- the complete 95% interval for tau_A lies inside [-0.002, 0.002];
- no permutation, pairing, leakage, fold-stability or overlap failure occurs.

This supports conditional prior-panel information for the specified recorded endpoint in the supported UKB analysis population and a calendar-era stability result. It does not support retaining prior values as a clinical policy, a treatment effect, a monitoring benefit, a threshold, or a disease-onset measure. Stable M2 nonlinear structure can strengthen understanding of the information shape but cannot rescue a failed transparent nested contrast.

Adverse evidence is a non-improving or worsening d_A, a nonnegative or reversed D, a gain explained by spacing M1a rather than prior values M1b, reversal under selection weighting, poor calibration, fold instability, early-to-late reversal, failure of the permutation controls, or tau_A outside the practical margin. This weakens the case for retaining prior values for this recorded-risk task; it does not prove that prior measurements have no value in another population or endpoint.

Inconclusive evidence includes any failed or unavailable code-domain/timing/ascertainment certificate, malformed or unpaired code/date positions, death-date disagreement, fewer than 100 target events, sparse cells, poor overlap or ESS, nonidentifiable bands, wide intervals, conflicting missingness/endpoint sensitivities, or an uncalibrated/unstable learned-model gain. “No qualifying record in the available source” under incomplete follow-up is inconclusive for the five-year hypothesis, not a negative clinical result.

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema/header integrity, full-array position handling, joins, flow counts, endpoint construction, event ordering, model fitting, held-out predictions, score arithmetic, calibration, bootstrap uncertainty, overlap/ESS and permutation behavior. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for disease onset, outpatient events, severity, complete ascertainment beyond the supplied evidence, assay and specimen validity, causal repeat-testing effects, action utility, clinical thresholds, external validation, and prospective policy evaluation.
