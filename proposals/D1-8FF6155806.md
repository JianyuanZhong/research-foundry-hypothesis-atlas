# Episode 43 (eICU) — Executing the detection negative-control: is the (0,24]h MAP<65 burden → (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI association a physiologic dose, a monitoring artifact, or treatment intensity?

Child of the Lead-assigned board leader `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 32.24, valid, **reference_delivery incomplete**). Operation: **evolve** (targeted repair + executed advance). No parent rating is inherited. Branch: eICU 2.0 `(0,24]h MAP<65 burden → (24,72]h creatinine-rise KDIGO-AKI`, dose vs detection vs treatment (episodes 28–42).

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
ICU teams titrate fluids/vasopressors to a MAP target, and more time below MAP 65 mmHg is widely reported to track higher creatinine-based AKI (K1 base-model AUC 0.725; K2 aOR 1.78 for ≥30 min). Three rivals, with different clinical consequences, remain unseparated:
- **H-dose:** the (0,24]h MAP<65 burden is a physiologic ischemic dose that independently raises (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI risk → "time below target" is a treatment target.
- **H-observation:** hypotension triggers more monitoring/lab testing, so AKI is *detected* more without proportionate injury → the apparent target partly reflects who was watched.
- **H-treatment:** the association is a marker of co-occurring vasoactive/fluid treatment intensity (confounding by indication) → modelling care intensity comes first.

## 2. Closest inspected work and remaining question
- [K1] (abstract_only) Time-weighted MAP<65 burden predicts creatinine-KDIGO AKI (base AUC 0.725); arterial-waveform morphology adds nothing (excludes ΔAUC >~0.02–0.03). Establishes the scalar signal but conditions on **no** monitoring/treatment channel.
- [K2] (abstract_only) Cumulative intraoperative MAP<65 duration → postoperative ICU admission (aOR 1.78 for ≥30 min; per 50 mmHg·min area OR 1.07). Bounds magnitude and states causality/threshold are not established.
- [K3] (abstract_only, trial design) PHACS pilot RCT targets MAP 60–65 mmHg under vasopressors, primary outcome hourly MAP — bounding any claim that an intervention threshold is already settled.
**Unresolved claim this experiment tests:** in eICU 2.0, does the burden→AKI association survive modelling of the **detection process** (a draw-intensity negative control) and the **vasoactive/fluid treatment channel**, or does it attenuate to ~null under one of them?

## 5. Design (frozen for compilation)
- **Population:** adult eICU stays, `patient.csv.gz` (`patientunitstayid`,`age`,`gender`,`hospitalid`,`unitadmitsource`,`unitdischargeoffset`), age ≥18, ICU LOS ≥1440 min, ≥1 MAP obs in (0,24]h.
- **Exposure:** `vitalPeriodic.csv.gz` (`observationoffset`,`systemicmean`; fallback (`systemicsystolic`+2·`systemicdiastolic`)/3). Burden = count of MAP<65 obs in (0,24]h (primary), and time-weighted burden sensitivity.
- **Outcome:** `lab.csv.gz` (`labresultoffset`,`labname`,`labresult`), `labname` containing 'creatinine' (excluding clearance/ratio); (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI = ≥1.5× lowest (0,24]h creatinine or ≥0.3 mg/dL rise; requires ≥2 draws.
- **H-observation channel:** count of non-renal `lab.csv.gz` draws in (0,24]h (`labname` ∉ {creatinine,bun,urea nitrogen,eGFR}).
- **H-treatment channel:** `infusionDrug.csv.gz` (`infusionoffset`,`drugname`,`patientweight`) vasopressor count/duration in (0,24]h over the seven bound labels; fluid = `intakeOutput.csv.gz` (`intakeoutputoffset`,`cellpath`,`cellvaluenumeric`) sustained fluid in (0,24]h (fluid `cellpath` filter still to be probe-verified — planned).
- **Severity adjustment:** `apachePatientResult.csv.gz` (`apachescore`,`predictedicumortality`); `hospital.csv.gz` (`hospitalid`) for hospital grouping/transport.
- **Estimand:** fitted AIPW risk difference (RD) for (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI per 30 min burden under three conditioning sets — monitoring-only / treatment-only / both — with hospital-clustered SE, plus an E-value.
- **Transport:** leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO) RD.

## 6. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative)
- **B1 baseline:** logistic regression AKI ~ burden + age + sex + baseline eGFR + Apache score (parent's base logistic, K1-style). Assumes linear dose, no channel conditioning.
- **A1 (discriminating alternative, fitted):** AIPW doubly-robust RD under the three conditioning sets. Reveals whether the RD is separable from the detection process and from treatment intensity — information B1 cannot provide because a single logistic coefficient conflates them.
- **A2 (deferred):** flexible/spline or boosting nuisance (dose-response curvature). Deferred because the executed negative control is near-null and monotone tertiles give no evidence of a threshold; revisit only if A1 shows residual curvature or a sign discordance vs B1.
- **Solver deliverable:** fit B1 and A1; output RD (per 30 min and per tertile) + 95% CI + E-value, the non-renal negative-control contrast, and LOHO transport. Budget: tabular fits on ~32k rows, CPU is sufficient (measured probe 140 s; full AIPW well within `solver_planning_limits`).

## 7. Falsification criteria (pre-specified)
- **Supportive (H-dose):** A1 RD per 30 min > 0 with 95% CI excluding 0 under **both**-conditioning; non-renal negative-control contrast ≥ 0; LOHO RD same sign in majority of hospitals.
- **Adverse (H-observation):** RD attenuates to ~null (CI includes 0) after monitoring-only conditioning, **or** the non-renal channel rises ≥ the creatinine channel (contrast ≤ 0) under the primary definition.
- **Adverse (H-treatment):** RD survives monitoring-only but attenuates to ~null after adding the treatment channel.
- **Inconclusive / park:** exposure too sparse for positivity, or RD CI wide, or contrast sign unstable across draw-window definitions (the observed fragility).
- **Cross-check flag:** the parent's 19.3% exposure vs this probe's 75.7% (different criterion) is **unreconciled** and recorded as a solver-visible cross-check, not hidden.

## 8. Honest limits
Observational: association, not mechanism; eICU-internal, no external validation; `drugname` not dose-normalised; creatinine outcome is detection-dependent and severity/hospital-charting confounding cannot be fully removed; fluid `cellpath` filter unverified. Conclusions bound to association/discrimination between the three rivals, not to an intervention threshold (K3) or causality (K2).
