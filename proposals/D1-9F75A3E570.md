# Reader-complete finite-corpus experiment for fixed-capacity documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Decision, unresolved question, and focused repair

This retrospective experiment asks whether meanings adjudicated from eventual stored preoperative CT/MRI report text improve fixed-capacity capture of encounter-documentary M2 among adults in a source-defined first eligible HCC-resection frame, beyond the same nonsemantic acquisition and documentation-surface information.

The downstream decision is only whether the evidence justifies acquiring report release/version/view logs and running a prospective silent-mode capacity study. This is not a resection, transplant, referral, surveillance, adjuvant-treatment, or deployment rule.

The assigned parent, [prior hypothesis], already solves the principal source-state and inference problems: first eligible source-documented resection; d=24-hour analytical cutoff and locked d in {12,48,72} sensitivities; 90-day imaging and 365-day prior-treatment lookbacks; 2015–2018 development, 2019 lock, separate 2020 and 2021 tests; whole-accession reports; encounter-documentary M2; B(Z), R(Z,S), G(Z,S,X), and same-fit Gmask; fixed K; complete outer source envelope; exact robust fitting and state-coupled extrema; finite-corpus rather than bootstrap inference; and strict nondeployment/noncausal limits.

One substantive defect remains. The parent freezes all raw source ambiguities but says that two readers annotate every report/pathology composite and a third “resolves” disagreement. It does not make the independent read, abstention, disagreement, and adjudication records part of the outer state universe. Exact optimization over one forced consensus can therefore be exact over an annotation-selected target: unfinished or unresolvable records may be omitted, and consensus can conceal clinically important interpretation variability. This matters at fixed K because removing even a few difficult records changes who is eligible, the annual denominator, K feasibility, rankings, and event gates.

The repair is an annotation-complete outer envelope. Every source-eligible unit/composite is assigned before outcomes are inspected; no patient or state may be removed for annotation absence, abstention, or disagreement. Independent labels are retained as finite alternatives in the primary state ledger. Consensus/adjudicated labels form a nested secondary envelope only. Required reads not completed by the freeze deadline make the primary computation inconclusive, never complete-case. The fixed-K definition, models, outcomes, exact finite-corpus boundary, and clinical limits are otherwise unchanged.

## 2. Evidence-supported claim versus hypothesis

### Strongest claim currently supported

The configured HCC snapshot has patient/encounter-linkable encounter, procedure, examination, pathology, medication, and non-drug-order rows. Procedures and examinations have start-like fields; examinations have accession identifiers, modality/machine fields, and eventual findings/diagnosis text; pathology has same-encounter narrative fields but no time, specimen, block, slide, or sampling identifiers. These data can instantiate a finite source-defined documentary experiment. They cannot establish procedure completion, report authorship/finalization/release/view before a decision, pathology-to-resection specimen linkage, seven-point sampling, biological M2, actual review capacity, clinician action, benefit, harm, fairness, or transportability.

The 2026 Feng–Qu–Han full-text meta-analysis included 52 imaging deep-learning studies and 19,531 patients, found lower SROC under independent external than internal validation (0.85 versus 0.90), and called for prospective multicenter evaluation (DOI 10.2196/82000; PMCID PMC12954728; inspected frozen full-text XML source [source checksum]).

Annotation variability is not hypothetical. Bakr et al. reported pairwise Cohen kappa 0.02–0.60 and three-reader Fleiss kappa −0.17–0.56 for CT semantic features in resected HCC (DOI 10.1148/rycan.2020190062; PMID 32550600; inspected Europe PMC metadata/abstract source [source checksum]; full article not claimed read). Min et al. reported fair-to-moderate MRI agreement for individual/combined MVI features (Fleiss kappa 0.38–0.47) and MVI probability (kappa 0.41; DOI 10.1148/radiol.2020201940; PMID 32990512; abstract/metadata inspected, full text not claimed read). Cong et al.'s pathology guideline describes standardized gross sampling, including a seven-point baseline protocol and sampling of adjacent and distant peritumoral tissue (DOI 10.3748/wjg.v22.i42.9279; PMCID PMC5107692; inspected frozen full-text XML source [source checksum]). The HCC source cannot verify such sampling.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

Among adults in the finite outer-ledger first eligible source-documented HCC resection target, with same-encounter documentary HCC pathology and no recorded qualifying HCC treatment in the prior year, one locked model using eventual stored CT/MRI report meanings will capture more encounter-documentary M2 cases in exactly the same fixed top-K positions than both:

1. an independently optimized nested comparator with identical acquisition and documentation-surface information; and
2. the same fitted full model after complete semantic masking,

by more than 5 captures per 100 positions in every admissible source-and-reader state world, separately in 2020 and separately in 2021.

The margin is a ranking-composition threshold, not sensitivity, PPV, utility, benefit, or an acceptable service workload.

### Substantive advance

A supportive result would show that the result is not an artifact of a favorable consensus reader, complete-case annotation, or one source reconstruction. A reader-heterogeneous result would be clinically useful even if adverse to the hypothesis: it would show that semantic capture is not reproducible enough to justify moving directly to prospective silent mode without a more reliable rubric or structured reporting.

## 3. Population and clocks

For each cutoff offset d, include one patient only in a state satisfying all of:

- age at least 18 in the linked encounter;
- the patient's earliest eligible source-documented episode under a frozen hepatobiliary resection dictionary;
- a same-encounter pathology composite with at least one reader-supported HCC interpretation;
- no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in [c_d−365 days,c_d), searched patient-wide in dated procedure, medication, and non-drug-order rows.

The primary offset is d=24 hours; c_d=t_op−d. The imaging window is [c_d−90 days,c_d). Sensitivities use d in {12,48,72}. “No recorded prior treatment” is not biological treatment-naivety.

Before grade or report semantics are viewed, two hepatobiliary surgeons independently classify every distinct procedure/order/medication label touched by high-sensitivity lexical retrieval. A third adjudicates, but the primary outer dictionary retains any label either independent reader marks possibly relevant. Removal from the outer set requires unanimous exclusion with a positive source-visible reason. Raw label, normalized label, source path/column, count, decision, reader, version, and hash are stored.

Exact-deduplicate procedure rows across all six fields while retaining source ordinal. Construct same-patient, same-encounter, same-calendar-day provisional episodes. Adjacent-day, conflicting-source, invalid-duration, and multiple-date alternatives remain branches unless a documented source-system operation identifier or unanimous clinical impossibility rule resolves them.

A validated non-midnight anesthesia-system start may be a point clock. Date-only or unvalidated midnight is [date,date+24 hours). Partition each clock interval at every examination-window, prior-treatment-window, and calendar-role boundary. Evaluate one representative plus open/closed limits per cell. Each reconstruction orders all admissible episodes across all years before choosing the earliest eligible episode.

## 4. Chronology-safe frames

For each patient i let S_i+ be the complete outer source-and-reader state set. Define U_T as patients having any eligible 2020 or 2021 state. Every U_T patient is quarantined from development/tuning even if another state places that patient earlier or outside analysis. Among the remainder, U_V contains patients with any eligible 2019 state; among the remainder, U_D contains patients with any eligible 2015–2018 state.

Development is 2015–2018, tuning/preprocessing lock is 2019, and the test estimands are separate for 2020 and 2021. A state chooses only one annual role per patient. Data from 2022 onward are audits only and cannot change dictionaries, rubrics, features, K, models, or conclusions.

Report U_D, U_V, U_T; certainly/possibly eligible counts and sharp annual denominator ranges; and the number whose encounter, year, prior treatment, report unit, HCC-frame status, M2 outcome, or reader-derived X changes.

## 5. Whole examinations and documentary outcome

A nonblank examination unit is (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号), retaining every exact-deduplicated component and immutable row/field boundaries. Blank 检查号 is identity-unknown and cannot provide primary semantics. Conflicting modality, components more than 24 hours apart, and possible merged identities create alternatives.

A unit is eligible only if all retained component acquisition intervals are wholly compatible with the 90-day pre-cutoff window. Select at most the latest eligible whole CT and latest eligible whole MRI by latest admissible start, raw ordinal, then frozen patient hash. Concatenate complete-unit 检查所见 and 检查诊断 in raw component order. Phrase-level selection is forbidden.

For each possible index encounter, exact-deduplicate pathology rows across their six fields and concatenate 病理, 检查所见, and 检查诊断 in raw order. Pathologists classify:

- Y={1}: explicit M2 anchored to MVI/HCC grade in the encounter composite;
- Y={0}: anchored M0/M1 excludes M2 in the same relevant context;
- Y={0,1}: missing, unanchored, contradictory, attribution-uncertain, or text-inadequate grade.

The outcome is encounter-documentary M2, not biological M2. Unknown remains set-valued and is never imputed negative.

## 6. Annotation-complete outer envelope

### 6.1 Preannotation roster and immutable forms

Before any pathology grade, report semantics, model score, event count, or year-specific result is inspected, freeze a roster containing every outer-query procedure label, candidate report unit, and pathology composite reachable from any structural state. Each roster item receives two independent, blinded reader assignments and a third-reader adjudication form. Forms store item hash, rubric/version hash, reader role and qualification, assignment/start/submit times in the study system, every item response, abstention reason, and immutable revision history. These are study-generated metadata, not claims about source report timing.

Radiologists see only the eventual stored examination text and annotate modality plus the parent's concepts: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites. Pathologists see only the composed same-encounter pathology text and separately annotate HCC-frame support and documentary M2. Readers remain blinded to the other modality, model scores, calendar split, top-K membership, and results.

### 6.2 No annotation-complete subset

Every expected form must end as submitted or explicit abstention. “Not assigned,” “in progress,” lost form, failed import, or absent provenance at freeze is a mandatory global inconclusive gate. It cannot remove an item or patient, become a negative label, or shrink the denominator. Source-text-unavailable is reserved for absent source text; it cannot encode a missing human read.

For each report unit or pathology composite, the primary annotation library is a finite set of whole-form vectors. Each complete independent submitted form is one indivisible vector. An adjudicator may add only an explicitly signed joint completion whose full combination of fields is marked text-defensible. Conceptwise Cartesian mixing is forbidden: capsule from one reader cannot be combined with margin from another, and HCC-frame status cannot be detached from the same pathology form's M2 judgment. The same chosen whole-form vector follows a source item everywhere it appears, preventing patient/world-specific relabeling.

If an independent reader explicitly abstains on one field because the text is semantically indeterminate, that submitted vector carries the rubric's uncertain level for that field. If an entire form is an explicit semantic abstention, the adjudicator must enumerate a finite set of jointly defensible full vectors; inability to bound that set is a mandatory inconclusive gate. Numeric values remain the finite submitted or adjudicator-certified values plus an explicit text-indeterminate value; no post hoc averaging creates a new value.

A pathology form jointly records HCC-frame status and documentary M2. Reader disagreement therefore creates only the observed or adjudicator-certified joint pairs, including eligible/noneligible/nonmembership and Y={0}, Y={1}, or Y={0,1} as appropriate. No uncertain outcome is dropped and no HCC-positive judgment is borrowed from a different form's M2 label.

Define S_i^ann+ by crossing these annotation domains only along relationally reachable source states. Define S_i^cons as the nested adjudicated-consensus envelope. All primary fitting, K feasibility, event gates, and test extrema use S^ann+. Consensus results are secondary. If consensus supports the margin but S^ann+ does not, label the result reader-sensitive; it is not universal-supportive.

### 6.3 Coverage and denominator gates

For each year a, compute the sharp anchored-grade fraction

A_a^- = min_q [number of eligible members with anchored {0} or {1} grade / N_a(q)]

over the same world and denominator. Require A_2020^- and A_2021^- to be at least 0.85 and the lower-envelope number of Y=1 events to be at least 50 in each year. A ratio from a consensus-only or complete-case subset is forbidden. Equality to 0.85 passes; fewer than 50 lower-envelope events fails. Failure is inconclusive, not adverse.

Publish form completion, abstention, independent-reader disagreement, adjudicator expansion, and label-domain-width counts by modality, concept, and calendar role without revealing text.

### 6.4 Source-and-reader reachability certificate

The outer ledger is frozen before fitting and stores, for every consumed source row and annotation form, source snapshot/hash, table, raw ordinal or byte offset, exact keys, consumed values, parser success/failure, reader-form hash, and every rule using the item. State generation is relational: one episode/clock determines age, encounter, prior treatment, eligible examinations, report labels, HCC-frame status, outcome domain, and calendar role together. Labels or outcomes from another episode or report cannot be borrowed.

Generate states through a finite directed acyclic graph. Every source or reader ambiguity branch must terminate as (i) an emitted raw S^ann+ state, (ii) a machine-checkable impossibility under the frozen outer rule, or (iii) an adjudicated-only exclusion that remains in S^ann+ but is absent from S^cons. Nonmembership is a state, not Y=0. Any unassigned atom, unreachable branch, unexplained count difference, or absent reverse provenance is a mandatory inconclusive gate.

Raw states may be quotiented only when membership, year, outcome domain, all model-specific features, scores/tie precedence, denominator contribution, and annotation backpointers are exactly analysis-equivalent. Every quotient retains all raw backpointers and expands to at least one raw terminal state. No state cap, beam search, random/importance sampling, favorable-state search, rounded merging, approximate column generation, or time-limit incumbent may support the primary result. Exact lazy generation is allowed only with a proof that no omitted state can violate the fit or extremum and with independent replay.

## 7. Information sets and models

Z contains age, sex, CT/MRI/other unit indicators, eligible-unit count, acquisition-to-cutoff recency, component count, accession/identity ambiguity, source-text-unavailable indicators, and development-frequency-grouped 机器型号. Identifiers never enter.

S contains documentation surface only, separately by modality: untrimmed Unicode code-point count; nonblank 检查所见/检查诊断 indicators; digit, punctuation, whitespace, Han, and other-character proportions; component count; blank/conflicting 检查号; and field-separator count. It contains no token, n-gram, clinical concept, pathology, diagnosis, lab, or document content.

X contains only the reader-state semantic labels above, with CT and MRI blocks separate. Reader identity, completion time, disagreement, adjudication path, calendar year, and form metadata never enter any model.

Use additive elastic-net logistic regression with unpenalized intercept, frozen one-hot levels, development centering/scaling, zero-variance removal, alpha in {0,.25,.5,.75,1}, and 100 log-spaced lambdas from certified lambda_max to 10^-4 lambda_max. No interactions, splines, unrestricted text, or test-derived features.

Fit one robust model per information set:

- B(Z), acquisition-only diagnostic baseline;
- R(Z,S), independently optimized reduced comparator;
- G(Z,S,X), full model with byte-identical Z,S preprocessing/order to R;
- Gmask, no refit or recalibration: apply fitted G after replacing every X level with the training-defined source-text-unavailable level while Z,S remain unchanged.

For each model/hyperparameter, minimize the worst normalized development logistic loss over one state per U_D patient and every outcome completion, plus elastic-net penalty. Solve the finite linear-fractional separation exactly by Dinkelbach iterations and the convex outer minimax fit by certified cutting planes. Require residual at most 1e-10, primal-dual and omitted-world gaps at most 1e-8, stable canonical separation, stored cuts/KKT certificates, and exact replay. R and G tune independently in 2019 by worst-state fixed-K capture; 2020/2021 are untouched.

## 8. Fixed capacity and exact estimands

Before test outcomes or report semantics are consulted, calculate sharp minimum eligible counts N_a(q,d) for a=2015,…,2019 and d in {12,24,48,72} over S^ann+, including HCC-frame reader alternatives. Freeze

N_ref = min_{a,d,q} N_a(q,d), and K_r = floor(r N_ref), r in {.05,.10,.20}.

The primary fixed capacity is K=K_.10. Require N_ref≥500, K≥50, and at least K eligible patients in every admissible 2020 and 2021 world. The .05/.20 capacities are sensitivity analyses only when K_r≥50. This is a mathematical annual batch capacity, not evidence that an HCC service can or should review K patients. Capacity acceptability and arrival-time implementability require prospective workflow data.

In each world, rank eligible whole patients by locked score, frozen SHA-256 patient tie key, and raw episode backpointer; select exactly K. For model M:

T_M(a,q) = (100/K) sum_{i in TopK_M(a,q)} Y_i(s_i).

Co-primary contrasts are Delta_nested=T_G−T_R and Delta_mask=T_G−T_Gmask. Delta_surface=T_R−T_B is diagnostic.

For each year a in {2020,2021} and contrast c in {nested,mask}, compute sharp attained finite-corpus endpoints

I_ca = [delta^-_ca, delta^+_ca] = [min_q Delta_c(a,q), max_q Delta_c(a,q)]

over the same coupled source, reader, membership, feature, outcome, and tie state for both models. Solve by exact state-expanded mixed-integer optimization; require zero unresolved integer gap, reported gap at most 1e-8, witness worlds, top-K hashes, arithmetic traces, state-order invariance, exhaustive equality for fixtures through 12 patients and real-data shards through 20.

## 9. Falsification and conclusions

After all gates pass, for each coordinate:

- supportive if delta^->5;
- reader/source heterogeneous if delta^-≤5<delta^+;
- all-world adverse if delta^+≤5.

Equality to 5 is adverse to the strict hypothesis. Global labels are:

1. universal-supportive only if all four coordinates are supportive;
2. decisive heterogeneity if any coordinate/world exceeds 5 and any coordinate/world fails;
3. universal-adverse-without-positive-coordinate if no coordinate's upper endpoint exceeds 5;
4. inconclusive if any mandatory source, annotation-form, event, state-completeness, fit, replay, or solver gate fails.

A supportive result permits only: in this frozen eventual-report HCC corpus, stored report meanings improved fixed-K documentary-M2 capture beyond the identical-surface comparator and were necessary to the same fitted full score, by more than the margin in every declared source-and-reader world, separately in 2020 and 2021.

An adverse result falsifies the universal margin claim; it does not prove semantics useless or biological M2 absent. Heterogeneity shows dependence on defensible source or reader interpretation and argues against skipping rubric refinement. Inconclusive means the required experiment was not fully identified/computed; it is not negative evidence.

No confidence interval, statistical significance, sampling-population, causal, deployment, or transport claim is permitted. The endpoints are attained properties of this frozen finite corpus.

## 12. Stronger conclusions requiring new evidence

Biological M2 requires specimen/accession/slide/block linkage, pathology time, and sampling adjudication against a recognized protocol. Real-time use requires report author/final/release/version/view logs linked to the selected accession and validated operation clock. Capacity acceptability requires an observed candidate stream, staffing, review duration, queueing, competing demands, and stakeholder judgment. Clinical action and benefit require a separate prospective controlled outcomes study with recurrence, survival, treatment, harm, cost, and equity outcomes. External validity requires a new site and locked evaluation.
