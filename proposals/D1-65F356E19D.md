# Six-hour landmark persistence of the documented hypotension–creatinine signal

## Scientific deliverable and substantive change

The future solver must newly construct an auditable six-hour eICU landmark cohort and 30-minute interval ledger, fit the parent’s matched hospital-aware competing-state models, and add a prespecified persistence outcome. Required outputs are: (1) the parent any-observed-rise burden gradient by documented pressor-record timing; (2) a new gradient for a rise confirmed by a distinct later creatinine result; (3) paired test-opportunity, death, alive-exit and confirmation-loss processes; and (4) hospital-held-out transport, calibration, uncertainty and falsification sensitivities. Completion requires computed ledgers, fits, standardized 48-hour contrasts, uncertainty and conclusions linked to outputs. No model has been fitted and no clinical result is claimed in discovery.

## Evidence-supported and unresolved claims

The strongest claim supported before fitting is data availability only. The frozen eICU 2.0 snapshot contains ICU-relative MAP observations, ICU-keyed laboratory rows with result and revision offsets, named infusion rows with offsets, ICU disposition, admission descriptors and hospital identifiers joinable on the keys below. Direct headers were checked on 2026-09-14. This does not establish infusion administration, causation, renal injury or transport.

The residual clinically important uncertainty is whether the parent’s “observed creatinine rise” is a persistent recorded signal or a transient value, revision artifact or single measurement selected by local testing practice. A distinct later result can test persistence without pretending to adjudicate biological kidney injury.

Hypothesis: among adult ICU stays with a qualifying creatinine before the first raw MAP below 65 mmHg and still observable beyond six hours, the 0–6-hour low-MAP burden association differs by documented pressor-record timing, and the prompt-versus-delayed/no contrast for a confirmed recorded rise has the same direction as the any-rise contrast and is not reduced by more than 50% after test opportunity and competing exits are represented.

A confirmed rise is the first post-landmark creatinine at least 0.3 mg/dL above baseline followed by a second qualifying creatinine with a distinct `labid`, at least 360 minutes later and no later than minute 2,880. A same-minute result, exact duplicate or revised version of the same `labid` cannot confirm. This is a persistence endpoint, not AKI or a treatment effect.

Support requires concordant direction for any-rise and confirmed-rise contrasts in development and the ordered alternative, a held-out patient-standardized confirmed contrast of at least 2 percentage points with a 95% hospital-cluster interval excluding zero, the same-sign equal-hospital estimate, and confirmation loss no greater than 50%. A reproducible null/reversal or loss >50% while any-rise remains positive supports a transient/documentation explanation. Sparse confirmation, inadequate overlap, pooled/equal-hospital disagreement, unstable fits or intervals including zero are inconclusive.

Exits at or before 360 are reported exclusions, not post-landmark no events.

Baseline is the earliest qualifying creatinine in [0,60] by smallest `labresultoffset), not the minimum value. Deduplicate exact `labid` rows; multiple valid rows at the selected offset use the median and report count. Exclude urinary creatinine, invalid numeric values and unknown units without conversion. A broader no-pre-low-baseline cohort is a sensitivity; no-low-MAP stays are descriptive only.

The 0–6-hour ledger uses raw periodic observations, duplicate stay/time median, and primary trapezoidal area-under-65 burden with gaps <=30 minutes bridged. Retain raw/bridged minutes, gaps, coverage, minimum MAP, runs, episodes, slope and recovery. First-low and pressor timing always use raw observations. Non-invasive MAP is a separate sensitivity, never pooled.

## Documented timing and outcomes

The first documented target record is the smallest `infusionDrug.infusionoffset` in [0,360] whose case-insensitive `drugname` matches norepinephrine, vasopressin, phenylephrine, dopamine or epinephrine. Categories: pre-low/concurrent (<= first low), prompt-after-low (> low and <=30 minutes), delayed-after-low (>30 through 360), and no record by 360. Same-minute MAP/infusion is pre-low. Rates, amounts, fluid and weight are audit fields only, with a positive-rate sensitivity; none is dose or administration evidence.

Let `end=min(2880, unitdischargeoffset)`; follow-up starts strictly after 360. A post-landmark test has `labresultoffset >360`, is at/before `end`, and precedes exit. Exactly 360 is baseline data; same-minute result and exit is exit. Any-rise is the first result >= baseline+0.3 mg/dL. Confirmation requires a distinct later `labid`, >=360 minutes after first rise, also >=baseline+0.3, by 2,880. Primary clock is `labresultoffset`; rerun with `labresultrevisedoffset` where present as sensitivity.

States are no qualifying test, qualifying test without rise, first rise, confirmed rise, unit death, alive discharge/transfer, and no event at boundary. Death is `unitdischargestatus="Expired"` or `unitdischargelocation="Death"`; other disposition is alive exit. A first rise that never confirms is a non-confirmed state. Report test-opportunity CIF, rise-given-test, confirmation-given-first-rise, death and alive-exit. No component is mediation and no state is latent physiology.

## Estimands, split and matched alternatives

For endpoint e in {any, confirmed}, let `G_h,c,e` be the standardized 48-hour CIF difference between burden P75 and P25 within hospital h and timing c, standardizing baseline creatinine, minimum MAP, coverage/trajectory, covariates and admission-risk descriptors. `D_h,e = G_h,prompt,e - G_h,delayed/no,e`; report delayed and no-record separately when supported. The persistence estimand is `D_confirmed`; report the paired difference and confirmation-loss ratio only when stable.

Assign hospitals 80/20 by deterministic `hospitalid` hash before fitting. If `uniquepid` spans hospitals, use connected hospital components and report coverage loss; no patient, `uniquepid` or hospital crosses. Held-out hospitals are never refit, tuned or recalibrated. Transport is inconclusive with <10 held-out hospitals, <5 supported hospitals, <50 landmark stays in a supported hospital, missing timing/overlap or inadequate confirmation.

Transparent baseline: regularized additive cause-specific pooled-logistic B0–B6 on the identical ledger for both endpoints. B0 includes minimum MAP, non-burden trajectory, coverage, baseline creatinine, demographics, admission/unit source/type, interval, hospital context and admission-risk descriptors; B1 adds burden; B2 timing and agent-family indicators; B3 burden-by-timing interaction; B4 prior-history testing/exit features; B5 removes the interaction as matched null; B6 adds hospital random interaction slopes/intercepts. Fit death/alive-exit and first-test processes. The scientific comparison is paired `D_any`/`D_confirmed`, not AUROC.

Substantive alternative: an ordered piecewise-exponential multi-state model on the identical cohort, inputs, split, standardization and uncertainty, with separate test, first-rise, confirmation, death and alive-exit intensities and hospital effects where estimable. Freeze pre-landmark low-MAP/pressor/recovery history at 360 and left-truncate follow-up there. It reveals whether timing is carried by testing, a first rise that fails to persist, confirmation timing or competing exit—information the additive baseline loses. Agreement in any-rise but not confirmed-rise is scientifically informative.

A learned sequence model is deferred unless both models leave reproducible shape-specific held-out miscalibration after process accounting and it evaluates the same paired estimands, calibration and uncertainty. A causal pressor branch requires validated administration/start, dose, indication, intent, richer confounding control and adjudicated renal outcomes.

## Uncertainty, falsification and evidence limits

Use 500 hospital-cluster bootstrap replicates plus `uniquepid`-clustered sensitivity. Report intervals for paired CIFs, `D_any`, `D_confirmed`, their difference, loss ratio when defined, hospital variance and prediction interval. Estimate test probabilities from prior-history covariates in grouped development folds, truncate inverse-opportunity weights at the development first percentile and compare weighted/unweighted results.

Emit hashes/headers, label/unit and agent maps, disposition map, all exclusions, cohort flow, ledgers, fits, transitions, convergence, effective samples, timing-stratified CIFs, process contrasts, held-out calibration/Brier/AUROC/AUPRC and every sensitivity. Sensitivities include no interpolation, non-invasive MAP, revised lab time, 3/12-hour confirmation gap, +0.3 versus 1.5-times baseline where calculable, 30/60-minute pressor cut, positive-rate record, agent map, connected-component split, Apache severity, 48-hour/unit boundary and broader baseline.

Support is only a reproducible association among six-hour histories and recorded persistence. Adverse is null/reversal, disappearance on confirmation, loss after test/exit adjustment or failed transport. A stable any-rise but unstable confirmation result indicates endpoint sensitivity; a stable confirmed result without timing modification is a useful negative result. Inconclusive includes sparse confirmation, unsupported ratios, overlap failure, too few hospitals, boundary effects, nonconvergence, extreme weights, pooled/equal-hospital disagreement or intervals including zero. None supports pressor equivalence/harm, causation or a biological renal null.

Computational claims include joins, offsets, filters, deduplication, confirmation logic, splits, fits, standardization, intervals, calibration, transport and sensitivity reproducibility. Clinical adjudication/another study is required for administration/start, dose, intent, complete capture, MAP target, renal reserve, urine output, KDIGO AKI, hemoconcentration and true kidney injury.
