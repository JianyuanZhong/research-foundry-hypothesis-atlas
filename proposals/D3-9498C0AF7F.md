# Provisional hypothesis: donor-aware macrophage-to-FLS communication in E-MTAB-8873

## Proposed relationship
In the available E-MTAB-8873 synovial coculture experiment, exposure of synovial fibroblast-like cells (FLS) to macrophages may induce a reproducible, condition-specific transcriptional response that is distinguishable from FLS-only and macrophage-negative controls after accounting for donor/context and repeated measurements. This is a hypothesis, not a computed result.

## Unresolved claim and falsifiable test
The unresolved claim is that macrophage exposure produces a reproducible FLS transcriptional signature beyond FLS-only, macrophage-negative, donor, and technical-context effects. The smallest feasible test is a donor-aware comparison of macrophage-positive versus FLS-only and macrophage-positive versus macrophage-negative conditions, with repeated/context structure preserved. A supportive result requires a reproducible direction and uncertainty estimate across eligible donors/contexts and a pattern not explained by the negative controls. An adverse result is a null or opposite pattern that persists after dependence-aware analysis. An inconclusive result includes insufficient donor replication, unstable contrasts, missing metadata, or uncertainty spanning clinically meaningful effects.

## Method alternatives
A simple baseline is a donor-aware summary contrast (for example, condition means or a mixed-effects model) with permutation uncertainty at the donor/context level. A substantive alternative is a hierarchical or regularized multivariate model that estimates feature-specific effects while borrowing information across the targeted panel and testing whether the response is coherent rather than isolated. The alternative could reveal structured pathway-level signal and distinguish broad state changes from sparse feature noise; it loses interpretability and requires more careful dependence handling. This draft does not claim either method has been fitted.
