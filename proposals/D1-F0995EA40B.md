Frozen falsification gates

Supportive for denominator transportability: at least 10 hospitals each have >=10 denominator rows and >=5 target rows at every represented landmark; >=50% of rows lie in those cells; target-receipt hospital eta-squared <=0.10; no landmark standardized coverage differs from the overall standardized value by >10 percentage points; leave-one-hospital-out direction relative to crude coverage never reverses; and inherited conditional-label/proxy gates cease to be adverse without relying on peak pressure.

Adverse: fewer than five hospitals meet common support or <50% of rows remain; target-receipt eta-squared >=0.25; standardized landmark range exceeds 20 points with direction reversal; leave-one-hospital-out conclusions reverse; conditional evaluation/no-trial dependence remains eta-squared >=0.25; or inherited peak-pressure dominance, hospital/landmark reversal, and endpoint-ascertainment failures persist.

Inconclusive: execution, identity auditing, or source coverage fails; five to nine common-support hospitals remain; estimates fall between thresholds; target conflicts or incomplete windows materially alter classification; or intervals/common-support loss permit both supportive and adverse interpretations. Exact equality to the inherited 540 is not required because the approved estimand changes first-stay selection, but every difference must be enumerated.
