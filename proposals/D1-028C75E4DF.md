# Exposure-observation-robust first-day monitored invasive-MAP burden and subsequent creatinine-defined AKI

## Status, revision, and actual deliverable

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It resolves the parent's remaining primary-estimand vulnerability with a frozen, executed, outcome-blind measurement experiment. No post-hour-24 creatinine value was read or constructed, no AKI label was made, and no MAP–AKI association was opened.

The fixed scientific question is retained: among one outcome-blind index eICU encounter per person, alive and in ICU at hour 24, does duration-normalized monitored invasive-MAP<65 burden during `[0,1440)` add held-hospital information beyond nadir for pair-timed creatinine-defined AKI strictly after hour 24? The claim remains descriptive/prognostic and noncausal.

The future solver must newly reproduce the frozen index and exposure-observation audit; construct the pair-timed endpoint; fit O0/O1, B0/B1/B2, and ordered observed-time T1; evaluate held hospitals and uncertainty; and link each conclusion to computed outputs. The executed diagnostic establishes only that the primary monitored-time summary is not materially distorted under the specified empirically patterned pseudo-missingness stress test. It does not solve the MAP–AKI question and does not validate pressure during actual undocumented arterial-line absence.

## Scientific opening, supported claim, and unresolved claim

The strongest evidence-supported claim is limited. ICU studies use heterogeneous hypotension definitions; most report hypotension–AKI associations, but pooled AKI evidence was not confirmatory [K1]. FINNAKI found lower first-day time-adjusted MAP associated with AKI progression in 423 severe-sepsis patients whose dense hemodynamics were prospectively validated [K2]. KDIGO defines AKI from timed creatinine changes or oliguria and cautions that incomplete, creatinine-only implementation misses or misclassifies cases [K3].

The unresolved primary hypothesis is:

> In the frozen later-tested index population, greater 24-hour-equivalent monitored invasive-MAP<65 burden is associated with higher held-hospital risk of pair-timed subsequent creatinine-defined AKI beyond a flexible MAP-nadir model, and this contrast is not explained by measured exposure-observation geometry, pressor-entry timing, outcome-testing opportunity, or hospital convention.

The leading scientific explanation is that sustained low pressure contains prognostic information lost by a single nadir. The strongest rival is a coupled measurement/care process: severity and treatment influence arterial-line retention, sampling density, gap boundaries, pressor charting, creatinine testing, and discharge, making apparent duration a surveillance signature. They differ observably. A burden signal should persist under outcome-blind exposure-observation stress tests, observation covariates, fixed-window testing, within-hospital analysis, and hospital holdout. A care-process signal should be materially changed by low-state/pressor-dependent masking or attenuate under observation and site controls. Neither adjusted persistence nor prediction identifies renal hypoperfusion as a mechanism.

Clinical importance is unchanged: if duration adds stable information beyond nadir, prospective studies and risk summaries should preserve cumulative hypotension rather than a single minimum. A precise adverse result would argue against promoting this noisier summary in this population. Neither result selects a MAP treatment target or proves benefit from raising pressure.

## Executed outcome-blind exposure-observation experiment

The protocol was frozen in `pre-execution-protocol.md` before the diagnostic result was inspected. A corrected final run used training-hospital gap templates only and included the prespecified low-state×pressor term. Job `[research job]` used 12 CPUs, 64 GiB, no GPU, and 106.75 seconds. The first run is superseded because its gap-template pool inadvertently included held hospitals and omitted the interaction; no threshold, population, or scientific gate changed.

The diagnostic reproduced 10,998 eligible people across 165 hospitals: 5,428/3,057/2,513 in train/validation/test. It identified 3,363 dense-proxy sequences, including 792 people in 29 test hospitals, and learned 5,738 natural internal-gap templates exclusively from training hospitals. A dense proxy required at least 276/288 bins, first/last support by minutes 30/1,405, and no natural gap over ten minutes; only its at-most-two-bin natural gaps were linearly filled. Five deterministic masks per person sampled training coverage and internal gap start/length conditional on previous low state, six-hour block, and pressor entry within ±30 minutes.

The transparent estimator D0 was the parent exposure, `24*n_low/n_observed`. D1 was a two-state first-order Markov bridge trained without outcomes. On identical binary MAP values, masks, clock blocks, and pressor entries, it conditioned missing-block low-state probabilities on observed endpoints, ordered state persistence, clock block, and pressor entry.

On held test hospitals:

- D0 signed bias was +0.159 h (95% hospital-cluster bootstrap interval 0.123–0.194), MAE 0.232 h, RMSE 0.478 h, p90 absolute error 0.703 h, Spearman 0.997, and training-p25/p75 exposure-class discordance 4.55%.
- D1 signed bias was −0.014 h, MAE 0.182 h, p90 absolute error 0.473 h, and Spearman 0.990. It reduced MAE by 21.5% (D0−D1 MAE interval 0.016–0.085 h), but classification discordance worsened to 9.67%, above the frozen 7.5% repair threshold. Its MAE remained under 0.24 h in every held hospital-size stratum.
- The small positive D0 bias is statistically detectable but does not cross any predeclared material gate: absolute bias is below 0.50 h, MAE below 1 h, p90 below 2 h, and class discordance below 10%.
- In the sparsest 202–229-bin mask stratum, D0 bias/MAE/p90 error/class discordance were 0.392/0.570/1.543 h/5.96%; all remain inside the frozen material gates, while error decreased monotonically with denser observation.

Discrete-time gap models also used held hospitals. For gap starts, adding prior low state, prior MAP, recent slope, and low×pressor improved log loss by 0.000805 (95% hospital-bootstrap 0.000471–0.001151), but standardized absolute changes were only +0.092 percentage points for low versus MAP 65–75 and −0.115 points for pressor entry against a 0.568% per-bin gap-start rate. For re-entry, O1 log-loss gain was not robust (interval −0.000080–0.000608); standardized changes were +0.572 points for prior low state and +1.111 points for pressor entry against a 4.70% per-missing-bin re-entry rate. Thus state/pressor dependence is detectable in parts of the observation process but remains below the frozen 5-percentage-point materiality gate.

Decision: **retain D0 monitored-time burden as primary**. D1 is a required ordered measurement sensitivity, not the replacement estimand, because its lower continuous error came with materially worse exposure-class preservation and it cannot validate truly absent measurements. The result supports robustness to the tested internal-gap process; it does not show observation is ignorable, resolve day-boundary absence, or identify actual pressure duration.

Inherited measurement evidence remains consequential. Logical full-clock burden intervals had median/p75/p90 widths 1.50/3.67/5.50 hours, 46.9% lacked one or both day boundaries, and post-hour-24 testing opportunity fell 11.3 points across logical-width quartiles. Therefore monitored time must never be called full-clock duration, logical width remains mandatory in the outcome-observation gate, and transport beyond later-tested patients remains provisional.

## Pair-timed strictly post-landmark creatinine outcome

Use rows with `lower(trim(labname))='creatinine'`, mg/dL system/interface units, and value 0.1–20 mg/dL; median-collapse same stay/offset values and report unit conflicts. Admission creatinine is the earliest valid value in `[-360,360]`. Require a value in `(-360,1440]`. ## Matched baseline, substantive alternative, and outcome-observation analysis

All models use the same indexed population, exposure/outcome clocks, raw sources, frozen hospital split, train-only processing, observation weights, and hospital-cluster uncertainty.

**B0 transparent baseline:** penalized logistic risk model with a training-set restricted cubic spline of nadir; age, sex, ethnicity, admission source, unit type, weight; admission/last pre-landmark creatinine, change, slope, count and timing; coverage, boundary/internal gaps, logical width, gap-after-low and pressor-adjacent gap features, six-hour sampling density, artifacts and missing indicators; and non-BP/non-creatinine `apacheApsVar` physiology.

**B1 monitored burden:** B0 plus a restricted cubic spline of M and frozen M×logical-width, M×nadir, and M×gap-after-low interactions. No feature search. The association estimand is held-test standardized risk difference and ratio setting M to training p75 versus p25 within 5-mmHg nadir strata and empirical common support while fixing measured covariates/observation features. The predictive estimand is paired held-test `ΔLL=logloss(B0)-logloss(B1)`.

**B2 treatment/measurement rival:** B1 plus day-1 pressor agent presence, entry counts, first/last entry, hourly masks, and gap-boundary adjacency. Attenuation is descriptive, not mediation, because pressors may be response, confounder, mediator, or collider.

**T1 substantive ordered alternative:** penalized functional logistic model using the identical population, target, split, B0 static inputs, and raw MAP/mask/pressor channels, plus 24 hourly low fractions, nadirs, masks/time-since-observation, gap starts/re-entry, pressor entries, longest run, episode count, and low→pressor and pressor→recovery transitions. A six-basis cubic functional coefficient with validation-selected second-difference penalty preserves order. Compare T1 with B1 by paired held-hospital log loss, calibration, and frozen ablations: permute hour order while preserving M/nadir; remove mask/time-since/gap channels; remove pressor channels. B1 loses order, fragmentation, recovery, and care-response co-occurrence; T1 can reveal whether those patterns transport, but cannot identify renal autoregulation or pressor effects.

**Outcome observation O0/O1:** in all 10,998 opportunity-cohort people, O0 predicts a test in `(1440,4320]` from landmark-available nadir, renal trajectory, non-BP severity, exposure-observation geometry, and training-hospital effects. O1 adds M and interactions. Fit out of fold and report calibration, Brier/log loss, overlap, testing/discharge/death by burden, nadir, logical width, D0 error-risk features, and hospital. Never use actual future test count, discharge, or death as baseline predictors. Required analyses include stabilized inverse-observation weighting truncated at 1st/99th percentiles, fixed 48-hour tested subset, ≥2 later tests, exclusion of first-hour post-landmark tests, pattern-mixture odds 0.5–2, and competing detected-AKI/live-discharge/death through day 7.

## Frozen gates and interpretation

### Exposure-observation gate

The executed D0 retain gate remains binding. A future exact rerun is materially adverse if held-test absolute bias is ≥0.50 h with interval excluding zero, MAE >1 h, p90 error >2 h, or p25/p75 class discordance >10%. D1 may replace D0 only if D0 is adverse and D1 reduces MAE ≥20%, has MAE ≤0.75 h, p90 ≤1.5 h, discordance ≤7.5%, and every hospital-size-stratum MAE ≤1 h. If D0 is adverse and D1 misses any criterion, do not open the association in the ≥202-bin cohort. A ≥90%-coverage, boundary-supported population may proceed only if D0 separately passes every retain gate there. Fewer than 500 dense proxies, fewer than 20 held-test hospitals, boundary-straddling uncertainty, or calibration failure is inconclusive and also stops outcome opening.

Detectable state dependence alone is not material distortion. A material state-observation result requires O1 held-test log-loss gain with interval excluding zero plus an adjusted low-state or pressor-entry risk difference ≥5 percentage points with interval excluding zero. The current final run passes retain and fails the material-state gate.

### Primary MAP–AKI claim

Support requires all of:

1. B1 p75-versus-p25 standardized risk difference >0 with 95% hospital-bootstrap interval excluding zero.
2. Held-test ΔLL versus B0 >0 with paired 95% interval excluding zero and no material calibration deterioration.
3. Direction persists under fixed-window, observation-weighted, pattern-mixture odds 0.75–1.5, within-hospital, equal-hospital, ≥90%-coverage/boundary-supported, triplet-exclusion, D1 exposure, and high-leverage-hospital removal.
4. No reversal under gap-after-low, pressor-boundary, and sampling-density interactions.

Adverse evidence is an upper 95% risk-difference bound ≤+1 percentage point together with no held-test log-loss gain, or a stable reverse result across observation/hospital checks. This argues against clinically meaningful incremental monitored-burden information, not that hypotension is harmless.

Inconclusive evidence includes an interval spanning zero and +1 point, inadequate support, sign changes under D1/fixed-window/logical-width/selection analyses, failed outcome-observation gate, poor calibration, or unstable hospitals. The inherited 11.3-point logical-width testing gradient provisionally fails opportunity-cohort transport; unless partition-level gates pass, any support is limited to later-tested patients.

T1 is supported only by paired held-test log-loss improvement over B1 with interval excluding zero, stable calibration and validation/test direction, and nonconstant temporal information surviving ablations. T1 failure leaves only the aggregate result. The latent full-clock M1/L1 path is supported only after its separate target-free measurement gates; it never overrides D0/B1.

Support establishes a transportable conditional prognostic association among later-tested index encounters. It does not establish causality, renal perfusion mechanism, treatment benefit, optimal threshold, actual line presence, measured-MAR observation, or complete KDIGO AKI. Those require device/line metadata, reliable delivered treatments and fluids/nephrotoxins, urine-output/RRT timing, pre-ICU renal history, clinician adjudication, external validation, and prospective study.
