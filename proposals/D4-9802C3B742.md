## Audit discrepancy and interpretation

The inherited `support-7-aggregate_selected_cells.py` does not fully implement the frozen repeat validity gate: its `hasrep` test checks repeat date, age, and non-null analytes but not positivity before initially setting `R`; it then only rejects non-finite derived eGFR, so negative/zero analytes could be incorrectly admitted. Its `complete_repeat_measurement_rows` is consequently a recheck rather than a certified result. The executed audit explicitly requires finite positive repeat analytes before computing outcomes and reproduces the parent’s 16,546 qualifying count. This is a code-level discrepancy, not evidence that the published counts are wrong; the independent repeat check agrees on 16,546. The inherited script also writes to a path outside this workspace, whereas this child’s outputs are local workspace artifacts.

The audit establishes computability and aggregate support only. It does not execute the frozen logistic models, 2,000-successful-replicate participant bootstrap, separation diagnostics, standardized risk differences, confidence intervals, p-values, Holm gates, or falsification analyses. Therefore no direction can be called supportive, adverse, or statistically inconclusive from these counts alone; the primary result remains pending execution. Sparse exposure-positive cells, especially Direction B (78 selected exposed participants, with 15 events), make the predeclared model/support/separation/bootstrap gates consequential. The correct current scientific conclusion is feasibility support with outcome inference not yet certified.
