# Episode 10: observed creatinine/Foley recovery discordance after 72 hours

## Decision and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive, data-bound successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the clinically important unresolved claim and repairs the remaining execution weakness rather than changing the question to fit a partial audit.

**Hypothesis.** Among adults in a first valid ICU stay who are alive and still in that ICU at a fixed 72-hour landmark, have an observed early creatinine rise, apparent creatinine recovery, documented early Foley oliguria, dense paired measurement support, and an observable same-admission outcome, failure of recorded Foley output to recover by 72 hours is associated with higher subsequent all-cause in-hospital mortality than recorded Foley-output recovery.

This is an observed prognostic association. It does not claim low recorded Foley output is true oliguria, that creatinine recovery caused harm, that an intervention would improve outcomes, or that an alert is clinically useful.

The clinical importance is a consequential measurement problem: falling creatinine can be read as renal recovery even when recorded output remains low. A reproducible association would justify prospective validation using complete urine capture, cystatin C or measured GFR, muscle/volume assessment, catheter-placement/recording audit, and treatment context. The substantive advance is a falsifiable MIMIC estimate of a precisely supported discordant phenotype and its observation-process robustness, not another generic mortality predictor.

Completion requires newly deriving and freezing:

1. a provenance and cohort-flow manifest;
2. dictionary/schema/member checks and complete raw/valid/duplicate/late-storage/unit/key/window support counts;
3. one row per eligible hospitalization with the phenotype, process measures, outcome and missingness;
4. unadjusted and pre-landmark-adjusted mortality contrasts with subject-clustered 95% intervals;
5. a newly fitted ridge baseline, temporal boosted-tree model, Foley-amount ablation and process-only model on the same subject split;
6. held-out performance, calibration, ablations, uncertainty and falsification outputs;
7. a decision record linking every scientific conclusion to named computed outputs.

No cohort size, event count, effect estimate or model result is claimed here.

## What is known and what remains unresolved

The expert seed `references/expert-seeds/cards/mimic-03.md` is explicitly untested and warns that measured kidney function is unavailable. Haines et al., *Comparison of Cystatin C and Creatinine in the Assessment of Measured Kidney Function during Critical Illness* (local PDF/text inspected at `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`) studied 38 mechanically ventilated patients and measured creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance and muscle ultrasound. It reported divergence between creatinine-based and measured kidney-function estimates during prolonged critical illness and a relationship between creatinine/cystatin-C change and muscle loss. That supports biological plausibility for creatinine misrepresenting recovery in selected critical illness; it does not establish MIMIC prevalence, Foley completeness, prognosis or mechanism.

Thus the strongest supported claim is that creatinine-based kidney assessment can diverge from measured kidney function during prolonged critical illness. The unresolved claim tested here is narrower: within the precisely observed MIMIC support population, does apparent creatinine recovery with nonrecovery of recorded Foley output predict later hospital death relative to concordant recovery? Only the conditional observed association in this MIMIC population is computationally checkable.

## Population, temporal boundaries and phenotype

Construct the eligible key set from small members. For each subject/hospitalization choose the earliest valid ICU stay by (intime, stay_id), retain the exact interval [intime, outtime), require adult anchor_age >=18, a valid admission join, t0=intime+72h < outtime, dischtime>t0, and no deathtime<=t0. Keep exclusions in `cohort_flow.csv`; do not replace an unsupported first stay with a later stay.

Primary measurement windows are half-open relative to intime: W0=[0,24h), W1=[24,48h), W2=[48,72h). Clinical charttime defines measurement timing; procedure starttime defines new RRT/ventilation timing. Primary support requires storetime<=t0. Late-stored charttime-valid rows are retained only in audit/sensitivity outputs and cannot silently enter the primary phenotype.

Creatinine uses positive values from 220615, 50912 and 52546. Hospital laboratory units must be audited for one compatible pattern; no unverified conversion. ICU unit behavior must be audited. Deduplicate exact same key/item/time/value/unit observations, retain source disagreement indicators, and use the median at identical clinical timestamps only after preserving source counts. Require at least one storage-available creatinine in every window. Let C0=min(W0), C1=max(W1), Cpeak=max(W0,W1), Clate=min(W2). Early rise is C1>=C0+0.3 mg/dL OR C1/C0>=1.5; apparent recovery is Clate<=Cpeak-0.3 OR Clate/Cpeak<=0.75. These are observed screens, not KDIGO stages.

For Foley, use nonnegative numeric 226559 values with audited mL units. Bin by relative clinical hour and sum valid nonduplicate volume per hour. Zero is observed zero; absent is missing. Require a positive W0 weight from 226512, otherwise 224639, in W0 with storage support; preserve source and time. Normalize total recorded W0/W2 volume by the full 24-hour window and W0 weight, never by observed hours. Require in W0 and W2 at least 18 distinct observed chart hours, span >=20 hours and maximum gap <=4 hours. Define early observed oliguria as W0 recorded output <0.5 mL/kg/hour; define W2 recovery as >=0.5 and nonrecovery as <0.5. Dense support is an auditability rule, not complete Foley capture.

The primary phenotype population requires early rise, apparent recovery, early observed oliguria, supported weight, dense W0/W2 Foley observations and a known/reconcilable post-t0 outcome. Discordant is creatinine recovered/Foley nonrecovered; concordant is creatinine recovered/Foley recovered. Report all other cells and lower-density/late-storage/source strata without replacing the primary cell.

## Outcomes and estimands

Primary outcome is deathtime with t0 < deathtime <= dischtime. `hospital_expire_flag` is a consistency check. Missing deathtime with flag=1, impossible dates or irreconcilable dates are outcome-unknown and reported, not imputed from discharge location. The primary estimand is group mortality risk, risk difference and risk ratio, discordant versus concordant, with subject-clustered percentile-bootstrap intervals. The adjusted estimand is a standardized associational contrast from ridge logistic regression using covariates available through 48h only: age, sex, care/admission context, W0 weight, C0/C1/rise severity, W0 recorded output/coverage, and pre-48 MAP/HR/RR/SpO2/lactate summaries, counts and missingness. Never adjust for W2 Foley, W2 density, Clate, post-t0 treatment or outcome-derived variables.

Secondary descriptive outcomes are first new qualifying RRT after t0 and before dischtime (the six dialysis procedure IDs above), later same-admission ICU readmission (a later icustays row with later.intime > index.outtime and before discharge), death-or-RRT and length of stay. They cannot replace the primary mortality outcome.

## Method comparison and falsification

Use the method comparison recorded in `episode10_renal_method_exploration.md`. Both models use all support-defined stays with known post-t0 death status, including both phenotype groups, and the same deterministic subject split: SHA-256(subject_id || “renal-discordance-episode10”), 60% train, 20% validation, 20% locked test. Ridge preprocessing/lambda and tree depth/learning rate are fit/tuned only on train/validation. Report held-out AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, reliability bins, grouped feature-family ablations and subject-cluster bootstrap intervals. The tree is a six-hour binned histogram gradient-boosted model; it is selected because temporal persistence/nonlinearity is scientifically relevant and CPU-bounded, not because complexity earns merit.

Process-aware falsifications are prespecified: density, maximum gap, source composition and storage lag by group; dense-threshold and lower-support sensitivity; storage-available versus charttime-only; combined versus ICU-only versus hospital-lab-only creatinine; absolute-only/relative-only/combined recovery thresholds; admission versus daily weight; exclusion of pre-t0 RRT; process-only versus output-amount model; within-stay Foley timestamp permutation preserving values/counts/density/windows; phenotype-label permutation within prespecified recovery/density strata; pre-landmark density/lag negative controls; and explicit timestamp/split leakage checks.

Supportive evidence requires >=50 stays and >=10 deaths per primary cell, reconciled outcomes, adjusted and unadjusted higher discordant risk with a 95% interval excluding the null, direction retained across dense/storage/source/threshold checks, and neither process-only nor timestamp permutation explaining the contrast. The temporal model must show stable calibration and incremental information from Foley amount beyond ridge and density-only features. This remains a conditional observed prognostic signal.

Adverse evidence is a precise null/reversal with adequate support, especially under dense storage-available measurement; attenuation attributable to source, density or lag is adverse to a biological discordance interpretation; temporal improvement without the prespecified contrast does not rescue the hypothesis. Inconclusive evidence includes insufficient support/events, outcome inconsistencies, differential observability not separable from phenotype, failed falsification, leakage, unstable uncertainty/calibration, source mismatch or incomplete no-sampling execution. Rules cannot be relaxed after results are seen.

## Evidence limits and dataset scope

MIMIC-IV core 3.1 supplies structured ICU/hospital observations and local discharge/radiology text. The catalog also configures EICU, UKB and HCC; they remain directly accessible and read-only, but are not pooled because pooling would change the population and estimand. No public query contains patient rows or notes. MIMIC lacks cystatin C/measured GFR, complete intake, complete urine capture, catheter placement/recording intent, validated volume status, treatment indication, images and raw waveforms. Notes/radiology text do not provide validated replacements for these labels.
