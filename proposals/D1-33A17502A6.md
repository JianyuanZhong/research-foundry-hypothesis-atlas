# All-eligible, availability-aware incremental preoperative M2 triage

## Clinical question and substantive advance

The clinical decision remains fixed: before the operation, should a patient be flagged for multidisciplinary review because their risk of pathology-defined high-risk MVI (M2) may warrant review of the operative plan or later validation? This is a triage flag, not a treatment recommendation.

The strongest evidence supported by the available literature and record is narrower: MVI is determined from pathology, MVI grade is relevant to HCC prognosis, and preoperative prediction is clinically interesting because pathology is unavailable at planning time. Li et al. (2025; DOI 10.1111/jcmm.70746; PMCID PMC12309289) was acquired as full-text XML and inspected; its abstract states that MVI diagnosis depends on pathological specimens and that MVI-related management remains controversial. The HCC source contains pathology narratives and preoperative CT/MR reports, but it has no raw images, no pathology timestamp, and no report-finalization or actual tumor-board timestamp.

The unresolved claim is not merely whether a model separates explicitly graded cases. It is whether the report-plus-lab extension adds clinically material net benefit over the structured tumor-burden/liver-reserve baseline in the target population of eligible HCC resections, including patients whose pathology report does not contain an explicit M0/M1/M2 grade and across the calendar/template eras in which grade availability changes.

The substantive repair is an availability-aware estimand. Patients with HCC pathology but no explicit MVI grade remain in the target population. They are never coded as M0, and their outcomes are not imputed as if the label were observed. The primary all-target delta-net-benefit result is a sharp, patient-level partial-identification interval over their unknown pathology-only endpoint. A secondary inverse-probability-of-label-observation estimate is reported only under demonstrable preoperative overlap and a stated missing-at-random assumption. Thus a narrow apparent gain among explicitly graded cases cannot be presented as transportable evidence when the all-target bound is wide or crosses harm.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Let B be a compact structured baseline and E be the nested report-plus-lab extension. Both use the same patients, development protocol, model family, preprocessing rules, threshold and locked-test rows.

The fixed operating threshold is p_t=0.30: flag if predicted M2 risk is at least 0.30. Net benefit is

  NB_m = (1/N) sum_i [I(Y_i=1) I(d_{mi}=1)
          - I(Y_i=0) I(d_{mi}=1) p_t/(1-p_t)].

The primary estimand is the paired incremental utility

  Delta NB_0.30 = NB_E(0.30) - NB_B(0.30).

The primary hypothesis is: in all eligible locked-test HCC resection patients from 2023-01-01 through the snapshot boundary 2025-11-11, Delta NB_0.30 is at least 0.01 after accounting for unknown MVI grades, with the complete-case observation mechanism not required for the primary claim. This is falsifiable in both directions: the lower all-target bound must exceed 0.01 to support the claim, and the upper bound below zero refutes a positive incremental-utility claim. If the interval spans either 0 or 0.01, the correct result is inconclusive.

For each target patient, let S=1 when same-encounter pathology contains HCC and exactly one explicit M0, M1 or M2 grade after the frozen label dictionary and conflict rules. For S=1, Y=1 for M2 and Y=0 for M0/M1. For S=0 among pathology-confirmed HCC resections, Y is unknown; positive-ungraded MVI, unknown grade and conflicting grade are not controls.

Let q_i=d_Ei-d_Bi, where q_i is -1, 0 or 1. The sharp observed-outcome-free bounds for the primary paired difference are computed row by row:

  lower contribution for S=0 = min(q_i, -q_i p_t/(1-p_t))
  upper contribution for S=0 = max(q_i, -q_i p_t/(1-p_t)).

Observed rows use q_i when Y=1 and -q_i p_t/(1-p_t) when Y=0. Summing these contributions over every target row and dividing by N gives the all-target lower and upper bounds. This is a computable partial-identification result, not a claim that the unknown grades have been recovered. Bootstrap resampling is at the patient level and recomputes both bounds, yielding uncertainty for the lower and upper endpoints.

The secondary transport estimate uses only S=1 labels and a stabilized observation weight from a model for S given pre-decision baseline variables, calendar era, report availability, procedure source and missingness indicators. It is admissible only if every locked-test era and prespecified modality stratum has adequate graded cases, effective sample size at least 100, no extreme weights after prespecified truncation at [0.1,10], and covariate overlap. It estimates Delta NB in all pathology-confirmed HCC resections under the untestable assumption Y is missing at random conditional on the pre-decision variables. A selection-bias sensitivity analysis varies the odds of M2 among S=0 relative to S=1 by fixed multipliers 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 within era/availability strata. This sensitivity is not a substitute for pathology review.

## Target population and time lock

Unit: one patient and the first valid liver-tumor resection.

1. From procedures, identify the first procedure per patient with a valid 开始时间 and a reviewed, frozen normalization of 手术 indicating partial, segmental, wedge, lobar, hemihepatic or liver-tumor resection. Emit all included and excluded procedure strings and counts before fitting. Exclude transplant, biopsy-only, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ-only and obvious non-HCC operations. A procedure containing a resection plus an additional procedure is retained if the resection component is explicit; transplant-containing strings are excluded.

2. Require age >=18 from the linked encounter. The disease-specific primary target requires same-encounter pathology evidence of HCC using both 患者主索引 and 就诊号, but does not require an MVI grade. Patients with no pathology-HCC evidence are excluded from the disease-specific target and are reported in a separate attrition table; they are not relabeled as M0. A secondary broad surgical-candidate table retains all adult untreated first liver resections and reports the even wider endpoint-unknown bounds.

3. Define t_0 as index procedure 开始时间 minus 24 hours. Require at least one eligible CT or MRI examination with 检查.开始时间 in [t_0-90 days,t_0). Require laboratories in [t_0-30 days,t_0). No source row timestamped at or after t_0 is a predictor. The acquisition timestamp is only an operational temporal eligibility surrogate: the source has no report sign-off, tumor-board, consent or operative-planning timestamp. This limitation remains a clinical-adjudication requirement.

4. For the primary untreated cohort, exclude any HCC-directed resection candidate with HCC-directed resection, transplantation, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, molecular therapy or immunotherapy in procedures, medications or non-drug orders during [t_0-365 days,t_0). A separately labeled previously-treated sensitivity cohort keeps those patients and adds treatment-history indicators. Untimed diagnoses and clinical documents are not used to establish prior treatment.

5. Use explicit calendar strata rather than assuming that the label process is stable. Development is index year 2015–2020, using only S=1 labels. Year 2021 is a prespecified bridge/calibration period. Year 2022 is a label-availability/drift audit and cannot be used to fit, tune, recalibrate or select a model because the preliminary scan found no explicit grade under the conservative parser. The locked test is index year 2023 through 2025-11-11. Patients and all their encounters belong to one period only. If the development or bridge period lacks the frozen minimum label counts, the run is inconclusive; it is not repaired by pooling across the 2022 template break.

6. The primary target includes every eligible pathology-confirmed HCC patient regardless of MVI-grade availability and regardless of missing predictors. Both B and E must produce predictions for identical locked-test rows. Use development-median imputation plus explicit missingness indicators. No missing-outcome row is removed from the primary bounds.

## Pathology endpoint and ascertainment

The sole endpoint is pathology-defined M2 versus M0/M1. Pathology is used only after prediction. The frozen dictionary must recognize the explicit MVI risk-grade formulations, including MVI提示风险分级：M2, M2(高危组), MVI风险分级：M1, MVI提示风险分级：M0 and their reviewed spacing/parenthesis equivalents. The dictionary must distinguish the actual grade following the risk-grade field from the explanatory template list that describes all M0/M1/M2 definitions.

For every same-encounter key, retain a machine-readable label audit with the matched phrase, source row hash, class, and conflict status. A single class is observed only when all retained matching rows agree on one of M0/M1/M2. Positive-ungraded MVI, narrative microvascular invasion without a grade, missing pathology, non-HCC pathology, and conflicting grades are S=0 or outside the disease-specific target as appropriate; none is assigned Y=0.

Before fitting, two clinically qualified Chinese-reading reviewers blinded to predictors independently review random samples of at least 100 parser-assigned M0, M1 and M2 cases and every conflict. A third reviewer resolves disagreements. Report PPV, sensitivity and Cohen's kappa. The parser gate is PPV >=0.95 and sensitivity >=0.90 per class. For S=0 cases, a separate blinded sample of at least 200 pathology narratives per calendar era may be reviewed to determine whether an explicit grade is present but missed or whether the available narrative supports a provisional available-text classification. This review does not create a true gold standard for cases lacking adequate slides or a standardized sampling protocol.

The primary endpoint cannot be made complete from this source alone. There is no pathology time field, no slide-level data, no specimen sampling protocol and no independent pathology registry. The all-target bounds are therefore the strongest computable claim. A definitive all-patient MVI endpoint requires pathology expert adjudication with access to the complete report and, for unresolved cases, slides and sampling protocol.

## Baseline and extension

B is an elastic-net logistic model containing:

- age (年龄) and sex (性别) from encounters;
- latest eligible CT and latest eligible MRI report before t_0: maximum lesion diameter, tumor-count category (1, 2, >=3), modality availability (CT only, MRI only, both), and explicit macrovascular tumor-thrombus language, each with unknown indicators;
- last exact-assay albumin (白蛋白), total bilirubin (总胆红素), INR (国际标准化比值) and platelet count (血小板计数) before t_0, each continuous with a missing indicator.

Do not call this Child-Pugh or ALBI. Ascites and encephalopathy are not sufficiently reliable and lab units are absent.

E contains all B variables plus rule-extracted capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement and enhancement-pattern indicators; development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF from 检查所见 and 检查诊断; and last exact-assay AFP (甲胎蛋白), ALT (丙氨酸氨基转移酶), AST (门冬氨酸氨基转移酶), GGT (γ-谷氨酰转移酶), ALP (碱性磷酸酶) and creatinine (肌酐), each with a missing indicator.

For imaging, combine the latest eligible CT and MRI deterministically: maximum diameter is the maximum reported across modalities, count is the maximum category, and a binary finding is present if either report is positive, absent only if one report explicitly negates it and neither is positive, otherwise unknown. Strip identifiers, dates, accession numbers, pathology/specimen language, explicit MVI/微血管侵犯 and copied-pathology segments. Exclude examinations labeled pathology/specimen. Audit the 100 largest-magnitude extension n-grams before test unblinding; any postoperative or endpoint proxy invalidates the run.

All continuous transforms, winsorization bounds, medians, TF-IDF vocabulary, penalties, bridge calibration map and threshold are frozen before the locked test. Because lab units are absent, do not use clinical cutoffs or pool differently named assays; report exact-assay/year distributions and repeat with within-assay/year ranks.

## Computation, overlap and gates

Fit B and E with the same nested elastic-net protocol and patient-disjoint temporal split. Generate row-level frozen predictions for every target patient, including S=0. The row-level output must include an opaque patient key hash, index year, S, observed Y when available, both probabilities, both flags, t_0, predictor availability indicators and source-row provenance; it must not contain direct identifiers.

Primary reports:

- all-target sharp lower and upper bounds for Delta NB_0.30 with 2,000 patient bootstrap 95% intervals;
- observed-label Delta NB and each model versus flag-all and flag-none, explicitly labeled as S=1 descriptive/complete-case quantities;
- all-target bounds versus flag-all and flag-none where identifiable under the same unknown-outcome logic;
- paired delta-AUPRC, AUROC, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV and Brier score only on observed labels and, separately, by the admissible IPW sensitivity;
- calibration-in-the-large and slope on observed labels, with IPW only when positivity passes;
- decision curves from p_t=0.20 to 0.40 as secondary displays; p_t=0.30 remains primary;
- all-target bounds and observed/IPW quantities by calendar year, CT-only/MRI-only/both, sex, age <65/>=65, AFP observed/missing, M0 versus M1 controls and report availability.

The all-target computation is eligible only if every target patient gets both predictions, labels and conflicts are independently audited, joins and patient uniqueness pass, all feature times precede t_0, leakage tests pass, and the bound calculation is reproducible from frozen row-level outputs. The IPW sensitivity additionally requires at least 100 observed grades per era/modality stratum, effective sample size >=100, stabilized weights within [0.1,10] before and after truncation, no empty propensity bins, and standardized mean differences <0.10 after weighting for the prespecified preoperative covariates. A failed IPW gate does not invalidate the primary bound; it prevents a transport point estimate.

Do not require a complete-case 80% grade-availability gate as if it were a scientific success criterion. Instead, report the actual grade-availability and report-availability process. If the all-target bound is too wide, this is an inconclusive transport result and evidence against immediate clinical use, not a reason to restrict the target post hoc.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires all of the following:

1. The lower endpoint of the all-target Delta NB_0.30 bound has a two-sided bootstrap lower 95% limit above 0.01.
2. The extension is better than flag-none and flag-all under the corresponding all-target bounds, and its observed-label and admissible IPW estimates point in the same direction.
3. The model is calibrated in the observed-label analysis (extension slope 95% CI within 0.80–1.20 and intercept within -0.10–0.10) and no prespecified year/modality stratum has a negative all-target incremental bound.
4. The label parser, report-feature adjudication, leakage, temporal-lock and independent recomputation gates pass.
5. If an IPW estimate is presented, its overlap and effective-sample-size gates pass and the conclusion remains supportive over the prespecified selection-bias multipliers.

The strongest permitted supportive conclusion is that, under the specified pathology endpoint and an explicitly bounded label-observation process, timestamp-eligible routine reports and labs add transportable M2-risk stratification beyond structured burden/liver-reserve variables and merit independent-center prospective validation. It does not establish that the flag changes resection extent, neoadjuvant/adjuvant therapy, recurrence, survival, quality of life, cost or harm.

Adverse evidence is either (a) the upper endpoint of the all-target Delta NB bound is below zero, which falsifies positive incremental utility in the eligible target, or (b) all-target bounds cross zero but observed and admissible IPW estimates show a reproducible negative increment with the upper uncertainty bound below zero in a prespecified era/modality. In the former case conclude that the proposed extension is not useful for the triage decision. In the latter case conclude that the available data do not support deployment and that selection/drift prevents a definitive target-population claim. Do not rescue an adverse result by changing p_t, restricting to explicitly graded cases, using development metrics or relabeling ungraded MVI as M0.

Inconclusive evidence includes bounds spanning material benefit and no benefit; no explicit grades or inadequate effective sample size in a locked era; failure of label or report adjudication; missing report availability at the actual decision; non-overlap; conflicting pathology; temporal leakage; or inability to link numeric conclusions to row-level outputs. Report the interval and the exact failed gate. A narrow complete-case estimate is not a resolution of an inconclusive all-target result.

Computationally checkable claims include source paths and snapshot hash, headers, ordinary-file/archive status, join keys, duplicate handling, attrition and filtering, patient-disjoint periods, all feature timestamps before t_0, frozen dictionaries and transforms, identical evaluation rows, model/threshold, sharp bound formulas, bootstrap uncertainty, overlap metrics, calibration, decision curves and links from conclusions to machine-readable outputs. Clinical truth of Chinese phrase interpretation, adequacy of pathology sampling, actual report availability at a care decision, acceptability of the 0.30 harm tradeoff and benefit from model-guided care require qualified review, raw pathology/imaging evidence, preference elicitation or a prospective impact/treatment study. The source lacks raw images/slides, pathology sampling protocols, actual decision times, treatment intent, tumor-board decisions, reliable surveillance, death registry, patient-reported outcomes and costs.

## Audit provenance

The preliminary read-only audit is recorded in hcc_mvi_transport_audit.json. It scanned the source headers and raw rows, with no source modifications. Under a conservative resection heuristic, it found 25,694 adult first-resection patients; 21,334 had HCC text in same-encounter pathology, 7,710 had one explicit MVI grade, and 17,978 had no explicit grade. The yearly single-grade counts were 1,234 (2020), 656 (2021), 0 (2022), 15 (2023), 469 (2024) and 611 (2025), with 0, 0, 0, 0, 0 and 1 M2 labels respectively in that preliminary parser. These counts are feasibility evidence and expose calendar/template drift; they are not final cohort attrition or model validation. A broad examination heuristic found any liver/abdominal CT or MR within the 90-day pre-t_0 window in 1,443/2,984 first resections in 2023, 1,480/2,942 in 2024 and 1,795/3,467 in 2025. The final compiler must rerun the frozen dictionaries and emit actual filtering.
