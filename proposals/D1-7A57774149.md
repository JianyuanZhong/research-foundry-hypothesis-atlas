# Documentary target-linkage and actionability layer for the frozen pulmonary routing protocol

## 1. Targeted repair and scope

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not change the parent’s scientific schema, denominator, target-selection order, routing rule, sampling design, inference, workload ceilings, or conclusion ceiling.

The parent’s strongest supported claim is narrow and valuable: in the frozen MIMIC frame, one exact supported atom-route cell can be tested as a reproducible stored-text construct using a sealed, positive-probability, finite-population, all-unit adverse audit. A stored-text route is not, by itself, evidence that a recommendation was actionable at departure, that anyone saw it, that an order or referral was made, that imaging occurred, or that care was appropriate or safe. The unresolved actionability gap is therefore not “does the route predict a clinical outcome?”—MIMIC cannot answer that—but whether the route’s target and timing have a separately measurable, target-linked documentary trace that is stronger near the prescribed interval than in preregistered falsification windows, while explicitly retaining missingness and outside-care ambiguity.

I add an **ancillary documentary target-linkage layer**. It is downstream of the frozen core design and is never used to define eligibility, select the target, form phase-1 strata, allocate phase-2 admissions, alter the route, or replace missing evidence. It can only support the existing conclusion ceiling: consideration of a separately governed prospective silent actual-workflow bridge, with operational outputs suppressed or `DEFER`. It cannot authorize `AUTO`, `RECON`, or any live action and cannot establish actual workflow, completion, clinical truth, appropriateness, responsibility, safety, benefit, or causality.

The layer distinguishes four claims that are otherwise easy to conflate.

1. **Stored-text reproducibility:** the inherited exact atom-route can be constructed and audited under the parent’s gates.
2. **Target-linked documentary observability:** a later MIMIC radiology artifact is text-adjudicated as referring to the same pulmonary target and is temporally compatible with the target’s prescribed interval.
3. **Actionability:** not identified by MIMIC. The data do not show that a clinician received, viewed, acknowledged, accepted, ordered, scheduled, or acted on a recommendation.
4. **Clinical truth or benefit:** not identified by MIMIC. A later report is not proof that imaging was completed, that a lesion was present, that a recommendation was correct, or that an outcome improved.

The falsifiable hypothesis is consequently modest: **conditional on an observable MIMIC radiology-documentation opportunity, the selected exact target atom will have a pre-specified excess of target-linked documentary artifacts in its due window over fixed placebo windows and route-permutation controls, with sufficiently low unresolved/discordant labeling and valid finite-population uncertainty to be a candidate for prospective silent workflow measurement.** Failure is adverse or inconclusive for this documentary construct; it is not evidence that care was absent, unsafe, or ineffective.

## 4. Ancillary index, clocks, and censoring

The ancillary layer is run only after `ACCESS_COMMITTED`. It receives the sealed target atom and the sampled admission IDs, but not the inherited route label, phase-2 C/S labels, endpoint results, reviewer preferences, or any downstream route decision. Its roster, codebook, and access are disjoint from all parent panels. It cannot feed back into allocation or target selection.

### 4.1 Index time and target due time

For every sampled admission that contains the sealed target unit, define the target’s documentary index `r0` from the already frozen pre-D reciprocal radiology chain used by the parent. `r0` is the chain member with the largest valid `storetime` strictly before `D`; ties are resolved by lexicographically smallest `(note_id, note_seq)`. This is a deterministic storage-time anchor, not a claim about when a clinician read the report. If no unique `r0` can be constructed from the parent’s sealed chain, the ancillary state is `K_U_INDEX` and no window is scored.

The target unit’s inherited due interval `m` is one of the parent-eligible exact calendar intervals (for example 3, 6, or 12 months). Define `calendar_add(t, m)` exactly as follows: convert the timestamp to its civil calendar date in the dataset’s recorded timezone convention; add `m` calendar months to year/month; set the day to `min(original_day, last_day_of_destination_month)`; preserve the timestamp’s hour, minute, second, and microsecond; and return the resulting timestamp. Month-end behavior is therefore deterministic and is not a 30-day approximation. The protocol records both the original `r0.storetime` and the computed `due`.

Define a 61-day due window as the half-open interval `[due - 30 days, due + 31 days)`. The 31-day post-due side includes the due date and is fixed before any labels. A report is eligible for the due-window documentary panel only if its `charttime >= r0.storetime`, its `storetime >= r0.storetime`, both timestamps are valid, and its `charttime` lies in the due window. The `storetime` must be no later than `due + 31 days`; this prevents a later-entered historical note from masquerading as timely documentation. These rules are about storage chronology and are not claims about imaging completion or viewing.

### 4.2 Observation horizon and administrative censoring

Before any ancillary labels are read, compute and seal `T_obs` as the maximum valid `storetime` in the complete read-only `note/radiology` source file, with the source hash and row count recorded. The horizon for an index is `H_i = min(T_obs, dod_i + 1 day)` when a valid patient `dod` exists after `r0.storetime`; otherwise `H_i = T_obs`. If `H_i < due - 30 days`, classify `DEATH_OR_HORIZON_BEFORE_WINDOW` and do not call it a documentary failure. If `due + 31 days > H_i`, classify `RIGHT_CENSORED_WINDOW` unless a complete due-window assessment is still possible under the exact stored timestamps. A `dod` date is an administrative censoring signal only; it does not establish cause, appropriateness, or a clinical outcome.

The same source file is searched by `subject_id`, not merely by `hadm_id`, so a later report in another admission can be observed. A null or different `hadm_id` is retained rather than dropped. This increases observable documentary coverage but does not turn an external or outside-care event into a MIMIC event. The index admission remains the frozen parent admission, and no later admission changes the population denominator.

### 4.3 Fixed report retrieval and overflow

For each subject and each predeclared window, retrieve all rows of `note/radiology` meeting the timestamp and horizon rules. Sort by `(charttime, storetime, note_id, note_seq)` using a fixed missing-time rule: missing `charttime` or `storetime` is ineligible for a positive timed observation and is retained in a missingness ledger. To make physician workload finite and known before labels, expose at most the first three eligible reports per window under that order. If four or more eligible reports exist, set `OVERFLOW_WINDOW` and retain the overflow count; do not choose a favorable subset, continue sampling, or infer from the first three. An overflow is inconclusive for that documentary cell, not evidence of actionability or failure.

The documentary panel sees the report text, its de-identified subject-window key, `charttime` and `storetime` bins needed for the frozen timing label, and a structured copy of the target unit’s non-route atom fields (`target_cluster`, `action_modality`, and due interval). It does not see whether the report was found in the due or placebo window, the inherited route, any phase-1/phase-2 label, or the admission outcome. The target-window role and negative-control role are assigned after the panel returns labels, using a sealed key. The panel cannot infer target selection from packet order.

## 5. Documentary labels and endpoint definitions

Two fresh independent reviewers, from a roster disjoint from the parent, label each retrieved report using a pre-sealed codebook. They label the report independently; disagreement is preserved. A third adjudicator may resolve only the operational compatible set under the parent’s adverse-error framework; adjudication is not a clinical gold standard and cannot erase the two original labels.

Each reviewer records the following atomic labels.

- `L_cluster`: `SAME`, `DIFFERENT`, or `UNKNOWN` for whether the report text refers to the same pulmonary target cluster as the frozen target. A `SAME` label requires an explicit anatomic/site or lesion reference compatible with the target; generic “follow-up” without a site is `UNKNOWN`.
- `L_modality`: `COMPATIBLE`, `INCOMPATIBLE`, or `UNKNOWN` for whether the observed report modality is compatible with the target’s inherited action modality. The label is based only on report metadata/text available in the radiology file; it is not a procedure-completion label.
- `L_temporal`: `IN_WINDOW`, `OUT_OF_WINDOW`, or `UNKNOWN` based on the deterministic source timestamps and the sealed due window. Reviewers cannot alter timestamps.
- `L_plan`: `PRESENT`, `ABSENT`, or `UNKNOWN` for whether the report text contains a target-related assessment or recommendation. `PRESENT` is documentary content only; it does not mean an order, referral, or plan was implemented.
- `L_conflict`: `YES` if the report contains mutually incompatible target/site or modality signals; otherwise `NO`, with `UNKNOWN` when text is insufficient.

The locked code derives a report state, with no reviewer route vote.

- `DOC_TARGET_LINKED` requires both independent reviewers’ compatible sets to contain `SAME`, `COMPATIBLE`, `IN_WINDOW`, and `PRESENT`, with no unresolved conflict.
- `DOC_NON_TARGET` requires both compatible sets to contain `DIFFERENT` or `INCOMPATIBLE` and no `SAME`/`COMPATIBLE` possibility.
- `DOC_UNKNOWN` is any remaining compatible set, disagreement, missing timestamp, missing text, or unresolved conflict.
- `DOC_TARGET_LINKED_CONCORDANT` is a secondary stricter state requiring exact agreement of both reviewers on all four positive components; it is not substituted for the primary compatible-set state.

An admission-level documentary state uses all retrieved reports for its target unit and all-unit adverse precedence within the ancillary layer.

- `TARGET_DOCUMENTED_IN_WINDOW` if at least one report is `DOC_TARGET_LINKED` and there is no `DOC_UNKNOWN` or conflicting report that could also be target-linked.
- `TARGET_DOCUMENTED_WITH_UNCERTAINTY` if a target-linked report exists but any other report or reviewer-compatible set remains unresolved.
- `NO_TARGET_LINKED_DOCUMENT` if every eligible report is `DOC_NON_TARGET` and there is no missingness/overflow; this means only that no such MIMIC document was observed.
- `NO_OBSERVABLE_REPORT` if no eligible radiology report exists for the subject in the searchable horizon. This is not `NO_TARGET_LINKED_DOCUMENT` and is not noncompletion.
- `K_U_DOCUMENT` for missing/ambiguous text or timestamps, overflow, linkage failure, incompatible panel access, or nonresponse.
- `DEATH_OR_HORIZON_BEFORE_WINDOW` and `RIGHT_CENSORED_WINDOW` are administrative observation states and are not failures.

The primary target-linked rate is a finite-population descriptive/documentary estimand, not a care-completion probability. It is reported among the design-defined observable-window subset and separately as an all-opportunity lower-bound category including `NO_OBSERVABLE_REPORT` and `K_U_DOCUMENT` as non-positive. The two denominators are never conflated.

## 6. Falsification controls

The target-linked layer has three controls, all fixed before labels and all evaluated by the same reviewers.

### 6.1 Temporal placebo window

For each target with a complete index, define an equal-length placebo window immediately preceding the due window by shifting the due-window endpoints backward by exactly one target interval `m` using the same `calendar_add` month-end function. The placebo window is retained only when both its endpoints are after `r0.storetime` and within the observable horizon. If not, it is `PLACEBO_UNAVAILABLE`, not a zero.

The placebo asks whether target-linked text is specifically concentrated near the inherited due interval rather than being equally common at an earlier fixed time. Because prior or unrelated documentation can occur in either window, a placebo excess is not expected to be identically zero; it is a falsification control for timing specificity, not a proof of workflow.

### 6.2 Deterministic route-permutation control

After target selection and access commitment, generate one decoy target for each eligible sampled target by assigning the target atom fields from the next target in the fixed registered atom order, wrapping at the end, while retaining the index subject and the same due interval. The decoy is never used in target selection, sampling, or any primary endpoint. A decoy report can be `DOC_TARGET_LINKED` only if it independently satisfies the same blinded textual cluster/modality rules. This tests whether reviewers or a codebook manufacture positive links from generic follow-up language. A decoy collision or unavailable decoy is recorded and does not trigger substitution.

### 6.3 Source- and clock-isolation controls

The locked analysis repeats the due-window classification using (a) `charttime` only, (b) `storetime` only, and (c) the required joint rule. Any positive result that appears only when a timestamp violating the joint rule is admitted is a clock artifact and fails the documentary gate. A report with `charttime < r0.storetime` but later `storetime` is explicitly counted as pre-index/ambiguous, never as a positive post-index document.

The primary contrasts are therefore pre-specified as follows, using design-weighted finite-population estimates and compatible-set adverse bounds.

- `Delta_time = P(target-linked | due window, observable) - P(target-linked | placebo, observable)`.
- `Delta_perm = P(target-linked | true target, due window) - P(target-linked | deterministic decoy, due window)`.
- `U_unknown`, the design-weighted proportion in `DOC_UNKNOWN`, overflow, incomplete observation, or reviewer nonresponse.
- `C_capture`, the design-weighted proportion with at least one eligible post-index radiology report in the searchable horizon, reported separately from target linkage.

These are documentary contrasts. They are not intervention effects, completion rates, safety estimates, or measures of clinician performance.

## 7. Sampling, weighting, and target-freeze compatibility

No new unit-level sample is introduced. The ancillary panel operates on whole admissions already selected under the parent’s sealed two-phase design. Every target unit in a phase-2 admission is exposed under the parent’s all-unit rule; if the admission has multiple target units, each is evaluated and the admission-level ancillary adverse state is the worst compatible state over all target units. A sampled admission with no target unit is retained in the parent core but has no ancillary target packet.

The documentary layer uses the parent’s sequential path probability `p_i = pi1_i * q_i` for admission-level totals and the parent’s unit multiplicity bookkeeping for target-unit summaries. It never uses verified-only or complete-case weights. A nonresponsive documentary packet remains `K_U_DOCUMENT` at its original path weight. No admission is deleted, replaced, topped up, or reclassified into a more favorable cell.

The target-freeze invariant is strengthened by a computational assertion: after `FRAME_SUPPORT_SEALED` and `TARGET_SEALED`, relabeling any phase-1 clinical labels, any post-D radiology text, any documentary panel labels, or any endpoint output must leave the target ID, target atom, phase-2 roster, and inclusion probabilities unchanged. Documentary fields are downstream-only and are not available to the target selector. Any violation aborts with `DEFER`.

Primary uncertainty uses the parent’s finite-population, stagewise design variance and at least 20,000 deterministic nested Rao-Wu-style replicates. Within each replicate, the first-stage sample is resampled within inherited cells, phase-1 bands are reconstructed under the registered frame-only target freeze, the sealed phase-2 SRS is reproduced, and documentary labels are carried only for admissions selected on that path. If a replicate has an undefined documentary denominator, the estimand is reported as undefined for that replicate rather than repaired by imputation. Exact pair probabilities are required for any variance estimator that requests them; zero pair probabilities fail closed as in the parent.

## 8. Workload ledger and feasibility certificate

The ancillary layer cannot silently consume an extra 240 physician hours. Its maximum charge is included before target freeze in the parent’s conservative certificate.

Let `A` be the set of phase-2 admissions that could contain the first target under every possible post-label phase-1 band partition permitted by the parent’s certificate. Let `Umax` be the frozen maximum number of target units per such admission. Let `Rmax = 3` be the fixed maximum number of reports per window, and let `Kdoc_max` be the maximum active minutes per report for two independent documentary reviewers, fixed in a synthetic-only rehearsal before any real labels. Let `Kadm_max` be the fixed packet/setup cost per target admission, including index verification, placebo/decoy packet preparation, and adjudication overhead. Means are prohibited.

The pre-label upper bound is

`T_doc_max = |A| * Umax * (2 windows + 1 decoy window) * Rmax * Kdoc_max + |A| * Kadm_max + fixed_doc_controls`.

The implementation must use the registered conservative bound, not the realized number of reports, to select a target. If the bound would violate the parent’s 32-week, reviewer, roster, or minute ceilings for any allowed phase-1 partition, the target is `NO_SELECTION`; the protocol cannot scan to a later target.

In the realized ledger, distinct admissions remain counted once:

`T = T_micro + T_floor + T_target_extra + T_non_target_extra + T_control_only`.

Role flags for core C/S/holistic packets and documentary packets can overlap an admission and do not duplicate `T`. Documentary fixed controls and calibration are added once to `fixed_controls_minutes`. The authoritative charge is

`raw_minutes = phase2_reading_minutes + fixed_controls_minutes + documentary_minutes`,

`reserve_minutes = ceil(0.15 * raw_minutes)`,

`charged_minutes = raw_minutes + reserve_minutes`.

The hard check is `charged_minutes <= 84,000`, with all parent admission, reviewer, weekly, roster, and 32-week constraints simultaneously satisfied. The documentary layer has no independent reserve. Any admission, report, reviewer, or fixed-control charge omitted from the ledger is a computational failure and maps to `DEFER`; no post-label substitution of an easier target or a cheaper packet is allowed.

The compiler must produce a ledger with at least these columns: `subject_id`, `hadm_id`, parent role flags, target-unit count, due-window report count, placebo report count, decoy report count, core minutes, documentary minutes, fixed-control minutes, reserve allocation, charged minutes, reviewer IDs/roster lane, and inclusion probabilities. The actual source-derived counts are to be generated in the execution job, not asserted in this proposal. The proposal therefore does not claim feasibility merely from the schema.

## 9. Gates and interpretation

Numerical margins are governance parameters, not clinical safety thresholds. Before execution, the Lead/compiler must freeze them in the protocol and fixture; they cannot be chosen after seeing MIMIC labels. If the requested simultaneous adverse margin or documentary precision cannot be justified within the parent’s available workload, the correct result is `DEFER`, not a relaxed threshold. The following gates are required.

### 9.1 Supportive gate

A cell is **supportive for the documentary construct only** if all of the following hold.

1. The parent target-freeze, allocation, roster, seal-order, positive-probability, route-concealment, and workload checks pass.
2. `T_doc_max` fits the parent’s frozen 84,000-minute, reviewer, weekly, roster, and calendar ceilings for every allowed post-label partition, and the realized ledger agrees with the authoritative equation.
3. The due-window and placebo-window denominators are defined for the registered estimand; right-censoring, death/horizon, no-observable-report, overflow, and `K_U` are separately reported rather than silently discarded.
4. The one-sided simultaneous finite-population adverse bound for `Delta_time` is above the frozen positive documentary margin, and the one-sided bound for `Delta_perm` is above the frozen decoy margin. The exact margin must be registered before labels and is not called a safety margin.
5. The upper simultaneous bound on `U_unknown` and reviewer-discordant/overflow states is below its frozen documentary-quality limit, with a separately positive shared-error breakdown as required by the parent. Any undefined pair probability or zero-probability estimator fails closed.
6. The temporal and source-clock controls agree with the joint clock rule; no positive signal depends only on a prohibited timestamp or generic text leakage.
7. The result is stable under the locked `charttime`, `storetime`, and joint-clock descriptive decompositions without crossing from supportive to adverse solely through unreported denominator changes.

A supportive result means: “the selected exact stored-text target atom has a measurable, target-linked MIMIC documentary contrast under this design, and the contrast is not falsified by the registered timing/decoy controls.” It permits consideration of a separately approved **prospective silent** bridge that measures real workflow events. It does not mean that the target was actionable, that care occurred, that imaging was completed, that a clinician saw the report, or that the route is safe or beneficial.

### 9.2 Adverse gate

The exact target atom-route cell is adverse for the documentary construct if any of the following is established under the registered simultaneous analysis.

- The due-window target-linkage contrast fails its lower-bound margin while the estimand is otherwise defined.
- The deterministic decoy control has a positive rate indistinguishable from or exceeding the true target rate under the adverse bound.
- The placebo window has an equal or greater target-linkage rate under the pre-specified contrast, indicating no target-specific timing signal.
- The route-concealed core audit fails, an all-unit adverse unit is ignored, a route leak occurs, or the target/roster/access freeze is violated.
- A supported atom has a reproducible high `DOC_UNKNOWN`, conflict, overflow, or shared-error-compatible range that prevents a bounded documentary claim.
- The workload ledger, positive-probability requirement, reviewer firewalls, calibration, or finite-population variance computation fails.

An adverse result blocks this exact atom-route cell from bridge priority. It cannot be converted to `RECON`, pooled with neighboring atoms, rescued by a favorable subgroup, or used to search forward for another cell. It is not evidence of absent follow-up, unsafe care, clinician failure, or clinical inappropriateness.

### 9.3 Inconclusive gate

The result is **inconclusive/DEFER** for `NO_SELECTION`, sparse or undefined windows, administrative censoring that leaves the registered denominator undefined, insufficient event/ESS support, no positive-probability path, nonresponse, overflow beyond the fixed cap, incompatible reviewer rosters, or a failure to pre-freeze the documentary margin/cost certificate. Inconclusive means additional source data, adjudication, or a prospective workflow study is required. It cannot be reported as a negative clinical finding.

## 10. Falsification and implementation checks

The execution job must abort rather than repair silently on any of these conditions.

- Any post-D radiology row enters the core population, canonical DS, parent route, target identity, phase-1 stratum, or allocation.
- Any documentary row changes the sealed target, target membership, phase-2 roster, route, inclusion probability, or parent endpoint.
- The same subject is joined on `hadm_id` only and thereby loses null or later-admission radiology rows; alternatively, a report from another subject is joined by an incorrect key.
- `calendar_add` is implemented as fixed days, has an unrecorded month-end convention, or differs between true and placebo windows.
- A report with `charttime < r0.storetime` is treated as post-index evidence, or a missing timestamp is silently imputed.
- `T_obs`, `dod` censoring, due/placebo boundaries, or the three-report cap is computed after viewing documentary labels.
- Reviewers see due/placebo role, inherited route, outcome, or another reviewer’s label; target-window packet order leaks the role.
- `NO_OBSERVABLE_REPORT`, right censoring, death/horizon, outside-care ambiguity, and `K_U` are collapsed into noncompletion or a positive result.
- A favorable subset of reports is chosen after labels, overflow is dropped, or a later admission is used to replace an unavailable report.
- The decoy target is chosen after observing its label, or the decoy is used to tune the codebook.
- Any estimator uses verified-only cases, deletes nonresponse, divides by zero/undefined pair inclusion probability, or calls conditional `q` an unconditional probability.
- Any charge, reserve, control, reviewer hour, or distinct admission is omitted or duplicated in the ledger.
- A conclusion uses words such as “completed,” “received,” “viewed,” “acted on,” “appropriate,” “safe,” “beneficial,” “caused,” or “clinician responsibility” without a separate prospective or clinical adjudication source.

Required failure output is a structured status with the violated invariant, affected cell, source hash, sealed seed, and `DEFER`/`NO_SELECTION`; it must not mutate the source or silently rerun with different windows, target, labels, or rosters.

- `documentary_window_roster.parquet`: admission/target IDs, `r0`, `due`, placebo boundaries, `H_i`, death/horizon state, deterministic report IDs, and overflow counts; no panel labels.
- `documentary_labels.parquet`: independent reviewer atomic labels, compatible sets, adjudication records, and sealed window-role key.

The reference execution must include a synthetic finite-population fixture in which (i) the true target has a due-window excess, (ii) placebo and decoy rates are high enough to fail, (iii) all reports are absent and correctly produce `NO_OBSERVABLE_REPORT` rather than noncompletion, (iv) a timestamp inversion produces `K_U` or exclusion, and (v) a post-label permutation cannot alter target or roster. It must verify path-probability normalization, HT denominator behavior, finite positive design variance where defined, and failure on zero pair probability. These checks establish computational feasibility only; they do not establish a clinical result.

## 12. Evidence ceiling and unresolved external requirements

MIMIC can provide a frozen pre-discharge stored-text route, later stored radiology text linked by `subject_id`, timestamp/order metadata, and an expert-coded documentary relationship under explicit uncertainty. It cannot provide actual departure time, report delivery or acknowledgement, named responsibility, patient preference, outside plans, orders/referrals/scheduling, completed imaging, images, lesion truth, clinical appropriateness, burden, safety, benefit, or causal outcomes. It also does not prove that a later radiology report arose from the original recommendation rather than an unrelated clinical episode.

A supportive result therefore motivates, at most, a prospective silent study that records actual departure, UI exposure, receipt/view/acknowledgement, clinician/team responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, patient preference, outside care, imaging completion and modality, images or adjudicated findings, burden, adverse events, follow-up, and downstream clinical outcomes. That study would require independent clinical adjudication and governance. A further intervention study would be required for causal benefit. None of those stronger conclusions is claimed here.

## 13. Expected substantive advance

The parent already makes the stored-text route difficult to validate by circularity, target leakage, uncontrolled sampling, or hidden workload. This child adds a distinct, falsifiable bridge without pretending that documentation is workflow: it tests whether the exact target and exact due timing have a reproducible, blinded, target-specific documentary trace, while separating no-record, censoring, ambiguity, semantic mismatch, and true target-linked text. The temporal placebo, deterministic route-permutation control, clock-isolation checks, all-unit adverse precedence, and shared-error-compatible bounds prevent a generic high documentation rate from being mislabeled as actionability.

Thus the protocol can produce three honest outcomes. It may show a bounded stored-text/documentary signal worth carrying into a prospective silent workflow study; it may show an adverse lack of target-specific documentary specificity and deprioritize the exact cell; or it may show that the available MIMIC evidence is too sparse, censored, ambiguous, or expensive to answer the question. None of the three outcomes is converted into a claim about actual workflow, safety, benefit, causality, or clinical truth.
