### Falsifiable hypothesis

For at least one support-qualified atom, the inherited route audit will pass and the new clinical measurement will show all of the following: locked implementation fixtures are error-free; independently written actionability and supersession panels discriminate every clinically decisive control family more reliably than they change labels under semantically invariant controls; route realization introduces no excess label instability over matched nuisance transformations; observed compatible sets do not contain an adverse decision need for candidate `AUTO`; and the atom has a nonzero finite-population shared-error breakdown point under simultaneous uncertainty.

Any supported atom with observed `K_R`, `K_N`, or `K_U` compatibility in a candidate-`AUTO` admission falsifies deterministic portable actionability for that admission and worsens the atom frontier. Any supported atom whose route-reveal excess instability is positive, whose decisive/invariant calibration contrast is not resolved, or whose shared-error breakdown point is zero cannot be nominated. Sparse support, imprecise bounds, infeasible packets, absent external shared-error evidence, or absent independent utility governance are **inconclusive and fail closed**, never rescued as `RECON` or by a favorable default.

## Finite ontology frozen before labels

The normative file `actionability_ontology_v1.yaml`, [source checksum], is supporting material and must be copied byte-for-byte into execution inputs. Governance may reject this ontology before packet construction, but may not amend it after seeing any real label. Rejection gives `NO_SELECTION`; a changed ontology requires a new protocol and sample-independent version.

Two actionability clinicians independently label each inherited unit from packet `C-P`: `C_PORTABLE_ACTIVE`, `C_CHOICE_ACTIVE`, `C_EXPLICIT_COMPLETE`, `C_EXPLICIT_STOP`, `C_UNDETERMINED`, or `C_PACKET_INVALID`. `C-P` contains the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, permitted admission/death/disposition context, relative within-admission chronology, and canonical DS context after independent redaction of every pulmonary target/action/timing/recipient/responsibility/replacement/completion/conflict span. It exposes neither the discharge target-plan realization nor a route cue. A label requires quoted spans and an explicit missing-facts checklist. Portable means only “no supplied-record material choice,” not guideline correctness.

Two separately rostered supersession clinicians independently enumerate from packet `S-P`: exact target linkage; action/modality; timing; polarity; status; and one of `S_EXACT_REPLACEMENT`, `S_EXACT_COMPLETION`, `S_EXACT_STOP`, `S_MODIFY_OR_COEXIST`, `S_RESTATEMENT`, `S_NONE_OBSERVED`, or `S_UNKNOWN`. `S-P` contains only the reciprocal recommendation and every pre-`D` target-linked DS statement with limited local context and relative order. Omission, paraphrase, author/service fields, or intuition cannot establish supersession. Exact supersession requires explicit target linkage, later timing, explicit replacement/completion/stop semantics, and no compatible coexistence/coreference reading.

No consensus overwrites individual labels. Locked code takes the Cartesian product of both C labels, both S labels, redaction audit, packet validity, and inherited boundary states. Any invalidity, unknown, or active-versus-no-action conflict gives `K_U`; any compatible completion/stop/exact replacement gives adverse-compatible `K_N`; wholly active sets with a material choice or modification give `K_R`; only a singleton portable-active set with none/restatement gives `K_P`. Any `K_U`, `K_N`, or `K_R` blocks `AUTO`; mixed `K_P/K_R` can only support inherited `RECON`; all `K_N` remains descriptively no-action but operationally `DEFER`. The new states only remove routes; they never rescue or rewrite an inherited route.

## Calibration-anchored measurement integrity, not clinical utility

Before real packets, an independent methods unit—not any label or route analyst—constructs and hashes 48 matched synthetic/semisynthetic pairs for each task from the ontology's six contrast families, with four independently worded surface forms and two concealed orders per contrast. No text is copied from a real `N_A` admission. Each clinical reviewer receives a balanced 24-pair qualification set; the remaining pairs are interspersed, concealed, as ongoing controls. Pair construction and expected ontology transitions are independently verified against the YAML. The fixtures cover portable versus material choice, active versus exact completion, active versus explicit stop, missing decisive evidence, exact replacement versus coexistence, completion versus restatement, exact target link versus unrelated plan, route-realization invariance, semantic paraphrase, nuisance, and non-target invariance.

Measurement-integrity gates are comparative and calibration-anchored rather than unexplained percentages:

1. schema, Cartesian mapping, all-unit precedence, pair generation, and expected-transition fixtures must have **zero implementation errors**; this is a logical correctness condition, not a clinical-risk tolerance;
2. within each task, use matched binary outcomes and the common max-statistic simultaneous 95% family to require `L95(P(correct decisive transition)) > U95(P(semantic-invariance flip))`; this tests whether the measurement distinguishes ontology signal from its own nuisance instability without asserting that either rate is clinically acceptable;
3. require `U95(route-realization flip) <= U95(matched nuisance/semantic flip)`; route display may not add detectable instability beyond the calibrated measurement floor;
4. report, without dichotomizing, every decisive accuracy, invariance-flip rate, pair difference, reviewer effect, and exact confidence interval. Missing control response is an error.

The 95% familywise level controls the probability of making any declared calibration contrast in the frozen family; it is a statistical error convention, not a stakeholder utility. No fixed `0.02`, `0.025`, `0.05`, `0.90`, or kappa grid is introduced as a new clinical-actionability threshold. Inherited thresholds remain untouched because changing them would redesign the principal experiment.

Real-case route-reveal, route-swap, nuisance/non-target, and exact-supersession controls remain locked and outcome-blind. They are summarized on the same continuous control-error axes. A route-swap or nuisance transformation cannot alter primary labels; infeasible transformations remain explicit and adverse. Passing synthetic controls shows only that reviewers and software respond to the frozen distinctions, not that real labels are clinically true.

## Admission-complete packet and panel feasibility certificate

After inherited deterministic unit inventories and the `<=900` admission draw are frozen but before any clinical label is visible, compute exact workload counts:

`P_real = 4*sum_{i in sample} U_i`,

where every unit receives two C and two S readings. Add all fixed qualification and interspersed control packets, redaction audits, and a 15% prespecified operational reserve; never reduce real-case coverage. A synthetic-only rehearsal with the final software records active reading time for every packet type. For each type use the maximum observed rehearsal time, not a favorable mean, and form

`H_plan = [sum_t P_t*max_time_t + fixed_meeting_and_audit_hours]*1.15/60`.

Before packet generation, independent operations governance must hash: an absolute total physician-hour budget `B_H`; a maximum weekly burden per reviewer; minimum C and S roster qualifications; signed roster-disjoint availability slots; reviewer-to-packet assignment; and an elapsed-calendar deadline. Require `H_plan<=B_H`, every assigned reviewer load below the registered cap, at least two fully independent C and two fully independent S readings for every unit, no roster overlap with inherited roles, and enough reserved slots to complete all selected admissions. Failure gives global `NO_SELECTION`; no apparent `AUTO`, large atom, easy packet, or responder subset may be preferentially reviewed. At analysis report observed time and a simultaneous finite-population upper workload bound by atom and route; an atom exceeding the registered prospective silent-study capacity is excluded. `B_H` and per-reviewer caps are resource constraints chosen and signed by operations, not disguised safety thresholds.

## Shared-error identifiability solved by an integer contamination frontier

MIMIC panel agreement cannot identify shared clinical error. Let `m_g` be the number of finite-population admissions in atom `g` whose true clinical decision need may lie anywhere outside all observed compatible sets because every clinical measurement shares the same error. Do not estimate `m_g` from agreement, disagreement, H1/H2/H3, latent classes, or synthetic controls.

For every integer `m=0,...,N_g`, solve the adverse finite-population program allowing up to `m` atom admissions to be reassigned, prioritizing high-weight records and retaining all units of an admission. Report simultaneous lower/upper curves for candidate-`AUTO` adverse compatibility, no-action/supersession compatibility, candidate-`RECON` active-choice compatibility, capture, and workload. Define each route's **breakdown count** `m*_gr` as the smallest integer at which its inherited-plus-clinical statement ceases to hold, and report `m*/N_g` with uncertainty. This complete curve is threshold-free and avoids an arbitrary kappa grid.

An external bound `M_g` may be used for a conditional nomination only if, before MIMIC labels, an independent governance panel registers a source that is (a) independent of these MIMIC artifacts and reviewers, (b) probability-ascertained in the target atom or explicitly transport-weighted, (c) adjudicated from actual-calendar target-linked workflow evidence unavailable in MIMIC, (d) capable of revealing errors shared by all retrospective panels, and (e) analyzed with a simultaneous finite-population/binomial upper bound. `M_g` is that externally supported upper count after transport uncertainty, not a vote or chosen grid point. If no qualifying source exists—as is currently the case—set the policy bound to `M_g=N_g`; no clinical-actionability route is nominated and the prospective mapper remains `DEFER`. This is an explicit identification result, not a pessimistic estimate.

## Threshold-free utility frontier and independent governance

For each atom, report the joint simultaneous confidence region over finite-population counts of `K_P,K_R,K_N,K_U`, inherited route, packet failure, control instability, physician hours, and every shared-error count `m`. Publish a decision table for actions `{DEFER, silent-AUTO-candidate, silent-RECON-candidate}` over a nonnegative utility vector

`lambda=(cost_portable_sent_to_recon, cost_choice_sent_to_auto, cost_no_action_sent_to_auto, cost_unknown_sent_to_auto, cost_no_action_sent_to_recon, physician_hour_cost)`.

For every `lambda` on a prespecified normalized simplex and every `m`, compute worst-case net utility relative to default `DEFER`, retaining uncertainty and all-unit precedence. Report the robust-positive, robust-negative, and indeterminate regions plus pairwise regret. This frontier is descriptive and threshold-free: it lets stakeholders see exactly which valuation and shared-error assumptions would change a decision.

No analyst, reviewer, sponsor, or model may choose `lambda` after labels. Before any utility-based nomination, a governance body independent of extraction, clinical panels, analysis, and operations must publicly register within the private protocol: named stakeholder representation (patient, pulmonary, transitions/primary care, radiology, safety, operations); conflicts; deliberation method; the exact `lambda` and rationale; whether silent `AUTO` and/or `RECON` is even an admissible action; the external `M_g`; and a signed decision rule. Nomination requires the simultaneous lower worst-case net utility at registered `(lambda,M_g)` to be strictly greater than zero versus `DEFER`, positive lower route burden, every inherited gate, every calibration gate, exact support, and workload feasibility. Equality, missing governance, missing external bound, or wide uncertainty gives `DEFER`. Utilities cannot override a measurement-integrity, support, seal, workload, or inherited safety failure.

## Finite-population computation and no-rescue logic

Retain the principal parent's Horvitz–Thompson totals, Hájek ratios, finite-population Taylor variance, exact hypergeometric/multivariate-hypergeometric inversion, and at least 20,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates within each noncensus design cell. ## Interpretation

**Supportive:** at least one support-qualified atom passes all inherited gates, calibration contrasts, complete clinical measurement, workload feasibility, and finite-population precision, and has a positive shared-error breakdown count. This supports a narrow statement that the frozen text route and route-concealed clinical measurement are distinguishable and robust over the reported `(m,lambda)` frontier. Only if independent pre-label governance also supplies qualifying `M_g` and `lambda`, and worst-case net utility is strictly positive, may that exact atom be nominated for a within-site prospective **silent** bridge. All other signatures remain `DEFER`.

**Adverse:** a supported atom contains observed candidate-`AUTO` units compatible with clinical choice, explicit completion/stop/replacement, or unknown; candidate `RECON` is compatible with no action; route display adds instability; decisive controls are not distinguishable from nuisance flips; a single adverse unit breaks the admission; the breakdown count is zero or below a registered external bound; or required workload exceeds capacity. The atom is excluded even if all text and clinical reviewers agree. Such a negative finding consequentially prevents inappropriate propagation or reconciliation workload.

**Inconclusive:** no feasible atlas within 900 admissions; sparse route events; low ESS; wide simultaneous bounds; packet/control/redaction defects; missing labels; no workload certificate; no qualifying external shared-error bound; or no independent utility registration. Inconclusive never implies safety, stability, actionability, or transport and cannot default to `RECON`; it forces `DEFER/NO_SELECTION`.

## Evidence ceiling and prospective data required

MIMIC cannot establish the artifact visible at physical departure, draft/edit/sign/transmit history, report delivery/viewing/acknowledgement, accountable clinician, patient understanding or preference, outside-system plans, target-linked orders/referrals/scheduling, completed imaging, duplicate imaging, radiation/contrast burden, downstream cascades, malignancy resolution, anxiety, workload displacement, safety, utility, or causal benefit. `storetime` and service codes remain storage/workflow proxies.

A stronger claim requires a separately approved consecutive-admission prospective silent bridge with actual calendar times and immutable event logs for report finalization/addenda, delivery/view/acknowledgement, discharge versions/sign/transmission, physical departure, named responsibility, target-linked replacement rationale, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, patient preference/competing-risk context, and follow-up outcomes. Route-blinded clinical adjudication and the same all-unit adverse rules are required; novel signatures remain `DEFER`. Only a later governed comparative study with prespecified safety monitoring, utilities, sample size, intervention receipt/correction, workload, follow-up completion, unnecessary imaging, patient-reported effects, diagnostic outcomes, and causal estimands could evaluate live clinical benefit.

The substantive advance is not another agreement panel. It is an executable separation of four questions that the repairable parent conflated: whether the ontology is mechanically and semantically measurable; whether every selected admission can actually be reviewed within the fixed design; how conclusions degrade under unidentified shared clinical error; and which stakeholder valuations, if independently registered, would justify only a silent next study. The experiment can therefore falsify actionability without pretending retrospective experts identify truth, and it fails closed wherever MIMIC cannot answer the clinical question.
