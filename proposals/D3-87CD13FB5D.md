# Working proposal (episode 23): Is the MerTK-blockade (UNC106238) response cell-autonomous in myeloid cells, or only manifest in macrophage->FLS co-culture?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis]
(valid; board leader, mu 29.40, gen 7; the D3-M2 reference-complete lineage).
Seed: D3-M2 (directional macrophage->FLS crosstalk), benchmarked by D3-M1 (composition vs
within-cell regulation) and D3-M3 (persistence vs remission).
Status: hypothesis-stage, data-bound. Screening in THIS episode is PLANNED, not executed.
NO confirmatory/causal result is claimed.

## 1. Unresolved question and why the redirection is non-duplicative
The D3-M2 lineage is saturated with E-MTAB-8873 co-culture evolutions (prior episode-22
critique: the leader is textually near-duplicate of earlier D3-M2 candidates). The parent's
own recorded residual limitation is that "UNC106238 selectivity and direct MERTK engagement
are untestable here" because the E-MTAB-8873 panel is a 445-feature targeted assay of a
co-cULTURE, so any inhibitor effect is confounded by the FLS partner and by composition.

This episode instead asks a *narrower, separable* question using an under-used dataset:

  Does MerTK blockade (UNC106238) produce a reproducible, donor-paired transcript response
  in MYELOID CELLS ALONE (E-MTAB-8316), or is an inhibitor-sensitive "resolution" program
  only observable when macrophages are co-cultured with FLS (E-MTAB-8873)?

This tests cell-autonomy (a cell-type-localization claim) rather than re-parameterizing the
co-culture contrast. K1 [Alivernini 2020] motivates the axis clinically (MerTK+CD206+ state
associated with drug-free remission) but does not test whether the axis is engaged
cell-autonomously. K2 [ontogeny preprint] gives the leading rival: stromal/FLS instruction of
myeloid state - if the monocyte-only response is weak but a co-culture response is strong,
that pattern favours a non-cell-autonomous (stroma-dependent) mechanism. K3 [Ghosh 2024]
bounds interpretation: an UNC106238-sensitive response is paralog/inhibitor sensitivity, not
proof of a MERTK-specific step.

## 2. Clinical importance
If the resolution-associated MerTK axis is engaged cell-autonomously in myeloid cells,
MerTK-directed pharmacology could act on the macrophage compartment independently of the
stromal niche; if it requires the FLS niche, a monocyte-only drug readout would mislead
biomarker and target-selection decisions. This is a consequential, falsifiable distinction.

## 4. Estimand and method
Unit: donor-paired log2 CPM pseudobulk vector per monocyte donor (none vs UNC106238),
E-MTAB-8316. Estimand: the mean within-donor UNC106238 effect on the monocyte transcriptome
and its reproducibility across donors (sign concordance / rank correlation across the 6 pairs).

Baseline: a pooled (donor-ignoring) difference of means + a sign-flip permutation null on
the data as-is. This baseline cannot separate a reproducible donor-consistent drug effect
from single-donor dominance - exactly the information the alternative must recover.

Substantive alternative: a donor-aware mixed model y ~ condition + (1|donor) on per-gene
log2 CPM with empirical-Bayes shrinkage, plus a low-rank (rank 1-3) factor model on the
donor x gene effect matrix, evaluated by leave-one-monocyte-donor-out. This reveals whether a
shared, cross-donor MerTK-blockade program exists versus donor-specific/idiosyncratic shifts.

Discriminating comparison for cell-autonomy: fit the same donor-aware model to the 5 donor-
matched Mpos+inhibitor vs Mpos+none pairs in E-MTAB-8873 (inherited binding) and compare the
paired effect-vector DIRECTION and reproducibility across the two datasets. Cell-autonomy is
supported if the monocyte-only effect is reproducible and its direction agrees with the
co-culture effect; it is challenged if the monocyte-only effect is absent/inconsistent while
the co-culture effect is robust (favouring a niche/stroma-dependent mechanism, K2).

## 5. Outcomes
Supportive: a reproducible, donor-consistent UNC106238 effect in monocyte-only E-MTAB-8316
(sign concordance across pairs, non-trivial low-rank structure surviving leave-one-donor-out)
with a direction consistent with the co-culture effect.
Adverse: no reproducible donor-consistent monocyte-only effect (effect dominated by one donor,
low-rank structure absent) while the co-culture contrast remains robust -> cell-autonomy
rejected; the axis is niche/co-culture-dependent (K2-favoured).
Inconclusive: small but non-zero monocyte-only effect swamped by donor variance, or only
4 donors / limited pairs -> report CIs, do not treat an imprecise null as refutation.

## 6. Falsification criteria
The cell-autonomy hypothesis is falsified if, at a pre-specified effect threshold, the
monocyte-only paired effect cannot be distinguished from the sign-flip null at the donor
level AND leave-one-donor-out reproducibility is at/below the pooled baseline; it is
weakened (not falsified) if the effect exists but its direction conflicts with the
co-culture effect.

## 7. Honest limits

- E-MTAB-8316 has NO LPS-free arm, so LPS-independent effects cannot be isolated.
