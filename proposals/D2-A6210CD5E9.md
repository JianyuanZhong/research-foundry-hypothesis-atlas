# MERTK archival evidence integrity: a target-development gate

**Episode 17 evolution of `[prior hypothesis]`.** Source/protocol audit and experiment design only; this episode has not rerun the expression scores or executed wet-lab/target-engagement work. The consequential decision is whether to treat a small published RNA contrast as evidence for MERTK-directed therapeutic development, or defer that rationale until compound identity and cell-compartment readout are adjudicated.

## What is known and what remains falsifiable

The strongest existing biological claim is context-dependent rather than uniformly directional. MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets are associated with RA remission and in-vitro fibroblast repair [K1]. A newer RA-FLS study reports MERTK/GAS6 knockdown attenuating several pathogenic FLS phenotypes [K2], while macrophage–fibroblast resolution literature supports measuring repair/efferocytosis separately from inflammatory output [K3]. These works motivate opposing compartment-specific predictions; they do not establish that MERTK inhibition is selective, beneficial, safe, or clinically effective.

Separately, E-MTAB-8316 source records report `UNC106238` at 250 µM, 2 h inhibitor exposure at the end of 4 h LPS stimulation in macrophages, PBS wash, then 48 h transwell coculture with FLS harvested for RNA. Its SDRF `Characteristics[cell type]` says monocyte while the linked description/protocol says FLS RNA is read out. E-MTAB-8873 reports a different assay and string (`UNC1062`) at 250 nM; it does not validate the 8316 reagent or dose. These are submitted annotations, not contemporaneous laboratory confirmation.

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** A deterministic recheck of the 12 E-MTAB-8316 libraries will find internally concordant matrix/design/SDRF joins and six vehicle–inhibitor pairs consistent with the reported macrophage-pre-treatment/FLS-RNA protocol, but will retain the source label conflict and lack any evidence validating actual compound identity/exposure or MERTK engagement. If the sample keys, condition pairs, or FLS readout cannot be reconciled, the parent score result is not correctly bound and must be repaired/excluded before interpretation. Concordance establishes annotation consistency only; it cannot prove what was pipetted or identify a mechanism.

The strongest rival to target-specific biology is compound/protocol/assay artifact: a wrong compound/unit annotation, off-target activity, high-dose toxicity, residual compound or nonspecific macrophage effects, plus donor/technical variation. PBS wash is reported but wash efficacy and carry-over were not measured. A macrophage-state-mixture account could explain remission-associated bulk macrophage differences, but cannot alone explain this FLS endpoint; 8316 contains no single-cell states. Association after adjustment would not establish mechanism.

The parent’s executed six-configuration reanalysis found the fixed inflammatory-associated score increased and ECM-associated score decreased in every inhibitor-minus-vehicle comparison. This is adverse/mixed to the archived RNA rationale, not evidence of MERTK-specific benefit or harm. There are four FLS donor clusters (`SA049`, `SA114`, `SA051`, `SA104`) and six nested monocyte specimens; six configurations are not six independent FLS donors. The current proposal preserves that result and does not repeat it.

## Interpretation and decision rules

- **Supportive:** Harbor joins/pairs/readout are internally consistent and independent lab records confirm compound, lot, dose/unit, schedule and FLS harvest. This only improves archive interpretation. Continue only to prospective engagement and orthogonal functional testing after expert review of a plausible non-toxic range.
- **Adverse:** reproducible matrix/design/SDRF key or pair mismatch, unresolved readout conflict, or source confirmation of wrong compound/dose/cell assignment. Repair computable binding if possible; otherwise exclude this archive as MERTK-specific evidence and park its compound-based rationale. This does not refute MERTK biology.
- **Inconclusive:** metadata agree but no original lab records, actual exposure, target engagement, wash/selectivity, viability, or functional endpoints are available, or donor uncertainty is wide. Keep “as annotated” language and defer; an imprecise null is not falsification.

Computational checks include exact joins, field values, duplicate/mate handling, pairing/donor structure, source-text concordance, and output-linked reporting. Compound actually used, cell purity, target-specific mechanism, therapeutic window, patient benefit and systemic safety require source/expert review, fresh donor-aware orthogonal loss/rescue, and further translational/clinical studies. The advance over prior work is a falsifiable data-integrity gate that prevents ambiguous pharmacology from being promoted as target-specific evidence—not a novel mechanism claim.
