# Can an all-hour excess-FiO2 signal survive phenotype and oxygen-record uncertainty without selecting on same-hour oxygenation?

## Status, scientific opening, and deliverable

This is a planned crossover of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`; no mortality association or model performance is reported. It preserves the former parent's pre-hour-24-standardized, all-hour exposure estimand and the latter parent's measurement-first phenotype/source-error design. It removes the latter parent's fatal same-hour adequacy filter: oxygenation observed at or after an FiO2 setting is a possible response, never a condition for that hour to enter the primary exposure.

ICU-ROX shows that an oxygen-target protocol can reduce oxygen exposure without significantly changing ventilator-free days in a broad ventilated ICU population [K1]. A mechanistic review distinguishes inspired hyperoxia from arterial hyperoxaemia and describes experimental oxygen–mechanical-stress synergy while emphasizing inconsistent clinical mortality evidence [K2]. LUNG SAFE shows incomplete, severity-dependent clinician recognition even when prospective Berlin-definition components were collected [K3]. The strongest supported claim is therefore limited: oxygen exposure is modifiable; inspired exposure and arterial oxygen are not interchangeable; and pulmonary-injury labels can be severity-dependent. These works do not establish oxygen toxicity in eICU, validate this pulmonary proxy, or validate recorded FiO2 as delivered airway FiO2.

The unresolved clinical hypothesis is:

**Among adult first ICU stays alive and still in ICU at hour 36, with a PaO2 80–120 mm Hg paired to FiO2 during hours 18–24, the pre-hour-24 acute-pulmonary-injury proxy modifies the association between mean all-hour excess FiO2 during hours 24–36 and seven-day post-hour-36 in-hospital death; a positive pulmonary interaction will remain distinguishable from phenotype/FiO2 error, evolving severity, clinician response, ventilation co-treatment, hospital practice, and observation processes.**

The primary estimand is descriptive: the pulmonary-minus-comparator difference in pre-hour-24-standardized seven-day death cumulative-incidence risk-difference slope per +0.10 in
`B_i=(1/12) sum_t (F_it-0.40)+` over all 12 exposure hours. Live hospital discharge competes. `F_it` is the true hour-level setting represented by an interval when uncertain. No same-hour SpO2, SaO2, or PaO2 determines whether an hour contributes. This is an exposure-response contrast among hour-36 survivors, not an identified treatment effect.

The new measurement hypothesis, tested first, is:

**Using blinded clinical labels, cross-source FiO2 pairs, and all-hour logical bounds, the simultaneous upper 95% bound on a pulmonary interaction producible by joint phenotype/FiO2 ascertainment alone is <1.5 absolute seven-day risk points per +0.10 excess FiO2, and the burden is measured precisely enough in both strata to resolve a clinically consequential 3-point interaction.**

Failure is informative: it means eICU cannot distinguish the proposed interaction from its measurement process under the frozen assumptions. It ends pulmonary-toxicity interpretation without changing the population, exposure hours, or outcome.

The required scientific deliverable is newly fitted/estimated output: a frozen cohort and 12-hour panel; phenotype review estimates; source-error and missing-hour burden intervals; transparent measurement/error envelopes; identical hospital folds; a calibrated ordered latent/sequential model; competing-risk interaction estimates; rival-prediction and falsification matrices; and a gate-governed supportive, adverse, or inconclusive conclusion. Completion never requires confirmation.

## Why this could change a clinical decision

A robust pulmonary-specific delayed signal would justify prospective phenotype validation and stratification in a future oxygen-titration trial. A precise adverse result would argue against enriching a trial with this eICU proxy. Broad all-hour bounds, source-dependent reversal, or failed phenotype validation would redirect resources toward device/chart validation rather than an oxygen-restriction trial. A signal explained by clinician response or ventilation would redirect the hypothesis toward treatment selection or combined ventilator management.

Leading and rival predictions are frozen before outcomes:

| Explanation | Distinguishing prediction |
|---|---|
| Delayed oxygen–mechanical-stress compatibility | After a pre-hour-24-standardized comparison, excess FiO2 precedes delayed deterioration; its lagged interaction with pressure/PEEP is stronger in validated injury; it survives source/hospital envelopes. |
| Evolving severity | Hypoxaemia, shock, or respiratory worsening precedes FiO2 escalation; pre-escalation deterioration predicts later burden; temporal representation removes the pulmonary pattern. |
| Clinician response | Treatment-head residuals and pre-decision state explain escalation; outcome association concentrates in settings strongly predicted from prior observed severity. |
| Ventilation co-treatment | Pressure/PEEP/tidal-volume/support changes travel with FiO2; ventilation burden, not oxygen lag, explains the transition or the oxygen interaction is not separable. |
| Observation practice | Burden or interaction follows chart presence, time since chart, source, entry delay, or hospital; downsampling/source balancing reproduces or reverses it. |
| Phenotype error | Review-supported injury crosses P0 strata differentially by severity/site; propagated label error can manufacture the interaction. |

Unmeasured intent, ambiguous simultaneous chart times, prior oxygen before hour 24, and unavailable delivered-airway measurements can leave rivals indistinguishable even after all models pass.

## Population, clocks, phenotype, outcome

Index `patient.patientunitstayid`. Include age >=18 (map `age='> 89'` to 90 for adjustment only), the first ICU stay in each `patienthealthsystemstayid` by lowest valid `unitvisitnumber` then `patientunitstayid`, and `unitdischargeoffset>2160`. Require alive/in ICU at minute 2160. Exclude impossible chronology, burns, documented extracorporeal support by minute 1440, and exact `carePlanGeneral.cplgroup='Care Limitation'`, `cplitemvalue='Comfort measures only'` by minute 1440. DNR, no-intubation, no-CPR, and EOL-discussion rows are retained as baseline indicators and sensitivities, not equated with comfort-only care.

Immutable boundaries:

- baseline and phenotype: ICU admission through minute 1440;
- anchor: last valid PaO2 80–120 in minutes 1080–1440, paired to closest valid FiO2 within ±60 minutes; equal-distance ties prefer preceding, then earlier FiO2;
- exposure: minute 1440 inclusive to 2160 exclusive, exactly 12 hourly bins;
- outcome origin: minute 2160;
- follow-up: `(2160,12240]`, seven days after hour 36.

P0 acute-pulmonary-injury proxy requires by minute 1440 both a frozen diagnosis/admission term (ARDS/acute respiratory distress, pneumonia, aspiration, pulmonary oedema, or acute respiratory failure) and anchor P/F <=300. Primary comparator has no frozen acute term, regardless of P/F. Term/PF>300 is discordant validation; no-term/PF>300 is a sparse sensitivity. The proxy is not Berlin ARDS.

Primary outcome is hospital death after minute 2160 through 12240 using `hospitaldischargestatus='Expired'` and `hospitaldischargeoffset`. Live hospital discharge in the interval is competing; still hospitalized at day seven is administrative censoring. Report Aalen–Johansen risks. Secondary ICU death and hour-36–72 respiratory deterioration are subordinate and require timestamp coverage. `actualventdays` is never a timestamped endpoint.

## Stage 1: measurement before association

### 1A. Outcome-blind census and clinician anchor

Publish cohort flow and, by P0 stratum, source, hospital, support class, and hour: valid/invalid FiO2, saturation, ABG, ventilator-setting and haemodynamic coverage; source disagreements; duplicates; event-to-entry/revision delays; time since last FiO2; diagnosis-source overlap; and hospital label/coverage distributions.

The inherited outcome-blind feasibility audit found 1,869 anchored stays: 804 P0-positive, 965 no-term, 100 discordant. Injury versus no-term had >=6 recorded FiO2 hours in 420/804 (52.2%) versus 454/965 (47.0%). Across 47 hospitals with >=10 anchored stays, P0 prevalence ranged 0.171–0.938, >=6-hour FiO2 coverage 0–1.00, and exposure-window ABG prevalence 0.063–0.739. These facts establish serious observability variation, not outcome association or differential error.

Review pre-minute-1440 packets only. Remove hospital, outcome, post-hour-24 data, P0 label, and exposure summary. Phase I: 160 balanced packets for one outcome-blind P0-to-P1 revision. Phase II: new probability sample of 600 (300 P1-positive/300 negative), stratified by P/F band, support, hospital label/coverage quartiles, and source signature, at most one packet per `uniquepid`. Two ICU clinicians independently review 30%, all disagreements/indeterminate and source-poor sentinels; a third arbitrates. They classify available-record-supported acute parenchymal injury (definite/probable/absent/indeterminate), support class, hydrostatic-only compatibility, and evidence timing. Report weighted PPV/sensitivity/specificity/NPV, indeterminate rate, agreement, and simultaneous subgroup intervals. Review validates consistency with available eICU evidence, not true ARDS.

### 1B. All-hour FiO2 construction without adequacy selection

Normalize FiO2 0.21–1.00 directly and 21–100 by division by 100; reject other values. Respiratory charting is the primary setting source. Lab FiO2 and `respiratoryCare.setapneafio2` are validation channels. Within source/hour use median event-time value. A prior respiratory setting may support a bin for at most 120 minutes; never carry future values backward. Record source, age, and entry/revision delay. Values from different sources within 30 minutes are concordance pairs; never average a discordant pair before error estimation.

For every stay and every one of 12 hours, construct `I_it=[L_it,U_it]` for excess `(F_it-0.40)+`:

- valid respiratory value: source/age-specific calibrated error interval, clipped to [0,0.60];
- multiple concordant sources: intersection only if nonempty; otherwise their union and a discordance flag;
- lab or respiratory-care value without respiratory chart: a separately calibrated validation-source interval, never presumed equivalent;
- no supported source: logical interval [0,0.60].

Thus `B_i` always exists as `[sum L_it/12, sum U_it/12]`. Missing hours are neither dropped nor coded as room air. Same-hour oxygenation is retained only as a post-setting response/observation variable. Strictly preceding oxygenation may label pre-decision state for temporal rivals and a secondary dynamic scenario, but never changes primary `I_it`.

### 1C. M0 transparent error envelope

M0 uses the same 12-hour panel, P1 review weights, outcome, folds, and pre-hour-24 covariates as M1. For each of 2,000 hospital-cluster bootstrap draws:

1. draw phenotype confusion parameters from simultaneous Phase-II sets, with indeterminate labels at both adverse vertices;
2. draw observed-source error from paired residual distributions stratified by source, source age, support, P0, and hospital-coverage quartile;
3. retain [0,0.60] for unsupported hours in the logical envelope; a separately labeled validation-bounded envelope uses empirical transition limits learned without outcomes;
4. profile the competing-risk interaction over latent P and `B_i` values allowed by the envelope, reporting lower/upper standardized slope and risk contrasts;
5. under a null in which mortality depends on baseline severity, hospital and latent pulmonary status but not FiO2, preserve fitted label/observation/source processes and estimate the maximum spurious pulmonary interaction.

Report the logical burden-width distribution, validation-bounded width, identification interval for the observed interaction, and simultaneous 95% error-only envelope. M0 is primary and auditable. Any tightening assumption is shown as a staircase, never substituted silently for logical bounds. M0 loses within-window order and cannot separate clinician response, co-treatment, and observation transitions.

## Stage 2: ordered latent/sequential alternative on identical inputs

Only if the source/phenotype prerequisites permit estimation, fit S1 on the identical stays, 12 hours, raw fields, P1 sample, outcomes, and five frozen hospital-held-out folds. No complete-case restriction and no same-hour adequacy selection are allowed.

Within hour t the order is explicit:

1. **Pre-decision state `S_t`:** inferred only from history ending before the first FiO2 setting time in that bin—prior FiO2, strictly prior PaO2/SaO2/SpO2, pH/PaCO2, haemodynamics, vasopressors, prior ventilation/support, and time-since-observation.
2. **Clinician-response head:** models latent FiO2 `F_t` from `S_t`, observed history, prior treatment, baseline P, and hierarchical hospital practice.
3. **Ventilation co-treatment head:** models support/PEEP/pressure/tidal-volume/rate changes jointly with FiO2. If order within a bin is ambiguous, treat them as a joint action; do not call one a confounder of the other.
4. **Observation head:** models whether/when FiO2 sources, ABG, saturation, ventilator settings, diagnoses, notes, and examinations appear, conditional on past observed state, action, and hospital.
5. **Response transition:** after action, predicts next-hour respiratory/hemodynamic state. Lagged excess FiO2, ventilation burden, and a prespecified oxygen × lagged-pressure/PEEP interaction enter here, never the pre-treatment state.
6. After hour 36, competing death/discharge heads use the inferred history.

Latent `F_t` emits respiratory-chart, lab, and respiratory-care values with source/hospital error. P1 clinician labels anchor pre-hour-24 latent pulmonary class. Outcome labels are withheld while treatment, source, observation, support-state, and next-hour physiology components are fitted. S1 must reproduce M0 observed-data contrasts on shared support and pass held-hospital calibration for FiO2 pairs, clinician labels, treatment density, observation hazards, support state, next-hour PaO2/SpO2, and event risk.

S1's primary scientific addition is decomposition, not predictive gain. Compare:

- FiO2 association before versus after representing clinician-response propensity;
- oxygen lag versus ventilation co-treatment and oxygen×mechanical-stress transition terms;
- complete-observation versus source-downsampled posterior predictions;
- outcome association beginning before escalation, 1–3 hours after, or later.

A secondary g-computation scenario caps `F_t` at 0.40 only when adequacy was documented strictly before the treatment decision; hypoxaemic and unknown hours remain observed. It is not the primary all-hour estimand and is called a model-based scenario under sequential exchangeability, consistency, positivity, ordering, and measurement assumptions—not a trial effect.

A structured Bayesian state-space model or constrained variational recurrent model is acceptable. Architecture is frozen by outcome-blind calibration, not AUROC. M1 cannot override M0 or rescue a failed gate.

## Baseline outcome analysis and matched evaluation

B0 is a cause-specific pooled logistic death/live-discharge model with restricted cubic spline for interval-valued all-hour burden, P1 pulmonary probability/stratum and interaction. Standardization uses only minute-1440-fixed variables: age, sex, ethnicity, admission source/type, unit type; hospital region/size/teaching and partial-pooling intercept; APACHE IV score/predicted mortality; anchor PaO2/FiO2/PF; baseline pH, PaCO2, lactate, renal/hepatic/haematologic measures; vasopressor/support status; pre-hour-24 PEEP/pressure/tidal-volume/rate; comorbidity/diagnostic groups; and treatment limitations. Report unadjusted and standardized Aalen–Johansen risks, hospital-cluster uncertainty, calibration, overlap, and exposure intervals.

A frozen attribution ladder adds hours 18–24 trends, then hour-24–36 severity/oxygenation, ventilation, and observation summaries. These are changed conditional contrasts, not total-effect repairs. Persistence does not prove toxicity; attenuation may reflect confounding control, mediation removal, measurement error, or collider conditioning.

All models use five folds grouped by `hospitalid`; `uniquepid` never crosses folds. Evaluate burden precision, calibration, Brier score/integrated calibration index, source agreement, treatment and observation calibration, physiology error, overlap/ESS, interaction interval, and error-only envelope. Small AUROC gain is irrelevant.

## Hard gates and falsification

Thresholds are frozen before outcomes and never relaxed:

1. Phase-II weighted P1 PPV and sensitivity each >=0.80 with simultaneous lower 95% bounds >=0.70; indeterminate <=15% overall and <=25% by documentation quartile. Simultaneous PPV/sensitivity differences across documentation quartiles/support classes <=0.15.
2. Paired-source median absolute FiO2 difference <=0.05, 90th percentile <=0.10, disagreement-probability difference by stratum/documentation quartile <=0.10; >=150 pairs per stratum from >=20 hospitals.
3. Report logical bounds for everyone. For association resolution, the validation-bounded median `B_i` width must be <=0.10 in each stratum and its 75th percentile <=0.15; no source family or five hospitals may provide >50% of weighted exposure information. Failure is an exposure-measurement result, not grounds to exclude sparse stays.
4. Both strata require >=40 seven-day deaths; generalized-propensity ESS >=100 per stratum and >=5% support in every prespecified decile.
5. M0 simultaneous upper 95% bound on maximum error-only interaction must be <1.5 risk points per +0.10. The observed clinically consequential interaction must remain distinguishable from zero and the error envelope. S1 must pass every held-hospital calibration gate and remain inside M0 measurement bounds.
6. Direction cannot be determined by five hospitals, event-versus-entry clock, one FiO2 source, or high-coverage sites.

Negative controls: hour-24–36 burden must not predict deterioration defined wholly in hours 12–18 after baseline adjustment; entry delay and LPM-O2 conditional on FiO2 must not reproduce the interaction; moving diagnosis cutoff from event to entry time must not create it. Report term-family removal, strict ARDS terms, P/F 200/300, invasive-support-only, source downsampling, lab-versus-respiratory FiO2, threshold 0.30/0.50/0.60, and high-coverage hospitals jointly. None may be promoted post hoc.

**Supportive:** all measurement/calibration/overlap gates pass; M0 and admissible S1 show a positive pulmonary interaction whose 95% lower bound exceeds the error envelope and includes approximately >=3 risk points per +0.10; FiO2 precedes delayed deterioration; ventilation and observation rivals do not reproduce it. This supports prospective validation/trial stratification, not causation.

**Adverse:** corrected estimates precisely exclude a 3-point interaction; the error envelope reproduces it; phenotype/source error is differential; temporal association begins before escalation; ventilation co-treatment explains it; or no pulmonary specificity remains. This argues against the proposed enrichment rationale.

**Inconclusive:** logical/validation bounds remain broad, review is unavailable, events/overlap/calibration fail, M0 and S1 disagree, or hospitals dominate. An imprecise null does not refute oxygen toxicity. Measurement failure is still a completed Stage-1 finding.

## Clinical adjudication, verification, resources, alternatives

Computationally checkable: cohort/clocks, range rules, joins, all-hour intervals, source pairs, review sampling weights, confusion tables, burden widths, bootstrap/error simulations, folds, calibration, overlap, competing-risk estimates, uncertainty, negative controls, and whether reported conclusions obey gates. An automatic verifier must reject numerically correct outputs with unsupported toxicity/ARDS/treatment claims and accept appropriately adverse or inconclusive interpretations.

Clinician review can assess whether available pre-hour-24 eICU evidence supports acute parenchymal injury and verify extracted pairs. It cannot establish bilateral opacities, exclude hydrostatic oedema uniformly, recover clinician intent, verify delivered airway FiO2, or establish oxidative injury. Those require images/full source charts, device validation, expert review, or a prospective study. Readiness verifies packaging and bounded probes, not the scientific result.

Measured discovery work reused an 8-CPU, approximately 62-second outcome-blind source audit. Future estimates are unverified: panel/review packets 1–2 hours on 8–16 CPUs/64–128 GiB; M0 and 2,000 cluster bootstraps 2–4 hours on 16 CPUs; S1 1–3 hours per fold on one allocated A100-80GB plus 8 CPUs/64 GiB, with checkpoints, or slower CPU Bayesian fitting. These fit the configured future-solver envelope (16 CPUs, up to 8 GPUs, 262,144 MiB, 28,800 seconds) only computationally; clinician review is external. GPU use is selected for repeated sequential fits, not scientific merit.

Unchosen alternatives: same-hour adequacy conditioning is rejected because it selects on a potential response; complete-case restriction is sensitivity only; coding missing FiO2 as 0.21 is rejected; saturation 100% cannot quantify arterial hyperoxaemia [K2]; diagnosis-only and P/F-only phenotypes are insufficient; a hospital-preference instrument is not credible; capture–recapture without clinical anchors is deferred because sources are dependent; marginal structural modeling is revisited only if hourly treatment/censoring positivity and ordering pass; a simpler hidden Markov model is acceptable if it meets identical targets, but a generic mortality RNN is rejected because predictive gain cannot distinguish rivals. Revisit a Berlin-ARDS claim only with imaging, PEEP/timing, and cardiac-failure adjudication.

Demonstration dispositions: Delphi is not selected because disease-token sequencing does not address source error or minute-level treatment ordering; ALADYNOULLI motivates longitudinal latent-state fitting but S1 is a bounded EHR-only adaptation, not a genetic or methodological reproduction; Oncoformer is not selected because images are unavailable and multimodal prediction does not resolve this measurement question.
