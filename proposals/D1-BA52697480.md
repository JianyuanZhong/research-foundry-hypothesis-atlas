## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats serum AFP. The program's
central claim is that a *rising within-patient AFP trajectory* adds prognostic information for time to local
progression beyond baseline AFP and beyond liver-reserve (ALBI) kinetics measured in the SAME draws. This bears on the
consequential decision "should serial AFP drive risk-adapted imaging intensity after LDT?" Parent A attacks outcome
misclassification of the lexical progression endpoint; parent B attacks informative measurement of the exposure.
These two threats are *mutually conditional*: A's bias correction assumes the AFP-draw process is non-informative and
B's observation correction assumes the outcome is validly classified. Neither parent can tell whether a surviving
signal is genuine biology or the residue of the *other*, untouched threat. That joint gap is the unresolved question.

## 2. Hypothesis (falsifiable)
- H1 (leading claim, inherited): a rising within-patient AFP slope on (index, t*] is associated with shorter time to
  radiologic local progression, adjusted for baseline AFP and same-draw ALBI slope.
- Rival R* (tested here): the surviving association is an artifact of the *un-corrected* threat — i.e. it survives
  misclassification correction only because measurement is informative, or survives observation correction only
  because the endpoint is misclassified (or both), so no single-threat correction is sufficient.

## 3. Inherited design (verbatim; not re-litigated)
- Population: HCC diagnosis (`diagnoses.诊断名称` contains 肝细胞癌/肝癌) AND >=1 liver-directed procedure.
- Join key `患者主索引`+`就诊号`. Index = earliest qualifying liver-directed procedure; landmark t* = index + 180 d (90 d sensitivity).
- Exposure: within-patient AFP and ALBI slopes fit ONLY on draws in (index, t*]; same-draw AFP + albumin + total-bilirubin
  pairing within +/-2 d on `labs.检验时间`; ALBI = 0.66*log10(bilirubin) - 0.085*albumin.
- Endpoint: modality-restricted abdominal local progression derived lexically from `examinations`
  (`检查所见`/`检查诊断`: 增大/进展/复发/新发/增多) with 转移 as competing event; retreatment proxy secondary;
  censor at `encounters.出院时间`. Four inherited rivals; conjunctive falsification; **no survival / no mRECIST claim.**

## 4. THE crossover element: a 2x2 threat-survival decision
On the SAME landmark cohort, compute the per-1-SD AFP-slope log-HR under four cells:
(1) naive (M4 only); (2) outcome-misclassification corrected (A: Se[0.70,0.95], Sp[0.80,0.98] Monte-Carlo, 20k draws);
(3) observation-process corrected (B: M5 joint model, IIW fallback); (4) **double-corrected** (compose IIW weights with the
misclassification bias propagation). Plus the **specificity/negative-control test** from B, strengthened with an
a-priori non-HCC, imaging-ascertained outcome (胆囊炎 cholecystitis and 肺炎 pneumonia, counted in this-session probe at
2,355 and 1,896 examination lines): a genuine tumor-activity signal must be *endpoint-specific* and must not predict the
negative-control event with the same sign/magnitude, since the negative-control event shares the observation process but
not HCC biology. Pre-specify a non-specificity margin m=0.15 on |logHR_NC - logHR_primary|.

### Falsification branches
- **Supportive**: audit agreement >= 0.80 (A) AND cell (2) HR CI excludes 1 AND cell (3) HR CI excludes 1 within a
  pre-set attenuation bound |Delta log-HR| <= 0.10 with pre-specified sign AND the negative-control HR CI includes 1
  (specificity satisfied) AND monotone quartile trend persists (p_trend<0.05, Q2-vs-Q1 CI excludes 1, >=30 events).
- **Discriminating-adverse (the new outcome this crossover creates)**: the signal survives exactly ONE of cells (2)/(3)
  but not the other, or the negative-control HR is non-null with the same sign within margin m -> the residual association
  is attributed to the *un-corrected* threat; claim downgraded to "ascertained association only".
- **Adverse (both-threat)**: cell (4) CI includes 1, OR the effect is confined to reports failing adjudication, OR the
  draw-count-only model explains the signal.
- **Inconclusive**: audit agreement < 0.80 (escalate to expert radiologist mRECIST adjudication or another study);
  M5 non-convergence without a valid IIW fallback; <30 interval-scheduled events; <20 negative-control events
  (then the specificity test is inconclusive, NOT adverse); <10/stratum (tertile fallback pre-declared).
- **Precise-null**: cells (2)-(4) all equivalent to M0 within +/-0.01 C-index.

## 5. Method comparison (recorded before selection)
- M0 (baseline): baseline-value landmark Cox (baseline AFP + baseline ALBI + age/sex/procedure type).
- M4 (parent primary): landmark Cox with mixed-model AFP-slope BLUP (recovers change-vs-level; shrinks sparse draws).
- M5 (B): shared-parameter joint model of the AFP-draw visiting process and progression hazard (+ IIW cross-check).
- Double-corrected cell (4): composes M5/IIW with the A misclassification propagation.
- What the added cells reveal that M0/M4 lose: whether a surviving trajectory signal is genuine tumor biology or the
  residue of an un-corrected ascertainment/observation process — a scientific uncertainty, not a predictive-gain chase.
