# Terminal convergent-validity audit for eICU ventilation care-plan labels

## Substantive child and clinical advance

This proposal is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent showed that sparse eICU care-plan receipt and the evaluation-versus-no-trial choice do not have reliable multicenter fitness under target-independent denominator reconstruction, variant-specific first-stay freezing, bounded explicit-airway definitions, and held-out-hospital validation. This child asks the narrower, clinically consequential question that remains scientifically justified: can the sparse care-plan object be validated by exact temporal convergence with a literal respiratory-workflow object in the same source system?

The strongest claim supported before this child is about structured documentation: the care-plan labels are sparse, site concentrated, and not transportably informative for the inherited post-classification chart proxy. The untested claim is not that either object describes bedside care, but that a target-independent eligible landmark or its exact frozen care-plan target anchor would recur in the same ICU stay and hospital near literal `RT Vent On/Off` transition values. If that convergence were reproducible across sites and preserved in bounded-airway variants, it would support a site-calibrated structured-measurement object for further validation. If the workflow systems have no hospital overlap or no estimable links, the measurement-validation lineage is terminal for this snapshot rather than an invitation to repeat weak association analyses.

No conclusion here concerns actual ventilation, spontaneous breathing trial delivery, daily extubation evaluation, extubation, liberation, quality, benefit, or causality. `Start`, `Off`, and `Suspended` are literal structured values and are kept separate; they are not treated as device-verified or bedside states.

## Temporal estimands and falsification

For every denominator row, the analysis records any RT Vent On/Off workflow row within `[L-1440,L+1440]`, decisive clusters within that interval and within `[L-360,L+360]`, and decisive clusters within `[L-360,L+360]` around the landmark. For target rows it additionally records links within `[anchor-360,anchor+360]`, anchor-specific Start/Off/Suspended indicators, Start in `[anchor-360,anchor)`, and Off or Suspended in `(anchor,anchor+360]`. The inherited care-plan window remains `[L,L+360]`; no outcome or RT event is used to select the denominator or label.

A symmetric stay-matched sham analysis centers windows at `anchor-1440` and `anchor+1440`, requiring each complete +/-360-minute window to lie within `[0,unitdischargeoffset]`. Observed target-anchor linkage is compared with both sham rates. Wilson 95% intervals quantify sparse rates. Repeated landmarks within a stay are correlated and are not independent causal observations.

A variant is supportive only if it has at least three hospitals with at least five targets and at least one linked target, at least ten linked targets overall, and observed linkage above both eligible shams. Overall support requires the unbounded variant and at least two of the three bounded variants to pass. A terminal adverse gate is prespecified for zero full-source hospital overlap between the exact-unbounded population and decisive RT workflow, or fewer than three unbounded hospitals satisfying target/link support. Otherwise the result is inconclusive. This gate is a measurement-fitness gate, not a clinical-null gate.

## Interpretation and stopping rule

The substantive advance over the parent is a convergent-validity test that does not alter eligibility, label timing, bounded-airway definitions, or semantic restrictions. It shows that the proposed RT crossover is not merely weakly associated: in this snapshot, the exact-airway care-plan denominator and literal decisive RT workflow have disjoint hospital support, making site-specific calibration and held-out transport impossible. This closes this particular structured-data measurement-validation lineage for eICU 2.0 rather than supporting repeated searches for a favorable association.

A supportive result would have justified a new, explicitly site-calibrated validation study. An adverse result, observed here, means the care-plan object must not be treated as a validated multicenter surrogate or used unqualified for cohorting, quality measurement, or decision support. An inconclusive result would have required more overlapping sites or data; the present zero-overlap gate is stronger than inconclusive for this design.

The result cannot establish bedside truth. Clinical adjudication, independently time-resolved device and airway data, direct SBT/extubation records, blinded chart review, or prospective multisite validation would be required to determine whether either documentation system corresponds to actual ventilation care or whether a bridge exists outside these tables. Computational verification can check the frozen keys, exact windows, source counts, transition extraction, hospital overlap, linkage rates, sham construction, and gate-conclusion consistency, but cannot adjudicate clinical semantics.
