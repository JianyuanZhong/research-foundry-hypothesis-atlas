# HCC Episode-15 child: observation-transport and downstream documentation falsification

Status: design only; no cohort count, endpoint prevalence, fitted coefficient, held-out loss,
effect estimate, or clinical conclusion is claimed. This is a substantive child of
[prior hypothesis]. It preserves the inherited HCC first-index population,
literal/semantic endpoint hierarchy, [43,181) outcome boundary, patient-disjoint temporal
split, and day-42 laboratory content. The new scientific advance is a transport estimand
and a prespecified negative-control falsifier for the remaining day-42 interpretation
boundary.

## Scientific opening, strongest supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the inspected evidence is limited. [K1] shows that a
selected, image-rich and externally tested cohort can support prediction relevant to
repeat-TACE decisions, but it does not validate this site's procedure rows or laboratory
trajectory. [K2] treats HCC treatment response as dynamic imaging and necrosis-aware
mRECIST/RECICL-style evidence, not as a code or laboratory surrogate. [K3] shows that a
recorded imaging outcome can change materially under undocumented outcomes, ascertainment,
exposure classification and treatment-selection sensitivity analyses. These works support
the importance of the decision and the need to audit ascertainment; they do not establish
a local EHR response signal.

The unresolved boundary is whether a day-42 trajectory contains reusable information about
a later coherent operational treatment episode, or whether it only identifies patients
whose local service is likely to keep observing them or whose care was already planned.
The distinction matters because using such a trajectory to trigger repeat-TACE review would
be clinically misleading if it predicts documentation opportunity rather than a
disease/treatment-related episode.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In the eligible first-index TACE population, the strict incremental gain from the
> pre-index-to-day-42 laboratory path over the terminal state remains positive for the
> triangulated operational endpoint O1 after transport to the full eligible index
> population under measured day-42 observation opportunity, and is not reproduced for
> prespecified post-landmark documentation or already-planned-care negative-control
> outcomes.

This is a predictive/descriptive hypothesis about recorded information. It is not a claim
of treatment intent, imaging response, treatment benefit, causal effect, mechanism,
survival, or transport outside this HCC source.

A result that survives transport and is not mirrored by either negative control would
justify a prospective study with scheduled laboratories, treatment scheduling/intent and
expert imaging adjudication. A result that is also strong for the documentation controls
would redirect the interpretation to observation/workflow. A null trajectory contrast
would narrow the claim to state or workflow if precision is adequate; an imprecise null
or failed support gate is inconclusive, not evidence that the assays have no biologic
value.

## Frozen endpoint hierarchy and operational limits

Retain the inherited endpoint dictionary before any held-out scoring.

O0 is the anchor: the first distinct-start later procedures row with normalized surgery exactly
tace and 43 <= start-t0 < 181. Its event time is the procedure start time.

O1 is primary: a later procedures row satisfying P_sem, where P_sem is exact normalized
tace, or a label containing hepatic artery and embolization plus at least one of chemotherapy or perfusion and none of
radiofrequency, microwave, ablation, biopsy, puncture or drainage. It must have the same patient master index and encounter number,
and an encounter link: encounters.Encounter Time within two elapsed days of the procedure
time, or encounters.admission time <= procedure time <= discharge time when all are valid. It also
requires at least one same-visit corroborator: an examinations.examination value matching the
frozen liver/hepatic-artery/upper-abdomen imaging dictionary with valid Start Time in
[t-42,t+7], or an orders.non-drug medical order value matching exact TACE or hepatic-arterial
embolization/chemotherapy/contrast terms with valid Order Time or Start Time in [t-42,t+7].
The event time remains the procedure time; corroborators are not response labels.
The [t-42,t+7] rule is primary episode corroboration, while [-42,-1], [-28,-1] and
[-7,-1] are pre-procedure-only falsification windows.

O2 remains conditional and secondary: O1 plus a medication row with valid medications.
start time in [t-1,t+1] on the same patient/visit, only if a pre-fit audit confirms
nonmissing medication, valid timing and key support, and an exact source-value or externally
documented dictionary. No medication is assumed TACE-specific. If the support gate fails,
O2 is unavailable/not estimable, not a negative and not a multiplicity null.

For every endpoint, a qualifying event is the earliest distinct start after duplicate
collapse. A qualifying row without the required corroborator is a near miss. A complete
negative requires obs_end >= t0+181; obs_end in [t0+43,t0+181) is right censoring. Report
untimed rows, missing encounter links, missing corroborator time, and key mismatches as
unsupported reasons. A literal O0-positive/O1-negative row remains in the O0 analysis
when follow-up is complete and is never silently dropped.

Materialize endpoint_event_audit.csv and near_miss_early_alias_audit.csv before fitting.
The audit must count, by split and endpoint, literal rows failing O1, each P_sem failure

conflicting labels, untimed rows, aliases, all early timed P_sem/exact-code rows, and

## Strict nested predictors and transport estimand

Use the eight exact lab labels: alpha-fetoprotein, albumin, prealbumin, total bilirubin, direct bilirubin,
alanine aminotransferase, platelets, and platelet count. For each label, select the latest valid
nonduplicate test time in [t0-365,t0) as B0 and rows in (t0,t0+42] as S42. Preserve
Qualitative Result, Quantitative Result, Specimen Type, inequalities, censoring tokens and availability; do not
invent numbers from qualitative/censored results or pool assays without units. T42
contains terminal changes plus ordered interval information from (0,14], (14,28] and
(28,42], with missingness and opportunity in P_obs rather than treated as assay values.

Define a strictly nested sequence, with no endpoint dictionary in any predictor:

- M0: inherited baseline demographics and index context from encounters, including
  age, sex, index timing and other prespecified index-context fields.
- B0: baseline assay states.
- P_obs: pre-index and through-day-28 assay opportunity/availability, valid-time and
  source-coverage indicators.
- P_care: non-endpoint care/workflow summaries through day 28 from dated encounters,
  orders, examinations and medications; it excludes all O0/O1/O2 dictionaries and all
  post-day-28 data.
- A0 = M0 + B0 + P_obs + P_care.
- A_snap = A0 + S42.
- A_term = A_snap + terminal B0-to-S42 changes and terminal transition categories.
- A_path = A_term + ordered knots/transitions across (0,14], (14,28] and (28,42].

Thus A_snap -> A_term -> A_path is strict. The confirmatory contrasts are
D_term,j = L(A_snap,j)-L(A_term,j) and
D_path,j = L(A_term,j)-L(A_path,j), with D_total,j =
L(A_snap,j)-L(A_path,j). Positive values are lower held-out loss after adding the
named trajectory information. The primary scientific contrast is D_path for O1, not a
small generic predictive improvement.

### Day-42 observation transport

Fit, inside each development fold and before endpoint modeling, a day-42 selection model
on all I. The numerator contains M0, B0, pre-index P_obs and pre-index P_care. The
denominator adds only non-content process marks through day 28: encounter/source-row
availability, dated lab/order/examination/medication opportunities and valid-time
indicators. It excludes every endpoint row/dictionary, corroborator content, post-day-28
assay value, future event and post-day-42 information.

For R42=1 use the fold-frozen stabilized weight
SW42 = Pr(R42=1 | numerator) / Pr(R42=1 | denominator). Apply frozen models to the
test set. Clip only at each development fold's 1st/99th percentile support limits and
record unclipped/clipped tails. A denominator with inadequate overlap, >5% clipping or
weighted ESS <100 is a transport-support failure.

The transported estimand for endpoint j and nested block q is the eligible-index
population's observation-standardized integrated predictive loss contrast:
Psi^TR_{j,q} = E_I[ L_j(A_previous,q) - L_j(A_q) ],
where the observed loss contributions are evaluated with R42 selection weighting and
the inherited, separately fitted follow-up observation/censoring weights. The weights
are applied to risk-set contributions through [43,181), are fold frozen and never use
endpoint evidence to model observation. The companion landmark estimand
Psi^LM_{j,q} is the ordinary complete-R42 held-out contrast without SW42. Agreement
between Psi^TR and Psi^LM is required for a robust interpretation; disagreement is
reported as observation sensitivity, not averaged away. This is transport/observation
standardization under measured positivity assumptions, not causal adjustment and not
proof of what would happen under a different care pathway.

Report R28 using the same definitions with information through day 28 and the same
[43,181) outcomes, but do not call it biologically equivalent to day 42. Freeze all
transport choices before test scoring.

## Downstream observation and planned-care falsifier

Construct two outcomes only for falsification; they are never predictors, selection-model
content, censoring-model content or endpoint corroborators.

F_obs is the first post-landmark local documentation opportunity in (t0+42,t0+63]:
a valid-time row in encounters (Visit Time, Admission Time or Discharge Time), labs (Test Time),
examinations (Start Time), orders (Order Time or Start Time) or medications (Start Time),
with the same patient master index; exclude procedures and exclude all rows used to define O0/O1/O2.
A duplicate patient/visit/time is one opportunity. This endpoint means continued local
recording, not clinical deterioration or treatment.

F_plan is the first order/planned-care signal in (t0+42,t0+63] for which an
orders row has a valid order time <= t0+42 and a valid start time in (t0+42,t0+63],
same patient and visit key, excluding endpoint dictionary terms. It is a narrower
negative control for care planned before the landmark. If no exact order timing supports
this construction, F_plan is unavailable, not recoded as a null.

Fit the identical frozen nested models, split, outcome window, weights, metrics and
uncertainty for O1, F_obs and supported F_plan. The falsifier is output-linked:

1. Adverse-to-clinical-trajectory: if the simultaneous max-|t| 95% interval shows a
   positive D_path for F_obs or F_plan, and its normalized loss gain is no smaller than
   the O1 D_path gain (paired test-prediction bootstrap comparison), the observation/
   planned-care explanation is supported and the O1 trajectory interpretation fails.
2. Observation-robust support: O1 D_path has a positive simultaneous lower bound under
   both Psi^LM and Psi^TR, while F_obs and supported F_plan have simultaneous intervals
   containing zero and their upper bounds remain below the O1 gain. This weakens, but
   does not eliminate, the observation explanation.
3. Mixed/inconclusive: one control is positive but smaller, one is unavailable, support
   or ESS fails, or all intervals are wide. Do not turn this into support by changing
   windows or controls.
4. The same comparison is repeated for O0 and the pre-procedure-only corroboration
   endpoints. Erasure of O1 after replacing [t-42,t+7] with [-42,-1], or after deleting
   (t0+35,t0+42] predictor rows, supports lead-time/documentation rather than path
   information.

The F_obs control cannot rule out all ascertainment: an O1 procedure may itself cause
local recording, and outside care is invisible. It specifically tests whether the
claimed day-42 gain is also a generic predictor of continued local recording. Clinical
intent, scheduled treatment, imaging response and treatment benefit remain unidentifiable.

## Model, uncertainty and learned alternative

Fit transparent elastic-net pooled-logistic discrete-time hazards for each endpoint and
each of A0, A_snap, A_term and A_path. Person-period rows inherit SW42 and any separately
fit follow-up censoring weight. Use the same model family, split, event windows, weights,
patient-level paired bootstrap, calibration, discrimination, Brier/integrated Brier,
log loss, risk-set/event counts, R42/R28 coverage, weight tails and ESS for O1, O0,
enabled O2, F_obs and F_plan. Use one predeclared max-|t| family across primary O1
nested contrasts and the two negative controls; endpoint/window sensitivities are
labeled and not used to redefine the hypothesis. The 0.005 log-loss resolution is only
a workflow audit resolution, not a clinical MCID.

The substantive alternative is a compact time-aware GRU over the identical allowed
event stream from t0-365 through t0+42. Each token contains source table, permitted
assay label, preserved numeric/qualitative/inequality content, relative time and time
since prior record; no narrative text, endpoint row, post-day-42 row or imaging claim is
used. Use separate O0/O1/enabled-O2/F_obs/F_plan hazard heads and matched ablations
A_snap, A_term and A_path. Train three development seeds only, freeze the patient-
disjoint test, use the same SW42 at scoring, and report paired held-out log loss, Brier,
calibration and seed range with the same patient bootstrap and falsifier family.

The GRU could reveal nonlinear ordered patterns, irregular timing and recovery-then-
worsening combinations lost by terminal knots and elastic-net terms. The elastic-net
remains confirmatory because its nested information increments, weights and negative
controls are auditable. A GRU-only gain is representation-sensitive, not evidence for a
trajectory mechanism; no model can recover missing assay units, imaging, intent, outside
care or medication indication.

Unverified planning estimates under the hardware contract: source materialization,
endpoint/negative-control audits, selection models, elastic-net fits, bootstrap and
permutations are approximately 1-4 hours on 4 CPU cores and 16 GiB; the three-seed GRU
sensitivity is approximately 2-6 hours on 4 CPU cores, 16 GiB and one allocated A100.
The primary requires no GPU. If the GRU is run, request gpus=1 and use cuda:0 inside the
allocated job; ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not evidence against feasibility. Discovery
and future solver budgets remain separate.

Not chosen: a larger transformer, narrative NLP, latent causal/intent model or
mechanistic liver-response model. The source lacks validated intent/scheduling labels,
imaging/report data, assay units/reference ranges, outside-care capture and reliable
death. These are dependency decisions, not blanket exclusions of neural or structured
methods. Revisit a deferred method only if those dependencies or an independent
response/adjudication set becomes available.

## Supportive, adverse and inconclusive interpretation

Supportive requires adequate O1 event/support, overlap and follow-up; positive simultaneous
lower bounds for D_path under both transport and landmark estimands; no positive,
O1-sized F_obs/F_plan falsifier; persistence in R28, late-record deletion and early-alias/
horizon controls; and directional reproduction by the GRU if feasible. This supports only
that recorded day-42 laboratory path information predicts an operational O1 episode under
the local observation regime.

Adverse is a positive O1 gain erased by SW42, by deleting late records, or by
pre-procedure corroboration; a gain only for O0; or an O1-sized positive negative-control
gain. It narrows the claim to documentation/workflow or endpoint coding and does not show
that laboratory assays lack prognostic value.

Inconclusive is inadequate O1/F-control support, unavailable F_plan, failed overlap,
>5% clipping, ESS <100, unstable censoring, wide uncertainty or inability to distinguish
complete negative from absent local observation. Freeze that result and seek linked
scheduling, imaging and outside-care data; do not rescue it by changing endpoint rules.

The source lacks treatment intent or scheduling, image files/radiology response, validated
examination interpretation, medication indication, assay units/reference ranges,
reliable death and outside-care capture. Claims of imaging response, treatment benefit,
causal effect, survival or external transport require expert adjudication, linked charts/
images, prospective scheduled measurements or another independent study.

## Alternatives and revisit record

Selected: elastic-net pooled-logistic hazard as the transparent confirmatory estimator,
because it directly exposes strict nested information increments, supports auditable
selection/censor weights and makes the observation falsifier easy to inspect.

Retained sensitivity: compact time-aware GRU with identical inputs, split, endpoints,
nested ablations, weights and uncertainty. It may reveal nonlinear ordered trajectories
lost by terminal summaries; it is deferred if resource/import or event-support checks
fail, and a GRU-only gain cannot override an adverse negative-control result.

Not selected: larger transformer, narrative NLP, latent causal/intent model and mechanistic
liver-response model. Revisit only if validated temporal intent/scheduling labels,
imaging/report data, assay units/reference ranges, outside-care capture or adjudicated
response become available. Revisit is also justified if the transport support audit shows
that measured day-28 observation opportunity is inadequate but an independent observation
source can be linked. These are explicit deferrals, not claims that the methods are
scientifically impossible.

## Three inspected works

The complete reference package is in key-references.json and the three attached UTF-8
excerpts. [K1] establishes the clinical importance and predictive precedent for repeat-TACE
decisions but is image-rich and selected. [K2] bounds response as imaging/necrosis-aware
and prevents relabeling an EHR procedure or assay as response. [K3] motivates missingness,
ascertainment and observation falsification because recorded outcomes can change under
reclassification. The child adds a transported day-42 trajectory estimand and downstream
documentation/planned-care falsifier; no citation is used as proof of local validity.
