# Independent transferability of context-selective MerTK perturbation

Status: substantive evolution of [prior hypothesis]; planned Harbor experiment only. No scientific screening result has been executed.

## Question, hypothesis and clinical importance

The inspected evidence supports three bounded claims. MerTK-positive synovial macrophage states are associated with remission biology and can induce fibroblast repair [K1]. A different inflammatory macrophage state can drive fibroblast invasiveness through tissue cross-talk [K2]. Fibroblast inflammatory programs are heterogeneous and niche-linked [K3]. None establishes the parent’s unresolved claim: that MerTK inhibition causes a larger FLS inflammatory harm response in a MerTK/CD206-positive context than in a MerTK/CD206-negative context, and that this interaction transfers to an independently verified perturbation assay.

The falsifiable hypothesis is therefore:

> In the primary E-MTAB-8873 RA type-B synovial FLS coculture, exact MerTK-inhibitor exposure has a larger FLS harm effect in the unambiguous Mpos context than in Mneg; an independent E-MTAB-8316 paired perturbation will show directionally coherent, target-relevant response only if its compound label, feature identity and paired biological unit are independently verified.

The clinically consequential decision is whether to prioritize state-stratified MerTK preservation/activation experiments. A supportive result justifies balanced orthogonal perturbation and functional testing; it does not establish a treatment, patient-selection rule, direct target engagement or clinical benefit. The substantive advance over the parent is a transportability gate: it distinguishes a valid primary FLS interaction from a source-specific monocyte perturbation response and prevents a false “external validation” created by matching donor-like strings.

## Estimands and identifiability gates

Normalize each source separately to log2(CPM + 1), preserving original feature IDs and never imputing absent genes.

Primary E-MTAB-8873 outcome:
`H_s` is the mean of feature-standardized IL6, MMP1, MMP3 and MMP14. Standardization parameters are frozen using eligible untreated, unambiguous co-culture reference rows. The primary estimand is

`Delta = [E(H | exact inhibitor, Mpos) - E(H | untreated, Mpos)] - [E(H | exact inhibitor, Mneg) - E(H | untreated, Mneg)]`.

The solver must emit a pre-fit gate with treatment/context cell counts, exact counterpart availability, donor-membership incidence rank, and estimability of the interaction. The gate fails if the model requires the ambiguous mixed rows, the preInhibitor tier, the metadata-conflicted untreated row, a random row split, or independent-donor assumptions. Given the verified design, only the R1 SA256A/SA259A pair supplies a complete same-membership exact-inhibitor Mpos/Mneg contrast; therefore a population-level causal interaction is not automatically identified. A failed or rank-deficient gate returns inconclusive, never a zero estimate.

The baseline is a transparent paired score/interaction analysis: calculate within-membership matched differences wherever explicitly supported, fit context, exposure and interaction with assay round only when rank permits, and represent FLS/macrophage memberships by an incidence matrix. Use cluster bootstrap or a bounded sign/permutation sensitivity over contributing membership clusters, leave-one-round-out and leave-one-membership-out analyses. Report H, each component, exact versus preInhibitor tiers, mapping scenarios, and controls. Ordinary donor t-tests and cell-level replication are prohibited.

The E-MTAB-8316 secondary estimand is source-specific:
`d_{f,m,g}=logCPM(UNC106238)_{f,m,g}-logCPM(none)_{f,m,g}`, for each of the six exact pairs, summarized within the four FLS donor clusters with monocyte donor nested dependence. It is not H, not Delta, and not an FLS outcome. A six-pair response can be reported only as exploratory paired evidence; it is not six independent donors.

The 8316 compatibility gate requires: (1) protocol/registry confirmation that UNC106238 is the intended perturbation and that the none/UNC106238 rows are paired units; (2) a named frozen Ensembl-to-gene annotation map if gene-level MERTK/MRC1/TREM2/SPP1 or inflammatory-marker claims are attempted; (3) verified correspondence of cell type and readout to the intended secondary question; and (4) no cross-source donor join. The currently inspected prepared files pass the pairing and donor-nesting checks but fail the configured feature-map requirement for gene-level target claims and do not expose an Mpos/Mneg context. The correct current plan is therefore bounded source-specific triangulation with an explicit non-pooling status, not a hierarchical cross-cohort Delta.

A shared hierarchical cross-cohort effect is scientifically identifiable only if both sources share a measured endpoint, intervention definition, context contrast, feature namespace/annotation and biological unit. They currently do not: 8873 measures targeted FLS coculture harm with transcript labels, while 8316 measures Ensembl-indexed monocyte samples under none/UNC106238 and has no context field. A model with source-specific intercepts cannot repair a missing common estimand. If a future named annotation and protocol adjudication make a common secondary signature defensible, fit source-specific effects with a hierarchical shrinkage sensitivity, never a forced pooled primary coefficient. Otherwise report non-pooling.

## Baseline versus substantive alternative

Both methods use the same source-specific normalized matrices, design fields, paired units and frozen manifests.

The baseline estimates the primary Delta transparently and the 8316 paired d contrasts without a learned prediction target. Its strength is auditable behavior under sparsity; it loses correlated marker structure and can be noisy when one membership drives a contrast.

The alternative is a source-stratified cross-classified multivariate model. For 8873, jointly model IL6/MMP1/MMP3/MMP14 with feature-specific exposure, context and interaction effects, crossed FLS/macrophage membership effects, and a shared harm factor; retain IL1B/MMP12/MMP13 and MERTK/MRC1/TREM2/SPP1 as contrasts. For 8316, fit a multivariate paired response only after the feature-map and protocol gates pass, with FLS-cluster and nested monocyte effects. The target is the same Delta (8873) and source-specific d-vector (8316), not a disease label. Compare signs, uncertainty, marker coherence and leave-cluster-out stability, not predictive accuracy. The alternative can reveal whether a response is a correlated program and whether partial pooling appropriately widens or shrinks uncertainty; the baseline loses that structure.

Do not fit the alternative if the 8873 rank gate fails or the 8316 feature map/units remain unresolved. This is a scientific deferral, not a mechanical simplification. Expected solver resources are 4–8 CPU cores, <16 GiB memory and minutes for the 33-by-11 primary feature table plus 12-by-feature paired secondary table; the estimate is unmeasured and must be bounded in the solver job. GPU is deferred because the bottleneck is identifiability and biological-unit compatibility, not matrix scale. A neural predictor is not selected: a predictive gain cannot distinguish context selectivity from donor/assay confounding and has no clinically meaningful target estimand here.

## Falsification and interpretation

Freeze material harm at 0.5 SD of H before fitting. Supportive requires a positive exact-exposure Delta above that threshold with uncertainty supporting Delta > 0.5 SD, stable admissible donor/mapping/round sensitivities, coherent primary components, no matching selective pattern in LPS or FLS-only controls, and a gated 8316 secondary response that is directionally compatible without asserting a shared effect. It supports balanced agonism/genetic-loss-of-function, protein/phospho-MerTK, viability, efferocytosis and fibroblast invasion or matrix-degradation follow-up.

Adverse means Delta is nonpositive, Mneg harm is at least as large, generic controls reproduce the apparent selectivity, or a validly gated 8316 perturbation response is discordant. This weakens the context-selective prioritization rationale and favors generic inflammation, composition, timing or assay-specific explanations.

Inconclusive means the primary rank/overlap gate fails, an admissible mapping changes sign, uncertainty spans zero or the material threshold, one membership cluster drives the result, exact and preInhibitor tiers disagree without protocol equivalence, or 8316 annotation/protocol compatibility remains blocked. The next decision is to obtain balanced matched Mpos/Mneg FLS–macrophage donor pairs with dose, exposure time, viability, protein/phospho-MerTK, efferocytosis, secreted mediators and direct FLS functional readouts.

Harbor can verify source-local joins, feature presence, paired contrasts, rank calculations, uncertainty outputs, compatibility status and rule-consistent conclusions. It cannot establish clinical benefit, direct molecular mechanism, viability, spatial localization, therapeutic window, or independent biological validity; those require expert adjudication, orthogonal perturbation and another study.
