# Time-blinded, recommendation-level adjudication of pulmonary-nodule handoff: separating safe propagation from clinician reconciliation

## Decision question and targeted repair

The inherited study asks whether a pulmonary-nodule recommendation that had ample observed storage-to-storage opportunity before departure is best addressed by structured propagation, clinician reconciliation, or neither. Its chronology and all-opportunity accounting are sound, but retrospective intervention mapping remains vulnerable to three related errors:

1. the same canonical discharge text can be used to infer both whether the recommendation remained actionable and whether it was propagated, creating circular labels;
2. an admission can contain more than one eligible recommendation or plan, while the clinical decision is made once per admission; treating recommendation rows as independent can inflate event counts and can call an admission safe for automation despite another conflicting recommendation;
3. a consensus label and a decision curve at an assumed threshold can obscure irreducible disagreement and the fact that MIMIC does not identify the harm ratio for an erroneous insertion versus a missed handoff.

This child preserves the parent’s frozen first-eligible, one-admission-per-subject denominator `N`; reciprocal report/addendum reconstruction; unique canonical discharge artifact; all-`N` `F/C_O/C_I/R/A/U_D` departure state; `G = DS_store - R_store`; exhaustive `V x H x Z` chronology; stale and post-departure exclusions; ordinary-discharge, radiology-lag, matched, and portable-comparator controls; source provenance; and strict noncausal limits. It changes only how retrospective clinical evidence is packeted, labeled, aggregated, and interpreted.

The strongest claim currently supported remains descriptive. The inherited complete-source audit streamed 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, and all 331,794 exact `DS` rows without row sampling. It found 3,764 broad retrieval-proxy pulmonary reports, 17 `DS` rows with missing `storetime`, and substantial ordinary discharge storage latency. These are feasibility and workflow diagnostics, not adjudicated pulmonary-opportunity prevalence, actionability, clinician awareness, appropriateness, or benefit.

The unresolved falsifiable claim is narrower than “the intervention is indicated”:

> Within the frozen ample, timely opportunity stratum, a reproducible, outcome-blinded recommendation-level procedure can identify admissions for which every relevant plan is compatible with clinician-confirmed structured propagation, separately from admissions requiring reconciliation; that separation remains useful after admission-level aggregation and worst-case assignment of unresolved labels.

A supportive retrospective result identifies a candidate workflow for prospective testing. It never licenses silent insertion and does not establish that a recommendation was clinically correct, seen, communicated, ordered, completed, or beneficial.

## Repair 1: freeze recommendation units without changing the admission denominator

### Recommendation census

Within each already frozen index admission, construct a discharge-outcome-blind recommendation manifest `Q_i` containing every independently eligible operative recommendation chain, not just the chain that supplied the inherited index clock. Build it using radiology and reciprocal detail links only, before reading any `DS` field. A recommendation unit is the tuple:

`q=(subject_id,hadm_id,chain_id,operative_note_id,operative_component_id,target_class,action,calendar_interval)`.

Collapse only exact duplicate payloads within the same reciprocal chain. Do not merge different chains merely because they share an interval, and do not split multiple nodules governed by one explicit common action/interval into fabricated independent decisions. Record `m_i=|Q_i|`, exact duplicates, non-equivalent payloads, and unresolved units. The inherited index recommendation `q_i*`, its operative `R_store`, `G`, `H`, `V`, `E_N`, `Y`, and `Z` remain unchanged; `Q_i` is an adjudication and multiplicity audit, not a new denominator.

If the historical manifest does not explicitly store `q_i*`, reproduce it by the denominator-building order already used to freeze `N` and verify its hash against the inherited manifest. The compiler may not introduce an outcome-dependent replacement. Failure to reproduce `q_i*` is a stop condition, not permission to choose the recommendation with the clearest discharge match.

### Two scales, no pseudo-replication

Recommendation-level labels are `L_iq`, with denominator `M=sum_i m_i`, and are descriptive. Every decision-facing burden, trigger accuracy, confidence interval, and event gate is admission-level with denominator `N`, clustered by `subject_id`. Report `m_i` and the fraction of `E_N` admissions with `m_i>1`; never describe `M` as the number of patients or opportunities. Recommendation-level resampling cannot substitute for subject-level resampling.

## Repair 2: four firewalled, time-blinded evidence packets

All packets are generated by code from the frozen manifests, versioned, checksummed, and scanned for prohibited fields. Panel assignments are randomized within interval and terminal-service strata. Reviewers cannot access other panel labels. No panel is asked which intervention should be deployed.

### Panel E: radiology eligibility and payload

Two radiology-trained reviewers independently receive only the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain with dates replaced by relative order tokens. They confirm the inherited eligibility rubric, operative component, target, action, and exact interval for every `q`. They do not receive discharge text, `D`, `DS_store`, `G/H/V`, later events, or another panel’s label. `U_R` remains set-valued/unknown rather than being forced eligible or ineligible.

### Panel C: context actionability without target-plan outcome

A separate span team first produces a departure-context packet from the canonical `DS` only when `DS_store<=D`. It removes every pulmonary-nodule, pulmonary follow-up, chest-imaging action/interval/date, named pulmonary/thoracic specialist, target-specific refusal, target-specific order, and target-specific plan span. Every removed byte offset and replacement token is logged. The packet includes the standardized Panel-E payload `{pulmonary target class, chest CT, due interval}` but not the original recommendation prose. It may include non-target admission problems, non-target goals of care, disposition, and the structured admission/disposition variables available by `D`.

For `DS_store>D`, missing text, or unresolved artifact time, no later-stored discharge prose is allowed to establish departure actionability. The context label is `K_U` unless another inherited pre-`D` source explicitly supplies the necessary non-target context; the current design has no such progress-note source. This prevents post-departure narrative from retrospectively making the recommendation active or inactive.

Two board-certified physicians with discharge experience independently assign:

* `K_A`: under the observable, non-target context, an explicit pulmonary follow-up disposition action would remain applicable if no target-specific resolution existed;
* `K_N`: a clear non-target contextual reason makes any such departure action inapplicable;
* `K_U`: available evidence cannot decide, including missing competing-risk, preference, outside-plan, or appropriateness facts.

They separately record confidence and the missing fact that could change the label. `K_A` is recommendation-level operational actionability under observed context, not guideline correctness or necessity. Consensus cannot turn missing evidence into `K_A`.

The span team sees neither `K`, `Z`, nor the study hypothesis. A third redactor audits a stratified 10% sample, oversampling multi-recommendation and apparent-plan admissions. Any residual target-plan leakage or material removal of non-target context above the inherited 2% packet-error margin stops actionability mapping.

### Panel P: discharge-plan evidence extraction without recommendation comparison

A new plan-evidence panel prevents the same task from both finding and validating a superseding plan. Two clinicians receive the canonical `DS` as it existed in the available row, but do not receive the radiology report, operative interval, `R_store`, `DS_store`, `G/H/V`, Panel-C label, content-fidelity label, downstream trace, or intervention framing. They mark every pulmonary target/action/timing/responsibility/refusal span and assign only an evidence form:

* `P_NONE`: no target-linked plan evidence;
* `P_ACT`: target-linked action evidence, with extracted action/modality, timing/date, responsibility, and status fields;
* `P_NOPURSUE`: explicit target-linked refusal or non-pursuit evidence;
* `P_AMBIG`: possible target plan whose target, action, timing, responsibility, or active status cannot be extracted reliably;
* `P_U`: text is unavailable/unreadable or reviewers cannot decide.

This panel does not call evidence equivalent, protective, conflicting, superseding, or appropriate. It cannot see the operative recommendation against which those judgments would be circularly optimized.

### Panel R: pairwise relation, not outcome discovery

Only after Panels E and P freeze labels, a separate pairwise-relation panel receives the standardized operative payload for one `q` and the pre-extracted Panel-P spans with a fixed local context window. It receives no unmarked discharge text, timestamps, gap bins, downstream trace, `K`, content-fidelity outcome, other recommendation relation labels, or lane label. It independently assigns:

* `S_0`: `P_NONE`, with no target-specific alternate resolution;
* `S_EQ`: an extracted target-linked plan is operationally equivalent or more definitive, no later/less protective than the operative payload, and has an executable next step;
* `S_DEC`: explicit target-linked informed non-pursuit;
* `S_CON`: extracted plan recognizes the target but conflicts in action, modality, or timing without an explicit rationale sufficient to establish replacement;
* `S_UN`: target linkage, timing, responsibility, active status, rationale, or adequacy cannot be resolved.

“Follow with PCP” alone is `S_0`; specialty mention without action/timing is `S_UN`; scheduled compatible CT is `S_EQ`; target-linked refusal is `S_DEC`; unexplained later or incompatible follow-up is `S_CON`. `S_EQ/S_DEC` can map to `R_text`; `S_CON` requires reconciliation; `S_UN` stays unknown. An artifact stored after `D` never establishes a departure superseding plan. A stale artifact never establishes incorporation of a later operative recommendation.

### Panel F: literal propagation/content relation

A fourth independent panel receives the standardized Panel-E payload and the pre-extracted Panel-P evidence, but not `K`, `S`, timestamps, outcomes, or intervention framing. It classifies only the literal relation:

* `F_q`: target, action, and exact compatible interval/date are faithfully represented;
* `O_q`: the operative payload is wholly absent;
* `I_q`: target/action/timing is present but incomplete or discordant;
* `U_q`: relation cannot be classified.

Panel F does not decide whether a different plan is clinically valid. Panel R does not decide whether literal propagation occurred. Their disagreement table is a required result, not an error to be erased.

## Set-valued recommendation labels and admission-level lanes

For every panel retain both independent pre-consensus ratings. A third adjudicator may add a rating and a documented reason but does not delete the original ratings. Define each unit’s compatibility set `C_iq` as all labels supported by at least one qualified rating plus `U` whenever the relevant packet is missing, leakage-compromised, or below the confidence gate. A consensus point label is descriptive only.

For each compatible joint assignment of `K`, `S`, and `F/O/I/U`, map recommendation `q` to:

* `AUTO_q`: `K_A & S_0 & O_q`;
* `RECON_q`: `K_A & (I_q or S_CON)`;
* `RES_q`: `S_EQ or S_DEC`;
* `NONE_q`: `K_N` without contradictory evidence;
* `UN_q`: all unresolved, incompatible, or insufficient-evidence combinations.

Do not let `S_UN` become `RECON_q` merely to increase review yield; it remains `UN_q` unless independently classifiable `I_q` or `S_CON` already establishes need for reconciliation.

Aggregate to one mutually exclusive admission lane, evaluated only as primary when the inherited index admission is in `E_N`:

1. `A_RECON`: at least one recommendation is definitively `RECON_q`, or two active recommendation payloads conflict/branch in a way that cannot be represented by one deterministic payload. A concurrent `AUTO_q` does not make the admission auto-safe.
2. `A_AUTO`: at least one `AUTO_q`; every other unit is `AUTO_q`, `RES_q`, or `NONE_q`; no unit is `RECON_q/UN_q`; and all auto payloads can be merged without changing target, action, due interval, or responsibility semantics. This means “eligible for clinician-confirmed prepopulation,” never silent insertion.
3. `A_RES`: no auto or reconciliation unit and every recommendation is reproducibly `RES_q` or `NONE_q`, with at least one `RES_q`.
4. `A_NONE`: every recommendation is reproducibly `NONE_q`.
5. `A_UN`: any remaining combination, including auto plus unresolved evidence, unresolved multiplicity, or panel disagreement that could change the intervention lane.

This precedence is deliberately asymmetric: uncertainty can block automation, while a definitive reconciliation need can remain actionable despite another unresolved recommendation. Publish the complete recommendation-to-admission crosswalk, including zero and impossible cells.

## Estimands and sharp disagreement bounds

All primary quantities use `N`, not `M`, and are reported per 100 frozen opportunities with subject-clustered simultaneous 95% intervals.

1. `B_AUTO=100*n(E_N & A_AUTO)/N`.
2. `B_RECON=100*n(E_N & A_RECON)/N`.
3. `B_RES`, `B_NONE`, and `B_UN` analogously.
4. The workload distribution among triggered admissions: number of recommendation units, plan spans, and mutually incompatible payloads per admission. This is secondary and never changes `N`.
5. The reclassification matrices from inherited `Z/Y` to recommendation labels and admission lanes, stratified by `m_i=1` versus `m_i>1`.
6. `Delta_mult=[P(A_AUTO|E_N,m_i=1)-P(A_AUTO|E_N,m_i>1)]` and the corresponding reconciliation contrast, descriptive of multiplicity sensitivity and not causal.

Compute exact finite-sample sharp lower and upper bounds for every burden and trigger confusion-matrix cell over all joint label assignments allowed by `C_iq`, respecting the admission aggregation precedence and all logical constraints. Use enumeration where feasible and an integer program otherwise; provide a brute-force oracle on small synthetic fixtures. Bounds must incorporate panel disagreement, packet leakage failures, missing text, unresolved chains, and recommendation multiplicity. Separately publish the parent-compatible simpler bounds for `K_U/S_UN/U_text` and show that the new joint bounds are never narrower without an explicit logical restriction.

Consensus-only estimates, majority-vote estimates, reviewer-specific estimates, and leave-one-reviewer-out estimates are sensitivity descriptions. The least favorable compatible bound governs any directional conclusion.

## Decision thresholds: identify a frontier, not a utility that MIMIC does not contain

Retain the parent’s three prospective rules for identifying auto-safe admissions: trigger none; trigger every classifiable `E_N` discordance; and the locked conservative auto trigger. Also retain the review-none, review-all, and locked reconciliation trigger. Calculate the inherited net-benefit display points `t={0.80,0.90,0.95}` for propagation and `t=0.50` for reconciliation, including conservative treatment of `A_UN`. However, these are sensitivity coordinates, not empirically identified clinical utilities.

For each trigger, publish the full threshold frontier on a fixed grid `t=0.50,...,0.99` and the analytic break-even intervals against none and all. For a rule with `TP(t)` and `FP(t)`, verify `NB_t=100/N*[TP-FP*t/(1-t)]`. Report the range of `t` over which the rule has a simultaneous lower-bound advantage over each baseline. If no nonempty threshold range survives compatible-label bounds, retrospective intervention selection is adverse. If a range survives but the institution has not prospectively chosen a threshold/harm ratio inside it, the result is utility-inconclusive, even when the inherited `t=0.90` display is favorable.

The parent’s `t=0.90` propagation and `t=0.50` reconciliation gates are retained as conservative feasibility stress tests. Passing them permits only the statement “this trigger is sufficiently separable to justify a governed prospective pilot under the stated utility.” It does not prove that those utility ratios are correct or that the intervention should be deployed. A prospective governance group independent of retrospective reviewers must pre-register the actual utility/threshold, including burden of false insertion, false reassurance, alert/review time, and missed active plan, before evaluating a live pilot.

## Event, reliability, and feasibility gates

Retain all inherited source and feasibility gates:

* at least 200 pulmonary opportunities in frozen `N`;
* `U_R<=5%` and `U_D<=5%`;
* no duplicate canonical `DS` key;
* at least 30 rows in `E_N`;
* at least 20 total certain `A_AUTO+A_RECON` admissions for a propagation conclusion;
* at least 10 certain admissions in a lane before its lane-specific decision curve is interpreted;
* observed `K_U+S_UN+U_text<=10%` within `E_N`, while the directional result also survives compatible-label bounds;
* at least 90% raw agreement and kappa at least 0.70 for eligibility, `K`, extracted plan-evidence form, `S`, literal content, and final lane; consensus cannot repair a failed gate;
* redaction error no greater than 2%.

Add the following multiplicity/adjudication gates:

* publish the number of admissions and events, never only recommendation counts;
* at least 10 multi-recommendation `E_N` admissions are required to interpret `Delta_mult`; otherwise multiplicity is described and bounded but no subgroup claim is made;
* at least 10% of packets are blindly repeated to estimate intra-rater stability; a greater than 0.10 absolute shift in any admission lane or failure of the same 0.70 kappa gate makes that lane inconclusive;
* at least 30 plan-bearing recommendation pairs across `S_EQ/S_DEC/S_CON/S_UN` are required to interpret the supersession relation. If fewer exist, `B_AUTO` may still be bounded, but claims that reconciliation is separable are inconclusive;
* no directional result if more than 5% of `E_N` admissions have lane-changing recommendation multiplicity that was absent from the inherited index recommendation representation;
* an `A_AUTO` conclusion requires at least 10 certain `A_AUTO` admissions, a strictly positive simultaneous lower bound for `B_AUTO`, and survival of the least-favorable joint-label bound. The inherited `t=0.90` net-benefit gate must also pass for pilot eligibility;
* an `A_RECON` conclusion requires at least 10 certain `A_RECON` admissions, positive conservative net benefit at the inherited `t=0.50`, and a simultaneous lower bound above zero for enrichment of true `A_RECON` under the review trigger versus the auto trigger.

Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates and one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covering all decision-facing burdens, threshold-frontier contrasts, enrichment contrasts, availability controls, and portable-comparator contrasts. Recompute sharp label bounds inside each replicate or use a validated conservative envelope. Perform the inherited two-way subject-and-reviewer bootstrap and reviewer leave-one-out analyses. Pointwise intervals cannot support directional claims.

## Falsification and verifier contract

The compiler and verifier must implement the following checks, including adversarial fixtures whose expected outputs are fixed before analysis.

1. **Source and denominator identity:** verify all hashes/schemas; scan complete sources; reproduce the frozen `N` and `q_i*` hashes; stop for drift, duplicate canonical `DS`, impossible time, or unresolved reciprocal-chain logic above the gate. Every `Y`, `J_N`, adjudication, lane, control, and appendix marginal uses the same `N`.
2. **Clock preservation:** check `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` row-wise; only the inherited `q_i*` `G` defines `H/E_N`. Adding recommendation units cannot move an admission into or out of `E_N`.
3. **No stale/delayed leakage:** no `G<0` or `DS_store>D` admission enters a primary content lane. Replacing delayed text with faithful text cannot change `Y=A`, `q_N(0)`, or `E_N`.
4. **Packet prohibited-field scan:** Panel C cannot contain target-plan spans or outcomes; Panel P cannot contain operative recommendation or clocks; Panels R/F cannot contain unmarked discharge text, `K`, outcomes, or lanes. Checksums and randomized packet assignments are required.
5. **Hindsight fixture:** inserting a target-specific plan or outside follow-up event after `D` must not change departure `K/S/lane`. A `DS_store>D` row cannot become departure-actionable from its later prose.
6. **Circularity fixture:** changing the operative interval while holding Panel-P extraction fixed may change Panel R/F pairwise relation, but must not change whether Panel P found a plan. Changing `Z` or the proposed intervention name must not change `K` or Panel-P extraction.
7. **Supersession fixtures:** PCP-only=`S_0`; specialist without action/timing=`S_UN`; scheduled compatible CT=`S_EQ`; target-linked refusal=`S_DEC`; unexplained incompatible interval=`S_CON`. `S_UN/S_CON` can never silently become resolved.
8. **Multiplicity fixtures:** `{AUTO,RES}` maps to `A_AUTO`; `{AUTO,RECON}` to `A_RECON`; `{AUTO,UN}` to `A_UN`; `{RES,NONE}` to `A_RES`; two incompatible active auto payloads to `A_RECON`; duplicate identical payloads within one reciprocal chain collapse once. Admission counts stay one in every fixture.
9. **Disagreement fixture:** two discordant reviewers must widen the compatibility set and cannot produce a narrower sharp interval than unanimous labels. Adjudication may add a label but cannot erase source ratings.
10. **Bounds oracle:** compare integer-program bounds with exhaustive enumeration for all synthetic admissions with up to four recommendation units. Reject any bound outside the oracle or any “supportive” conclusion that fails under a compatible assignment.
11. **Pseudo-replication trap:** duplicating recommendation rows within admissions must not change `N`, admission burden, event gates, bootstrap cluster count, or inferential precision.
12. **Decision-frontier math:** verify analytic break-even points against grid calculations; `FP=0`, `TP=0`, all-unknown, and all-triggered cases must be handled. A favorable point at `t=0.90` cannot be summarized as utility-identified when the governance threshold is absent.
13. **Unsupported-conclusion tests:** correct computation accompanied by claims of guideline appropriateness, report viewing, clinician omission, completed follow-up, causality, benefit, or deployment readiness must fail verification. A negative or inconclusive result must not be rewritten as supportive.
14. **Threshold/event behavior:** fewer than 10 certain lane events, fewer than 20 total lane events, failed reliability, wide bounds crossing zero, or no surviving threshold interval must force the corresponding inconclusive/adverse label even if consensus prevalence is high.
15. **Inherited controls:** reproduce calibration at `D,+24h,+72h,+168h`, radiology-lag and charttime placebo, `G>=12h` and `G>=72h` sensitivities, standardized-versus-matched reversal rules, comparator portability, and POE non-leakage. Adding/removing POE cannot alter any primary label.

Computationally checkable claims are source/manifest identity, chronology, packet contents, counts, mappings, agreement, sharp bounds, bootstrap intervals, threshold-frontier arithmetic, control construction, and whether reported result labels follow the locked gates. Clinical appropriateness, whether omitted context would alter a label, the true utility of false insertion versus omission, and whether a workflow improves care require clinical governance and another study; the automatic verifier must not claim to establish them.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

### Supportive for a clinician-confirmed structured-propagation pilot

Support requires all source, blinding, redaction, agreement, unknown, multiplicity, and event gates; at least 10 certain `A_AUTO` admissions; a simultaneous lower bound `B_AUTO>0`; an auto-safe lane that remains nonempty under least-favorable compatible labels; and superiority to none and all at the inherited `t=0.90` feasibility point. Report the full threshold range. If prospective governance chooses a threshold within the surviving range, this supports testing a clinician-confirmed prepopulation of target/action/due interval. It does not support silent auto-propagation or deployment.

### Supportive for a clinician-reconciliation pilot

Support requires all applicable gates; at least 10 certain `A_RECON` admissions; reproducible plan extraction and pairwise relation; positive conservative decision value at the inherited `t=0.50`; and lower-bound reconciliation enrichment over the auto trigger. This supports testing a side-by-side review of recommendation and existing plan. It does not establish that radiology should override a clinician plan.

### Supportive for a bundled pilot

If both lanes satisfy their separate rules and admission-level multiplicity frequently causes `{AUTO,RECON}` combinations, a bundled interface may be tested, but its propagation and reconciliation outputs and harms must remain separable. If delayed/stale/short-window states dominate, preserve the parent’s availability/timing interpretation rather than forcing a content intervention.

### Adverse

Structured propagation is adverse when the auto-safe sharp lower bound is zero, false-auto classifications eliminate decision value across the governance-relevant threshold range, valid resolutions or unresolved context dominate, or recommendation multiplicity commonly converts apparent auto cases to reconciliation/unknown. Reconciliation targeting is adverse when plan/conflict extraction is irreproducible, the locked trigger is not enriched over broad review, or decision value is nonpositive. Adverse retrospective findings can favor no discharge-content intervention, better context capture, real-time availability work, downstream tracking, or communication; they do not show that follow-up occurred.

### Inconclusive

Inconclusive results include source/manifest drift, inadequate `N/E_N` or event counts, sparse plan-bearing cases, failed redaction, prohibited-field leakage, unreliable reviewers, wide sharp bounds, too many unknowns, recommendation-unit ambiguity, threshold instability, lack of a prospectively governed utility, or comparator/overlap failure. Utility-inconclusive is distinct from phenotype-inconclusive: a trigger may be reproducibly characterized while MIMIC still cannot choose whether its benefit is worth its harms. Comparator specificity can be inconclusive while pulmonary mapping remains interpretable, and vice versa.

## Prospective evidence required

Retrospective MIMIC text can support only an outcome-blinded estimate of how available documentation would be classified and how much disagreement remains. It cannot reveal the actual information state or decision at departure. The next silent-mode prospective study must capture, at minimum:

* versioned radiology preliminary/final/addendum times and report-view events;
* versioned discharge-artifact creation, edit, sign/finalize, transmit, and departure-time snapshots rather than one eventual row;
* the exact recommendation unit(s) active at each decision time;
* UI trigger time, payload shown, clinician accept/modify/reject/defer action, reason, and time spent;
* target-linked order/referral, intended due date, responsible clinician/team, scheduling, and cancellation;
* communication to inpatient clinician, outpatient recipient, and patient, with receipt where measurable;
* contemporaneous preference, goals of care, competing illness, outside plan, and other facts needed for actionability;
* outside-system imaging and follow-up ascertainment;
* prespecified unsafe-insertion, conflicting-plan, alert-burden, timely-final-plan, completion, and patient-centered outcomes.

Before live evaluation, an independent governance group must define the false-insertion versus missed-plan utility and the acceptability threshold. A prospective silent phase should apply both retrospective packet logic and contemporaneous adjudication to estimate hindsight bias. Only then should a clinician-confirmed randomized or stepped workflow pilot compare structured prepopulation, side-by-side reconciliation, a separable bundle, and usual care as appropriate. Clinical experts must adjudicate unsafe insertions and plan conflicts; causal benefit, safety, adherence, malignancy outcome, and generalizability require that prospective study and cannot be inferred from MIMIC.

## Substantive advance and retained uncertainty

This repair does not change the inherited population, chronology, outcome states, clocks, controls, or evidence boundary. It makes the intervention-mapping layer decision-valid enough to falsify: target-plan evidence is extracted without seeing the recommendation; actionability is assessed from target-redacted evidence available by departure; supersession and literal propagation are independent pairwise tasks; every recommendation is represented while inference stays at the admission level; uncertainty is set-valued rather than erased by consensus; and decision curves expose rather than assume the utility threshold.

The remaining uncertainty is fundamental. A time-blinded retrospective packet is not the clinician’s contemporaneous information set, experts are not ground truth, one eventual discharge row is not a version history, and MIMIC lacks report viewing, UI exposure, communication, validated target-linked outpatient orders, outside care, and utilities. Thus even a supportive result means only that a specific, clinician-confirmed prospective workflow is sufficiently coherent to test.
