# Provisional hypothesis (episode 20 checkpoint)

## Proposed relationship

In the available E-MTAB-8873 RA macrophage–fibroblast coculture assay, the transcriptional response associated with macrophage pre-treatment by a MerTK inhibitor differs between MerTK/CD206-positive and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage contexts. The primary estimand is a donor-aware, condition-verified context-by-inhibitor interaction, not a generic mean-expression change. A secondary descriptive question is whether the interaction separates a macrophage-linked resolving/repair-associated program from a fibroblast-linked inflammatory or harmful program.

This is an association hypothesis about an assay response. It does not claim that UNC106238 acts directly on MerTK, that the response is causal, protective, clinically effective, or transferable to patients.

## Unresolved claim and falsifiable test

The unresolved claim is whether the context-by-inhibitor interaction is reproducible after donor-aware matched contrasts, condition verification, composition checks, and within-context treatment negative controls. The planned test compares a donor-aware additive baseline (main effects only) with a prespecified interaction alternative on the same samples and features. The interaction is supportive only if it is directionally and uncertainty-consistent across donors/contexts and exceeds the negative controls; a generic treatment response, donor/composition artifact, or failed condition verification is adverse to the specific hypothesis; low power, mixed donor contamination, or unstable feature definitions are inconclusive.
