# Prespecified cancer-site heterogeneity of adverse CBC trajectories in recorded malignancy cases

## Parent, scientific advance, and unresolved claim

This proposal is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent found concordant non-rejection, not equivalence, for a three-component adverse-oriented CBC trajectory contrast between 75 recorded CRC cases and 923 recorded non-CRC malignancy cases. The strongest supported claim is therefore limited: under the parent's declared models, no statistically detectable adjusted linear residual-moment signal was found in that broad selected-case comparison. That result does not show that CRC and other malignancies have the same trajectory, because aggregation can cancel site-specific effects and the selected-case design can induce collider bias.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is whether the broad non-rejection masks (a) a shared GI pattern involving CRC and other GI malignancies relative to non-GI malignancies, or (b) a CRC-specific pattern that differs from other GI malignancies. The substantive advance is a low-dimensional, outcome-independent, prespecified decomposition that distinguishes these explanations without introducing people lacking a recorded malignancy as controls or fishing across many cancer subtypes.

The analysis is descriptive/associational within selected recorded cases. It does not estimate prospective risk, incidence, screening performance, causality, mechanism, clinical utility, or patient benefit. The inherited population-prognosis gate remains `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED`.

## Site labels and feasibility audit

* `CRC`: any malignant `C18`--`C20` code on the frozen first malignant date;
* `other_GI`: no CRC code and any malignant `C15`--`C17` or `C21`--`C26` code on that date;
* `non_GI`: no `C15`--`C26` code on that date.

All exact-date malignant codes are retained for auditing. ## CBC trajectory variables and estimands

Inherited CBC semantics are unchanged: `30020` haemoglobin, `30040` MCV, `30080` platelet count, and `30070` RDW (not platelet). For each participant, calculate the inherited annualized slopes using `(instance1-instance0)/((L-B)/365.2425)` and orient adverse change as:

* `A_Hb = - annual Hb slope`;
* `A_MCV = - annual MCV slope`;
* `A_PLT = + annual platelet-count slope`.

To keep the experiment low-dimensional while representing the two prespecified biological patterns, form two fixed, outcome-independent trajectory dimensions before looking at site labels or outcomes:

* `Z_loss`: the equal-weight mean of the all-998-case standardized `A_Hb` and `A_MCV` values;
* `Z_infl`: the all-998-case standardized `A_PLT` value.

Each standard deviation is the pooled standard deviation of that trajectory variable in the frozen 998-case population, calculated once by a declared deterministic rule; no outcome, site label, model fit, or significance result enters the transformation. The signs are retained, so larger values mean more adverse blood-loss or thrombocytosis-oriented change. The two dimensions are not claimed to be validated biological mechanisms; they are prespecified summaries of the named CBC fields. RDW `30070` is not in either primary dimension and is reserved for an exploratory replacement audit only.

Let `C=(age_L, sex, hb1, mcv1, plt1, lag_late)`, exactly the parent's comparator set. Let `G_CRC` and `G_GI` be indicators for CRC and other GI respectively, with non-GI the reference. For each `g` in `{CRC, other_GI}` and each `k` in `{loss, infl}`, estimate the cross-fitted residual moment

`theta[g,k] = E[(I(group=g)-m_g(C)) (Z_k-g_k(C))]`.

Here `m_g(C)` is a cross-fitted multinomial nuisance probability and `g_k(C)` is a cross-fitted linear outcome nuisance; all nuisance fitting, standardization used for fitting, tuning, and predictions are fold-local and training-only. The target is a selected-case adjusted association/residual moment, not a causal effect or an unadjusted mean difference.

The two planned site contrasts are defined algebraically from the same four estimands, preserving the whole 998-case population:

1. `GI versus non-GI`: `theta[CRC,k] + theta[other_GI,k]` for each dimension `k`.
2. `CRC versus other GI`: `theta[CRC,k] - theta[other_GI,k]` for each dimension `k`.

The first asks whether GI-bearing cases jointly differ from non-GI cases. The second asks whether the CRC component differs from the other-GI component. All four component estimates and both two-dimensional contrast estimates are reported.

## Cross-fitting, hierarchy, and uncertainty

Use 20 prespecified repetitions with seeds `20260909` through `20260928`, five outer folds, and five inner folds. Outer folds are stratified by the six observed `group x lag_late` cells, not by group-by-lag-by-sex, because the smallest sex cell is five. Sex remains in `C`. Inner folds use the same group-by-lag stratification where feasible; if any required training/validation cell has fewer than five observations, the run fails the support gate rather than changing the design.

Use nested ridge nuisance tuning over `10^-4,...,10^4`, with the multinomial intercepts and `lag_late` unpenalized, and squared-error ridge for each trajectory nuisance. The declared repeated-cross-fit aggregation is to average each participant's four-dimensional held-out score contribution across the 20 repetitions, then use one participant-level heteroskedasticity-robust covariance and independent multiplier bootstrap. Repeated appearances are never treated as independent. Record all fold assignments, selected penalties, convergence, overlap, prediction ranges, calibration, and residual diagnostics as aggregate output.

The primary global gate is a four-degree-of-freedom Wald/multiplier test of all `theta[g,k]=0`, calibrated with 20,000 participant multipliers and a plus-one tail probability. If and only if the global gate rejects at two-sided alpha .05, test the two prespecified two-degree-of-freedom contrasts using a closed gate followed by Holm adjustment across the two contrasts. Report simultaneous 95% confidence regions for the four components and multiplicity-adjusted intervals for the two planned contrasts regardless of gate outcome. For the shared-GI interpretation, the CRC-versus-other-GI contrast additionally undergoes a two one-sided equivalence test with a fixed margin of 0.25 standardized trajectory-association units, chosen before execution as the largest difference regarded as practically small for this exploratory standardized estimand. Failure to establish equivalence is not evidence of a CRC-specific effect.

A rerun is computationally invalid if any required input/hash/population gate fails, any group-by-lag support cell is below five, any nuisance fit fails to converge, any score is nonfinite, any fold leaks observations, covariance inversion is ill-conditioned under the inherited numerical rule, or calibration diagnostics fail the prespecified thresholds. No alternate grouping, penalty, trajectory weight, fold scheme, or endpoint may replace a failed primary run; only the declared mixed-date exclusion sensitivity may be reported separately.

## Falsification and interpretation

A supportive shared-GI result requires the global gate and the GI-versus-non-GI contrast to reject with both dimensions in the adverse direction, while the CRC-versus-other-GI contrast is equivalent within the fixed margin. This supports a shared GI-associated pattern among selected recorded malignancy cases, not a mechanism or clinical utility.

A supportive CRC-specific result requires the global gate and CRC-versus-other-GI contrast to reject after multiplicity control, with a prespecified coherent adverse-direction difference in at least the blood-loss dimension or the inflammation/thrombocytosis dimension. A GI-versus-non-GI rejection without CRC/other-GI equivalence is classified as site heterogeneity rather than shared GI physiology.

An adverse result for the motivating broad-mask explanation is a global nonzero pattern with both planned contrasts inconsistent with the shared-GI and CRC-specific directional templates, or a multiplicity-controlled CRC-versus-other-GI difference opposing the anticipated direction. It does not establish that CBC trajectories are clinically irrelevant.

Inconclusive results include global non-rejection, failure of the shared-GI equivalence test, discordant dimensions, multiplicity-adjusted intervals spanning both meaningful and null regions, insufficient support, nuisance/calibration instability, or any invalidity gate. In particular, non-rejection alone is never called equivalence, conditional independence, or absence of a biological effect.

The slope-block permutation remains an implementation/calibration control only and is not a scientific randomization test. Any apparent pattern is falsified as a robust site-heterogeneity claim if it disappears under the declared sensitivity, has unstable signs across repetitions, or is driven by post hoc subtype regrouping.
