# Landmark same-draw AFP-vs-ALBI trajectory test for time to first liver-directed retreatment in HCC

Island: hcc (Chinese hospital HCC records). Episode 3. Child of [prior hypothesis].
Status: design + verified bindings + bounded label probe done; RESULTS ARE NOT COMPUTED HERE (discovery only).
Change from parent: replaces the naive within-patient slope with an explicit LANDMARK window, computes
AFP and ALBI slopes from the SAME draws, pre-specifies the two first-order threat tests
(surveillance/draw intensity; regression-to-the-mean/lead-time) and a falsifiable equivalence null.

## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy for HCC, routine follow-up panels repeat AFP, albumin and bilirubin.
The parent asked whether within-patient *trajectory* adds prognostic information beyond the
pre-treatment *baseline value*, and whether AFP kinetics add information beyond ALBI kinetics measured
in the same draws. That question remains open because the two designs closest to it (K1, K3) either did
not include AFP, did not adjust for how often a patient was sampled, or were too small to compare two
markers head-to-head [K1,K3]. K2 supplies the concrete opening: an AFP trajectory class that looked
prognostic univariably (HR 1.33) vanished after adjusting for baseline severity and stage (adj HR 1.01,
95% CI 0.77-1.33) [K2]. If AFP/ALBI kinetics behave like K2's class, an apparent trajectory effect is a
baseline-severity or sampling artefact, not new biology. Resolving this changes a real decision: whether
a hepatology/IR clinic should add trajectory-based triggers (early re-imaging / early switching) to its
post-TACE follow-up protocol, or keep baseline-value and fixed-schedule monitoring. A well-powered null
is itself decision-relevant.

**Tested hypothesis.** In patients surviving to a fixed landmark t* after the index liver-directed
procedure, the joint AFP-slope and ALBI-slope estimated from paired same-draw measurements in (index, t*]
predict time to first liver-directed retreatment after t*, beyond baseline AFP, baseline ALBI, age, sex,
treatment class and post-index draw count; and AFP-slope is not redundant with ALBI-slope.

**Strongest rival (leading alternative).** Any apparent trajectory effect is explained by (a) surveillance/
draw intensity and (b) regression-to-the-mean in within-patient slopes plus lead-time accumulation —
i.e. trajectory is a proxy for observation and baseline severity, not for tumour/liver biology. These
rivals predict (a) attenuation of the slope HRs once draw count is modelled and on a >=3-draws-in-window
restriction; (b) attenuation once a pre-index slope proxy is included.

## 2. Evidence vs conjecture (three inspected key works)

Established by inspected evidence: post-TACE ALBI trajectories separated OS/PFS and were an independent OS
risk factor in 501 BCLC B/C patients [K1]; a rising latent AFP class predicted survival univariably but
NOT after baseline/stage adjustment in 529 patients [K2]; a same-draw joint linear-mixed + Cox model of a
longitudinal marker is feasible and interpretable for PFS in TACE (n=30) [K3].
Conjecture tested here (not established by K1-K3): that same-draw AFP *and* ALBI slopes jointly carry
information beyond baseline values, treatment class and surveillance intensity, and are not redundant with
each other. [K1] had no AFP and no draw adjustment; [K2] is pre-diagnostic class membership, not a
same-draw post-treatment paired slope, and its adjustment result is the reason to doubt the claim; [K3] is
30 patients, one non-routine marker, no baseline-only comparison arm.

## 3. Design (frozen for compilation)

**Population.** Adults (age>=18) with (a) >=1 row in `diagnoses` with `诊断名称` matching
肝细胞癌|原发性肝癌|肝癌|肝恶性肿瘤 and `诊断类型` in {入院诊断,出院诊断,门诊诊断}; and (b) >=1 row
in `procedures` with `手术` matching a LIVER-DIRECTED THERAPY (TACE/栓塞/化疗栓塞, 切除, 消融/射频,
微波消融), EXCLUDING purely diagnostic 肝动脉造影 and non-liver procedures (中心静脉置管, 抗肿瘤免疫治疗).
Index date = the earliest parseable `开始时间` for such a procedure. Cohort expected ~37,764 HCC patients
with >=1 liver-directed procedure (parent).

**Temporal boundaries.** Index `开始时间` in 2010-07-27 .. 2026-01-01 (observed encounter range). Landmark
t* = index + 180 days. Window W = (index, t*]. Prediction horizon = t* .. last encounter (`出院时间`,
administrative censoring). Baseline window = 90 days before index.

**Exposure (the child's core change).** AFP-slope and ALBI-slope are estimated on the SAME draws: keep only
`检验时间` at which BOTH an exact-label 甲胎蛋白 row and (exact-label 白蛋白 row AND exact-label 总胆红素
row) occur within +/-2 days (draw co-timing tolerance); collapse each such draw to one AFP, one albumin,
one bilirubin value (median if duplicates). ALBI = 0.66*log10(bilirubin[umol/L]) - 0.085*albumin[g/L].
Per patient fit slope per 30 days of log10(AFP+1) and of ALBI over the paired draws in W (>=2 paired draws
required). This removes immortal-time/lead-time bias (all exposure data precede t*) and prevents
future-draw leakage. Sensitivity: also fit a linear mixed-effects model (random intercept + random slope)
on the same paired draws; report both.

**Outcome (primary, inherited and frozen).** Time from t* (landmark) to first liver-directed retreatment
after index, censored at last encounter; = earliest subsequent liver-directed procedure `开始时间` strictly
after t*. Patients retreated during (index, t*] are excluded (already-event / immortal-time). Mortality is
NOT an endpoint (death ascertainable in only ~29-863/43,815 — not reintroduced). Documented
复发|转移|进展 (~18,118 patients) has no reliable timestamp -> secondary flag-only, not an endpoint.

**Covariates.** baseline AFP (last 甲胎蛋白 <=90 d pre-index), baseline ALBI (last pre-index albumin+
bilirubin), age, sex, treatment class (first procedure class), post-index draw count (all lab draws in
(index, last encounter]), and for the RM test the pre-index AFP slope (from >=2 pre-index AFP draws).

**Models (same question, same data).**
- M0 (simple baseline): Cox on age, sex, treatment class, baseline AFP, baseline ALBI, post-index draw count.
- M1: M0 + AFP-slope. M2: M0 + ALBI-slope. M3 (head-to-head): M0 + AFP-slope + ALBI-slope.
- M4 (substantive alternative): same-draw joint model — linear mixed sub-model for log10(AFP+1) and for
  ALBI with random intercept+slope, linked to a Cox sub-model of the retreatment hazard at the landmark
  (shared random effects), fit on the paired draws in W. This recovers within-patient change and draw
  irregularity that M0 cannot express; it is compared with M1-M3 on held-out discrimination.

**Split / evaluation.** Deterministic patient-level 70/30 split by hashed 患者主索引 (leak-free). Fit on
train, report held-out Harrell C-index; uncertainty by 1000 patient-level bootstrap resamples. Report event
count and rate first (main feasibility risk).

## 4. Pre-specified threat tests

(a) Surveillance/draw intensity. Post-index draw count is in M0-M3. Sensitivity: restrict to patients with
>=3 paired draws in W (fixed minimum monitoring) and re-estimate slope HRs. Adverse to the hypothesis if
slope HRs attenuate toward 1 and/or lose significance under this restriction.
(b) Regression-to-the-mean / baseline-proxy. Restrict to patients with >=2 pre-index AFP draws; add the
pre-index AFP slope proxy to M3 and measure how much the post-index AFP-slope HR attenuates. Adverse if
the post-index slope HR moves substantially toward 1. K2's adjusted HR 1.01 is the prior expectation
under this rival.

## 5. Pre-specified falsification (equivalence null)

Declare the trajectory hypothesis FALSIFIED (equivalence null) if BOTH:
(i) the 95% CI of the AFP-slope HR AND the 95% CI of the ALBI-slope HR in M3 each include 1; AND
(ii) the change in held-out C-index from adding BOTH slopes to M0 has a 1000-resample bootstrap 95% CI
entirely within +/-0.01 (i.e. no material discrimination gain).
Report all four quantities (two HRs with CIs, delta-C-index, its CI). Supportive result: at least one slope
HR with CI excluding 1 in M3 AND delta-C-index CI lower bound >0. Inconclusive: mixed pattern (e.g. one HR
significant but delta-C-index CI straddling 0 / inside +/-0.01), or wide CIs from too few events -> do not
claim either direction; state the event-count-driven imprecision.

## 6. Baseline vs substantive alternative (recorded before selection)

M0 (baseline-value-only Cox) is adequate exactly if trajectories are only baseline proxies. The same-draw
joint model M4 is the substantive alternative because it estimates within-patient change from irregular,
unequal draws and lets the hazard use the current slope — information a baseline-value-only model cannot
represent. It could reveal that change (not level) carries risk, or that an apparent effect is a
draw-pattern artefact. Continuous slope (M1-M3) vs joint/mixed (M4) is compared on the same frozen data;
both are CPU-feasible (Cox + lme4/JM fits + 1000 bootstrap within the solver envelope; optional single-GPU
only if bootstrap repetitions are parallelised — no hard GPU requirement). Selection among M3/M4 is
decided by held-out C-index with the pre-specified falsification rule above, not by model complexity.

## 8. Limits, adjudication, missing evidence

- Primary outcome is a practice-pattern proxy (retreatment timing depends on TACE scheduling) — a stated
  limitation inherited from the parent; equivalence-null interpretation must not over-claim tumour biology.
  Progression/response (mRECIST) and mortality need imaging + registry/adjudication that are unavailable.
- No outcomes are computed in discovery; event rate is the first feasibility risk the solver must report.
- No unit column in labs -> no cross-assay pooling; ALBI computed in native units.
- External validation absent (single Chinese institution).
