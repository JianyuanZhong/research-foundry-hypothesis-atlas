# Complete-packet, version-faithful 0.80 patient-availability frontier for documentary-M2 review

## Targeted repair and hypothesis

This targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]` preserves the mature adult first-eligible HCC-resection population; coherent operation-clock and prior-treatment envelope; 24-hour primary cutoff and 12/48/72-hour diagnostics; 90-day imaging and 365-day treatment lookbacks; possibility quarantine; 2015–2018 development, 2019 locking and separate 2020/2021 tests; encounter-documentary M2; whole-accession reports and indivisible pathology composites; six corpus-wide reader books and nine global book pairs; patient-balanced locked B/R/G/same-fit-Gmask models; fixed absolute K; comparator closure; strict >5 capture margin; exact outer optimization; whole-input version hashes; and one full-roster mask restricted after whole-patient deletion.

It repairs two defects.

1. The parent's `C_PB=(1/N)sum_i[(1/n_i)sum_u r_iu]` is an equal-patient mean report fraction, not a patient-availability rate. If ten patients each have four of five reports, C_PB=.80 and patient-any reach=1, but no patient has the complete report packet consumed by the all-content model. Adding a same-threshold any-report gate would not repair this: `C_PB<=C_any`, so `C_PB>=.80` already implies `C_any>=.80`.
2. The named “one-hot” outcomes overlap. Coverage infeasibility may coexist with an accession-only artifact, and comparator-pattern labels may coexist with visibility brittleness. A verifier needs disjoint primary predicates, not prose ordering.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> In each of 2020 and 2021 and every coherent source/pathology/reader world, at least 80% of the complete eligible patient roster can have every eligible CT/MRI report unit proved finalized, intended-service-readable and byte-identical before the decision cutoff; and every full-roster whole-unit mask meeting that complete-packet threshold preserves all six G-versus-R, G-versus-B and G-versus-same-fit-Gmask documentary-M2 capture margins strictly above five, before deletion and after anchored removal of any one test patient.

The 0.80 threshold is exact: if A of N patients have complete packets, require `5A>=4N`. A margin equal to five fails. If 0.80 completeness is unattainable, the service-availability component is falsified, never passed vacuously.

This is a retrospective finite-corpus documentary-review stress experiment. It does not test biological MVI, treatment selection, actual workload, clinician action, recurrence, survival, safety, cost, fairness or benefit.

## Version-faithful complete-packet availability

For every eligible nonblank accession preserve:

`H_HCC(u)=SHA256(schema_version || parser_version || length-delimited ordered tuples(source_row_ordinal,field_name,exact_raw_bytes,V/A/E_lineage))`

over every exact-deduplicated `检查所见` and `检查诊断` component. It commits to component count/order, field/empty boundaries, parser lineage and bytes. Similar wording, impression-only equality, edit distance, equal score or reader judgment cannot substitute.

A bit `r_u=1` is permitted only if a same-snapshot external archive proves before c_d that the exact whole version was finalized, unretracted, uniquely crosswalked to the HCC accession, intended-service-readable and byte-identical at full pipeline input. Preliminary, amended-away, retracted, unmatched, inaccessible, nonidentical, source-unavailable and blank units cannot count. Missing archive evidence is unknown and fails the evidence gate; it is not silently zero. Hiding a unit replaces every body-derived S/Q/X block consistently in R/G/Gmask with its development unavailable block; B and acquisition-only Z stay fixed.

For full test-year world omega, let I be the complete eligible roster, N=|I|, and E_i all temporally eligible CT/MRI units before semantic-source filtering, including nonblank accessions and blank pseudo-units. Let n_i=|E_i|.

`a_i^all(r)=1` iff `n_i>0` and every u in E_i has r_u=1; otherwise zero.

Primary coverage:

`C_all(r,omega)=sum_i a_i^all(r)/N`.

Thus C_all>=.80 means at least 80% of all eligible patients have every report unit expected by the model. Zero-unit patients and patients with any blank, source-unavailable or unproved unit remain in N and contribute zero.

Mandatory diagnostics are:

- `C_frac=(1/N)sum_i 1(n_i>0)(sum_u r_u/n_i)`, the parent's equal-patient mean fraction;
- `C_any=(1/N)sum_i 1(sum_u r_u>0)`, any-report reach;
- `C_A=sum_i,u r_u/sum_i n_i`, legacy accession coverage.

Verify `C_all<=C_frac<=C_any` for every mask. These quantities answer different questions; none may be described as another.

Let r_max expose all and only externally proved releasable units. `C_all^max=C_all(r_max)`. Primary family:

`R_all,.80(omega)={r: r<=r_max and 5*sum_i a_i^all(r)>=4N}`.

Empty families never pass.

For each whole test patient j, remove j and all j states/outcomes/units, then restrict the same full-roster mask. Do not requalify coverage, recompute N/n_i/weights, expose replacement reports, refit/recalibrate, change K/ties/books/worlds, or alter survivor feature hashes. Require N-j>=K. Survivor-denominator coverage is descriptive only and cannot admit a baseline-ineligible mask.

## Exact falsification and genuinely one-hot output

Compile finite patient domains carrying membership/year/Y, Z/S/Q/X, report sets/status/hashes, scores, source/form/terminal hashes, ties and raw backpointers. Linearize complete status exactly for n_i>0:

- `a_i<=r_u` for every u;
- `a_i>=1-n_i+sum_u r_u`;

and set a_i=0 when n_i=0. Threshold arithmetic is integer `5sum a_i>=4N`.

For each year/world/global book pair/coordinate/deletion, exact MIP or certified equivalent chooses one coherent state per patient and one full-roster mask, ranks exactly K in both arms, and searches for integer failure `20*(captures_G-captures_comparator)<=K`. Comparator arms share world and mask.

Define:

- Q: all source/header/hash, adjudication-record, chronology, roster, N/K/event, leakage, fit, version-evidence, mask-invariance, replay, exact-solver and predicate-consistency gates pass; consistency includes rejecting `A and Fmax and not F80`;
- A: every required world has C_all^max>=.80;
- F80: an attained qualifying mask has any required undeleted/deleted margin <=5;
- Fmax: any required margin under that world's unique r_max is <=5.

When A holds, Fmax implies F80 because r_max belongs to the threshold family. Emit exactly one primary label:

1. **inconclusive** iff not Q;
2. **complete-packet threshold infeasible** iff Q and not A;
3. **complete-packet visibility-robust supportive** iff Q and A and not F80;
4. **maximum-content non-supportive** iff Q and A and F80 and Fmax;
5. **complete-packet visibility-brittle** iff Q and A and F80 and not Fmax.

These predicates are pairwise disjoint and exhaustive even for a malformed primitive tuple: a violation of `Fmax=>F80` makes Q false and therefore yields inconclusive. Assert exactly one label and the implication under A.

Also compute `b_all=max C_all` over every attained failing coordinate/deletion witness, or NONE if no failure. Given Q and A, support is equivalent to `b_all<.80` or NONE; equality fails. This direct calculation must agree with the threshold search. Use exact A/N values, not a .01 grid.

Legacy accession/fraction results, accession-only artifact, semantic-evidence-only, quota-policy-only, modality sensitivity and all-world adverse coordinates are nonexclusive secondary flags. They cannot rescue or replace the primary label. Their overlap is reported intentionally.

Require zero integer gap, exact rational replay, residual <=1e-8, complete rank-inversion constraints, all tied maximizers, raw/quotient replay, and brute-force agreement on fixtures through 12 patients and real shards through 20. Timeout, state cap, mask sampling, favorable incumbent, omitted world/book/deletion, missing witness, changed survivor feature or failed replay makes Q false.

The primary estimand is the exact frozen finite corpus. Bootstrap diagnostics cannot create a target-population confidence claim or change the exact label.

## Interpretation

Support means only that, within the frozen corpus and complete external version evidence, every allowed placement giving full eligible report packets to >=80% of patients preserves all six strict fixed-K documentary-M2 margins and survives anchored one-patient deletion. It does not establish clinician viewing or benefit.

Threshold infeasibility falsifies the specified complete-packet service requirement, not report timeliness. Maximum-content non-supportive means a required margin fails even with every proved report exposed. Visibility-brittle means maximum content passes, but an allowed >=80% complete-packet mask fails. Both falsify universal robustness; neither proves semantics useless or harmful. A coordinate is all-world adverse only if its upper endpoint is <=5.

Inconclusive means evidence/computation cannot resolve the finite-corpus hypothesis. Favorable AUC, an accession/C_frac pass or one favorable world cannot override it.

Dictionary correctness, reader qualifications, Chinese semantics, specimen linkage/sampling, biological M2, archive completeness, actual service/view access, workload, action, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, fairness and benefit require adjudication, missing source-system data, prospective silent workflow measurement, external cohorts or another study.
