# Proposal: A pre-action repeated-landmark feasibility trial of vasopressor discontinuation order

## Question, supported evidence, and unresolved claim

The clinically important question is whether, among adults receiving concurrent low-dose norepinephrine (NE) and vasopressin (VP) who are stable enough to consider de-escalation, initiating NE discontinuation first versus VP discontinuation first changes 6-hour clinically significant hemodynamic failure. The prespecified directional hypothesis is that NE-first lowers unfavorable 6-hour status by at least 5 absolute percentage points.

Existing evidence supports only a narrower claim. Prior observational studies and meta-analyses suggest that discontinuation order may be associated with short-term hypotension, but estimates are heterogeneous, recent weighted evidence is compatible with no difference, and no mortality benefit or universal causal superiority is established. The unresolved claim is a causal-like contrast at a genuinely pre-action clinical decision. The MIMIC experiment can test computability, action overlap, proximal outcome associations, and robustness; it cannot recover clinician intent, true bedside administration, exchangeability, adjudicated shock phenotype, or generalizability.

This successor combines the first parent’s boundary-aware outcome and evidence discipline with the second parent’s genuinely pre-action repeated landmarks. Its primary result is a gated feasibility conclusion, not an effect estimate manufactured despite failed positivity.

## Primary estimand and population

Use a sequence of nested trials in the first ICU stay of each adult `subject_id`. A candidate landmark is opened every 60 minutes while the same reconstructed NE/VP overlap remains active, beginning after at least six continuous hours of overlap. The first landmark is retained as a required simpler sensitivity; repeated landmarks are primary only as a design for feasibility, with dependence handled by subject-level clustering and equal patient-level weighting so prolonged episodes cannot dominate.

Eligibility must use only information with clinical time at or before the landmark:

1. Adult age, calculated from `hosp/patients.anchor_age + year(hosp/admissions.admittime) - patients.anchor_year`; preserve the MIMIC convention for ages over 89.
2. First ICU stay per subject, alive and physically in the ICU at the landmark.
3. Both NE and VP have positive, supported, plausible normalized doses at the landmark and 30 minutes earlier. Current doses are `0 < NE <= 0.20 mcg/kg/min` and `0 < VP <= 0.04 U/min`, and neither dose rises over the preceding 30 minutes.
4. No epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine interval overlaps the preceding two hours. Mechanical circulatory support exclusion and inotrope/RRT flags require the procedure/item dictionary and are mandatory before a final effect analysis; they were not completed in the feasibility audit.
5. In the two hours before the landmark, at least three valid MAP observations, at least three of four 30-minute bins represented, at least one MAP in the final 30 minutes, median MAP at least 65 mmHg, and no MAP below 55 mmHg.
6. No future stop, future restart, future ICU residence, post-landmark MAP, or survival condition may define eligibility.

The estimand is the 6-hour risk difference and risk ratio for unfavorable status under initiating NE-first versus VP-first within a common 60-minute grace period from the landmark. Assignment starts at the landmark. A treatment action is not required for entry; early death, rescue, or no action are observed consequences of the decision opportunity.

## Administration reconstruction and strategies

Read positive-rate rows from `mimic-iv-3.1/icu/inputevents.csv.gz` for NE item `221906` and VP item `222315`. Normalize only the following:

- NE `mcg/kg/min` directly; NE `mcg/min` divided by `patientweight` only when weight is 30–300 kg.
- VP `units/hour` divided by 60; VP `units/min` directly.

Reject unsupported units, nonpositive rates, invalid intervals, and implausible normalized values rather than reinterpreting them. The feasibility code used broad plausibility caps of 5 mcg/kg/min for NE and 0.2 U/min for VP and records rejected rows.

Within `stay_id,itemid`, union overlapping or abutting positive intervals. Bridge a positive gap of at most five minutes only when `orderid` or `linkorderid` continues and the prior row is not explicitly stopped, paused, or cancelled. Use `starttime` and `endtime` for clinical chronology; `storetime` is only a tie-breaker. A true stop requires no positive row beginning within the next 10 minutes. Retain `statusdescription`, `continueinnextdept`, and boundary-quality fields as evidence, not as stand-alone truth. The competing pressors are epinephrine `221289,229617`, phenylephrine `221749,229630,229631,229632`, and dopamine `221662`.

At each eligible landmark, clone the trial to two strategies:

- NE-first: a qualifying NE stop occurs within 60 minutes while VP remains active at the action.
- VP-first: the symmetric VP stop occurs within 60 minutes while NE remains active.

Before an action, neither clone is censored. At the first qualifying action, retain the compatible clone and artificially censor the incompatible clone; censor both at 60 minutes if neither action occurs; classify stops within five minutes as simultaneous and censor both for the primary contrast. Restart, rescue, death, and escalation remain outcome components, not eligibility exclusions. Artificial-censoring weights in 15-minute bins are required only if the action contrast remains estimable after the feasibility gates.

No analysis may define eligibility by looking backward from a future observed stop. No grace-period or dose criterion may be broadened after seeing the action counts.

## Primary endpoint and ICU-discharge rule

The primary endpoint is a binary unfavorable status by six hours after the landmark. Failure is the first of:

1. death;
2. restart or re-initiation of the first-stopped study agent;
3. a new competing pressor (epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine);
4. NE escalation by at least 0.05 mcg/kg/min above landmark dose or NE re-initiation after an NE-first stop;
5. two valid MAP values below 60 mmHg in separate 30-minute bins, separated by 5–120 minutes; or
6. ICU exit while any vasopressor is active, a rescue occurred, or `continueinnextdept` indicates infusion continuation.

ICU discharge is not censoring and is not handled with a second competing-risk model in the primary endpoint. Death by six hours is failure. An alive ICU exit before six hours is success only when all vasopressors are off, no rescue or prior failure occurred, and no infusion-continuation signal is present. An alive exit with any active pressor or continuation signal is failure. Alive patients remaining in the ICU without failure at six hours are success only when post-landmark outcome ascertainment is adequate. Patients without adequate MAP observation are reported as endpoint-indeterminate in an observation-bias sensitivity; the required rescue/death/pressor-exit composite remains monitoring-independent.

MAP uses `icu/chartevents.csv.gz` item `220052` (arterial) and `220181` (non-invasive), `charttime` rather than `storetime`, values 20–200 mmHg, and blank/zero `warning`. Duplicate timestamps prefer arterial. Post-landmark adequacy is at least four represented 30-minute bins and at least one MAP in the final two hours of follow-up. No last-observation carry-forward is used for the primary failure endpoint.

Report death, restart, rescue/competing pressor, NE escalation, MAP failure, and pressor-at-exit components separately, together with an ordinal status. Secondary outcomes may include MAP area below 65, 24-hour pressor-free status/hours, lactate change, ventilation, RRT, AKI, ICU/hospital discharge, and in-hospital death, but no secondary outcome supports a mortality-benefit claim.

## Analysis and feasibility gates

First publish attrition, action counts, action probabilities, MAP coverage, exit classifications, and dose rejection. Only if all gates pass may the study estimate a comparative effect:

- at least 200 unique eligible stays;
- at least 40 primary failures associated with each strategy;
- probability of each action by 60 minutes at least 0.05 in every retained major propensity stratum;
- absolute core weighted SMD below 0.10;
- strategy-specific ESS above 30% of initiators;
- acceptable artificial-censor weight tails, with no more than 1% above the prespecified truncation threshold;
- no clinically important arm-linked MAP observation imbalance;
- boundary-adjudication PPV at least 0.80 in each arm and arm difference below 0.10.

Use a transparent baseline propensity model and pooled logistic outcome model, with augmented inverse-probability weighting as the primary adjustment and cross-fitted flexible models as sensitivity. Cluster uncertainty by `subject_id`, retaining repeated landmarks and both clones within each subject bootstrap resample. Report unadjusted and adjusted risks, risk differences, risk ratios, confidence intervals, action counts, weight percentiles, ESS, balance, and missingness.

The completed audit already fails the marginal action diagnostic under the frozen design: among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, NE-first occurred 211 times (3.49%), VP-first 167 times (2.76%), simultaneous action 24 times (0.40%), and no action 5,638 times (93.34%). These are pooled landmark proportions, not conditional propensity-stratum proof, but both treatment probabilities are below the declared 5% gate. Therefore the prespecified current conclusion is comparative causal estimation infeasible unless a subsequent, non-data-adaptive conditional audit somehow demonstrates that the retained analysis population satisfies the gates. The appropriate output is a negative feasibility/descriptive study, not a causal estimate.

## Falsification and interpretation rules

Required diagnostics include pseudo-landmarks six hours before observed action labels while both drugs remain active; action and monitoring rates by care unit, hour, and anchor-year group; arterial-only and observation-density-restricted analyses; leave-one-care-unit and leave-one-anchor-year-group analyses; alternate 0/5/10/15-minute bridge rules, 5/15-minute stop windows, simultaneous windows, and MAP thresholds; and blinded clinical/pharmacy review of sampled boundaries. Any post-action information used for an outcome must be excluded from baseline eligibility.

Supportive results would require all gates to pass, a risk difference favoring NE-first by at least five points with a confidence interval excluding no difference, coherent component results, and no concerning increase in death, rescue, or RRT. They would establish at most a reproducible association in this MIMIC decision population and motivate prospective testing, not clinical superiority.

Adverse results include a VP-first advantage of at least five points or an NE-first reduction in recorded MAP failure accompanied by more rescue, death, or RRT; these falsify the directional hypothesis. Inconclusive results include failed positivity, insufficient events, poor balance or ESS, unstable weights, endpoint bounds crossing clinically important benefit and harm, differential monitoring, boundary misclassification, or direction reversal under reasonable rules. The present action-positivity result is already a valuable negative finding: the frozen MIMIC design does not currently support a credible comparative effect estimate.

## Limitations and required next evidence

The current audit does not compute mechanical-support exclusion, full phenotype covariates, baseline propensity models, artificial-censor weights, ESS, conditional action probabilities, primary failure counts, or an effect estimate. It does not adjudicate true administration, bag changes, clinician intent, shock phenotype, perfusion, or MAP-related clinical significance. Repeated landmarks are correlated and pooled action proportions are diagnostics only. ICU exit classification is preliminary and must inspect all pressors, rescue before exit, and continuation fields before endpoint compilation. MIMIC’s retrospective charting cannot establish exchangeability; any stronger clinical recommendation requires blinded adjudication and prospective, preferably randomized, evidence.

The negative feasibility conclusion is not a failure of the hypothesis. It means that this exact, non-leaking 60-minute discontinuation strategy lacks adequate observed action overlap in the available MIMIC snapshot, so the clinically consequential comparison should be taken to prospective protocol testing rather than rescued by backward-indexing, post-treatment selection, or data-adaptive relaxation of eligibility.
