# Prefix-only, capacity-paced allocation of preoperative review for documentary M2

## Unresolved question, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The clinical decision is narrow: as adults approach a first eligible HCC liver resection, can interpretable information in routine preoperative CT/MRI report bodies allocate a fixed, time-released supply of multidisciplinary review slots so that more patients whose resection pathology eventually documents high-grade microvascular invasion (M2) are reviewed than under laboratory-independent comparators?

The action is review allocation only. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant/adjuvant treatment, systemic or locoregional therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

**Strongest claim supported now.** Direct inspection of snapshot `[source checksum]`, its catalog/schema files, and every live CSV header establishes patient/encounter-linked demographics; two-source procedure rows with start/end fields; examination accessions with acquisition-like `Start Time`, modality/provenance fields, and eventual report bodies; untimed same-encounter pathology narratives; and dated medication/order records. The source has no report authored/final/release/view time or immutable version, pathology time/specimen/slide/block key or sampling protocol, appointment/MDT roster, review decision, capacity ledger, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, treatment effect, or patient benefit. Therefore the HCC data can test historical stored-content allocation under reconstructed arrival times, not actual decision-time report access or benefit from review.

Current full-text evidence supports importance, not this result. Neves and Soares (2026; DOI `10.3390/cancers18132028`, PMCID `PMC13360217`; frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reviewed 36 HCC early-recurrence AI studies: 35 were retrospective, only 6 externally validated, and calibration and clinical utility were inconsistently evaluated. Fan et al. (2026; DOI `10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944`, PMCID `PMC13187967`; frozen XML source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) found M2 in 106/621 resected patients and only modest LDH discrimination, explicitly calling for incremental-model and external/prospective validation. Neither study establishes routine-report semantic value, report visibility, sequential queue feasibility, or review benefit here. The ambition papers are demonstrations of rigor only; the Cell main article remains unavailable and is not claimed read.

The valid parent `[prior hypothesis]` fixes the number of reviews across source states, but its primary top-K queue ranks all 2020–2021 candidates together. An early patient can therefore lose a slot to a patient whose decision time occurs months later. That is a retrospective oracle, not a policy that could act when each patient arrives. The child makes a **prefix-only, calendar-paced policy** primary. It freezes an offered-slot budget and release schedule before test outcomes, permits each decision to depend only on the fitted model, the current patient's admissible pre-decision record, calendar time, and slots already used, and forbids future candidates. The parent's top-K result is retained only as an upper benchmark for anticipation optimism.

## Falsifiable hypothesis and estimands

Let `s` range over the complete pre-outcome certified outer set of coherent source states. One state jointly fixes operative episode/clock, selected encounter, eligibility and year, prior recorded treatment, report accession/clock/pattern/mask, pathology composite/outcome, and chronological arrival. Components may never be mixed across states.

Use separate calendar-year service cycles `y=2020,2021`. Before pathology grade or semantic feature extraction, compute `N_min,y=min_s N_y(s)`. Available-data sensitivity budgets are absolute integers `K_yq=floor(q*N_min,y)` for `q={0.05,0.10,0.20}`, frozen once and invariant across source states, models, and bootstrap refits. They are workload benchmarks, not evidence of real capacity. A later external audit may instead preregister absolute `K_ext,y`; HCC alone cannot support deployment.

For model M in {semantic S, same-fit masked S0, independent structured B}, define a frozen sequential policy `pi_M,q`. On each state-specific arrival `j`, ordered by `t_dec`, it can use only:
1. M's frozen score from information assigned to that arrival;
2. the number of slots already used in that calendar year;
3. the calendar day and a frozen cumulative release curve;
4. development/2019 tuning estimates frozen before 2020.

It cannot inspect future arrivals, test outcomes, future test-set score ranks, final annual N, or another model's decisions. The release curve `L_yq(d)=floor(K_yq*F_2015-2019(d))`, where `F_2015-2019(d)` is the pooled development/tuning empirical cumulative fraction of eligible annual arrivals by day-of-year, monotonized and frozen before test. On day d, at most `L_yq(d)` slots may have been used. Each model has a threshold `tau_Mq(d)` fitted only in 2015–2018 and selected/recalibrated in 2019 to maximize documentary-M2 capture per offered slot subject to the release curve and a utilization floor. If an arriving score meets the threshold and a released unused slot exists, allocate; otherwise do not. No end-of-year forced filling, recall, or replacement is allowed. Same-time ties use a frozen hash of `patient master index`; date-interval clock alternatives remain separate source states.

For offered K, define
`R_offer(M,s,y,q)=100/K_yq * sum_i A_i(M,s,y,q)Y_i(s)`,
`U(M,s,y,q)=sum_i A_i/K_yq`, and false reviews per 100 offered analogously. The two co-primary contrasts are:
- `D=R_offer(S)-R_offer(S0)`, attributing action changes to the semantic block within the exact fitted S pipeline;
- `C=R_offer(S)-R_offer(B)`, comparing complete independently optimized strategies at equal offered capacity.

**P-online-store:** under all selected eventual report bodies exposed, the one-sided 97.5% simultaneous lower endpoints for D and C exceed five additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered slots for every q and separately in both 2020 and 2021, while every model has worst-state utilization at least 0.90 and all validity gates pass.

**P-attainability:** P-online-store passes and S's online capture is at least 80% of its state-matched retrospective top-K oracle capture at every year/q, using a simultaneous lower bound for the ratio. This tests whether favorable oracle ranking survives the no-future-information constraint.

**P-access:** conditional on P-online-store, calculate the parent's exact CT/MRI adversarial visibility frontier for the online state machine. A nonempty frontier says only that stated exact-version access guarantees would suffice; it does not say they occurred.

The HCC-only hypothesis is falsified at a year/q if, with computation and evidence gates valid, the simultaneous upper endpoint for D or C is <=5; <=0 supports no/harmful incremental capture. An oracle-positive but online-adverse result falsifies operational attainability of the retrospective ranking result.
