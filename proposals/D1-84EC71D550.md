# Can outcome-blind calibration rescue the all-hour excess-FiO2 burden—or should eICU stop at measurement?

## Status, opening, and newly required deliverable

This is a measurement-first child of \`[prior hypothesis]\`. It preserves the parent's population, hour-24 pulmonary landmark, hours-24–36 all-hour exposure, hour-36 outcome origin, seven-day hospital-death/live-discharge endpoint, and descriptive pulmonary interaction estimand. It does not report or inspect mortality. It changes the execution order because a new outcome-blind diagnostic shows that the all-hour burden is not assumption-free point identified in a clinically important sparse-record tail and that a learned imputation surrogate does not outperform a transparent persistence baseline.

ICU-ROX establishes that an oxygen-target protocol can alter exposure without establishing benefit on ventilator-free days [K1]. A mechanistic review distinguishes inspired hyperoxia from arterial hyperoxaemia, describes experimental oxygen–mechanical-stress synergy, and emphasizes inconsistent clinical mortality evidence [K2]. LUNG SAFE shows incomplete, severity-dependent clinician recognition despite prospective Berlin-definition components [K3]. The strongest supported claim is therefore limited: oxygen exposure is modifiable; inspired exposure is not equivalent to arterial hyperoxaemia; and pulmonary-injury labels can depend on severity and ascertainment. Neither these works nor the eICU audit establishes oxygen toxicity, validates delivered FiO2, or validates the proposed pulmonary phenotype.

The unchanged unresolved clinical hypothesis is:

**Among adult first ICU stays alive and still in ICU at hour 36, with PaO2 80–120 mm Hg paired to FiO2 during hours 18–24, the pre-hour-24 acute-pulmonary-injury proxy modifies the association between mean all-hour excess FiO2 during hours 24–36 and seven-day post-hour-36 in-hospital death.**

The unchanged primary estimand, if measurement gates pass, is the pulmonary-minus-comparator difference in the pre-hour-24-standardized seven-day cumulative-incidence risk-difference slope per +0.10 in
\`B_i=(1/12) sum_t (F_it-0.40)+\`, with live discharge competing. This remains descriptive, not a treatment effect.

The new primary hypothesis is entirely outcome blind:

**Contiguous missing FiO2 blocks and the pre-hour-24 pulmonary phenotype can be measured with calibrated uncertainty narrow and nondifferential enough to resolve a 3-point pulmonary interaction: in each stratum, 90% prediction intervals for artificially masked FiO2 blocks achieve at least 88% simultaneous held-hospital coverage, the resulting validation-bounded burden has median width <=0.10 and 75th percentile <=0.15, and clinician-review phenotype gates pass.**

The actual scientific deliverable is newly estimated measurement evidence: an exact frozen cohort and 12-hour panel; logical burden intervals; held-hospital contiguous-block calibration for a transparent baseline and a structured alternative; cross-source agreement; an outcome-blind clinician review; and a continue/revise/abandon decision. Mortality analysis is not required for successful completion. A failed gate is an interpretable measurement result.

## Why the rival is strong and what the diagnostic established

The strongest rival is observation-driven apparent dose: respiratory settings are charted when support is changing or severe, while stable or noninvasive periods can be sparse. Pulmonary proxy positivity also follows diagnosis entry, ABG use, ventilation, and hospital practice. A positive mortality interaction could therefore be manufactured by differential exposure and phenotype ascertainment.

For an unsupported hour, the logical excess interval is \`[0,0.60]\`. Therefore each unsupported hour adds exactly \`0.60/12=0.05\` to the all-hour burden width before any error in recorded values. Three unsupported hours necessarily violate a width of 0.10. This is mathematical nonidentification under logical bounds, not evidence that the true setting varied.

The parent outcome-blind audit found 1,869 anchored stays: 804 injury-proxy, 965 no-term, and 100 discordant. Recorded FiO2 covered at least six hours in only 52.2% and 47.0% of the primary strata, with major hospital heterogeneity.

A new bounded reconstruction read no death/discharge fields. In a respiratory-chart-only anchored cohort with the permitted 120-minute carry, logical burden-width 75th percentiles were 0.20 in 906 injury-proxy stays and 0.40 in 1,093 no-term stays; only 60.5% and 53.9% respectively had logical width <=0.10. The median widths were 0.05 and 0.10, so the exposure is not fundamentally unidentifiable for every stay, but the sparse tail cannot support the proposed interaction without a validated tightening assumption. This reconstruction is deliberately not substituted for the final cohort: it used respiratory-chart FiO2 for anchor/support and did not complete every parent exclusion.

The matched pseudo-missing check used 3,078 directly observed respiratory-setting re-entry hours after gaps >120 minutes in five hospital-held-out folds. Last-setting persistence, falling back to the anchor, achieved MAE 0.0324 and 90th-percentile absolute error 0.10. A histogram-gradient-boosting alternative using hour, pulmonary stratum, P/F, anchor FiO2, prior FiO2/gap, and same-hour lab FiO2 was worse (MAE 0.0387; 90th percentile 0.101). Injury/no-term learned 90th percentiles were 0.0955/0.1074. Thus the learned model does not justify narrower intervals. In a broader diagnostic cohort, lab–respiratory pairs had median absolute difference 0 and 90th percentile 0.10 in both primary strata; disagreement >0.05 was 12.2% versus 11.6%. These are feasibility observations, not final calibrated estimates.

## Immutable population, clocks, phenotype, exposure, and outcome

Index \`patient.patientunitstayid\`. Include age >=18 (map \`age='> 89'\` to 90 for adjustment only), the first ICU stay in each \`patienthealthsystemstayid\` by lowest valid \`unitvisitnumber\` then \`patientunitstayid\`, and \`unitdischargeoffset>2160\`. Require alive/in ICU at minute 2160. Exclude impossible chronology, burns, documented extracorporeal support by minute 1440, and exact \`carePlanGeneral.cplgroup='Care Limitation'\`, \`cplitemvalue='Comfort measures only'\` by minute 1440. Other treatment-limit indicators remain covariates/sensitivities.

Boundaries remain:

- baseline and phenotype: admission through minute 1440;
- anchor: last PaO2 80–120 in minutes 1080–1440 paired to closest valid FiO2 within ±60 minutes; equal-distance ties prefer preceding, then earlier;
- exposure: minute 1440 inclusive to 2160 exclusive, 12 clock-hour bins;
- outcome origin: minute 2160;
- follow-up: \`(2160,12240]\`.

P0-positive requires a prespecified pre-1440 diagnosis/admission term (ARDS/acute respiratory distress, pneumonia, aspiration, pulmonary oedema, or acute respiratory failure) and anchor P/F <=300. The comparator has no acute term regardless of P/F. Term/PF>300 remains discordant validation. This is not Berlin ARDS.

If measurement gates pass, the outcome remains post-hour-36 hospital death through 12240 using \`hospitaldischargestatus='Expired'\` and \`hospitaldischargeoffset\`, with live discharge competing. No outcome field is opened during measurement fitting or model selection.

Normalize FiO2 0.21–1.00 directly and 21–100 by division by 100. Respiratory charting is primary; lab FiO2 and \`respiratoryCare.setapneafio2\` are validation channels. Within source/hour use the median event-time value. A prior respiratory setting supports a bin for at most 120 minutes; future values are never carried backward. Same-hour oxygenation never selects an hour.

## Smallest defensible measurement-first experiment

### A. Exact census and logical answer

Rebuild the parent's exact flow and reconcile every deviation from 1,869. For every stay/hour emit source, value, event time, entry/revision time, prior-setting age, support class, and \`I_it\`. Observed-source intervals include calibrated source error; unsupported hours retain logical \`[0,0.60]\`. Report burden widths by stratum, hour, hospital, source, support, and documentation quartile. The logical result is retained even if every later model fails.

This alone answers whether the burden is identified without assumptions. It does not identify missing settings.

### B0. Transparent persistence/conformal baseline

Create pseudo-missing episodes only from hours with a directly observed respiratory-chart target. Mask contiguous blocks of 1–4 hours, sampling gap length, hour, support, stratum, source availability, and hospital-coverage quartile to match the truly missing episodes. Remove the target and all measurements entered after the masked decision time.

Predict each masked setting by the last respiratory setting, with anchor FiO2 fallback. Do not carry it as truth. Estimate split-conformal intervals separately by gap bin (121–180, 181–360, >360 minutes/no prior), support class, P0 stratum, and source signature, with partial pooling only where a cell has <100 targets. Use five outer folds grouped by \`hospitalid\`; \`uniquepid\` never crosses folds. Within each training fold, reserve hospitals for conformal calibration. Evaluate target coverage, absolute error, interval width, bias, and coverage differences. Apply intervals—not point imputations—to truly missing hours and propagate them to \`B_i\`.

B0 is selected as primary because it was at least as accurate as the bounded learned surrogate and makes the identifying assumption visible: settings during unobserved gaps resemble settings at observed re-entry conditional on the frozen strata. It cannot recover undocumented within-gap changes.

### M1. Structured state/observation alternative on identical targets

Fit an outcome-blind hierarchical hidden-state or constrained recurrent model on the identical stays, masks, variables, folds, and targets. Inputs available strictly before the masked hour are prior FiO2 and gap; prior PaO2/SaO2/SpO2, pH/PaCO2; PEEP/pressure/tidal volume/rate; support/airway; haemodynamics/vasopressors; anchor P/F; hour; P0; source and entry-delay history; and hospital hierarchy. Separate heads predict latent FiO2 transition and whether/when each source is charted. Source emissions are calibrated against respiratory/lab/care pairs. The target is held-hospital masked FiO2 and observation timing, never mortality.

M1 can reveal setting transitions linked to physiologic/support change and whether sparse charting is informative—information B0 loses. It is admissible only with interval coverage at least as good as B0 in every primary stratum and gap bin, held-hospital observation calibration, no subgroup coverage deficit >0.05, and at least 0.02 narrower median per-hour interval or materially better calibration at equal width. Predictive gain alone is not biological evidence. The bounded gradient-boosting surrogate was inferior, so M1 is deferred unless B0 passes overall but fails specifically in changing-support gaps.

Estimated future compute: exact extraction and masks 1–2 hours on 8–16 CPUs/64–128 GiB; B0 plus 1,000 hospital bootstraps 1–2 hours on 16 CPUs; M1 1–3 hours/fold on one allocated A100-80GB plus 8 CPUs/64 GiB or a slower CPU state-space implementation. The completed DuckDB diagnostics took 15 and 25 seconds in ordinary CPU shell; these are measured, while future estimates are not.

### C. Outcome-blind pulmonary-phenotype review

Exposure calibration cannot validate P0. If B0/M1 burden gates pass, perform the parent's review before mortality: Phase I 160 balanced packets permits one P0-to-P1 revision; Phase II is a new probability sample of 600 (300 P1-positive/300 negative), stratified by P/F, support, documentation quartile, and source signature, at most one packet per \`uniquepid\`. Packets contain only pre-1440 relative-time structured evidence and hide hospital, P0, post-hour-24 data, exposure summary, and outcome.

Two ICU clinicians independently review 30%, all disagreements/indeterminate/source-poor sentinels; a third arbitrates. Classify available-record-supported acute parenchymal injury, support, hydrostatic-only compatibility, timing, and adequacy. This assesses consistency with available eICU evidence, not true ARDS.

## Frozen gates and falsification

Continue to mortality only if all pass:

1. B0 or admissible M1 achieves >=0.90 nominal interval coverage with simultaneous lower 95% bound >=0.88 overall and >=0.85 in each primary stratum, gap bin, support class, and documentation quartile; absolute coverage differences <=0.05.
2. After observed-source error is added, validation-bounded \`B_i\` width has median <=0.10 and 75th percentile <=0.15 in each stratum. Logical widths remain reported. No source family or five hospitals provide >50% of weighted exposure information.
3. Cross-source median absolute FiO2 difference <=0.05, 90th percentile <=0.10, and disagreement-probability difference by stratum/documentation quartile <=0.10, with >=150 pairs per stratum from >=20 hospitals.
4. Phase-II weighted P1 PPV and sensitivity each >=0.80 with simultaneous lower bounds >=0.70; indeterminate <=15% overall and <=25% by documentation quartile; PPV/sensitivity differences across documentation/support groups <=0.15.
5. The missingness model reproduces gap length, re-entry timing, source mix, and support transitions in held hospitals. Intervals calibrated on isolated masked hours must also cover contiguous 2–4-hour blocks. Entry-clock, event-clock, source-removal, dense-site downsampling, and leave-five-hospitals-out results must not breach gates.
6. Only after 1–5 may the unchanged parent mortality stage test event count, exposure overlap/ESS, error-only interaction <1.5 points, and the 3-point interaction.

Falsification:

- If B0 fails especially during support changes and M1 succeeds with calibrated observation transitions, revise the measurement model but do not change the clinical estimand.
- If both fail in the same gap/support cells, abandon the oxygen-toxicity branch in eICU; do not exclude those stays or call them room air.
- If exposure passes but clinician review fails, report a phenotype-measurement result and abandon enrichment by this proxy.
- If apparent precision arises only because intervals lie below the 0.40 threshold, additionally report FiO2-scale coverage and error; narrow excess intervals cannot hide poor FiO2 calibration.
- Source-pair agreement does not validate delivered airway FiO2; disagreement is adverse, agreement only supports consistency between recorded channels.

**Supportive measurement result:** all exposure and phenotype gates pass. This supports proceeding to the unchanged outcome experiment, not oxygen toxicity or trial enrichment.

**Adverse measurement result:** calibrated logical/validation widths remain above thresholds, differential coverage/source error persists, or phenotype performance precisely fails. This supports abandoning the eICU pulmonary-interaction branch and prioritizing device/chart or phenotype validation. It does not show oxygen is harmless.

**Inconclusive measurement result:** too few masked blocks/pairs/reviewed cases, wide calibration bounds, clinician review unavailable, or B0/M1 disagreement. Defer mortality and obtain missing evidence.

## Claims, verification, clinical limits, and alternatives

Computationally checkable: cohort/clocks/joins, masks, source pairs, logical widths, held-hospital prediction and interval coverage, burden-width gates, review weights/confusion tables, and gate-governed conclusions. A verifier must accept an adverse or inconclusive measurement-only conclusion and reject mortality/toxicity claims after any failed prerequisite.

Clinician review is essential for the pulmonary proxy. Delivered airway FiO2, imaging, hydrostatic contribution, clinician intent, undocumented changes, and oxidative injury remain unavailable. These require device/full-chart validation, expert review, or another study. Even a later positive interaction cannot establish causality or a treatment recommendation.

Alternatives not chosen: complete-case restriction changes the population; missing-as-room-air is unsupported; extending carry beyond 120 minutes as truth hides uncertainty; same-hour oxygenation selects on response; generic mortality prediction cannot resolve measurement; source agreement alone cannot validate delivery. The learned alternative is retained only for observation/transition structure; its bounded surrogate lost to persistence, so complexity earns no presumption. Revisit an anchored or observed-hours estimand only as an explicit future child because that would change the estimand, not repair this one.
