# Episode 95 successor: is a label-free source queue clinically informative at a fixed 10% review capacity?

## Substantive repair and deployment question

The assigned Episode 94 parent was valid and already preserved the audited cohort, exact five-table bindings, source-only fitting, copied-hospital uncertainty, deletion checks, and clinical evidence limits. Its remaining weakness was decision semantics: it described an ordered pair of primary contrasts, treated a target-label model as part of the apparent deployment estimand, and allowed the Dinf guard to blur the chosen D7 deployment horizon.

This successor asks one executable, rank-only deployment question:

> In each cross-practice direction, does a score trained only on the source practice produce a fixed-capacity queue whose D7 death yield exceeds an exactly defined random-review queue by at least 0.03 deaths per slot at c=0.10, while remaining no worse than 0.02 deaths per slot below a same-score model trained with labels from other hospitals in the target practice?

The primary clinical object is the source-only queue's D7 yield over random review. The local-label model is a historical transport benchmark: it estimates how much D7 queue yield is available to a model that has target-practice labels, but it is not a label-free deployment policy and its result cannot establish benefit. The R_process comparison tests the incremental contribution of the optional repeat lactate; it is secondary. Absolute-risk calibration is a separate, non-rescuing probability-use diagnostic. Dinf is a secondary horizon audit, not a second deployment outcome.

The strongest evidence available to this design is narrower than the deployment question. The inspected full text of Baysan et al. (2022, DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750, PMCID PMC9444407) reports modest incremental discrimination for 24-hour lactate in a selected sepsis setting; the inspected Sisk et al. (2020, DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242, PMCID PMC7810439) supports concern that whether and when measurements occur can carry prognostic information. Neither establishes cross-practice ranking, fixed-capacity value, EICU performance, or patient benefit. The available research-ambition files support longitudinal modeling, calibration and external transport as scientific standards, not this topic or a clinical conclusion. The Natural History and Bayesian article texts and supplements were inspected. The cancer main article and full STAR Methods were unavailable; only its metadata and supplement were inspected, and no claim about the unavailable main text is made.

## Hypothesis, estimand and deterministic states

Construct two frozen directions: Low-to-High and High-to-Low, where Low and High are defined below. Let h range over target-stratum hospitals, n_h be the eligible target cohort size, and k_h(c)=ceil(c n_h). At c=0.10, K_d(c)=sum_h k_h(c). Queue within each hospital, descending by the raw score and then ascending by patientunitstayid.

For direction d, define:

- Y^tr_d(c) = [sum_h sum_{i in Q^tr_full(h,c)} D7_i] / K_d(c), the source-only full-score queue yield.
- Y^rnd_d(c) = [sum_h sum_{i in Q^rnd(h,c)} D7_i] / K_d(c), where Q^rnd is the deterministic random-capacity reference specified below.
- Y^loc_d(c) = [sum_h sum_{i in Q^loc_full(h,c)} D7_i] / K_d(c), where Q^loc_full is scored by a model fit using target-practice labels but excluding each scored target hospital.

The single primary estimand is the worst-direction source-versus-random D7 advantage:
  
  theta_primary = min_{d in {Low-to-High, High-to-Low}} [Y^tr_d(0.10) - Y^rnd_d(0.10)].

The primary hypothesis is theta_primary >= 0.03. The 0.03 is a prespecified operational margin for this retrospective experiment, not a validated utility threshold or an estimate of preventable deaths.

The local-label comparison is a prespecified non-inferiority guard, not a second primary estimand:
  
  theta_local = min_d [Y^tr_d(0.10) - Y^loc_d(0.10)].

A portable rank must satisfy theta_local >= -0.02. This comparison is valid only as a conditional transport benchmark: it asks how close a label-free source rank comes to a target-practice, held-out-hospital historical rank under the same features, outcome, capacity, and tie rules. It does not compare two policies in a randomized deployment, does not measure model adaptation cost, and does not show that local labels would be available prospectively.

The source-vs-random queue is a necessary absolute informativeness check. The source-vs-local guard is a relative transport check. Neither is causal. The R_process increment and eventual-horizon audit cannot rescue a failed primary.

Use two-sided 95% copied-hospital percentile intervals. If all computational gates pass, classify the deployment result deterministically:

- **Supportive**: the lower endpoint of theta_primary is at least +0.03 and the lower endpoint of theta_local is at least -0.02, in both directions by construction of the minima; the source queue has finite scores and exact cardinality; deletion stability passes; and no opposite-direction contradiction exists. This supports only a retrospective, rank-only statement that the source score cleared the prespecified D7 queue margins in these EICU strata.
- **Adverse**: the upper endpoint of theta_primary is below +0.03, or the upper endpoint of theta_local is below -0.02. The first is failure of absolute D7 queue informativeness; the second is failure of the cross-practice non-inferiority guard. This does not show that a different score, capacity, or workflow would fail.
- **Inconclusive**: all other computable patterns, including intervals crossing either margin, deletion instability, sparse/support failure, only one direction meeting a margin, or a finite-replicate failure that does not meet the explicit not-computable rule below.

The state is **not-computable** before interpretation for provenance/schema/cohort leakage, nonfinite fitting or scoring, incorrect queue arithmetic, altered frozen definitions, wrong local exclusion, missing required artifacts, or fewer than 1,900 finite joint copied-hospital replicates. Not-computable is distinct from adverse evidence.

### Secondary outputs and horizon discipline

At c=0.10 report, but do not promote to the primary state:

1. theta_process = min_d {Y^tr_full_d - Y^tr_process_d}, testing whether numeric optional repeat lactate adds queue yield after process/availability features.
2. All prespecified stress capacities C={0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20}, with the exact same queue and uncertainty procedure. Stress capacities are sensitivity results; a favorable stress capacity cannot rescue a failed c=0.10 result and an unfavorable stress capacity cannot overturn a supportive primary state.
3. Dinf yields and source-versus-local Dinf differences at c=0.10. D7 is the sole deployment horizon because it is the prespecified near-term review outcome and is available under the L24 eligibility rule. Dinf is an eventual in-hospital outcome whose horizon varies with discharge and can conflate near-term and later deaths. If the upper endpoint of min_d[Y^tr_inf-Y^loc_inf] is below -0.02, report the deterministic tag **eventual-horizon discordant**; this tag neither changes a D7 supportive/adverse/inconclusive state nor permits an eventual safety or utility claim. If Dinf agrees, report **no detected eventual-horizon discordance**, not eventual benefit.
4. Random queues, raw all-patient rates, queue captures, false positives, substitutions, score overlap, and hospital-specific yields.

### Separate calibration diagnostic

Calibration is not required to accept a rank-only queue and cannot rescue it. For each direction fit R_full only in the source stratum, obtain the source-hospital-held-out offset delta_A by the deterministic root
sum_i[D7_i-expit(eta_i+delta_A)]-delta_A=0
using bisection on [-20,20], tolerance 1e-12, and apply p_tr=expit(eta_tr+delta_A). Fit the identical local reference with each scored target hospital excluded. In each direction and F_available cell, and at threshold z(p)=1[p>=0.10] including equality, report n, events, observed event rate, mean predicted risk, and E=mean(D7-p). Let q_tr and q_loc be the maximum absolute E over the four cells and G=q_tr-q_loc. A calibration diagnostic is supportive only if the upper 95% endpoint of q_tr is <=0.05, the upper endpoint of G is <=0.02, and every portable/local cell has at least 30 original patients; adverse if an upper endpoint is above its margin; otherwise inconclusive. It is reported as rank-only/calibration-only/dual-use diagnostic context and never changes the primary queue state. A calibration-supportive but queue-adverse result does not justify allocating review slots; a queue-supportive but calibration-adverse result forbids displaying p_tr as a validated probability.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

This child changes the scientific decision boundary, not the code wording: it defines one scalar primary D7 deployment estimand, makes random review the absolute benchmark, reclassifies the local-label model as a non-inferiority transport benchmark, freezes 10% as the only primary capacity, and makes Dinf/calibration explicitly secondary and non-rescuing. It preserves the parent's audited all-baseline/L24 optional-repeat cohort, exact paths, schema hashes, columns, joins, clocks, features, source-only fitting, copied-hospital uncertainty, deletion stability and evidence limits.

Even a supportive state would mean only that this retrospective source rank cleared the stated EICU D7 queue margins. It would not show that reviews were performed, that deaths were preventable, that a capacity existed, that a local model could be trained, that patients benefited, or that the score transports to another time, hospital or workflow. Adverse results would delimit this score and design, not prove no clinical queue can work. Inconclusive results require more data or a better powered/less heterogeneous study. Clinical adjudication, workflow and capacity data, prospective temporal/external validation, and an impact or randomized evaluation remain necessary for any clinical deployment or benefit claim.
