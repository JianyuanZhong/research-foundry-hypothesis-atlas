# v2 (repair): Observation-process vs physiologic dose for ICU MAP<65 → AKI

Child of `[prior hypothesis]` (episode 40). Repairs the two objections the blinded
critique raised against v1: (a) conditioning on post-exposure creatinine draws is **overadjustment**
(they lie on the detection path → attenuation even if H-dose holds); (b) a creatinine-draw count is
NOT a clean negative control because it rises with clinical concern under either hypothesis.
Everything else (parent, population, landmark, windows, outcome definition, bindings) is unchanged.

## Competing explanations (unchanged)
- **H-dose:** cumulative (0,24]h MAP<65 burden independently raises (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI risk.
- **H-observation:** the association is partly produced because hypotension triggers more monitoring
  and lab testing, so AKI is detected more often.

## Repaired discriminating test
1. **Primary estimand stays total-effect.** The main model does **not** adjust for post-exposure
   creatinine draws. Detection is handled by decomposition and negative controls, not by adjusting
   away the effect (overadjustment guard).
2. **Genuinely discriminating negative controls (observation channel must be broad).**
   Under H-observation, hypotension should raise monitoring of *all* kinds (a **non-renal** lab
   count, e.g. CBC/glucose draws in (24,72]h) about as much as the renal (creatinine) draw count;
   under H-dose, hypotension should raise creatinine/renal ascertainment (which captures true AKI
   incidence) **more** than non-renal monitoring. Pre-specified contrast: effect on creatinine draws
   minus effect on non-renal draws. Supportive of H-observation = near-symmetric increase;
   adverse (H-dose) = creatinine-draw association exceeds non-renal.
3. **Structural (non-mediator) adjustment only.** M1 adjusts for **hospital-level** mean charting
   intensity (`vitalPeriodic` rows per stay, computed as a hospital attribute, not the patient's
   own post-exposure draws) + static severity (`apachescore`, `predictedicumortality`) + hospital
   random effect. This removes practice-level observation confounding without touching the
   individual causal path.
4. **Detection-robust outcome sensitivity.** Re-estimate the burden→AKI effect on the subset with
   ≥2 creatinine measurements in (24,72]h (equal-ascertainment restriction), where AKI detection is
   not length-biased. Prediction: H-dose persists; H-observation collapses.
5. **LOHO transport** of the burden coefficient vs hospital-level charting intensity (unchanged).

## Falsification / interpretation (repaired)
- **Supportive of H-observation:** creatinine-draw and non-renal-draw associations ≈ equal
  (contrast CI includes 0) AND the burden→AKI effect is near-null in the ≥2-draw restriction.
- **Adverse (H-dose supported):** creatinine-draw association significantly exceeds non-renal AND
  burden→AKI persists in the ≥2-draw restriction.
- **Inconclusive:** insufficient non-renal-lab or monitoring-density variation, or CIs crossing the
  pre-specified equivalence margins (pre-declared useful-negative branch).
