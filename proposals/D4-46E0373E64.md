## Unresolved question and clinical advance

Among people returning for a repeat assessment who have preserved creatinine-based kidney function, does adding cystatin-C kidney estimation and low grip strength identify people who should receive limited additional kidney-risk review, because they have higher subsequent risk of a recorded inpatient acute kidney injury (AKI) diagnosis despite appearing low risk by creatinine alone?

The prespecified hypothesis is:

> In the repeat-assessment cohort with T1 eGFRcr >=90 and no prior recorded N17 or N18 diagnosis, a prespecified low-eGFRcys/low-grip discordance phenotype has higher five-year risk of a first recorded inpatient N17 diagnosis than people with concordant preserved eGFR, and a model adding eGFRcys and grip to the creatinine-only baseline captures at least 5 percentage points more AKI events in exactly the same K-person surveillance list, without material calibration harm and without reversal when the early-era fit is applied to the 2013 cohort.

This is a distinct organ-specific successor to the incumbent's broad C/R/M endpoint. The incumbent asks whether a prior general panel improves one shared list for any cardiovascular, renal, or metabolic event. This child asks whether a clinically recognizable renal measurement discordance is informative specifically in people whom routine creatinine screening would classify as preserved. It can inform selection for confirmatory cystatin-C measurement or renal-risk review; it does not test whether ordering the test or reviewing a person changes treatment or outcomes.

The expert UKB-03 seed is retained but repaired: the local snapshot supports creatinine, cystatin C, grip, and inpatient diagnosis code/date fields, but not KDIGO stage, measured GFR, reliable specimen dates, or a validated “severe AKI” label. The primary endpoint is therefore explicitly recorded inpatient N17, not severe AKI. CKD is a secondary endpoint/sequence only if support and temporal ascertainment pass.

## What is known versus what is tested

The strongest available evidence is structural and methodological. The catalog contains one-row-per-eid UKB tables with repeated field-instance columns. Literal headers contain fields 30700 (creatinine), 30720 (cystatin C), 46/47 (left/right grip), assessment date 53, and 259 position-matched diagnosis code/date pairs 41270/41280, plus death date 40000. Public UKB field pages retrieved during discovery describe 30700 as creatinine in micromol/L, 30720 as cystatin C in mg/L, 46/47 as hand grip strength, 41270/41280 as inpatient ICD-10 code/date pairs, and 40000 as registry death date. These public results are untrusted web evidence and the solver must recheck the cited field metadata before fitting.

The local evidence does not establish that discordance predicts AKI, that the fields were collected at exactly the same time, that N17 codes measure biochemical AKI severity, that a person without an N17 code is AKI-free, or that a fixed review list is clinically beneficial. The experiment tests conditional prognostic information and a fixed-capacity ranking, not causality, screening effectiveness, treatment benefit, biological reserve, or clinical utility.

## Population, landmark, exposure, and endpoint

Define T0=53-0.0 and T1=53-1.0. Require:

1. dictionary-certified interpretation of field 53 as comparable assessment-centre dates at both instances;
2. valid sex 31-0.0 and age at recruitment 21022-0.0;
3. T0<T1, 0.25<L=(T1-T0)/365.2425<=5 years, and T1 between 2010-01-01 and 2013-12-31;
4. nonmissing numeric T1 values for 30700-1.0, 30720-1.0, 46-1.0 and 47-1.0, and nonmissing T0 values for 30700-0.0 and 30720-0.0 only for a repeat-measurement sensitivity analysis;
5. a certified field interpretation and unit conversion: creatinine 30700 in micromol/L converted to mg/dL by division by 88.4 and cystatin C 30720 in mg/L; if this cannot be certified, stop before eGFR computation and report a structural certificate only;
6. no death date on or before T1 and no valid N17 or N18 code/date pair on or before T1. This excludes known recorded AKI/CKD history, not undiagnosed disease.

Compute age from 21022-0.0 as age at recruitment; do not treat field-instance numbers as dates. After the unit gate, let Scr be 30700-1.0/88.4 in mg/dL and Scys be 30720-1.0 in mg/L. Use the race-free 2021 CKD-EPI equations exactly as follows, with female=1 and male=0: eGFRcr = 142 x min(Scr/k,1)^a x max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) x 0.9938^Age x (1.012 if female), where k=0.7 and a=-0.241 for female and k=0.9 and a=-0.302 for male; eGFRcys = 133 x min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) x max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) x 0.9961^Age x (0.932 if female). The solver must print formula, units, sex coding and boundary tests in its manifest; no race coefficient is used. The primary eligible group is eGFRcr>=90 mL/min/1.73m2. Define discordance before seeing outcomes as eGFRcr>=90 and eGFRcys<60. Define low grip as the sex-specific bottom quintile of the mean of available left/right T1 grip; require both hands for the primary analysis and use a prespecified one-hand sensitivity. The primary phenotype is discordance plus low grip. The continuous exposure sensitivity uses eGFRcys-eGFRcr and grip without changing the primary cutoff.

The field metadata and timing gate must certify that the T1 blood measures and T1 grip are appropriate for a T1-landmark analysis. If specimen/assessment timing cannot be aligned, do not reinterpret field instance 1 as a dated T1 measurement; the primary study is fail-closed. CRP 30710-1.0 is used only in a prespecified sensitivity model to determine whether inflammatory burden plausibly explains the signal. No result may be called “low muscle mass” or “pre-illness reserve” from these variables alone.

For each j=0,...,258, pair 41270-0.j only with 41280-0.j. Validate that each nonempty code has a valid paired date and that dates are plausible. Normalize only after code-system certification: trim, uppercase, remove a decimal for matching, and recognize N17.* as AKI and N18.* as CKD. N17.0-N17.9 are recorded inpatient acute kidney failure diagnoses; no severity grade is inferred.

Let H=T1 plus five calendar years. The primary outcome is the earliest valid N17.* date in (T1,H]. Death before or on an N17 date is a competing event; death after N17 does not erase the AKI event. A participant with neither observed endpoint is “no qualifying record observed,” never disease-free. The primary estimand is five-year cumulative incidence of recorded inpatient N17 with death as a competing risk, estimated through cause-specific or Fine-Gray risk predictions only if the outcome ascertainment gate certifies observation through H for every included person. The default ranking model uses a multinomial outcome AKI-first, death-first, and no-event-observed, with cumulative-incidence validation.

Secondary endpoints are (a) first N18.* after T1, with N17 and death as competing events, and (b) among those with a valid post-T1 N17 before death, first N18.* within two years after that N17. The latter is descriptive/sequence evidence and is not interpreted causally because conditioning on AKI is post-exposure. If N18 support is sparse, it is reported inconclusive and not pooled with AKI. No outpatient CKD, KDIGO stage, laboratory AKI, dialysis, measured GFR, or “severe AKI” endpoint is claimed.

## Fixed-capacity decision estimand

Retain the incumbent's fixed-capacity logic because it serves the new decision claim: a service cannot perform confirmatory cystatin-C review for everyone. It is no longer a broad C/R/M shared list. Among the fixed eligible eGFRcr-preserved population, set K=floor(0.10*n_early) before inspecting outcomes and require K>=40 and n_late>=K. Use the same absolute K in early and late strata. Select exactly K unique people by held-out five-year AKI risk, with deterministic eid-hash tie breaking.

Early landmark is T1<=2012-12-31; late landmark is T1 in calendar 2013. Use a single participant-level split, integer(SHA256(UTF-8(decimal(eid)))) mod 5, frozen before fitting. All preprocessing, low-grip quintiles, eGFR formula validation, calibration, tuning, and K definition are training-fold/early-only as appropriate. The primary temporal test applies the early fitted model and fixed cutpoints without refitting to late participants; a late cross-fitted estimate is secondary.

M0 is the transparent routine-creatinine baseline: age, sex, T1 calendar year, L, eGFRcr, and T1 grip. It represents current routine information and defines eligibility, but does not use cystatin C. M1 is the prespecified mechanistic discordance model: M0 inputs plus eGFRcys, eGFRcys-eGFRcr, the binary discordance indicator, low-grip indicator, their interaction, and CRP only in a prespecified sensitivity version. Fit regularized logistic/multinomial risk models with the same folds and outcome categories. The primary M0-versus-M1 comparison tests information from the organ-specific discordance phenotype, not a tiny score improvement.

A matched learned alternative M2 uses exactly M1's primary inputs, participants, split, outcomes, and K policy in a shallow gradient-boosted model with a small training-fold-only grid and calibration. It may reveal nonlinear thresholds and eGFRcys-by-grip interactions that the prespecified mechanistic model loses. M2 is secondary diagnostic evidence and cannot rescue a failed or adverse M1. A dated sequence transformer is deferred because this export contains sparse field-position arrays, not a certified dated disease sequence; a latent GP trajectory model is deferred because the question is an interpretable cross-marker discordance and no verified genetics/assay-timing dependency is needed; a full multimodal model is deferred because raw imaging and the main demonstration's full methods are unavailable. A body-composition mechanistic model is deferred because the locally intended fields and units are not needed to answer the narrower discordance question and grip cannot substitute for measured muscle mass. Revisit these branches only if the named dependency becomes available.

## Uncertainty, falsification, and decision rules

Report held-out cumulative incidence, Brier score, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, time-dependent discrimination as descriptive secondary output, list yield and capture, list overlap/additions, and net reclassification of the K list. The primary contrast is Delta_capture = AKI capture(M1,K)-AKI capture(M0,K), with a two-sided 95% participant bootstrap stratified by early/late and fixed eligibility cells, preserving held-out predictions and K. Use 2,000 bootstrap replicates and Holm correction for the prespecified capture, yield, and temporal-transport family. Record failed-support replicates and omit no uncertainty silently.

Supportive requires all semantic, timing, code/date, ascertainment, integrity, and support gates; >=100 primary N17 events overall, >=40 in early and >=40 in late eligible strata where the estimate is reported, K>=40; late Delta_capture and its two-sided interval wholly above the 0.05 research margin; no early/late sign reversal; and calibration degradation relative to M0 no worse than 0.05 intercept or 0.10 slope. The 0.05 is a research decision criterion, not a validated clinical-benefit threshold. A clinically meaningful result must also show that the excess capture is concentrated in the prespecified discordance/low-grip phenotype, not merely in calendar year, spacing, or missingness.

Adverse means M1 has no gain, a negative/reversed gain, calibration harm, an effect explained by CRP/selection/spacing, no extra N17 events in the fixed list, or failure of the discordance-by-grip interaction in a prespecified effect-heterogeneity analysis. Inconclusive means failed field semantics or timing, inadequate follow-up ascertainment, malformed code/date pairing, sparse N17/N18 support, poor positivity/ESS in selection sensitivity, wide intervals, or contradictory recorded versus certified prospective modes. Null findings are scientifically useful: they would argue that cystatin C/grip discordance does not justify this fixed-capacity re-ranking under the specified data conditions.

Falsification controls are: permute outcome labels within era/fold; permute cystatin-C/grip blocks within current-risk, sex, interval, and selection strata; independently permute the discordance block while preserving M0 risk and missingness; use a pre-T1 negative-control endpoint; run complete-case and fold-local missingness versions; random-K selection; early/late label permutation; and a deliberately shifted code/date pairing that must fail integrity checks. Any persistent M1 increment after preserving M0 risk, eligibility, missingness, spacing, and workload while breaking the organ-specific linkage falsifies attribution to discordance. Report whether the N17 signal disappears after CRP adjustment, but do not interpret attenuation as causal mediation.

The solver must newly produce: source/header/key certificates; a field-semantics and unit certificate; cohort flow; exact formulas and feature ledger; code/date integrity report; exclusion and competing-risk ledger; fixed split and early/late manifest; fitted M0/M1/M2 models; held-out individual predictions; exact K-list ledgers; N17/N18/death event tables; bootstrap intervals; calibration and sensitivity outputs; all null controls; and an interpretation file linking every conclusion to computed output paths. Completion is either this package with uncertainty-linked supportive/adverse/inconclusive interpretation, or a complete fail-closed certificate naming the failed gate. Readiness or discovery counts are not a solved scientific result.

## Evidence limits and demonstration dispositions

The kidney-function paper read locally (Haines et al., CJASN 2023) studied 38 mechanically ventilated ICU patients with serial creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance, and ultrasound muscle measurements. It supports the physiological plausibility that creatinine-based estimation can be confounded by muscle loss in critical illness; it does not establish this UKB prognostic hypothesis, a population risk association, or an AKI endpoint. That paper's ICU design and measured-GFR resources are unavailable here.

Delphi is used only as methodological context: its verified learned structured disease-sequence work motivates learning, but UKB lacks a certified dated sequence, so M2 remains tabular. ALADYNOULLI demonstrates longitudinal latent modeling, but no new latent model is needed to test this organ-specific question and the demonstration's genetic analysis is not reproduced. Oncoformer demonstrates multimodal learning, but the UKB snapshot has no raw chest X-rays and the main article/complete STAR Methods remain unavailable; only the matched tabular alternative is retained. No unavailable paper text or supplementary method is claimed as inspected.

UKB-03 is preserved as the originating untested seed. UKB-09 is the adjacent repeated-measurement context, not evidence for the kidney claim. The parent incumbent's one-person-one-slot fixed-capacity framework is retained because it maps to confirmatory cystatin-C/renal review capacity; its broad C/R/M outcome, prior-panel hypothesis, and domain lists are not copied. All four configured dataset islands remain accessible, but this study uses only the four exact UKB inputs above. No source rows or clinical notes are sent to public search.
