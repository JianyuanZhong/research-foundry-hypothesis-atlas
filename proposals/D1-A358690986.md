# HCC M2 triage with an honest report-confirmed abstention policy

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and substantive repair

The historical decision remains narrow: 24 hours before a first eligible liver resection, can a frozen laboratory-independent strategy identify adults in this institution's retrospectively pathology-confirmed HCC resection frame whose resection specimen has pathology-defined high-grade microvascular invasion (M2), for multidisciplinary review? The output is a historical classification flag. It is not a recommendation for resection, neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy, surveillance, or any other treatment.

The strongest supported data claim is limited. The HCC snapshot has operation start times, examination acquisition times and eventual radiology text, assay-specific laboratory values with one field named `test time`, and untimed pathology text. Direct headers and the catalog show that `examinations` has no report-authored, finalized, released, or clinician-viewed time; `pathology` has no time or specimen identifier; and `labs` has no collection, entry, verification, release, view, unit, panel, or accession field. Thus an examination acquired before `t_dec` is acquisition-eligible only. Stored report text is not evidence that it was available at `t_dec`; `test time<t_dec` is not evidence of laboratory result visibility. Pathology text can support a pathology-report-observable endpoint after position-aware parsing and blinded clinical review, but cannot establish unsampled biological M2 without slides, blocks, specimen identifiers, and sampling protocol.

The inherited unresolved primary hypothesis is retained exactly as the S-versus-B clinical-value estimand:

**C.** In the whole 2020–2021 target frame, does the laboratory-independent semantic strategy `S` add at least 0.01 net-benefit units over the laboratory-independent structured/basic-report baseline `B` at threshold `p=0.30), under the exact joint uncertainty over radiology report availability and pathology grade? The same contrast must be positive across `p=0.25,ldots,0.35), and S must be compared with flag-all and flag-none. This is one net true-positive equivalent per 100 target-frame patients at the threshold's false-positive weight; it is not patient benefit.

The nested attribution hypothesis is also retained:

**A.** Does the identical radiology semantic block `C_R` add positive net benefit in `A=W+C_R` over `W`, under the same report and pathology uncertainty? This is incremental modeled information conditional on measured workflow/provenance, not semantic causality or proof of absent process confounding.

The single residual validity issue is the word “deployable” in the face of unobserved historical report access. The primary S–B bound remains a valid, exact information-set sensitivity contrast, but its state-specific action cannot be known for the historical patients because the snapshot does not contain a report-release/view indicator. Calling a favorable S–B bound a presently deployable policy would overstate transportability. The repair is therefore an explicit, conservative transport/abstention estimand, not a change to C, A, the population, clocks, endpoint, or primary model families.

Define an operational policy `Q` for a future workflow that exposes a per-selected-report access indicator at `t_dec`. Q acts only when all selected nonempty reports are verified accessible and the threshold action is invariant to every feasible report-availability pattern. Otherwise it abstains from the semantic flag. Abstention means no M2 flag, not treatment. Q is computable retrospectively as a policy family over the latent states and is the only policy for which a transport claim may be considered. Its historical coverage and net-benefit bounds make the report-state uncertainty decision-relevant rather than silently assuming either all or no reports were visible.

The transport hypothesis is secondary and cannot rescue C or A:

**T.** At `p=0.30`, and consistently on the prespecified `0.25–0.35` plateau, does Q improve net benefit over an acquisition-only baseline `B0` by at least 0.01 while retaining nonzero worst-case coverage? `B0` is a separately fitted, always-available fallback using only pre-report acquisition facts and demographics. T asks whether a conservative policy that abstains under unverified/unavailable reports has any decision-relevant historical operating envelope. It does not establish current clinical deployment, clinician response, or patient benefit.

## Population, clocks, and temporal split

The unit is one patient (`Patient Master Index`) and one first eligible index resection.

1. From `procedures`, select the earliest valid `Start time` whose normalized exact `Surgery` is in a frozen clinically reviewed dictionary of partial, segmental, hemihepatic, or liver-tumor resections. Exclude transplant, biopsy, puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and explicit repeat/recurrence operations. Break exact-time ties by `Encounter number`, then normalized `Surgery`. Emit every raw operation value and frequency, invalid time, tie, inclusion/exclusion, and assert one index per patient before outcome access.
2. Require age at least 18 and same-encounter pathology text explicitly identifying HCC in the index surgical encounter. This is a retrospectively resected, pathology-confirmed institutional frame; it does not prove preoperative HCC certainty, resectability, curative intent, or treatment naivety. Do not require an MVI grade or secure specimen linkage for inclusion. Missing grade, positive-ungraded MVI, conflict, or uncertain specimen linkage is `V=0), never an exclusion. Patients without same-encounter HCC-identifying pathology are outside the observable frame.
3. Set `t_dec = procedures.start time - 24 hours`. No record at or after `t_dec` may predict. Report 12-, 48-, and 72-hour-buffer sensitivities; none replaces the 24-hour primary.
4. A liver CT/MRI row in `examinations` is eligible only with valid `start time` in `[t_dec-90 days,t_dec)`. This is acquisition time only. Deduplicate exact rows and bundle by `(patient master index, encounter number, examination number, start time)); missing `examination number` creates a row-level bundle. Freeze CT/MRI dictionaries from exact `examination`. Select at most the latest CT and latest MRI bundle by `start time`, then `examination number`, then normalized `examination` for ties. Emit multiplicities and discarded bundles.
5. For diagnostic-only audit, a `labs` row is timestamp-eligible only with valid `test time` in `[t_dec-30 days,t_dec)`. Preserve last-row/source-order ties by exact `(test, specimen type)`. This window never gates or predicts in any primary or transport matrix. Do not infer units, combine assays by apparent meaning, or infer visibility.
6. The primary no-recorded-prior-treatment cohort excludes recorded HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `procedures`, `medications`, or non-drug `orders` during `[t_dec-365 days,t_dec)`, using frozen reviewed dictionaries and valid source times. Report source-specific exclusions and invalid times. This is not biological treatment-naivety. Previously treated patients are a labeled sensitivity cohort.
7. Fit on index date: development 2015–2018; select penalty and perform logistic recalibration in 2019; leave 2020–2021 untouched for test. Keep all records from each patient in one period and exclude 2013–2014. Frozen pipelines may score 2022 through snapshot end 2025-11-11 only for predictor/template drift and endpoint identifiability; no contemporary performance claim is permitted.
8. Freeze dictionaries, bundle/tie rules, text masks, semantic rules, pathology rules, report-state construction, Q action definition, preprocessing, penalties, calibration, thresholds, bootstrap family, permutation strata, and gates before reading test outcomes.

Harbor must record actual counts for every filter, missing/invalid clock, duplicate, join multiplicity, dictionary value, exclusion, selected/discarded bundle, latent state, Q abstention/action, and endpoint state. Sources remain read-only and row-level derivatives remain private workspace files.

## Pathology-only endpoint and ascertainment

Set `Y=1` only for pathology-defined M2 and `Y=0` for M0 or M1. `V=1` means a trustworthy index-specimen M0/M1/M2 assignment under the frozen position-aware parser and adjudication; otherwise `V=0`.

Assign grade only from the immediate selected value after an anchored field such as `MVI risk grading:`, stopping before definitions, explanatory bullets, historical clauses, or a new specimen section, or from a synonymous selected-value construction accepted in blinded development review. Handle negation, `not seen`, multiple specimens, and discordance. Unanchored M2, binary `microvascular invasion: present/absent`, positive ungraded MVI, blank vessel-number/distance fields, absent MVI language, definitions, and conflicts cannot assign M0/M1/M2. Pathology never predicts.

Two independently implemented parsers must agree on locked test records. Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to predictors and scores, independently review every parsed M2, every conflict, at least 150 parsed M0 and 150 parsed M1 sampled by year/template, and every test `V=0`; a third resolves disagreement. Reviewers may derive grade only from explicit number/distance language under the frozen definition. Require class-specific PPV >=0.98, sensitivity >=0.95, Cohen kappa >=0.90, and parser disagreement <=5%. Inadequate text, unresolved specimen linkage, or inadequate sampling remains `V=0). The final primary bounds enumerate `Yin{0,1}` for every `V=0`; no unknown case is silently treated as M0.

Raw slides, specimen identifiers, block counts, vessel-distance measurements, and sampling protocol are unavailable. Text adjudication can validate report ascertainment but cannot establish that resection tissue adequately sampled all MVI or that a missed focus was biologically absent. Any such claim requires slides/specimen linkage and expert pathology review in another study.

## Shared report-availability states

For patient i, let q_i be the number (0, 1, or 2) of selected CT/MRI bundles with nonempty eventual report text and let `Ω_i^R={0,1}^{q_i}`. One means the report is assumed finalized and visible at `t_dec`; zero means unavailable. This is a latent evaluation state, not an observed historical variable. Empty stored text has state zero. No probability, independence, monotonicity, or report-order assumption is imposed.

At state zero, mask every report-body-derived item for that bundle: size, lesion count, macrovascular thrombus, report length/sections/template, semantic findings, and unrestricted text. Retain only acquisition facts: valid acquisition time, modality from `Examination`, bundle count, distinct-`examination number` count without the identifier, and development-frequency machine-availability category. At state one, expose the stored report variables. Use the same r in every compared model for C and A; never choose different states for S, B, W, or A.

Development and 2019 recalibration use state augmentation: every patient contributes every feasible report state with total patient weight one, equally divided across states. Cross-validation, recalibration, bootstrap, and permutation are patient-grouped. Augmentation is a robustness construction, not evidence that any state occurred.

Laboratory variables have no primary latent state because they are absent from every primary and transport information set. Eventual-record lab models and per-row state-size audits are diagnostic only.

## Models and baselines

All principal C/A models are elastic-net logistic regressions fitted on the same `V=1` 2015–2018 patients with the same report-state augmentation. Fit a frozen alpha/penalty grid in 2015–2018; select the simplest setting within one bootstrap standard error of minimum patient-weighted 2019 log loss; estimate one weighted logistic recalibration intercept and slope in 2019 without feature refitting. All category handling, winsorization and scaling are development-only. Score every test patient under every feasible report state.

**B — primary structured/basic-report baseline.** Age and sex; CT/MRI indicators and development-frequency machine-availability category; and, only in report-available states, frozen extractions for maximum lesion diameter, lesion count (1, 2, at least 3), and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown indicators. B contains no lab, diagnosis, procedure, pathology, year, raw identifier, formatting/template or post-cutoff field.

**S — primary semantic strategy.** Exactly B plus `C_R), a frozen present/absent/unknown block from `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis`: complete/interrupted/absent capsule, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, and peritumoral hypointensity. S has no unrestricted n-gram, report/examination code, raw machine identifier, report formatting, accession, year, lab, procedure/diagnosis/pathology term, or post-cutoff content.

**W — workflow negative control.** Exactly B plus acquisition count, time since last acquisition, reviewed non-semantic protocol category from exact `examination`, development-frequency machine category, and in report-available states character/line/section counts and outcome-blind formatting family. Formatting family is built in 2015–2018 after masking clinical words, numbers, dates, identifiers, and values; unseen formats map to “unseen.” W receives no `C_R` and no lab field, and is not deployable.

**A — nested attribution model.** Exactly W+`C_R`, with `C_R` bit-for-bit identical to S's block. A-W is a workflow-conditional incremental contrast, not a causal effect.

**U — diagnostic text ceiling.** A plus development-fitted character 3–5-gram TF-IDF after removing identifiers, dates, accession strings, pathology/MVI terms and postoperative text. U is secondary and cannot rescue C, A, or T.

**B0 — transport fallback.** A separately fitted, always-available elastic-net logistic model using only age, sex, CT/MRI indicators, development-frequency machine category, acquisition count, and valid time since last acquisition. It uses no report body, report-derived unknown indicator, examination identifier, laboratory field, diagnosis, procedure, pathology, or post-cutoff content. Fit B0 on patient-level 2015–2018 data, select/recalibrate only using 2019 under the same frozen rules, and score once per test patient. B0 is not substituted for B in the primary S-B estimand.

## Conservative Q policy and transport estimand

For each test patient and threshold p, let `a_S(i,r,p)=1[score_S(i,r)>=p]`. Let `r^+=(1,ldots,1)` denote all selected nonempty reports verified accessible. Define:

`q_i^Q(r,p)=1` only when `r=r^+` and `a_S(i,r^+,p)=a_S(i,r',p)` for every `r'inΩ_i^R`; otherwise `q_i^Q(r,p)=0`.

Thus Q flags only with a verified access state and only when the threshold action would remain the same under every report-availability pattern. If q_i=0, Q always abstains. If any report is historically unavailable, Q abstains; the stored eventual text is never used to manufacture an action in a zero state. In an actual future implementation, the per-report access indicator must be recorded at `t_dec`; without that indicator, Q cannot be operationally claimed even if its retrospective bound is favorable.

For T compare Q with B0 using the same net-benefit contribution:
`d_i^{T}(y,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)][q_i^Q(r,p)-a_{B0}(i,p)]`, where `w=p/(1-p)`.
Report the exact per-patient latent-state bounds over `rinΩ_i^R`; for `V=0`, additionally range over `yin{0,1}`. The denominator is the entire 2020–2021 target frame N, including Q abstentions. Also report state-specific Q flag rate, abstention rate, and coverage `C_Q=N^{-1}Σ_i q_i^Q(i,r,p)`, with exact min/max over r. Coverage is an operating-envelope quantity, not evidence of clinical benefit.

The primary C and A bounds remain:
`d_i^k(y,r,p)=[y-w(1-y)][a_{M1}(i,r,p)-a_{M0}(i,r,p)]`;
`L_k=N^{-1}Σ_i min d_i^k`, `U_k=N^{-1}Σ_i max d_i^k`, with `y` also ranged for V=0. Compute C for S-B, A for A-W, and descriptive S-W, S-U, each model versus flag-none and flag-all. Compute T for Q-B0 and Q versus flag-none. Do not call coordinatewise endpoints a jointly attained latent world.

Use the primary threshold p=0.30 and evaluate p=0.20,0.21,…,0.40. The 0.25–0.35 interval is a prespecified robustness plateau, not a clinical preference. For C, support requires the simultaneous lower confidence bound for `L_{S-B}(0.30)` >=0.01, lower bounds >=0.01 at every plateau threshold, and superiority to flag-all and flag-none under their prespecified gates. For A, support requires the corresponding lower bound for `L_{A-W}` >0 across the plateau and a matched patient-grouped permutation result. For T, report but do not use to rescue C/A: T is transport-supportive only if its simultaneous lower bound is >=0.01 throughout the plateau and its simultaneous lower coverage bound is >0. T is adverse if its simultaneous upper net-benefit bound is <0.01 (or <0 for evidence against positive value), or if the coverage upper bound is 0. T is inconclusive if its net-benefit band crosses 0.01/zero or the coverage interval includes zero. If Q is favorable but coverage is low, state that the policy may be useful only in a narrow verified-report subset; do not translate it to population benefit.

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap resamples, recomputing state extrema and all fixed transformations, with one simultaneous maximum-deviation critical value over the declared threshold/contrast/family. Use patient-grouped, year-blocked permutation of outcome labels within prespecified strata (year, modality/provenance and report-state cardinality), refitting only the declared calibration/model pipeline as specified before unblinding. Report width decomposition into report-state and V=0 contributions. No complete-case analysis replaces the bounds.

## Falsification and interpretation

C is falsified when reliability/overlap gates fail, when its best-case upper limit is below 0.01 or below zero, when the simultaneous lower bound fails the margin across the plateau, or when the matched null is not exceeded under the prespecified gate. A is falsified independently by its corresponding adverse result. T is falsified as a transport hypothesis by an adverse Q-B0 bound or zero worst-case coverage, even if C is positive.

Supportive C with adverse or inconclusive T means S has a robust historical information-set contrast under latent report states, but no report-confirmed abstention policy is shown to operate with material coverage; it must not be called deployable. Supportive C and T means the data support a bounded historical contrast and a computable future workflow design with a report-access requirement; it still does not establish clinical benefit. Supportive A with adverse/inconclusive C means conditional information beyond measured workflow is present without a deployable clinical-value contrast. Supportive T with adverse/inconclusive C means Q may be operationally conservative but semantic increment over B has not been established. Adverse results falsify only their named prespecified hypothesis. Any band crossing its margin is inconclusive, not negative.

Neither C, A, nor T can establish improved treatment selection, recurrence, survival, quality of life, harms, capacity, cost-effectiveness, clinician response, or patient benefit. Those require source-system finalization/release/view logs, validated prospective workflow capture, stakeholder threshold elicitation, treatment and follow-up outcomes, and expert clinical study. Pathology text adjudication cannot establish sampling adequacy or true biological M2. The reserved catalog partition is inaccessible and is not external validation. The 2022–2025 score exercise is drift/identifiability diagnostics only.
