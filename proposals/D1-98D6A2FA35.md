# Episode 12: target-category calibration of the pH-conditioned SpO2 mapping

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

When released monitor SpO2 is 96–97% in invasively ventilated adults, does concurrent acidemia change the probability that observed PaO2 lies at or above 90 mm Hg by at least 10 percentage points among observations sharing the same hospital and similar measured oxygenation context?

This is a target-category calibration question, not another claim about average PaO2. The episode-11 parent estimated the acidemic-minus-normal difference in E[min(PaO2,120)] and found +5.497 mm Hg (95% interval −2.673 to +11.852). Its cap successfully limited leverage from a 556.4-mm Hg value, but its +10-mm Hg materiality threshold has no validated clinical interpretation. A restricted-mean difference is an area between survival curves and can be generated by many different shifts; crossing +10 does not itself show that any oxygenation category changed. The proposed successor therefore makes crossing a literature-defined PaO2 category boundary the outcome. The continuous restricted mean remains a prespecified secondary and cannot rescue the primary.

The inspected full-text XML of the 2026 OXY-BREATHES review defined conservative oxygen targets for study inclusion as SpO2 88–94% or PaO2 <=80 mm Hg and liberal targets as SpO2 >94%, PaO2 >=90 mm Hg, FiO2 1.0, or no upper threshold. Across nine trials and 20,447 mechanically ventilated adults, conservative and liberal targets did not substantially differ in 90-day mortality (RR 1.01, 95% CI 0.94–1.09), with important subgroup and evidence uncertainty. Thus 80 and 90 mm Hg are consequential trial-category boundaries, but the review does not establish that a single observed PaO2 >=90 is harmful, that these categories are universal bedside targets, or that moving an EICU observation between categories should trigger treatment.

A +10-percentage-point margin means at least one additional observation per ten maps into the review-defined >=90-mm Hg range under acidemia rather than normal pH in the measured-overlap population. That is more directly interpretable than ten millimetres of restricted-mean area, but it is still a prespecified population-level calibration margin—not a validated individual minimum clinically important difference or treatment threshold.

The decision relevance is whether the pH-conditioned mapping changes oxygen-target classification often enough to justify prospective synchronized validation and evaluation of whether a prior, independently available acid-base measurement improves oxygen assessment. Concurrent pH and PaO2 come from the same released laboratory panel proxy, so this experiment cannot justify a pre-draw alert, an additional arterial blood gas, lower FiO2, or any oxygen-treatment rule.

## Evidence already supported versus unresolved claim

The inspected full-text XML of Delgado et al. (Antioxidants 2026; DOI 10.3390/antiox15020235; PMCID PMC12938712) reports a retrospective single-tertiary-center cohort of 21,406 adults and 717,064 paired measurements. The paper describes higher PaO2 in acidotic states at comparable SpO2, modification by FiO2, and generally physiologic PaO2 at SpO2 96–97%. This supports physiologic plausibility and a large single-center concurrent association. It does not establish the EICU overlap contrast, multi-hospital reproducibility, a causal pH effect, toxicity, clinical utility, or treatment benefit.

The strongest EICU claim supported before the proposed inferential execution is computational and descriptive. An independent all-row audit of snapshot `[source checksum]` reproduced the parent's 822 SpO2-band rows, 705 acidemic/normal rows, and 237-row, 57-cell measured-overlap population. It also reproduced the parent's weighted PaO2 >120 risk difference (+2.955 points), restricted-mean difference (+5.497 mm Hg), ESS (87.7 and 105.7), and nine sites. At the proposed category boundary, substantially more observations contribute than to the ten-event >120 outcome: 34/110 acidemic and 28/127 normal rows have PaO2 >=90. The overlap-weighted probabilities are 33.353% versus 20.626%, RD +12.727 points. A 5,000-replicate copied-hospital/person bootstrap gave a 95% percentile interval of −11.613 to +31.897 points with 5,000/5,000 replicates defined. All nine leave-one-site-out estimates were positive (+6.533 to +15.936 points), as were earliest-person (+8.812), earliest-stay (+9.066), first-24-hour (+16.439), tighter-cell (+9.646), and 12-hour-cell (+11.510) rebuilds. ESS is 87.7 and 105.7 and the inherited maximum site weight is 45.3%. These are feasibility/reference-QC outputs from this same snapshot, not independent clinical confirmation. The interval contains both zero and +10, so the only allowed reference classification is inconclusive about the prespecified margin and direction.

**Primary hypothesis, frozen before compilation:** in the prespecified measured-overlap population, the symmetric overlap-weighted acidemic-minus-normal risk difference for `Y90 = 1[observed PaO2 >=90 mm Hg]` is at least +10 percentage points.

The tested claim is about a concurrent conditional mapping at one trial-category boundary. It is not that acidemia causes PaO2 to rise, that PaO2 >=90 is toxic, that the patient's intended target was liberal, or that pH-guided oxygen treatment improves outcomes.

- integer `hospitalid`;
- numeric released median SpO2 (96, 96.5, or 97);
- integer `floor(FiO2_percent/10)`;
- integer `floor(PaCO2/20)`, with missing PaCO2 represented by a distinct typed missing level; and
- integer `floor(draw/1440)` for ICU day 0, 1, or 2.

Do not concatenate strings as cell identity. Retain cells with at least one acidemic and one normal observation. Reconcile exactly 237 rows, 57 cells, 198 stays, 197 people, and nine hospitals: 110 acidemic and 127 normal rows. Hospital IDs are 122, 183, 184, 318, 394, 420, 443, 458, and 459.

For cell s with counts n1s and n0s, give each acidemic row `w=n0s/(n1s+n0s)` and each normal row `w=n1s/(n1s+n0s)`; normalize separately by exposure group for risks.

Define three mutually exclusive target-mapping categories:

- conservative-range proxy: observed PaO2 <=80 mm Hg;
- transition range: 80 < observed PaO2 <90 mm Hg;
- review-defined liberal-target range: observed PaO2 >=90 mm Hg.

These labels reference the OXY-BREATHES study-eligibility definitions. They do not identify clinician intent, oxygen exposure toxicity, or a universally correct target.

For exposure a, `R90_a = sum I(A=a) w Y90 / sum I(A=a) w`. The primary contrast is `RD90 = R90_1 - R90_0`. Report both risks, RD90, the complete three-category probability vector by group, raw event/non-event counts, group weight sums, ESS `(sum w)^2/sum(w^2)`, maximum normalized row weights, and each site's weight fraction.

## Uncertainty and diagnostics

Use 5,000 deterministic hierarchical bootstrap replicates, seed 20260911, conditional on the frozen 237-row overlap source:

1. sample its nine hospitals with replacement, taking nine draws;
2. within each sampled hospital copy, sample `uniquepid` clusters with replacement, drawing the original hospital's observed number of people and carrying all their rows;
3. assign every hospital copy and person copy a new identifier;
4. rebuild typed overlap cells using the new hospital-copy ID, discard replicate cells without both exposure groups, recompute symmetric weights, R90 values, and RD90.

Report defined and failed replicates and the percentile 95% interval. Pooling repeated hospital copies under the original hospital ID is a failure.

The primary interpretation is forced to inconclusive if more than 10% of replicates are undefined, either observed group ESS is <50, either group has fewer than 20 raw Y90 events or fewer than 20 non-events, one original site contributes >50% of observed total overlap weight, or deleting any site with at least ten overlap rows reverses the observed primary sign. A sign reversal under earliest-person, earliest-stay, first-24-hour, tighter-cell, 12-hour-cell, or any other required outcome-blind timing/documentation sensitivity is a robustness failure and also forces inconclusive language. Report all estimates even when a gate fails.

The all-source reference execution passes the stated count, ESS, concentration, bootstrap-definition, leave-site-out, and tested rebuild diagnostics, but RD90 +12.727 points (95% interval −11.613 to +31.897) is inconclusive because the interval contains +10 and zero. It supports neither a material category shift, a directional association, adversity to +10, nor “no association.”

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive for a material category-mapping shift** requires the lower 95% bound for RD90 to be >=+10 percentage points and all mandatory diagnostics to pass. The strongest allowed conclusion is: “Within this retrospective measured-overlap construction, acidemic observations had at least a 10-point higher probability than normal-pH observations of mapping to PaO2 >=90 mm Hg at released SpO2 96–97%.” It remains a concurrent association.

**Adverse to +10** means the upper 95% bound is <+10 points. If the interval is wholly above zero but below +10, report a smaller positive category association that is adverse to the material margin. If the upper bound is below zero, report an opposite-direction association. If the interval spans zero but remains below +10, report no resolved directional association and adversity to the +10 margin.

**Inconclusive about +10** includes an interval containing +10 or any mandatory diagnostic failure. If such an interval is wholly above zero, a positive association may be reported while its material magnitude remains inconclusive. A positive point estimate alone is not support.

Endpoint discordance has fixed meanings:

- Y90 support with PaO2 >120 adversity supports category remapping near 90, not increased severe-hyperoxemia probability.
- restricted-mean support with Y90 adversity supports a continuous distributional difference not large enough at the trial-category boundary.
- favorable full-705 calibration with overlap adversity means pH improved descriptive prediction in a broader population but did not establish the measured-overlap magnitude.
- Y90 support with no B2-vs-B1 held-out improvement means the overlap association did not yield generalizable predictive gain; target populations and estimands differ, so the discrepancy is not uniquely attributable to confounding.
- a favorable P/F sensitivity does not establish ARDS or oxygen-treatment benefit.

No result establishes tissue hyperoxia, oxidative injury, clinician target, mechanism, causal effect, benefit or harm from oxygen, or a treatment rule.
