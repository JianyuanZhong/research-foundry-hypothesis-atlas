# Source-bound respiratory-documentation endpoint falsifies a blanket predictive-fitness conclusion while not rescuing semantic expansion

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

Assessed parent `[prior hypothesis]` found that expanding two exact exported eICU `carePlanGeneral` Ventilation strings to all four outcome-blind `ventilated -` strings added only 0.10–0.13 percentage points of coverage in airway-bounded cohorts and worsened hospital-held-out prediction of a later non-peak respiratory-setting reduction. Assessed parent `[prior hypothesis]` further showed informative outcome observation: weighting did not rescue that endpoint, although the strictest airway result was selection-sensitive.

The strongest evidence-supported claim before this child was therefore narrow: for the inherited later **charted-setting-reduction proxy**, omitted exported Ventilation strings did not explain failure of the exact encoding. It remained unresolved whether the adverse predictive-fitness direction generalized to a substantively different, directly computable respiratory-documentation endpoint.

This child tests the falsifiable endpoint-robustness hypothesis: after the same target-independent cohort, landmarks, airway bounds, post-freeze process controls, exact encoding, broader vocabulary, pre-landmark baseline, and hospital-held-out design are frozen, the broader encoding will still fail to improve prediction of subsequent respiratory-documentation persistence. Two source-derived endpoints are used: at least one distinct respiratory-chart offset and, more strictly, at least two distinct offsets in the outcome window. A reversal would show that the earlier adverse predictive conclusion is endpoint-specific; failure to beat the exact encoding would separately show that the two additional strings still do not rescue semantic undercoverage.

This distinction matters clinically. It prevents failure for one chart-derived endpoint from being overgeneralized into a claim that the exported field contains no reproducible multicenter workflow information. Conversely, an association with future chart persistence must not be mistaken for actual ventilation, bedside evaluation, spontaneous breathing trial (SBT), extubation, readiness, care quality, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Computable estimand and analysis

For each endpoint and airway definition, deployment-evaluable hospitals are fixed to those with at least 20 rows and both endpoint classes. Every leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO) fold trains on all other hospitals and evaluates only the held-out hospital. No rows from a held-out hospital enter its preprocessing or fit.

Four logistic models use `C=1`, `max_iter=2000`, no class weighting, median imputation plus missing indicators and scaling for numeric fields, and one-hot encoding for landmark (and `label3` in the exact model):

1. inherited pre-landmark baseline;
2. baseline plus six generic post-freeze process features;
3. process model plus exact three-state `label3`;
4. process model plus all four outcome-blind `ventilated -` indicators.

Primary contrasts are broader minus process and broader minus exact log loss. Negative values favor the broader model. Both row-weighted micro loss and equal-hospital macro loss are reported, with hospital directional counts. Repeated landmarks are kept together in 100 stay-cluster bootstrap samples within each fixed held-out hospital. The bootstrap uses fixed cross-fitted predictions; unlike the primary parent's nested analysis, it does not refit training models and therefore quantifies held-out stay-composition uncertainty only, not training-fit or new-hospital uncertainty.

The endpoint-robustness interpretation is prespecified conceptually as follows. Persistence of the prior blanket adverse predictive direction would require the broader model not to reproducibly beat process controls across endpoints and airway bounds. A reversal requires negative micro and macro broader-minus-process point estimates with fixed-prediction 97.5th percentile below zero in unbounded and at least two bounded variants for both endpoint definitions. Semantic expansion is separately rescued only if broader also beats exact with the corresponding upper bound below zero. Discordance between those two comparisons is an informative partial reversal, not a semantic rescue.

The evidence-supported result is a **partial endpoint reversal**:

* adverse semantic-undercoverage evidence for the original setting-reduction endpoint remains intact: exhaustive expansion added almost no coverage and does not consistently outperform exact encoding;
* a blanket statement that these exported fields have no reproducible hospital-held-out predictive information is falsified for respiratory-documentation persistence, where exact/broader semantic indicators add information beyond generic process controls;
* because the alternative endpoints are high prevalence and themselves documentation states, this is workflow-prediction evidence, not clinical validation;
* fixed-prediction bootstrap and fixed observed hospitals make effect precision conditional on fitted models and this hospital set. Fully nested refitting or external hospitals would be required before a stronger transport claim.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive meanings

A supportive semantic-rescue result would require broader vocabulary to improve over both process and exact models across unbounded and multiple bounded analyses. It would motivate local semantic/interface adjudication, but would still not validate bedside meaning. That did not occur.

A uniformly adverse endpoint-robustness result would have shown no broader-versus-process gain for either respiratory-documentation endpoint, strengthening abandonment of the field for multiple structured outcomes. That did not occur either.

The observed partial reversal means the prior adverse direction is endpoint-specific while semantic expansion remains unsupported. If nested refitting materially widens broader-minus-process uncertainty, or performance reverses in new hospitals, transport would be inconclusive. If direct device/SBT/extubation adjudication disagrees with exported documentation, no clinical interpretation survives even if documentation prediction replicates.

## Verification boundary and limitations

An automatic verifier can check candidate-support hashes, byte identity to the published frozen parent input, required columns, row keys, raw `patient` identity joins, within-definition one-stay-per-person counts, full-window attrition, landmark counts, the complete 20,168,176-row endpoint scan, time inequalities, zero post-row-count mismatches, endpoint counts, hospital eligibility, strict LOHO separation, model specifications, micro/macro contrasts, and stay-cluster resampling.

It cannot establish that any selected row represents invasive ventilation at L; that an exported care-plan value corresponds to an actual daily evaluation, SBT, extubation decision, extubation, readiness, or liberation; that absence of charting is absence of care; that chart persistence is desirable or harmful; that care quality, benefit, harm, or causality is identified; or that results transport beyond observed hospitals. Those claims require direct time-resolved device, ventilator-mode, SBT, and extubation data; local interface/workflow metadata; blinded clinical adjudication; and external prospective validation.
