# Temporal ordering of hepatobiliary work-up versus intervention in the strictly available HCC record

## Scientific deliverable and advance

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the incumbent's strict-prior assay-content versus capture-intensity question, its all-admission population, and its seven-state procedure/discharge endpoint. The new scientific object is a natively timed, examination-aware workflow endpoint and a joint evaluation that asks whether that endpoint contributes information beyond the incumbent coarse transition.

The future solver must newly construct and freeze a patient-level cohort/eligibility ledger, an audited lexical examination/procedure dictionary, the ordered endpoint labels, nested feature matrices, patient split, locked predictions, marginal and joint proper scores, calibration, clustered uncertainty, and prespecified falsification outputs. No model was fit in discovery and no clinical conclusion is claimed.

## Hypothesis and unresolved question

The strongest supported claim is only that this HCC snapshot contains dated encounter, examination, procedure and laboratory rows that can be joined by the two-key identifier and used to describe recorded care. A preliminary scan found 42,692 eligible first-observed patients and substantial examination activity, but it does not establish clinical intent, completion, diagnosis, stage, result, benefit or utility.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In eligible adult patients at their first observed record-coded HCC admission, assay-specific content from strictly attributable prior laboratory rows improves calibrated prediction of the subsequent ordered hepatobiliary-workflow transition—hepatobiliary/upper-abdominal examination first, HCC-directed procedure first, discharge first, or no qualifying event by 72 hours—over matched strict capture/process history alone. A time-aware latent model adds information only if its increment persists after assay-free process controls and temporal permutation tests.

The unresolved claim is whether prior assay content identifies a clinically meaningful distinction in recorded diagnostic sequencing, beyond how intensely the institution captured care. The substantive advance over the incumbent is temporal/measurement specificity: the incumbent can see a procedure or discharge, but cannot distinguish a recorded hepatobiliary work-up preceding intervention from intervention without that recorded work-up. This is still a recorded workflow distribution, not a recommendation for a patient.

## New ordered workflow endpoint

Use only native examination 开始时间, native procedure 开始时间, and encounter 出院时间; do not use order rows as an endpoint or infer intent from them. Use half-open windows [t0,t0+24h) and [t0+24h,t0+72h).

Freeze the following text rules before locked labels. Normalize 检查 or 手术 values but retain the original string, normalized string, every matched token and ambiguous/unmatched audit. Define a primary hepatobiliary/upper-abdominal examination E when 检查 contains at least one anatomy token from 肝|肝脏|肝胆|胆|肝动脉|门静脉|上腹部 and at least one modality token from CT|CTA|CTV|MR|MRI|MRCP|DWI|磁共振|超声|彩超|造影|成像. A sensitivity uses the narrower preliminary rule from the discovery scan (肝|胆|腹部|上腹 crossed with CT|MR|MRI|磁共振|超声|彩超|造影), and a clinician-adjudicated dictionary if review is available.

Define an HCC-directed procedure P when 手术 contains at least one context token from 肝|肝脏|肝动脉|肝癌|肿瘤|胆 and at least one action token from 切除|移植|栓塞|消融|穿刺|活检|造影|灌注|置管|引流|治疗. The raw procedure string audit is mandatory because lexical inclusion is not clinical adjudication. Generic procedures remain in the incumbent endpoint's process/capture history but are not silently relabeled as HCC treatment.

For each anchor, sort valid in-stay E, P and discharge events by native time. If different event classes have an exact timestamp tie, assign the interval to unresolved rather than inventing order. The new 9-state label is E0/P0/D0 for examination, directed procedure, or discharge first in [t0,t0+24h); E1/P1/D1 for the corresponding first event in [t0+24h,t0+72h) when no early event was established; U0/U1 for missing or invalid qualifying time, exact cross-class tie, or an unknown time that could change the interval state; and N72 for no qualifying event before t0+72h. Discharge exactly at 72 hours is outside the primary window.

Retain the incumbent seven-state endpoint exactly as the parent specified: early procedure/discharge/unresolved, then later procedure/discharge/unresolved, or no event through 72 hours, using all procedures and native 开始时间 plus discharge. Do not substitute the new lexical P endpoint into the incumbent. For the direct beyond-incumbent test, also predict the joint categorical outcome Yjoint=(Yincumbent,Yworkflow), with fit-only category support and smoothing. If the cross-product is too sparse for stable proper scoring, declare that comparison inconclusive rather than collapsing states after seeing locked results.

A qualifying examination means a recorded examination initiation in the examination table; it does not mean the test was completed, interpreted, or clinically acted upon.

## Information sets and model comparison

Use the same anchors, strict ledger, labels, patient split and fit-only preprocessing for every model.

- A: age, sex, department, calendar era, admission time-of-day and missingness.
- Q: A plus only strict prior completed-encounter counts/durations, time since last encounter, source-specific row and distinct-encounter counts for labs/procedures/orders/medications, source-type coverage, missing/excluded-time counts, left-truncation and no-history indicators. Q contains no assay names/results and no procedure/order/medication names, doses, routes, frequency or status.
- E: Q plus assay-specific lab identity, qualitative result, valid assay-specific quantitative result, specimen type, recency, span, count, missingness and within-assay change/slope only with at least two distinct valid 检验时间 values. Do not pool quantitative values across assays; no units are available.
- H: transparent parent-comparable strict-history summaries, including descriptive prior event-type occurrence/recency/gaps.
- S: a fixed two-coordinate continuous-time latent event model over the exact strict event ledger used by E/H. Events carry source/type, relative availability time, payload mask, assay identity and only assay-specific lab payload; latent coordinates decay between events, with assay-specific emissions/noise and a supervised head for incumbent, workflow and joint targets. Fix latent dimension, decay family, regularization, missingness term and seed count before locked evaluation; tune only in selection data.
- S_Q: identical architecture and events with all assay identity and qualitative/numeric payload removed. It is the capture/process control.

The primary transparent comparison is regularized multinomial-logistic prediction of Yworkflow and Yjoint: E versus Q, with A as the floor and H as a descriptive sensitivity. The learned comparison is S versus E and S_Q versus Q. The alternative can reveal irregular spacing, nonlinear assay interactions, gaps and trajectory patterns that first/last/count summaries lose; S_Q determines whether this is only capture intensity. Use a joint categorical head for Yjoint, not post-outcome conditioning.

## Split, uncertainty and analysis

Use the frozen patient-level split SHA-256(normalized 患者主索引) modulo 100: 0–59 fit, 60–69 selection/calibration, 70–79 locked evaluation, 80–99 inaccessible. All rows for a patient share a bucket. Freeze endpoint dictionary, cohort, strict ledger, feature manifest, model dimensions and calibration before evaluating locked labels.

Primary results are paired locked changes in multiclass log loss and Brier score (E minus Q; lower is better) for Yworkflow and Yjoint, with incumbent seven-state scores reported alongside. Report calibration intercept/slope, reliability, observed/predicted probabilities for every state, prevalence and unresolved-state share. Use at least 1,000 patient-clustered paired bootstrap resamples, preserving all admissions per patient, for intervals. For Yjoint, require adequate fit/locked cell support and report cellwise support.

Prespecified heterogeneity and measurement checks are 30/180/730-day history windows, no-history and Q-intensity strata, assay-family support strata, calendar-era holdout description, strict versus timestamp-only versus anchor-linked histories, and within-assay rank/quantile transformation as a measurement-scale sensitivity. These are not external validation. No lab units means no ALBI/MELD-style physiologic score.

## Falsification and interpretation

Run and report:

1. shuffle complete strict histories across patients within each partition while preserving Q; an E-Q increment should disappear;
2. permute assay payload within assay and partition, preserving identity, times and counts;
3. permute assay identity while preserving values, times and counts;
4. shuffle event times within patient/source while preserving payload/counts;
5. reverse event order for S; Q/H should be unchanged apart from recency features;
6. compare strict, timestamp-only and anchor-linked histories; a large policy reversal makes availability interpretation inconclusive;
7. compare S_Q/Q; process-only reproduction falsifies a content-specific interpretation;
8. rerun the endpoint with an independently frozen narrow examination dictionary, exact tie-as-unresolved rule, missing-time sentinels and stratified expert review of matched strings if available;
9. audit rows before admission, after discharge, at 24/72-hour boundaries, duplicate patients across partitions, and any use of 检查所见/检查诊断 or post-admission data in features;
10. permute labels within calendar era and department; performance after permutation suggests leakage or label artifact;
11. require the marginal of Yjoint to reproduce separately computed incumbent/workflow labels and counts within numerical tolerance.

Supportive evidence requires a negative 95% clustered interval for E-Q on workflow and/or joint log loss/Brier without material calibration deterioration, attenuation under payload/history/time permutations, adequate non-U support, and no reproduction by Q/S_Q. For a stronger beyond-incumbent statement, the joint score must improve and the gain must remain in incumbent-discordant workflow cells; a gain confined to deterministic overlap is insufficient.

Adverse evidence is no E-Q gain, calibration harm, process-only reproduction, permutation persistence, major strict-to-timestamp reversal, dictionary instability, or endpoint leakage. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse lexical categories, dominant U states, wide intervals, high missing-time/left-truncation, poor joint-cell support, unstable era behavior, or disagreement between clinician review and the text rule.

Supportive computation establishes only incremental prediction of locally recorded workflow. Clinical interpretation requires expert adjudication of examination/procedure semantics and intent, audit-log or result-release evidence, laboratory units/reference ranges, stage and burden, treatment receipt/completion, patient outcomes, external validation and a prospective or causal study. No automatic verifier can establish those claims.
