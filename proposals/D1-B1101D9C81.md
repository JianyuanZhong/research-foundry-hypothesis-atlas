# Conditional transportability of two literal eICU ventilation care-plan labels

## Child question and substantive advance

This is an independent child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent found strong hospital dependence of the two exact structured labels and adverse or unstable chart-proxy behavior. The unresolved, narrower question was whether that dependence could be conditionally explained or calibrated by observable hospital metadata and strictly pre-decision documentation intensity, without changing the frozen cohort, labels, or decision times.

The falsifiable hypothesis was that, conditional on the already selected frozen target-labelled rows, adding hospital bed-count category, teaching status, region, and deduplicated documentation intensity measured before D would improve held-hospital calibration of the literal label and improve held-hospital calibration of the label-to-chart-proxy mappings. The alternative was that these coarse observables would not recover transportability, or that the available hospitals would not provide enough within-hospital label and outcome overlap to test recovery.

The bounded execution does not support a conditional rescue. Literal-label calibration improved on some aggregate metrics but failed the prespecified tail-calibration requirements and was therefore inconclusive rather than supportive. More importantly, only three hospitals had at least ten outcome-available rows under each label for every outcome examined, below the prespecified five-hospital gate. The robust association-mapping question therefore remains unidentifiable at the required hospital-level effective sample size, and predictions cannot manufacture site-level overlap. The overall conditional-transportability gate is adverse. This is a substantive negative result: the parent’s transportability failure is not rescued by the prespecified coarse metadata and parsimonious pre-D documentation features in this snapshot.

The strongest supported claim is limited to the frozen eICU cohort and literal computable artifacts: the selected metadata/documentation model did not meet the predefined held-hospital calibration gate, and the outcome-specific held-hospital mapping could not pass the required overlap gate. This does not establish that either label lacks clinical meaning, that differences are caused by documentation practice, or that the labels should never be used after site-specific adjudication.

## Held-hospital design and estimands

### Literal-label calibration

The binary label is `label_binary = 1` for `evaluation` and 0 for `no_trial`, conditional on membership in the frozen target-labelled cohort. Three fixed models were compared:

1. `landmark`: categorical landmark only.
2. `metadata`: landmark plus `numbedscategory`, `teachingstatus`, and `region`.
3. `metadata_doc`: metadata plus `log1p` documentation counts for the four pre-D features.

Each model is an L2-penalized logistic regression with `C=1.0`, `penalty='l2'`, `solver='liblinear'`, and `max_iter=2000`. There is no hyperparameter search. Within every leave-one-hospital-out training fold, categorical missing-value imputation, one-hot encoding, numeric median imputation, and scaling are fit on training rows only. Unknown held-hospital categories are handled by `handle_unknown='ignore'`.

For each represented hospital, all rows from that hospital are held out; no held rows enter preprocessing or fitting. Primary summaries are equal-site Brier score, equal-site calibration MAE, median site absolute calibration error, and 90th-percentile site absolute calibration error. Row-weighted metrics are secondary diagnostics. The label-calibration estimand is predictive calibration of a literal structured label, not its prevalence in all ventilated patients.

### Label-to-chart-proxy mapping

The inherited outcomes are used without semantic relabelling:

- `setting_primary`: `setting_any_reduction` among `setting_any_available` rows;
- `setting_no_peak`: `setting_reduction_no_peak` among `setting_available_no_peak` rows;
- `sedation_rate`: `sedation_rate_reduction` among rows with `sedation_rate_pair_n > 0`.

The baseline is a pooled label-plus-landmark model. The conditional model adds label, metadata, all four pre-D documentation counts, and prespecified interactions of label with metadata and documentation. Both are the same fixed L2 logistic specification and are evaluated by LOHO. Within-site risk-difference diagnostics compare the observed evaluation-minus-no-trial proxy rate with the predicted difference, but are interpreted only if at least five hospitals have at least ten outcome-available rows under each label.

The association-overlap gate is deliberately stricter than row-level prediction: at least five hospitals must have at least ten available rows under each label for both `setting_no_peak` and `sedation_rate`. If it fails, the mapping result is adverse-sparse-overlap and no apparent predictive improvement is treated as evidence of transportability.

## Prespecified gates

Label calibration is supportive only if, among held hospitals with at least five frozen rows, `metadata_doc` versus `landmark` achieves all of:

- at least 10% improvement in equal-site Brier;
- at least 20% improvement in equal-site calibration MAE;
- median site absolute calibration error at most 0.10;
- 90th-percentile site absolute calibration error at most 0.20.

It is adverse if equal-site Brier or calibration MAE worsens by at least 5%; otherwise it is inconclusive.

Association overlap is supportive only when at least five hospitals satisfy the ten-per-label outcome-availability requirement for both robust proxies. Fewer than five is adverse. This gate prevents a model from manufacturing hospital-level information from sparse or single-label sites.

After overlap passes, outcome mapping is supportive only if held-site equal-site Brier improves at least 10%, held-site risk-difference sign agreement is at least 80%, and risk-difference RMSE is at most 0.10. It is adverse if Brier worsens at least 5% or sign agreement is at most 50%; otherwise inconclusive. Overall conditional transportability requires supportive label calibration, supportive association overlap, and supportive mapping for both robust proxies.

## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive results would have required all label-tail calibration thresholds and at least five hospitals with adequate within-label outcome overlap, followed by robust held-site mapping performance. Those conditions were not met.

The adverse result means the prespecified coarse hospital metadata and four parsimonious pre-D documentation-intensity measures did not recover a validated common label/mapping system in this selected snapshot. It does not prove that documentation intensity is irrelevant, because omitted sources, unmeasured workflow, patient-mix differences, and coarse metadata may matter. It does establish that this specified rescue strategy failed its predefined validation gates.

An inconclusive result would have been appropriate if calibration neither met support nor adverse thresholds while overlap remained adequate. Here label calibration is inconclusive on its own, but the full conditional-transportability question is adverse because robust association overlap is insufficient and the mapping gates therefore cannot pass.

A future supportive claim would require additional hospitals with both labels and adequate outcome documentation, richer but still pre-D site/workflow variables prespecified before analysis, and replication in another multicenter dataset. Claims about literal clinical semantics require site dictionaries and clinician or abstractor adjudication. Claims about actual care, quality, appropriateness, causal effect, or benefit require separately validated clinical outcomes and a different study design.
