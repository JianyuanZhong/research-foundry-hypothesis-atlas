# Airway/device stress test of RT Vent Start versus Off/Suspended transitions

## Substantive child and falsifiable question

This is a targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`, preserving its strict 516-Start/738-Off-or-Suspended complete-window/conflict-clean event cohort, self-controlled windows, deduplication, shifted-sham/Continued controls, exact frozen-landmark non-overlap finding, and semantic boundary. The unresolved question is whether the inherited setting-family rise/fall has temporally ordered corroboration from a differently structured airway/device table, rather than merely being a consequence of the same respiratory-charting workflow.

**Hypothesis:** around a Start event, independently recorded airway/device evidence should appear or strengthen in the post-event window relative to the pre-event window; around Off or Suspended it should weaken. The test distinguishes (a) explicit airway type, (b) nonzero ventilator-history fields, and (c) treatment/note text. It prespecifies that a component is corroborative only if its source has an interpretable timestamp and value, and that a nonzero history offset is evidence of a recorded interval, not proof that support was active at the event.

The strongest claim inherited from the parent is temporal concordance of the setting-family marker with structured RT Vent On/Off documentation. This experiment tests the stronger, unresolved claim that the concordance is also reflected in value-aware airway/device evidence from `respiratoryCare`, with independent treatment/note signals providing negative or supportive context. It does not test clinical benefit or causal effects.

## Population and temporal design

The population is the inherited strict cohort of 1,254 decisive transition episodes (516 On/Start and 738 Off-or-Suspended) with complete ±6-hour observation and no opposite decisive transition within ±6 hours, as defined by the parent. No new cohort selection was based on airway evidence. Hospital is taken from the patient table, and patient/stay is the primary clustering unit.

For each episode at time `T`, every timestamped `respiratoryCare` row in `[T-360,T)` and `[T,T+360)` was included; 60-minute near-event windows `[T-60,T)` and `[T,T+60)` were additionally reported. No value was carried forward. The immediately preceding respiratoryCare row was separately recorded with its age as a stale-carry-forward diagnostic. A row with `airwaytype` blank is missing evidence, not negative evidence. A row with an explicit invasive airway type is airway-positive. A nonzero value in any ventilator-history field (`ventstartoffset`, `ventendoffset`, `priorventstartoffset`, `priorventendoffset`) is an **interval-recorded** indicator only. Zero is treated as an unspecified/ongoing sentinel and is not independently interpreted as active or inactive support. This avoids the invalid assumption that a zero end offset means a clinically completed interval.

Treatment and note matches were counted in the same pre/post and near-event windows using their own source timestamps. Keyword matches are not treated as bedside labels, and exact text was not copied into outputs. The parent’s Continued anchors and shifted shams remain available as negative controls, but this child’s primary airway result is the decisive-event pre/post contrast; any apparent corroboration must exceed what occurs around those controls in the same source-bound implementation.

## Estimands and stress tests

For each transition type and evidence component, report the proportion of episodes with at least one qualifying row in each window and the paired post-minus-pre change. Report the component union (explicit invasive airway OR interval-recorded) only as a descriptive corroboration state, with components always shown separately. Repeat for:

1. the full strict cohort;
2. opposite-transition-conflict-clean episodes (already primary by construction, with an explicit check);
3. episodes with `unitdischargeoffset >= T+360` and `>= T+720`;
4. each hospital, including the number of hospitals with at least 10 episodes of each type;
5. 60-minute near-event windows to test temporal ordering and reduce broad-window accumulation;
6. stale diagnostics: age and class of the last pre-event respiratoryCare row;
7. text/treatment negative controls and the parent’s Continued/shifted-sham anchors where source overlap permits.

The strongest support would require: Start’s explicit invasive-airway or interval-recorded evidence to increase after T, Off/Suspended’s evidence to decrease, the direction to persist in near-event and observation-complete strata, and no single hospital to determine it. A genuine airway corroboration would additionally require nonzero explicit airwaytype coverage—not merely history-field activity—and treatment/note timing that is not absent or wholly contemporaneous with the RT transition.

Adverse results are: no airwaytype observations around events; post-event interval fields rising after Off/Suspended; stale pre-event rows explaining most apparent post evidence; treatment/note signals absent or directionally inconsistent; strong site reversals; or equivalent movement around sham/Continued controls. These outcomes would support only common documentation workflow or semantic non-identifiability. Inconclusive results arise when source timing is too sparse, text is too ambiguous, or site coverage prevents stable contrasts. No result can prove intubation, extubation, active invasive ventilation, liberation, device connection, continuity, causality, or benefit.

## Full-source execution and results

The bounded full-source script is `[internal dataset path]`, [source checksum]. It scanned all four source members and produced `[internal dataset path]` ([source checksum]) and `[internal dataset path]` ([source checksum]).

The execution found 4,025 inherited event rows, of which the decisive strict cohort was 516 On and 738 Off; the remaining rows were retained only for cohort bookkeeping. It scanned 865,381 respiratoryCare rows, 3,688,745 treatment rows, and 2,254,179 note rows. Keyword matching found 217,360 treatment rows and 31,475 note rows somewhere in the source, but no treatment or note keyword hit fell in the tested decisive event windows (`txpost_any = 0` and `ntpost_any = 0` for both transition types). Thus text cannot corroborate these events in this snapshot.

Most importantly, explicit `airwaytype` was absent in every tested pre, post, near-pre, and near-post window for both On and Off groups: invasive, No Artificial Airway, and ambiguous airway-type indicators were all 0. This is an adverse availability result, not evidence of no airway. The inherited clinical question therefore cannot be resolved with airwaytype at these event anchors.

The ventilator-history indicator was observed in 13.76% pre versus 88.95% post for On (near-event 10.27% versus 87.02%), and 14.77% pre versus 67.75% post for Off (near-event 7.59% versus 64.77%). The analogous post-minus-pre changes are approximately +75.2 percentage points for On and +53.0 points for Off, both in the opposite direction to the hypothesized Off decrease. In the conflict-clean sensitivity, the corresponding changes were +76.5 points for On and +56.2 points for Off; complete 12-hour observation gave +75.25 and +53.83 points. The last pre-event row already contained a nonzero history field in 82.75% of On and 88.48% of Off episodes, demonstrating extensive potential stale/history carry-forward; this field is therefore not a valid active-state corroborator.

Because the available `respiratoryCare` device values are interval-history offsets whose interpretation is not a bedside state at T, and because explicit airwaytype and temporally local treatment/note text were unavailable in event windows, the primary corroboration hypothesis is **not supported**. The result is a useful falsification: the parent’s setting-family rise/fall persists as a documentation concordance, but independent airway/device evidence does not reproduce the expected bidirectional Start/Off pattern. The strict boundary is strengthened, not weakened: the setting marker and RT transition should not be used as validated invasive-ventilation or extubation states.

## Site robustness and interpretation

The script reports per-hospital component rates for all 12 hospitals and conflict-clean strata. Site estimates must be interpreted descriptively because the airwaytype component has zero event-window observations at every site and the history-field component may encode workflow-specific interval documentation. Site heterogeneity cannot rescue a component with no explicit airway observations. The parent’s hospital robustness of the setting-family signal remains a separate result and is not re-labeled as airway validation.

## What remains unresolved and required next

A clinical-state claim requires a separate study with device connectivity/waveform logs, ventilator mode and airway modality at transition, tube insertion/removal times, RT narrative adjudication, and blinded expert review of discordant episodes. That study should estimate PPV, NPV, sensitivity, specificity, and inter-rater agreement against a prespecified bedside gold standard, stratified by hospital and literal Off versus Suspended. In this snapshot, automatic computation can check source hashes, row counts, window boundaries, value mapping, event joins, stale ages, and site summaries. It cannot establish that any transition reflects intubation, extubation, active support, liberation, or benefit.

## Changed versus inherited

Changed: added a full-source, value-aware airway/device stress test; distinguished explicit airwaytype from nonzero history-field recording; measured near-event temporal ordering, stale carry-forward, complete observation, site coverage, and treatment/note availability; and made adverse and inconclusive criteria explicit. Unchanged: the parent’s strict event definitions, deduplication, complete-window/conflict rules, shifted sham/Continued controls, frozen 3,788-row cohort, exact non-overlap finding, and semantic prohibition against clinical-state or causal claims. The substantive advance is a falsifying calibration showing that the apparent setting/transition concordance is not independently corroborated by available airwaytype or local clinical text and that interval fields move in the wrong direction for Off/Suspended.
