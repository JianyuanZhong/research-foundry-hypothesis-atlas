# Discharge-proximal hepatic recovery and immediate return after observed TACE in HCC

## Status and hypothesis

This episode-9 child of `[prior hypothesis]` is an observational prognostic experiment. It does not estimate the causal effect of TACE, discharge timing, laboratory testing, monitoring, or a treatment change.

The primary unresolved claim is:

> Among adults with HCC who have a first observed TACE episode, are discharged, and have at least 96 hours between observed TACE start and discharge, does the discharge-proximal change in core liver-function laboratories add reproducible held-out information about a subsequent inpatient admission in days 0–7 after discharge beyond baseline/early post-TACE measurements and recorded measurement opportunity?

The clinically important timing question is whether any signal is concentrated in the immediate post-discharge horizon rather than reflecting a generic propensity for admission. Admissions in days 8–30 are a prespecified temporal contrast, not a competing-risk causal endpoint. The primary broad estimand remains the parent’s early trajectory question for any admission within 30 days; this child makes the discharge-aligned 0–7-day estimand the principal nested test.

A positive result would identify a reproducible risk marker for prospective discharge-monitoring research. It would not establish that discharge was unsafe, that a return was unplanned, or that delaying discharge or intensifying monitoring improves outcomes.

## Evidence boundary and advance

The inspected full-text evidence from Sanai et al., *Scientific Reports* 2026, DOI `10.1038/s41598-026-54466-4`, PMCID `PMC13457603`, source ID `[source checksum]`, supports only the narrower plausibility claim that baseline hepatic reserve and clinically relevant post-locoregional-treatment complications matter in HCC cohorts. It does not establish incremental value of a time-ordered routine laboratory trajectory at discharge in this HCC snapshot, nor benefit from a discharge intervention. The HCC laboratory table has no unit column, so a validated ALBI or clinical threshold is unavailable.

The advance is a falsifiable, time-anchored comparison of early post-TACE recovery with discharge-proximal change, specifically asking whether the latter predicts an immediate return after accounting for recorded observation intensity. A comparable non-overlapping pre-TACE placebo trajectory and timestamp perturbations test whether the apparent signal is TACE-timed rather than a generic trend or timestamp artifact.

## Outcomes and temporal estimands

Follow-up begins at index discharge. A qualifying admission has the same participant, a different encounter key, nonmissing `入院时间` strictly after discharge, and an admission time within the stated horizon. Do not call it unplanned, treatment-related, or recurrent HCC.

The nested primary outcome is any qualifying inpatient admission in days 0–7 after discharge. The temporal contrast is any admission in days 8–30 among those without a 0–7 admission, with horizon-specific counts and confidence intervals. The parent’s broad 0–30-day outcome is reported as a secondary continuity analysis. Choose the earliest admission by `入院时间`; later admissions are multiplicity descriptors and cannot relabel the first event.

For descriptive phenotype only, classify the first admission as liver-coded if any diagnosis on that encounter contains `腹水`/`腹腔积液`, `肝性脑病`/`肝昏迷`, `食管胃底静脉曲张`, or `消化道出血`; otherwise non-liver-coded. This is an operational code phenotype, not adjudicated decompensation. Require >=20 events before fitting any liver-coded model; otherwise report descriptive counts only and call it inconclusive. There is no mortality table, so no mortality competing-risk analysis is allowed.

## Baselines, observation controls, and analysis

For the primary nested comparison, D0 contains age, sex, index department, baseline core medians, early core medians, prior 365-day encounter count, and prior date-aligned recorded indicators for cirrhosis, portal-vein thrombosis, ascites, diabetes, and liver surgery. D1 adds the three discharge-minus-early core changes. Neither includes post-discharge information, post-index medication/order rows, later labs, or length of stay as a predictor.

The interpretation-controlled comparison adds the same prespecified observation-opportunity vector P to D0 and D1: per-assay valid-row count, distinct-timestamp count, span, and maximum timestamp gap in early and discharge windows; total valid core rows and distinct timestamps from TACE start to discharge; and distinct pre-discharge encounter keys represented by joined core-lab rows. P describes recorded measurement opportunity, not severity or indication.

Use regularized logistic regression as the primary model, patient-level five-fold cross-validation within discovery buckets, and a chronological 2010–2022 derivation versus 2023–2025-12-02 test split. Keep imputation, scaling, feature selection, and tuning inside folds. A shallow gradient-boosted model is a bounded functional-form sensitivity only. Report likelihood-ratio increment, out-of-fold AUROC/AUPRC/Brier and calibration intercept/slope, adjusted odds ratios per training-fold IQR, and bootstrap uncertainty with the number of resamples recorded. Require >=20 all-cause 0–7-day events before inferential nested modeling.

## Falsification and adequacy

1. Construct a non-overlapping placebo trajectory anchored at TACE start−28 days: placebo baseline [−42,−28 days) and placebo early [−28,−25 days], using identical assays, covariates, and outcome. A placebo gain comparable to the actual TACE-anchored gain weakens a TACE-timed interpretation.
2. Permute valid lab timestamps within participant and assay using a recorded seed, and run a time-reversal variant. A time-ordered signal should attenuate; these are computational falsifications, not clinical null tests.
3. Repeat complete-case analysis with at least two distinct timestamps per core assay per post-TACE window, minimum-observation and maximum-gap requirements, missingness indicators, and inverse-observation weighting. If D1’s gain disappears after P, the evidence supports a record-process marker, not a biologic recovery interpretation.
4. Repeat with the narrow TACE label subset and preserve every label classification, assay exclusion, parse failure, duplicate collapse, join multiplicity, and population attrition count.
5. Assert no post-discharge row enters features; exact two-key joins are nonmultiplying; timestamps and half-open windows are valid; the index encounter is never counted as a return.

## Interpretation and evidence limits

Call the nested hypothesis supportive only if the event gate passes, D1 improves held-out Brier/calibration or another prespecified incremental metric with uncertainty excluding zero, D1+P versus D0+P is directionally consistent, the chronological split does not reverse the result, and placebo/timestamp/density checks do not undermine timing. A favorable in-sample AUROC alone is not supportive.

Call it adverse/falsified if held-out information is absent, reverses chronologically, is explained by P, or is matched by the placebo/timestamp controls. Call it inconclusive if event or measurement gates fail, assay/procedure semantics are not reproducible, or uncertainty is too wide. If the 0–7 result is null but the 8–30 contrast is positive, retain only the later-horizon prognostic finding and reject the discharge-immediate claim. If 0–7 supports but 8–30 does not, that supports temporal concentration but not a safe-discharge threshold.

It cannot establish true decompensation, unplannedness, treatment intent, death absence, causality, safe discharge, or clinical utility.

## Missing evidence and required next study

The HCC snapshot lacks validated tumor stage, tumor burden/vascular invasion, performance status, Child–Pugh score, laboratory units, treatment dose/territory, scheduling intent, mortality ascertainment, dependable outpatient capture, and adjudicated outcomes. `clinical_documents` at `[internal dataset path]` (table `clinical_documents`, `table-66afca58512c2fca.json`) has narrative fields including `入院情况`, `诊疗经过`, and `出院情况` but no temporal column; `examinations` at `[internal dataset path]` (table `examinations`, `table-fd016d2731b9d6c6.json`) has `检查`, `检查所见`, `检查诊断`, `开始时间`; `pathology` at `[internal dataset path]` (table `pathology`, `table-0a4ee86a446c605c.json`) has pathology text but no time-varying outcome. These can support a separately designed blinded expert adjudication sample, not automatic truth labels. Identifier-only `vitals`, `transfers`, and `front_page` cannot supply missing physiology, transfer intent, or stage.

A supportive finding justifies prospective validation with units, adjudicated decompensation, treatment/scheduling data, mortality and outpatient capture, and a defined monitoring pathway. An adverse result argues against routine discharge trajectories as a stand-alone escalation rule. An inconclusive result identifies the missing evidence or event/measurement limitation; it is not evidence of no biology.
