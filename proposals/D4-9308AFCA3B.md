## Scientific question and advance

The inspected evidence supports only a bounded claim: muscle composition and strength can diverge from muscle quantity, and UKB MRI work has related thigh composition/distribution to grip, falls, and mortality. The unresolved claim is prospective:

> In adults with an MRI-anchored assessment, does bilateral posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration and contemporaneous grip strength add calibrated information about a later worsening in usual walking pace or later reported falls beyond bilateral thigh fat-free muscle volume, after accounting for baseline function and measured clinical rivals?

The leading explanation is that muscle quality/function contains information about future mobility vulnerability not captured by quantity. The strongest rival is measurement/selection: pain, arthritis, neurologic disease, disability, and the selective return of people for repeat assessment lower grip or pace and co-occur with MFI. Persistence of an increment after diagnosis-stratified and repeat-attendance checks is compatible with a muscle-quality signal, but cannot establish mechanism or causality.

This advances the closest inspected UKB MRI work [K2] from cross-sectional association to a date-ordered within-dataset outcome test; it uses direct continuous quality/function increments rather than assuming a composite sarcopenia label, which is important because the prospective osteosarcopenia evidence inspected [K3] is small/inconclusive. The consensus [K1] motivates the quantity-versus-quality distinction but does not test this UKB prospective estimand.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted evidence: (1) locked quantity-only and quantity-plus-MFI/grip models; (2) a same-input nonlinear learned comparator; (3) held-out calibrated absolute-risk contrasts, Brier/log scores and uncertainty; (4) diagnosis, timing, side-discordance and return-selection checks; and (5) a conclusion labelled supportive, adverse, or inconclusive and linked to computed output files.

## Analysis and falsification

Fit transparent endpoint-specific baselines: multinomial/ordinal regression for pace transitions and logistic plus ordinal sensitivity for falls, first Q-only and then nested Q+MFI, Q+grip and Q+MFI+grip, with prespecified age, BMI, height, weight, smoking, baseline outcome, and missingness rules. Do not include any post-T0 variable.

Fit a CPU gradient-boosted endpoint-specific alternative on exactly the same participants, exposures, covariates, missingness policy, T0/T1 rule and outcomes. Development-only tuning; locked temporal split by T0 (earliest 70% development, latest 30% holdout), with each `eid` in one split. The learned method can reveal nonlinear MFI/grip thresholds and quantity-by-quality interactions that additive regression loses; it cannot establish mechanism, causality, or clinical utility.

On the locked holdout, report calibration intercept/slope, calibration plots, Brier and log loss, rank metrics as secondary, the standardized profile risks, and 500-replicate participant bootstrap intervals (or a documented alternative if computationally necessary). If an endpoint has fewer than 100 usable holdout events, absent class support, failed positivity, or unstable calibration, label that endpoint inconclusive and do not change the split or outcome definition.

Rival checks:
1. full eligible cohort versus no pre-T0 inpatient joint/neurologic code, each diagnosis stratum, and code-adjusted sensitivity;
2. unweighted versus baseline-only inverse-probability return-to-assessment weighting, with weight/positivity diagnostics;
3. >=365 versus >=730 day lag, early versus late T1, and baseline cross-sectional associations labelled separately;
4. left/right MFI-grip discordance;
5. same-index 41270/41280 integrity and W00–W19 post-T0 descriptive sensitivity only;
6. development-only permutation of diagnosis labels preserving dates and censoring, expected to remove code-family-specific contrasts.

Supportive evidence requires an adequately supported endpoint with a positive calibrated Q+MFI/grip increment beyond Q-only on the locked holdout, a descriptive lower interval above 1 percentage point for at least one prespecified outcome, compatible direction across pace/falls sensitivities, no diagnosis-clean collapse, no recall-lag collapse, and no large return-selection shift. It supports external validation and decision-impact research, not deployment or mechanism.

Adverse evidence is a calibrated null/reversal, Q-only equivalence, a signal confined to baseline association, collapse after rival restriction, or a large selection shift. It weakens the available-data claim but does not show that muscle quality is irrelevant in other populations. Inconclusive evidence is sparse events, invalid coding, missing temporal order, poor positivity, broad intervals spanning zero and the descriptive boundary, or insufficient holdout support; an imprecise null is not refutation.

## Limits and alternatives

Computationally checkable: source/table/hash compliance, exact key joins, missingness, date ordering, cohort/transition counts, exposure construction, fitted estimates, calibration/Brier/log scores, bootstrap intervals, interaction shapes, and whether the final label follows the prespecified rules. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for actual fall timing/severity, pain and neurologic examination, outpatient disease, gait measurement, MRI segmentation validity, treatment effects, causal mechanism, transportability, and utility.

The transparent regression is retained because it exposes the quantity-adjusted absolute-risk estimand and remains auditable under sparse outcomes. The CPU boosted alternative is retained because nonlinear quality/function interactions are a substantive scientific uncertainty, not a small-AUC exercise. A deep image model is deferred because this catalog supplies derived MRI fields but no image payload. A trajectory model is deferred because the verified later support is one qualifying assessment after T0, not enough to identify a muscle trajectory. A causal mediation model is rejected because pain severity, actual falls and intermediates are not adjudicated. Revisit these alternatives only if image payload, repeated post-MRI measurements, or indication-rich clinical data become available.

Approximate solver budget: 8 CPU cores, 32–64 GiB RAM, 2–4 hours for selected-column parsing, exact joins, two endpoint baselines/boosted fits, temporal holdout and bootstrap.
