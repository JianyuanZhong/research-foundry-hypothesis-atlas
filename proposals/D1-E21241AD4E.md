# Prospectively powered simultaneous validation of all-randomized vasopressor pathway contrasts

## Question, advance, and claims

The decision is whether a locked EHR implementation can estimate arm risk differences (RDs) in a future randomized comparison of norepinephrine-first (NE-first) and vasopressin-first (VP-first) discontinuation without materially distorting any clinically distinct part of the pathway. The parent correctly retained every randomized first patient under assignment and separated implementation, rescue, death, alive ICU exit, stable completion, and EHR measurement error. Its unresolved operating flaw was that confirmation was fixed at 5,920 patients and designed only for a legacy composite while the new confirmatory claim required seven component bounds under a stricter multiplicity family.

This successor preserves the pathway estimands and repairs that incoherence before enrollment:

1. The legacy guardrail plus seven components form one prospectively fixed family of eight endpoints and 32 all-denominator error probabilities. Every probability receives a two-sided Clopper-Pearson interval with `alpha*=0.05/32=0.0015625`. There is no endpoint selection, alpha recycling, fallback composite, or component-specific success claim outside this family.
2. Confirmation is exactly 9,520 new patients, 4,760 per arm. An exact planning calculation at the parent's planning error rate 0.005 assigns each of 64 binomial count tails `(1-0.90)/64=0.0015625`. At `n=4,760/arm`, every count is simultaneously in 11--39 with union-bound probability at least 0.90. The worst familywise component envelope is `2(0.013209464597410034-0.0007115318015549744)=0.024995865591710117`, strictly below 0.025. At 4,759 it is 0.025001103655847776 and fails. This is assurance under a planning alternative, not a promised result.

The strongest existing evidence remains only that the MIMIC EHR endpoint is computable but sparse, restart-dominated, and transaction-sensitive. The unresolved hypothesis is prospective: in the locked configuration mixture, each of the eight EHR arm RDs differs from its pump/reference RD by less than 0.025 in absolute value under one simultaneous 95% family. This is an endpoint-measurement hypothesis, not a hypothesis that either stopping strategy is beneficial, safe, noninferior, or preferable.

## Frozen adverse evidence and unavailable truth

No MIMIC row is resampled and all inherited findings remain frozen. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, 211 (3.49%) were NE-first and 167 (2.76%) VP-first, both below the frozen 5% positivity gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort has 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate failures. These are sparse, observational, post-baseline-selected, and nonexchangeable groups.

Of 73 failures in 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvement, about 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, below the frozen 0.01 gate, with adverse later transport. The full 137-episode transaction audit reproduced all boundaries and 59 raw restarts; five symmetric reconstructions invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited shared-chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stops and 13/59 restarts, 10 NE-first and 3 VP-first, all at `Paused` boundaries. These findings concern EHR transaction instability, not physical pump delivery or intent.

MIMIC lacks randomization, protocol permissibility and assignment, pump-native delivered-rate truth, escrowed cross-system clock maps, adjudicated rescue intent, and reliable evidence that MCS is absent. Those exact data must be acquired prospectively.

## Population, randomization, and temporal boundaries

At least 12 hospitals and two adult ICUs per hospital where available must represent at least two pump-EHR interface families. Contracted expansion to at most 24 hospitals must occur before confirmation. A configuration is the immutable tuple `(site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major version, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version, clock-map method/version)`. Changes after confirmatory first enrollment are versioned and cannot silently pool.

Each configuration completes a 50-episode nonrandomized technical run-in. At least 95% require unique patient-pump-channel-drug linkage, complete pump export from two hours before candidate assignment through closure, and an escrowed clock map; at least 90% require contemporaneous intent material. One repeat using 50 wholly new episodes is permitted after repair; second failure excludes the configuration. Run-in patients enter no trial estimand.

Eligibility is fixed before assignment: age at least 18, alive in an adult ICU; linked pump-delivered NE and VP concurrently for at least six hours; at the start of the final 30 minutes before randomization NE greater than 0 and at most 0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP greater than 0 and at most 0.04 U/min, neither increased during those 30 minutes; no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours; at least three valid MAPs spanning at least three of four preceding 30-minute bins including the last, median at least 65 and minimum at least 55 mmHg; and a preassignment record that either strategy is clinically permissible.

The first eligible randomization per person is the only confirmatory unit. Randomization time `tr` is immutable. Assignment is 1:1 in configuration-stratified permuted blocks: `Z=0` NE-first, `Z=1` VP-first. Actual first stopped drug is postrandomization and never substitutes for `Z`.

The assigned drug should cease within 120 minutes. Reference qualifying cessation `t0_R` is the first linked pump-native delivered-rate time in `[tr,tr+120 min]` when the assigned drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below for five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Programmed rate without linked delivery is insufficient. Nonassigned cessation first is crossover; crossings within five minutes are simultaneous. Later adherence cannot erase either deviation.

Implementation is observed until `min(tr+120 min, death, ICU exit)`. Following reference assigned cessation, observe `(t0_R,min[t0_R+6h,death,ICU exit]]`; pump export, vital status, and location continue through `t0_R+6h` after earlier rescue so subsequent death/exit remains visible. Every source has an escrowed affine map specifying offset, drift, time zone, daylight-saving rule, fitting rows, residuals, and version. The ambiguity band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual capped at 60 seconds. Any uncertainty interval crossing an ordering, five-minute, 120-minute, or six-hour boundary is indeterminate, never rounded toward agreement.

## Immutable all-randomized pathway states

Each patient has one reference and one EHR implementation state:

- `AQ`: assigned-drug qualifying cessation by 120 minutes;
- `XO`: nonassigned drug qualifies first while assigned remains on;
- `SIM`: both qualify within five minutes;
- `NI_OVERRIDE`: timestamped override/abort and no qualifying cessation;
- `NI_NO_STOP`: no qualifying cessation or override while alive and in ICU at 120 minutes;
- `EXIT_PRE`: alive ICU exit before qualifying cessation;
- `DEATH_PRE`: death before qualifying cessation or the implementation boundary;
- `IND_I`: uncertainty can change the state.

Reference states use linked delivered pump rates, implementation records, vital status, location, and mapped clocks. EHR states use only locked EHR intervals, visible assignment/override codes, and EHR death/location feeds. Override is never inferred retrospectively from narrative.

For corresponding-source `AQ`, post-cessation state is exactly one of `RESCUE`, `DEATH_POST`, `EXIT_POST_OFF`, `NO_EVENT_6H`, or `IND_O`. Rescue means the assigned drug returns to threshold for at least five continuous minutes; reference rescue additionally requires two blinded adjudicators to classify deliberate treatment/prevention of worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion. Death and alive exit apply only before rescue and six-hour completion. If implementation is not `AQ`, post-state is structural `SNA`. Rescue followed by death remains first disposition rescue and also carries a subsequent-death flag.

The mutually exclusive disposition is `IMPLEMENTATION_DEVIATION`, `DEATH_PRE`, `EXIT_PRE`, `RESCUE_AFTER_AQ`, `DEATH_POST_AQ`, `EXIT_POST_AQ_OFF`, `STABLE_OFF_6H`, or `INDETERMINATE`. This is a nominal partition, not an ordinal endpoint. Alive exit is not declared success or failure, and death is never traded against rescue.

For arm `z`, each component has every randomized patient in its denominator:

- `H_impl=1{I_R=AQ}`;
- `H_rescue=1{C_R=RESCUE_AFTER_AQ}`;
- `H_death_pre=1{C_R=DEATH_PRE}`;
- `H_death_post=1{C_R=DEATH_POST_AQ}`;
- `H_exit_pre=1{C_R=EXIT_PRE}`;
- `H_exit_post=1{C_R=EXIT_POST_AQ_OFF}`;
- `H_stable=1{C_R=STABLE_OFF_6H}`.

Report `Delta_R,k=P(H_Rk=1|Z=1)-P(H_Rk=1|Z=0)`, exact/randomization-compatible intervals, arm-specific pathway distributions, and any death/exit through six hours including events after rescue. Conditional rescue among `AQ` patients is a selected diagnostic only and must appear next to implementation. No clinical scalar ranking is estimated.

The unchanged legacy measurement guardrail is `Y_R=1` for implementation deviation, pre-cessation death/exit, rescue, or post-cessation death; `Y_R=0` for stable six hours or post-cessation alive exit; indeterminate otherwise, with identical EHR mapping. It requires clinical-panel ratification and is not evidence that components are exchangeable.

## One simultaneous measurement family

For endpoint `j` (legacy plus seven components) and arm `z`, define all-denominator probabilities

`f_zj=P(H_Ej=1,H_Rj=0|Z=z)` and `g_zj=P(H_Ej=0,H_Rj=1|Z=z)`.

For legacy use `Y_E,Y_R`. Then `d_zj=f_zj-g_zj` and exact RD distortion is

`b_j=(Delta_E,j-Delta_R,j)=f_1j-g_1j-f_0j+g_0j`.

For all 32 probabilities, construct two-sided Clopper-Pearson intervals with `alpha*=0.0015625`; their Cartesian product has at least 95% Bonferroni coverage. For each endpoint,

`b_Lj=L(f_1j)-U(g_1j)-U(f_0j)+L(g_0j)`,

`b_Uj=U(f_1j)-L(g_1j)-L(f_0j)+U(g_0j)`,

and `B_Uj=max(|b_Lj|,|b_Uj|)`. The single global claim passes only if `B_Uj<0.025` for all eight endpoints. Equality fails. Failure of one endpoint cannot be rescued by another; an endpoint is not dropped for rarity, poor implementation, death, exit, or apparently favorable results. Report endpoint-specific bounds even after global failure, but label them descriptive within the failed family.

The exact fixed-N calculation is archived in `family_sample_size.json` ([source checksum]) from executable `family_sample_size.py` ([source checksum]), job `[research job]`. It uses exact binomial CDF/SF envelopes and beta-quantile Clopper-Pearson limits. This planning model assumes every one of the 32 error probabilities is 0.005. Different true rates, indeterminacy, configuration leave-outs, or finite observed counts may fail; the trial is not adaptively enlarged.

## Indeterminate labels and exact finite computation

Every patient remains in paired `C_E x C_R` tables including `INDETERMINATE`. The eight frozen binary mappings derive 3-by-3 tables. Unknown labels are allocated jointly as nonnegative integers compatible with aggregate cells. For every feasible completion compute all `f,g`, intervals, and eight distortion bounds. Passing requires one joint completion analysis whose maximum over all feasible completions is below threshold for every endpoint. Best cases from different completions cannot be combined. Return each maximizing value with an integer witness. Use aggregate sparse convolution/branch-and-bound; person enumeration is an oracle only with at most eight unknown labels. Timeout, absent witness, failure on exhaustive tiny-table tests, or marginally incompatible/noninteger imputation is inconclusive.

Indeterminacy over 2% overall, over 3% in an arm-configuration cell of at least 50, or differing by over two percentage points between arms is operationally adverse even if bounds pass. Every EHR-positive, EHR-negative, and EHR-indeterminate row requires reference verification; coding loss of capture as no event invalidates analysis.

## Pilot, confirmation, and accrual

After technical run-in, enroll exactly 1,200 consecutive first randomized patients, 600 per arm, over at most 12 months. Pilot patients never enter confirmation. Before error-table release require at least 100 patients/month in the final three months with one-sided 95% Poisson lower bound at least 85/month; at least 12 hospitals and two families; no hospital over 15%; at least 98% complete linkage/export/clock/vital records per arm; at least 95% determinate state pairs per arm; adjudicator raw agreement at least 85% and Gwet AC1 or kappa at least 0.70; and no safety/equipoise stop.

An independent statistician then recommends only stop or proceed unchanged. Stop if the conservative global-family completion has any endpoint bound at least 0.060; either arm's one-sided 95% upper bound exceeds 0.025 for any endpoint false-positive or false-negative probability; a death/exit/rescue component has observed absolute arm-differential measurement error at least 0.025; cancellation is adverse; or implementation is clinically uninterpretable. These are futility/safety gates, not validation. No pilot result changes sample size, alpha, threshold, mapping, window, eligibility, or pooling. Any change requires a new protocol, run-in, and pilot.

Confirmation enrolls exactly 9,520 new first randomized patients, 4,760 per arm, under one common start and database lock, or closes unsuccessfully at 60 months. There is no outcome-, endpoint-, event-, arm-, adherence-, error-, or denominator-dependent stopping, sample-size re-estimation, or extension. Confirmation requires 158.7 patients/month over 60 months. Before launch, signed contracts must project at least 175/month and the final six pilot months must average at least 150/month; otherwise the design is infeasible and does not launch. Failure to complete 9,520 by lock is inconclusive. Recruitment projections do not establish clinical equipoise or safety.

Implementation remains separate from accuracy. Assigned cessation below 60% in either arm, crossover/simultaneous cessation above 15%, an arm difference above 10 percentage points, or safety-board concern is adverse strategy evidence. It is not measurement error unless EHR and reference states disagree. Report every implementation/disposition state, actual first stopped drug, timing, reasons, subsequent death/exit, complete state transition tables, matching, timestamp agreement, and state contributions to each distortion. Pooled accuracy, PPV, NPV, conditional sensitivity, or a passing common component cannot replace the family gate.

## Transport, falsification, and interpretation

No hospital/configuration/family supplies over 20% of either arm; at least eight hospitals and both families contribute at least 100 per arm. Report configuration-specific transitions, hierarchical multinomial summaries, prediction intervals, and leave-one-hospital/family-out bounds. Each leave-out analysis uses the same locked alpha family and must have all eight bounds below 0.025; it is not covered by the primary family and is a prespecified transport gate. Configurations with at least 500 per arm require legacy bound below 0.05; sparse ones are inconclusive. Material assignment-by-configuration interaction or adjusted state-error range over 0.10 is adverse.

Later eligible episodes never enlarge the first-patient denominator. A recurrence requires a new six-hour dual-drug baseline at least 24 hours after prior `t0`, or randomization if no `t0`; it retains original assignment absent approved rerandomization before a new lock. Recurrences use assignment/configuration-stratified patient-cluster bootstrap and diagnostic marginal GEE only.

**Supportive:** all 9,520 patients are retained; the worst feasible joint completion has every one of eight simultaneous `B_Uj<0.025`; indeterminacy, implementation, cancellation, clock, adjudication, configuration, and leave-out gates pass. This supports only RD measurement accuracy for the locked endpoints/configuration mixture. Any later EHR RD interval must be widened by its endpoint-specific validated distortion bound. It does not support strategy benefit.

**Adverse:** completed confirmation has any `B_Uj>=0.025`, component-specific arm-differential error, cancellation, or harmful heterogeneity. Separately, poor implementation, more rescue, more death, altered exit, or safety concern is adverse clinical/strategy evidence in its own component and is never relabeled measurement error. A rare component whose bound fails remains adverse to the prespecified global claim, not omitted post hoc.

**Inconclusive:** governance does not ratify definitions; randomization/equipoise is not permissible; run-in, pump linkage, clocks, intent, MCS ascertainment, determinacy, optimization, or contracted accrual fails; 9,520 are not completed by 60 months; or a later use changes population, endpoint, strategy, configuration, clocks, or death/exit treatment. Inconclusive does not validate anything.

The verifier must reproduce preassignment eligibility, first-patient retention under `Z`, states and structural `SNA`, all eight indicators, complete verification, integer-compatible completions/witnesses, `alpha*=0.0015625`, exact Clopper-Pearson bounds, the 4,760-versus-4,759 planning arithmetic, phase separation, common lock, and transport gates. Adversarial tests must reject scalar collapse, post hoc endpoint choice, low rescue caused by nonimplementation, rescue improvement with increased death, treating exit as success/failure, overwriting death after rescue, crossover erasure, pilot reuse, adaptive N, and benefit language after measurement success.

Computation can establish row accounting, states from frozen inputs, exact bounds, fixed-N arithmetic, and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot ratify permissibility or value judgments, infer physical delivery from EHR, adjudicate intent automatically, prove MCS absence, judge safety/equipoise, transport to changed systems, or choose a strategy. Those claims require clinical governance, independent safety review, prospective modalities, and a separately powered randomized clinical study.
