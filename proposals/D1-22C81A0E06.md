# Episode 75 synthesis: fixed-slot precision with substitution yield as a decision-validity diagnostic

## Question, advance, and fixed hypothesis

The clinically consequential question is whether a silent review policy that can use an optional repeat lactate at a 24-hour landmark places more eventual hospital deaths into a fixed-capacity review queue than a policy matched for the amount and recency of non-lactate surveillance. This addresses a real allocation decision—who occupies scarce review slots—while separating prognostic ranking from any claim that a test was ordered, a result was seen, or care was improved.

The strongest available evidence is narrower. Baysan et al. (2022; DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750, PMCID PMC9444407, frozen source SHA-256 [source checksum]) validated a modest incremental association of 24-hour lactate with in-hospital mortality in a measured sepsis cohort: the APACHE IV recalibrated C-statistic was 0.62 and rose to 0.64 after adding 24-hour lactate. Sisk et al. (2020; DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242, PMCID PMC7810439, frozen source [source checksum]) reviewed informative presence/observation and reported that missing indicators and summary measures are common ways to represent the measurement process. These findings support possible late-lactate prognostic information and the need to represent observation intensity; they do not establish a fixed-workload gain, lactate-specificity after surveillance matching, external transportability, ordering value, preventability, or patient benefit.

The untested claim is therefore:

> In the audited adult EICU all-baseline/L24 population, at a landmark 24 hours after the first qualifying recorded norepinephrine/Levophed row, an outer-hospital-held-out model using matched non-lactate surveillance plus optional repeat-lactate process and value (R_full) captures at least 3 additional eventual hospital-discharge deaths per 100 fixed review slots than the matched-surveillance model without repeat-lactate process/value (R_attention) at a 10%-per-hospital quota.

The sole inferential margin is Delta_slot >= +0.03, where Delta_slot is the difference in captured eventual deaths divided by the same total number of assigned slots. Three percentage points is a transparent operational relevance anchor for a modeled review queue, not a validated utility threshold and not an estimate of lives saved.

## Why substitution yield is diagnostic, not a second primary gate

For hospital h, both policies select exactly k_h = ceil(0.10 n_h) people. Let A_h = Q_full,h - Q_attention,h be additions, R_h = Q_attention,h - Q_full,h be removals, and s_h = |A_h| = |R_h|. Define

- N_swap = sum_h [sum_{i in A_h} D_i - sum_{i in R_h} D_i];
- K = sum_h k_h;
- S = sum_h s_h;
- Delta_slot = N_swap / K;
- Psi_swap = N_swap / S, when S > 0.

Thus, exactly, Delta_slot = Psi_swap * (S/K). The numerator is identical, and the substitution fraction S/K is the churn created by the candidate policy. A separate positive Psi_swap threshold would be redundant as evidence of direction, and a fixed yield threshold could be misleading: a very high yield with tiny churn may have negligible fixed-capacity gain, whereas a useful fixed-slot gain spread over more substitutions can have a lower yield. Neither denominator represents a validated clinician utility function.

Accordingly:

- Delta_slot is the primary clinical-decision estimand and the only positive materiality gate.
- Psi_swap, total substitutions, substitution fraction, addition and removal death proportions, and their uncertainty are mandatory paired diagnostics.
- A descriptive concentration label is attached without changing the primary state: concentrated if the 95% lower bound for Psi_swap is at least +0.10 additional eventual deaths per 100 substituted slots; diffuse if its 95% upper bound is below +0.10; unresolved otherwise. +0.10 is a reporting anchor, not a support/rejection threshold.
- If S=0, Psi_swap is not estimable, but Delta_slot is computable and exactly zero; the positive primary hypothesis therefore cannot be supported. This is an adverse/inconclusive primary result, not a reason to relabel a mechanically computable experiment as a data failure.
- The verifier must check the identity above in every finite analysis and reject any report with inconsistent signs or numerators. It must not treat the concentration label as independent replication.

This hierarchy resolves the paired-actionability question: substitution yield is a useful decision-validity guard against overdescribing concentration, but it is not a coherent independent guard for accepting or rejecting the fixed-slot hypothesis under equal quotas.

## Queue estimands and baselines

For every retained hospital and model, rank outer-hospital-held-out raw linear predictors, breaking exact ties by ascending patientunitstayid. At 10% set k_h=ceil(0.10*n_h) and select the top k_h; call the two queues Q_full,h and Q_attention,h. Both are modeled silent policies, not observed usual care.

For any hospital set S:

- S_m,h = sum_{i in Q_m,h} D_i;
- P_m(S) = sum_h S_m,h / sum_h k_h;
- Delta_slot(S) = P_full(S) - P_attention(S);
- C_m(S) = sum_h S_m,h / sum_h sum_i D_i;
- Delta_capture(S) = C_full(S)-C_attention(S).

Delta_slot(all retained hospitals) is primary and is reported as 100 times the contrast: additional eventual discharge deaths per 100 equal-capacity review slots. Delta_capture is inherited secondary context, not the primary workload claim. Report stratum-specific Delta_slot for low/high availability and a hospital-macro mean of within-hospital precision differences as an equal-site portability stress test. Also repeat the queue at 20% (ceil(0.20*n_h)) as secondary; it cannot rescue a failed 10% primary.

For the paired diagnostic, report A_h, R_h, s_h, overlap, substitution fraction S/K, addition and removal death proportions, N_swap, Psi_swap when S>0, and 100*Psi_swap as net additional eventual deaths per 100 substituted slots. Report substitutions per net additional death only when the net numerator is positive. Report all model/hospital queue sizes, true positives, false positives, sensitivity, unflagged deaths, precision, and exact rank-switch/tie counts.

For each outer hospital, exclude it entirely from training. On remaining hospitals only, winsorize continuous predictors at training 1st/99th percentiles, standardize using training means and population standard deviations, and mean-impute missing continuous values to zero after scaling. Do not scale binary indicators. Fit unweighted logistic regression with intercept, L2 penalty, C=1, lbfgs, max_iter=10000, tol=1e-8, no class weights, seed 20260973. Score the held-out hospital using only fold constants. No finite training values, zero post-winsor variance, nonconvergence, or nonfinite prediction makes that required model noncomputable. Rank raw linear predictors; retain probabilities only for descriptive AUROC, Brier, log loss, and calibration plots. Do not call them calibrated or use thresholds/decision curves.

For uncertainty, use 2,000 copied-hospital refit bootstrap replicates, seed 20260973. Sample original hospitals with replacement; copies contribute copied evaluation rows and training weight, but all copies of an original hospital remain one exclusion group, so no prediction for that original hospital may train on any of its copies. Repeat preprocessing, four fits, scores, quotas, all estimands, substitutions, and 20% queues. Freeze original availability labels. Use percentile 95% intervals and report finite replicate counts; require >=1,900 finite replicates for pooled, both availability-stratum, and macro Delta_slot contrasts. For Psi_swap, report finite replicates and intervals when estimable; a zero-substitution replicate is undefined for Psi and is not silently converted to zero. Do not allow the diagnostic’s missingness to invalidate a computable primary unless the primary itself is noncomputable.

Use leave-one-original-hospital deletion as a stability diagnostic with already computed outcome-blind design membership. Report pooled, stratum, macro, and Psi_swap deletion values. For the supportive primary state, require at least 80% of finite pooled Delta_slot deletions, 70% of each availability-stratum deletions, and 70% of macro deletions to be positive. Deletions never rescue a failed interval.

## Single ordered result hierarchy

Apply the first applicable state.

1. **Not computable.** Source/header/hash mismatch; failure to reproduce the frozen flow without a documented mechanical explanation; missing required key or clock; wrong one-person, window, unit, revision, conflict, duplicate, range, or outcome logic; failed population gate; outcome leakage or copied-hospital leakage; altered feature list; incomplete scores/queues; nonfinite or nonconverged required fit; or fewer than 1,900 finite bootstrap replicates for a required primary Delta_slot contrast. Do not substitute person-random splits, measured-only primary analysis, changed windows, post-L24 values, calibrated ranks, or post hoc features.

2. **Supportive fixed-slot result.** All primary computation gates pass; pooled Delta_slot lower 95% bound >= +0.03; low- and high-availability Delta_slot lower bounds >=0; macro Delta_slot lower bound >=0; deletion criteria pass; and the pooled 20% Delta_slot upper bound is >=0. This supports a material prognostic queue increment beyond the locked surveillance comparator in this EICU snapshot, with internal site stress tests. Attach concentrated, diffuse, or unresolved substitution diagnostic as defined above; it does not alter this state.

3. **Material slot gain but site/process-limited.** The pooled lower bound reaches +0.03, but a required availability stratum or macro lower bound is <0, or its deletion criterion fails. This supports only a pooled within-snapshot association and rejects the site-robust portion of the hierarchy.

4. **Surveillance-explained.** The unadjusted R_full-R_state slot contrast reaches +0.03, but the primary R_full-R_attention upper bound is <+0.03. The naive increment does not survive the locked non-lactate surveillance comparator. This does not show that attention caused the signal.

5. **Opposite direction.** The primary Delta_slot upper bound is <0, or both availability-stratum upper bounds are <0. This falsifies benefit in the proposed direction for this population, landmark, comparator, quota, and discharge outcome.

6. **Adverse to materiality.** No earlier state applies and the primary interval is wholly nonnegative with upper bound <+0.03. The data permit a smaller nonnegative queue difference but reject the locked clinically interpretable margin.

7. **Inconclusive.** Every other computable result, including an interval spanning zero or +0.03, favorable point estimates without required lower bounds, discordant strata not meeting an earlier state, unstable deletions, or a non-opposite 20% result. Inconclusive does not support deployment and cannot be rescued by the descriptive yield, measured-only decomposition, AUROC, calibration, or capture metric.

A separately reported paired diagnostic may be concentrated (Psi_swap lower bound >=+0.10), diffuse (Psi_swap upper bound <+0.10), or unresolved. If S=0 in the point analysis, report Psi_swap=undefined, S/K=0, and diagnostic unavailable; the primary point contrast is exactly zero. The diagnostic must never upgrade an adverse/inconclusive primary state or downgrade a supportive one.

## Evidence limits, required next data, and verifier boundary

The structured sources can compute the frozen cohort, relative clocks, revision/conflict resolution, models, held-out scores, equal-capacity queues, paired substitutions, discharge mortality, uncertainty, and ordered state. They cannot establish adjudicated pressor administration/indication; lactate specimen type or assay quality; order, specimen, result-release, display, or clinician-view timestamps; clinician concern, actual review, workload, or baseline workflow; hospital calendar period; treatment decisions; review utility/cost weights; death time, cause, or preventability; counterfactual outcomes; harms; safety; fairness; cost-effectiveness; or external transportability.

The verifier can recompute source hashes/headers, population flow, temporal and revision rules, feature vectors, fold isolation and constants, held-out fits/scores, quotas, queues, additions/removals, the Delta_slot = Psi_swap*(S/K) identity, bootstrap intervals, deletion proportions, and the ordered label. It must test correct computations paired with unsupported conclusions and fixtures for supportive, site/process-limited, surveillance-explained, opposite, adverse-to-margin, inconclusive, zero-substitution, and noncomputable cases. It must reject claims that the experiment orders lactate, measures occult hypoperfusion, identifies preventable deaths, reflects bedside visibility/review, improves survival, is fair, or transports externally. It can verify that supportive, adverse, and inconclusive labels follow the locked numeric rules; it cannot adjudicate timestamp meaning, clinical preventability, actual workflow, patient utility, causal benefit, safety, or transportability.

A supportive result warrants only an independent prospective silent validation with synchronized order/result/display/review timestamps and calibration/implementation assessment. A policy that changes testing or treatment requires a later prospective impact study, ideally randomized for causal patient benefit. Clinical informatics and critical-care expert review are required before treating recorded offsets as workflow events or selecting a utility threshold.
