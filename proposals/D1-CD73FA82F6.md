# Patient-packet visibility frontier for comparator-closed, whole-panel documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Scientific decision and the consequential repair

This is a targeted Lead successor to assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's source-defined first-eligible HCC-resection population, coherent operative clocks, 24-hour primary cutoff and locked sensitivities, 90-day imaging and 365-day prior-treatment windows, possibility-based temporal quarantine, whole-accession report units, reversible clean-text lineage, six immutable corpus-wide reader books and nine globally fixed radiology-by-pathology pairs, indivisible pathology terminals, additive fixed-capacity models, three comparator-closed contrasts, strict margin of more than five documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed positions, exact attained finite-corpus extrema, source-state/reader uncertainty, and whole-patient singleton deletion.

The selected parent repairs the missing report-time problem with a scalar fraction over clean accessions. That scalar is still mismatched to the decision it is meant to inform: an overall fraction can be satisfied by releasing many accessions from other patients while a patient's preoperative packet has no visible report, and it removes source-unavailable accessions from the denominator even though an audit would experience them as missing workflow inputs. A patient/episode workflow requires a lower bound for every eligible decision packet, with the denominator fixed before looking at ranks or outcomes.

This child replaces only that operational bridge with the following unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis:

> Conditional on the parent's all-content hypothesis passing at confirmatory `K_.10`, there exists a preregistered minimum per-patient decision-packet visibility fraction `q* <= 0.80` such that, in each 2020 and 2021 world, whenever every eligible patient has at least `q*` of all decision-eligible CT/MRI whole-accession units visible by the cutoff, the locked `G_clean` queue retains all six comparator-closed, singleton-stable margins above five under every admissible placement of the remaining unavailable units, source state, pathology terminal, and whole-reader-book pair.

The threshold 0.80 is a prospective feasibility stress criterion, not measured coverage, capacity, or benefit. The HCC data compute the sharp finite-corpus `q*` frontier under this per-patient constraint; they do not establish that any report was released or viewed by the cutoff. A later accession-linked audit must certify the same patient-level lower bound, not merely an overall average.

The clinical advance is a decision-aligned go/no-go target. A favorable all-content result with a scalar `c*` could still conceal packet-level blind spots; a favorable `q*<=.80` result says exactly what every patient's report-access audit must establish before a silent-mode study. The stricter denominator also exposes whether source corruption, blank accessions, or missing reports—not only delayed release—make the workflow infeasible.

## 6. Patient-packet release frontier

This section is the only substantive addition and replaces the parent's scalar whole-corpus release constraint.

For each reachable clean or source-unavailable CT/MRI unit in year-a patient i and world `omega`, construct a deterministic decision-packet unit set `D_{ia}(omega)` from `examinations`: exact-deduplicate rows, retain raw ordinals and bytes, group nonblank `检查号` rows by `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)`, and represent each exact-deduplicated blank-`检查号` component as a distinct unresolved denominator unit keyed by its patient, encounter, raw ordinal and bytes. Include every such unit whose acquisition interval lies wholly in `[c_d-90d,c_d)`, whether its source is clean, empty, corrupt, contaminated, nonreversible, or unresolved. This is a workflow denominator, not a semantic availability label.

Let `C_{ia}(omega)` be the clean, reversible, noncontaminated subset of `D_{ia}(omega)`. For `u in C`, introduce a binary operational release bit `r_u`: `r_u=1` exposes the complete source-eligible accession exactly as in the parent, and `r_u=0` replaces every report-derived `S,Q,X_clean` block from that accession by the development-defined operational-unavailable block. A unit in `D-C` has fixed `r_u=0`; it cannot be made visible by analysis. Preserve acquisition-only `Z`, and apply the identical whole-unit mask in `R_clean`, `G_clean`, and the locked `Gclean_mask`; `B` is unchanged. No report component may be released separately. The same bits are used across arms, contrasts, capacities, books, and undeleted/deleted computations.

Define patient-level visible coverage as `v_ia(r) = sum(r_u for u in C_ia) / |D_ia|` when `|D_ia|>0`, and `v_ia(r)=0` when the packet is empty.
An empty packet is therefore uncovered, and a source-unavailable or blank-accession unit counts as a missing packet input. This prevents denominator laundering. For `q` in the sorted union `Q` of 0, 1, and every exact breakpoint `m/|D_ia(omega)|` for nonempty packets in both test years and all worlds, admissible release patterns satisfy `sum(r_u for u in C_ia(omega)) >= ceil(q |D_ia(omega)|)` simultaneously for every eligible patient.
There is no mean-patient, overall-accession, independence, missing-at-random, or equal-state-weight assumption. A `q>0` point is infeasible if any patient lacks enough clean units; it is not repaired by dropping that patient or changing the denominator. The feasible sets are nested as q increases.

For each year-by-contrast coordinate, q, coherent source/pathology/book world, and admissible patient-packet pattern, compute exact attained undeleted and singleton-deleted lower and upper endpoints. On deletion, remove the deleted patient's complete packet and impose the same q constraint separately on every remaining patient; do not recompute books, roles, models, `N_ref`, K, or preprocessing. Keep the fixed-capacity top-K estimand and all parent gates unchanged. A frontier point is undefined and the experiment is globally inconclusive if any required world lacks K patients, an exact optimizer, a complete packet ledger, or a feasible release pattern at the tested q.

Define `q*` as the smallest feasible breakpoint for which all six undeleted and all six singleton-deleted lower endpoints are strictly greater than 5. Report every attaining q witness, including the patient packets exposed/hidden and the last failing breakpoint. If no such breakpoint exists, q* is undefined. The parent all-content state must pass first; a partial packet pattern cannot rescue it. If q=1 is infeasible because some decision-eligible unit is intrinsically unavailable, that is explicitly reported as parent-compatible eventual-content performance but packet-infeasible operational evidence.

The operational hypothesis passes only if all parent confirmatory gates pass, q* exists, and q*<=.80. The .05/.20 K frontiers and 12/48/72-hour cutoffs are diagnostics and cannot rescue K_.10/24-hour failure. CT-only and MRI-only packet frontiers are descriptive and cannot rescue the joint primary.

The external audit target is now exact: for every eligible patient and every decision-eligible accession in `D`, link accession/version identity, finalization and release timestamps, and preferably clinician-view timestamp; define primary visibility as complete final content released and viewable by `c_d` (with a release-only secondary report that cannot rescue the primary); and calculate a one-sided lower bound on `v_{ia}` for each patient. The audit must include source-unavailable/blank-accession units in the denominator or prove they are not decision-eligible. An overall fraction, patient-average, post hoc exclusion, or report text without version lineage is insufficient. Current HCC data have none of these fields, so deployment readiness, historical availability, workflow feasibility, and clinician action remain unestablished.

## 7. Exact computation and falsification

Compile finite patient domains carrying membership/year/Y, Z/S/Q/X blocks, locked scores, the full `D`/`C` packet ledger, release masks, source/form/terminal hashes, ties, and raw backpointers. Conditional on each of nine books, exact MIP or equivalent certified separation chooses one coherent state per patient, whole-accession release bits, and exactly K selected patients per arm. Add the patient-packet constraints `sum(r_u for u in C_i) >= ceil(q |D_i|)` for every eligible patient in the current world; do not substitute a global accession constraint. Lazy rank-inversion constraints enforce each frozen score order. Optimize each contrast separately but use one coherent world within a contrast.

Require zero integer gap, numerical feasibility and transformed-loss residual <=1e-8, no violated rank inversion or packet constraint, raw/quotient replay, all tied maximizers, and exhaustive agreement with brute force on synthetic fixtures through 12 patients and real-data shards through 20. Test empty packets, blank accessions, intrinsically unavailable units, one-patient/multi-accession packets, and singleton deletion. No state cap, release-pattern sample, beam search, favorable incumbent, omitted book/world/patient, time-limited bound, or overall-coverage shortcut can decide the hypothesis.

One-hot confirmatory result:

1. `inconclusive`: any source/hash/header, roster/form/book, chronology, leakage, packet-denominator, N/K/event/anchoring, fit, replay, feasibility, or exact-optimization gate fails.
2. `visibility-robust patient-packet supportive`: all parent six coordinates are undeleted and singleton stable above 5, q* exists, and q*<=.80.
3. `all-content supportive but packet-infeasible/brittle`: all parent coordinates pass but q* > .80 or q* is undefined because a permitted patient packet cannot meet the required coverage.
4. `semantic-evidence-only`: both years' `Delta_R` and `Delta_mask` pass all-content/singleton gates but at least one `Delta_B` coordinate fails.
5. `policy-only`: both `Delta_B` years pass but not all semantic coordinates pass.
6. `falsified-localized`: gates pass and none above applies; emit patient, world, year, comparator, q, and deletion witnesses. Mark a coordinate all-world-adverse only when its upper endpoint is <=5.

Equality to 5 or q*=.80 passes the q threshold only if every capture margin is strictly greater than 5. A favorable AUC, pooled year, sensitivity capacity, adjudicated-only reader pair, average coverage, scalar accession fraction, or isolated release pattern cannot rescue failure.

Supportive result meaning: in the frozen eventual-report corpus, the locked full model retains more than five additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed positions over all three comparators in both test years, every declared world, and every singleton deletion, even under every whole-accession release placement satisfying the minimum q* coverage for every patient. This supports obtaining the specified accession/version release-and-view audit. Only if every patient clears that lower bound and a separate clinical-capacity review passes may prospective silent mode be considered.

Adverse or brittle meaning: the proposed universal patient-packet operational margin does not survive the prespecified source/release stress. It does not prove radiology semantics are useless, reports are late, or a different model/capacity cannot help. Inconclusive means the data, annotation, packet ledger, or computation cannot resolve the claim; it is not evidence for or against benefit. Even a supportive result is not evidence of biological M2, clinician action, treatment effect, safety, cost, fairness, or transportability.
