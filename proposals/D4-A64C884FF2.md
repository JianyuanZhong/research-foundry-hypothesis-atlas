# Gross-burden strategy challenge for fixed-capacity cystatin-C allocation

## Clinical decision, evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

The selected parent correctly separates conditional performance after a protocolized UACR-first step (W1), immediate UACR-free allocation (W0), and empirically observed pre-assay chronology (Wobs), which UK Biobank cannot identify. Its strongest remaining clinical weakness is that W1 and W0 are judged only against within-branch comparators. Even if both pass, the parent cannot say whether waiting for UACR finds enough additional low-filtration reclassifications to warrant considering the extra testing step rather than allocating the same cystatin capacity immediately.

The strongest claim supported before opening locked outcomes remains feasibility and timing nonidentifiability. The inherited full read-only reconstruction found 134,118 eligible participants (O: development 80,516; test 26,977; replication 26,625) and 130,257 with usable censor-aware instance-0 UACR (V, 97.12% overall; 97.18% test; 97.14% replication). This turn confirmed the complete catalog schemas and physical headers contain all required fields; the biological-samples header has 1,775 unique columns and no collection, result, order, date, or timestamp field. These facts establish calculability, coverage, and absent timing evidence—not benefit or real-world burden.

Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041) is available as inspected full-text XML in frozen source [source checksum]. It supports association and internal prediction of creatinine-cystatin differences in UKB, including grip among enriched predictors, but not fixed-capacity strategy superiority, UACR chronology, measured GFR, or benefit. The Europe PMC record and abstract for the KDIGO 2024 executive summary (Levin et al.; DOI 10.1016/j.kint.2023.10.016; frozen search source [source checksum]) support the clinical importance of CKD evaluation and implementation but do not prescribe this sequence. The full KDIGO PDF was acquired as source [source checksum]; passage extraction did not complete, so no claim here relies on having inspected that PDF text.

The new falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In each locked partition, a protocol that makes one UACR order attempt for every member of O, waits for the result, and applies the frozen AG score among usable-result participants (AG_V) will identify at least 2 additional combined-equation eGFR<60 reclassifications per 1,000 cystatin assays versus immediate application of the frozen UACR-free grip score to all O (G_O), with yield ratio at least 1.10, positive marker-discordance yield, and positive directional-swap yield. If inherited and direct N18 sufficiency rules pass, AG_V will add at least 1 reclassification followed by first 10-year inpatient N18 per 1,000 cystatin assays. Both strategies use exactly floor(0.20*|O_partition|) cystatin assays. The sequential strategy additionally incurs exactly |O_partition| gross protocol UACR orders under this one-attempt specification. All parent branch, robustness, lock, and evidence-limit rules remain mandatory.

This distinguishes label-yield advantage at a known gross testing requirement from net clinical or economic value. No defensible monetary or patient-utility exchange rate between a urine-testing episode and biochemical reclassification is available, so cost-effectiveness is not an endpoint.

## Substantive advance

The parent prevents calculable UACR from masquerading as pre-decision UACR. This successor directly asks whether protocolized UACR-first AG_V out-yields admissible immediate G_O at identical cystatin capacity, and exposes the exact gross urine-testing requirement for that gain.
