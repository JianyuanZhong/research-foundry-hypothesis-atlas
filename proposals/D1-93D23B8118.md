# Proposal: MIMIC restart labels as a bounded documentation signal, not a retrospective reference standard

## Change from the inherited candidate

This child keeps every inherited negative result and the noncausal boundary. It repairs one remaining ambiguity: it separates what MIMIC can compute about the *documentation process* from what it cannot identify about pump delivery or rescue intent. The retrospective phase is recast as a complete, prespecified concordance/discordance audit plus partial-identification analysis, with an explicit stopping rule if no reference-positive subset can be defended. It does not turn `inputevents` transactions, physiology, procedures, datetime events, or notes into administered-therapy truth.

The inherited findings remain frozen: assigned-strategy positivity failed (NE-first 211/6,040 = 3.49%; VP-first 167/6,040 = 2.76%); the observed first-stop arms and determinate event counts failed the inherited direct-comparison gates; the six-hour endpoint is restart-dominated (73 raw failures, 59 with restart, 36 restart-only); the compact prognostic/observed-order models showed essentially no incremental Brier information and adverse later-period stress transport; and all 137 episodes are MCS-unknown, not MCS-negative. No estimate here is causal and no stop-order recommendation follows.

## Falsifiable question and evidence boundary

**Question.** Within the inherited 137 first-stop episodes, can a prespecified combination of transaction metadata, symmetric interval reconstruction, charted MAP, procedure/datetime events, and available notes identify a subset in which an EHR restart is a sufficiently specific proxy for true resumed pump delivery for hemodynamic support, or at least identify informative bounds on its PPV?

The strongest retrospective claim that the supplied data support is only that 13/59 EHR restarts are *documentation-transition-compatible*: the first positive interval for the stopped agent begins within 60 minutes, shares `orderid` or `linkorderid` with the boundary row, and has a normalized dose within 20% of the boundary dose. This is a reproducible concordance pattern, not proof of a pump-off interval, delivery, bag replacement, or intent. Six of those 13 are restart-only raw failures, so the inherited endpoint sensitivity would change from 73 to 67 if—and only if—those six were provisionally treated as transition artifacts. The data do not establish that treatment.

The untested claim is stronger and clinically consequential: that any retrospective subset has PPV >=0.80 for *delivered stopped pressor after a genuine pump-off interval with hemodynamic-support intent*, or that PPV is symmetric between NE-first and VP-first. MIMIC cannot identify that claim because it has no pump-native delivery trace, consumable/channel transition log, timestamped clinician intent, or validated MCS state.

## Retrospective population and time rules

Use all 137 inherited adults, without resampling or eligibility repair: first qualifying non-simultaneous documented stop in the first ICU stay, after at least six hours of concurrent supported NE and VP while the comparator remains active. Time zero is the end of the reconstructed active interval for the first stopped agent. Preserve the inherited forward-time phenotype and dose/unit rules: NE item 221906 in `mcg/kg/min` or weight-normalized `mcg/min` with patient weight 30–300 kg and normalized dose <=5; VP item 222315 in units/min or units/hour divided by 60, <=0.2 units/min; positive intervals require positive rate and `endtime > starttime`. No future restart, physiology, ICU exit, death, or MCS field may enter eligibility.

For each episode, evaluate all supported positive rows for the stopped agent in `(t0, min(t0+6h, ICU outtime, death)]`. Retain the inherited raw composite unchanged: stopped-agent restart, competing/rescue pressor, NE escalation, repeated MAP <60, death, or active-pressor ICU exit. Preserve the one indeterminate episode. The primary descriptive endpoint is the raw label; the transition-screen endpoint is a sensitivity diagnostic only.

## Complete EHR concordance/discordance audit

For every episode and every candidate restart, retain the first qualifying row and the immediately preceding boundary row, including `starttime`, `endtime`, `storetime`, `rate`, `rateuom`, `amount`, `amountuom`, `patientweight`, `orderid`, `linkorderid`, `statusdescription`, `continueinnextdept`, `isopenbag`, `ordercategoryname`, `secondaryordercategoryname`, `ordercomponenttypedescription`, `ordercategorydescription`, `originalamount`, and `originalrate` where present. Do not collapse duplicate transactions. Report missingness and multiplicity for each field.

Define documentation concordance strata before examining aggregate outcomes:

* **D0, no restart:** no supported positive stopped-agent interval in the six-hour window.
* **D1, weak restart:** a positive interval exists but fails at least one continuity condition.
* **D2, transition-compatible:** gap <=60 minutes, same `orderid` or `linkorderid`, and normalized dose ratio 0.8–1.2.
* **D3, high-documentation-concordance (exploratory only):** D2 plus a boundary status in the locally observed pause/change/finished family, positive restart duration, no timestamp tie, and no `continueinnextdept=1` at either boundary. D3 is not a truth label; if its count is zero or small, stop rather than broaden it.

Report D strata separately by stopped agent and observed first-stop arm, and report all discordance patterns (status, order-chain, dose, gap, continuation, open-bag, storetime-versus-clinical-time). The earlier complete audit is retained: NE-first 46/84 restarts and VP-first 13/53; D2 counts 10 and 3 respectively; medians and quantiles are 60 (31.25–106.75) minutes for NE-first and 43 (28–81) for VP-first. These counts are documentation diagnostics and must be recomputed from the bound source files by the compiler, not copied as clinical truth.

## Physiology, procedure/datetime, MCS, and notes: what they can and cannot add

Use `icu/chartevents` only as time-local ascertainment context. Join on `(subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id)` and use `charttime` (with `storetime` retained as documentation timing). MAP item IDs are arterial 220052 and non-invasive 220181; apply the inherited validity and source-priority rules over `[t0-2h,t0]` and the six-hour outcome window. Charted MAP can establish that a low-MAP component was documented near a transaction; it cannot establish pump delivery, pump-off duration, or intent. It is post-boundary context and cannot be used to manufacture a reference label.

Use `icu/procedureevents` on the same ICU keys, with `starttime`, `endtime`, `itemid`, `statusdescription`, `continueinnextdept`, `orderid`, `linkorderid`, and location fields. Use only dictionary-confirmed MCS procedure IDs from `icu/d_items`; a procedure interval active at t0 is chronology/support evidence. Use `icu/datetimeevents` with `charttime`, `itemid`, `value`, `valueuom`, `warning`, again as chronology evidence. The frozen broad dictionary expression is `ecmo|extracorporeal|iabp|intra.?aortic|impella|lvad|rvad|ventricular assist|balloon pump|mechanical circulatory|centrifugal pump|durable vad`; non-positive functional/activity or procedure evidence remains `MCS-unknown`. The inherited audit found zero positive episodes under its positive rule and therefore 137 unknown, not 137 negative.

Available notes are not a retrospective medication reference standard. `note/discharge` has `note_id,subject_id,hadm_id,note_type,note_seq,charttime,storetime,text`; it joins to the hospitalization by `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, not to an ICU stay or transaction by a native key. `note/radiology` has the same narrative fields and may have absent `hadm_id`; its text can provide delayed contextual mention of devices or hemodynamics, not synchronized infusion delivery or decision intent. `discharge_detail` and `radiology_detail` contain field/value rows keyed by `(note_id,subject_id)` and have no event timestamp. Search these notes only in a prespecified, time-aware descriptive analysis for explicit medication/device-transition language, preserving note time and section/context; a lexical mention, omission, or discharge summary cannot adjudicate a six-hour restart. Do not claim ICU progress notes, MAR audit trails, pump logs, or bedside intent are available: they are not among the supplied files.

## Partial identification rather than fabricated truth

Let `L=1` be the raw EHR restart, `A` be a documentation stratum (D1/D2/D3), and `T=1` be the unavailable prospective reference event: stopped-agent delivery after a genuine pump-off interval with adjudicated hemodynamic-support intent. For any MIMIC-only stratum, the observed data identify `P(L=1|A,arm)` and the joint distribution of EHR/physiology/document fields, but not `P(T=1|L=1,A,arm)`.

The assumption-free PPV bounds are therefore `[0,1]` for every restart stratum, including D2 and any D3. This is a useful negative feasibility result, not a failure to analyze. Report the exact logical reason: no observed field is a validated measurement of `T`, and notes lack synchronized intent/delivery. Do not report a pseudo-PPV.

Provide a transparent sensitivity surface instead of an unjustified point estimate. For each arm and stratum, show implied PPV under external misclassification scenarios `q=P(T=0|L=1,A,arm)` from 0 to 1 (PPV = 1-q), and, separately, under a prospective pilot-calibrated interval for q. If external validation supplies bounds `q in [q_L,q_U]`, propagate them exactly to PPV `[1-q_U,1-q_L]`, with Wilson/Clopper–Pearson uncertainty for the observed restart frequency kept distinct from uncertainty in q. A PPV >=0.80 conclusion is permitted only when the lower external/reference-standard bound is >=0.80 in both arms and the arm-difference interval is below 0.10. MIMIC alone can never pass this gate.

A second partial-identification display bounds the fraction of raw failure labels that could be documentation transitions. The only data-grounded lower bound is the count of D2 restart-only events (6/73); it is a lower bound on *screen-positive labels*, not on true artifacts. Without assumptions, the fraction of raw failures that are true transition artifacts lies in [0, 1] (subject to the observed raw failure count), so the D2 count must not be described as an identified artifact fraction. Report the raw and diagnostic counts side by side.

## Staged experiment and stopping rules

### Stage 0: computational feasibility and audit lock

Run one complete, no-sampling scan of all inherited episodes and all rows in the relevant archive members, then freeze the code, item mapping, strata definitions, missingness table, and aggregate output. Verify that all required columns exist and that joins preserve one episode per inherited `stay_id`. Stop the retrospective branch if any required source member or key/time field is unavailable, if D3 requires post hoc thresholds, or if notes cannot be linked without silently assigning an ICU stay.

### Stage 1: MIMIC documentation diagnostic

Advance only a documentation report: raw endpoint counts, D0–D3 strata, transition-pattern frequencies, physiology concordance, MCS-unknown accounting, and note availability/mention counts. Stop any claim of retrospective endpoint validation if no stratum has an observed reference standard. A high-specificity *documentation subset* may be carried forward for prospective oversampling, but never labeled validated.

### Stage 2: prospective reference-standard pilot

At ICUs exporting synchronized pump-native traces, enroll consecutive qualifying first-stop episodes, retaining all restart and apparent non-restart episodes. Freeze a technical gap rule before opening outcomes; prespecify sensitivity to 1, 5, 10, 30, and 60-minute pump-off gaps. Capture channel/pump/drug/concentration, programmed and delivered rate, start/stop/pause/resume/alarms, bag/syringe and channel changes, EHR transaction fields, synchronized physiology, MCS device state, clock offsets, and timestamped clinician intent. Two blinded critical-care/pharmacy reviewers classify true resumed support delivery, uninterrupted transition, technical pause, transfer continuation, timestamp mismatch, no delivery, or indeterminate; adjudicate disagreements by a third reviewer.

Require at least 50 raw EHR restarts per observed arm unless a blinded pilot prevalence/precision calculation justifies more. Retain all non-restarts for sensitivity/NPV. The primary reference event requires both delivered stopped agent after a genuine pump-off interval and adjudicated hemodynamic-support intent; a pump-only secondary reference remains visible.

### Stage 3: decision gates

* **Supportive:** PPV lower confidence bound >=0.80 in each arm, absolute arm difference lower/upper interval contained below 0.10, indeterminate <10% per arm, stable across gap definitions and clock/export exclusions, and no concentrated site/workflow failure. This supports use of the EHR restart component as a measured endpoint in a new prospective study only; it does not establish causal order effects, safety, benefit, or MCS inactivity.
* **Adverse:** either PPV gate fails, arm asymmetry reaches 0.10, sensitivity is poor, or transition/transfer/clock errors materially dominate. Stop raw EHR restart as a validated endpoint; use pump-native delivery plus adjudicated intent in any later study. Do not reopen the failed MIMIC score or arm comparison.
* **Inconclusive:** reference indeterminacy >10%, insufficient arm-specific events, unsynchronized clocks, selective pump export loss, or incompatible workflows. Stop efficacy interpretation and improve capture or enlarge the pilot; never lower the PPV gate or convert D2 into truth.

## Claims that remain outside Harbor computation

Harbor can check interval reconstruction, transaction-field presence, deterministic strata, joins, time windows, counts, partial-identification algebra, and prospective confusion-matrix calculations. It cannot establish that an inputevent equals delivered drug, that a pump was off, that a bag change was uninterrupted, that a restart was rescue-intended, that MCS was absent, or that a future stop-order trial is ethical, safe, clinically beneficial, or causally interpretable. Those require synchronized prospective data, blinded expert adjudication, and a separate study.

## Bottom line

MIMIC supports a complete and useful negative feasibility result plus a transportable documentation-audit protocol. It does not support a credible retrospective validation subset or a nontrivial assumption-free PPV bound. The scientifically honest advance is to quantify the exact documentation concordance/discordance patterns, expose the unidentified latent endpoint, and stop retrospective truth claims while staging the smallest prospective reference-standard experiment capable of resolving it.
