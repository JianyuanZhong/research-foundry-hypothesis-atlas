## Targeted repair and substantive advance

## Hypothesis and estimand

Let `i` index the locked person-landmark rows. Exposure is `A_i=1` for the first exact `cplitemvalue` `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` in `[L,L+360]`, and `A_i=0` for `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`, with the parent’s contradictory-label rule retained. `D_i` is that row’s `cplitemoffset`; it is not replaced by `L`.

The primary estimand is the descriptive, hospital-held-out difference in the probability of a **persistent safety-preserving support reduction** in `(D+360,D+1800]`, conditional on a pre-D respiratory state and pre-D documentation opportunity. The primary prediction comparison is incremental held-out log loss of `baseline + opportunity + A` versus the nested `baseline + opportunity` model. This is an association and information estimand, not a treatment effect.

The falsifiable hypothesis is that `A=1` has a positive risk difference and improves both row-weighted and equal-hospital held-out predictive performance, while (a) not producing a comparable pre-D placebo trajectory, (b) not being explained by an opportunity-only model, and (c) remaining directionally present under the three pre-specified airway-recency strata. The exact thresholds below are locked before reading outcomes.

## Endpoint: deterministic persistent state, exits and missingness

### Fixed parser and value admissibility

Before outcome computation, freeze the following parser in a versioned JSON manifest. Normalize only Unicode whitespace, trim, lowercase, and collapse runs of whitespace; do not substring-match. The only accepted label-family aliases are the fixed, predeclared set below (the compiler must report observed raw labels mapped to each family and fail if an alias is absent rather than inventing one):

* `fio2`: `fio2`, `fi o2`, `fraction inspired oxygen`, `fraction inspired o2`;
* `peep`: `peep`, `peep/cpap`, `peep-cpap`, `peep cpap`;
* `pressure_support`: `pressure support`, `ps`;
* `set_rate`: `set respiratory rate`, `set rr`, `respiratory rate set`, `set rate`;
* `peak_pressure`: `peak inspiratory pressure`, `peak inspiratory pres`, `peak pressure` (retained only for ablation/negative control).

These aliases are not claimed to be clinically equivalent; they are an interface parser. Numeric parsing accepts a signed decimal/scientific number after removing only commas and a trailing `%`; values are invalid if nonnumeric, infinite, or outside fixed family bounds: FiO2 `(0,100]` (convert values `(0,1]` to percent only when the raw value has no percent sign and is numerically `<=1`); PEEP `(0,40]`; pressure support `(0,60]`; set rate `(0,80]`; peak pressure `(0,100]`. No unit conversion beyond this explicit FiO2 rule is allowed. Duplicate same-family observations at the same `respchartoffset` are collapsed by median after sorting by `(offset, respchartid)`; duplicate rows with the same ID and conflicting payload are retained in an audit and cause a parser error.

### Primary outcome

Define pre-D window `W0=(D-720,D]` and post-D window `W1=(D+360,D+1800]`; the six-hour gap prevents direct label-row adjacency. Require `unitdischargeoffset > D+1800` for an administratively complete follow-up window. For each support family `k ∈ {fio2, peep, pressure_support, set_rate}`, require at least two valid observations at distinct offsets separated by at least 30 minutes in both `W0` and `W1`. Let `m0k` and `m1k` be the medians. A family is a persistent reduction if `m0k-m1k ≥ τk`, with locked thresholds `τ_fio2=5` percentage points, `τ_peep=2`, `τ_pressure_support=2`, and `τ_set_rate=2` breaths/min, and if the post-window first-half and second-half medians each remain at least `τk` below `m0k` (each half must contain one valid observation; no interpolation).

The primary event `Y=1` requires at least one non-peak family to satisfy that persistent reduction **and** both safety signals to be observed at least twice in each window with separated offsets: post-minus-pre median `sao2` is no less than `-2` percentage points and post-minus-pre median `respiration` is no greater than `+4` breaths/min. This is a chart-derived, safety-preserving support trajectory; it is not extubation or liberation.

Endpoint status is four-valued and never silently dichotomized: `event` (`Y=1`), `none` (complete windows and all required safety/support observations but no qualifying family), `competing_exit` (death or discharge before or at `D+1800`, using `unitdischargestatus`/offset; report death and other discharge separately), and `truncated` (no competing exit but `unitdischargeoffset <= D+1800` or missing). A fifth `missing_observation` status applies when the administrative window is complete but the required respiratory/vital observations fail the two-observation rules. The primary risk is `event/(event+none)` among administratively complete, observation-complete rows; all statuses and arm/hospital counts are reported. A prespecified sensitivity treats `competing_exit` as a competing event in a cause-specific cumulative-incidence analysis by 1800 minutes; it is not reclassified as no event. No imputation of endpoint values is permitted.

Require independent respiratory-care corroboration only as a secondary endpoint, not the primary event, because it can itself be sparse and semantically uncertain. The corroboration indicator is a post-window `respiratoryCare` record with nonmissing `airwaytype` or a nonmissing ventilator interval field. Report the primary endpoint with and without this indicator as an availability-stratified sensitivity, without calling it device truth. Secondary outcomes include support-only persistence, each component, safety preservation, corroboration availability, observation density, and documentation lag.

## Predictors, leakage audit and held-out estimand

The baseline model uses only rows with timestamps `<=D`: pre-D medians/slopes for each support family and vital safety signal, last-valid time, counts, distinct measurement times, source-presence indicators, patient/hospital-independent age/sex/landmark indicators permitted by the parent recipe, and pre-D respiratory-care continuity. Opportunity features are pre-D counts, distinct labels, distinct offsets, source-family presence, median inter-record gaps, and entry-lag summaries. Values from `W1`, any care-plan rows other than the exposure action row, post-D treatment, and post-D airway fields are prohibited.

Before fitting, a machine-checkable leakage audit asserts `max(timestamp)` for every predictor column is `<=D`, records source table and row IDs, and checks no predictor artifact row joins to a post-D timestamp. The exposure is masked during baseline construction. A negative-control opportunity-only model contains counts/offsets/presence/lag but no respiratory values and no label semantics. The label model adds only the binary exposure to the baseline-plus-opportunity model.

Use leave-one-hospital-out folds: each fold holds out one hospital, training on all other hospitals; no hospital is split across train/test. Hospital folds are deterministic by ascending numeric/string `hospitalid`, and every row in a stay remains in one fold. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss, Brier score, calibration slope/intercept, AUROC only when defined, and risk differences. For the descriptive association, fit a prespecified pooled logistic model with landmark fixed effects and training-fold-standardized continuous predictors; do not fit hospital-specific effects that cannot transport to the held-out site. A sensitivity uses only hospitals with at least one row in each arm and an observation-complete row in each arm; the primary fold manifest is not outcome-selected.

Uncertainty is a stay-cluster bootstrap: resample distinct `patientunitstayid` with replacement within each training/evaluation construction, moving all landmarks together; repeat 2,000 times with seed `20260912`, percentile 95% intervals, and retain the fold-level estimates. For sparse hospital folds, report the estimate and interval as undefined rather than use a row bootstrap or a normal approximation. A separate 1,000-permutation null shuffles exposure within hospital-by-landmark strata among locked rows, preserving arm counts, while rebuilding fold predictions with the same seed and all parser artifacts unchanged.

## Falsification and gates

1. Apply the exact endpoint to the pre-D placebo pair `(D-1440,D-720]` versus `(D-720,D]`; a comparable label contrast is adverse to a newly emerging post-label trajectory.
2. Keep the post window fixed but shift the exposure clock to `D-720`; a comparable increment indicates lead-time/pre-existing trajectory or leakage.
3. Repeat after removing each support family and each safety signal; dependence on peak pressure, one component, one source family, or one hospital is not supportive.
4. Compare opportunity-only and value-based models; if documentation opportunity predicts as well as the label model, the incremental-state claim is adverse.
5. Repeat using airway-recency strata defined only by pre-L invasive evidence within 24, 12, and 6 hours. These are bounded sensitivities, not new cohorts.
6. The observed held-out label increment must exceed the 97.5th percentile of the frozen within-stratum permutation increments. This is a falsification check, not a causal test.

Call the result **supportive** only if endpoint support reaches at least five hospitals with at least ten observation-complete rows per arm, no hospital supplies more than 40% of either arm, the evaluation-minus-no-trial risk difference is positive with directions agreeing in at least 4/5 estimable hospitals, the label model improves both micro and macro log loss over baseline-plus-opportunity by at least 0.01 with a favorable stay-cluster interval, there is no comparable placebo/lead-time signal or component reversal, and at least 10% of both arms have independent corroboration. These gates do not establish clinical readiness.

Call it **adverse** if the endpoint is adequately supported but the increment is no better than opportunity-only, reverses across held-out hospitals, is comparable to placebo/permutation, or disappears in a component/source sensitivity. Call it **inconclusive** if support, arm overlap, corroboration, or uncertainty is too sparse, endpoint missingness/competing exits differ so strongly that the prespecified analyses cannot separate state from ascertainment, or bounded airway-recency results disagree. No relaxed threshold or outcome-guided relabelling is allowed.

## Evidence limits

The available data support only a reproducible association between an exact structured care-plan value and this explicitly defined chart-derived trajectory, if the computation passes all lineage checks. They do not establish clinician intent, daily extubation evaluation, SBT, extubation, reintubation, delivered device settings, causality, appropriateness, or patient benefit. Narrative note sections are removed; no raw waveforms, device logs, local interface dictionary, direct SBT/extubation/reintubation adjudication, or post-ICU follow-up is available. Stronger bedside claims require blinded clinician adjudication, site workflow/interface metadata, device-linked data, and prospective or external multicenter validation.

The three demonstrations in `references/research-ambition/README.md` motivate longitudinal, reproducible and uncertainty-aware design but do not supply eICU bedside evidence; this proposal does not claim to have inspected unavailable main-paper text or supplementary methods. No raw clinical rows are published or sent to public search. No result is claimed before execution.
