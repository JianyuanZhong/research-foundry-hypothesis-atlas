## Unresolved question and advance

The strongest available local evidence is structural: the snapshot contains two instances of eight numeric biomarker fields, assessment dates, age/sex, 259 position-matched inpatient diagnosis-code/date slots, and two death-date fields. The available metadata does not establish analyte names or units for the eight fields, specimen dates, disease onset, diagnosis validity, individual observation end, or complete five-year ascertainment.

The parent asks whether the preceding panel improves a constrained review queue for a broad recorded cardiorenal/metabolic endpoint. This child makes the clinical question more specific: after a current assessment, does retaining the immediately preceding panel identify people who are more likely to receive a first later recorded inpatient heart-failure diagnosis, beyond current values and repeat-assessment spacing? The advance is pathway-specific queue concentration, which can distinguish a heart-failure signal from a gain caused only by heterogeneous composite-event case mix. It remains a prognostic association with a recorded code, not a causal test of repeat testing, heart-failure biology, referral, treatment, or patient benefit.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis:

> Among eligible participants without a qualifying prior recorded heart-failure code at the current landmark who fall in a prespecified current-only middle-risk band, adding the prior panel to current values, age, sex, calendar year, and repeat spacing increases held-out capture and positive yield for a first later qualifying recorded inpatient heart-failure code at a fixed 10% review capacity, without material calibration harm; the paired increment has the same direction when models fitted through 2012 are applied without refitting to 2013.

A null or adverse result would support current values plus spacing for this recorded-heart-failure review task and would weaken the rationale for preserving the prior panel for this purpose. A supportive result would justify external validation and prospective impact research. Neither result establishes true heart-failure incidence, a screening threshold, the value of ordering repeat biomarkers, or improved outcomes.

## Method comparison, compute, and deliverable

The simple M1a/M1b comparison is primary because it preserves an interpretable question: does a prior panel add information beyond current measurements and repeat-selection/spacing? The matched M2/M2a alternative is scientifically substantive, not a score-chasing replacement: it can reveal threshold effects, biomarker discordance, and interactions with age, sex, calendar year, and spacing that additive terms lose. It uses the same participants, endpoint, folds, band, queue capacity, and uncertainty. Mechanistic organ scores are deferred because biomarker identities, units, and specimen timing are not locally certified. A transformer disease sequence is deferred because this design has sparse paired assessments, not a certified dated disease trajectory; it remains a possible later adaptation after sequence/event semantics are established.
