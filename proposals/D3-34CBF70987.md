# Provisional repair: donor-conditioned macrophage exposure in E-MTAB-8873

## Proposed relationship

Among type B synovial cells from female rheumatoid arthritis donors, exposure to MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages is hypothesized to induce a reproducible condition-specific transcriptional response relative to both FLS-only and MerTK/CD206-negative macrophage controls. The response is expected to be condition-specific rather than explained solely by donor identity, macrophage composition, treatment, batch, or targeted-panel measurement. This is an observational expression hypothesis, not a causal mechanism or clinical prediction.

## Experiment and falsification

Fit the prespecified donor-aware condition contrasts on the 33 records and 445 measured features, using log(count + small offset) and uncertainty at the contributing-donor/context level. Primary comparisons are positive macrophage versus FLS-only and positive versus negative macrophage, restricted to records with verified joins and defensible donor representation. Mixed macrophage records are secondary composition checks. LPS and inhibitor records are mechanistic context, not independent causal replicates. Use condition-preserving donor/context permutations and report effect estimates, intervals, donor counts, and negative-control behavior. A simple paired/stratified contrast is the baseline; a hierarchical or regularized feature model is the substantive alternative, retained because it can reveal coordinated pathway structure but cannot establish causality.

Supportive evidence would be a reproducible positive-versus-control signature with donor-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable signal in the negative-control contrast. Adverse evidence would be no separation, reversal, or failure under donor/condition permutations. Inconclusive evidence includes unresolved donor mapping, insufficient independent donor contexts, low detectability, or uncertainty too large to distinguish communication from composition/technical artifacts. No result is claimed here.

## Missing evidence and limits

The available targeted matrix does not measure unmeasured genes, and cell-level rows are not independent biological replicates. The source metadata does not provide a complete independent donor map for every mixed record, and the known SDRF conflict means donor attribution must be explicitly adjudicated or sensitivity-tested. Treatment, macrophage ratio/composition, batch, and panel/cell-count effects remain plausible rivals. No direct ligand-receptor action, causal mechanism, clinical phenotype, treatment benefit, or external validation is available.
