## Hypothesis to test

Patients with persistently elevated lactate but improving perfusion differ in risk and response to fluid administration from those with elevated lactate and worsening perfusion.

## Study population and main data

Patients with repeatedly elevated lactate; urine output, vasopressors, and liver function.

## Suggested approach

First validate the combined phenotypes, then investigate differences in the effects of subsequent fluid administration.

## Scientific significance

Avoid treating hyperlactatemia with different underlying causes as a single category.

## Main limitations

These measures cannot diagnose microcirculatory dysfunction; repeated measurements are selectively obtained.
