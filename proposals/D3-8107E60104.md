# MerTK state versus composition: a source-separated macrophage–FLS test

Episode 5 child of [prior hypothesis]. This is a planned Harbor experiment. The source audit was executed for availability and schema only; no biological fit, state score, or wet-lab experiment was executed.

## Scientific opening and falsifiable hypothesis

The important unresolved question is not whether RA contains macrophages associated with remission or fibroblasts associated with inflammation. The question is whether an apparent MerTK-conditioned FLS response is a within-macrophage-state signal, a difference in macrophage RNA contribution/cell mixture, or a generic activation/assay effect. That distinction changes whether a target-engagement experiment is warranted.

[K1] shows that a different macrophage program, HBEGF+, can promote an EGFR-dependent fibroblast-invasiveness phenotype. [K2] supports a clinically relevant MerTK-positive remission-associated macrophage state and an in-vitro fibroblast repair observation. [K3] shows that macrophage and fibroblast states can be co-structured by RA tissue context. Together they support biological plausibility and clinical importance, but none identifies a MerTK-specific causal signal in the configured targeted coculture matrix.

Hypothesis: a fixed MerTK-associated macrophage-state direction, estimated without FLS outcomes in macrophage-only E-MTAB-8322 and transferred only through genes measured in E-MTAB-8873, explains FLS inflammatory output beyond a macrophage RNA-contribution proxy. In E-MTAB-8873, the state interpretation is supported only if the fixed score remains associated with the prespecified FLS outcome after composition sensitivity, mixed-ratio checks, and donor/run dependence are accounted for, and if the parent Mpos-by-inhibitor interaction is not explained by a change in the composition proxy. If the score–outcome association is removed by the composition proxy or follows the known mixed ratios, the within-cell-state interpretation is falsified for this assay.

This is a direction-only cross-context hypothesis, not a claim that expression units, cell proportions, donor IDs, target engagement, or causal effects transfer between studies.

## Supported evidence, unresolved claim, and clinical advance

Supported by inspected evidence:

- RA macrophage programs can influence fibroblast behavior, including destructive invasiveness [K1].
- MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets are associated with remission biology and can induce a fibroblast repair response in a separate in-vitro context [K2].
- Macrophage and fibroblast states can co-occur in clinically distinct RA synovial contexts [K3].
- The executed source audit found a common, measured marker set in both configured sources: E-MTAB-8322 has Ensembl rows with gene symbols in its verified gene map, while E-MTAB-8873 has targeted gene/transcript rows for those markers.

Untested claim:

- The same MerTK-associated direction can be measured in macrophage-only E-MTAB-8322 and used as a fixed, rank/standardized proxy in E-MTAB-8873 to distinguish within-macrophage state from macrophage contribution/composition.

A supportive result would justify a balanced, orthogonally engaged MerTK perturbation study. An adverse result would prevent treating the current Mpos label or inhibitor contrast as evidence of a within-cell MerTK mechanism and redirect work toward mixture, generic activation, or assay reconstruction. Either result is clinically useful because it changes whether a target-specific resolution-restoring program should proceed.

This experiment still cannot establish MerTK binding or target engagement, inhibitor selectivity, direct ligand/receptor signaling, secretion, FLS invasion or matrix damage, tissue localization, flare prediction, treatment response, or patient benefit.

## Feasibility decision: what transfers and what does not

The transfer is valid only as a fixed marker direction over the measured intersection.

A prespecified score uses these exact gene symbols:

- positive MerTK-associated/resolution-side set P = {MERTK, CD163, MRC1, FOLR2, C1QA, C1QB, APOE}
- negative inflammatory/activation-side set N = {SPP1, TNF, IL1B}

The choice is frozen from the inspected MerTK remission/activation evidence and the assay’s verified available features; no E-MTAB-8873 FLS outcome is used to choose genes or weights. The score is S = mean(z_g for g in P) - mean(z_g for g in N), with equal weights. z_g is log1p library-size-normalized expression, standardized within the source and analysis unit. In E-MTAB-8322, S is computed per macrophage cell from the raw matrix and summarized per sample by the median and the prespecified upper-quartile fraction. In E-MTAB-8873, the same signed gene list is applied to the ten measured target rows after sample-level normalization, and is interpreted as a direction/rank proxy only. No absolute score calibration or expression-unit equivalence is claimed.

This is feasible because the executed audit verified all ten symbols in E-MTAB-8322 through the shared SA220.genes.tsv feature map, including ENSG00000153208 MERTK, ENSG00000177575 CD163, ENSG00000260314 MRC1, ENSG00000165457 FOLR2, ENSG00000173372 C1QA, ENSG00000173369 C1QB, ENSG00000130203 APOE, ENSG00000118785 SPP1, ENSG00000232810 TNF, and ENSG00000125538 IL1B. E-MTAB-8873 contains the corresponding exact rows MERTK|NM_006343.2|Reference_end, CD163|NM_004244.5|PolyA_1, MRC1|NM_002438.3|Reference_end, FOLR2|NM_000803.4|Reference_end, C1QA|NM_015991.2|Reference_end, C1QB|NM_000491.3|Reference_end, APOE|NM_000041.3|Reference_end, SPP1|NM_000582.2|Reference_end, TNF|NM_000594.3|Reference_end, and IL1B|NM_000576.2|Reference_end.

What does not transfer: a sample-level E-MTAB-8873 score cannot prove the number of macrophages, the fraction of Mpos cells, or a per-cell MerTK state. E-MTAB-8322 is macrophage-only and cross-sectional, not a whole-synovium composition reference. E-MTAB-8873 is a targeted, recorded FLS/coculture pseudobulk endpoint; its macrophage marker signal can be a mixture/contamination proxy, but it is not validated cell abundance. There is no cross-source donor join. The state score is therefore an assay-bounded proxy and a way to falsify an overstrong within-cell interpretation, not an independent validation cohort.

## Smallest Harbor experiment

### Stage 1: binding, score reproducibility, and QC

1. Verify one-to-one matrix/design joins, exact feature rows, shared gene-map row order, library size, detected-feature count, and cells per sample. Record every excluded record, including SA149/SA149P relatedness and the R1 donor conflict.
2. From the 27 raw E-MTAB-8322 sample matrices, normalize per cell, calculate S, and retain cell-level S, per-sample median S, upper-quartile fraction, and the number of cells. Use leave-one-donor-out summaries across the 25 verified donors; SA149/SA149P are sensitivity only. Do not use condition labels to fit weights.
3. Freeze the ten-gene set and equal weights. In E-MTAB-8873, calculate S and the separate unnormalized macrophage-marker fraction A from the measured rows. A is a composition/RNA-contribution proxy, not a validated cell fraction. Report the correlation and collinearity between S and A before any outcome model.
4. Use the terminal endpoint only. Do not impute a culture time, pretreatment duration, washout, or transition.

### Stage 2: state versus composition contrasts

For each E-MTAB-8873 sample, calculate the fixed eight-gene FLS outcome Y, S, A, library size, detected-feature count, cells, run, FLS donor component, macrophage contributing-donor string, and Mpos/Mneg/mixed-ratio/treatment labels.

Primary descriptive estimands:

- Within the three usable Mpos blocks, the inhibitor delta in Y and S, with donor/mixture-block bootstrap or a weakly informative interval; no ordinary independent-donor t-test.
- The single R1 interaction I = delta_Y(Mpos) - delta_Y(Mneg), reported as one bounded block-level contrast.
- A composition interpolation residual for each mixed-ratio sample: observed Y minus the prespecified 1:3 or 3:1 convex prediction from the closest pure Mpos/Mneg context, using the same run and FLS donor where the design permits. Report it as a stress test, not an independent validation.
- The residual state association of Y with S after adjusting for A, FLS-only context, run, library/QC variables, and the recorded contributing-donor structure. With three usable Mpos blocks and one Mneg block, this is a sensitivity description; it cannot supply a credible asymptotic p-value.

The state interpretation is weakened if S and A are nearly collinear, if mixed-ratio Y follows the linear composition prediction, if inhibitor shifts A without a stable state contrast, or if the Mpos interaction depends on one block or the donor-conflict record.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Transparent baseline: a fixed score plus donor-aware paired contrasts. Normalize each sample once with a prespecified library-size method; use raw counts for a negative-binomial sensitivity. Estimate Y gene-by-gene and as the fixed eight-gene mean; estimate S and A without supervised fitting to Y. Summarize Mpos deltas, the single Mneg interaction, mixed-ratio interpolation residuals, score/abundance collinearity, and leave-one-run/block-out sensitivity. This baseline preserves the estimand and makes composition adjustment inspectable.

Substantive alternative: a constrained reference-projection/latent-mixture model on the same 33 x 445 E-MTAB-8873 matrix. Use E-MTAB-8322 single-cell macrophage profiles to form two frozen macrophage endmembers from the upper and lower S quartiles, and E-MTAB-8873 FLS-only rows as the measured FLS reference. On the intersection of measured features only, fit a nonnegative, library-scaled reconstruction that estimates (i) macrophage RNA contribution and (ii) a continuous macrophage-state coordinate between the two endmembers. Fit the endmembers without E-MTAB-8873 outcomes; do not use donor labels across sources. The model target is reconstruction of measured count vectors, not prediction of a clinical label.

Use leave-one-run-out stress tests across R1, R2, and R5 and leave-one-mixed-ratio-out checks. Evaluate held-out reconstruction error, recovery of the known pure-versus-mixed ratio ordering, stability of the state coordinate, agreement with the fixed-score direction, and whether the Y association persists after the model’s contribution estimate. Bootstrap only over recorded contributing-donor blocks and technical rows nested within blocks; do not treat cells, genes, or wells as independent donors. Compare the alternative with the baseline on the same Y, S, A, I, mixed-ratio residual, and uncertainty outputs.

The alternative can reveal whether a nominal high score is mostly a larger macrophage RNA component or a change in the macrophage state coordinate; the fixed score loses that separation. It is not automatically preferred. It is deferred if the measured panel cannot reconstruct the FLS-only controls, pure/mixed controls do not order as recorded, endmembers are unstable under donor leave-out, or the state and contribution coordinates are non-identifiable. A ligand/receptor or mediation model is not useful here because direct ligand/protein, secretion, target engagement, and functional FLS endpoints are absent.

Compute plan: CPU only. The primary prepared matrix is 33 x 445; raw 8322 matrices are larger but sparse and can be streamed per sample. A 16-CPU, 32–64 GiB job should fit normalization, score calculation, constrained projections, and block bootstrap within hours; this is a reasoned estimate, not a measured full fit. No GPU is required. The future solver planning envelope remains separate (up to 16 CPUs, 8 GPUs, 262144 MiB, 28800 seconds). The discovery audit used two CPU jobs totalling about two seconds of measured runtime and did not execute the scientific fit.

Actual scientific deliverable: (1) a frozen gene-to-feature map and QC/exclusion table; (2) E-MTAB-8322 cell/sample state reproducibility summaries; (3) E-MTAB-8873 S, A, Y, paired deltas, single-block interaction, mixed-ratio residuals and block-aware uncertainty; (4) the fitted reference-projection model or a documented stability deferral; and (5) a classification tied to these outputs. Readiness and source inspection alone do not complete the experiment.

## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive pattern:

- The ten-gene score is reproducible in E-MTAB-8322 across held-out verified donors, with directionally coherent cell/sample summaries rather than a single sample artifact.
- In E-MTAB-8873, the Mpos inhibitor pattern and Y association remain after A/QC sensitivity, do not simply track the mixed-ratio interpolation, and are concordant between the fixed score and a stable reference-projection model.
- The single Mneg interaction is smaller than the Mpos interaction, while LPS controls do not reproduce the same pattern.

This supports a MerTK-associated, composition-resistant hypothesis for the measured endpoint and prioritizes orthogonal engagement/functional testing. It does not prove within-cell causality, target selectivity, or clinical benefit.

Adverse pattern:

- The score is unstable across E-MTAB-8322 donor-held-out summaries; S and A are near-collinear in E-MTAB-8873; mixed-ratio responses interpolate as composition predicts; Y disappears after A adjustment; the inhibitor shifts A rather than the state coordinate; the Mneg response is comparable; or the signal is LPS-like, QC-dependent, or single-block.
- The latent alternative attributes the signal to contribution rather than state or fails to reconstruct known controls.

This falsifies the within-cell-state interpretation for this assay and supports the composition/generic/off-target rival. It does not prove MerTK is irrelevant in RA.

Inconclusive pattern:

- A required gene-map or matrix/design join fails; marker rows are sparse or absent; source scaling makes S direction non-reproducible; state and contribution cannot be separated; R1 donor metadata cannot be resolved; the mixed-ratio controls lack a comparable pure context; or all uncertainty is dominated by one block.
- The score can be calculated but there is no independent evidence that an E-MTAB-8873 macrophage marker signal represents macrophage material rather than FLS response or technical carryover.

This narrows the claim to feasibility and identifies the missing evidence. It does not justify a favorable conclusion or a biological null.

Computationally checkable claims are feature availability, exact joins, score definitions, source-separated donor handling, exclusions, normalized values, paired contrasts, composition residuals, model reconstruction, held-out stress tests, uncertainty, and whether interpretation follows the prespecified rules. Clinical and mechanistic adjudication require expert review or another study: actual macrophage cell fractions, MerTK surface abundance/occupancy/phosphorylation, compound identity/selectivity, secreted mediators, viability, FLS invasion/matrix function, tissue localization, longitudinal flare, and patient response.

## Alternatives not chosen and revisit evidence

- Parent-only Mpos inhibitor contrasts are retained as the primary perturbational context but are insufficient for state-versus-composition identification because the Mneg inhibitor contrast is a single R1 block.
- A simple unadjusted Mpos-versus-Mneg expression comparison was not selected because it confounds state with contributing RNA and FLS donor mixture.
- A supervised predictive model was deferred: 33 targeted endpoint samples, one Mneg inhibitor block, and no clinical outcome make predictive improvement scientifically secondary and high risk for leakage.
- A ligand/receptor mediation model was deferred because HBEGF, EGFR, direct ligand/protein, secretion, and target-engagement rows are not in the bound E-MTAB-8873 panel.
- Cross-source donor matching was rejected because E-MTAB-8322 donor IDs are not the same namespace as E-MTAB-8873 contributing-donor strings.
- The learned/latent alternative should be revisited only if its held-out reconstruction and mixed-ratio ordering are stable and a verified cell-composition or orthogonal macrophage reference is available. A direct MerTK engagement assay, balanced Mpos/Mneg donors, recorded exposure/washout, and functional FLS readouts would justify a new orthogonal perturbation child.
