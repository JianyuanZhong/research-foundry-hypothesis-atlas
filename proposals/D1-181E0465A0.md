# Retrospective fixed-budget ranking potential for HCC documentary-M2 signals

## Targeted child and clinical question

This is a targeted two-parent child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`.

The residual defect is estimand/workflow mismatch. The HCC snapshot contains eventual stored examination text and completed resections, but it does not observe referral, report authored/final/release/view time, capacity, offered slots, queue entry, or review completion. Therefore a release curve, pseudo-arrival stream, utilization, or service-yield label would add an unsupported workflow story. This proposal removes that machinery from the primary experiment.

The clinical question is:

> Among retrospectively identified adult, recorded-treatment-naive HCC patients who undergo a first eligible resection, does a locked model using interpretable CT/MRI report semantics rank more documentary-M2 cases within a fixed number of ranking positions than the same fitted model with semantics masked and than an independently fitted nonsemantic baseline, when evaluated in two chronologically locked future years?

The clinically useful but bounded advance is a source-identifiable answer to whether report semantics add robust *ranking potential* for a scarce downstream review list. It can inform whether a future silent-mode review-list study is worth doing. It is not a measure of referral, report access, capacity, workload, workflow efficiency, clinical action, treatment, or patient benefit.

## Evidence boundary

Before this experiment, the strongest supported claims are data and literature claims, not the hypothesis:

- The HCC snapshot ([source checksum]) has linked encounters, procedure start/end fields, examination accession-like identifiers and start times, eventual stored examination text, untimed pathology text, and dated medication/order fields.
- It supports a retrospective completed-resection cohort and an explicitly time-ordered evaluation index. It does not identify the set of patients considered by a real service, who was referred or excluded, who did not complete resection, when a report was released or viewed, or how many reviewers/slots were available.
- Neves and Soares, *Cancers* 2026;18:2028 (DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028; PMCID PMC13360217; acquired source [source checksum]) reviewed 36 early-recurrence HCC AI studies, most retrospective, with few external validations and inconsistent calibration/clinical-utility reporting. This supports the need for temporal separation, calibration, and independent validation; it does not validate this endpoint or model.
- Fan et al., *Journal of Gastrointestinal Oncology* 2026 (DOI 10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944; acquired source [source checksum]) reported M0/M1/M2 counts in 621 resected HCC patients and modest LDH discrimination (AUC 0.610), while cautioning against changing surgical extent from LDH alone and requesting external/prospective validation. This motivates incremental multimodal ranking research but does not establish report-semantic value here.
- The research-ambition README and the available natural-history and Bayesian demonstrations were inspected for their emphasis on temporal structure and selection/uncertainty. For the Cell cancer demonstration, only metadata and the supplement are available; the main article and full STAR Methods were not read and are not evidence.

The unresolved claim is narrower: under a frozen, retrospective fixed-K ranking operation on a completed-resection index, eventual stored CT/MRI semantics improve documentary-M2 capture per ranking position. Signal, association, or a favorable pathology result alone cannot establish that claim.

## Hypothesis and estimand

Let (s) denote one complete coherent source state; (y\in\{2020,2021\}) a test year; (d\in\{12,24,48,72\}) hours the locked operative decision buffer; (h) a report-access uncertainty cell; and (M\in\{S,S0,B\}).

For every state, construct the completed-resection index stream without using pathology grade, report-body semantics, or test outcomes. Let (N_a(s)) be its size in development/tuning year (a\in\{2015,\ldots,2019\}). Freeze, before any test computation,

[
N_{ref}=\min_{s,a=2015\ldots2019}N_a(s),\qquad K_q=\lfloor qN_{ref}\rfloor,quad q\in\{0.05,0.10,0.20\}.
]

The strict confirmatory feasibility rule is (N_{ref}\ge500), (K_q\ge50), every coherent 2020/2021 state has at least (K_q) index members, and the outcome/adjudication gates below pass. K is an absolute number frozen from 2015-2019 only; it never uses test-year N, outcome, access, policy, or bootstrap results.

For a locked policy score (g_M(i;h)), rank all eligible members in a test-year state by descending score and then one frozen patient hash and raw episode backpointer. Let (R_K(M;s,y,d,h)) be the first (K_q) members in that ranking. The fixed-budget ranking-potential estimand is

[
T_M^{rank}=100,K_q^{-1}\sum_{i\in R_K(M)}Y_i,
]

documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed ranking positions. It is defined even though no real slot or service existed. The primary contrasts are

[
D_K=T_S^{rank}-T_{S0}^{rank},qquad C_K=T_S^{rank}-T_B^{rank}.
]

(D_K) is same-fit semantic attribution: S and S0 use identical fitted preprocessing, coefficients, calibration, threshold-independent score construction, and state rules; S0 masks semantic inputs and is not refit. (C_K) compares S to an independently fitted nonsemantic B. Neither is causal, a patient-level treatment effect, or a common-opportunity effect.

This operation is intentionally retrospective and non-deployable as written because it ranks the complete source-defined test-year index. Its estimand is identifiable from the source conditional on the completed-case index and stated access state; its limitation is precisely that it says nothing about whether a real service could have observed or acted on all members.

### Exact decomposition safeguard

All policies select exactly (K_q), so (U_M=1), (P_M=K_q^{-1}\sum_{i\in R_K(M)}Y_i), and (T_M^{rank}=100U_MP_M). Retain the parent’s symmetric product decomposition for audit:

[
V_U(S,X)=50(U_S-U_X)(P_S+P_X)=0,
]
[
V_P(S,X)=50(P_S-P_X)(U_S+U_X)=T_S^{rank}-T_X^{rank},
]

for (X\in\{S0,B\}). Thus every ranking-potential contrast is exactly a fixed-budget composition/ranking-set difference; there is no utilization contribution to interpret. This is an algebraic decomposition of two policy-specific ranking sets, not selection causality, mediation, equal workload, or a common online risk set. Report it because it prevents a favorable difference from being misdescribed as service capacity performance. Undefined or non-K ranking sets fail the cell rather than receiving an imputed utilization or prevalence.

## Examination unit and hypothetical report access

A nonblank examination source unit is formed before filtering, parsing, modality labeling, or masking as (patient master index, encounter number, examination number); retain every component row. Blank examination number rows remain in acquisition audits but their text is identity-unknown and masked. Conflicting modalities, clocks differing by >24 hours, or identity ambiguity generate coupled states containing both indivisible-accession and deterministic normalized (examination, start time calendar-date) partitions with raw backpointers.

A unit is eligible if its component clocks can place it in [(t_{dec}-90d,t_{dec})); the conservative deterministic state uses the maximum component start. Select at most the latest eligible CT and latest eligible MRI unit, with frozen raw ordinal/hash tie-breaks. Two blinded abdominal radiologists map examination to CT, MRI, non-target, or unresolved. Concatenate examination findings and examination diagnosis in frozen raw order. Visibility always exposes a complete source unit, never selected phrases or rows.

The source has no authored, final, release, view, or immutable-version field. Therefore h is not observed preoperative access. Evaluate the prespecified 0.05-grid access cells:

- (h_C): fraction of C-pattern members whose eventual CT unit is hypothetically exposed;
- (h_M): analogous MRI fraction;
- (h_A): at least one modality exposed among CM-pattern members;
- (h_B\le h_A): both modalities exposed among CM members.

O/C/M/CM members remain in every denominator. The access cell is a sensitivity/partial-identification state, not a report-release probability. H0 fully masks semantic fields; H1(h) exposes eventual stored text under an explicit hypothetical guarantee; S uses H1(h), while S0 masks semantics inside the same fitted S. The null boundary must make S and S0 bitwise identical. Do not call any h cell observed availability.

## Features, baselines, and locked analysis

S contains only prespecified interpretable semantic flags from the selected whole-unit text: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites, each present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned. B contains age, sex, CT/MRI/other acquisition indicators, eligible source-unit count, acquisition-to-decision recency, component count, report-identity ambiguity, and development-frequency machine-model category. B excludes report text, pathology, diagnoses, labs, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff facts. S adds the semantic flags to B’s nonsemantic feature set. Labs, diagnoses, clinical documents, vitals, and transfers have zero predictor lineage.

Fit S and B independently in 2015-2018 with patient-wise worst-state elastic-net logistic loss; retain preprocessing, coefficient, calibration, and state alternatives. Use 2019 only for locked calibration and a prespecified threshold-independent ranking-score lock. If a threshold is needed for a secondary binary list, choose one per policy/q from {0,0.005,...,1} in 2019 by documentary-M2 capture subject to a *reporting-only* fixed-K list convention; no threshold or test result may alter primary ranking. S0 inherits every S choice and masks only semantic fields; it is never refit. Calibration must be fit without test rows and must not use pathology rows that are in the future relative to the selected decision clock.

Primary baselines are S0 and independent B. Secondary diagnostics are deterministic first-come index-first K, seeded random K, age/sex-only, a nonsemantic acquisition-only B, and an outcome-aware retrospective top-K oracle. The oracle is an unattainable upper bound and cannot be called a comparator for deployment. No baseline is a real service or common-opportunity control.

## Documentary outcome and pathology uncertainty

For every exact-deduplicated pathology row on the selected encounter, compose pathology, examination findings, and examination diagnosis. A frozen position-aware parser sets Y=1 only for anchored selected M2 and Y=0 only for anchored M0/M1. Boilerplate, unanchored mention, missing/uncertain grade, M0/M1/M2 conflict, uncertain linkage, or inadequate documented sampling leaves Y in {0,1}; it is never silently discarded.

Two Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors adjudicate every test unknown/conflict and parsed M2; a third resolves disagreement. Report both the adjudicated envelope and the unresolved-outcome envelope, retaining state-wise extrema rather than combining favorable table/pathology choices. The endpoint is documentary M2 from all linked pathology rows; it is not biological M2, specimen-confirmed MVI, clinical diagnosis, or a causal effect. Pathology has no time, accession/specimen, slide, block, or sampling field, so clinical experts cannot reconstruct those absent facts.

## Inference, falsification, and strict result zones

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits. Each replicate rebuilds coherent states, development fits, 2019 calibration/score locks, report-access states, ranking sets, outcomes, and contrasts. K, semantic dictionary, access grid, margins, and result rules remain frozen. Preserve complete state-wise extrema within each replicate.

Construct one max-centered simultaneous 97.5% confidence family over D_K, C_K, their component terms, both test years, q values, all four clock buffers, all access cells, identity states, and prespecified null checks. Every reported row must include estimate, interval, state extrema, K, selected M2 counts, source-state count, outcome envelope, and raw result locator. No access cell/year/clock state is omitted because it looks unfavorable.

Run 1,000 development semantic-block permutations, stratified by year, modality pattern, source-unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and formatting family, with full refits. A semantic contrast must clear the predeclared simultaneous one-sided 0.025 null envelope. Entire semantic masking must produce bitwise S/S0 equality. Required gates include:

- N_ref >=500; every claimed q has K>=50; every coherent test state has K members;
- at least 50 adjudicated M2 events per claimed year envelope; anchored grade >=85%; all test unknowns adjudicated;
- parser kappa >=0.90, M2 PPV >=0.98 and sensitivity >=0.95;
- modality agreement >=0.95, unresolved modality <=1%; each claimed O/C/M/CM pattern >=100 members and 20 M2;
- semantic PPV/sensitivity >=0.85; no required-feature/pattern shift >15 percentage points from 2019;
- all-report/no-report calibration slope 0.80-1.20 and intercept -0.10 to 0.10;
- exact joins, coupled-state/backpointer completeness, patient quarantine, future-row invariance, optimizer/brute-force equality on N<=12 fixtures, zero forbidden predictor lineage, and <=2.5% failed bootstrap replicates.

Supportive H1-ranking requires every gate, a nonempty fully enumerated access set, and simultaneous lower limits >5 for both D_K and C_K in every claimed q, year, clock buffer, coherent state, and claimed h. The strict margin is additional documentary-M2 cases per 100 fixed ranking positions; equality at 5 is not supportive. If a report-access frontier is reported, it is the coordinatewise-minimal predeclared set of h cells satisfying this rule; it is not release or workflow performance.

Adverse requires otherwise-valid computation with a simultaneous upper limit <=5 for either universal primary contrast in a required scope, or an empty access set because all fully enumerated cells fail that bound. An upper limit <0 supports a bounded finding of lower ranking potential for S in that scope. A favorable oracle but adverse S says the ranking model did not approach the retrospective ceiling; a favorable S0/B result but adverse D_K says semantics added no robust incremental ranking potential. A positive arithmetic composition term is not a clinical selection effect.

Inconclusive includes intervals crossing 5, incompatible years/q/buffers/access states, failed adjudication/identity/calibration/permutation/temporal gates, missing source-state backpointers, undefined/non-K ranking sets, insufficient events, or absent conclusion-to-result linkage. Gate failure is not evidence against a biological association. Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive status must be reported separately for total ranking potential and decomposition attribution.

## Clinical limits and verifier contract

This experiment can computationally verify source hashes/headers, exact joins and filters, patient quarantine, operative-clock/state coupling, accession-first whole-unit construction, ranking-set size and hash ties, temporal fit boundaries, no future-row lineage, S/S0 bitwise null identity, independent B fit, bootstrap/permutation arithmetic, decomposition identity, result-zone linkage, and provenance locators.

It cannot establish true referral identity, report authorship/release/view/version, actual reviewer capacity or workload, pathology specimen linkage/sampling, biological M2, acceptable clinical tradeoffs, actions, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, fairness, or transportability. Those require expert adjudication, a representative timestamped preoperative roster including noncompleters/non-HCC cases, report-version and access logs, specimen-linked pathology, and prospective silent-mode/impact evaluation. Treatment and benefit are strictly outside this study.

Required fixtures include:

1. Fixed-K arithmetic: K=100, S has 40 documentary M2 and S0 27; D_K=13 captures/100 positions. The same result is obtained from (U_S=U_{S0}=1), (V_U=0), (V_P=13).
2. Equal ranking sets: S and X share all K members; D_K=0 and both decomposition terms are zero.
3. Swap symmetry: swapping S and X negates D_K and V_P exactly.
4. Non-K/undefined trap: fewer than K members or unresolved Y must not be filled with negatives; the cell is inconclusive or reported as an outcome envelope.
5. S0 null: complete semantic masking yields bitwise-identical S/S0 features, scores, ranking sets, counts, and D_K=0 in every state.
6. Future isolation: append arbitrary later rows; all prior features, scores, and ranking sets remain unchanged.
7. Accession/coupled-state trap: favorable clock, accession partition, visibility, and pathology outcome from different states must fail.
8. Rank ties/access monotonicity: exact patient-hash ties are deterministic; stronger hypothetical access may not violate the prespecified attainable-set monotonicity check.
9. Conclusion discipline: reject a correct positive D_K/C_K labeled referral performance, report-release benefit, workflow utilization, causal prioritization, biological M2, treatment effect, or patient benefit; accept only the bounded documentary ranking-potential statement. Correct adverse and inconclusive conclusions must also be accepted.
10. Provenance: every conclusion points to a computed row containing scope, interval, state extremum, K, counts, gate vector, source hashes, code version, and raw result locator.

Reference execution demonstrates computational feasibility and linkage only; it does not establish clinical truth.
