# UKB-03: Muscle quality (MFI) and functional decline beyond muscle volume and grip strength

## Unresolved clinical question
Sarcopenia is defined by low muscle mass AND low muscle strength. Current clinical practice focuses on muscle mass (DXA or MRI volume) as the primary marker. If muscle fat infiltration (MFI) — a measure of muscle *quality* — adds independent predictive information for functional decline (falls, slower walking pace) beyond muscle volume and grip strength, this would identify a new imaging biomarker and suggest that muscle quality is a distinct, modifiable target for intervention.

The unresolved claim: among participants with similar thigh muscle volume, does low grip strength combined with high MFI predict worse functional outcomes than low grip strength alone?

## Leading claim vs strongest rival
- **Leading claim**: Low grip + high MFI → worse functional outcomes than low grip alone (muscle quality matters independently of quantity). The Grip × MFI interaction should be significant after adjusting for muscle volume, age, sex, BMI, and smoking.
- **Strongest rival**: MFI is a proxy for age or general frailty. After adjusting for age, BMI, and smoking, the MFI effect disappears and the interaction attenuates to null.

## Discriminating observation
If the leading claim is correct, the Grip × MFI interaction term should remain significant (p<0.05) in a multivariable model adjusting for muscle volume, age, sex, BMI, and smoking. If the rival is correct, the interaction should attenuate to non-significance after these adjustments.

## Clinical importance
1. Identifies MFI (measurable by MRI) as a potential independent biomarker for functional decline
2. Suggests muscle quality (not just quantity) is a modifiable target for exercise/nutrition interventions
3. Informs clinical decisions about who to refer for sarcopenia treatment
4. MFI is increasingly available in clinical MRI protocols; if validated, it could augment current sarcopenia screening

## Analysis plan
1. **Baseline**: Logistic regression for falls (2296=2 vs 1), with Grip × MFI interaction term, adjusted for muscle volume (22403+22404), age, sex, BMI, smoking.
2. **Secondary**: Linear regression for walking pace (924), same model.
3. **Sensitivity**:
   - Use left thigh MFI (23355) instead of right
   - Restrict to participants with both left and right MFI
   - Adjust for comorbidities (if available from HRO)
4. **Learned alternative**: Random Forest or Gradient Boosting with SHAP for non-linear interaction detection.
   - What it reveals: whether the Grip-MFI relationship is linear (baseline) or has threshold/non-linear structure
   - Resource: Both fit on CPU in minutes (~40k rows). No GPU needed.

## Falsification criteria
- **Supportive**: Grip × MFI interaction significant (p<0.05) in the falls model, attenuated but not eliminated after full adjustment. Low-grip + high-MFI cell has highest fall prevalence.
- **Adverse**: No significant interaction, or interaction fully explained by age/BMI/smoking.
- **Inconclusive**: Borderline interaction (0.05<p<0.10), or insufficient falls events in the low-grip + high-MFI cell.

## Method comparison
- **Baseline**: Logistic/linear regression with prespecified covariates and Grip × MFI interaction.
- **Learned alternative**: Gradient Boosting + SHAP for non-linear interaction detection.
- **What the alternative reveals**: Whether the Grip-MFI relationship is linear or has threshold structure.
- **Resource**: Both fit on CPU in minutes (~40k rows). No GPU.
- **Selection reason**: The scientific question is whether the interaction exists and is independent of muscle volume. The baseline answers this directly. The learned alternative adds value by detecting non-linear structure.

## Unresolved issues
1. Falls (2296) is a single timepoint, self-reported, with a 1-year recall window
2. MFI is measured at a single timepoint; cannot model MFI trajectory
3. Muscle volume and MFI are from the same MRI scan; temporal relationship to outcomes is unclear
4. The MRI subset (~40k) is a self-selected group; generalizability to the full UKB population is limited
5. No clinical adjudication of falls (self-reported)
6. MFI units (HU) depend on MRI protocol; cross-site variability is a concern
