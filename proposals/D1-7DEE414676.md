# Structural documentation opportunity, not local synonym choice, explains sparse Ventilated care-plan export

## Unresolved question and advance

The assessed parent `[prior hypothesis]` established that an exhaustive, outcome-blind expansion of the exported Ventilation vocabulary added only 1–3 rows per inherited airway definition, all in one hospital, and did not improve hospital-held-out prediction of the inherited non-peak structured respiratory-setting reduction outcome. The remaining unresolved mechanism is whether sparse exact or broader `Ventilated -` receipt primarily reflects absence of a documentation opportunity rather than an omitted lexical synonym or a selective local workflow among hospitals that demonstrably use the Ventilation interface.

This child tests that mechanism directly. It preserves the parent’s independently selected, target-independent frozen denominator, the inherited landmark and freeze/classification/outcome windows, all four airway bounds, exhaustive outcome-blind vocabulary construction, hospital-aware interpretation, generic-care-plan and non-target Ventilation controls, and strict documentation-versus-bedside limits. The substantive advance is a falsifiable separation of four exported documentation states: broader target receipt; Ventilation-group opportunity without a broader target; generic care-plan activity without Ventilation activity; and no generic care-plan activity. It additionally reports hospital-by-eligible-stay interface footprints and landmark-specific sensitivity, rather than treating repeated landmark rows as independent opportunities.

The tested hypothesis is: among rows without a broader target, most exported absence is structurally explained by no generic care-plan or no Ventilation-group opportunity, while the presence of Ventilation activity provides common support for target receipt. A competing selective-workflow explanation predicts substantial absence despite Ventilation activity, low broader receipt conditional on Ventilation activity, and many hospitals with Ventilation opportunity but no broader target. Neither hypothesis asserts that any label represents ventilation, evaluation, SBT, extubation, readiness, quality, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Outcome-blind opportunity measures

The executable scans all 3,115,018 source rows in chunks using only `patientunitstayid`, `cplitemoffset`, `cplgroup`, and `cplitemvalue`. Strings are normalized by trimming, collapsing whitespace, and case-folding, with missing values preserved as `<na>`. The complete source-wide Ventilation vocabulary is enumerated before any outcome field is used. The primary broader target is the literal normalized prefix `ventilated -`, which yields exactly four values: `ventilated - chronic dependency`, `ventilated - rapid wean/extubation`, and the two inherited exact values. No target, value, hospital, frequency, or model criterion is selected using an outcome.

For every frozen `sid`/landmark key, the audit records in `[L,L+360]`: any care-plan row (`cp_any`), any non-target care-plan row (`cp_non_target_any`), all-row and non-target row counts, distinct groups, any Ventilation-group row (`vent_any`), any non-target Ventilation value (`vent_non_target_any`), Ventilation row/value counts, exact receipt, broader receipt, and the observed Ventilation strings. Generic opportunity is a process/interface opportunity, not care delivered. Non-target Ventilation is an explicit negative control for Ventilation-group activity that is not one of the four broader target values.

Mutually exclusive row states among absent-broader rows are:

1. `no_generic`: no carePlanGeneral row in the window;
2. `generic_no_vent`: generic care-plan activity but no Ventilation-group row;
3. `vent_group_no_broad`: Ventilation-group activity but no broader target;
4. `broad_target`: at least one broader target.

The primary structural share is the fraction of absent-broader rows in the first two states. Hospital footprints use all eligible rows for row-level summaries and collapse to `hospitalid` by eligible `sid` for stay-level opportunity summaries. A hospital has Ventilation opportunity if at least one eligible stay has any Ventilation-group row; a hospital has target support if at least one eligible stay has a broader target. This is an observed-export footprint, not a claim about the underlying EHR interface or bedside workflow.

## Executed analysis and prespecified gates

The executed source filter retained 138,620 rows belonging to frozen denominator stays and 5,507 rows in frozen care-plan windows; 4,798 unique frozen `sid`/landmark keys were audited. Reconstructed exact receipt agrees with inherited `parent.receipt` for all 22,421 inherited rows (zero mismatches). The private enriched row artifact contains the inherited rows plus documentation features.

Descriptive estimands are row-level structural shares and conditional exact/broader receipt rates, plus hospital-by-eligible-stay interface footprints. Landmark-specific tables report eligible rows, stays, hospitals, care-plan opportunity, Ventilation opportunity, exact receipt, broader receipt, and absent-broader rows lacking Ventilation opportunity. No causal model is fitted and no outcome is used to define documentation states.

The primary supportive gate, locked before interpretation, requires all of: at least 80% of absent-broader rows in `no_generic` or `generic_no_vent`; broader receipt at least 5% among rows with Ventilation opportunity; and at least five hospitals with Ventilation opportunity but no broader target. The competing selective-workflow adverse gate requires all of: less than 50% in those structural states; broader receipt below 5% conditional on Ventilation opportunity; and at least ten hospitals with Ventilation opportunity but no broader target. Otherwise the result is inconclusive. These are descriptive falsification gates for exported documentation opportunity and do not validate label semantics.

A separate common-support diagnostic reports hospitals with positive generic opportunity, positive Ventilation opportunity, positive non-target Ventilation activity, and positive broader-target support. Zero-opportunity hospitals are retained rather than discarded. Stay-level diagnostics are sensitivity checks against repeated-landmark weighting; landmark-specific results test whether the mechanism is stable at each inherited L. Sparse or zero cells are reported directly, not converted to evidence of clinical absence.

The prespecified interpretation is `supportive_structural_opportunity` for every airway definition. It supports the narrow claim that, in this exported snapshot and frozen cohort, sparse broader receipt is overwhelmingly located where no generic or Ventilation-group documentation opportunity is observed, while Ventilation opportunity supplies substantial common support for target receipt. It does not distinguish an absent upstream interface from an unrecorded opportunity, and it does not prove why a hospital did or did not document a value.

## Verification boundary and limits

An automatic verifier can check the parent and source hashes, complete-row scan, normalization, frozen joins, inclusive time windows, exact-receipt reconstruction, structural-state partition, stay collapse, landmark tables, common-support counts, and gate logic. It can verify that documentation features are outcome-blind in construction. It cannot determine whether an exported care-plan row corresponds to a true clinical state, whether an interface was available but unused, whether local workflow was selective for clinical reasons, or whether any target represents bedside evaluation, SBT, extubation, readiness, quality, benefit, harm, or causal effect.

The result is not evidence that patients lacked ventilation or that care was omitted. It is not a quality comparison, treatment-effect estimate, or transport claim. Stronger conclusions require local interface/data-dictionary review, direct time-resolved airway/device and SBT/extubation evidence, blinded chart adjudication, and prospective external multisite validation. The observed hospitals and source snapshot are fixed; common-support diagnostics do not establish generalization to unobserved hospitals.
