## Supportive, adverse, inconclusive, and no-rescue rules

The new claim is persistence of a positive low-UACR/N18 contrast across both prespecified horizons, not superiority by an arbitrary number.

- Supportive competing-risk persistence requires all inherited parent gates and new integrity checks to pass, and both simultaneous one-sided lower limits `L_CA1_5` and `L_CA1_9` to be strictly greater than 0. This supports only that AG1 has a higher fixed-denominator source-recorded low-UACR N18 cumulative-incidence yield than G1 at both five and nine years, with death counted as the competing first event.
- Adverse competing-risk persistence requires all checks to pass and at least one simultaneous one-sided upper limit `U_CA1_h` to be strictly less than 0. This is evidence that the positive contrast does not persist through at least one prespecified horizon. It does not show that G1 improves health, that AG1 causes harm, or that cystatin testing is useless.
- Inconclusive is every other result: one horizon crossing or touching zero, conflicting horizon directions without a confident negative horizon, sparse/degenerate bootstrap, failed integrity, noncomputability, or a parent gate that prevents interpretation. Inconclusive is not equivalence, no effect, safety, or absence of survival selection.
- A favorable five-year result cannot rescue an adverse or inconclusive nine-year result, and a favorable nine-year result cannot rescue failure at five years for the persistence label.
- The competing-risk label cannot rescue any inherited biochemical, five-year N18, nine-year N18, low-UACR, durability, or parent hierarchy failure. Emit all parent labels unchanged.
- Emit `low_UACR_CR_persistence_supported` only when the inherited parent nine-year low-UACR label supports and the new persistence label supports. Emit `low_UACR_CR_persistence_inconclusive` when the parent low-UACR label supports and the new label is inconclusive. Otherwise emit `not_rescuing_parent`.
- Never call any result causal benefit, causal harm, safety, prevention, kidney-specific benefit, CKD truth, measured-GFR accuracy, or mortality qualification.
