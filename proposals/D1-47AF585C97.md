# Storage reproducibility is not workflow actionability: a pre-registered bridge for the frozen pulmonary handoff gate

## Status and targeted successor

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s scientific schema exactly: the first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject pulmonary-opportunity population; reciprocal pre-departure radiology chronology; one canonical discharge (`DS`) artifact; unit-specific storage clocks; exhaustive departure/availability/eventual-text states; the recommendation-unit atom; all-unit adverse routing; routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, `NOACTION`, and nested `MIXED`; sentinel census; positive-probability stratified whole-admission sampling; hidden all-admission duplicate audit; finite-population estimands; component-seal and roster-disjointness rules; the inherited admission, challenge, packet, physician-hour, and packet-minute workload ceilings; and the prospective-silent-only conclusion ceiling.

No admission, eligibility criterion, chronology boundary, recommendation-unit definition, route predicate, sample allocation, estimand, baseline, or workload ceiling is changed. No source is modified. This amendment adds one narrow, pre-registered bridge using the same frozen units, primary/audit admissions, sealed component records, and existing review workload. It separates two claims that the parent’s route-purity result alone cannot establish:

1. **Storage reproducibility:** independent readers can reproduce the same target/action/interval unit and the same departure-documentation status from departure-available stored evidence.
2. **Text actionability:** conditional on that reproducible unit, the canonical DS contains an explicit, target-linked, non-conditional action and interval with a named responsible recipient or scheduling instruction, or an explicitly documented non-pursuit/replacement resolution.

The bridge then reports a third, non-actionability construct: which workflow/outcome facts are absent from the available MIMIC snapshot. It never treats storage as viewing, actionability as care delivery, or later documentation as completion. It is a measurement bridge, not an intervention evaluation.

## Clinical decision and substantive advance

A retrospective route may be reproducible because two readers recognize the same stored text, yet still be clinically unusable if the DS does not state an executable responsibility or interval. Conversely, an explicit DS plan may be reproducible as text while MIMIC provides no evidence that anyone saw it, accepted responsibility, scheduled imaging, communicated with the patient, obtained outside care, or achieved a clinical outcome. Confusing these layers can lead either to unsafe automatic propagation or to unnecessary reconciliation.

The unresolved question is therefore narrow and falsifiable:

> Among the parent’s frozen first-eligible pulmonary opportunities, does leakage-resistant, route-blinded storage reproduction predict a separately coded text-actionability state, and are apparent failures explainable by explicit missing workflow/outcome evidence rather than silently coded as non-action?

The advance over the parent is not a new route or a more favorable label. It is an explicit bridge from a reproducible stored recommendation to the strongest actionability claim the available records can support, while quantifying where that bridge terminates because workflow and patient-outcome evidence are unavailable. A supportive result can nominate only the unchanged prospective silent validation. It cannot authorize copying, orders, alerts, patient contact, or care changes.

## Evidence and unresolved hypothesis

The strongest evidence-supported claim is source feasibility, not clinical correctness. The frozen MIMIC snapshot contains admission/discharge times, patient age anchors, services/transfers/ICU descriptors, radiology report and detail text, and discharge report/detail text. The parent’s profile found 2,321,355 radiology rows (`RR=2,295,635`, `AR=25,720`), 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 `DS` rows and 331,794 unique admission keys, with 17 missing discharge `storetime` values and reciprocal-link fields in the radiology detail stream. These facts establish that the protocol can be attempted; they do not establish eligibility prevalence, route purity, actionability, appropriateness, safety, care delivery, or benefit.

The untested claim is that a route’s reproducible stored evidence is also a reliable indicator of a text-actionable handoff state, and that route-specific differences remain after independent actionability coding and explicit accounting for unavailable workflow/outcome facts.

### Primary bridge hypothesis

In the frozen population and inherited master/hidden-audit samples, the following will simultaneously hold, if the bridge is supported:

* the independently sealed radiology unit inventory and DS unit inventory have high exact, field-wise reproducibility under the inherited compatible-set rule;
* among admissions whose route is a robust singleton and whose active unit is reproducibly identified, the proportion with `ACTIONABLE_TEXT` is materially higher in `AUTO` than in `RECON`/`DEFER`, with the contrast estimated design-wise and its pre-registered simultaneous interval excluding the null governance margin;
* an independently staffed, route-blinded actionability control has no greater target-link/actionability rate than the target-linked unit by the pre-registered specificity margin;
* route assignment does not change when actionability fields are removed from the route derivation, and a route-scrambled negative control does not reproduce the target contrast;
* every apparent non-actionable case is assigned either a frozen text state (`INCOMPLETE_TEXT`, `REPLACED_TEXT`, `CONDITIONAL_TEXT`, `UNKNOWN_TEXT`) or a declared source-availability state, never silently coded as absent care; and
* the necessary workflow/outcome evidence vector is explicitly `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` where a source audit proves that the modality is not supplied, rather than `UNKNOWN` or a negative outcome.

The bridge is falsified by a confidence interval compatible with no clinically meaningful actionability contrast, by route-blinded controls reproducing the same contrast, by route changes after actionability is revealed, by unresolved unit matching that is preferentially resolved toward `AUTO`, by excess missingness/nonresponse, or by any inference that an unavailable workflow fact was observed.

## The additive storage-to-actionability bridge

### H1: canonical storage-reproducibility record

For each admission and every member of every compatible radiology inventory, serialize a unit as

`U=(target_cluster, action_or_modality, due_interval_or_date, polarity, operative_component)`.

Use the parent’s deterministic matching algorithm and fixed lexicographic serialization: normalize Unicode/case/whitespace, retain quoted source offsets, represent a calendar interval by `(unit_type, integer_value, calendar_anchor)`, and match only if target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, and polarity agree under the frozen tuple rules. Never match units solely by order, proximity, or route. Duplicate restatements in one reciprocal chain are one unit; distinct action/window combinations are distinct units. Unmatched units, ambiguous offset matches, malformed output, cross-admission identity, and missing source spans are `H1_UNKNOWN`, with both inventories retained.

For two readers or systems `r,s`, define field-wise agreement only on the fixed canonical unit matching; define `H1_REPRO=1` only when the matched unit set and all four decision fields agree and neither inventory contains an unmatched or boundary-uncertain unit. `H1_REPRO=0` is an observed disagreement. `H1_UNKNOWN` is nonresponse, unreadable packet, malformed output, or unresolved matching. The primary analysis propagates all compatible H1 states; it does not select a favorable panel.

### H2: explicit text-actionability record

Using the parent’s already sealed unit cards and canonical DS review, each independent plan reviewer records, for every possible unit, only the following atomic fields with exact DS spans:

* target-linked mention;
* compatible chest/thoracic action or modality;
* explicit interval/date equal to the unit’s required 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* an explicit responsible recipient/team or scheduling instruction;
* active versus historical/conditional status;
* explicit informed non-pursuit;
* explicit definitive replacement;
* cross-plan conflict.

The reviewer is blinded to route, stratum, clocks, other component labels, audit status, and later records. Dates are shifted to preserve the interval relation but conceal storage lag. `PCP follow-up` without action and interval is not executable; a generic specialty mention is insufficient; an order lacking validated target/due/recipient linkage does not resolve a unit.

Apply this mutually exclusive state table by locked code to each possible unit, retaining raw fields and reasons.

| State | Deterministic requirement | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `H2_ACTIONABLE` | target-linked, active, explicit compatible action, exact required interval/date, and explicit recipient/team or scheduling instruction; no contradiction | text-actionable only |
| `H2_REPLACED` | explicit target-linked definitive alternative or explicit non-pursuit, with no unresolved conflict | text-supported alternate resolution |
| `H2_INCOMPLETE` | target/action is present but interval, recipient, linkage, or active status is absent/discordant | needs reconciliation at most; not auto-copyable |
| `H2_CONDITIONAL` | recommendation depends on a condition, option, or future decision | not unconditional actionability |
| `H2_CONFLICT` | competing target-linked plans or incompatible action/interval/polarity | unresolved |
| `H2_UNKNOWN` | unreadable/missing DS, missing unit card, redaction corruption, reviewer nonresponse, or unresolved boundary | unknown, adverse |
| `H2_NA_NO_TARGET` | the fixed unit inventory is empty because the admission is `NOACTION` | not a denominator for unit actionability; retained for admission accounting |

`H2_REPLACED` is not counted as `H2_ACTIONABLE`; it is a resolved text state and follows the inherited route’s closure logic. `H2_NA_NO_TARGET` is never coded as a failed action or a success. For `TIMING`, actionability can be recorded descriptively, but timing prevents content routing under the unchanged `G_u` rule. For `DEFER`, all H2-compatible states remain adverse and no post hoc actionability label can rescue the route. An admission is `DOC_ACTIONABLE` only if every active unit is `H2_ACTIONABLE` or `H2_REPLACED`, there is no `H2_CONFLICT`/`H2_UNKNOWN`, and the robust unit inventory is singleton. An omission that is safe under inherited `AUTO` is specifically `DOC_OMISSION`, not actionability.

The reference audit repeats H1/H2 with fresh rosters and identical manuals. It is blinded to the index system and route. No consensus truth is created; the compatible reference state set is retained.

### H3: workflow/outcome evidence-gap vector

H3 is not a clinical outcome. Before labels, perform a source-availability audit against the catalog and exact schemas. For each admission, record these facts separately:

`viewed_by_recipient`, `DS_draft_edit_sign_transmit`, `physical_departure`, `responsible_clinician`, `target_linked_order_or_referral`, `scheduled_due_date`, `patient_communication`, `outside_plan_or_outside_care`, `follow_up_completed`, `imaging_result`, `clinical_harm_or_benefit`.

Each fact receives exactly one of:

* `OBSERVED_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCE`, only when the bound source has a semantically identified field/event and the event is present with valid time/linkage;
* `NOT_OBSERVED_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCE`, when the modality exists in a bound source but no qualifying event is found, with the search universe, keys, and time window recorded;
* `UNKNOWN_WITHIN_AVAILABLE_SOURCE`, for missing, malformed, contradictory, or nonresponse records in an available modality;
* `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`, when the source audit proves that no configured table/column/archive member supplies that modality;
* `NOT_APPLICABLE`, only for a frozen structural non-denominator case such as `H2_NA_NO_TARGET`, never for a route failure.

For this snapshot, `viewed_by_recipient`, draft/edit/sign/transmit history, responsible clinician for the recommendation, physical departure, validated target-linked order/referral, scheduling completion, communication, outside care/plan, follow-up completion, and causal harm/benefit are `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` as semantic modalities. Discharge text and storage timestamps are available but are not proxies for any of those facts. A recorded later radiology row is an `OBSERVED_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCE` documentation event only; it is not completion. Later radiology must be excluded from primary route derivation and may be analyzed only in a pre-registered secondary documentation-presence analysis using the parent’s frozen selected population and no outcome-based sampling.

For an available modality, absence is `NOT_OBSERVED_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCE`, never `NO CARE`, `NO COMMUNICATION`, `NO COMPLETION`, or `NO HARM`. H3 is descriptive and cannot enter the route executable or improve an H2 label.

## Independent route-blinded controls

Controls are pre-registered before any component label is released and use the same existing packets and workload ceiling; they do not create a new population or review arm.

1. **Target-permutation control.** Within each sampled admission, use a protocol-seeded permutation of unit tokens among radiology units while preserving action and interval marginals. The actionability reader receives the permuted token card and the same DS but is route-blinded. A target-specific actionability contrast that survives permutation is a falsification signal.
2. **Route-scramble control.** Independently permute the displayed route labels only in analysis shells, never in packets or primary derivation. H2 and H3 must not vary with the displayed route. Any route-dependent reader behavior or result-shell difference fails the bridge.
3. **Structural negative control.** From the same canonical DS, select a fixed pre-label non-target, non-pulmonary unit-like assertion with an explicit action and interval using a locked lexical/structural detector. If no eligible assertion exists, code `CONTROL_UNAVAILABLE` and report it; do not replace it after seeing labels. The control reader sees a neutral card and is blinded to target and route. Its target-link/actionability rate should be below the target-linked rate by a fixed specificity margin. It is a process specificity control, not a clinical comparator.
4. **Clock-isolation control.** Re-run H2 scoring with `G_u` and route labels withheld and verify that H2 does not change across `G_u` strata. This distinguishes timing from text actionability and prevents storage lag from being silently treated as an actionable attribute.

Controls are independent of the index route derivation and cannot rescue a failed bridge. `CONTROL_UNAVAILABLE`, packet leakage, or control-reader nonresponse is inconclusive, not a favorable zero.

## Estimands and analysis

The population denominator remains all `N_A` frozen admissions. Recommendation units remain clustered within admissions and are never sampled or weighted independently. Parent route burdens, component agreement, purity, capture, workload, and all inherited estimands are primary and unchanged.

The additive bridge estimands are design-based, admission-level quantities. For any bridge indicator `b_i`, estimate `T_b` and prevalence with the inherited master weights `w_i=1/pi_h`, and use the hidden audit weights `v_i=1/q_h` for audit-only comparisons. Report Horvitz–Thompson totals, Hájek ratios, exact event counts, denominator counts, maximum normalized weight, Kish ESS, finite-population variance, and at least 10,000 Rao–Wu resamples within frozen strata, carrying all units together. No complete-case deletion is permitted.

Define the primary target-actionability contrast on the set of robust singleton route admissions with a nonempty active unit and adequate clocks:

`Delta_A = P(DOC_ACTIONABLE | robust AUTO, eligible for H2) - P(DOC_ACTIONABLE | robust RECON or DEFER, eligible for H2)`.

This is a descriptive stored-text contrast, not a causal effect of routing. `TIMING` is reported separately because its defining fact is a storage clock and its H2 status cannot establish timing adequacy. `NOACTION`/`H2_NA_NO_TARGET` is retained in the all-population accounting and excluded from the conditional H2 denominator by rule, with its exclusion count and weight shown.

Estimate `R_H1`, the probability of `H1_REPRO` among route-eligible admissions; `R_H2`, exact and category-specific agreement for H2 across independent readers; `A_AUTO`, the proportion of robust AUTO admissions meeting `DOC_ACTIONABLE`; `A_RECON`, `A_DEFER`, and `A_TIMING` analogues; `MIX_H2`, any active unit in `H2_INCOMPLETE`, `H2_CONDITIONAL`, `H2_CONFLICT`, or `H2_UNKNOWN`; and `U_H3`, the weighted fraction of workflow/outcome facts coded `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` or `UNKNOWN_WITHIN_AVAILABLE_SOURCE`. Report the full joint table `(route, H1, H2, H3)` including zero and unknown cells.

The key held-out validation uses the untouched duplicate audit. The index route is derived without H2 actionability fields beyond the parent’s existing S attributes; reference H2 is derived independently. Define `P_AUTO^A` as the audit-weighted proportion of index `AUTO` admissions whose reference system has `DOC_ACTIONABLE` and no reference unsafe/unknown unit. Define `M_AUTO^A` as the audit-weighted proportion of index `AUTO` admissions that are non-actionable or H2-uncertain in the reference system. These are text-measurement quantities. The audit cannot be conditioned on an AUTO call for inclusion; all audit admissions are duplicated as inherited.

For the specificity control, define `S_control` as the paired difference in target-linked versus structural-negative-control actionability rates among admissions where both cards are available. If the control universe has zero positive support, the paired specificity estimand is undefined and the bridge is inconclusive; it is not assigned a favorable result. Target permutation is a sharp null check; exact permutation p-values are descriptive falsification diagnostics and do not replace the design-based interval.

Pre-register two-sided 95% simultaneous intervals using the inherited max-statistic family covering parent and bridge estimands. Governance-fixed bridge margins are `delta_action=0.10` absolute prevalence difference, minimum `R_H1=0.90`, minimum H2 route/category confirmation `0.90`, maximum AUTO reference non-actionable/uncertain contamination `0.05`, maximum target-permutation absolute contrast `0.02`, minimum target-versus-structural-control specificity difference `0.10`, and maximum decision-relevant packet leakage/over-redaction `0.02`. These are operational nomination margins, not harm thresholds or probabilities of clinical benefit. If governance does not freeze them before labels, the bridge is `NO_SELECTION`.

The parent route gates remain necessary and independent. A route cannot pass by satisfying the bridge if its inherited purity, reliability, ESS, precision, workload, retrieval, redaction, seal, or roster gate fails. For a two-lane silent nomination, inherited `AUTO` and `RECON` gates plus the relevant bridge gates are required. If AUTO fails the text-actionability gate but RECON passes, the admissible conclusion is reconciliation-only silent validation; no AUTO is rescued. If the bridge is underpowered or its controls unavailable, report parent results but make the bridge and any actionability-dependent nomination inconclusive.

## Interpretation

**Supportive.** All inherited source, denominator, sampling, masking, seal, roster, route, purity, ESS, precision, and workload gates pass. The hidden audit independently confirms the route-blinded H1/H2 bridge; `Delta_A` exceeds `delta_action` with the simultaneous interval; AUTO contamination is below 0.05; controls remain negative/specific; and every H3 unavailable fact is explicitly labelled as unavailable rather than inferred. This supports only a prospective silent validation of the unchanged routing and actionability measurement. It does not support automatic insertion, clinician-visible prompts, orders, communication, or a claim of benefit.

**Supportive for reconciliation-only.** RECON/DEFER captures non-actionable, conflicting, or uncertain text states with adequate independent confirmation, while AUTO either fails the actionability/purity gate or has too few events. This is clinically consequential evidence against copy-oriented routing and supports only silent reconciliation measurement, not intervention.

**Adverse.** AUTO frequently lacks explicit target/action/interval/responsibility text; H1 reproducibility does not translate into H2 actionability; target-permuted or structural controls reproduce the signal; route labels alter blinded H2 coding; independent readers discover additional units; or unknowns/nonresponse cross the locked margins. These reject the corresponding retrospective nomination and argue that stored reproducibility is not an actionable bridge.

**Inconclusive.** Retrieval undercoverage, excessive `U_R/U_D`, sparse route or control support, unavailable structural controls, audit exposure, roster/seal failure, packet leakage or over-redaction, undefined conditional denominators, ledger overflow, inadequate ESS/precision, or unapproved margins prevent the bridge conclusion. Absence of workflow/outcome data is itself a bounded limitation, not adverse evidence about whether care occurred.

## Evidence ceiling and required next study

The automatic verifier can check source/schema hashes, joins, time masks, reciprocal links, canonical matching, sampling probabilities, seals, rosters, route invariance, H1/H2 state logic, H3 source availability, controls, weighted estimators, uncertainty, workload, and whether claims match outputs. Clinical experts are still required to judge pulmonary eligibility, unit boundaries, target linkage, action semantics, and whether a replacement/non-pursuit statement is text-supported. Independent text agreement is not clinical truth.

MIMIC cannot establish actual viewing, draft/edit/sign/transmission history, physical departure, clinician responsibility, order/referral completion, scheduling, patient communication, outside care, adherence, follow-up completion, appropriateness, harm, or benefit. A later radiology note can establish only later documentation in this source, not imaging completion or outcome. A supportive result therefore freezes the protocol for a prospective silent study that collects report finalization and viewing, DS drafts/edits/signatures/transmission, physical departure, responsibility, target-linked orders/referrals and scheduling, outside-plan attestation, patient preference, competing-risk context, final imaging and clinical outcomes. Only after independent silent validation could a governed workflow study compare usual care with clinician-confirmed reconciliation or propagation; that later study must measure correction/dismissal, burden, responsibility, communication, completion, and harms.

## Relation to inspected demonstrations

The inspected text of Shmatko et al., *Learning the natural history of human disease with generative transformers*, DOI `10.1038/s41586-025-09529-3`, describes causal attention masks restricting a model to prior events and explicitly discusses biases from incomplete EHR collection. That motivates departure-frozen masking and explicit evidence-gap accounting, but it does not validate pulmonary handoffs or text actionability. The inspected reporting text of Urbut et al., *A Bayesian framework for longitudinal EHR and genetic discovery*, DOI `10.1038/s41586-026-10780-5`, emphasizes explicit likelihoods, inverse-probability weighting, correct hierarchical level, and uncertainty; those principles motivate admission-level design-based inference and compatible-set reporting, but the study did not evaluate discharge documentation or workflow. The available Cell demonstration was inspected only through its supplementary PDF/text; the main article and full STAR Methods were unavailable. Its supplement describes longitudinal multimodal modeling and missing-data/cohort-discriminator ideas, which motivate missingness controls only; it is not evidence of workflow completion or clinical benefit.

## Exact targeted advance and remaining uncertainty

The parent already protects against circular route labeling. This successor addresses the remaining decision-validity gap without changing its schema. It fixes the unit matcher and H2 truth table, separates `DOC_ACTIONABLE` from omission/replacement/conditional/unknown text, uses independent route-blinded controls, and converts unavailable workflow/outcome facts into an explicit H3 availability vector. Thus a pass can say precisely: “this stored recommendation is reproducible and text-actionable enough to merit silent validation,” while a failure can say “the apparent route is not actionable from the stored artifact.” Neither conclusion is allowed to become “care was delivered,” “the recommendation was appropriate,” “the patient benefited,” or “the route is safe in practice.”

Remaining uncertainty includes shared clinical interpretation despite roster separation, incomplete or lossy text, absent radiology images, source-dependent missingness, sparse rare route events, independent reference error, and the fundamental difference between storage time and human workflow. These require prospective collection, expert adjudication, and a subsequent outcome study.
