## Question, supported evidence, and unresolved stronger claim

Among adults already selected for a recorded first repeat TACE, the frozen experiment asks whether two leakage-safe intermediate-to-current within-assay direction indicators improve prediction of (a) availability of the fixed early panel after matching assay-specific measurement process and (b) the fixed concordant albumin-down/total-bilirubin-up transition among episodes with that panel observed, beyond remote-to-current directions, compact decision-time covariates, and matched history-process variables.

It is a concordant adverse-direction laboratory transition, not adjudicated deterioration, toxicity, decompensation, complication, or clinically important change.

These results support only a reproducible but sub-margin association in 131 selected observed-panel episodes. The unresolved stronger claim is whether these directions forecast an adjudicated hepatic outcome or generalize to the 111 episodes without the event-aligned post panel.
