# HCC measured-result content beyond capture intensity for the next recorded transition

## Successor decision and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent remains the strongest HCC direction: the available data can support an auditable information question about a recorded transition, while they cannot support active-HCC, treatment-intent, treatment-benefit or causal claims. A restart toward a pathway or plan outcome would rely more heavily on unvalidated lexical semantics and would be scientifically weaker.

I sharpen the parent’s primary contrast. Instead of treating assay identity and assay result as one indivisible “content” block, the experiment will separately estimate:

1. whether prior assay identity/occurrence adds information beyond prior capture intensity; and
2. whether the recorded result or value and its assay-specific trajectory add information beyond identity/occurrence and capture intensity.

The primary contrast is the second one, V-minus-I. The legacy V-minus-Q contrast remains a locked secondary contrast so this child remains comparable with the parent. This decomposition tests whether the information is in measured result content rather than merely in the hospital’s test menu.

The future solver must newly produce a frozen cohort and patient split; key, multiplicity and timestamp audit; strict-history and anchor-linked manifests; exhaustive seven-state labels; frozen Q/I/V feature manifests; fitted transparent A/L/Q/I/V models; fitted continuous-time S/S_I/S_Q models; locked patient-held-out probabilities; paired log-loss and Brier contrasts; calibration and clustered uncertainty; nested content/permutation falsifications; the gated order-procedure audit; and a claim-to-output table. Completion is established by these outputs and the uncertainty/falsification reports, not by a positive finding.

## Hypothesis, evidence and clinical importance

Public literature supports a narrower prior claim: routine liver-function measures have prognostic associations in HCC, while validated routine-laboratory tools for choosing among treatment pathways remain limited. EHR literature also documents that testing and recording processes can be informative. The local HCC data do not establish that any assay result measures liver reserve, active tumor, treatment eligibility or benefit.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults at their first locally recorded HCC-coded inpatient admission, strictly pre-admission assay-specific results/values and their dated within-assay trajectory improve the calibrated prediction of the complete next recorded procedure/discharge transition beyond admission context, first-12-hour current labs, strictly prior capture intensity, and prior assay identity/occurrence.

This is clinically relevant to whether previously recorded measurements contain incremental information for anticipating the next documented care transition, after accounting for how intensely the patient was observed. A supportive result could motivate prospective validation of result-aware transition support; it would not justify a treatment recommendation. The substantive advance is identifying whether measured results add information beyond both observation intensity and test selection, rather than reporting another generic EHR prediction improvement.

Evidence already supported:
- The HCC snapshot has native encounter admission/discharge times and native lab, procedure, order and medication time fields.
- Encounter, diagnosis, procedure and key-quality facts were audited in the parent support record.
- The local lab metadata has no separate unit column, so cross-assay numeric pooling and an unvalidated liver-reserve score are prohibited.

Not yet supported:
- V-minus-I, I-minus-Q, or V-minus-Q performance;
- clinical utility, transportability, survival, toxicity, response, treatment allocation, or causal effects;
- that a lexical procedure family means intent, recommendation, scheduling, receipt or completion.

This is a record-coding rule, not clinical adjudication.

Sort each patient’s HCC-coded encounters by native 入院时间 and then 就诊号. Keep the earliest HCC-coded record only if it is an adult admission with parseable native 入院时间 and 出院时间, nonnegative stay, and 入院时间 in [2011-01-01, 2026-01-01). If the earliest HCC-coded record has invalid admission timing, exclude the patient rather than replacing it with a later record. Set t0 to the anchor 入院时间. Keep early discharges and unresolved-time admissions in the cohort.

Use half-open windows:
- strict prior history: [t0 - 730 days, t0);
- current admission labs: [t0, t0 + 12 hours);
- early endpoint: [t0, t0 + 24 hours);
- later endpoint: [t0 + 24 hours, t0 + 72 hours).

A strict prior row must have a valid native event time in the prior window and link by (患者主索引, 就诊号) to an encounter with valid native 入院时间 strictly earlier than t0. This applies to labs, procedures, orders and medications. Anchor-linked rows with a pre-t0 event timestamp are excluded from the primary history even if their event timestamp is earlier than admission; report them in a frozen sensitivity branch. Also report invalid event times, invalid linked encounter times, duplicate/multiplicity and unmatched keys, boundary/tie rows, after-discharge rows, left truncation and no-strict-history counts.

The anchor-linked branch restores the event-time-only rule and labels anchor-linked rows separately. A t0-30d branch uses [t0 - 730 days, t0 - 30 days) as a negative control for peri-admission documentation. Neither branch can tune the primary model.

## Primary recorded endpoint

Construct an exhaustive admission-anchored seven-state terminal vector from procedures, native procedure 开始时间, and encounter 入院时间/出院时间. In [t0,t0+24h), assign:
- P0 when a valid procedure starts in the window and before discharge;
- D0 when discharge is at or before 24 hours before any established valid early procedure;
- U0 when missing or invalid procedure timing could change procedure/discharge ordering or window assignment;
- R24 otherwise.

Among R24, assign the analogous P1, D1, U1 and N72 states in [t0+24h,t0+72h). Use a fixed precedence declared before labels, including procedure-wins for exact discharge/procedure ties; rerun a discharge-wins sensitivity. Procedures before admission, after discharge, at boundaries, multiple procedures and midnight starts receive explicit counts. Missing timing is unresolved, never “no procedure.”

The primary estimand is the paired held-out difference score(V) - score(I) for the complete seven-state vector, with multiclass log loss and Brier score; lower is better. I-minus-Q and V-minus-Q are secondary nested contrasts. Any R24-conditional analysis is secondary and cannot replace the complete all-admission estimand.

## Information sets

All models use the same patients, endpoint, preprocessing policy and patient-level split.

- A: age, sex, admitting department, calendar era, admission time-of-day, admission missingness and strict-history availability.
- L: A plus assay-specific summaries of labs in [t0,t0+12h), using 检验, 定性结果, 定量结果, 标本类型 and 检验时间. Use first/last valid numeric result, change, elapsed time, count, density/missingness and slope only with two distinct times; retain qualitative results assay-specifically.
- Q: L plus strict prior encounter/source capture only: prior encounter counts, duration and active days; recency; source-specific row and distinct-encounter counts for labs, procedures, orders and medications; source-type count; missing-time count; left-truncation and no-history indicators. Q contains no assay names, results, values, procedure/order/medication names, doses, routes, frequencies or statuses.
- I: Q plus strict prior lab assay identity and occurrence summaries—assay identity, assay-specific count, recency, span, missingness and number of distinct assays—but no 定性结果 or 定量结果.
- V: I plus strict prior assay-specific 定性结果 and 定量结果 and dated change/slope where at least two distinct valid times exist. Numeric scaling is fit-only within assay. No numeric pooling across assays and no cross-assay physiologic score are permitted.

The primary nested interpretation is V-I: result/value content beyond test occurrence and capture intensity. I-Q asks whether test selection itself adds information. V-Q preserves the parent’s broad content contrast.

## Learned alternative and deferred mechanistic branch

Fit a low-dimensional continuous-time event model S to exactly the same strict prior rows used by I/V. Each event carries source/type, elapsed time, assay identity, payload mask and assay-specific lab payload; generic procedure/order/medication tokens represent recording process only. S_I has the same architecture, split, regularization and event times but removes lab values/results while retaining assay identity; S_Q removes both assay identity and results. The learned contrasts S-S_I and S_I-S_Q separate nonlinear measured-result information from test identity and observation structure.

The alternative can reveal order-sensitive trajectories, irregular spacing, nonlinear interactions, missingness patterns and representation uncertainty that first/last/count summaries lose. Fix latent dimension, decay family, masking/reconstruction term, shrinkage and seed count before locked evaluation; tune only on the selection split. Use supervised terminal-state negative log likelihood and report the same held-out log loss, Brier, calibration and clustered intervals. A learned model is not retained merely for a small score gain: it must be seed-stable, calibrated and add information beyond V/I in a prespecified contrast.

A mechanistic alternative is deferred, not silently invented. The source has no lab-unit column, validated reference ranges, pathology timing, stage or tumor burden. A cross-assay ALBI/MELD-like score would add unsupported assumptions. Revisit it only after expert review supplies assay-unit and clinical-semantic mappings. Revisit procedure/order interpretation only after chart review establishes order meaning, scheduling and intent.

## Split, analysis and uncertainty

Assign patients by SHA-256(normalized 患者主索引) modulo 100: buckets 0-59 fit, 60-69 selection/preprocessing/calibration, 70-79 locked evaluation and 80-99 inaccessible. All rows and admissions for a patient stay in one bucket. Fit-only dictionaries, scaling, quantiles, feature rules and calibration are frozen before locked evaluation; locked labels cannot choose them.

Report paired held-out multiclass log loss and Brier for all seven states, state-specific calibration intercept/slope, reliability curves, observed versus predicted probabilities and per-state contrasts. Use patient-clustered paired bootstrap with at least 1,000 resamples, preserving all admissions for sampled patients, for 95% intervals. Stratify descriptively by no strict history, 1-2 and at least 3 prior encounters, fit-only capture-intensity strata, and 30/180/730-day windows.

Supportive primary evidence requires a V-I interval below zero for log loss and/or Brier without material calibration deterioration; I not reproducing the V increment; attenuation after within-assay result/value permutation; stability across capture strata and windows; and no leakage. Stronger evidence requires a consistent strict-history direction in the anchor-linked sensitivity or a clear attenuation when anchor-linked rows are introduced, plus S-S_I not being explained by S_Q or S_I alone.

Adverse evidence is no V-I improvement, worse calibration, I reproducing V, persistence after within-assay result permutation, gain only with anchor-linked rows, concentration only in the excluded 30-day interval, forbidden-window leakage, patient split failure, or a learned gain reproduced by S_Q. Inconclusive evidence includes dominant U states, sparse strict-history patients/assays, wide patient-cluster intervals, severe left truncation, invalid/coarse times, feature dictionary ambiguity, or unstable learned fits. Inconclusive does not confirm the hypothesis.

Required falsifications include within-patient and fit-only assay-stratum permutation of values/results; assay-identity permutation; prior-history time permutation preserving counts; anchor-linked replacement; t0-30d exclusion; a forbidden [t0+24h,t0+72h) predictor model that must be labeled leakage and excluded from claims; shuffled patient IDs before splitting; and discharge/procedure tie precedence sensitivity.

## Gated order-procedure secondary layer

Retain, but do not let it redefine the primary result, the parent’s deterministic order-before-procedure audit. Normalize only frozen substring families in 手术 and 医嘱(非药品): embolization/TACE, resection/transplant, ablation, systemic/infusion, diagnostic/access, otherwise other. Preserve blank, multi-match and ambiguous strings.

A concordance requires the same exact key, nonblank order, valid order 开立时间 strictly before procedure 开始时间, and one shared deterministic family. Valid procedure start without valid order issue time is lower-confidence only. Freeze the gate before locked labels: at least 100 uniquely family-assigned, temporally resolvable procedure admissions; at least 80% timed procedures nonblank and exactly one family; at least 70% candidate orders with resolvable 开立时间; no family over 90% of concordant events; and at most 25% dependent on midnight ties or unresolved timing. If the gate fails, declare the secondary inconclusive or use only a prespecified generic any-order fallback with at least 100 resolvable events.

This endpoint is temporal concordance in recorded data. It is not intent, plan, recommendation, appropriateness, administration, completion or benefit. Order status, frequency, duration and medication fields remain process descriptors.

## Compute, feasibility and interpretation gates

The proposed solver envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB RAM, 8 allocated GPUs and 28,800 seconds. Expected unverified workload: chunked aggregation of the 2.2 GB labs and 2.19 GB orders in 1-4 hours; transparent fits and locked prediction in 1-4 hours; continuous-time model fitting in 2-8 hours CPU-first; and 1,000-plus patient-cluster bootstrap resamples in 1-4 hours. The diagnostic audit is measured separately from the future solver and used only to update feasibility counts. A GPU is optional: small transparent tabular fits are CPU-suitable; repeated sequence fits may use one allocated A100 with cuda:0 after a bounded probe. GPU use is not evidence of clinical value, and no discovery job trains proposer or solver weights.

The primary result is computationally checkable: cohort rules, row provenance, endpoint labels, model fits, held-out predictions, proper scores, calibration, intervals and falsification outputs. Clinical adjudication is required for active HCC, stage, indication, intent, procedure completion, result semantics, outside-care completeness and patient benefit. External validation, prospective utility and causal treatment effects require linked clinical evidence or another study.

If I reproduces V, conclude that assay selection/observation rather than recorded results explains the information. If S adds no seed-stable calibrated information beyond V, retain the transparent models. Revisit the deferred mechanistic or intent/plan branches only with expert review and missing clinical evidence.
