# A leakage-resistant probability audit of pulmonary-nodule admission routing: subject-disjoint development, sealed evidence panels, and independent route-purity challenge

## Decision bottleneck and substantive advance

The remaining question is not whether pulmonary-nodule recommendations can be found in MIMIC notes. It is whether the frozen admission routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` remain reproducible and textually safe when the labels used to derive a route cannot be influenced by seeing that route, another component label, or the evidence assigned to another component. The parent protects against direct route voting, but its discharge-plan reviewers receive the radiology abstraction, its context redaction and component adjudications can be iteratively repaired on the same sampled admissions, and its AUTO verifier can become a de facto consensus panel. Those paths can make a route appear pure because the component labels were formed with knowledge of the comparison they are supposed to support.

This child preserves the parent's frozen first-eligible all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum reconstruction, unique canonical discharge summary (`DS`), unit-specific storage clocks, exhaustive departure states, deterministic safety-sentinel census, positive-probability sampling in every nonsentinel stratum, review of every unit in every sampled admission, admission-level finite-population inference, and worst-unit routing. It changes the evidentiary architecture:

1. Manuals, ontologies, redaction rules, matching rules, route code, margins, and packet software are developed only on synthetic fixtures and a subject-disjoint non-`N_A` development corpus. No `N_A` discharge text or route label is opened before all artifacts are sealed.
2. The confirmatory audit is an untouched, prespecified probability sample of `N_A`; all deterministic sentinels are a census and every nonsentinel admission has positive inclusion probability. No sampled admission becomes training material, and no subject may occur in both development and audit.
3. Four mutually firewalled panels see different evidence views and emit only atomic, quoted labels. The radiology panel never sees the `DS`; the discharge-plan panel never sees radiology text or abstractions; the context panel sees a target-plan-masked `DS`; and an independent holistic challenge panel sees paired source evidence but no component labels, clocks, strata, routes, or expected direction.
4. Locked code constructs all permissible radiology-unit/discharge-proposition/context compatibility sets and derives routes. Reviewers cannot vote for, reconcile toward, or repair a route. Disagreement, boundary ambiguity, failed redaction, unmatched propositions, and unknowns expand the compatibility set adversely.
5. Route purity is tested against the independently sealed holistic challenge labels and by explicit leakage attacks, not against a consensus assembled from the same component labels.

The strongest evidence currently supports only source and workflow feasibility. The local parent scan found 2,321,355 radiology rows (2,295,635 `RR`, 25,720 `AR`), 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 discharge rows all typed `DS`, 331,794 unique `(subject_id,hadm_id)` discharge keys, no duplicate canonical keys, and 17 discharge rows with missing `storetime`. It also found 25,735 `parent_note_id` and 25,735 `addendum_note_id` detail rows. These counts support computability but do not establish semantic eligibility, route prevalence, route purity, or benefit.

Current literature shows why a transition-of-care workflow is consequential but does not resolve this question. The abstract of Fu et al. (J Imaging Inform Med 2025; DOI `10.1007/s10278-024-01357-7`, PMID 39806184) describes an EHR incidental-finding notification workflow with acknowledgment monitoring and follow-up orders; it does not validate discharge-summary route purity in MIMIC. The locally available Nature demonstration on disease trajectories explicitly uses cutoffs and separate data to avoid time leakage, and the locally available 2026 Bayesian EHR paper describes held-out individuals and fixed population parameters for prediction. Those are methodological precedents for untouched evaluation, not evidence for this clinical route. The main 2026 Cell cancer article was unavailable locally; only its supplement was inspected, and no claim here depends on unavailable main-text methods.

## Supported claim, unresolved claim, and falsifiable hypothesis

**Supported now:** the frozen MIMIC sources expose the exact report, addendum-link, admission, discharge artifact, text, and storage-time fields needed for a departure-frozen, subject-keyed audit. Existing evidence does not show that independently formed component labels yield the same admission route.

**Unresolved claim tested here:** after development is excluded at the subject level and each component is labeled from a sealed evidence view, locked compatibility-set code can derive admission routes that are reproducible, retain low independently measured unsafe `AUTO` contamination, and capture reconciliation/timing/uncertainty cases over the complete frozen `N_A` population with valid design-based uncertainty.

The hypothesis is:

> In the frozen first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity population, a subject-disjoint, untouched stratified probability audit will show that sealed atomic evidence labels and locked compatibility-set routing produce reproducible admission routes; the simultaneous finite-population upper bound on independently challenged unsafe `AUTO` routing is below 5%; reconciliation or deferral captures at least 95% of independently challenged incomplete, conflicting, mixed, interaction-dependent, boundary-uncertain, or context-unknown admissions; and all relevant gates continue to pass after disagreement, masking uncertainty, nonresponse, and every permissible compatibility assignment are carried adversely.

Any failed computable gate yields `NO_SELECTION` for the affected route. Even complete support can nominate only prospective silent validation of frozen code. It cannot establish recommendation appropriateness, safe insertion into care, clinician awareness, causal benefit, follow-up completion, or patient benefit.

The broad candidate frame contains every exact admission-linked radiology row with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]` and at least one pulmonary/nodule/opacity term, follow-up/recommendation term, 3/6/12-month or equivalent calendar expression, chest/thoracic CT expression, or locked high-recall exam code/name. All deterministic positives and parser/chain ambiguities receive independent dual eligibility review from radiology-only packets. Eligibility reviewers never see the `DS`, route strata, or later evidence.

The primary `N_A` is exactly one first eligible admission per subject, selected before route evidence:

* adult by the age formula above;
* an exact admission-linked report in the interval;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;

Review at least the finite-population sample such that zero misses yields a simultaneous one-sided 95% upper bound of 0.5 eligible misses per 100 negatives in each stratum, or census smaller strata. ## Stage 1: development firewall and immutable freeze

### Subject-disjoint development corpus

Development may use only (a) synthetic fixtures written before source review and (b) a locked probability sample of up to 150 adult exact admission-linked radiology candidates whose subjects are proven absent from the final `N_A` subject manifest because they fail semantic eligibility or have no eligible recommendation. It may not use later eligible admissions from an `N_A` subject. Development packets may exercise masks and component schemas but cannot supply confirmatory route estimates.

Before opening any confirmatory `N_A` `DS`, seal and hash:

* eligibility and unit manuals;
* action, timing, polarity, target, responsibility, resolution, and context ontologies;
* text normalization and deterministic span/redaction rules;
* packet constructors and allowed-field lists for every panel;
* component-label JSON schemas and quoted-span validation;
* compatibility relation and exhaustive enumeration algorithm;
* route precedence and all gates;
* sampling strata, seed derivation, variance code, and synthetic test fixtures;
* reviewer identities, panel assignments, access-control lists, and adjudication policy.

After sealing, no artifact may be tuned on an `N_A` audit label. A defect that changes eligibility, population, component semantics, matching, route, estimand, outcome, or gate requires a new protocol/version and a new untouched sample; it cannot be repaired in place. A mechanical packet correction that reveals no prohibited evidence is logged, independently approved, applied to every affected packet, and triggers full hash regeneration before labels are released. If investigators conclude that eligible development examples are essential, this study is inconclusive rather than borrowing audit subjects.

Required computable separation gates are exact: intersection of development and `N_A` `subject_id` hashes equals zero; intersection of any prior route-labeled subject and the audit sample equals zero; all freeze timestamps precede audit-seed revelation and first audit packet decryption; and all artifact hashes at analysis equal sealed hashes. Any violation stops all route claims.

### Outcome-blind safety strata and untouched probability audit

After `N_A`, canonical keys, reciprocal-chain status, clocks, and deterministic span inventory are frozen, but before any confirmatory component or holistic label exists, assign every admission to exactly one hierarchical sampling stratum:

1. `S_SENTINEL`, inclusion probability 1: any reciprocal-chain defect; missing/contradictory time; missing/unreadable canonical artifact; parser failure; `U_BOUNDARY` signal; at least two possible recommendation units; differing actions or due windows; negation/conditionality conflict; target-linked decision, non-pursuit, or definitive-treatment language; or discordant pulmonary-plan spans in the `DS`.
2. `S_TIMECLOCK`: no sentinel, but any deterministic unit has `G_u<24h`, unknown `G_u`, `DS_store>D`, or stale chronology.
3. `S_ZERO`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and no deterministic target-linked pulmonary plan span in the `DS`.
4. `S_PARTIAL`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and target text exists but action, timing, responsibility, polarity, or linkage appears absent/discordant.
5. `S_COMPLETE`: no sentinel, all clocks ample, and a target/action/timing/responsibility or replacement-plan pattern appears complete.

These are hidden sampling strata, not component labels or routes. Every `S_SENTINEL` admission is selected. In each other stratum sample admissions by simple random sampling without replacement using the sealed seed. For `S_ZERO`, take the larger of 300 or the finite-population worst-case 0.5 sample for ±4 percentage-point 95% precision, capped at `N_h`. For each other nonsentinel stratum, take the larger of 120 or the corresponding ±7-point sample, capped at `N_h`:

`n_h=ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25/{e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`.

Record `N_h,n_h,pi_h=n_h/N_h`, seed, ordered IDs, no replacements, packet completion, and reasons. The ceiling is 900 selected admissions. If mandatory sentinel census plus fixed samples exceeds 900, obtain resources or declare adjudication infeasible; never lower a sampling fraction or drop a sentinel after labels. Selected nonresponse remains weighted and adverse. Because `N_A` has one admission per subject, admission sampling is subject sampling; nevertheless, explicitly assert unique `subject_id` in `N_A` and zero audit/development subject overlap.

This is the sole confirmatory route audit. No preview, pilot, interim route frequency, power adaptation, margin change, or selective adjudication is allowed. Every selected admission goes to every required panel and every possible radiology unit in it is assessed.

## Stage 2: sealed evidence panels and independently sealed labels

Reviewer pools are disjoint across panels and from eligibility/development personnel. Reviewers sign that they have no access to another panel's packets or labels. All packet opens and exports are logged. Each reviewer works independently; discussion and consensus are prohibited before initial labels are sealed. A third opinion is an additional compatible label, never a replacement. No reviewer sees a candidate route or votes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, or `NOACTION`.

### Panel R: radiology recommendation units

Two physicians see only the complete reciprocal operative radiology chain for that admission. They see `note_type`, component order, and only the minimum relative timing needed to determine supersession; they do not see `DS` text, `DS_store`, `D`, `G/H/V/Y/Z`, admission sampling stratum, other panel labels, downstream notes, POE, or expected direction. Each independently enumerates every unit and records source-character offsets and verbatim spans for target cluster, action/modality, exact due interval/date, polarity/conditionality, operative component, and supersession.

The two sealed inventories define a set of possible report units, not a consensus. Split/merge or coreference disagreement creates both minimum and maximum inventories and `U_BOUNDARY`. A third R reviewer may add an inventory but cannot erase either initial inventory.

### Panel D: discharge-plan propositions without radiology anchoring

Two different physicians see only the canonical predeparture `DS` text and its internal section order. They do not see any radiology text, radiology abstraction, report timestamp, clock, context label, stratum, route, later note, or POE. They independently enumerate all pulmonary-target or plausibly target-linked plan propositions, including absent-plan status, and quote character offsets for target, action/modality, exact timing/date, responsible recipient/team or scheduling step, polarity, active/historical/conditional status, explicit informed non-pursuit, definitive replacement, and cross-plan conflict. They must also mark generic or unresolved linkage rather than infer it. Vague `PCP follow-up`, generic specialty mention, or an unlinked order is not an executable equivalent.

Panel D does not decide whether a proposition matches a radiology unit. Its sealed propositions are passed only to locked code.

### Panel K: target-plan-masked departure context

A redaction service with no labeling role applies the sealed dictionary plus span model to the canonical `DS`. It replaces, with length-preserving typed masks, every pulmonary target, nodule/opacity descriptor, chest-imaging action, interval/date, recipient/order/scheduling phrase, explicit pursuit/non-pursuit, definitive replacement, and cross-plan phrase. Mask types are hidden from reviewers as the single token `[MASK]`; original character counts, section boundaries, and hashes are retained only for audit. The context packet includes `admission_type`, `admission_location`, `discharge_location`, `hospital_expire_flag`, terminal service, ICU exposure, and the masked predeparture `DS`. It excludes radiology, clocks, D labels, routes, strata, later data, and POE.

Two context physicians independently assign only `K_OPEN`, `K_CLOSED`, or `K_UNKNOWN`, with a quote from unmasked non-target evidence or the exact missing fact. `K_CLOSED` means a departure-frozen observable stop such as documented comfort-only/hospice transition, death, explicit globally applicable non-pursuit, or definitive target treatment that survives masking because its non-target consequence is independently visible. It is not a judgment of guideline appropriateness. If masking removes evidence needed to distinguish open from closed, label `K_UNKNOWN`; reviewers may not request the hidden span.

A separate redaction-audit physician, disjoint from Panel K, compares source and masked packets for every `K_CLOSED` admission and a sealed 20% SRS of all other audit admissions. They record (i) residual target-plan leakage and (ii) removal of decision-relevant non-target context. Those labels never repair the packet; any event adversely expands context to `K_UNKNOWN` and contributes to leakage gates.

### Panel H: independent holistic route-purity challenge

Two additional physicians, disjoint from R/D/K and development, receive the operative predeparture radiology chain and canonical predeparture `DS` side by side, with source identity, absolute calendar dates, storage clocks, strata, component labels, route words, and downstream evidence suppressed. Relative component order is shown. They independently enumerate every radiology recommendation unit and answer only prespecified challenge questions with quotes:

* is any report unit missing from the discharge plan;
* would literal transfer of report wording leave a conflicting, incomplete, mixed, conditional, non-equivalent, or interaction-dependent plan;
* is any report/discharge boundary or linkage materially uncertain;
* is there explicit departure text showing resolution, non-pursuit, definitive replacement, or an unresolved stop;
* is timing/opportunity unknowable from the packet and therefore not safely classifiable as a content omission.

They do not assign a route and cannot see Panel R/D/K outputs. A challenge is `Q_UNSAFE` if either initial H reviewer identifies an incomplete/conflicting/mixed/interaction-dependent or missed minority unit; `Q_UNCERTAIN` if either identifies boundary, linkage, context, readability, or timing uncertainty; `Q_CLEAR_OMISSION` only if both enumerate the same complete unit inventory, every unit is an unconflicted literal omission, and neither records uncertainty; and `Q_NOACTION` only if both independently document explicit resolution/replacement/non-pursuit for every unit. Disagreement is retained as the union and adverse. Third H opinion may add evidence but cannot convert an initial unsafe/unknown label to clear.

Panel H is the independent safety reference for route purity and capture. It is still a text review, not clinical truth. It cannot determine actual report viewing, recommendation appropriateness, patient preference outside the artifact, responsibility, or prospective safety.

## Locked compatibility sets and route derivation

All source spans must validate against the panel's permitted packet hash. Labels without valid spans, except explicit absence/unknown, become unknown. Once labels are sealed, locked code performs normalization and matching; no reviewer or analyst can edit a pair.

For admission `i`, let `R_i` be every possible Panel-R inventory, `D_i` every possible Panel-D proposition set, and `K_i` both initial Panel-K labels plus any audit-induced unknown. Construct the full Cartesian compatibility set `Omega_i` over all initial/third inventories, propositions, contexts, and all permissible split/merge assignments. A radiology unit and discharge proposition are compatible only if every required relation is allowed by the sealed ontology:

* target linkage is explicit or the proposition explicitly quantifies all pulmonary nodules; generic pulmonary language is unresolved;
* chest/thoracic CT modality is exact or in the sealed equivalence class; generic imaging is not equivalent;
* due interval/date equals the unit's exact 3-, 6-, or 12-month window under sealed calendar arithmetic; a range crossing windows is discordant;
* polarity and active/conditional status agree;
* responsibility/scheduling is explicit when executability is claimed;
* a resolution requires explicit informed non-pursuit, definitive replacement, or fully equivalent plan evidence, never absence alone.

Many-to-one, one-to-many, unmatched, and ambiguous pairings are all enumerated. If exhaustive enumeration exceeds a sealed computational cap, the admission is `DEFER`; pruning by favorable score is prohibited. For every `omega in Omega_i`, locked code maps each unit to:

* `U_RESOLVED`: explicit `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, or fully equivalent `S_EQ` evidence;
* `U_OPEN`: `K_OPEN`, plan state `S_0`, `S_PART`, or `S_CON`, and known `G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING`: otherwise active/unresolved but `G_u<24h` or clock unresolved;
* `U_CLOSED`: `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER`: `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, unreadable evidence, boundary/linkage ambiguity, unmatched proposition ambiguity, redaction failure, or a compatible disagreement crossing statuses.

Admission precedence is immutable:

1. any `U_DEFER` gives `A_DEFER`;
2. else any `U_TIMING` gives `A_TIMING`;
3. else if all units are resolved/closed, `A_NOACTION`;
4. `A_AUTO` only if every open unit is wholly absent (`S_0`), all other units are reproducibly resolved/closed, all unit clocks are ample, and there is no boundary, linkage, redaction, or cross-unit conflict;
5. every incomplete, conflicting, interaction-dependent, or mixed admission is `A_RECON`; mark nested `A_MIXED` if a pure omission coexists with a reconciliation unit.

The robust route set is `{route(omega): omega in Omega_i}` augmented adversely by Panel-H challenges. `Q_UNSAFE` makes `AUTO` incompatible and adds `RECON`; `Q_UNCERTAIN` makes `AUTO` incompatible and adds `DEFER`; a timing uncertainty adds `TIMING` and `DEFER`. A decision-facing route is a singleton only if the augmented set has one label. Otherwise it is `A_DEFER`, while all compatible alternatives are reported. One unsafe minority unit controls the admission; duplication of safe units cannot alter route or weight.

For reproducibility, also derive two sealed lane routes: Lane 1 uses initial R1/D1/K1 and Lane 2 uses R2/D2/K2, with no cross-lane consensus. Reviewer assignment to lane is randomized before packets. The robust route remains primary; lane agreement is a reliability estimand, not a reference truth.

## Full-population estimands and untouched-audit workload

The finite population is always all frozen `N_A`; because one first eligible admission is retained per subject, the sampling unit, analysis unit, and subject are aligned. For stratum `h`, `w_i=1/pi_h`. Units never receive separate weights.

Estimate route totals and burdens for `r in {AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,MIXED}`:

`T_hat_r=sum_{i in audit} w_i I(R_i=r)` and `B_hat_r=100*T_hat_r/N_A`.

`MIXED` is nested within `RECON` and is not double-counted. Estimate all `Y`, `V/H/Z`, chronology, `E_A`, and non-`E_A` margins so exhaustive cells sum to `N_A`.

Primary safety quantities are:

* `C_AUTO=P(Q_UNSAFE or Q_UNCERTAIN | either sealed lane or robust code would otherwise call AUTO)`;
* `leak_AUTO=P(either lane calls AUTO | H reference is Q_UNSAFE or Q_UNCERTAIN)`;
* `Se_RECON=P(robust route in {RECON,DEFER} | Q_UNSAFE)`;
* `PPV_RECON=P(Q_UNSAFE | robust route=RECON)`;
* `PPV_TIMING=P(every unresolved blocking unit has G_u<24h or unresolved clock | robust route=TIMING)`;
* `Se_DEFER=P(robust route=DEFER | H or component compatibility contains decision-relevant uncertainty)`;
* `D_MIX=P(robust route in {RECON,DEFER} | H identifies a pure omission plus any unsafe minority unit)`;
* the rate at which H discovers an additional report unit absent from both deterministic and initial R inventories among nonsentinel admissions.

Denominators with zero estimated burden remain zero-event/precision failures, not perfect performance. Nonresponse, invalid spans, packet corruption, redaction failure, and unresolved H disagreement enter safety denominators adversely.

Report lane-to-lane weighted raw agreement, category-specific positive agreement, both directional confirmations, Gwet AC1, and kappa overall and by stratum. The primary confirmation for a route is the lower directional probability. Consensus-derived agreement is prohibited as a gate.

Measure untouched-audit workload without using development timing: eligibility packets; `N_h/n_h/pi_h`; sampled admissions and units; R/D/K/H initial and third reviews; redaction audits; invalid packets; minutes per packet from immutable open/submit timestamps, excluding idle intervals over 30 minutes; total physician-hours; units/admission; maximum normalized weight; Kish effective sample size overall and per route denominator; sentinel share; and design-weighted hours per 100 `N_A`. Report development effort separately and never call time saved a clinical benefit.

## Inference and baselines

Use exact stratified without-replacement Horvitz-Thompson totals and Hájek ratios with finite-population variance. Generate at least 10,000 Rao-Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates respecting strata and carrying every unit and all panel labels for an admission together. For zero rare events, also invert the stratified hypergeometric distribution and use the wider valid one-sided bound. Within every replicate, enumerate unknown-compatible lower/upper states before confidence limits.

A single max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers route burdens, lane agreement/confirmation, `C_AUTO`, `leak_AUTO`, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, `D_MIX`, missed-unit rate, redaction leakage, and over-redaction. Report point estimates and two-sided intervals except prespecified safety upper or sensitivity lower bounds.

Prespecified baselines, all evaluated with identical admission weights, are:

1. marginal-majority route;
2. hidden deterministic sampling stratum mapped `S_ZERO` to AUTO-like, `S_PARTIAL` to RECON-like, `S_TIMECLOCK` to TIMING-like, and sentinel to DEFER-like;
3. recommendation-unit majority, expected to miss unsafe minority units;
4. a deliberately leaky comparator in which Panel D receives the radiology abstraction;
5. favorable consensus that discards disagreement;
6. complete-case unweighted sampled estimates.

Baselines cannot nominate a route. The leaky comparator quantifies optimism: if it materially improves apparent AUTO agreement or purity, that is adverse evidence for the unseparated workflow, not support for the sealed route.

## Precise computable gates

Governance must approve margins before any audit packet opens. Defaults, if accepted unchanged, are `tau_AUTO=0.05`, minimum route confirmation/raw agreement `rho=0.90`, minimum AC1 `0.70`, `sigma_RECON=0.95`, `pi_RECON=0.80`, `pi_TIMING=0.95`, and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`. They are feasibility thresholds for silent validation, not acceptable harm rates.

### Global integrity and leakage gates

All must pass:

1. exact source/schema/denominator hashes, complete row counts or documented snapshot-consistent explanation, unique canonical `DS`, reproducible reciprocal links, and retrieval audit pass;
2. zero shared `subject_id` between development/prior route-labeled corpora and `N_A` audit; zero audit packet opened before freeze; zero post-freeze changes to label schema, mask, compatibility code, route code, estimand, or margins;
3. zero reviewer identity overlap across R, D, K, H, redaction audit, and development pools; zero unauthorized packet access/export in immutable logs;
4. automated allowed-field validation on 100% of packets: zero bytes from forbidden tables/time windows and zero route, stratum, other-panel label, expected-direction, post-`D`, later encounter, downstream outcome, or POE payload;
5. redaction audit with no confirmed direct pulmonary target/action/timing/recipient/pursuit phrase visible to K in any audited packet and simultaneous one-sided upper population bound `<=0.02`; decision-relevant non-target over-redaction upper bound `<=0.02`. Any audited leakage makes that admission adverse `K_UNKNOWN`; a direct route/stratum/other-panel label leak anywhere stops the study regardless of rate;
6. `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, at least 200 `N_A`, at least 60 `E_A`, audit effective sample size `>=150`, maximum normalized weight `max(w_i)/sum(w_i)<=0.10`, and simultaneous route-burden half-width `<=5` admissions per 100;
7. compatibility enumeration reproduces every route in sealed exhaustive fixtures, has zero order/reviewer-ID dependence, and agrees with brute-force enumeration on 100% of a generated test suite of at least 10,000 admissions; any cap overflow becomes DEFER;
8. all selected admissions, including every sentinel, receive all-unit R/D/H review or remain weighted adversely; no replacement or complete-case deletion.

### AUTO gate

Require at least 30 sampled otherwise-AUTO admissions and effective denominator size `>=100`; lower simultaneous lane confirmation/raw agreement `>=0.90`; lower AC1 `>=0.70`; upper `C_AUTO<=0.05`; upper `leak_AUTO<=0.02`; upper `D_MIX<=0.10`; upper nonsentinel additional-unit discovery rate `<=0.02`; no H-identified unsafe or uncertain unit remains compatible with robust AUTO; lower `B_AUTO>0`; and no adverse route reversal under the 72-hour clock sensitivity. A single observed unsafe case does not mechanically fail if its design-based simultaneous upper bound passes, but it can never be relabeled away.

### RECON gate

Require at least 30 sampled robust RECON and 30 H `Q_UNSAFE` admissions, effective size `>=80` in each denominator; lower lane confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `Se_RECON>=0.95`; lower `PPV_RECON>=0.80`; upper AUTO leakage among `Q_UNSAFE<=0.02`; lower `B_RECON>0`; and no unit-majority baseline may substitute for admission worst-unit routing.

### TIMING gate

Require at least 30 sampled robust TIMING admissions and effective size `>=80`; lower lane confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `PPV_TIMING>=0.95`; zero observed use of index-only `G` when another active unit has `G_u<24h` or unknown; lower `B_TIMING>0`; and exact clock-identity validation on every unit.

### DEFER gate

Require at least 30 sampled uncertainty-reference admissions and effective size `>=80`; lower lane confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `Se_DEFER>=0.95`; no adverse-compatible unknown routed AUTO; and lower `B_DEFER>0` if DEFER itself is nominated. A large DEFER burden is allowed but is evidence of limited automatable scope.

Routes pass independently and simultaneously. A two-lane silent validation requires both AUTO and RECON. RECON-only silent validation may be nominated when RECON passes and AUTO fails for contamination, multiplicity, leakage, or sparse burden. Missing governance approval or any global gate yields only `NO_SELECTION`.

## Leakage and computation falsification tests

1. Recompute source hashes and stream full files. Header/hash drift, unexplained count drift, denominator-manifest drift, or duplicate canonical `DS` stops analysis.
2. Prove reciprocal `RR`/`AR` links in both directions and within subject/admission. One-way or cross-key links cannot establish an operative component.
3. Verify `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u` for every unit. Perturbing the index clock cannot rescue a different unit.
4. Reproduce the seed, strata, `pi_h`, ordered sample, and sentinel census exactly. Dropping difficult, low-yield, nonresponsive, or high-weight packets fails.
5. Hash-set attack: insert a known duplicate `subject_id` into development and audit manifests; the build must abort before packet generation. Repeat with a later admission from an `N_A` subject.
6. Time attack: inject a post-`D` note, later faithful discharge text, or POE row into each packet constructor; 100% must be rejected. Adding/removing the optional POE appendix cannot alter any label, route, burden, or gate.
7. Cross-view canaries: place unique synthetic canary strings only in forbidden layers (`DS` for R, radiology for D/K, component labels/routes/strata for all panels). Automated packet scans and a blind packet-content audit must detect every canary; any escaped canary stops the study.
8. Mask attack: seed target/action/timing/recipient phrases both inside and outside dictionary forms in synthetic discharge text. Residual target-plan leakage and decision-relevant over-redaction must be detected and converted to unknown under the locked rule.
9. Route-word attack: include literal `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, expected direction, and deterministic stratum only in metadata. Any panel payload containing them is rejected before review.
10. Label-independence attack: permute reviewer IDs, panel order, proposition order, and unit order. Robust route and all gates must be invariant. Labels from another panel placed in a packet must trigger access/payload failure rather than improve agreement.
11. Compatibility brute force: generated cases cover all one-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-one, unmatched, split/merge, polarity, timing-window, and resolution combinations. Locked enumeration must equal exhaustive brute force; favorable pruning fails.
12. Worst-unit fixture: nine pure omissions plus one conflict must always be `A_MIXED` within RECON, never AUTO. Duplicating the nine omissions cannot change route or admission weight.
13. Independent-challenge trap: an H unsafe/unknown label must prevent robust AUTO even when all R/D/K labels agree. A third opinion or consensus cannot erase it.
14. Leakage optimism test: run the deliberately leaky comparator only after primary labels seal. Report the design-weighted difference in apparent AUTO confirmation and unsafe contamination. A simultaneous improvement greater than 5 percentage points demonstrates meaningful circularity in the comparator; it does not invalidate a passing sealed workflow, but omitting this result fails verification.
15. Negative-pair test: randomly pair each sampled radiology packet with a different-subject DS within sampling stratum and rerun only locked compatibility code, without human review. AUTO must not increase by more than 2 design-weighted points over true pairs; an increase or widespread generic matching falsifies linkage specificity and fails AUTO.
16. Design test: simulate finite populations with rare unsafe cases concentrated in sentinel, nonsentinel, high-weight, and mixed admissions. Weighted estimators must be unbiased within Monte Carlo error and simultaneous intervals must achieve at least 94% coverage in 2,000 simulations; unweighted estimates must visibly fail under unequal fractions.
17. Zero-event trap: no observed unsafe AUTO event must still produce a positive one-sided finite-population upper bound. A zero point estimate alone cannot pass.
18. Nonresponse trap: unreadable/unreviewed selected admissions remain weighted and adverse. Complete-case deletion or replacement must fail output validation.
19. Baseline trap: marginal majority, sampling stratum, unit majority, favorable consensus, or leaky review cannot nominate a route or override worst-unit routing.
20. Clock perturbation: report `G_u>=12h` and `>=72h` sensitivity without changing `N_A`. AUTO/RECON nomination requires no adverse reversal at 72 hours; stale/postdeparture artifacts remain non-content states.
21. Claim trap: an otherwise correct result accompanied by a claim of clinical appropriateness, safe automation, clinician error/awareness, causal benefit, communication, order completion, or patient outcome must be rejected by the verifier.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive** means the untouched subject-disjoint audit, source/retrieval/separation/leakage gates, finite-population precision, route reliability, and relevant safety bounds all pass simultaneously. AUTO support requires that independent holistic challenge, minority-unit detection, redaction uncertainty, and every compatibility assignment leave unsafe contamination below the locked margin. This supports only a prospective silent test of the frozen extractor and router.

**Supportive for RECON only** means reconciliation reproducibility and independent unsafe-content capture pass while AUTO fails because a minority unit, ambiguous matching, masked context, leakage sensitivity, or wide contamination bound prevents literal-copy nomination. This is a useful adverse finding for copy-oriented routing and may nominate a reconciliation-only silent study.

**Adverse** includes higher-than-margin independent unsafe AUTO contamination, leakage that improves apparent agreement, generic matching under negative pairing, recurrent extra units missed outside sentinels, route changes across sealed lanes, timing classified from the index unit alone, or failure of RECON/DEFER to capture H unsafe/uncertain cases. It rejects the affected retrospective route, not every future workflow.

**Inconclusive** includes retrieval undercoverage, hash or access failure before usable labels, insufficient events/effective sample size, wide simultaneous intervals, sentinel workload above 900, too many unknown artifacts, mask failure, reviewer nonresponse, compatibility explosion, missing governance margins, or any need to tune on audit subjects. An inconclusive AUTO result is not evidence that no workflow is needed.

## Evidence limits and required next study

MIMIC stores note artifacts and storage timestamps, not physical departure, report viewing, draft/edit/sign/transmission histories, clinician awareness, outside records, recommendation appropriateness, full preferences, responsibility assignment, or reliable order/referral/scheduling completion. `G_u` is storage-to-storage opportunity, not human viewing or editing opportunity. A canonical `DS` can be incomplete, and deidentified chronology cannot establish cross-subject calendar effects. Independent physicians provide a reproducible text reference, not clinical truth.

If retrospective gates pass, freeze extraction, reciprocal-chain logic, unit schema, masks, component manuals, compatibility enumerator, route precedence, and margins. A prospective silent study must enroll consecutive adult discharges and capture immutable report finalization/addenda and availability/viewing, every discharge draft/version/edit/sign/transmission time, physical departure, all candidate units, responsibility, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-plan attestation, preferences/competing risks, and final transmitted artifact. Route-blinded independent experts must assess confusion, AUTO positive predictive value with all uncertainty adverse, mixed-unit misses, timing, deferral capture, drift, and workload. Require at least 100 route-positive admissions and 30 per claimed route or a prespecified accrual stop.

Only after that silent validation could a governed trial compare usual care with clinician-confirmed structured prepopulation for validated AUTO cases and side-by-side reconciliation for validated RECON cases. Correction/dismissal, inappropriate insertion, fidelity, clinician burden, responsibility, communication, completed follow-up, and harms require prospective workflow data and clinical adjudication. No retrospective MIMIC computation can establish them.

## Advance over the parent and unresolved uncertainty

This child retains the parent's design-based workload solution and conservative clinical scope but removes the most consequential circularity. The confirmatory admissions are never used to form manuals or rules; development and audit subjects cannot overlap; component panels are evidence-firewalled; radiology and discharge propositions are labeled without seeing one another; context is separately masked; locked code enumerates compatibility instead of allowing reviewers to reconcile toward a route; and a fully independent holistic panel challenges purity without seeing component labels or routes. Explicit canary, negative-pair, access-log, masking, brute-force, and leakage-optimism tests make separation falsifiable.

Residual uncertainty remains. Subject-disjoint development from noneligible candidates may not expose every eligible linguistic form; the correct response is an inconclusive audit, not tuning on held-out cases. Masking can remove true context or leave indirect clues. Independent holistic review can still disagree and is not clinical truth. Probability sampling bounds unsampled risk but cannot prove no rare unsafe admission exists. Governance margins are operational choices rather than utilities. Thus even a complete pass establishes only that a retrospective text-defined gate is leakage-resistant, reproducible, and sufficiently bounded to merit prospective silent validation.
