# Independent, source-specific triangulation of context-selective MerTK harm

Status: substantive successor of [prior hypothesis]. Planned Harbor experiment only; no screening or scientific result has been executed.

## Opening and hypothesis

The inspected evidence supports a broad starting claim: MerTK-positive synovial macrophage states are associated with remission/repair and MerTK perturbation has been associated with increased FLS inflammatory transcripts [K1]. It does not establish that inhibitor harm is larger in MerTK/CD206-positive than MerTK/CD206-negative context, or that the observation transfers to an independently designed paired perturbation assay.

Primary hypothesis: in E-MTAB-8873 type-B synovial-cell cocultures, exact macrophage MerTK-inhibitor exposure produces a larger FLS harm response in MerTK/CD206pos than MerTK/CD206neg coculture after accounting for contributing FLS and macrophage memberships. Secondary triangulation hypothesis: within E-MTAB-8316, paired UNC106238 response in synovial monocyte preparations is directionally compatible with the primary perturbation response when summarized with a frozen verified gene map and nested donor-aware units. The secondary is not a claim that E-MTAB-8316 contains Mpos/Mneg contexts or FLS coculture harm.

The strongest rivals are generic inflammatory/stress signaling or composition, and timing/protocol mismatch. They predict similar perturbation direction in LPS controls, FLS-only samples, or all macrophage contexts; and they predict that any E-MTAB-8316 disagreement could reflect its different compound, monocyte label and absent time field. [K2] makes the macrophage-state rival consequential; [K3] shows why heterogeneous, niche-linked FLS programs must not be reduced to one transcript. The advance over [K1] is a prespecified identifiability gate plus a directional source-specific transfer check, not pooled external validation.

## Estimands, baseline and identifiability

Normalize each prepared matrix independently to log2(CPM + pseudocount), preserve identifiers and do not impute absent genes. Emit a manifest with source, sample ID, treatment tier, context, round, FLS membership, macrophage membership, nested donor unit, mapping status and exclusions.

Primary harm score H is the mean of feature-standardized IL6, MMP1, MMP3 and MMP14, standardized using eligible untreated coculture references only. Secondary outcomes are IL1B, MMP12 and MMP13 plus each component. MERTK, MRC1, TREM2 and SPP1 are a descriptive context panel, not proof of target engagement.

Primary estimand:
Delta = [E(H | exact inhibitor, Mpos) - E(H | untreated, Mpos)] - [E(H | exact inhibitor, Mneg) - E(H | untreated, Mneg)].

First report the rank/overlap gate: exposure-by-context cells, matched counterpart availability, FLS/macrophage membership incidence overlap, and whether Delta is estimable without the ambiguous untreated row or preInhibitor tier. Only the R1 SA256A/SA259A Mneg and Mpos pairs provide complete same-membership exact comparisons; one Mneg treated cluster and conflicting Mpos metadata mean a population-level interaction may be non-identifiable. Failure returns inconclusive, not zero.

Transparent baseline: construct within matched same-membership H differences, then fit a small fixed-effects model with context, exact exposure and interaction, adding assay round only when rank permits. Represent every FLS and macrophage donor as a membership incidence matrix. Use crossed random effects or penalized membership effects only after documenting rank. Report effects, intervals, component effects, donor-membership bootstrap, leave-one-round-out, leave-one-membership-out and admissible mapping sensitivities. Donor t-tests, cell counts as replication, random row splits, confounded age/sex adjustment and pooling exact/preInhibitor are prohibited.

Secondary estimand is not pooled with Delta. Conditional on the annotation gate, compute within-source log-expression differences for the six none/UNC106238 pairs, average repeated context measurements within each verified donor/condition as the registry directs, retain four FLS-cluster and six nested-monocyte strata, and fit a paired donor-aware response summary with no twelve-independent-donor claim. Compare direction and module coherence only. Without the map, output secondary_mapping_blocked.json with the missing dependency and stop that branch.

## Falsification and interpretation

Fix material threshold delta=0.5 SD of H before fitting. Supportive primary evidence requires positive Delta with uncertainty supporting Delta > delta (or posterior probability at least 0.90), coherent components, stable sign across mapping and membership/round holdouts, and no selective mimicry in LPS or FLS-only controls. A gated E-MTAB-8316 result is directionally concordant only if its mapped target/module response has the prespecified compatible sign across donor strata; it cannot rescue a failed primary rank gate.

Adverse evidence is nonpositive Delta, Mneg harm at least as large, generic LPS/FLS-only mimicry, or clearly discordant gated secondary direction. This weakens context-selective prioritization and favors generic inflammation, composition or timing.

Inconclusive evidence includes rank/overlap failure, missing Ensembl map, unreconciled secondary protocol/labels, uncertainty spanning zero or delta, sign changes across mappings, one cluster driving the effect, exact/preInhibitor disagreement or a non-identifiable alternative. Next test: balanced matched Mpos/Mneg perturbation across multiple FLS/macrophage donor pairs with dose, time, viability, MerTK protein/phosphorylation, efferocytosis, secreted mediators and direct FLS invasion or matrix-degradation readouts.

Harbor can check source joins, feature selection, paired units, estimand arithmetic, rank reports, mapping gates, uncertainty and conclusion compliance. It cannot establish direct mechanism, viability, spatial localization, therapeutic window, patient selection, clinical benefit or independent biological validity. Those require expert review, orthogonal agonism/genetic loss-of-function, protein/functional assays and an independent cohort. Clinical outcomes, medication response, protein/phospho-MerTK, efferocytosis, viability, dose-response and longitudinal time course are unavailable.
