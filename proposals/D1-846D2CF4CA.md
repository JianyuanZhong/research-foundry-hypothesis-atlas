# Alive-and-durably-liberated by day 7 after delivery-coherent post-shock IV loop initiation

**Parent:** `[prior hypothesis]`

## Substantive Episode-9 repair

This child preserves the parent's population, hour-48 landmark, 24-hour delivered-IV-loop strategies, corrected namespaced discovery split, clone-censor analysis, combined ICU/hospital RRT bounds, secondary VFD28 bounds, and evidence limits. It repairs one material outcome defect: the parent's "48-hour successful liberation" can remain a success even if invasive ventilation recurs or the patient dies later before day 7. It also calls only continuously ventilated patients definite failures, leaving some fully ICU-observed patients artificially unknown despite complete opportunity to observe the stated documented endpoint.

The primary endpoint is therefore replaced by **alive, documented durable liberation at day 7**. Success requires an accepted extubation by `t0+5 days`, continuous linked-ICU observation from that event through `t0+7 days`, no accepted re-intubation or invasive-ventilation recurrence, and survival through day 7. Death at any time by day 7 is failure, including death after 48 ventilator-free hours. Among patients continuously observed in linked ICU intervals through day 7, absence of a qualifying durable liberation is documented-endpoint failure even if the ventilation procedure stream is intermittent. ICU exit, ward time, external transfer, hospital discharge, and every positive ICU-interval gap before day 7 remain unknown rather than presumed success or failure. This is deliberately a narrow recorded endpoint, not clinical truth about extubation outside MIMIC.

This change is consequential: treatment that accelerates extubation but increases re-intubation or early death must not satisfy the primary benefit gate. It improves the decision relevance and tightens the computable classification without inventing ward or post-discharge observation.

## Evidence boundary, unresolved claim, and importance

Current evidence supports only that heterogeneous conservative-fluid/deresuscitation bundles can reduce recorded balance and may improve ventilator-free days. The indexed Silversides et al. 2017 systematic review (DOI `10.1007/s00134-016-4573-3`; frozen search source `[source checksum]`; full paper not inspected) and indexed RADAR-2 report (DOI `10.1007/s00134-021-06596-8`; frozen source `[source checksum]`; full text not inspected) do not establish the effect of isolated loop initiation at this landmark. The full Europe PMC XML of a 2026 RADAR-2 secondary analysis (DOI `10.1097/CCE.0000000000001404`; frozen source `[source checksum]`) reports imprecise kidney/perfusion results, and the inspected 2026 Orieux review XML (DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`; frozen source `[source checksum]`) treats ICU renal congestion as hypothesis-generating.

The unresolved claim tested here is: among selected ventilated adults stabilized after vasopressor-treated shock with substantial documented positive balance, initiating or re-initiating delivered ICU IV furosemide/bumetanide within 24 hours causes a clinically important increase in alive documented durable liberation at day 7 versus withholding every loop for 24 hours, without excess recorded mortality or observed new RRT. This could inform whether a pragmatic randomized trial is warranted; this observational experiment cannot establish efficacy, renal safety, standard care, or a treatment recommendation.

Retain subject/hospital/stay IDs, all times, item, status, and `continueinnextdept`; emit dictionary labels and per-status counts before use.

For each subject:

- `ALDL7_L=ALDL7_U=0` if death occurs in `(t0,t0+7d]`, regardless of earlier extubation.
- `ALDL7_L=ALDL7_U=1` only if an accepted extubation occurs in `(t0,t0+5d]`, ICU observation is continuous from that event through `t0+7d`, the subject is alive at day 7, and no accepted intubation or invasive-ventilation interval starts or overlaps after extubation through day 7. - If ICU observation is continuous from t0 through day 7 and no such event qualifies, set `ALDL7_L=ALDL7_U=0`. This is failure of the documented endpoint, not proof of clinical non-liberation.
- Otherwise set `ALDL7_L=0, ALDL7_U=1`; this includes ICU/ward gaps, ICU exit, live discharge, or external transfer before classification.

## Falsification and result meanings

Supportive requires the primary `RD_L` point >+5 points with 95% lower limit >0; upper 95% excess recorded 28-day death <+3 points; upper 95% excess observed day-7 RRT <+5 points in both date brackets; expected process direction; feasibility/positivity; acceptable missingness; and passed temporal falsification. It supports external validation and a randomized trial only.

Adverse means the 95% upper limit of `RD_U<=0`, a mortality-harm lower bound >=+3 points, or observed RRT-harm lower bound >=+5 points under the prespecified brackets. Early fluid removal cannot rescue respiratory failure, recurrence, death, or RRT harm.

Inconclusive includes `RD_L` failing support while `RD_U` permits benefit, wide sampling/identification intervals, benefit without both safety gates, date-bracket changes, endpoint/exposure sensitivity conflict, nonpositivity, pre-t0 imbalance, incomplete day-7 ICU observation, missing/unvalidated adjudication, or imaging interaction uncertainty. Nonsignificance is not equivalence or safety.
