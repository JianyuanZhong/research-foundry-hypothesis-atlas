# Temporal-direction, source-isolated, transport-gated successor for the frozen eICU respiratory-label design

## Status, inherited schema, and substantive advance

This proposal is a substantive successor of the assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen scientific schema exactly. It does not change the cohort, exposure, landmarks, decision time, exclusions, parser, endpoint states, all-row estimand, clustering, or descriptive/noncausal boundary.

The frozen population is 540 person-landmark rows from 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons in 74 hospitals. The first qualifying stay per `uniquepid` is retained; repeated landmarks within that stay remain rows and are clustered by `patientunitstayid`. Landmarks are `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission. The first exact target label in `[L,L+360]` is selected, with `D = cplitemoffset` (and verified `D == decision_offset`). The only arms are the exact strings `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` (`evaluation`) and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` (`no_trial`).

The inherited clinical-time windows remain `B=(D-720,D]`, `E=(D,D+720]`, and `T=(D+720,D+2160]`. The locked respiratory parser uses only FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate, with inherited aliases, finite/bounded parsing, FiO2 fraction/percentage conversion, duplicate and same-time conflict rules, minimum two observations at distinct offsets separated by at least 30 minutes per component, and at least three of four components per state. Fixed absolute thresholds remain FiO2 `0.10` fraction, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`. The ordered five-state all-row outcome remains

`K ∈ {competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.

Finite `unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160` has precedence, with death, discharge, transfer, and truncation visible as competing exits; invalid or unavailable exit time is separate and never means alive. The primary displays remain fixed-arm-denominator `p_k(a)` and `Δ_k = p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`; the conditional classifiable display `q_c(a)` and `δ_c` is secondary. These are associations with literal structured-chart constructs, not treatment effects or clinical truth.

The strongest supported claim before this experiment is only that the two strings are sparse, heterogeneous care-plan documentation and that a same-source label increment could arise from pre-D respiratory state, source-population opportunity, hospital workflow, entry timing, endpoint-source dependence, exits, or persistence across landmarks. No available evidence establishes an SBT, extubation evaluation, readiness, clinician intent, delivered setting, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The unresolved hypothesis is deliberately narrow and falsifiable:

> **After pre-D state and value-blind opportunity adjustment, the exact current label has a temporally directional and transportable incremental association with the locked post-D `K` trajectory that survives isolation of the endpoint source, does not predict outcome-blind pre-D or documentation-only placebo targets, is not explained by label persistence or repeated-landmark overlap, and is useful only for a bounded manual chart-review allocation task if prespecified held-out decision gates pass.**

The substantive advance over both parents is a single auditable temporal/source contract. It separates (1) a genuinely post-D signal from a pre-D or persistent documentation signal, (2) endpoint-source dependence from cross-source reproducibility, and (3) pooled prediction from an explicit supported-hospital transport estimand. It also makes operational relevance a prospective, fixed-capacity review-allocation question rather than an implied bedside decision. No results are invented.

## Temporal-direction contract and leakage barriers

Before fitting, emit three manifests: `pre_D_baseline.json`, `post_D_endpoint.json`, and `post_D_diagnostic.json`. Every baseline field must use source clinical time `< D`; no entry time may substitute for clinical time. Only M1 contains the exact current label. Care-plan fields other than exposure verification are forbidden everywhere downstream.

Use mutually exclusive blocks with a machine-readable one-to-one feature dictionary:

* `S`: value-derived finite pre-D respiratory state proxies: locked B-window component summaries/trends; valid pre-D airway facts from `respiratoryCare`; finite pre-D `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `respiration` summaries.
* `O`: value-blind opportunity: row counts, distinct clinical timestamps, distinct labels, spacing/window availability, missingness flags, entry-lag summaries, respiratory-care row/interval counts, vital row/timestamp counts and spacing, and treatment row counts.
* `C`: fixed design covariates only: landmark, age category, and explicitly inherited pre-D covariates.

No feature can appear in two blocks. Ambiguous, nonfinite, source-presence, or unvalidated text fields are O or excluded, never S. The label belongs to no block.

Fit deterministic leave-one-hospital-out folds, keeping all landmarks of a stay in one fold and never using hospital ID as a predictor. Train imputation, scaling, calibration, regularization, S/O maps, support decisions, and all cutpoints in training hospitals only. Fit the fixed nested multinomial family `M_C`, `M_O`/`Mopp`, `M_S`, `M_0=C+S+O`, and `M_1=M_0+exact current label`, with fixed low-dimensional logistic specification, grid, solver, tolerance, and deterministic tie-break declared before outcomes are read.

## Source isolation and temporal placebo tests

Create three predeclared views without altering the primary K definition:

1. `ALL`: all permitted sources and fields.
2. `RC`: respiratoryCharting/respiratoryCare state and opportunity fields plus C, excluding vitalPeriodic values.
3. `VP`: vitalPeriodic state and opportunity fields plus C, excluding respiratoryCharting and respiratoryCare value fields; only explicitly permitted value-blind counts/timestamps may remain.

All views use the same folds, nested models, clinical windows, and training-only transformations. `VP` is a provenance stress test against locked K and a separate summary-vital target, not a replacement endpoint. A gain that vanishes in the source-isolated view that removes label-adjacent endpoint opportunity is source-dependent, not state validation. A gain appearing in an independent view is only evidence of reproducible association with named chart constructs, never semantic confirmation.

Freeze and evaluate separately, with no target or post-D field entering predictors:

* `K_pre`: the locked parser trajectory from `P=(D-1440,D-720]` to B, using only pre-D respiratoryCharting clinical time and respiratoryCare airway corroboration. If required states are unsupported, assign `pre_unobservable`, never a negative.
* `O_pre_shift`: value-blind respiratoryCharting opportunity in P, requiring at least two distinct clinical timestamps spanning at least 30 minutes; unavailable is explicit.
* `O_E_RC`, `O_T_RC`: value-blind respiratoryCharting opportunity in E and T, each requiring at least two distinct clinical timestamps.
* `O_E_VP`, `O_T_VP`: value-blind vitalPeriodic opportunity in E and T, each requiring at least two finite rows at distinct offsets spanning at least five minutes.
* `K_future`: a future-anchor placebo with anchor `D_pl=D+720`, label retained as historical exposure, and a newly parsed trajectory in `(D_pl,D_pl+720]` and `(D_pl+720,D_pl+2160]`, with the same parser, exit precedence, and all-row accounting. Original label and future endpoint windows must not overlap silently; overlap, competing exit, and invalid anchor are explicit statuses.

The pre-D and opportunity placebos test temporal specificity and residual opportunity, not clinical outcome validity. A positive `K_pre`, `O_pre_shift`, or future-anchor increment is adverse to the corresponding clean post-D/state interpretation, while a null is merely reassuring. A future-anchor gain specifically blocks a contemporaneous interpretation and is reported as persistence, evolving state, or site process evidence without choosing among them. Cluster all placebo analyses by stay and report first-landmark-only sensitivity; support unavailable or overlap-dominated placebos are inconclusive rather than favorable.

Add a label-persistence audit that never changes the exposure: within each stay, report exact-label recurrence, time from prior same-label row, number of prior landmarks with a label, and whether current label equals the immediately preceding valid label. Compare M1 to M0 within first-landmark-only, no-overlap, and repeated-landmark strata. Do not let historical labels enter M1 unless explicitly designated as a diagnostic persistence feature; if a persistence feature is tested, it is a separate predeclared diagnostic model and cannot support a current-label increment. A current-label gain confined to recurrence or repeated landmarks is adverse to temporal specificity.

## Support, transport estimands, and falsification

Within each training fold, derive S and O scalar scores and deterministic tertile bins (median split, then documented feature-order sparse collapse if needed), crossed with landmark. Apply cutpoints to held-out rows without clipping. A supported cell requires at least 10 rows in each exact arm and at least 20 total, finite training maps, no unresolved block assignment, and explicit row/stay counts. Record held-out in-range, out-of-range, missing, and invalid status by feature and hospital.

Report the frozen all-row micro estimand separately. For transport, define the supported-hospital set `H*` before outcome comparison as hospitals having at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows in each arm and at least one supported S×O×landmark cell. Report two prespecified estimands, not one conflated average:

* `θ_micro(H*)`: row-weighted held-out M1-minus-M0 loss/Brier increment and fixed-denominator K contrasts over supported rows in `H*`.
* `θ_macro(H*) = |H*|^{-1} Σ_{h∈H*} θ_h`: equal-hospital mean of the same fold-local quantities, with each hospital's exact denominators visible.

Also report equal-cell and row-weighted supported-cell means, leave-one-supported-hospital-out influence, exact site directional reversals, label prevalence, and out-of-range contribution. The transport claim is unavailable unless at least five hospitals satisfy the per-arm support rule, represent at least 50% of supported rows, no site contributes over 25% of supported rows, and no more than 25% of supported hospitals reverse the prespecified increment direction. Residual S/O interpretation additionally requires at least five supported joint cells spanning at least five hospitals, both arms, and no cell above 25% of supported rows. Unsupported cells remain unavailable and are never merged, reweighted, or treated as negative evidence.

Preserve the parents' broad hospital-by-landmark opportunity-preserving permutation and complete-stay S/O-conditional label-vector permutation. Use seed `61061`, attempt at least 1,000 valid arrangements when feasible or enumerate all unique arrangements when fewer; assert unchanged stay IDs/signatures, vector lengths, arm counts, hospital membership, landmark/cell counts, and no stay splitting. Fewer than 20 valid global arrangements, fewer than five hospitals, a lost supported cell, or any failed invariant is inconclusive. An observed increment typical of either conditional null is adverse to residual patient-state information.

## Independent physiologic proxy and bounded operational relevance

Keep `vitalPeriodic` separate. Define `H_E` and `H_T` as `{alert,no_alert,physiologic_unobservable}`; opportunity requires two finite observations spanning five minutes, and alert requires at least two valid observations with `sao2 < 88` or `respiration > 30`. These are summary-vital alerts, not hypoxemia, tachypnea, respiratory failure, harm, or adjudicated outcomes.

For the only operational question, compare M0 and M1 probabilities for `Y_K=1{K=classifiable-escalation}`, `Y_HE=1{H_E=alert}`, and `Y_HT=1{H_T=alert}` on applicable supported denominators. The action is manual chart-review queue allocation, with fixed thresholds `0.10` through `0.90` by `0.10`, costs `{0.05,0.10,0.20,0.30,0.50}`, unresolved penalties `{0.25,0.50,1.00}`, unresolved review costs `{0.00,0.05,0.10}`, and capacities `{10%,20%,30%}`. Emit review/no-review/abstain tables against M0, treat-none, treat-all-observed-target, and deterministic fixed-capacity policies. Exits, invalid times, and unobservable outcomes remain unresolved.

Call a target operationally decision-relevant only if held-out M1 improves both `θ_micro` and `θ_macro` loss, has positive incremental net benefit over M0 at two adjacent fixed costs and one capacity, and has a stay-cluster bootstrap interval excluding zero. A stronger triangulated claim requires directional concordance for K and the separate H target in at least two supported overlap hospitals. This is not patient utility, treatment guidance, or a recommendation.

## Uncertainty, interpretation, and fail-closed status

Use fixed-seed `61061` stay-cluster bootstrap, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates. Resample complete first qualifying stays within hospital, carrying all landmarks, labels, predictors, K/H/placebo statuses together; refit fold-local pipelines and recompute support, transport, source views, null-compatible summaries, and review metrics. Never row-bootstrap or nest permutations in bootstrap. Fewer than 500 valid replicates makes uncertainty-dependent claims inconclusive.

Computational invalidity includes any source/schema/catalog/hash/member mismatch; absent/divergent frozen artifacts; duplicate or non-one-to-one joins; wrong exact labels, landmarks, D, parser, thresholds, inequalities, units, K/exit precedence; post-D leakage; duplicated block membership; fold or stay leakage; placebo overlap leakage; failed null invariants; unsupported claimed transport/action gate; or missing artifact-to-conclusion linkage. Emit `verification.status=invalid` and do not interpret estimates.

Supportive results establish only incremental prediction of the named structured K/vital constructs or bounded review prioritization, conditional on the exact artifact, denominator, held-out support, uncertainty interval, source-view persistence, temporal placebo nulls, conditional nulls, and transport gates. Adverse results mean the label is not robust for that construct under this design or is compatible with state, opportunity, site, exit, persistence, or source dependence. Inconclusive results identify failed feasibility, support, source availability, timing, exchangeability, calibration, bootstrap, or verification. No outcome can establish bedside evaluation, extubation intent, readiness, delivered ventilator settings, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The release lacks raw waveforms, synchronized device/workflow logs, local interface dictionaries, validated bedside labels, adjudicated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, post-ICU follow-up, and external prospective replication. The research-ambition references were inspected only within their availability limits; the cancer demonstration's main article and full methods remain unavailable. Expert adjudication, workflow metadata, synchronized device data, validated outcomes, and another prospective or external study are required for stronger clinical claims.
