# Provisional hypothesis checkpoint

Status: provisional; no cohort counts, fitted models, or clinical conclusions are claimed.

## Proposed relationship

Among adults in a first MIMIC-IV ICU stay with a documented AKI episode who remain under observation through a prespecified 48-hour landmark, a creatinine-improved but urine-output-impaired phenotype may have worse subsequent in-hospital death and/or persistent or worsening kidney dysfunction than a concordant creatinine-and-urine-output recovery phenotype. This is a prognostic association, not a causal or mechanistic claim.

## Uncertainties and missing evidence

The strongest rival is a monitoring/measurement artifact: creatinine sampling is clinician-selected, urine output is recorded only when collection/catheterization is present, and fluid balance summarizes treatment and monitoring rather than organ state. Severity and treatment selection are additional rivals. Exact creatinine item IDs/units, urine-output item IDs/units, input item conventions, and the d_labitems/d_items mappings must be verified against source headers before thresholds or cohort counts are interpreted. No bounded cohort feasibility audit has been run.

## Falsifiable test

Using only pre-landmark data, define creatinine improvement and urine-output impairment at the fixed 48-hour landmark, compare discordant versus concordant recovery for post-landmark in-hospital death and a prespecified kidney outcome, and assess incremental information versus a simple adjusted model. A time-aware model using ordered pre-landmark trajectories is a substantive alternative. Support requires a prespecified incremental association with uncertainty and persistence after monitoring-intensity adjustment; disappearance, reversal, or no incremental association is adverse; an imprecise null is inconclusive. Mechanistic or management claims require clinical adjudication or additional data.
