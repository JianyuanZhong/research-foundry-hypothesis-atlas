# Pre-illness creatinine–cystatin C discordance, grip strength, and hospitalized AKI

## Question and hypothesis

Evidence supports that eGFRcys−eGFRcr discordance is common and reflects filtration plus non-GFR determinants [K1], predicts broad adverse outcomes [K2], and can arise when acute muscle loss makes creatinine eGFR overestimate measured GFR [K3]. It does not establish prospective AKI risk in ambulatory adults.

Hypothesis: among UK Biobank participants with baseline 2021 CKD-EPI eGFRcr >=60 mL/min/1.73m2 and no prior inpatient N17 code, a 15-unit lower eGFRcys−eGFRcr predicts greater 10-year cumulative incidence of first hospitalized coded AKI after adjustment for eGFRcr and prespecified confounders; the contrast is larger at lower sex-specific grip strength. “Beyond current filtration” means incremental to eGFRcr, not conditioning simultaneously on eGFRcr, eGFRcys and their algebraic difference.

Resolving this could target cystatin-C testing or monitoring before nephrotoxic treatment/acute illness. It cannot establish causal benefit of testing. Rival explanations are inflammation, adiposity, smoking, diabetes, albuminuria, frailty and hospitalization/coding intensity.

## Estimand, baseline and learned alternative

Primary estimand: standardized 10-year competing-risk difference and ratio comparing delta −15 versus 0 at eGFRcr 90, at sex-specific 25th and 75th grip percentiles; report cause-specific HR and delta×grip interaction with 95% CIs. Death competes.

Baseline Cox sequence: eGFRcr+confounders; add delta (equivalent to adding eGFRcys given eGFRcr); add grip; add delta×grip. Never enter eGFRcr, eGFRcys and delta together. Use splines, Aalen–Johansen/g-formula risks, Fine–Gray sensitivity, bootstrap uncertainty and PH checks. Adjust age, sex, ethnicity, smoking, diabetes, BMI/waist, BP, CRP, albuminuria, glycemia, assessment year/center when verified. Do not impute exposure/outcome/dates; impute covariates only with complete-case sensitivity. Exclude first-year events and vary discordance definitions.

Alternative: same-input competing-risk gradient-boosted survival model, frozen 70/15/15 participant split stratified by AKI/death/sex. ## Falsification, outputs and interpretation

Lock thresholds before fitting. Association is supportive only if the lower 95% bound for adjusted risk difference (delta −15 vs 0) is >0; clinical usefulness additionally requires lower bound >0.5 percentage points plus improved held-out calibration/decision utility. It is adverse if upper bound <=0, or for utility if upper bound <0.5 points and no incremental utility; crossing intervals are inconclusive. Apply the same rule to the low- versus high-grip difference in discordance contrasts.

Required newly computed outputs: attrition/event tables; paired-field checks; eGFR distributions; baseline estimates and standardized risks; interaction; uncertainty; calibration/discrimination/decision metrics; sensitivities; machine-readable conclusion linked to outputs.

Supportive results justify prognostic enrichment research, not causation, “true GFR,” screening benefit or treatment. Adverse results refute stratification/modification but not assay accuracy. Inconclusive results (broad intervals, unstable splits, bad date pairing or censor date) require deferral. Clinical adjudication/additional studies are required for KDIGO AKI/stage, measured GFR, muscle mechanism, testing indication, treatment benefit and external transportability.
