# Residual instability before ICU-to-ward transfer: locked outcome opening and informative order null

Status: substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. No post-transfer outcome model, interaction, worklist result, or learned-model result has been computed. The parent’s exact outcome-blind audit is inherited unchanged.

## Supported premise, unresolved claim, and clinical advance

The strongest evidence supports a bounded premise, not the hypothesis. Longitudinal ICU-discharge models can outperform a snapshot score, while operational factors, treatment limitation, and composite-outcome components complicate interpretation [K1]. A systematic review finds potential value in longitudinal ICU-readmission predictors but substantial heterogeneity and risk of bias [K2]. Observation timing and frequency can themselves predict outcomes because EHR trajectories encode healthcare processes [K3]. The inherited audit additionally establishes that the frozen 49,157-transfer MIMIC-IV cohort is reconstructible and that raw `G` and temporally disjoint `G_pre` have adequate pre-outcome coverage, including 78.71% in 2017–2019 and 75.08% in 2020–2022. It does not establish an outcome association.

The unresolved hypothesis remains: among ICU-to-general-ward transfers with the same measured endpoint state at `t0`, unusually worsening rather than improving recent measured-value history changes calibrated 48-hour ICU-return/death risk and fixed-capacity ranking, especially when endpoint state and direction disagree.

The leading explanation is residual temporally ordered measured-value information. The strongest rivals are shared-measurement coupling/regression to the mean and treatment/documentation dynamics. Answering this would determine whether recent direction warrants consideration when prioritizing a fixed ward or rapid-response review queue. Even a supportive result would not show physiology, preventability, clinician intent, safe transfer, or benefit from monitoring, delayed transfer, or ICU retention.

This child closes the parent’s consequential remaining gap: before any outcome-directed fitting, it freezes a hash-bound feature and permutation package; a restricted stratification step then creates the previously unattached patient split without exposing row labels to feature design; and an outcome-independent informativeness gate determines whether endpoint-fixed permutations can support order attribution.

### Frozen feature formulas

E0 is the inherited ridge multinomial endpoint model: age, sex, admission type, source/last ICU, destination, latest service, ICU LOS, prior ICU count, transfer clock, final valid HR/SBP/MAP/RR/SpO2/temperature/oxygen flow/FiO2/device values with four-hour freshness, final GCS components, ventilation, vasopressor, and code-status state, plus training medians and missing flags. It excludes observation counts, distinct hours, store lag, caregivers, persistence, volatility, slopes, early-late differences, and worst historical values. Cross-fitted development and frozen later predictions define `C=logit[p_E0(R+D)]`.

For each of HR, BP, RR, SpO2, temperature, and oxygen support, convert valid measurements to the inherited abnormality margin (positive=worse), divide by the development IQR, and summarize six left-closed two-hour bins in `[t0-12h,t0)`. BP is the worse standardized SBP/MAP margin; oxygen is the maximum of frozen device-ordinal, FiO2, and flow margins. `G_j` is median `[t0-6,t0)` minus median `[t0-12,t0-6)`; require at least two observed bins in each half and average across at least four domains. `G_pre,j` is median `[t0-8,t0-4)` minus median `[t0-12,t0-8)`; require both two-hour bins in each half and at least four domains. E0-4 uses endpoint values only from `[t0-4,t0)`; a row at exactly `t0-4` enters E0-4 and never G-pre. `G_perp` is defined below. No last observation is carried backward.

## Effective-permutation contract

The inherited positional gate is acknowledged as tautological: raw-G eligibility already guarantees at least three movable nonfinal bins in four domains. It is not evidence that order randomization changes `G`.

Before labels, compute for every raw-G row:

- number of domains with at least two distinct nonfinal abnormality-margin summaries, treating values within `1e-8 × development IQR` as equal;
- number of unique `G_perm` values among 200 under the same tolerance;
- `SD_perm(G)`, scaled by development `SD(G)`;
- fraction of permutations with `|G_perm-G_obs| > 0.05 × SD_development(G)`;
- fraction exactly unchanged, reversal displacement, and the 5th/50th/95th percentiles of `G_perm-G_obs`.

A row is effectively perturbable only if at least four domains contain distinct nonfinal values, at least 10 unique `G_perm` values occur, scaled `SD_perm(G)>=0.05`, and at least 50% of permutations move G by more than `0.05×SD_development(G)`. The structural order-null gate passes only if at least 70% of raw-G rows are effectively perturbable in development, validation, pooled test, 2017–2019, and 2020–2022. Also report per-seed RMS displacement, changed-row fraction, observed/permuted G correlation, and reversal behavior; these are diagnostics, not tuning targets.

After labels open, report the full 200-seed null distributions and effective tail resolution, observed-minus-median-null estimates, paired subject-bootstrap intervals, and interval widths for interaction `I`, Brier gain, and quota capture. In each bootstrap replicate, resample subjects once, recompute the observed metric and all 200 null metrics on that same sample, then subtract the median null metric. The null is inferentially informative for support only when the lower bounds for interaction and Brier contrasts exceed zero and at least one supported quota's lower bound reaches 2 additional R+D events per 1,000 reviewed; it is informative against material order benefit when Brier's upper bound is <0.002 and every quota's upper bound is <2 per 1,000, with interaction/reversal behavior satisfying the coupling-adverse rule. If the structural gate fails, or if intervals include both material benefit and harm, order attribution is inconclusive. L2-D cannot rescue an ineffective null.

## Transparent residual-direction baseline

Construct `Z` exactly as inherited: six endpoint abnormality margins and freshness/missing flags, frozen E0 score `C`, destination, service, ICU source, transfer clock, ICU LOS, observed-bin counts, distinct hours, store-lag summaries, caregiver count, and frozen ventilation/vasopressor/oxygen transitions.

Fit `m(Z)=E(G|Z)` without using R+D as the nuisance target. Compare:

- an additive ridge model with standardized continuous terms, one-hot categorical terms, and the frozen spline bases; and
- a depth-3 histogram gradient-boosted regressor.

Use five subject-grouped development folds fixed by SHA-256(`"gperp-fold|20260923|subject_id"`) modulo 5. The nuisance ridge grid is `alpha in {1e-4,1e-3,1e-2,1e-1,1,10,100,1000,10000}`. The histogram-boosting grid is the Cartesian product `learning_rate in {0.03,0.1}`, `max_iter in {100,300}`, `l2_regularization in {0,1}`, and `min_samples_leaf in {20,100}`, always with `max_depth=3`; all random seeds are 20260923. These exact grids are committed in `model_contract.json`. Select the lowest mean RMSE model; if its mean is within one standard error of ridge's mean, select ridge. Use out-of-fold development nuisance predictions and one frozen development fit thereafter. Define `G_perp=(G-m_hat(Z))/SD_development_residual`. The R+D label is not used to choose the nuisance model, although `C` is explicitly an outcome-trained conditioning score.

The balance gate remains: in validation and every evaluation role, absolute standardized slope on the nuisance prediction <0.05, absolute Spearman correlation with `C` <0.05, and no endpoint/process block explains >1% additional residual variance. Failure is inconclusive nuisance control, not evidence for endpoint sufficiency.

Fit transparent `D1-perp = E0 + restricted-cubic-spline(G_perp) + C×G_perp` as the frozen ridge multinomial model. Use the same alpha grid above; select alpha by validation multinomial log loss after development fitting, with larger alpha winning exact ties. Knots are development 10th/50th/90th percentiles; communication contrasts use frozen development 30th/70th percentiles. It reports the sign/size of unusual direction at matched endpoint and the exact continuous interaction, Brier, calibration, and worklist shifts. Raw D1 and D1-pre remain mandatory comparators.

For every one of the 200 nulls, recompute the corresponding residual direction through the already selected nuisance model, refit the transparent outcome pipeline on permuted development data under the frozen penalty protocol, calibrate without changing model class, and evaluate the matching permutation in each unopened role. Thus the null includes fitting variability rather than merely permuting a fitted coefficient.

## Matched two-stream learned alternative

L2-D addresses the same question on the same raw-G-eligible transfers, labels, role map, endpoint context, and two-hour grid. Primary D1-perp versus L2-D comparisons use this common analysis set; broader L2-D coverage is secondary and cannot support the direction estimand.

The frozen two-stream causal TCN uses 12 final-24-hour two-hour bins:

- value stream: standardized observed/forward-filled values, with no mask/gap channels;
- process stream: masks, deltas, counts, strict store-lag, caregiver counts, and recorded support transitions, with no measured values;
- static stream: the same E0 context.

Retain two causal convolution layers, kernel 3, dilations 1/2, channels in {32,64}, dropout in {0,0.2}, and Adam learning rate in {1e-3,3e-4}. Use batch size 256, at most 100 epochs, validation multiclass log-loss early stopping with patience 10, and training seeds 20260923–20260927. Choose architecture by mean validation log loss across seeds, breaking ties toward 32 channels, dropout 0, and learning rate 1e-3 in that order; fit one scalar temperature on validation logits. Fit full, value+static, process+static, and static-only heads to the same raw three-state target. Report the matched adjudicated first-transition extension.

The alternative can reveal nonlinear late relapse, cross-domain lag, and opposing-domain patterns that scalar G-perp loses. For order falsification, apply the exact endpoint-fixed mappings to the value stream while leaving process/static streams unchanged. The transparent pipeline is refit for all 200 nulls. For bounded GPU cost, L2-D is refit on the first 20 prespecified null seeds for each selected architecture and one training seed; all 200 input-level diagnostics still apply. These 20 null fits are an ablation, not a substitute for the 200-null transparent attribution test.

Prefer L2-D only if it is calibrated and seed-stable, improves over D1-perp in both locked late eras on the common set, and its full/value gain exceeds both process-only and endpoint-fixed null heads. Otherwise prefer D1-perp. A latent-state/domain-adversarial model remains deferred because no independent physiology, policy, staffing, or transfer-intent signal identifies what such a representation removes; external-site or policy-change evidence would justify revisiting it.

## Prespecified interpretation

**Supportive.** All parent support/calibration/endpoint/transport rules pass; raw D1 is supportive; D1-perp has the prespecified concordant interaction with a 95% interval excluding zero in pooled test and both late eras; observed-minus-median-null interaction and Brier gain have positive paired intervals; observed quota capture exceeds the null by at least 2 R+D events per 1,000 reviewed at a parent-supported quota without overall capture loss; reversal opposes or materially attenuates the interaction; and either G-pre corroborates in pooled test or raw/G-perp persist in both no-support-change and observation-stable analyses. Learned value/full evidence, if claimed, must not be reproduced by process-only or permuted histories. This supports residual ordered measured-value information and consequential historical re-ranking only.

**Coupling-adverse.** Raw D1 looks favorable, but the informative endpoint-fixed null reproduces it: observed-minus-null interaction includes or favors zero, the 95% upper bound for Brier advantage is <0.002, reversal does not attenuate, and the upper bound for quota advantage is <2 events per 1,000 at every quota. If D1-perp also excludes the inherited material thresholds, the specified raw interaction is adequately explained by unordered repeated values plus endpoint coupling/mean reversion under this design. Do not rescue it with L2-D.

**Process-adverse.** The measured-value signal is absent or reversed in both no-support-change and observation-stable rows, while process-only or active-treatment patterns reproduce calibration, interaction, and worklist shifts. Report a treatment/documentation-associated predictor and reject residual-instability attribution. This still does not identify clinician intent or treatment effect.

**Inconclusive.** The structural or inferential permutation gate fails; G-perp balance fails; G-pre coverage falls below 70%; intervals include material benefit and harm; raw and residual directions conflict without a valid null explanation; late-era support/calibration fails; endpoint analyses disagree; or L2-D seeds are unstable. A G-pre null alone is not refutation. No post-opening resplit, new subgroup, altered window, relaxed domain, changed permutation, or threshold search is allowed.

Endpoint sufficiency and simple adequacy retain the inherited rules. Negative findings are valid outcomes. None of the four interpretations establishes mechanism or intervention benefit.
