# Cumulative hypotension duration beyond nadir as a predictor of incident AKI in eICU

Status: proposed Harbor experiment; no full study result has been computed.

## Scientific opening and hypothesis

The expert seed asks whether, at the same minimum blood pressure, longer-lasting hypotension is followed by more acute kidney injury (AKI). Inspected evidence makes a narrower claim: temporal hypotension trajectories have been associated with postoperative complications [K1], and KDIGO supplies creatinine-based AKI criteria [K2]. A randomized-target evidence synthesis shows that prognostic hypotension information cannot be interpreted as proof that raising a BP target improves AKI [K3].

Hypothesis H1: among adult eICU ICU stays with adequate early blood-pressure and creatinine ascertainment, cumulative time below MAP 65 mmHg has incremental, transportable prognostic information for a new creatinine-defined AKI event 6–48 hours after the exposure window, beyond nadir MAP, baseline/current severity, hospital, and BP/lab observation coverage.

Strong rival R1: duration is a proxy for current severity, measurement density, or treatment response. Sicker patients may have lower nadirs, longer low-MAP periods, more BP and creatinine measurements, more vasopressor changes, and more AKI. A residual association is descriptive; it does not prove renal hypoperfusion, causality, or a treatment threshold.

Clinical importance: if H1 survives these challenges, duration could improve how hypotensive episodes are summarized and how future monitoring or BP trials stratify risk. If it fails, clinicians should not treat duration as a portable renal-risk signal beyond nadir and severity. Either result changes the next study decision.

## Population, time and outcomes

Use only eICU; never join identifiers to HCC or MIMIC. Select first ICU stay per eICU uniquepid to prevent recurrent-stay leakage, with patientunitstayid as the within-eICU join key. Adults are age >=18. Index is ICU admission (unitadmitoffset=0). The primary exposure window is minutes 0–360. Require at least 12 valid BP bins and >=240 minutes from first to last covered bin; report all exclusions and never code absent observations as normotension. Exclude pre-index AKI or dialysis only for the incident-risk primary analysis; retain them in sensitivity/competing-event analyses.

Exposure is duration below MAP 65 in covered 5-minute bins, with no interpolation across gaps >10 minutes, plus nadir MAP, low-MAP area, number of episodes, first-low offset, observation count, median gap, covered-minutes fraction and BP modality. Primary valid range is MAP 20–200 mmHg; sensitivity 30–180. Use systemicmean from vitalPeriodic preferentially when both modalities occupy a bin and retain a modality flag; otherwise use noninvasivemean from vitalAperiodic. Threshold sensitivities are 60 and 70 mmHg and common-density resampling.

Primary outcome is new creatinine-defined AKI from 6–48 hours after index: >=0.3 mg/dL increase from the last valid pre-index creatinine in -24–0 minutes. Deduplicate revised lab results at the same clinical offset using the latest labresultrevisedoffset. A flagged sensitivity uses a first 0–6-hour creatinine baseline; it is never mixed invisibly with the primary cohort. Secondary outcome is >=1.5 times baseline within 7 ICU days. Audit urine output before claiming a full KDIGO endpoint; use intakeOutput and valid admission weight only if labels, units and timing pass readiness. Death, discharge, transfer, and missing follow-up are competing/censoring events, not automatic non-AKI. Report ascertainment, censoring and competing-risk/IPCW sensitivity.

## Analysis and method comparison

B0, the simple baseline, is a regularized logistic or discrete-time risk model with nadir MAP, initial MAP/HR, pre-index creatinine and missingness/source flags, age, sex, ethnicity, admission source/ICU type, hospital and prespecified process controls (BP coverage, count, median gap, modality). B0 excludes duration and all post-index variables.

B1 adds duration_low65, low-time fraction, number of episodes and first-low offset, using a restricted spline and fixed-nadir bands. The primary estimand is the held-out difference in log loss/Brier score and calibration between B1 and B0; AUROC/AUPRC are secondary. Use 60/20/20 hospital-level train/validation/test assignment with fixed seed, all stays for one uniquepid in one split, or repeated grouped-hospital cross-validation if hospital counts are inadequate. Bootstrap uncertainty is clustered by hospital and report leave-one-hospital-out heterogeneity.

Substantive learned alternative M uses the same 0–6-hour 5-minute sequence: MAP, HR, respiration, BP modality, pressor rates/indicators, fluid/output summaries only if semantics pass, and missingness/time-since-last-observation masks. Target is the same 6–48-hour AKI label, with 7-day AKI and competing events secondary. Fit a small GRU or temporal convolution (two layers, width 32–64, dropout, calibrated logistic head) on training hospitals with early stopping on validation hospitals. The information test is whether persistence, recovery slope, repeated episodes, oscillations or BP-treatment coupling reproducibly add signal after nadir is fixed—not whether a complex model wins by a tiny AUROC margin. GPU is optional; CPU tabular fits are expected, and one allocated A100 is reasonable only if the sequence fit materially benefits. Discovery probing has measured header-read time but not full compressed parsing; future resource estimates are unverified and must be measured by the solver.

Unchosen alternatives: latent trajectory classes are deferred because class count/shape can become exploratory; a renal state-space model is deferred because eICU lacks perfusion pressure, renal oxygenation, congestion imaging and adjudicated tubular biomarkers; a large transformer is deferred because it does not test a distinct scientific uncertainty. Revisit if M is robust but unstable in pattern attribution, or if those missing measurements are later supplied.

## Falsification and interpretation

Pre-register: within-hospital permutation of duration/trajectory within nadir bins; common-density thinning; BP modality strata; thresholds 60/65/70; pre-index negative-control creatinine-rise label; post-exposure observation/lab count as process controls; treatment-masked, treatment-only and vasopressor-adjusted ablations; and strict leakage audit excluding post-index labs, discharge, EOL and actual outcome fields.

Supportive: B1 improves held-out Brier/log loss and calibration over B0 with uncertainty excluding no improvement; duration-risk is monotone within fixed nadir bands; the result persists after process/treatment adjustment, common-density resampling and hospital holdout; M finds reproducible trajectory information. This supports a portable prognostic summary only.

Adverse: duration becomes null/non-monotone after adjustment, is confined to low-density/high-monitoring patients or one hospital, or disappears with treatment adjustment. This supports R1 and argues against duration as a robust renal-risk quantity.

Inconclusive: wide intervals, poor creatinine coverage, unstable hospital holdout or exposure-dependent censoring. This is not refutation; seek label adjudication/independent validation or defer to descriptive BP-process analysis.

## Required clinical limits and deliverable

The future solver must newly fit B0, B1 and M and emit cohort flow, label ascertainment, held-out predictions, coefficient/spline estimates, calibration and uncertainty tables, fixed-nadir duration curves, hospital heterogeneity and all falsification outputs. Readiness is not a solved result.

eICU lacks clinician-adjudicated AKI, reliable pre-ICU kidney function, complete fluid balance, arterial-line calibration, renal replacement indication, clinician intent, raw waveforms, staffing/bed context and independent external validation. It cannot establish causality, mechanism, a renal autoregulatory threshold, or that treating duration prevents AKI. Nephrology/critical-care review and a prospective or external validation study are required for those claims.

The ten imported eICU seeds were assessed repairable. Deferred alternatives are hospital vasopressor heterogeneity, testing-process transportability, hyperoxia, ventilatory burden, nighttime discharge, transfers, reduced testing, insulin/nutrition hypoglycemia and hospital recovery speed; each needs its own verified outcome/binding audit.
