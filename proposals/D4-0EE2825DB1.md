# Executed and independently audited continuous cystatin-C rescue after the adverse binary result

## Lineage, question, and strongest supported claim

This is an executed child of `[prior hypothesis]`, which preregistered one continuous follow-up after the adverse binary Direction-A result in `[prior hypothesis]`. No frozen population, time boundary, comparator, added feature, estimand, uncertainty procedure, decision gate, or evidence limit was changed.

The unresolved hypothesis was: **among the same selected qualifying UK Biobank first-repeat attenders with baseline creatinine eGFR at least 60, does the single continuous feature `Z = log(eGFRcys_0/eGFRcr_0)` add reproducible held-out information beyond the unchanged flexible creatinine comparator and improve outcome concentration at the inherited 10% capacity without exceeding operational calibration tolerances?** This matters because the failed binary threshold could have discarded clinically relevant ordering within both sides of 60. A successful result could have justified external replication of continuous discordance for follow-up triage, but could not have rehabilitated the binary feature.

The experiment is complete and the frozen classification is **inconclusive continuous rescue**. The strongest supported result is narrower: the continuous term produced a repeat-stable, conditionally precise held-out log-loss reduction and acceptable calibration under this internal protocol, but it did not establish improved 10% outcome concentration, did not attain the frozen +5-outcome operational floor, and was not positive in at least 8 of 10 capacity repeats. There was no prespecified contradiction sufficient for an adverse classification. Therefore this result supports incremental probabilistic information in the exact selected cohort but does not support the joint information-and-capacity rescue claim.

## Frozen models and execution checks

Each basis used training-fold 25th, 50th, and 75th percentile knots, training minimum/maximum boundaries, a centered constraint, and training-only column scaling. ## Frozen-gate conclusion and falsification

The joint hypothesis is **inconclusive**, because supportive classification required every information, capacity, calibration, stability, and computation gate to pass. Information, calibration, and computation passed, but capacity did not. No adverse condition was established. This completed result must not be reframed as supportive based on log loss alone, nor as adverse merely because capacity was uncertain and below the operational floor.

The result materially advances the parent evidence by separating two claims that the failed binary feature could not resolve:

- Supported under this exact same-snapshot internal protocol: the one-degree-of-freedom continuous log-ratio contains reproducible held-out probabilistic information beyond the inherited comparator.
- Still unsupported: that this information concentrates meaningfully more repeat low-eGFR outcomes into a fixed 10% follow-up capacity.

A stronger claim about triage usefulness requires a new externally replicated, deployable-horizon decision study with outcome ascertainment not conditioned on future attendance, clinically justified utility thresholds and costs, and preferably measured filtration or adjudicated longitudinal CKD outcomes. This analysis cannot establish causal benefit from cystatin-C testing, biological mechanism, assay traceability, chronic kidney disease, transport to nonattenders, or clinical utility.
