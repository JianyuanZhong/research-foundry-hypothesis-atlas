# HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B validation with a fail-closed surrogate-to-decision translation audit

## Episode 79 targeted child

This is a substantive, targeted child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE population, selected-event construction, assay-specific B/P/event/Y clocks, annual forward validation, locked nested ridge models, all-BP training-range partial-identification layer, and noncausal boundary. It does not change the population, temporal boundaries, estimand, model covariates, outcome definition, or primary fit.

The parent answers a narrow surrogate-assay question: whether contemporaneous pre-repeat assay P adds observed held-out prediction of the subsequent same-assay assay Y beyond remote baseline B and the locked pre-event covariates. The unresolved clinically important question is different:

> Even if P robustly predicts the near-term laboratory surrogate Y, is there any computable evidence in this HCC source that the signal is connected to a clinically consequential decision or patient-important outcome, rather than merely to assay persistence and recording patterns?

The proposed child makes that translation claim explicitly falsifiable without pretending that unavailable outcomes or treatment administration exist. It adds a **surrogate-to-decision translation audit** with three outputs kept strictly separate:

1. a primary unchanged prognostic result and missing-Y envelope;
2. an outcome-blind and temporally downstream audit of recorded decision-related activity, explicitly labeled as a workflow/recording proxy; and
3. a fail-closed `clinical_translation_status` that can be supportive only as a statement about computable evidence availability, never as evidence of benefit, utility, treatment response, or patient outcome.

The key advance over the parent is not another claim that downstream records equal care. It is an explicit decision-relevance test: the assay signal is not called clinically translatable unless a prespecified patient-important endpoint and validated assay units/interpretation are available. With this snapshot those requirements are absent, so the result is expected to be `translation_not_identifiable` even if the prognostic analysis is favorable. The computable proxy audit identifies exactly what additional prospective linkage would be needed and can falsify the weaker operational premise that assay observation is connected to subsequent recorded decision-related activity.

## Evidence-supported and untested claims

The strongest available evidence is the retained parent full-source audit, which reproduced the frozen reconstruction and identified one adequately supported future block (2022), with severe later loss of post-event assay observation and sparse systemic-record cells. It supports at most feasibility of a selected, short-horizon, noncausal prognostic comparison in the local supported calendar mixture. It does not establish that P improves a patient-important endpoint, that P changes a decision, that treatment was administered, or that the laboratory measurements have comparable clinical units or reference ranges. The parent partial-identification calculations remain planned unless separately executed; no new HCC result is claimed here.

The untested claim in this child is deliberately bounded:

> Conditional on the same outcome-blind BP-eligible episodes, assay-observed versus assay-missing states and pre-Y opportunity strata may differ in the subsequent pattern of *recorded* decision-related activity; if so, this can motivate a prospective implementation and outcomes study, but cannot establish that the assay caused a decision, that the decision was appropriate, or that a patient benefited.

A second, stronger claim is deliberately made non-estimable:

> Whether the P-beyond-B prediction increment improves treatment selection, survival, hepatic decompensation, tumor control, quality of life, or net clinical utility cannot be answered from this snapshot.

That explicit non-identifiability is a substantive falsification safeguard, not a failure to report a favorable association as clinical benefit.

## Translation audit: decision-related records without decision claims

### Translation target and unit

The unit is one selected repeat-TACE event × one assay among all `BP_eligible` rows, with one row per patient-assay-event after deterministic duplicate/tie handling. The assay state is `Y_observed` versus `Y_missing`; the pre-Y opportunity category is inherited and formed before reading Y using event-to-72-hour data only. The audit is secondary and descriptive.

Define two temporal phases:

* **Assay phase:** `(event_time, event_time+72 hours]`, used only for the inherited Y observation/completion proxy and opportunity indicators.
* **Decision-record phase:** `(event_time+72 hours, event_time+30 calendar days]`, which starts strictly after the complete Y window and therefore cannot leak into Y, P, model fitting, or bounds.

For each assay, origin/block, arm, Y state, and inherited opportunity stratum, report the following source-specific recorded indicators, retaining every BP-eligible episode in the denominator:

1. `recorded_additional_encounter`: an additional distinct (`Patient Master Index`,`Encounter Number`) encounter with valid `Encounter Time` in the decision-record phase; the selected event encounter is excluded.
2. `recorded_same_assay_repeat`: an exact `laboratory test` match to the assay (`albumin` or `total bilirubin`) with valid `laboratory test time` in the phase. Report this separately from `recorded_any_lab`; it is a record of a laboratory row, not evidence of monitoring adequacy or a clinically indicated repeat.
3. `recorded_any_lab`: any linked lab row with valid `Test Time`, with no clinical interpretation of its result.
4. `recorded_examination`: any linked examination row with valid `start time`; use row presence only and never narrative findings.
5. `recorded_nonmed_order`: any linked `orders` row with valid `order time`, else valid `start time`, in the phase. This is not a mapped treatment or test order.
6. `recorded_medication_entry`: any linked medication row with valid `start time` or `end time` in the phase. This is not administration, adherence, intent, dose received, toxicity treatment, or response.
7. `recorded_procedure_entry`: any linked procedure row with valid `Start Time`; retain a separate exact TACE-like text flag only under the inherited parser. This is not proof of TACE delivery or a clinical decision.
8. `recorded_document_on_additional_encounter`: a nonempty clinical-document row linked to an additional encounter whose valid encounter time falls in the phase. The document itself has no time and supplies no rationale.
9. `any_recorded_decision_related_activity`: the OR of items 1–8, reported only as a composite recording indicator and never as a clinical endpoint.

Report first valid timestamp and count of distinct linked composite keys per component, but label all counts as source-record counts. Do not use downstream records to fill B/P/Y or any covariate. A missing row is not evidence of no care; external care and administrative truncation remain possible.

### Prespecified translation contrasts and falsification

For each component, calculate only descriptive completion/recording fractions and absolute risk differences between Y-observed and Y-missing rows within each supported stratum. Report Wilson intervals when estimable, exact denominators, and patient-cluster bootstrap intervals only as descriptive uncertainty (seed and replicate count recorded; 2,000 where resources permit). Do not adjust, weight, mediate, or call these contrasts causal. Suppress/mark cells with fewer than five BP episodes or fewer than two patient clusters.

The audit has two predeclared falsification tests.

**Falsification A: assay-persistence versus broader-recording test.** A P signal that is only a laboratory-record persistence artifact should show, at most, a difference in `recorded_same_assay_repeat` without a reproducible difference in `recorded_additional_encounter`, `recorded_examination`, or `recorded_any_recorded_decision_related_activity`. This is a diagnostic pattern, not a formal proof of artifact. Conversely, a difference in broad recorded activity does not establish clinical relevance because access, billing, transfer, documentation, and illness severity are unmeasured.

**Falsification B: negative-control timing test.** Recompute the same component vector in a pre-event placebo phase of equal duration, ` (event_time-30 calendar days, event_time-2 days]`, excluding the B/P windows and selected event encounter. The placebo phase is not an outcome and cannot enter the primary models. If a purported post-Y recording contrast is at least as large and directionally inconsistent in this pre-event phase, label the operational bridge `temporal-artifact-sensitive`; this weakens any interpretation that the post-event pattern is specifically linked to the assay window. The negative control does not establish exchangeability, causality, or clinical utility.

For all phases, use the same source-level timestamp validity, deduplicated existence joins, and administrative coverage checks. The placebo is intentionally outcome-blind with respect to Y and uses no narrative text.

### Translation gate and utility contract

Emit a separate machine-readable `clinical_translation_status` with the following precedence:

* `unsupported`: source/hash/schema/join/clock assertion fails, or the requested denominator cannot be reconstructed.
* `inconclusive_proxy`: gates fail, cells are sparse, administrative coverage is incomplete, assay-specific directions disagree, or the placebo negative control is artifact-sensitive. A proxy contrast is not interpretable as a workflow bridge.
* `proxy_difference_only`: a reproducible post-event recorded-pattern difference survives the negative-control check, but the source lacks validated units/reference ranges, decision intent, administration/completion, and patient-important outcomes. This status may justify a prospective workflow/outcomes study only.
* `proxy_null_or_unstable`: no reproducible post-event recording difference or directions vary; this falsifies the weaker operational bridge but does not prove equivalent care or absence of outside care.
* `clinical_translation_not_identifiable`: the primary prognostic result may be favorable, but no patient-important endpoint, validated assay interpretation, treatment administration/intent, or utility inputs are available. This status is mandatory for any clinical-benefit or actionability question in this snapshot.

`clinical_translation_not_identifiable` dominates any favorable proxy label when a report asks whether P should guide treatment or improves outcomes. A robust positive all-BP Delta can only support robustness of a surrogate prediction under its mathematical training-range scenario; it cannot override this translation gate.

Do not construct a utility score, net benefit, decision curve, treatment threshold, or risk-benefit tradeoff. Such quantities require treatment options actually received, administration and adherence, harms, resource/cost weights, patient preferences, and a patient-important outcome. None is available here. Do not convert numeric albumin or bilirubin values into “normal/abnormal,” severity, liver reserve, or eligibility because no units or reference ranges are present and assay comparability is unverified. Do not interpret P, Y, or their changes as treatment response, toxicity, tumor response, or survival risk beyond the narrow statistical prediction estimand.

## What supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results mean

* **Supportive for the frozen surrogate association:** reconstruction and clock assertions pass; a supported block has an estimable prespecified G; the all-BP Delta is strictly positive throughout the declared training-range scenario; and no material ascertainment warning appears. This supports only selected short-horizon prediction of Y, not a clinical decision or benefit.
* **Surrogate falsified/adverse:** an adequately supported primary G is nonpositive, M1 repeatedly degrades, or the all-BP Delta is wholly nonpositive. This is adverse for incremental P-beyond-B prediction regardless of any recorded-care pattern.
* **Selection-sensitive:** observed complete-case G is favorable but the all-BP interval crosses zero, tipping displacement is near a support boundary, observed-versus-missing SMDs are material, or assay/calendar/arm observation differs. The surrogate claim remains selection-sensitive.
* **Operational bridge falsified:** post-event component differences are absent, unstable, or no larger than the pre-event placebo contrast. This weakens the notion that assay observation is connected to a distinct recorded workflow, but does not establish equivalent clinical care.
* **Proxy-only operational signal:** post-event recorded activity differs reproducibly and passes the negative-control check, but the result remains `proxy_difference_only` and `clinical_translation_not_identifiable`; it cannot support a treatment recommendation.
* **Inconclusive:** failed support gates, sparse cells, nonfinite timestamps or predictions, incomplete administrative coverage, low transport/observation ESS, influential patient clusters, unsupported training range, or dependence on the full-source range. Favorable local 2022 evidence cannot establish later-era transport.

Interpretation precedence is fail-closed: unsupported, then inconclusive, then surrogate adverse/selection-sensitive/robust labels, with the translation status appended. No proxy result can rescue a zero-crossing missing-Y envelope or upgrade an adverse primary result.
