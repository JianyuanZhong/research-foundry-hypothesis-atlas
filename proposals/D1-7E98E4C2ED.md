# Independent actionability and superseding-plan reference layer for pulmonary-nodule routing

## Decision question and targeted advance

The frozen decision is whether a support-qualified admission-level `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` route can be nominated for a prospective **silent** validation in the MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity population. This remains a retrospective audit of observable text and workflow proxies. It is not a test of guideline correctness, clinician performance, patient safety, follow-up completion, or clinical benefit.

The assessed parent already protects reproducibility with a first-eligible all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal pre-departure chronology, one canonical discharge artifact, exhaustive recommendation-unit states, split evidence views, an all-unit worst-case route, a pre-label support atlas, a roster-disjoint holistic challenge, perturbation controls, finite-population adverse bounds, and a strict noncausal ceiling. Those safeguards answer whether route-defining text labels can be reproduced and whether panels are sensitive to locked textual perturbations. They do not answer the more consequential decision question: **does a route correspond to a distinguishable, discharge-time intervention state, and is there an explicit later plan that supersedes the recommendation?** Several reviewers can agree that a recommendation exists while an actual discharge plan is absent, clinically inactive, contradicted, or too ambiguous to automate.

This child adds a genuinely independent, blinded clinical-actionability and superseding-plan reference layer. Its panels do not validate a route, vote on a route, see parent component labels, see the holistic challenge labels, or receive the support atom, sampling weight, or expected answer. They review independently assembled packets under new codebooks and produce two separate reference dimensions: (1) whether the available discharge artifact contains enough explicit information to nominate a *text-defined intervention mode*, and (2) whether an explicit later plan supersedes, modifies, or conflicts with the recommendation. The route is calculated only afterward by locked code from compatible sets. The reference layer is a separate measurement system, not a clinical gold standard; ambiguity is retained and propagated adversely.

## Supported and unresolved claims

The strongest claim supported by available data is that MIMIC provides admission-linked radiology notes, detail fields, discharge notes, admission/disposition/death fields, service-transfer proxies, ICU exposure, and note chart/store times from which a reproducible retrospective opportunity and route can be constructed. It also supports an independent textual review of whether a plan is explicitly present, whether a later text assertion supersedes it, and whether the packet contains the minimum observable fields required for a silent workflow nomination.

MIMIC does **not** establish that an intended action was clinically appropriate, that a responsible clinician saw or accepted it, that a patient received it, that an outside plan was absent, that the plan was communicated, or that an intervention improved care. A clinician reference label from these artifacts cannot prove any of those claims.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is narrower: among support-qualified atoms, does an independently reviewed route correspond to a reliably separable discharge actionability state, with `AUTO` restricted to explicit non-superseded plans, `RECON` reserved for active but reconciliation-dependent plans, and `DEFER` used whenever the artifact cannot distinguish an actionable plan from an inactive, superseded, or unknown state?

Primary hypothesis, frozen before labels:

> Every atom admitted to an `AUTO` or `RECON` allow-list will satisfy the inherited reproducibility gates and the independent reference-layer gates. In particular, no claimed `AUTO` atom will have an adverse-compatible high probability of requiring reconciliation, being superseded/contradicted, or being unknown; claimed `RECON` atoms will be distinguishable from `AUTO` by an active-but-reconciliation-compatible state; and atoms with unresolved actionability or superseding status will remain `DEFER`. A supported atom that fails this test is excluded even if every text panel agrees.

This is a nomination rule for a prospective silent test, not an assertion that the nominated intervention is clinically beneficial.

## Independent packet construction and firewalls

Reference packets are generated directly from the sealed raw source extract by a staff member or process that did not construct parent component labels, H1/H2/H3 holistic labels, or the route. The actionability and superseding panels have disjoint rosters from every inherited panel and from each other, independent packet ordering, independent pseudonyms, and independently hashed packets. They never see component labels, holistic labels, routes, route strata, atom identity, support status, sampling weights, expected labels, perturbation type, or post-departure text. Their codebooks are written independently of the parent's codebooks and are frozen and hashed before any packet is generated.

The actionability lane receives a whole-admission packet containing the reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, canonical DS text and permitted DS detail fields, permitted admission/death/disposition/time metadata, and explicit missingness indicators. It must not receive extracted target spans or a parent route. Reviewers answer only a new operational question: *from this artifact alone, what intervention mode, if any, could a prospective silent workflow test without silently inventing a target, interval, recipient, or active plan?*

The superseding lane receives an independently assembled packet with the same allowed raw evidence but a different order and layout, with recommendation and discharge-plan sections interleaved by original chronology rather than by parent unit. It asks only whether an explicit later statement replaces, modifies, conflicts with, or leaves unaddressed the earlier recommendation. It does not receive the actionability panel output. A third senior reference reader, roster-disjoint and blinded to both outputs and all routes, handles only pre-specified boundary/unknown cases; it cannot delete disagreement or form a favorable consensus. All raw panel outputs remain in the analysis.

To make the layer independent in decision target, not merely duplicated, neither panel is asked to label `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`. They label observable plan properties and a compatibility set. A route is computed only after their append-only seals by deterministic code. Panel agreement is a measurement diagnostic and never a clinical truth claim.

## Reference codebooks and adverse-compatible states

The actionability panel records, for each independently identified recommendation candidate and for the whole admission:

* `A1 explicit-propagatable`: target identity, intended action, relevant timing/interval, and an observable recipient/ownership cue are all explicit in the supplied artifact; no unresolved contradiction or target collision is present;
* `A2 active-but-reconciliation`: an active concern or intended action is explicit, but at least one target, timing, recipient, or conflict field needs clinician reconciliation before structured propagation;
* `A3 non-actionable/inactive`: the artifact explicitly closes, cancels, declines, or otherwise makes the recommendation inactive, or contains a completed/irrelevant action with no residual discharge action;
* `A4 indeterminate`: missing, contradictory, temporally ambiguous, or insufficient text prevents distinguishing A1–A3; and
* `A5 no-observable-plan`: a recommendation may be present but no discharge-time action is explicitly linked to it. This is not assumed safe or inactive.

The superseding panel independently records:

* `S0 none observed`: no explicit later plan supersedes or conflicts with the recommendation in the available pre-departure chain;
* `S1 explicit supersession`: a later clinician-authored text statement explicitly replaces, cancels, resolves, or changes the recommendation;
* `S2 competing plan/conflict`: a later plan exists but its relationship or priority is not explicit, or two plans remain competing;
* `S3 timing-only modification`: the plan is explicitly modified in timing/interval but remains active; and
* `S4 unknown`: chronology, missingness, or language prevents a determination.

These labels are descriptive evidence states, not judgments of appropriateness. Each reader may return a set of compatible states, not forced single labels. Boundary, missingness, target identity ambiguity, and responsibility ambiguity are always retained. A missing note or missing field is compatible with the adverse state, never with `A1` or `S0` by default.

The independent lane also records a minimal, pre-specified actionability tuple `(target, action, timing, recipient-cue, active/inactive polarity, replacement/conflict, evidence completeness)`, but it does not use the parent's target parser or unit IDs. One-to-many and duplicate-restatement possibilities are retained until a separate locked crosswalk; a duplicate restatement cannot create an additional actionable intervention. An admission is controlled by its worst compatible decision-relevant unit, preserving the parent's all-unit safety rule.

## Locked intervention nomination map

The reference layer nominates an intervention mode only after compatibility-set enumeration, never by panel vote:

* `PROPAGATE` is compatible only when every decision-relevant unit is compatible with `A1` and `S0` (or `S3` when the explicitly modified timing can be represented without changing target/action), no unit is compatible with `A2`, `A3`, `A4`, `A5`, `S1`, `S2`, or `S4`, and the parent route is `AUTO`.
* `RECONCILE` is compatible when at least one unit is compatible with `A2` or `S2`, while no unit is forced to `A3` and there is an active target/action signal; the parent route must be `RECON` or a more conservative route. It means clinician reconciliation is needed before any structured propagation, not that reconciliation is clinically beneficial.
* `NO_AUTOMATION` is compatible when the artifact explicitly supports `A3` or `S1`, or when the parent route is `TIMING`/`DEFER` and the reference evidence cannot establish an active, uniquely identifiable plan.
* `UNKNOWN_DEFER` is mandatory whenever a unit is compatible with `A4`, `A5`, `S2` without an active resolution, `S4`, a missing key field, a control-infeasible packet, or multiple incompatible intervention modes. It cannot be converted to `PROPAGATE` by consensus, imputation, model probability, or a neighboring atom.

These are deliberately conservative candidate modes. The panel cannot declare that a recommendation should be followed; it can only state which mode is textually defensible for a future silent test. Any conflict defaults to the more conservative mode, and the admission is assigned the worst mode across all units.

## Estimands and falsification criteria

The primary estimands are atom- and route-specific finite-population bounds, design-weighted to the complete inherited `N_A`:

1. the lower bound for `PROPAGATE` compatibility among parent `AUTO` admissions;
2. the upper bound for `RECONCILE`, `NO_AUTOMATION`, and `UNKNOWN_DEFER` compatibility among parent `AUTO` admissions;
3. the lower bound for active-but-reconciliation compatibility among parent `RECON` admissions;
4. the upper bound for unresolvable/superseded compatibility among claimed `RECON` admissions;
5. the complete-all-unit rate of actionability/supersession ambiguity and the absolute number per 100 frozen opportunities; and
6. reference-panel discordance and missingness, separately from parent-versus-reference route discordance.

For each sampled admission, construct the full compatible set from the actionability lane, superseding lane, senior boundary lane, and unit crosswalk. The primary lower bound counts only outcomes compatible with every required favorable state; the primary upper bound assigns every unresolved, missing, disagreement, and unknown pattern to the adverse nomination state. Use exact finite-population inversion for binary endpoints and 20,000 Rao–Wu resamples retaining all admission-level units and weights together. Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family across inherited gates, support-atom gates, actionability/supersession endpoints, parent/reference discordance, missingness, and workload. Do not use a latent-class model to narrow these bounds; without a pre-label external gold standard, common-mode expert error is not identifiable. A latent-class or majority analysis may be diagnostic only.

For a pre-approved atom to nominate `PROPAGATE`, its simultaneous lower bound for `PROPAGATE` must be at least 0.90, its simultaneous upper bound for `RECONCILE + NO_AUTOMATION + UNKNOWN_DEFER` must be at most 0.05, and the inherited parent route, holistic gates, control invariance, support, ESS, workload, and event gates must all pass. For `RECONCILE`, the simultaneous lower bound for active reconciliation compatibility must be at least 0.80, while the simultaneous upper bound for explicit inactive/superseded or unresolved states must be at most 0.15; this does not certify the action as appropriate. These margins are operational nomination thresholds, not clinical harm thresholds. If governance does not approve these margins before labels, no intervention mode is nominated.

A reference-layer failure is falsifiable: a claimed `PROPAGATE` atom is blocked if any adverse-compatible admission can require reconciliation/supersession/unknown above its gate; a claimed `RECONCILE` atom is blocked if active reconciliation cannot be distinguished from inactive or unknown states; and any route-invariant control failure blocks the affected atom. A passing parent component or holistic panel cannot rescue a failing reference atom.

## Controls and falsification fixtures

Reference packets include pre-label, separately sealed controls where raw text makes them feasible:

* a route-irrelevant formatting or unrelated-section permutation must preserve the actionability and supersession compatibility set and the downstream mode;
* a route-decisive one-field perturbation that explicitly adds or removes a superseding plan must change only the corresponding `S` state and locked mode;
* an actionability omission/addition pair must move between `A5/A4` and `A1/A2` only as specified by the locked manifest;
* a nuisance author/signature-like token change must not create an actionable recipient or superseding plan;
* a planted shared-wrong textual plan is a measurement stress test: all readers may agree, but absent independent evidence the compatible adverse envelope must remain wide and cannot certify `PROPAGATE`; and
* duplicate restatement versus genuinely distinct target fixtures must preserve the unit inventory and worst-unit admission rule.

Controls are never post-label constructed and cannot rescue real-case failure. Any reviewer exposure to expected answers, panel overlap, broken packet seals, route-visible material, transformation ambiguity, or favorable missingness assignment invalidates the affected atom/run. Unknown and nonresponse upper bounds are adverse-compatible rather than complete-case deleted.

## Interpretation and evidence ceiling

Supportive results require every inherited gate and reference-layer gate, at least one support-qualified atom with adequate independent challenge coverage, no adverse-compatible actionability/supersession failure, and a workload estimate within the separately approved review budget. They support only a prospective, within-site, actual-calendar **silent** bridge for the exact observable atom and mode. All unsupported, rare, novel, or reference-inconclusive atoms remain `DEFER`.

Adverse results include a supported `AUTO` atom with material `RECONCILE`, superseded, inactive, or unknown compatibility; failure to distinguish `RECONCILE` from `NO_AUTOMATION`; a route-invariant control flip; adverse missingness or nonresponse; wide bounds crossing the decision margin; or a workload failure. The affected atom is excluded despite favorable text reproducibility. An atom may be computationally well measured yet clinically unreleaseable.

Inconclusive results include too few independent reference cases, insufficient atom coverage under the fixed <=900 admissions, control infeasibility, broken blinding/seals, sparse events, nonresponse, wide partial-identification intervals, or absent pre-label identifiability conditions. Inconclusive is not evidence of safety, actionability, or transport.

Computationally checkable claims include source and schema hashes, row counts, joins, reciprocal chronology, canonical DS choice, `D` and `G_u` clocks, population membership, fixed sample and inclusion probabilities, atom membership, packet hashes, roster disjointness, codebook hashes, seal ordering, compatible-set enumeration, route/mode transition fixtures, finite-population estimates, simultaneous intervals, and workload accounting. Expert judgment is still required for whether the independent codebook adequately represents actionability, whether an ambiguous statement truly constitutes supersession, and whether an intervention mode is acceptable to governance. MIMIC cannot establish physical visibility, signature/finalization semantics, responsibility, communication, patient receipt, outside plans, clinical appropriateness, follow-up completion, harm, utility, causal benefit, or forward/external transport.

A passing retrospective layer must therefore be followed by a separately approved prospective silent bridge with actual dates, observed viewing/edit/sign/transmission, accountable team and patient communication, target-linked orders/referrals, scheduling and outside-plan capture, and eventual clinical outcomes. Only that further expert-governed prospective study could assess live safety, feasibility, utility, or benefit.
