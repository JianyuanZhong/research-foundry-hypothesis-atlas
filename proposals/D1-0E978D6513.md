# Discordant observed renal recovery after an early ICU renal insult — episode 4 successor

## Scientific deliverable

Newly derive and fit a MIMIC-IV v3.1 prognostic analysis. Completion requires:

1. a read-only provenance manifest and cohort-flow table;
2. an independently executed support audit with raw/valid rows, item-label checks, storage-time exclusions, duplicate resolution, hourly Foley coverage, weight availability, phenotype-cell counts and outcome counts;
3. the prespecified adjusted post-landmark death contrast between creatinine-recovered/Foley-not-recovered and creatinine-recovered/Foley-recovered stays, with 95% subject-cluster uncertainty;
4. newly fitted ridge-logistic baseline, nonlinear six-hour temporal model, and urine/fluid-channel ablation on the same subject-held-out split;
5. held-out AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, calibration curves, bootstrap uncertainty and grouped feature-family analyses;
6. threshold, measurement-density, creatinine-source, weight, early-RRT and within-stay urine-time permutation checks; and
7. a conclusion classified prospectively as supportive, adverse or inconclusive.

The support audit is a prerequisite, not a result. A corrected bounded diagnostic found 26,970 adult first ICU stays surviving to the 72-hour landmark after fixing a prior tuple-versus-subject lookup error, but it was stopped before final measurement and phenotype counts. No effect estimate or model result is asserted.

## Unresolved question and hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports only a measurement concern: the locally available critical-illness study by Haines et al. (CJASN 2023, DOI 10.2215/CJN.0000000000000203; readable source at `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`) reports that creatinine-based estimates can overestimate measured kidney function in selected critically ill patients, with muscle loss as a concern. That evidence does not establish Foley-output meaning, prevalence, prognosis or mechanism in MIMIC-IV.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults with an observed early creatinine-AKI-like rise who remain alive in their first ICU stay at 72 hours and have adequate paired measurements, creatinine recovery without adequate late Foley-recorded output recovery is associated with higher subsequent in-hospital mortality than recovery of both observed measures.

This is an association, not a treatment effect. It does not claim KDIGO AKI, true GFR, Foley patency, volume status, muscle loss, or that changing urine output or creatinine would change risk. Clinically, a reproducible signal could motivate confirmatory renal assessment and closer monitoring when a falling creatinine might otherwise be treated as recovery. The advance is a fixed, trajectory-defined test against concordant recovery with explicit measurement-density and leakage controls, rather than a creatinine-only inference.

## Population, time zero and phenotype

Use one row per first ICU stay within each admission: the earliest valid `icu/icustays.intime` for each (`subject_id`, `hadm_id`). Adults require `hosp/patients.anchor_age >= 18`; retain and report MIMIC's age-91 representation. Require matching `hosp/admissions` and `hosp/patients`, valid `intime < outtime`, and `t0 = intime + 72 hours < outtime`. Exclude documented `admissions.deathtime <= t0`. Group every model split and bootstrap by `subject_id`; repeated admissions are allowed but never split across train and test.

All clinical measurement times must satisfy `intime <= time < t0`. Rows with `storetime` are additionally accepted only if `storetime < t0`. No post-t0 feature, later ICU stay, discharge field, post-t0 treatment or outcome is used for prediction.

Creatinine is the union of:

- `icu/chartevents` itemid 220615, using `subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, charttime, storetime, valuenum, valueuom`;
- `hosp/labevents` itemid 50912, using `subject_id, hadm_id, itemid, charttime, storetime, valuenum, valueuom), joined to `hosp/d_labitems` for the label and to the index stay by `hadm_id` plus `intime <= charttime < outtime`.

Require finite values 0.1–30 mg/dL and report invalid, nonnumeric, nonpositive, out-of-range and incompatible-unit rows. At an identical clinical timestamp, use ICU 220615 over hospital 50912; collapse duplicates deterministically by source priority then median of remaining same-source values. Report source-specific sensitivity analyses.

Use half-open windows [0,24h), [24,48h), [48,72h). Require at least one creatinine in each. Let early be the minimum first-window value, rise the maximum second-window value, peak the maximum [0,48h) value, and late the minimum third-window value. Define an observed early creatinine-AKI-like rise as rise >= early+0.3 mg/dL OR rise/early >= 1.5. Define observed creatinine recovery as late <= peak-0.3 mg/dL OR late/peak <= 0.75. These are explicitly not KDIGO labels.

Foley output is only `icu/outputevents` itemid 226559, verified through `icu/d_items` as `linksto=outputevents`, `unitname=mL`. Use `stay_id, charttime, storetime, value, valueuom`; retain finite nonnegative values with `valueuom=mL), apply the same clinical/storage-time rules, bin by integer hour from `intime), and sum within hour. Missing hours are missing, not zero. Require at least 12 observed hours in each of [0,24h) and [48,72h); report an 18-hour sensitivity and all coverage strata.

Weights use `icu/chartevents` itemids 226512 (Admission Weight Kg) and 224639 (Daily Weight), finite 20–300 kg with compatible kg units. Use the latest qualifying weight at or before `intime+48h); retain no-weight stays in the audit but exclude them from the primary weight-standardized phenotype. Define late output recovery as late Foley mL divided by observed late hours and weight >= 0.5 mL/kg/observed hour. The primary comparison is within observed-AKI-like, creatinine-recovered stays with adequate Foley and pre-48h weight: Foley recovered versus Foley not recovered. Keep all four creatinine/output cells visible; do not merge creatinine-not-recovered cells into the primary groups.

## Outcomes and analysis

Primary outcome: `admissions.deathtime > t0` and `<= admissions.dischtime). Use `hospital_expire_flag` only as a consistency check, never as a predictor; count missing/inconsistent fields. Estimate the standardized associational risk difference:

`RD_obs = P(Y=1 | discordant, observed cohort) - P(Y=1 | concordant, observed cohort)`

standardized over the empirical pre-t0 covariate distribution using a prespecified logistic model. Use at least 500 subject-cluster bootstrap resamples for 95% intervals. Report risk ratio as secondary; retain robust Poisson/log-link only as a convergence sensitivity.

Secondary descriptions are: first new RRT after t0 and before discharge, using `icu/procedureevents` itemids 225441, 225802, 225803, 225805, 225809 and 225955 with `starttime >= t0); first later ICU readmission in the same admission among index-stay survivors, with death as competing event; and descriptive death/new-RRT composite. Report alive discharge as a competing event in a 14-day Aalen–Johansen/cause-specific time-to-event sensitivity. These procedure endpoints are charted support, not proof of renal indication.

Pre-t0 adjustment includes age, gender, admission type/location/insurance, first care unit and landmark elapsed time; creatinine levels/change/counts/density; missingness-aware MAP 220181, HR 220045, respiratory rate 220210 and SpO2 220277 from `icu/chartevents`; lactate 50813, 52442, 53154 from `hosp/labevents` verified by `hosp/d_labitems.label=Lactate`; pre-t0 vasoactive intervals from `icu/inputevents` (norepinephrine 221906, dopamine 221662, phenylephrine 221749/229630/229631/229632, epinephrine 221289/229617); ventilation 225792/225794 and pre-t0 RRT from `icu/procedureevents`; and an admission-coded CKD/ESRD proxy from `hosp/diagnoses_icd` joined to `hosp/d_icd_diagnoses`. Use exact item support and missingness reports, not undocumented substitutions.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The simple baseline is newly fitted ridge-penalized logistic regression for post-t0 death. It uses context, creatinine history, vitals/lactate summaries, support indicators, CKD proxy and missingness, but excludes Foley, weight-standardized output, input-minus-output and fluid-trajectory channels. This tests whether the discordance question adds information beyond transparent creatinine-centered severity.

The substantive alternative is newly fitted CPU `sklearn.ensemble.HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (or an equivalent fixed CPU tree booster) on the same subjects, target, time boundary and 80/20 subject-held-out split. In six-hour bins over [0,72h), compute last/mean/min/max/slope/variability/count/missingness for creatinine, Foley output, weight-standardized output where available, measured input-minus-output proxy, MAP, HR, respiratory rate, SpO2, lactate, vasoactive support, ventilation and RRT. Fit the full temporal model and an a priori ablation removing urine/output/weight/input-minus-output channels. Tune only within training subjects with grouped inner validation; calibrate within training folds; evaluate once on held-out subjects. Report AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, calibration intercept/slope, calibration plots and subject-cluster bootstrap intervals. Grouped permutation summaries are descriptive, not causal feature importance.

The alternative could reveal nonlinear timing and interactions—especially creatinine falling while output remains low—that a baseline snapshot and additive model lose. It is selected because this information directly tests the unresolved discordance mechanism at bounded CPU cost (approximately 2–4 CPU cores, <=8 GB RAM, and a few hours including streaming extraction/fitting). A neural sequence or Bayesian latent-state model is deferred: it would be scientifically revisitable only if repeated paired observations are sufficiently prevalent and the temporal interaction is stable; complexity or GPU use is not itself a clinical contribution. Revisit a joint cystatin-C/measured-GFR or mechanistic treatment study only if those missing measurements and indications become available.

## Falsification and interpretation

Before fitting, execute a support audit using the corrected subject join and exact archive hash. If either primary cell is not estimable, if death events are sparse, or if coverage/storage-time exclusions are strongly differential, do not replace the question with an easier endpoint: report inconclusive/no-go.

Run absolute-only and relative-only threshold checks; 12 versus 18 observed Foley hours; ICU-only, hospital-only and combined creatinine; pre-48h weight versus unweighted mL/hour; pre-t0 RRT exclusion/stratification; coverage-adjusted analyses; and within-stay permutation of Foley records across 72 hourly bins while preserving each stay's total volume and observed-bin count. If the association persists after late-versus-early timing is destroyed, the timing-specific discordance interpretation is falsified or dominated by total volume/measurement process.

Supportive requires a positive primary adjusted RD with an interval excluding zero, direction retained in measurement-dense and at least two source/threshold sensitivities, and a reproducible improvement in held-out Brier/calibration/risk stratification of the full temporal model over both baseline and urine/fluid ablation. Adverse means the primary contrast is near/below zero, reverses under coverage/source/threshold checks, or is preserved by time permutation; temporal model improvement alone cannot rescue it. Inconclusive means sparse cells/events, unstable estimates, strong phenotype-dependent observation, failed calibration or a failed support audit. All conclusions must cite computed output fields; the verifier can check joins, filtering, leakage, estimates, predictions and uncertainty, but cannot establish KDIGO AKI, true GFR, Foley patency, volume status, mechanism, treatment utility or external validity. Positive findings require clinician adjudication, cystatin C/measured GFR and prospective/external validation, and cannot justify starting, stopping, delaying or withholding dialysis, diuresis or fluids.
