# Does cystatin C identify a creatinine-masked AKI-surveillance group?

## Episode-5 successor and unresolved claim

This is a substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. The strongest directly relevant evidence I inspected is Haines et al. (CJASN 2023), available locally at `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`. In 38 mechanically ventilated adults, serial creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance and rectus-femoris ultrasound showed that creatinine-based eGFR could overestimate measured dysfunction after prolonged critical illness and muscle loss. That supports biological plausibility in a selected ICU sample. It does not establish the meaning of low grip in community adults, prognostic value in UK Biobank, or benefit from surveillance.

The falsifiable unresolved claim is:

> Among UK Biobank adults without a recorded renal event at biomarker assessment, a positive creatinine-based minus cystatin-C-based eGFR discordance (D), particularly with lower grip strength, predicts a clinically material excess of a first subsequent hospital-coded N17 event beyond routine creatinine-based information.

The consequential decision is whether this pattern is sufficient to prioritize repeat kidney testing or closer surveillance. This experiment estimates prognostic association and incremental information only. It cannot test whether ordering cystatin C, changing treatment, or surveillance improves patient outcomes.

## Exposure construction

Before fitting, verify the public official field evidence and source binding: field 30700 is continuous creatinine in µmol/L and field 30720 is continuous cystatin C in mg/L. The local catalog explicitly warns that many field-level units are absent; if this verification is not reproducible, stop with an inconclusive unit audit and do not infer units from distributions.

Convert creatinine to mg/dL as `Scr=30700/88.4`; set `Scys=30720` mg/L. Use 2021 CKD-EPI equations without race:

- Female: k=0.7, a=-0.241; male: k=0.9, a=-0.302.
- `eGFRcr = 142 × min(Scr/k,1)^a × max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) × 0.9938^Age × (1.012 if female else 1)`.
- `eGFRcys = 133 × min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499) × max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328) × 0.9961^Age × (0.932 if female else 1)`.

Define `D=eGFRcr-eGFRcys`; positive D is the hypothesized creatinine-reassuring direction. Standardize D with training participants only. Primary grip is the maximum valid left/right value; weaker-side and sex-standardized grip are prespecified sensitivities. BMI and weight are adjustment/sensitivity variables, not measured muscle-mass proxies.

## Outcomes and estimands

Primary endpoint: first post-index hospital record beginning N17, using `41270` and its same-suffix `41280` date. Secondary endpoint: first post-index N18 code. Ascertainment sensitivity: repeat the primary analysis with `41271`/`41281` primary inpatient arrays. Death from `40000` is a competing event and is also reported separately. Never call N17 “severe AKI,” persistent AKI, adjudicated AKI, or call N18 adjudicated CKD: KDIGO stage, urine output, inpatient laboratory trajectories and adjudication are unavailable.

The primary estimand is the five-year cause-specific N17 hazard and competing-risk cumulative-incidence contrast by continuous D and D-by-grip interaction. Report hazard ratios plus absolute five-year cumulative-incidence contrasts at training-set 10th and 90th D percentiles and low versus median grip profiles. Define clinically material before fitting as an absolute low-versus-median grip contrast of at least 0.5 percentage points at the 10th versus 90th D profiles. This is a decision-oriented information threshold, not evidence that surveillance benefits patients.

Fit transparent nested cause-specific Cox models, with death censored for the cause-specific hazard and retained in Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence:

- M0: age, sex, BMI, weight, primary grip and eGFRcr.
- M1: M0 plus eGFRcys (report D for interpretation, but never include algebraically redundant eGFRcr, eGFRcys and D together).
- M2: M1 plus D-by-grip interaction and prespecified restricted cubic splines for age and eGFRcr.

Use Fine–Gray as sensitivity only. Use participant-level SHA-256(`eid`) buckets 0–79 for deterministic 60/20/20 train/tuning/test partitions; buckets 80–99 are audit-only and not external validation. Estimate standardization, spline knots, missingness rules, thresholds and tuning only in training data. Bootstrap participants within each split for uncertainty.

## Matched learned alternative

Fit a CPU gradient-boosted discrete-time survival model using exactly the M0–M2 inputs, endpoint, split and five-year target, with shallow trees and tuning/early stopping on the tuning partition only. It asks whether nonlinear thresholds or D-by-grip heterogeneity missed by M2 changes calibrated risk stratification. It must report held-out five-year Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, the same absolute threshold contrasts, and accumulated-local-effect curves with participant-bootstrap intervals. A small C-index gain alone is not clinical value. Expected post-extraction budget is <=4 GB RAM and <=20 CPU minutes; GPU is unnecessary.

The repeat-landmark joint latent measurement-error/competing-risk model is explicitly deferred. It could distinguish persistent discordance from visit noise and regression to the mean, but exact repeat dates are not locally available and a prior full-column audit exceeded the job RSS limit. Revisit only after a chunked support audit demonstrates adequate repeat fields, events and convergence. Delphi-like disease-sequence generation and a Bayesian measured-GFR model are also deferred because this question is about biomarker increment and no measured-GFR anchor exists.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive evidence requires: (1) the prespecified positive D-by-low-grip contrast reaches 0.5 percentage points with uncertainty excluding a clinically negligible or reversed effect; (2) direction persists under primary-inpatient and broad renal-history sensitivities; (3) M1/M2 improve held-out calibration/Brier and threshold risk contrasts over M0; and (4) the learned model gives concordant risk curves. This supports incremental prognostic association in this selected UKB cohort only.

Adverse evidence is a null/reversed interaction, intervals compatible with effects below the threshold, loss of direction under ascertainment/history sensitivity, no held-out calibration improvement, or worsened calibration after cystatin C. That argues against prioritizing surveillance using this pattern in this setting; it does not prove cystatin C lacks biological value.

Inconclusive evidence includes unverifiable units, sparse N17 events, severe exposure missingness, invalid/unpaired dates, impossible administrative boundary, unstable bootstrap/split estimates, or failed model convergence. Do not substitute an easier endpoint for an inconclusive N17 analysis.

Computation can check source hashes, one-to-one joins, raw-value audits, unit/formula implementation, paired code/date ordering, risk sets, split integrity, fitted estimates, calibration, Brier scores, bootstrap intervals and whether interpretation follows the gates. It cannot establish true AKI severity, measured GFR, causality, treatment benefit, transportability, or benefit from surveillance. Those require adjudicated AKI and laboratory trajectories, measured-GFR/body-composition anchors, treatment data, expert review, and independent or prospective validation.
