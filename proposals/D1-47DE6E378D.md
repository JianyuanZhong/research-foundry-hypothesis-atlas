## Scientific opening and falsifiable hypothesis

The selected parent established a narrow operational question: does pre-landmark state/process information predict a later documented respiratoryCare interval start, after excluding a valid active interval at minute 360? The remaining interpretation boundary is that respiratoryCare may be a single interface/workflow stream. A respiratoryCharting row has a different table and distinct clinical-entry/documentation offsets, so concordance can test reproducibility across streams. It cannot turn either stream into a physiologic or treatment gold standard.

The strongest inspected evidence supports (i) eICU as a multi-center, linked archive in which only some continuously monitored information is archived [K1], (ii) temporally structured EHR prediction across centers while semantic harmonization remains a limitation [K2], and (iii) explicit, reproducible preprocessing for irregular structured ICU data [K3]. None validates the clinical meaning, completeness, or timing of these two respiratory extracts. The parent’s documented-start result is not inherited as an observed result.

Hypothesis:

> In the adult first-ICU S6 risk set with no valid documented active respiratoryCare vent interval at minute 360, a pre-landmark state/process vector predicts a later documented respiratoryCare interval start in (360,720] only if that start is reproducibly corroborated by an independently recorded respiratoryCharting observation. The process increment should remain directionally coherent in hospital-held-out evaluation after explicit respiratoryCharting availability weighting/stratification, discharge and end-of-life controls, and documentation-time falsification. A signal confined to chart availability, near-exit documentation, one hospital, or one timestamp convention falsifies transportable cross-stream corroboration.

The estimand remains documented, not clinical: primary event R_start_doc is a strict valid respiratoryCare interval-start boundary; the new endpoint C_start_doc is R_start_doc with an independent respiratoryCharting concordance opportunity and a frozen, outcome-blind concordance rule. Neither means invasive ventilation, respiratory deterioration, indication, treatment, or benefit.

This child changes the scientific test from “is there a later respiratoryCare start?” to “does the start survive independent-stream corroboration and explicit selection/exit controls?” A positive result supports reproducibility of a documented transition. It does not validate the parent’s broader clinical interpretation or establish causality.

## Leading explanation and rivals

The leading operational explanation is a reproducible support-documentation transition: early state/process information predicts a later respiratoryCare boundary and a temporally concordant charting observation.

Rivals make different predictions:

1. Availability/selection: P predicts whether respiratoryCharting is present, or whether the stay remains observable, rather than a start. P will predict chart availability and any chart row, but the C_start_doc increment will attenuate to the availability-adjusted estimate or disappear in chart-observable strata.
2. Discharge/end-of-life workflow: charting and respiratoryCare activity cluster before alive/expired ICU exit or after an end-of-life discussion. Concordance will be concentrated in pre-specified exit bands or EOL strata and will weaken when the charting window is required to be entirely before discharge and EOL status is controlled.
3. Backfill/timestamp artifact: respiratoryCare ventstartoffset and respiratoryCharting respchartentryoffset reflect documentation rather than care. Concordance will change materially when clinical-entry respchartoffset is used, or when entry time is permuted within hospital and coverage strata.
4. Baseline misclassification/duplicate conflict: an existing support episode is incorrectly counted as a new start. Starts and concordance will be unstable in R0_unknown, open-boundary, same-start conflict, or baseline-record-incomplete strata.
5. Genuine cross-stream operational corroboration: concordance is present in held-out hospitals, in adequately observed strata, across the two time conventions, and after exit/EOL controls, with no dominant availability explanation. Even this supports only a documented association.

The archive cannot fully distinguish clinical initiation from clinician choice, interface design, backfill, staffing, or end-of-life workflow.

## Interpretation and decision rules

Supportive results require all gates: strict start and charting audits are reproducible; the chart class is frozen without outcome inspection; at least five hospitals have positive support and chart opportunity; C_start_doc and C_entry_doc are directionally coherent in held-out hospitals; Q_P^T is coherent in elastic-net and boosting and exceeds the inherited negligible margin where estimable with a paired interval excluding it; the result is not explained by availability weighting, EOL strata, discharge-adjacent concentration, conflicts, timestamp substitution, shifted-window nulls, or one hospital; and generic C_anyrow_doc does not show a larger availability-only effect than the qualifying concordance. Support means early state/process information is associated with a reproducible, cross-stream documented respiratoryCare/charting transition.

Adverse results include adequate chart opportunity with no C_start_doc increment after T; R_start_doc positive but C_start_doc absent; concordance disappearing in the chart-observable or weighted analysis; concentration near discharge/EOL; a large clinical-entry/documentation-entry discrepancy; one-hospital dependence; or a learner-only improvement. These weaken the cross-stream claim. If R remains positive while C is null, retain only the parent’s bounded single-stream documented-start association. If C_anyrow is positive but semantic C is unavailable or null, retain a generic documentation concordance/availability claim, not support initiation.

Inconclusive/deferral means fewer than five viable hospitals, poor positivity or effective sample size, too few strict starts, unresolved chart vocabulary or contradictory same-offset rows, no independent chart opportunity, source-time dominance, or intervals spanning meaningful and negligible margins. An imprecise null is not refutation. Continue to blinded respiratory/critical-care adjudication only if the operational claim passes. Revise to an interface/availability study if controls dominate. Defer invasive, physiologic, treatment, causal, and deployment claims in all cases without device/order provenance, reliable clinical event times, and expert review.
