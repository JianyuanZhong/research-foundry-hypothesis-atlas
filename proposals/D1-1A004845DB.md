# Prospective silent validation before any pulmonary-nodule handoff intervention: live trigger reconstruction, clinician-confirmed admission routing, and explicit no-go gates

## Narrow decision and why the intervention trial is deferred

This child preserves the parent’s retrospective pulmonary-opportunity question and repairs only the next decision: **can the frozen two-stage admission-routing gate be reconstructed and validated prospectively, while completely silent, well enough to justify writing a separate intervention-trial protocol?** It does not test a discharge intervention, treatment, follow-up completion, cancer outcome, or clinical benefit.

The strongest claim currently supported is unchanged: a complete-source MIMIC scan established the availability and chronology of the bound tables and identified 3,764 retrieval-proxy pulmonary reports, while the inherited design defines a falsifiable, departure-frozen retrospective adjudication of all eligible opportunities. Those retrieval proxies are not adjudicated opportunities, and neither their prevalence nor any live trigger performance, clinician awareness, route accuracy, or intervention effect has been established.

The unresolved prospective hypothesis is:

> At each intended deployment site, a version-locked live pipeline can enumerate every admission satisfying the inherited pulmonary-opportunity definition, reconstruct report/addendum and discharge-version chronology, and assign a conservative admission route before discharge such that, against an independent clinician-confirmed departure-frozen reference, pure-omission routing has at least 95% positive predictive value, dangerous under-routing is no more than 1% of all eligible admissions and no more than 3% of reference-actionable admissions, exact admission-route accuracy is at least 90%, trigger sensitivity is at least 95%, unknown/defer remains no more than 15%, probability calibration does not drift by more than prespecified margins, and these claims meet precision gates separately across intended sites and services.

Every percentage above is an **operating acceptability margin for whether another experiment may be designed**, not a clinical utility, treatment threshold, or false-positive/false-negative exchange rate. Clinical governance, radiology, hospital medicine, surgery, patient safety, informatics, and the statistical lead must sign these margins and the machine-readable operating-threshold registry before the first confirmatory case. Failure to approve them is itself a no-go. No retrospective or prospective outcome-adaptive tuning is permitted.

A workflow trial is deliberately **not specified in this child**. Its intervention content, randomization unit, contamination strategy, outcomes, follow-up, and stopping rules depend on route performance, observed workflow topology, clinician ownership, and failure modes that MIMIC does not contain and silent validation has not yet measured. An underspecified trial would smuggle in unmeasured utilities. A new, governance-reviewed protocol may be written only after every mandatory silent-validation gate passes; passing P1 does not authorize deployment or imply benefit.

Medicine and surgery are mandatory transport strata; other services are retained but cannot be proposed for a later trial unless they independently meet the same gates.

At each site, create an immutable census of all adult inpatient encounters and every exact encounter-linked radiology report or addendum whose clinical `charttime` is in `[admittime-12h, actual_departure]`. ## Independent clinician-confirmed reference

The silent gate output, scores, thresholds, expected direction, and later clinical outcomes are unavailable to all reference reviewers. Reference packets are generated only after departure and contain a cryptographically logged snapshot of information that existed by departure: complete report/addendum lineage, every discharge version, encounter/service/responsibility chronology, qualifying UI events, target-linked orders/referrals/scheduling, and clinician confirmations. No post-departure imaging completion, diagnosis, cancer outcome, readmission, or later note may enter the reference.

Reference construction has three separated tasks:

1. **Opportunity and unit panel.** Two independent thoracic radiologists or equivalently qualified physicians review the complete prospective radiology census under the inherited eligibility rubric, reconstruct reciprocal/explicit lineage, and enumerate minimum/maximum recommendation-unit inventories. Every report in the temporal census is screened; there is no opportunity sampling. All possible positives and uncertainties receive dual review. A second reviewer also audits a prespecified 10% random sample of screen-negative reports at each site/service; any confirmed eligible miss expands dual review to all negatives in that site/service and triggers a no-go unless the complete census is repaired before database lock.
2. **Departure-state panel.** Two independent physicians experienced in discharge workflow assign the inherited `K`, `S`, and content states for every unit, quoting exact evidence. They do not vote for a route. Visibility and awareness are shown as separate evidence streams and cannot replace missing target/action/timing/responsibility. An uncorroborated post-sign recollection can only widen the compatible state set.
3. **Safety panel.** A separate three-clinician panel, including radiology and the destination clinical service, reviews every discordant live/reference route and classifies the routing error mechanistically. It identifies dangerous under-routing under the frozen definition below; it does not estimate harm, utility, or preventable cancer.

Retain every pre-consensus label. A third adjudicator may resolve the operational reference but cannot erase the two-reviewer compatible set. The deterministic reference route `R*` is generated with the inherited worst-case admission aggregation. Primary estimates count any reviewer-compatible adverse route as adverse; consensus-only estimates are secondary. Raw agreement must be at least 90% and Gwet AC1 or kappa at least 0.70 for eligibility, unit boundary, `K`, `S`, content state, pure omission, and derived route. If prevalence destabilizes kappa, raw agreement, AC1, and compatible-route stability still must pass. Consensus cannot repair a failed reliability gate.

## Live routes and exact error taxonomy

The live gate assigns one and only one admission route `Rhat` from `{AUTO,RECON,TIMING,NOACTION,DEFER}` by the same conservative unit-to-admission aggregation as the parent. `AUTO` remains only a **predicted pure-omission lane**; in P1 it causes no prepopulation, alert, message, or chart change.

The primary admission confusion matrix crosses `Rhat` with clinician-confirmed `R*`, retaining every cell and zero. Report exact-route accuracy, macro recall, class-specific sensitivity, positive predictive value, false-discovery rate, and one-versus-rest calibration. Route-unit analyses are secondary and patient/admission clustered.

### Pure-omission purity

`PPV_AUTO = P(R*=AUTO | Rhat=AUTO)`.

An admission is reference `AUTO` only if **every** active unresolved unit is a confirmed wholly omitted target/action/timing payload with at least 24 hours of inherited storage opportunity; every other unit is resolved/closed; and there is no incomplete plan, conflict, mixed route, unit-boundary uncertainty, missing context that changes routing, or unverified outside plan. Any compatible reviewer assignment to `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` counts against primary purity. The primary gate is the simultaneous one-sided 95% lower confidence bound `LCB(PPV_AUTO)>=0.95` overall and `>=0.90` at every intended site and mandatory service stratum.

### Dangerous under-routing

Dangerous under-routing is a process-safety error, not observed patient harm. It occurs when the live route would send an admission to a lower-review lane than any compatible reference route:

* `Rhat=AUTO` while `R*=RECON` because of conflict, incomplete plan, mixed units, interaction-dependent management, or uncertain supersession;
* `Rhat=NOACTION` while `R*` is `AUTO` or `RECON`;
* `Rhat=TIMING` when all active reference units actually had at least 24 hours of inherited opportunity and `R*` is `AUTO` or `RECON`;
* a missed/late live trigger for an admission whose reference route is `AUTO` or `RECON`.

`Rhat=DEFER` is not dangerous under-routing, although excessive deferral can fail feasibility. `AUTO` in place of `DEFER` is counted against pure-omission purity and is also dangerous when the compatible defer reason includes a possible conflict, mixed admission, unresolved target identity, or superseding-plan ambiguity. The safety panel records the exact mechanism.

The two primary rates are `DUR_ALL = dangerous under-routes/N_P` and `DUR_ACTIVE = dangerous under-routes/n(R* in {AUTO,RECON})`. Their simultaneous one-sided 95% upper bounds must be `<=0.01` and `<=0.03`, respectively, overall. Any intended site or mandatory service must have `UCB(DUR_ACTIVE)<=0.05`; no pooled result can rescue a failing stratum. A recurrent mechanism affecting two or more admissions, even if the aggregate bound passes, requires root-cause repair and a new fully independent silent-validation epoch.

### Unknown/defer

`DEFER_RATE = P(Rhat=DEFER or R*=DEFER or LINK_UNKNOWN or DOC_UNKNOWN)` over `N_P`; no denominator exclusion is allowed. The simultaneous 95% upper bound must be `<=0.15` overall and `<=0.20` at each intended site and mandatory service. Also report mutually exclusive reasons: report lineage, unit boundary, encounter linkage, responsibility, document version, missing UI coverage, external-plan uncertainty, clinician nonresponse, content uncertainty, and model abstention. Unknowns are never redistributed to favorable routes.

## Live eligibility and trigger reconstruction endpoints

The independent report census, not the live trigger list, defines the opportunity reference. Primary trigger endpoints are:

* eligibility sensitivity: reference-eligible admissions with a successful live trigger divided by all reference-eligible admissions;
* eligibility positive predictive value: reference-eligible among live eligibility-positive admissions;
* initial trigger latency from `EHR_available_time` to first execution end;
* material-addendum refresh sensitivity and latency;
* correct encounter assignment, operative component selection, recommendation-unit count, and final pre-sign version selection;
* pre-sign route availability among reference `E_P` admissions.

Simultaneous one-sided 95% lower bounds must be at least 0.95 for eligibility sensitivity, 0.90 for eligibility positive predictive value, 0.98 for encounter assignment, 0.95 for operative-component and unit-inventory exact reconstruction, and 0.95 for material-addendum refresh. At least 95% of successful executions must complete within 15 minutes, and at least 99% within 60 minutes. A trigger after signature is a miss for routing even if before physical departure.

False-positive trigger workload is reported as triggers per 100 adult discharges and review-minutes per trigger, with a two-sided 95% interval. No favorable workload threshold is inferred from MIMIC. Governance must prespecify a maximum sustainable value in the signed registry before P1; absence of that value or exceeding its upper confidence bound is a no-go for the tested workflow.

## Calibration, temporal drift, and transport

The locked gate must emit probability estimates without P1 recalibration. For eligibility and each route, report calibration-in-the-large, Brier score, log loss, calibration slope where estimable, reliability diagrams with prespecified bins, and an adaptive calibration error whose code and bins are frozen. Abstentions remain a separate outcome and are not dropped.

Calibration acceptance is based on absolute observed-minus-predicted risk within prespecified score bins: the simultaneous 95% upper bound for weighted expected calibration error must be `<=0.05` overall and `<=0.10` at every intended site and mandatory service with adequate counts. A calibration slope is descriptive unless there are at least 200 events for that one-versus-rest class; it cannot be used to waive the error gate.

Temporal drift is tested in consecutive, nonoverlapping blocks of 100 `N_P` admissions, plus calendar months descriptively. Compare each later block with the first locked block for eligibility prevalence, route composition, `PPV_AUTO`, `DUR_ACTIVE`, defer rate, score distribution, calibration error, trigger latency, missing-audit rate, service mix, and interface/software hashes. Use simultaneous intervals and prespecified population-stability and calibration contrasts. Absolute calibration-error increase greater than 0.05, route-prevalence change greater than 0.10 unexplained by measured case mix, a sign reversal in an admission-route contrast, any software/interface hash change, or any block failing a primary safety margin is drift. Drift cannot be repaired by post hoc recalibration inside P1; it requires a new version and new silent epoch.

Transport is a gate, not an interaction fishing exercise. Report all primary metrics separately by site and by the locked terminal-service families `medicine`, `surgery`, and `other`, and for ICU exposure. At least three sites must each contribute 75 `N_P` admissions; medicine and surgery must each contribute at least 60 overall and at least 20 reference-active admissions. Each route proposed for later use must have at least 30 predicted and 30 reference admissions overall, and at least 10 at every intended site. Hierarchical estimates may describe heterogeneity but cannot override a site-specific no-go. A route may be declared transportable only if all intended sites and mandatory services meet purity, dangerous-under-routing, defer, calibration, trigger, and audit-coverage margins without sign reversal or a site-by-route absolute accuracy difference above 0.10. Otherwise a later protocol must exclude the unsupported site/service or repeat P1 there; pooled success is insufficient.

## Sample size, precision, and analysis

The minimum cohort is 300 reference-eligible first admissions, with at least 100 reference-active (`AUTO+RECON`) admissions, 60 predicted `AUTO`, 60 reference `AUTO`, 60 predicted `RECON`, and 60 reference `RECON`, in addition to site/service counts above. These minima are not substitutes for precision. They permit approximately a 1% upper bound after zero events only near 300 observations and force useful lane-specific review, but actual decisions use the confidence-bound gates.

Use patient-clustered resampling that carries repeat admissions in sensitivity analyses, with site and service retained. For sparse safety errors use exact or score-compatible one-sided binomial intervals plus a patient-cluster bootstrap sensitivity; report the more conservative bound. Use at least 10,000 fixed-seed cluster-bootstrap replicates. One max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers eligibility sensitivity and PPV, encounter assignment, lineage/unit reconstruction, exact-route accuracy, `PPV_AUTO`, `DUR_ALL`, `DUR_ACTIVE`, defer rate, calibration error, addendum refresh, and intended site/service contrasts. Calibration plots and mechanism counts remain descriptive unless explicitly in that family.

In addition to the directional margins, require interval half-width `<=0.03` for exact-route accuracy and eligibility sensitivity, `<=0.04` for `PPV_AUTO`, `<=0.02` for `DUR_ACTIVE`, `<=0.05` for defer rate, and `<=0.05` for route prevalence at each intended site. If an event count is zero, report the nonzero upper bound. Counts below a gate are inconclusive, never evidence that a route or failure mode is absent.

No missing-data imputation is primary. Primary analyses assign missing or conflicting linkage, timestamps, responsibility, awareness, unit boundary, and target-plan evidence to `DEFER` and to the adverse compatible error state. Multiple imputation may be a labeled sensitivity only and cannot turn no-go into go.

## Silence, contamination, and operational safety monitoring

P1 software may read governed feeds and write only to an isolated research store. It must have no write token, order API, inbox endpoint, discharge-document insertion capability, clinician-facing dashboard, patient portal output, or production alert channel. Clinicians responsible for enrolled admissions cannot access predictions. Reference reviewers see outputs only after labels lock.

Automated daily checks reconcile every execution against API access logs, UI audit logs, message queues, chart writes, and user access. A `CONTAMINATION` event is any prediction, route, extracted payload, or study-specific prompt visible to a treating clinician or patient before departure; any gate-driven chart/order/message mutation; or any research form available before final signature. Contaminated admissions remain in `N_P`, are reported separately, and are assigned adverse compatible outcomes. Any chart/order mutation, any patient-facing exposure, or contamination in more than 1% of `N_P` is an immediate no-go. One accidental clinician exposure pauses accrual for investigation; restart requires independent confirmation that the pathway is closed.

Operational safety monitoring also captures feed latency, queue backlog, CPU/memory saturation, EHR performance degradation, privacy/identity mismatches, unauthorized access, report suppression, duplicate/retracted report handling, and system downtime. Any evidence that silent software delayed or altered clinical systems, any report delivered to the wrong encounter, any unauthorized access, or unexplained loss of source events pauses the study. These are system-safety outcomes, not treatment outcomes.

An independent monitoring group reviews only contamination, privacy, uptime, trigger completeness, and dangerous-under-routing mechanisms at fixed monthly intervals. It cannot tune thresholds or release favorable efficacy claims. Prespecified no-go rules apply symmetrically whether or not the hypothesis is supported.

## Explicit progression, no-go, and falsification rules

Progression means only permission to draft a separate intervention-trial protocol. Every item below must pass overall, at every intended site, and in every mandatory service where a future route would operate:

1. signed governance registry, frozen software/model/ruleset/interface hashes, and successful synthetic conformance tests before accrual;
2. complete all-opportunity census with no unresolved denominator or first-admission drift;
3. eligibility sensitivity `LCB>=0.95`, eligibility PPV `LCB>=0.90`, encounter assignment `LCB>=0.98`, exact operative-component/unit reconstruction `LCB>=0.95`, and addendum refresh `LCB>=0.95`;
4. exact admission-route accuracy `LCB>=0.90`;
5. `PPV_AUTO LCB>=0.95` overall and `>=0.90` by intended site/service;
6. `DUR_ALL UCB<=0.01`, `DUR_ACTIVE UCB<=0.03`, and site/service `DUR_ACTIVE UCB<=0.05`, with no recurrent dangerous mechanism;
7. defer/unknown `UCB<=0.15` overall and `<=0.20` by site/service;
8. calibration error `UCB<=0.05` overall and `<=0.10` by site/service, with no prespecified temporal drift;
9. reviewer reliability, compatible-route stability, UI coverage, clock integrity, silence, privacy, sample-size, and interval-width gates all pass;
10. every site/service/route intended for a later protocol independently meets transport counts and margins.

An **explicit no-go** occurs with any chart/order mutation, patient-facing exposure, wrong-patient/encounter delivery, unavailable governance threshold, denominator or software-version drift, timestamp/identity failure above its margin, trigger or route margin failure, lower-lane dangerous error recurrence, excess unknown/defer, calibration drift, failed site/service transport, reviewer instability, contamination above its margin, or inability to achieve required precision by maximum accrual. “No-go” means no intervention study using that version and scope. A corrected extractor is a new version and must undergo a new, independent silent epoch; favorable cases from the failed epoch cannot be reused for confirmation.

The hypothesis is falsified for the tested gate if live event reconstruction cannot reproduce the inherited opportunity and unit clocks, if independent clinician evidence does not support high-purity `AUTO`, if dangerous under-routing exceeds its bound, if uncertainty is too frequent, or if performance fails to transport. Falsification is informative: it can support reconciliation-only development, restriction to a narrower site/service, or abandonment of automated routing, but none is selected without a new protocol.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive** results require every progression gate. They support only this statement: the frozen live gate reconstructed eligible events and admission routes with prespecified accuracy, purity, safety-error, uncertainty, calibration, and transport precision while invisible to care, so a separate governed intervention protocol may be designed. They do not show that clinicians failed, that copying a recommendation is appropriate, that follow-up improves, that cancer is detected sooner, or that any intervention benefits patients.

**Adverse** results include low trigger recall, report/addendum or encounter mislinkage, `AUTO` contamination by incomplete/conflicting/mixed admissions, dangerous lower-lane assignment, excess defer, awareness/visibility ambiguity, calibration drift, or site/service nontransport. An adverse result rejects the tested live version or scope. A high defer rate with low dangerous under-routing means the gate may be conservative but operationally unusable; it is not a safety success sufficient for progression. High trigger accuracy with poor route purity supports event detection only, not routing.

**Inconclusive** results include insufficient reference-active events, wide simultaneous bounds, failure to reach site/service counts, incomplete audit coverage, unreliable adjudication, unresolved timestamps, or expiration of accrual before precision gates. Inconclusive is a no-go for intervention progression even when point estimates are favorable. It is not evidence of absence of opportunities or errors.

A low prevalence of `AUTO` with precise high purity means that pure omission can be identified but may be too uncommon for the intended workflow; whether that workload merits a trial requires a separately declared operational minimum, not an implied clinical utility. A high `RECON` burden does not prove reconciliation helps. Visibility without confirmed awareness, or awareness without faithful documentation, identifies a process state only and cannot identify intent or negligence.

## Computationally checkable versus clinically adjudicated claims

The verifier can recompute source and interface hashes; census membership after locked labels; first-admission selection; event ordering; report/addendum lineage integrity where explicit IDs exist; `G_store` and `G_visible`; execution latency; discharge-version selection; route generation from frozen component labels; admission confusion matrices; purity, dangerous-under-routing, defer, calibration and drift metrics; clustering; multiplicity; precision gates; site/service transport tables; contamination logs; and the deterministic go/no-go output. Synthetic adversarial cases must verify that a mixed admission never becomes `AUTO`, post-sign output never repairs a miss, duplicated units do not change admission routing, unknowns remain adverse/defer, and pooling cannot rescue a failed site.

The verifier cannot establish clinical appropriateness of the original recommendation, true clinician comprehension, validity of an outside plan, correct supersession, accountability, likely harm, treatment effect, or clinical benefit. Those require blinded clinician adjudication, governance, additional follow-up, or another study. Expert agreement is a reference standard with retained uncertainty, not biological truth.
