# Source-bound successor: patient-identity/content retention with an explicit as-of-visibility identification bound

Parent anchors: [prior hypothesis] (primary) and [prior hypothesis] (joint source/uncertainty safeguards).

## 1. Substantive repair and unresolved question

This child preserves the mature HCC design: the clinically narrow adult first-eligible hepatobiliary-resection cohort; the 24-hour preoperative decision cutoff; the 90-day CT/MRI acquisition window; the 365-day recorded-prior-treatment exclusion; patient-disjoint calendar roles; frozen 2019 preprocessing, models, books, capacity, tie keys and rational threshold grid; 2020-only threshold selection; untouched 2021 evaluation; whole-accession source states; coupled reader/pathology uncertainty; fixed-capacity ranking; comparator and anchored-deletion gates; and the nonclinical claim limits.

It repairs one remaining decision-relevant defect. The inherited source variable

    M = E * V(O)

can be read too strongly. It represents exposure of the eventual stored content under an onset state O. The configured HCC files do not establish that the content had been authored, finalized, released, retrieved, parsed, readable, or viewed before t_dec. Thus an aggregate or patientwise “complete source” result could still be mistaken for a claim about what was available to a clinician at the decision time.

The child separates three claims that must not be conflated:

1. patient identity retention: whether the fixed-capacity queue retains the same protected patients as the all-content reference queue;
2. stored-content certification: whether every eligible accession belonging to a protected patient is represented by a source-clean, exposed raw record under the inherited state;
3. as-of visibility: whether the required content was actually available/readable or viewed before t_dec.

The first two are executable on the frozen HCC snapshot. The third is represented by a finite, coupled lifecycle state and reported with exact identification bounds. Because the HCC snapshot lacks lifecycle fields, a worst-case visibility claim is not silently converted into an adverse clinical result: it is explicitly operationally unidentifiable and requires archive/workflow evidence.

The unresolved question is therefore:

> At fixed review capacity, does the apparent temporal retention of label-stable documentary-M2 patients remain true at the level of the patients selected—not merely the number of covered accessions—and is it robust to whole-accession identity/content ambiguity, while the data’s inability to establish pre-t_dec report visibility is correctly exposed rather than hidden?

This is clinically consequential only as a gate for whether an archive-linked silent-mode validation is justified. It does not claim that a clinician could have acted on a report, that HCC documentary-M2 is biological microvascular invasion, or that patients benefit.

Before execution, the strongest supported claim is structural. The read-only HCC snapshot contains linked encounter, procedure, examination, pathology, medication and order tables with the keys and fields needed for the retrospective documentary experiment. The catalog identifies HCC snapshot [source checksum]; its source files, schemas and limitations are bound below.

The snapshot can support:

The hypothesis being tested is not “stored reports were available to clinicians.” It is the narrower, falsifiable finite-corpus hypothesis in Section 4. The visibility question is a prespecified identification analysis and an explicit requirement for a later study.

## 3. Population, temporal boundaries and quarantine

### 3.1 Unit and first-eligible episode

The unit is one patient with one first eligible source-documented hepatobiliary resection episode.

Include an encounter with age at least 18 years and a procedure matching a hepatobiliary resection dictionary frozen after review by a hepatobiliary surgeon. Exclude any patient with an earlier qualifying resection. Exclude recorded transplant, TACE/embolization, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy in [t_op-365 days,t_op). Medication and order joins are patient-wide but retain their source encounter keys.

Ambiguous procedure dictionaries, duplicate episodes, missing clocks and contradictory clocks are not resolved in favor of inclusion or a favorable result. They create outer coherent source states or a required feasibility failure.

The primary encounter key is

    (患者主索引, 就诊号).

The primary operation clock is the clinically supported procedure/anesthesia start. A date-like case-record start is a separate interval clock [date,date+24 hours). Admission and discharge are never substituted for operation time. Define

    t_dec = t_op - 24 hours.

A CT/MRI examination is eligible only when its examination interval is wholly inside

    [t_op - 90 days, t_dec).

The locked 12-, 48- and 72-hour onset variants are descriptive sensitivities only and cannot rescue a failed 24-hour primary result.

### 3.2 Calendar roles

Use patient-disjoint roles:

- 2015–2018: development only;
- 2019: preprocessing, dictionaries, hyperparameters, model/book/queue/tie-key and rational-grid lock;
- 2020: threshold selection only;
- 2021: one untouched temporal evaluation at the frozen threshold;
- 2022 onward: audit only, never tuning.

A patient appearing, or potentially appearing because of an unresolved identity/clock state, in a later role is quarantined from an earlier role. No 2020 or 2021 label, state result, comparator, event count, failure pattern, frontier or feasibility result may influence model fitting, dictionary construction, grid construction or q*. A signed/hash-checked 2020 selection artifact must be frozen before any 2021 computation.

## 4. Falsifiable hypothesis and estimands

Use primary capacity rho=.10, d=24 hours, and the inherited rational threshold grid R. Let N_ref and K=floor(.10*N_ref) be frozen in 2019. Require N_ref>=500, K>=50, N>=K in every required test state, and at least 50 documentary-M2 events. rho=.05 and .20 are locked descriptive sensitivities only.

### 4.1 Frozen queues

For coherent source/operation/reader/pathology state w and threshold q:

- S_max(y,w) is the exactly ranked reference queue with all source-clean eligible accessions exposed;
- S_G(y,w,q) is the exactly K-position incomplete-content G queue;
- EMPTY positions remain EMPTY;
- anchored deletion removes every source row for one patient without refitting, reranking, backfill or requota; the deleted slot remains EMPTY.

All scores, binary64 bytes, model hashes, feature order, preprocessing, coefficients, penalties, tie keys, books and K are frozen. B, R, G and byte-identical unrefitted G_mask are retained. Patient identifiers, pathology labels, untimed notes, future rows, reader identity and test-derived dictionaries are forbidden predictors.

### 4.2 Protected patients and source states

For fixed nonpathology state a, retain the inherited pathology-book definitions:

U_LS(a) = {i: Y_i(a,b')=1 for every pathology book b'},

P_LS(a,b) = S_max(a,b) intersect U_LS(a),

P_M2(a,b) = {i: i in S_max(a,b), Y_i(a,b)=1}.

Retain P_common, J_common, J_LS, J_M2, J_DEC, q_REQ, q_LS, q_M2, q_DEC, their exact lower-threshold witnesses, nesting checks and nonvacuity rules. An empty P_common is diagnostic-only. An empty required P_LS is nonvacuity failure, not success.

A whole examination unit is the nonblank accession tuple

    (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号).

Preserve every raw examination row, field boundary and source ordinal. Never choose a representative row or deduplicate narrative content. Blank accession, component disagreement, ambiguous modality or irreversible parse failure makes that accession unavailable in that coherent state.

The inherited monotone onset state O records whether an eligible accession is exposed by 72 hours, first exposed in (72,48], (48,24], (24,12], or later/never. This is a source-content exposure state only:

    M_iak(q,w) = E_iak(q,w) * V_iak(O,w).

It must never be labeled “available,” “released,” “readable” or “viewed.”

Define the stored-content certificate A_i(q,w)=1 iff every eligible nonblank accession of patient i is source-clean and exposed under M at q. A patient with no eligible accession is not assigned A_i=1. Any blank-ID, contradiction, ambiguity, parse failure or unexposed eligible accession gives A_i=0. Emit all accession counts and failure witnesses.

### 4.3 Patient-level identity/content estimands

Let I_y be the union of unique patients in at least one eligible target-frame state in year y. Let D_y be the union of P_LS(y,w). If D_y is empty, the result is nonvacuous-inconclusive.

For each i in D_y, define:

    r_ID(i,y,q,w)   = 1{i not in S_G(y,w,q)}
    r_CONT(i,y,q,w) = 1{i not in S_G(y,w,q) OR A_i(y,w,q)=0}.

The state-coupled, one-patient losses are:

    L_ID(i,y,q)   = max over w in which i is eligible r_ID(i,y,q,w)
    L_CONT(i,y,q) = max over w in which i is eligible r_CONT(i,y,q,w).

The primary finite-corpus estimands are:

    theta_ID_y(q)   = sum_i L_ID(i,y,q)   / |D_y|
    theta_CONT_y(q) = sum_i L_CONT(i,y,q) / |D_y|.

The numerator is a number of distinct patients, never a number of state-patient cells, accessions or reader/pathology rows. The same coherent state must supply source clock, accession exposure, reader book, pathology book and all labels. No favorable source state may be combined with an unfavorable pathology or reader state from another world.

The decomposition is mandatory:

    theta_SRCONLY_y(q)
      = sum_i 1{L_ID(i,y,q)=0 AND L_CONT(i,y,q)=1} / |D_y|.

Thus a high aggregate Q cannot conceal loss of one protected patient, and a queue that retains the count but drops a protected patient fails the identity estimand.

### 4.4 Finite operational-visibility state and partial identification

For every eligible accession a of patient i, add a lifecycle status lambda_ia at t_dec from the finite set:

- U0: not authored or no report instance identifiable;
- U1: authored/stored but not finalized or released by t_dec;
- U2: released but not retrieved by the consuming workflow by t_dec;
- U3: retrieved but not parse-readable at t_dec;
- U4: readable/available in the workflow but not documented as viewed;
- U5: documented as viewed before t_dec;
- UX: contradictory or unresolved lifecycle evidence.

These are as-of statuses, not eventual states. In a future archive-linked study, an immutable report/version key, event timestamps and payload hash would constrain lambda. In the current HCC snapshot, no lifecycle column constrains it. Therefore the admissible operational state for a patient is the finite vector

    ell_i = (lambda_ia : a in A_i)
      in {U0,U1,U2,U3,U4,U5,UX}^{|A_i|},

coupled to the same source state w, with no patient/accession status mixing across queues, thresholds, readers or pathology books. The global state space is finite because the source contains finitely many raw accession rows. It may be evaluated by per-patient enumeration or dynamic programming; it must not be sampled or treated as a statistical replicate.

Define two explicit operational endpoints:

    AVAIL_i(ell_i) = 1{lambda_ia in {U4,U5} for every eligible a}
    VIEW_i(ell_i)  = 1{lambda_ia = U5 for every eligible a}.

For Z in {AVAIL, VIEW}, define:

    r_Z(i,y,q,w,ell)
      = 1{i not in S_G(y,w,q) OR Z_i(ell_i)=0},

    theta_Z_minus_y(q)
      = sum_i min over (w,ell) in Omega_i r_Z(i,y,q,w,ell) / |D_y|,

    theta_Z_plus_y(q)
      = sum_i max over (w,ell) in Omega_i r_Z(i,y,q,w,ell) / |D_y|.

The minimization and maximization are over the same admissible coupled source/lifecycle worlds; no component may be selected independently. Also emit the identification width

    W_Z_y(q) = theta_Z_plus_y(q) - theta_Z_minus_y(q).

With the current HCC evidence, the lifecycle component is unconstrained: if a protected patient has any eligible accession, a world in which all of that patient's accessions are U5 and a world in which at least one is U0–U3 or UX are both admissible. Hence the operational interval is generally wide even when theta_CONT=0. This is a result about missing evidence, not a claim that reports were unavailable. If an accession has no identifiable source row, it remains a source-content failure; it is not silently assigned U5.

The primary hypothesis does not condition selection on Z, because doing so would manufacture visibility from unavailable fields. Operational visibility is a required bridge assessment:

- if external lifecycle evidence is supplied, evaluate the locked q* at the observed/verified ell states;
- if it is absent, report [theta_Z_minus, theta_Z_plus], its width and operational_visibility_unidentified, and do not call the stored-content result operationally available.

### 4.5 Threshold selection and untouched temporal test

Define the 2020 selection gate G20(q)=1 iff all required provenance, clock, source-state, reader/pathology-book, nonvacuity, fixed-K, model-replay, comparator, random, deletion and inherited PASS gates pass; all required states have K certified queue positions; and

    theta_ID_2020(q)=0, theta_CONT_2020(q)=0.

Select exactly:

    q* = min{q in R: q<1 and G20(q)=1}.

The 2020 artifact freezes R, q*, every rejected lower threshold, its first failing witness, all patient-level losses, all source certificates, all queue hashes and all state hashes. If no q<1 passes, the sub-complete identity/content hypothesis is adverse after valid computation. A q*=1 is also adverse for the sub-complete hypothesis. No 2021 result or operational-visibility bound may influence q*.

Apply the byte-identical q* once to 2021. Compute theta_ID_2021(q*), theta_CONT_2021(q*), all required state and certificate witnesses, fixed-K queues, comparators, random margins and anchored deletions. Do not search a 2021 threshold, choose a favorable lifecycle state, pool years, average books or use a 2021 frontier.

The primary temporal hypothesis is:

> A rational q*<1, selected solely from 2020, passes the locked 2020 identity/content gate and, without any 2021 tuning, has theta_ID_2021(q*)=theta_CONT_2021(q*)=0, with all required fixed-capacity, comparator, deletion, source, reader and pathology gates passing.

This is falsifiable by one protected patient omitted from the queue or one protected patient with one unexposed/ambiguous/unclean eligible accession in any required coherent state.

## 6. Interpretation

Use this precedence:

1. computationally_inconclusive if replay, source/hash, ordinal, join, clock, quarantine, forbidden-feature, model/book/state, accession, score/tie, fixed-K, deletion, threshold, patient-loss, lifecycle-state, exact-arithmetic or prose-linkage verification fails;
2. feasibility_inconclusive if required N,K, event, eligible accession or complete-book conditions fail;
3. 2020_selection_adverse_no_subcomplete_identity_content_threshold if valid computation finds no q<1 satisfying G20, or q*=1;
4. inherited_comparator_or_deletion_adverse if a required comparator, random, source, reader, pathology or deletion gate fails;
5. temporal_nonreplication_adverse if valid q*<1 was selected but either theta_ID_2021(q*)>0, theta_CONT_2021(q*)>0, or a required 2021 certificate/K condition fails;
6. finite_corpus_patientwise_content_supportive if 2020 and untouched 2021 both have zero identity/content loss and all required gates and witnesses pass;
7. append operational_visibility_unidentified when the lifecycle archive/workflow evidence is absent or the identification interval is nonzero;
8. if lifecycle evidence is later independently supplied and the locked q* fails observed AVAIL/VIEW retention, report operational_visibility_adverse for that external validation, without changing the retrospective HCC result or retuning q.

Supportive means only that the fixed HCC snapshot reproduces a finite-corpus patient-level identity and stored-content property at the frozen threshold. Adverse means a specified patient-level identity/content or temporal gate failed. Inconclusive means computation, feasibility or the required operational evidence cannot support the relevant claim. In particular, an operational interval with a worst-case failure is not by itself evidence that reports were unavailable; it is evidence that availability is not identified.

Every current HCC result must append operational_metadata_unavailable. No output may call the result operationally available, biologically MVI-valid, clinician-action validated, causal, beneficial, safe, fair, transportable or deployable.

## 8. External evidence required for the stronger clinical bridge

A separate archive/workflow study is required before any claim about as-of availability or clinician access. It must provide, for every whole examination accession:

- immutable report and version identifiers, predecessor/amendment/retraction lineage and payload hashes;
- authored, finalized, released, ingestion, retrieval, parse-readable and view timestamps with timezone provenance;
- an as-of-t_dec payload snapshot, including missing/nonresponder accounting;
- workflow identity and evidence of whether the intended reader could access the report;
- expert adjudication of the hepatobiliary resection dictionary and Chinese radiology/pathology semantics;
- specimen/accession/time linkage and pathology adjudication if biological MVI is claimed;
- a predeclared target population, sampling frame, inclusion mechanism and independent patient-level endpoint ascertainment if future-patient uncertainty is claimed.

The locked HCC q* may be evaluated in that study, but cannot be reselected using the external results without a new prospectively declared protocol. If the external study observes a visibility failure, that is an operational adverse result; it does not retroactively turn the current HCC eventual-content calculation into a visibility result.
