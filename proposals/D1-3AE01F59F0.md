# Result: Episode-33 late-lactate nonclearance experiment is inconclusive under the frozen gates

This child executes [prior hypothesis] on the complete EICU snapshot without changing its early recorded norepinephrine/Levophed definition, 24-hour landmark, baseline and 12–24-hour lactate rules, binary <20% clearance exposure, late-value-conditioned overlap estimand, +10-percentage-point margin, covariates, hospital gate, 5,000-replicate copied-hospital/person refit bootstrap, leave-one-hospital-out deletion analysis, or noncausal limits.

The strongest result supported by the computation is narrow: the crude mortality difference associated with nonclearance was large, but after weighting toward measured overlap on absolute late lactate and recorded pre-window context, the point estimate was near zero and highly uncertain. The rule-conformant overlap-weighted risks were 44.71% for clearance and 43.79% for nonclearance, RD **−0.92 percentage points**, with a 95% bootstrap interval **−11.26 to +10.19 points**. This does not support the prespecified claim of at least +10 points. It also does not establish an adverse or opposite result, because the interval includes +10 and zero and required gates fail.

The deterministic primary label is **inconclusive**. Three independent reasons suffice: (1) the explicit age rule produces 1,506 measured and 1,229 retained people rather than the frozen legacy count gate of 1,468/1,198; (2) 177 fitted propensities fall outside [0.02,0.98], with range 0.000141–0.992760; and (3) the interval includes +10 points. Only 3/33 leave-hospital-out deletion RDs were positive, also failing the supportive site-direction condition. No sensitivity or count-reproduction audit rescues the primary result.

The substantive advance is resolution of why the unadjusted association cannot establish incremental risk information. Crude mortality was 32.11% in clearance and 57.60% in nonclearance (difference +25.49 points), but the late-value/context model nearly determines exposure for many people. The observed data therefore do not supply the frozen common-support target needed to separate a material binary-trajectory risk gradient from the absolute late lactate. This is evidence of nonestimability under the locked design, not evidence that lactate trajectory is useless.

## What the result means and does not mean

Supportive data would have required every gate, a lower confidence bound >=+10 points, and >=80% positive deletion RDs; that would support only a large, transportable recorded prognostic association and motivate prospective synchronized validation and a decision-impact study. Adverse data would require all gates and an upper bound below +10; it could reject the locked materiality claim without proving no association. Opposite data would require all gates and an upper bound below zero; it would not make nonclearance protective. These data are inconclusive because measured overlap and population-count gates fail and +10 remains inside the interval.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes/headers, complete-file scans, t0/person/landmark rules, lab release/conflict/tie handling, exposure and site gate, feature cutoffs, preprocessing, propensity model, weights, balance, ESS/concentration, bootstrap/deletions, sensitivities and deterministic classification. An automatic verifier must reject a supportive, adverse or opposite label for these outputs, any definitive interpretation after the overlap failure, and any causal or treatment-benefit claim.

EICU cannot adjudicate actual norepinephrine administration/start/dose, sepsis or shock truth, arterial versus venous lactate, assay indication, fluids, beta-agonists, hepatic clearance, source control, tissue perfusion, treatment limitations, exact death time/cause, or whether acting on trajectory helps. Those require clinical review and richer prospectively synchronized data. Decision utility requires a prospective impact study; treatment effects require randomized or credible target-trial evidence. The result is confined to this recorded, measurement-selected, 24-hour landmark population.
