## Parser, units, and repeated observations

Use respchartoffset as clinical time; retain respchartentryoffset only for lag diagnostics and never to place measurements. Trim outer whitespace and exact-match labels, case-sensitive, with no substring or synonym expansion. Components are FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`; PEEP: `PEEP`, `PEEP/CPAP`; pressure support: `Pressure Support`, `PS above PEEP`; set rate: `Vent Rate`, `VS RESP RATE`. Safety uses only vitalPeriodic numeric sao2 and respiration. Exclude peak/plateau/mean/pressure-control/total or spontaneous rate, oxygen-flow, and respiratoryCharting SaO2 labels from primary components.

Parse values only if the entire trimmed token matches `^[+-]?(?:\\d+(?:\\.\\d*)?|\\.\\d+)(?:[eE][+-]?\\d+)?$`; reject blanks, embedded units, ranges, inequalities and other strings, recording reason. No interpolation, carry-forward, or conversion. Retain PEEP and PS in [0,60], rate in [0,100], sao2 in [0,100], respiration in [0,200]. FiO2 is explicitly unit-ambiguous: retain only [21,100] for all three labels; values below 21, including 0.4, 2, and 3, are `unit_ambiguous_or_invalid`, not multiplied by 100; >100 invalid. Report label-specific rejection counts and a predeclared alternate unit audit, but do not tune conversion by outcomes.

For each component use pre-D intervals (D-1440,D-720] and (D-720,D], and post (D+360,D+1800]; six-hour gap fixed. Require at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by >=30 minutes per component-period. Improvement is any component reduction plus no adverse change in both safety signals: post SpO2 not >2 points below pre, respiration not >4 above pre. Missing either safety signal in either period is missing, not non-event. Export component indicators and mutually exclusive status.

## Missingness, exits, analysis, gates

Keep every frozen row; no unitdischargeoffset eligibility filter. If unitdischargeoffset <=D+1800, competing exit is death only if trimmed status equals Expired case-insensitively, otherwise discharge_transfer; unclassifiable status/location is truncation. Early exits cannot be event or observed non-event. Otherwise classify event, observed_nonevent, or missing reasons: no_pre_component, no_post_component, no_pre_post_safety, time_tie_conflict, parser_rejection_only, no_chart_source, source_error. respiratoryCare absence is not primary missingness. Report all statuses, component availability, lag, and exit/missingness by arm, landmark, hospital and recency variant. Primary RD is evaluation-minus-no-trial among observed endpoint rows, with exits/missingness displayed separately; fixed pre-D-only inverse-observation weighting and competing-exit multinomial display are sensitivities.

All predictors clinical time <=D; entry time cannot rescue leakage. Exclude post-D rows, endpoint availability, post-D care, treatment, and care-plan counts/values except frozen arm. Fail closed on violations. Use hospital-held-out baseline, label (arm-only increment), and opportunity-only models; micro and equal-hospital macro log loss/Brier, calibration, AUROC only when defined; stay-clustered bootstrap, patientunitstayid primary and uniquepid sensitivity.

Falsification: same-parser pre-D placebo, D-720 lead-time placebo, leave-one-component-out, chart-only versus both care-source sensitivities, all three recencies, documentation-density negative control, and 1,000 fixed-seed within-hospital-by-landmark arm permutations preserving counts. Overlap gate: >=5 hospitals, >=10 observed endpoint rows per arm, >=50% analyzed rows in those hospitals, no hospital >40% either arm. Supportive means primary and >=2 recencies positive, >=4/5 estimable hospital directions agree, label model improves over baseline+opportunity by >=0.01 in both micro and macro held-out log loss with stay-cluster 95% interval excluding zero, survives component/source ablations, placebos are absent/smaller, and permutation null is exceeded. Adverse means no advantage over opportunity-only, placebo/permutation-like, reversal across hospitals, or documentation-only signal. Inconclusive means sparse endpoint/overlap, interval spanning meaningful increment and zero, competing exits/missingness confounded, or airway-recency disagreement. These gates establish at most reproducible chart association; bedside intent, delivered settings, SBT/extubation and benefit/causality require adjudication or another study.
