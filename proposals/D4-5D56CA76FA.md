## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens repeatedly nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, FABP1, CHI3L1,
IL-6/CRP family) as disease-specific preclinical biomarkers. It is unresolved whether a
protein-diagnosis association marks a disease-specific process (claim A) or mainly a shared
systemic morbidity/inflammation axis elevated across many diagnoses (claim B) [K2]. Prediction is
not specificity: a signature predictive of one disease can be more parsimoniously explained by a
shared exposure and extend across unrelated conditions [K1]. Measured plasma proteins also carry
genetic/technical structure that can masquerade as shared systemic signal [K3].

The parent operationalized "shared" as predictive non-specificity via the held-out transfer gap
Delta_D = AUC(D) - mean_AUC(D'). Its strongest unexploited rival explanation, named in the rubric
under "Alternatives", is not addressed head-on: **chronological age (and sex) is a common cause
of both elevated circulating proteins and accumulated diagnoses**, so a large Delta_D that
survives sample splitting could still be produced by age/sex structure rather than by a
disease-preferential proteomic process. Two readings of the parent's own primary result therefore
remain indistinguishable: (B1) a genuine systemic-morbidity axis, or (B2) an aging/sex artifact
whose apparent non-specificity is just age-clocking. Distinguishing B1 from B2 decides whether a
circulating protein (or a composite axis) is a credible disease-specific early-detection target or
only a generic age/morbidity reporter - the same consequential decision the branch has been
building toward.

## This child's scientific delta: age/sex-decomposed transfer with a matched permutation null
Retain the parent's participant-level sample split and held-out transfer gap, and add:
1. **Age/sex residualization (new, decisive).** Residualize every standardized protein on
   age + age^2 + sex (fit on the discovery half only, applied to the held-out half). Recompute the
   transfer gap on residuals, Delta_D^resid, and report the erosion
   Delta_erosion = Delta_D^raw - Delta_D^resid per target disease D.
   - Claim A (disease-specific residual): Delta_D^resid > 0 and outside the null band.
   - Claim B1 (shared systemic axis beyond age): Delta_D^resid ~ 0 while a shared-axis term still
     predicts.
   - Claim B2 (aging/sex artifact): Delta_D^raw large but Delta_D^resid ~ 0, i.e. most of the gap
     is absorbed by age/sex residualization.
2. **Matched permutation null (new).** Build the Delta_D null by permuting the D label *within
   joint age-decile x sex strata* in the held-out half, so the null preserves the age/sex
   prevalence structure (replacing a generic permutation band that could be inflated by age).
3. **Positive-control disease pair (new).** Two pre-specified diseases expected to share
   inflammatory/metabolic biology act as a "should-transfer" reference to calibrate how much
   transfer is biological vs nuisance; the pre-specified unrelated-chapter set D' and the
   external-cause block V01-Y98 act as the "should-not-transfer" negative control.
4. **Age-band AUC (new).** Report AUC(D) within 10-year age bands to expose any discrimination
   that is only age-ordered.
The parent's secondary within-disease attenuation fraction AF is retained for comparison, but
Delta_D^resid vs its matched null is the primary falsification route.

## Baselines and substantive alternatives (method exploration, same question)
(i) age+sex only (baseline); (ii) flexible age spline (rival nuisance model); (iii) shared-axis
term added to each per-disease model; (iv) learned multi-protein elastic-net signature per disease
with cross-validation inside the discovery half. The elastic-net alternative is retained because
a single linear axis cannot recover non-linear, multi-protein, disease-specific structure; comparing
Delta_D^resid from the axis term vs the elastic-net signature tests whether any surviving residual
is a single-axis or a distributed multi-protein signal. Baseline vs alternative is a substantive
scientific comparison, not a performance chase.

## Estimands, decision rules, and falsification
Primary: Delta_D^resid = AUC_resid(D) - mean_resid_AUC(D') on the held-out half, with matched-null
band. Secondary: Delta_D^raw, Delta_erosion, and AF.
- Supportive of claim A: Delta_D^resid > 0 and excludes the matched-null band for a majority of
  included target diseases, and survives the elastic-net alternative.
- Supportive of claim B2 (aging/sex artifact): Delta_erosion is large and Delta_D^resid is inside
  the band for most diseases - an informative negative finding.
- Inconclusive: wide null bands, <the minimum included disease count, or sign instability across
  the age-spline and code-list sensitivity analyses.
Falsification: the child's mechanism-relevant prediction is refuted if the matched null (which
preserves age/sex structure) reproduces the raw gap, i.e. the gap is a nuisance artifact.
