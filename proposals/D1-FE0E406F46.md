# Working Proposal — Episode 6 (HCC island)

## Scientific question
In cirrhotic HCC patients undergoing TACE, does the EARLY (first post-procedure)
laboratory response — relative change in total bilirubin, ALT, platelets,
fibrinogen, albumin, hemoglobin versus the pre-procedure baseline — predict NEW
decompensation (new ascites, variceal/gastrointestinal bleeding, hepatic
encephalopathy, or liver failure) within 90 days, beyond baseline MELD and
pre-TACE portal-hypertension history?

## Rationale and opening
- The mature branch ([prior hypothesis], selected episode 5) tests
  the same early post-TACE lab deltas against UNSELECTED 90-day readmission.
  Discovery probes found only weak incremental signal (MELD-only AUC 0.504 →
  +0.037 with deltas; deltas-only GBM 0.567). Readmission is heterogeneous: most
  readmissions in a TACE cohort are for planned re-TACE or tumor follow-up, not
  hepatic decompensation.
- This episode's bounded probes (measured, see below) found the HCC dataset
  contains coded decompensation events in the diagnoses table (腹水 1,904;
  肝硬化失代偿期 2,750+; 消化道出血 1,311; 食管/胃底静脉曲张破裂出血 1,074+;
  肝性脑病 414; 肝衰竭 161) and a rich pre-TACE portal-hypertension history
  (脾大 38,313; 门静脉高压 14,006; 食管胃底静脉曲张 14,191; 门静脉血栓 1,569).
- Post-TACE hepatic decompensation is the complication that determines whether a
  patient can continue locoregional therapy or needs transplant listing. Published
  work (K1: Hsin 2011, 17.3% in HCC+ascites; K2: Leyh 2022, standard labs
  "difficult to predict") relies on small cohorts and baseline-only risk factors.
  Whether the EARLY POST-PROCEDURE lab response adds information beyond baseline
  MELD for this specific, clinically decisive outcome is the unresolved claim.

## Hypothesis
In HCC patients with cirrhosis undergoing TACE, the relative early post-procedure
change in bilirubin/ALT/platelets/fibrinogen/albumin/hemoglobin is independently
associated with new 90-day decompensation after adjusting for baseline MELD, age,
sex, and pre-TACE portal-hypertension history (prior varices, ascites,
encephalopathy, portal vein thrombosis, splenomegaly).

## Discovery probes (this episode, measured)
- encounters: 105,044 rows; patients with ≥1 TACE procedure: 19,596.
- Cohort with ≥90 days follow-up: n=8,542.
- NEW decompensation (diagnosis-coded) within 90 days of first TACE: 87 (1.0%).
- Pre-existing decompensation at index admission: 218 (2.6%).
- 死亡诊断 appears in only 29 encounters → no mortality outcome available.
- TIPS: 17 encounters → too rare as an outcome.
- Parent-branch probes (episode 5, reused): n=16,438 with pre+post labs;
  32.4% unselected 90-day readmission; MELD-only AUC 0.504; MELD+deltas LR AUC
  0.541; deltas-only GBM AUC 0.567.
- Central measurement uncertainty: the 1.0% diagnosis-coded decompensation rate is
  far below the 32.4% readmission rate, indicating substantial under-coding of
  decompensation in the diagnoses table. The readmission outcome is retained as a
  sensitivity arm; if the decompensation event count proves too small for stable
  estimation, the primary analysis falls to the inconclusive branch.

- Index event: first TACE per patient (min 开始时间 where 手术 contains 'TACE'

- Cohort: index TACE encounter with ≥1 pre-TACE lab and ≥1 post-TACE lab in the
  same encounter, and ≥90 days follow-up (max 出院时间 of later encounters).
- Exclusions: missing t0, missing encounter boundaries, no labs in either window.

## Temporal boundaries
- t0 = first TACE 开始时间.
- Pre-window: index admit ≤ t < t0 → last lab value per analyte.
- Post-window: t0 ≤ t ≤ index disch → first lab value per analyte.
- Outcome window: t0 < admit_next ≤ t0+90d.
- History window: all encounters admitted before index admit → portal-HT flags
  and pre-existing decompensation flag.

## Variables
- Baseline: MELD (总胆红素, 肌酐, 国际标准化比值/PT from labs), 年龄, 性别,
  pre-TACE portal-HT history flags (食管静脉曲张/胃底静脉曲张, 腹水, 肝性脑病,
  门静脉血栓形成, 脾大), pre-existing decompensation flag, baseline
  血小板计数/白蛋白/丙氨酸氨基转移酶/血红蛋白/纤维蛋白原.
- Early post-TACE: relative deltas d_analyte = (post − pre)/max(pre, ε) for
  总胆红素, 丙氨酸氨基转移酶, 血小板计数, 纤维蛋白原, 白蛋白, 血红蛋白.
- Primary outcome y90_decomp: any diagnosis containing 腹水 | 肝性脑病 | 肝衰竭 |
  消化道出血 | 上消化道出血 | 食管静脉曲张破裂出血 | 胃底静脉曲张破裂出血 |
  失代偿 (excluding 个人史/陈旧/恢复期/后遗症 terms) in an encounter admitted in
  (t0, t0+90d].
- Sensitivity outcome: unselected 90-day readmission (parent branch definition).

## Analysis plan (solver)
1. Cohort construction as above; report n, event counts, missingness, and the
   decompensation-vs-readmission rate gap (measurement audit).
2. Baseline model: logistic regression, MELD + age + sex + history flags
   (sub-cohort with complete MELD components) → AUC, Brier.
3. Extended model: baseline + 6 lab deltas (logistic; median imputation).
4. Learned alternative: HistGradientBoostingClassifier, same features, NaN-native.
5. Full-cohort deltas-only GBM (no MELD requirement) to address MELD missingness.
6. 70/30 patient-level train/test split (no patient in both).
7. Evaluation: AUC, Brier, logistic coefficients with 95% CIs, quartile
   decompensation rates for key deltas, calibration (mean predicted vs actual),
   feature importance for the GBM.
8. Falsification criteria:
   - SUPPORTIVE: extended-model AUC > baseline AUC + 0.02 in test, ≥1 lab delta
     significant (p<0.05) in the logistic model, and a graded quartile trend for
     ≥1 delta.
   - ADVERSE: extended-model AUC ≤ baseline AUC + 0.02, or no delta significant
     after adjusting for MELD and history flags.
   - INCONCLUSIVE: <100 decompensation events (underpowered), AUC difference
     0.01–0.02, or significant deltas but poor calibration.

## Baseline vs learned alternative
- Baseline (MELD + history flags): clinically established, interpretable.
- HistGBM: reveals threshold/interaction structure (e.g., a large relative
  bilirubin rise harmful only at high baseline bilirubin) that a linear model
  cannot express, and identifies which deltas drive the signal.
- What the alternative reveals: whether the decompensation signal is
  graded-linear or threshold/interaction-driven.

## Resource estimate
- Discovery probes (measured): full-file scans of encounters/procedures/diagnoses
  ran in 3–9 s each (4–8 CPU); cohort construction ~6 s. The labs scan (2.2 GB)
  is the dominant solver cost; expected <10 min on 8 CPU.
- Fitting: logistic + HistGBM on ≤10K rows × ~15 features → minutes on CPU.
- Solver budget: 16 CPU / 256 GB / 8 h (plenty). No GPU needed.

## Limitations (honest)
- Diagnosis-based outcome is a proxy: 诊断名称 is free-text Chinese coding
  (8,060 distinct terms); the term list may miss or over-include events. The
  1.0% vs 32.4% rate gap indicates substantial under-coding; the readmission
  sensitivity arm and the measurement audit are required.
- No mortality data (29 encounters with 死亡诊断).
- Single center; no imaging/tumor response; approximate day-1 lab timing.
- MELD missingness ~44% (parent probe) → full-cohort deltas-only arm.
- If the event count is <100, the primary outcome is underpowered and the study
  reports the inconclusive branch; the readmission analysis remains the powered arm.

## What results would establish
- Supportive: early post-TACE lab response is an independent, actionable marker
  of near-term hepatic decompensation → supports post-TACE lab-based triage for
  closer monitoring / transplant listing.
- Adverse: baseline MELD + portal-HT history capture the signal → do not add
  post-TACE labs to decompensation risk stratification.
- Inconclusive: under-coding or small event count → need cause-specific
  readmission coding or a larger/multi-center cohort before concluding either way.

## Key references
- [K1] Hsin et al. 2011, J Clin Gastroenterol (PMID 21666547): post-TACE liver
  failure in HCC with ascites — 17.3% incidence, CTP-B and GI bleeding as
  independent risk factors, 94% one-year mortality among liver-failure patients;
  baseline-only predictors, 2-week window.
- [K2] Leyh et al. 2022, Cancers (PMID 36230506): post-interventional hepatic
  decompensation "difficult to predict by standard laboratory liver function
  parameters"; LiMAx AUC 0.81 vs MELD/ALBI in a 50-patient RE cohort.
- [K3] Hund et al. 2023, JVIR (PMID 36481322): 30-day readmission after TACE in
  253 patients; baseline clinical status predictive; no post-procedure lab
  trajectory tested.

## Alternatives considered and deferred
- Keep parent's readmission-only outcome: deferred as primary (heterogeneous;
  planned re-TACE dominates); retained as sensitivity arm.
- Mortality outcome: unavailable (29 encounters with 死亡诊断).
- TIPS as outcome: 17 encounters — too rare.
- Neural sequence model (Delphi-style) over diagnosis/lab history: deferred —
  with ~1% event rate and 8.5K patients, the tabular LR/GBM comparison is the
  adequate first test; revisit only if the deltas signal is confirmed and larger
  data become available.
