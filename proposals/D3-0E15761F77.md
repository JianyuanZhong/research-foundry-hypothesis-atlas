## 1. Unresolved question and what changed this episode

The parent executed the transcriptome-wide H2' test (E-MTAB-8316 paired none-vs-UNC106238, job [research job]): zero DE genes at BH FDR<0.05 in 12,757 expressed genes, min q≈0.24, class medians |log2FC|≤0.03. The parent's unresolved claim was: **is this null power-bounded or biological?** and it named the detectable-effect analysis as the solver's first task. This episode **executed that task as managed compute** (job `[research job]`, state=succeeded, 4.33 s; script `work/lead/emtab8316_power_analysis.py` [source checksum]; result `work/lead/emtab8316_power_result.json` [source checksum]).

**Observed results (12,757 genes; 1,553 putative FLS-response / 11,204 macrophage-intrinsic, strict E-MTAB-8322 reference; 6 specimen pairs, 4 monocyte individuals):**

1. **Detection floor for a uniform stromal-output shift is Δ = 0.2 log2FC** (15% fold-change): adding a constant +0.2 to all treated log2-CPM values yields 2,595 BH-significant DE genes (177 FLS-response, 2,418 macrophage-intrinsic); Δ = 0.1 yields zero. A shift of the magnitude the LPS inflammatory-state axis actually produces in this experimental family (module score 1.64–1.81, episode-2 panel) is ~8× above the floor; at Δ=0.5 the design detects 9,717/12,757 genes (class-average per-gene power 0.81/0.87 at nominal α), at Δ=1.0, 12,304 genes (0.97/0.99).
2. **Per-gene minimum detectable effect (80% power, nominal paired-t α=0.05)**: median 0.42 log2FC (IQR 0.32–0.58) for putative FLS-response genes, 0.34 (IQR 0.24–0.49) for macrophage-intrinsic. At the Bonferroni family-wise level (α=3.9e-6, t_crit=21.6) the median single-gene MDE rises to 2.74 / 2.22 log2FC — the design's strength is **module-level (coordinated-shift) detection**, not single-gene family-wise detection.
3. **Label-permutation calibration (B=500 within-specimen treatment-label swaps)**: mean DE count 0.026, max 1, 97.4% of permutations produce zero DE genes. The pipeline's FDR control is therefore not silently over-conservative — it declares DE genes when a real coordinated shift exists (shift curve above) and essentially none under the sharp null.
4. **Individual-level (n=4) MDE**: median 0.49 (FLS) / 0.37 (MAC) log2FC at nominal α — donor-aware inference costs ~15% in detectable effect, not a qualitative change.
5. **Baseline reproduction check**: Δ=0 re-computation returned 0 DE genes (asserted in-script), matching the parent's executed result exactly.

**Adjudication per the pre-specified rule (fixed in the script before running): `intermediate_power_bounded`** — the null is NOT globally power-bounded (the design would have detected any uniform module shift ≥0.2 log2FC and, with ≥80% power per gene, single-gene effects ≥~0.34–0.42 log2FC), and it is NOT a full biological-scale null (sparse per-gene effects below ~0.34–0.42 log2FC, and any effect in the 0.1<Δ<0.2 uniform band, remain undetectable; 2.6% of permutations produced 1 false DE gene, so the strict calibration criterion was not met). **The claim therefore narrows to: acute MerTK-pathway inhibition produces no uniform stromal-output shift ≥0.2 log2FC and no single-gene effect of ≥0.34–0.42 log2FC (median) — while the live alternative axis in the same experimental family (macrophage inflammatory-state pretreatment, LPS) produces shifts ~8× the detection floor.**

**Clinical/mechanistic consequence.** The failed-resolution framework proposes single-arm resolution strategies including restoring MerTK-dependent efferocytic signaling [K1], harnessing efferocytosis-driven reprogramming [K2], and manipulating pro-resolving subsets [K3]. With the power bound now quantified, the executed null becomes decision-relevant: at the stromal-output level, acute single-arm MerTK-pathway modulation is **quantitatively dominated** by macrophage inflammatory-state reprogramming (no detectable shift at a floor 8× below the LPS-axis magnitude). This is exactly the pattern the redundant-circuit model predicts — "blocking any single mediator can lower inflammatory output while leaving resolution unrestored" [K1] — so the null is *consistent with* (not evidence against) redundancy-based circuit models, and deprioritization applies to MerTK as a *single* arm at the stromal-output level, not to combination or state-reprogramming strategies [K2][K3]. What the null still cannot exclude — sparse small per-gene effects (<0.4 log2FC), effects conditional on a non-LPS baseline (all 12 E-MTAB-8316 samples sit under LPS 1 ng/mL, where MerTK signaling may already be suppressed), and insufficient target engagement by UNC106238 at dose 250 — remains the explicit boundary of the claim.

## 5. Competing explanations and limitations

- **Sparse-effect escape**: the 0.2 log2FC floor is for *uniform* shifts; a perturbation acting on few genes requires per-gene MDE (0.34–0.42 median nominal) or, family-wise, 2.2–2.7 log2FC — small sparse effects remain formally undetectable.
- **Context dependence**: all samples under LPS 1 ng/mL; MerTK signaling may be LPS-suppressed, so the null is conditional on an inflammatory baseline (the non-LPS-baseline contrast exists only at the E-MTAB-8873 targeted-panel scale).
- **Target engagement**: UNC106238 dose 250 inhibition of MerTK in these cultures is unmeasured; a null is consistent with insufficient engagement.
- **Permutation design**: within-pair label swaps assume exchangeability under the sharp null within specimen — appropriate for the paired design but does not probe between-specimen structure; 2.6% of permutations yielded 1 DE gene (Monte-Carlo-scale marginal anti-conservatism; recorded, does not affect the floor-based claim).
- **Nested specimen factor** (episode-4 result, 75.2% specimen R²) still bundles FLS-donor, monocyte-donor and pair-level context — unresolved, mixed model deferred.
- **Classification error** (E-MTAB-8322 reference cohort/modality mismatch; scRNA dropout) — both strict and lenient thresholds agree in outcome at every executed Δ.
- **Ensembl-only feature space**; no wet-lab validation; no in-vivo or cross-cohort claims; known NOTCH3/HBEGF/PGDH findings untouched.

## 6. Falsification criteria for the refined claim (pre-specified for the solver)

- **Claim**: acute MerTK-pathway inhibition produces no uniform stromal-output shift ≥0.2 log2FC and no median-level single-gene effect ≥0.34–0.42 log2FC in RA synovial coculture under LPS.
- **Supportive (of the refined claim)**: the E-MTAB-8873 non-LPS-baseline UNC106238 contrast, analyzed donor-aware with this power framework, also shows effects below its own computed floor → the null extends to the non-inflammatory baseline; OR the variance-component mixed model reproduces the ≤5% treatment variance share.
- **Refuting**: a dispersion-aware sparse-effect analysis (or higher-powered replication with n≥12 pairs) finds FDR-significant per-gene shifts in the 0.2–0.4 log2FC band with donor-aware intervals excluding zero → the null was power-bounded at the sparse scale and the MerTK arm reopens.
- **Inconclusive**: mixed-model variance decomposition unstable under the nested design (specimen/donor collinearity) → composition-artifact and FLS-intrinsic questions return to the design stage (crossed-donor mixed-ratio coculture named as the missing design, unchanged from episode 2).
