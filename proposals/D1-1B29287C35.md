## 1. Unresolved question and the two competing explanations

Among ICU patients with *persistent* hyperlactatemia (index lactate ≥2.0 mmol/L, then a second lactate ≥2.0 mmol/L at t* within 6–48 h, within the ICU stay), does the **direction of perfusion change** (improving vs worsening, from MAP / vasopressor-load / urine-output trajectory) at the same achieved lactate level modify 7-day deterioration risk beyond lactate level and clearance?

The consequential clinical decision: should persistent hyperlactatemia be read as **ongoing hypoperfusion (escalate support)** or as **non-hypoperfusion stress hyperlactatemia (avoid over-resuscitation)**? [K3] argues the classic "high lactate = anaerobic hypoperfusion" reading is often wrong (aerobic/adrenergic stress), so the two readings are live. [K2] shows the same lactate value has context-dependent meaning but gives no quantitative context effect.

**Leading claim (main branch, H1):** perfusion *direction* carries prognostic information beyond level/clearance.
**Rival (this branch, H0):** any apparent context effect is a **measurement-selection + severity artifact** — a "sicker, more-closely-monitored patients look worse and get measured more" mixture. [K1] establishes that the *act and frequency* of laboratory testing is itself informative (informative presence) and can drive apparent predictor–outcome associations. This branch asks whether the rival fully explains the signal.

## 2. Rival-friendly expectation: sign and magnitude of the artifact

Mechanism and pre-declared expectation:
- Measurement intensity (lactate draws/day, MAP chartings/day, urine recordings/day) is positively associated with severity and with organ-support; both are positively associated with 7-day deterioration. So crude "worsening-context" arms will look worse **partly** because they are measured more (more chances to chart a falling MAP / a low urine hour).
- A *worsening* window by definition samples the low tail of MAP/urine more densely; a *non-worsening* window is not challenged, so clinicians order fewer confirmatory measurements.
- Predicted sign of numerator bias: the context coefficient is **positive and inflated** in an unadjusted model; adjustment for measurement intensity should **attenuate** it.
- Predicted order of magnitude (to be **estimated**, not assumed): if H0 is right, the M1 (lactate+context) − M2 (+measurement intensity) change in the context effect should be a large fraction (say ≥50%) of the M1 estimate, and the permutation control (M3) should reproduce a similar apparent context effect from a context variable that carries no perfusion information.

## 3. Population, landmark, temporal boundaries (SAME as main branch → comparable)

- Adults ≥18 (`patients.anchor_age + (year − anchor_year)`), first ICU stay.
- Persistent-hyperlactatemia landmark: index lactate ≥2.0 mmol/L (`labevents.itemid=50813`, `valuenum`), then a second lactate ≥2.0 mmol/L at **t\*** within **6–48 h** after the index, both within the ICU stay (`labevents.charttime` between `icustays.intime` and `icustays.outtime`; `labevents.hadm_id = icustays.hadm_id`).
- Predictor window: perfusion-context direction computed from measurements in `[t* − W, t*]`, W = 6 h (lock before the landmark; no future information).
- Outcome window: 7 days after t* — deterioration = in-ICU/hospital death (`admissions.deathtime`, `hospital_expire_flag`) OR sustained worsening organ support, adjudicated in the compile-time outcome spec.

## 4. Variables and data-derived quantities

- Lactate level/clearance: index and t* lactate (`50813`); clearance = (index − t*)/index.
- Perfusion context (rival-triggering variable): MAP trend slope (`chartevents` itemid `220052` arterial, `220181` non-invasive; mmHg); vasopressor load trend (norepi `221906`, epi `221289`, dopamine `221662`, dobutamine `221653`, vasopressin `222315`, phenylephrine `221749` in `inputevents`; norepinephrine-equivalent from `rate`/`rateuom`/`patientweight`); urine-output rate trend (items `226559,226560,226566,226627,226631,226713,227489` in `outputevents`; `value`/`valueuom`, per hour). Direction = sign/model of the within-window trajectory.
- **Measurement intensity (the confounder this branch foregrounds):** `n_lactate/W`, `n_MAP/W`, `n_urine/W`, and total charting density in the window — counts directly from `chartevents`/`outputevents`/`labevents` timestamps.
- Severity: admission SOFA-style organ-support/critical-illness markers available at/before t* (vasopressor presence, mechanical-ventilation proxy, RRT from `procedureevents`), `anchor_age`, and comorbidity count (`diagnoses_icd` + `d_icd_diagnoses`).

## 5. Method ladder (baselines + alternative)

| Model | Regressors | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| **M0** severity-only | age + severity markers at t* | baseline |
| **M1** + perfusion context | M0 + context-direction term (+ lactate level/clearance) | naive context signal |
| **M2** + measurement intensity | M1 + charting-frequency terms | does context survive adjustment for observation intensity? |
| **M3** permutation negative control | replace context direction with a permuted/irrelevant trajectory (same intensity distribution) | floor for a "spurious" context effect |

Primary estimand: incremental **ΔAUROC / ΔAIC** and the context coefficient for M1→M2, with bootstrap CIs. A simple interpretable logistic baseline is the main analysis; a bounded alternative (regularized/penalized model or a small gradient-boosted model with SHAP on the context term) is included **only** to check that M2's attenuation is not a functional-form artifact. Chosen after the scientific question; model complexity is a tradeoff, not merit.

## 7. Falsification criteria (prespecified)

- **SUPPORTIVE of rival H0 (context signal is spurious):** after adjustment for measurement intensity (M2), the context-direction effect is attenuated by ≥50% of the M1 estimate, its 95% CI includes 0, and the M3 permutation reproduces a comparable apparent effect. → interpretability claim **NOT supported** (informative negative).
- **ADVERSE to H0 (context signal survives):** context-direction effect remains with 95% CI excluding 0 after M2 (attenuation <25%), and M3 shows the expected near-null. → the main branch's interpretability claim gains support (association only; still not causal).
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** intermediate attenuation (25–50%), wide CIs, too few persistently-hyperlactatemic stays, or incomplete MAP/urine charting → cannot distinguish; specify additional data needs. Imprecise nulls do not refute.

## 8. What this can and cannot establish

- Establishes (computable): whether the perfusion-context association in this cohort is robust to measured observation intensity and severity — a validity check on the main branch.
- Does **not** establish: cause, or that a surviving association is true hypoperfusion (these measures cannot diagnose microcirculatory dysfunction; [K1][K2][K3] all bound this). Any causal/mechanistic claim needs a different design, clinical adjudication or another study.
