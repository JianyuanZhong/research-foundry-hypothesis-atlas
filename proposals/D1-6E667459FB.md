# Persistent charted respiratory-setting de-escalation: a transportability test after exact care-plan labels

## Parent lineage and substantive advance

This proposal is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`.

It preserves the first parent's validated adverse result: the exact eICU care-plan values `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` are not validated as a common multicenter phenotype in the frozen 540 person-landmark cohort. The successor does not reinterpret that result as proof of hospital-specific documentation conventions. It also preserves the second parent's central repair: replace a single minimum respiratory-setting value with an outcome-blind repeated-observation endpoint.

The clinical advance is a narrower, falsifiable question. Instead of asking whether a literal label means extubation readiness, liberation, or a bedside action, test whether it adds transportable information about a persistent, charted reduction in a numeric respiratory setting beyond pre-landmark physiology, therapy-setting history, and documentation opportunity. A supportive result would justify only a structured chart-prediction use. An adverse result would strengthen the case against using the labels as an unqualified multicenter phenotype. Neither result establishes delivered therapy, ventilation status, SBT performance, extubation, liberation, quality, benefit, harm, or causality.

## Claims and evidence boundary

The strongest evidence already supported by the parents is:

* In the exact frozen target-labelled cohort, labels are highly concentrated by hospital and operational mappings are peak-pressure dominated, site-sensitive, landmark-sensitive, and not independently validated as liberation outcomes.
* The prior single-minimum numeric endpoint is vulnerable to a transient, copied, or erroneous value.
* A repeated numeric endpoint is computationally feasible in the full adult first-stay scan, but broad-scan feasibility counts are not confirmatory evidence in the frozen cohort.

The unresolved claim tested here is:

> Conditional on the frozen parent cohort and exact decision time, adding the care-plan label to a pre-landmark/process model improves held-out-hospital prediction of a subsequent persistent reduction in at least one charted FiO2, PEEP, pressure-support, or set ventilator-rate value.

The claim is not that the label causes a reduction or identifies a clinically appropriate intervention. The relevant estimand is predictive transportability of a chart proxy.

## Primary endpoint and missingness

Use only `respiratoryCharting` to construct the primary endpoint, before inspecting care-plan semantics. Recognize exactly these canonical labels and ranges:

* FiO2: `FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, and `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`; convert percentages to fractions; valid range 0–1; threshold 0.05.
* PEEP: `PEEP`; valid range 0–50 cmH2O; threshold 2.
* Pressure support: `Pressure Support`; valid range 0–60 cmH2O; threshold 2.
* Set ventilator rate: `Vent Rate`; valid range 0–80 breaths/minute; threshold 2.

For each landmark L and component c:

1. Pre-window is `[L-360,L]`.
2. Post-window is `(L+360,L+1080]`; strict inequality prevents classification-window leakage.
3. Use the last valid pre-window value as baseline.
4. Require at least two valid post-window readings that are each at least the component threshold below baseline.
5. Require the qualifying readings to be separated by at least 120 minutes.
6. Require the median of all valid post-window readings for that component to remain at least the threshold below baseline.
7. The primary binary endpoint is one if any component qualifies.

Invalid, noncanonical, nonfinite, and out-of-range values are discarded. Inadequate observation is missing/unavailable, never a negative endpoint. Retain component, baseline, post count, qualifying count, separation, post median, and event status for audit. Exact duplicate component-time-value rows must be audited and either deterministically deduplicated or shown not to affect the endpoint. Separation must be calculated among qualifying readings, not merely among arbitrary post-window observations.

Prespecified sensitivity endpoints are component-specific events, the prior single-minimum endpoint, and airway-bounded versions. The latter must use independent source evidence and cannot silently change the primary denominator.

Fit three models independently in every leave-one-hospital-out fold:

1. Process baseline, excluding target labels.
2. Process plus exact labels.
3. Process plus the prespecified broader labels, only after raw-source construction makes this encoding nonidentical to exact labels.

Learn imputation, scaling, feature selection, and model parameters in training hospitals only. A held-out hospital is eligible only when it has at least 20 rows and both outcome classes. Report:

* row-weighted micro log-loss and Brier score;
* equal-hospital macro log-loss;
* AUROC and calibration as secondary descriptions;
* endpoint prevalence, component-specific event rates, and endpoint availability;
* hospital-by-hospital direction counts and label/event concentration;
* results by landmark and by each airway-bounded definition.

The primary transport estimands are broader-minus-process and broader-minus-exact performance differences, both micro and equal-hospital macro. The exact-label model is not a claim that the label is causal; it is the prespecified comparator.

Use 100 or more stay-cluster bootstrap replicates, sampling all landmarks from a stay together. Refit preprocessing and models inside each replicate and each hospital-held-out fold. Do not treat repeated landmarks as independent bootstrap clusters. Report percentile intervals and the fraction of replicates in which the label model is worse.

## Decision gates and interpretation

Supportive transportability requires all of the following in the frozen cohort: the label model improves both micro and macro log-loss over process and exact comparators in the unbounded definition and at least two airway-bounded definitions; nested 97.5% intervals for the primary improvements are below zero; at least five unbounded eligible hospitals and at least three eligible hospitals in two bounded definitions; no improvement is confined to one component or one hospital; and placebo/unrelated-control results do not show an equally strong generic documentation signal.

Adverse transportability is declared if the label model fails to beat process in every bounded definition, is pointwise worse in at least two bounded definitions with at least 0.75 bootstrap probability of worse macro loss, or has no stable advantage over the exact comparator. This means the labels are not validated as a transportable predictor of the persistent chart proxy; it does not prove absence of bedside information.

Inconclusive means mixed bounds, sparse eligible hospitals or events, intervals spanning zero, failure to create a nonidentical broader exposure, or disagreement between the persistent and single-minimum endpoints. A positive single-minimum result with an adverse persistent result indicates sensitivity to transient/copy artifacts and does not rescue a clinical interpretation.

Automatic verification can establish source hashes, schema columns, frozen artifact identity, joins, time inequalities, outcome-blind endpoint construction, parser ranges, repeated-reading logic, LOHO separation, bootstrap clustering, and arithmetic of gates. It cannot establish whether a charted value reflects delivered therapy, whether a patient was invasively ventilated, clinician intent, SBT delivery, extubation, liberation, appropriateness, quality, benefit, harm, or external transportability. Those require device-linked data, local workflow metadata, blinded clinical adjudication, or replication.
