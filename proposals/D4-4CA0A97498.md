## Estimand and implementation

For pair `q` in `{CRC versus other-GI, CRC versus non-GI}`, retain only its two groups, define `A=1` for CRC, and let `C=(age at L, sex, repeat Hb, repeat MCV, repeat platelet, lag_late)`. For each standardized marker `Z_j`, the frozen target is

`theta_qj = E_q[m_1j(C)-m_0j(C)]`,

where the outer expectation is over the empirical pair covariate distribution. The held-out AIPW score is

`phi_qj = m_1(C)-m_0(C) + A/e(C)*(Z_j-m_1(C)) - (1-A)/(1-e(C))*(Z_j-m_0(C))`.

This is a selected recorded-case associational standardization, not a causal effect. It assumes operational positivity and conditional mean exchangeability for identification; those assumptions are not clinically proven by computation.

Nuisances were pair-specific ridge logistic propensity models and arm-specific ridge linear outcome models, using penalties `10^-4,...,10^4`, the largest loss-tied penalty (`1e-12` tolerance), and unpenalized propensity intercept and `lag_late`. Continuous nuisance predictors used training-population means and `ddof=0` SDs. The execution used 20 repetitions of five outer folds, seeds 20260909--20260928, with `A|sex|lag_late` stratification in both pairs. Each participant contributed one held-out score per repetition; the 20 scores were averaged within participant before the estimate and covariance. Repeated appearances were never treated as independent.

For propensity gates, the 20 held-out predictions were averaged within participant before pair-level percentiles, range, weights, ESS, log loss, Brier score, and calibration were calculated. Repetition-level summaries were retained as diagnostics.

## Shared-overlap familywise inference

Inference used the union of the original 998 participants. In each of exactly 20,000 draws (seed 20280909), one standard-normal multiplier was assigned per union participant. The same draw was reused for each of the 75 CRC participants shared by the two pairs; participants absent from a pair contributed zero. Pair normalizers were 144 and 929. The draw-wise maximum absolute studentized perturbation across all six components supplied the global p-value and family-simultaneous critical value; pair-local maxima supplied descriptive subordinate tests. The hierarchy remained global family, then pair, then component.
