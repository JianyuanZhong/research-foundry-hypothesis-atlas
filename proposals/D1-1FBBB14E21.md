# Fixed offered-slot yield for HCC documentary-M2 review allocation

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and advance

The clinical question is whether interpretable semantics already present in routine preoperative CT/MRI report bodies could help a scarce multidisciplinary review service direct a fixed annual allotment toward resected HCC patients whose eventual pathology documents high-grade microvascular invasion (M2). The studied action is allocation of an additional review. It is not a treatment rule and does not estimate effects on surgical extent, transplantation, systemic treatment, recurrence, survival, harms, costs, or patient benefit.

The strongest claim currently supported by the available evidence is descriptive. HCC snapshot [source checksum] contains patient/encounter-linked demographics, completed procedures with imperfect start/end clocks, examination accessions with acquisition-like start times and eventual report text, untimed pathology narratives, and dated medication/order rows. It can support a temporally replayed benchmark inside a future-defined completed-resection cohort. It does not contain a preoperative referral/candidate roster, planned-operation/cancellation ledger, actual review slots or completions, report authored/final/release/view times or immutable versions, pathology times/specimen/slide/block keys or sampling protocols, clinician actions, treatments caused by review, outcomes, harms, costs, or benefits. Thus it cannot establish a real service opportunity set or workflow effect.

Current full-text evidence was acquired and inspected. Neves and Soares (2026; DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028; PMCID PMC13360217) reviewed 36 HCC early-recurrence AI studies: 35 were retrospective, six reported external validation, and calibration, decision-curve analysis, and clinical utility were inconsistent. Fan et al. (2026; DOI 10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944; PMCID PMC13187967) reported M2 in 106/621 resected HCC patients; LDH was associated with MVI severity but had modest discrimination (AUC 0.610 for M1/M2) and was proposed only as an adjunct pending external prospective validation. These papers establish clinical importance and the weakness of current deployment evidence, not report-semantic value or benefit in this snapshot.

The substantive advance is a falsifiable, capacity-normalized online replay that does not confuse policy-specific first-J acceptances with a common opportunity set. The primary endpoint counts useful cases per fixed slot offered over the same complete annual horizon and assigns zero yield to unused slots. It separates incremental report-semantic value from an independently fitted nonsemantic model and provides an exact descriptive decomposition into utilization and accepted-case composition without pretending either component is a causal contrast among common candidates.

The research-ambition README and availability limitations were inspected. The unavailable Cell main article and full STAR Methods were not read and are not evidence here.

## Design choice: why offered-slot yield is primary

A periodic weekly or monthly selection epoch is not defensible in this snapshot. To rank a common batch before each member's deadline would require a timestamped planned-surgery/referral roster identifying future candidates already known at that epoch. Waiting until the batch closes would cross some members' preoperative deadlines. The source has neither planned procedures nor referral timestamps. Calendar bins may release capacity, but decisions must remain arrival-by-arrival at each pseudo-deadline.

Likewise, the first J acceptances of two online policies are generally reached on different dates after different numbers of arrivals. Equal accepted counts do not create equal opportunity sets. First-J yield is retained only as a policy-specific prefix diagnostic and must be reported with the completion date and arrivals encountered. It is neither primary nor evidence of isolated prioritization.

## Fixed workload, policies, and estimands

Let s index a complete coherent source state. A state jointly fixes patient membership, operative episode and clock, encounter and age, prior recorded treatment, pseudo-arrival order, examination identity and clock, modality, report visibility, pathology composite, and documentary-M2 outcome. Components from different states may never be mixed.

For each state and historical year a=2015,...,2019, construct the completed-case pseudo-roster without parsing pathology grade or report semantics and let its size be N_a(s). Define N_ref=min_s min_a N_a(s). Freeze K_q=floor(q*N_ref) for q in {0.05,0.10,0.20} using only information dated no later than 31 December 2019, and apply the same absolute K_q in 2020 and 2021. Define J_q=ceil(0.90*K_q) for the secondary prefix diagnostic. A q is confirmatorily feasible only if K_q>=50, J_q>=45, and every coherent 2020/2021 state contains at least K_q pseudo-arrivals. Neither K nor J may depend on test-year roster size, outcomes, visibility, policy, or bootstrap refit.

Map each decision time to scaled year u=(t_dec-Jan1)/(next Jan1-Jan1) and 366 fixed bins. From 2015-2019 pseudo-arrivals only, freeze a monotonized cumulative release fraction F_ref(u) and L_q(u)=floor(K_q F_ref(u)), with L_q(1)=K_q. An unused released slot remains available for a later arrival, but rejected or capacity-blocked patients are never queued, recalled, replaced, or backfilled. No end-of-year filling is allowed.

For policy M in {S,S0,B} and pseudo-arrival i, allocation A_i(M) may use only the member's admissible score, scaled calendar time, cumulative released/used slots, and objects frozen by 31 December 2019. It may not use future arrivals, final test N, outcomes, future ranks, or another policy's actions.

S is the semantic model. S0 is not refitted: it masks only semantic inputs inside S's exact fitted preprocessing, coefficients, calibration, threshold, and state machine. Under all-semantic masking S and S0 must be bitwise identical. B is independently fitted and tuned without report-body semantics. S and B are fit in 2015-2018. In 2019, choose one constant threshold per S/q and B/q from {0,0.005,...,1} to maximize documentary-M2 captures subject to utilization at least 0.90 under the frozen release curve; ties choose fewer false reviews, then the larger threshold. S0 inherits S's threshold. The entire rule is frozen before 2020.

For a fixed year, q, buffer, access cell, and coherent state, define

n_M = sum_i A_i(M), z_M = sum_i A_i(M)Y_i,

U_M=n_M/K_q, P_M=z_M/n_M, and V_M=100*z_M/K_q=100*U_M*P_M.

The utilization gate makes n_M>0; otherwise P_M is undefined and inference is inconclusive. V_M is fixed offered-slot service yield: documentary-M2 captures per 100 precommitted slots, with unused slots contributing zero.

The two primary contrasts are Delta_S0=V_S-V_S0 and Delta_B=V_S-V_B. They compare the same K, release calendar, full annual pseudo-arrival stream, and outcome definition. They intentionally combine case selection and slot use, because both determine the clinical productivity of fixed offered capacity.

For each comparator R in {S0,B}, decompose the primary contrast exactly:

Psi_util(S,R)=50*(U_S-U_R)*(P_S+P_R),

Psi_case(S,R)=50*(P_S-P_R)*(U_S+U_R),

so Delta_R=Psi_util+Psi_case, in documentary-M2 captures per 100 slots. Psi_util is the utilization component. Psi_case is the accepted-case-composition component, called the case-selection component only as shorthand. It compares each policy's own accepted cases and may include downstream effects of different thresholding, slot exhaustion, and exposure to later arrivals. It is not a causal controlled direct effect and does not assume a common online opportunity set.

For the diagnostic prefix, let r_M(i) be M's acceptance ordinal and A_i^J(M)=A_i(M)1[r_M(i)<=J_q]. Report 100*sum A_i^J(M)Y_i/J_q, the pseudo-time and arrival index of M's Jth acceptance, and exact first-J patient backpointers. A between-policy prefix difference may be described only as a difference between policy-specific completed prefixes.

The retrospective outcome-aware top-K, first-come, frozen seeded lottery, none, and age/sex-only policies are controls. The oracle is never an online comparator or primary policy. Report S's state-matched attainability z_S/z_oracle when the oracle numerator is positive.

## Falsifiable hypothesis and interpretation zones

The unresolved hypothesis is:

H1 fixed offered-slot yield: there is a nonempty report-access frontier such that, for every claimed frontier cell, q, 2020/2021 year, prespecified decision buffer, and coherent source state, simultaneous 97.5% lower confidence limits for both Delta_S0 and Delta_B exceed five additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 offered slots; every policy uses at least 90% of slots and reaches J; S's lower confidence limit for state-matched oracle attainability exceeds 0.80; and every evidence gate passes.

Supportive H1 results establish only that eventual stored report semantics materially increased fixed offered-slot yield in a pretest-fixed completed-resection pseudo-roster, robust to enumerated source uncertainty. Attribution is separately labeled:

- selection-supported only if H1 is supportive and simultaneous lower limits for both Psi_case(S,S0) and Psi_case(S,B) exceed zero;
- utilization-supported only where the corresponding simultaneous lower limit for Psi_util exceeds zero;
- mixed/undetermined attribution whenever a component interval crosses zero;
- utilization-explained rather than better accepted-case composition if H1 is supportive but a case-component upper limit is at or below zero while utilization is positive.

None of these labels implies common candidate opportunities, causal benefit, or real service utilization.

Adverse evidence is a valid simultaneous upper limit at or below five for either primary contrast at the maximal exact-access cell, an upper limit for attainability below 0.80, or valid fully enumerated results showing no access cell can meet H1. A favorable case component with adverse offered-slot yield means composition gains did not create materially productive fixed capacity. A favorable offered-slot contrast with an adverse case component is a utilization result, not evidence of better case selection. If the oracle is favorable but online S is adverse, retrospective ranking did not survive chronological slot release.

Inconclusive results include any primary interval crossing five; any component interval crossing zero when component attribution is attempted; incompatible year/q/buffer results; an empty frontier caused by uncertainty rather than adverse bounds; or failure of any population, identity, adjudication, event, calibration, null, source-state, utilization, optimizer, or bootstrap gate. Negative and mixed results are publishable outcomes.

## Access uncertainty and analysis

Use exact access guarantees on a 0.05 grid: h_C for CT among C-pattern members, h_M for MRI among M-pattern members, h_A for at least one visible modality among CM members, and h_B<=h_A for both visible among CM members. O/C/M/CM patterns remain in denominators. For singly visible CM members, modality choice; accession partitions; clock, member, order, and outcome states; release counters; and actions stay jointly coupled. The frontier is the coordinatewise-minimal set of guarantees satisfying H1. It is a hypothetical exact-version access requirement, not evidence reports were then available.

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits that rebuild source states, models, calibration, 2019 tuning, thresholds, policies, outcomes, offered yields, components, and diagnostics. K, J, release bins, threshold grid, dictionaries, primary margin, and interpretation rules remain fixed. Use a max-centered 97.5% simultaneous family over both primary contrasts, any component invoked for attribution, both years, all q values, four buffers, access cells, coherent states, attainability, and null tests. Preserve coherent-state extrema within each replicate; never combine table-wise extrema.

Run 1,000 development/tuning semantic-block permutations stratified by year, modality pattern, source-unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, and formatting family, each with full refit. Required gates are: the K/J feasibility rules above; at least 50 adjudicated M2 events per claimed year envelope; anchored grade at least 85%; all test unknowns adjudicated; parser kappa at least 0.90, M2 PPV at least 0.98 and sensitivity at least 0.95; modality agreement at least 0.95 and unresolved at most 1%; each claimed pattern at least 100 members and 20 M2; semantic-feature PPV/sensitivity at least 0.85; no required-feature/pattern shift over 15 percentage points from 2019; all/no-report calibration slope 0.80-1.20 and intercept -0.10-0.10; each policy reaches J and worst-state U is at least 0.90; exact release accounting; complete state/backpointer replay; optimizer/brute-force equality on N<=12 fixtures; coordinatewise monotone access limits; zero lab/forbidden lineage; and no more than 2.5% failed bootstrap replicates.

## Missing evidence and claims requiring another study

A representative roster audit must include every considered referral, including cancellations, non-HCC pathology and noncompleters, with referral/presentation times, planned procedure and changes, deadline, exclusions, and HCC linkage. A capacity audit must contain offered slots, release times, reviewer roles/hours, queue/overflow rules, and completions. A report audit must contain accession/report identity, authored/final/release/view times, immutable version history, and body hashes. These are prerequisites for transport from pseudo-roster yield to real workflow yield.

Clinical experts must adjudicate operative identity, modality semantics, report concepts, pathology grade/specimen linkage, documentary adequacy, and acceptable capacity/margin. Biological M2, actionability, treatment choice, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, fairness, and benefit require additional source data, prospective silent-mode validation, and ultimately a randomized or pragmatic impact study.
