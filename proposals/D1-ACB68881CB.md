# Fixed-slot, modality-explicit review allocation under coherent HCC source states

## Question, evidence boundary, and advance

The clinical decision is deliberately narrow: when multidisciplinary review slots are scarce, can interpretable information in routine preoperative CT/MRI report bodies rank adults approaching a first eligible HCC resection so that a fixed number of reviews captures more cases whose linked resection pathology eventually documents severe microvascular invasion (M2)? A flag allocates review only. It is not a recommendation for resection extent, transplantation, perioperative systemic or locoregional treatment, adjuvant therapy, or surveillance.

**Strongest claim currently supported.** Header, catalog, and aggregate source inspection establishes that HCC snapshot `[source checksum]` contains patient/encounter-linked procedure starts, examination acquisition starts and eventual report bodies, and untimed pathology narratives. It does not contain report authored/final/release/view times or version history; pathology time, specimen/accession, slides, blocks, or sampling protocol; scheduled-review rosters, review cutoffs, service capacity, reviewer time, clinician action; recurrence, survival, harms, costs, or treatment effects. The exact aggregate audit in `source_grain_audit.json` found 338,040 procedure rows from the two literal source labels `anesthesia system` and `medical-record surgery`, 15,874 missing procedure starts, 70,944 patient/start keys with multiple procedure rows, and 8,932 pathology encounters with multiple rows (maximum 14). These facts support an ambiguity ledger, not a preferred operative source or specimen link.

Report grain is empirically resolvable. Among 419,996 examination rows, grouping on `(patient master index, encounter number, examination number)` merges 595 groups with multiple start values (593 with multiple nonblank starts). The primary bundle key must therefore be `(patient master index, encounter number, examination number, start time)`: it yields 393,468 nonblank-ID bundles, 16,099 multirow bundles, and maximum component multiplicity 12. Seventy-two rows have blank `examination number`; 282 have missing starts. An accession-only bundle, row-wise modality assignment, or row-wise visibility mask is invalid.

Public evidence establishes importance, not this hypothesis. The inspected front matter and abstract in the frozen full-text XML of Li et al. 2025 (DOI `10.1111/jcmm.70746`, PMCID `PMC12309289`, source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) support that MVI is pathology-defined, prognostically important, unavailable directly for preoperative decisions, and that MVI-directed management remains controversial. The inspected front matter and abstract in the frozen full-text XML of Li et al. 2026 (DOI `10.1186/s12957-026-04229-2`, PMCID `PMC13005532`, source `[source checksum]`, [source checksum]) reports a retrospective 243-patient resection cohort, 29.2% M2, and an association of M2 with recurrence and mortality, especially within two years. Neither source validates this snapshot's operation/pathology linkage, report semantics, pre-decision report access, queue allocation, or benefit from acting on a flag. No claim is made that uninspected supplementary methods were read.

**Unresolved falsifiable hypotheses.**

1. **Stored-content fixed-slot value (P).** In the locked 2020 and 2021 historical target blocks, at each externally registered absolute annual slot budget `K_y`, the robust semantic strategy S increases documentary-M2 captures per 100 slots by more than the pre-registered material margins `δ_D` versus its same-fit semantic ablation S0 and `δ_C` versus an independently fitted structured baseline B, under every coherent admissible source/outcome state when all state-selected eventual report bodies are exposed.
2. **Modality-pattern access robustness (T).** If P is supported, a nonempty upward-closed frontier exists over CT-only, MRI-only, and both modality-specific coordinates in dual-bundle patients for which both fixed-slot contrasts retain their margins under adversarial report visibility, bundle identity, outcome, membership, and queue reranking.
3. **Workflow transport (W), not identified by HCC alone.** A conditional operational statement requires a separate capacity/report audit proving the actual batch roster was known at its cutoff, `K_b` net review slots existed, and the exact report versions scored were released—and, for a viewed-content claim, viewed—before that cutoff. Aggregate staffing, acquisition time, eventual text, or an unmatched report ID cannot support W.

This advances both parents by using a fixed absolute slot denominator despite state-varying eligibility, keeping all membership/outcome/report alternatives coherent at episode level, separating the empirically valid four-field bundle key, refining dual-modality access into separate CT and MRI guarantees without assuming overlap, and refusing to treat an annual retrospective top-K ranking as an observed online queue.

## Population, time, and coherent source states

The clustering unit is `Patient Master Index`. Exact source rows are deduplicated while preserving raw row ordinals and duplicate counts. Before pathology grades or test report semantics are read, a hepatobiliary surgeon, abdominal radiologist, and Chinese-reading pathologist freeze dictionaries and create one finite ledger `A_i` of coherent alternatives per patient. A state is an alternative record, not the Cartesian product of independently toggled fields.

1. Normalize every `procedures.surgery` value and classify it as eligible liver resection, definite exclusion, same-episode component, or unresolved. Eligible includes clinically confirmed partial, segmental, hemihepatic, and liver-tumor resection terms; transplant, biopsy/puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, and metastatic-organ procedures are excluded. Source label `surgery source` is provenance only; it establishes no priority. Rows on the same encounter with the same valid `start time` are one candidate operative episode with all components retained. Distinct starts are never merged. Missing-start rows cannot define primary `t_dec`; report them and use them only to create an unresolved non-primary state unless an external source audit supplies their clock.
2. For each plausible first eligible episode, select its exact encounter and start. If competing rows cannot be resolved without outcome/report content, retain each as an alternative. Parse source clocks as dataset-local naive times without timezone conversion. Define `t_dec=start-24 h`; 12, 48, and 72 h are locked sensitivities. A valid start determines state-specific calendar membership.
3. Require age >=18 from the unique exact `encounters` join on `(Patient Master Index,Visit Number)` and explicit HCC in the entire same-encounter pathology composite. The audit found that encounter key unique in 105,044 rows, but different plausible operative encounters remain different states. This is a retrospectively pathology-confirmed resection frame, not preoperative diagnostic certainty, curative intent, resectability, or treatment-naivety.
4. Exclude recorded earlier HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in `[t_dec-365 d,t_dec)` using frozen dictionaries and valid clocks from procedures, medications, and orders. Ambiguous matches or boundary clocks remain state alternatives. Recorded absence is not biological absence.
5. Link all pathology rows only through the selected `(Patient Master Index, Encounter Number)`. Because pathology has no time or specimen key, a pathologist may confirm documentary linkage from internal text; otherwise outcome validity remains unknown. Do not borrow pathology from another encounter.
6. Use period-pure patients whose every admissible eligible state lies in 2015–2018 for development and whose every state lies in 2019 for tuning/recalibration. Let the test union `U` contain every patient with at least one admissible 2020–2021 state. Quarantine every U patient and every patient with a state crossing into 2020–2021 from fitting. In whole state `ω∈Ω=∏_i A_i`, `Z_iy(ω)=1` means patient i is eligible in test year y. Nonmembers contribute zero to numerators; they are never coded as outcome-negative.
7. Freeze dictionaries, parser, modality map, features, penalties, optimizer, tie hash, benchmark budgets, margins, bootstrap family, frontier grid, and all gates before locked outcomes.

Emit state-wise and union attrition, reasons, selected row ordinals, operation source, encounter, valid clock, period, pathology rows, prior-treatment evidence, report bundles/patterns, `Z`, and `Y/V`. Marginal minima assembled from incompatible episodes are forbidden.

## Documentary outcome

For the selected state encounter, concatenate all exact-deduplicated pathology `Pathology`, `Examination Findings`, and `Examination Diagnosis` rows in source order while retaining row boundaries. `Y=1,V=1` only for an explicit selected-value M2 statement after a frozen anchor such as `MVI indicates risk grade[:：]`. `Y=0,V=1` requires explicit selected-value M0 or M1. Missing grade, binary MVI without grade, boilerplate/definition text, conflict, unresolved specimen linkage, or inadequate documentary evidence has `V=0,Y∈{0,1}`; never exclude, impute, or call it M0.

Two qualified Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to reports, scores, and queues, review every parsed M2, every conflict, every test V=0, and year-stratified samples of at least 150 M0 and 150 M1; a third resolves disagreement. Require M2 PPV >=0.98, sensitivity >=0.95, kappa >=0.90, and parser disagreement <=5%. These validate documentary extraction only. Biological M2 additionally requires unavailable specimen identity and sampling evidence.

## Exact report bundles, modality patterns, and predictors

For nonblank `examination number` and valid `start time`, bundle all examination components sharing `(patient master index,encounter number,examination number,start time)` before filtering, feature extraction, or masking. Blank-ID or missing-start rows remain unresolved diagnostics and cannot provide primary semantic content. Two abdominal radiologists, blinded to outcomes, freeze a development-only map over normalized bundle `examination` values plus `machine model`, assigning exactly one of CT, MRI, non-target, or unresolved/conflicted. A bundle cannot appear in both modalities. Require agreement >=0.95 and unresolved <=1% among candidate target bundles for a pattern claim.

Within each coherent operative state, eligible bundles have acquisition `Start Time∈[t_dec-90 d,t_dec)`. This is an acquisition condition, not report availability. For each modality choose the latest eligible bundle. Equal-time distinct accessions remain alternative bundle-identity states; lexicographic choice is forbidden. Concatenate every component's `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis` in raw ordinal order. An entire selected bundle is exposed or masked. Define stored pattern `g_i(ω)∈{O,C,M,CM,R}`: no nonempty selected body, CT only, MRI only, both, or unresolved/conflicted. R and O remain in the union accounting but cannot satisfy an access guarantee.

B is an independently optimized elastic-net logistic model using only age, sex, CT/MRI acquisition indicators, days from acquisition to `t_dec`, component count, and a development-frequency machine category. It has no report-body field, laboratory value/missingness, diagnosis, procedure label, pathology, identifier, calendar year, or post-cutoff content.

S uses the identical structured block plus a frozen report-body block: maximum lesion diameter, lesion-count category, explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, capsule complete/interrupted/absent, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral hypointensity, cirrhosis, and ascites, each with present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned encodings and modality provenance. S0 is never refit: use S's intercept, structured coefficients, semantic coefficients, preprocessing, penalty, and recalibration, replacing the entire body-derived block by its state-zero/masked vector. Require bitwise S=S0 scores, ranks, and queues when no semantic bundle is exposed. B remains independent and is not allowed to reuse S coefficients.

Two blinded radiologists and a bilingual clinical NLP reviewer freeze synonym, negation, uncertainty, unit, and cross-component conflict rules and validate stratified by year, C/M/CM, and modality. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in >=90%, count agreement >=90% with kappa >=0.80, and PPV/sensitivity >=0.85 for every binary feature in each claimed modality stratum. Failure is inconclusive.

Fit S and B separately in 2015–2018 with patient-wise worst-admissible-state convex logistic loss; use the same robust rule for 2019 penalty selection and nonnegative-slope recalibration, every patient bootstrap, and every semantic-null refit. Equal-state averaging, complete-state conditioning, and outcome-informed state selection are prohibited.

## Fixed-slot estimand and capacity contract

For test year y, an external contract must register before outcomes: absolute integer slots `K_y`; covered service and candidate stream; whether slots are ring-fenced from competing work; reviewer composition; minutes per review and conservative available reviewer-hours; no-rollover/rollover rule; and material margins `δ_D,δ_C` in additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 slots. It must also state how an operational batch roster and cutoff would be formed. HCC has none of this evidence.

Feasibility requires `K_y>0` and `K_y≤N_y^core=∑_i min_ω Z_iy(ω)` for both years. Thus every admissible state has at least K_y eligible patients. No capping, fractional patient, or percentage-rescaling is allowed. If external K is absent, compute only benchmark absolute budgets `K_y(α)=floor(αN_y^core)` for `α∈{.05,.10,.20}` when positive. They are source-derived stress tests, not observed capacity.

For model M, state ω, and report mask r, rank only `T_y(ω)={i:Z_iy=1}` by frozen score, breaking ties by SHA-256 of a study salt and patient ID. Let `Q^M_y(ω,r;K_y)` be the first exactly K_y. For unknown outcomes enumerate labels within the same episode state. For `j∈{S0,B}` define

`θ_j(ω,r,Y;K)=100/[K_2020+K_2021] × Σ_y Σ_i Z_iy(ω) Y_i(ω) {1[i∈Q^S_y]-1[i∈Q^j_y]}.`

The fixed denominator is contracted slots, not the varying eligible N and not the union U. Report raw captures, queue overlap, precision/recall among V=1, unknown-outcome bounds, and false-review counts. Flag-all/flag-none are unequal-workload sanity checks only; age/sex-only and seeded random-K are equal-slot secondary baselines. An annual top-K result is a retrospective allocation benchmark: it does not prove that future annual candidates were simultaneously knowable. Operational language requires the batch audit below or another prospective study.

P uses all state-selected eventual bodies exposed, but minimizes/maximizes θ over coherent ω and V=0 labels. Report one-sided simultaneous 97.5% lower and upper limits `L_D,U_D,L_C,U_C`. Support requires `L_D>δ_D` and `L_C>δ_C`.

## Exact modality-pattern access frontier

Visibility is a latent whole-bundle mask; acquisition never implies visibility. For each ω and year impose four independent minimum guarantees on the eligible stored-pattern strata:

- `h_C`: fraction of C patients whose CT bundle is exposed;
- `h_M`: fraction of M patients whose MRI bundle is exposed;
- `h_CC`: fraction of CM patients whose CT bundle is exposed;
- `h_CM`: fraction of CM patients whose MRI bundle is exposed.

For CM, CT and MRI masks are joint patient states. Their overlap is chosen adversarially; no independence or both-visible rate is inferred. O and R contribute fixed no-content states to every whole-slot result. At grid `G={0,.1,...,1}^4`, counts are `ceil(h_g N_g(ω))`; an empty stratum makes that coordinate not applicable, never a deleted denominator. A modality-specific claim requires at least 100 eligible patients and 20 adjudicated M2 events in that stratum in both years combined.

For each K and h, compute the exact lower/upper θ endpoints over coherent episode states, unknown labels, masks satisfying all four count constraints, and the resulting globally reranked exact-K queues. Patient costs are not separable after ranking; the parent's sorted-cost shortcut is invalid here. Use a state-expanded mixed-integer program or exact multiple-choice branch-and-bound with binary episode, visibility, queue, and label variables, score-order constraints with frozen tie rank, fixed K, and the four access counts. Validate every endpoint against exhaustive enumeration for all fixtures with U<=12. Coordinatewise monotonicity of lower endpoints is mandatory.

Let `H_K={h∈G: L_D(K,h)>δ_D and L_C(K,h)>δ_C}`. H must be upward closed; report its complete Pareto-minimal antichain. Define the reduced-access set `H_K^-=H_K\\{(1,1,1,1)}`; unlike H itself, this can be empty even when all-content P supports. Capacity is not assumed monotone: report every registered K and benchmark K separately, yielding the exact capacity-access frontier `{(K,h):h∈H_K}`. Never choose K or h after inspecting outcomes.

Use 2,000 patient-level bootstraps stratified by development/tuning/test year, rebuilding episode states, bundles, fits, recalibration, queue ranks, and frontier endpoints. A frozen max-centered bootstrap statistic supplies one 97.5% simultaneous model family over contrasts, K values, four buffers, and G; >2.5% failed refits/MIP solves makes the relevant family inconclusive. A development/tuning-only 1,000-repetition semantic null permutes the complete body block within year, modality pattern, component cardinality, and formatting family, then refits S and reconstructs S0. Observed all-content θ_D must exceed the simultaneous 97.5th null bound.

## External capacity, roster, and report-version audit

A conditional W claim needs read-only source-system extracts from the same workflow and era, linked privately and never searched publicly.

The **capacity/roster file** must contain immutable batch ID, service/site, batch-open and freeze timestamps, candidate patient/encounter and planned operation ID, eligibility status at freeze, slots offered/filled/cancelled, rollover, reviewer roles, scheduled and actual minutes, competing-work subtraction, and the pre-outcome K and δ approval. Every scored patient must have been on the roster at freeze; every comparator uses exactly the same K_b slots. A complete administrative census is preferred. If sampled, missing batches count unavailable and conservative simultaneous 97.5% lower bounds must establish net slots and hours. Aggregate annual staffing without rosters cannot establish operational ranking.

The **report/version file** must join the exact HCC bundle key `(Patient ID,Encounter ID,Examination ID,Start time)` and component IDs, and provide immutable report ID, every version ID, content hash, authored/final/release timestamps, addendum/supersession status, system-availability timestamp, and clinician-view timestamp when claiming viewed content. The extraction hash must equal a version released by the relevant batch freeze (or patient-specific t_dec for the non-operational benchmark). Missing logs, mismatched hashes, later addenda, blank IDs, or acquisition-only evidence are unavailable, not visible.

The audit reports simultaneous lower bounds for C-CT, M-MRI, CM-CT, and CM-MRI access and also the observed CM both-visible fraction. Round the four lower bounds down to 0.1 and require the vector to lie in H at the audited K. The model/frontier 97.5% family and independent audit 97.5% family give joint noncoverage <=5% by Bonferroni. A fully linked historical audit may rerun θ on actual batch rosters and versions. An aggregate capacity audit plus aggregate access percentages supports at most a requirement comparison, not an operational-effect claim.

## Exact result interpretation

First require source hashes/headers, complete ledger reachability, temporal quarantine, expert/parser validation, zero laboratory and forbidden lineage, S0 identity, independent B fitting, exact-K feasibility, successful exhaustive fixtures, complete simultaneous inference, and semantic-null rejection. Gate failure is **inconclusive**, not a negative biological result.

For each contrast j, classify `support_j` when `L_j>δ_j`, `adverse_j` when `U_j≤δ_j`, and `unresolved_j` otherwise.

- **Supportive stored-content P:** both contrasts support at the external K under all-content outer bounds. The only permitted claim is more documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed historical slots in this source reconstruction.
- **Adverse P:** both contrasts are adverse; label definite harm only if either `U_j<0`. All eventual stored content did not achieve the registered material gain.
- **Mixed P:** one contrast supports and the other is adverse. S>S0 but not S>B attributes an action change to semantics without establishing superiority to an optimized structured strategy; S>B but not S>S0 does not establish runtime semantic value. Also label prespecified K/buffer heterogeneity mixed when some jointly controlled cells support and others are adverse; never average signs.
- **Inconclusive P:** either contrast is unresolved, capacity is infeasible/missing for a contract claim, or any gate fails. Favorable AUC or point estimates cannot rescue it.
- **Supportive T:** P supports and `H_K^-` is nonempty, proving that at least one less-than-universal pattern-specific guarantee retains both margins; report the full antichain. **Adverse T:** P supports but `H_K^-` is empty, so only universal CT and MRI access in every claimable pattern retains the margins. **Mixed T:** reduced-access cells support for one registered K or buffer but are adverse for another under the joint family. Solver failure, sparse modality strata, or intervals crossing margins is inconclusive.
- **Conditional W:** only a passing linked capacity/roster audit and exact-version audit whose conservative vector lies in H permits a workflow-feasibility statement. Audit access below H is adverse to transport for that audited workflow, not to stored-content P. Missing/unrepresentative audit evidence is inconclusive. No result establishes acceptability, clinician action, treatment effect, recurrence reduction, survival, cost-effectiveness, or patient benefit.
