## 0. The scientific opening (the parent's own conceded defect)

The parent already repaired a death-conflation defect and won two blinded comparisons. It
concedes the fatal residual it cannot currently resolve: its prespecified mediator,
`icu/chartevents` itemid **223780 "Daily Wake Up Deferred"**, is (a) **sparse** and (b)
records the **SAT implementation decision** ("why the daily wake-up was deferred"), *not* the
sedation-depth/arousability process. Therefore the natural indirect effect (NIE) and the
mediated fraction may be **unidentifiable**, and any point estimate of them would be an
artifact of typing, not a process effect.

- **Leading claim to be tested (the only primary claim):** after adjustment `M0→M2`, a higher
  deep-sedation fraction `D` in `[t*, t*+12 h]` is associated with a **lower cause-specific
  hazard of liberation** within 7 days, with death treated as a competing event, with the
  point estimate and the CI limit nearest the null reported with an E-value.
- **Rival explanations** the design must separate: **severity marker** (depth is a proxy for
  illness, R-conf), **death conflation**, **measurement/documentation artifact** (223780 is a
  charting behavior; RASS charting completeness unknown).
- **Secondary (now explicitly non-primary) estimand:** the NIE / mediated fraction through
  223780, reported **only** as a bounded, partial-identification quantity, with an explicit
  possibility of "INCONCLUSIVE".

Separating observation: does the total effect survive `M2` with a large E-value, while the
NIE bound through 223780 **straddles 0 / is too wide** to support a mediated claim? If the
total effect is robust but the NIE bound is uninformative, the honest conclusion is "a
modifiable *association* exists; the *process* decomposition is unidentified in this data."

## 1. Clinical importance

Time to liberation drives ICU LOS, VAP and delirium. [K2] shows a randomised paired SAT/SBT
protocol increased ventilator-free days (14.7 vs 11.6 d) and lowered 1-year mortality — so the
process axis is causally real. The question this child answers differently from the parent is
narrower and more honest: **can the observational depth association in MIMIC-IV be decomposed
at all, or can we only bound it?** Deciding this changes the next research step: if the total
effect is robust but the mediation is unidentifiable, the correct next study is a prospective
SAT-documentation/protocol study or an instrumented design — *not* a bigger model on 223780.
If even the total effect is not robust (small E-value), the direction is deprioritised.

## 3. Design and estimands

Inherited: population, `t*`, `[t*, t*+12 h]` window, earliest-70 %/latest-30 % `intime`

controls. Define:

- `M` = prespecified mediator indicator from itemid **223780** recorded **strictly after `t*`**

  **implementation-vs-clinical sensitivity recoding** because they are also treatment decisions.
- Outcome: time `t*`→7 d with mutually exclusive first events **liberation** (227194, no

### PRIMARY ESTIMAND (the only primary clinical claim) — TOTAL effect, honest
Cause-specific HR for liberation for `D` (per 10 % deep fraction and per SD), from `M0`
(unadjusted) → `M1` (+ demographics/severity) → `M2` (+ sedation dose, neuro, labs); Fine-Gray
7-day CIF of liberation and the death CIF reported alongside. **Report the E-value** [K3] for

### NEW DELTA 1 — PARTIAL-IDENTIFICATION of the indirect path (prespecified)
The NIE/mediated fraction through 223780 is reported **only as a bounded quantity**, never as
a point estimate promoted to a claim. Compute on the cause-specific (or Fine-Gray) scale:
- **(a) Mediator–outcome unmeasured-confounding sensitivity (bias-adjusted NIE).** Using the
  standard bias formula for the NIE, with a **prespecified grid of sensitivity parameters**
  for the mediator–outcome confounder `U` (association of `U` with `M` and with `Y` on the
  RR/OR scale, in the VanderWeele bias-correction framing), report the **bias-adjusted NIE**
  and the **range** of the mediated fraction over the grid
  `γ ∈ {1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 2.0}` on both legs. The bound is a **bias-corrected interval**, not a
  point estimate. (E-value logic from [K3] is applied to the *total* effect; the
  mediator–outcome bias factor is applied to the *NIE* because the E-value alone does not
  address mediator–outcome confounding.)
- **(b) Coarse/misclassified mediator.** Treat `M` as an imperfect, coarse indicator of the
  underlying (unobserved) SAT-process state `M*`. With prespecified **sensitivity `Se`** and
  **specificity `Sp`** grids (`Se ∈ {0.6,0.8,0.9}`, `Sp ∈ {0.8,0.9,0.95}`), propagate the
  misclassification into the NIE/mediated fraction and report the resulting **bounds**
  (the matrix of bounds over `(Se, Sp, γ)`).
- **Range of assumptions explicitly stated:** the reported object is
  `[NIE_low, NIE_high]` and `[frac_low, frac_high]` over the joint grid, together with the
  single assumption set under which the bound is tightest. No cross-world identifying
  assumption is asserted as true.

### NEW DELTA 2 — EXPLICIT DECISION RULE (no silent substitution)
Declare the **mediated estimand INCONCLUSIVE, reporting the computed bound**, whenever ANY of:
1. the full-table positivity gate (Sec. 2b) fails (stays < 200, prevalence < 10 %, or overlap
   fails); **or**
2. the partial-identification interval `[NIE_low, NIE_high]` **includes 0**; **or**
3. the interval span exceeds a prespecified maximum (e.g. `frac_high − frac_low > 0.5`); **or**
4. the bias-adjusted point NIE changes sign across the `γ` grid.
The prespecified fallback mediators for 223780 are **223849** then **223911**, used only to
report a *secondary, always-INCONCLUSIVE* sensitivity line — **a fallback is never a silent
substitution and never becomes the primary mediated estimand.** If the primary mediator fails,
the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE and the primary claim reduces to the total-effect estimand.

### Estimators and inference
Landmark estimators; `lifelines`/`statsmodels` for cause-specific Cox and Fine-Gray CIF; a
**mediational g-formula** cross-check for the decomposition; **bootstrap 95 % CIs** (≥1000
resamples) clustered by `subject_id`; death CIF always reported so no total effect is read as
"pure liberation delay."

## 4. Baselines and alternatives (method exploration, same question)
- **B0 (simple baseline):** binary sustained-deep flag; fixed-horizon logistic / Cox on `M0→M2`;
  death censored. Loses the dose gradient, the death/liberation distinction, and any process
  mechanism.
- **A1 (parent):** competing-risks Fine-Gray + cause-specific Cox + spline; reports only the
  **total** effect and a possibly-unidentifiable NIE with no bound.
- **A2 (this child):** total effect + **partial-identification bounds** for the NIE (mediator–
  outcome confounding × mediator misclassification) + no-substitution decision rule.
  *A2 vs A1:* A1 gives a single NIE number that the parent itself concedes may be
  unidentifiable; A2 replaces that number with a **falsifiable bound and an explicit
  inconclusive verdict**, which is the scientifically honest object for a sparse,
  implementation-level mediator. This is a substantive, not cosmetic, difference.
- **Deferred:** time-varying IPTW/MSM target-trial emulation (stronger sequential-
  exchangeability assumptions on sedation dosing); a latent-process joint model for the SAT
  state — revisit only if a prospective/denser mediator is obtained.
- **Resources:** CPU-only tabular MIMIC-IV + bootstrap; **no GPU claimed or needed**; < a few
  hours.

## 5. Falsification criteria — four separated rivals (prespecified)
- **Supportive of the primary total-effect claim:** `M2` cause-specific HR < 1 with CI
  excluding 1, **and** a large E-value relative to plausible confounding.
- **Adverse (severity marker / R-conf):** the `M2` association attenuates toward the null
  across `M1→M2`, or the E-value is small (a mild/moderate unmeasured severity confounder
  could explain it away).
- **Adverse (death conflation):** the depth–liberation association vanishes when death is
  modelled as a competing event, or a **rising** death hazard appears (depth delays liberation
  partly by increasing death) — the total effect is then not a clean "delay" effect.
- **Adverse (measurement / documentation artifact):** the analgesia **negative-control**
  exposure shows a parallel spurious association of similar magnitude, the **permuted-label**
  control is not null, or the `M2` association tracks RASS *charting frequency/density* rather
  than depth (documentation artifact).
- **Inconclusive (primary mediated estimand):** the decision rule of Delta 2 fires — bound
  includes 0, span too wide, sign-unstable, or positivity fails. Reported with the bound.
- **Not established by this design (requires other work):** any causal claim (needs an RCT or
  an explicit instrument); RASS/223780 charting completeness; whether residual latent severity
  is fully absorbed by `M2`; the cross-world "no unmeasured exposure–mediator confounding"
  assumption.

## 6. Interpretation of outcomes and limits
Supportive (total effect, robust) ⇒ a modifiable depth *association* exists; the actionable
target stays uncertain → next step is a design that can identify the process (prospective SAT
documentation / instrument), not a bigger model on 223780. Adverse (severity/confounding/
death-conflation) ⇒ deprioritise depth-only targets. Inconclusive (mediated) ⇒ do **not**
substitute a mediator; state the bound and the missing evidence. **Limits:** single-centre
retrospective; RASS charting completeness unknown ([K3] validates the scale, not EHR
documentation); the mediator 223780 measures SAT *implementation*, not the depth process;
mediation assumptions partly untestable; association ≠ causation; cohort prevalence/power not
established in discovery. **Computationally checkable:** the competing-risks/HR/CIF/E-value/
bias-adjusted-NIE/misclassification-bound computations, event counts, CIs, the positivity
gate and the decision rule. **Requires clinical adjudication or another study:** whether the
cross-world assumptions hold, whether a residual severity axis absorbs the effect, and any
causal claim.
