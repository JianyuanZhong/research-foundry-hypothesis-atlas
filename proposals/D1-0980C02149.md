# Actionability-gated pulmonary-nodule propagation failure with an action-semantic comparator hierarchy

## Clinical decision, supported claim, and unresolved hypotheses

The decision is not merely whether pulmonary-nodule recommendations and discharge summaries are textually discordant. It is which next intervention is defensible: a nodule-specific reconciliation tool, a general actionable-imaging recommendation workflow, an artifact-availability intervention, or a study of downstream communication/tracking. The assessed parent repaired the content clock but compared nodules with one pooled set of nonthoracic lesions whose actions could mean interval surveillance, delayed characterization, or organ-specific work-up. A pooled contrast can therefore manufacture or erase apparent nodule specificity. It also risks calling every textually eligible recommendation a propagation failure even when available pre-discharge evidence refutes actionability or is insufficient to establish it.

The strongest evidence currently supported remains narrow: complete-source feasibility audits found 331,794 unique `DS` admission keys, only 17 missing discharge `storetime` values, substantial ordinary discharge storage latency, and a broad retrieval-only pulmonary pool of 3,764 reports. A new unsampled retrieval-only audit for this child scanned all 2,321,355 radiology rows, linked 1,136,797 rows to an admission with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`, and found 3,927 broad nonpulmonary recommendation/action candidates in 3,803 admission keys and 3,696 subjects. The organ mentions were highly overlapping; 2,458 candidates also mentioned a pulmonary target, and both surveillance/stability and characterization language were common. These aggregate findings establish that controls may be retrievable and directly demonstrate why one heterogeneous pooled comparator is not decision-valid. They are not adjudicated clinical opportunities, comparator prevalence, or outcome estimates.

This successor separates three claims:

1. **Observable propagation phenotype:** among frozen pulmonary opportunities that experts judge discharge-handoff-actionable from the available pre-discharge evidence, and whose canonical discharge artifact was stored by discharge at least 24 hours after the recommendation component was stored, actionable nodule content is omitted or interval-incomplete/discordant.
2. **Action-semantic specificity:** that phenotype is greater than in a primary comparator with the same semantic carrier—unconditional, target-specific CT surveillance for interval stability at exactly 3, 6, or 12 months—not merely greater than a mixture of unlike nonthoracic actions.
3. **Intervention warrant:** MIMIC can estimate addressable text-discordance yield and distinguish nodule-specific, generic action-carrier, availability, and specificity-inconclusive patterns. It cannot establish actual report visibility, clinical appropriateness with a complete record, communication, ordering, follow-up, causal mechanism, intervention benefit, or a local deployment threshold.

The falsifiable primary hypothesis is:

> In the actionability-supported pulmonary stratum with a timely canonical artifact and `G = DS_store - R_store >= 24 hours`, the conservative lower-compatible discordance rate and the all-opportunity addressable burden exceed a prospectively locked workflow-capacity margin; nodule specificity is supported only if the excess over an adequately powered Tier-1 same-semantic comparator also exceeds its locked margin in every adequately supported comparator target family.

A robust absolute pulmonary result remains scientifically meaningful if controls are sparse or nontransportable, but **specificity is then inconclusive, never supported by comparator absence**.

## Frozen pulmonary all-opportunity denominator

Before reading discharge text, discharge `storetime`, later notes/events, comparator outcomes, or actionability labels, radiology-trained reviewers create and hash the pulmonary eligibility manifest. The first eligible admission per subject is the immutable all-opportunity denominator `N`; every subsequent pulmonary table retains all `N` rows.

Include adults with an admission-linked radiology report whose `charttime` is in `[admittime-12h,D]`, containing an incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity and a patient-specific, unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months later. Exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, explicit no-follow-up, conditional/optional plans, in-hospital death, hospice/comfort-only transition under the inherited rubric, and recommendations first introduced after `D`. Do not impute smoking pack-years, complete lesion measurements, cancer risk, appropriateness, or outside history.

Reconstruct report/addendum chains only from reciprocal, subject-consistent, admission-consistent detail links, preserving all ordinals. Both `charttime` and `storetime` of the recommendation-bearing component must be `<=D`. Select the last pre-`D` component materially establishing the unconditional action and interval. A material contradiction before `D`, contradictory/nonreciprocal links, missing chain node, unresolved recommendation time, or inability to identify the operative component is `U_R`, reported and bounded rather than called omission.

The canonical artifact is the unique `note/discharge` row with `note_type=="DS"` for `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. Any duplicate canonical key stops confirmatory computation; never select first, last, or longest. Missing artifact, unreadable text, missing `storetime`, or contradictory linkage is unknown; never substitute `charttime` for `storetime`.

Retain the exhaustive departure state:

* `F`: `DS_store<=D`, classifiable, and text contains the patient-specific nodule, chest/thoracic CT action, and exactly compatible interval/date;
* `C_O`: `DS_store<=D`, classifiable, but lacks the patient-specific actionable plan;
* `C_I`: `DS_store<=D`, classifiable, but has incomplete/discordant target/action/timing without documented alternate resolution;
* `R`: `DS_store<=D` and documents refusal/preferences, goals-of-care resolution, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, scheduled outside follow-up, or another adjudicated alternate resolution;
* `A`: known `DS_store>D`, regardless of eventual content;
* `U_D`: missing, unreadable, contradictory, or unclassifiable artifact/store time/linkage/text.

`F+C_O+C_I+R+A+U_D=N`. Later faithful text never converts `A` to `F`, and no later trace changes membership in `N`.

## Complete chronology and stale/delayed safeguards

For every pulmonary and comparator opportunity define `R_store` as the operative recommendation component's `storetime`, `DS_store` as the canonical artifact's `storetime`, and `G=DS_store-R_store` in hours.

Storage state `V` is `V_D` for `DS_store<=D`; `V_24` for `D<DS_store<=D+24h`; `V_72` for `D+24h<DS_store<=D+72h`; `V_168` for `D+72h<DS_store<=D+168h`; `V_late` for `DS_store>D+168h`; and `V_U` if unresolved. Gap state `H` is `H_stale` for `G<0`; `H_0_6`, `H_6_24`, `H_24_72`, or `H_72plus` for the corresponding positive intervals; and `H_U` otherwise. Eventual content `Z` is `F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text`, using the same content rubric regardless of storage time.

Publish the complete zero-filled all-`N` table `J_N=count(V,H,Z)/N`, its count version, and its deterministic crosswalk to the departure state. `V_D,H_stale` is never an opportunity for recommendation incorporation even if wording happens to match. `V_24/V_72/V_168/V_late` may describe eventual transitions but never enters a departure-time content estimand. The primary ample observed storage opportunity is `E={V_D and H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. The 24-hour boundary is a workflow-capacity benchmark, not a known human editing window; repeat at 12 and 72 hours. Keep `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` only as a checked decomposition, never as the content clock.

## Blinded actionability adjudication: eligibility is not propagation

After `N` is frozen, but before any content reviewer or analyst can access canonical `DS` text, `DS_store`, `G`, outcomes, comparator class, or post-`D` data, two independent clinicians (at least one thoracic radiologist and one clinician who routinely owns discharge follow-up) receive only the complete reciprocal pre-`D` radiology chain, earlier same-admission radiology text through `D`, admission/discharge times, terminal service, discharge disposition, and the minimum structured facts needed to apply the rubric. They do not receive the discharge summary or later traces. A third reviewer resolves disagreements only after pre-consensus labels are saved.

They separately label:

1. **Textual recommendation eligibility `L`**: `L_yes` if the operative chain contains the target-specific, unconditional, exact action/interval required for `N`; `L_no` if this was an eligibility error; `L_U` if chain semantics cannot be resolved.
2. **Discharge-handoff actionability `K`**:
   * `K_supported`: available pre-`D` evidence supports that the recommendation remained unresolved, patient-specific, feasible after discharge, and appropriate to carry forward as an action or explicit resolution;
   * `K_refuted`: available pre-`D` evidence explicitly shows completion before departure, material supersession, wrong-patient/target attribution, or another concrete reason it should not be propagated as that action;
   * `K_uncertain_context`: recommendation is textually eligible but missing prior imaging, outside history, complete risk factors, prognosis/preferences, or specialty-plan evidence prevents a defensible actionability judgment;
   * `K_unresolved_review`: reviewers cannot resolve the available evidence.
3. A reason vector: `already_completed`, `superseded`, `outside_plan_known`, `risk/history_missing`, `goals/preferences_missing`, `specialist-ownership_unclear`, `technical/feasibility_unclear`, or `other`, allowing multiple reasons.

`K_supported` does **not** mean guideline-concordant or clinically necessary on a complete record. It means only that the available pre-discharge record supports carrying the unresolved recommendation or its explicit resolution into handoff. `K_refuted` and both unknown categories remain in `N` and `J_N`; they cannot become faithful or discordant propagation outcomes by deletion. Report `count(K,L,V,H,Z)/N` and all reason counts.

Define the primary propagation-risk set `P_N={L_yes,K_supported,E}`. The conservative conditional propagation discordance is

`r_N = [n(P_N,C_O_text)+n(P_N,C_I_text)] / n(P_N)`,

with `R_text` and `U_text` retained in the denominator. The all-opportunity addressable burden is

`b_N = [n(P_N,C_O_text)+n(P_N,C_I_text)] / N`,

reported as failures per 100 frozen opportunities. Its reciprocal `NNR=1/r_N` is the number of actionability-supported, ample-gap records a perfect reconciliation process would need to review per observed discordant artifact; present infinity when the numerator is zero. These are upper-bound workflow-yield descriptors, not preventable harm or expected benefit. Also report lower/upper bounds assigning `U_text`, `K_uncertain_context`, `K_unresolved_review`, `L_U`, `H_U`, and `V_U` in every logically compatible favorable/adverse way. The directional label uses the least favorable compatible bound.

Discordance among `K_refuted` cannot support propagation failure; it is reported as recommendation-selection discordance. High `K_uncertain_context` supports a data/actionability-resolution need rather than a propagation claim. This separation prevents a reconciliation UI from being recommended when the primary need is richer clinical context or specialist adjudication.

## Action-semantic comparator hierarchy

Retrieve possible nonthoracic controls from the same complete radiology/detail universe, admission window, chain rules, adult criteria, and exact 3/6/12-month syntax without reading discharge text or outcomes. Freeze the first eligible comparator admission per subject, exclude pulmonary index admissions and all pulmonary `N` subjects from the primary comparison, and publish every retrieval/exclusion/unresolved count. Reviewers first assign a semantic tuple while blinded to `DS`, storage/gap state, pulmonary outcomes, and the tier that the tuple will produce:

`S=(commitment, object, action, purpose, temporal_form, resolution_state)` where

* `commitment` is unconditional / conditional / optional / unclear;
* `object` is a named incidental focal target plus prespecified organ family (`adrenal`, `renal`, `hepatic`, `pancreatic`, `thyroid`, `adnexal`, `splenic`, `breast`, `osseous`, `other`);
* `action` is CT / MRI / ultrasound / other;
* `purpose` is interval surveillance for stability / delayed characterization or reassessment / screening or staging / unclear;
* `temporal_form` is exact 3 / 6 / 12 calendar months or other;
* `resolution_state` is unresolved / resolved or superseded / unclear before `D`.

Two domain reviewers independently label the tuple and `L/K` using the same blinded actionability rubric. The tier is assigned mechanically only after labels are frozen:

* **Tier 1, same semantic carrier (`X1`)—the only primary specificity comparator:** `unconditional`, named incidental nonthoracic focal object, **CT**, explicit **interval surveillance for stability**, exact 3/6/12 months, unresolved, `L_yes`, and `K_supported`.
* **Tier 2, same intent but different action (`X2`):** identical to Tier 1 except action is MRI or ultrasound. This tests whether any signal generalizes across modality workflow; it cannot establish or rescue nodule specificity.
* **Tier 3, same timed recommendation syntax but different intent (`X3`):** unconditional CT/MRI/ultrasound for delayed characterization/reassessment at 3/6/12 months. It is a transportability stress test only and is never pooled with `X1` or `X2`.
* Conditional/optional, screening/staging, established surveillance, resolved/superseded, unclear-purpose, nonexact-time, or `K`-uncertain/refuted cases remain in the comparator cascade but outside a propagation comparator denominator.

No tier can borrow events from a lower tier to pass a precision gate. No post-outcome collapse of purpose, modality, interval, or organ is permitted. A pooled “all nonthoracic” contrast is forbidden.

Apply the identical unique canonical `DS`, `V/H/Z`, stale/delayed exclusion, content rubric, and all-opportunity table separately to `X1`, `X2`, and `X3`. Comparator fidelity requires its named target/organ, compatible imaging action, and exact interval/date. For each tier publish the full frozen denominator and `count(K,L,V,H,Z)/N_Xt`, including excluded-actionability and unknown categories.

For `X1`, standardize to the pulmonary `P_N` distribution using cells fixed before content review: gap band `24-72/>=72`, interval `3/6/12`, admission type, ICU indicator, LOS `0-1/>1-3/>3-7/>7` days, terminal-service family, and discharge-location family. Use the inherited deterministic fallback hierarchy and report every cell, fallback, weight, maximum normalized weight, and effective sample size. Up to four without-replacement deterministic controls per pulmonary case using the same ordered cells are a sensitivity. Covariates and fallback cannot be selected by content results.

Because organ biology and service ownership can still alter handoff, primary specificity additionally requires transportability across target families. Report each prespecified `X1` organ-family contrast and leave-one-family-out contrast. Require at least two organ families with adequate support, no family contributing more than 50% of effective comparator weight, no adequately estimated family contrast in the opposite direction, and no leave-one-family-out sign reversal. If these gates fail, `X1` is nontransportable and specificity is inconclusive rather than inferred from a weighted mixture.

## Estimands and decision-facing margins

All counts, margins, capacity assumptions, tier definitions, and gates are signed and hashed before outcome adjudication. Report estimates continuously even when no label is allowed.

1. **All-opportunity state:** `J_N`, inherited `Y`, and `count(K,L,V,H,Z)/N`; equivalent separate tables for each comparator tier. Every conditional numerator is also shown over its complete frozen denominator.
2. **Actionability composition:** `a_supported=n(L_yes,K_supported)/N`, `a_refuted`, and each unknown proportion with simultaneous uncertainty. This determines whether the bottleneck is selecting an actionable recommendation versus propagating it.
3. **Actionability-gated propagation:** `r_N` and `b_N` above, plus `NNR`. Also report ungated discordance only as a decomposition, never as propagation failure.
4. **Primary semantic specificity:** `r_X1^std` within `L_yes,K_supported,E_X1` and `Delta_sem=r_N-r_X1^std`; also report the all-opportunity burden difference `B_sem=b_N-b_X1^std` per 100 opportunities. Tier-2 and Tier-3 contrasts are separately named and descriptive.
5. **Availability:** preserve `q_g(b)=P(DS_store>D+b)` at `b=0,24,72,168` for pulmonary `N`, full ordinary controls `O`, standardized `O`, and deterministic matched `M`, with `Delta_A(b)=q_N(b)-q_O^std(b)` and missingness bounds.
6. **Short-window chronology:** preserve `r_N(H_0_6)-r_N(E)` as a secondary descriptor, never a prerequisite for absolute persistence.

The default planning margin for `r_N` is `m_yield=0.10`, explicitly interpreted as at least 10 observed discordant artifacts per 100 actionability-supported ample-gap records, or `NNR<=10`. This is a reconciliation-capacity benchmark, not a safety threshold. The specificity margin is `m_excess=0.05`, interpreted as at least five additional discordant artifacts per 100 records for a nodule-specific rather than generic tool. Before unblinding, a clinical-governance team must either ratify these defaults or lock locally justified alternatives using expected review capacity, alert burden, and implementation cost. Always publish decision curves for `m_yield` in `{0.05,0.10,0.20}` and `m_excess` in `{0.03,0.05,0.10}`. If no governance margin is locked, MIMIC may support existence/direction but the intervention-warrant label is operationally inconclusive.

A scientific “nonzero phenotype” requires the simultaneous lower compatible bound for `r_N` to exceed zero and cannot alone warrant intervention. A **capacity-relevant propagation phenotype** requires the simultaneous lower compatible bound for `r_N` to exceed the locked `m_yield`, at least five lower-bound addressable failures per 100 all `N` (`b_N>0.05`) so a tiny selected subgroup cannot drive the label, and all pulmonary gates. It is adverse to that capacity-relevant claim if the simultaneous upper bound for `r_N` is at or below `m_yield` or the upper bound for `b_N` is at or below 0.05; otherwise it is indeterminate.

A **nodule-specific propagation phenotype** requires capacity-relevant pulmonary propagation plus an adequate, transportable `X1` and simultaneous lower bounds `Delta_sem>m_excess` and `B_sem>0`. If `X1` is comparably high and the upper bound for `Delta_sem` is at or below the margin, this is adverse to nodule specificity but may support a generic same-action-carrier process. `X2` or `X3` may contextualize that finding but can never substitute for failed or sparse `X1`.

## Ordinary and chest controls retained unchanged

* `O`: every adult admission with one unique `DS` and nonmissing `dischtime/storetime`, excluding every pulmonary opportunity/index admission. It calibrates ordinary artifact storage latency, not content semantics.
* `I`: every exact admission-linked inpatient chest/CT radiology report in `[admittime-12h,D]` that fails the pulmonary recommendation retrieval screen, retaining `note_id,note_type,charttime,storetime`. It calibrates radiology storage lag, not recommendation actionability or visibility.
* `M`: up to four deterministic controls per pulmonary `N` selected without replacement and without storage outcomes using admission type, discharge-location family, ICU indicator, locked LOS bins, and terminal-service family; preserve shortfalls. Full `O` standardization is primary and `M` is sensitivity.

Compare `R_store-charttime` for pulmonary components, each semantic comparator tier, and `I`. `charttime` is descriptive/placebo only.

## Blinding, reliability, uncertainty, and feasibility gates

Eligibility/chain reviewers are blinded to discharge data, later events, and outcome labels. Actionability reviewers are additionally blinded as specified above. Separate content reviewers receive only the frozen target/action/interval/alternate-resolution abstraction and canonical `DS` text; they are blinded to `R_store`, `DS_store`, `D`, gap/delay state, downstream traces, observed comparator rates, and the study's expected direction. Target organ makes perfect pulmonary-versus-nonpulmonary masking impossible; report this limitation rather than claiming full blinding. Tier assignment is mechanical after semantic labels freeze.

Every candidate is independently dual reviewed with third-review resolution. Save pre-consensus confusion matrices and report raw agreement and prevalence-adjusted/weighted kappa separately for `L`, each `K` category/reason, semantic purpose, interval, chain component, and `F/C_O/C_I/R/U_text`. Directional conclusions require at least 90% raw agreement and weighted kappa at least 0.70 for `K`, semantic purpose, interval, and content; consensus cannot cure failed reliability.

Pulmonary gates: at least 200 adjudicated `N`; `U_R<=5%`; `U_D<=5%`; at least 30 in `P_N`; at least 20 pulmonary `C_O+C_I` events in `P_N`; no duplicate `DS`. `K_uncertain_context+K_unresolved_review>20%` makes the broad intervention mapping actionability-inconclusive, though category-specific estimates remain.

Tier-1 specificity gates: at least 100 adjudicated `X1` overall, at least 30 in its ample `K_supported` set, at least 20 discordant events across pulmonary/Tier-1 ample strata, no empty pulmonary-supported standardization cell, maximum normalized weight `<=0.10`, effective sample size `>=50`, at least two organ families each with 30 ample cases and 10 discordant events, no target family over 50% effective weight, and stable standardization/matching and leave-one-family-out results. Failure of any Tier-1 gate yields **specificity-inconclusive**, not nodule-specific support. Tier 2/3 adequacy does not repair it. Separately interpreted gap or organ bins require at least 30 observations and 10 discordant events.

Use subject-cluster bootstrap with fixed seed and at least 10,000 replicates. Resample subjects with all rows; recompute standardization weights and matched sets as appropriate. One replicate carries all `J`, `K/L` compositions, unknown-compatible bounds, `r_N`, `b_N`, `r_X1`, `Delta_sem`, `B_sem`, organ/leave-one-out contrasts, short-gap contrast, and all `Delta_A(b)`. Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family for decision-facing quantities. Pointwise intervals are display-only. Unknown bounds, not imputation, drive labels.

## Falsification and robustness

1. Recompute all source hashes, schemas, complete row/key diagnostics, and frozen-manifest hashes. A mismatch, impossible time, duplicate pulmonary canonical `DS`, or nonreciprocal canonical chain stops confirmation.
2. Verify one and only one `V`, `H`, `Z`, `Y`, `L`, and `K` state per frozen row; all joint tables must sum to their frozen denominator.
3. Verify `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G` row-wise. Only `G` may define content opportunity.
4. Deliberately faithful delayed artifacts must remain `Y=A` and outside `E`; deliberately matching or discordant `G<0` artifacts must remain `H_stale` and outside positive-window failure estimands.
5. Relabeling a `K_refuted` case from discordant to faithful must not change `r_N`; changing a blinded `K_supported` label legitimately must. No content field may enter `K` adjudication or code.
6. A synthetic 30% discordance rate in every positive gap bin must support absolute persistence while producing no required gap gradient.
7. A synthetic pulmonary 30% and Tier-1 30% pattern must produce generic same-carrier persistence/adverse-to-specificity, not null and not nodule-specific.
8. A synthetic pulmonary 30% pattern with only five Tier-1 ample cases, an empty standardization cell, one dominant organ, or adequate Tier 2 but inadequate Tier 1 must produce **specificity-inconclusive**. The program must not pool tiers or treat missing controls as low comparator risk.
9. A synthetic reversal confined to one adequately supported Tier-1 organ family or any leave-one-family-out sign reversal must block specificity.
10. Compare full standardization and deterministic matching. A sign reversal or absolute difference `>=0.05` in `Delta_sem` blocks specificity; the pulmonary absolute result remains visible.
11. Compare ordinary `O`, standardized `O`, and `M` at departure and +24/+72/+168 hours. A sign reversal, empty cells, low effective sample size, or standardized-versus-matched `Delta_A` difference `>=0.02` blocks availability prioritization.
12. Stratify by locked radiology storage-lag bins `<6,6-24,>=24h`; disappearance/reversal of specificity is adverse to specificity, not evidence of another mechanism. Repeat `charttime` as a labeled placebo only.
13. Repeat ample `G` thresholds at 12 and 72 hours and the linkage window at admission versus admission-minus-12-hours without adding cases to frozen `N`. A sign reversal makes the affected claim inconclusive.
14. Within locked admission/gap/interval cells, permute pulmonary versus Tier-1 labels; separately permute gap labels within pulmonary timely rows. Null-centered distributions are implementation checks, not inferential evidence.
15. Leave out each reviewer, semantic purpose label, interval, and organ family. Reviewer-driven or component-reversing conclusions are inconclusive.
16. Hash and compare `N` across eligibility, actionability, content, availability, controls, transitions, and downstream outputs. Comparator tier denominators must also remain fixed after assignment.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive patterns and intervention implications

**Supportive for an actionability-gated pulmonary propagation phenotype:** pulmonary gates pass; actionability reliability is adequate; the simultaneous least-favorable lower bounds exceed the locked `m_yield` for `r_N` and 0.05 for `b_N`. This supports a prospective reconciliation intervention study among actionability-supported recommendations. It does not show that an author saw or ignored a report or that correction improves follow-up or health.

**Supportive for a nodule-specific intervention study:** the pulmonary pattern above is present, Tier 1 alone passes every precision/overlap/organ-transportability gate, and lower bounds for `Delta_sem` and `B_sem` exceed their margins without reversals. This supports prospectively testing a nodule-targeted structured field/alert component with true UI, signature, viewing, communication, and order timestamps.

**Supportive for a generic actionable-imaging workflow study:** pulmonary and adequate Tier-1 same-carrier rates both exceed the capacity margin, but the nodule excess does not; compatible Tier-2 results strengthen generalizability but are not required. Test a cross-organ recommendation reconciliation workflow, not a nodule-only tool.

**Supportive for actionability resolution rather than propagation:** `K_uncertain_context` is common, actionability reliability is acceptable, and discordance concentrated in that category cannot satisfy `P_N`. ## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

This child preserves the parent's frozen all-`N` opportunity denominator, reciprocal recommendation chain, unique canonical artifact, `F/C_O/C_I/R/A/U_D`, complete `V x H x Z` state, `G=DS_store-R_store`, stale and delayed safeguards, ordinary/chest/matched controls, unsampled audits, unknown-compatible bounds, calibration, and strict noncausal limits.

The substantive repair is decision validity. Eligibility and available-record actionability are now blinded, explicit states rather than assumptions; propagation failure is estimated only in the supported stratum while every case remains visible over `N`. The heterogeneous comparator is replaced by a prespecified hierarchy with Tier 1 restricted to the same CT interval-stability semantic carrier, separate modality and intent stress tests, organ-family transportability gates, and an absolute prohibition on pooling tiers to rescue precision. Addressable failures per 100 opportunities and records reviewed per observed discordance make intervention capacity explicit, while governance-locked margins and decision curves distinguish a scientific phenotype from a deployment claim.

Uncertainty remains: available MIMIC notes may omit decisive prior imaging, outside plans, preferences, and ownership; reviewers cannot be fully blinded to target organ; `storetime` is not an editing/viewing interval; and Tier 1 may be too sparse or biologically nontransportable. Those failures produce actionability- or specificity-inconclusive results, not false support.
