# Discharge-plan fidelity after an interval-specific pulmonary-nodule recommendation: a complete-source, adjudication-gated MIMIC-IV experiment

## 1. Clinical question and bounded advance

A radiologist can recommend interval CT for an incidental pulmonary nodule, but a discharge artifact may fail to carry the patient-specific action and timing. The clinically consequential, identifiable bottleneck in MIMIC-IV is therefore **faithful propagation into the final discharge plan**, not “patient adherence.” The downstream question is whether that documented propagation is associated with a recommendation-timed chest CT that is observable in the same MIMIC system.

Prior evidence supports the existence of communication/tracking problems and suggests that multifaceted tracking may improve documented completion, but it does not establish that discharge-summary documentation causes surveillance, that any recommendation is guideline-appropriate, or that surveillance prevents malignancy. This experiment tests the narrower, falsifiable claims:

* **Primary process hypothesis:** among adults with an expert-adjudicated incidental pulmonary nodule and an unconditional recommendation for chest CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 months, a material fraction of final discharge summaries available after the index report does not faithfully preserve the patient-specific nodule, chest-CT action, and interval. The primary estimand is a multinomial disposition distribution and, secondarily, the faithful-versus-omitted proportion. A benchmark of 0.80 is an operational decision threshold, not an evidence-derived clinical standard.
* **Secondary observation-limited hypothesis:** faithful propagation is associated with a higher probability of a subsequent **MIMIC-observed** chest CT/CTA in the prespecified recommendation window than omission. This is an association conditional on documentation and measured covariates, not a causal effect and not all-system completion.

The substantive advance is to separate an auditable report-to-discharge process measure from an observation/capture-limited downstream signal, while preserving negative feasibility evidence and explicitly showing where clinical adjudication is indispensable.

## 3. Population and temporal algorithm

The unit is the **first eligible recommendation admission per subject**. No row sampling is permitted.

1. Link a radiology note to an admission when the keys match and `admittime - 12 hours <= charttime <= dischtime`; rerun with a zero-hour lower bound. Require adult age `anchor_age + year(admittime) - anchor_year >=18`, treating `anchor_age=91` as `90+` rather than an exact age.
2. Screen CT/CTA chest and abdominal CT with lung-base coverage using detail metadata (`field_name` in `exam_name`, `exam_code`) and a predeclared text fallback. Exclude lung-screening examinations.
3. Identify reports containing a pulmonary/lung nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity and an unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months. Lexical screening is only candidate generation.
4. Reconstruct report/addendum chains from detail links. For each base report, order linked versions by available `storetime` and `note_id`; concatenate the base text and only linked addenda stored by `dischtime` (and separately by index-summary ascertainment). A recommendation first introduced or materially changed after discharge is ineligible. If links are missing or contradictory, retain a `version_uncertain` flag and send to adjudication; do not silently choose the favorable version.
5. Exclude scheduled screening, explicit nodule surveillance/staging at index, known/suspected active lung-cancer work-up, conditional/optional recommendations, “no follow-up needed,” nonpulmonary nodules, active hospice/comfort-only transition, and death before exposure ascertainment. A report with prior local imaging is not excluded merely for missing history. A prior report establishing guideline-complete stability is adjudicated as an exclusion; otherwise retain history as `prior_history_unknown` and perform a >=2-year local-history sensitivity.
6. For each candidate admission, choose the latest eligible discharge summary stored by `dischtime+24h` using the stated tie-break. Require index report finalization no later than both discharge and summary `storetime` for the primary exposure cohort. Record candidates failing either boundary, summary availability by discharge/24h/7d, and death before ascertainment.

Two radiology-trained reviewers, blinded to discharge text and future reports, adjudicate eligibility and abstract: incidental status; true nodule versus infection/atelectasis/granuloma; indication; active versus conditional recommendation; interval; action; index exam class; location; size/type/multiplicity/newness or stability when stated; prior-history evidence; and whether an addendum changes the recommendation. A third reviewer resolves disagreements. Store both raw labels, consensus, disagreement, and adjudication reason.

## 4. Primary exposure/disposition estimand

Two transition reviewers, blinded to index report and future imaging, review the full final discharge text. They record patient-specific nodule mention, CT/thoracic action, interval/date, responsible service if stated, and documented alternate resolution. The algorithm matches independently abstracted fields to the index recommendation.

Define mutually exclusive dispositions:

* `faithful`: patient-specific nodule plus chest CT/unambiguous thoracic CT action plus the same 3/6/12-month interval or a compatible calendar date;
* `incomplete_or_discordant`: nodule mention without action, action without timing, or an incompatible interval without explicit resolution;
* `omitted`: no patient-specific actionable nodule follow-up content;
* `explicit_alternate_resolution`: documented refusal/preference, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, already scheduled outside follow-up, or a documented clinical decision superseding CT. This records documentation, not medical correctness.

Negated, historical, family-history, hypothetical, generic PCP follow-up, copied radiology prose without an active patient-specific plan, and boilerplate do not count as faithful. Report the full multinomial distribution among adjudicated eligible recommendations with an available final summary, and the cascade per 100 candidates: eligible report -> summary by discharge/24h -> disposition. The binary faithful-versus-omitted contrast excludes only alternate-resolution cases and is secondary to the multinomial result.

Primary process inference requires complete adjudication, no unresolved disagreements, candidate-screen sensitivity >=0.95 on adjudicated cases, and PPV >=0.90 for automated faithful/omitted labels with uncertainty. Require >=200 eligible recommendations for a formal estimate; otherwise provide exact descriptive proportions. Test the operational hypothesis against 0.80 only by reporting an exact 95% binomial interval and whether it lies wholly below 0.80; do not interpret 0.80 as a clinical norm.

## 5. Secondary same-system surveillance outcome

For each eligible recommendation, due date is `index charttime + interval` using calendar-month addition (`relativedelta`, not 30.4375-day approximation). Surveillance time zero is `max(dischtime, final_summary.storetime)`; no pre-zero report can be attributed to the discharge plan.

Using all later `note/radiology` reports for the subject, classify chest CT/CTA using detail `exam_name`/`exam_code` and text fallback. The primary secondary outcome is any report with `charttime > time_zero` and `due-42 days <= charttime <= due+42 days`. The report must be stored/available as a subsequent report; charttime is the event time. A blinded reviewer separately labels whether it is nodule-focused (comparison/surveillance/impression language referring to the index nodule), symptom-driven/acute, or indeterminate. Secondary descriptive outcomes are +/-30 and +/-90-day windows, any chest CT by due+42, time to first chest CT, premature imaging before due-42, acute-admission CT, and descriptive stability/escalation on the first follow-up. Do not infer malignancy or appropriateness from text.

Define observation, separately from the outcome, for each due window: `observed_through_window=1` if a later MIMIC radiology report or later admission occurs after `due+90 days`, or death is recorded before window end. Report four states for each exposure arm: timely CT; no timely CT with later same-system trace; no later trace (unknown/outside care/right-censored); death before window end. Death is a competing state, not failed surveillance. `dod` and in-hospital death are incomplete mortality evidence. Cross every disposition arm with observation state and timely CT; do not assign the unknown state outcome zero.

The secondary estimand is the standardized risk difference and risk ratio for MIMIC-observed timely CT comparing faithful versus omitted documentation, initially all eligible cases and with an observation-restricted descriptive analysis. Because observation may be a post-exposure collider, the observed-through-window restriction is not the sole primary analysis. An inverse-probability-of-observation sensitivity uses only pre-exposure variables and states its unverifiable missing-at-random assumption.

## 6. Analysis plan, positivity, and falsification

First tabulate adjudicated counts, disposition and annotation confusion matrices, interval distribution, report/addendum timing, summary timing, missing history, observation states, and arm overlap. Give exact or Wilson intervals for unadjusted risks. Adjust only with pre-summary variables: interval; exam coverage; abstracted size/type/multiplicity/newness; prior-year local chest CT and admission counts; age category; sex; race/language/insurance; admission type/location; length of stay; ICU exposure; transfer count/last unit; final service; discharge location; COPD/emphysema; cancer history excluding active lung cancer; tobacco-code availability; immunosuppression and severity proxies. No future report, observation marker, or outcome enters adjustment.

Use overlap-weighted logistic regression and regression standardization/doubly robust estimation only after displaying propensity distributions, 0.05/0.95 trimming, weighted standardized mean differences, effective sample size, and outcome events. Use bootstrap confidence intervals that resample at the subject level. If overlap is poor, use exact/stratified estimates by interval, exam class, prior utilization, and service; never extrapolate across empty cells. Prespecified gates for an adjusted surveillance claim are >=50 faithful and >=50 omitted, >=15 timely events in each arm, effective sample size >=40 per arm, no structural nonoverlap, key weighted absolute SMD <=0.10, and annotation PPV >=0.90. With <30 subjects per primary arm, <15 total timely events, failed gates, or severe disagreement, stop at descriptive results and call the association inconclusive.

Mandatory falsification/utilization diagnostics are:

1. A temporal placebo: chest CT in a pre-index/predicted pseudo-window that cannot be caused by discharge propagation. A similarly sized positive contrast flags utilization confounding, leakage, or extraction error.
2. Timely non-chest imaging as a generic system-engagement control. A comparable contrast indicates capture/utilization rather than nodule-specific handoff.
3. Continued same-system trace as an observation outcome. A large exposure association warns that differential capture may drive the apparent downstream contrast.
4. Nodule-focused versus any-chest and acute CT specificity. A result driven by acute/non-nodule CT is not support for the handoff mechanism.
5. Timing and cohort sensitivities: +/-30/+/-90 days; zero versus 12-hour admission linkage; strict summaries by discharge versus +24h and +7d; CTPA-only; exact interval subsets; exclude all cancer codes; require post-window local trace; and exclude CTs occurring only during later acute admissions. These are declared before seeing outcome estimates.

The dedicated `RECOMMENDATION(S)` heading is descriptive only. The complete detail audit shows no section labels, and the full-text heading proxy found only 8 among 1,022 summary-linked candidates (6 among 643 proxy-gated subjects; 2/6 faithful), so no heading-effect model is attempted unless adjudication unexpectedly yields >=50 per arm and positivity. It is not the intervention exposure.

## 7. Complete no-sampling feasibility evidence

The completed audit scanned all 2,321,355 `note/radiology` rows, all candidate-linked discharge rows, all 6,046,121 `note/radiology_detail` rows, all 331,794 `note/discharge_detail` rows, complete admissions and patients archive members, and longitudinal radiology for candidate subjects. Under broad lexical rules, it found 1,291 inpatient-window reports, 1,241 with CT metadata, and 1,194 first candidate subjects; 1,022 had a summary by 7 days, 770 by 24 hours, and 285 by discharge. After lexical leakage/crude cancer-hospice-death gates, 643 proxy subjects remained: 403 faithful, 188 omitted, and 52 partial/discordant. These are not clinical labels.

Among 394 proxy subjects with a later same-system trace, recommendation-timed CT was 44/253 (17.4%) in faithful versus 16/99 (16.2%) in omitted; any chest CT was 77/253 (30.4%) versus 30/99 (30.3%). In all 643 proxy-gated subjects, timed CT was 49/403 (12.2%) versus 17/188 (9.0%), while observation differed (62.8% versus 52.7%). Prior-year chest CT differed (11.7% versus 15.4%), and non-chest imaging in the observed subset was 36.4% versus 41.4%. Thus the audit supplies feasibility and a near-null crude downstream warning, not evidence of benefit or failure. The report-section proxy is infeasible as a principal branch.

Reuse, without rerunning unneeded scans, the audited artifacts and scripts supplied with the parent candidates: `[internal dataset path]`, `support-1-nodule_full_audit.json`, `support-2-audit_details.py`, and `support-3-detail_audit.json`. The audit output is feasibility evidence only; rerun in managed compute if a receipt/output is required in compilation, declaring the read-only radiology, radiology_detail, discharge, and archive inputs. Preserve the differing parent lexical counts (692 versus 1,194 first subjects) as screen-definition sensitivity rather than silently selecting the more favorable count.

## 8. Interpretation gates and limits

* **Supportive primary process result:** faithful proportion is below the operational 0.80 threshold with a precise interval and a meaningful incomplete/omitted fraction. This justifies prospective testing of an interval-specific discharge task, not a claim that the task improves care.
* **Adverse primary process result:** faithful proportion is at least 0.90 with the lower interval bound at least 0.80, or most apparent failures are documented alternate resolutions. The bottleneck likely lies in tracking, scheduling, notification, or cross-system exchange rather than discharge text.
* **Supportive secondary result:** adjusted faithful-versus-omitted difference is positive with a confidence interval excluding zero, all gates pass, nodule-focused results are directionally consistent, and placebo/generic/observation controls are materially weaker. This supports a prospective randomized or stepped-wedge documentation/tracking evaluation, never causal benefit, closure, guideline adherence, malignancy prevention, or survival.
* **Adverse secondary result:** adequate-precision difference is nonpositive, or any apparent contrast is confined to premature/acute/non-nodule CT. This argues against treating discharge text alone as sufficient and favors active tracking or better endpoint capture.
* **Inconclusive:** wide intervals (predeclare +/-10 percentage points), arm/event scarcity, poor overlap, unreliable annotation, timing sensitivity, discordant observation restrictions, or positive controls. The process distribution can remain reportable if its own gates pass; an unobserved patient is never called nonadherent.

Automatic verification can check snapshot/source hashes, exact archive members, joins, deduplication, addendum and `storetime` ordering, calendar due-date arithmetic, frozen cohort boundaries, arm/event/positivity gates, controls, confidence intervals, and conclusion-to-output linkage. It cannot establish true nodule identity, guideline appropriateness, conditionality beyond expert review, communication to the patient, referral/order intent, outside imaging, refusal when undocumented, complete mortality, malignancy, or benefit. Pixels, outpatient orders/visits, appointments, notification, patient preference, reliable pack-years, pathology/stage, and outside-system records require expert review or another prospective/linkage study.
