# Can conservative pulmonary-nodule handoff routes survive independent review? A blinded, all-opportunity reproducibility and purity audit before silent validation

## Decision bottleneck and targeted advance

The unresolved clinical-research decision is whether the inherited admission-level `AUTO/RECON/TIMING/DEFER` policy is reproducible and observably pure enough to justify even a prospective **silent** validation. A large retrospective count of apparent omissions would not answer that question. `AUTO` could be produced by reviewers jointly comparing the source recommendation with the destination discharge document, `RECON` could reflect subjective pairwise interpretation, consensus could conceal route-changing disagreement, and recommendation-unit summaries could hide one conflicting unit in an otherwise copyable admission.

This child preserves the parent's frozen first-eligible all-opportunity admission denominator, reciprocal pre-departure report/addendum chronology, one unique canonical discharge artifact, unit-specific recommendation-to-artifact clocks, exhaustive departure states, worst-case admission routing for mixed cases, exact source provenance, and strict noncausal limits. It does not change the clinical target or relax eligibility. Its substantive change is to make **route reproducibility and conditional text-defined purity** the primary experiment:

1. a production-like panel constructs candidate routes from independently reviewed source, destination, and redacted-context atoms; no reviewer compares source with destination or votes on a route;
2. a disjoint outcome-blinded challenge panel reviews paired departure-frozen evidence but never sees the production route, clocks, downstream records, expected direction, or prospective priority;
3. all initial reviewer labels, unit-boundary alternatives, and unknown-compatible assignments are retained, so route agreement and purity are partially identified rather than manufactured by consensus; and
4. workload, route-confirmed burden, false-nomination burden, ambiguity burden, and review time are reported per 100 members of the full frozen denominator, not only among selected or routed cases.

“Pure” here means only that a conservative candidate route is reproduced by independently derived, departure-frozen **observable text states**. It does not mean guideline appropriateness, clinical safety, usefulness, or benefit.

## Supported claim, unresolved hypothesis, and required evidence

### Strongest claim currently supported

The inherited complete-source, zero-sampling scan supports data availability, not a clinical prevalence result: 546,028 admissions, 2,321,355 radiology rows, 6,046,121 radiology-detail rows, 331,794 unique canonical `DS` admission keys, only 17 missing `DS storetime` values, substantial ordinary discharge storage delay, and 3,764 retrieval-proxy pulmonary reports. Direct inspection in this episode confirmed the exact headers and bounded rows in all five sources and streamed all detail rows: radiology detail contains `exam_code`, `exam_name`, `cpt_code`, `parent_note_id`, and `addendum_note_id`; discharge detail contains only `author`. The proxy reports have not been clinically adjudicated and do not establish the number of eligible opportunities, active omissions, routes, route purity, or benefit.

### Falsifiable hypothesis

> In the frozen first-eligible pulmonary-opportunity admissions, conservative admission routes generated from separately reviewed departure-frozen source, destination, and context atoms will be reproducible by a disjoint blinded challenge panel. Within the ample-opportunity stratum, robust `AUTO` nominations will have a simultaneous lower bound of at least 0.90 for independent text-defined route concordance, an upper bound of at most 0.10 for any challenge-compatible `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` state, and an upper bound of at most 0.10 for hidden mixed-admission routing. Robust `RECON` nominations will meet the same 0.90 concordance and 0.10 discordance gates. These results will remain stable under every individual-reviewer-compatible, unknown-adverse, and minimum/maximum unit-boundary assignment, with enough events and precision to justify prospective silent validation of the frozen gate.

The 0.90/0.10 thresholds are feasibility and nomination-purity margins, not clinical harm utilities. They must be approved and frozen by clinical governance before challenge labels are released; absent approval, burdens and bounds are estimable but no prospective nomination is made.

### Exact evidence needed

For every denominator admission the experiment needs: the complete reciprocal report/addendum chain and operative pre-departure component; every eligible recommendation unit and its source storage time; the unique canonical `DS` text and storage time; discharge time; admission/disposition context stored by departure; all individual panel labels with quoted spans and missing-fact reasons; minimum and maximum compatible unit inventories; derived route sets; packet manifests and hashes; review start/stop times; and the complete all-denominator chronology and route table. It does not use later outcomes to define truth.

## Frozen denominator, chronology, and clocks

Before any discharge text, discharge `storetime`, challenge label, eventual note, or downstream trace is accessed, construct and hash the radiology eligibility manifest. The primary denominator `N_A` is exactly the inherited first eligible admission per subject:

* adult;
* exact admission-linked radiology report with `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`;
* incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity;
* patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months;
* exclude screening, established surveillance, cancer staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional or optional plans, explicit no-follow-up, in-hospital death, hospice/comfort-only transition under the locked rubric, and recommendations first introduced after `D`;
* never infer nodule size, smoking, malignancy risk, life expectancy, preference, or recommendation appropriateness.

Eligibility is based on reciprocal, subject-consistent and admission-consistent report/addendum chains only. Each operative component must have `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. A later material pre-`D` component supersedes an earlier one only within that chain. Missing nodes, one-way or contradictory links, unresolved operative components, and missing or contradictory recommendation times are `U_R`; they remain in the cascade and bounds and can never become omissions. Eligibility reviewers see neither discharge data nor proposed routes.

The canonical destination is the unique exact `DS` row for `(subject_id,hadm_id)`. Missing destination, text, linkage, or storage time is `U_D`. `charttime` never substitutes for `storetime`.

For the denominator-defining operative component retain `R_store`, `DS_store`, and

`G = DS_store - R_store` in hours.

For every additional eligible recommendation unit `u`, retain `R_store,u` and

`G_u = DS_store - R_store,u`.

Retain the parent's exact `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, eventual `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}`, and departure `Y={F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}` definitions and publish every zero/unknown cell of `J_A=count(V,H,Z)/N_A`. The ample primary stratum remains `E_A={V_D and index H in (H_24_72,H_72plus)}`. Within it, any active unresolved unit with `G_u<24h` or unresolved clock forces `TIMING` or `DEFER`; it can never be called a content failure. No `G<0`, `DS_store>D`, stale artifact, or post-departure text enters a content-route estimand. Check row-wise that `D-R_store=(D-DS_store)+G`; this identity does not redefine opportunity.

## Recommendation units and worst-case admission aggregation

After `N_A` is frozen, source reviewers enumerate all independently eligible pulmonary recommendation units in each index admission. This nested inventory `N_R` never adds admissions or subjects. A unit is the smallest operative tuple

`U=(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity)`.

Co-mentioned nodules sharing one action and due window form one unit. Different actions or due windows are distinct. Repeated language and reciprocal addendum restatements are deduplicated; a material addendum change supplies the final operative version rather than a second event. If boundaries cannot be determined, retain `U_BOUNDARY` and both minimum- and maximum-unit compatible inventories. Consensus cannot erase either inventory.

Every unit records `(subject_id,hadm_id,unit_id)`, source `note_id`, chain-node IDs, quoted spans and offsets, target/action/timing/polarity atoms, `R_store,u`, `G_u`, and boundary provenance. The admission is always the intervention, resampling, and multiplicity unit.

## Two independent adjudication systems

All packets contain only data stored by `D`. Packet generators produce a row-level manifest, included storage times, text offsets, version, and hash. An automated prohibited-field scan plus human packet audit rejects any packet containing post-`D` material, downstream outcomes, POE, eventual route, other-panel labels, or expected direction. Reviewers are disjoint across production and challenge systems and declare conflicts. A 50-admission stratified pilot may refine only instructions and redaction; its labels are destroyed before the protocol, thresholds, seed, and hashes are frozen.

### System P: production-like atomization without pairwise comparison

Each stream has two independent primary physicians; a third adjudicator may add an opinion but never deletes initial labels.

**P-SOURCE** receives only the operative radiology chain and minimal timestamps sufficient to enforce pre-`D` inclusion, not the `DS`, `DS_store`, `G`, later records, or intended route. It independently establishes eligibility, final operative component, unit boundaries, target cluster, action, exact due interval/date, polarity, and source uncertainty.

**P-DESTINATION** receives only the canonical departure-frozen `DS`, with radiology text, report identifiers, recommendation timing, and route language withheld. It extracts every independently recognizable pulmonary target-linked destination plan into atoms: target, action/modality, timing/date, accountable recipient/scheduling, active status, non-pursuit, definitive replacement, and conflict. It may say no plan or unknown. It cannot search for source wording supplied by P-SOURCE.

**P-CONTEXT** receives admission/disposition variables and the canonical `DS` after a separate span team masks every pulmonary target, action, timing, recipient, order, preference, and specialist span. It assigns only `K_OPEN` (no observed non-target closure), `K_CLOSED` (explicit non-target global closure), or `K_UNKNOWN`, with quoted support and missing facts. This is observable compatibility, not appropriateness.

Frozen code then performs source-destination matching. A destination plan resolves a source unit only if target linkage, action, and timing are explicit and compatible, and responsibility or scheduling is explicit for an executable equivalent plan. The hierarchy remains `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, `S_EQ`, `S_CON`, `S_PART`, `S_0`, `S_UN`: explicit target-linked non-pursuit; explicit definitive replacement; executable same-or-more-protective plan no later than the source window; conflict/less-protective plan; incomplete plan; no target-specific plan; unknown. Generic “PCP follow-up” is `S_0`; specialty mention without linked action and timing is `S_PART` or `S_UN`; an unexplained incompatible interval is `S_CON`. No later evidence can upgrade a state.

For every admission enumerate the complete production compatible route set `R_i^P` across the Cartesian product of all initial P-SOURCE, P-DESTINATION, and P-CONTEXT labels, both unit inventories, and logical constraints. Do not assume reviewer independence probabilistically and do not weight label combinations by confidence.

### System C: disjoint outcome-blinded challenge review

Two new physicians independently receive the paired operative radiology evidence and canonical `DS`, plus the same non-target context available by `D`. Packets omit all P labels, P route sets, `G/H/V`, any word such as AUTO or RECON, downstream records, later note versions, thresholds, expected direction, and intervention-priority text. Packet order and within-packet source/destination order are randomized; reviewers cannot communicate.

Challenge reviewers do not vote on a route. For each source unit they separately record unit boundaries and the same atomic fields; then they record whether destination evidence is absent, partial, conflicting, explicit equivalent, explicit non-pursuit, explicit definitive replacement, or unknown, with quotations. A frozen crosswalk derives each reviewer's route. The two initial challenge reviews, adjudicator addition, unknown alternatives, and minimum/maximum inventories define `R_i^C`. Consensus is descriptive only.

A counterbalanced 10% random repeat, stratified by P route and unit count and presented after at least a four-week washout with new packet IDs, estimates intra-review reproducibility. Repeat labels never replace first-pass labels. Review software logs active minutes and interruptions without exposing labels to other reviewers.

### Redaction and leakage controls

Before labeling, lock the masking dictionary and manually audit a stratified 10% of P-CONTEXT packets, including all proposed `K_CLOSED` cases. Record both residual target-plan leakage and removal of non-target context. Seed adversarial target/action/timing/recipient phrases outside the initial dictionary. More than 2% packet errors, with the one-sided 95% upper bound above 2%, blocks all route conclusions. Challenge packets are unredacted where required for comparison but remain route- and outcome-blinded.

## Frozen route logic

For each compatible assignment and each active unit:

* `U_RESOLVED` for `S_DEC`, `S_DEF`, or `S_EQ`;
* `U_OPEN_OMIT` for `K_OPEN + S_0 + wholly omitted + G_u>=24h`;
* `U_OPEN_RECON` for `K_OPEN + (S_PART or S_CON or incomplete/conflicting content) + G_u>=24h`;
* `U_TIMING` for otherwise open unresolved content with `G_u<24h`;
* `U_CLOSED` for `K_CLOSED` without contradictory target evidence;
* `U_DEFER` for `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, unreadable content, unresolved clock, decision-relevant `U_BOUNDARY`, or incompatible reviewer assignments.

Aggregate pessimistically at admission level:

* `A_DEFER`: any compatible assignment or active unit is decision-relevant `U_DEFER`;
* `A_TIMING`: no defer, at least one `U_TIMING`, and no content route is allowed;
* `A_NOACTION`: all units reproducibly resolved or closed;
* `A_AUTO`: every active open unit is a wholly omitted `U_OPEN_OMIT`, every other unit is reproducibly resolved/closed, all clocks are at least 24 hours, unit boundaries are invariant, and no cross-unit conflict exists;
* `A_RECON`: any active unit is incomplete/conflicting or unit interactions prevent independent copying;
* `A_MIXED`: at least one pure omission and at least one reconciliation unit; it is always a named subset of `A_RECON`, never an AUTO success.

Admissions outside `E_A` retain their inherited chronology state and are reported as `A_NONAMPLE`; they cannot silently disappear. A production nomination is **robust** only when `R_i^P` is a singleton. Any production-compatible set crossing routes is `A_DEFER` for nomination, even if its consensus route is AUTO or RECON. The same conservative principle applies to challenge interpretation.

## Primary estimands: all-denominator reproducibility, purity, and workload

All burdens use `N_A`; no outcome-enriched sample is permitted. Report counts, rates per 100 `N_A`, every zero/unknown cell, and subject-clustered simultaneous 95% confidence intervals. Because there is one index admission per subject, subject and admission coincide in the primary denominator, but any nested recommendation units retain the admission bootstrap weight.

### Route workload over the full opportunity population

For `r in {AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,NONAMPLE}`:

`B_r = 100*n(P robust route r)/N_A`.

Also report:

* challenge-certain concordant burden `CB_r=100*n(P route r and R_i^C={r})/N_A`;
* ambiguity burden `AB_r=100*n(P route r and |R_i^C|>1)/N_A`;
* certain discordance burden `DB_r=100*n(P route r and r not in R_i^C)/N_A`;
* `B_MIXED`, every inherited `Y`, `V/H/Z`, and `J_A` cell, and the crosswalk from those cells to routes;
* review minutes and packets per 100 `N_A` for each panel, adjudication minutes, percent requiring third review, units per admission, and expected prospective silent adjudication hours per 100 screened opportunities.

These all-denominator quantities separate clinical-process burden from conditional purity. A route can be pure but too rare to justify infrastructure, or common but too ambiguous to nominate.

### Reviewer-compatible partial identification of route purity

For each robust P nomination `r`, define:

* certain match `M_i^L(r)=1` only when `R_i^C={r}`;
* possible match `M_i^U(r)=1` when `r in R_i^C`;
* certain adverse mismatch `E_i^L(r)=1` when `r not in R_i^C`;
* possible adverse mismatch `E_i^U(r)=1` when `R_i^C` contains any route other than `r`.

Then

`Purity_r in [sum M_i^L/n(P=r), sum M_i^U/n(P=r)]`

and

`Error_r in [sum E_i^L/n(P=r), sum E_i^U/n(P=r)]`.

Unknown-adverse bounds assign every challenge unknown or route-crossing set against the nomination. Unit-boundary bounds repeat calculations under the minimum and maximum compatible inventories. The confirmatory lower purity bound is the minimum and upper error bound the maximum across initial-reviewer combinations, unknown assignments, and inventories. Consensus purity is secondary and cannot pass a failed bound.

For AUTO report specific adverse components: challenge-compatible reconciliation, hidden mixed admission, timing violation, context closure/unknown, unresolved boundary, and apparent source-destination match created only by generic language. For RECON distinguish challenge-confirmed incomplete/conflicting evidence from challenge AUTO, resolved/no-action, timing, and defer. `D_MIX` remains

`P(P admission is RECON or DEFER | it contains at least one unit-level pure omission)`,

with a worst-case upper bound. This preserves the inherited prohibition on unit-majority automation.

### Reproducibility

Report all initial-reviewer confusion matrices and route-set overlap, raw exact route agreement, category-specific positive and negative agreement, Gwet AC1, kappa with prevalence warning, Jaccard overlap of compatible sets, reviewer-specific route rates, intra-review repeat agreement, and leave-one-reviewer-out results. Reliability is evaluated separately for eligibility, operative component, unit boundary, source atoms, destination atoms, `K`, `S`, content state, and final derived route. Agreement on abundant NOACTION or NONAMPLE cases cannot substitute for route-positive agreement.

### Baselines

Compare the production gate against four predeclared nonclinical baselines on the same `N_A`:

1. prevalence-only assignment of the most common route;
2. “copy every eligible recommendation” unit rule, aggregated both by unit majority and worst-case admission logic;
3. lexical overlap between source and `DS`, threshold trained only in the wiped pilot and then frozen;
4. index-unit-only routing that ignores additional units.

The gate must improve route-positive balanced accuracy and reduce false AUTO nominations versus each baseline under challenge-adverse bounds. Baselines cannot define truth and cannot support clinical deployment.

## Inference, gates, and prospective-nomination rule

Use a fixed-seed subject-cluster bootstrap with at least 10,000 replicates. Each replicate carries all route cells, compatible bounds, unit inventories, review workloads, timing controls, and baselines. Use a single max-statistic simultaneous 95% family for `B_AUTO`, `B_RECON`, `B_TIMING`, `B_DEFER`, `CB_AUTO`, `CB_RECON`, `DB_AUTO`, `DB_RECON`, lower purity and upper error for AUTO/RECON, `D_MIX`, and the two availability contrasts retained from the parent. Pointwise intervals are descriptive only.

A route can be nominated for prospective silent validation only if all common gates and its route-specific gates pass.

### Common gates

* `N_A>=200`, `U_R<=5%`, `U_D<=5%`, no duplicate canonical `DS`, at least 60 admissions in `E_A`, and all denominator marginals sum exactly to `N_A`;
* redaction and prohibited-field audits pass;
* at least 90% raw agreement and AC1 or kappa at least 0.70 for eligibility, operative component, unit boundary, source/destination atoms, context, and derived route; if kappa is prevalence-unstable, AC1 is primary but raw and compatible-set stability still must pass;
* at least 90% intra-review exact agreement on repeated derived routes;
* unknown content plus `K_UNKNOWN`, `S_UN`, unresolved clock, and `U_BOUNDARY` is at most 10% of `E_A`;
* every claimed all-denominator burden has simultaneous half-width at most 5 per 100 `N_A`; each claimed conditional purity/error bound has simultaneous half-width at most 10 percentage points;
* production and challenge route conclusions survive leave-one-reviewer-out, minimum/maximum unit inventories, unknown-adverse assignment, and `G>=72h` sensitivity without changing the prospective nomination;
* governance freezes the 0.90 purity/0.10 error and mixed-admission margins before challenge labels are unblinded.

### Route-specific gates

For AUTO: at least 60 robust P-AUTO admissions; simultaneous adverse lower purity bound `>=0.90`; simultaneous upper error bound `<=0.10`; upper bound for challenge-compatible RECON/TIMING/DEFER combined `<=0.10`; upper `D_MIX<=0.10`; and `CB_AUTO` has a lower bound above zero.

For RECON: at least 60 robust P-RECON admissions; simultaneous adverse lower purity bound `>=0.90`; upper error bound `<=0.10`; at least 30 challenge-certain incomplete/conflicting or mixed admissions; and `CB_RECON` has a lower bound above zero.

A two-lane nomination requires both route gates and at least 120 total robust AUTO+RECON admissions. A reconciliation-only nomination is allowed when RECON passes but AUTO fails because of mixed cases or impurity. AUTO-only nomination is allowed only if AUTO passes and RECON is demonstrably sparse with a sufficiently precise upper burden bound; it does not imply reconciliation is unnecessary clinically. If counts are below gates, intervals are wide, or bounds cross a route threshold, the answer is inconclusive rather than negative.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. **Source and schema invariance:** recompute all five source hashes and specified schemas; stream complete sources; stop on drift, impossible timestamps, missing required columns, or denominator-hash change.
2. **Key invariance:** duplicate canonical `DS` keys stop the confirmatory run; unmatched note-admission links and missing fields remain explicit diagnostics.
3. **Reciprocal chronology:** one-way, cross-subject, cross-admission, missing-node, or contradictory addendum links cannot define an operative component. Any component with `charttime>D` or `storetime>D` is prohibited.
4. **Clock test:** verify `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u` and the row identity for every unit. `charttime`, report age at departure, or index-unit opportunity cannot substitute for a short-clock additional unit.
5. **Departure freeze:** inserting any post-`D` text or downstream event into a packet fails the run. Faithful later text cannot change departure `A`, `E_A`, or a route.
6. **Panel firewall:** seed P-DESTINATION packets with source identifiers and P-SOURCE packets with destination phrases; any leakage blocks conclusions. Reviewers never directly vote AUTO/RECON.
7. **Challenge blinding:** seed route, clock, threshold, expected-direction, and later-outcome fields into prohibited-field tests. Discovery in a challenge packet invalidates it and triggers regeneration before labels are viewed.
8. **Boundary adversaries:** shared action/window across co-mentioned nodules is one unit; different windows are separate; repeated addendum wording is deduplicated; unresolved coreference yields both inventories.
9. **Hierarchy adversaries:** generic PCP follow-up is not equivalent; incomplete specialist mention is partial/unknown; scheduled compatible CT with timing and responsibility may be equivalent; explicit non-pursuit and definitive replacement require target linkage; unexplained conflict never resolves.
10. **Mixed-admission adversary:** one pure omission plus one conflict is `A_MIXED` inside RECON under every unit-majority result.
11. **Reviewer-compatible bounds:** forced consensus cannot narrow `R_i^P` or `R_i^C`. An apparent AUTO that changes under any compatible initial label becomes DEFER for nomination.
12. **Unknown worst case:** all decision-relevant unknowns are assigned adversely for lower purity and upper error. Favorable unknown assignment is descriptive only.
13. **Weighting trap:** duplicating units cannot alter admission burdens, bootstrap weight, purity denominator, or route.
14. **All-denominator trap:** deleting NONAMPLE, TIMING, DEFER, unknown, or challenge-discordant rows must fail sum checks. Conditional PPV alone cannot justify a route if all-denominator burden is too sparse.
15. **Baseline adversaries:** lexical copying and unit-majority rules must fail on generic language and mixed fixtures; prevalence-only predictions cannot pass route-positive agreement by exploiting NOACTION prevalence.
16. **Precision logic:** synthetic high-purity, high-impurity, bound-crossing, sparse, mixed, and challenge-discordant fixtures must yield respectively nomination, adverse, inconclusive, inconclusive, reconciliation-only/adverse-to-AUTO, and no-selection results.
17. **Chronology sensitivity:** `G>=12h` and `G>=72h` are displayed; only directionally compatible 72-hour results can pass. Threshold sensitivity never changes `N_A`.
18. **Noncausal language:** a machine-checkable conclusion linter rejects “appropriate,” “safe,” “prevented,” “improved outcomes,” “clinician knew,” “missed follow-up,” or causal equivalents unless explicitly negated as unidentifiable.

## Interpretation of possible results

### Supportive

A supportive result requires reproducible atomic labels, singleton production routes, disjoint challenge agreement, adverse-bound purity at or above 0.90, error and hidden-mixed bounds at or below 0.10, adequate all-denominator route burden, sufficient events, narrow simultaneous intervals, and stability under every reviewer/unknown/boundary/clock analysis. It supports only freezing the extractor and route policy for prospective silent validation.

If AUTO alone passes, a silent study may test whether the frozen gate continues to identify text-defined pure omissions in live workflow. It does not authorize insertion, alerting, or clinician-confirmed prepopulation. If RECON alone passes, a silent study may test reconciliation nomination and AUTO remains deferred. If both pass, a two-lane silent study may be nominated, with lane-specific metrics and no cross-lane pooling.

### Adverse

AUTO is adversely falsified for nomination if its upper error or mixed bound exceeds 0.10, challenge review finds route-changing incompleteness/conflict/timing/unknowns, atomic source-destination separation materially lowers apparent purity, or unit-majority routing differs from worst-case admission routing. RECON is adversely falsified if challenge reviewers usually find pure omissions, resolved plans, timing limitations, or unclassifiable evidence rather than incomplete/conflicting plans. Failure of both routes argues against investing in this retrospective gate as presently specified, not against the clinical importance of handoff failures.

### Inconclusive

Sparse route events, wide intervals, poor inter- or intra-review reliability, challenge-bound crossing, excessive unknowns, redaction leakage, source/key/chain failure, chronology sensitivity reversal, or absent governance approval is inconclusive. Zero observed errors with too few nominations is not proof of purity. A failed comparator is specificity-inconclusive only. Counts below a route gate are not evidence that the route does not exist.

## Required prospective study after a supportive result

MIMIC cannot establish live route performance. Prospective phase P1 must enroll consecutive eligible adult discharges and run the frozen gate silently, without changing care. Required new data include immutable report finalization and addendum events; report availability and viewing; discharge draft creation, version, edit, signature, transmission, and physical-departure times; candidate units; UI-rendered evidence; clinician/team responsibility; linked orders, referrals, and scheduling; outside-plan attestations; patient preferences and competing-risk context; and the final transmitted artifact.

Independent experts blinded to the system route and later outcomes must adjudicate the same departure-frozen reference. Primary P1 endpoints are route confusion matrices, lower-bound AUTO and RECON purity, hidden-mixed miss rate, abstention/defer rate, trigger timeliness, and adjudication workload. Require at least 100 route-positive admissions and at least 60 in each route claimed, or a prespecified maximum accrual period. Any temporal leakage, site drift, or failure of the preapproved purity margin stops progression.

Only a later governed trial can test clinician-confirmed prepopulation or side-by-side reconciliation. It must measure inappropriate insertion, corrections/dismissals and reasons, accepted artifact fidelity at signature, time and alert burden, responsibility assignment, communication, target-linked ordering/scheduling, completion, and safety outcomes with expert adjudication. MIMIC cannot estimate those effects.

## What is computationally checkable and what is not

A verifier can check hashes, columns, keys, complete filtering, first-eligible selection, chain reciprocity, storage-time cutoffs, unique `DS`, unit-clock arithmetic, packet prohibited fields, deterministic route crosswalks, preservation of individual labels, compatible-set enumeration, worst-case mixed aggregation, all-denominator sums, bootstrap clustering, interval families, event/precision gates, baselines, and whether reported conclusions match supportive, adverse, or inconclusive outputs.

A verifier cannot establish eligibility semantics, unit boundaries, target linkage, plan equivalence, supersession, appropriateness, safety, clinical urgency, missing outside plans, clinician awareness, or benefit. Those require blinded clinical adjudication, unavailable workflow evidence, prospective validation, governance, or another study. Expert agreement remains an observed reproducibility result, not ground truth.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, this child resolves the immediate decision bottleneck rather than presuming its routes are valid. It introduces a disjoint source/destination/context production system, a separate outcome- and route-blinded challenge system, no direct route voting, explicit compatible-set partial identification, route-specific all-denominator confirmed/ambiguous/discordant burdens, measured adjudication workload, counterbalanced intra-review repeats, and symmetric event/precision/purity gates. It preserves the parent's clinically important unit-specific clocks and makes mixed-admission worst-case routing an explicit purity component.

Uncertainty remains irreducible in MIMIC: the canonical stored `DS` is not a full contemporaneous chart; `G_u` is storage-to-storage rather than true editing opportunity; redaction can remove context; text-defined agreement is not appropriateness; and the 0.90/0.10 nomination margins require governance rather than being learned from outcomes. Therefore even a fully supportive result would justify only prospective silent validation of a frozen policy, never deployment or a claim of patient benefit.
