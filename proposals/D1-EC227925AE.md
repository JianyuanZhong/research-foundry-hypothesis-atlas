# HCC MVI under fixed information exposure and stable-template temporal testing

Status: substantive design child of [prior hypothesis]. This child preserves the clinical question, preoperative cutoff, recorded-grade target, and M1/M2-versus-M0 endpoint, but changes the temporal estimand and primary comparator after an independent aggregate audit. No MVI outcome model was fitted.

## Scientific opening and claim boundary

The clinical question is whether routinely observed preoperative values contain reproducible information about pathology-recorded HCC microvascular invasion (MVI) beyond a current snapshot. Static AFP, tumor size, and tumor margin have already supported useful preoperative MVI discrimination in external Chinese cohorts [K1]. MVI grading is histopathologic, and M2 has prognostic importance in a selected resection series using a defined sampling protocol [K2]. Routine-care observation is not scheduled: test presence, frequency, and intensity can encode illness and clinician behavior, and should be interpreted differently for prediction than explanation [K3].

The parent correctly restricted inference to explicitly graded pathology cases, but its “value-only” V, D-bag, and T-value models do not actually hold information exposure fixed. Extrema and variability change with the number of measurements; a set or sequence network can infer the number of days/tokens even without explicit count or timestamp features. Thus a gain over the latest snapshot could be generated by more opportunities to observe an abnormal value or by workup intensity, not value evolution.

The strongest supported claim is only that the dataset contains a large explicitly graded resection cohort, selective grade recording is template-era dependent, and routine-care measurement processes can be informative. The unresolved claim tested here is:

> Among explicitly graded, untreated, pathology-HCC first resections in the high-ascertainment 2016–2020 era with at least two strict numeric preoperative lab days, does a model receiving exactly two laboratory days and at most one value per exact label/day improve 2020 prediction of M1/M2 over an otherwise matched latest-day snapshot, after accounting for static severity/tumor-burden inputs and observation intensity?

A positive result supports incremental historical-value prediction under a fixed information budget. It does not establish tumor biology, biological change, causal mechanism, external transport, or treatment benefit. Those stronger claims require scheduled serial assays, validated imaging/pathology, and external prospective study.

## New aggregate evidence and why it changes the design

An independent read-only audit used the same source snapshot and first-resection/treatment rules. No outcome model was fitted and no identifiers, dates, raw values, or free text were emitted.

It exactly reproduced 9,084 explicitly graded pathology-HCC resections in 2014–2021 and 4,567 with strict numeric labs on at least two preoperative days: 2,210/935 events in 2014–2018, 992/463 in 2019, 845/325 in 2020, and 520/185 in 2021. Across the 9,084 graded cases, 9,078 had an HCC pathology row labeled as a surgical specimen; six training-era encounters were biopsy-only by the available `Pathology` descriptor and require exclusion/adjudication.

Among graded cases, 9,044 had any strict numeric day −90:−1 lab and 4,567 had at least two days. Distinct lab-day quantiles were 1/1/2/2/3/5/20 (minimum/25th/median/75th/90th/99th/maximum), while raw event-count quantiles were 1/60/85/108/128/203.6/1,112. Thus most “serial” patients have only two calendar days but many panel rows; unrestricted extrema, token sets, and learned sequences are strongly exposed to panel size and duplication.

No exact laboratory label had at least 20 patients with a strict numeric measurement in both a fixed early window (day −90:−31) and late window (day −30:−1). A conventional months-long marker-slope claim is unsupported. The feasible estimand is instead “additional earlier observed values” with fixed exposure, and it remains partly shaped by routine-care observation.

The exact grade parser also yielded 534 lexical discordance flags: 469 M0 encounters had an HCC pathology row containing “Vascular invasion (microscopic finding): present,” 65 M1/M2 encounters had a row containing the corresponding “absent,” and four had both. These are review flags, not proven label errors; multiple template sections/specimens can coexist. The source lacks pathology issue time, block count, and specimen correspondence.

Finally, grade ascertainment was 96–98% in 2016–2020 but 50.3% in 2021. Pooling 2020 and 2021 as one locked test can mask a template-era reversal. This child therefore uses 2020 as the locked primary test and 2021 as a mandatory template-stress set.

## Population, clocks, endpoint, and revised split

Index is the earliest recorded qualifying liver-resection start per patient. A qualifying `surgery` contains liver and resection and excludes transplant/donor, biopsy/puncture, drainage, abscess/cyst, and isolated cholecystectomy. Collapse exact duplicates. Include age ≥18, same-encounter pathology text explicitly supporting HCC, exactly one unique grade extracted after `MVI risk classification indicated:`, and no recorded procedure-defined embolization/intervention, ablation/radiofrequency/microwave, infusion/chemotherapy/radiotherapy, or targeted systemic treatment on days −180:−1.

Primary fitting excludes biopsy-only HCC pathology descriptors and retains surgical-specimen-linked cases. Require strict numeric labs on at least two distinct days in day −90:−1. Prediction cutoff is the start of day −1; day 0 and later are prohibited. Pathology is never a feature.

Primary outcome is recorded M1/M2 versus M0. Ordered M0/M1/M2 is secondary; M2 versus M0/M1 is exploratory. The frozen split is now train 2016–2018, validation 2019, locked primary test 2020, and template-stress test 2021. The sparse 2014 and transitional 2015 cases are era-stress/training-sensitivity data only; 2022–2025 remains report-only. All preprocessors, parsers, vocabularies, penalties, thresholds, hyperparameters, and model choices are learned in train and frozen through validation before either test is opened.

## Matched models and empirically distinguishable rivals

All models use identical patients and the same static inputs. The report-free version is primary until the tumor-report parser passes blinded validation.

1. **S-latest:** penalized logistic regression using age, sex, service, latest strict value and missing indicator for train-eligible exact labels, with splines for prespecified AFP, PIVKA-II, platelets, albumin, bilirubin, ALT, AST, INR/PT, GGT, ALP, WBC, hemoglobin, and creatinine. Add days since latest value and total distinct lab days as explicit observation-process covariates. These process variables are included in every model, so incremental comparisons do not gain them only in one arm.

2. **O-process rival:** S-latest static inputs with all values masked, retaining exact-label availability, distinct days, raw event count, panel size, recency, span, service, and year. This asks how much workup alone predicts.

3. **V2 fixed-exposure:** S-latest plus exactly one earlier calendar day. For each patient and each prespecified deterministic replicate, keep the latest eligible lab day and sample one earlier observed day uniformly from the patient's remaining days using a seed fixed before validation. Within a retained day and exact label, collapse strict numeric duplicates by median; then retain at most one value per label/day. Add earlier value, latest-minus-earlier, and paired-label availability. Do not provide date, gap, row count, or panel multiplicity to V2. Fit 20 train/validation-frozen subsampling replicates and average predictions; report between-replicate uncertainty. The matched S-latest prediction is repeated on the same patients.

4. **D2/T2 learned alternative:** a Deep Sets model over the two capped day sets and a two-step ordinal encoder, respectively, each with the same static inputs and no date/gap/count tokens. They can reveal nonlinear cross-marker and two-state interactions that penalized V2 loses. T2 must beat D2 and lose its gain when the two day embeddings are swapped; with only two days, it does not claim a rich trajectory.

5. **V-all/F-all/T-full exploratory ladder:** preserve the parent's all-observation summaries and learned model only as a process-sensitive contrast. Compare each with its observation-count-matched baseline and V2. Gain present only here is evidence for workup/intensity or exposure-count prediction, not biological trajectory.

This fixed-exposure design distinguishes the specific rival that more observed rows/days mechanically create more extrema, variance, or tokens. It cannot remove selection into having an earlier test or explain why either test was ordered. Therefore all results remain predictive and observational.

## Tumor burden and severity controls

[K1]'s AFP/size/margin score is a benchmark, not currently reproducible from named concepts alone. The examinations table provides dated report text but no images or validated structured tumor fields. Before any “beyond tumor burden” claim, a train-frozen parser must specify:

- latest eligible pre-cutoff report selected by `start time`; 
- maximum lesion diameter in centimeters from explicit lesion-linked dimensions, multiplicity (single/multiple), and smooth versus irregular margin/capsule;
- modality from `Examination`, with report identity by `Examination Number`;
- rejection of pathology, postoperative, MVI, or ambiguous multi-lesion contexts.

A blinded hepatobiliary/radiology review must sample at least 200 reports across years/modalities and all parser-positive categories. Require ≥95% leakage rejection; size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in ≥90%; and ≥90% agreement for multiplicity and margin. Report coverage and missingness by split/outcome. Until these pass, report-free S/V2/D2/T2 is primary, and conclusions must say “beyond latest routine labs and measured demographics,” not “beyond tumor burden.” If validated, add the same tumor fields to every arm and report the reduced AFP/size/margin benchmark.

Severity rivals are tested through latest liver reserve/inflammation/hematology values, demographics, service, and observation-process covariates. Association surviving adjustment is not mechanistic proof.

## Outcome parser and template falsification

Apply the exact grade regex only within HCC `examination findings`/`examination diagnosis` rows in the same patient/encounter as index resection. Require one unique grade. Primary pathology review samples 50 per grade plus every biopsy-only case and a stratified sample of the 534 lexical discordance flags across 2016–2020 and 2021. Review must verify HCC, resection specimen/index correspondence, grade, whether generic vascular-invasion fields refer to the same specimen/template section, and adequacy limits. Automated computation can check parser states; only blinded hepatopathology can resolve discordance.

Primary model results are repeated after excluding every lexical discordance flag. A material metric or calibration change, or <95% overall/<90% per grade-era parser agreement, makes outcome interpretation inconclusive. The 2021 stress set is reported separately and never pooled to rescue or reverse the 2020 primary conclusion.

## Estimands, analysis, and falsification

The primary contrast is V2 minus S-latest on identical 2020 patients, averaged over 20 locked subsampling replicates. Report paired AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, log loss, calibration intercept/slope, and decision net benefit at risks 0.20/0.30/0.40/0.50, with 2,000 patient bootstraps nesting the replicate average. Report 2019 validation and 2021 stress results, subgroup calibration when ≥20 events/cell, observation-count strata, and distribution of replicate effects.

Support requires 2020 V2 improvement of ≥0.02 AUROC and ≥0.01 Brier, both paired 95% intervals excluding zero, calibration slope 0.8–1.2, net-benefit gain at two adjacent thresholds without material harm elsewhere, no direction reversal in 2019, and a positive effect in at least 16/20 fixed replicates. It also requires V2 to improve over O-process, remain after discordance exclusion, and retain at least half its Brier gain after adding validated tumor burden if that parser passes.

A learned two-state claim additionally requires D2 or T2 to beat V2 in both AUROC and Brier with intervals excluding zero; an order-specific claim requires T2 to beat D2 and swapped-order T2 with ≥50% attenuation of its Brier gain.

Adverse evidence is precise exclusion of the V2 thresholds; gain only in V-all/F-all/T-full; O-process matching V2; instability across subsamples; disappearance after outcome-discordance exclusion or validated tumor burden; or reversal in 2019. These results park the biological-trajectory branch and may retain only an operational workup-intensity predictor.

Inconclusive evidence includes broad intervals, poor calibration, inadequate paired-label support after exact-label capping, failed tumor/pathology adjudication, or stable 2020 performance with major 2021 stress failure. The last indicates template dependence and blocks deployment but does not negate 2020 internal prediction.

Mandatory controls are outcome permutation, day −7 cutoff, latest-day-only input, report-free primary, exact-label-only processing, same-day median collapse, any-prior-recorded-treatment exclusion, 2014–2015 era stress, leave-one-training-year-out fits, feature-time leakage tests, and observation-only/value-masked controls.

## Deliverable, alternatives, compute, and verification

The scientific deliverable is newly fitted S-latest, O-process, 20-replicate V2, D2/T2, and exploratory all-observation models with locked 2019/2020/2021 predictions; parser/adjudication manifests; paired metrics; and a `claims.json` linking each conclusion to computed outputs and the rules above. The unrestricted V/F/T ladder is retained only as an exploratory process-sensitive contrast. GP/state-space models are deferred because two-day median support and missing units do not identify latent biology. Revisit a mechanistic trajectory study only with scheduled serial assays, stable units, validated imaging, and pathology sampling metadata.

The verifier can check source hashes/headers, joins, clocks, parser states, split isolation, one-value-per-label/day caps, fixed seeds, matched patients, nested bootstrap arithmetic, metrics, and whether conclusions obey supportive/adverse/inconclusive rules.
