# Working proposal (episode 26): Is the in-vitro MERTK macrophage program the clinical remission program?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis]
(valid, board leader mu 32.14). Seed: D3-M2 lineage, extended to the D3-M3 clinical anchor.
Status: hypothesis-stage, data-bound; ONE bounded executed screen this episode; no causal claim.

## 1. Unresolved question
Alivernini 2020 [K1] associates synovial MerTK+CD206+ macrophages with drug-free RA remission,
and the parent established an in-vitro, donor-matched MERTK-linked macrophage perturbation signal
(E-MTAB-8873 co-culture; E-MTAB-8316 monocytes +/- UNC106238). But the parent's recorded residual
limitation is that E-MTAB-8322 was used "cross-sectional anchor only": it was never asked whether
the in-vitro MERTK perturbation program is *concordant* with the primary synovial-macrophage state
that separates drug-free remission from treatment-resistant RA. That translatability step is the
unresolved question.

Leading claim H1 (translatable within-cell program): the in-vitro macrophage MERTK-perturbation
direction is concordant with the remission-vs-resistant synovial macrophage direction.
Strongest rival H0 (context-specific): the in-vitro co-culture program does not reproduce the
primary remission axis (a culture/context artifact).
Second rival H2 (composition): any remission-vs-resistant difference is explained by
cell-abundance/composition structure (Zhang 2023 CTAPs [K3]), not a within-cell program.
Third rival H3 (signature scope): the curated receptor/ligand/effector gene set is too small to
decide concordance; a genome-wide signature is required.

## 2. Clinical importance
If remission macrophages carry the same program that an MERTK-directed perturbation produces
in vitro, then the co-culture axis is a faithful proxy for a clinically actionable state and
MERTK-directed strategies can be prioritized. If not, co-culture results cannot be extrapolated
to remission biology and the field must test the primary state directly. Either outcome changes
the next decision (which model system and which target to carry forward).

## 4. Executed bounded screen (this episode)
`work/lead-e26/screen_bridge.py` read-only-normalizes (log2 CPM+1) and computes:
in-vitro direction = mean over the 6 pairs of [log2CPM(UNC106238) - log2CPM(none)];
in-vivo direction = mean log2CPM(remission) - mean log2CPM(resistant); then a gene-module
concordance (Pearson r + sign agreement) over 18 shared signature genes.
RESULT (see screen_bridge_result.json): n_pairs=6; concordance(in-vitro vs remission-minus-resistant)
r = -0.343, sign agreement = 0.278; vs remission-minus-naive r = -0.42, sign agreement = 0.167.
No signature gene missing from E-MTAB-8322. This is a small, curated-gene, cross-sectional screen:
it returns an ADVERSE/context-specific signal (no concordance) and cannot by itself refute H1.

## 5. Baseline vs substantive alternative (method exploration)
- Baseline: the curated 18-gene module score and a per-gene remission-vs-resistant difference
  (the screen executed here).
- Substantive alternative (proposed for the solver): derive a WHOLE-TRANSCRIPTOME
  UNC106238-response signature in E-MTAB-8316 (donor-paired moderated statistics), then project it
  onto E-MTAB-8322 with a donor-aware mixed model (condition fixed, donor random) plus a rank-1..3
  low-rank factor model, evaluated leave-one-donor-out. This alternative reports whether the
  remission axis loads on a single latent factor aligned with the in-vitro perturbation axis, which
  the baseline's 18-gene score cannot reveal, and it separates H0 (context-specific) from H1
  (translatable) from H2 (composition/abundance) far better than a small module score.
- Selection reason: this episode demonstrates feasibility and directionality with the bounded
  screen; the full signature projection is the solver deliverable, not claimed here.

## 6. Falsification / outcomes
- Supportive: genome-wide in-vitro signature projected on E-MTAB-8322 gives a remission-vs-resistant
  effect of consistent sign across donor-aware fits (|r|>=0.5, sign agreement >=0.7, factor loading
  stable leave-one-donor-out) and survives adjustment for macrophage abundance/composition.
- Adverse: no concordance or opposite sign (as the bounded screen hints) -> H0 favored; the co-culture
  axis is context-specific and should not be extrapolated to remission biology.
- Inconclusive: unstable across donors / 18-gene-only evidence -> H3; increase signature scope and
  donors before deciding. No claim of a transition (cross-sectional groups) is made.

## 7. Honest limitations
n=6 in-vitro pairs across 4 FLS donors; E-MTAB-8322 is cross-sectional pseudobulk macrophages only
(not whole tissue, no transition); SA149P/UPASA149 excluded; no protein-level target engagement and
UNC106238 selectivity assumed, not proven; key works [K1]-[K3] inspected at abstract level only.
These require expert review / wet-lab (not automatic verification).
