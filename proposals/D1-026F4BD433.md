# An independently anchored departure-status ledger for copied, tentative, completed, and superseded recommendations

## Decision question and targeted validity repair

The frozen decision remains whether any exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated only for a prospective **within-site silent validation** of the inherited `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes. This child preserves the first-eligible all-opportunity population, reciprocal pre-departure opportunity chain, canonical discharge artifact, exhaustive `Y,V,H,Z` states, exact support atoms, all-unit worst-case admission routing, fixed positive-probability at-most-900-admission design, adverse-compatible finite-population inference, and strict noncausal ceiling of parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`.

The parents establish independent textual reproducibility and route-concealed actionability/supersession measurements. Their consequential residual gap is **reference validity**. Panels can agree that a sentence is an active plan while it is copied boilerplate, merely tentative, documents an action already performed, or is superseded elsewhere in the same available admission artifacts. More panel agreement does not make the recommendation a clinically live obligation.

Available MIMIC evidence can support a narrower statement: an admission contains an explicit, target-grounded, future-directed textual action that is not contradicted by an explicit target-linked completion, cancellation, or replacement in the canonical discharge and admission-linked pre-departure radiology artifacts. MIMIC cannot prove that the action was clinically appropriate, still required at physical departure, visible, assigned, communicated, ordered, scheduled, executed, beneficial, or safe. The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is that at least one inherited support-qualified `AUTO` or `RECON` atom remains eligible after an independently rendered and independently adjudicated **departure-status ledger** assigns every challenged unit an adverse-compatible status and after high-specificity source anchors and copy-provenance controls pass. Failure excludes the affected atom; absence of identifying evidence is `UNKNOWN/DEFER`, not a favorable reference label.

A read-only lexical feasibility profile was run before proposal publication, solely to verify that the proposed reference threats occur in available fields. Among those nonrandom rows, 7,461 discharge texts contained a broad pulmonary/lung-nodule phrase; their normalized target/follow-up sentences included 7,492 occurrences belonging to fingerprints repeated at least twice. ## Presealed provenance ledger: objective facts, not clinical labels

Before any A1/A2/S1/H3 or new reference label is revealed, tokenize and hash every candidate recommendation span and its DS sentence using a frozen normalizer that changes only Unicode form, case, whitespace, punctuation spacing, and deidentification placeholders. Measurements, anatomy, laterality, modality, timing, negation, and modal verbs are preserved. Store no source text in public outputs. For each unit record:

1. exact and token-longest-common-subsequence match to its own reciprocal radiology recommendation span;
2. the number of distinct admissions and subjects containing the exact normalized DS sentence; `RECURRENT_5` means at least five distinct subjects;
3. whether the DS span has a target-specific grounding token matching the source chain: anatomy/laterality, measurement, modality, comparison, or explicitly linked interval;
4. DS section coordinates from a frozen section parser, with `SECTION_UNKNOWN` when headings are absent or ambiguous;
5. every same-admission radiology report after the source recommendation but before `D` and stored before `D`, with timestamps and target/action candidate links; and
6. every target-linked firm, tentative, completion, cancellation, replacement, and contradiction candidate span anywhere in the canonical DS, including spans outside the apparent follow-up section.

`RECURRENT_5`, source-copy similarity, or a section heading is never itself a favorable or adverse clinical label. Recurrent wording may be an appropriate template; verbatim source propagation may be faithful. These flags calibrate whether reviewers rely on generic language without target grounding and make copying visible rather than assuming it away.

## Independent departure-status reference packets and labels

Every admission in the inherited 300-admission actionability challenge receives this layer; no new admission is sampled. If the inherited challenge uses fewer than 300, use all of it and report the limitation. There are exactly two additional compact whole-admission reference readings per challenged admission, at most 600 readings total. Exceeding 600, roster insufficiency, or inability to include every unit is `REFERENCE_INFEASIBLE`; no atom passes. Packet creation, rosters, codebooks, parser, hashes, and order are frozen before labels.

Reference panels R1 and R2 are roster-disjoint from each other and from all inherited index, audit, A1/A2/S1/H1/H2/H3 panels. They receive independent pseudonyms, orderings, and independently written codebooks with no shared examples or training cases. Neither sees atoms, strata, weights, routes, inherited labels, other-panel labels, or expected control answers. R1 receives an **assertion-centered view**: each source recommendation, all DS target/action spans in original local context, section coordinates, and explicit timestamps. R2 receives a **trajectory-centered view**: the chronological reciprocal chain, all other admission-linked pre-`D`/pre-storage radiology reports, and the entire canonical DS with candidate spans unhighlighted. Both views display missingness and distinguish `charttime`, `storetime`, and `D`; neither implies that storage equals visibility.

For each inherited recommendation unit, each panel independently records an atomic tuple:

* target identity and whether a DS action is target-grounded (`EXACT`, `COMPATIBLE`, `MISMATCH`, `UNKNOWN`);
* force: `FIRM_FUTURE`, `TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `INFORMATIONAL`, `NEGATED_OR_CANCELLED`, `CONFLICT`, `UNKNOWN`;
* action, interval/timing, named recipient, and responsibility wording, each as observed/absent/contradicted/unknown;
* documented action status: `NOT_DOCUMENTED_COMPLETED`, `MATCHING_ACTION_PERFORMED_PRE_D`, `PERFORMED_BUT_NEXT_ACTION_REMAINS`, `COMPLETION_AMBIGUOUS`, `UNKNOWN`;
* replacement status: `NONE_EXPLICIT`, `EXPLICIT_REPLACEMENT`, `EXPLICIT_CHANGED_INTERVAL`, `COMPETING_PLANS`, `UNKNOWN`;
* provenance: `CASE_GROUNDED`, `RECURRENT_BUT_GROUNDED`, `RECURRENT_UNGROUNDED`, `SOURCE_COPY_GROUNDED`, `SOURCE_COPY_UNGROUNDED`, `PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN`; and
* one compatible set over `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`, `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, `R_TENTATIVE_ONLY`, `R_COMPLETED_NO_REMAINING_ACTION`, `R_SUPERSEDED`, `R_INACTIVE_INFORMATIONAL`, `R_CONFLICT`, and `R_UNKNOWN`.

`R_FIRM_ACTIVE` requires an explicit target-grounded future action, firm rather than solely tentative language, and no explicit matching completion without a remaining future action, cancellation, replacement, or competing plan in the supplied artifacts. It does not require that a clinician should act. `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE` requires a target-grounded firm future action but incomplete timing/recipient/responsibility or unresolved plan detail. A recommendation copied from radiology can enter either firm state only if target grounding is independently supported; recurrent ungrouded text cannot. `TENTATIVE_ONLY`, completion without an explicit remaining action, supersession, inactivity, conflict, and unknown are never converted to active by consensus.

No senior consensus collapses R1/R2. The primary reference compatible set is their union plus every state compatible with missing/corrupt source evidence. Favorable intersection is reported only diagnostically. R1/R2 are measurements, not a gold standard.

## High-specificity anchors and falsification controls

The following anchors use observable source relations and are deliberately narrower than clinical truth:

* **explicit negative anchor:** a target-linked cancellation/no-further-action statement, or an explicit replacement relation naming old and new action/interval, present verbatim in the DS;
* **documented completion anchor:** a later target-linked pre-`D`, pre-storage radiology report documents the matching recommended imaging action, and the DS contains no explicit next future action; this anchors documented completion, not clinical resolution;
* **tentative-only anchor:** every target-linked action clause uses only conditional/optional language and no firm future clause is present anywhere in the permitted artifacts;
* **firm textual anchor:** a dedicated plan/instruction span contains target, future action, firm modal/imperative, and timing or recipient, is target-grounded to the source chain, and no permitted artifact contains an explicit completion, cancellation, replacement, or contradiction; and
* **copy-provenance anchor:** exact cross-subject recurrence or exact source-to-DS copying as defined above; it anchors textual provenance only.

R1/R2 must classify anchor components without seeing anchor names. Locked controls are inserted into the reference packet queue before review: a copied firm sentence with target grounding preserved; the same sentence with target attributes swapped to an incompatible target; a firm-to-tentative modal change; an inserted explicit completion; an inserted explicit replacement; and a section/format-only perturbation. The copied grounded sentence must retain textual status, the target swap must lose grounding, firm-to-tentative must change force only, completion/replacement must change status only, and formatting must change nothing. Controls do not rescue failure on real cases and do not establish clinical correctness.

Use inherited sampling weights and joint admission clusters. The reference layer is conditional on inherited actionability-challenge inclusion and reports those conditional probabilities; it does not masquerade as a new simple random sample. Compute exact finite-population inversion for binary outcomes and 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates, retaining every unit and panel label from an admission together. Extend the single inherited max-statistic simultaneous 95% family to these estimands and controls. Lower bounds count only jointly observed favorable support. A missing anchor class is reported as not calibratable; it neither fails nor passes that class, but if the corresponding adverse state remains possible for route candidates, bounds remain wide and prevent nomination.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

A supportive result requires all inherited and new atom-specific gates and at least one fully supported exact atom. It supports only this statement: for the available MIMIC textual artifacts, the atom's route is reproducible and its candidate action units are explicitly target-grounded, firm and future-directed, without observed target-linked completion, cancellation, replacement, or contradiction under adverse-compatible reference review. That is sufficient only to nominate a prospective within-site **silent** workflow study; all unlisted signatures remain `DEFER`.

An adverse result is an atom-specific excess of tentative-only, completed, superseded, inactive, recurrent-ungrounded, mismatched, conflicting, or unknown units; a reference/actionability discordance bound crossing a gate; or an anchor/control falsification. It excludes that atom from nomination but does not prove clinical harm, negligence, or that the recommendation was inappropriate.

An inconclusive result includes sparse route or anchor classes, inability to inspect every unit, infeasible 600-reading workload, nonresponse, broken seals, ambiguous target matching, missing pre-`D` evidence, or wide compatible bounds. Inconclusive is not a pass and defaults to `DEFER`.

Even a perfect result cannot establish that a recommendation was a **clinically live departure obligation**. MIMIC lacks actual viewing, edit/sign/finalization semantics, responsibility assignment, conversations, patient preferences, external records, target-linked orders/referrals, scheduling, execution, appropriateness, malignancy, benefit, harm, or forward/external transport. The required next study is a separately approved actual-calendar prospective silent bridge capturing those workflow and clinical facts. Only expert-governed prospective evidence could support live use, safety, utility, or causal benefit.

## Targeted child repair: a closed nested ledger and workload contract

The parent is scientifically valid but its reference layer still leaves an implementation ambiguity: it specifies two readings and rich labels, yet does not make the nested inclusion ledger, per-admission task accounting, nonresponse policy, and compatible-world calculation a single executable contract. This child closes that gap without changing any frozen population, opportunity, state, atom, route, sampling, or safety rule above.

### Pre-label objects and an auditable workload ledger

The compiler first materializes a read-only `challenge_frame` from the inherited actionability-challenge draw. It contains exactly one row per distinct `(subject_id,hadm_id)` and the frozen inherited admission inclusion probability `piA_i` and pair probability `piA_ij`; duplicate note rows, subjects, or recommendation units never create another admission. The frame is sealed before either panel sees any text. Let `U_i` be the complete frozen recommendation-unit list for admission `i`, including every unit even when its route is later `DEFER`, and let `q_i=|U_i|`. No panel may omit a unit, split a unit differently, or request a new one.

Exactly two compact whole-admission readings are commissioned for each frame row: one R1 assertion-centered packet and one R2 trajectory-centered packet. Thus `N_read=2*n_challenge`, `n_challenge<=300`, and `N_read<=600`; there is no repeat, top-up, replacement, senior adjudication, tie-break, or third reading. A reading is one admission packet, not one sentence. The complete task ledger is sealed before labels:

* packet tasks: `2*n_challenge` (one packet per panel and admission);
* admission-status tasks: `2*n_challenge` (one status tuple per panel and admission);
* unit-status tasks: `2*sum_i(q_i)` (one tuple for every frozen unit in each panel);
* anchor/control tasks: a fixed, pre-counted set `C` inserted before queue randomization, with `C` reported separately and never used to rescue a real unit.

The declared workload is therefore `W=(N_read, 2*n_challenge, 2*sum(q_i), C)`. Before labels, the executor checks `n_challenge<=300`, `N_read<=600`, `sum(q_i)` against the declared atomic-task capacity `B_unit`, all packets render, and both panels have enough roster-disjoint reviewers. `B_unit` is a fixed protocol constant set before sampling and recorded in the manifest; it is not increased after seeing labels. If any check fails, if any unit cannot be rendered, or if a reviewer is unavailable, the run is `REFERENCE_INFEASIBLE`, every affected atom is `DEFER`, and no unit is silently dropped. This makes workload closure computable while preserving the parent's all-unit worst-case rule. The executor reports packet-hours and atomic tasks separately, rather than hiding unit burden inside a reading count.

There is no hidden work: parser execution, source hashing, packet generation, random ordering, and seal verification are machine tasks recorded in `C`; only the two prespecified panel readings and their atomic tuples are human reference work. Re-opening a packet, discussing a disagreement, or looking at the other panel is a protocol violation and invalidates the affected run. The reference layer is conditional on the inherited challenge, not a new population or a post-label enrichment.

### Nested inclusion probabilities and estimands

For every inherited draw, persist the exact realized first-order `piA_i=P(I_i=1)` and second-order `piA_ij=P(I_i=I_j=1)` supplied by the frozen inherited design, along with draw seed, stratum, sentinel status, and route-blind challenge indicator. The reference indicator is nested: `R_i=1` only if `I_i=1` and `i` is in the predeclared challenge frame. Its conditional probability is `piRgivenA_i=P(R_i=1|I_i=1)`, equal to 1 for the inherited 300-case challenge census and otherwise the frozen pre-label challenge-draw probability; it is never estimated from labels. Consequently the full-population Horvitz--Thompson weight is `1/(piA_i*piRgivenA_i)` for a reference-observed admission, while a challenge-frame conditional estimate uses `1/piRgivenA_i` and the inherited challenge-frame denominator. Never multiply by a second factor when the challenge is a census. Pair weights are the corresponding `1/(piA_ij*piRgivenA_ij)` (or the conditional pair probability), and all panel/unit observations from an admission remain one joint cluster in every variance replicate.

The executor writes a probability table before labels containing `(subject_id,hadm_id,stratum,I_i,R_i,piA_i,piA_ij,piRgivenA_i,piRgivenA_ij,weight,weight_pair)`. A zero or missing probability, a duplicate admission key, a probability inconsistent with the frozen draw, or a reference frame row absent from the inherited draw is a hard invalidation, not an imputation. Sampling is never altered in response to route, atom, text, packet failure, or reviewer label.

### Compatible-world computation and missingness closure

Each panel's tuple is retained as raw structured output with explicit `OBSERVED`, `ABSENT`, `CONTRADICTED`, or `UNKNOWN` for target, force, action, interval, recipient, responsibility, completion, replacement, provenance, and status. The parser and reviewer roster do not create a favorable value. For each unit `u`, construct `S_iu`, the union of every reference state compatible with both panel tuples and all missing/corrupt evidence. If a panel does not respond, its entire tuple is `UNKNOWN`; if one field is unreadable, only states compatible with that field's missingness are retained, but the missingness indicator itself is adverse in the release gate. The state `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` is in `S_iu` only when the explicit target, firm future action, and no-negative-anchor requirements are satisfied in the supplied artifacts; it is never inferred from agreement, recurrence, source copying, section, or a missing field.

For each atom `a` and inherited route `r`, enumerate the finite Cartesian product `S_a = product_{i,u in a} S_iu`, without assigning probabilities to worlds. For each world `w`, derive the admission route by the frozen worst-unit precedence and derive every binary gate indicator. Compute lower and upper design-weighted bounds by minimizing and maximizing the same estimator over `S_a`, with panel nonresponse, parser failure, packet corruption, and ambiguous target links included in the adverse states. The implementation may use a linear program only after encoding the finite state indicators and admission-level worst-unit constraints; an LP relaxation is prohibited unless its integrality and exactness are verified on fixtures. Unit worlds are coupled within admission, so no independent-unit factorization is permitted. If the world set or design-weighted optimization cannot be exhaustively represented, return `INCONCLUSIVE/DEFER`; do not sample worlds, use favorable imputation, or invoke a latent class model.

Reference-firm-active mass, firm-incomplete mass, each adverse-state mass, provenance mass, actionability discordance, anchor recognition, control violations, response/nonresponse, and workload are all reported with their compatible lower and upper bounds. The primary bounds are calculated twice: (1) conditional on the inherited challenge frame, and (2) lifted to the frozen all-opportunity population using the nested weights above. These are distinct estimands and may not be substituted for one another. The finite-population inversion and 20,000 Rao--Wu replicates are applied to the same joint admission clusters and inherited simultaneous max-statistic family; no panel-level pseudo-replication is allowed.

### Feasibility, roster, and atom gates

Before packet release, the roster manifest proves pairwise disjointness of R1, R2, and every inherited panel by immutable reviewer pseudonym, and records eligibility, assignment, queue order, and backup status. A backup may replace a non-starting reviewer only before that reviewer's first packet and only through a presealed deterministic assignment; any overlap or post-label substitution invalidates the run. Nonresponse is reported separately for packets, admissions, and units, and all nonresponse remains compatible with adverse status. A panel completion rate does not rescue another panel or an atom.

The parent gates are applied separately to every exact `(possible-calendar class, terminal curr_service, workflow class, route, atom)` with occupied support. For `AUTO`, every adverse-compatible unit must be `R_FIRM_ACTIVE`; the upper inherited-actionability/reference-discordance bound is at most 0.05, recurrent-ungrounded or target-mismatched at most 0.02, each occupied explicit-negative/completion/tentative anchor recognition lower bound at least 0.95, invariant-control violation at most 0.02, each decisive-control violation at most 0.05, reference missingness/nonresponse below 0.05 unless censused, and the inherited minimum challenge counts and effective sample size must pass. For `RECON`, every unit must be compatible with `R_FIRM_ACTIVE` or `R_FIRM_INCOMPLETE`, the corresponding discordance upper bound is at most 0.10, adverse-state upper mass at most 0.10, and all inherited support, event, weight, control, workload, and effective-sample gates pass. `TIMING` retains its inherited chronology precedence but fails on any non-active compatible state. `DEFER` is the output for every atom with insufficient support, infeasible workload, missing probability, sparse anchor class, broken seal, nonresponse beyond the gate, unresolved world bounds, or any failed gate.

These are conjunctive atom gates. A pooled estimate, neighboring atom, service grouping, favorable panel majority, reviewer kappa, recurrent text, source-copy flag, negative-control result, shrinkage, model, or prospective utility argument cannot rescue a failed or unsupported atom. A missing anchor class is `NOT_CALIBRATABLE`, not zero; if that missing class can contain an adverse route state, the atom cannot pass. Any single adverse-compatible unit controls its admission, and any adverse-compatible admission controls the atom's worst-unit route.

### Noncircular temporal boundary and deliberate omission of post-departure rescue

A longitudinal challenge is included only as a negative-control check on clock handling, not as evidence of a live obligation. Before labels, the executor creates a fixed `POST_D_EXCLUSION` fixture consisting of admission-linked radiology rows with `charttime>=D` or `storetime>=D`, plus rows with missing times. These rows are route-blind and withheld from both reference views. The fixture must never alter an opportunity, unit, `R_store,u`, label, or gate numerator. A separate deterministic parser test verifies that adding the fixture to a packet cannot change any presealed pre-`D` ledger field; if it does, the run fails. This is a bounded temporal negative control for leakage, not a claim about later care. No later report is used to rescue a completion, confirm a plan, or improve a route. The positive reference evidence remains restricted to admission-linked radiology with `charttime<D` and `storetime<D`, and the canonical DS as already frozen.

### Interpretation and checkable closure

Supportive results require the parent's full inherited gates plus this ledger's exact workload, roster, probability, compatible-world, and seal checks, and at least one fully supported exact atom. They support only nomination for a prospective within-site silent workflow validation of text-defined reproducibility. Adverse results exclude the affected atom and identify whether the failure was a firm-active contradiction, adverse reference state, missingness, anchor/control failure, nested-weight failure, or workload/roster infeasibility; they do not prove harm, negligence, inappropriateness, or clinical nonbenefit. Inconclusive results include any unresolved compatible world, sparse support, absent anchor class, nonresponse, infeasible task cap, invalid nested probability, or temporal-boundary leakage and default to `DEFER`.

Computationally checkable claims are row/key uniqueness, source and schema hashes, exact `D`, pre-`D` clock filtering, complete unit inclusion, the two-packet/600-reading cap, task ledger arithmetic, roster disjointness, inclusion probabilities, finite-world bound optimization, route precedence, and gate evaluation. Clinical liveness, actual viewing, assignment, communication, responsibility, order/referral, execution, appropriateness, patient preference, malignancy, safety, benefit, and calendar/external transport remain unavailable in MIMIC and require clinical adjudication or a prospective actual-calendar study. The temporal fixture can establish only absence of parser leakage under the frozen clock contract, never prospective truth.
