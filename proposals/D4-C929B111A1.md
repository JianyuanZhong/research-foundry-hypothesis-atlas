# Is the creatinine/cystatin-C eGFR discordance kidney-specific filtration information, or a non-GFR body-composition/inflammation composite wearing a kidney label?

Branch: EGFRDIFF (episode 2, `population_multiomics`, UK Biobank). Namespace: **`ukb672073`, join key `eid`, only.**
No join to `olink_dta` (`n_eid`) or `ukb671626`. Status markers: `[verified]` computed from frozen bytes in this

## 1. The opening, and the evidence for it

In one blood draw, UK Biobank measured serum creatinine (30700-0.0) and cystatin C (30720-0.0) in
**468,887** participants. The two race-free CKD-EPI 2021 estimates of *the same* quantity agree poorly:
corr(eGFRcr, eGFRcys) = **0.604**, mean eGFRdiff (cys - cr) = **-6.42**, SD **13.36** mL/min/1.73 m2, and
**47.5%** of participants differ by more than 10 mL/min/1.73 m2 `[verified by Lead at 09:12 UTC today from the
frozen parquet, work/lead/lead_check_egfr.json; independently consistent with K3's published 88+/-16 vs 95+/-13
and 25%/5% tails]`. This is not a rare edge case: **16,918** people have eGFRcr >= 60 but eGFRcys < 60, **2,775**
cross below 45, **432** cross below 30 (the metformin contraindication threshold), and **5,637** have
Cockcroft-Gault CrCl >= 50 mL/min while eGFRcys < 50 `[verified for the 60/30 crossings; the 45 and
Cockcroft-Gault counts are worker-computed]`. So a drug-dosing or referral decision flips, in tens of thousands
of people, on which assay is believed.

**Already established and NOT retested here.** That the discordance predicts death and adverse outcomes
[K2]; that its cross-sectional correlates in this very snapshot include thyroid disease, chronic inflammatory
disease, steroid use, body fat and smoking [K3]; and that KDIGO recommends a confirmatory cystatin C when an
eGFR-based clinical decision matters. We take all of this as given.

**The unresolved claim under test.** When eGFRcys falls well below eGFRcr in the same draw, *what is that
signal*?
- **(A) Kidney-specific filtration information.** eGFRcys is closer to true GFR; eGFRcr is inflated by low
  muscle mass. Correct action: believe the lower number - nephrology referral, renal dose reduction,
  avoid nephrotoxins.
- **(B) A non-GFR composite** of low muscle mass, inflammation, adiposity and poor nutrition, i.e. a general
  physiologic-reserve marker mislabelled as kidney function. Correct action: assess reserve/frailty; the renal
  dose reduction is *wrong* and may underdose.

K2 reports both an ESKD and a mortality association but never contrasts them; notably its mortality estimate
is 2-4x its ESKD estimate, the ordering (B) predicts, which K2 does not interpret. K3 shows non-GFR predictors
but has no outcomes at all. Neither work poses the specificity question. That gap is the opening.

**Observation that separates them.** (A) implies the discordance is *organ-anchored*: per-SD eGFRdiff should
predict kidney-anchored events at least as strongly as, and ideally more strongly than, events with no
filtration pathway. (B) implies the opposite ordering: it should predict frailty-anchored and all-cause
endpoints at least as strongly as kidney endpoints, because the information is about the body, not the nephron.
Both predict a non-zero association with everything - which is why the *contrast*, not the association, is the
estimand.

## 2. Primary estimand (prespecified, one number)

For each endpoint *k*, fit a Cox model on post-landmark time with covariates
{z(eGFRdiff), eGFRcr-cys level, age, sex} and take beta_k = per-SD log-HR for z(eGFRdiff), signed so that
*lower* eGFRdiff (cys below cr) is the risk direction.

> **DELTA = mean(beta_k over KIDNEY set) - mean(beta_k over NON-KIDNEY set)**, with a 95% interval from
> **2,000 participant-level bootstrap replicates** (resampling people, refitting all endpoints per replicate,
> so the kidney and non-kidney estimates stay correlated and the interval is on the difference itself).

Conditioning on the combined level eGFRcr-cys (not on either single marker) makes DELTA a contrast *at a given
level of estimated filtration*, which is exactly the clinical situation: two people with the same best-available
eGFR, one concordant and one discordant. Conditioning on a level changes the estimand and is stated as such,
not treated as proof of mechanism.

**Bands (prespecified).** SD(eGFRdiff) = 13.36, so 1 SD ~ 13.4 mL/min/1.73 m2, comparable to K2's 15-unit cut.
- **Supportive of (A) / kidney-specific:** DELTA >= +0.15 and the lower bound of its 95% CI > 0.
- **Supportive of (B) / non-GFR composite:** DELTA <= -0.15 and the upper bound of its 95% CI < 0.
- **Adverse to (A) specifically:** mean non-kidney beta >= 0.10 with CI excluding 0 while the kidney mean CI
  includes 0.
- **Inconclusive:** |DELTA| < 0.15, or a CI half-width > 0.15 (i.e. the data cannot resolve a 15% per-SD
  hazard-ratio difference). An imprecise null is inconclusive, **not** refutation of either reading.
0.15 on the log-HR scale is ~16% per SD: smaller than that would not change a referral or dosing decision.

## 3. Outcome sets (written down before the counting pass was run; see `feas_outcomes.py`)

**Kidney-anchored** (primary set): `AKI_N17` (ICD-10 N17); `KRT_kidney_failure` (N18.5, N19, Z49, Z94.0, Z99.2).
**Non-kidney, frailty-anchored** (the *sharpest* test, because neither has a filtration pathway from CKD):
`infection_hosp` (A40, A41, J12-J18, N39.0); `falls_fracture` (W00-W19, S72.0-.2, S32, S22, M80).
**Non-kidney, other:** `heart_failure` (I50, I11.0, I13.0, I13.2); `cancer_malignant_incident` (registry
40005-i.0 dates with 40006-i.0 codes C*, excluding C44); `all_cause_death` (40000-0.0).
**Secondary, excluded from the primary contrast:** `CKD_N18` (its coding is partly *definitional* in creatinine,
so it cannot arbitrate), `MI`, `stroke`.

What each non-kidney endpoint contributes: **falls/fracture** is the cleanest - sarcopenia and frailty cause it,
reduced GFR essentially does not, so a strong eGFRdiff-fracture association at a fixed eGFR level is hard for (A)
to absorb. **Infection** is intermediate: CKD is immunosuppressive, so it is partly downstream of filtration, but
cystatin C is also directly raised by inflammation, which makes it the endpoint where (B) and reverse-causation
overlap most - hence the landmark. **Heart failure** and **death** are the *weakest* specificity tests (both are
genuinely downstream of CKD; K2's companion paper found an eGFRdiff-HF association in CKD) and are reported but
down-weighted in interpretation: the primary DELTA is computed with equal weights across the four non-kidney
endpoints, and a prespecified sensitivity restricts the non-kidney set to {falls_fracture, infection_hosp,
cancer_malignant_incident}, i.e. drops heart failure and death.

**Incident albuminuria is NOT available** as a longitudinal endpoint: urine microalbumin 30500 and creatinine
30510 exist at instance 0 only (n with both ~143,412, 16.7% >= 3 mg/mmol `[worker-computed]`), with no repeat
uACR. It is therefore demoted to a cross-sectional concordance check, not a kidney endpoint. This is an honest
loss relative to the Lead's candidate list.

## 4. Population, temporal boundaries, exclusions

Population: all `ukb672073` participants with non-missing creatinine and cystatin C at instance 0
(**n = 468,887** `[verified]`), from 502,370 rows.

Time zero = baseline assessment date **53-0.0**. **Landmark at 2.0 years**: follow-up starts at
53-0.0 + 730.5 d and anyone with the endpoint, a kidney event, or death on or before the landmark is excluded.
Justification: cystatin C is acutely raised by inflammation and by occult illness, so a low eGFRcys can be a
*marker of disease already present*; a 2-year landmark removes the window in which that reverse-causation
pathway dominates while keeping ~10 years of follow-up. 2 y (not 1 or 5) because it exceeds the typical
resolution time of an acute inflammatory episode and the usual diagnostic interval for occult malignancy,
while costing only ~17% of person-time.

**Administrative censoring is asymmetric and is handled by two clocks, not averaged:** hospital episodes
(41280) end **2022-10-31**; death registry (40000) runs to **2022-12-19**. HES endpoints use the HES clock and
are additionally censored at death (competing risk); all-cause death uses the death clock. The script records
the *observed* maxima of both date fields so the assumed boundaries are checked, not assumed. The seven-week
mismatch is reported, never silently blended.

Exclusions, with counts to be reported from `feas_outcomes.json`: (i) kidney event (N18 / KRT / N17) on or
before the landmark - prevalent CKD or kidney failure must be out for incident kidney endpoints;
(ii) baseline eGFRcr-cys < 60; (iii) death on or before the landmark; (iv) for the cancer endpoint only,
malignancy on or before the landmark.

## 6. Baseline and the substantive alternative (same question, matched specificity)

**(a) Baseline - Cox/Poisson with the prespecified contrast.** Inputs: the table above. Fit: one Cox model per
endpoint, {z(eGFRdiff), eGFRcr-cys, age, sex}; DELTA plus 2,000 bootstrap replicates. Evaluation: DELTA and its
CI against the bands in section 2; per-endpoint log-HRs reported. Split: none needed (estimation, not
prediction); the composition arm is prespecified rather than selected. Budget: a single Cox fit on ~400k x 4-10
covariates is seconds; 2,000 bootstrap replicates x ~9 endpoints ~ 18,000 fits, **CPU, order 2-6 h on 8-16
cores**, embarrassingly parallel. **No GPU**: the design matrix is ~400k x 10 dense float64 (~32 MB) and the
bottleneck is many small Newton solves, not matrix throughput - GPU would add transfer overhead for no gain.
*What it loses:* it cannot say whether a small DELTA means "the discordance is genuinely non-specific" or
"the discordance is mostly measurement noise, which attenuates kidney and non-kidney coefficients unequally
because their event counts and follow-up differ".

**(b) Substantive alternative - latent-true-filtration measurement model.** Treat log(eGFRcr) and log(eGFRcys)
as two error-prone, differently-biased indicators of a latent filtration state F plus an assay-specific
non-target component: eGFRcr = a0 + a1*F + U_cr + e_cr, eGFRcys = b0 + b1*F + U_cys + e_cys, where e are
replication errors whose variances are **identified from the 16,546 instance-1 repeats** (ICC 0.869
creatinine, 0.885 cystatin C, 0.801 eGFRdiff `[worker-computed]`), and U are persistent non-GFR components.
Fit a two-stage structural model: measurement block on the repeat subsample, then a Cox outcome model in which
hazard depends on F-hat and on the residual assay-specific component, with multiple imputation of the latent
state propagating measurement uncertainty. **What it recovers that the baseline loses:** it separates
*attenuation by measurement error* from *attenuation by confounding/mediation*, and it splits the outcome
association into a part attributable to the latent filtration state and a part attributable to the
cystatin-C-specific non-target component - which is literally the (A) vs (B) contrast, at the level of the
estimand rather than the covariate list.
**Identifying assumption, bounded not asserted:** conditional independence of the two indicators' errors given
F (and given U). The repeat subsample **bounds** this rather than proving it: repeats taken at a different
visit conflate within-person biological drift with assay error, so the ICCs are *lower* bounds on reliability
and the implied error variances are *upper* bounds. We therefore report DELTA over a grid of assumed
reliabilities, from the observed ICC (maximum correction) to reliability 1.0 (no correction = the baseline), and
report the reliability value at which DELTA would cross each band. If no plausible reliability flips the
conclusion, the measurement-noise rival is defeated for this question; if a plausible one does, the direction
is declared unresolved. Fitting target: the latent measurement block by maximum likelihood on the 16,546
repeat pairs (minutes, CPU); the outcome stage as above. Split: measurement block fitted on the repeat
subsample, outcome stage on the full analytic set, so the two are not fitted on the same information.
Budget: **CPU, order 4-10 h**, no GPU, for the same reason.
**Selection:** run both. (a) is the primary estimand and must stand alone; (b) is the prespecified
sensitivity that makes "it is just noise" falsifiable. A nonlinear/neural outcome model was considered and
**deferred**, with the reason recorded: the scientific uncertainty here is about *what the exposure measures*,
not about functional form, and a flexible hazard model would improve discrimination without separating the two
readings. Revisit it only if DELTA proves strongly nonlinear in eGFRdiff (prespecified diagnostic: spline term
on z(eGFRdiff), reported, not used for selection).

## 7. Composition / inflammation / nutrition arm: mediator vs confounder, not mechanism

Covariates: 23099 body fat %, 21001 BMI, mean(46-0.0, 47-0.0) grip, log 30710 CRP, 30600 albumin,
20116 smoking. We report DELTA in the minimal model and in the +composition model, and the attenuation
fraction 1 - beta_plus/beta_min per endpoint. **Interpretation rules fixed in advance:**
- If composition adjustment is a **confounder** adjustment (the non-GFR traits cause both a high creatinine-based
  estimate and poor outcomes), adjustment moves DELTA toward (A).
- If it is a **mediator** adjustment (low muscle/inflammation is the pathway by which low filtration harms, or
  the pathway the discordance measures), adjustment removes signal that belongs to the exposure and biases
  DELTA toward (A) *artifactually*.
These are not distinguishable by fit. We therefore **do not** claim that an association surviving adjustment
identifies a mechanism; we report both estimands, name which is which under each reading, and note the
asymmetry that matters: **adjustment for noisy covariates under-adjusts**, so the "+composition" model is
biased toward "the kidney signal survives". A residual kidney-specific DELTA after adjustment is therefore an
*upper* bound on the kidney-specific component, not a demonstration of one.

## 8. Genetic arm, designed so both outcomes are interpretable

Enhanced PRS 26236 (eGFRcr) and 26237 (eGFRcys), available only in the 104,594-member testing subgroup
(26200-0.0); 99,362 merge with both assays `[worker-computed]`. Observed: corr(26236, 26237) = 0.437;
r2(prs_cys -> eGFRcys) = 0.093, r2(prs_cr -> eGFRcr) = 0.072, and **r2(prsdiff -> eGFRdiff) = 0.083 with
4.02 mL/min/1.73 m2 of eGFRdiff per SD of prsdiff** `[worker-computed; re-verification is a solver
deliverable]`. So the discordance has a substantial *heritable* component.
**What each outcome would and would not establish, stated now:**
- A heritable discordance component (already observed) **refutes** the strong version of (B) - "the discordance
  is purely acquired body composition and nutrition". It is **neutral between (A) and the weak (B)**, because a
  PRS for eGFRcys is partly a PRS for non-GFR determinants of the cystatin C protein itself (the CST3 locus is
  the paradigm case: variants that change circulating cystatin C without changing filtration).
- Therefore the informative analysis is *not* "is prsdiff associated with eGFRdiff" (answered) but whether
  prsdiff predicts the *outcomes* in the same organ-specific pattern as measured eGFRdiff. Concordant patterns
  support a shared latent cause; a prsdiff-eGFRdiff association with no outcome association supports a
  largely assay-level (CST3-like) component.
**Participant-level genotypes are absent from this snapshot, so Mendelian randomisation is blocked.** No
instrument-based causal language is used anywhere in this proposal; the PRS arm is an association and
decomposition arm only.

## 9. Accepted limitations (stated, not routed around)

1. **No measured GFR.** Iohexol/inulin clearance does not exist in this snapshot. The comparison that would
   settle (A) directly - does eGFRcys track measured GFR better than eGFRcr in discordant people? - is
   **unavailable**. Every conclusion is bounded by this: we can establish what the discordance *predicts* and
   how organ-anchored that prediction is; we cannot establish that either estimate is closer to true
   filtration. Resolving that requires an mGFR study (e.g. an iohexol cohort), not more computation here.
2. **UK Biobank healthy-volunteer selection**, ~5.5% response, so absolute rates are not population rates and
   the discordance distribution is shifted; the contrast DELTA is less vulnerable than any single beta, but
   selection on health correlates with both muscle mass and inflammation and could compress the non-kidney
   signal.
3. **Competing risk of death** is severe for KRT: Fine-Gray subdistribution models for kidney endpoints are
   reported alongside cause-specific Cox, and the primary DELTA uses cause-specific hazards with the
   interpretation that implies.
4. **Single-draw exposure**; no time-updated eGFRdiff for the whole cohort (unlike K2's annual CRIC measures),
   so trajectory information is lost.
5. **HES-based endpoints miss primary-care events** (uncoded AKI, conservatively managed kidney failure),
   biasing kidney counts down relative to death, which is registry-complete - a bias in the direction of (B)
   that must be stated when DELTA is negative.
6. **Requires clinical adjudication, not computation:** whether a given DELTA justifies changing renal dosing
   or referral policy; whether N17 coding in HES corresponds to clinically meaningful AKI; and whether the
   frailty endpoints are acceptable negative controls in nephrology practice. The verifier can check the
   computation and whether the stated conclusion matches the computed DELTA and its interval; it cannot check
   these three.
