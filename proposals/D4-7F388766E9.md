TITLE
Direction-specific incident threshold crossing between creatinine- and cystatin-C eGFR at the first repeat UK Biobank assessment

HYPOTHESIS
Among first-repeat attenders whose baseline target marker is at least 60 mL/min/1.73 m2, baseline discordance on the other marker predicts incident repeat target-marker status below 60. Direction A tests whether baseline eGFRcys < 60 predicts repeat eGFRcr < 60 among participants with baseline eGFRcr >= 60. Direction B tests whether baseline eGFRcr < 60 predicts repeat eGFRcys < 60 among participants with baseline eGFRcys >= 60. These are distinct eligible populations and are not pooled into a single bidirectional estimand.

DEFINITIONS AND ESTIMANDS
Let q=60, with all eGFR values retained unrounded and events classified strictly as <q.

Direction A:
S_A = first-repeat attenders satisfying baseline eGFRcr >= q.
X_A = I(baseline eGFRcys < q).
Y_A = I(repeat eGFRcr < q).
The estimand is the model-standardized risk difference E_{Z|S_A}[Pr(Y_A=1|X_A=1,Z)-Pr(Y_A=1|X_A=0,Z)], where Z includes continuous baseline eGFRcr, baseline age, sex, elapsed years, and only prespecified interactions. Standardization is over the observed covariate distribution in S_A.

Direction B:
S_B = first-repeat attenders satisfying baseline eGFRcys >= q.
X_B = I(baseline eGFRcr < q).
Y_B = I(repeat eGFRcys < q).
The estimand is the analogous standardized risk difference over the observed covariate distribution in S_B, adjusting for continuous baseline eGFRcys, baseline age, sex, elapsed years, and only prespecified interactions.

The primary tests are the two direction-specific risk differences, with the positive direction prespecified. They are co-primary and are the only members of the Holm family. A direction is not estimable if any exposure-by-outcome cell is empty or if the stated support diagnostics fail.

EQUATIONS

eGFRcr = 142*min(Scr/kappa,1)^alpha*max(Scr/kappa,1)^(-1.200)*0.9938^Age*sex_multiplier,
where Scr=field 30700/88.4 in mg/dL; for female sex=0, kappa=.7, alpha=-.241, multiplier=1.012; for male sex=1, kappa=.9, alpha=-.302, multiplier=1.

Use the 2012 CKD-EPI cystatin-C-only equation:
eGFRcys = 133*min(Scys/.8,1)^(-.499)*max(Scys/.8,1)^(-1.328)*.996^Age*sex_multiplier,
where Scys=field 30720 directly in mg/L; multiplier=.932 for female sex=0 and 1 for male sex=1. The combined equation is excluded. Unit tests against the frozen reference implementation are mandatory before fitting.

SUPPORT AND FALSIFICATION
Before inference, require at least one event and one nonevent in each exposure group for each direction, finite positive weights, adequate overlap, no unsupported standardization, acceptable calibration and no separation. If either direction fails, report that direction inconclusive without changing the cohort or threshold.

Supportive results require estimability, Holm-adjusted evidence in the prespecified positive direction, simultaneous intervals excluding zero, acceptable diagnostics, and stability between unweighted and measured-attendance analyses. This supports only incremental prediction of repeat protocol-biomarker incident threshold status among comparable first-repeat attenders. Directional asymmetry must remain directional and cannot establish bidirectional renal specificity.

Adverse results include a null or reversed estimate, disappearance after target-level adjustment, weight/floor dependence, poor calibration, or failed cell support. They falsify the stated incremental reclassification claim for that direction but do not prove absence of renal biology. Inconclusive results include sparse cells, separation, non-overlap, unstable weighting, failed equation/unit/sex certification, or unresolved assay interpretation.
