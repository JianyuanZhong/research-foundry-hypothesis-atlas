# Early physiology and instability of documented HCC pathway plans

## Scientific deliverable

The future solver must newly freeze an admission-anchored adult HCC cohort, assay and procedure dictionaries, and a two-window documented-order pathway state; fit the transparent baseline and the prespecified irregular coupled-state alternative; produce locked six-hour competing-risk predictions; estimate whether early laboratory information predicts subsequent *documented pathway change* beyond the initial documented pathway and pre-admission information; quantify whether that signal is reflected in the later recorded procedure family; and emit calibration, uncertainty, falsification, feasibility and claim-to-output tables. This is a substantive successor to [prior hypothesis]: the primary estimand no longer labels the later procedure itself as concordant or discordant with a possibly incomplete plan. It tests a directly observable intermediate—change in the documented pathway record—then reports procedure alignment only as a secondary descriptive outcome.

## Unresolved question and hypothesis

The available evidence supports only that the HCC snapshot contains dated encounters, diagnosis strings, laboratory results, non-drug orders and procedure strings. It does not establish pathology-confirmed HCC, tumor stage, intent, scheduling, multidisciplinary deliberation, appropriateness, treatment benefit, complications or complete outside-hospital capture. An order is a documented record, not intent or a recommendation.

Among adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission and a single clearly represented initial pathway family in the first 24 hours, does numeric hepatic/renal laboratory state observed in [0,24) hours predict a change in the *documented* pathway between [24,72) hours, beyond admission/pre-admission information, initial pathway and order-observation intensity?

Primary hypothesis: worsening or coupled hepatic/renal laboratory movement is associated with a higher probability of recorded pathway instability/change, and this association remains calibrated after accounting for the amount and timing of documentation. Formally, for the first documented pathway P0 in {E,A,S}, define R=1 when the [24,72) window contains a different single pathway family, or a multi-family/unassignable pathway state, and R=0 when it contains only P0. The primary estimand is the standardized held-out 72-hour risk contrast Delta_R = Risk(B1,R=1)-Risk(B0,R=1), with a prespecified minimum relevance of 0.01 absolute risk points and a two-sided paired patient-bootstrap interval. The secondary estimand asks whether early physiology predicts a later recorded E/A/S/X procedure or D discharge before such a procedure, and whether the observed procedure family is P0, the later documented family, or neither. These labels describe recorded workflow states, not clinical plan adherence.

A supportive result would identify an auditable point at which physiology is associated with documentation instability and could motivate prospective review of plan communication and missing-data capture. It would not justify automatically changing treatment. A null/adverse result would indicate that early numeric labs do not add reproducible information for this narrow documented-workflow signal, or that the signal is explained by observation intensity. An inconclusive result would reflect sparse pathway states/events, dominant unassignable orders, invalid times, missing procedure times, nonconvergence or uncertainty too wide to distinguish the prespecified contrast.

Include age >=18, valid native Admission Time and Discharge Time, nonnegative stay, admission in [2011-01-01,2026-01-01), discharge before 2026-01-01, and at least one frozen fitting-data lexical HCC diagnosis rule. Select the earliest eligible encounter per Patient Master Index by native Admission Time then Encounter Number; do not substitute a later encounter for an invalid earliest encounter. Report duplicate keys, unmatched joins, diagnosis multiplicity/type, invalid/ongoing times, overlap and cohort flow.

Require no valid-start E/A/S/X procedure in [0,24) hours. The primary exposure window is [0,24) from admission, the plan-transition window is [24,72), and the outcome window is [72,144). This buffer prevents the exposure from using post-transition information. Discharge before 72 hours is retained as an observed competing transition; it prevents a later plan-transition or procedure outcome. The primary R endpoint is therefore only defined among admissions with a valid [24,72) documentation window, continued local observation through 72 hours, and an assigned primary P1 state (P0, a different single family, or M); P1=N is a competing missing-documentation state. A separate admission-anchored secondary risk analysis that labels early discharge D0 rather than silently conditioning it away. The exact eligibility rule and missing-window bounds must be reported before model fitting.

## Recorded pathway states

Build a versioned dictionary in fitting data from orders.Orders (non-drug), using native orders.Order opening time. Qualifying pathway terms map only to E (therapeutic hepatic-artery embolization/interventional), A (liver-lesion ablation) or S (liver resection) when an exact-string rule is reviewable. Exclude routine nursing, diagnostic imaging, cancellations, and composite/unassignable strings from a single-family state; retain their counts and strings in the audit. Deduplicate within encounter, exact order string and opening time. Orders.Order status, order duration, frequency, start time and end time are audit/sensitivity variables, never proof of intent or completion.

Define the initial state P0 from qualifying orders opened in [0,24): P0=E/A/S if exactly one family is present; otherwise exclude the record from the primary clear-initial-state analysis and retain P=M (multiple/unassignable) or P=N (none) for descriptive/secondary analysis. Define the later state P1 analogously from orders opened in [24,72). R=1 for P1 different from P0 or P1=M; R=0 for P1=P0. P1=N is a distinct “no qualifying later documentation” state and must not be treated as stable care or absent intent. The solver must report P0/P1 transition counts, order density, status distributions, missing times, duplicate handling and the fraction of records with no later qualifying state. If routine/cancelled/unassignable records dominate, or fewer than 100 R=1 and 100 R=0 observations are available in the locked primary test, the primary claim is inconclusive.

Secondary procedure labels use procedures.Surgery in [72,144): E, A, S, X (mixed/other/unassignable), and D (native discharge before a qualifying procedure). Use native start time, never end time. A procedure before 24 hours excludes the admission; an event in [24,72) is retained in the plan-state audit and is a prespecified sensitivity/exclusion-bound, not allowed to contaminate the primary outcome. The first event wins. Administrative censoring is no qualifying procedure or D by 144 hours, or discharge after 144 hours. Report P0/P1 and E/A/S/X/D jointly; do not call any mismatch unplanned, inappropriate or treatment failure.

## Models, split, uncertainty and falsification

B0 is a transparent cause-specific pooled-logistic model with six-hour bins for R=1 versus R=0, using age, sex, admitting department of visit, admission-era descriptors, diagnosis-type descriptors, pre-admission encounter/procedure summaries, initial P0, pre-admission exact-assay nearest value/recency/count/missingness, and order-observation variables that are available before or during the relevant state definition. For the primary R contrast, documentation volume and timing must be represented symmetrically in both models so Delta_R is not simply a measurement-intensity effect.

B1 adds first/last/change/elapsed-time/slope (when at least two observations)/count/distinct-time/missingness/density summaries from exact numeric assays in [0,24). Both models use identical eligible patients, preprocessing, P0/P1 state definitions, patient split and locked test targets. The substantive alternative is an interpretable irregular continuous-time coupled latent-state model with assay-specific offsets/noise, patient shrinkage, irregular observation updates and an observation-intensity component, plus R and secondary E/A/S/X/D heads. Its hepatic-reserve and renal-stress names are modelling constructs, not diagnoses. It can reveal coupled movement, nonlinear timing and uncertainty from irregular sampling that first/last summaries lose. A small predictive gain is not substantive support unless the R contrast is stable and calibrated.

Assign all records by patient master index to fitting buckets 0–59, selection 60–69 and locked test 70–79 under the catalog participant-sha256-80-20/v1 policy; buckets 80–99 are inaccessible and are not external validation. Freeze dictionaries, assay semantics, state definition, model choices and preprocessing before one locked evaluation. Report AUROC/PR-AUC secondarily, but prioritize held-out Brier, calibration intercept/slope, observed-versus-predicted risks and paired patient-bootstrap intervals (at least 500 resamples) for Delta_R, state-versus-B1, transition probabilities, and secondary procedure CIFs. Report by P0=E/A/S and separately P=M/N, service and admission era as internal domain descriptions.

Required falsifications are: native-time leakage proof; permutation of lab timestamps preserving values/assay/count; removal/addition of measurement count, missingness, gaps and density; duplicate and same-assay/same-time handling; midnight procedure-start removal and missing-procedure-time bounds; leave-one-assay/order-family/procedure-family-out; permutation of P0/P1 state labels within service/era strata while preserving order volume; negative-control routine/central-line/venipuncture workflow endpoint; naive no-competing-risk sensitivity; and a sensitivity excluding procedures in [24,72). Persistence after lab-time or state-label permutation, strong dependence on one string/service/era, or disappearance after observation-process adjustment falsifies a physiology-specific interpretation.

## Evidence limits and method selection

Clinical adjudication remains essential for pathology confirmation, stage/burden/vascular invasion/resectability, performance status, intent/recommendation/scheduling, reason for pathway change or discharge, laboratory units/reference ranges, complications, death/transplant and outside-hospital capture. Text extraction is an unvalidated local lexical detector and clinical_documents/pathology lack native acquisition time for this question; images and waveforms are absent. The design cannot establish causal treatment effects, benefit, harm, recurrence, progression, organ failure, mortality or clinical utility. A prospective review study or external validation would be required for those claims.

The three demonstrations were read/dispositioned as method context, not topic requirements: Delphi supports dated structured-history evaluation and held-out calibration, but local HCC is not a reproduction and its UKB/Danish evidence is unavailable; ALADYNOULLI motivates an explicitly EHR-only irregular latent adaptation, while its genetic inputs are unavailable; Oncoformer supports modality-ablation thinking, but the main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and HCC images are absent, so only its accessible supplement is used as context. The expert-seed README was read; it supplies no HCC seed. The imported eICU/MIMIC/UKB seeds were assessed repairable but are not HCC parents.

The alternative record is deliberately question-specific. B0/B1 are CPU-first, using chunked CSV aggregation, sparse pooled hazards, state transition tables, CIFs and bootstrap. The coupled state model is also CPU-first within the future planning envelope (up to 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, 8 GPUs, 28800 seconds); a one-A100 allocated probe is permitted if repeated state fitting is materially slow, using cuda:0 only. These are planning estimates, not measured full-solver runtime. A GRU/temporal-attention model is deferred because the current uncertainty is whether an interpretable state adds information about documented pathway instability; revisit it only as a new child if structured residual timing error remains. Text/image/multimodal models and a validated clinical score are deferred for unavailable temporal labels, images, units or clinical semantics.

## Interpretation gates

Support requires adequate locked R=1/R=0 support, a prespecified Delta_R >=0.01 with an interval excluding zero, calibrated B1 improvement over B0, persistence across observation-intensity, assay, dictionary, timestamp, service and era checks, and, for the state model, stable improvement beyond B1. Adverse evidence is null/opposite Delta_R, poor calibration, dependence on measurement intensity or a single order string, survival of timestamp/state permutation, or instability under duplicate/time rules. Inconclusive evidence includes sparse or invalid windows, dominant M/N/unassignable states, missing times, nonconvergence or intervals too wide to distinguish the relevance threshold.

The computationally checkable conclusion is only whether the locked estimands and model comparisons were computed correctly with uncertainty and whether the recorded-workflow association meets these gates. Whether a pathway change reflected clinical reassessment, patient deterioration, scheduling, documentation correction or appropriate care requires expert review and data not present here.
