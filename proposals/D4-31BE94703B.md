## Inference, gates, falsification, and result meaning

Use the exact parent 2,000-draw participant-level paired bootstrap over canonical-eid-sorted O1_hold with fixed memberships/n1 and shared multiplicities; PCG64DXSM; seed equal to full unsigned big-endian SHA256 of UTF8 "ukb-grip-visit1-bootstrap-v1|"+b, b=0,...,1999; inherited studentized convention and percentile fallback. Compute Delta_5, Delta_9, and Delta_5to9 on every shared draw. Fewer than 95% finite draws, zero variance, or seed/membership/decomposition failure is inconclusive.

Before supportive/adverse interpretation require at least 20 K18_9 events in the selected union, six per list, eight across the symmetric difference, valid chronology, K18_5<=K18_9 for every participant, exact rosters/n1, and all source/join/bootstrap checks. Gate failure is inconclusive, never adverse.

The +2/1,000 margin is resolution-aware because one net event is 2.145923/1,000; it is not a validated utility threshold. This wrapper resolves an inherited prose ambiguity. Controlling rules are exactly:

- nine_year_supportive: gates pass, point Delta_9>=+2/1,000, and one-sided 95% lower limit >0.
- nine_year_adverse: gates pass and one-sided 95% upper limit <+2/1,000.
- nine_year_inconclusive_unresolved: otherwise, including equality at strict boundaries.

An interval merely crossing +2 does not itself assign the label; these exact rules do. Support requires uncertainty to exclude no enrichment; adverse excludes the minimum one-net-event planning claim. These states are mutually exclusive.

Always emit the immutable five-year label and nine-year label. Given known five-year inconclusiveness: nine-year support gives nine_year_supportive_five_year_unresolved; nine-year adverse gives five_year_unresolved_nine_year_adverse; otherwise both_horizons_unresolved. None is durable or overall support.

Support establishes only greater nine-year recorded inpatient N18 yield on this frozen AG1 list and justifies prospective adjudicated comparison. Adverse refutes the prespecified material nine-year enrichment claim, not that G1 improves health. Inconclusive leaves the question unresolved and is not equivalence.
