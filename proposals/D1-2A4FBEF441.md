## 1. The rival, stated so it can be wrong

- **Leading claim H1:** among patients discharged alive from the ICU, a *decoupled* dynamic
  instability footprint over `[t0-24h, t0)` adds held-out discrimination for 48 h unplanned ICU
  readmission / 7-day post-discharge death beyond (i) static severity and (ii) an explicit
  observation/monitoring-intensity control.
- **Rival H0 (this episode's target):** the footprint is a proxy for observation/monitoring intensity
  or care-escalation — sicker/observed-more patients are charted more often, mechanically inflating any
  dispersion/trend summary. Under H0 the increment is a **measurement-process** effect, not a distinct
  physiologic state.
- **Discriminating observation:** the *held-out incremental* ΔAUROC/ΔAUPRC of the decoupled dynamic
  block over the level block (a) before and (b) **after** equalizing monitoring cadence, and (c) when a
  value-free observation block M-obs is added as a competing predictor.

## 2. Decoupling design (four concrete constructs)

**D1 — Fixed-cadence aligned window (de-observation by geometry).** For each verified channel, resample
`[t0-24h, t0)` onto a **fixed hourly grid** (24 bins): one value per bin = the reading nearest the bin
centre, accepted only if within ±30 min; else the bin is missing. Dispersion/trend features are computed
on this grid (per-channel SD, IQR, range, and OLS slope over bin time), so every patient contributes at
most 24 aligned points per channel regardless of how many raw rows were charted. This removes the
mechanical `SD ∝ sqrt(N readings)` dependence. A **cadence-matched** variant uses the same K hourly
bins for all patients; a patient with sparse charting simply has more missing bins (handled by the
missingness feature, below), not a different variance scale.

**D2 — Per-patient standardized surprise (self-referenced residual, de-observation by normalization).**
For each channel and patient, fit a stay-level linear model `value ~ 1 + time` over **the whole stay**
(at or before `t0`), and define the window feature as the mean and 90th-percentile of the
**standardized residual** `z = (value - (b0 + b1·t)) / s_stay`. This is a within-patient z-score anchored
to that patient's own baseline and trajectory, so a swing is only "surprising" relative to *that*
patient — the feature no longer scales with how many times a healthy-looking constant was re-charted.
Raw dispersion (D1) and self-referenced surprise (D2) are kept as **separate blocks** so their
increments can be compared.

**D3 — M-obs measurement-process block (explicit rival features, value-free where possible).** Patient-level
features over `[t0-24h, t0)`, joined on `stay_id`:
- charting frequency: total `chartevents` rows/hour and rows/channel;
- distinct-caregiver count and caregiver-switches/hour from `chartevents.caregiver_id`;
- **documentation lag**: median/p90 of `storetime - charttime` (recording delay, not physiology);
- cadence regularity: SD of inter-reading interval per channel;
- **non-informative scheduled vitals**: count/share of consecutive readings per channel with
  `valuenum` unchanged (carried-forward routine entries) and count of readings flagged by
  `chartevents.warning`;
- lab/order process (from `hosp/labevents`): count of ordered labs/hour and share with
  `priority IN ('STAT','URGENT')` and `flag` set, and distinct `order_provider_id`.
  M-obs is used **two ways**: (i) as a *control block* added to M1 and to M2; (ii) as a *validity
  check* on the placebo in D4.

**D4 — Within-stay placebos (the footprint must not encode the schedule).**
- **P_sham (cadence-anchored, process target).** Target = top-tercile charting density in
  `[t0-24h, t0)` for that stay. *Required behavior:* the M-obs block predicts P_sham (validates the
  block), while the **decoupled dynamic block adds ≤ 0.005 AUROC beyond M-obs**. If the decoupled
  footprint predicts P_sham beyond M-obs, the footprint carries schedule information → H0 signal.
- **P_nonanchor (within-stay placebo outcome that monitoring cadence should NOT predict).** A same-stay
  composite event re-evaluated at a **non-discharge-anchored pseudo-landmark** `t0' = icustays.intime + 8h`
  with window `(t0', t0'+48h]` (same outcome definition, wrong anchor). By construction this endpoint is
  not the discharge-linked target. *Required behavior:* M-obs must **not** add ≥ 0.02 AUROC beyond M0 for
  P_nonanchor (monitoring cadence alone should not predict a non-anchored within-stay endpoint once
  severity is modeled), and the decoupled footprint must add ≤ 0.005. If M-obs predicts P_nonanchor
  strongly, M-obs is a generic severity proxy and the decoupling must be revisited before the main
  estimand is trusted.
- **Time-shuffle invariance (operator invariance).** Randomly permute the within-window `charttime` of
  each patient's vitals (marginals and counts preserved; M-obs unchanged) and recompute the dynamic
  block. If the genuine increment is *temporal-shape*, `Δ1` must fall toward the permuted-label level;
  if it barely moves, the increment is marginal-distribution, not trajectory.

## 3. Falsification ladder and analysis (locked split, paired inference)

Fit **nested** models on the **development 70 % only**, evaluate once on the **held-out 30 %**
(no re-splitting, no future information; split fixed at the parent's `t0 = 2169-10-11 22:55:40`):

- **M0 — static severity:** `anchor_age`, `admissions.admission_type`, ICU `los`, and first-ICU-day
  SOFA-surrogate components (labs: creatinine `50912`, platelets `51265`, WBC `51301`, bilirubin `50885`,
  lactate `50813`) plus Charlson/Elixhauser index from `hosp/diagnoses_icd` (`icd_code`,`icd_version`).
- **M1 — M0 + level means only:** last-24 h **means** of each verified vital (HR `220045`; SBP `220050`
  or `220179`; RR `220210`/`224690`; SpO2 `220277`; temp `223762`/`223761`; FiO2 `223835`; O2 flow
  `223834`/`227287`). No dispersion, no trend, no counts.
- **M2 — M1 + decoupled dynamics:** D1 fixed-cadence dispersion + D2 per-patient standardized surprise
  (with per-channel missing-bin count). This is the claim under test.
- **M2+Mub / competing controls:** M1+M-obs, M2+M-obs, M-obs alone.
- **Permuted-label negative control:** outcome labels shuffled within the development fold, refit, and
  evaluated — increments must collapse to ~0.

Primary estimands (held-out, primary outcome = 48 h unplanned readmission; secondary = 7-day
post-discharge death; combined = either):
- `Δ1  = AUROC(M2) − AUROC(M1)` — does decoupled dynamics add beyond levels.
- `Δ2  = AUROC(M2+M-obs) − AUROC(M1+M-obs)` — increment surviving the observation control.
- `Δobs1 = AUROC(M1+M-obs) − AUROC(M1)`, `Δobs2 = AUROC(M2+M-obs) − AUROC(M2)` — how much the
  observation block itself carries, before/after the decoupled footprint.
- AUPRC versions of each; **DeLong test** for correlated AUROCs (primary outcome), **1000-sample
  patient-level bootstrap** percentile CIs for every ΔAUROC and ΔAUPRC; calibration (slope + Brier)
  reported for M0/M1/M2.

**Alternative-method comparison (same question).** Interpretable nested logistic/GBM ladder above
(simple baseline) vs. a **sequence model A1** (small GRU, masked time-since-`t0` encoding) over the same
verified channels, evaluated on the identical held-out split. *What A1 can reveal that M2 loses:* whether
temporal **ordering/shape** (late-window acceleration vs. early spike) carries information that scalar
SD/slope compress away — a directly relevant discriminator between "instability trajectory" and "review
level." Inputs/target/split/eval are identical; A1 is trained on development only. Budget: the ladder is
CPU-feasible; A1 optionally uses **1 GPU** (declared below). **Deferral reason if not run:** if the CPU
ladder already resolves the rival at the predeclared boundaries, A1 adds cost without changing the
H0/H1 decision and is deferred to the solver envelope.

**Data-adequacy gate (checked before any conclusion).** Held-out combined events ≥ 300 (parent shows
~1,280) AND ≥ 80 % of cohort has ≥ 12 HR, ≥ 6 RR, ≥ 4 SpO2, ≥ 4 O2/FiO2 readings in `[t0-24h, t0)`.
If the coverage sub-gate fails, the result is **inconclusive by design**, not adverse.

## 4. Predeclared outcome boundaries (numeric; primary outcome; held-out)

**Supportive (H1 — decoupled dynamics is a real signal):** ALL of
1. `Δ1 ≥ 0.02` with bootstrap 95 % CI **lower bound > 0.005** (excludes 0 *and* the 0.01 adverse ceiling), AND
2. `Δ2 ≥ 0.015` with 95 % CI lower bound > 0.005 (≥ ~75 % of `Δ1` survives M-obs), AND
3. cadence-matched recomputation `Δ1_cadence ≥ 0.015` (increment survives when every patient is given the
   same nominal hourly cadence), AND
4. permuted-label `Δ ≤ 0.005` with CI including 0, AND
5. P_sham: dynamic block adds ≤ 0.005 beyond M-obs.

**Adverse (H0 — the rival explains the footprint):** ANY of
1. `Δ1 ≤ 0.01`, OR the 95 % CI **includes 0** (cannot reach the 0.02 target), OR
2. `Δ2 < 0.5 · Δ1` (M-obs removes > 50 % of the increment; equivalently the observation block closes the
   gap), OR
3. `Δ1_cadence ≤ 0.01` (increment vanishes under cadence-matched sampling), OR
4. `Δobs1 ≥ 0.5 · Δ1` while `Δ2` small (value-free observation alone reproduces the footprint), OR
5. P_sham: dynamic block predicts the cadence target with Δ > 0.01 beyond M-obs, OR P_nonanchor: M-obs
   predicts the non-anchored placebo with Δ ≥ 0.02 beyond M0.

**Inconclusive:** `0.01 < Δ1 < 0.02` with the CI spanning a boundary; OR bootstrap CI width > 0.03; OR the
data-adequacy/coverage gate fails; OR recalibration flips the sign of `Δ1`; OR the 7-day-death and
combined outcomes point in opposite directions with overlapping CIs.

**Why each threshold discriminates.** The `0.02 / 0.015` pair encodes "clinically-meaningful increment"
and "retained under the strongest control" — a mere severity correlate cannot pass (2). Rule (3) is the
*structural* falsifier: equalizing cadence is exactly what should destroy a count-proxy signal and leave
a self-referenced-surprise signal; a count-artifact that survives equalized cadence is a contradiction,
so collapse here is strong adverse evidence and survival is strong supportive evidence. `Δobs2` small
plus `Δ2 > 0` means the observation block adds nothing once the decoupled footprint is present — the
footprint is *not* measuring process. P_nonanchor checks that M-obs is a clean, non-generic control.
The thresholds are prespecified and are **not** to be tuned after seeing held-out values.

## 9. Remaining objections and what needs adjudication

- **Single most consequential remaining objection:** even a passing ladder cannot separate a genuine
  physiologic instability state from **integrated care-escalation** — monitoring, review and treatment
  are ordered in response to the same latent severity, so instability and process are *common effects* of
  one unobserved cause. M-obs captures **value-free charting count, caregiver identity and order
  priority**, but not *why* the extra measurements were ordered; a positive `Δ2` therefore still admits a
  "sicker-and-watched-both" explanation, and a null cannot rule out unmodeled escalation. Separating them
  needs the discrete-order/notes channel (rapid-response/consult orders, `labevents.priority` timing) and
  clinical adjudication — outside this design.
- "Unplanned" readmission and 7-day post-discharge death provenance (discharge destination, care-
  escalation vs. planned) require **clinical adjudication / notes**; structured fields are an
  approximation.
- Residual (unmeasured) treatment intensity is adjusted for, not removed → association / incremental
  prognostic-information only; **no causal or mechanistic claim.**
- All three key works are **abstract-only** inspections; full texts were not read.
- Cohort outcome counts are reused from the parent gate; per-stay `chartevents` coverage is **unverified**
  and is a first-class readiness check.
