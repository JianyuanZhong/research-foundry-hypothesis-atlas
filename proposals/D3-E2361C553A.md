## 1. The scientific opening

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) synovial tissue macrophages (STMs) split into functionally opposed
compartments: MerTK/CD206-positive STMs, enriched in sustained clinical remission, which induce a
repair/ECM program in fibroblast-like synoviocytes (FLS); and MerTK/CD206-negative STMs, enriched
in active disease, which induce an inflammatory FLS state. The source deposit states it directly
[OBSERVED, `E-MTAB-8873.idf.txt`]: "upon co-culture with MerTK/CD206neg an additional fifth cluster
(FLS5) emerged with inflammatory properties ... the MerTK/CD206pos population ... induced repair
response of FLS".

That finding has been carried into a **clinical proposition**: the *proportion* of MerTK+ STMs in
synovial tissue is proposed as a biomarker for whether a patient in remission can safely stop
therapy, with a published cut-off of 47.5% MerTK+ STMs (or a MerTK+:MerTK− ratio of 2.5) [K1].
That proposition contains an unstated and untested mechanistic premise:

> **The premise (S-label).** Fibroblast-instructing capacity is a property of the *sort label*.
> Any MerTK/CD206-positive STM is interchangeable with any other for the purpose of instructing
> fibroblasts; what varies between patients is how many of each you have.

If S-label is false — if instructing capacity is substantially a property of the **individual
donor** rather than of the subset label — then counting cells cannot be the right biomarker, and a
proportion threshold calibrated in one cohort will not transport. This is the opening. It is not
an abstract worry: the same literature establishes that cultured synovial *fibroblasts* retain a
stable, epigenetically imprinted, donor- and joint-of-origin identity across passages, including
differential cytokine responsiveness [K3]. Nobody has asked whether the macrophage side of the
same axis behaves the same way.

A second, orthogonal opening concerns what happens when **both** compartments act on the same
fibroblasts at once — the actual tissue situation, where remission synovium retains inflammatory
STMs and active synovium retains resolving STMs. The pure-subset arms fix the endpoints of a
dose-response curve and say nothing about its shape. Three combination rules make incompatible
predictions:

- **R-add (proportional integration).** The FLS program is linear in the resolving fraction *f*.
  A subset-ratio biomarker is linearly interpretable; partial shifts give proportional benefit.
- **R-pos (resolution dominance).** A minority of resolving STMs suffices (concave/saturating).
  Expanding or agonising the resolving compartment is a viable route to resolution.
- **R-neg (inflammation dominance).** A minority of inflammatory STMs suffices to block repair
  (convex/threshold). Then resolution requires *removing or reprogramming* the inflammatory
  compartment, expanding the resolving compartment is futile, and ratio biomarkers are
  threshold-like, not linear.

And a third layer, the mechanism class, which only the mixtures can separate. In a mixed well
every FLS sits in one shared soluble-mediator field but can be in physical contact with only one
subset (protocol: FLS 3000/well "in contact with" 3000/well sorted STMs, 48 h) [OBSERVED,
`E-MTAB-8873.idf.txt`]:

- **M-soluble.** Secreted mediators dominate; at *f* = 0.25 all FLS shift partially — a graded
  per-cell response with an intermediate mean.
- **M-contact (juxtacrine).** Instruction is local; a macrophage-fraction-sized subpopulation of
  FLS is instructed by one subset and the rest by the other — an anti-correlated two-axis *mosaic*
  whose components coincide with the pure-arm anchors.

### Dilution versus interaction — what the mixture design can and cannot mean

A fourth model must be named, because the measured data already make it the leading rival.
**R-dilution:** MerTK+ STMs are largely *inert* toward fibroblasts, and a mixture simply reduces
the MerTK− dose. If total macrophage number is constant, R-dilution and R-add make the **same**
prediction for the mean, so an additive result cannot distinguish instruction by MerTK+ cells from
passive dilution of MerTK− cells. The deposit contains no reduced-dose single-subset arm (e.g.
750 MerTK− STMs alone), so this confound is **not resolvable internally** [UNRESOLVED; specified
as a required control in a future titration].

Two independent [OBSERVED] results in this episode promote R-dilution from a formality to the
leading rival:

1. Pooled NMF on pure and fibroblast-only arms (`method_comparison_job.log`,
   job `[research job]`): the inflammatory chemokine program (CXCL1, IL6, CXCL8,
   CXCL3, CCL2, CXCL5, CXCL2, IER3) has mean weight 0.027 in FLS-only, **0.062** in pure MerTK−,
   0.041 in pure MerTK+ arms; the ECM/repair program (COL1A1, FN1, TGFBI, COL1A2, COL3A1) is
   0.322 / 0.284 / 0.316. The MerTK+ arm sits close to the uninstructed baseline on both axes.
2. On the a-priori polarity score, **the fibroblast-only arm lies at or slightly below the pure
   MerTK+ arm in both titration series** (A: −0.326 vs −0.316; B: −0.481 vs −0.422)
   (`f0_results.json`, job `[research job]`). Essentially the entire dynamic range
   between the poles is the MerTK− push, not a MerTK+ pull.

This qualifies, without contradicting, the published "induced repair response" description (which
rests on assays and genes this targeted panel does not contain). Its design consequence is
decisive: **the scientifically meaningful quantity in the mixture arms is a departure from
additivity — an interaction — not the slope.**

optimal cut-off, so it is equally compatible with R-add, R-pos and R-neg and says nothing about
the fibroblast transfer function. Item 4 means the mechanism question must be posed as the
*quantitative* architecture at intermediate proportions, not as the discovery that contact matters.

### Precision for the shape test [OBSERVED] — and three pre-registration defects it exposed

Delta-method approximation validated against a genuine cell-level nonparametric bootstrap on a
synthetic additive mixture built from held-out pure-arm cells (CI width 0.3828 vs 0.3674, ~4%
agreement), then 40,000 Monte-Carlo draws over {additive, R-pos, R-neg} × {graded, mosaic} ×
σ_between ∈ {0, 0.2226} at the observed pure-arm means/SDs and actual cell counts
(`precision_sim.json`):

- With **cell-level uncertainty only**: median 95% CI width on D(f) = **0.365–0.458**, wider than
  the entire 0.30-wide additive equivalence band. Simulated power to place the CI *inside* the band
  is **0.00** under every configuration. **Additivity is not a confirmable conclusion here.**
- Power to *exclude* the band: ~0.90 for strong resolution dominance at f = 0.25, ~0.88 for strong
  inflammation dominance at f = 0.75, but only **0.03–0.05** for weak inflammation dominance.
- With σ_between = 0.2226 propagated: CI widths **2.5–5.4**; all power ≈ 0. A donor-generalisable
  combination-rule claim is **not identified** by this deposit.

Three defects in the previous version of this proposal were found by that work and are corrected
below, in the proposal rather than quietly:

- **(D1)** The threshold "R-neg: D(0.25) ≤ 0.40" was self-contradictory: at f = 0.25 the additive
  band is [0.10, 0.40], nested inside D ≤ 0.40. Corrected to **R-pos: D(0.25) ≥ 0.60** and
  **R-neg: D(0.75) ≤ 0.40**. Only series A has both fractions; **series B cannot test R-neg at all**.
- **(D2)** The adverse criterion "the FLS-only reference lies outside the pure-arm range" was
  triggered by the pure arms themselves in 7/9 contrasts, contradicting section 5 where FLS-only is
  correctly an unexposed reference *outside* the f scale. That criterion is **deleted**; the
  measured position of FLS-only is instead reported as evidence for the R-dilution rival (section 1).
- **(D3)** The naive mosaic-vs-graded likelihood comparison is **uncalibrated**. On synthetic
  mixtures from held-out pure-arm cells, two matched one-parameter families (identical at w = 0, 1)
  are mis-selected at rates spanning 0%–100%, in *opposite* directions for series A and B (series A
  at w = 0.25, n = 1000: P(mosaic|mosaic) = 0.99 but P(graded|graded) = 0.00; series B at the same
  setting: P(graded|graded) = 1.00, P(mosaic|mosaic) = 0.01). Anchored weight recovery is precise
  (SD ŵ = 0.03–0.08 at n = 1000–2000) but biased (+0.09 series A, −0.04 series B, **+0.23** in the
  weakly separated donor-matched pair, all at w_true = 0.25). Corrected: a pre-registered
  **ŵ→w calibration curve built from pure-arm resampling only**, plus a parametric-bootstrap
  calibrated test that reports which families are *rejected* and both error rates.

## 3. Technical facts about the deposit that any analysis must respect [OBSERVED]

From `work/methods/qc_table.tsv` (33 samples, 20,562 raw cells, 19,952 passing) and the protocol
text:

1. **Zero cells fail the <100-molecule depth gate in any of the 33 samples.** The deposit is
   pre-filtered; the depth gate is a no-op and must be described as such, not as an applied filter.
2. **Cell-count discrepancy:** the IDF states "32141 FLS were evaluated"; the deposited matrices
   contain 20,562 cells. About a third of the described cells are not in the deposit. Must be
   disclosed; the sampling that produced the deposit is unknown.
3. **ITGB2 is not a clean macrophage marker in this panel** — the IDF itself reports an
   ITGB2/SELPLG-expressing FLS cluster. Dropping ITGB2 changes `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` from
   12.41% to 2.41% macrophage-dominant. **The no-ITGB2 gate is pre-registered as primary**; the
   ITGB2-containing gate is a sensitivity analysis.
4. **Gate specificity is bounded empirically:** fibroblast-only arms give 0.00% macrophage-dominant
   in 4/5 samples (0.90% in `R2_SA257A_FLS_only`), so the gate false-positive rate is ≤ ~0.9%.
5. **The mixture ratios are a filename inference, not a documented design variable.** The IDF never
   mentions mixed co-cultures at all — no ratio, and no total macrophage number for mixed wells.
   The SDRF annotates all three mixtures only as `Characteristics[growth condition]` = "co-cultured
   with MerTK/CD206neg and MerTK/CD206pos macrophages"; the `1Mpos`/`3Mneg` tokens appear **only in
   the Source Name/filename**, in no controlled field.
6. **Residual-macrophage check on mixing (the only mixture quantity computed).**
   Series A (R2/SA258R) is consistent with constant-total **substitution**: macrophage-dominant
   fractions 1.16 / 1.10 / 0.61 / 0.41 % at f = 0 / 0.25 / 0.75 / 1, versus 1.16 / 0.97 / 0.60 /
   0.41 % predicted by linear interpolation. Series B (R5/SA263R) is **not**: pure MerTK− 12.41%,
   **mixture 14.31%**, pure MerTK+ 0.88% (9.5% predicted); under the no-ITGB2 gate 2.41 / **5.39** /
   0.88 % versus 2.03% predicted. *The series-B mixed well carries more residual macrophage signal
   than its own pure MerTK− arm, under both gates.* That is incompatible with a constant total
   macrophage number and compatible with additive dosing or differential macrophage survival.
   **Consequence: series A is the primary titration; series B is a sensitivity arm, not a replicate.**
7. **No well-level replicate exists.** The three technical replicate wells share one sample tag, so
   between-well technical variance is structurally unestimable; the only handle on non-cell-level
   variance is four macrophage-donor pairs (~3 df).
8. **SDRF donor annotations are internally inconsistent** (the pure arm
   `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_Mpos` lists three individuals; `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA260R_Mpos_Inhibitor` never

10. `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA277A_Mneg_preLPS` has 62 raw / 52 passing cells — descriptive only.
11. Median depth in `*_preLPS` arms is ~2× the untreated arms (8.1k–11.4k vs 3.8k–6.3k), so LPS

## 4. The unresolved claims this experiment tests

### H1 — PRIMARY. Is fibroblast-instructing capacity a property of the MerTK/CD206 label, or of the donor?

**Claim under test (S-label, the premise of the subset-proportion biomarker):** on a
pre-registered, contamination-controlled per-cell fibroblast-program axis, the displacement
produced by a macrophage preparation is determined by its MerTK/CD206 sort label, and
donor-to-donor variation *within* label is small relative to the difference *between* labels.

**Rival (D-donor):** instructing capacity is substantially a donor-level property —
label explains a minority of the variation in what a macrophage preparation does to fibroblasts.

**Estimand.** R = τ_donor / δ_subset, where δ_subset is the mean within-FLS-donor, within-run
displacement between MerTK− and MerTK+ preparations, and τ_donor is the SD of displacement across
different macrophage donors of the *same* subset on the *same* FLS donor in the same run.
Pre-registered decision: **S-label supported if the upper confidence limit of R < 0.5; D-donor
supported if the lower confidence limit of R > 1.0; inconclusive in between** — and with four
donor pairs, inconclusive is an honestly likely outcome that must be reported as such.
A scale reference is pre-specified: δ_LPS (d = 1.0–2.7 on the same axis) is the magnitude of a
known-large perturbation, so R is interpreted against both δ_subset and δ_LPS.

**Why it is not already answered by this episode's feasibility run.** The [OBSERVED] σ = 0.2226 was
computed (i) on a fixed a-priori gene set, (ii) with no ambient-myeloid control, (iii) with no
interval on the ratio, (iv) pooling runs with different depths. The trained discriminant doubles
δ_subset (section 6); whether it also doubles τ_donor is unknown and decides the answer. The
contamination-controlled refit is the single most informative unexecuted computation in this design.

**Clinical consequence.** If D-donor holds, a tissue MerTK+ *proportion* cut-off (47.5% / ratio 2.5
[K1]) is measuring a variable whose mechanistic content varies between patients, which predicts
poor transportability of that threshold to new cohorts and argues that a withdrawal biomarker
should be a macrophage *state/function* measure rather than a cell count. If S-label holds, the
proportion biomarker has a mechanistic footing it currently lacks, and the combination-rule
question (H3) becomes the right next thing to measure. Either answer changes what to measure next
in the drug-free-remission decision — the most consequential open decision in RA management.

### H2 — Per-cell architecture of the mixed response (mosaic vs blended)

In mixed co-culture the fibroblast population is a **mosaic** of cells carrying the two
single-subset programs — a two-component structure in which the inflammatory-component weight
tracks the nominal macrophage fraction and the non-inflammatory component resembles the
*MerTK+-instructed* arm rather than the uninstructed FLS-only baseline — rather than a population
in which every cell carries a blended intermediate.

**Discriminating observable, refined to answer [K2].** Bimodality alone cannot separate contact
instruction from a soluble field: a uniform soluble TNF-α dose produces *digital* single-cell
activation whose responder fraction tracks dose [K2]. The observable that does separate them is the
**joint two-axis per-cell structure** in the mixture well, using independent repair- and
inflammatory-axis scores:

- Mosaic/private instruction predicts *negative* within-well correlation between the two axes, a
  two-component joint distribution whose components coincide with the two pure-arm anchors, and a
  calibrated weight near the nominal fraction.
- A shared soluble field — graded or digital — predicts that repair-axis signal is present in *all*
  cells including inflammatory responders (non-negative or independent axis correlation), with the
  non-responding component resembling FLS-only on the inflammatory axis but still repair-shifted.

Both features are invariant to a global additive well-level offset, which is why H2 survives the
precision problem that disables H3. Residual ambiguity (very short-range soluble gradients can mimic
private instruction) is acknowledged and deferred to transwell/conditioned-medium work (section 9).
**All H2 model selection must use the pure-arm-only calibration of defect (D3); an uncalibrated
likelihood comparison would manufacture a mechanism conclusion.**

### H3 — Combination rule on the mean (exclusion of strong dominance only)

The population-mean fibroblast program in mixed co-culture is not a linear function of *f*.
Pre-registered, corrected thresholds: **R-pos if D(0.25) ≥ 0.60; R-neg if D(0.75) ≤ 0.40**, against
the additive band |D(f) − f| ≤ 0.15. Series A (f = 0, 0.25, 0.75, 1) is the only series that can
test both directions; series B (f = 0, 0.25 only, and with the mixing anomaly of §3.6) is a
sensitivity arm.

**Pre-committed honesty constraint, from the measured precision study: additivity cannot be
confirmed by this design (simulated power 0.00) and must never be reported as the conclusion.**
The only available positive outcomes are exclusion of strong dominance in one direction (power
~0.9 at the favourable fraction) or an interval that excludes nothing (power 0.03–0.05 for weak
dominance). H3 is therefore an interval estimate with a pre-declared most-likely outcome of
*inconclusive*, retained because the interval itself — the first ever measured on this transfer
function — bounds how large a dominance effect can be.

**Exploratory bridge to the published cut-off.** The nominal fractions 0.25 and 0.75 *bracket* the
published 47.5% MerTK+ flare threshold [K1], so the sign of curvature between them is the closest
mechanistic counterpart this dataset can offer. Labelled exploratory: one outcome-optimised cut-off
from 22 patients and one usable co-culture series cannot establish correspondence.

### H4 — Cross-level test of H1 in tissue (independent data, independent donors, far better powered on donors)

E-MTAB-8322 contains whole-transcriptome per-cell synovial **macrophage** data from 27 samples
(25 with `donor_status` "verified"; SA149/SA149P flagged `possible_related_or_repeated` and excluded
from independence-assuming comparisons) across healthy control (n = 4), treatment-naive RA (n = 6),
treatment-resistant RA (n = 6), RA in remission (n = 7) and undifferentiated arthritis (n = 4)
[OBSERVED, `E-MTAB-8322.design.json`]. Two pre-specified uses:

- **H4a (the cross-level version of H1, the stronger arm).** After shared subclustering into
  MerTK/CD206-positive-like and -negative-like states (explicitly permitted for this assay by the
  registry), decompose the variance of the macrophage transcriptional state into a between-state
  (label) component and a between-donor-within-state component. S-label predicts label dominance;
  D-donor predicts a comparable or larger donor component. 25 verified donors give this arm far more
  donor-level resolution than the six macrophage donors in the co-culture deposit. It measures
  macrophage *phenotype* variance, not instructing capacity — a stated, non-fatal gap in the
  inference, since the co-culture arm measures capacity with few donors and this arm measures state
  with many.
- **H4b (compositional consistency with H3).** Per-donor resolving fraction f_tissue on a log-ratio
  scale: does it separate remission from active disease threshold-like (consistent with dominance) or
  with smooth overlap (consistent with additivity), and do treatment-resistant donors sit in the same
  regime as treatment-naive donors? With 6–7 donors per state this is descriptive and underpowered
  for a formal threshold; it can falsify a strong dominance reading but cannot confirm one.

Donor identifiers are **not** joined across E-MTAB-8322 and E-MTAB-8873 even though both use an
`SA...` namespace; these are separate source groups with distinct identifier namespaces.

### H5 — MerTK inhibition: demoted to a documented dead end [OBSERVED result]

The previous version proposed UNC1062 pre-treatment of MerTK+ macrophages as a mechanism anchor.
The measured arms do not support that use: inhibitor effects on the polarity score are
d = −0.093, −0.145, −0.205, −0.345 (i.e. *toward* repair, the opposite of the prediction) with one
+0.184, and **the off-target control — fibroblasts plus inhibitor, no macrophages present — gives
d = −0.120, the same magnitude as the "real" effects** (`f0_results.json`). The inhibitor arms are
measuring something that does not require macrophages. H5 is therefore **not** part of the test; it
is reported as a negative feasibility finding with its control, and the arms are available only as
a labelled exploratory sensitivity analysis. This is a case where the honest deliverable is a
closed door.

## 6. Variables, estimand, analysis [PLANNED for the solver]

**Unit of analysis.** The FLS cell, nested in co-culture sample, nested in FLS donor × run.
All primary contrasts are within run.

**Pre-processing (pre-registered).** (i) The <100-molecule depth gate is a documented no-op (§3.1).
(ii) Macrophage-dominant gate, **no-ITGB2 variant primary** (CD14, MRC1, MERTK, FCGR3A, C1QA, C1QB,
CD163 sum > fibroblast sum of COL1A1 ×2 probes, COL1A2, COL3A1, TGFBI, FN1), ITGB2-containing gate
as sensitivity. (iii) CP10K-equivalent within-cell normalisation. (iv) **Ambient-myeloid
adjustment**: the NMF MHC-II/myeloid program score (CD74, HLA-DRA, ITGB2, FCER1G, LIPA, HLA-DPA1,
CD14) entered as a per-cell covariate, plus a contamination-matched sensitivity analysis in which
arms are matched on residual myeloid content. This is mandatory, not optional: the myeloid program
co-varies with the exposure (§7, explanation 4).

**Axes.** Two axes are fitted and both reported:
- *A-priori axis* (transparent, fixed): s = mean z(13 inflammatory probes) − mean z(8 repair probes),
  z within run, from pure + FLS-only cells only. Already characterised [OBSERVED].
- *Trained axis* (primary, must be refit): CV L2-regularised logistic discriminant over the 445
  features separating pure MerTK− from pure MerTK+ cells within series, **refit on a
  contamination-controlled feature set with all myeloid features removed**. The current unrestricted
  fit reaches CV AUC 0.714 (A) / 0.768 (B) but its top weights include FOLR2, CCR1, CCL5, HLA-DQB1,
  CD209, PTPRC — so the ~2× gain in pole separation is **provisional until myeloid features are
  removed**, and verifying it is a named solver deliverable.

**Secondary readouts.** RANKL/OPG (TNFSF11/TNFRSF11B) ratio as a bone-erosion-relevant output;
CMKLR1 and MERTK/AXL as mediator-pathway readouts; PCNA/LDHA/PGK1/ENO1 (detected in 88–100% of
cells in every sample [OBSERVED]) for the differential-survival/proliferation rival.

**H1 analysis.** On the contaminated-controlled trained axis and on the a-priori axis:
δ_subset from the nine within-run contrasts; τ_donor from the four donor-replicate pairs by
method-of-moments with a parametric bootstrap that propagates the cell-level sampling error of each
arm mean; R = τ_donor/δ_subset with a bootstrap interval that honestly reflects ~3 df. Report
δ_LPS on the same axis as a scale reference. Pre-specified sensitivity: drop-R1 (SDRF annotation
anomaly, §3.8); gate variant; with and without myeloid adjustment. **No donor t-test** (registry
prohibition). Because τ_donor and macrophage-donor disease state are not separable here (§5), the
report must state R as an *upper bound on label sufficiency* that does not attribute the donor
component to any particular donor property.

**H2 analysis.** Two-axis per-cell joint structure in the series-A mixture wells: within-well
Spearman correlation between repair and inflammatory axes, compared against the pure-arm and
FLS-only wells; two-component mixture anchored on the pure-arm per-cell distributions with free
weight ŵ; **ŵ corrected through a calibration curve built only from pure-arm resampling** (defect
D3); a parametric-bootstrap-calibrated test that reports which of {mosaic, graded} is *rejected*
and both error rates at the realised cell counts (461–1364). Dispersion and bimodality statistics
reported but not used for selection.

**H3 analysis.** D(f) = (s(f) − s(0))/(s(1) − s(0)) within series, cell-level bootstrap plus a
sample-level model, against the corrected thresholds (R-pos D(0.25) ≥ 0.60; R-neg D(0.75) ≤ 0.40)
and the additive band. Series A primary; series B reported separately with its mixing anomaly.
Concordance between series is described, never pooled into a donor test.

**Composition-vs-regulation control (the D3-M1 rival).** Cluster FLS cells using the FLS-only arms
to recover state structure, assign co-culture cells, then decompose the change in s into a
state-proportion component and a within-state component. A proportion-driven result supports a
switch reading; a within-state-driven result supports graded regulation.

**Dose/ratio verification.** Realised mixing fraction from residual macrophage-dominant cells versus
the nominal filename label, under both gate variants; series A result already computed (§3.6) and
series B already falsifies constant-total mixing.

**H4 analysis.** Shared subclustering of E-MTAB-8322 macrophages across donors; states labelled by
MERTK/MRC1/TREM2/LYVE1 versus inflammatory markers. H4a: variance decomposition of per-cell state
scores into between-state and between-donor-within-state components (25 verified donors), with the
same R-style ratio and interval. H4b: per-donor f_tissue on a log-ratio scale, state separation
pattern and regime of treatment-resistant donors. Sensitivity: cluster count, dissociation-sensitive
cell types, exclusion of SA149/SA149P, per-donor cell-count weighting.

## 7. Competing explanations to be addressed explicitly

1. **FLS-intrinsic donor state, not the macrophage input, drives the response.** Both titration FLS
   donors are remission-derived; active-disease FLS donors have only pure and inhibitor arms.
   [K3] shows cultured synovial fibroblasts carry stable donor/joint-of-origin imprinting including
   differential cytokine responsiveness, so this is a substantiated rival, not a formality.
2. **Total macrophage dose, not subset fraction, differs in mixed wells.** Now partly *measured*:
   series A is consistent with substitution, series B is not (§3.6). The protocol documents no mixed
   well at all. [UNRESOLVED — expert question.]
3. **Macrophage-donor identity confounded with subset**, and in this deposit also with macrophage
   donor disease state. Series A is donor-matched between pure and mixed arms, which is why it is
   primary; the confound is the content of H1, not an aside.
4. **Composition / ambient-RNA artifact rather than fibroblast reprogramming.** Measured, not
   hypothetical: the macrophage-dominant fraction is systematically higher in MerTK− arms
   (1.2–12.8%) than MerTK+ arms (0.3–3.5%) and 0% in four of five fibroblast-only arms, and the NMF
   MHC-II/myeloid program is elevated in MerTK− arms [OBSERVED]. Ambient myeloid RNA therefore
   co-varies with the exposure and could by itself produce an apparent inflammatory shift. Handled by
   the two gate variants, the myeloid-program covariate, the contamination-matched sensitivity
   analysis, and the contamination-controlled refit of the trained axis.
5. **R-dilution rather than instruction** (§1): an additive mean cannot separate active MerTK+
   instruction from passive reduction of MerTK− dose, and the measured position of the FLS-only arm
   at/below the pure MerTK+ arm makes this the leading rival. Only a departure from additivity, or
   the H2 mosaic structure, carries interaction information. No reduced-dose single-subset arm
   exists [UNRESOLVED].
6. **Differential FLS survival/proliferation** rather than reprogramming — PCNA/LDHA/PGK1/ENO1
   available and pre-specified.
7. **Measurement scope.** 445 targeted features; NOTCH3, PDPN, THY1, CXCL12, HBEGF, PTGS2, VIM,
   CDH11 unmeasured. No claim may extend to them.
8. **Batch/run, sample-tag misassignment and doublets.** All primary contrasts within run;
   doublet-like cells gated; no well-level replicate exists to estimate technical variance (§3.7).
9. **Sequencing-depth confound in the LPS arms** (~2× depth, §3.11): depth matching required; LPS is
   a scale reference only.
10. **Deposit incompleteness.** 20,562 deposited cells versus 32,141 described (§3.2); the selection
    that produced the deposit is unknown and could be phenotype-related.
11. **Selection and system limits.** FACS sorting plus 48 h culture selects for adherent surviving
    FLS; a two-cell-type well omits lymphocytes, endothelium, spatial organisation and treatment.
    Ex vivo dominance need not equal tissue dominance.
12. **H4-specific.** Subset proportions are compositional estimates sensitive to dissociation and
    sorting, cross-sectional and treatment-confounded; they cannot establish causality, and H4a
    measures macrophage phenotype variance, not instructing capacity.

## 8. Baseline and the substantive alternative (method comparison) [measured]

**Baseline (simple, pre-specified).** Sample-level pseudobulk polarity score from
`prepared/E-MTAB-8873.counts.tsv.gz`; shape test on 4–5 points per series. Measured cost **0.006 s**
[OBSERVED]. Its decisive weakness is not speed but inference: the three technical wells share one
sample tag, so pseudobulk has **zero residual degrees of freedom** and cannot produce any interval
at all. It also integrates over cells and therefore cannot see the two-axis mosaic structure (H2),
cannot separate state-proportion from within-state change, and cannot separate ambient myeloid
signal from fibroblast biology.

**Alternative (substantive, same question).** Per-cell hierarchical mixture / latent-program model:
NMF programs plus a CV discriminant fitted on pure and FLS-only arms, applied to held-out mixture
cells, with an anchored two-component mixture weight. Measured on the real data
(job `[research job]`, CPU only): **265.3 s wall, 434.2 MB peak RSS on 8 CPUs** for
the whole pipeline — per-cell load of 17,004 × 445 in 1.1 s, eight NMF fits (k = 2–10) at 2.2–4.6 s
each, three CV discriminants in 0.22 s total, and 36 × 80 × 2 shape simulations in 235 s.

What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses, with numbers:
- **Inferential capacity.** The trained axis roughly doubles the pole separation, the denominator of
  D(f): series A d 0.368 → 0.773, series B 0.673 → 0.973, donor-matched series B 0.217 → 0.534
  (CV AUC 0.657–0.768). Provisional pending the contamination-controlled refit (§6).
- **A nuisance the baseline cannot see.** NMF at k = 5 (converged; held-out error 0.2975;
  train/test gap < 0.002) recovers ECM/collagen, chemokine, proliferation/glycolysis and MMP/TIMP
  programs **plus a myeloid/MHC-II program** (CD74, HLA-DRA, ITGB2, FCER1G, CD14) that is exactly
  the ambient-contamination rival of §7.4. Held-out reconstruction error is monotone from 0.3345
  (k = 2) to 0.2723 (k = 10) with no elbow, so k must be fixed by a pre-specified rule with
  sensitivity analysis, not by inspection.
- **Honest error rates.** Only the per-cell simulation could reveal defect D3 — that naive
  mosaic-vs-graded selection is uncalibrated with error rates spanning 0–100%. The baseline would
  have produced a confident mechanism claim with no way to detect this.

**GPU: not required, and this is measured, not assumed.** The entire pipeline is 265 s on 8 CPUs at
434 MB. A GPU would not change feasibility for 20k cells × 445 features. This is a workload
judgement, not a prohibition.

**Alternatives considered and not selected, with revisit triggers.**
- *scVI-style deep generative model*: with 445 targeted features and no batch-spanning integration
  need (all contrasts within run), its added capacity does not address H1–H3 and it would obscure
  the interpretability of the mixing weight. **Revisit if** a future episode admits whole-
  transcriptome co-culture data, or if NMF/mixture models fail to recover FLS state structure on the
  pure arms.
- *Mixed-effects pseudobulk model*: rejected because there is no well-level replication to support
  the random effect (§3.7). **Revisit if** replicate wells or additional donors become available.
- *Naive likelihood model selection for H2*: rejected by measurement (defect D3). **Revisit only**
  with the pure-arm calibration curve in place.

**Readiness criterion F1 (pre-specified, falsifiable).** Before H3 is interpreted at all, the
contamination-controlled trained axis must achieve CV AUC ≥ 0.70 within series A. The unrestricted
fit measures 0.714 (A) and 0.768 (B), so F1 is currently plausible but **not yet established under
myeloid-feature removal**. If F1 fails, H3 is reported as uninformative by design and only H1 and
H2's offset-robust features are interpreted.

## 9. Falsification criteria and outcome interpretation

**F0 (measurement anchor) — PASSED in this episode, with a bound.** Pure MerTK− vs MerTK+ arms
separate in the published direction in 9/9 contrasts, including both titration series (A d = 0.368,
B d = 0.673) [OBSERVED]. F0 is met, but with 64–88% per-cell overlap, so every downstream estimate
inherits that modest span. The previously stated FLS-only clause is deleted (defect D2).

**H1 supportive for S-label:** upper confidence limit of R = τ_donor/δ_subset below 0.5 on the
contamination-controlled axis. Meaning: the sort label captures most of the instructing signal, the
proportion-based biomarker has a mechanistic footing, and the combination rule (H3) is the right
next measurement. It would *not* establish that the published 47.5% threshold is correct, nor that
the same holds in tissue.

**H1 supportive for D-donor:** lower confidence limit of R above 1.0. Meaning: fibroblast-instructing
capacity is substantially a donor property; a MerTK+ *count* is a weak proxy for what the
compartment does; withdrawal biomarkers should be re-examined as state/function measures; and the
mixture arms do not cleanly represent "25% resolving" but "donor X's cells plus donor Y's cells".
It would *not* identify which donor property matters (state, age, treatment, tissue niche — none of
which are recorded for the macrophage donors here), and would not by itself invalidate the clinical
association in [K1], which is an outcome association independent of its mechanistic reading.

**H1 inconclusive (honestly likely):** R interval spanning 0.5–1.0 with four donor pairs. Report the
interval, δ_LPS as scale, and the number of macrophage donors a decisive test needs — the sample
size calculation itself is a deliverable.

**H2 supportive for mosaic:** negative within-well two-axis correlation in the series-A mixtures,
two components coinciding with the pure-arm anchors, and a calibrated ŵ near the nominal fraction,
with the graded family rejected at calibrated error rates. Predicts spatial patchiness of fibroblast
states around macrophage neighbourhoods in tissue (testable by imaging/spatial assays) and predicts
that conditioned medium will *not* reproduce the effect.
**H2 supportive for a shared field:** non-negative axis correlation with repair signal present in
inflammatory responders; predicts conditioned medium reproduces the effect. Per [K2] this remains
compatible with digital per-cell activation, so bimodality alone will not be reported as contact.
**H2 inconclusive:** neither family rejected at calibrated error rates — the expected outcome if the
contamination-controlled axis loses separation, and a legitimate result given one usable series.

**H3:** exclusion of strong dominance in one direction (CI excluding the additive band) is the only
available positive outcome, at the thresholds R-pos D(0.25) ≥ 0.60 / R-neg D(0.75) ≤ 0.40.
**Additivity must never be reported as confirmed** (measured power 0.00). An interval that excludes
nothing is reported as the first measured bound on this transfer function, with the required design
for a decisive test (≥5 ratios, ≥4 FLS donors including active disease, replicate wells, constant
and documented total macrophage number, plus a reduced-dose single-subset arm for R-dilution).
**Adverse for the whole titration:** series A and B disagree in direction, *or* the realised mixing
fraction cannot be reconciled with the nominal label (already true in series B), *or* F1 fails.
Then the honest conclusion is that this deposit cannot settle the combination rule.

**H4a supportive for S-label / D-donor** mirrors H1 at the tissue level with 25 donors; agreement
across the two levels strengthens either reading, disagreement localises the discrepancy to
phenotype-versus-capacity and is itself informative.
**H4b:** threshold-like separation of f_tissue compatible with an ex vivo dominance threshold
strengthens a dominance reading; smooth overlap is adverse for strong dominance. Neither establishes
causality; a null here does not refute the ex vivo result.

**H5:** already reported as a confounded arm (off-target control of the same magnitude as the
effects). A drug-associated expression change is not proof of direct target action.

## 10. Blinding, provenance and declared overlaps

**Pre-registration blinding held.** No fibroblast program score, differential expression, clustering
assignment or any other phenotype summary was computed or inspected for any of the three mixture
samples. Only `qc_all_samples.py` opens those matrices, and it computes cell counts, molecule depth
and marker-dominance fractions only; `f0_pure_arms.py` and `method_comparison.py` skip mixtures and
assert that no mixture sample entered the analysis; the pseudobulk matrix drops its three mixture
columns before any arithmetic, also asserted. Mixtures contributed only `n_pass_qc` to the precision
simulation and their residual-macrophage fractions to the mixing check — both design-verification
quantities, declared in advance as permitted, and neither is a fibroblast phenotype.

**Declared partial unblinding of the sibling branch.** The four donor-replicate pairs computed here
for variance estimation (P1–P4) are exactly the donor-state contrast of interest to the sibling
branch `work/intrinsic/` ([prior hypothesis]). That branch's question is therefore
**not fully blind**, because the σ = 0.2226 figure and the per-pair differences were computed and
read in this episode. This is recorded rather than concealed, and any claim from that branch must be
interpreted as partially informed by these values.

**Reference provenance.** [K1] and [K2] are `abstract_only` inspections with explicit limits; [K3] is
`full_text` for the sections named in its locator (Methods and Supplementary not inspected). Attached
excerpt bytes are hashed exactly as attached. The sibling branch attached a different, branch-local
reference set; the divergence is documented in its section 12 and the two sets are not merged.

## 11. What would require additional data, expert review or another study

- **Which donor property** underlies any donor component: macrophage-donor age, sex, treatment and
  disease duration are recorded nowhere in E-MTAB-8873 and cannot be recovered from the deposit.
- **Confirmation of the mixture ratios** and whether total macrophage number was constant: requires
  the original protocol or author contact. The IDF does not describe mixed wells at all.
- **Separating soluble from contact instruction**: transwell and conditioned-medium variants of the
  same co-cultures; short-range soluble gradients can mimic private instruction.
- **Identification of the mediator**: conditioned-medium fractionation, lipid-mediator measurement,
  targeted blockade.
- **Whether active-disease FLS respond with the same rule**: both titration donors are
  remission-derived.
- **Tissue-level validity**: spatial transcriptomics or imaging of MerTK+ and MerTK− neighbourhoods
  around fibroblast states.
- **Any clinical claim** about therapy withdrawal or biomarker thresholds requires a prospective
  cohort with outcomes; nothing here can establish it, and no automatic verifier can adjudicate it.
- **The 32,141-versus-20,562 cell discrepancy**: whether the deposit is a phenotype-neutral subset.
