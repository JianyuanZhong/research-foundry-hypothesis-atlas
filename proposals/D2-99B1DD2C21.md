# Hypothesis: context-dependent protective versus pathogenic response in E-MTAB-8873

## Proposed relationship

In the available E-MTAB-8873 macrophage–fibroblast intervention data, the intervention-associated transcriptional response will differ by cellular context: a reduction of a fibroblast-linked harmful program while preserving or strengthening a macrophage-linked protective/resolving state. The primary estimand is a donor-aware, condition-verified context-by-intervention interaction, not a generic mean-expression change.

This is a hypothesis, not an observed result. No dataset-specific effect has been computed in this episode. The question is motivated by the D2-T2 opening, but that seed is untested source material.

## Evidence and unresolved claim

The inspected evidence supports that MerTK-positive synovial tissue macrophage subsets are associated with remission and can induce repair responses in synovial fibroblasts [K1], that resolving macrophage function can alter inflammatory homeostasis [K2], and that macrophage–stromal repair circuits are context-dependent and can become maladaptive [K3]. This evidence does not establish that UNC106238 acts directly on MerTK, that the response is causal, that a protective state is preserved, or that an in-vitro pattern transfers to patients.

The unresolved claim is whether the intervention produces a reproducible separation between a protective and a pathogenic transcriptional state after accounting for donor, timing, composition, and measurement artifacts. A supportive result would establish only a context-dependent intervention-associated response in this assay. It would not establish direct target action, mechanism, clinical efficacy, or patient benefit.
