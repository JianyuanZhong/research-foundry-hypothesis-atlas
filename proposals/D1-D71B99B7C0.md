# Provisional repair: discordant AKI recovery in MIMIC-IV

Status: provisional child of [prior hypothesis]. No cohort counts, fitted models, or clinical results are claimed.

## Hypothesis

Among adults in their first MIMIC-IV ICU stay with a documented creatinine-based AKI episode and observation through a prespecified 48-hour landmark, a creatinine-improved but urine-output-impaired phenotype has worse subsequent in-hospital death and/or persistent or worsening kidney dysfunction than concordant creatinine-and-urine-output recovery. This is a prognostic association, not a causal or mechanistic claim.

The principal rival is measurement/monitoring artifact: creatinine sampling is clinician-selected, urine output is recorded only with a catheter or bedside collection, and fluid balance is a treatment/monitoring summary. The experiment must therefore test incremental information beyond pre-landmark severity and monitoring intensity, and must not infer tubular injury, fluid overload, or treatment benefit.

## Pre-landmark exposure construction

Time origin is ICU intime. The primary landmark is 48 hours after intime. All exposure variables use clinical timestamps at or before the landmark; no post-landmark measurement may enter the exposure. Patients lacking sufficient pre-landmark monitoring are retained in a coverage analysis and excluded only under a prespecified primary eligibility rule, with the exclusion reason reported.

Creatinine: use only blood serum creatinine items from d_labitems with label Creatinine, fluid Blood, category Chemistry. Candidate item IDs are 50912 and 52546. Before fitting, audit each item’s valueuom, numeric completeness, duplicate timestamps, and temporal coverage. The primary rule is to retain item 50912 if it is the dominant, numerically complete serum-creatinine item in the eligible admissions; retain 52546 only if the audit shows it is a valid serum-creatinine item with compatible units and non-overlapping coverage. If neither audit passes, do not silently merge them: report the measurement dependency and use a prespecified sensitivity analysis with the union of valid numeric blood creatinine measurements. Urine, ascites, joint, and pleural creatinine items are excluded.

AKI episode: define a prespecified baseline/reference window before the landmark using only valid blood creatinine values. The primary AKI rule is the KDIGO-style creatinine criterion: a rise of at least 0.3 mg/dL from the minimum pre-landmark baseline within 48 hours, or at least 1.5 times baseline within 7 days, with the 7-day criterion evaluated only when a qualifying baseline and follow-up value exist. The exact baseline window and handling of missing baseline are fixed before outcome analysis; no single post-landmark value defines AKI. If the snapshot lacks a reliable baseline/reference value, the cohort is not silently substituted with an unvalidated AKI label.

Urine output: include only numeric outputevents linked to d_items with linksto=outputevents and unitname mL, using the prespecified Foley/void/condom-catheter/straight-catheter/urine-and-GU-irrigant-out categories. The primary inclusion policy is Foley, void, condom catheter, straight catheter, and urine-and-GU-irrigant-out; suprapubic and nephrostomy outputs are a prespecified sensitivity category, not merged into the primary phenotype. Ureteral-stent items, text/estimated outputs, and non-numeric records are excluded from the primary volume calculation. Sum only values with valid numeric value/valueuom and clinical charttime in the landmark window; record whether monitoring was present and whether output was censored by ICU discharge. Absence of a row is not anuria.

Fluid input: do not calculate fluid balance from all inputevents. The primary fluid-input whitelist is restricted to d_items rows with linksto=inputevents, numeric amount/amountuom, and a prespecified fluid category/unit audit (for example crystalloid/colloid/IV-fluid labels with mL-compatible units). Medications, dialysis replacement/dialysate components, blood products, and non-fluid or text items are excluded from the primary fluid balance. Any retained item must have a documented unit conversion and a starttime/endtime interval; open bags and ambiguous status are handled by a prespecified sensitivity rule, not by imputing missing duration. If the item audit cannot establish a reproducible fluid whitelist, fluid balance is a secondary treatment/monitoring covariate only and is not used to define the exposure.

## Outcomes and leakage control

Primary outcome: in-hospital death through admissions.dischtime/deathtime, with hospital_expire_flag as a secondary consistency check. Follow-up begins at the 48-hour landmark and ends at hospital discharge, death, or a fixed 7-day post-landmark window, whichever occurs first; the exact censoring rule is fixed before analysis.

Secondary kidney outcome: persistent or worsening creatinine after the landmark, defined by a prespecified threshold relative to the landmark value and baseline, plus renal replacement therapy (RRT) if a reproducible RRT definition can be established from available procedure/input/clinical fields. RRT is not inferred from fluid input or creatinine alone. If no reproducible RRT definition is available, report kidney outcome as creatinine-only and preserve RRT as an unresolved dependency.

The primary comparison is creatinine-improved/urine-output-impaired versus concordant recovery. Secondary comparisons are discordance versus creatinine-improved/urine-output-recovered, and monitoring-stratified analyses. All covariates, trajectories, and monitoring indicators are computed only through the landmark.

## Analysis and falsification

Simple baseline: prespecified logistic regression or cause-specific Cox model with age, sex, admission type/location, pre-landmark severity, creatinine value/trajectory, urine-output category, fluid-balance category, and monitoring intensity. It tests whether discordance adds interpretable prognostic information beyond creatinine recovery.

Substantive alternative: a time-aware landmark model using ordered pre-landmark creatinine, urine-output, and fluid-balance trajectories (discrete-time survival or regularized recurrent/functional model), with the same patient split, outcome, calibration, and bootstrap uncertainty evaluation. It can reveal whether sequence and slope matter; it cannot establish mechanism. Compare incremental discrimination, calibration, and uncertainty against the baseline. CPU is appropriate for the baseline; a GPU is optional only if trajectory fitting is materially slower.

Supportive result: a prespecified incremental association for discordance with uncertainty, persistence after monitoring-intensity adjustment, and no material reversal in fixed sensitivity analyses. Adverse result: no incremental association, disappearance after monitoring adjustment, or reversal. An imprecise null is inconclusive. Clinical adjudication of true AKI, recovery, fluid overload, and treatment indication is unavailable and would be required for stronger mechanistic or management claims.
