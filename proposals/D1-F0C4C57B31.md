# A minimax support-atlas audit for domain-safe pulmonary-nodule admission routing

## Decision bottleneck and targeted advance

The clinical estimand is unchanged from `[prior hypothesis]`: in the frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity MIMIC population, can the admission-level `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` routes be reproduced with sufficiently low text-defined unsafe cross-route contamination to nominate only a prospective **silent** validation? Every recommendation unit in a sampled admission is reviewed, one unsafe minority unit controls the admission, and no conclusion concerns guideline correctness, clinician fault, actual safety, follow-up completion, causal benefit, or patient outcomes.

The parent prevents a route from validating itself through split evidence views, sealed atomic labels, independent all-route auditing, locked cross-product route derivation, and adverse finite-population inference. Its remaining consequential threat is **domain masking and unsupported transport**. A pooled `AUTO` contamination bound can pass while a later possible-calendar band or a particular service/workflow signature is unsafe. Conversely, requiring nominally adequate performance in every small subgroup can create an impossible 900-admission design and invite post hoc pooling, hierarchical borrowing, or favorable regrouping.

This targeted child preserves the parent's population, chronology, canonical discharge artifact, exhaustive states, unit clocks, component firewalls, all-unit worst-admission routing, route-independent audit, inherited route estimands and margins, fixed sample-size formulas, finite-population inference, and strict noncausal ceiling. It replaces pooled or loosely subgrouped release logic with a **pre-label support atlas and minimax certificate**:

1. construct conservative possible-calendar, exact terminal-service, and workflow-complexity signatures before any route label;
2. determine, solely from frozen frame counts and the inherited sample budget, which signatures are statistically auditable; rare, unknown, unseen, or unsupported signatures are never pooled into a release claim and prospectively default to `DEFER`;
3. allocate the unchanged within-stratum sample totals across auditable signatures and a residual envelope by deterministic maximin rules, retaining known positive inclusion and pairwise probabilities for every admission;
4. estimate each supported signature's contamination, minority-unit failure, capture, reproducibility, and workload with simultaneous adverse finite-population bounds;
5. let the worst supported signature, not the pooled average or a shrinkage estimate, govern nomination; and
6. bind any retrospective nomination to an explicit prospective bridge in which actual future dates are used, novel signatures are quarantined, and no route is exposed to clinicians.

This is deliberately narrower but more honest than unrestricted “transport.” A favorable result may nominate silent validation only for an explicit allow-list of supported within-MIMIC signatures. It cannot establish forward temporal or external-site transport.

### Unresolved claim

After preserving the parent's leakage-resistant audit, does the route retain acceptable text-defined purity, unsafe-state capture, reproducibility, and review workload in every **supported observable era/service/workflow signature proposed for silent validation**, or does pooled performance conceal a failing signature? Can an explicit support rule identify where retrospective evidence stops, rather than extrapolating into rare, unknown, or novel workflows?

### Falsifiable hypothesis

For at least one prespecified, support-qualified signature, and for every signature included in the proposed allow-list, all inherited pooled gates and the signature-specific gates will hold simultaneously under adverse assignment of cross-lane disagreement, missing review, redaction breach, unknown clock, unit-boundary ambiguity, and domain uncertainty. A supported signature failing any gate falsifies nomination for that signature and cannot be rescued by a favorable pooled estimate. An unsupported, rare, unknown, or novel signature is **not tested** and must remain `DEFER`; it is not evidence for or against stability. If no signature can be supported within the fixed design, the result is honestly `NO_SELECTION` rather than a pooled claim.

## Frozen inherited clinical experiment

The following parent definitions and safeguards are preserved exactly.

### Population and chronology

Before unredacted pulmonary discharge-plan review or route work, stream all radiology and detail rows and freeze extraction code, high-recall lexical/exam rules, included/excluded IDs, and a retrieval-negative audit seed. Candidate reports are exact admission-linked, have `charttime` in `[admittime-12h,D]`, and trigger the inherited pulmonary/nodule/opacity, recommendation, interval, thoracic-CT, or exam-code/name rules.

Two independent eligibility physicians see only the reciprocal radiology chain, permitted admission/death/disposition fields, and a context artifact from which pulmonary target/action/timing/recipient/order/preference spans were independently redacted. Initial labels are immutable; disagreement remains `U_R` and cannot be erased by consensus.

The inferential population `N_A` remains one first eligible admission per subject, sorted by `admittime` then `hadm_id`: adult; exact admission-linked report; incidental pulmonary nodule or indeterminate focal nodular opacity; patient-specific unconditional chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months. Exclude screening, established surveillance, staging/oncologic surveillance, conditional/optional plans, explicit no follow-up, in-hospital death, locked hospice/comfort-only transition, and recommendations first introduced after `D`. Do not infer nodule size, smoking, malignancy risk, life expectancy, recommendation appropriateness, or missing context. Later eligibility concern remains adverse `U_R`; it does not redefine `N_A`.

Report/addendum chains require subject- and admission-consistent reciprocal links: the `AR` parent relation and corresponding `RR` addendum relation must agree. Every operative component requires `charttime<=D` and `storetime<=D`. Missing nodes, one-way or contradictory links, unresolved operative text, or missing/contradictory times remain `U_R`.

A probability sample of deterministic retrieval negatives remains required, stratified by chest-CT exam name/code and pulmonary lexical fragments. Each stratum is sampled to make zero misses yield a simultaneous one-sided 95% finite-population upper bound of 0.5 missed eligible opportunities per 100 negatives, or is censused. Any confirmed miss, or an upper bound implying more than 5% denominator undercoverage relative to estimated `N_A`, blocks every route and requires a new dictionary and new confirmatory sample.

## Outcome-blind domain family and support atlas

Construct the complete domain manifest after `N_A`, canonical keys, clocks, deterministic unit inventories, and inherited sampling strata are frozen, but before sampling or any real component label. Hash code, source row-order convention, parse dictionaries, and every admission's signature. Domains are hidden from reviewers.

### Possible-calendar class

Never use raw `year(admittime)` alone. Parse `anchor_year_group` as inclusive real-year interval `[a_i,b_i]`, and let `q_i=year(admittime)-anchor_year`. Because the exact real anchor year and day mapping are unavailable, define a widened possible admission-year interval

`C_i=[a_i+q_i-1,b_i+q_i+1]`.

Assign:

* `E_EARLY` if `upper(C_i)<=2013`;
* `E_LATE` if `lower(C_i)>=2017`;
* `E_BRIDGE` otherwise; and
* `E_UNKNOWN` for missing, malformed, contradictory, or out-of-catalog data.

This distinguishes nonoverlapping possible early and late bands without claiming exact dates. `E_LATE` labels and outputs remain sealed until the protocol, complement output shell, margins, and signed no-change declaration are frozen. `E_BRIDGE/UNKNOWN` are not reassigned favorably.

### Exact terminal-service and workflow class

For each admission, retain exact `(subject_id,hadm_id)` service rows with nonmissing `transfertime<D`. Sort by `(transfertime,source_row_number)` only for reproducible path construction. The terminal service is the nonmissing `curr_service` at the greatest `transfertime`. Distinct terminal codes tied at the maximum time, absent eligible rows, missing codes, contradictory links, or parser failure give `SERVICE_UNKNOWN`; source row order cannot break a clinical tie. Preserve the exact observed code; do not regroup rare codes after labels.

Define pre-departure workflow:

* `W_SIMPLE`: exactly one valid service row, no distinct service change, and no admission-linked ICU row with `intime<D`;
* `W_COMPLEX`: at least two valid service rows with a distinct `curr_service` transition, or at least one admission-linked ICU row with `intime<D`;
* `W_INTERMEDIATE`: multiple valid rows without a distinct service change and no pre-`D` ICU exposure; and
* `W_UNKNOWN`: missing/contradictory service chronology, contradictory ICU time, or indeterminate status.

The primary **domain atom** is `g=(E, exact terminal curr_service, W)`. `admission_type`, `admission_location`, `discharge_location`, exact service-path sequence, transfer careunit, and ICU first/last careunit are prespecified diagnostic partitions. They cannot redefine a favorable atom or rescue a failure. Service codes are workflow/ownership proxies, not validated responsible teams.

### Deterministic support atlas

A frame atom is initially eligible for certification only if all components are known and its frozen population size is at least `max(30,0.02*N_A)`. This threshold is based only on support and does not imply adequate route events.

Within each inherited noncensus sampling stratum `h`, cross-classify admissions into every initially eligible atom and one `RESIDUAL_h` envelope containing all rare or unknown atoms. Determine feasibility before labels. Each occupied certifiable `(h,g)` cell must receive at least two sampled admissions, and each occupied residual cell at least two, unless censused. If the unchanged `n_h` cannot meet these minima, iteratively remove the smallest candidate atom from the certifiable atlas, tie-breaking lexicographically by `(E,service,W)`, and move it to residual until feasible. No atom can return after labels. Publish initial/final atom counts, removals, `N_hg`, and reasons.

An atom outside the final atlas—including every rare, unknown, or later novel signature—is **unreleasable**, not “negative.” Its records remain in pooled finite-population estimates and residual-envelope safety summaries, but no performance borrowed from residual, a service macro-group, an adjacent era, or a fitted model can certify it.

## Fixed maximin probability sample within 900 reviewed admissions

Retain the five inherited outcome-blind strata exactly: certainty `S_SENTINEL`, then `S_TIMECLOCK`, `S_ZERO`, `S_PARTIAL`, and `S_COMPLETE`. Sentinel criteria, precedence, and the fact that strata are not routes are unchanged.

Retain the parent's total sample sizes:

* census `S_SENTINEL`;
* for `S_ZERO`, take the larger of 300 or the finite-population sample size for worst-case proportion 0.5 and ±4 percentage-point 95% precision, capped at `N_h`;
* for every other noncensus stratum, take the larger of 120 or the corresponding ±7-point sample, capped at `N_h`; and
* `n_h=ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25/{e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`.

The sentinel census plus all formula samples must be at most 900 admissions. If it exceeds 900, obtain resources under a new approved protocol or declare feasibility failure. Never reduce a required sample, omit sentinels, sample units, replace admissions, or adapt to route prevalence, unsafe events, disagreement, response, or workload.

Within each noncensus `h`, allocate its fixed `n_h` across final certifiable cells and `RESIDUAL_h` by integer optimization performed before labels:

1. give every occupied cell `min(2,N_hg)`;
2. among certifiable cells, maximize lexicographically the minimum expected sampled support across atoms, then the minimum temporal margin, exact-service margin, and workflow margin;
3. allocate remaining positions as close as possible to proportional allocation while penalizing any projected normalized maximum weight above 0.10; and
4. break exact ties lexicographically by `(h,E,service,W)`.

The complete objective, constraints, solver/version, and unique solution are sealed. If no allocation can keep every claimable cell's projected normalized maximum weight at or below 0.10, the affected atom remains sampled but cannot be certified.

Within each `(h,g)` or residual cell, draw SRS without replacement using a seed derived from the protocol hash. For admission `i`, `pi_i=1` in census cells and otherwise `pi_i=n_hg/N_hg>0`; `w_i=1/pi_i`. Within a sampled cell, `pi_ij=n_hg(n_hg-1)/[N_hg(N_hg-1)]`; across independently sampled cells, `pi_ij=pi_i*pi_j`. A noncensus cell with `n_hg=1` cannot estimate internal variance and is unsupported. Freeze counts, allocation, seeds, ordered IDs, probabilities, and an explicit no-replacement list.

Every selected admission receives every inherited index, audit, redaction, and minority-unit review. The current expert-review frame is therefore fixed at no more than 900 distinct admissions; multiple component readings are recorded as physician-hours but do not change the admission ceiling.

## Preserved estimands and domain-conditional estimands

The inferential and sampling unit remains the first eligible admission; recommendation units are nested observations. Preserve all inherited pooled totals, burdens, chronology states, route agreement measures, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, `leak_AUTO`, additional-unit rate, deterministic-one-to-multiple error, and workload.

For route `r`, preserve `T_r=sum_i I(R_i=r)` and `B_r=100*T_r/N_A`, estimated by HT total `sum_s I(R_i=r)/pi_i`. Preserve the parent's adverse event definition: `d_i=1` if either initial index lane calls `AUTO`; `x_i=1` when `d_i=1` and the other index lane, any audit-compatible route, minority-unit safety audit, missing audit, redaction/boundary failure, timing limitation, or adverse unknown is compatible with `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER`. Then

`C_AUTO=sum_i x_i/sum_i d_i`,

estimated by the Hájek ratio of HT totals with numerator and denominator always reported. This is text-defined audit contamination, not clinical harm.

For every final atlas atom `g`, estimate the same quantities conditionally using

`T_yg_hat=sum_{i in s} w_i I(G_i=g)y_i`

and Hájek ratios of domain HT totals. Also estimate the atom's design-weighted physician-hours per 100 atom admissions, packet-time quantiles, nonresponse, number of units, certainty-stratum share, maximum weight, normalized maximum weight, and Kish effective sample size for every denominator.

Define atom-level certification vector

`Theta_g={C_AUTO,g,D_MIX,g,leak_AUTO,g,confirmation_g,AC1_g,Se_RECON,g,PPV_RECON,g,PPV_TIMING,g,Se_DEFER,g,workload_g}`.

For a proposed route allow-list `L`, define its minimax safety summaries as the **worst simultaneous confidence endpoint**, not the worst point estimate:

* `M_AUTO(L)=max_{g in L} U95(C_AUTO,g)`;
* `M_LEAK(L)=max_{g in L} U95(leak_AUTO,g)`;
* `m_CONFIRM(L)=min_{g in L} L95(confirmation_g)`;
* analogous minima for capture and maxima for workload.

No population weighting, standardization, hierarchical shrinkage, empirical-Bayes estimate, regression interaction, pooled denominator, or favorable consensus may satisfy a primary atom gate. Pooled estimates and early/late contrasts are required diagnostics, but direct atom-specific absolute margins govern release. A stable difference around an unsafe level does not pass; a noisy difference between two individually safe supported atoms does not by itself fail.

## Finite-population inference, multiplicity, and unknowns

Treat each `(h,g)` and residual cell as a survey stratum. For scalar total `T_y`, use

`V_hat(T_y_hat)=sum_hg N_hg^2*(1-n_hg/N_hg)*s_y,hg^2/n_hg`,

with zero census contribution. For a ratio `R`, use Taylor linearization `z_i=y_i-R_hat*d_i` and divide the corresponding total variance by `T_d_hat^2`. Report numerator and denominator totals; do not report a ratio with zero estimated denominator.

Generate at least 20,000 Rao–Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates independently within every noncensus design cell, retaining all units, views, labels, response states, and workload for an admission together; certainty admissions remain fixed. The same replicate drives pooled, atom, residual-envelope, minimax, diagnostic-domain, and workload quantities. Use a max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over all inherited pooled gates, every final atlas atom's primary route gates, and the minimax endpoints. The late possible-calendar challenge is included in that family. Secondary exact-code/path/admission/disposition diagnostics form a separately adjusted family and cannot rescue primary failure.

For rare or zero events, also invert the exact stratified finite-population distribution. Use hypergeometric inversion for unconditional binary totals and multivariate-hypergeometric enumeration of `{unsafe AUTO,safe AUTO,non-AUTO}` for `C_AUTO`, respecting cell sample counts. Use the widest valid simultaneous bound from exact inversion, bootstrap, and adverse partial identification. Zero sampled unsafe events yields a positive upper bound unless the entire relevant finite population is censused.

No selected admission is replaced. Missing, late, schema-invalid, unreadable, withdrawn, or access-failed reviews; missing safety audit; redaction breach; unknown clock; domain parse failure; service tie; and unit-boundary disagreement remain explicit. For every estimand, optimize all compatible exhaustive route/event assignments for sharp adverse lower and upper endpoints; use linear-fractional optimization for ratios inside each replicate. An index `AUTO` with missing audit evidence is unsafe for the upper bound. Unknown domain membership contributes to every compatible diagnostic domain and to residual, never to a favorable release atom. Complete-case, unweighted, model-assisted, and consensus estimates are diagnostics only.

The first-eligible rule requires unique `subject_id` in `N_A`; a duplicate stops the run rather than inviting a cluster correction. Balance reviewer assignments across era, service, and workflow before labels. Report reviewer-specific response, disagreement, and time, with a secondary reviewer-resampling sensitivity; primary bounds remain design-based and adverse.

## Prespecified certification and nomination gates

Governance must approve margins and the absolute workload budget before any real labels are released; otherwise `NO_SELECTION`. Retain inherited defaults: `tau_AUTO=0.05`, confirmation `rho=0.90`, AC1 `0.70`, `sigma_RECON=0.95`, `pi_RECON=0.80`, `pi_TIMING=0.95`, and `sigma_DEFER=0.95`. These are operational silent-validation prerequisites, not acceptable harm rates.

Every route first must pass all inherited pooled source, retrieval, denominator, chronology, redaction, seal/access, event, effective-sample-size, precision, and adverse-bound gates. In particular retain at least 200 `N_A`, at least 60 `E_A`, overall design ESS at least 150, normalized maximum weight at most 0.10, and simultaneous all-`N_A` burden half-width no greater than 5 per 100. Broken firewalls, absent governance, canonical duplication, or mandatory review above 900 blocks all selection.

An atom `g` is route-claimable only if it is in the final pre-label atlas, all domain fields are known, at least 30 sampled route/reference-positive admissions exist as applicable, at least 10 arise outside the sentinel census, route-denominator ESS is at least 50, normalized maximum weight within the atom is at most 0.10, and the route burden has a positive simultaneous lower bound. Lack of these conditions is unsupported, never a pass.

For each claimable atom:

* **AUTO:** at least 30 sampled candidate-`AUTO` admissions; lower confirmation/raw-agreement bound `>=0.90`; lower AC1 `>=0.70`; upper `C_AUTO<=0.05`; upper `D_MIX<=0.10`; upper `leak_AUTO<=0.02`; no safety-auditor-confirmed unresolved minority unit remains compatible with `AUTO`; and upper workload below the approved absolute budget.
* **RECON:** at least 30 index `RECON` and 30 audit unsafe-content admissions; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `Se_RECON>=0.95`; lower `PPV_RECON>=0.80`; upper `AUTO` leakage among these cases `<=0.02`; and workload pass.
* **TIMING:** at least 30 index `TIMING`; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `PPV_TIMING>=0.95`; no use of an index-only clock when another active unit is under 24 hours; and workload pass.
* **DEFER:** at least 30 independent-audit uncertain-reference admissions; lower confirmation `>=0.90`; lower `Se_DEFER>=0.95`; and no adverse-compatible uncertain admission routed `AUTO`.

The proposed allow-list for a route is exactly the set of pre-label atlas atoms that independently pass. The minimax endpoints over that set must also pass. There is no unrestricted nomination unless every occupied known atom passes and the simultaneous upper population burden of residual/unknown atoms is below 1%; otherwise silent validation must enforce the explicit allow-list and map all other signatures to `DEFER`. At least one atom and a positive lower bound on its route burden are required; an empty allow-list is `NO_SELECTION`.

Two-lane silent validation requires atom-level passes for both `AUTO` and `RECON`. A `RECON`-only allow-list may be nominated if its atoms pass while `AUTO` fails. A pooled pass cannot rescue a supported failing atom; a passing neighboring atom cannot rescue an unsupported one.

## No-selection rules

After protocol hash and before interpretation, prohibit:

1. changing population, first-eligible choice, `D`, reciprocal chains, canonical `DS`, unit schema, clocks, states, views, matching, route precedence, estimands, margins, or gates;
2. regrouping exact service codes, moving a rare/unknown atom into the atlas, changing calendar cutoffs, or redefining workflow after labels;
3. route-, event-, disagreement-, response-, workload-, or result-based top-ups, replacements, stopping, atom merging, or sample reallocation;
4. restricting audit to `AUTO`, sentinels, omissions, or apparent difficult cases;
5. sampling or weighting recommendation units;
6. using index evidence to repair audit labels or audit evidence to repair index labels;
7. favorable unit matching, consensus deletion, complete-case deletion, response weighting, or model imputation in a primary gate;
8. certifying a rare, residual, unknown, absent, or newly observed signature through pooling, adjacency, standardization, shrinkage, or clinical intuition; and
9. claiming forward or external transport from shifted dates or one-site retrospective text.

Any affected route/atom is invalidated. Data may motivate a new child, but cannot be silently reused as untouched confirmation.

## Falsification and adversarial verification

1. Recompute source/schema hashes, row counts, `N_A`, unique subjects, reciprocal chains, clocks, exhaustive states, domains, atlas, sample allocation, `pi_i`, `pi_ij`, and ordered draw from sealed inputs. Unexplained drift stops analysis.
2. Shifted-date trap: two subjects with the same raw shifted year but different `anchor_year_group` must follow their widened possible intervals. Boundary-crossing intervals must be `E_BRIDGE`; malformed values must be unknown.
3. Service trap: post-`D` rows cannot alter workflow; tied distinct terminal codes, missing times/codes, and absent rows become unknown. Source order cannot resolve a tie.
4. Atlas trap: create many small atoms. The deterministic support-pruning algorithm must move atoms to residual before labels and never merge them after seeing outcomes.
5. Allocation trap: rerun the integer optimizer and SRS from hashes/seeds. Processing order or labels cannot change cells, sample sizes, or IDs.
6. Positivity trap: every admission, including residual/unknown, must have `pi_i>0`; pairwise probabilities and no-replacement behavior must match the declared design.
7. Domain-masking trap: place unsafe `AUTO` events only in one supported late/service/workflow atom at a rate that leaves pooled `C_AUTO<0.05` but atom `C_AUTO>0.05`. Pooled analysis may pass; that atom and unrestricted nomination must fail.
8. Rare-domain trap: put all failures in a rare residual atom. The experiment may estimate a residual burden, but must never certify that atom or let a macro-group pass expose it to `AUTO`.
9. Shrinkage trap: make an unsupported atom's model estimate favorable through borrowing. The model result cannot alter certification.
10. Workload trap: keep pooled hours acceptable while doubling one atom's workload. That atom must fail without contaminating evidence for independently passing atoms.
11. Unknown trap: concentrate missing service/date/clock/audit/boundary evidence in high-weight unsafe admissions. Adverse bounds must block favorable certification.
12. Zero-event trap: zero unsafe events in a noncensus atom must yield a positive finite-population upper bound.
13. Multiplicity trap: nominal atom intervals may pass while simultaneous intervals fail; only simultaneous adverse limits govern.
14. Minority-unit trap: nine pure omissions plus one conflict must be `A_MIXED` within `RECON`; duplicating safe units changes neither route nor weight.
15. Differential-verification trap: every apparent route receives identical audit components and safety review.
16. Leakage/incorporation traps: forbidden packet canaries, shared pseudonyms, altered append-only labels, route-before-seal execution, or index-informed audit adjudication must fail controls and leave primary results unchanged or invalidate the run.
17. Nonresponse trap: concentrate missing labels among unsafe high-weight cases; favorable complete-case estimates cannot pass adverse bounds.
18. Finite-population trap: small synthetic populations with unequal cell fractions must recover known HT totals and nominal exact/simulation coverage; unweighted estimates should fail.
19. Prospective-signature trap: a novel service code or workflow combination must map to `DEFER`, never to its nearest retrospective atom.
20. Claim checker rejects exact-calendar trend, external-site generalizability, guideline appropriateness, clinician awareness/failure, live safety, causal benefit, follow-up completion, or patient outcome language.

The verifier can mechanically check sources, joins, times, domain/atlas code, fixed allocation, probabilities, packet/access separation, seals, route code, finite-population estimators, exact/bootstrap bounds, adverse assignments, minimax gates, allow-list mapping, and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish clinical truth, service ownership, appropriateness, utility margins, actual viewing/editing/communication, external transport, or benefit.

## Interpretation

**Supportive** means the inherited pooled audit passes and at least one support-qualified atom independently satisfies every relevant simultaneous adverse route and workload gate; all atoms in the stated allow-list pass, and minimax endpoints pass. This supports only a prospective silent test of the frozen route process in those exact observable signatures, with all other signatures forced to `DEFER`.

**Adverse** means a supported atom has adequately precise contamination, leakage, missed unsafe/uncertain states, poor reproducibility, minority-unit failure, or excessive workload beyond a gate. That atom is excluded even if pooled performance is favorable. A domain-specific negative result is consequential because it prevents a pooled average from licensing an unsafe workflow.

**Inconclusive** includes no feasible atlas within 900 admissions, sparse route events, poor atom ESS, wide simultaneous bounds, excessive unknown/residual burden, retrieval undercoverage, nonresponse, broken seals, unapproved workload budget, or unavailable exact calendar mapping. Unsupported is not equivalent to adverse, but it still cannot be released. Failure to reject heterogeneity is not evidence of transport.

## Executable prospective actual-calendar silent bridge and evidence ceiling

The retrospective result is only a nomination. It does not enroll patients, expose a route, or authorize a clinical change. A separately approved bridge must be executable without borrowing either retrospective shifted dates or retrospective labels. The bridge has the following locked protocol; a later site protocol may add safeguards, but may not relax these definitions and still call itself this bridge.

### Enrollment frame and calendar window

Choose the participating hospital and the first Monday after governance signs the protocol hash. The enrollment window is the next **26 consecutive ISO weeks (182 consecutive 24-hour days)**, with no holiday, service, volume, or result-based pauses. The frame is every inpatient discharge from the participating hospital whose physical departure timestamp `D_p` falls in `[00:00 Monday week 1, 24:00 Sunday week 26]`, including readmissions as separate discharges and regardless of whether a route fires. Adult means age `>=18` on `D_p`, determined from the site's immutable registration date-of-birth/age field; an unknown adult status is an explicit `UNKNOWN_ADULT`, retained in the denominator and not silently excluded. A discharge is enrolled when the discharge ledger records `D_p`; late data cannot backdate enrollment.

Every consecutive adult discharge enters a census execution ledger with a bridge identifier, pseudonymized admission/discharge key, calendar week, terminal service and workflow signature, report-finalization timestamps, discharge-artifact version/hash, route execution status, and all subsequent observation states. The primary bridge population is this complete frame. The route-eligible subset is computed by the already frozen pulmonary retrieval, reciprocal-chain, unit, clock, and eligibility rules using only information finalized by `D_p`; no retrospective first-eligible substitution is made in the bridge, and no discharge is removed because a route is absent. If a site cannot produce a complete consecutive-discharge ledger, the bridge is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not a convenience sample.

The retrospective `E` possible-calendar class is not applied to actual dates. For descriptive comparison only, use the actual ISO discharge date and prespecify one calendar contrast before enrollment: weeks 1–13 versus weeks 14–26. A bridge signature is eligible for a claimed retrospective atom only when the site has a signed field dictionary showing semantic equivalence for exact terminal service, workflow inputs, report finalization, and discharge artifact versioning. A changed service code, unavailable field, changed workflow meaning, or novel exact `(calendar-band, terminal-service, workflow)` combination is `BRIDGE_UNKNOWN` and maps to `DEFER`; nearest-code, macro-service, and model-based mapping are forbidden.

### Sealed execution before clinician action

At each discharge, an automated process snapshots the final eligible radiology chain and final discharge artifact at the first timestamp at which both are immutable and available to the bridge, and records `T_snap`. If an addendum is signed before physical departure, it is part of that snapshot; an addendum after `T_snap` is a later observation and cannot revise the departure route. The frozen extractor creates the complete unit inventory and all four routes at `T_route`, signs a content hash, and appends it to an append-only ledger. `T_route` must be before the earliest target-linked clinician order, referral, scheduling action, patient instruction transmission, or other protocol-defined clinician action after `T_snap`. The system has no user interface, alert, inbox placement, order-writing permission, recommendation field, or API write-back. Clinicians and patients are not told the route during enrollment.

A monitor checks clocks against an independently sourced event log. If `T_route` is missing, after an action, based on a mutable artifact, or unverifiable because of clock disagreement, the execution is `ROUTE_EXECUTION_UNKNOWN`; it remains in the census and is adverse-compatible with the unsafe state for the relevant retrospective gate. It is never re-run to obtain a favorable timestamp. The sealed route and its hash are released to reviewers only after the action lock and the predeclared review packets are sealed. Emergency care is never delayed, changed, or withheld by this study.

### Prospective labels: documentation is not actual care

Two independent, roster-disjoint reviewers receive the same route-blinded packets and the same two lanes used retrospectively. They label target-linked documentation in the artifact as atomic `target`, `action/modality`, `due interval/date`, `recipient/responsibility`, `polarity`, replacement/conflict, and missing/unknown. A plan written in a discharge document is a **documentation** observation only. It is not evidence that anyone saw it, agreed with it, transmitted it, ordered it, scheduled it, completed it, or that it was clinically appropriate.

A separate, pre-specified workflow ledger records **actual-care** observations without using the silent route to prompt them: artifact signed/transmitted and to whom; order/referral placed; responsible service acknowledgement; scheduling offer and appointment; patient-facing communication; completion or cancellation; and any documented competing plan. Each is timestamped and source-tagged. Actual-care observations are never fed back into route construction or used to repair a target-linked documentation label. A clinician's later correction, acceptance, or dismissal is an actual-care event and a descriptive endpoint, not a correctness label. Expert adjudication of appropriateness, responsibility, communication sufficiency, or whether an outside action truly occurred is explicitly outside the automatic bridge claim.

### Fixed audit sample, allocation, and workload ledger

Route computation is a census. Independent audit uses a fixed, route-blind sample selected immediately after the adult-discharge ledger closes each week by protocol-hash-keyed randomization of ledger sequence number. Selection uses only week, discharge sequence number, and pre-route administrative exact terminal-service/workflow cells; it cannot use route, route-positive status, documentation content, reviewer response, outcome, or workload. Before enrollment, the protocol fixes the weekly target

`q_w = 20` for weeks 1–4, `q_w = 23` for weeks 5–12, and `q_w = 24` for weeks 13–26,

so `4×20 + 8×23 + 14×24 = 600` exactly. For week `w`, after its complete adult-discharge ledger closes with `N_w` discharges, the realized sample size is the sole predeclared census cap `n_w=min(q_w,N_w)`. Thus the bridge selects at most 600 discharges; a short week reduces the realized total, including in week 26, and no unfilled slot is transferred to a later week, recovered by extending enrollment, or filled after any route, label, response, outcome, or workload is seen. If `N_w>0`, every discharge in that week has strictly positive first-order inclusion probability; if `N_w<=q_w`, the week is a census with probability one. All selected discharges are assigned every route, documentation, redaction, timing, and minority-unit audit, including route-negative and apparently easy cases; an uncompleted mandated audit is retained as missing and assigned adversely rather than replaced.

Within each week, let `N_wh` be the frozen count in exact pre-route terminal-service/workflow cell `h`. A protocol-specified dependent-randomized rounding step assigns integer cell counts `n_wh` satisfying `sum_h n_wh=n_w` and `n_wh` equal to either `floor(n_w N_wh/N_w)` or `ceil(n_w N_wh/N_w)` (with the protocol-keyed draw resolving the required remainders); selection within each cell is without replacement by the protocol-keyed permutation. This gives route-blind balance on the exact service/workflow cells to within one discharge while giving even a cell with a proportional target below one a prespecified positive chance of receiving a slot rather than deterministically excluding it. Marginal first-order inclusion probabilities are strictly positive for every discharge in every nonempty week; exact first- and second-order probabilities under the locked rounding-plus-within-cell design, including any zero second-order probabilities induced by a one-slot cell, are computed from frozen `N_wh`, algorithm version, and key and recorded before packets open. Cells are never merged or redefined after labels. The full frame, cell counts, weekly and cell target counts, ordered IDs, protocol key commitment, inclusion and pairwise probabilities, and no-replacement selections are sealed before any audit packet is opened. Survey-weighted bridge estimands use those design probabilities and the realized sample total `n=sum_w n_w<=600`; the census execution ledger is separately reported as a process census. No top-up, replacement, label-responsive stopping, cross-week transfer, or reallocation for sparse route-positive or adverse cells is allowed.

Governance sets an absolute bridge workload budget before enrollment: at most **600 selected discharges, 1,200 physician-hours, and 26 calendar weeks**. The ledger records per selected discharge reviewer identity, lane, start/stop time, packet version, number of operative units, disagreement, nonresponse, senior minority-unit review, and adjudication time. Physician-hours are summed from actual immutable timestamps and include both lanes and senior review; packet-time quantiles and hours per 100 selected discharges are reported by exact claimed atom. If completing mandated review would cross 1,200 physician-hours, the bridge is fail-closed `INCONCLUSIVE`: work may stop at the signed safety/budget boundary, but every resulting unreviewed selected discharge remains in its design denominator as missing/adverse-compatible. The cap cannot be met by dropping, replacing, or de-selecting difficult cases, and no protocol-mandated review may increase `n` above 600.

The bridge has a prespecified accrual sufficiency requirement, not an adaptive sampling rule. In the realized design-weighted audit of `n=sum_w min(q_w,N_w)<=600` selected discharges, there must be at least **100 actually reviewed route-positive admissions overall and at least 30 actually reviewed route-positive admissions in every claimed retrospective atom**, with at least 10 per atom sampled in weeks 5–26, and at least 30 actually reviewed route-negative admissions overall. These are unweighted event-count minima in addition to positive design-weighted atom denominators and ESS requirements; missing or incomplete audits count toward none of them. Failure to meet any requirement after week 26, whether because `n<600`, route events are sparse, an audit is incomplete, or the physician-hour boundary is reached, is `INCONCLUSIVE`; it is not evidence of route purity and no favorable atom is retained. If this fixed at-most-600 selection cannot yield the counts, a new separately approved bridge may be proposed, but this bridge cannot be extended, topped up, or reallocated.

### Endpoints, observation windows, and censoring

The primary endpoint is the parent-defined, route-independent, adverse text-audit vector among route-eligible admissions in the realized selected sample `n=sum_w min(q_w,N_w)<=600`: `C_AUTO`, `leak_AUTO`, mixed/adverse-unit rate, reproducibility/confirmation, `Se_RECON`, `PPV_RECON`, `PPV_TIMING`, `Se_DEFER`, redaction and clock failures, and workload. Its finite-frame estimands use the locked first-order weights over that realized `n`; unselected discharges remain represented through the design weights, while selected admissions with missing or incomplete review remain in denominators under inherited adverse partial identification. It is estimated with the same all-unit worst-admission logic, design weights, missing-label partial identification, exact/finite-population bounds, and simultaneous 95% family as the retrospective experiment. The primary bridge gate is the same atom-specific minimax gate; the route must pass on actual-calendar weeks 1–26 and in the prespecified half-window diagnostic without a claim of trend or transport.

Secondary endpoints are explicitly separated by time and construct. At `T_snap`/physical departure, report target-linked documentation presence, atomic agreement, artifact finalization, responsibility field, and route execution timing. By **7 days after `D_p`**, record whether a target-linked order/referral or documented communication occurred. By **30 days**, record scheduling offer/appointment and documented clinician acknowledgement. By **180 days**, record completion, cancellation, replacement, competing plan, and patient death where observable. These are descriptive actual-care process endpoints, not outcomes caused by the silent route. The 7-, 30-, and 180-day windows are anchored to `D_p` and use event timestamps, not note entry time alone.

A missing field or missing source feed is `UNKNOWN`, not a negative. An unobservable outside-system order, appointment, communication, or completion is `OUTSIDE_CARE/UNOBSERVED`; it is not imputed to completion or noncompletion. Death before a window's endpoint is an absorbing `DEATH_BEFORE_WINDOW` competing state; it is reported separately and is not treated as successful completion, missingness, or route failure. Discharge to hospice/comfort care, transfer to another facility, withdrawal, and readmission are recorded as distinct disposition/competing states. Administrative loss of contact is `CENSORED_AT_LAST_OBSERVATION`. For safety-oriented text-audit gates, unknown, outside-care, and unverifiable execution are assigned adversely over all compatible states; for descriptive actual-care proportions, report numerator, denominator, censoring/outside-care/death counts and bounds under best- and worst-compatible assignments. No inverse-probability-of-censoring, imputation, or complete-case result can satisfy a primary gate unless a separate protocol and validated observation model are approved in advance.

### Baselines and interpretation gates

The identical bridge sample, packets, weights, and uncertainty are applied to: (1) the frozen retrospective route code; (2) a route-free documentation baseline that reports whether a target-linked plan exists in the discharge artifact; (3) a predeclared simple lexical/exam baseline; and (4) the contemporaneous usual-care process ledger, described without intervention. A clinician-confirmed review is the independent reference, not a treatment comparator. Differences are descriptive agreement/process contrasts; because the route is silent and not randomized or displayed, no baseline comparison estimates benefit, safety, or causal effect.

Before enrollment, governance must sign all gates and the claim shell. A **supportive bridge** requires a complete consecutive-discharge ledger, valid pre-action seals, no unresolved data-access or role-firewall breach, the same inherited pooled gates, every claimed atom's simultaneous adverse bounds within its inherited margins, positive atom denominator/ESS, all sufficiency counts, no execution-after-action failures, and workload within budget. It supports only a second, separately governed silent study or a protocol review for a later interventional study in the exact passed signatures; it does not support deployment or transport.

An **adverse bridge** is any supported atom exceeding a contamination, leakage, missed-uncertain-state, reproducibility, timing, or workload margin, or any reproducible breach showing route execution could precede clinician action. That atom is barred even if pooled or documentation-only baselines pass. An **inconclusive bridge** includes sparse route events, insufficient atom counts, incomplete consecutive frame, wide simultaneous bounds, censoring/outside-care that prevents the declared endpoint, unknown semantic mapping, broken seals, or missing governance. Inconclusive is neither safety nor failure; it still forbids release. A novel or unsupported signature is always `DEFER`, never adverse evidence against a known atom and never a pass by analogy.

### Bridge falsification tests and evidence ceiling

The prospective verifier must replay synthetic fixtures and the actual append-only ledger before unblinding interpretations. It must demonstrate that: (i) an event timestamp just before an action passes while one just after it is `ROUTE_EXECUTION_UNKNOWN`; (ii) a late addendum cannot revise the already sealed route; (iii) duplicate or missing discharge-ledger entries invalidate consecutiveness; (iv) the deterministic weekly targets reproduce `4×20+8×23+14×24=600`, every realized `n_w=min(q_w,N_w)`, and `n=sum_w n_w<=600`; (v) protocol-keyed balanced sampling reproduces exact first- and second-order inclusion probabilities without route-conditioned top-ups, and every discharge in a nonempty week has positive first-order probability; (vi) a short or late week loses unfilled slots without transfer, extension, replacement, or denominator inflation; (vii) a failing week in weeks 5–26 or exact service/workflow atom blocks that atom despite a passing pooled result; (viii) a novel service code maps to `DEFER`; (ix) target-linked documentation without an order, transmission, scheduling, or completion is not counted as actual care; (x) outside-care, death, and censoring remain distinct and adverse-compatible for safety gates; (xi) reviewer nonresponse, redaction breach, missing audit, or a 1,200-hour stop cannot improve a bound or a sufficiency count; (xii) route-negative and easy cases receive the same audit assignment; and (xiii) changing only actual-care outcomes cannot change the sealed route or primary text-audit labels.

The verifier can check the consecutive calendar frame, timestamps, hashes, packet isolation, selection probabilities, route code, documentation/actual-care separation, missingness states, finite-population estimators, simultaneous bounds, gates, and whether the written conclusion follows from outputs. It cannot establish that a documented plan was appropriate, that a clinician saw or acted on it, that an outside event occurred, that the route is safe, or that an intervention would improve outcomes. Those claims require clinical adjudication, validated cross-system outcome capture, and a separately governed randomized or otherwise causal study.

Only a bridge satisfying its fail-closed gates can justify considering such a later study. Even then, the evidence ceiling remains strict: retrospective shifted-date MIMIC text plus this actual-calendar silent bridge can nominate a bounded workflow validation, not establish guideline correctness, clinical appropriateness, safety, benefit, follow-up completion, clinician awareness/failure, or transport to another date, service, institution, or patient population. A later interventional study must separately measure clinician action, patient preferences and competing risks, communication and responsibility, order completion, harms, and patient-centered outcomes.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The parent already supplies a leakage-resistant, support-atlas retrospective audit. This child converts its previously “separately resourced” bridge into a calendar-locked operating protocol: an exact 26-week consecutive adult-discharge frame; deterministic weekly targets of 20 for weeks 1–4, 23 for weeks 5–12, and 24 for weeks 13–26 (`600` planned exactly), with week-specific census caps yielding a realized route-blind audit `n<=600`; sealed positive first-order and exact second-order design probabilities; and a 1,200-hour ledger. Route execution remains sealed before any clinician action; target-linked documentation remains separate from actual care; endpoint windows remain departure, 7, 30, and 180 days; and unknown, outside-care, death, censoring, incomplete audit, and budget exhaustion remain fail-closed. The repair adds explicit no-transfer/no-top-up handling for short and late weeks and binds sufficiency counts and weighted estimands to the realized `n`, while preserving the frozen MIMIC population, exact source bindings, chronology, canonical `DS`, exhaustive states, support-atlas atomization, all-unit adverse route logic, retrospective workload ceiling of <=900 admissions, and noncausal ceiling.

The changed claim is narrower and falsifiable: a passing retrospective allow-list must survive this exact actual-calendar silent process in the same semantically verified signatures before anyone may design a later intervention. A failure blocks the affected signature; sparse or unobservable evidence blocks release without being labeled adverse. Remaining uncertainty includes imperfect semantic equivalence across sites, incomplete outside-care capture, correlated reviewer error, whether documentation predicts meaningful care, and the absence of clinical appropriateness or patient-outcome adjudication. Therefore even a complete bridge pass cannot be called clinical truth, safety, benefit, or transport.
