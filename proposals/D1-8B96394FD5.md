# Working proposal (Lead) — MIMIC-IV island, episode 2

Candidate origin: expert seed `[starting question]` ("Asynchronous organ recovery"), developed and sharpened by the Lead.
Status: design frozen for compilation; data bindings verified against the dataset catalog; dataset rows NOT yet analysed (discovery budget).

## 1. Unresolved question

After circulatory stabilization in sepsis/septic shock, some patients recover kidneys and lungs together, while others recover one bed while the other stays impaired ("asynchronous organ recovery"). Does the *cross-organ asynchrony* of recovery at a pre-specified decision time carry prognostic information beyond the single-snapshot severity of the patient at that same decision time?

## 2. Observed vs conjectured

Observed evidence (inspected):
- Sepsis is operationalized by a *static* 2-point SOFA increase and septic shock by vasopressor need + lactate > 2 mmol/L [K3]. Static snapshots define the syndrome but say nothing about recovery asynchrony.
- Recovery has its own dynamics: WBC decay and delayed platelet recovery define a universal inflammatory-recovery trajectory that predicts adverse outcomes across trauma, ischaemia and infection [K1]. This supports "recovery is an informative dynamic process", not shown at organ level in ICU sepsis.
- Trajectory modelling in 47,936 Sepsis-3 ICU patients finds rapid/slow/deterioration latent classes and beats static severity scores for deterioration prediction [K2]. This is the closest prior work: it establishes trajectory classes but does not test whether *asynchrony between two organs* adds information over a snapshot.

Conjecture to test (currently unsupported): asynchrony is not just "sicker at t*" — it is a distinct, predictive state.

## 3. Clinical importance / what changes if resolved

If asynchrony predicts deterioration independent of snapshot severity, the bedside decision "this patient's numbers look better, can we de-escalate?" would need to weigh which organs recovered, not only the total score. If it does not, de-escalation can safely continue to be snapshot-driven, and the expert seed's premise is refuted — a useful negative result that prevents costly trajectory-monitoring interventions.

## 4. Population and temporal boundaries (MIMIC-IV 3.1)

Source archive: `baidu_downloads/eicu_mimic/mimic数据库/mimic-iv-3.1.zip` ([source checksum]).
- Base cohort: adults (anchor_age >= 18, `mimic-iv-3.1/hosp/patients.csv.gz`) with an ICU stay (`icu/icustays.csv.gz`; keys `subject_id`,`hadm_id`,`stay_id`; time fields `intime`,`outtime`,`los`) and hospital admission (`hosp/admissions.csv.gz`; `admittime`,`dischtime`,`deathtime`,`hospital_expire_flag`).
- Infection/sepsis anchor: suspected infection from `hosp/microbiologyevents.csv.gz` (`charttime`,`spec_type_desc`,`org_name`) paired with antimicrobial `hosp/prescriptions.csv.gz` (`starttime`,`drug`) or `hosp/emar.csv.gz`; Sepsis-3 shock anchor from vasopressors in `icu/inputevents.csv.gz` (`starttime`,`endtime`,`itemid`,`rate`,`rateuom`; vasopressor itemids via `icu/d_items.csv.gz`).
- Index time t* (decision time): first timestamp within 72 h of ICU `intime` meeting ALL of: MAP >= 65 mmHg sustained 2 h (`icu/chartevents.csv.gz` itemid 220052/220181), no vasopressor dose increase over the preceding 4 h and dose at/below a low norepinephrine-equivalent threshold, and lactate fallen (<=2.0 mmol/L or >=20% below the pre-t* peak; `hosp/labevents.csv.gz` itemid 50813 via `hosp/d_labitems.csv.gz`).
- Follow-up window: 7 days from t*, censored at `outtime`/`dischtime`/death.

## 5. Exposure and comparison

At t*+24 h classify each organ axis as recovered / not recovered:
- Renal: creatinine (`labevents` itemid 50912) at t*+24 h <= 1.25x pre-t* minimum, AND urine output >= 0.5 mL/kg/h over (t*, t*+24 h] (`icu/outputevents.csv.gz` urine itemids; `patientweight` from `inputevents`/`procedureevents`).
- Respiratory: PaO2/FiO2 >= 300 or SpO2/FiO2 >= 315 at t*+24 h, or liberation from invasive ventilation (`icu/procedureevents.csv.gz` ventilation itemids; chartevents SpO2 itemid 220277, FiO2 223835).
Asynchrony group = exactly one axis recovered. Reference groups = both recovered; sensitivity = neither recovered.

## 6. Outcomes

Primary composite within 7 days of t*: deterioration = death (`admissions.deathtime`/`hospital_expire_flag`), vasopressor re-initiation/escalation after de-escalation (`inputevents`), new renal replacement therapy (`procedureevents`/`chartevents` dialysis itemids), or reintubation/invasive ventilation after liberation.
Secondary: organ-support-free days to day 7; 28-day mortality.

## 7. Baselines and the substantive alternative

- Baseline (simple, interpretable): snapshot severity at t* only — SOFA(t*), lactate(t*), vasopressor dose(t*), age, comorbidity/ICD count (`hosp/diagnoses_icd.csv.gz`) — as a logistic/Cox model.
- Alternative (mechanistic/dynamic): add the two-axis recovery state and asynchrony indicator, estimated with time-varying covariate Cox or a small neural/GRU sequence model over longitudinal vitals+labs, fitted with the same inputs and split.
- Discriminating question: does the dynamic model reveal information the snapshot loses — i.e., does asynchrony add discrimination/calibration beyond SOFA(t*)? Simple methods are retained as the primary falsification baseline; complexity is only justified if it tests that question.

## 8. Analysis

Temporal split: fit on 2008-2016 admissions, hold out 2017-2019 by `admittime`. Multivariable Cox/logistic with prespecified covariates; report adjusted HR and AUROC/calibration with bootstrap CIs; incremental value by delta-AUROC and likelihood-ratio test vs the snapshot-only baseline. Prespecify negative control: repeat using a randomly permuted asynchrony label (must show no incremental value).

## 9. Falsification criteria

- Supportive: asynchrony associated with deterioration (adjusted HR > 1, CI excluding 1) AND incremental AUROC > baseline with LR p < 0.05, robust to measurement-frequency adjustment.
- Adverse/refuting: association vanishes after adjustment for SOFA(t*) and vasopressor dose, or incremental value ~0 with CI excluding a clinically meaningful gain -> seed premise not supported.
- Inconclusive: too few patients meet the stabilization criterion, or measurement selection (lactate/creatinine only in monitored patients) is confounded with recovery status; report as inconclusive, do not rescue with post hoc definitions.

## 10. Limitations / required adjudication

- This is a retrospective association; it does NOT identify a mechanism, and Sepsis-3 [K3] is a definition, not evidence of causal organ interaction.
- Measurement is selective (labs ordered when clinicians are worried) — a rival explanation for asynchrony that must be adjusted for (measurement-frequency covariate) or reported as residual confounding.
- No physiological mechanism (e.g., microcirculatory) is measured; K1's inflammatory dynamics [K1] are an analogy, not proof of organ-level asynchrony.
- Clinical uptake (de-escalation decision change) requires prospective/expert review, not this dataset.

## 11. Method alternatives not chosen and why

- Full target-trial emulation of de-escalation: not chosen because the estimand here is prognostic, not causal; documented for a future episode.
- Complex deep sequence models: deferred as the primary analysis to protect interpretability; included as a bounded alternative for the asynchrony-vs-snapshot discrimination question.
