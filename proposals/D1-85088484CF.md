# Executable POD2 ordered-recovery experiment for recorded invasive fluid intervention

Status: substantive executable child of [prior hypothesis]. The population, 48-hour landmark, 2018–2025 era, and full-census 300-event context-supported recorded-intervention endpoint are unchanged. No outcome model has been fitted and no predictive result is claimed.

## Clinical opening, evidence boundary, and hypothesis

At postoperative hour 48 after a first recorded HCC liver resection, clinicians must decide whether the early course warrants intensified respiratory/fluid review and imaging. Intervention-requiring pleural or abdominal collections are consequential after hepatectomy: Wang et al. included thoracentesis and abdominal drainage in a clinician-adjudicated 30-day complication composite [K1], Sakamoto et al. defined clinically relevant post-hepatectomy pleural effusion by thoracentesis or thoracic drainage [K2], and Clavien–Dindo grading treats invasive intervention as grade III [K3]. These works do not validate this extract’s codes as unplanned complications.

Strongest claim already supported: the executed full-census audit found 300 of 326 candidate procedures with independent postoperative examination, procedure-specific order, or treatment-course context after excluding seven unrelated/ambiguous transhepatic-biliary records, three pre-existing-fluid-only records, and sixteen procedure-only unresolved records. This supports a reproducible phenotype of context-supported recorded postoperative invasive pleural/abdominal fluid intervention. It does not establish unplanned intent, causation by hepatectomy, chronicity, or Clavien–Dindo status.

Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis: among adults still hospitalized and free of the recorded endpoint at 48 hours after their first recorded HCC liver resection, prespecified ordered summaries of 0–48-hour oncotic/synthetic, inflammatory/injury, and renal/fluid-handling recovery improve prediction of first context-supported recorded invasive pleural/abdominal fluid intervention in (48,336] hours beyond a transparent latest-POD2-state model.

This is a prognostic temporal-information hypothesis. It is not a causal claim about albumin, diuretics, inflammation, fluid management, or a biological mechanism.

Clinical advance: a supportive result would identify a compact, auditable POD2 recovery phenotype worth prospective validation and clinical adjudication. An adverse result would favor a simpler latest-state model and abandon the ordering claim for this endpoint. An inconclusive result would identify sparse repeated measurement, observation practice, drift, or endpoint interpretation as the next bottleneck. The advance over [K1–K3] is the locked landmark, a context-corroborated timestamp-native endpoint, and a direct nested test of ordered history versus current state.

## Common preprocessing and leakage controls

Only records with times in [T0,T0+48h] may form predictors. Duplicate same-label/same-time numeric labs are median-collapsed after counting duplicates. Values are parsed only from quantitative result. For each exact label, development-era 1st/99th percentiles winsorize values, then development-era median/IQR standardize them; zero IQR drops the channel with a logged reason. No unit conversion, cross-label pooling, or forward fill is permitted.

Common static inputs are centered age, sex, log1p valid operation duration plus missing flag, centered operation year, laparoscopic lexical flag, and coarse major-resection flag (hemihepatectomy, extended/major, or at least three named segments). Common observation/treatment inputs are each primary lab’s latest 24–48h missingness and hours since latest value; three domain-specific 0–48h measurement counts plus total unique lab timestamps; and, for each five medication groups, presence and first-start hour. Missing standardized lab values are set to zero after adding missingness; missing first-start hour is zero after adding presence. These rules are frozen before any 2024–2025 outcome is read.

## Same-split model ladder

### B0: transparent latest-state baseline

Fit an unweighted ridge logistic regression with an unpenalized intercept to the common static/observation/treatment inputs and the latest standardized value in [24,48h] for each of the twelve exact primary labs. No data-driven interaction or feature selection is allowed. Report every coefficient on the standardized scale.

Choose ridge lambda from {0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100} using leave-one-calendar-year-out folds within 2018–2021, minimizing mean validation Brier score and choosing the largest lambda within one standard error of the minimum. This uses 73 development events but a fixed feature set and shrinkage rather than unstable stepwise selection. After all architecture choices are frozen, refit on 2018–2022 (106 events).

### T1: prespecified low-dimensional ordered trajectory-summary model

T1 nests B0 and adds exactly twelve prespecified features; it cannot drop or change the B0 block.

Create four half-open 12-hour bins [0,12), [12,24), [24,36), [36,48], using the last value per exact assay per bin. Positive values always mean more adverse. Define three equal-weight domain scores when at least two component assays are observed in a bin:

- oncotic/synthetic O: mean of −albumin, +international normalized ratio, +total bilirubin;
- inflammatory/injury I: mean of +Neutrophil Count, −Lymphocyte Count, +Procalcitonin, +Alanine Aminotransferase, +Aspartate Aminotransferase;
- renal/fluid-handling R: mean of +creatinine, +urea, and absolute standardized deviation of sodium from the development median.

For each domain d, add: delta_d, the mean score in evaluable late bins [24,48] minus the mean score in evaluable early bins [0,24]; history_d, the time-weighted mean across all evaluable bins minus the last evaluable domain score; and available_d, indicating that at least one early and one late bin exist with at least 12 hours span. Unavailable delta/history values are zero after adding available_d. Add three coupling terms max(delta_O,0)×max(delta_I,0), max(delta_O,0)×max(delta_R,0), and max(delta_I,0)×max(delta_R,0). Thus the added block is 3 deltas + 3 histories + 3 availability flags + 3 coupling terms.

Fit joint ridge logistic regression. Hold the B0 penalty at its development-selected value and select a separate trajectory-block lambda from the same six-value grid by the same leave-one-year-out development Brier rule. Refit the frozen T1 on 2018–2022. This model can reveal whether direction, accumulated history, and concordant multiorgan deterioration discarded by the latest-state baseline carry useful information. It cannot identify physiology.

### D1: conditional sparse dynamic-factor alternative

D1 is secondary and may be fitted only if Gate DF below passes. It uses the same four 12-hour bins and adverse-oriented assay matrix, with masks but no outcomes, to fit a masked linear-Gaussian dynamic factor model with K in {2,3}, diagonal observation noise, sparse loadings, and a transition matrix constrained to spectral radius below 0.98. Candidate loading penalties are {0.01,0.1}. Fit five fixed seeds on 2018–2021; select K/penalty only by 2022 masked negative log likelihood. The supervised head nests B0 and uses factor state at 48h, factor change from first to last evaluable bin, and per-factor innovation RMS (3K features), with ridge selected by the same development folds.

D1 can reveal whether a stable learned cross-assay state captures temporal structure lost by the fixed domain summaries. It is not a mechanistic model and cannot rescue a failed T1 primary test.

## Frozen phase gates

G0 — data integrity, required before any fit: all listed hashes and required headers pass; joins are unique where required; context_audit.py exactly reproduces N=17,733 and split events 73/33/44/150; no predictor time exceeds T0+48h; patient overlap across splits is zero. A failure is a computational repair, not permission to alter the estimand.

G1 — summary observability, always reported but not a stop gate: in 2018–2022 report patients/events with available_d for each domain and with at least two available domains. Adequate support is at least 50 of 106 events and at least 40% of all patients with two-domain ordered summaries. T1 is still fitted if this fails, but a null or unstable result is automatically inconclusive rather than adverse.

Gate DF — outcome-neutral dynamic-factor feasibility/stability: D1 is allowed only if (a) at least 2,000 development patients and 30 development events have at least three evaluable bins spanning at least 24 hours and at least two domains; (b) the chosen model improves 2022 masked NLL by at least 5% over per-assay development-median plus linear-time prediction; (c) after Hungarian factor matching, at least four of five seeds have loading Tucker congruence at least 0.85 and patient-state ICC at least 0.75; (d) no single assay contributes more than 60% of any factor’s squared loading norm; and (e) all accepted fits converge and satisfy the transition constraint. If any condition fails, record D1 as prespecified deferred and complete B0/T1 without it.

G2 — tuning freeze: 2022 outcomes may diagnose coding or numerical failure but may not change features, endpoint, thresholds, or metric definitions. Record B0/T1/D1 tuning metrics. Proceed to refitting if predictions are finite and nonconstant. Do not stop because a point estimate is unfavorable.

G3 — calibration: after refitting on 2018–2022, fit intercept and slope recalibration only in 2023, separately for each model. Freeze the recalibration mapping. Report its uncertainty; do not tune architecture in 2023.

G4 — locked test: open 2024–2025 outcomes once after all preceding artifacts and hashes are frozen. Confirmation is not required.

Clinical adjudication is not G0–G4 and never blocks this computational experiment.

## Controls and falsification

- U1 unordered-history control: B0 plus each domain’s pre-48h mean and standard deviation and the three availability indicators, using the same split and ridge rule. T1 must outperform U1 to attribute gain to ordering/coupling rather than additional historical values.
- G0-latest nonlinear control: histogram gradient boosting on B0 features only; max leaves {3,7}, minimum leaf {50,100}, learning rate 0.05, 200 iterations, chosen by development leave-one-year-out Brier score.
- Order ablation: for each assay/patient, keep the latest 24–48h value and its time fixed and randomly permute all earlier values over that patient’s earlier observed timestamps; retain every value, timestamp, mask, count, and medication input. Repeat 100 times and apply the frozen T1 coefficients without refitting. Report the proportion of observed T1 Brier gain removed.
- Timing-only control: static variables, masks, time-since, counts, and medication indicators/times, with all lab values removed.
- Treatment-indication control: refit B0/T1 without medication features. No medication coefficient may be interpreted as treatment effect.
- Missingness controls: values-only models and the subset with at least two ordered domains. If gain is confined to masks/timing, do not call it biological recovery.
- Endpoint sensitivities: inherited S1 all 326 candidates; S2 primary plus 16 procedure-only unresolved; pleural and abdominal components; examination-free support; order-free support; union versus cross-source corroborated events. None can replace a failed primary.
- Era/source controls: annual risk/event/coverage and procedure-source tables; 2020–2023 development/calibration sensitivity tested in 2024–2025. Cross-source corroboration is a sensitivity, not an independent replication cohort.
- Negative controls: label permutation within year × first-event procedure source and a +28-day within-encounter timestamp shift where possible. Any above-chance negative-control result triggers inconclusive classification and leakage investigation.

## Evaluation and uncertainty

Primary estimand: locked-test paired Brier difference ΔBrier=T1−B0. Also compute T1−U1 and, if available, D1−T1. Report log loss, AUROC, AUPRC, calibration intercept/slope with calibration plot, and decision-curve net benefit at 1%, 2%, 3%, and 5%.

Use 2,000 paired patient bootstraps stratified by test calendar year for frozen-model 95% intervals. Use 200 end-to-end bootstrap refits, resampling patients within calendar year separately in development, tuning, calibration, and test, for model-fitting sensitivity of B0/T1; D1 receives at least 50 end-to-end refits only if Gate DF passes. Report event counts, effective sample sizes, convergence/failure counts, and the full bootstrap distributions. No significance-only or subgroup-rescue interpretation is allowed.

Supportive requires all of:

1. T1−B0 Brier at most −0.0010 with paired 95% CI below zero;
2. absolute AUPRC gain at least 0.02 with paired 95% CI above zero;
3. locked-test T1 calibration slope 0.8–1.2 and intercept −0.20–0.20 after the frozen 2023 mapping;
4. T1 beats U1 in Brier with CI below zero, and order ablation removes at least 50% of the T1-versus-B0 Brier gain;
5. timing-only and nonlinear-latest controls do not match T1; direction agrees for S1/S2 and examination-free/order-free definitions without catastrophic annual/source reversal.

This supports prognostic value of ordered early recovery for a recorded context-supported intervention, not an unplanned complication, mechanism, or clinical utility.

Adverse requires adequate G1 support, stable endpoint/source coverage, and either a T1−B0 interval whose lower limit is greater than −0.0010 (excluding the prespecified meaningful benefit), or no T1 advantage over U1 plus preservation of at least 75% of apparent gain after order ablation. Prefer B0 and abandon the ordering claim for this endpoint. D1 cannot reverse this judgment.

Inconclusive includes failure of G1, intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, failed calibration, strong drift/source reversal, gain confined to timing/missingness, endpoint-definition reversal, excessive bootstrap/seed failures, or positive negative controls. Obtain better repeated measurements, endpoint review, or external data; do not call an imprecise null refutation.

## Adjudication as a separate interpretation layer

Two hepatobiliary clinicians may independently review the parent’s frozen deidentified stratified set: 100 primary events across era/component/source and pre-existing-fluid/early-drain-care strata, all 26 excluded or unresolved candidates, and 24 enriched candidate-negative encounters. Labels are unplanned post-hepatectomy complication treatment; planned/prophylactic drain care; chronic pre-existing fluid management; duplicate-only record; unrelated procedure; or insufficient evidence. Reviewers remain blinded to 0–48h predictors and model scores.

If strict-positive observed PPV is at least 0.80, Wilson lower bound at least 0.70, binary-label kappa at least 0.70, and no excluded stratum has at least 0.50 unplanned-complication yield, the result may additionally be described as applying to a phenotype that is predominantly adjudicated as unplanned complication treatment in the reviewed sample. Otherwise retain only the recorded-intervention claim and report disagreement. The enriched negatives cannot estimate population sensitivity. Even a passing sample does not establish causation, grade for every event, prospective utility, or transportability.
