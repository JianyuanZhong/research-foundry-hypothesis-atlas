# Opportunity-controlled transport and review-prioritization test for the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Status, unresolved question, and clinical decision boundary

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` (primary parent) and `[prior hypothesis]` (compatible decision-oriented parent). It preserves the Lead-frozen scientific schema exactly: 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons, 74 hospitals; first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`; landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission; the first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`; `D = cplitemoffset`; inherited pre-landmark exclusions; exact literal labels; repeated landmarks clustered by `patientunitstayid`; fixed B/E/T windows, parser, thresholds, and descriptive noncausal estimand.

The strongest evidence available supports only that the two literal care-plan values are sparse, hospital-heterogeneous structured documentation and that respiratory-chart associations can be generated by documentation opportunity, persistence, site composition, and competing ICU exits. It does **not** establish that either value denotes an SBT, extubation evaluation, clinician intent, delivered ventilator settings, readiness, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or a causal intervention. The unresolved, falsifiable claim is narrower: after controlling for pre-`D` respiratory state and the opportunity to document it, does the exact current label add portable information about a later structured respiratory trajectory and an independently defined physiologic-alert proxy, enough to improve a prespecified allocation of scarce manual chart-review slots?

The clinical decision value is deliberately operational and noncausal. The action is `R=1`, prioritize the row for manual review of the available respiratory chart, versus `R=0`, do not prioritize it in a fixed review queue. This experiment does not observe whether review occurred, whether a clinician changed care, or whether a patient benefited. A positive review-allocation score means only that a label-containing model ranks rows for these two structured chart constructs better under the frozen policy; it cannot justify extubation, ventilator changes, discharge, withholding treatment, or a bedside recommendation.

## Independent physiologic-alert triangulation

This is secondary and does not alter the cohort, `K`, parser, or primary estimand. From `vitalPeriodic`, after the same exit precedence and without using value information to define opportunity, define `V_E` and `V_T` as at least two finite vital rows at distinct `observationoffset` values in the respective window with span at least 5 minutes. Retain separate `sao2` and `respiration` counts and missingness. Parse only finite `sao2` in percent and `respiration` in breaths/min. A window-specific alert requires at least two distinct valid observations satisfying `sao2 < 88` or `respiration > 30`; a one-point signal is descriptive only. If units/convention cannot be verified from the source audit, the proxy is unavailable and its decision conclusion is inconclusive.

For each window separately, `H_w ∈ {alert,no_alert,physiologic_unobservable}`; `no_alert` requires valid opportunity and no alert. Exits and invalid exit time remain separate. Never pool E and T or convert lack of opportunity to no-alert. Report all-row `P(A,K,H_E)` and `P(A,K,H_T)`, target-opportunity denominators, discordant cells, and arm contrasts. Agreement is triangulation of two structured chart signals, not proof of a clinical event.

## Models, transport envelope, and opportunity-controlled portability

Use leave-one-hospital-out folds. All rows from a `patientunitstayid`, including repeated landmarks, remain in one fold. The all-row metric never hides unresolved states.

Add a fixed opportunity-controlled transport envelope. Define the scalar pre-D opportunity score before outcome reading as a deterministic rank of row count, distinct clinical times, and missingness indicators from the permitted pre-D opportunity fields. In each training fold, create fixed quantile bins with deterministic sparse-bin collapse; apply those cutpoints unchanged to the held-out hospital. Do not use endpoint, post-D value, label, or favorable direction. Within each held-out hospital and exact landmark, report M1-minus-M0 loss increments separately by low/high opportunity stratum when support permits, as well as the aggregate. A stratum with fewer than 10 rows per arm for the stated target is unsupported and is reported, not merged post hoc.

Retain all 74 hospitals descriptively, including sites with one arm or zero target opportunity. Define eligible sites before effect direction as at least 10 rows per exact arm among classifiable non-competing rows and, for an all-row target, at least 10 rows per arm for that target. If fewer than five sites meet a target's criterion, the site-balanced target envelope is unavailable/inconclusive. For eligible sites report unweighted median, equal-site mean, 10%-trimmed mean only with at least 10 sites, IQR, sign concordance, and leave-one-site-out maximum change. Do not remove sites because of direction.

For review prioritization, use a single global threshold/cost grid fixed before reading arm contrasts: review if held-out `p >= tau`, no review otherwise; and an unresolved-aware policy review if `p >= tau_high`, no-review if `p <= tau_low`, abstain otherwise. Evaluate `tau,tau_low,tau_high` and review costs `c ∈ {0.05,0.10,0.20,0.30,0.50}`, unresolved-review costs `c_u ∈ {0.00,0.05,0.10}`, and unresolved no-review penalties `u ∈ {0.25,0.50,1.00}`. Also evaluate fixed queue capacities at 10%, 20%, and 30% of the applicable opportunity-supported rows, with deterministic ties by `patientunitstayid` then landmark. Capacity is applied separately within each held-out hospital and landmark using the same global score ordering; this prevents a large hospital or label-rich site from supplying all review slots. No site-specific threshold or post-result capacity is permitted.

In the observed-target view, review plus target scores `+1`, review plus observed non-target `-c`, no-review plus observed target/non-target scores `0`; in the unresolved-aware all-row view, abstention scores `0`, no-review plus unresolved scores `-u`, and review plus unresolved scores `-c_u`. These are transparent operational scores for allocating chart-review slots, not clinical utility or benefit.

## Falsification, opportunity control, and persistence

Before interpreting any label gain, run all tests from machine-readable artifacts:

1. Broad within-hospital-by-landmark label permutation preserving stay clusters.
2. Opportunity-preserving permutation within hospital-by-landmark-by-training-fold opportunity strata, retaining arm counts, landmark structure, and repeated-stay clustering. Require at least 20 valid nonempty strata; fewer is unavailable/inconclusive. Record attempted/valid strata and permutations. If observed M1-M0 is typical under this null, it is adverse to a patient-state or review-prioritization interpretation even if the broad null is unfavorable.
3. The whole-stay sequence/persistence-preserving null if exact landmark-wise support permits; infeasibility is inconclusive, not approximated.
4. Placebo `(D-1440,D-720]` versus B and exposure shift to `D-720`, retaining the locked outcome windows.
5. Source/component ablations, late-horizon, airway-corroborated and non-corroborated sensitivities, fixed pre-D observation-weighted sensitivity, and the as-entered clock audit. Clinical offset remains primary; late-entered observations are not recoded as stable or worsening.
6. Exit timing/status/location, non-exit unobservability, source density, timestamp, component count, and entry-lag decomposition.

A label increment confined to exit, unresolved/unobservable rows, source density, entry lag, one component/source/site, repeated landmarks without first-landmark support, or an opportunity-preserving null pattern is adverse to the stronger patient-state or decision interpretation. A comparable/larger placebo increment or prespecified site-direction reversal is adverse. Failed positivity, unsupported permutation, inadequate physiologic opportunity, and unavailable clocks are inconclusive rather than negative evidence.

## Uncertainty and fail-closed interpretation gates

Use stay-cluster bootstrap, fixed seed `61061`, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates, refitting the complete held-out pipeline and recomputing state, transport, and review metrics. Report attempted, valid, failed, effective clusters, threshold-specific support, site-support loss, and opportunity-stratum support. Never use a row bootstrap or replace an unsupported site replicate with one. All intervals are descriptive uncertainty intervals, not proof of benefit.

Invalid computation includes parent artifact/hash mismatch; wrong source/schema/catalog hash; duplicate or non-one-to-one joins; wrong exact labels/landmarks; boundary inequality errors; parser or vital-unit failure; post-D leakage; care-plan use outside exposure verification; failure to assign exactly one K/H state where applicable; exit-precedence violation; fold or stay leakage; or a decision score not reproducible from held-out state probabilities.

Primary feasibility is supportive only if at least 40% of all 540 rows are classifiable and at least 10 hospitals have both exact labels among classifiable rows; below 20% or below five such hospitals is adverse feasibility, and intermediate support is inconclusive. The transport gate requires at least five eligible hospitals with at least 10 classifiable non-competing rows per arm representing at least 50% of supported rows, no more than 25% directional reversals, and no single hospital contributing more than 25% of supported rows. For opportunity-stratum claims, both low/high strata must meet their support rule in at least five held-out hospitals; otherwise report aggregate transport only and mark the opportunity-conditioned claim inconclusive.

A **supportive transported review-prioritization result** for a named target requires all of: valid computation; M1 improves both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss for all-row K/H state accounting and the target-opportunity view; incremental operational score is positive versus M0 at at least two adjacent costs and at least one fixed-capacity policy; the stay-cluster interval excludes zero with at least 500 valid replicates; no opportunity-preserving, persistence, placebo, source, or site falsification; and the gain is not confined to unresolved states. For the stronger patient-state interpretation, K and the independently tested H_E or H_T must show prespecified directional concordance in at least two supported overlap hospitals. If only one target passes, call it a partial structured-chart result. If H is unavailable, the K review result may remain computationally valid but triangulation is inconclusive.

Adverse output means no robust incremental information for the named structured review task or an explanation by opportunity, persistence, exits, or site composition. It does not show that bedside evaluation was absent, unnecessary, harmful, or beneficial. Inconclusive output means inadequate support, opportunity, transport, calibration, uncertainty, clocks, or null feasibility. Conclusions must link to computed `heldout_metrics.json`, `decision_curve.json`, `physiologic_triangulation.csv`, `transport_envelope.json`, `permutation_summary.json`, `bootstrap_summary.json`, and `verification.json`.

## What changed from the assigned parents

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, this child retains the frozen cohort, exposure, D, parser, B/E/T windows, K-state precedence, all-row exits, site-balanced transport, opportunity-preserving null, as-entered audit, and uncertainty gates, while making downstream use explicit through a fixed-capacity, held-out manual-review allocation policy that is applied separately within each hospital and landmark. It adds a required opportunity-stratum portability check rather than treating aggregate transport as sufficient, and it prevents unresolved states from being silently treated as negatives or rewarded as targets.

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, it retains the independent vitalPeriodic E/T alert triangulation and action-by-state accounting, but clarifies that K, H_E, and H_T are separate targets with separate opportunity denominators and transport gates; it adds fixed global policy thresholds, hospital/landmark capacity control, low/high opportunity portability, and explicit support rules before any review-prioritization claim. The result remains a descriptive chart-proxy experiment, not a bedside decision rule or causal study.
