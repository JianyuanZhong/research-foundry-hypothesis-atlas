## 1. Unresolved question

After circulatory stabilisation in sepsis/septic shock, some patients recover kidney and lung function together
while others recover one organ bed while the other stays impaired ("cross-organ recovery asynchrony"). The parent
candidate tests whether asynchrony at a prespecified decision time t* predicts 7-day deterioration **beyond the
same-time snapshot severity**. The unresolved — potentially fatal — objection is *explanatory*: observed
asynchrony could be (a) genuine organ-specific divergence, or (b) an artefact of a **single shared latent
recovery trajectory** sampled with **organ-specific measurement intensity**, because sicker/more-monitored
patients get more labs and are therefore likelier to be labelled "impaired". The parent adjusted for severity
but did not separate these two rivals. This episode adds a falsifiable discriminator between them.

## 2. Observed vs conjectured

Observed (inspected):
- [K1] Inflammatory recovery follows a **universal** trajectory (exponential WBC decay, delayed PLT growth)
  across infection, ischaemia and trauma, and deviation predicts adverse outcome (9x RR of sepsis death).
  Recovery is a *shared* dynamic, which supports the "shared latent trajectory" rival.
- [K2] Sepsis subphenotypes can be defined from **dynamic inter-organ interaction trajectories** in the 48 h
  after diagnosis (MIMIC-III/eICU). Organ-interaction dynamics therefore carry information — but the output is
  a clustering, not a test of whether cross-organ asynchrony adds prognostic value over snapshot severity, and
  it does not isolate measurement-intensity confounding.
- [K3] Creatinine-based assessment **over-estimates** kidney recovery during critical illness (cystatin C is
  less confounded by muscle loss); creatinine "recovery" can therefore be discordant with true kidney function —
  an explicit measurement rival for why renal and respiratory recovery disagree.

Conjectured (to test): cross-organ recovery asynchrony at t* carries prognostic information **beyond** (i)
snapshot severity at t* and (ii) a patient-specific observation-intensity proxy.

## 3. Population and temporal boundaries

- Population: MIMIC-IV 3.1 adult (>=18 y) ICU admissions with a sepsis/septic-shock phenotype: suspected
  infection (antibiotic + culture within the standard window) or ICD-coded sepsis, plus SOFA >= 2 rise or
  vasopressor + lactate > 2 mmol/L. Index ICU stay per subject_id.
- t0 = ICU `intime`. Circulatory-stabilisation time t* = first time after t0 with MAP >= 65 mmHg sustained
  >= 2 h without vasopressor-dose escalation (parent rule).
- Classification landmark = t*+24 h, using only data in (t*, t*+24 h]. Outcome window = (t*+24 h, t*+7 d].
  No future information; prespecified to avoid leakage.

## 4. Rival-discriminating design (the advance over the parent)

Same outcome, same split; nested model ladder:
- M0 Snapshot severity: SOFA + lactate + vasopressor dose at t* (parent baseline).
- M1 Severity + asynchrony class (2x2 renal-improved x respiratory-improved, each by prespecified deltas).
- M2 Shared-latent-trajectory rival: a single latent recovery factor fitted to the same longitudinal markers
  (creatinine slope, urine-output trend, PaO2/FiO2 trend, lactate decay) — the rival's prediction.
- M3 Asynchrony + observation-intensity proxy: number of creatinine/ABG measurements, total chartevents, and
  time-to-first measurement in the window — the measurement-selection rival.
Development/holdout temporal split of ICU admissions (early years development, final years held out).

Discriminating statistics:
- Delta AUROC / AUPRC and calibration of M1 over M0 (does asynchrony add signal?);
- M1 vs M2 (is the signal organ-specific divergence or a shared latent trajectory?);
- attenuation of the asynchrony coefficient when M3 covariates are added (<25% = robust, >=60% = artefact);
- permuted-label negative control (shuffle asynchrony classes within severity deciles) should null the gain.

Interpretation of the three informative outcomes:
- M1 beats M0 and M2 and survives M3 -> organ-specific divergence adds information; shared-trajectory and
  measurement rivals weakened (not excluded; still observational).
- M1 beats M0 but not M2 -> signal is consistent with one shared latent trajectory; asynchrony is a convenient
  re-expression, not evidence of organ-specific divergence.
- M1 gain vanishes under M3 -> asynchrony is largely measurement-intensity/selection artefact.

## 5. Method exploration (baseline vs substantive alternative)

Baseline = snapshot logistic/SOFA (M0). Substantive alternatives: M2 latent-trajectory mixed-effects/state-space
model (mechanistic), and an optional M4 GRU sequence model over the 24 h marker series to capture non-monotone
recovery. Method comparison tests the scientific uncertainty above, not a predictive gain chase. Deferred: full
trajectory clustering (latent class) needs a larger fitting budget; the sequence extension would use 1 GPU but
is not required for the primary explanatory ladder. Recorded as deferred alternatives, not bans.

## 7. Falsification criteria

Supportive: M1-vs-M0 AUROC gain >= 0.02 with bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 AND M1-vs-M2 gain CI excluding 0 AND
asynchrony-coefficient attenuation < 25% after M3 adjustment AND permutation control null (gain CI includes 0).
Adverse: no M1-vs-M0 gain (CI includes 0), OR the gain is fully explained by M2/M3.
Inconclusive: gain present but imprecise (wide CI), asynchrony class counts too small (<50/class), or organ-support
data too sparse to classify recovery.

## 8. Solver deliverable (what must be newly fitted/estimated)

Fit M0–M3 on the development split; report held-out AUROC/AUPRC/calibration and the three discriminating
contrasts with bootstrap CIs, the permutation control, and per-class counts. Required outputs: metrics table,
coefficient-stability table, calibration plot data, class-count table. Reference solution optional.

## 9. Limitations

Measurement-selection adjusted, not removed; creatinine recovery confounded by muscle loss [K3] and renal
replacement; t* rule may not generalise; single-centre retrospective; association surviving adjustment does not
identify a mechanism; no causal claim.
