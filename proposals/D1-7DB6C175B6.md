# Discordant observed renal recovery after early ICU AKI: a data-bound noncausal prognostic experiment

## Scientific deliverable

Newly fit and estimate a MIMIC-IV v3.1, one-row-per-index-stay prognostic analysis. The deliverable is complete only when it contains:

1. a read-only-source provenance manifest and reproducible cohort-flow table;
2. item-level support, invalid-value, storage-time, measurement-density, missingness, and phenotype-cell counts;
3. the prespecified noncausal association between discordant and concordant observed recovery, with uncertainty and competing-event summaries;

6. threshold, coverage, creatinine-source, early-RRT, weight, and within-stay time-permutation sensitivity/falsification analyses; and
7. a conclusion classified as supportive, adverse, or inconclusive under the rules below.

## Unresolved clinical question and hypothesis

The strongest directly inspected evidence supports a measurement concern, not the proposed MIMIC claim. Haines et al., *CJASN* 2023 (DOI `10.2215/CJN.0000000000000203`; local readable extraction `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`) studied 38 selected mechanically ventilated patients with sequential creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance, and rectus-femoris ultrasound. Their results support concern that creatinine-based kidney-function estimates can overestimate measured function during prolonged critical illness and that this relates to muscle loss. They do not establish the prevalence, Foley-output meaning, prognosis, or mechanism of the proposed phenotype in MIMIC-IV.

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is:

> Among adults with an observed early creatinine-AKI-like rise who remain alive in their first ICU stay at a 72-hour landmark and have adequate paired creatinine/Foley measurements, observed creatinine recovery without adequate late Foley-output recovery is associated with higher subsequent in-hospital mortality than observed creatinine recovery with adequate late Foley-output recovery.

This is a prognostic association conditional on observed measurement availability. It is not a causal effect of creatinine recovery, urine output, Foley placement, fluid management, dialysis, or any treatment. Its clinical importance is that a reproducible discordance signal could identify patients for confirmatory renal assessment and closer monitoring at a time when a falling creatinine may otherwise be interpreted as recovery. The substantive advance over the existing evidence is a preregistered, trajectory-defined comparison against concordant recovery with explicit measurement-density and leakage controls, rather than an inference from creatinine alone or a claim that a surrogate is true GFR.

## Population, unit, and landmark

Use only the MIMIC-IV v3.1 snapshot. The primary unit is the first ICU stay within each `hadm_id`, identified by the earliest valid `icu/icustays.intime`; later ICU stays in the same admission are not alternative index rows. A subject may contribute more than one admission, but every fitting split is grouped by `subject_id`.

Retain an index stay if all of the following hold:

- `hosp/patients.anchor_age >= 18`, retaining MIMIC's documented `91` representation for ages above 89 and reporting it;
- the index `icustays` row has nonmissing `stay_id`, `subject_id`, `hadm_id`, `intime`, and `outtime`, with `outtime > intime`;
- the matching `hosp/admissions` and `hosp/patients` rows exist;
- `t0 = intime + 72 hours` is strictly before `outtime`; and
- there is no documented admission `deathtime <= t0`.

The index admission need not be an ICU admission type, and patients are not restricted to infection or shock because those labels are not required for this prognostic question. The primary estimand is therefore the observed, measurement-defined risk contrast among eligible first ICU stays, not risk among all hospital patients with KDIGO AKI.

All predictor clinical times must satisfy `intime <= time < t0`. To guard against data-entry leakage, rows with a nonmissing `storetime` are additionally retained only when `storetime < t0`; the audit reports how many otherwise eligible measurements fail this check. No outcome, later ICU row, post-`t0` procedure, post-`t0` treatment, or discharge information is a predictor. Subject-shifted MIMIC timestamps are compared only within subject/stay, never across subjects.

## Observed creatinine-AKI-like phenotype

Creatinine is assembled from both sources below:

- ICU `icu/chartevents`, itemid `220615` (“Creatinine (serum)”), using `subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, charttime, storetime, valuenum, valueuom`;
- hospital `hosp/labevents`, itemid `50912` (“Creatinine”), using `subject_id, hadm_id, itemid, charttime, storetime, valuenum, valueuom`, joined to `hosp/d_labitems` to verify the dictionary label and joined to the index stay by `hadm_id` plus timestamp containment `intime <= charttime < outtime`.

For item-identified creatinine, retain finite positive numeric values in a predeclared plausible range of 0.1–30 mg/dL. The ICU dictionary describes item 220615 as numeric with `unitname=None`, so its item label—not a missing unit string—is the assay identity; hospital item 50912 is required to have a compatible `valueuom` when supplied. Invalid, nonnumeric, nonpositive, out-of-range, and incompatible-unit rows are counted and excluded. At an identical clinical timestamp, collapse duplicate ICU rows by their median; if no ICU value exists, use the median of hospital-lab values. ICU values have source priority only when both sources are present at that timestamp. Preserve source-specific counts for sensitivity analysis.

Define three half-open windows relative to `intime`:

- early: `[0,24h)`;
- rise: `[24h,48h)`; and
- late/recovery: `[48h,72h)`.

Let `C_early` be the minimum collapsed creatinine in the early window, `C_rise` the maximum in the rise window, `C_peak` the maximum in `[0,48h)`, and `C_late` the minimum in the late window. Eligibility requires at least one valid value in each of the three windows. Define **observed early creatinine-AKI-like rise** as:

```
C_rise >= C_early + 0.3 mg/dL
or
C_rise / C_early >= 1.5.
```

This is deliberately called observed AKI-like, not KDIGO AKI: no reliable prehospital baseline, complete oliguria ascertainment, or clinical adjudication is available. Record the absolute and relative rises rather than retaining only the binary label.

Define **creatinine recovery** as:

```
C_late <= C_peak - 0.3 mg/dL
or
C_late / C_peak <= 0.75.
```

The OR rules are fixed before fitting. Continuous creatinine changes and alternative absolute/relative-only definitions are sensitivity analyses, not post hoc relabeling.

## Selective Foley-output measurement and discordance

Urine output is restricted to `icu/outputevents` itemid `226559` (“Foley”), verified in `icu/d_items` as `linksto=outputevents` and `unitname=mL`. Use `stay_id, charttime, storetime, value, valueuom`. Retain finite nonnegative values with `valueuom=mL`, the same pre-`t0` clinical/storage-time rules, and no other output item as a substitute.

Bin Foley rows by the integer hour offset from `intime`, aggregate multiple valid rows within an hour by summing their recorded volume, and count that hour as observed if at least one valid row exists. Missing hours are missing—not zero urine. The primary measured-output cohort requires at least 12 distinct observed Foley hours in both `[0,24h)` and `[48,72h)`; a prespecified measurement-dense sensitivity requires at least 18 in each. Report the number of raw rows, valid rows, observed hours, and eligible stays by window and by phenotype. This explicitly targets a measurement-defined estimand and does not generalize automatically to AKI patients without dense Foley charting.

For weight standardization, use `icu/chartevents` itemids `226512` (“Admission Weight (Kg)”) and `224639` (“Daily Weight”), with `stay_id, charttime, storetime, valuenum, valueuom`. Retain finite positive values in 20–300 kg with compatible kg units. The primary late rate uses the latest qualifying weight at or before the start of the late window (`intime+48h`); this prevents a late-window denominator from being chosen after the urine trajectory it standardizes. If no such weight exists, the stay remains in the unweighted coverage audit but is not assigned the primary weight-standardized phenotype. An explicitly labeled unweighted mL/hour analysis and a latest-pre-`t0`-weight sensitivity are reported separately.

Let `U_late` be total valid Foley mL in the late window divided by the number of observed late Foley hours, and let `W_48` be the primary weight. Define late Foley-output recovery as `U_late/W_48 >= 0.5 mL/kg/observed hour`. This is a charted Foley-output proxy, not a validated KDIGO urine-output label.

The primary comparison is restricted to the observed-AKI-like, creatinine-recovered subgroup with primary paired coverage:

- **discordant exposure**: creatinine recovered and late Foley output not recovered;
- **concordant reference**: creatinine recovered and late Foley output recovered.

The four creatinine/urine combinations are retained in the flow and descriptive table. The two creatinine-not-recovered cells are not silently merged into either primary group. A secondary four-level association uses them only if each cell has estimable support. Early-output rate/change is descriptive and an exploratory covariate; it does not redefine the primary late discordance.

## Outcomes and noncausal estimand

The primary outcome is **death after `t0` and before or at the admission `dischtime`**, with an event only when `deathtime > t0` and `deathtime <= dischtime`. `hospital_expire_flag` is used as a consistency check and is never used as a predictor; inconsistent flag/time rows are counted, and the exact event-time endpoint is not manufactured from the flag alone. Report death timing and the proportion with missing/inconsistent death fields.

The primary association estimand is the adjusted, standardized difference in observed in-hospital-death risk between discordant and concordant recovery among the primary measurement-defined cohort:

```
RD_obs = standardized P(Y=1 | discordant, observed cohort)
       - standardized P(Y=1 | concordant, observed cohort).
```

This notation is associational: it does not mean that assigning a phenotype or changing a treatment would change risk. Fit a prespecified logistic model with phenotype plus only pre-`t0` covariates, obtain the standardized risk difference and risk ratio over the empirical covariate distribution, and calculate 95% subject-cluster bootstrap intervals (at least 500 resamples, with the resampling unit `subject_id`). If a binomial/log-link model fails to converge, retain logistic standardization as primary and report a robust Poisson/log-binomial alternative; do not change the estimand.

Treat alive discharge as a competing event in the secondary time-to-event description: report cause-specific Cox estimates for death with discharge censoring and Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence for death versus alive discharge through 14 days after `t0` or discharge, whichever occurs first. A 14-day in-hospital death indicator is a secondary fixed-horizon endpoint. For all estimates, distinguish the selected measurement-defined cohort from the eligible observed-AKI-like cohort.

Secondary outcomes are:

- **new renal-replacement support after `t0`**: a procedure whose `starttime >= t0` and `starttime < dischtime` in `icu/procedureevents`, for itemids `225441` (Hemodialysis), `225802` (Dialysis - CRRT), `225803` (CVVHD), `225805` (Peritoneal Dialysis), `225809` (CVVHDF), or `225955` (SCUF); rows use `stay_id, starttime, endtime, itemid, storetime`. A procedure already active or started before `t0` is prevalent support, not new support. This is a charted procedure endpoint, not proof of a renal indication;
- **first ICU readmission during the same admission**, a later `icustays` row with the same `subject_id,hadm_id` and later `intime` after index `outtime`, analyzed only among patients discharged alive from the index ICU; death before readmission is a competing event;
- a descriptive death-or-new-RRT composite; and
- ICU/hospital length of stay, descriptive only because discharge and death induce selection and competing-risk problems.

No post-`t0` secondary outcome is used in feature construction. RRT beginning in the 72-hour feature window is a pre-landmark severity/treatment indicator and is handled in the prespecified sensitivity analysis below.

## Covariates and leakage controls

The association model includes a fixed case-mix set measured before `t0`:

- `patients.subject_id, anchor_age, gender`;
- `admissions.subject_id, hadm_id, admission_type, admission_location, insurance`;
- `icustays.first_careunit, intime`, and elapsed ICU time at the landmark;
- creatinine early/rise/peak/late values, absolute and relative changes, counts, and measurement-density indicators;
- pre-landmark summaries and missingness indicators for MAP item `220181`, HR `220045`, respiratory rate `220210`, and SpO2 `220277` in `icu/chartevents`;
- lactate itemids `50813`, `52442`, and `53154` in `hosp/labevents`, verified by `hosp/d_labitems.label=Lactate`, with `hadm_id, charttime, storetime, valuenum, valueuom` and index-stay containment;
- any pre-`t0` exposure interval from `icu/inputevents` for norepinephrine `221906`, dopamine `221662`, phenylephrine `221749,229630,229631,229632`, and epinephrine `221289,229617`, joined to `icu/d_items` and using `starttime,endtime,storetime,statusdescription`;
- pre-`t0` invasive ventilation `225792` and non-invasive ventilation `225794` from `icu/procedureevents`;
- pre-`t0` RRT using the six procedure itemids above; and
- an admission-coded CKD/ESRD proxy from `hosp/diagnoses_icd(subject_id,hadm_id,icd_code,icd_version)` joined to `hosp/d_icd_diagnoses(icd_code,icd_version,long_title)`, using a version-aware dictionary-text rule for chronic kidney disease/end-stage renal disease/renal failure.

The measured-fluid-volume sensitivity proxy sums valid `icu/inputevents.amount` rows with `amountuom=mL`, restricting itemids through `icu/d_items.linksto=inputevents` and `unitname=mL`, and subtracts valid `icu/outputevents.value` rows with `valueuom=mL`. It is not called a diagnosis of congestion and is never required for the primary phenotype.

For all interval data, clip exposure to `[intime,t0)`; use only rows with a clinical time before `t0`, and, when `storetime` exists, before `t0` as well. Do not use `admissions.deathtime`, `hospital_expire_flag`, `admissions.dischtime`, later ICU stays, post-landmark procedures, or post-landmark treatment as predictors. Report item-level support and missingness before and after every filter so selective measurement is visible.

## Baseline versus substantive temporal alternative

The simple baseline is a newly fitted ridge-penalized logistic regression for the primary post-`t0` in-hospital-death outcome. Its inputs are the demographic/context variables, observed-AKI/creatinine summaries, pre-landmark vital/lactate summaries, vasoactive/ventilation/RRT indicators, CKD proxy, and missingness indicators. It intentionally excludes all Foley-output, weight-standardized-output, input-minus-output, and fluid-trajectory variables. It retains a transparent creatinine-centered risk estimate and tests whether the renal discordance information is beyond ordinary severity and creatinine history.

The substantive alternative is a newly fitted nonlinear temporal model, not a reproduction of Delphi or another demonstration: CPU `sklearn.ensemble.HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (or a Harbor-equivalent CPU tree booster fixed before fitting) on the same eligible subjects, outcome, leakage boundary, and split. For each six-hour bin in `[0,72h)`, use last, mean, minimum, maximum, within-bin slope, variability, count, and missingness for creatinine, Foley mL/hour, weight-standardized Foley output when available, measured input-minus-output proxy, MAP, HR, respiratory rate, SpO2, lactate, vasoactive support, ventilation, and RRT. Include the same baseline variables as non-temporal context. Fit:

1. a full temporal model; and
2. an a priori ablation removing all urine-output, weight, and input-minus-output channels while leaving the split, folds, target, and all other inputs unchanged.

Use a fixed 80/20 subject-level held-out test set, with nested subject-grouped cross-validation inside the training subjects for regularization and model settings. Calibrate only inside training folds (Platt or isotonic selected in inner validation); evaluate the held-out set once. Report AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, calibration intercept and slope, calibration curves, and subject-cluster bootstrap intervals. Use grouped permutation summaries by channel family rather than causal feature importance. The comparison is scientifically substantive because it asks whether nonlinear timing and interactions—especially falling creatinine with persistent low measured Foley output—carry calibrated prognostic information that a creatinine snapshot loses. It is not a leaderboard claim and cannot rescue a failed primary phenotype contrast.

Actual scientific completion therefore requires newly estimated `RD_obs`, newly fitted baseline and temporal/ablation models, and their held-out predictions and uncertainty outputs. A support audit alone is not completion; no model result is to be substituted by a literature or reference-execution envelope.

## Analysis, sensitivity, falsification, and interpretation

Before modeling, publish a flow table from all first adult ICU stays through landmark eligibility, observed-AKI-like eligibility, paired Foley coverage, weight availability, and the two primary phenotype cells. Include raw and valid row counts for every item group, unit distributions, storage-time exclusions, duplicate collapse counts, missingness by phenotype, and outcome incidence/timing.

Prespecified sensitivity analyses are:

- absolute-only and relative-only creatinine recovery/rise rules;
- 12 versus 18 observed Foley hours per window;
- ICU-creatinine-only versus hospital-lab-only versus combined creatinine;
- primary weight versus unweighted mL/hour versus latest pre-`t0` weight;
- exclusion of any RRT procedure beginning before `t0`, and stratification by pre-landmark RRT;
- adding coverage/hour-count indicators to the association model; and
- early-output trajectory and measured-fluid proxy as descriptive/additional covariates.

A within-stay urine-time permutation falsification reassigns valid Foley records among the 72 pre-landmark hour bins while preserving each stay's total volume and number of observed bins, then rederives the phenotype and repeats the unadjusted contrast over prespecified permutations. If the discordant association persists unchanged after late-versus-early timing is destroyed, the specific temporal interpretation is falsified or likely dominated by total volume/measurement process. This does not prove absence of confounding. A source/threshold direction reversal is also adverse evidence.

Results are classified in advance:

- **Supportive:** the primary adjusted discordant-versus-concordant risk difference is positive with a 95% interval excluding zero; the direction remains positive in the measurement-dense and at least two threshold/source sensitivities; and the full temporal model has reproducibly better held-out Brier score or calibration/risk-stratification information than the urine/fluid ablation and creatinine-centered baseline, with bootstrap uncertainty. A small AUROC change alone is not supportive.
- **Adverse:** the primary estimate is precisely near or below zero, reverses under measurement-dense coverage, or is erased by source/threshold checks; or the time-permuted phenotype preserves the apparent signal, undermining the proposed timing-specific interpretation. Temporal-model improvement alone cannot rescue an adverse primary phenotype contrast.
- **Inconclusive:** primary cells or deaths are sparse, coverage is strongly phenotype-dependent, storage-time/invalid-value exclusions materially change support, confidence intervals are wide, calibration fails, the two primary cells are not estimable, or results are unstable across reasonable coverage/weight rules. An archive audit with inadequate support is itself a no-go for a definitive claim, not permission to replace the phenotype with an easier endpoint.

The automatic computation/verifier can check source hashes and members, joins, item labels, filtering, windows, split integrity, no post-`t0` features, estimates, predictions, uncertainty, and whether conclusions match outputs. It cannot adjudicate KDIGO AKI, Foley patency, true GFR, muscle loss, volume status, RRT indication, treatment indication, clinical utility, or external validity. Positive findings require clinician-reviewed renal phenotyping with cystatin C/measured GFR, reliable weight and volume status, and prospective/external validation. They cannot justify starting, stopping, delaying, or withholding dialysis, diuresis, fluids, or other treatment.

## Demonstration and method provenance

The three research-ambition demonstrations were read only to position the method: Delphi supports the idea of comparing dated structured trajectories with snapshots, but its UKB cohort and Danish validation are unavailable; ALADYNOULLI's genetics/full multimodal inputs are unavailable and an EHR-only latent model would be a different estimand; Oncoformer's full image experiment and complete main methods are unavailable, so no multimodal reproduction is claimed. This proposal is a bounded MIMIC adaptation, not a reproduction. The expert MIMIC discordant-AKI seed is an untested question, not evidence; Haines is used only for the explicitly stated measurement concern.

## Alternatives not selected and revisit evidence

A neural sequence model or joint latent-state Bayesian model is deferred because the immediate scientific uncertainty is temporal/output discordance under selective charting, which the six-hour tree model can test with auditable inputs and bounded CPU resources. Revisit a latent-state model if at least 20% of eligible stays have repeated creatinine and Foley observations in all three windows and the temporal model shows a stable timing interaction not captured by six-hour summaries. Revisit a joint cystatin-C model or mechanistic mediation study only when cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle/volume assessment, and treatment indications become available. Diuretic-initiation, transfusion, sedation, and causal fluid/RRT questions remain deferred because treatment indications and time-varying confounding are not adequately observed.
