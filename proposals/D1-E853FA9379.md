# Pre-registered compatible-set and finite-workload repair of the blinded MIMIC pulmonary routing challenge

## 1. Scope, parent, and unchanged scientific schema

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It repairs only the two remaining operational weaknesses identified in that candidate: (i) thresholds, category vocabularies, and adjudication rules must be frozen rather than left for later governance approval; and (ii) the adverse-compatible-set analysis and finite review-workload ledger must be executable from labeled records. It does **not** change the inherited population, clinical estimand, recommendation-unit definition, chronology, routes, sampling frame, or evidence ceiling.

The frozen decision remains whether an exact admission-level signature can be nominated for a prospective **silent** validation of text-defined `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` routing. This is a reproducibility and contamination audit. It is not a test of guideline appropriateness, clinician performance, actual patient safety, follow-up completion, communication, clinical benefit, or causality. A retrospective pass never authorizes an alert, order, referral, copied text, or patient-facing action.

The inherited schema is locked as follows.

* Population: the complete first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity MIMIC pulmonary/nodule discharge population `N_A`.
* Chronology: admission through `D=dischtime`; reciprocal report/addendum chains and every operative text field must be pre-`D`; exactly one canonical `DS` artifact per admission.
* Unit: the smallest operative `(target cluster, action/modality, due interval/date, polarity)` tuple; units are nested within admissions and are never sampled or weighted independently.
* Clocks: `Y={F,C_O,C_I,R,A,U_D}`, `V={V_D,V_24,V_72,V_168,V_late,V_U}`, `H={H_stale,H_0_6,H_6_24,H_24_72,H_72plus,H_U}`, and `Z={F_text,C_O_text,C_I_text,R_text,U_text}` exactly as inherited. For each unit, `G_u=DS_store-R_store,u`; the identity `D-R_store,u=(D-DS_store)+G_u` is checked row-wise.
* Route logic: sealed split-view component labels are combined only by locked Cartesian-product code. Precedence is `DEFER` over `TIMING` over `NOACTION`; `AUTO` requires every active unit to be wholly omitted, ample, reproducibly resolved/closed, and free of boundary, redaction, or conflict evidence; all incomplete, conflicting, interaction-dependent, and mixed admissions are `RECON`, with `A_MIXED` nested in `RECON`. One unsafe minority unit controls the admission.
* Estimand: the finite-population, admission-level adverse indicator and inherited route burdens/confirmation/capture/workload quantities, with atom-conditional versions for the exact pre-label atom `g=(E,terminal curr_service,W)`. Recommendation units are not an alternate estimand.
* Design: inherited five strata, fixed positive-probability two-phase whole-admission verification, no route-triggered verification, no replacements, and no post-label allocation. Total distinct reviewed admissions is at most 900.
* Ceiling: even complete support means only nomination of a prospective, within-site, silent validation for the exact observed signatures; all novel or unsupported signatures map to `DEFER`. No conclusion may claim exact calendar transport, external generalizability, clinical truth, appropriateness, responsibility, live safety, benefit, or causality.

## 2. Supported evidence, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

The available data support construction of the above frame and observable storage/service/ICU proxies. They do not establish physical departure visibility, finalization semantics, responsible-team identity, expert-label correctness, communication, order execution, or benefit.

The unresolved claim is whether a support-qualified exact atom that passes the parent component audit and the roster-disjoint holistic challenge remains admissible under an explicitly adverse-compatible interpretation of disagreement, unknownness, and correlated adjudicator error, while its entire finite review burden fits a fixed budget.

The falsifiable hypothesis is: at least one pre-label support-qualified atom, and every atom in any claimed allow-list, passes all inherited gates plus the frozen holistic, control, compatible-set, and workload gates. A supported atom failing any gate is excluded even if pooled, consensus, or model-based diagnostics pass. An atom that cannot meet the pre-label support or challenge design is unsupported/inconclusive, not negative evidence and not eligible for pooling.

## 4. Pre-label state machine and frozen category vocabulary

The following categories are protocol constants. They are written, hashed, and signed before any real `N_A` packet is labeled. A label outside a permitted set is `INVALID_LABEL`, not silently recoded.

### 4.1 Universal evidence/status categories

Every required field has exactly one of `OBSERVED`, `ABSENT`, `CONTRADICTED`, `INDETERMINATE`, `MISSING_ARTIFACT`, `UNREADABLE`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. `MISSING_ARTIFACT`, `UNREADABLE`, and `INDETERMINATE` are adverse-compatible unknowns; they are never favorable absence. `NOT_APPLICABLE` is permitted only where the codebook's dependency table says the field is structurally inapplicable.

Every reviewer response has exactly one of `LABELLED`, `NONRESPONSE`, `PACKET_CORRUPT`, or `ACCESS_FAILURE`. A nonresponse is not a negative label. Every transformation control has exactly one of `FEASIBLE`, `INFEASIBLE_STRUCTURE`, `INFEASIBLE_SPAN`, or `NOT_RUN_SCHEMA_ERROR`; the last three are adverse-compatible for a claimed atom.

### 4.2 Atomic adjudication categories

Each of H1, H2, and H3 records the following fixed fields. Categories are deliberately measurement categories, not assertions of clinical truth.

* `target`: `PULMONARY_NODULE`, `FOCAL_PULMONARY_OPACITY`, `OTHER_PULMONARY_TARGET`, `NO_TARGET`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `action`: `THORACIC_CT`, `OTHER_IMAGING`, `REFERRAL_OR_CONSULT`, `CLINICIAN_REVIEW`, `NO_ACTION`, `CONFLICTING_ACTION`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `timing`: `INTERVAL_3_MONTH`, `INTERVAL_6_MONTH`, `INTERVAL_12_MONTH`, `OTHER_EXPLICIT_INTERVAL`, `NO_TIMING`, `CONFLICTING_TIMING`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `recipient`: `PATIENT`, `OUTPATIENT_CLINICIAN`, `SPECIALIST`, `SYSTEM_OR_ORDER`, `NO_RECIPIENT`, `CONFLICTING_RECIPIENT`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `responsibility`: `EXPLICIT_RESPONSIBLE_ROLE`, `EXPLICIT_NO_RESPONSIBILITY`, `NOT_STATED`, `CONFLICTING_RESPONSIBILITY`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `polarity`: `RECOMMENDATION`, `PROHIBITION`, `CONDITIONAL`, `INFORMATIONAL_ONLY`, `CONFLICTING_POLARITY`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `replacement`: `NO_SUPERSEDING_PLAN`, `EXPLICIT_SUPERSEDING_PLAN`, `POSSIBLE_SUPERSEDING_PLAN`, `CONFLICTING_PLANS`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `conflict`: `NONE`, `WITHIN_ARTIFACT_CONFLICT`, `CROSS_ARTIFACT_CONFLICT`, `CROSS_UNIT_CONFLICT`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `unit_boundary`: `SINGLE_UNIT`, `MULTIPLE_UNITS`, `DUPLICATE_RESTATEMENT`, `UNRESOLVED_BOUNDARY`, `UNKNOWN`.
* `departure_artifact`: `DS_PRESENT_AND_TIME_KNOWN`, `DS_PRESENT_TIME_UNKNOWN`, `DS_MISSING`, `DS_UNREADABLE`, `DS_CONTRADICTORY`.
* `support_assertion` for H3: `SUPPORTED_BY_PACKET`, `CONTRADICTED_BY_PACKET`, `INDETERMINATE_FROM_PACKET`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
* whole-admission recommendation: one of `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, `DEFER`, `NOACTION`, `UNKNOWN`; this field is diagnostic only. No reviewer vote can override the locked route code.

For each component lane, the inherited labels remain separate: eligibility `ELIGIBLE`, `INELIGIBLE`, `UNKNOWN`; context `OPEN`, `CLOSED`, `UNKNOWN`; plan matching `MATCHED`, `OMITTED`, `REPLACED`, `CONFLICTING`, `UNKNOWN`; clock `AMPLE`, `UNDER_24H`, `STALE`, `POST_DEPARTURE`, `UNKNOWN`; and redaction/access `PASS`, `BREACH`, `UNKNOWN`. The exact unit extraction and inherited exclusion rules are frozen, not reinterpreted through this vocabulary.

### 4.3 Frozen support and disagreement predicates

For each admission `i`, route `r`, and H panel `k`, compute a deterministic Boolean `support_{ikr}` from the atomic fields and the locked route table. It is `1` only when every required field is present, internally nonconflicting, and exactly agrees with the route's pre-label atomic signature; any `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTING_*`, `UNRESOLVED_BOUNDARY`, or missing required field makes it `0` and records the reason. Define `agree_i=1` only when all applicable H1/H2/H3 atomic values match exactly after the predeclared `NOT_APPLICABLE` dependency rule. Define `discord_{ir}=1` when any parent-derived route/component state and any holistic route-compatible field differ or are unknown in a way that could change `r`.

No reviewer may introduce a new category, merge a rare category, or use a favorable interpretation after labels. The complete codebook, dependency table, route table, parser version, and category hash are part of the pre-label manifest.

## 5. Population, chronology, domains, and fixed sampling

Construct `N_A`, retrieval-negative audit, reciprocal pre-`D` chain, canonical DS, all inherited `Y/V/H/Z` cells, unit clocks, and all five outcome-blind strata exactly as in the parent. Use the parent's conservative possible-calendar class:

`C_i=[a_i+q_i-1,b_i+q_i+1]`, where `[a_i,b_i]` is parsed from `anchor_year_group` and `q_i=year(admittime)-anchor_year`; `E_EARLY` if `upper(C_i)<=2013`, `E_LATE` if `lower(C_i)>=2017`, `E_BRIDGE` otherwise, and `E_UNKNOWN` for malformed/missing data. The exact domain atom is `g=(E, exact terminal curr_service, W)`, with `W_SIMPLE`, `W_INTERMEDIATE`, `W_COMPLEX`, and `W_UNKNOWN` exactly as inherited. Exact service ties and contradictory ICU times are unknown; no macro-service regrouping is allowed.

Freeze the complete frame and final support atlas before any real label. An initial atom requires known component fields and `N_g>=max(30,0.02N_A)`. Within each inherited stratum, reserve at least two sampled admissions per occupied candidate atom when feasible; if fixed sample totals cannot support this, move the smallest candidate atom to `RESIDUAL_h`, with deterministic lexicographic tie-breaking. Rare, unknown, residual, and future novel atoms can never be released.

Retain the inherited sample sizes: sentinel census; `S_ZERO` the larger of 300 or the finite-population worst-case ±4-point formula; every other noncensus stratum the larger of 120 or the corresponding ±7-point formula, capped at `N_h`; and

`n_h=ceil[N_h*1.96^2*0.25/{e_h^2*(N_h-1)+1.96^2*0.25}]`.

The sentinel census plus formula samples must be `<=900`; otherwise the run is `NO_SELECTION`. Within each fixed `(h,g)` cell use the pre-label maximin/proportional integer allocation and an SRS without replacement seeded by the protocol hash. Store `pi_i`, `pi_ij`, weights, ordered draws, and no-replacement lists. No top-up, replacement, adaptive allocation, atom merge, or route-triggered review is permitted.

## 6. Challenge selection and sealed panels

Let `m` be the number of distinct admissions in the inherited sample and let `K` be the number of occupied final-atlas atoms with at least two sampled admissions. Fix the holistic challenge size before labels as `n_H=ceil(m/3)`. If `2K>n_H`, set `CHALLENGE_INFEASIBLE=1`; no atom may pass a holistic gate. Otherwise choose exactly two admissions per occupied atom by a deterministic hash-minimum rule, then fill the remaining `n_H-2K` slots by global hash order. The choice uses only frozen frame, atom, stratum, and sample identifiers, never labels or outcomes. Challenge inclusion probabilities conditional on the inherited draw are recorded and the inherited design weights remain primary.

Each challenge admission receives H1 and H2 whole-admission packets built independently from the sealed raw extract and a third H3 packet after H1/H2 append-only sealing. H1/H2 rosters, packet order, pseudonyms, and codebooks are disjoint and independently hashed; H3 is roster-disjoint and sees no component labels or routes. H1/H2/H3 labels, response states, and packet hashes remain separate. All selected admissions, not only apparent AUTO cases, receive the inherited components and the same safety review.

Controls are generated from a locked transformation manifest before any label: a route-irrelevant pair, route-decisive one-field pair, recommendation omission/addition pair, and nuisance-format/author-token pair when the pre-label parser proves the required span feasible. Controls are never used to rescue a real case. A route-invariant flip or a route-decisive violation is a falsification signal.

## 7. Executable compatible-set partial identification

### 7.1 Observed record and allowed latent states

For each challenged admission `i`, define the observed record

`O_i=(P_i,H1_i,H2_i,H3_i,Q_i,M_i)`

where `P_i` is the complete sealed parent component output (both lanes, all unit states and response statuses), `Hk_i` is the complete atomic vector above, `Q_i` is the locked-control result vector, and `M_i` is the packet/seal/missingness metadata. The analysis does not posit that any panel is a gold standard.

Define a finite latent state `z_i=(r_i^*,v_i^*,e_i^*,c_i^*)` from the following frozen sets:

* `r_i^* ∈ {AUTO,RECON,TIMING,DEFER,NOACTION,UNKNOWN}` is the route state compatible with the supplied evidence, not clinical truth.
* `v_i^* ∈ {SUPPORT,ADVERSE,UNRESOLVED}` is the measurement-validity state for the queried route. `SUPPORT` is allowed only if every required observed field is nonmissing, nonconflicting, and route-compatible; `ADVERSE` is allowed whenever at least one adverse-compatible field, disagreement, control failure, or unknown could change the route; `UNRESOLVED` is allowed for any remaining pattern.
* `e_i^* ∈ {NONE,SHARED,INDEPENDENT,UNKNOWN}` is the error-pattern label. `SHARED` is retained as a possible explanation for unanimous panels; it is never assigned a favorable point estimate.
* `c_i^* ∈ {CONTROL_PASS,CONTROL_FLIP,CONTROL_VIOLATION,CONTROL_INFEASIBLE}` is fixed by the control manifest and observed responses.

The compatible set is enumerated, not fitted:

`K_i(O_i)={z in Z: C_schema(z,O_i)=1, C_route(z,O_i)=1, C_missing(z,O_i)=1, C_control(z,O_i)=1, C_seal(z,O_i)=1}`.

The constraints are executable truth tables included in the protocol package. `C_schema` rejects out-of-vocabulary categories, duplicate fields, impossible dependencies, and invalid response statuses. `C_route` applies the inherited unit-level route precedence and requires a route change only through a locked route-decisive field. `C_missing` preserves every nonresponse, packet corruption, redaction breach, unknown clock, and infeasible control as compatible with the adverse state. `C_control` enforces the expected direction of each control without treating a successful control as truth. `C_seal` rejects any post-label construction or roster/codebook overlap.

A unanimous H1/H2/H3 record may therefore have both `e^*=NONE` and `e^*=SHARED`; no assumption of conditional independence is used. If `K_i(O_i)` is empty, the run is invalid for that admission and the affected atom cannot be certified.

### 7.2 Admission-level compatible indicators

For each route `r`, define deterministic functions on a latent state:

* `A_{ir}(z)=1` if `r_i^*=r` and `v_i^*=ADVERSE`;
* `S_{ir}(z)=1` if `r_i^*=r` and `v_i^*=SUPPORT`;
* `D_{ir}(z)=1` if `r_i^*=r` and `r_i^*` is discordant with any parent route/component state or unresolved holistic state;
* `L_{ir}(z)=1` if an AUTO-compatible state is also compatible with `RECON`, `TIMING`, or `DEFER` under the same observed record;
* `W_i(z)=1` if any adverse-compatible state entails a nonresponse, unknown, control infeasibility, or missing safety evidence.

For each finite population, the compatible assignment set is the Cartesian product `K(O)=×_i K_i(O_i)` subject only to fixed survey weights and the inherited unit/admission precedence. No cross-admission independence, exchangeability, or latent-class assumption is added. The “lower” assignment chooses the least adverse allowed state for the target quantity; the “upper” assignment chooses the most adverse allowed state. This is a measurement-consistency interval, not a clinical-truth interval.

For a binary total `T_f=sum_i f_i`, calculate

`L(T_f)=min_{z in K(O)} sum_i f_i(z_i)` and `U(T_f)=max_{z in K(O)} sum_i f_i(z_i)`.

For design-weighted estimation in stratum/cell `c`, calculate the same minima and maxima over sampled labels using HT contributions `w_i f_i(z_i)`, independently within every Rao-Wu replicate. For a ratio `R=T_a/T_b`, enumerate the finite set of attainable `(T_a,T_b)` pairs, discard only `T_b=0` with an explicitly reported undefined ratio, and take `min T_a/T_b` and `max T_a/T_b` over `T_b>0`. This is exact linear-fractional enumeration for the declared finite categories; an equivalent integer linear program may be used only if it returns identical endpoint checks on all fixtures.

For each route and atom report, before confidence intervals, the compatible endpoints for holistic agreement, parent-versus-holistic discordance, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, `leak_AUTO`, route capture, missingness/control infeasibility, and workload. The primary adverse endpoint is the upper compatible endpoint for an adverse quantity and lower compatible endpoint for required support. Exact finite-population inversion is then applied to each compatible binary total; the reported gate endpoint is the widest valid simultaneous endpoint across compatible-set optimization, exact inversion, and the 20,000 Rao-Wu resamples. Missing and unknown records widen the interval rather than entering a complete-case denominator.

The primary analysis reports no latent-class prevalence. A three-rater latent-class model, if shown, is a labeled diagnostic only. It cannot narrow `K_i`, alter a gate, or certify an atom unless an independent external validation constraint is obtained before labels; no such constraint is assumed here.

### 7.3 Explicit interpretation of correlated error

If all panels agree, the compatible set may still contain a shared-error state. Therefore agreement alone cannot establish truth. The challenge can support only the narrower claim that observed route-defining assertions are mutually reproducible and that all adverse-compatible states allowed by the locked evidence rules remain within operational bounds. A shared-wrong-label fixture must produce high agreement but a nonzero adverse-compatible upper bound that blocks certification when the planted shared-error state is permitted by the fixture constraints.

## 8. Frozen thresholds and certification gates

All numbers below are protocol constants; they are not to be approved, tuned, or justified after labels. They are operational nomination margins, not clinical harm thresholds.

### 8.1 Global inherited gates

`N_A>=200`; eligible index opportunities `E_A>=60`; overall design ESS `>=150`; normalized maximum admission weight `<=0.10`; simultaneous all-population burden half-width `<=5` per 100; sentinel plus all required samples `<=900`; retrieval-negative upper undercoverage `<=5%`; and no duplicate canonical key, broken seal/firewall, or mandatory review beyond the ledger budget.

### 8.2 Atom and route gates

A claimed atom must be in the final pre-label atlas, have known domain fields, at least 30 sampled candidate-route admissions, at least 10 non-sentinel challenge admissions for that route when feasible, route-denominator ESS `>=50`, positive simultaneous route burden, and normalized maximum atom weight `<=0.10`. Each route uses the following frozen values.

* All claimed routes: simultaneous lower H1/H2/H3 support agreement `>=0.90`; simultaneous upper parent-versus-holistic discordance `<=0.05` for `AUTO` and `TIMING`, `<=0.10` for `RECON` and `DEFER`; route-invariant control-flip upper bound `<=0.02`; route-decisive control-violation upper bound `<=0.05`; challenge missingness/nonresponse/control-infeasibility upper bound `<0.05` unless the atom is fully censused; and upper physician-hours and packet-time below the ledger budgets.
* `AUTO`: lower confirmation `>=0.90`, lower AC1 `>=0.70`, upper compatible `C_AUTO<=0.05`, upper `D_MIX<=0.10`, upper `leak_AUTO<=0.02`, and no adverse-compatible state leaves an unresolved minority unit as AUTO.
* `RECON`: lower confirmation `>=0.90`, lower `Se_RECON>=0.95`, lower `PPV_RECON>=0.80`, and upper AUTO leakage `<=0.02`.
* `TIMING`: lower confirmation `>=0.90`, lower `PPV_TIMING>=0.95`, and no route can rely on an index-only clock while another active unit is under 24 hours.
* `DEFER`: lower confirmation `>=0.90`, lower `Se_DEFER>=0.95`, and no adverse-compatible uncertain admission may be AUTO.

The allow-list is exactly the set of atoms independently passing all relevant gates. A pooled pass, favorable consensus, neighboring atom, sentinel-only result, macro-service result, model imputation, shrinkage, or latent-class point estimate cannot rescue a failing atom. If no atom passes, result is `NO_SELECTION`. Unsupported/unknown/residual/novel signatures default to `DEFER`.

## 9. Finite workload ledger

The workload ledger is frozen before sampling and is evaluated both as a reservation and against actual append-only logs. The hard limits are:

* `B_admissions=900` distinct admissions;
* `B_physician=1,400` physician-hours across all roles, including challenge duplicates and controls;
* `B_packet=84,000` packet-minutes (1,400 hours) of packet generation, QA, and adjudication access; and
* `B_control=4*B_H` control-pair reviews, where `B_H=n_H` is fixed before labels. A control-infeasible case still consumes its parser/QA time and is not deleted.

The charge table is fixed in minutes per reviewed admission or pair. It is a planning upper bound and also a required accounting unit:

| task | fixed charge |
|---|---:|
| each eligibility lane (`R-I` or `R-A`) | 8 min |
| each context lane (`K-I` or `K-A`) | 5 min |
| each departure-plan lane (`S-I` or `S-A`) | 12 min |
| redaction/access audit | 6 min |
| minority-unit safety audit | 10 min |
| each holistic duplicate H1/H2 | 15 min |
| H3 adjudication | 12 min |
| each feasible or infeasible control pair | 5 min |
| packet construction, hash, and QA per admission | 8 min |

For a sampled admission `i`, let `I_i=1` and `H_i=1` if challenged. The reserved minutes are

`p_i = 2(8+5+12) + 6 + 10 + H_i(2*15+12+4*5) + 8`.

Thus the complete fixed ledger is

`L_phys = sum_i I_i p_i`,

with role-specific subtotals retained; `L_packet` is the same sum split into packet/access versus physician adjudication according to the role ledger, and `L_challenge=sum_i I_i H_i(2*15+12+4*5)`. The compiler must emit `ledger.csv` with one row per admission, all task counts, planned minutes, actual start/stop minutes, response status, and reason for any difference. The run fails closed with `NO_SELECTION` if the pre-label reservation exceeds either hard budget, if an actual charge is missing, or if actual cumulative time exceeds a hard limit. Physician-hour summaries are not replaced by averages or complete-case timing; upper atom workload uses the compatible maximum task burden and design weights.

The ledger is additive: no task may be charged twice for a duplicate restatement, but H1/H2/H3 are separate readings and do count separately. Multiple units in one admission do not increase the admission count; unit-dependent reading time is recorded as a secondary diagnostic and cannot waive the admission cap. If the fixed `n_H` or the final atlas makes any atom's planned challenge charge exceed its per-atom allocation, that atom is challenge-infeasible and cannot pass; there is no adaptive borrowing of hours from another atom. Prospective silent bridging has a new budget and is outside this ledger.

## 10. Finite-population inference and multiplicity

Treat each `(h,g)` and residual cell as a survey stratum. Use the inherited HT totals, Hájek ratios, exact hypergeometric inversion for binary totals and route categories, and at least 20,000 Rao-Wu rescaled bootstrap replicates, retaining all labels, unknowns, workload, and controls for an admission together. Certainty admissions remain fixed. The same max-statistic simultaneous 95% family covers inherited pooled gates, all atom gates, compatible endpoints, holistic disagreement, controls, and workload. Secondary macro-domain, exact-code, path, favorable-consensus, complete-case, unweighted, and latent-class results cannot satisfy a primary gate.

For every primary quantity report the finite population target, sample estimator, design weights, compatible lower and upper endpoint, exact bound where applicable, simultaneous 95% interval, effective sample size, and denominator. A zero or undefined denominator is reported as `UNESTIMABLE`, never replaced by zero. Unknowns, nonresponse, packet corruption, and control infeasibility are retained as adverse-compatible states.

## 12. Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

A supportive result requires all global inherited gates, at least one atom with positive route burden, every claimed atom's simultaneous compatible-set and control gates, and the full additive ledger within limits. It supports only a prospectively tested, silent, within-site validation of the exact allow-list signatures; all else is `DEFER`.

An adverse result is a supported atom crossing a frozen contamination, disagreement, control, missingness, capture, or workload gate. That atom is removed despite favorable pooled results. A run-level adverse result includes retrieval undercoverage, broken firewalls, canonical duplication, invalid categories, or ledger overflow.

An inconclusive result includes sparse route events, challenge infeasibility, empty or very wide compatible sets, nonresponse, unknown clocks, inadequate ESS, no certifiable atom, or absent evidence required for a requested claim. Inconclusive is not evidence of safety, truth, or transport.
