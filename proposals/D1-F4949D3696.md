# A pre-registered, label-independent control universe for target-linked pulmonary discharge handoff

## Parent, purpose, and scope of this repair

This is a targeted repair child of `[prior hypothesis]`, with parent `[prior hypothesis]` retained as the scientific protocol. The repair addresses three executable gaps identified in assessment: the within-admission non-target control universe was not fully computable before labels, exact H+ atom matching and control support were not frozen, and the actionability separation/specificity and workload margins were not operationally registered.

The repair does **not** change the inherited scientific schema. It preserves, without substitution or pooling:

- the first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity MIMIC pulmonary frame;
- the reciprocal pre-discharge report/addendum chronology and `D = dischtime` boundary;
- exactly one canonical discharge summary (DS) per eligible admission;
- the exact target atom `g=(E, terminal curr_service, W)` and its route;
- sealed route-concealed C/S panels, inherited H1/H2/H3 and holistic controls;
- route-independent unit enumeration and all-unit adverse precedence;
- pre-label target selection and the inherited target-freeze state machine;
- positive-probability, two-phase, whole-admission sampling with no unit sampling;
- the additive distinct-admission workload ledger, including fixed controls and one charged reserve;
- finite-population Horvitz–Thompson/Hájek inference and nested design uncertainty; and
- the strict conclusion ceiling: at most a stored-text reproducibility and target-linked discharge-documentation result that can nominate a separately governed prospective **silent** bridge. No result establishes actual workflow, report delivery or viewing, responsibility, patient preference, follow-up completion, clinical appropriateness, safety, benefit, or causality.

The only substantive change is to make the secondary target-linked handoff falsification layer fully pre-registered and executable. Every control, lexical/atom rule, margin, support requirement, and worst-case time charge below is fixed before any phase-1 clinical label is exposed. No label, H outcome, reader preference, observed time, or favorable subgroup may alter the target, controls, estimand, thresholds, sample, or ledger.

## Clinical question and evidence ceiling

The inherited route asks whether an exact pulmonary recommendation can be measured reproducibly in stored MIMIC text. The remaining actionability gap is whether that exact recommendation is preserved in the canonical pre-discharge DS, the artifact that could carry a plan forward. The falsifiable secondary question is:

> In the frozen exact target atom-route, is the target's modality-and-due-interval instruction explicitly preserved in the canonical DS at a rate at least 20 percentage points higher than the pre-registered within-admission non-target recommendation control, while route-scrambled packets produce no more than 5% apparent exact matches?

The outcome is **exact stored-text handoff**, not delivery or action. A supportive result means that the target atom is more specifically represented in the canonical DS than a matched non-target recommendation under this protocol. It does not mean that a clinician saw, accepted, ordered, scheduled, communicated, or acted on it, or that follow-up was clinically indicated. A negative result falsifies this exact target-linked documentation construct for the selected cell and blocks any silent-bridge nomination for that cell. An under-supported or otherwise invalid result is inconclusive and maps to `DEFER`; it is not converted into a favorable claim.

The strongest evidence already supported by the available sources is narrower: the files contain admission, service, ICU-proxy, radiology-note, radiology-detail, discharge-note, and discharge-detail fields from which the inherited stored-text frame and a canonical-DS documentation audit can be constructed. The sources do not observe actual departure, delivery/viewing, named responsibility, orders, referrals, scheduling, outside care, completed imaging, images, clinical truth, safety, benefit, or causal outcomes. This repair makes no claim that they do.

## Fully computable, label-independent within-admission control universe

### Control purpose and domain

The control is a documentation-specificity control, not a clinical negative control. It is designed to ask whether exact modality-and-interval language is preferentially preserved for the frozen pulmonary target rather than for another recommendation with the same modality and interval in the same admission. Matching modality and interval makes generic DS language a meaningful falsification threat. A control is never substituted for an absent target and never changes the primary target estimand.

For each admission `i` in the complete inherited frame with a sealed target unit, define the candidate universe `C_i^0` before labels as follows. Every criterion is a deterministic function of source rows, source text, the inherited frozen atom parser, and the already sealed target; none uses phase-1 or H labels.

1. **Admission and chronology.** The candidate's radiology note belongs to `(subject_id,hadm_id)`, participates in the inherited reciprocal report/addendum chain, has `charttime < D` and `storetime < D`, and is not post-D. Its canonical DS is the unique inherited DS for the same `(subject_id,hadm_id)`.
2. **Unit identity.** The candidate is an inherited radiology recommendation unit with the same immutable unitization as the target units. The unit key is the inherited source provenance tuple, including `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id,field_ordinal)` where a detail row exists, plus the inherited deterministic text-span/ordinal key for multiple units in one field. No unit is created by a H reader.
3. **Positive recommendation grammar.** The unit must contain a patient-specific, affirmative recommendation span recognized by the frozen recommendation grammar: a recommendation verb from `{recommend, advised, suggest, should obtain, follow-up with, repeat}` or its frozen local equivalents, within the registered token window of a modality and due-interval expression. The grammar rejects a span if a negation, historical marker, family-history marker, hypothetical marker, uncertainty marker, optional/conditional marker, screening/surveillance/staging marker, or explicit no-follow-up marker scopes over the recommendation. The parser is a deterministic eligibility tool, not a diagnosis model; ambiguous or unmatched spans are absent from `C_i^0`, not H outcomes.
4. **Exact control atom.** The grammar must yield a canonical control atom `c=(E_c,M_c,W_c,P_c)` with a nonempty non-target cluster `E_c`, an action modality `M_c`, a due interval/date `W_c`, and affirmative polarity `P_c=positive`. The control is eligible only if `M_c=M*` and `W_c=W*` under the exact equality rules below. This deliberately restricts the universe to controls matched on the target's modality and interval, rather than calling arbitrary follow-up text a control.
5. **Non-target disjointness.** The candidate unit must not be a target unit, must not have the target's frozen cluster `E*`, and must not overlap the target's source span or report/addendum unit. If deterministic parsing yields both target and non-target concepts in one inseparable span, the candidate is excluded as ambiguous. A later H reader cannot reclassify it.
6. **No duplicate representations.** Report/addendum records belonging to one inherited recommendation unit are one candidate, ordered by the inherited canonical report/addendum and source ordinal rule. Duplicate detail rows do not create additional controls. A control's provenance retains every contributing note/detail key, but its sampling identity is unique.

The full admission-level control universe is `C^0 = {(i,c): c in C_i^0}`. Define `c_i` as the first candidate in each `C_i^0` under the fixed order `(report charttime, report storetime, note_seq, note_id, field_ordinal, unit ordinal, source span start)`. If `C_i^0` is empty, define `c_i=NONE` and record `control_absence=structural`. This absence is determined before labels and cannot be treated as a missing H response. For every sampled admission with `c_i != NONE`, exactly that one control unit is processed. No post-label top-up, replacement, second control, or choice among alternatives is allowed.

### Frozen lexical and atom dictionaries

The compiler must embed and hash a versioned dictionary before `FRAME_SUPPORT_SEALED`. It is a finite data object, not a model fitted to labels. The dictionary contains:

- Unicode NFKC, lowercase, whitespace-collapse, and punctuation normalization rules;
- modality tokens and canonical forms, with exact phrase matching and longest-match precedence;
- due-interval tokens for the inherited target vocabulary, with numeric forms and their fixed canonical representation;
- recommendation, negation, uncertainty, historical, hypothetical, optional/conditional, screening, surveillance, staging, and no-follow-up token lists;
- cluster lexicons and phrase boundaries used by the inherited target/support parser; and
- the parser version and SHA-256 hash.

Normalization is deterministic: Unicode NFKC; lowercase; replace all non-alphanumeric separators by one space except decimal/date separators needed by the date grammar; collapse whitespace; preserve token positions in the original text; and do not use spelling correction, embeddings, a language model, or post-label dictionary edits. Longest exact modality and interval matches win; ties are resolved by dictionary row order. A candidate with two incompatible modality or interval parses is excluded from `C_i^0`.

For this protocol, two atoms have equal modality only if their canonical dictionary IDs are equal, not merely clinically similar. Two intervals are equal only if their canonical IDs are equal: `3 months` is not equal to `12 weeks`, and a due date is equal only to the same normalized ISO date. No inferred date arithmetic converts a different expression into `W*`. The target and each control therefore have a deterministic frozen `(E,M,W)` before H readers see any packet.

The lexical parser supplies candidate eligibility and packet navigation only. It does not decide H+, H-, or H_U and cannot overrule either H reader. The parent's unvalidated cirrhosis detector is not used.

### Control-support certificate before labels

At `TARGET_SEALED`, compute the following counts using the full inherited frame and the already selected `g*`, not H labels:

- `N_T`: number of frame admissions containing at least one target unit in `g*`;
- `N_C`: number of frame admissions with `c_i != NONE` after the exact control rules;
- `N_TC`: number of frame admissions containing the target and `c_i != NONE` (the paired control-support domain);
- `N_TC,h`: the corresponding counts in each inherited phase-1 design stratum `h`; and
- `N_TC,master`: the number of paired-control admissions actually present in the sealed outcome-blind phase-1 master.

The target may be selected only if all of the following hold, in addition to every inherited target rule:

- `N_TC >= 120` full-frame paired-control admissions;
- `N_TC,master >= 60` in the sealed phase-1 master;
- `N_TC,h >= 1` in at least two inherited phase-1 strata and `N_TC,master` is represented in at least two such strata;
- every paired-control admission has a positive inherited first-stage probability and is eligible for a positive conditional phase-2 probability under every possible post-label band partition allowed by the inherited worst-case certificate; and
- the worst-case H packet and fixed-control workload certificate below fits the inherited `T` and time, reviewer, weekly, roster, and 32-week constraints.

These thresholds are operational support requirements, not claims about safety or clinical importance. They are fixed before labels. If the first frame-ordered target fails any condition, return `NO_SELECTION`; do not choose a later target, relax the control rule, use an admission-level substitute, or report superiority without a control contrast. Consequently, a selected target has a pre-label-defined positive-support control universe, while a target with no control support has a registered and reproducible `NO_SELECTION` outcome rather than a post hoc inconclusive contrast.

Control absence among non-target admissions remains a descriptive frame count. It is not evidence that target handoff is specific. Because the target selection certificate requires `N_TC` support, structural absence cannot silently define the selected target's primary contrast.

## Exact H outcome and atom-preserving matching rules

The H layer is secondary to, and does not replace, inherited C/S/H1/H2/H3 safeguards. For each sampled whole admission, two fresh independent H readers inspect the frozen source report unit and canonical DS packet. H rosters are disjoint from phase 1, C, S, task, inherited, and control-codebook rosters. The packet does not reveal whether the unit is target or control, its route, phase-1 labels, or other sampled outcomes. Target and control packets use identical layout, text limits, provenance presentation, and random packet order.

Each H reader assigns exactly one status to each packet.

- `H+`: the canonical DS contains an affirmative, explicit, atom-preserving instruction matching the packet's frozen cluster `E`, modality `M`, and due interval/date `W`, with a marked DS sentence or detail-field span.
- `H-`: the DS is readable and linked, but no exact affirmative match exists, or it explicitly negates follow-up, uses a conflicting modality or interval, or makes a different/conditional recommendation.
- `H_U`: the DS or source unit is unreadable/truncated, source-to-DS linkage or chronology is unresolved, atom identity is ambiguous, the reader cannot apply the dictionary/codebook, the reader does not respond, or the two-reader protocol is otherwise incomplete. `H_U` is not converted to H- or H+.

An H+ match is exact only under all of the following checks.

1. **Cluster identity.** The DS span contains a positive concept phrase from the frozen cluster lexicon for the packet's `E`, with the same target object/cluster boundary. A generic word such as `nodule`, `lesion`, `opacity`, or `follow-up` without the packet's frozen cluster evidence is not sufficient. Cross-sentence inference, clinical synonym invention, and assumed coreference are prohibited. If the reader cannot identify an exact permitted cluster span, the status is H_U rather than H+.
2. **Modality identity.** The same DS instruction contains the exact canonical modality dictionary ID `M`; a clinically related modality or an unspecified `imaging` phrase does not match. A modality in a separate unrelated DS section does not count.
3. **Interval/date identity.** The same instruction contains the exact canonical interval/date ID `W`. An interval or date in a different instruction does not match. `W*` is not inferred from admission date, discharge date, or a vague temporal phrase. For an exact date, the normalized ISO date must match; for an interval, the canonical interval token must match.
4. **Affirmative scope.** The cluster, modality, and interval must occur in the same affirmative instruction span or in adjacent DS fields linked by the fixed field-ordinal rule. Negation, uncertainty, historical, hypothetical, optional/conditional, or “if clinically indicated” scope over any required atom component disqualifies H+.
5. **Attributable provenance.** The reader marks `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id,field_ordinal,charttime,storetime,D)` and the exact DS character span or detail-field key. A missing attributable span cannot be H+.

The reader sees the packet's fixed atom dictionary row and may use a pre-label codebook with positive and negative examples. No H reader assigns a clinical route or clinical appropriateness. Automated flags are audited navigation aids only.

For the unit-level binary documentation indicator, define `A_i=1` only if both H readers independently assign H+ with valid spans and exact matching; otherwise the unit is not declared positive. If one or both readers assign H- or H_U, retain the reader-level statuses and classify the unit as non-positive for the conservative admission gate. For the target admission, `F_H(i)=1` if any target unit is H- or H_U, if any target unit is not jointly H+, or if any required target H packet is missing. This preserves all-unit adverse precedence: a minority target omission or uncertainty blocks a positive admission handoff designation. For the control, retain the same `A_i` definition but do not reinterpret a control H- as a clinical adverse event.

Reader disagreement is never majority-resolved into H+. A pre-label adjudication rule is deterministic: if both readers are H+ and the independent span checks pass, retain `A=1`; if both are H- and the packet is readable, retain `A=0`; every other disagreement, missing span, unresolved link, or nonresponse is `H_U` for the conservative target-failure gate and is retained as non-positive for the specificity rate. An optional third adjudicator may describe disagreement only if roster, time, and access were pre-registered; it cannot rewrite the two-reader primary statuses or rescue an H_U.

## Paired target/control estimands and positive-support behavior

Let `S_TC` be the frozen full-frame set of admissions with at least one target unit in `g*` and `c_i != NONE`. The first control is fixed by `c_i`; `S_TC` is not defined using H outcomes. The secondary finite-population quantities are:

- target exact-handoff rate `\theta_T = |S_TC|^{-1} sum_{i in S_TC} A_{Ti}`;
- control exact-handoff rate `\theta_C = |S_TC|^{-1} sum_{i in S_TC} A_{Ci}`; and
- paired specificity separation `\Delta = \theta_T - \theta_C = |S_TC|^{-1} sum_{i in S_TC}(A_{Ti}-A_{Ci})`.

The paired estimand deliberately uses the same admission domain for both arms. It does not compare target admissions with a different population of control-only admissions and does not condition on a post-label control response. If a target admission has multiple eligible non-target controls, only its first deterministic `c_i` contributes; alternatives are not searched.

For every selected admission `i`, the target and control packet indicators are observed together whenever `c_i != NONE`; their inclusion probability is the admission's sequential `p_i=pi1_i q_i`, with the inherited distinction between first-stage `pi1_i`, conditional second-stage `q_i`, and any integrated unconditional probability. The paired HT total is

`HT_Δ = sum_{i observed in S_TC} (A_Ti - A_Ci) / (pi1_i q_i)`,

and the paired Hájek contrast is `HT_Δ / HT_TC`, where `HT_TC = sum_{i observed in S_TC} 1/(pi1_i q_i)`. Target and control first-order totals are also reported separately with the same frozen support domain. Nonresponse and H_U remain at original path weight and are not complete-case deleted.

The phase-2 design must give every occupied control-support subcell positive admission inclusion probability. If an occupied subcell has zero or undefined `q_i`, allocation is invalid and fails closed. For any requested second-order variance or covariance quantity, retain `pi1_ij` and conditional `q_ij` separately. A requested pair with `q_ij=0` or undefined positive support is reported as undefined and that pair-based interval or contrast fails closed; no division by zero and no claim of pair estimability is made. This does not invalidate separately estimable first-order totals when their probabilities are positive. No product of conditional factors is described as a marginal unconditional pair probability; complete nested enumeration or equivalent integration is required for any unconditional claim.

The primary inherited admission adverse estimand, routes, target cell, and inference remain exactly those of the parent. The paired H contrast is an additional gate and cannot rescue a failing inherited primary route.

## Margins and gates frozen before labels

The following numerical operating margins are fixed at protocol compilation and written to the pre-label manifest. They are governance/design thresholds for this stored-text experiment, not clinical safety thresholds, effect estimates, or probabilities of clinical benefit.

- **Exact handoff adverse margin:** the upper one-sided simultaneous 95% bound for the target admission H-failure rate must be `<= 0.10` for a supportive handoff result. Any target H- or H_U under all-unit precedence counts as failure.
- **Specificity separation margin:** the lower one-sided simultaneous 95% bound for `Delta` must be `>= 0.20` (20 percentage points) for a supportive target-versus-control result.
- **Scrambled specificity ceiling:** the upper one-sided simultaneous 95% bound for the route-scrambled H+ rate must be `<= 0.05`.
- **Support floor:** at least 120 full-frame paired-control admissions, at least 60 paired-control admissions in the sealed phase-1 master, and paired-control representation in at least two inherited phase-1 strata are required before target selection.
- **Effective support floor:** the inherited effective verified target count, event support, ESS, positive-probability, shared-error, and workload gates remain mandatory; the added paired contrast must have positive denominator support and a finite simultaneous interval. An interval that is undefined, unbounded, or not calculable under the registered design is inconclusive, not favorable.
- **Multiplicity:** all target handoff, target-control, and scrambled-control gates are evaluated with one pre-registered simultaneous one-sided 95% max-statistic adjustment over the finite family of reported exact target/control/scrambled quantities. The family is frozen before labels; no favorable endpoint may be selected after inspection.

A favorable contrast must satisfy the margin with the lower simultaneous bound, not merely have a positive point estimate. If `Delta` is positive but its lower bound is below 0.20, the added actionability gate fails as inconclusive, and no bridge nomination is permitted. If the simultaneous bound is below zero, the exact target-linked specificity claim is adverse. If the target rate is below the control rate, the claim is adverse even if the inherited route passes.

The route-scrambled control is generated before H labels using the committed cryptographic seed. For each eligible control packet, replace the packet's modality and interval fields by a deterministic permutation of the fixed target/control atom pool while retaining source and DS provenance and marking the packet as non-clinical. The scrambled atom must never equal the original atom, and its tokens must be selected without inspecting DS text or H outcomes. A scrambled H+ is a specificity failure, not a clinical event. If the permutation has no valid derangement for a required packet, the control construction is invalid and the added gate is inconclusive; it is not repaired after labels.

## Workload and ledger freeze before labels

The added H/control workload is included in the inherited whole-admission ledger. The following cost inputs are frozen before phase-1 labels:

- `t2_max_minutes_per_unit`: the maximum active minutes per target or control unit from synthetic-only rehearsal, not a mean and not estimated from real labels;
- `Umax_d`: the maximum inherited recommendation-unit count in every pre-label stratum `d`;
- `Cmax_i`: the deterministic number of H packets per admission under the sealed control rule, equal to zero or one for the within-admission control, plus the fixed route-scrambled packet charge;
- fixed H codebook/calibration, duplicate, negative-control, and roster charges;
- two-reader staffing multiplier and pre-registered nonresponse/re-read charge, if any; and
- the inherited reviewer, weekly, roster, and 32-week ceilings.

Before labels, enumerate every possible post-label phase-1 band partition allowed by the inherited state machine. For each partition, compute the maximum target and control packet count under the fixed floor, sentinel census, and target-quota rules. The target certificate must use the maximum of these costs, not an expected or mean cost. If any possible partition exceeds the available budget or cannot reach the inherited 120–300 distinct-admission range, the target is `NO_SELECTION`.

After labels are sealed, solve the inherited allocation exactly as before, including occupied `d=(h,z)` and target/control role subcells. The authoritative distinct-admission equation remains:

`T = T_micro + T_floor + T_target_extra + T_non_target_extra + T_control_only`.

Each distinct admission is counted once even when it carries target, control, calibration, or route-scrambled roles. The inherited rules remain binding: `120 <= T <= 300`, target quota at most 180, no unit sampling, no replacement, no post-label top-up, and no duplicate admission count. Fixed controls and all H packet minutes are included once in `raw_minutes`; the charged reserve is exactly

`reserve_minutes = ceil(0.15 * (phase2_reading_minutes + fixed_controls_minutes))`,

and

`charged_minutes = phase2_reading_minutes + fixed_controls_minutes + reserve_minutes <= 84,000`.

`T_control_only` is retained only for pre-registered fixed calibration or negative-control admissions in the inherited ledger; it may not substitute for a missing within-admission control, expand `S_TC`, or rescue a target that fails the pre-label control-support certificate. Controls on sampled target admissions never create a second admission count. If the exact role overlap or cost is not represented in the sealed ledger, the protocol fails closed.

### Pre-label workload/support certificate algorithm

The compiler must evaluate workload feasibility as a finite, source-only certificate, not as a statement that the eventual labels will be cheap. Let `K` be the complete set of inherited phase-1 strata and let `B` be the finite set of all phase-1 label-band partitions permitted by the inherited allocation state machine, including every boundary case created by the fixed floor and sentinel-census rules. For each `(k,b)`, enumerate the complete inherited phase-1 master roster and every allowed phase-2 occupied subcell using only frame fields, the frozen target/control membership hashes, and the registered band definitions; assign each potential admission the maximum number of target, control, scrambled, duplicate, calibration, and fixed-control packets it could receive under that partition. Use `ceil` at each packet-to-minute conversion, and use the larger of the two-reader active-time bound and the registered reread/nonresponse charge. The certificate stores, for every `(k,b)`, `T(k,b)`, `phase2_reading_minutes(k,b)`, `fixed_controls_minutes(k,b)`, `reserve_minutes(k,b)`, `charged_minutes(k,b)`, reviewer-hours, weekly minutes, roster counts, and all positive-support indicators. The governing values are componentwise maxima across `(k,b)`, not averages, simulated expected values, or values learned from labels.

The compiler must also run a pre-label synthetic rehearsal against the frozen packet layout and codebook using only generated text with known atom statuses. The rehearsal is a usability/time calibration and must not use MIMIC text, labels, observed reader times, or any value derived from the target cell. It must report the number of packets, two-reader active minutes, reread/nonresponse charge, codebook/calibration charge, and packet-layout failures. A failed rehearsal, an unbounded or missing `t2_max_minutes_per_unit`, or any partition with `charged_minutes > 84,000`, `T` outside `120..300`, reviewer-hours above 1,400, or weekly/roster/32-week ceilings yields `NO_SELECTION`; no lower-cost allocation may be chosen after labels. The manifest must retain the complete partition certificate and the exact integer arithmetic, including `reserve_minutes = ceil(0.15 * (phase2_reading_minutes + fixed_controls_minutes))`; it must not round the reserve down or apply it after a budget decision.

The support certificate and workload certificate are jointly evaluated before `PHASE1_LABELS_SEALED`. Thus a target can be selected only when (i) the exact source-only paired domain is positive in every required design path, (ii) all allowed post-label allocations preserve positive conditional support, and (iii) the worst-case complete ledger fits. This is a feasibility gate, not an outcome gate: it cannot be weakened because a label is favorable, and a passing certificate does not establish reviewer validity, workflow feasibility, or clinical safety.

## Sampling, inference, and uncertainty

The inherited phase-1 master remains at most 900 distinct admissions, with deterministic maximin SRS within its five inherited outcome-blind strata and the inherited first- and second-order probabilities. Phase-2 sampling remains conditional on the realized phase-1 sample and sealed labels, with all occupied role subcells receiving the inherited fixed floors or census treatment. H packets are attached to selected whole admissions; no recommendation unit is sampled independently.

Store, for every admission and packet, `pi1_i`, `pi1_ij`, conditional `q_i`, conditional `q_ij`, sequential path weight, target/control membership hash, support-domain hash, and ledger role. Use sequential HT/Hájek estimates at original path weight. Compute design uncertainty with the inherited law-of-total-variance procedure and at least 20,000 deterministic nested Rao–Wu-style replicates, reconstructing phase-1 bands and conditional phase-2 SRS. Every replicate must rebuild neither target nor control from labels: it replays the sealed frame, target, control dictionary, and allocation rules. A replicate that yields zero support for a requested quantity reports that quantity as undefined rather than silently substituting a different domain.

The compiler must report, separately and without pooling:

- target H-failure total and denominator under all-unit precedence;
- target exact-handoff rate and simultaneous interval;
- control exact-handoff rate on the same frozen `S_TC` domain;
- paired `Delta` and its simultaneous interval;
- the route-scrambled H+ rate and its simultaneous upper bound;
- structural control absence counts and support counts; and
- all inherited primary route estimands and gates.

No shrinkage, adjacent atom borrowing, consensus rescue, completion-case analysis, or control replacement is permitted. H_U and nonresponse are not deleted. A missing DS or unresolved source linkage is H_U for a sampled target packet and remains a documentation uncertainty; it does not imply missing care or nonadherence.

## Clinically meaningful next-study bridge (without retrospective overclaim)

A supportive retrospective result does not trigger an alert or an order. It produces an atom/route-specific nomination for a separately approved prospective **silent** validation whose protocol is fixed before any live exposure. The nomination must carry the frozen atom IDs `(E*,M*,W*)`, route, source and DS documentation estimates, simultaneous bounds, H-U/nonresponse rates, support and workload certificates, and every adverse unit; it may not be generalized to adjacent pulmonary findings, modalities, intervals, services, or hospitals. A failed or inconclusive atom remains excluded or `DEFER` and cannot be promoted by pooled performance.

The next study must use actual calendar timestamps and collect evidence unavailable here for each eligible opportunity: (1) report availability and delivery/view audit logs with user role and timestamp; (2) target-linked order, referral, scheduling, completion, and result-review events; (3) named accountable service/clinician and patient preference or refusal where applicable; (4) outside-care linkage and loss-to-follow-up ascertainment; (5) the exact UI presentation and any edit/copy/override; and (6) independent clinical adjudication of whether the recommendation was appropriate, whether the finding was real, and whether the eventual action and timing were safe. The silent system must suppress alerts, orders, copied text, referrals, and patient-facing communication; it may log a shadow recommendation and its atom-preserving match only.

Before that study begins, governance must freeze a target-cell-specific primary feasibility estimand: among prospectively eligible opportunities with a source report delivered and a resolvable accountable workflow, the proportion for which the shadow atom is available to the responsible workflow at the prescribed interval, with delivery/view, action, completion, and clinical-appropriateness outcomes reported separately. The retrospective result is a selection gate only. The prospective study must report absolute denominators, delivery/view ascertainment, action and completion with right-censoring and outside-care unknowns, safety adjudications, and adverse events; it must not use retrospective DS H+ as a surrogate for any of these. A pre-registered progression rule should require adequate target-cell support, no unresolved high-severity safety signal, and prespecified precision for availability and workflow measures; if delivery/view logs, responsibility, or clinical adjudication are missing, the result is `DEFER`, not evidence of benefit. Only a later governed comparative study, with an intervention, contemporaneous control, actual-calendar follow-up, and patient-level clinical outcomes, could test alert utility, safety, equity, or benefit.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

**Supportive added handoff evidence** requires all inherited primary gates and every added gate to pass: target H-failure upper bound `<=0.10`; positive paired support; simultaneous lower bound for `Delta >=0.20`; scrambled H+ simultaneous upper bound `<=0.05`; valid independent H rosters and seals; and no chronology, target-freeze, dictionary, packet, ledger, or nonresponse invariant failure. This supports only that the exact target recommendation is more specifically preserved in canonical DS text than the matched non-target control under this frozen protocol. It permits consideration of a separately approved prospective silent workflow bridge with outputs suppressed or `DEFER`, never a live alert, copied recommendation, order, referral, or patient-facing action.

**Adverse added evidence** includes any target H- or H_U under the admission all-unit rule when the target cell is otherwise sufficiently supported; a target H-failure bound above 0.10; a simultaneous `Delta` bound below 0 or a control rate at least as high as the target; a scrambled H+ upper bound above 0.05; a target/control exact-match failure; or any inherited primary route failure. The exact cell is excluded from bridge priority. H_U is not converted to RECON, and no later cell or subgroup may be searched for a favorable result. An adverse stored-text finding remains not a claim of unsafe care, because clinical truth and workflow are unavailable.

**Inconclusive evidence** includes `NO_SELECTION`, fewer than the pre-label support floors, lack of positive first-order support, zero/undefined requested pair support, undefined or excessively wide simultaneous intervals, failed calibration or adjudication, nonresponse or incomplete packets, dictionary or source-link ambiguity, invalid target/control freeze, workload or roster failure, or structural absence in a domain for which the protocol did not obtain the required support certificate. These map to `DEFER` and require additional data or a separately governed design. They do not establish that the recommendation was clinically wrong, that follow-up did not occur, or that outside care was absent.

## Falsification and verification contract

The protocol must fail closed on any of the following:

- target or control membership selected, altered, or replaced after clinical labels;
- a control candidate selected by an H outcome, route, reader preference, or favorable DS appearance;
- a control failing the exact pre-label modality/interval/non-target grammar but being retained;
- target and control sharing an inseparable source unit or target cluster;
- generic `follow up`, a related modality, a different interval, negated text, conditional text, historical text, or uncertain text being scored H+;
- missing or non-attributable DS span being scored H+;
- a control absent from the frozen `S_TC` domain being imputed, duplicated, or replaced by a control-only admission;
- support floors checked after labels or relaxed after a sparse result;
- any margin, multiplicity family, derangement seed, or workload time changed after labels;
- route leakage, H roster overlap, packet identity leakage, or post-D evidence;
- omission of an adverse or uncertain minority target unit;
- H nonresponse or U status deleted by complete-case analysis;
- an estimator dividing by zero or undefined `q_i`, `q_ij`, or support denominator;
- conditional probabilities described as unconditional marginal probabilities;
- fixed controls or the single 15% reserve omitted from the charged ledger;
- duplicate admission counting, unit sampling, post-label top-up, or mean-time substitution;
- use of discharge location, death, ICU, services, or transfers as evidence of completed follow-up; or
- any conclusion about delivery, viewing, responsibility, action, completion, appropriateness, safety, benefit, or causality.

The compiler must emit machine-readable provenance for every target and control packet: `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id,field_ordinal,charttime,storetime,D)`, source text-span/field key, canonical `E/M/W` IDs, control-candidate order, target/control membership freeze hash, dictionary hash, H reader statuses, marked DS span or reason code, inclusion probabilities, support-domain membership, scrambled seed/permutation ID, and ledger role. It must emit a pre-label manifest containing the numerical margins, support thresholds, simultaneous family, cost constants, and worst-case partition certificate.

The verifier must independently test:

1. source joins, one-admission-per-subject ordering, pre-D chronology, reciprocal chain, and unique canonical DS;
2. target immutability under phase-1 label permutation;
3. deterministic candidate-universe reconstruction from source-only fields;
4. exact control disjointness, modality/interval matching, first-order ordering, duplicate suppression, and structural absence;
5. pre-label support counts and `N_TC` certificate;
6. exact H+ span, cluster, modality, interval, polarity, and provenance rules;
7. all-unit target adverse precedence and conservative H disagreement handling;
8. positive `pi1_i` and conditional `q_i` support, zero-pair fail-closed behavior, and correct paired HT/Hájek weights;
9. route-scrambled derangement construction independent of H labels;
10. simultaneous margins and no post-label threshold changes;
11. distinct-admission ledger, fixed-control charges, one charged 15% reserve, reviewer/weekly/roster/deadline ceilings; and
12. nested variance replay with at least 20,000 deterministic replicates.

A fixture can establish only computational feasibility of these mechanics. It cannot establish that a reader's H judgment is clinically true, that a DS instruction was delivered or acted on, or that a prospective bridge is safe or beneficial. Those claims require prospective delivery/view logs, orders/referrals/scheduling, named responsibility, patient preference, outside-care linkage, completed imaging, images, and independent clinical adjudication that are not available in this MIMIC snapshot.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

The child advances the selected protocol from an underspecified claim that target-linked discharge documentation might be compared with a non-target control to a fully frozen paired-control experiment. The control universe is now a source-derived, deterministic, label-independent set matched exactly on modality and interval, with fixed non-target disjointness, first-control ordering, support counts, and a pre-label `NO_SELECTION` rule when the contrast cannot be supported. H+ now requires exact frozen cluster, modality, interval, affirmative scope, and attributable DS provenance. The 20-point separation margin, 5% scrambled specificity ceiling, 10% target-failure margin, simultaneous family, and worst-case packet/workload certificate are registered before labels. Structural absence, zero support, disagreement, nonresponse, and budget failure have deterministic adverse or inconclusive handling rather than inviting post hoc rescue.

The inherited population, route, chronology, canonical artifact, all-unit safeguards, two-phase design, ledger, inference, and silent-bridge-only evidence ceiling are unchanged. The remaining uncertainty is fundamental and explicit: MIMIC stores text rather than actual workflow; timestamps are subject-shifted; a DS omission can represent missing documentation, outside care, a decision not to pursue follow-up, or an unresolved artifact; the H readers are not a clinical gold standard; and operational margins are not safety thresholds. Even a supportive result would justify only consideration of a separately approved prospective silent validation, never a live intervention or a claim of clinical benefit.
