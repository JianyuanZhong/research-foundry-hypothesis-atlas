## Decision-relevant repair and unresolved question

The parent asks whether a dictionary-certified signed deterioration vector improves a fixed-capacity review list beyond current values and repeat spacing. Its scientific question is important, but the primary estimand fails if the local evidence cannot certify, for every field, the clinical worsening direction. The catalog explicitly states that most per-field labels, units and coding are absent. This successor removes only that unverifiable direction assumption.

The new question is:

> Among participants in the parent’s frozen central current-risk band who have two comparable measurements of the prespecified eight-field panel, does the magnitude of observed prior-to-current profile movement—computed without assigning any analyte a worsening direction—improve held-out fixed-capacity capture and yield of the parent’s subsequent recorded circulatory endpoint beyond current levels and repeat spacing, and does that increment transport from assessments through 31 December 2012 to the 2013 assessment cohort without refitting?

This is a clinically consequential monitoring question: if a second panel is retained, should review capacity be informed by a large cross-field movement between the prior and current panel even when the direction of change cannot be clinically certified? It could inform whether repeat history contains decision-relevant information for prioritizing review. A positive result would justify prospective work on repeat-panel review; it would not establish that movement is biological instability, disease progression, treatment response, or clinical utility. A null result would weaken the case for retaining a second panel for this recorded-risk review task, within the supported population and endpoint.

The primary feature is therefore called **observed absolute profile movement**, not deterioration, worsening, instability, or disease trajectory. It is a direction-free statistical representation of paired numeric observations. “Discordance” is used only for the prespecified difference between the prior and current standardized profiles, not as a clinical diagnosis.

These facts do not establish analyte identities, biological mechanisms, clinical worsening directions, specimen timing, disease onset, outpatient disease, kidney disease, multimorbidity, treatment, assay error, or complete follow-up. The experiment tests only whether a reproducible direction-free representation of the paired numeric values has incremental conditional association with the specified recorded endpoint under the fixed-capacity policy.

The untested claims are:

1. Absolute standardized paired movement adds held-out fixed-K target-event capture/yield beyond current values and the verified T0-to-T1 interval.
2. Any increment is not solely an artifact of repeat-panel selection, current risk, interval length, marginal prior values, regression to the mean, or calendar/assay practice.
3. A nonlinear model can reveal prespecified concentrated, threshold-like or current-by-movement patterns that a one-number additive baseline loses, while retaining the same scientific contrast and temporal safeguards.

No event count, fitted estimate, clinical recommendation, treatment effect, biological interpretation or external-validation claim is made here.

## Direction-free feature construction

For each training fold f and each field j, compute a location m_{j,f} and positive scale s_{j,f} from eligible training-fold T0 values only. Use the prespecified robust scale s=IQR(T0)/1.349; fail closed if it is nonfinite or zero. No global negative-code rule is permitted. Values are numeric only after the field-specific certificate has identified valid/missing values.

For an eligible person i evaluated by fold f, define:

u_{ij}=|x_{ij,1}-x_{ij,0}|/s_{j,f}, for j=1,...,8,

where x_{ij,0} is the T0 value and x_{ij,1} is the T1 value. The primary direction-free movement summary is

U_i = sqrt((u_{i1}^2+...+u_{i8}^2)/8).

U is the RMS Euclidean distance between the paired profiles after field-local training-fold scaling. It is invariant to reversing the direction of any field, has no analyte-direction label, and must not be interpreted as worsening. For the final early fit applied to late data, m and s are recomputed once using all eligible early training data and then frozen for late application. No late distribution or outcome determines scaling.

Secondary preregistered representations are the eight u_j values, max_j(u_j), and count_j[ u_j>=1 ]. They are descriptive/alternative predictors and do not replace the scalar U primary contrast. The threshold 1 is a scale unit, not a clinical threshold. No feature is imputed, direction-signed or winsorized after looking at outcomes.

A separate representation audit may use fold-local percentile displacement if units are certified as unavailable but numeric cross-instance comparability and missing meanings are certified. That audit is not evidence for the primary U hypothesis and cannot rescue a failed primary comparability/timing gate.

## Models and substantive method alternatives

All models use the same people, endpoint, split, frozen A band, K, held-out evaluation and uncertainty.

- M0: current eight-field panel + age + sex + T1 calendar year; used only to define A.
- M1a transparent baseline: current panel + age + sex + T1 calendar year + L.
- M1u primary transparent movement model: M1a + U_i in a regularized multinomial T/D/N model.
- M1v transparent vector sensitivity: M1a + the eight u_j values, with no signs.
- M2a matched learned baseline: shallow gradient-boosted multinomial T/D/N model with exactly M1a inputs.
- M2v matched learned movement model: the same booster and prespecified small depth, learning-rate, regularization and tree-limit grid with exactly M1a plus the eight u_j inputs.

M1u is the primary scientific contrast because it is an interpretable one-number test of whether direction-free profile movement adds information beyond current levels and repeat spacing. M1v tests whether field-specific magnitudes add information that RMS aggregation loses. M2v is substantive rather than decorative: it can reveal prespecified nonlinear thresholds, concentration in a subset of fields and current-level-by-movement interactions that additive M1v may average away. M2v cannot substitute for the primary M1u contrast, and a learned gain alone cannot establish instability, deterioration or a clinical mechanism.

The baseline-versus-learned comparison is scientifically matched: same target, same data, same split, same A band, same capacity, same uncertainty and same movement information. The learned model is selected only to test whether nonlinear distribution of direction-free movement changes the scientific conclusion, not to chase a small predictive score. A latent mechanistic/aging model is deferred because the local catalog lacks certified analyte labels, units, specimen timing and a validated mechanistic target. A causal treatment or repeat-testing policy analysis is deferred because treatment, indication and randomized testing data are not bound.

## Primary estimands, uncertainty and falsification

For model M and era e, let R_{M,i} be the held-out target probability and B_{M,i}(K) indicate membership among the K largest R in A. For the unweighted analysis and separately with frozen selection weights w_i:

C_A(M,e)=sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T)B_{M,i}(K) / sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T)

P_A(M,e)=sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=T)B_{M,i}(K) / sum_i w_i B_{M,i}(K).

The primary contrasts are:

Delta_C_abs(e)=C_A(M1u,e)-C_A(M1a,e)
Delta_P_abs(e)=P_A(M1u,e)-P_A(M1a,e)
tau_C_abs=Delta_C_abs(late)-Delta_C_abs(early).

The primary hypothesis is Delta_C_abs(late)>0.05, with a two-sided 95% paired participant-bootstrap interval wholly above the prespecified five-percentage-point research margin, no early-to-late direction reversal, and tau_C_abs within [-0.05,0.05]. The margin is an operational study decision margin, not a validated clinical-benefit threshold. Report K, selected counts, capture/yield numerators and denominators, missed-target rate, D capture/yield, and reviewed/non-reviewed T/D/N frequencies. Report unweighted and selection-weighted estimates separately.

Use 2,000 paired participant bootstrap replicates, resampling within fixed era-by-Q cells while retaining the split, endpoint, A/Q labels, K, held-out predictions, selections and weights. Report failed-support replicates. Use a declared Holm adjustment for the primary capture, yield and transport family; report calibration, Brier/log loss, one-vs-rest calibration intercept/slope, reliability and resolution for every model and era. A secondary bootstrap refits the early pipeline and reapplies it to late data to quantify training uncertainty.

Preregister these falsification and integrity checks:

- permute endpoint labels within fold and era while preserving class counts;
- permute U within era-by-A-by-L-quartile strata, preserving current values, interval, risk band and movement marginal distribution;
- independently permute each prior panel vector within era-by-A-by-L-quartile strata and recompute U, breaking within-person pairing while retaining current levels and interval;
- permute prior-level vectors as the parent information-null control;
- permute early/late era labels;
- compare against random K selection in A;
- deliberately break same-position code/date pairing and verify mandatory integrity failure;
- verify that independently sorting outcome codes and dates is never accepted;
- compare complete-case results with a fold-local missingness audit, without allowing post-outcome imputation into the primary result;
- report spacing-quartile contrasts and movement-by-spacing descriptive interaction, without changing the primary estimand;
- run calibration, resolution, fold-instability, tie-handling, overlap, ESS and policy-arithmetic checks.

If movement permutation leaves the primary increment intact, the attribution to paired movement is falsified. Endpoint or era permutation producing systematic policy gain invalidates implementation. A random-K gain is an implementation warning. Reversing analyte directions is not a sensitivity because the primary feature has no directions; any signed deterioration analysis remains a separately gated parent continuity result.

## Interpretation boundaries

Supportive evidence requires all source, join, endpoint, death, comparability, missingness, specimen-timing and five-year-ascertainment gates; identical feasible K; adequate support and overlap; calibrated held-out predictions; positive Delta_C_abs(late) beyond 0.05 with the complete adjusted interval above the margin; no early-to-late reversal; consistent selection-weighted direction; stable fold-level behavior; and null permutations that remove the signal. This supports only a conditional, capacity-normalized association between observed two-assessment numeric profile movement and capture of the specified recorded inpatient T endpoint in the supported UKB A-band population.

Adverse evidence includes no increment, a negative or reversed increment, a gain explained by current level, spacing, prior level or selection weighting, disappearance after movement permutation, poor calibration, fold instability, temporal reversal, or invalid null controls. It weakens the case for retaining repeat-panel history for this recorded-risk task but does not prove no value for another population, endpoint or workflow.

Inconclusive evidence includes absent comparability/missingness/timing/ascertainment certification, malformed arrays, death disagreement, sparse events, poor ESS/overlap, broad intervals, conflicting missingness sensitivities, assay-scale concern, or an unstable learned model. It is also inconclusive if movement predicts the endpoint but cannot be separated from regression to the mean, treatment, assay drift, repeat-selection bias or calendar-practice change with the available data.

Computationally checkable claims are source/header/schema integrity, joins, flow counts, exact endpoint construction, event ordering, fold-local movement construction, held-out fits, K selection, capture/yield arithmetic, calibration, bootstrap intervals, overlap/ESS and permutation behavior. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for analyte meaning beyond the approved dictionary, specimen validity, biological instability, disease onset, severity, outpatient disease, kidney disease, multimorbidity, treatment effects, action utilities, clinical thresholds, complete ascertainment beyond supplied certification, external validation and prospective policy evaluation. No automatic verifier can establish those claims. The two measurements cannot identify a turning point or mechanism.

The 2013 cohort is an internal temporal application within the same UKB discovery partition, not external validation. Selection weights address only the declared repeat-panel/eligibility sensitivity model. They do not repair participation bias, treatment indication, changing ascertainment or transport outside UKB.

## Method-selection record and compute

The scientific deliverable is fixed before fitting: newly verify the four-table audit and certificates; create fold-local direction-free u_j and U features; fit M1a/M1u/M1v and matched M2a/M2v; generate one held-out prediction and fixed-K policy-selection record for each eligible A-band eid in early and late eras; estimate Delta_C_abs, Delta_P_abs and tau_C_abs with uncertainty; complete calibration, selection-weighted and falsification outputs; and finish with uncertainty-quantified estimates under passed gates or an auditable fail-closed report naming the missing dependency.

The simple baseline is M1a because it represents the review information already available at the current assessment: current values, age/sex and elapsed repeat spacing, without any prior-current movement. The selected transparent alternative M1u adds one prespecified RMS direction-free movement quantity, yielding a direct estimand rather than relying on a learned score. M1v preserves field-specific magnitudes for sensitivity. The matched learned M2v uses the same baseline and the same direction-free inputs and may reveal nonlinear threshold/concentration/interaction structure that the additive model loses. Its resource budget is five-fold tabular multinomial fitting, a small CPU-suitable boosted grid, selection weighting, fixed-K evaluation and 2,000-replicate paired bootstrap, with up to 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB memory and 28800 seconds. This estimate is unmeasured for the exact implementation; no full study was run in discovery. CPU is selected because the planned models are small tabular fits and no GPU-dependent method is required. The deployment inventory includes eight A100-SXM4 80-GB devices, but GPU use is neither required nor rewarded and would be revisited only if a bounded measurement showed that repeated fitting materially changes feasibility.

The signed parent M1d/M2d deterioration models are explicitly not selected as the new primary because the local catalog does not certify eight analyte-specific worsening directions. The raw unsigned vector is not called deterioration and is not given a clinical interpretation. A latent factor, mechanistic aging model, disease-sequence model, genetics, imaging and multimorbidity endpoint are deferred because the required verified inputs or adjudication are unavailable. Revisit these alternatives only after a certified field dictionary/specimen audit, adequate outcome adjudication and a distinct prespecified estimand.
