For each ordered assay median-bin [0,12), [12,24), [24,36), [36,48]; A=1 only for at least two bins spanning at least 12 hours. Then h is midpoint span, M=absolute first-to-last rate, and D=signed rate; otherwise h=M=D=0. Standardize h/M/D among available development records, then zero-fill unavailable records while retaining A.

Common inputs: age, sex, log valid operation duration plus missingness, year, laparoscopic and coarse major-resection flags; per-label missingness/time; ordered-assay row/bin counts and A; total unique lab timestamps; presence and first-start hour for frozen albumin, loop-diuretic, vasopressor, antibiotic, and hemostatic lexicons.
