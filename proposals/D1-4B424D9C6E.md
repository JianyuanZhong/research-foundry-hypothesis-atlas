# Retrospective fixed-K corpus-content ranking of documentary M2 in HCC

## Targeted repair and clinical question

This is a targeted successor to [prior hypothesis]. The single consequential residual defect is latent-state leakage into the estimand: the parent correctly says report access is unobserved, but then indexes results by access fractions that do not identify which patients' reports are exposed. Different allocations at the same fraction can change the top-K. Related wording also calls a procedure-plus-pathology record a completed resection, an operation-derived offset a decision time, and same-encounter untimed pathology postoperative, although none is observed.

The repair keeps the same question but separates an identifiable retrospective content experiment from an explicitly unidentified access experiment:

> In adults with a source-documented first eligible HCC resection episode and no recorded HCC treatment in the prior year, does a locked model using eventual stored CT/MRI report content from examinations acquired before an operation-derived analytical cutoff place more encounter-documentary M2 cases in a fixed top-K than (i) the same fitted model with all semantic inputs masked and (ii) an independently fitted nonsemantic model, in each of two future calendar years?

This is case-cohort enrichment/ranking conditional on future-observed resection and HCC pathology records. It is not screening, referral, triage, a preoperative decision rule, or a causal effect. Its value is to decide whether the stored report corpus contains enough stable incremental signal to justify collecting report-version/access logs and testing a prospective silent-mode list. It cannot show that such a list was available, actionable, beneficial, or safe.

## Evidence boundary and unresolved claim

The strongest claim already supported is documentary. HCC snapshot [source checksum] links patient/encounter records, procedure start/end fields, examination accessions and acquisition-like starts, eventual examination text, untimed same-encounter pathology text, and dated medication/order fields. It supports a retrospective resection-plus-pathology cohort and temporal testing based on operation and examination fields. It does not contain operation scheduling/cancellation/completion status, clinical decision time, report authored/final/release/view/version times, a referral roster, reviewer capacity, pathology time or specimen/slide/block/sampling identifiers, recurrence, survival, or benefit.

Current literature motivates but does not answer the hypothesis. Neves and Soares (Cancers 2026;18:2028, DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028; PMCID PMC13360217; acquired full-text source [source checksum]) found 35/36 reviewed HCC early-recurrence AI studies were retrospective, only 6/36 externally validated, and calibration/clinical utility were inconsistently reported. Fan et al. (Journal of Gastrointestinal Oncology 2026, DOI 10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944; acquired HTML source [source checksum]) reported 346 M0, 169 M1 and 106 M2 cases among 621 resected HCC patients and modest LDH discrimination (AUC 0.610), while calling for external/prospective validation and cautioning against changing surgery from LDH alone. Neither validates this source, endpoint, features, or model.

The unresolved claim is that eventual report content, within the future-defined source cohort, adds more than 5 documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed ranking positions over both baselines and is robust to source, operative-clock, semantic, and outcome uncertainty. A preoperative-access claim additionally requires patient-level immutable report versions with authored/final/release/view times linked to an observed decision timestamp. Biological or resection-specimen M2 requires specimen-linked timed pathology or primary slide/block review.

The research-ambition README was inspected for availability and provenance. The Cell main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; only metadata and supplement are present, so they are not cited as inspected evidence.

## Population and finite patient-level source states

The patient key is 患者主索引; encounter linkage is (患者主索引, 就诊号). There is one index record per patient. Exact duplicates may be removed only after retaining raw ordinal, multiplicity, unmatched-row audit, and backpointer.

Two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician, blinded to test outcomes and scores, freeze a dictionary of eligible HCC resections and reconcile 手术 and 手术来源. A procedure record is called a source-documented resection episode, not a completed operation. Eligibility requires:

1. age at least 18 on the linked encounter;
2. the patient's earliest eligible resection episode;
3. at least one same-encounter pathology row whose composed text explicitly documents HCC after text adjudication; and
4. no recorded earlier HCC resection/transplant/TACE/ablation/radiotherapy/targeted therapy/immunotherapy in [c_d-365 days,c_d), searched patient-wide in procedures, medications and non-drug orders using their dated fields.

This deliberately conditions on a future-observed resection record and same-encounter HCC pathology. People without surgery/pathology, with cancelled/planned operations not distinguishable in source, or outside the extract are not controls. Recorded-treatment-naive is not biological treatment-naive. Untimed diagnoses may corroborate wording but cannot date treatment, define negatives, or enter predictors.

For each patient create finite admissible alternatives. A validated non-midnight procedure timestamp is a point; date-only or unvalidated midnight is [date,date+24h). Missing, competing, adjacent-day and source-discordant procedure rows remain alternatives. Never infer anesthesia or completion from 手术来源. Examination times are handled analogously. Only boundary-relevant interval endpoints are needed.

For d in {12,24,48,72} hours define c_d=t_op-d. This is an analytical cutoff, not a decision, report-release, referral, or availability time. Twenty-four hours is the labeled central analysis; robustness requires all four. A complete state jointly fixes resection episode, operation endpoint, calendar partition, prior-treatment status, encounter, examination partition/timing/modality, text interpretation, membership, and outcome. Extrema may be optimized, but marginally favorable choices from different states cannot be combined.

Development is 2015-2018, tuning/locking is 2019, and 2020/2021 are separate tests. Any patient whose admissible state reaches a later partition is quarantined from every earlier fit. Patients ambiguous between test years remain coupled alternatives, not duplicates. Later years are drift/ascertainment audits only.

## Examination units and report-content operation

Construct units before filtering as (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号), retaining every component row. Blank 检查号 is identity-unknown and semantics are masked. Conflicting modalities, component starts more than 24 hours apart, or merged-identity evidence create coupled alternatives for indivisible accession and deterministic normalized (检查, calendar date of 开始时间) units, all with backpointers.

An acquisition is eligible when its admissible 开始时间 lies in [c_d-90 days,c_d); boundary overlap creates alternatives. Select at most the latest CT and latest MRI whole unit by maximum component start, frozen raw ordinal, then patient hash. Two blinded abdominal radiologists freeze 检查 as CT, MRI, other, or unresolved. Concatenate every component's 检查所见 and 检查诊断 in raw order; never expose selected phrases.

Primary content state E exposes all eventual stored text of selected units. This is a retrospective data operation after cohort assembly, not evidence the text existed before c_d. Blank text is source-text-missing, never imputed as feature absent. Null state 0 replaces every semantic input with source-text-unavailable while retaining nonsemantic acquisition facts.

Semantic features are prespecified statements: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites. Each is present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned when text exists, or source-text-unavailable. Two Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists review ambiguous test parses; a third resolves wording disagreements. Review establishes what stored text says, not when it was authored/viewed.

## Models, K, estimand, and baselines

Nonsemantic features are age, sex, CT/MRI/other acquisition indicators, eligible-unit count, acquisition-to-cutoff recency, component count, identity ambiguity, source-text-missing, and development-frequency 机器型号 category. Exclude report semantics, pathology, diagnoses, labs, clinical documents, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff facts.

S adds semantics to the nonsemantic set. B is an independently fitted nonsemantic elastic-net logistic model. S0 is not refit: it applies fitted S with all semantics source-text-unavailable. S versus S0 is same-fit input ablation; S versus B asks whether a full semantic model beats a separately optimized nonsemantic model. Neither is causal.

Fit S and B independently in 2015-2018 by minimizing patient-clustered robust logistic loss, with each patient's contribution the maximum loss over admissible feature/outcome alternatives. Select penalty/mixing from a frozen grid in 2019 by minimum fixed-K capture over coherent 2019 states, ties favoring stronger regularization. No test/post-cutoff fact enters fitting. Calibration is secondary and cannot alter ranking or conclusions.

For state s, offset d, and year a, let N_a(s,d) be eligible index size without M grade or semantic content. Freeze:

N_ref = min over s,d,a=2015..2019 of N_a(s,d), and K_q=floor(q*N_ref), q in {.05,.10,.20}.

Confirmatory q=.10; .05/.20 are multiplicity-controlled sensitivities. Require N_ref>=500, primary K>=50, and every test state to contain K. Rank by score, frozen patient hash, then raw episode backpointer.

For M in {S(E),S0,B}, coherent Y(s), define:

T_M(s,y,d,q) = 100/K_q times the sum of Y_i(s) among topK(M;s,y,d,q).

This is documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed positions in the future-defined source cohort. Primary contrasts are D=T_S(E)-T_S0 and C=T_S(E)-T_B. Since all choose exactly K, utilization is identically one and no service/utilization term is interpreted.

Secondary baselines are age/sex-only, acquisition-only, first-K by analytical index order, 1,000 seeded random-K lists, and an outcome-aware oracle. The oracle is a retrospective ceiling. No baseline represents existing care, equal opportunity, workload, capacity, or action.

## Patient-level report-access uncertainty: secondary partial identification

No access fraction enters the primary estimand. For secondary stress testing, each patient has whole-unit masks (A_CT,A_MRI); absent modalities are zero. In CT-only, MRI-only, and each corresponding modality among dual-modality members, prescribe marginal exposure fractions alpha_CT and alpha_MRI from {0,.25,.50,.75,1}. Convert fractions to integer counts by a frozen largest-remainder rule. A(alpha) contains every patient-level allocation satisfying those counts, including all allowed CT/MRI overlaps.

For each allocation, apply already-fitted S with only allocated whole units semantically visible. Define I_D(alpha)=[minimum D, maximum D] and I_C analogously, taking extrema jointly over allocation, source state, and outcome state.

Solve extrema exactly by mixed-integer optimization. Each patient has one access-state variable and top-K indicator; precomputed scores for none/CT/MRI/both link to access state; exactly K are selected; a score threshold plus frozen tie key enforces top-K; count constraints enforce alpha. Nonzero gap, timeout, or disagreement with brute-force fixtures makes a cell inconclusive. This is allocation uncertainty, not release probability or missing-at-random.

Alpha=(1,1) must reproduce S(E), and (0,0) S0, bitwise. A cell is allocation-robust only if simultaneous lower bounds exceed 5 for both contrasts across every allocation and primary state. Actual fractions/allocations are absent, so even this does not establish preoperative feasibility.

## Encounter-documentary M2 and pathology uncertainty

Compose 病理, 检查所见, 检查诊断 over every exact-deduplicated pathology row sharing selected (患者主索引,就诊号). Pathology has no time or specimen/accession key. The endpoint is encounter-documentary M2, not postoperative, resection-specimen, reconstructed histology, or biological M2.

A frozen position/context parser assigns Y={1} only when M2 is anchored to microvascular invasion/HCC grade; Y={0} only for anchored M0/M1 without M2; and Y={0,1} for missing grade, unanchored mention, boilerplate, conflict, uncertain HCC attribution, or inadequate text. Two blinded Chinese-reading pathologists adjudicate all parsed test M2 and every text-resolvable unknown/conflict; a third resolves disagreement. Missing time/specimen/sampling cannot be adjudicated. Remaining unknowns retain both values in the coherent state and are never dropped or coded negative.

Report all-row documentary, parser-only, and adjudicated set-valued envelopes. Require at least 85% anchored adjudicated grade, at least 50 M2 events per year in the lower envelope, and primary conclusions robust to every outcome completion. Failure is inconclusive, not evidence against biological MVI.

## Inference, gates, and falsification

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits. Each rebuilds population alternatives, robust development fits, 2019 tuning, units, semantic states, top-K sets, and outcome envelopes. K, dictionaries, features, alpha grid, margin, and rules stay frozen. Construct max-T simultaneous one-sided 97.5% bounds over D/C, both test years, four offsets, coherent source/outcome states, and confirmatory q=.10. q sensitivities and access sets are separately labeled simultaneous families.

Run 1,000 year/modality/unit-count/identity/format-stratified semantic-block permutations with complete refits. Gates: exact hashes/joins; one record/patient; quarantine/future-row invariance; K feasibility; complete state/backpointer ledger; M2 parser PPV>=.98 and sensitivity>=.95; semantic-feature PPV/sensitivity>=.85; modality agreement>=.95 and unresolved<=1%; at least 50 lower-envelope M2/year; anchored grade>=85%; no required feature/pattern shift >15 percentage points from 2019; semantic permutation rejection at familywise one-sided .025; bitwise full-mask identity; independent B fitting; exact MIP/brute-force agreement for N<=12; zero forbidden lineage; and <=2.5% failed bootstraps.

The confirmatory hypothesis is the conjunction D>5 and C>5 in 2020 and 2021 at q=.10, for every offset and coherent state/outcome completion.

- Supportive: every gate passes and every required simultaneous lower bound is strictly >5. This supports robust retrospective corpus-content ranking potential only.
- Adverse to the robust hypothesis: gates pass and any required contrast/year/offset has simultaneous upper bound <=5. This falsifies the universal >5 claim. Upper bound <0 supports worse documentary ranking only in that scope. Mixed favorable/adverse cells are heterogeneity, not proof semantics are generally useless.
- Inconclusive: neither rule holds; bounds cross 5, source/outcome envelopes straddle it, or any feasibility/adjudication/permutation/provenance/optimization gate fails.

Labels are separate for the content experiment and each hypothetical access cell. A positive estimate alone is never supportive; gate failure never becomes a biological negative conclusion.

## Verifier contract and claim limits

The verifier can check hashes/headers, joins, filters, patient uniqueness, quarantine, interval/state coupling, accession construction, forbidden lineage, fit/tuning boundaries, K/ties, S(E)/S0 identities, independent B fit, bootstrap/permutation/MIP arithmetic, envelopes, gates, and conclusion locators. Each conclusion row carries year, d, q, K, N, top-K M2 envelope, contrast/bounds, state IDs, gates, source/code hashes, and raw result locator.

Fixtures test: K=100 with 40 versus 27 gives 13/100; equal sets give zero; swapping negates; fewer than K/unknown outcomes are not padded; full mask is bitwise S0; later rows do not change earlier results; incompatible operation/examination/access/pathology alternatives cannot mix; alpha endpoints reproduce E/0; MIP equals brute force at N<=12; and numerically correct results labeled referral performance, report availability, preoperative utility, treatment effect, biological M2, recurrence benefit, workflow efficiency, or patient benefit are rejected. Correct bounded supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations must pass.

No verifier/reference execution can establish operation completion, real-time report access, specimen linkage, biological M2, clinician action, acceptable tradeoffs, fairness, transportability, recurrence, survival, harm, or benefit. These require expert review, new timestamped/versioned data, and prospective silent-mode/impact study. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility and conclusion discipline, not truth.
