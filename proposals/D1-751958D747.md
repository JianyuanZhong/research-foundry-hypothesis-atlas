# Compiler-ready successor: frozen eICU respiratory-label proxy with pre-D ascertainment diagnostics and competing exits

## Status and substantive change

This is a substantive successor of the two assigned assessed-valid parents `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead-frozen scientific schema exactly: 540 person-landmark rows, 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays, and 74 hospitals; landmarks `{1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after unit admission; the first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`; the two literal labels; the inherited exclusions; the first qualifying decision time `D`; the fixed raw thresholds and windows; and a descriptive, noncausal conditional association/predictive-increment estimand.

The only additions are compatible safeguards. First, an outcome-blind ascertainment diagnostic is fitted using training hospitals only and information available by `D` (including pre-D source opportunity and pre-D measurement history). It diagnoses whether endpoint observability differs by arm/site and whether any apparent label increment is plausibly documentation-opportunity dependent; it does not redefine the denominator, impute endpoints, or become a causal adjustment. Any observation-model positivity or support failure is fail-closed and makes the corresponding standardized diagnostic inconclusive. Second, death, discharge/transfer, and unclassifiable truncation before the endpoint horizon are explicitly retained as competing exits rather than being dropped, censored, or called non-improvement. No computed result is claimed and no inherited finding is treated as a result of this experiment.

## Clinical question, evidence boundary, and unresolved claim

The narrow question is whether, among the already locked exact-label person-landmarks, `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` versus `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` contains reproducible information about a later persistent, safety-preserving, independently charted respiratory-state trajectory beyond strictly pre-D patient state and documentation opportunity, and whether that incremental chart association transports to hospitals not used for fitting.

The available lineage evidence supports only that the literal care-plan values and respiratory-chart mappings are sparse, heterogeneous, and documentation-opportunity dependent in parts of this snapshot, and that validated bedside SBT/extubation status, delivered ventilator settings, reintubation, and blinded clinical adjudication are unavailable. It does not establish clinician intent, actual trial performance, readiness, liberation, benefit, harm, or quality. The unresolved claim tested here is therefore a chart-proxy claim: conditional on the frozen labelled denominator, pre-D state, and pre-D opportunity, the label has a positive, reproducible association with the prespecified later trajectory and a held-out-hospital predictive increment. A positive result is not a treatment effect or a clinical decision rule.

## Endpoint windows, repeated observations, and exit precedence

Use half-open clinical windows exactly: `P=(D-1440,D-720]`, `B=(D-720,D]`, and `O=(D+360,D+1800]`. A component or safety window is observed only with at least two valid observations at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes; use the median of all valid observations, record counts, unique times, first/last time, maximum gap, and rejection/missing reasons, and never interpolate or carry forward. Deterministically collapse exact duplicates; conflicting values at one clinical offset are retained as `time_tie_conflict` and cannot silently create a transition.

A support reduction is the inherited raw-scale decrease comparing B with O: FiO2 at least `0.05`, PEEP at least `2`, pressure support at least `2`, or set rate at least `2`, with at least one non-peak component. Safety passes only when both `sao2` and respiration satisfy the inherited limits: post SpO2 no more than 2 percentage points below the pre-D median and post respiration no more than 4 breaths/min above the pre-D median. Missing either safety signal in either required window is endpoint-missing. Airway corroboration requires at least one O-window `respiratoryCare` row with nonempty `airwaytype` or a nonmissing ventilator interval field; it is corroboration, never device truth.

Classify every frozen row in this fixed order before arm contrasts or dropping rows.

1. If `patient.unitdischargeoffset` is nonmissing and `<= D+1800`, assign one competing exit. Trim and case-fold `unitdischargestatus`; exact `Expired` is `death`. Otherwise a classifiable status/location is `discharge_transfer`; an unclassifiable status/location is `truncation`. Also flag exit `<=D+360` as no post-washout opportunity. An exit before the endpoint horizon is never improvement or ordinary observed non-event.
2. Among rows without a competing exit, assign `endpoint_missing` when required repeated support, safety, or airway corroboration is absent. Record one mutually exclusive first reason and all flags: no pre-D support, no post-D support, no pre/post safety, no airway corroboration, parser rejection, time-tie conflict, source absence, and source error.
3. Otherwise assign `persistent_improvement` if non-peak support reduction, safety pass, and airway corroboration all hold.
4. Otherwise assign `observed_non_improvement` when all primary requirements are complete but improvement is false, including safety failure.

Report the four-state distribution (persistent improvement, observed non-improvement, endpoint missing, competing exit, with death/discharge-transfer/truncation subtypes) by arm, hospital, and landmark before complete-window contrasts. Do not drop rows because endpoint missingness or exits differ between arms. Report the unweighted all-row state vector, complete-observed RD, competing-exit counts/proportions, and fixed airway/support-only/setting-only sensitivities. The primary endpoint remains the single O window; any early/late persistence check is diagnostic only.

Fit transformations, missing indicators, coefficients, and any calibration only in training hospitals. ## Falsification and decision gates

Without changing the frozen parser, cohort, thresholds, labels, D, or windows, run the P-versus-B pre-D placebo, D-720 lead-time placebo, stricter 60-minute observation separation, leave-one-component-out and source-family ablations, documentation-density negative controls, fixed airway-recency sensitivities (24, 12, 6 hours before L), and the valid within-hospital-by-landmark label permutation. Report every attrition and reason. Airway-transition sensitivity uses only a predeclared exact `No Artificial Airway` respiratoryCare rule or exact treatment candidate followed by 48 hours without invasive-airway respiratoryCare records; it is unavailable/inconclusive if fewer than 10% of rows in each arm are observed.

Call the result computationally **supportive** only if: lineage and parser gates pass; at least five hospitals each have at least 10 complete primary endpoint rows per arm and those hospitals contain at least 50% of complete rows, with no one hospital contributing more than 40% of either arm; the primary complete-observed RD is positive with at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions positive; label-added held-out prediction improves both micro and equal-hospital-macro log loss by at least 0.01 over the identical state-plus-opportunity model with a stay-cluster 95% interval excluding zero; the increment survives both placebos, component/source ablations, and the valid permutation null; pre-D ascertainment diagnostics show adequate overlap and do not explain the value-based gain; competing exits do not explain the contrast; and airway corroboration is present in at least 10% of each arm. This supports only reproducible incremental information in this structured chart construct.

Call it **adverse** when support and overlap are adequate but the prespecified chart-proxy claim fails: no held-out increment or RD, negative/permutation-compatible or placebo-sized increment, hospital direction reversal, dependence on one source/component/site, documentation-density gain without value-based trajectory gain, or disappearance after the pre-D ascertainment diagnostic. This does not prove absence of bedside evaluation or that documentation is purely administrative.

Call it **inconclusive** if parent identity/source/parser verification fails; fewer than five supported hospitals or no held-out arm contrast; endpoint, airway, or competing-exit support is too sparse; observation positivity/support fails or weights are unstable; ascertainment cannot be separated from state; fixed variants disagree; or the stay-cluster interval spans both zero and the meaningful increment. No relaxed thresholds, post-hoc label expansion, endpoint-derived adjustment, post-D observation weighting, complete-case-only assertion, pooled row bootstrap, or redefined denominator is allowed.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, this successor adds an explicit, pre-D-only, training-hospital-fitted ascertainment diagnostic and fixed fail-closed positivity/support rules, plus an explicit competing-exit state contract and exit decomposition. Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, it does not promote a post-D observation-weighted estimand to primary and does not use post-D opportunity history to adjust the outcome; this preserves the Lead-assigned narrow descriptive estimand and avoids introducing an additional untestable selection assumption into the primary claim. It retains the useful observation-process and competing-exit accounting as diagnostics and sensitivity analyses. The frozen cohort, exact labels, landmarks, D, endpoint raw thresholds/windows, parser, and all interpretation limits remain unchanged. Remaining uncertainty is whether the frozen 540 rows provide enough complete, independently charted endpoint support and pre-D overlap for the diagnostics; failure is correctly reported as inconclusive, not repaired by changing the scientific schema. Clinical meaning remains unvalidated in these structured eICU files.
