# Route-blinded documentation-to-actionability bridge: executable successor to [prior hypothesis]

## Decision question and scientific boundary

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's scientific schema: the frozen first-eligible, all-opportunity adult pulmonary-nodule discharge population; one admission per subject; reciprocal pre-departure radiology report/addendum chronology; the exact canonical discharge-summary (DS) artifact; recommendation units; unit-specific opportunity clocks; exhaustive departure/availability/lag/eventual states; the atom `g=(possible-calendar class, terminal curr_service, workflow class)`; the text-defined routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER` with inherited precedence; support-atlas construction; positive-probability stratified admission sampling; all-unit review and adverse worst-unit routing; finite-population design-weighted inference; hash-rank challenge selection; the inherited admission, packet-minute, and physician-hour ceilings; split/sealed panels; held-out probability audit; compatible-set inference; no-rescue rules; and a prospective-silent-only conclusion ceiling.

No population, temporal boundary, recommendation-unit definition, route predicate, allocation, outcome, causal estimand, or workload ceiling is changed. This version repairs the remaining gap by making a route-blinded bridge executable before route labels are revealed. It separately estimates (a) reproducibility of a stored target/recommendation across independent source artifacts and (b) explicit text-actionability readiness. It also reports an evidence-gap vector for workflow and outcome facts that are not present in the configured MIMIC sources.

The falsifiable claim is deliberately narrow: among frozen opportunities for which both source artifacts are observable, can independently staffed readers reproduce the same recommendation-unit identity and does the canonical DS explicitly contain enough target/modality/timing text to be *text-actionability-ready*? This does not test whether the recommendation is clinically appropriate, delivered, scheduled, completed, understood, followed, safe, beneficial, or causal.

## Source availability audit and evidence ceiling

Before labels, the compiler creates a versioned source-availability manifest from the exact catalog and headers. For each of eight workflow propositions it records one of `OBSERVABLE_IN_PACKET`, `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`, or `NOT_ASCERTAINABLE_FROM_AVAILABLE_FIELDS`; the second value is used only after the predeclared catalog/header audit establishes that no configured source contains the proposition. The eight propositions are: (1) recommendation text exists in the DS, (2) an order/referral was placed, (3) a responsible recipient/team received it, (4) an appointment was scheduled, (5) the patient was notified or understood it, (6) follow-up occurred, (7) the intended imaging/procedure was completed, and (8) clinical safety/benefit or disease outcome occurred. Only (1) is directly observable in the bridge. The others are not clinical labels; they are evidence-gap fields. A missing note, missing detail row, death, outside care, or right censoring is never converted into `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` and never interpreted as non-delivery or non-completion. It receives a separate packet-state code (`MISSING_ARTIFACT`, `DEATH_BEFORE_WINDOW`, `RIGHT_CENSORED`, `NO_MIMIC_OBSERVATION`, or `UNKNOWN`).

The strongest claim supported by the available data is therefore a reproducible relation between text artifacts and structured episode clocks. The unresolved claim tested here is only reproducible explicit documentation and text-actionability readiness. Clinical truth, delivery, receipt, scheduling, completion, safety, benefit, and causality require prospective workflow capture, expert clinical adjudication, patient-level linkage, or another study.

## Route-blinded bridge panels

All panel rosters are disjoint from one another, from the route-labeling panels, and from any reviewer who selected an AUTO verifier. Panel packets contain an immutable packet ID, admission/opportunity ID, source-view seal, and only the fields allowed below. No panel sees a route, atom, support atlas, previous panel labels, or any derived outcome.

* `H1` receives the masked pre-departure radiology artifact(s) and records a recommendation-unit inventory. Each unit has a temporary local ID and fields `target_class`, `finding_identity`, `modality`, `interval_class`, `due-class`, `conditionality`, `negation`, `source_note_id`, and `source_span`; it may use only radiology text/detail and the inherited chronology.
* `H2` receives the masked canonical DS artifact and independently records the same inventory fields plus explicit actionability fields. It cannot see radiology text, H1 labels, or route labels.
* `H3` receives a route-blinded packet containing only the canonical DS text/detail and the already frozen target-unit key supplied by the compiler, with source spans hidden from H1/H2. It records explicit text actionability and the evidence-gap statuses. H3 does not vote on route and does not participate in the reproducibility functional.

### Deterministic matching and reproducibility functional

Matching is frozen before any labels. Normalize Unicode and whitespace; lowercase only for matching; preserve negation and numeric interval tokens; map equivalent modality and interval expressions through a versioned dictionary; and serialize each unit as the lexicographically ordered tuple
`(target_class, finding_identity, modality, interval_class, due_class, conditionality, negation)`.

Within each artifact, duplicate serialized tuples are retained with ordinal suffixes after sorting by `(source_note_storetime, source_note_charttime, note_seq, note_id, source_span_start, source_span_end, serialized_tuple)`. H1 and H2 inventories are matched by exact serialized tuple and one-to-one ordinal order; no fuzzy, semantic, pooled, consensus, or post-label rematching is allowed. An artifact with an absent or unresolved field is a distinct `UNRESOLVED` unit and cannot match a resolved unit. `D_REPRO=1` only if the H1 and H2 sorted inventories have identical cardinality and every position has identical serialized tuple and explicit interval/conditionality values. `D_REPRO=0` otherwise; if either required artifact is unavailable or a unit is `UNRESOLVED`, the admission is `D_REPRO=INCONCLUSIVE`, not a negative reproducibility result. H3 is explicitly excluded from `D_REPRO`; it is analyzed separately and in the joint compatible-set analysis. This prevents actionability judgments from being circularly folded into documentation agreement.

## Explicit text-actionability readiness

H3 applies a preregistered field table independently to each matched or target-keyed unit. A field is `PRESENT`, `ABSENT`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`; missingness is not imputed. For a unit with a recommendation, `target` and `modality` are required. `interval/due class` is required for `RECON` and `TIMING` text units and is `NOT_APPLICABLE` only for a genuinely non-follow-up `NOACTION` unit. `conditionality/contingency` is required when the text is conditional. `responsible service/recipient`, `order placement`, `appointment`, `patient notification`, completion, safety, and benefit are not retrospectively scored as absent because they are not in the available packet; they are coded `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` in the evidence-gap vector. For an explicit `DEFER` text unit, target and reason-for-deferral are eligible; modality and interval are `NOT_APPLICABLE` unless expressly stated. For `NOACTION` or zero-unit packets, no recommendation fields are eligible: the result is `ACTIONABILITY_NOT_APPLICABLE`, while any claimed non-actionable target linkage remains an adverse mismatch. An `AMBIGUOUS` eligible field is adverse for the strict readiness endpoint and retained as a separate uncertainty state for compatible-set analysis.

Define `A_TEXT=1` only when every eligible field in the target unit is `PRESENT`, no eligible field is `AMBIGUOUS`, and no target/modality/interval contradiction is present. `A_TEXT=0` for an observed, eligible unit failing that rule. `A_TEXT=INCONCLUSIVE` for unavailable/zero-unit/structurally non-eligible packets. This is explicitness of stored text, not clinical actionability in the ordinary clinical sense.

## Endpoints, controls, and inference

The primary bridge outputs are admission-level, all-unit-adverse indicators: (i) `D_REPRO` for the complete H1/H2 inventory, (ii) strict `A_TEXT` for every eligible target unit, and (iii) their joint compatible state `(D_REPRO,A_TEXT)` with unresolved/missing states retained. The inherited route-purity, missed-unit, capture, workload, and probability-audit endpoints remain unchanged. A single unresolved or adverse recommendation unit controls the admission under the inherited worst-unit rule.

The compiler calculates design-weighted finite-population estimates using the inherited positive inclusion probabilities and replication/inversion method. Report point estimates, simultaneous confidence bounds, support counts, and exact denominators separately for each route/atom cell and for the joint compatible state. No cell is pooled or rescued. If a denominator, inclusion probability, panel seal, or control support is invalid, the corresponding result is `INCONCLUSIVE`.

Controls are pre-label and route-blinded: (a) within-admission artifact swaps that pair a target with a different canonical DS, (b) deterministic route-scrambled target keys, and (c) non-pulmonary recommendation units constructed by the same matcher. Their purpose is to detect lexical or join artifacts, not to establish truth. A positive-control fixture with exact copied target/modality/interval text must yield `D_REPRO=1,A_TEXT=1`; a modality or interval mutation must yield `D_REPRO=0` or `A_TEXT=0`; an unavailable artifact must yield `INCONCLUSIVE`. These synthetic fixtures are compiler validation, not patient evidence.

Before labels, freeze numerical gates: minimum support is 30 admissions per nominated atom-route cell where available; otherwise the cell cannot pass. A retrospective nomination additionally requires the lower simultaneous bound for strict `D_REPRO` and `A_TEXT` to meet the inherited prespecified threshold, the route-scrambled control to remain below its prespecified specificity ceiling, zero roster/seal violations, and no ledger overflow. If any gate is not met, the result is adverse or inconclusive as appropriate and the cell remains `DEFER`. The thresholds and exact confidence procedure must be written to the manifest before opening packets; they cannot be tuned to observed labels.

## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive results mean only that independent readers can reproduce a text-defined target and that the DS often states eligible target/modality/timing fields, under the sampled MIMIC documentation process. They justify at most a prospective silent validation of documentation, with new workflow fields collected prospectively. They do not justify an alert, order, referral, patient-facing message, or claim of safe or beneficial routing.

Adverse results include low reproducibility, missing or contradictory interval/target text, high route-scrambled specificity, unresolved shared-error states, or any leakage/seal/ledger failure. They falsify the corresponding documentation nomination and force `DEFER`; they do not prove that clinicians failed to communicate or that a recommendation was clinically wrong.

Inconclusive results include absent or censored artifacts, sparse positive-probability cells, non-identifiable compatible sets, unavailable workflow evidence, or failed computational fixtures. They preserve uncertainty and require more data or a prospective design. In particular, no retrospective endpoint can establish delivery, receipt, scheduling, follow-up completion, patient understanding, safety, benefit, appropriateness, or causality. Those claims require clinical adjudication and/or prospective silent workflow instrumentation with outcome ascertainment.
