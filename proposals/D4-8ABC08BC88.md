## Question and evidence boundary

The falsifiable question is:

> Among UK Biobank adults with certified baseline creatinine and cystatin-C measurements, baseline creatinine-based eGFR at least 60, and a complete qualifying exported instance-1 observation, does baseline cystatin-C/creatinine eGFR discordance improve baseline-only prioritization of the observed repeat creatinine-equation kidney-status state beyond age, sex, and baseline creatinine eGFR?

The action represented is prioritization for a repeat kidney-function assessment. The estimand is conditional on the selected complete repeat-attender cohort and marginal over its observed 2-to-8-year schedule. It is not an estimate of whether ordering a test improves outcomes.

The strongest claim supported by the local files is only structural: the `ukb672073` rectangular export contains the required columns in the listed tables and is joinable by `eid`, subject to execution of the source/schema audit. The local metadata explicitly leaves renal analyte identity, units, missing-value meanings, assay/platform provenance, cross-instance comparability, and exact date semantics unresolved. Nothing here establishes CKD, chronicity, progression, measured GFR, kidney failure, causal utility, acceptable testing harms, benefit, cost-effectiveness, or transportability. A result is a biomarker-derived repeat threshold state, not a clinical diagnosis.

## Versioned, executable CKD-EPI definitions

The protocol constant set is frozen as **`CKD-EPI-2021-race-free-v1`**, corresponding to the 2021 race-free CKD-EPI equations. The certificate's equation evidence must identify and hash-freeze the authoritative equation source and must match every constant below. The implementation uses double precision, no intermediate rounding, age in completed years or the exact certified age convention, and output in mL/min/1.73 m². No race term is permitted.

Let `F=1.012` for certified female sex code 0 and `F=1` for certified male sex code 1. For creatinine, `k=0.7` and `alpha=-0.241` for female, and `k=0.9` and `alpha=-0.302` for male. After a certificate-authorized conversion to Scr in mg/dL:

```
eGFRcr(Scr, age, sex) =
  142 * min(Scr/k, 1)^alpha * max(Scr/k, 1)^(-1.200)
      * 0.9938^age * F
```

For cystatin C in the certificate-required mg/L unit, with `Fcys=0.932` for female and `Fcys=1` for male:

```
eGFRcys(Scys, age, sex) =
  133 * min(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-0.499) * max(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-1.328)
      * 0.996^age * Fcys
```

For the combined equation, use `alpha_combined=-0.219` and `k=0.7` for female, `alpha_combined=-0.144` and `k=0.9` for male, and `Fcomb=0.963` for female and `1` for male:

```
eGFRcr_cys(Scr, Scys, age, sex) =
  135 * min(Scr/k, 1)^alpha_combined * max(Scr/k, 1)^(-0.544)
      * min(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-0.323) * max(Scys/0.8, 1)^(-0.778)
      * 0.9961^age * Fcomb
```

The apparent difference between `0.996` in the cystatin-only equation and `0.9961` in the combined equation is intentional and must not be harmonized. The equation certificate must explicitly bind age 0 to `population[21022-0.0]`, age 1 to `assessment[21003-1.0]`, sex to `population[31-0.0]`, and the creatinine/cystatin inputs to the four `biological_samples` columns. It must state input units and the exact conversion. If and only if the certificate says field 30700 is µmol/L, use `Scr_mg_dL = raw_30700 / 88.4`; if it is already mg/dL, use it unchanged; any other unit requires a certificate-specified conversion. No cystatin conversion is applied unless explicitly certified. Apply conversion once, retain raw values privately for audit, and never use a hidden or value-dependent conversion.

Before biomarker calculation, apply only certified field-specific missing/invalid codes. Do not globally recode negative values. Values that remain nonfinite or nonpositive fail the assay gate. A second independent implementation must reproduce aggregate raw nonmissing, certified-missing, nonpositive/nonfinite, and positive-finite counts by field and instance. The equation contract does not establish measured GFR or clinical assay validity.

* `ASSAY_EQUATION_PASS` verifies certified units/conversions, equation version and constants, exact field map, certified missing handling, and finite positive inputs. Failure emits no eGFR, biomarker summary, mask, cohort, event, exposure, prediction, or model metric.
* `TEMPORAL_CERT_PASS` verifies certified date representation, deterministic parsing, strict ordering, and interval rule. Failure emits no cohort or outcome quantities.
* `COHORT_RECONSTRUCTION_PASS` verifies one-to-one joins, sequential masks, finite values, and anchors as diagnostics. Any mismatch stops reconstruction; anchors never modify rows.
* `ANALYSIS_PASS` requires fold isolation, converged finite fits, all declared falsifiers and gates, complete uncertainty computation, and conclusion-to-output branch consistency.

A neutral technical stop is neither adverse nor inconclusive biological evidence. Synthetic toy data may test parser, digest, equations, folds, and state transitions, but must be separately labeled and never merged with UKB results.

## Baseline-only model and primary contrasts

The deployment feature set is frozen before any repeat outcome is read. Use paired five-fold cross-fitting, stratified within the four baseline `E x Y` cells, over ten fixed repeats. Use the same fold assignments for both models and all outcomes. Seeds are `[520241,520242,520243,520244,520245,520246,520247,520248,520249,520250]`; the compiler records assignments privately and never publishes identifiers.

Construct the baseline comparator `M0` with sex, baseline age, and baseline `eGFRcr_0`. To make the former natural-cubic specification executable, use a restricted cubic spline for age with training-fold quantiles `(0.05,0.35,0.65,0.95)` and one for eGFRcr with `(0.05,0.275,0.50,0.725,0.95)`, Harrell truncated-power basis with the first and last knots as boundary knots, and the conventional linear-tail restriction. Center and scale continuous columns using training-fold means/SDs only; zero variance, coincident knots, nonfinite design values, or nonconvergence is a fit failure. Include sex and the resulting fixed spline columns; no other covariate is allowed.

`M1` is exactly `M0` plus one training-fold-standardized linear `Z` column. No repeat value, interval, attendance, missingness after baseline, additional field, outcome-guided transformation, or alternate threshold is permitted. Use unweighted L2 logistic regression with a fixed penalty and no tuning or class weighting; set the non-intercept penalty to `lambda=1.0` on the training-standardized design, with an unpenalized intercept. This fixed penalty and basis are part of the protocol, not selected from results. Fit separately for `Ycr`; repeat the frozen pipeline for `Ycys` and `Ycomb` as secondary analyses.

For each of the ten repeats, obtain paired out-of-fold probabilities. At every prespecified capacity `q=0.01,0.02,...,0.20`, select `K_q=ceil(q*N)` eligible participants by descending out-of-fold risk, with ties broken by ascending SHA-256 of `UTF-8("direction-A-capacity-v3|" + eid)`. Keep the tie key and eids private. Report event capture, fraction of all observed events captured, PPV, and paired incremental capture

`DeltaC(q) = C_M1(q) - C_M0(q)`.

Use 4,000 paired participant-bootstrap draws (seed `520251`), recomputing the complete grid and calibration contrasts, with a fixed maximum-attempt rule and a declared failure if fewer than 4,000 valid draws complete. Report simultaneous 98% max-|T| bands across the capacity grid and the prespecified primary contiguous region `q in [0.05,0.15]`. The inherited `q=0.10` is reported for continuity, never selected as the sole success point. Also report calibration-in-the-large, calibration slope, Brier score, and risk-bin calibration, with intervals conditional on the cross-fitted predictions. Any subgroup directional statement requires at least 1,000 participants and 20 events.

For transparency only, report the assumption-indexed net-benefit curve at `p_t={0.001,0.0025,0.005,0.0075,0.010,0.015,0.020,0.030,0.040,0.050,0.075,0.100,0.150,0.200}` using `NB=TP/n - FP/n * p_t/(1-p_t)`, plus test-all and test-none. These are not clinical utility estimates: the exchange rate is external and no testing actions, costs, harms, preferences, or benefits are observed. No threshold recommendation may be made.

## Falsification and integrity tests

1. **Independent assay/equation reproduction.** A separately implemented parser/converter/equation path must reproduce aggregate field-specific counts and eGFR finite-value status. Any disagreement blocks biological and predictive interpretation.
2. **Leakage audit.** Static and runtime audits must prove that `M0` and `M1` contain only baseline fields and `Z`; removing or permuting `t`, dates, repeat fields, attendance, and completeness must not change feature matrices, folds, tuning (none), or ranking inputs.
3. **Fold-aware outcome label null.** Perform 200 within-baseline-stratum permutations of `Ycr` using seed `520252`. Reconstruct the same stratified folds and refit both models for every permutation, recomputing the full capacity grid. Compare the observed statistic with its statistic-specific empirical null using randomized-plus-one tail fractions; do not compare only with zero. The primary support gate fails if the observed curve is not beyond the prespecified 98% favorable-tail null envelope.
4. **Predictor null.** Permute `Z` within baseline eGFR deciles and sex using seed `520253`, refit with identical folds and preprocessing, and require gains to collapse toward the empirical null. Persistent gains signal leakage or implementation error.
5. **Reversed-label negative control.** Repeat the frozen procedure for `1-Ycr`; a discordance-specific claim cannot rely on an equally large gain for the artificial reversed target.
6. **Timing and outcome sensitivity.** Report the three interval bands and each prespecified secondary equation outcome without changing the primary estimand. A finding confined to one interval or one unadjudicated equation is not a general kidney-status claim.
7. **Selection sensitivity.** Compare complete-case and predeclared weighted descriptive risks. Large dependence on selection is uncertainty, not evidence of causal missingness correction.
8. **Repeat stability.** Report each outer-repeat contrast; no claim is supported by a single repeat, and a prespecified minimum of 8 of 10 repeat-specific primary gains in the same direction is required for support.

## Concrete interpretation gates

**Supportive predictive prioritization.** Support requires all certificate/equation/source states to pass; exact cohort reconstruction to pass; finite converged fits; M0 to beat the intercept-only prevalence comparator; at least 8/10 repeat-specific `DeltaC` curves to favor M1; the simultaneous 98% lower band for `DeltaC(q)` to be positive throughout the contiguous `[0.05,0.15]` region; no material calibration deterioration; completed bootstrap and all null/leakage/predictor audits; and no conclusion branch inconsistent with output artifacts. A concordant direction for at least one prespecified secondary status strengthens but is not required. The permitted conclusion is only reproducible baseline conditional ranking information for the protocol-defined repeat status among `S_A`.

**Adverse predictive result.** After authorization and successful execution, classify as adverse if M1 is reproducibly worse in capacity capture or calibration while M0 passes, or if repeat-specific contrasts consistently favor M0 and the primary interval is wholly negative. This is evidence against incremental prioritization under this frozen comparator and selected cohort, not evidence that cystatin C has no value in other clinical uses.

**Inconclusive biological result.** Classify as inconclusive if the primary bands cross zero in the capacity region, signs are unstable across repeats or prespecified strata, the capacity gain occurs only at isolated points, sparse cells or unstable fits prevent the declared analysis, bootstrap completion fails, or null/sensitivity diagnostics conflict without identifying a technical fault. No threshold or testing recommendation follows.

**Neutral technical stop.** Missing/contradictory certificate evidence, digest mismatch, source/schema mismatch, uncertified units or missing codes, assay/platform incomparability, unresolved date semantics, equation mismatch, or state-machine failure is a technical stop. It is neither adverse evidence nor an inconclusive biological finding.

Even a supportive result cannot establish that ordering a repeat test improves patient outcomes, causal clinical utility, acceptable false-positive burden, cost-effectiveness, CKD/chronicity/progression, measured-GFR accuracy, assay interchangeability in practice, or transportability. Those require clinical adjudication, repeated clinical records or albuminuria, measured GFR or an appropriate reference standard, observed testing decisions, external utility data, external validation, and preferably a prospective decision-impact or randomized study.

## Verification and privacy limits

The verifier can check exact paths, source/schema hashes, table IDs, headers, one-to-one joins, certificate canonical and raw-byte digest, evidence coverage and contradiction flags, state ordering, certified missing-code handling, equation constants and field maps, sequential masks, no-future-variable feature construction, fold isolation, fixed spline/penalty specification, capacity grid, null completion, bootstrap arithmetic, and conclusion-to-output branch consistency. It cannot establish that a frozen evidence source is truthful beyond its provenance, that an assay is clinically valid or comparable, that eGFR equals measured GFR, that a threshold is CKD or chronicity, why participants attended, outcomes for nonattenders, causal utility, clinical benefit/harm, or transportability.

All participant-level projections, fold assignments, bootstrap/permutation arrays, weights, audit extracts, tie keys, and eids remain private workspace/job artifacts. Publish only aggregate counts, hashes, certificate provenance, curves, uncertainty bands, and audit status. No clinical rows, notes, eids, or private source content may be sent to public search or included in a candidate proposal.
