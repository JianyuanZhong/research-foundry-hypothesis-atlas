## Unresolved question and advance

The parent asks whether waist and grip provide sex-specific information for cardiovascular/renal versus infection outcomes at the same BMI and age. The unresolved clinical decision is narrower and more consequential: after a biochemical panel is already available, do the inexpensive measurements still add calibrated risk information, or are they only proxies for biochemical risk? This determines whether collecting waist/grip could rationally complement a panel for risk review; it does not estimate the effect of ordering a test.

The falsifiable primary hypothesis is that, conditional on baseline age, sex, BMI, calendar year and a valid current biochemical panel, adding waist and grip has a nonzero held-out proper-score increment for at least one endpoint, and that the waist-versus-grip increment differs by sex or endpoint. The null is zero incremental value after biochemical adjustment. The direction is not assumed. The substantive advance is a nested measurement-value comparison, not a predictor leaderboard or reproduction of the expert demonstrations.

Available evidence supports only parseable local fields, read-only files, horizontal `eid` linkage and code/date arrays. It does not establish analyte identity, specimen timing, disease onset, complete observation, or that a score increment would improve care.

## Interpretation and falsification

Support requires passed semantic, timing, code/date and ascertainment gates, at least 100 endpoint events and adequate sex-specific overlap/ESS, a held-out Delta_A,k interval beyond the practical margin, and stability across folds, weighting, missingness and endpoint definitions. Adverse evidence is a null or reversed increment, instability, or a gain explained by selection/calibration leakage. Failed gates, sparse cells, absent timing evidence or ambiguous clinical codes are inconclusive and trigger a fail-closed certificate.

A supportive result motivates clinician-reviewed endpoint adjudication and prospective validation; it does not establish that waist/grip causes risk, that ordering either test benefits patients, an optimal threshold, disease onset, mechanism, or transportability. A negative result discourages adding these measures for this recorded-risk task but does not disprove biological association. Clinical truth requires adjudication, complete follow-up and another study.
