# Proposal: competing liver-directed actions after an early post-TACE trajectory

## Successor objective and actual scientific deliverable

This is a substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the HCC island, one index episode per patient, the day-45 landmark, the paired-AFP estimand, and the observation-process safeguards. It repairs an important endpoint ambiguity: absence of a repeat TACE is not necessarily routine surveillance or treatment success, because another liver-directed procedure may be the next recorded action. The study will model the first subsequent recorded care pathway rather than treating every non-repeat-TACE patient as biologically equivalent.

The future solver must newly construct the cohort and fit a locked-test comparison of:

1. **B0**, a transparent competing-action discrete-time model using pre-index history and measured capture features;
2. **B1**, B0 plus the prespecified early AFP/hepatic trajectory;
3. **B2**, B1 plus only post-index observation-process proxies known by day 45;
4. **M3**, an irregular-time joint latent trajectory/observation model with competing cause-specific action hazards; and
5. a separately labeled all-index missingness-transport sensitivity.

The required new outputs are one patient-level cohort and split manifest, cause-specific predictions and cumulative-incidence estimates for each action category, trajectory and observation parameters, calibration/log-score and uncertainty comparisons, the non-liver capture-control analysis, and the pre-index negative-control analysis. A machine-checkable interpretation file must link each conclusion to computed outputs and a prespecified rule. This discovery proposal does not claim to solve the clinical question.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports only these narrower facts: the HCC source contains structured HCC diagnoses, TACE-named procedures, dated laboratory measurements, and dated examination records; source-level inspection found 338,040 procedure rows, 336,570 exact `肝细胞癌` diagnosis rows, 28,159,928 laboratory rows, and 419,996 examination rows. The five relevant assay names are observed. The local data do not establish residual viable tumour, radiologic response, Barcelona stage, treatment intent, survival, or complete outside-care capture. The demonstrations motivate longitudinal disease-history and observation-aware modeling, but they do not validate this HCC endpoint.

The primary hypothesis, frozen before fitting, is:

> Among adults with a structured HCC diagnosis and a first recorded TACE-like procedure who have parseable paired AFP measurements, an early transformed-AFP improvement accompanied by non-deteriorating hepatic-marker proxies is associated with a lower cause-specific hazard and 365-day cumulative incidence of a subsequently recorded repeat TACE-like procedure. The association will persist after adjustment for measured observation intensity, will be more specific to liver-directed actions than to a non-liver procedure, and will not be explained entirely by a pre-index analogue of the trajectory.

This is an adjusted prognostic association with a local recorded action. It is not evidence of recurrence, treatment response, causal benefit, or a clinical decision rule.

## Population, time zero, and outcomes

Use one index episode per patient. In `procedures`, identify the earliest row whose `手术` matches case-insensitive `TACE`, contains `动脉化疗栓塞`, or contains both `肝动脉` and `栓塞`. Retain the raw name and source. Collapse qualifying rows for the same patient within 24 hours into one episode, while retaining row/name counts. Use `开始时间` as index time; use joined encounter time only as a declared fallback when procedure start is absent, and report fallback use.

Require:

- age >=18 and nonmissing `性别` at the index encounter;
- `诊断名称` containing the literal `肝细胞癌` on an encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after index;
- exact joins on (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`);
- index time no later than 2025-01-01;
- adequate local ascertainment through day 365; patients without sufficient local observation are excluded from the primary binary/cumulative-incidence analysis and counted explicitly, never coded as no event.

The landmark is day 45. No record after day 45 may enter a prediction feature. The primary event is the first subsequent TACE-like episode in days 46–365. The competing liver-directed category is the first procedure in that interval whose `手术` contains `切除`, `消融`, `射频`, or `微波`, but does not satisfy the TACE-like rule. The capture-control category is the first procedure not in either liver-directed category. These are recorded action categories, not biological outcomes. A cause-specific discrete-time hazard and cumulative incidence will be reported for each category; the competing categories prevent “no repeat TACE” from being interpreted as a favorable state.

Also report a secondary broad liver-directed-action outcome combining TACE-like and alternate liver-directed procedures, and the parent’s literal-name sensitivity restricted to `TACE` or `动脉化疗栓塞`. Keep all outcome-name rules frozen before fitting.

## Features and estimands

The primary paired-biomarker cohort requires at least one parseable numeric `甲胎蛋白` in days -30 to -1 and one in days 7 to 45. Use the last pre-window and first early-post values. Define, before fitting, `z_pre=log1p(AFP_pre)`, `z_post=log1p(AFP_post)`, and the binary transformed improvement as `z_post <= 0.5*z_pre`; retain continuous `z_post-z_pre`, raw-scale values only descriptively, inequality flags, and assay missingness. The threshold is on the transformed proxy, not a claimed raw-percent clinical response.

For each hepatic assay retain last pre and first early-post values when available. Define non-deterioration before fitting as early >= pre for albumin and platelets, and early <= pre for total bilirubin and the coagulation-ratio proxy. The joint binary phenotype is transformed AFP improvement plus non-deterioration in at least two of those four assays. These are within-assay directions only; no clinical reference ranges or units are imposed. Keep assay-specific continuous changes and missingness indicators.

B0 is a regularized cause-specific discrete-time hazard/multinomial model containing age, sex, index department, index procedure family, pre-index HCC/cirrhosis/portal-vein/liver-metastasis/lung-metastasis/bone-metastasis/ascites diagnosis indicators and counts, prior liver-directed procedure counts, pre-window assay values and missingness, and measured follow-up capture through day 45. B1 adds the frozen phenotype and continuous trajectory changes. B2 adds only day-45-known process variables: distinct encounter dates, lab rows and dates, assay-specific days-to-first measurement and availability, relevant-examination counts/days-to-first/examination-family indicators, and optional explicitly labeled completed-record proxies from orders. Do not call these access, adherence, appropriateness, or true surveillance.

M3 is the substantive alternative. It uses two low-rank latent proxy states over days -30 to 45 (tumour-activity proxy informed by log1p AFP; hepatic-function proxy informed jointly by the four hepatic assays), irregular-time random intercepts/slopes or a low-rank state-space representation, and an observation submodel for whether each assay/examination is recorded conditional on prior measurements, baseline covariates, and latent state. It links posterior day-45 states, slopes, state uncertainty, capture intensity, baseline history, and procedure family to competing day-46 onward hazards. Future observations may not smooth the day-45 state. M3 tests whether asynchronous trajectory shape plus measurement opportunity adds information lost by snapshots; it cannot recover true tumour burden, reserve, response, or access.

The all-index estimand is separate. Among all otherwise eligible index patients, fit a training-only model for being paired-biomarker complete, inspect overlap, and use stabilized inverse-probability weights truncated at the 1st/99th percentiles to transport the complete-case association. If overlap fails, label it inconclusive.

The negative-control exposure uses a pre-index analogue, defined only for a sensitivity analysis: last numeric AFP in days -120 to -91 and first numeric AFP in days -90 to -46, with the same transformed-scale improvement and hepatic non-deterioration rules. It is not called a response to TACE. An association of this analogue with subsequent actions is evidence that generic pre-treatment severity or selection explains the primary association; it does not invalidate the usefulness of a prognostic signal, but it falsifies a specific post-TACE-response interpretation.

## Splits, evaluation, uncertainty, and falsification

Use patient-level calendar-year splits based on index procedure start when complete: 2010–2022 fitting, 2023 tuning, and 2024–2025-01-01 locked test. Otherwise use fixed participant-hash fitting/tuning/test buckets and record the fallback. No patient may cross splits. All parser rules, imputation, penalties, latent settings, and weights are learned without locked-test outcomes.

For B0, B1, B2, and M3 report, separately for repeat TACE, alternate liver-directed action, non-liver capture control, and the broad liver-directed outcome: adjusted cause-specific hazard ratios or multinomial contrasts, 365-day cumulative incidence, AUROC/PR-AUC where meaningful, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope, and mean log score. Use patient bootstrap or another declared robust interval method. The key incremental comparison is B2 versus M3 on the same paired cohort and competing-action targets; a 0.02 mean-log-score difference is a model-comparison margin, not clinical utility.

Supportive evidence for the primary recorded-action claim requires: repeat-TACE phenotype contrast <=0.80 with a 95% interval entirely below 1.0; direction preserved under literal naming and 90-/180-day windows; less than 30% attenuation after B2 and missingness weighting; stronger specificity for liver-directed than non-liver action; no adverse competing-action reversal that changes the pathway interpretation; and M3 improving locked-test mean log score over B2 by at least 0.02 with an interval excluding zero without materially worse calibration. The negative-control exposure should be materially weaker or absent if a post-index-specific interpretation is to be retained. These thresholds are operational evidence rules, not treatment thresholds.

Adverse evidence includes a null/reversed repeat-TACE association, >=30% attenuation after B2 or weighting, strong phenotype-by-testing interaction, comparable association for the non-liver capture control, a pre-index analogue as strong as the post-index phenotype, instability under name/window/assay rules, poor calibration, or an M3 gain that disappears after leakage and observation controls. If alternate liver-directed action is common and has an opposite association, the single repeat-TACE conclusion is adverse/incomplete and the competing pathway must be reported.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few paired patients or events in the locked test, failed weight overlap, wide intervals, high unknown follow-up, missing/date-incomplete examination data, failed M3 convergence, or lack of clinician review. Inconclusive means unresolved, not confirmation.

## Clinical advance and evidence limits

A supportive result would show that an early, explicitly defined biomarker trajectory is associated with a subsequent liver-directed care pathway beyond measured recording intensity, and would clarify whether the signal predicts repeat TACE specifically or simply predicts any later action. The negative-control exposure and non-liver procedure make the post-TACE interpretation more falsifiable. This could motivate prospective, imaging-adjudicated validation of early reassessment strategies.

The available records cannot establish viable tumour, radiologic response, treatment intent, BCLC stage, death, outside-care procedures, true decompensation, causal benefit, or clinical utility. They also cannot establish that a non-liver procedure is biologically unrelated or that a recorded “no event” means surveillance. Those claims require time-aligned expert imaging/pathology review, mortality and claims linkage, treatment intent, external validation, and prospective follow-up. The verifier can check cohort construction, outcome categorization, leakage, model fitting, uncertainty linkage, and whether interpretation follows from outputs; it cannot establish those clinical facts.

## Reference dispositions, alternatives, and compute

The inspected natural-history demonstration and its local readable text support a bounded adaptation of structured disease-sequence learning, but its source population and outcome differ, the paired HCC cohort is much smaller, and no external HCC cohort is available. A transformer is deferred, not banned; revisit it with a larger validated outcome set or external test cohort. A simple sequence model would be scientifically useful only as a later event-order sensitivity, not as a substitute for the current observation-aware competing-action question.

The inspected Bayesian demonstration and local readable article support the scientific alternative of latent longitudinal states with an explicit observation likelihood. This proposal is an EHR-only adaptation and does not reproduce its genetic analysis. The cancer demonstration’s main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not claimed to have been read; its accessible supplement supports considering missingness-invariant/lab-only comparisons, represented here by B2, M3’s observation process, and the capture controls. HCC images are unavailable, so no multimodal reproduction is proposed.

B0/B1/B2 and M3 use the same cohort, landmark, action definitions, splits, and uncertainty scale. B0/B1/B2 are interpretable and audit incremental phenotype/process information. M3 can reveal asynchronous latent trajectory and measurement-process structure that last-value features lose. CPU is selected because the transparent models and low-rank state-space alternative are tabular/iterative rather than a large neural sequence model; no GPU is scientifically required. The hardware inventory exposes eight A100 GPUs, but ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not evidence of unavailability and no GPU probe is needed for this proposed low-rank study. Future resource estimates are unverified: plan within the configured 16-CPU, 262144-MiB, 28800-second solver envelope, with checkpoints and a declared fallback if M3 does not converge. The episode’s streaming source audit was a measured discovery diagnostic; it was not a fit or evidence for the hypothesis.

The imported expert seed cards are for UKB, MIMIC, and eICU, not HCC; they were retrieved and assessed as ordinary candidates but cannot supply HCC rows or variables. They are not used as parents or as evidence for this design.
