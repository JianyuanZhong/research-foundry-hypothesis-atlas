Status: proposed, not fitted. Parent: `[prior hypothesis]`. This is a substantive execution child, not a change in the scientific population or endpoint: it preserves the incident C18–C20 prediagnostic reserve-versus-CRC/non-CRC competing-risk question while making the readiness gate, paired outcomes, selection falsification and baseline/learned comparison deterministic.

## 1. Scientific opening, supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

The inspected literature supports three bounded points. Frailty is associated with mortality among colorectal-cancer survivors [K1]; low preoperative handgrip/frailty is associated with postoperative complications and loss of independence [K2]; and CRC-specific and non-CRC mortality have distinguishable competing-risk patterns, with tumor stage/residual disease and patient vulnerability pointing in different directions [K3]. None of these abstracts tests whether an objectively measured reserve profile *before diagnosis* is associated with a durable non-cancer mortality pattern rather than merely recording occult cancer decline, age/comorbidity, or selection.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among UKB participants assessed in 2006–2010 who are free of valid paired C00–C97 cancer records at assessment and later develop incident C18–C20 cancer, lower assessment-time measured reserve, with diagnosis at least 730 days later, is associated with a larger five-year cumulative-incidence increase in non-cancer death than in CRC-specific death after prespecified age, sex, BMI, smoking, calendar-time, comorbidity and subsite adjustment.

This is a prognostic/descriptive association hypothesis. It is not a claim that low reserve causes death, identifies treatment intolerance, reflects a validated frailty construct, or explains a biological mechanism. A positive result would be a clinically relevant boundary test: it would justify external validation and prospective prediagnostic reserve measurement, not a treatment rule.

The leading rival is occult-cancer timing: near-diagnosis loss of reserve may be an early manifestation of cancer, cachexia or intensified observation. Other rivals are age/comorbidity confounding, volunteer/inclusion selection, exposure missingness, diagnosis/cause coding, and CRC stage/treatment. [K3] makes the last rival consequential, but the bound UKB data cannot adjudicate it.

## 3. Population, paired outcomes and estimand

Set `b_i = 53-0.0` and retain assessment dates from 2006-01-01 through 2010-12-31. A cancer record is valid only if the same slot has a parseable `40005-k.0` date and a trimmed uppercase ICD-10 `40006-k.0` code. Report malformed, unpaired and discordant slots; never repair them by borrowing a neighboring slot. Exclude participants with any valid paired C00–C97 record on or before `b_i`.

For incident CRC, select the earliest valid post-`b_i` C18, C19 or C20 date as `t0_i`. Retain all tied earliest slots and use the lowest slot, then lexicographically smallest normalized code, only for deterministic subsite labeling. A source audit using this rule found 6,094 assessment-window participants with a valid paired cancer on/before assessment, 2,219 post-assessment CRC cases with no such prior cancer, and 329/493/1,397 of those cases in the 0–<2, 2–<5 and >=5-year diagnosis-lag strata. These are screening counts before reserve completeness and documented follow-up gating, not fitted estimates.

Primary eligibility is diagnosis lag `t0_i-b_i >= 730` days, valid reserve exposure under the prespecified missingness rule, and documented five-year follow-up support. The primary timing window is 2–<5 years plus >=5 years; the 0–<2-year stratum is an adverse reverse-causation check, and >=5 years is a prespecified durable-timing sensitivity, not a replacement for the primary.

Death must use the paired slot `40000-j.0` date and `40001-j.0` underlying cause. Parse valid dates, retain only deaths after `t0_i`, choose the earliest date, and on a date tie prefer a usable ICD-10 cause then the lowest slot while recording all tied slots. Unknown means missing, malformed or non-ICD-10 cause. Classify first death as: CRC-specific C18/C19/C20; other cancer C00–C97 excluding C18–C20; non-cancer outside C00–C97; or unknown. Unknown is a separate competing event in the primary analysis. Sensitivities classify unknown as non-cancer and censor at unknown death. V01–Y89 external-cause death is a separately reported endpoint-specific negative control, not automatically folded into target non-cancer death. Death on/before `t0_i` is an impossible ordering: exclude it from primary and report it.

The primary estimand is the adjusted five-year cumulative-incidence differential:
`Delta5 = [CIF_nonCRC(R=Q25) - CIF_nonCRC(R=Q75)] - [CIF_CRC(R=Q25) - CIF_CRC(R=Q75)]`,
with other-cancer and unknown death competing, Q25/Q75 fixed from training data, and standardization over the locked eligible population. Report the one-, three- and five-year CIFs, `Delta5`, and the difference between cause-specific reserve log-effects; use 95% participant-bootstrap intervals. A positive `Delta5` supports the stated differential association. These are standardized prognostic associations, not causal effects. Fine–Gray and cause-specific estimates are descriptive sensitivity parameterizations, not interchangeable causal estimands.

## 5. Baseline versus learned alternative

Both models use the identical participant split, outcome construction, covariates, reserve components, missingness indicators and diagnosis anchor. The comparison tests whether reserve is adequately represented as an approximately linear scalar or instead has stable thresholds/component combinations; it is not a leaderboard exercise.

- **B0/B1 interpretable baseline:** one-year discrete-time cause-specific complementary-log-log hazards for CRC, non-cancer, other-cancer and unknown death through five years. B0 contains age using prespecified restricted cubic-spline knots, sex, BMI, smoking, calendar year, Charlson count, subsite and interval. B1 adds one linear one-SD R term and its pre-specified missingness indicators. Convert hazards to CIFs by the competing-risk recursion. Freeze B0/B1 before inspecting test predictions.
- **B2 learned alternative:** CPU gradient-boosted multinomial discrete-time hazard model over the same person-period rows and four outcome states, with the same B1 inputs plus the four reserve components, interval and prespecified reserve-by-diagnosis-lag interactions. Use a small fixed grid (200/400 trees, depth 2/3, learning rate 0.03/0.05) chosen on validation integrated Brier score only. Report endpoint-specific permutation importance and held-out accumulated-local-effect curves; do not treat them as mechanisms.

Use a fixed participant-level 70/15/15 train/validation/locked-test split, seed 36, assigned by deterministic eid hashing and stratified only after participant assignment by event type and diagnosis year. No person-period rows may cross splits. Fit scaling, imputation, tuning and Q25/Q75 on training/validation only; read the locked test once.

Report cause-specific CIF calibration intercept/slope, one/three/five-year and integrated IPCW Brier scores, time-dependent AUC, one-SD reserve effects, `Delta5`, event counts, model coefficients, learned effect tables and paired participant-bootstrap uncertainty with at least 500 resamples. Treat an absolute locked-test integrated-Brier improvement of 0.01 with an interval above zero as a pre-specified practically meaningful predictive improvement, but do not require B2 improvement to support a descriptive association if B1 is stable. A predictive improvement without a stable reserve endpoint contrast is only a prediction result.

## 6. Falsification and interpretation

Run each check once under a frozen specification and retain all adverse results.

1. **Occult timing:** repeat 0–<2, 2–<5 and >=5-year lag strata. A signal confined to 0–<2, or absent at >=5, is adverse to durable prediagnostic reserve and supports occult disease/observation timing.
2. **Age/comorbidity:** compare the prespecified flexible-age/Charlson model with age-only, age-plus-comorbidity, and age-band analyses (40–59, 60–69, >=70 where supported). Large attenuation or a reserve-by-age reversal indicates confounding or effect heterogeneity, not a host-reserve mechanism.
3. **Missingness/selection:** compare complete-four, min-three, imputed-indicator, grip-only and laboratory-only R. Model availability of complete R from assessment age, sex, BMI and smoking among all assessment-window participants; apply stabilized inverse-probability-of-observation weights only as a sensitivity and report overlap/extreme weights. Compare included incident cases with assessment-window participants and report missingness by event/lag. This tests measured availability selection; it cannot remove UKB volunteer selection or MNAR bias.
4. **Coding:** report every unpaired/malformed cancer or death slot, tie, unknown cause and impossible ordering. Compare paired `40005/40006` diagnosis anchoring with the earliest concordant pre-`t0` C18–C20 `41270/41280` occurrence where date/code are both usable. Material instability is adverse to the coding interpretation.
5. **Competing-risk and cause:** compare CRC, non-cancer, other-cancer, unknown and external-cause patterns; compare diagnosis-anchored competing-risk CIFs with naive censoring of competing deaths. A matching external-cause signal or CRC-dominant pattern weakens the proposed differential reserve interpretation.
6. **Leakage/permutation:** shuffle R within sex once, permute outcomes once with fixed seed, and run fixed exposure/outcome permutation probes. Retained held-out signal indicates leakage or coding contamination and invalidates the corresponding result.
7. **Stage/treatment boundary:** do not proxy stage or treatment with death cause, diagnosis lag, comorbidity or reserve. If the result is CRC-specific, disappears after timing checks, or differs by subsite, label stage/treatment as unresolved and do not reinterpret it as biology.

**Supportive:** readiness passes; in the >=2-year primary cohort lower R yields positive `Delta5` with an interval excluding zero; direction is compatible in >=5 years; the contrast is not reproduced for CRC death; cause, coding, missingness-weighted and complete/imputed analyses are materially consistent; and no matching external-cause signal or permutation leakage appears. This supports a robust prognostic regularity only. B2 may add evidence of stable nonlinear reserve patterns, but its predictive gain alone is not support.

**Adverse:** `Delta5` is zero/reversed, CRC death is at least as strongly associated, the effect is timing-only, it is driven by missingness or one assay, it tracks age/comorbidity, is unstable under paired coding/unknown-cause rules, or matches external-cause/permutation signals. Report which rival this supports and abandon the durable-reserve interpretation.

**Inconclusive:** readiness fails; deaths/events are sparse; clinically important intervals are wide; causes or coding are unstable; test calibration fails; or weighted analyses lack overlap. Do not call this a null. Continuing requires documented linkage completeness, expert outcome review, stage/treatment linkage or an independent cohort.

Computationally checkable claims are source binding, paired parsing, cohort flow, lag counts, model outputs, calibration, uncertainty and interpretation-to-output consistency. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for histologic confirmation, cause-of-death validity, stage, treatment, recurrence, treatment tolerance, mechanism, causality and generalizability outside UKB.

## 7. Alternatives and resource decision

The CPU baseline is retained because it directly estimates the differential competing-risk estimand and makes age/comorbidity and cause contrasts inspectable. The CPU boosted alternative is retained because it can reveal clinically meaningful reserve thresholds or component combinations that a scalar R erases while remaining feasible on the same inputs and split. A transformer/sequence model is deferred: no validated dated repeated reserve/laboratory trajectory is bound, and it would add representation complexity without separating a distinct rival explanation. A mechanistic renal-muscle model is deferred because validated mechanistic labels and treatment outcomes are absent. Revisit these alternatives only if repeated dated measures, stage/treatment evidence or an independent cohort is admitted, or if B2 shows stable nonlinear patterns requiring external mechanistic testing.

The measured discovery audit used a short CPU Parquet scan (about 11 seconds for selected columns on the available shell); full fitting, 500 bootstrap resamples and calibration are unmeasured. Plan 4–8 CPUs, 16–32 GiB RAM and under two hours as a provisional CPU estimate, subject to a readiness probe; the future solver envelope remains up to 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds. GPU is not needed for these tabular models. No fitted model or solver result is claimed here.
