# Episode 13 proposal — UKB17 posterior-thigh MFI and later falls report

## Question and clinical importance

Among UK Biobank `ukb672073` participants attending body MRI visit 2 with a valid visit-3 falls response, does higher bilateral posterior-thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) at visit 2 have a positive adjusted association with a later self-reported fall, beyond visceral adiposity, thigh-muscle distribution and measured baseline frailty/function? Require visit 3 after visit 2 by at least 366 days; the visit-3 item recalls the preceding year. Falls can cause injury, disability and loss of independence. A robust association would motivate independent prospective validation of muscle-quality phenotyping; a precise adverse/null association would lower its priority. This is not a true-event risk, causal effect, treatment benefit or clinical utility claim.

**Evidence already supported.** The inspected UKB thigh-MFI abstract reports broad mortality and incident-disease associations, not this lagged falls contrast [K1]. The closest overlap reports thigh-muscle distribution associations with falls/function [K2], so broad thigh-MRI/falls novelty is not justified. A myosteatosis review leaves aging versus disease/disuse explanations unresolved [K3]. The new question is whether visit-2 MFI contributes a time-ordered association beyond measured VAT, thigh distribution and baseline function; the cited works do not establish this contrast.

**Hypothesis and rival.** Among selected returnees with valid coded outcome and complete prespecified fields, 1-SD higher visit-2 bilateral posterior-thigh MFI is positively associated with any visit-3 falls report after adjustment. Strongest rival: MFI marks pre-existing frailty, inactivity/disuse, illness or prior falls; recall/reporting error or selection into MRI and follow-up may also create/distort the pattern. Adjustment and prespecified shape/heterogeneity checks probe measured proxies only, not unmeasured confounding, reverse causation or selection.

## Analysis and alternatives

Baseline: adjusted logistic model for any reported fall, linear 1-SD MFI, and fixed measured covariates: VAT, body fat, BMI, age, sex, smoking, activity, walking pace, grip, prior falls, and a nonredundant posterior/anterior muscle distribution ratio derived from volumes `22403`–`22406`. Do not enter redundant muscle components, total and ratio together. Report MFI OR/interval, counts, model diagnostics and participant-bootstrap uncertainty.

Substantive alternative: on the same rows/outcome fit low-degree penalized logistic GAM with prespecified smooth MFI plus hierarchical shrinkage MFI-by-prior-falls and MFI-by-grip interactions, retaining the same fixed covariates. This can reveal curvature or concentration among measured frailty-proxy strata that a linear common slope averages away; it cannot distinguish frailty, disuse and reporting explanations or identify a biological mechanism. Fix functional complexity before outcome inspection, tune only within training data, and report curves/interactions with uncertainty; defer if sparse support makes them unstable rather than alter cohort/outcome. Full cohort bootstrap target is 1,000 resamples with failures reported. A secondary fixed-seed participant-level 80/20 holdout evaluates both models on identical rows; train/tune only on 80%, report holdout Brier, log loss, calibration-in-the-large/slope and discrimination with uncertainty. This is internal diagnostic, not external validation; small predictive differences do not determine clinical value.

A transformer/sequence model adds no established information for one MRI exposure and coarse retrospective response; it cannot supply event dates, validate reports or resolve selection. The GAM directly tests question-relevant nonlinear shape and measured frailty heterogeneity. Future solver estimate: CPU 4, 16 GiB, <=7,200 sec for chunked raw read, model fits and bootstrap; unmeasured planning only. No GPU is justified.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive: positive adjusted MFI contrast with uncertainty excluding the prespecified null, adequate valid-code/covariate support, stable direction/shape across logistic and GAM, and no dependence on sparse strata or failed diagnostics. Supports only an association among selected returnees with self-reported falls, motivating prospective validation. Adverse: precise null/opposite association across both models with adequate support weakens this report-level hypothesis, not all true-fall associations or equivalence absent a justified margin. Inconclusive: wide uncertainty, sparse categories, unresolved -3/missing semantics, instability, calibration failure or material method disagreement; no confirmation/refutation.

New solver deliverable: fit both models and report flow/category counts, baseline estimates, GAM curves/interactions, uncertainty, diagnostics, paired holdout metrics and conclusions linked to computed outputs. Joins, filters, estimates and whether interpretation follows outputs are computationally checkable. Event truth, dates/counts/injury, response accuracy, nonresponse mechanism, unmeasured selection, causality/mechanism, transportability and clinical thresholds need prospective records/diaries, clinical adjudication, expert review and/or independent/interventional work; they are not automatically established by a verifier.
