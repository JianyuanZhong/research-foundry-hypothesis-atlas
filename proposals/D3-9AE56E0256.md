# Can the E-MTAB-8873 inhibitor contrast be interpreted?

## Scientific question and deliverable

**Question.** Do the two putatively matched, 48-hour E-MTAB-8873 Mpos untreated-versus-macrophage-UNC1062 sample pairs support even a block-level FLS expression association, and—if admitted—does the inhibitor-associated multigene shift resemble loss of the untreated Mpos-specific profile more than generic activation or an unrelated panel shift?

**Untested hypothesis.** Only if the source-to-sample audit passes, both R2 and R5 block contrasts will point in the same direction on a prespecified measured Mpos-associated FLS response profile; the inhibitor shift will be more aligned with the within-assay untreated Mpos-versus-Mneg profile than with the available inflammatory-control pattern. A result is descriptive for these two blocks, not a population effect. The panel cannot establish on-target MERTK action or a ligand/receptor mechanism.

**Deliverable.** (1) an auditable admission/deferral table reconciling each candidate condition to SDRF source row, sample tag, prepared matrix column, source archive member, contributor roles, and treatment protocol; (2) two block-specific paired effect vectors plus a transparent fixed-feature score; and (3) if the audit admits the comparison, multivariate directional comparisons of each inhibitor vector against same-assay Mpos-versus-Mneg and available LPS-control vectors. No p-value, donor-population estimate, or causal conclusion is a deliverable.

## Evidence boundary and competing explanation

Prior work supports macrophage–FLS cross-talk and remission-associated MERTK-positive macrophage/FLS repair observations [K1]; a distinct HBEGF–EGFR macrophage–FLS mechanism has functional support [K2], underscoring that a specific route needs more than correlated transcripts. The RA synovial atlas reports substantial cellular heterogeneity [K3], limiting generalization from a targeted ex-vivo panel. These works do not establish the UNC1062 contrast in this dataset. Inspections reused from the assessed parent are abstract-level, not full-text claims; source snapshots and receipt hashes are attached unchanged.

**Strongest evidence-supported claim:** E-MTAB-8873 records an ex-vivo 48-hour FLS/STM co-culture experiment with Mpos, Mneg and macrophage pretreatment conditions; its prepared assay contains 33 sample columns and 445 measured features. The submitted metadata labels R2 and R5 Mpos untreated/inhibitor rows as contributor-matched pairs. The protocol says inhibitor pretreatment used 250 nM UNC1062 and describes technical triplicates for the broader co-culture assay.

**Not yet supported:** that these two pairs are independent biological replicates, that treatment conditions are randomized or protocol-equivalent across R1/R2/R5, that the sample-to-tag-to-source files prove the same FLS/STM preparations, or that an expression shift is MERTK-specific. The source protocol also reports six biopsies digested but five active RA and five remission participants in five matched pairs; this is not a verified mapping to the experiment's contributors. SDRF sample tags recur across multiple sample names, so a tag alone cannot identify the sample globally. R2 and R5 contributor lists share SA266A across different roles (R2 STM contributor; R5 FLS contributor), so the two blocks cannot be treated as independent donor replicates merely because their sample names differ.

Leading rival: donor/culture/run structure, tag/sample mapping, treatment handling or generic activation explains apparent similarity or contrast. A true MERTK-dependent effect and nonspecific compound carryover remain observationally indistinguishable here.

**Simple baseline:** after admission only, retain original `feature` IDs and assay units (RSEC molecules-per-cell); apply `log2(value + 1)` as in the parent proposal and estimate each within-block inhibitor-minus-untreated contrast. **Substantive multivariate alternative:** use the same audited, transformed feature matrix and same primary samples to estimate vector direction and profile geometry, rather than compressing immediately to the fixed mean score. Define untreated Mpos-minus-Mneg response vectors only within source-verified comparable contexts and the R1 LPS-pretreated-versus-untreated vector as a generic activation reference; compare each primary inhibitor-minus-untreated vector with these reference vectors using cosine similarity and projection/residual magnitude, displaying the complete measured-feature contributions. With only two primary blocks and non-independent/reused contributors, this is a descriptive pattern diagnostic, not fitted prediction, inferential test, or evidence of a mechanism. It reveals whether an inhibitor shift is broadly aligned with loss of the measured Mpos profile or instead has a distinct/generic activation geometry that a one-score baseline loses. If comparability requirements fail, report that this alternative is not estimable rather than substituting unmatched controls.

Alternatives not selected: inferential mixed models, machine learning and pathway enrichment cannot manufacture independent units from two overlapping contributor blocks; unmeasured GAS6/PROS1, protein secretion, MERTK target engagement, viability, and a washout/rescue are unavailable. No GPU is justified. Estimated future execution: CPU, <=2 cores, <=4 GiB RAM, <=10 minutes for file binding and fixed low-dimensional computations; estimate unmeasured but data size is only 33 x 445. This discovery audit does not constrain a separately budgeted replication study.

## Decision and falsification

**Supportive, conditional:** admission succeeds and both block vectors show concordant attenuation on the prespecified response set; multivariate geometry is more consistent with the audited Mpos-reference loss than the LPS-reference vector and is not driven by a single feature. This supports only a descriptive, two-block inhibitor-associated FLS transcript pattern worthy of a new experiment. It does not show a general biological effect or MERTK-specific mechanism.

**Adverse:** the audit succeeds but block directions oppose, show no predeclared attenuation, or the observed profile is more aligned with the generic LPS-reference pattern than with loss of the Mpos reference. This weakens the proposed response pattern for these samples; it does not refute MERTK biology.

**Inconclusive/deferral:** any unresolved donor/tag/archive/technical-replicate mapping; treatment protocol mismatch; fewer than two usable pairs; evidence that the apparent pairs are non-comparable or a single shared technical unit; or non-comparable reference vectors. Wide/noisy estimates also remain inconclusive. In this state, no biological association claim is warranted—only a catalog and provenance finding.

**Next required experiment if signal merits continuation:** independently replicate across prospectively paired RA FLS/STM donors, randomize matched Mpos macrophage preparations to vehicle or macrophage-restricted MERTK perturbation, quantify target engagement/viability, wash inhibitor before co-culture, and include a direct FLS-only residual-drug exposure control, Mneg and LPS controls. Add a rescue (genetic or pharmacologic with justified specificity), measure macrophage ligands and supernatants (including GAS6/PROS1 and cytokines), FLS RNA plus protein/phosphosignaling and a functional matrix/repair endpoint. The present panel cannot adjudicate these. Stronger named ligand/receptor or clinical claims require that follow-up and expert review; treatment benefit requires a separate clinical study.

Computationally checkable: exact binding, source-row mapping rules, transformations, fixed-set calculations, geometry, and whether interpretations match the outputs. Expert/protocol adjudication is required for true cell identity, contributor role, treatment/washout equivalence and biological versus technical replication. Causal target specificity, functional repair and clinical impact are not automatically verifiable from this dataset.
