# Calendar-transportable assay information for the next recorded HCC care pathway

## Successor decision and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of incumbent `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the incumbent's strict-prior assay-content-versus-capture-intensity question and its prespecified calendar-shift test, while making the primary recorded target more decision-proximal: the competing type and timing of the next locally recorded pathway after an HCC-coded admission.

The future solver must newly produce:

1. a frozen adult first-locally-recorded-HCC admission cohort, patient-disjoint random and calendar partitions, and exact key/timestamp/multiplicity/unmatched-row audits;
2. an exhaustive admission-anchored competing pathway-time label with explicit discharge competition, unresolved timing, procedure-family lexical audit and tie sensitivity;
3. frozen A/L/Q/I/V feature manifests and fitted transparent models, where V adds strict-prior assay content and I contains assay identity/occurrence but no results;
4. matched learned S/S_I/S_Q irregular-time models predicting the same pathway-time estimand;
5. locked random-split and later-calendar predictions, proper scores, calibration and patient-clustered uncertainty;
6. department-, procedure-source- and era-stratified heterogeneity reports;
7. value/result, assay-identity, history-time, patient-label and leakage falsifications, plus the secondary order-to-procedure timing audit;

9. a claim-to-output table that separates computationally checkable findings from clinical claims requiring adjudication or another study.

Completion is established by those frozen labels/manifests, locked predictions, uncertainty/falsification reports and interpretation table. A positive result is not required.

## Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest available evidence supports only that routine measurements and EHR capture patterns can be associated with subsequent recorded care. The incumbent establishes a careful information question, not a clinical conclusion. The verified HCC snapshot does not establish active HCC, stage, tumor burden, liver reserve, treatment indication, intent, receipt, response, toxicity, survival, benefit or utility. The local research-ambition material supports using dated structured histories and longitudinal latent models as possible adaptations, but its reported external cohorts, genetic inputs, images and complete original methods are not available for an HCC reproduction. No such reproduction is claimed.

The unresolved clinical-information question is:

> Among adults at their first locally recorded HCC-coded inpatient admission, do strictly pre-admission, assay-specific laboratory results and trajectories improve calibrated prediction of which competing pathway is next recorded and how soon it is recorded, beyond admission context, current-admission laboratories, prior capture intensity and prior assay identity/occurrence; does that result-content increment remain present in the later calendar era and across admitting-department/documentation regimes?

The primary estimand is the held-out paired difference
`Delta_VI = score(V) - score(I)`, with lower log loss and Brier score better, for the complete pathway-time distribution. The directional hypothesis is `Delta_VI < 0` on the locked later-era evaluation, with no material calibration deterioration and attenuation under result permutation. The incumbent's V-minus-I estimate for its seven-state endpoint remains a secondary, directly comparable estimand. The new primary target is more decision-proximal only in a bounded operational sense: distinguishing a near-term discharge, diagnostic/access record, or named procedure-family record and its timing could inform what record stream should be reviewed or coordinated next. It does not identify a recommendation, intent, treatment receipt or benefit.

Support requires the later-era result-content increment to be lower on both prespecified primary proper scores with a 95% patient-clustered interval whose upper limit is below zero, stable calibration, and attenuation when prior lab payload is permuted within assay/fit strata. A consistent result across sufficiently supported department and procedure-source strata strengthens the transport claim. A random-split improvement alone is not support.

Adverse evidence is a later-era increment that is absent or reverses, calibration that materially worsens, a similar gain from I or Q, persistence after result permutation, or reproduction by the result-free process model S_Q. This would indicate that the apparent gain is historical capture/process signal or does not transport. Inconclusive evidence includes inadequate calendar/state support, more than half the labels unresolved, unstable or miscalibrated fits, wide patient-clustered intervals, inability to assign the first procedure family deterministically, or a failed timing audit. Inconclusive is not confirmation.

Only computation can establish cohort/partition correctness, temporal safety, feature nesting, label construction, score/calibration/interval calculations, heterogeneity and falsification attenuation. Expert chart review is required to establish that lexical procedure families are clinically recognizable, and additional data/studies are required for active disease, biologic mechanism, intent, treatment choice, treatment benefit, utility, safety, external transportability and patient outcomes.

## Population, temporal safety and partitions

Normalize Unicode and surrounding whitespace in keys and diagnosis text. Mark an encounter HCC-coded when normalized `诊断名称` equals `肝细胞癌` or contains the case-insensitive English phrase “hepatocellular carcinoma”. This is a record-code rule, not diagnosis adjudication.

For each patient, sort HCC-coded encounters by native `入院时间`, then `就诊号`. Retain only the earliest HCC-coded record if it is an adult admission (`年龄 >= 18` and `<120`), has parseable native `入院时间` and `出院时间`, nonnegative stay, and `入院时间` in [2011-01-01, 2026-01-01). If that earliest HCC-coded record has invalid admission timing, exclude the patient rather than substitute a later record. Keep early discharges and unresolved procedure timing. Set `t0 = 入院时间`.

Primary windows are half-open:

- strict prior: [t0 - 730 days, t0);
- current laboratories: [t0, t0 + 12 hours);
- pathway observation: [t0, t0 + 72 hours).

A strict-prior child row must have a valid native event timestamp in the prior window and join to an encounter whose native `入院时间 < t0`. For labs use `检验时间`; for procedures use `开始时间`; for orders use `开立时间` for capture and retain `开始时间` for the order audit; for medications use `开始时间`. Rows are not eligible merely because their encounter key is linked. Anchor-linked pre-t0 rows are a labelled sensitivity, not primary history. Report invalid event times, invalid linked encounter times, boundary/tie rows, after-discharge rows, left truncation, no-history and all multiplicity/unmatched counts. Include the incumbent's [t0-730 days, t0-30 days) negative-control history branch.

The random split is SHA-256(normalized `患者主索引`) modulo 100: 0-59 fit, 60-69 selection/preprocessing/calibration, 70-79 locked evaluation, 80-99 inaccessible. All admissions for a patient remain together; dictionaries, assay scaling, feature selection, calibration and thresholds are fit-only/selection-only.

The fixed calendar shift is development 2012-2018, selection 2019-2020, and locked later-era evaluation 2021-2025. The 2011 records are descriptive only. Report cross-era patient overlap and exclude any overlapping patient ID from the temporal estimand before fitting. Require at least 200 eligible admissions per partition, 20 examples of every nonempty pathway-time mark, and at most 50% unresolved labels; otherwise the calendar result is inconclusive and boundaries cannot be moved post hoc. Evaluate the new target by department and `手术来源` strata when each stratum has at least 200 admissions; do not use future `手术来源` as a predictor.

## Primary competing recorded-pathway and timing outcome

Construct an exhaustive, admission-anchored label from procedure starts and discharge timing. The target is documentary, not clinical treatment.

First determine whether any valid procedure start occurs in [t0, min(t0+72h, discharge)] using native `procedures.开始时间`. A valid start before discharge and before 72 hours is an event. Exact procedure/discharge ties use procedure-wins precedence; rerun discharge-wins as a sensitivity. Procedures before admission, after discharge, at exactly 72 hours, and multiple rows are retained in audits.

If no valid procedure precedes discharge within 72 hours:

- `D`: discharge occurs by 72 hours before any established valid procedure;
- `N72`: no valid procedure by 72 hours and discharge is after 72 hours.

If a missing/invalid procedure start could change whether or which event is first, assign `U` unresolved rather than treating it as no procedure. If multiple earliest rows have different family marks, assign an explicit `combined_or_ambiguous` mark, not an arbitrary family.

For the earliest resolved procedure time τ, assign one deterministic mark from the literal `手术` text after Unicode/whitespace normalization:

- `resection_transplant`: contains `移植` or `切除`;
- `locoregional`: contains `TACE`, `栓塞` or `灌注`, when no other treatment-family token is present;
- `ablation`: contains `消融`, `射频` or `微波`, when no other treatment-family token is present;
- `systemic_infusion`: contains `免疫治疗`, `靶向治疗` or `化疗`, when no other treatment-family token is present;
- `diagnostic_access`: if no treatment-family token is present and contains `穿刺`, `活检`, `置管`, `插管`, `引流` or `造影`;
- `other`: nonblank procedure text matching none of the preceding rules;
- `combined_or_ambiguous`: two or more treatment-family rules match one earliest string or simultaneous earliest rows conflict.

The family map is a frozen lexical identification rule, not a validated procedure ontology. The primary joint outcome cells are each resolved event mark crossed with [0,6), [6,12), [12,24), [24,48), or [48,72) hours after t0, plus N72 and U. D receives the bin containing discharge. Report event-time distributions and cumulative incidence for every mark at 24, 48 and 72 hours. The incumbent's exhaustive P0/D0/U0/R24 then P1/D1/U1/N72 seven-state construction is rerun unchanged as a sensitivity, so the successor's result remains comparable to the parent.

The primary target is more actionable than “any procedure” only as an operational recorded-pathway forecast. Its clinical recognizability must not be asserted without expert adjudication of a frozen sample.

## Nested information sets

All models use identical patients, labels, preprocessing policy, outcome cells and split.

- **A**: age, sex, admitting `就诊科室`, calendar era, admission time-of-day, admission missingness and strict-history availability.
- **L**: A plus assay-specific current-lab summaries in [t0,t0+12h): first/last valid numeric result, change, elapsed time, count, density/missingness, slope only with two distinct valid times, and assay-specific qualitative results. Quantitative values are never pooled across assays.
- **Q**: L plus strict-prior capture intensity only: row and distinct-encounter counts, active days/span, recency, source-specific counts for labs/procedures/orders/medications, missing-time counts, left-truncation and no-history indicators. Q contains no assay names, lab results/values, procedure/order/medication names, doses, routes, frequencies or statuses.
- **I**: Q plus prior lab assay identity/occurrence summaries: assay identity, assay-specific count, recency, span, missingness and distinct-assay count, with no qualitative result or quantitative value.
- **V**: I plus strict-prior assay-specific `定性结果` and `定量结果`, including dated within-assay change/slope only when at least two distinct valid times exist. Scaling is fit-only within assay; no unvalidated physiologic score is constructed.

The primary nested comparison is V versus I; Q is the capture-only comparator and I versus Q tests assay identity/occurrence. A versus L separates admission context from current-lab content. Procedure/order names and any outcome-window fields are forbidden from all predictors. An anchor-linked history branch is sensitivity only.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

The auditable baseline is regularized multinomial logistic regression over the joint pathway-time cells (and N72/U), with the penalty, solver, imputation/missingness indicators and maximum iterations fixed before locked evaluation. Fit A/L/Q/I/V on the same patient split, selecting preprocessing and penalty only in the selection partition. Report coefficient/feature manifests and a score audit. Direct multiclass softmax is preferred for transparency; if the solver uses a discrete hazard factorization, it must output the same joint probabilities and be evaluated identically.

The learned alternative is a fixed low-dimensional continuous-time marked event encoder S. It consumes the same strict-prior event rows and current-lab rows, with source/table type, elapsed time, assay identity where permitted, payload mask and assay-specific lab payload. A decayed irregular-time state update and a terminal mark/time head are fit to the same joint target with censoring/no-event and unresolved-quality handling declared in advance. Fix latent dimension (suggested 64), decay family, regularization, masking/reconstruction term, optimizer, seed count and early-stopping rule before fitting; tune only on selection data. S_I removes prior lab values/results but retains assay identity; S_Q removes assay identity and payload while retaining process/timing. Integrate its continuous-time output to the same five bins and N72/U cells.

S can reveal order-sensitive irregular trajectories, nonlinear interactions between current results and history, and structured missingness uncertainty lost by first/last/count summaries. It is not a scientific advance merely because it scores slightly better: require seed stability, proper-score improvement or parity with the same V-minus-I direction, and calibration. The primary scientific comparison is information content under a documentation/calendar shift, not model complexity.

## Analysis, uncertainty and falsification

For random and calendar-held-out evaluations, report paired V-minus-I, V-minus-Q and I-minus-Q differences in multiclass log loss and multiclass Brier score, with 95% patient-clustered paired bootstrap intervals using at least 1,000 resamples and all admissions for sampled patients. Also report integrated Brier scores for cumulative incidence at 24/48/72 hours, per-mark/time-cell results, observed versus predicted cumulative incidence, calibration intercept/slope or reliability summaries, and prediction intervals/seed variation for S. Preserve all predictions so every interpretation links to computed outputs.

Prespecified falsifications are:

1. permute prior quantitative/qualitative payload within assay and fit strata while preserving counts, assay identity and timestamps;
2. permute prior assay identities while preserving the capture process;
3. permute prior history timestamps within patient while preserving row counts and assay/result values;
4. replace strict-prior rows with anchor-linked pre-t0 rows;
5. use the t0-30-day negative-control history exclusion;
6. shuffle patient IDs before the split;
7. run a deliberately forbidden future-window predictor as a labelled leakage sentinel only, never as evidence;
8. rerun procedure-wins versus discharge-wins ties and report boundary/midnight sensitivity;
9. compare S_Q and Q to establish whether a result-free process model explains the V increment.

The result-content claim is supportive only if the later-era V-minus-I proper-score intervals meet the co-primary direction gate, calibration does not materially deteriorate, the increment is attenuated by payload permutation, and the learned and transparent alternatives do not disagree in scientific interpretation. If one metric is supportive and the other adverse, report mixed evidence, not a confirmation. If data support, unresolved labels, model stability or semantic audit gates fail, report inconclusive. If later-era performance reverses or the permutation fails to remove the increment, report adverse evidence for result-content identification under the stated design.

The order layer is secondary and cannot establish intent. For procedure admissions, inspect `orders.开立时间` and `orders.开始时间` against `procedures.开始时间` on exact keys. A concordance requires a nonblank order, valid order `开立时间 < procedure 开始时间`, one shared frozen family, and no reliance on order status as proof of completion. Require at least 100 uniquely family-assigned procedure admissions, at least 70% of candidate orders with resolvable `开立时间`, at least 80% of timed procedure records with nonblank/exactly-one family assignment, no family over 90% of concordant events, and at most 25% dependence on ties/unresolved times. If the gate fails, call this audit inconclusive and leave the primary endpoint unchanged.

## Availability limitations and deferred alternatives

The HCC catalog gives no image files, raw waveforms, usable vitals or transfer payload. Clinical documents and pathology are narrative and untimed. Lab numeric results have no separate unit field, and no validated assay reference ranges, stage, tumor burden, treatment exposure, response, toxicity, survival or external validation are available. These omissions block claims about physiology, intent, benefit and clinical truth.

The accessible research-ambition material was used only to justify a bounded dated-sequence/latent-trajectory alternative. The UK Biobank training trajectories and Danish validation associated with Delphi, the genetic analysis associated with ALADYNOULLI, and the full article/STAR Methods and imaging associated with Oncoformer are not HCC inputs and are not reproduced here. A mechanistic lab-score branch is deferred until units/reference ranges and expert assay mapping exist. A primary order-based target is deferred because order text/status and timing cannot validate intent; it remains an audit. A missing dependency or expert adjudication that resolves these limitations justifies revisiting those branches; otherwise the solver must report the documentary result only.
