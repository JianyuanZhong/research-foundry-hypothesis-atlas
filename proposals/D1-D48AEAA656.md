## Scientific question, evidence boundary, and advance

The strongest currently supported claim is structural feasibility, not the clinical hypothesis. The outcome-blind audit reconstructed 49,157 first eligible transfers among 40,014 subjects, instantiated a subject-isolated split, and showed 99.43%-99.57% effective endpoint-fixed perturbability in every pooled and late-era role against the frozen 70% gate. Median null diversity was 133-135 unique G values, median scaled permutation SD 0.596-0.624, median materially moved fraction 0.955, and G-pre coverage 74.99%-84.48%. This establishes that the cohort, split, and order-null are computable and nondegenerate. It does not establish association, precision, calibration, order attribution, physiology, preventability, or benefit from changing transfer or monitoring.

Inspected work establishes that longitudinal ICU-discharge models can outperform a snapshot score while operational factors, treatment limitation, and composite components complicate interpretation [K1]; prior ICU-readmission models are heterogeneous in population, outcome, method, and bias [K2]; and observation timing and frequency can carry outcome information because EHR trajectories reflect healthcare processes [K3]. None establishes the present hypothesis.

The unresolved hypothesis is: among eligible ICU-to-general-ward transfers at the same measured endpoint state at t0, unusually worsening rather than improving recent measured-value history changes calibrated 48-hour ICU-return/death risk and fixed-capacity review ranking, especially when endpoint state and direction disagree.

The leading explanation is residual ordered measured-value information. The strongest rivals are shared-measurement coupling/regression to the mean and recorded treatment, observation, documentation, clinician-concern, and transfer-selection processes. The substantive advance is one matched attribution experiment that distinguishes endpoint sufficiency, recorded-process prediction, endpoint coupling, scalar ordered-value information, and nonlinear ordered-value information. It remains prognostic and noncausal.
