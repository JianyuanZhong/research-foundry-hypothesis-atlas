## Unresolved question, hypothesis and clinical importance

The strongest claim supported by the inspected evidence is that baseline metabolic measurements associate with later coded cardiometabolic disease and that structured time-to-event models can represent progression. The available evidence does not establish that an assessment-linked within-person change between two UKB panels adds information beyond the current panel, or whether any added signal is coherent metabolic worsening versus disagreement among HbA1c, triglycerides and HDL. It also does not establish biological slopes, adjudicated onset, causal effects or benefit from repeat testing.

Primary hypothesis:

> Among UK Biobank participants aged 40–69 years with exactly one of diabetes, cardiovascular disease or chronic kidney disease at both valid assessment-linked panels, who have complete paired primary assays and remain in the same one-domain state at the second panel, the preceding metabolic change adds route-specific prognostic information for the first subsequent additional domain beyond second-panel current levels, existing-domain identity, disease duration and prespecified covariates. Conditional on added information, a coordinated-worsening component and marker-discordance components have distinguishable associations with the transition route.

This matters because a patient at a repeat-assessment encounter has a current metabolic panel, but may also have a prior panel. Resolving whether the prior-to-current trajectory adds clinically relevant risk information—and whether a composite burden or discordant marker pattern carries that information—could guide what should be validated in future repeat-testing risk tools. The proposed result remains an internal prognostic finding in an observed repeat-assessment stratum. It is not a recommendation to repeat testing, an intervention effect, or a policy threshold.

The substantive advance is a dimension-matched decomposition under the same competing-risk architecture. `C` represents coordinated worsening across the three markers; orthogonal `D1,D2` represent disagreement that a single common score loses. A positive `C) result supports coherent burden as the unresolved signal; a positive conditional `D) result supports marker-specific discordance. A null result would be clinically informative within the selected stratum: second-panel current levels may be sufficient for the tested prognostic purpose.

## Matched current-level baseline and substantive alternative

All models use the same cohort, `d1) time zero, competing-event architecture, explicit death hazard, censoring, participant split, preprocessing, uncertainty procedure and locked evaluation.

B0 is the simple current-level baseline. It fits route-specific cause-specific Cox hazards for each existing-domain to `SECOND_DOMAIN_UNIQUE) transition and an explicit death hazard. Predictors are *d1 current* HbA1c, triglycerides and HDL; existing domain; existing-domain duration; age, sex and gap; BMI `21001-0.0`, systolic BP `4080-0.0`, diastolic BP `4079-0.0`, smoking `20116-0.0` and center `54-0.0`. Continuous terms use fit-frozen splines. No d0-to-d1 change enters B0. A d0-current-level-only fit may be reported as an algebraic descriptive sensitivity, not the primary comparator.

Construct fit-partition standardized worsening changes (z_H,z_T,z_R) for HbA1c, triglycerides and reverse HDL. Define:

- (C=(z_H+z_T+z_R)/sqrt{3}), coordinated worsening;
- (D1=(z_H-z_T)/sqrt{2});
- (D2=(z_H+z_T-2z_R)/sqrt{6}).

If empirical fit-partition covariance is nonzero, orthogonalize using a transform frozen before locked evaluation. M_common adds only (C) to B0. M_full adds (C,D1,D2), with only prespecified domain interactions if route support is adequate.

The scientific tests are nested:

- M_common versus B0: does coherent worsening add information beyond d1 current levels?
- M_full versus M_common: does marker discordance add information beyond coherent worsening?
- M_full versus B0: does the full dated-change representation add information at all?

The contrast is substantive rather than a complexity contest. B0 answers “are current values enough?” M_common can reveal a coherent burden signal obscured by separate marker noise; M_full can reveal a route-relevant disagreement that a common score loses. A two-panel recurrent/neural sequence model is deferred because two observations do not provide sequence structure and extra capacity cannot resolve date-proxy timing or return selection. Reconsider it only with at least three verified dated assay panels and evidence of stable added information. A four-instance slope/mixed-effects model is deferred because assay instances 2/3 are not verified in biological_samples.
