# Episode 49 v3: recent recorded pressor-to-stability alignment

Parent: `[prior hypothesis]`

## Decision, defect, and advance

This child preserves the clinically consequential hour-48 question: among fluid-accumulated adults still invasively ventilated, is starting or restarting an ICU-recorded furosemide/bumetanide delivery in the next 12 hours associated with a clinically meaningful improvement in bounded recorded 48-hour airway-event-free extubation, without crossing renal or terminal-harm margins?

It repairs one population defect. The parent calls its cohort post-shock but accepts any valid pressor interval from ICU admission through `t0-6h`. At fixed `t0=intime+48h`, a brief early infusion can qualify a patient after more than a day without recorded pressor delivery. That mixes recent transition from resuscitation with remote/transient early exposure and changes the population to which the policy association applies.

A treatment-blind discovery audit showed material impact before outcomes: of 7,663 frozen broad hour-48 ventilated opportunities, 1,607 passed the parent pressor-history plus six-hour-stability rule; 843/1,607 (52.5%) had last valid recorded pressor delivery more than 24 hours before t0, including 381 more than 36 hours before t0. The repaired rule retained 764 broad opportunities. In the remote group, 136 had ≤1 and 415 had ≤6 summed qualifying-row hours; these secondary duration sums do not union linked/overlapping segments and are descriptive. Median last-delivery recency was 24.88 hours. No loop arm or outcome was read and no treatment effect was estimated.

Primary eligibility now requires valid pressor delivery in `(t0-24h,t0-6h]` while preserving six recorded pressor-free hours. Call this the *recent-pressor/stable recorded population*. “Post-shock” is shorthand, not adjudicated shock. The 24-hour cutoff is a phase-alignment convention, not a validated biological threshold; 12-hour, 36-hour, legacy remote-inclusive and confirmation-time full-pipeline sensitivities test dependence on it.

## Supported versus unresolved claims

The strongest supported claim is narrow: exact MIMIC streams can identify a record-defined recent pressor-to-stability transition, and the parent rule admits a large remote-history group. MIMIC also contains computable recorded loop, airway, multisource renal and disposition streams. This does not establish shock, congestion, perfusion adequacy, actual receipt, benefit or safety.

The unresolved falsifiable claim remains:

> In the prespecified recent-pressor/stable, multisource-no-active-RRT measured-overlap discovery population, assignment-compatible A12 follow-up increases the lower bound of five-day recorded 48-hour airway-event-free extubation by more than 0.05 versus B12, while one-sided upper limits remain below +0.03 for recorded creatinine rise and creatinine-or-death, +0.05 for multisource RRT and RRT-or-death, and +0.03 for recorded terminal/hospice/acute-transfer harm.
