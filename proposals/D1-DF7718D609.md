# Feasibility-resolved deferral of marker-history-specific HCC surveillance selection

Status: substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The N=15,575 day-60 cohort, outcome-independent 44-week grid, comparator hierarchy, and biological claim limits are unchanged. An aggregate-only audit newly constructed the inherited exposure H and attempted a minimal transparent full-grid fit. H fails the inherited positivity, era, service, and held-out event-support gates. No recurrence outcome was constructed or fitted, and no scientific null or target-specific association is claimed.

## Scientific opening and claim hierarchy

The strongest clinical claim supported by inspected evidence is that incomplete AFP response at a 4–6-week postoperative visit was associated with worse recurrence-free and overall survival among 549 selected resected HCC patients with preoperative AFP above 400 ng/mL [K1]. It does not establish residual tumor, ignorable follow-up availability, or surveillance benefit.

Laboratory-order presence and timing were strongly associated with survival in a large two-hospital EHR study [K2]. A methodological review distinguishes patient presence from longitudinal informative observation and cautions that explanatory claims require explicit treatment of the recording process [K3]. These works justify studying observation; they do not identify why a postoperative HCC patient was tested.

The unresolved hypothesis remains: among adults at a fixed day-60 landmark after first observed HCC resection, does pre-landmark marker history predict later HCC-targeted laboratory measurement beyond local contact, generic testing, liver-injury monitoring, oncology-panel bundling, service, and era? A target-specific association would expose selection in biomarker-followed cohorts. A precise shared association with A, N-L, L-I, and O-TM would favor retention, severity, or bundling. This audit does not resolve the hypothesis; it establishes that the inherited H cannot support the prespecified falsification experiment in this snapshot.

## Fixed design

The inherited population is frozen: adults with earliest qualifying liver resection in 2012–2023, same-encounter non-uncertain HCC diagnosis, and no recorded HCC-directed procedure or strict-dictionary systemic treatment during index+1 through day 60. Existing transplant, donor, biopsy/puncture, cyst/abscess, repeat/recurrent wording, duplicate-procedure, and early-treatment rules are retained. Prior treatment in days −180:−1 remains a covariate.

All 15,575 patients contribute 44 bins from day 61 through 365 (685,300 patient-weeks). A comes only from encounter intervals/dates and is modeled, never required for eligibility. Outcomes remain weekly L-M (AFP/PIVKA-II/AFP-L3), N-L (sodium/creatinine/hemoglobin), L-I (ALT/AST/bilirubin/albumin/INR/platelets), and exact O-TM (CEA/CA19-9). Full-grid L-M specificity against each comparator remains primary; A=1 composition is secondary. Hepatic versus chest CT/MR remains secondary and cannot rescue failed laboratory H.

## Executed audit and fatal gates

Numeric parsing is not the bottleneck. Valid preoperative/day-15:60 rows were 11,361/639 for AFP, 8,353/305 for PIVKA-II, and 3,843/96 for AFP-L3. Exact-label paired support was only 262 AFP, 143 PIVKA-II, and 46 AFP-L3 patients.

After requiring train-supported exact-label/era ranks and priority anchoring, H existed for 243/15,575 patients: 219 AFP and 24 PIVKA-II. Split support was 156 train, 24 validation, 26 locked test, and 37 reserve. Era support was 96 in 2012–16, 89 in 2017–19, 58 in 2020–21, and zero in 2022–23. AFP-L3 never anchored H.

The train-frozen outer-quartile contrast retained only 60 train patients (30/30), 11 validation (6/5), 11 test (6/5), and 20 reserve. This is far below the inherited requirement of 100 effective patients per contrast. The two main index services contained only 33 and 68 outer-quartile patients across all splits; a third contained one.

No leakage or duplicate problem was found. Fatal problems are sparse same-label day-15:60 pairing, absent 2022–23 rank support, non-estimable label-by-era and H-by-service terms, and held-out outcome separation. Validation low-H had zero L-M and O-TM events. Locked test had one event per H arm for each of L-M, N-L, L-I, and O-TM.

A deliberately minimal stacked binomial diagnostic used the identical full grid, outer-quartile patients, mark indicators, H×mark, four-week bands, era, and coarse service. It converged on 10,560 training rows with 21 coefficients and condition number 21.0. This is numerical—not scientific—feasibility: only 60 patients and 9–15 training event-weeks per mark/H arm informed it. Validation had 0–7 and test exactly one event per arm/mark. The fit omitted the full clinical interaction set and yielded no valid standardized specificity inference.

Crude training patterns were nearly identical across target and comparators: LM and O-TM each had 9 low-H/14 high-H event-weeks; N-L and L-I each had 10/15. This is compatible with bundling but is not adverse evidence against specificity because H support is inadequate.

## Decision and interpretation

The confirmatory baseline and U+Q+B versus U+Q+B+V alternative must not be fitted or interpreted on this H. Repeated weeks cannot repair patient-level positivity; regularization cannot create absent eras, services, or held-out events. Pooling raw assays, using day 0:14, merging labels, lowering gates, dropping held-out evaluation, or redefining H after outcomes would change the scientific design.

The hypothesis result is inconclusive. The current-data feasibility result is adverse: this snapshot cannot execute the prespecified H-specific LM-versus-NL/LI/O-TM experiment. A supportive scientific result is unavailable. An adverse scientific result would require precise specificity intervals below the materiality thresholds; those intervals cannot be estimated. This audit is not evidence that target-specific surveillance is absent.

Reopen only with enough same-label preoperative/day-15:60 pairs to provide at least 100 effective patients per exposure/comparator contrast, at least 20 patients and nonzero outcomes per primary mark in each analysis split, supported label/era ranks including 2022–23, and service strata without separation. Assay platform/unit metadata or prospective standardized 4–6-week measurement would strengthen reopening.

Marker-observed versus absent remains computable (14,845 versus 730), but it tests ascertainment state rather than trajectory. Promoting it to primary would change the estimand and requires a Lead-owned child.

## Preserved matched methods

If gates later pass, the transparent baseline remains mandatory: stacked full-grid recurrent-mark regression with mark intercepts, week spline, both continuous H ranks, H×mark, era/service interactions, lagged A/mark history, measured severity, cluster-robust inference, and bootstrap g-computation. It reveals which comparator falsifies specificity but compresses persistent recording states.

The substantive alternative remains U+Q+B versus U+Q+B+V on identical inputs and split. U captures contact, Q liver/severity monitoring, B oncology bundling, and V residual HCC-target enrichment constrained away from A/N-L/L-I/O-TM. It could reveal residual target covariance lost by regression, but cannot rescue absent H support or identify biology/intent.

Future estimates remain baseline 8–16 CPUs, ≤64 GiB, 1–2 hours; state model one allocated A100, 16 CPUs, ≤128 GiB, 4–6 hours. The audit/minimal fit took 115 seconds on ordinary CPU. No GPU probe is warranted while positivity fails.
