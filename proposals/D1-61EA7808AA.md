# HCC successor: pre-procedure versus post-procedure corroboration of the day-42 trajectory

Status: design only. No cohort count, endpoint prevalence, fitted coefficient, held-out loss,
effect estimate or clinical conclusion has been computed. This is a substantive successor
to [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the parent's first-index TACE
population, O0/O1/O2 hierarchy, strict nested baseline comparison, day-42 transport
estimand, [43,181) outcome, patient-disjoint temporal split, downstream documentation and
planned-care negative controls, and clinical limits. The new decision is a single
lead-time test: is O1 corroboration present before the recorded procedure, or does it
first appear after the procedure?

## Clinical opening and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest supported claims are bounded. [K1] shows that an image-rich, selected,
multicenter cohort can predict repeat-TACE-relevant prognosis, but does not validate this
source's procedure or laboratory records. [K2] bounds clinical response to dynamic,
necrosis-aware imaging criteria. [K3] shows that a recorded imaging endpoint can change
under undocumented outcomes, exposure classification and ascertainment. The parent
proposal therefore supports only a possible predictive/descriptive association between
the day-42 laboratory path and a locally recorded operational episode.

The unresolved ambiguity is narrower and consequential: O1 requires same-visit
examination/order corroboration in [t-42,t+7]. If the corroborator precedes the procedure,
it is at least temporally compatible with a pre-procedure episode being recorded. If it
first appears at or after the procedure, it may be a documentation echo generated by the
procedure itself. Neither proves intent, response, benefit or severity, but the distinction
changes whether the next investment should be prospective clinical adjudication or
workflow/scheduling data.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis:

> Among eligible first-index TACE patients, the strict day-42 path increment for a
> semantically corroborated later procedure whose corroboration is available before that
> procedure (O1-pre) is greater than the corresponding increment for a procedure with no
> qualifying pre-procedure corroboration and corroboration first appearing at/after the
> procedure (O1-post-only), on the same held-out patients, transport estimand and
> [43,181) horizon.

Formally, for q=path, \\(D_{pre}=L(O1pre;A_{term})-L(O1pre;A_{path})\\) and
\\(D_{post}=L(O1post;A_{term})-L(O1post;A_{path})\\); positive means lower loss after
adding ordered path information. The primary test is \\(Delta_{lead}=D_{pre}-D_{post}>0\\),
with a positive O1-pre increment required for the strong supportive interpretation.
This is not a causal contrast between patients and not evidence of a biologic mechanism.
O1-all remains the inherited operational endpoint and is reported as an anchor.

[K1] supplies clinical decision relevance, [K2] prevents relabeling a record as response,
and [K3] supplies the ascertainment rival. This proposal adds a time-order falsifier and
an explicit next-data decision, not a reproduction or extension of any cited study.

## Frozen O0/O1 dictionaries and the new pre/post endpoints

Retain the parent's frozen semantic procedure predicate P_sem:

- normalized `procedures.Surgery` is exactly `tace`, or contains `hepatic artery` and `embolization`
  plus at least one of `chemotherapy` or `perfusion`;
- exclude labels containing `radiofrequency`, `microwave`, `ablation`, `biopsy`, `puncture` or `drainage`.

O0 is the first distinct-start later procedure with normalized `Surgery == tace` and
43 <= start-t0 < 181. O1-all is the first later P_sem row with the same patient and
visit key, the inherited encounter link, and at least one corroborator in [t-42,t+7].
The encounter link is either `encounters.encounter time` within two elapsed days of procedure
start, or valid `Admission Time <= t <= Discharge Time`; missing discharge does not satisfy the
second route. Event time is always procedure start, never corroborator time.

Use the parent's frozen type/order dictionaries, applied only to type/order names:

- E is an `examinations.Examination` value matching the frozen liver/hepatic-artery/
  upper-abdomen imaging type terms; use only valid `examinations.start time`.
  `Examination Findings` and `Examination Diagnosis` are not used as response labels.
- Q is an `orders.non-drug orders` value matching exact TACE or hepatic-arterial
  embolization/chemotherapy/contrast terms; use valid `orders.Order Time` as order
  availability and valid `orders.start_time` as execution timing.

Define mutually exclusive corroboration classes for each P_sem procedure at t:

1. C_pre=1 if E has `Start Time` in [t-42,t-1], or Q has either `Order Time` or
   `Start Time` in [t-42,t-1], on the same patient/visit key. An order opened before
   t but started after t still counts as C_pre because the pre-procedure availability
   is the tested boundary; retain separate open/start indicators.
2. C_post=1 if C_pre=0 and E has `start time` in [t,t+7], or Q has
   `order time` or `start time` in [t,t+7], on the same patient/visit key.
3. If both pre and post evidence exists, classify as pre and retain post evidence in the
   audit. If neither exists, the P_sem row is an O1 near miss. A missing time or key is
   an unsupported reason, not evidence of absence.

O1-pre is the first eligible P_sem event with encounter link and C_pre. O1-post-only
is the first eligible P_sem event with encounter link, C_pre=0 and C_post=1. O1-all is
the union anchor. These are endpoint definitions, not predictors: all corroboration
after t0 and all endpoint dictionaries remain excluded from A0 through A_path. O2 is
unchanged and conditional: O1 plus a same-visit medication row with valid
`medications.start time` in [t-1,t+1], only after its support audit confirms nonmissing
timing and a documented source dictionary; medication is not assumed TACE-specific.

A qualifying event is the earliest distinct start for that endpoint after duplicate
collapse. A complete negative requires obs_end >= t0+181. obs_end in [t0+43,t0+181)
is right censoring. A literal O0-positive/O1-negative row remains in O0 and is not
silently dropped. Rows with invalid/missing procedure or corroborator time, missing
encounter link, or key mismatch are counted as unsupported. Materialize
`endpoint_event_audit.csv` with counts by split and endpoint for P_sem aliases,
C_pre/C_post source and timing, order-open versus order-start timing, both-class rows,
near misses, duplicate starts, early aliases, untimed rows, and censor reasons before
fitting.

This resolves one ambiguity only. It cannot recover treatment intent, scheduling beyond
order timestamps, imaging findings/response, medication indication, outside care or
death.

## Strict nested predictors and observation transport

Use the same predictor blocks and no endpoint dictionary in any block:

- M0: index context from encounters, including `Age`, `Sex` and index timing.
- B0: latest nonduplicate value for each of the eight exact lab labels
  `alpha-fetoprotein`, `albumin`, `prealbumin`, `total bilirubin`, `direct bilirubin`,
  `alanine aminotransferase`, `platelets`, `platelet count` in [t0-365,t0).
- P_obs: pre-index/through-day-28 assay opportunity, source coverage and valid-time
  indicators.
- P_care: non-endpoint dated encounter/order/examination/medication process summaries
  through day 28; exclude all O0/O1/O2 dictionaries and endpoint rows.
- A0=M0+B0+P_obs+P_care.
- A_snap=A0+S42, where S42 is through-day-42 lab state.
- A_term=A_snap plus terminal B0-to-S42 changes and transition categories.
- A_path=A_term plus ordered transitions/knots in (0,14], (14,28], (28,42].

Preserve quantitative and qualitative lab content, inequalities, censoring tokens,
sample type and availability. Do not invent values, pool assays without units, or use
the absence of a lab as a lab value. `labs.test time` is the only lab time.

Fit the parent's fold-frozen stabilized day-42 selection weight on all I. The numerator
uses M0, B0, pre-index P_obs/P_care; the denominator adds only non-content process
opportunity/valid-time marks through day 28. Exclude endpoint dictionaries, corroborator
content, post-day-28 assays and all future information. For R42=1,
SW42=Pr(R42|numerator)/Pr(R42|denominator), with 1st/99th development-fold clipping
only. Record tails, overlap and weighted ESS. >5% clipping, inadequate overlap or ESS
<100 is a transport-support failure. Report the ordinary complete-R42 landmark
estimand and the eligible-index transported estimand separately; do not average them.
Apply the same separately fitted follow-up observation/censoring weights to risk-set
contributions in [43,181).

Retain the parent's falsifiers unchanged: F_obs is the first non-procedure local
documentation opportunity in (t0+42,t0+63] from valid encounter/lab/examination/order/
medication times, excluding O0/O1/O2 rows; F_plan is a supported order opened by t0+42
and started in (t0+42,t0+63], excluding endpoint terms. These are outcomes only, never
predictors, transport content, censoring content or corroborators. If F_plan timing
support is absent, it is unavailable, not a null.

## Models, comparison, uncertainty and compute

The confirmatory model is an auditable elastic-net pooled-logistic discrete-time hazard
for O0, O1-all, O1-pre, O1-post-only, enabled O2, F_obs and supported F_plan under every
nested block. Person-period rows use identical split, horizon, SW42 and censoring
weights. Report held-out ordinary and transported log loss, Brier/integrated Brier,
calibration, discrimination, risk-set/event counts, coverage, ESS and weight tails.

The primary output is the predeclared paired test-prediction contrast
D_lead=D_pre-D_post for A_term to A_path, with D_pre and D_post also reported under
landmark and transport estimands. Compare O1-pre and O1-post-only within the same
patient-level test bootstrap; use one max-|t| simultaneous 95% family across D_pre,
D_post, D_lead, O1-all and the two supported negative controls. Use 2,000 patient
bootstrap resamples with the frozen test predictions; report the paired distribution,
not just separate intervals. The 0.005 log-loss difference remains an operational
resolution for audit, not a clinical MCID. O1-pre and O1-post-only each require the
parent support gate of at least 50 overall events, at least 15 development and 15 test
events, nondegenerate follow-up/observation support and weighted ESS >=100; otherwise
the pre/post decision is inconclusive.

The substantive learned alternative is a compact time-aware GRU over exactly the same
allowed event stream from t0-365 through t0+42: source table, allowed assay label,
preserved numeric/qualitative/inequality content, relative time and time since prior
record. No endpoint row, post-day-42 content, narrative findings, imaging claim or
medication indication is included. Use separate O0/O1-all/O1-pre/O1-post-only/O2/F_obs/
F_plan hazard heads and matched A_snap, A_term and A_path ablations, three fixed
development seeds, the same patient-disjoint test split, SW42 at scoring, bootstrap,
calibration and falsifier family.

The GRU can expose nonlinear ordering, irregular timing and recovery-then-worsening
patterns lost by elastic-net terminal/path summaries. It cannot distinguish true intent
from a post-procedure echo, recover outside care or create imaging response. A GRU-only
gain is representation-sensitive and cannot override the pre/post falsifier. Elastic-net
is selected as the confirmatory estimator because its nested increments, timing features,
weights and negative controls are auditable; GRU is a matched sensitivity, not a
complexity bonus.

Planning estimates, not measured execution: materialization, endpoint audits, weights,
elastic-net fits and 2,000-resample bootstrap are approximately 1-4 hours on 4 CPUs and
16 GiB. Three-seed compact GRU sensitivity is approximately 2-6 hours on 4 CPUs, 16 GiB
and one allocated A100. The primary does not require a GPU. If the GRU is run, request
gpus=1 and use cuda:0 inside the allocated job; ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not a
feasibility result. Discovery and future solver envelopes remain separate.

Not selected: larger transformer, narrative NLP, latent intent model and mechanistic
liver-response model. The source lacks validated scheduling/intent labels, imaging/report
data, assay units/reference ranges, medication indications, reliable death and outside
care. Revisit those methods only if those dependencies or an independent adjudication
set becomes available. This is a dependency decision, not a blanket exclusion of neural
or structured methods.

## Decision gates and claims

Supportive lead-time result requires: both endpoint support and transport overlap pass;
D_pre has a simultaneous lower bound above zero; D_lead has a simultaneous lower bound
above zero; D_post is not O1-all-sized and the paired negative controls do not match
O1-pre; results are directionally stable in R28, late-record deletion, corroboration
windows [-28,-1] and [-7,-1], and early-alias/horizon sensitivities. A GRU directionally
consistent with the elastic-net strengthens reproducibility but is not required to call
the operational result supportive. Interpretation: day-42 path information is more
compatible with an episode already represented before the later procedure than with
post-procedure documentation alone, under measured local observation. Continue to a
prospective study with scheduled pre-procedure labs, treatment intent/scheduling and
expert imaging adjudication.

Adverse lead-time result is D_post >= D_pre, or a positive D_post with an O1-all-sized
gain, especially if D_post survives transport while D_pre does not; or F_obs/F_plan
matches or exceeds the O1-pre gain. Interpretation: O1 corroboration is compatible with
post-procedure recording or generic workflow, so redirect the next study toward
scheduling/order provenance, outside-care capture and documentation mechanisms. This
does not show that the laboratory trajectory lacks prognostic value.

Inconclusive means either endpoint fails support/overlap/ESS, F_plan is unavailable,
censoring or weights are unstable, simultaneous intervals are wide, or O1-pre/post
classification is materially unsupported. Do not change windows, dictionaries, model
family or split to rescue it. Seek a bounded chart/imaging/intent adjudication sample
before selecting a clinical trigger. A null with wide uncertainty is not evidence
against a trajectory.
