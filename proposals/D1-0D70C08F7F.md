## Unresolved question and clinical importance

The available HCC snapshot supports a consequential but currently unresolved
prognostic question: after an adult's first observed HCC-coded inpatient
admission, does early hepatic/renal physiology identify the next locally recorded
care transition beyond admission and prior-record information, and does that
information remain useful when the transition is classified into a reproducible
procedure family? This could inform whether a future, prospectively adjudicated
monitoring or referral study should treat early laboratory trajectories as
decision-relevant, rather than relying on static admission information.

The strongest claim supported by the evidence is only data availability: encounters
have admission/discharge times; labs have assay names, numeric/qualitative results
and native collection times; procedures have names and native start/end times;
orders have names, open/start/end/status/frequency fields; and all bind to
encounters on (患者主索引, 就诊号). A bounded header/source audit confirms the
actual columns below. No local field proves pathology-confirmed HCC, stage,
indication, scheduling, completion, treatment intent, response, or outside-hospital
capture. The unresolved claim is therefore a recorded-data association and
incremental prediction claim, not a treatment-effect claim.

Primary hypothesis: among all eligible first observed admissions, measurements
during [0,24) hours after admission improve held-out, calibrated prediction of
the first procedure-table transition in [0,72), beyond admission/pre-admission
information, after retaining early discharge and unresolved procedure times as
competing states. Secondary hypothesis: when a timeable later row is assigned to
an auditable family, the early-physiology increment differs by family and by
whether a single matching pre-24 order was recorded. This secondary result is
inconclusive if the local dictionary cannot be frozen or is dominated by
unresolved/multi-family rows.

This advances existing knowledge by making the all-admission estimand robust to
documentation and semantic failure, while explicitly testing whether the
clinically tempting family interpretation adds information rather than silently
substituting a locally invented treatment label.

## Outcomes and estimands

For each admission, use procedure presence and native 开始时间 without reading
procedure text for the primary outcome. Within [0,72) and before discharge, assign
the first orderable row using mutually exclusive precedence:
P0 = first qualifying procedure in [0,24); P1 = first in [24,72) with no P0;
Uproc = no established P0/P1 but a procedure row with missing/invalid start
could alter the window; D0 = no prior state and discharge before 72 hours; N0 =
no prior state, discharge at/after 72 hours, and no procedure by 72 hours.
A valid event remains P0/P1 even if another unresolved row exists later, but the
audit reports it. Rows after discharge/72 hours are retained only in timing
audits. The primary target is the full {P0,Uproc,P1,D0,N0} distribution and the
standardized P1 probability over every locked-test admission.

Freeze, in fitting/selection data only, an auditable secondary procedure dictionary.
Candidate exact-string families are embolization/interventional E, ablation A,
resection S, and mixed/unassignable X. If exact semantic support is inadequate,
collapse to procedure-versus-other or declare family contrasts inconclusive; never
force a string into a family. For rows in [24,72), report E/A/S/X only as
recorded-family labels. Use orders opened in [0,24) only to define recorded
evidence: K when exactly one E/A/S family is present, M for multiple or
potentially therapeutic unassignable terms, N for none, U for potentially
qualifying text with invalid/missing 开立时间. A matching order is documentation
concordance, never intent or completion. Family contrasts are secondary and
cannot change P0/Uproc/P1/D0/N0.

## Models, alternative and uncertainty

Use one patient-level split: buckets 0-59 fit, 60-69 select prespecified
preprocessing/regularization/state dimension, and 70-79 locked evaluation. No
test outcomes select dictionaries or features.

B0 is transparent cause-specific pooled logistic/competing-risk modelling with
age, sex, admitting department, calendar era, admission time-of-day, diagnosis
descriptors, prior encounter count, and pre-admission nearest/count/recency/
missingness summaries for exact assays. B1 adds only valid numeric/qualitative
assay inputs in [0,24): first/last/change, elapsed time, count, missingness,
density and supported within-assay slope. B0 and B1 share target, rows, split,
preprocessing and uncertainty.

B2 is a substantive irregular longitudinal hierarchical state model on the same
B1 inputs: assay-specific latent hepatic-reserve and renal-stress coordinates
with assay-specific offsets/noise, patient shrinkage, irregular time, posterior
uncertainty, level/slope/coupled movement, and cause-specific P0/Uproc/P1/D0/N0
heads. It may use a low-dimensional smooth/continuous-time state, selected only
in 60-69. It must not treat lab channels as commensurate, and no orders,
medications, later labs, procedures, discharge, or documents enter predictors.
B2 can reveal nonlinear ordering, irregular timing, coupled physiology and
heterogeneity that B1's endpoint summaries lose; B1 remains the required
scientific comparator. CPU-first is appropriate for CSV aggregation, sparse
pooled logistic fits, competing-risk calculations and low-dimensional state
fitting. The future solver envelope is 16 CPUs/256 GiB/up to 8 GPUs/28,800 s;
these are planning facts, not measured runtime. Full feature construction,
state convergence, 500-bootstrap inference and domain strata remain unverified.
If repeated state fitting materially benefits from acceleration, request one
allocated A100 and use cuda:0; GPU is optional and not a scientific criterion.
A GRU/transformer remains deferred unless B2 residuals show reproducible
ordering error after opportunity adjustment and event support is adequate.

Report locked multiclass log loss, Brier score, state calibration intercept/slope,
observed-versus-predicted state probabilities, standardized P1 contrast, and
B2-minus-B1 changes. Use >=500 patient-clustered paired bootstrap resamples,
preserving split and declared locked-prediction/refit policy. Report overall and,
only with adequate support, era/service calibration and family/tier contrasts.

## Falsification and interpretation

Emit row/time provenance proving all B1/B2 labs are in [0,24), all primary
procedure states use native 开始时间, and no post-24 fields enter prediction.
Permute lab timestamps within patient and assay, then values within assay; a
physiology-specific signal should attenuate. Permute terminal states within
era/service strata; score gains should disappear. Add/remove count, gaps,
missingness, density and valid-time fractions; attenuation indicates observation
opportunity. Repeat duplicate/same-time, midnight/00:00, and missing-time rules;
give earliest-plausible and no-event Uproc bounds. Remove one exact procedure
string at a time and preserve X. Permute K/M/N/U tiers within era/service.
Use a same-source routine/central-line/venipuncture workflow endpoint, if support
is sufficient, as a workflow negative control. Compare with a naive model that
ignores competing discharge/Uproc only as a sensitivity.

Supportive primary evidence requires a coherent audit, adequate state support,
calibrated B1, positive B1-B0 held-out score improvement with interval excluding
zero, stable P1 direction, and attenuation under lab and terminal-label
permutations without domination by opportunity variables or Uproc. B2 support
requires calibrated improvement over B1 and stable state interpretation; complexity
alone is not support. Adverse evidence includes null/opposite score differences,
poor calibration, persistence after permutations, observation-process dominance,
negative-control equivalence, one-string dependence, or D0/Uproc sensitivity.
Inconclusive evidence includes sparse states, high Uproc, unstable dictionaries,
nonconvergence, wide intervals, missing units/reference ranges, or inadequate
domain support.

Computationally checkable outputs are hashes/paths/schemas, flow and join audits,
dictionaries, timestamps, split integrity, convergence, predictions, scores,
calibration, uncertainty and falsifications. Clinical adjudication or new data
are required for diagnosis confirmation, stage, tumor burden, resectability,
indication, intent, completion, response, complications, mortality, transplant,
outside care, treatment benefit, clinical utility, causal effects, and deployment.

## Demonstration dispositions and alternatives

The available Delphi article XML/text was inspected for its dated-token
architecture, masked same-time attention, held-out design and calibration-oriented
evaluation; it motivates the temporal comparison but UKB/Danish validation is
unavailable. The ALADYNOULLI article text was inspected for its GP latent
trajectory, Adam fitting and subset holdouts; its genetic inputs are unavailable,
so B2 is an explicitly EHR-only adaptation, not reproduction. Only the cancer
supplement was inspected: its lab/image ablations and baseline comparisons
motivate matched modality comparisons; the main article and complete STAR
Methods remain unavailable, and HCC images are absent. Thus no paper is used as
a topic requirement or as evidence that this HCC hypothesis is true.

The alternative not chosen as primary is a text/image multimodal model: the
needed HCC images and validated time-stamped clinical labels are absent.
A causal treatment-effect design is deferred because indication, eligibility,
receipt, counterfactuals and adjudicated outcomes are absent. A validated liver
score is deferred because lab units/reference ranges are absent. These are
dependency-based deferrals, not blanket bans on neural networks, GPUs or
structured data. The actual deliverable is newly fitted B0/B1/B2 models,
all-admission state probabilities, calibrated uncertainty, semantic sensitivity
audits and falsification results; only those outputs establish completion.
