
# Support-anchored lagged discordant-AKI successor

Status: proposed substantive child of [prior hypothesis]; no cohort scan, model fit, or full-cohort computation was run.

## Scientific repair

The inherited six-hour lag removes boundary co-temporality, but “pre-landmark support” remains only an underspecified covariate/process diagnostic. A discordant state can be selected or produced by recorded treatment/support: higher administered input, more intensive monitoring, and earlier kidney support may change urine and creatinine observations before the landmark.

Unresolved claim:

> Among adults with creatinine-defined AKI who reach a prespecified 24-, 48-, or 72-hour landmark in their first eligible ICU stay, does lagged creatinine/urine/recorded-balance discordance predict seven-day hospital death or ICU-recorded RRT within strata of pre-landmark recorded support intensity, and does the contrast differ between low- and high-support strata?

This is a prognostic/measurement question, not a treatment-effect question. The strongest supported claim is only that AKI is heterogeneous and that volume control, kidney support, observation and recovery context matter [K1]. The leading rival is pre-landmark treatment/support selection and surveillance: more administered input or support may both create the recorded discordance and identify greater severity. A second rival is unmeasured biology/creatinine measurement error. A recovery interpretation should retain a component contrast within adequately supported support strata and across lagged landmarks; a support-selection explanation predicts attenuation, interaction, or state differences concentrated in high recorded-support and high documentation strata. Neither result establishes mechanism, true GFR, total-body volume, or a causal effect.

The substantive change is an explicit, time-indexed support audit using available fields in icu/inputevents and icu/procedureevents, with support-stratified component estimands and support-by-state interaction. It does not silently adjust away a treatment pathway: support is measured before the risk clock, reported as a selection/observation variable, and interpreted as an explanatory rival. The parent lag, washout accounting, first-stay rule, component endpoints, and current-window sensitivity are retained.

## New pre-landmark support contrast

Define support interval S_L=[t0,Ltime-6h), ending before washout and the component outcome clock. Summarize compatible administered input volume from icu/inputevents using the row rule above, normalized by nearest valid pre-endpoint weight when available; input-row count; compatible-mL-row count; unresolved/non-mL count; any-input indicator; itemid/category composition from ordercategoryname, secondaryordercategoryname, ordercomponenttypedescription, ordercategorydescription, amountuom, totalamountuom and statusdescription; and timing overlap. These are recorded administration fields, not treatment intent.

Also summarize pre-L ICU-recorded RRT indicator/timing from the same procedure item set, retaining incident RRT before L in the flow, and non-renal labevents plus non-weight/CVP chartevents density separately from input/output opportunity.

Prespecify support strata from the fitting partition: no compatible input; below-median positive input; at/above-median positive input. If a stratum has fewer than 50 people or fewer than 10 events for a component, report counts and an inconclusive estimate rather than merge post hoc. Repeat with quartiles and input-row-count strata. These are descriptive support/observation strata, not randomized treatment groups and not a causal adjustment.

The primary contrast is the overall lagged component risk difference. The key rival check is the same contrast within support strata plus state-by-support interaction. Supportive evidence requires a nontrivial, directionally consistent component contrast at two landmarks and in adequately supported strata, without concentration in high-support/high-density patients. Attenuation restricted to high support or density supports treatment/surveillance selection. A support interaction is not a causal effect-modification claim.

At L, the risk set contains patients alive, in the clipped index ICU interval and without pre-L incident RRT. Count deaths, ICU exits, RRT, missing creatinine, insufficient opportunity, support categories and washout events; do not assign later states after washout. The result is a survivor/ICU-landmark prognostic estimand, not an onset-cohort or causal estimand.

For hospital death D, use the first valid hosp/admissions.deathtime in [Ltime,Ltime+7d) and no later than valid dischtime; death remains observable after ICU exit while the admission is active. Alive discharge competes; alive in hospital at day 7 is administrative censoring. hospital_expire_flag is a consistency check only. Missing/inconsistent death time is an exception. If deathtime=dischtime, classify death only when flag/time is internally consistent; otherwise count an exception.

This is not all hospital RRT. ICU exit alive and hospital death before RRT compete; earliest valid time wins, with death winning a tie. A start exactly at ICU exit or death is not an RRT event and is a tie exception. Missing starttime is unobservable and separately counted. RRT after ICU exit is not backfilled.

Estimate seven-day cumulative incidence by Aalen–Johansen and standardized risk differences (discordant minus concordant), overall and by support stratum. For D, competing causes are death and alive discharge. For R, causes are RRT, alive ICU exit and death. Report event, competing-event, administrative-censoring and timing-exception counts. Death-or-ICU-RRT is exploratory only and displays both component counts.

## Falsification and uncertainty

Report support-stratum size, event support, unknown-state size and outcome rates. Fit a process-only diagnostic replacing renal discordance with quartiles of pre-landmark non-creatinine labevents plus non-weight/CVP chartevents density; report input/output opportunity separately. A comparable process-only gradient or strong attenuation after support/density stratification weakens renal-recovery interpretation. This is a surveillance/severity diagnostic, not a negative-control outcome.

Use six-hour discrete-time cause-specific hazards only as an implementation; retain the competing-event estimand and Aalen–Johansen audit. Use subject-clustered bootstrap or influence-function uncertainty across landmarks and support strata. Estimate 24-to-48 lagged state transitions only among subjects classifiable at both times, retaining pre-48 outcomes in the flow.

Supportive results do not prove intrinsic renal recovery, benign fluid status, or treatment benefit/harm. Adverse results include disappearance after support stratification, a contrast confined to current-window or contaminated item sensitivities, process-only gradients as large as the kidney contrast, or ICU-RRT differences driven by surveillance/exit. Inconclusive results include sparse support/event cells, wide clinically important intervals, large unknown opportunity, inconsistent death times, or sequence improvements without a reproducible component contrast.

## Baseline and learned/mechanistic alternative

Transparent primary baseline: fixed summaries of the same pre-L/A_L inputs—creatinine peak/level/slope and AKI stage; urine rate, bin masks and item composition; administered compatible input volume, input-row count, unresolved/non-mL count, category/item composition and support strata; weight, exploratory CVP; pre-L RRT/support indicators; ICU timing, admission type, age, sex, baseline creatinine; non-renal density and missingness. Fit separate six-hour cause-specific discrete-time hazards for hospital death and ICU-RRT with stated competing causes. Fit support-by-state interactions and standardize held-out cumulative incidence/risk differences. Output contrasts, event counts, calibration, Brier score and cluster-bootstrap intervals.

Substantive learned alternative: a small GRU or temporal convolution with separate cause-specific heads receives the same six-hour bins from t0 through Ltime-6h: creatinine, urine values/masks, input volume/composition, weight/CVP, RRT/support indicators, non-renal counts and missingness. It cannot see washout or post-L data. Target is the discrete-time cause-specific state for hospital death, alive discharge, ICU-RRT and ICU exit. Use a subject-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test split, keeping all admissions for a subject together. Evaluate held-out component cumulative-incidence calibration, time-dependent Brier, cause-specific discrimination, support-stratum calibration and state/support contrasts with bootstrap uncertainty on identical test subjects.

The baseline is primary because it makes the estimand and support strata auditable. The learned model can reveal within-window timing, persistence and support-response trajectories lost by fixed summaries; it is not selected for a small predictive gain. Plan CPU work at 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds; expected 1–6 hours is unverified until a bounded readiness probe. An A100 is optional acceleration, not required. Revisit only if the learned model changes endpoint-specific trajectory or support-stratum calibration with adequate events; defer for sparse cells or an AUROC-only gain.

## Availability limits and alternatives not chosen

MIMIC lacks cystatin C/measured GFR, muscle mass, tubular biomarkers, adjudicated total-body volume or insensible losses, treatment intent, complete ward RRT, reliable post-ICU urine collection, and prospective goals-of-care information. icu/inputevents provides recorded administered fields, not a complete treatment record. chartevents support proxies require dictionary verification and are not a validated severity score.

The current-window [L-24h,L) exposure remains a sensitivity. The mixed death-or-ICU-RRT union remains exploratory because its clocks differ. A causal treatment analysis, inverse-probability correction for ward RRT, and a single learned sequence as primary are deferred because treatment intent, complete support capture and adjudicated endpoints are unavailable. Revisit with complete treatment/observation modeling, post-ICU RRT capture and clinical adjudication.

## Audit findings motivating the child

- The parent’s schema names were valid, but the exposure/support contract did not define actual support fields, timing, unit rule or missingness. Without that contract the treatment/support rival could not be tested.
- icu/outputevents has value, not valuenum; numeric parsing must be explicit.
- icu/inputevents contains amount and totalamount plus interval/rate fields. Summing both, or summing an untrimmed amount for a boundary-spanning row, would double count support and change the estimand. The child fixes row precedence and interval clipping.
- “Overlapping admission” could allow ICU intervals extending past hospital discharge. The child clips ICU observation and reports the discrepancy.
- First-stay, blank-hadm, death/discharge ties, ICU-exit/RRT ties, missing times and pre-L RRT are deterministic flow categories.
- These are substantive feasibility repairs: without explicit pre-landmark support exposure, the contrast cannot distinguish renal-recovery information from treatment/support selection. The child changes the estimand to a support-stratified prognostic comparison while preserving the clinical question and limits.
