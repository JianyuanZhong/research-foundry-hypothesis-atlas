# Congestion-guided early IV loop initiation after shock stabilization

## Question and falsifiable hypothesis

Positive fluid balance is associated with poor ICU outcomes, but this does not show that removal improves outcomes. RADAR-2 (Silversides et al., DOI 10.1007/s00134-021-06596-8; Europe PMC source `[source checksum]`) randomized 180 patients in a feasibility trial: conservative fluid/active deresuscitation lowered balance, but clinical outcomes were underpowered and the mortality estimate could not exclude harm. The frozen full-text 2026 Orieux review (DOI 10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083; source `[source checksum]`) calls ICU renal congestion hypothesis-generating, without validated diagnostic criteria, targets, or outcome-driven interventional evidence. The strongest supported claim is balance reduction, not respiratory benefit or renal safety.

**Hypothesis:** among adult, mechanically ventilated, loop-naive ICU patients at hour 48 who are off vasopressors after prior pressor-treated shock, have >=5% documented net accumulation, adequate urine output, potassium >=3.0 mmol/L, and no RRT, an actually administered IV loop initiated within 12 hours increases VFD28 versus no loop through 24 hours, without increasing 7-day death or new RRT. Benefit is larger when a baseline text-derived chest phenotype shows pulmonary vascular/interstitial congestion. An observational result cannot establish causality, renal congestion, or standard care.

## Target trial and eligibility

Use the first ICU stay per `subject_id`; do not require a future 72-hour stay. Set `t0 = icu/icustays.intime + 48h` and require `outtime > t0`. Use only information available at or before t0:

- Adult by `patients.anchor_age`/`anchor_year` at `intime`; retain MIMIC’s top-coded age 91.
- `procedureevents` item 225792 (“Invasive Ventilation”) has `starttime <= t0 <= endtime`.
- At least one prior positive administration of pressor IDs 221289 epinephrine, 221662 dopamine, 221749 phenylephrine, 221906 norepinephrine, or 222315 vasopressin; no pressor interval overlaps t0. Verify mappings in `d_items`.
- Median valid MAP in `(t0-3h,t0]` >=65. MAP IDs are frozen from `d_items`; parent candidates 220052/220181/225312 must be checked against this snapshot. Missing MAP excludes.
- Event-level net accumulation from `intime` through t0 >=50 mL/kg, with weight 30–300 kg.
- No loop administration anywhere in index ICU/hospital observation before t0 (furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide, ethacrynic acid). This full washout replaces the parent’s 6-hour washout and addresses prevalent-user/carryover bias. eMAR has no `stay_id`; use an eMAR record for this rule only when `hadm_id` and `charttime` place it unambiguously in the index ICU interval.
- No RRT before t0: procedure IDs 225441 Hemodialysis, 225802 CRRT, 225803 CVVHD, 225805 Peritoneal Dialysis, 225809 CVVHDF, or 225955 SCUF with `starttime < t0`.
- Urine output >0.1 mL/kg/h in `(t0-6h,t0]`, requiring auditable output capture; latest potassium in `(t0-12h,t0]) >=3.0. Missing eligibility values exclude.
- No ECMO/other protocol-defined support overlapping t0 by frozen procedure labels. Do not use discharge diagnoses or post-t0 notes to exclude comfort-focused care; missing timestamped goals-of-care information is an explicit limitation.

No future ICU length, post-treatment weight, discharge diagnosis, or post-t0 measurement enters eligibility.

## Balance, exposure, and separability

Balance is computed from event records. From `icu/inputevents`, use numeric `amount`, `amountuom`, `starttime`, `endtime`, and `statusdescription); convert recognized mL/L only. Use amount, not rate or totalamount, retaining rate, totalamount, orderid and linkorderid for audit. Exclude explicit not-started/not-given rows and tabulate statuses before freezing that rule. The archive confirms relevant statuses include `FinishedRunning`, `ChangeDose/Rate`, `Stopped`, `Paused`, and `Bolus`. Primary balance uses events ending by each boundary; straddling events are excluded and included in a duration/rate sensitivity. Never count amount and rate twice.

From `icu/outputevents`, use numeric `value`, `valueuom`, `charttime`, and `itemid`, convert recognized volumes, and sum measured outputs; absence is missing, not zero. Require a valid output record in every pre-t0 6-hour bin; a stricter analysis requires valid input and output capture in all eight bins. Report unit conversion, duplicates, coverage, and missingness. Weight is first valid Admission Weight (parent mapping 226512), then first Daily Weight (224639), mapped in `d_items`; `inputevents.patientweight` is sensitivity only. This is documented net balance, not total body water.

Strategy A is actual IV loop initiation in `(t0,t0+12h]`, after which care is unrestricted. Strategy B is no loop through `t0+24h`. Primary exposure uses actual eMAR administrations of furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide, or ethacrynic acid: join `hosp/emar` to `hosp/emar_detail` on `subject_id,emar_id,emar_seq`; require normalized loop medication text, `event_txt` of Administered or Delayed Administered, positive numeric `dose_given`, `complete_dose_not_given` not indicating failure, and normalized IV `route`. This preserves the all-loop clinical strategy and excludes orders, prescriptions, holds, and oral-only therapy. ICU `inputevents` IDs 221794 Furosemide (Lasix) and 228340 Furosemide (Lasix) 250/50, confirmed in `d_items` as mg medications, are an administration/concordance source for furosemide only. Report eMAR/input overlap, discordance, missing detail and route; a sensitivity analysis requires concordance for furosemide. Because ICU items do not cover all loop drugs, they cannot alone define the primary all-loop strategy.

Clone each eligible person to both arms at t0. Censor A for non-initiation at 12h and B at first loop through 24h, while alive. Retain death before possible administration as a competing event (VFD=0), rather than censoring it as artificial nonadherence; follow-up ends at death. This is a grace-period clone-censor-weight design. The estimand is the per-protocol mean VFD28 difference (A minus B) in the eligible population, conditional on consistency, positivity, and no unmeasured confounding.

## Outcomes and congestion timing

VFD28 runs from t0 to day 28: death known by day 28 is zero; otherwise subtract clipped invasive-ventilation (225792) days from 28. Death uses the earliest available `admissions.deathtime`/`patients.dod` and `hospital_expire_flag`. Since core MIMIC has no post-discharge ventilation, name this **VFD28 with alive-discharge carry-forward**, assuming alive discharge means no later invasive ventilation. Mandatory sensitivities are hospital-observed-through-day-28 VFD, alive/extubated days while observed, in-hospital extubation by day 7 (early discharge is unknown, not failed extubation), and mortality/ICU or hospital-free days. Do not call the carry-forward measure true VFD without this caveat.

Primary safety is 7-day death or new RRT, with components separate and RRT/death treated as competing causes. Secondary renal outcomes use creatinine and potassium from `hosp/labevents` mapped by `hosp/d_labitems`. KDIGO creatinine AKI through day 7 is >=0.3 mg/dL in 48h or >=1.5x a pre-t0 reference (lowest prior-7-day or most recent pre-t0 by frozen hierarchy). Report stages, creatinine change, K<3, and MAP<65 separately. Do not use urine-output AKI as primary because output capture is the feasibility concern; death before AKI and post-discharge missing labs are handled as competing/unknown, not normal.

The radiology source is the separate ordinary read-only `[internal dataset path]` ([source checksum]), not an archive member. Columns are `note_id,subject_id,hadm_id,note_type,note_seq,charttime,storetime,text`. `radiology_detail.csv.gz` has `subject_id,note_id,field_name,field_value,field_ordinal` and no independent time. Select the latest chest report with `charttime <= t0` and `charttime > t0-12h`; require `storetime <= t0` when present and show charttime-only sensitivity. Join by subject+hadm; if hadm is absent, assign only in one unique admission/ICU interval, otherwise unknown. Freeze section/negation-aware categories: pulmonary vascular/interstitial edema/congestion; isolated pleural effusion; neither/unknown. This is text-derived, not confirmed congestion. Two blinded clinicians should review >=200 stratified reports with third adjudication; without it, no clinical congestion claim. MIMIC-CXR images, VExUS, echo, examination, intra-abdominal pressure, and renal venous pressure are unavailable.

## Analysis, confounding, and gates

Create clone-hour rows through hour 24. At each hour use clinical-time records available by the hour and retain `storetime` for leakage sensitivity. Estimate stabilized inverse-probability adherence/censoring weights with pooled logistic models containing treatment history and lagged MAP, urine output, balance, creatinine, lactate, respiratory support, potassium, and pressor status. These factors are treatment predictors and may be treatment-affected; use a marginal structural analysis, not ordinary post-treatment adjustment. Baseline covariates include age, sex, race, admission type/year, first unit, prior-admission comorbidity, weight/balance, pressors, MAP, heart rate, temperature, FiO2, PEEP, oxygenation, CVP when present, labs, sedation, crystalloid/albumin, and baseline radiology. Prior comorbidity is `diagnoses_icd` joined to `d_icd_diagnoses` only for admissions completed before index.

Primary estimator is standardized weighted mean VFD difference with subject-clustered robust uncertainty and 500 subject-level bootstrap replicates. Report unadjusted and baseline-standardized differences descriptively; a naive treated-within-12h versus untreated analysis is prohibited as causal. Report weight distributions, 1st/99th truncation, balance, arm-specific ESS, adherence probabilities, and outcome ascertainment. Sensitivities: 10% accumulation; continuous balance; 6h/24h windows; cardiac surgery/prior heart-failure exclusions; no albumin co-initiation; eMAR/input concordance; strict complete accounting; first ICU per hospitalization; calendar interaction; quantitative bias analysis. Report radiology stratum estimates and an interaction interval, not a subgroup p-value.

The parent’s preliminary pass found 1,122 broad candidates before final refinements, 116/212/348 ICU furosemide initiations by 6/12/24h, and a nearly identical radiology pass found 599 recent reports/357 crude term matches. These are feasibility counts, not results. Recompute final attrition after every new-user, balance, MAP, urine, potassium, RRT, and actual-exposure rule. Before outcome modeling, declare infeasible/inconclusive if either arm has <100 observed adherers, ESS<75, >10% adherence probability <0.01, or material balance/outcome missingness. A proposed change to population, windows, estimand, outcomes, analysis, or falsification returns to the Lead.

Supportive requires VFD estimate >=+1 day with 95% CI excluding zero, upper CI for excess death/new RRT <+5 percentage points, expected more-negative 24h balance, adequate positivity, no material pre-t0 placebo signal, and reliable VFD sensitivity. Imaging support additionally requires interaction support and blinded review. This supports external validation/enriched RCT, not practice. Adverse is VFD upper CI <=0 or safety-harm lower CI >=+5 points. Inconclusive includes all other patterns, wide intervals, benefit with safety harm, nonpositivity, failed falsification, exposure discordance, or uncertain VFD; nonsignificance is not equivalence. Falsification is a weighted pre-t0 placebo balance/creatinine/urine slope that should not differ after adjustment; process checks are more-negative 24h balance and increased urine output under A, not efficacy proof.
