# MERTK cell-context therapeutic-window hypothesis: archival RNA update

**Status: hypothesis plus one completed descriptive reanalysis; no causal or wet-lab test has been executed.** The clinically consequential unresolved question is whether selective MERTK inhibition can reduce inflammatory macrophage-to-fibroblast-like synoviocyte (FLS) signaling in an inflammatory context without impairing resolution-associated macrophage functions, or whether apparent compound-linked effects reflect off-target action, exposure/toxicity, carry-over, donor composition, or assay artifacts. The result could guide whether MERTK merits cell-restricted preclinical development versus deferral; it cannot justify systemic inhibition or clinical use.

## Existing evidence versus the unresolved claim

The strongest claim already supported by the inspected literature is that MerTK-positive synovial macrophage subsets associate with rheumatoid arthritis (RA) remission/stromal repair context [K1], MERTK has broader homeostatic and immune-regulatory roles [K2], and synovial matrix remodeling can affect immune patterning [K3]. These observations motivate separate inflammatory-benefit and resolution-harm outcomes; they do not prove MERTK causality, selectivity, or a therapeutic window. K1 is abstract/metadata-only; K2 is abstract-only and a cancer-focused review; K3 is one inspected Introduction passage, not the full paper.

**Hypothesis to test prospectively:** Under verified, non-toxic target engagement, MERTK loss reduces inflammatory FLS output in inflammatory myeloid–FLS coculture, while loss in remission-associated synovial macrophages impairs efferocytosis and/or matrix-repair support. The strongest rival is that drug-associated effects arise from compound off-target activity or concentration/carry-over/toxicity, or from donor/state/measurement differences rather than MERTK function. Orthogonal MERTK loss, guide-resistant WT versus kinase-dead rescue, engagement and off-target controls, and separately measured function make these explanations observably different. Drug-only effects without genetic/rescue concordance favor the compound-artifact rival. Even concordant ex-vivo results would not establish in-vivo selectivity, clinical benefit, or safety.

## Completed archival Harbor computation: narrow scope and result

Managed compute job `[research job]` succeeded in 2.70 seconds using 2 CPUs and 8 GiB, reading only the read-only prepared `E-MTAB-8316.counts.tsv.gz` and `E-MTAB-8316.design.json`. It checked 12 exact sample columns, 58,302 features, the six prespecified pairs (MK3/MK4, MK7/MK8, MK11/MK12, MK15/MK16, MK19/MK20, MK23/MK24), treatment labels and same-FLS pairing. Normalization was log2 CPM with 0.5 prior count; two fixed scores were unweighted means of four specified Ensembl feature IDs each. The complete per-pair output and calculation summary are attached as `mertk_archive_pair_scores.csv` and `mertk_archive_summary.json`.

Observed descriptive pattern: the inflammatory-labelled score increased in all six inhibitor-minus-vehicle configurations (mean +1.717 log2-CPM score units; median +1.748); the ECM-labelled score decreased in all six (mean −0.293; median −0.314). These signs persisted when averaging within each of the four FLS donor clusters. The nominal pair-level intervals are not treated as inferential evidence: six configurations are not six independent FLS donors, because SA049 and SA114 recur; there are four FLS donor clusters and six nested monocyte donors. This is an adverse/mixed result for the proposed *archival transcript rationale* (not support for the anticipated inflammatory suppression and not evidence of MERTK-specific functional harm). It should de-prioritize any argument based on the desired archived RNA direction and motivates checking the annotated exposure before biological interpretation.

The computation did **not** independently re-annotate the Ensembl-to-symbol mapping; feature IDs were present and the predefined mappings were assumed. The unusual 250 µM UNC106238 annotation requires source-team confirmation of compound, units, lot, exposure schedule, and washout; actual free exposure, target engagement, selectivity, toxicity, and carry-over are unavailable. RNA abundance is not cytokine secretion, matrix repair, efferocytosis, viability, target engagement, or clinical outcome. This published-data reanalysis is neither independent replication nor a causal test. Supporting/adverse/inconclusive solver conclusions must link to computed outputs and treat four donor clusters—not six experiments—as the independent grouping for sensitivity; uncertainty is descriptive and a wide interval would not refute the causal hypothesis.

## Prospective computable study and decision rules

A Stage-0 pharmacology gate verifies UNC106238 identity, actual free concentration, stability, engagement, AXL/TYRO3 off-target readouts, washout/carry-over, and viability; stop the drug arm if no engaged non-toxic range is demonstrated. Then cross independent RA myeloid donors with FLS donors and compare inflammatory monocyte-derived macrophages with remission-associated sorted synovial macrophages under randomized vehicle, qualified dose/second-chemotype, two independent MERTK-loss reagents, non-targeting controls, and guide-resistant WT versus kinase-dead rescue. Include FLS-only exposure and residual-drug-spike controls. Donor is the inferential unit; wells are nested. A proposed 24 donors (12 active RA, 12 sustained remission) is a planning target only, contingent on tissue access and blinded variance/power review; clinician experts must adjudicate state and margins.

Measure target engagement, off-target activity and viability alongside 24-hour FLS inflammatory protein outputs (IL-6, IL-8/CXCL8, MMP3), 60–90-minute macrophage efferocytosis, and 48-hour FLS matrix repair (closure plus collagen/fibronectin deposition or contraction). Estimate within-donor perturbation effects and context interaction with prespecified hierarchical models and donor-cluster bootstrap; show all donor values, 95% uncertainty intervals and QC. Set smallest-effect and harm/equivalence margins with clinicians/lab experts before unblinding. Support requires engagement, genetic/drug concordance, WT but not kinase-dead rescue, and context-specific benefit/harm beyond expert margins. Adequate engagement/precision with no inflammatory benefit or broad harm is adverse to target prioritization. Failed engagement/rescue, toxicity, poor yield, invalid assays or intervals spanning benefit and harm margins are inconclusive. Stronger claims require independently recruited donors, expert assay/state adjudication, dose/pharmacology validation, and later delivery/in-vivo/clinical studies.

## Method choice, alternatives, and resource plan

The archived baseline is the fixed six-pair transcript-score contrast above. The scientifically substantive alternative is transcriptome-wide paired count effects plus predeclared, correlation-aware inflammatory/ECM gene-set coherence and leave-one-FLS-donor-out sensitivity. It could reveal coherent pathway changes or directions missed by eight fixed genes, but adds multiplicity/annotation/model sensitivity and cannot fix the exposure or causality gap. Defer it for this frozen deliverable: only four repeated FLS clusters and no prespecified decision rule for acting on discovered pathways. A donor-slope model is not identified well enough by four clusters; don't mistake model complexity for more independent information. For the future functional study, a nonlinear exposure–engagement–function model could estimate context-specific Emax/EC50 and benefit/harm windows beyond paired donor contrasts, but only if engagement assays, sample size and held-out-donor calibration are adequate; otherwise use the donor-paired baseline and defer the alternative. No GPU is justified for the 12-sample archival computation. The measured job took 2.70 seconds with 2 CPUs/8 GiB; future transcriptome-wide CPU budget is estimated <10 minutes and <=8 GiB (unmeasured), while prospective model time is unmeasured and depends on collected donors/assays; solver planning envelope is 16 CPUs, 8 GPUs, 256 GiB and 8 hours, not a discovery requirement.

Actual scientific deliverable is the newly computed fixed-score paired RNA contrasts, per-pair outputs and donor-cluster sensitivity, alongside a fully specified prospective orthogonal perturbation design. Completion of the archival computation does not complete or resolve the target hypothesis. Continue only to resolve exposure/engagement and validate assays; revise if feature identity or protocol is wrong; defer if donor material or expert margins are unavailable; abandon the target rationale if adequately engaged genetic loss/rescue shows no benefit or unacceptable broad harm. Three earlier clinical demonstration papers are not adapted because their longitudinal clinical, imaging, or genetic outcome modalities are not present in these molecular assays. The two imported D2 seed questions were assessed as repairable, and their context-dependence/benefit-harm concepts are represented here; neither seed text is treated as evidence.
