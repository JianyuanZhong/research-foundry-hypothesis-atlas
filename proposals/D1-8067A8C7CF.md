### Exact finite-support inference

If exact enumeration has fewer than 50 distinct assignments, report the complete support and mark inferential resolution unavailable. Repeated-landmark cluster integrity, arm counts, and stratum membership are mechanically checked for every assignment.

The null is supportive only if the observed label increment is more favorable than the predeclared 97.5th percentile of the valid null for both primary scoring rules, while the parent positive-information gates also pass: favorable stay-cluster interval excludes zero, both micro and equal-hospital macro improvements occur for all-row `K` and classifiable trajectory analyses, and at least two frozen airway/observation sensitivities pass without more than 25% held-out hospital-direction reversals.
