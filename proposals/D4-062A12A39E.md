## Question, hypothesis and substantive advance

The assessed parent asks whether direction-free movement between two eight-field biomarker panels adds five-year fixed-capacity prioritization beyond current values, prior values, repeat spacing, calendar timing and a measured repeat-observation propensity. Its pooled circulatory endpoint is useful, but a pooled increment can conceal a clinically important distinction: a movement signal that helps prioritize a first cardiac/heart-failure record need not help prioritize a first cerebrovascular record.

This successor tests:

> Among UK Biobank participants with a valid paired panel and no recorded incident circulatory event or death by the current assessment, is the incremental fixed-capacity capture from adding longitudinal movement to current/prior/spacing/selection information different for a first recorded cardiac/heart-failure pathway versus a first recorded cerebrovascular pathway over the next five years?

The primary falsifiable hypothesis is a pathway-heterogeneity hypothesis. Let Delta_C be the movement-minus-prior-controlled increment in held-out capture at a fixed review capacity for the cardiac/heart-failure category, and Delta_V the corresponding increment for cerebrovascular disease. The prespecified contrast is Theta_CV = Delta_C - Delta_V. The directional working hypothesis is Theta_CV > 0, because a blood-biomarker movement representation may be more informative for a cardiac/heart-failure pathway; this direction is not supported by local fitted evidence and is not assumed in interpretation. The primary test must report the two-sided interval for Theta_CV and the two one-sided directional possibilities. A practically meaningful positive difference is defined in advance as Theta_CV > 0.05 with the simultaneous 95% lower bound above 0.05, alongside a positive cardiac increment. A practically meaningful negative difference is defined symmetrically as Theta_CV < -0.05 with the simultaneous upper bound below -0.05. If neither criterion is met, the pathway distinction is not established.

This is not a test of whether ordering a repeat test causes benefit, whether a biomarker is worsening, or whether a patient should receive a treatment. It is a conditional prognostic and review-list estimand in the repeat-observed population. Its advance over the parent is that it asks whether movement changes the composition of a fixed-capacity review queue in a disease-family-specific way, while preserving the parent’s pooled contrast for continuity.

## Population and temporal boundaries

Let T0 = 53-0.0 and T1 = 53-1.0. A dictionary must certify field 53 as an assessment-date landmark and certify that its two instances are comparable landmarks. Require valid 31-0.0 sex and 21022-0.0 recruitment age, T0 < T1, and 0.25 < L = (T1 - T0)/365.2425 <= 5 years. Require T1 <= 2013-12-31.

For the measured repeat-selection population, include every T0-complete participant with a valid T0 date and all eight T0 biomarker cells, whether or not a valid T1 panel is observed. Define S=1 only if valid T1 timing and all eight T1 cells pass the same range and missingness rules. Do not define S using future outcomes. Fit the repeat-selection model only from T0 panel, recruitment age, sex and T0 calendar year.

The primary pathway cohort is S=1 with complete sixteen-cell paired panels, valid timing, and no qualifying C/V/O event or death on or before T1. Participants with any qualifying incident circulatory category before or on T1, or death on or before T1, are excluded. Keep the parent’s original cohort construction and endpoint as a separately labelled continuity analysis; do not silently redefine a parent result using the pathway endpoint.

Split by landmark date:

- Early fitting/application: T1 <= 2012-12-31.
- Late application: 2013-01-01 <= T1 <= 2013-12-31.

The late data are never used to fit a model, imputation, percentile map, propensity, hyperparameter, risk-band cutpoint or calibration. For each participant, H is T1 plus five calendar years using explicit calendar-date addition and leap-day handling. Late outcomes may extend after 2013 only if five-year ascertainment is certified.

## Fail-closed first-recorded pathway endpoint

For every j=0,...,258, pair 41270-0.j only with 41280-0.j. Never sort either array independently, nearest-date match, or combine positions across participants. Normalize each nonempty code by trimming whitespace, uppercasing, and taking its ICD-10 category before any decimal.

The endpoint dictionary must certify that the paired arrays represent diagnosis codes and diagnosis dates and that ICD-10 category parsing is valid. Define the mutually exclusive first-event categories:

- C: earliest qualifying category in I20, I21, I22, I23, I24, I25 or I50 (cardiac/heart-failure pathway);
- V: earliest qualifying category in I60, I61, I62, I63 or I64 (cerebrovascular pathway);
- O: earliest other valid category in I00-I99 not in C or V (other recorded circulatory pathway);
- D: death after T1 and no later than H if it precedes or shares the date of the first qualifying circulatory event;
- N: no qualifying I00-I99 event or death recorded through H under the source rule.

For a nonempty code without a valid same-position date, or a nonempty date without its same-position code, record the position and fail closed for accepted participants and the required audit. Do not treat an unparsable or non-ICD code as O. A date-only missingness rule cannot convert unknown coding into absence. Parse 40000-0.0 and 40000-1.0 as calendar dates; if both exist they must agree, and disagreement or an unparsable value is an integrity failure. A qualifying event before death takes precedence; same-day event and death is D. Non-circulatory codes are not counted as C/V/O, but their presence does not create evidence of disease-free status.

The primary pathway estimand is therefore a competing recorded-event estimand among C, V, O, D and N. It is not true onset, severity, recurrence, outpatient completeness or causal disease transition. The parent pooled continuity outcome remains its exact target family C+V with death competition, so the successor can show whether a new pathway distinction changes the parent’s conclusion.

Require evidence that each participant has outcome follow-up complete through H. If completeness cannot be established from snapshot provenance, return all parsing and censoring audits but mark interpretation of N, five-year risk and pathway capture inconclusive. ## Movement and measured repeat-selection adjustment

Within each early training fold, fit an empirical percentile map F_j using eligible training-fold T0 values only. Apply it unchanged to current and prior values in the held-out early fold and late era; clip outside-range values to training endpoints and report clipping. For each field:

u_ij = |F_j(x_i,T1) - F_j(x_i,T0)|.

Set B_i = mean_j(u_ij), movement burden, and H_i = SD_j(u_ij), movement-magnitude discordance. These are direction-free representations, not analyte diagnoses. The secondary field-resolved representation retains all eight u_ij without naming biological directions.

Fit p_i = P(S=1 | T0 panel, age, sex, T0 year) with prespecified regularized logistic regression inside early training folds. Apply it unchanged to held-out early and late participants. Use stabilized weights w_i = P(S=1)/p_i, truncated only at early 1st and 99th percentiles, and report both weighted and unweighted results. This adjusts measured repeat availability only; it does not remove unmeasured testing indication, access, utilization, treatment, specimen handling or assay drift. Report propensity overlap, standardized differences, extreme weights and effective sample size.

## Nested models, pathway prediction and fixed capacity

Create one deterministic participant-level five-fold hash split on eid in the early era. All imputation, range rules, percentile maps, selection models, penalties, booster settings, calibration and cutpoints are training-fold-only. Late data are no-refit application data.

Fit the following transparent multinomial competing-event models to C/V/O/D/N:

- P0: current eight-field T1 panel, age, sex and T1 calendar year;
- P1s: P0 plus L, T0 calendar year and fold-local logit repeat propensity;
- P1p: P1s plus eight T0 absolute biomarker values;
- P1m: P1p plus B and H (primary movement model);
- P1u: P1p plus all eight u_ij (secondary movement-pattern model).

The P1p versus P1m comparison is the primary incremental movement contrast. P1m versus P1s is retained as the parent continuity contrast, with the parent’s original pooled endpoint recomputed exactly. P1u tests whether a field-resolved movement pattern adds information beyond movement burden/discordance, without supporting a mechanism claim.

Use matched shallow gradient-boosted multinomial C/V/O/D/N models:

- P2s has exactly P1s inputs;
- P2p has exactly P1p inputs;
- P2m has exactly P1m inputs;
- P2u has exactly P1u inputs.

The additive and boosted model within each pair must share people, split, target, A, K, weights and evaluation. P2m is scientifically useful only because it can reveal pathway-specific thresholds, saturation and interactions that an additive model averages away. A learned gain without an additive gain is evidence about functional form, not proof of clinical utility or biology.

### Frozen decision population and capacity

Preserve the parent’s decision population: use early out-of-fold P1s predictions for the parent pooled C+V target, and define the central ambiguity band A by q20 <= r < q80. The lower and upper tails are r < q20 and r >= q80. Apply the early q20/q80 cutpoints unchanged in 2013. No movement, late outcome or late quantile may define A.

Before inspecting outcome results, set K = floor(0.10 * n_A,early). Require K >= 40, n_A,late >= K, at least 100 pooled C+V events overall and at least 30 events in each of C and V for the pathway heterogeneity analysis, finite weights and adequate weighted overlap/ESS. If these support gates fail, pathway inference is inconclusive and no threshold is relaxed after seeing results.

For each model M, era e and family f in {C,V}, form the family-specific review list R_Mf,e by selecting exactly K participants with the largest held-out predicted probability p_Mf within A; deterministic eid-hash tie breaks are mandatory. This is a fixed-capacity pathway-specific queue, not a generic discrimination score. Also report a secondary shared queue selecting exactly K by p_MC + p_MV, then measure C and V capture in that same queue. The shared queue is descriptive of a single circulatory review capacity; the family-specific queues are the primary estimands for pathway heterogeneity.

For family f, define weighted capture and yield:

C_f(M,e) = sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=f) 1(i in R_Mf,e) / sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=f)

P_f(M,e) = sum_i w_i 1(Y_i=f) 1(i in R_Mf,e) / sum_i w_i 1(i in R_Mf,e).

Define:

- Delta_f(e) = C_f(P1m,e) - C_f(P1p,e);
- Theta_CV(e) = Delta_C(e) - Delta_V(e);
- tau_f = Delta_f(late) - Delta_f(early), temporal transport;
- the same contrasts for P2m versus P2p and for shared queues.

Also report weighted/unweighted family yields, multiclass Brier score, classwise log loss, calibration intercept/slope, reliability, resolution, observed/predicted C/V/O/D/N frequencies and the parent pooled C+V continuity contrast. Capture and yield are review-list quantities, not estimates of treatment benefit.

## Uncertainty and analysis

Use paired participant bootstrap intervals with 2,000 replicates, resampling within fixed era-by-A strata while retaining the frozen split, A, K, predictions, weights and review-list construction. Report failed-support replicates. Use a declared simultaneous interval or multiplicity adjustment for the two primary family increments, Theta_CV and the key learned comparison. A separately labelled full-pipeline bootstrap may refit fold processing but cannot replace the primary held-out analysis.

Report early and late estimates separately before any pooled summary. Require stable signs and materially similar family ordering across folds; inspect leave-one-fold-out results. The late result is internal temporal application, not external validation.

- permute B/H within frozen A while preserving current levels, L, propensity, missingness and K; a retained primary gain falsifies movement attribution;
- permute the prior panel across participants before constructing movement while leaving current values unchanged; a retained gain indicates leakage or confounding;
- replace prior with current values, making u=0; any residual movement increment is an implementation failure;

- break same-position code/date pairing deliberately and require the integrity gate to fail;
- permute early/late labels and repeat transport;
- compare complete-case and fold-local missingness policies, with the primary policy fixed before outcomes;
- compare deterministic random-K lists and verify that they do not systematically outperform;
- verify no late value, outcome, quantile, cutpoint or model parameter entered early fitting.

A gain disappearing under movement/prior permutation or after measured-selection weighting is adverse to the movement-specific claim. A gain surviving label/era permutations, or an endpoint integrity gate that does not fail when deliberately broken, is adverse to computation validity.

## Interpretation rules

Supportive pathway evidence requires:

- valid source hashes, headers, unique-eid joins and no unexplained required-field disagreement;
- certified assessment timing, paired-panel comparability, field ranges/missingness and endpoint code/date semantics;
- complete five-year ascertainment and valid death reconciliation;
- K, event, overlap and ESS support gates;
- calibrated held-out predictions and stable fold behavior;
- a simultaneous interval showing a prespecified pathway difference of at least 0.05 in either direction, with the corresponding family increment in that direction, and no early-to-late sign reversal;
- concordant fixed-capacity yield and, where evaluated, shared-queue behavior;
- null permutations that remove the observed signal.

This supports only a conditional association: in the supported repeat-observed UKB population and the frozen current-risk band, movement’s recorded-event prioritization differs between the two prespecified disease-family pathways. It would motivate pathway-specific prospective monitoring research, not a clinical protocol.

Adverse evidence includes no pathway difference, a difference explained by prior levels, spacing or measured repeat selection, disappearance under movement or family-label nulls, poor calibration, random-list superiority, fold instability, temporal reversal, or invalid endpoint handling. This weakens the case for pathway-specific movement-aware prioritization for this population and endpoint; it does not prove that no movement is useful in another population or endpoint.

Inconclusive evidence includes absent or conflicting field dictionaries, incompatible assessment timing, malformed paired arrays, unknown ICD coding, death disagreement, incomplete horizon follow-up, sparse C/V events, infeasible K, poor overlap/ESS, broad intervals, unstable clipping or missingness sensitivity, or inability to separate movement from regression to the mean, analytical variation, treatment, assay drift or unmeasured testing selection.

Computationally checkable claims include source/schema integrity, joins, filtering, date arithmetic, paired endpoint parsing, event ordering, model fitting, no-refit application, movement construction, propensity weighting, frozen A/K, list membership, capture/yield arithmetic, calibration, uncertainty and null behavior. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for actual disease onset, outpatient completeness, symptom/severity context, treatment effects, action thresholds, causal effects, prospective benefit and external validity. The arrays do not establish a biological transition. An automatic verifier cannot establish those stronger claims.

## Alternatives, deferred branches and compute

The selected transparent baseline is P1p because it is the strongest same-input counterfactual before movement: current levels, prior levels, timing and measured repeat selection are retained. P1m is selected as the primary representation because B/H are auditable without unsupported analyte direction or units. P2m is retained as the matched learned alternative because thresholds and interactions may be clinically relevant to pathway separation. P1u/P2u are sensitivity analyses for whether the aggregate movement summary hides a field-pattern effect.

Signed worsening is deferred because the local catalog does not certify per-field labels, units, missing-value meanings, collection timing or biological direction. Raw absolute differences are deferred because unverified scales can make the result unit-dependent. A latent GP/sequence or mechanistic model is not rejected for complexity, but it would require a richer validated chronology or a distinct adjudicated target. Raw imaging, raw waveforms, narrative clinical text and sequence variants are unavailable; genomics is derived metadata, so a multimodal reproduction is not claimed. The expert UKB-09 seed was inspected as conceptual context, but its broad metabolic-deterioration claim did not supply verified field semantics or a better endpoint than this bounded pathway adaptation.
