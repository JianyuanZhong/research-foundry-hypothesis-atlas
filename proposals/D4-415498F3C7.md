## Controlling parent, audit finding, and single repair

No participant is reselected, reweighted, refilled, dropped, or assigned a sex-specific quota.

The parent's most consequential still-computable clinical limitation is pooled generalization. AG1 includes grip strength, a strongly sex-structured measurement; recorded sex also enters the frozen equations and prediction matrix. The parent can therefore support overall low-UACR prognosis enrichment even if AG1 loses low-UACR kidney-event yield relative to G1 in one recorded-sex group and gains more in the other. That would materially qualify a proposal to carry the unchanged rule into a prospective study.

The new independently falsifiable hypothesis is:

> On the exact frozen n1=466 AG1 and G1 lists, neither the female-tagged nor male-tagged contribution to nine-year observed-A1 inpatient-N18 yield is worse under AG1 by a net whole event: for each recorded sex, the simultaneous one-sided 95% lower confidence limit for AG1 minus G1 is strictly above -1000/466 events per 1,000 fixed cystatin slots.

The threshold m=1000/466=2.1459227468 per 1,000 is a resolution-aware one-net-event noninferiority margin, not a validated fairness, utility, or benefit margin. Because each point estimate is an integer multiple of m, support implies no observed net whole-event decrement in either recorded-sex contribution and uncertainty excluding a decrement of one or more events. Equality at -m is inconclusive.

This is a qualification, not a rescue. Its independent recorded-sex label is always emitted, but a clinically stronger `low_UACR_supported_and_recorded_sex_noninferior` synthesis requires the parent's complete `nine_year_low_UACR_complementarity_supported` conjunction as well as this new supportive label.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive results

Let m=1000/466.

`recorded_sex_noninferiority_supportive` requires all gates and, for both s in {F,M}, point Delta_s>-m and the simultaneous one-sided 95% lower limit L_s>-m. Because observed point differences are multiples of m, this means neither sex has an observed net whole-event decrement and uncertainty excludes a decrement of one or more events.

`recorded_sex_noninferiority_adverse_female` requires all gates and the simultaneous one-sided 95% upper limit U_F<-m. The analogous rule defines `..._adverse_male`; emit both if both pass. This is evidence that AG1 loses more than one female- or male-tagged low-UACR N18 event relative to G1 on the 466-slot scale. It does not show that G1 improves health or that cystatin C lacks value.

`recorded_sex_noninferiority_inconclusive_unresolved` applies otherwise, including sparse selected events/swaps, wide or boundary-touching intervals, point deficit without a conclusive upper limit, zero variance, failed integrity/decomposition, or noncomputability. It is not equivalence, fairness, or evidence of no sex difference.

Always emit the unchanged parent biochemical, five-year, nine-year, A1, and complementarity labels plus the independent sex label. Then emit exactly one synthesis:

- `low_UACR_supported_and_recorded_sex_noninferior` only if parent `nine_year_low_UACR_complementarity_supported` and sex noninferiority are both supportive;
- `low_UACR_supported_but_recorded_sex_adverse` if the parent conjunction supports and either sex is adverse, naming the group and net-event direction;
- `low_UACR_supported_but_recorded_sex_unresolved` if the parent conjunction supports and the sex label is inconclusive;
- `recorded_sex_layer_not_rescuing_parent` for every other parent state, while retaining the independent sex label.

A sex-adverse result qualifies generalization but does not erase a correctly computed pooled parent result. A sex-supportive result cannot rescue any adverse or inconclusive parent biochemical, five-year, nine-year, or A1 component.

## Interpretation limits and stronger evidence required

Support means only that, within this frozen retrospective list experiment, neither recorded-sex contribution is inferior to G1 by a net whole low-UACR inpatient-N18 event under the declared simultaneous margin. Adverse evidence identifies a recorded-sex-specific list-yield deficit that should block unqualified generalization of the unchanged rule. Inconclusive evidence leaves that qualification unresolved. None proves sex/gender fairness, nondiscrimination, biological mechanism, individual benefit, causal testing effect, persistent A1 or CKD, complete outcome capture, cost-effectiveness, or transportability.

True clinical and equity claims require clinically timed repeated eGFR/UACR, measured GFR, outpatient/primary-care and kidney-replacement outcomes, individual censoring/emigration, chart/nephrologist adjudication, gender identity and broader sex representation, ethnicity/socioeconomic intersectional analyses, access/acceptability/harms/utility data, external validation, expert nephrology and equity review, and a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study with patient-centered outcomes.

Alternatives were rejected prospectively: recurrent N18 is too sparse (the prior all-row audit found only 6 events); N18-first is chiefly an administrative coding-order qualification; a repeat-UACR endpoint lacks specimen timing and is return-to-assessment selected; and cost/resource exchange cannot be identified. These cannot repair the parent with the available snapshot without sparsity, post hoc selection, or a changed estimand.

- promoting descriptive H to a confirmatory interaction/fairness test;
- calling sparse, wide, zero-variance, boundary-equal, or integrity-failed results adverse/equivalent;
