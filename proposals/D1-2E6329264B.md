# Generation-B — Method comparison & strongest rival (MIMIC episode 46)

Status: **methods/alternatives note only. NO cohort rows, prevalence, power or RD/Delta were computed.**
All numbers below are either (a) COMPUTED itemid/table presence probes (see `binding-verification.md`) or

Frozen question (inherited, unchanged): after a culture/susceptibility result returns at landmark `t_R`,
does early antimicrobial de-escalation vs continued broad-spectrum coverage show a non-harmful adjusted
28-day-mortality risk difference (RD) overall and in the SOFA-trajectory stratum, with interaction
`Delta = RD(non-improving) - RD(improving)`?

Estimand and decision bands are reused from the parent (`[prior hypothesis]`) without change.

## (a) BASELINE B0 — interpretable covariate-ladder PS-adjusted model (pre-`t_R` only)

**Scientific role:** transparent, low-variance "current standard" estimate of the adjusted RD and Delta that a
clinician or guideline panel could act on, built only from information a real decision-maker has *at* `t_R`.

**Design**
- Population: adults ≥18, first `icu/icustays.stay_id` per `hosp/patients.subject_id`, non-null `hadm_id`,
  qualifying microbiology result, empiric broad-spectrum exposure pre-`t_R`.
- Landmark `t_R` = earliest qualifying `hosp/microbiologyevents.storetime` (organism identified `org_name`
  non-null OR `interpretation` ∈ {S,I,R}) with `storetime` in `[icustays.intime, icustays.intime + 72h]`.
- Exposure E=1 (de-escalate): total antimicrobial spectrum-breadth score decreases ≥1 within `(t_R, t_R+48h]`
  and no new broad-spectrum agent added; E=0: score unchanged/increased. Reconstruct ongoing coverage from
  `prescriptions.starttime/stoptime`, cross-checked in `emar.medication` / `pharmacy.medication`.
- Modifier M: pre-landmark partial-SOFA trajectory, dichotomized improving vs non-improving, from the change
  between `(t_R-48h, t_R-24h]` and `(t_R-24h, t_R]`.
- Outcome Y: 28-day all-cause mortality from `t_R` (`admissions.deathtime` else `patients.dod` with the
  `anchor_year`/`anchor_age` offset).

**Model**
- Covariate ladder M0 (age/anchor offset, `gender`, `admission_type`) → M1 (spec_type_desc, organism/gram,
  baseline SOFA) → M2 (trajectory M, spectrum-breadth at `t_R`, vasopressor use, monitoring intensity).
  **All covariates strictly pre-`t_R`** — this is the leakage-control guarantee B0 makes.
- Propensity score: logistic PS for E on M2; RD via IPW (and PS-as-covariate as a sensitivity). Logistic
  (binary Y) and Cox/time-to-28-day both reported.
- RD with bootstrap 95% CI (resample by `subject_id`); Delta from stratum-specific RDs (and an E×M product
  term as a check).

**What B0 gives up (the honest limitation that motivates A1):** B0 classifies treatment by a *time-fixed*
exposure assessed over `(t_R, t_R+48h]` while the outcome clock starts at `t_R`. Any patient who dies before
being de-escalated is coded E=0, and E=1 requires surviving long enough to be narrowed, so B0 can absorb
**immortal-time** and **confounding-by-indication** structure that the rival H2 is entirely about.

## (b) SUBSTANTIVE ALTERNATIVE A1 — target-trial emulation with time-varying confounding control

**Scientific role:** the method that can *reveal what B0 loses* — immortal-time bias and time-varying
confounding affected by prior treatment between `t_R` and the 28-day outcome.

**Why B0 cannot represent this:** the two dominant post-landmark mechanisms are
1. **Immortal time / guarantee-time bias.** Defining E over the 48-h window while starting follow-up at `t_R`
   guarantees survival during the window for E=1, biasing B0 toward apparent protection (H1). A fixed
   de-escalation-time stratification cannot remove it.
2. **Time-varying confounding affected by prior treatment.** After `t_R`, new cultures, evolving organ
   function, and new antibiotic starts jointly shape whether a patient is de-escalated *and* the mortality
   risk. Conditioning on those post-landmark variables inside a standard regression (e.g. an "evolving SOFA"
   adjustment) opens collider/over-adjustment paths; B0's pre-`t_R`-only ladder avoids that path but
   therefore cannot adjust at all for the evolving post-landmark process.

**Design (clone-censor-weight TTE, with IPTW-AIPW and g-computation as complementary implementations)**
- Target trial: eligibility at `t_R`; strategies (a) de-escalate within 48 h of `t_R` and remain narrowed,
  (b) continue broad-spectrum through 48 h; time zero `t_R`; follow-up 28 days; outcomes as in B0.
- **Sequential cloning** at each time step (e.g. every 6–12 h) of still-eligible patients; **censor** at
  deviation from the assigned strategy; **weight** by inverse probability of (i) remaining uncensored and
  (ii) treatment received, using **pre-`t_R` covariates plus the evolving post-landmark covariates as
  weight inputs, never as plain regression adjusters.**
- Time-varying inputs for weights: daily SOFA components (MAP 220052/220181, GCS 220739/223900/223901,
  RASS 228096, urine 226559/226560/226561/226563, dialysate 224154 from `chartevents`/`outputevents`; labs
  creatinine 50912, lactate 50813, platelets 51265, WBC 51301, bilirubin 50885 from `labevents`), vasopressor
  status (`inputevents`), and new culture results (`microbiologyevents`).
- Estimators reported side-by-side: (i) clone-censor-weight / MSM (IPTW), (ii) AIPW augmentation,
  (iii) g-computation via the iterated conditional (g-)formula on the same time grid.
- Outputs: marginal adjusted RD (overall) and `Delta` by the pre-`t_R` trajectory stratum, each with
  bootstrap 95% CI. Diagnostic: weight truncation/ESS, adherence over time.

**Pre-registered interpretation of B0 vs A1 (this is the scientific comparison, not a bake-off):**
- If A1's RD is materially **less protective than B0's**, the B0 "safety" is at least partly an immortal-time
  / time-varying-confounding artifact → supports H2.
- If A1 and B0 agree within CI, the pre-`t_R` snapshot is adequate *for this question* and B0 remains a valid,
  interpretable primary — a genuinely informative, publishable negative methodological result.

## (c) SECOND ALTERNATIVE A2 — latent pre-landmark organ-trajectory (state-space / growth-mixture) — **DEFERRED**

**What A2 would reveal that B0 and A1 cannot:** B0 compresses the entire pre-landmark course into one
dichotomous change (M), discarding shape and misclassifying near-threshold patients; A1 uses the *measured*
post-landmark SOFA at face value, so its weights inherit the same measurement error and charting-missingness
that MAR/ICAR assumptions only partially fix. A latent-trajectory model (discrete latent-class growth
mixture, or a linear-Gaussian state-space / shared-parameter model coupling a latent organ-dysfunction state
to the hazard) would separate a *true latent* trajectory from charting noise and define a data-driven
"non-improving" phenotype that is more transportable and less sensitive to the arbitrary M cutoff.

**Fitting target:** joint model for pre-landmark repeated SOFA components/vitals/labs; latent class or latent
state → modifier assignment; secondary extension to a shared-parameter survival model.

**Deferral reason (why not first):** with only two pre-landmark observational windows feeding the modifier and
no guarantee of complete post-landmark repeated structural data, the latent state is weakly identified and
could overfit. The dominant threat named by the rival (H2) is time-varying confounding by indication, which
A1 targets directly. **Revisit trigger (recorded, not abandoned):** (1) A1 shows substantial residual
within-stratum heterogeneity, or (2) the dichotomous M is severely misclassified (e.g. relative to a
latent-class assignment). Only then does A2 earn its added complexity.

## (d) STRONGEST RIVAL to the safety claim — confounding by indication / care-trajectory (H2)

**Rival:** clinicians de-escalate the patients they judge to be *already improving*. The apparent "safety"
of de-escalation is then created by prognosis, not by the narrowing decision; and harm may be concentrated in
patients who are **not** improving, in whom narrowing can delay adequate therapy.

**Observation that separates the leading claim (H1) from H2:**
- **Interaction sign.** H1 predicts `Delta ≈ 0` (non-improving not worse than improving). H2 predicts
  `Delta > 0` (harm appears only/chiefly in the non-improving stratum).
- **Negative-control exposure.** Discontinuation of a *non-antimicrobial chronic* medication at the same
  landmark, analysed identically. H2 predicts this also looks "protective" (a marker of care trajectory);
  H1 does not.
- **Time-varying adjustment.** If the protective average RD in B0 **attenuates toward or past the null**
  under A1's proper time-varying/censoring control, H2 gains support over H1.

**Exact estimates that would falsify the safety claim (H1):**
- Overall `RD ≥ +0.03` with 95% CI lower bound `> 0` (de-escalation associated with higher adjusted 28-day
  mortality), **OR**
- `Delta = RD(non-improving) - RD(improving) ≥ +0.03` (safety does not hold in the stratum that matters), **OR**
- negative-control-exposure `RD ≤ -0.02` (an implausibly "protective" control → residual confounding).

## (e) Prespecified supportive / adverse / inconclusive bands (reused from parent; agreed)

- **Supportive (non-harmful):** `RD ≤ +0.02` AND RD upper 95% CI `≤ +0.05` AND `Delta ≤ +0.02` AND
  negative-control-exposure `RD ≤ +0.01`.
- **Adverse (harm):** `RD ≥ +0.03` with 95% CI lower bound `> 0` **OR** `Delta ≥ +0.03`.
- **Inconclusive:** otherwise (including imprecise estimates whose CIs cross both thresholds, or sparse
  strata). Declared a *genuine* outcome, not a failure.

B0 and A1 are each classified against these bands; a **discordant classification between B0 and A1 is itself
the informative result** and is reported as such (supports the immortal-time/confounding-artifact rival).
