## 1. Unresolved question, opening, clinical importance

After liver-directed therapy (TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, follow-up repeats serum AFP,
albumin and bilirubin. The still-open question in this branch: does the *within-patient AFP
trajectory* add prognostic information beyond **baseline AFP** and beyond **liver-reserve (ALBI)
kinetics measured in the SAME draws**, for time to radiologic progression? The parent's hardest
objections were (a) outcome circularity (predicting a retreatment decision from the labs that drive
it) and (b) detection-timing / informative-observation bias (AFP alone can prompt an earlier scan).

**The unresolved objection this child attacks (the advance):** the branch's own primary endpoint is
a *lexical progression flag pooled across all examination reports*. A bounded probe this episode
(2026-10-13) shows that pool is **ascertainment-heterogeneous and mixing different clinical events**:

- Only ~19,871 of 300,000 sampled `examinations` rows are the abdominal liver study
  `上腹部平扫+增强+DWI+MRCP`; the same pool contains `胸部平扫` 20,506 and `胸部正位` 32,949
  (non-hepatic), so a flag over all rows implicates scans of unrelated anatomy.
- In 检查诊断, the term `转移` (metastasis / "metastatic") is by far the most common descriptor
  (20,762), exceeding local-tumour descriptors `增大` 9,108, `复发` 4,026, `进展` 2,921, `新发` 618,
  `增多` 1,089. A composite that ORs these conflates **local tumour progression** with **metastatic
  spread**, which are distinct events with different prognostic meaning.

This is a scientific estimand defect, not formatting: if the composite event is dominated by
metastasis/noise, the AFP-trajectory contrast is attenuated and misattributed, so neither a positive
nor a null result can be trusted. The advance is a **prespecified, modality-restricted endpoint** with
local progression and metastasis separated as *competing* events, plus a modality-stratified
robustness requirement. This is a different result than the parent could produce and directly
changes interpretation.

**Clinical importance:** if AFP kinetics carry local-progression information independent of same-draw
liver reserve, that supports AFP-informed re-imaging / treatment-escalation timing after LDT. If the
signal exists only for a metastasis-inclusive composite, the correct reading is that AFP is tracking
systemic spread, and the monitoring and imaging policy differs. A clean null (liver reserve alone)
argues for de-emphasising routine AFP-only trajectory monitoring. These are distinct decisions.

## 4. Method comparison (recorded before selection)

- **M0 baseline (simple):** landmark Cox on baseline AFP + baseline ALBI grade + age/sex/treatment
  type + surveillance (draw/diagnostic) intensity. Level-based prognosis only; cannot separate a
  rising trajectory from a high-but-flat one, nor AFP from liver reserve.
- **M4 substantive (learned/mechanistic alternative):** joint linear-mixed-effects submodels for
  log AFP and for ALBI fit on (index, t*], with empirical-Bayes slopes entered into the
  landmark/time-dependent Cox. Recovers **change vs level** and **AFP vs same-draw liver reserve** —
  exactly the information M0 loses; the increment (delta C-index, bootstrap CI) is reported vs M0.
- **Deferred alternative (not chosen now):** full shared-parameter joint longitudinal-survival model
  with a latent link (as in [K2]); deferred to keep the two-stage estimator's failure modes
  transparent. Evidence that would justify revisiting it: a substantial AFP-slope x ALBI-slope
  interaction or interval-censored progression timing that the two-stage estimator cannot absorb.
- **Also deferred:** a competing-risks subdistribution model (Fine-Gray) for metastasis vs local
  progression as a formal machinery upgrade; the primary keeps cause-specific hazard + a
  pre-declared composite sensitivity for interpretability, with Fine-Gray as the named upgrade once
  event counts are known.

## 5. Analysis, falsification, and what each result would mean

Primary model: landmark Cox at t* for time to first **local-progression** event, exposure =
mixed-model log-AFP slope, adjusted for same-draw ALBI slope, baseline AFP, baseline ALBI, age/sex,
treatment type, and draw/diagnostic intensity. Time-dependent Cox on the same exposure as a required
second estimator (landmark vs time-dependent can disagree, [K3]). Secondary: metastasis competing
event and retreatment concordance.

- **S1 (supportive):** local-progression AFP-slope HR CI excludes 1 (HR>1) AND holds in the
  time-dependent Cox AND holds in BOTH MRI and CT modality strata AND delta-C-index over M0 is
  positive with bootstrap CI excluding 0. Reading: AFP kinetics carry local-progression information
  not explained by liver reserve or scan-triggering.
- **S2 (adverse):** association present only for the metastasis-inclusive composite, OR only in one
  modality stratum, OR vanishes in the time-dependent Cox, OR only the ALBI slope is significant.
  Reading: the AFP signal is detection timing / systemic-spread / modality artefact, not local
  tumour activity.
- **S3 (falsification/null):** both slope HR CIs include 1 AND delta-C-index bootstrap CI within
  +/-0.01 of M0. Reading: trajectories add nothing over baseline levels. A wide-CI S3 is
  **inconclusive**, not adverse — event rate is the solver's first feasibility output.
- **Inconclusive/fallback:** if the restricted-endpoint event count is too small or the lexical
  precision/recall audit fails its pre-set agreement floor, fall back to the retreatment proxy and
  report the analysis as inconclusive for radiologic progression.

## 6. Rivals, limits, unascertainable evidence

Rivals each mapped to a distinct test: baseline reserve (adjust baseline AFP/ALBI); surveillance
intensity (adjust draw/diagnostic counts); regression-to-the-mean (two-draw vs three-draw slope);
measurement/detection timing (E_early vs E_late stratum inherited from the parent; time-dependent
Cox); **endpoint heterogeneity (this child's modality + descriptor restriction)**.

Honest limits: the progression descriptor is a LEXICAL extraction from report text and remains
UNVALIDATED (frozen metadata validates only a cirrhosis lexical detector), so a pre-registered manual
precision/recall audit, an agreement floor and an explicit fallback are mandatory; mortality and
mRECIST-based PFS are UNASCERTAINABLE here -> NO survival claim; single institution; lab units absent;
**NO outcomes are computed in discovery**. Clinical adjudication of progression reports (and any
causal claim) require expert review or another study; the automatic pipeline can only establish
computation and internal consistency.
