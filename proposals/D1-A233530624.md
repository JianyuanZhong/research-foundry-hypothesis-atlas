# Procedure-context robustness of early hepatic reserve after first TACE

## Unresolved question and hypothesis

After a first qualifying TACE for HCC, does the early trajectory of routine hepatic-reserve assays add prognostic information for a new local-record hepatic-decompensation encounter beyond pre-TACE state/history, the index procedure bundle, and the care process that determines post-TACE testing?

The falsifiable primary hypothesis is that an adverse trajectory (albumin or platelets decreasing and/or total bilirubin or INR increasing from T0-14:-1 to T0+1:+7) improves held-out proper scoring and has an adverse-direction association with a new broad coded decompensation record on T0+8:+90 after adjustment for pre-TACE observability, index procedure context, and post-TACE testing/follow-up. The signal must remain directionally similar in severe-term, repeat-confirmed, and care-concordant endpoint tiers and under missing-post-assay sensitivity.

This is a prognostic-information hypothesis about a local coded endpoint. It is not a causal effect of TACE, a claim that the record represents adjudicated liver failure, a mortality estimate, or a treatment/monitoring threshold. The substantive advance over the parent is a prespecified test of whether the trajectory contributes information after the procedure bundle is represented, rather than allowing procedure complexity or repeat intervention to stand in for reserve.

## What is supported and what remains unresolved

The source supports dated procedure starts/ends, encounter admission/discharge times, repeated dated laboratory results, medications, and encounter-linked diagnosis labels. The source audit found 338,040 procedure rows across 43,062 patients and many TACE-related labels, including exact TACE, 经导管肝动脉化疗栓塞术（TACE）, 肝动脉化疗栓塞, 动脉化疗栓塞, and combined angiography/embolization variants. Feasibility and event support remain compiler outputs subject to no-go gates.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is only that a time-safe, patient-held-out comparison can be computed from these local records. It does not establish that the trajectory is a biological reserve state, that TACE caused it, or that a coded diagnosis is clinically adjudicated decompensation. The unresolved claim tested here is incremental prognostic information conditional on measured index procedure context and observation.

## Population, time origin, and exact bindings

Use discovery buckets 0-79 only, assigned by SHA-256(ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1 + NUL + hcc + NUL + 患者主索引) mod 100. Split, fit, and bootstrap by 患者主索引; buckets 80-99 are inaccessible and are not external validation.

Read-only source files and tables:

- [internal dataset path] -> procedures, columns 患者主索引, 就诊号, 手术, 开始时间, 结束时间, 手术来源.
- [internal dataset path] -> encounters, columns 患者主索引, 就诊号, 年龄, 性别, 就诊时间, 入院时间, 出院时间, 就诊科室.
- [internal dataset path] -> diagnoses, columns 患者主索引, 就诊号, 诊断名称, 诊断类型.
- [internal dataset path] -> labs, columns 患者主索引, 就诊号, 检验, 定量结果, 定性结果, 标本类型, 检验时间.
- [internal dataset path] -> medications, columns 患者主索引, 就诊号, 用药, 单次用药剂量, 单次用药剂量单位, 频次, 开始时间, 结束时间, 用药方式, 药品类型.

Join every one-to-many table to encounters only on (患者主索引, 就诊号) after table-level aggregation. Diagnoses have no native time field and use linked encounter 入院时间 only as a record-time proxy. Labs use 检验时间; procedures use 开始时间/结束时间; medications use 开始时间/结束时间. The source snapshot has local encounter dates with exact dates withheld, so all time is relative within patient.

A qualifying TACE row has nonmissing 开始时间 and 手术 equal case-insensitively to TACE or containing one of 化疗栓塞, 肝动脉化疗栓塞, or 动脉化疗栓塞. Deduplicate same-patient/same-encounter qualifying rows and define T0 as the earliest qualifying start time for each patient. At T0 require age >=18 and an HCC diagnosis containing 肝细胞癌 or 肝癌 on the T0 encounter or on a linked encounter with 入院时间 in [T0-180,T0). Exclude broad decompensation terms 腹水, 肝衰竭, 肝功能衰竭, 失代偿, 肝性脑病, 肝昏迷 on linked encounters in [T0-180,T0), and exclude a procedure containing 切除, 消融, 射频, or 微波 with 开始时间 in [T0-7,T0+7]. These are interval exclusions, not lifetime exclusions.

Require at least one numeric pre-TACE primary assay, but never require a post-TACE assay: exact lab names are 白蛋白, 总胆红素, 国际标准化比值, and 血小板计数; parse only numeric 定量结果, with no unit conversion or cross-assay pooling. Pre-window is [T0-14,T0), post-window is (T0,T0+7]; same-time records are excluded. Retain per-assay median, count, median relative time, parse failures, and missingness. Context assays 肌酐, 钠, 甲胎蛋白 are descriptive/sensitivity inputs only.

## Procedure-context exposure and outcomes

At T0, derive an index procedure-context vector using only procedures rows for the T0 patient/encounter with nonmissing 开始时间 in [T0,T0+24 hours]. This is context, not a treatment dose or causal exposure. Prespecify total procedure-row count; distinct procedure-label count; indicators/counts for 造影, 插管, 导管, 栓塞, 化疗栓塞, TACE, 肝动脉, and 麻醉; and indicators for combined labels containing +. Also retain whether any additional qualifying embolization/TACE row begins in (T0,T0+7]. The latter is a post-index care-process sensitivity variable and must not be used as a pretreatment causal covariate. If fewer than 30% of target patients have usable index procedure context, context-adjusted analyses are secondary/inconclusive rather than imputed.

The primary broad outcome is the first diagnosis containing any broad decompensation term above on a linked encounter whose 入院时间 proxy lies in (T0+7,T0+90]. A negative requires no event through day 90 and a linked encounter with 入院时间 or 出院时间 >=T0+90. No-event/no-anchor is unknown, never a negative. Positive patients remain positive without a later anchor.

Refit with the same folds and no-go rules:
- severe-term: 肝衰竭, 肝功能衰竭, 肝性脑病, 肝昏迷; report 腹水 and 失代偿 separately;
- repeat-confirmed: broad terms on at least two distinct encounter IDs separated by >=1 day in day 8-90;
- care-concordant: broad event plus a medication containing 螺内酯, 呋塞米, 托拉塞米, 乳果糖, or 利福昔明, with medication 开始时间 on the event encounter or a patient-linked encounter from event day through +7 days. This is contextual corroboration only; administration, indication, and dose are not inferred;
- diagnosis-type and admission-versus-discharge proxy strata.

Unknown outcomes are excluded from the primary binary score only with a train-fold follow-up model and pattern-mixture sensitivity. Patients without a post assay remain in the target and are represented by missingness/latent prediction, never by a post-test negative.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Use deterministic patient-level 60/20/20 train/tune/test folds within discovery buckets.

B0 is a current-state/history and procedure-context penalized logistic model: pre-window assay medians, assay counts/timing/missingness, age, sex, prior encounter count, prior HCC/decompensation history, diagnosis-type stratum, index department, index procedure-context vector, and train-fold post-assay/follow-up propensity features. B1 adds post-window assay availability/count/timing and follow-up/process features. B1 is the primary observation-aware baseline.

A1 is a constrained irregular longitudinal reserve model fitted only on train/tune patients and actual lab times. Train-fold robust scaling, assay-specific residual variance, latent pre/post states, and empirical-Bayes uncertainty are fitted without test data. Albumin and platelets load positively; bilirubin and INR negatively; albumin loading is fixed to +1 for scale. Missing post assays receive fold-safe latent prediction from pre-state and the fitted phase transition. A1 adds reserve change, posterior uncertainty, and cross-assay discordance to B1.

A2 is a signed train-fold robust change score with the same adverse signs, added to B1. A2 is a transparent mechanistic comparator: if A2 matches A1, complex representation is not scientifically necessary; if A1 adds reproducibly beyond A2, irregular timing, measurement uncertainty, and discordance carry information. Both alternatives use the same cohort, folds, outcome labels, and held-out predictions.

The three demonstrations are dispositions, not claims of reproduction. Delphi/natural-history motivates a bounded structured-sequence adaptation but its reported UKB/Danish setting and full transport are unavailable; it is represented here only by A1 dated state transition. ALADYNOULLI motivates latent longitudinal structure, but genetic inputs and an unqualified reproduction are unavailable; A1 is explicitly EHR-only. Oncoformer cannot be reproduced because the main paper/complete STAR Methods and imaging modality are unavailable; no images exist in HCC, so a multimodal model is deferred. A lab-only adaptation is covered by A1/A2 only as a measurement question, not as reproduction.

## Estimands, uncertainty, and scientific deliverable

Primary estimands are the adverse-IQR A1 reserve-change odds ratio and paired held-out log-loss/Brier differences A1-B1 and A2-B1. Report calibration intercept/slope and reliability; AUROC/AUPRC are descriptive. Use 200 patient-level bootstrap replicates when event support permits, refitting train-fold transforms/weights as feasible. Report unweighted and stabilized inverse-probability-of-follow-up results, weight truncation, positivity, and effective sample size. Use ten fold-safe latent/pattern-mixture draws or equivalent posterior prediction for missing post assays.

The newly fitted deliverable must include B0/B1/A1/A2 parameters and transformations; procedure-context support and missingness; latent loadings/residuals/uncertainty; every target patient's held-out prediction; bootstrap intervals; endpoint-tier and diagnosis-time strata; follow-up and post-assay weights; pattern-mixture shifts of +/-1 train-fold robust SD; permutation and process-only controls; and simulation recovery of latent sign/scale under the observed irregular sampling pattern. A CPU job with 2 CPUs, 8 GB RAM, and <=1,800 seconds after streaming aggregation is the target; no GPU is scientifically required.

## Falsification, interpretation, and limits

Run process-only B1 versus B0; A1/B1 with procedure context removed; permutation of post-lab values across patients preserving assay, phase, count, and procedure context; complete-post-assay versus pre-assay target comparison; unweighted versus follow-up-weighted and pattern-mixture results; any-primary versus strict all-four coverage; severe/repeat/care-concordant tiers; admission versus discharge proxy; index-procedure-context support audit; and simulation recovery.

Confirmatory support requires >=30 broad events overall, >=8 test events, >=40 target patients with a numeric post-primary assay, >=5 events among post-observed patients, usable procedure context in >=30% of the target, acceptable weight positivity/ESS, successful A1 recovery, adverse-direction A1 association, and improvement in both proper scores over B1 without material calibration harm. The gain must not be reproduced by process-only or permuted controls, and must not materially reverse under endpoint or +/-1-SD missingness checks. These are gates for a supportive interpretation, not evidence of biological causation.

Supportive means incremental prognostic information for this locally coded outcome conditional on measured procedure context and observation assumptions. Adverse means null/reversal, calibration or proper-score harm, process-only/permutation replication, a large complete-case discrepancy, or loss on specificity tiers. Inconclusive means sparse outcomes, unavailable procedure context, failed latent recovery, poor positivity, wide intervals, or missingness bounds spanning materially different conclusions. A supportive result cannot recommend TACE selection, monitoring, or treatment.

Computationally checkable claims include parsing, cohort flow, temporal joins, procedure-context construction, fold isolation, fitting, recovery, held-out scores, calibration, weights, endpoint tiers, and falsification outputs. Clinical adjudication is required to establish true hepatic failure, medication indication/administration, disease severity, death capture, and whether any score improves care. Causal treatment effects, unmeasured tumor burden/dose confounding, external transport, clinical utility, and prospective benefit require expert review, richer imaging/dose data, an independent cohort, or a prospective study.
