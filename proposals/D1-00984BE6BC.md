# Fail-closed temporal negative-control and source-isolation successor for the frozen eICU respiratory-label design

## Lineage, frozen decisions, and substantive advance

This is a targeted, compiler-ready child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the Lead-frozen scientific schema exactly; it does not replace the cohort, exposure, landmarks, decision time, endpoint, estimand, or noncausal boundary.

The frozen population is exactly 540 person-landmark rows from 443 distinct first qualifying ICU stays/persons in 74 hospitals. Retain the first qualifying stay per `uniquepid`; retain all five landmarks `L ∈ {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes and repeated landmarks within that stay; cluster every row-level analysis by `patientunitstayid`. Select the first exact target label in `[L,L+360]`, with `D = cplitemoffset` and an assertion that `D == decision_offset`. The only arms are the literal strings `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation` (`evaluation`) and `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial` (`no_trial`).

The fixed clinical windows remain `B=(D-720,D]`, `E=(D,D+720]`, and `T=(D+720,D+2160]`. The inherited parser uses only FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate, with the frozen aliases, finite/bounded parsing, FiO2 fraction/percentage conversion, duplicate and same-time conflict rules, minimum two observations at distinct offsets separated by at least 30 minutes per component, and at least three of four components per state. Thresholds remain FiO2 `0.10` fraction, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`.

The ordered five-state all-row endpoint is unchanged:

`K ∈ {competing_exit, classifiable-de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.

Finite `unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160` has precedence and death, discharge, transfer, and truncation remain visible competing exits. Invalid or unavailable exit time is a separate administrative status and never means alive. Primary displays remain fixed-arm-denominator `p_k(a)` and `Δ_k = p_k(evaluation)-p_k(no_trial)`; conditional classifiable `q_c(a)` and `δ_c` remain secondary. These are associations with literal structured-chart constructs, not treatment effects, bedside truth, or causal effects.

The strongest evidence-supported claim before execution is only that the two strings are sparse, heterogeneous structured documentation and that associations may be generated by pre-D state, source opportunity, hospital workflow, entry timing, endpoint-source dependence, exits, missingness, or repeated documentation. No available evidence establishes an SBT, extubation evaluation, readiness, clinician intent, delivered ventilator setting, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The unresolved hypothesis remains narrow and falsifiable: **after pre-D state and value-blind opportunity adjustment, the exact current label has a temporally directional, cross-hospital incremental association with the locked post-D `K` trajectory that survives independent source isolation and explicit availability/support audits, while failing outcome-blind pre-D and lagged-label negative controls.** A separate, bounded question is whether this association can improve fixed-capacity manual chart-review allocation. That is a review-queue exercise, not patient utility or treatment guidance.

The substantive repair over both parents is an auditable registry and fail-closed execution contract. It prevents a negative control from accidentally sharing target-window measurements, prevents source-isolation views from silently sharing values or row identifiers, makes every kind of missingness visible rather than a hidden complete-case filter, and separates feature support from outcome availability and transport support. It also makes an apparently favorable primary increment automatically non-interpretable when a temporal placebo, source audit, overlap audit, or uncertainty gate fails. No empirical result is asserted.

## Locked feature blocks and two-clock leakage contract

Before any arm-stratified output, emit `pre_D_baseline.json`, `post_D_endpoint.json`, and `post_D_diagnostic.json`. Every primary predictor must use clinical time `< D`, except the exact current label in M1. `respchartentryoffset` may appear only in value-blind pre-D entry-lag opportunity summaries or diagnostics; it may never substitute for `respchartoffset` in inclusion, parsing, state, endpoint, or placebo timing. No source row may be selected by entry time and then treated as clinically observed without recording both clocks.

Declare a machine-readable one-to-one feature dictionary with mutually exclusive blocks:

* `S`: finite pre-D value-derived respiratory proxies, including locked B summaries/trends, valid pre-D airway facts from `respiratoryCare`, and finite pre-D `vitalPeriodic.sao2` and `respiration` summaries. These are chart measurements, not bedside diagnoses.
* `O`: value-blind opportunity and missingness features, including row counts, distinct clinical timestamps, distinct labels, spacing/window availability, parser/source-presence flags, entry-lag summaries, respiratory-care row/interval counts, vital row/timestamp counts and spacing, and treatment row counts.
* `C`: fixed design covariates, limited to landmark, age category, and explicitly inherited pre-D covariates.

A value, label, identifier, target status, endpoint row ID, post-D field, or care-plan field cannot be in a predictor block. Ambiguous/nonfinite/unvalidated text is O or excluded, never S. The exact current label belongs to no feature block. Assert every feature has one and only one block and that no feature name or source column appears in two blocks.

Fit deterministic leave-one-hospital-out folds, assigning all landmarks of a stay to one fold and never using hospital ID as a predictor. Use fixed low-dimensional multinomial models `M_C`, `M_O=C+O`, `M_S=C+S`, `M_0=C+S+O`, and `M_1=M_0+current exact label`. Fit imputation, scaling, regularization, calibration, feature maps, bins, support, and cutpoints only in training hospitals. Cache one held-out prediction and its complete fold/feature provenance per row.

Assert: no post-D source row; no endpoint row identifier in a predictor; no source value from an excluded family; no care-plan field; and no feature computed from a union/join that reintroduces an excluded value. Compare `ALL` versus `RC` for the locked `K` target and `ALL`/`VP` for the independent vital target; use `VP` versus `RC` as a provenance stress test, not as semantic validation. A gain that disappears when the label-adjacent source family is removed is source-dependent. Also emit a source-ablation table that holds folds, transformations, target masks, and row denominators fixed wherever applicable. If denominators differ because a view lacks opportunity, report the difference explicitly; never compare a view's apparent gain as though it were the same population.

## Auditable negative-control registry

Create `negative_control_registry.json` before fitting any model. Each entry must contain target name, anchor, source tables/columns, clinical-time inequalities, required opportunity, allowed predictor clock, forbidden fields, target status categories, cluster unit, overlap rule, expected interpretation, and exact output artifact. The registry contains the following frozen diagnostics.

### 1. Pre-D trajectory placebo

Define `K_pre` from an earlier transition, using the locked parser and respiratoryCharting clinical time in `P=(D-1440,D-720]` and B `(D-720,D]`, with respiratoryCare only as the inherited airway corroboration. If either required state is unsupported, assign `pre_unobservable`, never a negative. No post-D field, exit field, current label field, care-plan field, or future status may enter `K_pre`.

To prevent target leakage that would make this placebo tautological, its diagnostic models use a **pre-placebo feature clock**: eligible predictors are strictly before `P_start = D-1440` (plus fixed C and value-blind prior opportunity), not B or P values. The exact current label may enter M1 only as the registered exposure. This diagnostic model is named `M0_pre/M1_pre`; it does not silently reuse the primary M0 feature matrix. If a pre-P value is not in the frozen dictionary it is excluded from the primary model and may be listed only in the placebo manifest. The primary model is never altered by this placebo.

A reproducible label increment on `K_pre`, after its own support and availability gate, is adverse to the claim that the post-D increment is temporally specific residual-state information. It may indicate residual state, opportunity, selection, persistence, or leakage; it does not identify which. A null is only reassuring about temporal direction and cannot establish clinical meaning. Sparse or overlap-dominated `K_pre` is inconclusive, not a favorable null.

### 2. Value-blind pre-D opportunity placebo

Define `O_pre_shift` from respiratoryCharting rows in `P`, using only `respchartoffset`, row identity, and value-blind timestamp/spacing rules: at least two distinct clinical timestamps spanning at least 30 minutes; otherwise explicit `unobservable`. Do not use values, label semantics, care-plan rows, or post-D data. Its predictors must be strictly before `P_start` for the same anti-tautology reason, with a separate registered opportunity model. A label increment is adverse to a claim that the primary increment is not residual documentation process; a null is not clinical validation.

### 3. Future-anchor lagged-label placebo

Set `D_pl=D+720`, retain the original exact label as a historical exposure, and parse a newly registered trajectory in `(D_pl,D_pl+720]` and `(D_pl+720,D_pl+2160]` with the same parser, thresholds, exit precedence, and all-row statuses. Do not use any care-plan row after D, any future label, any placebo target, or any post-D endpoint value as a predictor. Predictors may use only the registered pre-`D_pl` source history; historical label recurrence is a separate diagnostic, never a primary feature.

Record `overlap_original_endpoint`, `overlap_placebo_endpoint`, `competing_exit_before_anchor`, `invalid_anchor`, `insufficient_opportunity`, and `placebo_unobservable` separately. A future-anchor increment is adverse to a contemporaneous interpretation and is reported only as persistence, evolving state, or site process evidence without selecting among them. Null is merely reassuring. If fewer than the registered support threshold of valid non-overlapping rows is available, the placebo is inconclusive rather than null.

### 4. Documentation pseudotargets and label persistence

Register post-D value-blind opportunity targets `O_E_RC`, `O_T_RC`, `O_E_VP`, and `O_T_VP` using only source row counts, distinct clinical timestamps, and spacing (at least two distinct timestamps; vital observations additionally require finite rows spanning at least five minutes). These are documentation-process targets, not outcomes. Record availability and overlap, and interpret a label gain as adverse to a clean opportunity-residualization claim.

Within each stay, audit exact-label recurrence, prior same-label timing, prior valid-label count, and current-versus-immediately-previous label. Compare the registered primary M1/M0 only in first-landmark-only, no-overlap, and recurrence strata. Historical labels never enter M1. A persistence-confined gain is adverse to temporal specificity, not evidence of a bedside action.

### 5. Conditional label-vector nulls

Retain both inherited null families. Under fixed seed `61061`, permute exact labels only within held-out hospital-by-landmark strata sharing training-derived S/O cells, preserving arm counts. Separately permute complete exact-label vectors among complete stays with identical label-independent ordered signatures over landmarks, S/O bins, and pre-D availability/status. Signatures contain no K, H, exit, post-D observability, placebo target, endpoint status, or care-plan field beyond the label being permuted.

For every arrangement assert unchanged stay IDs/signatures, vector lengths, arm counts, hospital membership, per-landmark/cell counts, and no stay splitting. Attempt at least 1,000 valid arrangements when feasible or enumerate all unique arrangements when fewer. Fewer than 20 valid global arrangements, fewer than five hospitals, lost supported cells, or any failed invariant is inconclusive. An observed increment typical of an exchangeable S/O conditional null is adverse to residual patient-state information; the null envelope is not a confidence interval and is never nested inside bootstrap replicates.

## Uncertainty, falsification, and fail-closed interpretation

Use fixed-seed `61061` stay-cluster bootstrap, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates. Resample complete first qualifying stays within hospital, carrying all landmarks, labels, predictors, K/H/placebo statuses and availability masks together; refit fold-local pipelines and recompute support, source views, transport, null-compatible summaries, and review metrics. Never row-bootstrap or nest permutations inside bootstrap. Fewer than 500 valid replicates makes uncertainty-dependent claims inconclusive.

The run is computationally invalid if any source/schema/catalog/hash/member check fails; frozen artifact is missing/divergent; join is duplicate or non-one-to-one; exact label, landmark, D, parser, threshold, unit, inequality, K precedence, or exit status is wrong; a post-D value leaks into a predictor; an entry clock replaces a clinical clock; feature blocks overlap; folds or stays leak; source masks are not reproducible; placebo target and predictor clocks overlap; availability masks are outcome-selected; null invariants fail; support or review gates are falsely claimed; or a narrative conclusion lacks artifact, denominator, hospital/cell support, and uncertainty linkage. Set `verification.status=invalid` and do not interpret estimates.

Supportive results establish only incremental held-out prediction of named structured K/vital constructs, conditional on exact provenance, all-row accounting, source-view persistence, negative-control nulls, support/transport gates, and uncertainty. Adverse results mean the exact label is not robust under this design or remains compatible with state, opportunity, site, exit, missingness, persistence, or source dependence. Inconclusive results identify failed feasibility, source availability, overlap, support, exchangeability, calibration, bootstrap, or verification. A positive pre-D, opportunity, lagged-label, persistence, conditional-null, or source-dependent result blocks the corresponding stronger interpretation. No result establishes bedside evaluation, extubation intent, readiness, delivered settings, treatment, benefit, harm, quality, or causality.

The release lacks raw waveforms, synchronized device/workflow logs, local interface dictionaries, validated bedside labels, adjudicated SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, post-ICU follow-up, and external prospective replication. `vitalPeriodic` is a five-minute summary source. Expert adjudication, workflow/device metadata, validated outcomes, and another study are required for stronger clinical conclusions.
