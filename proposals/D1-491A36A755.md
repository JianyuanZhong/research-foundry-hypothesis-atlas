## 0. Hypothesis vs. supported conclusion

Supported (NOT re-tested here as novel): SHR / "relative" (acute-on-chronic) glycemia is
*associated* with short-term mortality in MIMIC-IV and other ICU cohorts [K2]; HbA1c-derived
glycemic-gap terms can improve model fit while adding only modest DISCRIMINATION beyond
absolute glucose [K3]; lab-test order-presence/timing alone predicts survival, so
measurement/observation processes can manufacture an apparent effect [K1]. This is settled
descriptive regularity, not a mechanism.

Untested claim this experiment tries to FAIL (H-relative): at the same absolute admission
glucose `G_adm` and severity, the relative component `RG = log(G_adm) − log(Gc)` carries
incremental 28-day-mortality DISCRIMINATION that is (i) not attributable to `G_adm` and
(ii) not produced by same-stay HbA1c measurement selection / informative-observation or by
HbA1c measurement invalidity. Read as incremental prognostic information and separability —
not mechanism and not causation.

Strongest rival (H0): the RG increment is a measurement-selection / observation-intensity
artifact (who was tested, testing cadence) of the exact form demonstrated by [K1], rather
than a value-carrying physiological signal.

## 1. What this child adds (the two repairs)

The parent is mature but carries two documented, non-blocking gaps. This child converts both
into falsifiable, first-class design elements:

- **R1 — anchor-yield power gate (was never computed).** The HbA1c-independent chronic-
  glycemia anchor (`Gc_anchor`) population may be sparse; the informative-negative reading
  depends on adequate yield. This child makes it a PREDECLARED COMPUTABLE gate (§7) with a
  minimum and an explicit UNDERPOWERED→INCONCLUSIVE fallback, so a null is interpretable.
- **R2 — within-subject discordance falsifier (new).** Among subjects with ≥2 ICU stays, test
  whether the *within-patient* change in RG tracks the within-patient change in 28-day
  mortality risk. Confounding-by-indication / observation-intensity are between-patient
  phenomena; if the between-subject RG increment does NOT replicate within-subject, H0 is
  favoured. The parent's cross-sectional design cannot deliver this separation.

Primary estimand, population, temporal boundaries, outcomes and the locked split are
**inherited unchanged** from the parent.

- **G_adm** = first `hosp/labevents` glucose itemid **50931** (sens 50809, 52027, 52569) in
  `[t0−6h, t0+24h]` from `valuenum` (mg/dL).
- **Gc** = same-stay HbA1c itemid **50852** (sens 50854) in `[t0−24h, t0+24h]`
  (used only for the negative-control exposure arm).
- **Gc_anchor** (HbA1c-independent) = most recent glucose or HbA1c in an EARLIER `hadm_id`
  (by `subject_id`, `charttime < t0`, 365-day lookback) OR an outpatient `hosp/omr` value

- **Outcome**: 28-day all-cause mortality from `hosp/admissions.deathtime`; interval-censored
  at `deathtime`-null → `dischtime`.
- **Split**: locked earliest-70% / latest-30% by `intime` (date-shift = ordering proxy).

## 4. Predeclared artifact signature (negative control FIRST)

If the apparent independent RG effect is a measurement-selection/informative-observation
artifact [K1], we predeclare its signature BEFORE fitting:
- **S1 (label permutation).** Permute HbA1c-derived `Gc` across subjects (instrumentation and
  set of measured labs fixed); refit M1→M2. Under a genuine value signal the increment
  COLLAPSES to the equivalence margin; under selection it is driven by *who got measured*.
- **S2 (value-free negative-control exposure).** A measured-HbA1c / anchor-availability
  indicator alone (no value) should NOT reproduce the value-carrying RG increment beyond the
  equivalence band; if it does, H0 is favoured.
- **S3 (negative-control outcome).** A prespecified outcome not plausibly caused by glycemia
  (bounded composite) must show no RG association; a non-null RG→NCO association is
  predeclared as residual confounding/selection.
- **S4 (within-subject discordance, NEW).** Between- vs within-patient increment comparison
  (§1 R2).

## 5. Estimand and models

- **M0** = `G_adm` + severity + age/sex (+ admission_type). **M1** = M0 + `RG`. **M2** =
  M1 + measurement/care-intensity propensity (IPW or covariate). **M3** = negative controls.
- **Primary estimand**: ΔAUC(M0→M1) and ΔMcFadden-R²(M0→M1), each with 1000-bootstrap 95% CI,
  plus the adjusted RG coefficient. **Equivalence/non-inferiority margin**: ΔAUC ≤ 0.005 and
  RG coefficient CI inside ±0.05 per unit = a declared INFORMATIVE NEGATIVE.
- **Decisive contrast**: Δ(M1→M2) is the R-select discriminator; whether it survives M2 and S4.

## 6. Method comparison (recorded before selection)

- **B0** interpretable penalized-logistic (static severity) and **B1** (+ glicemia levels) —
  the simple baseline. B1 tests whether level alone explains any signal.
- **A1 (substantive alternative)** measurement-selection **joint model**: an explicit
  observation-process submodel (P(measured HbA1c | severity, care-unit, ordering) feeding the
  outcome model), or equivalently a shared-parameter/propensity model. A1 reveals whether the
  RG coefficient is identified once the testing process is explicitly modelled — information
  B1 loses because it conditions on the observed measurement (collider/selection) rather than
  modelling it.
- Inputs: same frozen tables. Target: 28-day mortality. Split: locked 70/30. Evaluation:
  held-out ΔAUC/ΔAUPRC, calibration (slope, Brier), 1000-bootstrap CIs; B0/B1 as contrasts.
  Resource: CPU-only, ~4c/16 GiB, labevents streamed once; A1 adds a small EM/variational
  outer loop. Revisitable with GPU only if A1 fails to converge.
- **Selected**: B0/B1 primary + A1 as the substantive alternative with an explicit revisit
  condition (deferred if the anchor gate is underpowered).

## 7. Predeclared falsification rules

- **R1 anchor gate (COMPUTABLE)**: declare minimum `N_anchor` (subjects with usable
  Gc_anchor) and `deaths_anchor`. If `N_anchor < min` or `deaths_anchor < min_events`, the
  study is UNDERPOWERED → **INCONCLUSIVE** (not adverse). NOT-COMPUTED at discovery.
- **Supportive (H-relative)**: ΔAUC(M0→M1) ≥ 0.005 with bootstrap CI lower bound > 0 AND the
  increment survives M2 (≥75% retained) AND S1 collapse ≥ margin AND S3 null AND S4
  within-subject increment has the same sign and ≥50% of the between-subject increment.
- **Adverse (H0)**: ΔAUC(M0→M1) ≤ 0.005 (equivalence) OR M1→M2 removes ≥50% of Δ OR the
  value-free control exposure reproduces the increment within the band OR S4 reverses/absorbs
  the within-subject increment.
- **Inconclusive**: wide CI spanning the margin, coverage/anchor gate fail, or R1 underpowered.
