## Exact availability and preference surface

For a full-roster configuration z, patient i has eligible whole units `E_i`, count `n_i`, and exposed count `m_i(r)` under binary mask r. Forced-zero units always have r=0. Define

`s_i^alpha(r)=1[n_i>0 and m_i(r)>=ceil(alpha*n_i)]`.

The feasible mask family is

`R_z(alpha,q)={r: sum_i s_i^alpha(r)>=ceil(q*N_z)}`.

Zero-unit patients remain in `N_z` and have s=0 even at alpha=0. Qualify masks on the full roster before anchored whole-patient deletion; after deleting patient j and all j rows/states/outcomes/units, retain every survivor mask bit, model, book, K and tie rule without requalification.

Enumerate exact ceiling-equivalence classes. Use `A={0} union {k/n_i: n_i>0,1<=k<=n_i}` and `Q={k/N_z:0<=k<=N_z}`, unioned across required states. Each class is represented with explicit open/closed rational endpoints so `ceil(alpha*n_i)` and `ceil(q*N)` are constant; never interpolate decimals. If `alpha' >= alpha` and `q' >= q`, then `R_z(alpha',q')` is a subset of `R_z(alpha,q)`. This set inclusion justifies monotonicity of universal minima, not monotonicity of any patient's score or outcome.

Independently define `r_max,z`: expose every HCC-exposable whole unit and no forced-zero/source-unavailable unit. It exists for every z even if no patient-summary cell such as (1,1) is feasible.

For every z and feasible cell compute exact top-K documentary positives `h_M`, event count E and roster N. Retain:

- comparator margins `100(h_G-h_C)/K` for C in {B,R,Gmask}, strictly greater than 5;
- random enrichment numerator `N*h_G-K*E`, strictly positive;
- `P_y(alpha,q)=min h_G/K` over every z and r in the cell;
- `D_C,y(alpha,q)` and `E_y(alpha,q)`, the corresponding attained minima.

For rational `p=a/b`, G beats no review exactly when `b*h_G>a*K`. Equality fails. A year-cell is closed when all three D minima exceed 5 and E is positive. Its strict support interval is `[0,P_y)`. The cross-year support set `F_both` contains the same feasible (alpha,q,p) only when both year-cells are closed and `p<min(P_2020,P_2021)`.

The threshold-independent hypothesis is: the r_max comparator/random gates pass separately in both years and `F_both` is nonempty. It is falsified by an attained r_max comparator/random witness or, after r_max passage, by exact proof that every feasible cell has a comparator/random failure or P=0. A failed feasible low-availability cell is a reported boundary witness, not by itself a global falsifier when another cell belongs to F_both.
