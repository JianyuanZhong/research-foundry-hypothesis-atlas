## Status and scope

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent remains controlling except for the exact replacements below. The defect repaired is that acute kidney failure (N17*) can satisfy the downstream clinical-relevance gate together with chronic kidney disease (N18*). Raw grip and frailty may predict acute hospitalization independently of occult chronic filtration impairment, so an N17-driven result cannot support the intended chronic-kidney interpretation.
