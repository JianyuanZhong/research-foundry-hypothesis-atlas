# Independent active-status and clinical-anchor layer for the frozen pulmonary discharge-handoff protocol

## Purpose and locked inheritance

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]` (with `[prior hypothesis]` retained as the immediately preceding protocol). It makes one substantive repair: the existing exact canonical-discharge match and matched-control contrast still cannot distinguish an active recommendation from one explicitly superseded before discharge, and its text agreement is not independently anchored to an identifiable clinical finding. The addition below supplies a blinded, source-bound active/superseded decomposition and an independent clinical-anchor audit.

Nothing in the frozen scientific schema changes. The following remain immutable: the first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject, all-opportunity pulmonary denominator; the reciprocal pre-discharge chronology; `D=admissions.dischtime`; the exact atom `g=(E, terminal curr_service, W)` and route; canonical DS selection; all-unit adverse precedence; source-only target/control freeze; matched controls; two-phase positive-probability whole-admission sampling; finite-population HT/Hájek inference; the additive ledger and one 15% reserve; and the strict silent-validation-only ceiling. The new labels are secondary post-allocation measurements. They cannot select, replace, relabel, or rescue the target, route, unit universe, control, sample, thresholds, or primary inherited estimand.

## Question, supported claim, and unresolved claim

The inherited experiment can test whether a frozen exact pulmonary atom is reproducibly represented in the canonical DS more often than an exact modality-and-interval matched non-target control. It cannot establish that the index recommendation was still active at discharge, that a later text did not supersede it, or that its object was independently clinically identifiable rather than a generic lexical pattern.

The added falsifiable question is: among frozen target units, how often do independent reviewers classify the recommendation as (i) anchored to a specific radiologic finding and (ii) active at `D`, rather than explicitly superseded or unresolved, and does exact DS preservation differ between these pre-specified states? A supportive result is a target-cell-specific documentation decomposition showing that exact DS preservation is concentrated in independently anchor-positive/active units, with low supersession misclassification and no route/control leakage. This remains a stored-text construct. It is not evidence that the finding is real, that follow-up is appropriate, that anyone saw the recommendation, or that care was delivered or safe.

## Independent panels and masked packets

Use three roster-disjoint panels: (A) an anchor panel, (S) an active/supersession panel, and the inherited H readers. Panels A and S are disjoint from phase 1, C/S route panels, H, codebook authors, and each other. Their codebooks, packet order, and access logs are sealed before labels. No panel sees phase-1 labels, route, target/control role, sampling stratum, H result, reader result, or favorable outcome.

### Panel A: independent clinical anchoring

Panel A receives only the frozen radiology source unit and its permitted `radiology_detail` fields, with provenance and timing but without canonical DS text, phase-1 labels, route, or later reports. It may use only a pre-registered finite codebook. It assigns exactly one of:

- `A+`: an explicit, patient-specific, radiologically identifiable object/cluster is stated in the unit, and the recommendation's modality and due interval are unambiguously linked to that object;
- `A-`: the unit is readable and contains an affirmative recommendation, but the object is generic, non-patient-specific, or not linked to the recommendation under the codebook; or
- `A_U`: unreadable/truncated, contradictory or ambiguous object identity, unresolved source linkage, missing required field, nonresponse, or codebook failure.

`A+` does not mean the object is true on imaging and does not mean the recommendation is guideline-appropriate. Panel A cannot use a later DS phrase or inferred coreference. Each positive label requires an exact source character span or detail-field key for object and recommendation, canonical `E/M/W` IDs, and `(subject_id,hadm_id,note_id,field_ordinal,charttime,storetime,D)` provenance. Disagreement between two independent A readers is `A_U`; no majority rescue. A two-reader jointly positive status `A_joint+` is the only positive anchor status used in the primary decomposition; all other statuses remain non-positive/unknown as specified below.

### Panel S: active versus superseded status

Panel S receives the frozen source unit, all later pre-D reciprocal radiology units in the same admission, and the canonical DS and DS-detail packet. The packet marks the index unit and chronology but hides route, target/control role, H results, and panel-A labels. It uses a separate hierarchy, applied in order:

1. `S_SUPERSEDED` only if a later pre-D report/addendum or canonical DS explicitly negates, discontinues, replaces, or changes the target object, modality, or due interval, with an attributable span/field key;
2. `S_ACTIVE` if the target object and affirmative atom remain applicable at `D`, no explicit superseder exists in the supplied pre-D packet, and the reviewer can identify the applicable instruction with attributable provenance;
3. `S_U` for ambiguity, competing unresolved instructions, absent/unreadable later evidence, uncertain coreference, incomplete packet, or nonresponse.

A later mention with the same atom is not a superseder. A generic DS follow-up statement without target-linked object/modality/interval is not proof of active status. A statement that the patient already completed an action is not treated as a superseder or as evidence of completion. If two S readers disagree, the status is `S_U`; they cannot convert an explicit adverse span into active by consensus. Panel S reports the decisive source span and exact temporal ordering; it does not judge appropriateness, delivery, viewing, ownership, or completion.

To prevent DS/common-mode circularity, emit two S views: `S_source_only`, based on index plus later radiology evidence, and `S_full`, which additionally permits canonical DS text. The primary active/superseded status is `S_source_only`; `S_full` is a prespecified sensitivity description. Thus DS preservation is not used to manufacture the status it is later compared against. If the source-only packet lacks later radiology evidence, that is `S_U`, not active by default. If an explicit contradiction exists in source-only evidence, it is `S_SUPERSEDED` even if DS copies the original recommendation.

## Locked joint states and estimands

For each target unit, retain the cross-classified state `(A_joint+, S_source_only)` with all combinations of positive, negative, superseded, and unknown. Do not delete unknowns. The inherited primary H outcome, target/control estimands, route, and all-unit adverse rule are unchanged. In particular, a target unit that is `S_SUPERSEDED` is still retained in the inherited all-unit H failure calculation; the new decomposition cannot rescue an inherited adverse result.

Report these pre-specified finite-population secondary quantities on the frozen target cell and the same frozen paired-control domain where applicable:

- proportions of target units/admissions in `A_joint+`, `A_U`, `S_ACTIVE`, `S_SUPERSEDED`, and `S_U`;
- exact DS H+ rate stratified by `(A_joint+,S_ACTIVE)`, `(A_joint+,S_SUPERSEDED)`, and all remaining states, with unknowns retained;
- the admission-level rate of `H+ AND A_joint+ AND S_ACTIVE`, and the adverse rate of `H- or H_U` among `A_joint+ AND S_ACTIVE`;
- a source-only active documentation contrast `H_rate(S_ACTIVE)-H_rate(S_SUPERSEDED)` and its design-weighted interval, reported only if both domains have positive inclusion support; and
- the matched-control H rate and route-scrambled specificity rate, unchanged from the parent.

These are descriptive/decompositional finite-population estimands, not causal effects. Use the inherited sequential admission weight `1/(pi1_i q_i)` and retain `pi1`, conditional `q`, and second-order factors separately. H, A, and S nonresponse/unknown states remain on their original path weights; no complete-case or favorable-state analysis is permitted. A requested interval with zero/undefined support is `UNDEFINED/DEFER`, never a substituted comparison. The primary inherited target and control estimates remain separately reported and cannot be redefined as the active subset.

## Pre-registered bridge rule and interpretation

A supportive retrospective result requires all inherited gates and the parent paired-control/scrambled-control gates, plus: (1) valid independent A and S seals and roster separation; (2) positive weighted support for `A_joint+ AND S_ACTIVE`; (3) no target admission is declared bridge-eligible unless every required target unit is jointly `A+` and `S_ACTIVE`; and (4) the active-state and anchor-state rates, uncertainty, and every `A_U/S_U/S_SUPERSEDED` unit are carried in the nomination. This rule is conservative and does not alter the inherited primary estimand. If the active/anchor domain is unsupported, or any required status is unresolved, the result is `DEFER`; it is not treated as inactive or safe. `S_SUPERSEDED` prevents that unit from being called an active handoff opportunity in the secondary bridge nomination, but does not erase it from the frozen all-opportunity denominator or inherited adverse route.

An adverse result is a high rate of `A-`, `A_U`, `S_SUPERSEDED`, or `S_U` in a target cell, a lower active-state DS rate than the matched control, or a discrepancy showing that DS H+ is frequently present despite source-only supersession. These findings falsify the intended active target-linked documentation construct, not clinical appropriateness or safety. An inconclusive result includes unsupported active states, missing later-source coverage, packet/roster/seal failure, nonresponse, undefined weighted intervals, or inability to distinguish contradictory text; it maps to `DEFER`.

A supportive result can nominate only a separately governed prospective silent bridge for the exact `(E*,M*,W*)` and route. The prospective bridge must use actual-calendar delivery/view logs, named accountable owner, target-linked order/referral/scheduling/completion and result-review events, UI presentation/edit/override records, patient preference/refusal, outside-care and loss-to-follow-up ascertainment, the actual source images or an independent image-based adjudication, and clinical adjudication of appropriateness and timing. MIMIC has none of these. No retrospective A+, S_ACTIVE, H+, DS copy, `deathtime`, `dod`, discharge location, service, ICU, transfer, prescription, or medication record is a proxy for delivery, action, completion, clinical truth, safety, benefit, or causality.

Before labels, synthetic-only rehearsal must establish finite worst-case minutes for both panels, including two readers, disagreement handling, reread/nonresponse charge, provenance marking, and the inherited H/control/scrambled charges. Enumerate every allowed post-label phase-1 partition exactly as in the parent and use componentwise maxima. If the added roles cannot fit under the worst-case certificate, select `NO_SELECTION`; never reduce roles, sample units, top up after labels, or use observed time.

The verifier must independently test source-only reconstruction of later-unit inventory; strict pre-D and same-admission chronology; A packet exclusion of DS/later evidence; S source-only versus S-full separation; exact supersession hierarchy and spans; immutable target/control membership under label permutation; roster disjointness and packet masking; all-unit inherited H precedence despite supersession; positive inclusion probabilities and weighted state contrasts; unknown/nonresponse retention; and complete ledger/reserve accounting. Fixtures can test parser, joins, state machine, and estimators only. They cannot establish that A+ is a true imaging finding, that S_ACTIVE means clinically appropriate, or that any recommendation was delivered, viewed, owned, ordered, completed, safe, or beneficial.

## Substantive advance and remaining uncertainty

This successor prevents two clinically consequential category errors without changing the frozen question: treating canonical-DS omission as a failure when the source record explicitly superseded the recommendation, and treating lexical target/DS agreement as independent clinical anchoring. It does so with a source-only active-state panel, a DS-enabled sensitivity panel, an independent radiology-only anchor panel, explicit unknowns, exact spans, roster separation, all-unit adverse retention, finite-population decomposition, and a prospective bridge that requires actual workflow and image/clinical evidence.

MIMIC still lacks source images, reliable contemporaneous workflow clocks, delivery/view logs, named ownership, patient preference, orders/referrals/scheduling, outside-care linkage, completed follow-up, and clinical outcomes. Subject-shifted timestamps prevent cross-subject calendar inference. Therefore even a fully supportive result remains only an exact, target-cell-specific stored-text and active-documentation nomination for silent prospective validation; it cannot establish clinical truth, actionability in practice, safety, benefit, causality, or transport.
