# A sentinel test stops premature H-INV fitting for eICU laboratory-process portability

## Status and scientific decision

This substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` preserves the fixed adult first-stay population, 24-hour landmark, seven-day index-hospital mortality endpoint, 14-laboratory panel, whole-hospital transport estimand, source-only target prohibition, and noncausal limits. It reports a completed, predeclared fold-0 sentinel rather than a full ten-fold result.

The sentinel does not establish the parent's necessary raw-process transport problem. In five overlap-qualified target hospitals, raw observation features added mortality log-loss utility both internally and externally, but the median transport loss was -0.00025 (hospital-bootstrap 95% interval -0.00456 to 0.00278). At the same time, raw process history strongly improved held-hospital repeat-measurement prediction after abnormal results. Rank-12 source-template projection lowered independent linear and tree hospital leakage to within the 0.02 margin, but it lost more repeat skill than allowed and did not improve transport. Therefore fitting H-INV now would answer a method question before establishing the scientific problem it is meant to solve.

The revised deliverable is staged. Stage 1 completes the inexpensive transparent ten-fold confirmation with B-V, B-RAW and B-ZR. H-INV is stopped for the initial run and is unlocked only if Stage 1 establishes a positive raw transport loss while confirming that raw observation history retains abnormality-responsive information and that transparent projection cannot preserve it without leakage. This is a deferral based on evidence, not a claim that recurrent models are infeasible or that hospital transport never occurs.

## Evidence-supported claim, tested claim, and unresolved claim

The strongest prior evidence supports only that laboratory ordering/timing can contain healthcare-process signal beyond values [K1], eICU mortality models can change performance across hospitals [K2], and recurrent models can learn from values, masks, and elapsed times [K3]. None establishes that this particular first-day process block has excess transport loss, or that a recurrent invariant representation is needed.

The sentinel tested:

> In a source-only fold-0 development experiment, raw first-day laboratory process features will have positive source-internal utility but lose that utility in unseen hospitals, and source-template residualization will reduce that loss without erasing abnormality-triggered repeat information or hiding hospital identity.

That conjunction was not supported. The remaining full-study hypothesis is narrower:

> Across all ten prespecified whole-hospital folds, is there a reproducible positive target-standardized transport loss for raw first-day laboratory process utility that persists after severity, interface, end-of-life, and disposition checks and is large enough to justify learning a nonlinear shared-response representation?

A supportive full Stage 1 result would justify H-INV. A precise absence of raw transport loss would end this branch. A positive raw transport loss coupled with no abnormality-responsive repeat signal would identify routine workflow, not a patient-responsive component, and also stop H-INV. Imprecision remains inconclusive.

## Completed bounded diagnostic

### Frozen data and cohort

The run used eICU snapshot [source checksum]. It reproduced 81,176 eligible stays, 72,158 people, 69 hospitals, 80,581 known outcomes, 595 unknown outcomes, and 4,897 seven-day deaths. Eligibility remained age >=18 (mapping `> 89` to 90), `unitvisitnumber == 1`, both ICU and hospital offsets >1440, hospitals with >=500 eligible landmarks determined before person exclusion, and exclusion of every person represented at more than one eligible hospital. No laboratory result was required.

Inputs were ICU minutes 0-1440; minute 1440 was included. A lab was available only if `0 <= labresultoffset <= 1440` and `max(labresultoffset, nonmissing labresultrevisedoffset) <= 1440`. The run retained 1,572,450 plausible numeric panel rows before removing 5,069 exact stay/name/time/value duplicates, leaving 1,567,381 rows.

The deterministic size-balanced ten-fold manifest was constructed for all 69 hospitals. Compute limits permitted only a one-fold sentinel: fold 0 contained six unseen target hospitals and 8,056 stays; five hospitals passed the inherited target-standardization overlap gates. All models and representations were fit on source hospitals only. Target rows contributed only frozen evaluation predictions and post-prediction standardization weights.

### Identical comparisons

All mortality comparisons used a fixed L2 logistic head (`C=0.1`), the L2 endpoint of the parent's elastic-net family, on identical rows and splits:

- **B-V:** fixed common covariates plus source-scaled first/last/min/max value summaries, with source-median imputation and no observation indicators.
- **B-RAW:** B-V plus source-scaled analyte counts, first/last times, and occupied two-hour bins.
- **B-ZR:** B-V plus process residuals after an outcome-blind source-only ridge model of process from B-V, projected away from the source-hospital template SVD. Rank was chosen without mortality from {0,12} in this sentinel; the full experiment retains {0,1,2,4,8,12}.

The repeat task was outcome-blind: among stay-analyte pairs measured during hours 8-12, predict another measurement during hours 12-16 from fixed covariates, current value/abnormality and prior process history. Hospital auditors were independently fit linear and random-forest classifiers, compared with a case-mix/value-only auditor. Neither target aggregate statistics nor target labels selected a rank or model.

### Computed results

Among the five overlap-qualified targets:

- B-RAW median source-internal utility over B-V was 0.00426 log-loss units (95% interval 0.00227 to 0.00612).
- B-RAW median external utility was 0.00608 (0.00225 to 0.00683).
- Raw transport loss `D_RAW = U_RAW^I - U_RAW^E` was -0.00025 (-0.00456 to 0.00278), so this sentinel neither supports positive excess transport nor proves its absence.
- With APACHE/APS severity fields added, median raw transport loss remained -0.00151 (-0.00348 to 0.00124). Excluding first-day EOL records left median external raw utility essentially unchanged at 0.00613.
- Raw process utility for the four-state day-7 disposition endpoint was descriptively larger: median external log-loss gain 0.02639 (0.01844 to 0.03934). This supports a disposition/workflow rival but does not prove that mortality utility is caused by disposition.
- In 23,118 held-target repeat tasks, B-RAW improved log loss over patient state by 0.05636 overall, 0.08666 in 9,691 abnormal tasks, and 0.03449 in 13,427 normal tasks. The process signal is therefore not only clock frequency, although clinician intent remains unobserved.
- Raw process features increased source-hospital balanced accuracy over the case-mix/value auditor by 0.0841 for the linear probe and 0.0607 for the tree probe.
- Rank-12 B-ZR reduced those advantages to 0.0147 and 0.0056, within the 0.02 leakage margin, but failed the combined gate: source-internal repeat log loss was 0.0290 worse than raw; in target abnormal tasks B-ZR lost 0.02060 log-loss utility relative to raw.
- B-ZR external mortality utility was only 0.00040, versus 0.00608 for B-RAW. Its median reduction in transport loss was -0.00249 (-0.00492 to 0.00196), adverse in direction and imprecise.

Thus raw process history carries patient-responsive and site-decodable information, while aggressive linear projection trades one against the other. What was not observed is the necessary excess raw transport loss. These results do not support invariance, clinician concern, causal testing effects, or deployment.

## Revised staged experiment

### Stage 1: transparent confirmation

Run all ten fixed whole-hospital folds with the parent's full source-only preprocessing, five imputations, nested elastic-net tuning, ranks {0,1,2,4,8,12}, 2,000 paired hospital/person bootstrap replicates, target standardization, and all artifact, severity, EOL, sparse/no-lab and four-state disposition gates. B-V, B-RAW and B-ZR use identical source rows, target rows and mortality heads. No H-INV parameters are fit.

Stage 1 unlocks H-INV only if all hold:

1. B-RAW has positive source-internal utility and `median_h D_RAW(h) > 0` with its 95% interval above zero.
2. Held-hospital abnormal repeat gain is positive and exceeds normal-value/clock repeat gain.
3. Positive transport loss persists after exact duplicate collapse, conservative revised-time availability, clinician-approved unit/range harmonization, common-system restriction, severity adjustment, first-day EOL exclusion and disposition interpretation.
4. At least 30 outcome-supported hospitals pass QC/overlap, with no dominance by a single hospital or fold.
5. B-ZR either fails both independent leakage margins or loses >0.01 repeat log loss relative to B-RAW, establishing a residual nonlinear separation problem.

Stage 1 stops the branch if the upper 95% bound for raw transport loss is <=0, raw source utility is nonpositive, or repeat signal is not abnormality-responsive. It declares B-ZR sufficient and does not fit H-INV if B-ZR reduces transport with positive target utility, both leakage advantages <=0.02, and repeat loss <=0.01. Otherwise uncertainty, overlap failure, fold heterogeneity, unit-map disagreement, or gate dominance is inconclusive and calls for data/adjudication rather than a recurrent fit.

### Conditional Stage 2: H-INV

Only after the unlock gate, fit the parent's 16/32-unit hierarchical GRU measurement-hazard model on identical source folds and inputs: past-only values/abnormality, prior masks/counts/elapsed times, common covariates, source-hospital random intercepts and rank {0,2,4} slopes. The target is next-bin 14-analyte masks; mortality labels do not train the representation. At targets, hospital effects are fixed to the source population mean and no target statistic is fit.

H-INV must beat B-ZR on abnormal repeat retention at comparable independent linear/tree leakage, reduce raw transport loss with positive target mortality utility and calibrated/net-benefit improvement, and remain selective to source-template transplant rather than generic MCAR deletion. Failure is erasure, hidden site identity, no transport reduction, nonpositive target utility, or artifact/disposition dominance. Convergence or precision failure is inconclusive.

Stage 1 is estimated at 8-12 CPUs, 64-96 GiB and 2-3 CPU-hours; the measured sentinel took 106 seconds in an ordinary 120-second shell after the managed allocation was cancelled before execution for episode-budget exhaustion. Conditional Stage 2 remains an unverified 4-7 A100 GPU-hour estimate plus 2-3 CPU-hours and must use an allocated GPU. The scheduler cancellation is an infrastructure/budget fact, not model infeasibility.

## Results interpretation and claims boundary

- **Supportive:** full Stage 1 establishes raw excess transport and responsive signal; Stage 2, if unlocked, preserves that signal, suppresses independent site leakage, and improves target transport/calibration. This supports predictive portability in this eICU setting only.
- **Adverse:** no precise raw transport problem; routine/disposition signal dominates; projection or H-INV erases responsive repeats; independent leakage remains; or target utility/transport worsens. A negative result is a valid reason to use B-V or raw models with explicit external validation.
- **Inconclusive:** intervals straddle gates, fewer than 30 hospitals pass overlap/QC, unit/interface adjudication changes direction, folds/seeds disagree, or models fail convergence.

An automatic verifier can recompute cohort/time logic, source-only fitting, split identity, repeat and mortality log losses, direct-standardization weights, leakage probes, bootstrap intervals, and gate-to-claim consistency. It cannot establish unit plausibility, clinician concern or intent, causal effects of ordering, fairness, modern external validity, or deployment safety. Those require clinical laboratory adjudication, order-entry/protocol/staffing evidence, contemporary external data, and prospective review.

## Method alternatives and decision record

The simple alternatives are fully specified: B-V tests whether values suffice; B-RAW tests whether process adds portable utility; B-ZR tests whether a linear source-template subspace can be removed. The conditional learned alternative targets next-mask sequence likelihood and could retain nonlinear ordered response that projection loses. The sentinel shows exactly that projection suppresses responsive repeat information, but also fails to show the raw transport defect that would make solving this tradeoff consequential. H-INV is therefore deferred, not rejected for complexity or GPU use.

Revisit H-INV only after Stage 1 passes its unlock gate. Revisit richer physiology before H-INV if severity/interface controls eliminate raw transport. Revisit causal policy questions only with order reasons, treatments, staffing/protocol data or a credible intervention. No outcome, subgroup, rank or threshold may be changed to rescue the claim.
