## 1. Unresolved question (sharpened)

In HBV-cirrhosis-dominant HCC inpatients undergoing hepatic resection, do **POD 0–2 liver-function trajectories** predict **severe post-hepatectomy dysfunction at POD 3–7** beyond **both** (a) static preoperative liver reserve **and** (b) the **operative-information set available at the end of surgery** (resection extent/approach/complexity, measurable from procedure names)?

### Why this sharpening is the substantive episode-4 advance
The parent's comparison isolated trajectory vs preop reserve only. But its own threats section named the two strongest rivals without measuring them. Episode 4 measured both, in the source data:
- **Rival 1 (operative extent proxy) is material:** among 2,513 qualifying encounters, the severe-dysfunction event rate is **12.5% after anatomic/major resections vs 3.8% after partial/minor resections** (>3-fold). A trajectory that merely proxies "a bigger operation was performed" carries no information the surgeon does not already have at skin closure. Without model B, a supportive parent result would be uninterpretable.
- **Rival 2 (transfusion/hemodilution + albumin replacement) is material:** blood-product orders are frequent, and **human-albumin infusion is near-universal (2,409/2,513 = 96% of encounters; 92% started before day 3)**. Exogenous albumin masks true synthetic-function decline → **ALB trajectory features are demoted to descriptive/sensitivity**; TBIL and PTR/INR trajectories are primary.

### Clinical importance
PHLF is the leading cause of death after hepatectomy [K1]. The 50-50 criteria become decision-relevant only on day 5 [K1]. Early trustworthy information at POD 0–2 could shift escalation, monitoring intensity, and transplant discussions by days. Existing early-prediction evidence requires specialized assays (ATIII decline ≥30% at POD 3, OR 3.04, N=151 [K3]) or 55-variable ensembles with regeneration biomarkers (AUC up to 0.904, N=1071 [K2]). Whether the **interpretable shape of routine POD 0–2 labs** adds information beyond what is already known at end of surgery — preop reserve **plus the operation actually performed** — remains unisolated in a large HBV-dominant population. The sharpened estimand is the deployable one: what does the lab monitor add to the surgeon's own knowledge at that moment?

4. **Albumin infusion near-universal:** 10,053 medication rows (人血白蛋白/白蛋白/Plasbumin/AlbuRx/Flexbumin/Octapharma variants) in 2,409/2,513 encounters (96%); 2,306 (92%) started before day 3 → ALB trajectory features contaminated by replacement therapy in nearly all encounters.

Pre-POD-3 product orders are near-universal (99.6% encounters — routine preop reservation orders, uninformative), but product orders placed during POD 0-2 occur in 6.1% of encounters and mark a high-risk subgroup (event rate 17.3% vs 9.1%, crude ~1.9x): a management-response marker that must be adjusted, not treated as pure artifact. ## 3. Study design (Harbor experiment)

- **Population:** 2,513 qualifying encounters (first qualifying encounter per patient; 70/30 patient-level stratified split).
- **Exposure:** POD 0–2 trajectory features for TBIL and PTR/INR (slope, value at POD 1/2, change from baseline, persistence index), range-validated (parent §2.5 rules: PTR/INR 0.5–5, TBIL 0–1000, ALB 10–60; zeros→missing).
- **Primary outcome:** peak TBIL >50 µmol/L within POD 3–7 (n=246, 9.8%). Severity gradient TBIL>30 (n=881) and 2×-baseline variants as sensitivity.
- **Model A (parent's baseline):** logistic on preop reserve (preop TBIL, ALB, platelets, age, sex, cirrhosis/HBV flags).
- **Model B (new, end-of-surgery information set):** A + operative covariates: extent class (anatomic-major vs partial-minor vs ablation), vascular/thrombectomy, cholecystectomy, laparoscopic, count of concurrent procedures.
- **Model T (primary scientific test):** B + POD 0–2 trajectory features. **Primary comparison: T vs B** (incremental ΔAUC/IDI/NRI with bootstrap 95% CI). Parent's A-vs-T comparison retained as secondary.
- **Artifact controls:** transfusion-exposure covariate (any product by POD 2, per refined classifier); albumin-infusion covariate (binary + pre-POD-3 indicator); TBIL/PTR-only trajectory sensitivity (dropping ALB features); within-anatomic-major stratified analysis (n≈1,723, ≈215 events).
- **Snapshot comparators:** day-5 snapshot model (38% coverage; missingness reported) and last-value-before-POD-3 model, both refit with B covariates.
- **Learned comparator (conditional):** GBM on the same features if linear ΔAUC ambiguity remains.
- **Falsification criteria:** Supportive = significant incremental ΔAUC of T over B (bootstrap CI excluding 0), robust to permutation control, transfusion/albumin adjustment, and extent stratification. **Adverse = ΔAUC CI includes 0 over B → the early trajectory is redundant with the surgeon's end-of-surgery knowledge; informative boundary, contradicting the parent's stronger claim.** Distinguishing sub-result: significant over A but not over B → apparent trajectory signal is operative-extent proxying (parent claim refuted in strong form). Inconclusive = effective events <100 or extent×trajectory interaction unstable.
- **Computable vs not:** incremental-information question computable from bound data; mechanism (regeneration failure vs perioperative insult) and clinical ISGLS adjudication are not; outcome remains a lab-defined analog.

## 7. Threats and alternatives
Operative-extent confounding (now adjusted, primary comparison); transfusion/hemodilution bilirubin load (adjusted + sensitivity); albumin replacement masking ALB decline (ALB demoted; TBIL/PTR primary); residual unmeasured operative severity (blood loss, Pringle time absent — claimed); informative measurement frequency (≥1 measurement required in all windows; measurement-count adjustment); single-center HBV-dominant external-validity limit; lab-defined outcome analog (no mechanism claim). Positive ΔAUC over B would NOT establish mechanism — requires ICG/volumetrics.
