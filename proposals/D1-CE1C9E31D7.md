## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / resection / ablation) for HCC, surveillance couples serial serum AFP with scheduled cross-sectional imaging. The parent asks whether the within-patient post-LDT AFP slope predicts time to local progression, and whether it does so specifically when the landmark imaging is **clean** (no progression descriptor). That is the *surveillance-adjunct* decision: whether serial AFP should intensify imaging when the current scan is uninformative.

- **H1 (leading claim):** among patients with a clean landmark imaging (IMGneg), a higher within-patient AFP slope over (index, t*] still predicts a higher hazard of subsequent progression — an **imaging-independent early AFP signal**.
- **Strongest rival R_concordance (imaging-concordance artifact):** AFP rises in patients whose landmark/interval imaging already carries sub-threshold suspicion, so within the stringently IMGneg stratum the slope HR attenuates to ~1 while it remains elevated in IMGpos.
- **This child's added rival R_measurement (the decisive one):** IMGneg is defined by the **absence** of lexical progression words in examination text, while the outcome is the **presence** of the **same words**. A single shared lexical pipeline that under-records subtle findings can both assign true progressors to IMGneg and then score them as events via the identical words — **manufacturing** the association. This is a measurement/detection-bias explanation distinct from R_concordance and from informative observation.
- **Secondary rival R_timing:** an apparent IMGneg signal is produced by intervening scans in (t*, event) scheduled *because* AFP rose (informative observation; the parent's surveillance-intensity machinery is reused, not replaced).

The added M6 element makes H1 and R_measurement **differentially observable**: if the association is real it survives requiring independent corroboration; if it is a shared-text artifact it collapses when the endpoint no longer relies solely on the same words that define the stratum.

## 2. Inherited frozen design (verbatim; not modified)
Population = HCC diagnosis + ≥1 liver-directed procedure; index = earliest qualifying procedure; landmark t* = index + 180d (90d sensitivity).
Exposures: per-patient AFP slope and ALBI slope fit ONLY on same-draw AFP + albumin + total-bilirubin in (index, t*]; same-draw pairing within ±2d on `检验时间`; ALBI = 0.66*log10(bilirubin) − 0.085*albumin.
Primary endpoint (parent): modality-restricted abdominal **local-progression** event {增大,进展,复发,新发,增多} on `检查所见`/`检查诊断`, with 转移 (metastasis) as competing event; censoring via `出院时间`; retreatment proxy secondary; four rivals; conjunctive falsification; no survival/mRECIST claim.

## 3. Element (a) — folded-in measured attainability and explicit thresholds
Reused **read-only** from the frozen probe of `[prior hypothesis]` (work/gen-imaging-conditional/, 52 s bounded CPU probe). This episode did **not** re-run the probe; the figures below are the sibling's measured values.
Landmark imaging status from `examinations` over window **[t*−45d, t*+45d]**:
- **IMGneg** = a qualifying abdominal cross-sectional/US study whose `检查所见` AND `检查诊断` contain **none** of {增大,进展,复发,新发,增多,转移};
- **IMGpos** = a qualifying study containing a progression/metastasis descriptor;
- **IMGmissing** = no qualifying study in the window.

**Measured (sibling probe):** qualifying abdominal imaging in [t*−45d,t*+45d] = **1,481/35,339 (4.2%)**; **IMGneg 602 / IMGpos 879 / IMGmissing 33,858**; post-landmark progression events **IMGneg 117 / IMGpos 561** (of 522 slope-estimable IMGneg).

Primary re-fit: the frozen landmark Cox (per-1-SD AFP slope + ALBI slope + first modality + age/sex + draw intensity) re-estimated **within the IMGneg stratum**, reporting the AFP-slope HR and 95% CI. Pre-specified heterogeneity: landmark-imaging-status × AFP-slope interaction with a 1-df LRT. Reused from the parent: (a) surveillance-intensity covariates over (t*, censor]; (b) AFP-independent ALBI-slope negative control; (c) intensity-adjusted re-test (ΔHR + 95% CI).

Explicit branch thresholds (anchored to the measured probe; replaces the parent's generic ≥30):

- **Supportive:** M6 IMGneg AFP-slope **HR > 1, 95% CI excludes 1, ≥75 corroborated IMGneg events**, monotone quartile trend (p_trend < 0.05), and the IMGneg/IMGpos interaction not significant — **and** survives corroboration (no >30% attenuation vs M0).
- **Adverse = R_measurement (shared-lexical-pipeline / detection bias):** IMGneg HR > 1 under **M0** but ≈1 under **M6**, **OR** lexical-vs-corroborated event **concordance < 0.6** → the imaging-independent signal is a shared-lexical-pipeline artifact.
- **Adverse = R_concordance:** M6 IMGneg HR ≈1 (CI includes 1) while IMGpos HR > 1, or interaction p < 0.05 with IMGpos > IMGneg.
- **Inconclusive guard (pre-declared):** fires when **IMGneg corroborated events < 50** OR **IMGmissing > 50%** (observed ≈96%) OR landmark images lack dated `开始时间` OR discordance unresolvable → report descriptive HR with CI, do NOT test heterogeneity.
- **Precise-null:** M6 IMGneg HR within ±0.10 of 1 with a tight CI (upper bound < 1.5) and a flat ALBI negative control; a wide CI that merely includes 1 is Inconclusive, not Precise-null.
- **Selection guard (mandatory):** because IMGneg is defined by *having* a study in [t*−45d,t*+45d], report IMGneg vs the full indexed cohort on surveillance-intensity covariates and fit the intensity-adjusted model; a large, explained imbalance is labelled, not silently adjusted away.

## 4. Element (b) — the ONE added decision-changing element: corroborated endpoint (M6)
**M6 corroborated progression = ≥2 of three conditionally-orthogonal indicators:**
1. **lexical imaging progression** — `examinations` `检查所见`/`检查诊断` contains {增大,进展,复发,新发,增多};
2. **sustained AFP rise** — AFP ≥2× vs the landmark draw, sustained (≥2 consecutive qualifying draws), **or** crossing a pre-specified absolute threshold (declared a priori by the solver from the observed AFP distribution before fitting; a single rising draw alone does not qualify);
3. **repeat liver-directed procedure** — a second liver-directed procedure (`procedures` resection/TACE/ablation labels; exclude diagnostic-only) after t*.

The three indicators are chosen to be conditionally orthogonal: (i) is free-text imaging, (ii) is a lab trajectory, (iii) is a procedure record — so requiring ≥2 removes reliance on any single detection channel. Primary estimand = per-1-SD AFP-slope HR **within IMGneg under M6**, plus a pre-specified **M0 (lexical-only) vs M6** comparison.

## 5. Method alternatives (baseline vs substantive alternative)
- **M0 (baseline)** = parent's lexical-only landmark Cox within IMGneg + imaging-status interaction.
- **M6 (alternative)** = corroborated-endpoint landmark Cox within IMGneg (≥2 orthogonal indicators) + imaging-status interaction; report concordance between M0 and M6 event indicators.
- **What M6 reveals that M0 loses:** M0's event and its IMGneg stratum are built from the *same words*, so M0 can neither separate a genuine imaging-independent AFP signal from a shared-text artifact nor quantify over-recorded/under-recorded progression. M6 breaks the shared channel: the slope must predict an outcome corroborated by at least one non-lexical channel (lab trajectory, procedure). Attenuation of the HR under M6, or low M0↔M6 concordance, is direct evidence for R_measurement.
- **Deferred alternative (recorded):** a joint latent-class / shared-parameter observation-event model estimating sensitivity/specificity of the lexical endpoint and latent true progression. **Revisit condition:** revisit once M0↔M6 concordance is estimable and nonzero (a saturating concordance of ~1 would make latent-class Se/Sp non-identifiable and the model uninformative).
- **Budget:** CPU-only, minutes to low tens of minutes; GPU not required.

## 7. Falsification branches (pre-declared; supportive / adverse / inconclusive)
- **Supportive:** M6 IMGneg HR > 1, CI excludes 1, **≥75 corroborated IMGneg events**, monotone quartile trend, survives corroboration (≤30% attenuation vs M0) → H1 is supported after removing the shared-channel artifact; AFP may add imaging-independent early signal when the scan is clean.
- **Adverse R_measurement:** IMGneg HR > 1 under M0 but ≈1 under M6, **or** M0↔M6 concordance < 0.6 → the "imaging-independent AFP signal" is a shared-lexical-pipeline / detection-bias artifact; H1 is refuted in its current form.
- **Adverse R_concordance:** M6 IMGneg HR ≈1 (CI includes 1) while IMGpos HR > 1, or interaction p < 0.05 with IMGpos > IMGneg → the signal is confined to imaging-suspicious patients.
- **Inconclusive:** IMGneg corroborated events < 50, or IMGmissing > 50%, or undated landmark images, or discordance unresolvable → descriptive HR only; no heterogeneity claim.
- **Precise-null:** M6 IMGneg HR within ±0.10 of 1 with tight CI and flat ALBI negative control.

Interpretation guard: an imprecise null is Inconclusive, not refutation.
