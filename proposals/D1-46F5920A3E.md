# Persistent numeric therapy-setting endpoint for the Ventilation-label lineage

## Change and unresolved clinical question

This is a targeted successor to `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`, preserving the frozen adult first-stay landmark denominator, post-freeze care-plan classification, four airway-bound definitions, five landmarks, hospital-held-out evaluation, and strict proxy limits. The remaining gap is not vocabulary coverage or generic documentation: the existing endpoint is a single minimum respiratory-chart setting decrease, which can be driven by one copied, transient, or erroneous value. The decision-relevant unresolved question is whether Ventilation care-plan labels add transportable information about a **persistent numeric therapy-setting de-escalation** beyond pre-landmark therapy/physiology and documentation opportunity.

The falsifiable hypothesis is that exact or outcome-blind broader Ventilation labels improve hospital-held-out prediction of a subsequent persistent reduction in at least one numeric respiratory therapy setting, compared with the same pre-landmark physiologic/therapy baseline and generic documentation controls. The strongest existing evidence supports only that labels can correlate with structured chart activity and that the prior single-minimum proxy is sensitive to airway-bound definitions. It does not support a bedside extubation, SBT, liberation, appropriateness, quality, causal, or benefit claim. This proposal tests only the narrower numeric endpoint claim.

## Target-independent numeric endpoint

Read `respiratoryCharting`, source `[internal dataset path]`, [source checksum], ordinary gzip CSV member, schema `[internal dataset path]`. Required columns are `patientunitstayid`, `respchartoffset`, `respchartvaluelabel`, and `respchartvalue`; join on `patientunitstayid = sid`; time is `respchartoffset`.

Recognize only canonical labels: FiO2 (`FiO2`, `FIO2 (%)`, `Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2)`), PEEP (`PEEP`), pressure support (`Pressure Support`), and set ventilator rate (`Vent Rate`). Parse finite numeric values; convert FiO2 percentages to fractions; discard out-of-range values (FiO2 0–1 after conversion, PEEP 0–50, pressure support 0–60, rate 0–80). For each component with a valid pre-landmark observation in `[L-360,L]` and a post-window observation, use the last pre-landmark value as baseline. A component qualifies for a persistent decrease if at least two post-window observations are at least the component threshold below baseline, the qualifying observations are separated by at least 120 minutes, and the post-window median is at least the threshold below baseline. Thresholds are 0.05 FiO2, 2 cmH2O PEEP, 2 cmH2O pressure support, and 2 breaths/minute set rate. The primary binary endpoint is one when any paired component qualifies. Retain component identity, baseline, post median, minimum, count, and timing for audit; do not redefine the cohort by paired availability. The single-minimum endpoint remains a prespecified sensitivity, not the primary outcome.

This endpoint is a structured therapy-setting proxy, not a ventilator-mode or patient-state measurement. It cannot establish invasive ventilation, spontaneous breathing trial, extubation, durable liberation, readiness, appropriateness, benefit, harm, or bedside intent.

## Baselines and estimand

Use the inherited exposure-blind pre-landmark therapy/physiology features (`pre_fio2`, `pre_peep`, `pre_pressure_support`, `pre_vent_rate`, `pre_chart_n`, `pre_distinct_labels`, `pre_robust_n`) and landmark indicators. Add generic opportunity controls computed without using care-plan values as clinical semantics: `cp_any`, `cp_log_rows`, `cp_distinct_groups`, `vent_any`, `vent_log_rows`, and `vent_distinct_values` from `[L,L+360]`. Compare process baseline, exact labels, and broader labels. Fit each model independently in every leave-one-hospital-out fold, with preprocessing learned only in training hospitals. Require an eligible held-out hospital to have at least 20 rows and both endpoint classes. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log-loss deltas, Brier score, AUROC, calibration, endpoint prevalence, component-specific results, and hospital-direction counts. Sample all landmarks for a stay together in 100 stay-cluster bootstrap replicates. A confirmatory run must nested-refit preprocessing and models inside each bootstrap replicate; fixed-prediction intervals are feasibility evidence only.

The primary transport estimand is the held-out-hospital performance difference of broader versus process baseline and broader versus exact model, pooled micro and equal-hospital macro, under each inherited airway definition. The estimand is predictive transportability of this structured endpoint, not treatment effect or clinical utility.

These are descriptive feasibility counts from the full adult first-stay scan, not the parent-frozen analytic cohort, not a causal association, and not evidence of bedside de-escalation. They show that the repeated-observation endpoint is computable and has nonzero events; the prespecified LOHO comparison on the frozen cohort remains the falsifiable analysis.

## Decision gates

Supportive transportability requires, in the parent-frozen cohort, broader labels to improve both micro and macro log-loss over process controls and over exact labels in the unbounded definition and at least two airway-bounded definitions; the nested stay-cluster 97.5th-percentile interval for each primary improvement must remain below zero, with at least five eligible unbounded hospitals and at least three eligible hospitals in two bounded definitions. Component-specific improvement must not be confined to one unreplicated component or a single hospital.

Adverse transportability is declared if broader labels fail to beat process in every bounded definition, are pointwise worse in at least two bounded definitions with nested stay-cluster probability of worse macro loss at least 0.75, or show no stable advantage over exact labels. This would support not using the exported labels as a transportable predictor for persistent therapy-setting reduction, while not proving absence of bedside information.

Inconclusive means mixed endpoint or airway-bound results, insufficient eligible hospitals/events, intervals spanning zero, or a difference between single-minimum and persistent endpoints. A positive single-minimum result but adverse persistent result specifically indicates sensitivity to transient/copy artifacts and does not rescue the clinical interpretation. A persistent-label signal that disappears under independent respiratory-care corroboration, temporal perturbation, or nested refitting is also inconclusive or adverse rather than supportive.

It cannot determine whether a respiratory chart value reflects delivered therapy, whether a patient was invasively ventilated, or whether a clinician intended or benefited from de-escalation. Those claims require device-level data, SBT/extubation records, local interface/workflow metadata, blinded clinical adjudication, and external hospital validation.
