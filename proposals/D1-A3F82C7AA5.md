# Early physiologic–documented-plan discordance in HCC care transitions

## Scientific deliverable

The future solver must newly construct and lock an admission-anchored cohort, exact order/procedure dictionaries, and patient partitions; fit B0, B1 and a prespecified irregular latent-state alternative; produce locked six-hour competing-risk predictions; estimate plan-concordant and plan-discordant transition CIFs, the prespecified laboratory incremental contrast, calibrated uncertainty and falsification analyses; and provide a claim-to-output table separating computationally checkable findings from claims requiring clinical adjudication.

This is a substantive successor to [prior hypothesis]. It preserves that candidate's first-observed admission anchor, 0–24-hour laboratory window, pre-72-hour plan-proxy window, 72–144-hour outcome buffer, early-discharge competing event, exact HCC data bindings, and patient-level split. The scientific change is the primary estimand: early laboratory information is tested for a prespecified excess ability to identify transitions that do not match a single documented pre-72-hour pathway proxy, rather than only for an average modality-specific increment.

## Unresolved question and hypothesis

The available evidence supports only that this snapshot contains HCC-coded diagnosis strings, dated laboratory results, dated procedures and dated non-drug orders. It does not establish that an order is intent, that a diagnosis is pathology-confirmed HCC, or that a procedure is appropriate, elective, urgent, beneficial or caused by the observed physiology.

The unresolved, falsifiable question is:

> Among adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient admission, does numeric hepatic/renal laboratory state observed in [0,24) hours add more calibrated information for a first recorded liver-directed transition during [72,144) hours when that transition has no matching single pathway proxy in orders opened before 72 hours, compared with a transition that matches the documented proxy?

The primary hypothesis is that early hepatic/renal physiology is especially informative for a plan-discordant recorded transition. Formally, after adjustment for admission/pre-admission covariates and the pre-72-hour documented-plan proxy, the standardized laboratory increment for the cause “procedure family does not match the single documented pathway family” (Q) will exceed the increment for a matching procedure (C):

  H = Delta_Q - Delta_C > 0,

where Delta_Q and Delta_C are standardized 72–144-hour competing-risk CIF differences between B1 and B0 under the same locked test covariates. The prespecified minimum relevance threshold is H >= 0.01 absolute CIF points over this horizon, with a two-sided paired patient-bootstrap interval reported; this is a study relevance criterion, not a clinical utility threshold. The primary target is the clear-plan stratum P in {E,A,S}; a full-cohort proxy-mismatch analysis including no-plan and multiple-plan admissions is secondary.

This tests a clinically consequential uncertainty: whether a physiology-aware safety/reassessment signal could flag a pathway change that is not visible in the documented care-plan proxy. A supportive association could justify prospective review of whether early laboratory review should prompt multidisciplinary reassessment or additional data capture before pathway commitment. It would not justify changing treatment, infer intent, call a procedure unplanned, or claim benefit or harm. An adverse or null result would support retaining the documented-plan proxy as sufficient for this narrow recorded-workflow prediction task, subject to the same evidence limitations.

Exclude an admission with any valid-start E/A/S/X procedure in [0,72) hours. An invalid or missing procedure `开始时间` is unknown and is not evidence of no early procedure; report a missing-time bounds/sensitivity analysis. Do not condition the primary cohort on discharge after 72 hours.

The admission-anchored competing process is:

- D: discharge at its native `出院时间` before a qualifying E/A/S/X procedure and before 144 hours, including discharge before 72 hours. D is an observed-care transition, not treatment failure.
- E: first qualifying therapeutic hepatic-artery embolization/interventional family procedure with native `开始时间` in [72,144) hours.
- A: first qualifying liver-lesion ablation family procedure with native `开始时间` in [72,144) hours.
- S: first qualifying liver resection family procedure with native `开始时间` in [72,144) hours.
- X: first mixed, transplant/other liver-directed or unassignable procedure family event in [72,144) hours.
- C or Q: a relabeling of an E/A/S/X event according to its pre-72 plan relationship, not an additional event. The family-specific E/A/S/X label remains reported.
- Administrative censoring: no D or E/A/S/X event by 144 hours, or discharge after 144 hours.

The first event wins. A procedure before 72 hours excludes the admission rather than becoming an outcome. A discharge before 72 hours is D and prevents a later procedure outcome. Procedure starts are used, never end times. Bin procedure events and D in six-hour intervals for pooled-logistic estimation while retaining exact times for audit. Report event counts and risk time by P, C, Q, D and X; the primary clear-plan analysis is inconclusive if the locked test has fewer than 100 Q and 100 C events overall, or if any selected modality estimate is too sparse to support its stated interval.

## Prespecified plan proxy and discordance definition

Freeze a versioned order dictionary in fitting data, with any semantics review completed using the fitting and selection buckets only. It is built from `orders.医嘱(非药品)` and native `orders.开立时间` in [admission, admission+72h); if an order has no valid `开立时间`, it cannot establish a plan proxy. Use `orders.医嘱状态`, `医嘱期限`, `频次`, `开始时间` and `结束时间` only for audit and sensitivity, not as proof of completed treatment. Deduplicate within encounter, pathway family, exact order string and opening time. Exclude routine nursing, diagnostic imaging, cancellations and composite/unassignable orders from qualifying single-family proxies; retain their counts and exact strings in the audit.

The order dictionary assigns qualifying pathway terms to E, A or S only when a clinician-reviewable exact-string rule is frozen. Let P be:

- E, A or S when exactly one of those pathway families is represented by at least one qualifying order;
- M when two or more families are represented, or a potentially therapeutic pathway string is unassignable;
- N when no qualifying E/A/S family is represented.

P is a documented order-pattern proxy, not intent, recommendation, scheduling, completed therapy or ground truth. The primary clear-plan stratum is P in {E,A,S}; M and N are retained for descriptive and secondary full-cohort analyses, not silently treated as a reliable “no plan.”

For a primary clear-plan admission, an outcome procedure with family Y in {E,A,S} is concordant C when Y=P and discordant Q when Y != P. X is Q because it has no matching single E/A/S proxy. Thus Q means “no matching documented pathway proxy,” not unexpected, unplanned, inappropriate or clinically discordant care. D and censoring remain competing events. The secondary all-cohort target defines Q as any E/A/S/X outcome for which P is not the same single family (including P=N or M), and C only when P=Y in {E,A,S}; it reports P=N/M separately so missing documentation is not conflated with a negative clinical plan.

The solver must show exact dictionary terms, exclusions, family counts, order-status distributions, duplicate rates, plan-family alignment and the proportion of P=E/A/S/M/N before any model result. If routine, duplicate, cancelled or unassignable records dominate qualifying proxies, the plan-conditioned interpretation is inconclusive.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

B0 is a transparent cause-specific pooled-logistic competing-risk model with six-hour bins and one cause head each for C, Q and D (plus X reporting/sensitivity as specified by the event labeling). B1 adds the prespecified early-lab summaries above to B0. Both use identical eligible patients, outcome bins, preprocessing, missingness handling, P strata, patient split, fitting/selection discipline and locked test evaluation. B1 is the primary laboratory increment comparator.

The substantive alternative is an interpretable irregular continuous-time coupled-state model, fit on exactly the same patient records and split as B0/B1. It has two low-dimensional latent coordinates labelled hepatic-reserve and renal-stress only as modelling constructs, assay-specific offsets/noise (not pooled units), patient shrinkage, irregular elapsed-time observation updates and an observation-intensity component. It uses the same exact assay tokens, timestamps, qualitative/missingness indicators, density inputs, P and all B0 covariates, with cause-specific C/Q/D (and X sensitivity) heads. It estimates latent levels, recent changes, coupled movement, plan-conditioned risks and posterior/predictive uncertainty. The model can reveal nonlinear timing, coupled hepatic/renal movement and uncertainty caused by irregular sampling that first/last/slope summaries lose. These labels are not diagnoses or validated physiologic states.

The alternative must be compared on the same target, same P stratum(s), same patient-level split and same locked predictions, not selected because it is more complex. Report whether it improves held-out calibration, integrated Brier/CIF error, Q-versus-C risk stratification and the primary H estimate beyond B1; a small predictive gain without a stable discordance estimate is not substantive support. A GRU/temporal-attention model is explicitly deferred: it could model ordering interactions, but the immediate uncertainty is whether an interpretable coupled state adds information about plan mismatch; revisit it only as a new child if the state model leaves structured residual timing error. Text/image models are deferred because local images are unavailable, clinical documents have no native time and lexical extraction is unvalidated for this endpoint.

## Splits, uncertainty and analysis

Assign every record by `患者主索引` to fitting buckets 0–59, selection buckets 60–69 and locked test buckets 70–79; buckets 80–99 are inaccessible. No patient crosses partitions. Fit all model parameters and frozen dictionaries only in 0–59; choose assay semantics, dictionary details, state dimension, regularization, binning and observation-process terms using 0–59 plus 60–69; lock all preprocessing before one evaluation in 70–79.

Report on locked test patients:

- cause-specific six-hour hazards and competing-risk CIFs for C, Q, D, the original E/A/S/X labels and administrative censoring;
- standardized absolute CIFs and B1-minus-B0 contrasts for Q and C, H=Delta_Q-Delta_C, and secondary E/A/S contrasts;
- integrated Brier score, cause-specific Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and observed-versus-predicted CIF in prespecified risk bins; AUROC/PR-AUC are secondary only;
- results separately for P=E, P=A and P=S when supported, plus P=M/N descriptive strata and the full-cohort secondary definition;
- paired patient-level bootstrap 95% intervals with at least 500 resamples for B1-B0, state-B1, CIFs, H and the clinically relevant secondary contrasts. Preserve competing events and refit or use a documented locked-prediction bootstrap procedure consistently;
- event prevalence, event support, missingness, exact assay support, measurement density and plan-proxy support, with no claim of external validation.

The primary supportive pattern is a coherent, held-out laboratory increment for Q larger than C, H point estimate at least 0.01 with a two-sided interval excluding 0, adequate Q/C support, calibrated B1 improvement over B0, and persistence across P=E/A/S, assay/dictionary leave-one-out, measurement-intensity adjustment, service/era descriptions and timestamp falsifications. State-model support additionally requires a stable H or Q calibration/CIF improvement beyond B1 with uncertainty; improved AUC alone is insufficient.

Adverse evidence is a null or opposite H, poor held-out calibration, no laboratory increment, disappearance after adjustment for measurement intensity or P, persistence after time permutation, negative-control endpoint association, strong dependence on one order/procedure string, department or era, or instability under duplicate/time handling. Inconclusive evidence is fewer than 100 locked Q or C events, sparse P strata, high X or missing procedure times, nonconvergence, wide intervals unable to distinguish a meaningful discordance contrast from zero, dominant routine/cancelled/unassignable orders, incomplete discharge capture, or unfrozen dictionary semantics. Negative findings remain scientifically informative.

## Falsification and leakage tests

The solver must predeclare and emit:

1. A proof that every primary lab observation has native `检验时间` in [0,24) and every primary procedure start is in [72,144); no discharge field or post-outcome variable is a predictor.
2. Within-patient permutation of lab timestamps while preserving assay/value pairs and observation count; a genuine timing/state result should attenuate, while persistence indicates measurement-intensity or patient-membership signal.
3. Removal/addition of measurement count, missingness, gap and density terms; report whether H is driven by the observation process.
4. Duplicate and same-assay/same-time handling sensitivity, midnight-start removal and procedure-time missingness bounds.
5. Leave-one-assay-out, leave-one-order-family/string-out and leave-one-procedure-family-out analyses; report whether one sparse dictionary member drives Q.
6. Plan-proxy label permutation among admissions matched on admitting service and admission era, preserving order volume; a result that survives wholesale P scrambling is evidence against a plan-specific interpretation.
7. A negative-control endpoint built from qualifying-independent routine/central-line/venipuncture workflow terms, with no claim that it is clinically null; strong replication weakens specificity.
8. Retention of X and D as competitors and comparison with naive analyses that ignore competing risks only as a labelled sensitivity.
9. The selected-but-conditioned continued-inpatient-at-72-hour landmark analysis as sensitivity, explicitly contrasted with the admission-anchored primary.
10. A post-landmark order/medication audit only; no post-72-hour order or medication enters a primary predictor.
11. Calibration and H by service and admission era as internal domain descriptions, not external validation.
