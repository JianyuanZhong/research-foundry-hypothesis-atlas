# UK Biobank metabolic progression: support-aware confirmation of assessment-linked change

## Successor purpose and scientific deliverable

This is a targeted successor to `[prior hypothesis]` (episode 26, parent selected by the Lead). It preserves the parent’s five-source UK Biobank binding, the d1 current-level versus d0-to-d1 change estimand, the single-domain metabolic-progression question, competing death, source-integrity checks, participant-level locked split, and explicit clinical evidence limits.

It repairs the latest confirmed-transition endpoint. A rule requiring two records 30–365 days apart is clinically sensible as a robustness check, but a naïve implementation can condition on a post-d1 first code, discard people who have too little follow-up to confirm, or call a boundary-limited record “unconfirmed.” The repaired endpoint is a support-aware confirmation process:

- the d1 cohort is formed without knowledge of any future code or confirmation;
- the first post-d1 code is an intermediate, time-varying state, not an eligibility requirement;
- confirmation is a later same-domain code/date pair 30–365 days after that first code;
- a death before confirmation is a competing absorbing event;
- a first code less than 365 days before the observation boundary is boundary-censored for confirmation opportunity, not a negative confirmation;
- a full 365-day observed opportunity with no second record is reported as no confirmation within the prespecified window, not as disease absence.

The actual future scientific deliverable is a frozen participant-level exposure and event-process file, newly fitted matched B0 and M_change models for both recorded and confirmation-qualified endpoints, locked predictions and uncertainty, confirmation-opportunity and boundary diagnostics, falsification outputs, and an output-linked conclusion classified as supportive, adverse, or inconclusive. Readiness, a source audit, convergence, a nonzero coefficient, or a gain in one metric is not completion. No full-cohort fit or result has been run or is claimed here.

## Unresolved clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

The strongest evidence available to this proposal supports a narrower claim: baseline metabolic indices are associated with later coded cardiometabolic disease, and UK Biobank analyses can describe disease progression with survival or multistate models. A frozen Europe PMC search inspected for this proposal returned a 2026 UK Biobank abstract reporting associations of a triglyceride-glucose index with disease and multistate progression; that result concerns a different index, population definition, and outcome and does not establish the incremental value of two-panel within-person change. The search record is `[source checksum]`; only its returned bibliographic/abstract material was inspected, not a full article audit.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among UK Biobank participants aged 40–69 with exactly one established coded domain—diabetes, cardiovascular disease, or chronic kidney disease—who have a valid second assessment and remain in the same domain at d1, does assessment-linked change in HbA1c, triglycerides, and HDL add route-specific prognostic information beyond d1 levels, domain identity, disease duration, and prespecified covariates for a first subsequent additional domain, and does that signal persist when the additional domain is required to have a second same-domain code/date record within 30–365 days?

This is an internal prognostic claim in the observed, repeat-assessment, assay-complete population. It is not a causal effect of changing a biomarker, a biological slope, adjudicated disease onset, proof of two independent clinical encounters, a recommendation to repeat testing, a monitoring-benefit claim, or a policy threshold.

The clinically important distinction is between a one-off administrative transition and a coded condition that persists long enough to generate a second qualifying record. If current values predict both, the evidence is more consistent with reproducible coded progression; if change predicts only the first code, the apparent signal is endpoint- or ascertainment-fragile. If current levels absorb the change signal, that supports the narrower practical conclusion that two-panel change adds little internal prognostic information for this selected purpose.

The primary incremental estimand is the contrast between B0 and M_change under the same cohort, state process, censoring, split, weighting, and evaluation. E1 (earliest recorded transition) is the recorded-code anchor. E2 (first confirmation-qualified transition) is the prespecified robustness estimand and is interpreted only with its confirmation-opportunity and boundary diagnostics. A result may be supportive for E1 but adverse or inconclusive for robust E2 progression.

## E1 recorded transition

For every target domain absent at d1, identify its earliest valid paired code/date after d1. If exactly one new domain shares the earliest date, classify `SECOND_DOMAIN_UNIQUE` with its route from the existing domain. If two or more new domains share the earliest date, classify `SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI` and retain the member set; never assign a multi-domain tie to an arbitrary route. A same-day death takes precedence over a new-domain event in the primary rule; reverse precedence is a labeled sensitivity.

Let `C_obs` be the maximum finite date among the outcome date arrays `41280-*`, `41281-*`, and death `40000-*` used in the analysis. `C_obs` is an observed-data boundary, not automatically a validated administrative closure. Without source-level closure evidence, report an observed-boundary estimand and do not make an unqualified five-year incidence, calibration, or alert claim. A death is absorbing and competing, not ordinary censoring.

## E2 confirmation-qualified transition: repaired estimand

E2 is a confirmation-qualified coded endpoint, not adjudicated disease persistence.

For each domain k absent at d1:

1. Let t1k be the earliest valid post-d1 paired code/date for k. Multiple slots on the same date are one date occurrence, not independent confirmation.
2. Let t2k be the earliest later valid paired code/date for the same k satisfying 30 ≤ t2k − t1k ≤ 365 days. The two records must have distinct dates; distinct slots with the same date do not qualify.
3. If t2k exists before the observation boundary, k has a confirmation-qualified transition at t2k. The event date is t2k, while t1k is retained as the first-code time.
4. The participant’s E2 event is the earliest t2k across absent domains. Ties at the earliest confirmation date are `CONFIRMED_MULTI` with the member set; a unique domain is `CONFIRMED_UNIQUE`. A same-day death takes precedence over confirmation.
5. Death before t2k, including death while a domain is pending confirmation, is an absorbing competing event. No confirmation is imputed after death.

The solver must represent E2 as a joint state process rather than censoring everyone who fails to confirm:

- state 0: d1, alive, stable in the established single domain, no confirmed additional domain;
- state P(k): first code for absent domain k has occurred and is awaiting a possible confirmation;
- state P(S): a simultaneous first-code set S is being tracked when multiple absent domains first occur on the same date;
- state C(k) or C(S): the first confirmation-qualified transition, with unique or tied member set;
- state U(k): a complete 365-day opportunity elapsed after t1k without a qualifying second record;
- state D: death;
- state B(k): confirmation opportunity is censored by the observation boundary before 365 days.

The participant-level analysis may use a parsimonious supported implementation of this graph, but it must preserve the distinction between P, U, B, and D. U(k) is known “no confirmation within 365 days” for that domain; it is not disease absence and is not ordinary censoring. B(k) is boundary censoring and must never be relabeled U(k). A participant can have U(k) for one domain while another absent domain later produces a confirmation; the implementation must not incorrectly remove the person from all future confirmation risk. If route/state support is too sparse for the full graph, the solver may report only the aggregate first-confirmation estimand, with route claims marked inconclusive; it may not silently revert to a future-confirmation-selected cohort.

The complete-opportunity rule is explicit. For a first code at t1k, define wk=t1k+365 days:

- if t1k < wk ≤ C_obs and the participant is alive/observable through wk with no t2k, record U(k);
- if C_obs < wk and no t2k is observed, record B(k) at C_obs; do not call this lack of confirmation opportunity;
- if death occurs before wk or before t2k, record the appropriate death transition;
- if t2k occurs before wk and before C_obs, record confirmation at t2k.

No participant is excluded from the d1 cohort because t1k is late or because wk lies beyond C_obs. The proportion and covariate distribution of U versus B pending episodes are reported. A secondary complete-opportunity analysis may restrict to episodes with wk≤C_obs, but because this is selected by a post-d1 event time it is descriptive/sensitivity only and cannot be the primary confirmation estimand. No inverse-probability correction can manufacture missing confirmation records without an observed administrative closure mechanism.

For both E1 and E2, follow the d1 process until the applicable event, death, B/censoring, C_obs, or five years after d1. A five-year statement requires documented administrative closure. Otherwise the solver reports only horizons supported by the observed boundary and labels any five-year display as conditional on the unverified closure assumption.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

Every E1 and E2 model uses the same eligible participants, state/event definitions, death process, time zero, censoring, split, transformations, weights, uncertainty procedure, and locked evaluation. The comparison changes only the information available to the fitted prognostic model.

### B0: simple current-level baseline

B0 fits the supported route/state-specific cause-specific hazards required by E1 and the supported transitions of the E2 state process. Predictors are d1 HbA1c, triglycerides, HDL, existing domain, existing-domain duration, age, sex, d1–d0 gap, BMI `21001-0.0`, SBP `4080-0.0`, DBP `4079-0.0`, smoking `20116-0.0`, and center `54-0.0`. Continuous terms use fit-frozen restricted cubic splines; categorical missingness uses a fit-defined rule and is reported. B0 contains no d0-to-d1 change.

For E2, B0 is fitted jointly across the d1-to-first-code and pending-to-confirmation portions of the supported process, or with an explicitly equivalent multistate estimator. It must generate d1-origin predictions for confirmation by each reported horizon without conditioning on eventual t1 or t2.

### M_change: mechanism-inspired coordinated change plus discordance

This alternative is not a validated biological latent model. It tests a substantive distinction: whether a coherent deterioration signal or marker-specific disagreement contains information that current d1 levels lose.

Let z_H, z_T, and z_R be fit-partition standardized annualized changes in HbA1c, triglycerides, and reverse-HDL. Define:

- C=(z_H+z_T+z_R)/sqrt(3), coordinated deterioration;
- D1=(z_H−z_T)/sqrt(2);
- D2=(z_H+z_T−2z_R)/sqrt(6).

If empirical covariance requires it, estimate an orthogonalizing transform in fit data and freeze it. Fit `M_common=B0+C` and `M_change=B0+C+D1+D2` using the identical route/state architecture and only prespecified domain interactions where support passes the gate.

The joint M_change-versus-B0 contrast tests whether dated change adds prognostic information. M_common-versus-B0 tests whether a coherent burden component adds information. M_change-versus-M_common tests whether discordance adds beyond coordinated worsening. The alternative can reveal a shared movement that separate current-level terms obscure, or a marker-specific pattern that a single burden score loses. If D states/routes are unsupported, the discordance claim is inconclusive rather than omitted without disclosure.

A fit-only residualized-change sensitivity regresses each change on its d1 value and gap, then refits the frozen pipeline. Main and biological_samples copies cannot identify assay reliability because they are duplicated export fields, not independent replicates; no reliability correction is claimed.

A recurrent/attention model is deferred for a scientific reason: two verified assay panels do not support meaningful sequence structure, and additional capacity cannot solve date-proxy timing, confirmation opportunity, or return selection. Reconsider only with at least three verified dated assay panels or a bounded diagnostic showing stable incremental scientific information beyond C/D under the same endpoint checks. A formal latent measurement-error model is deferred until assay replicates, laboratory QC, or a validated external error model exists.

### Interpretation rules

Supportive for a specified endpoint requires:

1. at least 20 events for that endpoint overall and at least 5 locked-test events, with no claimed route/state below the prespecified support floor of 10 fit/tuning events and 3 locked-test events;
2. stable five-source joins, source rule, event/tie construction, and adequate IPCW positivity/ESS where weighting is used;
3. a prespecified worsening-direction interval excluding the null for C or discordance, plus a locked-test improvement in paired Brier/calibration or at least 10% relative net benefit at a threshold frozen before locked evaluation;
4. no reversal under assay-completeness weighting, source-concordant restriction, or the one-year lag, and no comparable signal in appendicitis or within-state permutation;
5. for E2 specifically, a reported count and distribution of complete versus boundary-limited opportunities; no five-year language without closure provenance.

A supportive common-burden conclusion requires C to add beyond B0 without requiring D. A supportive discordance conclusion requires D1/D2 to add beyond C with adequate route/state support. A supportive robust-progression conclusion requires the E2 result to be supported; E1-only improvement is not sufficient.

Adverse means no incremental change information on a supported endpoint, worse locked performance, disappearance after duration or selection/source adjustment, a comparable negative-control or permutation signal, or an adequately supported E1 signal that disappears or reverses under E2. This falsifies the selected conditional coded-event prognostic claim, not all metabolic biology and not a causal intervention hypothesis.

Inconclusive means inadequate E1/E2 events, sparse routes or state transitions, failed positivity/ESS, unstable joins or ties, unresolved date disagreement, substantial E2 boundary limitation, poor convergence/calibration, intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, or materially divergent selection/timing/phenotype sensitivities. If E2 is underpowered, report E1 as a recorded-code result and mark robust clinical confirmation inconclusive. A threshold result without expert-approved action and clinical-utility evidence is inconclusive for policy value.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes/headers, table and field bindings, joins, canonical-source agreement, flow, paired code/date construction, E1/E2 state logic, confirmation opportunity classification, death handling, C_obs/boundary handling, fitted models, locked predictions, uncertainty, and falsification outputs. Clinical onset, whether records represent independent encounters, code validity, reasons for return or testing, treatment/adherence, severity, causal effects, monitoring benefit, transportability, nonreturner generalization, and policy utility require adjudication, unavailable evidence, expert review, or another study.

## Alternatives, demonstrations, seed, and revisit record

- The simple B0 is retained because it answers the clinically legible question of whether current d1 values are sufficient.
- M_change is retained over the parent’s unequal marker-change versus one-axis comparison because its C/D decomposition has comparable degrees of freedom and isolates coordinated worsening from discordance under identical route/death/state hazards.
- E2 is retained as a confirmation-qualified robustness estimand, but repaired into a d1-origin joint state process with explicit U, B, and D states. The evidence that would justify revisiting it is verified administrative closure, independent encounter identifiers, adjudicated outcomes, or enough confirmation support for stable route/state estimates.
- A recurrent/attention sequence model is deferred because only two verified panels are available. Revisit with at least three dated panels and evidence of incremental information beyond C/D.
- A latent measurement-error model is deferred because duplicated main/biological-sample export fields are not independent replicates. Revisit with assay reliability/QC or an external validated error model.
- Genomics, imaging, accelerometry, proteomics beyond the listed assays, narrative text, treatment/adherence, and external validation are not verified dependencies and are not silently substituted.
- Causal monitoring or treatment-policy analyses are deferred because clinical utility, treatment, adherence, adjudicated onset, and expert thresholds are unavailable.

It motivated this line but does not prove it.
