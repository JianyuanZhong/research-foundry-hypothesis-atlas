# Coupled-state, fixed-capacity ranking of encounter-documentary HCC M2 from eventual report content

## Scientific question, evidence boundary, and advance

The decision this experiment informs is whether the stored HCC report corpus contains sufficiently stable, clinically interpretable signal to justify a later prospective silent-mode study of a fixed-capacity multidisciplinary review list. It does **not** create such a list for care.

The strongest claim the available evidence already supports is documentary: HCC snapshot `[source checksum]` links patient and encounter records, source-documented procedure rows with start/end fields, CT/MRI examination rows with acquisition-like starts and eventual stored text, untimed same-encounter pathology text, and dated procedure/medication/order evidence. This permits a retrospective, future-defined resection-plus-HCC-pathology frame and ranking of an encounter-documentary M2 label. It does not establish operation completion, a clinical decision time, report finalization/release/view/version, pathology timing or specimen identity, biological M2, review opportunity, clinician action, treatment, recurrence, survival, harm, or benefit.

Current evidence motivates but does not answer the question. Xu et al. (Cancer Medicine 2026;15:e71749, DOI 10.1002/cam4.71749; inspected full-text XML source `[source checksum]`) derived a simple MVI score from meta-analytic predictors and reported C-statistics of 0.805 and 0.808 in two Chinese validation cohorts of 538 and 111 people. That study tests a different endpoint, predictors, population, and performance target; it does not test eventual report wording, M2 ranking at fixed capacity, temporal transport in this source, or clinical benefit. Neves and Soares (Cancers 2026;18:2028, DOI 10.3390/cancers18132028; inspected full-text XML source `[source checksum]`) found 35/36 reviewed HCC early-recurrence AI studies retrospective, only 6/36 externally validated, with calibration and clinical utility inconsistently evaluated. That broader literature reinforces the need for temporal validation and bounded claims but does not validate this experiment.

The unresolved, falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In each of 2020 and 2021, among adults in a source-documented first eligible HCC resection episode with no recorded HCC treatment in the preceding year, a locked model using blinded-reader labels of what eventual stored CT/MRI report text says will capture more than five additional encounter-documentary M2 cases per 100 fixed ranking positions over both a same-fit fully masked control and an independently fitted nonsemantic baseline, for every prespecified operation-derived cutoff and every coherent source/outcome state; meanwhile, a documentation-surface model will not clear that five-per-100 margin.

This advances existing knowledge by testing incremental *stored-content ranking potential* under a fixed review capacity and by requiring that the apparent gain survive a documentation-form negative control, temporal holdouts, joint source uncertainty, and set-valued pathology. It is a retrospective corpus experiment, not preoperative prediction performance, referral performance, a causal effect, or service utility.

The research-ambition availability README was inspected. The Cell demonstration's main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; only metadata and a supplement are present, and no unavailable paper text is claimed as read.

## Required population and temporal boundaries

The person key is `患者主索引`. Same-encounter linkage is the pair `(患者主索引, 就诊号)`. Every derived row retains source file, physical row ordinal, exact-row multiplicity, deduplication group, unmatched-join status, and a backpointer. Exact duplicate payload rows may be collapsed only after that ledger is written. There is at most one index episode per person in each coherent state.

Before outcomes or model scores are inspected, two hepatobiliary surgeons and one surgical informatician, blinded to test-year outcomes and scores, freeze dictionaries for eligible liver resections and prior HCC resection/transplant/TACE/ablation/radiotherapy/targeted therapy/immunotherapy. They classify the values of procedure `手术` jointly with `手术来源`; a source label is not interpreted as anesthesia, completion, or clinical intent.

A person-state is eligible when all of the following hold:

1. age from the linked encounter `年龄` is at least 18;
2. the selected procedure alternative is that person's earliest source-documented eligible resection episode;
3. at least one pathology composite from the same encounter explicitly documents HCC after stored-text adjudication; and
4. patient-wide dated procedures, medications, and non-drug orders contain no recorded prior treatment in `[c_d-365 days, c_d)`.

This is recorded-treatment-naive and conditioned on a future-observed procedure-plus-pathology frame. People without these records, including non-resected candidates, are neither controls nor outcome-negative cases. Untimed diagnoses may corroborate vocabulary only; they cannot date treatment, determine negatives, or enter a predictor.

For `d ∈ {12,24,48,72}` hours, define `c_d=t_op-d`. The 24-hour offset is central; all four are confirmatory robustness coordinates. `c_d` is an analytical cutoff, not a scheduling, decision, referral, report-release, or view time.

Procedure `开始时间` is a point only after format/source validation when it is non-midnight and internally consistent. Date-only or unvalidated midnight values represent `[date,date+24h)`. Competing eligible procedure rows and discordant `手术来源` values remain alternatives. If start is absent but a linked end and encounter bounds exist, they define a conservative finite operation interval, never an imputed start; if no finite source-supported interval can be formed, that person's boundary state is unresolved and any result depending on it is inconclusive. Only equivalence-class boundaries induced by source timestamps, window edges, and calendar splits need enumeration.

Development is 2015–2018, 2019 is tuning/locking, and 2020 and 2021 are separate untouched tests. Partition is determined by the admissible operation episode/clock. Any person whose alternatives reach a later partition is quarantined from every earlier fit. A person ambiguous between 2020 and 2021 is one coupled alternative, never duplicated. Data from 2022 onward are drift/ascertainment audits only.

## One coupled uncertainty state space

For each person `i`, construct a finite set `Ω_i`. One element `ω_i` jointly fixes:

- selected encounter and eligible procedure row(s);
- operation-clock interval/equivalence class and calendar partition;
- whether this is the earliest eligible episode and whether the person is in or out of the retrospective frame;
- prior-treatment classification at the same `c_d`;
- examination grain, component membership, modality, acquisition interval, cutoff eligibility, and latest-unit selection;
- the full-unit stored-text reader labels, including any unresolved label alternatives;
- the exact same-encounter pathology-row composite, HCC attribution, and `Y_i∈{0,1}` documentary-M2 label; and
- in secondary access analyses, that person's whole-unit CT/MRI exposure assignment.

A global state `s=(ω_1,…,ω_n)` is feasible only when all source-sharing constraints, duplicate/backpointer constraints, access-count constraints, and one-record-per-person constraints hold. Every population size, model input, top-K set, and outcome count is evaluated in one feasible `s`. Extrema over `s` may be solved by enumeration or optimization, but minima or maxima from incompatible clocks, report partitions, memberships, or pathology completions may never be combined. The state ledger records a stable state ID and all parent source-row IDs.

This single construction is the uncertainty analysis. Separate “best clock,” “best report,” and “best label” envelopes are forbidden.

## Examination units and blinded stored-text adjudication

Build report units before filtering. The preferred indivisible unit is `(患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号)` for nonblank `检查号`, retaining all component rows. A blank accession is identity-unknown and cannot expose semantics. Conflicting modalities, component starts separated by more than 24 hours, repeated accession evidence, or merged-identity evidence create coupled alternatives between the complete accession bundle and a deterministic normalized `(患者主索引, 就诊号, normalized 检查, calendar date of 开始时间)` partition. The normalized partition is a sensitivity alternative, not permission to cherry-pick component rows.

An examination start is point or interval coded by the same non-midnight/date-only rule. A unit is eligible only when its state lies in `[c_d-90 days,c_d)`; overlap with either edge creates in/out alternatives. Within a state retain at most the latest eligible CT and latest eligible MRI by maximum component start, then raw ordinal, then frozen salted patient hash. Concatenate every component's `检查所见` and `检查诊断` in physical-row order. Exposure is always of a complete selected unit, never selected phrases.

Two Chinese-reading abdominal radiologists independently label every selected unit while blinded to pathology, model score, partition, and the other reader. A frozen bilingual rubric asks only what the stored wording says about: maximum lesion diameter, lesion count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites. Codes are present, explicitly absent, uncertain, not mentioned, or source text unavailable. A third blinded radiologist reviews every disagreement.

These labels are documentary annotations, not image findings, diagnoses, biological truth, or evidence of real-time availability. No radiology-feature PPV, sensitivity, “accuracy,” or truth gate is permitted because there are no images or independent feature gold standard. Agreement and disagreement counts may be reported as reproducibility descriptions only. If the third reader cannot resolve wording, the case is labeled inconclusive for that feature and all rubric-consistent codes remain alternatives in `Ω_i`; it is never forced to present/absent. Missing or incomplete reader work makes the semantic result inconclusive. A supportive study result must remain supportive across all residual finite label alternatives.

Primary exposure `E` reveals all eventual stored text/reader labels of selected units after cohort construction. It does not imply that text existed before `c_d`. Blank text is source-text-unavailable. Exposure `0` masks every semantic label to source-text-unavailable while retaining nonsemantic acquisition facts.

## Encounter-documentary M2

For the selected encounter, compose `病理`, `检查所见`, and `检查诊断` over all exact-deduplicated pathology rows in raw order. The table has neither pathology time nor specimen/accession/slide/block/sampling identifiers. The outcome is therefore *encounter-documentary M2*, not postoperative, resection-specimen, reconstructed histology, or biological M2.

Two Chinese-reading pathologists, blinded to reports, scores, and model outputs, independently label the composite. `Y={1}` requires an M2 expression anchored by context to HCC microvascular invasion grade; `Y={0}` requires anchored M0/M1 without anchored M2. A third pathologist reviews disagreements. Conflicts, boilerplate, unanchored mentions, inadequate text, uncertain HCC attribution, or disagreement remaining after the third reader yield `Y={0,1}` in `Ω_i`. Missing time, specimen identity, and sampling adequacy cannot be adjudicated and are never reconstructed. Unknowns are not dropped or coded negative.

Report all-row documentary, reader-adjudicated, and set-valued outcome counts. At least 85% of each test-year cohort must have an anchored reader-adjudicated grade, and the lower event envelope must contain at least 50 M2 cases per test year. Otherwise the experiment is inconclusive. This is an ascertainment gate, not evidence about biological MVI prevalence.

## Models, baselines, fixed K, and estimand

All preprocessing is learned in 2015–2018 and frozen after 2019. Continuous variables use development medians and robust scaling; categorical levels with fewer than 20 development persons map to “other”; missingness is explicit. The coefficient grid is elastic-net logistic regression with mixing `{0,0.5,1}` and 30 log-spaced penalties fixed before fitting.

- `B`, independently fitted nonsemantic baseline: age, sex, CT/MRI/other acquisition indicators, eligible-unit count, acquisition-to-`c_d` recency, component count, identity ambiguity, source-text-missing, and development-frequency `机器型号` category.
- `C`, independently fitted content model: all `B` features plus the blinded-reader documentary labels above and explicit semantic-unavailable indicators.
- `C0`: the fitted `C` model applied without refitting after every semantic input is set to source-text-unavailable. It is a same-fit attribution control.
- `F`, independently fitted documentation-surface negative control: `B` plus whole-unit character count; presence of `检查所见` and `检查诊断`; digit, punctuation, whitespace, and Chinese-character proportions; component-row count; and identity/source-format indicators. It excludes words, n-grams, embeddings, selected phrases, and every reader label.

Pathology, diagnoses, labs, clinical documents, identifiers, year, post-cutoff data, report-release assumptions, and test outcomes are forbidden predictors. `B`, `C`, and `F` are fit separately in 2015–2018 by minimizing the sum across people of the maximum logistic loss over each person's admissible development alternatives. Hyperparameters are chosen in 2019 by maximizing the minimum fixed-K capture across coherent 2019 states, with ties favoring stronger regularization then a frozen grid order. Calibration slope/intercept are reported as secondary diagnostics and cannot change ranking or conclusions.

For state `s`, offset `d`, and year `a`, let `N_a(s,d)` be index size constructed without M grade or report semantics. Before test outcomes or text are read, freeze

`N_ref = min_{s,d,a∈2015,…,2019} N_a(s,d)`, and `K_q=floor(q N_ref)`, `q∈{.05,.10,.20}`.

The confirmatory capacity is `q=.10`; the others are sensitivity capacities. Require `N_ref≥500`, `K_.10≥50`, and at least `K_q` people in every state reported for that q. Scores are ordered by value, frozen salted patient hash, then raw episode backpointer.

For `M∈{C(E),C0,B,F(E)}`,

`T_M(s,y,d,q)=100 K_q^{-1} Σ_i Y_i(s) 1{i∈topK_M(s,y,d,q)}`.

It is encounter-documentary M2 cases per 100 fixed positions in the future-defined source cohort. The primary contrasts are

- `D_C=T_C(E)-T_C0` (same-fit semantic attribution),
- `A_C=T_C(E)-T_B` (independent nonsemantic comparison), and
- `A_F=T_F(E)-T_B` (documentation-surface negative control).

Because all models select exactly K, utilization is identically one; these are differences in list composition, not workload or mediation. Secondary baselines are age/sex-only, acquisition-only, first-K by frozen analytical order, 1,000 seeded random-K lists, and an outcome-aware oracle labeled as an unattainable retrospective ceiling.

For each year and offset define the robust coordinates `θ_D=min_s D_C(s)`, `θ_A=min_s A_C(s)`, and `θ_F=max_s A_F(s)`. The confirmatory hypothesis is the conjunction `θ_D>5`, `θ_A>5`, and `θ_F≤5` for both years and all four offsets at q=.10.

## Exact patient-level whole-report allocation bounds

Report access is absent from the source and does not enter the primary estimand. Secondary sensitivity asks what results are possible under exact patient-level allocations of eventual whole-unit content.

Within every coherent source state, classify people as O, CT-only, MRI-only, or CT+MRI. For each `(α_CT,α_MRI)∈{0,.25,.50,.75,1}²`, derive exact integer CT exposure counts jointly for CT-only and dual strata, and MRI counts jointly for MRI-only and dual strata, by Hamilton largest remainder: floor each stratum's `α n_g`, set the modality total to nearest integer with exact .5 ties downward, then assign remaining units by descending fractional remainder with a frozen single-before-dual tie order. Store the resulting four integers, not only fractions.

The allocation set contains every binary patient assignment satisfying exactly:

- the CT count among CT-only people;
- the MRI count among MRI-only people;
- the CT count among dual-modality people; and
- the MRI count among dual-modality people.

CT/MRI overlap among dual-modality people is unrestricted except by those margins, so all Fréchet-compatible overlaps are included. O members remain in the denominator. Allocation reveals or masks each selected whole unit; it cannot reveal phrases or components.

For each feasible allocation, apply already-fitted `C` and `F` using precomputed none/CT/MRI/both score states. `B` is unchanged. Jointly over allocation, source state, reader alternatives, and pathology label define min/max bounds for `D_C`, `A_C`, and diagnostic `A_F`. A single mixed-integer program uses one state variable per person, one access-state variable, model-specific top-K indicators, exactly-K constraints, exact marginal counts, and indicator constraints enforcing the score-plus-tie top-K order. It must terminate at zero optimality gap and pass a post-solution rank audit. Timeout, nonzero gap, or incompatible solver solutions makes that cell inconclusive.

`(1,1)` must reproduce `C(E)` and `F(E)`; `(0,0)` must reproduce `C0` bitwise for C scores/ranks/counts. For every toy population with `N≤12`, MIP extrema must equal exhaustive patient-allocation enumeration. No fraction or allocation is called observed release, view, access probability, or missing-at-random. The coordinatewise-minimal robust cell may be reported only as a hypothetical stored-content frontier.

## Joint inference and falsification

Use 2,000 patient-cluster bootstrap refits. Each replicate resamples people and rebuilds population/state alternatives, development fits, 2019 selection, report units, reader-label alternatives, top-K lists, and pathology envelopes. Dictionaries, reader rubrics, feature set, penalty grid, K rule, tie rule, alpha grid, practical margin, and result rules remain frozen. More than 2.5% failed replicates makes the family inconclusive.

The primary simultaneous family contains `θ_D,θ_A,θ_F` for two test years and four offsets at q=.10. In each bootstrap replicate recompute the relevant state minimum or maximum; never bootstrap already-marginalized rows. Construct a single max-centered, two-sided band using the 97.5th percentile of the maximum absolute bootstrap deviation across all 24 coordinates. Report lower and upper simultaneous limits for every coordinate. This conservative band supports both lower-margin and upper-negative-control decisions without separate unadjusted tests.

The q=.05/.20 capacities form one separately labeled simultaneous sensitivity family. The 25 access cells form another separately labeled simultaneous family over both years, four offsets, and all three contrasts. They cannot rescue the primary family.

Run 1,000 semantic-block permutations in development/tuning, stratified by year, modality pattern, source-unit count, identity ambiguity, and frozen documentation-surface bins. Permute complete reader-label vectors while preserving report surface, refit C, and recompute the joint minimum content contrasts. The observed content result must exceed the familywise one-sided .025 null envelope; F is not permuted. This is a dependence check, not proof of biological meaning.

Required computation/adjudication gates are: exact source and code hashes; live-header match; raw-ordinal/backpointer completeness; one record per person; unmatched-row audit; full finite coupled-state ledger; quarantine and future-row invariance; frozen dictionaries and rubrics; complete two-reader review and third-reader review of every disagreement; residual inconclusive reader labels retained as state alternatives; N/K feasibility; anchored-grade and lower-event requirements; complete whole-unit masking; bitwise `C(0)=C0`; independent B/C/F fits; zero forbidden lineage; semantic permutation rejection; exact-K/tie audit; allocation MIP optimality and small-N brute-force equality; and bootstrap failure at most 2.5%. Feature agreement has no PPV/sensitivity/accuracy gate.

Result rules are mechanical:

- **Supportive:** every gate passes; every primary simultaneous lower limit for `θ_D` and `θ_A` is strictly greater than 5; and every simultaneous upper limit for `θ_F` is at most 5. This supports robust retrospective eventual-corpus content ranking potential only.
- **Adverse:** gates pass and either (a) any required simultaneous upper limit for `θ_D` or `θ_A` is at most 5, falsifying the universal content-margin claim, or (b) any simultaneous lower limit for `θ_F` is greater than 5, falsifying the documentation-surface condition. An upper content limit below zero supports worse documentary ranking in that coordinate only. Mixed years/offsets are heterogeneity, not a general null.
- **Inconclusive:** neither decisive rule holds; any interval crosses its decision boundary; coherent states or reader/pathology alternatives straddle a boundary; or any provenance, adjudication, feasibility, optimization, permutation, temporal, or bootstrap gate fails. Gate failure is never evidence against biological MVI.

Access cells receive their own supportive/adverse/inconclusive labels using their simultaneous family. An empty supportive frontier says no enumerated allocation is allocation-robust; it does not estimate actual access.

## Required derived data, verifier contract, and claim limits

Execution requires: frozen surgeon/informatician dictionaries; a radiology annotation file with unit ID, two independent labels, disagreement flags, third-reader label, residual-inconclusive code, reader blinding attestation, and source backpointers; an analogous pathology composite annotation file; a complete coupled-state ledger; model/preprocessing objects; bootstrap/permutation/MIP traces; and a machine-readable result table. Missing expert files are essential unavailable evidence and make the semantic experiment inconclusive, not automatically negative.

Every conclusion row must carry year, d, q, K, N envelope, model/contrast, point estimate, simultaneous lower/upper limits, state/allocation IDs, top-K documentary-M2 envelope, gate vector, source/code/model hashes, and a raw-output locator. The verifier must derive the allowed conclusion label from those fields; free-text conclusions without locators fail.

Mandatory fixtures include:

1. live-header and source-hash mismatch failures, duplicate preservation, unmatched joins, and one-person uniqueness;
2. a midnight operation interval crossing a cutoff/year, an accession with conflicting component clocks/modalities, and an ambiguous pathology composite; only enumerated compatible joint states pass;
3. a counterexample where separately chosen favorable clock/report/pathology extrema produce a false margin; the mixed result must be rejected;
4. `K=100`, 40 versus 27 M2 gives 13 per 100; equal top-K counts give zero; swapping models negates the contrast; fewer than K is never padded;
5. deterministic tie ordering and invariance of development/tuning outputs to added 2020+ rows;
6. complete semantic masking yields bitwise-identical `C(0)` and `C0` features, scores, ranks, counts, and traces;
7. independent-fit checks for B/C/F and rejection of words, pathology, diagnoses, labs, year, identifiers, or post-cutoff lineage;
8. allocation endpoints reproduce E/0, arbitrary dual-modality overlap is included, exact marginal counts hold, and MIP equals exhaustive enumeration for every N≤12 fixture;
9. surface-preserving permutation keeps F inputs and formatting strata unchanged while semantic vectors move;
10. numerically correct supportive, adverse, and inconclusive examples map to the rules above; a correct computation labeled “preoperative availability,” “referral performance,” “biological M2,” “treatment effect,” “workflow benefit,” “recurrence reduction,” “survival benefit,” or “safe deployment” is rejected.

Automatic verification can establish provenance, joins, filters, state coupling, masking, fixed-K arithmetic, model lineage, optimization, bootstrap/permutation calculations, gates, and whether a submitted conclusion follows from computed outputs. It cannot establish operation completion, report access, pathology specimen identity/sampling adequacy, biological M2, clinical action, acceptable workload/tradeoffs, fairness, transportability, recurrence, survival, harm, or benefit.

A supportive result justifies collecting immutable report-version/finalization/release/view logs and testing a prospectively defined silent list that includes non-resected eligible candidates and measures capacity, clinician action, false-positive consequences, downstream care, and outcomes. Biological M2 requires specimen-linked timed pathology or primary slide/block review. Clinical benefit requires a separate prospective impact study and expert review. An adverse result discourages this stored-content ranking strategy in the tested source and years but does not prove radiology semantics are generally useless. An inconclusive result identifies whether the limiting evidence is source identity, timing, adjudication, label ascertainment, allocation, temporal drift, or precision.
