# HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B validation with a prospective assay-completion and downstream recorded-care audit

## Two-parent successor and substantive repair

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE population, event construction, exact B/P/event/Y clocks, annual forward blocks, locked nested ridge models, outcome-blind support funnel, assay-separated missing-Y identification analysis, and noncausal primary estimand. The primary question and estimand are unchanged:

> Among selected repeat-TACE episodes with a remote baseline assay B and a pre-repeat assay P, does P improve held-out prediction of the subsequent same-assay laboratory value Y beyond B and the locked pre-event core?

Albumin (`白蛋白`) and total bilirubin (`总胆红素`) remain separate assays throughout. The primary observed complete-triplet standardized held-out RMSE gain remains `(RMSE0-RMSE1)/SD_test(Y)`, with the inherited training-only preprocessing, inner patient-level tuning, fixed pre-origin predictions, patient-frequency bootstrap, and training-range all-BP missing-Y bounds. None of the added audit variables enters event selection, M0/M1 fitting, the primary test endpoint, its denominator, its bootstrap, or the deterministic bounds.

The remaining clinically consequential limitation is operational: an unobserved Y may mean that the assay was not completed in the source system, that the patient had no recorded contact, or that care occurred elsewhere. A favorable complete-case prognostic result is not operationally interpretable unless the assay-completion process and subsequent recorded care are described. This successor therefore adds a protocol-ready, prospective-time audit: define completion by the event-time assay deadline, then audit downstream recorded-care capture after that deadline. It converts missing-Y from a purely statistical nuisance into a measurable care-record pathway signal while refusing to call that signal completed care, a patient-important outcome, or a benefit.

No new HCC audit numbers are claimed here. All added results are downstream compiler outputs from the frozen sources.

## Strongest supported claim, unresolved claim, and clinical importance

The inherited executed evidence supports only feasibility of a selected short-horizon, noncausal prognostic comparison. It found an adequately supported local 2022 block but substantial later outcome loss and sparse systemic-record support; it does not establish robust incremental prediction, missing-at-random observation, assay clinical interpretability, or transport. The parents' deterministic missing-Y and ascertainment contracts remain unexecuted analyses, not findings.

The unresolved operational claim is prespecified and falsifiable:

> For each assay, among outcome-blind BP-eligible repeat-TACE episodes, is Y completion within 72 hours different across observable event-to-follow-up opportunity strata, arms, and calendar blocks, and is absence of Y followed by measurably different **recorded-care capture** during days 3–7 and 8–30 after the event?

This matters because a monitoring assay that is only observed in a selected subset cannot be treated as a routinely available downstream signal. A completion audit can identify whether the missing-Y problem is concentrated in episodes without a contemporaneous recorded opportunity or persists despite one. The downstream audit can distinguish, at the level of recorded data, “no Y observed but later system contact exists” from “no Y observed and no later captured activity,” which are operationally different gaps. It cannot distinguish uncompleted care from outside-system care or establish that any clinical action was appropriate.

## Frozen population, clocks, and primary estimand

Reconstruct and assert exactly 319 systemic-record patients, 1,491 comparator patients, 159 selected systemic and 526 selected comparator repeat events, and complete triplets albumin 40/118 and total bilirubin 41/119 by arm. Event selection must not inspect labs, examinations, clinical documents, orders, medications, Y, or any future outcome.

For each assay separately, B is the latest finite uncensored value in [TACE2 calendar day -30, calendar day -1]; P is the latest finite value in the selected repeat encounter in [event time -72 hours, event time); Y is the nearest finite valid value to event time +24 hours in (event time, event time +72 hours], with the inherited deterministic tie rule. Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time` when Y exists and positive P lead. Create BP eligibility after B and P but before reading Y. Use origins 2021-01-01, 2022-01-01, 2023-01-01, and 2024-01-01; training is `event_date < origin` and the future block is `[origin, origin+365 days)`.

Fit the inherited locked models only on observed pre-origin complete triplets.

```
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
```

Use training-only age median plus age-missing indicator, sex coding, variance filtering, centering/scaling, inner leave-one-patient-out alpha selection over `[0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100]`, and ridge. Preserve the observed complete-triplet standardized held-out RMSE gain, fixed pre-origin predictions, original test SD denominator, and patient-frequency bootstrap. Preserve the assay-specific all-BP training-range affine missing-Y bounds and piecewise-linear common-displacement stress test. A bound is an identification region, not a confidence interval.

## Prospective-time assay-completion audit

This is a historical implementation of a prospective protocol, with every label defined as it would be available from the event clock. For each assay and BP-eligible episode, define the completion deadline `D = event_time + 72 hours` and set:

`C_a = 1` exactly when the deterministic Y selector finds a finite uncensored assay value for assay a in `(event_time,D]`; otherwise `C_a = 0`, meaning **not observed in this source window**, not necessarily not done. Y selection and C are assay-specific and never pooled.

Before attaching C, construct mutually exclusive, pre-Y opportunity strata using only information with permitted timestamps:

1. `exam+followup`: a valid examination `开始时间` in `(event_time,D]` and an additional distinct encounter with valid `就诊时间` in that interval;
2. `exam_only`: examination but no additional timed encounter;
3. `followup_only`: additional timed encounter but no examination;
4. `document_only`: nonempty linked document for the selected encounter, with neither timed indicator (document presence is not timed); and
5. `none`: none of these indicators.

For transparency, also emit binary flags (not mutually exclusive) for a timestamped non-medication order in the window and any timestamped medication order in the window. These are recorded-order opportunity signals, not assay requests, administrations, or completed care. The exact lab C remains the only assay-completion variable.

For every assay, origin, future block, arm, and opportunity stratum, report `N_BP`, `n_C1`, `n_C0`, completion fraction, and Wilson 95% interval; mark/suppress cells below five. Report the inherited funnel `selected event -> has_B -> has_P -> BP_eligible -> C1/Y_observed -> complete triplet`, with denominators unchanged. Prespecify two-sided descriptive contrasts of completion across arms, calendar blocks, and opportunity strata, plus observed-versus-missing SMDs for B, P, age, sex, P-lead hours, TACE2-to-repeat days, and block. These are ascertainment diagnostics, not MAR evidence, inverse-probability weights, or causal comparisons.

The operational flag is a workflow-audit flag only: mark `completion_gap` if overall or arm-specific completion is below 80%, if the 95% interval is not estimable, or if completion differs by at least 20 percentage points between any supported opportunity stratum (minimum 10 BP rows per compared stratum). Mark `opportunity_gap` if C=0 is common despite a timed exam+follow-up opportunity. These thresholds trigger review of the recording pathway; they do not recommend a test, treatment, or policy and are not utility thresholds.

## Prespecified downstream recorded-care audit

Downstream care is evaluated only after the completion deadline, so it cannot define or leak into C. Use two disjoint windows relative to the same event:

* `D1 = [event_time+72 hours, event_time+7 days)`;
* `D2 = [event_time+7 days, event_time+30 days)`.

For each window, construct row-level indicators using only valid timestamps and deduplicated linked encounters:

* `encounter_capture`: an additional encounter with valid `就诊时间` in the window;
* `exam_capture`: an examination row with valid `开始时间` in the window;
* `lab_capture`: any linked laboratory row with valid `检验时间` in the window, with no claim that it is the target assay;
* `medication_order_capture`: any linked medication row with valid `开始时间` or `结束时间` in the window, explicitly an order record rather than administration;
* `nonmed_order_capture`: any linked non-medication order with valid `开立时间`, `开始时间`, or `结束时间` in the window, explicitly an order proxy;
* `procedure_record_capture`: any linked procedure with valid `开始时间` in the window, explicitly a recorded procedure and not proof of delivery or intent; and
* `document_capture`: a nonempty clinical-document row linked to an encounter whose valid `就诊时间` falls in the window. Because documents have no native timestamp, this is encounter-derived presence only.

Define `any_recorded_care_capture` as the OR of these components and also report each component and the number of captured component types. Do not describe this composite as follow-up completed, clinical surveillance, treatment, recovery, safety, or benefit. For each assay separately, report these indicators and Wilson intervals by C=1 versus C=0, opportunity stratum, arm, origin/block, and exact D1/D2 window, retaining all BP-eligible episodes in the denominator. Do not condition on a downstream result or use downstream records in M0/M1, the Y bounds, or completion strata.

The downstream comparison is descriptive and two-sided. Report risk differences and, where estimable, prevalence ratios with exact denominators; no adjustment, weighting, mediation analysis, or causal language is allowed. A larger capture fraction among C=0 may indicate delayed testing or a recording mismatch; a smaller fraction may indicate no in-system contact or outside care; either pattern is compatible with multiple mechanisms. The only operationally defensible output is a prespecified flag for where record reconciliation or prospective completion monitoring should be considered, not a patient-level action rule.

Define `completion-and-care audit status` per assay/block as:

* `supported operational description`: BP gate passes, C fractions and D1/D2 component denominators are computable, and no required timestamp/identifier assertion fails;
* `operationally concerning`: supported description plus a completion/opportunity gap or a large (at least 20 percentage-point) C=1 versus C=0 difference in any D1/D2 capture component with at least 10 episodes per group;
* `inconclusive`: failed BP/arm gate, sparse comparison cells, nonfinite timestamps, no eligible episodes, or source coverage ambiguity that prevents the requested denominator; and
* `unsupported`: a source/hash/schema/clock or duplicate-safe join assertion fails.

`Operationally concerning` is not an adverse clinical outcome and `supported operational description` is not evidence of benefit. The audit must not override the primary result or rescue a global missing-Y interval that crosses zero. Interpretation precedence is: unsupported, then inconclusive, then adverse/selection-sensitive/robust primary prognostic labels from the frozen analysis, with the care-audit status appended as a separate operational descriptor.

## Falsification and fail-closed verification

The compiler/verifier must include synthetic and boundary tests that:

1. assign Y exactly at event, event+72h, event+7d, and event+30d and verify the strict/open and half-open window rules;
2. prove BP eligibility and opportunity strata are created before reading Y and before any D1/D2 record;
3. verify C=0 is never recoded as zero, a negative assay, or proof that care did not occur;
4. verify albumin and bilirubin have separate rows, denominators, models, completion flags, bounds, and summaries;
5. verify an encounter/examination/document/order cannot be substituted for a target assay result;
6. verify a document's native absence of time prevents it from being assigned a Y timestamp or a document-authored downstream time;
7. verify medication and non-medication orders and procedures are reported as recorded proxies, never administration, intent, or completion;
8. verify all downstream flags start at event+72h and cannot alter the completion endpoint, primary fits, fixed test SD, bootstrap, or missing-Y bounds;
9. verify all-BP denominators are retained when Y is missing, small cells are marked, deterministic bounds are not labeled confidence intervals, and out-of-range observed future Y invalidates rather than widens the training-range bound; and
10. run positive, adverse, selection-sensitive, and inconclusive branches: favorable complete-case gain with a zero-crossing global envelope remains selection-sensitive; nonpositive gain remains adverse despite favorable care strata; low completion or differential care capture remains an operational descriptor, not evidence of treatment failure or patient harm.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes and headers, duplicate-safe joins, cohort/event denominators, clocks, completion funnels, opportunity strata, timestamped downstream flags, interval arithmetic, and fail-closed statuses. The following remain unavailable or not estimable from these data: laboratory units, reference ranges and clinical interpretability; assay ordering intent; actual specimen collection/administration; TACE delivery and intent; medication administration, adherence, toxicity, and treatment response; tumor burden, imaging findings and response (text is deliberately not mined); mortality, hepatic failure, readmission as an adjudicated patient-important endpoint, quality of life, functional status, costs, utility, and net benefit; external-care capture; causal effects; and any patient-level actionability or clinical benefit. Clinical adjudication, linked external records, and a prospective implementation cohort would be required for those claims.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to both parents, the substantive repair is a staged, compiler-ready operational audit: assay completion is fixed at a prospective event-time deadline, and post-deadline recorded-care capture is measured in two prespecified windows with exact source-level indicators, denominators, thresholds, and fail-closed statuses. This makes the missing-Y problem clinically operationally relevant without imputing Y, changing the frozen P-beyond-B estimand, pooling assays, using outcome leakage, or overstating routine records as completed care. No new empirical finding is reported. The unresolved uncertainty remains whether recorded completion patterns represent true patient care, whether missingness is external versus in-system, whether assay values are clinically interpretable across sites/units, and whether any prognostic association affects patient-important outcomes or decisions; those questions require adjudication and prospective or externally linked data.
