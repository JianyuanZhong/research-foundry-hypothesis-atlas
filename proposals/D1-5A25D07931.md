# Persistent Deep Sedation After Stabilization Delays Liberation from Mechanical Ventilation: A Falsifiable MIMIC-IV Experiment

**Parent:** [prior hypothesis] (expert seed 6)
**Dataset:** MIMIC-IV 3.1 (frozen archive, [source checksum])
**Status:** Data-bound proposal with verified itemids, prespecified landmark + phenotype, executed bounded feasibility probe, and three inspected key references. No full analysis was run.

---

## 1. Hypothesis

### Supported claim (established by prior literature, not re-tested here)

- Sedation depth (measured by RASS/SAS) is associated with adverse ICU outcomes. [K1] Amangeldy et al. (2026) used MIMIC-IV v3.1 (31,139 first ICU admissions) and found that nighttime RASS scores were among the most influential predictors (SHAP) of a composite outcome including prolonged mechanical ventilation (>7 days), 30-day mortality, and discharge to skilled nursing. RASS was an independent predictor after adjustment for age, HFRS, and comorbidities.
- Sedative choice (dexmedetomidine) is associated with both mortality benefit and prolonged mechanical ventilation in septic patients. [K2] Wei et al. (2025) analyzed MIMIC-IV + eICU-CRD (5,176 PSM pairs) and found dexmedetomidine reduced 28-day mortality (HR 0.595) but prolonged MV duration (median 78 h vs 51 h, P < 0.001), ICU LOS, and hospital LOS. This demonstrates that sedative exposure is a meaningful, measurable variable in MIMIC-IV that is associated with both mortality and MV duration in opposite directions.
- Post-landmark RASS-defined sedation depth is a measurable, clinically meaningful phenotype. [K3] Ding et al. (2026) used a 24-h landmark design in aSAH patients and showed that post-landmark RASS-defined examinability (RASS -2 to 0) was 12 percentage points higher with early dexmedetomidine (95% CI 5–19), and that findings "remain vulnerable to confounding by indication and do not establish a drug-specific causal effect." This validates the landmark + RASS phenotype approach and explicitly names the confounding-by-indication limitation.

### Unresolved claim (this experiment tests)

**Among mechanically ventilated adult ICU patients who have reached basic circulatory/respiratory stabilization (a prespecified landmark), continued deep sedation (RASS ≤ -4 in ≥ 50% of measurements in the 24–48 h post-landmark window) independently delays successful liberation from mechanical ventilation, after adjustment for baseline severity and sedative context.**

This is distinct from the supported claims above: [K1] used admission-time RASS as a predictor (not post-landmark); [K2] used sedative drug (not measured sedation depth) and found a mortality benefit but MV prolongation; [K3] used a landmark design but in a different population (aSAH) and a different outcome (examinability, not MV liberation).

## 2. Study Design

### 2.1 Population

- Adult ICU stays (patients.anchor_age ≥ 18) with ≥ 1 invasive mechanical ventilation episode (icu/procedureevents, itemid = 225792 "Invasive Ventilation").
- Feasibility probe: 30,653 adult MV stays; 23,830 with MV ≥ 24 h (eligible for post-landmark analysis).

### 2.2 Stabilization landmark (prespecified)

The **first time** during the first 72 h of the MV episode that ALL of the following are met:
1. **MAP ≥ 65 mmHg** for ≥ 6 consecutive hours (chartevents itemid 220052 arterial or 220181 NIBP; arterial preferred).
2. **Off or minimal vasopressors**: no infusion of norepinephrine (221906), vasopressin (222315), epinephrine (221289), phenylephrine (221749), or dopamine (221662) for ≥ 6 consecutive hours (inputevents).
3. **FiO2 ≤ 0.6** (chartevents itemid 223835) for ≥ 6 consecutive hours.

**Rationale:** This landmark identifies patients who have achieved basic hemodynamic and respiratory stability, reducing immortal-time bias (patients who never stabilize are excluded) and focusing the analysis on the period where sedation depth is a potentially modifiable factor rather than a reflection of acute instability. The 6-hour window reduces noise from transient fluctuations.

**Limitation:** FiO2 coverage is ~26.6% (prior seed-10 probe). Stays without FiO2 data in the landmark window will be excluded or handled via sensitivity analysis. The solver should report the landmark-reachable subset size.

### 2.3 Exposure phenotype (prespecified)

Among stays that reach the landmark:
- **Continued deep sedation**: ≥ 50% of RASS measurements (itemid 228096) in the 24–48 h post-landmark window at RASS ≤ -4.
- **Lighter sedation**: all other stays (including those with < 50% deep, or no RASS measurements in the window).
- Requires ≥ 3 RASS measurements in the 24–48 h window for classification; otherwise "insufficient data" (excluded from primary analysis, reported).

**Rationale:** RASS ≤ -4 (unresponsive to name or voice) is the standard definition of deep sedation. The 24–48 h window captures the period immediately after stabilization when sedation weaning decisions are made. The ≥ 50% threshold avoids classifying a single deep measurement as "continued."

### 2.4 Outcome

- **Primary:** Time from landmark to successful liberation from mechanical ventilation (end of MV episode, procedureevents.endtime for itemid 225792). Censored at ICU discharge (icustays.outtime) or in-hospital death (admissions.deathtime), whichever occurs first.
- **Secondary:** In-hospital mortality (admissions.deathtime IS NOT NULL).

### 2.5 Covariates

- **Baseline severity:** age (patients.anchor_age), APACHE II/IV if available (chartevents), sepsis/shock diagnosis (hosp/diagnoses_icd, ICD-10 A40–A41, R65.2), lactate (hosp/labevents, itemid 50813) in first 24 h.
- **Sedative context (indication proxy):** total hours of propofol (222168), midazolam (221668), fentanyl (221744), dexmedetomidine (225150) infusion in the 24 h pre-landmark (inputevents).
- **MV context:** MV duration before landmark (hours), FiO2 at landmark, MAP at landmark.

### 2.6 Analysis plan

1. **Baseline model:** Cox proportional hazards for time to MV liberation (or logistic regression for 7-day liberation), adjusting for age, APACHE/sepsis, lactate, MV duration before landmark, FiO2 at landmark.
2. **Extended model:** Add the sedation-depth phenotype (continued deep vs lighter) and its interaction with baseline severity (APACHE/sepsis).
3. **Alternative:** XGBoost or random forest for non-linear effects and incremental discrimination (C-index).
4. **Sensitivity:** (a) Restrict to stays with ≥ 5 RASS measurements in the window; (b) Use SAS (223753) as secondary exposure; (c) Propensity score matching on baseline covariates; (d) E-value for unmeasured confounding.

### 2.7 Falsification criteria

- **Supportive:** Adjusted HR (or OR) for continued deep sedation > 1.2 with 95% CI excluding 1.0, phenotype present in ≥ 10% of the landmark-reachable cohort, and the association is attenuated but not eliminated by sedative-context covariates.
- **Adverse:** No association after adjustment (HR 0.9–1.1, CI including 1.0), or the association is fully explained by baseline severity/sedative context (HR becomes 1.0 after adding these covariates).
- **Inconclusive:** < 5% of MV stays reach the landmark with sufficient RASS data; or the phenotype is present in < 5% of the landmark cohort; or the CI is wide (HR 0.7–1.5) due to small sample.

### 2.8 Strongest rival and how it is addressed

**Confounding by indication:** Sicker patients are kept deeply sedated. Addressed by:
- Landmark design (excludes patients who never stabilize).
- Adjustment for baseline severity (APACHE, sepsis, lactate).
- Sedative-context covariates (propofol/midazolam/fentanyl/dex hours pre-landmark) as indication proxies.
- Interaction terms (sedation × severity).
- E-value for unmeasured confounding.

**Residual confounding:** No measured indication for sedation (e.g., status epilepticus, elevated ICP, severe ARDS). The interaction with severity and the E-value quantify but do not eliminate this. [K3] explicitly names this limitation.

## 5. Limitations

- Single-center (MIMIC-IV, BIDMC); no external validation.
- FiO2 coverage ~26.6%; landmark-reachable subset may be smaller than the full MV cohort.
- No measured indication for sedation; residual confounding by indication remains.
- RASS is a nurse-assessed scale; inter-rater variability is possible.
- The landmark rule is prespecified but not validated; the solver should report sensitivity to landmark parameters.
- No GPU or model training was performed in this proposal; the analysis is a standard survival regression.
