# Incremental decision value of lactate-by-perfusion at the first live ICU exit

## Scientific deliverable and advance

This is an independent, decision-centered MIMIC-IV adaptation anchored to assigned parent [prior hypothesis]. It retains the parent's deterministic first eligible live exit, dual clinical/recording-time rule, endpoint-independent coverage gate, competing-risk ascertainment, selection audit and noncausal limits. Its new question is:

> Immediately before a live ICU exit, does adding a prespecified lactate-by-measured-perfusion phenotype to current-state severity capture materially more patients who experience ICU return or timed death within 48 hours, when enhanced-review workload is fixed at 5%, 10% or 20% of the covered population?

The future solver must newly produce either a gate-passing held-out incremental-decision analysis or a complete prespecified gate-failure audit. It must fit B0 (current-state severity), B1 (generic history/process), Bphen (B1 plus frozen phenotype) and learned J1 on identical rows, split, labels and uncertainty procedure; then report decision, competing-risk, calibration and falsification outputs. No fitted result is claimed.

This advances the parent from association/model score to whether the phenotype changes who would be prioritized for limited enhanced monitoring or escalation review. The estimand is policy-independent event capture, not alert benefit, treatment effect, discharge appropriateness, preventability or a bedside policy.

## Evidence boundary, hypothesis and estimands

Verified evidence establishes only that MIMIC-IV 3.1 contains ICU stays/admission endpoints, repeated lactate and organ labs, MAP, vasoactive intervals, urine output and clinical/recording timestamps. Subject-specific date shifting preserves within-subject intervals but invalidates cross-subject calendar alignment. Direct archive inspection verified the dictionary labels/table links below. The seed and parent are hypotheses/design precedents, not evidence that the phenotype is prevalent, valid, prognostic or useful. Prevalence, support, overlap and every fitted quantity remain unmeasured.

The hypothesis is that persistent lactate has less adverse-transition signal when measured perfusion improves than when it worsens. The held-out 48-hour association estimand, with first ICU return R, timed death D and alive discharge S competing, is:

theta_RD = [CIF_RD(P,I)-CIF_RD(NL,I)] - [CIF_RD(P,W)-CIF_RD(NL,W)].

P is lactate >=2.0 mmol/L in both halves; NL has both medians defined and is not P; I is MAP rise >=5 mmHg with target-pressor minutes nonincreasing; W is MAP fall <=-5 mmHg with target-pressor minutes nondecreasing. Hypothesis theta_RD < 0; the meaningful null region is [-0.02,+0.02]. A negative estimate inside it is not meaningful support. Prespecified theta_R and theta_D use the same cells and competing S; each claim requires component support.

For the primary incremental decision estimand, on the known-ascertainment held-out test set let A=1 when first known transition is R or D by 48 hours. Let Top_k(M) be the highest-risk k fraction under model M, ties resolved by subject_id/stay_id. For k={0.05,0.10,0.20}, estimate both the workload-specific PPV increment and event-capture increment:

DeltaPPV_k = P(A=1 | Top_k(Bphen)) - P(A=1 | Top_k(B0)),
DeltaCapture_k = P(Top_k(Bphen) | A=1) - P(Top_k(B0) | A=1).

B0 is current-state severity. B1-to-Bphen is a secondary nested contrast beyond generic history/measurement process. Workloads are frozen before predictions/outcomes are inspected. S competes in CIF analysis; U is excluded from the primary decision denominator and handled by fixed bounds.

Incremental operational support requires G_cov/G_sup, improved transition-aware Brier or calibration for Bphen versus B0, and positive DeltaCapture with a subject-bootstrap 95% interval wholly above a prespecified 0.05 absolute capture increment at at least one workload without deterioration at the other two; report DeltaPPV alongside it. This threshold is a decision-scale criterion, not universal utility. Physiologic interaction support additionally requires theta_RD wholly below -0.02 and its direction pattern. Better capture without theta supports only retrospective prediction, not perfusion biology. Supportive theta without capture is non-operational for this decision.

## Population, time zero and endpoint

Use only MIMIC; HCC, eICU and UKB stay directly available read-only and are not mixed. Join icu/icustays to hosp/admissions on (subject_id,hadm_id), and patients on subject_id. Retain anchor_age>=18, including MIMIC's 91 representation; report missing joins.

For each hadm_id sort ICU rows by (outtime,intime,stay_id), then choose the first row, before coverage/outcome/model inspection, with finite intime<outtime, valid adult/admission joins, >=13 hours of history, and known live t0=outtime: deathtime null with explicit hospital_expire_flag=0, or deathtime>t0. Missing flag plus missing death time is not survival. Death at/before t0, reversed/missing time, short history and unknown death timing get distinct flow reasons. Never substitute a later exit.

Set tL=t0-1h. Features use [t0-13h,t0-1h], early [tL-12h,tL-6h], late (tL-6h,tL]. Boundaries are exact. Outtime defines landmark only. Prohibit outtime, los, last_careunit, future transfers, discharge fields, notes, dod and post-tL rows from features. Current care unit may come only from the transfers interval covering tL.

For H=t0+48h, candidates in (t0,H] are: R=min intime of a different stay_id in the same admission; D=nonmissing deathtime; S=nonmissing dischtime with explicit hospital_expire_flag=0. Tie order D, R, S; retain candidates/tie decisions. N is no R/D/S with known follow-up through H, an ascertainment state not an event. U is unresolved ordering/follow-up, including expire flag 1 with missing deathtime or missing flag where survival cannot be established. Primary analysis uses R/D/S/N; U is never imputed. Report U-to-N and U-to-death-at-t0+1-second bounds.

## Matched methods and information gained

All methods use identical gated rows, landmark, labels, split, fixed workloads and subject bootstrap. Preprocessing/calibration are training-only.

- B0 current-state baseline: penalized discrete-time cause-specific complementary-log-log hazards for R/D/S using gender, anchor_age, anchor_year_group, admission_type, admission_location, insurance, first_careunit, late-half physiology/organs, late missingness and process counts. Exclude discharge_location, dischtime, deathtime, hospital_expire_flag, outtime, los, last_careunit, dod and future rows.
- B1 history/process baseline: B0 plus order-blind early/late and 12 one-hour-bin summaries, masks/counts for lactate, MAP, pressor, Foley and organ fields. It tests generic history/documentation intensity.
- Bphen transparent alternative: B1 plus frozen P/NL, I/W/indeterminate, early/late lactate/MAP, pressor changes, organ values/availability and prespecified interaction. B0-to-Bphen and B1-to-Bphen distinguish current-state and generic-history increments.
- J1 learned alternative: GRU hidden size 32, fit training-only on 12-bin lactate, MAP, pressor minutes, Foley, organs, masks/process plus context, targeting the same discrete-time R/D/S transitions. Validation-only stopping; untouched test until final prediction. Evaluate same workloads, CIFs, capture/PPV, Brier/calibration and bootstrap intervals.

J1 tests whether fixed threshold cells adequately summarize nonlinear longitudinal coupling and timing; B0 loses this order and Bphen discretizes it. It cannot name latent states as microcirculatory physiology. Bphen is primary/auditable; J1 is a substantive alternative/sensitivity. A larger transformer is deferred because J1 tests order at bounded scale while capacity cannot recover treatment intent/proxy validity. A fluid-response model is deferred because inputevents lack clinician intent, responsiveness and treatment counterfactuals. No method is rejected for using neural networks/GPUs.

Expected future resources: 4-16 CPUs, 64-256 GiB and several hours for 10.55-GB compressed archive scanning/manifests; tabular fitting/bootstrap likely CPU-feasible, but scan time, support, q, calibration and runtime are unverified. J1 may use one allocated A100, 16 CPUs, <=64 GiB, <=8h, or CPU fallback; GPU need is unverified. Allowed envelope: 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, up to 8 GPUs, 28800s, concurrency 2. ## Analysis, falsification and interpretation

Report Aalen-Johansen R/D/S CIFs at 24/48h, theta contrasts, B0/B1/Bphen/J1 calibration intercept/slope, transition-aware Brier, secondary AUROC/AUPRC, fixed-workload capture/PPV and paired differences. Subject bootstrap preserves split/admission clustering; give 95% intervals. Unweighted first, q-weighted descriptive sensitivity separately. Every table states target, denominator, workload and tie rule.

Fixed falsifications: source/member/schema/dictionary checks; duplicate/pressor-overlap handling; first-exit/join audit; clinical/store-time leakage; early/late permutation preserving masks/counts; outcome permutation within partitions; MAP-only, pressor-only, arterial-MAP-only, Foley, lactate-4.0, 24/48h, 4/6/8 bins and charttime-only sensitivities; process-only Bmask; care-unit, anchor-year, measurement-intensity strata; complete ascertainment/U bounds; q support/ESS; deterministic split/target audits. Gain surviving outcome permutation, confined to Bmask or disappearing under dual-time availability is adverse to physiologic interpretation. S-only gain is not R/D monitoring evidence.

Supportive: fixed gates pass, theta_RD interval wholly below -0.02 with direction/component support, and incremental-decision criterion passes. Adverse: reversed theta, no incremental capture/calibration, Bphen no better than Bmask, process-only explanation, permutation resistance, or frozen-sensitivity instability. Inconclusive: gate failure, high U/indeterminate fraction, insufficient component events, unresolved joins/units, q failure for selection-robustness, calibration failure, or interval width >0.06 for theta/primary capture increment. Never redefine the study after these outcomes.

Computationally checkable: hashes/members, schemas/item links, flow, coverage, endpoint/tie manifests, split integrity, predictions, CIFs, capture/PPV, calibration/Brier, intervals, q and falsifications. Clinical adjudication/another study is required for microcirculatory dysfunction, lactate cause, intent, fluid response, preventability, monitoring benefit, utility, false-alert acceptability, deployment, causal treatment/discharge effects and transportability. Maximum claim: retrospective prognostic association and policy-independent capture in covered MIMIC patients.
