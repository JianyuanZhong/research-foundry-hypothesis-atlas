# Postoperative NUC exposure and EARLY vs LATE recurrence after curative resection of HBV-related HCC: a pre-specified recurrence-phase hazard decomposition of the NUC x HBV-DNA stratum contrast

Island: **hcc** (single-institution Chinese hospital records; snapshot `[source checksum]`).
Episode 36. Operation: **evolve** of the assessed-valid parent `[prior hypothesis]` (postoperative
NUC-exposure / early-recurrence branch). The parent's **population, temporal boundaries, exposure, outcome and M0/M1
analysis are inherited VERBATIM**; **exactly ONE** substantive decision-changing element is added (Section 4A):
a pre-specified **EARLY (<=24 mo, occult-residual) vs LATE (>24 mo, de-novo) recurrence-hazard decomposition** with a
**signed stratum contrast** (NUC x HBV-DNA x recurrence-phase), whose purpose is to separate a *replication-specific
causal benefit* from *confounding-by-indication / surveillance*. The parent's `reference_delivery` is complete and is
inherited. **No HR, IPTW weight, CATE, contrast or recurrence outcome is computed in discovery.**

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After curative resection of HBV-related HCC the recurring bedside decision is whether to start/continue a
nucleos(t)ide analogue (NUC). Antiviral therapy suppresses HBV replication and cuts new HCC incidence, yet residual HCC
risk persists under long-term viral suppression, and HCC after hepatectomy recurs through two mechanistically distinct
routes - early **occult residual disease** vs late **de novo carcinogenesis** ([K3], [K2]). The closest prior work ([K1],
Jeong 2025, abstract only) reports that the NUC *regimen* chosen was not associated with recurrence-free survival after
resection, leaving the NUC-vs-no-NUC exposure and its early/late, stratum-specific structure unresolved. **[K2]**
(Mingazzini 2026) frames recurrence as occult residual (early) vs de novo (late); **[K3]** (Ibrahim 2026) states NUC
suppresses viral replication but residual HCC risk persists, with direct (viral) and indirect (cirrhosis) carcinogenesis.
The added element turns this two-route mechanism into a **falsifiable, pre-specified estimand split**.

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival (pre-specified, inherited and sharpened by 4A)
- **H_effect (leading):** NUC exposure started in the early postoperative window lowers **early (<=24-mo) recurrence**,
  and the benefit is **larger where replication is active** (higher baseline HBV-DNA) - a NUC x HBV-DNA stratum contrast;
  it is attenuated where cirrhosis/indirect carcinogenesis dominates.
- **R_indication (strongest rival):** the crude association is **confounding by indication** - sicker, higher-viral-load,
  better-monitored patients are more likely to be put on a NUC, so any "benefit" tracks measured/unmeasured prognostic
  factors and surveillance intensity, and the stratum contrast vanishes once those are balanced.
- **R_null:** no causal effect; null after overlap weighting.
**What the new element adds:** H_effect predicts the protective NUC HR is **concentrated in the EARLY (occult-residual)
phase and graded by HBV-DNA**; R_indication/surveillance predicts a **near-null, phase-flat and stratum-flat HR** (or a
detection artifact). The signed phase x stratum contrast is therefore a quantity on which the two rivals differ.

## 4. Estimand and analysis (baseline vs substantive alternative; M0/M1 inherited)
- **M0 (baseline):** landmark Cox for early recurrence ~ NUC + age + sex + cirrhosis + baseline AFP + tumour size
  (single-cohort, conventional adjustment).
- **M1 (primary, target-trial-emulation) [inherited substantive alternative]:** clone-censor-weight / IPTW-overlap-weighted
  Cox of the same landmark estimand, with a pre-specified NUC x HBV-DNA (and NUC x cirrhosis) interaction; secondary
  causal-forest CATE. M0 adjustment gives one conditional HR and cannot separate indication from stratum modification;
  M1 balances the indication-driven covariate distribution and yields the stratum-specific contrast.
- **4A (ADDED, the ONE decision-changing element). Pre-specified EARLY vs LATE recurrence-hazard decomposition.**
  1. Define two non-overlapping recurrence phases after t0: **EARLY** event in `(t0, t0+24 mo]` (occult-residual route)
     and **LATE** event in `(t0+24 mo, ...]` (de-novo route), death as competing event throughout. Fit a **phase-stratified
     (piecewise) hazard / time-varying Cox** with a NUC x phase interaction, retaining the M1 overlap weights.
  2. Primary reported quantities: `HR_NUC,early` (<=24 mo) and `HR_NUC,late` (>24 mo) with 95% CIs.
  3. **Signed stratum contrast:** `Delta = logHR_NUC,early(high-HBV-DNA) - logHR_NUC,late(high-HBV-DNA)` (and the
     corresponding low-HBV-DNA contrast). Negative `Delta` = benefit concentrated in the early/replication-active phase.
  - **Pre-committed decision rule:** the timing-stratified pattern is **replication-specific** only if `HR_NUC,early<1`
    with a 95% CI excluding 1 in the high-HBV-DNA stratum AND the signed contrast `Delta<0` (early benefit > late). A
    phase-flat/stratum-flat pattern supports R_indication/surveillance instead.

## 5. Falsification (branches tied to computed outputs)
- **Supportive of H_effect:** overlap-weighted NUC `HR_NUC,early` < 1 (95% CI excludes 1) AND the signed contrast shows
  early>late with a protective NUC x HBV-DNA interaction; negative-control outcome shows no NUC "effect".
- **Adverse (R_indication):** crude HR < 1 but overlap-weighted HR crosses 1 / reverses; OR a protective HR that is
  phase-FLAT and stratum-FLAT across HBV-DNA -> generic, not replication-specific.
- **Adverse (detection/surveillance):** the NUC early benefit disappears when imaging-cadence / contact-intensity is added.
- **Inconclusive:** HBV-DNA modifier too sparse (<pre-declared minimum events/stratum) or positivity/overlap violated ->
  pre-declared inconclusive (cirrhosis fallback).
- **Precise null:** tight HR CIs around 1 in both phases and strata.
