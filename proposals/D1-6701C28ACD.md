## Exact windows, parsing, and independent trajectory

Use clinical offsets and the frozen disjoint windows relative to each row's `D`:

* baseline `B = (D-720,D]`;
* early `E = (D,D+720]` (0--12 hours);
* late `T = (D+720,D+2160]` (12--36 hours).

`respchartoffset` is the respiratory clinical time. `respchartentryoffset` is never substituted for clinical time and is retained only for predeclared documentation-lag diagnostics. A boundary audit must prove that the open/closed inequalities above are used; when a respiratory observation and an exit share a boundary, exit precedence below wins.

Before reading any label-stratified endpoint result, enumerate all source-wide `respchartvaluelabel` and `respcharttypecat` values and matched/unmatched counts, all `airwaytype` values and interval-presence patterns, and discharge status/location values. Freeze a machine-readable parser/mapping manifest from this source-wide scan. For each of the four frozen components—FiO2 (including the frozen source-label aliases), PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate—parse only finite numeric values. The manifest must specify whitespace/case normalization, FiO2 fraction/percentage conversion and admissible bounds, malformed/out-of-bound handling, duplicate rows, and same-time conflicts; malformed or ambiguous values are missing and no result-dependent unit conversion is permitted.

Retain all valid component observations in each window with count, distinct clinical offsets, first/last offset, median, and parsing reason. A component is observed iff it has at least two valid observations at distinct offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. A state is observed iff at least three of four components are observed. Missing components are missing, never zero, unchanged, or carried forward. Component changes compare the B median with the T median using fixed absolute thresholds: FiO2 fraction `0.10`, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`. A component is increased/decreased only when its threshold is met; otherwise it is neutral.

The mutually exclusive trajectory function for a row whose exit status is not competing is:

1. **Sustained de-escalation** iff B and T are state-observed, at least two components decrease, no component increases, and the E escalation check is false.
2. **Escalation** iff B and T are state-observed and at least two components increase, or the frozen invasive-airway rule detects a newly documented invasive airway in E or T after no invasive evidence in B.
3. **Stable/mixed** iff B and T are state-observed and neither of the first two conditions holds.

The E escalation check is a deterministic binary predicate, not a missing-value rescue: it is true only when an invasive-airway observation/active interval satisfying the frozen source-wide lexical and interval rule occurs in E or T and no qualifying invasive evidence occurs in B. Absence of an E/T row cannot itself create escalation. If E is insufficient to evaluate a required escalation check under an implementation of the frozen rule, the row is not called de-escalation; it is assigned `non_exit_unobservable` unless the independent B/T increase or airway rule assigns escalation. This fail-closed exception and its reason are recorded. The primary respiratory trajectory never uses care-plan fields, treatment strings, or post-`D` features. `respiratoryCare` supplies the prespecified airway corroboration/airway sensitivity and does not silently fill missing numeric settings. Its lexical term list, missingness rule, and interval rule (`ventstartoffset <= time` and missing or `ventendoffset >= time`) are frozen before arm summaries. A nonmissing active-interval-only sensitivity is retained.

## Total outcome-state algebra and primary estimand

Let `i = 1,...,540` index frozen person-landmarks, `A_i ∈ {e,n}` be the exact label, `H_i` hospital, `S_i` `patientunitstayid`, and `τ_i = D_i+2160`. Let `X_i` be the pre-`D` feature vector, constructed only from the manifest-listed pre-`D` fields. Define an exit indicator

`C_i = 1{unitdischargeoffset_i is finite and unitdischargeoffset_i <= τ_i}`.

For `C_i=1`, retain `unitdischargestatus`, `unitdischargelocation`, and the exact exit offset and add `exit_before_washout = 1{unitdischargeoffset_i <= D_i+720}`. A row with `unitdischargeoffset <= D` is still an exit and cannot become an endpoint class. An unavailable/invalid discharge boundary is not silently treated as alive; it is recorded as an administrative follow-up failure and assigned `non_exit_unobservable` only under the explicit conservative rule below, with a separate `invalid_exit_time` reason. No post-`D` clinical measurement can override an exit.

Define `K_i` in the ordered set

`K = {competing_exit, classifiable_de-escalation, classifiable-escalation, classifiable-stable/mixed, non_exit_unobservable}`.

The endpoint-state function is evaluated in this exact order:

* `K_i = competing_exit` if `C_i=1`. This includes death, discharge, transfer, and any ICU exit at or before `τ_i`; subtype fields are descriptive only and never infer cause or post-ICU respiratory state.
* Otherwise, if the full B/E/T rules above yield one and only one trajectory class, set `K_i` to its corresponding `classifiable-*` value.
* Otherwise set `K_i = non_exit_unobservable`, with component/window/airway/invalid-boundary reason flags. This includes alive/in-ICU through `τ_i` rows lacking the minimum B/T observations or a determinable required E airway check. It does not mean stable, improvement, escalation, or a negative clinical outcome.

For every row write exactly one `K_i`; assert `sum_k 1{K_i=k}=1`. Report all 540 rows, including exit subtypes, exact exit interval, follow-up validity, B/E/T counts, missingness, and opportunity. The primary all-row descriptive estimand is the five-vector

`p_k(a) = (1/N_a) * Σ_i 1{A_i=a} 1{K_i=k}`, where `N_a = Σ_i 1{A_i=a}` and `k ∈ K`.

The primary label contrast is the vector `Δ_k = p_k(e) - p_k(n)` for all five states, with uncertainty clustered by `patientunitstayid`; it is not a causal effect. The displayed classifiable trajectory contrast is defined only when its support gate passes:

`q_c(a) = Σ_i 1{A_i=a,K_i=classifiable-c} / Σ_i 1{A_i=a,K_i∈classifiable}`, for `c ∈ {de-escalation, escalation, stable/mixed}`;
`δ_c = q_c(e)-q_c(n)`.

The complete-case quantity `δ_c` must always be accompanied by `p_k(a)`, denominators, competing-exit probability, and non-exit-unobservable probability. It cannot replace the all-row vector. No inverse-observation weighting is part of the primary estimand. A fixed pre-`D` inverse-observation-weighted sensitivity may estimate classifiable-state composition among non-exit rows, but only with training-fold fitting, fixed 1st/99th-percentile capping, explicit positivity checks, and an inconclusive result if support fails; it cannot reclassify missing rows or erase exits.

## Held-out procedure and estimands

The primary transport analysis is leave-one-hospital-out. For held-out hospital `h`, fit all preprocessing, imputation, scaling, regularization, probability estimation, and observation weighting on training hospitals only. Hospital ID is a fold/stratum, never a patient predictor; no held-out target-rate calibration or hospital-specific intercept is imported. Fit `M0` and `M1` separately in every fold and generate held-out probabilities for all five `K` states. The primary predictive estimand is the paired held-out difference in proper scoring rules for `Y=K`: row-weighted micro log loss and Brier score, plus equal-hospital macro versions, `M1-M0` with direction defined as improvement when loss decreases. Report fold rows, stays, both labels, all five state counts, classifiable support, exit counts, and whether each contrast is estimable.

A fold may contribute an arm contrast only if both exact labels and both relevant classifiable support conditions are present in its held-out rows. Broad all-row prediction metrics may be reported for a fold with one arm, but no arm contrast or direction is manufactured. Report calibration only where finite support permits it. Separately report metrics for the all-row exit/observability subdistribution and for classifiable trajectory rows; an apparent label gain confined to exit or documentation prediction is not a patient-state gain. Repeated landmarks remain together in every uncertainty resample.

Use a stay-cluster bootstrap with fixed seed `61061`, minimum 500 and target 1,000 valid replicates, refitting the complete fold pipeline in each replicate; report attempted, valid, failed, and effective-cluster counts before any gate. Run stay-cluster label permutations within hospital-by-landmark strata only when both exact labels and finite cluster support exist. Preserve arm counts and repeated-landmark structure. If fewer than 20 valid strata exist, permutation inference is unavailable/inconclusive, not a null result.

## Falsification and mechanical decision gates

Run all gates from machine-readable artifacts before interpreting `Δ` or model increments; no result-dependent threshold or mapping change is allowed.

1. **Artifact and independent-endpoint integrity.** Pass only if parent hashes, joins, exact labels, boundaries, source manifest, parser, and column isolation pass; primary trajectory code has no post-`D` care-plan access, treatment-string use, outcome-guided mapping, or post-`D` baseline feature. Any failure invalidates endpoint computation.
2. **Total-state accounting.** Every one of 540 rows has exactly one `K_i`; `competing_exit` precedes all observation/class rules; no exit or non-exit unobservable row is called stable/mixed. A failure is invalid, not inconclusive.
3. **Observability and exit feasibility.** Supportive feasibility requires at least 40% of all 540 rows classifiable and at least 10 hospitals with both labels among classifiable rows; below 20% classifiable or below five such hospitals is adverse feasibility; intermediate values are inconclusive. Report exit and non-exit unobservability separately, and never call either a respiratory outcome.
4. **Transport support.** Do not claim transportability unless at least five hospitals each have at least 10 classifiable, non-competing rows under each label and those hospitals contain at least 50% of all classifiable, non-competing rows. Retain all 74 hospitals in descriptive tables regardless of support.
5. **Incremental information.** A computationally supportive label-information result requires `M1` to improve both micro and equal-hospital macro held-out log loss for the five-state all-row outcome and for the classifiable trajectory analysis in the unbounded primary plus at least two frozen airway/observation sensitivities; the stay-cluster interval must exclude zero in the favorable direction and no more than 25% of estimable held-out hospitals may reverse direction. If gains occur only in `competing_exit` or `non_exit_unobservable`, conclude documentation/exit information only, not patient-state information.
6. **Trajectory coherence.** The evaluation-minus-no-trial direction for de-escalation and the stringent safety/airway-concordant sensitivity must agree in at least two prespecified overlap hospitals with at least 10 rows per label, with no early/late reversal. A settings-only gain disappearing under independent airway or observation sensitivity is adverse to the stronger patient-state interpretation.
7. **Placebos and documentation negative control.** Recompute the identical change rules on pre-`D` placebo `(D-1440,D-720]` versus `B`, and shift decision time to `D-720` while retaining the locked outcome window. A comparable/larger placebo increment or hospital-direction reversal is adverse to a newly informative post-label interpretation. Predict post-window source density, distinct times, and entry lag from label plus pre-`D` features; opportunity-only improvement without value-based trajectory improvement is adverse to the patient-state claim.
8. **Source/component ablation and permutation.** Remove each setting family and independently remove airway corroboration. A result surviving only one field, one source, one hospital, or an invalid permutation stratum is not supportive. Compare observed increments with the valid within-hospital-by-landmark permutation distribution; unavailable permutations are inconclusive.

Supportive results establish only incremental prediction of this structured eICU chart construct. Adverse results show failure of added information for this endpoint in this snapshot, not absence of bedside evaluation or harm. Inconclusive results identify failed artifact recovery, inadequate observation, competing exits, sparse hospital overlap, parser/source instability, failed positivity, or unavailable permutation support. Stronger claims require adjudicated bedside SBT/extubation/reintubation outcomes, synchronized device logs, local interface dictionaries, complete workflow data, post-ICU follow-up, expert review, and external/prospective replication.
