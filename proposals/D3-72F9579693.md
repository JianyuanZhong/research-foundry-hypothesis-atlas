# MerTK-pathway state versus macrophage-subset composition as the driver of fibroblast inflammatory output in RA synovial coculture

Seed: D3-M2 (Directional cell communication) — evolved child
Domain: Disease Mechanisms & Pathway Hypotheses (episode 1)

## 1. Unresolved question

Refractory rheumatoid arthritis is increasingly framed as failed inflammation resolution maintained by a reciprocal stromal-myeloid circuit: imprinted pathological fibroblasts plus a macrophage compartment depleted of pro-resolving, efferocytic (MerTK+) subsets [K1]. Macrophage-subset balance is thought to drive synovial inflammatory output [K3], and efferocytosis-dependent macrophage state (MerTK signaling) is proposed as the resolution engine that instructs stromal cells [K2]. These two mechanisms are confounded in vivo and in standard coculture:

- (A) **Composition**: the *fraction* of inflammatory MerTK/CD206neg macrophages determines FLS inflammatory output.
- (B) **Pathway state**: macrophage MerTK *signaling activity per cell* determines FLS inflammatory output, independent of subset fraction.
- (C) **FLS-intrinsic**: FLS donor identity dominates; apparent macrophage effects reflect stromal heterogeneity.

**Which arm dominates is not established by any inspected work** [K1][K2][K3]: all three are reviews proposing the circuit or the subset framework without controlled perturbations that separate abundance from signaling state. E-MTAB-8873 uniquely contains both matched-donor mixed-ratio cocultures (3Mpos:1Mneg vs 1Mpos:3Mneg, same FLS and same two macrophage donors) and macrophage-pretreatment perturbations (MerTK inhibitor UNC106238; LPS; inhibitor-then-LPS) with FLS-only baselines — the exact factorial needed to discriminate A vs B vs C.

**Clinical consequence**: if composition (A) dominates, restoring MerTK+ macrophage abundance/efferocytosis is the rational therapeutic axis; if pathway state (B) dominates, single-ligand or MerTK-pathway-directed intervention on the macrophage side could suffice without changing subset numbers; if FLS-intrinsic (C) dominates, macrophage-directed strategies will have limited stromal impact. This directly informs which resolution-restoring strategy in difficult-to-treat RA to prioritize [K1][K2].

## 2. Hypotheses (unresolved claims to be tested)

- **H1 (composition)**: the FLS inflammatory module (IL6, CXCL8, IL1B, CCL2, CCL20, LIF; FLS-dominant matrix module MMP1, MMP3, MMP12, MMP13, MMP14 — all verified present in the 445-feature panel) rises monotonically with the MerTKneg macrophage fraction: FLS_only < Mpos < 3Mpos:1Mneg < 1Mpos:3Mneg < Mneg.
- **H2 (pathway state)**: pharmacologic MerTK inhibition (UNC106238) of macrophages before coculture shifts the FLS inflammatory module (direction pre-specified at analysis time from the Mpos-vs-Mneg contrast) and reduces the marginal composition effect (negative interaction between Mneg fraction and inhibitor).
- **H3 (FLS-intrinsic, rival)**: FLS donor identity explains more variance in the FLS inflammatory module than any macrophage condition factor.

Leading claim: A and B are separable competing explanations [K1][K2]; the experiment's purpose is to discriminate them, not to confirm either.

## 4. Analysis design (computable; CPU-only, minutes of compute)

1. Load 8873 counts; sum duplicate-region features per gene; log1p per-million normalize within sample; z-score per gene across samples.
2. Define two a-priori modules from the panel: M_flame (FLS inflammatory: IL6, CXCL8, IL1B, CCL2, CCL20, LIF, MMP1, MMP3) and M_mac (macrophage-state: MERTK, MRC1, plus macrophage-dominant markers identified from FLS_only vs coculture specificity). Mixed-well pseudobulk contains both FLS and macrophages, so cell-of-origin attribution is marker-based and stated as such — this is a declared limitation, not a hidden assumption.
3. **Baseline**: donor-paired descriptive contrasts (Mneg vs Mpos; each pretreatment vs untreated, within same FLS donor and replicate batch where pairs exist), reported as module-score effect sizes with bootstrap CIs over genes.
4. **Substantive alternative**: composition-dose linear model — module score ~ Mneg fraction {0 (Mpos), 0.25, 0.75, 1 (Mneg)} + inhibitor indicator + fraction×inhibitor + FLS donor random effect + replicate batch; compares monotone dose-response (H1) against a step change predicted by subset identity, and quantifies the inhibitor interaction (H2). Variance partitioning for H3.
5. Positive control: Mneg_preLPS vs Mneg must elevate M_mac (validates module responsiveness); FLS_Inhibitor vs FLS_only isolates any direct FLS-side inhibitor effect.
6. E-MTAB-8316 secondary: transcriptome-wide paired UNC106238 contrast, averaged repeated contexts per donor/condition first, checking whether any FLS-response genes change beyond macrophage-intrinsic targets.
7. No trained predictive model is proposed for the primary analysis (n=32; predictive utility is not the question); a learned model is deferred — a dose-response mixed model is the scientifically adequate instrument. This deferral is explicit, not a GPU/complexity avoidance.

## 5. Falsification criteria (pre-specified)

- **Supportive for H1**: monotone ordering of M_flame across {Mpos, 3:1, 1:3, Mneg} in ≥3 of 3 adjacent steps AND Mneg > Mpos with donor-adjusted uncertainty intervals excluding zero.
- **Adverse for H1**: flat or non-monotone dose response with overlapping intervals — composition hypothesis weakened; if H2 effect persists while H1 fails, pathway state is favored.
- **Supportive for H2**: paired inhibitor-vs-untreated difference in M_flame within same FLS donor+replicate exceeding the pre-specified margin, in the same direction as the Mneg-vs-Mpos contrast.
- **Adverse for H2**: inhibitor changes M_mac but not M_flame (macrophage state changes without FLS-output change).
- **Inconclusive**: FLS donor variance share >50%, or paired contrasts unavailable (donor confounding between subset and FLS donor), or mixed-ratio n=1 limitation dominates (R2 pair is a single replicate; R5 1:3 is unpaired) — then report as inconclusive and specify the missing design (more mixed-ratio replicates with crossed donors) needed.
- Stronger conclusions (in-vivo relevance, therapeutic claims) require expert clinical adjudication and additional data — explicitly out of scope for this computational study.

## 6. Competing explanations and limitations

- Mixed-well pseudobulk: FLS vs macrophage attribution is marker-based only; panel is 445 genes, unmeasured genes unavailable (e.g., TNF, HBEGF, AREG, OSM are NOT in the panel — claims about those mediators are out of scope).
- Mixed-ratio contrast is n=1 paired donor set (R2, FLS SA258R, macrophages SA267R/SA266A) + 1 unpaired (R5) — the composition gradient is therefore an ordering check, not an independently replicated dose-response.
- Donor reuse across cell types (SA266A, SA277A) and replicate batches R1/R2/R5 create dependence; contributing-donor modeling required; no claim of 12 independent donors.
- Inhibitor may have off-target effects (UNC106238 selectivity outside measured scope); FLS_Inhibitor sample (n=1) partially controls direct FLS effects.
- Known NOTCH3/HBEGF/PGDH RA findings are NOT the subject of this proposal and are not rebranded here; this is a MerTK-subset/state question. Co-expression alone would not establish ligand directionality — directionality here rests on the one-sided pretreatment logic (macrophage pretreated, FLS readout), which is stated as inference, not proof.
