# Clinically decision-anchored fixed-lock validation of the vasopressor-restart endpoint

## What is inherited and what changes

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. Every frozen MIMIC finding, source binding, prospective full-verification requirement, fixed-lock separation, recurrence rule, clock rule, adjudication rule, indeterminate handling, transport safeguard, and noncausal evidence boundary is retained below. The change is to the primary acceptance criterion: PPV is descriptive and no longer a prerequisite acceptance metric. Acceptance is based principally on arm/configuration-specific sensitivity and specificity and on an exact, prespecified bound and simulation of distortion that the locked endpoint could introduce into a later randomized risk-difference (RD) or risk-difference noninferiority (NI) decision.

The clinical decision is whether this EHR endpoint can be used in a future trial comparing a randomized stopping-order policy (NE-first versus VP-first) without allowing endpoint misclassification to alter a clinically consequential conclusion. Validation cannot estimate the treatment effect, establish equipoise, or establish endpoint importance.

## Evidence boundary and frozen retrospective findings

The unresolved prospective hypothesis is that, among the first qualifying patients in both **observed first-stop strata** (the drug stopped first, NE or VP), the locked EHR restart label has adequate, configuration-specific sensitivity and specificity for intent-qualified pump rescue, and its induced distortion of a prespecified future randomized contrast is no more than 0.01 absolute risk difference over the prespecified event-rate/effect region. A second gate requires that the distortion cannot reverse an otherwise clinically clear NI or inferiority decision under the prespecified rule. PPV and NPV are reported descriptively because they depend on future prevalence.

Frozen MIMIC results are not pump validation and remain unchanged. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, assigned actions were rare: 211 NE-first (3.49%) and 167 VP-first (2.76%), both below the 5% gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort had 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate composite failures. Arms were nonrandomized, nonexchangeable, and too small for causal comparison. Of 73 failures among 136 determinate episodes, 59 included a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 were restart-only. MCS status was unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvements (approximately 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, below the 0.01 gate), with adverse later transport; no score or subgroup advance was retained.

The complete transaction audit used all 137 inherited episodes and all rows remaining after declared stay, item, and time filters, recovered all 59 raw restarts, and found that five symmetric reconstruction rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices. The inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries. These are EHR-label instability findings, not pump truth. Status, chain, dose, caregiver, `storetime`, open-bag/transfer fields, and intermittent MAP cannot establish delivered drug, genuine cessation, rescue intent, false-positive rate, or MCS absence. The identity linking prevalence, sensitivity, and PPV remains enforced, but it is not an acceptance criterion.

## Primary estimand and validation strata versus future randomized arms

Let `A` be the stopped-drug stratum in validation (`A=NE` if NE is stopped first and VP continues; `A=VP` conversely), `C` the locked site/ICU/pump-EHR configuration, `E` the locked EHR restart endpoint, and `R` intent-qualified pump-native rescue. Estimate `Se_{a,c}=P(E=1|R=1,A=a,C=c)` and `Sp_{a,c}=P(E=0|R=0,A=a,C=c)`; report PPV/NPV only as prevalence-specific descriptions.

These are **not randomized treatment arms**. Observed first-stop strata are used because the measurement mechanism depends on which drug is stopped, and these are the only prospective strata needed to emulate the endpoint under each possible future stopping-order assignment. No NE-versus-VP accuracy difference is interpreted causally. The validation cohort must accrue both strata consecutively; no enrichment, arm-specific stopping, or selection by E or R is allowed.

In the future trial, `Z=NE` or `VP` is randomized stopping-order assignment, with the same pump-native `t0`, EHR algorithm, six-hour window, and configuration definitions. The transport assumption being tested is measurement transport, not treatment transport: conditional on `A=Z`, configuration, protocol-defined baseline cells, and complete capture, validation `Se` and `Sp` apply to the trial endpoint. Because validation A is observational, this assumption requires overlap and explicit monitoring of baseline cells; it does not require or claim exchangeability of the observed validation patients.

The primary validation unit is each patient's first qualifying cessation across all ICUs in the confirmatory phase. Every later qualifying episode is fully collected and verified, but is a separate clustered secondary estimand and cannot rescue a failed first-patient claim. If a future protocol allows repeated endpoints, extension requires the separate recurrent gate below.

## Clinically anchored distortion estimand

For a future randomized assignment `z`, let `p_z=P(R=1|Z=z)` be the true intent-qualified rescue risk and `q_z=P(E=1|Z=z)` the observed locked EHR endpoint risk. For one configuration (or a specified configuration mixture),

`q_z = f_z + c_z p_z`, where `f_z=1-Sp_z` and `c_z=Se_z+Sp_z-1`.

The endpoint-induced RD distortion is

`B(p_NE,p_VP;Se,Sp) = (q_NE-q_VP) - (p_NE-p_VP)`

`= (Sp_VP-Sp_NE) + (Se_NE+Sp_NE-2)p_NE - (Se_VP+Sp_VP-2)p_VP`.

This explicitly includes differential false-positive rates and differential sensitivity/specificity; a pooled accuracy or PPV margin cannot bound it. For a future configuration mixture with known design weights `w_{z,c}`, use `q_z=sum_c w_{z,c}[1-Sp_{z,c}+(Se_{z,c}+Sp_{z,c}-1)p_{z,c}]` and `p_z=sum_c w_{z,c}p_{z,c}`. If the future weights are not fixed, the acceptance bound takes the supremum over the prespecified weight simplex (or the protocol's documented lower/upper weight constraints), never over a post hoc favorable mixture.

Before the first calibration patient, a clinician/statistician panel must escrow the planning region from the future trial protocol and an external planning source, not from validation outcomes: each arm's true rescue risk `p_NE,p_VP ∈ [0.05,0.30]`, and true RD `D=p_NE-p_VP ∈ [-0.05,0.05]` (the intersection with the square is used). The region is deliberately centered on event rates at which a rescue endpoint could be consequential and includes both clinically harmful and beneficial order effects; the validation does not claim that these are MIMIC prevalences. If the future protocol or external planning source justifies a different region, it must be locked before validation and all formulas below rerun; it cannot be changed after labels are seen.

The future NI margin is `Δ_NI=0.03` absolute risk. The idealized large-sample decision boundary is `D>-0.03` for NI and `D<-0.03` for inferiority; the boundary itself is indeterminate. For clinical decision-reversal assessment, define clearly NI as `D≥-0.01` and clearly inferior as `D≤-0.05`; `-0.05<D<-0.01` is an explicitly inconclusive zone around the NI boundary. These values must be clinically approved and escrowed with the region. Validation does not replace the future trial's sampling confidence interval or multiplicity procedure.

### Exact acceptance gates

Using the joint confidence set for all arm-by-configuration sensitivities and specificities (one-sided 95% lower bounds for each sensitivity and specificity, with a prespecified simultaneous construction across the finite supported configuration cells), calculate the worst-case absolute distortion

`B_max = sup_{(p_NE,p_VP) in region, (Se,Sp) in joint confidence set, allowed w} |B|`.

The primary endpoint-acceptability gate is `B_max ≤ 0.010` RD units, with equality acceptable only if the prespecified confidence-set convention includes it. The maximum is computed exactly at polytope vertices because B is affine in p and in each sensitivity/specificity coordinate conditional on weights; if weight uncertainty is present, enumerate its simplex vertices. A brute-force grid is only a verification display, not the primary calculation.

The decision-reversal gate is evaluated jointly, not by comparing separate arm metrics. For every vertex/corner of the same joint confidence set, every allowed configuration mixture, and a prespecified fine grid plus interval proof over the region, compute the deterministic reversal indicator
`1{D≤-0.05 and D+B>-0.03} or 1{D≥-0.01 and D+B≤-0.03}`.
The gate requires zero possible reversals. Thus a small average bias cannot conceal a direction-specific reversal. As a finite-trial operating characteristic, additionally simulate the planned future trial at 10,000 seeds for a prespecified sample size, randomization ratio and NI analysis, drawing `E` conditionally on `R` using the accepted sensitivity/specificity corners. Among clearly inferior and clearly NI scenarios, the two-sided probability that the E-based decision differs from the R-based decision must be ≤2%; if the planned future trial design is not yet fixed, this simulation is reported but cannot support a future-trial operating claim. Scenarios in the inconclusive zone are expected to be inconclusive and are not counted as failures.

The sensitivity/specificity precision gates are also retained and made subordinate safeguards: each arm-specific lower bound for sensitivity and specificity must exceed 0.80, and the NE-minus-VP difference envelopes for each metric must be strictly inside (-0.10,0.10). PPV/NPV have no 0.80 acceptance gate. Failure of either distortion/reversal gate is adverse even if the six conventional gates pass. If the confidence set is too wide to classify either distortion gate, the result is inconclusive rather than supportive.

## Consecutive population, clocks, endpoint, and reference

Recruit at least six adult ICUs with synchronized smart-pump, monitor, and EHR exports. Each configuration completes a fresh 50-episode technical run-in after definitions lock. At least 95% of run-in episodes must have unique patient-pump-channel-drug linkage, measured clock maps, and complete pump export from two hours before index through the six-hour window; at least 90% must have contemporaneous intent evidence. A failed configuration repeats with 50 new episodes; a second failure makes it feasibility-only. Run-in episodes are excluded.

After run-in, capture every consecutive qualifying cessation regardless of E or R. Eligibility uses only information available at cessation: adult ICU patient; linkable pump-delivered concurrent NE and VP for six continuous hours; during the final 30 minutes NE >0 and ≤0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 and ≤0.04 U/min with neither increasing; no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours; at least three valid MAP observations spanning three of four prior 30-minute bins including the final bin, median MAP ≥65 and minimum MAP ≥55; and a non-simultaneous first cessation while the comparator continues pump-native delivery. Simultaneous crossing within five minutes and known procedure/transport pauses are excluded and counted. Actual MCS type, active/standby state and setting are captured; MCS absence is never inferred from silence.

`t0` is the first pump timestamp at which the first-stopped drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min (NE) or 0.005 U/min (VP) and remains below for five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Programmed rate alone is insufficient. The observable target is `(t0,min[t0+6 hours,death,ICU exit]]`; death/exit closes determinately only with complete capture through closure. A technical/export/linkage gap before closure makes R indeterminate. An escrowed affine source-clock map includes offset, drift, timezone/DST, fitting rows, residuals and immutable version; the configuration boundary band is the run-in 99th percentile absolute residual capped at 60 seconds, and a wider required band fails qualification.

The EHR algorithm is frozen before reference adjudication. MIMIC-compatible mappings are NE item 221906, retaining mcg/kg/min and converting mcg/min using valid weight 30–300 kg (reject >5 mcg/kg/min), and VP item 222315, converting units/hour by 60 (reject >0.2 U/min). E=1 requires a new positive stopped-drug interval with `endtime>starttime` and mapped `starttime∈(t0,t0+6 hours]`, before death/exit; an interval spanning t0 is not new. A mapped timestamp uncertainty set crossing t0, six hours, or closure yields E=? under the primary rule. Chain/status/storetime/caregiver/open-bag/transfer/order fields cannot repair E.

R=1 requires linked delivered pump rate for the stopped drug to reach its index threshold continuously for five minutes after the off interval within the observable window **and** two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators to conclude from timestamped contemporaneous evidence that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion. Bag replacement, channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, technical/procedural pause, mistaken-stop correction, and documentation-only segmentation are reference-negative. Blinded adjudicators see traces, workflow, cointerventions and intent evidence but not E, diagnostics, aggregate accuracy, or each other's label. Labels include rescue-positive, pump resumption without rescue intent, technical/workflow continuation, no resumption, clock/export/linkage failure, and intent-indeterminate; a blinded third adjudicator resolves disagreement. After the first 100 calibration episodes, raw agreement <85% or both Gwet AC1 and kappa <0.70 suspends accrual; clarification cannot inspect E×R accuracy. A second failure makes operations inconclusive. Pump-only reference is secondary and cannot replace intent-qualified R.

## Disposition, recurrence, indeterminate, and matching safeguards

Every screened cessation receives exactly one disposition: eligible first patient, eligible recurrent episode, simultaneous stop, known procedure/transport pause, other ineligibility, capture/linkage failure, or duplicate transition. Every eligible episode has E and R in `{0,1,?}`; no complete-case deletion. Report the full 3×3 table by stopped-drug stratum, phase, site, configuration, interface family, person, and first/recurrent status.

For confirmation, enumerate every joint binary completion of all unknown labels subject only to determinate data. Compute Se, Sp, PPV, NPV and distortion gates for each completion; use the joint envelope, never incompatible metric-specific imputations. Exact integer/corner optimization may replace person-level enumeration only after equality is unit-tested against exhaustive tables with 0–8 indeterminate patients. Indeterminacy >5% overall, >7.5% in any contributing arm-by-configuration cell with ≥20 first patients, or a stratum difference >5 percentage points is adverse operational evidence and prevents support.

All later episodes are collected. A recurrent episode requires both drugs to satisfy the six-hour baseline again and t0 at least 24 hours after prior t0; transitions within 24 hours or without a new baseline remain in the prior episode and are counted. Report episode-per-patient, sequence, interepisode time, switching, first/recurrent prevalence and accuracy, and cluster count. Use ≥10,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates stratified by stopped-drug stratum/configuration and marginal GEE with patient clusters and configuration fixed effects. A first-versus-recurrent difference >0.10 in sensitivity or specificity makes extension to repeated episodes adverse.

For secondary timing/event analysis, map all times and use one-to-one maximum-cardinality, minimum-total-absolute-lag bipartite matching within person, episode, drug and window under a locked ±10-minute caliper. Ties are earlier pump time, earlier mapped EHR time, then immutable row ID. Matching cannot cross t0, closure, six hours, person, episode or drug and cannot change primary E or R.

## Fixed-lock sample-size and confirmation logic

After run-in, fully verify 1,200 consecutive first qualifying patients as calibration, excluded from confirmatory accuracy. A firewall statistician may see only stratum, E-denominator membership, R-denominator membership, indeterminate flags, configuration, first/recurrent flag and calendar accrual—not E×R cells, accuracy, differences or narratives. For four denominator probabilities (NE/E-positive, VP/E-positive, NE/R-positive, VP/R-positive), compute the Jeffreys Beta(0.5,0.5) 1.25th percentile. For each lower prevalence, find the smallest N with at least 0.975 probability of 220 denominator observations and lock Nconfirm to the maximum. This is a planning operating target, not an inferential alpha claim. Add protocol-identical configurations only after run-in and before lock. If Nconfirm>6,000, fewer than six sites/two families exist, or completion is not possible within 48 months, declare infeasible. Calibration may repeat once only after documented configuration expansion, with 1,200 new first patients.

Before the first confirmatory patient, escrow participating configurations, Nconfirm, common start and common lock. Enroll every consecutive qualifying first patient; fully verify all recurrent episodes. Lock after exactly Nconfirm or 48 months, with hard cap 6,000 confirmatory first patients (7,200 including calibration). No arm-specific closeout, denominator stopping, early success/futility, unblinded look, alpha spending, or extension after labels are known. A metric denominator <220 is inconclusive. Infrastructure/adjudication failure is inconclusive.

The inherited planning stress test is retained: using frozen MIMIC arm shares (84/137 NE, 53/137 VP) and E-positive fractions (46/84 NE, 13/53 VP), at common 15% reference prevalence an oracle fixed requirement was 4,293, only 69.2% of 3,000 calibration simulations were feasible under the 6,000 cap (median feasible lock 5,424); at 20%, 99.7% were feasible (median 4,101); at 10%, the VP denominator reached 220 by 6,000 with only 0.800 probability; at 5%, the cap was impossible. These are planning stress tests, not prevalence estimates. The relation `P(E=1|A)×PPV=P(R=1|A)×Sensitivity` remains an interpretation check; low prevalence with similar E positivity is adverse evidence against joint accuracy, not a reason to accrue indefinitely.

## Configuration transport and inference

A configuration is site, ICU, pump vendor/model/software major, integration engine, EHR medication module/build, drug-library version and clock-map method/version. Changes create new configurations and fresh run-in; pre/post data are not silently pooled. At lock require ≥6 sites and ≥2 families; both strata at ≥4 sites and ≥2 families; no site/configuration/family >30% of any metric denominator; each stratum-metric denominator ≥40 in ≥3 configurations and ≥20 in ≥4. For every supported configuration (denominator ≥40), conservative lower bounds for sensitivity and specificity exceed 0.70. A prediction interval below 0.70, adjusted range >0.15, or Holm-significant interaction with absolute difference >0.10 is adverse. Leave-one-site/config/family-out analyses must retain all global accuracy and distortion gates. Sparse support is inconclusive. A configuration with linkage/clock/export failure >5% in its latest 50 episodes repeats run-in; after confirmation repair cannot re-enter the confirmatory mixture. Two reliability failures or fewer than four active sites makes operations inconclusive.

Arm-specific Wilson one-sided 95% lower bounds are used for sensitivity and specificity, with strict >0.80 gates. NE-minus-VP metric differences use 90% Miettinen–Newcombe score intervals with continuity correction and the joint-completion envelope strictly inside (-0.10,0.10). Use a configuration-stratified patient bootstrap (≥10,000) as sensitivity; reversal of any pass is inconclusive. Report prevalence, PPV/NPV, E/R prevalence discrepancy, McNemar discordance, pump-only performance, timing, documentation strata, and the full cornerwise decision-distortion table.

## Interpretation

Supportive means all strict sensitivity/specificity, symmetry, full-verification, fixed-lock, indeterminate, recurrence, clock, adjudication, configuration, exact `B_max≤0.010`, zero-corner-reversal, and prespecified future-trial simulation gates pass. It supports only use of E as an endpoint component for the explicitly enumerated configurations and population. Adverse means any completed accuracy/symmetry gate fails, Bmax exceeds 0.010, any clinically clear NI/inferiority corner can reverse, low prevalence is incoherent with observed E positivity, differential errors are material, recurrent performance is worse, or supported heterogeneity violates transport. Inconclusive means insufficient denominators, excessive or differential indeterminacy, a wide confidence set unable to classify Bmax/reversal, infeasible accrual, capture/linkage/clock/adjudication failure, sparse configuration support, or operations halt.

Computation cannot establish true pump delivery if linkage is false, rescue intent, actual MCS absence, clinical importance, safety, equipoise, treatment benefit, or the validity of the clinician-chosen event/effect region. Those require device governance, expert adjudication, external planning data, or the subsequent randomized study. The substantive advance is a decision-relevant validation claim: rather than treating prevalence-dependent PPV as portable clinical utility, it asks whether plausible arm/configuration-specific misclassification can be proven too small to change a consequential future randomized contrast.
