# Episode 32 proposal: transport- and plan-aware incremental AFP signal after a recorded TACE-like index

## Parent and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s first encounter-constrained timed TACE-like index, all-index early competing transitions, L=14/L=45 component-resolved AFP/albumin/total-bilirubin profile, C2 procedure-plus-order concordance, non-liver procedure control, local-history and observation-opportunity controls, patient-temporal evaluation, and strict recorded-care evidence boundary.

The remaining clinically consequential uncertainty is transport through two linked selection processes. First, AFP is only observed in a locally measured subset; missingness can reflect contact, care setting, clinician choice, or outside care. Second, a later concordant TACE-like record may already have a locally documented plan. A profile that predicts C2 only among frequently measured or plan-positive patients is not evidence that the profile can identify a reproducible post-index reassessment pathway for the broader eligible population.

The future solver must newly construct and estimate, not merely reproduce prose:

1. A row-level manifest of every eligible index patient, including early C2/C1/C0/D/N transitions, incomplete profiles and observation loss.
2. Training-frozen AFP, albumin and bilirubin assay rules; L=14 and L=45 panel-observation indicators; local-history and source-specific observation opportunity.
3. The parent AFP-incremental C2-minus-N standardized contrast on (a) complete-panel patients, (b) the all-index population with an observation model, and (c) plan-positive versus plan-negative recorded contexts.
4. A transparent competing-risk baseline and a substantive irregular-time latent multi-output model fitted to the same target, split, outcomes and common-support rules.
5. Locked predictions, CIFs, calibration/log score, patient-clustered uncertainty, overlap/effective sample size, transport-weight sensitivity, plan-cell counts, permutation/placebo and endpoint-timing falsifications.
6. An interpretation manifest mapping every conclusion to an output, interval, endpoint/context definition, split and frozen criterion.

Completion requires these outputs or an explicit infeasibility/positivity/convergence record. No fitted result is claimed in this proposal.

## Strongest supported evidence and unresolved claim

Available evidence supports data availability and operational recorded endpoints only. The HCC snapshot contains dated procedures, non-drug orders, examinations, encounters and laboratory records, linked by (Patient Master Index, Visit Number). A bounded read-only audit found 338,040 procedure rows, 322,166 with procedure start times, 7,781 procedure labels, frequent explicit embolization/TACE-like labels, and nonzero TACE, hepatic-artery, embolization, CT, MRI and ultrasound terms in the first 200,000 order rows. This establishes feasible fields and vocabulary, not outcome prevalence or clinical validity.

The unresolved claim is:

> Among all eligible patients after a locally recorded TACE-like index, does AFP trajectory add liver-specific information beyond albumin/bilirubin trajectory, prior local history and observation opportunity when the estimand is transported to the full eligible index population, and is the incremental contrast preserved outside a locally recorded future-plan context?

Primary hypothesis, stated noncausally: after standardizing to the full-index covariate distribution under a prespecified observation model, favorable versus discordant AFP change has a directionally lower 320-day CIF for strict concordant C2 than for a matched non-liver recorded procedure N, with a clinically meaningful AFP-specific C2-minus-N contrast. The contrast is expected to be weaker or absent in plan-positive strata than in plan-negative recorded-plan strata. This is a falsifiable recorded-pathway hypothesis, not a claim about viable tumor, response, treatment intent or benefit.

Clinical importance: if the signal transports beyond measured patients and is not explained by a pre-existing recorded plan or generic local procedure capture, it would justify prospective testing of laboratory-triggered reassessment workflows. If it disappears after transport weighting, plan adjustment, or negative controls, applying it to surveillance would risk mistaking measurement access or scheduling for disease-specific information.

## Population, temporal boundaries and information sets

Use one first-index episode per patient. Aggregate each child table before joining. Build 24-hour within-patient/within-encounter procedure episodes from `procedures` using `surgery start time`; retain all tied rows and invalid/missing times in an audit. Candidate index labels are training-frozen literal `TACE`, `transarterial chemoembolization`, or both `hepatic artery` and `embolization`; hepatic angiography alone is not therapeutic. Require a `diagnoses` row whose `Diagnosis Name` contains `Hepatocellular Carcinoma`, linked to an encounter whose `Visit Time` is within [t0-180 days,t0+7 days]. This is diagnosis ascertainment by encounter time, not disease onset. Require t0 <= 2025-01-01. Retain every eligible index patient regardless of later measurement, early transition, or local follow-up.

Primary post-boundary outcomes run from L through t0+365; the all-index multi-state analysis begins at t0 and retains early absorbing events. Report CIFs at 30, 90 and 320 days after t0 and conditional post-L estimates, clearly labeled.

The focal exposure is exact-name AFP `Alpha-fetoprotein`; albumin `Albumin` and total bilirubin `Total Bilirubin` are adjustment components. Use assay/specimen-specific numeric values, training-only standardization and log1p change where valid; do not pool raw values across assays without units, and do not call the profile ALBI/MELD or a validated reserve score. Preserve qualitative, inequality and missing categories.

## Observation transport and plan context

Define panel observation A=1 when the required AFP, albumin and bilirubin measurements satisfy the frozen L-boundary rules. A=0 is retained in the all-index cohort; it is not assumed to mean stable disease or outside care.

Construct pre-index opportunity O from dated local records in (t0-365,t0): distinct encounter dates/keys; counts/presence of labs, orders, examinations, medications, procedures, documents and pathology; days with any record; and valid encounter department/admission/discharge fields. These are local capture measures, not complete utilization or access. Add prior recorded liver-directed episode count/days since last valid episode and prior HCC-encounter count, while acknowledging that outside treatment is unobserved.

Define a training-frozen recorded-plan proxy P using `orders` rows created in [t0,t0+45 days] by `order time`. Primary qualifying screen: literal `TACE`, `transarterial chemoembolization`, or `hepatic artery` with therapeutic `embolization`/chemotherapy. Keep imaging-only, angiography-only, and `interventional`-only labels as broad/ambiguous controls. Preserve `order status`; non-canceled status is sensitivity only. P=0 means no qualifying local plan was recorded, never “unplanned”.

Define C2 as a strict timed TACE-like procedure episode linked on both keys to a qualifying order whose `Order time` or `Start time` is within [-24,+24] hours of the earliest valid procedure time. C1 is a strict procedure without concordant order; C0 is broad/ambiguous liver-directed; D diagnostic/technical; N timed non-liver procedure; O_loss no observed local record after a prespecified 90-day gap, with 30/180-day sensitivities. Keep unknown labels separate. For later C2, R is an optional context label if a qualifying liver/upper-abdominal examination/order from `examinations` occurs in the preceding 30 days; it is a descriptor of an observed event, not a predictor or mediator.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

All models use the same patients, boundaries, endpoints, common-support rule, patient split and uncertainty procedure.

Baseline B_transport is a regularized discrete-time cause-specific competing-risk model. It includes history, age/sex, pre-index observation opportunity, measurement counts/timing, missingness, early transition indicators and plan-process variables, but no raw lab trajectory for the observation model. Fit the observation model on training data only; report positivity, calibration and effective sample size. Then add the AFP/albumin/bilirubin component states to estimate full-index standardized CIFs and the incremental AFP-specific C2-minus-N contrast under stabilized inverse-observation weights. Also report complete-panel and unweighted analyses as estimands, not substitutes.

Substantive alternative M_joint is an irregular-time multi-output state-space/marked-event model. It jointly models latent AFP, albumin and bilirubin trajectories, assay/specimen observation error, exact measurement times, qualitative/inequality indicators, local contact intensity, plan-process observations through day 45 and competing C2/C1/C0/D/N transitions. It outputs full-index and plan-stratified CIFs and posterior/bootstrapped uncertainty. It can reveal asynchronous nonlinear latent change and informative testing that the categorized baseline loses. It is selected for the measurement-selection scientific uncertainty, not for complexity or GPU use. CPU-first is appropriate; GPU use is optional only if a solver measures a benefit.

Alternative not chosen: a transformer over clinical documents is deferred because document/pathology rows lack usable row-level time and outcomes remain unadjudicated; image/multimodal models are impossible because image files are absent; ALBI/MELD/mechanistic reserve modeling is deferred because units, INR, validated thresholds and reserve adjudication are absent; causal treatment-policy and survival analyses require intent, assignment, completion, mortality and outside-care data. Revisit only if those dependencies arrive.

## Split, uncertainty, falsification and interpretation

Use a patient-level temporal split: 2010-2022 fit, 2023 tune, 2024 through the cutoff locked test when support permits; otherwise a deterministic patient-hash split fixed before fitting. No patient crosses splits. Freeze lexical screens, assay rules, time windows, sparse-cell/overlap thresholds, tuning and margins before locked evaluation. Use patient-clustered bootstrap or influence-function intervals, never row bootstrap.

Supportive computation requires: parent continuity; nontrivial full-index panel support and overlap; an AFP-specific C2-minus-N contrast beyond albumin/bilirubin and observation-only baseline; weaker generic-N, placebo and timestamp-permuted contrasts; materially weaker or absent plan-positive contrast than plan-negative, if cells support it; stable L=14/L=45 and ±6/24/72-hour C2 sensitivities; calibrated predictions; and a converged joint model with prior/observation sensitivity. This supports only a robust association with a locally recorded pathway and motivates prospective adjudication.

Adverse computation includes no AFP increment after observation adjustment, equal/stronger N and placebo effects, plan-only baseline explaining the contrast, concentration in complete/frequent-measurement patients, poor overlap or unstable weights, C2 window/status dependence, or latent-model reversal. These favor capture, scheduling, coding or measurement explanations.

Inconclusive computation includes sparse C2/N or P strata, positivity failure, insufficient follow-up, timestamp/assay ambiguity, temporal-split failure, nonconvergence/prior domination, or intervals crossing the prespecified margin. It does not establish absence of tumor response or absence of treatment benefit.

Falsifications: within-patient assay-time permutation; pre-index placebo windows; order-time permutation preserving order text/status; non-liver and diagnostic endpoint controls; strict-versus-broad and C1/C2/C0 sensitivity; same-encounter versus cross-encounter concordance; ±6/24/72-hour order windows; exclusion of index-encounter and first-24-hour orders; canceled versus non-canceled order status; 30/90/180-day observation-loss gaps; and complete-panel versus all-index transport comparison. A preserved association in time permutations or unrelated endpoints is adverse.

The verifier can check source paths, schema IDs, headers, two-key aggregation, information boundaries, no leakage, frozen lexical rules, CIF/competing-risk calculations, predictions, calibration, uncertainty, overlap, permutations and conclusion-to-output links. It cannot determine intent, order completion, technical success, radiologic/pathologic response, viable tumor, recurrence/progression, liver failure, death, survival, benefit, utility, appropriateness, outside care or external validity. Those require clinical adjudication, linked mortality/outside-care data and prospective or external validation.

## Method exploration record

The simple baseline and substantive alternative answer the same AFP-incremental full-index question. The baseline is CPU-first regularized discrete-time competing risk with transparent observation weighting; it is expected to be auditable but its runtime is not measured. The alternative is the irregular-time latent state-space/marked-event model; it uses exact asynchronous measurements and an observation process to estimate what categorical complete-panel analyses lose. Its runtime, memory and convergence are unverified; a future solver may use at most 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB and 28800 seconds, with one allocated A100 only if measured useful. Selection is deferred to convergence, predictive scoring, calibration and prior sensitivity. A nonconvergent or prior-dominated latent fit cannot override the baseline.
