# Episode 93 successor: a cross-practice review score must earn both its queue and its probability threshold

## Substantive repair

Candidate-129 makes portable-versus-local absolute calibration the primary replacement decision. Candidate-4eb0 makes bidirectional fixed-capacity ranking the primary transport decision, with calibration as a gate. Their unresolved deployment defect is that neither makes a calibration pass clinically meaningful for a capacity-limited review service. An intercept correction cannot change within-hospital ordering. Thus a score can pass calibration while adding no queue value, or rank a useful queue while its displayed probability is shifted.

This successor changes the primary estimand while preserving the audited all-baseline/L24 optional-repeat cohort, source bindings, clocks, outcomes, and frozen features. The primary decision is:

May a receiving ICU use a source-stratum-trained, label-free, repeat-lactate prognostic score in the opposite repeat-practice stratum for both (a) a 10%-capacity mortality-review queue and (b) a displayed 10% D7-risk threshold, without first obtaining target-hospital outcome labels?

These are co-primary, non-interchangeable uses. Calibration cannot count as queue utility; ranking cannot validate a probability. The target-label recalibration is a historical reference, not a prospective deployment method.

## Unresolved question, evidence, and advance

At 24 hours after an early norepinephrine entry, repeat lactate may be prognostic partly because practices measure it differently. A receiving ICU needs to know both whether a score trained in one outcome-blind measurement environment transfers to another and what kind of use remains defensible when transport is incomplete.

The strongest available evidence is narrower. Baysan et al. (2022; DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750; PMCID PMC9444407) reported modest added discrimination from 24-hour lactate in a selected sepsis derivation/validation setting, with recalibrated C-statistic 0.62 to 0.64. This does not establish this EICU cohort, cross-practice transport, a 10% threshold, or review benefit. Sisk et al. (2021; DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242; PMCID PMC7810439) reviewed informative presence/observation and measurement-process predictors and noted that guidance remains needed about when to use them. It does not establish that EICU repeat availability is clinician attention or that a transported score improves care.

The untested claims are: (1) bidirectionally, adding the numeric repeat-lactate score to the process-aware score materially improves a fixed 10% review queue; (2) a source-only intercept yields a displayed probability with acceptable absolute error and no more than a 0.02 disadvantage versus held-out local-label recalibration; and (3) both properties hold simultaneously. Their separation is clinically informative.

No execution result is assumed. The research-ambition README was inspected for all three demonstrations and availability limits. The cancer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not treated as read. Its supplement and metadata are not evidence for this proposal.

## Primary estimands

Freeze hospitals as low versus high repeat availability before outcome fitting. Let d be low-to-high or high-to-low transfer, A its source stratum, and B its target stratum.

### Capacity-limited queue

Fit source-only R_full and R_process. For target hospital h define k_h=ceil(0.10*n_h). Rank each model's raw linear predictor descending, breaking ties by ascending patientunitstayid. Let Q_full,h and Q_process,h be exact top-k sets.

Define:

Delta7_d = [sum_h {sum(D7 in Q_full,h) - sum(D7 in Q_process,h)}] / sum_h k_h.

This is additional observed D7 deaths per occupied review slot from the numeric-repeat score over the process-aware score. The operational materiality margin is 0.03 per slot, not a validated treatment benefit or utility.

Also define:

U7_d = mean over target hospitals h of [mean(D7 in Q_full,h) - mean(D7 among all eligible people in h)].

This prevents a favorable contrast against a weak comparator from sufficing. The queue component requires in each direction lower95(Delta7_d)>=0.03 and lower95(U7_d)>=0.02. A random-within-hospital queue is reported as a separate baseline.

Dinf is secondary. Require lower95(DeltaInf_d)>=-0.02 in each direction as a no-material-eventual-death-tradeoff guard. This is not a safety claim.

Report c in {0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20} secondarily, but c=0.10 is primary and no favorable alternate capacity rescues it.

### Label-free absolute-risk threshold

For source-trained R_full eta_0, estimate portable source offset delta_A from nested source-hospital-held-out D7 predictions and apply it to targets:

p_tr = expit(eta_0 + delta_A).

For the historical local-label reference, keep eta_0 fixed. For each target hospital h estimate delta_B,-h from D7 labels in all other target hospitals and score h:

p_loc = expit(eta_0 + delta_B,-h).

No target label enters p_tr, and no label from h enters its p_loc. The local reference asks how much is lost by foregoing local outcomes; it is not a label-free deployment method.

For direction d, repeat availability s in {0,1}, and z(p)=1[p>=0.10], including equality, define:

E_d(p;s,z)=mean(D7-p | target d, F_available=s, z(p)=z),
q_d(p)=max over s,z of abs(E_d(p;s,z)),
G_d=q_d(p_tr)-q_d(p_loc).

The threshold component requires in each direction upper95(q_d(p_tr))<=0.05 and upper95(G_d)<=0.02, at least 30 original target people in each of that direction's four p_tr cells and four p_loc cells (eight cells per prediction type across both directions), and deletion stability. The margins are prespecified analytic screens, not guideline or utility thresholds.

Report per cell n, events/non-events, flagged fraction, observed mean risk, mean p_tr, mean p_loc, E_tr, E_loc, and their difference. Report threshold workload and threshold-versus-capacity set overlap descriptively; workload cannot rescue calibration.

Both intercepts must preserve eta_0 within-hospital ordering and every fixed-capacity queue exactly, apart from documented numerical ties. This is an implementation audit, not queue evidence. Queue utility is independently tested against R_process above.

### Co-primary decision

Supportive dual-use transfer requires all of the following in both directions: Delta7 lower bound>=0.03; U7 lower bound>=0.02; DeltaInf lower bound>=-0.02; q_tr upper bound<=0.05; G upper bound<=0.02; all cell, replicate, deletion, and leakage gates pass.

Queue support with threshold failure permits only a rank-only historical statement and forbids calling p_tr a validated 10% probability. Threshold support with queue failure permits only a calibration-screen statement and does not justify allocating review slots to R_full. Neither partial pattern supports dual use.

## Falsification and evidence limits

Not-computable precedes interpretation for any source/schema/hash/member/key or audit mismatch; altered cohort, clock, feature, fold, threshold, capacity, tie, or outcome rule; post-L24 or target-label leakage; nonfinite fit/root/score; wrong copied-hospital exclusion; wrong cell or queue arithmetic; broken intercept rank invariance; missing artifacts; or fewer than 1,900 finite replicates.

If computable:

- Supportive dual use: every co-primary and guard criterion passes in both directions.
- Adverse queue portability: upper95(Delta7_d)<0.03 or upper95(U7_d)<0.02 in either direction.
- Adverse threshold portability: lower95(q_tr,d)>0.05 or lower95(G_d)>0.02 in either direction.
- Adverse eventual-death tradeoff: upper95(DeltaInf_d)<-0.02.
- Inconclusive: all other computable patterns, including boundary crossing, sparse cells, deletion instability, or non-decisive directional discordance.

The verifier can recompute population, clocks, fits, leakage exclusions, queues, cells, uncertainty, states, and whether conclusions match outputs. It must reject support based on point estimates, one direction, one favorable capacity, pooled cell error, calibration alone, ranking alone, local-label results alone, or separate non-joint uncertainty. Test fixtures must include correct calculations paired with unsupported dual-use conclusions, plus appropriate interpretations of supportive, adverse, and inconclusive combinations.

Automatic verification cannot establish true norepinephrine administration or indication; lactate specimen, assay, order, release, or viewing validity; actual review, staffing, workload, action, preventability, fairness, safety, treatment effect, external/temporal transport, or patient benefit. EICU D7 is source-defined in-hospital death by H7, not post-discharge mortality. vitalPeriodic is a five-minute summary, not raw waveform; public narrative sections are removed.

Stronger conclusions require medication/laboratory adjudication, synchronized workflow and capacity data, stakeholder-defined utility and action thresholds, subgroup review, independent temporal/external validation, and an impact or randomized study. Reference execution establishes computational feasibility only, never scientific truth.
