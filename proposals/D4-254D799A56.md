## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens repeatedly nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, FABP1, CHI3L1,
IL-6/CRP family) as disease-specific preclinical biomarkers. It is unresolved whether a
protein–diagnosis association marks a **disease-specific process (claim A)** or mainly a **shared
systemic morbidity/aging axis** elevated across many diagnoses (claim B). This matters clinically:
if the signal is shared, single-protein "disease biomarkers" will not be disease-specific and
early-detection programmes built on them will over-diagnose the multi-morbid; if a disease-specific
residual survives, it is a legitimate target.

## The new rival this episode separates (the substantive advance)
Prior episodes (12–34) established a shared component exists (first-PC variance fraction f1 ≈ 0.17
vs null ≈ 0.004) and pre-specified the held-out cross-disease **transfer-gap** estimand Δ_D. The
strongest remaining *measurement/mechanism* rival, never separated, is that this "shared axis" is
**just a canonical acute-phase/inflammation protein cluster** (CRP/IL-6/SAA/…), i.e. inflammation,
not a distributed systemic-morbidity axis. A discriminating observation exists on which the two
rivals differ: **does the shared component survive removal of a pre-specified acute-phase block?**

## Estimand, baseline vs learned alternative
- **Δ_D** = held-out cross-disease **transfer gap**: difference in proper score (ΔAUC / proper
  scoring rule, participant-level split) between (i) a **learned disease-specific elastic-net /
  latent-axis model** fit on a discovery half and evaluated on held-out diseases, and (ii) a
  **1-D shared-axis baseline** (PC1 score) plus an **age+sex baseline**. Null band = 1,000
  within-panel permutations of the disease labels preserving age-decile × sex × case-count strata.
- **Block-removal coherence criterion**: recompute f1 after removing the acute-phase block; pre-specified
  claim-B coherence requires the effect to survive (f1 change small) AND Δ_D inside the null band.
- Why learn: the baseline captures only a single shared severity axis; the learned alternative can
  reveal disease-specific multi-protein structure and whether transfer degrades — information the
  1-D baseline loses by construction.

## Pre-specified outcomes
- **Supportive of H_B (shared/distributed):** block-removal f1 change < 0.02 **and** Δ_D within the
  95% permutation band, with acceptable power (each included disease ≥200 cases).
- **Adverse (H_A disease-specific):** Δ_D above the 95% permutation band with the shared-axis baseline
  failing to close it.
- **Inconclusive:** wide permutation band / underpowered strata / Δ_D estimate whose CI spans the band.
Both H_B and H_A are informative; a clean null remains valuable.

## Falsification / what would change the next decision

- Revise toward disease-specific targeting if Δ_D clears the band.
- Abandon/defer if the axis is shown to be a technical global factor or coverage/power is inadequate.
