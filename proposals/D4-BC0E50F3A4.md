# UKB 24: Distinct early-warning signals from pancreatic volume and fat under normal glycemia

Admission: feasibility_required. Verify participant overlap, measurement dates, missingness and event support before committing this design.

## Hypotheses to Be Tested

When HbA1c is normal and pancreatic fat is similar, those with smaller pancreatic volume have a higher future risk of diabetes, with a pattern different from that in those with high liver fat.

## Study population and key data

Individuals without diabetes or definite pancreatic disease at imaging; pancreatic volume, pancreatic and liver fat, biochemistry, and follow-up.

## Basic Approach

Model pancreatic volume by body size, compare the joint predictive value of volume and fat, and separately exclude recent diabetes and pancreatic disease.

## Significance of the Research

Explore organ structural heterogeneity before abnormal glucose metabolism.

## Main Challenges

Volume cannot directly represent β-cell mass; segmentation error and innate differences in body size must be addressed.

## Competing Explanations

Occult pancreatic disease or overall body size causes small body volume and diabetes to occur simultaneously.

## Minimal validation and falsification criteria

First audit volumetric segmentation and extreme values; if lagged analyses do not support it, do not interpret it as a stable antecedent structure.
