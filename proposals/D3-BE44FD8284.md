## Unresolved claim and falsifiable test

The unresolved claim is whether macrophage exposure, rather than donor identity, macrophage composition, treatment, batch, or targeted-panel measurement, produces a reproducible FLS transcriptional signature.

Primary test: compare macrophage-positive with FLS-only, and macrophage-positive with macrophage-negative, within the same FLS donor context where available. Use the 33 records with verified sample_id joins, preserve full source feature identifiers, and treat the contributing donor/context rather than cells as the uncertainty unit. Estimate log-expression contrasts with a small count offset, use donor-aware paired/stratified or contributing-donor random-effects analysis, and use condition-preserving donor permutations for uncertainty. Pre-LPS and inhibitor conditions are mechanistic context, not independent causal replicates.

Supportive result: a reproducible condition-specific signature with donor-aware uncertainty, separation from both controls, and no comparable signal in the negative-control contrast. Adverse result: no separation, reversal, or failure to survive donor/condition permutations. Inconclusive result: unresolved donor mapping, insufficient independent donor contexts, low detectability, or uncertainty too large to distinguish communication from composition or technical artifacts.
