# Target-independent care-plan label receipt under value-aware documentation-state restrictions

## Substantive advance and unresolved question

This proposal is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It propagates the first parent's value-aware present-state uncertainty into the second parent's target-independent care-plan receipt analysis while preserving the inherited denominator exactly. It adds an exact row-key reconciliation, a raw-source reclassification of post-freeze target labels, state-stratified receipt, concentration and common-support diagnostics, selection-overlap metrics, and stay- and hospital-clustered uncertainty for prespecified contrasts.

The clinically consequential measurement question is whether the very low receipt of exact ventilation-related care-plan labels is a property of the locked target-independent cohort, or is materially altered by selecting rows with positive value-aware documentation; and whether any apparent difference is reproduced by an airway-independent proxy rather than by airway recency that mechanically defines the overlap groups. This matters before interpreting care-plan labels as evidence about documentation practice, clinician intent, or any downstream quality question. The experiment is designed to falsify a claim about a documentation denominator, not to establish a clinical ventilation episode or treatment benefit.

The testable hypothesis is: exact target-label receipt remains sparse under the locked denominator and does not materially increase in a way that is robust to hospital clustering when the denominator is restricted to an airway-independent positive state. A secondary hypothesis is that any increase under the airway-containing/value-aware restriction is selection by documentation state and not evidence of a clinically meaningful difference.

The strongest evidence-supported claim from the completed computation is narrower: in this frozen 3,788-row denominator, exact target-label receipt is 110/3,788 (2.904%); it is 98/2,453 (3.995%) among value-aware-positive rows, 50/1,492 (3.351%) among protocol-positive rows, and 105/3,564 (2.946%) among airway-independent-positive rows. The airway-independent restriction therefore changes receipt by only +0.04 percentage points relative to the locked union, with clustered intervals crossing zero. Target labels are highly concentrated by hospital and have very limited cross-label common support. These are documentation observations only. They do not establish true ventilation, an SBT, daily extubation assessment, quality, causality, benefit, or transportability.

The unresolved clinical claims are whether the exact care-plan values represent active invasive ventilation, whether they represent a delivered spontaneous breathing trial or merely a plan/documentation choice, whether absence of a label means absence of care, and whether any documentation contrast reflects care quality or outcomes. Those claims require blinded clinical adjudication and separate outcome/confounding analyses.

## Locked population and estimand

The inherited population is frozen before inspection of the target labels and before application of the new state restrictions. It contains 3,788 unique `(patientunitstayid, landmark)` rows, 2,071 stays, and 77 hospitals, with mutually exclusive overlap groups:

- `unbounded_only`: 1,148 rows;
- `retained_24h_only`: 1,462 rows;
- `retained_6h`: 1,178 rows.

The five fixed landmarks are 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes. The inherited present documentation state is evaluated in `[L-360,L)`. No stay is reselected and no membership rule is changed. Repeated landmarks from the same stay remain linked in clustered analyses.

The primary outcome is binary receipt of either exact target value in the post-freeze window `[L,L+360]`, inclusive:

- `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`;
- `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`.

Receipt is classified from the earliest exact target in the window. If both exact target values occur at that earliest offset, the row is retained as target receipt and separately flagged as a conflict; other ventilation values without an exact target are `other_ventilation_label`; no ventilation row is `no_target_label`. This is an exact care-plan label outcome, not an adjudicated SBT or ventilation outcome.

The primary estimands are target-receipt rates within the locked union and within three prespecified restrictions:

1. `locked_union`: all inherited rows;
2. `value_aware_positive`: `state_present_calc == positive` from the value-aware parent;
3. `protocol_positive`: `p_protocol_state == positive` from the protocol proxy;
4. `airway_independent_positive`: `p_ind_state == positive` from the airway-independent proxy.

Secondary estimands are receipt rates within every present-state stratum (`positive`, `negative`, `ambiguous`, `conflicting`) for each proxy, rates within each overlap group, pairwise row/stay/hospital selection overlap, hospital target concentration, exact-label common support, and risk differences with stay and hospital clustering.

## Results

### Exact label receipt and concentration

In the locked union, 79 rows received the daily-evaluation label, 31 received the no-daily-trial label, 219 had another ventilation care-plan value, and 3,459 had no ventilation value. Thus exact target receipt was 110/3,788 (2.904%; stay-cluster 95% interval 2.359% to 3.508%; hospital-cluster interval 0.849% to 4.882%).

By overlap group in the locked union:

- `unbounded_only`: 21/1,148 (1.829%); 13 daily-evaluation and 8 no-daily-trial labels;
- `retained_24h_only`: 50/1,462 (3.420%); 35 and 15 respectively;
- `retained_6h`: 39/1,178 (3.311%); 31 and 8 respectively.

Receipt was concentrated in 18 hospitals, leaving 59 of 77 target-free. Hospital HHI was 0.464, effective target-hospital count 2.16, and hospital 420 supplied 74/110 targets (67.27%). Only five hospital-landmark cells (one hospital) met the common-support rule of at least five targets and both exact labels, containing 1,014 denominator rows and 74 targets. The stricter hospital rule (at least ten targets and both labels) also retained only one hospital and 1,014 rows. Therefore pooled receipt rates should not be interpreted as broadly transportable across hospitals.

### Alternative documentation-state restrictions

Across all overlap groups, receipt was:

- value-aware-positive: 98/2,453 (3.995%; stay interval 3.193% to 4.861%; hospital interval 1.091% to 5.839%);
- protocol-positive: 50/1,492 (3.351%; stay interval 2.447% to 4.321%; hospital interval 0.648% to 4.978%);
- airway-independent-positive: 105/3,564 (2.946%; stay interval 2.355% to 3.585%; hospital interval 0.770% to 4.968%).

Risk differences relative to the locked union were +1.091 percentage points for value-aware-positive (stay interval +0.742 to +1.452; hospital interval +0.073 to +1.744), +0.447 points for protocol-positive (stay -0.241 to +1.136; hospital -0.466 to +1.032), and +0.042 points for airway-independent-positive (stay -0.092 to +0.150; hospital -0.276 to +0.219). The value-aware increase is therefore a selection effect in the operational denominator and is not reproduced by the airway-independent restriction.

Within the positive restrictions, the group-specific rates were similar to the locked result: value-aware-positive was 19/579 (3.282%) in unbounded-only, 44/913 (4.819%) in 24h-only, and 35/961 (3.642%) in 6h; protocol-positive was 4/126 (3.175%), 8/228 (3.509%), and 38/1,138 (3.339%); airway-independent-positive was 19/1,079 (1.761%), 48/1,402 (3.424%), and 38/1,083 (3.509%). Protocol-positive versus airway-independent-positive differed by +0.405 points overall (stay interval -0.289 to +1.063; hospital -0.438 to +0.973), while value-aware-positive versus airway-independent-positive differed by +1.049 points (stay +0.702 to +1.388; hospital +0.134 to +1.637).

### State strata and ambiguity

The value-aware state had 2,453 positive rows, 11 negative rows, 1,324 ambiguous rows, and no conflicting rows. Receipt was 98/2,453 (3.995%) in positive rows, 1/11 (9.091%) in negative rows, and 11/1,324 (0.831%) in ambiguous rows. By group, value-aware-positive receipt was 19/579 (3.282%) in unbounded-only, 44/913 (4.819%) in 24h-only, and 35/961 (3.642%) in 6h; value-aware-ambiguous receipt was 2/569 (0.351%), 6/545 (1.101%), and 3/210 (1.429%), respectively. The negative strata are too small for stable inference and are retained as a warning rather than pooled into a positive interpretation.

The protocol state had 1,492 positive, 12 negative, 2,263 ambiguous, and 21 conflicting rows. Receipt was 50/1,492 (3.351%) in positive rows, 0/12 in negative rows, 60/2,263 (2.651%) in ambiguous rows, and 0/21 in conflicting rows. All 21 protocol-conflicting rows occurred in the retained-6h group; they received no exact target and 12 had another ventilation label. The airway-independent state had 3,564 positive, 27 negative, 191 ambiguous, and 6 conflicting rows. Receipt was 105/3,564 (2.946%) in positive rows, 0/27 in negative rows, 5/191 (2.618%) in ambiguous rows, and 0/6 in conflicting rows.

### Selection overlap

Relative to the locked union, value-aware-positive retained 2,453/3,788 rows (64.76%; row Jaccard 0.648), 1,403/2,071 stays (stay Jaccard 0.677), and 67/77 hospitals (hospital Jaccard 0.870). Protocol-positive retained 1,492/3,788 rows (39.39%; row Jaccard 0.394), 1,002/2,071 stays (0.484), and 45/77 hospitals (0.584). Airway-independent-positive retained 3,564/3,788 rows (94.09%; row Jaccard 0.941), 1,960/2,071 stays (0.946), and 76/77 hospitals (0.987).

Value-aware-positive and protocol-positive overlapped on 1,283 rows, 892 stays, and 45 hospitals; their row/stay/hospital Jaccards were 0.482/0.590/0.672. Value-aware-positive and airway-independent-positive overlapped on 2,310 rows, 1,335 stays, and 67 hospitals; Jaccards were 0.623/0.658/0.882. Protocol-positive and airway-independent-positive overlapped on 1,437 rows, 977 stays, and all 45 protocol-positive hospitals; Jaccards were 0.397/0.492/0.592. These overlaps quantify that protocol-positive restriction is a different and much smaller selected population, whereas airway-independent-positive is close to the locked union.

## Falsification gates and interpretation

Supportive evidence for a documentation-denominator effect would require a materially different receipt rate under the airway-independent restriction, a directionally consistent contrast with both stay- and hospital-cluster intervals excluding zero, adequate cross-label and cross-hospital common support, and persistence after accounting for selection overlap. Those gates were not met: the airway-independent versus locked risk difference was near zero with both intervals crossing zero, and common support was concentrated in one hospital.

Adverse evidence for a claim that target receipt is selectively absent from the old-airway group would be similar or higher airway-independent receipt in `unbounded_only` than in a shorter-lookback group, or loss of the contrast after airway removal. The computed airway-independent rates were 1.761% unbounded-only, 3.424% 24h-only, and 3.509% 6h; the overall unbounded-versus-6h difference is small and uncertain, while the pooled receipt outcome remains dominated by hospital 420. This is adverse to a strong claim of a robust airway-independent receipt deficit, but not evidence that any group was clinically ventilated.

The overall decision is inconclusive for clinical interpretation and adverse for upgrading the operational receipt pattern into a clinical claim. Exact target labels are sparse and site-concentrated; other ventilation labels outnumber exact targets; and the positive states are documentation proxies. A supportive computational result could justify only a claim about recorded label receipt. No result permits claims about true ventilation, SBT delivery, quality, causality, benefit, or transportability.

## Required next evidence

Clinical adjudication is essential. A blinded, stratified sample should include positive, ambiguous, negative, conflicting, target-receipt, no-target, and present/future-transition rows, with review of tube/device insertion and removal, ventilator mode and settings, NIV modality, temporal continuity, transfer/discharge boundaries, and care-plan intent. The adjudication should estimate agreement and predictive values for each proxy, not assume that settings or labels establish invasive support. A separate, prespecified outcome study with confounding control would be required for quality, causal, benefit, or patient-outcome claims.

All automatic conclusions are limited to row lineage, source parsing, time windows, exact label counts, state strata, overlap, concentration, common support, and bootstrap uncertainty. Source data remain read-only; no note text was used in this downstream analysis or sent to public search.
