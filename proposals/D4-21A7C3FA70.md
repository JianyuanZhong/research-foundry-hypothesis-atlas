# Benchmarked grip-triggered cystatin C allocation: testing policy value beyond known UK Biobank discordance

## Clinical decision, prior evidence, and unresolved claim

The decision is which adults with apparently preserved creatinine-based kidney function should receive cystatin C when a primary-care or outpatient service can assay only 20% of eligible patients.

The strongest available evidence already supports more than the inherited proposal acknowledged. Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041; full-text XML inspected as frozen source `56d8e743…`) studied 468,969 UK Biobank participants, included grip strength among candidate predictors, found grip independently associated with the direction of creatinine–cystatin eGFR difference, and reported an internally validated enriched model AUC near 0.75. Yim et al. (Front Med 2022; PMCID PMC9747759; full-text XML inspected as `e7f3681c…`) linked low muscle mass to creatinine–cystatin discordance in 138 hospitalized Korean patients, but used cystatin-based eGFR rather than measured GFR as reference. These studies support association and prediction. They do not establish that grip improves detections crossing a clinically consequential threshold at a fixed assay budget, that it beats a strong no-grip allocation rule, that incremental detections later experience kidney events, or that testing improves outcomes.

The substantive advance is therefore not “grip predicts discordance.” It is a benchmarked policy experiment:

> Among UK Biobank adults aged 40–69 with 2021 race-free creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m² and no previously recorded inpatient N17/N18 kidney diagnosis, a policy adding measured grip to age, sex, BMI and eGFRcr will, at an identical 20% assay budget, detect more people whose combined creatinine–cystatin eGFR is below 60 than a development-selected best no-grip policy, with the absolute and relative gain replicated in two untouched participant partitions. If event counts permit, it will also increase the number per 1,000 assays who both cross that laboratory threshold and subsequently have a first recorded N17/N18 inpatient kidney event within 10 years.

This is a hypothesis about incremental allocation yield. A positive result would not show that grip measures muscle mass rather than frailty, that combined eGFR equals measured GFR, that a code is adjudicated disease, or that cystatin testing or treatment prevents events.

The inherited parent’s complete aggregate audit found 134,025 eligible records after prior N17/N18 exclusion, 2,905 laboratory targets, 9,690 subsequent kidney events, and 1,038 target-plus-event records. These counts support feasibility only. The prior fixed-threshold pilot was adverse and remains an explicitly rejected exploratory branch; it cannot rescue, falsify, or tune this learned-policy experiment.

Create one row per `eid` by one-to-one horizontal joins. The operational pre-assay population O requires:

- parseable baseline assessment date `53-0.0`;

- no position-matched inpatient ICD-10 N17* or N18* code in `41270-0.i` whose paired `41280-0.i` date is on or before time zero.

O must not require cystatin C. A participant with any pre-baseline N17/N18 code whose paired date is missing or invalid has unresolved prior status and is excluded from the primary no-recorded-disease population; report this attrition. Absence from these inpatient arrays is only absence of a recorded inpatient diagnosis, not proof of no kidney disease or no outpatient diagnosis.

The retrospective label-complete set L is O with positive baseline cystatin C `30720-0.0`. Fit target models only in development L, but score and select all O in every partition. Report |O|, |L|, missing-label counts, and distributions of all non-cystatin predictors by label availability.

For every selected participant without valid cystatin, calculate policy-specific and paired sharp bounds. For laboratory yield, missing H ranges from 0 to 1. For joint yield, because K is independently observed, missing H contributes from 0 to K; a selected participant whose kidney-event chronology is itself unresolved contributes from 0 to H, or 0 to 1 if both H and K are unresolved. Bounds must preserve overlap between policies rather than bounding each policy independently. Every support gate must pass its adverse joint bound. If more than 1% of any locked O lacks H, or if code/date defects make more than 0.5% of selected participants’ K status unresolved, classify the affected gate inconclusive even if extreme bounds agree.

Time zero is the actual value of `53-0.0`; an instance index is never interpreted as elapsed time. Convert creatinine from µmol/L to mg/dL as Scr=`30700-0.0`/88.4. Compute 2021 CKD-EPI eGFRcr:

`142 × min(Scr/k,1)^a × max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200) × 0.9938^age × 1.012(if female)`,

where k=0.7 and a=-0.241 for female sex, and k=0.9 and a=-0.302 for male sex.

Compute 2021 CKD-EPI combined eGFRcr-cys:

`135 × min(Scr/k,1)^a × max(Scr/k,1)^(-0.544) × min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.323) × max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.778) × 0.9961^age × 0.963(if female)`,

where k=0.7 and a=-0.219 for female sex, and k=0.9 and a=-0.144 for male sex. The primary label H is 1 when eGFRcr-cys<60. It is a single-occasion laboratory reclassification, not CKD and not measured GFR.

The primary subsequent outcome K is the first position-matched N17* or N18* inpatient code dated in (time zero, time zero+10 years]. Scan all i=0,…,258 pairs; do not independently minimize code and date arrays. The earliest valid event qualifies. Death is the earliest valid `40000-0.0` or `40000-1.0`; death before or on the same day as the first qualifying kidney event is competing, so K=0. A post-baseline N17/N18 code with an invalid paired date makes K unresolved, not negative. Baseline dates end in 2010, so all fixed windows end by 2020. No recorded death is not proof of being alive, and person-level emigration or loss-to-follow-up is unavailable.

## Frozen policies and independent partitions

Before fitting, assign each O participant using:

`h = int(SHA256(utf8("ukb-grip-cys-v4|" + canonical_decimal_eid)).hexdigest(),16) % 100`.

Canonical `eid` is its base-10 integer string with no sign, whitespace, decimal point, or leading zero; reject noncanonical/unparseable identifiers and report them. Use h<60 for development, 60≤h<80 for locked test, and h≥80 for locked replication. Keep all participant modalities together and verify zero overlap. This new namespace is frozen because earlier aggregate and threshold audits touched the scientific line, although no fitted-policy locked result was available.

Define three no-cystatin policies:

- E: lowest-eGFRcr-first, breaking exact ties by the hash below.
- B: a ridge logistic model using age, sex, BMI, eGFRcr, restricted cubic splines for continuous predictors, and no outcome variables.
- G: the identical B pipeline plus maximum valid left/right grip and sex×grip. If only one hand is valid, use it. Maximum grip is a pragmatic reproducible measure, not a validated muscle-mass assay.

For B and G, spline knots are the development L 5th/35th/65th/95th percentiles. Select ridge penalty by nested five-fold participant-level cross-validation within development L; freeze folds, knots, preprocessing, coefficients, penalty, and complete-case behavior. K, death, all locked cystatin values, and all locked outcomes are inaccessible during fitting. B and G must differ only by the grip terms and penalty selected through the same procedure.

Choose the primary no-grip comparator C before opening either locked partition. Assign five outer development folds by a recorded namespace-separated eid hash. For each fold, fit B only on the other four folds' L, score that held-out fold's O, and let B and E each select exactly floor(0.20×|O_fold|). Sum observed target numerators and selected-O denominators across all five held-out folds and calculate joint adverse missing-label bounds. C is whichever has the higher adverse-bound out-of-fold H yield per 1,000 assays; an exact tie chooses E. Then refit B and G once on all development L for locked scoring. Record the comparator choice and never choose a different comparator by locked partition. This guard prevents an incremental claim against B when the simple lowest-eGFRcr policy is better.

In each locked partition, each policy independently ranks every member of O and selects exactly n=floor(0.20×|O_partition|). Break ties with `SHA256(utf8("ukb-grip-cys-tie-v4|" + canonical_decimal_eid))`, hexadecimal interpreted as an integer, ascending. Selection is frozen before reading that partition’s cystatin or outcome fields. Also report B and E even when not C, random 20% (analytic expectation and deterministic repeated draws), oldest-first 20%, and universal testing. Report G∩C plus equal-size G-only and C-only swap sets; similarly report G/B swaps.

## Estimands, uncertainty, and falsification

For policy S with selected indicator S and denominator n:

`Y_H(S)=1000×Σ(S×H)/n`

`Y_HK(S)=1000×Σ(S×H×K)/n`.

There are two co-primary laboratory contrasts. The nested contrast ΔH_B=Y_H(G)-Y_H(B), RH_B=Y_H(G)/Y_H(B) isolates the incremental policy value of adding grip to the same modeling pipeline. The benchmark contrast ΔH_C=Y_H(G)-Y_H(C), RH_C=Y_H(G)/Y_H(C) tests whether the resulting policy also beats the development-selected strongest no-grip rule; when C=B these contrasts coincide. Support requires in each locked partition and for both distinct contrasts: adverse-bound ΔH≥2 additional cases per 1,000 assays, adverse-bound RH≥1.25, and two-sided 95% paired intervals excluding 0 and 1 respectively. A zero comparator yield makes its ratio undefined and that ratio gate inconclusive, never infinite.

The clinical-enrichment estimands are ΔHK_B=Y_HK(G)-Y_HK(B) and ΔHK_C=Y_HK(G)-Y_HK(C); when C=B they coincide. If event sufficiency passes, each distinct pooled locked-partition adverse-bound point estimate must be ≥1 additional H×K case per 1,000 assays and its prespecified paired 95% interval must be above 0. Show each partition separately, but do not demand a positive partition-specific claim when sparse-event rules prohibit one. These are target-positive event-enrichment contrasts under allocation policies, not effects of testing.

Use 2,000 paired participant bootstrap resamples within each locked partition and a pooled bootstrap stratified by partition; do not refit frozen models. Provide percentile intervals and BCa as sensitivity. Decompose every ΔH and ΔHK exactly as G-only gains minus comparator-only losses for both B and C. Report target prevalence, sensitivity, PPV, calibration intercept/slope, Brier score, assay counts, and overlap. A calibration slope outside 0.8–1.2 in either locked partition is a deployment warning and prevents an overall supportive classification.

For clinical description, calculate 10-year Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence of K with death competing in G-only, C-only, overlap, all selected, and full O, split by H where counts permit. Cause-specific Cox and Fine–Gray models are explanatory only and cannot replace yield gates.

Before interpreting the clinical contrasts, require at least 100 observed H×K events in the union selected by G, B, or C across both locked partitions and at least 20 observed H×K events in every distinct G-only and comparator-only swap set used for ΔHK_B or ΔHK_C. Fewer than 30 H×K events in a partition prohibits a positive partition-specific statement. Sparse results remain reportable but the affected clinical contrast is inconclusive; population, endpoint, budget, or threshold may not be changed after counts are seen.

Complexity-matched falsification is quantitative. Generate 100 frozen development datasets by permuting grip within sex×age-decade×BMI-quintile×eGFRcr-quintile strata, refit the complete G pipeline under recorded seeds, and score locked O. The observed ΔH_B must exceed the 97.5th percentile of permutation ΔH_B in each locked partition; otherwise classify the incremental-grip laboratory claim inconclusive for model-complexity/leakage concern. The permutation distribution should center near zero; systematic displacement triggers pipeline failure.

Additional non-rescue checks are: maximum versus mean and minimum valid grip; 2012 eGFRcys/eGFRcr≤0.70, where `eGFRcys=133×min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.499)×max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-1.328)×0.996^age×0.932(if female)` and eGFRcr is the 2021 value defined above; 10% and 30% budgets; one- and two-year outcome lags; and exact repeat measurements. For persistence, use `53-1.0`, `30700-1.0`, and `30720-1.0` only when the actual repeat date is 90 days to six years after baseline and before K; advance age by elapsed days/365.25 and report repeat H among baseline H. Differential repeat availability makes this descriptive. No secondary target, budget, lag, grip definition, or repeat result can rescue a failed primary gate.

Integrity falsifications require exact code/date positions, exact assay counts, no participant overlap, no cystatin/outcome predictor, frozen comparator choice, and frozen tie hashes. Any failure invalidates the run.

## Result classification

A fully supportive result requires both locked partitions to pass both distinct laboratory contrasts under adverse missingness bounds, acceptable calibration, the permutation criterion, consistent swap decompositions, and no integrity failure; if event sufficiency passes, both distinct pooled clinical contrasts must also pass. This supports prospective evaluation of this minimalist grip-informed allocation policy.

Laboratory support with either adverse clinical contrast means grip finds more biochemical reclassifications but does not beat every relevant no-grip policy in reclassified people who later experience recorded kidney events. Laboratory support with either sparse or imprecise clinical contrast is clinically inconclusive. Either result forbids a claim of clinically consequential enrichment.

Adverse evidence includes ΔH≤0, RH≤1, failure of either minimum effect threshold against B or C, a comparator outperforming G, G-only yield no better than the corresponding comparator-only yield, consistent partition disagreement, or either ΔHK≤0 with adequate events. An adverse result is useful: it argues against collecting grip for this allocation decision.

Inconclusive evidence includes wide intervals, an undefined ratio, missing-label or unresolved-K limits, a gate that fails only under adverse sharp bounds, poor calibration, inadequate event counts, failed permutation specificity, or material dependence on grip definition. “Not significant” with wide uncertainty is not equivalence.

The observed policy result can support only incremental allocation performance in this UKB research population. Even full support cannot identify grip’s mechanism: grip may proxy muscle mass, frailty, illness, nutrition, or measurement context. Chen et al. already showed association in this same cohort, so this study cannot be described as an external replication of that association. The two locked partitions are internal replication only.

## Verification boundary and required further study

A computational verifier can check source hashes and headers; one-to-one joins; attrition; equations; code/date positions; death competition; hash partitions and tie breaks; development-only comparator choice; target/outcome isolation; exact assay denominators; observed yields and joint sharp bounds; swap decomposition; bootstrap intervals; calibration; permutation controls; event sufficiency; and whether the stated classification follows the frozen gates.

Verifier fixtures must include: (1) correct laboratory support with adverse ΔHK; (2) sparse ΔHK requiring clinical inconclusiveness; (3) wide laboratory intervals requiring inconclusiveness; (4) a missing-label pattern where naive complete-case yield supports but the joint adverse bound fails; (5) B loses to E in development so C must be E; (6) same-day death competes; (7) supportive numbers followed by the unsupported conclusions that grip proves sarcopenia or testing prevents CKD; and (8) a correctly computed adverse result accurately interpreted as evidence against collecting grip. The verifier must reward conclusion-result coherence, not hypothesis confirmation.

A verifier cannot establish measured-GFR accuracy, persistent CKD, diagnostic validity of N17/N18 codes, complete hospital follow-up, mechanism, transportability, assay economics, downstream actions, or patient benefit. Those claims need measured-GFR comparison, repeat outpatient laboratory data, chart and nephrology adjudication, complete linkage/censoring review, cost and workflow data, an external cohort, and ultimately a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.
