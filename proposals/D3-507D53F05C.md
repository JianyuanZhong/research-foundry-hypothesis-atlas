# Working proposal (episode 29): Is the RA resolution macrophage program a titratable, MerTK-reversible paracrine axis — or a composition/stimulus bystander?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: evolve. Parent: [prior hypothesis]
(episode-28 selection; valid; single-cell composition-vs-within-cell, E-MTAB-8322).
Also anchors on board leader [prior hypothesis] (mu 33.1).
Seeds: D3-M2 lineage (directional macrophage->FLS perturbation), D3-M1 (composition vs within-cell),
D3-M3 (persistence vs remission clinical anchor).
Status: hypothesis-stage, data-bound; planned discriminating test; NO executed screen this episode. No causal claim.

## 1. Unresolved question and why it matters
Alivernini 2020 [K1] showed that MerTK+CD206+ synovial tissue macrophages (STM) characterise
drug-free RA remission and that MerTKpos STM "induced the repair response of synovial
fibroblasts in vitro", with a low MerTKpos proportion predicting flare after treatment
cessation. The lineage (candidate-4db24c12..., then episode-28 candidate-a567afb37) reframed this
as a cross-sectional macrophage program vs the remission-vs-resistant axis, and recorded two
unresolved limitations: (i) a binary/pseudobulk presence contrast with no cell-state resolution,
and (ii) no test of whether the response is an ACTIVE, drug-reversible signalling step.

A consequential clinical decision hangs on this: if the resolution program is an active,
dose-titratable and MerTK-inhibitor-reversible paracrine axis, the MerTK pathway is a rational,
in-principle pharmacologically targetable lever to consolidate remission; if it is instead a
passive composition/abundance or a stimulation-driven bystander, then MerTKpos frequency is a
marker, not a mechanism, and target-engagement strategies would be misdirected.

## 2. Leading claim and strongest rivals (separable by one dataset)
Leading claim H1 (active titratable-reversible axis): in E-MTAB-8873 the FLS transcriptional
response to MerTK/CD206pos macrophages (a) increases monotonically with the Mpos fraction of the
co-culture and (b) is attenuated within-donor by pre-treating macrophages with a MerTK inhibitor.
Rivals:
- H0 composition/bystander: the FLS response tracks TOTAL macrophage presence, not Mpos fraction;
  no monotonic Mpos-fraction dependence and no inhibitor effect. Supported in principle by the
  CTAP framework of Zhang 2023 [K2], where bulk tissue variance is dominated by cell-type abundance.
- H2 stimulus/donor-intrinsic: the response is driven by LPS stimulation or by FLS donor-intrinsic
  state, not by Mpos fraction (Korsunsky 2022 [K3]: fibroblast phenotype transfer across donors/
  contexts can fail).
- H3 measurement/target: UNC106238 (E-MTAB-8316) / the 8873 inhibitor arm is not a functional
  MerTK-pathway perturbation in these cells, or the 445-feature targeted panel lacks the relevant
  MerTK-pathway transcripts; predicted by a global (non-selective) inhibitor effect or by absent
  MERTK module coverage.

## 4. Estimand, population, temporal boundaries
Population: E-MTAB-8873 FLS co-culture samples with a same-FLS-donor comparator set; unit of
inference is the FLS donor (macrophage donors cross within an FLS donor -> dependence modelled, not
treated as independent). Cross-sectional assay; no longitudinal/time field exists, so transition
claims are out of scope.
Primary estimand A (dose/abundance shape): direction of the FLS module-score vs Mpos-fraction across
the titration series (Spearman rho within FLS donor; supported H1 = monotonic positive).
Primary estimand B (MerTK-reversibility): within-donor paired difference Delta = module(FLS+Mpos,
macrophage pre-treated with MerTK inhibitor) - module(FLS+Mpos, none), over donor-matched pairs
(SA256A x2, SA257A, SA266A; n>=4 pairs). H1 predicts Delta < 0 (attenuation).
Cross-modality check C: overlap between the MerTK-inhibitor-responsive monocyte signature from
E-MTAB-8316 (none vs UNC106238, 6 within-specimen pairs) and the resolution/repair module defined
from E-MTAB-8322 (remission minus resistant); H1 predicts substantial overlap, H3 predicts none.

## 5. Simple baseline vs substantive learned/mechanistic alternative (same question)
Baseline: per-donor pseudobulk module score (curated MerTK/resolution-repair gene set drawn from
the 8322 remission-vs-resistant contrast, intersected with the 445 panel) compared across Mpos
fraction and inhibitor arms; paired Wilcoxon / sign test. This answers only "is the module up?".
Alternative (mechanistic variance decomposition): donor-aware linear mixed model on per-sample
log-CPM with random effect for FLS donor (and macrophage donor nested), fixed effects for Mpos
fraction, total macrophage presence, stimulation (LPS), and inhibitor; plus a rank-1..3 factor
model / PLS on the 445 features to test whether a SINGLE latent paracrine axis explains the
titration, with leave-one-FLS-donor-out generalisation. What the alternative adds that the baseline
loses: it attributes signal to the correct experimental factor (Mpos FRACTION vs TOTAL macrophage
presence vs LPS vs donor identity) and makes a falsifiable prediction about latent dimensionality,
which a single module score cannot.
Resource budget: CPU-only is adequate (<=72 samples x 445 features; mixture/factor fits and
permutation tests are seconds-to-minutes; no GPU need demonstrated, so none requested).

## 6. Falsification criteria and outcome meanings
- Supportive (H1): monotonic positive Mpos-fraction association in a majority of FLS donors with a
  permutation p<0.05, AND Delta<0 in >=3 of 4 inhibitor pairs, AND the Mpos-fraction term retains a
  non-zero variance component after adjusting for total macrophage presence and LPS.
- Adverse (H0/H2): no monotonic Mpos-fraction association and no inhibitor attenuation, while total
  macrophage presence and/or LPS and donor identity carry the variance -> the resolution signal is a
  composition/stimulus bystander, not a titratable MerTK-dependent axis.
- Inconclusive: MERTK / MerTK-pathway transcripts absent or unscorable in the 445-panel; or the
  inhibitor effect is global (affects most features) rather than pathway-selective (H3); or donor
  dependence cannot be resolved with <=5 FLS donors.
An imprecise null (few donors, targeted panel) does not refute H1; it bounds it.

## 7. Explicit limits
Planned, not executed. Targeted 445-feature panel -> unmeasured genes unavailable; "MERTK module
coverage" must be checked before interpretation. Pseudobulk per sample; macrophage donor dependence
modelled, not eliminated; <=5 FLS donors => limited power and no independent-donor claim. UNC106238
selectivity and direct target engagement are unproven here (H3). In-vitro co-culture is not the
joint; any Mpos->FLS repair link is a candidate mechanism. Cross-sectional design cannot establish
the remission/resistant transition (D3-M3 remains a clinical anchor only). [K1] full text inspected
(journal-club rendering); [K2],[K3] inspected at abstract level. Wet-lab/expert review required
before any target-engagement or clinical claim.
