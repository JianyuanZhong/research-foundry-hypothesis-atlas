# Provisional E-MTAB-8873 macrophage-context hypothesis

## Proposed relationship

In the frozen E-MTAB-8873 targeted assay, FLS cells co-cultured with MerTK/CD206-positive macrophages have a reproducible, donor-set-aware difference in the measured transcript state from FLS cells co-cultured with MerTK/CD206-negative macrophages, beyond the recorded cell count and the complete set of contributing donors. This is a context-associated hypothesis, not a causal macrophage-mechanism claim. No expression contrast or model has been computed.

The clinically important unresolved question is whether an apparent macrophage-context signal is reproducible after accounting for donor composition, rather than being generated by a few donor sets, unequal cell counts, or a malformed donor assignment. A positive result would motivate a follow-up perturbation or independent-cohort study; it would not by itself establish a pathway or treatment effect.

## Prespecified contrasts and analysis

The primary comparison is restricted to `treatment == none` and condition exactly equal to `co-cultured with MerTK/CD206pos macrophages` or `co-cultured with MerTK/CD206neg macrophages`. The five candidate pairs are:

1. `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mneg` versus `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA259A_Mpos` (491 versus 368 cells).
2. `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mneg` versus `R2_SA257A_FLS_SA266A_Mpos` (396 versus 383 cells).
3. `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA266A_Mneg` versus `R2_SA258R_FLS_SA267R_Mpos` (1469 versus 1207 cells; macrophage donor composition differs).
4. `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` versus `R5_SA263R_FLS_SA278R_Mpos` (540 versus 455 cells; macrophage donor composition differs).
5. `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mneg` versus `R5_SA266A_FLS_SA277A_Mpos` (1144 versus 1060 cells).

The analysis unit is the complete contributing-donor set, not a row. A stricter sensitivity analysis retains only pairs with identical contributing-donor sets and the same FLS donor; if none remain, the donor-specific interpretation is not identified and the result is downgraded to context-associated. The malformed/ambiguous `R1_SA256A_FLS_SA260R_Mpos` donor record (`donor_id=SA256A | SA256A, SA260R` with conflicting source metadata) is excluded from the primary matched set and reported as a data-quality limitation. Mixed Mpos/Mneg samples and LPS/inhibitor samples are not substitutes for the primary none-treatment contrast.

For each pair, calculate Mpos-minus-Mneg on log(count + 1) with `cells` as a prespecified offset (or an equivalent count model using `cells`), preserving every feature-instance key. Fit a simple regularized linear model of log count with condition, cell-count offset, and complete contributing-donor-set indicators. Fit a structured hierarchical alternative with a fixed context effect and random effects for the complete contributing-donor set (and, if estimable, FLS donor), with feature-level uncertainty. The alternative can reveal whether direction is reproducible across donor sets and how much variation is donor-set/sampling variation, which the fixed-indicator baseline loses.

Evaluate paired contrast direction, feature-level uncertainty, held-out donor-set generalization, and sensitivity to excluding the malformed record and restricting to identical-donor-set pairs. The scientific deliverable is a newly fitted contrast/decomposition with uncertainty and a sensitivity report, not a claimed biological result.

Supportive evidence would be a stable context direction with uncertainty that survives donor-aware fitting, cell-count adjustment, and the stricter sensitivity subset. Adverse evidence would be disappearance after donor-set matching or cell-count adjustment, a result driven by one donor set, or reversal in the matched subset. Inconclusive evidence includes too few independent donor sets, unstable estimates, failed feature reconciliation, or inability to separate context from treatment/batch. Neither outcome establishes causality, a pathway, whole-tissue composition, effects on unmeasured genes, or clinical benefit.

## Evidence status and missing evidence

The literature pass found three potentially relevant public works, but the available tooling returned only search metadata/abstracts and frozen acquisition records at the point of this checkpoint; full-text inspection and claim-specific excerpts were not completed. The exact three-work key-reference file and source snapshots therefore remain missing and must not be claimed as inspected. The literature cannot currently be used to assert that E-MTAB-8873 has been previously analyzed or that the proposed contrast is novel.

The dataset itself is a targeted 445-feature assay and recorded pseudobulk samples. It does not provide complete batch/processing-order covariates, independent biological replication beyond these donor sets, whole-tissue cell abundances, or direct perturbation validation. Clinical adjudication of macrophage phenotype and independent validation would require additional data.
