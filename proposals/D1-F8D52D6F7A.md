# Does hypotension DURATION add AKI risk beyond DEPTH? An executed informativeness bound for a depth-conditioned, dual-ascertainment test in eICU 2.0

Lead working proposal — eICU 2.0 island, **episode 31**. Operation: **evolve** of the valid,
delivery-complete leader `[prior hypothesis]` (mu 32.09). Status: **design + bounded
executed feasibility probe**; the full estimator (beta, E-value, LOHO) is NOT executed here — this is
readiness plus a computed informativeness bound, not a solved result.

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
ICU teams titrate fluids and vasopressors to a mean arterial pressure (MAP) target. A time-weighted
MAP<65 burden predicts creatinine-based KDIGO AKI ([K1] AUC 0.725), and graded MAP<65 duration tracks a
downstream outcome ([K2] aOR 1.78 for >=30 min). Both treat burden/ duration as a scalar, so the
decision-facing question is unresolved: **once the deepest MAP a patient reached is known, does the
cumulative time/area below threshold add risk?** If yes, "time below target" is a monitoring target
beyond a single worst value; if no, depth alone suffices and duration-based alarms add no information.

## 2. Evidence structure: leading claim vs strongest rival
- **H1 (duration adds beyond depth):** duration of MAP<65 adds AKI risk *within* depth strata.
- **R1 (collinearity/severity gradient):** "duration" only tracks depth and global severity; within-stratum
  duration ORs are null and any signal sits only in the shallow (least severe) stratum.
- **R2 (detection/order artifact):** the creatinine-channel association reflects lab-ordering/monitoring
  intensity (the [K3] surrogate-process warning), not hypoperfusion.

Difference on which they diverge: within-min-MAP-stratum duration ORs (+ duration x min-MAP interaction)
and concordance across two disjoint AKI-ascertainment channels (creatinine vs urine output).

## 3. Frozen design (from parent candidate-520b1d0e)
- **Population:** adult eICU 2.0 stays, `patient.age`>=16, alive at a 24 h landmark
  (`patient.unitdischargeoffset`>=1440 min), with a baseline creatinine in (0,24]h and a follow-up
  creatinine in (24,72]h. Join key `patientunitstayid`.
- **Exposure:** MAP burden in (0,24]h from `vitalPeriodic.systemicmean` (`observationoffset` minutes):
  min MAP, minutes <65, time-weighted area <65.
- **Outcome:** KDIGO creatinine AKI in (24,72]h from `lab` (`labname`~creatinine, `labresultoffset`,
  `labresult`); channel-disjoint confirmatory outcome KDIGO urine-output criterion from `intakeOutput`
  (`intakeoutputoffset`,`cellpath`,`celllabel`,`cellvaluenumeric`).
- **Adjustment:** `apachePatientResult.apachescore`/`predictedicumortality`, age, sex, hospital fixed
  effects; leave-one-hospital-out (LOHO) transport; creatinine-sampling-intensity control; negative-control
  outcome.
- **Baselines/alternatives:** B1 depth-only logistic; B2 additive duration+area; **A1 (child's test)**
  depth-conditioned duration ORs within min-MAP quintiles + duration x min-MAP interaction; A2 bounded GBT
  trajectory features. Deep sequence/neural model deferred ([K3]: sparse-sampling underdetermination).
- **Falsification:** supportive = within deep *and* mid min-MAP strata duration OR per 30 min >1 (CI excl. 1)
  AND concordant across channels. Adverse-R1 = null within every stratum, signal only in the shallow stratum.
  Adverse-R2 = creatinine signal absent in the urine-output channel and tracking sampling intensity.

## 4. Executed bounded probe (NEW this episode)
Read-only scan of the real eICU 2.0 files (`patient.csv.gz`, `lab.csv.gz`); job
`[research job]` (cpus=8, mem=32768, CPU), artifacts `work/probe_ep31.json`. Results:
- `patient`: **200,859** stays, **208** hospitals, 200,678 age>=16, **132,900** with landmark>=1440 min,
  **109,907** expired.
- `lab`: 39,132,531 rows, **1,291,042** creatinine rows; **190,393** stays with any creatinine;
  **169,801** with a (0,24]h baseline; **139,001** with a (24,72]h follow-up; **129,353** with both;
  **8,269** stays with follow-up/baseline ratio >=1.5 (**crude creatinine-AKI 6.39%**).
- **Informativeness bound (standardized-logistic MDE, alpha=0.05 two-sided, 80% power; hospital-clustered
  inflation m~622/ICC):** whole cohort MDE OR naive **1.032** / ICC0.05 **1.198** / ICC0.10 **1.288**;
  a min-MAP quintile (n~25,870, ~1,654 events) MDE OR naive **1.074** / ICC0.05 **1.496** / ICC0.10 **1.761**.
  **Verdict: INFORMATIVE** — the cohort is powered for the design's targeted within-stratum OR (~1.15-2.0),
  so a within-stratum null is genuine adverse-to-H1 evidence, not just underpowering.
- **Unexecuted here:** the `vitalPeriodic.csv.gz` (1.78 GB) MAP-stratum sizing did not complete inside the
  bounded probe window and is reported as PENDING, not assumed. Exact min-MAP quintile sizes, the fitted
