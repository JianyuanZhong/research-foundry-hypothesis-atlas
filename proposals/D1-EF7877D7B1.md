# MIMIC-IV proposal: discordant renal recovery at ICU discharge

Status: planned, not executed. This is a prognostic observational experiment, not a causal treatment study.

## Hypothesis and importance

Among adults discharged alive from a first ICU stay lasting at least 48 hours, creatinine improvement without concurrent urine-output recovery in the final 24 hours before ICU discharge identifies excess 48-hour risk of ICU readmission or in-hospital death beyond current severity and support.

This matters because step-down decisions are made while creatinine is changing, yet creatinine can fall through dilution or reduced production and urine output is influenced by treatment and documentation. [K1] supports creatinine and urine output as complementary AKI criteria but does not establish this discharge-risk phenotype. [K2] reports measured-clearance evidence that creatinine-based eGFR can overestimate function after ICU muscle loss. [K3] reports that dynamic renal assessment can aid risk stratification while incremental biomarker benefit is modest/inconsistent. The unresolved claim is incremental, short-horizon prognostic value and reproducibility of observable discordance, not measured GFR, renal mechanism, or benefit from delaying discharge.

Rivals are creatinine artifact, urine collection/diuretic/documentation artifact, residual severity and clinician discharge selection, chronic kidney disease/renal reserve, and survivor selection. A positive result is a risk marker only; a null result with a narrow interval argues it adds little beyond severity; an imprecise result is inconclusive.

## Population and time

Use MIMIC-IV 3.1 snapshot [source checksum]. Join hosp/patients to hosp/admissions on subject_id, then icu/icustays on subject_id and hadm_id. Retain each subject's first ICU stay, anchor_age at least 18, valid intime<outtime, los at least 2 days, and outtime no later than dischtime. Retain patients alive at ICU discharge. Set t0 to icustays.outtime. Every feature is strictly in [t0-24 hours,t0); follow-up begins at t0.

Require two creatinine observations six hours apart, the last in the feature window, and at least one urine record. Exclude or sensitivity-test renal replacement therapy before t0. Define C_peak as maximum pre-t0 creatinine, C_last as the last value in the feature window, and improvement as C_peak minus C_last at least 0.3 mg/dL. Sum nonnegative Foley/Void/Condom-Cath urine; low output is less than 400 mL per 24 hours, explicitly an unweighted proxy. Compare improved/high output (reference), improved/low output (discordant), not-improved/high, and not-improved/low.

Primary outcome is first admissions.deathtime in (t0,t0+48h] or later icustays.intime for the same subject_id and hadm_id in that interval, censored at dischtime. Secondary outcomes are each component, 7-day events, and in-hospital death. The estimand is the standardized observational risk difference between discordant and concordant groups over measured pre-index covariates, not a treatment effect.

## Baseline, learned alternative, and evaluation

B0 is penalized logistic regression for the 48-hour outcome with age, ICU LOS, peak/last creatinine, urine output, sex, admission type, last-six-hour MAP, available lactate/platelets, support indicators, and missingness indicators. B1 adds the four-level discordance group. Report standardized risks, risk difference, risk ratio, 95% intervals, Brier score, log loss, discrimination, and calibration.

The substantive alternative is a three-state multivariate hidden Markov/state-space model on 6-hour bins from ICU admission to t0, using the same routine measurements, support indicators, and missingness indicators. Fit only on training subjects and estimate last state, occupancy, transitions, and time since transition. Do not call states mechanisms. Use a fixed subject-level 70/15/15 train/tune/test split and 200 patient bootstrap intervals. If the test set has fewer than 200 subjects or 30 events, defer HMM inference and report baseline-only feasibility. The alternative can distinguish repeated discordant occupancy from transient measurement noise and fixed-threshold timing loss; it is not selected for a small AUROC gain.

Falsification checks are exposure permutation within baseline-risk/LOS/admission strata; 12- and 48-hour windows; continuous creatinine slope; weight-indexed output only if verified; sparse-documentation and RRT exclusions; creatinine-only, output-only and interaction models; a pseudo-landmark 24 hours earlier; and charting-density/ICU-type strata. Support requires a stable adjusted signal and reproducible latent transitions. Adverse results are null, explained by B0, or reversed after measurement/RRT adjustment. Inconclusive results are sparse cases, low events, unstable item sets, wide intervals, or poor state separation. Computation cannot establish true GFR, CKD, tubular injury, complete diuretic exposure, discharge rationale, mechanism, causality, or generalizability; those require adjudication or another cohort.
