# Repeated discordance and physical reserve: is the baseline kidney signal durable and clinically informative?

## Successor claim and clinical decision

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s corrected UK Biobank biomarker bindings and coded-outcome limits, but changes the unresolved question from “does one baseline interaction predict an event?” to:

> Among participants who are alive and free of recorded renal events at a later UKB assessment, does the creatinine-versus-cystatin-C eGFR discordance/grip interaction represent a reproducible person-level phenotype, and does using the repeat measurement materially improve 5-year risk estimation for a first hospital-coded AKI event when death is treated as a competing event?

The consequential decision is whether a single apparently reassuring creatinine result plus low physical reserve is enough to justify repeat kidney assessment or closer surveillance. The proposed study can inform prognostic evidence for that decision; it cannot test whether ordering cystatin C or changing treatment improves outcomes.

The strongest available evidence is the read local critical-illness study, `references/expert-seeds/papers/kidney-function/article.readable.txt`. In 38 mechanically ventilated patients it used serial creatinine, cystatin C, iohexol clearance and rectus-femoris ultrasound; at ICU discharge creatinine-based eGFR overestimated measured dysfunction and the creatinine-to-cystatin-C relationship tracked muscle loss. That supports biological plausibility in a selected ICU population. It does not establish stability in community participants, prognostic value for future AKI, or the meaning of grip-defined reserve. The parent established only a baseline association design. Neither result supports the successor claim that the signal is durable or clinically incremental.

The falsifiable hypothesis is that baseline discordance and low grip have a reproducible latent component, and that a repeat-enhanced model has a held-out 5-year AKI cumulative-incidence improvement of at least 1 percentage point for a prespecified high-risk profile over a baseline-only model, with the repeat-updated discordance-by-grip contrast retaining direction and non-negligible uncertainty bounds. The 1-point threshold is an operational study threshold for “material information,” not a universal clinical-utility claim.

## Exposures and repeat stability

For each visit, compute CKD-EPI 2021 eGFRcr from creatinine, age and sex and CKD-EPI 2021 eGFRcys from cystatin C, in mL/min/1.73 m², using the no-race coefficient formula verified by the compiler. Preserve the exact formula and units in the run manifest. Define:

`D_v = eGFRcr_v - eGFRcys_v`

where positive D means creatinine is more reassuring than cystatin C. Grip `G_v) is the maximum of valid left/right values at the same instance. Also retain the weaker side, the left/right discordance, BMI (`assessment.21001-*`) and weight (`assessment.21002-*`) for sensitivity and selection descriptions. Age is `population.21022-0.0`; sex is `population.31-0.0).

The primary stability analysis estimates the repeat correlation and agreement of continuous `D_0,D_1), `G_0,G_1), their changes, and the sex-standardized D-by-grip contrast using participant bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. It reports an intraclass correlation (two-way absolute agreement), Bland–Altman bias and limits, and the proportion whose interaction-risk stratum changes. A prespecified descriptive phenotype is high discordance (`D >= 15)) plus sex-specific low grip (below the training-set 20th percentile); the 15-unit rule is secondary and is not used to claim diagnostic thresholds. Primary inference remains continuous.

The repeat clinical models compare, at the `t1) landmark:

- **M0, transparent baseline-only:** mean kidney function, `D_0), `G_0), `D_0 × G_0), age, sex, BMI and weight at t0, with restricted cubic splines for mean eGFR and age. This is the parent signal evaluated in the repeat-landmark survivor population.
- **M1, transparent repeat-enhanced:** M0 plus `D_1,G_1,Delta D=D_1-D_0,Delta G=G_1-G_0), and the prespecified repeat interaction `D_1 × G_1). It tests whether the second assessment adds information rather than merely refitting a more flexible curve.
- The high-discordance/low-grip contrast is reported as an absolute 5-year risk contrast for low, median and high grip profiles, with profiles fixed from training data.

Fit cause-specific Cox models for AKI and death and estimate 5-year AKI cumulative incidence with Aalen–Johansen; Fine–Gray is a sensitivity analysis, not a replacement for the competing-risk estimand. Report cause-specific hazard ratios, 5-year cumulative-incidence differences, calibration slope/intercept, integrated Brier score and calibration plots. Use 95% bootstrap intervals stratified by event and repeat status. Compare M0 and M1 only on a participant-level held-out test set. A repeat-enhanced model is clinically informative only if the predeclared 1-point high-risk contrast is supported and calibration is not degraded; a small discrimination gain alone is insufficient.

Because repeat attendance and survival to `t1) select participants, report baseline characteristics, event/death before `t1), and overlap/positivity. As a sensitivity analysis, fit stabilized inverse-probability-of-repeat weights from baseline age, sex, eGFRcr/eGFRcys, D, grip, BMI, weight and baseline renal-code history; truncate at the 1st/99th percentiles and show weighted versus unweighted results. This does not correct unmeasured selection or make the survivor-landmark estimand causal.

## Substantive alternative and method selection

The selected alternative is a joint longitudinal measurement-error/competing-risk model, not a more elaborate predictor chosen for a small score gain. It uses the same `D_0,D_1,G_0,G_1), age, sex, BMI, weight, `t0,t1), and the same post-`t1) AKI/death definitions and split. A bivariate latent person-level state estimates stable discordance and reserve plus visit-specific noise and change:

- observed D and grip are modeled as latent persistent state plus correlated measurement error and an individual change term;
- the repeat interval is included in the change component;
- cause-specific AKI and death hazards use the posterior/empirical-Bayes latent state and its interaction;
- fitting uses maximum likelihood with Laplace or adaptive quadrature, with participant-level bootstrap uncertainty; no outcome information enters exposure construction.

This model can reveal what M0/M1 lose: whether an apparently large baseline interaction is mostly visit noise, whether true persistent discordance is associated with risk after accounting for regression to the mean, and whether death preferentially removes high-risk participants before repeat measurement. It is a mechanistic measurement model, not evidence that D measures true GFR or that grip measures muscle mass.

Use the same 60% train / 20% tuning / 20% held-out test split as the parent, assigned by a deterministic SHA-256 hash of `eid) and never using `eid) as a predictor; accessible discovery buckets 0–79 are used, with reserved 80–99 not called external validation. Estimate thresholds, standardization, model choices and baseline weights in training only. The latent model is selected for scientific adequacy because the unresolved issue is reliability and attenuation, with an expected budget of <=4 GB RAM, <=60 CPU minutes after extraction, and no GPU. Completion requires: the frozen repeat cohort and selection table; repeat reliability estimates; fitted M0/M1 and latent-model parameters; held-out 5-year CIF/Brier/calibration outputs; uncertainty intervals; and the prespecified interaction and 1-point clinical-information contrast. If fewer than 1,000 repeat-landmark participants, fewer than 100 post-`t1) AKI events, or the latent fit fails predeclared convergence/positive-definite checks, report the latent branch as infeasible/inconclusive and do not silently substitute another question.

The boosted survival model from the parent is deferred. It could detect nonlinear thresholds, but by itself cannot distinguish a stable phenotype from measurement noise or repeat-attendance selection; revisit it only if M0/M1 and the latent model show a stable, clinically sizeable signal and a threshold question remains. A broad Delphi-like sequence model is deferred because the exact deliverable is measurement reliability, not diagnosis-order prediction. A Bayesian measured-GFR latent model is deferred because no measured-GFR anchor is available. These are not claims of scientific inferiority.

## Outcomes, falsification and interpretation

The primary event is the first post-`t1) hospital code beginning N17 in `health_outcomes.41270-0.*), paired by the identical suffix with `41280-0.*), with date > `t1) and <= `T_admin`. The stricter sensitivity outcome requires N17 in `41271-0.*) paired with `41281-0.*`. N18 is a secondary coded outcome. A single code is not called persistent CKD. Death is the earliest parseable `40000-0.0/40000-1.0) date after the relevant landmark and is a competing event for AKI/CKD and a separate outcome. Causes `40001-0.0/1.0` and `40002-0.*` are descriptive only. Code/date pairing is mandatory; a code without a paired date is not an event.

Supportive results require all of the following: repeat reliability is above zero with a reasonably precise interval; the continuous D-by-grip direction is concordant at both visits or is recovered in the latent persistent state; M1 or the latent model improves held-out 5-year AKI calibration/Brier performance over M0; the 1-point contrast has a lower confidence bound above zero for the prespecified profile; and the result is not removed by primary-diagnosis, 365-day repeat, IPW, or renal-history sensitivities. This supports durable prognostic information in the selected repeat-landmark population only.

Adverse results are low repeat agreement, regression-to-the-mean elimination of the interaction, reversal between visits, no clinically material held-out improvement, calibration degradation, or disappearance under primary-code/death/IPW sensitivity. These falsify the durable/incremental hypothesis in this snapshot and argue against using one baseline discordance-plus-grip reading to prioritize surveillance. A null association is not evidence that cystatin C lacks biological value.

Inconclusive results are sparse events, large missingness, poor overlap between repeat attendees and the baseline cohort, date/instance misalignment, nonpositive-definite latent fits, wide intervals spanning both no effect and the 1-point threshold, or unstable results across splits. Inconclusive means the question remains unresolved.

Computational verification can check source hashes, one-to-one `eid) joins, row counts, field validity, repeat dates, paired code/date ordering, exclusion timing, competing-risk risk sets, split integrity, fitted parameters, bootstrap intervals, reliability, Brier scores and calibration. It cannot establish AKI severity or KDIGO stage, measured GFR, that discordance is caused by muscle loss, that grip is a valid muscle-mass proxy, causal treatment benefit, clinical utility, transportability, or a recommendation to order cystatin C. Those require adjudicated AKI stages and laboratory trajectories, measured-GFR and body-composition anchors, medication/treatment data, expert clinical review, external validation and preferably a prospective decision study.
