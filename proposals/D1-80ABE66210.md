# Hospital-held-out eICU mortality transport: patient state, documentation process, and residual case mix

Status: complete proposed child of [prior hypothesis]. No fitted study result is claimed. This is a descriptive prognostic transportability experiment, not a causal study of ordering, documentation, or mortality.

## Scientific opening and unresolved claim

The inspected evidence supports three narrower observations: rich EHR representations can predict mortality across centers [K1]; missing indicators can add prognostic information but may shift across time or settings [K2]; and an externally validated critical-care mortality model can retain discrimination while local EHR patterns improve calibration, under substantial harmonization burden [K3]. None identifies whether an early laboratory-result process signal reflects patient physiology/severity, hospital documentation and measurement practice, or residual case mix and selection.

The unresolved question is: after a fixed six-hour ICU landmark, does adding early laboratory-result presence/timing create a hospital-held-out mortality liability primarily because it encodes patient state, a hospital/process distribution, or residual case mix not captured by the value representation?

Leading explanation: the observed process vector partly captures hospital-specific measurement/documentation practice. Strong rivals are (a) process variables are patient-aligned proxies for latent acuity not represented by selected values, and (b) selection induced by stays observed beyond six hours and hospitals meeting the size threshold. Hospital-held-out folds address transport and repeat-patient leakage but do not randomize measurement practice or eliminate residual severity.

### Falsifiable hypothesis

Among the prespecified adult first eligible ICU stays, aligned value-plus-process VP-GBM has greater internal-to-hospital-held-out deterioration than value-only V-GBM. The documentation/process interpretation is supported only if:

1. the paired deterioration contrast is positive with a 95% hospital-cluster bootstrap interval excluding zero for at least one primary metric (AUROC, Brier, or absolute calibration-slope error);
2. VP-GBM has lower held-out decision-curve net benefit than V-GBM at at least two of 5%, 10%, and 20%, without a corresponding adverse value-only calibration pattern; and
3. process-permuted diagnostics retain a site/process transport penalty more than patient-level incremental discrimination. If state-conditioned permutation removes the incremental held-out signal while unstratified permutation retains a gap, residual case mix linked to observed value state is more plausible than a pure workflow explanation. If state-conditioned permutation retains a gap, a hospital/process distribution is consistent but within-stratum unmeasured acuity remains viable.

The claim is falsified if VP-GBM is no less transportable or useful than V-GBM and no primary negative-control pattern remains. A held-out VP-GBM advantage with both permutations collapsing it supports patient-aligned information, not biological causation. Discordant or imprecise results are inconclusive.

## Clinical importance and decision boundary

An unvalidated mortality score that relies on local result-capture practice can change who crosses a trigger for senior review, goals-of-care discussion, or monitoring review. The experiment does not recommend a treatment and cannot estimate benefit from any action.

The prespecified deployment gate is conservative: process features are not eligible for silent prospective evaluation if, at 5%, 10%, or 20%, the paired held-out net-benefit contrast (VP-GBM minus V-GBM) has a lower 95% hospital-cluster bound below zero, or if process-model calibration deterioration is worse than value-only. The next action is value-only use or site validation/recalibration. If the interval spans zero, defer rather than declare equivalence. Advancement would require a non-worse interval, acceptable calibration, and clinical review; this is governance, not evidence of clinical benefit.

## Features, models, and matched alternatives

Within each outer training fold, select the 30 analyte keys with greatest distinct eligible-stay coverage, requiring >=1% training-stay coverage; report fewer if applicable. Learn medians/categories only in training. V consists of age, gender, ethnicity, admissionweight, and latest finite numeric value per selected analyte in [0,360]. P consists of per-analyte result presence, valid-result count, distinct-offset count, first/last valid offsets, total valid rows and total distinct offsets. P is recorded result process, not confirmed ordering.

V-Logit is fixed elastic-net logistic regression on static variables plus latest values: the simple additive baseline. V-GBM is a fixed histogram gradient booster on V. VP-GBM is the same implementation, preprocessing, seed, class-weight policy and hyperparameters with aligned P added; the paired difference isolates the process channel without held-out tuning. T-Logit is the substantive temporal alternative: same static variables, three bins [0,120], (120,240], (240,360], last value/presence/count per analyte-bin, fixed elastic-net fit. It can reveal whether chronology and delayed documentation are important information lost by latest-value summaries. All models use the same cohort, folds, outcome, metrics and bootstrap.

A temporal neural sequence model was considered and deferred. It could preserve richer ordering and nonlinear interactions, but lab has no order/specimen/collection/interface/rationale semantics, so capacity would not identify the process mechanism. Revisit only if T-Logit and VP-GBM show reproducible timing signal, event support per hospital is adequate, and one bounded readiness fit confirms resources. This is a scientific deferral, not a rejection of neural or GPU methods.

## Prespecified rival-discrimination controls

Apply independent within-hospital permutation to held-out hospitals, without labels; record seeds/manifests; fit fixed VP-GBM.

New state-conditioned process permutation: obtain V-only risks from fitted V-GBM; freeze outer-training V-score quintile cutpoints; within hospital x quintile permute complete P vectors. For singleton cells leave unchanged and report their number/fraction. Fit the same fixed VP-GBM on permuted training data and independently permute held-out hospital cells before prediction. This preserves hospital and coarse observed value-state process distributions but breaks patient alignment conditional on V.

## Interpretation, limits, and continuation

Supportive: positive primary deterioration interval, worse held-out process-model calibration or net benefit at >=2 thresholds, and a compatible control pattern. Say “consistent with documentation/process transport liability,” never that documentation caused death or that changing testing changes mortality.

Adverse: process model is at least as transportable/useful, or aligned process retains held-out value while both permutations collapse. This favors stable patient-aligned information or another explanation, not proven biological stability.

Inconclusive: intervals span zero, too few hospitals/events, unstable analyte coverage or repeat exclusions, mostly singleton permutation cells, or conflicting discrimination/calibration/control patterns.

Computationally checkable: source headers/schema bindings, cohort counts, time-window enforcement, joins, split integrity, prediction completeness, metrics, bootstrap, and rule-conformant interpretation. Clinical adjudication/another study is required for order intent, specimen collection, interface outages, bedside severity not measured in labs, end-of-life decisions, meaning of missingness, causal effects, and clinical impact. Silent prospective deployment with order/interface fields and expert review is required before clinical use.
