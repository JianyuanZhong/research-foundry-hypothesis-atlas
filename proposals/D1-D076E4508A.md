# Route-concealed actionability and target-linked supersession: a parsimonious fixed phase-2 audit

## Decision question, evidence boundary, and falsifiable hypothesis

The decision is whether an exact MIMIC pulmonary-opportunity atom may be nominated for a prospective, within-site **silent validation** of the inherited admission routes `AUTO`, `RECON`, `TIMING`, and `DEFER`. Nothing in this retrospective experiment authorizes an alert, copied recommendation, order, referral, scheduling, communication, or patient-facing action. The estimand is limited to reproducibility of observable text-defined route inputs and contamination/actionability structure; it is not clinical correctness, appropriateness, safety, benefit, workflow execution, viewing, or a causal effect.

The available evidence supports construction of the parent’s first-eligible, all-opportunity admission frame using linked MIMIC radiology/discharge artifacts, reciprocal chronology, service/ICU workflow proxies, and timestamps. It cannot establish physical departure visibility, responsibility, communication, patient preference, external plans, actual viewing, or execution. The unresolved claim is therefore narrow and falsifiable: among the fixed probability sample of inherited sampled admissions, do disjoint route-concealed readers identify an explicit target-linked active discharge action, and do they identify an explicit target-linked superseding plan? The primary hypothesis is that at least one support-qualified `AUTO` or `RECON` final atom will satisfy the inherited adverse gates and these observable actionability/supersession gates. A gate-crossing adverse-compatible bound, a route-invariant control flip, or a route-decisive control violation falsifies the corresponding nomination; agreement is a measurement result, not a clinical gold standard.

## Fixed phase-1 and parsimonious phase-2 probability sample

Construct the parent’s phase-1 sampled admission set and final pre-label atom `g=(possible-calendar class, terminal curr_service, workflow class)` before exposing any packet or label. Preserve its exact allocation: sentinel census; `S_ZERO` is the larger of 300 and the parent finite-population +/-4-point formula; every other inherited stratum is the larger of 120 and its parent +/-7-point formula, capped at `N_h`; if the total exceeds 900, return `NO_SELECTION`. There is no top-up, replacement, adaptive allocation, route/label enrichment, atom merging, or complete-case substitution.

Define the **phase-2 sampling unit** as one distinct `(subject_id,hadm_id)` admission in the inherited phase-1 sample, with all of that admission’s eligible recommendation units and all four route-concealed component views attached. Let `n1` be the number of distinct phase-1 sampled admissions after all parent seals and before any phase-2 packet rendering. If `n1=0`, return `NO_SELECTION`. Otherwise set `m=min(300,n1)` and draw one simple random sample without replacement of exactly `m` admissions from the entire phase-1 sampled-admission set using the sole predeclared seed `ACTSUP-v2` (cryptographically recorded protocol hash plus pseudorandom-generator version). This is not atom- or route-enriched. The only phase-2 inclusion probabilities are

`pi2_i=m/n1` for every phase-1 admission `i`, and `pi2_ij=m(m-1)/(n1(n1-1))` for every distinct pair when `n1>1` (with the obvious census convention when `m=n1`). The final analysis weight is the inherited phase-1 admission/design weight multiplied by `1/pi2_i`; do not reweight by observed labels, route, response, or atom. Retain the known phase-1 and phase-2 probabilities, including the zero phase-2 probability for an unselected admission, and do not report an atom-level phase-2 estimate unless its required denominator and positive inclusion support are present.

This uniform phase-2 sample deliberately replaces the parent’s ambiguous proportional atom allocation and “two per occupied atom” exception. It is feasible by construction and does not promise sparse-atom precision. For any stated atom-route nomination, require at least 30 phase-1 sampled candidate-route admissions and at least 10 phase-2 challenged admissions for that route; if that condition is not met, the atom is `INCONCLUSIVE/NO_SELECTION` for actionability and defaults to `DEFER`, with no top-up or substitution. The absence of phase-2 coverage is a design limitation, not evidence for or against actionability.

## Exactly specified packet and control workload

For every selected admission, seal one pseudonymized **real composite admission packet** before any labels. It contains four separately rendered, independently ordered views of the same permitted pre-`D` evidence: A1 active-action, A2 independent active-action, S1 supersession, and H3 holistic challenge. Each view is read exactly once by one reviewer from its designated disjoint roster; no reread, tie-breaker, repeat case, or extra reviewer is permitted. Thus the fixed real workload is exactly `m` composite admission packets, `4m` component-view readings (at most 1,200), and zero additional real-case readings. “One whole-admission reading” means one reviewer opening and completing one complete composite packet view; component-view readings are counted separately and cannot be relabeled as four whole-admission readings.

For each selected admission, create exactly four blinded paired control comparisons, one per control type, with both versions in each pair read once by the designated control reader: (C1) route-irrelevant formatting/section-order/nuisance-token perturbation; (C2) one-field route-decisive replacement or timing perturbation; (C3) masked/inserted recommendation or plan perturbation with the locked compatibility-set transition; and (C4) target-linked plan versus wording/provenance-matched non-target plan. Therefore control workload is exactly `4m` paired comparisons, `8m` condition packets, and `8m` control-condition readings (at most 2,400); there are no control repeats or post-label controls. Control readers are blinded to type and expected transition. C1 must preserve unit inventory and route; C2 may change route only as specified by the inherited locked Cartesian-product compatibility table; C3 has its predeclared compatible-set transition; C4 must alter target linkage only. Any C1 invariant flip or unlisted C2/C3/C4 transition is a control failure and cannot be rescued by real-case agreement.

The fixed maximum is therefore 300 distinct phase-2 admissions, 1,200 real component-view readings, 1,200 paired-control comparisons, and 2,400 control-condition readings. Record packet hashes, codebook hashes, roster identities as pseudonyms, packet order, seal order, elapsed packet time, and physician-hour totals. If the declared operational resource ceiling is exceeded, or any selected admission cannot produce all four views and all four controls, stop and report `INCONCLUSIVE/NO_SELECTION`; never add cases, relax blinding, collapse rosters, or alter gates. If fewer than 300 phase-1 admissions exist, the census phase 2 uses all of them and reports the resulting precision limitation.

## Concealed views, labels, and route derivation

All packets are generated from sealed raw extracts before labels and contain no route, component abstraction, atom, stratum, weight, outcome, post-`D` text, POE, or expected answer. A1 and A2 record only target, requested action, timing, recipient, responsibility language, polarity, and departure-artifact status. S1 retains recommendation wording and target-linked plan spans while hiding all route and other component outputs. H3 receives the complete packet only after A1/A2/S1 are append-only sealed and records supported, contradicted, or indeterminate for each asserted field. Rosters are disjoint and independently coded; H3 is a measurement system, not an oracle.

Use the inherited exhaustive actionability categories: `A1_ACTIVE_PORTABLE`, `A2_ACTIVE_RECONCILE`, `A3_SUPERSEDED_EXPLICIT`, `A4_RESOLVED_OR_INACTIVE`, `A5_NO_TARGETED_ACTION`, `A6_UNKNOWN`; and supersession categories `S0_NONE`, `S1_EXPLICIT_REPLACEMENT`, `S2_EXPLICIT_ALTERNATIVE_INCOMPLETE`, `S3_COMPETING_CONTRADICTORY_PLANS`, `S4_UNKNOWN`. Duplicate restatements are one recommendation unit; distinct target/action/timing units remain distinct. Uncertainty is a compatible set and never forced favorable. A3 dominates. A unit may enter `AUTO` only where inherited route is AUTO and every adverse-compatible panel output is A1 with S0; A2, A3, S2, S3, S4, or any unresolved minority maps to `RECON` or `DEFER` under the inherited precedence. Parent route labels are derived only after every component label is sealed using the locked inherited compatibility table. No reviewer assigns a route.

## Inference, fixed governance rule, and falsification

Report inherited design-weighted unit/admission estimates, all-unit worst-admission estimates, capture, `C_AUTO`, `D_MIX`, route discordance, actionability, explicit supersession, response/missingness, and the exact workload. Use exact finite-population inversion for binary outcomes and 20,000 Rao-Wu replicates with all admission-level labels retained together. Use one max-statistic simultaneous 95% family over inherited gates, atom gates, discordance, actionability, supersession, controls, missingness, workload, and minimax endpoints. Primary intervals are adverse-compatible: only jointly observed support enters the lower endpoint, while unresolved/disagreement patterns enter the adverse upper endpoint. Latent-class and kappa results are diagnostics only and cannot narrow bounds.

The frozen governance/nomination rule is mechanical, not a post hoc reviewer threshold. A support-qualified atom is eligible for prospective silent validation only if every inherited support/event/effective-sample-size/weight/simultaneous-bound/route/workload gate passes; it has the stated 30 candidate-route and 10 phase-2 challenged-admission minima; lower simultaneous A1/A2/H3 agreement is at least 0.90; upper parent-versus-validation route discordance is at most 0.05 for AUTO/TIMING and 0.10 for RECON/DEFER; upper adverse-compatible active-but-superseded-or-unknown mass is at most 0.05 for AUTO and 0.10 for RECON; upper C1 invariant flip is at most 0.02 and upper decisive-control violation at most 0.05; packet nonresponse and control infeasibility upper bounds are below 0.05 unless the relevant inherited atom is censused; and workload and positivity pass. A supported AUTO atom with any adverse-compatible unresolved or superseded unit is blocked. Every failing, unobserved, rare, residual, novel, or undercovered atom is assigned `DEFER`; pooled, neighboring, macro-service, sentinel, favorable-consensus, imputed, shrunken, latent-class, and negative-control results cannot rescue it. The thresholds are governance rules for nomination, not clinical harm or benefit thresholds.

Supportive results permit only a prospective silent validation of the exact observable signature, with all other signatures `DEFER`. Adverse results exclude the affected atom from nomination or invalidate the affected control if a simultaneous bound crosses its gate; they do not prove harm. Inconclusive results include sparse phase-2 coverage, unavailable controls, nonresponse, broken seals, wide bounds, or workload/positivity failure and are not evidence of safety. Any route-before-seal computation, packet leakage, roster overlap, post-label control creation, favorable unknown assignment, prohibited POE use, or changed sampling rule invalidates the run.

## What this cannot establish

MIMIC cannot establish actual viewing, signature/finalization semantics, departure visibility, communication, responsibility, patient preference, outside plans, order/referral execution, scheduling, appropriateness, follow-up, malignancy, harm, utility, benefit, or causality. The audit measures observable textual action structure and target-linked supersession only. A passing atom requires a separately approved prospective actual-calendar silent bridge measuring viewing, edits, signatures, transmission, responsible team, orders/referrals, scheduling, outside plans, and outcomes before any live clinical, safety, utility, or causal claim.
