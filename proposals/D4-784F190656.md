FROZEN EXPOSURES, MODELS, AND ESTIMANDS
Sort by eid only for deterministic processing. Assign fold K_i = uint64(first eight bytes of SHA-256("renal-persistence-v3-20260911:" || decimal eid), big-endian) modulo 10. Freeze and report fold counts before outcomes are modeled. Original eid-derived folds remain fixed in every bootstrap and matched-control intersection; all copies of a resampled participant remain in its original fold.

All analytes are natural-log transformed after positivity checks. Every restricted cubic spline (RCS) has five training-sample knots at empirical quantiles (0.05, 0.275, 0.50, 0.725, 0.95), using a linear term plus the three standard restricted truncated-cubic terms, an intercept outside the basis, and natural linear tails without clipping held-out values. For ordered knots t1<...<t5 and j=1,2,3, define h_j(x)=[(x-t_j)_+^3-((t5-t_j)/(t5-t4))(x-t4)_+^3+((t4-t_j)/(t5-t4))(x-t5)_+^3]/(t5-t1)^2; the basis is x,h1,h2,h3. Quantiles use the default linear empirical interpolation. Non-strict knots or rank deficiency are a failed fit, not silently changed degrees of freedom.

Within each held-out fold, P1 fits training-fold OLS of baseline log cystatin-C on RCS(baseline log creatinine), recruitment age, sex, and age-by-sex. It predicts held-out cystatin-C; observed-minus-predicted residuals are divided by the nuisance fit's training residual SD sqrt(SSE/(n-p)). P2 analogously residualizes baseline log creatinine on RCS(baseline log cystatin-C). No held-out observation enters its nuisance fit or knot estimation.

For P1 define Y1=(repeat log creatinine-M1)/S1, where M1 and S1 are respectively the mean and sample SD across the complete frozen primary cohort. For P2 analogously standardize repeat log cystatin-C. This common scale is used for all stacked held-out observations and replaces the parent's ambiguous fold-specific outcome units. Beta1 is primary-cohort repeat-log-creatinine SD per training-fold residual SD higher baseline cystatin-C conditional on creatinine; beta2 is the reciprocal cystatin-C outcome estimand. Bootstrap replicates recompute M/S to include standardization uncertainty.

Fit one stacked OLS per direction, never average fold coefficients. P1 regressors are intercept, cross-fitted R_cys|cr, RCS(baseline log creatinine), recruitment age, sex, age-by-sex, RCS(elapsed years), and nine fold indicators. P2 substitutes reciprocal markers. RCS knots for these stacked outcome regressions are computed once on the frozen complete primary cohort; in a bootstrap they remain at original values, while nuisance-fold knots are recomputed because nuisance learning is part of the resampled pipeline. Report beta, HC3 diagnostic SE/CI, partial R-squared against the same model excluding the residual exposure, and ten-fold held-out RMSE and Gaussian log predictive density differences computed using training-fold outcome regressions. Predictive metrics supplement and never replace inference.

FULL-PIPELINE BOOTSTRAP, MULTIPLICITY, AND MATERIALITY
Use one seeded joint participant bootstrap stream with B=2,000 successful replicates (minimum 1,000 only if resource limits are explicitly reported), sampling n primary participants with replacement. Refit both directional nuisance and stacked models in each replicate while retaining eid folds; recompute nuisance knots/scales and outcome M/S. A replicate fails if any fold has zero test observations, any nuisance or stacked design is rank deficient, a residual/outcome SD is nonfinite or <=0, or either coefficient is nonfinite. Draw replacements until B successes or 1.25B attempts. More than 2% failures or failure to obtain B successes is an inferential certification failure and yields inconclusive results. Record seed, attempts, failures by reason, software versions, and all aggregate estimates.

For direction d, let beta_d be the original estimate and beta_bd bootstrap estimates. Construct the centered one-sided p-value p_d=[1+sum_b I(beta_bd-beta_d<=-beta_d)]/(B+1). Apply Holm to the two p-values in ascending order, with deterministic P1-before-P2 tie-breaking; adjusted sorted values are max of preceding raw multiplier values and capped at one. A shared signal requires both Holm-adjusted p<=0.05.

Let s_d be the bootstrap SD of beta_bd. For simultaneous one-sided 95% lower bounds, compute q_L as the empirical 0.95 quantile (nearest-rank, conservative higher order statistic) of max_d[(beta_d-beta_bd)/s_d], and L_d=beta_d-q_L*s_d. For simultaneous two-sided familywise 95% intervals compute q_A from max_d|beta_bd-beta_d|/s_d and report beta_d plus/minus q_A*s_d. Also report direction-specific percentile 95% intervals. Zero/nonfinite s_d is a certification failure. Shared observable persistence requires both Holm rejections and both L_d>0.

The provisional smallest relevant association remains 0.05 outcome SD per residual-exposure SD and is not a validated clinical threshold. "Material bidirectional association" requires both simultaneous L_d>0.05. If statistical criteria pass but either point estimate is below 0.05 or either bound includes 0.05, label magnitude small or uncertain and never clinically useful. A simultaneous upper bound below 0.05 in either direction falsifies material bidirectional persistence even if a smaller association is positive.
