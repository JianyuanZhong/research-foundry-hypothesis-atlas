# Working proposal (episode 27): Composition shift or within-cell program in the RA remission-vs-resistant synovial macrophage axis?

Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: generation (mechanistic, substate-resolved emphasis).
Parent: [prior hypothesis] (valid; board leader). Seed: D3-M1 (+D3-M3).

## 1. Unresolved question, importance, advance beyond the parent
The parent ([prior hypothesis]) tested whether an in-vitro MERTK macrophage
program is concordant with the remission-vs-resistant axis, but its OWN recorded residual limitation
is that E-MTAB-8322 was used **cross-sectional pseudobulk macrophages only, with no cell-state
resolution**. Alivernini 2020 [K1] itself reports the remission/active/refractory states differ in the
**frequencies** of nine discrete STM clusters (composition language), while the clinical value of a
macrophage program to a next decision (which state to target, which model to carry forward) depends on
whether remission vs resistance is a **composition shift** (fraction of resolution-like MerTK+/CD206+
vs inflammatory vs lipid-associated substates) or a **within-cell regulatory program** co-existing in
the same substates. Zhang 2023 [K2] shows that cell-type **abundance phenotypes (CTAPs)** are a major
axis of RA synovial heterogeneity and predict treatment response, so a pure abundance explanation is
the strongest rival. This is unresolved and consequential: if the axis is composition, a therapy should
change substate proportions (differentiation/recruitment); if it is within-cell, the same substates are
present but transcriptionally rewired (a distinct target set). The advance over the parent is using
E-MTAB-8322 at **single-cell / per-donor resolution** to decompose variance into substate composition
vs within-substate (within-cell) components, and to test whether any within-cell component is
donor-intrinsic rather than group-driven.

## 2. Hypotheses and discriminating observations
- H1 (within-cell program, composition-adjusted): after fixing substate composition, remission- vs
  resistant-derived macrophages of the SAME substate differ in a coherent expression program.
  Discriminator: a within-substate (per-cell-type pseudobulk) condition contrast survives adjustment
  for per-donor substate fractions with a consistent sign across donors (repeatable, donor-aware).
- H0 (composition): the group difference is fully explained by differing SUBSTATE FRACTIONS; within
  each substate, condition contrast ~0. Discriminator: fraction terms carry the signal; marginalizing
  over donor composition removes the group effect.
- H2 (donor-intrinsic state): the within-cell program, if present, is a stable property of the donor
  state (remission vs resistant) reproducible across held-out donors, not of the individual sample.
  Discriminator: leave-one-donor-out predicts the held-out donor's group from its cells (AUC>0.5,
  stable loadings); if the effect is donor-specific noise, LODO collapses to chance.
- H3 (annotation/technical artifact): observed substate structure or within-cell effect is an
  annotation/technical artifact (clustering granularity, ambient RNA, 10x version, cell-cycle,
  doublets). Discriminator: the within-cell result is unstable to reasonable clustering resolutions
  / annotation gene sets, or disappears after per-donor aggregation [K3] and permutation-null testing;
  concordance of substate markers is driven by a few donors or by library-level covariates.

## 4. Estimand and analysis: simple baseline vs substantive alternative
Estimand: for the specified macrophage-only population (27 minus exclusions), the group (remission
vs resistant) difference in the synovial macrophage state, decomposed into
(i) a COMPOSITION component (per-donor fractional abundance of annotated macrophage substates:
resolution-like MerTK+/CD206+, inflammatory, lipid-associated) and (ii) a WITHIN-CELL component
(average expression of a substate-adjusted program within each substate); plus whether the within-cell
component is donor-intrinsic (generalizes to held-out donors).

SIMPLE BASELINE (must run first): (a) per-donor substate fractions (marker-score / clustering
annotation) -> group contrast of fractions (Mann-Whitney / per-donor trend) and (b) cluster-level
pseudobulk (sum counts per donor x substate) -> condition contrast per substate via a donor-aware
model (condition fixed, donor random) with DESeq2/edgeR-style or limma-voom on donor x substate
pseudobulk. Reports: which substates shift in fraction (H0 evidence) and whether a within-substate
gene/score effect exists (H1 evidence) at the crude annotation used.

SUBSTANTIVE ALTERNATIVE (justified, not a small predictive gain): a donor-aware
composition-vs-within-cell variance decomposition plus a generative mixture model.
- Fit per-cell expression of a curated substate-marker panel + program genes; model per-cell
  expression as substate assignment (mixture) x condition effect, with donor as random effect
  (hierarchical / mixed model, e.g. a latent-variable Gaussian mixture or a linear mixed model with
  donor random intercept & slope). Estimate the fraction of total group-discriminating variance
  attributable to composition (mixing weights) vs within-substate (component means), i.e. a
  variance-decomposition ratio, with bootstrap CIs over the 13 non-healthy RA donors.
- Strength vs baseline: the baseline fixes a crisp annotation and can either (i) make the result an
  artifact of the clustering granularity or (ii) miss a within-cell program that is diluted in
  pseudobulk; the mixture alternative marginalizes over annotation uncertainty and directly estimates
  the composition/within-cell split, exposing whether a "within-cell program" is real after
  accounting for composition -- information the fixed-annotation baseline cannot reveal.
- Split / uncertainty: HELD-OUT-DONOR (leave-one-donor-out) cross-validation; predict the held-out
  donor's group from its cells at fixed composition vs composition-free representation. Uncertainty
  from donor-level bootstrap and the permutation null.
- Permutation null: permute donor group labels (respecting group sizes, 5= / 6=, with 1000+ draws)
  to calibrate the within-cell effect and the LODO AUC; report empirical p and FDR across substates.
- Baseline comparison: report the gain of the mixture/LMM decomposition over the fixed-annotation
  pseudobulk contrast in out-of-sample donor classification and in stability of the estimated
  composition/within-cell ratio across clustering resolutions (e.g. Leiden resolutions 0.4-1.2).

## 5. Falsification criteria
SUPPORTIVE (H1/H2): within-substate condition effect of consistent sign across donors (donor-aware
model, FDR<0.05), composition/within-cell variance ratio materially >0 for within-cell, LODO donor-group
prediction AUC > 0.5 with stable loadings, and survival after per-donor aggregation [K3]. Would
establish a candidate within-cell regulatory program (still not a causal mechanism, no transition).
ADVERSE (H0 favored): group signal carried by substate fractions; within-substate contrasts ~0 or
sign-inconsistent; per-donor aggregation removes the effect -> the axis is a composition/abundance
shift, not a within-cell program. Rules against H1 within this dataset; does not prove no program
exists elsewhere (panel/assay limits).
INCONCLUSIVE (H3): within-cell effect unstable to clustering resolution, driven by <3 donors, or
explained by library/technical covariates; LODO collapses to chance -> annotation/technical artifact;
requires larger donor n, independent cohort, or expert adjudication of substate identity.
Note: cross-sectional states cannot establish a longitudinal transition (D3-M3 caution); none of
these outcomes establishes mechanism, direct target action, or clinical benefit.
