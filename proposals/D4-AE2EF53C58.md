# Corrected CBC semantics and an explicit audit-only outcome for sparse-CBC colorectal-cancer prognosis

## Question, advance, and present evidentiary status

This child repairs a material field-identity error in `[prior hypothesis]` and resolves what the presently available files imply for its endpoint-observability gate. UK Biobank Data-Field `30070` is **red blood cell distribution width (RDW), percent**, not platelet count; Data-Field `30080` is **platelet count, 10^9 cells/Litre**, not RDW. Therefore every platelet landmark value and platelet slope in the primary model must use `30080`, while `30070` is secondary RDW only. No previous analysis that interpreted `30070` as platelets or `30080` as RDW is scientifically interpretable under those labels, even though the corrected and stale three-marker complete-pair totals happen to be equal.

The clinically important unresolved hypothesis remains:

> Among UK Biobank repeat attenders aged 40–75 with paired protocolized haemoglobin, mean corpuscular volume (MCV), and platelet measurements, who are alive and free of malignant cancer through day 365 after the repeat assessment, does the prespecified joint trajectory of falling haemoglobin, falling MCV, and rising platelet count add prognostic information for colorectal cancer (CRC) during days 366–1825 beyond age, sex, and repeat-assessment CBC levels; and is that increment greater than for first non-CRC malignant cancer?

The strongest claim supported now is narrower: the exact local columns exist; current authoritative UK Biobank field pages identify their meanings, units, instancing, and outcome-field relationships; and a full-row local scan finds 18,379 participants complete at instances 0 and 1 for corrected haemoglobin/MCV/platelet fields. The experiment has not established prognostic value. More importantly, the local export has no release/extraction/Showcase manifest, and no document available in this episode ties cancer- and death-registry coverage endpoints to this exact export. Current field pages label fields 40000, 40005, and 40006 as accruing; field 40005 additionally warns that the most recent 12–18 months are incomplete. Thus the coverage-certificate gate currently resolves to **uncertified audit-only**, not inferential population follow-up. This is a falsifiable result of the gate, not permission to regard absent events as non-events.

A later release-matched coverage certificate can activate the locked inferential branch below without changing the scientific hypothesis. Until then, Harbor must execute only schema, semantics, completeness, cohort-flow, and recorded-event audits and must not estimate coverage-valid incidence, hazard, discrimination, calibration, IPCW quantities, PPV, or review-capacity yields.

The question matters because subtle multi-year CBC changes could potentially prioritize people for further CRC assessment before overt symptoms, but a positive association in selected repeat attenders would still be prognostic evidence only. It would not establish screening efficacy, referral benefit, FIT or colonoscopy performance, stage shift, mortality benefit, cost-effectiveness, or clinical utility.

## Cohort, temporal boundaries, and recorded endpoints

Define `E=L+366 calendar days` and `H=L+1825 calendar days`. Washout is the half-open interval `[L,E)`; an event exactly on `E` belongs to follow-up. This delayed entry prevents CBC measurements at `L` from predicting cancers already recorded during the following year.

The locked primary population requires:

6. for inferential analysis only, `E` within certified observation.

## Falsification, interpretation, and verification limits

Unit tests must fail if 30070 is labelled or used as platelet count, if 30080 is labelled or used as RDW, if cancer date/code arrays are cross-paired, if day `E` is placed in washout, if post-death diagnoses are treated as observable events, or if a maximum observed date is used as censoring. A marker-range identity check must flag if local RDW-like ranges appear under the platelet alias or platelet-like ranges under RDW. In the certified model branch, permute full endpoint processes within sex and five-year age strata and rerun all fold-local steps; systematic retained increment indicates leakage. An impossible-temporal check predicting pre-`L` CRC with post-`L` logic must trigger a date/cohort audit if it appears predictive.

Supportive inferential evidence would require a passed coverage certificate, adequate certified events, adverse-direction joint slope association with uncertainty excluding the null, a positive paired out-of-fold discrimination increment without Brier/calibration deterioration, and clean falsification checks. CRC specificity additionally requires `D>0` with an interval excluding zero and compatible Brier results. Adverse evidence would be a precise null or opposite-direction association with no positive prediction increment, or a precise `D<=0`; this would argue against this sparse protocolized trajectory in repeat attenders, not against all clinically timed CBC models. Broad intervals, unstable censoring weights, discordant metrics, inadequate events, or the current failed coverage gate are inconclusive regarding the prognostic hypothesis.
