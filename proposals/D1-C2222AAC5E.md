# Two-phase validation of MIMIC discharge-note `storetime` as a departure-time availability proxy

## Decision question, clinical value, and advance

For an inpatient incidental pulmonary-nodule recommendation already available by discharge, a hospital could invest first in (i) making a canonical discharge artifact final and retrievable before the patient physically leaves or (ii) reconciling interval-specific nodule content into an artifact that is already available. The parent candidate uses `DS.storetime <= admissions.dischtime` to distinguish these observable process signals, but the decisive construct is whether the departure-time version was actually final and retrievable at physical departure. MIMIC contains neither physical-departure time nor signing, version, role-specific visibility, release, or audit-log events. Therefore a MIMIC `storetime` contrast alone must not nominate an availability intervention.

This successor makes construct validation integral rather than optional. It has two linked phases:

1. **Retrospective MIMIC triage:** preserve the frozen all-opportunity denominator, reciprocal report/addendum reconstruction, sole-DS rule, mutually exclusive exported-record states, unresolved-state bounds, and common follow-up clock. Estimate only a storage/export-timestamp and exported-text signal.
2. **Prospective timestamp/version crosswalk:** on a new, probability-sampled discharge cohort in the same EHR and extraction pipeline, compare the MIMIC-analog storetime status with a prespecified gold-standard departure-time availability state. Use the resulting stratified misclassification matrix either to estimate a calibrated departure-time state distribution or, when transportability or precision fails, to partially identify it. The intervention-prioritization estimand changes only after this calibration.

The design does not test communication, patient receipt, clinician action, surveillance adherence, recommendation appropriateness, causality, cancer outcomes, or benefit.

## Strongest supported evidence, unresolved hypotheses, and unavailable evidence

### What current evidence supports

The exact MIMIC snapshot can support complete reconstruction of within-subject note and admission timestamps and exported note text. Inherited full-source audits found 2,321,355 radiology rows; 331,794 `DS` rows with one `DS` per admission key; 86,609 DS rows stored by administrative discharge, 147,800 stored after discharge through 24 hours, 76,977 after 24 hours through 7 days, 20,329 after 7 days, and 17 with missing `storetime`; and 25,735 reciprocal relation edges, of which 25,600 were fully resolved chronological same-admission `RR -> AR` pairs and 135 involved absent nodes. These are feasibility and timestamp-distribution findings. The fact that classifiable delayed DS rows had `charttime <= dischtime` is compatible with several documentation/export workflows and proves none of them.

Published work supports that incidental-finding handoff and tracking are clinically important but not that discharge text causes follow-up. Kwan et al. reported absent interval imaging in 35/65 patients with an explicit follow-up suggestion and an association between discharge-summary mention and timely imaging (PMID 30794133; PMCID PMC6625441). The FIND before/after program report observed more follow-up after program implementation but explicitly involved tracking processes rather than an isolated timestamp mechanism (PMID 36759382; PMCID PMC10287591). A 2025 systematic review found heterogeneous interventions and substantial risk of bias in much of the nonrandomized evidence (PMID 40081655; PMCID PMC12264345). These observations motivate a decision-valid process audit; they do not validate MIMIC `storetime` as visibility or establish benefit.

### Unresolved validation hypothesis

Let `S=1` when the MIMIC-analog canonical DS has known `storetime <= administrative dischtime`; let `S=0` when it has known `storetime > administrative dischtime`. Let `G=1` only when the exact canonical DS version intended for the transition is final and retrievable in the prespecified clinician-facing production UI at the adjudicated physical-departure instant; otherwise let `G=0`, with unresolved truth kept separate.

The primary phase-2 hypothesis is that `S` is decision-valid for `G`: probability-weighted `P(S != G) <= 0.10`, with a simultaneous upper 95% confidence limit no greater than 0.10, and both predictive values `P(G=1|S=1)` and `P(G=0|S=0)` having simultaneous lower 95% limits at least 0.80. The thresholds are prospective workflow-decision tolerances, not universal clinical-quality standards; report sensitivity at misclassification tolerances 0.05 and 0.20 and predictive-value thresholds 0.70 and 0.90.

A second, explicitly conditional hypothesis concerns intervention prioritization after calibration. Define the true departure-time all-opportunity contrast

`Delta_G = P(G=0) - P(G=1 and departure-visible content is C_O or C_I)`.

Availability-first support requires the lower simultaneous 95% bound for `Delta_G` to exceed +0.05 across prespecified calibration and transport-sensitivity regions. Content-first support requires the upper bound to be below -0.05. The 0.05 margin remains an operational prioritization margin, not a claim of benefit.

### Required evidence absent from all configured datasets

The prospective phase requires data not present in MIMIC, EICU, HCC, or UKB: an objectively captured physical-departure instant; administrative discharge-event provenance; immutable note artifact and version identifiers; creation, save, signature, finalization, export/store and release times; status at each version; a read-only role-specific UI retrieval/audit event at departure; the exact text/version visible then; later version lineage; EHR and extraction-pipeline version; and audit sampling probabilities. Patient-facing packet, portal release, health-information-exchange transmission, and recipient-system receipt are distinct optional dimensions and cannot substitute for clinician-facing retrievability. Human review is needed when physical-departure sources disagree or the artifact/version semantics are ambiguous.

Without these unavailable data, phase 1 ends in a storage/export triage result and no availability-intervention selection.

## Phase 1: boundary-safe retrospective MIMIC triage

### Population and fixed report knowledge state

Scan all 2,321,355 radiology rows; no row sampling. Lexical retrieval is high-sensitivity screening only. Two radiology-trained reviewers independently adjudicate all screened candidates while blinded to DS text, DS timing, and later care.

Include adults age at least 18 with a base CT/CTA chest or abdominal CT including lung bases, linked to an admission on `(subject_id, hadm_id)`, with `admittime - 12 hours <= base.charttime <= dischtime`. Require an incidental pulmonary/lung nodule or indeterminate focal nodular pulmonary opacity and an affirmed, patient-specific, unconditional recommendation for chest/thoracic CT at exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months. Exclude screening, explicit pre-existing nodule surveillance, staging/oncologic surveillance, actively managed primary pulmonary malignancy, nonpulmonary nodules, conditional/optional recommendations dependent on unavailable risk, explicit no-follow-up recommendations, hospice/comfort-only transition, in-hospital death, and a pre-discharge CT explicitly satisfying or superseding the recommendation. Do not infer Fleischner eligibility, smoking pack-years, appropriateness, or stability from absent data.

Build a static relation-integrity graph using both `parent_note_id` and `addendum_note_id`; require reciprocal, same-subject, same-admission `RR -> AR` relations in the primary analysis. A globally absent required endpoint, contradictory relation, nondeterministic cycle, or missing required boundary timestamp is `U_R`. A known addendum with `storetime > dischtime` is late, not missing: it is excluded from the discharge-boundary report state and retained only in a post-boundary transition table. Every report component used to establish eligibility must have nonmissing `storetime <= dischtime` and `charttime <= dischtime`; a component with post-discharge `charttime` or irreconcilable timestamp ordering is unresolved rather than silently converted into pre-discharge knowledge. A recommendation first introduced only after discharge is not eligible. No future addendum text, negation, or interval change may affect `N` or the strict state.

Select the first eligible opportunity per subject by base `charttime`, then base `storetime`, then `note_id`. Freeze `N` before inspecting DS timing, DS text, post-discharge addenda, or later trace. Repeat the cascade at a zero-hour admission boundary and using `edregtime/edouttime`; reversal is boundary sensitivity, not a stable finding.

### Sole artifact and exported-record proxy states

Validate the complete DS source before analysis. In this snapshot, the sole `DS` row per `(subject_id, hadm_id)` is the canonical exported artifact. Never select an earlier draft or a later row by outcome. If a refresh contains multiple rows, stop and freeze a version rule before analysis.

MIMIC does not provide immutable version lineage. Consequently, even when `storetime <= dischtime`, the sole exported row's text is not proven to be the exact text visible at departure. Phase-1 labels are therefore explicitly starred exported-record proxy states, not true departure states. Apply this deterministic, mutually exclusive precedence to every member of `N`:

1. `U_D*`: no linked sole DS, missing `storetime`, contradictory linkage, unreadable text, or inability to classify the exported row. Unknown is not delayed or omitted.
2. `A_S*`: sole DS exists and known `storetime > dischtime`. This means only “not observed stored by the administrative boundary.” Later faithful text cannot retroactively change it.
3. `R*`: known `storetime <= dischtime` and exported text explicitly documents an alternate resolution that clearly supersedes the index CT plan, such as documented refusal/preference, goals-of-care decision, specialist takeover with a concrete alternative, or already-scheduled outside follow-up. If an active CT plan coexists and is not superseded, retain a resolution flag and classify the active plan below.
4. `F*`: known `storetime <= dischtime` and the exported text has an active patient-specific pulmonary-nodule mention, chest/thoracic CT action, and exactly the same 3/6/12-month calendar interval or unambiguous equivalent due date.
5. `C_I*`: known `storetime <= dischtime` and the exported text has a patient-specific nodule/follow-up signal but lacks action or usable timing, or has discordant interval/modality without explicit superseding resolution.
6. `C_O*`: known `storetime <= dischtime` and the exported text has no active patient-specific actionable nodule plan.

Tag `artifact-before-recommendation` when DS `storetime` precedes the first recommendation-bearing report component. This remains a stale/update chronology subtype, not an exclusion and not evidence that a writer ignored information already available. Preserve raw `charttime-dischtime`, `storetime-dischtime`, and recommendation-to-artifact differences. DS `charttime` never back-imputes storage or visibility.

Report the full all-`N` vector `(U_D*, A_S*, R*, F*, C_I*, C_O*)`. Define only the phase-1 triage contrast

`Delta_S* = P(A_S*) - P(C_I* or C_O*)`.

This is a multinomial exported-data contrast. It is not `Delta_G`, and no result from it alone selects an availability intervention. The conditional exported-content vector `(R*,F*,C_I*,C_O*)/N_obs*`, where `N_obs*=R*+F*+C_I*+C_O*`, is descriptive and cannot replace the all-opportunity denominator.

Two blinded transition reviewers abstract exported DS content before radiology fields and chronology are revealed. Require complete consensus, raw agreement at least 0.80, and Gwet AC1 at least 0.70 for eligibility, exact interval, alternate resolution, and content state. Persistent disagreement yields reviewer-specific bounds. Screening requires sensitivity above 0.95 with lower Wilson limit above 0.90; automated replacement requires each relevant PPV above 0.90 with lower limit above 0.80.

Use 9,999 fixed-seed subject bootstraps and simultaneous intervals for the phase-1 state vector and `Delta_S*`. Require `N>=200`, at least 20 `A_S*` and 20 `C*` events, `U_D*<=5%`, interval width at most 0.10, and all adjudication gates for a precise triage signal. Assign unresolved DS states entirely to `A_S*` and then entirely to `C*` for extrema of `Delta_S*`; bounds crossing a margin are inconclusive. These bounds address missing phase-1 state, not the unobserved `S`-to-`G` construct error.

At +24 hours and +7 days, report transitions from `A_S*` to later exported `F*/C_I*/C_O*/R*` without changing the strict state. Report post-discharge report addenda separately; they never alter eligibility or strict content. Because DS version lineage is unavailable, do not call later exported text the version that existed at its nominal earlier `storetime`.

## Phase 2: prospective stratified departure-time validation and content-version crosswalk

### Setting, cohort, and exact prospective fields

Conduct the calibration at the data-contributing institution using the same production EHR note type and the extraction pipeline that generates MIMIC-analog `charttime` and `storetime`. A different vendor, note workflow, extraction mapping, or institution is external evidence only and cannot directly adjust historical MIMIC without an explicit transport analysis.

During a prespecified 12-month enrollment window, create a minimal timestamp registry for every adult live hospital discharge with a canonical DS artifact. Select the first discharge per patient. Exclude only in-hospital death, discharge against medical advice when the ordinary DS workflow is not applicable, and episodes lacking permission to collect audit logs; report every exclusion. The general discharge cohort validates the timestamp mechanism with adequate event yield. In parallel, enroll a census of all prospectively identified incidental pulmonary-nodule opportunities meeting the phase-1 clinical rules; this nested cohort validates content-version mapping in the target clinical use case. Clinical opportunity identification is blinded to the final calibration classification.

For every registry episode collect: `site_id`, pseudonymous `patient_id`, `admission_id`, service/ward, ICU exposure, discharge destination, weekday/weekend and shift; `t_admin` plus source-system/event identifier and resolution; MIMIC-analog `t_store`, `t_chart`, and extraction job/version; immutable DS artifact/version IDs; every version's `t_create`, `t_save`, `t_sign`, `t_final`, status and lineage; physical-departure source times; automated UI-probe times, user role, artifact/version returned and status; departure-time text snapshot hash; release/transmission fields if separately studied; audit-selection stratum and inclusion probability; and adjudication flags. Raw protected logs remain local and read-only.

Define physical departure `t_phys` from a direct, workflow-neutral exit scan or validated bed-departure sensor. Triangulate it with transport completion and bed-management departure. Agreement within five minutes confirms `t_phys`; disagreement over five minutes, missing primary sensor data, or only an administrative discharge order yields `U_P`, not a chosen timestamp. Report 0-, 5-, and 15-minute tolerance sensitivities. A local clinical operations and informatics panel must validate that the physical event definition corresponds to leaving inpatient care.

At `t_phys`, a preauthorized read-only automated probe using the prespecified ordinary downstream-clinician role queries the production UI for the canonical DS artifact. `G=1` requires that the intended transition version is both marked final/signed according to the locally validated workflow and retrievable with identical artifact/version ID at or immediately before `t_phys`. A draft, inaccessible artifact, wrong version, or first retrieval after departure is `G=0`. Probe failure, ambiguous canonical-version semantics, clock desynchronization beyond one minute, or unresolved `t_phys` is `U_G`. Synchronize systems to a documented clock source and retain raw resolution. Availability is retrievability, not proof that anyone viewed, understood, transmitted, or acted on the note.

For the nested nodule cohort, two blinded reviewers classify the exact departure-visible text snapshot into `R_G, F_G, C_IG, C_OG` using the phase-1 codebook. They do not inspect later versions. If `G=0`, content is not assigned at departure. Thus the true mutually exclusive all-opportunity states are `(U_G, A_G=G0, R_G, F_G, C_IG, C_OG)`.

### Probability sampling and precision

Because `S=0` may be uncommon or concentrated in lag strata, capture registry and audit-log fields universally, then select audited episodes with known probabilities after `S` is computed. Stratify by `S`; signed `t_store-t_admin` bins (`<=-6h`, `(-6h,0]`, `(0,2h]`, `(2,24h]`, `>24h`); medical/surgical service; ICU exposure; weekday/weekend; discharge shift; and artifact-before-recommendation status in the nested cohort. Randomly sample within strata and oversample `S=0` and boundary-near episodes. Use inverse inclusion probabilities for population estimates and retain unweighted cells.

Target at least 300 audited `S=1` and 300 audited `S=0` general discharges, with at least 50 classifiable episodes in every prespecified lag bin used for calibration and at least 50 instances of each true state `G=0` and `G=1`. This gives roughly six-percentage-point or better worst-case binomial half-width within each 300-member proxy group before multiplicity; final enrollment is increased if design effects or unresolved truth prevent that precision. Accrue at least 150 nested nodule opportunities and at least 20 departure-time `A_G` and 20 `C_G` events before estimating `Delta_G`; otherwise only timestamp validation and broad content bounds are reported. These are precision gates, not evidence that the study will necessarily accrue enough rare nodule opportunities.

### Calibration matrix, matrix adjustment, and partial identification

Within each prespecified stratum `h`, estimate the weighted matrix

`K_h[s,g] = P(S=s | G=g, h)`

and report sensitivity `P(S=1|G=1)`, specificity `P(S=0|G=0)`, both predictive values, misclassification risk, and administrative-to-physical boundary discordance. Separately decompose errors caused by `t_admin != t_phys` from errors caused by `t_store` not matching UI final/retrievable status. Use survey-weighted subject bootstrap intervals simultaneous over primary metrics and strata. Do not pool strata whose likelihood-ratio interaction test or clinically material absolute error-rate difference exceeds 0.10.

For the nested cohort, estimate a full observed-to-true matrix mapping `(A_S*,R*,F*,C_I*,C_O*)` to `(A_G,R_G,F_G,C_IG,C_OG)`. This simultaneously addresses storetime-status error and future-version/exported-text error. Estimate it with a constrained multinomial likelihood; do not naively invert an ill-conditioned matrix. Require all matrix columns represented, condition number below 20, no fitted negative cell, effective sample size at least 50 per primary proxy status, and bootstrap convergence in at least 99% of replicates.

A matrix-adjusted historical MIMIC estimate is permitted only if: the calibration uses the same institution, EHR note workflow, DS definition, clock semantics, and extraction mapping; every MIMIC calibration stratum has support; standardized calibration covariates cover at least 95% of phase-1 opportunities; no primary stratum-specific error differs by more than 0.10 without explicit standardization; and the informatics panel documents version compatibility. Standardize `K_h` to the phase-1 distribution of service, ICU, discharge destination, signed lag bin, and chronology using only fields available in both phases. Fit the historical latent state vector by constrained likelihood in each bootstrap replicate, then compute `Delta_G`.

If any transport, positivity, matrix-stability, or sample-size gate fails, do not point-adjust. Instead, construct partial-identification bounds by linear programming over all latent state probabilities consistent with: phase-1 observed multinomial confidence regions; simultaneous confidence intervals for each audited matrix cell; `U_D*` and `U_G` extrema; and transport deviations `|K_h^MIMIC-K_h^audit| <= delta_T` at `delta_T=0, 0.05, and 0.10`. Report sharp lower and upper bounds for `P(A_G)`, `P(C_G)`, and `Delta_G`. Without a defensible finite transport bound, report the logical worst-case bounds, even if vacuous. Never substitute a pooled point matrix merely to obtain a decision.

The prospective nested cohort's directly weighted `Delta_G` is the primary target-site estimate. A transported historical MIMIC estimate is secondary because deidentification prevents usable cross-subject calendar-era alignment and historical workflow compatibility may be unresolvable.

## Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive outcomes

### Storetime validation

* **Supportive:** the simultaneous upper limit for weighted `P(S!=G)` is at most 0.10; both predictive-value lower limits are at least 0.80; no key stratum exceeds the prespecified 0.15 error tolerance; unresolved truth is at most 5%; and results are stable to physical-departure tolerance and sampling weights. This supports using `storetime <= administrative dischtime` as a calibrated proxy for the narrowly defined final/retrievable clinician-UI construct in that workflow. It does not establish communication or transmission.
* **Adverse:** the lower confidence limit for misclassification exceeds 0.20, the upper limit for `P(G=0|S=0)` is below 0.50, administrative-versus-physical boundary mismatch explains a large share of labels, or delayed `S=0` artifacts are commonly already final/retrievable at departure. Then the MIMIC delay signal is principally a timestamp/extraction warning and must not motivate a finalization intervention.
* **Inconclusive:** estimates lie between thresholds; calibration cells are sparse; `U_G>5%`; strata disagree; clocks or version semantics are unresolved; the audit is from a nontransportable workflow; or confidence/transport regions span both useful and poor proxy performance.

### Calibrated process prioritization

* **Availability-first support:** nodule-cohort adjudication and precision gates pass and the lower 95% bound for `Delta_G` exceeds +0.05 both directly in the target-site audit and throughout the prespecified transport/partial-identification region. This supports prospective testing of a departure-time final/retrievable-artifact check. It does not show communication, adherence, causality, or benefit.
* **Content-first support:** the upper bound for `Delta_G` is below -0.05 throughout the region, and alternate resolutions do not account for the content signal. This supports prospective testing of structured interval-specific content reconciliation in an already retrievable departure version.
* **Adverse to either single target:** both `P(A_G)` and `P(C_G)` are low with narrow upper bounds, or explicit alternate resolutions dominate. The discharge artifact is unlikely to be the major measured process bottleneck; tracking, scheduling, direct communication, or cross-system exchange requires a separate study.
* **Inconclusive:** the interval crosses either prioritization margin; calibration changes the sign of `Delta_S*`; future-version content error is material; direct and transported estimates disagree; unresolved states or sampling/design effects are large; or event/annotation/positivity gates fail. The correct action is further audit or a bundled/factorial prospective workflow test, not selection from a phase-1 point estimate.

A large positive `Delta_S*` followed by poor `S` validation is explicitly adverse to interpreting the parent result as availability. A small or negative `Delta_S*` with strong calibration can still be scientifically useful by redirecting attention toward content or other processes.

## Common downstream observation clock and limits

Same-system imaging is optional, secondary, and cannot choose the phase-2 intervention. For every phase-1 `N`, define due date as base examination `charttime` plus exactly 3, 6, or 12 calendar months using calendar arithmetic. Use `dischtime` as the single time zero for all MIMIC groups; no `max(dischtime, DS.storetime)` or state-specific clock is allowed in the primary appendix. Count a MIMIC-observed nodule-focused chest CT only when `charttime > dischtime` and within due date +/-42 days, with +/-30 and +/-90-day sensitivities. Assign every `N` to timed CT, recorded death before window end, continued MIMIC trace, or no later trace/unknown using fixed precedence and interval bounds for date-only death. Do not condition on later trace or call absence nonadherence. Temporal-placebo, generic non-chest imaging, acute-CT separation, and observation-state imbalance are falsification checks; failure makes this appendix inconclusive.

## Automatic verifier versus expert and external-study tasks

An automatic verifier can check phase-1 source hashes, headers, archive member, exact keys and fields; complete-row scan; cutoff-limited reciprocal graph; exclusion of known late addenda from boundary knowledge; artificial future-addendum fixtures; first-subject selection; sole-DS rule; deterministic `U_D*/A_S*/R*/F*/C_I*/C_O*` precedence; stale chronology tags; exact calendar arithmetic; common `dischtime` follow-up clock; unresolved extrema; bootstrap calculations; and whether phase-1 conclusions say “storage/export signal” rather than availability.

Given a frozen prospective calibration extract, the verifier can check sampling weights, `S` and `G` derivations from supplied fields, matrix cells, stratum support, condition number, constrained estimation, transport gates, linear-program bounds, sensitivity regions, and whether supportive/adverse/inconclusive language matches computed outputs. Adversarial fixtures must include a post-discharge addendum that cannot alter `N`; a DS row nominally stored pre-discharge whose later version differs; administrative discharge before and after physical departure; a delayed storetime with a UI-visible final artifact; missing departure truth; duplicate DS versions; and a deliberately unsupported availability or adherence conclusion that must fail verification.

Clinical/informatics experts must establish that the chosen artifact is canonical, the UI role and final status are meaningful, the direct sensor represents physical departure, and version lineage is complete. Radiology/transition reviewers must adjudicate incidental status, recommendation interval, alternate resolution, and departure-visible content. No automatic verifier can establish what a clinician or patient actually saw or understood, undocumented intent, communication, transmission, outside imaging, appropriateness, adherence, cancer outcome, or benefit. Those claims require additional direct measurement, expert review, and, for effects of an intervention, a randomized, stepped-wedge, or factorial prospective study with outside-system capture.

## Falsification criteria and stopping rules

The validation claim is falsified if its prespecified upper error bound exceeds 0.10, either predictive-value lower bound is below 0.80, or calibration is materially heterogeneous beyond the stated stratum tolerance. The availability-first decision is falsified if the calibrated/partially identified lower bound for `Delta_G` does not exceed +0.05. Content-first is falsified if the upper bound does not fall below -0.05. Calibration sign reversal, inability to synchronize clocks, incomplete version lineage, over 5% unresolved truth, nontransportable workflow, failure of reviewer agreement, matrix condition number at least 20, or insufficient events forces an inconclusive result. Future text or addenda changing a strict boundary state is a computation failure, not merely a sensitivity result.

The strongest claim available before phase 2 is therefore deliberately narrow: MIMIC can quantify a storage/export-timestamp and exported-text decomposition in a clinically important nodule-handoff denominator. The unresolved claim tested prospectively is whether that status identifies a final, retrievable artifact at physical departure and, after calibration, whether true unavailability or true departure-visible content failure is the larger measured process opportunity.
