# UKB 12: Expression of genetic risk for coronary heart disease across different lipoprotein backgrounds

Admission: feasibility_required. Verify participant overlap, measurement dates, missingness and event support before committing this design.

## Hypotheses to Be Tested

Among people with high coronary heart disease PRS, coronary heart disease risk still differs between those with low ApoB but higher inflammation and those with low ApoB and low inflammation.

## Study population and key data

Participants without coronary heart disease at baseline; standard CAD PRS, ApoB, Lp(a), CRP, and medication history.

## Basic Approach

Compare the joint risk of the prespecified PRS, ApoB, and inflammation; restrict the review to those not using lipid-lowering drugs; and test whether Lp(a) can explain the difference.

## Significance of the Research

Distinguish the lipid and non-lipid components of genetic risk.

## Main Challenges

A single lipid measurement cannot represent lifetime exposure; medications and prior risk affect current levels.

## Competing Explanations

Low ApoB results from treatment in high-risk individuals; residual risk is not due to an inflammatory pathway.

## Minimal validation and falsification criteria

First examine the distributions by medication use and medical history; if the corresponding pattern is absent in untreated participants, consider treatment selection preferentially.
