# Residual instability before ICU-to-ward transfer: endpoint-fixed falsification of trajectory-state discordance

Status: proposed substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. No outcome model has been fit, no late-era outcome has been opened, and no discordance result is claimed. This child preserves the frozen 49,157-transfer population, `t0`, strict final-24-hour information set, raw 48-hour endpoint, adjudicated first-transition extension, pooled and temporal transport analyses, source bindings, opening order, and primary continuous endpoint-state-by-direction question. It repairs the strongest remaining interpretation gap: the endpoint score `C` and direction `G` reuse the same late measurements, while the prior D1-versus-TCN comparison did not give the simple model a matched observation/treatment-process control.

## Scientific opening, evidence boundary, and substantive advance

The closest inspected evidence supports only bounded premises. A longitudinal MIMIC-IV/external-hospital model outperformed SWIFT for seven-day ICU readmission/death, but operational factors, DNR status, and the composite components remained unresolved [K1]. A systematic review found advantages for longitudinal models while populations, windows, endpoints, and methods varied widely [K2]. EHR measurement timing and frequency encode healthcare process as well as patient state [K3]. The parent's exact audit additionally supports cohort and measurement feasibility: 49,157 eligible transfers, 2,607 raw R+D events, and 46,935 transfers/2,277 events with at least four current and paired domains in a bounded extraction. None of this establishes trajectory gain, discordance, physiology, safe transfer, or monitoring benefit.

**Strongest supported claim.** The frozen data can compute endpoint state, recent direction, observation process, treatment history, raw/adjudicated outcomes, and locked temporal comparisons with adequate overall support. Literal absolute discordance extremes are asymmetric and sparse; the prior probe found only 72 concerning/improving transfers with 13 events.

**Unresolved claim tested.** Conditional on transfer-time endpoint-state risk, does observed recent measured-value worsening increase and improvement decrease 48-hour R+D risk, especially when endpoint state and direction oppose one another, beyond what is produced by endpoint reuse, regression to the mean, and recorded observation/treatment process?

**Leading explanation.** Recent measured-value history carries residual prognostic state information not represented by the endpoint snapshot.

**Strongest rival.** Because `C` contains final values and `G` compares late with earlier values, shared measurement error and regression to the mean can create an apparent `C×G` pattern even if temporal order is irrelevant. Clinician-triggered measurement, store lag, treatment starts/stops, transfer selection, and goals of care can create the same pattern. A TCN advantage does not isolate this rival because it changes representation and capacity while adding process features.

**Advance.** Add two smaller decisive comparisons before interpreting the TCN: (1) a nested recorded-process/treatment model at the same simple-model stage, and (2) endpoint-fixed within-person sham histories that preserve each patient's endpoint value, observation schedule, and multiset of prior values but destroy their temporal order. This directly asks whether the observed D1 correction exceeds the correction expected from endpoint coupling and recorded care process. It remains prognostic evidence, not mechanism.

**Clinical decision.** At `t0`, rank transfers for fixed-capacity ward/rapid-response review over 24/48 hours. Support would justify external and prospective evaluation of a discordance-aware review rule. Adverse evidence would favor endpoint/process models and discourage trajectory-based re-ranking. Neither result proves that review, delayed transfer, or ICU retention helps.

## Frozen endpoint state and direction

### Endpoint-state severity C

Fit `E0` as in the parent: a ridge multinomial endpoint model containing S0 static context and transfer-time measured endpoint state—age, sex, admission type, source/last ICU, destination, latest service, ICU LOS, prior ICU count, transfer clock, final valid HR/SBP/MAP/RR/SpO2/temperature/oxygen flow/FiO2/device value with four-hour freshness, final GCS components, current ventilation/vasopressor, and code-status state. It excludes historical counts, distinct hours, process changes, store-lag history, caregiver history, persistence, runs, transitions, volatility, slopes, early-late differences, and worst prior values. Development medians and missing flags are retained.

`C=logit(p_E0(R+D))` uses out-of-fold development predictions, frozen validation predictions, and untouched evaluation predictions. No in-sample development logits are allowed.

### Recent measured-value direction G

Use six fixed two-hour bins in `[t0-12h,t0)`. For HR, BP, RR, SpO2, temperature, and oxygen support, define the parent's fixed clinical abnormality margin, positive when worse, divided by the development IQR. BP uses the worse standardized SBP/MAP margin. Oxygen uses the maximum standardized FiO2, flow, and frozen device-ordinal margins; missing oxygen remains unknown.

For domain `j`, `G_j=median(margin)` in `[t0-6h,t0)` minus the median in `[t0-12h,t0-6h)`, requiring at least two observed bins in each half. `G=mean(G_j)` across at least four eligible domains. Positive is worsening and negative improving. All transforms and dictionaries freeze in development without outcomes.

The continuous primary uses restricted cubic splines for `C`, `G`, and their tensor interaction, knots at development 10th/50th/90th percentiles. Report
`I=[risk(C30,G70)-risk(C30,G30)]-[risk(C70,G70)-risk(C70,G30)]`,
plus direction contrasts at C30 and C70 and a predeclared 5×5 C/G grid.

Development-only communication cells remain:
- reassuring/worsening: `C<=q30(C), G>=q70(G)`;
- concerning/improving: `C>=q70(C), G<=q30(G)`;
- concordant extremes reverse direction;
- all middle rows remain in the continuous analysis.

No endpoint, direction, cell, or threshold changes are permitted after validation or test inspection.

## Simple baseline, process control, and endpoint-fixed sham

### E0: endpoint-only baseline

E0 answers whether endpoint state and static transfer context suffice. It is the common anchor for all simple comparisons.

### P1: recorded observation/treatment-process comparator

Fit `P1=E0+P` with ridge multinomial regression on the same raw three-state target and identical split/calibration protocol. `P` contains no measured physiologic values beyond E0. It includes, for each frozen domain and each six-hour half, observed-bin count, distinct chart hours, time since last observation, median and 90th-percentile nonnegative `storetime-charttime` lag, negative-lag flag, and distinct caregiver count; plus late-minus-early changes in those quantities. It also includes final-12-hour starts, stops, active duration, and transition counts for frozen vasopressor and ventilation items, derived only from interval rows available before `t0`; latest service/destination/clock remain in E0.

Fit `D1P=P1+spline(G)+C×G` with the same spline basis, penalty-selection grid, target, and calibration as D1. Comparisons have distinct meanings:

- `D1-E0`: primary prognostic value of direction beyond endpoint state;
- `P1-E0`: predictive value of recorded observation/treatment process;
- `D1P-P1`: measured-direction value conditional on the recorded process variables;
- `D1P-D1`: whether adding process improves or destabilizes the direction model.

P1 is a sensitivity comparator, not a causal adjustment set. Treatment and observation may mediate, respond to, or measure deterioration; conditioning on them changes the descriptive estimand. Therefore attenuation in D1P does not prove that the D1 signal is spurious, but it blocks the stronger claim that direction contributes independently of recorded process.

### EP-sham: endpoint-fixed within-person order falsification

Generate 20 outcome-blind permutations using seeds `2026092401` through `2026092420`. For each transfer and domain:

1. retain the exact E0 endpoint value, its timestamp/freshness, all masks, chart/store times, counts, caregiver identifiers, and treatment rows;
2. retain the multiset of observed abnormality margins in the six direction bins;
3. leave the latest observed direction-bin value fixed;
4. permute the remaining observed values among that same domain's occupied pre-latest bin positions; domains with fewer than four permutable observed bins remain unchanged and are flagged;
5. recompute `G_j^*`, `G^*`, C/G cells, and the complete D1 spline/cell design;
6. fit, tune, and calibrate a full `D1-sham_k=E0+spline(G^*)+C×G^*` using exactly the D1 pipeline, with each permutation carried consistently through development, validation, and locked tests.

This preserves endpoint reuse, individual value distribution, missingness pattern, observation density, store lag, and model capacity while destroying the actual pre-transfer order. It does not preserve every autocorrelation or cross-domain synchrony and is therefore a falsification control, not a generative null model.

Compute `Z_order` as the observed D1 gain or interaction minus the median of the 20 sham values, with the full sham range and rank. A result is order-specific only if the observed direction estimate has the prespecified sign and exceeds at least 19 of 20 shams in pooled test and combined locked late eras; both individual late-era point contrasts must favor observed order. Bootstrap uncertainty for observed-minus-sham uses paired subject resampling and the median-sham prediction per subject. Do not use permutation rank as a standalone p-value or ignore effect size.

Mandatory audit: report the fraction of rows/domains changed by each permutation, correlation of `G` with each `G^*`, C/G cell transitions, and whether endpoint values, masks, process features, and outcomes are byte-identical. If fewer than 70% of direction-eligible rows have at least four permutable observations in at least four domains, EP-sham cannot falsify endpoint coupling and this interpretive question is inconclusive; no alternate sham may be invented after outcomes are seen.

## Learned alternative and selection

Retain the parent's `L2-D` two-stream causal TCN on the same twelve two-hour bins, static E0 inputs, raw target, splits, five seeds, validation early stopping, and temperature scaling:

- value stream: standardized observed/forward-filled values, excluding masks/gaps;
- process stream: masks, deltas, counts, strict store-lag and caregiver features, excluding values;
- static stream: E0 context;
- mandatory full, value+static, process+static, and static-only heads;
- order permutation with final bin fixed and patient-value-sequence permutation within destination, E0-risk decile, and development density quintile.

L2-D asks whether nonlinear relapse, cross-channel ordering, or opposing organ directions add beyond D1P. It does not adjudicate the simple-model artifact by itself. Select L2-D only if calibrated, stable across seeds, and it beats D1P in both locked late eras while full/value evidence exceeds process-only and permutation controls. Otherwise prefer D1P or D1 according to the falsification results.

Deferred: a latent physiological/process model. Without independent sensors, staffing/policy labels, or transfer-intent adjudication, latent separation is not identifiable and could merely relabel care process. Revisit with an external site, continuous monitor feed, or observed policy change.

**Inconclusive.** Intervals include material benefit and harm; coverage, cell, or sham-permutability gates fail; shams are too correlated with G to destroy order; late-era estimates conflict; calibration fails; endpoint sensitivities disagree; or TCN seeds are unstable. An imprecise null is not refutation.
