## 3. Population, clocks, and chronology-safe superframes

For each offset `d in {12,24,48,72}` hours define the analytical cutoff `c_d=t_op-d`; `d=24` is confirmatory. It is an analytical anchor, not an observed decision, report-release, or report-view time. A validated nonmidnight timestamp is a point. Date-only and unvalidated midnight values are half-open civil-day intervals. Invalid, missing, competing, adjacent-day, and discordant values create explicit alternatives. All temporal predicates in one state must share a feasible integer-second witness; independent pairwise choices may not be spliced.

For every patient, reconstruct and order all source-supported candidate procedure episodes before calendar filtering. In a complete state, include the patient only if:

1. age is at least 18 in the selected encounter;
2. the selected episode is the earliest eligible source-documented hepatobiliary procedure episode under the frozen dictionary;
3. the all-row same-encounter pathology composite explicitly supports HCC;
4. no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy is found patient-wide in `[c_d-365d,c_d)`.

A procedure row supports only a source-documented procedure entry. “No recorded prior treatment” is not biological treatment-naivety. A nonmember state contributes neither numerator nor denominator and is never coded `Y=0`.

Roles are 2015–2018 development, 2019 tuning/lock checking, separate 2020 and 2021 tests, and 2022+ audit only. Before fitting, compute each patient's complete set of reachable roles from the outer envelope. The possible-test superframe contains every patient reachable in 2020 or 2021 and also retains states placing that patient after 2021 or outside the cohort; none may enter development or tuning. Within one state a patient occupies at most one role and at most one test year. A 2020/2021-ambiguous patient remains one coupled alternative and cannot appear twice. This priority is a leakage safeguard, not a claim about the true date.

## 4. Pre-outcome ambiguity ledger and certified envelopes

Before opening pathology grade, report semantics, model losses, period counts, or test outcomes, create immutable `ambiguity_ledger.parquet`. Every exact-deduplicated source row that can affect episode construction, eligibility, role, clock breakpoint, prior treatment, examination unit, modality, selected report, pathology composite, outcome domain, or denominator receives:

- patient and encounter keys and examination key where present;
- every required raw field, normalized field, parsed value or explicit parse failure;
- every dictionary/rubric rule consuming it;
- duplicate group, join multiplicity, candidate partitions, and sealed raw backpointer.

Two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician freeze a high-sensitivity procedure/prior-treatment dictionary before outcomes. A label enters the outer query set if either surgeon or the frozen high-sensitivity rule considers it possibly relevant. Outer exclusion requires unanimous agreement and an explicit label-grounded reason. Residual source ambiguity remains a state alternative, semantic uncertainty becomes an explicit `uncertain` category, and unresolved M2 becomes `{0,1}`. For patient `i`, `Omega_i^+` is the outer envelope generated from every query-set procedure value, allowed episode partition, feasible clock cell and boundary, treatment alternative, whole-accession partition/modality, encounter-matched pathology composite, membership/nonmembership alternative, and allowed outcome completion. `Omega_i^*` is the nested adjudicated envelope after clinically impossible alternatives are removed with signed reason, reviewer identity, and provenance; therefore `Omega_i^* subseteq Omega_i^+`. The primary claim uses `Omega^+`; `Omega^*` is secondary. Support only in `Omega^*` is “adjudication-sensitive/inconclusive,” never source-robust.

State generation is a finite DAG. Every ledger atom must reach exactly one of: an emitted raw outer state, a documented deterministic outer impossibility, or an adjudicated-only exclusion that remains reinstated in `Omega_i^+`. The certificate stores forward reachability, reverse state-to-atom provenance, all branch counts and reasons, patient state cardinalities as arbitrary-precision integers, minimum/maximum year denominators, and hashes of source, parser, dictionaries, review forms, raw states, quotient states, solver matrices, bootstrap weights, and results.

Any unreachable atom, unclassified branch, empty required patient domain, unexplained count mismatch, truncation, or missing state makes the primary result inconclusive.

## 7. One exact robust fit per model

Use additive elastic-net logistic regression with unpenalized intercept, no interactions, splines, or free text. One-hot levels and reference categories are frozen. The grid is `alpha in {0,.25,.5,.75,1}` and 100 log-spaced lambdas from development `lambda_max` to `10^-4 lambda_max`. Ties prefer larger penalty, fewer nonzero coefficients, smaller alpha, then lexical serialization.

To avoid a hidden state-specific transform, freeze one pre-outcome outer-development transform. Each possible development patient contributes total weight one divided equally across its distinct eligible fitting-quotient states. Numeric means/scales and categorical development frequencies are computed from that patient-balanced multistate matrix; zero-variance removal is logged. This transform is used unchanged in every fit, state, year, and bootstrap. The `Z,S` columns, levels, scaling, missing codes, and order are byte-identical for `R` and `G`.

For model `M` and candidate hyperparameter `h`, compute one canonical robust development fit:

`beta_M(h) = argmin_beta [ max_{q in Q_D^+, n_D(q)>0} {sum_i a_i^D(s_i) ell(y_i(s_i), beta; phi_M(s_i))/n_D(q)} + lambda_h P_alpha(beta) ]`.

The state choice jointly fixes membership, encounter, clock, features, and outcome; a patient's loss and membership cannot come from different states. `B` uses `Z`; `R` uses `Z,S`; `G` uses `Z,S,X`. For each model independently, choose its hyperparameter by maximum exact worst-world 2019 fixed-K capture using only 2019 states and the frozen tie order. Candidate-grid fits are tuning internals; the published scoring object for each model is one coefficient/intercept/preprocessing hash. If the convex objective has multiple minimizers, choose among exact minimizers by minimum coefficient L2 norm and then lexical coefficient order; failure to certify this canonical choice is inconclusive.

The normalized worst-world loss is separated exactly. For trial `c`, maximize `sum_i a_i(s_i)[ell_i(s_i)-c]` over one state per patient; Dinkelbach iteration finds the maximum ratio. A cutting-plane epigraph master alternates convex robust fits and exact separation until primal/dual and separation gaps are each `<=1e-8`. Exhaustive enumeration must agree through 12-patient fixtures and all real-data shards through 20 patients. Local optima, approximate state sampling, a nonzero gap above tolerance, timeout, dropped patients, or unresolved separation are inconclusive.

`Gmask` is never fit, tuned, or recalibrated. Apply the one fitted `G` after replacing every `X` category—including not-mentioned and uncertain—with its training-defined source-text-unavailable level. Keep every `Z,S` value, transform, coefficient, and intercept unchanged.

## 8. Fixed-K estimand and exact state-coupled extrema

For state `q`, offset `d`, year `a`, and `r in {.05,.10,.20}`, let `N_a(q,d)` be eligible size before pathology grade, report semantics, or test outcome is consulted. Freeze exactly

`N_ref = min_{q in Q^+, d in {12,24,48,72}, a in 2015..2019} N_a(q,d)`,
`K_r=floor(r N_ref)`.

The confirmatory capacity is `r=.10`; require `N_ref>=500`, `K_.10>=50`, and at least `K_.10` eligible patients in every required 2020 and 2021 outer state. The .05/.20 capacities are sensitivities only when `K_r>=50`; they cannot redefine or rescue the primary capacity.

Within a state, rank eligible whole patients by the locked score, SHA-256 patient tie key, then raw episode backpointer, and select exactly K. For `M in {B,R,G,Gmask}`,

`T_M(q,a)=100/K * sum_{i in TopK_M(q,a)} y_i(q)`.

The two co-primary contrasts are:

- `Delta_nested(q,a)=T_G(q,a)-T_R(q,a)`;
- `Delta_mask(q,a)=T_G(q,a)-T_Gmask(q,a)`.

`Delta_surface=T_R-T_B` is diagnostic and cannot supply or veto semantic support.

For each `c in {nested,mask}` and `a in {2020,2021}`, solve exactly:

`D^-_{c,a}=min_{q in Q_a^+} Delta_c(q,a)`,
`D^+_{c,a}=max_{q in Q_a^+} Delta_c(q,a)`.

Each mixed-integer solve chooses exactly one state per test-superframe patient, one shared outcome completion, and exactly K top-ranked patients under each model. Rank-order constraints enforce the complete score/tie-key order; selected outcomes are linearized without allowing model-specific outcome choices. Nonmembers contribute neither numerator nor candidate slots. The solver returns a certified optimum, a raw-expandable state assignment, both top-K patient-set hashes, and an optimizer-witness SHA-256 containing source, model, state, year, contrast, K, and objective. State ordering permutations and exhaustive fixtures through 12 patients must reproduce both endpoints exactly. A gap above `1e-8`, infeasibility, fewer than K, or replay failure is inconclusive.

The adjudicated-envelope extrema are computed separately and cannot replace outer endpoints.

## 9. Bootstrap inference on eight extrema and semantic falsification

Use 2,000 deterministic paired whole-patient bootstrap replicates stratified by the frozen reachable-role signature. A sampled patient carries all rows, all outer/adjudicated states, and one common multiplicity across models, contrasts, and years; clones receive deterministic tie suffixes and share the chosen state/outcome. Every replicate rebuilds the patient-balanced transform, robustly refits/tunes one `B`, `R`, and `G`, derives no-refit `Gmask`, and exactly recomputes all eight outer extrema.

Let `E` contain the four minima and four maxima. Using the bootstrap standard deviation `s_e`, form one-sided simultaneous 97.5% max-T bounds over all eight endpoints:

`L_e=Ehat_e-q_.975[max_e((Ehat_e-E*_be)/s_e)] s_e`,
`U_e=Ehat_e+q_.975[max_e((E*_be-Ehat_e)/s_e)] s_e`.

Require all 2,000 replicates, finite nonzero `s_e` unless an exact finite-state proof supplies the endpoint, `L_e<=Ehat_e<=U_e`, and order consistency for minimum/maximum bounds. No failed replicate is dropped or replaced; any bootstrap fit, separation, extremum, state, or replay failure makes the family inconclusive. Report percentile bounds as a diagnostic for extrema nonregularity; a prespecified material max-T/percentile conflict is inconclusive rather than selectively resolved.

Run 1,000 development/tuning-only semantic-null permutations. Move each patient's complete `X` state collection, never individual rows or states, within frozen strata of reachable-role signature, modality-pattern support, unit cardinality, identity ambiguity, state cardinality, and deciles of `S`. Keep `Z,S`, outcomes, and source rows fixed; robustly refit and retune. Compute `H=min_{c,a}(D^-_{c,a}-5)`. The p-value is `(1+count(H_perm>=H_obs))/1001`; universal support requires `p<=.025`. Any failed permutation is a gate failure. This tests semantic alignment conditional on the frozen surface/state structure; it does not validate report concepts against images.

## 10. Existence, universality, and mutually exclusive conclusions

All predicates use the same valid eight-endpoint simultaneous family and margin `m=5`. For each contrast-year coordinate define:

- **universal support** `U_ca` iff `L(D^-_ca)>5`: every outer state clears the margin;
- **existence support** `E_ca` iff `L(D^+_ca)>5`: at least one outer state clears the margin;
- **adverse counterexample** `F_ca` iff `U(D^-_ca)<=5`: at least one outer state does not clear the strict margin;
- **universal adverse** `A_ca` iff `U(D^+_ca)<=5`: no outer state clears the margin.

Equality is adverse because the hypothesis is strictly greater than 5. `U_ca` implies `E_ca`; `A_ca` implies `F_ca`. An `E_ca & F_ca` coordinate is certified threshold heterogeneity across admissible source states. These are state-reconstruction statements, not biological effect modification.

For a single contrast-year, assign exactly one label:

1. `contrast-year-supportive` if `U_ca`;
2. `contrast-year-heterogeneous` if `E_ca & F_ca`;
3. `contrast-year-adverse-without-existence-support` if `F_ca & !E_ca`;
4. `contrast-year-inconclusive` otherwise.

For each year, “supportive” requires universal support for both contrasts; otherwise aggregate existence and counterexample predicates across its two contrasts using the same heterogeneous/adverse/inconclusive order.

After all gates, assign exactly one global label:

1. **Universal-supportive:** all four `U_ca` and permutation `p<=.025`.
2. **Decisive-threshold-heterogeneity:** not universal-supportive, at least one `E_ca`, and at least one `F_ca`, whether across state, contrast, or year.
3. **Universal-adverse-without-certified-positive-coordinate:** at least one `F_ca` and no `E_ca`. Report the stronger “no outer state clears the margin” sublabel when every required coordinate has `A_ca`.
4. **Inconclusive:** no `F_ca` but universal support is not established; all four `U_ca` with valid `p>.025`; or any required gate failure.

The result contains a one-hot four-label vector. A valid adverse or heterogeneous result is not penalized for contradicting the hypothesis. One supportive year and one uncertain year is inconclusive; supportive/adverse across years or nested/mask discordance is heterogeneity. Years and contrasts are never averaged and cannot compensate for each other.

`Delta_surface`, offset/capacity sensitivities, the adjudicated envelope, and hypothetical access cells use separate simultaneous families and cannot change the primary label. Surface-positive means documentation process may matter and raises transportability concern; it neither proves nor refutes semantic contribution.
