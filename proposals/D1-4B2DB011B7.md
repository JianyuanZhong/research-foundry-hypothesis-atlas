## 1. Targeted successor and falsifiable question

This is a targeted successor to assessed-but-repairable `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that candidate's adult first eligible resection population, fixed-capacity retrospective ranking estimand, 24-hour primary cutoff, 12/24/48/72-hour clock stress tests, whole-accession report units, reader/pathology envelope, 80/80 stored-content mask, locked comparator-closed models, and whole-patient deletion check.

The repair addresses one scientific defect only: the prior next-step taxonomy was not disjoint. Its rules could label the same result as primary-pass/offset-fail, visibility-fail, clock-fail, and pathology-fail, and its "maximum-content" branch did not state which uncertainty worlds it quantified. Here the uncertainty dimensions are explicitly factorized, their projections are defined, and one precedence-ordered label is emitted for every run.

The bounded clinical question is:

> Among adults at a first eligible HCC liver resection, can a locked semantic score from routine preoperative CT/MRI report content rank more same-encounter pathology-linked documentary-M2 cases in a fixed number of review positions than a structured acquisition baseline, a report-surface/availability model, and the same semantic model with report-derived inputs masked?

The action represented by the score is allocation of a scarce hypothetical preoperative review position. It is not a recommendation for resection extent, transplantation, systemic or locoregional therapy, surveillance, or any treatment.

The primary hypothesis, evaluated in 2020 and 2021 separately at the 24-hour cutoff, is that the semantic ranking G has a greater fixed-K documentary-M2 capture than each comparator by more than 5 cases per 100 ranking positions, and has positive fixed-K enrichment over random allocation, in every admissible source-consistent state. The stress hypothesis asks whether that statement remains true at all four prespecified offsets and after the stored-content and whole-patient deletion checks.

A falsifier is exact: any required state with
`100(h_G-h_C)/K <= 5` for a comparator C in {B, R_clean, G_mask}, or with `N h_G <= K E` for random allocation, falsifies the corresponding universal predicate. Equality is failure. No average over states, favorable reader choice, or nominal confidence interval can rescue it.

## 2. Evidence and advance

Before this experiment, the strongest claim supported by the HCC snapshot is documentary and computational: it contains linked encounter/procedure/examination/pathology records from which one can construct a retrospective, outcome-blind comparison of locked rankings over stored narrative units. It does not establish that a radiology report was finalized, released, readable, or viewed before surgery; it does not identify a pathology specimen, block, slide, sign-out time, or sampling adequacy; it does not contain images, biological MVI adjudication, recurrence, survival, clinician action, review capacity, workload, harm, cost, or treatment benefit.

The available current literature is consistent with the need for this distinction. Liu et al. (2026, PMCID PMC13037511, acquired source `[source checksum]`) reports a retrospective two-center DCE-MRI radiomics study of 306 resected HCC patients, with an external-center AUC of 0.723 for a combined VETC/MVI pattern endpoint and potential utility by calibration/DCA. That article uses MRI images and a pathological vascular-pattern reference unavailable in this HCC island; it therefore motivates external validation but does not establish this report-semantic endpoint. The research-ambition demonstrations likewise motivate rigorous longitudinal and uncertainty-aware work, but are not evidence for HCC documentary M2. The supplied Cell cancer demonstration's main paper and STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not cited as inspected evidence.

The substantive advance is a clinically interpretable evidence gate. A robust stored-content result can justify a narrowly specified archive-and-clock audit; a failure is assigned to the first uncertainty axis that can explain it; and no result authorizes silent mode or patient-care use. Thus the experiment informs whether to spend resources resolving report timing, operation chronology, pathology linkage, or nothing at all, rather than conflating these claims.

## 6. Factorized uncertainty states

Construct a fixed union roster before evaluating scores. The state is a product
`z=(C,P,V,D)), with shared source-byte and parser lineage:

1. C, the chronology axis, contains offset d, operation-clock interpretation, operation membership, year, and prior-treatment window interpretation. C states obey temporal ordering and source-implied impossibility constraints.
2. P, the endpoint axis, contains the whole pathology form, HCC membership, documentary-M2 terminal, pathology reader book, and radiology-by-pathology pair. P states never mix a radiology book with a pathology terminal from another book and never select a favorable row.
3. V, the stored-content axis, contains full eventual-content exposure and the nested 80/80 patient-balanced exposure masks. V is counterfactual exposure of exact eventual stored whole-accession inputs, not observed historical release/view.
4. D is deletion: the undeleted roster plus every required whole-patient singleton deletion. Removing patient i removes every eligible unit, state, score, outcome, and accession attached to i; it never requalifies the survivor roster and never changes fits, K, books, ties, or state definitions.

The axes are orthogonal for reporting, but the primary calculation evaluates their admissible Cartesian product after removing impossible combinations. A state registry records the exact reason for every removed combination. Full-content means V exposes every eligible nonblank accession; 80/80 means for patient i with n_i units and m_i exposed units, `m_i >= ceil(4n_i/5)`, with `5 sum_i 1[n_i>0 and m_i>=ceil(4n_i/5)] >= 4N` on the full roster before deletion. Blank, nonreversible, contaminated, and source-unavailable units are forced zero. Content masks never change B/Z.

For each z, calculate binary roster size N_z, documentary-M2 count E_z, and exact top-K captures h_M,z for G, B, R_clean, and G_mask. Store f_M,z=K-h_M,z, integer witnesses, source-state hash, and raw backpointers. Report:
`Delta_C(y,d)=min_z 100(h_G-h_C)/K`,
`epsilon(y,d)=min_z (N_z h_G-K E_z)/(K N_z)`,
`p_safe=min_z h_G/K`, and
`B_max=max_z (K-h_G)/h_G`, with +infinity when h_G=0.
These are finite-corpus ranking quantities, not calibrated probabilities or biological risks.

For attribution, use the same locked G fit for G versus G_mask; the independently fitted B and R_clean comparisons remain comparator checks. Do not attach ordinary bootstrap coverage to a min/max/rank pipeline; any bootstrap or permutation is descriptive only.

## 7. Exact one-hot result contract

Define the following predicates from stored exact outputs. “Pass” always means every state in the named universal set passes both the semantic-vs-comparator margin and random-enrichment inequality, with the required roster and grade gates.

- Q: computation, replay, parser losslessness, leakage, fit sealing, tie, state registry, mask, deletion, witness, and arithmetic checks pass.
- A: every required state has N>=K+1, a qualifying mask, grade fraction >=.85, and at least 50 IN1 cases.
- M: in every full-content state (V=full), across all C and P states and all deletions D, G beats each comparator by >5/100 and has positive random enrichment.
- V80: in every qualifying 80/80 state, across all C and P states and all deletions, the same predicates pass.
- C: in every full-content state, across all P and D, the predicates pass for every offset and clock interpretation; the 24-hour primary is separately reported.
- P: in every full-content state, across all C and D, every reader/pathology book-pair state passes.
- D: all required whole-patient singleton deletions pass under the relevant full-content and 80/80 gates.

Emit exactly one label by this precedence, evaluated after all exact outputs are available:

1. `computationally_inconclusive` if Q=false.
2. `patient_sufficiency_infeasible` if Q=true and A=false.
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse` if Q,A=true and M=false. This is an attained stored-content falsifier; do not authorize archive validation of G.
4. `visibility_brittle_signal_adverse` if M=true and V80=false. The stored-content result depends on a counterfactual mask; authorize only a report archive/access audit.
5. `clock_bridge_adverse` if M,V80=true and C=false. Authorize only a validated operation-clock/chronology audit. Because C quantifies all offsets and clock alternatives, there is no separate overlapping “primary-pass but offset-fail” label.
6. `pathology_reader_bridge_adverse` if M,V80,C=true and P=false. Authorize only pathology specimen/linkage, sampling, and reader-adjudication evidence.
7. `deletion_fragile_signal_adverse` if M,V80,C,P=true and D=false. The finite-corpus signal is concentrated in one or more patients; authorize composition/independent-cohort work, not a clinical conclusion.
8. `threshold_independent_signal_supportive_frontier_estimable` if Q,A,M,V80,C,P,D are all true in both test years. This authorizes one bounded external archive-and-clock audit and nothing clinical.

If a label's corresponding witness is absent, or a branch has a non-lossless parser, omitted state, changed survivor set, changed fit, or timeout, Q is false and the label is computationally inconclusive. The precedence is part of the estimand: simultaneous failures are reported in an auxiliary failure ledger, but the primary conclusion is only the one label above. Therefore labels are exhaustive and mutually exclusive.

The 24-hour result, all-offset result, every axis minimum, and the first failing witness are still printed. “Supportive” means only a robust finite-corpus stored-content ranking frontier and permission to perform the specified archive audit. “Adverse” means a falsifier or dependence on an identified unresolved evidence axis; it does not prove absence of biological association. “Inconclusive” means no scientific direction.
