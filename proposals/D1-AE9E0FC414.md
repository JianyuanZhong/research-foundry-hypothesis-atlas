The inspected full Europe PMC XML of Kuriyama et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13476641`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100120`, frozen source `[source checksum]`, retrieved [source checksum]) reports 26 randomized trials and 1,652 participants. It found mostly low- or very-low-certainty evidence, with clinically heterogeneous comparisons largely in heart-failure and post-cardiovascular-surgery populations. It does not answer initiation/restart versus avoidance in this post-shock ventilated phenotype. The inspected full XML of Orieux et al. 2026 (PMCID `PMC13218125`, DOI `10.1016/j.aicoj.2026.100083`, frozen source `[source checksum]`, retrieved [source checksum]) explicitly treats ICU renal congestion as hypothesis-generating and states that interventional evidence and clinical utility remain unestablished. These papers support uncertainty and plausibility only; they do not support benefit, safety, the five-point margin, or causality. The three research-ambition demonstrations were inspected only through their availability README; none is used as clinical evidence, and no unavailable cancer-paper body or STAR Methods is claimed as read.

## Remaining clinical measurement defect and smallest repair

The corrected Episode 73 audit fixes null boundaries and stale provenance, but it leaves a clinically important distinction implicit. The primary exposure uses the clinical `starttime` of an accepted `icu/inputevents` row, while `storetime` is not a gate. Official MIMIC documentation (read from the frozen public source, not inferred from the rows) says that `amount/amountuom` and `rate/rateuom` describe drug delivery over the clinical interval and that `storetime` is when the observation was manually entered or validated. Therefore a loop start can be a valid recorded-delivery row yet have been entered after the delivery began. It is suitable for a retrospective recorded-policy contrast, but it cannot automatically be relabeled as a decision observable at the bedside.

The documentation was acquired/read at source id `[source checksum]`, URL `https://mimic.mit.edu/docs/iv/modules/icu/inputevents.html`, source [source checksum]. It supports the field semantics only; it does not establish receipt fidelity, clinician intent, or the absence of unrecorded administrations.

The minimal repair is not to change membership of the frozen primary `E_R` population, the clinical A12/B12 clone assignment, `V0`, `C0`, outcomes, or estimand. It adds an independently rebuilt `E_DA` decision-observable audit and makes it a required gate for any language about a bedside initiation/avoidance decision:

- Retain the exact `E_R` eligibility and primary clinical-time arm: the first repaired qualifying onset `s_A` in `(t0,C0)`, or no such onset through `C0` for B12. Keep equal A12/B12 clones and all existing pause/link/ambiguity rules.
- For A12, set `DA_A=1` only if the onset row has nonmissing, parseable `storetime` and `storetime <= s_A`. If the onset is formed from a linked/touching segment sequence, every row needed to establish that first onset must satisfy the same condition; a missing or late store is `DA_A=0`, not a repaired start.
- For B12, set `DA_B=1` only if every valid loop row with clinical `starttime <= C0` that could contradict avoidance has parseable `storetime <= C0`; any such row stored after `C0` makes the B clone decision-availability unknown. Rows with clinical onset after `C0` do not invalidate B12.
- Do not use `storetime` to move clinical onset, reopen a later stay, define `V0`/`C0`, or alter the primary `E_R` arm. Report `E_R` and `E_DA` counts, late/missing-store reasons, and their subject-level overlap. Refit folds, histories, predictors, weights, endpoint bounds, and all bootstrap draws from `E_DA` if its feasibility gate passes.
- `E_DA` is feasible only with the inherited minima (N>=200, >=100 A12 satisfiers, >=100 B12 completions/shared closures, ESS>=75 per arm, >=100 definite H120 successes and failures, weighted unknown <=0.10 per arm, absolute arm unknown difference <=0.05, and all temporal/positivity/calibration/balance/ambiguity/contamination gates). This is a measurement/interpretation repair, not treatment-effect estimation. The substantive advance is an answer to a clinically consequential prerequisite: whether the proposed recorded policy is actually reconstructable at the time a clinician would have acted. It prevents a late chart entry from being treated as contemporaneous knowledge while preserving the selected scientific schema.
