# Evolution (episode 37): Does the resolution-like macrophage signal require a permissive FLS recipient?
Domain: disease_mechanisms. Operation: evolve. Parent (assigned by Lead): [prior hypothesis] (valid, board leader, mu 34.07). Seed axis D3-M2 (with D3-M1/D3-M3 anchors).

## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
Alivernini 2020 (cited in the parent lineage; NOT directly inspected this episode) associated MerTK+CD206+ synovial macrophages with drug-free RA remission. The parent established a composition-vs-within-cell decomposition (E-MTAB-8322) and an in-vitro MerTK-perturbation test (E-MTAB-8316, UNC106238). It left open the recipient side: is the Mpos effect a fixed macrophage-intrinsic program, or does it require a permissive/responding FLS state? K1 shows fibroblast-defined matrix niches gate resolution; K2 proposes a reciprocal stromal-myeloid "failed-resolution" circuit; K3 shows fibroblast state change can itself shift macrophage polarization in coculture. If resolution requires a permissive recipient, a macrophage-only therapeutic readout would mis-stratify patients. This is separable experimentally by an Mpos x FLS-donor interaction.

## 2. Falsifiable hypothesis
H-fixed: Mpos macrophages produce a donor-independent effect on the FLS inflammatory/matrix transcript program (significant Mpos main effect, no significant Mpos x FLS-donor interaction).
H-permissive (rival): the Mpos effect is conditional on FLS-donor identity (significant interaction with responder/non-responder FLS donors).
The experiment tests H-fixed against H-permissive.

## 4. Design
- Population: E-MTAB-8873 coculture samples with a defined FLS donor and Mpos or Mneg macrophage stimulus, treatment=none as primary; LPS and MerTK-inhibitor arms as secondary contrasts. FLS-only samples are the unstimulated baseline. Mixed pos/neg samples excluded from the primary contrast.
- Temporal boundaries: not applicable (in-vitro, no longitudinal time index); groups are cross-sectional culture conditions, not disease transitions.
- Variables: response = per-sample FLS effector program score; predictors = macrophage state (Mpos vs Mneg), FLS donor identity, macrophage donor identity, log(total cells/umi proxy), treatment.
- Baseline (simple): pooled Mpos-vs-Mneg group-mean difference on the FLS program score (and per-gene).
- Substantive alternative (donor-aware): mixed model with FLS-donor and macrophage-donor random intercepts, log-depth covariate, plus low-rank factor adjustment for residual composition; interaction term Mpos x FLS-donor; leave-one-FLS-donor-out; donor bootstrap CI and label-permutation null. The alternative separates a shared NAC program from a recipient-dependent one; the baseline cannot.
- Outcomes: supportive of H-fixed = significant Mpos main effect, interaction CI covering 0 in most leave-one-out fits, consistent sign across FLS donors; supportive of H-permissive = significant interaction and non-overlapping responder/non-responder effect CIs; inconclusive = wide CIs spanning both models or unstable leave-one-out signs.

## 5. Falsification and interpretation
- Falsifies H-fixed if the Mpos effect reverses or vanishes in >=2 of the FLS donors' leave-one-out fits and the interaction CI excludes 0.
- Falsifies H-permissive if the interaction is consistently null and per-donor effects share sign and rough magnitude.
- A supportive-fixed result establishes an association in coculture, not tissue-context causality; a permissive result establishes recipient-dependence in vitro, not that clinical remission requires the FLS state. Both require expert review of Mpos purity/MerTK-inhibitor selectivity and a tissue-context test.

## 6. Method comparison rationale
Exact inputs and fitting target are as above; CPU-only fit is adequate (<=33 samples, 445 features; seconds to minutes). Baseline loses: it pools FLS donors and cannot tell a shared macrophage program from a recipient-gated one, and cannot detect donor-attributable variance. The donor-aware alternative with an explicit interaction is scientifically necessary because the central question IS the interaction. Not chosen: a deep sequence model - no sequence data here and no added mechanistic discrimination beyond the interaction estimand.

## 7. Method/claim scope
Claim stages: hypothesis stage; observational/in-vitro conditional-association design. No result is claimed as executed - the interaction fit, leave-one-out, bootstrap and permutation null are the solver deliverable. No causal, transition, or clinical-stratification claim is made.
