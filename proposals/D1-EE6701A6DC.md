# Patient-composition breakdown frontier for fixed-quota documentary-M2 review

## 1. One targeted weakness and the decision

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that candidate's clinical estimand and all of its primary population, clocks, source worlds, temporal quarantine, endpoint, reader books, models, masks, quota, comparator closure, exact extrema, and anchored whole-patient singleton deletion.

Exactly one remaining weakness is repaired: the parent reports an attained finite-corpus worst-state (p_safe/B_max) frontier, but does not quantify whether the result is carried by a small number of patients. Thus a passing stored-content result can be mathematically exact yet composition-fragile, leaving no prespecified rule for deciding whether it is stable enough to justify the next evidence step.

The next evidence step is deliberately narrow: an external, accession-level report-version/release/view audit, followed only if that audit supplies the missing operational evidence by a later prospective silent-mode review study. This experiment does not authorize triage, treatment, resection, transplantation, surveillance, or clinical deployment.

The single repaired decision question is:

> In each test year, after the parent’s source/reader/mask/deletion envelope is applied, how many coherent whole-patient deletions are required before locked semantic G loses its threshold-independent equal-capacity advantage or its stored-content frontier, and does that breakdown number meet an outcome-blind stability requirement set before outcome inspection?

A stable result means only that the retrospective stored-content ranking is composition-stable under the declared finite-patient stress. It does not establish report visibility, biological M2, workflow feasibility, benefit, or transport.

### Untested claim

The untested claim is not “G detects biological high-grade MVI.” It is:

1. G’s strict (>5)-per-100 fixed-position advantage over B, R, and same-fit Gmask, plus its random-(K) enrichment, persists under every coherent deletion set up to a predeclared patient-composition tolerance; and
2. the resulting retrospective signal is therefore sufficiently stable to justify the specified external report-version/release/view audit.

The first claim is computable in HCC. The second is a conditional evidence-governance decision and is permitted only when an outcome-blind stability contract exists. Neither claim establishes clinical utility or transport.

## 4. Stability contract and next-evidence decision

Before reading test outcomes, create `stability_requirement.json`. It must contain:

- the exact action being evaluated: allocation of (K) retrospective review positions before first eligible resection;
- a minimum deletion tolerance (q_{min,y}), specified as an integer or rational fraction of the union-ledger patients, separately for 2020 and 2021 or explicitly common;
- whether the contract requires the comparator and random breakdown frontiers, or also a supplied (p_U=a/b) frontier;
- panel roles, date, method, disagreement resolution and sign-off;
- hashes of the fixed proposal, population/mask/reader/model artifacts and contract.

The contract cannot be populated from HCC outcomes, p-values, (p_{safe}), (B_{max}), or a post hoc deletion count. HCC cannot validate that the tolerance is clinically reasonable.

The readiness rule is:

- `external_audit_eligible` only if the parent quality/availability gates pass, the parent’s threshold-independent primary passes in both test years, and every required exact breakdown count is at least the contracted (q_{min,y}) (strictly greater than or equal to it), with no solver, lineage, or witness failure.
- `composition_fragile_external_step_not_supported` if the parent primary passes but any contracted breakdown requirement is falsified by an attained deletion witness.
- `stability_bridge_pending` if no valid pre-outcome contract is supplied. The exact breakdown curve is still reported, but cannot be converted into “stable enough.”
- Any parent adverse or inconclusive label takes precedence; a stability result cannot rescue it.

If eligible, the one authorized next step is a protected external accession-level report audit. It must enumerate the same first-eligible HCC-resection frame at an external site or independently held institutional extract and, keyed by protected `检查号`, capture report/version identifiers, predecessor links and hashes, authored/final/release/amendment/retraction/ingestion/readability times, the actual 24-hour visibility indicator, the validated operation clock, and the denominator of eligible accessions. It must preserve unavailable/missing as unknown and not impute visibility from acquisition or eventual text. It should be blinded to G scores and outcomes until the audit specification is frozen. This audit establishes operational availability evidence only; it does not establish biological M2, benefit, workload acceptability, or clinical action.

A later prospective silent-mode study requires that audit, external pathology adjudication with specimen linkage/sampling review, a locked report-visibility rule, a prespecified review roster and workload measure, and independent outcome capture. The current HCC experiment cannot substitute for those requirements.

## 7. Exact computation of the new frontier

Compile a finite domain with patient IDs, membership/year/Y/Z/S/Q/X, whole-unit hashes and backpointers, source/form/terminal hashes, sealed scores/ties, mask bits, the parent anchored deletion, and every allowed whole-patient deletion indicator. Patient identifiers are internal protected keys and never appear in public queries or published outputs.

For every year, source world, reader pair, full-roster mask, required anchored deletion, and deletion budget (qle Q_y):

1. remove D across all rows and states;
2. retain the already qualified full-roster mask bits and locked artifacts;
3. apply all parent sufficiency gates;
4. compute exact top-K lists for G, B, R and Gmask with shared comparator state, ties and K;
5. emit (N,E,h,f,delta,epsilon,p_{safe},B_{max}) and the integer predicates;
6. solve for the minimum (|D|) producing each breakdown predicate, with a certified exact MILP/branch-and-bound or an exhaustively equivalent finite solver.

The optimizer must jointly couple a deletion indicator to patient membership, units, all states, all masks and all comparator arms. It may use exact quotienting only with bidirectional raw-state reachability and lossless replay. It must not use sampled deletion sets, greedy influence rankings, a continuous relaxation, a bootstrap quantile, or a different fit after deletion.

Replay the returned minimum witnesses and all parent frontier landmarks using exact integer arithmetic. Required fixtures include: (D=arnothing); a one-patient comparator witness; ties at K; deletion of a positive in/out of top K; zero-unit and forced-zero patients; (h_G=0); (b h_G=aK); (N h_G=K E); a frontier that survives and one that breaks; and a contract with (q_{min}=0). Synthetic brute force must agree through 12 patients and real-shard exact replay through 20 patients. Timeout, sampling, omitted states, invalid survivor masks, changed models, incomplete witnesses, rational loss or replay failure makes the computation inconclusive.

## 8. Results and falsification

Emit the parent’s one-hot computational label unchanged:

1. `computationally_inconclusive` if any parent or new exact gate fails;
2. `patient_sufficiency_infeasible` if the parent’s A gate fails;
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse` if all-exposable content fails the comparator or random criterion;
4. `visibility_brittle_signal_adverse` if all-exposable content passes but a qualifying 80/80 mask fails;
5. `threshold_independent_signal_supportive_frontier_estimable` if Q/A pass and no comparator/random witness exists.

Add a separate, disjoint `composition_status`:

- `composition_fragile` when a valid contract is met at (q=0) but an attained witness occurs below the contracted tolerance;
- `composition_stable_for_external_audit` when all contracted breakdown counts meet tolerance and every exact gate passes;
- `stability_bridge_pending` when no outcome-blind contract exists;
- `composition_inconclusive` when the new solver/replay/feasibility gate fails.

A supportive primary with `composition_stable_for_external_audit` justifies only the specified external report audit. A supportive primary with `stability_bridge_pending` justifies preference elicitation and protocol preparation, not the claim that the signal is stable enough. A composition-fragile result says the retrospective ranking is sensitive to patient composition and does not justify that external step from this experiment. A primary adverse result is an attained falsification of the locked documentary ranking criterion, not evidence that no biological signal exists. A primary or composition inconclusive result supports no escalation. No label is allowed to call (p_{safe}) calibrated risk, (B_{max}) an observed burden, documentary M2 biological MVI, eventual text visible, or the external audit a clinical benefit study.

If a valid stakeholder interval ([p_L,p_U]) is supplied, retain the parent’s strict rule (p_U<min_y p_{safe,y}), and additionally report (q^{pref}_y(p_U)). Equality fails. If the interval is absent, preference status remains `bridge_pending`; the patient-composition contract cannot replace clinical preference evidence.
