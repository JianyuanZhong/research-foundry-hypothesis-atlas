## Unresolved question and clinical importance
Plasma-proteomic screens nominate single circulating proteins (GDF15, CHI3L1, IL-6/CRP family) as disease biomarkers. Competing explanations for a robust cross-disease protein signal:
- **A (disease-specific):** the protein carries a process preferential to disease *D*.
- **B (shared systemic axis):** it indexes a trans-disease allostatic-load / multimorbidity state [K2, K3].
- **C (treatment/operative correlate):** it tracks operative management / care intensity rather than disease biology [K1].

Unresolved question: after removing the shared healthy-vs-diseased and co-occurrence structure, does any individual protein retain a **disease-preferential residual at a fixed operative state**? This decides whether a nominated protein is a disease-biology candidate or only a marker of being co-diagnosed / under operative treatment.

## Leading claim vs strongest rival, and where they differ
- Leading claim: some proteins retain a disease-preferential residual (A) beyond shared axes and beyond operative state.
- Strongest rival: the residual collapses once **operative/treatment state (C)** and shared multimorbidity (B) are fixed.
- Discriminating observation: the diseased-only case-case transfer-specificity gap **Δ_D** (primary) and its treatment-safe counterpart **Δ_D^treat** (secondary), evaluated against a within-stratum permutation null. A survives if Δ_D^treat exceeds the null band; C is favored if the raw Δ_D is positive but Δ_D^treat collapses.

## WHAT IS NEW THIS EPISODE (computed, read-only): the treatment-safe stratum is nearly NOT IDENTIFIED
The parent defined Δ_D^treat by a **pre-registered rule**: a disease×procedure pair (D,P) qualifies as a treatment-safe stratum iff n(P)≥200, n(D)≥500, n(P∩D)≥500 and disease-lift ≤1.05. The parent argued satisfiability from **marginal** counts only (e.g. OPCS H22=10,499; ICD I10=18,061). This episode computes the **joint** structure the rule actually requires, within the 53,014 UKB-Olink participants (`work/lead-ep26/diag_joint.py` → `work/lead-ep26/diagnostics.json`, runtime 18 s, read-only):

- N = 53,014; 1,199 OPCS-4 blocks, 1,731 ICD-10 blocks.
- Blocks with n(P)≥200: **195**; blocks with n(D)≥500: **296**; joint pairs evaluated: **57,236**.
- Pairs satisfying the full rule (n(P∩D)≥500 **and** lift≤1.05): **4**.

The four qualifying pairs are `Z30×X99` (lift 0.926), `I10×Q18` (0.980), `I10×Q07` (1.002), `Z86×Q18` (1.018). Their anchors are largely **non-biological**: `X99` = OPCS "unclassified operations" (25,941 participants); `Z30`/`Z86` = ICD-10 management/history Z-codes, not diseases. **Interpretation (adverse to the branch's favored design):** the parent's co-primary treatment-safe contrast Δ_D^treat is **identified for at most 4 disease×procedure strata and is effectively NOT IDENTIFIED for any biologically interpretable disease with a well-powered unrelated procedure anchor.** This computed fact demotes Δ_D^treat from a co-primary estimand to a narrowly enumerated sensitivity analysis and keeps the **primary claim on Δ_D** (shared-axis B vs disease-specific A), which remains testable across the 195/296 blocks. It is consistent with rival B/C partly explaining cross-disease protein signals [K2, K3, K1] and cannot be distinguished from them by marginals alone.

## Baseline vs substantive learned alternative (method comparison)
- **Baseline:** a 1-D shared-axis score (first PC / aggregate inflammation score, ProAL50-style [K2]) asks whether a protein adds anything beyond the shared axis.
- **Learned alternative:** an elastic-net / supervised latent-axis residual model fitted on a training half, evaluated on a held-out half, with disease label as target and shared axis+age+sex residualized out. It can reveal **protein-specific, non-collinear residuals** that the 1-D aggregate collapses into one direction; a sparse subset recovering Δ_D would support A.
- **Why it matters:** the 1-D score loses per-protein residual structure; a positive learned Δ_D with a null 1-D baseline distinguishes A from B.
- **Deferral note:** a bounded learned model is not fitted in this discovery episode; it is the solver deliverable (see outputs). A GPU is not required for the estimator; a CPU fit on the 53,014-subset is the proposed default. This is recorded, not executed.

## Analysis (solver-specified)
Per (protein, disease-pair): residualize protein on shared axis + age + sex; compute Δ_D across diseases on the held-out half; permutation null (1,000 within-stratum permutations) for the discrimination band; for the 4 qualifying (D,P) strata compute Δ_D^treat. Outputs: `results.json` (per-protein Δ_D, Δ_D^treat with CI), `split_manifest.json`, `code_list.json` (protein→disease mapping).

## Falsification criteria
- **Supportive (A):** Δ_D for a non-trivial protein set exceeds the 1,000-permutation null band on the held-out half.
- **Adverse (B/C):** Δ_D within the null band (shared axis suffices), or raw Δ_D>0 but Δ_D^treat collapses to the null band in the ≥1 qualifying stratum (treatment state explains it).
- **Inconclusive:** held-out power insufficient at the pre-registered ≥200-case threshold, or no stratum qualifies (→ treatment-safe claim NOT IDENTIFIED, as computed here for biologically interpretable pairs).
