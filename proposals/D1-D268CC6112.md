# Episode 94 successor: can a repeat-lactate review queue cross ICU testing practice without local outcome labels?

## Unresolved question and clinical importance

An ICU considering a mortality-review or escalation-review service must decide whether a prognostic queue developed elsewhere can rank its own patients when repeat-lactate testing is less or more common. This is a concrete deployment decision: a fixed number of review slots must be assigned, and a receiving hospital may not yet have enough outcome labels to refit a model. The unresolved claim is not that repeat lactate causes better outcomes, nor that an observed lactate is always clinically available. It is whether the *ordering* produced by a source-practice model retains material seven-day event yield in the opposite practice environment, compared with the same model trained with held-out labels from that target environment.

The strongest available evidence is narrower. The inspected full text of Baysan et al. (2022, DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750, PMCID PMC9444407) reports modest incremental discrimination for a 24-hour lactate in a selected sepsis setting (recalibrated C-statistic 0.62 to 0.64). The inspected literature on informative observation, including Sisk et al. (2020, DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242, PMCID PMC7810439), supports the concern that what is measured and when it is measured can itself carry prognostic information. Neither establishes cross-practice transport, a fixed review capacity, the EICU cohort, or patient benefit.

The inspected research-ambition demonstrations motivate longitudinal modeling, calibration, and external transport, but are not a topic or method mandate. The Natural History and Bayesian articles were inspected through their available article text and supplements. For the cancer demonstration, the main article and full STAR Methods were unavailable through the checked endpoints; only its metadata and supplement were inspected, and they are not used to support a claim about the main paper's methods.

This proposal advances the parents by making one decision primary—fixed-capacity ranking transport—and treating absolute-risk calibration as a separate, explicitly non-rescuing diagnostic. A calibration shift can leave queue membership unchanged; conversely, a useful ranking need not justify displaying a probability. The design therefore avoids an uninterpretable composite while still reporting the evidence needed for a hospital that wants both uses.

## Falsifiable hypothesis and estimand

Freeze hospitals into two outcome-blind strata using the complete constructed cohort's repeat availability: Low is strictly below the median 0.5029411764705882 and High is at or above it. Analyze both directions, Low-to-High and High-to-Low.

The primary hypothesis is:

> In each direction, a model fit only in the source repeat-practice stratum and containing the numeric optional repeat lactate will place at least 0.03 more D7 deaths per occupied review slot than the otherwise identical source-stratum process-aware model, and its event yield will be no worse than 0.02 D7 deaths per slot below a same-score model fit using target-stratum labels with target-hospital holdout, at the primary 10% review capacity.

The first contrast tests whether the numeric repeat value adds decision information after repeat availability, age, missingness, and non-lactate surveillance are represented. The second tests whether that information remains useful across practice strata without local outcome labels. Both are necessary for the *portable incremental queue* claim; neither is a treatment effect.

For target hospital h, n_h is the eligible target population and k_h(c)=ceil(c n_h). Use c=0.10 as the sole primary capacity. Prespecified stress capacities are C={0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20}; no favorable stress capacity can rescue a failed primary result. Rank within hospital by the raw linear predictor descending, ties by ascending patientunitstayid.

Let Q_full^tr(h,c) be the source-only R_full queue, Q_process^tr(h,c) the source-only R_process queue, and Q_full^loc(h,c) the target-stratum hospital-held-out R_full queue. Define K_d(c)=sum_h k_h(c), and:

Delta_process_d(c) =
 [sum_h sum_{i in Q_full^tr(h,c)} D7_i -
  sum_h sum_{i in Q_process^tr(h,c)} D7_i] / K_d(c).

Delta_transport_d(c) =
 [sum_h sum_{i in Q_full^tr(h,c)} D7_i -
  sum_h sum_{i in Q_full^loc(h,c)} D7_i] / K_d(c).

The primary estimand is the ordered pair at c=0.10 in both directions. Support for portable incremental queue value requires the lower endpoint of Delta_process_d(0.10) to be at least +0.03 and the lower endpoint of Delta_transport_d(0.10) to be at least -0.02 in each direction, with all gates and deletion stability passing. The +0.03 and -0.02 are prespecified operational margins for this experiment, not guideline thresholds, validated utilities, or estimates of preventable deaths.

Use Dinf with the same queue contrasts as a secondary eventual in-hospital-death tradeoff guard. Require its lower endpoint to be at least -0.02 for the portable full-versus-local contrast in each direction; failure is adverse for the guard, not evidence of safety. Report random-within-hospital queues, raw all-patient event rates, captures, false positives, substitutions and overlap as diagnostics. These baselines cannot replace the paired estimands.

## Separate probability-use diagnostic

For each direction, train R_full only on source-stratum hospitals. Estimate a source offset delta_A from source-hospital-held-out D7 predictions, using the deterministic penalized root
sum_i[D7_i-expit(eta_i+delta_A)]-delta_A=0
by bisection on [-20,20], tolerance 1e-12. Apply p_tr=expit(eta_tr+delta_A) to the target. This offset and all portable preprocessing use source labels only.

As a historical reference, fit the same R_full pipeline in the target stratum while excluding each target hospital from its calibration/model training, and obtain p_loc for that held-out hospital. Target labels may enter this reference only; they must never enter p_tr, portable preprocessing, source strata, queues, or model selection.

Within each direction, repeat-availability status s in {0,1}, and threshold z(p)=1[p>=0.10] including equality, report each cell's n, D7 events, observed event rate, mean predicted risk and E=mean(D7-p). Define q_tr as the maximum absolute E across the four target cells. Define G=q_tr-q_loc, where q_loc is computed analogously. The calibration diagnostic is acceptable only if the upper 95% endpoint of q_tr is <=0.05, the upper endpoint of G is <=0.02, and every portable and local-reference cell has at least 30 original patients. These are analytic screens, not clinical thresholds. Calibration never rescues a failed queue result and a queue result never validates a displayed probability.

If computable:
