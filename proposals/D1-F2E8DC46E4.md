## 6. Exact q_req and the integer contract repair

For each primary year a and world omega, define the 24-hour eligible bundle A_iomega={u:E_iomega,24,u=1}; I_iomega=1{|A_iomega|>0}; and n_A,omega,a=sum I_iomega. Patients with I=0 remain in N and ranking but not in the availability denominator. n_A=0 is feasibility-inconclusive, not a zero denominator convention.

A patient is complete-covered only when every eligible whole accession is exposed:

C_iomega(O)=1{I_iomega=1 and sum_{u in A_iomega} M_iomega,24,u=|A_iomega|}.

Let c_omega,a(O)=sum C_iomega(O) and Q_omega,a(O)=c_omega,a(O)/n_A,omega,a. A patient with a partial bundle, an unmatched version, or an unresolved audit state is not complete-covered. All identities and partial subsets remain adversarial.

Let R be the exact union of all rational k/n_A,omega,a for k=0,...,n_A, across retained 2020/2021 worlds. For q in R, Z(q) is every complete coupled state with Q_omega,2020>=q and Q_omega,2021>=q in its own denominator. PASS(q)=1 only if every inherited gate passes for every state in Z(q). PASS is monotone because Z shrinks as q increases.

If maximum content fails, q_req is undefined. Otherwise:

q_req=min{q in R:PASS(q)=1}, and r_bundle=1-q_req.

q_req is named the **deterministic critical patient-complete coverage ratio**, not a measured lower bound, availability estimate, confidence bound, capacity, or preference.

The new operational artifact is a complete integer contract matrix. Store q_req as reduced integers (q_num,q_den). For each retained (omega,a), compute exactly

m_req,omega,a = ceil(q_num*n_A,omega,a/q_den).

Emit one row containing world hash, year, N, n_A, m_req, q_num, q_den, maximum-content covered count n_A, the immediately lower attainable count/fraction, source-ledger hash, accession-ledger hash, onset-state-space hash, and boundary witness hash. The exact contract predicate for any fully classified matched archive census is c_exact,omega,a>=m_req,omega,a for every retained cell. Equality passes the coverage predicate, although each documentary performance margin still requires strict >5. This integer form is the canonical audit interface; rounded percentages are display-only.

The immediately lower global q_prev must fail when it exists, with a complete raw witness. The compiler must also emit a cellwise staircase table showing every count at which ceil(q*n_A) changes. This prevents denominator drift, percent rounding, favorable-world selection, or treating accession-level coverage as patient-complete coverage.

## 7. Computation and falsification

Read every row of every bound HCC source; sampling is none. Record all deterministic filters and exclusions. Compute q_req by complete enumeration, exact branch-and-bound, or an exact multi-valued decision diagram. Cache states only when exact score bits, eligibility, complete-bundle indicators, endpoint terminals, and cutoff outputs match, with reversible hashes. No heuristic incumbent is a result.

Primary one-hot interpretation:

1. `computationally_inconclusive`: source replay, coupled-state generation, exact ranking, search certificate, quotient/integer-contract replay, or boundary witness fails.
2. `feasibility_inconclusive`: computation completes but any N, K, event, or n_A requirement fails.
3. `maximum_content_signal_adverse`: all clean stored content fails any 24-hour comparator, random, or deletion gate in either year. This falsifies the locked documentary ranking claim.
4. `complete_bundle_robustness_adverse`: maximum content passes but q_req=1. This falsifies a positive missing-bundle reserve.
5. `hcc_documentary_robustness_supportive_bridge_pending`: maximum content passes, q_req<1, q_prev fails when it exists, every witness verifies, and the integer matrix replays.

A supportive result establishes only a finite-corpus worst-case documentary reserve and an exact archive evidence target. An adverse result does not establish patient harm or invalidate biological imaging predictors. An inconclusive result does not count as adverse or supportive. Report N, n_A, q_req, r_bundle, every m_req, H_G, K-H_G, all comparator/random margins, deletion minima, and raw attained/failing witnesses by year/world. The 12/48/72-hour trajectories use the same onset assignments, are sensitivity-only, and cannot veto or rescue the 24-hour conclusion.

## 8. External archive audit: exact and probabilistic evidence are separate

No archive bridge is executed with HCC. A future outcome-blind bridge must supply the exact (patient master index, visit number, examination number) crosswalk; HCC source ordinals and hashes; immutable version/predecessor/amendment identifiers; complete-version hashes; authored, finalized, released, ingested, retrievable, readable, retracted times; timezone and clock provenance; operation-clock validation; and scoring-input hash. “Readable” means technically retrievable by the locked pipeline, not viewed by a clinician.

The audit target is the finite HCC patient frame in each emitted year/world cell. Patient is the sampling unit; every eligible accession for a sampled patient must be resolved. Unknown, nonresponse, unmatched lineage, or inaccessible component counts as not covered in a census and cannot be silently removed from a probability sample. A source world may be removed only by preregistered validation evidence that logically rules it out; otherwise all worlds remain.

Archive-stage labels are disjoint:

1. `archive_inconclusive`: crosswalk, versions, clocks, denominator, sampling frame, multiplicity, or integer replay fails.
2. `exact_coverage_contract_not_met`: a complete matched census has c_exact<m_req in any retained cell. This means the sufficient coverage contract is not met; it does not by itself show a documentary gate fails.
3. `exact_audited_documentary_adverse`: the exact census onset state is compatible and direct replay fails a primary fixed-K gate.
4. `exact_coverage_bridged_documentary_supportive`: a complete matched census has c_exact>=m_req in every retained cell, or direct exact replay passes all primary gates. This is a deterministic bridge only for the frozen corpus and locked pipeline.
5. `probabilistic_coverage_not_established`: a valid probability audit has a simultaneous lower covered-patient count bound l_omega,a<m_req in any cell.
6. `probabilistic_coverage_supported_at_95pct`: a preregistered probability audit with positive inclusion probabilities, patient-cluster sampling, retained nonresponse, and a design-based simultaneous one-sided 95% bound satisfies l_omega,a>=m_req in every retained cell.

The sixth label is explicitly a repeated-sampling confidence statement, not a deterministic guarantee that every compatible archive state passes. Report the sampling design, inclusion probabilities, finite-population frame, nonresponse, confidence construction, multiplicity family, l, n_A, m_req, and slack l-m_req for every cell. Do not use an iid binomial interval for unequal-probability sampling. An aggregate accession percentage, marginal timestamp histogram, rounded q estimate, or unmatched patient sample cannot satisfy any bridge.

Even an exact supportive bridge leaves `clinical_action_status=not_identified`. A prospective silent-mode workflow study must measure report delivery to intended users, viewing/action, real staffing and competing workload, delays/failures, downstream decisions, harms, costs, and patient outcomes; stakeholders must define utilities. Treatment effects and patient benefit require another study.
