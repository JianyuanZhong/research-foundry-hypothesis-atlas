# MerTK specificity repair: a donor-blocked macrophage–FLS interaction test

Episode 4 child of [prior hypothesis]. This is a planned Harbor experiment. No biological fit or wet-lab experiment was executed here.

## Scientific opening and hypothesis

The clinically important question is unchanged: can a MerTK-associated macrophage state condition rheumatoid-arthritis (RA) fibroblast-like synoviocyte (FLS) behavior in a way that could motivate resolution-restoring therapy for difficult-to-treat or relapsing RA? The strongest inspected evidence is not yet target-specific. [K1] reports an HBEGF+ macrophage–FLS axis with EGFR-dependent fibroblast invasiveness, but it is a different macrophage program. [K2] reports MerTKpos synovial macrophage subsets associated with remission and an in-vitro FLS repair response. [K3] shows that macrophage and fibroblast states are structured within clinically distinct synovial contexts. These observations support a plausible macrophage–FLS mechanism and its clinical importance; they do not establish that a compound-labeled MerTK perturbation changes FLS communication through MerTK.

Hypothesis: in the prepared E-MTAB-8873 endpoint matrix, the recorded inhibitor effect on a prespecified inflammatory FLS program is larger for MerTK/CD206-positive (Mpos) macrophage cocultures than for the matched MerTK/CD206-negative (Mneg) coculture. Formally, for a matched donor/run block k, let delta_g(k) = normalized FLS outcome under macrophage pretreatment with the recorded MerTK inhibitor minus the untreated outcome. The specificity observable is the interaction I_g = delta_g(Mpos) - delta_g(Mneg), with the same construction for the eight-gene inflammatory score. A MerTK-conditioned interpretation predicts negative I and coherent negative Mpos deltas; generic activation, compound-wide off-target effects, donor mixture, cell-composition differences or assay artifacts predict a comparable Mneg response, a response that tracks LPS rather than Mpos status, or loss of the effect after donor/QC adjustment.

This is a falsifiable molecular-state hypothesis, not a claim that MerTK blockade improves RA, that MerTK is a direct ligand–receptor mediator, or that the recorded compound is selective. The repair matters clinically because falsely treating an association or off-target drug response as MerTK causality could misdirect a resolution-restoring program, whereas a reproducible state-specific interaction would justify a target-engagement and functional coculture study.

## What is already supported versus unresolved

Supported by the inspected evidence:

- RA synovial macrophage programs can affect fibroblast behavior, including destructive invasiveness [K1].
- MerTK-positive macrophage subsets are associated with remission biology and can induce a fibroblast repair response in a separate in-vitro context [K2].
- Macrophage and fibroblast states can be co-structured by clinically meaningful synovial tissue context [K3].

Unresolved and tested here:

- Whether the E-MTAB-8873 inhibitor contrast is Mpos-specific rather than a generic compound effect.
- Whether the FLS response survives a matched Mneg comparison and donor/contributing-mixture sensitivity.
- Whether the inhibitor response is compatible with generic macrophage activation, as indexed by available LPS-pretreated samples.

Still not established by this experiment:

- MerTK target engagement, compound selectivity, direct binding, ligand identity, intracellular signaling, secreted mediator changes, FLS invasion or matrix damage, tissue localization, flare prediction, treatment response or clinical benefit.
- A temporal transition or persistence mechanism. The design JSONs have no numeric sampling-time field or culture-duration/washout table; the temporal boundary is the recorded terminal endpoint for each sample only.

## Analysis, baseline and substantive alternative

First produce a binding/QC audit. Require a one-to-one matrix/design join; list every excluded record; verify the three Mpos pairs and the single R1 Mneg pair; preserve the donor conflict and mixed-ratio records; report per-sample cell count, detected-feature count and library size. Do not filter an absent gene into a presumed zero.

Normalize the E-MTAB-8873 matrix with one prespecified library-size/size-factor method, log-transform for the score, and retain raw counts for a negative-binomial sensitivity analysis. The baseline estimates the eight-gene score and each gene with donor-aware paired contrasts. The primary estimand is the signed Mpos delta across the three usable Mpos blocks, summarized by paired values and donor/mixture bootstrap or a weakly informative Bayesian interval. The specificity estimand is the single R1 interaction I_R1; it is reported as a bounded block-level contrast, never as replicated Mneg inference. The LPS contrasts and mixed-ratio samples are prespecified negative/composition checks. There are too few independent blocks for an asymptotic p-value or an independent-replication claim.

The substantive alternative uses exactly the same samples and outcomes: a strongly regularized hierarchical multivariate latent-state model with the eight-gene inflammatory score as an anchored loading block and the remaining measured rows allowed to contribute only through shrinkage-controlled factors. Random effects represent FLS donor, macrophage/contributing mixture and run when identifiable. Its target is an Mpos-by-inhibitor state interaction, not a clinical label. Fit it on donor clusters and run leave-one-cluster-out stress tests across R1/R2/R5; with three usable clusters this is a fragility diagnostic, not external validation. Compare baseline and alternative on signed Mpos delta, the R1 interaction, gene-level coherence, donor/mixture resampling uncertainty, sensitivity to the donor conflict, and whether the alternative's signal is a generic activation or technical factor.

The alternative is scientifically substantive because separate per-gene tests can miss a distributed correlated FLS state and can fail to reveal that a nominal score is carried by one donor or technical module. It is not selected as the sole method because three usable Mpos blocks and one Mneg interaction block cannot support an unconstrained latent model. It is deferred if shrinkage diagnostics or leave-one-cluster-out fits are unstable. A ligand–receptor/mediation model is also deferred: HBEGF/EGFR and direct ligand/protein, secretion, target-engagement and functional data are absent from the primary panel. Evidence to revisit it is a verified ligand-containing assay, balanced independent donors, protocol exposure/washout, direct MerTK engagement and functional FLS endpoints.

Resource estimate: CPU is appropriate. The targeted matrix is only 33 x 445, and a 16-CPU, 32–64 GiB allocation should fit the baseline and small regularized alternative; this is a reasoned estimate, not a measured full fit. No GPU is required. The configured future solver envelope is up to 16 CPUs, 8 GPUs, 262144 MiB and 28800 seconds; discovery did not spend that envelope, and no full scientific fit was executed.

Actual scientific deliverable: a completed binding/QC audit; newly fitted donor-aware per-gene and score estimates for the three Mpos pairs; the R1 Mpos-versus-Mneg interaction with uncertainty appropriate to one block; prespecified LPS/mixed-ratio sensitivity outputs; a fitted and stress-tested multivariate alternative or a documented stability deferral; and a supportive/adverse/inconclusive classification tied to those computed outputs. Readiness alone is not completion.

## Interpretation and falsification

Supportive evidence requires all of the following as a pattern, not one favorable gene: (1) negative Mpos inhibitor deltas in all or nearly all usable pairs; (2) coherent negative movement of the fixed eight-gene score; (3) a negative R1 interaction because the Mneg inhibitor effect is smaller, not merely an Mpos change; (4) no comparable signal in LPS-pretreated controls or mixed-ratio composition checks; (5) persistence under donor/run/cell-count/library-size sensitivity; and (6) concordance between baseline and a stable, donor-stressed multivariate alternative. This supports a MerTK-conditioned communication hypothesis for this endpoint and justifies the next wet-lab test. It still does not prove target specificity, direct mechanism or clinical benefit.

Adverse evidence is an absent/inconsistent Mpos effect, a comparable Mneg interaction response, a pattern reproduced by LPS, loss after donor/QC adjustment, dependence on the conflicting R1 record, or a multivariate factor localized to technical/library-size or composition variation. That falsifies the proposed MerTK-specific interpretation of this assay and redirects work toward generic activation, donor-specific biology, compound pharmacology or assay validity. It does not prove MerTK is irrelevant in RA.

Inconclusive evidence includes failure of exact sample/design joins, inability to resolve contributing donors or exposure/vehicle/washout, sparse or unreliable prespecified rows, unstable alternative fits, or absence of target-engagement information. A null from the single R1 interaction is imprecise and is not biological refutation. The next action is protocol recovery and the balanced wet-lab test below, not favorable reclassification.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes and paths, header/design joins, measured-row availability, filtering, normalized values, contrasts, uncertainty, sensitivity outputs, alternative-model stability and whether a conclusion matches the prespecified classification. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for target engagement, direct signaling, secreted mediators, invasion/matrix function, tissue localization, flare, treatment response and patient benefit. A verifier must reject a numerically supportive computation paired with an unsupported claim of MerTK selectivity or clinical efficacy.

## Concrete next wet-lab test

Run the smallest donor-blocked factorial that removes the current ambiguity: the same RA FLS and macrophage donor blocks crossed by macrophage state (Mpos versus Mneg) and macrophage pretreatment (vehicle, UNC106238/recorded inhibitor, orthogonal MerTK perturbation such as MerTK siRNA or a validated blocking antibody, and LPS generic-activation control), with FLS-only wells for baseline. Use at least four independent FLS donors and four independent macrophage donors, randomized/blinded wells and technical replicates nested within donor blocks; do not count wells as independent donors. Record compound identity, concentration, vehicle, pretreatment duration, washout and culture endpoint.

Before interpreting FLS output, verify MerTK surface abundance and pathway engagement (for example, MerTK occupancy or ligand-stimulated phosphorylation with a prespecified assay), macrophage viability and activation. On the same recorded endpoint measure FLS IL-6, CXCL8, MMP3 and MMP9, the eight-gene panel, secreted protein and a functional invasion/matrix assay. The decisive comparison is whether UNC106238 and the orthogonal MerTK perturbation produce the same Mpos-specific interaction while LPS does not and viability/vehicle controls are clean. If pharmacologic and orthogonal effects disagree despite verified engagement, classify the compound response as off-target or non-MerTK until resolved. A rescue or second chemotype is a follow-up, not required for this smallest test.

## Method and branch decisions

Primary baseline chosen: donor-aware fixed score plus per-feature paired interaction, because it is transparent, estimand-aligned and honest about one Mneg block.

Substantive alternative retained: hierarchical anchored multivariate factor model, fitted to the same 33-sample/445-feature input with donor/run shrinkage, leave-one-cluster-out stress testing and uncertainty comparison. It can reveal distributed state coherence and heterogeneity that the baseline loses, but its capacity is intentionally constrained by the sample design.

Not chosen: an unrestricted neural model, ligand–receptor mediation model or cross-dataset classifier. The reason is scientific adequacy, not a blanket complexity or GPU prohibition: the targeted panel lacks ligand/protein/engagement variables and the design cannot identify an unrestricted representation or a clinical prediction target. Evidence for revisiting these choices is the balanced orthogonal wet-lab experiment, verified target engagement, functional FLS outcomes, resolved donor/protocol metadata and independent donor replication.

The three demonstration papers in the research-ambition references were used as ambition context, not as a topic or method list. They are not reproduced here. This proposal is a targeted child repair of the valid parent and does not alter the macrophage–FLS clinical question.
