## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance

An ICU stay with more time below MAP 65 mmHg is often reported to carry higher AKI risk. The parent frames two rivals:
- **H-dose:** cumulative (0,24]h MAP<65 burden is a physiologic ischemic dose that independently raises (24,72]h KDIGO-AKI risk.
- **H-observation:** hypotension triggers more monitoring/lab testing, so AKI is *detected* more, inflating the association without proportionate true injury.

Clinically these imply opposite decisions: under H-dose, minimizing time below MAP 65 is a treatment target; under H-observation, the "target" partly reflects who got watched. Evidence is mixed: a minute-by-minute MAP-band study in cardiac surgery found **no** band→AKI association [K1]; a post-intubation hypotension cohort found hypotension **was** associated with increased AKI [K3]; monitoring intensity is independently outcome-associated and susceptible to measurement-frequency artifact [K2]. Prior work does not test the detection process directly. The parent's discriminating test (creatinine-draw effect minus non-renal-draw effect; >=2-creatinine-draw restriction) was left **unexecuted**.

## 2. Deliverable

## 4. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative)

- **Baseline B1 (parent):** logistic AKI ~ burden + severity + hospital FE — *assumes* detection is exchangeable.
- **Substantive alternative A1 (measurement/negative-control decomposition):** joint count model (Poisson/NB) of (24,72]h renal vs non-renal draw counts on burden; estimand = difference in log-rate; plus doubly-robust AIPW burden→AKI estimate on the >=2-draw restriction. *Reveals:* whether the baseline "effect" is separable from differential ascertainment — information B1 cannot provide.
- **Learned alternative A2 (deferred):** GBT/spline dose–response with hospital-clustered CIs (energy/threshold check); deferred because A1 is the minimal test that can change the decision (non-estimability), and A2 was established in prior episodes.

## 5. Falsification / interpretation (pre-specified)

- **Non-estimable (park branch):** near-zero burden variance within hospital, or non-renal draws present in few stays / essentially constant.
- **Supportive of H-observation:** creatinine-draw and non-renal-draw associations ≈ equal (contrast CI includes 0) AND burden→AKI near-null in the >=2-draw restriction.
- **Adverse (H-dose):** creatinine-draw association significantly exceeds non-renal AND burden→AKI persists in the restriction.
- **Inconclusive:** insufficient channel variation or CIs crossing pre-specified equivalence margins (declared useful-negative).

## 6. Executed results (this episode, eICU 2.0, frozen sources)

Source: `[research job]` → `work/lead-ep41/probe_out/probe_summary.json` (streamed; the earlier v1 job failed on RSS — a disclosed infrastructure limit, not scientific).

- **Cohort (D1):** 200,859 patient rows → **132,782 adult stays with unitdischargeoffset>=1440** across **208 hospitals**; 130,569 have (0,24]h vital rows.
- **Exposure coverage / non-estimability risk is REAL:** only **25,192 / 130,569 (19.3%)** monitored stays have **any** MAP<65 reading in (0,24]h; median burden = 0 (25th–75th pct = 0). The exposure is sparse — a within-hospital dose test will rest on a minority subgroup. This is a concrete estimability caution for the parent.
- **Outcome ascertainment (D3):** ascending creatinine draws (24,72]h: mean 1.79, median 2, max 28; **63.03% have >=2 draws** (83,698) → the equal-ascertainment restriction is well populated.
- **Negative-control channel (D1):** non-renal draws mean **11.75**, median 9, max 215; **88.5%** of stays have >=1; 11.5% have zero → the broad-monitoring channel carries usable variation.
- **Raw contrast (D2), restricted to 32,889 stays with >=6 MAP observations:** Spearman(burden, creatinine-draws) = **+0.121**; Spearman(burden, non-renal-draws) = **+0.185**; **contrast = -0.063**.
- **Burden tertiles:** mean creatinine draws 1.86→2.19→2.24 (low→high); mean non-renal draws 13.10→16.70→17.59. Both channels rise with burden, **non-renal rises at least as much**.

**Reading (bounded):** the observation channel IS estimable (rich non-renal variation; adequate creatinine ascertainment), so the branch is **not** parked for non-estimability. But at the raw level the direction is **symmetric-to-broad-monitoring**, i.e. it leans toward H-observation rather than the parent's pre-specified adverse(H-dose) pattern (creatinine-draw effect exceeding non-renal). The parent's fitted negative-control test therefore has a non-trivial prior of returning "not creatinine-specific". This is descriptive/raw only — not the fitted, adjusted estimand.
