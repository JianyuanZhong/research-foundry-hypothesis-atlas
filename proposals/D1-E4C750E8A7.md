# Is post-rise renal-support heterogeneity source-concordant after leakage-controlled surveillance adjustment?

## Scientific deliverable and substantive advance

The future solver must newly construct an auditable eICU first-rise cohort and source-specific event ledger, then estimate whether hospital heterogeneity in newly documented post-rise renal-support persists after exact pre-rise surveillance adjustment and is reproducible across two operationally distinct documentation channels.

The primary noncausal estimand is the supported-hospital 90th-minus-10th percentile spread in the standardized 48-hour cumulative incidence of the first strict post-rise renal-support record from either source:

1. H_E_raw = Q90_h(q_E,h) - Q10_h(q_E,h), before surveillance adjustment;
2. H_E_surv, the same spread after standardizing hospitals to a common, leakage-controlled pre-rise surveillance/source-capture regime; and
3. A_surv = H_E_raw - H_E_surv, with a joint hospital-cluster uncertainty interval.

The source-concordance deliverable is mandatory, not an optional subgroup analysis: treatment-only H_T_surv, high-specificity intake/output-only H_IO_surv, their difference and hospital ordering, source-first order, waiting time, and the proportion of either-source events corroborated by the other source within 30, 60, and 120 minutes. These are distinct documentation streams from the same stay, not independent clinical gold standards.

Primary hypothesis: among adult eICU unit stays with a first qualifying recorded creatinine rise and no strict support record through that rise, a clinically consequential-looking hospital spread in newly documented post-rise support remains after leakage-controlled pre-rise surveillance adjustment and shows qualitatively concordant hospital variation in both treatment and high-specificity intake/output documentation.

The actual scientific deliverable is a frozen cohort and source ledger; exact support dictionaries; pre-rise scores and no-leakage audit; paired additive and ordered-process fits; raw and adjusted union/source-specific standardized CIFs; source concordance/discordance and corroboration outputs; competing-exit decomposition; held-out-hospital transport; 500-replicate uncertainty; and a prespecified supportive, adverse, or inconclusive interpretation. Discovery fitted no model and claims no result.

This advances the parent by resolving whether its post-rise support signal is a clinically consequential, source-concordant process or mainly a local capture artifact. It does not claim biological AKI, dialysis initiation, treatment delivery, treatment effectiveness, renal benefit/harm, quality, or causality.

## Evidence-supported versus unresolved claims

The inspected frozen eICU snapshot and full catalog support the availability of ICU-keyed laboratory, vital-sign, treatment, intake/output, disposition, hospital, infusion, and severity fields with ICU-relative offsets. The parent’s unrestricted read-only audit found candidate renal-support records in both treatment and intake/output sources, but those counts are feasibility checks, not event rates in the target cohort and not evidence of clinical treatment.

The strongest available claim is that the snapshot can encode a recorded creatinine-rise process, pre-rise and post-rise renal-support documentation, source labels, ICU-relative time, hospital identity, and competing unit exits. A treatment row does not establish administration, initiation, dose, duration, indication, or intent. An intake/output row does not establish a procedure, treatment session, or complete observation. Absence from either source is not absence of care, and chronic dialysis before ICU admission is not fully adjudicated.

The unresolved claim is the observational one stated above: after measurable case-mix and pre-rise surveillance adjustment, does hospital variation persist in the union endpoint and reproduce across both source channels? A source-concordant result supports residual heterogeneity in a documented process; a source-discordant result supports ascertainment heterogeneity; neither establishes clinical appropriateness or biological renal injury.

It motivates testing transport sensitivity to observation opportunity, but its broad prediction framing has no specified renal-support outcome and is less clinically discriminating here. It is retained as a revisit branch, not selected as a parent or as evidence of the hypothesis.

If discharge and an event share a minute when U <= 2880, discharge takes precedence. Never use hospitaldischargeoffset or concatenate later ICU stays.

For the primary incident-support risk set, exclude a stay with any strict treatment- or intake/output-source support record at ICU time 0 through r, inclusive. Keep this group in a labelled ongoing-or-new documentation analysis, without inferring unobserved pre-ICU support absence.

## Two source definitions and concordance ledger

Treatment source T is a case-folded normalized treatment.treatmentstring match to an exact, frozen high-specificity modality dictionary containing explicit renal dialysis/hemodialysis, CVVHD, CVVH, CAVHD, peritoneal dialysis, SLED, or equivalent explicit renal modality strings. Exclude catheter insertion/access surgery, line or flush language, generic renal terms, and ultrafiltration described only as fluid removal. Freeze and print retained and excluded observed strings before any fit. Deduplicate exact treatmentid; use treatmentoffset as the event clock. activeupondischarge is descriptive only and cannot establish receipt or initiation.

Intake/output source IO uses only the exact case-insensitive intakeOutput.cellpath or celllabel values Dialysis (ml)|In, Dialysis (ml)|Out, CRRT Actual Pt Fluid Removed, CRRT Out, CRRT - UF removed, CRRT- UF removed, or Hemofiltration, with finite nonzero cellvaluenumeric or finite nonzero dialysistotal. Do not use an unlabeled dialysistotal row alone. Deduplicate exact intakeoutputid; use intakeoutputoffset as primary clock and retain intakeoutputentryoffset for sensitivity. cellvaluetext is audit context only. Generic urine, catheter, Foley, voided-amount, and occurrence labels are not strict support.

The event ledger stores the first eligible T, first eligible IO, their union event, both-source flag at the same time, source-first direction, later corroboration, and waiting time. A corroborated event has both sources in the same stay within a prespecified 60-minute window; 30- and 120-minute windows are sensitivities. Later source evidence is not backdated. The union is not promoted as source-concordant clinical evidence if one source supplies more than 80% of union events, if source-specific hospital ordering is reversed/unstable, or if the source-specific analysis is below its support gate.

This design treats treatment and intake/output as independent capture channels for a robustness test, not statistically independent observations and not a gold-standard comparison. A source-concordant association can still reflect shared case mix or shared documentation practice.

Urine-labelled intake/output rows are an observation-process audit only. The catalog has offsets and values but no uniformly valid collection interval or time-varying denominator. Do not call low urine output oliguria, apply a KDIGO urine criterion, or combine urine labels into the endpoint.

## Leakage-controlled pre-rise surveillance adjustment

Construct the reference cohort from all parent-eligible first-rise stays before applying the primary no-prior-support exclusion. For each reference stay j, using only strict T and IO records in ICU interval [0,r_j], define pre-rise source indicators P_Tj, P_IOj, and P_Ej=1-(1-P_Tj)(1-P_IOj).

For index stay i in hospital h, calculate leave-one-stay-out hospital scores from reference stays in the same hospital, excluding i:

- S_T(h,-i): pre-rise treatment-source documentation proportion;
- S_IO(h,-i): pre-rise intake/output-source documentation proportion; and
- S_E(h,-i): pre-rise union proportion.

Freeze numerators, denominators, dictionaries, and any empirical-logit shrinkage constant before fitting. Use source-specific scores as primary (one source-specific score plus its prespecified source interaction per fit); use the union score only as a collinearity-aware secondary representation. Report score numerators/denominators, leave-one-out behavior, overlap, zero/one hospitals, and unsupported hospitals. The score is an observed documentation regime, not patient need.

Patient-level pre-rise observation covariates frozen at r are qualifying creatinine-test count, time since prior qualifying test, valid-lab metadata completeness, and source-specific pre-rise support-surveillance indicators. No post-rise lab, support, confirmation, or exit field enters any predictor, score, threshold, standardization, or split decision.

## Estimands, source-support gates, and interpretation gates

For hospital h, estimate the 48-hour CIF of first union support after r, with recorded unit death and alive unit exit competing and unknown-status exit censored. Standardize each hospital to the development primary-risk-set distribution of age/age-censored flag, gender, ethnicity, unit admission source, unit type, baseline and first-rise creatinine, rise delta, time since prior creatinine, pre-rise test count, early MAP ledger, documented pressor timing, admission severity, and hospital bed category/teaching status/region without hospital identity. infusionDrug timing/name is descriptive documentation only.

Define the primary supported-hospital gate as at least 50 primary risk-set stays and at least 10 union support events per hospital, with at least 10 supported hospitals for a union spread. For source-specific concordance, use the intersection of hospitals meeting the same stay/event gate for each source. If that intersection has fewer than 10 hospitals, source concordance is inconclusive rather than rescued by lowering thresholds. If a source has fewer than 10 events in the required analysis, report it descriptively and do not claim replication.

Supportive evidence requires all of the following:

1. H_E_surv >= 5 percentage points with a two-sided hospital-cluster interval excluding zero and at least 50% of H_E_raw remaining when the raw spread is estimable.
2. On the source-supported hospital intersection, both H_T_surv and H_IO_surv are positive with the same qualitative direction as the union; where estimable, each has an interval excluding zero.
3. The joint interval for H_T_surv-H_IO_surv lies within [-5,+5] percentage points and the bootstrap median hospital-rank correlation is at least 0.50. These are documentation concordance margins, not treatment thresholds.
4. The process decomposition shows that the adjusted result is not explained by repeat-test opportunity, source capture alone, corroboration failure, or recorded unit exit, and the qualitative conclusion transports to held-out hospitals.

If source-specific events are too sparse for item 2, the union may still be estimable, but the conclusion is “adjusted union heterogeneity with source validation inconclusive,” not source-concordant clinical evidence. A union result dominated by one source, a source difference outside the equivalence band, or reversed/unstable ordering is adverse to source concordance and supports ascertainment heterogeneity. A large raw spread that attenuates below the margin after surveillance adjustment supports a capture explanation.

Report the parent’s recorded-confirmation CIF and renal-escalation composite as secondary comparators: first distinct later confirmation (minimum six-hour gap) or strict support. Confirmation is an observation process and must not replace support-only estimation.

## Matched transparent baseline

Fit regularized cause-specific pooled-logistic models on identical 30-minute intervals, cohort, source dictionaries, risk sets, covariates, hospital split, common standardization distribution, competing exits, and uncertainty procedure.

Transitions/outcomes are parallel cause-specific processes for treatment-only support, IO-only support, union support, both-source corroboration, distinct confirmation, recorded unit death, alive unit exit, and unknown-status censoring. Use prespecified linear/spline elapsed-time terms, common clinical slopes, transition/source-specific hospital random intercepts, and the selected pre-rise surveillance score in adjusted fits.

Prespecify nested fits:

- B0: clinical covariates and common elapsed time, no hospital heterogeneity;
- B1: B0 plus source-specific hospital effects, producing raw source/union spreads;
- B2: B1 plus patient-level pre-rise testing opportunity;
- B3: B2 plus one frozen source-specific leave-one-stay-out surveillance score and source interaction;
- B4: B3 without hospital-by-time slopes as a stability reference;
- B5: B3 using the standardized rather than local surveillance score.

Derive standardized CIFs, spreads, attenuation, source differences, corroboration, observed-versus-predicted curves, calibration and Brier scores. This baseline is transparent and directly auditable, but its parallel causes lose the order and waiting-time information needed to distinguish treatment-first, IO-first, and later corroborated sequences.

## Ordered/mechanistic alternative

Fit a piecewise-exponential ordered multi-state model on exactly the same cohort, time grid, source dictionaries, split, covariates, standardization distribution, hospital support gate, and 500-replicate uncertainty procedure.

From first rise, represent:

- first rise -> repeat-test opportunity -> confirmed recorded rise;
- first rise -> treatment-only documentation;
- first rise -> IO-only documentation;
- either-source documentation -> corroboration when the other source occurs within 60 minutes;
- each non-exit state -> recorded unit death or alive unit exit; and
- unknown-status exit -> censoring.

Keep first-rise-to-repeat, repeat-to-confirmation, post-rise-to-support, source-first order, and support-to-corroboration waiting time distinct. Use transition-specific shrunken hospital effects and the same surveillance score. The model can reveal whether residual variation is carried by testing opportunity, treatment capture, IO capture, corroboration, confirmation, or competing exit—mechanistic information the additive baseline loses. Agreement on adjusted union and source-specific spreads is robustness; disagreement is model uncertainty and must be reported.

A learned longitudinal/sequence model is deferred, not prohibited. It may be revisited only if both prespecified models show reproducible shape-specific held-out miscalibration or missing temporal structure in this same source-decomposed support estimand, source-specific event support is adequate, and the learned model can preserve the strict gate, no-leakage score, source decomposition, calibration, uncertainty, and hospital-held-out transport. Its fitting time and GPU requirement are unverified; no GPU is required for the proposed tabular models.

## Split, transport, uncertainty, and falsification

Assign hospitals to 80% development and 20% held-out sets using a fixed, documented hash of hospitalid before cohort summaries, score thresholds, missingness handling, fitting, or standardization. Keep any uniquepid connected hospital component together and report coverage loss; no patient component crosses the split. Use a second fixed hash as sensitivity. This study-defined hospital split must not be conflated with the catalog’s reserved participant partition, which is inaccessible and is not an external validation set.

Fit and standardize only in development hospitals. Apply frozen coefficients, dictionaries, score rules, gates, and cutpoints to held-out hospitals; do not tune or recalibrate on held-out outcomes. Set unobserved held-out hospital random effects to the prespecified population mean. Report held-out observed-versus-predicted union/source/confirmation/repeat/death/alive-exit CIFs, calibration, Brier score, event counts, timing overlap, and process discrepancies. Transport is inconclusive with fewer than 10 held-out hospitals, sparse source events, inadequate overlap, extreme weights, failed convergence, or calibration failure.

Use 500 hospital-cluster bootstrap replicates for union/source spreads, attenuation, source differences, rank correlation, corroboration, model differences, and transport summaries, with a uniquepid-clustered sensitivity. Preserve cohort, ledger, dictionary, score, fit, and bootstrap checkpoints. Report missingness, effective sample size, source event counts, supported hospitals, shrinkage, overlap, and convergence.

Falsification tests include source discordance (one source positive and the other null/reversed, source difference outside [-5,+5], or rank correlation below 0.50); process discordance (unstable treatment-first/IO-first ordering or corroboration that disappears at 30/60/120 minutes); surveillance falsification (adjusted spread disappears, score overlap fails, or result changes materially when patient-level testing opportunity is added); temporal falsification (revised-lab clock, 3-hour/12-hour confirmation gap, unit boundary, or event-at-discharge rules change the qualitative result); measurement falsification (raw versus bridged MAP, periodic versus non-invasive MAP, strict versus broad fluid-removal definitions, or status handling reverses the result); and transport falsification (held-out calibration or process curves fail despite development fit).

Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive labels must be tied to computed outputs and uncertainty. Inconclusive includes sparse source events/intersection, fewer than 10 supported hospitals, unstable bootstrap, near-collinearity, non-overlap, dependence on NULL-status handling, failed convergence, semantic dictionary instability, or held-out failure. A null adjusted spread is adverse to the hypothesis, not proof of no biological renal injury or no treatment need.

Computationally checkable claims are source/schema hashes, headers, exact dictionaries, joins, deduplication, unit verification, offsets and boundary rules, cohort flow, pre-rise-only score audit, split integrity, ledger states, fitted parameters, standardized CIFs, concordance, uncertainty, calibration, transport, and sensitivity reproducibility. Clinical adjudication or another study is required to determine biological AKI, renal replacement initiation, actual treatment delivery, indication, dose, intent, chronic dialysis before ICU, urine meaning, completeness of source absence, appropriate care, hospital quality, causal effects, treatment thresholds, or policy implications.

## Alternatives, resource budget, and selection record

The simple additive model is selected as the transparent baseline because it directly supplies auditable source-specific/union CIFs, competing exits, standardization, calibration, and attenuation. The ordered multi-state model is selected as the substantive alternative because source order, waiting time, corroboration, repeat testing, and competing exits are the scientific uncertainty; the additive baseline loses that process structure. Both use identical inputs, cohort, split, estimand, and bootstrap.

The learned sequence model is retained but deferred under the explicit held-out-misfit rule above. The urine/KDIGO branch is deferred until collection-duration semantics and a valid time-varying denominator are externally validated. A causal pressor branch is deferred because infusionDrug has timing/name but not administration, dose, indication, intent, or complete exposure. Biological AKI and dialysis-initiation claims require adjudication and external validation.

Measured discovery evidence is limited to parent read-only scans of large treatment/intakeOutput files, approximately 1–2 minutes per file, plus schema/catalog inspection; these scans counted feasibility labels and did not estimate target-cohort event rates. Full source-ledger construction, hierarchical additive fitting, multi-state fitting, 500 bootstraps, and transport are unmeasured. The future solver envelope is 16 CPU, 262144 MiB memory, up to eight NVIDIA A100-SXM4 80-GB GPUs, and 28800 seconds. CPU-first is appropriate for tabular fits; GPU use is optional only after an allocated profiling probe and must use cuda:0. Discovery’s 7200-second budget is separate from this future solver envelope.
