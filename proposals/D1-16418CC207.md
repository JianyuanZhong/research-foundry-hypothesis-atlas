## 1. Unresolved question and clinical importance
After curative resection of HBV-related HCC the recurring bedside decision is whether to
start/continue a nucleos(t)ide analogue (NUC). Antiviral therapy suppresses HBV replication and cuts
new HCC incidence, yet residual HCC risk persists under long-term viral suppression, and HCC after
hepatectomy recurs through two mechanistically distinct routes - early **occult residual disease** vs
late **de novo carcinogenesis** ([K3], [K2]). The unsettled question is not "do antivirals help at all"
but: **in the early postoperative window, does being ON a NUC reduce early (<=24-month) recurrence, and
is that effect confined to strata where active viral replication dominates (high baseline HBV-DNA) rather
than pervasive (generic) or absent?** The closest prior work reports that the NUC *regimen* chosen was not
associated with recurrence-free survival after resection ([K1]), which leaves the NUC-vs-no-NUC exposure and
its early/late, stratum-specific structure unresolved. Resolving it informs a real decision: whether NUC
status should be treated as a recurrence-modifying exposure and whether HBV-DNA/cirrhosis should stratify it.

## 2. Leading claim vs strongest rival (pre-specified)
- **H_effect (leading):** NUC exposure started in the early postoperative window lowers early (<=24-mo)
  recurrence, and the benefit is **larger where replication is active** (higher baseline HBV-DNA), i.e. a
  NUC x HBV-DNA stratum contrast; it is attenuated where cirrhosis/indirect carcinogenesis dominates.
- **R_indication (strongest rival):** the crude association is **confounding by indication** - sicker,
  higher-viral-load, better-monitored patients are more likely to be put on a NUC, so any "benefit" tracks
  measured/unmeasured prognostic factors and surveillance intensity, and the stratum contrast vanishes once
  those are balanced.
- **R_null:** no causal effect; null after overlap weighting.
H_effect and R_indication differ on a signed quantity: H_effect predicts a protective NUC HR that is
**larger in the high-HBV-DNA stratum**; R_indication predicts a near-null, stratum-flat HR after weighting.

## 4. Estimand and analysis (baseline vs substantive alternative)
- **M0 (baseline):** landmark Cox for early recurrence ~ NUC + age + sex + cirrhosis + baseline AFP +
  tumour size (single-cohort, conventional adjustment).
- **M1 (primary, target-trial-emulation) [substantive learned/mechanistic alternative]:** clone-censor-weight /
  IPTW-overlap-weighted Cox of the same landmark estimand, with a pre-specified NUC x HBV-DNA (and NUC x cirrhosis)
  interaction; secondary **causal-forest CATE** on the HBV-DNA/cirrhosis strata. What M1 reveals that M0 loses:
  M0 adjustment estimates a single conditional HR and cannot separate confounding-by-indication from a genuinely
  *stratum-modified* effect; M1 balances the indication-driven covariate distribution and yields a stratum-specific
  (HTE) contrast whose sign/size is the discriminator between H_effect and R_indication. A deferred, not-chosen
  alternative (restricted-mean-survival g-computation under a marginal structural model) is recorded as a revisit
  option if positivity is adequate.
- **Selection/deferral reason for method:** M0 alone cannot answer the stratum-modification question; M1 is a
  bounded CPU fit on the available cohort, no GPU needed. Approx budget: seconds-minutes per fit; envelope well
  within solver_planning_limits; no deep model required for this tabular, moderate-N question.

## 5. Falsification (Supportive / Adverse / Inconclusive / precise-null)
- **Supportive of H_effect:** overlap-weighted NUC HR (early recurrence) < 1 with 95% CI excluding 1, AND the
  NUC x HBV-DNA interaction protective (higher HBV-DNA -> larger benefit), robust to era, surveillance-intensity
  and AFP adjustment; negative-control outcome (e.g. an unrelated event) shows no NUC "effect".
- **Adverse (R_indication):** crude HR < 1 but overlap-weighted HR crosses 1 (or reverses) -> confounding by
  indication; or a protective HR that is stratum-FLAT across HBV-DNA -> generic, not replication-specific.
- **Adverse (detection/surveillance):** NUC "benefit" disappears when imaging-cadence / contact-intensity is added.
- **Inconclusive:** HBV-DNA modifier too sparse (<pre-declared minimum events/stratum), or positivity/near-violation
  of overlap, or wide CI spanning 1 with the interaction unestimable -> narrow the claim to the cirrhosis fallback.
- **Precise-null:** tight CI centered on 1 across strata -> NUC status (as defined) does not modify early recurrence.
Conjunctive rule: Supportive requires concordance of the weighted HR, the interaction direction and the negative control.

## 6. Rivals and honest limits
Rivals: confounding by indication; surveillance/detection intensity; healthy-initiation; era/treatment drift;
immortal time (removed by windowed exposure). Limits: the recurrence endpoint is lexical and requires radiologist
mRECIST adjudication; NUC vs never-NUC and NUC start classification require pharmacy/chart clinical adjudication;
HBV-DNA availability is unmeasured (sparsity pre-declared); single institution; no outcome computed in discovery;
causal interpretation would need expert review or another study. Claims computationally checkable: the weighted HRs,
interaction, CATE and negative control. Claims needing adjudication/other data: true recurrence timing (mRECIST),
true NUC adherence, HBV-DNA completeness.
