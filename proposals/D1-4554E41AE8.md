# Timestamp-provenance and assay-anchoring repair for the frozen HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B validation

## Parent, scope, and unchanged estimand

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent’s selected adult HCC repeat-TACE population, systemic-record/comparator ontology, one-event-per-patient construction, exact B/P/event/Y clocks, annual forward blocks, locked nested ridge models, outcome-blind support and assay-specific missing-Y bounds, and noncausal evidence boundary. It does **not** change the estimand, model covariates, fitting rows, denominator, bootstrap, or the definition of a selected assay value.

The unresolved measurement question is narrower and clinically material:

> When a finite albumin or total-bilirubin value is selected for B, P, or Y, does the available record support the intended temporal interpretation of that value as an assay near the specified pre-repeat or post-event clock, rather than merely a value attached to an encounter, order, document, or later verification record?

The source contains a laboratory-row time, but it does not expose separate specimen-collection, accession/result, or verification/release times. The repair therefore makes the provenance limitation executable instead of silently treating `检验时间` as specimen time. It can establish only a **recorded laboratory-row-time anchor**. It cannot establish specimen collection, analytic completion, result verification, clinical review, treatment intent, or administration.

No text field, `标本类型`, qualitative result, order status, encounter field, examination field, or document field is converted into a specimen-validity or result-verification flag.

## Native-time parser and provenance vocabulary

1. Preserve each raw timestamp string and source data-row ordinal. Parse timestamps only with an explicit local-calendar parser accepting `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` and `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`; reject empty, malformed, impossible, or non-finite values. No timezone conversion or invented timezone is allowed.
2. A second-resolution timestamp is an exact instant for the local-clock calculations. A minute-resolution timestamp represents the half-open interval `[t, t+1 minute)` for provenance auditing; it may remain in the inherited primary reconstruction only if the parent parser accepts it, but it cannot support an exact boundary claim. Date-only or lower-precision values are `coarse_time` and are not eligible for a strict provenance-supported label.
3. A laboratory row is `native_lab_time_valid` only when `检验时间` parses successfully at minute or second precision. `定量结果` must independently satisfy the parent’s finite, uncensored numeric parser for that row to be an assay candidate. `标本类型` may be reported descriptively but its presence never proves specimen validity.
4. Each selected row receives exactly one provenance label from this ordered set:
   * `native_recorded_time`: valid native `labs.检验时间`; no claim about when the specimen was collected, analyzed, or verified.
   * `native_recorded_time_coarse`: valid but below second precision; exact boundary interpretation is withheld.
   * `missing_or_invalid_native_time`: no usable native laboratory time; the row cannot be selected as B, P, or Y by this repair.
   * `encounter_context_only`: a linked encounter has usable dates, but they are contextual and cannot replace a missing laboratory time.
   * `out_of_encounter_context`: native lab time exists but is outside a valid linked `[入院时间,出院时间]` interval; retain the parent’s row/clock result for estimand preservation, but flag it and do not describe it as encounter-concordant.
   * `encounter_bounds_unavailable`: native lab time exists but one or both valid admission/discharge bounds are absent or inconsistent; do not infer concordance from `就诊时间`.

`native_recorded_time` is the strongest available label, not “specimen-anchored.” No label in this dataset may be called specimen-collected, result-released, verified, or clinically obtained.

## Frozen primary selectors and the added audit

Run the parent reconstruction first and assert its inherited denominators and clocks. For each assay separately, the primary selectors remain exactly:

* `B`: latest finite uncensored assay in the TACE2 calendar-day window `[day -30, day -1]`, ordered by native `检验时间`.
* `P`: latest finite assay in the selected repeat encounter in `[event time -72 hours, event time)`.
* `Y`: nearest finite assay to `event time +24 hours` in `(event time, event time +72 hours]`, with the parent’s deterministic tie rule.

Require the inherited strict relation `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time` when Y exists and positive P lead. The event time remains procedure `开始时间`; no encounter/order/document time can modify it. Missing native lab time means no candidate row, never a zero, negative assay, or substituted encounter time. No audit field may enter M0/M1, training-fold preprocessing, missing-Y bounds, or bootstrap sampling.

For every selected B/P/Y and every rejected candidate that is nearest to a clock boundary, emit an audit row with:

`patient_id`, `encounter_id`, `assay`, `role`, `source_row_ordinal`, raw `检验时间`, parsed precision, `lab_time`, `event_time` where applicable, `encounter_就诊时间`, `入院时间`, `出院时间`, `native_time_status`, `encounter_context_status`, `clock_membership`, `distance_to_clock_boundary_seconds`, `specimen_type_nonempty`, `quantitative_parse_status`, and `provenance_claim_allowed`.

The primary selector must be byte-identical when all non-lab timestamps are removed from its input. This invariance is a required implementation assertion and prevents accidental anchoring to encounter, order, examination, or document times.

### Duplicate and conflict rules

Do not silently collapse laboratory rows. Audit duplicate groups by (`患者主索引`,`就诊号`,`检验`,`检验时间`,`定量结果`,`定性结果`,`标本类型`) and separately by (`患者主索引`,`检验`,`检验时间`,`定量结果`) across encounters. Preserve source row ordinals. The inherited deterministic selector may choose one tied finite row by: target-distance or latest-time rule, then earliest raw `检验时间` representation, then smallest source row ordinal. If tied rows at the selected timestamp have different finite values, set `same_time_value_conflict=1`; retain the parent-selected value for estimand preservation but mark the provenance audit `ambiguous_same_time_value`. A block containing such a selected conflict cannot receive a provenance-supported interpretation without adjudication.

### Encounter-context checks

For a linked encounter with valid `入院时间 <= 出院时间`, calculate whether `lab_time` is inside the closed interval `[入院时间,出院时间]`; calculate separately whether it is before admission or after discharge. Do not use `就诊时间` as a substitute bound. For outpatient/partial encounters with no valid interval, report `encounter_bounds_unavailable`. These checks are descriptive consistency checks only. They neither exclude a primary row nor prove specimen collection within the encounter.

## Negative controls and falsification tests

All controls are diagnostics; none changes the primary estimand or supplies a second outcome.

1. **Encounter-time substitution placebo.** Re-run only the B/P/Y row selector in a shadow table after replacing each usable lab timestamp by its linked encounter `就诊时间`, while retaining values, rows, event dates, and all primary model inputs unchanged. Do not fit models. Report selector disagreement, BP-eligibility disagreement, and Y-observation disagreement by assay/role/block. Any nonzero disagreement is evidence that the result depends on an unlicensed encounter-time substitution; it is not evidence that encounter time is an assay time.
2. **Order-time substitution placebo.** For each lab row with a linked encounter, identify the nearest non-drug order timestamp among `开立时间`, `开始时间`, and `结束时间` only for a diagnostic shadow selector. If no order exists, record `no_order_context`; if multiple orders tie, retain all counts rather than inventing a link. Replace lab time with the nearest order time in the shadow selector and report disagreement. Because the orders table has no laboratory accession/result key, no order-based row may enter the primary selectors or models. A large disagreement or a high fraction of unavailable nearest-order context is a provenance limitation, not a result.
3. **Untimed-document negative control.** Assert that adding/removing all nonempty `clinical_documents` rows, including both duplicate admission-diagnosis columns, leaves every B/P/Y identity, clock, and model row unchanged. A document row has no temporal column and therefore cannot anchor an assay. Any change is a fatal leakage/implementation error.
4. **Examination/procedure clock separation control.** Assert that `examinations.开始时间`, procedure `结束时间`, and order timestamps are used only in their parent-defined roles and cannot change lab candidate ordering. Recompute the selectors using a lab-only timestamp projection and compare exact row ordinals. Any mismatch is `timestamp_role_contamination` and invalidates the block’s computation.
5. **Pre-event impossibility control.** For every selected P and Y, assert native lab time is strictly before event and strictly after event, respectively, under the parent windows. Count any candidate that would enter the opposite side if its linked encounter `就诊时间` or order time were used. Such candidates are recorded as clock-discordant and are never recoded into the intended role.
6. **Delayed-window negative control.** On the same BP-eligible rows, describe assay rows in `(event+72 hours,event+144 hours]` using native lab time only. This delayed window is not Y, not an outcome, and not a model feature. A favorable primary result that disappears only when the delayed recording window is used cannot be interpreted as evidence about the primary assay clock; it flags recording-process sensitivity.
7. **Clock-shift diagnostic.** Shift native lab times by exactly +24 hours and -24 hours in a shadow selector, with event and values fixed. Report row/eligibility disagreement and do not fit or report a gain. This tests sensitivity to timestamp displacement; it cannot distinguish specimen delay from data-entry delay.

The controls must be run separately for albumin and total bilirubin, by arm, and by annual forward block. Small cells are reported as counts only and are not pooled across assays. No threshold is invented to turn an order or encounter match into clinical validity.

## Fail-closed status and interpretation precedence

Produce one aggregate provenance record per assay/origin/future block with exact counts for selected B/P/Y, native-valid, coarse, missing/invalid, encounter-concordant, out-of-encounter, unavailable bounds, same-time conflicts, and every negative-control disagreement. Use these statuses in strict precedence:

1. `computationally_invalid` if any required primary selector, clock, duplicate-safe join, or lab-only invariance assertion fails; do not emit a model result for that block.
2. `timestamp_provenance_unsupported` if selected values lack valid native `检验时间`, include selected same-time value conflicts, or any conclusion requires substitution of an encounter/order/document/examination timestamp. Do not repair by dropping the affected rows or changing the complete-triplet denominator.
3. `timestamp_provenance_limited` if native row times are available but are coarse, outside encounter bounds, or encounter bounds are unavailable. The inherited recorded-time estimand may still be computed if otherwise valid, but it must be named a recorded-row-time association only.
4. `recorded_time_compatible` only when all selected assay rows have valid native times, no selected same-time value conflict, all inherited strict clocks pass, and no non-lab timestamp affects selection. This still does **not** mean specimen-anchored or clinically verified.

The parent’s primary evidence labels remain subordinate to this status. A supportive parent RMSE gain with `recorded_time_compatible` supports only an incremental association for recorded laboratory-row values in the selected population and calendar block. It cannot support a clinical assay-timing, treatment-response, causal, safety, utility, actionability, transport, or benefit claim. A favorable complete-case gain with `timestamp_provenance_limited` or `timestamp_provenance_unsupported` is selection/timing-sensitive or unsupported, respectively, regardless of its p-value or bootstrap interval. An adverse gain remains adverse for the recorded-row-time estimand but does not prove B suffices clinically. A zero-crossing missing-Y bound, failed support gate, nonestimable SD, failed negative control, out-of-range future Y, or provenance status above `recorded_time_compatible` is inconclusive for any stronger clinical interpretation.

Even the strongest available status cannot establish specimen collection time, result/verification time, assay units or reference range, specimen quality, transfusion/fluid effects, tumor burden, disease severity, completed follow-up, external-care capture, treatment intent/administration, causal effects, or patient benefit. Those claims require source fields, clinical adjudication, or an independently timestamped/prospective study.

## What changed and what remains uncertain

Relative to `[prior hypothesis]`, this child adds a source-bound, assay-specific timestamp provenance contract: it distinguishes the sole native laboratory-row time from encounter, order, examination, procedure, and untimed-document clocks; preserves the parent selectors and estimand; adds deterministic parser, duplicate/conflict, boundary, and row-level audit rules; adds seven executable negative controls; and imposes fail-closed interpretation precedence. It does not claim any new empirical HCC result. The central unresolved limitation remains that HCC exposes no specimen-collection, accession/result, verification, units, reference-range, or clinical adjudication fields. Full-source execution of the audit and the parent model/bound pipeline is still required before even the recorded-row-time robustness status can be reported.
