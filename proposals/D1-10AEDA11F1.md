# Ascertainment-calibrated early hepatic trajectory after first TACE

## Scientific question and falsifiable hypothesis

For adults with hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) receiving a first recorded transarterial chemoembolization (TACE), does early post-procedure change in liver-function assays provide reproducible prognostic information beyond current post-TACE state and history, after accounting for both selective laboratory testing and selective 90-day local-record follow-up?

The primary hypothesis is:

> In adults with a first qualifying TACE, no coded hepatic decompensation in the preceding 180 days, and numeric primary assays in both prespecified peri-TACE windows, a latent worsening trajectory from days -14:-1 to +1:+7 is associated with higher risk of a new coded hepatic-decompensation encounter on days +8:+90 and improves patient-held-out proper scoring and calibration over a current-state/history baseline. The association and incremental performance must remain within a prespecified sensitivity range when the probability that a non-event is ascertainable by day 90 is modeled rather than treated as random.

This is a prognostic-information hypothesis about a local coded endpoint. It is not a claim that TACE causes decompensation, that absent records mean recovery, that the lexical diagnosis equals adjudicated liver failure, or that the result establishes a monitoring or treatment threshold.

## What is known and what remains unresolved

A current Europe PMC full-text acquisition was read for Sanai et al., Scientific Reports 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-54466-4, PMCID PMC13457603. That multicenter TACE/TARE study defined hepatic decompensation with clinically adjudicable events such as ascites, variceal bleeding and portosystemic encephalopathy, and reported post-locoregional-therapy decompensation and baseline liver reserve as clinically important. It does not establish that a short, routine-laboratory trajectory predicts a coded outcome in this HCC snapshot.

The strongest local evidence currently supports feasibility of repeated dated laboratory observations, TACE procedure timestamps, encounters and diagnosis labels. It does not support a validated tumor stage, imaging response, complete mortality endpoint, diagnosis timestamp, or adjudicated decompensation outcome.

The unresolved claim is incremental and observation-aware: whether a within-person peri-TACE trajectory contains information beyond the current post-TACE state/history, and whether that information persists after addressing the fact that test intensity and return to care may be consequences of early illness. The substantive advance is an explicit two-part observation correction: assay observation is modeled as in the parent, while 90-day non-event ascertainment is treated as missing and sensitivity-analyzed. This separates a biological trajectory signal from “sicker patients were tested and followed more often.”

## Population and temporal boundaries

Use only discovery buckets 0-79 under the catalog partition rule:

sha256("ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1" + NUL + "hcc" + NUL + 患者主索引) mod 100.

All partitions and resampling are by 患者主索引; reserved buckets 80-99 are inaccessible and are not an external validation set.

1. From procedures, select rows where 手术 equals TACE case-insensitively or contains 化疗栓塞. Require a parseable 开始时间 and a matching encounters row. Deduplicate same-patient/same-encounter qualifying rows, then select the earliest qualifying start per patient as index.

2. Require an adult index encounter (年龄 >=18) and at least one diagnoses row containing 肝细胞癌 or 肝癌, linked to the index encounter or a pre-index encounter. Because diagnoses has no timestamp, use the linked encounter’s 入院时间, otherwise 就诊时间 or 出院时间, only as a record-time proxy and report this limitation.

3. Exclude any patient with a coded term containing 腹水, 肝衰竭, 肝功能衰竭, 失代偿, 肝性脑病 or 肝昏迷 on a linked encounter whose proxy time is in days -180:-1. Exclude liver-resection or ablation procedure rows in days -7:+7. Preserve diagnosis-type strata rather than silently treating all labels as equivalent.

4. The primary exposure cohort requires at least one numeric 定量结果 for each of 白蛋白, 总胆红素, 国际标准化比值 and 血小板计数 in both windows: pre-TACE days -14:-1 and early post-TACE days +1:+7. Use exact 检验 names, parse numeric 定量结果 only, retain assay-specific specimen type, counts and relative timing, and never pool assays or infer units. A broader partial-coverage landmark cohort is exploratory only.

5. Define a status-observed indicator S. S=1 if either (a) a linked coded decompensation event occurs in days +8:+90, or (b) no such coded event is found and a later encounter has 入院时间 or 出院时间 at least 90 days after index. S=0 is not assigned to patients without this evidence; their outcome is missing for the local-record estimand. A coded event makes S=1 even if later follow-up is absent.

6. The primary outcome Y is the first new coded decompensation term in days +8:+90, with no matching term in days -180:-1. The secondary outcome is a descriptive adverse composite of new coded decompensation or a death-coded diagnosis in +8:+90, with components separate. Death is not assumed when no death code is found and is not a primary endpoint.

The bounded audit over the exact files found 15,358 discovery patients with a valid first qualifying TACE start, 15,343 linked adult index encounters, 15,205 with an HCC diagnosis proxy, 289 pre-index coded-decompensation exclusions and 14,916 before assay/follow-up restrictions. Among the broader primary-assay landmark, 11,282 had numeric primary assays in both broad windows, but only 5,379 had an ascertainable status under the parent rule; 46 had a coded decompensation event and no death label was observed in +8:+90. The stricter all-four-assays-both-windows audit count was 4,583. These are aggregate feasibility counts, not fitted results; the compiled implementation must recompute and report its own flow.

## Observation-aware estimands and analysis

For each assay and window, calculate train-fold-fitted median, IQR, numeric count, median relative time, specimen distribution, parse failures and missingness. No unit conversion is permitted.

The simple current-state baseline is a train-fold ridge logistic regression for Y among S=1. It uses the day +1:+7 current assay medians for the four primary assays plus creatinine and sodium when present, age, sex, pre-index encounter count/recency, prior coded liver-disease history, and pre-index medication context. It uses train-fold-only imputation/missingness indicators and standardization. The baseline does not use post-outcome information.

The substantive alternative is a jointly fitted Bayesian assay-specific latent state-space model using the same six assay streams. Each assay has its own scale and measurement error; the model represents irregular times, a latent pre-index level, a post-TACE transition and a posterior day-7 change. A separate observation component models assay availability, count and timing. A second ascertainment component models S from information available no later than day +7: pre-index care intensity/history, demographics, index procedure/context, early assay values and assay observation features. The event model then uses the latent trajectory and propagates its posterior uncertainty. This alternative can reveal discordant recovery, noisy measurements and whether the trajectory is distinct from care-process intensity—information discarded by a current-state regression.

To repair outcome-follow-up selection, estimate q(X)=P(S=1|X through day +7) on training patients only and use stabilized inverse-ascertainment weights for the primary weighted outcome analysis, with fold-defined clipping and a complete-case sensitivity. Do not interpret these weights as recovering events outside the local system. Because missingness may depend on unobserved worsening, run a selection-model sensitivity in which:

logit P(S=1 | X, z) = logit q(X) + delta*z,

where z is the standardized latent day-7 worsening (or its baseline-observed analogue) and delta is prespecified as -1, -0.5, 0, 0.5 and 1. Report how the trajectory odds ratio and held-out score difference change across this range. A result that is only present at an implausibly favorable ascertainment assumption is not supportive.

Use deterministic patient-level 60/20/20 train/tune/test splits within discovery buckets, with split hashes. Fit all preprocessing, ascertainment models, imputation, priors, transitions and clipping rules without test access. Evaluate baseline and alternative on exactly the same patient-held-out test patients and status definition. Report the primary trajectory odds ratio per train-set IQR worsening with patient-bootstrap 95% intervals, paired test-set changes in log loss and Brier score, calibration intercept/slope/reliability, AUROC/AUPRC as descriptive measures, and decision-curve net benefit at 5%, 10% and 20% reporting thresholds. These thresholds are not clinical treatment targets.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted baseline and latent/observation/ascertainment models, not a proposal-level score. Completion requires cohort flow, assay/event/status counts, fitted coefficients and posterior/observation/ascertainment summaries, held-out predictions, uncertainty intervals, sensitivity curves over delta, and falsification outputs. No fitted result is claimed in this proposal.

## Falsification and interpretation

Run these prespecified checks:

- A process-only model using assay counts, missingness, timing and encounter-follow-up proxies but no assay values. Similar or better performance than the trajectory model indicates a care-process explanation.
- Remove observation-process variables and compare with the full model. A gain that disappears only when those variables are removed is not evidence of biological trajectory information.
- Permute post-index lab times within patient while preserving values and assay identity. A retained trajectory association indicates leakage or a timing artifact.
- A reverse-temporality negative control using a coded decompensation outcome before index and post-index trajectory features in a cohort not excluded for that prior window. Predictive performance for a past outcome indicates leakage or selection contamination.
- Stratify, without overinterpreting, by diagnosis type and by ascertainment pattern. Large incompatible changes identify label/process heterogeneity requiring review.

Supportive results require an adverse-direction trajectory association with useful precision, a proper-score improvement whose patient-bootstrap interval excludes no improvement at the prespecified primary ascertainment assumption, no material calibration harm, and broadly consistent direction over the delta sensitivity range and after removing process-only information. This would support incremental prognostic information for the local coded endpoint only.

Adverse results are a precise null or reversal, no proper-score improvement, worse calibration, or a gain explained by process-only features, time permutation, or a narrow favorable ascertainment assumption. That would falsify the proposed incremental claim in this snapshot, not prove that hepatic reserve is biologically irrelevant.

Inconclusive results include fewer than a prespecified minimum event count in a test fold, unstable/capped ascertainment weights, inadequate follow-up, strong diagnosis-type heterogeneity, wide intervals, or a nonidentified latent posterior (for example, non-finite/weakly separated transition and observation parameters or materially divergent fits across initializations). Inconclusive means no clinical recommendation and no conclusion that the hypothesis is true or false.

## Limits requiring clinical review or another study

The local endpoint is a lexical coded diagnosis linked to an encounter-time proxy, not a chart-adjudicated event. The available detector does not establish comprehensive diagnosis extraction, coreference or implicit temporality. The snapshot lacks validated ascites grading, variceal bleeding documentation, encephalopathy adjudication, laboratory units, tumor stage/size/response, complete death capture, outside-hospital records, images and waveforms. Clinical documents and pathology are not time-indexed sufficiently for this experiment. A clinical hepatology review of sampled index and event records would be needed to estimate positive-predictive value and distinguish true decompensation from coding artifacts. An independent health system or registry would be needed for transportability. A prospective study would be needed for causal TACE effects or benefit from an action triggered by monitoring.

## Alternatives considered and deferral record

- The parent’s observation-aware latent model is retained and extended because the audit exposed the separate 90-day ascertainment problem.
- A transformer/disease-history model is deferred: it could encode richer event ordering, but its added predictive complexity does not directly identify whether follow-up selection explains the trajectory signal. Revisit if a stable event/time corpus remains after the ascertainment repair and a pre-specified comparison targets a clinically substantive decision.
- An ALBI-style mechanistic score is deferred because the local metadata has no lab units; applying the published formula would invent units. Revisit only after unit verification or expert laboratory mapping.
- Image or pathology models are deferred because images are absent, pathology has no time field and neither supplies validated treatment response in this snapshot.
- Clinical-document NLP is deferred from the primary model because documents are narrative and untimestamped; it may be useful only in a separately authorized blinded adjudication study.

The child therefore selects an explicit ascertainment-calibrated latent trajectory experiment, not complexity for its own sake. The evidence that would justify revisiting a deferred alternative is stable event support, validated units or adjudicated labels, time-indexed clinical text, images, or an external cohort.
