## Scientific question and claim boundary

Among UK Biobank participants attending imaging visit 2 with usable thigh MRI, does higher MRI-derived thigh muscle fat infiltration (MFI) add a clinically meaningful prospective association with later falls report beyond MRI visceral adipose tissue (VAT), body size, baseline function, and measured activity/comorbidity? Leading explanation: MFI may mark muscle quality/function vulnerability not captured by abdominal adiposity. Strong rival: MFI is chiefly a marker of obesity, inactivity, baseline weakness/illness, and healthy-volunteer MRI selection; any apparent association is confounding/reverse causation or ascertainment. Both predict association before adjustment; they differ on whether MFI retains a stable, precise within-VAT/body-size contrast after adjustment, a washout/lag, and baseline function control. These observational comparisons can establish descriptive/prognostic distinctiveness, not muscle-fat causation or treatment benefit.

The tested claim is *incremental conditional prognosis*, not that changing MFI prevents falls. Current source evidence here supports field availability only. Null imprecise estimates are inconclusive, not refutation.

## Estimand, alternatives, and computation

Primary estimand is the adjusted association of a one-SD higher visit-2 composite thigh MFI with visit-3 report of any fall in the strictly post-index 12-month recall interval, conditional on VAT and prespecified pre-index covariates, summarized as adjusted risk difference and odds ratio with 95% uncertainty intervals. Compare the MFI coefficient/partial contribution against standardized VAT in the same model; do not label the contrast a causal effect. Risk differences use standardized predictions over the eligible analytic population.

**Simple baseline (primary):** logistic regression for visit-3 falls with linear standardized VAT and MFI, age, sex, BMI, baseline walking pace/activity when available, baseline grip and baseline falls status (for inclusive sensitivity); report covariate specification exactly. Use participant-level fixed 20% holdout (seed frozen before fitting) for prognostic assessment, with remaining 80% used for fitting; no repeated participant records cross splits. On training portion, fit models and any transformations; report held-out AUC, Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and log loss alongside the adjusted association and bootstrap 95% intervals. Do not use AUC change alone as clinical importance.

**Substantive alternative:** prespecified generalized additive logistic model using penalized smooths of MFI and VAT plus a low-rank tensor-product MFI×VAT interaction, with same cohort/covariates/split. The alternative tests nonlinear thresholds and whether MFI’s association changes across VAT levels—information a linear additive model cannot express. It is a flexible descriptive/prognostic surface, not a biological mechanism. Tune smoothing only within the training set via nested participant-level CV; freeze the held-out set. Compare held-out log loss/Brier/calibration and the joint smooth/interaction uncertainty to the baseline, using bootstrap resampling of held-out predictions/participants. Plot adjusted risk contrasts over observed common support; do not extrapolate sparse extremes. If events or common support are inadequate, defer the tensor interaction and report no reliable nonlinear resolution rather than simplifying silently.

A clinically meaningful positive result requires a robust MFI association with interval excluding the null and a prespecified meaningful standardized-risk contrast, directionally consistent across baseline and flexible model, not merely a tiny metric gain. Falsify a distinct signal if the conditional association and standardized risk contrast are near-null with intervals excluding the prespecified minimally important difference, and the flexible model shows no reproducible surface/interaction. If intervals span both meaningful benefit and null/adverse values, call inconclusive. Adverse result (higher MFI associated with lower falls risk) challenges the directional hypothesis and triggers assessment of selection, activity, measurement coding, and survivor bias; it does not imply protective muscle fat. Supportive result justifies replication/clinical adjudication, not causal or intervention claims.
