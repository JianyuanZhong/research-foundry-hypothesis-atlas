# Storage-to-actionability bridge for the frozen pulmonary handoff audit

## Purpose and inherited constraints

This is a narrow successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. It repairs one residual decision-validity gap: the parent can establish whether the frozen, admission-level route atom is reproducible from separated retrospective documentation and whether held-out route purity survives a leakage audit, but it does not yet say whether a reproducible packet contains an explicit next-action specification, nor does it distinguish that documentation property from the real workflow and patient outcomes that MIMIC does not observe.

The parent design remains controlling. The following are immutable and are not redefined by this proposal: the first-eligible adult, one-admission-per-subject pulmonary-opportunity population; the reciprocal pre-departure radiology chain; the canonical exact discharge-summary artifact; every unit-specific storage clock; exhaustive departure states; the deterministic safety-sentinel census; positive-probability admission sampling and its inclusion probabilities; all-unit review within a sampled admission; the frozen atom `g = (possible-calendar class, terminal curr_service, workflow class)`; `AUTO/RECON/TIMING/DEFER` precedence; all-unit adverse routing; support-atlas construction; the parent route and route-purity estimands; finite-population/compatible-set inference; the existing challenge selection and review rows; the existing workload ceilings; and the prospective-silent-only, noncausal evidence ceiling.

The bridge is a pre-registered recoding and control inside already selected, already budgeted route-blinded packet reviews. It adds no admission, reviewer, review pass, packet, field acquisition stream, adaptive top-up, route, atom, baseline, chronology, primary outcome, or causal estimand. Its results are secondary diagnostics and cannot change a primary route or rescue a failed atom.

## Evidence-supported and untested claims

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence and the parent protocol is a storage-to-storage claim: after the stated eligibility, chronology, and masking rules, a discharge opportunity can be represented by a reproducible admission-linked packet and a route assignment can be audited against independently staffed, held-out component review. This is evidence about documentation representation and retrospective reproducibility.

The unresolved claim tested by this child is narrower: among those same frozen opportunities, does the packet explicitly specify the fields needed for a receiving clinician to identify a next action, and is the apparent documentation reproducibility robust when an independent control reconstructs action units without seeing the parent route, atom, weights, or other panel output? The prespecified alternative is that some route/atom signatures are reproducible yet textually incomplete, creating an actionable documentation gap even before any claim about delivery or benefit.

MIMIC cannot establish that a clinician viewed the information before departure, that a note was final or signed, that a recommendation was communicated, that an order/referral was executed, that responsibility was accepted, that follow-up was scheduled or completed, that the patient could follow the plan, or that a clinical outcome or harm resulted. Absence of these facts is an evidence ceiling, not a negative clinical outcome. `A_TEXT_COMPLETE` below is therefore an explicit-text property and must never be called clinical actionability, appropriateness, safety, completion, or benefit.

## Frozen unit and route-blinded bridge record

For each already selected challenge admission, use the same sealed packet and the same pre-departure boundary `D` as the parent. The packet contains only the parent's permitted reciprocal radiology material, canonical discharge artifact and permitted discharge-detail artifact, admission/disposition fields, and the predeclared source-availability manifest. It excludes route, atom, stratum, sampling weight, challenge status, parent labels, route summaries, post-`D` text, and any outcome summary.

The bridge uses two independently staffed and roster-disjoint control lanes, embedded as fixed fields in the existing parent challenge rows. They do not vote a route and do not receive any earlier component result.

* **DOC lane (independent documentation reconstruction).** DOC inventories the retained recommendation units and codes, per unit, only the predeclared target cluster, action/modality, due interval, polarity, recipient/responsibility, replacement rule, conflict status, and supporting artifact span. Each field is one of `EXPLICIT`, `ABSENT`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNREADABLE`, or `UNKNOWN`; `UNKNOWN` is reserved for nonresponse, packet corruption, unresolved chronology/linkage, or an ambiguity the codebook cannot resolve. DOC does not infer a field from service, transfer, discharge location, store time, or generic text.

* **ACT lane (independent explicit-actionability reconstruction).** ACT performs its own inventory before seeing DOC output and applies a separately sealed codebook. It asks whether a receiving clinician could identify a concrete next action from the supplied artifact without inventing a target, action/modality, due interval, recipient/responsibility, replacement rule, or conflict resolution. It returns `A_TEXT_COMPLETE`, `A_TEXT_CONDITIONAL`, `A_TEXT_INCOMPLETE`, or `A_TEXT_UNKNOWN`, plus the same field-level reason vector. `A_TEXT_COMPLETE` means all required fields for every retained unit are explicit and internally nonconflicting in the text; it does not mean anyone received, accepted, executed, or benefited from the plan.

The lanes are independently rostered, independently ordered, and append-only sealed. The bridge is not considered independent merely because two labels agree: shared-wrong states remain compatible. The bridge codebook, roster, packet order, source hashes, and seal ordering are fixed before any parent route, atom, weight, primary outcome, or compatible-set result is opened.

## Deterministic unit matching and exact algebra

The residual ambiguity in the prior actionability bridge is repaired here by a fixed canonical matcher. No reviewer is allowed to decide after seeing the other lane how two units correspond.

For each lane, normalize only the prespecified lexical representation: Unicode normalization, case folding, whitespace collapse, and the locked codebook's synonym map. Do not add clinical synonymy or use an embedding/model. A unit key is the tuple

`K(u) = (target_cluster, action_modality, polarity, due_interval_code, recipient_code, replacement_code, conflict_code, source_note_id, source_span_ordinal)`.

`source_note_id` and `source_span_ordinal` are packet-local, deterministic identifiers, not extra evidence. The canonical retained unit inventory is sorted lexicographically by `(target_cluster, action_modality, polarity, due_interval_code, recipient_code, replacement_code, conflict_code, source_note_id, source_span_ordinal)`. Duplicate keys are retained with multiplicity; they are never silently collapsed. If either lane has duplicate or unresolved keys, matching is `UNKNOWN` for the affected unit rather than resolved by order of appearance.

Match DOC and ACT inventories by the exact first seven semantic components of `K(u)` after normalization, with one-to-one multiplicity matching in sorted order. A disagreement in any non-unknown semantic component, unmatched multiplicity, unresolved duplicate, or source-span disagreement that changes the unit boundary is a mismatch. H3 is not used to manufacture or adjudicate a DOC/ACT unit; it is an evidence-boundary audit applied after the two inventories are append-only sealed. This explicitly separates the reproducibility functional from workflow availability.

Let `U_i` be the parent's inherited canonical distinct-unit list for admission `i`, after the parent's reciprocal-restatement handling. Let `F_i` be the required fields for all units in `U_i` under the frozen codebook. Define:

```text
D_REPRO_i = 1 iff both DOC and ACT produce valid inventories, the canonical
             inventories have identical one-to-one matched unit multiplicities,
             and every non-unknown field in F_i agrees exactly.
D_REPRO_i = 0 iff both inventories are valid and a prespecified adverse
             mismatch is observed.
D_REPRO_i = UNKNOWN iff either lane has nonresponse, packet corruption,
             unresolved duplicate/linkage/chronology, or another unknown.

A_TEXT_COMPLETE_i = 1 iff ACT marks every required field in every retained
                    unit EXPLICIT and internally nonconflicting, with no
                    inferred field.
A_TEXT_COMPLETE_i = 0 iff ACT is valid and at least one required field is
                    ABSENT, CONTRADICTED, UNREADABLE, or otherwise incomplete.
A_TEXT_COMPLETE_i = UNKNOWN iff ACT cannot validly complete the inventory.
A_TEXT_GAP_i = 1 iff D_REPRO_i=1 and A_TEXT_COMPLETE_i=0.
```

The primary bridge estimand is the design-weighted, compatible-set bounded ratio

`G_text = P_w(A_TEXT_GAP = 1 | D_REPRO = 1, frozen atom g, inherited route r)`,

with numerator and denominator kept jointly compatible. The denominator is not replaced by complete cases, and marginal extrema may not be combined when they come from incompatible label states. Report the joint matrix of `(D_REPRO, A_TEXT_COMPLETE, A_TEXT_GAP)` by frozen atom and inherited route, using the parent's inclusion probabilities, finite-population intervals, and simultaneous-compatible-set rules. Challenge-only results remain explicitly conditional on challenge inclusion.

Special states are fixed before labels. For an inherited `NOACTION`/zero-unit record, DOC and ACT must each independently return a valid empty inventory. If both do so, `D_REPRO=1`; `A_TEXT_COMPLETE=NA` and `A_TEXT_GAP=NA`, never favorable-matched to a complete action. If either lane cannot establish a valid empty inventory, `D_REPRO=UNKNOWN` and `A_TEXT_COMPLETE=NA`. For `DEFER`, `RECON`, or any route with unresolved fields, `UNKNOWN` remains `UNKNOWN`; it is not recoded to `COMPLETE` or to a clinical failure. A route-independent presentation or whitespace perturbation must not alter the result.

## Evidence-boundary vector

A second, explicitly non-clinical output is an availability vector for facts that would be necessary to turn explicit text into a real workflow claim. It is coded from the frozen packet and source-availability manifest only, after DOC and ACT are sealed. Each field is one of `OBSERVED_IN_SOURCES`, `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES`, `CONTRADICTED`, or `UNKNOWN`:

`VIEWED_BEFORE_D`, `FINALIZED_OR_SIGNED`, `COMMUNICATED`, `ORDER_OR_REFERRAL_EXECUTED`, `RESPONSIBLE_TEAM_ACCEPTED`, `SCHEDULED_OR_HANDOFF_CONFIRMED`, `PATIENT_OR_CAREGIVER_CAPACITY_PREFERENCE`, and `POST_DEPARTURE_FOLLOWUP_OR_OUTCOME`.

The audit is predeclared to classify the first seven as `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` unless a specifically bound source column/event can demonstrate the fact; `storetime`, service, transfer, discharge location, note text, or death alone never demonstrates it. The eighth is `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` unless an explicitly bound follow-up/outcome source is available. `UNKNOWN` is used for a source row that exists but is unreadable, internally contradictory, or cannot be linked under the frozen keys. `CONTRADICTED` is used only when a bound source contains an explicit contradiction; it is not assigned merely because a field is absent.

The vector is not an outcome endpoint. Report `E_WORKFLOW_GAP=1` whenever any required workflow field is `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` or `UNKNOWN`, and `E_OUTCOME_GAP=1` whenever the follow-up/outcome field is `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` or `UNKNOWN`. These are evidence-availability flags. The required table is `P_w(E_WORKFLOW_GAP=1 | D_REPRO=1, A_TEXT_COMPLETE=1, g, r)` with the exact unavailable fields listed; it must not be described as care failure.

## Executable pre-label protocol

1. Verify the snapshot, all source hashes, catalog schemas, archive members, and read-only status. Extract only the parent's frozen population, `D`, reciprocal radiology chain, canonical DS artifact, pre-`D` structured context, and the already selected sample/challenge set. Hash extraction manifests.
2. Reconstruct the parent's atlas, strata, positive inclusion probabilities, sample, route-blinded challenge reservation, atom reservation, and workload ledger byte-for-byte. Abort rather than regenerate if any population, sample, route, atom, chronology, or workload hash differs.
3. Before any labels are opened, hash and seal separate DOC and ACT codebooks, the normalization map, the exact seven-component semantic matcher, lexicographic serializer, zero-unit/DEFER NA table, source-availability manifest, packet order, and roster assignments. Neither lane sees the other lane, a route, atom, weight, parent component label, or primary result.
4. Execute synthetic fixtures through the complete parser and reference interpreter before real packets. The fixture set is below; every fixture has an expected state and is a required pass gate.
5. Append DOC and ACT fields to the existing fixed challenge rows only. No extra row, reviewer, pass, admission, or time allowance is permitted. If a field cannot be completed within the existing row maximum, record `UNKNOWN`/`BRIDGE_INFEASIBLE` and apply the inherited adverse-compatible rule; never extend the workload.
6. Seal DOC and ACT outputs, then generate the evidence-boundary vector from the predeclared source manifest. Compute bridge fields, inherited route, and primary outcomes in separate code paths. The bridge code has no write access to route or atom assignments.
7. Run an independent reference interpreter and audit exact joins, pre-`D` filters, canonical matching, duplicate multiplicity, zero-unit handling, joint compatible states, finite-population weights, and challenge-only conditioning. Store hashes for codebooks, fixtures, packets, rosters, labels, compatible states, and results.

## Fixtures and controls

The following fixtures are synthetic and contain no clinical records. They are mandatory executable controls, not evidence.

1. **Identical complete unit:** DOC and ACT see one identical unit with explicit target, action, due interval, recipient, replacement, and no conflict. Expected `(D_REPRO=1, A_TEXT_COMPLETE=1, A_TEXT_GAP=0)`.
2. **Missing recipient:** identical otherwise, recipient absent in both valid inventories. Expected `D_REPRO=1, A_TEXT_COMPLETE=0, A_TEXT_GAP=1`.
3. **One-lane disagreement:** DOC has due interval `7d`, ACT has `14d`. Expected `D_REPRO=0`, not a favorable consensus.
4. **Unknown/nonresponse:** ACT omits a required field because the packet is corrupted. Expected `D_REPRO=UNKNOWN`, `A_TEXT_COMPLETE=UNKNOWN`; no complete-case deletion.
5. **Duplicate multiplicity:** two identical semantic units in DOC and one in ACT. Expected mismatch/adverse `D_REPRO=0` when both valid; if duplicate identity cannot be resolved, `UNKNOWN` per the duplicate rule. The implementation must not collapse multiplicity.
6. **Order permutation:** same units in different textual order. Expected identical canonical serialization and unchanged results.
7. **Whitespace/case perturbation:** locked normalization only. Expected unchanged results.
8. **Decisive one-field perturbation:** remove only the due interval from an otherwise complete unit. Expected only `A_TEXT_COMPLETE` to change from 1 to 0; `D_REPRO` changes only if the two lane inventories now disagree or are invalid.
9. **Zero-unit NOACTION:** both lanes explicitly and independently return empty inventory. Expected `D_REPRO=1`, `A_TEXT_COMPLETE=NA`, `A_TEXT_GAP=NA`.
10. **Zero-unit unresolved:** one lane returns empty and the other `UNKNOWN`. Expected `D_REPRO=UNKNOWN`, not complete.
11. **DEFER unresolved:** an unresolved field in a DEFER packet. Expected `UNKNOWN` under the bridge and no recoding to complete or to clinical failure.
12. **Workflow absence:** complete explicit text but no bound evidence of viewing, signing, communication, execution, acceptance, scheduling, capacity, or follow-up. Expected `A_TEXT_COMPLETE=1`, `E_WORKFLOW_GAP=1`, `E_OUTCOME_GAP=1`; this is evidence limitation.
13. **Proxy trap:** add service, transfer, discharge-location, storetime, death, or order-like text after `D` without a bound workflow event. Expected no change to any observed workflow field.
14. **Contradiction:** two explicit units conflict on replacement or target. Expected `A_TEXT_COMPLETE=0`; the DOC/ACT matching state is adverse or unknown according to the valid/ambiguous rule, never silently resolved.
15. **Shared-wrong:** DOC and ACT agree on the same incorrect unit while a hidden gold fixture records the intended distinction. Expected the compatible-set implementation to retain the shared-wrong state; agreement alone must not narrow it away.
16. **Route-blinded invariance:** supply the same packet under different hidden route/atom/weight metadata. Expected byte-identical bridge labels and outputs.

## Falsification and gate criteria

The bridge is falsified or fails its computational gate if any reviewer receives route, atom, stratum, weight, parent label, or other outcome information before sealing; if DOC/ACT rosters overlap or seal ordering is broken; if a bridge field changes the frozen population, route, atom, sampling, chronology, adverse precedence, primary estimand, or workload; if canonical matching depends on reviewer order, free-form synonymy, or the other lane's output; if duplicates are collapsed; if a route-invariant formatting perturbation changes a result; if a prescribed one-field perturbation changes unrelated fields; if missing evidence is imputed as observed; if `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AVAILABLE_SOURCES` is reported as a clinical no; if marginal compatible extrema are combined from incompatible states; if a pooled result rescues an atom-level failure; or if any output calls `A_TEXT_COMPLETE` care delivery, appropriateness, safety, benefit, or follow-up.

The bridge endpoint is **supportive** only when at least one exact inherited atom has a finite, sufficiently narrow compatible-set bound for the joint `D_REPRO`/`A_TEXT_COMPLETE` matrix, the gap estimate is stable under independent interpreter and all predeclared controls, and the unavailable workflow fields are explicitly enumerated. Support means only that a prospective silent protocol can be made more clinically informative by collecting those named fields; it does not support deployment, safety, efficacy, causal benefit, or transport.

It is **adverse** when an atom has a substantial or wide documentation-to-text-actionability gap, valid DOC/ACT disagreement, unresolved unit identity, or a route-invariant control failure. The affected atom is not nominated for a prospective silent bridge unless the missing field and adverse-compatible handling are added to that future protocol. If all adverse-looking results are solely `E_WORKFLOW_GAP` caused by unavailable MIMIC event streams, report an evidence ceiling rather than clinical failure and do not alter the parent route.

It is **inconclusive** when challenge cases are insufficient, response or packet corruption is nontrivial, seals or rosters fail, timestamps or reciprocal links remain unresolved, compatible-set intervals are too wide, or an unavailable-versus-unknown classification cannot be audited. Inconclusive is neither evidence of safety nor evidence of non-actionability.

## Clinical consequence and required next evidence

A passing bridge makes the retrospective result more consequential by identifying whether the stored recommendation itself is sufficiently explicit to be transcribed into a prospective workflow specification, and by naming the exact missing observables that prevent a clinical actionability claim. It does not turn storage into workflow. A separately governed prospective silent study must collect event provenance/timestamps for actual viewing/availability, finalization/signature, communication, responsible-team acceptance, orders/referrals, scheduling or handoff, patient/caregiver preference and capacity, outside-plan visibility, and post-departure follow-up/outcomes. Expert clinical adjudication is needed for appropriateness and safety, and an outcome study is needed for harm, utility, or benefit.

No conclusion from this bridge may claim care delivery, correct routing, clinical appropriateness, safety, patient adherence, follow-up completion, benefit, causal effect, external validity, or temporal transport. The only permitted retrospective conclusion is about reproducibility of the frozen documentation representation, explicit text completeness, and the documented absence or uncertainty of the workflow/outcome evidence required for a later study.
