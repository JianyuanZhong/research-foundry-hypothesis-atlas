# Recovery-context mismatch after first ICU discharge: competing escalation and death pathways

## Scientific deliverable

The solver must newly fit and audit a leakage-safe MIMIC-IV 3.1 study of whether the clinical meaning of recorded support withdrawal depends on contemporaneous physiologic recovery at the first ICU outtime. The scientific output is an estimand and uncertainty package, not a leaderboard score.

Completion requires all of the following outputs:

1. `run_manifest.json`: catalog, source, snapshot and schema hashes; archive members; verified item labels; locked cohort, timing, bridge, tie, q and topology rules; software versions; and output hashes.
2. `cohort_audit.parquet`: one row per adult subject's earliest valid ICU stay, with eligibility, temporal-integrity, 12-hour-window, observation coverage, support-state, physiology-state and exclusion flags.
3. `hourly_features.parquet`: twelve left-closed one-hour bins in [outtime-12h, outtime), with chart-time physiology, support overlap, procedure corroboration, observation masks, source-channel indicators and no post-outtime predictors.
4. `recovery_context_ledger.parquet`: each half-window's respiratory and vasoactive support evidence, observed non-positive versus uninformative absence, q recodings, respiratory/hemodynamic instability burden, and the mutually exclusive concordant/mismatch phenotype.
5. `transfer_outcome_audit.parquet`: every later ICU candidate through the 48-hour/discharge boundary, every candidate bridge row, careunit topology, event ordering, and lower/upper-bound labels.
6. `bound_cif.parquet`: 6-, 24- and 48-hour cumulative-incidence functions for documented-bridge ICU return (L), any later ICU return (U), death, and alive discharge; pairwise compatible contrast intervals; bound widths; and the corresponding recovery-context contrasts.
7. Locked patient-level test predictions from the transparent competing-hazard baseline and the same-input observation-aware two-domain hidden semi-Markov alternative, with calibration, IPCW Brier scores, subject-bootstrap uncertainty, split stability and model-comparison outputs.
8. `falsification_outputs.parquet` and `interpretation_map.parquet`: every reported claim linked to a computed estimate, horizon, event count, overlap/ESS, 95% uncertainty interval, L/U bound width, sensitivity result and evidence limitation.

No fitted association, risk difference, calibration result or clinical conclusion is claimed in this proposal. Readiness and source checks establish packaging feasibility only.

## Unresolved clinical question

The parent evidence supports a narrower methodological claim: recorded support, observation intensity and transfer topology can be represented separately; documented non-ICU bridging is a lower-bound marker for a later ICU return, while any later ICU return is an upper-bound marker. The inherited feasibility audit found 52,591 eligible adult first outtimes, 2,661 later ICU returns within 48 hours, 2,060 with a qualifying bridge and 601 without one, including 294 returns with multiple bridge candidates. Those are feasibility/topology counts, not exposure associations and not evidence that any return was unplanned or preventable.

The unresolved clinical claim is:

> Among adults discharged from their first ICU stay, a recent recorded withdrawal of respiratory or vasoactive support is most clinically concerning when the corresponding respiratory or hemodynamic physiology remains unstable in the final six hours; this recovery-context mismatch has a different competing-event profile from concordant support withdrawal, with respiratory mismatch more return-dominant and hemodynamic mismatch more death-dominant.

This is an association hypothesis about the meaning of a documented transition, not a causal claim about stopping treatment. It is falsifiable if the mismatch groups have null/reversed compatible contrasts, if the pattern disappears after observation/process adjustment, or if it is not stable across outcome-topology and missingness assumptions.

The primary estimand is the standardized, absolute difference in first-event CIF at each of 6, 24 and 48 hours after outtime between each mismatch phenotype and concordant recovery. Report separately:

- L_h: documented non-ICU bridge followed by the first later ICU stay;
- U_h: any later ICU stay;
- D_h: in-hospital death before return;
- A_h: alive hospital discharge before return.

For the latent transition-compatible ICU-escalation event T_h, do not choose a midpoint. For phenotype groups A and B, report the nonparametrically compatible interval:

[
[L_{A,h}-U_{B,h}, U_{A,h}-L_{B,h}].
]

The primary directional test asks whether the lower/upper-compatible interval for respiratory mismatch versus concordant recovery is positive for ICU-return-compatible escalation and whether the death contrast is larger for hemodynamic mismatch than respiratory mismatch. A joint mismatch phenotype is prespecified but interpreted only when event support and positivity permit. The parent’s respiratory-versus-vasoactive withdrawal comparison remains a secondary decomposition: it asks whether the same mismatch pattern differs by domain, rather than treating all residual instability as one score.

A nonzero U contrast with a null or adverse L contrast is evidence only for an aggregate return association. It is adverse for the stronger claim that the phenotype marks a documented post-ICU escalation pathway, because direct ICU-to-ICU movement or an unobserved route may be mixed into U. A larger death contrast does not establish treatment limitation, delayed rescue, or discharge error.

## Recovery-context phenotype

Construct domain support evidence separately in the preceding half [outtime-12h, outtime-6h) and final half [outtime-6h, outtime):

- Respiratory-positive evidence: valid `icu/chartevents` O2 flow >2 L/min (item 223834), FiO2 >0.21 (223835), PEEP >0 (220339), or a corroborating intubation/extubation/NIV procedure event. Procedures corroborate a transition only and are never treated as continuous ventilation.
- Vasoactive-positive evidence: valid overlap in `icu/inputevents` for norepinephrine 221906, epinephrine 221289/229617, dopamine 221662, phenylephrine 221749/229630/229631/229632, or vasopressin 222315.

For each domain and half, classify positive evidence, observed non-positive evidence, uninformative absence, contradiction, or invalid evidence. Require at least two respiratory and two MAP observation bins per half for the primary complete-observation population. Recode only uninformative absence over q in {0, .10, .25, .50, .75, 1.00}; never recode observed non-positive values. Retain inadequate, active and discordant strata in the audit and report them rather than silently excluding them.

Define a domain as “recently withdrawn” when it is positive in the preceding half and has no positive evidence in the final half, subject to the q scenario and adequate evidence rule. “Durable recorded-off” requires no positive evidence in either half with adequate evidence in both halves. The exposure ledger stores domain-specific status rather than collapsing missingness into off.

Define final-six-hour physiology from chart-time bins:

- respiratory instability burden is the fraction of valid final-half hourly bins with SpO2 <90% (220277) or respiratory rate >=30/min (220210);
- hemodynamic instability burden is the fraction of valid final-half hourly bins with MAP <65 mmHg, using arterial MAP 220052 and non-invasive MAP 220181;
- a bin is invalid for the relevant burden if no usable measurement is present; report the denominator and do not count missing as stable.

The primary mismatch classification is mutually exclusive and hierarchical:

1. concordant recovery: both domains are durable recorded-off or recently withdrawn, with no corresponding final-half instability burden above the locked threshold;
2. respiratory mismatch: respiratory support is recently withdrawn or durable-off but respiratory instability remains, without hemodynamic mismatch;
3. hemodynamic mismatch: vasoactive support is recently withdrawn or durable-off but hemodynamic instability remains, without respiratory mismatch;
4. multisystem mismatch: both corresponding burdens remain unstable;
5. active/discordant/uninformative or otherwise unclassifiable.

The threshold is locked before test evaluation as more than one of the six final-half bins abnormal, with sensitivity thresholds of any abnormal bin and more than two bins. The primary analysis also reports the continuous burden interaction to avoid making a single cutoff carry the entire inference. Support-transition status (durable-off, recent respiratory withdrawal, recent vasoactive cessation, joint recent withdrawal) is crossed with mismatch status only as a prespecified secondary analysis; sparse cells are not merged post hoc.

This phenotype is a proxy for recorded treatment/documentation and physiology. It does not prove that a clinician intentionally stopped support, that support was truly absent, or that the patient was ready for discharge. The q grid, source-channel masks and topology variants are part of the estimand's uncertainty rather than nuisance details.

## Analysis plan

### Directly estimable baseline

Fit pooled one-hour cause-specific discrete-time hazards for L return, U return, death, and alive discharge, with a separate aggregate-return model. The baseline uses the same recovery-context phenotype, physiology summaries, support ledger, masks, observation/process variables, admission context, age, gender, anchor-year group, ICU LOS and prespecified support-by-mismatch interactions. Use nested models:

- physiology/support only;
- observation/mask/process only;
- full prespecified model.

The baseline is transparent, auditable and directly standardized to the common primary population. It loses the order and duration of transitions, but directly estimates observable event CIFs and preserves the L/U partial-identification logic. Do not use a generic prediction score as the primary result.

Standardize test predictions to the primary complete-observation population. Report group-specific CIFs and absolute contrasts at 6/24/48 hours, death and discharge CIFs, L/U compatible intervals, calibration intercept/slope, IPCW Brier score, event counts, overlap, positivity and effective sample size. Use subject-level bootstrap intervals, retaining the bound operation inside each bootstrap replicate, and repeat fixed split seeds 17/29/43.

### Scientifically informative alternative

Fit an observation-aware, factorized two-domain hidden semi-Markov model (HSMM) on the same 12 hourly bins and exact source ledger. Respiratory and hemodynamic states have explicit duration/persistence and withdrawal transitions; emissions use the same physiology and support variables; a separate observation model uses masks, row counts and source-channel availability. The post-landmark component has ICU, documented non-ICU and unknown-location topology states, with observable hazards aligned to L, U, death and discharge.

Train/fits states, durations and regularization on train/validation only. Evaluate locked test outputs over the declared q and transfer-capture/false-bridge sensitivity grid. The model may estimate a latent transition-compatible hazard only as a model-based sensitivity result; it must be clipped/rejected if outside [L,U], and its latent state is not called truth.

This alternative can reveal whether mismatch is a short-lived recording artifact or a persistent, ordered, domain-specific state; whether respiratory and hemodynamic discordance have distinct durations; and whether observation intensity explains the apparent phenotype. The baseline's fixed summaries lose exactly that sequence/duration information. Agreement between methods is stronger evidence that the finding is not a representation artifact, but disagreement is scientifically informative and must be reported rather than resolved by selecting the favorable model.

Use CPU for extraction, ledger construction, baseline fitting and bootstrap. The future solver envelope is up to 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB RAM, 8 allocated GPUs and 28,800 seconds; the discovery budget is 7,200 science seconds. A single allocated A100 may be used for repeated HSMM optimization only after a bounded profile demonstrates benefit, explicitly using `cuda:0` inside the allocation. The 1–4 hour HSMM estimate is unverified; the inherited 10–30 minute extraction and 30–60 minute baseline/bounds estimates are also planning estimates, whereas the source/header/item audit and inherited topology counts are measured. No GPU is required by the scientific question, and no proposer/solver weight training is authorized.

## Falsification and sensitivity

Pre-register the following checks and preserve all outputs:

- timestamp and subject-leakage audit; no predictor after outtime and no row-level split;
- within-window time permutation of support/physiology and a landmark-shift negative control;
- `storetime` negative control: it may diagnose delayed documentation but must not carry the clinical signal;
- mask/process-only, physiology/support-only, and full-model comparisons;
- remove procedure corroboration, then analyze each respiratory channel separately;
- q grid, observation-coverage thresholds, instability thresholds, 6/12-hour windows, tie tolerances, bridge-duration rules, careunit-only/interval bridge rules, and ICU-unit matching;
- transfer-careunit permutation and a +6-hour transfer-time shift;
- pre-landmark pseudo-return/topology negative control;
- discharge-as-censoring versus discharge-as-competing-event sensitivity;
- first-careunit, admission-type, anchor-year, ICU-LOS and transfer-density strata;
- positivity/ESS and test event counts for every phenotype/horizon;
- HSMM latent-state entropy, duration stability and whether every latent output remains within the L/U envelope.

The hypothesized hierarchy is falsified if the respiratory-versus-hemodynamic mismatch contrast is null or reversed in the worst-case compatible interval, if it is unstable at 6/24/48 hours, if it collapses after observation/process adjustment, if it depends on one chart channel or one bridge rule, or if transfer-topology permutation preserves it. If only U is positive while L is null, reject the stronger documented-escalation interpretation. If a signal survives time shuffling, storetime substitution or transfer permutation, treat it as measurement/topology confounding rather than supportive clinical evidence.

Supportive evidence requires adequate events and overlap, a prespecified directional compatible interval with a subject-bootstrap 95% interval excluding zero, stability across q and topology frontiers, baseline/HSMM agreement on the observable outcomes, and disappearance under appropriate permutation controls. Even then, the conclusion is a retrospective association useful for monitoring prioritization and discharge-review hypothesis generation; a very small nonzero risk difference does not establish clinical utility.

Adverse evidence includes reversal after q or observation adjustment, a U-only signal, dependence on a single support channel, persistence under permutation, non-positivity, or latent estimates outside [L,U]. Inconclusive evidence includes wide compatible intervals, sparse mismatch strata, unstable bounds, large L/U widths, high latent entropy, poor overlap, or method disagreement without a prespecified adjudication rule.

## Clinical advance and unavailable evidence

The expert MIMIC-10 seed asks whether residual oxygen need or fluctuating vital signs predict ICU return/death and explicitly flags treatment limitation, destination, and competing death. This child advances it by defining the decision-relevant mismatch between recorded support transition and contemporaneous physiology, by separating return-compatible escalation from death, and by retaining topology/missingness uncertainty. The MIMIC-01 asynchronous-recovery seed is a plausible alternative, but a kidney-specific study would require verified creatinine/urine-output recovery definitions and would change the primary question; it remains deferred for this child. MIMIC-07 antimicrobial de-escalation is deferred because indication, source control, result-availability timing and treatment intent are not adequately adjudicated here.

The three research-ambition demonstrations informed the design only as methodological examples. The natural-history sequence work supports comparing a dated-sequence representation with an auditable baseline; this is a bounded adaptation, not a reproduction, and its Danish/genetic/lifetime claims are unavailable here. The Bayesian longitudinal work motivates an observation-aware latent trajectory adaptation; its genetic and multi-biobank validation inputs are unavailable. For the cancer demonstration, only the accessible supplement and metadata were inspected; the main article and full STAR Methods were unavailable, so no stronger main-paper method claim is made and chest-X-ray/image reproduction is deferred.

Essential unavailable evidence includes clinician intent, treatment-limitation orders/goals of care, explicit ICU-discharge readiness, ward/intermediate-care monitoring, oxygen-device detail and bedside adjudication of deterioration, reason for ICU return, planned versus unplanned transfer, source control, raw waveform data, MIMIC-CXR images, external validation, and causal treatment assignments. MIMIC timestamps are deidentified subject-specific shifts; within-subject intervals are usable, but cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid. The local text extraction is an unvalidated bilingual lexical-context detector for cirrhosis only, so narrative text is not used to infer these unavailable clinical concepts.

The study cannot establish causality, discharge appropriateness, preventability, surveillance adequacy, treatment limitation, or a discharge/monitoring policy. Those stronger conclusions require expert adjudication, better transfer and ward-monitoring evidence, external validation, and preferably a prospective or causal study. Evidence that would justify revisiting deferred alternatives is a future snapshot with validated creatinine/urine-output item mappings and sufficient kidney-recovery events for MIMIC-01, or adjudicated antimicrobial indication/source-control and result-availability timestamps for MIMIC-07. New imaging/waveform or ward-monitoring data would justify a separate modality/transition study, not silent expansion of this one.
