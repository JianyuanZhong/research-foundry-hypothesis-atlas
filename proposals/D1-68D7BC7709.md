The remaining estimand problem is real and has two parts.

For patient i, let n_i be the number of expected report units in the eligible preoperative window, m_i the number whose exact whole accession is visible under a permissible mask, and r_u in {0,1} the unit visibility bit. The former patient-balanced mean is

C_PB = (1/N) sum_i 1(n_i>0)m_i/n_i.

Any-report reach is

A_reach = (1/N) sum_i 1(m_i>0).

Since m_i/n_i <= 1(m_i>0) for every patient with n_i>0, and both are zero when n_i=0, C_PB <= A_reach. Thus C_PB >= 0.80 already implies A_reach >= 0.80. A same-threshold reach-and-mean-completeness gate is mathematically redundant. It can be reported for clinical readability, but it cannot resolve a new availability requirement.

The complete-packet coordinate

C_all = (1/N) sum_i 1(n_i>0 and m_i=n_i)

is not redundant. It distinguishes a patient with one of two visible reports from a patient with all reports visible, but as a binary gate it puts every incomplete patient in one bucket and hides whether the missing burden is one of two reports or 204 of 205. The proposed replacement is therefore a disjoint packet profile plus three predeclared, nested service routes. No scalar mean is used as the primary availability contract.

The unresolved clinical-research hypothesis is:

> The fixed-capacity semantic documentary-M2 advantage of G over its locked acquisition-only, report-surface and same-fitted semantic-mask comparators has a highest robust report-service level that can be identified from disjoint patient packet states: complete packet, high partial packet, or any report. This level, if any, remains valid in each of 2020 and 2021 across every coherent source world, every global reader-book pair, every admissible whole-accession mask at that level, and before and after anchored deletion of any one test patient.

The hypothesis is falsified at a route if any required route-admissible mask, source world, reader pair, year, or anchored deletion has a contrast of at most five captured documentary-M2 cases per 100 fixed review positions. Equality at five fails. An empty route family is not a pass: it is a separately reported feasibility failure. A route can be called robust only after exact universal extrema are certified.

The 0.80 threshold and the high-partial cutoff below are prespecified evidence-service design criteria, not safety thresholds or standards supported by this HCC source. The experiment asks which report-retrieval investment can support a finite-corpus documentary robustness claim; it does not test whether a clinician saw a report, whether a report was timely, biological M2, treatment selection, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or patient benefit.

## Why this changes an evidence-investment decision

The source supports a clinically consequential choice between three evidence-investment pathways:

1. complete-packet archival/crosswalk work, where every eligible report unit for at least 80% of patients is available;
2. high-partial retrieval work, where at least 80% of patients have at least 80% of their expected report units, but some remain incomplete;
3. minimum-reach retrieval work, where at least one eligible report is available for at least 80% of patients.

These are not asserted to be clinically interchangeable. The profile makes their differences explicit. A complete-packet robust result supports investing in a full version/readability census before a silent workflow study. A high-partial-only result says that full-packet assurance is not supported but a near-complete retrieval experiment is the most defensible next data investment. An any-report-only result supports only a lower-cost exploratory data-retrieval study, with missing-report burden treated as unresolved. No route can authorize deployment or clinical action.

## Packet profile and exact route families

For a full eligible roster I(omega), N=|I|. Let E_i(omega) contain every temporally eligible exact-deduplicated CT/MRI unit, including nonblank accession units and retained blank pseudo-units, before semantic-source filtering. Let n_i=|E_i| and m_i(r)=sum over u in E_i of r_u. Source-unavailable or externally unproved units have r_u forced to zero. A patient with n_i=0 remains in N and is structurally uncovered.

Define five mutually exclusive, exhaustive patient states:

- S0, structurally uncovered: n_i=0;
- S1, unreached: n_i>0 and m_i=0;
- S2, low partial: n_i>0, 0<m_i<n_i and 5m_i<4n_i;
- S3, high partial: n_i>0, 0<m_i<n_i and 5m_i>=4n_i;
- S4, complete packet: n_i>0 and m_i=n_i.

The high-partial boundary is exact: equality at four-fifths is S3. A patient with one of two units and a patient with one of 205 units are both partial, but their exact m_i/n_i values and remediation burdens are retained. The profile is

p_k(r,omega) = (1/N) sum_i 1(S_k(i,r,omega)), k=0,...,4,

and the verifier must assert sum_k p_k=1. The profile, including the low/high partial split, is a primary output for every route witness, not a post-hoc narrative.

For each mask r, define:
- f_i=1(S4);
- h_i=1(S3);
- q_i=1(m_i>0), equivalently S2, S3 or S4;
- a_i=1(n_i>0 and m_i=n_i), equivalently S4.

Define three exact nested availability families, each restricted by r<=r_max(omega), where r_max exposes all and only externally proved releasable units:

- complete-packet family F_C(omega) = {r: 5 sum_i f_i >= 4N};
- high-partial family F_H(omega) = {r: 5 sum_i(f_i+h_i) >= 4N};
- reach family F_R(omega) = {r: 5 sum_i q_i >= 4N}.

These families are nested F_C subset F_H subset F_R. They are not interchangeable. F_C requires complete report packets for at least 80% of the all-roster patients; F_H requires at least 80% of patients to have at least 80% of their expected units, either complete or high-partial; F_R requires at least one exact whole report for at least 80% of patients. Patients with no expected unit, blank pseudo-units or forced-unavailable units cannot receive credit unless they satisfy the relevant exact conditions. No family is conditioned on report-bearing patients.

The former C_PB, A_reach and accession-weighted coverage are recomputed on identical masks as secondary diagnostics. The verifier must assert C_all <= C_PB <= A_reach and must not call these quantities equivalent. The old dual C_PB/reach gate is removed from the primary contract. A mean fraction cannot rescue a failing packet-profile route, and a complete-packet pass cannot erase the low/high partial distribution.

## Exact falsification and one-hot result

For each route L in {C,H,R}, year, world, global book pair, coordinate and deletion, enumerate or certify the minimum margin

M_L = min_{admissible source state, r in F_L, coordinate, deletion} 100/K [captures_G - captures_comparator].

A route is availability-feasible only if its family is nonempty in every required world. A single admissible failure at or below five is adverse for that route; equality fails. Use the same world and mask across comparator arms. Define C_L as route L is feasible and robust. Define V_L as route L is feasible but adverse. Define I_L as route L is infeasible in at least one required world. The primary result is exactly one of:

1. inconclusive: Q is false or required route extrema cannot be certified;

## Interpretation and evidence limits

Complete-packet robust means that, within the frozen finite corpus and externally proven version/readability universe, every allowed mask giving complete packets to at least 80% of all eligible patients preserves every locked fixed-K documentary-M2 margin through anchored singleton deletion. It supports prioritizing full-packet archive/crosswalk evidence and a prospective silent workflow study. It does not prove report timeliness, clinician viewing, biological M2, benefit or deployment readiness.

High-partial robust only means the complete-packet route was not robust, but every mask giving at least 80% of patients at least 80% of expected units was robust. It identifies a near-complete evidence-investment route; it does not claim that missing reports are clinically irrelevant or that any particular missing component is dispensable. The p2/p3/p4 profile must be used to plan the next study.

Any-report robust only means the two stronger routes were not robust, but every mask giving at least one exact report to at least 80% of patients was robust. This is a minimum-reach documentary result. It does not establish that one report is clinically sufficient, and it cannot justify a complete-packet or clinical-service claim.

No 80%-route feasible means the specified 80% service criteria cannot be met in at least one source world under the available external version evidence. It does not prove reports were late or that the data are clinically unusable. No tested route robust means availability can be attained for at least one route but the universal documentary-M2 margin claim fails for every tested route; it does not prove radiology is useless, semantics harmful, or patient outcomes worsened. Inconclusive assigns no scientific direction.

Dictionary correctness, Chinese semantic truth, reader qualification, operative identity, pathology specimen linkage and sampling, archive completeness, report finalization/readability, clinician view, workload, action, treatment effect, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, fairness and patient benefit require expert adjudication, missing source-system evidence, stakeholder review, prospective silent-mode workflow measurement, external cohorts or another study. The reserved catalog partition is inaccessible and is not external validation. The three configured MIMIC, eICU and UKB datasets remain unpooled because no patient crosswalk or compatible first-resection/eventual-Chinese-report/untimed-pathology documentary-M2 frame exists.
