# Early Post-TACE Hepatic Deterioration as a Prognostic Marker in HCC

## Question
In patients with hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) who undergo transarterial chemoembolization (TACE), does a composite measure of early hepatic deterioration during the first 7 days after the procedure predict subsequent hepatic decompensation, treatment failure, or death within 90 days, after adjustment for baseline liver function, tumor burden, and post-procedure observation intensity?

## Clinical importance
TACE is a common locoregional treatment for HCC, but a subset of patients experience early hepatic deterioration that may signal a need for escalation to systemic therapy or closer surveillance. Identifying these patients could inform treatment-selection decisions and resource allocation. The unresolved question is whether the early laboratory trajectory itself carries prognostic information beyond baseline liver function, or whether it merely reflects more intensive post-procedure monitoring.

## Evidence and closest prior work
- [K1] Decraecker et al. (2026) review the limitations of Child-Pugh scoring and the potential value of dynamic liver function monitoring and ALBI grade in HCC patients.
- [K2] The ALBI grade has been validated as a prognostic marker in HCC, but it is a static measure and does not capture early post-treatment changes.
- [K3] TACE outcomes are influenced by baseline liver function, tumor burden, and post-procedure complications, but the prognostic value of early hepatic deterioration is not well characterized.

## Design
### Population
Patients with HCC who undergo TACE, with at least one laboratory measurement within 7 days after the procedure.

### Temporal boundaries
- Baseline: pre-TACE laboratory values and tumor burden.
- Early window: first 7 days after TACE (sensitivity: 3-day, 5-day, 10-day).
- Outcome window: 90 days after TACE.

### Variables
- Early hepatic deterioration composite: bilirubin, albumin, INR (ALT/AST excluded due to zero early rows).
- Baseline covariates: liver function, tumor burden, post-procedure observation intensity.
- Outcomes: hepatic decompensation, treatment failure, death within 90 days.

### Analysis
- Baseline: logistic regression with baseline covariates.
- Alternative: time-aware survival model with the early hepatic deterioration composite.
- Sensitivity: 3-day, 5-day, 10-day windows.
- Decision-curve analysis for clinically actionable treatment escalation.

### Falsification
A null or adverse association after adjustment would falsify the hypothesis.

## Prognostic vs. causal distinction
The study estimates a prognostic association and does not establish a causal treatment effect.

## Sensitivity analysis
The 7-day window is tested with 3-day, 5-day, and 10-day alternatives.

## Decision-curve analysis
The composite is evaluated for clinically actionable treatment escalation.
