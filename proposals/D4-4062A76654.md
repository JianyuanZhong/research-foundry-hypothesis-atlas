# UK Biobank metabolic progression: executable confirmation opportunity and source-canonical change

## Child purpose and scientific deliverable

This draft is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`, the assigned assessed-valid UKB parent. It preserves the clinically meaningful question—whether assessment-linked change in HbA1c, triglycerides, and HDL adds prognostic information beyond current levels for progression from one cardiometabolic domain to another—and repairs the remaining estimand ambiguity around confirmation.

The repair is strict:

- a diagnosis date is an event date, never a last-contact date;
- the cohort-wide maximum diagnosis/death date (`C_source`) is descriptive only;
- an alive participant with no later code is never labelled “no confirmation” unless a directly supported participant-level observation/closure basis covers the required window;
- unsupported alive episodes are `OPPORTUNITY_UNKNOWN`/right-censored and are displayed, not converted to negatives;
- robust confirmation claims are conditional on the directly opportunity-supported subset. If that subset is empty or too small, E1 may still be evaluated but robust E2 is inconclusive.

The future solver must newly fit, estimate, or construct:

1. a frozen five-source header, source-hash, duplicate-`eid`, overlap, and overlapping-field-agreement audit;
2. a deterministic d0/d1 population and exposure file with exact code/date pairing and source roles;
3. participant-domain event records for first additional-domain recording, confirmation, death, and opportunity status;
4. matched baseline and mechanistic change models under identical splits, preprocessing, censoring, and locked evaluation;
5. an opportunity-stratified E2 table and, only when supported, a competing-risk confirmation estimate;
6. participant-level uncertainty, sensitivity/falsification outputs, and an output-linked conclusion classified as supportive, adverse, or inconclusive.

A readiness check, nonzero coefficient, or improved metric is not completion and no scientific result is claimed in this design handoff.

## Unresolved question, strongest supported claim, and hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the available evidence is narrower: baseline metabolic indices and coded disease histories can be associated with later coded cardiometabolic disease, and structured survival/state models can describe administrative progression. The available evidence does not establish that a dated within-person change between two assessment-linked UKB panels adds information beyond the later current levels, nor that a first administrative code is persistent clinical disease.

The unresolved hypothesis is:

> Among UK Biobank participants aged 40–69 with exactly one established coded diabetes, cardiovascular-disease, or chronic-kidney-disease domain at each of two valid assessment-linked panels, does annualized change in HbA1c, triglycerides, and HDL add route-specific prognostic information beyond d1 levels, existing domain, disease duration, and prespecified covariates for a first subsequent additional coded domain; and, among episodes with directly supported opportunity for observation, does the first additional-domain code receive a later same-domain confirmation 30–365 days later?

This is a conditional internal prognostic hypothesis in the observed repeat-assessment and assay-complete population. It is not a causal effect of changing a biomarker, a biological slope, adjudicated onset, proof of independent clinical encounters, treatment benefit, or a surveillance-policy threshold.

The primary estimand is E1: d1-origin cumulative incidence of the first valid additional coded domain, with competing death and simultaneous-domain ties represented explicitly. E2 is a robustness estimand: after a first additional-domain code, cumulative incidence of a later distinct same-domain code 30–365 days later, with death competing and unsupported observation opportunity reported as unknown. E2 cannot be interpreted as a population incidence of confirmed disease unless direct opportunity is established for the relevant non-confirmed episodes.

The expert UKB-09 seed motivates this question but is untested and cautions that two measurements cannot identify a turning point. This design retains that caveat: the changes are assessment-linked contrasts, not observed biological trajectories.

## Direct source inspection and data construction

A bounded source inspection read the first five rows of each exact CSV header and the first 20,000 rows of `health_outcomes`. All five sources began with the same sampled eids and required fields. In the outcome probe, 272,021 ICD-10 code/date cells were paired and ISO-date-valid, 72 cells had a code without a date, and 14,204 of 20,000 rows had non-chronological date order. This is an availability/logic audit, not a cohort estimate; it is why every code/date suffix is scanned rather than assuming array order. The full solver must repeat this audit over all rows.

Normalize codes without a global negative-code recoding rule:

- diabetes: ICD-10 E10–E14 or ICD-9 250;
- cardiovascular disease: ICD-10 I20–I25, I50, I60–I69 or ICD-9 410–414, 428, 430–438;
- chronic kidney disease: ICD-10 N18/N19 or ICD-9 585;
- negative control: ICD-10 K35 or ICD-9 540.

A valid event requires a non-missing code and date in the same suffix/position. Preserve code-only, date-only, invalid-date, duplicate-date, pre-landmark, same-day, and simultaneous-domain records in an audit. Do not infer a date from another slot.

Define d0 = `assessment.53-0.0` and d1 = `assessment.53-1.0`. Eligibility is:

- age 40–69 inclusive at recruitment, valid sex, and `eid` present in all five sources;
- valid d0 and d1 with d0 < d1 and 365–3,650 days between them;
- normalized agreement of `main` and `assessment` date copies where both are present;
- exactly one target domain with a first valid paired date no later than d0, and still exactly one target domain by d1;
- no death on or before d1;
- finite canonical HbA1c, HDL, and triglycerides at biological-samples instances 0 and 1.

The primary time origin is d1. The existing domain duration is (d1 minus its first valid paired date)/365.25. Participants who acquire an additional domain between d0 and d1 are excluded from the primary stable-domain cohort and may appear only in a labelled secondary flow.

Use d1 assay levels and annualized changes over the assessment-date gap: (x1−x0)/((d1−d0)/365.25). Reverse HDL before change construction so larger change means worsening. These are not specimen-time slopes. Fit-only transformations, missingness indicators, and standardization are frozen in fit data and then applied unchanged.

## Executable E1 and opportunity-safe E2

### E1: first recorded additional domain

For each participant, scan every valid paired code/date after d1 for each absent domain. The earliest date for each domain is its first recorded event. The earliest domain across absent domains is E1. If multiple absent domains share that earliest date, emit `SECOND_DOMAIN_MULTI` and its member set; do not assign a route. If death is on the same date, death takes precedence in the primary analysis, with reverse precedence sensitivity. Death is absorbing and competing. Pending future first codes remain in the d1-origin risk set; entry is never conditioned on eventual t1.

### E2: opportunity-supported confirmation

For each E1 domain episode k with first additional-domain date t1k, define the confirmation horizon Hk = t1k + 365 days. The first later distinct date t2k for the same domain satisfying 30 ≤ t2k−t1k ≤ 365 is a confirmation. Multiple slots on one calendar date count as one date, not independent encounters. A participant-level E2 event is the earliest qualifying confirmation across absent domains; same-date confirmations are `CONFIRMED_MULTI`.

For every t1k episode, emit these mutually exclusive audit states:

1. `CONFIRMED`: a valid same-domain t2k is actually present in the paired outcome arrays before Hk. This is an observed positive event and does not require inventing a last-contact date.
2. `DEATH_BEFORE_CONFIRMATION`: a death date occurs after t1k and before any qualifying t2k, with death precedence for same-day ties. Death is a competing transition, not an ordinary no-confirmation censor.
3. `NO_CONFIRMATION_SUPPORTED_OPPORTUNITY`: no qualifying t2k is present and an independently documented participant-level observation/closure basis covers Hk.
4. `OPPORTUNITY_UNKNOWN`: no t2k is present but the required observation basis cannot be established. This includes an alive episode ending at a diagnosis date, an episode after the descriptive source maximum, and any episode for which only the global source boundary is available.

`C_source`, defined as the descriptive maximum finite date among diagnosis/date/death fields, may be emitted for audit and boundary plots only. It is never an individual last-observation date. A participant’s last diagnosis date is never used as last contact. The current metadata provides no per-person last-contact field, temporal-column declaration, source-release closure manifest, or validated administrative cutoff, so many or all alive no-confirmation episodes may remain `OPPORTUNITY_UNKNOWN`.

Death after Hk may be retained as a separately labelled “death-observed-after-window” diagnostic, but it does not silently prove complete diagnosis ascertainment through Hk; the solver must state the source evidence used before promoting it to supported opportunity. If a future catalog/source audit verifies an administrative cutoff or person-level follow-up field, it must be named, hashed, and frozen before use.

The primary E2 estimand is therefore conditional: among E1 episodes with directly supported complete opportunity, estimate the cumulative incidence of `CONFIRMED` by 365 days with death competing. Episodes with `OPPORTUNITY_UNKNOWN` are excluded from this conditional denominator and reported in the flow; they are not negatives. If there is no defensible supported-opportunity basis, E2 model performance and a robust-confirmation conclusion are inconclusive by design. Report nonparametric lower/upper descriptive bounds for the full E1 episode set: confirmed events as the lower count, and confirmed plus opportunity-unknown episodes as the upper possible count; do not call those bounds a fitted incidence estimate.

E2 predictions, when estimable, originate at d1 and are generated for the full d1 eligible cohort using the fitted state architecture; evaluation is restricted to the prespecified supported-opportunity subset and separately reports coverage/unknownness. The solver must not select participants at d1 using future t1/t2 or evaluate only people who eventually confirm.

## Matched baseline and substantive alternative

The same d1 cohort, event definitions, death precedence, opportunity rules, preprocessing, participant split, weights, uncertainty procedure, and locked test are mandatory for all comparisons.

**B0, the simple clinical baseline.** Fit route/state-specific cause-specific hazard models using d1 HbA1c, triglycerides, HDL, existing domain, disease duration, recruitment age, sex, d0–d1 gap, BMI, SBP, DBP, smoking, and assessment center. Prespecified restricted cubic splines may be used if fit and frozen in the training partition. B0 answers whether current metabolic state plus routine covariates is enough.

**M_common, mechanistic coordinated-change alternative.** Add a fit-standardized common deterioration score C from the three annualized changes (reverse-HDL change included), with a prespecified sign convention. C tests whether coherent multi-marker worsening adds information beyond current state.

**M_change, discordance extension.** Add two orthogonal, fit-only contrasts D1 and D2 spanning marker-specific discordance after removing the common change axis. The primary incremental contrast is M_change−B0; C−B0 asks whether coordinated change matters; M_change−M_common asks whether marker discordance reveals a clinically different route that current levels and common burden lose. These alternatives reveal trajectory information that B0 intentionally discards, and they test a substantive uncertainty about progression mechanism rather than merely seeking a small predictive gain.

A recurrent/attention sequence model is deferred for this question because only two verified assay panels are used: extra capacity cannot identify a turning point or resolve assessment-linked date proxies, selective repeat measurement, or confirmation opportunity. Reconsider it only with at least three independently verified dated panels and evidence that it adds information beyond C/D under the same opportunity-safe endpoint. A latent error model is also deferred because main and biological-samples copies are export duplicates, not independent replicates; assay QC or an external reliability model is required.

## Splitting, uncertainty, evaluation, and falsification

Hash participants, never rows, using SHA-256(namespace `ehr-hypothesis-discovery-v1`, dataset, eid) modulo 100: 0–59 fit, 60–69 model/threshold decisions, 70–79 locked test, and 80–99 reserved and unread until the primary analysis is frozen. All code rules, source selection, standardization, splines, weights, state simplification, opportunity promotion, and review thresholds are fit/tuning decisions.

Freeze a participant-level artifact containing d0/d1, gap, domain, duration, canonical assays and changes, split, all first-domain dates, E1 ties, t1/t2, death, `C_source`, opportunity evidence, E2 state, censoring, and provenance flags. It must be sufficient to recompute every table without rereading source rows.

Report:

- E1 and, if estimable, supported-opportunity E2 cumulative incidence with death competition at horizons justified by source evidence;
- route/state coefficients and intervals;
- integrated/time-dependent Brier score, calibration, discrimination, restricted mean event-free survival, and frozen 5%, 10%, and 20% risk operating-point summaries;
- paired 200-resample participant bootstrap intervals for C−B0, M_change−B0, and M_change−M_common;
- opportunity coverage, unknown fraction, lower/upper E2 descriptive bounds, and effective sample size/positivity for any weighting;
- source agreement, repeat-assessment/assay completeness flow, simultaneous-event and death-tie audits.

Mandatory falsification and sensitivity checks are d1+365 delayed entry; 0-, 2-, and 5-year outcome lags where supported; alternate d0–d1 gaps; alternate domain definitions; within-stratum permutation of change vectors; ICD-10 K35/ICD-9 540 negative control; source-concordant and main-only assay analyses; E1 versus E2; reversed death precedence; duplicate/same-date confirmation handling; opportunity-stratified tables; and an assertion that no `OPPORTUNITY_UNKNOWN` is coded as `NO_CONFIRMATION_SUPPORTED_OPPORTUNITY`, no death while pending is ordinary censoring, and no E2 evaluation set is formed by future confirmation.

Supportive evidence requires at least 20 E1 events overall and 5 locked-test events; route claims require at least 10 fit/tuning and 3 locked-test events in that route/state. It also requires stable joins/ties, adequate positivity and effective sample size, a worsening-direction interval excluding the null for a prespecified incremental contrast, and a locked paired improvement in Brier/calibration or at least 10% relative net benefit at a frozen threshold. The finding must persist under source-concordance, completeness, and lag sensitivities and show no comparable negative-control or within-stratum permutation signal. E2 support additionally requires a non-trivial directly supported-opportunity sample, an opportunity-coverage audit, and consistency between the conditional estimate and the opportunity-stratified/boundary reports. E1-only support is explicitly not robust-confirmation support.

Adverse evidence is no incremental information on a supported endpoint, worse locked performance, reversal or disappearance after duration/selection/source adjustment, a negative-control/permutation signal, or an E1 improvement that disappears in the opportunity-supported E2 analysis. Inconclusive evidence is sparse events/routes, failed positivity, unstable joins/ties, unverified opportunity, substantial `OPPORTUNITY_UNKNOWN`, poor convergence/calibration, intervals spanning meaningful benefit and no benefit, materially divergent timing/selection/phenotype sensitivities, or no defensible E2 supported-opportunity denominator. Inconclusive does not mean metabolic biology is absent.

This is fixed tabular ingestion, survival/state fitting, and 200 participant bootstrap resamples, so CPU-first is scientifically proportionate; a GPU is not required and GPU use would not establish clinical value. The time/resource estimate is planned rather than measured; only the bounded source probe is measured. An analytic/sandwich interval is an explicitly disclosed fallback if the declared bootstrap cannot fit, not permission to drop the opportunity audit or shrink the population.

## Evidence limits and next study

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes and headers, join/duplicate audits, code/date pairing, panel flow, event/tie logic, opportunity classification, model fitting, locked predictions, uncertainty, and falsification outputs. The dataset cannot establish clinical onset, independent encounters, code validity, treatment/adherence, causal effects, monitoring benefit, nonreturner generalization, transportability, or policy utility. It has no verified raw imaging, waveform, narrative clinical text, sequence-variant bundle, or validated per-person last-contact/administrative-closure field in the catalog. Clinical adjudication of a sample of E1/E2 episodes, a verified outcome-source closure/last-contact record, and external or prospective validation would be required before treating a supportive result as a clinical surveillance strategy.

Even a supportive result is an internal coded-event prognostic finding. It would justify clinical adjudication and a prospective follow-up study, not a diagnosis or monitoring policy.
