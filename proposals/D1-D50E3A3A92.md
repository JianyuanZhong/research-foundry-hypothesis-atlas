# Residual instability before ICU-to-ward transfer: matched residual-null and two-stream attribution lock

Status: substantive analysis/falsification child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The measured structural null and deterministic split are inherited unchanged. No E0, outcome association, observed-minus-null contrast, worklist result, or learned outcome model has been fit.

## Decision and scientific claim boundary

The executed evidence now suffices to freeze the endpoint-linked Harbor experiment. It establishes that the exact cohort, role map, pre-outcome histories, and endpoint-fixed order perturbations are computable and nondegenerate; it does not support the outcome hypothesis.

The strongest supported claim is structural feasibility. The frozen cohort contains 49,157 first eligible transfers among 40,014 subjects. The deterministic outcome-by-destination subject split has no role crossing. Among raw-G rows, 99.43%–99.57% are effectively perturbed in each pooled or late-era role, compared with the 70% gate; median null support is 133–135 unique G values, median scaled null SD is 0.596–0.624, and the median materially moved fraction is 0.955. G-pre coverage remains above 70% in every required role. These results license endpoint-linked fitting but do not establish association, discrimination, calibration, order attribution, physiology, preventability, or benefit from changing transfer or monitoring.

The unresolved hypothesis remains: among ICU-to-general-ward transfers at the same measured endpoint state, unusually worsening rather than improving recent ordered measured-value history changes calibrated 48-hour ICU-return/death risk and fixed-capacity review ranking. The leading explanation is residual ordered measured-value information. The strongest rivals are mathematical coupling/regression to the mean from shared endpoint measurements and treatment/documentation processes encoded in the EHR.

The inspected literature supports the clinical relevance of longitudinal ICU-discharge prediction [K1], heterogeneous and bias-prone prior performance [K2], and healthcare-process signals as a consequential rival [K3]. None establishes this hypothesis.

## Matched two-stream L2-D alternative

The substantive method question is whether scalar G-perp loses nonlinear lag, opposing-domain patterns, or cross-domain timing that matter to the same fixed-capacity decision. It is not whether a neural model can obtain a small generic predictive gain.

Analysis set, labels, split, static E0 context, and evaluation roles are identical to D1-perp. The primary comparison uses the common raw-G-eligible set; broader L2-D coverage is secondary and cannot support the direction estimand.

Inputs are frozen as follows:

- Value stream: 12 two-hour bins over `[t0-24h,t0)` for the same six signed domain margins. Within a transfer/domain, causal forward fill is allowed only after the first observed bin; leading missing bins receive the pooled-development domain median. Values use pooled-development centers/scales. No mask, gap, count, caregiver, store-lag, or treatment channel enters this stream.
- Process stream: observed masks, time since last observation, counts, strict store-lag summaries, caregiver counts, and recorded ventilation/vasopressor/oxygen transitions on the same 12-bin grid. No measured value or abnormality margin enters this stream.
- Static stream: exactly the E0 context.

The model remains a two-layer causal TCN with kernel 3, dilations 1/2, channels {32,64}, dropout {0,0.2}, Adam learning rate {1e-3,3e-4}, batch size 256, at most 100 epochs, and validation multiclass log-loss early stopping with patience 10. For each of eight architectures, fit the full head with seeds 20260923–20260927 and select by mean validation log loss, breaking ties toward 32 channels, dropout 0, then learning rate 1e-3. For the selected architecture, retain the five full fits and fit value+static, process+static, and static-only heads with the same five seeds. One scalar temperature per head/seed is fit on validation logits. Thus the observed delivery contains 40 architecture-search full fits plus 15 selected-architecture ablation fits; selected full fits are reused, not duplicated.

For L2-D only, generate a second deterministic mapping family from the same 200 seed integers. Within each transfer/domain, permute all observed nonfinal positions among the 12 value bins, preserve the final observed value, exact value multiset, missing positions, process stream, static stream, split, and era, and use the same PCG64 source/target ordering. Write `l2_order_permutation_manifest.json` and input-level displacement diagnostics. Fit the selected architecture's full and value+static heads with seed 20260923 for the first 20 frozen null seeds: 40 bounded null fits. Process+static and static-only predictions are invariant and are not refit. An ineffective learned-input null makes only the learned order claim inconclusive; L2-D cannot rescue a failed transparent structural or inferential null.

Report on each common evaluation set:

- calibrated L2 full minus D1-perp Brier and fixed-quota capture;
- full minus process+static value contribution;
- value+static minus static-only value contribution;
- full/value observed minus corresponding 20-null median;
- five-seed between-fit dispersion and paired subject-bootstrap intervals;
- raw and adjudicated endpoints and both locked late-era roles.

AUROC and log loss are secondary diagnostics. Prefer L2-D only if calibration passes, all five seeds are stable under the inherited criterion, the Brier improvement over D1-perp has a positive paired interval in both late eras, at least one already supported quota gains >=2 R+D events/1,000 reviewed without overall capture loss, full beats process+static by the same material criteria, and observed full/value gains are not reproduced by the learned nulls. Otherwise retain D1-perp. A tiny predictive gain, process-only gain, or gain confined to one era does not justify the learned alternative.

## Quantitative process-rival rule

For each model and role define value-over-process Brier gain as
`Brier(process+static)-Brier(full)`
and quota gain as R+D captured by full minus process+static per 1,000 reviewed, using paired subject bootstraps.

Process reproduction is established for the learned comparison when the 95% upper bound of value-over-process Brier gain is <0.002 and every 2%/5%/10% quota upper bound is <2 events/1,000, while process+static reproduces the favorable calibration/ranking pattern. For the transparent comparison, the inherited no-support-change and observation-stable sensitivities remain primary because D1-perp has no process-only head.

Process-adverse requires absent or reversed measured-value evidence in both no-support-change and observation-stable rows and process reproduction by the rule above. It supports only a treatment/documentation-associated predictor. It does not identify clinician intent, a treatment effect, or bed/staffing pressure.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive requires all inherited support, calibration, endpoint, and transport rules; supportive raw D1; concordant D1-perp interaction with intervals excluding zero in pooled test and both late eras; positive matched observed-minus-median-null interaction and Brier intervals; >=2 additional R+D events/1,000 reviewed at a supported quota without overall capture loss; attenuating/opposing reversal; and either G-pre corroboration or persistence in both no-support-change and observation-stable rows. L2-D evidence, if claimed, must additionally pass the exact value-over-process, D1-perp, seed, era, and 12-bin null rules above. This supports residual ordered measured-value information and consequential re-ranking only.

Coupling-adverse requires a favorable raw D1 reproduced by the informative matched null: observed-minus-null interaction includes or favors zero, Brier advantage has a 95% upper bound <0.002, reversal does not attenuate, every quota upper bound is <2 events/1,000, and D1-perp excludes inherited material thresholds. This rejects the specified order attribution in favor of unordered repeated values plus endpoint coupling/mean reversion under this design. L2-D cannot rescue it.

Process-adverse follows the quantitative process-rival rule plus the inherited no-support-change and observation-stable sensitivities.

Inconclusive includes structural or inferential null failure, observed G-perp balance failure, learned-input null failure for an L2 claim, G-pre coverage <70%, intervals spanning material benefit and harm, unexplained raw/residual conflict, late-era support or calibration failure, endpoint disagreement, unstable L2-D seeds, or materially discordant conclusions under the numeric oxygen sensitivity. An imprecise null or G-pre null alone is not refutation.

No post-opening resplit, new subgroup, altered window, relaxed domain, changed permutation, changed nuisance family, or threshold search is allowed. Negative and process-adverse results are valid scientific outcomes.
