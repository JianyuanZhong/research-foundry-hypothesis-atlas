# A pre-label actionability and superseding-plan decision analysis for pulmonary discharge handoff

## Targeted repair and decision question

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It retains their frozen pulmonary discharge-handoff estimand and combines their strongest safeguards: prospective-contract packet replay, source-bound canonical serialization, temporal and terminal-service stress testing, pooled-domain masking prevention, route-concealed sealed review, outcome-blind controls, positive-probability whole-admission sampling, finite-population adverse inference, and a strict prospective-silent-only ceiling.

The unresolved bottleneck is not whether a pulmonary statement can be found in a stored note. It is whether the statement is sufficiently actionable, current, and non-superseded to support a documentation-level disposition among `AUTO`-candidate, clinician reconciliation (`RECON`), and `DEFER`, without mistaking a present sentence for an executable clinical plan. Existing checks show deterministic placement, storage-time availability, and packet serialization, but do not distinguish an explicit current plan from a historical recommendation, a plan superseded elsewhere in the admission, or an atom whose timing, recipient, or scope is too ambiguous for automatic forwarding.

### Falsifiable hypothesis

For each inherited first-eligible pulmonary opportunity, a source-bound atom can be assigned conservatively from information available by discharge:

* `AUTO-CANDIDATE` only when the exact target-linked atom is actionable under a pre-label rubric, has no unresolved superseding or competing plan, and passes every source, chronology, and serialization gate;
* `RECON` when the target-linked plan is potentially actionable but a qualified reviewer must reconcile a competing, superseding, or materially ambiguous plan; and
* `DEFER` when actionability cannot be established, source availability or chronology is inadequate, the atom is not current, or any safety-critical ambiguity remains.

The hypothesis is that this disposition can be reproduced with high agreement and low adverse unresolved rate across held-out `anchor_year_group` and terminal-service domains, while preserving the inherited exact atom and route. This tests documentation/workflow triage readiness only. `AUTO-CANDIDATE` is not permission to send care and is not evidence of delivery, viewing, responsibility, appropriateness, safety, completion, benefit, transport, or causality.

## Supported and untested claims

Available MIMIC evidence supports admission-linked notes, radiology report/addendum chronology, discharge-note storage timestamps, structured discharge-detail reconstruction, admission boundaries, and terminal-service joins. It supports a retrospective stored-documentation and simulated-packet estimand.

It does not establish delivery, viewing, assignment to a responsible person, interface rendering, acceptance by a patient or clinician, clinical appropriateness, safety, completion, benefit, outside care, scheduling, actual UI state, or patient preference. `curr_service` is a workflow-domain descriptor, not clinician ownership. `storetime` is a database-availability proxy, not a delivery/view timestamp. The newly tested claim is narrower: can blinded adjudicators decide whether documentation is sufficiently current and action-ready for each disposition, and can a fixed rule map that adjudication to `AUTO`-candidate, `RECON`, or `DEFER` without optimistic rescue?

## Packet construction and pre-label adjudication

Retain parent cutoffs `D-24h`, `D-6h`, `D-1h`, `D`, `D+1h`, `D+24h`; only `D` is the decision packet. A permitted row is visible iff non-null `storetime <= cutoff`; `charttime` never makes late text visible. Unavailable canonical DS at `D` yields `DS_UNAVAILABLE`, cannot yield `B=1` or `AUTO-CANDIDATE`, and later cutoffs never relabel D. Every record is append-only per `(hadm_id, atom_id, packet_cutoff, parser_version)`, with source IDs/times, cutoff, atom, inherited route, `B`, serialization, adjudication fields and disposition. Exact identity duplicates are idempotently deduplicated; altered text/ordinal conflicts are `DUPLICATE_CONFLICT` and fail closed. Missing time, malformed reconstruction, missing DS, non-identifiable source or parser ambiguity is `DEFER`.

Two independent qualified clinical reviewers see the source-bound D packet, atom provenance, and bounded local context, but not route, `B`, domain, prior decisions, post-D text, or outcomes. They review before route labels and B are opened. A third senior adjudicator sees only the first-review disagreement and resolves under the frozen rubric; no later evidence is allowed. Calibration examples are separate and cannot alter rules or labels. Reviewers record source note IDs and evidence spans as provenance only.

Each unit records explicit `UNKNOWN` where needed:

* `target_match`: exact, partial/near, absent, unknown;
* `action_presence`: `EXPLICIT_CURRENT_PLAN`, `EXPLICIT_CONDITIONAL_PLAN`, `HISTORICAL/COMPLETED`, `NEGATED`, `DISCUSSION_ONLY`, `ABSENT`, `UNKNOWN`;
* `action_scope`: target, modality/test, and interval/contingency explicit and atom-preserving: `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, `UNKNOWN`;
* `temporal_currentness`: current at D, explicitly future, completed/superseded, stale/unclear, unknown;
* `supersession_status`: `NONE_FOUND`, `EXPLICITLY_SUPERSEDES`, `CONFLICTING_CURRENT_PLAN`, `DUPLICATE_SAME_PLAN`, `UNKNOWN`;
* `operational_ambiguity`: none, recipient/scope/timing ambiguity, contradictory evidence, incomplete atom, source conflict, unknown;
* `actionability`: `ACTIONABLE`, `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`, `NOT_ACTIONABLE`, `UNKNOWN`.

The rubric does not ask whether the plan is medically correct, appropriate, safe, beneficial, completed, or likely to help. Clinical appropriateness is explicitly not assessed and cannot upgrade a documentation state. Reviewers must search every permitted report/addendum and canonical DS context under inherited chronology, not only the target sentence.

## Frozen disposition map

1. `AUTO-CANDIDATE` requires exact target match, `EXPLICIT_CURRENT_PLAN`, complete target/modality/interval atom (or an inherited documented no-interval policy), `NONE_FOUND` or `DUPLICATE_SAME_PLAN`, no source conflict/unknown, D availability, inherited `B=1`, exact serialization, and no all-unit adverse precedence.
2. `RECON` requires exact target and a potentially actionable plan, but a competing/superseding plan, conditional or incomplete scope, timing/recipient ambiguity, or reviewer disagreement remains resolvable by clinician reconciliation. It cannot be assigned merely because a reviewer doubts medical appropriateness.
3. `DEFER` is mandatory for absent/partial target, historical/completed, negated, discussion-only, not-actionable, unknown, missing/late DS/source, duplicate conflict, malformed time, unresolved disagreement, unresolved supersession, any unsafe/unresolved unit under worst-unit precedence, `B=0`, non-exact serialization, or any information-boundary failure. D+ text cannot rescue D.
4. Preserve inherited `TIMING` as a separate route flag. It cannot override DEFER or turn a future textual plan into delivery evidence. Route/actionability disagreement emits `ROUTE_ACTIONABILITY_CONFLICT` and DEFER; no vote or favorable choice.

`AUTO` is therefore only `AUTO-CANDIDATE` in this study: no sending, ordering, scheduling, or clinical recommendation occurs.

## Decision-analysis layer

Compare three fixed policies on the same sealed units: `P0 DEFAULT-DEFER` (all defer); `P1 DOCUMENTATION-AUTO` (fixed map permits AUTO only when all conditions pass, otherwise RECON/DEFER); `P2 CLINICIAN-RECON` (every potentially actionable exact atom not meeting AUTO goes to reconciliation, while missing/conflicting/unknown remains DEFER). Report finite-population admission- and unit-level counts/rates for AUTO, RECON, DEFER; target/control rates; units requiring reconciliation; median/IQR and maximum per-admission reconciliation burden; disagreement/unknown and supersession burden; and D-to-first-available latency. Workload is a documentation-review descriptor, not clinician time or a utility claim. For worst-unit admission reporting use `DEFER > RECON > AUTO`; also publish mutually exclusive unit counts.

Primary estimand: finite-population design-weighted proportion of target opportunities that are AUTO-candidates under the fixed map at D. Secondary: proportion classifiable as AUTO or RECON rather than DEFER; supersession/competing-plan rate; disagreement/unknown rate; and reconciliation workload. Use inherited phase inclusion probabilities, HT totals, Hájek ratios, exact finite-population design variance where supported, and nested phase resampling. Report sampled/census denominators, effective sample size, two-sided 95% design intervals, missingness and admission-level adverse results. No post-label reweighting, imputation, model, or threshold tuning.

## Controls and fixed gates

In addition to parent controls, run: (1) supersession-direction reversal, which must flag impossible chronology or DEFER and never AUTO; (2) currentness ablation, where removing currentness cues must reduce AUTO to RECON/DEFER; (3) synthetic competing-plan injection, which must produce RECON/DEFER, never AUTO; (4) exact-vs-altered duplicate control, where altered identity content is conflict/DEFER; (5) route/domain permutation after sealing, with unchanged dispositions and null domain contrasts; (6) late-arrival monotonicity, where late rows cannot change D; (7) inherited non-target/atom-scrambled negative controls with AUTO specificity margin; and (8) reviewer leakage audit. Synthetic packets are code tests and excluded from the finite-population estimand.

Before labels, freeze these operational nomination gates: every supported domain has lower 95% design bound at least 0.90 for AUTO-map agreement among attempted classifications; upper 95% bound at most 0.05 for unresolved actionability/supersession/disagreement among proposed AUTO and for missing/late/conflicting source in each fixed control family; D availability lower bound at least 0.90; serialization lower bound at least 0.98; target-vs-route-scrambled/negative-control AUTO false-positive absolute difference no more than 0.05 with simultaneous intervals; no supported domain more than 0.10 worse than pooled estimate; and every deterministic manifest, chronology and leakage check passes. Domains with fewer than 30 sampled admissions or 10 finite opportunities are INCONCLUSIVE, not merged. Reconciliation workload is reported in every domain; no retrospective capacity threshold is invented. Global AUTO nomination requires every supported domain/control to pass; no pooled rescue. A failed AUTO gate does not establish that RECON or DEFER is clinically safer.

## Interpretation and remaining uncertainty

Supportive results nominate only a separately governed prospective silent shadow run, where delivery, viewing, responsibility, UI rendering, actual reconciliation, patient preference, appropriateness, safety, downstream action, scheduling, completion, outside care, and independent clinical adjudication must be measured. Adverse results (positive from unavailable/conflicting/superseded/ambiguous data, chronology violation, domain/control failure, leakage, or inability to distinguish current from historical text) withhold AUTO nomination; they do not prove harm or superiority of RECON/DEFER. Sparse support, missing times/DS, non-identifiable joins, excessive unknowns, or wide intervals are INCONCLUSIVE; no dropping, recoding, later-text rescue, or threshold change.

Computationally checkable claims are source integrity, joins, chronology, packet availability, serialization, parser determinism, adjudication agreement, supersession classification, fixed-policy counts, workload descriptors, uncertainty, domain contrasts and controls. Delivery, viewing, responsibility, appropriateness, safety, completion, benefit, transport and causality require prospective event logs, expert review and possibly a later governed comparative study. The substantive change is the pre-label actionability/currentness/supersession layer and explicit AUTO-versus-RECON-versus-DEFER policy analysis; MIMIC's inability to establish real-world care remains explicit.
