## Frozen cohort, event, and exposure construction

Parse ISO dates and finite numerics. Impute birth date to the 15th of the recorded birth month and calculate completed years at `L`; require age 40–75 inclusive. Require `365 <= L-B <= 2922` calendar days and complete finite values at instances 0 and 1 for 30020, 30040, and 30080. Define `E=L+366 days`, `H=L+1825 days`, washout `[L,E)`, and follow-up `[E,H]`; an event on day `E` is in follow-up.

Trim and uppercase ICD-10 codes and remove dots for matching. Malignancy is C00–C97 and CRC is C18–C20 including descendants; non-CRC malignancy is C00–C97 excluding C18–C20. Exclude people with first recorded malignancy or death before `L`, then exclude first recorded malignancy or death in `[L,E)`. For event classification, collapse exact `eid/date/code` duplicates after preserving suffixes. If CRC and non-CRC codes occur on the same first date, classify CRC primarily and report an exclusion sensitivity. Cancer after death is excluded. Cancer and death on the same date is death-first primarily and cancer-first sensitivity because within-day order is unavailable.

For marker `m`, define `slope_m=(m_1-m_0)/((L-B)/365.2425)`. Orient the three trajectory terms so larger values are adverse: `-slope_Hb`, `-slope_MCV`, and `+slope_platelet30080`. Use each as a continuous one-degree-of-freedom term. No data-driven cut points or interactions are allowed. Comparator variables are age at `L`, sex, and instance-1 Hb, MCV, and platelet-30080. The trajectory model adds exactly the three oriented slopes. CBC imputation is prohibited. Standardization is training-fold-local.

Report, but do not automatically discard, extreme assay values; field-identity range reversal must fail the semantic test.

Supportive fallback evidence requires the full support tier, clean unit and permutation checks, negative `Delta_logloss` with interval excluding zero and `p<0.05`, then positive `Delta_AUC` with interval excluding zero, with no material calibration deterioration within recorded cases. Compatible adverse-oriented coefficient signs strengthen consistency but are not required individually. This would support discrimination of recorded CRC versus recorded non-CRC case type conditional on diagnosis lag; it would not support population prognosis or screening.

Adverse evidence is a precise non-improvement or deterioration in log loss, a precise non-positive AUC increment after the hierarchy opens, or consistently opposite trajectory behavior. It argues against this sparse repeat-attender trajectory for the specified recorded-case contrast, not against all CBC use. Inconclusive evidence includes insufficient classes, broad intervals, metric discordance, unstable folds, failed falsification, or same-day ambiguity sensitivity that changes the conclusion. `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED` makes the population-prognosis hypothesis inconclusive regardless of fallback findings.
