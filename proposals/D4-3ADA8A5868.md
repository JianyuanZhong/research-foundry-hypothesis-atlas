## Falsification and stop tests

1. Field audit: certificate, source hashes, field-instance mapping, missing-code handling, unit conversion, exact equations, and independent aggregate counts must agree. Failure yields neutral stop.
2. Leakage audit: static feature-list/code audit proves that no future field enters modeling; a deliberately leaked model is debugging-only.
3. Fold-aware label-null: 200 within-baseline-stratum outcome permutations, seed 520242; reconstruct folds, refit both models, and recompute all statistics. Use statistic-specific randomized-plus-one tail fractions and a 98% favorable-tail envelope, not a zero null.
4. Predictor-null: permute `Z` within baseline eGFRcr deciles and sex under identical fitting rules; persistent gains indicate leakage or implementation error.
5. Reversed-label negative control: rerun for `1-Ycr`; a discordance conclusion cannot rely on an equally large artificial-target gain.
6. Timing and equation sensitivity: report all interval bands and secondary equation-defined statuses separately. A direction isolated to one interval or one unadjudicated equation is not a general kidney finding.
7. Selection sensitivity: compare complete-case and predeclared weighted descriptive results; selection dependence is uncertainty, not a causal correction.
