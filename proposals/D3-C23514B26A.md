# D3-M2 repair: directional macrophage–fibroblast communication in inflammatory synovial coculture

## Seed and question

Seed D3-M2 asks which competing macrophage–fibroblast or endothelial–fibroblast mechanism is distinguishable by perturbation. This repair preserves that question but makes the testable claim directional and donor-aware. The primary hypothesis is about macrophage-to-fibroblast signaling; endothelial–fibroblast communication is retained only as a secondary, non-primary comparison because the bound matrices do not provide a verified endothelial-cell component.

## Evidence-supported claim versus untested hypothesis

The inspected literature supports a broader claim: active rheumatoid synovium contains reciprocal stromal–myeloid communication, and a failed-resolution framework can explain persistence of inflammation [K1]. Fibroblast-derived IGFBP5 reduction has also been associated with pathogenic fibroblast states and altered macrophage polarization [K2]. A cross-tissue transcriptomic analysis supports shared myeloid-inflammatory programs but does not establish directionality in an RA coculture [K3].

The unresolved, falsifiable claim is narrower: in matched-donor inflammatory synovial coculture, macrophage-derived inflammatory ligand expression (measured in E-MTAB-8322) predicts fibroblast inflammatory state changes (measured in E-MTAB-8873) better than a composition-only baseline, supporting directional macrophage-to-fibroblast communication.

## Hypothesis

In matched-donor inflammatory synovial coculture, macrophage-derived inflammatory ligand expression (measured in E-MTAB-8322) predicts fibroblast inflammatory state changes (measured in E-MTAB-8873) better than a composition-only baseline, supporting directional macrophage-to-fibroblast communication.

## Method

1. **Pseudobulk construction**: Aggregate E-MTAB-8322 macrophage-only pseudobulk and E-MTAB-8873 FLS/coculture pseudobulk by donor.
2. **Feature selection**: Identify inflammatory ligand genes in E-MTAB-8322 and inflammatory state genes in E-MTAB-8873 using the targeted panel.
3. **Donor-aware modeling**: Train a donor-aware model on macrophage-only pseudobulk expression (E-MTAB-8322) to predict the inflammatory signal in the targeted macrophage–FLS coculture data (E-MTAB-8873).
4. **Baseline comparison**: Compare the donor-aware model against a composition-only baseline.
5. **Falsification**: If the composition-only baseline performs equally well or better, the hypothesis is not supported.

## Falsification criteria

- **Supportive**: The donor-aware model trained on macrophage-only pseudobulk expression (E-MTAB-8322) predicts the inflammatory signal in E-MTAB-8873 better than a composition-only baseline.
- **Adverse**: The composition-only baseline performs equally well or better than the donor-aware model.
- **Inconclusive**: The difference in performance is not statistically significant, or the targeted panel does not provide sufficient gene coverage for the analysis.

## Limitations

- The targeted panel limits gene coverage.
- SA149/SA149P relatedness is unresolved.
- The hypothesis is a directional association, not a causal claim.
- Timing of measurements is unresolved.
