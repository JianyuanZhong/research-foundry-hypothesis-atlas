# Version-faithful effective-visibility frontier for comparator-closed documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Targeted successor and consequential unresolved issue

This is a targeted successor to assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves without modification the parent's source-defined mature first-resection population; point/interval operative clocks; 24-hour primary cutoff and 12/48/72-hour sensitivities; 90-day CT/MRI and 365-day prior-treatment windows; possibility-based temporal quarantine; whole-accession radiology units; reversible clean-text lineage; six complete immutable corpus-wide reader books and nine globally fixed radiology-by-pathology pairs; indivisible pathology terminals and encounter-documentary M2 endpoint; additive frozen models; acquisition-only, nonsemantic-surface, and same-fit semantic-mask comparators; historical fixed K; strict margin of more than five additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 fixed positions; exact attained finite-corpus extrema; whole-patient singleton deletion; source-state and reader uncertainty; and all non-causal evidence limits.

The parent repaired the absence of report timing by computing a worst-placement frontier for a binary whole-accession release bit. One consequential bridge defect remains: its `r_u=1` exposes the complete eventual `检查所见/检查诊断` accession body stored in the HCC snapshot. A timing log showing that “a report” was released before the cutoff does not establish that this eventual body was then available. The visible object may have been a preliminary version, may later have been corrected or supplemented, may not have been readable by the scoring service, or may not crosswalk to the eventual accession. Calling such an accession released can therefore clear the parent's `c*` while scoring information that was not present at decision time.

The live HCC examination schema has only `患者主索引,就诊号,检查,检查所见,检查诊断,开始时间,机器型号,检查号`. `开始时间` is acquisition-like. There is no report-instance ID, version ID, predecessor, authored time, preliminary/final status time, release time, addendum/correction/retraction record, target-system availability time, clinician-view time, or version-body hash. A full CSV-aware scan in job `[research job]` read all 419,996 examination rows: 392,854 nonblank `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` groups, 16,534 multirow groups, 595 groups with multiple distinct `开始时间`, 72 blank-accession rows, and 419,821 rows with a nonempty report body. These are source-grain facts, not cohort counts or release evidence.

The successor tests this sharper hypothesis:

> Conditional on every inherited all-eventual-content confirmatory gate passing at `K_.10`, there is a smallest effective-visibility fraction `c_F* <= 0.80` such that all six comparator-closed undeleted margins and all six whole-patient singleton lower margins remain strictly above five in 2020 and separately in 2021 under every admissible source/pathology/reader world and every placement of unavailable whole accessions, provided at least `c_F*` of the world's releasable accessions have a pre-cutoff finalized version that was readable by the intended scoring service and is pipeline-input-identical to the eventual HCC accession.

Equality at 0.80 is allowed only when every margin remains strictly greater than five. “Finalized,” “readable,” and “pipeline-input-identical” are not observed in HCC. Within Harbor, the experiment computes the deterministic effective-visibility requirement under a conservative all-or-none mask. A later version archive can test whether the same finite corpus meets that requirement. No generic release fraction may be substituted.

Clinical importance is operational and narrow. A queue can appear robust when eventual reports are masked at random or adversarially, yet still fail when the preoperative system sees an earlier report version. The repair converts report timing plus content identity into a single auditable acceptance condition. It can justify purchasing a version/log census or stopping that investment. It does not establish biological M2, actual workflow capacity, clinical action, treatment benefit, or deployment readiness.

## 5. The sole scientific repair: finalized pipeline-identity availability

For each reachable clean radiology unit u, compute an immutable eventual pipeline-input hash

`H_HCC(u)=SHA256(schema_version || length-delimited ordered tuples(source_row_ordinal, field_name, exact raw bytes, V/A/E lineage) for every exact-deduplicated 检查所见 and 检查诊断 component)`.

The hash includes component cardinality and order, field boundaries, empty fields, raw bytes, canonical parser version, and parser lineage. It excludes direct identifiers from its published representation but is linked internally to the protected unit hash. Two bodies are primary-equivalent only if their complete `H_HCC` values match. Semantic-only agreement, impression-only agreement, edit distance, model-score equality, or one reader's judgment is not primary equivalence; those may be diagnostics but cannot turn a zero into a one.

Introduce `e_u`, an effective-visibility intervention bit:

- `e_u=1`: expose the entire eventual unit exactly as in the parent.
- `e_u=0`: preserve acquisition-only `Z`; replace every report-body-derived `S,Q,X_clean` block from u by its development-defined operational-unavailable block in both `R_clean` and `G_clean`; apply the same S/Q mask to `Gclean_mask`; leave B unchanged.
- source-unclean, blank-accession, corrupt, contaminated, nonreversible, or otherwise unreleasable units remain unavailable for every e and are not in the releasable denominator.
- the identical e pattern is used in every arm of a contrast, every book, capacity, and paired undeleted/deleted computation. Reader interpretation of an exposed unit stays fixed by the selected corpus-wide book.

This mask is still distinct from `Gclean_mask`: `e_u=0` removes every input requiring access to the report body, whereas `Gclean_mask` removes semantic blocks while retaining surface/availability information from an effectively visible report.

In HCC, e is never observed or imputed. In the later audit, an accession may receive `e_u^audit=1` only when all of the following are established:

1. a stable report-instance/accession crosswalk links the version archive to exactly one HCC `(患者主索引,就诊号,检查号)` unit and all its components;
2. before that patient's same inherited cutoff, a version had an authenticated final status—not draft or preliminary—and had been released to and retrievable by the intended scoring service;
3. the complete version body transformed by a frozen, validated export mapper has `H_version(u)=H_HCC(u)`;
4. version history is complete through the HCC snapshot export, including corrections, addenda, retractions, merges, and splits, with timezone and clock provenance;
5. no log-integrity, duplicate-identity, or source-world ambiguity prevents the determination.

A complete log proving no qualifying version yields `e_u^audit=0`. Missing logs, an incomplete version chain, an unresolved crosswalk, or an unvalidated export mapping is not zero; it makes the audit certificate inconclusive. A preliminary report before cutoff followed by a matching final report after cutoff is zero. A final report before cutoff followed by a content-changing addendum that appears in HCC is zero because the pre-cutoff hash differs. The scoring policy evaluated here must fail closed and mask nonqualifying versions; performance of a system that ingests preliminary or non-equivalent text is outside this experiment.

## 6. Effective-visibility frontier and falsification

For year a and coherent world omega, let `A_a(omega)` be the parent's complete set of releasable clean whole accessions attached to eligible patients. Let C be the sorted union of 0, 1, and every exact breakpoint `m/|A_a(omega)|` across both years and worlds. At c, admissible intervention patterns satisfy

`sum_{u in A_a(omega)} e_u >= ceil(c |A_a(omega)|)`.

No independence, missing-at-random, version-stability, modality, patient, or state-weighting assumption is made. The feasible sets are nested under the “at least” constraint. Optimize every year-by-contrast undeleted and singleton-deleted endpoint over one coherent source/pathology/book world and all admissible e patterns. After deletion, project out all of the patient's units, use `ceil(c |A_a(omega minus j)|)`, and retain the same K. Every bound must be attained.

Define `c_F*` as the smallest breakpoint for which all six undeleted lower endpoints and all six singleton lower endpoints are strictly greater than five. If inherited all-content `e=1` fails, no frontier result may rescue it. If any required world, ledger, fit, replay, K gate, or exact solve is incomplete, the result is inconclusive.

The binary HCC optimization is intentionally numerically identical to the parent's release-mask frontier. The compiler must prove byte-for-byte agreement of all frontier bounds and witnesses after renaming the intervention. A difference indicates an accidental change to the settled design. The scientific change is the testable meaning of one and the matching external evidence contract, not a post hoc change to models, population, or threshold.

Primary support requires all inherited all-content gates, existence of `c_F*`, and `c_F*<=.80`. The .05/.20 capacities, 12/48/72-hour cutoffs, CT-only/MRI-only restrictions, semantic-only body equivalence, and actual-pattern replays are diagnostics and cannot rescue `K_.10/24h`.

`q_F(a,omega,j)=sum e_u^audit / |A_a(omega minus j)|`

and `q_F,min` as the minimum over 2020, 2021, all admissible worlds, and undeleted plus singleton projections. ## 8. One-hot Harbor results and interpretation

Apply gates in this order:

1. `inconclusive`: any source hash/header, roster/form/book completeness, chronology, leakage, N/K/event/anchoring, fit, parser/hash replay, patient deletion, or exact-optimization gate fails.
2. `version-faithful-visibility-robust comparator-closed supportive`: all inherited parent coordinates are undeleted and singleton-stable above five, `c_F*` exists, and `c_F*<=.80`.
3. `all-content supportive but effective-visibility-brittle`: all inherited coordinates pass but `c_F*>.80`.
4. `semantic-evidence-only`: both years' `Delta_R` and `Delta_mask` pass all-content/singleton gates but at least one `Delta_B` coordinate fails.
5. `policy-only`: both years' `Delta_B` pass but not all semantic coordinates pass.
6. `falsified-localized`: computation completes and none above applies; report year, comparator, e fraction, source/book world, and deletion witnesses. A coordinate is all-world adverse only if its attained upper endpoint is at most five.

Supportive means only that in this frozen eventual-report corpus, the locked full model retained the strict comparator-closed documentary-M2 margin even if any accessions below a sufficient finalized-pipeline-identical availability fraction were masked. It supports acquiring the exact version/log census. It does not say such versions existed at cutoff.

Brittle/adverse means the proposed universal margin did not survive the prespecified stored-content stress. It does not prove reports were late, preliminary reports were wrong, radiology semantics are useless, or another model/capacity cannot help. Inconclusive means the data, annotations, or exact computation could not decide the hypothesis; it is not evidence for or against benefit.

After external data are supplied, report separately `bridge_certified`, `bridge_not_cleared`, or `bridge_inconclusive`. Even `bridge_certified` establishes retrospective availability of pipeline-identical final bodies only in this snapshot. Prospective operational utility still requires a silent-mode cohort with real candidate flow, validated operation clocks, actual workload/slots, latency, abstention, clinician action, and safety review. Patient benefit requires a controlled outcomes study.
