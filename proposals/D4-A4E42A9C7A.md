# Repeat-attender CBC trajectories and an ascertainment-safe recorded-case CRC contrast

## Version purpose

This proposal is an execution-ready successor combining the corrected UK Biobank CBC semantics from `[prior hypothesis]` with the ascertainment-safe recorded-case fallback from `[prior hypothesis]`. It corrects all primary exposure bindings: field `30080` is platelet count and is the primary platelet exposure; field `30070` is RDW and is secondary only. Any result produced under the reversed aliasing is discarded. The current local export lacks an exact-release registry/death completeness certificate, so the active branch is explicitly `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED` until such a certificate is supplied.

## Clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

Among UK Biobank repeat attenders aged 40–75 with protocolized paired CBC measurements, does a prespecified joint trajectory of falling haemoglobin, falling MCV, and rising platelet count predict a first recorded CRC during days 366–1825 after the repeat assessment beyond age, sex, and repeat-assessment CBC levels? Is the incremental signal larger for CRC than for first non-CRC malignant cancer?

The field semantics and a routine-data cancer demonstration do not establish this prognostic or CRC-specific claim. The clinically consequential unresolved claim is incremental, temporally valid CRC discrimination after a delayed entry/washout period. The safe analysis available under unresolved ascertainment is a recorded-case contrast: among eligible participants with a valid first recorded malignant diagnosis in the analysis window, do the same CBC trajectories discriminate recorded CRC from recorded non-CRC cancer? This conditional contrast does not estimate population CRC risk, incidence, survival, PPV, calibration, or clinical utility.

## Population, time origin, and outcomes

Let `B=53-0.0` and `L=53-1.0`. Require valid dates and `365 <= L-B <= 2922` days. Impute birth date to the 15th of the recorded birth month, require valid birth year/month, and include age 40–75 inclusive at `L`; retain recruitment-age consistency as an audit/sensitivity. Define `E=L+366 calendar days` and `H=L+1825 calendar days`. Washout is `[L,E)`; day `E` belongs to follow-up.

Require finite paired haemoglobin, MCV, and 30080 platelet values at both visits. Exclude recorded malignant cancer or death before `L`, and exclude recorded CRC, non-CRC malignancy, or death in `[L,E)`. The certified population branch additionally requires `E` to lie within certified observation.

For every suffix, preserve the exact date/code pair. Never sort date and code arrays independently. Trim and uppercase codes, validate syntax before removing dots, then classify malignant codes C00–C97 and CRC codes C18–C20 including children. Non-CRC malignant cancer is C00–C97 excluding C18–C20. Same-date CRC and non-CRC records are classified as CRC primarily, with a mixed-code sensitivity. Cancer after the participant’s earliest death is not an observable endpoint and is audited. Same-day cancer/death is death-first primarily and cancer-first sensitivity because within-day order is unavailable.

The fallback retains only participants with a valid first recorded malignant diagnosis in `[E,H]`, after the same eligibility/history gates and death-first handling. People without a recorded case are never controls and never assigned a negative outcome. Its estimand is `P(recorded CRC | recorded malignant cancer in [E,H], eligible history and observed fields)`. Diagnosis-lag strata `0–365` and `366–1459` days from `E` are descriptive only and do not establish lead time or incident status.

## Exposures and locked estimands

For each of haemoglobin (`30020`), MCV (`30040`), and platelet count (`30080`), calculate

`(value_instance1 - value_instance0) / ((L-B)/365.2425)`.

The primary trajectory model contains exactly the three continuous slopes, with adverse-direction interpretation prespecified as falling haemoglobin, falling MCV, and rising platelets. The comparator contains age at `L`, sex, and repeat-assessment haemoglobin, MCV, and platelet count 30080. RDW 30070 and its slope are secondary only, requiring four-marker completeness and never substituting for 30080.

If and only if an exact-release coverage certificate passes, fit locked low-dimensional ridge-penalized cause-specific Cox models with delayed entry at `E`; estimate cumulative incidence with Aalen–Johansen competing death and alternate malignancy, with Fine–Gray sensitivity. Use identical participant-level repeated outer splits for comparator and trajectory models. Scaling, permitted imputation, penalty selection, censoring estimation, truncation, and calibration are fold-local. Report paired out-of-fold discrimination and integrated Brier changes only when support permits, and compare the CRC increment with the non-CRC increment using `D = incremental CRC AUC - incremental non-CRC AUC` and participant-bootstrap intervals.

When coverage remains unresolved but semantics pass, run only the locked binary recorded-case model among recorded cases (CRC versus non-CRC), with the same comparator, three-slope increment, repeated participant-level splits, fold-local preprocessing, paired out-of-fold AUC/log-loss change, and participant-bootstrap intervals. Calibration is conditional on recorded cases only and is not PPV or population risk.

Support gates are frozen. Certified branch: at least 60 CRC events permits locked prediction analyses; 30–59 permits parsimonious association and descriptive censoring-aware analyses; fewer than 30 makes incremental prediction inconclusive. Fallback: at least 50 recorded CRC and 100 recorded non-CRC cases permits the locked case-case increment; 25–49 CRC or 50–99 non-CRC permits only an imprecise parsimonious comparison; below either minimum permits audit and class counts only.

## Coverage gate and interpretation

The current gate is `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED`. A certified branch may activate only after an authoritative document tied to this exact extraction or demonstrably version-matched release identifies cancer/death sources and complete-date boundaries, England/Wales/Scotland applicability and source membership, delayed registration, absent-value semantics, and release/extraction identity. Until then, do not infer cancer-free follow-up, population incidence, hazards, AUC, Brier, calibration, IPCW, PPV, sensitivity, capacity, utility, or censor at the maximum observed date.

Support requires the applicable gate, adequate event/class support, adverse-direction association with uncertainty excluding the null, positive paired out-of-fold increment without supported Brier/calibration deterioration, and clean falsification checks. CRC specificity additionally requires `D>0` with an interval excluding zero. A precise null or opposite direction is adverse for this sparse repeat-attender design, not for all CBC models. Broad intervals, unstable weights, discordant metrics, low support, failed semantics, or unresolved coverage are inconclusive.

Required falsification tests fail on 30070-as-platelet or 30080-as-RDW aliases, cross-suffix pairing, inclusion of day `E` in washout, post-death endpoint treatment, maximum-date censoring, absent-case controls, or non-fold-local preprocessing. Under certified coverage, permute full endpoint processes within sex/five-year-age strata. Under fallback, permute CRC/non-CRC labels within diagnosis-lag and age/sex strata and rerun all fold-local steps. A retained above-null increment after permutation suggests leakage or coding contamination. Predicting pre-L cancer with post-L logic is an impossible-temporal check.
