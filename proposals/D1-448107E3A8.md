Critically, in all 22 episodes the selected placebo timestamp is exactly the assay-specific `P` timestamp for both assays. This follows from the frozen data and clocks: `P` is the latest assay-specific row in the same encounter from 72 hours before the event to strictly before the event, and the available selected common panels in the placebo window all coincide with that `P` panel.

Therefore this extraction does **not** provide an independent earlier trajectory measurement. The exact contrast possible is only an arm-adjusted difference in the selected pre-event/`P` assay level between future `early_signal=1` and `early_signal=0` among the 22 episodes having the placebo panel. Under the locked falsification rule, this placebo result blocks a strong persistence interpretation. It does not alter the late panel or justify changing the control after inspection.
