# Timing-identifiability repair for the fixed-capacity observed-UACR cystatin-C allocation experiment

## Clinical decision, strongest supported claim, and unresolved hypothesis

A service with capacity to order cystatin C for only 20% of adults with baseline creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m2 may face two different decisions: allocate immediately without a urine result, or deliberately obtain a usable UACR first and allocate only after that result is returned. The parent correctly compares frozen A_V, AG_V and G_V scores on a common usable-UACR pool V, but UK Biobank does not record when urine was collected or resulted, or when a cystatin-order decision would have occurred. Nonmissing instance-0 UACR therefore proves calculability, not pre-decision availability.

The strongest claim supported before opening any locked outcome is feasibility plus a negative timing-identifiability finding. The inherited audit found O=134,118 and censor-aware instance-0 UACR usable for 130,257 (97.12%), including 97.18% of test and 97.14% of replication. The biological-samples schema and physical header contain the required urine, creatinine and cystatin fields, but the schema declares no temporal columns and the 1,775-column source header contains no collection, result, date or timestamp column. Thus every member of V has unknown—not prior—UACR timing. Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041; full-text XML inspected as frozen source [source checksum]) supports that characteristics in UKB are associated with creatinine–cystatin eGFR differences and that an internally validated enriched model had AUC about 0.75. It does not establish fixed-capacity policy value, UACR chronology, measured GFR, persistence, or clinical benefit.

The unchanged unresolved performance hypothesis is the parent's hypothesis, now explicitly conditional on a protocolized UACR-first workflow:

> In each locked partition, if a future service first obtains a usable UACR and waits for it before allocating cystatin C, then among the unchanged V pool and at the unchanged absolute capacity n_p=floor(0.20×|O_p|), AG_V will identify at least 2 additional combined-equation eGFR<60 reclassifications per 1,000 assays versus A_V, with yield ratio at least 1.10 and positive marker-discordance yield; G_V will be noninferior to A_V at -1 reclassification per 1,000. If inherited N18 event sufficiency passes, AG_V will add at least 1 reclassification followed by first 10-year inpatient N18 per 1,000 and G_V will be noninferior at -1 per 1,000. These results must replicate and all inherited all-O, B/F/T, placebo, missingness, calibration, sex-stability, UACR-robustness and N18 rules must pass.

UKB cannot test the separate temporal hypothesis that UACR was actually available before cystatin allocation. That claim is not treated as false and is not silently assumed; it is nonidentifiable in this snapshot. The exact additional data required are participant-level urine collection time, UACR result-release time, the intended allocation or cystatin-order time, and timestamps showing that every other policy input was available by that decision. Repeat UACR and clinical-action data would additionally be required for persistence and management claims.

## Computable information-state audit

Let tau_alloc denote the time at which the cystatin-C allocation list would be committed. Let t_U_collect and t_U_result denote urine collection and usable UACR result-release times. For each i in V define:

- R_i=prior only if t_U_result<=tau_alloc (and collection is not later than result);
- R_i=post only if t_U_result>tau_alloc;
- R_i=unknown if either required time is unavailable.

Do not use UKB instance 0, horizontal colocation, nonmissing UACR, recruitment date 53-0.0, cystatin availability, or a later N18 diagnosis to infer R_i. They do not order urine relative to an unrecorded allocation decision.

The frozen snapshot lacks t_U_collect, t_U_result and tau_alloc. The required schema audit therefore deterministically reports, in every partition:

- `timing_fields_present=false`;
- `assignable_prior_count=0` and `assignable_post_count=0`, meaning no row is classifiable, not that no sample was collected before or after;
- `timing_unknown_count=|V_p|` and `timing_unknown_fraction=1.0`;
- `observed_preassay_estimand_evaluable=false`.

This is a computable source-availability result. It must be produced before labels are opened and cannot be altered by outcome results.

## Three workflow estimands and admissible comparators

### W1: protocolized UACR-first workflow

A future service explicitly collects urine and waits for a usable UACR before committing the cystatin list. Conditional on that externally imposed sequence, the unchanged parent V experiment is the correct benchmark: compare AG_V:A_V for incremental grip and G_V:A_V for substitution, using the same V_p and exact O-based n_p.

Results must be called “conditional UACR-first allocation performance.” They may motivate a prospective workflow comparison. They may not be called evidence that UKB participants had UACR available before cystatin allocation, that the workflow is feasible or cost-effective, or that testing improves care.

### W0: immediate allocation before UACR is known

V is not an admissible candidate pool because V membership itself requires knowing whether the urine result is usable. Therefore neither A_V, AG_V nor even G_V can support an immediate pre-UACR decision. Although the frozen G_V score contains no UACR, its V-restricted ranking is selected from a result-defined pool.

For W0, the only admissible evidence in this experiment is the inherited all-O comparison of the UACR-free frozen policies G versus B/F/T at the same absolute 20% O-based budget. No new score, refit, endpoint or threshold is introduced. A favorable G_V:A_V result cannot rescue an adverse inherited all-O G result or be relabeled as pre-UACR actionability.

### Wobs: empirically observed pre-assay UACR workflow

The target would compare A/AG/G among people whose usable UACR is documented as resulted before tau_alloc. It requires the unavailable timestamps above. No Wobs yield, contrast, ratio, confidence interval or deployment conclusion may be reported from this snapshot. The parent V results cannot fill this cell.

## Outcomes, analysis and decision rules

All computations and clinical endpoints remain exactly those of the parent. For selected set S with denominator n_p:

Y_H(S)=1000×sum(H_i)/n_p;
Y_D(S)=1000×sum(D_i)/n_p;
Y_H18(S)=1000×sum(H_i K18_i)/n_p.

Report the parent's rates, paired contrasts, feasible ratios, overlap/Jaccard, exact selected counts and directional swap decompositions. Use the unchanged 2,000 participant-paired no-refit bootstrap, simultaneous uncertainty family, joint missing-H/D bounds, >1% cystatin-missingness gate, six frozen UACR variants and N18 sufficiency rules.

A result is supportive for conditional W1 performance only if every parent gate passes: both locked V partitions satisfy the adverse-bound AG_V:A_V H increment of at least 2/1,000, point ratio at least 1.10 with simultaneous interval above 1, H interval above 0, positive directional-swap H yield, positive D in each lock with pooled interval above 0, G_V:A_V H noninferiority above -1/1,000, no variant sign reversal, every inherited all-O gate, and sufficiently observed supportive N18 gates including AG_V:A_V at least 1 H×K18/1,000 and G_V:A_V noninferiority above -1/1,000. The result label is `conditional_uacr_first_supportive`; `observed_preassay_supportive` must remain false.

A result is supportive for W0 only when the unchanged inherited all-O G:B/G:F/G:T rules pass. Its label is `immediate_no_uacr_supportive`. It says that a UACR-free allocation policy merits prospective evaluation, not that all its UKB inputs were available in real time or that cystatin testing benefits patients.

A result is adverse for W1 when precision is adequate and a parent adverse criterion is met: either lock excludes the AG materiality/ratio target, shows no positive increment, shows G inferior beyond its margin, or sufficiently observed N18 fails. It is adverse for W0 when an inherited all-O G gate fails with adequate precision. Conditional adverse performance remains informative despite unknown urine timing: imposing a UACR-first workflow would not repair a policy that fails within the conditional benchmark.

A result is performance-inconclusive under the unchanged parent rules when intervals cross a margin, locks disagree, V coverage/capacity fails, labels or swaps are sparse, a feasible denominator can be zero, N18 is insufficient, a censoring variant reverses sign, or an integrity/calibration/sex-stability check fails. Unknown urine timing is reported separately as `observed_preassay_not_identifiable`; it must not be mislabeled as ordinary statistical imprecision and must not erase a valid conditional supportive, adverse or inconclusive W1 result.

If W1 and W0 are both supportive, the strongest conclusion is that both a planned UACR-first strategy and a UACR-free immediate strategy merit prospective comparison under fixed cystatin capacity. It does not choose between them because UKB contains no turnaround time, total testing burden, actions, harms or utility. If W1 is supportive but W0 adverse, waiting for UACR may be necessary for the observed allocation gain. If W0 is supportive but W1 adverse, adding or conditioning on UACR does not validate the V strategy. If either is inconclusive, report that branch as unresolved.

## Substantive advance

The parent showed how to compare fixed-capacity policies among people with computable UACR. This repair prevents that result-defined pool from masquerading as a real pre-assay state. It separates a computable planned UACR-first benchmark from an immediate no-UACR decision and an unidentifiable observed chronology, while preserving every frozen scientific component. Support can now justify only the workflow it actually represents; adverse conditional performance remains falsifying; missing timing is exposed as missing evidence rather than invented temporality.
