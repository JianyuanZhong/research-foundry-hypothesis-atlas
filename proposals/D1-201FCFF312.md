# Care-plan labels and an executable independent respiratory trajectory with explicit exit accounting

## Targeted evolution and unresolved weakness

This is a targeted child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves their frozen eICU population (540 person-landmarks, 443 unique first qualifying stays/persons, 74 hospitals), the five landmarks, exact two-label exposure, first-label decision time D, inherited exclusions, 36-hour B/E/T windows, setting/airway trajectory labels, noncausal interpretation, hospital-held-out evaluation, and proxy limitations.

The important remaining weakness is not the hypothesis or endpoint definition but executable ascertainment. The prior proposal says to require `unitdischargeoffset > D+36h` and then analyze rows whose B and T settings are observed, but it does not specify how every frozen landmark is accounted for when discharge/death/transfer occurs before D+36, when the ICU record ends, or when a window is incompletely charted. That can silently select longer-stay, more-observable patients and can make missingness appear to be stable/mixed. This child makes the competing-exit and observation process an explicit, auditable part of the estimand without changing the frozen cohort or calling any exit a respiratory trajectory.

The unresolved hypothesis remains: among frozen labelled person-landmarks, does the exact care-plan label add reproducible information about a subsequent persistent respiratory support trajectory beyond pre-D respiratory state and documentation opportunity? This is predictive/associational, not a causal effect and not a test of whether an SBT, extubation evaluation, extubation, or benefit occurred.

## Endpoint and explicit observation/exit accounting

For each frozen row define B = `(D-720,D]`, E = `(D,D+720]`, and T = `(D+720,D+2160]` minutes. Keep the original endpoint exactly: for each of FiO2, PEEP, pressure support, and set rate, take the median of finite values in a window; a component is observed only with at least two measurements separated by at least 30 minutes. A state is observed when at least three of four components are observed. Changes from B to T use fixed thresholds: FiO2 absolute decrease/increase >=0.10 fraction, PEEP >=2, pressure support >=2, set rate >=2. Sustained de-escalation requires observed B and T, at least two decreases, no increase, and no two-component E escalation. Escalation requires observed B and T and at least two increases, or new invasive evidence in E/T after no invasive evidence in B. Stable/mixed is all other **classifiable** cases, including one-component/opposing changes. It must not be assigned to an unobserved case.

Define an independent follow-up disposition for every one of the 540 rows before outcome filtering, using only D, `patient.unitstayid`-linked `unitdischargeoffset`, source timestamps, and the respiratory/vital rows:

1. `full_followup`: `unitdischargeoffset > D+2160` (strictly after the T endpoint); the full T window is administratively available.
2. `early_exit`: `unitdischargeoffset <= D+2160` and `unitdischargeoffset > D`; report exact offset and raw `unitdischargestatus`/`unitdischargelocation`. This is a competing exit, not stable/mixed, and cannot be called successful or failed liberation.
3. `no_followup_at_D`: `unitdischargeoffset <= D` or missing/invalid discharge timing. This is an administrative competing exit/data-integrity category; retain it in the audit and do not impute a trajectory.
4. `full_followup_unobserved`: `full_followup` with fewer than two separated measurements for enough components in B or T. Report B/T component-level missingness. This is unclassifiable observation, not stable/mixed.
5. `full_followup_classifiable`: `full_followup` with the original three-class endpoint (de-escalation, escalation, stable/mixed).

The primary trajectory estimand is the original three-class distribution conditional on `full_followup_classifiable`; it is not silently changed to a composite of exit and trajectory. The required all-540 ascertainment table and label/hospital-stratified counts are part of the primary result. In parallel, fit the prespecified baseline and label-augmented models on the same fixed analysis denominator, while reporting a competing-exit sensitivity that treats `early_exit` and `no_followup_at_D` as a fourth, explicitly named `competing-exit` outcome and `full_followup_unobserved` as a fifth `unclassifiable-observation` outcome. Do not merge these categories with stable/mixed. The sensitivity is a missingness/selection diagnostic, not a claim that exit has respiratory meaning.

No post-D care-plan record may influence disposition, endpoint mapping, or observation weights. Do not use post-D respiratory row counts to condition inclusion beyond the already defined endpoint-observed status. If inverse-probability-of-observation weighting is used, estimate it inside each hospital-held-out training fold from pre-D variables only, truncate only at a predeclared bound (0.05, 0.95), and report unweighted results as primary; never use an outcome-derived weight. If a compiler cannot implement this without leakage, omit weighting and report the fixed-denominator competing-exit sensitivity rather than inventing one.

Safety concordance remains secondary and unchanged: among classifiable rows, median `sao2 >= 90` and median respiration `<=30` in both E and T, each with at least two observations. The stringent endpoint is sustained de-escalation plus this flag, with its denominator explicitly restricted to adequate vital observation.

## Baseline, transport, and falsification

The baseline model uses only pre-D information: landmark, baseline setting medians/counts/missing indicators, baseline invasive airway, pre-D SaO2/respiration, age category, and inherited pre-D ICU/admission covariates. A label model adds only the two-level exact label. A process sensitivity adds pre-D respiratory documentation density. Use hospital-held-out folds (eligible held-out hospital has >=20 frozen rows and >=2 trajectory classes), row-weighted micro log loss, equal-hospital macro log loss, multiclass Brier, calibration, and class rates. Cluster repeated landmarks by `patientunitstayid`; use 100 stay-level nested bootstrap refits. Record fold membership, all denominators, class/exit counts, and hashes.

Use placebo decision time D-1440 with the original label fixed and nonoverlapping placebo B/E/T windows. The endpoint implementation must be identical and care-plan-blinded. Run airway-definition sensitivity and a source-label mapping sensitivity fixed before outcome stratification.

Supportive result requires all of: (a) independent-outcome integrity pass; (b) >=40% of all 540 rows `full_followup_classifiable` and >=10 hospitals with both labels represented among classifiable rows; (c) exact label improves both micro and macro log loss in the full fixed analysis denominator and both airway sensitivities, with nested 97.5% delta below zero and no sign reversal in >25% eligible held-out hospitals; (d) directionally consistent sustained de-escalation and stringent safety-concordant contrasts in at least two of overlap hospitals (248, 252, 420 when each label has >=10 classifiable rows), with same early/late direction; and (e) post-D contrast exceeds the D-1440 placebo by >=5 percentage points without hospital-direction reversal. Report any supportive model result as incremental prediction of this chart-derived trajectory only.

Adverse result means independent integrity violation (invalid analysis), or <20% classifiable, or <5 hospitals with both labels, or no improvement in either bounded sensitivity with nested probability >=0.75 that the label worsens macro loss in at least two definitions, or hospital-direction reversal, or a comparable/larger placebo contrast. A comparable exit/unclassifiable rate across labels does not by itself support or refute label meaning; a strongly label-differential exit/observation process is adverse to a naive trajectory interpretation and must be reported as ascertainment bias. Fewer than 10 hard ETT-removal/reintubation concordant events across five hospitals is adverse only to that secondary validation, never evidence that liberation did not occur.

Anything between these gates is inconclusive, including adequate classifiability but sparse overlap, unstable endpoint mapping, sparse safety observations, or disagreement between the three-class analysis and the competing-exit sensitivity. Inconclusive results specify whether the next requirement is synchronized device data, site metadata, clinician adjudication, or external replication.

## Evidence and verification limits

Prior evidence supports only that the exact care-plan values are sparse/hospital-concentrated and that earlier short-horizon chart proxies were peak-dominated or site-unstable; it does not support the hypothesis tested here. This experiment can computationally verify frozen cohort identity, label/D reconstruction, pre-D isolation, source mappings, windows, thresholds, every-row disposition, class assignment, missingness, competing-exit sensitivity, hospital-held-out separation, bootstrap clustering, and gate arithmetic. It cannot establish that a care-plan value denotes an SBT/extubation evaluation, that numeric chart entries are delivered settings, or that a trajectory is clinical improvement. Those claims require device-linked data, synchronized orders, validated units, expert adjudication, or another study. No computed result is asserted here.
