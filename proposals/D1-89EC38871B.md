# Episode 25 result-bearing successor: the locked four-hour lactate experiment is not identifiable in EICU

## Resolution, clinical importance, and advance

The Episode-24 parent asked whether early lactate nonclearance is already a decision-relevant prognostic marker by four hours, before the selected eight-hour MAP-recovery phenotype. Its locked primary hypothesis was that, among adult four-hour survivors after an early recorded norepinephrine row and with baseline lactate at least 2 mmol/L, less than 20% lactate clearance at 2–4 hours is associated with at least a 10-percentage-point higher standardized probability of hospital death than at least 20% clearance. Full support required a positive estimate in at least 80% of finite leave-one-hospital-out refits. This is a prognostic association, not an effect of treatment or proof of tissue hypoperfusion.

The exact complete-source feasibility execution resolves a necessary antecedent claim: **EICU cannot estimate that locked multi-hospital four-hour contrast under its prespecified gates.** Only three hospitals meet the outcome-blind requirement of at least five people in each exposure group, versus eight required; after that gate there are 62 people (22 clearance, 40 nonclearance) and only 7 deaths in clearance versus 22 in nonclearance, versus at least 20 deaths required in each group. The primary model, bootstrap interval, and leave-site-out estimates are therefore deliberately not computed. The result is **inconclusive**, not adverse and not opposite-direction.

This is a substantive advance over Episode 24's permissive preliminary scan, which counted 888 broad lactate pairs and 262 permissive monitor-context records across 53 hospitals but explicitly did not apply final revision/conflict, baseline-lactate, vital, site, and event rules. It prevents an apparently attractive early-actionability study from producing an unstable, selected three-site estimate or changing its window/cutoff after seeing feasibility.

The strongest existing EICU evidence remains the separate eight-hour selected phenotype: 216 people in ten hospitals, adjusted nonclearance-minus-clearance mortality RD +24.389 points, 5,000/5,000 copied-cluster replicates, and 95% interval +6.121 to +42.882. That supports a positive noncausal association in that late phenotype, but the prespecified +10-point magnitude was inconclusive. It does not establish the four-hour claim.

Current literature also leaves timing and meaning unsettled. Full-text XML was inspected for Rebai et al. (Diagnostics 2026; DOI 10.3390/diagnostics16152463; PMCID PMC13464868), a single-center severe-trauma study using H0, H4 and H12 arterial lactates that explicitly reported survivorship-bias concerns for delayed clearance and required prospective external validation. Full-text XML was also inspected for Diab et al. (Front Med 2025; DOI 10.3389/fmed.2025.1679297; PMCID PMC12675444), a single-center sepsis cohort using a 10% clearance cutoff; its adjusted mortality association was borderline and it called for prospective work on cutoffs. Neither study establishes this EICU norepinephrine-selected four-hour hypothesis, a 20% cutoff, a 10-point risk-difference margin, or treatment utility.

5. Require at least three distinct `vitalPeriodic.observationoffset` values in [t0-60,t0), each with numeric `systemicmean` and `heartrate`; collapse duplicates by median and summarize baseline medians.
6. Before reading outcome values for hospital selection, retain hospitals with at least five X=0 and five X=1 people. The experiment requires at least eight retained hospitals and 50 people; after freezing the target it additionally requires at least 20 deaths in each group.

## Executed reconciliation and stopping result

The complete-source audit read all required rows and recorded no patient sampling:

- 14,303 adult one-person early-norepinephrine four-hour survivors under the deterministic stay rule;
- 664 people with valid baseline-high and selected 2–4 hour lactate pairs under the strict unit/revision/conflict rules;
- 205 people after the required baseline-vital context;
- three exposure-balanced hospitals after the locked site gate: hospital 199 (9 clearance, 12 nonclearance), 420 (7, 6), and 449 (6, 22);
- final gate target 62 people: 22 clearance with 7 deaths and 40 nonclearance with 22 deaths.

The ≥8-hospital gate and ≥20-deaths-in-each-group gate fail. No outcome model may rescue a population-gate failure. In particular, the crude death proportions (7/22 and 22/40) are selection diagnostics only and are not an effect estimate for the prespecified measured-overlap target.

## Locked estimand, models, and falsification rules

Had all population gates passed, the primary estimand would remain the nonclearance-minus-clearance standardized hospital-mortality RD in the measured-overlap target. The primary exposure model is the parent's organ-context model—not whichever tier is most favorable—with fixed C=10 L2 logistic regression, pooled 1st/99th percentile winsorization and scaling, missing indicators, and hospital indicators. Context is restricted to values available before `Q=min(F4_offset,t0+120)=t0+120`: age, gender, B, t0, baseline MAP/heart rate, F4 elapsed time, creatinine, total bilirubin, AST/ALT maximum, BUN and missingness. Recorded pressor-name proxies are secondary only. Overlap weights are `1-e` for X=1 and `e` for X=0, normalized within group.

If feasible, uncertainty would use seed 20260924 and 5,000 copied-hospital then copied-`uniquepid` resamples within hospital/exposure, refitting preprocessing and weights. Required gates also include group ESS ≥40, propensity [0.02,0.98], maximum person-weight share ≤5%, hospital-weight share ≤35%, all model-column absolute SMD ≤0.10, and ≥95% bootstrap convergence.

The frozen interpretations remain:

- supportive: every gate passes, RD interval lower bound ≥+10 points, and ≥80% finite site deletions are positive;
- adverse to ≥+10 magnitude: every gate passes and interval upper bound <+10;
- opposite direction: every gate passes and interval upper bound <0;
- inconclusive: any population/event/support/balance/convergence/site gate fails, computation is unstable, or the interval contains +10.

The observed classification is inconclusive by population and event gates. It neither estimates nor falsifies the four-hour association.

## Evidence boundaries, next study, and verifier

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes/headers, complete-file scans, t0/person/landmark selection, lactate unit/revision/conflict/tie rules, vital offsets, exposure counts, outcome-blind hospital gate, death counts, and the mandatory inconclusive classification. The verifier must include fixtures where a correct count is paired with an unsupported adverse/supportive conclusion, and where supportive, adverse, opposite and inconclusive synthetic outputs are interpreted correctly. Reference execution proves only computation and feasibility status.

EICU cannot adjudicate true norepinephrine start/administration/dose, arterial versus venous lactate, assay indication, treatment intent, fluids, beta-agonists, liver clearance, tissue perfusion, source control, treatment limitations, exact death time/cause, or clinical actionability. Clinical review is required for those concepts. Resolving the four-hour magnitude now requires independent, larger, prospectively synchronized data with administered vasopressor dose, fluids, organ support, lactate specimen/indication, perfusion findings and treatment limitations. A decision-impact study is required for utility; a randomized trial or credible target trial is required for treatment effects.
