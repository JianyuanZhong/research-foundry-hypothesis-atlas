# Proposal: a pathway-coherent post-TACE recorded-care boundary

## Parent, objective, and substantive repair

This is a substantive successor to assessed valid parent [prior hypothesis]. It preserves the parent’s capture-inclusive HCC design: one first TACE-like index per patient, the day-45 landmark, a day-46 through day-365 follow-up boundary, patient-level temporal splitting, a transparent capture-adjusted baseline, a trajectory model, an irregular-time alternative, administrative end-of-local-observation stress testing, a non-liver capture control, and a locked operating boundary.

The remaining consequential validity problem is endpoint coherence. The parent’s primary “first recorded liver-directed action” combines repeat TACE, resection/transplant, ablation, radiotherapy, and compound procedure strings. These actions can represent different clinical pathways, and a compound row can contain several interventions or diagnostic/technical components. A single favorable alert-burden result for that composite could therefore be an artifact of pathway mix or coding vocabulary rather than a reproducible post-TACE retreatment boundary.

The repair is to make the primary endpoint a narrower, auditable TACE-containing retreatment episode, and to model alternate liver-directed pathways as competing recorded states. The broad liver-directed composite remains a secondary descriptive estimand, not the basis for a clinical interpretation. Same-episode mixed therapeutic bundles are not silently assigned to TACE or to an alternate pathway: they receive their own competing category. This is an endpoint repair, not a claim that the local record identifies treatment intent, tumor response, or treatment need.

## Evidence-supported claim, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the inspected local evidence is operational. The HCC source has linkable encounter, procedure, diagnosis and dated laboratory records. The source audit found 105,044 encounter rows and 338,040 procedure rows, with 7,781 distinct raw procedure names; child tables repeat encounter keys and must be aggregated before joining. The procedure vocabulary contains recognizable TACE-like strings as well as resection, ablation, radiotherapy, biopsy, drainage and mixed bundles. A direct read-only vocabulary scan counted 106,963 TACE-lexicon rows; under the conservative liver-context family rules below it counted 62,912 resection-family rows, 18,754 ablation-family rows and 561 radiotherapy-family rows. These counts are row-level lexical availability checks, not clinical adjudication, patient counts, indications or outcomes.

The evidence does not establish that any procedure was indicated for residual tumor, progression, recurrence, or treatment failure. It also does not establish whether a procedure was completed outside this local record. The unresolved scientific claim is narrower and testable:

> Among adults with an HCC diagnosis and a first recorded TACE-like procedure who remain free of a liver-directed therapeutic episode through day 45, does the early AFP/hepatic trajectory add reproducible information for the later first recorded TACE-containing retreatment pathway beyond history and local-observation features, while remaining specific to liver-directed care rather than generic continued recording?

The primary hypothesis is:

> On the locked temporal test, B2 (capture-adjusted baseline plus the prespecified early AFP/hepatic trajectory) will reduce the alert fraction versus B0 by at least 10 absolute percentage points at 90% sensitivity for the first TACE-containing retreatment episode during days 46–365, with a 95% interval excluding no reduction, and the reduction will remain directionally favorable in the continued-local-observation stratum. The reduction will not be of comparable magnitude for the non-liver capture-control outcome or the pre-index placebo trajectory.

The pathway-specific secondary hypothesis is that the trajectory boundary’s signal is not merely a generic predictor of any recorded intervention: the model must report separate cumulative incidence and calibration for alternate liver-directed and mixed-therapeutic pathways. A large aggregate gain that is confined to alternate or mixed pathways, or that disappears when procedure vocabulary is conservatively adjudicated, is adverse to the primary interpretation.

The irregular-time model M3 is retained as a substantive alternative, not as a complexity requirement. It tests whether asynchronous biomarker shape, discordance and measurement intensity provide information beyond B2’s summary category for this same TACE-specific competing-state estimand.

## Population, index, landmark, and temporal boundaries

Use the parent population unless a prespecified data-quality failure prevents it.

- Join HCC child tables to encounters on (patient master index, encounter number) after aggregating each child table to that key. Preserve raw rows for audits and never form a many-to-many join.
- Construct a procedure episode within patient by grouping qualifying procedure rows whose parsed Start Time values are within 24 hours of one another, retaining all names, start/end times, sources and encounter keys. A shared encounter key is retained as an audit field but does not replace the event-time rule. Missing start time cannot define an episode time and is reported separately.
- Define the index as the earliest episode containing a TACE-like row: case-insensitive literal TACE, a procedure name containing transarterial chemoembolization, or a name containing both hepatic artery and embolization. Preserve the exact raw names and the rule hit. Index episodes that also contain another therapeutic family are retained but flagged as composite index episodes.
- Require age >=18 and nonmissing sex in the joined index encounter.
- Require a diagnosis name containing literal Hepatocellular carcinoma in a joined encounter from 180 days before through 7 days after index. Diagnosis time is inherited from the joined encounter because the diagnoses table has no time field.
- Include index dates through 2025-01-01, so a complete 365-day horizon is available.
- A patient is day-45 eligible only if there is no post-index episode after the first 24 hours through day 45 containing a liver-directed therapeutic family. Report early TACE, alternate and mixed episodes separately; do not silently censor them as missing.
- The primary follow-up is index day 46 through day 365. The day-45 landmark and capture-inclusive interpretation are unchanged.

The primary estimand is the first-event cumulative incidence of a TACE-containing retreatment episode by each time through day 365 among day-45-eligible patients, with alternate liver-directed, mixed therapeutic, non-liver capture-control and administrative end-of-local-observation states explicitly represented. The primary operating boundary is evaluated at day 365. This is a prognostic estimand for recorded local care, not a causal treatment effect and not a claim about biological response.

## Frozen pathway ontology and endpoint rules

Apply the following lexical rules to normalized Unicode text after whitespace normalization. Keep a row-level audit with the original name, normalized name, every rule hit, and any excluded token. No unvalidated NLP or narrative clinical inference is permitted.

1. TACE_like: the parent TACE rule above.
2. resection_or_transplant: a liver-context occurrence of liver within 12 characters of resection, liver transplant or transplant; also match explicit liver-lobe/segment resection names. Exclude names whose only match is cholecystectomy, non-liver organ resection, or a generic resection without a liver-context token.
3. ablation: a liver-context occurrence of liver, liver tumor or liver lesion within 12 characters of ablation, radiofrequency, microwave or cryoablation. A biopsy-only name is not ablation.
4. radiotherapy: a liver-context occurrence of liver, liver tumor, hepatic artery or portal vein within 12 characters of radiotherapy, radiation, radioactive seeds, iodine-125, gamma knife or stereotactic. Generic radiotherapy without a liver-context token is not classified as liver-directed.
5. other_liver_therapeutic: only an additional prespecified, liver-context therapeutic term documented in the vocabulary audit before locked evaluation. If no defensible rule is available, retain the row as unclassified rather than inventing a category.
6. Biopsy, imaging guidance, angiography, drainage, catheter placement and other technical or diagnostic terms alone are not therapeutic liver-directed families. They can accompany a therapeutic family and remain in the raw bundle audit.
7. non_liver_procedure: a procedure episode with no liver-directed therapeutic family. It is a capture-control state, not a biological competing event.

Classify each episode as mutually exclusive using this precedence:

- TACE_rechallenge: at least one TACE-like row and no resection/transplant, ablation, radiotherapy or other-liver-therapeutic row in the same episode. Biopsy, angiography and technical components do not disqualify it.
- alternate_liver_directed: at least one alternate liver therapeutic family and no TACE-like row.
- mixed_liver_therapeutic: TACE-like plus any alternate liver therapeutic family, or two alternate therapeutic families in the same episode.
- non_liver_procedure: no liver therapeutic family.
- unclassified_procedure: a nonmissing-time episode whose terms cannot be classified by the frozen rules. It is reported and handled in sensitivity analyses, not silently assigned to a clinical pathway.

The primary event is first TACE_rechallenge. The first alternate_liver_directed and first mixed_liver_therapeutic episodes are competing recorded pathways. The broad union of these three categories is a secondary “any classified liver-directed therapeutic episode” outcome only. The first non_liver_procedure is a capture-control competing state for first-event analysis and is also reported as a separate outcome; it is not interpreted as a clinical competitor to TACE. Exact same-time ties between event categories are a tie state for the primary ordered analysis, with both possible assignments in sensitivity analyses. A procedure episode containing only a biopsy or drainage is not counted as liver-directed treatment.

This hierarchy repairs clinical coherence without claiming that the TACE category identifies intent. It also prevents an aggregate composite from masking pathway-specific failure.

## Capture-inclusive observation state

Retain the parent’s administrative ascertainment stress test. From joined encounter rows, parse nonmissing Visit time, Admission time and Discharge time; define a contact timestamp as the maximum valid timestamp in that encounter. Do not use name, identity-card, insurance-card or phone fields as predictors.

After removal of any earlier event relevant to the current risk state:

- A patient enters the first-event state at the first classified procedure episode during days 46–365.
- If no classified procedure occurs, define end_of_local_observation as one day after the last valid dated encounter contact after day 45 when that time is on or before day 365. If there is no dated encounter after day 45, set the state time to day 46. If the last contact is day 365, there is no end-of-observation event within the horizon.
- Patients with no procedure and no end-of-local-observation are observed_no_action through day 365.
- Report unclassified procedure episodes separately. For the primary endpoint, do not treat them as TACE or alternate treatment; in a sensitivity analysis censor at the first unclassified episode with IPCW and report positivity diagnostics.
- Repeat the state definition using Encounter Time alone because Admission Time is missing in 3,587 audited encounter rows and Discharge Time is missing in 252.

The continued-local-observation stratum is prespecified as at least one dated contact in days 276–365 and no administrative end-of-local-observation before day 365. If the stratum has too few TACE-rechallenge events for a confidence interval, the stability result is inconclusive, not relaxed.

## Predictors and leakage control

Use the parent’s frozen day-45 inputs, with the corrected pathway endpoint:

- AFP: last parseable numeric value in days -30 through -1 and first in days 7–45, using log1p, continuous change, the parent’s improvement indicator and missingness. Do not pool assay units.
- Albumin, total bilirubin, the observed coagulation-ratio assay label used by the parent, and platelets: last pre-index and first early-post value; preserve assay identity and missingness. Do not infer units, calculate ALBI/MELD, or relabel an assay.
- The primary trajectory category remains AFP improvement plus non-deterioration in at least two hepatic proxies; retain continuous changes and pair availability for B2/M3.
- Day-45 capture features: encounter counts/timing and departments, dated laboratory row/day counts, assay availability, dated examination opportunity, non-liver procedure counts, unclassified-procedure counts and declared dated order features only as a sensitivity. An order is not completed care.
- Examination narrative fields are excluded from primary prediction. Dated examination opportunity may be used as an observation feature, with an audit of hepatobiliary lexical availability only if frozen before locked evaluation.

All parsing, family rules, imputation, observation-state construction, censoring models, threshold selection and model specifications are frozen before the locked test.

## Matched baseline and substantive irregular-time alternative

Use identical population, episode ontology, competing states, observation definition, feature freeze, split, threshold targets and uncertainty procedure for all models.

- B0: a regularized discrete-time cause-specific hazard model with age, sex, department, pre-index procedure/diagnosis history, assay summaries and missingness, day-45 capture features and the same competing states: TACE_rechallenge, alternate_liver_directed, mixed_liver_therapeutic, non_liver_procedure and end_of_local_observation. It yields cause-specific predictions, competing-risk cumulative incidence and the TACE-specific operating boundary.
- B2: B0 plus the frozen AFP/hepatic trajectory, continuous changes, pair availability and biomarker missingness. B0-to-B2 is the primary incremental scientific comparison.
- M3: a low-rank irregular-time state-space model with separate latent activity and hepatic-function trajectories, patient intercepts/slopes, assay-specific observation submodels, observation intensity and the same five-state hazards. Fit it to the identical day-45-to-day-365 recorded event process, with the observation process as a model component rather than an unexamined nuisance. Require posterior state/slope uncertainty and output predictions for every competing state. M3 can reveal asynchronous shape, discordance and measurement intensity that B2’s thresholded summary loses; it cannot recover tumor burden, treatment intent, access, outside care, death or radiologic response.
- The primary selection is B0/B2 as transparent anchors because the endpoint repair and capture diagnostics are the scientific uncertainty. M3 is retained because its additional information has a mechanistic measurement-process interpretation, not because it is more complex.
- A transformer is deferred: the present uncertainty is procedure-pathway validity and local ascertainment, and extra sequence capacity would not establish endpoint coherence. Revisit only with validated code semantics, larger independent longitudinal data or an external site. Causal treatment-effect models and radiology-text/image models are deferred because intent, eligibility, benefit, mortality/outside-care linkage, images and adjudicated response labels are unavailable.

The actual scientific deliverable is newly fitted B0, B2 and, if feasible, M3 parameters plus patient-level competing-state predictions and a predeclared TACE-specific decision boundary. Completion is not a completed clinical solution.

## Splits, boundary, uncertainty, and falsification

Use patient-level temporal splits by index date: 2010–2022 fit, 2023 tuning, and 2024–2025-01-01 locked test. If date support prevents this, use deterministic participant hashing and label it internal. No patient may cross splits.

For sensitivity targets 80%, 90% and 95%, choose on the tuning set the largest predicted-risk threshold whose training-only-IPCW sensitivity for TACE_rechallenge is at least the target. The largest threshold minimizes alerts subject to sensitivity. If no threshold reaches a target, record boundary failure. Never retune on locked data. Repeat threshold selection within tuning bootstrap resamples and provide a fixed-threshold sensitivity analysis.

Report on the locked test:

- cause-specific and competing-risk-aware sensitivity, alert fraction, PPV, false-alert fraction and alerts per TACE-rechallenge;
- TACE, alternate, mixed, non-liver and end-of-observation cumulative incidences;
- calibration-in-the-large, calibration slope, Brier and log score for each state;
- patient-clustered bootstrap intervals, IPCW overlap/truncation/calibration diagnostics and threshold uncertainty;
- pathway-vocabulary counts, unclassified episodes, same-time ties and index composite status;
- boundary results in the continued-local-observation stratum and the last-contact-time sensitivity;
- non-liver capture-control and pre-index placebo results.

Supportive evidence requires the prespecified >=10-point B2-versus-B0 alert reduction at 90% TACE sensitivity with an interval excluding no reduction; no direction reversal in the continued-observation stratum; no comparable improvement for the non-liver control or pre-index placebo; no material dependence on the last-contact definition; and no pathway-specific adverse pattern in alternate/mixed cumulative incidence or calibration. M3 is supportive only if it converges, is calibrated, and produces a prespecified >=5-point lower alert fraction than B2 at 90% TACE sensitivity with an interval excluding no reduction while passing the same pathway and capture checks. This is a model-information criterion, not a clinical-utility claim.

Adverse evidence includes no or reversed TACE-specific gain; gain confined to administrative disappearance; continued-observation reversal; a comparable non-liver/placebo gain; materially changed results when unclassified episodes are censored; unstable procedure-family rules; poor IPCW overlap; threshold drift or calibration failure; a large mixed/alternate pathway contribution that explains the aggregate result; or M3 nonconvergence. These outcomes argue against the proposed recorded-care specificity.

Inconclusive evidence includes too few day-45-eligible TACE-rechallenge events, too few events in the continued-observation stratum, sparse mixed/alternate states, high unclassified or missing-time rates, unresolved same-time ties, materially different last-contact state definitions, unstable bootstrap intervals, or insufficient calendar support. Do not lower margins or change lexical windows after seeing locked outcomes.

The interpretation file must map each conclusion to computed output fields and the corresponding prespecified rule. It must separately state whether the primary TACE-specific hypothesis was supportive, adverse or inconclusive, and whether the secondary pathway and capture checks passed. Computation can establish reproducible local recorded-care stratification, procedure-family sensitivity and observation diagnostics. It cannot establish recurrence, viable tumor, radiologic response, treatment failure, treatment benefit, survival, death, outside-care status, treatment intent, clinical utility, a recommendation to repeat TACE, or external validity.

Unavailable evidence requiring another study or expert review includes expert adjudication of procedure indications and bundles, validated treatment-intent and eligibility fields, radiology images/reports with response adjudication, tumor number/size/burden, death and outside-care linkage, external institutional data, validated laboratory units where needed, and a prospective silent or impact evaluation. A positive computation should motivate that next study; it does not substitute for it.

## Compute and method-selection record

The discovery budget is 7,200 science seconds. The parent’s all-row encounter/procedure audit measured 3.27 seconds with 8 CPUs and 32 GiB; this is a measured audit only, not a full fit. The proposed future solver envelope is up to 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB and 28,800 seconds. Full preprocessing, bootstrap duration, M3 convergence and GPU benefit are unverified.

B0/B2, procedure-episode construction and bootstrap summaries are expected to use CPU with up to 16 CPUs and 64–128 GiB, but exact runtime is unverified. M3 should first use sparse CPU fitting with checkpoints. If a bounded feasibility probe shows repeated state-space optimization materially benefits from acceleration, request one allocated A100 through ehr_job_submit, use cuda:0 within the allocation, and declare inputs/outputs. Ordinary-shell CUDA absence is not evidence against GPU feasibility; no GPU is needed for the transparent models. No proposer or solver weights are trained.

The selection record is explicit: B0 is the transparent reference and B2 isolates the trajectory contribution; M3 is retained because it can reveal irregular biomarker shape and informative observation intensity lost by a category. The transformer, causal treatment-effect model and radiology-text/image model are deferred for the evidence-bound reasons above. Revisit them only with validated pathway/intent labels, response adjudication, mortality/outside-care linkage, external data, or a materially larger independent cohort.
