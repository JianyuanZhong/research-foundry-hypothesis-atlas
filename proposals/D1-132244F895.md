# Admission-anchored documented-plan-conditioned HCC pathway information

## Scientific question and hypothesis

The leading HCC design showed a useful unresolved question: whether first-day laboratory trajectories add information beyond pathway-related orders already documented. Its primary cohort, however, required patients to remain hospitalized at 72 hours, conditioning on a downstream care-process state and excluding early discharge. This proposal repairs that estimand while preserving the 48-hour predictor-to-event buffer.

Among adults with a first observed HCC-coded inpatient encounter, does numeric laboratory information observed in [0,24) hours after admission add reproducible, calibrated information beyond admission/pre-admission variables and a pre-72-hour documented-care-plan proxy for the first locally recorded transition during [72,144) hours? The transition is embolization/interventional (E), local ablation (A), resection (S), mixed/ambiguous liver-directed procedure (X), or discharge before an E/A/S/X event (D). The primary hypothesis is that a worsening early hepatic/renal-reserve pattern has a larger standardized incremental association with E than with S; the contrast may be null or reversed.

This is a prognostic association with recorded local workflow, not treatment choice, indication, intent, appropriateness, benefit, toxicity, recurrence, progression, or causal effect. The clinical advance is to test whether early physiology contains decision-relevant information after documented workflow is accounted for, while estimating the full admission-anchored transition process instead of selecting only patients who remain hospitalized. A positive result would motivate prospective adjudication of whether such information should trigger pathway reassessment; it would not justify treatment recommendations.

The strongest supported claim is data availability and feasibility: the snapshot contains encounters, HCC-coded diagnosis strings, dated laboratory rows, dated procedure rows and dated non-drug orders. The unresolved claim is incremental, temporally insulated, pathway-specific prognostic information. HCC coding is not pathology confirmation and order strings are not validated intent labels.

Require at least 100 locked E, A and S events; otherwise report inconclusive primary modality estimates. ## Estimands, baselines and substantive alternative

Estimate six-hour cause-specific hazards and competing-risk cumulative incidence functions (CIF_E, CIF_A, CIF_S, CIF_X, CIF_D), starting at admission. Report standardized incremental CIF contrasts for labs beyond B0, especially Delta_E-S (lab-associated E contrast minus S contrast), with E-A and S-A secondary contrasts. Cause-specific hazard ratios are not marginal risks and are not treatment effects.

B0 is a transparent cause-specific pooled-logistic competing-risk model using age, sex, admitting 就诊科室, admission-era/time descriptors, diagnosis-type descriptors, prior encounter/procedure summaries, exact-assay pre-admission nearest value/recency/count/missingness, and pre-72-hour E/A/S plan indicators. No post-24-hour lab or post-72-hour information enters B0.

B1 adds first/last value, change, elapsed time, count, distinct timestamp count, missingness and slope (only with >=2 timestamps) for each exact assay in [0,24), plus assay-specific measurement density. B0 and B1 use identical patients, bins, preprocessing and patient-level split.

The substantive alternative is a low-dimensional irregular continuous-time coupled-state model with the same assay tokens, assay identity, elapsed time, qualitative-result/missingness indicators, density and B0 covariates. It estimates separate latent hepatic-reserve and renal-stress states with assay-specific offsets/noise, patient shrinkage, irregular gaps and E/A/S/X/D heads, yielding latent levels, recent slopes, coupled contrast, posterior uncertainty and plan-conditioned predictions. This can reveal nonlinear timing, coupled movement and uncertainty lost by B1 summaries. It is not a diagnosis. A GRU/temporal-attention sensitivity is deferred because the immediate scientific uncertainty is incremental information after plan adjustment and temporal buffering; revisit only as a new child if residual evidence shows the state model misses relevant ordering interactions. Text/image models are deferred because local images are unavailable, documents/pathology have no native time, and expert labels are absent. No method is rejected because it is neural or GPU-capable.

Fit parameters only in 0–59; choose dictionary, assay semantics, state dimension, regularization, binning and observation-process terms in 0–59 plus 60–69; lock all preprocessing and evaluate once in 70–79. CPU-first is appropriate for sparse aggregation, pooled logistic fits, CIFs, bootstrap and a low-dimensional state model. The future solver envelope is 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, up to 8 allocated A100 GPUs and 28800 seconds; discovery did not measure full lab construction, state fitting or 500-bootstrap duration, so checkpoint derived tables and fits. GPU use is optional and must use allocated cuda:0; it is not evidence of scientific value.

## Evaluation and falsification

On locked test patients report Brier/integrated Brier, calibration intercept/slope and observed-versus-predicted CIF by prespecified risk bins for each cause; AUROC/PR-AUC only secondarily. Report absolute CIFs, prevalence, standardized contrasts and paired patient-bootstrap 95% intervals with at least 500 resamples for B1-B0, state-B1, CIFs and Delta_E-S. Report calibration/Brier/CIF error separately for adequately supported departments and admission eras; this is internal domain description, not external validation.

Falsification gates are: compare with/without plan proxies; report plan-family alignment, discordance, X, D and censoring and fail if routine/duplicate/cancelled orders dominate; permute laboratory times within patient and exact assay while preserving values; add/remove measurement count, missingness and gaps; repeat duplicate and same-assay/same-time handling; remove midnight procedure starts; leave out one procedure/order dictionary member at a time; use central-line/venipuncture workflow terms as a negative-control endpoint; retain X and D as competitors and compare only as sensitivity with naive analyses that ignore competing events; prove no primary lab is outside [0,24) and no primary outcome is before 72h; report early-discharge frequency and compare with the selected-but-conditioned landmark sensitivity; keep post-landmark orders and medications out of primary predictors and report them only in leakage audits.

Supportive evidence requires a coherent adjudicable dictionary, adequate events, leakage-free splits, calibrated held-out B1 improvement over B0, a nonzero Delta_E-S meeting its prespecified two-sided uncertainty and one-percentage-point relevance threshold, and persistence after timestamp, observation-process, dictionary, department/era and negative-control checks. State-model support additionally requires calibrated/CIF or risk-stratification improvement over B1 with uncertainty. Adverse evidence includes null/opposite contrast, poor calibration, no incremental value, disappearance after plan/observation adjustment, timestamp-permutation persistence, negative-control performance, modality/dictionary instability or dependence on one department/era/string. Inconclusive evidence includes inadequate event/lab support, high X or missing-time burden, incomplete discharge/outside capture, unfrozen semantics, nonconvergence or intervals too wide to distinguish a meaningful contrast from zero. None of these QA results establishes clinical benefit or truth.

## Demonstration and seed disposition

The Delphi demonstration supports dated structured-history modelling and held-out calibration, but its UK Biobank trajectories and Danish validation are unavailable here; this proposal adapts the temporal idea to HCC labs without claiming reproduction. The ALADYNOULLI demonstration supports an interpretable irregular latent longitudinal adaptation; its genetic component is unavailable, so only the explicitly revised EHR-only state model is considered. The Oncoformer supplement supports modality ablation/comparison, but the main article and complete STAR Methods are unavailable and local HCC images are absent; no multimodal reproduction is claimed. The expert-seed library has no HCC cards, so no seed question is imported.

Compilation/readiness is packaging validation, not a scientific result.
