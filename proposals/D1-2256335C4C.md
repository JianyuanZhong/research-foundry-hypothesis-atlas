# Decision-validity bridge and fail-closed packet contract

## Status, parent, and scope

This is a targeted successor to `[prior hypothesis]`, whose assessed-valid protocol is the parent. It preserves the parent's frozen scientific schema: first-eligible adult, one admission per subject, all-opportunity denominator; reciprocal pre-discharge chronology; canonical exact discharge-summary artifact; unit-specific clocks and exhaustive states; exact recommendation-atom and admission-level worst-unit route estimands; sealed route-concealed panels; all-unit adverse precedence; positive-probability two-phase whole-admission design; exact compatible-set inference; reconciled workload ledger; and a prospective-silent-only ceiling on conclusions.

The sole amendment repairs the remaining documentation-to-decision and implementation dependency. A MIMIC note can establish what was documented in an available artifact, but cannot establish that a clinician saw, accepted, communicated, scheduled, or completed a recommendation. The protocol therefore adds a deterministic, auditable *decision-availability bridge* with three explicitly distinct observable states and a fail-closed packet/provenance contract. It does not redefine the frozen route estimand, add a clinical-action outcome, or claim workflow exposure, safety, benefit, transportability, or causality.

All additions below are binding when more specific than inherited language. The versioned protocol hash, source snapshot, code commit, dictionary version, panel assignment, and all thresholds must be sealed before any label, route summary, or outcome summary is opened.

## Question and claims

The unresolved, falsifiable question is whether a pulmonary radiology recommendation that is routeable under the frozen protocol is followed by an auditable discharge-documentation bridge that differs by the protocol's worst-unit route, and how much of the apparent routeable documentation gap is actually unresolvable because the available records cannot prove a decision opportunity.

The strongest claim supported by these data is only an association among timestamped radiology text, timestamped discharge documentation, structured admission/unit/disposition records, and blinded expert labels. The experiment tests whether route strata have different rates of observable documentation states under a prespecified denominator. It cannot test whether clinicians acted, whether care was safe or beneficial, or whether routing caused any outcome.

## Frozen population and chronology

Use the inherited first eligible adult pulmonary opportunity, with one admission per subject and all eligible opportunities in the denominator. Retain the parent's exact pulmonary concept and opportunity definition; do not substitute a lexical classifier. Apply the parent's reciprocal pre-discharge chronology: the eligible radiology report and its recommendation atoms occur before the admission's discharge boundary, and the canonical discharge artifact is selected without using post-boundary information to alter opportunity eligibility or route.

For each admission, define `T_discharge = hosp/admissions.dischtime` and retain the parent's unit clock from `icu/icustays.intime/outtime` and/or `hosp/transfers.intime/outtime`. Every event is ordered by its relevant source timestamp, with deterministic ties broken by source-specific stable identifiers (`note_id`, then `note_seq`; `transfer_id`; `stay_id`). Do not use cross-subject calendar alignment. A note with `charttime > T_discharge` is post-discharge evidence and cannot be used to establish a pre-discharge decision opportunity. A note with `charttime <= T_discharge` but `storetime > T_discharge` is retained as a timestamp-quality exception: it may be used only in the predeclared sensitivity analysis and is never silently treated as contemporaneous knowledge.

## New bridge estimand

For every recommendation atom `a` in eligible admission `h`, retain the inherited route `R(h)` and define a *documentation bridge state* using only the canonical discharge artifact and its auditable timestamp/provenance:

* `BRIDGE_ACK`: the sealed panel labels an explicit, atom-specific discharge-documentation acknowledgment that preserves the atom's actionable meaning (including an explicitly documented plan, follow-up interval, or responsible service when present).
* `BRIDGE_MENTION`: the artifact contains a clinically related mention, but the panel cannot establish atom-specific acknowledgment or an actionable plan.
* `BRIDGE_NONE_OR_UNAVAILABLE`: no qualifying mention is found, or the canonical artifact is unavailable/empty/truncated/unlinkable under the fail-closed rules.

`BRIDGE_NONE_OR_UNAVAILABLE` is intentionally a union for the primary observable state: it prevents missing documentation from being misrepresented as documented non-action. Report its two components separately (`NONE` and `UNAVAILABLE`) and use the paired estimands `P(BRIDGE_ACK | a)`, `P(BRIDGE_MENTION | a)`, and `P(UNAVAILABLE | a)`. The primary route-level result remains the inherited worst-unit route estimate; bridge states are an additive descriptive/decision-validity endpoint and do not replace the inherited adverse route endpoint.

A separate structured *decision-availability flag* is computed without clinical interpretation:

* `AVAILABILITY=0`: linked, nonempty, nontruncated canonical artifact with a valid charttime and a predeclared text-integrity pass.
* `AVAILABILITY=1`: artifact missing, empty, duplicated without a deterministic winner, unlinkable to the admission, or fails integrity checks.

This is an evidence-availability state, not evidence of care failure. No imputation converts `AVAILABILITY=1` into `BRIDGE_NONE`.

## Canonical artifact and packet contract

The canonical artifact must be selected by a pure function of the frozen admission and available source rows, before panel review:

1. From `note/discharge`, retain rows with the same `subject_id` and `hadm_id` as the admission and `note_type='DS'` (if `note_type` is present and exact); reject rows with null identity keys. If the inherited protocol has a narrower exact note-type rule, that rule takes precedence.
2. Order candidates by `charttime` ascending, then `storetime` ascending with nulls last, then `note_seq` ascending, then `note_id` ascending. Select the last row whose `charttime <= T_discharge`; if none exists, set `AVAILABILITY=1` and do not select a post-discharge substitute.
3. Preserve the full selected `text` byte-for-byte in a sealed, access-controlled packet; compute `sha256(text)` and record row identity, source ID, source hash, protocol version, and extraction status. The analysis table contains only the hash and predeclared derived fields until the text-concealed panel phase.
4. If multiple rows tie on every ordering field, require identical text hashes; otherwise set `AVAILABILITY=1` and issue a duplicate-conflict diagnostic. Never resolve a conflict by analyst preference.
5. Treat empty/whitespace-only text, decoding failure, and an explicit source truncation marker as unavailable. Record the exact reason code.

`note/discharge_detail` is not silently concatenated into the canonical note because it has no `hadm_id` or time field. It may be used only as a separately labeled structured corroboration source, joined on (`subject_id`,`note_id`) to the selected discharge note, with `field_name`, `field_value`, and `field_ordinal` preserved. Its presence cannot repair a missing or invalid canonical text artifact. This prevents an apparently complete packet from being manufactured by an ambiguous join.

The blinded annotation packet for each opportunity contains: admission key, eligible report key, report `charttime`, `T_discharge`, route-concealed unit stratum, selected discharge-note key/hash, availability reason, and the minimum text span windows needed for labeling. It excludes `discharge_location`, downstream outcomes, and route labels from the route-concealed panel until all atomic labels are sealed. A packet manifest must show one row per eligible admission-opportunity and a zero-row/duplicate-row check.

## Labeling and leakage controls

Two independently trained reviewers label each atom and bridge state from the sealed packet using the parent's ordered atomic categories and no synonyms. They must label `ACK`, `MENTION`, `NONE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, and `OUT_OF_SCOPE` separately before any reconciliation. `OUT_OF_SCOPE` is not a bridge state and is excluded only according to the parent's predeclared atom eligibility; it cannot be used to rescue a negative result.

Reviewers see no route, unit identity, discharge disposition, structured outcomes, or other panel's labels. They may not infer an action from medication orders, transfer patterns, later notes, or discharge location. A text span qualifies as `ACK` only if it is atom-specific and temporally framed as an intended plan or documented follow-up; generic disease mention, copied problem-list language, or retrospective history is at most `MENTION`. The panel records span offsets and a short coded rationale, not free-form post hoc reinterpretation.

Adjudication is a deterministic rule, not a gold-standard claim. If reviewers disagree, a third blinded adjudicator sees only the packet and disagreement codes. If the adjudicator cannot resolve the disagreement under the dictionary, the atom is `INCONCLUSIVE`; it contributes to the compatible-set uncertainty rather than being forced into `ACK` or `NONE`. Reviewer disagreement rates and all unresolved labels are reported.

## Analysis, baselines, and falsification

The primary analysis is the inherited exact finite-population, positive-probability two-phase estimate over all eligible opportunities, with the parent's route-concealed sampling probabilities, compatible-set enumeration, all-unit adverse precedence, and workload ledger unchanged. Add bridge states as finite categorical outcomes and compute exact compatible-set intervals for route-stratified `ACK`, `MENTION`, `NONE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, and `INCONCLUSIVE` rates. Use the same atom-level and admission-level aggregation rules as the inherited estimand; do not replace the exact design with a regression-adjusted complete-case analysis.

Prespecified descriptive baselines are (i) route-stratum-wide bridge-state proportions, (ii) the same proportions restricted to `AVAILABILITY=0`, explicitly labeled complete-artifact descriptive estimates, and (iii) a negative-control timing analysis using reports whose `charttime` is after `T_discharge`, which must not enter the primary denominator. The complete-artifact analysis is not a correction for missingness and cannot support a claim about undocumented care.

Falsification and audit checks are executable:

* all eligible admissions have exactly one population row and one inherited route state;
* every selected discharge note has matching (`subject_id`,`hadm_id`,`note_id`) and `charttime <= T_discharge`;
* no post-discharge note enters a primary packet;
* `discharge_detail` joins are one-to-many only through (`subject_id`,`note_id`) and never alter canonical selection;
* bridge-state counts sum to the number of labeled atoms, while availability reason counts sum to admissions;
* route-concealed panel manifests cannot be reconstructed from text hashes or packet order;
* permuting reviewer IDs leaves compatible-set intervals unchanged;
* replacing all report timestamps by post-discharge timestamps yields zero primary opportunities, and any nonzero count fails the run;
* a synthetic fixture with an identical text hash but conflicting source row identity is rejected, not deduplicated;
* exact rational lower and upper bounds agree with exhaustive enumeration on the parent's T0-T13 and O0-O4 fixtures, extended with B0-B7: valid ACK, related-only mention, absent text, post-discharge-only note, late-storetime exception, duplicate conflict, unresolved adjudication, and out-of-scope atom.

Any failed invariant blocks interpretation and is reported as an executable failure, not as an inconclusive clinical result.

## Interpreting outcomes

**Supportive outcome.** If route strata show a reproducible difference in `ACK` or `MENTION` intervals, with narrow enough compatible-set intervals and all timing/integrity checks passing, this supports an association between the inherited route strata and observable discharge-documentation states. If `UNAVAILABLE` is low and similar across strata, the decision-availability bridge is more informative. It still does not show that a recommendation was acted upon or that routing would improve care.

**Adverse outcome.** If intervals overlap materially, if `UNAVAILABLE` is common or route-differential, if `INCONCLUSIVE`/disagreement is high, or if timestamp/duplicate failures are concentrated in a route, the protocol does not justify a route-specific documentation conclusion. A high `NONE` rate with poor availability is an evidence-coverage finding, not proof of missed follow-up. If the inherited adverse-compatible route signal is absent, that is a valuable negative result and does not invalidate the bridge.

**Inconclusive outcome.** Empty opportunity strata, zero-probability cells, failed packet invariants, wide compatible sets, or unresolved adjudication above the sealed margin yield `INCONCLUSIVE` for that estimand. Do not collapse it into adverse or supportive. A successful computation with wide intervals remains scientifically inconclusive.
