# Episode 87: temporally locked HCC documentary-M2 confirmation with a unique-patient worst-state loss

Parent: [prior hypothesis]

## 1. Targeted repair

This proposal preserves the parent's adult first eligible hepatobiliary-resection population, operation and acquisition clocks, 90-day imaging and 365-day recorded-prior-treatment windows, patient-disjoint temporal roles, frozen models and reader/pathology books, whole-accession source-content states, fixed-capacity queues, q_REQ/q_LS/q_M2/q_DEC diagnostics, comparator gates, deletion checks, exact finite-corpus bookkeeping, and nonclinical evidence limits.

It makes two coordinated repairs.

First, the parent allowed the same threshold to be selected using both 2020 and 2021. That makes 2021 partly a tuning set and cannot test temporal nonreplication. All model, preprocessing, book, queue, tie-key and rational-threshold-grid choices remain frozen by the 2019 lock. A single threshold q* is selected using 2020 only. It is then frozen byte-for-byte and evaluated once in untouched 2021. No 2021 row, label, state count, feasibility count, comparator result, or threshold candidate may affect q*.

Second, deterministic source, clock, reader and pathology states are not sampling replicates. Treating them as multiplicity coordinates and applying a Clopper–Pearson family to them does not create future-patient uncertainty. The primary loss is therefore a unique-patient worst-state composite: each patient contributes at most one loss, equal to one if any admissible deterministic state loses that patient's protected documentary-M2 queue position or fails that patient's complete source-content certificate. State sensitivity is retained by the maximum, not by pretending states are independent observations.

A flaw in the proposed repair would be to call the resulting 2021 proportion a 95% population guarantee merely because it is patientwise and exact. That would still be invalid for this fixed, non-probability HCC corpus. This proposal corrects it: 2020/2021 results are exact finite-corpus replication results. A future-patient probability or confidence statement is explicitly inconclusive unless a separate study supplies a sampling frame, inclusion mechanism, exchangeability/independence assumptions, and prospective endpoint ascertainment.

## 2. Clinical question and falsifiable hypothesis

When review capacity is fixed, can a site choose one sub-complete stored-report coverage threshold before seeing a later year's data such that the resulting semantic queue retains all source- and label-stable documentary-M2-positive patients while every retained position is patientwise source-content-complete?

This matters because aggregate report coverage can look adequate while one high-risk patient's one required accession is missing, and a queue can preserve a count while dropping a particular documentary-positive patient. If the property survives a genuinely untouched year, it identifies a defensible target for an archive-linked silent-mode validation. If it fails in 2021, a 2020 success was temporal overfit and should not motivate deployment.

Primary hypothesis, with rho=.10 and d=24 hours, is:

> There is a rational q*<1, selected only from 2020 using the locked protocol, for which the 2020 unique-patient worst-state composite loss is zero and all inherited source, reader, pathology, fixed-K, comparator and source-content gates pass; when the identical q* and frozen queue machinery are applied without any 2021 tuning, the 2021 unique-patient worst-state composite loss is also zero and all required gates pass.

This is a retrospective, documentary-M2, finite-corpus hypothesis. It is not a hypothesis about biological microvascular invasion, actual report viewing, clinician action, treatment, recurrence, survival, safety, cost, fairness, or patient benefit.

The strongest claim supported before execution is only structural feasibility: the read-only HCC snapshot contains linked encounter, procedure, examination, pathology, medication and order tables with the identifiers and fields needed to construct this retrospective experiment. The full source scan recorded 105,044 encounters, 338,040 procedures, 419,996 examinations, 46,395 pathology rows, 4,097,517 medication rows and 16,730,319 order rows; these counts are provenance checks, not clinical validation. Existing accessible literature supports temporal/external validation as an important discipline, but does not establish this HCC queue. The natural-history article and methods-bearing XML were inspected locally; the Bayesian article text was inspected locally. For the cancer demonstration, only the available supplement and metadata were inspected; the main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable and are not cited as read.

## 6. New estimand: one loss per patient, worst over states

Let I_y be the union of unique patient IDs that belong to at least one eligible target-frame state in year y. Let D_y be the union of unique patients in P_LS(y,w) over all required coherent states w. If D_y is empty, the result is nonvacuous-inconclusive; no zero-event claim is allowed.

For a patient i in D_y, define the state-specific composite loss

d_{iy}(q,w) =
1{i in P_LS(y,w)} *
1{ i not in S_G(y,w,q) OR A_i(y,w,q)=0 }.

The unique-patient worst-state loss is

L_{iy}(q) = max over required w in which i is eligible of d_{iy}(q,w),

and the primary finite-corpus estimand is

theta_y(q) = [sum over i in D_y of L_{iy}(q)] / |D_y|.

The numerator is an integer number of distinct patients, never a count of state-patient cells. The OR is substantive: a protected patient is lost if the fixed-capacity queue omits that patient in any admissible state or if any of that patient's eligible accessions is not source-content-certified in that state. A state where i is not eligible contributes no loss for i; eligibility ambiguity itself remains a required source/feasibility gate and cannot be used to delete a difficult state. Emit each patient's loss, the witness state(s), queue rank/EMPTY status, accession exposure counts, P_LS/S_max membership and stable hashes.

This estimand preserves exact finite-corpus state sensitivity while eliminating multiplicity over deterministic ambiguity states. Do not use state count as a sample size, do not average states, do not let multiple rows or books give one patient multiple votes, and do not apply Clopper–Pearson or a family-wise alpha over states. The fixed corpus has no implied superpopulation sampling distribution.

The selection gate for 2020 is:

G20(q)=1 iff all required 2020 provenance, clock, state, book, nonvacuity, fixed-K, comparator, deletion, model-replay and inherited PASS(q) gates pass; every required state has K certified queue positions; and theta_2020(q)=0.

Select exactly one threshold by the predeclared rule

q* = min { q in R : q<1 and G20(q)=1 }.

The direction and burden meaning of q are inherited; choosing the smallest qualifying rational q is the least stored-content threshold satisfying the decision contract. The set R, exact rational arithmetic, q*, all rejected lower thresholds and their first failing witnesses are frozen in a signed 2020 selection artifact. If the set is empty, there is no selected threshold and the 2020 joint threshold hypothesis is adverse, provided computation and feasibility were valid. If the minimum is q*=1, the sub-complete hypothesis is adverse. No alternate q may be reported as a primary rescue.

After q* is frozen, evaluate exactly the same estimand and gates in 2021:

theta_2021(q*), K-certified positions, q-independent model/comparator checks, all source/reader/pathology states, and anchored deletion results. No 2021 threshold grid search, frontier minimization, or favorable state selection is permitted. The primary temporal confirmation requires theta_2021(q*)=0, K-certified positions in every required state, and all inherited comparator/deletion/source gates.

## 7. Exact finite-corpus replication versus future uncertainty

A successful 2021 result means exact replication in the specified 2021 HCC snapshot under the frozen states, books, models, capacity and documentary endpoint. It does not mean that future patients will have the same loss rate. An adverse 2021 result is genuine temporal nonreplication even if 2020 passed; it cannot be rescued by choosing q_2021, pooling years, averaging states/books, or reporting only favorable deletion/source cells.

A future-patient uncertainty claim is a separate estimand and is inconclusive here. To identify it, a subsequent archive-linked study must predeclare a target population and calendar window, a complete sampling frame, patient inclusion probabilities or an explicit iid/cluster sampling mechanism, exchangeability assumptions across sampled and future patients, independent patient-level endpoint ascertainment, report version/finalization/release/retrieval/readability/view timestamps, nonresponder accounting, and a prespecified design-based or model-based interval. If clustering by patient/site/year is present, the interval must respect that clustering. The current HCC snapshot provides none of these guarantees: it has local encounter dates rather than exact released dates, no report lifecycle/view telemetry, no report version lineage, no specimen/accession/time pathology linkage, and no documented sampling frame. Therefore no 95% future coverage, population probability, calibration, or transport claim may be printed from theta_2020 or theta_2021.
