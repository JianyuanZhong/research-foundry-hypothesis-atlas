# Identity-robust report coverage for the high-priority documentary-M2 queue

## 1. Successor, unresolved question, and clinical advance

This is a targeted child of \`[prior hypothesis]\`. It preserves that candidate's adult first-eligible-resection population, 24-hour confirmatory frame, whole-accession report units, coupled operation/source/identity/onset/reader/pathology worlds, fixed-\(K\) comparator/random/anchored-deletion gates, conditional \(q_{\rm req}\) contract, and nonclinical evidence limits.

The remaining decision-validity problem is concentration. A statement such as “at least \(q\) of eligible patients have a complete report bundle” does not by itself say whether the patients without a complete bundle are concentrated in the high-priority queue. If report exposure is selective, the stored-content semantic ranking can look useful while the patients for whom its ranking matters are precisely those falling back to \(G_{\rm mask}\). The parent’s \(q_{\rm req}\) is retained as a necessary global regression contract, but this child makes the identity pattern of exposure an explicit primary stress.

The clinical question is:

> At a fixed 24-hour pre-resection review capacity, does semantic CT/MRI report content retain a documentary-M2 capture advantage when complete report bundles may be missing preferentially among the patients that a masked semantic queue would prioritize?

The substantive advance is an auditable archive/workflow requirement that protects the high-priority queue, rather than accepting an aggregate report-coverage percentage. It can determine whether an archive integration is worth a silent-mode validation study, while remaining agnostic about report release, clinician viewing, action, treatment, and patient benefit.

The strongest claim supported before this experiment is only structural: HCC contains patient/encounter-linked procedure rows, examination rows with acquisition-like times, whole-accession identifiers, machine labels and narrative fields, same-encounter pathology narratives, and dated medication/order records. It has no report authored/final/released/viewed time or version history, no pathology specimen/slide/block/time linkage or sampling adequacy, and no capacity ledger, clinician action, treatment, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or utility. The unresolved claim is conditional documentary robustness of a queue under adversarial patient-specific report exposure—not biological MVI discrimination or clinical benefit.

## 2. Falsifiable hypothesis and estimands

Let \(I_{i\omega}=1\) indicate at least one eligible whole CT/MRI accession for patient \(i\) in source/operation world \(\omega\) at 24 hours, and let \(C_{i\omega}=1\) indicate that every such eligible accession is exposed under an onset/access state. Keep \(N_{\omega,a}\), \(n_{A,\omega,a}=\sum I_{i\omega}\), \(e_{\omega,a}=n_A/N\), and the parent’s conditional \(Q_{\omega,a}=\sum C/n_A\) for years \(a=2020,2021\).

Fit and freeze the parent’s \(B(Z)\), \(R(Z,S,Q)\), \(G(Z,S,Q,X)\), and same-fit \(G_{\rm mask}\) pipelines. For a test-year state \(z\), let \(r_i^G(z)\) be the full-content score and \(r_i^{M}(z)\) the operational masked score: \(G\) for a patient with a complete exposed eligible bundle and the same fitted \(G_{\rm mask}\) input otherwise. The queue is the exact top \(K=K_{.10}\) patient list under \(r^M\), with the parent’s frozen score/tie serialization and anchored deletion rule.

For \(j\in\{1,\ldots,K\}\), define the operational prefix \(P_j(z)\) as the first \(j\) patients in that masked queue and
\[
  \pi_j(z)=j^{-1}\sum_{i\in P_j(z)} C_{i\omega}.
\]
This is an exposure diagnostic, not a claim that a report was viewed. The queue’s documentary capture remains
\(H_G(z)=\sum_{i\in P_K(z)}Y_i\), where \(Y_i\) is the fixed pathology-book documentary-M2 terminal.

Define a finite, auditable prefix contract \(\boldsymbol{\pi}\succeq(p_1,\ldots,p_K)\) by requiring \(\pi_j(z)\ge p_j\) for every \(j\), year, coupled source state, reader/pathology book pair, onset state and deletion state. The confirmatory contract uses the predeclared two-level family
\[
p_j =
\begin{cases}
p_{\rm early}=0.80,&1\le j\le \lceil K/2\rceil,\\
p_{\rm all}=0.60,&\lceil K/2\rceil<j\le K,
\end{cases}
\]
with exact integer inequalities \( \sum_{i\in P_j}C_i\ge\lceil j p_j\rceil\). These thresholds are workflow stress thresholds, not observed capacity or stakeholder utilities. They are locked before any test outcome is read; \(p_{\rm early}=0.80\) protects the highest-priority half and \(p_{\rm all}=0.60\) prevents the rest of the queue from being treated as irrelevant.

Primary hypothesis, tested separately in 2020 and 2021 and jointly with one simultaneous error budget:

> At \(d=24\) hours, the maximum stored-content queue passes the parent’s comparator, random, and anchored-deletion gates, and there exists a nonempty, identity-aware prefix contract \(\boldsymbol{\pi}\) with positive missing-bundle tolerance under which the semantic queue’s documentary-M2 capture advantage remains \(>5\) per 100 offered slots against \(B\), \(R\), and \(G_{\rm mask}\), in every compatible coupled state.

The exact frontier is
\[
b_{\rm pref}^\star=\max\{b\in\{0,\ldots,K\}:
\text{all primary gates pass for every state with the prefix contract and at most }b
\text{ missing complete bundles in the fixed eligible frame}\}.
\]
The primary prefix contract is feasible only if its integer requirements are met in each state; \(b_{\rm pref}^\star>0\) is the positive reserve claim. Report \(b_{\rm pref}^\star\), the parent \(q_{\rm req}\), and the identity-specific boundary witnesses together. Do not substitute \(q_{\rm req}\) for the prefix contract: the former is a global conditional fraction; the latter protects the actual queue.

The mask family is exact, not a random missingness model. For each coupled state, enumerate or solve to certified optimality over all patient-level exposure masks with (i) the specified prefix inequalities, (ii) no exposure outside an eligible whole accession, (iii) no splitting of an accession bundle, and (iv) at most \(b\) incomplete eligible patients. Any incumbent gap, mask cap, unproved pruning, or coordinatewise mixing of states is computationally inconclusive. On small synthetic and source-backed fixtures, brute-force enumeration must agree with the certified integer formulation.

## 4. Falsification and interpretation

Use mutually exclusive one-hot labels:

1. \`computationally_inconclusive\`: lossless source replay, state generation, prefix feasibility, exact mask optimization, tie replay, simultaneous inference, or verifier fixtures fail.
2. \`feasibility_inconclusive\`: any \(N\), \(K\), event, \(n_A>0\), or prefix integer denominator gate fails.
3. \`maximum_content_adverse\`: maximum stored content fails an inherited 24-hour comparator, random, or deletion gate.
4. \`coverage_contract_adverse\`: maximum content passes, but \(b_{\rm pref}^\star=0\) or no permitted prefix contract is feasible; no positive identity-aware reserve is shown.
5. \`queue_robust_supportive\`: maximum content and all inherited gates pass, a positive \(b_{\rm pref}^\star\) exists, every exact prefix/boundary witness and simultaneous lower bound supports the strict margins, and the parent’s structural \((N,n_A,e)\) certificate is complete.
6. \`mixed_or_fragile\`: one predeclared year/contrast/prefix/deletion family is supportive while another is adverse, or the global contract passes only after relaxing a predeclared prefix; report the failing coordinates and do not collapse them into support.
7. \`archive_bridge_pending\`: the HCC computation is valid but no exact linked report-version/release/retrieval/readability audit certifies the exposure masks. This is not adverse evidence about semantic signal.

Supportive means only a finite-corpus, adversarial, documentary queue reserve. Adverse means the stated universal documentary claim is falsified, not that reports have no biological value. Inconclusive means the data or computation cannot decide. The verifier must reject a global \(q_{\rm req}\) or average coverage number attached to a supportive label when prefix counts and patient identities are absent, and must reject conclusions that ignore a failing year, deletion, contrast, or prefix.

No result identifies actual report release, finalization, ingestion, retrieval, readability, viewing or clinician action. A real archive bridge must supply the exact \((patient master index,encounter number,examination number)\) crosswalk, raw source-row ordinals and hashes, whole-version/predecessor/amendment/retraction identifiers, authored/final/released/ingested/retrievable/readable timestamps with timezone provenance, and patient-level disposition for \(I=0\), incomplete and complete strata. A probability audit must sample the fixed HCC-derived patient and accession frame, retain nonresponders, and produce simultaneous lower bounds for each required queue prefix; an overall lower bound is insufficient. The archive bridge cannot redefine the HCC population, operation, modality, accession grain or pathology frame.

Clinical adjudication is required for the procedure and prior-treatment dictionaries, operation-clock alternatives, CT/MRI eligibility, report semantic labels and pathology terminal labels. Additional expert review is required for specimen-level MVI validity and whether documentary M2 is an acceptable surrogate. A prospective silent-mode workflow study is required for actual availability, viewing, queueing, staffing, competing workload, clinician actions, harms, costs, treatment changes, recurrence, survival and patient utility. Until those data and stakeholder-defined utilities exist, \`clinical_action_status=not_identified\`; the experiment cannot recommend treatment, resection extent, transplant, surveillance, or a clinical service.
