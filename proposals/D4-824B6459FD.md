# Fixed-capacity observed-UACR test of raw-grip cystatin-C allocation

## Clinical decision, evidence boundary, and unresolved hypothesis

A service that can order cystatin C for only 20% of adults with baseline creatinine eGFR (eGFRcr) 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m2 needs to know whether grip strength changes whom it should test after using the kidney information already available. The leading parent added UACR to the comparison, but its A and AG policies rank all eligible people by imputing unavailable UACR. That is a valid all-population prediction experiment; it cannot alone show that grip adds value after a urine result is actually usable, or that grip can substitute for a usable UACR policy.

The strongest evidence available before opening any locked label is limited. Chen et al. (Kidney Medicine 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.xkme.2024.100796; PMCID PMC10986041; full-text XML inspected as frozen source [source checksum]) studied 468,969 UK Biobank participants. It supports that demographic, clinical, laboratory and lifestyle characteristics are associated with creatinine–cystatin eGFR differences and that an internally validated enriched model had AUC about 0.75. It does not establish a fixed-budget policy, incremental value beyond usable UACR, substitution for UACR, measured-GFR validity, or benefit from testing. The inherited read-only audit supports feasibility only: O contains 134,118 people and censor-aware baseline UACR is usable for 130,257 (97.12%), including 97.18% of locked test and 97.14% of locked replication. Physical headers and catalog schemas contain every required field. No locked H, D, K18 or policy yield was opened.

The unresolved compound hypothesis is:

> In each locked partition, after restricting every already-frozen score to the same participants with a usable censor-aware baseline UACR while preserving the absolute cystatin budget n=floor(0.20×|O_partition|), AG_V will identify at least 2 additional combined-equation eGFR<60 reclassifications per 1,000 assays versus A_V, with yield ratio at least 1.10 and positive marker-discordance yield; G_V will be noninferior to A_V at a margin of -1 reclassification per 1,000. If N18 event sufficiency passes, AG_V will add at least 1 reclassification followed by first 10-year inpatient N18 per 1,000 and G_V will be noninferior at -1 per 1,000. These results must replicate and all inherited all-O, B/F/T, placebo, missingness, calibration, sex-stability and N18 rules must also pass.

AG_V:A_V tests incremental raw-grip information after a usable UACR. G_V:A_V tests substitution among people for whom the UACR score can actually be applied. The latter is not a claim that UACR collection and grip collection have equal cost or burden.

## Usable-UACR stratum V and unchanged assay capacity

Use only baseline instance-0 urine fields. V is the subset of O satisfying either:

1. finite positive urine albumin 30500-0.0 mg/L and finite positive urine creatinine 30510-0.0 µmol/L; or
2. blank albumin, albumin result flag 30505-0.0 trimmed exactly to `<6.7`, and finite positive urine creatinine.

For numeric albumin a, UACR=1000a/c mg/mmol, where c is urine creatinine. For the exact low-result flag, the primary policy value is a=3.35 mg/L. A numeric-plus-flag contradiction is an integrity failure. Missing/nonpositive creatinine, missing albumin without that exact flag, another albumin flag, or a nonfinite ratio is outside V and is counted. Field 30515-0.0 is the urine-creatinine result flag and must never be parsed as albumin. "Usable" means computable under this prespecified interval-censoring rule, not clinically timed or persistent albuminuria.

## Analysis, uncertainty, and decision gates

Use 2,000 participant-paired bootstrap resamples within each lock without refitting. Extend the inherited max-|t| simultaneous family to prespecified V contrasts for H, the H ratio, D and H×K18, and pool locks with partition-stratified paired inference. Report point estimates, standard errors, simultaneous interval endpoints, bounds, selected-label availability and swap counts.

A selected participant with missing H/D contributes 0 or 1 to sharp lower/upper bounds. For paired contrasts, compute joint extrema from participant-level coefficients using one common unknown assignment for a shared participant; never subtract marginal bounds. For H×K18, a missing H contributes 1 to an upper bound only when K18=1. Compute exact feasible ratio extrema; a feasible zero denominator is inconclusive. Retain the inherited rule that >1% missing valid cystatin in O prevents a full allocation conclusion.

Biochemical V support requires in each lock:

- adverse-joint-bound Delta_H(AG_V:A_V) >=2/1,000;
- point yield ratio >=1.10 with simultaneous 95% interval above 1;
- simultaneous 95% interval for Delta_H above 0;
- positive H yield in AG_V\A_V versus A_V\AG_V;
- Delta_D(AG_V:A_V)>0 in each lock and pooled paired 95% interval above 0;
- one-sided simultaneous 95% lower bound for Delta_H(G_V:A_V)>-1/1,000; call superiority only when the two-sided interval is above 0.

The V N18 gate is evaluable only with at least 100 observed-label H×K18 events in A_V∪AG_V∪G_V across both locks and at least 20 in each pooled directional swap set for AG_V:A_V and G_V:A_V. Missing H cannot satisfy event counts. When sufficient, require adverse-bound Delta_H18(AG_V:A_V)>=1/1,000 with pooled two-sided 95% interval above 0, and the one-sided lower bound for G_V:A_V above -1/1,000. At least 30 events in a partition are required for a positive partition-specific N18 statement. Positive N17, a composite, development results or longer follow-up cannot rescue N18.

Every inherited all-O gate remains mandatory. C/CG, HbA1c, B_V and T_V are non-rescuing diagnostics.

## Falsification and interpretation

The compound hypothesis is supported only if all inherited all-O gates pass, both locked V partitions pass the biochemical AG increment and G noninferiority gates under joint adverse bounds, no frozen UACR variant reverses either contrast, N18 sufficiency passes, and the V N18 gates pass. This supports only prospective comparison of allocation strategies in a UKB-like setting.

A result is adverse when precision is adequate and either lock shows AG_V no better than A_V, the simultaneous upper interval excludes the prespecified 2/1,000 or 1.10 materiality target, G_V is inferior beyond -1/1,000, or N18 is sufficiently observed and fails its materiality/noninferiority gate. If all-O AG:A passes but V AG_V:A_V is adverse, the apparent increment is imputation/population dependent and cannot support the observed-UACR decision. If AG_V beats A_V but G_V is inferior, grip may complement UACR but cannot substitute for it. If G_V is noninferior but AG_V is not materially superior, grip may warrant prospective study as a lower-information substitute among people with usable UACR, but not as an incremental marker.

A result is inconclusive—not adverse—when intervals cross a decision margin, V coverage is below 95%, |V_p|<n_p, labels or directional swaps are sparse, N18 sufficiency fails, a feasible ratio denominator can be zero, locks disagree, a censoring variant reverses sign, or a join, date, flag, leakage, calibration or sex-stability integrity check fails. Biochemical support with insufficient N18 is reported as "biochemical allocation supportive; chronic-kidney consequence unresolved," never full support. Sensitivity results cannot rescue primary failure.

## Verification boundary

Fixtures must include: swapping 30505 with 30515; wrong UACR units; treating `<6.7` as measured 6.7; admitting an imputed-UACR participant into V; changing V across variants; selecting 20% of V instead of n_p; refitting in V or locks; unequal candidate pools/budgets; using cystatin, H, K18, death or future assessment in V or a score; marginal rather than joint missing-label bounds; rounded eGFR or altered hashes; mispaired diagnosis/date arrays; N17 censoring N18; same-day death after N18; and unsupported clinical conclusions after correct arithmetic. Test correct computations paired with incorrect claims, plus appropriate supportive, adverse and inconclusive narratives.

The automatic verifier can check hashes/headers, joins, O and V, equations, dates, score reuse, candidate pools, exact budgets, leakage, policy membership, bounds, uncertainty, event sufficiency, gates and whether reported conclusions follow computed outputs. It cannot establish that baseline UACR was clinically available before ordering, that albuminuria or low eGFR persisted, that combined eGFR equals measured GFR, that N18 is adjudicated CKD, that grip measures muscle mass, that costs/harms are acceptable, or that testing changes management or outcomes. Those claims require clinically timed repeat urine testing, measured GFR and body-composition data, outpatient/chart and prescription/action data, nephrology and equity review, costs, external transport validation, and a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.
