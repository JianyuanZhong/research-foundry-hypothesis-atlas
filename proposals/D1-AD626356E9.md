# Care-plan ventilation labels and an observable persistent respiratory-state trajectory

## Assigned evolution and clinical advance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the frozen lineage and does not reopen the already-settled label-vocabulary/transportability question. The clinical question remains: **within the locked labelled person-landmarks, does the exact care-plan label contain reproducible information about a subsequent persistent respiratory patient-state trajectory, beyond pre-label state and documentation opportunity?** This is a descriptive information/association estimand, not a causal treatment effect and not a claim about bedside SBT, extubation, readiness, benefit, or harm.

The substantive repair is to make the experiment executable even when the parent row artifacts are not delivered, without silently changing the cohort. It supplies a deterministic, versioned source rebuild with a byte-identity check and a hard lineage-divergence stop. It also makes endpoint observability explicit and less dependent on a rare multi-domain intersection: the primary endpoint is a repeated numeric, non-peak support trajectory with a separate safety-preservation endpoint and an explicit four-state observation/exit outcome. The prior multi-domain composite and airway-corroborated endpoint remain prespecified secondary/contextual analyses, not a reason to relabel a sparse primary result.

The strongest inherited evidence supports only sparse, hospital-concentrated structured label use, peak-pressure dominance in an earlier chart-setting composite, and inadequate independent liberation ascertainment. The untested claim here is narrower: after pre-D adjustment and genuine hospital holdout, label receipt adds information about a persistent numeric support trajectory. Any supportive result remains evidence about this eICU chart construct only.

## Frozen population, exposure, and estimand

The target population is exactly the parent lineage: 540 person-landmarks, 443 unique first qualifying ICU stays, and 74 hospitals, retaining all five landmarks `L = {1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200}` minutes after ICU admission. Repeated landmarks within `patientunitstayid`/`sid` remain together for inference; they are not independent replications. The exposure is the first exact `carePlanGeneral.cplitemvalue` in `[L,L+360]` equal to either:

* `evaluation`: `Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation`;
* `no_trial`: `Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial`.

`D` is that row's `cplitemoffset`, never `L`; same-time contradictory target labels retain the inherited exclusion. The primary descriptive estimand is the evaluation-minus-no-trial difference in probability of a persistent repeated numeric support reduction during `O = (D+360,D+1800]`, conditional on pre-D respiratory state and documentation opportunity. A secondary predictive estimand compares held-out log loss/calibration for state-plus-label versus the identical pre-D state-plus-opportunity model without the label. No all-ventilated prevalence or causal contrast is estimated.

## Outcome-blind parser freeze and endpoint observability

Before reading any label-arm counts or comparing outcomes, perform a full-source enumeration of `respiratoryCharting.respchartvaluelabel`. Write every raw label, normalized label, matched family, unmatched status, and parser-config SHA-256 to `parser_freeze.json`. The parser mapping is fixed, case/whitespace normalization is fixed, and no arm-specific synonym or threshold may be added after seeing endpoint counts. Required table/schema bindings are:

* `patient.csv.gz`, table `patient`, key `patientunitstayid`; use `uniquepid`, `hospitalid`, `unitvisitnumber`, `age`, `unitdischargeoffset`, `unitdischargestatus`, `unitdischargelocation`.
* `carePlanGeneral.csv.gz`, table `carePlanGeneral`, key `patientunitstayid`; use `cplgeneralid`, `cplitemoffset`, `cplgroup`, `cplitemvalue` only for exposure/lineage verification.
* `respiratoryCharting.csv.gz`, table `respiratoryCharting`, key `patientunitstayid`; use `respchartoffset` for clinical time, `respchartentryoffset` only for lag opportunity, and `respchartvaluelabel`, `respchartvalue`, `respcharttypecat`, `respchartid` for fixed parsing.
* `vitalPeriodic.csv.gz`, table `vitalPeriodic`, key `patientunitstayid`; use `observationoffset`, `sao2`, `respiration` as five-minute summary safety measurements, not waveforms.
* `respiratoryCare.csv.gz`, table `respiratoryCare`, key `patientunitstayid`; use `respcarestatusoffset`, `airwaytype`, and ventilator interval fields only for corroboration/availability.
* `treatment.csv.gz`, table `treatment`, key `patientunitstayid`; use `treatmentoffset`, `treatmentstring`, `activeupondischarge` only for the secondary airway validation sensitivity.

Numeric parsing accepts only finite numeric values after the frozen trim rule and field-specific bounds. Primary support families are FiO2, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set respiratory rate; peak inspiratory pressure is excluded from the primary because inherited evidence found it dominant and semantically uncertain. Bounds, unit assumptions, duplicate handling, and timestamp tie rules are recorded before label use. No unit conversion is inferred.

For each component and window, retain valid count, distinct clinical-time count, first/last offset, median, and maximum gap. A window is observed only with at least two valid observations at distinct offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. This prevents a single copied value from creating a transition. The six-hour washout between D and O prevents classification-time charting from defining the outcome.

### Primary repeated numeric endpoint

Let `B=(D-720,D]`, `P=(D-1440,D-720]`, and `O=(D+360,D+1800]`; `O12=(D+360,D+1080]` is a fixed horizon sensitivity. A row has a **repeated support-reduction event** when at least one of the four non-peak support families is observed in both B and O and its O median is lower than its B median by a fixed threshold: FiO2 `>=0.05`, PEEP/PEEP-CPAP `>=2`, pressure support `>=2`, or set rate `>=2`. The qualifying component must have at least two observations in each window; the reduction is a window-level persistent median difference, not a single timestamp or copied value. Report each component and the union endpoint separately.

The primary analysis reports the event only among rows with a fully observed support endpoint and no competing exit before `D+1800`. It never treats discharge/death/truncation as improvement or ordinary non-event. For all 540 rows, report mutually exclusive states: (1) repeated support reduction, (2) observed no reduction, including safety deterioration in the safety-preserving sensitivity, (3) endpoint missing because required numeric observations are unavailable, and (4) competing exit before the window closes. Also report discharge/death status, `unitdischargeoffset`, and no-post-washout opportunity (`<=D+360`).

A prespecified **safety-preserving sensitivity** requires the repeated support event plus `vitalPeriodic` safety measurements observed in both B and O, with O median `sao2` not falling >2 percentage points and respiration not rising >4 breaths/min. If safety is unavailable, the row remains missing for that sensitivity; absence is not safety. The prior multi-domain endpoint requiring respiratoryCare corroboration is retained as a secondary strict endpoint. An airway transition based on exact `No Artificial Airway` or a frozen treatment-string rule is validation context only and is unavailable/inconclusive if support is below 10% in either arm.

This separation makes the endpoint observable even if airway corroboration is sparse while preserving a clinically safer secondary interpretation. It does not claim that a charted numeric value is a delivered setting or that support reduction is liberation.

## Leakage control, opportunity adjustment, and held-out analysis

All predictors are strictly pre-D. The state model uses B/P numeric medians and trends, pre-D availability, counts, distinct clinical times, source presence, inter-record gaps, and `respchartentryoffset-respchartoffset` lag. The opportunity-only model uses pre-D counts/times/source presence/lag but no respiratory numeric values, care-plan strings, or post-D rows. The state-plus-label model adds only the binary exact label. No post-D count, endpoint component, treatment string, care-plan feature, or outcome-derived feature enters a baseline model. Post-D opportunity is reported descriptively and in a selection-sensitive inverse-observation-weighted sensitivity whose weights use pre-D variables, hospital, and landmark only, with fixed 1st/99th truncation.

Primary transport evaluation is leave-one-hospital-out. For each held-out hospital, all parsing summaries, imputation, standardization, regularization, missingness models, and calibration are fit on training hospitals only; no held-out target rate or label rate is used. Report row-weighted micro and equal-hospital macro log loss/Brier score, calibration, AUROC only when both classes exist, risk differences, support/missing/exit distributions, and fold-specific directions. A label-added gain must be compared with the identical state-plus-opportunity baseline, not merely a label-only model.

Uncertainty uses stay-cluster bootstrap (`sid`/`patientunitstayid`) within hospital strata, moving every landmark of a sampled stay together; no row bootstrap. Use fixed seed 61061 and 1,000 refits when feasible, otherwise at least 500 with the shortfall declared before outcome comparisons. Label permutations are within hospital-by-landmark strata with both arms, preserving arm counts and stay structure; fewer than 20 valid strata makes the permutation gate inconclusive rather than permitting a manufactured null.

## Falsification and hard computational gates

Run the same frozen parser and thresholds for:

1. a pre-D placebo (`P` versus `B`) and a lead-time placebo shifting exposure to `D-720`;
2. leave-one-component-out support analyses, including a peak-pressure ablation only as a negative/semantic sensitivity;
3. documentation-opportunity negative controls for post-window row density, unique times, and entry lag;
4. source-family ablations and the strict safety/airway secondary endpoint;
5. the 60-minute separation and first/last-summary sensitivity for copied persistence; and
6. within-stratum label permutations when valid strata exist.

The experiment is **computationally supportive** only if: lineage mode is byte-identical or deterministic-lineage-match with no row/D/label divergence; parser and source hashes verify; at least five hospitals each have at least 10 complete primary endpoint rows per label and at least 50% of complete rows are in those hospitals; both arms are present in estimable held-out folds; the event risk difference is positive with at least 4/5 estimable hospital directions positive; state-plus-label improves both micro and macro held-out log loss over state-plus-opportunity by at least 0.01 with a stay-cluster interval excluding zero; the gain is not comparable to the pre-D/lead-time placebo or valid permutation null and does not reverse under component/source ablation; and competing exits/missingness are reported and do not explain the value-based gain. The strict safety/airway endpoint may be supportive only if its own availability gate passes.

It is **adverse** only when endpoint support is adequate and a prespecified failure reproduces in a bounded sensitivity: no label gain beyond opportunity-adjusted state, hospital reversal, placebo/permutation explanation, one-component dependence, or documentation-only association. It is **inconclusive** for any lineage divergence, absent parser/source verification, fewer than five supported hospitals, no valid held-out arm contrast, sparse/unequal competing exits or missingness, failed positivity, unavailable strict corroboration, or intervals spanning meaningful gain and no gain. No relaxed threshold or outcome-guided parser is allowed.
