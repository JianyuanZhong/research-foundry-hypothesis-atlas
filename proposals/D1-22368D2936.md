# Proposal: Test restart-label robustness symmetrically, then validate rescue against pump-native delivery

## Unresolved question and clinical advance

This child preserves the parent’s negative feasibility findings and does not reopen a causal comparison. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, assigned actions remained rare: 211 NE-first actions (3.49%) and 167 VP-first actions (2.76%), both below the frozen 5% gate. The 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The inherited observed-stop cohort contains only 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate failures, respectively. Observed stop order is not assigned treatment, and these arm and event counts remain inadequate for a direct comparison.

The strongest evidence-supported claim is that the six-hour endpoint is computable but restart-dominated and sensitive to how adjacent EHR infusion transactions are joined. There are 73 raw failures among 136 determinate episodes; 59 failures involve a stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. The parent’s narrow transition screen marked 13 restarts, including 6 restart-only labels, leaving 67 failures when only those 6 labels were removed. The new complete symmetric transaction audit used all 137 inherited episodes without sampling. Depending on the prespecified continuity rule, 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 inherited stop boundaries cease to be stops. Thus retrospective EHR rules can reveal material label instability, but they cannot determine whether any interval represents pump-off time, resumed delivery, or rescue intent.

The unresolved hypothesis remains prospective and falsifiable:

> Among consecutively observed adults meeting the frozen dual-low-dose norepinephrine (NE) plus vasopressin (VP) stop phenotype, an EHR positive interval for the stopped drug beginning within six hours has positive predictive value (PPV) at least 0.80 for true resumed pump delivery undertaken for hemodynamic support in each observed-stop arm, with an absolute arm PPV difference below 0.10.

Resolving this question is clinically consequential because restart contributes to 59/73 raw failures. A future discontinuation-order trial should not use an endpoint that primarily records bag changes, brief pauses, order reconciliation, transfer continuation, or chart segmentation. Conversely, showing that EHR restarts usually correspond to adjudicated rescue would support an auditable endpoint for a later trial. Neither result identifies a preferable stopping order.

## Frozen evidence that does not advance

All inherited negative findings remain binding:

- Assigned-strategy positivity failed: NE-first 211/6,040 (3.49%); VP-first 167/6,040 (2.76%). No-action landmarks are not controls.
- The observed cohort remains underpowered: 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate failures.
- The endpoint remains restart-dominated: 73 raw failures, 59 involving restart, 36 restart-only, 13 narrow transition-compatible restarts, 6 transition-compatible restart-only labels, and 67 failures after removing only those 6.
- Mechanical circulatory support (MCS) remains unknown in 137/137 episodes. A non-positive MCS record is never treated as evidence of absence.
- The compact M1 model improved formal 2017–2019 holdout Brier score by only about 0.000174, with uncertainty spanning zero. M2 improved on M1 by only about 0.000111, far below the 0.01 gate. Transport in 2020–2022 was adverse. No score, low-risk threshold, or subgroup advances.
- EHR transactions are neither pump delivery nor clinician intent. Observed order is not treatment assignment. No causal effect, bedside recommendation, or safety claim is estimated.

## Stage 1: executed Harbor-computable symmetric audit

### Population, index, and temporal boundaries

Use the inherited cohort without resampling or eligibility repair: one first qualifying non-simultaneous documented stop in the first ICU stay for each of 137 adults. Time zero (`t0`) is the inherited end of the first-stopped agent’s reconstructed positive interval, after at least six hours of concurrent NE and VP while the comparator remains active. Eligibility was established at or before `t0`: alive and in ICU; NE `>0` and `<=0.20 mcg/kg/min`; VP `>0` and `<=0.04 U/min`; neither increasing in the prior 30 minutes; no active epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours; and the inherited pre-stop MAP coverage and stability rule. Future restart, MAP, comparator continuation, exit, and death are outcomes or ascertainment variables, not eligibility.

The raw outcome window remains `(t0, min(t0 + 6 hours, ICU exit, death)]`. The inherited raw composite includes stopped-agent restart, rescue/competing pressor initiation, confirmed NE escalation, repeated MAP below 60 mmHg, death, or pressor-active ICU exit. One episode is indeterminate. This child does not replace or retrospectively optimize that endpoint.

### Dose normalization

Rules are identical for both observed-stop arms. A positive interval requires `rate > 0`, a supported rate unit, and `endtime > starttime`.

- NE item 221906: retain mcg/kg/min directly; divide mcg/min by recorded `patientweight` only for weights 30–300 kg; reject normalized values above 5 mcg/kg/min.
- VP item 222315: divide units/hour by 60; retain units/min; reject values above 0.2 units/min.

`orderid` and `linkorderid` are documentation-continuity signals only. `statusdescription`, `storetime`, `caregiver_id`, `isopenbag`, and `continueinnextdept` are diagnostics, not delivery truth.

### Symmetric reconstruction rules

For the stopped agent, apply each rule identically to adjacent normalized positive rows. Starting at the inherited boundary row, connect a subsequent row only when the selected bridge criterion is met. If the boundary connects forward, inherited `t0` is invalidated under that reconstruction; it is not relabeled as a successful stop. This tests ascertainment stability rather than outcome benefit.

- **R0 row-exact:** bridge overlap or exact contiguity only (`gap <= 0 minutes`).
- **R1 chain-15:** bridge a gap `<=15 minutes` when either row pair shares an `orderid` or `linkorderid` token.
- **R2 chain+dose-60:** bridge a gap `<=60 minutes` when a chain token is shared and the normalized dose ratio is 0.8–1.2. This is the parent’s narrow transition-compatible definition applied as a reconstruction rule.
- **R3 chain-60:** bridge a gap `<=60 minutes` when a chain token is shared, without dose concordance.
- **R4 dose-60:** bridge a gap `<=60 minutes` when the dose ratio is 0.8–1.2, without requiring a shared chain.

These are bounded sensitivity definitions, not competing clinical truths. R0 is the least permissive, R2 is the inherited narrow screen, and R3/R4 separately expose dependence on chain and dose assumptions. None may be selected post hoc as a corrected endpoint.

The audit therefore establishes an ascertainment range: plausible but unvalidated transaction-joining choices invalidate 0.0% to 16.8% of inherited indices and 0.0% to 39.0% of raw restart indices. Under the parent’s narrow R2 rule, 13/137 indices (9.5%) and 13/59 restart indices (22.0%) are unstable. This is not a false-positive rate because no pump-native reference standard exists.

Transaction metadata deepens, rather than resolves, the concern:

- Of 59 restart boundaries, 35 were labeled `Paused` and 24 `Stopped`; all 13 R2-invalidated boundaries were `Paused`, while 0/24 `Stopped` boundaries met R2.
- The subsequent row was labeled `ChangeDose/Rate` in 41/59, `Paused` in 10, `FinishedRunning` in 4, and `Stopped` in 4.
- A shared chain was present in 30/59 and 20% dose concordance in 41/59. The same caregiver was recorded in 46/59.
- `isopenbag=0` and `continueinnextdept=0` at both boundary and restart in all 59; these fields provide no discrimination in this sample.
- Boundary `storetime-endtime` had median -32 minutes and range -976 to 107 minutes; negative and highly variable transaction lags make `storetime` unsuitable as delivery truth.
- Charted MAP existed between `t0` and restart for 49/59. A broad chronological physiology signal (any MAP <65 or last MAP at least 10 mmHg below the pre-index median) occurred in 38/59. Only 17/59 met the review-priority combination of `Stopped`, distinct chain with at least a 10-minute gap, and this physiology signal. This is a review stratum, not a validated rescue label.

The strict R2 instability is concentrated among `Paused` rows, but that does not prove that `Stopped` rows are true clinical cessations. Likewise, deterioration before a charted restart may reflect indication, treatment response, measurement timing, or unrelated care and cannot establish rescue intent. Most restarts remain unresolved under every narrow rule. Retrospective validation is therefore impossible with MIMIC alone.

### Reproducibility and retrospective falsification checks

The executable retrospective component must reproduce, from the frozen cohort and source members:

1. 137/137 usable inherited boundaries and 59/59 raw restarts;
2. the five rule-summary rows above, including R2 invalidation of 13 indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first);
3. 35 `Paused` and 24 `Stopped` raw-restart boundaries, with all 13 R2-invalidated boundaries in the `Paused` stratum;
4. 38/59 broad pre-restart physiology signals and 17/59 review-priority records; and
5. complete availability of the listed transaction fields among raw restart pairs, while recognizing that field presence is not semantic validity.

A mismatch falsifies computational reproduction and requires code/provenance repair. Agreement supports only the aggregate transaction audit, not the prospective endpoint hypothesis.

## Stage 2: prospective pump-native endpoint validation

### Population and enrollment

At ICUs capable of synchronized smart-pump and EHR export, screen consecutive adults forward in time using the same phenotype: supported concurrent NE and VP for at least six hours; NE `>0` to `0.20 mcg/kg/min`; VP `>0` to `0.04 U/min`; no increase in either in the prior 30 minutes; no epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine active in the prior two hours; at least three MAP observations spanning three of four prior 30-minute bins including the final bin; pre-stop median MAP at least 65 mmHg and minimum at least 55 mmHg; alive and in ICU. Actual MCS state is captured but never inferred from a missing EHR record.

An episode enters when a clinician executes or documents a non-simultaneous first stop while the comparator remains active. Treatment is observed, not assigned. Keep all consecutive eligible episodes, including apparent EHR non-restarts, so sensitivity, specificity, and negative predictive value can be estimated without verification-bias sampling.

The EHR candidate-positive definition remains the first normalized positive interval for the stopped drug with `starttime` in `(t0, min(t0+6h, ICU exit, death)]`. The primary reference-positive definition requires: (a) pump-native evidence of a genuine off interval followed by delivered stopped drug above a prespecified minimal delivered-dose threshold, and (b) adjudicated intent to resume hemodynamic support. A secondary pump-only reference omits intent so technical validity and intent validity remain distinguishable.

### Exact additional data

For each episode collect, with source identifiers and synchronized timestamps:

1. **Pump-native delivery:** pump/channel identifiers, drug-library identity, concentration, programmed and delivered rate, start, stop, pause, resume, bolus, alarm, channel association, seconds-level timestamps, and export/downtime gaps.
2. **Consumable/workflow transitions:** bag or syringe identifiers where available, replacement, empty-container, line or concentration change, channel handoff, pump replacement, procedure/transport pause, unit transfer, and reconciliation.
3. **EHR medication transactions:** drug identifier, `starttime`, `endtime`, `storetime`, rate/unit, amount/unit, `totalamount`/unit, `patientweight`, `orderid`, `linkorderid`, `statusdescription`, `isopenbag`, `continueinnextdept`, caregiver and order/administration audit trail.
4. **Clinician intent:** timestamped intended stop, permanent discontinuation versus temporary pause/titration, restart decision, reason, rescue intent, and relation to hypotension, perfusion concern, procedure, transport, or correction. Missing intent remains unknown.
5. **Physiology and cointerventions:** continuous or high-frequency MAP with arterial/non-invasive source and quality flags, all vasoactive pump channels, fluid/bolus events, death and ICU exit; lactate and perfusion findings are context, not substitutes for the reference label.
6. **MCS truth:** device type, insertion/removal, active/standby state, flow/support setting, and device/registry source confirmation for IABP, Impella, ECMO, LVAD/RVAD, or other support.
7. **Clock and capture quality:** mappings among pump, monitor, EHR, and adjudication clocks; measured offsets; daylight-saving/time-zone handling; export gaps; and periods when linkage is not possible.

### Blinded reference standard

Generate a time-local packet for every consecutive stop, centered on the EHR boundary and six-hour window. Two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators inspect pump traces, workflow transitions, physiology, cointerventions, and audit trail while blinded to aggregate arm results and model predictions. Classify each candidate as:

- true resumed delivery for hemodynamic support;
- uninterrupted delivery split by EHR documentation or consumable/channel transition;
- brief technical or procedural pause without rescue;
- transfer continuation;
- clock/timestamp mismatch;
- EHR interval without delivered drug;
- no EHR interval but pump-native restart; or
- indeterminate.

Report raw agreement and chance-corrected agreement; a third reviewer resolves disagreement. Prespecify the minimum pump-off duration and minimal delivered-dose threshold after a blinded technical run-in and before any arm-specific PPV is opened. Always report sensitivity analyses at 1, 5, 10, 30, and 60 minutes of pump-off time. Do not change the primary threshold after viewing PPV.

### Staged sample, estimands, and stopping rules

**Technical run-in.** At each site, process the first 50 consecutive eligible stops without examining arm-level PPV. A site proceeds only if at least 95% of eligible windows have linkable pump/EHR clocks and at least 90% have complete pump export over the candidate window. Otherwise pause recruitment, repair capture, and repeat a new 50-episode run-in. If capture cannot meet both gates, that site stops and contributes feasibility data only.

**Validation accrual.** Accrue at least 50 EHR restart candidates in each observed-stop arm, retaining all intervening EHR-negative stops. After 30 restart candidates in an arm, an independent monitoring analyst may stop that arm for clear adverse validity only if the multiplicity-adjusted one-sided 98.75% upper binomial confidence bound for PPV is below 0.80. There is no early declaration of success. Stop the study as inconclusive if indeterminate adjudication exceeds 10% in either arm after at least 30 candidates and cannot be reduced by resolving data-capture defects.

At 50 candidates per arm, report results but continue accrual when the 95% confidence interval for the arm PPV difference is too wide to distinguish the 0.10 symmetry margin. Continue up to 250 restart candidates per arm or 24 months, whichever occurs first. Failure to resolve the margin by that cap is an inconclusive result, not symmetry. This staged design preserves the inherited minimum while making precision failure and operational futility explicit.

Primary estimands are EHR-restart PPV against the intent-inclusive reference separately in NE-first and VP-first episodes and the absolute arm difference, with score/exact 95% intervals. The endpoint-validity hypothesis is supported only if, at final analysis:

- point-estimate PPV is at least 0.80 in each arm, with lower confidence bounds reported;
- the absolute PPV difference is below 0.10 and its 95% interval does not include differences of 0.10 or larger;
- indeterminate adjudication is below 10% in each arm;
- no material site, unit, year, pump-system, or capture-gap concentration is present; and
- interpretation is stable across prespecified off-duration sensitivities and after excluding clock/export failures.

Secondary estimands are pump-only PPV, sensitivity, specificity, negative predictive value, confusion matrices, and artifact fractions attributable to pause, bag/syringe/channel change, transfer, and clock mismatch. Prespecified diagnostic strata are boundary `Paused` versus `Stopped`, shared versus distinct chain, R2-positive versus R2-negative, and presence versus absence of pre-restart physiology. These strata test transport of retrospective diagnostics; they do not define treatment groups or authorize post hoc endpoint cleaning.

### Supportive, adverse, and inconclusive interpretations

**Supportive:** both arms satisfy PPV and symmetry gates, indeterminacy and missing capture are low, sensitivity is acceptable, and results are stable across sites and thresholds. This supports use of the restart component in a later prospective study with the validated capture process. It does not validate the other composite components, prove clinical importance, establish safety, or identify a superior stopping order.

**Adverse:** either arm’s PPV is below 0.80, the arm difference is at least 0.10, sensitivity is poor, or a material fraction of EHR restarts are uninterrupted delivery, technical pauses, workflow transitions, or timestamp artifacts. Raw EHR restart is then falsified as a sufficiently valid symmetric endpoint. Future studies must use pump-native delivery plus adjudicated intent or a newly validated rule.

**Inconclusive:** intervals remain too wide, the 0.10 margin is unresolved, more than 10% are indeterminate, clock/export gates fail, VP-first restart accrual is inadequate, workflows are nontransportable, or actual MCS state remains unavailable. The remedy is better capture, additional sites, or another study—not lowering the PPV gate or retrospectively relabeling MIMIC outcomes.

## Verification boundary and frozen conclusion

An automatic verifier can check cohort counts, item/unit normalization, time windows, adjacency logic, rule-specific invalidations, transaction distributions, MAP chronology, provenance hashes, and prospective confusion-matrix calculations. It cannot determine whether an EHR interval was delivered, whether a pause was clinically intended, whether a restart was rescue, whether MCS was truly absent, or whether randomization is safe and ethical. Those require synchronized device data, clinical adjudication, and expert review.

The retrospective result is adverse for the idea that a single EHR continuity rule can repair the endpoint: reasonable symmetric rules move 0 to 23 stop indices, transaction fields do not provide a reference standard, and most restarts remain unresolved. The inherited positivity, power, MCS, and prognostic gates remain failed. This proposal advances only a staged endpoint-validation experiment. It estimates no causal discontinuation-order effect and recommends neither NE-first nor VP-first.
