# Episode 73 successor: a total all-row estimand for the frozen eICU respiratory-label trajectory

## Scientific question and evidence boundary

The unresolved, falsifiable question is whether either exact care-plan label adds reproducible information about a subsequent, independently charted respiratory-support trajectory after accounting for respiratory state and documentation opportunity available before the label decision time, and whether that information survives leave-one-hospital-out evaluation. This is a descriptive predictive/associational question, not an intervention effect. The strongest existing evidence in this lineage is limited to sparse, hospital-concentrated literal labels and unstable earlier proxy associations; it does not establish that either string denotes an SBT, extubation evaluation, readiness, delivered ventilator settings, benefit, harm, or quality.

I inspected the available research-ambition material. The readable text of Shmatko et al., *Learning the natural history of human disease with generative transformers* (Nature 2025) motivates longitudinal prediction and explicit competing future events, but its UK Biobank/Danish disease-token setting does not validate this eICU label or respiratory endpoint. No unavailable main paper or supplementary method is used as evidence. The present experiment advances that general trajectory principle by making observation, exit, and structured-chart ambiguity explicit rather than treating an observed trajectory as a complete clinical history.

## Exact endpoint windows and source-independent trajectory

Use clinical offsets, always relative to `D`, and preserve the frozen windows:

* baseline `B=(D-720,D]`;
* early `E=(D,D+720]`;
* late `T=(D+720,D+2160]`.

`respchartentryoffset` is never a clinical time. It is retained only for a pre-D documentation-lag feature/diagnostic. All displayed intervals are machine-audited for strict/open and inclusive/closed boundaries.

The four primary numeric families are FiO2 (including only the source-wide frozen aliases), PEEP/PEEP-CPAP, pressure support, and set ventilator rate. Before any arm-stratified output, enumerate every source-wide `respchartvaluelabel` and `respcharttypecat`, matched and unmatched counts, every `airwaytype`, and every patient discharge status/location. Freeze the family map and invasive-airway lexical rule from that enumeration; no label-stratified frequency or outcome may alter it.

The parser manifest is frozen before endpoint extraction. Trim whitespace and case-normalize only as declared in that manifest; parse finite numeric values; treat empty, malformed, ambiguous, and out-of-bound values as missing. For deterministic unit handling: a FiO2 numeric value in `[0,1]` is a fraction, a value in `(1,100]` is divided by 100, and anything outside `[0,100]` is invalid; PEEP/PEEP-CPAP is valid only in `[0,40]` cmH2O, pressure support only in `[0,60]` cmH2O, and set rate only in `[0,100]` breaths/min. A same-component/same-clinical-offset collision is reduced to the median of all valid parsed values at that offset; if none is valid it is missing. The manifest records every parse reason and counts. These plausibility bounds are parsing bounds, not outcome thresholds.

For every family and each B/E/T window, retain valid value count, distinct offsets, first and last clinical offset, median, and maximum gap. A component is observed only if it has at least two valid values at distinct clinical offsets separated by at least 30 minutes. A respiratory state is observed only if at least three of four components are observed. Missing is never zero, unchanged, or imputed. Component change is the B-median to T-median change with unchanged frozen thresholds: FiO2 absolute fraction `0.10`, PEEP `2 cmH2O`, pressure support `2 cmH2O`, and set rate `2 breaths/min`.

Define each family as `decrease`, `increase`, or `neutral` only when its threshold is met. The primary three-class trajectory is mutually exclusive and assigned only after endpoint-status precedence below:

1. `sustained_de-escalation`: B and T states observed; at least two families decrease; no family increases; and the early airway check is not positive and is not indeterminate for purposes of this class.
2. `escalation`: B and T states observed and at least two families increase, or a newly documented invasive airway occurs in E or T after no invasive evidence in B under the frozen airway rule.
3. `stable/mixed`: every other B/T-state-observed row, including one-family change, opposing changes, no threshold change, or an indeterminate early check that prevents de-escalation but does not supply positive escalation evidence.

The early check is three-valued and printed for every row: `positive` if the frozen invasive-airway rule identifies an E/T event after no B evidence; `negative` if no such event is present and the required airway records/interval comparisons are determinate; `indeterminate` otherwise. Indeterminate E observation can prevent only `sustained_de-escalation`; it never converts an absent B/T state into a trajectory and never creates escalation. This makes the parent sentence “E missingness can only prevent de-escalation” executable without treating missing charting as unchanged.

## Falsification and interpretation gates

Run all gates from machine-readable outputs before interpreting label direction.

* **Integrity:** parent hashes, exact labels/landmarks/D, one-to-one identity, parser freeze, status precedence, no post-D baseline fields, and no care-plan/treatment endpoint leakage must all pass.
* **Observability:** supportive feasibility requires at least 40% of 540 rows classifiable and at least 10 hospitals with both labels among classifiable rows; below 20% classifiable or below five such hospitals is adverse feasibility; intermediate support is inconclusive. Competing exits and non-exit unobservability are reported separately.
* **Incremental information:** computational support requires label-added improvement in both micro and equal-hospital macro loss for the all-row five-cell outcome and the classifiable trajectory outcome in the primary plus at least two frozen endpoint sensitivities, with stay-cluster uncertainty excluding zero in the favorable direction and no reversal in more than 25% of estimable supported hospitals. A gain confined to exit/unobservability or documentation density does not support a patient-state interpretation.
* **Trajectory coherence:** evaluation/no-trial direction for de-escalation must agree with the non-peak-support and airway-corroborated sensitivities in at least two overlap hospitals with at least 10 rows per label, without early-versus-late reversal.
* **Placebos:** repeat the same change rules for `(D-1440,D-720]` versus B and use the lead-time shift to `D-720` with the retained outcome window. A comparable or larger placebo increment is adverse to a newly informative post-label interpretation.
* **Documentation negative control:** predict post-window row density, distinct clinical times, and entry lag from pre-D features and label. Opportunity-only improvement without a value-based trajectory improvement supports workflow/documentation opportunity, not patient-state information.
* **Ablation/permutation:** remove each setting family and airway corroboration in turn; compare the observed label increment with the valid permutation distribution. A result surviving only one source, one component, one hospital, or invalid permutation strata is not supportive.

Supportive results establish only incremental prediction of this eICU structured-chart construct. Adverse results show failure of added information for this endpoint in this snapshot, not absence of an evaluation or harm. Inconclusive results identify failed parent artifacts, insufficient observation, competing exits, sparse hospital overlap, parser instability, nonpositivity, or unavailable permutation support. No threshold relaxation, vocabulary expansion, endpoint relabeling, post-hoc mapping, or conversion of missingness into stable/mixed is permitted.
