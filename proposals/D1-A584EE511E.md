# Record-defined stress and the observed association of red-cell transfusion in ICU anemia

## Status and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive MIMIC-IV child of the repairable imported expert seed [prior hypothesis] ([starting question]). It preserves the seed's clinical question: at similar hemoglobin, might the relationship between red-cell transfusion and subsequent deterioration differ when oxygen supply/demand stress is present? It converts that question into a bounded, falsifiable, explicitly noncausal observed-risk experiment.

No cohort size, prevalence, fitted coefficient, risk, calibration result, or clinical conclusion is claimed here. The future Harbor solver must newly fit the two prespecified model families and report:

1. an auditable flow from all ICU stays through each eligibility, coverage, bleeding-proxy, exposure, and outcome rule;
2. a first qualifying decision episode per patient/admission with exact t0, hemoglobin, PRBC exposure, stress phenotype, covariates, competing outcomes, and unresolved follow-up;
3. held-out standardized observed-risk contrasts by stress state, with component outcomes, calibration, overlap and subject-level uncertainty;
4. a transparent aggregate baseline, a matched chronological learned alternative, process-only and chronology controls, and a conclusion ledger linking every conclusion to computed outputs;
5. support-gate status, rejected rows/reasons, unit and time audits, and a record of what the data cannot adjudicate.

The actual deliverable is a new estimate of heterogeneity in the association of a six-hour PRBC start with a 24-hour observed deterioration outcome, not a fitted treatment-effect model and not a bedside transfusion policy.

## Unresolved question and evidence boundary

The strongest evidence available before this experiment is not evidence for the proposed interaction. The expert seed identifies a consequential uncertainty and warns about indication confounding and incompletely observed ischemia. A current public-web search returned model-mediated summaries of MINT, in which 3,504 patients with acute myocardial infarction and hemoglobin below 10 g/dL were randomized to restrictive or liberal strategies: the primary 30-day death/recurrent-MI result did not conventionally reach significance, although its point estimates favored liberal transfusion. A separate search summarized the 2025 AABB AMI guidance as conditional and low-certainty. These are public-search summaries, not locally acquired paper text; no claim of having inspected the full MINT article or guideline is made. Other searched observational work argues that hemoglobin alone does not guarantee improved tissue oxygenation. None establishes this MIMIC hypothesis.

The untested claim is:

> Among adult ICU decision episodes with hemoglobin 6.5–8.0 g/dL and no recent PRBC exposure, is the adjusted observed 24-hour deterioration association of starting PRBC within the next six hours different between a high and low record-defined stress phenotype, after conditioning on the same pre-decision physiology, severity, bleeding proxies, care context, and observation intensity?

For stress stratum s, let Delta_s be the held-out model-standardized observed-risk difference between the labels T=1 (a valid PRBC start in [t0,t0+6h)) and T=0 (no valid PRBC start in that interval), retaining each subject's measured pre-t0 covariates. The primary heterogeneity contrast is H = Delta_high - Delta_low. H is an association contrast under model standardization. It is not a counterfactual effect, benefit, harm, appropriateness, treatment recommendation, or proof of ischemia-specific effect. Later transfusion after the six-hour window is recorded as a secondary contamination/censoring descriptor; T=0 means no observed start by six hours, not “never transfused.”

A positive H in the prespecified direction would motivate prospective adjudication of physiologic context in transfusion trials. A null would argue that these available EHR proxies do not reliably stratify the observed transfusion association. A reversal would be clinically important but would first be treated as an indication/process signal requiring replication. Either direction is scientifically useful only if support, calibration, and falsification gates pass.

## Population, time zero, and temporal rules

Use MIMIC-IV 3.1. Join icu/icustays to hosp/admissions on (subject_id, hadm_id), and to hosp/patients on subject_id. Keep adults with anchor_age >=18; preserve MIMIC's anchor_age=91 representation of age >89. Select the first ICU stay in each hospital admission with a qualifying primary hemoglobin and enough follow-up for the exposure window; within ties sort by intime, outtime, then stay_id. Keep at most one episode per subject across the entire analysis, choosing the earliest qualifying admission/stay deterministically. Report a sensitivity allowing one episode per admission, but never mix admissions across subject partitions.

The primary hemoglobin is the first hosp/labevents item 51222 (Hemoglobin, Blood, Hematology) with finite numeric valuenum, accepted g/dL unit, valid charttime, and 6.5 <= value <= 8.0 during the selected ICU stay. t0 is that result's clinical charttime. The nearest valid result in the preceding six-hour feature window is used for covariate summaries; if multiple candidates are equally near, choose latest charttime and then smallest labevent_id. A blood-gas hemoglobin, item 50811, is a prespecified sensitivity source, not silently pooled with item 51222.

Require at least 18 hours from t0 to ICU outtime or in-hospital death, and require the complete [t0,t0+6h) exposure ascertainment interval unless death or ICU exit occurs first. Such episodes are retained in an exclusion/censoring flow and are not assigned T=0. Exclude any PRBC administration overlapping the preceding 12 hours or still running at t0. Exclude a record-defined major-bleeding proxy before t0: procedureevents item 229620 (Massive Transfusion) in the prior 12 hours; a cumulative recorded PRBC amount >=2,000 mL in the prior six hours when amount/amountuom are valid and comparable; or an active operative/trauma signal from a frozen, label-reviewed ICD procedure/diagnosis list in hosp/procedures_icd and hosp/diagnoses_icd. If the code list cannot be frozen before outcome inspection, the code-based exclusion is omitted and only the two direct event proxies are used, with that limitation reported. The remaining cohort is called “no observed major-bleeding proxy,” never “nonbleeding.”

Every pre-t0 feature row must have clinical time <=t0 and storetime <=t0. For interval rows, require finite starttime < endtime, storetime <=t0, and clinical overlap with the feature window. A charted-before-but-stored-after row is excluded from the primary feature set and included only in a charttime-only sensitivity. All windows are within-subject relative windows; the metadata says MIMIC timestamps are subject-specific shifted and cross-subject calendar alignment is invalid.

## Exposure and record-defined stress

T=1 is any valid inputevents PRBC row whose starttime is in [t0,t0+6h), with itemid 220996 (Packed Red Cells), 225168 (Packed Red Blood Cells), 226368 (OR Packed RBC Intake), or 227070 (PACU Packed RBC Intake). Require a treatment-compatible statusdescription, finite starttime, and an administration-compatible row; audit endtime, amount, amountuom, rate, rateuom, totalamount, order fields, and duplicate/link order fields. Do not infer a unit from an order without an administration-compatible event. Primary exposure is any start, not dose. Secondary descriptions are first start, >=1,000 mL when recorded units support it, and exclusion of OR/PACU IDs. FFP, platelets, and other blood products are not PRBC exposure.

T=0 is no qualifying PRBC start in [t0,t0+6h). Later starts are not relabeled as T=1. The solver must report the proportion of T=0 episodes receiving PRBC in [t0+6h,t0+24h] and a secondary analysis censoring at that first later start; neither secondary is causal. MIMIC does not identify clinician intent, refusal, blood availability, completed-unit status, exact indication, or whether an order was clinically administered.

Define a primary record phenotype S using a 12-hour pre-t0 window. It is an EHR stress proxy, not measured oxygen delivery/consumption, tissue hypoxia, ischemia, or microcirculatory failure.

Supply-side signals:

- lactate >=2 mmol/L using hosp/labevents itemids 50813, 52442, or 53154, after unit validation;
- MAP <65 mmHg using icu/chartevents itemids 220052, 220181, or 225312, requiring at least two distinct valid clinical times when used as a repeated signal;
- positive-duration vasoactive input using icu/inputevents itemids 221289/229617 (epinephrine), 221662 (dopamine), 221749/229630/229631/229632 (phenylephrine), 221906 (norepinephrine), 222315 (vasopressin), with 221653 and 221986 included only if dictionary/unit validation confirms the intended agent;
- troponin is not a primary stress component because units and positivity thresholds are not guaranteed comparable. Troponin-T chartevents item 227429 and hospital troponin items 51002, 51003, and 52642 are sensitivity components only, using local reference/units rather than an invented universal cutoff.

Demand/support signals:

- HR >=110 using chartevents item 220045;
- RR >=24 using item 220210;
- FiO2 >=0.40 using item 223835, or PEEP >=5 using item 220339, only when units are valid;
- an active invasive or non-invasive ventilation interval in procedureevents item 225792 or 225794, with start/end/store-time rules above.

High observed stress requires either at least one supply-side and one demand/support signal, or at least two independent supply-side signals. Low observed stress requires no high-stress rule and adequate coverage of both domains: at least one valid supply-side observation and one valid demand/support observation plus at least two valid physiologic observations overall. All remaining episodes are stress-indeterminate/insufficient and remain in the flow but are excluded from the primary H contrast. Missingness is not zero exposure or zero stress. Thresholds, unit acceptance, signal precedence, and duplicate handling are frozen before outcome/model inspection. Report each component separately.

## Covariates, outcomes, and ascertainment

The pre-t0 adjustment set is common to both models. It includes hemoglobin and its time since measurement; lactate, MAP, HR, RR, SpO2, FiO2, PEEP, pressor exposure, ventilation support and measurement counts; creatinine (hosp/labevents 50912/52546), platelets (51265/53189), INR (51237/51675), pH (50820), and bicarbonate (50882) when units and finite values pass audit; recent non-PRBC input totals only when units are interpretable; age, gender, anchor_year_group, admission_type, ICU first/last care unit, time from intime to t0, hospital day, and prior hospitalization/ICU count; transfer context; frozen diagnosis/procedure indicators for infection, cardiac/respiratory disease, surgery and bleeding; and counts, missingness, time-since-last-observation, and store lag. Diagnoses use hosp/diagnoses_icd joined to hosp/d_icd_diagnoses on (icd_code, icd_version); procedures use hosp/procedures_icd joined to hosp/d_icd_procedures on the same code keys. No feature after t0 is adjusted for. Discharge and radiology text are excluded from the primary whitelist; no note-based ischemia or bleeding adjudication is claimed.

The primary outcome Y is any first event in (t0,t0+24h] among: in-hospital death; new invasive ventilation; new vasoactive support; or new renal replacement therapy. Death uses admissions.deathtime, checked against hospital_expire_flag. New invasive ventilation requires no qualifying procedureevents item 225792 or 224385 in the pre-t0 six-hour window and a post-t0 start of item 225792 or intubation item 224385. New vasoactive support requires no active target-pressor interval immediately before t0 and a qualifying post-t0 inputevents start. New renal replacement uses procedureevents item 225441 (hemodialysis), 225802 (CVVHD), 225803 (CVVH), 225805 (peritoneal dialysis), 225809 (CVVHDF), or 225955 (SCUF) after t0 when the item is present and unit/status/time-valid; the solver must include its exact item audit and not silently substitute an unverified code. Report death before nonfatal support in mutually exclusive first-event tables.

Secondary outcomes are the components separately, the same composite through 48 hours, and a competing first-transition table: death, nonfatal escalation, ICU exit/readmission before ascertainment, known no event, and unresolved U. Alive hospital discharge is not itself recovery. Post-ICU outcome capture is incomplete; U is never recoded as no event. Report crude T×S rates before standardization.

## Matched transparent and chronological alternatives

All models use the identical eligible subjects, feature whitelist, t0, target, split, outcome ascertainment, unit rules, and test set.

B0, the transparent baseline, is a penalized logistic model for Y with T, S, T×S, a restricted spline for hemoglobin, and the complete prespecified covariate blocks. It uses training-only scaling, imputation, spline knots and penalty selection. On the held-out set, set T to 1 and 0 for each subject while retaining measured covariates, then average within each S stratum to obtain Delta_s and H. This is a standardized model association, not a counterfactual treatment effect. Report Brier score and calibration intercept/slope overall and within T×S, as well as overlap and component-outcome estimates.

M1 is a substantive masked chronological GRU, hidden size 32 or 64, five fixed seeds, over twelve one-hour bins in the same 12-hour pre-t0 window. It receives the same physiologic values, interval pressor/ventilation exposure, missingness masks, counts, time-since-last-observation, and pre-index context as B0; T is a separate post-index label input. No post-t0 rows enter the tensor. Two treatment-label heads produce the same model-standardized Delta_s and H, and the same calibration/component outputs are required.

B0 loses within-window order and irregular co-movement: it cannot distinguish lactate rising before pressor initiation from lactate falling after support, nor whether an oxygen-support escalation preceded the hemoglobin measurement. M1 can reveal nonlinear ordering and trajectory information beyond the fixed stress phenotype. That information is clinically substantive only if it stabilizes H, improves calibration, survives chronology controls, and separates from B0; a small predictive gain alone is not clinical value. Bmask, a process-only model retaining masks, counts, store lag and care/measurement variables while removing physiologic values, is required to test whether H is documentation intensity or indication selection.

Use a subject-level 70/15/15 train/validation/test split, stratified only on pre-index T, S, event, and anchor_year_group; all records for a subject remain in one partition. Freeze all preprocessing before test scoring. If support permits, repeat as leave-one-anchor-year-group-out transport sensitivity. A GPU is not mandatory: B0 and archive extraction are CPU-first; M1 may use one allocated A100 with cuda:0 if a bounded pilot shows repeated CPU fits exceed the future solver envelope. The configured discovery limit is 7,200 science seconds and two concurrent jobs. A numeric future solver planning envelope is not present in this invocation's inputs.json, so full-study runtime and memory are unverified; no result is claimed from this discovery work.

## Support, uncertainty, and falsification

Before interpreting H, require in each S×T cell at least 100 episodes and 20 known Y events; at least 80% known outcome ascertainment per primary cell; propensity overlap for observed T of at least 0.05 after predeclared trimming; effective sample size at least 100 per cell; and no impossible-unit or post-t0 leakage audit failure. These are estimability gates, not evidence of clinical validity. If a gate fails, report flow, descriptive rates, and model feasibility, but mark H not estimable; do not merge stress groups or change thresholds after looking at outcomes.

Use 1,000 subject-level bootstrap replicates, resampling subjects and all their eligible rows together. Refit B0 and M1 when feasible. If the solver reports conditional-prediction rather than full-refit bootstrap, label it separately, report completed replicates and failures, and do not present it as full model uncertainty. Report intervals for Delta_low, Delta_high, H, component risks, calibration and the proportion of later transfusion in T=0.

The clinical-materiality threshold is |H| >=0.05 absolute 24-hour risk points. Supportive evidence requires: all gates pass; B0 and M1 give concordant H direction; the two-sided interval excludes the null region in the hypothesized direction; calibration is acceptable; component results are not driven by one unsupported endpoint; and Bmask, treatment permutation, exposure-time shift, and chronology shuffles do not reproduce the signal. This supports only a replication/prospective adjudication hypothesis.

Adverse evidence is a reproducible null or reversal, model disagreement, worsening calibration, H that is comparable in Bmask, or persistence after treatment/time/chronology falsification. This argues against this MIMIC phenotype as a reliable stratifier, not against biological transfusion-effect heterogeneity. Inconclusive evidence is sparse cells, high U, poor overlap, failed unit/availability audit, unstable bootstrap, or anchor-year disagreement; it requires better data or expert review rather than a more complex model.

Falsifications and sensitivities are frozen before fitting:

1. permute T within hemoglobin band, stress stratum, anchor_year_group and partition;
2. shift treatment labels 24 hours earlier while preserving patient/process structure;
3. shuffle early/late order in M1 while preserving values, masks, counts and marginal exposure;
4. compare Bmask and measurement-intensity-only models;
5. use non-PRBC blood-product rows selected from inputevents labels/ordercategoryname as an exposure-process negative control, explicitly not a biologic negative control;
6. repeat with item 50811 hemoglobin, lactate threshold 4 mmol/L, 7.0–8.0 g/dL, charttime-only availability, troponin omitted, troponin added only with valid local units, and OR/PACU PRBC IDs omitted;
7. repeat the stress definition with arterial MAP only, MAP/pressor-only supply signals, and each stress component separately.

## Required expert review and claim limits

MIMIC cannot observe complete active bleeding, transfusion intent, blood availability/refusal, completed-unit receipt, ECG-confirmed ischemia, symptoms, coronary anatomy, echo/cardiac output, direct oxygen delivery or consumption, microcirculation, adjudicated myocardial infarction, or all post-ICU outcomes. Troponin and diagnosis codes are record proxies, not adjudicated ischemia. PRBC item rows may reflect documentation and setting; OR/PACU rows are a prespecified sensitivity, not proof of bedside administration. The local text extractor is explicitly unvalidated for general diagnosis extraction and is not used to fill these gaps.

Even a stable positive H would establish only an association between an indexed PRBC start and observed 24-hour deterioration that differs across an operational record phenotype in this MIMIC snapshot. It cannot establish causal benefit/harm, an optimal transfusion threshold, treatment appropriateness, preventability, net benefit, ischemia-specific biology, or transportability. Stronger conclusions require clinical adjudication of bleeding and ischemia, prospective measurement of oxygen-supply/demand state, a target-trial-quality treatment record with intent and censoring control, and preferably randomized or externally validated data.

The principal deferred alternatives are a causal g-formula/TMLE or dynamic treatment regime (not credible without intent, time-varying confounding, censoring and support validation); note-based ischemia/bleeding classification (no validated local adjudicator); and a large transformer (complexity cannot recover missing clinical evidence and does not answer a distinct uncertainty). A causal model may be revisited only if expert review supplies a new estimand and additional treatment/indication evidence. M1 may be deferred for the concrete tensor/runtime reasons above; B0 remains the minimum scientifically interpretable experiment.
