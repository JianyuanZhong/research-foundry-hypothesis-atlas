# Calendar-robust measured-result information for the next recorded HCC transition

## Episode-31 successor decision and scientific deliverable

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`. The parent established the strongest currently supported HCC question: whether strictly prior assay-specific results add information beyond test identity/occurrence and measurable capture intensity for an exhaustive, admission-anchored recorded procedure/discharge transition. This child retains that estimand and adds a clinically consequential unresolved claim: whether the increment survives a later documentation and practice regime.

The solver must newly produce:

1. a frozen adult first-locally-recorded-HCC inpatient cohort, strict-prior and anchor-linked history manifests, duplicate/multiplicity/unmatched-key audit, and patient-disjoint calendar partitions;
2. the exhaustive seven-state transition labels and precedence/timestamp audit;
3. frozen A/L/Q/I/V feature manifests and fitted transparent A/L/Q/I/V models;
4. matched irregular continuous-time S/S_I/S_Q fits at the same information sets;
5. locked predictions for both a random patient-held-out evaluation and a calendar-held-out evaluation;
6. paired log-loss and Brier contrasts, calibration, patient-clustered uncertainty, capture-stratum and era-stratum results;
7. value/result, assay-identity, history-time and patient-label falsifications, plus the gated order-procedure audit;
8. a claim-to-output table separating computationally checkable conclusions from claims requiring adjudication or another study.

Completion is established by these frozen manifests, predictions, uncertainty/falsification reports, and interpretation table. A positive score is not required for completion.

## Unresolved question, hypothesis, and clinical importance

Existing evidence supports only a narrower claim: routine liver-related measurements can be associated with HCC prognosis, and EHR testing/recording intensity can itself be informative. The local files do not establish active HCC, liver reserve, stage, tumor burden, indication, treatment intent, treatment receipt, response, toxicity, survival, or benefit. The accessible research-ambition material also shows that learned longitudinal models can exploit dated structured histories, but its reported training evidence is from other datasets and is not a claim about this HCC snapshot; the cancer demonstration's main article and complete STAR Methods are unavailable here, so no reproduction claim is made.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> Among adults at their first locally recorded HCC-coded inpatient admission, strictly pre-admission assay-specific results/values and their within-assay trajectories improve calibrated prediction of the complete next recorded procedure/discharge transition beyond admission context, current-admission laboratories, prior capture intensity, and prior assay identity/occurrence; this V-minus-I increment remains directionally present and calibrated in admissions from a later calendar era held out from fitting.

The first clause is the parent information hypothesis. The new second clause tests transport across calendar eras in which data capture, assay ordering, coding and procedure documentation may change. That matters before any future result-aware transition support could be trusted for contemporary coordination: a random split can look good while learning a historical documentation regime.

A supportive result is a V-minus-I improvement on the locked later-era evaluation (lower multiclass log loss and/or Brier, 95% interval below zero, without material calibration deterioration), with attenuation under within-assay result permutation and consistency across prespecified capture-intensity strata. This supports only calendar-robust incremental information about local recorded care. It does not establish that a clinician should choose a procedure or that using the information improves outcomes.

An adverse result is no later-era increment, calibration failure, reversal relative to the random split, reproduction of the V increment by I or Q, persistence after value permutation, or a learned gain reproduced by S_Q. This would falsify the temporal-robustness claim or indicate that apparent result information is observation/process artifact.

An inconclusive result includes too few later-era admissions or transition states, dominant unresolved timing states, severe left truncation/sparse strict history, coarse timestamps that defeat ordering, unstable fits, wide clustered intervals, or a failed secondary semantic gate. Inconclusive is not confirmation. Stronger claims about clinical utility, treatment benefit, transport beyond this institution, and biologic meaning require chart adjudication, units/reference ranges, stage/pathology/tumor burden, treatment exposure and outcomes, prospective validation, or another study.

## Population and temporal design

Use HCC snapshot `[source checksum]` and catalog [source checksum]. All listed HCC sources are ordinary read-only CSVs; no archive member is used.

Join `diagnoses` to `encounters` on the exact key (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). Normalize Unicode and surrounding whitespace in keys and diagnosis text, and preserve matched strings, diagnosis type, duplicate keys and unmatched keys. Define HCC-coded as normalized `诊断名称` equal to `肝细胞癌` or a case-insensitive substring match to hepatocellular carcinoma. This is a reproducible record-coding rule, not clinical adjudication.

Sort each patient's HCC-coded encounters by native `入院时间`, then `就诊号`. Retain the earliest HCC-coded record only when it is an adult admission (`年龄` >=18 and <120), with parseable `入院时间` and `出院时间`, nonnegative stay, and admission in [2011-01-01, 2026-01-01). If that earliest HCC-coded record has invalid admission timing, exclude the patient rather than substituting a later encounter. Keep early discharges and unresolved-time admissions.

Set t0 to `入院时间). Primary windows are half-open:

- strict prior history: [t0 - 730 days, t0);
- current labs: [t0, t0 + 12 hours);
- early endpoint: [t0, t0 + 24 hours);
- later endpoint: [t0 + 24 hours, t0 + 72 hours).

A primary strict-prior row has valid native event time in the prior window and joins to an encounter whose native `入院时间` is strictly earlier than t0. Apply this to labs, procedures, orders and medications. Anchor-linked rows with pre-t0 event timestamps are excluded from primary history and retained as a labelled sensitivity branch. Report invalid event times, invalid linked encounter times, duplicate/multiplicity, unmatched keys, boundary/tie rows, after-discharge rows, left truncation and no-history counts. Add the parent’s [t0-730d,t0-30d) negative-control branch.

The primary random split remains SHA-256(normalized `患者主索引`) modulo 100: 0-59 fit, 60-69 selection/preprocessing/calibration, 70-79 locked evaluation, 80-99 inaccessible. All rows for a patient remain together; fit-only dictionaries/scaling/rules/calibration are frozen before locked evaluation.

The new calendar-shift evaluation uses the native t0 year, without tuning on locked outcomes: development years 2012–2018, selection years 2019–2020, and locked later-era evaluation years 2021–2025. Patients must be disjoint across these calendar partitions; report any cross-era patient overlap and exclude overlapping patient IDs from the temporal estimand before fitting. If any partition lacks at least 200 eligible admissions, at least 20 examples of each nonempty terminal state, or has >50% unresolved labels, report temporal evaluation as inconclusive and do not replace it with a post hoc boundary. The 2010–2011 records are a descriptive early-era sensitivity only because the cohort requires 2011+ and those years have minimal encounter volume. Calendar boundaries are fixed before fitting and cannot be moved to obtain support.

## Outcome

Construct an exhaustive admission-anchored seven-state terminal vector from `procedures.开始时间` and `encounters.入院时间`/`出院时间`. In [t0,t0+24h), assign P0 when a valid procedure starts in-window and before discharge; D0 when discharge is at or before 24 hours before any established valid early procedure; U0 when missing/invalid procedure timing could change ordering or window assignment; otherwise R24. Among R24, assign P1, D1, U1 and N72 analogously in [t0+24h,t0+72h). Use fixed procedure-wins precedence for exact discharge/procedure ties and rerun discharge-wins as sensitivity. Procedures before admission, after discharge, at boundaries, multiple procedures and midnight starts receive counts. Missing timing is unresolved, never no procedure.

The primary estimand is the paired held-out V-minus-I difference in multiclass log loss and Brier score for the complete seven-state vector. Lower is better. The parent-comparable V-minus-Q and I-minus-Q contrasts are secondary. A later-era V-minus-I contrast is the new transportability estimand; it must be reported alongside, not substituted for, the random split. R24-conditional results are secondary only.

## Information sets and alternatives

All models use the same patients, labels, preprocessing policy and split within each evaluation design.

- A: age, sex, admitting department, calendar era, admission time-of-day, admission missingness and strict-history availability.
- L: A plus assay-specific summaries of `labs.检验`, `定性结果`, `定量结果`, `标本类型`, `检验时间` in [t0,t0+12h): first/last valid numeric result, change, elapsed time, count, density/missingness and slope only with two distinct times, plus assay-specific qualitative results.
- Q: L plus strict prior capture only: encounter/source counts, duration/active days, recency, source-specific row/distinct-encounter counts for labs/procedures/orders/medications, source-type counts, missing-time count, left-truncation and no-history indicators. Q contains no assay names, results, values, procedure/order/medication names, doses, routes, frequencies or statuses.
- I: Q plus prior lab assay identity/occurrence summaries (assay identity, assay-specific count, recency, span, missingness and number of distinct assays), but no qualitative results or quantitative values.
- V: I plus strict-prior assay-specific `定性结果` and `定量结果`, including dated within-assay changes/slopes when at least two distinct valid times exist. Numeric scaling is fit-only within assay; no cross-assay pooling or unvalidated physiologic score is allowed.

The transparent baseline is the prespecified A/L/Q/I/V feature summary with regularized multinomial logistic regression or another fixed, auditable multiclass model declared before fitting. It answers the nested scientific question directly and is the adequacy floor.

The substantive learned alternative is a low-dimensional continuous-time event model S using the same strict prior rows, event source/type, elapsed time, assay identity, payload mask and assay-specific lab payload; S_I removes lab values/results but retains assay identity; S_Q removes assay identity and values while retaining process/timing. Fix latent dimension, decay family, masking/reconstruction term, shrinkage and seed count before locked evaluation; tune only on selection data. Evaluate supervised terminal-state negative log likelihood, held-out Brier/log loss, calibration and patient-clustered intervals. The alternative could reveal order-sensitive irregular trajectories, nonlinear interactions, missingness patterns and representation uncertainty that first/last/count summaries lose. It is not retained for a small score gain alone: it must be seed-stable, calibrated, and show the same nested content contrast beyond S_Q.

A mechanistic ALBI/MELD-like branch is deferred because the actual lab schema has no separate unit column or validated reference ranges, and the catalog does not provide stage, tumor burden, pathology timing or a clinically reviewed assay map. Revisit only if expert review supplies those dependencies. This is a question-specific deferral, not a blanket ban on mechanistic models or neural networks.

## Analysis, uncertainty and falsification

Report paired random-split and later-era contrasts with 95% patient-clustered paired bootstrap intervals (at least 1,000 resamples, preserving all admissions for sampled patients), state-specific calibration intercept/slope, reliability curves, observed/predicted probabilities, and per-state contrasts. Stratify descriptively by no strict history, 1–2 and >=3 prior encounters, capture-intensity strata fixed in fit data, and 30/180/730-day history windows.

Required falsifications are: within-patient and fit-only assay-stratum permutation of values/results; assay-identity permutation; prior-history time permutation preserving counts; replacement with anchor-linked rows; t0-30d negative-control exclusion; forbidden future-window predictor model explicitly labelled leakage; shuffled patient IDs before splitting; and discharge/procedure tie-precedence sensitivity. The temporal claim additionally fails if the locked later-era gain is absent, reverses, loses calibration, or is explained by a process-only model.

The order-procedure layer is secondary and gated. Normalize only frozen substrings in `procedures.手术` and `orders.医嘱(非药品)` into embolization/TACE, resection/transplant, ablation, systemic/infusion, diagnostic/access, and other, preserving blank/multi-match/ambiguous strings. A concordance requires same key, nonblank order, valid order `开立时间` strictly before procedure `开始时间`, and one shared family. Require >=100 uniquely family-assigned procedure admissions, >=80% timed procedures nonblank/exactly-one family, >=70% candidate orders with resolvable `开立时间`, no family >90% of concordant events, and <=25% dependence on midnight ties/unresolved timing. If it fails, secondary is inconclusive and primary remains unchanged. This is temporal documentary concordance, not intent, recommendation, receipt, completion or benefit.

## Compute and interpretation boundaries

The solver planning envelope is at most 16 CPUs, 262,144 MiB RAM, 8 allocated GPUs and 28,800 seconds. Expected but unverified workload is 1–4 hours for chunked aggregation of 2.2 GB labs and 2.19 GB orders, 1–4 hours for transparent fits/locked predictions, 2–8 hours CPU-first for the continuous-time alternative, and 1–4 hours for >=1,000 clustered bootstrap resamples. A GPU is optional; a GPU implementation may be used for the learned sequence model only if a bounded probe confirms the cached `ehr-campaign-gpu:20260908` environment and the solver declares gpus=1. No discovery result here establishes full-study feasibility or any scientific conclusion.

Computationally checkable claims are cohort/partition correctness, no forbidden-window leakage, feature nesting, score/calibration/interval calculations, permutation attenuation, and whether later-era V-minus-I survives the prespecified gates. Clinical adjudication or another study is required for active disease, intent, treatment choice, benefit, utility, biologic mechanism, external transportability and patient safety.

## Demonstration dispositions

- Delphi/natural-history: its dated structured-history idea is a useful learned alternative, but the reported UKB training trajectory and Danish validation are unavailable for this HCC adaptation. Use only the matched HCC S/S_I/S_Q EHR adaptation; do not call it a reproduction.
- Bayesian/ALADYNOULLI: the latent longitudinal structure is a scientifically substantive alternative, but its genetic analysis cannot be reproduced because verified HCC genetics are absent. The EHR-only continuous-time adaptation is retained; the mechanistic lab-score branch is deferred for missing units/reference ranges.
- Oncoformer: the accessible supplement supports multimodal ablations but the main paper and full STAR Methods are unavailable, and HCC has no local images. No multimodal reproduction is proposed; lab-only/structured temporal modeling is used only as the bounded adaptation above.

## Selection rule

The primary scientific deliverable is the later-era locked estimate of calibrated V-minus-I information, jointly reported with the random-split estimate and process/result falsifications. Select V-minus-I as supportive only when the prespecified later-era interval, calibration and permutation gates pass. Otherwise report adverse or inconclusive evidence exactly as defined; never convert readiness or a random-split gain into clinical truth.
