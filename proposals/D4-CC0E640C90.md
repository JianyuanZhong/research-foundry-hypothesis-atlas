# Prospective direct pairwise subtype experiment: CRC versus other-GI and CRC versus non-GI

## Lineage, purpose, and substantive advance

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` under the Episode 19 goal. It preserves that candidate's frozen selected-case population, time rules, CBC semantics, outcome construction, covariates, adverse orientation, `FAIL_UNCERTIFIED` status, and evidence limits. It does not reinterpret the executed raw local score blocks or any of their p-values. The prior three-group global non-rejection remains prior evidence only: it was a valid test of unspecified three-group residual heterogeneity, not evidence for either named pairwise absence.

The repair is prospective and mathematical. Each named comparison is now defined on its own pair-restricted population and has a direct covariate-standardized mean-difference estimand. The score is an augmented inverse-probability/standardization score for the pair, rather than a raw block of a mutually exclusive three-group residual moment. Consequently, changing the third group's trajectory distribution cannot change the estimand for a given pair except through the explicitly defined pair population and covariate distribution. No pairwise result from the parent is carried into the new analysis.

Clinically, the unresolved question is whether the adverse-oriented repeat-to-repeat CBC trajectory signal, if present among recorded malignancies, is localized to CRC relative to other gastrointestinal malignancy and/or to malignancy outside the GI site group. This could inform whether a future protocolized CBC signal is disease-localizing rather than merely a generic cancer-associated signal. The available experiment cannot establish screening benefit, early detection, mechanism, or prospective cancer risk.

## Direct pairwise estimands

For pair `q=(a,b)` in `{(CRC,other-GI),(CRC,non-GI)}`, retain only participants with `G in {a,b}`. Let `T_q=1` for `a` and `0` for `b`, let `C=(age at L, sex, repeat Hb, repeat MCV, repeat platelet, lag_late)`, and let `Z=(Z_Hb,Z_MCV,Z_PLT)` be the three fixed-scale standardized slopes. The target for component `j` is the covariate-standardized direct mean difference

`theta_{qj}=E_q[m_{aj}(C)-m_{bj}(C)]`,

where `m_g(C)=E_q[Z_j | T_q=g,C]` and the expectation is over the empirical covariate distribution in the pair-restricted cohort. Positive values mean the CRC group has a more adverse-oriented trajectory than the comparator for that component. This is a selected recorded-case association, not a causal effect.

The estimand is identified under pairwise consistency, positivity `0<e_q(C)<1`, and conditional mean exchangeability as an associational standardization condition. It is directly interpretable in pair-specific standard-deviation units and does not depend on the excluded third group's outcome mean. The pair-specific nuisance propensity is `e_q(C)=P_q(T_q=1|C)`, with `m_a(C)` and `m_b(C)` estimated separately. Use the cross-fitted augmented score for each participant:

`phi_{qj}=m_a(C)-m_b(C) + I(T_q=1)/e_q(C)*(Z_j-m_a(C)) - I(T_q=0)/(1-e_q(C))*(Z_j-m_b(C))`.

Estimate `theta_q` by the mean of held-out `phi_q`; estimate its covariance with participant-level influence contributions, not repeated fold appearances. The pairwise score is mathematically tied to the named contrast. Do not replace it with `I(T=1)-e(C)` times an outcome residual, a raw CRC score block, or a CRC-minus-other-GI block from the three-group analysis. A cross-fitted partially linear implementation may be used only if its algebra is proven equivalent to the displayed AIPW estimand before execution.

Use 20 repetitions of five-fold cross-fitting with seeds 20260909--20260928, preserving the parent convention of averaging each participant's score over all held-out appearances before one covariance estimate. Splits are pair-specific but must be deterministic, participant-level, and stratified by pair group, sex, and `lag_late` whenever every cell permits; otherwise use pair group with disclosed fallback. All preprocessing, tuning, clipping decisions, and nuisance fitting are fold-local. Ridge logistic propensity and ridge linear outcome regressions are the constrained default, with the parent's penalty grid `10^-4,...,10^4` and largest tied penalty. No outcome or labels are used to choose contrasts, transformations, gates, or analysis branches.

Report both unstandardized annualized `S` differences and the primary standardized `theta`, but the primary inferential object is the three-vector `theta_q` for each pair. Also report the pairwise covariance, component intervals, and the observed pair sizes and complete-case sizes. Do not infer that standardized differences represent clinically important thresholds: no minimal clinically important difference is available here.

## Multiplicity and interpretation hierarchy

The primary family is the six component hypotheses `theta_{CRC,otherGI,j}=0` and `theta_{CRC,nonGI,j}=0`, `j in {Hb,MCV,PLT}`. Use one participant-level multiplier distribution jointly over all six components, with a two-sided max-statistic global p-value and simultaneous 95% intervals. The global gate is alpha 0.05.

If and only if the six-component global gate rejects, test each pair's three-dimensional null with its within-pair max-statistic p-value. A pair-localization claim opens only when the global gate and that pair's gate both reject. If a pair gate opens, component-level claims require the component's simultaneous-within-pair interval to exclude zero and pass the same support/calibration gates. This closed hierarchical rule controls the intended familywise error without treating the two pairs as independent. The pair labels and directions are fixed before execution. If the global gate does not reject, component estimates and intervals remain descriptive and neither pair is interpreted; importantly, this is not equivalence.

A prior global non-rejection from the parent cannot be used as a gate for this new experiment, and this new experiment cannot retroactively rescue the parent's raw labels. The primary decision is solely the prespecified six-component direct-pairwise analysis.

## Support, calibration, and numerical gates

Before interpreting any p-value or interval, all of the following must pass separately for both pairs:

1. Exact pair construction, no duplicate `eid`, complete finite outcomes/covariates, correct 75/69/854 parent counts before pair missingness, and source/hash/suffix audit.
2. Every cross-fitted nuisance fit converges and gives finite predictions and scores; no fold has zero members of either pair group. If a required fold cannot support both groups, the pair is `UNSUPPORTED` rather than silently pooled or re-folded after seeing outcomes.
3. Held-out propensity support: the 1st and 99th percentiles of `e_q(C)` lie in `[0.05,0.95]`, no held-out probability lies outside `[0.01,0.99]`, the maximum AIPW weight is at most 100, and effective sample size for each treatment arm using inverse-propensity weights is at least 40. These are prospective interpretability gates, not post hoc trimming rules; failure means no pairwise inferential claim.
4. Calibration: held-out propensity log loss must be finite and its calibration slope must lie in `[0.25,4]`; report calibration intercept, slope, Brier score, and comparison with the intercept-only model. Outcome nuisance fits must have finite held-out residuals and no fold with singular design. Poor discrimination of group membership is not itself evidence against a mean difference, but catastrophic calibration invalidates the operational score gate.
5. Covariance is finite positive definite; condition number is below `10^10`; multiplier Monte Carlo has at least 20,000 draws and Monte Carlo standard error for the reported p is recorded. No gate is reopened because a numerical result is favorable.

If a gate fails, report `PAIRWISE_UNSUPPORTED_OR_UNCERTIFIED` with the exact failed condition and retain estimates only as exploratory diagnostics. Do not apply clipping, change the pair population, drop a comparator, or substitute RDW after inspecting results.

## Falsification and robustness checks

All are fixed before looking at inferential results and cannot open the primary gate.

- Recompute the same score with the adverse orientation reversed; estimates must reverse sign and the joint two-sided decision/p-values must be invariant within numerical tolerance. Failure indicates an implementation error.
- Run a label-permutation diagnostic within pair strata of sex, lag status, and coarse age-at-L deciles, refitting both nuisances and preserving the exact split/penalty schedule. This tests the complete pipeline under a stronger random-label null; it is a calibration diagnostic, not evidence that disease subtype labels are exchangeable.
- Run a pre-specified outcome permutation within each pair while retaining covariates and labels, refitting the complete pipeline. The observed statistic should be nonextreme under this construction; otherwise investigate leakage or score coding.
- Compare AIPW estimates with a fold-local outcome-standardization-only estimator and a pairwise unadjusted standardized mean difference. Large unexplained disagreement is a diagnostic and blocks localization until resolved; agreement is not proof of causality.
- Repeat with RDW `30070-0.0/1.0` replacing platelet only as an explicitly secondary exploratory outcome, never as the primary platelet result. Repeat with lag omitted only as a sensitivity analysis, clearly post-landmark and not a prospective-risk analysis.
- Verify exact sign, date/code suffix pairing, post-death exclusion, duplicate-key checks, and no use of parent raw local score columns. Verify that both pair estimates are calculated on their own pair-restricted rows and that the non-GI third group cannot enter the CRC-versus-other-GI score.

## Evidence-bounded result meanings

Supportive evidence requires the global six-component gate, the relevant pair gate, all support/calibration/numerical gates, coherent sign and magnitude, and no failed falsification. It would support a covariate-standardized association between the specified CBC trajectories and subtype membership among these selected recorded malignancy cases. It would not support prospective cancer risk, CRC screening, early diagnosis, pathology-confirmed subtype, mechanism, sensitivity/specificity, referral benefit, mortality benefit, cost-effectiveness, or transportability.

Adverse evidence means a sufficiently precise, predeclared clinically meaningful exclusion would be required to claim compatible absence, but no clinical equivalence margin exists and the small CRC/other-GI samples and weak prior CRC calibration make such a claim unavailable. Therefore a non-rejection, even with narrow computational intervals, is `INCONCLUSIVE_NONREJECTION`, not equivalence or biological identity. A failed support/calibration gate is `UNCERTIFIED`, not adverse evidence.

Inconclusive evidence includes a closed global gate, a closed pair gate, broad intervals, discordant sensitivity/falsification diagnostics, or inadequate support. The prior result is specifically summarized as `GLOBAL_NONREJECTION_WITH_UNCERTIFIED_RAW_PAIRWISE_LABELS`; it is not combined numerically with this new experiment.

## What remains unavailable
