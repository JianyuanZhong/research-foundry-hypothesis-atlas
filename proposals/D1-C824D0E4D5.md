# Independent laboratory audit of literal RT Vent Off versus Suspended: ABG monitoring changes, controls, and physiologic uncertainty

## Substantive child, clinical importance, and exact unresolved claim

This is a substantive child of assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]` and assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves their literal RT event extraction, exact-record deduplication, deterministic earliest-anchored 30-minute clustering, fixed windows, competing observation states, setting/recurrence findings, hospital concentration diagnostics, common-support rule, and safeguards against treating repeated events as exchangeable. The substantive advance is an independent-domain convergent-validity audit in the eICU `lab` table: same-time arterial-blood-gas-like panels, paired PaCO2/pH/PaO2 values, a general-laboratory negative outcome, a negative-time sham, a literal Start direction control, first-episode restriction, and stay-resampled uncertainty.

This measurement question is clinically consequential because the inherited evidence strongly distinguishes the two structured chart values but comes largely from respiratory-chart workflow. Literal Off was followed by much less late setting-family charting than Suspended, yet recurrence was site-sensitive, repeated episodes were nonexchangeable, and no independent airway/device standard was available. Before either value is used in respiratory cohort construction, an independently charted domain should show a temporally specific difference rather than merely echoing respiratory documentation.

The strongest claim already supported is that `Off` and `Suspended` are noninterchangeable structured respiratory-charting regimes within a small, concentrated set of hospitals. The unresolved hypothesis tested here was narrower: around the literal event time, Off would show a larger reduction than Suspended in the probability of independently charted plausible pH-plus-PaCO2 panels from the fixed pre-six-hour to post-six-hour window; that contrast should be larger than its negative-time sham, persist in first episodes, and not simply duplicate a general-laboratory change. Paired values were expected to provide only exploratory physiologic convergence. None of these hypotheses asserts extubation, ventilator liberation, successful liberation, reintubation, or a bedside ventilation state.

## Preserved population and temporal boundaries

Exact decisive tuples `(patientunitstayid, respchartoffset, respchartid, normalized literal value)` are deduplicated, sorted by stay and time, and clustered from the earliest remaining record through anchor +30 minutes. A cluster containing Start plus an Off-direction value is a mixed-direction conflict. A cluster containing both Off and Suspended is semantically ambiguous. Neither enters the Off-versus-Suspended comparison.

The inherited population requires a patient row, nonmissing ICU discharge boundary, `T >= 360`, and `T <= unitdischargeoffset`. It contains 1,063 episodes in 749 stays at 12 hospitals: 713 Off and 350 Suspended. The fresh reconstruction retained the known 4,120 exact-unique decisive records and 4,094 clusters: 2,711 Start, 1,007 Off, 366 Suspended, 9 conflicts, and 1 Off-plus-Suspended ambiguity. Later Starts remain eligible because excluding them would condition on an inherited outcome.

Primary windows are fixed at `[T-360,T)` and `[T,T+360)`. The primary complete-observation cohort requires `unitdischargeoffset >= T+360`, yielding 991 episodes: 647 Off and 344 Suspended. Earlier death/live discharge/truncation states remain explicit in the inherited parent and are not silently reclassified; this six-hour audit simply excludes incomplete post-windows. First-episode sensitivity uses `episode_ordinal = 1`, retaining 579 Off and 107 Suspended complete episodes.

## Computable variables, outcomes, estimands, and controls

At one exact `(patientunitstayid, labresultoffset)`, a plausible ABG panel requires numeric pH in `[6.5,8.0]` and paCO2 in `[10,150]`. Exact measurement tuples `(stay, offset, labname, numeric result)` are deduplicated; if multiple nonidentical values remain for one name and offset, their median resolves the same-time value. This construction prevents separately timed pH and PaCO2 values from being combined. The audit found 9,120 plausible same-time panels after removing 10 exact target-lab duplicates.

The primary binary outcome is at least one plausible panel in each fixed window. Within each literal label, change is `P(post panel)-P(pre panel)`. The primary descriptive estimand is the Off-minus-Suspended difference between these changes. Stay-level 3,000-replicate bootstrap intervals retain all repeated episodes whenever a stay is resampled. This is a difference-in-differences-style description, not a causal effect: labels, case mix, illness trajectory, ordering practice, and hospital workflow are unrandomized.

Controls and sensitivity analyses are prespecified as follows:

- General-laboratory negative outcome: at least one numeric creatinine or WBC x 1000 result in each window, analyzed identically. This tests whether any contrast is wholly explained by nonspecific lab workflow; it is an imperfect control because laboratory types follow different schedules and indications.
- Fixed negative-time sham: pseudo-index at `T-1440`, require actual `T >= 1800`, complete pseudo pre/post windows, pseudo-index +360 within ICU stay, and no clean or mixed decisive RT cluster within +/-360 minutes. This yielded 613 pseudo-events (375 future-Off, 238 future-Suspended).
- Start direction control: clean literal Start clusters with complete six-hour pre/post observation, yielding 626 events. It asks whether monitoring moves in the opposite chart direction, without assigning bedside meaning.
- First episode: restrict the primary complete cohort to `episode_ordinal=1` to reduce repeated-event bias.
- Site support: common-support hospitals require at least five complete six-hour events under each label. Site-specific contrasts are shown rather than treating dominant hospital representation as transportability.
- Paired values: among episodes with panels in both windows, select the last pre panel and first post panel and report within-event PaCO2, pH, and PaO2 changes. Off-minus-Suspended median-change intervals use stay-resampled 3,000-replicate bootstrap. These are selected, sparse physiologic descriptions, not proof of a support transition.

## Executed results

### Primary monitoring result

For Off, ABG panel presence fell from 422/647 = 65.2% pre-event (Wilson 95% 61.5%-68.8%) to 228/647 = 35.2% post-event (31.7%-39.0%), a -30.0-point change. For Suspended, it changed from 41/344 = 11.9% (8.9%-15.8%) to 48/344 = 14.0% (10.7%-18.0%), a +2.0-point change. The Off-minus-Suspended change contrast was -32.0 points, with stay-bootstrap 95% interval -37.8 to -26.5 points.

The contrast persisted in first episodes: Off changed -32.1 points (68.4% to 36.3%) and Suspended +0.9 points (21.5% to 22.4%), giving -33.1 points. Across the five common-support hospitals (63, 66, 68, 71, and 73), every site-specific contrast was negative, ranging from -6.7 to -75.0 points. This supplies directional within-overlap replication, but not a common magnitude or broad hospital transportability; the inherited HHI analysis remains adverse, and hospital 73 supplied most events.

### Negative controls and temporal specificity

General-laboratory presence decreased after both labels: Off from 41.3% to 18.5% (-22.7 points) and Suspended from 43.0% to 10.5% (-32.6 points). Its Off-minus-Suspended change contrast was +9.8 points (stay-bootstrap 95% +1.6 to +18.2), opposite in sign to the ABG contrast. Four of five common-support sites were positive and one slightly negative. Thus nonspecific laboratory workflow changed substantially, but it does not reproduce the label-specific ABG direction.

At the T-24-hour sham, ABG presence changed -9.1 points in the future-Off group and -4.2 points in the future-Suspended group. The sham Off-minus-Suspended contrast was -4.9 points, with stay-bootstrap 95% -11.9 to +2.2 points. The actual-event -32.0-point contrast was much larger, supporting temporal specificity within these selected future-index cohorts. Sham construction depends on future event eligibility and therefore is a falsification control, not a causal counterfactual.

For the Start direction control, ABG panel presence rose from 30.0% to 34.5% (+4.5 points), while general-laboratory presence rose from 14.2% to 24.3% (+10.1 points). This is directionally compatible with increased monitoring around literal Start and contrasts with the Off-associated ABG decline, but it remains structured ordering behavior rather than proof of ventilation initiation.

### Paired physiologic values

Paired panels were available for 200/647 Off events but only 20/344 Suspended events. Off median PaCO2 change was 0 mmHg (IQR -2 to +3), median pH change -0.010 (IQR -0.030 to +0.010), and median PaO2 change -9 mmHg (IQR -28.5 to +9). Suspended medians were +2.5 mmHg PaCO2 (IQR -1 to +6), -0.035 pH (IQR -0.0625 to +0.0125), and +4.5 mmHg PaO2 (IQR -8.25 to +17.75).

Off-minus-Suspended median-change estimates were -2.5 mmHg for PaCO2 (stay-bootstrap 95% -5 to +1), +0.025 for pH (-0.020 to +0.055), and -13.5 mmHg for PaO2 (-26 to -3.5). PaCO2 and pH intervals include zero. The PaO2 contrast does not establish deterioration, extubation, or oxygen-support change: only 20 Suspended pairs contribute, FiO2 completeness and oxygen device at the paired measurements are not required, ABG ordering is selected, and PaO2 depends strongly on oxygen delivery, timing, illness, and treatment. This signal requires clinical adjudication rather than mechanistic interpretation.

## Prespecified interpretation gates and result

A supportive independent-documentation result required an actual-event ABG change contrast whose stay-bootstrap interval excluded zero, the same direction in at least four of five common-support hospitals, persistence in first episodes, and an absolute magnitude at least 10 points greater than the fixed sham; the general-lab control must not reproduce a similarly negative contrast. All gates were met: -32.0 points (interval excluding zero), five of five negative sites, -33.1 points in first episodes, versus -4.9 points at sham, while the general-lab contrast was +9.8 points. The strongest warranted interpretation is that literal Off and Suspended are associated with different, temporally localized **independent laboratory-ordering trajectories** as well as different respiratory-chart trajectories.

An adverse specificity result would have occurred if general labs reproduced the negative ABG contrast, the actual contrast resembled sham, first-only analysis reversed it, or common-support sites lacked directional agreement. Those falsifications did not occur. Nevertheless, the substantial decline in general labs after both labels and very different pre-event ABG prevalence (65.2% versus 11.9%) show strong baseline selection and workflow differences; the result is not a causal estimate and does not isolate physiology.

A physiologic convergence claim required adequate paired coverage under both labels, concordant PaCO2 and pH differences with uncertainty excluding zero, and interpretable oxygen exposure for PaO2. Those conditions failed. Only 20 Suspended events were paired, PaCO2 and pH contrasts were uncertain, and PaO2 lacks required FiO2/device context. Physiologic convergent validity is therefore **inconclusive**, despite the strong monitoring-frequency result.

## Strongest supported claim, unresolved claim, and clinical advance

The substantive advance over the parents is an independent-table result: their Off/Suspended distinction is not confined to respiratory-chart setting activity or recurrence. Around literal Off, plausible same-time pH-plus-PaCO2 panel charting drops sharply; around Suspended it does not. The difference is temporally localized relative to a fixed negative-time sham, persists in first episodes, has shared direction at all five common-support hospitals, and is not mirrored by the general-lab negative outcome. This makes a purely respiratory-table artifact less plausible and strengthens the conclusion that the literal labels mark different documentation/monitoring contexts.

The unresolved clinical claim remains whether either literal value identifies invasive ventilation status, airway removal, actual ventilator discontinuation, extubation, durable or successful liberation, subsequent reintubation, clinical benefit, or safety. Laboratory sampling frequency cannot prove any of these. PaCO2, pH, PaO2, and FiO2 can reflect severity, case mix, oxygen delivery, noninvasive support, ordering practice, treatment, and workflow. Exact additional evidence required is independently timestamped ventilator connection and mode, invasive versus noninvasive support, tube insertion/removal, oxygen device and FiO2 at each sample, extubation/reintubation times, RT/nursing narrative, clinician intent, and blinded adjudication stratified by hospital, literal label, and episode order. A separate adjudication study would estimate sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, inter-rater agreement, and site transportability.

Automatically checkable claims are source/artifact hashes, full-source rows scanned, exact lab-name counts, event normalization, exact deduplication, anchored 30-minute clusters, conflict exclusions, inherited identity reproduction, patient joins, fixed windows, complete-observation filtering, same-offset panel construction, plausibility ranges, exact lab deduplication, binary outcomes, sham exclusion, first-episode filtering, site counts, and bootstrap calculations. An automatic verifier cannot establish bedside ventilation state, extubation, invasive modality, liberation, treatment intent, causal effect, appropriateness, safety, benefit, or physiologic mechanism.
