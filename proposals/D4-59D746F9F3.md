# Certified, compilation-ready baseline renal-marker discordance experiment

## Lineage, unresolved question, and substantive advance

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It does not broaden the population, endpoint, or evidence claims. It preserves the narrow baseline-only, observed-exported-repeat estimand:

> Among adults with a certified baseline creatinine/cystatin-C panel, baseline equation-derived creatinine eGFR at least 60, and a qualifying observed exported repeat assessment 2–8 years after baseline, does baseline creatinine–cystatin-C discordance improve out-of-fold prioritization of a low creatinine-equation eGFR state at that repeat beyond sex, age, and baseline creatinine eGFR, after explicit attendance, timing, and repeat-measurement artifact checks?

The clinical decision represented is prioritizing a repeat kidney-marker assessment. A positive result supports only a reproducible signal for the observed exported-repeat state among qualifying repeat attenders. It does not support CKD, chronicity, measured GFR, fixed-horizon risk, ordering or treatment benefit, causal utility, assay accuracy, or transportability. Field meaning, units, missingness, assay/matrix comparability, and the distinction between an exported instance-1 record and a person's first clinical repeat remain evidence gates, not biological conclusions.

The parent was substantively strong but bound its schemas to another attempt workspace and left the equation/certificate contract partly implicit. This version repairs those compilation blockers by (1) binding every source and schema to the exact current workspace and recording both file and internal schema hashes, (2) defining a machine-readable certificate and canonical digest procedure with an explicit all-or-nothing permission state, and (3) freezing executable 2021 CKD-EPI equations, units, test vectors, restricted-cubic basis dimensions, and numerical tolerances. No population, temporal boundary, estimand, outcome, or claim is broadened.

## Certificate contract and fail-closed permission machine

The compiler requires a separate immutable input `[internal dataset path]` (not present in the current workspace, so the default result is a neutral certification stop). The certificate is canonicalized as UTF-8 JSON with recursively sorted object keys, array order preserved, no insignificant whitespace, and no `certificate_sha256` member. Its SHA-256 is recomputed over those canonical bytes and compared to the supplied digest in a sidecar manifest or invocation argument; a self-declared top-level status is ignored and rejected if present. The certificate itself must be immutable after hashing.

Required top-level keys are exactly:

```text
certificate_schema_version, certificate_id, catalog_sha256, snapshot_id,
created_at, required_fields, field_evidence, cross_instance_evidence,
deficiencies, certificate_sha256
```

`required_fields` must be exactly these nine field-instance identifiers, with no duplicates:

```text
31-0.0, 21003-0.0, 21003-1.0, 53-0.0, 53-1.0,
30700-0.0, 30700-1.0, 30720-0.0, 30720-1.0
```

For every required field, `field_evidence[field]` contains exactly one record with `table_id`, `source_id`, `source_sha256`, `schema_path`, `schema_file_sha256`, `schema_sha256`, `semantic_label`, `instance_index`, `array_index`, `data_type`, `valid_range`, `missing_tokens`, `sex_concept`, `age_concept`, `date_concept`, `analyte_concept`, `matrix_concept`, `unit_concept`, `assay_concept`, `method_concept`, and `evidence_items`. `missing_tokens` is an exhaustive list of exact raw tokens; no global negative-code recoding is allowed. Non-applicable concepts must be JSON null plus an explanation in `non_applicable_explanations`.

Each `evidence_items` record has exactly `evidence_class`, `directness`, `source`, `source_version`, `retrieval_date`, `artifact_sha256`, and `claim_text`. Allowed `evidence_class` values are `DIRECT_FROZEN`, `VALIDATED_HARMONIZATION`, `ASSUMED`, `MISSING`, and `CONFLICT`; allowed `directness` values are `DIRECT`, `INFERRED`, and `ASSUMED`. `ASSUMED` evidence can never authorize analysis. A field authorizes scientific execution only if all applicable semantic requirements are supported by non-assumed direct/validated evidence and no missing or conflict remains.

`cross_instance_evidence` must contain separate claims for: (i) instance 1 is an exported repeat record and not evidence of the first clinical test, (ii) baseline/repeat date comparability, (iii) creatinine cross-instance comparability, and (iv) cystatin-C cross-instance comparability. Each claim uses the same evidence classes and directness and must be supported; otherwise certification is incomplete. The certificate must establish analyte identity, specimen matrix, units, valid range, missing codes, and assay/platform or validated measurement method separately for both instances of both analytes. Unsupported unit conversions, duplicate records, unknown keys/classes, conflicting claims, hash mismatches, malformed dates, and any unlisted field are hard failures.

The executor derives exactly one state, in this order:

```text
SOURCE_INTEGRITY_FAIL
  -> STRUCTURAL_READY
  -> FIELD_CERTIFICATE_MISSING | FIELD_CERTIFICATE_INVALID
     | FIELD_CERTIFICATE_INCOMPLETE | FIELD_CERTIFICATE_CONFLICT
```

`SOURCE_INTEGRITY_FAIL` and all certificate states before `CERTIFIED_READY` are `NO_SCIENTIFIC_RESULT`. Before `CERTIFIED_READY`, outputs are limited to schema/source hashes, exact headers, aggregate row/key/join checks, synthetic parser/equation/model/fold/quota/arithmetic/bootstrap/permutation tests, and lineage checks. They must not contain eids, participant distributions, biomarker values, value-attached field names, eGFR, cohort masks, dates or elapsed-time distributions, outcomes/counts, predictions, folds, weights, selected sets, or metrics. ## Exact equations and numerical contract

All analytes must be converted only by a certificate-authorized identity conversion into mg/dL. If units are not certified as mg/dL or an authorized conversion is absent, stop. Let `Scr` and `Scys` be positive serum creatinine and cystatin-C in mg/dL, `Age` be years, and `s=1` for female and `s=0` for male. Use float64 and natural powers.

2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation:

```text
k = 0.7 if s=1 else 0.9
alpha = -0.241 if s=1 else -0.302
sex_multiplier = 1.012 if s=1 else 1
 eGFRcr = 142 * min(Scr/k,1)^alpha * max(Scr/k,1)^(-1.200)
          * 0.9938^Age * sex_multiplier
```

2021 CKD-EPI cystatin-C equation:

```text
eGFRcys = 133 * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.323)
           * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.778)
           * 0.9961^Age * (0.932 if s=1 else 1)
```

2021 CKD-EPI creatinine-cystatin-C equation:

```text
eGFRcr_cys = 135 * min(Scr/k,1)^alpha * max(Scr/k,1)^(-0.544)
              * min(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.323) * max(Scys/0.8,1)^(-0.778)
              * 0.9961^Age * (0.963 if s=1 else 1)
```

The combined equation is descriptive secondary context only and is not substituted for the primary creatinine outcome. The baseline discordance is `Z = log(eGFRcys_0 / eGFRcr_0)`. No rounding is applied before thresholding; report full float64 values privately and aggregate results publicly. Synthetic mandatory equation vectors (absolute tolerance `1e-10`, relative tolerance `1e-10`) are: `(Scr,Age,sex)=(0.9,50,male) -> eGFRcr=104.0490129932253`; `(0.7,60,female) -> 98.94831465668567`; `(1.2,70,male) -> 65.0565950204461`; `(Scys,Age,sex)=(0.8,50,male) -> eGFRcys=116.21314799600616`; `(0.7,60,female) -> 111.2250331094537`; `(1.0,70,male) -> 79.75272605816541`. Combined vectors `(0.9,0.8,50,male) -> 111.04033757644632`, `(0.7,0.7,60,female) -> 107.36626871737116`, and `(1.2,1.0,70,male) -> 73.82048812375221` are secondary implementation checks.

## Decision gates and interpretation

Support requires `CERTIFIED_ANALYSIS_COMPLETE`, exact source/schema/certificate integrity, exact dimensions and lineage, calibration without material M1 harm, no null/sentinel replication, repeat stability, and both inherited practical floors: DeltaNB(2%) point at least `.001` with lower 98% bound above zero, and delta event yield at 10% point at least `5` with lower 98% bound above zero, with at least 8/10 favorable repeats on each axis. The artifact layer must also show directionally consistent positive incremental contrasts for `Ycr` and at least one nonidentical secondary (`Ycys` or `Yboth`) and no material attendance-weighting reversal. This can support only the narrow observed-repeat prioritization signal; it cannot promote a marker-general or assay-robust claim.

Adverse requires certified reproducible negative incremental evidence with upper interval below zero and at least 8/10 adverse repeats, material M1 calibration harm, or a prespecified reproducible null/control replication explaining the apparent gain. Practical-floor failure with intervals crossing zero is scientifically inconclusive. Poor positivity, incomplete controls, nonfinite computation, unresolved certification, technical failure, or anchor mismatch is operational/technical inconclusive. A null secondary with positive Ycr is not automatically adverse to the narrow Ycr estimand but blocks any marker-general/assay-robust interpretation. Certification failure is `NO_SCIENTIFIC_RESULT`, never adverse biology.

The verifier can check literal paths and hashes, headers, certificate state, equation vectors, units declared by certificate, dimensions, baseline-only lineage, deterministic folds/ties/quotas, arithmetic, null completion, and claim-to-gate consistency. It cannot adjudicate whether a certificate's external assay evidence is clinically correct, establish measured-GFR validity, diagnose CKD, infer chronicity, recover non-attender outcomes, estimate benefit/harm or utility, validate patient thresholds, or establish transportability. Those require expert review, technical replicates/external laboratory linkage, adjudicated longitudinal data, or another prospective study.
