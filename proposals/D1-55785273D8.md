# HCC observation-aware response trajectory and recurrence ascertainment

## Status and substantive successor

This is a substantive child of [prior hypothesis]. The parent correctly treats surveillance and observation bias as a rival to response-driven prognostic information, but its primary model still conditions on a composite of measured surveillance and response while leaving the observation process itself as a descriptive audit. The successor makes the observation process an explicit two-part estimand: first, whether an explicit response is observed and what its measured ascertainment pattern is; second, whether response adds held-out recurrence-risk information conditional on that measured opportunity. It does not claim to identify unobserved surveillance, true residual viable tumor, or a causal treatment effect.

The clinically consequential question is whether an early response trajectory can be used to prioritize follow-up or treatment reassessment in routine-care HCC, rather than merely reflecting who is observed more often. A positive result would justify prospective label validation and a surveillance-stratified clinical study; a null or observation-dependent result would discourage treating EHR response text as a biological endpoint.

## Evidence-supported versus unresolved claims

The inspected works support that baseline tumor burden, AFP, liver reserve, treatment heterogeneity, and imaging features are prognostic or informative in retrospective HCC cohorts [K1]–[K3]. [K1] specifically reports a multicenter prognostic model using tumor burden, ALBI grade, AFP and PNI; [K2] reports complementary CEUS/MRI features but expert integrated interpretation rather than a prespecified algorithm; [K3] explicitly treats detection and immortal-time bias as threats in hospital follow-up. They do not establish incremental value of an EHR-derived response trajectory in this local cohort, nor do they validate the local response/recurrence labels. The unresolved claim is narrower: after prespecified measurement of response-window observation opportunity, does explicit response trajectory improve held-out prediction of subsequently documented recurrence/progression?

This is a prognostic estimand, not a causal or mechanistic estimand. Adjustment for measured observation can reduce but cannot eliminate differential ascertainment, and a stable association cannot identify residual viable tumor or treatment resistance.

## Cohort and temporal boundaries

T0 is the earliest qualifying first locoregional procedure in procedures (手术, 开始时间), prespecified as TACE/embolization, thermal ablation, hepatic resection, or other locoregional treatment; ambiguous terms remain other. Require HCC diagnosis in diagnoses (诊断名称, 诊断类型) or an adjudicated imaging/diagnosis report. Exclude prior qualifying treatment in the available record. Baseline is 90 days before through T0 and includes age, sex, treatment category, pre-T0 tumor-burden proxy, cirrhosis/liver-disease proxy, AFP, liver-function labs, and prior treatment count. No post-T0 field enters baseline.

The response window is T0+30 through T0+90 days. A liver imaging report in examinations (检查, 检查所见, 检查诊断, 开始时间) must explicitly support complete response, partial response, or indeterminate; missing is not complete response. Follow-up starts at the last response-window imaging date (or T0+90 if none), ends at first explicit post-response recurrence/progression, loss to follow-up, documented death, or administrative end 2026-01-01. Recurrence requires dated post-response imaging or a diagnosis linked to a valid post-response encounter; history, postoperative status, and orders alone are not outcomes. Primary analysis requires 180 days observable follow-up; report exclusions and use all-follow-up inverse-probability-of-censoring as secondary.

## Observation-process estimand

Derive, without using post-response outcome text, the following measured variables for every patient:

1. Response ascertainment: whether at least one liver imaging report in T0+30–T0+90 explicitly supports complete, partial, or indeterminate response; report date, modality, number of reports, and whether the report is complete/partial/indeterminate.
2. Observation opportunity: all dated encounters and imaging reports in T0–T0+365, number and timing of liver imaging reports in T0–T0+90 and T0+90–T0+365, and interval from T0 to first post-response imaging.
3. Surveillance orders: dated orders in orders (医嘱(非药品), 开立时间, 开始时间, 结束时间, 医嘱状态, 频次) that request liver/upper-abdominal imaging or related surveillance, used only as an observation-process descriptor and never as an outcome.
4. Treatment-selection proxies: treatment category/intensity at T0, subsequent qualifying procedures, and temporally valid orders/medication exposure when available. These are prognostic/selection descriptors, not causal treatment effects.

The primary estimand is the held-out change in 1- and 2-year documented recurrence/progression risk and integrated Brier score when adding explicit response trajectory to a model containing baseline variables plus the measured observation-opportunity variables. The key contrast is response trajectory among patients with adequate response-window surveillance versus patients without it. A secondary analysis restricts to adequate surveillance and reports the unadjusted versus observation-adjusted association.

The observation audit must report, by response category and surveillance stratum: number with explicit response, number with post-response imaging, number with any encounter, follow-up duration, and number with documented recurrence. It must also compare recurrence ascertainment across response categories after accounting for measured observation opportunity. This can distinguish a signal concentrated in intensively observed patients from one that persists across measured strata, but it cannot identify unmeasured outside-hospital surveillance or label validity.

## Baseline and substantive alternative

Baseline: penalized Cox or pooled logistic model using only pre-T0 variables (age, sex, treatment, tumor-burden proxy, liver disease, AFP, liver function, time since diagnosis), with missingness indicators. Fit on earlier index years, tune on a middle validation year, and evaluate on held-out later years; never split by row. Report integrated Brier score, time-dependent AUC, calibration intercept/slope, risk differences, and bootstrap intervals.

Alternative: patient-level gradient-boosted survival model adding response category and longitudinal summaries (AFP slope/level, liver-function change, imaging count/timing, residual-disease indicators) from T0 through the response window, plus the measured observation-process variables. The alternative is scientifically useful because it tests whether temporal response information adds what baseline and measured observation intensity lose; it is not selected for complexity. A latent residual-versus-de-novo mechanistic model is deferred because local data cannot distinguish etiologies without validated imaging adjudication, pathology/genomics/ctDNA. CPU is appropriate; GPU is not needed. Planned solver envelope: 16 CPUs, 262144 MiB, 2 GPUs, 28800 seconds; no model has been fitted.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive: response remains associated after observation adjustment and the response-augmented model improves held-out calibration/Brier score with stable direction across treatment strata, calendar periods, and adequate-surveillance strata; recurrence ascertainment is not materially different across response groups after accounting for measured observation opportunity.

Adverse: association disappears after observation adjustment, is confined to one modality or one observation stratum, reverses across periods, or the alternative is worse than baseline; this favors burden, selection, or ascertainment explanations.

Inconclusive: sparse adjudicated response/recurrence, wide uncertainty, poor calibration, or excessive missingness; this does not refute biology. A null in poorly observed patients is not evidence that response has no biological meaning.

Computation can check joins, dates, leakage, surveillance definitions, model fitting, and conclusion/output consistency. It cannot establish mechanism, treatment benefit, residual viable tumor, or an action threshold. Expert radiology/clinical adjudication of response, recurrence, tumor burden, and treatment intent is essential; prospective validation and ideally imaging/pathology or molecular residual-disease data are needed for stronger claims.
