Candidate: evolution child of `[prior hypothesis]` (board top, mu 31.9, sigma 6.7, valid, refs complete).
Origin: expert seed `[starting question]` ("Asynchronous organ recovery").
Status: design frozen. Every itemid below was re-read by the Lead this episode from the frozen archive

No clinical truth is claimed; this is a hypothesis plus a discriminating test.

## 1. Unresolved question

After circulatory stabilisation in sepsis/septic shock, some patients recover kidney and lung function
together while others recover one organ bed while the other stays impaired ("cross-organ recovery
asynchrony"). Episodes 3–8 built a ladder M0 snapshot severity < M1 asynchrony < M2 shared latent
recovery factor < M3 observation intensity. The **unresolved defect is circularity**: the parent's M2
fits the "single shared recovery factor" *from the same renal and respiratory limbs* that define M1, so
"asynchrony adds beyond M2" cannot separate genuine organ-specific divergence from one common trajectory
sampled unevenly.

> Revised question: does a prespecified renal-vs-respiratory recovery-asynchrony index at t\* add
> information beyond (M0) same-time snapshot severity and beyond (M2\*) a shared recovery axis identified
> **independently** from the acute inflammatory response (WBC decay / platelet recovery), i.e. a rival
> that is not built from the two organs being compared?

## 2. Observed vs conjectured

- Observed [K1]: acute inflammatory recovery is a largely *universal single trajectory*
  (exponential WBC decay, delayed platelet growth); deviation gives 9x RR of sepsis death.
  Real: a shared recovery axis is measurable and prognostic. Not established: whether cross-organ
  (renal vs respiratory) divergence carries information *beyond this external axis*.
- Observed [K2]: septic ICU patients are heterogeneous and can be partitioned into
  subphenotypes from MIMIC-IV/MIMIC-III. Real: data-driven sepsis subphenotyping is prior art.
  Not established: a prespecified asynchrony index tested against an external shared axis.
- Observed/bounding [K3]: creatinine-based GFR is biased in critical illness with large
  muscle-mass change; the renal limb of "recovery" is partly measurement-driven. Conjectured (to test):
  residual asynchrony is genuine organ divergence, not only creatinine artefact / measurement intensity.
- Observed (archive, this episode): all itemids below exist in `d_labitems`/`d_items` of the frozen zip.
  Conjectured: enough septic stays have both limbs and ≥1 CBC measured around t\* to fit the ladder.

## 3. Why it matters / advance

If asynchrony adds nothing beyond an *independent* shared recovery axis (M2\*) plus observation
intensity (M3), then the intuitive "one organ is lagging" reading of recovery is an artefact and should
not drive prognostication — a useful negative result that the parent's circular M2 could not deliver.
If it survives, a cheap two-organ asynchrony index at a fixed post-stabilisation landmark is a genuine
prognostic contrast. The advance over [K1] and [K2] is the *non-circular, discriminating* ladder.

recovery time, signed; renal recovery = creatinine falling toward baseline and/or urine output rising;
respiratory recovery = falling FiO2 / P/F-ratio improvement without escalation of ventilatory support.

Ladder (fit on one locked temporal split: earliest 70% of stays by `icustays.intime` = development,
latest 30% = holdout):
- M0 = snapshot severity at t\*.
- M1 = M0 + signed asynchrony index.
- M2\* = M0 + independent inflammatory recovery axis.
- M3 = M2\* + organ-specific observation intensity.

Primary contrasts (on holdout): ΔAUROC(M1−M0) and ΔAUROC(M1−M2\*), each with 1000-sample bootstrap 95% CI;
plus a permuted-label negative control (null centred at 0).

## 5. Method comparison (baseline vs substantive alternative)

- Simple baseline: prespecified logistic regression on M0 covariates; asynchrony entered as a single
  signed scalar. Interpretable, low-variance, matches the clinical claim.
- Substantive alternative: a **joint latent-variable / dynamic-factor model** that estimates the shared
  recovery axis from the multivariate lab time series (WBC, platelets, lymphocytes, ± renal/respiratory
  markers) with per-organ measurement-intensity terms, so M2\* is *estimated* rather than hand-derived.
  Rationale: a hand-derived WBC-decay score may be misspecified; a flexible estimated shared axis is a
  harder rival. If the asynchrony signal survives even this, it is not an artefact of axis misspecification.
- Scientific uncertainty tested: whether measured cross-organ divergence is *additional* to a shared
  recovery process — not a search for a small AUROC gain.
- Budget: cohort build + M0/M1/M2\*/M3 logistic fits + bootstrap + one bounded latent-factor fit are
  CPU-feasible (tabular); GPU is not required and is not requested. Solver envelope per `solver_planning_limits`
  (16 CPU/256 GB/28800 s) is ample and unverified until compilation.

## 6. Falsification criteria

- Supportive: on the locked holdout, ΔAUROC(M1−M0) ≥ 0.02 with 95% CI excluding 0 AND
  ΔAUROC(M1−M2\*) ≥ 0.02 with 95% CI excluding 0; permutation null centred at 0.
- Adverse (falsifies "asynchrony is additional information"): ΔAUROC(M1−M2\*) ≤ 0.005 with the CI contained
  within the prespecified equivalence margin (±0.01) → the asynchrony signal is absorbed by the external
  shared axis / measurement intensity.
- Inconclusive: CIs wide or crossing the margin (class prevalence/power unresolved in discovery).

## 7. Alternatives, limits, deliverable

Serious rivals addressed: (a) single shared trajectory + organ-specific measurement intensity (M2\*/M3);
(b) creatinine measurement bias/discordant AKI (K3) via marker-mismatch and same-organ negative controls;
(c) t\*-definition (vasopressor-weaning) timing artefact via sensitivity to the MAP/vasopressor rule.
Explicitly UNAVAILABLE: cystatin C (no marker-independent renal truth standard in this archive) — needs
another dataset; late-window labs forbidden by leakage control.
Limits: single-centre retrospective; measurement selection adjusted/tested, not removed; no causal or
mechanistic claim; prevalence/power not established in discovery.
Deliverable the solver must produce: the cohort with the locked split, the M0/M1/M2\*/M3 fits, the two
primary contrasts with bootstrap CIs, the permutation control, and the M3 + negative-control results —
reporting supportive/adverse/inconclusive per the thresholds above.
