# Fail-closed temporal and clinical anchoring audit for the frozen HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B experiment

## Successor, scope, and substantive repair

This is a targeted successor of `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves the parent's frozen adult HCC repeat-TACE population, literal treatment-record ontology, event reconstruction, exact B/P/event/Y clocks, one-event-per-patient rule, annual forward blocks, locked nested ridge estimand, outcome-blind support and missing-Y identification contract, and strict noncausal evidence boundary. It does not change the primary cohort, event, model, outcome, denominator, training procedure, or interpretation of the inherited complete-triplet analysis.

The remaining clinically consequential uncertainty is more basic than model fit: when a numerical laboratory row is selected as B, P, or Y, does the record provide enough exact temporal and encounter linkage to treat it as a measurement occurring in the intended clinical episode, rather than merely a numerically parseable row? The HCC laboratory table supplies a value, assay label, specimen-type field, laboratory timestamp, and patient/encounter keys, but it does not supply units, reference ranges, analyzer identity, collection-versus-result semantics, specimen quality, or treatment intent. A valid timestamp and a nonempty specimen field therefore do not prove clinical validity. This successor makes that limitation executable and fail-closed.

The new audit is an **anchoring qualification layer**, not an unannounced redefinition of the primary estimand. Every inherited selected B/P/Y row receives a deterministic status: `confirmed_encounter_anchored`, `time_key_only`, `missing_encounter_time`, `outside_encounter_interval`, `ambiguous_encounter_link`, `missing_specimen_metadata`, `nonfinite_or_unparseable`, `exact_assay_mismatch`, or `no_candidate`. The primary result remains the parent's exact numeric selector and may be reported only with its original limitations. A secondary anchoring-restricted replication is estimable only if all required assay-role rows meet the predeclared confirmed status and all anchoring gates pass. If anchoring cannot be established, the secondary result is `inconclusive` or `unsupported`; no row is silently repaired, imputed, relabeled, or discarded to obtain support.

The principal falsifiable question remains:

> Among selected adult HCC repeat-TACE episodes with a finite, exact-assay remote baseline B and contemporaneous pre-repeat P, does P add short-horizon prognostic information for the subsequent same-event assay Y beyond B and the locked pre-event core, and does that result persist when the selected laboratory rows are restricted to episodes with independently auditable temporal and encounter anchoring?

This is a recorded-scale prognostic association. It is not a treatment-effect, treatment-response, toxicity, safety, mortality, hepatic-failure, utility, clinical-benefit, action-rule, transport, or causal claim.

## What is supported versus unresolved

The strongest inherited evidence is the executed outcome-blind full-source reconstruction and observation audit: 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator patients; 159 and 526 selected repeat events; 242 outcome-blind BP-eligible episodes per assay; and 158 complete albumin versus 160 complete total-bilirubin triplets overall. The inherited audit found adequate support for the 2022 future block but sparse support and severe outcome observation loss in later blocks. The inherited formula artifact tested missing-Y arithmetic and edge cases, not HCC outcomes or clinical meaning. Thus the available evidence can support at most a selected, short-horizon recorded prognostic association in the supported calendar mixture; it cannot establish stable calendar transport or clinical utility.

The untested claim is stronger: that the apparent incremental signal is not an artifact of temporal misanchoring, duplicate or conflicting encounter linkage, or unverified clinical context of the laboratory row. The present data can test exact database linkage and timestamp consistency. They cannot validate assay units, reference ranges, specimen adequacy, collection semantics, disease severity, tumor burden, treatment intent/administration, or patient-important outcomes. Any stronger claim requires clinical adjudication, source-system metadata, or an independent prospective/external study.

## Frozen cohort, event, clocks, and estimand

Reconstruct and assert exactly 319 systemic-record patients, 1,491 comparator patients, 159 and 526 selected repeat events, and assay-specific complete triplets of albumin 40/118 and total bilirubin 41/119 by arm. Event selection must not inspect labs, examinations, clinical documents, or future outcomes.

For each selected event and separately for each assay:

* `B` is the latest finite, uncensored exact-assay row in the TACE2 calendar-day window `[TACE2_date - 30 days, TACE2_date - 1 day]`.
* `P` is the latest finite exact-assay row in the selected repeat encounter in `[event_time - 72 hours, event_time)`.
* `Y` is the nearest finite exact-assay row to `event_time + 24 hours` in `(event_time, event_time + 72 hours]`, using the inherited deterministic tie rule and the inherited same-event encounter requirement.

Require `B_time < P_time < event_time < Y_time` where Y exists and require positive P lead. Missing Y is missing, never a negative assay. Use origins `2021-01-01`, `2022-01-01`, `2023-01-01`, and `2024-01-01`; training events satisfy `event_date < origin`, and each future block is `[origin, origin + 365 days)`.

Fit only in pre-origin training using the unchanged locked models:

```
M0: Y ~ B + systemic-record indicator + age + sex indicators
       + TACE2-to-repeat days + P-lead hours
M1: M0 + P
```

Use training-only age median imputation plus an age-missingness indicator, sex coding, variance filtering, centering/scaling, inner leave-one-patient-out alpha selection over `[0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100]`, and ridge fitting. The primary estimand is unchanged observed complete-triplet standardized held-out RMSE gain:

```
G = (RMSE_0 - RMSE_1) / SD_test(Y)
```

Predictions are from fixed pre-origin fits. Anchoring labels, anchoring-restricted sensitivity analysis, missing-Y bounds, and outcome-blind diagnostics cannot alter the primary fitted rows, endpoint, model, bootstrap denominator, or fold assignments.

## New executable anchoring contract

### 1. Normalize and preserve source identity

For every laboratory row retain source file, source row number, exact original assay string, trimmed assay string, exact patient and encounter keys, raw `定量结果`, parsed finite value, raw `标本类型`, parsed laboratory timestamp, and the role/candidate window (`B`, `P`, or `Y`). Trimming surrounding whitespace is the only assay normalization. Do not map synonyms, Chinese variants, abbreviations, or fuzzy matches to either target assay. A row whose trimmed assay is not exactly `白蛋白` or `总胆红素` is not a candidate.

Parse timestamps with one declared parser and timezone convention. Empty, malformed, non-finite, or ambiguous timestamps are invalid. Do not infer laboratory time from encounter time, admission time, discharge time, procedure time, document text, or file order. Equal timestamps are not ordered by source row number for clinical purposes.

### 2. Safe composite encounter linkage

Create a canonical encounter ledger from `encounters` keyed by (`患者主索引`, `就诊号`). For each key, retain the set of distinct tuples (`就诊时间`, `入院时间`, `出院时间`, `就诊科室`) after exact duplicate collapse and report duplicate/conflict counts. A laboratory row has an `ambiguous_encounter_link` status if its key maps to more than one distinct encounter tuple that could change the temporal classification. A missing key or key with no encounters is `unanchored_key_mismatch` and is not an anchored observation.

For a unique linked tuple, define `encounter_time_valid` only when `就诊时间` parses finitely. Define `interval_valid` only when both `入院时间` and `出院时间` parse, `出院时间 >= 入院时间`, and neither is an impossible timestamp under the declared parser. If one or both interval endpoints are absent or invalid, do not substitute `就诊时间` or a fixed duration; mark the interval as unavailable.

### 3. Role-specific temporal checks

The inherited selector remains the source of the role and the event clocks. The anchoring audit adds the following checks without changing that selector.

* For B, verify the lab timestamp lies in the inherited `[TACE2_date - 30 days, TACE2_date - 1 day]` window. If the linked encounter has a valid interval, require `入院时间 <= B_time <= 出院时间` for confirmed clinical anchoring. Also report whether B's encounter key equals the TACE2 procedure encounter key, but do not require that equality because the frozen B window is intentionally remote.
* For P, verify the lab timestamp lies in `[event_time - 72 hours, event_time)`, has positive P lead, and its composite key equals the selected repeat-event encounter key as required by the frozen design. If the linked encounter has a valid interval, require `入院時間 <= P_time <= 出院時間` for confirmation.
* For Y, verify the lab timestamp lies in `(event_time, event_time + 72 hours]`, is selected by the inherited nearest-to-`event_time + 24 hours` rule, and has the inherited same-event encounter key. If the linked encounter has a valid interval, require `入院时间 <= Y_time <= 出院时间` for confirmation.

A candidate outside its inherited role window is `outside_frozen_clock`, even if its encounter interval appears plausible. A candidate inside the role window but outside a valid linked encounter interval is `outside_encounter_interval`. A candidate with no valid encounter interval is not promoted to confirmed status merely because its laboratory timestamp is valid.

### 4. Deterministic status precedence

Apply this precedence, retaining all failure flags rather than hiding secondary failures:

1. `exact_assay_mismatch`;
2. `nonfinite_or_unparseable` (numeric value or timestamp);
3. `unanchored_key_mismatch`;
4. `ambiguous_encounter_link`;
5. `outside_frozen_clock`;
6. `outside_encounter_interval`;
7. `missing_specimen_metadata` when `标本类型` is empty after trimming;
8. `confirmed_encounter_anchored` when exact assay, finite value/time, unique composite link, valid role clock, valid encounter interval, and in-interval laboratory time all hold;
9. `time_key_only` when exact assay, finite value/time, unique composite link, and role clock hold but encounter interval is unavailable;
10. `missing_encounter_time` when the key is unique but `就诊时间` is unavailable, even if the inherited role clock holds.

`time_key_only` and `missing_encounter_time` are informative failure states, not affirmative clinical anchoring. A nonempty `标本类型` changes only the metadata flag; it never rescues a temporal or linkage failure. If same patient/encounter/assay/time candidates have conflicting finite values or conflicting specimen metadata, mark the role `ambiguous_same_time_conflict` and do not choose by file order. Exact duplicate rows with identical assay, value, timestamp, and specimen metadata may be collapsed after counting; they do not create extra observations.

If a row has a valid role clock but no interval endpoints, it may remain in the inherited primary selector, because excluding it would change the frozen cohort. It cannot enter the anchoring-confirmed replication. If any required role in an episode fails confirmation, that episode is not partially counted as confirmed; for assay-specific triplets, the episode-level status is `not_fully_anchored`.

### 5. Clinical-context fields are descriptive only

Report, without interpretation, whether the linked encounter has a nonempty `就诊科室`, valid admission/discharge interval, and whether the laboratory `标本类型` is nonempty. These are recorded-field availability indicators. Do not call a department evidence of HCC care, a specimen string evidence of specimen validity, a procedure key evidence of treatment administration, or a laboratory row evidence that a clinician reviewed or acted on a result. No clinical-document text, examination text, or diagnosis timing may be used to upgrade a laboratory anchor.

### 6. Anchor audit outputs and gates

Emit a machine-readable, aggregate-only audit with one row per assay-role, origin/block, arm, and anchoring status containing counts, denominators, fractions, and suppressed-cell flags. Also emit a role-level episode ledger with pseudonymous internal episode IDs only; do not publish patient identifiers or raw note content. Required checks include:

* every inherited selected B/P/Y row is accounted for exactly once per assay and role;
* status counts sum to the role denominator;
* confirmed episode counts never exceed inherited selected or complete-triplet counts;
* no role with a missing Y is relabeled observed by anchoring;
* no anchor status changes event selection, BP eligibility, Y observation, model rows, or missing-Y denominators;
* all duplicate/conflicting encounter-key counts are reported before joins;
* no document-only record supplies a timestamp;
* all timestamps used in role windows are native `检验时间`, procedure `开始时间`, or encounter/examination fields explicitly listed above.

Use two prespecified gates for the anchoring-restricted replication, applied separately by assay and calendar block:

* **Episode gate:** at least 20 complete, fully anchored triplets overall and at least 10 in each arm in the training set and future block, with all inherited outcome-blind support gates passing.
* **Anchoring-coverage gate:** at least 80% of inherited complete triplets in that block are fully anchored, and no arm has less than 70% fully anchored triplets. These are design thresholds, not estimates of clinical validity.

If either gate fails, retain the complete primary result as originally defined but label the restricted replication `inconclusive` or `unsupported` (with the exact failed gate). Never drop unanchored rows from the primary to make a gate pass. The coverage thresholds do not prove validity; they only prevent a tiny selected anchored subset from being presented as confirmation.

For each assay and block report paired counts for inherited complete triplets, fully anchored triplets, each failure status, and the paired difference in M0/M1 predictions for episodes that meet the restriction. The restricted analysis must use the exact inherited model specification, training-only preprocessing, patient-level folds, and held-out RMSE estimand, with a frozen role restriction declared before looking at Y values. If the restriction changes training and future row membership, refit only within the prespecified restricted design and report it as a secondary sensitivity analysis; it cannot replace the primary result.

## Retained outcome-blind ascertainment and missing-Y contract

Retain the parent's outcome-blind funnel for every assay, origin, block, and arm: `selected event -> has_B -> has_P -> BP_eligible -> Y_observed -> complete triplet`, with at least 20 BP-eligible episodes overall and at least 10 per arm in training and future for the support gate. Define the existing pre-Y categories from `examinations` valid `开始时间` in `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]`, an additional distinct `encounters` key with valid `就诊时间` in that interval, and nonempty `clinical_documents` presence for the selected encounter. These remain recording/opportunity proxies. They cannot upgrade a laboratory anchor and cannot be used in either prognostic model, Y imputation, missing-Y bounds, or weighting.

Retain the parent's assay-separated fixed-prediction missing-Y bounds. Fit M0/M1 on observed finite complete pre-origin triplets, freeze predictions for all future BP-eligible rows, use the all-BP denominator `N` (or arm-specific `N_a`), and set the temporal range only from finite training Y. If the training range is empty/degenerate or observed future Y lies outside it, mark the bound `unsupported` without widening the range. For missing row i, retain the exact endpoints and affine contrast:

```
d_i(y) = (y-p0_i)^2 - (y-p1_i)^2
       = 2*y*(p1_i-p0_i) + p0_i^2-p1_i^2

Delta_lower = [sum_observed d_i + sum_missing min(d_i(L),d_i(U))] / N
Delta_upper = [sum_observed d_i + sum_missing max(d_i(L),d_i(U))] / N
```

Retain model-specific endpoints, conservative non-primary RMSE enclosure, common-displacement piecewise-linear roots, declared-seed patient bootstrap, and the parent's stop rules. Anchoring statuses are not missing-Y imputations, do not alter `N`, and do not convert deterministic identification regions into confidence intervals.

## Falsification and interpretation branches

### Supportive, but bounded

A result is **anchoring-consistent supportive** only if source hashes, headers, duplicate-safe joins, frozen denominators, clocks, and model assertions pass; inherited outcome-blind and complete-triplet gates pass; the primary estimand is finite and prespecified; the anchoring-restricted episode and coverage gates pass for the assay/block; the restricted and primary M1 gains have the same favorable direction with uncertainty compatible with the declared estimand; and the missing-Y Delta interval is strictly above zero throughout the declared training-range scenario. This supports only reproducible recorded-scale short-horizon prognostic association in the selected calendar mixture. It does not prove the analytes are clinically comparable, that values are valid specimens, or that measuring P improves care.

A primary favorable result with a failed anchoring gate is **anchoring-inconclusive**, not supportive confirmation. A favorable value in `time_key_only` rows is not evidence of clinically anchored measurement.

### Adverse

Classify as **adverse** if an adequately supported primary or anchoring-restricted comparison is nonpositive, M1 degradation repeats across prespecified supported blocks, or the missing-Y bound crosses zero. A materially worse restricted result than the primary, with adequate paired support and no computational failure, is an adverse anchoring-sensitivity signal: the original gain may depend on rows whose temporal/encounter context is not confirmed. This challenges incremental prediction or its measurement robustness; it does not prove B alone is sufficient or establish a mechanism.

### Selection-sensitive

Classify as **selection-sensitive** when complete-triplet performance is favorable but the Delta interval crosses zero, anchoring coverage differs materially by arm/era, failure statuses are concentrated in one assay or arm, observation and anchoring status are associated with pre-Y predictors, or primary and restricted gains materially disagree while both are estimable. This is a warning about recorded measurement/observation selection, not proof of biological heterogeneity or clinical harm.

### Unsupported or inconclusive

Use **unsupported** for hash/header failure, invalid parser, unresolved key conflict, nonfinite required predictions, invalid/degenerate training range, future Y outside training range, or an unresolvable clock/duplicate conflict. Use **inconclusive** for failed support or anchoring gates, insufficient patient clusters/zero test SD, low effective sample size, suppressed cells, unavailable encounter intervals, no fully anchored triplets, or results depending on an optional full-source range. Do not replace these statuses with a numerical zero or an affirmative negative finding.

### Explicit negative controls

1. **Pre-event anchoring placebo:** run the same encounter-interval/status machinery on a prespecified pre-event pseudo-window of equal duration where no post-event Y can contribute. A high rate of purported post-event anchoring in an impossible or time-shifted role indicates a selector or timestamp bug. This is a computation/recording control, not a biological null.
2. **Delayed-window timing control:** repeat only the audit (not the primary endpoint) in `(event_time+72 hours,event_time+144 hours]`. It must not be substituted for Y. Similar status patterns can reveal recording intensity but cannot validate the intended Y window.
3. **Encounter-key permutation control:** within calendar block and assay, permute laboratory encounter keys using a declared seed while retaining laboratory timestamps and values. Confirmed anchoring should collapse toward the key-overlap baseline. This diagnoses dependence on composite linkage; it is not an exchangeability or causal test.
4. **Cross-assay discordance control:** never require albumin and total bilirubin to share units, specimen type, encounter, or anchor status. Report their status tables separately. Unexpectedly identical value/selection patterns may expose a coding or join error, but discordance is not evidence of disease biology.
5. **Exact-duplicate control:** rerun after collapsing only exact duplicate laboratory rows. Any change beyond duplicate accounting fails the implementation audit.

All controls are outcome-blind wherever they assess anchoring. They cannot establish that a test was collected rather than resulted, that a specimen was valid, or that a clinician acted on it.
