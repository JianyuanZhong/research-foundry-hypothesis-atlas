TITLE
Strict-screen feasibility and liberation-marker concordance for daily evaluation versus explicit no-trial decisions in eICU: a bounded descriptive study

CHANGE FROM PARENT
The parent’s comparative emulation is not estimable under its prespecified positivity criteria after strict pre-landmark screening. This child therefore makes feasibility and measurement validity the primary, explicitly noncausal estimands. It does not claim that either label is an SBT, true extubation, or an intervention effect. A future comparative study may proceed only after additional adjudicated data or a prospectively validated operationalization.

CLINICAL QUESTION AND EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
Among adult eICU stays with an invasive-ventilation care-plan state at 24, 48, 72, 96, or 120 hours after ICU admission, how often are an explicit daily-extubation-evaluation label and an explicit no-daily-trial label observed after a strict pre-landmark respiratory/physiologic screen, do hospitals contain both labels, and how concordant are postdecision care-plan, RT Vent Off, and ETT-removal markers?

The strongest claim supported before this analysis is that daily readiness assessment/SBT protocols are accepted elements of ventilator liberation; that evidence does not establish the effect of a care-plan label in heterogeneous routine care. The parent’s preliminary unrestricted counts suggested many labels, but did not establish strict-screen availability, within-hospital overlap, or marker validity. The unresolved, falsifiable claim addressed here is that the eICU snapshot contains enough strictly screened, contemporaneously overlapping person-landmarks and sufficiently concordant liberation markers to support the parent’s planned comparative emulation.

DATA AND EXACT COMPUTATION
Snapshot: eICU 2.0 snapshot [source checksum].

Read-only source directory: [internal dataset path] 2.0数据/

Files and required columns used by the bounded scan:
- patient.csv.gz: patientunitstayid, age, hospitalid, unitdischargeoffset.
- carePlanGeneral.csv.gz: patientunitstayid, cplitemoffset, cplgroup, cplitemvalue. Exact Ventilation values were mapped to eval = “Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation”, no_trial = “Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial”, chronic, rapid, spontaneous-adequate, spontaneous-tenuous, and NIV states. Exact (offset,state) duplicates were deduplicated.
- respiratoryCharting.csv.gz: patientunitstayid, respchartoffset, respchartvaluelabel, respchartvalue. FiO2 labels were FiO2, FIO2 (%), O2 Percentage, and Set Fraction of Inspired Oxygen (FIO2); PEEP labels were PEEP and PEEP/CPAP; RR labels were RR (patient), Total RR, Vent Rate, f Total, Resp Rate Total, and VS RESP RATE; RT Vent On/Off was used for the explicit off component.
- vitalPeriodic.csv.gz: patientunitstayid, observationoffset, sao2, respiration, systemicmean.
- vitalAperiodic.csv.gz: patientunitstayid, observationoffset, noninvasivemean.
- treatment.csv.gz: patientunitstayid, treatmentoffset, treatmentstring; strings containing endotracheal tube/ETT plus removal/extubation were retained as a candidate removal component and separately inventoried.

All event offsets are minutes from ICU admission. Landmarks were exactly 1,440, 2,880, 4,320, 5,760, and 7,200 minutes. Eligibility used only events strictly before L. The latest pre-L care-plan state had to be eval or no_trial. The first exact decision in [L,L+360] minutes determined the observed label; conflicting labels at the same earliest offset were excluded. Stays discharged at or before L were excluded. Adults were age >=18; missing age was not imputed into eligibility. FiO2 values 21–100 were divided by 100, values 0.21–1.0 were retained, and other numeric values were rejected.

Strict screen: latest FiO2 in [L-360,L) <=0.50; latest PEEP <=8; latest RR in [L-120,L) between 8 and 35; at least two SpO2 values in vitalPeriodic in [L-120,L) with median 88–100; at least one respiration value and at least one combined invasive/noninvasive MAP value in that window; median MAP >=65. The scan did not implement the parent’s vasopressor-class trajectory criterion, so these results are an upper bound on the cohort obtainable after adding that criterion. No respiratory-care ventendoffset value was interpreted as extubation.

Postdecision marker inventory was limited to the first 72 hours after the decision: carePlanGeneral “Spontaneous - adequate”; respiratoryCharting RT Vent On/Off = “Off”; and candidate ETT-removal treatment strings. These are documentation proxies only. The scan did not upgrade any proxy to true extubation and did not estimate causal effects.

PRESPECIFIED DESCRIPTIVE OUTPUTS
1. Flow/attrition: total patients, stays with selected state, candidate person-landmarks, ambiguous decisions, discharge exclusions, and strict-screen person-landmarks.
2. By-label counts and landmark distribution.
3. Hospital overlap: number of hospitals with both labels, and counts under minimum 1, 5, 10, and 20 person-landmarks per label. Report all hospitals, not only those retained.
4. Missingness and availability for every strict-screen component by label and landmark.
5. For each label, counts of each liberation-marker component and their union; pairwise component agreement among events, not agreement with clinical extubation.
6. A machine-readable feasibility verdict with no numerical effect estimate.

FALSIFICATION / DECISION RULES
- Comparative emulation feasibility is falsified if the strict cohort fails the parent’s minimum overlap requirement of at least 10 hospitals with at least 10 observations per label, or if either label has fewer than 200 unweighted person-landmarks before weighting. It is inconclusive, not supportive, if marker components are too sparse or discordant to validate the intended outcome.
- The simplified descriptive study is considered executable if the flow, overlap, missingness, and marker counts are reproducible from the declared files. Its result is a feasibility/measurement conclusion only.
- Any future causal or comparative conclusion is prohibited when positivity fails, when RT-off/ETT-removal concordance is sparse, or when outcome documentation density differs materially by label. Care-plan-only results must be presented separately from explicit-off/removal results.
- Supportive finding: adequate overlap and substantial cross-component concordance would justify a separately designed comparative study, but would not support benefit or causality.
- Adverse finding: absent overlap, sparse explicit markers, or low concordance means the eICU care-plan labels cannot operationalize comparative liberation effectiveness without additional evidence.
- Inconclusive finding: sufficient labels but missing/discordant markers or unresolved chart-time issues; obtain manual adjudication, richer ventilator streams, or prospective data.

OBSERVED BOUNDED RESULTS
Full gzip streams were scanned with no row sampling. The output contains 200,859 patients, 183,173 stays with any selected care-plan state, 1,961 candidate person-landmarks, 2 same-offset ambiguous decisions, and 424 strict-screen person-landmarks: 235 eval and 189 no_trial across 60 hospitals. Only 4 hospitals had at least 5 observations of each label; only 3 had at least 10 of each; only 1 had at least 20 of each. Thus the parent’s requirement of >=10 hospitals with >=10 per strategy fails decisively, before vasopressor restriction, first-stay restriction, cloning/weighting, or covariate adjustment. The parent’s >=200-per-strategy threshold also fails for no_trial (189).

Within the 424 strict person-landmarks, the 72-hour documentation components were, for eval versus no_trial respectively: care-plan spontaneous-adequate 121/65; RT Vent Off 1/0; candidate ETT-removal 19/2; union 125/66. Pairwise co-occurrence counts were care-plan plus RT-off 1, care-plan plus ETT-removal 16, and RT-off plus ETT-removal 0. These counts demonstrate that the broad care-plan marker is not interchangeable with explicit RT-off or treatment removal. The RT-off component is especially sparse. The bounded output does not establish durable liberation, extubation, reintubation, or patient benefit.

LIMITATIONS AND REQUIRED NEXT EVIDENCE
The bounded scan omitted vasopressor trajectory, EOL censoring, delayed chart-entry sensitivity, full invasive-ventilation algorithm, recurrence confirmation, and confounder adjustment; these omissions can only reduce the final cohort or alter documentation patterns. Treatment strings are candidate markers and require clinical adjudication. Respiratory-care ventendoffset is unusable as a standalone endpoint because zero is overwhelmingly a default/missing value. Airway protection, secretion burden, planned procedures, clinician rationale for no trial, actual SBT performance, true extubation/reintubation, and post-ICU outcomes are unavailable or require manual review. The appropriate advance is to prevent an unsupported causal analysis, quantify exactly why it is not currently identifiable, and direct the next study toward adjudicated, prospectively timed ventilator and airway events.
