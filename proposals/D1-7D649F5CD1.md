## Hypothesis to test

The effects of diuretic treatment differ between patients with stable perfusion and fluid accumulation and those with ongoing hypoperfusion.

## Study population and main data

Patients with fluid accumulation; diuretics, vasopressors, and fluid input/output.

## Suggested approach

Compare initiating versus deferring diuretics at successive decision times through target-trial emulation.

## Scientific significance

Identify diuretic-initiation windows that warrant further trials.

## Main limitations

Confounding by indication is strong; time-varying confounding must be addressed.
