# Episode 85 successor: threshold-safe transport of an optional repeat-lactate risk score

## Question, advance, and falsifiable hypotheses

At the 24-hour reassessment after a recorded early norepinephrine/Levophed entry, can a model using an optional repeat lactate be transported between ICUs with different repeat-testing practices? The selected Episode 84 parent separates fixed-capacity rank transport from mean-risk calibration, but calibration-in-the-large can average away error at the exact probability threshold used to trigger review. The unresolved clinical decision is therefore two-dimensional: may a score reorder a fixed number of review slots, and may its displayed probability be trusted at a pre-specified action threshold?

The strongest available evidence is narrower. Baysan et al. (2022, DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750, PMCID PMC9444407) was inspected in frozen full-text Europe PMC XML (source SHA-256 [source checksum]): in 4,440 MIMIC critically ill adults with sepsis, adding 24-hour lactate to recalibrated APACHE IV changed C-statistic 0.62 to 0.64. This supports modest incremental prognosis in a lactate-measured cohort, not cross-practice rank transport, threshold validity, workflow benefit, or causality. Sisk et al. (2021, DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242, PMCID PMC7810439) was also inspected in frozen full-text XML ([source checksum]); its review found missing indicators or observation summaries in 24/36 prediction studies and supports representing informative observation, not treating EICU counts as verified attention, orders, workload, or clinician concern.

Inherited rank hypothesis H-rank: in both directed transfers low→high and high→low and every capacity c in {0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.125, 0.15, 0.175, 0.20}, adding numeric repeat F to R_process increases source-defined seven-day in-hospital death capture by at least 0.03 deaths per occupied slot. Delta7(d,c) is [D7 events in Q_full minus D7 events in Q_process] divided by K_d(c), and tau7 is the minimum over both directions and capacities. Support requires lower95(tau7) >= 0.03, lower95(tauInf) >= 0, no capacity with upper95(Delta7) < 0, and inherited deletion checks.

New threshold hypothesis H-threshold: using the frozen R_full score without target recalibration, the same 0.10 predicted-D7-risk threshold has no more than 0.05 absolute observed-minus-expected risk error in each target direction x F_available status x threshold-side cell. Let p=expit(eta_full), z=1[p>=0.10], and E(d,s,z)=mean(D7-p | target direction d, F_available=s, z). Define q_thr=max over d in {low-to-high, high-to-low}, s in {0,1}, and z in {0,1} of |E(d,s,z)|. This is threshold-side calibration-in-the-large, not individual calibration, fairness, or clinical utility. The threshold is an analytic action screen, not a stakeholder-validated treatment or review threshold. The R_process threshold table is descriptive and cannot rescue or veto H-threshold.

Require each of the eight R_full threshold cells to contain at least 30 target people in the original data; otherwise H-threshold is inconclusive, not a queue failure. Do not impose event-count selection or alter the cohort based on D7; zero events are retained. Report each cell’s n, D7 events/non-events, flagged fraction, mean observed risk, mean predicted risk, E, and exact threshold ties. Support requires all eight cells n>=30, at least 1,900 finite joint copied-hospital replicates, and upper95(q_thr)<=0.05. It is adverse when lower95(q_thr)>0.05; otherwise inconclusive.

Interpretation is ordered by intended use. H-rank and H-threshold both supported means both computational screens pass in these EICU hospitals. H-rank supported with H-threshold adverse means a rank-only fixed-slot queue may be portable while a displayed 10% probability threshold is not; local labeled recalibration/validation is required before thresholding. H-rank supported with threshold inconclusive permits only the rank conclusion. H-threshold without H-rank cannot establish incremental queue value. Any adverse rank state is reported before a threshold-positive result. All other computable patterns are inconclusive. Adverse evidence falsifies only the specified transport screen, not general lactate prognostic value.
