This repair does not infer that a report was authored, finalized, released, retrieved, read or available to a clinician. Those lifecycle fields are absent and remain an explicit evidence limit.

The new shadow state is evaluated at that frozen q* in 2020 and 2021. Thus the repair cannot use 2021 identity failures to tune q*, and cannot turn a later identity audit into a new model or threshold.

## 2. Unresolved question and falsifiable hypothesis

Unresolved question: is the apparent temporal retention of protected documentary-M2-positive patients a property of the fixed-capacity queue, or can it be produced by patient-key collisions, cross-patient accession collisions, contradictory duplicate report content, or source rows whose stored content cannot be assigned unambiguously to one patient?

This matters because fixed capacity makes one identity/content ambiguity capable of changing a patient’s rank, source certificate, and protected capture. A high aggregate stored-content coverage number does not establish patient-level attribution or stable availability. Resolving this defect determines whether the 2020-to-2021 result is a reproducible property of the frozen retrospective corpus or only a property of its untested identity/content representation.

Primary hypothesis:

> At the inherited q* (<1), the finite HCC queue has zero unique-patient worst-state loss not only under the declared source key and stored-content rule, but also under the predeclared conservative patient identity/content/availability shadow state, in both 2020 and untouched 2021, with all inherited gates passing.

The estimand is a retrospective finite-census property. It is not a claim about biological MVI, report viewing, clinician action, treatment, benefit, safety, fairness, deployment, transportability, or future-patient performance.

Before execution, the strongest supported claim is only that the configured snapshot contains the identifiers, time fields, report-like examination content and pathology text needed to construct this audit. The source has no report version, finalization, release, retrieval or view timestamps and no specimen/accession/time linkage for pathology. Therefore the experiment can test attribution and stored-content consistency, not clinical report availability or biological MVI.

## 6. New repair: exact identity/content/availability shadow state

The shadow state is a deterministic audit layer over the same read-only rows. It adds no clinical feature and no new label. It uses raw strings, normalized copies and cryptographic digests only for state construction; retain both raw and normalized values and all row ordinals.

### 6.1 Patient identity ambiguity

Construct encounter-level identity signatures from nonblank values in:

- 身份证号 (identity-number signature);
- the joint signature (姓名, 手机号, 医保/就诊卡号), requiring all three nonblank.

Normalization is limited to documented Unicode whitespace trimming, full-width/half-width normalization, and exact case-preserving string hashing; no fuzzy matching, transliteration, probabilistic linkage or inferred demographic values. An identity signature is an audit signal, not ground truth.

For each signature, retain the set of raw 患者主索引 values and all encounter row ordinals. A target patient i is identity-ambiguous in state w if:

1. one nonblank identity-number signature maps to more than one 患者主索引;
2. one complete joint signature maps to more than one 患者主索引;
3. i has two different nonblank identity-number signatures or two different complete joint signatures across its encounter rows; or
4. an eligible examination accession key (就诊号, 检查号), considered without the patient field, occurs under more than one 患者主索引.

Conditions 1–4 are conservative ambiguity flags, not proof of an actual merge or misidentification. They are retained as adverse source-state witnesses. The declared patient key remains the primary analysis key; no alias is silently merged and no record is silently reassigned.

### 6.2 Content identity and duplicate conflict

For every examinations row, create a payload digest over the exact encoded field values and boundaries for:
检查, 检查所见, 检查诊断, 开始时间 and 机器型号. The patient, encounter, accession, source ordinal and raw bytes are retained outside this digest. For each raw accession tuple, retain the set of payload digests, blank/nonblank status of the payload fields, modality values, row ordinals and source-file hash.

An eligible accession is content-ambiguous if any of the following holds:

- the same raw accession has more than one payload digest;
- the same raw accession contains both blank and nonblank payload fields across rows;
- the same ((就诊号,检查号)) occurs under multiple patient keys;
- a raw accession has an irreconcilable modality or time disagreement under the inherited parser; or
- its source row cannot be reproduced byte-for-byte from the declared file and ordinal.

Identical repeated payloads are not deleted or treated as independent evidence; their multiplicity is retained and they receive one availability status. A conflicting accession is not repaired by selecting the longer, newer-looking or favorable row.

### 6.3 Conservative patient certificate

For each primary patient i, state w and year y define

[
C^{ICA}_{iy}(w)=1
]

only when every eligible accession in the inherited patient-level window has a unique, non-conflicting patient attribution and stable payload digest under 6.1–6.2, and no identity ambiguity condition applies to i. It is 0 otherwise. The certificate includes the exact condition, raw keys, row ordinals, payload hashes and source-file hash. This is a certificate of internally consistent stored source content, not a certificate that a clinical report existed in a finalized or viewable workflow.

The shadow state has two coupled availability labels:

- **stored-content observed**: the inherited M=E*V(O) state, which is usable for the documentary experiment;
- **lifecycle availability unknown**: always retained as unknown because the HCC source has no authored/finalized/released/retrieved/viewed timestamps.

The second label is a mandatory evidence status, not a reason to relabel stored content as available. Any prose using “report available,” “read,” “viewed,” “released” or equivalent is invalid unless an external source supplies those fields.

[

The maximum is over required coherent coupled states in which i is eligible. There is exactly one contribution per patient, never one per state, row, accession or reader. ## 9. Falsification rules and result precedence

The identity/content hypothesis is falsified by any required 2020 or 2021 patient with (d^{ICA}=1), even when the inherited d=0, because that is precisely a patient-level robustness failure. A single cross-patient identity collision, conflicting payload or unresolved source assignment is not silently resolved in favor of retention.

Return one primary status in this order:

1. **computationally_inconclusive**: source/schema/hash/ordinal replay, declared-join, raw-byte digest, temporal quarantine, forbidden-feature, coupled-state, book/model, exact-score/tie, fixed-K, EMPTY, patientwise-max, output-linkage or q*-freeze check fails;
2. **feasibility_inconclusive**: required N/K/event/book/nonvacuity conditions fail;
3. **selection_adverse_no_subcomplete_threshold**: inherited valid computation finds no q<1 with G20=1, or q*=1;
4. **inherited_gate_adverse**: comparator, random-K, deletion, model replay, source-state or inherited PASS gate fails;
5. **identity_content_temporal_nonreplication_adverse**: inherited 2020 selection is valid but (	heta^{ICA}_{2020}(q^*)>0) or (	heta^{ICA}_{2021}(q^*)>0), including any patient-level certificate failure;
6. **finite_corpus_identity_content_supportive**: q*<1, inherited and repaired losses are zero in 2020 and untouched 2021, all required states/certificates/queues/comparators/deletions pass, and exact witnesses replay;
7. append **lifecycle_availability_evidence_inconclusive** to every status because no source field establishes report finalization, release, retrieval or viewing.

Supportive means only that the specified fixed HCC snapshot has a temporally replicated documentary-M2 queue property under a conservative stored-content attribution audit. Adverse means the property is not robust to the identified patient-level source ambiguity; it does not prove semantic imaging is clinically useless. Inconclusive means computation, feasibility or lifecycle evidence cannot support the requested interpretation.

## 12. Verifier adversaries and claim limits

The verifier must recompute or inject tests for:

- changing only a 2021 row or identity collision cannot change q*, any 2019 hash or frozen 2020 selection;
- duplicated identical accession rows remain one patient contribution and are not used as independent votes;
- conflicting duplicate payloads, blank/nonblank mixtures, cross-patient (就诊号,检查号) collisions and unreproducible rows force C^{ICA}=0 with a witness;
- one patient with high aggregate Q but one ambiguous eligible accession is lost under the repaired estimand;
- two different patient keys sharing an exact identity signature are never silently merged or assigned favorably;
- same patient-key rows with conflicting nonblank identity signatures are flagged, never resolved by frequency;
- an EMPTY queue position cannot be backfilled and K-1 certified positions fail;
- state, reader, pathology and source components cannot be mixed;
- patientwise max, not state/accession/row multiplicity, determines theta and theta^ICA;
- q_2021, pooled years, favorable identity state, favorable reader/pathology book, average state and favorable deletion cannot rescue a failure;
- lifecycle_unknown cannot be printed as report available, released, viewed or read;
- pathology, clinical documents, future rows and reader identity never enter predictors;
- q*=1 cannot produce supportive sub-complete prose;
- a finite-census theta value cannot be described as a future-patient probability or confidence guarantee.

Automatic verification can establish source lineage, exact identity/content rules, state construction, finite-census queue arithmetic, threshold freeze/replay, and whether conclusions follow from computed witnesses. It cannot establish that conservative identity signatures reflect true human identity, that a stored narrative is clinically semantically correct, that pathology is specimen-linked biological MVI, that a report was finalized/released/retrieved/viewed, that clinicians acted, or that the queue is useful, safe, fair, cost-effective, transportable or beneficial. Those claims require expert adjudication, missing source-system lifecycle/specimen evidence, external archive audit, linked outcomes and/or a separate prospective silent-mode or decision-appropriate interventional study.
