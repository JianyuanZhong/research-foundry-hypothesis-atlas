# Provisional hypothesis: discordant recovery after acute kidney injury

Status: provisional and untested. This draft is registered to preserve the scientific direction; no cohort count, effect estimate, or literature conclusion is asserted here.

## Proposed relationship

Among critically ill adults with acute kidney injury (AKI), creatinine-based recovery may be discordant from recovery in urine output or fluid status. The hypothesis is that a patient whose creatinine trajectory appears to improve but whose urine output remains low or whose fluid status does not improve has worse subsequent outcomes than a patient with concordant improvement in both domains.

This is a hypothesis about prognostic discordance and the information lost by creatinine-only recovery assessment. It is not yet a claim that fluid status or urine output causes outcomes, that discordance is a biological mechanism, or that an intervention should be changed.
