# Exact per-world availability–preference frontier for preoperative HCC documentary-M2 review

## Audit finding and substantive repair

The assigned parent is scientifically valid and its HCC frame is retained. I found one consequential inferential defect in the availability/preference bridge: the proposal describes a binary full-roster mask (e) and a preference-safe quantity (p_safe), but does not uniquely define (i) the finite set of admissible mask worlds, (ii) dominance between coverage curves, or (iii) whether “captures over both test years” is a per-year worst case or a pooled numerator. A pooled numerator would have units of captures across two cohorts, while (pK) is a one-year review-capacity quantity. The resulting frontier could therefore be mathematically non-reproducible and clinically misinterpreted.

The successor makes one repair: it defines an exact indexed world and evaluates every preference and scientific predicate within each fixed held-out year, cutoff, uncertainty state, and deletion projection. The two test years are combined only by a minimum across per-year results, never by summing captures. The availability lattice is explicitly a finite sensitivity object over whole source-clean accessions; it is not treated as observed archive availability or as an implementable policy until the external audit succeeds.

This is clinically consequential because a review service could appear worthwhile under pooled two-year counts or under a favorable report mask while failing in a particular year, reader interpretation, or patient-deletion projection. The repaired result answers the decision-relevant question: what patient-coverage profiles and minimum per-slot documentary-M2 yield survive every declared source and reader world at fixed capacity?

## Supported claim, unresolved claim, and hypothesis

The strongest claim supported by the inspected HCC snapshot is narrow: it contains patient/encounter-linked procedures with start/end fields, examination rows with acquisition-like start times and CT/MRI report text, same-encounter pathology narratives, and dated medication/order records. The source catalog and live headers support the stated joins and fields. The snapshot has no report authored/final/release/view timestamps or immutable version chain, no images, no pathology specimen/slide/block/time key, no validated report-access history, no MDT capacity or clinician-response record, and no treatment, recurrence, survival, harm, cost, or patient-benefit outcome. Therefore the study can test only a retrospective documentary endpoint under a locked ranking; it cannot establish biological MVI, causal benefit, clinical workflow utility, or patient benefit.

Current public context reinforces the need for this boundary: a frozen Europe PMC/web-mediated review excerpt reports sparse external validation and inconsistent calibration/clinical-utility evaluation for HCC prediction models, while the attempted vascular-invasion review page was inaccessible. Those excerpts are untrusted context, not a substitute for HCC source evidence or an inspected full paper.

The falsifiable hypothesis is:

> In adults approaching a first eligible liver resection, at each fixed capacity (K), there exists a nonempty finite patient-coverage/availability frontier on which the locked semantic model (G) exceeds independently fitted report-surface (R), acquisition-only (B), and exact capacity-matched random review by the preregistered margin in every held-out year, cutoff-specific uncertainty world, and whole-patient deletion projection; and at least one frontier cell has a strictly positive worst-world documentary-M2 capture rate per review slot that exceeds an externally elicited outcome-blind preference threshold.

Support is only for this documentary ranking-and-allocation estimand. It is not support for changing resection, treatment, surveillance, transplant, or any patient outcome.

## Repaired finite availability lattice

For the union of the primary and locked sensitivity rosters, let U* be the finite set of all source-clean, nonblank, whole CT/MRI accessions attached to any eligible patient and wholly inside at least one declared 90-day window. For cutoff d, A_{i,d} is patient i's accession set in that cutoff's window. The full lattice is the finite set E = {0,1}^{U*}.

For e in E, e_u=1 exposes every byte and field of accession u; e_u=0 replaces the complete report-derived block by the locked unavailable block in R and G, applies the same surface mask to G-mask, and leaves B unchanged. Source-unclean units are forced to zero and cannot be exposed. One e is shared across models, years, reader pairs, all cutoff projections, and deletion projections. Each cutoff uses its own projected A_{i,d} and eligible roster, but the same global mask bits. This is a mathematical sensitivity lattice, not an observation that the HCC source records real archive visibility.

For each patient and cutoff define exact patient coverage
q_{i,d}(e) = (sum of e_u over u in A_{i,d}) / |A_{i,d}| if |A_{i,d}|>0, and 0 otherwise.
The patient-balanced coverage object Q_d(e) is the ordered vector of all q_{i,d}(e), including zero-accession patients. Accessions are not given more weight than a patient. Patient deletion uses frozen full-roster A_{i,d}, so it cannot improve coverage by changing denominators.

For the primary cutoff d=24, define the frontier vector V(e) = (Q_24(e), p_safe,24(e), -B_G,24(e)); locked sensitivity frontiers use the analogous d-specific vector. A cell e dominates e' if every coordinate of Q_d(e) is at least the corresponding coordinate of Q_d(e'), p_safe,d(e) >= p_safe,d(e'), and B_G,d(e) <= B_G,d(e'), with at least one strict inequality. The availability-preference frontier is the set of nondominated quotient cells after raw-mask replay. No arbitrary 80% visibility cutoff is primary; parent global-accession and 80% values remain regression landmarks only.

## Exact world-wise estimands and gates

A world is
[
w=(a,d,s,r,\delta),
]
where (a\in\{2020,2021\}) is the held-out year, (d) is one separately reported cutoff, (s) is an admissible coupled clock/report/pathology state, (r) is one of the nine fixed reader pairs, and (\delta\in\{\varnothing\}\cup\{\text{delete patient }j:j\text{ eligible}\}) is the whole-patient deletion projection. The same (K), model fit, tie rule, and full-roster mask are used in all (w) within a declared primary or sensitivity analysis. Every retained world must have N(w)>=K; if a held-out or deletion roster has fewer than K patients, K is not reduced and that analysis is computationally inconclusive.

Let (C_M(w,e)\in\{0,\ldots,K\}) be the number of documentary-(Y=1) patients in the exact top (K) of model (M) in world (w), using one patient per review position. Let the capacity-matched random comparator be the exact finite-population expectation
[
C_{rand}(w)=K\,n_Y(w)/N(w),
]
under a uniform (K)-patient review subset, computed as an exact rational number rather than Monte Carlo. It is a benchmark for enrichment, not a clinical action or causal control.

For each mask, define the per-year/state/deletion lower capture
[
H_G(e)=\min_{w\in W_{24}} C_G(w,e),
quad p_{safe}(e)=H_G(e)/K,
quad B_G(e)=\max_{w\in W_{24}}(K-C_G(w,e)).
]
The primary (W_{24}) contains only (d=24); (d=12,48,72) are reported as locked sensitivity frontiers and a separate joint-worst sensitivity. The minimum is over individual worlds, so no two-year capture counts are pooled. (p\in[0,1]) is an externally elicited outcome-blind minimum acceptable documentary-M2 captures per review position relative to no review, whose attributed capture count is zero. Harbor never chooses (p).

The exact preference predicate is
[
\forall w\in W_d:\ C_G(w,e)>pK.
]
Equality is not favorable. If stakeholders provide an interval ([p_L,p_U]), a cell is action-compatible only if (p_U<p_{safe}(e)); if (p_L\le p_{safe}(e)\le p_U), the result is preference-heterogeneous; if (p_L>p_{safe}(e)), the cell fails the stated preference contract. A pooled two-year ratio is never reported as (p_{safe}).

For every frontier cell, report exact rational values and witnesses for:

- (C_G-C_R>5), (C_G-C_B>5), and (C_G-C_{rand}>5), each in every (w), with the inherited strict five-capture margin;
- the same gates under every singleton whole-patient deletion, which is a robustness family and does not mean every individual patient must have a five-capture effect;
- (G-G	ext{-mask}), the report-surface and semantic contribution contrast;
- (B_G(e)), attained year/cutoff/state/reader/deletion, and the first failing predicate if any.

The scientific contrast gates are separate from the preference predicate. A high (p_{safe}) cannot rescue a comparator failure, and comparator success cannot establish clinical utility.

## External bridge and limits

A protected external accession/version audit is required to supply, for matched whole accessions, predecessor/version IDs and hashes; authored/final/release/amendment/retraction/ingestion/readability timestamps; timezone provenance; report-access state at each cutoff; an exact whole-pipeline hash crosswalk; a validated operation clock; and a reconciled denominator and sampling protocol. The audit must produce a patient-level lower coverage curve with uncertainty, not a convenience estimate. A failed or unreconciled audit leaves status `bridge_pending` or `not_estimable_from_HCC`, regardless of the computational label.

Experts must adjudicate the procedure dictionary, Mandarin semantic parser, radiologist/pathologist qualifications, pathology sampling and specimen linkage, and biological MVI meaning. Stakeholders must elicit (p_L,p_U) while blinded to outcomes and model ranking; they must also specify burden, cost, delay, and harm weights if those are to enter the decision. A prospective silent-mode multicenter study is required for clinician response, treatment effects, transportability, safety, and patient benefit. No such stronger claims are made here.
