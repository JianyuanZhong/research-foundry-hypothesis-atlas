## Meaning of results and evidence limits

Supportive nine-year durability would mean that the frozen grip-augmented allocation identifies more participants with a recorded inpatient N18-before-death event over the longer horizon than AF1 at the same quota, and that this survives the prespecified uncertainty and event gates. It would strengthen the case for a prospective study of grip as an inexpensive triage input and suggest that a five-year signal is not merely short-term. It would not prove measured-GFR accuracy, persistent CKD, mechanism, treatment benefit, cost-effectiveness, safety, equity, or causality.

Adverse nine-year durability would falsify the one-event longer-horizon enrichment claim while leaving open a short-term parent signal, a different outcome definition, or a true but unobserved clinical effect. Inconclusive results would be scientifically useful: they would indicate that this snapshot and 62-person policy exchange cannot resolve durability, not that AFG1 and AF1 are equivalent.

Computational QA can establish exact population/list construction, source agreement, chronology parsing, endpoint counts, fixed-denominator estimands, bootstrap intervals, and whether conclusions obey the label rules. It cannot establish completeness of inpatient capture, outpatient CKD, repeated/persistent disease, measured GFR, biological mechanism, specimen chronology, clinical actionability, cost/burden, fairness, transportability, or patient benefit. Those require repeated timed creatinine/cystatin/UACR, measured GFR, complete outpatient and censoring linkage, chart/nephrologist adjudication, device/workflow/cost review, external validation, and ultimately a prospective implementation or randomized testing-strategy study.
