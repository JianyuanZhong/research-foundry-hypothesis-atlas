# Episode 9 child: temporal and competing-care boundary for the day-42 HCC assay-state experiment

## Scientific question and clinical decision

Among patients observable 42 days after a first locally recorded exact-code TACE, does the recorded assay-state increment over matched observation and care-process information predict a later locally recorded exact-code repeat-TACE event as a durable recorded-state signal, or is it concentrated in near-term operational care and/or distorted by non-TACE procedures and informative loss of observation?

The primary estimand and decision are unchanged from the parent:

- A = M0 + P_obs + P_care.
- B = A + S.
- D_state = held-out weighted log loss(A) - held-out weighted log loss(B) for the first repeat TACE in [43,181).

Positive D_state means that the recorded assay channel adds patient-held-out predictive information for this operational endpoint. It does not mean tumor response, treatment intent, treatment benefit, causality, severity, or a recommendation to repeat TACE.

The Episode-9 advance is a bounded interpretation stress test. It asks whether the same D_state is present in both an early [43,91) and a late [91,181) event-time component when a first non-TACE procedure is retained as a competing operational event, and whether the conclusion changes under the prespecified all-source observation-censoring weights. The competing event is not a control outcome, response label, planned-care label, or treatment failure. Exact codes are the only semantics allowed.

Clinical next-data decision:

1. If the primary gate passes, D_state remains supported after the seven-day lead-time control, and a supported late TACE increment remains directionally consistent in the cause-specific and competing-risk reports despite NC competition and censoring weighting, acquire expert-linked imaging response plus scheduling/intent and outside-care data next. This would justify testing whether the recorded increment maps to a clinically adjudicated state; it would not justify a care rule.
2. If the increment is confined to [43,91), attenuates after the process packet or day-36–42 removal, is reproduced by timing/value permutations, or disappears when NC is retained as a competing event or when censoring weights are applied, prioritize scheduling/intent, outside-care capture, and observation-process data. The result would be adverse to a durable-state interpretation but would not establish that care was planned.
3. If early/late or NC support gates fail, if weight ESS is inadequate, or if intervals are too wide, report inconclusive for that decomposition and obtain linkage/adjudication before changing interpretation. Do not pool a failed component into the other window or relabel an unsupported null.

## What is known, unresolved, and falsifiable

[K1] is the closest predictive work inspected: a 310-patient, three-center CT radiomics/deep-learning study reported repeat-TACE prognosis prediction with internal and external cohorts, but its target used imaging and survival context unavailable here, and performance fell in external cohorts. It supports the possibility that state-relevant information can predict downstream TACE-related outcomes; it does not establish that a local assay record or repeat-TACE code is a biological state or response.

[K2] bounds the endpoint interpretation: the inspected abstract describes dynamic CT/MRI, necrosis-aware mRECIST and RECICL criteria as treatment-response measures. This supports the claim that response requires modality- and criterion-specific imaging. It does not validate the HCC tables' examination rows or the operational repeat-TACE code as response.

[K3] is the consequential limitation inspected: its abstract reports recorded imaging-response differences but shows bounds changing with undocumented outcomes and states that missingness, imaging ascertainment, exposure classification and treatment-selection confounding prevented a comparative treatment-effect conclusion. It supports treating recorded outcomes and observation as vulnerable to process bias; it does not establish a mechanism in this HCC snapshot.

The strongest available claim is therefore only that an assay channel may add reproducible recorded information about a later operational repeat-TACE record. The unresolved claim is narrower and falsifiable: after exact process features, temporal lead-time stress, a mutually exclusive first non-TACE competing event, and all-source censoring weights are handled, does a positive assay increment persist in the late component? A late increment is compatible with durable recorded state, residual severity, or continued workflow; it is not a mechanistic result. An early-only increment is compatible with near-term workflow or ordered care as well as rapid state information, so timing alone cannot identify intent.

The experiment falsifies the durable-recorded-state interpretation for this snapshot if a supported positive D_state is absent under the primary gate, or if the apparent signal is reproduced by assay timestamp/value permutations, is removed by the seven-day lead-time control, is no larger than process-only information, or is restricted to early events and disappears after retaining NC competition or censoring weights. These outcomes still do not prove the rival explanation. An imprecise null is inconclusive, not refutation.

## Models and estimands

### Primary and temporal/competing estimands

Fit the unchanged primary A/B models and report:

- D_state for Y_T over [43,181), with ordinary and all-source/assay-observation weighted paired test loss.
- D_E_state = L_E(A) - L_E(B) for first Y_T in [43,91), and D_L_state for first Y_T in [91,181) among patients still free of Y_T and C_NC at day 91 in the competing-risk risk set.
- D_E_workflow and D_L_workflow for P_obs/P_care versus M0, to distinguish a process increment from the assay increment without treating either as a causal effect.
- Cause-specific discrete-time hazards for Y_T and C_NC, plus Aalen–Johansen cumulative-incidence predictions for Y_T through days 90 and 180 under A and B. These are predictions for the recorded population, not interventions.
- A censoring stress contrast comparing the same A/B predictions under ordinary scoring and parent-specified all-source inverse-probability-of-censoring weighting. Report weight distribution, truncation rule, and weighted ESS learned in development folds. Do not assume administrative follow-up after `obs_end`.

For the temporal/competing decomposition, the risk set is event-free of both Y_T and C_NC. A C_NC event is carried as a competing cause, not censored as if no event occurred. The primary parent estimand remains reported on its original risk set and censoring rule. The late estimand is conditional on surviving/event-free through day 90 and therefore is not comparable to a causal “late treatment effect.”

### Baseline versus learned alternative

The transparent baseline is a cause-specific elastic-net pooled-logistic discrete-time hazard with fixed bins [43,91) and [91,181), using the same A/B channels, risk sets, weights, split, and paired metrics. It is primary because event counts, channel increments, competing-event handling, censoring weights, and calibration are auditable.

The substantive learned alternative is a compact irregular-event GRU with two cause heads (Y_T and C_NC), elapsed-time decay, and the same dated (-365,+42] stream. Each event token may contain only event category/label, audited numeric assay value, qualitative/inequality flag, relative time, elapsed time since prior event, decay, and channel tag. Fit the GRU with the same A/B ablations, cause-specific discrete-time likelihood, development-only normalization, cross-fitted observation/censoring weights, and three fixed development seeds. Evaluate paired patient-held-out loss, Brier/integrated Brier, cause-specific calibration, and Aalen–Johansen CIF error/intervals. The GRU can reveal nonlinear assay trajectories and interactions between timing, process marks, and competing events that pooled summaries lose; it cannot identify intent, imaging response, outside care, units, or mechanism. It is a sensitivity for the same scientific question, not a reason to select the largest score.

Freeze one patient-level 80/20 split before fitting, stratified only on the primary Y_T indicator, seed 271828; no patient crosses partitions. Freeze IDs, algorithm and counts in `frozen_patient_split.json`. Use 314159 and 161803 as fixed sensitivity splits, never select the favorable one. ### Support, precision, and multiplicity

The inherited primary gate remains at least 50 Y_T events overall, 15 test Y_T events, 100 test patients, 15 development Y_T events, complete timing/duplicate/obs_end audits, finite weights, and weighted ESS >=100.

Each new early/late cause-specific component requires before interpretation: at least 15 events of that type in development and 15 in untouched test, at least 100 relevant test patients where the risk set is defined, finite weights, and weighted ESS >=100. The NC competing cause additionally requires the parent NC support threshold of at least 50 events overall, 15 test events, 15 development events, 100 test patients, finite weights, and weighted ESS >=100. Failure produces an explicit `inconclusive_support_failure` row; no component is pooled or replaced.

The primary D_state remains the sole primary decision. The parent W_packet and NC secondary family remains under its existing support and precision rules. New early/late/competing outputs form one prespecified interpretation family with max-|t| patient-bootstrap intervals across the named temporal contrasts, reported separately from the primary family. A component is called temporally discriminating only when its two-sided 95% patient-bootstrap interval has half-width <=0.05 in log-loss contrast and <=0.02 in Brier contrast, with finite calibration. These are precision rules, not clinical thresholds. AUROC/AUPRC are descriptive.

- alternate only the parent-allowed valid `obs_end` definitions and retain undated strict-TACE exclusions;

Supportive evidence for a durable recorded-state increment requires the primary gate, familywise lower 95% bound for D_state >0, acceptable calibration, ordinary and weighted direction agreement, no comparable assay-permutation increment, and a supported late D_L_state direction that is not eliminated by retaining C_NC or censoring weights. ## Evidence boundary and next study

The available snapshot has no imaging payload, mRECIST/RECICL adjudication, treatment intent or scheduling, outside-care capture, assay units/reference ranges, mortality, harms, costs, transfers, or expert severity review. `检查所见` and `检查诊断` exist but are not validated response labels and are excluded from response labeling and NLP. `手术` is an operational code; neither exact TACE nor non-TACE procedure tokens encode intent.

A supportive result warrants a linked imaging/intent/outside-care adjudication study. An adverse result prioritizes workflow and observation linkage. An inconclusive result warrants better capture and larger support. None establishes tumor response, causal effect, treatment benefit, mortality, harm, cost, mechanism, or a treatment rule.
