# Common-arrival-prefix, equal-workload HCC documentary-M2 review allocation

## Change from the assessed parent

The parent correctly fixed the number of offered review slots with an absolute, state-invariant integer K_yq, and added a completed-review prefix J_yq=ceil(0.90K_yq). Its remaining substantive weakness is that R_J(M) is computed from each policy's own first J accepted patients. The policies can therefore complete those J reviews on different calendar/arrival prefixes. A policy that waits, rejects more early patients, or reaches its Jth review later can be compared on a different opportunity set. That is a valid descriptive “first J completions” quantity, but it is not the cleanest test of incremental prioritization at a common online opportunity set.

This child preserves the parent’s fixed completed-review prefix and adds a paired common-arrival-prefix replay. The primary equal-workload estimand now evaluates all policies on the same chronological arrival prefix ending when the slowest primary policy has completed J. The policy never observes that stopping point and cannot use future arrivals. The parent’s per-policy first-J quantity is retained only as a diagnostic for completion-time dependence, not as evidence of semantic prioritization.

## Clinical question and evidence boundary

The decision is whether a scarce preoperative multidisciplinary review service should prioritize adults approaching a first eligible HCC resection using routine CT/MRI report semantics, so that more reviews identify patients whose eventual resection pathology documents high-grade microvascular invasion (documentary M2).

The action is review allocation only. This experiment does not recommend wider resection, transplantation, neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment, locoregional or systemic therapy, surveillance, or any other care.

The strongest claim supported by this HCC snapshot is that it contains patient/encounter-linked procedures and encounters, examination accessions with acquisition-like timestamps and report text, untimed pathology narratives, and dated medication/order records. The snapshot does not contain report authored/final/release/view times or immutable versions, pathology time/specimen/slide/block identifiers or sampling protocol, an MDT roster or capacity ledger, clinician review/action, treatment, recurrence, survival, harms, cost, or benefit. Therefore the computable claim is historical stored-content allocation under an explicitly reconstructed arrival process. It is not evidence that the report was visible at the decision time, that the workload is deliverable, or that review changes patient outcomes.

The demonstrations in references/research-ambition/README.md are rigor examples, not evidence for this HCC question. The cancer main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable as stated in that README; no claim here depends on having read them.

## Hypothesis and estimands

Let s be a complete, source-consistent outer state. A state jointly fixes the operative episode and clock, selected encounter and year, recorded prior-treatment history, examination accession/component clock and modality pattern, report-body mask, pathology composite/outcome, and chronological arrival order. No field may be taken from one state and another field from a different state.

Use separate calendar service cycles y=2020 and 2021. Before using pathology grades or semantic features, define

N_min,y = min_s N_y(s), and K_yq = floor(q N_min,y), q in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}.

These are frozen available-data workload benchmarks, not observed hospital capacity. They are absolute integers shared by every model, source state, and bootstrap replicate. If an external capacity audit later supplies preregistered absolute slots, it may replace these sensitivity benchmarks only in a new approved design.

Primary policies are:

- S: structured baseline plus the prespecified report-semantic block.
- S0: the exact fitted S pipeline with only semantic fields masked, retaining coefficients, preprocessing, calibration, thresholds, and release logic.
- B: independently fitted structured, report-body-free baseline.

All policies use the same state-specific eligible roster, release schedule, calendar information, and integer budget. A policy may use only the current patient's admissible pre-decision record, its frozen score, the observed arrival day/clock available under that source state, slots already used, and its frozen release schedule. It cannot use future arrivals, final annual N, future test ranks, outcome labels, another policy's actions, or the evaluation stopping point.

### Primary common-arrival-prefix equal-workload estimand

For each state s, year y, capacity fraction q, access cell, and tested decision buffer, run a separate J-capped online replay with J_yq=ceil(0.90K_yq).

The cap is an analysis workload cap: after a policy completes J_yq reviews it becomes inert in this replay. It does not receive additional patients, although chronological arrivals continue for policies that have not yet completed J_yq. This prevents a fast policy from consuming extra reviews before the common comparison ends.

Let a_M,s(i) be the policy action at ordered arrival i, and let r_M,s(i) be its acceptance ordinal. Let l_M,s,J be the arrival ordinal of policy M's J_yq-th accepted review. If any primary policy fails to reach J_yq before the service-cycle boundary, the state/cell is not supportive and is classified as inconclusive for the joint claim. Otherwise define the common stopping ordinal

l_s,J = max over M in {S,S0,B} of l_M,s,J.

The common comparison prefix is the same first l_s,J arrivals for all three policies in state s. The stopping ordinal is computed only after replay and is never exposed to a policy during replay. Each policy has exactly J_yq accepted cases within this shared opportunity prefix. Define

R_common_J(M,s,y,q) =
100/J_yq times the sum over i <= l_s,J of 1{r_M,s(i) <= J_yq} Y_s(i).

The primary equal-workload contrasts are

D_common_J = R_common_J(S) - R_common_J(S0),
C_common_J = R_common_J(S) - R_common_J(B).

They are additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 reviews at the same workload and under the same arrival opportunity prefix. This is a prioritization/yield estimand, not a treatment-effect estimand.

Retain the parent’s per-policy first-J result,

R_own_J(M) = 100/J times the sum over i of 1{r_M(i) <= J}Y(i),

as a secondary completion-time diagnostic. A large R_own_J contrast without a large R_common_J contrast is evidence that the apparent gain depends on different completion horizons, not robust common-prefix prioritization.

### Offered-slot service estimand

In a separate replay with the same release curve and absolute K_yq, each policy may act until the service cycle ends or K_yq is reached. Define

R_offer(M,s,y,q) = 100/K_yq times the sum over i of A_M,s(i)Y_s(i),
U(M,s,y,q) = K_yq^{-1} times the sum over i of A_M,s(i).

The offered-slot contrasts are D_offer=R_offer(S)-R_offer(S0) and C_offer=R_offer(S)-R_offer(B). These measure service yield including utilization; they must not be substituted for the common-prefix equal-workload contrasts.

The service replay and the common-prefix replay use independent, frozen counters. A policy that reaches J early in the common-prefix replay is not credited with later reviews in that estimand. A policy that underuses K in the offered replay is not padded, recalled, or filled with future patients.

### Access frontier

A report is visible only when the entire selected accession body is exposed; component rows cannot be split. Retain the parent’s four exact guarantees on a 0.05 grid:

- h_C: C-pattern CT visibility.
- h_M: M-pattern MRI visibility.
- h_A: CM patients with at least one modality report visible.
- h_B <= h_A: CM patients with both reports visible.

For each access cell, fit one development-only policy version per cell using the same patient-wise worst-state objective over the complete development state envelope, freeze it before 2020, and apply it without refitting across all admissible 2020/2021 test states. The policy receives only the masked report fields; the hidden visibility mask, pathology outcome, state label, and adversarial stopping ordinal are never features. The frontier optimizer may jointly choose visibility masks, modality choices for singly exposed CM cases, clock/order alternatives, membership, and outcomes, but it may not alter a policy action after seeing Y.

The primary access frontier is the smallest coordinatewise guarantee for which, in every y, q, tested clock buffer, and admissible coupled state, both common-prefix contrasts and both offered-slot contrasts have one-sided simultaneous lower limits above 5 additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 reviews, every policy reaches J, and worst-state offered utilization is at least 0.90. If no grid cell satisfies all conditions, the frontier is empty.

### Hypothesis and falsification

The primary hypothesis is:

There is a nonempty access/capacity frontier, and at every claimed frontier cell the simultaneous lower limits for D_common_J, C_common_J, D_offer, and C_offer exceed 5 in both 2020 and 2021 for every tested q and clock buffer, every primary policy reaches J, and worst-state utilization is at least 0.90.

A secondary attainability check compares online S capture with a state-matched retrospective top-K oracle. Its simultaneous lower ratio must exceed 0.80 for every year and q. Oracle-positive but common-prefix-negative results are adverse for operational attainability.

Falsification is prespecified:

- A valid simultaneous upper bound at or below 5 for any required common-prefix or offered-slot contrast at any claimed year/capacity/access cell falsifies the joint claim at that cell.
- An empty frontier falsifies the access-capacity hypothesis for this source-state envelope.
- An attainability upper bound below 0.80 falsifies transport from retrospective ranking to the chronological online policy.
- Supportive offered-slot contrasts with a common-prefix contrast upper bound at or below 5 indicate utilization/completion-horizon dependence, not established semantic prioritization.
- Intervals crossing 5, failed state or adjudication gates, or inability of any policy to reach J are inconclusive rather than positive or negative.

## Population, clocks, and online arrival process

The person unit is 患者主索引; same-encounter joins use (患者主索引, 就诊号). Raw duplicate rows remain in a raw-ordinal ledger. Two hepatobiliary surgeons and a surgical informatician freeze the operative dictionary and reconcile 手术来源 values from the surgery system and case-record surgery rows. A clinically linked valid anesthesia-system 开始时间 is exact. An unvalidated case-record midnight/date proxy is represented as [d,d+24h). Missing and competing clocks remain explicit state alternatives.

Eligibility is a retrospective ascertainment rule for the fixed evaluation roster: age at least 18 at the selected encounter; all-row same-encounter pathology explicitly supporting HCC; and no recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy during [t_dec-365d,t_dec), using state-matched dated procedures, medications, and orders. This is not biological treatment-naivety and is not a pathology-based online feature. Population membership is frozen before queue replay; the queue can use neither pathology nor post-cutoff fields.

Set t_dec=t_op-24h as primary and run 12-, 48-, and 72-hour sensitivities. Develop in 2015–2018, tune and freeze thresholds and release logic in 2019, and test 2020 and 2021 separately. A patient with any possible later-period state is quarantined from earlier fitting. 2022 onward is drift-only and cannot rescue the primary result.

Arrivals are ordered by the state-specific t_dec. The policy-visible time is the validated exact clock when one exists and otherwise the released date/day bucket; it is not an analyst-invented exact time. Same-time ties use a frozen hash of 患者主索引. If an interval can cross an arrival or release boundary, enumerate every defensible order/boundary state. The policy is run separately on each state’s realized chronological order but never receives the hidden state label or interval endpoint.

For year y, freeze an annual release curve before test replay:

L_yq(d) = floor(K_yq F_2015–2019(d)),

where F is the pooled development/tuning empirical cumulative fraction of eligible arrivals by day-of-year, monotonized and frozen before 2020. At an arrival on day d, the policy may not have used more than L_yq(d) slots. Thresholds and any score-to-action rule are frozen using 2015–2018 development and 2019 tuning only. There is no end-of-year filling, recall, substitution, future-rank use, or outcome-based release. The common-prefix stopping ordinal is evaluation-only.

## Report unit, endpoint, and predictors

Before modality filtering or semantic extraction, group every nonblank examination accession by (患者主索引, 就诊号, 检查号), retaining every component row and raw ordinal. An accession is eligible only if the state-specific clock places every component in [t_dec-90d,t_dec). The primary conservative clock uses the maximum valid component 开始时间; missing/conflicting component clocks remain alternatives. Select at most the latest eligible accession per modality. Blinded abdominal radiologists map each bundle to CT, MRI, non-target, or unresolved. Concatenate 检查所见 and 检查诊断 in frozen source order. Blank 检查号 rows are logged and excluded from semantic units, never silently treated as separate reports.

Compose all deduplicated pathology rows on the selected encounter using 病理, 检查所见, and 检查诊断. A frozen position-aware parser assigns Y=1 only to an anchored selected-value M2 and Y=0 only to an anchored M0/M1. Boilerplate, unanchored mentions, missing/uncertain grade, conflicts, uncertain specimen linkage, or inadequate documented sampling yields Y in {0,1}, never silent exclusion. Two Chinese-reading pathologists blinded to predictors adjudicate every parsed M2 and every test unknown/conflict, with a third resolving disagreements. The endpoint is documentary M2, not biological M2.

The structured baseline B uses only state-selected age, sex, CT/MRI/O acquisition indicators, eligible accession count, acquisition-to-decision recency, component count, and development-frequency machine category. It excludes report body, laboratories, diagnoses, pathology, identifiers, year, and post-cutoff facts. S adds frozen interpretable fields for lesion diameter/count, capsule, margin, arterial hyperenhancement, washout, peritumoral enhancement/hypointensity, satellite lesion, portal/hepatic-vein tumor thrombus, cirrhosis, and ascites, coded present/absent/uncertain/not-mentioned. S0 is the exact S pipeline after masking only those semantic fields; fully masked S and S0 must have bitwise-identical preprocessing, scores, actions, and D equal to zero.

Report first-come, periodic lottery, no-review, age/sex-only, and retrospective top-K policies as negative/control benchmarks. The retrospective top-K policy is an oracle benchmark only and is never presented as deployable.

## Inference, uncertainty, and validity gates

For each bootstrap replicate, resample patients as whole clusters within the fixed calendar split, rebuild the lossless state ledger, refit development models, retune only in 2019, freeze the resulting test policy, and replay every coupled test state. Keep the original K_yq integers frozen; if a replicate cannot support a required K, mark that replicate failed rather than changing denominators.

Within each replicate and each state, compute the common-prefix and offered-slot statistics first. Then form the prespecified worst-state statistic for each year, q, buffer, and access cell. Apply one max-centered simultaneous 97.5% family to D_common_J, C_common_J, D_offer, C_offer, utilization, attainability ratios, and all tested frontier cells. The state minimum is therefore inside the bootstrap statistic; it is not a post-hoc pointwise minimum combined with ordinary confidence intervals. More than 2.5% failed bootstrap replicates invalidates support.

Use 1,000 development/tuning semantic-null permutations stratified by year, modality/pattern, accession cardinality, and formatting family, with full refitting and S0 reconstruction. Report the null envelope as a diagnostic; it cannot convert an adverse online capture result into support.

Support requires, at minimum:

- N_min,y >= 500 for each claimed year.
- At least 50 adjudicated documentary-M2 events per claimed year envelope.
- Anchored-grade rate at least 85% and every test unknown/conflict reviewed.
- Parser kappa at least 0.90, M2 PPV at least 0.98, and sensitivity at least 0.95.
- Modality agreement at least 0.95 and unresolved modality at most 1%.
- At least 100 patients and 20 documentary-M2 events in each claimed modality pattern.
- Semantic-feature PPV and sensitivity at least 0.85.
- No required-feature or pattern shift greater than 15 percentage points from 2019 to either test year.
- All/no-report calibration slope 0.80–1.20 and intercept -0.10–0.10.
- Exact slot/release accounting, complete state/backpointer replay, optimizer/brute-force agreement on small fixtures, monotone access limits, and zero laboratory or forbidden-lineage use.

Supportive results require all gates, a nonempty frontier, every primary policy reaching J, simultaneous lower limits above 5 for both common-prefix contrasts and both offered-slot contrasts in both years at every q and claimed frontier cell, worst-state utilization at least 0.90, and attainability lower limits above 0.80. The conclusion is limited to robust historical stored-report semantic value under the specified prefix policy and common-arrival-prefix equal workload. It does not establish report visibility, acceptable workload, clinical action, or patient benefit.

Adverse results include a valid simultaneous upper bound at or below 5 for any required contrast, an empty frontier, or an attainability upper bound below 0.80. If only offered-slot contrasts support while common-prefix contrasts are adverse, label the result utilization or completion-horizon explained. If only some years, capacities, buffers, patterns, or access states support, the joint hypothesis fails and results are reported cell-by-cell; years and capacities are not averaged into support.

Inconclusive results include uncertainty crossing the margin, a failed adjudication/calibration/null/state/bootstrap/optimizer gate, incomplete state backpointers, failure to reach J, or absent/mismatched external audit evidence. A favorable AUC, AUPRC, calibration, retrospective oracle, or all-stored-content point estimate cannot rescue an inconclusive or adverse common-prefix result.
