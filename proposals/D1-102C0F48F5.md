## 1. Unresolved claim vs already-supported conclusions

Already supported (NOT re-tested): ICU readmission models are only moderately discriminative
and confounded by non-actionable proxies [K1]; dynamic *variability* of a time-varying analyte
carries prognostic information in critical illness [K2]; ordered/continuous vital-sign learning
is feasible and can beat episodic snapshot monitoring for near-term deterioration [K3]. None of
[K1]-[K3] separates pre-discharge within-window variability/trend from the mean level of the
SAME signals, none includes a value-free observation-intensity negative control, and none
targets the ICU-discharge decision with a 7-day readmission/death outcome.

Unresolved, falsifiable claim tested (H-instability): in the fixed pre-discharge window
[outtime-12h, outtime], within-window variability/trend and rising O2 requirement of continuously
charted vitals carry prognostic information for 7-day ICU readmission or in-hospital death
BEYOND (i) the mean/last level of the same signals and (ii) observation/review intensity.

Strongest rival (R-level/measurement): apparent "instability" is fully explained by discharge
severity level and by who gets watched/charted (denser, wider documentation is outcome-correlated
but value-free).

Distinct predictions: H-instability -> Delta-AUROC(B1-B0)>margin AND M-obs clearly worse than B1
AND Delta-AUROC(A1-B1)>margin (ordering/shape/cross-signal timing add beyond scalar summaries).
R-level -> B1 approx B0 (equivalence) and/or M-obs approx B1; A1 approx B1. Both may partly hold
(decompose, do not force a single accept/reject). Observation intensity is TESTED, not removed.

## 4. Baselines and substantive learned/mechanistic alternative (same cohort, split, label, test set)

- B0 (interpretable, level-only): L2 penalized logistic on last pre-discharge MAP, HR, RR, SpO2,
  FiO2, temp, vasopressor-active flag, ventilation-active flag, age, sex, ICU LOS.
- B1 (primary instability, interpretable): B0 + within-window per-signal SD, IQR, range, CV,
  Theil-Sen slope, fraction outside reference band, FiO2 slope / O2-device escalation, PLUS
  per-signal measurement counts and charting gaps (so B1 is not merely being-watched).
- A1 (substantive learned alternative): small masked recurrent encoder (GRU 64-128, 1-2 layers)
  over the irregular multivariate 12h series of the SAME itemids, with per-signal observation
  masks and a time-since-last-value channel so cross-signal coupling timing is learnable;
  fallback irregular-time attention or GP summary. What A1 reveals that B1 loses: SD/IQR/slope
  are permutation/shape-invariant and discard the ORDER, waveform shape and cross-signal timing.
- M-obs (value-free negative control): observation frequency/density/gap-timing only, no values.
- Label-permutation control.

Estimand: primary Delta-AUROC(B1-B0) and Delta-AUROC(A1-B1) with predeclared equivalence margin
0.01; calibration slope/intercept; decision-curve net benefit at observed rate; subject_id-clustered
bootstrap 95% CI. SUPPORTIVE (Delta>margin, CI excludes 0, M-obs worse) / ADVERSE (Delta within
+/-margin i.e. equivalence, or M-obs approx B1) / INCONCLUSIVE (underpowered or discordant).
Predictive utility only — no causal/mechanistic claim.

readmission provenance requires clinical adjudication.
