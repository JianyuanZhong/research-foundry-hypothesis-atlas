# Assay-semantic validity audit for the frozen HCC repeat-TACE P-beyond-B experiment

## Parent, status, and material repair

This is a substantive successor to `[prior hypothesis]`. The primary question, population, selected repeat-TACE event, assay clocks, annual forward blocks, locked models, estimand, and noncausal evidence boundary are frozen. The child does not replace the parent’s explicit missing-Y partial-identification contract and does not claim that the new audit has been executed.

The unresolved weakness addressed here is assay semantic validity and clinical interpretability. The HCC laboratory table supplies a numeric result but no unit or reference-range column. In addition, the same exact assay label can potentially contain repeated records, different specimen types, qualitative-result text, method/platform changes, or values that are not safely comparable across encounters. A finite numeric string is therefore not sufficient evidence that B, P, and Y are measurements on one clinically interpretable scale. This matters because the frozen endpoint is a standardized prediction error for the subsequent assay value: a favorable gain could be a data/measurement-process artifact rather than a stable physiologic signal.

The strongest claim inherited from the parent is only that, conditional on the reconstructed selected event and observed complete triplet, the specified numeric fields can be compared prognostically in a selected calendar mixture; the parent’s audit supports one local 2022 cutoff and leaves later transport inconclusive. The unresolved claim tested by this child is narrower and falsifiable: within each assay, are the selected B/P/Y values semantically homogeneous enough—by exact assay label, specimen metadata, qualitative-result status, duplicate structure, and calendar period—that the frozen P-beyond-B comparison is not driven by an identifiable measurement-semantic stratum? This is still a prognostic data-validity question, not a claim of clinical recovery, treatment response, or benefit.

## Computable semantic audit, defined before reading future Y

Run the following checks on all raw rows with exact assay label `白蛋白` or `总胆红素`, then attach flags to selected B/P/Y rows by their source row identity and composite encounter key. The checks are descriptive and outcome-blind until the ordinary Y value is selected by the frozen clock.

1. **Parser and field-role audit.** Record whether `定量结果` is blank, finite numeric, nonfinite, censored/inequality-like, or nonnumeric; independently record whether `定性结果` is blank or nonblank. The parent’s finite uncensored parser remains the only eligibility parser. A row with a finite quantitative result and a nonblank qualitative field is not silently recoded; it receives `qualitative_co_present=1` and remains in the parent only if it passes the inherited parser. Record parse-failure counts by assay, year, arm, and B/P/Y role.
2. **Metadata availability audit.** Report missingness and distinct nonblank values of `标本类型` by assay and calendar block. Treat an empty specimen field as unknown, never as serum/plasma or as evidence of equivalence. Because units and reference ranges are absent, emit `unit_unavailable=1` for every row and do not infer units from `定量结果`, `标本类型`, or magnitude.
3. **Duplicate and replicate audit.** Before selecting B/P/Y, group exact assay rows by (`患者主索引`,`就诊号`,`检验`,`检验时间`,`标本类型`) and separately by (`患者主索引`,`就诊号`,`检验`,`检验时间`). Report multiplicity, finite-value range, and disagreement among duplicate numeric values. For exact-time replicates with more than one finite value, use the parent deterministic source-row tie rule for the primary row and retain a `replicate_disagreement` flag; never average or choose the value using Y.
4. **Specimen-stratum audit.** For each assay and each role (B, P, Y), cross-tab nonblank specimen type and unknown specimen type, including systemic/comparator and annual block. Do not interpret specimen labels as validated units or reference ranges. If a single nonblank specimen stratum has at least 20 BP-eligible episodes overall and at least 10 in each arm, report it as a descriptive support stratum; otherwise mark it sparse rather than pooling it with another type.
5. **Calendar semantic-drift audit.** In training only, calculate assay-specific distributions of finite values, absolute consecutive same-patient differences where the parent clocks permit them, and rank/quantile distributions by calendar block and specimen stratum. Report standardized mean differences and empirical quantile differences as measurement-process diagnostics, not as evidence of physiologic change. A sudden block/specimen shift is a warning, not proof of a unit conversion or clinical deterioration.
6. **Role-consistency audit.** For each selected triplet, report whether B, P, and Y have the same known specimen type, any unknown type, or different known types. This is a prespecified semantic contrast. It does not delete mixed-type rows from the primary estimand. If counts allow, rerun the exact frozen models in the sensitivity sets `same_known_specimen` and `all_three_known_same_specimen`; retain the parent primary as the estimand and label these as transport/measurement sensitivity analyses. No sensitivity set is interpreted if it fails the inherited event, arm, finite-prediction, or observed-Y gates.
7. **Rank-scale falsification sensitivity.** As a secondary measurement-robustness check only, within each training origin and assay map each numeric assay value to a training-fold empirical rank or normal score, applying the identical frozen mapping to the future block and assigning out-of-range future values to boundary ranks. Refit M0/M1 with the transformed B/P/Y under the same nested procedure. This does not replace the raw-scale endpoint and is not a claim that rank values are clinically calibrated. If raw-scale support disappears while rank-scale support remains (or vice versa), classify the result as scale-sensitive rather than supportive.
8. **Negative semantic controls.** Repeat the exact row-role and duplicate audit for a prespecified set of nearby non-target exact labels selected from the raw `检验` vocabulary without inspecting outcomes, with no claim that those assays are clinically exchangeable. A target-only anomaly that is absent in controls is a warning for target assay semantics; broad anomaly across controls indicates a general laboratory recording issue. The control labels and selection hash must be frozen before reading Y and reported for reproducibility. Controls cannot enter the target model.

No unit conversion, reference-range normalization, winsorization, imputation, text mining, or specimen-based exclusion is allowed in the primary or partial-identification analysis. The only permitted numeric parser is the parent finite uncensored parser on `定量结果`.

## Prespecified falsification and interpretation rules

The semantic audit is falsified as reassuring if any of the following occurs: selected complete triplets contain an unreported nonfinite/censored value after the declared parser; duplicate exact-time rows disagree materially under the predeclared tolerance; a target assay has a sparse or changing specimen composition that is associated with the observed incremental gain; raw and rank-scale conclusions disagree materially; the gain is restricted to mixed/unknown specimen triplets; or target-only anomalies are present relative to the frozen negative controls. The tolerance for duplicate disagreement must be declared in source units only if units are available; because they are not, use an exact-string disagreement flag and report numeric ranges rather than inventing a clinically meaningful threshold.

Interpretation precedence is:

* **Supportive but measurement-qualified:** inherited reconstruction and gates pass; raw primary G is estimable; the parent Delta bound is strictly positive over its declared training-range scenario; semantic flags are rare, stable across blocks/specimen strata, and raw versus rank sensitivity is concordant. This supports only a selected short-horizon prognostic association on an uncalibrated local assay scale, not clinical severity or benefit.
* **Measurement-sensitive:** the raw primary result is favorable but semantic strata, rank transformation, duplicate disagreement, or calendar drift changes its sign or magnitude materially. Report the selected complete-triplet association as measurement-process sensitive, not robust.
* **Adverse:** an adequately supported raw primary result is nonpositive or repeated M1 degradation occurs; a semantic sensitivity cannot rescue it. This is adverse for the prognostic claim, not evidence that the assay lacks clinical value in other settings.
* **Selection-sensitive:** the raw result is favorable but parent Delta crosses zero, missing-Y observation differs, or semantic validity is correlated with Y observation. Do not upgrade it to supportive.
* **Inconclusive/unsupported:** arm or calendar gates fail, cells are sparse, range bounds are unsupported, predictions are nonfinite, unit/reference-range evidence is required, or no meaningful semantic stratum can be evaluated. Do not pool incomparable types to manufacture precision.

A favorable semantic audit cannot establish assay units, reference intervals, analytic method, calibration to severity, treatment administration or intent, imaging response, mortality, hepatic failure, completed follow-up, external-care capture, causality, safety, utility, actionability, or patient benefit. Those require laboratory metadata, clinical adjudication, expert review, and an independent temporal/prospective or external cohort.

## Compiler/verifier contract

The compiler must emit deterministic, aggregate-only JSON/CSV with source hashes, schema hashes, parser version, selected control-label hash, row-role counts, unknown/mixed specimen counts, duplicate multiplicity/disagreement counts, semantic sensitivity denominators, rank-map training ranges, raw and sensitivity G/CI results, inherited Delta and tipping outputs, and status/error codes. No direct identifiers, free-text laboratory values, or clinical documents may be emitted.

The verifier must check that (a) all primary denominators and clocks equal the frozen parent; (b) semantic flags were computed from the declared raw columns without changing event selection; (c) units were marked unavailable rather than inferred; (d) duplicate handling never creates many-to-many joins or averages rows; (e) rank maps are fitted only before each origin; (f) future outcome values are not used for audit thresholds or event selection; (g) semantic sensitivities do not replace the primary estimand; (h) a raw/rank or specimen-stratum disagreement yields measurement-sensitive/inconclusive status; and (i) no output calls an assay value a clinical outcome or a noncausal association a treatment effect.

Computationally checkable claims are source/header/schema hashes, parser flags, duplicate-safe row linkage, timing, denominators, specimen/qualitative/duplicate distributions, training-only rank transformations, primary and sensitivity arithmetic, missing-Y endpoint bounds, and status precedence. Unit identity, analytic platform equivalence, reference intervals, biological severity, completed care, and clinical utility remain uncheckable from this snapshot and must not be claimed.
