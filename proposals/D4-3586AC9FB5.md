# UKB 36: Reserve before cancer diagnosis and non-cancer mortality after diagnosis

Admission: feasibility_required. Verify participant overlap, measurement dates, missingness and event support before committing this design.

## Hypotheses to Be Tested

Within the same cancer type, those with low pre-disease muscle strength and poor cardiorenal reserve have a more pronounced increase in post-diagnosis risk of non-cancer death, not just an increase in cancer mortality.

## Study population and key data

Individuals with pre-illness assessments and follow-up for incident colorectal cancer; grip strength, kidney function, registry, and cause of death.

## Basic Approach

Using the date of diagnosis as the starting point, compare the competing risks of cancer and non-cancer death, stratified by the interval between pre-disease measurement and diagnosis.

## Significance of the Research

Discuss host reserve separately from tumor burden.

## Main Challenges

Without complete staging and treatment data, treatment tolerance cannot be attributed; selection of cancer cases may introduce bias.

## Life Sciences

Tumor development, host reserve, and hematopoiesis

## Competing Explanations

Occult advanced tumors may already have reduced reserve, or differences in stage and treatment may account for mortality differences.

## Minimal validation and falsification criteria

First, verify the measurement using data collected well before diagnosis; if the association is limited to the period close to diagnosis, reinterpret it as a disease prodrome.
