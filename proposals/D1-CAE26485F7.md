# Post-LDT AFP slope: tumour burden, periprocedural release, or treatment-schedule selection? A same-population restricted-slope test

"decay-clean M_decay" design). The parent's frozen design (Sections 2-3) is inherited **verbatim**; this child adds exactly ONE

## 1. Unresolved question, clinical importance, and the advance over the parent
Clinicians repeat AFP after liver-directed therapy (LDT: TACE / ablation / resection) and must decide when to re-image. The
parent tests whether the within-patient AFP slope over (index, t*=index+180d] carries progression information beyond baseline,
inside an imaging-negative landmark stratum (IMGneg). The parent then *revised the slope definition* to a decay-clean slope
M_decay (draws >=30d after every LDT) to purge the periprocedural AFP pulse (R_decay, bounded by [K2]).

**The gap this child closes.** M_decay changes TWO things at once: the slope definition (which draws enter the OLS) AND the
eligible **patient population** — only patients who happen to have >=2 draws >=30d after every procedure can supply M_decay.
That decay-clean-eligible subset is selected by *treatment schedule and draw timing*, so an M0-vs-M_decay difference cannot be
attributed to tumour biology vs selection. This child names the rival **R_selection**: *the M_decay association is attributable
to the treatment-schedule / patient-composition change induced by requiring decay-clean draws, not to tumour burden.* [K3]
supplies the general danger (crude association attenuating once exposure/timing discipline is imposed). The advance is a
**same-population** contrast: re-estimate the *all-draw* slope M0_res on the **identical** decay-clean-eligible subset, plus a
pre-declared procedure-intensity adjustment, so slope definition and population are separated.

## 2. Inherited frozen design (verbatim from parent)
- **Population:** HCC diagnosis (diagnoses `诊断名称`) AND >=1 liver-directed procedure (procedures `手术`).
- **Index** = earliest qualifying LDT `开始时间`; **t\*** = index + 180d (90d sensitivity).
- **Exposure (parent M0):** per-patient OLS slope of log10(AFP) over draws with `检验时间` in (index, t\*]; AFP = labs `检验`='甲胎蛋白',
  numeric `定量结果`; **same-draw** pairing of albumin + total bilirubin within +/-2d of `检验时间`; ALBI = 0.66*log10(bilirubin) - 0.085*albumin;
  AFP/albumin/bilirubin look-alike exclusions (甲胎蛋白异质体/AFP-L3, 前白蛋白, 糖化白蛋白, 白蛋白/球蛋白/白球比, 总胆红素 etc.).
- **Landmark imaging status** over `检查`-`开始时间` in [t\*-45d, t\*+45d]: IMGneg = a qualifying abdominal study whose `检查所见` AND
  `检查诊断` contain none of {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多, 转移}; IMGpos otherwise; IMGmissing if none.
- **Outcome:** modality-restricted abdominal **local progression** (lexical event = any of {增大, 进展, 复发, 新发, 增多}) with
  `转移` as competing event; censor via encounters `出院时间`. Secondary: retreatment proxy. M7 corroboration endpoint unchanged.
- **Covariates/controls:** age, sex, baseline tumour/procedure variables, surveillance-intensity (draw count, imaging cadence),
  ALBI-slope negative control; four rivals; conjunctive falsification; **no survival / mRECIST claim**.
- **Inherited M_decay element** (parent Section 4): decay-clean slope D = OLS slope of log10(AFP) over draws >=30d after EVERY LDT
  `开始时间` (45d sensitivity), re-estimated on the same IMGneg landmark Cox, with a 1-df slope-definition (M0 vs M_decay) x slope LRT.

## 4. The ONE pre-specified, decision-changing element: same-population restricted M0_res + procedure-intensity adjustment
1. **Define the decay-clean-eligible population** E = IMGneg patients who supply **>=2 decay-clean draws in (index, t\*]** (>=30d after
   every LDT; 45d sensitivity set E45). This is the parent's M_decay analysis set. **E is used for BOTH M0_res and M_decay** — identical rows.
2. **M0_res** = the parent's **all-draw** OLS slope of log10(AFP) (all draws in (index, t\*], periprocedural ones INCLUDED),
   re-estimated as the exposure in the **same** IMGneg landmark Cox, **restricted to E**. Same estimator, same population as M_decay;
   only the *definition of the slope* differs.
3. **Procedure-intensity adjustment (pre-declared):** add LDT count in (index, t\*] as a covariate (`procedures` count of qualifying LDT
   `开始时间` in that window) to BOTH the M0_res and M_decay Cox fits, and report the adjusted slope HRs.

**Decision the element changes.** The primary contrast is **M0_res vs M_decay, SAME patients** (slope-definition effect, population held
fixed). The parent's **M0 (full cohort) vs M0_res** contrast is the *population-selection* effect, and **M0_res vs M_decay-adjusted** vs
**M0_res-unadjusted** isolates the *procedure-intensity* effect. A difference that survives all three is attributable to slope
definition (tumour-biology vs periprocedural-release), not to R_selection.

## 5. Baseline vs alternative (method comparison, recorded)
- **Baseline (parent) M0:** landmark Cox with all-draw AFP slope on the full IMGneg cohort.
- **Alternative (this child):** restricted **M0_res** (all-draw slope on E) vs **M_decay** (decay-clean slope on E), each with/without the
  pre-declared procedure-intensity covariate, plus the same-population slope-definition LRT.
- **What the alternative reveals that the baseline loses:** the baseline cannot tell whether an M0/M_decay gap is slope *definition* or
  *population selection*; M0_res makes those two effects separately estimable. This tests a substantive uncertainty (attribution), not a
  small predictive gain.
- **Deferred alternative:** shared-parameter joint model of the AFP trajectory with an explicit periprocedural pulse term (\u00e0 la [K2]);
  **revisit condition:** if E is large enough to fit M_decay but the procedure-intensity-adjusted effect is intermediate (neither clearly
  null nor clearly positive), model the pulse explicitly rather than dropping draws. CPU-only, minutes-scale; **no GPU required**.

## 6. Pre-committed branches (on M0_res vs M_decay within IMGneg, SAME population E; conjunctive)
- **Supportive (tumour-burden attribution survives selection control):** in E, M_decay HR>1 with 95% CI excluding 1, M0_res HR>1 with CI
  excluding 1, same-population slope-definition LRT **p>0.05**, attenuation |M_decay vs M0_res| **<40%**, procedure-intensity adjustment
  leaves M_decay CI excluding 1, and >=20 M_decay events. => the post-LDT slope is not merely periprocedural release nor a
  treatment-schedule-selection artefact; adjunct AFP-guided re-imaging is worth prospective testing.
- **Adverse = R_selection (pre-declared numeric thresholds):** EITHER (a) **population shift** — M0_res CI includes 1 while full-cohort M0
  CI excludes 1 (i.e. restricting to decay-clean-eligible patients already nulls the association), OR (b) **procedure-intensity removal** —
  adding the LDT-count covariate moves M_decay HR by **>=30%** toward 1 so its CI includes 1 while the unadjusted CI excluded 1, OR
  (c) **definition mismatch** — M_decay CI includes 1 (or sign reversal) with >=20 events while M0_res CI excludes 1. => the parent's
  association is substantially a treatment-schedule/composition effect; the "early tumour-burden warning" claim is **retracted** for this cohort.
- **Inconclusive (bound to the MEASURED probe, Section 7):** **<50% of IMGneg-eligible patients have >=2 decay-clean draws**, or **<20
  decay-clean events**, or procedure `开始时间` missing for the decay map. The probe measures **0.37% (58/15,807) IMGneg patients with >=2
  gap-30 decay-clean draws** (0.11% at gap-45) — far below 50%, and the derived event count cannot reach 20 — so **this branch is
  MEASURED-triggered on snapshot 3f97e661.** Consequence: the estimand is **not resolvable** on this snapshot; **the parent's M_decay
  claim must be explicitly demoted** (no decision about tumour-burden vs release may be read from it). This is an attainability result,
  **not** a null and **not** evidence against the tumour-burden claim.
- **Precise-null:** M0_res and M_decay both within +/-0.10 of 1 with tight CIs and a flat ALBI-slope control, on E. => no AFP-slope
  incremental value; reassign monitoring weight to imaging/other markers.
- Every branch is reported with the *also-computed* full-cohort **M0** HR, so a reader can attribute any change correctly.

## 8. Falsification and what each outcome would / would not establish
The design **can overturn** the parent (Adverse = R_selection arm) and, on the measured probe, **already demotes** its M_decay estimand
(Inconclusive). Supportive would establish only that the association survives release-purge AND selection control **in this single-centre
lexical endpoint**; it would **not** establish causation, mRECIST PFS, or clinical benefit. Inconclusive is not evidence against the claim.
A precise-null is not proof of no signal at unmeasured horizons.

## 9. Data-resolvability limits and required adjudication (honest)
- Progression is a **lexical** endpoint (`检查所见`/`检查诊断` descriptors); radiologist **mRECIST adjudication** is required for any
  clinical-truth claim. No mortality/PFS ⇒ no survival claim.
- Labs have **no units**; `定量结果` parsed as numeric. Single institution.
- The **decay-clean population is intrinsically tiny** on this snapshot (measured 0.37% IMGneg, gap-30), which is why R_selection cannot
  be separated from slope definition here without either a larger window/horizon, a periprocedural-window-free sampling design, or the
  deferred joint pulse model — this is a **missing-dependency statement**, not a redefined question.
- Causal interpretation of any surviving association requires expert review or another study.
