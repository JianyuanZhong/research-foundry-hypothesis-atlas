## Unresolved question and clinical importance (unchanged)
In a critically ill patient on empiric broad-spectrum antimicrobials, when culture/susceptibility
results return, is **early de-escalation** on average safe, **and** is any apparent safety a
confounding-by-indication artifact that hides harm in patients whose organ function is **not yet
improving**? Consequential, guideline-relevant: continuing broad coverage drives resistance/
toxicity, but premature narrowing in a still-deteriorating patient may delay adequate therapy.

### Power/precision implied at the observed N (formula stated)
Landmark cohort n≈42,834; assume ~50/50 exposure and p≈0.135 mortality. From
SE(RD)=sqrt(p1(1-p1)/n1 + p2(1-p2)/n2):
- **Overall RD**: SE ≈ 0.33 pp; 95% CI half-width ≈ **0.65 pp**; minimal detectable RD at
  80% power ≈ **0.9 pp**. The overall safety estimand is amply powered (RD≈0.02 gives z≈6).
- **Interaction Delta = RD(non-improving) - RD(improving)**, dichotomized trajectory
  (~21,417 per arm): SE(Delta) ≈ **0.66 pp**; minimal detectable |Delta| at 80% power ≈
  **1.9 pp**.

## Consequence for the falsification rules (the real advance)
The design's discriminating power is **asymmetric**:
- The **overall** arm can hear RD as small as ~0.9 pp → the parent's overall bands
  (supportive RD<=+0.02 with upper CI<=+0.05; adverse RD>=+0.03) are **detectable and
  falsifiable** at the observed N.
- The **interaction** arm cannot. A supportive rule requiring Delta<=+0.02 sits at the
  detection boundary, so a small-but-real interaction would be called "supportive" purely
  because it is underpowered. Therefore the child prespecifies:
  1. **Primary diagnostic** = interaction of exposure with the *continuous* pre-landmark
     partial-SOFA slope (not the dichotomized split), which recovers interaction power; the
     dichotomized Delta is secondary/reporting.
  2. **Supportive** = overall RD<=+0.02 AND upper CI<=+0.05 AND continuous-slope interaction
     CI excludes a harmful (+0.03) slope-scaled Delta AND negative-control RD<=+0.01.
  3. **Adverse** = overall RD>=+0.03 with lower CI>0, OR interaction indicating harm in
     non-improvers with lower CI>0.
  4. **Inconclusive** = everything else, now a *bounded* statement: at n≈42,834 the interaction
     arm cannot exclude |Delta|<0.02, so a near-null interaction is inconclusive, NOT supportive.

## Consequential secondary finding: the bottleneck is exposure measurement, not N
99.65% of stays have a prescription in-window, but the raw `prescriptions.drug` strings are
non-antimicrobial-dominated; a validated antimicrobial→spectrum dictionary is the binding
feasibility constraint for the spectrum-breadth exposure. The gate therefore reports ANY
prescription as a lower bound only; the antimicrobial-specific prevalence is **NOT COMPUTED**
and requires an external/validated spectrum mapping (e.g. ATC/WHO or an adjudicated list).
