# Assay-specific remote-history prediction after repeat TACE, with a locked two-assay concordance rule

## Targeted two-parent repair and clinical question

This child preserves the frozen repeat-TACE construction and the clinically relevant reverse nesting from both parents, but removes the leader's likely-infeasible joint-score primary. The unresolved question is:

> Among adults already selected for a recorded first repeat TACE and an observed complete albumin or total-bilirubin triplet, does the remote pre-TACE2 value `B` add reproducible prediction of the next observed same-assay value `Y` after the contemporaneous pre-repeat value `P` and a compact decision-time core are known?

This is a prognostic monitoring question. A clinician who already has `P` should not need `B` unless the older value contains incremental information. The primary analyses are therefore assay-specific, continuous, and parsimonious. A prespecified concordance rule governs whether the two assays may be summarized as a corroborated panel finding. The clinically evocative albumin-down/bilirubin-up pattern remains descriptive because its inherited binary fold audit was sparse; it is not allowed to become a classifier or an arm-interaction endpoint.

The strongest claim already supported is feasibility, not association: complete-source audits reconstruct 319 systemic-record and 1,491 comparator patients, 159 and 526 selected first repeat-TACE events, 40/118 albumin triplets, and 41/119 total-bilirubin triplets. A fresh full-source, outcome-blind fold audit (`[research job]`) scanned every required source row with the normative strict uncensored parser and confirmed those counts. Deterministic arm-balanced five-fold assignment gives held-out albumin support of 8 systemic patients in every fold and 24/24/24/23/23 comparator patients; bilirubin support is 9/8/8/8/8 and 24/24/24/24/23. No outcome direction, predictor-outcome association, or model was inspected in that audit.

The unresolved falsifiable claim is that adding `B` to the locked current-state model improves cross-fitted prediction of `Y`. Support would establish only patient-specific serial predictive information for a next observed laboratory value in this selected, observed cohort. It would not establish toxicity, hepatic failure, a treatment effect, clinical utility, or a reason to alter TACE or systemic treatment.

## Normative assay parser, duplicate rule, and B/P/Y selectors

Trim `quantitative result` and accept only a signed decimal or scientific number with an optional leading `<`, `>`, `≤`, or `≥`; commas, trailing text, and other coercions are rejected. Retain the inequality marker, but primary inference uses exact uncensored rows only. `specimen type` is not a unit. Report parser attrition, invalid clocks, censored rows, specimen types, and unmatched composite encounter keys.

For duplicate rows sharing patient, encounter, exact assay label, and `Test time`, preserve source order. If all parsed numeric values and censoring markers agree, retain the final source-order row; otherwise exclude the entire discordant group. The fresh strict audit found seven duplicate assay-timestamp groups and zero discordant groups, but execution must recompute these facts.

For each selected event and assay define exactly:

- `B`: latest valid uncensored result on TACE2 calendar days −30 through −1; TACE2 day 0 is excluded.
- `P`: latest valid uncensored result in `event_encounter` during `[event_time−72 hours,event_time)`; the event timestamp is excluded.
- `Y`: valid uncensored result in the same `event_encounter` during `(event_time,event_time+72 hours]` nearest to nominal `event_time+24 hours`; choose the earlier timestamp on an exact distance tie.

Retain all three timestamps. The assay-specific complete-triplet cohort requires `B`, `P`, and `Y` for that assay under these selectors. Albumin and bilirubin are analyzed on their own independently selected timestamps and patient sets; common timestamp is a sensitivity, not a primary restriction. Expected strict triplets are 40 systemic/118 comparator for albumin and 41/119 for total bilirubin.

No outcome transformation is performed. `Y` itself, not `Y−P`, a fold-specific rank, or a joint score, is the response in every fold. This fixes the endpoint across folds and avoids mathematical coupling of an outcome change with predictor `P`. Continuous predictors are standardized using training-fold means and standard deviations only; predictions and errors are returned to the assay's native numeric scale before computing performance. Because units/reference ranges are absent, effect sizes are interpreted only as within-assay standardized predictive performance, not clinical thresholds.

## One locked primary estimand per assay

Use the same assay-specific complete-triplet patients and the same folds in both nested models:

`M0: Y ~ P + arm + age + sex + TACE2_to_repeat_days + P_lead_hours`

`M1: Y ~ P + B + arm + age + sex + TACE2_to_repeat_days + P_lead_hours`

`P_lead_hours = (event_time−P_time)` and is positive. `B` occurs exactly once. Do not add `P−B`, slopes, recency, history counts, interactions, specimen type, broad covariates, missingness-as-signal from laboratory availability, post-event data, or outcome-informed variables. The inherited rich-history analysis and pseudo-current control remain negative feasibility findings and are not fit: prior direction-blind audits found only 18 patients per arm with two historical assay timestamps and only 2 systemic/4 comparator pseudo-current candidates per assay.

Encode age as numeric; within each outer training fold, median-impute age and include a fixed age-missing indicator. Encode sex with fixed `female` and `unknown/other/missing` indicators, using literal male as reference; do not create outcome-dependent categories. Remove a variance-zero indicator from both nested models within a training fold under one deterministic rule and report it. Standardize continuous predictors using training data only.

Fit ridge linear regression with an unpenalized intercept and penalties `{0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100}`. Select the penalty separately for each model and outer training fold by inner leave-one-patient-out mean squared error; on an exact tie choose the smaller penalty. All imputation, variance-zero removal, scaling, penalty selection, and fitting occur inside the outer training data. No alternate model may replace ridge after outcomes are inspected.

For assay `a`, the sole primary continuous estimand is

`Delta_a = [RMSE_a(M0) − RMSE_a(M1)] / SD_a(Y)`,

where both RMSEs use the paired pooled out-of-fold predictions from the frozen primary five-fold assignment, and `SD_a(Y)` is the sample standard deviation across the complete pooled evaluation set for that assay. The denominator is computed once for evaluation, not separately by fold or model. Higher `Delta_a` favors remote history. Report native RMSE and MAE for each model, `Delta_MAE_a = [MAE(M0)−MAE(M1)]/SD(Y)`, cross-fitted prediction variance, calibration intercept/slope, selected penalties, fold-level errors, and arm-stratified descriptive errors. There is no arm-specific or treatment-pathway interaction estimand.

## Outcome-blind arm-balanced folds

The primary assignment is deterministic and independent of all assay values. Separately within each assay and frozen pathway arm:

1. Compute `SHA256("hcc-repeat-tace-b-beyond-p-v1-r00" + "|" + assay_label + "|" + patient_key)`.
2. Sort ascending by the hexadecimal digest.
3. Assign zero-based sorted rank modulo five as the outer fold.

The fresh complete-source audit confirmed primary fold sizes:

- albumin systemic `8/8/8/8/8`, comparator `24/24/24/23/23`;
- total bilirubin systemic `9/8/8/8/8`, comparator `24/24/24/24/23`.

These are audit expectations and must be recomputed. Require at least five held-out patients from each arm in every fold, at least 20 total training patients per arm, and nonzero pooled `SD(Y)`. Do not require each held-out fold to contain a particular outcome direction or nonzero fold-specific variance, because the response is continuous and performance is pooled. Do not merge folds, reassign patients, restrict to common panels, or change the endpoint after values are seen.

As a prespecified partition sensitivity, repeat the complete nested pipeline for salts `r01` through `r19`, using the same within-arm hash-sort-round-robin construction. These repetitions are all reported and never used to choose the primary salt. A supportive primary result additionally requires at least 16 of 20 repetitions to have `Delta_a > 0` and the median repeated-partition `Delta_a` to be at least 0.05. A result whose sign changes in more than 4 repetitions is partition-sensitive/inconclusive.

## Uncertainty and multiplicity

The primary 95% interval for each `Delta_a` is a 2,000-replicate fixed-seed (`20260909`) stratified patient bootstrap of the frozen r00 out-of-fold tuples `(arm, Y, prediction_M0, prediction_M1)`. Sample with replacement separately within arm, preserve each patient's paired outcomes/predictions, recombine arms, and recompute RMSE, `SD(Y)`, and `Delta_a`. This interval is explicitly conditional on the fitted cross-validation pipeline; it does not pretend to include every source of model-selection or fold uncertainty. Report the estimable fraction and require at least 95% estimable replicates.

A 200-replicate full-pipeline stratified bootstrap is a mandatory uncertainty sensitivity: bootstrap patients within arm, append a deterministic replicate-instance suffix to duplicate patient keys, reconstruct five balanced folds with the r00 rule, rerun all training-only preprocessing and inner penalty selection, and recompute `Delta_a`. Report its percentile interval and estimable fraction. If its interval reverses the primary point-estimate sign, or fewer than 90% of full-pipeline replicates are estimable, the result is instability-inconclusive. This sensitivity cannot upgrade a nonsupportive primary result.

Albumin and total bilirubin are one prespecified two-assay family. Compute the prespecified one-sided bootstrap tail probability `p_a = (1 + number of estimable paired-bootstrap Delta_a values <= 0) / (1 + number of estimable replicates)` and apply Holm correction across the two assays. Report unadjusted and Holm-adjusted tail probabilities and unadjusted two-sided percentile 95% intervals; label these as bootstrap uncertainty measures rather than exact finite-sample tests. Assay-specific branches remain interpretable; a panel-level claim must satisfy the concordance rule below.

## Prespecified multivariate concordance rule

The joint rule combines conclusions, not native assay values and not patients. No joint score is fit.

- **Concordant supportive panel prediction:** both `Delta_albumin` and `Delta_bilirubin` are at least 0.05; both are positive in at least 16/20 partition repetitions; both Holm-adjusted one-sided p-values are below 0.05; both `Delta_MAE` values are nonnegative; neither calibration, linkage, influence, full-pipeline uncertainty, timing, or selection gate fails.
- **Concordant adverse degradation:** both assays have r00 95% intervals wholly below zero. This supports only that adding remote history degraded prediction under the locked models.
- **Concordant precise null for a design-sized increment:** both assays have r00 interval upper bounds below 0.05 after all computational gates pass. This rules out, with the stated uncertainty convention, a gain of at least 0.05 standardized RMSE in this selected cohort and model family.
- **Discordant or single-assay result:** report the two assay branches separately and prohibit a broad hepatic-panel statement. One supportive assay cannot compensate for degradation, imprecision, or failed gates in the other.

The margin 0.05 is a design convention for standardized prediction error, not a clinically important difference.

## Leakage, linkage, timing, influence, and selection checks

1. **Temporal and join assertions.** Assert `B` is on TACE2 days −30 to −1, `P_time < event_time`, `Y_time > event_time`, `P` and `Y` share `event_encounter`, no selected row is duplicated by an encounter join, and no preprocessing or model sees held-out values. Any violation stops inference.
2. **Patient-specific linkage destruction.** For each assay, perform 500 fixed permutations. Within each arm and outer fold, permute paired `(B,B_time)` among patients, preserving `P`, `Y`, covariates, arms, and folds; rerun M1 training and prediction with the identical pipeline. Support requires observed `Delta_a` to exceed the 95th percentile of the permutation distribution. If not, report no demonstrated patient-specific remote increment.
3. **Locked timing sensitivity.** Refit, without selecting among results, for latest pre 24/48/168 hours, first eligible pre within 72 hours, post nearest +48 hours, first eligible post, calendar days +1–3, concordant duplicates only, and common-stage timestamps. Report counts and identify identical selections so they do not masquerade as independent corroboration. A sign reversal in any variant retaining at least 75% of primary patients, or fewer than four empirically distinct estimable variants, makes a supportive claim timing-sensitive/inconclusive; it cannot make an adverse or null primary result supportive.
4. **Influence.** In an OLS transparency fit with the same columns, report leverage, Cook's distance, and DFBETA for `B`; report leave-one-patient-out changes in `Delta_a`. Flag Cook's distance `>4/n` or absolute DFBETA `>2/sqrt(n)`. If deleting all flagged patients reverses the primary sign, or any single deletion changes `Delta_a` by more than 0.05, classify as influence-sensitive/inconclusive.
5. **Observation selection.** The primary estimand is explicitly conditional on selected repeat TACE and an observed complete triplet; no weighting can recover absent outcomes. As a sensitivity among all 159/526 selected repeat-event patients, fit a five-fold cross-fitted logistic model for assay-triplet observation using only arm, age, sex, TACE2-to-repeat days, and repeat-event calendar year. Use stabilized inverse-observation weights truncated at the prespecified 1st/99th percentiles to recompute the nested OOF metrics among observed patients. Report predicted-probability range, standardized balance, weight maximum/concentration, and arm-specific ESS. The weighting gate fails if any predicted probability is below 0.05 or above 0.95, systemic ESS is below 30, comparator ESS is below 80, one patient carries over 10% of total weight, weighted `Delta_a` reverses sign, or `|weighted Delta_a−unweighted Delta_a| > 0.05`. Failure yields selection-sensitive/inconclusive rather than a favorable monitoring claim. It does not estimate a treatment effect or remove collider bias.
6. **Current-state benchmark.** M0 must always contain `P`. A B-only versus B+P comparison may be shown as ordinary persistence but cannot answer or replace the primary reverse-nested question.
7. **No unsupported negative control.** The pseudo-current check remains non-estimable at 2 systemic/4 comparator candidates per assay. No non-target assay is introduced without a separately documented, pre-outcome ontology and support audit. Absence of such a control blocks event-specific or mechanistic claims, not the generic predictive estimand.

## Deterministic branch precedence

Apply this order separately to each assay, then apply the two-assay concordance rule:

1. **Reconstruction-inconclusive:** source/snapshot mismatch; unexplained failure to reproduce 319/1,491 arms, 159/526 events, or 40/118 and 41/119 triplets; parser, duplicate, join, or clock violation.
2. **Computationally inconclusive:** held-out arm support below five, pooled outcome variance zero, failed training-only preprocessing/cross-fitting, fewer than 95% estimable primary bootstraps, fewer than 90% estimable full-pipeline bootstraps, or non-executable locked model.
3. **Selection-, timing-, partition-, or influence-sensitive/inconclusive:** any corresponding prespecified gate fails. These labels take precedence over a favorable point estimate.
4. **Adverse degradation:** all prior gates pass and the r00 95% interval for `Delta_a` lies wholly below zero, or calibration materially worsens under the locked check. State that remote `B` worsened prediction under this model, not that history is harmful.
5. **Supportive assay-specific evidence:** all prior gates pass; `Delta_a >= 0.05`; the unadjusted 95% interval excludes zero; the Holm-adjusted one-sided p-value is below 0.05; `Delta_MAE_a >= 0`; linkage destruction does not reproduce the gain; at least 16/20 partitions are positive with median at least 0.05; and timing, influence, full-pipeline, calibration, and selection checks are nonadverse. State only reproducible incremental prediction of the next observed same-assay value.
6. **Precise null:** all prior gates pass and the r00 95% interval upper bound is below 0.05. Report no detectable increment of the prespecified size in this selected cohort/model, not equivalence, no biology, or no possible clinical value.
7. **No demonstrated remote increment:** the point estimate is below 0.05 or linkage destruction is comparable, without a precise-null interval or adverse interval. Ordinary persistence by `P` may explain performance.
8. **Inconclusive:** the interval spans both meaningful degradation and `+0.05`, or any remaining prespecified ambiguity exists.

A binary-panel result never overrides these branches. A panel statement is permitted only by the locked concordance rule. No arm-specific or pathway-effect claim is permitted.

## Clinical evidence limits and verifier boundary

The available HCC data do not establish laboratory units/reference ranges, adjudicated hepatic decompensation or complications, Child–Pugh/MELD context, tumor burden or radiographic response, technical TACE success or intent, transfusion/albumin infusion/fluid balance, infection or bleeding, medication administration/adherence/dose, outside care, or mortality. Diagnoses are lexical encounter proxies without native timestamps. Conditioning on a recorded repeat procedure and complete observed assays can induce selection and collider bias. The next observed value is an observation-process endpoint.

A verifier can check source hashes, full scans, exact paths/columns/keys, cohort and event construction, parser and duplicate handling, B/P/Y clocks, fold digests and support, training-only preprocessing, nested ridge fits, paired OOF metrics, bootstrap and Holm arithmetic, permutation, timing, influence and selection gates, and branch precedence. It cannot establish clinical importance, toxicity, hepatic failure, causation, treatment benefit, administration, mechanism, utility, or policy. Stronger conclusions require unit-validated laboratories, expert-adjudicated decompensation/complication outcomes, verified exposures and procedure details, complete follow-up, and prospective/external calibration and decision-utility validation.
