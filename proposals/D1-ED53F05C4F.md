# Episode 87 successor: does repeat-lactate status require its own local calibration?

## Unresolved question, clinical importance, and substantive advance

A source-trained late-lactate risk score can be useful for ordering a fixed number of patients while its displayed absolute risks are wrong in the receiving hospital. The selected Episode 86 parent tested a concrete remedy: one target-hospital-held-out intercept shift for the entire target stratum. Episode 84 established why pooled calibration can hide opposing errors between people with and without an observed repeat lactate. The remaining clinically actionable question is whether a single local intercept is still too coarse: should a receiving hospital use separate, outcome-validated intercepts for the two observable repeat-status states, or does that extra complexity fail to improve transport?

This matters because a probability threshold can determine who is flagged for scarce review, and because a repeat result is not missing at random in routine care. A status-specific calibration remedy could repair absolute-risk displays without changing patient ordering; failure would argue for collecting more local labels, revising the model, or avoiding thresholded probability displays. The advance is substantive: it tests an explicit deployment remedy for measurement-process heterogeneity, nested within held-out hospitals, rather than adding another predictor or relabeling the same pooled calibration result.

The strongest external evidence inspected here is narrower. Baysan et al. (2022, DOI 10.1097/CCE.0000000000000750, PMCID PMC9444407; frozen full-text Europe PMC XML, source SHA-256 [source checksum]) reported a modest increase in C-statistic from 0.62 to 0.64 after adding 24-hour lactate to recalibrated APACHE IV in a measured sepsis cohort. Sisk et al. (2020, DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocaa242, PMCID PMC7810439; frozen full-text Europe PMC XML, [source checksum]) reviewed informative presence/observation methods. These findings motivate incremental lactate prognosis and observation-process features; they do not establish cross-hospital calibration, status-specific recalibration, fixed-capacity utility, or patient benefit. The research-ambition README was inspected: the natural-history and Bayesian demonstrations have local full articles, while the cancer demonstration's main article and full STAR Methods remain unavailable; only its supplement and metadata may be cited, and none is needed for this hypothesis.

The strongest EICU evidence before this experiment is feasibility, not support for the new claim: the inherited complete-source audit yielded 2,302 people in 30 hospitals, 1,163 valid repeats, 607 D7 events and 779 eventual deaths. The frozen low-repeat stratum has 15 hospitals/1,011 people/380 repeats/229 D7 events/782 non-events; the high-repeat stratum has 15/1,291/783/378/913. In target strata the F_available cells are feasible (low target: 631/122 and 380/107 people/D7 events; high target: 508/118 and 783/260). These counts establish that the proposed evaluation can be computed, not that status-specific offsets work.

## Primary hypothesis and estimand

For each directed transfer d in {low-to-high, high-to-low}, fit the inherited source-only R_full model and obtain target raw linear predictors eta_i and p_source,i=expit(eta_i). Define a fixed, outcome-blind threshold-side label z_i=1[p_source,i >= 0.10], including equality. This partition is fixed before either local calibration method is fit; it prevents each method from selecting a favorable evaluation subgroup.

There are two cross-fitted calibration remedies:

1. Pooled local intercept: for each held-out target hospital h, solve on all target people in other target hospitals
   sum over i not in h of (D7_i - expit(eta_i + delta_pool,h)) - delta_pool,h = 0.
2. Status-specific local intercept: for each held-out h and s in {0,1}, where s=F_available, solve using only people in other target hospitals with F_available=s
   sum over i not in h,F_available=s of (D7_i - expit(eta_i + delta_status,h,s)) - delta_status,h,s = 0.

Both roots use deterministic bisection on [-20,20], tolerance 1e-12, with the -delta penalty giving a unique finite root even under separation. A held-out hospital's D7 labels never enter its own intercept. The p_pool and p_status predictions are expit(eta+delta_pool,h) and expit(eta+delta_status,h,s). These are historical-label validation procedures, not unsupervised prospective deployment claims.

For method m in {pool,status}, direction d, status s, and fixed source-score side z, define the person-pooled error
e_m(d,s,z) = mean over target(d), F_available=s, z_i=z of (D7_i - p_m,i).
Define q_m(d)=max over s,z of absolute e_m(d,s,z), q_pool^*=max over d q_pool(d), q_status^*=max over d q_status(d), and the worst-direction improvement
I_* = min over d [q_pool(d)-q_status(d)].
The primary hypothesis is:

**H_status-cal:** status-specific held-out intercept recalibration reduces worst status-by-source-threshold-cell absolute calibration error by at least 0.02 and leaves the worst-direction error at or below 0.05:
lower95(I_*) >= 0.02 and upper95(q_status^*) <= 0.05.

The 0.02 improvement and 0.05 error limits are operational, prespecified decision screens, not validated safety, utility, or guideline thresholds. A source-score partition is used for paired calibration comparison; it is not a claim that the threshold itself is clinically validated.

Require on the original target data, for every d,s,z cell, n>=30, at least 10 D7 events and 10 non-events, all p values finite, and for each held-out hospital/status calibration fit at least 50 training people with at least 10 events and 10 non-events. If these predeclared gates fail, the calibration hypothesis is inconclusive/nonestimable, not rescued by pooling cells. Report all cells, including zero or sparse cells, rather than selecting a favorable subset.

Secondary audit: because each calibration adds a constant within target hospital/status, it is monotone in eta and must preserve within-hospital R_full ranks. Recompute the inherited equal-capacity queues at C={0.05,0.075,0.10,0.125,0.15,0.175,0.20}, with k_h(c)=ceil(c n_h), ascending patientunitstayid as the exact-score tie-break, and verify byte-identical membership and D7/Dinf counts between R_full, p_pool and p_status. This audit cannot establish clinical impact. Report the parent R_process-versus-R_full queue quantities descriptively, including D7/Dinf, additions/removals, overlap, M, rho, G, Psi and hospital deletions; it cannot rescue a failed calibration hypothesis.

## Uncertainty, robustness, and result classification

Run 2,000 copied-hospital refit bootstrap replicates, seed 20260973. Sample original hospitals with replacement independently within each frozen stratum, preserving low/high labels. Refit source preprocessing and R_full in each replicate. For every target-hospital holdout, fit pooled and status-specific offsets using only copies whose original hospital differs from the held-out original hospital; a copied version of the held-out hospital is excluded. Recompute eta, p_source, fixed z, p_pool, p_status, all e cells, q_pool, q_status and I_*. A replicate is finite only if every required source fit, score, root, cell and functional is finite and all original cell/training gates hold. Require >=1,900 finite joint replicates. Use two-sided percentile 95% intervals from the joint replicate records; never combine marginal intervals or select a favorable direction/cell.

Run the inherited fixed-design leave-one-original-hospital-out deletion with frozen source-hospital predictions and report q_pool, q_status, I_* and all queue invariance checks. Deletion instability cannot rescue a confidence bound. Report whether one hospital dominates event counts, calibration errors, offsets, threshold crossings or queue substitutions.

Ordered states:

1. Not computable: source/hash/header/schema/key mismatch; exact 2,302/30/1,163 or 607/779 audit mismatch; changed clocks, revision/conflict/tie rules, feature origins, folds, source-only preprocessing or fixed z; target-label leakage; nonfinite/nonconvergent source fit or offset root; failed original cell/training gates; wrong queue cardinality; fewer than 1,900 finite joint replicates; or missing required artifacts.
2. Adverse absolute calibration: computation is valid and lower95(q_status^*)>0.05. The status-specific remedy fails the absolute-risk screen; do not infer that lactate lacks prognosis or that no other recalibration can work.
3. Adverse insufficient improvement: computation is valid, lower95(q_status^*)<=0.05 but upper95(I_*)<0.02. Status-specific calibration is within the screen but does not materially improve on the simpler pooled local intercept.
4. Supportive status-specific remedy: computation is valid, lower95(I_*)>=0.02 and upper95(q_status^*)<=0.05, with all queue invariance/deletion audits passing. This supports only the cross-fitted EICU calibration pattern.
5. Inconclusive: every other computable pattern, including intervals crossing either margin, deletion instability without a contradictory confidence bound, or a sparse/nonestimable cell. If q_pool already meets 0.05, status-specific support still requires the improvement gate; it is not awarded for confirming a favorable parent.
