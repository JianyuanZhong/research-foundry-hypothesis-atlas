# Cross-fitted transport support and explicitly bounded outcome-observation interpretation for frozen HCC repeat-TACE validation

## Parent, goal, and material repair

This is a substantive child of `[prior hypothesis]`, preserving its frozen HCC repeat-TACE, P-beyond-B complete-triplet prediction schema. The parent correctly separated outcome-blind calendar support from post-event outcome observation and found that only the 2022 forward block is adequately supported; 2023 and 2024 cannot establish transport, with 2024 especially compromised by outcome observation (albumin 14/70, bilirubin 16/70 observed among outcome-blind BP-eligible events, and only 1/9 and 2/9 systemic outcomes).

The material remaining computational weakness is that the parent’s calendar-overlap propensity and transport ESS are fit and evaluated in-sample on the pooled training-plus-test BP-eligible rows. This does not leak the value of Y, but it lets the same rows define the support model and receive propensity predictions. With small systemic arms (for example, 8 training systemic episodes in the 2021-origin diagnostic and 22 in the 2022-origin diagnostic), the resulting range, outside-range fraction, and ESS can be optimistic and unstable. The parent also computes `age_missing` after age imputation, making that indicator identically zero in the diagnostic feature frame. The parent’s outcome-observation model is correctly training-only, but its inverse-observation ESS remains a diagnostic and must not be mistaken for an identification correction.

This child repairs transportability diagnostics by requiring strictly out-of-fold calendar-membership predictions and arm-stratified support summaries, while preserving the estimand, rows, clocks, outcomes, and model fits. It also makes the missingness-indicator order explicit and independently verifies the Y-window assertion. These are diagnostic repairs, not a new scientific schema or a post hoc exclusion rule.

## Scientific question and claims

The falsifiable question remains: among selected adult HCC repeat-TACE events with the prespecified complete triplet, does the contemporaneous pre-repeat assay P improve forward prediction of the subsequent same-encounter assay Y beyond remote baseline B and the locked decision-time core, and does that incremental prediction transport to later calendar mixtures with adequate pre-outcome support and outcome observation?

The strongest evidence currently supported is narrower: in the 2022 forward block, the parent’s locked complete-triplet analysis found favorable albumin and bilirubin P-beyond-B prediction, and the outcome-blind funnel had 29 BP-eligible events (10 systemic, 19 comparator) with 28 observed outcomes for each assay. This supports incremental short-horizon prediction in that selected 2022 mixture, subject to the repaired overlap diagnostics. It does not establish a treatment effect, causal effect, clinical outcome benefit, safety, response, utility, actionability, or transport to 2023/2024.

The unresolved claim tested by this child is whether calendar support remains adequate after honest out-of-fold support assessment, and whether apparent favorable complete-triplet estimates are interpretable when the Y-observation funnel is selective. A favorable RMSE result in a block with failed out-of-fold support or severe Y-observation loss is descriptive, not transport evidence.

## Falsification and interpretation

Supportive transport evidence requires a reconstruction pass, both outcome-blind and complete-triplet support gates, stable cross-fitted calendar support without material arm-specific warnings, no severe Y-observation warning, and a locked RMSE gain with uncertainty compatible with improvement. Even then, the claim is limited to incremental short-horizon prediction in the selected repeat-TACE population and the tested calendar mixture.

Adverse evidence includes a gated RMSE interval at or below zero, repeated M1 degradation in adequately supported blocks, cross-fitted support collapse, or outcome-observation collapse/large measured observed-versus-missing imbalance. This would challenge incremental prediction or its transport interpretation, not prove B alone is sufficient and not establish a mechanism.

Inconclusive evidence includes failed arm support, cross-fitted propensity non-overlap, low support ESS, influential episodes, low observation fraction, low observation ESS, denominator/clock drift, or absent assay units. The inherited results already make 2023 and 2024 transport inconclusive; the child prevents optimistic in-sample overlap summaries from weakening that conclusion. A favorable point estimate with one or two complete systemic outcomes remains descriptive only.

Computation can verify source hashes, full scans, schema columns, literal filters, joins, duplicate handling, clocks, non-overlapping blocks, outcome-blind eligibility, cross-fitted support calculations, observation funnels, nested fitting, predictions, metrics, bootstrap, drift, and influence. It cannot validate laboratory units/reference ranges, clinical severity, tumor burden, imaging response, TACE rationale, medication administration, transfusion/fluid exposure, outside-care capture, mortality, causal effects, clinical utility, or actionability. These require expert adjudication and independent temporal, external, or prospective data with validated assay metadata and clinically meaningful endpoints.
