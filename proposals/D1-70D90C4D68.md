# Absolute-capacity, accession-valid CT/MRI review allocation for documentary M2

## Episode-22 synthesis: decision, evidence, and unresolved claim

The decision is which adults should occupy a scarce multidisciplinary-review queue before a first eligible hepatocellular-carcinoma (HCC) liver resection. The target is pathology-documented high-grade microvascular invasion (documentary M2). The proposed action is review allocation only. It is not a recommendation for wider resection, transplantation, ablation, systemic or adjuvant treatment, or surveillance.

**Strongest claim supported before testing.** Direct inspection establishes that the HCC snapshot contains patient-linked encounters, procedure starts, examination acquisition starts and eventual CT/MRI narratives, and untimed same-encounter pathology narratives. Current full-text literature supports that MVI is pathology-defined, prognostically relevant, unavailable directly before surgery, and imperfectly predicted. Li et al. (2025; DOI `10.1111/jcmm.70746`, full XML frozen as `[source checksum]`) describes persistent prediction and management uncertainty. Huang et al. (2026; DOI `10.3389/fonc.2026.1821034`, full HTML `[source checksum]`) reports only fair binary-MVI discrimination (AUC 0.740/0.781) from a complete-case CT/AFP model with raw-image review and defined pathology sampling. Fan et al. (2026; DOI `10.21037/jgo-2025-aw-944`, full XML `[source checksum]`) reports M2 in 17.1% of 621 hepatectomy patients but weak standalone LDH discrimination and cautions against treatment use. None validates this source’s M2 text, routine-report semantics, access at the decision time, review capacity, or clinical benefit.

The source itself lacks report-authored, final, release, delivery or clinician-view times; report versions/hashes; pathology times, specimen IDs, slides or sampling details; observed review capacity/hours; treatment response; recurrence; survival; harms; cost; and patient benefit. Its laboratory clock has undocumented semantics and no collection/final/release/view fields or units, so laboratories have zero primary lineage.

**Unresolved stored-content hypothesis H1.** In the locked 2020–2021 service frame, at one absolute integer review capacity K held fixed across every admissible source state and bootstrap refit, an interpretable CT/MRI semantic strategy S will capture more documentary-M2 cases than both (i) its same-fitted semantic ablation S0 and (ii) an independently fitted structured/basic-report baseline B by more than 5 additional documentary-M2 cases per 100 reviews, with simultaneous uncertainty.

**Unresolved access-robustness hypothesis H2.** If H1 is supported under all stored eligible report bodies, there is a nonempty upward-closed set of minimum access guarantees for CT-only, MRI-only and dual-report patients under which the same two fixed-K margins remain supported even when an adversary chooses every permitted missing-report and unresolved-outcome state.

**Unidentified workflow claim H3.** HCC rows cannot establish that the exact body analyzed was final, released and viewed by `t_dec`, or that K fits a service. H3 is allowed only conditionally after independent, representative, exact-version report-system and capacity audits satisfy the frozen contracts and joint error rule below.

The substantive advance is not a more complex predictor. It tests whether routine stored report concepts improve allocation of an equal number of scarce reviews, while repairing percentage-based workload, pooled-access, report-grain and pathology-row errors. It preserves an auditable distinction between semantic attribution (S versus same-fit S0), whole-strategy value (S versus independent B), stored-content feasibility, decision-time transport, and clinical benefit.

## Frozen population, time, and coherent source states

The unit is one patient (`患者主索引`), one source-reconciled first eligible resection, and one locked service unit spanning 2020-01-01 through 2021-12-31. Build a pre-outcome ambiguity ledger before inspecting MVI grade, report concepts, scores or queue capture.

A liver-surgery panel, blinded to report bodies and MVI, freezes normalized exact dictionaries for partial, segmental, hemihepatic and liver-tumor resections and exclusions for transplant, biopsy/puncture, ablation-only, gallbladder-only, metastatic-organ surgery, and explicit repeat/recurrence procedures. Exact duplicate procedure rows are collapsed but source ordinals and counts remain auditable. Reconcile same-day procedure components and plausible duplicate representations of one operation rather than selecting the first row. Prefer a validated `手麻系统` clock when it is demonstrably linked to the event. A midnight/date-like `病案手术` value without validated time means interval `[date,date+24h)`, not midnight certainty. Competing plausible event links, time intervals and first-resection choices become certified alternatives.

For every certified patient state s:

- choose one coherent index operation/encounter and its time or interval;
- require age at least 18 from the unique `encounters` key;
- require explicit HCC support in the selected encounter’s complete pathology composite, while concealing the MVI grade during capacity construction;
- define `t_dec = operation start - 24 h`; use 12, 48 and 72 h only as frozen sensitivities;
- recompute the report accession(s), stored pattern, pathology composite, M2 status, recorded prior-treatment status, calendar period, eligibility and denominator together;
- exclude recorded prior HCC resection, transplant, TACE, ablation, radiotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy during `[t_dec-365 d,t_dec)` using state-matched valid clocks in procedures, medications and orders. This is recorded treatment absence, not biological treatment-naivety.

Let U be the fixed union of patients eligible in at least one certified 2020–2021 state. Any patient with any possible test membership is quarantined from development and tuning. Develop on 2015–2018, tune penalties and recalibrate only on 2019, and evaluate U once. Exclude 2013–2014. Use 2022 through snapshot end only for frozen-parser/template drift diagnostics. A state’s eligible set is `E_s subset U`, with `N_s=|E_s|`; nonmembers contribute neither numerator nor denominator and are never coded as M0. The state graph must preserve shared causes—operation choice determines encounter, `t_dec`, period, pathology composite, prior-treatment window and report eligibility. Independent marginal toggles that create impossible combinations are forbidden.

Every dictionary, reconciliation rule, source-state edge, temporal split, parser, feature, preprocessing step, model family, capacity rule, access grid, optimizer, resampling seed and interpretation rule is frozen before test M2 labels are exposed.

## Absolute service capacity

The preferred primary capacity is an **absolute integer K** supplied before any outcome review by an external service contract for the locked 2020–2021 calendar/service unit. The contract must name the service, eligible workflow, queue interval, K, reviewer roles, per-case time assumptions, total reviewer-hour bound, maximum tolerable queue and the five-per-100 materiality margin (or a different margin justified and frozen before outcomes). K cannot be inferred from HCC prevalence, scores, model performance or a state-specific denominator. It must satisfy `1 <= K <= min_s N_s`; otherwise fixed-workload comparison is infeasible and H1 is inconclusive.

If that contract is absent, first certify the source states and HCC eligibility while keeping MVI grades and report semantics hidden, then compute once

`N_min = min_s N_s`,
`K_j = floor(q_j N_min)`, for `q_j in {0.05,0.10,0.20}`.

Require every `K_j >= 1`. These are sensitivity capacities, not observed service capacities. Each resulting integer is frozen and held unchanged across source states, access masks, models, calendar diagnostics and all bootstrap refits. Never recompute `q_j N_s`, never round a percentage separately by state, and never use fractional slots. If any original or bootstrap state has `N_s < K`, that capacity has an **inconclusive feasibility failure**; do not shrink K or drop the state.

Preserve all component rows, all normalized `检查` and `机器型号` values, and the set `T_a` of distinct parsed `开始时间` values. An accession is never split because its component rows have different times.
3. Two abdominal radiologists blinded to outcome freeze a development-only map from the complete accession-level label/machine tuple to exactly one of CT, MRI, non-target or unresolved/conflicted. Multiple times, the 17 multiple-machine accessions, cross-modality labels or reuse suggesting distinct acquisitions require adjudication. If one report identity cannot be certified, keep the accession unresolved; do not duplicate it into CT and MRI. Require agreement at least 0.95 and at most 1% unresolved among candidate target accessions for a modality-pattern claim.
4. For a certified accession, a state may select one clinically defensible acquisition anchor from `T_a`; this changes eligibility/order only, never accession membership or body text. Blank-accession rows remain unresolved and cannot become singleton primary reports.
5. Within each operation state and modality, select at most the latest accession whose certified anchor is in `[t_dec-90 d,t_dec)`; tie-break by anchor, normalized accession, then a frozen hash of the accession tuple. The acquisition clock is only an eligibility proxy.
6. Concatenate every selected accession row’s `检查所见` then `检查诊断` in raw-ordinal order with explicit field and row delimiters. A visible bit exposes or masks the entire accession body. “Visible” in the computable experiment means eventual stored-body exposure, not real decision-time access.

For each state, define stored-pattern `g_i(s)`: O (no selected nonempty target accession), C (CT only), M (MRI only), or CM (one CT and one MRI). C and M each permit unavailable/exposed states; CM permits none, CT only, MRI only and both; O is fixed. Empty-body, non-target and unresolved accessions remain audited acquisition facts but cannot supply primary body features or satisfy access. All compared models see the identical state and CT-then-MRI bit order.

Report exact duplicates, blank IDs/times, all 595 multiple-time accessions, time spans/cutoff crossings, labels/machines per accession, modality disagreements, unresolved units, selected/discarded accessions, component counts, ties, empty bodies and yearly O/C/M/CM counts before test outcomes.

## Models, same-fit attribution and independent baseline

Fit interpretable elastic-net logistic models under patient-wise worst-admissible-state convex loss. Use the same robust rule in development, 2019 tuning/recalibration, semantic-null refits and every bootstrap. Equal-state averaging, complete-case outcome deletion, and conditioning on report availability are forbidden.

- **B, independently optimized baseline:** separately tune, fit and recalibrate age, sex, acquisition modality/count/recency, report-cardinality indicators and development-frequency machine category; when a body is exposed, it may also use frozen maximum lesion diameter, lesion-count category and explicit macrovascular tumor thrombus, each with unknown indicators. It may not use the semantic block below. Its penalty, coefficients and calibration are independent of S.
- **S:** use the same eligible feature universe as B plus a frozen report-concept block `C_R`: capsule complete/interrupted/absent, irregular/non-smooth margin, satellite/peritumoral lesion or enhancement, arterial hyperenhancement, washout and peritumoral hypointensity, each represented as present/absent/uncertain/not mentioned by modality.
- **S0, same-fit ablation:** never refit. Copy S’s intercept, coefficients, preprocessing, penalty and recalibration exactly, replacing only `C_R` with its frozen masked vector. Body-derived B features remain as S saw them. Require bitwise equality of S and S0 scores whenever no semantic accession is exposed.
- **Baselines:** top-K B and S0 are co-primary. Also report a frozen hash-random K queue and a prespecified no-feature age/sex/acquisition queue. Flag-all and flag-none are workload/capture sanity references only because they do not use K when `K<N_s`; they cannot support an equal-capacity claim. An outcome-informed oracle is a clearly labeled ceiling, never a comparator.

Laboratories, pathology, diagnosis text, procedure names, identifiers, calendar year and post-`t_dec` content cannot enter predictors. The entire labs table and all laboratory-derived missingness/process variables have zero primary lineage. Diagnoses may corroborate audit counts only. Prove lineage mechanically.

Two blinded liver radiologists plus a bilingual clinical-NLP reviewer freeze synonyms, negation, uncertainty and units and adjudicate report features by year, modality and stored pattern. Require size agreement within 5 mm or 10% in at least 90%, count agreement at least 90% with kappa at least 0.80, and PPV and sensitivity at least 0.85 for every binary concept in each claimed modality stratum. Failure makes semantic value inconclusive.

## Fixed-K estimands

For model M, coherent state s, shared report mask r and fixed K, let `Q_M(s,r,K)` be the K eligible patients with highest frozen scores. Ties use a salted cryptographic hash of `患者主索引`, fixed before outcomes; no pathology may break a tie. Require `|Q_M|=K` for S, S0, B and every K-queue baseline in every state and refit.

For an admissible outcome assignment y shared across contrasts, define

`Capture_M = sum_{i in Q_M} y_i`,

`D_K = 100 [Capture_S-Capture_S0]/K`,

`C_K = 100 [Capture_S-Capture_B]/K`.

Thus each contrast is **additional documentary-M2 captures per 100 reviews**; the materiality margin is exactly 5. Also report absolute capture difference, queue overlap, captured M2, precision `Capture/K`, recall against all documentary M2 in `E_s`, and false-review count. Unknown outcomes are varied coherently over `{0,1}`; the same patient’s value cannot differ between queues or contrasts within a state.

The primary stored-content endpoint exposes every selected nonempty accession in each state. For each K and contrast compute the exact lower and upper extrema over certified source states and unknown outcomes, then simultaneous confidence limits. H1 requires the simultaneous one-sided lower limits of both D_K and C_K to exceed 5. The random/no-feature contrasts and discrimination/calibration metrics are secondary diagnostics; favorable AUROC cannot rescue queue failure.

## Modality-pattern access frontier

For `g in {C,M,CM}`, define `N_g(s)` within each eligible state and a frozen grid `h_g in {0,.05,...,1}`. The access constraint is at least `ceil(h_g N_g(s))` exposed patients in that pattern. O has no coordinate and remains a fixed unavailable contribution. In CM, “exposed” means CT only, MRI only or both; the adversary may select whichever yields the worst valid queue contrast. This explicitly protects against a favorable pooled coverage that hides dependence on one modality.

At each `h=(h_C,h_M,h_CM)`, minimize and maximize D_K and C_K jointly over:

- the certified, source-coupled operation/eligibility/report/pathology states;
- all accession masks satisfying the three minimum guarantees;
- all CM none/CT/MRI/both states;
- all admissible unknown-outcome assignments;
- exact top-K ranking under the shared mask.

Use a state-expanded mixed-integer program with deterministic tie constraints (or an independently proved equivalent exact algorithm). Verify every endpoint against exhaustive enumeration for fixtures with union N at most 12, including O, C, M, all four CM states, variable `N_s`, unknown Y and `N_s<K`. A relaxation or heuristic cannot support H2.

Let `H_K` contain grid points where simultaneous lower limits for both D_K and C_K exceed 5. Because each point protects against every mask satisfying “at least” its guarantees, `H_K` must be coordinatewise upward closed; machine-check this and report the Pareto-minimal antichain. A violation is an implementation failure. `N_g=0` makes that coordinate unavailable, not grounds to delete patients. A modality-specific interpretation requires `N_g>=100` and at least 20 adjudicated M2 events; otherwise H2 is inconclusive for that stratum. Report a pooled any-report frontier only as a secondary comparison.

## Exact result categories and permitted conclusions

Classify D_K and C_K separately, then the joint claim.

- **Supportive stored-content:** every validity gate passes and simultaneous lower limits for both D_K and C_K are strictly greater than 5 at the external K (or, without a contract, at a named fixed sensitivity K). Conclude only that eventual routine CT/MRI report concepts improved historical equal-capacity allocation for documentary M2 relative to same-fit ablation and independent B. A sensitivity result is not a service-capacity claim.
- **Supportive access robustness:** stored-content support holds and `H_K` is nonempty. Conclude only that its reported CT-only/MRI-only/dual minimum-access antichain suffices under the registered adversarial states. It does not show those guarantees occurred.
- **Adverse:** with all validity gates passed, both simultaneous upper limits are at or below 5, ruling out the registered material gain for both attribution and complete strategy. Report a contrast as harmful if its upper limit is at or below 0. If H1 supports but `H_K` is empty, report an adverse access-robustness result: no tested guarantee short of unavailable stronger assumptions preserves the margins.
- **Mixed:** one contrast has lower limit above 5 while the other has upper limit at or below 5; D supports but C does not means semantic action changes do not beat optimized B, while C supports but D does not means refitting/shared features rather than runtime semantic contribution may explain value. Also label results mixed when valid fixed K values, temporal buffers or modality patterns produce opposing supportive/adverse categories. Do not recommend S.
- **Inconclusive:** either primary interval crosses 5 without the joint supportive/adverse pattern; any gate fails; source states are incomplete; semantic/pathology validation fails; events are sparse; more than 2.5% refits fail; the optimizer/replay fails; `K<1`; or any original/bootstrap state has `N_s<K`. Missing capacity evidence makes service feasibility inconclusive but does not erase a clearly labeled sensitivity computation. Missing report audit makes decision-time transport inconclusive regardless of stored-content support. Positive AUC, complete-case results or eventual text treated as visible cannot rescue an inconclusive result.

No category licenses treatment claims.

## External capacity and exact-version decision-time transport

Capacity/workload language requires the external pre-outcome service contract that supplied K, plus evidence that the named reviewers can deliver K reviews within its hour and turnaround bounds. HCC has none of these fields.

Decision-time transport requires a representative source-system audit from the same calendar, service and workflow, using the identical union population, source-state rules, `t_dec`, accession key, all-row bundle, modality map, latest-accession rule and O/C/M/CM patterns. For every selected accession it must retrieve:

- immutable report/accession/version identifier or content hash matching the exact body analyzed;
- authored/finalized time and status;
- release/delivery time;
- clinician-specific view time or auditable delivery-to-the-review-team event.

Acquisition time, order time, a later final report, unmatched text, or “a report existed” is insufficient. Without a view/delivery event, only release availability—not decision-time clinical access—can be described.

Estimate, with patient clustering and calendar/workflow stratification, the fraction whose exact analyzed version was final, released and viewed/delivered by `t_dec` separately for C and M; within CM report none, CT-only, MRI-only and both, plus the conservative any-visible fraction used by `h_CM`. Missing logs count as not demonstrated. Construct simultaneous one-sided 97.5% audit lower bounds and round them down to the 0.05 grid. A conditional H3 statement is allowed only when the resulting `(h_C,h_M,h_CM)` lies in `H_K`, the exact-version and capacity contracts pass, and populations/units match.

Use a 97.5% simultaneous model family and a separate 97.5% simultaneous audit family, giving joint noncoverage at most 5% by Bonferroni; a preregistered joint resampling method may replace it. The permitted conclusion is conditional historical workflow transport for this service and capacity—not deployment effectiveness, clinician action, treatment utility or patient benefit.
