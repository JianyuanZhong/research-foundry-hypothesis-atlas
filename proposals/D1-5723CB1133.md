Therefore the untested claim is limited to a prespecified recorded-interval association, not a treatment effect or airway benefit.

## Population and V0 construction

Use the earliest adult ICU stay ordered by (intime,stay_id), with the parent’s age, admission, stay, pressor, MAP, weight, urine, potassium, balance, ECMO, RRT and pre-t0 loop-course gates. A valid interval requires nonmissing parseable starttime/endtime, `starttime<endtime), finite duration, and `starttime<=t0<endtime). Accept only statusdescription `FinishedRunning` or `Stopped`. Exclude `Paused`, missing/unknown statuses, and any invalid or order-only/cancelled representation; never infer acceptance from value, originalamount, isopenbag, orderid or linkorderid. The observed source partition contained FinishedRunning, Stopped and Paused; absence of a literal cancellation status is not evidence that cancellation equals clinical nonventilation.

Define:
- `V0_unique`: exactly one valid accepted item-225792 row crossing t0.
- `V0_ambiguous`: two or more valid accepted rows crossing t0, regardless of whether their starts or ends match.
- `V0_absent_or_rejected`: no valid accepted crossing, including Paused-only crossing, invalid timing, missing status or key mismatch.

Only V0_unique enters the primary E_R. Do not select the earliest, latest, longest, largest-value, FinishedRunning-preferred, or max-endtime row among multiple crossings. A subject with V0_ambiguous is excluded with a named reason, not assigned a boundary by undocumented semantics. This is a conservative validity repair and may reduce feasibility.

For V0_unique, define `C0=min(t0+12 hours,V0.endtime)`. Treat V0.endtime as the endpoint of the uniquely selected recorded procedure interval, not as extubation, tube removal, airway independence or a clinician-known future state. The parent’s half-open convention remains: treatment starts are eligible strictly in `(t0,C0)); a start exactly at C0 is primary ambiguous; an event after C0 or after later ventilation re-entry does not reopen the index window.

## Baselines, falsification and verification

The primary falsification comparator is the inherited Episode-61 X-anchored boundary `C=min(X,V.endtime)`, holding all other clauses fixed. Material reversal is a branch change, airway bound movement >=0.05, or midpoint sign reversal with both absolute midpoints >=0.02. Additional inherited balance, pressor, treatment-form, recording-lag, weight, endpoint, observation and denominator baselines remain.

The verifier must check:
- exact three-key same-stay linkage;
- unique V0 selection and exclusion of every multiple-crossing accepted stay;
- Paused-only and unknown-status crossing never becomes V0;
- V0 storetime>t0 cannot enter E_T/M/G, while it may enter explicitly retrospective E_R;
- future storetime never moves starttime, C0, loop onset, X, or a clinical event;
- an accepted V0 ending without X is unknown airway follow-up, not success;
- post-V0 loop before X is B/closed and does not reopen after re-entry;
- X cannot change eligibility, arm, clone entry, denominator history or weights;
- exact C0 loop is ambiguous;
- unknown outcomes are not negatives and W48 is not continuous observation;
- numerical support paired with causal benefit, actual administration, renal safety, durable extubation, equivalence or clinical recommendation fails verification.

Branches:
- Supportive-recorded: all computation/minima/validity gates pass, airway lower bound >+0.05, no Holm adverse family, harm margins pass, no material C0/X reversal, and feasible recording-time diagnostics do not invalidate interpretation.
- Adverse-FWER: validity passes and a prespecified adverse family is Holm-rejected; this is an observational record concern.
- Margin-falsified: validity passes, no adjusted adverse family, and primary airway upper bound <=+0.05; this rejects the recorded-proxy margin only.
- Endpoint-boundary-inconclusive: V0 cannot be uniquely constructed or C0/X materially reverses.
- Temporal/measurement-validity-inconclusive: Jaccard, E_M retention, observation, ambiguity, contamination, positivity, balance, calibration or endpoint gates fail.
- Recording-time-inferential-infeasible: E_M or E_G fails minima, especially because V0 rows are recorded after t0; E_R remains retrospective only.
- Statistically inconclusive: valid computation meets neither directional branch.
- Primary infeasible: source, schema, state-machine, population, endpoint, bootstrap or E_R minimum failure.

Computationally checkable claims are source hashes/headers, dictionary labels, key joins, V0 uniqueness/status/storetime classes, C0 and loop ordering, cohort membership, clone transitions, bounded endpoint states, weights, bootstrap/Holm accounting and final prose-to-output consistency. Actual ventilation, extubation, tube removal, airway readiness, clinician awareness, medication receipt, perfusion, congestion, urine accuracy, KDIGO AKI, exchangeability, causality, transportability and actionability require expert adjudication, richer notes/airway evidence, external validation and another study. The proposal remains observational and makes no bedside recommendation.
