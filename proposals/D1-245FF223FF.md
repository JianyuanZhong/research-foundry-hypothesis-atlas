# Exact local-evidence and whole-patient deletion robustness of documentary-M2 capture

## 1. Targeted successor and clinical decision

This child evolves assessed-valid `[prior hypothesis]`. It preserves that parent's first-eligible HCC-resection population, operation-clock states and lookbacks, chronology quarantine, canonical radiology `V/A/E` lineage, whole-unit context quarantine, two-independent-plus-adjudicated radiology and pathology forms, indivisible whole pathology forms, `B/R_clean/G_clean/Gclean_mask` models, repaired regularization grid, fixed capacity, strict >5-per-100 margin, separate 2020 and 2021 tests, source bindings, and evidence limits.

The parent can establish an attained worst-case margin over a frozen finite corpus. It cannot say whether that margin is carried by one unusually influential patient, and its statement that there is no confidence coverage leaves a practical gap: an all-world lower endpoint just above 5 may be mathematically robust to source and text interpretation yet too composition-fragile to justify the next costly prospective study. Conversely, attaching an ordinary bootstrap interval to a minimax, top-K, reader-dependent-membership estimand would silently add exchangeability, smoothness, and resampling-frame assumptions not supplied by this snapshot.

This successor therefore adds an exact, finite-corpus whole-patient deletion breakdown radius. It asks whether every co-primary margin remains above 5 after removing any one test patient, while allowing the remaining patients to take every admissible source and item-local whole-form completion. This is a stronger and still honest gate for deciding whether to obtain report release/view logs and run a prospective silent-mode capacity study. It is not a confidence interval or evidence of transportability, treatment benefit, or deployment readiness.

### Unresolved falsifiable hypothesis

> Among adults in the frozen first-eligible source-documented HCC-resection frame, the locked `G_clean` score using source-byte-traceable, context-quarantined meanings from eventual stored whole-accession CT/MRI text improves encounter-documentary M2 capture by strictly more than 5 per 100 fixed review positions over both independently fitted `R_clean` and same-fit `Gclean_mask`, in every admissible source and item-local whole-form evidence completion, separately in 2020 and 2021; and each of these four lower-margin claims remains above 5 after deletion of any one whole test patient.

The first conjunct is the parent's finite-corpus partial-identification claim. The new conjunct tests single-patient composition robustness. It is falsified by one exact patient-deletion/source/form witness at or below 5 even if the undeleted parent endpoint is favorable.

The advance is substantive: it separates three axes that the parent left adjacent but unresolved.

1. Item-local evidence ambiguity is handled by attained extrema, with no probabilities.
2. Whole-patient composition fragility is handled by an exact deletion radius, with no resampling distribution.
3. Sampling-population uncertainty remains unidentified because the snapshot supplies neither a probability sample nor a target-site sampling frame.

A corpus-wide “reader snapshot” is not introduced. The forms establish defensible alternatives for each item, not a latent reader identity that must govern every item. The Cartesian completion is therefore described as a conservative evidence-completion envelope, never as an observed global reader or a distribution of unseen readers.

For patient `i`, compile one finite coherent domain `D_i`. ## 6. New exact whole-patient deletion radius

Let `U_T` be the frozen test patient superset. For deletion set `D subseteq U_T`, let `omega\D` remove each patient in D from all of that patient's states and both arms while leaving the locked models and K unchanged. Only deletion sets for which at least K eligible patients remain in year a under the selected completion are feasible.

For each coordinate define

`q_e^*=min{|D| : D subseteq U_T, omega in Omega_local, N_a(omega\D)>=K, Delta_e(omega\D)<=5}`.

Thus:

- `q_e^*=0` exactly when the undeleted universal margin already fails;
- `q_e^*=1` gives an attained one-patient witness that breaks the margin;
- `q_e^*>1` proves every single whole-patient deletion retains the strict margin over all remaining source/form completions;
- equality to 5 is failure;
- if no adverse deletion is feasible, report `+infinity` with a certificate rather than a capped search.

Also report the identity-protected witness hash, whether the deleted patient occupied either arm's original or replacement top K, the two before/after top-K hashes, outcomes, score ranks, local form/source backpointers, and the exact contrast arithmetic. Do not call `q_e^*/|U_T|` a probability.

The confirmatory successor requires both `delta_e^{0,-}>5` and `q_e^*>1` for all four coordinates. The full radius is reported because a radius of 2 and 20 have different practical meanings, but the predeclared gate only asks whether one patient can reverse the conclusion; it does not invent an arbitrary population-confidence level.

## 7. Exact computation without a global-reader assumption

### Robust fitting

At a trial coefficient vector and Dinkelbach parameter, each patient domain supplies a finite set of coherent numerator/denominator contributions. Maximizing the transformed normalized loss separates by patient:
`max_omega sum_i [L_i(beta,d_i)-eta H_i(d_i)] = sum_i max_{d_i in D_i}[L_i(beta,d_i)-eta H_i(d_i)]`.
Use the inherited convex cutting-plane outer fit and exact patient-domain separation, with all tied maximizers retained. Repeated accession/composite consistency is enforced inside `D_i`, not by a global reader variable. Tuning repeats this exact separation on 2019. No item, state, form, or tied maximizer may be sampled or capped.

### Top-K and deletion extrema

After fitting, every patient-state alternative has fixed eligibility, Y, and a score for each arm. Solve the undeleted endpoints and `q_e^*` as state-expanded mixed-integer programs. Variables choose exactly one coherent local state for each retained patient, one deletion bit shared by both arms, and exactly K selected eligible states per arm. A selected lower-ranked state while a retained eligible higher-ranked state is unselected is forbidden. Generate these rank-inversion constraints lazily from the precomputed exact total order `(linear predictor, SHA-256 tie key, raw episode backpointer)`; continue until no violated inversion exists. The objective for `q_e^*` minimizes the integer sum of deletion bits subject to `Delta_e<=5`.

Require zero unresolved integer gap, numerical feasibility gap at most `1e-8`, independent raw-state replay, quotient replay, state/order invariance, complete source/form/deletion backpointers, exhaustive equality on synthetic fixtures through 12 patients and real-data shards through 20, and a second implementation that enumerates all zero/one-deletion cases for the required `q_e^*>1` gate. Any disagreement, timeout, omitted equality state, or unresolved bound is inconclusive. A favorable incumbent cannot support the hypothesis; an adverse incumbent cannot falsify it without an attained feasible witness.

This formulation is finite and exact conditional on the frozen dictionaries, forms, and numeric tolerances. It does not make the finite experiment statistically representative.

## 8. Falsification and interpretation

After all source, annotation, event, fit, and solver gates pass:

1. `stable-universal-supportive`: all four undeleted lower endpoints exceed 5 and all four deletion radii exceed 1.
2. `finite-corpus-supportive-but-single-patient-fragile`: all four undeleted lower endpoints exceed 5, but at least one radius equals 1. This supports the parent finite-corpus claim but falsifies the successor and weakens the case for prospective escalation.
3. `decisive-heterogeneity`: at least one undeleted world/coordinate exceeds 5 and at least one fails. Fixed-source radiology-only and pathology-only witnesses are retained from the parent and identify sensitivity, not truth.
4. `universal-adverse-without-positive-coordinate`: every undeleted upper endpoint is at most 5.
5. `inconclusive`: any mandatory source, roster, form, event, fit, state-completeness, replay, or exact-solver gate fails; this label has precedence.

A stable supportive result permits only: in this frozen eventual-report corpus, the fixed-K documentary-M2 margin exceeded 5 under every declared item-local source/form completion in both years and was not reversed by removal of any one whole test patient. It does not provide a p value, confidence interval, sampling-population effect, unseen-reader guarantee, or external validity.

A fragile result means the all-world finite-corpus claim depends on retaining every patient in at least one coordinate. It does not prove the deleted patient is erroneous or that semantics have no value. Heterogeneity falsifies universal robustness; universal adverse falsifies the strict margin; neither rules out smaller documentary gains. Inconclusive is not adverse evidence.

## 11. Evidence needed for stronger conclusions

A sampling-population confidence statement requires a defined target population, consecutive ascertainment or known inclusion probabilities, site/calendar coverage, and a prespecified inferential model validated for this nonsmooth robust top-K estimand. A new locked temporal or external cohort is preferable to pretending the deletion radius is confidence coverage. Unseen-reader robustness requires another locked multi-reader study. Biological M2 requires pathology time and specimen/accession/slide/block links plus documented sampling-site/extent review under a recognized protocol. Real-time utility requires accession-linked report version/final/release/view logs and validated operation clocks. Capacity acceptability requires a prospective candidate stream, staffing, review duration, queueing, and stakeholder judgment. Treatment benefit requires a separate controlled outcomes study.
