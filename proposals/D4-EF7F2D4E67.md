## Hypothesis to test

Within the same cancer type, people with poorer muscle strength and cardiac or kidney function before diagnosis experience more infections and heart-failure events after diagnosis.

## Suggested approach

Start follow-up at cancer diagnosis and analyze subsequent infection, heart failure, and death.

## Scientific significance
