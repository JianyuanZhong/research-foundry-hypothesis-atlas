# Prevalence-aware prospective validation of an EHR restart endpoint against intent-qualified pump rescue

## Decision question, frozen evidence, and unresolved hypothesis

The prerequisite clinical decision remains whether a binary EHR-derived “restart of the first-stopped vasopressor within six hours” is sufficiently accurate, complete, and interface-transportable to be an endpoint in a later discontinuation-order trial. This study does **not** ask which vasopressor should be stopped first.

All inherited negative MIMIC findings are frozen. Among 6,040 MAP-eligible repeated landmarks, assigned actions were rare: 211 norepinephrine-first (NE-first; 3.49%) and 167 vasopressin-first (VP-first; 2.76%), both below the 5% gate; 5,638 no-action landmarks are not controls. The observed-stop cohort has 84 NE-first and 53 VP-first episodes, with 49 and 24 determinate composite failures. The arms are neither randomized nor exchangeable and are too small for a direct causal comparison. Of 73 failures among 136 determinate episodes, 59 include a charted stopped-agent restart and 36 are restart-only. MCS status is unknown in all 137 episodes. Compact temporal models had negligible Brier improvement (about 0.000174 for M1 over M0 and 0.000111 for M2 over M1, below the 0.01 gate) and adverse later transport; no score or subgroup advances.

The no-sampling transaction audit of all 137 inherited episodes recovered all boundaries and 59 raw restarts. Five symmetric reconstruction rules invalidated 0, 1, 13, 16, or 23 stop indices; the inherited chain-plus-dose rule invalidated 13/137 stop indices and 13/59 restart indices (10 NE-first, 3 VP-first), all at `Paused` boundaries. These are frozen findings of transaction-label instability only. Status, chain, dose, caregiver, `storetime`, and intermittent MAP do not establish pump delivery, a genuine stop, rescue intent, or a false-positive rate.

The unresolved prospective hypothesis is:

> In both observed first-stop strata, a locked episode-level EHR restart test has PPV and sensitivity greater than 0.80 for intent-qualified pump-native rescue, and each NE-first minus VP-first metric difference is strictly within ±0.10, under full consecutive verification and conservative indeterminate bounds.

The strongest possible supportive result validates only this restart component at the studied interfaces. It cannot establish that rescue causes harm, that stopping order causes rescue, that either order is preferable, or that a later randomized strategy is safe or beneficial.

## Full-consecutive population and repeated episodes

Recruit at least six adult ICUs with synchronized smart-pump, monitor, and EHR exports. Before validation, each site completes a fresh 50-episode technical run-in under the already locked thresholds. At least 95% of run-in episodes must have unique patient-channel-drug linkage, measured clock mappings, and complete pump export from two hours before index through the observable six-hour window; at least 90% must have a contemporaneous intent source. Infrastructure may be repaired, but labels, thresholds, windows, and estimands may not be changed. A failed site repeats the run-in with 50 new episodes; a second failure makes it feasibility-only. Run-in episodes are excluded.

The validation cohort contains **every consecutive qualifying cessation**, regardless of the later EHR or reference result. Qualification uses only information available at the cessation:

1. age at least 18 years, alive in an adult ICU;
2. pump-supported concurrent NE and VP delivery for at least six continuous hours immediately before cessation;
3. at the beginning of the final pre-index 30 minutes, NE >0 and ≤0.20 mcg/kg/min and VP >0 and ≤0.04 U/min, with neither increased during that interval;
4. no pump-delivered epinephrine, phenylephrine, or dopamine in the prior two hours;
5. at least three valid MAP observations spanning at least three of the four prior 30-minute bins, including the final bin, with median MAP ≥65 and minimum MAP ≥55 mmHg; and
6. a non-simultaneous first cessation of one study drug while the comparator continues pump-native delivery.

Both drugs crossing below threshold within five minutes is a simultaneous stop and is excluded and counted. Known procedure/transport pauses are excluded and counted. Actual MCS device type, active/standby state, and support are captured; absence is never inferred from missing documentation.

Unlike the parent, the primary analysis does not discard later qualifying episodes from the same person. All qualifying episodes are retained because a future trial endpoint may recur and because first-person-only sampling can distort interface and prevalence estimates. A new episode requires both drugs again to meet the full six-hour concurrent-delivery eligibility and must begin at least 24 hours after the preceding `t0`. Candidate cessations within 24 hours or without a new six-hour baseline belong to the existing episode and are counted as within-window or post-window recurrent transitions, not new index episodes. Person identity and episode sequence are retained. Confidence intervals and models use patient-cluster bootstrap or cluster-robust methods; a first-episode-per-person analysis is a prespecified sensitivity analysis. No person or episode is outcome-dependently selected.

## Independent clocks, index test, and reference standard

### Pump-native index and observable window

`t0` is the first pump timestamp at which the first-stopped drug falls below 0.005 mcg/kg/min for NE or 0.005 U/min for VP and remains below threshold for at least five continuous minutes while the comparator remains above threshold. Drug-library identity, concentration, patient, channel, weight, and delivered-rate stream must be linkable; a programmed rate alone is insufficient.

The target window is `(t0, t0+6 hours]`. Death or ICU exit before six hours is a determinate closure **only if** pump and EHR capture are complete through the closure; no subsequent in-ICU rescue is then possible. A technical/export/linkage gap before the minimum of six hours, death, or ICU exit is indeterminate, not negative. Clock mappings are estimated before validation and represented as source-specific affine mapping plus observed residual error, time zone, and daylight-saving handling.

### Locked EHR index test

The EHR algorithm is run and frozen before reference adjudication. It uses the local fields mapped to the MIMIC-compatible specification:

* NE item 221906: retain mcg/kg/min; divide mcg/min by contemporaneous valid weight 30–300 kg; reject normalized rate >5 mcg/kg/min.
* VP item 222315: divide units/hour by 60; retain units/min; reject normalized rate >0.2 U/min.

`E=1` when a new positive stopped-drug interval has EHR `starttime` in `(t0,t0+6 hours]` after applying the prospectively frozen pump-to-EHR clock map, with `endtime > starttime`. The mapped timestamp is used for both window classification and matching; an event is never moved by an analyst after labels are seen. A row already spanning `t0` is not a new interval. Thus pump rescue hidden within a spanning EHR row is `E=0` and may be a false negative. Chain, status, storetime, caregiver, open-bag, and transfer fields do not repair the primary test.

Predefine a clock-boundary uncertainty band equal to the site/source run-in 99th percentile absolute residual, capped at 60 seconds. An otherwise qualifying EHR start whose mapped 95% timestamp uncertainty set crosses `t0` or `t0+6 hours` is index-indeterminate (`E=?`) for conservative primary bounds; deterministic mapped-time classification and exclusion of these cases are sensitivity analyses. A site with a required band above 60 seconds fails clock qualification.

### Intent-qualified pump-native rescue reference

`R=1` requires both:

1. after the qualifying off interval, linkable delivered pump rate for the stopped drug again reaches at least 0.005 mcg/kg/min (NE) or 0.005 U/min (VP) continuously for at least five minutes within the target window; and
2. two independent critical-care/pharmacy adjudicators determine from timestamped contemporaneous evidence that resumption deliberately treated or sought to prevent worsening hypotension or impaired perfusion after cessation.

Bag/syringe replacement, pump/channel exchange, transfer continuation, reconciliation, procedural pause, correction of a mistaken stop, and uninterrupted delivery segmented only in documentation are reference-negative for intent-qualified rescue. The pump algorithm runs before adjudication. Adjudicators see pump/monitor traces, workflow events, cointerventions, and contemporaneous intent evidence but not `E`, EHR transaction diagnostics, aggregate accuracy, or each other's label. They classify rescue-positive, pump resumption without rescue intent, workflow/technical transition, no pump resumption, clock/export failure, or intent-indeterminate. A third blinded adjudicator resolves disagreement. Raw agreement, Gwet AC1, and kappa are reported. After the first 100 validation episodes, raw agreement <85% or both chance-corrected measures <0.70 triggers manual clarification without opening accuracy and a restart on new episodes; old episodes remain feasibility data. A second failure makes the study inconclusive.

A secondary pump-only reference removes intent. It diagnoses technical EHR-to-pump validity but cannot substitute for the clinical target.

## Event matching without altering episode labels

Episode labels are primary. Secondary event analysis enumerates every pump resumption crossing the locked threshold after a qualifying five-minute off spell and every new positive EHR interval in the six-hour window. Within each episode and drug, sort both lists by mapped time and perform one-to-one maximum-cardinality, minimum-total-absolute-lag bipartite matching under a locked ±10-minute caliper; ties are resolved by earlier pump time, then earlier EHR time, then immutable source-row identifier. No event can match twice, and matches cannot cross patient, episode, drug, `t0`, death/exit, or the six-hour boundary. Report unmatched pump events, unmatched EHR events, duplicates, signed lag, median and 95th-percentile absolute lag. Event matching cannot create, remove, or repair episode-level `E` or `R`.

## Estimand hierarchy and indeterminate bounds

For arm `a`, the six confirmatory components are:

* `PPV_a = P(R=1 | E=1, arm=a)` for NE-first and VP-first;
* `Sensitivity_a = P(E=1 | R=1, arm=a)` for NE-first and VP-first;
* `Delta_PPV = PPV_NE - PPV_VP`;
* `Delta_Sens = Sens_NE - Sens_VP`.

All episodes receive the same reference process. Report complete three-by-three counts for `E∈{0,1,?}` and `R∈{0,1,?}` by arm, site, interface, person, and first/recurrent status. The primary conservative bounds are obtained by enumerating all binary completions of indeterminate cells in each arm, subject only to observed determinate labels. For each completion compute the metric and confidence interval. The conservative arm-specific lower confidence bound is the minimum over completions. For each arm difference, take the union envelope of score confidence intervals over joint completions: its lower endpoint is the minimum lower limit and its upper endpoint the maximum upper limit. This joint envelope replaces incompatible metric-specific single imputations and prevents indeterminacy from producing apparent accuracy or equivalence. If exhaustive enumeration is large, the verifier may use mathematically equivalent corner/optimization code whose equality is unit-tested on small tables. Determinate-only estimates are secondary.

Secondary outputs are determinate confusion matrices, specificity, NPV, prevalence `P(R=1|arm)`, index positivity `P(E=1|arm)`, indeterminate fractions, paired E-minus-R prevalence discrepancy with patient-cluster interval, McNemar discordance for determinate pairs, pump-only performance, event matching, and documentation strata (`Paused`/`Stopped`, shared/distinct chain, R2 compatible/incompatible, MAP deterioration). These strata are diagnostic and cannot redefine the test.

## Confidence, equivalence, multiplicity, and analysis sets

For each arm-specific PPV and sensitivity component, use the one-sided 95% Wilson score lower bound and require it to be **strictly greater than 0.80**. For each metric difference, use a 90% Miettinen-Newcombe/Newcombe hybrid-score interval and require it to lie **strictly inside (-0.10,0.10)**. At exact boundaries, the component fails. The six-component claim is a prespecified intersection-union test: all four accuracy and both equivalence components must pass on conservative bounds. No multiplicity correction is needed for this conjunctive global claim because any component failure defeats it. No individual component may be described as confirmatorily established when the global claim fails; individual intervals are reported descriptively. Threshold/off-duration/clock sensitivities, pump-only analyses, recurrence analyses, event matching, and site/interface analyses are nonconfirmatory; false-discovery language is prohibited. The four planned arm-by-site/interface interaction families use Holm-adjusted two-sided 0.05 tests and simultaneous 95% intervals.

The primary repeated-episode point estimates are episode-weighted because the future endpoint is episode-level. Patient-cluster bootstrap intervals with at least 10,000 resamples are reported as a dependence sensitivity; if they reverse any confirmatory pass, the global conclusion is inconclusive. Generalized estimating-equation and first-person-only estimates are secondary. Sites enter as prespecified strata; pooled estimates are not allowed to conceal an interface failure.

## Prevalence-aware denominator targets and operationally attainable accrual

The target remains 220 EHR-positive episodes per arm for PPV and 220 reference-positive episodes per arm for sensitivity. At denominator 220, at least 186 correct classifications are required for a one-sided 95% Wilson lower bound >0.80. If true accuracy is 0.90, component power is about 99.6%; a fixed equal-denominator Wald planning approximation gives about 93.3% power for one metric's ±0.10 equivalence test. These are component planning properties, not global power and not clinical truth.

The parent's 2,000-total cap is not coherent with its own frozen planning signal. In the retrospective audit, VP-first constituted 53/137 episodes and only 13/53 were EHR-positive. Holding those proportions purely as a stress scenario, 2,000 total episodes yield about 190 VP-first EHR-positive episodes and only a 1.3% binomial probability of reaching 220; approximately 2,511 total episodes are needed for 90% probability. MIMIC provides no reference rescue prevalence. At the same VP-first arm share, 220 VP-first reference positives require about 6,174 total episodes for 90% probability if prevalence is 10%, 4,112 if 15%, 3,082 if 20%, and 2,463 if 25%. These are scenario calculations, not prospective prevalence estimates.

Accordingly, use a blinded internal-pilot accrual plan rather than claiming fixed feasibility:

1. Enroll and fully verify the first 600 validation episodes across all sites. This is not a disposable pilot; episodes remain in the final analysis.
2. A statistician independent of adjudication and accuracy reporting may view only arm, `E` denominator membership, `R` denominator membership, indeterminate status, site/interface, patient recurrence, and calendar accrual—not the `E×R` cross-tab or any accuracy estimate.
3. Using Beta(1,1)-binomial posterior predictive simulation separately for each site's arm share, E-positive prevalence, R-positive prevalence, indeterminate probability, and monthly accrual, estimate the probability of reaching all four 220-denominator targets by 48 months and by 6,000 total episodes under currently active sites. The operational projection is planning only and does not change inference.
4. Continue unchanged if predictive probability by 48 months is ≥0.80 and no arm/metric requires >6,000 projected episodes. Expand sites/interfaces under the identical protocol if probability is 0.20–<0.80. Stop as accrual-infeasible if probability is <0.20 or any target's posterior median required total exceeds 6,000. Repeat the blinded projection at 1,200 and 2,400 episodes. No unblinded accuracy look, alpha spending, early success, accuracy-based futility, threshold change, positive enrichment, or denominator subsampling is allowed.
5. Final administrative lock is the earliest of all four targets attained followed by a common 30-day closeout, 6,000 validation episodes, or 48 months after the first site enters validation. Analyze every episode accrued through lock, including faster-arm excess. Failure to reach all four targets is inconclusive, not adverse accuracy evidence.

Quarterly reports expose only the permitted blinded accrual fields. Enrollment-rate and site-expansion decisions are not inferential looks, so no alpha is spent. This plan explicitly permits an honest infeasibility result when rare VP-first positives or rescue prevalence make full verification unattainable.

## Site/interface transport gate

The target of transport is a **pump-EHR interface configuration**, defined before analysis by site, pump vendor/model and software major version, integration engine, EHR medication-administration module/build, drug-library version, and clock-mapping method. A software/interface change creates a new configuration stratum and requires a 50-episode run-in; it cannot be pooled silently with its predecessor.

A pooled transportable claim requires at least six sites, at least two pump-EHR vendor/configuration families, both stop arms represented at at least four sites and two interface families, and no site or interface family contributing >30% of any of the four metric denominators. Each arm-metric must have denominator ≥40 in at least three sites and ≥20 in at least four sites; otherwise pooled accuracy may be reported but transport is inconclusive. Report site/interface estimates, leave-one-site-out and leave-one-interface-family-out results, hierarchical binomial estimates, and 95% prediction intervals.

For every sufficiently supported site/interface stratum (denominator ≥40), the conservative one-sided lower bound must exceed 0.70 for each observed arm-metric, and leave-one-site/interface-out analyses must preserve the global gates. A 95% prediction interval extending below 0.70, adjusted site/interface accuracy range >0.15, or Holm-significant interaction accompanied by an absolute difference >0.10 is adverse for a transportable raw-EHR endpoint even if pooled gates pass. If support is too sparse to classify these rules, transport is inconclusive, not homogeneous. A result may be supportive only for explicitly enumerated validated configurations.

Stop site accrual for linkage, clock, or pump-export failure in >5% of its latest 50 episodes and require a new run-in before resumption. Stop globally as operationally inconclusive after two adjudication-reliability failures or inability to maintain four validation sites.

## Falsification and interpretation

**Supportive:** all six conservative confirmatory gates pass; all denominator, capture, clock, adjudication, recurrence-dependence, and site/interface transport gates are met; and no prespecified clock/off-duration sensitivity reverses the result. This supports use of the locked restart component only in the enumerated validated interfaces.

**Adverse:** a completed conservative arm-specific PPV or sensitivity lower bound fails, an equivalence envelope is not wholly within ±0.10, paired errors are directionally material, event matching exposes systematic missing/spurious events, or adequately supported interface heterogeneity crosses the adverse transport rules. High PPV cannot rescue low sensitivity. No favorable retrospective continuity rule may be selected post hoc.

**Inconclusive:** denominators are not attained; a confidence bound touches/crosses a threshold; conservative indeterminate envelopes prevent a decision; recurrence-robust intervals reverse a pass; clock, linkage, capture, intent, reliability, site concentration, or transport support fails; or blinded accrual projections/administrative caps show the study cannot attain full verification. Inconclusive is not evidence of accuracy or symmetry.

A verifier can check cohort logic, repeated-episode spacing, labels supplied by the prospective files, complete tables, conservative envelopes, score intervals, matching, blinded accrual gates, transport gates, and whether conclusions follow from outputs. It cannot establish pump delivery, rescue intent, true MCS absence, clinical importance, safety, equipoise, or causal stopping-order benefit. Those require device evidence, expert adjudication, and separate prospective/randomized work.
