TITLE
Daily extubation-readiness evaluation versus an explicit no-trial decision and near-term durable ventilator liberation in eICU: a multicenter sequential-landmark emulation

CLINICAL QUESTION AND FALSIFIABLE HYPOTHESIS
Among adult ICU stays that remain invasively ventilated 24-120 hours after ICU admission and satisfy an oxygenation/hemodynamic screen using only measurements observed before a daily landmark, does a documented plan for "Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation," compared with the contemporaneous explicit state "Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial," predict more durable liberation from invasive ventilation within 72 hours without more recurrent invasive ventilation or death?

Hypothesis: after adjustment for measured pre-landmark severity, respiratory support, sedation/vasopressor exposure, trajectory, hospital, and day of ventilation, daily-evaluation decisions will be associated with a higher 72-hour cumulative incidence of durable documented liberation than explicit no-trial decisions. This is falsified if the adjusted effect is null/adverse with clinically informative precision, fails outcome-definition concordance, is reproduced for a pre-exposure placebo outcome, or depends on hospitals without within-hospital exposure overlap.

WHY THIS IS UNRESOLVED AND IMPORTANT
The 2024 AARC spontaneous-breathing-trial guideline (Roberts et al., Respir Care; PMID 38443142; PMCID PMC11285503; DOI 10.4187/respcare.11735) supports standardized daily readiness assessment and, when appropriate, SBT completion before noon, but grades the timing recommendation as very-low-certainty evidence. Trials and guidelines support protocolized readiness assessment generally; they do not establish the comparative effect of an evaluation versus a documented no-trial decision at a standardized ventilator-day decision point across heterogeneous routine-care hospitals. Delayed recognition may prolong ventilation, whereas testing patients with neurologic, oxygenation, or circulatory contraindications could lead to failed liberation.

Strongest claim already supported: systematic daily assessment/SBT protocols are accepted elements of adult ventilator liberation and can identify readiness that clinician recognition alone misses.
Untested claim: among screen-stable eICU patient-days, the observed daily-evaluation care-plan state is associated with earlier durable liberation, without an adverse recurrence/death signal, across hospitals.
The eICU experiment can establish only a temporally ordered, adjusted multicenter association involving a structured care-plan decision. It cannot establish that an SBT actually occurred, that the patient was clinically extubatable, or that changing the care-plan decision would cause benefit.

TARGET-TRIAL EMULATION
Population:
- Adult age >=18; map age "> 89" to 90 for adjustment and flag it.
- First qualifying ICU stay per uniquepid in the primary analysis; all stays with patient-clustered inference in sensitivity analysis.
- ICU stay lasting beyond its landmark.
- Evidence of ongoing invasive ventilation before each landmark from a prespecified state algorithm. High-specificity evidence is the most recent carePlanGeneral Ventilation state being either daily evaluation, no daily trial, or chronic dependency; or explicit respiratoryCharting RT Vent On/Continued plus invasive ventilator settings; or Oral/Nasal ETT/tracheostomy in respiratoryCare plus ventilator settings/treatment. Non-invasive ventilation alone does not qualify.
- Exclude chronic ventilator dependency as the latest state, tracheostomy already present before landmark in the primary analysis, documented EOL discussion before landmark, implausible offsets, and prior durable liberation.

Sequential landmarks/time zero:
- Landmarks L = 24, 48, 72, 96, and 120 hours after ICU admission (1440, 2880, 4320, 5760, 7200 minutes).
- Use only information with clinical event offset < L for eligibility/confounding. A stay can contribute at successive landmarks until liberation, death, discharge, EOL discussion, or day 5.
- Exposure has a 6-hour assignment grace period [L, L+360]. Clone each eligible person-landmark to both strategies; censor a clone when the first qualifying care-plan decision in the grace period is inconsistent with its strategy, and use inverse-probability-of-adherence weights. If both contradictory labels occur at the same earliest offset, classify ambiguous and exclude from the primary analysis. As a simpler prespecified secondary analysis, use the first qualifying label in the grace window and start follow-up at that label's cplitemoffset.

Screen-stable primary cohort (all measured strictly before L):
- Latest normalized invasive FiO2 in prior 6 hours <=0.50. Normalize values 21-100 as percentages and 0.21-1.0 as fractions; reject other implausible values.
- Latest PEEP/PEEP-CPAP in prior 6 hours <=8 cm H2O.
- Median SpO2 in prior 2 hours >=88% and <=100%, with at least two readings.
- Latest respiratory rate in prior 2 hours 8-35/min.
- MAP >=65 mm Hg using median invasive/noninvasive mean pressure in prior 2 hours, or no hypotensive reading <60 in that window.
- No increase in number of simultaneously charted vasopressor classes in the prior 6 hours versus the preceding 6 hours. Vasopressor class presence is based on infusion records for norepinephrine, epinephrine, vasopressin, phenylephrine, dopamine; because infusionDrug records are point charting rather than reliable start-stop intervals, this criterion and any inferred activity window are sensitivity analyses unless empirically validated.
- Measurements must exist for FiO2 and PEEP. The strict complete-screen cohort additionally requires SpO2, RR, and MAP. The broader primary cohort requires FiO2/PEEP and models missing SpO2/RR/MAP explicitly; report both and require directional concordance.
- Neurologic airway protection, secretions, planned procedures and complete sedation contraindications are not reliably available and therefore cannot be used to declare true SBT eligibility. Available exact-label RASS/GCS/sedation fields and sedative infusions are adjustment variables and sensitivity exclusions, not proof of eligibility.

Strategies/exposure:
A. First Ventilation-group carePlanGeneral label in the grace period is exactly "Ventilated - with daily extubation evaluation".
B. First label is exactly "Ventilated - with no daily extubation trial".
The exposure is a documented care-plan decision, not a completed SBT. Explicit respiratoryCharting SBT Method/RSBI/weaning fields validate interpretation in the subset but do not define the broad exposure.

Follow-up:
From L under cloning (or decision offset in secondary analysis) through 72 hours for the primary outcome, and through 7 days/ICU discharge for secondary outcomes. Death and ICU discharge are competing/truncating events as specified below.

OUTCOMES
Primary: 72-hour durable documented liberation proxy. Liberation onset is the earliest of:
- carePlanGeneral Ventilation state "Spontaneous - adequate" after an invasive state;
- respiratoryCharting RT Vent On/Off value "Off" (case-insensitive); or
- treatmentstring exactly within the endotracheal-tube-removal hierarchy,
provided there is no high-specificity recurrent invasive-ventilation marker for the subsequent 48 hours. A "Ventilated - rapid wean/extubation" label alone is not liberation. Live ICU discharge before completing 48 hours counts as liberated only if a liberation marker preceded discharge and unitdischargelocation is not another ICU; otherwise status is truncated/unknown, not automatically successful.

Primary competing outcome: ICU death before durable liberation. Estimate 72-hour cumulative incidence/risk difference with death as competing event; also report the composite of no durable liberation or death.

Secondary:
- Time to first liberation marker.
- Recurrent invasive ventilation within 48 hours and within 7 days after a liberation marker (proxy for reintubation), requiring a new RT Vent Start/Continued, ETT insertion treatment, invasive care-plan state, or invasive airway plus ventilator settings after >=6 hours without invasive markers. Report alternate gaps of 1, 12, and 24 hours.
- Ventilator-free days through day 7, bounded by observable ICU follow-up; death receives zero.
- ICU mortality, hospital mortality, ICU length of stay from unitdischargeoffset.
- Explicit SBT/weaning documentation within 24 hours as a process endpoint, not a patient benefit endpoint.

Outcome-definition validation is mandatory: report each component, agreement between care-plan transition and ETT-removal/RT-Vent-Off markers, and results under (i) care-plan-only, (ii) explicit-off/removal-only, and (iii) union algorithms. If component definitions disagree materially or one strategy has markedly different outcome-documentation density, the clinical liberation endpoint is inconclusive.

COVARIATES AND TEMPORAL FEATURES
At admission: age, sex, ethnicity, weight, admission diagnosis, surgical/medical status, ICU type/source, transfer status, APACHE score/predicted mortality where available, APACHE ventilation flags, comorbidities, hospital bed category/teaching/region.
Before each L: ventilation day; hours since first invasive marker; current and 6/12/24-hour summaries/slopes for FiO2, PEEP, SpO2, RR, tidal volume, pressure support; pH/PaCO2/PaO2/lactate; HR/MAP; temperature; creatinine/bicarbonate/hemoglobin/WBC; sedative/opioid classes (propofol, dexmedetomidine, midazolam, fentanyl) and vasopressor classes; exact-label neurologic/sedation values; prior evaluation/no-trial labels and transitions; measurement counts and time since last measurement. Never use post-L covariates or future APACHE summary outcomes.

ESTIMANDS AND ANALYSIS
Primary estimand: standardized 72-hour risk difference and risk ratio for durable documented liberation under assignment to daily evaluation versus explicit no trial at an eligible landmark, averaged over the observed screen-stable person-landmark population. This is an observational per-decision estimand, not an effect of actual SBT performance.

- Emulate nested trials at each landmark with cloning/censoring/weighting for the 6-hour grace period. Model adherence/censoring using only history available up to each interval; truncate stabilized weights at the 1st/99th percentiles and report effective sample size and alternatives (0.5/99.5, no truncation).
- Fit pooled discrete-time cause-specific outcome models with flexible landmark/day terms and hospital fixed effects; use doubly robust standardization/TMLE or augmented IPW as the main estimator. Cluster uncertainty by uniquepid and hospital using a multiway bootstrap or hospital bootstrap with patient resampling.
- Propensity/adherence models include all listed pre-L covariates and hospital. Do not report a causal estimate when positivity fails. Report standardized mean differences before/after weighting, weight distribution, and overlap by hospital/day.
- Primary hospital set requires both strategies and at least 10 person-landmarks per strategy; a >=20 sensitivity threshold is prespecified. Analyze other hospitals descriptively only. Compare fixed-effect pooled results, random-effects hospital-specific estimates, and leave-one-hospital-out estimates. Report between-hospital heterogeneity rather than treating hospital practice variation as randomization.
- Secondary first-qualifying-decision analysis uses overlap weighting and the same temporal covariates. It is explicitly more vulnerable to documentation-time selection.
- Multiple landmarks from a stay are retained with patient clustering; first eligible landmark only is a sensitivity analysis.
- Prespecified subgroup interactions: ventilation day (1-2 vs 3-5), FiO2 <=0.40 vs 0.41-0.50, PEEP <=5 vs 6-8, medical vs surgical, neurologic admission diagnosis, and high vs low hospital evaluation propensity. These are exploratory with simultaneous uncertainty.

MISSINGNESS AND DATA QUALITY
- Report availability and time-since-last-measurement by exposure, hospital and landmark for every eligibility/covariate/outcome component.
- Do not code missing physiology as normal. The strict screen is complete-case by design; the broader cohort uses missing indicators plus within-hospital/landmark multiple imputation for covariates, never exposure/outcome.
- Normalize label-specific units before aggregation and retain source label/unit flags. Winsorization is limited to prespecified physiologic plausibility bounds, with rejected values counted.
- Hospitals with systematic absence of FiO2/PEEP or one exposure are excluded from comparative estimation but retained in the flow diagram.
- Chart-entry-time sensitivity requires respchartentryoffset/nursingchartentryoffset <= L when present, to test delayed documentation.

NEGATIVE CONTROLS, SENSITIVITIES, AND FALSIFICATION
1. Temporal placebo outcome: transition to spontaneous-adequate/RT-Vent-Off/ETT-removal in the 24 hours before L. A post-L exposure should not predict this after adjustment. A material association in the same direction indicates residual severity/documentation-time bias.
2. Future-exposure placebo: among person-landmarks without a current qualifying decision, use the next day's label to "predict" current 0-6-hour physiology improvement; association indicates trajectory/confounding and is not evidence of benefit.
3. Exposure validation: in stays with explicit SBT Method, RSBI, weaning-protocol, Sedation Wean, or ETT Sedation Vacation documentation, compare 24-hour occurrence after the two labels. Daily evaluation should enrich, but nonconcordance demonstrates it is not synonymous with completed SBT.
4. Outcome variants and recurrence gaps as above; exclude tracheostomy, neurologic diagnoses, deep sedation where validated, vasopressor exposure, EOL discussions; require continuous ICU observation; use first stay/first landmark.
5. Quantitative bias analysis/E-value for an unmeasured contraindication with plausible prevalence and strength; interpretation remains associative regardless.
6. Hospital-specific documentation-density adjustment and leave-hospital-out estimates.

Hard falsification/inconclusive criteria:
- Inadequate positivity: after hospital/day restriction, either strategy has <200 effective weighted person-landmarks, >10% of weights are truncated, or substantial covariates retain absolute SMD >0.10.
- Temporal placebo risk-difference magnitude >=50% of the primary adjusted risk-difference magnitude or statistically incompatible with zero in the same direction.
- Direction reverses between strict and broad screens, between care-plan-only and explicit-marker outcome algorithms, or after exclusion of any single dominant hospital, without an explained clinical interaction.
- Fewer than 10 hospitals have >=10 observations per strategy after final screen, or explicit outcome-marker agreement is too sparse to estimate recurrence reliably.
- A precise null/adverse estimate (95% CI excludes a clinically relevant +5 percentage-point 72-hour liberation benefit) falsifies the proposed beneficial association in this population. Imprecision spanning meaningful benefit and harm is inconclusive, not supportive.

INTERPRETATION RULES
Supportive: adjusted daily-evaluation assignment has a higher 72-hour durable-liberation risk, no increase in 48-hour recurrence or death, adequate overlap/balance, null temporal placebo, directional concordance across strict/broad screens and outcome algorithms, and no single-hospital dependence. Conclusion permitted: a robust multicenter association supports prospective evaluation of timely readiness assessment. Not permitted: daily evaluation/SBT causes liberation or should be mandated.

Adverse: lower liberation or higher recurrence/death with daily evaluation, robust across definitions. This may reflect harmful premature testing or residual indication/documentation bias; it argues against assuming benefit and motivates clinical adjudication, not immediate abandonment of protocols.

Inconclusive: limited overlap, sparse no-trial decisions, discordant ventilation markers, large missingness, placebo failure, hospital dependence, or confidence intervals spanning material benefit and harm. Conclusion: eICU cannot resolve the decision with this operationalization.

CLAIMS REQUIRING OTHER EVIDENCE
Whether an SBT truly occurred; clinician reasons for withholding it; airway protection, secretion burden, planned procedures and exact goals of care; true extubation/reintubation adjudication; post-ICU reintubation; and causal clinical benefit require manual clinical adjudication, more granular ventilator/EHR data, or a prospective trial. An automatic verifier can check cohort construction, temporal ordering, labels, model outputs, diagnostics and whether conclusions follow from prespecified rules; it cannot establish bedside eligibility or causal truth.

ACTUAL FEASIBILITY AND SAMPLING ALREADY PERFORMED
Full scans, not samples:
- patient.csv.gz: 200,859 ICU-stay rows.
- carePlanGeneral.csv.gz: 3,115,018 rows; selected cplgroup="Ventilation", seven predeclared values, excluded negative offsets, and deduplicated exact (offset,state). There were 51,862 daily-evaluation rows in 40,422 stays and 14,907 no-daily-trial rows in 7,860 stays. In the preliminary first-label window 24-120 hours (not readiness restricted), daily evaluation: 6,444 stays, 171 hospitals, 111 hospitals with >=10, with 1,739 (26.99%) transitioning to spontaneous-adequate or rapid within 48 hours; no trial: 1,926 stays, 115 hospitals, 27 hospitals with >=10, 404 (20.98%) with that crude proxy. There were 1,193 no-trial-to-evaluation transitions in that window across 95 hospitals. These crude proxies are not treatment effects and "rapid" is not used as liberation in the final endpoint.
- respiratoryCharting.csv.gz: all 20,168,176 rows and 194 labels. FiO2 3,075,308 rows/75,234 stays; PEEP 1,464,808 rows/45,541 stays; RT Vent On/Off 60,101 rows/2,741 stays; Total RSBI 7,017 rows/1,179 stays; SBT Method 1,435 rows/632 stays; Weaning Assessment Criteria Collaboration 2,691 rows/191 stays; CCU Ventilator Weaning Protocol Started 1,864 rows/167 stays; Sedation Wean 1,560 rows/143 stays. Thus explicit SBT fields are too sparse for the broad primary exposure but useful for validation.
- respiratoryCare.csv.gz: all 865,381 rows/44,772 stays. Oral ETT appeared in 61,942 rows/8,110 stays; tracheostomy in 8,468 rows/1,028 stays. ventendoffset was 0 in 865,224 rows, precluding naive use.
- treatment.csv.gz: full scan; 117,481 mechanical-ventilation hierarchy rows, 19,578 ventilator-weaning rows and 8,760 endotracheal-tube-removal rows (row counts, not unique events).
- infusionDrug.csv.gz: full scan confirmed abundant propofol, fentanyl, midazolam, dexmedetomidine, norepinephrine, vasopressin and epinephrine charting with heterogeneous names/units.
- nurseCare.csv.gz: all 8,311,132 rows screened for SBT/weaning/extubation/sedation-interruption terms, with no relevant matches. An unqualified nurseAssessment match for "spontaneous" referred to neurologic stimulus, so lexical narrative inference is prohibited.

The final strict-screen person-landmark size and event count have not yet been computed. The experiment must report the full attrition table before fitting effects; feasibility above supports attempting the emulation but does not guarantee adequate positivity after objective screening.
